The '''AT-ST Unit Expansion''' ('''SWL08''') is an expansion to ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'', an asymmetrical two player infantry-based wargame published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]]. As indicated by the name, it includes a scale model of the eponymous Imperial walker. It was first announced on [[October 25]], [[2017]], and was released on [[March 22]], [[2018]]. An updated version, the [[AT-ST Walker Unit Expansion]], was later released in [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
===Online summary===
''The power of the Empire is visible on any subjugated world&ndash;few images are more representative of Imperial power than the shining white armor of a Stormtrooper or the rumble of a walker stomping through city streets or across the battlefield. Soon, you'll be able to supplement your Stormtroopers with another symbol of Imperial oppression: the All Terrain Scout Transport!''

''An AT-ST is an awe-inspiring sight to the average foot soldier. It towers above the battlefield, stalking inimically forward on mechanized legs and raining a hail of fire and death from its laser cannons and powerful weapons systems. With the AT-ST Unit Expansion for Star Wars™: Legion, you'll find a single finely sculpted, massive AT-ST miniature, which can be assembled to loom over the battlefield and provide a rallying point for your entire army. Alongside this beautiful miniature, you'll find an assortment of all the unit cards and upgrade cards that you'll need to fully integrate the AT-ST into the Empire's war machine&ndash;and your armies.''

===Back blurb summary===
''The intimidating Imperial AT-ST returns to Star Wars: Legion! A walking weapons platform, the All Terrain Scout Transport is a terrifying sight on the battlefield. With their diversity of armaments, AT-SIs are effective against multiple different targets, from infantry in cover to heavily armored vehicles. With their long legs, AT-STs are rarely impeded by obstacles as they stride into battle and rain laser fire down upon their enemies.''

''Additionally, the miniature in this pack now features new customization options, allowing players to assemble it with General Weiss or Chewbacca in the top hatch. Includes both the Imperial unit card and the Mercenary Ewok unit card.''<ref name="AT-ST unit" />
