''' Chloa''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who lived in the [[city]] of [[Tavuu]] on the [[planet]] [[Radhii]] at the [[time]] [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] was reported [[Death|dead]] and the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] was [[Battle of Endor|destroyed]].<ref name="Turning Point" />

She was a [[Parent|mother]] and [[Marriage|wife]] of [[Gorlan Seba]] who was an expert guide of the [[Zoess]], a large [[forest]]. Her husband was forced to lead a team of [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] after [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebels]] who escaped [[Prison|imprisonment]] through the Zoess forest.<ref name="Turning Point" />
