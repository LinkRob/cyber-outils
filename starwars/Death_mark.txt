==History==
After the [[Westhill Palace]] was invaded and the [[starship]] [[Trailblazer]] was stolen by [[Canto Bight]] criminal [[Kay Vess]], [[Sliro Barsha]], leader of the [[Zerek Besh]] crime syndicate and owner of the starship and mansion, issued a death mark against Vess.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

When [[Han Solo]] did not pay off his debts to [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]], Jabba placed a [[death]] mark on him. The death mark was so large that Solo felt he had no choice but to leave the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] in order to pay off his debt. This plan was thwarted, however, when the Rebels were [[Battle of Hoth|attacked]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] and Solo was forced to flee along with [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and the [[droid]]s [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]] in the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. [[Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]] of the Empire then commissioned [[Boba Fett]], a [[bounty hunter]], to hunt down Solo.<ref name="Episode V" />

Following the [[Battle of Endor]], Governor [[Ubrik Adelhard]] locked down the [[Anoat sector]] and established the [[Iron Blockade]] to prevent the sector from hearing of [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]'s demise. Anyone who did - including a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] and his [[Riley|sister]] who stumbled across [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]'s message of the battle's outcome to the galaxy - subsequently had a death mark placed on them and was relentlessly hunted down by Adelhard's top enforcer [[Commander]] [[Bragh]] and his [[Purge Trooper (Anoat sector)|Purge Troopers]]. To prevent the information from spreading, any individual or known associate of those who knew of the truth was also tracked down and killed. In an attempt to have the death mark from her and her sibling removed, Riley and the [[Ivax Syndicate]] struck a deal with Bragh and allowed the Empire to capture the leaders of the syndicate's rivals, the [[Noble Court]] and the [[Kouhun]], in exchange for amnesty.<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref>

The [[Trandoshan]] [[smuggler]] [[Feresk Tssat]] earned a death mark when he was hired by an Imperial [[governor]] to acquire a painting. The governor in turn showed the painting to [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] who noticed that, unbeknownst to Feresk, the painting was a counterfeit. To escape the death mark, Feresk joined the Rebel Alliance as a [[starfighter]] [[New Republic pilot|pilot]].<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>

[[Pathfinders (Navigators' Guild)|Pathfinder]] [[navigator]] [[Qilori]] of Uandualon believed the [[Chiss]] [[Junior Commander]] Thrawn earned a death mark by [[Skirmish in the Kinoss system|alerting]] the [[Lioaoin Regime]] that they were [[Lioaoin pirate campaign|hunting pirates]] at the [[Lioaoin heartworld]]. [[Standard year|Years]] later, [[General]] [[Yiv]] the Benevolent of the [[Nikardun Destiny]] told Thrawn at the [[battle over Primea]] that he had "earned death" for [[Raid on Rapacc|capturing]] one of his [[Nikardun blockade frigate|warships]].<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref>
