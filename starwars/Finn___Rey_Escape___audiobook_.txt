'''''Finn & Rey Escape!''''' is the unabridged audio version of the [[canon]] young-readers book [[Finn & Rey Escape!|of the same name]]. The audiobook was published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[September 19]], [[2024]].<ref name="Audible" />

==Publisher's summary==
''When Finn and Rey find themselves on the run, they must work together to escape from the evil First Order. Listen as the action unfolds in this exciting Star Wars audio story!''<ref name="Audible" />
