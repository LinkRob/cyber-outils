The '''All Terrain Mobile Artillery (AT-MA)'''<ref name="IncrediBuilds" /> was a model of four-[[Limb|legged]] base defense [[walker]] used by the [[First Order]] to defend [[Starkiller Base]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> While it resembled the [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] once used by the [[Galactic Empire]] in terms of size and weaponry, the AT-MA was primarily designed for defense, rather than transport or assault.<ref name="IncrediBuilds" /> Several of these walkers were deployed across the [[planet]] to protect its installations, and at least three of them were present during [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]]'s speech before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of the [[Hosnian system]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> Several All Terrain Mobile Artillery walkers could be stored in the ground vehicles storage area of a [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]].<ref name="TFAICS">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:FirstOrder.png|thumb|left|Teaser image, walkers on the right.]]
Originally created for the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']],<ref name="Episode VII" /> the All Terrain Mobile Artillery was first revealed in a promotional image shared by ''Star Wars'' Twitter and Google+ accounts on [[August 10]], [[2015]].<ref name="First Order">{{Twitter|starwars|status/630804948306010112|Star Wars|quote=The First Order assembles. #TheForceAwakens|archivefile=File:The First Order Assembles Twitter.png}}</ref> It was first identified in ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: AT-ACT Deluxe Book and 3D Wood Model]]'', a ''[[Star Wars IncrediBuilds]]'' woodshop activity book written by [[Michael Kogge]]<ref name="IncrediBuilds" /> and released on [[May 23]], [[2017]].<ref name="IncrediBuilds release date">{{SW|url=news/6-behind-the-scenes-details-of-the-rogue-one-incredibuilds-book-and-model-sets|text=6 Behind-the-Scenes Details of the ''Rogue One IncrediBuilds'' Book and Model Sets}}</ref>
[[File:DroidWalker.png|thumb|right|250px|Concept art of the All Terrain Mobile Artillery by James Clyne.]]
Concept art for vehicles similar to the walkers appears in ''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''.<ref name="The Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref>
