'''Croce''' was a terrestrial celestial body<ref name="AlienArchive" /> located in the [[Croce system]], a part of the [[Yminis sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It was situated in grid square S-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Croce was relatively [[Temperature|cool]] and had both [[water]] and nearby [[cave]]rns on its surface.<ref name="AlienArchive" />

Croce was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Crocin]], a [[species]] of [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Reptile|reptilians]]<ref name="AlienArchive" /> who spoke the [[Croci]] [[language]].<ref name="YouTube" /> The Crocin lived in [[City|cities]] built in Croce's caverns and were not used to bright [[Star|sunlight]]. The Crocin<ref name="AlienArchive" /> [[spacer]]<ref name="SoloGuide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> [[Karjj]] hailed from Croce, a fact that was noted in the posting of a [[bounty]] issued for<ref name="Datapad">{{DatapadCite|''[[Star Wars: Batuu Bounty Hunters]]''|BatuuBountyHunters-Karjj.png}}</ref> him<ref name="SoloGuide" /> by the [[time]] of the [[First Order]] [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Datapad" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GEDates">''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' dates the [[Battle of Batuu]], which is depicted in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] via the attraction ''[[Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance]]'', to 34 [[Standard year|years]] after {{Film|IV}}, which corresponds to [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore the events of Galaxy's Edge in general, which includes the events of ''[[Star Wars: Batuu Bounty Hunters]]'', must also take place that year.</ref>

At one point, Croce was also mentioned in an entry of an [[Unidentified artist|artist's]] [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]] dedicated to the Crocin. The journal was restored, expanded, and displayed at the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="AlienArchive" /> at some point no earlier than 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Croce was introduced in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]'' by [[Natalie Clubb]] and [[Katrina Pallant]].<ref name="AlienArchive" />
