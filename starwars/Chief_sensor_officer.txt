'''Chief sensor officer''',<ref name="Thrawn" /> also known as a '''sensor chief''',<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> was a [[sensor officer]] [[Assignment|position]] within the [[Imperial Navy|starfleet]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. His or her duty was to watch a [[starship]]'s [[sensor]] station. [[Senior Lieutenant]] [[Hammerly]] held the post aboard the [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]] ''[[Thunder Wasp]]'', although [[Executive officer|First Officer]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] briefly assumed it during the [[Battle over Umbara|mission to Umbara]].<ref name="Thrawn" />
