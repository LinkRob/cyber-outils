During the [[High Republic Era]], the '''Battle for Naboo''',<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 2" /> also known as a '''mission to liberate Naboo''',<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 3>[[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 3|''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 3]]</ref> was fought when the [[Tempest Breakers]] guild of [[bounty hunter]]s [[Lourna Dee]] and [[Quin Carree]], as well as the [[Sephi]] [[Tey Sirrek]], carried out a mission to<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 1" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Naboo]]&mdash;which was under Nihil [[Second occupation of Naboo|occupation]]&mdash;to find surviving members of [[Nihil]] [[Baron]] [[Boolan]]'s [[Children of the Storm]] who had scattered following Boolan's arrest. While on Naboo, the three were found by Nihil, though [[Jedi Master]] [[Kelnacca]] and<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 1" /> his<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 2" /> [[Padawan]] [[Yarzion Vell]] arrived to help them. Afterward, the Jedi agreed to help the three in their mission, with the Tempest Breakers and Sirrek agreeing to help liberate the planet in return.<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 1" />
