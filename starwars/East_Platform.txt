The '''East Platform''' was a landing platform located in [[Cloud City]], a [[mining]] colony floating in the [[Life Zone]] of the [[planet]] [[Bespin]]. The [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] landed there with his [[starship]] ''[[Slave I]]'' when he came to retrieve the [[carbonite]]-encased [[Han Solo]].<ref name="Episode V" />
