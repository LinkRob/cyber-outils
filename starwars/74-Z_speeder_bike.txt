On Endor, scout troopers used these bikes for recon and patrol missions, though the densely packed trees of the Sanctuary Moon's forests made for hazardous conditions at high speeds.|The ''[[Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''|<ref name="Galactic Explorer's Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref>}}
The '''74-Z speeder bike'''<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> was a model of [[speeder bike]] used by the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Databank" /> and later used by the [[Galactic Empire]] for combat reconnaissance purposes during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. They were commonly piloted by [[scout trooper]]s<ref name="RotJ" /> or regular [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Skywalker Strikes Part III">[[Star Wars (2015) 3|''Star Wars'' (2015) 3]]</ref> They were able to jam [[comlink]]s.<ref name="RotJ" />

==Description==
[[File:FineArtPrints-Endor-Chase.png|thumb|left|250px|A scout trooper, a typical pilot for the 74-Z, pilots one of the speeder bikes.]]
The 74-Z speeder bike was one of the longest produced [[speeder bike]] models built by the [[Aratech Repulsor Company]]. A militarized variant of Aratech's popular [[74-Y]] speeder bike, the 74-Z was first fielded by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> It was the successor to Aratech's [[BARC speeder]], which was also created for the Grand Army and used in the [[Clone Wars]]. Despite that,<ref name="HC 5">{{HelmetCollectionCite|5|Databank A-Z}}</ref> the 74-Z saw its first test under fire at the [[First Battle of Geonosis]],<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> the first battle of the Clone Wars.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Since that battle, the speeder bike served with countless military and paramilitary groups, [[mercenary]] crews, and criminal organizations. It was, perhaps, most famous for being the [[Imperial Army]]'s ride of choice for reconnaissance and [[scout trooper]]s, who made good use of its speed and maneuverability.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

Like its discontinued civilian ancestor, the 74-Z was built for speed rather than livability. It had a relatively fragile twin-spar frame that mounted the powerful Aratech [[VV318 Scoris repulsorlift engine]]. A pair of tiny ion turbines supplied thrust, and a remarkably intelligent fly-by-wire system controlled the forward-mounted steering vanes. A mid-mounted maneuver control unit kept the bike upright and level. The pilot sat in the rear of the speeder over the [[repulsorlift]] engine. The bike had a set of handlebars and rear-set foot pegs with foot-controlled yaw and pitch controls for the pilot to operate. The instrument panel was rudimentary at best, with indicators for heat, boost, [[coolant]], and turbine pressure. Under [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] service, the 74-Z was equipped with a forward-mounted [[Ax-20 blaster cannon]], on-board [[Comlink|com-links]], simple [[Photoreceptor|optical sensors]], and a close-range com link [[jammer]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

==History==
[[File:SpeederChase-2015StarWars3.png|thumb|right|300px|On a stolen 74-Z during the [[Assault on Cymoon]], [[Luke Skywalker]] is chased by stormtroopers on 74-Zs]]
The 74-Z was used by the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Databank" />, following the [[Siege of Saleucami]], one was ridden by [[Jedi Master]] [[Stass Allie]] before she was [[Death|killed]] [[Order 66|by]] her [[Neyo|own]] [[Unidentified clone lieutenant|men]],<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> and later by the [[Imperial Army]], notably at the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="RotJ" />

Around [[9 ABY]], scout troopers [[AP-1982]] and [[JS-1975]] of [[Moff]] [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Gideon's Imperial Remnant]] used two white 74-Z speeder bikes on the [[planet]] [[Nevarro]] in order to acquire [[Din Grogu|Grogu]]. One of these bikes would later be used shortly after by the [[assassin droid]] [[IG-11]] to protect Grogu and [[Clash on Nevarro|attack]] [[Gideon]] and his forces.<ref name="Chapter 7">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 7: The Reckoning}}</ref>

Three [[Standard decade|decades]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], 74-Z speeder bikes could be found on the [[black market]].<ref name="Cross">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
