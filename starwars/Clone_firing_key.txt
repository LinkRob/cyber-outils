'''Clone firing keys''' were one of the early initiatives taken into consideration by the [[Kaminoan]]s when developing the equipment for [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. They were used to code the equipment specifically to the [[Cloning|clones']] genetic sequence in order to prevent looting and [[Saboteur|sabotage]]. However, the notion was later discontinued as a result of unnecessary costs, despite the savings from having a single genetic sequence shared throughout an army of identical [[trooper]]s, but prototypes of the firing key were created.<ref name="Rise" />

In various cases, firing keys were commissioned for clone units that operated deep behind enemy lines, and weapons that were installed with the firing key read the genetic sequence of the combatant who held it and would not fire unless the sequence matched that of the authorized wielder. The cost of the firing key was 250 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clone firing keys were mentioned in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" />
