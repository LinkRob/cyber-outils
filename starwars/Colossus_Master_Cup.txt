The '''Colossus Master Cup''' was a race hosted on [[Castilon]] in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> The race was made up of several rounds, and the [[pilot]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] would eventually win the race, which allowed him to move on to the more difficult race, the [[Dameron Xtreme Cup]].<ref name="Racer" />

{{Event-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Colossus Master Cup first appeared in the non-[[canon]]ical [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Resistance Racer]]''. The game has the option to race as [[Kazuda Xiono]] or [[Tamara Ryvora]]. This article assumes Kazuda, who is the default choice, is correct.<ref name="Racer" />
