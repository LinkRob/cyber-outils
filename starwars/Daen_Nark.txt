'''Daen Nark''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Zabrak]] and the matriarch of the [[Blacklip Pirates]]. When the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Iron Blockade|blockaded]] the [[Anoat sector]], she and her crew were trapped there. Nark reluctantly accepted that she had to make allies in the [[sector]] to survive.<ref name="Daen" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
Several [[Standard month|months]] into the blockade, Nark was recruited by [[the Smuggler]], who sent the pirate matriarch on a variety of missions across the sector.<ref name="Daen" />
{{EndGame}}
