'''Ansh''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Ithorian]] who worked for [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection]].<ref name="MH" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> she worked on disguising [[Ram Jomaram|Ram]] as a member of the [[Scarlet Skull Cult]]. While she disguised Ram she hummed and muttered to herself. She was noted as cheerful.<ref name="MH" />
