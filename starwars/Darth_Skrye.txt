'''Darth Skrye''' was a [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> [[Sith Lord]]<ref name="Sith Lord">''[[Star Wars Made Easy]]'' establishes that the title of [[Darth]] is given to [[Sith Lord]]s. As the [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]] establishes that Darth Skrye held the title, they must therefore be a Sith Lord.</ref> who held the title of [[Darth]] in a [[Force vision]] of many possible colliding futures experienced by the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Dooku]] after encountering the last coven of the [[Presagers of Hakotei]] during a [[Mission to Asusto|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Asusto]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> in [[82 BBY]].<ref name="Date 82">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'' establishes that the [[mission to Asusto]] took place immediately following the [[funeral of Countess Anya]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[82 BBY]].</ref> In the vision, Dooku heard Skrye announcing that the [[Cauldron]] was opening and that the [[Sith]] were reborn, alongside the sound of a [[planet]] tearing in two and a large explosion. As the vision continued, Skrye's voice overlapped with many others<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> until Dooku emerged, having [[Death|killed]] the Presagers with [[Force lightning]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

When questioned about his vision by [[Jedi Master]] [[Lene Kostana]], Dooku told her that he had seen the Sith, but Kostana reassured him that what he had witnessed were only possibilities that could be prevented. Dooku later documented the events on Asusto in his [[Dooku's journal|journal]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="DJL-JournalsDate">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Dooku allowed his recently recruited [[Sith assassin]], [[Asajj Ventress]], to find his journal during her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|investigation]] into his sister [[Jenza]]'s location, and she listened to the portion that mentioned his vision<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> of Darth Skrye. Skrye shared their name with the [[Hand of Skrye]], an artifact uncovered by Kostana and her Padawan, [[Sifo-Dyas]], prior to the Asusto mission.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Skrye appeared in a vision within a flashback sequence in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-script-by-cavan-scott/9780593157664|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> Their name was given in the [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script]],<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> published on [[October 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Penguin" /> According to Scott, Darth Skrye was a creation of fellow author [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|cavanscott|status/1278361935340281856|[[Cavan Scott]]|quote=Fun fact. Darth Skrye is a @claudiagray creation.|archivefile=File:ScottSkrye.png}}</ref>
