The '''Battle of Parein II 4'''<ref name="Selection (II)" /> was fought on the [[Parein II 4|fourth]] moon of the planet [[Parein II]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. During the battle, [[Jedi General]] [[Sannen]] led the [[Republic Military]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] and his forces,<ref name="English Battle Droids Encyclopedia" /> which included Grievous's [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s. Putting them to their first real test, the MagnaGuards made their deadly debut during the battle, and the Republic's forces were utterly crushed. Grievous led his [[troop]]s at the front.<ref name="Battle Droids Encyclopedia" /> Sannen bravely tried to attack the cyborg directly, but he never got the chance; the [[Jedi]] was instead stabbed through his throat by the [[electrostaff]] of one of the MagnaGuards.<ref name="English Battle Droids Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
