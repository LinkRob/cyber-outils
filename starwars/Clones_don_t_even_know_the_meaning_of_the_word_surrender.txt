"'''Clones don't even know the meaning of the word surrender'''" is the twelfth in a series of comics on the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] website. It was placed on the website in early [[2009]]. The comic is the sequel to "[[A droid in the hand is worth two in the asteroid belt]]." "[[Do Clones dream of Electric Mynocks?]]" is the next comic in the series.

==Plot summary==
The comic begins with the eponymous group of clones continuing their search for [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] outside their [[Nu-class attack shuttle/Legends|shuttle]] on a desert [[Planet/Legends|planet]]. The clones don't see any droids, even though their scanners say otherwise, when one spots three [[B2-series super battle droid/Legends|super battle droids]] and an [[Armored Assault Tank/Legends|Armored Assault Tank]]. A [[B1-series battle droid/Legends|battle droid]] in the tank tells the clones to surrender. The [[Clone Commander/Legends|clone trooper commander]] responds by ordering the shuttle into the air and his troops to open fire. The clones destroy the super battle droids and climb onto the AAT while looking for a weak spot. Before they can destroy the tank, however, the droid inside flees out the back hatch, leaving victory for the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]]. After failing to get any information about R2-D2 from the captured battle droids, the commander notices that their shuttle hasn't returned. Believing that they have been abandoned, the clones commandeer the AAT with their [[Gunner/Legends|gunner]] acting as pilot. The commander tries to keep up morale inside the cramped cockpit just as their tank appears on someone else's [[Viewscreen/Legends|viewscreen]].
