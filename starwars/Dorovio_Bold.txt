'''Dorovio Bold''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who flew as a [[Rebel pilot]] in the [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps|Starfighter Corps]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Fleet]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Bold had fair skin, [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s and black [[hair]].<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" />

==Biography==
Dorovio Bold was a pilot who join the Rebel Alliance to fight against the Empire and she took part in the Battle over Endor and fought against the Imperial Fleet. She took part of the group of pilots led by [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] to destroy the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star II's]] main reactor. 

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I've lost my main stabilizer! I can't pull out!|Lines from Vivienne Chandler's unused scene|<ref name="Making">''[[The Making of Return of the Jedi]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Vivienne_Chandler-ROTJ_set.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Chandler on the set]]
Dorovio Bold was portrayed by [[Vivienne Chandler]], in cut scenes from [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. Footage of Chandler filming scenes in the cockpit during the [[Battle of Endor]] suggests that she was one of the pilots making the final assault on the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star]], and that she would have been killed when her fighter suffered fatal stabilizer damage. That footage was finally released in [[2013]] in the digital edition of ''[[The Making of Return of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="Making" />

Bold was not named in the film itself, but received identification in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary]]'', which was released on [[September 25]], [[2005]]. The name was then confirmed to be [[canon]] in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'', which was released on [[April 4]], [[2017]].

Bold's helmet first appeared as Luke Skywalker's blast shield helmet in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''A New Hope'']],<ref name="Costumes">''[[Star Wars Costumes: The Original Trilogy]]''</ref> and the prop was again reused as [[Darnell Reggs]]' helmet in the Legends video game [[Star Wars: Rebel Assault II: The Hidden Empire|''Rebel Assault II: The Hidden Empire'']].<ref name="RAII video">{{LucasArts|url=products/rebel2/reb_vid.htm|text=''Rebel Assault II'' - Live Action Video}}</ref> A helmet of a similar design was used by pilots [[Nozzo Naytaan]] and [[Attico Wred]] in [[2016]]'s ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Rogue One VG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
