The '''Endor Engineer''' was a costume owned by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sprocket]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Prodigal Programmer|standard costume]]. This [[Clothing|costume]] resembled the uniforms worn by the [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|Rebel Pathfinders]] during the [[Battle of Endor]], just scaled down to accommodate his size, and including a [[Color|dark gray]] and green [[Sprocket's datapad|datapad]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Endor Engineer was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Sprocket, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.18.0 during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Endor Ranger]].<ref name="Hunters" />
