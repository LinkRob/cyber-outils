'''Charger''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] as part of the [[501st Legion]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. They were part of a team sent on a [[Battle of Lola Sayu|mission]] to [[Lola Sayu]] to free Republic prisoners from [[The Citadel (episode)|The Citadel]]. As the team scaled a cliff to reach the facility, Charger's foothold crumbled underneath them as they neared their entry point, causing them to fall and [[Death|die]] on an [[electro-mine]].

==Biography==
===Early life===
Charger was one of the many [[Clone trooper|clones]] of [[Jango Fett]] grown on [[Kamino]].<ref name="Clone troopers" />

===Infiltrating the Citadel===
Over one [[Standard year|year]] into the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Even Piell]] was [[Capture of Even Piell|captured]] by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] and [[Prison|imprisoned]] in the [[Citadel]] on [[Lola Sayu]]. Charger was one of the clones assigned to a rescue team under the command of [[Jedi General|generals]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] that would [[Battle of Lola Sayu|infiltrate]] the prison and save Master Piell.<ref name="TC" />

The team was frozen in [[carbonite]] and had reprogrammed [[OOM pilot battle droid|battle droids]] fly a [[shuttle]] to fool the enemy life form scanners and get the frozen team on Lola Sayu. Once there, the team was unfrozen and the mission started.<ref name="TC" />

In order to get into the facility, the group had to free climb a wall covered with [[Electro-mine|electromines]] to reach a small entrance platform. A [[droid]] patrol came to the platform and locked the door with a [[ray shield]]. Fortunately, Skywalker's [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] was able to crawl through a ventilation shaft and deactivate the shield. The team then climbed to the entrance platform. Charger was one of the last team members to reach it. Unfortunately, when fellow clone [[Fives]] tried to help Charger up, the trooper stepped on a loose piece of stone that immediately broke off, causing Charger to lose their balance and fall straight into an electromine, [[Death|killing]] them instantly. This alerted the entire facility and made it more difficult for the team to reach the [[prisoner]]s.<ref name="TC" />

==Personality and traits==
As a clone of Jango Fett, Charger stood at 1.83 [[meter]]s.<ref name="Clone troopers" />

==Equipment==
Charger wore [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] with the [[Color|blue]] markings of the [[501st Legion]]. They used a standard [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] and wore a [[backpack]].<ref name="TC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Charger appeared in "[[The Citadel (episode)|The Citadel]]," the eighteenth episode of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series' [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]]. The clone was voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]], who voiced all clone troopers in the series.<ref name="TC" />
