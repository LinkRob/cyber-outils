The '''Charon River'''<ref name="Mando VG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> was an underground [[Magma|lava flow]] that ran between the [[planet]] [[Nevarro]]'s surface and its [[city]]'s [[Nevarro sewer|sewer]].<ref name="Chapter 8" />

{{Nature-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Charon River first appeared in the [[Chapter 8: Redemption|eighth episode]] of [[Jon Favreau]]'s [[2019]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.<ref name="Chapter 8" /> Design supervisor [[Doug Chiang]] subtly designed Nevarro's lava river tunnel system to reflect the human respiratory system, with two tunnels leading deeper into the sewers, like lungs, and one tunnel leading out towards the light, akin to the mouth or eye.<ref name="Chapter 8 guide">{{SW|url=series/the-mandalorian/chapter-8-guide|text="Redemption" Episode Guide &#124; The Mandalorian}}</ref>
