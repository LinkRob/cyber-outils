The '''Class Five infraction''' was a category of crime outlined<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> in the [[Imperial Penal Code|Imperial Legal Code]]<ref name="Build R2 8">{{BuildR2Cite|8|Droid Directory}}</ref> during the [[Imperial Era]]. It was the least severe of the five crime categories described in the penal code,<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> but could include severe punishments such as a six-month [[prison]] sentence which was handed out on [[Ringo Vinda]] for crashing into and defacing a statue of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>
