'''Arvina''' was an astronomical object located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Besalisk]] [[Fetya]] operated an [[Fetya's Hollow|outpost]] on Arvina, working for the [[Hidden Hand]].

==History==
At some point, the [[Besalisk]] [[Fetya]] set up shop on Arvina and operated as an [[arms dealer]], supplying many clients for decades. When [[Hunt for Vader|hunting]] down [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], [[Beilert Valance]] and his [[Beilert Valance's crew|crew]] of [[bounty hunter]]s visited [[Fetya's Hollow]] on Arvina in order to acquire [[sonic cannon]]s. When the deal went sour, Fetya and her crew threatened to kill Valance and his crew, but he allowed the [[Galactic Empire]] to track him there. The Empire destroyed Fetya's Hollow and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Torture|interrogated]] and [[Death|killed]] Fetya.<ref name="Target Vader 2" />
