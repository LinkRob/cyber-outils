'''Aggrocrab''' was a [[species]] that lived in the [[jungle]], [[beach]]es, and [[water]]s of the tropical [[planet]] [[Aynaboni]]. They had a [[blaster]]-resistant shell covered in spikes and large pincers. Many of them were encountered by [[Clone Force 99]] during a [[Crab Heist|mission]] to acquire a large crate.

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Tech, we caught something. And I think it's big.|Omega to Tech after noticing the fishing line being grabbed by a crab|<ref name="Spoils" />}}
[[File:CrabsAttackOmega.png|thumb|left|300px|The crabs had large pincers that they used to protect their underside.]]
The [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="Spoils" /> of giant [[crab]]s inhabited the [[jungle]],<ref name="Spoils Audio" /> [[beach]]es, and nearby [[water]]s of an island on<ref name="Spoils" /> [[Aynaboni]].<ref name="Aynaboni">{{Databank|aynaboni|Aynaboni}}</ref> The crabs had a [[Color|red]] and orange<ref name="Spoils" /> shell<ref name="Spoils Audio" /> covered in blue, triangular spikes and large, purple pincers edged with similar spikes, two [[eye]]s, two [[antenna]], six [[Limb|legs]], and a [[mouth]] consisting of three flaps. The crabs had green [[blood]]. They could be [[Death|killed]] by being shot in the underside with a [[Zygerrian energy bow]]. The crabs' shell and pincers could resist [[blaster]] [[fire]], so the crabs would cover their underside with their pincers. They were capable of damaging the<ref name="Spoils" /> modified [[Omicron-class attack shuttle|''Omicron''-class attack shuttle]],<ref name="Marauder DB">{{Databank|marauder|''Marauder''}}</ref> the ''[[Marauder (Omicron-class)|Marauder]]''.<ref name="Spoils" />

==Behavior==
The crabs [[Sleep|slept]] in a pod and would collectively chase anyone who woke them. They could be very aggressive and would often roar.<ref name="Spoils" />

==History==
{{Quote|I thought the beach was gonna be relaxing.|Wrecker to Clone Force 99 after they escaped from the crabs|<ref name="Spoils" />}}
[[File:CrabsSwarmBadbatch.png|thumb|right|300px|Several crabs swarming around Hunter, Wrecker, and Echo]]
Between [[19 BBY]] and [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|201}}</ref> [[Clone Force 99]] members "[[Hunter]]," "[[Wrecker]]," and "[[Echo]]" acquired a large crate from the jungle during a [[Crab Heist|heist]]. However, a large pod of<ref name="Spoils" /> Aggrocrabs<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=spoils-of-war-concept-art-gallery|text="Spoils of War" Concept Art Gallery &#124; The Bad Batch}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref> was awoken by Wrecker. Six of the crabs then chased the [[Cloning|clones]] to the beach while snapping their pincers. While Hunter blasted a crab, he requested firepower and a pickup from "[[Tech]]" in the ''Marauder''. Meanwhile, three crabs landed on the [[Starship|ship]] and attacked it with their pincers, and another crab grabbed the ship's<ref name="Spoils" /> [[fishing]] line.<ref name="Spoils Audio" /> [[Omega]], the groups's youngest member, jumped off two crabs and shot the crabs off the ''Marauder'' with [[Omega's Zygerrian energy bow|her energy bow]] before shooting the crabs on the ground. While running to the ship, Omega shot a crab and jumped off its shell to reach the fishing line, and she continued shooting the crabs. The ''Marauder'' arrived at Hunter, Wrecker, and Echo's location and blasted the crabs while Omega provided cover fire. The three clones kept shooting the crabs while being pulled onto the ship via the fishing line. The ''Marauder'' then flew away from the island.<ref name="Spoils" />

==Behind the scenes==
The crab creatures were first pictured in the first trailer for the [[The Bad Batch Season 2|second season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' animated series, released on [[May 29]], [[2022]].<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|6JX5OW8cOec|Star Wars: The Bad Batch &#124; Season 2 Teaser Trailer &#124; Disney+}}</ref> They first appeared in "[[Spoils of War]]," the first episode of the season,<ref name="Spoils" /> which aired on [[January 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="BB S2">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-bad-batch-season-2-trailer-key-art|text=Clone Force 99 Is Back in New ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 2 Trailer}}</ref>
