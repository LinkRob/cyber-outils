'''Celwis''' was a [[planet]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] and a stronghold of the [[Xodlak family]].<ref name="Greater Good" /> It was located beyond the eastern edge of [[Chiss Space]]<ref name="Location">{{LesserEvilMap}}</ref> in the [[Celwis system]] in the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Though Celwis was economically valuable, the world was [[Politics|politically]] insignificant. While [[Jixtus's plan|plotting]] to lead the [[Chiss]] into a civil war, [[Haplif]] of the [[Agbui]] resided on Celwis for a time in order to manipulate the Xodlak [[Councilor (Chiss Ascendancy)|Councilor]] [[Xodlak'uvi'vil]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

Haplif enticed Councilor Lakuviv with apparently abundant [[nyix]] jewelry. The Agbui successfully coaxed Councilor Lakuviv into staging a coup against Celwis' [[Patriel]] [[Xodlak'oo'nifis|Lakooni]], whom the renegade Councilor impersonated to declare a [[family emergency]]. Lakuviv thus summoned all eligible Xodlak-family [[Chiss officer|officers]] and [[Chiss warrior|warriors]] from the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] to assemble at Celwis, forming the [[Task Force Xodlak]] from the remains of the historic [[Xodlak fleet]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

Local [[Farmer|rancher]] [[Xodlak'phr'ooa]] "Lakphro" suspected foul play on Haplif's part, seeing the [[Nyix brooch|nyix jewelry]] Haplif had given officials as bribery. He confronted Haplif as the Agbui waited to leave Celwis with his cohorts. Haplif responded by trying to [[Death|kill]] Lakphro, but Lakphro killed Haplif in self-defense. In spite of Haplif's death, his plan nearly came to fruition with the [[Hoxim incident]], where Xodlak, [[Pommrio family|Pommrio]], and [[Erighal family|Erighal]] forces all arrived to claim the Agbui mining world of [[Hoxim]] for their own families. However, thanks to the work of Lakphro, [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], and [[Irizi'in'daro|Lakinda]], a civil war was prevented.<ref name="Greater Good" />

[[Celwis's largest moon]] had a cliff wall concealing an abandoned [[Chiss frigate]] from the days when the Xodlak was a [[Ruling Families|Ruling Family]].
