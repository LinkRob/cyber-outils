{{Quote|We can put this defeat to political use. The charred ruins of Ryloth will demonstrate to the galaxy the cost of a Republic victory.|Count Dooku|<ref name="Liberty" />}}
During the final stages of the [[Battle of Ryloth]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]] ordered [[Emir]] [[Wat Tambor]] to carry out a '''firebombing campaign''' against civilian targets, intending to turn a looming defeat for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] into a sign of what [[Galactic Republic]] victory would cost [[the galaxy]]. The operation was carried out by [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class Droid Fighter/Bombers]] that bombed [[Twi'lek]] villages in range of the [[capital]] of [[Lessu City]]. [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] led his [[starfighter]]s against the [[bomber]]s as the campaign carried on. As the [[Battle of Lessu]] concluded, Dooku had the bombers rush for Lessu, intending to wipe out the gathered [[Grand Army of the Republic|Republic forces]], [[Twi'lek Resistance]] forces, and civilians, even if Tambor was caught in the blast. However, Skywalker and his [[Padawan]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], were able to take out the bombers before they could begin their attack run.<ref name="Liberty" />

After the battle, the bombing runs were used as fuel by both sides' respective [[propaganda]] machines. The CIS framed the attack runs as necessary efforts to root out the local militia, which it painted as a dangerous force that, had it not been for the Republic, could have been dealt with in less extreme ways. The Republic, meanwhile, condemned the apparent "leave nothing but ashes" tactic the CIS had adopted. The Republic's telling proved to be more convincing, yet the CIS narrative still presented the chilling idea that, if locals tried to resist their occupiers, they would lose everything.<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref>
