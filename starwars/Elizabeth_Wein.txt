'''Elizabeth Eve Wein''' is the author of "[[Change of Heart]]," a short story which appears in the anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]'', celebrating the 40th anniversary of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="Elizabeth Wein story">{{Twitter|EWein2412|status/908740179548147713|Elizabeth Wein|quote=um, does this help? *runs to hide*|archivedate=20190905030546}}</ref> She has also written the junior novel [[The Last Jedi: Cobalt Squadron|''Cobalt Squadron'']], a tie-in to to the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''The Last Jedi'']], which was released on the [[December 15|same day]] as the film and provided the backstory for new characters introduced in it, [[Rose Tico|Rose]] and [[Paige Tico]].<ref name="Cobalt">{{SW|url=news/new-star-wars-the-last-jedi-books-and-more-revealed-at-sdcc-2017|text=New ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Books and More Revealed at SDCC 2017}}</ref>

She has a private [[Wikipedia:Pilot licensing and certification|pilot's license]], and ever since she first flew, she wanted to write about it.<ref name="NYCC">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2017-new-thrawn-novel-revealed-and-more-lucasfilm-publishing-panel|text=NYCC 2017: New Thrawn Novel Revealed and More from Lucasfilm's Publishing Panel}}</ref>
