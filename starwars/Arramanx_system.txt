'''Arramanx system''' was located in the [[Cronese Mandate]] [[sector]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The [[Star system|system]] was situated in grid square S-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> members of the [[Rokana Raiders]] [[pirate]] group had raided a [[corvette]] while it was in the system.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' {{C|''A Pirate's Fortune'' DLC}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Arramanx system was first mentioned in the [[canon]] continuity in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune|text=Steal a Look at ''Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune'', Available May 15}}</ref> In the game, the system was mentioned in one of several ambient conversations that the player can over hear while at the [[Khepi Tomb]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

The system was originally introduced in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity in the [[2009]] ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' reference book by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Essential Atlas">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
