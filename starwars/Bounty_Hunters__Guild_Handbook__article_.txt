"'''Bounty Hunters' Guild Handbook'''" is an article in [[Star Wars Insider 99|''Star Wars Insider'' 99]] by [[Jonathan Wilkins]]. It presents information regarding the [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunters]] of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' universe in the [[in-universe]] format of a [[Bounty Hunters Guild Handbook]].<ref name="Star Wars Insider 99">"Editor's Welcome," [[Star Wars Insider 99|''Star Wars Insider'' 99]], page 5</ref> The article was later reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2012|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2012]].
