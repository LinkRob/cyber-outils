{{Quote|Governor Haveland and her people have had a great deal of trouble with them in the Esaga sector.|[[Grand Admiral]] [[Balanhai Savit]]|<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />}}
The '''Esaga sector''' was a [[sector]]<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> in the [[Mid Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], it was governed by [[Moff]] [[Haveland]]. It was known for its problems from [[gralloc]]s, [[Species|creatures]] similar to [[mynock]]s that attacked [[starship]]s. In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Treason date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Thrawn: Treason]]'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> the [[Third Fleet]] of the [[Imperial Navy]], under [[Grand Admiral]] [[Balanhai Savit]], launched an [[Anti-piracy campaign in the Esaga sector|anti-piracy campaign in the region]]&mdash;though it was actually a part of the Grand Admiral's corrupt scheme to steal components of the top-secret [[Project Stardust]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />
