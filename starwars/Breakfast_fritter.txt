'''Breakfast fritters''' were a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] [[Diet|edible]] by the [[Chiss]] [[species]].<ref name="Greater Good" /> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' take place in [[18 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the Chiss [[Xodlak'phr'ooa]], [[core name]] "Lakphro"; his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Lakansu]]; and their [[Gender|daughter]] [[Lakris]] ate the fritters on the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[planet]] [[Celwis]]. While eating, Lakansu admonished her daughter for speaking while her [[mouth]] was full with the food. Before leaving to meet with their new [[Agbui]] neighbor, [[Frosif]], Lakris grabbed two fritters, prompting her [[Parent|mother]] to tell her to ensure their new [[alien]] neighbors could digest the food before she offered one to her new friend.<ref name="Greater Good" />

==Behind the scenes==
Breakfast fritters appeared in ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'', the [[2021]] second installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Greater Good" />
