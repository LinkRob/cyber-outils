A '''Flaming Tentacle''' was a type of drink. While aboard the [[casino]] [[sail barge]] ''[[Pelagic]]'' on the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]], the [[Chagrian]] [[Gender|woman]] and leader of the [[pirate]] group known as the [[Vile]] [[Nanna Frouj]] lamented having to drink "swill" and wished for a Flaming Tentacle as a beverage instead. When she spoke to the [[Jedi Master]] Ki-Adi-Mundi, who was undercover as a member of the [[Staved Skulls]] pirate group, about the situation, the [[Cerean]], who was unfamiliar with the drink, inquired as to if it was a refreshment, prompting Frouj to list other beverages she wanted.<ref name="The Living Force" />
