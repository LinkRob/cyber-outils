A '''bagpipe''' was a [[Music/Legends|musical]] instrument that could be bought in sets for around 200 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]]. They could be kept by individuals in small apartments aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|DS-1 Orbital Battle Station]] around the time of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Star Wars: Tiny Death Star" />

==Behind the scenes==
The bagpipe was mentioned in the [[2013]] [[Canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Tiny Death Star]]'', developed by [[Disney Mobile]]. In real-life, [[Wikipedia:Bagpipes|bagpipes]] are [[Wikipedia:Woodwind instrument|woodwind instruments]] prominent in [[Wikipedia:Culture of Europe|European]] and [[Wikipedia:Culture of Asia|Asian cultures]].<ref name="Star Wars: Tiny Death Star" />
