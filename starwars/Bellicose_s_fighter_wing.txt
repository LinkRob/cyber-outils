A '''fighter wing''' of the [[Imperial Starfighter Corps]] was stationed aboard the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Bellicose (Imperial-class)|''Bellicose'']] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="Visions 2" /> and as a wing within the [[Galactic Empire]], contained six [[squadron]]s,<ref name="TIE models set">''[[Star Wars: TIE Fighter Deluxe Book and Model Set]]''</ref> including [[Beta Squadron]]. When the ''Bellicose'' was frantically chasing a [[Unidentified rebel spy (Bellicose)|spy]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], Beta Squadron was initially deployed to target the spy's [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]] and damaged it.<ref name="Visions 2" />

However, [[Commander]] [[Tylux]] deployed his vessel's entire [[fighter wing]] to capture the spy. As a result of the dangerous conditions and counterattacks by the spy, the fighter wing was destroyed.<ref name="Visions 2" />
