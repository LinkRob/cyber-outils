[[Matriarch]] '''Catriona Graf''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who was the head of the [[Graf family]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Catriona had a [[Xylan Graf's mother|daughter]] and a grandson, [[Xylan Graf|Xylan]]. She conspired with the [[scientist]] [[Chancey Yarrow]] and the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s, using the [[Graf Enterprises|family company]] to build [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]]'s [[Lourna Dee (cruiser)|starship]], as well as assisting in Yarrow's experiments in constructing a [[gravity well projector]]. When the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]] discovered Catriona and the Grafs' duplicity, the family's assets were frozen, but Catriona escaped justice aboard [[Everbloom]], the family's mobile estate, and met with Yarrow to discuss her [[Chancey Yarrow's laboratory|new lab]] in the [[Hynestian sector]].<ref name="OOTS" />
