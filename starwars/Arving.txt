'''Arving''' was an individual who flew as a [[pilot]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Battle of Endor]]. In [[4 ABY]], as the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second]] [[Death Star]] [[space station]] was about to obliterate and create a shockwave, Arving shouted in the [[Comm unit|comms]] of his [[starfighter]] to evacuate immediately to escape the explosion. The [[Gender|female]] pilot [[Ca Isiora]], who flew alongside Arving, assumed he learned how to fly from analyzing the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="GTS" />
