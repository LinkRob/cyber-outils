'''Arashell Sar''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Youngling|child]] who worked as a stable hand&mdash;one of several street urchins abandoned on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]] and employed by [[Bargwill Tomder]]&mdash;at the [[fathier]] stables in [[Canto Bight]]. Her fellow stable hands included [[Temiri Blagg]] and [[Oniho Zaya]].<ref name="TLJVD" /> During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], Sar and Blagg listened to Zaya as he told them a story about [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] who stood alone against the [[First Order]] at the [[Battle of Crait]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Arashell Sar first appeared in the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], the second film in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]] directed by [[Rian Johnson]], and was portrayed by [[Sara Heller]], who was listed as "Stable Kid" in the film's credits.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> The character's name is an anagram of the actress' name.
