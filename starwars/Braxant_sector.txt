The '''Braxant sector''' was a [[sector]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It contained the [[Anthan system]]<ref name="Appendix" />, which itself contained the [[planet]] [[Anthan Prime]] which was orbited by many [[moon]]s.<ref name="Shadows and Secrets, Part 12">[[Darth Vader (2015) 12|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 12]]</ref>

{{Sec-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Braxant sector had its first appearance in [[canon]] in [[Darth Vader (2015) 8|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 8]], the eighth issue of the [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2015)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']].<ref name="Shadows and Secrets, Part 8">[[Darth Vader (2015) 12|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 12]]</ref> However it was first identified as the Braxant sector in the reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>

In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], it first appeared in ''[[Specter of the Past]]'', the first book in the ''[[Star Wars: The Hand of Thrawn Duology]]'' by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Specter">''[[Specter of the Past]]''</ref>
