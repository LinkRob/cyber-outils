"'''Belted In: Incursion'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year three of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was the first adventure in the [[Belted In trilogy]] and was written by [[Jae Walker]] and [[Victor Long]].

==Plot summary==
The heroes are sent by [[Nirama]] to get supplies past a [[Thaereian military]] blockade to a remote [[Cularin Militia]] base in the [[Cularin asteroid belt]]. However, when they arrive, they instead find a squad of Thaereian troops waiting for them.
