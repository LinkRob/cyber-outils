The '''Convocation Chambers''',<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> also known as the '''Convocation Building''',<ref name="The High Republic 4">[[The High Republic (2022) 4|''The High Republic'' (2022) 4]]</ref> were location in [[Jedha City]] where the [[Convocation of the Force]] held their meetings.<ref name="Peace and Unity">{{StoryCite|story=Peace and Unity|book=The High Republic (2022) 1}}</ref> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{PeaceandUnityDate}}</ref> a [[Yacombe Delegate|Yacombe envoy]] visited the chambers in order to join the Convocation, but they were attacked by the [[Fallanassi]] and the [[Matukai]] [[representative]]s.<ref name="Peace and Unity" />

Over a month later,<ref name="Battle of Jedha">''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]''</ref> [[Werth Plouth]], the [[Herald (title)|Herald]] of the [[Path of the Open Hand]], visited the Convocation Chambers demanding the council to be dissolved. He then held a speech in front of the building stiring up the crowd against the Convocation.<ref name="The High Republic 4">[[The High Republic (2022) 4|''The High Republic'' (2022) 4]]</ref>
