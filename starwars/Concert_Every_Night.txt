'''''Concert Every Night''''' was a [[Painting|poster]] used to promote a series of concerts by a [[Unidentified human singer|human singer]] on [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. It showed the singer performing on stage, set against a luminous multi-[[color]]ed background. The caption was written in [[Aurebesh]] and read "concert every night"<ref name="LT" /> – "call 050506."<ref name="Concept">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/lethal-trackdown-concept-art-gallery|text="Lethal Trackdown" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 7}}</ref> It appeared in such diverse places as [[Terminal 24]], on a [[hover taxi]], in [[Moshi Bar]],<ref name="LT" /> in an [[Unidentified Coruscant cantina|unidentified cantina]],<ref name="Pursuit of Peace">{{TCW|Pursuit of Peace}}</ref> next to a [[holobooth]],<ref name="To Catch a Jedi">{{TCW|To Catch a Jedi}}</ref> on a wall on [[Level 1312]],<ref name="The Wrong Jedi">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref> and on a wall on [[Level 1313]].<ref name="Gone with a Trace">{{TCW|Gone with a Trace}}</ref>

{{Visionsstart}}
An old ''Concert Every Night'' poster from the Clone Wars was used to decorate the interior of ''[[The Rolling Gales]]'', a [[starship]] that served as the mobile headquarters and concert stage of the [[band]] [[Star Waver]].<ref name="Tatooine Rhapsody">{{Visions|Tatooine Rhapsody}}</ref>
{{Visionsend}}
