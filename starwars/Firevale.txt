'''Firevale''' was a [[city-state]] on the planet [[Gansevor]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], Firevale was already centuries old. It began being built in a box canyon as a mining town when the very precious [[Baudraxite]] was discovered there. [[Baudraxite]] was only found in Firevale which quickly made it their main export, trading it with other city states on the planet in exchange for everything from food to technology. Over the centuries the mineral became a vital part in the planets energy infrastructure. Many of the planets inhabitants held ill will against the people of Firevale as they were not generous with trading the crucial resources with other states.<ref name="The Blade 2" />
