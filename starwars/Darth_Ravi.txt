'''Darth Ravi''', known as '''Darth Ravi the Bold''', was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Sith Lord]] considered ancient by the time of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Echoes of Fear 1" /> The [[Echo Stone]] was hidden from Darth Ravi on [[Angcord]].<ref name="Quest of the Jedi" /> Darth Ravi was a powerful Sith Lord in the [[dark side of the Force]]. He was a feared lightsaber duelist and was skilled in both [[Force lightning]] and telekinesis.<ref name="Echoes of Fear 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DarthRaviCover-THRAEchoesFear1.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Darth Ravi as he was featured on the cover of ''Echoes of Fear'', his first visual depiction.]]
Darth Ravi was first mentioned in a flashback in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] one-shot ''[[The High Republic Adventures: Quest of the Jedi]]'', written by [[Claudia Gray]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Quest of the Jedi" /> on [[April 26]], [[2023]].<ref name="Quest of the Jedi release">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3010-780/star-wars-the-high-republic-adventures--quest-of-the-jedi|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures&mdash;Quest of the Jedi}}</ref> Prior to the comic's release, an excerpt of the one-shot which included mention of Darth Ravi was released as part of an article on the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[StarWars.com|website]] on [[April 9]] of the same year.<ref name="Quest of the Jedi excerpt">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-dark-horse|text=SWCE 2023: Dark Horse Reveals a ''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'' Phase III Teaser, New Jaxxon Art, and More}}</ref>

Ravi later appeared for the first time in the [[The High Republic Adventures – Echoes of Fear 1|first issue]] of the comic miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Echoes of Fear]]'', written by [[George Mann]], and illustrated by [[Eduardo Mello]].<ref name="Echoes of Fear 1" /> After making his visual debut via [[Eduardo Mello]]'s work on the comic's cover, which was revealed before the comic's release on [[Dark Horse Comics]]' website,<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3014-600/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures--Echoes-of-Fear-1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures--Echoes of Fear #1}}</ref> the comic was officially released on [[September 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3014-600/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures--Echoes-of-Fear-1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures--Echoes of Fear #1}}</ref> However, it saw an accidental early release on [[August 21]] of that year as its contents were featured in the digital edition of [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 9|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 9]].<ref name="Echoes Error">{{Twitter|SWSplashPagePod|status/1826354357077770438|''Star Wars'' Splash Page|date=2024-08-21|quote=Strictly FYI, hearing from some listeners that the digital copy of #StarWars #TheHighRepublicAdventures Phase III #9 is in fact Echoes of Fear #1 (of 4). Fairly certain Dark Horse is aware and a fix is in the offing. #StarWarsComics|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/i5QFv}}</ref>
