The '''bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar''' occurred late in the [[Clone Wars]]. [[Jedi Knight]] [[Barriss Offee]]&mdash;having become disgruntled with the fact that the [[Jedi Order]], traditionally guardians of peace, were leading the [[Galactic Republic]]'s war against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]&mdash;carried out the bombing. The attack [[Death|killed]] six [[Jedi Knight]]s, a number of [[clone trooper]]s, and members of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple's]] maintenance crew. A number of suspects were considered before the evidence implicated [[Ahsoka Tano]], Padawan of [[Jedi General|General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], who was [[Trial of Ahsoka Tano|tried]] for murder and [[treason]]. Skywalker later discovered that it was Offee who carried out the attack, vindicating his Padawan.

==Prelude==
[[File:AhsokaEta2.png|thumb|left|250px|Ahsoka Tano and Anakin Skywalker were recalled from the front to investigate the bombing of the Jedi Temple.]]
As the [[Clone Wars]] continued throughout [[the galaxy]], many citizens of the [[Galactic Republic]] started to disagree with the ways of the [[Jedi]], and no longer viewed them as [[Law enforcement agency|peacekeepers]] due to their new role as [[general]]s leading the fight against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name=" Barriss databank">{{Databank|barriss-offee|Barriss Offee}}</ref> [[Jedi Knight]]<ref name="100 Objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref> [[Barriss Offee]], former [[Padawan]] to [[Jedi Master]] [[Luminara Unduli]], grew to harbor similar views&mdash;ultimately believing that both the Jedi and the Republic had lost their way. As a result of this mindset, she sought to make a statement against the Jedi by detonating a bomb within the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]].<ref name=" Barriss databank" /> Enlisting the help of citizen [[Letta Turmond]], a radical anti-war activist,<ref name="Letta databank">{{Databank|letta-turmond|Letta Turmond}}</ref> Offee gave Turmond [[NM-K reconstitutor]] nano-droids to feed to her husband, [[Jackar Bowmani]], turning Bowmani into a living bomb. Bowmani was a munitions expert in the Jedi Temple, giving Turmond and Offee direct access to the temple hangar.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" />

==Bombing==
{{Quote|We have to look at the possibility that it could have been anyone in the temple, even a Jedi.''"<br />"''If a Jedi this is, gone to the dark side they have.|Mace Windu and Yoda, discussing the possibility that a Jedi could have bombed the temple|<ref name="Sabotage" />}}
[[File:Epguide517.png|thumb|right|250px|Tano and Skywalker investigated the crime scene at the behest of the Jedi Council.]]
Jackar Bowmani was working in the Jedi Temple [[hangar]] when the nano-[[droid]]s within him detonated, [[Death|killing]] Bowmani and multiple bystanders.<ref name="Sabotage" /> The casualties who died as a result of the explosion were six Jedi, including [[Jedi Knight]] [[Tutso Mara]], as well as several [[clone trooper]]s and maintenance workers.<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" /> In addition to the fatalities, several workers were injured by the attack and subsequently taken to the temple's medical facility.<ref name="Sabotage" />

As rumors began to spread of possible Jedi involvement in the attack, [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] recalled [[Jedi General|General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his Padawan [[Ahsoka Tano]] from their [[Defense of Cato Neimoidia|defense]] on [[Cato Neimoidia]]. Upon their return to [[Coruscant]], the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] assigned Skywalker and Tano the task of discovering the culprit behind the attack. As neither of them were present in the temple at the [[time]] of the bombing, Yoda believed that they could be trusted to lead the investigation.<ref name="Sabotage" />

[[File:JediAndRusso-S.png|thumb|left|250px|The droid Russo-ISC assisted Skywalker and Tano in analyzing the crime scene and collecting eyewitness accounts.]]
Skywalker and Tano were partnered with [[Russo-ISC]], a [[SP-4 ISC crime scene analysis droid|crime scene analysis droid]], to investigate the crime, and Tano and Russo began to interview the witnesses. During these interviews, Tano and Russo learned about potential suspect Jackar Bowmani, who went missing after the bombing. After informing Skywalker about the suspect, Skywalker found Letta Turmond in a protest that was occurring outside the Temple and brought her in for questioning. She claimed that Bowmani could not have been involved, but after further investigation nano-droids were discovered at the site of the bomb and later in Bowmani's bloodstream, allowing the Jedi to believe that Bowmani himself was the bomb. Skywalker and Tano went to Bowmani and Turmond's house to further investigate and found traces of nano-droids in the food, confirming their suspicions. The Jedi were interrupted by Turmond, who ran when asked to come in for further questioning. Once captured, Turmond was arrested for the bombing of the Jedi Temple and sent to prison. With Turmond locked away, any suspicions that a Jedi could have been involved were lost.<ref name="Sabotage" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Barriss Offee was wrong about a lot of things. She let her anger cloud her judgement and she tried to justify her actions without considering their wider effects. She was afraid of the war and she didn't trust people she should've listened to. But she had a point about the Republic and the Jedi. There was something wrong with them, and we were too locked into our traditions to see what it was. Barriss should've done something else. She shouldn't have killed anyone, and she definitely shouldn't have framed me for it, but if we'd listened to her&mdash;really listened&mdash;we might have been able to stop Palpatine before he took power.|Ahsoka Tano reflects on Barriss Offee's betrayal|<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref>}}
[[File:WolffeStunsAhsoka.png|thumb|right|250px|Tano became a suspect in the bombing of the Jedi Temple as a result of being captured in a warehouse filled with nano-droids.]]
Shortly after the [[Funeral of the Jedi Temple bombing victims|funeral ceremony]] for the Jedi victims of the attack,<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" /> Offee killed her co-conspirator and framed Tano for the deed.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" /> Tano escaped from Republic custody in the hope of proving her innocence,<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" /> but was consequently hunted as a fugitive by the Jedi and Coruscant Guard.<ref name="TCAJ">{{TCW|To Catch a Jedi}}</ref> Following her [[Capture of Ahsoka Tano|capture]], Tano was expelled from the Order and subsequently placed on [[Trial of Ahsoka Tano|trial]] for murder and [[treason]]. Although the charges were dismissed after Skywalker [[Duel at the Jedi Temple|apprehended]] Offee and forced her to make a full confession, Tano declined the Council's offer of reinstatement and chose to seek a new path instead.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" /> Unduli was shocked by Offee's actions and tried to find comfort in her wartime service, working to repair the [[Wookiee]] city [[Kachirho]] from Separatists attacks and prepare it for a coming [[Battle of Kashyyyk|Separatist invasion]]. She spent months at work and came to understand Kachirho's terrain in that time.<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>
