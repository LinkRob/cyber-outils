'''Cally Pon''' was a [[Rebel trooper|soldier]] who served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Alliance Army|army]], stationed at [[Echo Base]] on<ref name="Beyond Hope" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> [[Hoth]]. During the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Battle of Hoth|invasion]] of Hoth<ref name="Beyond Hope" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> Pon used an [[A280 blaster rifle]] that constantly overheated. The [[soldier]] later called out the approach of enemy Imperial [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtroopers]], which proceeded to engage the rebel defenders. The rebel ground forces eventually abandoned their positions<ref name="Beyond Hope" /> and numerous personnel of Echo Base [[Code K-one-zero|evacuated]] aboard [[starship]] [[transport]]s.<ref name="Good Kiss">{{StoryCite|story=A Good Kiss|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Cally Pon appeared in the short story "[[Beyond Hope]]," written by [[Michael Moreci]] as a part of the [[2020]] short story collection ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''.<ref name="Beyond Hope" />
