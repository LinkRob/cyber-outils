'''''Endor''''' was a strategy board game released by the [[Tsukuda Hobby]] company, exclusively in Japan,<ref name="Cargo Bay" /> in [[1983]].<ref name="Blog" /> It was based on the ground portion of [[Battle of Endor/Legends|Battle of Endor]]. The game was designed by [[Atsutoshi Okada]].<ref name="Blog" />

Tsukuda Hobby also released similar games for the other [[original trilogy]] films: [[Death Star (board game)|''Death Star'']] and [[Hoth (board game)|''Hoth'']].
