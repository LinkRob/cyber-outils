The '''chindinkalu plant''' was a [[plant]] native to the [[planet]] [[Kirdo III]]. [[Droopy McCool]], a [[Kitonak]] [[musician]] and a [[Homeworld|native]] of the planet, used a hollow chindinkalu plant to craft the [[chindinkalu flute]] that he played as a member of the [[Max Rebo Band]].<ref name="SWCT" />

==Behind the scenes==
The chindinkalu plant first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] as part of Droopy McCool's chindinkalu [[flute]],<ref name="Return of the Jedi">{{Film|VI}}</ref> though the actual name of the plant was not used in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] until it was identified in Droopy McCool's Base Series 1 card, which released on ''[[Star Wars: Card Trader]]''<ref name="SWCT" /> on [[March 11]], [[2015]].<ref name="release">{{ToppsDigital|subdomain=www|url=swcardtrader/article/545bbb5771100031fdcbef4e|text=Base Series 1 is here!|author=The Senate|date=2015-03-11}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the plant was identified as the [[chidinka]] in the [[StarWars.com]] article ''[[Slugthrowers: An Overview of Popular Music and Musicians in a Galaxy Far, Far Away]]''.<ref name="Slugthrowers">{{SW|url=news/slugthrowers-an-overview-of-popular-music-and-musicians-in-a-galaxy-far-far-away-part-1|series=Slugthrowers: An Overview of Popular Music and Musicians in a Galaxy Far, Far Away|text=Part 1|nolive=1}}</ref>
