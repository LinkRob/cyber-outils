The '''''Droch''-class boarding ship''',<ref name="TVE" /> also referred to as the '''''Droch'' dropship''', the '''Separatist boarding ship''',<ref name="Cloak of Darkness Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/cloak-of-darkness-episode-guide|text="Cloak of Darkness" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> '''droid landing pod''',<ref name="Rookies Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/rookies-episode-guide|text="Rookies" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> or '''pod-hunter''',<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> was a model of [[boarding craft]]<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" /> manufactured by [[Colicoid Creation Nest]]<ref name="EncyclopediaStarfighters" /> and used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />

During the early stages of the [[Malevolence Campaign|''Malevolence'' campaign]], the craft were used to hunt down and destroy the escape pods of destroyed Republic fleets so the existence of the ''[[Malevolence]]'' would be kept secret.<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> They also used to deploy troops on board enemy vessels<ref name="Cloak of Darkness">{{TCW|Cloak of Darkness}}</ref> and stations, including the [[Rishi Station]]<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref> and [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Tranquility]]'',<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" /> the ''[[Coronet]]''.<ref name="VoT">{{TCW|Voyage of Temptation}}</ref> However, to fully exploit the potential of the boarding ship required a level of genius like that of [[General]] [[Grievous]].<ref name="Ships (I)">{{EncyclopediaCite|Republic and Separatist Ships (1)}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The ''Droch''-class [[Boarding craft|boarding ship]]<ref name="TVE" /> also referred to as the ''Droch'' dropship, the Separatist boarding ship,<ref name="Cloak of Darkness Episode Guide" /> droid landing pod,<ref name="Rookies Episode Guide" /> or pod-hunter,<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> manufactured by [[Colicoid Creation Nest]]<ref name="EncyclopediaStarfighters" /> was one of many boarding and utility [[Starship|vessels]] used the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval forces]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. They were small, [[starfighter]]-sized vessels with a bell-shaped [[hull]] loosely based on the [[toothspike raptor]] from the [[planet]] [[Colla IV]],<ref name="Rise" /> and were 18.5 [[meter]]s in length and had an approximate radius of 8.8 meters.<ref name="EncyclopediaStarfighters" /> Its [[engine]]s were mounted where a bell's yoke would be located, and around its base were four long, retractable spikes measuring approximately 11.4 meters in length. Centered inside the base was a hatch that provided access to a cramped passenger compartment and a single-seat flight deck.<ref name="Rise" />

As its name suggested, the ''Droch''-class was designed for boarding actions against enemy ships. Launched en masse from their mother ships, they could carry six fully equipped [[troop]]s or [[battle droid]]s,<ref name="Rise" /> and they could fly at their targets at a maximum speed of 850 [[Kilometer per hour|kilometers per hour]],<ref name="EncyclopediaStarfighters" /> where they would proceed to use their spikes to pierce a target ship's hull and anchor themselves. Once a Droch had attached, a plasma cutting ring burned a hole through the hull, and a boarding tube extends from the hatch, where the troops would proceed to enter the target ship. These vessels were also armed with four [[turret]]-mounted twin light [[laser cannon]]s, and were equipped with close-ranged [[sensor]]s and had one [[Standard day|day's]] worth of consumables.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Droch''-class boarding ship first appeared in "[[Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence'']],"<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> the second episode of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''' [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]], which originally aired on [[October 3]], [[2008]].<ref name="Epguide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode002.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> The ship was later identified in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'', written by [[Adram Bray]], [[Cole Horton]], and [[Tricia Barr]], and published by [[Dorling Kindersley]].<ref name="TVE" />
