'''Carok Hittak''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Jedi]] of the [[Jedi Order]] who owned a [[Silandra Sho's shield|plasma shield]] that would later be owned by [[Jedi Master]] [[Silandra Sho]] and her [[Padawan]], [[Rooper Nitani]].<ref name="SotJ" /> Around [[379 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Nitani was searching for the [[Plasma shield|shield]] after Sho hid it as part of her [[Rooper Nitani's Jedi Trials|Jedi Trials]]. She recalled stories about the [[Shield (physical armor)|shield's]] previous owners that her Master had told her, including the story of when Hittak went on a quest so far beyond the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] that it took him almost two [[Standard decade|decades]] to return. Until his [[death]], Hittak maintained that the shield was the one thing that had kept him going while he was lost. Nitani considered Hittak to be legendary.<ref name="SotJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Carok Hittak was mentioned in the short story "[[Shield of the Jedi]]," written by [[George Mann]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="SotJ" />
