Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[T'onga's crew|crew]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]] '''captured''' the former [[Black Sun]] member [[Stolak Vandello]] aboard the [[Star Cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' after he borrowed a number of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] from his former employers so that his lover [[R'Tess]] could have a better life away from the pollution of their [[homeworld]] of [[Balosar]]. The crew were hired by the Black Sun to either capture or [[Death|kill]] Vandello, though after he successfully escaped the clutches of [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] and [[4-LOM]], Vandello was punched in the face by [[Wooro]], and ultimately surrendered. After the crew allowed Vandello and R'Tess witness a [[sungrazer-comet]] collide with the [[star]] [[Daca]], they took Vandello to the Black Sun and R'Tess noted [[Standard year|years]] later that she did not see him again, and suspected that the Black Sun had [[Death penalty|executed]] him.<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 5" />
