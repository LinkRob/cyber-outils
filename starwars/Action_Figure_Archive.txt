"'''Action Figure Archive'''" was a department of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine that covered ''[[Star Wars]]'' collectibles and statues available from officially licensed companies such as [[Hasbro]], [[Sideshow Collectibles]], and [[Master Replicas]]. It appeared in issues 50, 51, and 60 as "'''Action Figure Update'''", and reappeared as Action Figure Archive in issues 74-76, before being renamed to [[Jabba's Collection]] starting with issue 77.
