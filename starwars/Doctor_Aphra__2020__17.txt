'''''Doctor Aphra'' 17''' is the seventeenth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. It was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 5]], [[2022]].

==Publisher's summary==
''EVOCATIONS! DOCTOR APHRA and SANA STARROS stumble upon a STRANGE RITUAL&hellip;And STRANGER ENEMY! Will they fall victim to a practitioner of an ANCIENT CULT?''

==Opening crawl==
{{OpeningCrawl
|name=EVOCATIONS
|text=Rogue archeologist Doctor Aphra and smuggler Sana Starros have been put through their paces&ndash;
from encounters with Crimson Dawn, to being betrayed by Just Lucky and Ariole, to surviving an explosive
brush with an ancient piece of Ascendant technology called the Thought Dowser, those two have been busy.

As if things haven't been crazy enough, unbeknownst to Aphra and Starros, Ronen Tagge is back&hellip;.}}
