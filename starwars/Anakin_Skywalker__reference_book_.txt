"'''Anakin Skywalker'''" is a volume in the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series of reference books published by [[De Agostini]]. It was published on [[June 8]], [[2021]].<ref name="De Agostini" />

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''Raised as a slave from his earliest childhood, Anakin Skywalker always aspired to be something more. He possessed a great sense of justice, an extraordinary knack for mechanics, and surprising acumen for someone so young. All of this was mainly due to his great affinity with the Force. Only one encounter with Jedi Knight Qui-Gon Jinn was enough for the Master to think that Anakin might be the Chosen One to bring balance to the Force. Young Skywalker would fulfill his dream of becoming a Jedi and this would mark a turning point in the course of galactic history that no one could have foreseen.''<ref name="De Agostini" />

==Continuity==
The booklet's information on [[Yerbana]] consists of a French translation of the English [[Wookieepedia]] article on the [[planet]].

The booklet states that the "business on [[Cato Neimoidia]]" referenced by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] was an operation undertaken by Kenobi and Skywalker just before the [[Battle of Yerbana]],<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date 1">"[[Old Friends Not Forgotten]]" establishes that the [[Battle of Yerbana]] occurs immediately prior to the [[Battle of Coruscant]], which is dated to [[19 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> This corresponds with the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] depiction of the event in [[James Luceno]]'s [[2005]] novel ''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]''.<ref name="Labyrinth of Evil">''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]''</ref> However, the [[2022]] novel ''[[Brotherhood (novel)|Brotherhood]]'' depicts a [[canon]] version of the [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Kenobi and Skywalker)|incident]]<ref name="Cover reveal">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-brotherhood-cover|text=Anakin and Obi-Wan Are Ready for Battle on the Cover of ''Star Wars: Brotherhood'' &ndash; Exclusive Reveal}}</ref> occurring in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Date 2">''Brotherhood'' is set between the [[First Battle of Geonosis|beginning]] of the [[Clone Wars]] and the [[Battle of Christophsis]], as the novel's plot involves [[Anakin Skywalker]] becoming a [[Jedi Knight]], a rank he attained between those events. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates both events to [[22 BBY]], ''Brotherhood'' must be set in that year.</ref> All the same, author [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]] stated in an interview fans were free to continue to see "that business" as a late war matter, as was presented in ''Labyrinth of Evil''.<ref name="Chen interview">{{WebCite|url=https://www.talkingbay94.com/2022/05/11/mike-chen-author-of-star-wars-brotherhood|text=Mike Chen: Author of Star Wars - BROTHERHOOD|accessdate=2022-05-12|work=''Talking Bay 94''|archivedate=20220511161716}}</ref>
