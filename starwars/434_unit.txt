{{Quote|This one old 434 unit had the most spectacular recipe for Cavaellin creams&mdash;snappy little cookies from the Cavaell system filled with exotic fruit.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
The '''434 unit''' was a type of [[Cooking droid|kitchen droid]]. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] encountered an old 434 unit that knew a recipe for [[Cavaellin Spice Creams]]&mdash;a type of [[cookie]] from the [[Cavaell system]]&mdash;that Tuggs considered spectacular.<ref name="Cookbook" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs included the recipe as well as a mention of the [[droid]] in his first [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', claiming the recipe was to [[Death|die]] for.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], a 434 unit was mentioned in the [[2019]] cookbook ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which was written by [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] and [[Marc Sumerak]].<ref name="Cookbook" /> The droid originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was known as the [[434-FPC Personal Chef Droid]] and was first mentioned in ''[[Galladinium's Fantastic Technology]]'', a [[1995]] supplement published by [[West End Games]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="GFT">''[[Galladinium's Fantastic Technology]]''</ref>
