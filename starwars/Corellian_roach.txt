A '''Corellian roach''' was a creature that was hard to [[Death|kill]]<ref name="Aphra 40" /> and partially shared its name with the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Corellia]].<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-marvel|text=SWCE 2023: Marvel Reveals ''Star Wars: Dark Droids'' Horror Event and More}} states that the events of [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']], including [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 40|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 40]], take place between the events of {{Film|V}} and {{Film|VI}}, which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] crashed the [[Tagge Corporation]] [[flagship]] ''[[Acquisitor]]'' into the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Rocedila]].<ref name="Aphra 39">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 39|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 39]]</ref> Afterward, Tagge Corporation<ref name="Aphra 40" /> [[Executive officer|leader]] [[Lady]]<ref name="Aphra 39" /> [[Domina Tagge]] offered condolences to<ref name="Aphra 40" /> the [[smuggler]]<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> [[Sana Starros]], believing Aphra to have been killed. Once Starros informed<ref name="Aphra 40" /> [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Captain]]<ref name="Aphra 37">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 37|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 37]]</ref> [[Magna Tolvan]] of Tagge's belief, Tolvan compared Aphra to a Corellian roach, noting that if anyone was able to cheat death it was the archaeologist. Shortly after, Starros traveled to Rocedila and rescued Aphra, who was in fact alive.<ref name="Aphra 40" />

The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 40" /> on [[January 31]], [[2024]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/113611/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2020_40|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020) #40}}</ref>
