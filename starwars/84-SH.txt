'''84-SH''' was a timid<ref name="droid">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> [[Color|gray]]-plated [[BB-series astromech droid|BB-series]] [[astromech droid]] in the service of the [[Resistance]]. It was stationed on the [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] above the planet [[D'Qar]] shortly after the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], where it was repaired alongside other BB units.<ref name="Repair Bay" /> [[R0-GR]] would later include 84-SH in his book ''[[Droidography]]''.<ref name="droid" />

==Equipment==
84-SH had an [[internal orbiculate motivator]] powered by a [[Selenium]] drive. On SH's body, it featured multiple cicular [[tool-bay disk]]s, surface [[sensor]]s and cooling vents for the motivator. SH's head stayed on its body with the use of [[magnet]]ic balancing. On SH's head it had a receiver [[antenna]].<ref name="Repair Bay" />
