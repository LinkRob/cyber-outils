'''Cerulean gemstones''' were a type of [[Color|cerulean]] colored gemstone that were embedded into the bodies of [[Kashyyyk clarion]]s by the [[Wookiee]]s of the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]].<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Various costumes worn by the [[Wookiee]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Grozz]] during the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] included cerulean gemstones as a part of their appearance, including the [[Furious Warmonger]], the [[Kashyyyk Champion]], and the [[Life Day Wookiee]].<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Cerulean gemstones were first created as a prop for the film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']],<ref name="ROTSVD">''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> although they are not visible in the final cut of the film released in [[2005]].<ref name="ROT">{{Film|III}}</ref> The gemstones then first appeared in [[canon]] in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Adam Bray]], [[Tricia Barr]] and [[Cole Horton]].<ref name="VE" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]], the gemstones had previously appeared in the 2005 reference book ''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="ROTSVD" />
