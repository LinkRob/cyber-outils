{{Quote|I realize that you have been successful in shipping lommite through Malastare, and circling around Bestine to reach Fondor and the Core.|[[Dooku]], to [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]|<ref name="TRK">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>}}
The '''Bestine system''' was located in the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]. It contained the [[planet]] [[Bestine IV]], as well as the [[Merchant|trading]] outpost [[Kestic Station]]. Located in grid square M-14, the [[star system]] was on the path of the [[Corellian Trade Spine]] [[hyperspace route]].

==Description==
The Bestine system<ref name="SWCT" /> was located in the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]<ref name="Beginner Game" /> and contained<ref name="Ultimate" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="TRK">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> [[Bestine IV]] in the fourth [[orbit]]al position,<ref name="Ultimate" /> both of which were located in the grid square M-14<ref name="Beginner Game" /> of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Rebel Dossier">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''</ref> The [[Merchant|trading]] outpost [[Kestic Station]] was also located on the edge of the [[star system]],<ref name="SWCT" /> in close proximity to Bestine.<ref name="XWing">{{BuildXWingCite|20|Starfighter Aces}}</ref> The [[Corellian Trade Spine]] [[hyperspace route]] ran through the Bestine system, passing through Bestine IV and several other worlds.<ref name="Beginner Game" />

==History==
{{Quote|Rearguard actions across the board&hellip; Twenty-First was on Bestine.|[[Sergeant]] [[Maximian Ajax]], to [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]]|audio=Twenty-First.flac|<ref name="TC" />}}
In the waning [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], [[lommite]] shipments took place from the planet [[Eriadu]] to the [[Core Worlds]]. During their journey, the [[Starship|ships]] carrying the [[ore]] circled around Bestine in order to reach the Core.<ref name="TRK" /> At some point during the [[Imperial Era]], [[Jek Tono Porkins|Jek Porkins]], a [[Merchant|trader]] from Bestine IV, was forced to leave his [[homeworld]] and fought against the [[Galactic Empire]] as a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="SWCT-Jek">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Jek Porkins - X-Wing Pilot}}</ref> During the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="TC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date">In ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', the [[Mission to Bestine IV (Twenty-First)|mission to Bestine IV]] occurs in the same [[Standard week|week]] as the [[Coyerti campaign]]. The section of ''Twilight Company'' including the [[Coyerti]] campaign is set twenty-two [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Clone Wars]], which [[Mission to Mustafar|ended]] in [[19 BBY]], according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. The Coyerti campaign, and thus the mission to [[Bestine IV]], must have therefore occurred in [[3 ABY]].</ref> the Rebel Alliance's [[Twenty-First]] [[Mission to Bestine IV (Twenty-First)|traveled]] to Bestine IV as part of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet's]] [[Mid Rim Retreat|withdrawal]] from the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="TC" /> [[Yipsit]], a [[Shahkirin]] [[Technician|mechanic]], grew up on Bestine IV before leaving at some point to join the [[Resistance]] in their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[First Order]].<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Bestine system was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2015]] digital card trading application [[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]].<ref name="SWCT" /> The [[StarWars.com]] article "[[Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?|Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?]]," written by [[Mark Newbold]] and published on [[January 29]], [[2016]], provided the system's first visual depiction.<ref name="Where-TFA">{{SW|url=news/where-in-the-galaxy-are-the-worlds-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?|int=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Bestine system/Legends|Bestine system]] was first mentioned in ''[[Galaxy Guide 1: A New Hope]]'', a [[roleplaying game]] supplement to [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. The book was written by [[Grant S. Boucher]] and released in [[1989]].<ref name="GG1">''[[Galaxy Guide 1: A New Hope]]''</ref>
