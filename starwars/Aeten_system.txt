==Behind the scenes==
The Aeten system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published on the [[StarWars.com]] website on [[June 24]], [[2025]],<ref name="Appendix" /> as part of the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]].<ref name="Galaxy">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> The [[Aeten system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the reference book [[Star Wars: Empire at War: Prima Official Game Guide|''Star Wars: Empire at War'': Prima Official Game Guide]], published by [[Prima Games]] on [[February 14]], [[2006]].<ref name="EaW">[[Star Wars: Empire at War: Prima Official Game Guide|''Star Wars: Empire at War'': Prima Official Game Guide]]</ref>
