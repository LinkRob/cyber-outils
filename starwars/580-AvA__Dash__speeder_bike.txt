The '''580-AvA "Dash" speeder bike''' was a variant of the [[610-AvA "Dart" speeder bike]]. It was produced by the [[Aratech Repulsor Company]] and sold for 1,700 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. The "Dash" was easy to fly and was used by the forces loyal to [[Jerserra]].<ref name="GoD" />
