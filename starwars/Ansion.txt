'''Ansion''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Helmet Collection 73">{{HelmetCollectionCite|73|Databank A-Z}}</ref> located in the [[Ansion system]], a [[star system]] located in the [[Churnis sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was the junction<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> of the [[Namadii Corridor]] [[hyperspace route]]<ref name="Namadii" /> and another route that ran from [[Keitum]] to the [[Rago system]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref>

A [[Border dispute on Ansion|border dispute]] occurred on Ansion<ref name="Attack of the Clones" /> during the [[Separatist Crisis]], which, along with turmoil on the planet [[Ando]], was enough to stagger [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> 

==Behind the scenes==
Ansion first appeared in ''[[The Approaching Storm]]'', a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel written by [[Alan Dean Foster]] as a tie-in to the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]<ref name="The Approaching Storm">''[[The Approaching Storm]]''</ref> and released on [[January 29]], 2002.<ref name="The Approaching Storm release date">{{SWArchive|url=eu/lit/novel/f20020129/index.html|text=''Approaching Storm'' Interview and Excerpt}}</ref> It became [[canon]] when it was mentioned in the aforementioned film.<ref name="Attack of the Clones" />
