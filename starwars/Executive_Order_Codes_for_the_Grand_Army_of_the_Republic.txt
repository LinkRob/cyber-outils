The '''Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic''',<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> also known as the '''150 general orders''', were a set of directives encoded on [[Behavioral modification biochip|inhibitor chips]] implanted into the brain of  every [[clone trooper]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Along with the [[Supreme Chancellor]], the [[Galactic Senate]]'s [[Republic Security Council]] was capable of activating any of the orders.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> [[Order 65]] was a directive that declared the Supreme Chancellor an enemy of the Republic,<ref name="Order">{{EA|subdomain=forums|url=blog/swgoh-game-info-hub-en/kit-reveal-jocasta-spell-on-nu/11866073|text=Kit Reveal: Jocasta Spell On Nu|author=CG_Meathead|date=2025-02-04}}</ref> while [[Order 66]] declared the [[Jedi Order]] enemies of the Republic.<ref name="End of the Clone Wars">{{EncyclopediaCite|The End of the Clone Wars}}</ref> Order 66 was issued by Supreme Chancellor [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], secretly a [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], and implemented by the Republic's [[clone trooper]]s in hunting down and eliminating all [[Jedi]]. Order 66 led to the [[Great Jedi Purge]] and the end of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The 150 general orders were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> The orders first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], where they were identified as [[Contingency Orders for the Grand Army of the Republic: Order Initiation, Orders 1 Through 150]] in the [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith (junior novelization)|junior novelization]] of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], authored by [[Patricia C. Wrede]] and published in [[2005]].<ref name="Episode III junior novel">[[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith (junior novelization)|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'' junior novelization]]</ref>
