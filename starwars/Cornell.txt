'''Cornell''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who was the brother of [[Tomar Nuq Sin]] and the uncle of [[Karr Nuq Sin]]. He was a [[teacher]] at a [[Taeltor Province trade school|trade school]] in [[Taeltor Province]] on [[Merokia]]. When Tomar Nuq Sin wished to take his son out of his [[Unidentified school (Merokia)|school]] due to problems with [[headache]]s, he contacted Cornell, making arrangements to send him to the trade school.<ref name="Force Collector" />
