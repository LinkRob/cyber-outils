'''Cyruss Okent''' was a [[Jedi Knight]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Okent was promoted to the rank alongside several others at a [[Anakin Skywalker's Knighting Ceremony|ceremony]] held at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[First Battle of Geonosis|beginning]] of the war<ref name="Brotherhood" /> in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="SWT">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Shortly thereafter, Okent was shipped out to join a [[Cyruss Okent's clone battalion|clone battalion]] repelling a Separatist [[Strike on Bracca|strike]] on<ref name="Brotherhood" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> [[Bracca]], making them the only one of the new Knights to miss a reception held for the group in [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] [[Emperor's office|office]] the next [[Standard day|day]].<ref name="Brotherhood" />
