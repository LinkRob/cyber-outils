'''Fillithar''' were a [[snake]]-like [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="BL" /> hailing from the world of the [[Fillithar (planet)|same name]]. As a serpentine life-form, they lacked hindquarters. Their tail, however, was prehensile.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], the Fillithar [[Jedi Master]] [[Simmix]] trained the [[Padawan]] [[Cohmac Vitus]].<ref name="ITD" /> Around [[3 BBY]], a [[Gender|male]] Fillithar named [[Sssamm Ashsssen]] was part of the [[Apprentice Legislature]] along with [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> Twenty [[Standard year|years]] into the reign of the [[New Republic]], the Fillithar were represented in the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]]. Around the time of the so-called [[Napkin Bombing]], their [[senator]] sided with the [[Populists|Populist]] faction.<ref name="BL" />
