A '''laboratory''' was constructed by the wealthy [[Graf family]] on an [[asteroid]] in the [[Hynestian sector]] for the use of the [[hyperspace]] [[scientist]] [[Chancey Yarrow]]. The Grafs offered Yarrow the laboratory as part of a deal struck between them in which she would work for the family<ref name="OOTS" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> after a [[Battle at Gravity's Heart|battle]] in the [[Berenge sector]].<ref name="OOTS" />
