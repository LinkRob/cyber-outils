The '''Cthon''' were a troglodytic [[species]] that did not rely on visual senses. As such, the weapon known as the [[flashstick]], which emitted a bright flash of blinding light when used, had no effect on them.<ref name="G&G" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cthon were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2019]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] reference book ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''.<ref name="G&G" /> The [[Cthon/Legends|species]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first mentioned in the [[1994]] novel ''[[Jedi Search]]'' by [[Kevin J. Anderson]]<ref name="Search">''[[Jedi Search]]''</ref> and were given the name Cthon in the [[2001]] novel ''[[Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter]]'' by [[Michael Reaves]].<ref name="Maul">''[[Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter]]''</ref>
