The '''Analysis Rooms''' were the analysis spaces of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]], falling under the overall jurisdiction of the [[Jedi Archives]] and their [[Chief Librarian]]. They were used to locate the provenance of exotic objects, among many other tasks. Staffed with [[SP-4 analysis droid|SP-4]] and [[JN-66 analysis droid]]s, the Analysis Rooms could access the [[computer]] system of the Jedi Archives to match identification indices, and were equipped with [[multispectral reader]]s as well as [[polysensitive grasper]]s capable of detecting even the slightest odor traces. In order to avoid contamination that could cause false readings from the very sensitive equipment, the Analysis Rooms were hermetically sealed to [[Species|organics]].<ref name="CL" /> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] used the analysis chambers in the Jedi Temple's decontaminated clean room, while he searched for clues to the identity of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s attempted [[Zam Wesell|assassin]].<ref name="Episode 2" />
