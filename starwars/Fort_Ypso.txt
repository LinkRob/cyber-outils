'''Fort Ypso''', also known as '''Ypsobay Trading Company headquarters''',<ref name="Tales from Vandor" /> was a snowy [[mountain]] village on the [[planet]] [[Vandor]].<ref name="EW Solo Guide" /> The buildings were built into the side of a mountain and could be reached by wooden bridges. It contained [[the Lodge]], a dimly lit saloon with a gambling hall and a droid-fighting arena.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

{{City-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The bridges of Fort Ypso were inspired by the bridges of Presbyterian Church, the [[Wikipedia:Washington (state)|Washington]] town in the [[1971]] anti-Western film [[Wikipedia:McCabe & Mrs. Miller|''McCabe & Mrs. Miller'']].<ref name="Art of Solo">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
