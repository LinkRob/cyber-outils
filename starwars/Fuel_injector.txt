A '''fuel injector''' was a component found in some [[speeder]]s. In [[Rey's speeder]], a fuel injector ring was located near the speeder's combustion chamber. [[Light Infantry Utility Vehicle]]s and [[V-4X-D ski speeder]]s also possessed fuel injectors.<ref name="Cross">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref><ref name="ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> after crash landing on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]], [[Toshara]], [[Kay Vess]] took a job for [[Crimson Dawn]] in exchange for new fuel injectors that she needed to repair her [[EML-850 light freighter]], the ''[[Trailblazer]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
