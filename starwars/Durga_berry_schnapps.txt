'''Durga berry schnapps''' were a beverage made from [[Durga berry|Durga berries]] that was [[Diet|potable]] by [[Shani]]. It was served in jugs at a [[Temple on Hynestia|bar]] on<ref name="Sana Starros 4" /> the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Hynestia Prime|Hynestia]].<ref name="Sana Starros 4" />

Around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SanaStarrosDate}}</ref> the Shani [[bounty hunter]] [[Deva Lompop]] and<ref name="Sana Starros 4" /> the [[smuggler]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Sana Starros]] were [[Skirmish on Hynestia|ambushed]] by [[Imperial Military|forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] at the [[Cantina|establishment]], forcing them to retreat to behind its bar where the jugs of Durga berry schnapps were kept. When Lompop began to search through the jugs, Sana questioned what she was doing as her [[Starros Clan|aunt]]<ref name="Sana Starros 4" /> [[Mevera Starros]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> had been shot and needed their help. In response, the Shani told her that she was looking for a drink, finding a jug of Durga berry schnapps. After Sana angrily questioned her for drinking despite their current situation, Lompop drank some of the beverage and noted to never pass up a free drink, before informing Sana of a plan for them to escape.<ref name="Sana Starros 4" />
