'''Alrrark''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Wookiee]] from [[Kashyyyk]] who [[Wookiee warrior|fought]] alongside his ruler, [[King]] [[Grakchawwaa]], during the [[Clone Wars]]. After the war, [[enslaver]]s raided Kashyyyk and tried to capture Alrrark, but he managed to fight his way to freedom and fled to the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Eventually, the Wookiee found himself stranded in the [[Anoat sector]] when the [[Galactic Empire]] established a [[sector]]-wide [[Iron Blockade|blockade]].<ref name="Uprising" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
Several [[Standard month|months]] into the blockade, Alrrark joined the crew of a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] from [[Burnin Konn]], assisting them on various missions throughout the Anoat sector.<ref name="Uprising" />
{{EndGame}}
