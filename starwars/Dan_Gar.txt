The '''Dan'Gar''' were a political party on the [[planet]] [[Desinta]] some time between [[42 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Date">[[Resolve (short story)|"Resolve"]] is set after [[Dooku]]'s departure from the [[Jedi Order]], dated to 42 years before the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] by ''[[The Star Wars Book]]'', which corresponds to [[42 BBY]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system|dating system]] used by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. The story is also set prior to [[Qui-Gon Jinn]]'s [[death]] in the [[Battle of Naboo]], which is dated to [[32 BBY]] by ''Galactic Atlas''.</ref> The group were a political opposition to the worlds nominall elected leader, [[Prefect]] [[Aminar]]. The group also purchased [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Lizel Liit]] when the girl was put up for auction by [[spice]] runner [[Zarah Bliss]].
