'''Ameron''' was a creature whose durable skin was used in [[clothing]]. While on<ref name="Lando - Double or Nothing 1" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Planet">{{BuildXWingCite|63|Starfighter Aces}}</ref> [[Batuv]] in [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Lando - Double or Nothing 1|''Lando - Double or Nothing'' 1]], which features [[Batuv]], to [[10 BBY]].</ref> the [[smuggler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] visited the stall of the [[Trandoshan]] [[Merchant|vendor]] [[Borkus (Trandoshan)|Borkus]] with the intent of purchasing a [[cape]]. After Borkus offered Calrissian a [[Color|blue]] cape of what he claimed to be ameron skin, the smuggler claimed that the cape did not resemble ameron skin, but instead [[zelk]] husk. Taking it as an accusation of lying, Borkus threatened to turn Calrissian into a cape.<ref name="Lando - Double or Nothing 1" />

Calrissian returned to his [[starship]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', not long afterward, having been hired by the [[Kristiss' group|freedom fighter]] [[Kristiss]] to smuggle [[weapon]]s to the [[Petrusian]]s on<ref name="Lando - Double or Nothing 1" /> the planet<ref name="Build 60">{{BuildXWingCite|60|Starfighter Aces}}</ref> [[Kullgroon]]. They were chased by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], leading Calrissian's [[droid]] [[L3-37]] to note that<ref name="Lando - Double or Nothing 1" /> she<ref name="Planet" /> could attempt to cover the vessel in ameron skin in lieu of repairing their [[deflector shield]]s, as their ship was also in need of other costly maintenance.<ref name="Lando - Double or Nothing 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
The skin of ameron was mentioned in the [[Lando - Double or Nothing 1|first issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Lando - Double or Nothing]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series, which was written by [[Rodney Barnes]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Lando - Double or Nothing 1" /> on [[May 30]], [[2018]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/67558/star_wars_lando_-_double_or_nothing_2018_1|text=Star Wars: Lando - Double or Nothing (2018) #1}}</ref>
