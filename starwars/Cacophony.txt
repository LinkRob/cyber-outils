The '''''Cacophony''''' was a [[Nihil]] [[Stormship]]<ref name="ShadowsofStarlight">[[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 3|''The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight'' 3]]</ref> under the command of [[Melis Shryke]]. The ship was equipped with a [[Path engine]], which allowed it to traverse [[hyperspace]] through the [[Stormwall]].<ref name="EyeofDarkness" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4]]|one [[Standard year|year]] and two weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}} Since the events of [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 6|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 6]] are set immediately following the fourth issue and depict the [[expansion of the Stormwall]], the events in ''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'' following the expansion must take place in the same year.</ref> the vessel was stolen by [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]], who used it to traverse past the Stormwall to return to [[Galactic Republic]]-controlled [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="EyeofDarkness" />
