The '''assembly room''' was a room located at the heart of the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]] [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]]. The station's [[Dedication of the Starlight Beacon|dedication ceremony]] was held in the room<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> in [[232 BBY]]. During the station's destruction in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[private investigator]] [[Sian Holt]] crashed her [[Sian Holt's ship|ship]] crashed through the room's [[Viewport|window]] and landed it in the room so she and [[Jedi investigator]] [[Emerick Caphtor]] could investigate the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures that the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s had been using against the [[Jedi]].<ref name="Trail of Shadows 5">[[The High Republic: Trail of Shadows 5|''The High Republic: Trail of Shadows'' 5]]</ref>
