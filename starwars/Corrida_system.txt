The '''Corrida system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Corridan sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]]. It was located in grid square J-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Corrida system was first mentioned in the second iteration of the [[Star Systems of the Galaxy]],<ref name="Appendix" /> an appendix of star systems written by [[Jason Fry]] and released on [[StarWars.com]] on [[October 13]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref> It was created to account for [[Corrida]], a world mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] video game ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''.<ref name="TOR">{{TORcite|type=location|Bloodgouge's Lair|Tatooine}}</ref>
