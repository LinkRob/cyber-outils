'''Crost''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Twi'lek]] from the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] who was a member of the [[Free Ryloth Movement]] during their [[Mission to Ryloth|attempt]] to [[assassin]]ate [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. He was a member of [[Isval]]'s crew, who were responsible for infiltrating the damaged [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Perilous]]'' disguised as a repair crew and destroying it. The group managed to successfully reach the [[Starship|ship's]] [[hyperdrive]] and set charges, escaping in an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[escort boat]] before the ship exploded. Crost was [[Death|killed]] when Vader caused the boat to crash into Ryloth's surface.<ref name="LOTS" />
