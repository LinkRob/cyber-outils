'''Ak-Mal''' was a [[Weequay]] [[mercenary]] loyal to [[Teemo|Teemo the Hutt]]. Ak-Mal was born into [[Slavery|enslavement]] and worked as muscle for the [[Desilijic]] [[kajidic]]. Because of his impressive skills, Ak-Mal was able to earn his freedom and a generous loan in exchange for a period of working exclusively for Teemo.<ref name="Friends Like These" />
