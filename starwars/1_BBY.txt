==Events==
*[[Ellian Zahra]] becomes the student of [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], defeating [[Hannevik]] and [[Beldin]].<ref name="Star Wars 2020 7">[[Star Wars (2020) 7|''Star Wars'' (2020) 7]]</ref> {{Circa}}<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
*The Empire begins mining for [[kyber crystal]]s on [[Jedha]]. The [[journalist]] [[Calliope Drouth]] tries to warn the rebellion of this using coded language. {{Circa}}<ref name="Timelines" />
*[[Alliance High Command]] sets up a [[Great Temple|base]] on [[Yavin 4]],<ref name="Yavin date">{{InsiderCite|203|A Certain Point of View}} states that [[Alliance High Command]] set up a [[Great Temple|base]] on [[Yavin 4]] one [[Standard year|year]] before the events of {{Film|IV}}, which corresponds to 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore, the base on Yavin must have been set up in 1 BBY.</ref> after the evacuation of [[Dantooine base]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> While Yavin 4 was already being used as a base by the time of the [[Battle of Atollon]],<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref> the gathering of various rebel cells on the moon formed the core of High Command.<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" />
*The [[Hunt for Vader]] occurs.<ref name="Target Vader 1" /><ref name="Timelines" />
*The [[Hutt Clan]] and [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] are engaged in a series of conflicts known as the [[trade wars]]. Under the orders of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], [[Greedo]] is sent to [[Mygeeto]] to capture a [[Unidentified Givin codebreaker|Givin codebreaker]] from the [[Vice Chairman]] of the Banking Clan, [[Anolo]], but the mission ends in failure when the codebreaker dies. Greedo is spared from Jabba's wrath when [[Dok-Ondar]] agrees to buy the [[Ki-Adi-Mundi's lightsaber|lightsaber]] of [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]], which Greedo had recovered during the mission.<ref name="GE2" /><ref name="GE2 Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Dok-Ondar]]'s purchase of [[Ki-Adi-Mundi's lightsaber]], depicted in [[Galaxy's Edge 2|''Galaxy's Edge'' 2]], to 1 BBY.</ref>
*[[Alrich Wren]] is [[Mission to Mandalore|rescued]].<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Heroes of Mandalore]]''</ref>
*[[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] efforts to resurrect [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] with the [[Bright Star]] are ruined.<ref name="Vader VR 3" /><ref name="Timelines" />
*[[Sabine Wren]] passes the [[Darksaber]] on to [[Lady]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze|Bo-Katan]] 
*[[Kanan Jarrus]] is killed on [[Lothal]]
*[[Lothal]] is [[Liberation of Lothal|liberated]] from the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Family&Farewell">{{Rebels|Family Reunion – and Farewell}}</ref>
*[[Luthen Rael]] learns of [[Project Stardust]] from [[Lonni Jung]].<ref name="Make It Stop" />
*Following his attempted suicide, Rael is taken to the [[Lina Soh Hospital]] on Coruscant by orders of [[ISB Supervisor|Supervisor]] [[Dedra Meero]]. Shortly after, he is killed by [[Kleya Marki]].<ref name="Make It Stop" />
*[[Major]] [[Lio Partagaz]] sends an [[Imperial Security Bureau|ISB]] [[ISB Tactical|Tactical]] strike force to apprehend Marki, but they are stopped and Marki is rescued by Rebel Alliance operatives [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], [[Ruescott Melshi]] and [[K-2SO]].<ref name=":0">''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' - "[[Jedha, Kyber, Erso]]"</ref> 
*Major Partagaz commits suicide instead of receiving punishment for his failure. Meanwhile, Dedra Meero is [[Unidentified Imperial factory prison|imprisoned.]]<ref name=":0" />
*[[Jyn Erso]] is [[Liberation of Erso|liberated]] by the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Rogue One" />
*The [[Galactic Empire]] conducts its first test of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star's]] [[Superlaser|weapon]] on [[Jedha]], fully [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroying]] [[Jedha City]].<ref name="Rogue One" />
*Open warfare in the [[Galactic Civil War]] [[Battle of Scarif|begins]].<ref name="Rogue One" />
*The Alliance to Restore the Republic wins its first major victory,<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> thanks to the actions of [[Rogue One]], against the Galactic Empire by stealing the [[Death Star plans|plans]] to the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], at the Battle of [[Scarif]].<ref name="Rogue One" />
*After she received the plans to the [[Death Star]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] gives them to [[R2-D2]] before being [[Secret mission to Tatooine|captured]] by the Empire and [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] on the Death Star.<ref name="ANH" />
*[[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] [[Dissolution of the Imperial Senate|dissolves]] the [[Imperial Senate]] and establishes a royal dictatorship.<ref name="ANH" />
*The [[Trade Federation]] is then dissolved.<ref name="BMF 31">{{BuildFalconCite|31|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>
*[[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Luke Skywalker]] join the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="ANH" />
*[[Owen Lars]], [[Beru Whitesun Lars]] and [[Nebit's tribe|Nebit’s Tribe]] are killed.<ref name="ANH" />
*The Empire successfully tests the newly fully operational superlaser of the Death Star and [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroys]] the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]].<ref name="ANH" />
*Luke Skywalker, Obi-Wan Kenobi, [[Han Solo]], and [[Chewbacca]] [[Rescue of Princess Leia|rescue Princess Leia]] aboard the Death Star.<ref name="ANH" />
*Obi-Wan Kenobi is killed by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] aboard the Death Star.<ref name="ANH" />

==Behind the scenes==
Before the release of ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'',<ref name="Timelines" /> it was noted that ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' and the end of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' occurred in 0 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].<ref name="D23 Timeline">[[:File:Years-Timeline.png|D23 timeline]]</ref> However, ''Timelines'' pushed their events into 1 BBY, leaving [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] alone in 0 BBY.<ref name="Timelines" /> Then ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events that prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]] in ''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'' to 1 BBY, Battle of [[Yavin 4|Yavin]] to "0," and the [[Royal Award Ceremony]] to [[0 ABY]] respectively.<ref name="Galactic Atlas 2025">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>
