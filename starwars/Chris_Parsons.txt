'''Chris Parsons''' is an actor who portrayed [[K-3PO]], [[4-LOM]],<ref name="SWI 49 Trail">{{InsiderCite|49|Trail of the Bounty Hunters}}</ref> [[R-3PO]], and [[E-3PO (Bespin)|E-3PO]]<ref name="SW Interviews">{{WebCite|url=https://starwarsinterviews.com/original-trilogy/episode-v-the-empire-strikes-back/chris-parsons-4-lom|text=Chris Parsons (4-LOM)|author=[[Dennis Pellegrom]]|date=2016-08|work=[[Star Wars Interviews]]|archivedate=20230205202937|nolive=1}}</ref>, as well as [[Chase Wilsorr]]<ref name="Wilsorr">{{Twitter|username=cparsonsactor|url=status/701469022064353280|name=Chris Parsons|date=2016-02-21|quote=I think the other 2 have got a few more dollars than I have!! [commenting on a picture with [[Harrison Ford]] and [[Carrie Fisher]] with him in the middle as the character Chase Wilsorr]|archivedate=2023-02-15|https://archive.ph/ABHDV}}</ref> in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] when he was 18 years old. In addition, Parsons acted as [[C-3PO]]'s double and stand-in in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="SW Interviews" /> He is also listed on a call sheet as a stand-in [[BTL Y-wing starfighter|Y-wing]] pilot for ''Return of the Jedi''.<ref name="Callsheet">{{WebCite|url=https://propstore.com/mobile/product/star-wars-ep-vi-return-of-the-jedi/revenge-of-the-jedi-call-sheet-40a|text=Revenge Of The Jedi Call Sheet - 40A|archivedate=2021-11-24|archiveurl=https://archive.vn/SWFx0}}</ref>
