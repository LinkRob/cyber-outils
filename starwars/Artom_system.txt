The '''Artom system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Nuon e Safyd sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It was located in grid square P-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Artom system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Star system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned as the [[Reidi Artom I system]] in the ''[[Living Force Campaign Guide]]'', a [[2001]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' supplement written by [[Robert Wiese]] and [[Andy Collins]] and published by  [[Wizards of the Coast]].<ref name="LFCG">''[[Living Force Campaign Guide]]''</ref> It was then identified as the Artom system in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
