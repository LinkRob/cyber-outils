The '''''Auric''''' was a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[YT-1760 small transport|YT-1760 Small Transport]] active during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. [[Pilot]]ed by [[Commander]] [[Lina Graf]], the ''Auric'' had a five member [[Lina Graf's crew|crew]] that included the [[technician]] [[Skritt]], [[Lieutenant]] [[Thom Hudd]], the former [[bodyguard droid]] [[XM-G3]] and Graf's long-time companion, the droid [[CR-8R]]. The ship was forced to crash-land on [[Mustafar]] after taking damage during a [[Fact-finding mission to the Outer Rim|mission]], where it sank into [[Magma|lava]] and was destroyed.

==History==
{{Quote|Shields are down and the power core's about to blow. We need somewhere to land, and fast.|Lina Graf|<ref name="Tales from Vader Castle 1" />}}
[[File:AuricUnderAttack-TalesFromVadersCastle1.png|thumb|left|200px|''Auric'' under fire from TIE fighters]]
While on a [[Fact-finding mission to the Outer Rim|mission]] to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]],<ref name="Tales from Vader Castle 2">[[Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle 2|''Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle'' 2]]</ref> the ''Auric''{{'s}} crew found themselves pursued by a group of three [[TIE series|TIE]] [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|fighters]]. Serving as the [[Starship|ship's]] [[gunner]], [[XM-G3]] successfully destroyed the enemy fighters, but not before they took out the ''Auric''{{'s}} [[Deflector shield|shields]] and power core, along with the port [[Engine|thruster]]. With these critical systems out, [[Lina Graf]] was forced to make an emergency landing on the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]]-controlled [[Volcano|volcanic]] [[planet]] of [[Mustafar]]. The ''Auric'' landed in one of Mustafar's [[Magma|lava]] fields and quickly [[Temperature|overheated]], forcing the crew to evacuate, despite warnings from [[CR-8R]]. After the ship had been evacuated, it sank into the lava and was destroyed, leaving the crew stranded on Mustafar.<ref name="Tales from Vader Castle 1" />

Years later, while serving as [[Head Curator]] of the [[Graf Archive]], Lina Graf recorded in her [[Journal of Lina Graf|journal]] that her experience on Mustafar was the most frightening of all her adventures.<ref name="Tales from Vader Castle 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Auric'' first appeared in the [[2018]] comic [[Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle 1|''Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle'' 1]].<ref name="Tales from Vader Castle 1" /> Notably, the ship shares its name with [[Auric Graf]], the [[Parent|father]] of its [[captain]] Lina Graf.<ref name="The Escape">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Escape]]''</ref> The ''Auric'' marks the first [[canon]] appearance of the [[YT-1760 small transport/Legends|YT-1760 small transport]], a ship that was previously exclusive to [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Manual" /><ref name="MFOWM">''[[Millennium Falcon Owner's Workshop Manual]]''</ref>
