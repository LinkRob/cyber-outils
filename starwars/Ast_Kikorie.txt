'''Ast Kikorie''' was a location<ref name="Rebel Files" /> in the [[Ast Kikorie system/Legends|Ast Kikorie system]] in the [[Moddell sector]], a subsector of the [[Zuma sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Prior to the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Ast Kikorie was documented by [[Corporal]] [[Ractivelle]] of the [[Stardust unit]] as part of a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] report on the navigation of the Moddell sector in preparation for the Rebel Alliance's attack on the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

Before the Battle of [[Endor]], the map of the Moddell sector and the accompanying reports would be archived in ''[[The Rebel Files]]'', a central data depository containing secret rebel documents and information.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">Since ''[[The Rebel Files]]'' were buried on the same [[Standard day|day]] as the [[Battle of Endor]], which according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' takes place in [[4 ABY]], and because ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' establishes that ''The Rebel Files'' were rediscovered 30 [[Standard year|years]] after the battle, it can be determined that ''The Rebel Files'' were uncovered in [[34 ABY]].</ref> ''The Rebel Files'' were rediscovered and ultimately given to the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
