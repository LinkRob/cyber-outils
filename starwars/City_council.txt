{{Quote|When the city council built this shelter, they installed an emergency exit behind there. You have to pry up the floor next to it and hit the switches in a special sequence to open the door. Don't mess up or you'll have to start over again!|Kunpar Vasivola|<ref name="EFV" />}}
A '''city council''' operated in [[Lonisa City]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the council built an [[Unidentified underground prison|emergency storm bunker]] in Lonisa City, creating the structure with a mosaic depicting Valo's natural beauty. [[Jedi Master]] [[Kunpar Vasivola]] created an emergency exit system requiring several switches to turn on in a particular sequence to activate, which the [[city]] council installed.<ref name="EFV" />

In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> the [[Scarlet Skulls]] crew attempted to [[Rescue of Kunpar Vasivola|rescue]] Vasivola from the building, which had by then been converted into an underground [[prison]] by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Occupation of Valo|occupying]] Valo. The Jedi Master informed his [[Padawan]], [[Ram Jomaram]], of the council's emergency exit as they fled from Nihil pursuers, indicating that if he messed up, the activation would restart. However, a [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] with them&mdash;[[Tep Tep]]&mdash;felt the system was not optimal. Vasivola was offended, indicating he had designed the system, which had been approved by other Masters from the [[Valo Temple]] before the city council installed it.<ref name="EFV" />
