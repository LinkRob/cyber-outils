A '''carp''' was a type of creature with fins. While [[Catastrophe in the Seikara Caverns|onboard]] a [[Eternity Enterprises barge|pleasure barge]] operated by the company [[Eternity Enterprises]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dekien]], the former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] noticed the [[Sail barge|barge's]] billowing [[Color|gold]] sails and mentally noted that they were similar to a carp's fins.<ref name="Ronin" />

==Behind the scenes==
A carp was mentioned in the [[2021]] non-[[canon]] novel ''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]'', written by [[Emma Mieko Candon]].<ref name="Ronin" /> In the real world, a [[Wikipedia:Carp|carp]] is one of several types of [[Wikipedia:Fish|fish]] species native to [[Wikipedia:Eurasia|Eurasia]].<ref name="Carp">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/animal/carp-fish-species|text=Carp|work=the [[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica|''Encyclopædia Britannica'']]|archivedate=20241228065840}}</ref>
