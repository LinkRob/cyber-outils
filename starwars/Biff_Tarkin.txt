'''Biff Tarkin''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] individual and a member of the [[Tarkin family/Legends|Tarkin family]]. They were the son of<ref name="Insider" /> the [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|male]]<ref name="Wilhuff">{{DB|character|grandmofftarkin|Tarkin, Grand Moff}}</ref> [[Wilhuff Tarkin/Legends|Wilhuff Tarkin]], a [[Grand Moff/Legends|Grand Moff]] of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. They were active in [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]] at some point<ref name="Insider" /> after the [[Declaration of a New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] in [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]] and before the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]] in [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]].<ref name="Dates">{{InsiderCite|137|Launchpad|Launch Pad}} states that the events of ''[[Star Wars Detours]]'' would have taken place between the events of {{Film|III}} and {{Film|IV}}, which corresponds to between [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]] and [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]] per ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Biff Tarkin was mentioned in the [[Star Wars Insider 137|115th issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine<ref name="Insider" /> released on [[October 23]], [[2012]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanMagazines|url=t/star-wars-insider/us/137|text=Star Wars Insider Issue #137}}</ref> They were set to appear in ''[[Star Wars Detours]]'',<ref name="Insider" /> an indefinitely postponed<ref name="New Direction">{{SW|url=news/a-new-direction-for-lucasfilm-animation|text=A New Direction for Lucasfilm Animation}}</ref> animated series created by [[George Lucas]], the creator of ''[[Star Wars]]''.<ref name="announcement">{{SWArchive|url=fans/media/newseries_announcement/index.html|text=New ''Star Wars'' Animated Series in the Works}}</ref> Biff Tarkin would have been voiced by [[Zachary Levi]].<ref name="Insider" />
