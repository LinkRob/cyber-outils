'''Arakurth''' were a type of gigantic [[worm]] with three [[claw]]-like [[Tooth|teeth]] protruding from their [[mouth]]. They were [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[Color|green]] [[gas]] [[cloud]] of the [[Typhonic Nebula]]. In [[35 ABY]], the [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] [[Hyperspace skipping|hyperspace skipped]] the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' into the [[nebula]]'s [[Megafauna Chasm]] and encountered an arakurth. The ''Falcon'' managed to avoid the worm, but two [[First Order]] [[TIE/wi Interceptor]]s pursuing the [[Light freighter|freighter]] collided with the creature.

==Biology and appearance==
Arakurth were a type of monstrous<ref name="CGttSWG" /> [[Realspace|space]]<ref name="Junior">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> [[worm]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[Color|green]] [[gas]] [[cloud]] of the [[Typhonic Nebula]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]. Measuring over four hundred [[meter]]s (1,312 [[Foot|feet]]) in length, the gigantic creatures had a chasmic,<ref name="CGttSWG" /> [[tooth]]-rimmed<ref name="TROSEE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> [[Mouth|maw]] with three larger, [[claw]]-like teeth protruding from it with equal, triangular spacing. Their skin was brown and roughly textured with bumps with folds around the mouth.<ref name="CGttSWG" />

==History==
{{Quote|Last jump, maybe forever.''"<br />[Klaud screams]<br />"''Hold on!|Poe Dameron and Klaud encounter an arakurth|<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref>}}
[[File:PoeDameron-HaA2.png|thumb|left|300px|An arakurth tried to ingest the ''Millennium Falcon'' in Megafauna Chasm.]]
Arakurth dwelt in<ref name="CGttSWG" /> the [[atmosphere]]<ref name="TROS AD">{{Film|IX}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> of the [[nebula]]'s [[Megafauna Chasm]] and posed a great threat to any [[starship]] that trespassed there, almost guaranteeing [[death]] to anyone who sighted one. Rumors spread about the gigantic worms patrolling the chasm, which was how the area earned its name.<ref name="CGttSWG" />

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}} to [[35 ABY]].</ref> the [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] [[Hyperspace skipping|hyperspace skipped]] the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', a [[Resistance]] [[YT-1300 light freighter]], into an arakurth's vicinity in the chasm while being pursued by two [[First Order]] [[TIE/wi Interceptor]]s.<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> The worm opened its maw directly in their path in order to ingest them, but just before the ship was devoured,<ref name="Junior" /> Dameron turned and skipped again as, within the ''Falcon''{{'s}} [[cockpit]], the [[Trodatome]] [[Klaud]] screamed at the looming monster. The two [[TIE series|TIE fighters]] failed to avoid the arakurth, colliding with its open mouth<ref name="TROS" /> and ramming down its gullet<ref name="TROSEE" /> before exploding. The worm then roared and dove deeper into the gas.<ref name="TROS" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:LEGOArakurth-LSS.png|thumb|right|300px|A LEGO arakurth]]
An arakurth first appeared in the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. The creature was not identified in the film,<ref name="TROS" /> but received a name in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''.<ref name="CGttSWG" />

In the [[2022]] non-[[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', an arakurth appears during the Episode IX portion of the game in a scene<ref name="LEGO">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''</ref> matching the events of the film.<ref name="TROS" /> After the ''Falcon'' jumps, however, it is depicted as arriving in several more locations than in the film, with the [[Light freighter|freighter]] facing into a large creature's mouth similarly to that of the arakurth in each. The creatures are an [[exogorth]], a [[sarlacc]], and a [[rathtar]].<ref name="LEGO" />
