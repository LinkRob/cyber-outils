'''Fool's Priorite''' was a substance that could be passed off as [[priorite]], a valueable mineral found on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]]. A [[Unidentified charlatan|charlatan]] at one point tried to sell Fool's Priorite as priorite to the [[merchant]] [[Doma Dendra]] of [[Dendra's Antiquities]] on Koboh. The [[fortune teller]] [[Tulakt]] stopped the sale after realizing that it was a con, having noticed that the seller was wearing long [[Clothing|sleeves]] despite the heat in order to perform sleight of hand. Dendra was grateful to Tulakt for stopping her from buying the Fool's Priorite.<ref name="Survivor" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> Tulakt told the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] about the attempted Fool's Priorite sale while at the [[cantina]] [[Pyloon's Saloon]] on Koboh.<ref name="Survivor" />
{{EndGame}}
