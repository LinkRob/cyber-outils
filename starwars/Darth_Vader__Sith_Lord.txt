'''''Darth Vader: Sith Lord''''' is a [[canon]] young-readers reference book in [[Scholastic]]'s ''[[Backstories]]'' series. It was written by [[Jason Fry]] and published on [[February 28]], [[2017]]. The book's story is told as an [[in-universe]] [[Hologram|holographic]] log once recorded by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and since recovered by [[New Republic Intelligence]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Who is Darth Vader? How did the once great Jedi become a fearsome villain? Who are the allies and foes Darth Vader has faced? In this biography&mdash;complete with black-and-white illustrations, timelines, and character profiles&mdash;young readers will delight in learning the complete history of the Sith Lord.''
