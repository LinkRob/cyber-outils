Using a skill known only to him, he forged kyber crystals into one, solid blade. This was the very first lightsaber.|Naare|<ref name="A Hero Discovered" />}}
'''Baird Kantoo''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who lived as a [[Jedi Master]] prior to the formation of the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]]. Kantoo sought to craft a weapon suitable for the [[Jedi]], so he used his knowledge of [[the Force]] to [[Kyber crystal building|forge several kyber crystals into a single blade]], creating the [[Kyber Saber]]&mdash;[[the galaxy]]'s first [[lightsaber]]. The weapon magnified Kantoo's Force abilities a thousandfold, and when Kantoo realized the threat the Kyber Saber possessed, he shattered the blade into seven pieces. Kantoo gave the blade's fragments to six [[Jedi Knight]]s, who hid the [[Kyber crystal|crystals]] across the [[galaxy]]. Kantoo later joined the [[Force Builders]], and he recorded the secret to building with kyber crystals in a [[holocron]], which was hidden at the site of the Kyber Saber's destruction.

For [[Millennium|millennia]], Kantoo's story was preserved in the [[Jedi Archives]], and it was uncovered by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Vader enlisted [[Naare]], a [[Sith]] [[agent]], to locate the crystals, and she began her quest on the [[planet]] [[Nal Kapok/LEGO|Nal Kapok]]. During this [[time]], Naare encountered a young, [[Force-sensitive]] boy named [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]], to whom the crystals called. To gain Freemaker's assistance, Naare claimed she was a Jedi on a mission to recover the Kyber Saber before [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] could. Freemaker agreed to help, and Naare soon collected the seven fragments, rebuilding the weapon. However, Freemaker learned Naare's true nature, and he destroyed the Kyber Saber in a river of [[Magma|lava]] on [[Sullust]], honoring Kantoo's decision and considering the Jedi a hero. Later, Freemaker discovered Kantoo's hidden holocron and was deemed worthy of learning the Master's Force technique.

==Biography==
===Crafting the first lightsaber===
{{Quote|When Kantoo realized the potential danger of the Kyber Saber, he smashed his blade to pieces. He gave the Kyber Saber crystals to trusted Jedi Knights to hide in distant corners of the galaxy so that no one would ever again possess the power of the Kyber Saber.|Naare|<ref name="A Hero Discovered" />}}
[[File:Hiding_the_Kyber_Saber.png|thumb|left|275px|Baird Kantoo has the Kyber Saber fragments hidden.]]
Prior to the creation of the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]], Baird Kantoo was a wise [[Jedi Master]] in [[Jedi Order]]. Seeking to craft a weapon worthy of the [[Jedi]], Kantoo used a secret [[The Force|Force]] technique to forge several [[kyber crystal]]s into a single, solid blade&mdash;the [[Kyber Saber]], a weapon which amplified his power a thousandfold.<ref name="A Hero Discovered" /> However, while on an [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Unidentified planet (Baird Kantoo)|planet]],<ref name="The Pit and the Pinnacle" /> Kantoo's power escaped him,<ref name="A Hero Discovered" /> and when he inadvertently destroyed the [[planet]]'s [[Unidentified moon (Baird Kantoo)|moon]],<ref name="The Pit and the Pinnacle" /> the Master came to terms with the danger that his weapon possessed. To ensure the Kyber Saber would not be used again, Kantoo smashed the blade into seven pieces, keeping the hilt and giving the remaining fragments to six [[Jedi Knight]]s whom he trusted. The Knights hid the Kyber Saber [[crystal]]s across [[the galaxy]], and Kantoo's story was recorded in [[Naare's holocron|a holocron]], which was preserved in the [[Jedi Archives]].<ref name="A Hero Discovered" />

Sometime after Kantoo's incident with the Kyber Saber, the Master joined the [[Force Builders]],<ref name="The Pit and the Pinnacle" /> a group of [[Force-sensitive]] [[architect]]s and shipwrights.<ref name="The Tower of Alistan Nor">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Tower of Alistan Nor}}</ref> During his [[time]] with the Force Builders, Kantoo stored his knowledge of building with kyber crystals in [[Baird Kantoo's holocron|another holocron]]. Eventually, this holocron was safeguarded in a [[Jedi temple (Baird Kantoo)|Jedi temple]] at the site of the Kyber Saber's destruction.<ref name="The Pit and the Pinnacle" /> Although Kantoo's weapon was unsuitable for use, the Kyber Saber signaled a landmark in the history of the Jedi, credited with being the first [[lightsaber]].<ref name="A Hero Discovered" /> The lightsabers which followed were powered by kyber crystals,<ref name="Lightsaber Crystal">{{Databank|lightsaber-crystal|Kyber Crystal (Lightsaber Crystal)}}</ref> and the [[sword]]s became the weapon of the Jedi.<ref name="A Hero Discovered" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|We have theories as to where Master Kantoo's Jedi hid the Kyber Saber crystals, but so does the Emperor.|Naare|<ref name="The Mines of Graballa">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Mines of Graballa}}</ref>}}
[[Millennium|Millennia]] after the destruction of the Kyber Saber, the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] located the holocron of Kantoo's story within the Jedi Archives. Believing that the Kyber Saber could end the [[Galactic Civil War]] and ensure the [[Sith]]'s control of the [[galaxy]], he enlisted [[Naare]], a Sith [[agent]], to locate its seven pieces. Naare began her [[Mission to Nal Kapok|search]] on the [[planet]] of [[Nal Kapok/LEGO|Nal Kapok]], where she met [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]], a young Force-sensitive boy with a natural connection to the Kyber Saber crystals. Wishing to gain Freemaker's trust, Naare told Kantoo's story to the boy, and she claimed that she was a Jedi trying to find the Kyber Saber crystals before [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] could. Freemaker agreed to help Naare find the crystals, and they began [[Quest for the Kyber Saber Crystals|their quest]].<ref name="A Hero Discovered" />

[[File:Baird-Kantoo-holocron.png|thumb|right|275px|Rowan Freemaker discovers Baird Kantoo's holocron.]]
Through Freemaker's ability, Naare quickly found the seven crystals, and during an [[Empire Day]] celebration, the Sith agent rebuilt the Kyber Saber, [[Skirmish on Coruscant (Kyber Saber)|wreaking havoc]] at the [[Imperial Palace]] on the planet [[Coruscant]]. When Freemaker learned of Naare's true identity, he used his connection to the Kyber Saber crystals to overpower her, taking the weapon for himself. With his siblings [[Zander Freemaker|Zander]] and [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi]], Freemaker traveled to<ref name="Return of the Kyber Saber">{{FreemakerAdventures|Return of the Kyber Saber}}</ref> [[Sullust]],<ref name="Return of the Return of the Jedu">{{FreemakerAdventures|Return of the Return of the Jedi}}</ref> where he honored Kantoo by throwing the Kyber Saber into a river of [[Magma|lava]], destroying the weapon.<ref name="Return of the Kyber Saber" /> Sometime later, Freemaker [[Mission to obtain a Kyber crystal|visited]] the world on which the Kyber Saber was initially destroyed, and the boy found Kantoo's holocron in the Jedi temple. The holocron presented a [[Hologram|visage]] of Kantoo, and it revealed the secret of building with kyber crystals, also issuing a warning of the destructive potential. Grasping the possible dangers, Freemaker was initially hesitant to use the technique. However, after talking to his sister Kordi, the boy concluded that Emperor Palpatine could not be defeated without taking the risk, and he constructed an amalgam of various kyber crystals to power his family's [[starship]], the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']].<ref name="The Pit and the Pinnacle" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|&hellip;Master Kantoo was right. Nobody should have this much power. A hero knows when to lay down his weapon.|Rowan Freemaker|<ref name="Return of the Kyber Saber" />}}
A human male with light-[[color]]ed skin and long, gray [[hair]], Kantoo was a wise Jedi Master. After creating the Kyber Saber, Kantoo saw its potential danger, so he smashed the weapon and gave the pieces to trusted Jedi Knights, believing that no one should possess that amount of power. Millennia later,<ref name="A Hero Discovered" /> Kantoo was regarded as a hero by Freemaker, who destroyed the Kyber Saber.<ref name="Return of the Kyber Saber" /> When Freemaker found Kantoo's holocron, the boy was deemed worthy, and the holocron revealed the skill of building with kyber crystals.<ref name="The Pit and the Pinnacle" />

==Powers and abilities==
{{Quote|The Kyber Saber was an impressive weapon. It magnified Kantoo's powers a thousandfold.|Naare|<ref name="A Hero Discovered" />}}
[[File:Using_the_Kyber_Saber.png|thumb|left|275px|Baird Kantoo bisects a moon with the Kyber Saber.]]
Kantoo was a gifted Jedi Master, and he discovered how to build with kyber crystals, which he used to forge the Kyber Saber. With the weapon, Kantoo's power was magnified a thousandfold.<ref name="A Hero Discovered" /> Kantoo kept the skill a secret, later storing the knowledge in a holocron.<ref name="The Pit and the Pinnacle" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|Kordi, the Kyber Saber was this fantastic, amazing weapon, but it led to that. ''[Freemaker gestures at the destroyed moon.]'' And worse.|Rowan Freemaker, to his sister|<ref name="The Pit and the Pinnacle" />}}
As a Jedi Master, Kantoo wore angular, tan [[Jedi apparel|Jedi robes]] and a brown [[cloak]]. Kantoo wanted to create a weapon worthy of the Jedi Order, so he forged several kyber crystals into a solid, blue-hued blade, which became known as the Kyber Saber. The Kyber Saber was able to multiply one's skill by a thousand, which Kantoo deemed too dangerous, leading him to shatter the blade and hide its fragments. Millennia later,<ref name="A Hero Discovered" /> the Kyber Saber was reconstructed by the Sith agent Naare, and she nearly conquered the galaxy through the weapon's power. Naare's plot was stopped by Freemaker, who had a natural connection to the Kyber Saber crystals, giving him control of the blade.<ref name="Return of the Kyber Saber" />

==Behind the scenes==
Baird Kantoo first appeared in "[[A Hero Discovered]]," the pilot episode of the [[Canon|non-canon]] animated television series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'', which premiered in [[2016]].<ref name="A Hero Discovered" /> Kantoo later appeared in "[[The Pit and the Pinnacle]]," the eighth episode of the show's [[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures Season Two|second season]]. In "The Pit and the Pinnacle," Kantoo was voiced by [[Trevor Devall]].<ref name="The Pit and the Pinnacle" />

''The Freemaker Adventures'' credits Kantoo with creating the first lightsaber.<ref name="A Hero Discovered" /> Within the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the first lightsaber, known as the [[First Blade]], was crafted by the [[Weapon Master]] of the [[Je'daii Order]],<ref name="The Old Republic">{{TORcite|type=class|Jedi Consular|Trials in Kaleth|Tython}}</ref> while its precursor, the [[Forcesaber]], was forged by the [[Rakata/Legends|Rakata]].<ref name="Dawn of the Jedi">[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi 0|''Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi'' 0]]</ref>
