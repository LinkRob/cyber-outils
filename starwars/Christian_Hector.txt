'''Christian Hector''' is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] author who wrote several short ''[[Star Wars]]'' comic stories in the [[LEGO Star Wars (magazine)|''LEGO Star Wars'' magazine]] from [[Blue Ocean Entertainment]], including stories like "[[Virus Alert!]]" in [[2015]],<ref name="LSW5" /> "[[Trouble at Mos Eisley]]" in [[2016]],<ref name="LSW13">[[LEGO Star Wars 13|''LEGO Star Wars'' 13]]</ref> and "[[Save the Millennium Falcon!]]" in [[2017]].<ref name="LSW20">[[LEGO Star Wars 20|''LEGO Star Wars'' 20]]</ref>
