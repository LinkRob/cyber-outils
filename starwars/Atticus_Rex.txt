'''Atticus Rex''' was a [[bounty hunter]] who worked for the [[Haxion Brood]] crime syndicate.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> Rex was sent to hunt the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] during his [[second mission to Zeffo]]. The two fought, and Rex successfully captured Kestis and [[BD-1]], and brought them to the [[Haxion Fight Pit]] on [[Ordo Eris]]. The two fought again, and Kestis [[Death|killed]] Atticus Rex.<ref name="Manga" />
