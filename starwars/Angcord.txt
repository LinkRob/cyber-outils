'''Angcord''' was a [[planet]] in [[the galaxy]] mostly covered in [[water]], with numerous rocky islands blanketed with [[Color|green]] [[tree]]s. During the [[High Republic Era]], a pair of [[Jedi]] traveled to Angcord to search for ancient artifacts. The legendary [[Echo Stone]] was hidden on Angcord at some point before the High Republic Era.<ref name="Quest of the Jedi" />

==History==
At some point before the [[High Republic Era]], the Echo Stone was hidden on Angcord from the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Ravi]]. During the High Republic Era, [[Jedi Master]] [[Barnabas Vim]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Vix Fonnick]] traveled to Angcord to search for the Echo Stone. After discovering that the Echo Stone was corrupting the planet and the [[Lady of Angcord]] they destroyed it and scattered its pieces in different places across the galaxy.<ref name="Quest of the Jedi" />

==Behind the scenes==
Angcord first appeared in the [[2023]] [[canon]] comic ''[[The High Republic Adventures: Quest of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="DH">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3010-780/star-wars-the-high-republic-adventures--quest-of-the-jedi|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures&mdash;Quest of the Jedi}}</ref> ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' originally placed Angcord in the Inner Rim Territories,<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> and when the Angcord system was added to the [[Star Systems of the Galaxy]] appendix of [[star system]]s<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[October 13]], [[2025]],<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref> that placement was repeated; however, the update to the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on the same day instead erroneously moved Angcord to the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref>
