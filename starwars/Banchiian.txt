'''Banchiians''' were a diminutive [[Bird|avian]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] native to the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Banchii]]. A primitive species, the Banchiians did not have a concept of personal property, believing that everything on Banchii was for everyone to share, and did not speak [[Galactic Basic Standard]]. When Banchii was settled by offworlders during the [[High Republic Era]], the Banchiians took utensils and dishes from the settlement, leading to their discovery by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Lily Tora-Asi]], her [[Padawan]] [[Keerin Fionn]], and the [[Jedi Initiate|younglings]] [[Nima Allets|Nima]] and [[Viv'Nia Nia'Viv|Viv'nia]].<ref name="The Banchiians" />
