A '''CoMar Tri-Tracker chip''' was a type of [[computer]] chip used in military-grade [[targeting computer]]s. By the time of the [[Cold War]], they were used primarily in older-model devices, such as the targeting computer that ran the ''[[Colossus]]'' platform's defense [[turbolaser]]s. Due to being military hardware, they were considered rare and expensive for any civilians looking for them.<ref name="Synaras Score" />

==Behind the scenes==
