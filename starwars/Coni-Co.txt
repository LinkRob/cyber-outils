{{Quote|You can ride Coni-Co. He's young, but he knows the winds as well as any full-grown wind-truster.|Tonn-Glon, to Luke Skywalker|<ref name="The Legends of Luke Skywalker">''[[The Legends of Luke Skywalker]]''</ref>}}
'''Coni-Co''' was the name given to a young [[wind-truster]] who was the personal mount of the [[Lew'elan]] [[Tonn-Glon]]. [[Luke Skywalker]] rode the wind-truster while attempting to circumvent the globe of [[Lew'el]].<ref name="Legends" />

==Behind the scenes==
In ''[[The Legends of Luke Skywalker]]'', Coni-Co is described as being [[Color|scarlet]]: "([[Tonn-Glon]]) caressed the crest ridge of his scarlet mount and urged him to keep up." However, in [[J.G. Jones]]'s chapter illustration, the [[wind-truster]] is depicted as a white bird.<ref name="Legends" />
