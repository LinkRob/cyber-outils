==Behind the scenes==
The Abregado debris field first appeared, albeit unidentified, in "[[Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence'']]," the second episode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]],<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> which originally aired on [[October 3]], [[2008]].<ref name="Rising Malevolence air date">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/rising-malevolence-episode-guide|text="Rising Malevolence" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> It was first identified in the episode's accompanying guide on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Concept Art Gallery" />
