'''Blackwoods''' were a type of [[tree]] that grew in the [[forest]]s of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. In the first [[Standard year|year]] of the Empire's [[Imperial Era|reign]] after its [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]]<ref name="MoF" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the scent of blackwoods and [[pine]]s drifted in on the breeze through the balcony windows of [[Eastwind Manor]] on Alderaan while [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] was staying there.<ref name="MoF" /> Alderaan was [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], blackwoods were mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', which was written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first instalment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Blackwood/Legends|they]] appeared in the [[1997]] novel ''[[Planet of Twilight]]'' by [[Barbara Hambly]].<ref name="PoT">''[[Planet of Twilight]]''</ref>
