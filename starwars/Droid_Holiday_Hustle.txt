"'''Droid Holiday Hustle'''" is a [[Canon|non-canon]] animated [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short released on [[November 18]], [[2022]] on the LEGO YouTube and [[StarWarsKids.com]] channels. It is part of the ''[[LEGO STAR WARS: Celebrate the Season]]'' series.<ref name="SWKids" />

==Official description==
''C-3PO and R2-D2 hustle across the galaxy in all modes of transportation to get to their holiday dinner on time.''<ref name="Droid Holiday Hustle" />

==Plot summary==
[[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]] race to [[Mos Eisley]]'s [[spaceport]] but are too late to catch their departing [[shuttle]]. They attempt to board a [[TUG-b13|quadjumper]] but a [[human]] [[Gender|boy]] does not allow them to board since it is full. They then approach a sleeping [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[X-wing starfighter]] pilot. R2-D2 wakes up the sleeping pilot and shows him a [[hologram]] map of their destination. The pilot allows the two droids to ride in the [[astromech socket]] of his starship.

During the [[hyperspace]] journey, C-3PO clings onto dear life and causes R2-D2 to miscalculate the jump. The X-wing exits hyperspace prematurely and crashes on a snowy rocky world. They are approached by an [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT walker]], which allows them to ride with several passengers. Later, the droids find passage aboard a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]].

Later on [[Endor]], the droids are carried by [[Ewok]]s who attempted to follow the coordinates displayed by R2-D2. The droids then fly various transports including an [[A-wing starfighter]], an [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunship]], and finally the ''[[Razor Crest]]''. The two droids ride down a [[turbolift]] where they join a family dinner consisting of [[Han Solo]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Chewbacca]], and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]].
