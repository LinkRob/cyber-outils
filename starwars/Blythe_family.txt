The '''Blythe family''' was a family of [[human]]s. Originating on the [[Core Worlds|Core World]] [[Alderaan]], a small branch of the family, consisting of [[Ottoh Blythe|Ottoh]] and [[Erika Blythe]] and their children [[Ronn Blythe|Ronn]] and [[Bailen Blythe|Bailen]], relocated to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Elphrona]] to live a simpler life. However, they were then [[Raid on Elphrona|targeted]] for kidnapping by members of the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s so they could be ransomed to their wealthy relatives. The true purpose of the raid, however, was to capture a [[Jedi]], as Elphrona was known to host an [[Elphrona Outpost|outpost]] of the [[Jedi Order]]. Erika, Ronn and Bailen were rescued by the Jedi [[Porter Engle]], [[Indeera Stokes]] and [[Bell Zettifar]], but [[Jedi Master]] [[Loden Greatstorm]] was captured while attempting to rescue Ottoh. Subsequently, [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] [[Death|killed]] Ottoh, as Ro had the Jedi prisoner he had wanted. Erika and her children moved back to live with their relatives on Alderaan.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
