'''Chirpa''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Ewok]] who served as the [[chief]] of the [[Council of Elders (Ewok)|Council of Elders]] and Ewok [[Bright Tree tribe|tribe]] that lived in [[Bright Tree Village]] on the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]].<ref name="Princess">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref>

==Biography==
[[File:ChiefChirpa-SoH.png|thumb|left|200px|Chief Chirpa]]
After his father, [[Buzza]], served as [[chief]], he gave his son the throne<ref name="Night of the Gorax" /> and Chirpa served for 42 seasons before the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="SW:AEYNtK" /> Chirpa was also [[Marriage|married]] to [[Ra-Lee]]<ref name="SW.com" /> and had a daughter named [[Kneesaa a Jari Kintaka|Kneesaa]].<ref name="SW:AEYNtK" />

During the Battle of Endor, he allowed a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|strike team]], led by [[General]] [[Han Solo]], to become part of the tribe. The Ewoks also agreed to help the Rebels on their mission to destroy the [[Death Star's shield generator|generator]] powering the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star's]] [[deflector shield]].<ref name="Episode VI" />

He was succeeded as Chief of the tribe by Kneesaa.<ref name="SW:AEYNtK" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Personality and traits==
Like all Ewoks, Chirpa was short. He had [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s and a coat of gray and white [[Hair|fur]].<ref name="Episode VI" /> He was an experienced, fair-minded ruler.<ref name="Card Trader" /> Chirpa had a pet [[Chirpa's iguana|iguana]].<ref name="218 Launchpad">{{InsiderCite|218|Launchpad}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Chief Chirpa was played by [[Jane Busby]] in the [[1983]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], the final installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]].<ref name="Episode VI" /> Originally, Chief Chirpa's name was Chief Chiepa in the early script incarnations of ''Return of the Jedi''.<ref name="Former DB">{{DB|character|chiefchirpa|Chirpa, Chief}}</ref>

In [[2015]], [[Chuck Wendig]], author of the novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], expressed interest in potentially writing stories about [[Omi]], [[Malakili]], or Chirpa. However, he ultimately chose to write about Malakili instead in his sequel novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''.<ref name="Chuck Wendig's War">{{InsiderCite|160|Chuck Wendig's War}}</ref>
