'''Cycler rifles''' were crude yet reliable [[slugthrower rifle]]s designed to fire [[Slug|solid projectiles]]. They could hit targets at extreme ranges and penetrate [[Deflector shield|energy shields]]. Used primarily by primitive cultures and hunters, they were strong enough to tear whole limbs from a body.<ref name="Battlefront" />

Cycler [[rifle]]s were notably used by the [[Tusken Raider]]s of [[Tatooine]]. The Raiders had been observed sniping at passing [[podracer]]s in the desert. At long range, the cycler rifles proved powerful enough to destroy the [[IPG-X1131 LongTail|podracer]] of [[Teemto Pagalies]], causing it to crash during the [[Boonta Eve Classic]].<ref name="TPM" /> Among Tusken clans, the cycler rifle was the less popular weapon, because despite the fact that the rifle could hit targets reliably at long ranges, it had a crude design and the fire rate was typically slow.<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> [[Boba Fett]] possessed an old cycler rifle in the aftermath of his apparent death during a [[Rescue of Han Solo|battle]] at the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]]. [[Migs Mayfeld]] later used it to destroy [[Juggernaut Five]] and its cargo of [[Rhydonium]].<ref name="Chapter 15">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 15: The Believer}}</ref>
