'''Crane transports''',<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> also referred to as '''walker cranes''',<ref name="Art of Outlaws">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Outlaws]]''</ref> were specialized quadrupedal [[walker]]s that moved on mechanical [[Limb|legs]] and utilized at least one crane,<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> generally for salvage<ref name="Mando 11">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}}</ref> or scrapping-related purposes. [[Heavy industrial crane transport]]s,<ref name="Mando 15">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 15: The Believer}}</ref> [[OI-CT]]s,<ref name="Solo" /> and [[Mobile loading gantry|mobile loading gantries]]<ref name="Mando 15" /> were all types of crane transports.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crane transports first appeared in the [[2018]] movie ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Solo" /> though they were not formally identified until the ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' released in late [[2024]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />
