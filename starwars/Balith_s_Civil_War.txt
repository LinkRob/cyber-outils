'''Balith's Civil War''' was a conflict that took place on the [[planet]] [[Balith]] during the [[Clone Wars]], leading to requested intervention from the [[Galactic Republic]]. Under orders of the [[Jedi High Council]], [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and<ref name="Assassin" /> the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Third Legion]] were dispatched to the conflict. Skywalker and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] departed [[Coruscant]] while Skywalker's [[Padawan]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], stayed behind by the orders of the [[Jedi High Council]]. Skywalker eventually returned from the conflict after learning that Tano had protected [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] &ndash; Skywalker's secret [[Marriage|wife]] &ndash; from an assassination attempt at the hands of [[Aurra Sing]] during the [[Alderaan Refugee Conference]].<ref name="Assassin" /> At one point, refugees from the war passed through the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planetoid]] [[Sídi]], where they paid the innkeeper [[Rank]] to use the [[abandoned military relay tower]] there to search for good news, but heard nothing. Rank's daughter [[Bug (Sídi)|Bug]] learned the passcode to get into the tower by spying on him after following the group unnoticed.<ref name="Bug">{{StoryCite|story=Bug (short story)|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Balith civil war was first mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]] episode [[Assassin (episode)|"Assassin"]].<ref name="Assassin" />
