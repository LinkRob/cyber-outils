'''25 Years of Collecting Droids and Ewoks!''' is an article by [[Pete Vilmur]] that was published on the [[StarWars.com]] website on [[September 7]], [[2010]]. The article explores the merchandising that surrounded the [[Star Wars: Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO|''Star Wars Droids'']] and [[Ewoks (television series)|''Star Wars: Ewoks'']] animated cartoons from [[Nelvana]]. The article includes numerous images that depict toys, games, concept art, and other collectibles based on characters from the cartoons.
