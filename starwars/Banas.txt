'''Banas''' was a rugged [[planet]] with a cold environment<ref name="Comp Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> that was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Hyperspace Stories 2" /> and on the frontier of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s territory<ref name="Comp Encyclopedia" /> during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] [[Mission to Banas|traveled]] to Banas to investigate if the planet was suitable for a new base for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Banas was also the [[homeworld]] of a [[Unidentified Banas species|sentient species]].<ref name="Hyperspace Stories 2" />
