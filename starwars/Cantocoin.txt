'''Cantocoins''' were a type of [[gambling]] chip<ref name="The Ride" /> made from precious metal.<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> They were used as [[currency]] in the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|Canto Casino]] in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="The Ride" /> The coins came in three sizes of flat circular disc, with the largest being gold in [[color]], the second largest being silver, and the smallest being bronze.<ref name="TLJVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cantocoins were created for the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="TLJVD" /> which released on [[December 15]], [[2017]];<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-viii-to-open-december-15-2017|text=''Star Wars: Episode VIII'' to Open December 15, 2017}}</ref> however, they appeared prior to the film's release in the short story "[[The Ride]]" by [[John Jackson Miller]], which was released in the anthology book [[Canto Bight (novella collection)|''Canto Bight'']]<ref name="The Ride" /> on [[December 5]]<ref name="Book release">{{SW|url=news/check-out-the-cover-and-new-details-of-journey-to-star-wars-the-last-jedi-canto-bight|text=Check Out the Cover and New Details of Journey to ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' - ''Canto Bight''}}</ref> as part of the ''[[Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]'' promotional campaign.<ref name="The Ride" /> The props used in ''The Last Jedi'' were made from molded plastic. The gold coins were 2.25 inches wide, the silver coins were 1.75 inches wide, and the bronze coins were 1.5 inches wide.<ref name="Auction">{{WebCite|url=https://entertainment.ha.com/itm/movie-tv-memorabilia/props/star-wars-episode-viii-the-last-jedi-disney-2017-3-cantocoin-props/a/7456-11065.s|text=Star Wars: Episode VIII - The Last Jedi (Disney, 2017), (3) "Cantocoin" Props|work=[[Wikipedia:Heritage Auctions|Heritage Auctions]]|archivedate=20260426183006}}</ref>
