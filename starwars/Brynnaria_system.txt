The '''Brynnaria system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square O-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Brynnarian Forest Salad]]|system=Brynnaria system}}</ref> of the [[Brynnarian Forest Salad]].<ref name="Origin">''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]''</ref>
