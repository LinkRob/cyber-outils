'''Christo''' was a [[Quarren]] [[Gender|male]] who served as a [[senator]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Loyal to the constituents who had appointed him to the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]], Christo placed their needs before those of all others during his career. When Senator [[Halle Burtoni]] proposed an [[Republic military enhancement bill|increase]] in [[clone trooper]] production, Christo initially considered voting for the bill, especially after several opponents to the measure were pressured into supporting it by [[bounty hunter]]s. However, after a meeting in his [[Christo's apartment|apartment]] and an impassioned senatorial address from Senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], he rethought his position.<ref name="Pursuit of Peace" />
