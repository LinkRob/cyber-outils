'''''An Ewok Adventure''''' is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]]/coloring book published by [[Golden Books]] in [[1997]]. It adapts the scene from [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Return of the Jedi'']] of the [[Endor strike team/Legends|Endor strike team's]] capture by the [[Ewok/Legends|Ewoks]] during the [[Battle of Endor/Legends|Battle of Endor]]. The book was penciled and inked by artist [[Guy Dorian]].

In [[2012]], the comic was reprinted in ''[[Star Wars Omnibus: Droids and Ewoks]]''.
