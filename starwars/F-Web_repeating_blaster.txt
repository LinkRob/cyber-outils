The '''F-Web repeating blaster''' was a type of [[repeating blaster]] produced by [[BlasTech Industries]]. It cost 8,500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]<ref name="Smugglers Guide" /> and was equipped with a small [[deflector shield generator]] which could deflect small arms fire, but required a third person to operate. It was the successor to the [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]]. It was not as efficient as the [[E-Web (15)]].<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" />
