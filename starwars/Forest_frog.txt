A '''forest frog''' was a type of frog found on the [[moon]] [[Endor]]. They had a silvery back, which allowed them to blend in with the shadowed forest.<ref name="Day" /> In [[4 ABY]]<ref name="Date">"[[One Normal Day]]" takes place concurrent to {{Film|VI}}, the events of which are dated to [[4 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> [[Wicket Wystri Warrick|Wicket Warrick]] caught two plump forest frogs.<ref name="Day" />
