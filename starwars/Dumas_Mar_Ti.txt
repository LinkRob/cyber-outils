'''Dumas Mar'Ti''' was a [[Kaleesh]] [[Gender|female]] who [[captain]]ed the [[starship]] ''[[Athos]]'' during the [[High Republic Era]]. She once served as a member of the [[Byne Guild]].

[[Standard week|One week]] after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]] of the [[Starlight Beacon]], Dumas Mar'Ti visited a searcher craft on the [[Beach|shoreline]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Eiram]]. She spotted [[Affie Hollow]], who had previously visited the craft with her [[pilot]], [[Leox Gyasi]]. As Affie recognized Mar'Ti, the latter interrupted her, expressing disappointment over Affie's continued reference to her [[Parent|mother]], [[Scover Byne]], despite her past actions that led to the guild leader's imprisonment. The conversation highlighted Mar'Ti's lingering resentment towards Affie and the challenges faced by guild members. [[Pikka Adren]] joined Affie, possibly sensing the tension. Despite her anger, Mar'Ti refrained from lashing out, while the group awaited their [[sensor wristband]]s. Later, in a [[cantina]] on [[Kennerla]], Mar'Ti confronted her feelings with Hollow, who urged her to direct her anger towards the true culprits—the [[Nihil]] and her mother. Hollow proposed a collaborative approach among guilds to combat the Nihil, emphasizing the potential of shared resources and intelligence. Mar'Ti expressed skepticism about trusting all guild members, yet after contemplating the plan, she agreed to consider it, provided Affie bought her a drink first.

==Biography==
===Byne Guild Captain===
Dumas Mar'Ti was a [[Kaleesh]] [[Gender|female]] [[captain]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. She was a member of the [[Byne Guild]] and captained her own [[starship]], the ''[[Athos]]''.<ref name="After the Fall" /> After [[Affie Hollow]] revealed her foster [[Parent|mother]] and guild leader [[Scover Byne]] was responsible for the misuse of indentures to [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="Into the Dark">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref> the Byne Guild was disbanded.<ref name="Out of the Shadows">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref> Mar'Ti was angry to have lost the entire guild just because of one person's crimes and blamed Hollow for ruining the lives of her and every other guild employee.<ref name="After the Fall" />

===Reuniting with Affie Hollow===
[[Standard week|One week]] after the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]]'s [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]], on the<ref name="After the Fall" /> [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Into the Dark" /> [[planet]] [[Eiram]], Mar'Ti visited one of the small searcher craft on one of the world's [[Beach|shorelines]]. In a push forward for new assignments, she noticed and abruptly turned toward Affie Hollow, whom she recognized<ref name="After the Fall" /> and peered carefully upon.<ref name="After the Fall German" /> Earlier, Affie and her [[pilot]] [[Leox Gyasi]] had traveled to Eiram and visited the craft. After a [[Standard minute|minute]], Affie recognized her face and recalled when she came aboard with her a few times with her mother, but Mar'Ti interrupted her before she could finish, expressing disappointment that Affie continued to refer to Byne as her mother despite her work in putting the guild leader [[Prison|behind bars]]. Hollow tried to explain herself, but Mar'Ti responded by recounting the hard times she and other guild employees had been through instead. [[Pikka Adren]] stepped up to Affie's side in their conversation at that precise moment, with it unclear if she did so because she had known a Kaleesh before or if it was simply because she had good instincts. Mar'Ti was caught between her rage and the opportunity to scream but eventually slumped. As if nothing had happened, they all waited up for their [[sensor wristband]]s.<ref name="After the Fall" />

===Residing on Kennerla and joining Hollow===
[[File:AffieHollow.jpg|thumb|right|230px|Dumas Mar'Ti's rage with Affie Hollow for imprisoning Scover Byne caused her to hold a grudge for months, and this impacted Hollow hard, keeping her up for the following few nights.]]
For [[Standard month|months]], the Kaleesh Captain held onto her grudge against Affie. While based on the planet [[Kennerla]], she visited the end of the bar in a grungy [[cantina]], where she sipped a smoking [[Color|purple]] drink. Mar'Ti was still angry with Hollow, who then found her and encouraged her to be as angry as she wanted, but be angry at the ones who genuinely messed up their lives&mdash;first her mother and then the [[Nihil]]. Hollow insisted that they could not defeat the Nihil alone but could as a group, pointing out that the Byne Guild did not need to exist for Scover Byne alone.<ref name="After the Fall" />

Explaining further, Hollow reasoned that each Guild ship could protect the others, trade shipments, and share intelligence with each other and the Republic. Told that, if they they were to reclaim even a third of the former Byne Guild ships, there would be a great deal of pressure put upon the Nihil and information for their allies in the Republic and the Jedi, Mar'Ti was unsure of what to say but warned not every guild member was someone she'd want to ally with without oversight. Assured that not everyone would join their cause, Mar'Ti thought it over for a long moment, just long enough to feel awkward, until shaking Affie's hand and displaying openness to the idea, only asking that she buy her a drink first.<ref name="After the Fall" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You've taken care of your mom, haven't you? I'm a reptile; I know cold-bloodedness when I see it.|Dumas Mar'Ti, to Affie Hollow|<ref name="After the Fall" />}}
Dumas Mar'Ti was<ref name="After the Fall" /> a tall<ref name="After the Fall German" /> Kaleesh female. She was enraged that Affie Hollow continued to refer to Scover Byne as her mother after she had imprisoned her. Mar'Ti believed that her [[sin]]s were between herself and her [[god]]s. As a [[reptile|reptilian]], she knew when someone was being cold-blooded. She did not want to associate with everyone in the Guild without some oversight. Upon hearing Affie Hollow call Scover Byne her mother despite all she had done, Mar'Ti folded her [[Limb|arms]] and looked down at Affie, with the Kaleesh's [[eye]]s seeming as cold as a [[Local day|night]] on [[Mygeeto]]. When Hollow thought of a word to describe the Nihil, she was unsure if the Kaleeshi had the specific orifice she settled on: "[[Fexsnatcher]]s".<ref name="After the Fall" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
Dumas Mar'Ti first appeared in the short story [[After the Fall]], written by [[Claudia Gray]] as part of the anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published on [[July 27]], [[2021]], as part of the multimedia project ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]''.<ref name="After the Fall" />
