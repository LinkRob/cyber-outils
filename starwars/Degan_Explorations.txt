'''Degan Explorations''' was a corporation of parent pharmaceutical company [[Chiewab Amalgamated Pharmaceuticals]] that served as a [[scout]]ing and exploration organization. During the [[Clone Wars]], Degan Explorations scouted the [[Iakar system]] and uncovered rare chemical compounds that would be suitable for [[bacta]] replication and other medicinal uses on [[Iakar]]. Chiewab established a [[Chiewab research installation|research facility]], yet Chiewab's competitors, [[Fabreth Medical]] and [[Merisee Prime (company)|Merisee Prime]] were able to intercept transmissions and set up their own operations.<ref name="Dawn" />

The [[Iakaru]] were forced to defend their homes and [[forest]]s, but corporate security forces [[Massacres on Iakar|massacred]] resistance and were victorious.<ref name="Dawn" />
