'''Arieli''' was an [[Expansion Region]] [[planet]] under the [[Galactic Empire]]'s rule. After a local insurgency sabotaged the Imperial [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]] [[Peacemaker (Arquitens-class light cruiser)|''Peacemaker'']] there, the [[Light cruiser|cruiser]] began firing on the world's municipal buildings. The [[light freighter]] ''[[Mighty Oak Apocalypse]]'' did a supply run to the planet during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Description==
Arieli was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Rise" /> located in an [[Arieli system|eponymous system]] within an intersecting portion of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It lay inside the [[Authala sector]] at the coordinates P-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" />

==History==
{{Quote|On Arieli, the refueling equipment for an Arquitens-class light cruiser was sabotaged by a local insurgency group, meaning the vessel could not leave the planet’s atmosphere. While awaiting new equipment, the captain ordered his gunners to begin targeting municipal buildings such as schools, libraries, and hospitals.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Arieli was under Imperial rule. A local insurgency operated on the world and sabotaged the refueling equipment aboard the Imperial [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]] [[Peacemaker (Arquitens-class light cruiser)|''Peacemaker'']], making it unable to leave the [[atmosphere]]. While the [[Light cruiser|cruiser]] waited for new equipment, the [[Unidentified Imperial captain (Peacemaker)|captain]] ordered his [[gunner]]s to start targeting municipal buildings like [[Library|libraries]], [[medcenter]]s, and schools. The Imperial reinforcements that subsequently arrived joined in on this.<ref name="Rise" />

[[File:MOA.png|thumb|left|300px|The ''Might Oak Apocolypse'' (pictured) did a supply run to Arieli]]
Between [[0 ABY]] and [[1 ABY]],<ref name="0-1 ABY">''[[Lost Stars]]'' places [[Thane Kyrell]]'s visit to Arieli between his [[Desertion|defection]] from the [[Galactic Empire]] and him meeting [[Wedge Antilles]]. ''[[Star Wars Super Graphic: A Visual Guide to a Galaxy Far, Far Away]]'' dates those events to zero and one [[Standard year|years]] after the events of {{Film|IV}} respectively, which correspond to [[0 ABY]] and [[1 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], the [[light freighter]] ''[[Mighty Oak Apocalypse]]'', or ''Moa'', made a relief supply run to Arieli. The ''Moa''{{'s}} [[Pilot|copilot]] and [[navigator]], ex-[[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]] [[Thane Kyrell]], took part in the delivery. The ''Moa'' later brought medical supplies to the planet [[Oulanne]]<ref name="Lost Stars" /> in 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="0-1 ABY" /> where Kyrell met the [[Rebel pilot|Alliance pilot]] [[Wedge Antilles]]. When asked by Antilles about where else he had done relief runs, Kyrell recalled the missions to Arieli and a few other victims of the Empire's war crimes like [[Dinwa Prime]], [[Ivera X]], and [[Zeitooine]]. Antilles, having visited some of those locations himself, went on to recruit Kyrell into the Alliance.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

The events at Arieli were logged in an [[After-action report|After Action Report]] for the ''Peacemaker'', which found itself in the "Imperial Navy" section of the [[Hall of Imperial Register|Imperial Archives]]. After reading the file, the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] summarized the events at Arieli in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[35 ABY]]<ref name="35 ABY">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''. In the book, he remarked that when an enemy eluded them, the Empire would mercilessly target ones they could reach instead.<ref name="Rise" />
