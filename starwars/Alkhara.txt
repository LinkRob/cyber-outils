'''Alkhara''' was a fearless bandit who, at some point in history, appropriated the [[B'omarr Order|B'omarr]] [[Boba Fett's Palace|monastery]] of [[Tatooine]]'s [[Northern Dune Sea]] for his own use. He hired [[Tusken Raider]]s to murder members of the local police<ref name="CL" /> [[garrison]]. He then betrayed the Tuskens by having them [[Death penalty|executed]].<ref name="217 POV">{{InsiderCite|217|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> This began a long blood feud between colonists and sand people. During his 34-[[Standard year|year]]-long occupation of the monastery, Alkhara cut a road from the citadel to the [[Western Dune Sea]] and [[Great Pit of Carkoon]], and also added battlements, dungeons, and a [[Alkhara's spire|nine-story tower]] to the building. Eventually, the bandit was driven off Tatooine by the [[Hutt]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], who turned the monastery hideout into his personal palace.<ref name="CL" />
