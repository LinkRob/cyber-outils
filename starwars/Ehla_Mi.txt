''' Ehla Mi''' was a [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Rutian/Legends|Rutian]] [[Twi'lek/Legends|Twi'lek]] [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]]. She was stationed on [[Quesh]] during the [[Cold War (Galactic Republic–Sith Empire)|Cold War]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|reconstituted Sith Empire]]. There she taught Republic citizens how to create artifacts and relics.<ref name="TOR" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ehla Mi appears only in the game files for ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' as her character was cut from the game.
