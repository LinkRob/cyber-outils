The '''Arts District''' was a location on [[Coruscant]]. [[Imperial Intelligence]] operative [[Haki Zeophrine]] at one point<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> in [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|11}}</ref> planned to see a show in the Arts District, but eventually decided to follow [[Bail Prestor Organa]] instead.<ref name="MoF" />

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Arts District was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" />
