'''Breha''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] from the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Aquilae (The Star Wars)|Aquilae]]. She was the [[Marriage/Legends|wife]] of [[King/Legends|King]] [[Kayos]] and bore the title of [[Queen Mother (The Star Wars)|Queen Mother]]. She bore three children: [[Leia (The Star Wars)|Leia]], [[Biggs (The Star Wars)|Biggs]] and [[Windy (Aquilae)|Windy]].<ref name="TSW1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Her name was eventually used for the name of Leia's adoptive mother [[Breha Organa/Legends|Queen Breha Organa]] in ''Revenge Of The Sith''.
