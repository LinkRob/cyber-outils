The '''FMR-385 cargo speeder''', also called an ''' Imperial transport truck''' was a type of [[Cargo speeder|speeder]] [[speeder truck|truck]] used in the [[mining]] operations of the [[Galactic Empire]] on the [[planet]] [[Mapuzo]]. The vehicle could also serve as a [[troop transport]] for local [[stormtrooper]]s. The [[Condluran]] named [[Freck]] owned an [[Freck's truck|FMR-385]] that he used to ferry [[stormtrooper]]s between various locations.<ref name="SWEDK" />
