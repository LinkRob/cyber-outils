'''Aeos Prime''', also simply known as '''Aeos''', was an [[ocean]] [[planet]] in the [[Aeos system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], [[Homeworld|home]] to the aquatic [[Aeosian]] [[species]]. A group of Aeosians allowed the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] to construct an [[Aeos Prime rebel outpost|outpost]] on the planet, which was later [[Attack on Aeos Prime|attacked]] by the [[First Order]] during its [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the Resistance.

Some [[time]] after the attack, the [[space station]] ''[[Colossus]]'' attempted to find refuge on Aeos Prime, but a tribe of Aeosians initially did not welcome them due to various misunderstandings. The Aeosians eventually allowed the ''Colossus'' and its inhabitants to stay on the planet after they discovered that the ''Colossus'' was not intentionally hostile, and shortly after a mutually beneficial agreement was made between the Aosians and the ''Colossus.'' Later they were discovered by the First Order, who returned to Aeos to attack and/or capture the ''Colossus''. With the help of the Aeosians, the space station managed to [[Battle of Aeos Prime|escape]] into [[hyperspace]]. The First Order later initiated orbital bombardment on an area of the planet in retaliation for aiding the ''Colossus''.

==Description==
Located in the [[Aeos system]]<ref name="No Place Safe" /> of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="The New World" /> Aeos Prime,<ref name="No Place Safe" /> or simply Aeos, was a terrestrial [[planet]] covered in vast [[ocean]]s, surrounded by a [[planetary ring]]. The ocean surface of Aeos was broken up by small islands, and a number of rocky [[cave]]rns and tunnels were located beneath the surface.<ref name="The New World" />

Aeos Prime was [[Homeworld|home]] to the [[krakavora]],<ref name="The New World" /> a [[species]] of aquatic creature that could fly using webbed wings, which the Aeosians often used as transportation.<ref name="Krakavora">{{Databank|krakavora|Krakavora}}</ref> The [[atmosphere]] of Aeos Prime was breathable to a number of [[species]], including the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Aeosian]]s and [[human]]s.<ref name="The New World" />

==History==
===Refuge===
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Aeosians allowed the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] to operate a [[Aeos Prime rebel outpost|rebel outpost]] on Aeos Prime. By the [[New Republic Era|time]] of the [[New Republic]], the outpost had been abandoned. During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]],<ref name="The New World" /> the First Order learned of the old rebel outpost on Aeos Prime. Wishing to destroy any old rebel bases to prevent the Resistance from making use of them,<ref name="Rebellion Outpost">{{Databank|rebellion-outpost-on-aeos|Rebellion Outpost on Aeos}}</ref> the First Order [[Attack on Aeos Prime|attacked]] Aeos Prime, destroying an [[Unidentified Aeosian village|Aeosian village]].<ref name="The New World" />

[[File:ColossusAeos-TheNewWorld.jpg|thumb|left|270px|The ''Colossus'' on Aeos Prime.]]
Seeking a place to dock and refuge from the First Order, the [[space station]] ''[[Colossus]]'', [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Captain]] [[Imanuel Doza]], arrived above Aeos Prime. Doza, unaware that the planet was inhabited, chose to claim Aeos as the new home of the ''Colossus''. While on a reconnaissance mission, [[Kazuda Xiono]] and [[Griff Halloran]], members of the ''Colossus''{{'s}} [[Ace Squadron]], discovered the devastated village, and Xiono learned that the Rebellion had once resided on the planet. However, the two Aces were captured by the Aeosians, who believed that they were behind the attack on the village, launching an attack on the ''Colossus''.<ref name="The New World" />

===Under attack===
The [[Unidentified Aeosian Queen|Aeosian queen]] wanted Xiono and Halloran to be fed to the krakavora as punishment, but decided to let them live after Xiono used medicine to heal an injured Aeosian. The [[queen]] visited the ''Colossus'', calling off the attack and choosing to help the citizens of the station in return for medicine, allowing the ''Colossus'' to stay on Aeos Prime.<ref name="The New World" />

However, the First Order were alerted to the presence of the ''Colossus'' when Xiono, attempting to return to his Resistance comrades, was spotted by a First Order [[Spider probe droid|probe droid]]. The First Order, who had been [[Hunt for the Colossus|hunting]] the station for some [[time]], sent a [[Star Destroyer]], the ''[[Thunderer]]'', to [[Battle of Aeos Prime|attack]] the ''Colossus'', launching a number of [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] and [[TIE/se Bomber]]s. The Aeosian queen chose to remain behind and help the ''Colossus'' escape, and the Aeosians helped to defend the station from the [[TIE series|TIEs]]. The ''Colossus'' managed to clear the atmosphere of Aeos Prime, and jumped into [[hyperspace]], escaping the First Order while the Aeosians retreated beneath the ocean.<ref name="No Place Safe" />

[[File:AeosBombardment-TheEscape.jpg|thumb|right|270px|The First Order bombard Aeos Prime.]]
While the ''Colossus'' managed to escape unharmed,<ref name="No Place Safe" /> the First Order would not let the Aeosians go unpunished for harboring the Resistance on their world. Some time after the ''Colossus''{{'s}} escape from Aeos Prime, the First Order dispatched a number of [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyers]] to the Aeos system. As the Aeosians looked up at the incoming [[fleet]], the First Order [[Suppression of the Aeos system|opened fire]] on the village that helped the Resistance, [[Orbital bombardment|bombarding]] the planet from [[orbit]]. The orbital bombardment destroyed the village and the surrounding land, leaving devastation visible from [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="The Escape">{{Resistance|The Escape (episode)}}</ref>

==Inhabitants==
Aeos Prime was home to the Aeosians, a [[Sentience|sentient]] aquatic species. For some time, the planet also harbored members of the Rebel Alliance, and [[Standard year|years]] later, the residents of the ''Colossus'' space station.<ref name="The New World" />

==Locations==
The Aeosians resided in tunnels beneath the surface of Aeos Prime. An Aeosian village was located on the planet, and fell victim to a First Order attack. A former outpost of the Rebellion was also located on Aeos Prime. The ''Colossus'' space station docked on Aeos for some time,<ref name="The New World" /> but was later forced to escape the planet.<ref name="No Place Safe" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:AeosPrime-ConceptArt.jpg|thumb|left|230px|Concept art of Aeos Prime.]]
Aeos Prime first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' episode "[[The New World]],"<ref name="The New World" /> which aired on [[January 12]], [[2020]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes/the-new-world-no-place-safe|text=The New World/No Place Safe}}</ref> While "The New World" gave the planet's name as Aeos,<ref name="The New World" /> it was identified as Aeos Prime in the following episode, "[[No Place Safe]],"<ref name="No Place Safe" /> which aired on the same day.<ref name="Disney Press" />

Concept art of Aeos Prime's surface was created by [[Jason Pichon]], while [[Jim Moore]] created concept art of the planet as seen from space.<ref name="Concept Art">{{SW|url=the-new-world-concept-art-gallery|text="The New World" Concept Art Gallery}}</ref> To illustrate the Aeosians' balance of technology and nature, the design team added veins of [[gold]] in the sea rock of the planet's tunnels, visually tying into the gold metal of the Aeosians' [[armor]] and weapons.<ref name="The New World Bucket's List">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-new-world-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 7 Fun Facts from "The New World" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref>

In earlier drafts of "[[The Escape (episode)|The Escape]]," the First Order were to attack [[Dendro IV]] in the episode's opening sequence rather than Aeos Prime. The location of the First Order's attack was changed to Aeos after the development of the two episodes it featured in, to connect the series finale to earlier episodes and better give a face to the consequences of the First Order's actions.<ref name="Bucket's List">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-escape-part-1-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 5 Fun Facts from "The Escape - Part 1" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref> However, the episode's [[StarWars.com]] concept art gallery included an image captioned as art of Aeos following the attack, but its appearance differs to that of the ocean planet, and is labeled "planetballDendroDstrd."<ref name="Concept Art 2">{{SW|url=the-escape-part-2-concept-art-gallery|text="The Escape: Part 2" Concept Art Gallery}}</ref>
