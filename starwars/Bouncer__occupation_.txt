A '''bouncer''' was an individual who provided security outside certain establishments. By [[34 ABY]], the [[Casino Security (Canto Casino)|Casino Security]] of the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|Canto Casino]] in the coastal [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on [[Cantonica]] was composed of plainclothed bouncers lead by the head of security [[Pemmin Brunce]].<ref name="TLJ: VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
