An '''amplifying chamber''' was an attachment for [[blaster]]s that increased the size of a weapon's chamber, allowing the weapon to emit a more powerful blast. Amplifying chambers often increased the wear of a blaster and could lead to component breakdown. Models of amplifying chamber included the [[Rothana Heavy Engineering|Rothana]] [[RX-99 AmpChamber]] and the [[BlasTech Industries]] [[A-13 Acceleration Chamber]].<ref name="COTR" />
