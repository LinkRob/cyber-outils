The '''Alderaan military''', commonly known as the '''Alderaanian military''', was the armed forces on the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] took most of their inspirations from [[Alderaanian]] military traditions. These included [[Rebel command insignia|rank insignia]] and uniform from [[Alderaanian consular security]].<ref name="UVG" />
