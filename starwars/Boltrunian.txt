'''Boltrunians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] which existed in [[the galaxy]]. Some Boltrunians were present in the [[medcenter]] of the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]] when [[Administrator]] [[Velko Jahen]] arrived and was escorted through the facility by [[Okana]].<ref name="First Duty" /> They originated from the [[Boltrunia system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Planetnamia" />
