'''Ebor''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual<ref name="HFZ" /> of the [[Desilijic|Tiure family]], being the [[Reproduction|son]] of the [[Hutt]]s [[Mama]] and [[Papa]]<ref name="Super Graphic" /> and the elder brother of Hutt<ref name="HC 80">{{HelmetCollectionCite|80|Databank A-Z}}</ref> [[crime lord]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars New">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure]]. Ebor was more well-mannered than his sibling, which Mama appreciated.<ref name="HFZ" /> Ebor also had [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure's father|another brother]], who was the [[Parent|father]] of Hutt<ref name="Super Graphic" /> crime [[lord]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars New" /> [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]].<ref name="Super Graphic" /> Of the children, Ebor was Mama's favorite.<ref name="HC 80" />

Ebor [[Death|died]] while Ziro was still growing up. Although he would be as avaricious as any other Hutt who wanted to work in crime, Ziro&mdash;whose whole life was defined by paranoid jealously of other Hutts and by whatever he did not own&mdash;was forced to work in the shadow of all his brother had accomplished.<ref name="HC 80" /> In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> as Ziro was [[Showdown on Teth|hunted]] by both the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Grand Hutt Council|Hutt Council]], Ziro arrived at his mother's [[Mama the Hutt's house|house]] on the [[planet]] [[Nal Hutta]] at [[Diet|dinnertime]], during which Mama recalled Ebor's manners and hoped that his [[soul]] was at rest.<ref name="HFZ" />
