'''Alden Ehrenreich''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] actor who played [[Han Solo]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Cast">{{WebCite|url=https://deadline.com/2016/05/star-wars-han-solo-movie-alden-ehrenreich-frontrunner-cast-1201737425|text='Star Wars' Han Solo Film: Alden Ehrenreich Lands The Lead In Spinoff|author=Anita Busch|date=2016-05-05|work=[http://deadline.com Deadline]|archivedate=20200803112817}}</ref> He is signed to play the character in two more films.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.esquire.com/entertainment/movies/a19991800/solo-a-star-wars-story-han-solo-movie-sequels-alden-ehrenreich|text=Alden Ehrenreich Will Return as Han Solo After ''Solo''|author=Miller, Matt|date=2018-04-24|work=[https://www.esquire.com esquire.com]|archivedate=20200803121705}}</ref>

In an interview with The Big Event podcast, Alden Ehrenreich talked about using [[Wookieepedia]] to research for his upcoming role as [[Han Solo]], taking note of several articles including [[Jizz]], [[Bureau of Ships and Services]], and the instruction manual for the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', among others.<ref name="Alden Interview">{{WebCite|url=http://thebigevent.sfchronicle.libsynpro.com/alden-ehrenreich-interview|text=The Big Event:Alden Ehrenreich interview|format=[[Wikipedia:Podcast|Podcast]]|work=[http://thebigevent.sfchronicle.libsynpro.com The Big Event]|archivedate=20191219075326}}</ref>
