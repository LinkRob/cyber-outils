The '''D-29 repulsor rifle''' was a type of repulsor rifle manufactured by [[SakTek]]. Able to knock large targets across a room or ocasionally simuluate high- and low-gravity environments, the large repulsor weapon was tuned to narrow, longer-range areas of effect to maximize effectiveness while reducing power drain, rarely utilized by bounty hunters, law enforcement, and pranksters.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

Used on traditional hunts, the D-29 was also exported as a crowd control and riot-suppression weapon. It was medium-ranged and cost 1,550 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
