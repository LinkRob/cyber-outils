The '''DL-19C blaster pistol''' was a model of [[blaster pistol]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]]. Equipped with a [[stun]] setting, it cost 450 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. Unlike its predecessor, the extremely popular and successful [[DL-18 blaster pistol]], the DL-19C was designed as a specialized and highly adjustable weapon aimed at a much more niche market.<ref name="SM" /> The design team chose to elevate customizability above all else, including the power, accuracy, durability, and capacity that most weapon [[engineer]]s strove for.<ref name="G&G">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref>

The weapon was designed for weapon hobbyists, perfectionists and other individuals who were interested in being able to adjust every aspect of a weapon to their personal tastes. Adjustable aspects included the sensitivity of the trigger, the energy outflow from [[Energy cell|charge pack]], and the shape of the [[barrel]].
Produced in limited numbers, discerning fire-arm enthusiasts often used ownership of a DL-19Cs as a signal to recognize others of their ilk.<ref name="SM" />

==Behind the scenes==
The DL-19C blaster pistol was first mentioned in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Special Modifications]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="SM" />
