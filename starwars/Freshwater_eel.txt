A type of '''freshwater eel''' lived in several waterways on the [[planet]] [[Siline]]. These [[eel]]s were eaten by [[Silinese]] due to their appetizing flavor. They were known for their hooked, curling [[tooth|teeth]] which they used to filter tiny [[crab]]s from the water for [[diet|food]]. Though they were not predatory towards Silinese, they were known to bite hands which got too close to their lairs.<ref name="TCC" /> 

During the [[survival level|survival portion]] of the [[Crowning Convocation]] [[Crowning Convocation (Jecki and Yord)|held]]<ref name="TCC" /> in [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]] strategized her approach to finding [[foodstuff|food]]. Though her primary strategy was to find [[uavin tree]]s for their [[uavin fruit|fruit]], her backup plan was to [[fishing|fish]] for the eels in a brook she found.<ref name="TCC" />
