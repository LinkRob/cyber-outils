'''Blast Points''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]''&ndash;centric podcast hosted by Jason Gibner and Gabe Bott that first debuted in [[January]] [[2016]]. The podcast seeks to discuss obscure aspects of the onscreen ''Star Wars'' universe, and its behind-the-scenes history in a unique way, aiming to be both humorous and analytical. As of [[2026]], Blast Points has released over 470 episodes. Several notable ''Star Wars'' creators interviewed by the podcast include<ref name="Email" /> [[Tony Gilroy]], [[Alan Tudyk]],  [[Matthew Wood]], [[David W. Collins]], [[Tony McVey]], [[Kristin Baver]], [[Phil Szostak]], [[David Acord]], [[Patricia Rose Duignan]], [[Stephen J. Sansweet]], [[Kirk Thatcher]], [[Margit Pfeiffer]], and [[Jonathan W. Rinzler]].<ref name="Email" /><ref name="Episode Count" />

The podcast attended [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|Celebration Anaheim]]<ref name="Email" />&mdash;held between [[May 26]]th and [[May 29]]th, 2022.<ref name="Preponement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-anaheim-2022-dates-changing|text=''Star Wars'' Celebration Anaheim 2022 Dates Changing}}</ref> There, they co-hosted the Creatures of ''Return of the Jedi'' panel with [[Tom Spina]] on the Twin Suns stage<ref name="Email" /> on [[May 28]]th.<ref name="FanthaTracks">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fanthatracks.com/news/conventions-events/celebration-anaheim-2022-check-out-your-podcast-stage-shows|text=Celebration Anaheim 2022: Check out your Podcast Stage shows|work=[https://fanthatracks.com Fantha Tracks]|archivedate=20220503212435}}</ref> Blast Points also hosted fan panels on ''[[George Lucas' Super Live Adventure]]'' and on the HoloNet News Stage at [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan]],<ref name="Email" /> which was held [[April 18]]&mdash;[[April 20|20]], 2025.<ref name="Twitter Announcement">{{Twitter|starwars|status/1645449155752480768|[[Star Wars]]|quote=see you in Japan #StarWarsCelebration|archivedate=20230410154801}}</ref>
