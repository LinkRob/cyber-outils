'''BSO 401.72''' was the local radio station for [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. It broadcast droid battles from nearby [[Galma]], as well as [[dejarik]] tournaments, [[Podracing|podraces]], and popular [[music]] from around [[the galaxy]].<ref name="BSO radio">{{GEAttr|BSO 401.72}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
BSO 401.72 was introduced as a background audio loop used in various locations throughout [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="BSO radio" /> a theme park land that first opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=2019/05/foodie-guide-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Foodie Guide to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref>
