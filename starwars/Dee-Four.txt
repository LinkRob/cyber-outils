'''Dee-Four''' was a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[protocol droid]]. Between [[2 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]], it traveled to the [[asteroid]] outpost "[[Z-LOQ]]" with the rebel [[Fulcrum|spy]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]] to receive information about [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] technology from [[Grand-engineer]] [[Lony Coleema]]. After Coleema transferred the information to Dee-Four, the [[engineer]] was kidnapped by the [[IG-88 assassin droid]] [[IG-88B|IG-88]]. They were later rescued by Kallus and Dee-Four, who brought Coleema onboard the [[starship]] ''[[Glimmer of Hope]]''.

==Biography==
{{Quote|I am receiving the data, mistress, er, sister Coleema. It is like a shooting voice in my head.|Dee-Four to Coleema|<ref name="A Rebel Bounty" />}}
[[File:GlimmerOfHope-ARebelBounty.png|thumb|right|250px|Dee-Four (left) and Alexsandr Kallus (middle) saved Lony Coleema (right) on the ''Glimmer of Hope''.]]
Dee-Four was a [[protocol droid]] who served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="A Rebel Bounty" /> Between [[2 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">"[[A Rebel Bounty]]" takes place after [[Alexsandr Kallus]]'s betrayal in "[[Zero Hour]]," which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[2 BBY]], but before [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba's]] death, which ''Timelines'' dates to [[4 ABY]]. Therefore, "A Rebel Bounty" takes place between 2 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and 4 ABY.</ref> Dee-Four traveled to the [[asteroid]] outpost "[[Z-LOQ]]" with<ref name="A Rebel Bounty" /> [[Alexsandr Kallus]],<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|alexsandr-kallus|Alexsandr Kallus}}</ref> a rebel [[Fulcrum|spy]], to meet [[Grand-engineer]] [[Lony Coleema]] and receive information about the newest [[Galactic Empire]] technology. After Dee-Four referred to Coleema as "mistress," the [[engineer]] corrected the [[droid]], saying they preferred the term "sister." Coleema claimed they were in possession of information regarding new Imperial long-range detection technology and improved attack capabilities.<ref name="A Rebel Bounty" />

The grand-engineer shared it directly into the droid's memory systems, and Dee-Four said that receiving the data was like a shooting voice in its head. After completing the transmission, Kallus and the droid left the outpost, leaving a [[Subspace transceiver|subspace transponder]] to Coleema in case they needed to contact the Alliance in the future. After Dee-Four and Kallus left, Coleema was kidnapped by the [[bounty hunter]] [[IG-88 assassin droid]] [[IG-88B|IG-88]]. The [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] brought them to a [[Unidentified planet (Holowan droid foundry)|planet]] where the [[Holowan droid foundry]] was located to access their [[brain]], but the grand-engineer managed to escape. Sometime later, Kallus and Dee-Four found Coleema on the planet and brought them onboard the [[starship]] ''[[Glimmer of Hope]]'' after the reprogrammed droid [[Are Zero Are Zero]] activated the subspace transponder. On the ship, Dee-Four welcomed the engineer and told them where they had found them.<ref name="A Rebel Bounty" />

==Characteristics==
Dee-Four had [[Color|gray]] plating and yellow [[sensor]]s that turned pink while receiving data from Coleema.<ref name="A Rebel Bounty" />

==Equipment==
