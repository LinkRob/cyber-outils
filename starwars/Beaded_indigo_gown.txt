[[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s '''beaded indigo gown'''<ref name="SWTVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> was part of her extensive [[Padmé Amidala's wardrobe|wardrobe]] when she served in the [[Galactic Senate]] as the representative of the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] people and their [[homeworld]] of [[Naboo]]. It consisted of a patterned [[Clothing|overdress]] over an underdress with a beaded bodice. It had shades of [[Color|indigo]], blue, and purple;<ref name="Episode II" /> the sober colors blended with ornate details and rich fabric.<ref name="TCVD NE">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> after the [[bounty hunter]] [[Zam Wesell]]'s first [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|attempt to assassinate]] her, [[Senator]] Amidala wore the gown to visit [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] office.<ref name="Episode II" /> Her attire was accessorized with a gold-toned choker necklace that was a gift from the [[Naboo Royal Advisory Council]] and a severe [[hair]]style that conformed to diplomatic etiquette.<ref name="TCVD NE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Padmé Amidala's beaded indigo gown was created for the [[2002]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="Episode II" />
