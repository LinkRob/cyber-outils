"'''Age of ''Empire'''''" is an interview with [[Edward Summer]] that was written by [[Jonathan W. Rinzler]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 141|''Star Wars Insider'' 141]] on [[April 30]], [[2013]]. It is the third part of a series, following "[[A Flash of Inspiration]]" and "[[Summer Blockbuster]]" in issues 139 and 140.
