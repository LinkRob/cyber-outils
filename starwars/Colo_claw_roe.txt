'''Colo claw roe''' were the [[egg]]s of [[colo claw fish]] that could be served as a [[Foodstuff|delicacy]]. Small eggs in [[Color|orange]] were served alone as '''colo claw caviar''' and also alongside a large yellow egg in a bowl. The roe also existed in green.

==Description==
{{Quote|If you have some extra credits, why not try some colo claw caviar.|L-X1|<ref name="SWH" />|audio=LX1ColoClawCaviar-SWH.wav}}
Colo claw roe<ref name="JS" /> were a [[Foodstuff|delicacy]] that could be served as colo claw caviar.<ref name="SWH">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> The [[egg]]s of the [[colo claw fish]]<ref name="JS" /> had multiple [[color]]s, including green and orange.<ref name="Solo" />

==History==
[[File:ColoClawCaviar-SWH.png|250px|thumb|right|An advertisement for the colo claw caviar was displayed at the Grand Arena.]]
In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Solo Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[10 BBY]].</ref> the leader of the crime syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]] [[Dryden Vos]] offered the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] and Vos' own [[lieutenant]] [[Qi'ra]] colo craw roe, following the pair's return to Vos' [[flagship]], the ''[[First Light]]'', after a [[Raid on Kessel|raid]] they pulled on the [[planet]] [[Kessel]].<ref name="Solo" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Survivor Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> at the [[cantina]] [[Pyloon's Saloon]], the [[Latero]] [[pilot]] [[Greez Dritus]] offered [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] a handful of dishes, including the colo claw roe, [[Nuna drumstick|deep-fried nuna legs]], and [[ormacheck]], to be prepared for a celebration once they [[Quest for Tanalorr|reached]] the planet [[Tanalorr]]. Kestis doubted the Latero's ability to prepare the dishes, but Drituz remained confident.<ref name="JS" />
{{EndGame}}

In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Hunters Date">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> during the competitive sporting show [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]], the announcer [[droid]] [[L-X1]] read out a sponsor for the colo claw caviar throughout various matches. Following the construction of the [[Coruscant Underworld (battlefield)|Coruscant Underworld]] battlefield at the [[Grand Arena]] on the planet [[Vespaara]], an advertisement for the colo claw caviar was displayed within.<ref name="SWH" />

==Behind the scenes==
Colo claw roe first appeared in the [[May]] [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', though it was unnamed.<ref name="Solo" /> A [[StarWars.com]] blog post by Ashley Barry-Biancuzzo specified in [[June]] that the "egg-like treat" from ''Solo'' was made from colo claw fish and "like the caviar of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' universe." It was also compared to the [[Wikipedia:Chinese cuisine|Chinese]] delicacy known as the "[[Wikipedia:Century egg|century egg]]" or "thousand-year-old egg"<ref name="Blog">{{SW|url=news/9-star-wars-foods|text=9 ''Star Wars'' Foods That Make Our Stomachs Rumble}}</ref>&mdash;named 皮蛋 in [[Wikipedia:Chinese characters|Chinese]], it is a dark, jelly-like savoury [[Wikipedia:Duck|duck]], [[Wikipedia:Quail eggs|quail]], or [[Wikipedia:Chicken|chicken]] egg preserved over several weeks or months in a traditional method dating from the [[Wikipedia:Ming dynasty|Ming dynasty]].<ref name="HK">{{WebCite|url=https://www.bbc.com/travel/article/20151208-the-rotten-egg-people-love-to-eat|text=The 500-year-old snack|author=Kate Springer and Chan Sin Yan|date=2015-12-18|work=[[Wikipedia:BBC Online|BBC Travel]]|archivedate=20250319001308}}</ref> The ''Star Wars'' food was later named in [[Star Wars video games|video game]]s as "colo claw roe" in [[2023]]'s ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''<ref name="JS" /> and "colo claw caviar" in [[2024]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''.<ref name="SWH" />
