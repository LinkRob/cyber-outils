'''Bleena''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Slicer/Legends|slicer]] who lived on the planet [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]. After the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]], he worked for [[Lady/Legends|Lady]] [[Valarian/Legends|Valarian's]] lieutenant [[Kavas Urdano]].<ref name="SWG:SK" /> In [[1 ABY/Legends|1 ABY]],<ref name="SWG_timeline">{{SWG_timeline}}</ref> Urdano tasked a spacer with recovering Bleena's tool set that was stolen from him by a band of [[Jawa/Legends|Jawa]]. Kavas Urdano later used Bleena to slice the bank account of [[Ind]], another lieutenant of Lady Valarian and the main rival of Kavas.<ref name="SWG:SK" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bleena was a cut [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]] (NPC) in the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']], a [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online roleplaying game]] developed by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment and published by [[LucasArts]],<ref name="SWG:SK" /> prior to its closure on [[December 15]], [[2011]].<ref name="Shutdown">{{SonyCite|url=playerinfo|text=IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT ''STAR WARS'' GALAXIES™}}</ref>

Bleena was intended to be part of the cancelled [https://swglegends.com/forums/showthread.php?55817-*Friday-Feature-Valarian-Themepark-Walkthrough!*Valarian Themepark]. This questline was initially planned to be introduced in the game with the "[[W:c:swg:Publish 23 Notes for 31 August 2005|Publish 23]]" to replace the original "[[W:c:swg:Valarian Theme Park (pre-NGE)|Valarian Quest Series]]."<ref name="SonyCite-Valarian Quest Update">{{SonyCite|url=content.jsp?page=Current%20Publish|text=Valarian Quest Update}}</ref> Although the old "Valarian Quest Series" was deactivated with the release of Publish 23 on [[August 31]], [[2005]],<ref>{{SonyForumsCite|url=swg/board/message?board.id=swgpatchnotes&message.id=770|text=Update Notes: Publish 23}}</ref> the new Themepark was never activated.<ref name="SonyCite-In Development">{{SonyCite|url=content.jsp?page=In%20Development|text=In Development}}</ref> Several NPCs of the Valarian Theme Park were implemented into the game but Bleena was not known to have appeared on the live servers.<ref name="SWG:P25">{{SWGcite|exp=NGE|type=other|Publish 25}}</ref> Unused game files pertaining to this questline indicated that Bleena was intended to be part of a new quest named "Thrust."<ref>tatooine_knives_thrust.stf</ref>
