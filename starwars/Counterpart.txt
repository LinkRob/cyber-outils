A '''counterpart''' was one of a group of two or more [[droid]]s who had worked together for a long period of time. [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] were a famous pair of droid counterparts.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope" /> [[0-0-0]] and [[BT-1]] were another pair of droid counterparts.<ref name="DD Squad 3">[[Dark Droids: D-Squad 3|''Dark Droids: D-Squad'' 3]]</ref>
