'''Fremmet''' was a [[human]] [[commander]] in the [[Neo-Imperial forces|Neo-Imperial]] [[Barro's Imperial remnant|forces]] of the [[warlord]] [[Barro]]. During or after [[11 ABY]], the [[mercenary]] [[Din Djarin]] and his [[Gender|son]] [[Din Grogu]] hunted down Barro for the [[New Republic]], forcing the warlord to flee in a trio of [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)|All Terrain Armored Transports]]. Fremmet [[Vehicle commander|commanded]] one of the [[walker]]s as they moved along a narrow [[mountain]] pass on the [[Unidentified ice planet (Outer Rim)|ice planet]] where Barro was based, but Djarin caught up to them. The mercenary boarded Fremmet's walker and dispatched all the [[cold weather assault trooper]]s on board before breaching the [[cockpit]] hatch and causing an explosion that [[Death|killed]] Fremmet.

==Biography==
===Incoming Mando===
{{Quote|We missed him! He's under us.''"<br />"''We have him. Fire.|Barro and Fremmet|audio=WeHaveHim-Fremmet-MandoAndGrogu.flac|<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />}}
Fremmet was a [[human]] who served as a [[commander]] in a [[Barro's Imperial remnant|group]] of [[Neo-Imperial forces]] forces led by [[warlord]] [[Barro]] during the [[New Republic Era|reign]] of the [[New Republic]]. Barro was based out of an [[Unidentified Imperial base (Outer Rim)|Imperial base]] on an [[Unidentified ice planet (Outer Rim)|ice planet]] from which he claimed tribute from the local [[prefect]]s.<ref name="Mando&Grogu" /> During or after [[11 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate"/> the [[Mandalorian]] [[mercenary]] [[Din Djarin]] and his adoptive [[Gender|son]], [[Din Grogu]], [[Attack on Barro's Imperial remnant|hunted]] down Barro for the New Republic. Djarin was able to defeat the Imperial forces stationed in the base, causing Barro to flee in a trio of [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)|All Terrain Armored Transports]] along a narrow [[mountain]] path.<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />

Fremmet acted as the the [[vehicle commander]] of the second [[walker]] from its [[cockpit]], communicating with Barro in the lead walker via comms. As they progressed, Djarin and Grogu rode a stolen [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]] down the mountainside in front of them and then charged at the Imperial forces. Evading fire from Barro's walker, Djarin and Grogu dashed underneath the vehicle, which the warlord reported to Fremmet. The commander then had their walker open fire but also failed to take down the attackers. The mercenary and [[Youngling|child]] then reached the [[Walker Three|third walker]] and destroyed it with explosives before using a [[jetpack]] to fly onto the exterior of Fremmet's walker.<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />

===Something's on the roof===
{{Quote|Something's up there. He's on the roof! Can you see him? Take him out!|Fremmet|audio=OnTheRoof-Fremmet-MandoAndGrogu.flac|<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />}}
After Fremmet reported the loss of the walker to Barro, the warlord ordered the commander to send all their forces to fight the mercenary. As alarms sounded and all of the [[cold weather assault trooper]]s in Fremmet's walker began to prepare for combat, Djarin cut through the rear door of the vehicle's hold and then [[Death|dispatched]] all of the [[trooper]]s inside, also shooting one of the two [[Vehicle Command Crewer|pilots]] in the cockpit with Fremmet before it could be sealed off from the hold. Fremmet stood in silence, breathing heavily and staring at the sealed hatch as Barro demanded an update, wanting to know if Fremmet's men had killed the mercenary. <ref name="Mando&Grogu" />

[[File:FremmetDeath-MandoAndGrogu.png|thumb|left|300px|alt=An explosion erupts from the viewport of an All Terrain Armored Transport's cockpit among snowy mountains.|Fremmet was killed when Din Djarin caused the head of the commander's walker to explode.]]
The commander only responded upon hearing the Mandalorian moving across the roof of the walker's cockpit, desparetly demanding that Barro's walker shoot the mercenary off. The warlord ordered his walker's two [[pilot]]s onto the roof to fire on Djarin, but it was too late, as the mercenary began breaching the roof entry hatch above Fremmet. The commander cried out to Barro ineffectually over comms before the entire head of Fremmet's walker exploded, killing the commander. Djarin then killed Barro and the remaining Imperials as well.<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|He's breaching the hatch. He's breaching the hatch!''"<br />[Fremmet's walker explodes]|quoteend=0|Fremmet's last words|audio=Breaching-Fremmet-MandoAndGrogu.flac|<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />}}
Fremmet became increasingly panicked as the Mandalorian dispatched the other Imperials. The commander had light skin, [[Color|gray]] [[eye]]s, and a graying [[Hair|beard]].<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />

==Equipment==
While commanding the All Terrain Armored Transport, Fremmet wore [[Imperial officer battle armor]], including a white [[Armor|chestplate]], [[pauldron]]s and [[helmet]] with black [[goggles]] and white [[belt]] over a gray [[Imperial officer's uniform]]. The commander had a black [[blaster pistol]], an [[SE-14r light repeating blaster]], in a belt [[holster]].<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fremmet was portrayed by [[Joel Stoffer]] in the [[2026]] film ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''. The character's name was not spoken in the film but appeared in the credits.<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />
