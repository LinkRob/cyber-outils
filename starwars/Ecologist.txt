An '''ecologist''' was a type of [[scientist]] who studied ecology. The [[human]] [[Elping Tsoo]] was an ecologist for the [[Galactic Republic]] before being trapped in the [[Occlusion Zone]].<ref name="Survivors" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Nexus_of_Power_front_cover.png|thumb|right|250px|Ecologists were first mentioned in the current ''Star Wars'' canon via ''Nexus of Power''.]]
The occupation of ecologist was introduced in the current [[canon]] of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise in the [[2016]] sourcebook ''[[Nexus of Power]]'', published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for use with the ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="NoP">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref> An ecologist, [[Kell Tolkani]], subsequently appeared in the short story "[[A Naturalist on Hoth]]," which was written by [[Hank Green]] and published in the [[2020]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''.<ref name="Naturalist">{{StoryCite|story=A Naturalist on Hoth|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends continuity]], [[Ecologist/Legends|ecologists]] were introduced via ''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]'', a [[1989]] sourcebook authored by [[Troy Denning]] for use with [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="GG41">''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]''</ref> The ecologist [[Plett]] then made a full appearance, albeit as part of a vision, in the [[1995]] novel [[Children of the Jedi (novel)|''Children of the Jedi'']] by [[Barbara Hambly]].<ref name="ChildrenOfTheJedi">[[Children of the Jedi (novel)|''Children of the Jedi'']]</ref> Finally, [[Lisa Smedman]]'s [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure "[[Little Empires]]," published in the [[1996]] West End Games supplement ''[[The Kathol Outback]]'',<ref name="KatholOutback">''[[The Kathol Outback]]''</ref> featured the first illustration of an ecologist, [[Chandra Hobat]].<ref name="LittleEmpires">{{DarkStryder|adventure=Little Empires|book=The Kathol Outback}}</ref>
