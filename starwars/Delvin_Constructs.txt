'''Delvin Constructs''', known simply as '''Delvin''', was a supply company. It was based in the [[Corporate Sector]], and was best known for its line of [[modular base structure]]s and its patented [[Lockslab]] system. The company also sold various interior and exterior fitting kits for their bases, and [[environmental adaptation kit]]s that could seal and protect their base structures from adverse conditions.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
Delvin Constructs was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Lead by Example]]'', a [[2016]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] sourcebook published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Lead">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars: Empire at War: Prima Official Game Guide]]'', a [[2006]] paperback guide for the ''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]'' [[Star Wars video games|video game]], published by [[Prima Games]].<ref name="Prima Guide">[[Star Wars: Empire at War: Prima Official Game Guide|''Star Wars: Empire at War'': Prima Official Game Guide]]</ref>
