'''B1-series rocket battle droids''', also known as '''B1 rocket battle droids''', '''rocket battle droids''', or '''pod hunter droids''', were a modified version of the [[B1-series battle droid]] used to hunt down and destroy enemy [[escape pod]]s.

During a [[Malevolence Campaign|series of attacks]] launched by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Malevolence]]'' under the command of [[General]] [[Grievous]] and Confederate [[Head of State]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] in the [[22 BBY|first year]] of the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]], five B1-series rocket [[battle droid]]s were used as a means to hunt down any and all survivors that may have escaped their [[Starship|vessels]] aboard escape pods. However, during a [[Battle of Abregado|battle]] in the [[Abregado system]], which saw the destruction of [[Jedi General]] [[Plo Koon]]'s [[Plo Koon's fleet|fleet]] at the hands of the ''Malevolence'', the rocket droids and their [[Droch-class boarding ship|''Droch''-class boarding ship]] were destroyed by Koon and three [[clone trooper]]s that had survived the battle.

Rocket battle droids saw later use on [[Florrum]] in [[20 BBY]], during Grievous's [[Invasion of Florrum|invasion]] of the [[planet]].

==Description==
B1-series rocket [[battle droid]]s, otherwise known as pod [[hunter]]s, were a 1.9 [[meter]] tall variant of the [[B1-series battle droid]] upgraded for the sole purpose of destroying [[escape pod]]s. They were equipped with [[E-5 blaster rifle]]s, [[rocket pack]]s, and [[fusioncutter]]s, and were [[Painting|painted]] [[Color|orange]] and black in order to distinguish them from other B1s.<ref name="Collapse" /> The B1-series rocket battle droids' rocket packs were hardwired into their backs and they enabled the droids to maneuver in [[Realspace|space]]. The droids' fusioncutters were built into their arms, while they also had head-mounted lamps.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

When operating in a group, they were capable of [[pilot]]ing [[Droch-class boarding ship|''Droch''-class boarding ships]] that could attach to most pod designs and breach pods to depressurize the interior, which often [[Death|killed]] the occupants without having to utilize [[blaster]]s. For the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], which relied on secrecy to survive, this was a distasteful tactic.<ref name="Collapse" />

Despite being well-equipped for specialized and grisly operations, B1 rocket battle [[droid]]s had a variable track record. Sometimes [[Super battle droid rocket trooper|B2 super rocket troopers]] were used in their place, with their B1 counterparts being relegated to other short-range space missions or used as aerial [[trooper]]s during planetary encounters.<ref name="Collapse" />

==History==
{{Quote|Go get 'em, boys... Let's cut this can open!... There they go!|Rocket battle droids infiltrating Pod 1977|<ref name="RM" />}}
[[File:Rocket_Battle_Droids.png|thumb|left|250px|B1-series rocket battle droids in action.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|102}}</ref> during the [[Battle of Abregado]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and [[General]] [[Grievous]] deployed a group of rocket battle droids in a ''Droch''-class boarding ship to hunt for survivors and or witnesses of the battle. The droids eventually came across [[Pod 1977]] and proceeded to latch onto the pod. From a nearby pod, [[Jedi Master]] [[Plo Koon]] and a small group of [[Clone trooper|clones]] watched as the droids cut open the pod, sending the two clones inside hurtling through space. The droids' attention then turned towards the Jedi Master, however, and they attempted to infiltrate the pod. Koon, with the help of clone troopers [[Sinker]] and [[Boost]] engaged the droids in combat. After a short confrontation, Koon severed the boarding ship's hold on the pod, resulting in the ship crashing into nearby debris.<ref name="RM" />

In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">{{TCWcanondates|509}}</ref> General Grievous used B1-series rocket battle droids on [[Florrum]] during the [[Invasion of Florrum|invasion]] of the [[planet]].<ref name="ANB">{{TCW|A Necessary Bond}}</ref> Later in the war, they were also deployed on [[Kashyyyk]] during the [[Battle of Kashyyyk]].<ref name="Star Wars Journeys: Beginnings">''[[Star Wars Journeys: Beginnings]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
B1-series rocket battle droids first appeared in the "[[Rising Malevolence]],"<ref name="RM" /> the second episode of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''' [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]], which originally aired on [[October 3]], [[2008]]. They were voiced by [[Matthew Wood]], who also portrayed General Grievous.<ref name="Epguide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode002.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref>
