{{Quote|This reminds me of that time I tracked down the Belmont diadem&mdash;''"<br />"''I don't care!|Phee Genoa and Hunter|<ref name="Entombed" />}}
The '''Belmont diadem''' was an artifact tracked down by the [[pirate]] [[Phee Genoa]]<ref name="Entombed" /> by [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|205}}</ref> When she later went on a [[Discovery on Skara Nal|treasure hunt]] with the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]] in the [[Kaldar Trinary system]], the booby traps Genoa encountered at the entrance to the ancient [[superweapon]] [[Skara Nal]] reminded her of her search for the [[Crown|diadem]]. [[Hunter]], the Batch's leader, interrupted Genoa's reminiscence by snapping that he did not care as he was irritated by the several near-[[Death|lethal]] situations the group had already encountered.<ref name="Entombed" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Belmont diadem was mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' [[The Bad Batch Season 2|second season]] episode "[[Entombed]],"<ref name="Entombed" /> which premiered on [[Disney+]] on [[January 25]], [[2023]].<ref name="Season 2">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-bad-batch-season-2-trailer-key-art|text=Clone Force 99 Is Back in New ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 2 Trailer}}</ref>
