"'''A Princess on Lothal'''" ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]]: "'''Eine Prinzessin auf Lothal'''") is an episode of the [[German Rebels audio dramas]] adapting the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[A Princess on Lothal]]". It was released on [[January 13]], [[2017]] by [[Universal Music Group]] as part of the album [[Star Wars Rebels Episode 12 - Legacy / A Princess on Lothal]]. The episode was written by [[Gabriele Bingenheimer]], directed by [[Marian Szymczyk]] and includes narration by [[Hanns Jörg Krumpholz]].<ref name="Kiddinx" />
