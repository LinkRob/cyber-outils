'''Forest walkers''' were gentle, nocturnal [[Limb|quadrupeds]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[forest]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Corvus]].<ref name="CGttSWG" /> They could be observed [[Diet|feeding]] on the upper branches of [[tree]]s, which they were easily able to reach due to their long [[Limb|leg]]s. A pair of forest walkers startled [[Din Djarin]] while he and [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] were on the planet searching for [[Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="Chapter 13" />
