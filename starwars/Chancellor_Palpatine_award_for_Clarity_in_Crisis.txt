The '''Chancellor Palpatine award for Clarity in Crisis''' was an award bestowed by [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. [[Hamma Elad]], an [[artist]] and leading member of the [[Commission for the Protection of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]], was a three-[[time]] recipient of the award.<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Chancellor Palpatine award for Clarity in Crisis was first mentioned in the book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="Propaganda" /> and released on [[October 25]], [[2016]].<ref name="Propaganda Amazon">{{Harper|url=products/star-wars-propaganda-pablo-hidalgo?variant=32210373345314|text=Star Wars Propaganda}}</ref>
