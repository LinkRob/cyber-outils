{{Quote|Neimoidians have a rich, beautiful culture. Look around us&mdash;we are surrounded by it. The intricate carving of the goddesses Aven and Maradaine as they tell the Unraveled Tale.|Minister Alluv Eyam|<ref name="Brotherhood" />}}
'''Aven''' was a [[Deity|goddess]] worshipped by the [[Neimoidian]] [[species]]. Alongside fellow [[Gender|goddess]] [[Maradaine]], Aven was known for telling the [[Unraveled Tale]]. A [[Statue of Aven and Maradaine|statue]] of the two goddesses was a major landmark in [[Zarra]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Cato Neimoidia]].<ref name="Brotherhood" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']] is set between the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] and the [[Battle of Christophsis]], both of which are dated to [[22 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> [[Minister]] [[Alluv Eyam]] of the [[Cato Neimoidian government]] mentioned the statue of Aven while ruminating about Neimoidian culture at a [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Kenobi and Skywalker)|trial]] being held for [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who had been framed for crimes relating to the [[Cadesura disaster|destruction]] of Zarra's [[Cadesura district]].<ref name="Brotherhood" />

==Behind the scenes==
