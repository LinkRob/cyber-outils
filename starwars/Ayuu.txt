'''Ayuu''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[gergilla]] owned by the treasure [[Hunting|hunter]] [[Lens Kamo]]. While attempting to escape the [[laboratory]] of [[Baron]] [[Yasto Attsmun]], he was injured and captured by the baron. Attsmun used [[cybernetics]] to control Ayuu to attack Kamo and a [[Unidentified droid repair technician|droid repair technician]], however they were able to free Ayuu from his cybernetic control.<ref name="Last Call" />
