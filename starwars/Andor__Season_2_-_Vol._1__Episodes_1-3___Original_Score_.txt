'''''Andor'': Season 2 &ndash; Vol. 1 (Episodes 1-3) [Original Score]''' is the soundtrack for the first to third episodes of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'': "[[One Year Later]]", "[[Sagrona Teema]]", and "[[Harvest]]". It was composed by [[Brandon Roberts]] and released for digital streaming on [[April 25]], [[2025]].
