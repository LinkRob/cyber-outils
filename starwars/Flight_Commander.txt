'''Flight Commander'''<ref name="Armada">{{Armada|set=Interdictor Expansion Pack|cardname=Flight Commander}}</ref> was a role in the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. A Flight [[Commander]] instructed a [[speeder bike]] class at the [[Royal Imperial Academy]] that included [[Thane Kyrell]], [[Ciena Ree]], and [[Nash Windrider]]. Following the [[Battle of Jakku]], Windrider served as a Flight Commander aboard the [[attack cruiser]] [[Garrote (attack cruiser)|''Garrote'']].<ref name="Lost Stars" /> By the time of his [[death]]<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="VPDate">{{VictorysPriceDate}} Furthermore, since [[Garl Lykan]]'s [[death]] takes place before the Battle of Jakku, it must also take place in that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> [[Garl Lykan]] of the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] served as a Flight Commander. At [[Lykan's funeral]], the late [[pilot]]'s [[Squadron Four (Shadow Wing)|squadronmate]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Nord Kandende]] was promoted to Flight Commander in his place.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
A Flight Commander first appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Lost Stars]]'', written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />
