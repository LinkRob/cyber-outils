'''Errin''' was an old [[Gender|male]] individual who worked for [[Jabba's criminal empire]]. Prior to joining [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba's]] forces, Errin workeed for the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]]. He was saved [[Sion]] from the [[Black Sun]], and gave him a job in Jabba's palace.<ref name="FACPOV" />

The crew was on security detail when Jabba sentenced the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] to be executed in the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]]. When the prisoners tried to escape, Errin led the charge to try and apprehend Skywalker.<ref name="FACPOV" />
