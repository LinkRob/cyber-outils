'''Dar Champion''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who was elected the [[District Advocate]] for the [[planet]] [[Lanz Carpo]]. He was the former boyfriend of [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], whom he had met during regent training.

==Biography==
===Early life===
Dar Champion met [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] when she attended a summer mock congress while in regent training. They entered into a relationship, but Leia stood him up before the graduation dance, seemingly over [[Dar Champion's father|Dar's father]] being indicted by the [[Imperial Senate]] for embezzlement and abuse of office. Though Leia didn't formally break up with him, Dar didn't see his girlfriend again until the [[Galactic Civil War]]. His father's conviction led to the entire family being indicted as co-conspirators, and Dar spent years to exonerate himself and [[Dar Champion's mother|his mother]].<ref name="Star Wars 68" />

===Reunion with Leia===
{{Quote|Remember when we found out you had a wife?''"<br />"''We've been over this. Sana wasn't my wife.''"<br />"''Okay, fine&hellip;then Dar Champion is not my boyfriend.|Leia Organa and Han Solo|<ref name="Star Wars 68" />}}
Dar was elected the [[District Advocate]] of [[Lanz Carpo]] in the [[Core Worlds]], around the time of the [[Mission to Shu-Torun (Rebel Alliance)|Mission to Shu-Torun]] conducted by members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Following that mission, [[General]] [[Carlist Rieekan]] dispatched Leia and [[Han Solo]] to Lanz Carpo on a [[Mission to Lanz Carpo|mission]] to provoke a battle between [[Boss]] [[Carpo]] and the [[Galactic Empire]], hoping that Leia could use her past relationship with Champion as the key to accessing Carpo's [[Comm Center]]. After arriving on-world, Leia and Han were extorted by [[Red Carpo Guard]]s of the [[Carpo Crime Syndicate]], but were saved by Champion who attacked and subdued the guards. He helped Leia up as she explained that Dar had once been her boyfriend, much to Han's consternation.<ref name="Star Wars 68" /> Following the altercation, the guards were taken away and Dar was interviewed by [[Banny]]. However, before the suspects could be processed, a counterorder from [[Magistrate]] [[Eck]] was delivered, allowing the guards to go free, bailed out by Boss Carpo.<ref name="Star Wars 69" />

Champion offered Leia and Han drinks to make up for their spoiled evening, and discussed his family history. He came to realize that the pair had feelings for one another, and lent them use of the honeymoon suite at the [[Grand Regent]].<ref name="Star Wars 69" />

==Personality and traits==
Despite the rule of the Carpo Crime Syndicate on Lanz Carpo, Dar strove for justice in his work as District Advocate.<ref name="Star Wars 69" />
