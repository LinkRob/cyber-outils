'''Farbreini MicroElectronics Ltd.,'''<ref name="LbE" /> or just '''Farbreini MicroElectronics''',<ref name="CL2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> was a boutique electronics firm based on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="LbE" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}}, as depicted on the page of [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] that mentions Farbreini MicroElectronics, to [[22 BBY]].</ref> the company had a [[Farbreini MicroElectronics branch office|branch office]] near the [[Huosnoughow Foundation]] on Coruscant.<ref name="CL2016" /> The firm produced the [[BPEA-1A Cascader]], a relatively simple piece of electronic wizardry that allowed those in the public [[eye]] to have an extra level of control over their appearance. The device was available during the [[Galactic Civil War]] and consisted of a [[holoprojector]] connected to a patented projection matrix called the [[electronic molecular excitation net]].<ref name="LbE" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current [[canon]], Farbreini MicroElectronics Ltd. was first mentioned in ''[[Lead by Example]]'', a ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement written by [[John Dunn]] and published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]] for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="LbE" /> The company originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was introduced as [[Farbreini MicroElectronics Limited]] in ''[[Galladinium's Fantastic Technology]]'', a [[1995]] [[West End Games]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Galladinium">''[[Galladinium's Fantastic Technology]]''</ref>
