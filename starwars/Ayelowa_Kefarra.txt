'''Ayelowa Kefarra''' was an individual who was the [[chief justice]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="MoF" /> by [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|42}}</ref> She had been appointed to that position by [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] planned to have former [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] operative [[Soujen Vak-Nhalis]] testify in front of Kefarra on [[Coruscant]] about the lies spread by the Empire regarding the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="MoF" />

==Personality and traits==
Even though Kefarra was appointed by Palpatine, Organa considered her to be a faithful [[judge]] who would not deny facts when they were shown to her and Organa believed that she would and make sure that there was an unimpeachable record of everything.<ref name="MoF" />
