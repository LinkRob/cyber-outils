'''''Darth Vader's Mission: The Search for the Secret Plans''''' is young reader [[Star Wars Action Toy Book]] produced by [[White Heat]] and published in [[September]] [[1997]] by [[Fun Works Play To Learn]]. The book consists of sixteen full-color illustrated comic-style pages with a cutout in the middle through which a rubbery [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] action figure is suspended on a cord. Comic ballons are used to make the figure saying its lines. ''The Search for the Secret Plans'' was written by [[Jane Paley]] and produced by [[White Heat]], with art by [[Ken Steacy]].

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''When the plans for the Death Star are stolen, Darth Vader pursues Princess Leia's ship and uses the power of the Force on someone who doubts him. On board pages.''
