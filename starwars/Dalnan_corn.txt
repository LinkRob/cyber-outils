'''Dalnan corn''' was a type of [[corn]] from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dalna]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|religious]] group cultivated the [[Plant|crop]] while living as [[farmer]]s on the world. While [[Mission to Dalna (Sun and Forte)|visiting]] Dalna after the [[Battle of Jedha]]<ref name="Cataclysm" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Aida Forte]] noticed that the Dalnan corn was starting to rot on its stalks. When she theorized about the farmers' inattentiveness, her colleague, the [[Jedi Master]] [[Creighton Sun]], agreed.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dalnan corn first appeared in [[The High Republic: Cataclysm|''Cataclysm'']], a novel written by [[Lydia Kang]] and published in [[2023]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Cataclysm" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The [[2026]] out-of-universe recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Drinks]]'', written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Jenn Fujikawa]], included a Dalnan Latte beverage featuring corn milk that was inspired by the in-universe Dalnan corn.<ref name="Drinks">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Drinks]]''</ref>
