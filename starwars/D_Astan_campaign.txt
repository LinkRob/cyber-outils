In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the final [[Standard year|year]]<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> of the [[Clone Wars]], a '''campaign''' occurred within the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[D'Astan sector]] by the [[Galactic Republic]], during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]] offensive<ref name="BMF 70" /> launched by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] amid the conflict.<ref name="Old Friends">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref> During the campaign, the Republic made several coordinated efforts in order to concentrate their military engagements on both the [[sector]] and the [[Serenno system]] within. However, despite the democratic government's concentrated efforts in the sector and [[star system]], the Republic was unsuccessful in their attempts to capture Separatist [[Head of State|leader]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[homeworld]] of [[Serenno]], following multiple waves of [[Attacks on Serenno|attacks]] on the [[planet]].<ref name="BMF 70" />

==Behind the scenes==
The D'Astan campaign was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 70|seventieth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 70" /> in a section written by [[Mark Newbold]],<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> published around [[May 4]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|70}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Serenno theater]] was introduced in ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', a [[2009]] reference book authored by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> It was later identified in ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'', a [[2012]] reference book authored by Fry and [[Paul R. Urquhart]].<ref name="TEGTW">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref>
