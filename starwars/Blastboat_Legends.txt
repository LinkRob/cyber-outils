A '''blastboat''' was a small combat-capable [[Starship/Legends|starship]]. It filled the gap between the [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighter]], and more heavily-armed [[Capital ship/Legends|capital ships]]. Blastboats were fast enough to intercept intruders, but strong enough to engage and survive well-armed and armored opponents. Blastboats could operate in both [[Realspace/Legends|realspace]] and [[Atmosphere/Legends|atmosphere]].

The most well known and widespread class of blastboat was the [[GAT Blastboat series]] manufactured by [[Sienar Fleet Systems/Legends|Sienar Fleet Systems]]. It served as the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire's]] standard gunship for patrol and point missions.

==Behind the scenes==
In various Polish translations, both ''[[Gunship/Legends|Gunship]]'' and ''Blastboat'' vessels are called ''kanonierka'' ([[Gunboat/Legends|Gunboat]]), blurring the distinction in these translations between the classification of [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/Legends|LAAT]]-like ships and the [[GAT-12 Skipray Blastboat]] (a heavy [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighter]]).
