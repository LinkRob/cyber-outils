An '''Amulet of the Robb''' was a type of [[Color|brown]] amulet worn by [[Senator]] [[Tynnra Pamlo]], who was from<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> the [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Tynnra Pamlo">{{Databank|senator-tynnra-pamlo|Senator Tynnra Pamlo}}</ref> Taris<ref name="ROUVG" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> It shared its name with the [[Clone Wars]]-era Tarisian Senator [[Kin Robb]]<ref name="JTB">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> and [[New Republic]] Taris Senator [[Andrithal Robb-Voti]].<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
