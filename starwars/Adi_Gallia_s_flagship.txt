A [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] was in service to the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Republic Navy|naval forces']] [[Open Circle Fleet]], where it also served as the '''flagship''' for [[Jedi Master]] [[Adi Gallia]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. In [[20 BBY]], the Venator had traveled to the [[planet]] [[Aleen]], where [[clone trooper]]s of the Republic's [[104th Battalion]] delivered the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] and the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] to Gallia's [[flagship]]. Sometime after receiving the two [[droid]]s, the Star Destroyer was [[Battle of Patitite Pattuna|ambushed]] near the planet [[Patitite Pattuna]] by a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[fleet]] led by the [[Kaleesh]] [[General]] [[Grievous]], who boarded the Venator and captured Gallia, resulting in its destruction after her capture.

==Description==
{{Quote|They've hit the main reactor. Prepare to evacuate.|Adi Gallia, to a clone navigation officer|<ref name="Nomad Droids" />}}
[[File:AdiGalliaFlagshipHangar-ND.png|thumb|left|250px|The hangar bay of Adi Gallia's flagship]]
As a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[capital ship]] retained a propulsion system of eight [[Ion engine|ion drive]] thrusters mounted at the [[Starship|vessel's]] stern,<ref name="Engine note" /> as well as two command [[Bridge (starship)|bridges]].<ref name="Nomad Droids" /> At the top of each bridge were two [[hyperwave comm scanner]]s used for communications,<ref name="Comm" /> while the interior of one bridge possessed a large room that retained a [[holotable]] at its center, as well as several [[Hologram|holographic]] projection screens and a series of [[viewport]]s that provided the bridge crew a panoramic view of the surrounding [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Nomad Droids" />

Furthermore, the [[cruiser]]<ref name="Build X-Wing 47" /> also retained at least a single room designated for the quarters of onboard personnel, which had several display screens along its back wall and a single chair within, while the Venator also retained several [[blast door]]s controlled by panels directly next to the doors. The Star Destroyer also possessed an internal communications system used to prompt its passengers and crewmembers to conduct an immediate evacuation of the vessel.<ref name="Nomad Droids" />

Powered by a main reactor, the Venator was also armed with eight port and starboard [[DBY-827 heavy turbolaser turret]]s and two port and starboard medium dual [[turbolaser]] [[cannon]]s.<ref name="Weapons note" /> In addition to its armament, the Star Destroyer was also in possession of a main [[hangar]] bay, from where its complement of [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter]]s, [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]]s and [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] [[gunship]]s were carried behind a series of [[Color|blue]] lighted [[force field]]s. Furthermore, along its port and starboard [[hull]], the Venator was emblazoned with the symbol of the Republic's [[Open Circle Fleet]].<ref name="Nomad Droids" />

==History==
{{Quote|Nonessential personnel evacuate immediately. Nonessential personnel evacuate immediately.|A clone trooper, ordering all nonessential personnel to evacuate|<ref name="Nomad Droids" />}}
[[File:Gallias-Venator-Attacked.png|thumb|right|270px|Adi Gallia's flagship comes under attack by a Separatist fleet.]]
By [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer had become the [[flagship]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Adi Gallia]],<ref name="Nomad Droids" /> a [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi High Council|High Council]]<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> who helped lead the [[91st Mobile Reconnaissance Corps]] during the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the Galactic Republic and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Nomad Droids" /> By 20 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the Republic undertook a [[Mission to Aleen|relief mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Aleen]] under the supervision of [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe|CC-3636]], otherwise known as "Wolffe," of the Republic's [[104th Battalion]].<ref name="Nomad Droids" /> Gallia also arrived at Aleen for the mission.<ref name="Build X-Wing 47" />

Following the mission's completion, Wolffe and his [[clone trooper]]s delivered the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] and the astromech droid [[R2-D2]], who had assisted in the relief mission, to the ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer,<ref name="Nomad Droids" /> and Gallia intended to use the Star Destroyer to return<ref name="Build X-Wing 47" /> to the Republic [[capital]] planet of [[Coruscant]]. While en route to Coruscant, the Venator was [[Battle of Patitite Pattuna|ambushed]] by a Separatist [[fleet]] near the planet [[Patitite Pattuna]] under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of the [[Kaleesh]] [[General]] [[Grievous]].<ref name="Nomad Droids" /> A [[Grievous's Providence-class carrier/destroyer (Patitite Pattuna)|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] supported by two [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]], the fleet was more than a match for the single Republic cruiser.<ref name="Build X-Wing 47" />

Upon the arrival of Grievous's fleet, the Separatist warships was able to strike the port hull of Gallia's flagship, which resulted in significant damage to the main reactor and prompted the Jedi Master to order an immediate evacuation of all onboard personnel. As the onboard personnel made their way to the [[escape pod]]s and [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]]s, the two Republic droids, in an attempt to locate an escape pod, wandered throughout the entire Star Destroyer. While wandering, the two droids found Master Gallia locked in battle with the [[cyborg]] general, whom had managed to incapacitate the Jedi Master after a brief [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] in a hallway within the Venator, while 91st clone troopers attempted to fend off the Separatist general's [[B1-series battle droid]]s.<ref name="Nomad Droids" />

[[File:GalliaVsGrievous-ND.jpg|thumb|left|270px|Adi Gallia engages General Grievous in a duel aboard her flagship.]]
As Grievous's [[warship]]s continued to fire upon the Venator, several Republic [[starfighter]]s, [[bomber]]s and gunships were deployed from its main hangar to fend off attacking [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class droid starfighters]], including one BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber that had been piloted by C-3PO and R2-D2. However, despite the Republic's efforts,<ref name="Nomad Droids" /> the Kaleesh managed to best Gallia,<ref name="Build X-Wing 47" /> taking her captive aboard his [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]], while the three Separatist warships pressed fire on the Venator, resulting in its destruction as it exploded.<ref name="Nomad Droids" />

==Commanders and crew==
{{Quote|All pilots to your stations. All flight crews, man your stations.|A clone trooper, over the internal comms|<ref name="Nomad Droids" />}}
The ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer, serving as her flagship, was commanded by Jedi Master Adi Gallia during the second year of the war and was maintained by a crew of clone troopers and [[clone navigation officer]]s. It was also crewed by [[R-series]] [[astromech droid]]s, as well as several [[clone trooper flight crew]]s, who operated within the Venator's main hangar bay.<ref name="Nomad Droids" />
