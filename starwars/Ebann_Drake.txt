'''Ebann Drake''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[arms dealer]] who led an arms-[[Smuggler|smuggling]] ring on [[Kaderin]]. He was a distant cousin of [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]] of the [[Tagge Corporation]], whose company was privately involved with Drake. Drake was invited to take part in what he claimed was an incredible opportunity, mentioning the event to Tagge, but did not make contact after departing aboard the luxury [[starship]]<ref name="Aphra 10" /> ''[[Opal Empress]]''.<ref name="Aphra 11" /> Tagge sent the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] and the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]] to find Drake and ascertain what the opportunity was, hoping to take advantage of it if her cousin did not.<ref name="Aphra 10" /> After boarding the ''Opal Empress'', Aphra and Starros found that Drake and the other passengers aboard had been [[Death|killed]] by the [[parasite]]s known as [[cymote]]s.<ref name="Aphra 11" />
