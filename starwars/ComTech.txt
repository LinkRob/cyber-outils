'''ComTech''' was a company that manufactured various series of [[speaker]]s<ref name="ROUVG" /> and [[microphone]]s. Its products included the [[Sensa-mic|Sensa-Mic]], fitted to [[K-22995 light flight helmet]]s worn by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel pilot|pilots]],<ref name="SWCT Archetype Helmets">{{SWCT|set=Archetype - The Helmets of Star Wars|cardname=Rebel Pilot}}</ref> the [[series III speaker]], one of which was built into the [[Mandalorian armor|helmet]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]],<ref name="HelmetColl" /> and the [[Series IV speaker]], which were built into the [[Stormtrooper armor|helmets]] worn by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="ROUVG" />
