'''Charles Soule''' is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] writer, and novelist, as well as a Creative Consultant for [[Lucasfilm]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|You may know me from having written something you've read.|Charles Soule|<ref name="BluSky">{{Bluesky|username=charlessoule.bsky.social|name=[[Charles Soule]]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/9lyeA}}</ref>}}
Charles Soule authored the [[Star Wars: Lando (comic series)|''Star Wars: Lando'']] and ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan and Anakin]]'' miniseries for [[Marvel Comics]] before later writing for the ''[[Star Wars: Poe Dameron]]'' series, the [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2017)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'' (2017)]] series, and the ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Kylo Ren]]'' miniseries.<ref name="Official Website" /> He went on to write Marvel's mainline [[Star Wars (2020)|''Star Wars'']] run set after [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars: ''Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/marvels-star-wars-comic-relaunch|text=Marvel's ''Star Wars'' Comic To Relaunch with Writer Charles Soule}}</ref>

His story "[[Red Four (comic story)|Red Four]]" was included in [[Marvel Comics 1000|''Marvel Comics'' #1000]], a comic issue released to celebrate Marvel Comics' eightieth anniversary.<ref name="Marvel 1000">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-joins-the-landmark-marvel-comics-1000|text=''Star Wars'' Joins the Landmark ''Marvel Comics'' #1000}}</ref>

He is currently working on ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' with [[Cavan Scott]], [[Claudia Gray]], [[Daniel José Older]], and [[Justina Ireland]]. As part of ''The High Republic'', Soule wrote his first ''Star Wars ''novel, which launched the multimedia publishing campaign. ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' was published in January 2021.<ref name="Celebration">{{StarWarsShow|sP6SaKn2WuM|Star Wars Celebration Chicago 2019 Live Stream - Day 4 &#124; The Star Wars Show LIVE!}}</ref> He then concluded the first phase of The High Republic initiative as the writer for the 2-part comic mini series [[Star Wars: The High Republic: Eye of the Storm|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'']], published from January to March of [[2022]].<ref name="Issue 1-Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/96252/star_wars_the_high_republic_-_eye_of_the_storm_2022_1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic - Eye of the Storm (2022) #1}}</ref><ref name="Issue 2-Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/96253/star_wars_the_high_republic_-_eye_of_the_storm_2022_2|text=Star Wars: The High Republic - Eye of the Storm (2022) #2}}</ref> 

It was revealed on [[April 27]], 2022, during a [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] exchange, that Soule had been named a Creative Consultant for [[Lucasfilm]].<ref name="Creative Consultant">{{Twitter|CharlesSoule|status/1519404156838158337|Charles Soule|quote=Guess this is getting out there - very excited about this new role! It's been a blast so far, and is letting me do some really cool stuff in the galaxy far, far away.<br />TL;DR - I'm officially a Creative Consultant for Lucasfilm, and it's cool.|archivedate=20220515092502}}</ref>
