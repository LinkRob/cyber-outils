The '''Demirian stronghold''' was a fortress on [[Demir]], used by the [[Demirian]]s during the [[First Order]]'s [[Attack on Demir|attack]] on their [[planet]]. The First Order's [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] sacrificed her [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] to draw the Demirians out of the stronghold, before calling in [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] to [[Death|kill]] all the combatants.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />
