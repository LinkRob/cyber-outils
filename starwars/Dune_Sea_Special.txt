"'''Dune Sea Special'''" was a popular [[music]] piece.<ref name="GEG" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> on the [[Standard day|day]] that [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] met [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]], the band [[Figrin D'an and the Modal Nodes]] performed that piece at [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in [[Mos Eisley]], after playing "[[Mad About Me]]."<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> Even after the Modal Nodes moved on, the [[cantina]]'s house bands kept playing "Dune Sea Special," along with other hits.<ref name="GEG" />
