'''Brass''' was a metal that could notably be used in the construction of [[lightsaber]]s. For example, [[Agen Kolar]]'s [[Agen Kolar's lightsaber|lightsaber]] was made of brass and [[durasteel]]. Brass could also be combined with [[manganese]] to create an [[Manganese brass|alloy]].<ref name="SWL">''[[Star Wars Lightsabers: A Guide to Weapons of the Force]]''</ref> Part of the bar pipework at [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] was also made of brass.<ref name="The Luckless Rodian">{{StoryCite|story=The Luckless Rodian|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> The [[yacht]] of [[Arsin Crassus]] had brass and [[carmine]] piping.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> [[Kittik]] used a brass [[cane]] to walk due to his advanced age.<ref name="QFTHC">''[[The High Republic: Quest for the Hidden City]]''</ref>
