The '''Clan Vizsla Warrior''' was a set of [[armor]] worn by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Aran Tal]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Clan Tal Warrior|standard costume]]. Though part of [[Clan Tal]] himself, Aran wore this set of [[Mandalorian armor|armor]] with a hope of reconciliation between his [[Mandalorian clan|clan]] and [[Clan Vizsla]], sporting their traditional [[Color|white]], gray, and red color scheme.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Clan Vizsla Warrior was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Aran Tal, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 1.0.0 alongside Season 1 and the game's global launch on [[June 4]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Clan Vizsla MT-97]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing the Vizsla Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
