During the later [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Clone Wars]], a '''battle''' took place on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> that saw [[Galactic Republic]] forces attack a [[Akiva production plant|Separatist droid factory]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Kay Vess' crew]] passed through the ruins of the battle, with [[BX-series droid commando]] [[ND-5]] falling back into old programming and thinking he was part of the battle as he passed through the factory and the remains of [[B1-series battle droid]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Norra Wexley's team]] later passed through the ruined factory during the [[rebellion on Akiva]]. Wexley recalled the factory as a site made during the late Clone Wars,<ref name="Aftermath" /> but it had actually been in operation since at least [[22 BBY]]<ref name="Outlaws note">"[[Confederacy of Independent Systems|CIS]] Transmission 04" in ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' establishes that the Akiva production plant was in operation while the [[BX-series droid commando]] was still undergoing preliminary tests. As the BX was introduced to the frontlines around the events of "[[Rookies (episode)|Rookies]]," which is set in [[22 BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', the Akivan factory must have been in operation by 22 BBY.</ref> before the Republic shut it down<ref name="Outlaws" /> late in the war.<ref name="Aftermath" />
