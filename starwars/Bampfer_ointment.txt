==Behind the scenes==
Bampfer ointment appeared in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 12|twelfth issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']]. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Caio Filipe|Caìo Filipe]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]<ref name="THRA 12" /> on [[November 20]], [[2024]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-805/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-12|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #12}}</ref>
