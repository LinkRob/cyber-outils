The '''Cathedral of the Winds''' was a [[music]]al point of interest in [[the galaxy]]. In her [[Maz Kanata's Journal|journal]]<ref name="Hope & Courage" /> written no earlier than [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Forces of Destiny: Tales of Hope & Courage]]'' establishes that [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[in-universe]] [[Maz Kanata's Journal|journal]] was written after she met [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] during {{Film|VII}}, which occurred in [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]] wrote that she had heard the beautiful music of the Cathedral of the Winds, and that it was among the most wondrous things she had witnessed in her life, alongside the [[Forbidden Gardens of Nuswatta]].<ref name="Hope & Courage" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cathedral of the Winds was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Forces of Destiny: Tales of Hope & Courage]]'', a [[2017]] replica journal book written by [[Elizabeth Schaefer]].<ref name="Hope & Courage" /> The [[Cathedral of Winds/Legends|Cathedral of Winds]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[1994]] novel [[Dark Apprentice (novel)|''Dark Apprentice'']], the second book of the [[Star Wars: The Jedi Academy Trilogy|''The Jedi Academy Trilogy'']] written by [[Kevin J. Anderson]].<ref name="Dark Apprentice">[[Dark Apprentice (novel)|''Dark Apprentice'']]</ref>
