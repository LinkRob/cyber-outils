The '''Chamber of the Sun''' was a partially underground chamber on [[Livno III]], and the location where the [[Breeze Globe|Breeze]], [[Rain Globe|Rain]] and [[Solstice Globe]]s were held. With the help of [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[L3-37]], [[Zel Gris]] and [[Rinetta Gan]] returned the Solstice Globe to the chamber, saving the [[planet]].<ref name="Lando's Luck" />
