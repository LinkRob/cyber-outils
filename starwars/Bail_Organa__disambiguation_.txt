'''Bail Organa''' can refer to:

==Canon==
*'''[[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]]''', [[Alderaanian]] [[Senator]], [[Viceroy]] and [[First Chairman]], founding member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], husband of [[Breha Organa]] and adoptive father of [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].
*'''[[Bail Organa's resistance movement]]''', a resistance formed by Bail Organa shortly after the formation of the Galactic Empire. would be the precursor to the Rebel Alliance.
*'''[[Bail Organa's Christophsis refugee camp]]''', a refugee camp set up by Bail Organa on [[Christophsis]] during the [[Clone Wars]].

*'''''[[Bail Organa (CR90 corvette)|Bail Organa]]''''', a [[CR90 corvette/Legends|CR90 corvette]].
*'''''[[Bail Organa (Star Destroyer)|Bail Organa]]''''', the flagship of [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] [[Fleet Group Two]].
*'''[[Bail Organa's Christophsis refugee camp/Legends|Bail Organa's Christophsis refugee camp]]''', used by the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] in the [[Battle of Christophsis/Legends|Battle of Christophsis]].
