'''Batuuan spider-roach''' was a type of [[spider-roach]] that could be [[Diet|eaten]] as [[Foodstuff|food]]<ref name="Battle of Jedha script" /> and shared its name with the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Batuu]]. A [[Unidentified meat trader|meat trader]] [[Merchant|sold]] crunchy Batuuan spider-roach in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] on<ref name="Battle of Jedha script" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[moon]] [[Jedha]]<ref name="Battle of Jedha script" /> by [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> while a group of [[wargaran]] creatures were loose in the [[city]], the trader was bored and listed crunchy Batuuan spider-roach among the food items they had for sale.<ref name="Battle of Jedha script" />

==Behind the scenes==
Batuuan spider-roach was mentioned in ''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]'', an audio drama written by [[George Mann]]<ref name="Battle of Jedha">''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]''</ref> and released on [[January 3]], [[2023]], as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="First listen">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-high-republic-the-battle-of-jedha-audiobook-original-first-listen|text=Two Jedi Knights Look to Negotiate a Truce in ''Star Wars: The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha'' &ndash; First Listen}}</ref> The spelling of the creature's name was confirmed with the release of the audio drama's [[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha (script)|script]],<ref name="Battle of Jedha script" /> published on [[February 14]] of the same year.<ref name="First listen" />
