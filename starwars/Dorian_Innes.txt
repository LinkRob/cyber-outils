'''Dorian Innes''' was a [[Gender|trans male]] [[medic]]al officer in the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He was assigned on a Republic and [[Jedi Order]] [[Mission to Angoth|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Angoth]] to capture one of the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures from the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]. Innes accompanied [[Jedi Master]] [[Mirro Lox]], his [[Padawan]] [[Amadeo Azzazzo]], the [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] [[Ashton Vol]] and [[L-77]], and an RDC team aboard the [[Pacifier-class sector patrol cruiser|''Pacifier''-class transport ship]] [[Invictus (Pacifier-class)|''Invictus'']].<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dorian Innes first appeared in the [[2024]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Tears of the Nameless]]'', written by [[George Mann]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=SWCE 2023: 9 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref> In the novel, Innes tells Amadeo Azzazzo about coming out to his [[Dorian Innes's father|father]], a scene Mann included as he felt it was important for people to be able to see themselves in novels.<ref name="George">{{Bluesky|georgemann.bsky.social|post/3lgcl2jzpzs2c|[[George Mann]]|date=2025-01-22|quote=Thank you so much for this. It's *exactly*why I wanted to include that scene in the book. It's so important for everyone to be able to see themselves in books, film, TV. Especially in these troubled times.|archivefile=File:GeorgeMann-DorianInnes-Bluesky.png}} {{C|In response to: "@georgemann.bsky.social as the parent of a trans kid, I am bawling listening to Tears of the Nameless, to Dorian talk to Amadeo about his coming out to his dad. I love seeing people I love in real life in the universe that has comforted me for as long as I jave lived. Thank you #ProtectTransKids"}}</ref>
