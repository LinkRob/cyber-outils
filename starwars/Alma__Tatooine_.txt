'''Alma''' was an individual who stashed a [[Canto Pass]] game piece, "[[Dud III]]", in a container on a ledge of a rock formation in the [[North Jundland Wastes]] region of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] discovered the stash and stole the game piece.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Alma was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. Their stash contains one of the collectible trinkets that can be used to customize the player's [[EML-850 light freighter]], the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', or their [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
