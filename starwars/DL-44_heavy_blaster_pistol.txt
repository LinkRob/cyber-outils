The '''DL-44 heavy blaster pistol''', also known more simply as a '''DL-44 blaster pistol''', was a type of [[heavy blaster pistol]] produced by [[BlasTech Industries]].<ref name="LaunchBay" /> 

==Description==
{{Quote|Hokey religions and ancient weapons are no match for a good blaster at your side, kid.|Han Solo|<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>}}
[[File:Tobias_Beckett_AtG.png|thumb|left|250px|Tobias Beckett wielding the DL-44 blaster in rifle configuration.]]
The DL-44 heavy blaster pistol was used during the [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]]. It was considered one of the most powerful [[blaster pistol]]s in [[the galaxy]], delivering massive damage at close range; however, it overheated quickly.<ref name="Planets Battlefront">{{EA|subdomain=starwars|url=starwars/battlefront/planets|text=''Star Wars'' Battlefront Planets|nolive=1}}</ref> The DL-44's [[capacitor]] can charge a double-power laser-bolt without overheating. Its firepower is powerful for a pistol, without compromising accuracy, making it ideal for use by military forces, as well as [[bounty hunter]]s and [[smuggler]]s.<ref name="Comprehensive Guide">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> The DL-44 was easily modifiable<ref name="DB DL44">{{Databank|dl-44-blaster-pistol|DL-44 Blaster Pistol}}</ref> which allowed Solo to make a number of modifications on his own, including, at one point, removing the blaster's motion-sensitive barrel [[scope]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> The pistol could also be [[Reconfigurable blaster|reconfigured]] into a [[blaster rifle]] by including an extended [[barrel]] with the existing flash-suppressor, dissembled the existing [[D3x macroscope|D3x]] [[macroscope]], and adding an attachable [[stock]].<ref name="Solo" />

==History==
[[File:HanEndor.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Han Solo made use of a modified DL-44 throughout his life.]]
During the Clone Wars, the [[mercenary]] [[droid]] [[AD-W4]] used one while [[Operation on Hissrich|fighting]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] on [[Hissrich]]. The [[Twi'lek]] [[Freedom Fighter]] [[Cham Syndulla]] wielded one such pistol as well.<ref name="LoR">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref> During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] used this model.<ref name="Brothers of the Broken Horn" /> In the trenches of the [[Mimban Campaign|besieged]] planet [[Mimban]], the criminal [[Tobias Beckett]] made use of [[Han's heavy blaster pistol|a DL-44]]. Thereafter, he gave it to the young [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]],<ref name="Solo" /> who used it for the rest of his life.<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]] owned a [[Ezra's heavy blaster pistol|DL-44]] during the [[Standard year|years]] leading up to the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Steps Into Shadow" /> Rebel [[pilot]]s [[Biggs Darklighter]] and [[Wedge Antilles]] both carried this blaster pistol.<ref name="SWGOH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> Former rebel fighter [[Zaina]] wielded a modified DL-44 identified as the [[Veteran Blaster]] within the [[Grand Arena]] as a [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]].<ref name="SWH" />

When [[Ben Solo]] joined the [[Knights of Ren]], he was given a new uniform with a [[holster]] holding a blaster that looked exactly like his father's and uncle's.<ref name="TRoKR3" /> During the [[Cold War]], [[Venisa Doza]] carried a DL-44 in a holster in her hip.<ref name="Rendezvous Point" /> [[Kazuda Xiono]] used a DL-44 on multiple occasions, one was given to him by [[Poe Dameron]] when they explored [[Station Theta Black]],<ref name="Station Theta Black">{{Resistance|Station Theta Black (episode)}}</ref> and when they explored [[Sector Epsilon 51-3]] where Xiono used it to take out a [[Spider probe droid]].<ref name="The Core Problem">{{Resistance|The Core Problem}}</ref> [[Synara San]] toss Xiono a DL-44 that belonged to the [[Warbird gang]].<ref name="The Mutiny">{{Resistance|The Mutiny}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The DL-44 action static prop used in the [[2015]] [[sequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] was made from cast resin with metallic and wooden features painted on to the armature and grip. It measured 12.5 inches by seven inches by three inches. The sequel trilogy version can be distinguished from the prop used in the [[original trilogy]] by the fact that the end of its barrel is painted black rather then silver.<ref name="Auction">{{WebCite|url=https://entertainment.ha.com/itm/movie-tv-memorabilia/props/star-wars-episode-vii-the-force-awakens-disney-2015-harrison-ford-han-solo-action-dl-44-blaster/a/7456-11055.s|text=''Star Wars: Episode VII - The Force Awakens'' (Disney, 2015), Harrison Ford "Han Solo" Action DL-44 Blaster|work=[[Wikipedia:Heritage Auctions|Heritage Auctions]]|archivedate=20260426190441}}</ref>
