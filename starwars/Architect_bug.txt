'''Architect bugs''' were a type of flying [[insect]] found on the [[planet]] [[Siline]]. Like much of the indigenous life on Siline, architect bugs interfaced with [[crystal]]s, excreting tiny crystalline filaments to weave combs into their hive. Architect bugs built hives within [[forest]]s, often inside hollow portions of [[tree]]s, such as in low-lying root knees. The sparkling bugs were known to engage in familial cannibalism, frequently eating their young if the hive was under threat.

To the [[Silinese]], having an architect bug population density of three hives per one hundred trees was considered to be a sign of a healthy forest. Culturally, the bugs came to represent industriousness and tenacity. Indeed, many Silinese survivors of natural disasters got architect bug etch [[tattoo]]s. Architect bugs were represented on the flag of one of Siline's oldest nations. The bugs were neither [[poison]]ous nor a [[foodstuff|food]] source for the Silinese. 

During the Siline [[Crowning Convocation]] in [[134 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref>, the Silinese teenager [[Rhos Arthyst]] showed [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]] a hive of architect bugs, educating her on the history and significance of the creatures.<ref name="TCC" />
