A [[Plant|crop]] with certain traits could be designated as an '''AgriStar-modified crop''' by the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|9}}</ref> the [[Alvadorjian]] operative [[Soujen Vak-Nhalis]] listened to local news stations on the [[planet]] [[Li'eta]] via the [[Comm unit|comms]] on a [[skimmer]]. One of the channels he flicked to was broadcasting two speakers, both debating the merits of AgriStar modified-crops. However, Vak-Nhalis switched to another channel shortly afterward.<ref name="MOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
AgriStar-modified crops were mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MOF" />
