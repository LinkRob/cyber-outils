'''Bryton James''' is an American actor who voiced [[Zare Leonis]] on the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''.<ref name="Breaking Ranks">{{Rebels|Breaking Ranks}}</ref> Previous acting credits include a starring role in ''The Young and the Restless'', a guest starring role on ''The Vampire Diaries'', and a starring role on ''Family Matters''.
