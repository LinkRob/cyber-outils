'''Desertion''' was the act of abandoning one's role within a military organization without permission. People who deserted from such forces were known as '''deserters'''. Notable deserters included [[Cut Lawquane]], [[Clone Force 99]], [[Thane Kyrell]], and [[Han Solo]]. Desertion was generally considered an act of [[treason]].

Desertion to an opposing or enemy force was specifically known as '''defection''', with those who defected to other factions known as '''defectors'''. However, the definition of defection included both military and civilian personnel, such as [[senator]]s like [[Lux Bonteri]].

==Description==
Desertion was when a member of a military organization abandoned their role without permission.<ref name="The Deserter">{{TCW|The Deserter}}</ref> It was also known as going AWOL.<ref name="The Solitary Clone">{{TBB|The Solitary Clone}}</ref> When someone deserted to an opposing or enemy force, it was known as defection.<ref name="Tipping Points">{{TCW|Tipping Points}}</ref> Individuals who committed desertion were known as deserters,<ref name="The Deserter" /> and those who defected were called defectors. Unlike desertion, which applied only to military personnel, defection could also be done by civilians.<ref name="Tipping Points" /> Desertion was considered an act of [[treason]] by governments such as the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="War-Mantle" />

==History==
===High Republic Era===

During the [[Soikan conflict|conflict]] which encompassed the [[planet]] [[Soika]], the [[Velko Jahen's father|father]] of [[Velko Jahen]] defected from the [[Livtak Union]] to the [[Gagic Alliance]] after discovering the Union was planning something he disapproved of. This was how he met the [[Velko Jahen's mother|woman]] who became Jahen's [[Parent|mother]].<ref name="First Duty">{{InsiderCite|issue1=201|issue2=202|Starlight: First Duty}}</ref>

===The Clone Wars===
[[File:CutLawquaneHS-SWE.png|thumb|left|200px|Cut Lawquane deserted from the Grand Army of the Republic.]]
Shortly after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] which kickstarted the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], a Republic [[troop transport]] was [[Attack on a Republic troop transport|attacked]] by Separatist forces. The sole Republic survivor was a [[clone trooper]] who eventually became known as [[Cut Lawquane]], who deserted in the aftermath as he felt his life had no meaning since his team was [[Death|dead]]. Cut [[Marriage|married]] [[Suu Lawquane]] and became a [[farmer]] on [[Saleucami]]. During the [[Battle of Saleucami]], he met [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] of the [[501st Legion]] after Rex was injured and his men left him at the [[Lawquane's Farm|Lawquane farm]] to recover. Rex considered reporting Cut, but eventually chose not to after they fought off a [[BX-series droid commando|commando droid]] attack on the farm together.<ref name="The Deserter" />

When Jedi Master [[Tenth Brother|Prosset Dibs]] declared his intent to no longer serve the Jedi Council due to the Order serving themselves and follow his own path, [[Mace Windu]] declared that he is choose to desert the Jedi Order and threaten if he turns his back and run, then he’ll won’t hesitate to hunt him down.<ref name="JoRMW4">[[Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu 4|''Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu'' 4]]</ref>

On the [[waterworld]] [[Mon Cala]], divided between the [[Mon Calamari]] and [[Quarren]] [[species]], support for the opposing sides in the Clone Wars fell along species lines. Quarren [[chief]]tain [[Nossor Ri]] led his people to join the Separatists after the [[Death|murder]] of [[King]] [[Yos Kolina]], feeling that Kolina's son, the Mon Calamari [[Prince]] [[Lee-Char]], was unprepared to lead.<ref name="Water War">{{TCW|Water War}}</ref> Lee-Char eventually deduced that Separatist [[ambassador]] and [[Battle of Mon Cala|invader]] [[Riff Tamson]] had murdered his [[Parent|father]], accusing Tamson in front of Ri, who had developed doubts as Tamson's forces occupied the [[planet]]. Ri turned against the Separatists for good during Tamson's attempted execution of Lee-Char, as the prince had suspected he would, assisting in fighting off the invaders. In the aftermath, Ri swore loyalty to Lee-Char at his coronation.<ref name="Prisoners">{{TCW|Prisoners}}</ref>

===Order 66 and the aftermath===
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the Clone Wars came to an abrupt end when Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], truly the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], issued [[Order 66]], a secret command which compelled the clone troopers of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] to turn on and [[Death penalty|execute]] their [[Jedi]] [[commanding officer]]s.<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> The order was enforced through secretly implanted [[Behavioral modification biochip|inhibitor chips]] which controlled the [[Cloning|clones']] behavior against their will, giving them no choice in their actions.<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref> A few clones were immune to the chips or otherwise free of the [[brain]]washing, however, and many of them chose to desert<ref name="Battle Scars">{{TBB|Battle Scars}}</ref> from Palpatine's new [[Galactic Empire]] rather than serve it.<ref name="ROTS" />

Rex, who had by that time been promoted to [[Clone Commander]], was en route to [[Coruscant]] with former [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref> onboard the ''[[Tribunal]]'',<ref name="TCW CE">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> intending to deliver the captured renegade [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] to the [[Jedi High Council]]. When the order was [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|issued]], he was able to resist his chip's effects briefly,<ref name="Shattered" /> having learned of their existence from the [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Fives]], who had been [[Mission to Level 1325|silenced]] after finding out the truth about what the chips were for and that Palpatine was behind it.<ref name="Orders" /> Rex had not understood Fives' warning at the time,<ref name="Battle Scars" /> as Fives had been drugged into incoherency,<ref name="Orders" /> but he realized what was happening and was able to tell Tano to "find Fives" before the chip took him over. With the help of three [[astromech droid]]s, she found a grievance report Rex had filed about Fives' death in which he mentioned the chips, and was able to capture him and surgically remove it, freeing him from the mind control.<ref name="Shattered" /> The [[332nd Division]] turned on Rex when they realized he was no longer attempting to kill Tano, but she and Rex escaped from the crashing [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]]. With everyone onboard the ''Tribunal'' dead in the aftermath,<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> Tano and Rex parted ways and separately went into hiding from the Empire.<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref>

[[File:BadBatch-S2Promo.png|thumb|right|300px|Most of the members of Clone Force 99 became deserters.]]
Most of the members of the irregular [[clone commando]] [[squad]] [[Clone Force 99]]&mdash;nicknamed the "Bad Batch"&mdash;were immune to Order 66 due to either their status as [[genetically defective clone]]s or, in [[Echo]]'s case, [[cybernetics]] implanted<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> while he was a [[prisoner]] of the [[Techno Union]].<ref name="A Distant Echo">{{TCW|A Distant Echo}}</ref> The sole exception was the team's [[sniper]]: [[Crosshair|CT-9904]] "Crosshair's" chip did work, but was less active than those of standard clones due to his [[gene]]tic mutations. With their loyalty in question after the rise of the Empire, [[Admiral]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] dispatched them on a [[Mission to Onderon (Clone Force 99)|mission]] to test their obedience, which they refused to carry out after finding out that their targets included civilians and former Republic fighters. After returning to [[Kamino]] as team leader [[Hunter]] wanted to rescue [[Omega]], a unique unmodified clone the squad had met earlier, they were arrested, and Crosshair was taken away on Tarkin's orders to have the effects of his chip enhanced, making him totally loyal to the Empire. The rest of the Batch [[Escape from Kamino|escaped]] imprisonment with Omega, forced to leave their brainwashed former comrade behind.<ref name="Aftermath" />

Some time after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the Empire, as the new government moved forward with [[Project War-Mantle|plans]] to phase out the clones in favor of recruited [[soldier]]s, some clone [[commando]]s were tasked with training the new [[Stormtrooper|"TK troopers"]] at secret facilities like [[Imperial Base on Daro|one]] located on the otherwise-uninhabited planet [[Daro]]. It did not take long for one of the instructors there, [[Captain]] [[Gregor]], to decide he did not want to be involved in what the Empire was doing, and he attempted to desert. He made it out of the base while being chased by Imperial troopers, but was only able to plant a distress beacon before being recaptured. The beacon sent a signal to Rex, who was a friend of Gregor's, but as he was unable to go himself, Rex tasked the Bad Batch with [[Extraction on Daro|rescuing]] Gregor. The squad successfully infiltrated the base and freed Gregor, but Hunter was captured after being separated from his team, ordering the others to escape without him.<ref name="War-Mantle" /> The Batch subsequently regrouped and set out to [[Rescue of Hunter|rescue]] him from the Empire.<ref name="Return to Kamino">{{TBB|Return to Kamino}}</ref>

In the months after the Empire's formation, increasing numbers of "reg" clone troopers began to question the new regime and go AWOL, something [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Cody]] discussed with Crosshair before their [[Mission to Desix|mission]] to the planet [[Desix]]. The sniper was firm in his belief that clones who chose to do so were traitors. Crosshair's actions in killing [[Governor]] [[Tawni Ames]] during the mission on orders from Imperial governor [[Grotton]] disillusioned Cody, who had attempted to offer Ames a peaceful resolution. After returning to [[Coruscant]], Cody asked Crosshair if the Empire was really doing good things for the galaxy. He himself went AWOL that very night, and Crosshair was informed of Cody's disappearance by [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart|Rampart]] the next day. Rampart found it curious that clones around Crosshair, such as his former squad, had a tendency to disappear.<ref name="The Solitary Clone" />

===Imperial Era===
[[File:WhenHairyMetSolo-SWBC51.png|thumb|left|250px|Han Solo met Chewbacca while imprisoned for alleged insubordination.]]
During the reign of the Galactic Empire, the Empire conducted a [[Mimban Campaign|brutal campaign]] to conquer the peaceful planet [[Mimban]] for its abundant natural resources. [[Han Solo]] served as an [[infantry]] [[corporal]] in the [[Imperial Army]] during that campaign, where he noted that "we're the invaders" against the native [[Mimbanese]]. After his commander [[Staz]] was killed, Solo retreated and encountered [[Tobias Beckett]] and his [[Beckett's gang|gang]] of [[Thief|thieves]]. Solo realized they were impersonating Imperial military personnel and asked them to take him with them, but instead Beckett reported Solo to his superiors for insubordination. Han was to be [[Death penalty|executed]] for his alleged insubordination by being fed to "[[Chewbacca|the beast]]" whom the Empire kept prisoner on Mimban. Han used his knowledge of [[Shyriiwook]] to convince Chewbacca to work with him to free themselves, and the pair joined Beckett's crew. Thus, Solo became a deserter.<ref name="Solo movie">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

Imperial [[Minister]] [[Maketh Tua]] of [[Lothal]] sought to defect from Imperial service after discovering the [[Lothal Jedi Temple|true reason]] for the Empire's interest in the backwater Outer Rim planet. To that end, she contacted the [[Spectres]], a local rebel cell, asking them for their help extracting her in exchange for the Imperial secret. However, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] used Tua to set a [[Siege of Lothal|trap]] for the rebels, and Tua was killed in a bombing which the rebels were blamed for before she could properly defect or give them her information.<ref name="The Siege of Lothal">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''</ref>

[[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]] began to doubt his loyalty to the Empire after an encounter with the rebel [[Garazeb Orrelios]] on the ice [[moon]] of [[Bahryn]].<ref name="The Honorable Ones">{{Rebels|The Honorable Ones}}</ref> This doubt eventually drove him to become a [[Fulcrum]] agent for the rebellion, leaking information to the [[Phoenix Cell]] about the Empire's doings in the [[Lothal sector]].<ref name="An Inside Man">{{Rebels|An Inside Man}}</ref> He covertly aided the rebel [[Sabine Wren]] when she [[Escape from Skystrike Academy|infiltrated]] [[Skystrike Academy]] to assist in the defection of cadets [[Wedge Antilles]], [[Derek Klivian]], and [[Rake Gahree]], although Gahree was killed during the first, unsuccessful attempt at escape. Wren was highly skeptical of Kallus' motives, but let him live as she, Antilles, and Klivian fled.<ref name="The Antilles Extraction">{{Rebels|The Antilles Extraction}}</ref> The Phoenix rebels, who included the Spectres, eventually found out that Kallus was Fulcrum.<ref name="An Inside Man" /> Kallus stayed inside the Empire as long as he could, but was eventually discovered to be a rebel spy by [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], and arrested after he attempted to alert the rebels to an [[Battle of Atollon|attack]] on their [[Chopper Base|base]]. During the battle, Kallus escaped the ''[[Chimaera]]'' by goading [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]] into ordering him thrown out an [[airlock]], being picked up in an [[escape pod]] by the ''[[Ghost]]''.<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

Several months after the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]], [[Imperial Navy]] [[lieutenant]] [[Thane Kyrell]] deserted the Empire after witnessing the [[Slavery|enslavement]] of the [[Bodach'i]], having been developing doubts. Kyrell invited his childhood friend [[Ciena Ree]] to join him, but, bound by the [[First Wavers|First Waver]] belief in never breaking a vow under any circumstances, she stayed with the Empire, choosing only to fake Kyrell's death on their [[homeworld]] [[Jelucan]] as a favor to him. Nine months after his desertion, Kyrell met members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] on [[Oulanne]], led by Wedge Antilles, and accepted an offer to join the rebellion.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>

Around the time of the [[Battle of Hoth]], Imperial soldier [[Kendy Idele]] and her entire unit mutinied against the Empire on [[Miriatin]]. Only a third of the mutineers, including Idele, survived, and they went on to join the Rebel Alliance. In the process, Idele was reunited with Kyrell, who had been a classmate of hers at the [[Royal Imperial Academy]] on Coruscant, and was both surprised and happy to see him as she had assumed him dead.<ref name="Lost Stars" />
