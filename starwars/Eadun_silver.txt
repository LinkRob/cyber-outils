'''Eadun silver''' was a valuable type of [[silver]] originating from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Eadu]] that could be used to make [[necklace]]s. In [[49 BBY]], [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Gehren Rand]] traded her [[Gehren Rand's family|family's]] [[Gehren Rand's necklace|Eadun silver necklace]] to the [[Black Sun]] [[Lieutenant (criminal)|lieutenant]] [[Nodrus Cay]] in exchange for passage offworld so that she could save her [[Gehren Rand's father|father]], whom she believed to be in danger. Cay took the item into her possession, later explaining its value to Rand's friend, fellow [[youngling]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].

==Description==
{{Quote|It's ''Eadun silver''. Worth more than a ''month'' of smuggling spice, I'll tell you.|Nodrus Cay, to Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="OW1" />}}
Eadun silver was a rare<ref name="SWcom" /> and valuable type of [[silver]] with a [[Color|silvery-blue]] color that could be used to make [[necklace]]s. The substance originated from<ref name="OW1" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Eadu]].<ref name="SWcom" /> A small jewelry-sized amount was worth more than a [[Standard month|month]] of [[spice]] [[Smuggler|smuggling]]<ref name="OW1" /> by [[49 BBY]].<ref name="Date">The flashbacks in [[Obi-Wan 1|''Obi-Wan'' 1]] occurred when [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was eight [[Standard year|years]] old. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates Kenobi's [[Reproduction|birth]] to [[57 BBY]], the flashbacks must have taken place in [[49 BBY]].</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|Is ''that'' what you've been hiding under your mat?''"<br />"''It's from my ''family''&mdash;''"<br />"''You ''know'' you can't keep it. Master Yoda said we ''must'' avoid attachments&mdash;''"<br />"''Well, I'm getting rid of it ''now''. [[File:ObiWanGehrenRand-ObiWan1.png|thumb|left|250px|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Gehren Rand argue about the latter's Eadun silver necklace.]]
At some point during the [[Republic Era]], [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Gehren Rand]] was given a [[Gehren Rand's necklace|necklace]] made of the material by one of her [[Gehren Rand's family|family]] members,<ref name="OW1" /> whom she cherished dearly.<ref name="SWcom" /> She kept<ref name="OW1" /> the dearly held possession<ref name="SWcom" /> under her [[sleep]]ing mat in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="OW1" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> planet [[Coruscant]], despite being encouraged to not keep sentimental items.<ref name="OW1" /> In 49 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Rand began having [[Force vision|dreams]] of her [[Gehren Rand's father|father]] suffering and in pain, becoming determined to save him. Rand hired [[Nodrus Cay]]&mdash;a ranking [[Lieutenant (criminal)|lieutenant]] in the [[Black Sun]] criminal syndicate&mdash;to take her offworld to save her [[parent]], offering her family's Eadun silver necklace as [[Currency|compensation]]. Cay agreed, taking the jewelry into her possession within an [[Standard hour|hour]] of their initial meeting.<ref name="OW1" />

As Rand departed the [[Jedi temple|Jedi Temple]], her fellow [[youngling]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] followed her into the [[Coruscant Underworld]] and met with the lieutenant. Cay explained the terms of her deal with Rand to Kenobi, holding up the Eadun silver necklace in her possession and expounding on its value. Kenobi berated his friend for owning the item, mentioning that [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] would say it was an attachment, going against the [[Jedi Code]]. Not long after, having discovered that both children were [[Force-sensitive]], the lieutenant attempted to force the pair into her service, commenting that she had only accepted Eadun silver as [[transport]] payment when she thought Rand was a "street [[rat]]." Kenobi and Rand quickly made their escape from the situation, leaving the necklace behind.<ref name="OW1" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|For the pendant, there was no specific description about it other than it's made from rare and valuable Eadun silver.|Ario Anindito|<ref name="SWcom" />}}
[[File:Eadunsilver-conceptfull.jpg|thumb|right|215px|Ario Anindito's concept designs for Gehren Rand's Eadun silver necklace]]
Eadun silver first appeared in a flashback in the [[Obi-Wan 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan (comic series)|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series, which was written by [[Christopher Cantwell]], illustrated by [[Ario Anindito]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="OW1" /> on [[May 4]], [[2022]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/101187/star_wars_obi-wan_2022_1|text=Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi (2022) #1}}</ref> In a [[StarWars.com]] article released the same day, Anindito revealed several conceptual designs of his for Rand's pendant, as the script's ambiguity allowed him to customize its design. He mentioned that each one featured Eadu rock reliefs similar to those visible in<ref name="SWcom" /> the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology]] film,<ref name="YearByYear">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="SWcom" />

Out of all the models shown in the article, the artist indicated that the third&mdash;a teardrop model for the item, which was labeled "tears shape w/ Eadu rocks relief"&mdash;was eventually chosen to reflect Rand's tears and feelings of longing as the pendant was given to her by someone she loved deeply. Anindito also felt that the necklace was a dearly held possession of the youngling's due to the memories it evoked, considering it a piece of lore that knitted together her ''[[Star Wars]]'' backstory well.<ref name="SWcom" />
