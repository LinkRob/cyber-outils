Between [[232 BBY]] and [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] of the [[Jedi Order]] '''engaged''' the [[Drengir]]&mdash;[[Sentience|sentient]] [[carnivorous plant]]s who [[Force power|used]] the [[dark side of the Force]]&mdash;on the [[planet]] [[Selvernis]]. The engagement was part of the Jedi Order's response to the [[Drengir crisis|Drengir threat]], after the dark side [[Force-sensitive|users]] had been seeded across [[the galaxy]] by the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s to distract the [[Jedi]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> when [[Cair San Tekka]], a member of the [[San Tekka clan]], presented Kriss with several potential terminus points for the plague known as the [[blight]] on a [[map]], she noticed that Selvernis was one such point. The Jedi Master then realized a connection: she had personally fought the Drengir on Selvernis before the blight's appearance, recalling the battle and suggested that the planet could be significant in understanding the blight's origins and spread.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

==Behind the scenes==
The engagement on Selvernis was mentioned in the [[2024]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref>
