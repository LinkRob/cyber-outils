The '''Asteroid Dreadnought''' was a rumored [[Imperial Navy]] [[dreadnought]] that was camouflaged as a [[Asteroid|space rock]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Note">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' establishes the report mentioning the Asteroid Dreadnought was created between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] learning about the [[Galactic Empire]] having a [[superweapon]] and the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. The fourth arc of [[Andor Season 2|''Andor'' Season 2]], which takes place in [[1 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:AndorDate|here]], depicts the Alliance learning about the Empire's superweapon. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' to 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. Therefore, the report must have been created in 1 BBY.</ref> [[Colonel]] [[Haxen Delto]] of [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service]] wrote a report to [[Chief of Intelligence|Chief of Rebel Intelligence]] [[General]] [[Airen Cracken]] on Imperial [[superweapon]] projects, and included a section in the report regarding the rumors of the dreadnought.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

In the report, Delto stated that he would not dismiss the rumors of the [[starship]], despite noting that the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] source inside [[Patriim]] was unreliable. The report was included in ''[[The Rebel Files]]'', a data repository containing a non-electronic copy of sensitive information which would be [[Excavation on Durkteel|uncovered]] [[Standard decade|decades]] later on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Durkteel]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Asteroid Dreadnought was first mentioned in the boxed set and reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', which was released in [[2017]] and authored by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> The dreadnought originated in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] as the ''[[Eye of Palpatine]]'', introduced in the novel [[Children of the Jedi (novel)|''Children of the Jedi'']] that released in [[1995]].<ref name="Children">[[Children of the Jedi (novel)|''Children of the Jedi'']]</ref>
