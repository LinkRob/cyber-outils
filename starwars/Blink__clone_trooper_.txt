'''Blink''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served in [[Cardiff Baye's battalion|a battalion]] under [[Clone Commander]] [[Maximus (Clone Commander)|Maximus]] and [[Jedi General]] [[Cardiff Baye]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Blink participated with the unit in the [[battle on Covenanon]] and [[Invasion of Katou|a mission]] to [[Katou]]. During the first night on Katou, Blink held the night watch after he had lost a competition with his brothers. Shortly into his shift, he was killed by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] and dragged away. The next day, Blink's fellow clones searched for him and were lured by Grievous into a cave. Like his team members, he wore [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] adorned with [[Color|light red]] markings. Additionally, he also [[Tattoo|had]] two red stripes on his chin.<ref name="HS:Grievous" />
