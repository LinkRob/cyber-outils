"'''Black Spire: Return to a Shattered Planet'''" is a short story by [[Delilah S. Dawson]] that was published in the paperback of ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'' on [[June 30]], [[2020]]. It features [[Vi Moradi]], [[Chewbacca]], [[Kriki]], and [[Pook]] as they travel to [[Parnassos]] to rescue [[Siv]] and [[Torbi]].
