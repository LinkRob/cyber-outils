An '''armillary sphere''' was an ancient instrument consisting of an arrangement of metal rings used to show the apparent progression of [[constellation]]s through the sky. The [[gangster]] [[Dryden Vos]] of the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate possessed several such devices, including [[Alderaanian armillary sphere|one]] from [[Alderaan]], which he kept in the private [[Dryden's study|study]] of his [[Kalevala]]n [[yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]''.<ref name="Official Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
