'''CT-720''' was a [[bounty hunter]] who once worked with fellow [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunters]] [[BT-823]] and [[Beilert Valance]]. Valance was later reminded of his work with CT-720, BT-823, and a few other hunters in a conversation with the former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Cavic]] sometime prior to the [[Hunt for Vader|hunt]] for the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]<ref name="Target Vader 6" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Cavic called the bounty hunters a "solid bunch."<ref name="Target Vader 6" />

==Behind the scenes==
CT-720 was mentioned in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Target Vader 6|''Target Vader'' 6]], which was written by [[Robbie Thompson]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Target Vader 6" /> on [[December 11]], [[2019]]. An excerpt of the comic, which included the mention of CT-720, was released on [[StarWars.com]] beforehand on [[December 4]] of that year.<ref name="Excerpt">{{SW|url=news/doctor-aphra-40-target-vader-6-exclusive|text=Doctor Aphra Says Goodbye in ''Doctor Aphra'' #40 - Exclusive}}</ref>
