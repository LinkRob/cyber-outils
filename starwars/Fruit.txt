{{Quote|Fruit always tastes nice when it's ripe. But it can't stay like that. Every nice piece of fruit will rot on the branch if it hangs there long enough.|[[Glen Taffral]]|<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>}}
'''Fruits''' were a popular type of [[Foodstuff|food]] in [[the galaxy]]. They were eaten raw or cooked in dishes and were considered a treat and a priced delicacy during times of war.<ref name="Visual">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Fruit contained [[seed]]s<ref name="Empire's End" /> and grew on [[tree]]s<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> and [[plant]]s.<ref name="Galactic Bake-Off">''[[The High Republic Adventures: Galactic Bake-Off Spectacular]]''</ref> A much loved dish was [[jogan fruitcake]], which called for the main ingredient, [[jogan fruit]], to be both baked in the batter and used as a topping.<ref name="Visual" /> Fruit could also be dried and eaten as a snack.<ref name="Aftermath" /> A proverb used by the time of<ref name="Galactic Bake-Off" /> [[232 BBY]]<ref name="Bake-Off Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the flashbacks of ''[[The High Republic Adventures: Galactic Bake-Off Spectacular]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> went "the plant that stays hidden in the soil never bears delicious fruit."<ref name="Galactic Bake-Off" />

Fruit could be used as a non-lethal weapon; when the [[First Order]] occupied the refueling station ''[[Colossus]]'', the local residents protested by pelting the [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] with fruit at the [[Colossus marketplace|marketplace]].<ref name="New Trooper">{{Resistance|The New Trooper}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Rey_with_fruit.png|thumb|left|240px|A Fuji apple with Romanesco broccoli protruding out of it.]]
The fruits appearing in the scene where [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] are dining in [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] include a [[Wikipedia:Pear|pear]], a [[Wikipedia:Pineapple|pineapple]], an [[Wikipedia:Orange (fruit)|orange]], and a [[Wikipedia:Carambola|starfruit]].<ref name="EII">{{Film|II}}</ref>

Among the fruits [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] is eating at [[Takodana Castle]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] is a [[Wikipedia:Fuji (apple)|Fuji apple]] with [[Wikipedia:Romanesco broccoli|Romanesco broccoli]] protruding out of it. The bowl of fruit sitting on [[Maz Kanata]]'s table include a [[Wikipedia:Cucumis metuliferus|horned melon]] and a [[Wikipedia:Passiflora ligularis|sweet granadilla]].<ref name="EVII">{{Film|VII}}</ref>
