'''Brewhada''' was a [[Wookiee]] toymaker who was captured by the [[Rodian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Greedo]]. After being tricked by [[Bennun Glau]] to capture Brewhada instead of the [[Sunburst gem]], Greedo presented the Wookiee to a confused [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]]. Greedo, making up an excuse not to be [[Death|killed]] by Jabba, informed the [[Hutt]] that Brewhada could make toys for [[Rotta]]. The Hutt laughed at Greedo and Brewhada started to work for Jabba.<ref name="Hyperspace Stories 6" />
