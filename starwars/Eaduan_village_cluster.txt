The '''Eaduan village cluster''' was located on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Eadu]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="UVG" /> Although Eadu had a hostile and rainy climate,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> the southern hemisphere, where the [[City|village]] cluster was located, had fertile soil and was less inhospitable than the northern hemisphere. The village cluster had about 2.5 million inhabitants,<ref name="Comprehensive Guide" /> nearly all of Eadu's population. The villagers were mostly [[farmer]]s and [[nerf herder]]s,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> which Eadu was known for.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|eadu|Eadu}}</ref> The inhabitants were unaware of the significance of their world,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> which was the location of a [[Eadu Energy Conversion Laboratory|top-secret laboratory]] where research was conducted to create the [[Mk I Superlaser|superlaser]] for the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first Death Star]].<ref name="UVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Eaduan village cluster was first mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="UVG" />
