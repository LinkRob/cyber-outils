'''Darth Devastator''', born '''Dev Greebling''' and nicknamed '''Darth Dev''', was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]]<ref name="Part One" /> [[Gender|male]] individual who was the older brother of the [[nerf herder]] [[Sig Greebling]]. Alongside Sig, Dev discovered the artifact known as [[The Cornerstone of the Galaxy]] in a [[Jedi temple]], resulting in [[the galaxy]] becoming mixed up.<ref name="Part One" /> In this twisted version of the galaxy, Dev Greebling was the [[Sith Lord]] commanding the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]], Darth Devastator. After finding himself in the new galaxy, Sig approached him upon seeing him, unaware that he was no longer the brother he knew. Darth Devastator captured him, along with [[Yesi Scala]]. Devastator ordered his apprentice, [[Darth]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], to help interrogate the two, until [[Bobarian Afol|Jedi Bob]] came to rescue them.
