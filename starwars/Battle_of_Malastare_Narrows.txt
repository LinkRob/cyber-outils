The '''Battle of Malastare Narrows''' took place at [[Malastare Narrows]] in [[34 BBY]]. The [[Corporate Alliance]] [[Admiral]] [[Trench]] [[blockade]]d the [[planet]] [[Malastare]], and a [[Galactic Republic]] [[fleet]], in which the [[human]] [[Wullf Yularen]] served as one of the leading [[officer]]s, attacked Trench's [[Corporate fleet (Malastare)|forces]]. Trench repulsed them with ease, causing significant losses to the Republic. Ultimately, the issue was settled when a [[Kep-She's task force|task force]] [[Commanding officer|led]] by [[Jedi Master]] [[Kep-She]] destroyed [[Trench's flagship]] and supposedly [[Death|killed]] Trench. However, Trench survived the battle and later faced Yularen at the [[Battle of Christophsis]] in [[22 BBY]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|A corporate fleet was blockading Malastare, a fleet led by Trench.|Wullf Yularen|audio=AFleetLedByTrench-CaM.flac|<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />}}
In [[34 BBY]], over a [[Standard decade|decade]] before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Timelines" /> a [[Corporate Alliance]] [[Corporate fleet (Malastare)|fleet]]<ref name="HC 78" /> [[Commanding officer|led]] by the [[Harch]] [[Admiral]] [[Trench]], the Alliance's best commander, formed a [[blockade]] around the [[planet]] [[Malastare]]. An attempt was made to resolve the situation [[diplomat]]ically but was unsuccessful.<ref name="XWing94" />

==The battle==
{{Quote|He tore our ships apart. We barely escaped with our lives. In the end, a Jedi-led task force moved in to settle the matter. Trench's ship was destroyed and we assumed he went down with it.''"<br />"''Are you sure it's him?''"<br />"''It's possible. Not every captain goes down with his ship.|Wullf Yularen and Anakin Skywalker|audio=HeToreOurShipsApart-CaM.flac|<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />}}
[[File:AdmiralTrench-TCWs2BR3.png|thumb|left|200px|Admiral Trench led the Corporate fleet during the battle.]]
A [[Republic Navy|naval]] [[fleet]] of [[Galactic Republic]] [[starship]]s, in which the [[human]] [[Wullf Yularen]] served as one of the leading [[officer]]s, attacked Trench's fleet at [[Malastare Narrows]]<ref name="XWing94" /> on several occasions. Trench pushed the Republic back multiple [[time]]s and inflicted significant losses<ref name="HC 78" /> before claiming victory<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> with contemptuous ease,<ref name="XWing94" /> destroying most of Yularen's forces<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> and thus becoming a recognized [[Navy|naval]] officer.<ref name="USWNE" />

Eventually, the matter was resolved when a [[Kep-She's task force|task force]]<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> led by [[Jedi Master]] [[Kep-She]]<ref name="C&MTG" /> moved in on the Corporate fleet. The [[Jedi Order|Jedi]]-led [[task force]] destroyed [[Trench's flagship]], with the admiral being presumed [[Death|dead]],<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> and restored peace to Malastare.<ref name="XWing94" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|The last time I saw that symbol, well, let's just say I lost a lot of good men that day.|Wullf Yularen, on Trench's unmistakable insignia|audio=LostALotOfGoodMen-CaM.flac|<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />}}
In the battle's aftermath, Yularen left the navy and joined the [[Senate Bureau of Intelligence]], with his [[Dream|nightmares]] being haunted by Trench, the individual most akin to a nemesis for Yularen.<ref name="XWing94" /> However, Trench revealed that he had survived the battle when he led the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Christophsis blockade|blockade]] at the [[Battle of Christophsis]] during the Clone Wars<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> where he again fought against Yularen. During the Battle of Christophsis, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] recalled Trench's supposed death at the battle, and Yularen relayed his experience of the battle to Skywalker.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Malastare Narrows was first mentioned in "[[Cat and Mouse]],"<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> the sixteenth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|second season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', which aired on [[March 26]], [[2010]].<ref name="Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode216.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Cat and Mouse}}</ref> The [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 78|seventy-eighth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]'' magazine,<ref name="HC 78" /> published around [[July 12]], [[2016]],<ref name="France">{{HCdatesFR|78}}</ref> and written by [[Iain Lowson]],<ref name="HC-Credits">{{EmailCite|email=File:HCSV-Email-2025-07-26.png|author=[[Iain Lowson]]|date=2025-07-26}}</ref> alternatively identified the battle as the Battle of the Malastare Narrows.<ref name="HC 78" />
