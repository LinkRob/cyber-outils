The '''"Impact" repulsor cannon''', also known simply as the '''Impact cannon''', was a model of [[repulsor cannon]] manufactured by [[Nova Design Arms]]. Designed as an alternative to other forms of non-lethal [[weapon]]ry, the Impact [[cannon]] utilized a modified [[speeder bike]] [[repulsorlift]] to fire tight, narrow beams of energy to disrupt a target's actions at medium ranges.<ref name="Gadgets" /> However, this effect only lasted as long as the beam remained focused on the target.<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> Nova Design [[engineer]]s installed a short-ranged [[Grappling hook|grapple]] onto the weapon to help stabilize it. The Impact cannon's design was inspired by makeshift [[repulsor gun]]s.<ref name="Gadgets" />

Costing 1,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and considered inferior to other non-lethal forms of weaponry, the "Impact" repulsor cannon became popular among [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement agencies]] and [[bounty hunter]]s who were concerned about collateral damage while trying to subdue a difficult target.<ref name="Gadgets" /> In addition, the weapon's ability to pacify targets allowed [[bounty]] [[hunting]] teams to use unconventional tactics to capture their targets.<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> The Impact cannon was particularly helpful against [[Wookiee]]s, [[Trandoshan]]s, and [[Gamorrean]]s.<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Impact" repulsor cannon was first mentioned in [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']],<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> a [[2017]] supplement for the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="A Fistful of Credits">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/2/2/a-fistful-of-credits|text=A Fistful of Credits}}</ref>
