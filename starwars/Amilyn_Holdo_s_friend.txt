An individual was the '''friend''' of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]]<ref name="Star Wars 29" /> [[captain]]<ref name="Star Wars 22">[[Star Wars (2020) 22|''Star Wars'' (2020) 22]]</ref> [[Amilyn Holdo]] and owned a [[Amilyn Holdo's friend's mansion|mansion]] on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Spira]].<ref name="Star Wars 29" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-marvel|text=SWCE 2023: Marvel Reveals ''Star Wars: Dark Droids'' Horror Event and More}} states that the events of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars (2020)|''Star Wars'']], including [[Star Wars (2020) 29|''Star Wars'' (2020) 29]], take place between the events of {{Film|V}} and {{Film|VI}}, which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Holdo borrowed the mansion from<ref name="Star Wars 29" /> her<ref name="Star Wars 22" /> friend for a few [[Standard day|days]] so she could attend an [[Auction on Spira|auction]] to acquire a [[strikeship]] that had belonged to the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization as part of a plan to help solve the Rebel Alliance's [[fuel]] supply issues. Holdo also brought fellow rebels [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], [[Lobot]], and [[Chewbacca]] with her, stating that they needed a break from the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Star Wars 29" />

After the rebels lost the auction, they [[Thief|stole]] the [[Starship|ship's]] [[Path engine]] device and returned to the mansion but were tracked by a<ref name="Star Wars 29" /> [[Unidentified TIE squadron (Spira)|squadron]]<ref name="Star Wars 30">[[Star Wars (2020) 30|''Star Wars'' (2020) 30]]</ref> of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. As the rebels fled, the [[TIE series|TIEs]] destroyed the mansion, prompting Holdo to exclaim her friend was going to "kill her" for the destruction of the mansion.<ref name="Star Wars 29" />

==Behind the scenes==
Amilyn Holdo's friend was mentioned in the [[Star Wars (2020) 29|twenty-ninth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars (2020)|''Star Wars'']]. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 29" /> on [[November 2]], [[2022]]. Prior to the release of the comic, the friend was mentioned in a [https://www.starwars.com/news/marvel-star-wars-29-exclusive-preview preview article] of the issue that was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[October 27]] of the same year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-29-exclusive-preview|text=The Rebel Alliance Looks to the Nihil for Help in Marvel's ''Star Wars'' #29 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref>
