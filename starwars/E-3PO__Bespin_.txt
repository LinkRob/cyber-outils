'''E-3PO'''<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|161|Jedi Challenge!}}</ref> was a [[Color|silver]]<ref name="The Empire Strikes Back" /> [[3PO-series protocol droid]]<ref name="So You Want to Be a Jedi?" /> with yellow [[photoreceptor]]s who was present in [[Cloud City]], a [[tibanna]] gas [[mining]] colony that floated in the [[Life Zone]] of the [[planet]] [[Bespin]], shortly after the [[Battle of Hoth]].<ref name="The Empire Strikes Back" /> E-3PO was equipped with a [[TechSpan I module]] which allowed him to directly access Imperial networks.<ref name="SWE CGSWG" /> After exiting a room of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s, he was greeted by a fellow 3PO unit known as [[C-3PO]]<ref name="The Empire Strikes Back" /> but responded with a [[Huttese]] [[E chu ta|insult]]<ref name="So You Want to Be a Jedi?" /> and continued down the hallway. After calling the protocol [[droid]] rude, C-3PO heard an [[R2-series astromech droid]] inside the room that E-3PO had exited and, believing that it was his friend [[R2-D2]], entered to investigate the sound. However, instead of finding R2, he was blasted to pieces by stormtroopers.<ref name="The Empire Strikes Back" />

==Behind the scenes==
E-3PO was portrayed by [[Chris Parsons]] in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], who also portrayed [[4-LOM]], [[R-3PO]], and [[K-3PO]] in the same film.
