'''Basso Mak''' was an [[Utai]] warrior who worked with the [[Kendoh Gang]], led by [[Kendoh Voss]]. While on the [[Wayward Comet]] [[Fuel|refueling]] [[Space station|station]], [[Remex Io]], a member of the crew, spotted the [[Resistance]] operative [[Finn]]. Io reported to Voss, who contacted the [[First Order]] asking to claim the [[bounty]] on him.<ref name="Allegiance 1" /> Eventually, he was killed by Finn during the [[mission to the moon of Avedot]].<ref name="Allegiance 4">[[Allegiance 4|''Allegiance'' 4]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Basso Mak first appeared in [[Allegiance 1|''Allegiance'' 1]], a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Luke Ross]],<ref name="Allegiance 1" /> and published on [[October 9]], [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/76701/journey_to_star_wars_the_rise_of_skywalker_-_allegiance_2019_1|text=Journey to Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker - Allegiance (2019) #1}}</ref> Mak's surname was not revealed in the comic itself, but was given in a behind-the-scenes section.<ref name="Allegiance 1" />
