The '''Corubalni Hoard''' was a fabled treasure hoard that shared its name with the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[scout]] [[Arhul Corubalni]], who first discovered the [[planet]] [[Vandor]] where the [[Corubalni Spaceport]]<ref name="Vandor" /> and [[Corubalni Pass]] were located.<ref name="Solo OG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> The [[big game hunter]] [[Iothene Jacontro]] searched for on the [[planet]] [[Vandor]]. During his hunt, Jacontro released [[wampa]]s into the Vandor outback as trophies for others hunters and then visited [[the Lodge]] at [[Fort Ypso]] with his [[Jacontro's gang|gang]] for the first [[time]]. While staying overnight at the Lodge, the gang got into a fight with the [[Valarine School]] of [[Selkath]] [[pirate]]s and in the morning, Jacontro woke to find half his men [[Death|dead]] and the other half arrested.<ref name="Vandor" />

Needing a new crew, the hunter hired a large number of the world's deadbeats, swindlers, liars, and [[Thief|thieves]] before setting out to search for the hoard, ignoring warnings from the Lodge's [[bartender]], [[Tivoche Bilure]], that the hoard did not exist. The group failed to find the hoard and Jacontro gave up on treasure hunting and returned to the Lodge after his [[Limb|arm]] froze solid and he lost his whole gang. He later became obsessed with tracking down a [[vastadon]] he believed lived on the world and Bilure wrote about both obsessions in a [[Midnight's book|book]] he wrote about his [[time]] working at the Lodge.<ref name="Vandor" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Corubalni Hoard was first mentioned in the [[2018]] replica journal ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]'', which was written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Vandor" />
