[[Standard month|Months]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], the '''Battle of Nag Ubdur''' took place when [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Defense Force|forces]] attempted to liberate the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]]-held [[Planet|world]] of [[Nag Ubdur]]. The [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] in the [[Ubdur system]] were cut off from the rest of the Empire, and thus would fight with an increased desperation. The Imperials were initially able to inflict heavy casualties on the Republic forces, including a bloody [[Ambush at Govneh Ridge|ambush]] at [[Govneh Ridge]], but eventually would be forced to retreat to defensive entrenchments ten [[kilometer]]s away from the [[Ubdurian]] [[merchant]] [[city]] of [[Binjai-Tin]]. In the process, they razed the city and [[Massacre at Binjai-Tin|massacred]] its inhabitants. During the campaign, the Imperials were desperate enough to use their [[TIE series|starfighters]] in [[Death|suicide]] attacks against New Republic positions.

==Prelude==
During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Nag Ubdur]] came under [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] rule. The planet was home to the native [[Ubdurian]]s along with [[Keldar]] and [[Artiodac]] [[refugee]]s. Under Imperial rule Nag Ubdur was exploited by the Empire since its bedrock was flecked with [[zersium]], an [[ore]] used in the manufacture of [[durasteel]]. The Empire strip-mined the planet and regarded it as a valuable asset during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="LD" />

Imperial rule on Nag Ubdur was harsh and exploitative. The residents of the Ubdurian [[merchant]] [[city]] of [[Binjai-Tin]] were not allowed to carry [[blaster]]s and had to give a percentage of their income to the Empire. Following the [[Battle of Endor]], the planet became a battleground between the Empire and the [[New Republic]], the successor government to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="LD" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] launched a brutal counterattack in order to secure Nag Ubdur's supply of zersium. This coincided with the decline of Imperial rule throughout [[the galaxy]].<ref name="LD" />

==War of Attrition==
{{Quote|Right. We encountered Imperial forces on the Govneh Ridge&mdash;it's a, like a plate shift where the ground bulges, and these tall crystals grow alongside it, and the Imperials were&hellip;they were waiting for us. They came out of nowhere. It was intense. My squad leader, Hachinka, she got it in the neck&mdash;a blaster shot hit her and the spray caught me in the face and&mdash;|Rorith Khadur recalling the ambush|<ref name="LD" />}}
As the Empire and New Republic battled for control of the planet, the Empire [[Massacre at Binjai-Tin|massacred]] the inhabitants of Binjai-Tin as part of a scorched earth policy. Members of the [[New Republic Thirty-First]], a unit of the [[New Republic Defense Force]], were [[Ambush at Govneh Ridge|ambushed]] by Imperial forces on the [[Govneh Ridge]]; a plate shift where tall [[crystal]]s grew. The unit's [[squad leader]] [[Hachinka]] was shot in the neck during the fighting. Her comrades including the [[Kupohan]] [[Private]] [[Rorith Khadur]] managed to evacuate her to the New Republic lines. Despite losing many men, the Thirty-First managed to secure the ridge.<ref name="LD" />

The following [[Standard day|day]], the New Republic Thirty-First arrived at the city of Binjai-Tin to discover that its inhabitants had been [[Death|massacred]] by the retreating Imperial forces. Private Khadur later recounted his story to the [[Queen of the Core Network]] [[journalist]] [[Tracene Kane]] and the [[Trandoshan]] camera operator [[Lug]]. Sensing Khadur's distress, Lug gave him the [[tooth]] of a [[zlagfiend]] as a good-luck token. Having finished recording Khadur's story, Tracene and Lug attempted to find a guild tower to broadcast their recording.<ref name="LD" />

Before Tracene and Lug could reach the guild tower, a wave of Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] and [[TIE/IN interceptor|TIE Interceptors]] launched a [[Death|suicide]] attack on the New Republic positions in Binjai-Tin. Numerous New Republic personnel were killed and maimed during the attack. Despite losing an arm, Khadur managed to hold on to Lug's gift. However, Lug was crushed to death beneath the [[solar collector]] of a [[TIE series|TIE fighter]]. Tracene survived the attack but was greatly affected by the loss of her friend Lug. In Lug's honor, she began recording a transmission dedicated to his memory.<ref name="LD" />

==Aftermath==
The Battle of Nag Ubdur was part of the New Republic's [[Galaxy|galactic]]-wide rebellion against the remnants of the Empire. By 5 ABY, the Imperial presence in the Outer Rim had been reduced to the remote [[Exterior]] [[sector]] and the worlds of [[Belladoon]], [[Korrus]], and [[Zhadalene]]. Imperial losses [[Siege of Arkanis|on]] [[Arkanis]] [[Defense of Kuat|and]] [[Kuat]] convinced [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] to secretly contact [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]], [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], and [[Commodore]] [[Kyrsta Agate]] to propose [[Imperial–Republic peace talks|peace talks]] between the two rival governments. These peace talks were however a ruse by [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]] to [[assassin]]ate the New Republic leadership on [[Chandrila]]. Following the [[Liberation Day (New Republic)|Liberation Day]] [[Attack on Chandrila|attacks]],<ref name="LD" /> New Republic and Imperial forces [[Battle of Jakku|clashed]] at [[Jakku]].<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Nag Ubdur and the Massacre at Binjai-Tin first appeared in [[Chuck Wendig]]'s [[2016]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', the second installment in [[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy|The Aftermath Trilogy]]. The battle is told through the point of view of Tracene Kane, a minor character.
