The '''Battle of Argul''' was a battle fought during the early [[Standard year/Legends|years]] of the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].

==The battle==
The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]] blockaded the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Argul]] and forced its people to surrender to them. The [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] responded by sending [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone troopers]] to recapture the world. However, they soon began to lose ground and the planet's [[City/Legends|cities]] became the sites for much [[Blood/Legends|bloody]] fighting. The Republic began an evacuation of the cities, while at the same time being forced to fall back. [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] had been participating in the battle and he left to try and find reinforcements. While he was gone, the Separatist [[Commander/Legends|Commander]] [[Drogen Hosh]], who was commanding their forces there, moved his troops in position for a final assault, and he sent an offer to a group of [[Padawan/Legends|Jedi Padawans]] to let them surrender to him. However, soon after, Yoda arrived with reinforcements, turning the tide of the battle in the Republic's favor.
