"'''Adelphi Jukebox'''" was a musical track played in the background in "[[Chapter 21: The Pirate]]" of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.<ref name="Chapter 21">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 21: The Pirate}}</ref> It was written in [[Olys Corellisi]].<ref name="Adelphi jukebox tweet">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1678184134798106625|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=This did not end up getting covered anywhere else, so it seems okay enough to share. The song on the Adelphi jukebox was written in English by the show staff and I translated it into Olys Corellisi — Old Corellian. It required a fair amount of invention but that was the intent.|archivedate=20230712085703}}</ref>

The song was first written in English by the staff of ''The Mandalorian'', and [[Pablo Hidalgo]] translated it to Old Corellian, which required a fair amount of invention from him.<ref name="Adelphi jukebox tweet" />
