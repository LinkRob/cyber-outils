'''Dewback troopers''',<ref name="Star Wars: Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> also known as '''Dewback Riders''',<ref name="Galactic Defense">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''</ref> were [[Desert stormtrooper|sandtroopers]] who utilized [[dewback]]s as mounts. They used jolts of electricity to control the beast, forcing them to batter down the property of those opposed to the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Star Wars: Commander" /> In addition, Dewback troopers were known to utilize [[flamethrower]]s<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> like the [[CR-24 Flame Rifle]].<ref name="Flame Rifle">{{Legion|set=Dewback Rider Unit Expansion|cardname=CR-24 Flame Rifle}}</ref> They could also used [[T-21 light repeating blaster]],<ref name="Repeating Blaster">{{Legion|set=Dewback Rider Unit Expansion|cardname=T-21 Blaster Rifle}}</ref> or the [[RT-97C heavy blaster rifle]].<ref name="Heavy Blaster Rifle">{{Legion|set=Dewback Rider Unit Expansion|cardname=RT-97C Blaster Rifle}}</ref>
