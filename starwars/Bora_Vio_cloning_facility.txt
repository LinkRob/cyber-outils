The '''Bora Vio cloning facility'''<ref name="Bounty Lost Concept">{{SW|url=bounty-lost-concept-art-gallery|text="Bounty Lost" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> was maintained and later abandoned by the [[Kaminoan]]s on [[Bora Vio]]. Early in the [[Imperial Era]], Kaminoan [[Prime Minister]] [[Lama Su]] arranged a rendezvous at the facility between his [[aide]] [[Taun We]] and the [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] so Bane could hand over the young [[Cloning|clone]] girl [[Omega]], whom he had retrieved. However, another hunter, [[Fennec Shand]], was sent to the facility to [[Rescue of Omega|prevent]] the exchange from happening, resulting in a fight between the two hunters, which ended when Omega was rescued by [[Clone Force 99]].<ref name="Bounty Lost" />

The Bora Vio cloning facility included [[embryo tube]]s, which contained corpses of sentient beings judged to be "failed experiments."<ref name="Bounty Lost" />
