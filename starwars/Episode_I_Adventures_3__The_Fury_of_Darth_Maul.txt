'''''Episode I Adventures 3: The Fury of Darth Maul''''' is a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] junior novel written by [[Ryder Windham]]. It is the third volume of the ''[[Star Wars: Episode I Adventures]]'' series, and it was published by [[Scholastic]] in [[November]] [[1999]], with an accompanying gamebook. A sequel, [[Episode I Adventures 4: Jedi Emergency|''Jedi Emergency'']], was published the following [[December|month]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Someone has stolen something that belongs to Darth Sidious.''

''Now he wants it back.''

''And when a Sith Lord wants something, there's only one person he can trust to get it &ndash;''

''Another Sith Lord.''
