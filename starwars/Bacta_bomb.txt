A '''bacta bomb''' was a hand-thrown device consisting of a canister that contained a synthetic healing fluid called [[bacta]]. Upon impact, the [[Grenade|bomb]] vaporized into a mist that healed those within range. Both the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] used such devices during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Behind the scenes==
The bacta bomb was first introduced in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''{{'s}} expansion pack, [[Star Wars Battlefront#Star Wars Battlefront: Outer Rim|Outer Rim]]. The bomb is obtained through the aptly titled [[Hutt]] contract [[Medic]], and will unlock after the player has reached an objective score of 5,000.
