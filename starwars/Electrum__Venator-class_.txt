The '''''Electrum''''' was a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] that served in the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. Originally stationed at the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]]'s [[Ryloth deepdock|deepdock]], the [[Star Destroyer]] was loaned out to serve in the [[Sentinel Base's flotilla|flotilla]] of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] installation known as [[Sentinel Base]]. In [[14 BBY]], while under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Captain]] [[Burque]], the ''Electrum'' was chosen by Sentinel's commanding [[Imperial officer|officer]], [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], to react to a [[distress signal]] from the nearby [[Rampart Station]].

Tarkin, correctly assuming the signal was fake, ordered Burque to take the ''Electrum'' to the edge of [[Sentinel Base system|Sentinel Base's system]] via [[microjump]]. After Burque complied, Sentinel Base was [[Attack on Sentinel Base|attacked]] by a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell's]] [[Berch Teller's warship|warship]] and [[Droid starfighter|droid fighters]]. Upon Tarkin's command, the ''Electrum'' returned, with its [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] [[Starfighter combat|engaging]] the enemy [[starfighter]]s. Although the rebel [[warship]] fled the scene shortly after the Venator's return, Burque's crew later managed to salvage debris from the [[droid]] fighters for analysis.

The ''Electrum'' was a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] equipped with [[sublight engine]]s and a [[hyperdrive]], and armed with [[weapon]]s systems. It carried [[squadron]]s of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] aboard its dorsal [[hangar]] bay, and additionally featured a [[holoprojector]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

==History==
===On loan to Sentinel Base===
{{Quote|Parking lot is nearly empty, sir. We have the ''Salliche'', the ''Fremond'', and the ''Electrum''.|A comm specialist lists Sentinel Base's available starships to Wilhuff Tarkin|audio=Sentinel-parking.flac|<ref name="Tarkin" />}}
[[File:Ryloth-Homecoming.png|thumb|left|200px|The ''Electrum'' was on loan to Sentinel Base from the planet Ryloth's (pictured) deepdock.]]
Sharing its name with a [[Electrum|type]] of [[crystal]],<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> the Star Destroyer ''Electrum'' served under the [[Imperial Navy]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Starship|vessel]] was stationed at the<ref name="Tarkin" /> [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Ryloth">{{Databank|ryloth|Ryloth}}</ref> [[Ryloth]], at a [[Ryloth deepdock|deepdock]] there. However,<ref name="Tarkin" /> by [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> the ''Electrum'' had been loaned out to serve in the [[Sentinel Base's flotilla|flotilla]] of [[Sentinel Base]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] facility [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] which oversaw the construction of the Imperial [[battle station]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> later known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Rogue One guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> While stationed at Sentinel, the Star Destroyer was commanded by [[Captain]] [[Burque]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

At some point that [[Standard year|year]], most of the [[flotilla]]'s other [[capital ship]]s, including the ''[[Core Envoy]]'', Sentinel Base's [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]], were away escorting supply [[convoy]]s to the planet [[Geonosis]]&mdash;the site of the battle [[Space station|station's]] construction. With most of its ships away, the base received a [[Holovid|holotransmission]] [[Distress signal|reporting]] that the nearby [[Rampart Station]], a marshaling depot for supply ships bound for Geonosis, was under attack. When Tarkin arrived at Sentinel Base's command center to investigate the transmission, the Moff correctly suspected the holovid to be fake, due to the presence of a distinctive bar of interval noise. Promising Rampart reinforcements, Tarkin then asked a [[Unidentified comm specialist|comm specialist]] which of Sentinel's vessels were available to him. The [[specialist]] reported that the only ships left were the ''Electrum'', the [[frigate]] [[Salliche (frigate)|''Salliche'']], and the [[Spacetug|tug]] ''[[Fremond]]'', the latter two of which were both vacant and parked in stationary [[orbit]]s over the [[Sentinel moon|moon]] Sentinel Base was situated on.<ref name="Tarkin" />

===The attack===
{{Quote|Keep those hyperspace coordinates at the ready, Captain. But right now I want you to execute a microjump to the Rimward edge of this system. Do you understand?''"<br />"''Understood, Governor.|Wilhuff Tarkin and Burque|audio=Burque-Microjump.flac|<ref name="Tarkin" />}}
[[File:Moff-Tarkin.jpg|thumb|right|200px|The ''Electrum'' was stationed at Sentinel Base, which was commanded by Moff Wilhuff Tarkin (pictured).]]
After considering his options, Tarkin eventually ordered the specialist to contact Captain Burque. Speaking to the captain via [[hologram]], Tarkin asked Burque if he knew of the situation at Rampart, to which the captain replied that he did, and that the ''Electrum'' was prepared to travel to the station through [[hyperspace]] upon the Moff's command. Tarkin, however, ordered Burque to keep Rampart's coordinates at the ready, but instead commence a [[microjump]] to the Rimward edge of [[Sentinel Base system|Sentinel Base's system]], where the captain would await further orders.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Burque then asked if the ''Electrum'' should be in plain sight or obscure. Tarkin replied that it likely would not matter, although it would be preferable to find something to hide behind. After Tarkin then revealed he was expecting trouble, the pair's communication ended, with the ''Electrum'' commencing its ordered microjump shortly after. Not long after the Star Destroyer departed, a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell's]] 920-meter-long, heavily armed [[Berch Teller's warship|warship]]&mdash;the same one seen attacking Rampart in the fake transmission&mdash;reverted from hyperspace 200,000 [[kilometer]]s from Sentinel Base. The [[Unidentified specialist|specialist]] reporting the details of the vessel to Sentinel's command center then exclaimed that it was a good thing the ''Electrum'' had been dispatched&mdash;it would have been destroyed by the enemy's arrival.<ref name="Tarkin" />

When the [[carrier]] then launched [[droid starfighter]]s to [[Attack on Sentinel Base|attack]] Sentinel Base, including [[Droid tri-fighter|tri-fighters]] and [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]], Tarkin ordered the ''Electrum'' to return. As the base's ground-based [[artillery]] began to destroy the enemy [[Starfighter|fighters]], and just as the [[warship]] started to retreat, the ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer returned, and Tarkin asked for Burque to be informed that his [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilots]] were going to enjoy a target-rich environment.<ref name="Tarkin" />

===Imperial victory===
{{Quote|I trust that this is the trouble you were expecting, Governor.''"<br />"''Actually, Captain, most of this is quite unexpected. Therefore, I hope you'll do your best to incapacitate the carrier rather than destroy it. No doubt we can glean something by interrogating the crew.''"<br />"''I'll be as gentle with it as I can, Governor.|Burque and Wilhuff Tarkin|audio=Burque-Trouble.flac|<ref name="Tarkin" />}}
Burque himself then appeared via hologram, and Tarkin told him that the ''Electrum'' should merely incapacitate the warship so that its crew could be interrogated. Subsequently, squadrons of newly minted [[TIE series|TIE]] fighters were launched from the dorsal bay of the Star Destroyer to [[Starfighter combat|engage]] the [[droid]] starfighters. Just as Tarkin was contacted by Rampart Station, who confirmed they had not been attacked, the enemy warship jumped to hyperspace before the ''Electrum'' could get off a disabling shot. As a result, the attackers' droid fighters were sent spinning out of control, making them even easier targets for the TIEs.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Disappointed, Tarkin ordered Burque to gather debris for analysis and to snare a few intact droids, warning the captain that they could still be liable to [[Self-destruct mechanism|self-destruct]]. The ''Electrum''{{'s}} salvage teams recovered a vulture droid whose auto-destruct mechanism had been damaged during combat. The droid was then analyzed in Sentinel Base's maintenance hangar, revealing limited intelligence on the enemy. Tarkin, deeply troubled by the attack, went on to pursue leads on the rebel cell, [[Berch Teller campaign|eventually]] managing to [[Battle near the Gulf of Tatooine|defeat]] the group.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Commanders and crew==
{{Quote|Excuse me for asking, sir, but are we expecting trouble?''"<br />"''Always, Captain.|Burque and Wilhuff Tarkin|audio=Burque-Excuse-Me.flac|<ref name="Tarkin" />}}
During the vessel's [[time]] at Sentinel Base, the ''Electrum'' was commanded by Captain Burque. Although confused when Moff Tarkin ordered him not to aid Rampart Station, the captain nonetheless followed his superior's orders. The ''Electrum'' was also crewed by Imperial Starfighter Pilots and salvage teams.<ref name="Tarkin" />
