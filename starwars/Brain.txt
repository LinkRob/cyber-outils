[[File:BomarrBrainWalker-ROTJ.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The bottled brain of a B'omarr monk]]
The '''brain''' was an organ in lifeforms that served as the center for consciousness and reasoning. [[Species]] like the [[Cerean]]s<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> and the [[Siniteen]] had larger brains than average.<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> The Siniteen named [[Pons Limbic]] was nicknamed "Brainiac" as he could mentally calculate [[hyperspace]] jumps.<ref name="AbEverything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref>

Members of the [[B'omarr Order]] had their brains removed and placed in jars filled with nutrient fluid, which could then be attached to [[spider]]-like [[droid]] walkers, allowing the monk to move about.<ref name="AbEverything" />

[[Darth Sidious]] had the [[clone trooper]]s' brains implanted with [[behavioral modification biochip]]s to enact [[Order 66]].<ref name="Conspiracy">{{TCW|Conspiracy}}</ref> The [[AJ^6 cyborg construct]] was a brain implant used by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] workers to improve productivity, but at the cost of an individual's personality.<ref name="Empire Day">{{Rebels|Empire Day (episode)}}</ref>
