{{Quote|Yes, sir. Closest principal planets are Thustra and Aquaris.|A [[Communications officer|comms officer]] informs [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] of the planets closest to the ''Carrion Spike's'' location|<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>}}
'''Aquaris''' was a [[Waterworld|oceanic]] [[planet]]<ref name="TUC">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref> located in the [[Aquaris system]] in the [[Sumitra sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It and [[Thustra]] were the closest principal planets to the location of the [[Mission to Murkhana|stolen]] [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[corvette]] ''[[Carrion Spike]]'' while it was at the location designated as [[LCC-4-4-7]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> Aquaris had a notable criminal presence, and was located near the [[Perlemian Trade Route]]. The former [[pirate]] [[Queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] marked it as the location of an outlaw garage on a map that she included in the [[book]] that became known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> The planet was the origin point of the [[Aquaris Freeholders]], who forced the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] to make them a gourmet meal after his [[freighter]] and finding no [[spice]] aboard; Tuggs used a variety of ingredients he had found on Aquaris to create the [[Aquaris Broth]].<ref name="TUC" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[Aquaris/Legends|Aquaris]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity,<ref name="TEGtPaM">''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]''</ref> and was recanonized by its mention in the [[2014]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], which also mentions the [[Cvetaen system]],<ref name="Tarkin" /> Aquaris's system in the Legends continuity. However, the novel does not explicitly place Aquaris in the Cvetaen system,<ref name="TEGtPaM" /> and the [[2025]] "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" lists both Aquaris and Cvetaen separately.<ref name="Appendix" />
