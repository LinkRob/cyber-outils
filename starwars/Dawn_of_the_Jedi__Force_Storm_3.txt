'''''Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm'' 3''' is the third issue in the [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm|''Force Storm'']] story arc of the ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series and the third issue of the series in total. It is set in the year [[25,793 BBY]], in a [[Pre-Republic era|time before]] the polarization of the [[Light side of the Force/Legends|light]] and [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark sides]] of [[The Force/Legends|the Force]]. This issue was released on [[April 18]], [[2012]].

==Publisher's summary==
''The balance of the Force on Tython has been thrown into turmoil. A ship has fallen from the sky, bringing with it a strange, dark, and hostile individual. Three young Je'daii who felt "called" to the site of the crash now fight for their lives with their swords and the Force. But live or die, the storm that will engulf the homeworld of the Je'daii has already begun!''

==Plot summary==
''Master Ketu and Ranger Rori Fenn discuss a spaceship that has recently crashed landed on Tython. Master Ketu confirms that all who perished in the crash were force sensitive but unbalanced in favor of darkness. They can only speculate further and conclude that the crash site must be investigated. Master Keto sends Rori to rendezvous with Master Quan-Jang and Ranger Hawk Ryo at the Rift.

Shae Koda, Sek’nos Rath, and Tasha Ryo arrive at the crash site first, being summoned by visions of Xesh in Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm 2, and they immediately face off against him when they arrive. The Je’daii wield durasteel blades, forged by Master Madog, which are easily cut through by Xesh’s forcesaber. They exchange force powers and Sek’nos manages to steal the forcesaber. Xesh claims that they cannot activate it but is rebuked when Shae swings down on him with the activated forcesaber. Xesh avoids the attack and retreats deeper into the Rift towards the Abyss. Tasha is outvoted 2 to 1 and the group decides to pursue Xesh without waiting for the masters to arrive.

The official Je’daii recon unit led by Master Quan-Jang arrives at the crash site. Master Kora Ryo is remote support and she logics a connection between the crashed ship and the brewing force storm, which is causing creatures across the planet to lash out aggressively. The team find a crashed escape pod and find the blades of Shae Koda and Sek’nos Rath. Their injured mounts also come out of hiding. Master Quan-Jang and Rori Fenn both sense something in the abyss and follow the trail of the younger Je’daii.

The posse of younger Je’daii make it to the Abyss of Ruh unable to make contact with anyone via phone. Xesh begins to weaken and is trying to recall if someone may have sabotaged the Rakata ship. His memory does not serve him and he passes out.

We are left with Daegen Lok observing the growing force storm from the moon, Bogan.''
