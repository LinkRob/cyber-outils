A '''battle''' occurred between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] and [[Imperial Navy|forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] at the [[Enrivi system]] in [[3 ABY]]. An Imperial [[light cruiser]], backed by a [[squadron]] of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], ambushed the [[starship]]s of the 61st. However, the 61st obliterated the Imperial forces, wiping out the entire crew of the [[cruiser]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|We have engine problems, because my ship keeps being attacked. And my ship keeps being attacked because we have engine problems.|M2-M5, to [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]]|<ref name="Twilight" />}}
[[File:Twilight_Company_patch.png|thumb|left|250px|The 61st Mobile Infantry (patch pictured) was hunted by the Galactic Empire in the Metatessu sector.]]
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] [[Battle in the Kontahr sector|engaged]] an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] reconnaissance [[squadron]] in the [[Kontahr sector]]. There, the 61st's [[CR90 corvette]], the ''[[Thunderstrike]]'', received damages to its [[hyperdrive]], which caused it to leak a trail of [[hypermatter]]. The trail was discovered by the Empire after the 61st departed the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Coyerti]] over two [[Standard week|weeks]] later.<ref name="Twilight" />

Meanwhile, Imperial [[Captain]] [[Tabor Seitaron]] was hunting the [[battle group]] from his<ref name="Twilight" /> [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> ''[[Herald]]'' in hopes of capturing the Imperial [[Governor]] [[Everi Chalis]], who had defected to the 61st and began helping the rebels. Taking advantage of the hypermatter trail, the captain had a dozen [[officer]]s aboard the ''Herald'' liaise with Imperial [[starship]]s across the [[Metatessu sector]]. The 61st was first [[Battle in the Metatessu sector|attacked]] three [[Standard day|days]] after departing Coyerti in a [[Unidentified star system (Metatessu sector)|star system]] at the edge of the sector before swiftly escaping.<ref name="Twilight" />

==The battle==
The 61st arrived in the [[Enrivi system]] thirty [[Standard hour|hours]] later, hoping to put in for repairs to their ships. The Imperial forces, comprising of a [[light cruiser]] and a squadron of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], ambushed the ''Thunderstrike'' along with its escort, the [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunship]] ''[[Apailana's Promise]]'' and its two [[X-wing starfighter]]s, upon their arrival. Despite one X-wing being crippled from the battle thirty hours before, the ''Thunderstrike'' and its escort came out victorious, obliterating the Imperial forces. The shockwave of the [[cruiser]]'s explosion destroyed its own [[escape pod]]s.<ref name="Twilight" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|That trail was a stroke of fortune, but surely wars are not won through luck?|[[Prelate]] [[Verge]], to Tabor Seitaron|<ref name="Twilight" />}}
Following the battle, the 61st's Captain [[Micha Evon]] expressed his regret of the number of Imperial casualties caused. Despite that, the [[soldier]]s of the 61st celebrated the victory, toasting their [[pilot]]s and [[gunner]]s, who did not participate in the festivities in fear that they would be needed again soon. Their suspicions were confirmed when the 61st was [[Battle in the Chonsetta system|attacked]] nineteen hours later in the [[Chonsetta system]]. The 61st's top [[Technician|mechanic]], [[M2-M5]], would then discover that they were being tracked through a hypermatter trail being left by the ''Thunderstrike''{{'s}} damaged hyperdrive, which was eventually repaired after the battle group [[Raid on an Imperial heavy freighter|raided]] an [[Unidentified Imperial heavy freighter|Imperial heavy freighter]] in the [[Redhurne system]].<ref name="Twilight" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle in the Enrivi system appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Twilight" />
