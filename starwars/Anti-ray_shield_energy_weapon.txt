'''Anti-ray shield energy weapons''' were weapons that utilized an unusual energy frequency which was being researched by the [[Galactic Empire]] to see if they could create a weapon to bypass [[ray shield]]s, as they were virtually impervious to all but expensive ordnance such as [[proton torpedo]]es. It was unclear whether they managed to succeed before its downfall, or even if such development was remotely possible to achieve. It, alongside [[ship-scale disruptor]]s and a [[restraining bolt-override signal]], were among the research materials that members of the [[First Order]] were trying to locate, and members of the [[Resistance]] were trying to destroy alongside the Imperial [[Laboratory|lab]] notes relating to the research were to be found at.<ref name="A Call for Heroes">{{FFG|story=A Call for Heroes|book=Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|text=''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game}}</ref>
