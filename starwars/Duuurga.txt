'''Duuurga''' was the second [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] of the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]] [[Crime lord/Legends|crime lord]] [[Durga Besadii Tai]] created by [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]]. Cloned from samples of the original Hutt collected by [[Luuke Skywalker]], a clone of [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], who had [[Death/Legends|died]] in [[9 ABY/Legends|9 ABY]], Duuurga was created in place of Durga's first clone, [[Duurga]]. Thrawn had enjoyed pitting Duurga in arm-wrestles against [[Chewbaacca]], a clone of the [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]], which eventually ended in the Hutt having one of [[Gender/Legends|his]] arms pulled out of its socket. After Duuurga was cloned, Thrawn did not let Chewbaaca near him.<ref name="AA" /> Like the original Durga, Duuurga was 3.7 [[Meter/Legends|meters]] tall, had no [[Hair/Legends|hair]] and [[Color/Legends|orange]] [[Eye/Legends|eyes]].<ref name="NEGTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Duuurga was mentioned in "[[An Apology]]," a non-[[canon]] short story by [[Timothy Zahn]] that was released on [[April 1]], [[2012]] as an April Fool's Day joke.<ref name="AA" /> While the original Durga identified as male,<ref name="DS">[[Darksaber (novel)|''Darksaber'']].</ref> the short story did not specify if Duuurga did the same. In "An Apology," actions taken by characters after they were cloned were performed by clone imposters. Durga was not replaced before his death, and he could not play a role in another ''Star Wars'' story afterward; therefore, his clones did not. Duuurga was referred to in the present tense in "An Apology," meaning that Duuurga was still alive when "An Apology" was written in [[44 ABY]].<ref name="AA" />
