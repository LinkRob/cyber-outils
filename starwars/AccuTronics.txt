'''AccuTronics''', also simply '''Accutronics''', was a company that produced the [[Advanced Repair Unit]], an [[armor]] [[self-repair system]] attachment available during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="SM" /> The company also manufactured [[TDA-series droid assistant]]s, which included modified versions acting as [[command bridge droid]]s and [[4D-M1N]] aboard the ''[[Colossus]]'' [[refueling station]]<ref name="SWE" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
AccuTronics was mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Special Modifications]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="SM" /> The [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' established "Accutronics" as an alternate spelling.<ref name="SWE" /> The company originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook]]'', a [[1990]] roleplaying supplement written by [[Paul Murphy]] for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="RAS">''[[The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook]]''</ref>
