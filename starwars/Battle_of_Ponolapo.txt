{{Quote|At the Battle of Ponolapo a small rebel fleet had ambushed an Imperial convoy.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise and Fall" />}}
The '''Battle of Ponolapo''' was a battle of the [[Galactic Civil War]] fought by the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], a small [[Rebel Alliance Navy|rebel fleet]] ambushed an Imperial [[convoy]] being escorted by the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Imperial-class)|''Steadfast'']], [[Death|killing]] its [[commanding officer]], [[Commodore]] [[Freylon]] and destroying most of the convoy.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|The ships that had been pulled from service to deal with Crimson Dawn had left gaps in the Imperial fleet deployments that had still not been rebalanced.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise and Fall" />}}
Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Battle Date" /> the criminal organization [[Crimson Dawn]] fought a [[War against the Sith|war]] with the [[Galactic Empire]]. Although the Empire defeated Crimson Dawn, it had pulled [[starship|ships]] from service to do so, leaving gaps in [[Imperial Navy|fleet]] deployments. At the same [[time]], the Empire was fighting against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Although both sides were engaging in a number of small skirmishes, both the Empire and rebels were unable to win a decisive engagement to shift the balance of power in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> 

==Battle==
{{Quote|Ordinarily the escorting Star Destroyer ''Steadfast'' would have been accompanied by several Gozanti or Arquitens light cruisers but, in this case, it was alone. As a result, the rebels were able to destroy most of the Imperial shipping and damage the ''Steadfast''&mdash;even managing to kill Commodore Freylon&mdash;on the way out.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise and Fall" />}}
One such skirmish was the Battle of Ponolapo, which shared its name with<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> the [[Ponolapo system]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> and occurred between 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] and 4 ABY<ref name="Battle Date" /> when a small [[Rebel Alliance Navy|rebel fleet]] ambushed an Imperial [[convoy]] being escorted by<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Steadfast (Imperial-class)|''Steadfast'']]. Due to the conflict with Crimson Dawn, the ''Steadfast'' did not have its usual accompaniment of several [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti'']] or [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens'']] [[light cruiser]]s, which allowed the rebels to destroy most of the convoy and damage the ''Steadfast'', [[Death|killing]] its [[commanding officer]], [[Commodore]] [[Freylon]].<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

==Aftermath==
After the battle, Commodore [[Scaanos]] replaced Freylon as the commanding officer of the ''Steadfast.'' This infuriated [[Captain]] [[Enric Pryde]], the longest serving crewmember of the ''Steadfast'', who believed he should have received the command instead. The [[Local day|day]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="The Steadfast Soldier" /> in 4 ABY,<ref name="SteadFast Date">The events of "[[The Steadfast Soldier]]" take place the [[Standard day|day]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]]. As ''Timelines'' also dates the events of [[Shattered Empire 1|''Shattered Empire'' 1]], which also take place the day after the battle, to the same [[Standard year|year]], "The Steadfast Soldier" must also take place in 4 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> Pryde recalled the battle while thinking about his dislike of Scaanos. Pryde believed that Freylon's carelessness was the reason for his death at the battle.<ref name="The Steadfast Soldier" />

A file on the Battle of Ponolapo was located in the [[Rebel Alliance Archives]], and information on the battle was also contained in Freylon's [[Commodore Freylon Obituary|obituary]], which was stored in the "Imperial Navy" section of the [[Imperial Archives]]. The [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] referenced both files when<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> he<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> mentioned the battle in his [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Ponolapo was first mentioned in the short story "[[The Steadfast Soldier]]", written by [[Adam Christopher]] and published in the [[2023]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="The Steadfast Soldier" /> The story, which took place in 4 ABY,<ref name="SteadFast Date" /> established that the battle occurred at least four [[Standard year|years]] before its events.<ref name="The Steadfast Soldier" /> However, the [[2024]] reference book, ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]],<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> placed the battle between Crimson Dawn's war against the Empire and the Battle of [[Endor]], meaning it must have occurred in either 3 ABY or 4 ABY.<ref name="Battle Date" /> This article assumes ''Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''{{'s}} placement of the battle is correct.
