"'''Chapter 9: Strange Allies'''" is the ninth episode of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'' and was released on [[May 4]], [[2026]] alongside "[[Chapter 10: The Dark Lord]]".<ref name="Twitter" />

{{Media-stub}}

== Official description ==
''Trust is tested as newly forged allies navigate the only way out.''

==Plot summary==
===A new ally===
The episode opens with the [[Galactic Empire]] imposing a full lockdown on the planet [[Janix]], with personal and public transportation being forbidden on the penalty of death. As night falls, [[TK stormtrooper]]s march through the streets of [[Janix city]] while [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] (LAAT/i) gunships fly above. 

At [[Nico Deemis' headquarters]], [[Maul]] and his followers gather around the table as they commune with [[Dryden Vos]] via [[holoprojector]]. Vos is aware of their predicament with the Empire and offers a proposition to Maul. Maul is wary of trusting [[Crimson Dawn]] since the crime syndicate has betrayed him in the past. In return for helping Maul and his [[Shadow Collective]] escape Janix, Vos asks Maul to help him overthrow and kill [[Boss]] [[Rintero]], the current leader of the Crimson Dawn. Maul is impressed and accepts Vos's offer. Vos agrees to send coordinates to a rendezvous point but warns Maul that there is only a small window of escape. Maul promises that he will be at the rendezvous point. 

Maul speaks with [[Rook Kast]] and tasks her with making preparations to evacuate offworld while he attends to [[Devon Izara]]. Kast reminds Maul that the city is under Imperial lockdown. Maul replies that [[Captain]] [[Brander Lawson]] could help provide a way to escape the Imperial lockdown. Maul gazes out through the window at the cityscape below before clenching his fist and closing his eyes. 

===Maul's offer===
Meanwhile, Lawson along with [[2B0T]] ("Two Boots"), [[Rylee Lawson]], [[Devon Izara]] and [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]] take shelter in a warehouse. Two Boots remarks that his police-issued [[comlink]] has been deactivated and surmises that he is now a fugitive. Brander says that they are in this situation together. While Izara is sleeping, she experiences a [[Force vision]] of Maul speaking to her. Izara awakes and startles the others including Master Daki, who asked what happened. 

Izara explains that she dreamt that she was following someone through the streets of Janix city. They arrived at a statue of a woman who was holding a spear and a shield. Brander thinks that the statue was the [[Armistice Fountain]], which is nearby. Master Daki believes that someone has planted the image in her mind while Brander suspects that this is a trap. Izara disagrees. Daki warns that this is a suggestion planted by Maul while Izara thinks that it is a chance to escape. Sensing movement, Brander warns that they have to keep moving. 

The group leave the warehouse as [[Marrok]] and the [[Eleventh Brother]] enter the warehouse. Outside, the fugitives make their way to the Armistice Fountain where they encounter Maul. Master Daki demands that the [[Dark side of the Force|dark sider]] reveal his intentions. Maul says that he has come to offer them a way offworld. He is joined by Kast, [[Looti Vario]], [[Icarus]] and the remaining [[Mandalorian]]s emerge from the darkness. Brander expresses discomfort with Maul and his followers, prompting Maul to say that he only wants them to work together to escape. 

[[Spybot]] arrives and displays a [[hologram]] of a map of Janix city and its environs. Maul explains that their plan is to rendezvous with a ship that will pick them up on the outskirts of the city. Brander points out that the Empire have imposed a full lockdown on the city. Maul suggests that Brander may know an alternative route and proposes an alliance as a means of escaping the planet. In private, Brander discusses Maul's offer with Rylee, Two Boots and the Jedi. Two Boots is suspicious of Maul. Daki thinks they should accept Maul's escape plan but warns them to be cautious of Maul. 

===An alliance of convenience===
While the two groups navigate through the streets of Janix city together, [[Looti Vario]] strikes a conversation with Rylee. Brander is disdainful of the [[crime lord]] but Vario responds that he never imagined they would be on the same side together. Brander remarks that he is amazed that Vario has survived for this long. At the front of the line, Spybot speaks in [[Binary]] while Kast translates. She warns that this route has no access out of the city. Maul asks Brander about the reliability of the route. 

Brander affirms his route and tells them that there is a power station in the next district, which is built over an underground fissure that will take them past the Empire. Master Daki speaks with his Padawan Izara and asks if she is hiding something from him, saying that Maul would not have been able to read her thoughts unless there is a connection between the two of them. Izara explains that Maul saved her and Rylee's lives and brought them to safety after escaping from the casino. In response, Daki suggests that Maul saved her to serve his own goals. 

As they approach the power station, they find it guarded by several [[TK stormtrooper]]s and [[speeder bike]]s. Master Daki advises patience while Maul counters that their transport will not wait for them. Kast orders Spybot to jam the Imperials' communications while Maul approaches the stormtroopers with [[Maul's lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. The stormtroopers attempt to call for help but their comms are jam. Stormtroopers riding on bikes charge at Maul but he slices through one of the vehicles with his lightsaber. Kast shoots a second speeder bike. 

The Lawsons and Jedi join the fight. Rylee manages to shoot a stormtrooper off his bike before his father can do so. Maul charges through one of the legs of an [[All Terrain Armored Cannon]] (AT-AC) walker before slicing through the rear engines. The walker stumbles and falls before exploding, killing the driver inside. Izara and Daki watch with concern while Brander tells Rylee to be careful with his weapon.

===Going underground===
Meanwhile, the Inquisitors sense that the fugitives are making their escape through the Force. As the group enter the power station, Brander says that he learned about the fissure under the plant while pursuing a fugitive two years ago. Bander opens a drain cover which leads to a manhole. At Kast's prompting, Izara and Daki agree to descend into the manhole first. They are followed by the Lawsons, the Shadow Collective and Maul, who closes the covering before they continue their journey through the underground tunnels. 

Brander warns Two Boots that Maul and his followers may double-cross them and to stay alert. Meanwhile, Kast asks Maul what he plans to do with the elderly Master Daki. Maul tells her to have more respect for the Jedi Master and says that they need him for now. Outside the power station, stormtrooper reinforcements arrive in LAAT/i gunships while a [[Stormtrooper Commander]] surveys the burning wreckage of the AT-AC walker. Marrok and the Eleventh Brother arrive and are briefed by the commander, who tells them that they have searched through the power station but have found no sign of the fugitives.

The Inquisitors enter the facility where stormtroopers are combing through the motors. The two Inquisitors stumble upon the grated manhole covering. Meanwhile, the fugitives stumble upon an acidic lake, which Brander and Two Boots explained are illegally dumped toxic waste from the city. Two Boots quips about reporting the toxic waster dump. Master Daki thinks that the acidic lake is too far to jump and Maul concurs. Rylee discovers a broken-down skiff while Spybot finds another. Despite Vario's skepticism, they manage to reactivate the damaged skiffs and proceed with a plan that involves two people cross the acidic lake at a time.

After arguing with Spybot, the Lawsons travel across the acidic lake on the skiff. During the journey, Brander apologizes to his son for getting him involved in this situation. Rylee says it not his dad's fault. Brander still blames himself and apologizes for not being there for his son due to his work, which has finally caught up with him. Rylee says that at least they got to spend time as a family together. The two chuckle while Spybot grumbles at them in Binary. Rylee expresses annoyance. The Lawsons are followed by Vario and Two Boots, the latter grumbling about sharing a ride with a criminal.

===Skirmish by the lake===
Sensing trouble, Master Daki heads back towards the tunnels. He is joined by Maul and Izara, who asks what is going on. Daki warns them that something is approaching. The three ignite their lightsabers and prepare for a stand. Shortly later, Marrok and the Eleventh Brother exit the tunnels and charge at the Jedi and Maul. While the Force users fight, Kast and the other Shadow Collective members including Icarus raise their blasters as stormtroopers advance towards the pier. The Lawsons, Two Boots and Vario exchange fire with the stormtroopers, who set up position on the other side of the acidic lake. 

Following a gun exchange, Maul orders Kast to cross the acidic lake on the skiff. She and another Mandalorian depart on the skiff while another Mandalorian mercenary is hit by blaster bolts and falls beside the lake. Kast and the Mandalorian's skiff is hit but they are able to continue. The Lawsons, Vario and Two Boots provide covering fire for them. On the other side, Maul continues fighting against Marrok. He warns the [[Inquisitor]] that he is merely a disposable pawn of [[Darth Sidious]], whom the [[Sith Lord]] will discard once he has served his usefulness. Marrok kicks him but Maul continues fighting.

Spybot attempts to intervene but Marrok hurls [[Marrok's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] at the droid, slicing through his chassis. Spybot calls for his master before disintegrating and falling into the acidic lake. Maul continues fighting with Marrok while the two Jedi fight with the Eleventh Brother. With most of the others having made it across to the lake, Master Daki tells his Padawan to join the others. Izara [[Force jump]]s over a skiff and jumps onto the pier on the other side of the lake. She uses the Force to levitate the remaining skiff. 

Back on the other side, stormtroopers mop up the remaining Mandalorian mercenaries. Icarus grabs a stormtrooper and uses him as a sentient shield against his comrades. Making a last stand, he hurls several daggers at stormtroopers and charges at three of them, plunging with them into the acidic lake. Maul uses the Force to hurl his lightsaber and take out several stormtroopers. At Maul's urging, Master Daki Force jumps over the remaining skiff and lands safely on the opposite pier where he reunites with Izara. 

===Enter Darth Vader===
During his final round, Maul splits his twin-bladed lightsaber and parries with the Inquisitors. At the urging of Izara who is trying to keep the remaining skiff levitated, Maul Force jumps over the lake. Marrok hurls his lightsaber at Maul but misses and it instead hits the skiff, which sinks into the acidic lake. Maul lands safely on the opposite pier while the Inquisitors and stormtrooper minions stand down. Marrok orders the stormtrooper commander to inform [[Anakin Skywalker|him]] that the fugitives are headed his way. 

The fugitives emerge in a foggy forest. Two Boots asks Brander about their rendezvous point but they are unable to see due to the fog. Kast and one of her Mandalorian warriors go ahead to scout what lies ahead. Izara speaks with Maul and tells him that she is sorry to about their loss. Just then, they hear the cries of Kast and the Mandalorian warrior. Kast returns and warns them that someone is out there before being dragged away by an unseen force. She screams in terror as she is killed. The group watches as [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] emerges from the fog, with the life support system in [[Darth Vader's armor|armor]] making ominous breaths.
