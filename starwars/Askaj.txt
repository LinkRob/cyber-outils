'''Askaj''',<ref name="EE" /> also known as '''Askaji''',<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> was a [[planet]]<ref name="EE" /> located in the [[Askaj system]] in the [[Cegul sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Askajian]] [[species]]. On Askaji, the native Askajians lived in tribes led by a [[chief]].<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> By [[5 ABY]], it had joined the [[New Republic]] and was represented in the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] by [[Senator]] [[Grelka Sorka]].<ref name="EE" />
