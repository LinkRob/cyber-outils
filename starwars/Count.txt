'''Count''' was a title of [[Gender|male]] [[Noble|nobility]]. [[Dooku]], a [[Sith Lord]] and the political leader of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Episode II" /> was [[Count of Serenno|the count]] of the planet [[Serenno]]. Dooku reclaimed his title of count, along with his [[House Serenno|ancestral]] lands and wealth on Serenno, after he abandoned the [[Jedi Order]],<ref name="Serenno">{{Databank|serenno|Serenno}}</ref> [[Death|killing]] his brother [[Ramil]], who had taken the title following the death of their father, [[Gora]].<ref name="DJL">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Chrodber]] also had the title of count of Serenno.<ref name="TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> The [[thorilide]] mogul [[Denetrius Vidian]] was also a count. As such, he was addressed as "my [[lord]]."<ref name="AND" /> [[New Republic]] [[senator]] [[Jogurner]] held the title of count.<ref name="BL">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>
