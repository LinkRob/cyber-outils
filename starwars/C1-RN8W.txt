'''C1-RN8W''' was an [[astromech droid]] of the [[C1-series astromech droid|C1 series]] with a [[Color|black]] chassis and rainbow-colored detailing. This [[droid]] lived in [[Unidentified domed city|the domed city]]<ref name="Toy" /> located on the [[planet]] [[Plazir-15]].<ref name="Chapter 22">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 22: Guns for Hire}}</ref> In [[9 ABY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Chapter 22: Guns for Hire]]'' takes place in [[9 ABY]], and the droid's background location pulls directly from this episode.</ref>, C1 travelled to the [[Peacekeeping zone]]<ref name="Toy" /> on the same day [[Din Djarin]] and [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] visited the metropolis.<ref name="Chapter 22" /> 

It had three retractable grasping arms, one in its chassis and two stored in its head module. There were three [[Sensor|sensors]] built-in, two black and one purple. From its middle, C1 had a central wheel instead of a retractable third foot. While it was missing some panels and had exposed wiring, C1-RN8W functioned well enough to travel.<ref name="Toy" />
