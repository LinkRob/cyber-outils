The '''Forbidden District''' was the district located on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]] that bordered the [[Sith Citadel]]. It housed derelict [[Shipyards of Exegol|shipyard forges]] that dated back to the old [[Sith Wars]] for control of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles, New Edition" />

Shortly after the rise to power of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]], the [[Sith Lord]] began to modernize these facilities as part of his long-view contingency to keep a bastion of power secret from even his [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] cronies. Clandestine shipments of materiel from corporate coconspirators<ref name="Complete Vehicles, New Edition" /> within the executive boards of [[Sienar-Jaemus Fleet Systems|Sienar-Jaemus]] and [[Kuat-Entralla Engineering|Kuat-Entralla]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> updated these facilities to produce [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] and new [[TIE/dg starfighter]]s. When active, these automated forges can work tirelessly, far from the view of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles, New Edition" />
