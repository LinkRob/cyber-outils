During the [[High Republic Era]], a '''wedding''' was held on the [[Planet|world]] [[Quarzite]] for the [[Abda's brother|brother]] of a [[Kage]] [[Gender|female]] and their [[Abda's brother's spouse|partner]]. The [[Abda|sister]] felt that the event was the biggest [[Marriage|wedding]] that her [[Abda's clan|family]] had ever thrown. She eventually grew to dislike weddings after realizing that she would be expected to follow the same path in life as her brother, including marrying.<ref name="Convergence" />

By [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the sister had left her [[homeworld]] and joined the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] and chose to take the name of Abda to symbolically distance herself from her past.<ref name="Convergence" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> while infiltrating a pre-[[Wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran|wedding]] party hosted for the [[E'roni]] [[Princess]] [[Xiri A'lbaran]], Abda [[Brain|mentally]] recalled her brother's wedding and her feelings about it.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
The wedding of Abda's brother was mentioned in [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], a [[2022]] novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Convergence" />
