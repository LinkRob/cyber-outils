'''Durafiber''' was a durable fabric used in various applications in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> It could used to create [[Environment suit|EVA suits]]. A variant of durafiber was [[superstretch durafiber]], used in an EVA suit worn by [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]].<ref name="Last Shot" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a stockpile of durafiber was stored at a [[Crimson Dawn]] [[Crimson Dawn Hideout (Boulder Forest)|hideout]] in the [[Boulder Forest]] region of [[Toshara]], a [[moon]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. During the same [[Standard year|year]], the [[Quarren]] rogue [[merchant]] [[Shuma (merchant)|Shuma]] sold elastic durafiber from her [[Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport]] for 1400 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] heard about the Crimson Dawn stockpile and traveled to it after upgrading her [[S-57 Cardinal]] to make a jump over a [[water|river]] leading to the hideout. Once there, she retrieved the fabric and used it to construct a new pouch to hold [[bacta vial]]s and [[grenade]]s. Vess later bought a roll of elastic durafiber from Shuma and used it to further improve her storage of [[bacta]] and grenades.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Durafiber first appeared in the [[canon]] continuity in the [[2018]] novel ''[[Last Shot]]'' by [[Daniel José Older]].<ref name="Last Shot" /> Durafiber fabric and elastic durafiber fabric were first pictured as an upgrade item in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The player must obtain durafiber fabric in order to unlock the "Scoundrel's Pouch" upgrade, which lets them carry an extra bacta vial and grenade. Afterwards, they need to obtain the elastic durafiber fabric to unlock the "Outlaw's Pouch" and carry one more of each.<ref name="Outlaws" />
