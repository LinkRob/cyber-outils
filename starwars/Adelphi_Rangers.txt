The '''Adelphi Rangers''', also known as '''Adelphi squadron'''<ref name="Chapter 21" /> and the '''New Republic Rangers''',<ref name="Insider 5 Years">{{InsiderCite|228|Making History: Five Years of The Mandalorian|Making History: Five Years of ''The Mandalorian''}}</ref> was one of the few [[starfighter]] [[squadron]]s of the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] that protected the interests of the [[New Republic]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] during the [[New Republic Era|Age of the New Republic]].<ref name="TMAG" /> Based from an [[Adelphi Base|outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Adelphi]], the Adelphi Rangers were comprised of both [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] veterans who fought in the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="TMVG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]</ref> and new recruits,<ref name="Adelphi Base DB">{{Databank|adelphi-base|Adelphi Base}}</ref> all under the command of [[Colonel]] [[Ward]].<ref name="TMAG" /> [[Captain]] [[Carson Teva]] was an officer in the squadron. Other members of the squadron include [[Lieutenant]] [[Reed]],<ref name="Chapter 21" /> [[Trapper Wolf]], [[Sash Ketter]], [[Jib Dodger]]. The Adelphi Rangers utilized [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s.<ref name="Chapter 6">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 6: The Prisoner}}</ref> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|6}}</ref> the [[New Republic]] sent the squadron to [[Bothan-Five incident|investigate]] a [[Homing beacon|tracking beacon]].<ref name="Chapter 6" />

==Description==
The Adelphi Rangers, also designated as Adelphi squadron, was a [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] of the [[New Republic]] that was active in the [[New Republic Era|post-Imperial era]]. The Adelphi Rangers shared its name with the terrestrial [[planet]] [[Adelphi]], which hosted the Rangers' [[Adelphi Base]]. The squadron was equipped with [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s and [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]]s, and was staffed by [[New Republic pilot]]s with both orange and blue [[flight suit]]s. The Rangers were also staffed by multiple [[New Republic officer|officers]], including [[Captain]] [[Carson Teva]], [[Lieutenant]] [[Reed]],<ref name="Chapter 21" /> [[Trapper Wolf]], and other individuals.<ref name="Chapter 24">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 24: The Return}}</ref>
