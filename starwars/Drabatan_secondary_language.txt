A secondary<ref name="Visual Guide" /> '''language''' was used by the [[Amphibian|amphibious]] [[Drabatan]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Pipada]].<ref name="DoR" /> When situations called for more hushed tones,<ref name="Visual Guide" /> Drabatans switched to this [[language]], which was quieter and simpler than their primary language [[Drabatese]].<ref name="DoR" /> To speak the language, they passed air through their [[vocal pouch]]es to emit croaks that they could vary in pitch.<ref name="Visual Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Drabatan's secondary language was first mentioned in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''.<ref name="Visual Guide" />
