'''Epic Games''' is an interactive entertainment company.<ref name="About" /> It has been involved with the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise over the years indirectly, through the uses of their own [[Wikipedia:Unreal Engine|Unreal Engine]] on various interactive products, and directly,<ref name="Disney X Epic">{{DisneyCompany|url=disney-and-epic-games-fortnite|text=Disney and Epic Games to Create Expansive and Open Games and Entertainment Universe Connected to Fortnite}}</ref> with collaboration on their games ''[[Fortnite]]''<ref name="Mando x Fortnite">{{SW|url=news/the-mandalorian-joins-fortnite|text=The Mandalorian Joins the Hunt in a New Season of ''Fortnite''}}</ref> and ''[[Rocket League]]'',<ref name="RL Droids">{{SW|url=news/rocket-league|text=''Rocket League'' Announces New ''Star Wars'' Collab}}</ref> on the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'',<ref name="Mando">{{WebCite|url=https://www.dga.org/Craft/DGAQ/All-Articles/1902-Spring-2019/Cameron-Favreau-Innovators.aspx|text=The Innovators|date=2019|work=[https://www.dga.org dga.org]|archivedate=20200108144257}}</ref> and on ''Star Wars'' attractions at Disney Parks.<ref name="Disney X Epic" />

On [[February 7]], [[2024]], it was announced that [[The Walt Disney Company]] and Epic Games were entering an major agreement over an investment of 1.5 billion dollars in Epic Games equity shares, along with a planned project under the umbrella of ''Fortnite'' to create new games and a persistent, innovative and shared entertainment universe linking the franchises owned by Disney, including ''Star Wars''.<ref name="Disney X Epic" />
