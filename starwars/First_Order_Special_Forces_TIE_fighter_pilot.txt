'''First Order Special Forces TIE fighter pilots''',<ref name="ECGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> also called '''First Order SF TIE Pilots''',<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> or simply '''Special Forces pilots''', were [[Special forces|elite]] [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|pilots]] that served in the [[First Order Special Forces]] branch.<ref name="TFA VD" /> They utilized specialized [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]s during combat. Along with elite starfighter training, like other [[First Order stormtrooper|troopers]], they were also heavily trained in [[blaster]]s and marksmanship.<ref name="Episode VII" />

Special Forces pilots wore a similar uniform to their regular [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|pilot]] counterparts, consisting of a [[Color|black]] [[Flight suit|jumpsuit]] with a chestplate with [[life support]] gear, [[pauldron]]s on the shoulders, [[gauntlet]]s, a utility [[belt]], [[boot]]s, and a helmet.<ref name="TFA VD" /> Special Forces pilots could be distinguished by the two red vertical stripes on their [[helmet]]s. Pilots were typically issued with a [[SE-44C blaster pistol]],<ref name="Episode VII" /> which could be carried in a [[holster]] on their belt when not in use.<ref name="TFA VD" />
