'''''A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy''''' is a [[canon]] junior novel written by [[Alexandra Bracken]], with cover art by [[Khoa Ho]] and interior art by [[Ralph McQuarrie]] and [[Joe Johnston]]. It is a retelling of the film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], and it was first published on [[September 22]], [[2015]] by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]. In addition to print, the title was released as an [[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy (audiobook)|audiobook]] read by [[Marc Thompson]] and [[Rebecca Soler]].

==Publisher's summary==
"''The galaxy is at war''.

''Although the Rebel Alliance has won a few battles against the Empire, hope is fading. The Empire is about to finish building the greatest weapon the galaxy has ever seen&mdash;the Death Star. The rebels' only chance to defeat it now lies in the unlikely hands of a princess, a scoundrel, and a farm boy. . . . ''

''Acclaimed, ''New York Times'' bestselling author Alexandra Bracken delivers a captivating retelling of ''Star Wars: A New Hope'' like you've never experienced before. Since the premier of the original film, Princess Leia, Han Solo, and Luke Skywalker have become iconic, larger-than-life characters. ''The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy'' takes a deeper look at these three heroes as they join forces to defeat the evil that threatens their entire galaxy.''"

==Development==
Author [[R.J. Palacio]] was slated to write the book, but due to scheduling conflicts, she was replaced by the book's current author, [[Alexandra Bracken]].<ref name="Author Change">{{WebCite|url=http://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20141113006802/en/Disney-Publishing-Worldwide-Announces-Bestselling-Author-Alexandra|text=Disney Publishing Worldwide Announces Bestselling Author Alexandra Bracken to Pen ''STAR WARS EPISODE IV: A NEW HOPE'' Illustrated Novel|work=Disney Publishing Worldwide press release|accessdate=November 14, 2014|archivedate=20191105050631}}</ref>

The title was marketed for several months with the subtitle "''Being the Story of Luke Skywalker, Darth Vader, and the Rise of the Rebellion''," but this subtitle was dropped in listings not long after the announcement of the change in author to Alexandra Bracken. The title was later pushed from its original publication date of [[June 23]], [[2015]] to [[September 22]], 2015.

On [[June 5]], 2015, Disney revealed the cover artwork for the titles in the series.<ref name="coverart">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-adaptation-covers-revealed-presenting-the-new-original-trilogy|text=''Star Wars'' Adaptation Covers Revealed: Presenting the New Original Trilogy}}</ref>

==Continuity==
The title includes material adapted from the [[Star Wars (radio)|radio dramatization]] of ''A New Hope''.<ref name="radio">{{Twitter|alexbracken|status/636708724061696000|[[Alexandra Bracken]]|quote=I was lucky enough to be allowed to adapt from the very cool ANH radio drama for The Princess, The Scoundrel, and The Farmboy|archivefile=File:Bracken Radio Tweet.png|nolive=1}}</ref>

The section ''The Farm Boy'' is not internally consistent with itself regarding the duration of events. On some pages, Luke thinks that he only knew Obi-Wan for one day or lost his family the day prior to meeting Leia; on others, it has been a few days.

This book reveals that [[Han Solo]] couldn't have been more than ten years older than [[Luke Skywalker]]. Since Luke was born in [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'',</ref> that would place Han's birth circa [[29 BBY]]. However, information revealed in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' contradicts this by setting Solo's birth circa [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Solo: Official Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
