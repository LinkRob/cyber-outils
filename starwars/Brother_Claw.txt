'''Brother Claw''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[crime lord]] who controlled the [[Brother Claw Gang]] on the [[mining]] [[Planet|world]] [[Burnin Konn]] during the [[Iron Blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[Trade Spine League]] [[starship]] ''[[Mother of Pearl]]'' was shot down in Brother Claw's territory during the blockade, causing the gangster [[Dapp|Happy Dapp]] to send a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] into the criminal's territory to recover the vessel's cargo.<ref name="SW:U" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brother Claw was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'' which was released on mobile platforms in [[2015]].<ref name="SW:U" />
