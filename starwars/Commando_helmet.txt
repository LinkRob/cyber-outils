'''Commando helmets''' were a type of [[Color|black]] and gold riot [[helmet]] worn by [[Enforcer (occupation)|enforcers]] working for the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime [[syndicate]] along with matching [[armor]].<ref name="Solo Guide />

==Description==
The commando helmets<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> were primary [[Color|black]] with gold colored piping that circled its [[duraplex]] visor. When in its open configuration, the visor split down the middle and slid out to each side.<ref name="Solo Guide /> They could be worn by members of various [[species]] including [[Hylobon]] and [[human]]s.<ref name="Vader 18">[[Darth Vader (2020) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 18]]</ref>

==History==
By [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Crimson Date">''Crimson Climb'' starts with the flashbacks seen in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the flashbacks to [[13 BBY]]. The book carries on for about a year and a half, placing its ending round [[12 BBY]].</ref> the [[Hylobon Enforcers]] who guarded the [[Crimson Dawn]] [[Crime lord|leader]] [[Dryden Vos]] wore the helmets along with matching [[armor]].<ref name="Crimson Climb">''[[Crimson Climb]]''</ref> 

Around [[12 BBY]],<ref name="Crimson Date" /> Vos and the human [[Qi'ra]], along with four Hylobon guards wearing commando helmets attended a gathering of [[Five Crime Syndicates|crime syndicates]] on the planet [[Nightsend]]. Towards the end of the gathering, a faction of the [[Black Sun]] syndicate attacked the gathering in a effort to gain control of their syndicate. In order to escape the attack, Vos, Qi'ra, and the Hylobons had to endure the severe cold on the planet's surface; with [[Aemon Gremm]], one of the Hylobons, lending his helmet to Qi'ra to allow her to survive the cold.<ref name="Crimson Climb" />

By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an individual named [[Jodara]] had stashed a commando helmet in a container on a platform in the [[Crimson Dawn District (Mirogana)|Crimson Dawn district]] of [[Mirogana]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]], the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] inflitated the Crimson Dawn district, discovered the stashed helmet, and stole it for herself.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The commando helmets were developed for the film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', directed by [[Ron Howard]] from a screenplay by [[Lawrence Kasdan]] and [[Jon Kasdan]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> Prior to the film's release on [[May 25]], [[2018]],<ref name="Film Announced">{{SW|url=news/christopher-miller-and-phil-lord-to-helm-han-solo-anthology-film|text=Christopher Miller and Phil Lord to Helm Han Solo Anthology Film|nolive=1}}</ref> the helmets appeared as part of 3.0 update to the mobile [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Force Arena]]'' developed by [[Netmarble Games]], published by [[Disney Interactive]], and released on [[May 23]], [[2018]].<ref name="3.0">{{WebCite|url=http://www.mobirum.com/article/detail?cafeId=StarWarsForceArena&bbsId=1571&id=1154362|text=3.0 Update Details|author=CM Paul|date=2018-05-23|archiveurl=https://archive.is/nCDAU}}</ref> The helmets were identified as commando helmets in the "Star Wars Outlaws: Building An Open World" trailer for the video game ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]], produced by [[Ubisoft]]<ref name="Outlaws"/> and released on [[August 23]], [[2024]].<ref name="Building An Open World">{{UbisoftYouTube|42EygZb2sBk|Star Wars Outlaws: Building An Open World|archivedate=20250905061510}}</ref>
