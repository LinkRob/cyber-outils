'''DeTECHt''' was a company that manufactured the [[security scanner]]s used in<ref name="SDCC 2023 panel" /> the [[grand concourse]]<ref name="KenobiIVAD">{{Kenobi|Part IV}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Fortress Inquisitorius]] on the [[moon]] [[Nur]]<ref name="SDCC 2023 panel" /> and in the [[Officers Quarters|Officer's Quarters]] of the [[II-0810 Satellite Station]] as in [[9 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{KenobiDate|IV}}</ref> The [[scanner]]s were capable of detecting weapons, [[bomb]]s, hazardous materials, and other threats.<ref name="SDCC 2023 panel" />

==Behind the scenes==
DeTECHt was created for the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide|Star Wars Dawn of Rebellion The Visual Guide]]'' by authors [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[Emily Shkoukani]]. The page mentioning DeTECHt was revealed prior<ref name="SDCC 2023 panel" /> to the book's [[November 7]], [[2023]] release<ref name="Penguin Retail">{{PenguinComics|url=book/?isbn=9780744087345|text=Star Wars Dawn of Rebellion The Visual Guide}}</ref> at a [[Lucasfilm]] publishing panel at [[Wikipedia:San Diego Comic Con|San Diego Comic Con]] and in an article on [[StarWars.com]] covering it on [[July 21]], 2023.<ref name="SDCC 2023 panel" />
