The '''Cyban Front''' was a criminal organization that worked with the [[Droid Gotra]] during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]]. During the [[Auction of Rur]] on the [[Sorca Retreat]], the Cyban Front, representing various organizations, sent two [[droid]]s to acquire the [[Rur Crystal|Rur crystal]] so that it could be destroyed.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 9" /><ref name="Doctor Aphra 10">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 10|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 10]]</ref> The droids failed to win the crystal, and were both destroyed in a [[Skirmish aboard the Sorca Retreat|skirmish]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 12|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 12]]</ref><ref name="Doctor Aphra 13">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 13|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 13]]</ref> that took place after [[0-0-0]] and [[BT-1]] inserted the Rur crystal into [[Unidentified blue Cyban Front droid|one]] of the Cyban Front droids. Inside a new mechanical body, Rur proceeded to rampage throughout the station, killing numerous people before being defeated by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" />
