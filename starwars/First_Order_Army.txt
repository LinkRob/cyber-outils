The '''First Order Army''' was the [[Army|ground-based]] branch of the [[First Order military]]. In addition to maintaining order throughout [[First Order]] [[Realspace|space]], the First Order Army spearheaded the conquest and colonization of the [[Unknown Regions]]. In order to quickly and efficiently raise a capable army, the First Order abandoned the conscription practices of the [[Galactic Empire]], and instead kidnapped and indoctrinated infants throughout [[the galaxy]]. The Unknown Regions provided a haven for the hermit state to effectively train and mobilize these child soldiers into an army of fierce [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] with a zealous loyalty to the [[Supreme Leader]].

The First Order's rearmament policy violated the terms of the [[Galactic Concordance]], which saw the [[Imperial Military]] dismantled in the aftermath of the [[Galactic Civil War]], but continued nonetheless as the [[New Republic]] sought to avoid the escalation of the [[Cold War]] into open warfare. With the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] unwilling to risk another armed [[Galaxy|galactic]] conflict, [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] formed the [[Resistance]] to oppose the First Order's rise to power. The First Order Army discarded the facade of a defense force in the wake of the New Republic's destruction through the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]] in [[34 ABY]], and embarked on a campaign to conquer the galaxy which was initially overseen by Supreme Leader [[Snoke]]. However, command of the First Order Army was usurped by [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], the Supreme Leader's [[Dark side of the Force|dark]] apprentice, who [[Death|assassinated]] his master during the [[Battle of Oetchi]]. By using [[the Force]] to [[Force choke|coerce]] [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]], Ren secured the Army under his control as the new Supreme Leader of the First Order.

In addition to Ren, the First Order Army served under the command of [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]], whose title gave him authority over both the Army and the [[First Order Navy]]. Under the leadership of Ren, much of the galaxy was conquered by the forces of the First Order. However, the First Order lacked the resources to subjugate the remaining holdouts until the discovery of the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Sith cult|cult's]] [[Sith Eternal army|secret army]] of [[Sith trooper]]s as well as a [[Final Order|vast armada]] on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]]. Ren planned to supplement his forces with that of the [[Sith cultist]]s in order to gain full control over the galaxy, thereby transforming the First Order into a true empire. Following the [[Battle of Exegol]], however, the [[Sith Eternal military]] was in ruins and the galaxy rose in [[Uprising against the First Order|open rebellion]] against the First Order.

==History==
===Origins===
[[File:IWillSucceedWhereYouFailed-AORKyloRen1.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The First Order army was formed in the years following the defeat of its predecessor, the Imperial Army.]]
Following the defeat of the [[Galactic Empire]] at the [[Battle of Jakku]] and its [[Imperial Instruments of Surrender|surrender]] with the signing of the [[Galactic Concordance]], the [[First Order]] and its [[First Order military|military]] eventually formed in the [[Unknown Regions]] where it plotted its eventual return to power.<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" /> The First Order Army<ref name="Children from Tehar">{{Resistance|The Children from Tehar}}</ref> was descended from the [[Imperial Army]], which served as the ground forces of the "Old Empire."<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> The Army was responsible for enforcing order throughout First Order [[Realspace|space]] and the colonization of the Unknown Regions.<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" />

The First Order utilized [[propaganda]] to indoctrinate its forces from a young age,<ref name="Stormtroopers Databank">{{Databank|first-order-stormtroopers|First Order Stormtroopers}}</ref> causing them to become fiercely loyal to the First Order and their [[Supreme Leader]].<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] considered his soldiers well trained and reliable because they had been programmed since birth.<ref name="Episode VII" />

===Cold War===
{{Quote|They also said that someone called ''Kylo Ren'' was leading the First Order army. Does that name mean anything to you?''"<br />"''You could say that. General Organa will want to hear about this immediately.|[[Kazuda Xiono]] and [[Ello Asty]]|<ref name="Children from Tehar" />}}
[[File:AtmosphericAssaultLanderLoaded-TFA.png|thumb|right|350px|The First Order used conscripted children to form a new generation of stormtroopers.]]
During the [[Cold War]], the [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] of the First Order Army saw action against the [[Resistance]] in various locations and operations including a [[Mission to Ovanis|failed operation]] on [[Ovanis]] in an attempt to find [[Lor San Tekka]], an explorer who had part of the [[map to Skywalker]], a [[Hologram|holographic]] [[star map]] that led to the missing [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 1">[[Poe Dameron 1|''Poe Dameron'' 1]]</ref><ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> First Order [[soldier]]s fought [[Resistance pilot]]s on [[Spalex]] after the pilots found them harassing the [[Spalexian|natives]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 17">[[Poe Dameron 17|''Poe Dameron'' 17]]</ref> Stormtroopers also quelled the [[Strike at Pressy's Tumble|strike]] at [[Pressy's Tumble]].<ref name="Before the Awakening" />

The First Order Army also saw action against other enemies such as the [[Benathy]]. During the [[Benathy expansion]] in [[Wild Space]], a massive First Order ground force of stormtroopers and [[First Order All Terrain Armored Transport|All Terrain Armored Transports]] led by First Order [[Warlord]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] was deployed to the Benathy homeworld of [[Benath]] to bring an end to their expansion. Following a [[Battle against the Benathy (First Order)|decisive battle]], the Benathy's "[[god]]," a [[Unidentified Zillo Beast (Benathy)|Zillo Beast]], was defeated by the First Order and the [[Benathy kingdom]] submitted to the Order.<ref name="Age of Resistance">[[Age of Resistance - Kylo Ren 1|''Age of Resistance - Kylo Ren'' 1]]</ref>

On another occasion, Kylo Ren led the First Order Army during a [[Attack on Tehar|massacre]] on the [[planet]] [[Tehar]]. The sole survivors were [[Kel]] and [[Eila]], who fled to the ''[[Colossus]]'' space station on the planet [[Castilon]]. There, they met Resistance [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]], who alerted the Resistance leadership about the activities of the First Order Army and Ren.<ref name="Children from Tehar" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> First Order stormtroopers were deployed during the [[Attack on Tuanul|attack]] on [[Tuanul]] in an attempt to secure the [[Map to Skywalker|map]] that led to [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> The First Order Army was led by General Armitage Hux, who also commanded [[Starkiller Base]]. Substantial First Order army forces including [[walker]]s were stationed on the [[Water|snowy]] planet, which doubled as a [[superweapon]]. The First Order Army and other branches including the [[First Order Navy]] were involved in the construction of the massive superweapon.<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" />

===War with the Resistance===
The First Order orchestrated a [[Warbird gang|pirate]] [[Torra Doza's abduction|kidnapping]] of Colossus resident [[Torra Doza]], which they believed justified leaving a small [[Colossus garrison|security detail]] of stormtroopers to [[Occupation of the Colossus|occupy the Colossus]],<ref name="Dangerous Business">{{Resistance|Dangerous Business}}</ref> though the force's number substantially grew over time.<ref name="The Core Problem">{{Resistance|The Core Problem}}</ref>
Not long after the occupation began, the First Order used Starkiller Base to [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroy]] the [[Hosnian system]],<ref name="Episode VII" /> including the [[New Republic]] capital of [[Hosnian Prime]].<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> In the aftermath, [[Team Colossus|Colossus resistance]] forces led by Xiono and former [[Rebel pilot]] [[Jarek Yeager]] expelled the First Order occupation forces and took the ''Colossus'' into uncharted space.<ref name="No Escape 2">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 2}}</ref> In the wake of Hosnian Prime's destruction, First Order stormtroopers also saw action against the Resistance during the [[Battle of Takodana]] in a failed attempt to capture the Resistance [[astromech droid]] [[BB-8]], who was carrying the map to Luke Skywalker. The First Order military sustained many casualties during the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], where their superweapon would ultimately be destroyed by the Resistance, including numerous stormtrooper losses.<ref name="Episode VII" /> As the First Order moved to conquer the galaxy and crush the Resistance,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> it had ten thousand battle-ready troops at its disposal.<ref name="TLJ Season">{{EASWYouTube|n0-ye4LXWts|Star Wars Battlefront II: The Last Jedi Season}}</ref>

[[File:Greatest-Villians-Phasma.png|thumb|left|250px|Captain Phasma aboard the ''Supremacy'' with her soldiers shortly before her demise.]]
The First Order Navy, led by their flagship the ''[[Supremacy]],'' [[Battle of Oetchi|chased down]] the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]] using [[Hyperspace tracker|hyperspace tracking]] from their base in the [[Ileenium system]] to the [[Oetchi system]]. First Order stormtroopers caught Resistance agents [[Rose Tico]] and [[Finn]] attempting to disable the tracker. Tico and Finn were about to be [[Death penalty|executed]] in the hanger when the Resistance flagship the [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] [[Holdo maneuver|rammed]] into the First Order fleet. A battle ensued in the fiery hanger which led to the death of the stormtroopers' leader, [[Captain]] [[Phasma]]. Also on the ''Supremacy'' Kylo Ren seized control of the First Order Army from General Hux, shortly after assassinating Supreme Leader [[Snoke]]. Hux protested Ren's actions at first, enraged that Ren thought he could command Hux's Army, prompting Ren to [[Force choke]] him, coercing the General's allegiance to the First Order Army's new Supreme Leader.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Ren's rise to power would leave Hux isolated and deprived of much of the influence he gained during Snoke's rule. During his reign, Ren promoted the career of [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]], granting the former [[Imperial officer]] the power to command both the First Order Army and [[Navy]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

The remaining Resistance forces retreated to the [[planet]] [[Crait]] where they would seek refuge in an abandoned [[Crait outpost|rebel base]]. In the following [[Battle of Crait]], significant First Order ground forces including stormtroopers, [[First Order snowtrooper|snowtroopers]], [[First Order All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-ATs]], [[First Order All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-STs]], [[All Terrain MegaCaliber Six]] walkers, [[All Terrain Heavy Hauler]]s, and a [[Superlaser siege cannon]] assaulted the Resistance outpost on [[Crait]]. The Resistance stood no chance and sustained heavy casualties, but the last survivors escaped due to the appearance of Jedi Master Luke Skywalker, who was actually [[Force projection|projecting]] himself across the galaxy via [[the Force]]. His former master's appearance distracted Supreme Leader Ren and bought enough time for the Resistance to escape, though the battle would still end in the death of Luke Skywalker. A large group of stormtroopers led by Ren entered the Resistance base, but it was too late, and the surviving Resistance fighters jumped to [[hyperspace]] on their ship, the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="Episode VIII2">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

Despite the Resistance's escape from Crait, the First Order conquered a large amount of territory throughout the galaxy,<ref name="TROS VD" /> becoming an Empire in all but name.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> However, the First Order lacked the resources of its predecessor, the Galactic Empire,<ref name="TROS VD" /> leaving a number of free worlds unconquered by [[35 ABY]].<ref name="35 ABY">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' as taking place one [[Standard year|year]] after the "[[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]]." As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the Starkiller Incident to [[34 ABY]], it can be deduced the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' take place in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> This changed when Ren discovered the hidden [[Sith]] world of [[Exegol]], where the [[Sith Eternal]] had cultivated an [[Sith Eternal army|army]] of elite [[Sith trooper]]s as well as a [[Final Order|fleet]] of [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|planet-killer]] [[Star Destroyer]]s. Ren estimated that the [[Sith cult]]'s [[Sith Eternal military|forces]] in addition to his remaining forces in the First Order would grant him the ability to rule the First Order as a genuine empire.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:Kylo-Stormtroopers.png|thumb|right|250px|Large swathes of the galaxy fell to the forces of the First Order led by Supreme Leader Kylo Ren.]]
Ren simultaneously focused on his goal to turn [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] to the [[dark side of the Force]], having learned of her [[Palpatine family|lineage]] as the grandchild of [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]]. After tracking Rey and her Resistance allies to the planet [[Pasaana]], Ren deployed a contingent of the First Order's ground forces including [[First Order jet trooper|jet troopers]], stormtroopers, and [[125-Z treadspeeder bike|treadspeeder bikes]] along with the [[Knights of Ren]] to capture her. Jet troopers riding treadspeeders pursued Rey and her comrades [[Poe Dameron]], [[Finn]], [[Chewbacca]], [[C-3PO]], and [[BB-8]] through the desert, sustaining casualties.<ref name="Episode IX" />

The [[First Order-Resistance War]] ended in the First Order's defeat. The Sith Eternal's forces were destroyed by the Resistance and the [[Citizens' Fleet]] in the [[Battle of Exegol]], after which the First Order's remaining forces were attacked on various worlds in a pan-galactic [[uprising against the First Order]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Organization==
===Stormtroopers===
{{Main|Stormtrooper (First Order)}}
====Overview====
[[File:Stormtroopers-attack-Jakku.png|thumb|left|220px|Following in the footsteps of their Republic and Imperial predecessors, stormtroopers served as infantry soldiers in the First Order army.]]
The First Order army<ref name="Children from Tehar" /> was largely composed of stormtroopers; inspired by the memory of the Galactic Empire's stormtroopers as well as the [[clone trooper]]s that served the [[Galactic Republic]], First Order stormtroopers represented the latest generation in the history of white armored soldiers in the days of the [[New Republic Era]]. The First Order trained its troops from birth, using combat simulations and live-fire exercises to encourage battlefield improvisation, whereas the Empire's standard routine produced soldiers that were considered less dangerous than their First Order descendants.<ref name="Stormtroopers Databank" />

====Variants====
In addition to the basic infantry unit, the First Order army fielded various types of stormtroopers such as snowtroopers who wore specialized armor for icy climates,<ref name="Snowtroopers Databank">{{Databank|first-order-snowtroopers|First Order Snowtroopers}}</ref> [[First Order SCUBA trooper|SCUBA troopers]] who were equipped with specialized armor for underwater patrols,<ref name="No Escape 1">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 1}}</ref> [[First Order heavy trooper|megablaster heavy assault troopers]] who wielded heavy ordinance weapons, [[First Order flametrooper|flametroopers]] who wielded [[flamethrower]]s,<ref name="Episode VII" /> [[First Order riot control stormtrooper|riot control stormtroopers]] who were assigned to keep order on First Order worlds,<ref name="Riot Control Databank">{{Databank|first-order-riot-control-stormtroopers|First Order Riot Control Stormtroopers}}</ref> rocket troopers who served as aerial infantry,<ref name="Battlefront II" /> [[First Order stormtrooper executioner|executioner troopers]] who carried out executions of traitors,<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and [[First Order stormtrooper gunner]]s.<ref name="TFA ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> The First Order Army had at least one battalion of Sith troopers and [[Sith Jetpack Trooper]]s at its disposal, the 105th Battalion.<ref name="SWCT" />

====Commando units====
The Army utilized specialized stormtroopers for special operations. [[First Order Raider]]s were highly skilled hunters tasked with requiring [[Sith artifact]]s for Supreme Leader Kylo Ren.<ref name="Raider Databank">{{Databank|first-order-raiders|First Order Raiders}}</ref> The [[First Order Special Forces]] had a [[Elite stormtrooper|variant of stormtrooper]] that drove [[elite speeder bike]]s for high-risk missions.<ref name="Hasbro">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Force Awakens (toy line)|pack=Elite Speeder Bike with Special Edition Stormtrooper|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/Elite-Speeder-Bike-Vehicle-The-Force-Awakens-Hasbro.asp}}</ref>

===Other positions===
Other positions in the army included [[First Order officer]]s<ref name="Episode VII" /> and the subset of [[vehicle commander]]s, [[First Order Walker Driver]]s, [[engineer]]s, and [[gunner]]s.<ref name="TLJ ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>

===Rank structure===
{{Main|Rank insignia of the First Order}}
Both infantry and armored units were commanded by trained officers. Stormtroopers used [[pauldron]]s to signify their rank. [[Squad leader]]s wore white pauldrons, [[sergeant]]s wore black, and [[officer]]s wore red.<ref name="TLJVD" /> The [[trooper]] [[Pyre]] held the rank of [[commander]] and had co-command over a [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]],<ref name="Into the Unknown">{{Resistance|Into the Unknown}}</ref> the ''[[Thunderer]]''.<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> The [[Allegiant General]] commanded both the army and navy of the First Order.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
