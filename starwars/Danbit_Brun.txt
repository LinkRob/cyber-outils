'''Danbit Brun''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[lieutenant]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] who served as a [[Deflector shield|shield]] relay systems operator onboard the [[Shield Gate]] positioned above the [[planet]] [[Scarif]]. His job required him to carefully monitor field dynamics to prevent dangerous build up from [[stellar wind aurorae]]. In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /><ref name="ROUVG" /> he was [[Death|killed]] when the remnants of the ''[[Persecutor]]'' and the ''[[Intimidator]]'', two [[Star Destroyer]]s destroyed in the [[Battle of Scarif]], drifted into the gate and destroyed it. Brun had fair skin and [[Color|gray]] [[hair]] and wore a black version of the [[Imperial military uniforms|Imperial technician's uniform]] with a [[Hat|cap]].<ref name="RO" />

==Behind the scenes==
Danbit Brun first appeared in the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which was released in North America on [[December 15]], [[2016]]. He was not identified in the film, but was named by the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released the [[December 16|day after the film]].
