"'''Chapter 7: Call to Oblivion'''" is the seventh episode of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'' and was released on [[April 27]], [[2026]] alongside "[[Chapter 8: The Creeping Fear]]". Its title was revealed, as "Chapter 7: Call to the Oblivion," on [[March 17]], 2026 by the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' Twitter account.<ref name="Twitter" />

==Official description==
''As enemies close in, Maul is forced to make a stand.''

==Plot summary==
===On the run===
The episode opens with an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] hovering over [[Janix city]] while [[TK stormtrooper]]s march through the streets. The Imperial interim authorities impose an overnight curfew and halt public transportation. In the underlevels of Janix city, Maul leads [[Devon Izara]] and [[Rylee Lawson]] through a maglev train tunnel. When Rylee asks if they can trust Maul, she warns him that they can't trust him. When the two discuss escaping, Maul reminds them that they are still being hunted by the [[Inquisitor]]. He warns that if they are putting themselves in danger if they choose to make their own way. 

As they travel deeper into the underlevels, Izara demands to know where Maul is taking them and says that she has to take Rylee back to his father [[Brander Lawson]]. Maul replies that they are free to go once they reach his [[Maul's new safe house|camp]] and adds that he has a [[starship]] that can take them offworld. When Rylee asks if Izara is considering his offer, she reassures him that she is not and tells Rylee to stay close to him. Hearing movement from behind, the trio quicken their pace. 

With Imperial stormtroopers sweeping through the streets of Janix city, Brander believes that the younglings are heading towards [[Sector 10]] and suggests they head there. [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]] thinks they should not be hasty and seeks to reassure Brander that Izara will protect his son. Brander is about to blame Daki for their predicament but the Jedi Master reassures him that they will find the younglings before escaping offworld. 

When Daki asks about Brander's underworld contact [[Rheena Sul]], he replies they have lost touch with her since the raid. The two decide to head to Sector 10. Meanwhile, Maul compliments Izara for fending off the Inquisitor [[Marrok]], prompting her to credit Master Daki. She then asks Maul about his [[Darth Sidious|former master]] who had cast him aside. Maul describes his master's betrayal as devastating but says that was not the deepest wound that he inflicted on him. When Izara asks, Maul confirms that his master's betrayal as is still alive. Shortly later, they are greeted by [[Spybot]], who treats Maul with affection but is hostile towards Izara and Rylee.

===The escape attempt===
Elsewhere, Marrok receives news that a transport carrying another Inquisitor is enroute to Janix. Back at Maul's new safehouse, they are greeted by [[Looti Vario]], who is hauling a crate. In the command center, the group encounter [[Rook Kast]], who glares at both Izara and Rylee. She informs Maul that Imperial forces are closing on their second hideout and recommends evacuating the base. Maul agrees and insists on bringing the two fugitives along. 

Izara tells Rylee to follow her and they exit the command center before Maul can arrange the logistics, After Rylee avoids falling down an unfenced platform, the two continue running only to run into Maul again. When Izara reminds Maul that she told them they could leave to find her master, Maul angrily reminds them that they are on the run from the Empire. He offers her the opportunity to help defeat her enemies but only if she comes with him. 

Izara disagrees that Maul's plan to take out the Empire will succeed, prompting the [[Dark side of the Force|dark sider]] to offer to reveal his plan only if she leaves with him. Maul's remaining followers also converge on them. Eyeing the end of the causeway, Izara motions for Rylee to come with her. Before they can move, Maul uses [[the Force]] to hold her in place. Izara uses her Force powers to break free and ignites [[Devon Izara's lightsaber|her lightsaber]], leading to a lightsaber confrontation.

===Skirmish with the Empire===
Their lightsabers attract the attention of a [[Viper probe droid]], which contacts Imperial forces. Spybot spots the Viper droid and attacks it. Kast warns Maul and the others as an [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] (LAAT/i) gunship carrying stormtroopers descends on the platform. Maul abandons his confrontation with Izara and comes to the aid of Kast and the other mercenaries. Facing a common enemy, Izara joins Maul in fighting off the stormtroopers, who advance with [[blaster rifle]]s. Maul's Zabrak associates [[Scorn]] and [[Icarus]] arrive with reinforcements and shoot down the gunship, which crashes onto the platform. 

More LAAT gunships arrive with reinforcements. Rylee is arrested by a couple of stormtroopers who evacuate him in their gunship. Izara jumps onto a port on the gunship hull but falls off and lands at the mouth of a drainage above the platform. She and Maul stare at each other before fleeing.  

Elsewhere, [[Lieutenant]] [[Blake]] informs Marrok that their forces have engaged with the fugitives in [[Sector Two-X]]. Shortly later, a [[Phi-class shuttle|''Phi''-class shuttle]] arrives and lands at the landing field nearby. The [[Eleventh Brother]] exits the ship. 

===A bittersweet reunion and Maul's escape===
Meanwhile, Brander and Daki reach the mag-lev tunnel where Rylee and the others had traveled earlier. Brander compliments Daki for his correct hunch and confides that he had once worked with a [[Jedi]] years ago and never believed they were [[treason|traitors]]. Daki is heartened by Brander's words and thanks the lawman. The two soon encounter Devon Izara, who has escaped Maul. She tells them that Rylee was captured by stormtroopers, who escaped in a gunship. 

As Brander heads back, Daki asks who else was with her. Izara is unwilling to answer and insists on rescuing Rylee. Daki says that the Jedi presence and has made things worse for the Lawsons. He suggests that the Jedi extricate themselves from the situation so that the Empire would be more lenient towards the Lawsons. Izara is unwilling to abandon the Lawsons to the Empire. Brander tells the Jedi that he is going to rescue his son and leave Janix regardless of whether they help or flee. Brander proposes they start with the [[Janix Civil Defense HQ]]. After Izara volunteers to help Brander rescue his son, Daki agrees to come along. 

After Maul kills the last stormtrooper, Kast orders her [[Mandalorian]] mercenaries to retreat and to travel lightly. A Mandalorian named [[Kebris]] protests and insists that he and his friends be allowed to take their crates of [[Currency|credits]] with them. Kast reasserts her authority and the trio comply. The group including Vario retreat into the tunnels below.

===Imperial custody===
At the Janix police headquarters, the stormtroopers frogmarch Rylee into the main office, which is staffed by [[Tactical Defense Droid]]s under the supervision of stormtroopers. Rylee manages to briefly speak with his father's colleague [[2B0T]] ("Two Boots"), who is sporting a new pair of regulation boots. They are interrupted by Lieutenant Blake, who tells them that Rylee is accused of abetting Imperial fugitives. 

Two Boots expresses disappointment with Rylee. When Blake orders that Rylee be detained in a holding cell, Two Boots protests this is against protocol but Blake threatens the police droid with reprogramming via [[memory wipe]]. Rylee convinces Two Boots to cooperate with Blake before being led away. The droid watches with concern while Rylee is led away into the holding block. 

===Challenging times===
Elsewhere, Kebris begins to question Maul's authority, prompting Kast to reassert her authority by elbowing him in the throat and warning him of the consequences of insubordination. Vario catches up and pants. In private, Maul suggests killing off Kebris. Kast tells Maul she is loyal to him but warns that some of the commandos are mercenaries whose loyalty is flexible. Maul says that the recent events on Janix are an obstacle in his greater plan. 

Kast expresses her concern that Maul's interest in Izara is clouding his judgment but Maul vouches for her combat skills. Kast warns Maul that Izara is still loyal to her Jedi Master Daki but Maul thinks that she can still be won over. When Maul tries to reassert his authority, Kast warns that the allegiances of mercenaries can also be severed. 

Outside the Janix police headquarters, Daki warns Brander it will be difficult to breach the precinct. Brander contacts Two Boots via [[comlink]] and asks about his son. To avoid attracting Imperial attention, Two Boots hides the buzzing comlink inside a box full of [[datacard]]s on his desk.

===Facing oblivion===
Maul and the Shadow Collective reach a bridge overlooking a waterfall in Janix's forests. Imperial stormtroopers and LAAT gunships catch up with the group. Maul and his followers manage to take shelter behind a gate before the advancing stormtroopers can catch up with them. The Inquisitors arrive and land on a platform on the other side of the bridge. The two gunships hover on the left side of the bridge while the Inquisitors advance on Maul. 

He [[Force jump]]s to a lower bridge below and the Inquisitors give chase. Maul fights back with double-bladed lightsaber but is unable to match the two dark siders on his own. He retreats into a nearby cave while the others fight against the stormtroopers and gunships above. Spybot grabs a stormtrooper and throws him into the pool below.  

[[Icarus]] manages to shoot one of the gunship pilots with his [[blaster cannon]]s, causing the gunship to crash into the waterfall. Stormtroopers push their way through the gate and advance on Maul. Inside the cave, Maul ambushes the Eleventh Brother and the two fight amidst the rocks and waterfalls. Marrok comes to his comrade's aid and kicks Maul, who gets up and duels with him. 

Above, Vario retreats with the Mandalorians as stormtroopers advance across the bridge. [[Scorn]] gets Icarus to provide covering fire while he joins Kast and the Mandalorians in fending off the Imperials. Kebris and two followers take the opportunity to desert the group and steal Maul's [[Kom'rk-class fighter/transport|''Kom'rk''-class fighter/transport]], the [[Hex (Kom'rk-class)|''Hex'']]. Vario tries to follow them but is rebuffed. Over the [[intercom]], Kebris farewells Lady Kast, who curses him for his treachery.

Before Kebris and his followers can take the ship into the upper atmosphere, their ship is hit by several rockets fired by two advancing LAAT gunships, causing it to crash and explode. While Imperial reinforcements converge on the Shadow Collective forces, their Inquisitors cause further damage to Maul's mechanical legs, causing him to fall to the ground. Scorn comes to his leader's aid and attacks the Inquisitors but is impaled through the chest by the Eleventh Brother's lightsaber.

Maul and Icarus watch with horror as Scorn falls to his knees. The Eleventh Brother uses the Force to hurl Scorn out of the cave. Icarus gives a cry of despair. Marrok and the Eleventh Brother corner Maul at the edge of the cave entrance. The former Sith raises his hands but Marrok counters that the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor]] has ordered his death. Before they can execute him, a defiant Maul uses the Force to bring the rocks holding the cave up down on them. The two Inquisitors retreat before the cave collapses while the screen fades into darkness.
