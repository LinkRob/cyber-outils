'''Are Zero Are Zero''', nicknamed '''Roro''', was a [[protocol droid]].<ref name="ARB" /> By [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">"[[A Rebel Bounty]]" takes place after [[Alexsandr Kallus]]'s betrayal in "[[Zero Hour]]" but before [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba's]] death in {{Film|VI}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]]. ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'' places the events of "Zero Hour" seventeen [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], which occurred in [[19 BBY]] according to ''Galactic Atlas'', meaning that "Zero Hour" takes place in [[2 BBY]], and "A Rebel Bounty" takes place between 2 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and 4 ABY.</ref> the [[droid]] was stationed out of commission inside the [[Holowan droid foundry]] on a terrestrial [[Unidentified planet (Holowan droid foundry)|planet]].<ref name="ARB" /> Between [[2 BBY]] and 4 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Grand-engineer]] [[Lony Coleema]] was kidnapped by the [[IG-88 assassin droid]]<ref name="ARB" /> [[IG-88B]]<ref name="IG-88B">{{ForceCollection|IG-88B|star=2}}</ref> and brought into the foundry. After waking up, Coleema saw Are Zero Are Zero and reprogrammed it. After thanking them, the droid told the [[engineer]] where they were.<ref name="ARB" />
