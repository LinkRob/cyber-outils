"'''And Leebo Makes Three'''" is a short story written by [[Michael Reaves]] and [[Maya Kaathryn Bohnhoff]] that appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 128|''Star Wars Insider'' 128]]. The story serves as a prelude for the novel ''[[Shadow Games]]''.

==Plot summary==
[[Dash Rendar/Legends|Dash Rendar]] and [[Eaden Vrill]] purchase a navigational droid, [[LE-BO2D9/Legends|Leebo]], to assist them on their ship, the [[Outrider/Legends|''Outrider'']]. It becomes evident that Leebo was previously owned by the Syndicate and carries a secret which both the Syndicate and [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire]] are eagerly trying to acquire, leaving Dash, Eaden, and Leebo firmly fixed in the crosshairs of Syndicate boss [[Hox Bilan]]. The crime boss has sent a band of thugs, including the [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] [[Kravengash]], to pursue them and return Leebo to the Syndicate&mdash;by any means necessary.
