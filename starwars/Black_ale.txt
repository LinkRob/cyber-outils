'''Black ale''' was a popular [[Mandalorian]] [[alcohol]]ic beverage. The [[Sheb Niktose/Canon|Sheb Niktose]] cantina on [[Vlemoth Port]], located within the Mandalorian settlement of [[Arumorut]], served mugs of black ale for six [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] each. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Arumorut resident [[Teroch]] could be found on occasion at the Sheb Niktose, drinking black ale with his Mandalorian peers.<ref name="FLT" />
