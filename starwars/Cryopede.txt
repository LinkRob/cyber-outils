'''Cryopedes''' were a type of creature found in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> cryopedes got into the office of [[Wen Delphis]], [[crime lord]] of the [[Sixth Kin]] gang, in [[Club Tarsus]] through the building's vents. She complained of the infestation and a [[Unidentified Club Tarsus technician|technician]] at the club made plans to clear them out, but lost their keycard and so went for a drink in the club with [[Unidentified Sixth Kin enforcer|one]] of the Sixth Kin's enforcers instead. The enforcer brought up Delphis's complaints about the cryopedes and offered to help the [[technician]] find the keycard after another round of drinks.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cryopedes are mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The player has the option to overhear the conversation about the creatures during the game's main storyline and use the information to steal a keycard and enter Delphis's office through the vents; however, they can also gain entry to the office by bribing or distracting the [[Bouncer (occupation)|bouncer]] [[Vrell]] and continue the storyline without hearing the conversation.<ref name="Outlaws" />
