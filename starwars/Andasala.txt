'''Andasala'''<ref name="MVG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Outlaws" /> in the [[Andasala system]] in the [[Cor'ric sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> where a large [[Skirmish on Andalasa|skirmish]] between [[Void Wings]] and the [[Night Fangs]] occured during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], leaving ony a single survivor. A member of the [[Rokana Raiders]] [[pirate]] group heard about the fight and told a comrade about it while guarding the [[Khepi Tomb]] in the [[Khepi system]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Andalasa was mentioned in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune|text=Steal a Look at ''Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune'', Available May 15}}</ref> The [[Andasala|world]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned as "Andasala" in ''[[Galaxy Guide 11: Criminal Organizations]]'', a [[1994]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[Rick D. Stuart]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="GG11">''[[Galaxy Guide 11: Criminal Organizations]]''</ref> The spelling Andalasa was then introduced in ''[[Pirates & Privateers]]'', a [[1997]] supplement for the roleplaying game written by [[Timothy S. O'Brien]] and also published by West End Games.<ref name="P&P">''[[Pirates & Privateers]]''</ref> The [[2026]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'' introduced the corrected spelling of Andasala.<ref name="MVG" />
