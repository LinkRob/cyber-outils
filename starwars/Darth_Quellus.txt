'''Darth Quellus''' was a [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] who served the [[Sith Empire]]. The [[Sith Lord]] was dispatched as an [[emissary]] to [[Aaloth]], a [[Twi'lek]] colony alongside his [[Sith]] minions to [[Occupation of Aaloth|conquer]] the [[planet]]. According to folklore, he used the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] to curse their [[candle]]s to literally burn black. This curse was so potent, that even the [[Star|sun]] was blackened. Eventually, Quellus was forced to escape on his [[starship]] when he suffered burn injuries.<ref name="Kindling" />
