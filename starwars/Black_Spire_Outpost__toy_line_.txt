'''Black Spire Outpost''' is the name for the toy line and merchandise line sold at much of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], based on the name of the [[Black Spire Outpost|fictional outpost]] Galaxy's Edge is located in. These products are sold in various locations in the themed area including [[Toydarian Toymaker]] and [[Resistance Supply]]. The items are branded with the name "Black Spire Outpost" printed in [[Aurebesh]] letters on the brown and orange packaging.<ref name="WDWNT Toydarian" /><ref name="WDWNT Resistance">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2019/06/photos-every-merchandise-item-from-the-resistance-supply-at-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=PHOTOS: Every Merchandise Item (with Prices) From Resistance Supply in Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|work=WDW News Today|archivedate=20230103005341}}</ref>

Other product lines are dedicated to some specific shops such as [[Droid Depot]], [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities]], and the [[Creature Stall]].
