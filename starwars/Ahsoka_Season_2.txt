'''''Ahsoka'' Season 2''' is the second season of the live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]''. It will air exclusively on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Announcement" /> in early [[2027]].<ref name="Premiere" /> On [[January 9]], [[2024]], the second season was announced to be in development by [[Dave Filoni]] together with the announcement of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''.<ref name="Announcement" /> At [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan]] in [[April]] [[2025]], Filoni said he would begin filming the season after he returned from Celebration.<ref name="Filming">{{Twitter|sw_holocron|status/1913038828195238397|''Star Wars'' Holocron|date=2025-04-18|quote=Dave Filoni says he will film AHSOKA Season 2 when he gets back from Japan|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/txml1}}</ref> At Celebration it was also announced to have a [[2026]] release date.<ref name="Concept Teaser">{{CelebrationTrailer|[[Ahsoka Season 2|''Ahsoka'' Season 2]] concept teaser|Celebration Japan (2025)}}</ref> This was reaffirmed by [[Star Wars Insider 237|''Star Wars Insider'' 237]],<ref name="Insider 237">{{InsiderCite|237|Launchpad}}</ref> however it was later delayed to 2027.<ref name="Premiere" />

Around Celebration, the [[Disney+]] content slider mistakenly listed a release date of Fall 2025 for a "Season 3,"<ref name="Disney+ Image 1">[[Disney+]] content slider {{C|see [[:File:Ahsoka-S3-DisneyPlusSlider.png|this image]]}}</ref> an update that was meant for ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''.<ref name="YJA Announcement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-young-jedi-adventures-season-3|text=''Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures'' Season 3 Coming This Fall!}}</ref> This was later changed to list 2026,<ref name="Disney+ Image 2">Updated [[Disney+]] content slider {{C|see [[:File:Ahsoka-S2-DisneyPlusSlider.png|this image]]}}</ref> and was later removed.

==Development==
On [[October 7]], [[2025]], [[Ezra Bridger]] actor [[Eman Esfandi]] confirmed that filming had wrapped and posted a video of two extras in [[stormtrooper armor]], confirming the appearance of standard [[stormtrooper]]s in the season.<ref name="EsfandiWrap">{{Instagram|username=emanesfandi|url=reel/DPf0DJACLXa/|name=[[Eman Esfandi]]|date=2025-10-07|quote=That’s a wrap, mate.|archivefile=File:EmanEsfandi-AhsokaS2Wrap-Instagram.png}}</ref> According to a fan who then attended Comic Con Scotland on [[October 12]], [[Rosario Dawson]] revealed Season 2 had a smaller budget than [[Ahsoka Season 1|''Ahsoka'' Season 1]] yet would depict a larger scale than Season 1.<ref name="GemmRosario">{{Twitter|username=Gemm1319|url=status/1977391693784555864|name=Gemm1319|date=2025-10-12|quote=Ahsoka s2 has a smaller budget than s1 but goes much much bigger says Rosario.|archivedate=20251012151132}}</ref> Dawson also reportedly confirmed that she had been filming scenes with [[Hayden Christensen]] a week before the panel and reiterated that filming, by that point, was officially complete.<ref name="GemmRosario2">{{Twitter|username=Gemm1319|url=status/1977380513728917774|name=Gemm1319|date=2025-10-12|quote=Rosario and hayden were filming last week, and Rosario officially says filming has wrapped!|archivedate=20251012142707}}</ref>
