'''Becky Smoochenbacher''' was a resident of the [[Upper Ring]] section of the [[space station]] known as [[The Wheel]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. Smoochenbacher and fellow Wheel resident [[Zander Freemaker]] had romantic feelings for one other. However, neither of them believed the other felt the same. Freemaker often mentioned his feelings for Smoochenbacher around his [[Freemakers/LEGO|family]], much to his sister [[Kordi Freemaker]]'s annoyance. After Freemaker left the Wheel with his family in [[4 ABY]] and did not return, Smoochenbacher joined the Empire as an [[Imperial weapons technician]].

Smoochenbacher was eventually deployed to the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]] above the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. The Freemakers&mdash;who had since joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]&mdash;were captured aboard the [[Death Star]] after a failed attack on the station. The [[Kyber Saber]] was taken from Zander's [[Jedi]] brother [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] and was given to Smoochenbacher with orders to deliver it to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. She instead freed the Freemakers, switching sides and joining the Rebellion. Zander and Smoochenbacher confessed their love for each other and shared several kisses before separating with the promise to meet again on Endor. They fulfilled their promise and met after the destruction of the Death Star and death of the [[emperor]]. Years later in [[34 ABY]], Smoochenbacher and Freemaker's daughter [[Moxie Freemaker/LEGO|Moxie Freemaker]] shared her eagerness to help the [[Resistance]] against the [[First Order]] as Smoochenbacher and Zander had helped the Rebels against the Empire.

==Biography==
===The Galactic Empire===
{{Quote|My name is Zander Freemaker, and I come from The Wheel, and I like starships, and pickles, a-and Becky Smoochenbacher from the Upper Ring!''"<br />"''It hasn't done anything to you yet.|Zander Freemaker and Darth Vader|<ref name="Zander">{{FreemakerAdventures|Zander's Joyride}}</ref>}}
[[File:ZanderConfesses-Joyride.png|thumb|right|300px|Becky Smoochenbacher had romantic feelings toward Zander Freemaker (pictured).]]
Becky Smoochenbacher resided on the [[Upper Ring/LEGO|Upper Ring]] of [[the Wheel]] [[space station]]<ref name="Zander" /> at some point between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{FreemakersDate}}</ref> She had a crush on fellow Wheel resident [[Zander Freemaker]], but believed he had no idea who she was.<ref name="ROTJ" /> When Freemaker was captured by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer]] [[Vendetta (Imperial-class)|''Vendetta'']], he was threatened with torture by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]&mdash;who assumed Freemaker was a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Upon seeing an [[IT-O interrogation droid]], Freemaker quickly confessed his name, residency at The Wheel, his love for [[pickle]]s, and affinity for Smoochenbacher, requesting that Vader kept that information between them.<ref name="Zander" /> 

In 4 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> The Empire placed a [[bounty]] on Zander's brother [[Rowan Freemaker]], and they alongside their sister [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]], decided not to return to the Wheel.<ref name="Kyber">{{FreemakerAdventures|Return of the Kyber Saber}}</ref> Smoochenbacher joined the Galactic Empire's [[Imperial Navy|Navy]] as an [[Imperial weapons technician]] after the Freemakers did not return.<ref name="ROTJ" />

===Zander's return to the Wheel===
{{Quote|I just can't wait to see it all again; Freemaker Salvage and Repair, underground podraces, Becky Smoochenbacher...''"<br />"''No, no, no, no. No Becky, no smooching. We can't see anyone who knows us.''"<br />"''Oh, that's not a problem. She has no idea who I am.|Zander and Kordi Freemaker|<ref name="Return" />}}
[[File:CoolZander-ReturnWheel.png|thumb|left|300px|Upon returning to the Wheel, Zander was excited to see Smoochenbacher.]]
Zander, Kordi, and Rowan&mdash;who had since joined the Rebel Alliance&mdash;later returned to the Imperial-occupied Wheel in search for an [[energy matrix activator]] for the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']] [[starship]]. While approaching the station, Zander shared his excitement to see everything on the wheel again, including Smoochenbacher. Kordi then quickly reminded Zander that they could not be seen by anyone they used to know from the Wheel. Zander then shared his belief that Smoochenbacher had no idea who he was.<ref name="Return" />

After finding their way onto the Wheel, the Freemakers traveled through the ventilation shafts to their old [[Freemaker Salvage and Repair]] hangar, where they believed the matric activator would be found. The family then saw the hangar had been transformed into [[Wick Cooper's Caf Bean and Kitsch Klatch]] [[cantina]]. Realizing they had time before they were able to enter the busy cantina, Zander began to ponder connecting with Smoochenbacher, but Kordi threatened to throw him out of an [[airlock]] if he finished his sentence.<ref name="Return" />

[[File:BeckySmoochextinguisher-ReturnWheel.png|thumb|right|300px|Zander Freemaker professes his love to a fire extinguisher he believed to be Smoochenbacher]]
Later, when fleeing on a [[speeder]] from [[Graballa/LEGO|Graballa]] the [[Hutt]]'s [[bounty hunter]]s through a market on the Wheel, Zander spotted whom he believed to be Smoochenbacher before Kordi reminded him to keep his [[eye]]s front. Not paying attention, Zander crashed the speeder and in a daze believed he was looking up at Smoochenbacher, confessing his love to her. Kordi then interrupted him, informing the dazed [[Technician|mechanic]] that he was talking to a [[fire extinguisher]] with a mop on top of it. Undetered, Zander reached for the fire extinguisher and yelled that nothing could extinguish his love for her before being dragged away by Kordi. When leaving the station, Zander spoke to himself bidding Smoochenbacher farewell, believing that despite the [[Galactic Civil War]] keeping them apart, he knew in his [[heart]] that they would be together again. Rowan then spoke up, reminding Zander that they were never together to begin with, which Zander defensively yelled was a technicality.<ref name="Return" />

===End of the war===
{{Quote|Get to your ship as fast as you can. I will find you on Endor.|Smoochenbacher freeing the Freemakers|<ref name="ROTJ" />}}
After crash landing on a [[Unidentified planet (Baird Kantoo)|planet]]<ref name="Pinnacle">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Pit and the Pinnacle}}</ref> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Escape">{{FreemakerAdventures|Escape from Coruscant}}</ref> while fleeing from the Imperial [[droid]] [[M-OC/LEGO|M-OC]], the Freemakers discovered and obtained [[kyber crystal]]s needed for the ''Arrowhead''. Thrilled, Zander asked if they could now finish building the ''Arrowhead'', win the Galactic Civil War to save [[the galaxy]], and get him a date with Smoochenbacher. Rowan replied saying they could do two out of those three, and Zander reclined in his chair saying he does not need to save the galaxy.<ref name="Return" />

During the [[Battle of Endor]], Smoochenbacher was present on the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]] above the [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. The Freemakers&mdash;alongside the [[Mon Calamari]] shipbuilder [[Quarrie]]&mdash;crashed their ship ''[[StarScavenger]]'' into a hangar of the [[Death Star]] where they were captured soon after. Placed in [[stuncuffs]], they were marched through a hall on the station to a detention cell when Smoochenbacher encountered the group and was given Rowan's [[Kyber Saber]] by a [[stormtrooper]]. The stormtrooper then commanded her to deliver the saber to [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. Ignoring the stormtrooper's command, Smoochenbacher made her way to the Freemaker's holding cell and told the group that they needed to get off of the Death Star immediately. She removed the stuncuffs from each of them as Zander asked who she was, not recognizing her with the [[Jawbone helmet|helmet]] on.<ref name="ROTJ" />

[[File:SmoochenmakerReunited-ROROTJ.png|thumb|left|300px|Smoochenbacher and Freemaker reunited on Endor after the destruction of the Death Star II.]]
She removed the [[helmet]] and said that she was someone who loved him as Zander sat with his [[mouth]] hanging open, releasing multiple gasps. Smoochenbacher told Freemaker that she left the Wheel after he did not return, and admitted that she was on the wrong side after discovering his affiliation with the Rebel Alliance. Finally able to form words, Zander yelled her name. Smoochenbacher was surprised&mdash;believing he had no idea who she was&mdash;and told him that she had a crush on him forever. They kissed several times before Smoochenbacher told the group they would need to return to their ship as fast as they could, promising to meet them on Endor. She then ran out of the cell and Zander fainted. After the death of the [[Emperor]] and the destruction of the Death Star, a celebration was held on Endor. Becky found Zander among the crowd and the two shared a kiss.<ref name="ROTJ" />

===Later life and legacy===
{{Quote|What were you thinking, Freemaker?''"<br />"''Well, sir, my aunt, my uncle, and my parents all fought for the Rebellion, so I guess I'm just eager to help the Resistance. Maybe a little too eager.|General Mundu and Private Moxie Freemaker|<ref name="All-Stars" />}}
[[File:FreemakerFriends-ScoutingLeia.png|thumb|right|300px|Smoochenbacher and Zander's daughter, Moxie Freemaker, was enthused to help the Resistance as her parents helped the Rebellion.]]
At some point, Freemaker and Smoochenbacher had a [[Youngling|child]], [[Moxie Freemaker/LEGO|Moxie Freemaker]].<ref name="All-Stars" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="All-StarsDate">The events of "[[Scouting for Leia]]" occur simultaneously to {{Film|VII}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of to [[34 ABY]]. As [[Moxie Freemaker/LEGO|Moxie Freemaker]] is recruited into the [[Resistance]] in "[[The Power at Jakku]]"&mdash; which occurs before Scouting for Leia&mdash;she must have joined by that date.</ref> Moxie went on to join the [[Resistance]] against the [[First Order]],<ref name="All-Stars" /> a successor to the Galactic Empire.<ref name="CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> In 34 ABY,<ref name="All-StarsDate" /> Moxie was stationed at the [[Resistance base]] on the moon of [[D'Qar]] with her companions [[Ka-Pao]] and the [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|R0-GR]], the Freemaker family droid. Together, the trio attempted to speed up delivery of starship parts on Moxie's [[Parts-U-Needer-Speeder]], but caused more harm than good when R0-GR accidentally provided the wrong parts. The group was interrogated by [[Lieutenant]] [[Mundu]], asking what they were even thinking. Moxie admitted that she was simply eager to help the Resistance, as Smoochenbacher and the Freemakers before her had helped the Rebellion.<ref name="All-Stars" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Ha. B-B-B-Becky Smoochenbacher?''"<br />"''You know my name? I thought you had no idea who I am. I have had a crush on you forever|Zander and Smoochenbacher before they smooch|<ref name="ROTJ" />}}
A [[human]], Smoochenbacher had light skin and wavy [[Color|blond]] [[hair]] with brown eyebrows. She was attracted to the mechanic Zander Freemaker but believed he did not even know she existed prior to their meeting on the Death Star II. When Zander left the Wheel, she joined the Galactic Empire but defected to join the Rebel Alliance when she discovered Zander fought for them, realizing she was on the wrong side.<ref name="ROTJ" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|Take this to the emperor at once|A stormtrooper giving Smoochenbacher the Kyber Saber|<ref name="ROTJ" />}}
As a member of the Galactic Empire, Smoochenbacher wore the black jumpsuit of an Imperial weapons technician along with the uniform's helmet, [[glove]]s, and [[boot]]s. While on the Death Star II, she was given the Kyber Saber and was commanded to deliver it to the Emperor. Instead, she brought it back to Rowan Freemaker when freeing the family.<ref name="ROTJ" />
