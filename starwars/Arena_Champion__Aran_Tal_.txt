The '''Arena Champion''' was a set of [[armor]] worn by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Aran Tal]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Clan Tal Warrior|standard costume]]. This [[Mandalorian armor|armor]] was ornate, and alongside the [[Arena Heroics]] [[blaster pistol]]s, it was seemingly earned from achieving great feats within the [[Grand Arena]].<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arena Champion was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Aran Tal, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 1.0.0 update alongside Season 1 on [[June 4]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Arena Heroics]]. This costume could be unlocked by reaching Tier 80 on the Season 1 Arena Pass' premium track.<ref name="Hunters" />
