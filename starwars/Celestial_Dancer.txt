'''''Celestial Dancer''''' was a [[YT-2000 light freighter/Legends|YT-2000 light freighter]].

==History==
The ''Dancer'' was used during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]] on [[Pamorjal]] by the [[Pamorjal Freeman's League]], a resistance group that opposed the operations of mega-corporations on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]]. In [[3 ABY/Legends|3 ABY]], some members of the league attacked a group of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] fighters and destroyed their [[Starship/Legends|ship]], as they had been tricked by [[Thaum Rystra]] into believing that the Alliance posed them a threat. The League members soon realized their mistake and gave the ''Dance'' to the Alliance agents, to replace their lost ship. They flew it to a [[ZXK-100346 system/Legends|distant system]] and confronted Rystra there.

They continued to used in over the next few years, following the establishment of the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]]. In [[7 ABY/Legends|7 ABY]], they flew it to the [[Tascollan Nebula/Legends|Tascollan Nebula]], where they confronted Rystra again, inside the [[Temple of Pain]], which they landed the ''Dancer'' in.
