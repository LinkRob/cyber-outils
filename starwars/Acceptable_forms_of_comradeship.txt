'''Acceptable forms of comradeship''' was a piece of instructional material circulated among [[Imperial Military]] personnel. It advised [[soldier]]s that although a level of comradeship to their peers was expected, feelings of family-like kinship would be frowned upon by senior [[officer]]s. It was used by [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] as a source for his post-[[Galactic Civil War|war]] [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' and featured in the chapter "Fracture and Cinder".<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Acceptable forms of comradeship was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' which was published in [[July 9]], [[2024]] by [[Dorling Kindersley]].<ref name="Rise" />
