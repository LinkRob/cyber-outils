The '''Alliance Civil Government''' was the governmental command council of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], existing alongside the [[Alliance Military]]. It was led by [[Mon Mothma]], the [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] of the Rebel Alliance. Within the Civil Government was the [[Alliance Cabinet]], made up of six members with different areas of responsibility. It was the aim of the Alliance Civil Government to [[New Republic|restore]] the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Galactic Senate]], upon which the Civil Government modeled itself.

==Description==
The Alliance Civil Government functioned as the political leadership of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], alongside the [[Alliance Military]] that acted as the paramilitary force of the Rebellion. The Civil Government was formed around the ideals of democracy, with its goal being to restore the [[Galactic Republic]] that was supplanted by the [[Galactic Empire]], and bring about a true democratic [[Galactic Senate]] once more.<ref name="Ultimate Visual Guide" /> A degree of resistance could at times exist between the governmental and military sides of the Alliance, with one rebel naval crewmember writing off [[Minister]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] as someone who only gave the occasional anti-Imperial speech.<ref name="The Bridge">{{StoryCite|story=The Bridge|book=Age of Resistance Special 1}}</ref>

At the head of the Civil Government was its Chief of State, [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]]. Under her leadership was the [[Alliance Cabinet]], representing military and civilian representatives. The cabinet, six individuals in total, represented diverse points of view in order to encourage debate and disagreement in a democratic form of government. This sometimes led to gridlock within the Civil Government, so Mothma, though wary of the same type of executive power wielded by the Galactic Empire that the Alliance stood in opposition to, would sometimes have to wield a firm hand in ensuring that decisions were reached.<ref name="Ultimate Visual Guide" />

The members of the Alliance Cabinet each had different responsibilities in Alliance affairs. Senator [[Nower Jebel]] of [[Uyter]] served as the Alliance [[Minister of Finance (Alliance)|Minister of Finance]]. [[Vasp Vaspar]], senator of the [[Taldot sector]], was the [[Minister of Industry]]. Senator [[Tynnra Pamlo]] of [[Taris]] served as [[Minister of Education]]. [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], former senator of [[Alderaan]], also served in the cabinet, and he was one of the earliest initiators of rebellion as well as an early organizer of direct resistance against the Empire.<ref name="Ultimate Visual Guide" /> As a minister in the Rebel Alliance, Amilyn Holdo gave anti-Imperial speeches and was transported via rebel starships, such as the arms smuggling [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Candor]]''. Governmental figures such as Holdo were supposed to avoid the dangers of combat, although Holdo proved herself a capable military leader and assumed command over the ''Candor'' despite initial doubts from crewmembers.<ref name="The Bridge" />

==History==
[[File:Rebel_Fleet_above_Scarif.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Rebel Alliance Fleet was sent to Scarif.]]
The Alliance Civil Government was formed sometime prior to the [[Battle of Scarif]] and acted as the political half of the Rebel Alliance command system. The ultimate aim of the Civil Government, and of the Alliance itself, was to restore the Republic and its Senate. Before the Battle of Scarif heralded in the beginning of open warfare, however, some members of the Alliance state were of the belief that the rebellion needed to enter negotiations with the Empire, hoping that they could convince [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] to give up his power.<ref name="Ultimate Visual Guide" /> In [[1 BBY]], members of the Civil Government debated how to respond to the threat of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. Ultimately, the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] was sent to the planet [[Scarif]] to aid in the theft of the [[Death Star plans]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

Former [[Apprentice Legislator]] Amilyn Holdo joined the Alliance and secured the position of minister, in which position she gave anti-Imperial speeches on some days and went from location-to-location aboard rebel ships. However, one [[Quarren]] crewmember aboard the CR90 ''Candor'', a vessel upon which Holdo ventured aboard<ref name="The Bridge" /> between around [[2 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> held a low opinion of the civil government member, believing she was more focused on coloring her hair instead of helping the Alliance. Holdo, however, would prove such doubts wrong when the ''Candor'' suddenly fell under [[Attack on the Candor|attack]] by an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]]. Holdo quickly made her way to the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] and, discovering [[Captain]] [[Jardar]] [[Death|dead]], assumed command as the highest ranking Alliance member aboard. Thanks to her quick thinking, the ''Candor'' managed to escape the [[Star Destroyer]] and even disable it, much to the surprise of a senior rebel leader. Having already heard positives about Holdo from her friend [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and suddenly realizing they were true, the senior rebel awarded Holdo a military command as the ''Candor''{{'s}} new captain.<ref name="The Bridge" />

The Rebellion's goals were achieved towards the end of the [[Galactic Civil War]], when the Rebel Alliance gave rise to the [[New Republic]] and a new [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>
