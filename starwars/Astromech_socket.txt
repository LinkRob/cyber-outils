The '''astromech socket''',<ref name="galtrivia">{{SW|url=wings-of-the-master-trivia-gallery-2|text="Wings of the Master" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> also known as the '''astromech slot''', '''astromech port''', '''droid slot''', or '''droid socket''', was a component of some [[starship]]s, usually [[snub-fighter]]s, where an [[astromech droid]] could be attached [[Hull|externally]] to provide piloting assistance via a [[computer]] [[Scomp link|interface]] within the socket, and to provide maintenance to the vessel when required.

Astromech sockets were typically present near a ship's core mechanical compartment, namely the [[engine]]s, of which could be in close proximity to the socket for maintenance accessibility.

In some cases, like the ''[[Phantom]]'', an astromech was needed for a ship to enter [[hyperspace]].<ref name="galtrivia" />
