The '''Forosnag Attack Battalion''' was a [[battalion]] of the private security force [[Sef Con]] that used creatures known as [[forosnag]]s. The battalion was called to assist when [[Han Solo]], [[Chewbacca]] and [[Peekpa]] [[Slicer|sliced]] into the [[Grimdock Archive]] during a [[Blockade of Substation Grimdock|standoff]] at [[Substation Grimdock]]. Peekpa sliced into the [[prison]] itself, opening the floor below the battalion's troopers. The troopers and forosnags who survived the fall were [[Death|killed]] and eaten by the [[bastak]]s that inhabited the lower levels of Substation Grimdock.<ref name="Last Shot" />
