The '''Fifteenth Era''' was a period of [[time]] in the history of the [[Unknown Regions]]' [[Garwian Unity]] nation.<ref name="CR" /> By [[19 BBY]],<ref name="FlashDate">The flashback sequence in which [[Dame]] [[Mimott]] shows her craftsmanship to [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] and [[Ar'alani]] takes place prior to the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', which are set in [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Garwian]] [[dame]] [[Mimott]] showcased an article of [[clothing]] to the visiting [[Chiss officer]]s [[Ar'alani]] and [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]]&mdash;[[core name]] "Thrawn"&mdash;that was created by a refurbished [[Mimott's sewing machine|sewing machine]] from the Fiteenth Era.<ref name="CR" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fifteenth Era was mentioned in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="CR" />
