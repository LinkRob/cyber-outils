'''Cavas''' was a [[Color|beige]] [[gas giant]] [[planet]] located within the [[Bormea sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Galactic Empire]] carried out an incursion into the Bormea sector, using jamming ships to cause communications blackouts for the [[New Republic]]. [[Commander]] [[Lindon Javes]] sent [[Vanguard Squadron]] to investigate these blackouts over Cavas and re-establish communications.

==Description==
[[File:Cavas-and-moons-Squadrons.png|thumb|left|200px|Cavas and its moons]]
Cavas was a [[gas giant]] [[planet]] located in the [[Brentaal system|Brentaal System]], within the [[Bormea sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]]. It was [[Color|beige]] with streaks of light green. It was orbited by two moons, one blue and one red.<ref name="Squadrons" />

==History==
{{Quote|As you've no doubt experienced, the Empire is jamming communications across the sector. I fear these blackout could be a prelude to invasion.|Commander Lindon Javes briefs Vanguard Squadron|<ref name="Squadrons" />}}
[[File:Cavas-X-wings-arrive-Squadrons.png|thumb|right|200px|Vanguard Squadron arrive over Cavas]]
Four years after [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]], the [[Galactic Empire]] sent [[IGV-55 surveillance vessel|IGV-55 jamming ships]] to Cavas, to cause communications blackouts for the [[New Republic]] operating in the Bormea Sector. [[Commander]] [[Lindon Javes]] feared of an Imperial invasion, and sent [[Vanguard Squadron]] to investigate these blackouts and re-establish communications. After finding the wreckage of a New Republic scout ship, Vanguard Squadron soon encountered the Imperial jamming ships, which were escorted by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s and a [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]]. Vanguard destroyed the jamming ships, re-establishing communications, and called for reinforcements. Two New Republic [[CR90 corvette]]s arrived and the remaining Imperial ships were finally destroyed. The Imperial incursion was therefore pushed back and communications across the Bormea Sector restored.<ref name="Squadrons" />
