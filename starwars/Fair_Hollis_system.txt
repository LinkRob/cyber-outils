The '''Fair Hollis system''' was located in the [[Karthakk sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square Q-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fair Hollis [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

The [[Fair Hollis system/Legends|Fair Hollis system]] was originally meant to be introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via the [[2012]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'' by Jason Fry and [[Paul R. Urquhart]]; however, the sidebar that would have mentioned the [[Star system/Legends|star system]] was cut before the book's release. The information regarding the system was later released in [[2014]] as part of [[Star Wars: The Essential Guide to Warfare Author's Cut|''Star Wars: The Essential Guide to Warfare'' Author's Cut]], a series of articles published on the StarWars.com [[Star Wars Blog|Blog]] by Fry and<ref name="Author's Cut">{{SW|url=news/the-essential-guide-to-warfare-authors-cut-part-7-the-grand-army-of-the-republic|series=Star Wars: The Essential Guide to Warfare Author's Cut|text=Part 7 &mdash; The Grand Army of the Republic}}</ref> [[Del Rey]] editor<ref name="Celestials">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-guide-to-warfare-authors-cut-the-celestials|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Guide to Warfare'' Author's Cut — The Celestials|int=Star Wars: The Essential Guide to Warfare Author's Cut}}</ref> [[Erich Schoeneweiss]] containing various cut content from ''The Essential Guide to Warfare''.<ref name="Author's Cut" />
