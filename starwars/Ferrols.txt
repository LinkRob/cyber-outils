The '''Ferrols''' were a family of [[human]]s who [[Homeworld|hailed]] from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]]. At some point, a legend claimed they were in control of the [[E'ronoh monarchy]] before eventually being unseated by the [[E'ronoh royal family|A'lbaran]] line.<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="THRCE">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> [[Viceroy]] [[Ferrol]] and his [[Gender|son]], [[Rev Ferrol]] were part of the family.<ref name="Convergence" />
