The '''Alpha-3E "Esk"''' was a sub-model of the [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wing starfighter]], which served the [[Republic Starfighter Corps]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. This model was equipped with [[ion cannon]]s<ref name="Stay Sharp" /> as well as [[laser cannon]]s.<ref name="V-wing" /> The Alpha-3E "Esk" was meant to operate as an exceptional pursuit craft.<ref name="Stay Sharp" /> At least one Alpha-3E "Esk" [[starfighter]] saw combat, firing its ion cannons at a [[YV-865 Aurore-class freighter|YV-865 ''Aurore''-class freighter]] near a terrestrial body.<ref name="V-wing" />
