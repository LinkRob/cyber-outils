'''''Behind the Mac: Skywalker Sound''''' is a [[Wikipedia:Documentary film|documentary]] short film produced by [[Wikipedia:Apple Inc.|Apple Inc.]] that explores several aspects of [[Skywalker Sound]]'s creation of effects used throughout ''[[Star Wars]]'' media.<ref name="Announcement" /> The film was first announced on [[April 29]], [[2022]] on Apple's official YouTube channel,<ref name="Teaser">{{YouTube|video=arDDZjcbaXI|text=Behind the Mac: Skywalker Sound Teaser &#124; Apple|channel=@Apple|name=Apple}}</ref> where it was later released on [[May 4]].<ref name="Video" />
