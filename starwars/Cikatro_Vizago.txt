'''Cikatro Vizago''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Devaronian]] [[crime lord]] who ran the [[Broken Horn Syndicate]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Vizago's operations included [[Smuggler|smuggling]] and extortion, with his rule backed by dangerous [[IG-RM bodyguard and enforcer droid]]s. On several occasions, Vizago employed the [[Spectres|rebel crew]] of the [[starship]] ''[[Ghost]]'', who performed several smuggling runs in exchange for either [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] or Imperial intelligence.

The Devaronian crime lord also possessed various acquaintances, such as fellow crime boss [[Azmorigan]] and the [[human]] smuggler [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]. Despite his shady dealings, Vizago helped [[Ezra Bridger]] rescue his master [[Kanan Jarrus]] from Imperial captivity after the latter identified himself as a [[Jedi]]. Vizago and Calrissian later abandoned Lothal after a skirmish with Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s in the dilapidated village of "[[Lothal re-settlement camp 43|Tarkintown]]." Sometime later, Vizago temporarily lost control of his own ship to the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]], and was forced to call in Bridger's favor in order to retake it.

The Specters later solicited Vizago's help in smuggling them into Lothal. As punishment, Vizago was sentenced to penal labor aboard the [[Mining Guild]] [[ore crawler]] [[Crawler 413-24]]. Vizago cross paths with the Spectres, who freed him and the other slaves. Out of gratitude, Vizago joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]]. He contributed to the [[Liberation of Lothal]] by helping [[Mart Mattin]] and [[Wolffe]] to summon a pod of [[purrgil]] to attack [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]].

==Biography==
[[File:Indoumodo_forests.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Vizago encountered the Graf siblings and lost a horn to the bounty hunter Momdatha.]]
Cikatro Vizago was [[Reproduction|born]] in [[45 BBY]]<ref name="Birth year" /> and later worked in [[Twin Horns Storage]], named after his own horns, which served as a front for criminal operations led by [[Rask Odai]]. He encountered [[Lina Graf]], [[Milo Graf]], and [[CR-8R]] when they were being harassed by a thug and after he overheard that they need to find a transmission he took them to Odai. Odai took CR-8R's head for his collection as a price for the transmission. The Graf siblings constructed a plan with their new friend [[Shalla Mondatha]] to steal CR-8R's head back. Mondatha smuggled them past Vizago and another henchman [[Rom]] in the storage building by hiding them in a crate full of beetles to hide their life signs. The Graf twins stole CR-8R's head back but Mondatha broke into the storage building anyway. Mondatha was revealed to be 'The Shade', a bounty hunter that was hunting the Graf siblings, who shot Rom and Odai's bodyguard [[IG-70]] and shot one of Vizago's horns off asking where the Graf children were. After the loss of his horn, Vizago renamed and reorganized the Twin Horn Storage to the [[Broken Horn Syndicate]].<ref name="The Steal">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Steal]]''</ref>

===Trip to Seylott===
Vizago found the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' after buying a shipment of arms from [[Ephant Mon]]. Desiring to increase his wealth, he decided to go after one of [[Maz Kanata]]'s treasure troves on [[Seylott]]. He partnered up with [[Azmorigan]] and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]. When they arrived on the planet, Vizago discovered it was under [[Black Sun]] lockdown. He passed his group off as wandering nomadic surveyors while attempting to find the treasure trove, but was confronted by Black Sun thugs. Unfortunately for Azamorigan and Vizago, the Black Sun did not fall for this and chased them off with [[Vibro-ax|vibroaxe]]s.<ref name="SG">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>

===Dealing with the Spectres===
{{Quote|Keep going.''"<br />"''I could. Or I could stop and trade the rest of the bounty&hellip;for another bit of intel you're been begging after.''"<br />"''The Wookiees?''"<br />"''The Wookiees.|Vizago, Kanan Jarrus, and Hera Syndulla|<ref name="Spark of Rebellion" />}}
[[File:Cikatro_Vizago_Weapons.png|thumb|right|220x220px|Vizago inspects a stolen Imperial Blaster]]
Following the [[Spectres]]'s [[Mission in Capital City|theft]] of [[E-11 medium blaster rifle|Imperial blasters]], Vizago informed the crew of the ''[[Ghost]]'' about the location of [[Wookiee]]s held prisoner by the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]].<ref name="Spark of Rebellion" /> Vizago later offered payment to the rebels again, on the grounds that they could [[Mission to steal Imperial weapons|steal]] a shipment of [[T-7 ion disruptor rifle]]s for him. However, the Empire interrupted their dealings, and the crime lord and his [[IG-RM bodyguard and enforcer droid|droids]] fled with half of the shipment without paying.<ref name="DiD">{{Rebels|Droids in Distress}}</ref>

==== Calrissian's farm ====
At some point, Vizago sold a [[Lando Calrissian's farm|farm]] to [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]].<ref>{{Rebels|Idiot's Array (episode)}}</ref> However, Calrissian never paid Vizago. At some point later, Vizago played [[sabacc]] with Calrissian, [[Jho]], and [[Ria Clarr]] at [[Old Jho's Pit Stop]] on Lothal. In the game, Vizago bet a pile of credits in order to force the others to fold, having assumed the Calrissian, who was low on credits would not be able to match. However, Calrissian bet his new [[9000 Z001 landspeeder|Ubrikkian 9000]] [[landspeeder]] to match Vizago's bet. With a new [[Sabacc (hand)|hand]], Calrissian was able to win the [[Sabacc pot|pot]] and claim the winnings, hoping to repay Vizago with it. Vizago was unhappy with his loss and one of his [[IG-RM bodyguard and enforcer droid|IG-RM bodyguard and enforcer droids]] attempted to intervene, forcing Jho to activate a blaster door to prevent its entry. At that moment the game was interrupted by the arrival of an [[K79-S80 Imperial Troop Transport|Imperial Troop Transport]] with [[Stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] looking for Clarr, who had recently defected from the Empire. Vizago hid while Calrissian and Jho dealt with the stormtroopers. After they left, Calrissian and Vizago looked for Clarr, who had stolen the winnings, and as they found out shortly afterward, Calrissian's landspeeder, and fled.<ref name=":0">{{InsiderCite|158|Rebel Bluff}}</ref>

Following the landspeeder using the information it put out on Calrissian's [[chronometer]], they boarded Vizago's ship, the [[Broken Horn|''Broken Horn'']], and flew to [[Lothal re-settlement camp 43]], dubbed "Tarkintown". There they found Clarr had given away Calrissian's speeder which took off and was distributing the winnings to the local citizenry. Vizago, angered by the loss of the winnings, drew his [[blaster pistol]] and fired into the air, dispersing the townsfolk. He then threatened Clarr with the pistol, until Calrissian intervened. They were interrupted by the return of Calrissian's Ubrikkian, followed by the Imperial Troop Transport. The landspeeder came into the town at full speed and crashed into the [[moisture vaporator]] the three had been standing around. The troop transport arrived and the stormtroopers disembarked, confronting Calrissian and Vizago. Calrissian, who had spotted Clarr going around the back of the troop transport, claimed the two were rebels, to which Vizago vehemently disagreed. Clarr then used the transport to shoot some of the stormtroopers before she herself was injured. The townfolk then came out and [[Skirmish in Tarkintown|attacked]] the stormtroopers, killing them.<ref name=":0" /> 

With Clarr heading the townsfolk as they prepared for the Imperial reprisal, Calrissian and Vizago returned empty-handed to the ''Broken Horn'' and planned time off planet until the heat from the Imperials had calmed down. Calrissian, still owing Vizago the money for the farm, offered to play sabacc again for it.<ref name=":0" />

===Rescuing Jarrus===
Following the Empire's [[Raid on the communications tower|capture]] of [[Kanan Jarrus]], [[Ezra Bridger]], [[Sabine Wren]], and [[Garazeb Orrelios]] later approached Vizago for information on where the Imperials were holding Jarrus. Vizago admitted that he knew not where Jarrus was and was skeptical of providing information that could help with the Empire tightening security on [[Lothal]], which deeply impacted his operation. Bridger then asked him if he knew why the Empire was so determined to pursue the ''Ghost''{{'s}} crew or why an [[The Grand Inquisitor|Inquisitor]] came to Lothal, revealing that he and Jarrus were both [[Jedi]]. Disbelieving his claim, Vizago began laughing this off, until he witnessed Bridger using [[the Force]].<ref name="RR">{{Rebels|Rebel Resolve}}</ref>

[[File:WellConnected-Leg.png|thumb|left|250px|Vizago informed Ezra that the Empire was using courier droids.]]
Realizing that Bridger meant business, Vizago made Bridger promise to return him a favor at a later date and brought him aboard his ship, the ''Broken Horn'', where they solidified their deal by bowing to each other. The [[Devaronian]] then explained that because the Empire had destroyed Lothal's [[Imperial Communications Center]], the Empire was forced to resort to passing messages via [[R4 courier droid|courier droids]] and by intercepting the communication logs they carried the rebels might discover where Jarrus was located. Vizago then sent Bridger off, telling him that he would collect on his favor at a later date.<ref name="RR" />

===Trouble with Hondo Ohnaka===
Later, Vizago undertook a mission to deliver five power generators to the [[Jablogian]] crime lord [[Azmorigan]] on the frigid planet of [[Nixus]]. On the way, Vizago encountered the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]], who [[stun]]ned him and then imprisoned him on his own ship. Ohnaka also managed to seize Vizago's ship the ''Broken Horn'' and disable his droids. Before being knocked out, Vizago managed to send a distress signal that was picked up by the rebels Bridger and [[C1-10P|Chopper]]. When the two rebels confronted Ohnaka, the Weequay pirate claimed that Vizago had lost the ''Broken Horn'' in a [[sabacc]] game.<ref name="BOTBH">{{Rebels|Brothers of the Broken Horn}}</ref>

Ohnaka then convinced Bridger and Chopper to assist him in a mission to sell power generators to Azmorigan. After escaping Azmorigan, Bridger and Chopper discovered Vizago in a holding cell in the ''Broken Horn''{{'s}} brig. Vizago revealed the truth and enlisted Bridger and Chopper's help in reclaiming his ship. Reclaiming his master controller from Bridger, Vizago used his droids to attack Ohnaka, Bridger, and Chopper. Ohnaka managed to escape on the rebels' shuttle, the ''[[Phantom]]''. Furious at losing both his proceeds and merchandise, Vizago dispatched Bridger and Chopper back to the planet Garel in an [[escape pod]]. Unknown to Vizago, the ''Phantom'' had been programmed to fly back to its mother ship, the ''Ghost''. The rebels allowed Ohnaka to keep his ill-gotten proceeds but seized the power generators.<ref name="BOTBH" />

===Smuggling the Spectres===
Needing to get back to Lothal, the Spectres hired Vizago to smuggle them through the Imperial blockade aboard his ship, the ''Broken Horn'', in return for a supply of [[puffer pig]]s. Though Vizago was though initially confident that his permits and credentials would get him through the blockade, the [[Imperial Navy]] still detected unauthorized lifeforms aboard Vizago's ship. Upon landing at [[Capital City spaceport]], Vizago's ship was anchored with [[gravity lock]]s and searched by a squad of [[stormtrooper]]s. However, the Spectres escaped by setting loose Vizago's puffer pigs. Not wanting to lose his livestock, Vizago tried to grab one of his pigs but ended up startling it, trapping himself aboard.<ref name="The Occupation">{{Rebels|The Occupation}}</ref>

[[File:Ezra_Vizago_and_the_liberated_workers.jpg|thumb|right|220x220px|Vizago in the mining guild.]]
As punishment for smuggling rebels aboard his ship, Vizago was sentenced to indentured servitude aboard the [[Mining Guild]] [[ore crawler]] [[Crawler 413-24]]. Vizago and other prisoners were forced to work under the lash of the [[Trandoshan]] foreman [[Proach]] and [[captain]] [[Seevor]]. Despite his predicament, Vizago was confident that the rebels would rescue them. Vizago's hopes were fulfilled when the Spectres and local rebel leader [[Ryder Azadi]] boarded the ore crawler. Following a prolonged struggle, the Spectres managed to kill Proach and Seevor while narrowly escaping an Imperial patrol. Vizago bragged to the other prisoners that he knew his rebel friends would come to rescue him, and even though Jarrus initially went along with it despite having no idea that Vizago was aboard, the Devaronian eventually learned that they hadn't come to save him after all. He was initially displeased by this revelation, but in the end was thankful that they rescued him at all. After their liberation, Vizago claimed the captured mining crawler as his own while the other former prisoners joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="Crawler Commandeers" />

===Joining the Rebellion===
[[File:Ore_crawler_skirmish.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Vizago joined the Spectres to liberate Lothal.]]
Following his liberation, Vizago joined the Spectres and their [[Lothal resistance group]] allies, who had set up a base in a [[Lothal cliff dwelling|cliff dwelling]]. Prior to the [[Liberation of Lothal]], Vizago informed Bridger that General Hera Syndulla wanted to speak with him. Vizago was privy to Bridger and Azadi's discussions on how to liberate Lothal from Imperial rule. Azadi's plan involved pretending to betray the rebels and luring [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]] and her forces into a trap at the rebel cliff dwelling.<ref name="A Fool's Hope">{{Rebels|A Fool's Hope}}</ref>

Pryce subsequently attacked the Lothal rebel base with a fleet of [[LAAT/le patrol gunship|patrol transports]], stormtroopers, and [[jumptrooper]]s. During the battle, Vizago expressed regret that he had met Bridger as the Imperials encircled the rebel forces on top of the ore crawler. Vizago survived the destruction of the ore crawler but he and the other rebels were taken prisoner by Pryce's forces. However, Pryce's forces were defeated by Syndulla's reinforcements and a pack of loth-wolves. Following Pryce's capture, the rebels proceeded with the next stage of their plan.<ref name="A Fool's Hope" />

===Liberation of Lothal===
While the main rebel force departed to attack the Dome, Vizago stayed behind with the former [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]] and [[Mart Mattin]], who were tasked by Hera with evacuating the team after they had completed their mission to lure the Imperial garrison aboard and launch the Dome. However, the three were attacked by [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] [[Noghri]] [[assassin]] [[Rukh]], who knocked Vizago to the ground before knocking Wolffe and Mattin unconscious. Despite being driven off by the [[White Loth-wolf]], Rukh managed to steal their patrol transport and attempted to sabotage the rebels' plan.<ref name="FRAF">{{Rebels|Family Reunion – and Farewell}}</ref>

[[File:PurrgilFleet-FRaF.png|thumb|right|250px|Vizago flying with Wolffe and Mart in the ''Ghost'', leading a pod pf purrgil.]]
After the three awoke, Mattin informed Vizago and Wolffe about Bridger's secret contingency plan in the event that Thrawn had discovered their plan to launch the Dome. The three departed aboard the Spectres' ship ''Ghost'' to fulfill that mission, which involved transmitting a coded transmission to a pod of [[purrgil]]. The purrgil wiped out Thrawn's [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]] and then followed the ''Ghost'' to Capital City. After the purrgil pod carried Bridger, Thrawn, and the ''Chimaera'' into unknown space, Vizago and his comrades aboard the ''Ghost'' evacuated their rebel comrades from the Dome. Wren then detonated the Dome, wiping out the Imperial garrison and ending Imperial rule on Lothal.<ref name="FRAF" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You help me, and you'll have a Jedi owing you a favor.''"<br />"''Hmm. Whatever I ask?''"<br />"''Within reason.''"<br />"''No deal.|Ezra Bridger and Cikatro Vizago|<ref name="RR" />}}
Vizago was only truly interested in money and, according to [[Hera Syndulla]], would "sell his [[Parent|mother]] to [[Jawa]]s for a couple of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]." A strong believer in business, he possessed a few, if somewhat twisted, professional scruples that seemed to benefit only him. When paying the ''Ghost''{{'s}} crew a bounty he withheld half in exchange for a small piece of information disclosing the location of a group of Wookiee captives. Vizago once fled an attacking Imperial force after receiving half a shipment of disruptors, stiffing the Ghost Crew and stating he never paid for half a shipment. He furthermore expressed his hope that they would survive to continue bargaining with him, while cynically expressing they would nonetheless be no great loss. His sense of humor was good, if somewhat dark.<ref name="Rebel Visual Guide" />

[[File:VizagosGang-SWRTVG.png|thumb|left|250px|Vizago with his IG-RM bodyguard droids]]
As an information broker he valued information and knew how to bargain to put himself one step ahead of his clients. Of all the ''Ghost''{{'s}} crew he appeared to respect Bridger the most, for his cunning. Despite his firm underworld ties and inherent self-interest, he showed some small degree of compassion, expressing genuine remorse for being unable to help Bridger find Jarrus. He carried a powerful [[Vilmarh's Revenge]] [[blaster pistol]] and piloted a ship named the ''Broken Horn'' after his syndicate.<ref name="Rebel Visual Guide" /> Although he lost his Vilmarh's Revenge when he got taken into custody, he later regained possession of it and also used a [[DL-44 heavy blaster pistol]].<ref name="A Fool's Hope" />

Viewing himself as a businessman, Vizago was willing to smuggle the Spectres in Lothal in return for a payment of puffer pigs.<ref name="The Occupation" /> Despite being imprisoned and forced to work aboard a Mining Guild ore crawler, Vizago remained optimistic that his rebel friends would rescue him. Vizago's hopes were fulfilled when the Spectres boarded the ore crawler, killed the Trandoshan overseers, and liberated the prisoners.<ref name="Crawler Commandeers" />

Though Vizago expressed regret joining the rebels when Governor Pryce's forces overwhelmed the rebel base,<ref name="A Fool's Hope" /> he remained loyal and contributed to Bridger's plan to ending Imperial rule on Lothal. Vizago quipped that Bridger's contingency plan to use purrgil to defeat Thrawn's fleet would work because "it had Ezra written all over it."<ref name="FRAF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cikatro Vizago appears in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' series, where he is voiced by [[Keith Szarabajka]]. Cikatro's body and vestiaries are based on [[Ralph McQuarrie]]'s [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|Episode IV]] concept art for [[Chewbacca]].

===Non-canon history===
In ''[[Angry Birds Star Wars II]]'', he betrayed the team of rebels he was working with to the Empire. He then fought against the Empire.

In the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Droid Tales]]'' episode "[[Mission to Mos Eisley (episode)|Mission to Mos Eisley]]," he was seen buying the T-7 ion disruptor rifles from the Spectres. He then fled as Agent Kallus arrived in an [[All Terrain Defense Pod|AT-DP walker]].
