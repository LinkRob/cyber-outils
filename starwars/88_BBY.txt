The following events took place in or around the [[Standard year|year]] '''88 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3189 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7889''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*By around this point, [[Jedi Master]] [[Keeve Trennis|Trennis]] had left the [[Jedi Order]] and became known as a member of the [[Lost Twenty]].<ref name="DJL Date" />
*[[Dooku]] and [[Sifo-Dyas]] break into the [[Archive of Forbidden Artifacts]] within the [[Jedi Archives]] in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">According to ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]'', [[Dooku]] was eighty [[Standard year|years]] old at the [[time]] of the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the battle in [[22 BBY]], thereby placing Dooku's [[Reproduction|birth]] in [[102 BBY]]. The flashback in ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'' in which Dooku and [[Sifo-Dyas]] break into the [[Archive of Forbidden Artifacts]] takes place when Sifo-Dyas is fourteen years old. ''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' also states that Sifo-Dyas is the same age as Dooku, meaning he was also born in 102 BBY and thus placing the event in [[88 BBY]].</ref>
*Dooku and Sifo-Dyas are tasked with deciphering the [[Lothal Papyri]] as punishment for breaking into the Archive of Forbidden Artifacts.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
*[[Lene Kostana]] secretly gives Dooku a fragment of ''[[The Bestiary of Darth Caldoth]]'' in order to trigger memories of a [[Force vision]].
*Dooku and Kostana duel on the [[contemplation balcony]] of the Jedi Temple.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
