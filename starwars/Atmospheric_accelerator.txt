An '''atmospheric accelerator''' a device made by [[Selo Rovak]] to allow the [[Galactic Empire]] harvest the winds on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]. The [[Imperial wind harvester]] built on the moon required the accelerator to function.<ref name="Outlaws" />

By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Rovak was shunned by her people in [[Kadùa Village]] for working with the Empire. As a result, she relocated to a [[Selo's Shop|shop]] in the [[Mirage (Toshara)|Mirage]] region of the moon.<ref name="Outlaws" /> Later in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> Rovak was approached by the off-worlder [[Kay Vess]] who was hoping she would upgrade a [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]]. After telling Vess that the atmospheric accelerator would really upgrade her bike's speed, she gave Vess two magentic distrupters to help her break into the wind harvester and steal the atmospheric accelerator. Vess later used the distrupters on the facilities outer turbine and made her way through the facility to the accelerator. After she took the accelerator, the facility shut down. When Vess returned to Rovak, she installed the accelerator into Vess's bike, giving the bike the ability to boost.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
An atmospheric accelerator first appeared as an quest item in the quest "The Mechanic" in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
