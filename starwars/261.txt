'''261''' was a [[Color|black-and-gold]] [[R-series]] [[astromech droid]] in service of the [[Galactic Empire]]. At some point before the [[Battle of Yavin]], 261 was among the many droids used to maintain operations aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]]. However, the astromech went rogue, stealing secret documents and escaping in a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]]. The Imperials then hired the [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], who traced 261 to [[Tatooine]] and retrieved it from a clan of [[Jawa]]s. The rogue astromech was then escorted back to the Empire.<ref name="BH" />
