An '''Amnesty uniform'''<ref name="Chapter 19 AD">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 19: The Convert}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> was worn by members of the [[New Republic Amnesty Program]]. At least two versions of the uniform existed. One consisted of a blue single-breasted tunic with a standing collar and piping down the left and right sides of the chest and back, as well as a t-shirt and slacks with similar piping down to the knees. This uniform was worn with a belt similar to that from an [[Imperial military uniforms|Imperial military uniform]], without the [[officer's disk]] on the buckle. Another uniform consisted of a jacket with four front pockets, a t-shirt, and trousers. Both uniforms were worn with black socks and shoes.<ref name="Chapter 19" />

Members of the Amnesty Program wore red, white, and black pins depicted an elongated [[aurek]] on the left breast of their tunics or jackets.<ref name="Chapter 19" />

During his stay at [[Amnesty Housing]], [[Doctor]] [[Penn Pershing]] additionally had blue pajamas, consisting of a t-shirt, trousers, and a robe that tied at the front.<ref name="Chapter 19" />
