The '''Fearless and Inventive Squad Pack''' ('''SWP22 ''') is an expansion for [[Atomic Mass Games]]' miniatures game [[Star Wars: Shatterpoint|''Star Wars'': Shatterpoint]]. It was released on [[January 26]], [[2024]], [[2023]], and contains miniatures of [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Boushh]] ([[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]), [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[R2-D2]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Four iconic heroes join'' Star Wars™: ''Shatterpoint in this new pack! With the Force as his ally, Jedi Knight Luke Skywalker leads this squad of Rebels as a Primary Unit. He is joined by Leia Organa in her Boussh disguise as a Secondary Unit while Lando Calrissian and R2-D2 join forces as a Supporting Unit. Based upon these characters' daring mission to rescue Han Solo in Return of the Jedi, they form a full Galactic Civil War-era squad that works particularly well with characters with Rebel Alliance tags. ''<ref name="Asmodee" />
