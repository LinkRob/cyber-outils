The '''Dette Lawnic Dance Academy''' was a famous dance academy on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. At some point prior to their attendance of a party on the [[yacht]] known as the ''[[First Light]]''<ref name="Official Guide" /> in [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events on the ''[[First Light]]'' in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[10 BBY]].</ref> the [[Twi'lek]] couple [[Hado Gwin]] and [[Boshti Anilee]] trained there for one [[Standard year|year]] before entering the competitive circuit, where they won [[medal]]s in three [[sector]]s.<ref name="Official Guide" /> In [[1 BBY]], Alderaan was [[Destruction of Alderaan|annihilated]]<ref name="Destruction" /> by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Dette Lawnic Dance Academy was mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Official Guide" />
