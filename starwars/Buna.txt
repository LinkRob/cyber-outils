'''Buna''' was a terrestrial astronomical object that was [[orbit]]ed by a small, [[Color|red]] [[Little red moon over Buna|moon]]. Twenty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Sith rebellion]], as several individuals walked the road to [[Osou spaceport]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Genbara]], a [[Unidentified aunty (apothecary chest)|woman]] carrying an apothecary chest shared a rumor about [[Death|bodies]] going missing on Buna's [[moon]]. Allegedly, the [[Unidentified lord (Buna)|local lord]] had sent some of his trusted [[Jedi]] to deal with [[pirate]]s in a [[Unidentified village (little red moon over Buna)|village]] there; when more Jedi were sent after the reports stopped, they found that all of the pirates, Jedi, and villagers had vanished from Buna's moon and there were no [[bone]]s to send to the families. While disregarded as hearsay by one of the people in the group, the [[Gender|woman]] insisted it was true because she had heard it on the [[HoloNet]].<ref name="Ronin" />
