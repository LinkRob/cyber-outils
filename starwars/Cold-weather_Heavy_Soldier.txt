'''Cold-weather Heavy Soldiers''' were [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Cold-weather Soldier|cold-weather]] [[Heavy Soldier]]s that were trained for combat in sub-zero conditions, wielding [[DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle]]s<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> or [[Z-6 rotary blaster cannon]]s. They wore light [[armor]] to maintain mobility, and thus required protection from more durable units.<ref name="SWC" />
