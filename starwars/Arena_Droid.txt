'''Arena Droids''', also referred to as '''Target Droids''', '''Ally Droids''', or '''Training Droids''', were multipurpose [[droid]]s utilized for various activities and roles during the competitive sporting show [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]].<ref name="SWH" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Arena Droids first appeared in the [[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', which was developed by [[NaturalMotion]]'s [[BossAlien]] and published by [[Zynga]]<ref name="SWH" /> on [[November 16]], [[2021]], as a soft-launch to select countries,<ref name="Soft Release">{{Twitter|ZyngaStarWars|status/1460656552734605317|[[Zynga]] Star Wars|date=2021-11-16|quote=We are excited to announce Star Wars: Hunters is now entering soft launch and is available on Android for players in India, Philippines, Malaysia & Indonesia.|archivedate=20220625084625|archivefile=File:ZyngaStarWars-SoftLaunchDate-Twitter.png}}</ref> before the global release on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Global">{{SWYouTube|4dZcq6m3y40|Star Wars: Hunters Launch Date Reveal Trailer}}</ref> The droid model was designed by [[Wikipedia:Concept art|concept artist]] [[Ross Burt]].<ref name="Concept ArtStation">{{ArtStation|subdomain=rossburt|url=projects/YGr3zX|name=[[Ross Burt]]|text=Star Wars Hunters - Arena Droid|date=2024-12-13|quote=We wanted a Droid that we could use within the Arena training grounds, run by the owner Balada the Hutt. Initially that Hutt connection took me down the route of a thug style Droid, incorporating elements of armour from the various Hutt enforcers and skiff guards, and playing with the idea of the one-eyed gatekeeper droid, giving it a cycloptic feel. As it developed, it moved away from feeling bulky and more towards Battle Droid style cannon fodder, a droid Balada would have in plentiful supply for our Hunters to blast away at, and took inspiration from droids like Ev-9d9.|archivefile=File:RossBurt-ConceptArenaDroid-ArtStation.png}}</ref>
