'''''Among Clouds''''' was a romance [[book]] stored on the [[Resistance]] [[freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. The cover of the book was brown, white, and orange, and it depicted the [[smuggler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] offering [[flower]]s to the [[droid]] [[L3-37]], only to be faced with refusal. The book's cover gave the title in [[Aurebesh]], with a heart visible behind the logo.<ref name="LEGOTSS" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date Note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|VIII}} to [[34 ABY]]. ''Among Clouds'' is visible during a cutscene in ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'' adapting the events of ''The Last Jedi'', meaning its appearance must also take place in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> following the [[Battle of Crait]], the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] found and began reading the book during the Resistance's escape from the battle after becoming disinterested in the [[sacred Jedi texts]]. He found the book amusing.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
