'''Ena'''<ref name="Light in the Darkness" /> was a [[planet]]<ref name="Beware the Nameless">''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''</ref> [[Homeworld|home]] to the [[Enami]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the planet was situated within [[Galactic Republic]] [[Realspace|space]]. After the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], the [[Hutt Clan]], encroaching on Republic space, sent a force led by [[Tregarra]] the [[Hutt]] to occupy Ena, stealing [[Foodstuff|food]] from the Enami. Republic and [[Jedi Order]] forces commanded by [[Jedi Master]] [[Keeve Trennis]] eventually [[Liberation of Ena|liberated]] the planet and rescued the Enami.<ref name="Light in the Darkness" />
