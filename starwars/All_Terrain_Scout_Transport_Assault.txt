The '''All Terrain Scout Transport/Assault''' ('''AT-ST/A''') was a [[walker]] and direct assault variant of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]. It featured an advanced [[gyro stabilizer]] system and reinforced feet, which made it slightly taller than the standard AT-ST model. It measured 10 [[meter]]s in height and only had one [[blaster cannon]]. All of its modifications made it more suitable for direct attacks than the AT-ST.<ref name="Walkers and Ground Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
The All Terrain Scout Transport/Assault was first mentioned in the ''[[The Essential Guide to Vehicles and Vessels]]'', a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book released in [[March]] [[1996]].<ref name="Amazon">{{RandomHouseOld|url=delrey/starwars/catalog/display.pperl?isbn=9780345392992|text=Star Wars: The Essential Guide to Vehicles and Vessels}}</ref> The AT-ST/A was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Walkers and Ground Vehicles of the Empire and First Order]], a booklet in a series of canon reference booklets titled the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']], published on [[May 11]], [[2021]].<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|lang=fr|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Official Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref>
