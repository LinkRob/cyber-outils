After the [[Skirmish aboard the Chimaera|break out]] aboard the ''[[Chimaera]]'', the [[Galactic Empire]] found the [[Grysk Hegemony]] colonizing the [[moon]]s surrounding the [[planet]] of [[Mokivj]]. The battle would lead to the rescue of eight [[Force-sensitive]] [[Chiss]] [[Gender|girls]] who were held in [[hibernation chamber]]s down in the old [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Mokivj droid factory|droid factory]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

[[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] took his personal [[legion]], the [[1st Legion]], down to the planet's surface to take out the Grysk holding the Chiss children hostage within the [[factory]]. Meanwhile, [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] dealt with the [[starship]]s above.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />
