'''BDM spydroids''' were surveillance [[Droid/Legends|droids]] employed by the [[Star Tours/Legends|Star Tours]] travel agency. They were used to make sure the agency's repair droids were doing their jobs.<ref name="Starlog" />

==Behind the scenes==
The only reference to BDM droids can be found in an article in an [[1987]] issue of [[Starlog (magazine)|''Starlog'']] magazine detailing the original [[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] attraction. The article was written by [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney Imagineering|Walt Disney Imagineering]] employees, but it is unknown if the droids actually appeared in the maintenance areas of the ride's queue.<ref name="Starlog" />
