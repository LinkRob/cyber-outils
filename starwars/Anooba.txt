'''Anoobas''' were a [[species]] of vicious and [[Diet|carnivorous]] [[canine]]-like [[desert]] [[mammal]]s. The species had a varying tones of [[Color|gray]] [[Hair|fur]] covering its body. Additionally, anoobas also had a distinct lower jaw that was complemented with chin tusks. To survive in the wild, anoobas traveled in packs of 10 to 12 members. The species hunted preys by stabbing them with its chin tusks, then tearing it apart using its long [[claw]]s and fangs.

Throughout [[the galaxy]], anoobas had been used and domesticated for varying purposes. The [[bounty hunter]] [[Embo]] had domesticated an anooba named [[Marrok (anooba)|Marrok]] to assist him in his jobs. Several anoobas were being used to hunt fugitives at the [[Citadel]] prison, though most were killed during the [[Battle of Lola Sayu]] except for one.

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Monster hunters and mercenaries&hellip;lovers and lords&hellip;I don't think you've ever met someone you wouldn't throw to the anoobas to save your skin.|[[0-0-0]], to [[Chelli Lona Aphra]]|<ref name="Aphra 27">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 27|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 27]]</ref>}}
[[File:Anooba_TGTB.jpg|thumb|right|225px|Taxidermic bust of an Anooba]]
Anoobas were vicious<ref name="Databank" /> [[canine]]-like<ref name="Bestiary" /> [[desert]] [[mammal]]s known to be [[Diet|carnivorous]].<ref name="SWE" /> The quadruped species could reach up to 2.7 [[meter]]s in length.<ref name="Everything" /> Anoobas possessed a pronounced lower [[Mouth|jaw]], sharp [[Tooth|teeth]],<ref name="Databank" /> a long tail,<ref name="Citadel rescue" /> and thick patches of [[Hair|fur]] across their spines, and they were able to let out a fearsome growl.<ref name="Databank" />Their hind legs were smaller than their front legs.<ref name="Citadel Rescue Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/citadel-rescue-episode-guide|text="Citadel Rescue" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> Anoobas had a chin tusk that jutted out on their lower jaw<ref name="Everything" /> and long [[claw]]s and fangs.<ref name="SWE" /> Small anoobas lacked a chin tusk.<ref name="Everything" />

Anoobas could be found on [[Tatooine]], [[Nal Hutta]] and throughout the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]].<ref name="Databank" />

==Behavior==
Wild anoobas could be dangerous and they traveled in packs of 10 to 12 members.<ref name="Everything" /> They stabbed their prey with their chin tusks and then tore the prey apart using their long claws and fangs.<ref name="SWE" /> Anoobas could be trained to be pets,<ref name="Databank" />guards,<ref name="Citadel rescue" />or hunters.<ref name="Season 5 Bestiary">{{SW|url=news/monsters-of-the-clone-wars-a-season-five-bestiary|text=Monsters of ''The Clone Wars'': A Season Five Bestiary|nolive=1}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:Anooba-Bestiary.jpg|250px|thumb|left|An anooba]]
The [[Kyuzo]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Embo]] owned an anooba named [[Marrok (anooba)|Marrok]], whom he had trained to assist him in his jobs.<ref name="Databank" />Marrok was loyal to Embo and well-trained. He helped Embo during combat and retrieved Embo's [[hat]] that was used as a [[weapon]] and a shield.<ref name="Marrok Databank">{{Databank|marrok-creature|Marrok (creature)}}</ref> Marrok was one of the smaller anoobas that lacked the prominent chin tusk.<ref name="Bestiary" /> Marrok had died by the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Jakku]]<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> but by the [[New Republic Era]], Embo owned another anooba<ref name="FebTrailer">{{SWYouTube|IHWlvwu8t1w|The Mandalorian and Grogu &#124; Official Trailer &#124; In Theaters May 22}}</ref> named [[Keibu]].<ref name="75451HuttDroidShowdown">{{LEGOCite|num=75451|name=Hutt Palace Sentry Droid Showdown}}</ref>

The [[Phindian]] [[Osi Sobeck]] kept a pack of dark anoobas which he used to hunt down fugitives on the planet [[Lola Sayu]]. During a mission by the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] to rescue prisoners of war from the [[Citadel]] prison, an [[Unidentified Anooba|anooba]] was able to pounce on [[Jedi Master]] [[Even Piell]] while he was distracted by pursuing droids. The beast struggled with Piell on the ground before stabbing him in the chest with its tusk, leading to his death. When [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] discovered the Anooba's ambush, she used [[the Force]] to [[Force push|push]] the Anooba past a nearby cliff to its death in the [[Magma|lava]] below.<ref name="Citadel rescue" />

[[Sabine Wren]], a [[Mandalorian]] and member of the ''[[Ghost]]'' [[Spectres|rebel crew]], painted an anooba insignia on [[Sabine Wren's armor|her]] [[Mandalorian armor|armor]].<ref name="SMRS">''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]''</ref>

In [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[Sliro Barsha]], a [[director]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Security Bureau]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> wore a [[cape]] that used black Annobas fur.<ref name="Outlaws Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Visual Guide]]''</ref>.

Around [[34 ABY]], [[Dok-Ondar]] kept a stuffed anooba head mounted on the wall of his [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities|store]].<ref name="Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge">{{GEAttr|Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Anooba-CR.jpg|thumb|250px|A pair of anoobas kept at the Citadel by Osi Sobeck]]
Anoobas first appeared in the episode "[[Citadel Rescue]]," the twentieth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]] of the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]] television series, which was released on [[March 11]], [[2011]].<ref name="Citadel Rescue Old Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode320.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Citadel Rescue}}</ref>

The anooba was a concept designed for the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']] by concept artist [[Terryl Whitlatch]], who explored the anooba as a possible creature on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Citadel Rescue Guide" /> For ''The Clone Wars'', they were designed by [[David Le Merrer]].<ref name="ArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=davidlm|url=artwork/4er9q|name=[[David Le Merrer]]|text=concept arts for Star Wars, the Clone Wars (2008-2012)|date=2017-04-19}}</ref> The interior of the anooba's [[mouth]] reused the resized mouth from the [[gundark]] model. In "Citadel Rescue", the anoobas were kept in cages that were visually inspired by the [[Wikipedia:Velociraptor|velociraptor]] cages in the [[1993]] film ''[[Wikipedia:Jurassic Park (film)|Jurassic Park]]''.<ref name="Citadel Rescue Guide" />
