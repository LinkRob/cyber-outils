'''Antigrav''',<ref name="OWM">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> also known as an '''antigravity generator''',<ref name="ArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=clintyoung|url=artwork/BkQqll|name=[[Clint Young]]|text=Star Wars: Hunters VisDev|quote=Previs for Star Wars: Hunters-I was Principal Concept Artist on the Natural Motion / Zynga project. Designed and concepted all environment biomes / arenas also characters / weapons and various call outs and POIs.|date=2025-05-18|archivedate=20260303231236}}</ref> was a piece of technology used to counteract [[gravity]]. Antigrav worked by creating antigravitional emanations called [[Repulsor field|repulsorfields]], which act as a negative gravity field that pushes against the natural gravity of a [[planet]].<ref name="OWM" /> Workers in the [[Merj spaceport]] used individual [[Anti-grav container|antigrav containers]] to offload cargo.<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>
