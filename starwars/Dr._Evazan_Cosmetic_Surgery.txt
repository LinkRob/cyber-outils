'''Dr. Evazan Cosmetic Surgery''' was a [[Reconstructive surgery|cosmetic surgery]] clinic operated by [[Doctor|Dr.]] [[Cornelius Evazan]] in [[Pons Ora]], a small [[City|settlement]] located on the remote [[desert]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Abafar]]. By the [[Standard year|year]] [[20 BBY]], it had gone out of business and the building that once housed it was available for lease.

==Description==
Dr. Evazan Cosmetic Surgery was a [[Reconstructive surgery|cosmetic surgery]] clinic located in [[Pons Ora]], a small [[City|settlement]] located on [[Abafar]],<ref name="Missing in Action" /> a remote [[desert]] [[Planet|world]] located in the [[Sprizen sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> An [[Aurebesh]] sign that read "DR. EVAZAN" was located on the corner of the business, and another one that said "COSMETIC SURGERY" was positioned above its entrance. The clinic was situated near the base of the [[Pons Ora water tower]], across the street from [[Admiral Snackbar]] and in close proximity to apartment [[6C]]. When Dr. Evazan Cosmetic Surgery closed down, an Aurebesh sign that read "OUT OF BUSINESS" was placed on its front door and another one that said "FOR LEASE" was put on the establishment's window.<ref name="Missing in Action" />

==History==
Dr. Evazan Cosmetic Surgery was operated by [[Doctor|Dr.]] [[Cornelius Evazan]].<ref name="Missing in Action" /> By the [[Standard year|year]] [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> it had gone out of business and the building that once housed it was available for lease. [[D-Squad]], a [[squad]] of [[droid]]s under the command of [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]] in service to the [[Galactic Republic]], passed by the closed down Dr. Evazan Cosmetic Surgery while walking though Pons Ora after escaping<ref name="Missing in Action" /> [[the Void]], a featureless and seemingly endless wasteland that covered the entirety of Abafar.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />
