'''Bounty Hunter''' was a card matching game played by [[youngling]]s. On board the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'', Bounty Hunter was offered as an alternative to the more adult [[Sabacc]] in the [[Sublight Lounge]].<ref name="SWGS Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)|''Star Wars'': Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)]]</ref>
