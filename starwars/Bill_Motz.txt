'''William Robert "Bill" Motz''' is the creator and executive producer of the animated television series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' and ''[[LEGO Star Wars: All-Stars]]'' alongside writing partner [[Bob Roth]]. He has also served as the writer of the [[2016]] TV-short ''Rufus'' and the [[2013]] film [[Wikipedia:Swindle (2013 film)|''Swindle'']]. He is the co-creators of the 2021 Disney Channel series ''[[Wikipedia:The Ghost and Molly McGee|The Ghost and Molly McGee]]''.
