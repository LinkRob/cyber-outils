The '''Fentersohn system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Expansion Region]]. It was located in grid square I-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and shared its name with the [[Fentersohn run]].<ref name="Rogue">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref>

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Fentersohn system was first mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].
