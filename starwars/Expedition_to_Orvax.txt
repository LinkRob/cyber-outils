By [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Gella Nattai's Pathfinder team|Pathfinder team]] led by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]] undertook an '''expedition to Orvax''' to make contact with the [[Unidentified Orvax species|sentient species]] of the [[planet]] [[Orvax]]. After the [[Outer Rim Exploration Team|team's]] equipment began disappearing three [[Standard day|days]] into the expedition, Nattai insisted they carry through with the mission of making contact with the [[species]]. The decision, however, resulted in the rest of Nattai's team being hurt, with one member, the [[Jedi]] [[Neverez]], bruising his tailbone. Ultimately, the team failed to make contact with the [[Sentience|sentient]] species, and the Jedi Knight came to view the mission as a failure, and was reassigned to take part in a [[mission to Eiram]] to deliver medical supplies to the planet [[Eiram]].<ref name="Convergence" />
