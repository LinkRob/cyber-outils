'''Cilia''' (singular '''cilium''') were short ultra-sensitive [[hair]]s that could be found in certain organisms, including the [[Melitto]]<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> from [[Li-Toran]].<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref> The specialized cilia that lined the body of a Melitto had sensory functions.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" />
