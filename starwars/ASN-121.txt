'''ASN-121''' was a modified [[ASN courier droid]] used as an [[assassin droid]] by the [[Clawdite]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Zam Wesell]]. Shortly before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|start]] of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[droid]] was used in an [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|assassination attempt]] on the life of [[Naboo]] [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], though it failed at its task and was ultimately destroyed by Zam when [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Knight|Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] attempted to use it to reach her.

==Biography==
{{Quote|We'll have to use something more subtle this time, Zam. My client is getting impatient.|Jango Fett|<ref name="Attack of the Clones" />}}
At the height of the [[Separatist Crisis]] in [[22 BBY]], [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] and his partner, [[Zam Wesell]], were employed by [[Head of State|Confederate Head of State]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] to [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|assassinate]] [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] in order to sway her rival, [[Trade Federation]] [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]] to [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|his cause]]. After failing to [[Death|kill]] her by detonating [[J-type diplomatic barge|her starship]] on [[Coruscant]], Fett met up with Wesell at [[Standard day|night]] to supply her a tube containing two [[Poison|venomous]] [[kouhun]]s as a more discreet manner of eliminating their target.<ref name="Attack of the Clones" />

Zam inserted the kouhun-containing tube into ASN-121's delivery apparatus, after which it immediately [[Repulsorlift|flew]] off to complete its task. Upon reaching the bedroom of the sleeping Amidala's [[Padmé's Apartment|apartment]], the [[droid]] [[Laser|burned]] a small circular hole in the window through which to dispense the kouhuns. Avoiding detection by Amidala's [[Astromech droid|astromech]], [[R2-D2]], the creatures nearly succeeded in killing the senator until they were sensed and quickly killed by [[Padawan|Jedi Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]. Upon the kouhuns' deaths, ASN-121 attempted to retreat, but was tackled by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] launching himself at it through through the window.<ref name="Attack of the Clones" />

The droid, with Kenobi clinging to it, hastily returned to Wesell, trying and failing to dislodge the [[Jedi]] on the way by smashing him into a building wall. Upon nearing the bounty hunter's location, ASN-121 was targeted by Zam using the advanced scope of her [[KiSteer 1284 projectile rifle]] for several seconds before finally being locked onto and destroyed in a single shot, sending Kenobi plummeting through the [[Coruscanti]] traffic.<ref name="Attack of the Clones" />

Shortly after its destruction, ASN-121's owner and destroyer, Zam, was killed by Jango to prevent her from divulging his identity to Kenobi and Skywalker.<ref name="Attack of the Clones" />
