'''Ebony thorn trees''' were a type of [[Color|ebony]]-colored [[thorn tree]] featuring green leaves.<ref name="VP" /> By [[5 ABY]],<ref name="V Date">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> an ebony thorn [[tree]] was kept in a [[glass]] tube aboard the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]''.<ref name="VP" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="V Date" /> the [[New Republic pilot]] [[Wyl Lark]] leaned against the case during a meeting aboard the [[Starship|vessel]].<ref name="VP" />

==Behind the scenes==
An ebony thorn tree appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the second installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="VP" />
