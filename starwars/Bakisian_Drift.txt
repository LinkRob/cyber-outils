The '''Bakisian Drift''' was an electrical anomaly<ref name="S&S">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> located in the [[Tragan Cluster]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], a convoy owned by [[Twin Suns Transport Services]] lost a [[GR-75 medium transport]] while traversing the drift. Word then got out about the lost craft and the lucrative cargo available to anyone that could navigate the Drift, attracting hostile [[scavenger]]s.<ref name="S&S" />

==Behind the scenes==
In ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Bakisian Drift was first mentioned in an adventure hook in the [[2020]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Starships and Speeders]]''.<ref name="S&S" /> The [[Bakisian Drift/Legends|Drift]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in ''[[Galaxy Guide 12: Aliens — Enemies and Allies]]'', a [[1995]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' released by [[West End Games]].<ref name="GG8">''[[Galaxy Guide 12: Aliens — Enemies and Allies]]''</ref>
