'''Alpha Twelve''' was a [[callsign]] utilized by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Alpha Flight Group]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. At one point, it was used by a [[Gender|male]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
