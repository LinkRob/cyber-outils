'''Devaronians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] from the [[planet]] [[Devaron]]. They exhibited great [[Gender|sexual]] dimorphism, with the males typically being bald and sporting horns, and the mostly hornless females displaying a full head of [[hair]]. [[Cikatro Vizago]], a [[crime lord]] on [[Lothal]] and the leader of the [[Broken Horn Syndicate]], was a male Devaronian.

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:TressHacnua-ToL.png|thumb|left|250px|Many female Devaronians, like [[Tress Hacnua]], had vestigial horns only.]]
Hailing from the [[planet]] [[Devaron]], the Devaronians were a [[species]] of [[Sentience|sentient]] [[humanoid]]s, characterized by two [[Limb|legs]], a torso, a single head, two arms with hands, fingers, and an opposable thumb.<ref name="DiD" /> The Devaronian head featured a [[nose]], a [[mouth]] filled with pointed [[Tooth|teeth]], and two forward-facing [[eye]]s,<ref name="Rebel Visual Guide" /> which notably came in shades of black,<ref name="Repair" /> blue,<ref name="Chapter 6" /> brown,<ref name="Infested" /> dark brown,<ref name="ANH" /> green,<ref name="Forces of Destiny—Hera" /> pink,<ref name="Mace Windu 1" /> [[Color|red]],<ref name="Rebel Visual Guide" /> and yellow.<ref name="Kada Jahr" /> Both sides of the face were flanked by two pointed [[ear]]s that stuck out slightly.<ref name="Rebel Visual Guide" />

The species had two [[Gender|sexes]], male and female, which were primarily dimorphic. Many males had short horns growing from the top of their heads, while most females were mostly hornless<ref name="Rebel Visual Guide" /> with small vestigial bumps on their foreheads<ref name="NoP" /> along with a full head of [[hair]].<ref name="Kada Jahr" />. However, some females, such as the [[crime lord]] [[Isa Durand]],<ref name="Paths Unknown">{{TBB|Paths Unknown}}</ref> and others<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> possessed full sets of horns as well.<ref name="Paths Unknown" /> Some female Devaronians possessed a thin coat of gray fur on their body.<ref name="Out of the Shadows">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref> Male Devaronians were typically bald— however, some of them grew sideburns<ref name="DiD" /> as well as beards.<ref name="Sisters" />

Devaronians had black [[blood]], and were [[silver]]-based due to the unique blood filtration and cleansing system of the species. Processed through two [[liver]]s, their bodies were constantly cleansed of toxins and carcinogens which allowed them to be highly resistant to [[poison]] and consume [[Foodstuff|foods]] toxic to other species. To this end, [[sulfur]] was used as a [[stimulant]] on Devaron to enhance speed and strength, because inhaling it caused the substance to enter the bloodstream rapidly. Their livers struggled to eliminate sulfur from their system, meaning that long-term use could be dangerous.<ref name="NoP" /> Their skin was also immune to [[fire]] and extremely high temperatures.<ref name="Chapter 6" /><ref name="But What Does He Eat?">{{StoryCite|story=But What Does He Eat?|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>

==Society and culture==
[[File:Devaronian_prisoner_SaV.png|200px|thumb|left|A tattooed Devaronian prisoner]]
Devaronians lived under a matriarchal government on their [[homeworld]]; though males were not prohibited from holding positions of power, few desired to do so. The biological dimorphism of the species carried over into their culture, wherein females tended to be more settled and serious minded, while males longed to wander the stars. Devaronian males were extremely dedicated to their families despite their wanderlust, and were known to send their earnings they accumulated abroad back to their families to support them.<ref name="NoP" />

[[Force-sensitive|Force-sensitivity]] was found in higher frequencies among Devaronians than in other species. Despite this, no strong [[The Force|Force]]-based tradition emerged on Devaron or among their colonies. The [[Jedi Order]] established the [[Temple of Eedit]] on Devaron to train the Forceful [[Youngling|children]] there, with females making up the larger percentage of students as males desired to travel more than the [[Jedi]] culture permitted.<ref name="NoP" />

==Devaronians in the galaxy==
{{Quote|It's alright! You and the Devaronian split up. Find Mando and kill him. Then find a way back to the ship!|Qin to Burg and Xi'an regarding Din Djarin during the Rescue of Qin|<ref name="Chapter 6" />}}
[[File:Burg-CEUEEd.png|thumb|right|220px|Burg, a male Devaronian mercenary]]
Developing [[hyperdrive]] technology on Devaron independently from extraterrestrial assistance, the Devaronians had explored and interacted with [[the galaxy]] before the [[Galactic Republic]] ever made contact with their region of [[Realspace|space]]. To this end, Devaronians were a common sight across the galaxy for tens of [[Millennium|millennia]] before the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="NoP" />

Devaronians have become increasingly prominent in the less civilized portions of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] over the last few decades, in part because of the Empire's distrust of [[alien]]s of all sorts. Under the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], a Devaronian named [[Cikatro Vizago]] led the [[Broken Horn Syndicate]], a [[Lothal]]-based criminal organization. The [[Spectres|crew]] of the [[starship]] ''[[Ghost]]'' occasionally relied on Vizago for funding and information.<ref name="Rebel Visual Guide" /> [[Roland Durand]] was a Devaronian male criminal who forcefully took over [[Ciddarin Scaleback]]'s Parlor, on [[Ord Mantell]]. Another Devaronian named [[Yelkin]] worked as a [[miner]] on the [[thorilide]] mines of [[Cynda]].<ref name="A New Dawn">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref> Shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="ANH" /> a red-skinned male Devaronian named [[Kardue'sai'Malloc]]<ref name="Force Collection">''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]''</ref> was present in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina|the cantina]] of [[Mos Eisley]] the [[Standard day|day]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Luke Skywalker]] met [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]].<ref name="ANH" /> During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|V}}</ref> the female Devaronian named [[Torro Sbazzle]] worked as the [[Executive Chef]] of [[Cloud City]].<ref name="But What Does He Eat?" /> During the [[New Republic Era]], a Devaronian named [[Burg]] operated as a [[mercenary]].<ref name="Chapter 6">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 6: The Prisoner}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The devil-faced Devaronians first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], in which one of them was seen in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina|Chalmun's Cantina]]. The Devaronian patron, whose mask was created by [[Rick Baker]], was added to the scene during the additional photography that filled out the cantina booths with exotic aliens.<ref name="Cantina Roll-Call">{{SWArchive|url=episode-iv/bts/article/f20030502/index.html|text=Cantina Roll-Call: Shedding Light on Some Alien Aliases|int=Cantina Roll-Call: Shedding Light on Some Alien Aliases}}</ref>

The [[2014]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]'', written by [[Adam Bray]], specifies that male Devaronians have horns while females are hornless.<ref name="Rebel Visual Guide" /> However, the [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'' then depicted female Devaronians with horns.<ref name="Uprising" /> The Devaronian female [[Isa Durand]] was depicted with horns in "[[Paths Unknown]]," the second episode of the ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]''{{'s}} [[The Bad Batch Season 3|third season]],<ref name="Paths Unknown" /> which premiered on [[February 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Watch">{{SW|url=news/the-bad-batch-season-3-trailer|text=Watch the ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 3 Trailer}}</ref> This article assumes that the precedent of all Devaronians with horns automatically being male, as established in ''Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide'', has since been [[Retroactive continuity|retconned]].<ref name="Rebel Visual Guide" />
