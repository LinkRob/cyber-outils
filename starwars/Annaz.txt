'''Annaz''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Falleen]] [[gangster]] who was part of the [[Malandro syndicate]] on the [[planet]] [[Tangenine]]. Her syndicate extorted places like the [[Lauren Mel Coelho's family's farmhouse|farm]] owned by [[Remba]] and [[Kelven]]. When their [[farm]] failed to pay up, Annaz [[Death|killed]] them and left their children, [[Brand|Lauren Mel Coelho]] and [[Julien]] orphaned. Lauren took a job for the Malandro syndicate in order to pay for treatment for Julien but got caught and landed in [[Prison|jail]] leaving Julien to die of his illness. When the [[Galactic Empire]] came to Tangenine, they realized the Malandro syndicate was too embedded into the system and had [[bounty hunter]]s clear the way for them. Lauren returned as a [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] named Brand and attacked and [[torture]]d Annaz.<ref name="IB" />
