[[Thane]] '''Chulok''' was a [[gangster]] who led an [[Chulok's organization|organization]] on the [[planet]] [[Metagos]]. They were the union of the [[Farakai]] [[Chu]] and the [[Rodian]] [[Lok (Rodian)|Lok]], who met while working in the [[Mining|mines]] of the planet and eventually bonded. According to [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], they were a deadly warrior who hid behind a [[clown]]ish act. In a message delivered to Master [[Mace Windu]] after his own [[death]], Jinn gave Windu a [[Mission to Metagos|mission]] to Metagos that included breaking Chulok's power, warning his fellow Jedi of the gangster.<ref name="The Glass Abyss" />
