The '''F-143 emergency speeder''', also known as an '''F-143 firefighting speeder''', '''Coruscant fire suppression ship''', '''emergency firespeeder''', '''emergency fire suppression speeder''', '''firespeeder''',<ref name="Beginnings" /> or '''fireship''' (also phrased '''fire ship'''), was a model of [[speeder]] utilized for [[fire]] suppression.

==Characteristics==
[[File:FirespeedersInvisibleHand-CineFex102.png|thumb|left|300px|Fireships dousing the ''[[Invisible Hand]]'']]
The F-143 emergency speeder was a fire suppression ship manufactured by [[Rothana Heavy Engineering]]. Designed to provide essential disaster relief, these speeders carried several tanks of fire-fighting solutions such as water, foam spray,<ref name="Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles" /> and fire retardant agents, as well as miscellaneous fuel-specific mixtures on the ship's undercarriage.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2020" /> These fire-fighting solutions were released through pressurized dousers mounted to the vehicle's four wingtips. A [[tractor beam projector]] was fitted below the heat-resistant cockpit to shift debris during emergencies<ref name="Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles" /> and specialized [[repulsorlift]]s on the exterior assisted the ship's maneuvering thrusters in navigating debris fields.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2020" /> There were also flashing emergency lights<ref name="Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles" /> and a [[siren]] mounted on the [[hull]] to warn passerby ships of the emergency.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2020" />

The interior was equipped with a medical bay where crewmembers could treat serious injuries quickly,<ref name="Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles" /> as well as space for multiple firefighters and rescue personnel. A forward [[airlock]] bay had several [[antigrav]] life jackets and [[Environment suit|emergency space suits]] stored and ready to be dropped at a moment's notice.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2020" />

==History==
[[File:FireshipsNaboo-ShatteredEmpire3.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Naboo firespeeders during Operation Cinder]]
{{Quote|&mdash;Get the dousers on it!''"<br />"''We need more ''help'' here, mobilize all the fire teams!|Fire teams during the attack on Naboo|<ref name="Shattered Empire III" />}}
The [[Galactic Republic]] made use of fireships during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" /> crewed by members of the [[Coruscant Rescue Ops]] equipped with radiation-proof suits and handheld fire suppression hoses.<ref name="Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles" />

Several fireships were mobilized in an attempt to put out the flaming wreck of the ''[[Invisible Hand]]'' as it crash-landed on [[Coruscant]] at the conclusion of the [[Battle of Coruscant]]. Fire crews followed the wreck down to an airstrip landing, dousing flames on the hull with their dousers.<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" />

The [[Royal House of Naboo]] also maintained fire teams, who were mobilized during the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Operation: Cinder]] following the [[Battle of Endor]]. The teams' dousers were brought to bear on the fires caused by typhoon-strength storms.<ref name="Shattered Empire III" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|When the ship ''[the Invisible Hand]'' crashes, there can be fireships pouring foam on it.|George Lucas, at a meeting with artists working on ''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith''|<ref name="Making of ROTS">''[[The Making of Star Wars Revenge of the Sith]]''</ref>}}
The emergency fire suppression speeder was conceived on [[February 7]], [[2003]] by [[George Lucas]] for his [[2005]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Making of ROTS" />
