A '''DX-92 compressor''' was a type of [[compressor]] that could be acquired at a [[supply depot]] aboard the [[First Order]] [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|refueling station]] ''[[Titan]]''.<ref name="Station to Station" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 ABY">{{ResistanceDate|2|11}}</ref> a [[Unidentified First Order stormtrooper (supply depot)|First Order stormtrooper]] requested a DX-92 compressor from a [[KXFO-series security droid|First Order security droid]] at the supply depot aboard the ''Titan''.<ref name="Station to Station" />

==Behind the scenes==
The DX-92 compressor was mentioned in "[[Station to Station]],"<ref name="Station to Station" /> the eleventh episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season Two|second season]] of the television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[December 15]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney release">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref>
