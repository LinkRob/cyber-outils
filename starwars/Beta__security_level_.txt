'''Beta''' was a security level in use by the [[Imperial Military]] on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]]<ref name="Of MSE-6 and Men">{{StoryCite|story=Of MSE-6 and Men|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> It was lower than [[Alpha One]] and higher than [[Lambda (security level)|Lambda]]. [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] upgraded [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]] [[TK-421]]'s security level from Lambda to Beta.<ref name="Of MSE-6 and Men" />
