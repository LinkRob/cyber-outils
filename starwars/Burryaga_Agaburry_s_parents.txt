The '''parents''' of [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]], [[Burryaga Agaburry]], were [[Wookiee]]s who lived on the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. When Agaburry was very young, he was afraid to climb the [[tree]]s of his [[homeworld]]. Agaburry's parents encouraged the young Wookiee to persevere as they climbed with him, teaching him a valuable lesson on bravery.<ref name="Brave" />
