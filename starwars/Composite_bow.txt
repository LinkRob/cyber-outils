'''Composite bow'''<ref name="Opportunity Nox">{{ArenaNews|url=bc2f9118acf24276ad7e894671217e6f-en-gb|text=Opportunity Nox}}</ref> was a type of [[bow]] that could launch physical arrow projectiles. The [[Zabrak]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Nox]] used a Composite Bow called the [[Wild Bow]] during the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition within the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" />
