'''Aeosians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Aeos Prime]]. One tribe of Aeosians were ruled by a [[Unidentified Aeosian Queen|queen]]. During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], representatives of the ''[[Colossus]]'' including [[Kazuda Xiono]] and [[Griff Halloran]] met with their ruler.<ref name="The New World" />

==Biology and appearance==
Aeosians were a bipedal species with long [[limb]]s and webbed hands and feet. They had flat faces with large, forward-facing [[eye]]s and somewhat [[humanoid]] [[nose]]s and lips. They had four small spikes on their chins. Aeosians had smooth skin that could be various shades of blue or green in color. On their heads, Aeosians possessed a dorsal fin that ran from the peak of the forehead to the base of the skull, as well as fins that curved along their cheekbones. Fins could also be observed on the backs of their calves, on the outer sides of their feet, and on their necks.<ref name="The New World" />

Aeosians were [[Gender|sexually]] dimorphic, with males possessing broader skulls, necks, and shoulders than the more slender females.<ref name="The New World" />

==Technology==
Despite the planet's low-tech appearance, at least one tribe of Aeosians was known to have access to relatively modern technology.<ref name="The New World" />

The tribe used [[Aeosian spear|vibro-blade spears]]<ref name="VB">{{Databank|aeosians|Aeosians}}</ref> that could be activated, causing electricity to arc around the blade. At least while activated, the spears were capable of disabling an aged [[B2-series super battle droid]] and blocking blaster bolts. The spears had a second point above that main blade, the tip of which glowed [[Color|blue]] when the spear was activated. These spears had a gold material fashioned onto them, mostly around the point between the staff and the spearhead. A variant of this [[spear]] was used by the [[Unidentified Aeosian Queen|tribe's queen]], which had more gold material fashioned onto it, and two points from the side edges of the main blade, giving it the appearance more akin to a [[trident]].<ref name="The New World" />

The Aeosian tribe also used energy gates to keep prisoners.<ref name="The New World" />
