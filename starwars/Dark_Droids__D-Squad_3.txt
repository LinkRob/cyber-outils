'''''Dark Droids: D-Squad'' 3''' is the third issue of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Dark Droids: D-Squad]]''. The issue was written by [[Marc Guggenheim]], illustrated by [[Salva Espín]] and [[Israel Silva]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 22]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''TWO HEROES OF THE CLONE WARS REUNITE! A ''DARK DROIDS'' TIE-IN! R2-D2 continues to assemble his new D-SQUAD to fight the deadly DROID SCOURGE! Now R2-D2 is looking for a way to help his friend C-3PO&hellip;but does he find LOVE instead? Featuring the return of QT-KT!''
