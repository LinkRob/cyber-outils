'''Filin''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] criminal who worked with his older brother, [[Karrst]]. Due to the many [[Death|murders]] they committed, and due to Karrst's [[Force-sensitive|sensitivity]] to [[the Force]], the [[Knights of Ren]], a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] organization led by an individual known as [[Ren (human)|Ren]], sought to recruit Karrst into their ranks. Ren required Karrst to kill his brother to prove his worth, but Filin instead killed Karrst, attempting to join in his place. However, as Filin was not Force-sensitive, Ren killed him.<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 1" />
