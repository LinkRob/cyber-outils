'''EG-127''' was a [[Color|green]]-plated [[GNK-series power droid]] manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], it served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and was stationed in [[Echo Base]],<ref name="Datapad" /> the Alliance's headquarters on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]]. When the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Battle of Hoth|attacked]] Echo Base in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> it was the first battle EG-127 had participated in. The [[droid]] tried to power as much equipment as possible, as it believed the Alliance was depending on it. It saved an internal data record of the event, including an image of the base's interior.<ref name="Datapad" />

EG-127 survived the battle and ultimately joined the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Datapad" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> during the [[First Order]] [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the planet [[Batuu]], EG-127 was amongst the Resistance forces stationed on the world. It worked at [[Resistance Supply]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] where it stood next to a Resistance [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]<ref name="RS">{{GEAttr|Resistance Supply}}</ref> with fellow power droid [[EG-30]] storing power for the Resistance.<ref name="Datapad" />

==Behind the scenes==
EG-127 first appeared as a prop within the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="RS" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The prop is capable of emitting various sound effects.<ref name="RS" /> The droid simultaneously appeared in the [[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad]] application launched alongside the themed land, where it was initially identified as EG-16, but later renamed to EG-127,<ref name="Datapad" /> while ''Galaxy's Edge'' staff were originally told to refer to it as 16-SK.<ref name="GEStaff">{{GEStaff|Resistance Supply|store}}</ref> Only the Hollywood Studios Datapad entry clarifies that EG-127 is the green GNK-series droid.<ref name="Datapad" /> Hollywood Studios also has a third unnamed GNK-series power droid stands with EG-127 and EG-30.<ref name="RS" />
