'''Crashpoint Peak''' was the nickname of a hill in [[Farodin Woods]] on the [[planet]] [[Valo]], so nicknamed because local daredevils liked to try [[speeder]] tricks by the hill. [[Crashpoint Tower]], the planet's [[comm tower]], was located on the peak.<ref name="RtCT" />
