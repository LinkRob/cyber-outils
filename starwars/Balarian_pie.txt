'''Balarian pie'''<ref name="Dangerous Business Subtitles">{{Resistance|Dangerous Business}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref> was a type of [[pie]].<ref name="Dangerous Business" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|1|14}}</ref> when the [[human]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] was tasked with briefly running the [[Office of Acquisitions]]<ref name="Dangerous Business" /> aboard the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]'',<ref name="Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref> he confidently remarked that the job would be as "easy as Balarian pie."<ref name="Dangerous Business" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Valeria indeed came from the plot summary mentioned; I suspect I checked it shortly after the episode aired, recorded it in my notes and never saw the correction. May delete it or it may live on.|Jason Fry|<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-09.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-09}}</ref>}}
[[File:StarWarsResistance-SeasonOne.jpg|thumb|right|220px|The Valeria system was created by Jason Fry in reference to Balarian pie, which was first introduced in ''Star Wars Resistance''{{'s}} first season.]]
Balarian pie was first mentioned in "[[Dangerous Business]],"<ref name="Dangerous Business" /> the thirteenth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'',<ref name="Number">{{InsiderCite|190|Resistance Revisited|''Resistance'' Revisited}}</ref> which aired on [[January 20]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> The mention was first included in a preview for the episode released on the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel on [[January 16]], 2019.<ref name="Pay Attention">{{SWYouTube|R5NyB_bAZI8|Pay Attention - "Dangerous Business" Preview &#124; Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> The spelling of the pie's name was confirmed in the episode's subtitles.<ref name="Dangerous Business Subtitles" /> In "Every Time Kaz Falls and Yells in ''Star Wars Resistance''," an installment of the ''[[Star Wars By the Numbers]]'' web series on [[StarWarsKids.com]], Balarian pie is mistakenly identified as "Volarian pie."<ref name="StarWarsKids.com">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Time Kaz Falls and Yells in Star Wars Resistance|video=yxjypXuiJPw|nolive=1}}</ref>

Author [[Jason Fry]] created the [[Valeria system]] as the origin point of Balarian pie, based on a misspelling,<ref name="Fry" /> and introduced it in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by Jason Fry.<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> Following the appendix's release, Fry worked with contributors from the free-to-edit, unofficial online ''Star Wars'' encyclopedia known as [[Wookieepedia]] to confirm the intended origins of several star systems created for the appendix.<ref name="Fry" />

One such system was the Valeria system, which the author confirmed originated from<ref name="Fry" /> a [[January 21]], 2019 revision of the plot summary for "Dangerous Business" on Wookieepedia, which misspelled Balarian pie as "Valerian pie."<ref name="WookSummary">{{WookCite|url=Dangerous_Business?oldid=8056162|text=January 21, 2019 revision of the "Dangerous Business" article}}</ref> He further clarified that he had likely checked the plot summary shortly after it was posted and never updated his notes, indicating that the Valeria system may be removed from future versions of the appendix or may continue to remain present.<ref name="Fry" /> Due to the aforementioned error, this article refrains from assuming the Valeria system is the [[canon]]ical origin point of Balarian pie.
