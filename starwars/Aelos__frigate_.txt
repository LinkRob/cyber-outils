The '''''Aelos'''''<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> was a [[Nikardun blockade frigate|blockade frigate]] utilized by the [[Nikardun Destiny]]. The [[frigate]] had been stationed by the [[Nikardun]] at the [[Paccosh]] [[homeworld]] [[Rapacc]] in the [[Rapacc system]], but was captured by the Paccosh after the downfall of [[General]] [[Yiv]] of the Nikardun Destiny. The vessel was placed under the command of [[Uingali foar Marocsaa]] of the [[Marocsaa subclan]], the symbol of which&mdash;the [[Chimaera (symbol)|chimaera]]&mdash;was painted on the underside of the frigate.<ref name="Greater Good" />
