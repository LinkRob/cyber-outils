The '''Badawans''' were a group of [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|Dark Side]] acolytes that [[Count/Legends|Count]] [[Dooku/Legends|Dooku]] and [[Palpatine/Legends|Darth Sidious]] recruited for their "Dark Padawan Academy" on the volcanic planet [[Mustafar/Legends|Mustafar]]. The Badawans proved to be too unruly for Dooku, [[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]], [[Asajj Ventress/Legends|Asajj Ventress]], and [[Poggle the Lesser/Legends|Poggle the Lesser]] to handle; forcing them to seek the help of Darth Sidious. Darth Sidious traveled to Mustafar and began disciplining the Badawans. However, his lesson was interrupted by the arrival of [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] forces including the former [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone]] [[Jek-14/Legends|Jek-14]], who had allied himself with the Jedi. The Badawans and their Sith masters were defeated by Jek-14 and left on a magma rock.
