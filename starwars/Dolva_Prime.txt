'''Dolva Prime''' was a terrestrial astronomical object known for its [[grass]]lands and [[crystal]]s.<ref name="Prairie Sureshot" /> It was located in the [[Semagi sector]]'s [[Dolva Prime system]] within the [[Mid Rim Territories]] in grid square I-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SW Galaxy" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SWH Date">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Diago Velaar]]<ref name="BftA">''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]''</ref> warned that individuals must either be able to blend in or find a crystal and take cover in order to survive in the grasslands on Dolva Prime.<ref name="Prairie Sureshot" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Dolva_Prime.jpg|250px|thumb|right|Dolva Prime in ''Star Wars'' Legends]]
Dolva Prime was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] via the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', which was developed by [[NaturalMotion]]'s [[BossAlien]]<ref name="SWH">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> and initially published by [[Zynga]] on [[November 16]], [[2021]], as a [[Wikipedia:Soft launch|soft launch]]<ref name="Soft">{{Twitter|ZyngaStarWars|status/1460656552734605317|[[Zynga]] Star Wars|date=2021-11-16|quote=We are excited to announce Star Wars: Hunters is now entering soft launch and is available on Android for players in India, Philippines, Malaysia & Indonesia.|archivedate=20220625084625|archivefile=File:ZyngaStarWars-SoftLaunchDate-Twitter.png}}</ref> before the global release on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Global">{{SWYouTube|4dZcq6m3y40|Star Wars: Hunters Launch Date Reveal Trailer}}</ref> The game was later shut down on [[October 1]], [[2025]].<ref name="Offline">{{Hunters|url=news/sunset-on-vespaara|text=Sunset on Vespaara}}</ref> The mention of Dolva Prime was featured in the description of Velaar's [[Prairie Sureshot]] costume,<ref name="Prairie Sureshot" /> which was added on [[January 9]], 2024.<ref name="Update 0.19">{{Hunters|url=news/0-19-patch-notes|text=Game Update: January 9th, 2024}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Dolva Prime/Legends|Dolva Prime]] first appeared in ''[[Star Wars Adventures: Boba Fett and the Ship of Fear]]'', a graphic novel written by [[Jeremy Barlow]], illustrated by [[Daxiong]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="SWA Boba">''[[Star Wars Adventures: Boba Fett and the Ship of Fear]]''</ref> on [[April 27]], [[2011]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Books/16-619/Star-Wars-Adventures-Boba-Fett-and-the-Ship-of-Fear|text=Star Wars Adventures: Boba Fett and the Ship of Fear}}</ref>
