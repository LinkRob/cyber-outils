The '''Dulok Wafare''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Bounty Hunter (Imara Vex)|standard costume]]. The [[Clothing|outfit]] and [[armor]] resembling a member of the [[Dulok]] [[species]] from the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]], consisting primarily of varying shades of [[Color|green]], with red, yellow, orange, brown, tan, and gray accessories, painted designs, and other details, as well as a glowing orange visor on the [[helmet]], [[bone]]-like elements on the helmet and neck area, and a boxy green [[shoulder-mounted armament]] with a black payload housed inside.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dulok Wafare was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Imara Vex, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 0.18.0 within the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Dulok Rifle]].<ref name="Hunters" />
