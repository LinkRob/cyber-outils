'''Covenanon''' was a terrestrial astronomical object that was located near the [[Wild Space]] [[planet]] [[Katou]]. During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], it was the site of a [[Battle on Covenanon|heated battle]] that saw [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cardiff Baye]]'s unit make significant strides against the Separatist forces before he was reassigned to Katou.
