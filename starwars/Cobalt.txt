'''Cobalt''' was one of the substances that soil on the [[planet]] [[Lah'mu]] contained. The planet's [[Water|groundwater]] was unpleasant to drink due to the soil's [[boron]], [[chlorine]], cobalt, [[iron]], [[nitrogen]], and [[zinc]] content.<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> The substance shared its name with a color featured in the names of various [[Clothing|costumes]] and [[weapon]]s used by [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]]s of the [[Grand Arena]] on [[Vespaara]] including [[Sentinel]], [[Aran Tal]], [[Zaina]], [[Utooni]], and [[Sprocket]]<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date2">{{HuntersDate}}</ref>
