A [[Rhen-Orm]]<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> '''biocomputer''' was a device used by [[Aurra Sing]] to gain extrasensory data. It allowed her to track multiple threats simultaneously and to line up distant shots with her [[sniper rifle]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> At some point, [[Pirate Queen]] [[Gunda Mabin]] captured [[Hondo Ohnaka]] and took multiple items from him, including a Rhen-Orm biocomputer module. Inventory of the assets taken was recorded in the [[book]] that would become known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
