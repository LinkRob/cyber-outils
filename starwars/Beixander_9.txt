'''Beixander 9''' was a small lonely [[moon]]<ref name="Pirate's Price" /> located in the [[Beixander system]] within [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] in grid square H-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was covered in [[ocean]]s and hills, one of which was home to a [[Beixander 9 settlement|settlement]]. [[Bazine Netal]] was directed to Beixander 9 after acquiring the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' from [[Hondo Ohnaka]], but [[Mahjo Reeloo]] had incapacitated her real contact, taking the ''Falcon'' back to Ohnaka on [[Batuu]].<ref name="Pirate's Price" />
