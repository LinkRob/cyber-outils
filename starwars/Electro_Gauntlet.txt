An '''Electro Gauntlets'''<ref name="CBOE">{{Legion|set=Cad Bane Operative Expansion}}</ref> was a type of [[shock glove]] that produced a debilitating electric shock to whomever the wearer touched. [[Bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] owned a pair of Electro Gauntlets<ref name="A&A" /> which he used to immobilize [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] on [[Teth]] while attempting to evade capture.<ref name="Hunt">{{TCW|Hunt for Ziro}}</ref>
