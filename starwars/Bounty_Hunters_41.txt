'''''Bounty Hunters'' 41''' is the forty-first issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Davide Tinto]] and [[Arif Prianto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 13]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''A FATE WORSE THAN DEATH! &ndash; A ''DARK DROIDS'' TIE-IN! T'ONGA and ZUCKUSS have been BETRAYED&hellip;and CAPTURED! A mindwiped VALANCE has terrifying plans for his former crewmates. But one LAST HOPE for salvation may come from the unlikeliest of allies.''
