'''GB-BD'''<ref name="SoloOG" /> was an [[GB adminmech droid]]<ref name="Kids" /> that was forced to work in the criminal [[Pyke Syndicate]]'s [[spice]] [[Spice mines of Kessel|mines]] on the [[planet]] [[Kessel]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on [[Kessel]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to nine years after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the formation of the Empire to [[19 BBY]]; therefore, the events on Kessel in ''Solo'' must take place in [[10 BBY]].</ref> GB-BD was freed from [[slavery]] when its [[restraining bolt]] was removed during a [[Raid on Kessel|slave revolt]] in the [[Mining|mines]] started by the [[droid]] [[L3-37]].<ref name="Solo" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Personality and traits==
Like other [[Adminmech droid|adminmech]] [[droid]]s, GB-BD was programmed to be driven by efficiency and quotas and to never question orders.<ref name="SoloOG" />
