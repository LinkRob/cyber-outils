'''Acklays''' were [[Amphibian|amphibious]] [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[crustacean]]s with six deadly claws and razor-sharp teeth [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Vendaxa]]. They were used as execution beasts by the [[Geonosian]]s, and one attempted to [[Death|kill]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] in the [[Petranaki Arena]] prior to the outbreak of the [[Clone Wars]].

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Their species traits make them well suited to hunting on land, and their aquatic nature enables them to live beneath the waters of Vendaxa.|From the journal of [[Ardis San Tekka]]|<ref name="Bestiary" />}}
[[File:Acklay.png|thumb|left|250px|A profile view of an acklay]]
The acklay was a [[Sentience|non-sentient]] mix of [[crustacean]] and [[reptile]]<ref name="Absolutely" /> with [[Amphibian|amphibious]] traits that allowed it to inhabit the [[water]] and land of its [[homeworld]] of [[Vendaxa]]. The gigantic creature was protected by a hard, shell-like carapace<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> of [[Bone|bony]] nodules. They walked on six hardened, skin-covered claws, and had grappling [[Limb|hands]]. Its mouth was filled with razor-sharp [[Tooth|teeth]],<ref name="SWCE" /> and used an organ beneath its chin to sense the body electricity given off by its prey, which it would then spear with its pointed legs.<ref name="USW" /> The [[species]] had stretchy stomachs<ref name="SWCE" /> and three [[eye]]s.<ref name="AOTC" />

==Behavior==
{{Quote|Acklays are highly predatory creatures.|From the journal of Ardis San Tekka|<ref name="Bestiary" />}}
Acklays were [[Diet|carnivorous]],<ref name="Journey Through Space" /> predatory creatures, and though they typically lived underwater on [[Vendaxa]], they came on land to hunt the planet's plains for [[lemnai]].<ref name="SWCE" /> Surprisingly agile,<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|acklay|Acklay}}</ref> acklays scuttled around on their six pointed legs, using an organ beneath their chins to sense the body electricity of their prey. They speared their prey with their claws,<ref name="USW" /> biting through them with their sharp teeth<ref name="SWCE" /> and snapping limbs<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> or swallowing them into their stretchy stomachs.<ref name="SWCE" /> Vicious creatures possessing agile fury and bestial rage,<ref name="Databank" /> acklays were known for their bite,<ref name="PSatFB">''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]''</ref> and were strong enough to bite through a [[polearm]] and smash a [[stone]] pillar to the ground.<ref name="AOTC" />

Acklays were known to be used as killer beasts in execution arenas, such as the [[Petranaki Arena]] on [[Geonosis]]. There, [[Geonosian]] [[picador]]s kept the creatures under control with jabs from [[static pike]]s,<ref name="USW" /> and at other times the beasts were chained.<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>

==Acklays in the galaxy==
[[File:Acklay_Obi-Wan.jpg|thumb|right|250px|[[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] facing an acklay]]
Acklays' ferocity and insatiable appetites made them popular creatures for arena fights on the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]]<ref name="VE" /> and at least two were kept in the [[Petranaki Arena]] as of [[22 BBY]]; the mated pair shared a pen under the stadium,<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> and were utilized as killer beasts by the [[Geonosian]]s.<ref name="USW" /> [[Unidentified acklay (Petranaki Arena)|One]] of these acklays was unleashed during the attempted execution of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The acklay was steered towards Kenobi by its [[picador]],<ref name="AOTC" /> and was a formidable opponent for the unarmed Jedi,<ref name="VE" /> although the [[Jedi Knight]] outmaneuvered the beast, causing one of its powerful claw strikes to shear the chain binding him to a pillar. The acklay pursued Kenobi, rending holes in the arena floor with vicious stabs of its clawed legs. Kenobi geared himself with a picador's [[polearm]], though the acklay shrugged off his strikes and bit the spear in two.<ref name="Databank" /> Ultimately, Kenobi bested the beast after obtaining a [[lightsaber]] once [[Mace Windu]]'s [[Jedi assault team]] arrived to battle [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[battle droid]]s. Kenobi sheared through the acklay's legs, and finished the dangerous beast off with a downward stab with his blade.<ref name="AOTC" />

Acklays were present in the Petranaki arena during celebrations for the Eve of [[Meckgin]] and [[Poggle the Lesser]]'s homecoming, and were chained alongside [[reek]]s while Poggle announced the [[Stalgasin hive]]'s involvement in the [[Project Stardust|Death Star project]]. Even the execution beasts quieted as Poggle made his speech to the hushed arena.<ref name="Catalyst" />

Acklays were considered rare during the [[Imperial Era]]. One acklay was contained on a [[Class four container transport]], and it was meant to be transported to the [[Emperor's personal zoo]] before being released by the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]].<ref name="Endangered, Part 1">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=7|story=Endangered, Part 1}}</ref><ref name="Endangered, Part 2">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=8|story=Endangered, Part 2}}</ref> Acklays were also considered prized specimens in the menageries of the galaxy's elite <ref name="Bestiary" />

==Behind the scenes==
Obi-Wan Kenobi's fight with the acklay in [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'' Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] was inspired by the 1961 movie ''[[Wikipedia:Mysterious Island (1961 film)|Mysterious Island]]'', in which a shipwrecked sailor battles a giant crab.<ref name="Absolutely" />

In the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' episode "[[Crossing Paths]]," after the [[Battle of Hoth]], [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] distracted an acklay on [[Felucia]] in order to recover a part of their starship. Rowan used Force-sensitive flowers that attracted bugs that the acklay liked in order to distract it and recover the part.

''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'' states that acklay are amphibious crustaceans<ref name="USW" /> while ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'' states that they are [[reptile]]s.<ref name="VE" /> Furthermore, [[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]] mentions they are "amphibious reptilian crustaceans."<ref name="Bestiary" />
