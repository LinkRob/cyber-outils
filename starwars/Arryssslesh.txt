'''Arryssslesh''' was an [[Elia-An]] [[medicine artist]] who lived on [[Oanne]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Although a [[Unidentified translator droid|translator droid]] could not translate her name, it was similar to the [[Shyriiwook]] word for "chiming bells." When the [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Burryaga Agaburry]] and [[Bell Zettifar]] embarked on a [[Mission to Oanne|mission]] to save the Elia-An after their planet fell close to the [[Stormwall]] barrier of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders']] [[Occlusion Zone]] territory, Arryssslesh met with the two [[Jedi]]. Arryssslesh insisted her people could not leave Oanne, showing the Jedi a [[forest]] of [[nativity tree]]s and a [[Grandfather tree]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
