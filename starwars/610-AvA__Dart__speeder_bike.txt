The '''610-AvA "Dart" speeder bike''' was a type of [[speeder bike]]. It was produced by the [[Aratech Repulsor Company]] and sold for 2,400 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. It was popular because it had a collapsible frame. The "Dart" had a variant named the [[580-AvA "Dash" speeder bike]].<ref name="GoD" />
