A '''diffuse-Ion accelerator''' was a component that could be installed in a [[speeder bike]] to increase its top speed while boosting. Adding a diffuse-Ion accelerator to a [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder]] bike cost 1000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and required fifteen units of [[slivian iron]], and eight [[direct-energy circuit]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics}}
During [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] paid for the services of a [[speeder]] [[Technician|mechanic]] to upgrade her speeder bike with a diffuse-Ion accelerator and eventually with a [[ion hyper accelerator]], providing the necessary parts.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}
