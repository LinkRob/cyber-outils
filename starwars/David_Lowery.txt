'''David Lowery''' is an American filmmaker who directed the episode "[[Way, Way Out Past the Barrier]]" for ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]''.<ref name="SC directors" /> Lowery directed the films [[Wikipedia:Ain't Them Bodies Saints|''Ain't Them Bodies Saints'']], [[Wikipedia:Pete's Dragon (2016 film)|''Pete's Dragon'']], [[Wikipedia:A Ghost Story|''A Ghost Story'']], and [[Wikipedia:The Green Knight (film)|''The Green Knight'']].

According to Lowery, [[SM-33]] is meant to be a reference to [[Wikipedia:Mr. Smee|Smee]] from ''[[Wikipedia:Peter and Wendy|Peter Pan]]''. Lowery previously directed the [[2023]] Disney film ''[[Wikipedia:Peter Pan & Wendy|Peter Pan & Wendy]]'', which also featured [[Jude Law]] as [[Wikipedia:Captain Hook|Captain Hook]].<ref name="EmpireOnline" />
