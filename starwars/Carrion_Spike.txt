The '''''Carrion Spike''''' was a [[corvette]] and the personal [[starship]] of [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] while he was stationed at [[Sentinel Base]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was later captured in [[14 BBY]] by the disaffected former [[Republic Intelligence]] [[agent]] [[Berch Teller]] and his [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]]. Berch used the ''Carrion Spike'' in a [[Berch Teller campaign|campaign]] against [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[space station]]s, in which its abilities were unparalleled. Later the ship was abandoned by the rebels at [[Carida]] after they discovered their inside source within the Empire, [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]], was planning a betrayal.

After [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] exposed Rancit's treason, the ''Carrion Spike'' was recovered. By [[3 BBY]], the ship had been restored and was back in Tarkin's possession. At some point after the [[Battle of Jakku]], the ''Carrion Spike'' was discovered in the abandoned [[Rothana Imperial Shipyards]] by [[Terex]], who became the leader of the [[Ranc Gang]] and later an agent of the [[First Order]]'s [[First Order Security Bureau|Security Bureau]]. The starship served as Terex's personal transport during his campaign against the [[Resistance]] [[starfighter]] [[pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] and his [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]]. The ''Carrion Spike'' was destroyed by the First Order after Terex embarked on an unsanctioned campaign to destroy the Resistance.

==Description==

===Design===
An [[IPV-2C Stealth Corvette]], the ''Carrion Spike'' was originally designed by [[Sienar Design Systems]],<ref name="SWEOS" /> and was built specifically for and in collaboration with [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] by [[Sienar Fleet Systems]], [[Cygnus Spaceworks]], and [[Theed Engineering]]. The ship's weak point was the spine where the main fuselage connected with the aft flare. Only concentrated laser fire while lowering the [[deflector shield]]s would weaken the structure.<ref name="Tarkin" /> Its design was based on that of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Stealth ship (Galactic Republic)|stealth ship]] from the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>

The ''Carrion Spike'' was normally crewed by eighteen crew members, but could also be crewed by a [[captain]] and communications officer. If the need arose, six people were enough to crew the ship.<ref name="Tarkin" />

The ''Carrion Spike'' had a command cabin that could hold up to six people. It was designed by Tarkin to be more spacious than ships of a similar type. The cabin featured chairs for acceleration command, a pilot, a copilot, and a comm officer. The command cabin contained the ship's controls, which included the [[sensor]] suite display, the [[navigation computer]] display, rectenna controls, [[alluvial damper]] controls, controls for the reverse trigger acceleration compensator, and air lock overrides. The command cabin was connected to an afterdeck, and two hatches led to the austere main cabin, which contained a round conference table and six chairs. The main cabin had a starboard [[turbolift]] that connected it to the [[engine]] room on level one. However, when the turbolift was inoperable, the engine room could be accessed by a set of stairwells.<ref name="Tarkin" />

The ship's engine room contained a fuel bay that stored the ship's [[paralight tracker]], a [[HoloNet]] transceiver that parsed commands from the ship's navigation computer, effectively doubling as a [[homing beacon]]. The engine room was connected via a narrow cofferdam to cargo hold one, one of the larger cargo holds on the ship. The ''Carrion Spike'' additionally possessed at least one other cargo hold, barracks and personal lockers for the ship's crew, and an aft maintenance bay, as well as a vehicle pool for [[T-44 landspeeder|T-Forty-Four landspeeders]] that was equipped with a repair bay, a medical bay, a galley, and several toilets and showers.<ref name="Tarkin" />

===Engines and propulsion===
The ''Carrion Spike'' featured a Class One [[hyperdrive]] that utilized [[alluvial damper]]s. Its Class One hyperdrive made the [[Starship|ship]] the fastest one in the [[Imperial Navy]], while its state of the art full authority navicomputer allowed the corvette to travel from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] to [[Coruscant]] without reverting to [[realspace]] to retrieve routing data from relay stations or [[hyperspace beacon]]s. By contrast, less advanced ships had to exit [[hyperspace]] to check navigation data while traveling from one [[Star system|system]] to another.<ref name="Tarkin" />

In addition, the ''Carrion Spike'' was equipped with powerful state of the art [[Ion engine|ion turbine]] [[Sublight engine|sublight arrays]]. The ships sublight engines and maneuvering jets made the ship far more manuevarble than other ships of her size, enabling the ''Carrion Spike'' to outmaneuver Imperial warships and starfighters. In addition, the ship could utilize [[repulsorlift]]s to land.<ref name="Tarkin" />

===Armaments and stealth systems===
The ''Carrion Spike'' was armed with [[turbolaser]]s, [[ion cannon]]s, [[laser cannon]]s, [[pintle gun]]s, and [[proton torpedo]]es, which utilized [[computer]]-assisted fire control.<ref name="Tarkin" /> In [[34 ABY]], its laser cannon could be controlled by battle computers.<ref name="Poe Dameron 13">[[Poe Dameron 13|''Poe Dameron'' 13]]</ref> The ''Carrion Spike'' had the ability to fire while cloaked, making it look like green packets of energy was firing from deep space.<ref name="Tarkin" /> By [[14 BBY]] the devices where made of rare [[stygium crystal]]s from the volcanic<ref name="TIE manuel">''[[TIE Fighter Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> [[Wild Space]]<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> planet [[Aeten II]]<ref name="TIE manuel" /> The ships cloaking systems made it essentially invisible to ordinary scanners, and could contain its reflection and heat signatures. The [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] was able to track the ship at some point, but only because he could sense his [[meditation chamber]] which was on the ship.<ref name="Tarkin" />

===Other systems and features===
The ''Carrion Spike'' considered to be one of the most sophisticated starship in the Imperial Navy, and was equipped with a wide variety of state of the art systems, including was equipped with an armored hull, and a system of advanced deflector shields, including both [[ray shield]]s and [[particle shield]]s. It was additionally equipped with a state of the art countermeasures suite, internal, external, and emergency lighting, proximity alarms, internal security systems, a sensor suite, a hypercomm unit, a rectenna array, jamming equipment, blast-tinted windows, reverse triggering acceleration compensators, a hypercomm unit and Imperial HoloNet and encrypter. The ship weapons, sublight engines and hyperdrive where powered by a power plant. In order to land, the ''Carrion Spike'' possesd landing gear and a landing ramp.<ref name="Tarkin" /> The [[Resistance]] [[technician]] [[Oddy Muva]] was able to lock the crew out of the ship's control systems on one occasion and use it to destroy [[Terex]]'s "[[Ugly|Uglies]]."<ref name="Poe Dameron 13" /> The ship was also equipped with several [[escape pod]]s with airlock controls.<ref name="Tarkin" /> The ''Carrion Spike''{{'s}} [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] could seat three crew.<ref name="Poe Dameron 10" />

===Terex's modifications===
[[File:TerexPhasma-PoeDameron2.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Terex's slave harem aboard the ''Carrion Spike'']]
The [[First Order]] [[agent]] Terex installed a luxurious suite aboard the ''Carrion Spike''. He housed several [[alien]] [[Slavery|slaves]]. The suite house a [[hologram]] unit which Terex used to communicate with Captain [[Phasma]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 2" /> Terex also had a special chamber storing a collection of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Armor|helmets]] and [[blaster]]s as well as his [[Ranc Gang]] armor, which was a modified [[stormtrooper]] [[Stormtrooper armor|armor]] set. Terex also could use [[Authorization Code TK-603]] to disable the ship's communications, engines, and weapons systems.<ref name="Poe Dameron 8">[[Poe Dameron 8|''Poe Dameron'' 8]]</ref> The only way to regain control of these systems was to replace the central computer systems, a huge undertaking that could only be done in deep dock. The ship also had an internal communications system which allowed the bridge crew to contact Terex's personal chamber.<ref name="Poe Dameron 10" />

==History==
===Creation===
[[File:CarrionSpike-2017DarthVaderAnnual.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The ''Carrion Spike'' above Geonosis]]
Inspired by the Republic stealth ship from the Clone Wars,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> the ''Carrion Spike'' served as the personal [[transport]] of [[Grand Moff]] Wilhuff Tarkin during the [[Imperial Era]]. He named the vessel for the [[Carrion Spike (location)|Carrion Spike]] of his homeworld, [[Eriadu]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> Following an investigation on [[Geonosis]], [[Darth]] Vader met the-then Moff Tarkin on the ''Carrion Spike'' above the planet. There he reported his findings to Tarkin and questioned his new [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|project]], with Tarkin deducing that he wanted to be the sole technological marvel in the galaxy.<ref name="Vader Annual 2">[[Darth Vader Annual 2|''Darth Vader Annual'' 2]]</ref>

In 14 BBY,<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> Tarkin traveled aboard the ''Carrion Spike'' to Coruscant after being summoned for a meeting with [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. While traveling through hyperspace, Tarkin convened an impromptu inspection of the Imperial officers and enlisted ratings aboard the ship. After discovering that [[Ensign]] [[Baz]] had been secretly consuming [[spice]] in the vehicle pool, Tarkin had him relieved of duty and confined to the crew berth for the remainder of the voyage. He also instructed [[Commander]] [[Cassel (commander)|Cassel]] to perform a flash inspection of all barrackers and personal lockers.<ref name="Tarkin" />

While Tarkin met with the Emperor and Darth Vader at the [[Imperial Palace]], the ''Carrion Spike'' was berthed at an Imperial Palace landing field. After the Emperor assigned Moff Tarkin and Lord Vader to [[Mission to Murkhana|investigate]] a communications cache on [[Murkhana]], Tarkin dispatched most of the crew with the exception of the ship's captain and communications officer back to [[Sentinel Base]]; Tarkin's posting. Darth Vader brought a contingent of stormtroopers and one of his [[Darth Vader's meditation chamber|meditation spheres]] aboard the ship.<ref name="Tarkin" />

On Murkhana, the ''Carrion Spike'' landed at a former [[Corporate Alliance]] landing field. While Tarkin and Vader visiting the communications cache, the stormtroopers under [[Sergeant]] [[Crest]] and the crew guarded the ship. The former [[Republic Intelligence]] agent&ndash;turned&ndash;insurgent leader [[Berch Teller]] and his [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]] used their communications equipment to lure the stormtroopers away from the ship. The insurgents then boarded the ''Carrion Spike'' and killed the crew before fleeing into hyperspace. The insurgents were aided by the [[Sugi]] [[crime lord]] [[Faazah]], who supplied them with specialized [[fuel cell]]s.<ref name="Tarkin" />

====Berch Teller's campaign====
[[File:Tarkin-Vader.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Moff Tarkin and Lord Vader land on Murkhana]]
In hyperspace, Teller and his fellow insurgents decided to turn the ''Carrion Spike'' into a symbol of resistance against the Empire. Teller's crew included the [[Gotal]] pilot [[Salikk]], the [[Koorivar]] operations officer [[Cala]], the [[Mon Calamari]] engineer Dr. [[Artoz]], the [[journalist]] [[Anora Fair]], and the [[Zygerrian]] film director [[Hask Taff]]. The shipjackers were either survivors and witnesses of the [[Antar Atrocity]], a mass murder which had been instigated by Tarkin following the Clone Wars. They were secretly supported by Tarkin's rival [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]], who supplied them with weapons, communications equipment, and intelligence on Imperial targets. Rancit wanted to use the rebel insurgency to gain favor with the Emperor and elevate himself to Moff.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Tarkin and Vader pursued the ''Carrion Spike'' in Faazah's stolen ship ''[[Parsec Predator]]''. With the Empire on their tail, Teller brought the ''Spike'' to [[Galidraan III]], where the Empire had a [[Galidraan Station|space station]]. Using the ship's stealth systems, the insurgents [[Attack on Galidraan Station|caught]] the Imperial defenders by surprise and inflicted heavy casualties on the station's [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|V-wing]] and [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter|ARC-170 starfighters]]. The ''Spike'' also disabled the ''Parsec Predator'' but not before the latter inflicted damage on the ship's spine. Before fleeing into hyperspace, the insurgents also destroyed the station's hyperspace buoy and jettisoned Lord Vader's meditation chamber.<ref name="Tarkin" />

The insurgents then [[Attack on Lucazec|attacked]] [[Tagge Corporation|TaggeCo's]] mining facilities on [[Lucazec]]. Anora and Hask produced [[holovid]]s of the attack that were streamed on HoloNet. With the ''Carrion Spike'' running low on fuel, Teller's dissidents traveled to [[Phindar]] in the [[Mandalore sector]]; one of the few places that could support the corvette's unique fuel requirements. Rancit had warned the insurgents that the Empire was waiting for them at [[Gromas]] in the [[Perkell sector]]. Despite Teller impersonating an Imperial officer, the Phindar administrator managed to alert Moff Tarkin and Lord Vader to the ship's presence. Following a brief [[Battle in the Phindar system|dogfight]], the insurgents destroyed the [[Phindar tanker facility|orbital tanker]] by detonating a concealed explosive device before fleeing into hyperspace.<ref name="Tarkin" />

The ''Carrion Spike'' sustained significant damage to its hull and exterior lights as a result of the Phindar dogfight. Part of the ship's rectenna array had also been ripped way by a [[tractor beam]]. Most of the electronic appliances in the galley and medical bay were destroyed. Parts of the ship were inaccessible due to air lock damage and radiation leaks. While the ship's weapons and stealth systems, sensor suite, hyperdrive, and navicomputer still functioned, the shield generators were only functioning at 50 percent capacity. After examining the ship's engine room, Artoz discovered that the Imperials had been tracking the ''Carrion Spike'' using a [[paralight tracker]] on the ship's navicomputer, hidden inside an empty fuel cell.<ref name="Tarkin" />

To put the Empire off their scent, Teller transmitted hyperspace coordinates claiming that the ''Carrion Spike'' was traveling to the [[Obroa-skai system]]. Teller also got the [[Lantillies]] information broker and fellow insurgent [[Knotts]] to send the [[YT-1000 light freighter]] ''[[Reticent]]'' as a decoy. Later, the insurgents used the ''Carrion Spike'' to [[Nouane engagement|attack]] the Imperial facility in the [[Nouane system]]. However, Vice-Admiral Rancit had dispatched reinforcements to bolster Imperial facilities across the galaxy. The ''Spike'' was repulsed by [[laser]]s and barely managed to escape into hyperspace. As a result, the ship sustained considerable damage to its systems.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Realizing that Rancit had double-crossed them, Teller's insurgents abandoned the ''Carrion Spike'' and transferred to [[Berch Teller's warship|Teller's modified]] ''Providence''-class Dreadnought. They then set the ''Carrion Spike'' on autopilot to [[Carida]] under the pretext of attacking the [[Carida Academy|Imperial Academy]] there. To cover his collusion with the insurgents, Rancit attempted to destroy the ''Carrion Spike'' with the [[Secutor-class Star Destroyer|''Secutor''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Conquest (Secutor-class)|''Conquest''{{'s}}]] laser cannons. However, Lord Vader exposed Rancit's treason and stopped him from destroying the ''Carrion Spike''. After Vader had executed Rancit, Tarkin repossessed his damaged corvette. Tarkin then [[Battle near the Gulf of Tatooine|defeated]] Teller's insurgents as they were ambushing an Imperial convoy near the [[Gulf of Tatooine]]. Following the capture of the insurgents with the exception of Teller, Tarkin confronted them about the damage done to his corvette.<ref name="Tarkin" />

====Pick up on Chandar's Folly====
Following a successful [[Hunt on Chandar's Folly|hunt]] on [[Chandar's Folly]], Tarkin called for the ''Carrion Spike'' to pick him and Vader up in the [[Stormlands]]. The ''Carrion Spike'' swiftly arrived and as it lowered towards the two, lightning continuously struck it. Tarkin told Vader he would take him to a repair bay on the corvette to have him fixed and as the ''Carrion Spike'' was landing, Vader choked Tarkin to show him that he had not entirely won.<ref name="Dark Lord 18">[[Darth Vader (2017) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 18]]</ref>

====Other journeys====
By [[3 BBY]], the ship was back in regular use as a transport by Tarkin. He traveled in it on a surprise visit to [[Alderaan]], claiming to the [[House of Organa|Organas]] that it had suffered unexpected engine trouble.<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>

At one point, Tarkin brought the ''Carrion Spike'' back to Eriadu, where he waited at the ship while sending three protégées he had mentored on a test to determine which one of them was the worthiest. The victor of the contest was [[Ellian Zahra]], who successfully hunted an [[Eriadu veermok]] as well as her competition, having perceived that eliminating the other two competitors was the hidden part of Tarkin's test.<ref name="Star Wars 7" />

====Zahra's mission====
Tarkin later learned of a [[warlord]], [[Burnium Ro]], based on [[Ikkrukk]], who used rhetoric and imagery styled after the historical [[Nihil]] marauders. Due to the Nihil's reputation as agents of chaos, Tarkin saw them as a threat to Imperial order. However, as he had duties aboard the [[Death Star]], he dispatched [[Lieutenant Commander]] Zahra to [[Mission to Ikkrukk|destroy]] [[Burnium Ro's stronghold|Ro's stronghold]], lending her the ''Carrion Spike'' for the mission.<ref name="Star Wars 7" />

===Agent Terex===
====Serving the First Order====
At some point following the [[death]] of Tarkin during the [[Battle of Yavin]], the ''Carrion Spike'' ended up at the [[Rothana Imperial Shipyards]]. Sometime after the [[Battle of Jakku]], the ''Carrion Spike'' was discovered by the former stormtrooper Terex, a survivor of the battle. Terex, along with the former stormtrooper [[Corlac]], had joined a criminal organization called the Ranc Gang. Terex was determined to restore the Old Empire and recognized the ''Carrion Spike'' as Tarkin's former [[flagship]]. He set about repairing the ship's engines, [[life support]], cloaking device, surveillance, and weapons. Using the ship's surveillance systems, he was able to eavesdrop on a conversation between Corlac, [[Bett]], and [[Wenda]]. After learning that Corlac and the others planned to kill him, Terex used the ''Carrion Spike''{{'s}} laser [[cannon]]s to incinerate the command deck where they were meeting, killing them.<ref name="Poe Dameron 10" />

[[File:AgentTerexXiClassLightShuttle-SWZ.png|thumb|left|270px|A ''Xi''-class light shuttle departs the ''Carrion Spike''.]]
Terex then took control of the gang and became a notorious crime lord in that region of space. After his underlings ambushed a cargo vessel, they submitted a set of [[First Order stormtrooper armor|stormtrooper armor]] from the First Order.<ref name="Poe Dameron 10" /> Later in [[32 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that [[Poe Dameron]] began leading [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] on missions two [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. The [[mission to Ovanis]] was the first mission that [[Color|Black]] [[Squadron]] undertook as stated in [[Poe Dameron 1|''Poe Dameron'' 1]]. The mission to Megalox Beta takes place immediately after the [[mission to Ovanis]] so it must therefore also take place in [[32 ABY]].</ref> Terex had become an agent of the [[First Order Security Bureau]]. Terex fitted the ''Carrion Spike'' with a luxurious personal suite, that was staffed by several alien slaves, who served his every whim.<ref name="Poe Dameron 2" /> These slaves including the [[Abednedo]] [[Sowa Chuan]], the [[Marriage|wife]] of Muva had been kidnapped by Terex to use as leverage against people he considered useful.<ref name="Poe Dameron 12">[[Poe Dameron 12|''Poe Dameron'' 12]]</ref>

Terex was aboard his personal suite on the ''Carrion Spike'' when Phasma contacted him via hologram and assigned him with capturing [[Poe Dameron]], who had [[Operation: Sabre Strike|stolen information]] that [[Treason|treacherous]] [[New Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Erudo Ro-Kiintor]] had been trying to pass to the First Order.<ref name="Poe Dameron 2" /> Later, Terex offered to fly the crime [[lord]]s [[Parent|Papa]] [[Toren]], [[Kan Be]], and [[Isin]] away from [[Megalox]] [[prison]] on the ''Carrion Spike'' if they helped him to kill Dameron and his [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] while storming [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus]] the [[Hutt]]'s [[Fortress of Grakkus the Hutt|fortress]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 5">[[Poe Dameron 5|''Poe Dameron'' 5]]</ref>

After Dameron and [[Color|Black]] [[Squadron]] [[Mission to Megalox Beta|escaped]] offworld with Grakkus, Terex contacted the crew of the ''Carrion Spike'' to arrange a pick-up. Abandoning his former crime associates to [[Megalox Beta]]'s high [[gravity]], Terex used the ship to ram through the [[Megalox space station]], disabling the gravity shield around Megalox prison. As the prison guards departed in [[shuttle]]s to evacuate the [[prisoner]]s, Terex ordered the ''Carrion Spike'' to open fire on them. Black Squadron however turned around and inflicted heavy damage on the ''Carrion Spike''. As a result, Terex reluctantly acceded to crew's request that they cloak and retreat into hyperspace. Later, Terex disobeyed Phasma's orders to return to First Order Space and instead decided to continue his campaign against Dameron and the Resistance.<ref name="Poe Dameron 6">[[Poe Dameron 6|''Poe Dameron'' 6]]</ref>

====Return to the Ranc Gang====
[[File:Kaddak-PoeDameron11.png|thumb|right|200px|The ''Carrion Spike'' above Kaddak surrounded by Terex's "Uglies"]]
In [[34 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> following the events of Megalox, Terex's First Order crew completed repairs to the ship. However, Terex refused to inform his crew when they would be returning to First Order Space. After reflecting on his past experiences during the Battle of [[Jakku]], Terex received news from his crew that Phasma was seeking a report into the events at Megalox Beta. Terex shot the interface comm unit and activated Authorization Code TK-603, which locked the First Order crew out of the communications, engines, and weapon systems. He then took the ship to the Outer Rim world of [[Kaddak]], the headquarters of the Ranc Gang.<ref name="Poe Dameron 8" />

Terex landed his ship at [[Sliver|The Sliver]] on [[Level 72]], where his crew attempted to restart the communications, engines, and weapons systems. They were unable to do so since they needed to replace the central computer systems, an operation that could only be done in [[deepdock]]. After killing [[Wisper]] and retaking control of the Ranc Gang,<ref name="Poe Dameron 9">[[Poe Dameron 9|''Poe Dameron'' 9]]</ref> Terex returned with his [[gangster]]s who took over the ship. The First Order [[corporal]] objected that the ''Carrion Spike'' was First Order property. However, Terex responded that he would use the Ranc Gang to destroy the Resistance. Terex then took his ship for a spin and ordered the inhabitants of Kaddak to [[Mission to Kaddak|capture]] Dameron.<ref name="Poe Dameron 10" />

However, Dameron managed to escape with [[C-3PO]] and the [[BX-series droid commando]] [[N1-ZX]] aboard the [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] ''[[Black One]]''. Terex's crew managed to lock onto Poe's [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] using the ship's advanced sensors. Muva stowed aboard the ''Carrion Spike''<ref name="Poe Dameron 10" /> in an attempt to rescue his wife.<ref name="Poe Dameron 12" /> In space, Terex commanded a [[fleet]] of "Uglies," [[starfighter]]s cobbled up from various ships, from the bridge of the ''Carrion Spike''. He informed his fellow Ranc colleagues including [[Zumgi]] and [[Brrang]] that they would attack Dameron's starfighter once he had dropped back into realspace. Terex's plan was to follow Dameron back to the [[Resistance base]] and destroy the Resistance. Terex had misled Dameron into believing that N1-ZX contained intelligence on the location of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 11">[[Poe Dameron 11|''Poe Dameron'' 11]]</ref>

====Last stand against the Resistance====
[[File:BattleOverDesertPlanet-PoeDameron13.jpg|thumb|left|175px|The ''Carrion Spike'' and the Ranc Gang's fleet were destroyed by the First Order]]
Later, Phasma contacted Terex by hologram and ordered him to abandon his mission on the grounds that Snoke had forbidden open hostility by the [[First Order military]] [[Cold War|against]] forces aligned with the New Republic. Terex ignored her orders and switched off the transmission. He then had Chuan serve him a drink. Meanwhile, Muva ambushed Zumgi in the ''Carrion Spike''{{'s}} corridors and ordered the gangster to take him to his wife. Meanwhile, Dameron realized Terex's plan to lure him into a trap and exited hyperspace above an [[Unidentified desert planet|uncharted desert world]]. Terex realize that Dameron had outsmarted him and commanded his "Uglies" to kill the [[Resistance pilot]]. Taking damage, Poe's X-wing crashed-landed on the desert world.<ref name="Poe Dameron 11" />

While Terex led a landing party to hunt down Dameron and the [[droid]]s, he left his [[lieutenant]] Brrang in charge of the ''Carrion Spike'' and his fleet of "Uglies." Muva managed to reunite with Chuan. After freeing the other slaves, Muva wanted to confront Terex only to learn from Zumgi that the crime lord had descended to the surface. Shortly later, Black Squadron arrived in their starfighters and sighted the ''Carrion Spike'' and its "Uglies." [[Gambling]] they had the upper hand, Brrang ordered the other Ranc Gang's "Uglies" to [[Battle over a desert planet|attack]] Black Squadron.<ref name="Poe Dameron 12" />

During the subsequent dogfight, Muva and the slaves manages to escape in escape pods. Before leaving, Muva locked the crew out of the control systems and ordered the ship's battle computers to target any ship without a Resistance transponder. Black Squadron protected the escape pods but lost one of their [[pilot]]s [[L'ulo L'ampar]] to enemy fire. Eventually, Terex's crew managed to regain control of the ''Carrion Spike'' which launched a frontal attack on Black Squadron. However, a First Order [[Maxima-A-class heavy cruiser|Maxima-A class heavy cruiser]] exited hyperspace with a complement of [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s and destroyed the ''Carrion Spike'', killing all hands aboard. The First Order had dispatched forces to apprehend Terex for defying the First Order and embarking on an errant mission. Dameron handed Terex to Commander [[Malarus]] and taunted him about the destruction of his flagship.<ref name="Poe Dameron 13" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Carrion Spike'' first appeared in the [[2014]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 96|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 96]] claimed that the ''Carrion Spike'' was the original stealth ship from "[[Cat and Mouse]]", and that it was remodeled and given to Tarkin in [[17 BBY]].<ref name="17 BBY">{{BuildFalconCite|96|Starship Fact File}} states that the ''Carrion Spike'' was remodeled and given to [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] two [[Standard year|years]] after the end of the [[Clone Wars]], which ended in [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore, the ''Carrion Spike'' must have been remodeled and given to Tarkin in [[17 BBY]].</ref> However, the vessel was featured under Tarkin's ownership in [[Darth Vader Annual 2|''Darth Vader Annual'' 2]],<ref name="Vader Annual 2" /> which occurs in [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> and the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' clearly established the two to be separate ships.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />
