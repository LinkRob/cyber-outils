'''Bak Rychuk''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] that served in the [[New Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. [[Lieutenant]] Rychuk flew as part of [[Shepherd Squadron]] under the [[callsign]] [[Shepherd Two]] with his [[astromech droid]], [[Zeetoo-Zeetoo]]. Rychuk, flying a [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]], participated in the [[Battle of Fondor Shipyards|assault]] on the [[Fondor Shipyards]] and the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="The Ghost Ship" />
