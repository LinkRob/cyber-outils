'''Bog Broth''' was a type [[soup]] created by the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] after trying fungus and herbs that were growing in a swamp on the [[planet]] [[Dagobah]]. After making a culinary tour of the [[galaxy]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs included a recipe for the dish in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> which was published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> In the book, he states it was a less pungent option over his [[Rootleaf stew]] recipe.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bog Broth were created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the dish makes use of [[Wikipedia:Vegetable oil|vegetable oil]], chopped [[Wikipedia:Onion|yellow onion]], chopped [[Wikipedia:Carrot|carrots]], [[Wikipedia:Garlic|Garlic cloves]], [[Wikipedia:Ginger|ginger]], [[Wikipedia:Broth|beef broth]], [[Wikipedia:Shiitake|shiitake mushroom]], [[Wikipedia:Sesame oil|sesame oil]], [[Wikipedia:Agaricus bisporus|white mushrooms]], [[Wikipedia:Bamboo shoot|Bamboo shoots]] and diced [[Wikipedia:Scallion|scallions]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
