'''Blue Meekas''', sometimes referred to as '''meekas''', for short, were playful [[Species|creatures]] found within the [[Blue Forest]] on the [[planet]] [[Tenoo]]. Meekas were drawn to shiny objects such as [[holocamera]]s and would try to take them. Despite their propensity for [[thief|thievery]], their playful nature meant they would sometimes play catch with stolen items and their [[sentience|sentient]] owners.<ref name="The Night Lights of Tenoo" />

Between around [[232 BBY|232]] and [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|302B}}</ref> when [[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]], and [[Nubs]] joined [[Padawan]] [[Celesta Kami]] on a camping journey into the Blue Forest to observe the annual electromagnetic phenomenon, the [[Night Lights of Tenoo]], a group of meekas were drawn to the Jedi's shiny objects, including Kami's holocamera. The Padawan's pursuit of refuge from the meekas, ultimately chasing down one that stole her holocam, led to her and the [[youngling]]s falling into a ravine as the Night Lights began. During the phenomenon, Kami took advantage of the meekas' enjoyment of playing catch. She tossed one a [[grappling hook]] up to where it was perched at the top of the ravine. When the creature threw it back, it looped the cable around a [[tree]] branch, allowing for the Jedi to use the hook and cable as a pulley system to raise each other out of the ravine and enjoy the dazzling lights.<ref name="The Night Lights of Tenoo" />
