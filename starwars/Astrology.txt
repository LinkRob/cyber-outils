'''Astrology''' was the belief that the movements of [[planet]]s and the positions of [[star]]s could influence someone's personality and future. Astrological systems were developed on many worlds, including [[Coruscant]] and [[Shili]]. By the [[Imperial Era]], most people dismissed it as superstition. The [[Gatalenta]]n [[Apprentice Legislator]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] believed in astrology, and liked to study and compare systems from various planets.<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan" />
