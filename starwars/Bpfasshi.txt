The '''Bpfasshi''' were a [[humanoid]] [[species]] associated with the [[religion]] the [[Truthsayers of Bpfassh]].<ref name="THR 1" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic (2022) 1|''The High Republic'' (2022) 1]] to [[382 BBY]].</ref> [[Unidentified Truthsayer of Bpfassh|one member]] of the species who was a Truthsayer confronted [[Ran Yaltan]] for pretending to be a Truthsayer in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]].<ref name="THR 1" /> They originated from the [[Bpfassh system]].<ref name="Planetnamia" />
