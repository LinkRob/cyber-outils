'''Charde''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Politics/Legends|politician]] who lived during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]. Sometime following the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]] in [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]], Charde hired the male [[Nikto/Legends|Nikto]] [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] named [[Soris]] with [[Assassin/Legends|assassinating]] his political rival, a male [[Otolla/Legends|Otolla]] [[Gungan/Legends|Gungan]] named [[Nalskel]], of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial administration]]. However, Nalskel hired another bounty hunter, who succeeded in both [[Death/Legends|killing]] Soris and Charde.<ref name="SWG" />
