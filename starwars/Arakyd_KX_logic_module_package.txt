The '''Arakyd KX logic module package''' was a device that could be implemented into the head of a [[KX-series security droid]]. The package was requested to be installed, amidst a series of recommended repairs, into the reprogrammed [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[droid]] [[K-2SO]] after members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] began complaining about the droid. [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] however, objected to and vetoed its implementation.<ref name="TRF" />
