'''Bloch''' was a hermit who lived on the forest moon of Endor. In his old age, he encountered a [[mutant]] [[Wolf/Legends|Wolf]] named [[Wilk (wolf)|Wilk]]. Wilk had somehow acquired [[Sentience/Legends|sentience]], and Bloch taught the wolf how to speak. With Bloch as his teacher, Wilk learned about the world around him. Wilk eventually left Endor to explore the wider [[The galaxy/Legends|galaxy]].
