'''Amleto''' was an [[Elomin]] [[spy]] who lived during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. She had [[Color|red]] skin and orange [[eye]]s. The Elomin wore a light-colored [[Clothing|tunic]], a [[belt]] over brown pants, and a brown neck [[scarf]]. Amleto also wore a gray and green mask over the lower half of her face, and a brown band on her right [[Limb|arm]].<ref name="Bucket's List" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The script originally featured an additional spy. Her name: Amleto. An Elomin. But she was ultimately cut from the arc.|''[[Bucket's List]]''|audio=Amleto-BucketsList.flac|<ref name="YouTube" />}}
Amleto was created for "[[Breakout]],"<ref name="Bucket's List" /> the thirteenth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season Two|second season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[December 29]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> She was first indirectly mentioned in the installment's accompanying episode guide within its concept art gallery.<ref name="ConceptArt">{{SW|url=breakout-concept-art-gallery|text="Breakout" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref>

The character was fully designed but eventually cut from the final version of the episode. Instead of Amleto meeting Resistance spies [[Norath Kev]] and [[Kazuda Xiono]] on<ref name="Bucket's List" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Varkana">{{Databank|varkana|Varkana}}</ref> [[Varkana]], the pair instead encountered the market [[Merchant|vendor]] [[Darrb]] while trying to evade First Order capture.<ref name="Breakout">{{Resistance|Breakout}}</ref> At one point, "Breakout"'s writer&mdash;[[Steven Melching]]&mdash;had also written the episode to feature [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] as part of the group with Kev, Xiono,<ref name="MelchingWook">{{WookYouTube|_aRy5PWaiPY|Wookieepedia Interviews - Steven Melching (Part 1)}}</ref> and Amleto<ref name="Bucket's List" /> on Varkana. Dameron was eventually drafted out of the script due to scheduling conflicts with Dameron's actor, [[Oscar Isaac]], and because the episode seemed "too crowded,"<ref name="MelchingWook" /> while Amleto reached design stage before being cut.<ref name="Bucket's List" />

Amleto was first fully mentioned in the article "Bucket's List Extra: 4 Fun Facts from 'Breakout' &ndash; ''Star Wars Resistance''," which was posted on [[StarWars.com]] on [[December 30]], 2019.<ref name="Bucket's List" /> Concept artwork of the character was shown in the article's accompanying video on the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel, which was released the same day.<ref name="YouTube" /> [[Lucasfilm Animation]] artist [[Aaron Pham]] created a concept [[:File:AmletoDarrbhouse-BreakoutConceptGallery.jpg|illustration]] titled "verkDomicileAmleto" for "Breakout" on [[May 7]], [[2018]].<ref name="ConceptArt" />
