'''Ami-Kat-Ayama''' was a [[Cerean]] [[Gender|female]] [[Jedi Initiate]] active during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Brotherhood" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']] is set between the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] and the [[Battle of Christophsis]], both of which are dated to [[22 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> a few [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[First Battle of Geonosis|beginning]] of the conflict between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Ayama and her [[Mill Alibeth's Initiate Clan|Initiate Clan]] were scheduled to undergo [[Gathering|the Gathering]] on the sacred [[planet]] [[Ilum]], where they would harvest [[kyber crystal]]s and build their own [[lightsaber]]s. Ayama was excited, and said she hoped to construct a [[Double-bladed lightsaber|saberstaff]].<ref name="Brotherhood" />
