'''Dade Hetkins''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] [[New Republic soldier|soldier]] of the [[New Republic]].

==Biography==
===Battle of Endor===
Dade Hetkins and his team were assigned to cleanup duty in the [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[Battle of Endor|destruction]] of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second death star]]. While on patrol, a single [[scout trooper]] threw a [[thermal detonator]] near Hetkins, which took off the lower half of his [[Limb|right leg]]. His team was able to triage his leg and save Hetkins' life.<ref name="Life Debt" />

===Dawn of the New Republic===
After his injury, Hetkins found himself in the veteran hospital in [[Darropolis]] on the [[planet]] [[Hosnian Prime]] under the care of [[Doctor]] [[Arsad]]. The doctor fit him with a [[Cybernetics|prosthetic]] lower leg to replace the one lost on [[Endor]]. Although the prosthetic foot provided sensations, he was aware of the differences. Before allowing him to leave, the doctor asked Hetkins a series of questions to verify his mental health. He gave lies to most of the questions due to his eagerness to leave, but the doctor's [[FX-7 medical assistant droid|FX-7]] droid revealed Hetkins' deception. The doctor prescribed group therapy and a new droid, [[QT-9]], as a companion for Hetkins.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==Personality and traits==
Hetkins was not happy losing his leg on Endor and was also not thrilled with his new metal prosthetic, although he would learn to live with it. Even though he had a poor outlook on life, he genuinely did not have [[Death|suicidal]] thoughts.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dade Hetkins was a minor point-of-view character who first appeared in [[Chuck Wendig]]'s [[2016]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''.
