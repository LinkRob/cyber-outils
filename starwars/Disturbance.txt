"'''Disturbance'''" is a short story that was published in the [[2020]] anthology book ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''. It was written by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]] and focuses on [[Darth Sidious]]. The [[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back (audiobook)|audiobook]] version of the story was read by [[Sam Witwer]].<ref name="Preview">{{WebCite|url=https://soundcloud.com/penguin-audio/audiobook-excerpt-disturbance-by-mike-chen-read-by-sam-witwer|text=Audiobook Excerpt: "Disturbance" by Mike Chen, read by Sam Witwer|work=Sound Cloud|archivedate=20201107013426}}</ref>
