The '''''6-CD Audio Box: Episodes I-VI''''' ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]]: '''''6-CD Hörspielbox: Episoden I-VI''''') was a CD box set first released in 2006 that collected [[WortArt]]'s six single-disc audio adaptations of the original and prequel trilogies. A second edition in redesigned packaging was issued on [[December 7]], [[2016]].
