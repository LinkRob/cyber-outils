'''Ashes of Malachor''' was a consumable substance supposedly made by [[smuggler]]s who found the lost [[planet]] of [[Malachor]]. The Ashes of Malachor were made from the pulverized bones of the [[Jedi]] and [[Sith]] who [[Great Scourge of Malachor|fought]] there many [[Standard year|years]] before. The ashes had trapped dark essence within&mdash;which, if consumed, increased the consumer's [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] prowess and power at the cost of their own morality.<ref name="UP" />
