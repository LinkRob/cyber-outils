'''''Destination Alderaan''''' was a travel advertisement for the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]], showing a [[human]] dressed in [[Color|white]] and standing in front of the [[city]] of [[Aldera]], accompanied by the words "DESTINATION ALDERAAN" written in white, [[Aurebesh]] characters. It was one of several ads displayed on an electronic billboard on the [[hover taxi]] that [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Plo Koon]] took while traveling through the [[Coruscant Underworld]].<ref name="Lethal" />
