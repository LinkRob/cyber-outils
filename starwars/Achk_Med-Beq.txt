'''Achk Med-Beq'''<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> was a [[human]], present in the [[Outlander Club]] on [[Coruscant]] and conversing with [[Ayy Vida]] and [[Lunae Minx]], when [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] showed up in [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|pursuit]] of the [[changeling]] [[assassin]] [[Zam Wesell]].<ref name="AotC" /> Med-Beq was a known associate of [[Dannl Faytonni]], who also attended the club that night. By the time Kenobi and Skywalker were assisting Wesell at the back of the establishment, Med-Beq and Faytonni were patrolling the second floor of the Outlander conjuring.<ref name="Complete Locations" />

==Behind the scenes==
Achk was portrayed by [[Ahmed Best]], who also played [[Jar Jar Binks]] throughout the [[prequel trilogy]].<ref name="AotC" /> He shares the cameo with [[George Lucas]]'s daughter [[Katie Lucas]], who plays [[Lunae Minx]]. In an interview on ''[[The Star Wars Show]]'', Best stated that Beq was related to the [[Jedi Master]] [[Kelleran Beq]], who Best portrayed in ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Temple Challenge]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', though no [[canon]]ical source has yet confirmed the relation.<ref name="Kelleran Beq">{{StarWarsShow|fKdMtEiHGnM|Ahmed Best and Mary Holland Talk Jedi Temple Challenge, And We Celebrate The Empire Strikes Back!|sw_url=video/ahmed-best-and-mary-holland-talk-jedi-temple-challenge-and-we-celebrate-the-empire-strikes-back}}</ref>
