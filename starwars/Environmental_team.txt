An '''environmental team''',<ref name="Yoda 3" /> also known as a '''science team''',<ref name="SoS" /> was a [[Galactic Republic]] team sent out to investigate whenever a [[planet]]'s environment was in danger or seemed to be unbalanced. During the [[High Republic Era]],<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Standard decade|decades]] after [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The flashbacks of [[Yoda 3|''Yoda'' 3]] take place [[Standard decade|decades]] after the flashbacks of [[Yoda 1|''Yoda'' 1]] and [[Yoda 2|''Yoda'' 2]], which in turn take place during [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]] of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' as established by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. That book dates the events of Phase II to [[382 BBY]].</ref> [[Grand Master]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Yoda]] and [[Jedi Master]] [[Pra-Tre Veter]] talked about requesting the [[Unidentified Supreme Chancellor (Pra-Tre Veter)|Supreme Chancellor]] to send an environmental team to<ref name="Yoda 3" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Turrak]].<ref name="Yoda 3" />

After the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|fell]] into the planet [[Eiram]]'s [[ocean]]<ref name="SoS" /> in [[230 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> environmental teams regularly tested the planet's [[water]]s for pollution. In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="SoS Date">{{THRContent228BBY|SOS}}</ref> during a [[mission to investigate the blight]], such a science team was sent out alongside the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] to test Eiram's water and how the sunken [[space station]] had affected the local environment.<ref name="SoS" />

==Description==
Environmental teams were sent out by the [[Galactic Republic]] to protect a [[planet]]'s environment. The [[chemist]] [[Perrik Droft]] was a member of a science team that investigated the wreckage of [[Starlight Beacon]] on [[Eiram]]<ref name="SoS" />

==History==
===Turrak and Eiram===
[[File:YodaPraTreVeter-Yoda3.png|thumb|right|220px|Pra-Tre Veter and Yoda discussing the need to send an environmental team to Turrak]]
[[Standard decade|Decades]] after [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[High Republic Era]], [[Grand Master]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Yoda]] helped to resolve a [[Raids on the Scalvi|conflict]] between the [[Scalvi]] and the [[Crulkon]]<ref name="Yoda 3" /> on the [[Galactic Frontier]]<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-comics-december-2022|text=Yoda Goes to a Strange World and More from Marvel's December 2022 ''Star Wars'' Comics &ndash; Exclusive}}</ref> planet [[Turrak]]. After the Scalvi [[Bree Menaren]] found out that the Crulkon were starving due to the dissapearance of the [[fish]] that they survived on, Menaren told his [[Scalvi village|people]] to end the war and help their neighbors by giving them [[Foodstuff|food]]. When Yoda told his fellow [[Jedi High Council|Council]] member [[Pra-Tre Veter]] about the lifeless [[ocean]] on Turrak, [[Jedi Master|Master]] Veter offered to speak with the Republic [[Unidentified Supreme Chancellor (Pra-Tre Veter)|Supreme Chancellor]] about sending an environmental team to the planet.<ref name="Yoda 3" />

In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] was attacked and destroyed by [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s while orbiting the planet [[Eiram]]. The wreckage of the station crashed into the planet's [[ocean]] near the city [[Barraza City|Barraza]], disrupting the local environment around the city by polluting the water. In the tragedy's aftermath, the Galactic Republic regularly sent out science teams to protect the environment by testing the [[water]] to confirm that no pollutions had leaked from Starlight Beacon's wreckage.<ref name="SoS" />

===Trouble on Eiram===
{{Quote|The fish stocks are depleted and the fishing nets are coming up empty. There are people saying that something toxic is leaking out of the wreckage, killing everything it touches.''"<br />"''But the water is beeing screened regularly. Even now, the science team from our ship is about to carry out further tests to be certain.|Fido Blick and Eve Byre|<ref name="SoS" />}}
[[File:Innovator.png|thumb|left|350px|The Innovator (pictured) transported a science team to Eiram]]
During a [[mission to investigate the blight]]<ref name="SoS" /> in [[228 BBY]],<ref name="SoS Date" /> the research [[starship|vessel]] ''[[Innovator]]'', carrying [[Jedi]] and [[Republic Defense Coalition]] personnel, received a [[Distress signal|distress call]] from Eiram's [[Jedi outpost (Eiram)|Jedi outpost]]. When the group arrived on Eiram, an environmental team consisting of RDC volunteers was sent to investigate the planet Eiram. With the help of the ''Innovator''{{'s}} scientific equipment and knowledge, the science team was able to examine in more detail how the wreckage of Starlight Beacon had affected the local environment. While [[Jedi Master]] [[Eve Byre]] was investigating the distress call, she met the local [[Duros]] [[Fido Blick]], who told her that in the recent [[Standard week|weeks]], people of [[Barraza City]] have gone missing and that the [[Eirami]] [[Anti-Jedi sentiment|blame the Jedi]] for that. Byre reassured him that the water was screened regularly and that the science team from the ''Innovator'' is carrying out further tests.<ref name="SoS" />

Later, an attack happened in which two members of the science team on Eiram went missing, and [[chemist]] [[Perrik Droft]], the only person who witnessed the attack, was unconscious. [[Petty Officer]] [[Orlis]] of the Republic Defense Coalition tried to contact Byre to inform her about the attack, but Byre herself was missing. The [[Republic Defense Coalition officer|RDC officer]] eventually reached [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Kildo]], whom he informed about the missing RDC volunteers. The [[Youngling]] suggested that Orlis should continue to try to find out who attacked the science team.<ref name="SoS" />

===Return of the Drengir===
[[File:Jawless.png|thumb|right|260px|The Drengir (pictured) were responsible for the attacks]]
After researching, the Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Cam Lindon]] and the Younglings Kildo and [[Tep Tep]] found out the the [[Drengir]] were behind the attacks and the missing people. Some of the victims were freed by the Jedi, but others, including the two members of the environmental team and Jedi Master Byre, were still missing. Orlis believed it must have been the Drengir who attacked the science team from the ''Innovator'', but until the still unconscious Perrik Droft was well enough to be woken up from his coma, he could not be certain.<ref name="SoS" /> 

The Jedi and the Eirami eventually fought the Drengir, and defeated them, driving them back into the the ocean and freeing the rest of the missing people. Three days later, the science team reported that the fish stock was already increasing. Nontheless, Petty Officer Orlis and the science team were ordered to stay on Eiram to keep continuously running tests.<ref name="SoS" />

===The Blight===
{{Quote|At this point, your scientific team recommends the evacuation of infected planets.|Keven Tarr, to Lina Soh|<ref name="Trials of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref>}}
In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Trials Date">{{IntotheLightDate}}</ref> the young inventor [[Avon Sunvale]] was chosen by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Reath Silas]] to be the [[scientist]] of the [[Kashyyyk team]], a team sent to the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]] by the [[Jedi Order]] to [[Mission to Kashyyyk (blight)|investigate]] its affliction with the [[Blight]]. Sunvale was surprised by Silas' decision to to send a science team and she told Silas that she was not confortable beeing the sole scientist on the mission. But the Jedi Knight assured her that she was the best candidate and that other scientists from top research institutes were on call.<ref name="Into the Light">''[[The High Republic: Into the Light]]''</ref>

During a meeting in the [[Galactic Senate Chamber|Grand Convocation Chamber]], [[Master Analyst]] [[Keven Tarr]] informed [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] that her scientific team recommended the evacuatiuon of [[planet]]s infected with the Blight.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />
