'''Bakiska's''' was a data repeater service that operated on the planet [[Lothal]] during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]]. Bakiska's was known to operate a data warehouse in [[Capital City]]'s [[Lower Gallo]] area. The [[Vocational School for Institutional Security]] student and rebel sympathizer [[Merei Spanjaf]] maintained an account with Bakiska's. After the Imperial authorities managed to track down one of the [[snooper]] programs she had used during a data breach at the Imperial [[Transportation Ministry]], Merei and her classmate [[Jix Hekyl]] toyed with the idea of deleting her account at Bakiska's.<ref name="IJ">''[[Servants of the Empire: Imperial Justice]]''</ref>

After Imperial investigators (which included Merei's mother [[Jessa Spanjaf|Jessa]]) closed in on three repeater services, Merei and Jix attempted to infiltrate Bakiska's warehouse and sabotage the machine storing Merei's account with a [[pulse-mag]]. However, they were stopped by the local security guards and evicted from the warehouse. Merei and Jix's actions angered their boss, the [[Gray Syndicate]] [[crime lord]] [[Yahenna Laxo]], who retaliated by ordering Merei to accompany a [[bounty hunter]] to arrest the elderly poet [[Holshef]], who was paying the Syndicate to hide him from the authorities. Before Laxo could carry out his order, he and his associates with the exception of Merei and Jix were [[Assault on the Gray Syndicate|killed]] by Imperial forces.<ref name="IJ" />
