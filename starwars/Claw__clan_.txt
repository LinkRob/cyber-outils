The '''Claw''' were a clan that were rivals to the [[Scyre]] clan on the [[planet]] [[Parnassos]]. When [[Brendol Hux]] of the [[First Order]] crash-landed on the planet, the Claw helped the crew of Hux's [[Escape pod|emergency pod]] onto a plateau.<ref name="Phasma" />

==Description==
The Claw was a clan on the [[planet]] [[Parnassos]] rivaled against the [[Scyre]] clan. The Claw had about twice as many members as the Scyre. Like the Scyre, they spoke a [[Scyre dialect|dialect]] of [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]] native to Parnassos.<ref name="Phasma" />

==History==
At the time of [[Phasma]]'s ascent to lead the Scyre, the Claw clan was led by the [[Dug]] [[Balder]].<ref name="Phasma" /> In [[24 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> they attempted to kidnap a child named [[UV-8855|Frey]] from a smaller rival clan, the Scyre. They failed and lost a dozen warriors to [[Phasma]] and her warriors. A short while later a [[First Order]] [[Starship|ship]] carrying [[Brendol Hux]] was shot down by [[Parnassos' automated defense system]] and Brendol's escape pod landed in the [[Claw territory]]. Balder and his men rescued Brendol and his three [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]]. But as he attempted to negotiate with Brendol, Phasma and her warriors snuck into the village and intervened. Phasma then killed Balder and a dozen of his warriors before escaping with Brendol and his men and only took two loses.<ref name="Phasma" />

Phasma unfortunately couldn't convince her tribe to join her and Brendol to find the ship so she and four of her warriors took Brendol and his men and abandoned the Scyre. The Scyre leader [[Keldo]] (Phasma's brother) then united the Scyre and the Claw to pursue Phasma and the group. They caught up with them at a fence where Phasma and the others had stopped to talk to the [[Gand]] [[Churkk]]. Phasma's group had lost one warrior [[Carr]] and a stormtrooper and when the two clans ambushed them they killed another warrior [[Gosta]]. Phasma and the rest managed to get away and the two clans had to cut through the fence to continue. They engaged them again at the site of Brendols ship where they battled until it was just Keldo, Frey against Phasma, [[Siv]] and Brendol. Phasma killed Keldo and took Frey with her when Brendol's superiors arrived. They left Siv behind with the bodies of the Claw and Scyre.<ref name="Phasma" />
