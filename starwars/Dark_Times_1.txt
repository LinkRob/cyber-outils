'''''Dark Times'' 1''' is the first issue of the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Times]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series. It was released on [[November 8]], [[2006]]. It was written by [[Randy Stradley]] under the name Mick Harrison from a plot he also created, under the name Welles Hartley, illustrated by [[Douglas Wheatley]], and colored by [[Ronda Pattison]].

==Publisher's summary==
''The dark times are here. Even as the worlds of the former Republic are drawn into the iron grip of Palpatine's Empire, the galaxy seems adrift in a sea of chaos, lawlessness, and despair.''

''On Coruscant, Darth Vader waits for his Master to reveal his vision for the future. But is there a plan beyond obtaining power? On New Plympto, Jedi Master Dass Jennir leads a Nosaurian army in a war they have already lost. Is this what it now means to be a Jedi?''

''Dark Times #1 is the gateway to a new era in ''Star Wars'' history, an era where the future is grim, evil is on the rise, and all paths seem to lead nowhere&hellip;''
