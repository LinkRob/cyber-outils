The '''FSI-200 Fear Stick''', or simply the '''fear stick''', was a sharp weapon that was similar in size to a [[stylus]] or [[hypodermic injector]] and caused a powerful sensation of fear in a person stabbed with one. The device, which cost 1,500 credits, was popular with con artists and interrogators and were undetectable to scanners when powered down.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] came across a fear stick during her [[Jaylen Vrax's campaign|quest]] to find members for the [[Heist on Canto Bight]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fear sticks were first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
