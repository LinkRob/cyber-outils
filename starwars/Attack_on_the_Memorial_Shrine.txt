The '''attack on the Memorial Shrine'''<ref name="OSWM">{{GermanFanCite|106|Database}}</ref> was a bombing of the [[New Mandalorians|New Mandalorian]] [[Memorial Shrine]] in the heart of [[Mandalore]]'s [[capital]] [[city]], [[Sundari]], by the [[Death Watch]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" />

==The bombing==
The bomb was set off by a [[Death Watch bomber|member]] of the [[Death Watch]] [[terrorist]] organization while [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] and [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] strolled through the gardens at [[Peace Park]] on [[Sundari]], [[Mandalore]]. Many people were injured in the blast.<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" />

The Death Watch bomber left a [[hologram]] displaying the terrorist group's symbol in the explosion's wake. Master Kenobi and the [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protectors]] escorted Duchess Satine away from the carnage. Kenobi then ordered everyone present to be questioned by security forces. At that [[time]], the bomber ran off to flee, before being cornered by the Jedi at a balcony. The terrorist fired on Kenobi with a [[blaster pistol]] until the Jedi Master disarmed him. Rather than being arrested and [[Prisoner|imprisoned]], the bomber jumped off the balcony to commit [[Death|suicide]].<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" />
