During the [[Imperial Era/Legends|Imperial Period]], the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] '''enslaved''' the population of the [[Forest/Legends|forested]] [[Planet/Legends|world]] of [[Shar'Ack]]. The natives of Shar'Ack, the [[Shaliz'Na]], were a [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Species/Legends|species]] of artisans addicted to [[Alcohol/Legends|alcohol]]. Following their [[Slavery/Legends|enslavement]], the Galactic Empire took control of all [[Liquor/Legends|liquor]] manufactured on and imported to their planet.<ref name="POL" />
