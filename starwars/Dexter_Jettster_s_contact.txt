An individual was a '''contact''' for the [[Besalisk]] [[Dexter Jettster]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Edge of Balance Vol 4" /> In [[228 BBY]]<ref name="Date">[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 4|''The High Republic: The Edge of Balance'', Vol. 4]] takes place immediately after the events of [[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 3|''The High Republic: The Edge of Balance'', Vol. 3]] and concurrent with ''[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance: Premonition]]'', which take place in [[228 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:THRContent228BBY|here]].</ref> Jettster was contacted by his friend [[Jedi Master]]<ref name="Edge of Balance Vol 4" /> [[Savina Besatrix Malagán]]<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 5">[[The High Republic Adventures (2022) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2022) 5]]</ref> who sought the location of the [[Nihil]] [[scientist]] [[Niv Drendow Apruk]] as his [[Niv's ship|ship]] had [[Shosho|two]] [[Zimtok|individuals]] the Jedi Master and<ref name="Edge of Balance Vol 4" /> her<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> companions&mdash;the [[ZZ-model droid|droid]]<ref name="Edge of Balance Vol 4" /> [[ZZ-10]],<ref name="Precedent">''[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance: Precedent]]''</ref> Jedi Master [[Arkoff]], and the Nihil [[Desertion|deserter]] [[Maktan Plynt]]&mdash;were looking for. After stating he did not know Apruk, the Besalisk informed Malagán he would put out word for the Nihil to be found.<ref name="Edge of Balance Vol 4" />

Not long after, the contact spotted a [[Starship|ship]] they believed belonged to Apruk, and learned that he was heading back to<ref name="Edge of Balance Vol 4" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[planet]] [[Banchii]] and informed Jettster&mdash;who had since met up with Malagán and her companions&mdash;of their discovery. As the Besalisk informed Malagán about the contact's information, she noted Jettster's info was second to none. Upon fidning out that Apruk was headed back to Banchii, the group also went to Banchii and rescued the individuals they sought.<ref name="Edge of Balance Vol 4" />
