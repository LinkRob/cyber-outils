"'''Back From the Depths!'''" is an interview of [[Sam Witwer]] regarding his portrayal of the [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] [[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]] in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series. The interview appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 132|''Star Wars Insider'' 132]]<ref name="sw132" /> and was later reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2014|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2014]]<ref name="se2014" /> and [[The Best of Star Wars Insider Volume 4|''The Best of Star Wars Insider'' Volume 4]].<ref name="bosw" />
