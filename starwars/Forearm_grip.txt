A '''forearm grip''' was type of [[weapon]] attachment that was used by many professional [[Mercenary|mercenaries]]. The attachment was designed to allow an individual better usage of their weapon in close quarters and was often paired with a [[spread barrel]] to optimize [[blaster rifle]]s.<ref name="GG">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref>
