The '''Chargona campaign''' was a battle that involved the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]]<ref name="BTC" /> sometime by the [[Standard year|year]] [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="BTC" /> It took place in the vicinity of the [[Expansion Region]]'s [[Chargona system]].<ref name="Systems">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> When the 61st left the [[planet]] [[Haidoral Prime]] aboard the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]'' that year, veterans of the Chargona campaign passed around rumors of a coming last stand against a [[blockade]] of [[Star Destroyer]]s on the edge of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]], which were overheard by the rebel [[sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]].<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Chargona campaign was mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="BTC" />
