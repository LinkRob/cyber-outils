'''Cassian Andor's blaster''' was a [[BlasTech Industries|BlasTech]] [[A280-CFE convertible heavy blaster pistol]] used by [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]].<ref name="UVG" /> The [[blaster]]'s primary function is that of a hand-held sidearm, which he carried in a thigh [[holster]], but it could also accept [[barrel]] and [[stock]] attachments to convert it into a [[sniper rifle]] or an [[assault rifle]].<ref name="Rogue One" />
