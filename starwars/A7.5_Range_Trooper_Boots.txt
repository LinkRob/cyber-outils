The '''#A7.5 Range Trooper Boots''' were a model of [[magnetomic gription boot]]s, specialized [[magnet]]ic [[boot]]s utilized worn by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[range trooper]]s over their [[vambrace armor]]. The heavy-duty gription boots were magnetized, allowing [[Vandor]]'s range troopers on security duty to inspect the conveyex whilst the transport was in motion.

==Description==
<nowiki>#</nowiki>A7.5 Range Trooper Boots were a model of [[magnetomic gription boot]]s utilized by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[range trooper]]s assigned to<ref name="Gription boot DB">{{Databank|gription-boots|Gription boots}}</ref> the [[20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport]] operating on the [[planet]] [[Vardos]].<ref name="Conveyex DB">{{Databank|imperial-conveyex-transport|Conveyex}}</ref>

The boots were composed of standard [[armor]]ed footwear similar to the one worn by [[stormtrooper]]s, but surrounded with [[servomotor]]s on each ankle<ref name="100 objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref> needed to operate the boots while the powerful magnets on their soles were on,<ref name="Card Trader">{{SWCT|set=Solo: A Star Wars Story - Gear & Gadgets|cardname=Range Trooper's Magnetomic Gription Boots}}</ref> causing the boots to normally weigh 4.5 [[kilogram]]s each.<ref name="100 objects" /> The boots were linked to an interface control, which was typically located on a [[trooper]]'s vambrace armor plate<ref name="Gription boot 2020">[[Star Wars Day-at-a-Time Calendar 2020|''Star Wars'' Day-at-a-Time Calendar 2020]]</ref> and could instantly activate both boots' mag-seals with the press of a button, visually indicated by twin lights on each shin. A handle at the front shin allowed troopers to carry their boots with ease when not using them. Although the oversized servomotor and magnet assemblies appeared rough at first glance, they were designed to allow for ease of maintenance in the troopers' harsh operating deployments away from any dedicated [[technician]]s.<ref name="100 objects" />

==History==
In 10 BBY,<ref name="Timeline">{{InsiderCite|215|A Certain Point of View}} places ''Solo: A Star Wars Story'' ten [[Standard year|years]] before [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], which corresponds to [[10 BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> [[captain]] [[Denwade Banevans]] and his squad of range troopers utilized magnetomic boots as part of their standard equipment. During guard duty on a conveyex carrying a cargo of [[coaxium]] [[fuel]] they were [[Heist on Vandor|attacked]] by a group of [[smuggler]]s attempting to steal the coaxium and successfully utilized their boots to stay secure on the train and fight them off; however the smugglers detached a carriage and removed it on board an [[Y-45 armored transport hauler]].<ref name="Solo" />
