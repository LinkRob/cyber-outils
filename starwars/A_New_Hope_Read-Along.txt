'''''A New Hope Read-Along''''' was a read&ndash;along adaptation of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] following the release of the [[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition|Special Edition]] of the [[original trilogy]]. It was released on [[January]], [[1997]]<ref name="Amazon" /> by [[The Walt Disney Company|Walt Disney Records]] and used a full cast.<ref name="Read-along" />

==Publisher's summary==
''Relive the adventure! Disney Read Along Star Wars: A New Hope is the finest in children's audio entertainment. It features original music and vivid sound effects. The companion 24-page book has over 40 images from the movie. The set builds vocabulary and encourages independent reading. It is perfect for quiet times, trips, and gift giving throughout the year.''<ref name="Amazon" />

==Plot summary==
{{Plot-link|Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope}}

==Development==
The story includes material new to the Special Edition, such as [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo's]] encounter with [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba the Hutt]]; however, none of the book's photos are from the new Special Edition scenes.<ref name="Read-along" />

==Legacy==
The text and recording, including the voice cast, was reused for the [[2015]] [[canon]] adaptation ''[[A New Hope Read-Along Storybook and CD]]''.<ref name="2015 version">[[A New Hope Read-Along Storybook and CD|''A New Hope'' Read-Along Storybook and CD]]</ref>
