The original '''Dark Troopers''' were [[cyborg]] [[clone trooper]]s who had over seventy percent of their body replaced by mechanical components.<ref name="Sideshow" /> A subsequent iteration were [[Phase II Dark Trooper]]s, [[stormtrooper]]s<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> who used a heavily [[armor]]ed and modified body suit. The [[Dark Trooper project]] eventually viewed the [[human]] within as a weakness, and so the [[Phase-III Dark Trooper|third generation design]] transitioned to fully advanced [[battle droid]]s.<ref name="Sideshow" />
