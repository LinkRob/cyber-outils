'''265 BBY''', or '''265<ref name="Davenspon" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Faddaff Davenspon]], a [[Gender|female]] [[Deymasollian]]<ref name="Djin">{{EncyclopediaCite|Din Djarin and Other Characters of the Underworld}}</ref> who eventually became an outlaw [[Technician|tech-for-hire]]<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> by [[35 ABY]], was [[Reproduction|born]] around that year.<ref name="Davenspon" />

==Behind the scenes==
265 BBY was indirectly mentioned in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was authored by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
