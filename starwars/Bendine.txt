'''Bendine''' was a [[starship]] manufacturing company that produced the capsule-shaped [[XR12]] spacecraft. Bendine was active during the [[Republic Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], and one such XR12, the [[Satellite Liberator/Canon|''Satellite Liberator'']] ended up with the [[Toydarian]] [[junk dealer]] [[Watto]] in the settlement of [[Mos Espa]] on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="SW:CL" />

In [[5 BBY]], the mechanic and [[black market]] dealer [[Bix Caleen]] came to [[Repaak Salyard]] and asked for a [[Bendine Mesh-Tech filter]], a secret code-phrase to access a hidden junction box located in the shop.<ref name="Andor 1" />
