"'''Countdown'''" was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/sk x1 experimental air superiority fighter]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. He had short brown hair and light-colored skin.<ref name="Starfighter Missions" /> "Countdown" was stationed on [[Scarif]] during the [[Battle of Scarif|battle]] near the [[Citadel Tower]], where they entered a [[Starfighter combat|dogfight]] with an [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]].<ref name="TIE Striker expansion" />
