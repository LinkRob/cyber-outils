'''Blast Knuckles''' were a type of heavy, [[armor]]ed glove that was equipped with small, integrated [[blaster]] emitter, which was fired from impact-sensitive plating along the knuckles when the wearer landed a punch, delivering a devastating blast. The blaster emitter was tiny, with almost no [[galven circuit]]ry implemented, giving it a maximum range of mere [[centimeter]]s. However, while effective, the Blast Knuckles were also dangerous for the wielder, who was prone to suffering the effects of overheating or even splash damage should the glove bunch awkwardly.<ref name="Gadgets" />
