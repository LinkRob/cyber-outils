'''Flat-blast artillery shells''' were a type of explosive weapon capable of inflicting massive damage on population centers, food sources, and bodies of water. The shells made a distinctive sound upon exploding, and were used extensively in a [[Sunrise's civil war|civil war]] on the [[planet]] "[[Sunrise]]" that decimated its population.

==Description==
Flat-blast artillery shells were an explosive weapon that heavily damaged settlements, [[Foodstuff|food]], and [[water]] sources. Their explosions made a distinctive sound that resembled that of the zipper on some jackets owned by [[Chiss]]. The sound similarity was detected by some [[Agbui]] who'd witnessed the shells used in war, as well as a waveform comparison.<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref>

==History==
[[Haplif]], an Agbui agent of [[Grysk Hegemony]] operative "[[Jixtus]]," instigated a [[Sunrise's civil war|civil war]] on the [[planet]] "[[Sunrise]]" and pushed flat-blast artillery shells on both sides. Within six months of arriving, Haplif successfully engineered Sunrise's civil war and caused destruction of most or all of its [[Homeworld|native]] [[The Magys's people|population]], with much of its devastation from the shells.<ref name="Greater Good" />

Agbui mid-ager [[Frosif]] was present for the Sunrise civil war and became traumatized by the sound of the shells' explosions. As a result, Chiss mid-ager [[Lakris]]'s "magic trick" involving her jacket's zipper triggered trauma in Frosif. [[Senior Aide]] [[Xodlak'ji'iprip]] analyzed the zipper sound, finding a resemblance with flat-blast artillery shell explosions. This led Lakjiip and Lakris's [[Parent|father]] [[Xodlak'phr'ooa]] to suspect the Agbui they hosted on [[Celwis]] were refugees rather than peaceful "cultural nomads" as they claimed.<ref name="Greater Good" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flat-blast artillery shells were mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Greater Good" />
