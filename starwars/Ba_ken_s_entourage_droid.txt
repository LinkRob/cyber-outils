'''Ba'ken's entourage droid'''<ref name="ReleaseEvent">{{YouTube|video=FUYb52fpIFg|text=【トークノーカット】浪川大輔、ファイルーズあい、黒沢ともよ、監督9名が登壇！『スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ』Volume3 配信直前イベント|channel=@cinematoday|name=シネマトゥデイ|date=2025-10-28}}</ref> served as a levitating [[guard droid]] for the [[alien]] [[Ba'ken]] on the [[planet]] [[Zenarita]]. It had [[Color|red]] [[sensor]]s on a cylindrical grey-plated head and body with an orange-colored sphere protruding from the bottom. The front of its body had two buttons and two compartment hatches. One of the hatches housed a three-pronged [[Limb|arm]] that could extend out and hold a [[blaster]].<ref name="Smuggler" />

At some point between [[2 BBY]] and [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VisionsDate|307}}</ref> the [[droid]] levitated near a booth where Ba'ken was lounging and drinking when the [[human]] [[smuggler]] [[Chita]] confronted<ref name="Smuggler" /> him<ref name="SmugglerAD">{{Visions|The Smuggler (short film)}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> about her [[Chita's ship|ship's]] systems being deactivated. When Chita grew aggressive toward Ba'ken and reached for her [[blaster pistol]], the droid pointed its own blaster at her head. She quickly surrendered and left the [[cantina]] shortly thereafter.<ref name="Smuggler" />

==Behind the scenes==
The guard droid first appeared in "[[The Smuggler (short film)|The Smuggler]]," a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Trigger (animation studio)|Trigger]], was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Smuggler" /> on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="ReleaseDate">{{SW|url=news/why-we-love-star-wars-visions|text=Why We Love the Unique Stories of ''Star Wars: Visions''}}</ref>

Before its appearance in the short, the droid was first revealed in concept art<ref name="Celebration">{{SWYouTube|83cNchT1gdA|Star Wars Celebration - DAY 3 Replay|livestream=p20GM68wyC4}}</ref> by [[Tadashi Hiramatsu]]<ref name="Concept6">{{SW|url=the-smuggler-concept-art-gallery-star-wars-visions-volume-3|text="The Smuggler" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars: Visions Volume 3}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref> during day three of [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan]]<ref name="Celebration" /> on [[April 20]] of the same year.<ref name="CelebrationDate">{{SW|url=news/visions-presents-the-ninth-jedi|text=Volume 3 Release Date and New ''Star Wars: Visions'' Spin-off Series}}</ref> At the ''Star Wars: Visions'' release event a [[October 28|day before]] the short's release, the droid was labeled in concept art as バッケン取り巻きドロイド (''Bakken torimaki doroido'')  in [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]], which translates to  "Ba'ken's entourage droid" in [[Wikipedia:English language|English]].<ref name="ReleaseEvent" />
