'''Admiral of the Fleet''',<ref name="Dawn" /> also identified as '''Admiral of the Navy''',<ref name="Imperial Officers and Agents">{{EncyclopediaCite|Imperial Officers and Agents}}</ref> was used in the [[Imperial Navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[Admiralty (Imperial)|Admiralty's]] [[Imperial Navy High Command|Fleet Command]] branch had authority over most of the Navy personnel, which ranged from the lowest recruit, to the [[Admiral]] of the Fleet.<ref name="Dawn" /> [[Chief of the Imperial Navy]] [[Conan Antonio Motti]] was the Admiral of the Fleet after climbing the Imperial hierarchy, more so by tradition and family relationships than his merits.<ref name="Imperial Officers and Agents" />
