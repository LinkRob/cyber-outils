'''Abandoned Mine''' was a [[mine]] west of the [[Pillar Forest]] in the [[North Jundland Wastes]] region of the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the mine had been abandoned and had the entrance sealed by a grate. The materials to apply a unity paint scheme to a [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]] were left in a container in the mine.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
During 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] used her [[fusioncutter]] to break into the mine and retrieve the materials.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The mine appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' published by [[Ubisoft]]. It was only accessible after the player completes the "Legacy" quest in which they are granted a fusioncutter.<ref name="Outlaws" />
