'''Energetic Ewok''' was an [[Ewok]] of the [[Bright Tree tribe]] on the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. They received a potion from the [[village Doctor]] to help them sleep, but it caused them to grow a second [[nose]] instead.<ref name="LEGO" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Energetic Ewok first appeared in the [[2022]] [[canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', in the side mission "Ubbla the Weather".<ref name="LEGO" />
