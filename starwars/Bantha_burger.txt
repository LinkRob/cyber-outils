'''Bantha burgers''' were a type of [[burger]] made using the meat of [[bantha]]s.<ref name="AEYNTK" />

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bantha burgers were first mentioned in the [[2015]] ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]'', a reference book written by [[Adam Bray]], [[Kerrie Dougherty]], [[Cole Horton]], and [[Michael Kogge]],<ref name="AEYNTK" /> before later having a recipe created for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

During the ''[[Star Wars]]'' Festival limited time event in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Fortnite Brick Life]]'',<ref name="SW Festival">{{FortniteWeb|url=news/star-wars-hits-lego-fortnite-brick-life-and-odyssey|text=Star Wars Hits LEGO® Fortnite Brick Life and Odyssey!}}</ref> the bantha burger became a craftable food item by adding bantha meat, [[tomato]], a [[vegetable]], and bread on a grill.<ref name="Brick Life">''[[LEGO Fortnite Brick Life]]''</ref>
