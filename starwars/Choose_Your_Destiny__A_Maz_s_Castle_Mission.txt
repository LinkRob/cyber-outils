'''''Choose Your Destiny: A Maz's Castle Mission''''' is a [[canon]] audiobook written by [[Cavan Scott]] and part of the ''[[Star Wars: Choose Your Destiny]]'' series. The audiobook was released exclusively on [[Wikipedia:Amazon|Amazon]] FreeTime Unlimited in [[2019]].<ref name="Website" /> As of June 2024, it is exclusively available as an Alexa Skill with an Amazon Kids+ subscription.<ref name="Skill">{{Amazon|url=/Star-Wars-Choose-Your-Destiny/dp/B07LCZGLD7|text=Star Wars: Choose Your Destiny|archivedate=20240615023033}}</ref> 

There is one canon ending and there are 14 non-canon endings. The path to the canon ending is AA,[B,AB,CB,CAB],[B,AB],AA,[B,ABA],BBAABA,[BA,ABB,CBB],AA  (the brackets indicate a choice of paths). Therefore there are 48 different paths to the canon ending, taking between 26 to 28 minutes to reach the ending. The total duration of all story segments is approximately 52 minutes and there are 591 total paths possible.
