'''Caleb Arseneaux''' is a producer and [[Star Wars video games|video game]] designer who managed the production of both [[Star Wars: Jedi Challenges]] and [[Star Wars: Puzzle Droids]] in [[2017]]. He worked for [[The Walt Disney Company]] from [[May]] [[2016]] until [[August]] [[2018]], before leaving to work for [[Wikipedia:Blizzard Entertainment|Blizzard Entertainment, Inc.]], though he now serves as an executive producer for [[Bungie]] as of [[November]] [[2021]].<ref name="LinkedIn" />
