[[Sergeant]] '''Detral''' was an individual who served within the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="THRA 14" /> Around [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Detral led his squad, which consisted of the member [[The Warden|Herwint Tiks]], on a mission somewhere in [[the galaxy]]. However, the mission was at risk due to faulty managing of the squad by Detral, who was emotional during the time of the operation. Sergeant Detral was [[Death|killed]] as a result of his actions, which led the remainder of the group to successfully complete the mission without him, albeit the cost for life of all but Tiks in the squad and a subsequent uprising following the mission.<ref name="THRA 14" />

Later on, Tiks met with [[General]] [[Abediah Viess]] of the Nihil, who requested his presence to converse about his recent mission. When asked if Tiks was the [[commanding officer]] for the squad, Tiks declined and mentioned Detral, describing the actions of the late sergeant that placed the squad in danger. Tiks continued to talk about Detral's demise during the mission and the triumph that occurred following his death. Viess, who comprehended what Tiks explained, then asked for Tiks to join her band of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] for the Nihil after the uprising would be crushed by the organization, with Tiks agreeing to the offer.<ref name="THRA 14" />

==Behind the scenes==
Sergeant Detral was first mentioned in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 14|fourteenth issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']]. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Harvey Tolibao]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]<ref name="THRA 14" /> on [[January 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-807/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-14|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #14}}</ref>
