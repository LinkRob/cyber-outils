'''Bevelyn''', nicknamed "'''Bev'''," was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who lived during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. At some point, they met and [[Marriage|married]] a human male named<ref name="Star Wars 27" /> [[Jon Melton]].<ref name="Star Wars 26" /> The couple had two [[Youngling|children]], [[Reen (Bevelyn)|Reen]] and [[Zelly]].<ref name="Star Wars 27" /> Sometime later, Bev and Melton became [[Sleeper Agent|spies]] for Crimson Dawn within the Empire due to their dislike at where the Empire was headed and eventually came to work aboard the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] during its construction, with Bev as a cargo worker, and Melton as a [[lieutenant]] and [[aide]] to [[Moff]] [[Tiaan Jerjerrod]], the [[Death Star]]'s [[Commanding officer|commander]].<ref name="Star Wars 26" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars (2020) 26|''Star Wars'' (2020) 26]] 26 take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> Qi'ra activated her agents within the Empire and instructed them to cause chaos for the Empire.<ref name="Star Wars 26" /> Bev and Melton decided to [[Desertion|defect]] from the Empire and [[Mission to reveal the Death Star II|inform]] the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] about the existence of the Death Star in exchange for safe passage and protection.<ref name="Star Wars 27" /> The pair then made plans to leave the [[Space station|station]] aboard a [[Scopium|shuttle]] with their children to inform the Rebellion.<ref name="Star Wars 26" />
