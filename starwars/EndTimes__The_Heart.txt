"'''EndTimes: The Heart'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year five of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign. It was the first adventure in the [[EndTimes trilogy|''EndTimes'' trilogy]] and was written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] and [[Jae Walker]].

==Plot summary==
An important conference is planned in the [[Cularin system/Legends|Cularin system]], but it is believed that the [[Believers]] have a mole, who will give them details of its location and allow them to attack it. The heroes are sent to attend a decoy conference and later track down and destroy a Believers cell in [[Tolea Biqua]].
