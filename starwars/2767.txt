'''2767''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] in the [[Trade Federation Droid Army]]. In [[32 BBY]], he was part of the forces deployed to the [[city]] of [[Theed]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] by the Federation during its [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]] of the world. 2767 was part of a group of [[battle droid]]s led by the [[OOM command battle droid]] [[2163]] that were tasked with guarding the [[Naboo Royal Starship]] in the [[Theed Hangar]].<ref name="tpm" />

The [[droid]]s were soon approached by a group led by the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], who was attempting to transport [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] off world using the royal [[starship]]. After 2163 ordered Jinn and his companions be arrested, the [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] cut down 2767, allowing the Queen to escape.<ref name="tpm" />

==Behind the scenes==
2767 first appeared in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. In the film, 2767's designation is not spoken, but the numerals are visible on the droid's [[comlink booster pack]],<ref name="tpm" /> which translate to 2767.<ref name="Complete Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>
