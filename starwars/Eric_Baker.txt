'''Eric Baker''' is a props and set dressing artist who served as creative director of set dressing and props for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] for [[Walt Disney Imagineering]]. Baker led a team of 15 artists who traveled the world in search of inspiration for creating both copies of [[Black Spire Outpost]]. Baker collaborated closely with ''[[Star Wars]]'' series artists to maintain the authenticity of his designs. He also visited the set of {{Film|VIII}} to help learn the "language" of the franchise.<ref name="One Day Book" />
