The '''Federal District Police Academy''', also known as '''FedPoAcad''', was an academy for [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]] located in the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]'s [[Federal District]]. The [[human]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo|Tanivos Divo]] earned a scholarship to the institution and graduated as valedictorian before joining the [[Coruscant Security Force]] by [[26 BBY|7951]] [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] He also spoke before the academy's graduating class in [[24 BBY|7953 C.R.C.]] His [[Gender|daughter]], [[Andressa Divo]], then attended during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and joined the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] after graduating.

==Description==

The Federal District Police Academy, also known as FedPoAcad, was a [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]] academy in the [[Federal District]] of<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. Its graduates could join the [[Coruscant Security Force]] and later the [[Imperial Security Bureau]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==History==
{{Quote|Tan Divo retired with honors within the early years of the Empire. His daughter, Andressa, was eager to continue the family tradition of service, and enrolled in the same academy as her father. Upon graduation, however, a new avenue was available to Andressa that was not to her father: the Imperial Security Bureau.|Exantor Divo, ''Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious''|<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />}}
[[File:Andressa_Divo_SaV.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Three Imperial Security Bureau agents in black armor and uniform stand at attention in a row with Andressa Divo at the center in front of rows of stormtroopers. A gray wall is visible in the background.|Andressa Divo (center) joined the Imperial Security Bureau after graduating from the Federal District Police Academy.]]
Watching his [[Tanivos Exantor Divo's father|father]] be extorted by criminals while growing up on [[Level 3215]] of Coruscant during the [[Republic Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] instilled a rigid sense of justice and fairness in the [[human]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo|Tanivos Divo]], who earned a scholarship to the academy at the age of sixteen after applying himself to his academic studies. He graduated as valedictorian and spoke from a podium before the 4,500 newly minted [[Deputy|deputies]] of that session's roster.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

In his speech, he spoke of the importance of policing and why the students had chosen that career. Divo began his career as a patrolman within the Coruscant Security Force and went on to become a [[lieutenant]] by [[26 BBY|7951]] [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] and later an [[inspector]]. He also spoke before the academy's graduating class of [[24 BBY|7953.4 C.R.C.]]<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

After Divo retired with honors in the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]], his [[Gender|daughter]], [[Andressa Divo]], enrolled in the Federal District Police Academy. Eager to continue the [[Divo family]] tradition of service, Andressa was devoted to justice and absorbed the speeches of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and his cabinet, aiming to join the Imperial Security Bureau while studying at the academy. By [[12 BBY|7965 C.R.C.]], she had achieved her goal after graduating, eventually also earning the rank of inspector.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Federal District Police Academy was mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and included an illustration of the academy by [[Raph Lomotan]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Coruscant Police training academies]] for the [[Coruscant Security Force/Legends|Coruscant Security Force]] were mentioned in ''[[Galaxy at War]]'', a [[2009]] supplement for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' published by [[Wizards of the Coast]].<ref name="GAW">''[[Galaxy at War]]''</ref>
