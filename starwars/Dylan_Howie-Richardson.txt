'''Dylan Howie-Richardson''' is a freelance graphic designer, concept artist, and video editor<ref name="LinkedIn" /> who has previously worked on [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi|''Obi-Wan Kenobi'']], designing a number of possible background aliens for the series, and is attributed with the creation of [[Gorg Singo]],<ref name="ArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=django42|url=artwork/G85bG3|name=[[Dylan Howie-Richardson]]|text=Obi-Wan Kenobi alien concept designs (1)|date=2022-11-28|quote=Work I did for the Obi-Wan Kenobi series on Disney+ designing makeup prosthetics. Featured are designs of aliens for Mos Eisley in Episode 1.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/F5h7f}}</ref> as well as and finalizing [[Ayo Tam]]'s design using a [[Miles Teves]] head sculpt as a basis.<ref name="Smith FX">{{Facebook|username=p/Smith-FX-100063583262385|url=photo/?fbid=875445987918169&set=pb.100063583262385.-2207520000|name=[[Shaun Smith]]|date=2023-12-27|quote=Ayo Tam was one of several background characters we did on the Obi-Wan series. This was originally sculpted by Miles Teves for a completely different project that did not come to fruition. I had Dylan Howie-Richardson sketch out an alien based on the sculpture and thus Ayo Tam was born into the colorful Star Wars universe! He only appears in one scene of the series, walking in the background - let me know if you spot him!|archivedate=20240516152129}}</ref>
