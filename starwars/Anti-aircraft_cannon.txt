'''Anti-aircraft cannons''' were a type of weapon designed to destroy [[starship]]s. These cannons were employed by the [[Galactic Republic]]'s experimental [[IPV-2C Stealth Corvette|stealth ship]], which took part in the [[Battle of Christophsis]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> [[J-1 proton cannon]]s were a type of anti-aircraft cannon.<ref name="HC">{{HelmetCollectionCite|35|Helmets}}</ref> The [[Galactic Empire]] utilized the [[All Terrain Anti Aircraft]]<ref name="Starships">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> as well as manned stationary [[turret]]s for anti-air defense.<ref name="Believer" />
