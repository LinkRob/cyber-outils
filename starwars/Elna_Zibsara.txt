'''Elna Zibsara''' was a [[human]] [[pilot]]. She fought with the [[New Republic]] at the [[Battle of Jakku]], but later retired from service. Following the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]], [[Temmin Wexley]] recruited the veteran pilot into the [[Resistance]], where Zibsara specialized in training new recruits. The name "Poe" was inscribed in [[Aurebesh]] on her [[flight helmet]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
