A '''blast vest''' was a type of [[armor]] that could be worn over the torso.<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[Commander]]s of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] sometimes wore blast vests during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="A&A">''[[Allies and Adversaries]]''</ref> A trio of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] including [[Strater]] all wore blast vests when visiting the bar ''[[Serendipity]]'' in search of the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' during the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="Smugglers">''[[Smuggler's Run: A Han Solo & Chewbacca Adventure]]''</ref> The [[Ozrelanso]] [[Plesko Marno]] wore a [[Color|red]] armored blast vest with a [[data "cold box" ring module]] on the back<ref name="TROS VD" /> while in the [[Spice Runners' Den]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]]<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}} to [[35 ABY]].</ref>
