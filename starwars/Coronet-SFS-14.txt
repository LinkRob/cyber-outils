'''Coronet-SFS-14''' was a large [[Color|gray]] [[factory]] belonging to the companies [[Santhe Corporation|Santhe]] and [[Sienar Fleet Systems]]. It was located in [[Coronet City]] somewhere between [[Narro Sienar Boulevard]] and [[Coronet City Spaceport|Coronet Spaceport]] on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. A row of four [[desalination tank]]s were constructed alongside the factory.<ref name="SoloOG" />

In [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events on [[Corellia]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[13 BBY]].</ref> the criminals [[Han Solo|Han]] and [[Qi'ra]] raced past the factory in a [[Thief|stolen]] [[M-68 landspeeder]] while trying to escape [[Moloch]] of the [[White Worms]] gang, who also drove past the factory<ref name="Solo" /> in his [[A-A4B truckspeeder]].<ref name="SoloOG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coronet-SFS-14 first appeared in the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="Solo" /> and received identification in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> The Corellia [[speeder]] chase locations in ''Solo'' were filmed at [[Wikipedia:Fawley Power Station|Fawley Power Station]] or [[Wikipedia:Tilbury|Tilbury]] in [[Wikipedia:England|England]].<ref name="Making of">''[[Industrial Light & Magic Presents: Making Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
