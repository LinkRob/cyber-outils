'''Baleen''' was a body part found on [[aiwha]] and used to eat [[Krill (creature)|krill]].<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> While some [[purrgil]] had [[Tooth|teeth]] scattered throughout their [[mouth]]s,<ref name="The Call">{{Rebels|The Call}}</ref> others had full mouths of baleen.<ref name="PartFive">{{Ahsoka|Part Five: Shadow Warrior}}</ref>

{{Science-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Baleen was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'', a [[2017]] reference book written by [[Tricia Barr]], [[Adam Bray]], and [[Cole Horton]].<ref name="TVE" /> It first appeared in "[[Part Five: Shadow Warrior]]," the fifth episode of the [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]'',<ref name="PartFive" /> which released on [[September 12]], [[2023]].<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/ahsoka-theatrical-event|text=Watch ''Ahsoka'' in Theaters at the Midseason Fan Celebration Event}}</ref> Baleen was mentioned prior in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity in the [[2010]] ''[[The Unknown Regions]]'' supplement to the ''[[Saga Edition]]'' role-playing game.<ref name="TUR">''[[The Unknown Regions]]''</ref> In real life, [[:Wikipedia:Baleen|baleen]] is a material that [[:Wikipedia:Baleen whale|baleen whales]] have instead of [[:Wikipedia:Tooth|teeth]], which allows them to filter-feed prey such as [[:Wikipedia:Krill|krill]] and [[:Wikipedia:Plankton|plankton]].<ref name="BaleenIRL">{{WebCite|url=https://us.whales.org/whales-dolphins/what-is-baleen|text=What is baleen?|work=Whale and Dolphin Conservation|archivedate=20230708200802}}</ref>
