The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''10 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3267 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7967''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*"[[Vader's Many Prosthetic Parts]]," a song written by [[Nakari Kelen]]'s [[Nakari Kelen's mother|mother]] and performed by the group [[Hakko Drazlip and the Tootle Froots]] that mocks [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], is published. Just [[Standard day|days]] after its release, the Empire sends the entire group to the [[Spice mines of Kessel|Spice mines]] of [[Kessel]].<ref name="Heir">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref>
*[[Han Solo]] deserts from the [[Galactic Empire]] and meets his lifelong friend and companion [[Chewbacca]] on [[Mimban]].<ref name="Solo" />
*Han Solo does the [[Kessel Run]] in less than 12 [[parsec]]s using the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to evade pursuing [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] following a [[raid on Kessel]].<ref name="Solo" />
*[[Qi'ra]] replaces [[Dryden Vos]] as figurehead of the [[Crimson Dawn]] after [[Death|killing]] him.<ref name="Solo" />
*Han Solo wins the ''Millennium Falcon'' from [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] in a game of [[sabacc]].<ref name="Solo" />
*Nine year old [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] runs away for the first time. She hides in the woods for a [[Standard week|week]] before being found by her parents' [[Alderaan Guard|guards]]. She later referred to the incident as the "best week of her life."<ref name="SW33">[[Star Wars (2015) 33|''Star Wars'' (2015) 33]]</ref>
*The [[Gorothite|Gorothites]] of [[Goroth Prime]] are enslaved by the Empire and mine [[Hyperbaride|hyperbarides]]. This releases a dangerous amount of radiation onto the planet and its inhabitants.<ref>[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire|''Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire'']]</ref>
