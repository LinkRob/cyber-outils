'''Alderaanian rose cake''' was a type of [[cake]] that shared its name with<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]<ref name="Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref> and was [[Diet|edible]] by [[human]]s and [[Hutt]]s.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> while in the [[Greylark family]]'s [[Greylark residence|residence]] on<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> the Core Worlds planet<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> [[Coruscant]], the human [[Zenny Greylark]] and the Hutt [[Churo]] ate Alderaanian rose cake covered in [[candied petal]]s and drank [[green milk]] to celebrate after learning Churo's [[Devirsivik Clan|sister]] [[Dahara Devirsivik Norri]] would arrive to take him [[Nal Hutta|home]] that [[Local day|night]]. Norri took longer to arrive than Churro had expected and they both continued to eat more cake as they waited.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />
