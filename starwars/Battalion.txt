A '''battalion''' was a military formation within different branches. Both the [[Separatist Droid Army|armies]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> and the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] deployed battalions of troops during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Episode III" /> The Confederacy integrated [[DSD1 dwarf spider droid]]s into their droid battalions.<ref name="SWE Tanks Droids">{{EncyclopediaCite|Droid Tanks and Vehicles, and Other Mechanicals}}</ref> A single Republic [[regiment]] was composed of four battalions, and furthermore, those battalions were made up of four [[Company|companies]].<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> Republic [[brigade]]s also included multiple battalions.<ref name="The End Battalions">{{EncyclopediaCite|The End of the Clone Wars}} {{C|Original French text: "Par conséquent, la flotte de Destroyers stellaires et les bataillons de soldats clones qui accompagnèrent le Général Kenobi sur Utapau étaient trois fois plus importants que le contingent qui aurait été déployé en temps normal pour prendre un petit système stellaire."}}</ref>

In the [[Stormtrooper Corps]], a battalion consisted of four [[Company|companies]], giving one a total of 800 [[stormtrooper]]s. A [[regiment]] consisted of four Imperial battalions.<ref name="SuperGraphic">''[[Star Wars Super Graphic: A Visual Guide to a Galaxy Far, Far Away]]''</ref> Battalions could be led by a [[major]].<ref name="Dark Lord 14">[[Darth Vader (2017) 14|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 14]]</ref> The [[Imperial Army]] also made use of [[heavy battalion]]s, which made use of [[armored cavalry]] such as [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]s, [[K79-S80 Imperial Troop Transport|Imperial Troop Transports]], and [[All Terrain Defense Pod]]s. [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] [[Unidentified armored division|armored division]] included heavy battalions, which served him during the [[Battle of Atollon]].<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref> The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] utilized maintenance battalions as part of its military organization. Wandering [[Technician|mechanics]] were always welcome in these units.<ref name="Fully Operational">[[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']]</ref> Standard infantry battalions were also used by the Alliance.<ref name="Vader Down">[[Vader Down 1|''Vader Down'' 1]]</ref>

Battalions were also used by [[Starfighter Corps]] as part of their operational structures. The [[First Order]] deployed a [[Unidentified TIE fighter battalion|TIE fighter battalion]] against the [[Resistance]] in a [[skirmish over D'Qar]].<ref name="A Quick">{{Resistance|A Quick Salvage Run}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
According to the "[[Secrets of Spaceflight]]" section in [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 93|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 93]]<ref name="Build93">{{BuildFalconCite|93|Secrets of Spaceflight}}</ref> and its update in [[Star Wars: Millennium Falcon 93|''Star Wars: Millennium Falcon'' 93]], stormtrooper battalions were composed of 820 individuals<ref name="Falcon93">{{FalconCite|93|Secrets of Spaceflight}}</ref> whereas ''[[Star Wars Super Graphic: A Visual Guide to a Galaxy Far, Far Away]]'' gave the number of 800 stormtroopers per battalion.<ref name="SuperGraphic" />
