'''Crunchies'''<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e5-trivia-gallery|text="The Axe Forgets" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> were a type of [[Stone|pebble-sized]] [[cereal]] that was edible to [[human]]s. [[Color|Blue]] and purple in color, the spherical treats could be served in bowls of [[blue milk]]. Former [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]] [[Deputy Inspector]] [[Syril Karn]] was given crunchies by his [[Eedy Karn|mother]] in [[5 BBY]] after he was let go from [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces|Pre-Mor Enforcement]]. Karn at first refrained from consuming the dish, instead plucking one of the crunchies from his [[milk]] and giving it a thorough inspection. Eventually, however, Karn ate his crunchies.<ref name="Axe" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|[The cereal] tastes like dreams and unicorns. It's like the best thing ever!|Kyle Soller|<ref name="EW">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/tv/andor-space-cereal-syril-karn-kyle-soller|text=The space cereal on ''Andor'' tastes like 'dreams and unicorns'|author=Ross, Dalton|date=2022-11-03|archivedate=20221103161736}}</ref>}}
Crunchies first appeared in "[[The Axe Forgets]]," the fifth episode of the [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.<ref name="Axe" /> Although unnamed in dialogue, they are identified in both the episode's script<ref name="Trivia" /> and descriptive audio.<ref name="Axe AD">Per the Audio Description of "[[The Axe Forgets]]" available on [[Disney+]]</ref>

The cereal box was designed by<ref name="Twitter" /> graphic designer<ref name="Kassa">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> [[Barry Gingell]], who created several prototypes made out of cardboard to get the flap right, before adding a stripe and a window.<ref name="Twitter" /> Set dec advanced prop modeler<ref name="Kassa" /> [[Holly Martin]] then created the box from [[Wikipedia:Styrene|styrene]].<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|BazatronPrime|status/1580318944522833921|[[Barry Gingell]]|date=2022-10-13|quote=That's my box 😁 Saw cereal in the script & had an idea for a SW style box. Made some prototypes out of card to get the flap right. Next one had a window. Next one had a better window. Added a stripe. Done. Holly Martin made it out of styrene for me. Upside down in this scene 😊|archivedate=1580318944522833921}}</ref> The cereal pieces used are the blue and purple pieces from the real-life cereal [[Wikipedia:Trix (cereal)|Trix]] Trolls World Tour with Marshmallows.<ref name="Amazon">{{Amazon|url=Trix-Trolls-World-Marshmallows-Cereal/dp/B083W1JHS4|text=Trix Trolls World Tour with Marshmallows Cereal, 9.7 oz}} {{C|[https://web.archive.org/web/2/https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/717q6uRawRL._AC_SL1200_.jpg Photograph] for comparison}}</ref> Prop master [[Martyn Doust]] viewed the cereal as a very mundane, everyday piece of life. He added a bowl and spoon, which he considered the basic parts of cereal.<ref name="Props">{{SW|url=news/andor-props|text=The Stories Behind 5 Memorable ''Andor'' Props}}</ref> Before filming, the props department asked Syril Karn's actor, [[Kyle Soller]], if there was anything he did not eat, and he replied there was not. They then showed him the cereal, which he thought looked like "''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:Cocoa Puffs|Cocoa Puffs]]." Soller said the cereal was surprisingly delicious and tasted like "dreams and unicorns."<ref name="EW" />
