'''Chad''' was a [[planet]] located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]] that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Chadra-Fan]] [[species]].<ref name="Chadra-Fan" /> A [[Color|blue]] [[dressing]] known as [[Chadian dressing]] was from this world.<ref name="The Visual Dictionary" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chad was first mentioned in ''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]'', a [[1989]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] sourcebook written by [[Troy Denning]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Alien Races">''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]''</ref> The planet became [[canon]] when it was mentioned in the [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry for the Chadra-Fan species that was published on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Chadra-Fan" />
