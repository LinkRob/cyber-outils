A '''class 11 power core''', was a type of [[power core]] that could transfer non-standard electric currents. Standard [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] equipment during the [[Imperial Era]] was incompatible with that type of power core.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> at least one class 11 power core had been stored at the [[Imperial Comm Station]] on [[Toshara]], a [[moon]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. [[Waka]], a [[Rodian]] [[Technician|mechanic]] on Toshara, sent [[Kay Vess]] to steal the power core from the base. After she stole the power core to the bases security office, Vess proceeded to the use it to access the wreckage of the ''[[Castellan]]'', a [[High Republic Era]] ship that crashed on the moon.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Class 11 power cores first appeared as a quest item as part of "The Wreck" main quest in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
