'''Brayzak''' was a [[Pyke]] [[gambler]] that was invited to the [[Okala Five]] [[Kessel Sabacc]] tournament aboard the [[casino]] [[cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> He was a favorite to win the tournament and a [[Mon Calamari]] [[bookie]] aboard the ship was offering 2&ndash;to&ndash;1 [[Gambling|odds]] on him winning. Brayzak had [[Color|blue]] skin, black eyes and had a habit of clearing his throat when he cheated at Sabacc.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brayzak first appeared in the "Wild Card" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[November 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Wild Card released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/1Q4y5lMJwdUUt6qXzbWKPI/star-wars-outlaws-game-update-showcase-wrapup|text=Star Wars Outlaws Game Update Showcase Wrap-Up}}</ref>
