'''Akivian herb juice''' was a [[Foodstuff|beverage]] made for special events by rural communities on [[Akiva]], a [[jungle]] [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a bottle of herb juice could be sold for 280 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bottles of Akivian herb juice appeared as inventory items in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
