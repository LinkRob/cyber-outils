'''''Correlian''''' <small>''[sic]''</small> '''''YT-1300''''' was a decorative [[Painting|poster]] put up in [[Ezra Bridger]] and [[Garazeb Orrelios]]' cabin aboard the ''[[Ghost]]''. It showed a [[YT-1300 light freighter]], accompanied by an [[Aurebesh]] caption; however, the word "[[Corellia]]n" was misspelled "Correlian".<ref name="Fighter Flight" /> The same text was printed in [[Color|red]] on the [[Light freighter|freighter]]'s [[hull]].<ref name="Steps">{{Rebels|Steps Into Shadow}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Correlian'' <small>[sic]</small> ''YT-1300'' [[Painting|poster]] was originally created for the [[cockpit]] of the [[TR-RL3 Terrapin-class bulk tanker|Turtle Tanker]] in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Brothers (episode)|Brothers]]."<ref name="Presley">{{Instagram|username=phattro|name=[[Pat Presley]]|url=p/CvFtTd5RP0i|date=2023-07-24|archivefile=File:PatPresley-GhostRooms3-Instagram.png}}</ref> However, the poster was cut from the final episode.<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref> It later debuted in ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Fighter Flight]]."<ref name="Fighter Flight" />
