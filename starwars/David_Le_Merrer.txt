'''David Le Merrer''' worked as a visual development artist on the TV series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' at [[Lucasfilm Animation]]. He notably designed the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple's]] [[Chamber of Judgment/Legends|Chamber of Judgment]],<ref name="TCW1">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep520|text="The Wrong Jedi" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide}} {{C|slide 4}}</ref> a [[Theed plasma refinery|plasma refinery]] on [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]],<ref name="TCW2">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep418|text="Crisis on Naboo" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide}} {{C|slide 9}}</ref> and the [[Barriss Offee's quarters/Legends|private quarters]] of [[Barriss Offee/Legends|Barriss Offee]].<ref name="TCW3">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep520|text="The Wrong Jedi" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide}} {{C|slide 3}}</ref>{{Mediacat|imagecat=images by David Le Merrer}}
