'''Aca's Funnel''' was a stone structure located at [[Acablas]], on the [[planet]] [[Auratera]].<ref name="NoP" />

==Overview==
Built by an ancient group over the cenote at Acablas, Aca's Funnel was an irregularly shaped elliptical cone that served as a focusing point of the vergence below. The opening in the bottom of the Funnel was off-center, and allowed the elements down into the cenote. A rocky formation in its natural state, the funnel was paved over to form a winding stairway that descended to the opening.<ref name="NoP" />
