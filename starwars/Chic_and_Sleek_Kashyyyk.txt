'''Chic and Sleek Kashyyyk''' was a hygiene product designed for the [[Wookiee]]s of the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]]. It was advertised on the [[BSO 401.72]] radio station that broadcast in [[Black Spire Outpost]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]].<ref name="GEStaff">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] has official backstory elements that, though not directly published for the public, are officially canon details that can be obtained verbally from cast members working in the land.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Chic and Sleek Kashyyyk Wookiee shampoo was first mentioned in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at Disneyland Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="GEStaff" />
