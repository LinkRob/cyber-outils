The '''Forbidden Acolytes''' were an organization mentioned by the self-crowned [[king]] of [[Cloud City]], [[Yathros Condorius the First]] when he was held back while attempting to confront [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], the [[Baron Administrator]] of the [[city]]. Condorius thought that the Forbidden Acolytes or the [[Invisible Cartel]] might have led Calrissian to surrender Cloud City to the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="The Man Who Built Cloud City">{{StoryCite|story=The Man Who Built Cloud City|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>
