'''Alistyr''' was a [[Silinese]] aide to the [[Silinese War Council]] who,<ref name="TCC" /> in [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> escorted the [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Master|Master]]s [[Elishe]] and [[Sol]], as well as their [[Padawan]]s [[Jecki Lon]] and [[Yord Fandar]], from a meeting with the War Council to [[Jedi's suite|their suite]]. Alistyr stayed behind after his fellow aides left to offer the Jedi refined information on the history of the [[Crowning Convocation]]. He also gave the guests a tour of their suite. Alistyr had small burnished, cream-yellow facial crystals similar to citrine.<ref name="TCC" />
