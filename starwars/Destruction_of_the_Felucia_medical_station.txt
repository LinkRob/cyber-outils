During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] '''destroyed''' [[Felucia Medical Station HCTFF2]], a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Haven-class medical station|''Haven''-class medical station]] that was [[orbit]]ing [[Felucia]].<ref name="BH" />

After the Republic lost contact with the medical station, they deployed [[Jedi General|General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Jedi Commander|Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] in a [[T-6 shuttle]] to investigate. However, they were detected by an [[automated vulture droid deployment station]] the Confederacy had left. Their [[shuttle]] was attacked by the [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]], leading to its crash and the [[Jedi]] becoming involved in a [[Skirmish on Felucia|skirmish]] against [[Captain]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s [[pirate]] [[Ohnaka Gang|gang]].<ref name="BH" />
