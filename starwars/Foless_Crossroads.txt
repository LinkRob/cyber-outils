The '''Foless Crossroads''' was the name given<ref name="Where" /> to the junction of the [[Shipwrights' Trace]] and [[Corellian Trade Spine]] [[hyperspace route]]s.<ref name="Junctions" /> The [[Foless system]] in the [[Colonies]] was located at the junction of the two routes,<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref>  giving the crossroads its name.<ref name="Where" />
