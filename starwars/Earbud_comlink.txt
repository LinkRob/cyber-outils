'''Earbud comlinks''',<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> also called '''ear comlinks''',<ref name="HH" /> '''In-Ear Comlinks'''<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> or '''earpieces''',<ref name="MandalorePlot">{{TCW|The Mandalore Plot}}</ref> were small [[comlink]]s that could fit within a user's [[ear]]. They could be activated by touching an ear<ref name="HH" /> or by clicking one's [[Tooth|teeth]]. Once activated, earbud comlinks allowed their user to talk normally or subvocally. They were powered by low-output [[power cell]]s and had significantly less range than normal comlinks<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> and could work in tandem with another comlink like at the wrist<ref name="MandalorePlot" /> or a display in a [[starship]] [[cockpit]].<ref name="AiWS">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Nest]]''</ref> Earbud comlinks costed 75 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and were popular among [[Spy|spies]], [[ambassador]]s, and the criminal underground.<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" />

==History==
[[File:Ear comlink Satine - TheManalorePlot.png|thumb|left|An earpiece in the ear of Satine Kryze while on Concordia|150x150px]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] used an earpiece to secretly communicate with his colleague [[Jar Jar Binks]] during their [[mission to Toydaria]] and notiy him when to start a distraction.<ref name="Supply">{{TCW|Supply Lines}}</ref> The [[bounty hunter]] [[Cato Parasitti]] used one to maintain contact with her accomplice, [[Cad Bane]], while undercover during their [[Raid on the Jedi Temple|heist]] at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]].<ref name="HH" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] gave [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] an earpiece to communicate with him while he investigated the [[Concordian mines]] and she stalled the [[Governor]] [[Pre Vizsla]].<ref name="MandalorePlot" /> While undercover as the bounty hunter [[Rako Hardeen]] at the [[Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center]], Kenobi used an earpiece in tandem with a comlink which had been hidden under a training bench to covertly contact Masters [[Mace Windu]] and [[Yoda]] and inform them about his mission status.<ref name="Deception">{{TCW|Deception}}</ref>

In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''Adventures in Wild Space: The Nest'' takes place in between ''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Escape]]'' and ''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Steal]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[18 BBY]].</ref> [[Milo Graf]] used an earpiece in conjunction with the controls of [[Graf family|his family]]'s [[Mu-class shuttle|''Mu''-class shuttle]] the ''[[Whisper Bird]]'' to pick up a transmission that was being broadcasted from the nearby [[planet]] [[Ikari]].<ref name="AiWS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Earbud comlinks first appeared in "[[Holocron Heist]]", the premier episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="HH" /> released on [[October 2]], [[2009]].<ref name="HH EG old">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode201.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Holocron Heist}}</ref> They were later identified as earpieces in the episode "[[The Mandalore Plot]]",<ref name="MandalorePlot" /> In-Ear Comlinks in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'',<ref name="VE" /> and earbud comlinks in the [[2018]] ''[[Cyphers and Masks]]''<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Accept Your Mission">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/8/16/accept-your-mission|text=Accept Your Mission}}</ref> The latter name had first been used in the [[2010]] ''[[Galaxy of Intrigue]]'' hardcover supplement to the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game Saga Edition Core Rulebook]]'' by [[Wizards of the Coast]].<ref name="GOI">''[[Galaxy of Intrigue]]''</ref>
