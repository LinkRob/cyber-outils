The '''abandoned tapcaf''' was a [[Tapcafe/Legends|tapcafe]] establishment that had been ruined by a [[Fire/Legends|fire]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] in [[44 ABY]]. [[Wraith Squadron]] re-appropriated it for use as a safe house under the guise of renovating the facility. They did some restoration on the building.
