This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]]. [[Clave Rollek]] was a member of this species.<ref name="A Jedi or a Pirate" />

==Biology and appearance==
This [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] was distinguished by their [[Color|blue]], scaly skin, that featuring a glittery sheen, as well as their white [[Hair|hair]].<ref name="A Jedi or a Pirate" />

==Species in the galaxy==
One member of this species, [[Clave Rollek]], had taken up residence on the [[planet]] [[Yarrum]], operating as a [[pirate]] in and around [[Yarrum Tower]]<ref name="A Jedi or a Pirate" /> by [[232 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|201B}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="A Jedi or a Pirate" />

==Behind the scenes==
The species first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'' [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|Season Two]] episode "[[A Jedi or a Pirate]]", which premiered on [[August 14]], [[2024]].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/young-jedi-adventures-season-2-and-nubs-shorts|text=''Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures'' Returns for Season 2}}</ref>
