The '''AT-AT Racer''' was a bodily configuration owned by the [[droideka]] [[Team Slingshot droideka|member]] of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]] that she could swap for her standard [[Beloved Paintwork|frame]] during the the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. This configuration vaguely resembled the appearance of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT]], with [[Water|snowy]], [[Armor|armored]] plating colored in varying shades of [[Color|gray]]. Its [[Limb|feet]] also resembled an AT-AT's, with large, custom blasters, a glowing red visor, and glowing red and white components on both the [[sensor]]s and seat.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
AT-AT Racer was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Air Taxi Racer]].<ref name="Hunters" />
