'''Ace Landers''' is a children's author. He wrote ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Darth Maul's Mission]]'', ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Anakin to the Rescue!]]'' and ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Vader Chronicles]]'' and ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Save the Galaxy!]]'' He is also the author of [[LEGO Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (Scholastic)|''LEGO Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith'']] and ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Tales of the Rebellion]]''.
