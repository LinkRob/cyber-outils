The '''''Chronicles of Brus-bu''''' were one of the ancient [[sacred Jedi texts]] that detailed an alternate usage of Force healing that could heal a damaged [[kyber crystal]]. [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] used the technique to heal the crystal of [[Skywalker lightsaber|Anakin Skywalker's lightsaber]], which had been cracked during the [[Battle of Crait]].<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
