The '''Army of the Dead''' was a force of [[Nightsister Zombie]]s on [[Dathomir]] who answered the call of the [[Nightsisters]] to fight against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the Separatist [[Battle of Dathomir|invasion]] of the [[planet]], the Nightsisters' [[homeworld]].

==History==
The army was summoned by [[Daka]] during the [[Battle of Dathomir]] to combat [[Grievous]] and his [[Separatist Droid Army|forces]] from wiping out the [[Nightsisters]]. The tide was turning in the favor of the Nightsisters until Grievous stormed the hidden [[cave]] and murdered Daka, causing the undead army to die and the remaining witches being outnumbered. The Nightsisters were massacred, and the village was left in ruins.<ref name="Massacre" />
