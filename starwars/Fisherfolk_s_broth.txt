'''Fisherfolk's broth''' was a type of [[broth]] sold in [[Kijimi City]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Kijimi]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the broth was sold for seventy five [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] at the [[Koori]] [[Foodstuff|food]] stall in [[Kijimiko Square]] in Kijimi City. It was also used as an ingredient for [[Duradan Soup]] which was also sold in Kijimiko Square.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 ABY, the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] and her [[Merqaal]] companion [[Nix]] share a meal of Duradan Soup made with Fisherfolk's broth in Kijimiko Square.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fisherfolk's broth was first mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. It was mentioned as part of the description of Duradan Soup as well as on a sign.<ref name="Outlaws" />
