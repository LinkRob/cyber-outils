The '''Chief of the Imperial Navy''',<ref name="AEYNTK" /> also known as the '''head of naval operations''',<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|admiral-motti|Admiral Motti}}</ref> was one of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Joint Chiefs of the Imperial Military|Joint Chiefs]] in the [[Imperial Navy]]. [[Admiral]] [[Conan Antonio Motti]] held the position until his [[death]] at the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="AEYNTK" /> Additionally, the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star I's]] [[Death Star's Imperial Navy garrison|Imperial Navy garrison]] was led by Chief Motti.<ref name="Inside Intel">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-inside-intel-the-death-star|text=''Star Wars'' Inside Intel: The Death Star|int=Star Wars Inside Intel}}</ref>
