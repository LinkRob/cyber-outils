{{Quote|You see the yard on the way in? You shouldn't have; we went through a lot of trouble to hide it. Anyway, we've refitted ships for Hutts, pirates, Rebels, you name it, with the Empire none the wiser.|Lira Hardin, on the shipyard|<ref name="Friends" />}}
Established by [[Lira Hardin]] and her team of [[engineer]]s above the [[planet]] [[Xorrn]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Ferra sector]], the '''Ferra Sector Shipyard''' was a small, 800-[[meter]]-long repair slip concealed in Xorrn's [[orbit]]. Although its name was perhaps overstated, it kept the [[Galactic Empire]] looking in other locations for a much larger facility. The yard was a pillar of webbed metalwork hidden along the exposed underside of an [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class transgalactic military assault ship]] left over from the [[Clone Wars]], with a 100-meter-long armature that slid along its entire length.<ref name="Friends" />

The primary function of the shipyard was to repair starships that were unable to utilize ports under Imperial control or oversight. However, it was also responsible for refitting [[bulk freighter]]s and haulers with [[armor]], weaponry, and increased [[hangar]] space to transform cargo ships into combat vessels for usage by [[Mercenary|mercenaries]], [[pirate]]s, and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. The engineer team accomplished this through judicious usage of shipyard [[droid]]s. Together, they mounted weaponry produced in [[Foundry Four]] on the surface and convert cargo bays into [[hangar]]s.<ref name="Friends" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ferra Sector Shipyard was introduced in the [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] adventure book ''[[Friends Like These]]'' as part of the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]].<ref name="Friends" />
