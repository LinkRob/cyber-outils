The '''Dandoran Campaign''' was a military campaign of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Following the [[Battle for Tatooine]], [[Alliance High Command]] sought to understand why the [[Galactic Empire]] was interested in the [[planet]] [[Dandoran]]. [[Colonel]] [[Jennica Pierce]] was tasked with investigated, and as such dispatched her [[Tatooine rebel cell|rebel forces]] to disrupt the Imperial operations. The campaign began with the destruction of multiple Imperial [[garrison]]s on the western continent, but eventually the conflict escalated. During the campaign, Imperial forces occasionally attacked rebel forces on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="SWC" />
