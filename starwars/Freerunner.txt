The '''Freerunner''' was a heavily [[armor]]ed, modular [[repulsorcraft]] with rotating gun platforms manufactured by [[Kelliak]]. Although its open-air cockpit limited environmental deployments, it saw use in the [[Alliance Army|ground forces]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> With its resilience and firepower, the Freerunner was the strongest armored vehicle in the Alliance's arsenal.<ref name="GD">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''</ref>
