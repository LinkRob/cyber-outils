The '''Empire Saber Pattern''' was a custom [[lightsaber]] [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]] owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Rieve]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Sith Saber Pattern|standard lightsaber]]. Unlike most of her hilts, this one was shorter, featuring minimal flair.<ref name="SWH" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Empire Saber Pattern was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Rieve's Sith Saber Pattern, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Imperial Apprentice]]. This wrap could be unlocked by purchasing it individually within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="SWH" />
