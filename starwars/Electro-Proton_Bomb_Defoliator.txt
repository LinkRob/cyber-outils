The '''Electro-Proton Bomb/Defoliator''' was a [[bomb]] rumored to be under development by the [[Imperial Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research]] led by [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was based off of the [[electro-proton bomb]] used by the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]], and was combined with technologies found in the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[defoliator]]. [[General]] [[Airen Cracken]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] believed it to be highly dangerous, knowing that Krennic employed ex-Separatist [[scientist]]s in his agency.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
