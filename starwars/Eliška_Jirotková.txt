'''Eliška Jirotková''' is a student and [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice actor]] who worked on [[Wikipedia:Czech language|Czech]] [[Wikipedia:Dubbing|dubs]] of several ''[[Star Wars]]'' series. Jirotková voiced additional roles for two episodes of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|second volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' non-[[canon]] animated series, and [[Master Thief|master thief]] [[Ace Kallisto]], [[miner]] [[Ayva Ackler]], and the [[Mirialan]] [[Pak Relda]] in the ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'' animated series. In live-action, the voice actor voiced [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]] in the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''.

==Biography==
[[File:Visions 2 poster.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Eliška Jirotková voiced additional roles in the Czech dub of ''Visions'' Volume 2.]]
Eliška Jirotková was born between [[July]] and [[August]] of [[2006]]<ref name="Age" /> and is a student at the [[Wikipedia:Prague Conservatory|Prague Conservatory]]<ref name="Bio" /> and [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice actor]]<ref name="LF" /> who voiced additional roles in the [[Wikipedia:Czech language|Czech]] [[Wikipedia:Dubbing|dub]] of [[Studio Mir Co., Ltd.|Studio Mir's]] "[[Journey to the Dark Head]]"<ref name="JDH">{{Visions|Journey to the Dark Head}}</ref> and [[Studio La Cachette]]'s "[[The Spy Dancer]],"<ref name="TSD">{{Visions|The Spy Dancer}}</ref> the fifth and sixth episodes of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|second volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' non-[[canon]] animated anthology television series, respectively. Both episodes premiered on [[May 4]], [[2023]]<ref name="Visions">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-2|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Release Date, Studios Revealed}}</ref> and the Czech dub was produced by [[Studio Virtual]].<ref name="JDH" /><ref name="TSD" />

Jirotková then moved from ''Visions'' to the localization of the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'' animated series for pre- and grade schoolers, starting with voicing [[Master Thief|master thief]] [[Ace Kallisto]] in "[[The Jedi and the Thief]]," the first part of the [[The Jedi and the Thief / The Missing Kibbin|seventh episode]],<ref name="TJTT" /> which also aired on May 4, 2023.<ref name="D23 May">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-may-2023|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in May 2023}}</ref> Kallisto was given Jirotková's voice again in "[[The Thieves of Tharnaka]]," the second part of the season's [[Off the Rails / The Thieves of Tharnaka|twelfth episode]],<ref name="TTT">{{YJA|The Thieves of Tharnaka}}</ref> which premiered on [[August 2]], 2023.<ref name="More YJA Episodes">{{ThisWeek|aYGTZacheog|A Look at Ahsoka, More Young Jedi Adventures Episodes, and More!}}</ref> In addition to Kallisto, the voice actor also dubbed [[miner]] [[Ayva Ackler]] in "[[Mystery of the Opal Cave]]," the first part of the [[Mystery of the Opal Cave / Clash|fifteenth episode]] of the series,<ref name="MOC">{{YJA|Mystery of the Opal Cave}}</ref> which aired on [[November 8]], 2023.<ref name="More YJA Episodes2">{{DisneyPlus|subdomain=press|url=news/next-on-disney-plus-november-2023|text=Next on Disney+: November 2023}}</ref> Jirotková went back to to voicing Ackler in [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|season two's]] [[Mine and Ours/The Andraven Circuit|seventh episode's]] first part "[[Mine and Ours]]."<ref name="MAO">{{YJA|Mine and Ours}}</ref> In the second season, Jirotková also voiced the [[Mirialan]] [[Pak Relda]] in the [[The Wild Aklyrr/Lys' Lost Lightsaber|tenth episode's]] second part "[[Lys' Lost Lightsaber]]."<ref name="LLL">{{YJA|Lys' Lost Lightsaber}}</ref> Both episodes aired on [[August 14]], [[2024]]<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/young-jedi-adventures-season-2-and-nubs-shorts|text=''Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures'' Returns for Season 2}}</ref> and the Czech dub of all five of the episodes was produced by the production company [[Iyuno, Inc.|Iyuno]].<ref name="TJTT" /><ref name="TTT" /><ref name="MOC" /><ref name="MAO" /><ref name="LLL" /> When the second half of the season aired on [[March 19]], [[2025]],<ref name="Season 2 Resume">{{SW|url=news/young-jedi-adventures-season-2-and-nubs-shorts|text=''Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures'' Completes Season 2 This March - New Images Revealed}}</ref> Jirotková voiced Kallisto in both the [[Home Sweet Temple|second part]] of the [[Tenoo's Fastest / Home Sweet Temple|fifteenth episode]]&mdash;with the character credited as Eso&mdash;<ref name="HST">{{YJA|Home Sweet Temple}}</ref> and the [[The Bounty Hunter and the Thief|second part]] of the [[A Mission to Remember / The Bounty Hunter and the Thief|twenty-second episode]].<ref name="BHT">{{YJA|The Bounty Hunter and the Thief}}</ref> Iyuno agained provided the episode's dubs.<ref name="HST" /><ref name="BHT" />

After the first season of ''Young Jedi Adventures'', Jirotková worked on live action, providing the voice of [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]]&mdash;portrayed by actor [[Dafne Keen]]&mdash;in the Czech dub of the live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', again produced by Studio Virtual. The voice actor worked on the series<ref name="LF" /><ref name="RJ" /><ref name="Day" /><ref name="Night" /> with other family members&mdash;Jirotková's sisters<ref name="Bio2">{{Instagram|username=segry_jirotkovy|name=Eliška, Terka, Anďa a Jolanka|archivefile=File:SegryJirotkovyBio.png}}</ref> [[Anděla Jirotková|Anděla]] and [[Jolana Jirotková]] also had dubbing roles in the series. Jirotková voiced the character in "[[Lost / Found]]"<ref name="LF" /> and "[[Revenge / Justice]],"<ref name="RJ">{{Acolyte|Revenge / Justice}}</ref> the first and second episode of the series that both aired on [[June 4]], 2024.<ref name="LF EPG">{{SW|url=series/the-acolyte/lost-found-episode-guide|text="Lost / Found" Episode Guide &#124; The Acolyte}}</ref><ref name="RJ EPG">{{SW|url=series/the-acolyte/revenge-justice-episode-guide|text="Revenge / Justice" Episode Guide &#124; The Acolyte}}</ref> Jirotková additionally voiced the character in "[[Day (episode)|Day]]"<ref name="Day">{{Acolyte|Day (episode)}}</ref> and "[[Night]],"<ref name="Night">{{Acolyte|Night}}</ref> the fourth<ref name="Day EPG">{{SW|url=series/the-acolyte/season-1-episode-4-day-episode-guide|text="Day" Episode Guide &#124; The Acolyte}}</ref> and fifth<ref name="Night EPG">{{SW|url=series/the-acolyte/season-1-episode-5-night-episode-guide|text="Night" Episode Guide &#124; The Acolyte}}</ref> episodes of the series that aired on [[June 18]]<ref name="Day EPG" /> and [[June 25]] of the same year, respectively.<ref name="Night EPG" />
