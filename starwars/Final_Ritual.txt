The '''Final Ritual''' was a concept of the [[Khepi]] faith related to their decision to construct a [[Khepi Tomb|Great Tomb]] hidden in the [[Sayma Nebula]] rather than evacuate their [[Khepi (planet)|homeworld]] when they learned of its impending [[Destruction of Khepi|destruction]]. The Final Ritual was referenced by [[Lor San Tekka]] in the [[holobook]] "[[History of the Khepi]]" which was published in [[20 BBY]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
