'''Fedoras''' were a type of hat,<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> with a wide-brim.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|bulduga|Bulduga}}</ref> The [[Ithorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Bulduga]] fashioned himself with one when he went to [[Serenno]] to attend a [[Tournament on Serenno|tournament]] inside [[Moralo Eval]]'s [[Box]], hosted at [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[Castle Serenno|castle]]. However, his fedora caught the [[eye]]s of [[Cad Bane]], who admired the headgear. Bulduga tried to draw his [[blaster]] but proved too slow and was outgunned by the [[Duros]] bounty hunter and was [[Death|killed]]. Bane subsequently claimed Bulduga's fedora as his own.<ref name="TB">{{TCW|The Box}}</ref>

In "[[Friends and Enemies]]," the sixteenth episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'s}} [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|fourth season]], Cad Bane was looking for a new hat. He briefly considered Indiana Jones' iconic fedora.<ref name="Friends and Enemies">{{TCW|Friends and Enemies}}</ref>
