'''Chess''' was a variation of [[Shah-tezh]], a strategy [[board game]] that was created eons before<ref name="Empire's End" /> [[20 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> Due to its origins, chess shared core mechanisms with other Shah-tezh variants such as [[dejarik]] and [[moebius]]. The [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] mentioned chess during a conversation with one of his servants, [[Gallius Rax]].<ref name="Empire's End" />
