'''''5-Minute Star Wars Stories Strike Back''''' is a collection of twelve young-readers ''[[Star Wars]]'' stories. It was published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[December 15]], [[2017]], as a follow-up to ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories]]''.

==Publisher's summary==
''A brand new collection of twelve action-packed retellings that span the entire Star Wars saga&mdash;including two tales from Star Wars: The Last Jedi! These exciting stories can each be read in just five minutes&mdash;perfect for galactic adventures at lightspeed!''

==Contents==
''5-Minute Star Wars Stories Strike Back'' is a collection of twelve stories adapting parts of all eight films of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga released at the time, spanning from [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']] to [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="5-Minute Star Wars Stories Strike Back">''5-Minute Star Wars Stories Strike Back''</ref> Several of the stories featured art by [[Pilot Studio]] that was previously published in the [[World of Reading]] young readers ''[[Trapped in the Death Star!]]'',<ref name="Trapped in the Death Star">''[[Trapped in the Death Star!]]''</ref> ''[[Captured on Cloud City]]'',<ref name="Captured on Cloud City">''[[Captured on Cloud City]]''</ref> [[Finn and Poe Team Up! (book)|''Finn and Poe Team Up!'']]<ref name="Finn and Poe Team Up!">[[Finn and Poe Team Up! (book)|''Finn and Poe Team Up!'']]</ref> and [[The Fight in the Forest (book)|''The Fight in the Forest'']]<ref name="The Fight in the Forest">[[The Fight in the Forest (book)|''The Fight in the Forest'']]</ref> accompanied by new retellings of the events written for older children.<ref name="5-Minute Star Wars Stories Strike Back" />
