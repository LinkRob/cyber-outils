The '''Feriae Junction''' was a celestial body<ref name="Aphra 6" /> located in the [[Thesme sector]]'s<ref name="Systems" /> [[Junction system (Outer Rim Territories)|Junction system]]<ref name="Fry" /> within an intersecting portion of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> Situated at the coordinates O-5 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it lay along the [[Hydian Way]]<ref name="TFABG" /> [[super-hyperroute]],<ref name="BMF 48">{{BuildFalconCite|48|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> which connected it to the [[Toprawa system|Toprawa]] and [[Celanon system]]s,<ref name="TFABG" /> and had its own [[Star|sun]].<ref name="Aphra 6" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 6|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 6]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> in retaliation for the [[D'Armin Syndicate]]'s [[Attack on a Tagge Corporation supply ship|attack]] on a [[Tagge Corporation]] [[supply ship]], [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]] gave orders for a [[battleship]] to cut the syndicate's forces off at the Feriae Junction and disable their [[engine]]s and [[weapon]]s before pushing them into the sun after a [[Standard day|day]].<ref name="Aphra 6" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Rferiae.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Depiction of Feriae Junction in ''Star Wars'' Legends]]
The Feriae Junction was introduced to the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] via mention in a flashback sequence of the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 6|sixth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series, written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 6" /> on [[November 25]] of that year. The mention of the celestial body in the issue was included in its preview on [[StarWars.com]] a [[November 18|week before]].<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-doctor-aphra-6-exclusive-preview|text=There's a New Bounty in Marvel's ''Doctor Aphra'' #6 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref> The [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Feriae Junction/Legends|Feriae Junction]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity,<ref name="WotC">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=swminis/article/huntwithin4|text=The Hunt Within: Valance's Tale: Part 4|int=The Hunt Within: Valance's Tale: Part 4}}</ref> where it first appeared in the [[Star Wars (1977) 27|twenty-seventh issue]] of the [[1977]] [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'']] comic series, which was written by [[Archie Goodwin]], illustrated by [[Carmine Infantino]], and published by Marvel Comics<ref name="Star Wars 27">[[Star Wars (1977) 27|''Star Wars'' (1977) 27]]</ref> on [[June 26]], [[1979]].<ref name="Marvel Star Wars 27">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/53817/star_wars_1977_27|text=Star Wars (1977) #27}}</ref>

While the 1979 comic identified the planet as "Junction,"<ref name="Star Wars 27" /> it was later given the full name "Feriae Junction" in "[[The Hunt Within: Valance's Tale: Part 4]]," a [[Wizards.com]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] article containing "Mission 4: The Doom of Ultaar," a scenario for use with the [[Wizards of the Coast]] [[Star Wars Miniatures|''Star Wars'' Miniatures]] game. The article was written by [[Jason Fry]] and published on [[January 7]], [[2005]].<ref name="WotC" /> The [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' boxed set [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]] introduced a mapping of the "[[Junction system (Outer Rim Territories)|Junction system]]" into the current canon.<ref name="TFABG" /> No connection had been made between Feriae Junction and the Junction system in canon until an email by Fry that specified locations originating in Legends continuity could be considered to be in systems of the same name as they had in Legends.<ref name="Fry" />
