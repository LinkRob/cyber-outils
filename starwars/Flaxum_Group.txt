The '''Flaxum Group''' was an organization that operated in [[the galaxy]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> during [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The group backed the [[Quarren]] [[Ultus]] in the [[Okala Five]] [[Kessel Sabacc]] tournament that was held aboard the [[casino]] [[cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''<ref name="Outlaws" /> in 3 ABY.<ref name="OutlawsDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Flaxum Group was first mentioned in the "Wild Card" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[November 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Wild Card released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/1Q4y5lMJwdUUt6qXzbWKPI/star-wars-outlaws-game-update-showcase-wrapup|text=Star Wars Outlaws Game Update Showcase Wrap-Up}}</ref>
