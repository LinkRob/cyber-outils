'''Freerock''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Expansion Region]] and [[the Interior]] region, situated within [[Wild Space]] near the [[Unknown Regions]]. Formerly nameless, it was home to an [[Unidentified outpost|abandoned monastery–turned–outpost]]. Four [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Jakku]] in [[5 ABY]], where the [[Galactic Empire]] had suffered a final defeat, Freerock was a haven for [[refugee]]s of the battle and ex-Imperials, including the former [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]] [[Bansu Ro]].

[[Nath Tensent]]&mdash;an ex&ndash;[[New Republic]] [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] and ex&ndash;[[TIE series|TIE]] [[pilot]] before that&mdash;went to Freerock and found Ro at the [[Rim's Edge]] [[cantina]], offering the ex-Imperial a place in his new [[Nath Tensent's crew|crew]]. At the same time, [[Wyl Lark]], who had served with Tensent in the New Republic, found his old [[Alphabet Squadron|squadronmate]] and offered for him to return to service with the [[Politics|government]]. Tensent, feeling he had no place in the New Republic, refused to return, and the pair politely parted ways. Between [[34 ABY|34]] and [[35 ABY]], the [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] visited the Rim's Edge on Freerock and tried its [[Rim's Edge (beverage)|signature drink]], going on to make an entry about it in his [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]''.

==Description==

Freerock was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> situated in an [[Freerock system|eponymous star system]]<ref name="Systems" /> the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Galaxy" /> and [[the Interior]] region<ref name="Interior" /> at the coordinates H-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Galaxy" /> It was located within the reaches of [[Wild Space]],<ref name="Systems" /> near the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==History==
{{Quote|Got to say, I'm real curious about how you found me.''"<br />"''It's not important. It's good to see you.|Nath Tensent and Wyl Lark reunite|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], when Freerock was a nameless planet,<ref name="Victory's Price" /> the corrupt Imperial [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]]<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> [[Nath Tensent]] visited [[Rim's Edge]] [[cantina]] at Freerock's [[Unidentified outpost|outpost]], a former monastery.<ref name="Victory's Price" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the Empire suffered a final defeat at the hands of the [[New Republic]] in the [[Battle of Jakku]], in which Tensent served as a [[New Republic pilot]] and fought against the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. Four [[Standard month|months]] after the battle, Freerock had since earned its name and was a haven for [[refugee]]s from the planet [[Jakku]],<ref name="Victory's Price" /> with former New Republic and Imperial personnel sharing war stories at the Rim's Edge.<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref> [[Pirate]]s, explorers, fortune seekers,<ref name="Victory's Price" /> and [[smuggler]]s were similarly found at the cantina, some scheming plan with one another.<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" /> One of the ex-Imperials there was [[Bansu Ro]], who had served as a [[TIE series|TIE]] [[pilot]] in the 204th.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

[[File:BansuRo-TIEFighter4.png|thumb|left|250px|Nath Tensent tracked Bansu Ro (pictured) down on Freerock.]]
After those four months following Jakku, Tensent, having retired from service in the New Republic, returned to Freerock in search of Ro, having recruited two other veterans from the 204th, [[Creet]] and [[Nord Kandende]], for his new [[Nath Tensent's crew|freelancing crew]]. The ex&ndash;New Republic pilot found and approached his quarry at the Rim's Edge cantina, where Ro was initially poised to draw his [[vibroknife]] on his visitor. Tensent then gave his pitch to the ex-Imperial, bringing up the recruitment of Creet and Kandende as an incentive. Meanwhile, [[Wyl Lark]], who had served with Tensent in the New Republic, had tracked down his former [[Alphabet Squadron|squadronmate]] to Freerock.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

[[T5]], Tensent's [[astromech droid]], interrupted Tensent and Ro to inform the former about Lark, and Tensent offered to speak with the ex-Imperial at the establishment [[Roderick's]] in a few [[Standard hour|hours]] if the latter was interested in the offer to join Tensent's crew. Tensent spoke with Lark at a decaying cloister on the other side of the outpost, where the latter showed concern that Tensent was returning to the criminal life that he had led while he served the Empire. Tensent tried to justify his decisions and the two offered each other places in their respective groups. Following an exchange of polite words, they parted ways.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

At some point between [[34 ABY|34]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UCDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] once visited the Rim's Edge cantina on Freerock, finding the eclectic atmosphere there similar to that to his former workplace, [[Takodana Castle|Maz's castle]] on the<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" /> planet<ref name="Takodana DB">{{Databank|takodana|Takodana}}</ref> [[Takodana]]. The chef tried the cantina's signature drink of the [[Rim's Edge (beverage)|same name]], which took his mind back to Maz's castle. Tuggs wrote an entry for the drink in his [[Book|cookbook]], the ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', briefly describing his impression of Rim's Edge while he was on Freerock.<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" />

==Locations==
{{Quote|You think about what I said. Swing by Roderick's in a few hours if you want to talk more.|Nath Tensent, to Bansu Ro|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
Freerock was home to a monastery that was eventually abandoned, later becoming an outpost. During the [[New Republic Era|era]] of the New Republic, at least two establishments, the Rim's Edge cantina and Roderick's, were located in the outpost.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
Freerock appeared in ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the third and final novel in [[Alexander Freed]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy, which was published in [[2021]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
