'''Beep Boop Bites''' were a type of [[candy]]. They had an [[Color|orange]] wrapper with "Beep Boop BITES" written in [[High Galactic]] and an [[astromech droid]] on it.<ref name="Card Trader" />

==Behind the scenes==
Beep Boop Bites were featured in the [[2023]] [[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]] set ''Halloween 2023 - Classic''.<ref name="Card Trader" />
