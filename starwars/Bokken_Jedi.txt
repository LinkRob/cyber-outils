"'''Bokken Jedi'''" was a term used by the fallen [[Jedi]] [[Baylan Skoll]] to describe Jedi [[Padawan]]s who were taken on and [[Jedi training|trained]] after the [[Operation: Knightfall|fall]] of the [[Jedi Order]]. Skoll considered [[Ezra Bridger]] a Bokken Jedi. At the same [[time]],<ref name="Part Six" /> he<ref name="Baylan Skoll">{{Databank|baylan-skoll|Baylan Skoll}}</ref> regarded his own apprentice, [[Shin Hati]], to be more than a Jedi, thereby excluding<ref name="Part Six" /> her<ref name="Shin Hati">{{Databank|shin-hati|Shin Hati}}</ref> from the description.<ref name="Part Six" />
