'''Cathar''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Vader 10">[[Darth Vader (2017) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 10]]</ref> in the [[Cathar system]] in the [[Quelii sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> A prominent part of the culture of Cathar's natives was the [[Blood Hunt]], a coming-of-age ritual.<ref name="Beware">''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''</ref>

Cathar was the [[homeworld]] of [[Scape Loewig]], a [[Force-sensitive]] child whose name was included in [[aurebesh]] in a [[memory crystal]] recovered by [[Jedi]] [[Chief Librarian]] [[Jocasta Nu]] following the [[Order 66|destruction]] of the [[Jedi Order]]<ref name="Vader 10" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Shortly after joining the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] from the [[Imperial Academy]], the [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]] [[Yrica Quell]] participated in a patrol on the outskirts of Cathar with her new [[squadron]].<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> Around [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ShadowFallDate}}</ref> she was reminded of the patrol while flying through a [[Cerberon debris field|debris field]] in the [[Cerberon system]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> Cathar shared its name with the [[Cathar]] species, which included [[Dhina]], who served as an [[Ensign]] and [[Com-scan|comscan]] [[officer]] aboard the [[New Republic]] refitted [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]''<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> in 5 ABY.<ref name="Date 2">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref>
