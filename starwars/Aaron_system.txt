The '''Aaron system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Tharin sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was situated at the coordinates S-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Aaron system originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, and was recanonized when it was included in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

The Aaron system originates from a typo for [[Auren]], a moon of [[Ingo/Legends|Ingo]] that appears in the [[1988]] video game [[Star Wars: Droids (video game)|''Star Wars: Droids'']].<ref name="Droids Game">[[Star Wars: Droids (video game)|''Star Wars: Droids'' video game]]</ref> The moon was mistakenly known as Aaron within the fandom,<ref name="Atari Wars">{{InsiderCite|40|Atari Wars}}</ref> and ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' introduced an [[Aaron system/Legends|Aaron system]],<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> based on this typo.<ref name="Droids Re-Animated">{{SW|url=news/the-droids-re-animated-part-2|series=The Droids Re-Animated|text=Part 2|nolive=1}}</ref> Additionally, Auren from the ''Droids'' game is a moon of Ingo, meaning it would be in the [[Ingo system/Legends|Ingo system]].<ref name="Droids Game" />
