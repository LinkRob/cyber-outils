'''Endurance and Agility''', also known as '''E&A''', was an athletic course program at the [[Jelucan preparatory academy]], which prepared prospective [[Imperial cadet]]s for the [[Imperial Academy]]. It involved difficult obstacle courses that were held [[Standard month|monthly]]. Students who sustained injuries were removed from the program. [[Thane Kyrell]] and [[Ciena Ree]] attended one E&A obstacle course where Thane almost collapsed due to injuries he had sustained from a beating given by his [[Parent|father]] [[Oris Kyrell]] the previous night.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>
