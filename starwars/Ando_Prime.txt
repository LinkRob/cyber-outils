'''Ando Prime''' was an [[Water|ice]]-covered [[planet]]<ref name="Complete Locations" /> located in the [[Ando system]] in the [[Lambda sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was the site of the [[Ando Prime Centrum]] [[podracing]] course.<ref name="Complete Locations" /> The back of the [[PIP droid]] known simply as [[Pip]], the companion to the [[Meknek]] and former [[Jedi]] [[Verosha Aniseya]],<ref name="Revenge Justice">{{Acolyte|Revenge / Justice}}</ref> was detailed with a sticker of an Ando Prime podracing track during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Insider230">{{InsiderCite|230|Pip: The Making of the Star Wars Multitool|Pip: The Making of the ''Star Wars'' Multitool}}</ref> By [[132 BBY]],<ref name="132Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> the sticker had grown worn.<ref name="Insider230" />

In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="DateFilm">{{FilmDate|II}}</ref> shortly before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]], a race was broadcast live from on Ando Prime in the [[Outlander Club]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Complete Locations" /> Following the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] visited Ando Prime and several other worlds in an attempt to gain intelligence on the [[Galactic Empire]]'s attempts to shore up connections with criminal organizations it had previously supported. While on the worlds, Solo wore a disguise and asked around about Imperial activity, but failed to learn anything on Ando Prime.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>

[[File:AndoPrime-StarWarsGalacticRacer.jpg|left|200px|thumb|Racers racing repulsorcrafts across Ando Prime's snowy terrain.]]
During the [[New Republic Era|era]] of the [[New Republic]], races using [[skim speeder]]s, [[Landspeeder]]s, and [[Speeder bike]]s took place on the Ando Prime, with the racer [[pilot]] [[Shade]] participating in such races.<ref name="Reveal article">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galactic-racer-reveal-trailer|text=We're Going Back to Jakku in the Trailer for ''Star Wars: Galactic Racer'' - Exclusive Interview|archivedate=20251212045257}}</ref>
