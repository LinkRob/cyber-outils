The '''Dutiful Soldier''' was a set of armor worn by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Loyalist Trooper|standard costume]]. This [[armor]] featured unique design consisting of chrome [[Color|red]] and yellow lines.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dutiful Soldier was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Sentinel, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Dutiful E-Web]]. This wrap could be unlocked by purchasing it individually within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
