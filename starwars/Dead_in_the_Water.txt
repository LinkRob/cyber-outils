"'''Dead in the Water'''" is an adventure in [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] series. It is included in the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion Game Master's Kit|Game Master's Kit]], released on [[July 3]], [[2014]].

==Publisher's summary==
''What begins as a routine cargo run takes a drastic turn for the worse, and player characters will eventually find themselves fighting for their lives, as their ship careens out of control and into the whirling suction of a black hole.''

''Player characters must contend with overwhelming odds, and some surprising obstacles if they wish to survive–and make it back to the Alliance, cargo in tow. ''Dead in the Water'' provides a thrilling, death-defying adventure for both new players, and experienced roleplayers alike.''

==Plot summary==
A team of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] operatives has been assigned to the [[Frigate/Legends|frigate]] ''[[Shadow Raptor]]'' in order to take possession of a group of [[Droid/Legends|droids]], which were actually programmed by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. They take over the ship, which becomes disabled and trapped by a [[Black hole/Legends|black hole]]. Captain [[Hylo Sortuli]] orders the team to prevent vital intelligence from getting captured. They escape the ship and return to [[Port Tooga]] to uncover the [[Imperial Intelligence/Legends|Imperial Intelligence]] plot.
