'''Aris''' was a rustic<ref name="TROS VD" /> terrestrial<ref name="THR 9" /> [[planet]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> located in the [[Aris system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Albarrio sector]],<ref name="Appendix" /> along the<ref name="AVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Braxant Run]] [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="Braxant" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], Aris was one of the locations where the [[Unified Republic Task Force]] [[Battle on Aris|dealt]] with [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] in the aftermath of the [[Valo Atrocity|attack on Valo]].<ref name="THR 9" /> It was the [[homeworld]] of the [[human]] [[Resistance]] member [[Byrco Hansid]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[Aris/Legends|Aris]] originated in the [[1989]] roleplaying book ''[[Riders of the Maelstrom]]'',<ref name="Riders">''[[Riders of the Maelstrom]]''</ref> and was recanonized by its mention in the [[2019]] ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''.<ref name="TROS VD" /> Aris was located in the [[Albarrio system/Legends|Albarrio system]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity,<ref name="Riders" /> but the [[2025]] "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix introduced both an [[Albarrio system]] and an Aris system, both located in the Albarrio sector.<ref name="Appendix" />
