'''Ceela''' was a [[Color|pink]] fluid that was found on the [[Volcano/Legends|volcanic]] [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Vernost (planet)|Vernost]]. A "miracle-liquid," it was capable of helping [[Plant/Legends|plants]] grow at faster rates, [[Engine/Legends|engines]] run quicker, and prolonging [[Human/Legends|Human]] life to a potentially infinite amount of [[Time/Legends|time]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] battled over the liquid. Rebel [[X-wing starfighter/Legends|X-wing starfighters]] attempted to gather Ceela from the canyons of the planet as they were shot at by Imperial [[Turret/Legends|turrets]].<ref name="PopSci" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ceela was planned to appear in ''[[Vernost]]'', a canceled [[Star Wars video games|video game]] played on the "Mirage" flight simulator as a collaboration between [[LucasArts]] and Hughes Training. Prior to the cancellation of the game, information on the story was released in the "Hughes Training Premieres Star Wars Simulation" article in the [[1993#February|February 1993]] issue of the fifth volume of [[Wikipedia:Larry Flynt Publications|Larry Flynt Publications']] [[Wikipedia:VideoGames & Computer Entertainment|VideoGames & Computer Entertainment]] magazine, written by David S. Moskowitz.<ref name="VG&CE">{{BookCite|container=Hughes Training Premieres Star Wars Simulation |title=[[Wikipedia:VideoGames & Computer Entertainment|VideoGames & Computer Entertainment]]|contributor=Moskowitz, David S.|version=ed. 8|number=vol. 242|publisher=[[Wikipedia:Larry Flynt Publications|Larry Flynt Publications]]|date=[[1993#February|February 1993]]|issn=1059-2938|link=https://www.retromags.com/magazines/usa/video-games-and-computer-entertainment/videogames-computer-entertainment-issue-49/}}</ref> The name Ceela was provided in the "Virtual Reality Pod" article in the May 1993 issue of the 8th edition of [[Wikipedia:Bonnier Corporation|Bonnier Corporation's]] [[Wikipedia:Popular Science|Popular Science]] magazine, written by Richard A. Marini.<ref name="PopSci" />
