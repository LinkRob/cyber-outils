'''''Doctor Aphra'' 5''' is the fifth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. It was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Marika Cresta]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 28]], [[2020]]. It features the story "'''Fortune and Fate Part 5: Power Play'''," in which [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] and her team face [[Ronen Tagge]] in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]].

==Publisher's summary==
''POWER TO RULE THE GALAXY? RONEN TAGGE has the legendary RINGS OF VAALE. Will he destroy the priceless artifacts or fall to their temptation?. If they truly give him the power to rival the EMPEROR, what hope do APHRA and crew have to stop him?''

==Plot summary==
Inside [[Ronen Tagge]]'s [[Canto Bight]] penthouse, [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Doctor Aphra]] is stunned by his collection of rare artifacts. Tagge reveals his prize: the [[Rings of Vaale|Ring of Vaale]], now paired with its long-lost twin which his family has owned for generations. He explains that upon reading [[Eustacia Okka|Professor Okka]]'s research, he understood he possessed half of a legendary set and orchestrated the mission to unite them, intending to destroy both to create an epic ending for their saga.

Sensing an opportunity, Aphra taunts Ronen about living in his family's shadow, tempting him with the legends of the rings' power—eternal life and strength to rival the [[Emperor]]. She proposes a test: use the rings on Okka in his disintegration chamber. If Okka survives, the legends are true; if not, he still gets to destroy them. While Tagge agrees, the droid, [[TA-418]] discreetly slices into a nearby terminal.

Okka survives the test, convincing Tagge of the rings' immense power. "If they could make me more powerful than the emperor, they will make me a God," he declares before snatching the rings back. At that moment, Aphra kicks Tagge into the disintegration chamber and begins manipulating the controls. As Tagge's soldiers storm the room, Aphra's crew provides cover. After a moment of hesitation, Okka presses the final button, triggering a massive explosion that rips through the penthouse.

The blast is broadcast as breaking news, prompting [[Just Lucky]] to abandon her brother at the casino and rush to the scene. She finds the penthouse in ruins and orders her crew to intercept Aphra's team, who are already making their escape. Surveying the damage, Lucky mutters, "This is going to be a hell of a thing to explain to [[Domina Tagge|Domina]]."

Aboard their ship, Aphra attempts to make amends with Okka by revealing she stole enough artifacts to restore Okka's tenure at the [[Shadow University]]. Okka reveals she has her own souvenirs: extensive scans of Vaale's catacombs and a valuable item she took from the penthouse. They both acknowledge they are now prime targets for the Tagge dynasty.

The story concludes with two developments: on Canto Bight, Lucky discovers Ronen Tagge is alive but severely injured. Meanwhile, aboard her flagship, a furious Domina Tagge receives the news, sets a course for Canto Bight, and issues a chilling command: "Bring me all of your information on Doctor Aphra."
