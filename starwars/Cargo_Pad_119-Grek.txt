'''Cargo Pad 119-Grek''' was a cargo pad located on the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]], used by the [[Galactic Empire]] for the nearby [[Imperial Refinery]]. The cargo pad was [[Liberation on Kashyyyk|attacked]]<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> in [[14 BBY]]<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> by [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]] and Jedi survivor [[Cal Kestis]] in their attempt to liberate the [[Wookiee]] [[prisoner]]s at the refinery.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==History==
{{Quote|Who are you?''"<br />"''Someone making the Empire angry."|Cal Kestis and Saw Gerrera|<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date note /> Cargo Pad 119-Grek was attacked by the [[Partisans]] led by [[Saw Gerrera]] to disrupt [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] supply lines. During a mission to find [[Tarfful]], [[Jedi]] survivor [[Cal Kestis]] aided the Partisans in their attack. After having hijacked an [[All Terrain Armored Transport]], Cal Kestis advanced on Cargo pad 119-Grek and destroyed several Imperial vehicles. Cargo Pad 119-Grek was then taken over by the Partisans.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
