"'''C-3PO's New Arm'''" is a short story in the anthology ''[[Galactic Adventures Storybook Collection]]''. The story, written by [[Rebecca L. Schmidt]] and illustrated by [[TomatoFarm]], adapts the [[Marvel Comics]] one-shot [[Star Wars Special: C-3PO 1|''Star Wars Special: C-3PO'' 1]] by [[James Robinson]].
