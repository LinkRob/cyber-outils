'''Cortosis swords''' were rare swords made of [[cortosis]], an ore that could block or even disable [[lightsaber]] blades. The weapons existed in numerous styles and contained varying amounts of cortosis, but the practice of making them may have been<!--The source uses this wording. It is not speculation.--> as old as the practice of making lightsabers itself. The weapons cost 1,350 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" /> The [[Null Blade]] was a cortosis sword.<ref name="Aphra 25">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 25|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 25]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Cortosis swords were mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
