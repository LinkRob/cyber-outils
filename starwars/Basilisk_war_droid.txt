The '''Basilisk war droids''', also known as '''Mandalorian Basilisks''', were fearsome, heavily armored four-legged droids used by the [[Mandalorian crusaders|Mandalorian Crusaders]]. According to the [[Qel-Droma Epics]], at one point in time, the Mandalorian Crusaders served under the command of the fallen [[Jedi Knight]] [[Ulic Qel-Droma]] out of the [[Iron Citadel]] on [[Empress Teta]], and rode Basilisk war droids into combat.<ref name="Nexus of Power">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Basilisk war droid was first mentioned in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''Nexus of Power''.<ref name="Nexus of Power" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Basilisk war droid/Legends|Basilisk war droid]] first appeared in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War 1|''Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War'' 1]].<ref name="TOTJ1">[[Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War 1|''Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War'' 1]]</ref> It was a semi-sentient droid that the [[Mandalorian/Legends|Mandalorians]] used to successfully conquer many [[Planet/Legends|planets]] during their [[Mandalorian Crusades|crusades]].
