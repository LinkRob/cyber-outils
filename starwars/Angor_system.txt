The '''Angor system''' was located in the [[Myto sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> It was situated in grid square K-4 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was in close proximity to the Outer Rim [[planet]] [[Dantooine]] and the [[Sinsang system]].<ref name="BMF 37">{{BuildFalconCite|37|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Angor [[Star system|system]] was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" section of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 37|thirty-seventh issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 37" /> published around [[September 16]], [[2015]]<ref name="BMFdates">{{BMFdates|37}}</ref> and written by [[Tim Veekhoven]].<ref name="SM">{{DMCite|dm=File:Credits-TimVeekhoven-Twitter.png|creator=[[Tim Veekhoven]]}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Angor system/Legends|Angor system]] was introduced in [[The New Jedi Order: Vector Prime|''Vector Prime'']], a [[1999]] novel authored by [[R. A. Salvatore]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: The New Jedi Order]]'' series.<ref name="Vector Prime">''[[The New Jedi Order: Vector Prime]]''</ref>
