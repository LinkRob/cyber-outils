A '''centrifuge''',<ref name="Complete Locations" /> also known as a '''centrifuge machine''',<ref name="SW">{{SW|url=confined-trivia-gallery-star-wars-the-bad-batch|text="Confined" Trivia Gallery &#124; The Bad Batch|quote=The centrifuge machine in the Tantiss lab was designed to look like a clock face. A literal ticking clock...}} {{C|slide 9}}</ref> was a type of machine used to separate liquid.<ref name="Complete Locations" /> 

==History==
The [[XJ-6 airspeeder]] was equipped with afterburner centrifuges that channeled [[Atmosphere|air]] streams through them.<ref name="Complete Vehicles">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> 

The [[Klegger Corp Mining Facility]] processed liquid [[ore]]s in the form of [[Magma|lava]], by pouring them into large tanks and then sending them to a multi-level centrifuge for separation. The centrifuge would concentrate and extract [[metal]]s from the lava, which was sourced via extraction technology, and manually from [[DLC-13 mining droid|mining droids]] and [[Mustafarian]] workers. However, each source was treated separately.<ref name="Complete Locations" /> 

As part of her job at [[Tantiss Base]]'s [[Advanced Science Division]], [[Nala]] Se operated a centrifuge machine that spun containers containing [[blood]] samples.<ref name="Confined" /> 

[[Nova Blaster]], an [[alcohol]]ic drink served at [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]], was made from a combination of [[Corellian brandy]] and [[Ipellria firewater]] mixed together in a centrifuge.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
