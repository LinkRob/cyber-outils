{{Quote|What's a fractal radio network?''"<br />"''A highly encrypted&mdash;and therefore highly ''secure''&mdash;means of ''communication'' dependent on a certain kind of ''code crystal''.|Jar Jar Binks and Kelleran Beq|<ref name="Jar Jar 1">[[Jar Jar 1|''Jar Jar'' 1]]</ref>}}
A '''fractal radio''' was a specialized communications device that allowed for highly-encrypted communications. The simple device was easy to use but hard to trace.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|fractal-radio|fractal radio}}</ref> The rebel leader [[Luthen Rael]] used a [[Kleya Marki's fractal radio|fractal radio]] in his shop, the [[Galactic Antiquities and Objects of Interest]], to communicate with various rebel contacts. Rael's assistant [[Kleya Marki]] frequently monitored the radio.<ref name="ST" /> In addition to receiving transmissions from operatives, the radio could be used to monitor listening devices.<ref name="Friends">{{Andor|I Have Friends Everywhere}}</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|Secure, ''private'' communiqués will allow for the truth to be shared and the ''opposition'' to be organized. If that happens, then we have a means to always ''fight back''. We can stop the ''Separatists'' once and for all. One day, if necessary, such a network could have a ''galaxy-wide impact''.|Kelleran Beq|<ref name="Jar Jar 1" />}}
[[File:FractalRadio-SagronaTeema.jpg|thumb|left|240px|Kleya Marki's fractal radio.]]
Near the start of the [[Clone Wars]] around [[22 BBY]], a [[technician]] named [[Mira Bridger]] created a network of fractal radios, using a new type of mineral in the mines, which they named [[cyphristal]]s that could be used for highly encrypted and secure communication. Through the network was new, they soon learned that its very existence had upset powerful individuals, including [[Galactic Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], and that a two [[Bounty hunter|bounty hunters]] were hired to [[Death|kill]] her. After rescuing Bridger and suspecting Palpatine has greater plan, Binks asked [[Jedi Master]] [[Kelleran Beq]] to start forming a fractal radio network.<ref name="Jar Jar 1" />

In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="One Year Date">"[[One Year Later]]" dates the first three episodes of ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' [[Andor Season 2|Season 2]], including the [[Wedding of Stekan Sculdun and Leida Mothma]] on [[Chandrila]] in the same episode, to [[4 BBY]].</ref> Marki return to the gallery from [[Chandrila]] to use the fractal radio to listen for any communication from either [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] or [[Porko]] on the status of the [[TIE Avenger prototype|TIE Avenger]].<ref name="ST">{{Andor|Sagrona Teema}}</ref> Two days later, the fractal radio was hailed by Andor, who furiously stated that his rendezvous with Porko was botched nor could he fly the ship, and demanded a new drop-off location. Subsequently, he announced his intention to call home, but Marki forbade him, admitting that [[Mina-Rau]] was under a signal blockade, prompting Andor to cut contact to go and investigate.<ref name="Harvest">{{Andor|Harvest}}</ref>

In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Been to Ghorman Date">{{Andor|Ever Been to Ghorman?}} dates the second arc of [[Andor Season 2|''Andor'' Season 2]] to [[3 BBY]].</ref> Marki connected the radio to a listening device hidden in the [[Tinian Codex]] in [[Davo Sculdun]]'s [[Davo Sculdun's gallery|gallery]] and overheard a conversation between Sculdun and [[Mishko]], who had discovered the [[Coryio Chalice]] was a forgery, prompting a furious Sculdun to order a re-appraisal of his entire collection. Marki quickly disconnected the radio upon noticing a pair of customers outside the antiquities shop.<ref name="IHFE">{{Andor|I Have Friends Everywhere}}</ref>

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="DestructionDate">"[[Make It Stop]]" dates the fourth and final three episodes of ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' [[Andor Season 2|Season 2]], including [[Luthen Rael]]'s destruction of the Fractal Radio to [[1 BBY]].</ref> as [[ISB Supervisor]] [[Dedra Meero]] was about to expose him as Axis, Rael resorted to pouring acid over the fractal radio, effectively destroying any links to his contacts. Meero eventually noticed smoke coming from the back room, she called in her [[ISB Tactical]] team to save the potential evidence.<ref name="MIS">{{Andor|Make It Stop}}</ref> Later, an ISB technician examined what remained of the radio and commented on how it was piggybacking on Imperial signals for years. Upon confirming that the transmitter was still functional, Supervisor [[Avril Heert]] used it to trace Marki's [[Axis network safehouse|location]] while she was communicating with Andor.<ref name="WEK">{{Andor|Who Else Knows?}}</ref>
