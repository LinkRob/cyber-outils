'''Brazatta Key''' was one of the islands in the [[Windward Chain]] of the [[Gem Cities of Kwenn]] on the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]]. It contained an industrial zone and a [[spaceport]], and was home to a number of manufacturers that exported goods to the rest of Kwenn. Brazatta was also the home port of the [[casino]] [[sail barge]] ''[[Pelagic]]'', which traveled between the other islands of the Gem Cities. It was connected via bridges to the [[Langdam Key|Langdam]] and [[Vorah Key]]s.<ref name="The Living Force" />
