The '''''Dissident''-class light cruiser''' was a type of [[warship]] that served in the [[First Order Navy]] by around<ref name="Before" /> [[31 ABY]]. In that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">According to ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', [[Poe Dameron]] joined the [[Resistance]] three [[Standard year|years]] prior to the events of {{Film|VII}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Since Dameron's story in ''[[Before the Awakening]]'' depicts him joining the Resistance shortly after seeing the ''Dissident''-class [[Light cruiser|cruiser]], the story must take place around [[31 ABY]].</ref> [[New Republic]] [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] encountered a number of [[First Order]] [[starship]]s at a staging point in the [[OR-Kappa-2722]] [[Star system|system]], which included a ''Dissident''-class [[Light cruiser|cruiser]]. Dameron then briefly [[Skirmish in OR-Kappa-2722|engaged]] several First Order [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] before fleeing the system.<ref name="Before" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Dissident''-class light cruiser appeared in the [[2015]] [[canon]] novel ''[[Before the Awakening]]'', written by [[Greg Rucka]] as a prelude to [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="Before" />
