The '''Boltiga system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]]<ref name="Interior" /> that fell within grid square M-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It contained the terrestrial astronomical object [[Boltiga VII]], which served as a [[mining]] [[Colonist|colony]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. A [[Unidentified rebel spy (Ukio Bread Incident)|rebel spy]] convicted of five counts of high [[treason]] in the [[Ukio Bread Incident]] was sentenced to life imprisonment on Boltiga VII<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>
