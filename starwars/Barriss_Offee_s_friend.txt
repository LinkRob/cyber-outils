An '''old friend''' of the former [[Jedi]] [[Barriss Offee]] lived during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. After she was approached by a [[human]] [[Marriage|couple]] that were fleeing from the [[Inquisitorius]] &mdash; a guild of [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Dark side adept|adepts]] &mdash; because their [[Reproduction|son]] [[Micah]] was [[Force-sensitive]], Barriss sent the family to meet with her friend, believing that she would understand how best to proceed. While Offee remained behind to hinder the [[Fourth Sister]], her two attendants were able to reach her [[starship]] and escape with Micah and his parents, fleeing into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="TWO" />
