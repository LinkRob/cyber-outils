'''Cynra Basho''' was a [[Rutian]] [[Twi'lek]] [[merchant]] who operated a [[Cynra Basho's fabric shop|fabric shop]] in the [[South Stretch Worker's District|Worker's District]] of the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. During the [[Canto Cup]] [[fathier]] race in the [[city]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Basho opened the store and had a vial of [[bacta]] available for purchase. The [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] noted with surprise that Basho's store was open during the cup. The Twi'lek had [[Color|blue]] skin and purple [[eye]]s and wore a brown outfit during the race including a [[Clothing|shirt]], [[belt]], headwrap, pants, and [[boot]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cynra Basho appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
