'''Anakin Skywalker's Podracer''' was a [[Podracer]] custom-built by a young [[Anakin Skywalker]] as a [[Slavery|slave]] on [[Tatooine]]. It was used to secure his freedom and the replacement [[hyperdrive]] components for [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Amidala's]] [[Naboo Royal Starship|ship]] during the [[Boonta Eve Classic]].<ref name="Episode I" />

==Characteristics==
The blue-and-silver [[Podracer]], built by [[Anakin Skywalker]], was equipped with a custom specification pair of [[620C racing engine]]s made by [[Radon-Ulzer]] [[Repulsorlift]].<ref name="Ultimate SW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Skywalker's racer followed the basic design of other [[podracing]] machines, with two high-powered engines hauling a small [[cockpit]] behind them.<ref name="Databank" /> The engines were linked by an [[energy binder]] and connected to the cockpit with [[control cable]]s manufactured by [[Steelton]].<ref name="AEYNTK" /> The vehicle was smaller and slimmer than other Podracers. The cockpit's total length was 3.15 [[meter]]s and the engines were 7 meters in length.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|anakin-skywalkers-podracer|Anakin Skywalker's Podracer}}</ref> While the engines were originally waste items discarded by [[Watto]], Skywalker developed a new type of [[fuel]] spraying device and a system that efficiently fed fuel to the fuel chamber which greatly improved the thrust.<ref name="Ultimate SW" /> The Podracer reached a maximum [[Kilometer per hour|speed]] of 947 [[kilometer]]s per hour and was able to rise to an altitude of 105 meters with the help of repulsorlifts.<ref name="AEYNTK" />

==History==
In the [[Boonta Eve Classic (32 BBY)/Legends|Boonta Eve Classic]] race in [[32 BBY]], Skywalker used his custom-built podracer to defeat the [[Dug]] [[Sebulba]] and win his freedom from slavery.<ref name="Episode I" />

==Behind the scenes==
In ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Droid Tales]]'', this Podracer was given the non-[[canon]]ical name ''Destiny's Favorite''.<ref name="exit">{{DroidTales|Exit from Endor}}</ref>
