The '''Arcana Star Map'''<ref name="DB" /> was a [[compass]] that projected a map of the [[Pathway to Peridea]] which was recovered by the Jedi [[Ahsoka Tano]] from the [[Nightsisters' Temple]] on [[Arcana]]. Tano provided the [[star map]]<ref name="DB" /> to an old friend, [[Sabine Wren]], from whom it was stolen by the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Shin Hati]], a [[mercenary]] aligned with the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Morgan Elsbeth]].<ref name="Part One" /> Elsbeth used the star map<ref name="DB" /> to chart the way to [[Peridea]] using the [[Seatos reflex point]] before another one of her mercenaries, [[Baylan Skoll]], destroyed it to prevent being followed.<ref name="Part Four" /> After recovering, [[Ahsoka Tano]] used [[Psychometry]] on the damaged star map, learning that Wren had left [[Seatos]] with the two mercenaries aboard the ''[[Eye of Sion]]'' to get to [[Peridea]].<ref name="Part Five">{{Ahsoka|Part Five: Shadow Warrior}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Arcana Star Map first appeared in the [[Part One: Master and Apprentice|first episode]] of [[Ahsoka Season 1|the first season]] of the live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]'', which was created, written and directed by [[Dave Filoni]]<ref name="Part One" /> and released on [[August 22]], [[2023]].<ref name="Release">{{Twitter|starwars|status/1692552080764887366|Star Wars|quote=Thank you to all the fans who celebrated with us at our @AhsokaOfficial fan events around the world last night! We're excited to announce that new episodes of #Ahsoka will now launch Tuesdays at 6PM PT, starting with our two-episode premiere on August 22, only on @DisneyPlus.|archivedate=20230818152836}}</ref> It remained unidentified in the episode, but received a name in it's entry in the [[Databank]] on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="DB" /> The map serves as a key "[[Wikipedia:MacGuffin|MacGuffin]]" for the series.<ref name="Beals2">{{Instagram|username=patrick_beals_conceptart|name=[[Patrick Beals]]|url=p/DLly5bTRJZk|date=2025-07-02|quote=The Star Map! It’s what we call a “MacGuffin” in the film industry- an item that everyone is looking for/trying to get. This one took a while to materialize, as it was key, and @dave.filoni was very particular with it as it was such a big story point- the absolutely rad @katarinakushin had done a few early passes where the inside was a sort of clockwork orrery.- then @fatdragonfilms pitched the more Rubik’s cube version it ultimately became. The markings tie into the temple set where it was found. Modeled in Rhino and Zbrush, rendered in Blender. Phew! I almost missed Tano Tuesday! Property Master: Josh Roth @fatdragonfilms Design Direction: @doug_chiang |archivefile=File:ArcanaStarMap-PatrickBeals2-Instagram.png}} {{C|Reply by Beals: "Also fun fact- I spent a LOT of time making sure all those lines connected and lined up until Dave pointed out that the ball is never in a “solved” state- it’s just about the 3 planets lining up.  We were out of time, so I just scrambled up all the pieces, flipping them over and around, so there *IS* a possible configuration where all the pieces on the ball line up 😂"; other reply by Beals: "It actually was the other way around- I took the artwork they did for the temple and put it on the ball 👍"}}</ref> The star map was designed by [[Patrick Beals]] for property master [[Josh Roth]] and art director [[Doug Chiang]] and was built by the series' in-house props department.<ref name="Beals1">{{Instagram|username=patrick_beals_conceptart|name=[[Patrick Beals]]|url=p/CwhHCKwuTye|date=2023-08-29|quote=Seems like everyone is enjoying the first couple eps of Ahsoka so far!  I’m not cleared to release the artwork so here are some images of some prop designs I did for the incredible Property Master Josh Roth @fatdragonfilms All this stuff was built in-house by the amazing Ahsoka Props Department! 1/2 -Star Map!  This went through a few iterations- @psyence.fiction at Lucasfilm had done an early pass with a sort of mechanical orrery inside so I was trying to figure that out, then it shifted to the sort of Rubik’s cube puzzle it ultimately became.  I took some bits of the artwork Art Department did for the chamber and wrapped it to a sphere. Came out beautiful!|archivefile=File:AhsokaProps-PatrickBeals-Instagram.png}}</ref> It had originally been designed by [[Kat K. Velasco]] and [[Katarina Kushin]] with the inside being a mechanical "clockwork orrery",<ref name="Beals1" /><ref name="Beals2" /> which Beals tried to figure out<ref name="Beals1" /> until Roth pitched it as a [[Wikipedia:Rubik's cube|Rubik's cube]] like version.<ref name="Beals2" /> Beals then took some of the art that had been done for for the Arcana temple and wrapped it on the sphere.<ref name="Beals1" /><ref name="Beals2" /> The artwork was [[Wikipedia:3D modeling|3D modeled]] in [[Wikipedia:Rhinoceros 3D|Rhinoceros 3D]] and [[Wikipedia:ZBrush|ZBrush]] and [[Wikipedia:Rendering (computer graphics)|rendered]] in [[Wikipedia:Blender (software)|Blender]]. Beals originally spent a huge amount of time making sure that the markings on the map connected and lined up, until Filoni pointed out that it would never be seen in a fully solved state and only three planets would line up. As he was out of time, he then just scrambled up the pieces and flipped them around so they were misaligned.<ref name="Beals2" />
