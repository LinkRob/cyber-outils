'''Boulder Forest''' was a mountainous region of [[Toshara]], a [[moon]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The region was located north of the [[city|town]] [[Jaunta's Hope]] and west of [[Mirogana Valley]]. The region was dotted with a number of rock formations, including the [[Fallen Mound]] and the [[Haskà Circle]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]] had constructed a [[Imperial wind harvester|wind harvester]], several small outposts, including [[Imperial Outpost 44|outpost 44]] and [[Imperial Outpost 62|outpost 62]], and a [[Imperial Comm Station|comm station]] protected by two ground based [[turbolaser]]s within the region. The area around the comm station was referred to as the [[Howling Strip]] and was located in the northern side of the Boulder Forest. By the same year, the [[Crimson Dawn]] [[syndicate]] possessed [[Crimson Dawn Hideout (Boulder Forest)|hideout]] located in the mountains in the south of the region. The ruins of a structure, known as [[Runner's Gate]], was also located in the west of the Boulder Forest region.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

During 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] raided the comm station while looking for a [[power generator]] to repair the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her stolen [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
