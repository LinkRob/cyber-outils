'''Direct action operatives''', also colloquially known as '''rangers''', were the non-[[Clone trooper|clone]] [[agent]]s of [[Republic Intelligence]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. [[Matese]] and his team were recruited due to their experience in the [[Judicial Forces]]. Matese's team of rangers was assembled by [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]] for the [[mission to Merj]], during which the rangers kidnapped two Separatist [[Morseerian]] [[scientist]]s. The two Morseerians were used as an [[Vallt Rescue Operation|exchange]] for [[Galen Walton Erso]] and his family.<ref name="Catalyst" />
