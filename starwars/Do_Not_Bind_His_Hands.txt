'''''Do Not Bind His Hands''''' was a [[Galactic Republic]] campaign [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] produced by the [[Commission for the Protection of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Created by the [[artist]] [[Venthan Chassu]], it depicted then-[[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] looking to the side in an impressive manner in front of the [[Republic crest|Republic symbol]] against a [[Color|yellow]] background, with the font declaring "Do Not Bind His Hands," and below that, "Grant Him the Authority He Needs To Assure Total Victory," with "Victory" being written in a significantly larger manner than the rest of the sentence.<ref name="Propaganda" />
