'''William "Bill" George''' is an [[Industrial Light & Magic|ILM]] visual-effects artist who has worked on numerous ''[[Star Wars]]'' and [[Lucasfilm]] projects. His work includes designing the [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing fighter]] for {{Film|VI}}. The droid [[BG-81]] was named after George and designed based on a [[1981]] picture of him wearing a striped shirt.<ref name="Wings">{{SW|url=wings-of-the-master-trivia-gallery-2|text=Wings of the Master Trivia Gallery}}</ref>

At [[Celebration VI]] in [[2012]] George hosted a panel with [[Steven Spiegel]] discussing the development of ''Star Tours: The Adventures Continue''.<ref name="Exploring">{{SW|url=news/exploring-the-creature-cantina-at-celebration-vi|text=Exploring the Creature Cantina at Celebration VI}}</ref>
