'''Ahoec''' was a [[Mon Calamari]] [[Gender|female]] and the sister of [[Loku Kanoloa]]. She was an aspiring [[diplomat]]. In an effort to escape the [[Galactic Empire]], she joined a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] convoy transporting civilians to a hidden rebel world. The Empire ambushed the convoy, and the transport she was on managed to jump to [[hyperspace]] and escape the battle. Eventually, the [[hyperdrive]] gave out near the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], where the damaged ship crashed. Ahoec, along with the other survivors, set up a camp where they stayed before being attacked by [[Captain]] [[Terro]] and his forces.<ref name="Legends" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ahoec first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Assault]]'' companion app ''[[Legends of the Alliance]]'', in the "Jabba's Realm" campaign. Ahoec has a chance of being one of the eight crash survivors present during the first mission, or guaranteed if the player chooses Loku Kanoloa to be in the [[squad]]. Depending upon the actions and success of the player during the missions, Ahoec's fate can vary. Ahoec can end up being [[Slavery|enslaved]] by [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]], eaten by a [[rancor]], left behind at Terro's camp, or successfully have escaped Tatooine with the squad.<ref name="Legends" />
