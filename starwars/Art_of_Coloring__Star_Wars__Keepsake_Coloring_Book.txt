'''''Art of Coloring: Star Wars: Keepsake Coloring Book''''' is a children's activity book that was released as part of [[Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi|Journey to ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'']]. It was written and illustrated by [[Katie Cook]] and published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[September 1]], [[2017]].

==Publisher's summary==
''A collection of Katie Cook's adorable artwork that spans the entire Star Wars saga, carefully adapted for a coloring experience that can be enjoyed by Jedi Masters and young Padawans alike.''
