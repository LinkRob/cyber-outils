A '''Comfort Unit''' was a shelter and [[Cantina|tavern]] stocked with cheap local beverages established by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Aldhani Garrison|garrison]] on the [[planet]] [[Aldhani]].<ref name="Eye" /> By [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> multiple Comfort Units had been arranged in the path of the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Dhani]] [[pilgrim]]s as they traveled out of the planet's [[Lowlands]] to witness the celestial event the [[Eye of Aldhani]]. The Comfort Units were designed to waylay the Dhani and reduce the attendance of the Eye, their celebrations of which stood as an obstacle to the Empire's expansion efforts.<ref name="Eye" />
