'''Armo Malou''' was a [[Gender|male]]<ref name="TLJ Selects" /> [[Armo Malou's species|alien]]<ref name="Topps" /> who worked as head<ref name="TLJ Selects" /> [[Bartender|barkeeper]]<ref name="Stickers">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Ultimate Sticker Collection]]''</ref> at the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|Canto Casino]] in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="TLJVD" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[34 ABY]]. As [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] carries on immediately from the events of ''The Force Awakens'', it can be assumed to take place in the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> he was present in the [[casino]] when the [[Resistance]] operatives [[Finn]] and [[Rose Tico]] entered.<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> Malou's job as barkeeper required great skill, as the casino's patrons included many different [[species]] from all across [[the galaxy]], all with different tastes.<ref name="Stickers" /> A consummate professional, Malou invented many famous [[cocktail]]s, including the [[Stinky Wampa]], the [[Clouds of Bespin]] and the [[Xagobah Sour]].<ref name="TLJ Selects" /> He wore a green [[Clothing|waistcoat]] with yellow patterning<ref name="Topps" /> in casino livery<ref name="TLJVD" /> over a white shirt with a red belt, as well as black pants with red and yellow patterning. Malou had gray skin, brown [[hair]] and black [[eye]]s.<ref name="Topps" />

==Behind the scenes==
Armo Malou first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="TLJ" /> which was released on [[December 15]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-viii-to-open-december-15-2017|text=''Star Wars: Episode VIII'' to Open December 15, 2017}}</ref> The character was not named in the film,<ref name="TLJ" /> but received identification on a card in the [[2017 Topps Star Wars: The Last Jedi|2017 Topps ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'']] set released on [[December 20]].<ref name="Topps" />
