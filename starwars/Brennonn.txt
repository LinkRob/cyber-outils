'''Brennonn''' was a [[Wookiee]] who resided on the planet [[Kashyyyk]]. She became friends of the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] sometime between [[68 BBY]] and circa [[58 BBY]], when he was still a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]. Brennonn and Jinn shared adventures together and she even saved the Jedi's life on multiple occasions. Around [[41 BBY]], Brennonn met Jinn and his own Padawan, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], during a [[Life Day]] gathering, though the celebration was interrupted shortly afterward by a group of [[Trandoshan]] [[Hunting|hunters]]. Brennonn fought off a hunter but Jinn and several Wookiees were captured. Brennonn and Kenobi successfully [[Rescue of Qui-Gon Jinn|rescued]] Jinn and the trio returned to the Life Day festivities.

==Biography==
{{Quote|I was a Padawan learner myself when I first met Brennonn. We had quite the adventures.|Qui-Gon Jinn|<ref name="Adventures 3">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=3|story=Life Day (comic story)}}</ref>}}
Brennonn met [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] when he was only a [[Padawan]] learner<ref name="Adventures 3" /> sometime between [[68 BBY]] and circa [[58 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The two shared adventures and Brennonn rescued the Padawan on two occasions, though Jinn insisted they saved each other on the second occasion.<ref name="Adventures 3" />

Around [[41 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Life Day (comic story)|Life Day]]" to around [[41 BBY]].</ref> Jinn, now a [[Jedi Master]], introduced Brennonn to his padawan, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], at a [[Life Day]] celebration on<ref name="Adventures 3" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="KashyyykDB">{{Databank|kashyyyk|Kashyyyk}}</ref> [[Kashyyyk]]. Jinn was thankful to Brennonn for permitting him and Kenobi to join her in the Life Day festivities but was concerned about [[Balfus]]'s disappearance. As Jinn pondered on the circumstances surrounding Balfus's disappearance, [[Trandoshan]]s descended upon the Wookiee's festivities and began capturing participants for their [[Hunting|hunt]]. Brennonn defended herself from the [[hunter]]s, throwing one Trandoshan away from her. When another held Brennonn at blasterpoint, Jinn destroyed the [[Blaster rifle|blaster]] with [[Qui-Gon Jinn's lightsaber|his]] [[lightsaber]] but was [[Prisoner|captured]] himself.<ref name="Adventures 3" />

Brennonn discussed how to [[Rescue of Qui-Gon Jinn|free]] Jinn and the captured Wookiees with Kenobi, who asked Brennonn to explain how the situation related to the teachings of her elders. When Kenobi came to understand how the teachings related to an earlier lesson of Jinn's, he formulated a plan. Kenobi surrendered himself to the Trandoshans, using himself as a distraction so he could unlock Jinn's [[prison]] using [[the Force]]. After Jinn was freed, Brennonn and a group of Wookiees ambushed the Trandoshans, surrounding them. Brennonn offered a peacful solution to the fighting, requesting the hunters lay down their [[weapon]]s.<ref name="Adventures 4" />

Later, when Jinn and Kenobi returned to the Life Day celebrations, Brennonn greeted the two Jedi again with a hug. Jinn assured Brennonn that gratitude was not needed and she would have done the same for him. As Brennonn departed from the two, Jinn assured her they would join her.<ref name="Adventures 4" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|She's saying you're fortunate it's Life Day, otherwise you wouldn't be offered a peaceful solution.|Qui-Gon Jinn translating Brennonn for Korgan|<ref name="Adventures 4" />}}
Brennonn was a Wookiee with [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]] and brown eyes. She wore braids in hair. Brennonn was friendly, often greeting her friends, old and new, with hugs. Brennonn spoke [[Shyriiwook]] and understood [[Galactic Basic Standard|Basic]]. Brennonn was a brave Wookiee, saving Jinn on three occassions.<ref name="Adventures 3" /><ref name="Adventures 4" /> She showed mercy to Korgan and his hunters because of Life Day.<ref name="Adventures 4" />

==Equipment==
Brennonn wore a blue [[pauldron]] with a gold trim on either side that each had an accompanying brown strap that ran across her [[Breast|chest]] and formed a cross. She wielded a black [[bowcaster]] when she helped rescue Jinn from the Tardoshans.<ref name="Adventures 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brennonn first appeared in an accompanying comic story of the [[Star Wars Adventures (2020) 3|third issue]] of the [[Star Wars Adventures (2020)|''Star Wars Adventures'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]], "[[Life Day (comic story)|Life Day]],"<ref name="Adventures 3" /> published on [[February 3]], [[2021]].<ref name="Previews World">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2020-3/digital-comic/888167|text=Star Wars Adventures (2020-) #3|nolive=1}}</ref> The comic story was written by [[Michael Moreci]], illustrated by [[Megan Levens]] and [[Charlie Kirchoff]], and served as the first of a two-part story<ref name="Adventures 3" /> that continued with "[[Life Day Part 2]]" in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2020) 4|fourth issue]]<ref name="Adventures 4" /> on [[March 24]] of the same year.<ref name="Previews World2">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2020-4/digital-comic/893337|text=Star Wars Adventures (2020-) #4|nolive=1}}</ref>
