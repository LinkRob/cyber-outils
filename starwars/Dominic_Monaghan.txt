'''Dominic Bernard Patrick Luke Monaghan''' (born [[December 8]], [[1976]]) is an [[Wikipedia:English people|English]] actor who appeared as [[Beaumont Kin]] in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="Dominic Monaghan">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/heat-vision/star-wars-episode-ix-casts-dominic-monaghan-1137403|text=Dominic Monaghan Joins ''Star Wars: Episode IX''|author=Couch, Aaron|date=2018-08-24|work=''[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|The Hollywood Reporter]]''|archivedate=20200730112918}}</ref><ref name="Dominic Tweer">{{Twitter|DomsWildThings|status/1033092070280454146|Dominic Monaghan|date=2018-08-24|quote=Boom! Told ya. @starwars #becurious https://ift.tt/2NlL5NW|archivedate=20200522142742}}</ref> He is known for his roles as the Hobbit [[Wikipedia:Merry Brandybuck|Merry]] in the [[Wikipedia:The Lord of the Rings (film series)|''The Lord of the Rings'' trilogy]], [[Wikipedia:Charlie Pace|Charlie Pace]] on the drama series [[Wikipedia:Lost (TV series)|''Lost'']], the Mutant [[Wikipedia:Chris Bradley|Chris Bradley]] in the superhero film [[Wikipedia:X-Men Origins: Wolverine|''X-Men Origins: Wolverine'']], and for hosting the nature program ''[[Wikipedia:Wild Things with Dominic Monaghan|Wild Things with Dominic Monaghan]]''.

In an interview with the ''Distraction Pieces'' podcast of ComicBook.com, Monaghan revealed that his part in Episode IX is a minor one, giving that he would only appear in the film's first act and possibly briefly later on.<ref name="Dominic's Role" /> He has been a ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan since he was little.<ref name="World Premiere">{{SWYouTube|IXZpoaVUGJw|Live From The Red Carpet Of Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker}}</ref>
