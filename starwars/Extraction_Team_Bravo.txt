'''Extraction Team Bravo''', also referred to as '''Bravo Team,''' was a military unit that served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] under the command of [[General]] [[Davits Draven]] during the lead-up to the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Battlefront VR" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Rogue One date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' in [[1 BBY]].</ref> the extraction team was sent on a mission to [[Liberation of Erso|break out]] [[Jyn Erso]]&mdash;a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] criminal who would later become a key figure in the [[Battle of Scarif|theft]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[Death Star plans|plans]]&mdash;from a [[Imperial Detention Center & Labor Camp LEG-817|labor camp]] that was used by the [[Galactic Empire]] on the [[planet]] [[Wobani]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

After a successful extraction on the planet's surface, their [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|U-wing]] [[transport]], ''[[Bravo One]]'', encountered a minor technical setback and was unable to jump to [[hyperspace]]. The team took refuge in an [[asteroid field]] in the [[Wobani system]] and a priority transmission to all available Rebel vessels was sent by [[K-2SO]]<ref name="Battlefront VR" />&mdash;a [[KX-series security droid]]<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> who formerly served the Empire.<ref name="K-2SO DB">{{Databank|k-2so-kay-tuesso|K-2SO (Kay-Tuesso)}}</ref> An [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing starfighter]] [[squadron]] called [[Red Flight (Rebel Alliance)|Red Flight]] answered their call and saved them from incoming [[Imperial Navy|Imperial forces]].<ref name="Battlefront VR" />

==Behind the scenes==
Extraction Team Bravo first appeared in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Battlefront|''Star Wars Battlefront'': Rogue One: X-wing VR Mission]], a downloadable virtual reality add-on to the [[2015]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''Star Wars Battlefront''.<ref name="Battlefront VR" />
