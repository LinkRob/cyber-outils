'''Amos''' was an individual who served as a [[captain]] within the [[Imperial Navy|naval forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Amos was in [[Commanding officer|command]] of a [[Desevro fleet|fleet]] that protected [[Desevro]], and led it from his [[flagship]], the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|ISD]] ''[[Tormentor]]''. The [[fleet]] was ultimately destroyed due to Amos' carelessness, and he [[Death|perished]] when the ''Tormentor'' was destroyed in a [[battle above Desevro]].<ref name="Squadrons" />
