{{Quote|A contemporary artistic depiction of the bosses|"Index of Organizations"|<ref name="OSWM 117" />}}
An '''artistic depiction''' of the leaders of [[Crimson Dawn]] was created during the [[New Republic Era]]. The contemporary art depicted [[Qi'ra]] in the center, with [[Dryden Vos]] on the left and [[Maul]] on the right. It was included in "[[Index of Organizations]]," a survival compendium sent out by [[Carson Teva]] to [[New Republic]] recruits.<ref name="OSWM 117" />

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The artwork of Crimson Dawn leaders was initially a variant cover for [[Solo: A Star Wars Story Adaptation 5|''Solo: A Star Wars Story Adaptation'' 5]], which was drawn by [[W. Scott Forbes]].<ref name="Solo Adaptation">[[Solo: A Star Wars Story Adaptation 5|''Solo: A Star Wars Story Adaptation'' 5]]</ref> It was revealed to be an [[in-universe]] artwork in "[[Database]]," a department in [[Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin 117|''Star Wars - The Official Magazine'' 117]].<ref name="OSWM 117" />
