The '''Collector's case''' was a custom-designed [[Cargo container|container]] made by [[First Order]] technicians for [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]].<ref name="TROSVD" />

==Specifications==
The case had a mag-sealed, chilled interior, making [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] helmet shielded from radiation and sensor scans. There is also an access keypad which allows Kylo to open the box.<ref name="TROSVD" />
