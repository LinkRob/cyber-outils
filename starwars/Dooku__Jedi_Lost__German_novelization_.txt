'''''Dooku - Der verlorene Jedi''''' ('''''Dooku: Jedi Lost''''' in [[Wikipedia:English language|English]]) is a [[Wikipedia:German language|German]] novelization of the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', which was originally written by [[Cavan Scott]]. However, the novelization differs greatly from the original audio drama and its [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script]] beyond just translation, changing several scenes and referencing a number of elements from [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], as well as continuity errors such as mentions of the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] that did not exist during the events of the audio drama.

According to Senior ''[[Star Wars]]'' Lore Advisor [[Leland Chee]],<ref name="Leland Chee">{{Bluesky|holocronkeeper.bsky.social|/|Keeper of the Holocron|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/7r2h9|archivefile=File:LelandChee-Profile-Bluesky.png}}</ref> he was never made aware of the German novelization and that it was likely never reviewed by their team.<ref name="Leland">{{WookDiscord|discordname=.holocronkeeper|name=Leland Chee|date=2025-04-09|messagelink=https://discord.com/channels/397848917036761090/492457166771322881/1359322012740358346|quote=First I've heard of it. I doubt it came to our team for review.|archivefile=File:LelandChee-DookuJediLostGermanNovel-Discord.png}} {{C|In response to: "@HolocronKeeper Not sure if it is something your [''sic''] allowed to answer, but are you aware of the german novelization of <nowiki>[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]] and its canonicity? I'm asking due to the confusion the book has somewhat caused canon wise due to its supposed deviation from the source material and exclusive recanonization to several Legends subjects including [[Sorzus Syn]], [[rakghoul plague]], and the [[Muur Talisman]].</nowiki>"}}</ref>
