'''Conning towers''' or '''command towers''' were towers found on the exterior of several of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[warship]]s that contained the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]].<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> One Imperial model with a conning tower was the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]], and during the [[Battle of Hoth]] the ''Imperial II''-class ''[[Tyrant]]'s'' conning tower was taken out when the vessel was hit with an [[Ion cannon|ion]] blast from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[v-150 Planet Defender]].<ref name="ESBjn">[[Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back (paperback novel)|''Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back'' junior novelization]]</ref> As the conning towers of the Imperial warships were so exposed, they were not included in the design of the [[First Order]]'s [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]].<ref name="TFAVD" />

==''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers==
Sat upon an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers']] command tower was either a [[tractor beam targeting array]] ([[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyers]]<ref name="DiD-book">[[Droids in Distress (book)|''Droids in Distress'' chapter book]]</ref>) or a [[communications tower]] ([[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]]<ref name="Shattered Empire, Part III">[[Shattered Empire 3|''Shattered Empire'' 3]]</ref> and upgraded<ref name="BS">{{InsiderCite|issue1=149|issue2=150|Blade Squadron (short story)}}</ref> ''Imperial I''-classes<ref name="UltimateSW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>), which was flanked by two [[deflector shield]] [[Deflector shield generator|generator]] globes.<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Blueprints|cardname=Star Destroyer}}</ref>
