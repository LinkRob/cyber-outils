"'''Blind Jedi's Bluff'''" is a short comic story published in [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic 6.29|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic'' 6.29]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Obi-Wan's solo mission to get information from a Separatist traitor leads him straight into a trap in the latest Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic Adventure. A flash-bomb leaves him temporarily blind in the throes of combat&mdash;but will disability prove a barrier to victory?''
