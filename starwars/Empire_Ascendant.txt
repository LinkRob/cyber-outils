"'''Empire Ascendant'''" is a [[canon]] short story that appears in ''[[The Prequel Trilogy Stories]]''. It was written by [[Andy Schmidt]].<ref name="TPTS" /> It adapts [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] from [[Order 66]] to [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s arrival on [[Mustafar]].
