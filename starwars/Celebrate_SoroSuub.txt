'''Celebrate SoroSuub''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] adventure ''[[No Place Like Home]]''. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[November 27]] [[2002]] and took the form of an announcement by [[Miim Te'Suub]] on the plans for the upcoming celebration of the anniversary of the opening of production on [[Edic Bar]] by the [[SoroSuub Corporation/Legends|SoroSuub Corporation]].
