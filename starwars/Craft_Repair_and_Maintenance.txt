'''Craft Repair and Maintenance'''<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/friends-and-enemies-trivia-gallery|text="Friends and Enemies" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 11}}</ref> was a business on at the [[Orondia Refueling Station]]<ref name="Friends" /> and [[Stobar Spaceport]] during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Brothers" /> and [[Horizon Base]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Forgotten" /> Decades later, the business was advertised on the chassis of [[R4-G77]], an [[astromech droid]] owned by [[Hype Fazon]], a [[racer]] [[pilot]] on the ''[[Colossus]]'' [[Fuel|refueling]] [[Space station|station]].<ref name="Designing">{{SW|url=news/designing-star-wars-resistance|text=Designing ''Star Wars'': ''Star Wars Resistance''|author=[[Kristin Baver]]|date=2019-03-11}} {{C|Slide 12}}</ref> The business logo was also used to decorate Fazon's [[racing starfighter]], the ''[[Green Ace]]'', while the tool motif from the logo was printed on the trousers of Fazon's [[flight suit]].<ref name="Bucket">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-high-tower-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 9 Fun Facts from "The High Tower" - ''Star Wars Resistance''|date=2018-10-29}} {{C|Slide 6 — 7}}</ref> An advertisement for the company was seen on the exterior of [[Vranki's Hotel and Casino]], a [[space station]] in the [[Voxx Cluster]].<ref name="Voxx">{{Resistance|The Voxx Vortex 5000}}</ref>

The business' logo was written in [[Aurebesh]],<ref name="Forgotten" /> although the second word was misspelled as "repai'''t'''.<ref name="Trivia" /> At least two different [[color]] palettes were used, with the ads at the Orondia Refueling Station,<ref name="Friends" /> Horizon Base<ref name="Forgotten" /> R4-G77 and the ''Green Ace'' being yellow and orange,<ref name="Bucket" /> while the one at Stobar Spaceport switched from yellow to purple.<ref name="Brothers" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Signage at the fueling station includes: Eat at Koe's, Bounty Emporium: Always Open, Bounty Never Sleeps; Security Services; Speeder Sales; The Pilot's Lounge (Live Band Every Night); Craft Repair and Maintenance; Repair Rack Droid Services; Orondia Tours; and Meteor Cafe|StarWars.com Trivia Gallery for "Friends and Enemies"|<ref name="Trivia" />}}
Craft Repair and Maintenance was first mentioned in an advertisement at the [[Orondia Refueling Station]] in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Friends and Enemies]]."<ref name="Trivia" /> However, it is too far away to read in the final episode.<ref name="Friends" /> Another ad appears at [[Stobar Spaceport]] in the episode "[[Brothers (episode)|Brothers]]," although the last two words are almost illegible.<ref name="Brothers" /> A third advertisement can be seen at [[Horizon Base]] in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[The Forgotten Droid]]," although only the first two words are seen.<ref name="Forgotten" /> A fourth ad was supposed to appear on [[Havoc Outpost]] in the ''Star Wars Rebels'' episode "[[Blood Sisters]],"<ref name="Concept Sisters">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/blood-sisters-concept-art-gallery|text="Blood Sisters" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> but it does not appear in the final episode.<ref name="Sisters">{{Rebels|Blood Sisters}}</ref>
