The '''Czerka Vibroknuckler''' was a model of [[vibroknuckler]] produced by the [[Czerka Arms]] corporation. The knucklers utilized a [[vibroblade]] that ran along the wearer's knuckles, which meant that the wearer's punches could inflict serious or even [[Death|deadly]] wounds on opponents. The vibroknucklers cost 350 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Czerka vibroknuckler was mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
