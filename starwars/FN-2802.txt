'''FN-2802''' was a [[human]] who served in the [[First Order]]'s [[FN Trooper Corps|FN Corps]] as a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] in [[35 ABY]]. The [[trooper]] identified the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan|apprentice]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and her companions as [[Resistance]] operatives, and attempted to [[Mission to Pasaana (Resistance)|capture]] them while stationed on the [[planet]] [[Pasaana]] during the [[Festival of the Ancestors]]. However, FN-2802 was [[Death|killed]] by [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], who shot the [[soldier]] in the head with an arrow.

==Biography==
{{Quote|I've located the Resistance fugitives. All units, report&hellip;|FN-2802 is killed while trying to report his discovery|<ref name="IX year" />}}
FN-2802 was the designation of a<ref name="Episode IX" /> [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> who served as a [[stormtrooper]] in the [[First Order]]'s<ref name="Episode IX" /> [[FN Trooper Corps|FN Corps]]<ref name="BTA" /> during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Episode IX" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="IX year" /> FN-2802 was in the [[Forbidden Valley]] on the [[planet]] [[Pasaana]] during the [[Festival of the Ancestors]]. After [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] had the First Order forces on the planet alerted to the presence of the [[Jedi]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and her allies, FN-2802 spotted the group as they attempted to make their way through the crowded festival back to their [[starship]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:FN-2802_Death.png|thumb|left|200px|FN-2802 was killed by Landonis Calrissian.]]
The stormtrooper aimed<ref name="Episode IX" /> his<ref name="TROS EE" /> [[blaster]] and ordered the group to stop, which they did. As FN-2802 attempted to report his discovery of the enemy over comms<ref name="Episode IX" /> to his [[squad]],<ref name="Junior">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> he was [[Death|killed]] by local hermit [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Landonis Calrissian]], who fired a single bolt from his [[prod pistol]] straight through the [[eye]] of the stormtrooper's [[helmet]]. As the surrounding festival goers reacted in shock and horror to FN-2802's death, Calrissian led Rey and her allies away to safety.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Equipment==
FN-2802 wore [[First Order stormtrooper armor]] with the [[web gear]]<ref name="Episode IX" /> of a [[First Order heavy trooper|Megablaster heavy assault trooper]],<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> but only carried a [[F-11D blaster rifle]] at the [[time]] of his death.<ref name="Episode IX" /> He used a [[comlink]] when contacting his squad.<ref name="Junior" />
