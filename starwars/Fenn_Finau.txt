'''Fenn Finau''' was a [[bounty hunter]] who fought [[Cal Kestis]]. He was the brother of [[Masi Finau]], and the siblings worked as a bounty hunting team alongside their droid [[KLE-0]]. The brothers were rumored to hold the highest death count in the [[Abregado system]], although they were accused on cheating by combining their individual scores.<ref name="Survivor" />
