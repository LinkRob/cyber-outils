'''Arr-Nine-Nineteen''' was a [[droid]] who claimed to be a [[Jedi hunter]], but was a [[Hutt Clan]] [[spy]] in reality. During the [[Imperial Era]], he was one of several individuals hired by the [[Hidden Hand]] crime syndicate's leader, [[Gwi]], as part of a [[Beilert Valance's crew|team]] tasked with [[Hunt for Vader|hunting down]] the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. When the [[cyborg]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]] was introduced to the team, he [[Death|destroyed]] Arr-Nine-Nineteen, and the droid's true purpose was revealed to the rest of the team.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Is&hellip;is there a problem?''"<br />"''Yeah. My third rule. No droids.|Gwi and Valance, right before Arr-Nine-Nineteen is destroyed|<ref name="Target Vader 1" />}}
At some point, Arr-Nine-Nineteen was built with a [[spy]]ing device inside his body and tasked by the [[Hutt Clan]] to conduct espionage on the [[Hidden Hand]] criminal syndicate. He eventually forged the reputation of a [[Jedi hunter]] and was hired by the Hidden Hand's leader, [[Gwi]], to join a [[Beilert Valance's crew|team]] of [[bounty hunter]]s created to [[Hunt for Vader|hunt down]] and [[Death|kill]] the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Target Vader 1" />

[[File:ArrNineNineteenDestroyed-TargetVader1.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Arr-Nine-Nineteen is destroyed by Beilert Valance.]]
When recruiting the [[cyborg]] [[Beilert Valance]],<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Gwi described the [[droid]] as a "[[Jedi]] [[Hunting|hunter]] who never retired." Upon seeing Valance, Arr-Nine-Nineteen was burned by the cyborg's [[palm blaster]]. Gwi claimed that the team needed the droid, but Valance revealed that the droid was a mole for the [[Hutt]]s, stating that Arr-Nine-Nineteen's career as a Jedi killer was falsified before removing the spying device from inside his body and crushing it. Valance and his team then began their mission despite Arr-Nine-Nineteen's destruction.<ref name="Target Vader 1" />

==Characteristics==
Arr-Nine-Nineteen was a masculine-programmed droid with four arms and plating [[color]]ed in blue, white, brown, and red, with red [[photoreceptor]]s.<ref name="Target Vader 1" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|He was no Jedi killer. What he ''was'', though&hellip;was up to his bolts in debt to the Hutts.|Valance, revealing Arr-Nine-Nineteen's true purpose|<ref name="Target Vader 1" />}}
Arr-Nine-Nineteen wore a red [[cape]] and several equipment pouches around his torso. He was able to utilize multiple [[blaster]]s simultaneously. Within his body, he carried a device that was used to spy on the Hidden Hand.<ref name="Target Vader 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arr-Nine-Nineteen appeared in the [[Target Vader 1|first issue]] of the [[2019]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book series]] ''[[Star Wars: Target Vader]]'', written by [[Robbie Thompson]], illustrated by [[Marc Laming]] and [[Cris Bolson]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> on [[July 3]], 2019.<ref name="Marvel-TV1">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/77664/star_wars_target_vader_2019_1|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #1}}</ref> Prior to the comic's release, the character was introduced indirectly via a mention of Valance's team by assistant editor [[Tom Groneman]] at a panel held at [[Celebration Chicago|''Star Wars'' Celebration Chicago]] in [[April]] 2019 that was later detailed in a [[StarWars.com]] article published on [[April 15]].<ref name="SWCC">{{SW|url=news/swcc-2019-marvel-star-wars-panel|text=SWCC 2019: Marvel's ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge #1 Art and More Revealed}}</ref>
