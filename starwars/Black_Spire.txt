The '''Black Spire''' was a [[petrified tree]] found in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. It was considered the heart of the outpost and was located next to [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities]] and [[Savi's Workshop]]. It was also the namesake of the outpost and an ancient gathering place for locals to tell stories. The spire was seen as a symbol of hope and rebirth, as it had a live [[tree]] growing from its center.<ref name="SWGE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Black Spire was named on trading cards available at [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] [[December 16]], [[2017]] "Galactic Nights" session, which covered locations for the then-upcoming [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] attraction. It shared a card with Docking Bay 7. Although the map card containing it lacked an Aurebesh phrase itself, the other map cards, in particular those relating to the [[Oga's Cantina|cantina]], [[Smuggler's Alley]], [[ancient ruins]] and [[Saka Homestead]], alluded to the landmark via their respective Aurebesh phrases.
