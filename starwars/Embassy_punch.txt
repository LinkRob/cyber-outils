'''Embassy punch''' was a type of [[Color|turquoise]] beverage [[Diet|consumable]] by [[human]]s. During the [[Imperial Era]], it could be found on<ref name="Announcement" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] within the [[Chandrilan Embassy]]. One of the household's residents, the [[Chandrilan]] [[Perrin Fertha]], took a liking to the drink.<ref name="Announcement" />

In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|107}}</ref> Embassy punch was served at a party hosted in the Chandrilan Embassy and was imbibed by several guests. While at the event, the Chandrilan [[banker]] [[Tay Kolma]] enjoyed a [[glass]] of the beverage while conversing with Fertha's [[Marriage|wife]], [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]]. As Kolma discussed his [[Politics|political]] leanings with Mothma, he apologized for expounding on his somewhat anti-[[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] leanings, blaming his loose-tongued speech on an abundance of Embassy punch.<ref name="Announcement" />
