'''Beska''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who worked for the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]. She was one of [[ISB Supervisor|Supervisor]] [[Lonni Jung]]'s agents, who reported to [[Lagret]] at the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]]. [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] had her and two other agents — [[Charval]] and [[Selko]] — perfom an extraction to safely escort [[Mon Mothma]] off [[Coruscant]]. Beska and the other agents did not know the team was under Organa's personal command. Unbeknownst to Organa, Charval and Selko, Beska was a plant with orders to prevent Mothma's escape. As one of Supervisor Jung's agents, [[Luthen Rael]] was made aware of the team's compromise and sent [[Cassian Jeron Andor|another agent]] to intercept.

After Charval overheard Beska communicating with Lagret, she pretended she was just contacting "work." Charval became suspicious about Beska and requested her to hand him the communication device. She feigned a handoff, then [[Death|killed]] Charval.

After Mothma's speech about the [[Ghorman Massacre]], Beska intercepted her escort by Cassian Andor and attempted to [[Escape from the Senate|arrest]] her while drawing a [[blaster]]. She ordered Andor, Selko and everyone else to stand down, as she was taking Mothma into custody for an ISB authorized arrest. However, Andor killed her with a shot to the temple from [[Clem's blaster pistol|his blaster]] after she was distracted by [[Erskin Semaj]] calling her a "rebel spy."<ref name="Welcome Rebellion" />

==Personality and traits==
Beska was a human female with [[Color|blue]] [[eye]]s and brown [[hair]] tied in a bun. She was loyal to the [[Galactic Empire]], serving the Imperial Security Bureau. She also served as a spy who infiltrated [[Bail Organa's resistance movement]].<ref name="Welcome Rebellion" />

==Equipment==
Beska wore a billowy blue tunic.<ref name="Welcome Rebellion AD" /> She was armed with a blaster.<ref name="Welcome Rebellion" />

==Behind the scenes==
Beska first appeared in "[[Welcome to the Rebellion]]", the ninth episode of [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''. She was portrayed by [[Ana Ularu]].<ref name="Welcome Rebellion" />
