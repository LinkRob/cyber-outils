The '''''Black Ace''''' was a modified [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]] [[pilot]]ed by [[Griff Halloran]].<ref name="Databank" /> Halloran used it both as a [[Racing starfighter|racer]] and in the defense of the ''[[Colossus]]''. The ship was lost for a short time during the [[Platform Classic]],<ref name="TPC">{{Resistance|The Platform Classic}}</ref> but was later recovered and used later in the [[Bibo incident|attack]] on [[Bibo]]'s [[Bibo's mother|mother]].<ref name="Bibo">{{Resistance|Bibo (episode)}}</ref>
