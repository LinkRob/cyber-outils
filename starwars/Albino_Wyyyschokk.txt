The '''Albino Wyyyschokk''' was a rare subspecies of [[Wyyyschokk]] native to the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]]. Faster and more aggressive than normal Wyyyschokks, these massive [[spider]]s were found in dark, subterranean areas where they could live for [[Standard century|centuries]]. The species was capable of spitting venom, which it used to disable its prey before feeding on their [[brain]]s.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

While exploring Kashyyyk, [[Cal Kestis]] was attacked by an Albino Wyyyschokk. He had to fight it off, eventually [[Death|killing]] the creature.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Albino Wyyyschokk appeared in the [[2019]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''.<ref name="Fallen Order" />
