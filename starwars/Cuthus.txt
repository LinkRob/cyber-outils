'''Cuthus''' was a [[human]] individual working for [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] in [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on [[Tatooine]] as of [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]]'s [[Mission to Ro Mira|mission]] to [[Ro Mira]]. Cuthus hired the [[Anzellan]] [[Yaya Shram]] to collect a [[bounty]] and retrieve the [[coaxium ultra]] formula from Ro Mira, and reminded Shram to be discreet for fear of risking failure and Jabba's wrath.<ref name="Mace Windu 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cuthus first appeared in [[Mace Windu 2|''Mace Windu'' 2]], the second issue of the [[2024]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Mace Windu]]'' written by [[Marc Bernardin]] and illustrated by [[Georges Jeanty]], [[Dexter Vines]], and [[Andrew Dalhouse]].<ref name="Mace Windu 2" /> Cuthus shares his name with the surname of [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] character [[Naroon Cuthus]], who was also a servant of Jabba.<ref name="Galaxy Guide 5">''[[Galaxy Guide 5: Return of the Jedi]]''</ref>
