[...] ''[It] is a Morellian 35c repeater. It’s not even a blaster, you get me? It’s technically a ''cannon''.|[[Denic]], to Baze Malbus, on the latter's repeating cannon|<ref name="GotW" />}}
'''Baze's repeating cannon''' was an [[MWC-35c "Staccato Lightning" repeating cannon]] used by the freelance [[assassin]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[soldier]] [[Baze Malbus]]. It weighed 30 [[kilogram]]s and was [[Color|scarlet]] and silver in color. The weapon contained the firepower of five [[Laser rifle/Legends|laser rifles]] in one and could hold up to 35,000 blaster shots. Its ability to fire both quickly and accurately led to the name "Staccato Lightning."<ref name="UVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the [[2017]] [[Guardians of the Whills (novel)|''Guardians of the Whills'']] novel, [[Denic]] tells Malbus that the cannon can fire 40,000 rounds before needing a reload.<ref name="GotW" /> The [[2021]] manga adaptation ''[[Guardians of the Whills: The Manga]]'' changed the line to state 35,000 rounds,<ref name="Manga">''[[Guardians of the Whills: The Manga]]''</ref> matching the information provided in ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''.<ref name="UVG" />
