The '''electroshock dart''' ,<ref name="Chapter 22">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 22: Guns for Hire}}</ref> also known as the '''electro-dart''',<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> was a type of [[dart]] designed to stun a target by sending an electric current through the victim's body. [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] fired a dart from one of her [[Mandalorian vambrace]]s against Commissioner [[Helgait]] after it was discovered that he was responsible for the malfunctioning of droids on the [[planet]] [[Plazir-15]].<ref name="Chapter 22" />
