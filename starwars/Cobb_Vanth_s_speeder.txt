'''Cobb Vanth's speeder''' was a custom-built [[repulsorcraft]] vehicle that was made from a modified [[620C racing engine|620C]] [[podracing]] [[engine]]. [[Cobb Vanth]], the [[marshal]] of the [[Tatooine]] [[City|town]] [[Freetown|Mos Pelgo]], used the [[speeder]] to travel the [[Jundland Wastes]] and protect his people.<ref name="DB" /> He [[pilot]]ed the craft during his [[Mission to Tatooine (Din Djarin)|attempts]] to [[Death|kill]] a [[krayt dragon]] that had terrorized his town<ref name="Chapter 9">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|9|the appearance of [[Cobb Vanth]]'s [[speeder]]}}</ref>
