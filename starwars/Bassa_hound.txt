'''Bassa hounds''' were a type of heavy-hoofed [[dog]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], a bassa hound belonged to the personal [[zoo]] of [[Harra]] the [[Hutt]] on the [[planet]] [[Vodran]]. By the [[New Republic Era]], bassa hounds were trained as [[hunting]] companions at [[Kergans Academy]]. In [[34 ABY]], [[Resistance]] member [[Mattis Banz]] went on a [[Mission to Vodran|mission]] to Vodran and had a run-in with a bassa hound and Harra's other creatures.

==Biology and appearance==
Bassa hounds were a type of [[dog]] distinguished by their heavy hooves. They could be trained as [[hunting]] companions.<ref name="Join the Resistance" />

==History==
In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], bassa hounds were mentioned in the phrase "Why keep a bassa hound and bark yourself?," which was considered by the [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Pan Eyta]] as he waited for his [[navigator]] to answer a [[Comm unit|communications]] call.<ref name="Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>

[[File:Harra_the_Hutt.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Harra the Hutt kept a bassa hond in her personal zoo.]]
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], at least one bassa hound was kept by [[Harra]] the [[Hutt]] in her personal [[zoo]] on the [[swamp]] [[planet]] [[Vodran]]. The animals were set loose after the Empire [[Imperial incursion|arrived]] on the planet, and Harra fled. By the [[New Republic Era]], bassa hounds were used in hunting lessons at the expensive [[Kergans Academy]] on the planet [[Kergans]]. The [[Zeltron]] [[Lorica Demaris]] attended the school and hated the activity.<ref name="Join the Resistance" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date 2">The mention and appearance of bassa [[Dog|hounds]] in ''[[Join the Resistance]]'' take place during the [[mission to Vodran]] which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]].</ref> during the [[Cold War]] between the [[Resistance]] and the [[First Order]], Resistance member [[Mattis Banz]] embarked on a [[Mission to Vodran|mission]] to Vodran and had a brief, dangerous encounter with Harra's former menagerie as the animals stampeded away from a pack of [[rancor]]s. A bassa hound was among the creatures, and it nearly took off Banz's head when it galloped by.<ref name="Join the Resistance" />

==Behind the scenes==
A bassa hound first appeared in ''[[Join the Resistance]]'', a junior novel written by [[Ben Acker]] and [[Ben Blacker]] and published in [[2017]] as the first volume in the ''[[Star Wars: Join the Resistance]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Join the Resistance" />
