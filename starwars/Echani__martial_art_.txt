'''Echani''' was a form of [[Unarmed combat|martial art]]. It was taught to every [[stormtrooper]] and [[Imperial officer]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] as part of standard training, which also included the art of [[Zavat]] and the [[Imperial Combat Exercises]]. [[Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]]'s personal [[Aide|assistant]] [[Adea Rite]] attempted to use her training in the arts on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]] in order to subdue the escaped [[prisoner]] [[Wedge Antilles]]; however, she was defeated when Antilles accidentally shot her with a [[blaster]] during the brawl.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> The energetic fighting style of former [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] and [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Magistrate]] [[Morgan Elsbeth]] combined the [[Echani firefist]] style with [[Dathomiri mistdance]] and [[Coruscant]]i [[Hijkata]].<ref name="MandalorianVG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> The [[Praetorian Guard|Elite Praetorian Guards]] of the [[First Order]] were trained in Echani unarmed forms, along with other forms of martial arts.<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

The art shared its name with the [[Echani]] [[species]].<ref name="Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
''Echani'' was first introduced in the [[Crimson Empire 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Crimson Empire]]'', which was written by [[Mike Richardson]] and [[Randy Stradley]]<ref name="Crimson">[[Crimson Empire 1|''Crimson Empire'' 1]]</ref> and published on [[December 10]], [[1997]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54039/star_wars_crimson_empire_1997_1|text=Star Wars: Crimson Empire (1997) #1}}</ref> It was [[canon]]ized by the novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], which was written by [[Chuck Wendig]] and released in [[2015]].<ref name="Aftermath" />
