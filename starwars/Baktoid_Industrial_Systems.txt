'''Baktoid Industrial Systems''' was a vehicle manufacturing company. It was founded following [[Baktoid Combat Automata]]'s absorption into the [[Galactic Empire]], by some of the company's former [[engineer]]s and executives. It produced the [[Versatile-Athletic Walker]], and was later absorbed into the Empire as well. Some of its employees were later offered asylum by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].

==History==
Following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the manufacturing company [[Baktoid Combat Automata]] was taken over and absorbed into the new Empire. Displeased with their new rulers, a number of Baktoid's leading [[engineer]]s and executives left to form a new company, Baktoid Industrial Systems.<ref name="Forged in Battle" />

One of the company's first products was the experimental [[Versatile-Athletic Walker]], an eye-catching [[walker]] inspired by the [[zalaaca]] [[species]] from the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. Only a small number of those walkers were ever produced, as the Galactic Empire absorbed the new company as well. Fleeing employees eventually met up with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], who offered them asylum from the Empire. Following these events, rumors began to spread that almost all of the Versatile walkers were donated to the [[Alliance Military]] as a sign of appreciation.<ref name="Forged in Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
Baktoid Industrial Systems was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Forged in Battle]]'',<ref name="Forged in Battle" /> a [[2016]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Soldier On!">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2016/8/17/soldier-on-1|text=Soldier On!}}</ref> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the ''[[Legacy Era Campaign Guide]]'', a [[2009]] hardcover supplement for [[Wizards of the Coast]]'s ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'', written by [[Rodney Thompson]], [[Sterling Hershey]], and [[Gary Astleford]].<ref name="Legacy Era">''[[Legacy Era Campaign Guide]]''</ref>
