'''Cularin Emergency Announcement''' is an article written by [[August Hahn]] and [[Cynthia Hahn]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] [[Wizards.com|website]] on [[December 8]], [[2005]] and took the form of a message holo-recording made by the [[Senator/Legends|Senator]] [[Lavina Durada-Vashne Wren|Lavina Wren]], announcing the succession of the [[Cularin system/Legends|Cularin system]] from the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]].
