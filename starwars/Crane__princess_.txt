'''Crane''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[princess]]. Orphaned and exiled following the [[Destruction of the Kingdom of Biwaji|destruction]] of [[Kingdom of Biwaji|her kingdom]] by the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]], she later became an operative for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]]. Her childhood guardian was the [[astromech droid]] [[R9-TR2|Tor-Tu]], who remained her companion and partner in the Rebellion. He could transform into mechanical [[armor]] that allowed Crane to fly. While stationed at a secret [[Unidentified rebel base (Crane)|rebel base]] on the [[planet]] [[Joetz]], Crane and Tor-Tu tracked Imperial operations in the area. While conducting a [[Reconnaissance mission on Joetz|reconnaissance mission]], the pair rescued [[Woopas]], the only survivor of an Imperial attack on a [[Unidentified village (Joetz)|village]] in the planet's polar region. When they encountered an enormous Imperial [[Dual excavator drill-equipped mining crawler|mining crawler]] and its [[AT-ATQ|walker]] escorts, Crane had Tor-Tu engage his armor mode, which allowed her to destroy the entire force and end the Imperial occupation of the planet.

==Biography==
[[File:BiwajiBurns-TheSongOfFourWings.png|left|thumb|250px|Crane and her mother watch Biwaji burning.]]
Crane was a princess of the [[Kingdom of Biwaji]], where she lived with [[Crane's mother|her mother]]. When she was just a child, the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] [[Destruction of the Kingdom of Biwaji|attacked and destroyed]] the kingdom. As fires blazed up all around them, her mother told Crane to escape without her. She told the droid Tor-Tu to take care of her daughter, then stayed behind to await her fate. Crane never saw her again.<ref name="Four Wings" />

When Crane was older, she joined the Rebellion against the Empire. She was with the Rebels at [[Unidentified rebel base (Crane)|their hidden base]] on the planet Joetz. An Imperial force landed in a snowy polar region of the planet. The base's commander, [[Admiral]] [[Basil Kiucee|Kiucee]], sent Crane on a [[Reconnaissance mission on Joetz|reconnaissance mission]] to scout out the Empire's activities and determine whether the Rebels had been discovered. Crane drove out in a [[speeder bike]] with Tor-Tu riding along in the sidecar. On the way, Admiral Kiucee warned Crane not to make too much noise; she should keep her engine quiet and refrain from playing music in Tor-Tu's [[record cube]] feature. Crane played her music anyway. She also corrected the admiral when he addressed her as "Princess:" the kingdom was gone, she argued, and it was better to leave it in the past.<ref name="Four Wings" />

[[File:TheSongOfFourWings-thumb.png|thumb|right|250px|Crane, Tor-Tu, and Woopas escape the village.]]
Crane and Tor-Tu came upon a [[Unidentified village (Joetz)|ruined village]], which she concluded must have been destroyed by the Empire. Under the floor of a house, they found [[Woopas]], a tiny [[Gigoran]] child cowering in fear amidst four-winged [[paper crane]]s that he had made. Crane recognized them as representations of his people's guardian deity. She persuaded the child to trust her and follow her to safety. Outside, the three fought their way past a patrol of Imperial [[Cold weather assault trooper|Snowtroopers]] and escaped in the speeder bike. Out on the frozen plain, they discovered an enormous Imperial [[Dual excavator drill-equipped mining crawler|mining crawler]]. Tor-Tu scanned the monstrosity and determined that the Empire was using it to mine the valuable hyperfuel [[coaxium]]. Realizing that the Imperials had come to Joetz to extract the mineral, not hunt for the Rebels, Crane reported back to Admiral Kiucee.<ref name="Four Wings" />

Just then, a pair of [[AT-ATQ|modified AT-AT walkers]] emerged from under the ice and began firing on Crane's speeder. They sparked memories of the walkers that had destroyed her home. Evading their fire, she spoke to Woopas and gathered her courage. They both survived for a reason, she said, before moving to attack the walkers. When the bike's blaster proved ineffective, Woopas used his [[sling]] to hurl a [[thermal detonator]]. Crane flew close to one of the walkers and Woopas launched the explosive into its neck joint, destroying it. Now the second walker opened fire with [[seismic charge]]s that sent Crane's speeder hurtling through the air. She called on Tor-Tu to engage his armor mode. The droid divided into segments that re-formed around Crane's body, equipping her with a flying suit of mechanized armor. The armor's four wings were tipped with [[laser cannon]]s, but a malfunction prevented them from firing. Woopas, riding on the underside, was able to drew on [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis/Legends|telekinetically]] reconnect a power cable. When the weapons came back online, Crane aimed at a small port on the side of the mining crawler and fired. The blast destroyed the transport, and its explosion took out the second walker.<ref name="Four Wings" />

After the battle, the Rebels had to evacuate the planet. Crane flew [[Crane's YT-2400 light freighter|her freighter]] away from the planet with Tor-Tu and Woopas riding with her. She was again listening to music: this time to a [[Unidentified song (Crane)|song her mother had sung]] back home. Now she wanted to remember what was lost. Speaking with Admiral Kiucee about the battle, she told him that she had no idea how her lasers had fixed themselves. Tor-Tu then told her to look at Woopas: the child was elevating one of his paper cranes with the Force. Crane now understood what had happened. She smiled as she entered [[hyperspace]] to travel to their next mission with the Rebels.<ref name="Four Wings" />

==Personality and traits==
Early in her work with the Rebellion, Crane wanted to forget her past and concentrate on the mission at hand. She asked Admiral Kiucee not to use her royal title, and she kept only a few reminders of her childhood, including Tor-Tu and her hair tie. When she began to care for Woopas, who like her was the sole survivor of his people, her attitude began to change. Just as Woopas could draw strength from his people's legends, Crane saw that she could also find purpose and resolve in memories of the family and kingdom she had lost. She drew on this strength in her battle against the Empire, and later she had Tor-Tu play a song that her mother had sung long ago.<ref name="Four Wings" />

==Skills and abilities==
Princess Crane was an formidable warrior skilled in several forms of combat. While equipped with her droid Tor-Tu in his armor mode, she could prevail in aerial combat against much stronger foes. She could [[pilot]] a [[speeder bike]] in combat and was able to maneuver it to set up Woopas for a perfect shot with his sling. She was capable of fighting on the ground with her [[blaster]], even outnumbered. Besides her flying armor and speeder, she could pilot a [[freighter]] in space.<ref name="Four Wings" />

==Equipment==
[[File:CraneArmor-TheSongOfFourWings.png|left|thumb|300px|Crane flies with Tor-Tu.]]
Crane wore pieces of salvaged [[stormtrooper armor]]. Her [[goggles]] were made of the eyepieces of a trooper's helmet, and she wore the base of the helmet around her neck. Other pieces covered her chest and upper back, which she wore under a white cloak.<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-visions/the-song-of-four-wings-episode-guide|text="The Song of Four Wings" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars: Visions Volume 3}}</ref> She carried a [[Defender sporting blaster pistol]].<ref name="Filmmaker Focus">{{SWYouTube|series=Star Wars: Visions Filmmaker Focus|text=THE SONG OF FOUR WINGS|video=v7TAhtiWDok}}</ref>  

Crane's [[astromech droid|astromech]], [[R9-TR2]], was a one-of-a-kind droid that had been with her since her childhood. Besides his unusual ability play [[record cube]]s, "Tor-Tu" could engage a special armor mode that transformed Crane for aerial combat. Wearing the droid as armor, Crane was equipped with four wings attached to thrusters and tipped with [[laser cannon]]s, resembling a miniature [[X-wing starfighter]]. She had an additional maneuvering thruster on each leg. During flight she wore a [[flight helmet]] with a built-in [[heads-up display]]. Crane also [[Pilot|piloted]] a [[Crane's YT-2400 light freighter|YT-2400 light freighter]] and a speeder bike. The speeder had a sidecar that could accommodate Tor-Tu and space to stash her flight helmet.<ref name="Four Wings" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crane appeared in "[[The Song of Four Wings]]", a [[Canon|non-canon]] short film directed by [[Hiroyasu Kobayashi]] that released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume ]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Project Studio Q]], released on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]]. The character was first introduced at the ''Star Wars: Visions'' panel at [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan]] on [[April 19]], 2025.<ref name="Day 3">{{SWYouTube|p20GM68wyC4|Star Wars Celebration LIVE! 2025 - DAY 3}}</ref> She is voiced by [[Manaka Iwami]] in the original [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]] version and by [[Stephanie Hsu]] in the English dub.<ref name="Getty">{{Getty|subdomain=assets.dam|url=s75j56ckkw879rfqfnxr49s/v/1291165528/original/VISIONS%20SEASON%203%20Final%20Production%20Brief.pdf?Expires=1761418361&KeyName=gcs-bfldr-prod&Signature=yVNCOpLKX0CT4Ufu0FjQLOANhQs=|text=VISIONS SEASON 3 Final Production Brief|date=2025-10-22|nolive=1}}</ref>

Kobayashi first conceived of Crane as a ''Star Wars'' adaptation of the "[[wikipedia:ja:MS少女|mobile suit girl]]" character type, and her battle against the walkers and mining transport was imagined as one in which the mechs were the main characters.<ref name=CinemaToday>{{WebCite|url=https://www.cinematoday.jp/news/N0151763|text=『スター・ウォーズ』プリンセスがドロイドアーマー装着！メカ愛とロマンあふれるアニメ誕生の裏側|date=2025-11-08|work=CINEMATODAY|language=Japanese|archiveurl=https://archive.is/YrQhV}}</ref> Crane's design incorporates elements from ''Star Wars'' film characters, in particular [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Princess Leia]] and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]. Her defender blaster exactly duplicates the one Leia used in ''[[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|A New Hope]]'', while her costume and braid are more subtle nods to the character. Crane's goggles made of Stormtrooper armor match what Rey wore in ''[[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|The Force Awakens]]''.<ref name="Filmmaker Focus" /> Her flying mech armor gives her the appearance of a human [[X-wing starfighter]].<ref name=CinemaToday />
