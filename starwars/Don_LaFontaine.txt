'''Don LaFontaine''' was a voice actor famous for his work in over 5,000 movie trailers and hundreds of thousands of television and radio commercials. Known for his frequent use of the phrase "In a world...," LaFontane parodied himself on numerous occasions, including a [[Wikipedia:GEICO|GEICO]] commercial which LaFontaine said changed his life, having attached a face and name to his well-known voice.<ref name="ABC">{{WebCite|url=http://abcnews.go.com/Entertainment/Story?id=3038652&page=1|text=It's That Announcer Guy From the Movies|author=Lee, Rebecca|date=2007-04-13|archivedate=20080925035934}}</ref>

LaFontaine was the narrator of the [[1995]] VHS release of ''[[The Making of Star Wars]]'', which was a mail-in prize offered by the Kellogg Company. His voice replaced that of the original narrator, [[William Conrad]]. For the [[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition|Special Edition]] re-release of the [[original trilogy]] in [[1997]], LaFontaine recorded several TV-spots and commercials, as well the behind-the-scenes introductions for the VHS releases.
