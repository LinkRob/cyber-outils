The '''Chancellor's Archive''' was a memorial [[archive]] located in the auxiliary areas of the [[Emperor's office|Chancellor's suite]] in the [[Imperial Executive Building|Senate Office Building]]. It included files and correspondence of previous [[Supreme Chancellor]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]], which were stored in [[Hologram disc|holodiscs]] and were accessible only to the office's incumbent.<ref name="CL2016" />
