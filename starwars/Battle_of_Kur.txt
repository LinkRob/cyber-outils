The '''Battle of Kur''',<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> also known as the '''battle at Kur''',<ref name="THR 1" /> was a battle fought between an alliance of the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Jedi Order]] and forces from the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]] against [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s at the [[Kur Nebula]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> in [[232 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> The battle had been set up by [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]], who sent [[Tempest Runner]] [[Kassav Milliko]] and his [[Kassav's Tempest|Tempest]] to their [[death]]s in order to conceal the Nihil's true numbers. During the battle, the [[Trandoshan]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Sskeer]] lost an [[Limb|arm]], and Master [[Jora Malli]], the commander of the [[Jedi]] contingent, was killed.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> Afterwards, it was noted by [[Jedi]] including Master [[Avar Kriss]]<ref name="THR 1" /> and Sskeer's recently promoted former [[Padawan]] [[Keeve Trennis]] that Sskeer had been seriously affected by the battle, suffering trauma and acting unusually.<ref name="THR 2">[[The High Republic (2021) 2|''The High Republic'' (2021) 2]]</ref>

==Prelude==
Around [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[hyperspace]] travel in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] was disrupted by the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], an incident in which the [[freighter]] ''[[Legacy Run]]'' was destroyed in hyperspace near the [[Hetzal system]], causing its debris to fall into the [[Star system|system]], imperiling its forty billion inhabitants. Only a swift [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]] rescue effort prevented the system from being destroyed entirely. In the aftermath, more debris from the [[starship]] continued to [[Emergences|Emerge]] throughout the Outer Rim, prompting [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] to order extensive restrictions on hyperspace travel while a [[Emergences task force|task force]] investigated the cause of the catastrophe.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
