'''Efrem LX''' was a [[droid]] who was active during the [[time]] of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. In [[35 ABY]], Efrem LX was present in [[Kijimi City]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]] at the same [[time]] as several Resistance operatives undertook a [[Mission to Kijimi|mission]] on the planet. Shortly afterward, Kijimi was [[Destruction of Kijimi|destroyed]] as a show of force by the [[Sith Eternal]].

==Biography==
In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' places the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]]. As the Hosnian Cataclysm is dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'', the events of ''The Rise of Skywalker'', including Efrem LX's appearance, take place in [[35 ABY]].</ref> during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]],<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> Efrem LX<ref name="Topps" /> was present in [[Kijimi City]], a [[city]] on the [[Temperature|frigid]] [[planet]] of [[Kijimi]]. The city was under [[Occupation of Kijimi|occupation]] by the First Order regime, whose patrols displeased many of its citizens.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> During the [[time]] that Efrem LX was on Kijimi, the Resistance members [[Poe Dameron]], [[Finn]], and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] [[Mission to Kijimi|visited]] the planet in search of the [[droidsmith]] [[Babu Frik]]. As Efrem LX stood in the streets of Kijimi City, watching a passing group of First Order [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]], the Resistance operatives were led past the droid's location by [[Zorii Bliss]],<ref name="TROS" /> leader of the criminal gang known as the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

Shortly afterward, Efrem LX was present inside the [[Spice Runners' Den|den]] of the Spice Runners, watching as Bliss walked by with the Resistance members. Some time later, once the Resistance operatives had escaped Kijimi, the planet was targeted by the [[Sith Eternal]], a [[Sith cult|cult]] of [[Sith cultist|Sith loyalists]] led by the reborn [[Darth Sidious]], in a show of force against [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TROS" /> The [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Derriphan]]'' of the Sith Eternal's fleet, the [[Final Order]],<ref name="TROS VD" /> was dispatched to [[Destruction of Kijimi|destroy]] Kijimi, obliterating the planet with its [[axial superlaser]].<ref name="TROS" />

==Characteristics==
Efrem LX was a droid with a [[humanoid]] figure. The droid had a singular [[Color|blue]] [[photoreceptor]] on the right side of its face, and had plating colored dark gray and bronze.<ref name="Topps" />

==Equipment==
During the time of the war between the First Order and the Resistance,<ref name="TROS VD" /> Efrem LX possessed a [[blaster pistol]] and carried various pieces of equipment strapped to its torso and legs.<ref name="Topps" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|This guy is a some kind of military combat SWAT version of a protocol droid. If that can be a thing.|Jake Lunt Davies|<ref name="Instagram1" />}}
[[File:EfremLX_concept.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Concept art of Efrem LX by Jake Lunt Davies]]
Efrem LX appeared in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> The character was first identified on a card in the ''[[2019 Topps Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker Series 1]]'' trading card set, released by [[Topps|The Topps Company, Inc.]]<ref name="Topps" /> on the same day as the film.<ref name="CardCon">{{CardCon|url=2019-topps-star-wars-the-rise-of-skywalker-trading-cards|text=2019 Topps Star Wars The Rise of Skywalker Series 1 Trading Cards}}</ref> Efrem LX's card in [[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]] capitalizes the droid's name as "Efrem Lx."<ref name="SWCT">{{Topps|set=2019 Topps Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker Series 1|cardname=Efrem Lx}}</ref>

Concept art for Efrem LX was created by [[Jake Lunt Davies]], who described the character as a [[Wikipedia:SWAT|SWAT]] military combat version of a [[protocol droid]]. [[Mohsen Nouri]] portrayed the droid in ''The Rise of Skywalker'', wearing a full-body suit. Various pieces of the suit, including the head, were modeled and sculpted by [[Andre Gilbert]] and [[Scott Riley]],<ref name="Instagram1" /> who used digital assets for [[C-3PO]] as a starting point, adding details from Lunt Davies' original design.<ref name="Instagram2">{{Instagram|username=jakeluntdavies|url=p/CC_cxcCjPZA|name=[[Jake Lunt Davies]]|date=2020-07-23|quote=More of EFREM LX. The design process can be quite fluid and organic. @mentaltentacle and @clayofthejackal were using c3p0 digital assets as the starting blocks for our pieces and adding the details from my design by eye. At various stages I'd get screengrabs and do a paint over to make aesthetic adjustments or fill in missing design elements.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/mIvDb}}</ref> [[Olivia Whitaker]] was responsible for molding and casting in fiberglass, while [[Fiona Pollard]] created the body suit containing various pieces of technology. The final suit was assembled and painted by [[Sean Schofield]].<ref name="Instagram1" />
