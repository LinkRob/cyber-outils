'''Assemblers''' were a group that [[information broker]] [[Prima Wessex]] thought weren't worth the attention of the [[Whisper Network]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Assemblers were first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and released in [[2018]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> The group originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[1998]] novel ''[[The Mandalorian Armor]]'', which was written by [[K. W. Jeter]].<ref name="Mandalorian Armor">''[[The Mandalorian Armor]]''</ref>
