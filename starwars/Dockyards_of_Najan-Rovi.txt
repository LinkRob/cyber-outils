The '''Dockyards of Najan-Rovi''' were an assortment of [[Shipyard|dockyards]] [[orbit]]ing the [[gas giant]] [[Najan-Rovi]]. It harbored many [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] luxury [[transport]]s for the higher ups in the Empire. In [[3 ABY]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliances]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] carried out [[Operation Ringbreaker]] where they attacked many Imperial places including [[Mardona III]], [[Xagobah]] and [[Sullust]]. The Dockyards of Najan-Rovi were one of the early targets and the Sixty-First came with their [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]'', its escort the [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunship]] ''[[Apailana's Promise]]'' and two [[X-wing starfighter]]s. The dockyards lacked a [[stormtrooper]] [[battalion]] and relied on [[Imperial Navy Trooper|Imperial Navy troopers]] and [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] and it [[Attack on the dockyards of Najan-Rovi|fell]] in less than a [[Standard day|day]]. The Sixty-First left what was left of it with almost a hundred burning luxury transports.<ref name="Twilight Company" />
