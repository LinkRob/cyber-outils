An '''accu-accelerator''' was a component used in [[blaster]]s, [[engine]]s,<ref name="USW" /> and [[speeder]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> Accu-accelerators were used in [[starship]] [[ion engine]]s. Some were scrounged by [[Jawa]] [[scavenger]]s to construct their [[ion blaster]]s; the components, along with a [[droid]] [[restraining bolt]], were added to the [[blaster]]'s firing mechanism.<ref name="USW" /> The accelerators were also used in [[Moll K-19T power generator]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Accu-accelerators were first mentioned in the [[canon]] continuity in the [[2015]] [[Dorling Kindersley]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''.<ref name="USW" /> They later appeared as upgrade materials in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

Accu-accelerators were first mentioned in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[1991]] roleplaying supplement, [[Cracken's Rebel Field Guide (sourcebook)|''Cracken's Rebel Field Guide'']].<ref name="CRFG">[[Cracken's Rebel Field Guide (sourcebook)|''Cracken's Rebel Field Guide'']]</ref>
