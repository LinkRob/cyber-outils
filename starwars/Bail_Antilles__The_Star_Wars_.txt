'''Bail Antilles''' was a [[Human/Legends|human]] galactic trader and a confederate of the [[Aquillian Ranger/Legends|Aquillian Ranger]] [[Clieg Whitsun]]. He was as a rugged-looking man wearing [[Hair/Legends|fur]] and golden ornaments.

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Whitsun walks into the glass and chrome splendor of one of the famous nightclubs of Alderaan. He moves to the long mirrored bar, and sits next to a rough-looking man, BAIL ANTILLES, dressed in the distinctive gold and furs of the Galactic traders.|Extract from "The Star Wars: Rough Draft"|<ref name="rough">[[The Star Wars rough draft|''The Star Wars'' rough draft]]</ref>}}
The name "Bail Antilles" first appeared in [[May]] [[1974]], in the 132-page [[The Star Wars rough draft|rough draft]] to the film that would become [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="rough" /> [[1975|One year later]], in the ''[[Adventures of the Starkiller, Episode I: The Star Wars]]'', Antilles became an Aquillian Ranger himself and the co-[[Pilot|pilot]] of the story's main protagonist, [[Luke Skywalker/Legends#Origins of Luke|Luke Starkiller]].<ref name="Second draft">''[[Adventures of the Starkiller, Episode I: The Star Wars]]''</ref> The revised fourth draft established the character of "Bail Antilles, [[Viceroy]] and Chairman of the Alderaan system" as the father of [[Princess/Legends|Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] from the planet [[Alderaan]].<ref name="ANHRFD">{{WebCite|url=https://www.starwarz.com/starkiller/star-wars-the-adventures-of-luke-starkiller-revised-fourth-draft|text=A New Hope Revised Fourth Draft|archivedate=20240518134536}}</ref>

When Princess Leia's homeworld was renamed Alderaan, the name "[[Ogana Major|Ogana]]" was slightly respelled and attributed to the royal family.<ref name="Alderaan Databank">{{DB|location|alderaan|Alderaan}}</ref> Bail Antilles was accordingly renamed [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], and went on to be mentioned by name in the [[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|novelization]] of ''A New Hope'',<ref name="ANHN">[[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|''Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope'' novelization]]</ref> although he was only referred to simply as Princess Leia's father in the film itself.<ref name="Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref> In [[Star Wars (1977) 2|''Star Wars'' (1977) 2]], the second issue of the film's [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] [[Star Wars (1977)|adaptation]] by [[Marvel Comics]], Leia's father was erroneously referred to as "Bail Antillies [sic], Viceroy of Alderaan."<ref name="Star Wars 2">[[Star Wars (1977) 2|''Star Wars'' (1977) 2]]</ref> Eventually, Bail Antilles was established as [[Bail Antilles|a separate character]] from Organa during the development of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]], specifically as Organa's predecessor as the senator of Alderaan.<ref name="MisID">{{Blog|holocron|29|Mistaken Identities}}</ref>
