'''Diane deGroat''' (born [[May 24]], [[1940s#1947|1947]])<ref name="USM" /> is an illustrator who painted watercolor illustrations for [[The Ewoks Join the Fight (storybook)|''The Ewoks Join the Fight'']], a retelling of the [[Battle of Endor/Legends|Battle of Endor]] from [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].
