'''Blood''' was a liquid substance present in most [[Sentience|sentient]] beings, though not all [[species]] had the same [[color]] of blood. [[Human]]s,<ref name="RotS">{{Film|III}}</ref> [[Togorian]]s,<ref name="Yoda 6">[[Yoda 6|''Yoda'' 6]]</ref> [[Aqualish]],<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> and [[Iktotchi]],<ref name="Identity Crisis">{{TBB|Identity Crisis}}</ref> for example, had red blood; while [[Rodian]]s,<ref name="TCWSphere">{{TCW|Sphere of Influence}}</ref> [[Duros]],<ref name="CotF">{{TCW|Children of the Force}}</ref> [[Trandoshan]]s,<ref name="TRB">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref> [[Lokori (species)|Lokori]],<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> and some [[Pyke]]s had green blood.<ref name="Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor">{{BOBF|Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor}}</ref> Other Pykes instead possessed yellow blood<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> as did [[Geonosian]]s.<ref name="Inquisitor">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''</ref> [[Kiffar]] had scarlet blood,<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> [[Devaronian]]s had black blood<ref name="NoP">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref> and [[Anzellan]]s had purple blood.<ref name="LOTJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

By testing a sample of someone's blood, it was possible to get a [[midi-chlorian]] count and determine how [[Force-sensitive|sensitive]] one was to [[the Force]].<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> Human blood was said to have a characteristic metallic taste.<ref name="Rebel Rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref> [[Bloodburn]] was a rare blood disease that affected some [[starship]] [[pilot]]s.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> The emotions of sentient beings of flesh and blood were driven by chemicals in their bloodstream. However, stone-based beings who had no blood, such as the [[Kakran]], processed information differently and did not experience strong emotions, such as fear.<ref name="SW72">[[Star Wars (2015) 72|''Star Wars'' (2015) 72]]</ref> [[Karabbac]] blood was used by [[Kaleesh]] [[Soldier|warriors]]<ref name="HC 8">{{HelmetCollectionCite|8|Helmets}}</ref> to smear<ref name="Star Wars 100 Objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref> their [[Kaleesh mask|fearsome masks]] as war paint<ref name="HC 8" /> before battle.<ref name="Star Wars 100 Objects" /> Poisons could also seriously harm humans if they entered into their bloodstream.<ref name="TMAG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref>
