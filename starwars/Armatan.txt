'''Armatan'''<ref name="Shades of Reason">{{TCW|Shades of Reason}}</ref> was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Mandalorian]] who served in the [[Mandalorian Government Council]] of the [[New Mandalorians]] during the [[Clone Wars]] as [[Minister of the Interior]]. Armatan served during the [[Mandalorian black market conspiracy|black market conspiracy]] instigated by [[Prime Minister]] [[Almec]],<ref name="Corruption" /> and was still serving during [[Death Watch]]'s [[takeover of Mandalore]] and Duchess Satine's removal of power.<ref name="Shades of Reason" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Armatan<ref name="Shades of Reason" /> first appeared in the episode "[[Corruption (episode)|Corruption]]" but was only identified as the Minister of Interior.<ref name="Corruption" /> He was later identified in the credits of "[[Shades of Reason]]" as Armatan.<ref name="Shades of Reason" /> He is voiced by [[Corey Burton]].
