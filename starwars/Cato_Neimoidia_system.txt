The '''Cato Neimoidia system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Quellor sector]] of the [[Colonies]] region. It contained the [[planet]] [[Cato Neimoidia]] and its two [[moon]]s, which [[orbit]]ed the [[star]] [[Neri]],<ref name="BMF 31" /> and sat at N-11 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="TFABG" /> One of the three [[Purse world|Purse Worlds]], Cato Neimoidia served as the headquarters of the [[Trade Federation]].<ref name="Ultimate" />
