"'''Cav'''" was the nickname of an [[Special Commando Advanced Recon Trooper]] in [[Task Force 99]], a [[squad]] within the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps]]. Cav served as the squad's melee specialist, capable of attacking targets in close quarters combat. Cav participated in Task Force 99's mission to the [[Ghost Moon]] in order to [[Skirmish on the Ghost Moon|eliminate]] a group of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebels]] hiding there.<ref name="SW21" />
