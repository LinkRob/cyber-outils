[[Gungan]] [[Professor]] [[Thaddeus Wolk]] was '''assassinated''' on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] by a [[Twi'lek]] [[Unidentified Twi'lek assassin|assassin]] who was part of the organized [[pirate]] group called the [[Nihil]]<ref name="OOtS" /> in [[231 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> Wolk was targeted by the Nihil for correctly theorizing that the marauders, with the help of his former student [[Chancey Yarrow]], had created [[hyperspace]] [[Gravity well projector]] [[superweapon]] in the [[Berenge sector]]. His theory was true, as Yarrow and the Nihil had built the [[Gravity's Heart]] [[Space station]] and superweapon there. Shortly before the assassination, Wolk's building on Coruscant had been bombed, leading to [[Jedi]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] and [[Imri Cantaros]] attempting to rescue Wolk from the building. However, Wolk was shot and killed by the assassin before they could escape.<ref name="OOtS" />
