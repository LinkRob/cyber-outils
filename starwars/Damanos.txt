'''Damanos''' was an ash-gray [[planet]]<ref name="Ghosts of Maul" /> within the [[Damanos system]], a [[star system]] in the [[Belderone sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> that contained a [[Sith castle]]. The former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]],<ref name="Ghosts of Maul" /> now turned [[crime lord]],<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> [[Mission to Damanos|visited]] the planet during the [[Imperial Era]], he discovered the secrets inside its [[Sith]] [[castle]].<ref name="Ghosts of Maul" />
