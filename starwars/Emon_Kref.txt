'''Emon Kref''' was a [[soldier]] who held the rank of [[private]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Kref was present during the [[Battle of Hoth]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="FACPOV" />

==Biography==
Emon Kref was born on [[Koshaga]]. The planet was engaged in a war that had lasted as long as anyone could remember, with his [[Emon Kref's father|father]] serving as a [[general]] in the [[Koshagan People's Movement]]. Eventually, the [[Galactic Empire]] arrived and deployed its forces, overwhelming the [[Lowlands (Koshaga)|Lowlands]] and taking many prisoners, including Kref's father. A [[Time|week]] later, Kref met a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] recruiter and joined the Alliance.<ref name="FACPOV" />

Kref eventually arrived at the Rebellion's [[Echo Base]] on [[Hoth]]. Kref utilized an [[A295 blaster rifle]] that had an unreliable [[Blaster power pack|energy pack]]. It was known to fall out and Kref was forced to create a makeshift bandage for his weapon using cord and tape. During the [[Battle of Hoth]], he received orders from [[Sergeant]] [[Trey Callum]] and fought alongside [[Andry Ked]] and helped him escape the battle on a transport after Ked was wounded.<ref name="FACPOV" />
