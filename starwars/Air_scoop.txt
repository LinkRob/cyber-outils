'''Air scoops''' were components that could be used in the construction of [[podracer]]s.<ref name="EoSaOV" /> The scoops acted as wind brakes and gave additional control when cornering in a vehicle. The [[Slavery|slave]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] modified his custom [[Anakin Skywalker's Podracer|podracer]] by adding triple [[Color|yellow]] air scoops ranged around both the [[Engine|primary turbine]] intake on each of its [[620C racing engine]]s.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" />

Skywalker had to wait a long time before finding metal plates and a hydraulic system that would be strong enough to make the scoops work. [[Gender|He]] eventually acquired hydraulic struts of [[Tyria/Canon|Tyrian]] make from a military surplus lot that his owner, the [[junk dealer]] [[Watto]], purchased from an [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[arms dealer]]. He then used the scoops in the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]]<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> that took place in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Air scoops first appeared in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] they were first identified in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]'', which was written by [[Landry Q. Walker]].<ref name="EoSaOV" /> The name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used in the 1999 reference book [[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary|''Star Wars: Episode I The Visual Dictionary'']], which was written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]].<ref name="TPMVD">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
