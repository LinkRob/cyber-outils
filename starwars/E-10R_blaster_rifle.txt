The '''E-10R blaster rifle''' was a model of [[blaster rifle]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]] used by [[range trooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]] on [[Vandor]] and [[Banas]].

==History==
Manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]], the [[rifle]] was a very robust variant of the [[E-10 blaster rifle]].<ref name="Official Guide" /> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> several [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[range trooper]]s were armed with [[BlasTech Industries|BlasTech]] E-10R [[blaster rifle]]s on [[Vandor]] during a [[Heist on Vandor|heist]] on a [[20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport|conveyex]] by [[Tobias Beckett]]'s [[Beckett's gang|gang]].<ref name="Solo: A Star Wars Story">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> A pair of range troopers stationed at [[Sienar Test Facility 73]] in [[4 BBY]] carried E-10Rs.<ref name="One Year Later" /> During the [[mission to Banas]] at some point between [[1 ABY]] and [[2 ABY|3 ABY]]<ref>As ''[[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|Star Wars Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back]]'' takes place in [[3 ABY]] and [[1 ABY]] is equivalent to immediately after ''[[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope]]'', the events of ''[[Hyperspace Stories 2]]'' and the [[Mission to Banas]] must take place between 1 ABY and 3 ABY.</ref>, range troopers under [[Commodore]] [[Kurtruk]] wielded E-10Rs against [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="HS2">[[Hyperspace Stories 2|''Hyperspace Stories'' 2]]</ref>
