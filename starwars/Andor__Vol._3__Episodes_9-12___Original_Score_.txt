'''''Andor'': Vol. 3 (Episodes 9-12) [Original Score]''' is the soundtrack for the ninth to twelfth episodes of [[Andor Season 1|Andor]]: "[[Nobody's Listening!]]," "[[One Way Out]]," "[[Daughter of Ferrix]]," and "[[Rix Road (episode)|Rix Road]]." It was composed by [[Nicholas Britell]] and released for digital streaming on [[December 9]], [[2022]].<ref name="Vol3 Announcement" />
