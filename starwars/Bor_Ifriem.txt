'''Bor Ifriem''' was a bor of the [[Mairan]] [[species]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]] for interrogations. Around [[1 ABY]], the bor was used to interrogate [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]], an inmate of the [[wreckage-prison]] [[Accresker Jail]]. The mental [[Torture|torturing]] from Bor Ifriem led Aphra to reveal the infestation of the [[fungus]] [[gundravian hookspore]]s in the [[prison]].

The Empire resultantly abandoned Accresker Jail and left Bor Ifriem half frozen there. The [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] soon began searching for Aphra's companion [[Magna Tolvan]] aboard the wreckage-prison after she had revealed her knowledge of a conspiracy regarding the [[Sith]] that Aphra had told her. Tolvan resultantly had Aphra alter her memory using Bor Ifriem to convince her she had killed her cohort, the bor being [[Death|murdered]] afterwards.

==Biography==
===Torture of Aphra===
{{Quote|You know the ''bor'' can do far more than merely ''extract'' memories, inmate. He could ''delete'' your mother, at our command. He could ''rewrite'' her. Change her into a ''devil. Propagandize your entire ''childhood''. He could leave you unable to trust the veracity of ''anything'' within your mind.|The interrogation officer, to Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 22" />}}
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Bor Ifriem was one of three bors of the<ref name="Aphra 21" /> [[Sentience|semi-sentient]]<ref name="Star Wars Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> [[Mairan]] [[species]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]] for carrying out interrogations. When not in use, the bor was frozen in [[Water|ice]]<ref name="Aphra 21" /> using a [[coolant grille]].<ref name="Aphra 24">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 24|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 24]]</ref> Around [[1 ABY]],<ref name="1 ABY">{{Aphra2016Date|21}}</ref> Bor Ifriem was defrosted at the Imperial [[wreckage-prison]] [[Accresker Jail]] for the interrogation of the inmate [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]],<ref name="Aphra 21" /> known as "Joystick Chevron" to the Imperial personnel there. An [[Unidentified Imperial interrogation officer|interrogation officer]] at the [[prison]] and his [[Unidentified lieutenant (Accresker Jail)|lieutenant]] had found Aphra was hiding something big from them, but since the inmate was managing to resist their probes, the lead interrogation officer chose to elevate their form of [[torture]].<ref name="Aphra 20">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 20|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 20]]</ref>

[[File:BorIfriemLikesYou-2016DoctorAphra22.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Chelli Aphra is subjected to Bor Ifriem]]
After Bor Ifriem was freed from the ice,<ref name="Aphra 21" /> Aphra was subjected to the creature, the interrogation officer remarking that the bor liked her. The creature embraced the inmate with his tentacles and began reading her mind, feeding on the inmate's resistance to him. The interrogation officer bragged about the effects to "Joystick Chevron," asserting that the Mairan race had a gift of "emotional flagellation." Bor Ifriem put Aphra through flashbacks of all the actions she felt guilty and shameful about, the officer telling her that confession would be the only relief she would find from it.<ref name="Aphra 22" />

Aphra attempted to dissuade the interrogation officer and his [[lieutenant]] by claiming she was thinking about her [[Lona Aphra|mother]]. As such, the officer threatened the inmate to have Bor Ifriem delete her memories of her [[Parent|mum]] or alternatively rewrite them, the Mairan inducing illusions in Aphra's [[Brain|mind]] of her mother in alternate realities. The inmate began slowly breaking into a confession, more visions of her past flowing through her mind. She finally confessed that a colony of the highly infectious [[fungus]] [[gundravian hookspore]]s was spreading at Accresker Jail, the lieutenant confirming that she was telling the truth.<ref name="Aphra 22" />

===Dispose of after use===
{{Quote|But ''Bor Ifriem'' here? He can read minds, Tolvan. He can change minds&mdash;and he can wipe them. I have to ''forget'' about everything I knew about Vader and his plans.|Aphra tries to convince Magna Tolvan to have Bor Ifriem wipe some of her memory|<ref name="Aphra 25">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 25|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 25]]</ref>}}
After learning of the hookspores, the Empire chose to [[Skirmish aboard Accresker Jail|decommission]] Accresker Jail and send it on a collision course with the<ref name="Aphra 22" /> [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]-sympathizing [[planet]] [[Tiferep Major]]. Bor Ifriem was left behind, improperly frozen, when the Imperial personnel of Accresker Jail's [[prison-tug]] abandoned the [[cruiser]] and allowed it to crash into the wreckage-prison. When breaking into the wreckage of the prison-tug, Aphra and her companions, "[[Cornelius Evazan|Lopset Yas]]," [[Magna Tolvan]] and [[Sana Starros]], discovered the bor half-buried in an ice-block, Tolvan calling it sloppy work.<ref name="Aphra 23" />

[[File:TolvanMemoires-2016DoctorAphra25.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Magna Tolvan convinced Chelli Aphra to have Bor Ifriem alter her memories.]]
As a last resort, Tolvan persuaded the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to come to Accresker Jail after revealing to him over comm that she knew of his conspiracy to replace [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]],<ref name="Aphra 24">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 24|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 24]]</ref> which she had been informed of by Aphra.<ref name="Aphra 21" /> Vader temporarily stopped the wreckage-prison's collision course with the [[tractor beam]] of his [[flagship]], the ''[[Executor]]'', and boarded Accresker Jail in search of Tolvan. Fearing Vader's wrath, Aphra led Tolvan to Bor Ifriem,<ref name="Aphra 24" /> asking her companion to erase her knowledge of Vader's conspiracy using the bor.<ref name="Aphra 25" />

Tolvan, though, convinced Aphra to have the former's memory altered by Bor Ifriem to believe she had killed the latter in order to purge Vader's lead on Aphra. Aphra followed through, using the bor on her companion. Once Tolvan's memory was altered, Aphra left her for Vader, while [[Death|killing]] Bor Ifriem. When the [[Sith]] [[Lord]] found Tolvan, he heard the latter's account of false events, being tricked by it. After departing Accresker Jail, he allowed the wreckage-prison to be released from the tractor beam and crash into Tiferep Major with the dead Bor Ifriem aboard.<ref name="Aphra 25" />
