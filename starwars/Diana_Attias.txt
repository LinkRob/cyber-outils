'''Diana Attias''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] [[Wikipedia:Editing|book editor]] who is credited for co-editing ''[[The Empire Strikes Back Notebook]]'' ([[November]] [[1980]]) with [[Lindsay Smith]]. Attias is the wife of prominent [[Wikipedia:Television director|television director]] [[Wikipedia:Dan Attias|Daniel Attias]] and the mother of [[Wikipedia:David Attias|David Attias]], a former [[Wikipedia:University of California, Santa Barbara|University of California, Santa Barbara]] student who killed four people with his vehicle in the [[2001]] [[Wikipedia:Isla Vista massacre|Isla Visla massacre]].<ref name="Murder">{{WebCite|url=https://www.sfgate.com/news/article/I-Am-Angel-of-Death-Cops-suspect-ramming-was-2948049.php|text='I Am Angel of Death' / Cops suspect ramming was intentional|author=Finz, Stacy|date=2001-02-26|archivedate=20191231014124}}</ref>
