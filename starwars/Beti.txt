'''Beti''' was a [[doll]] owned by the [[human]] [[Sylvestri Yarrow]] during her [[Youngling|childhood]]. When her [[Parent|mother]] [[Chancey Yarrow|Chancey]] later gifted her a [[blaster rifle]], Sylvestri named it "[[Beti (rifle)|Beti]]" after the doll as a joke.<ref name="OotS" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> Sylvestri recalled naming the blaster after the doll after she pulled the [[weapon]] out of its [[holster]] when she, her co-[[pilot]] [[Neeto Janajana]], and the [[security droid]] [[M-227]] discussed their options when the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s wanted to scan their [[Switchback|ship]] while in the [[Berenge sector]].<ref name="OotS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Beti was mentioned in the young-adult novel [[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows|''Out of the Shadows'']], written by [[Justina Ireland]] for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project<ref name="OotS" /> and published on [[July 27]], [[2021]], as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Chronological Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> The doll's name was spelled "Betty" in a preview chapter published in the novel ''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]'', written by [[Claudia Gray]]<ref name="ITD">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref> and released on [[February 2]] of the same year.<ref name="Chronological Guide" />
