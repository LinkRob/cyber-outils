{{Quote|I'm searching for a way to heal ''Nal Hutta''. A legend speaks of a plant with miraculous healing properties that grows here on Eroudac. The ''quiraawa flower.|Churo to Sul-Nath-Rune, on Eroudac's quiraawa flower|<ref name="Heart" />}}
'''Eroudac''' was a terrestrial [[planet]] located in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] and [[the Slice]] regions of [[the galaxy]]. It was [[orbit]]ed by a [[Color|pink]] [[Eroudac's moon|moon]], and its terrain featured [[crystal]] [[mountain]]s and [[Ocean|seas]]. Due to [[Standard century|centuries]] of natural disasters, the world's healing [[quiraawa flower]] eventually became widely considered extinct. However, some legends claimed the [[flower]]s had been preserved in some form.

During the [[High Republic Era]], a [[Eroudac Jedi temple|Jedi temple]] operated on Eroudac. Several [[Jedi Master]]s and [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] were stationed there by [[230 BBY]], including the [[youngling]] [[Jamil Sollis]]. However, the [[Jedi]] evacuated the [[Jedi temple|temple]] in 230 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] following the [[Jedi High Council]]'s recall of all Jedi to the [[Core Worlds]] planet [[Coruscant]], upsetting Sollis as he struggled to adjust away from home. In [[228 BBY]], [[Churo]] the [[Hutt]] traveled to Eroudac in search of a quiraawa flower to heal his [[homeworld]], [[Nal Hutta]]. With the help of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Sul-Nath-Rune]], Churo eventually found a group of native [[Heart of Eroudac]], who explained that he could not take a quiraawa flower from Eroudac without harming the world. Knowing this, the Hutt decided to leave Eroudac with Rune to seek a different solution to help Nal Hutta.

At some point no earlier than [[21 ABY]], the [[Garsea family]] settled on Eroudac and established themselves in the [[Eroudac Citadel]]. The family's [[Gender|daughter]], [[Izal Garsea]], attended a [[Eroudac academy|local academy]] while there to appease her [[Izal Garsea's father|father]] but often spent most of her time in a local [[Eroudac spaceport|spaceport]] instead. Sometime after her [[parent]]s [[Death|died]], Izal left the planet after failing a [[Smuggler|smuggling]] job for [[Unidentified genetic science professor|one]] of her academy [[professor]]s.

==Description==
{{Quote|It had ancient ruins and a pink moon.|Izal Garsea talking about Eroudac|<ref name="ACOF" />}}
[[File:Eroudac-GETravelersGuide.jpg|thumb|right|270px|Eroudac's location in the Mid Rim]]
Eroudac was a small terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="ACOF" /> located in the [[Doldur sector]]'s [[Eroudac system]], in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]] regions of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TheSlice" /> The planet was situated in grid square P-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Eroudac was [[orbit]]ed by a [[Color|pink]]-colored [[Eroudac's moon|moon]] that shone light down on the planet below<ref name="BTN" /> as well as at least one other [[moon]]. The world's terrain featured multicolored<ref name="Heart" /> [[crystal]] [[mountain]]s, [[Temperature|warm]] [[Ocean|seas]],<ref name="BTN" /> and highlands. [[Plant|Flora]] included the [[quiraawa flower]], a type of [[flower]] with healing properties, as well as other [[tree]]s and other ground vegetation. Eroudac's fauna included multiple varieties of colorful [[insect]]s and other creatures. The world had a pink<ref name="Heart" /> Type I [[atmosphere]] breathable to [[human]]s,<ref name="Atmosphere" /> [[Hutt]]s, [[Cerean]]s, and [[Heart of Eroudac]].<ref name="Heart" />

==History==
===Mountain Jedi temple===
{{Quote|I've heard of it. But they are said to have become extinct after Eroudac's centuries of natural disasters.|Sul-Nath-Rune about the quiraawa flower|<ref name="Heart" />}}
For many [[Standard century|centuries]], Eroudac suffered from natural disasters, which threatened to drive the quiraawa flowers on the world to extinction. According to one legend, the last remaining quiraawa flower on Eroudac was preserved by a [[Unidentified priestess|priestess]] at the top of one of the world's mountains. Another version of the legend claimed that the flowers only appeared once in a [[Standard decade|decade]] under the light of Eroudac's waning moons. Both versions were known to some individuals<ref name="Heart" /> by [[228 BBY]],<ref name="HeartDate">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}} "[[The Adventures of Churo the Hutt and the Heart of Eroudac]]" establishes its events occur after the events of ''Beware the Nameless'', therefore meaning they must take place no earlier than 228 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. ''[[The High Republic: A Valiant Vow]]'' depicts [[Churo]] working as the protégé of [[Glenna Kip]], who he had not heard of during "The Adventures of Churo the Hutt and the Heart of Eroudac." Therefore, "The Adventures of Churo the Hutt and the Heart of Eroudac" must be set before ''A Valiant Vow''. The events of ''A Valiant Vow'' take place nearly two years after the destruction of Starlight Beacon, meaning they and the events of "The Adventures of Churo the Hutt and the Heart of Eroudac" must also take place in 228 BBY per the aforementioned reasoning.</ref> by which time the quiraawa flower became widely considered extinct.<ref name="Heart" /> By the [[High Republic Era]], Eroudac was home to a [[Eroudac Jedi temple|temple]] of the [[Jedi Order]], which was built into the side of one of the world's crystal mountains.<ref name="BTN" /> By [[230 BBY]],<ref name="BTNDate">Per the reasoning [[:Template:StarlightDate|here]], the events of ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', which take place a [[Standard year|year]] and a half after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]] of [[Starlight Beacon]], must take place in [[228 BBY]]. As ''Beware the Nameless'' establishes that [[Jamil Sollis]] grew up at the [[Eroudac Jedi temple]] prior to the [[Jedi Order]]'s recall to [[Coruscant]], an event ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]], he must have been stationed there by 230 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> several [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] and [[Jedi Master]]s were present there, though the [[Jedi temple|temple's]] population was notably less than that of<ref name="BTN" /> the [[Core Worlds]] planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]]'s [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] at that [[time]].<ref name="BTN" />

[[File:JamilSollis-BewaretheNameless.png|thumb|left|260px|Jamil Sollis (pictured) lived in Eroudac's Jedi temple during the High Republic Era.]]
One of the [[Jedi]] [[youngling]]s who considered Eroudac home was [[Jamil Sollis]], who received rigorous [[Jedi training|training]] from the temple's Jedi Masters. While there, he stayed in the facility's dormitories and enjoyed looking up at the pink light of Eroudac's moon through a skylight. At one point, Sollis also swam in Eroudac's warm seas and experienced more chilling [[water]]s pushed in by a wave.<ref name="BTN" /> In 230 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="BTNDate" /> the [[Jedi High Council]] recalled all Jedi to Coruscant and implemented the [[Guardian Protocols]], hoping to protect the Jedi Order against the dangers of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] and their [[Living Force|Force]]-[[Diet|consuming]] [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures.<ref name="THRCE">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Sollis and the other younglings on Eroudac evacuated the world with their Jedi Masters aboard a [[Starship|ship]] and were informed of the threat they faced as they traveled to Coruscant.<ref name="BTN" />

While stationed in Coruscant's Jedi temple<ref name="BTN" /> in 228 BBY,<ref name="BTNDate" /> Sollis reminisced on missing Eroudac and its temple, having felt out of place and unable to focus in his new environment. The youngling later participated in a [[Mission on Palagosal|mission]] to the planet [[Palagosal]], often thinking about the crystal mountains of his home when overwhelmed. He felt that his fellow youngling [[Tep Tep]]&mdash;who had been displaced from her original [[Valo Jedi temple|temple]] on<ref name="BTN" /> the Outer Rim planet<ref name="THRCE" /> [[Valo]]&mdash;understood what it felt like to miss home and sought her out to talk about his feelings.<ref name="BTN" />

===Search for healing===
{{Quote|I believe it is the will of the Force that our paths have crossed. Out of everyone on the planet, it is you who helped me while I was injured. I will repay your kindness.|Sul-Nath-Rune to Churo, while helping him open a cavern oasis in the Quiraawa Mountains|<ref name="Heart" />}}
[[File:ChuroSulNathRuneEroudac-THRAAnnualPhaseIII.jpg|thumb|right|275px|Churo and Sul-Nath-Rune on Eroudac]]
Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="HeartDate" /> the Hutt [[scientist]] [[Churo]] looked for a way to heal<ref name="Heart" /> his [[homeworld]]<ref name="BTN" /> [[Nal Hutta]], which had been [[Attack on Nal Hutta|infested]] by [[Drengir]],<ref name="Heart" /> a type of [[carnivorous plant]].<ref name="BTN" /> As part of his endeavor, he spent time researching at the [[library]] of [[Senator]]<ref name="Heart" /> [[Rina Greylark]],<ref name="BTN" /> learning of the Eroudac legend of a [[priestess]] preserving the world's last quiraawa flower on a mountaintop. Around the same time, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Sul-Nath-Rune]] and his [[Sul-Nath-Rune's unit|unit]] visited Eroudac on a [[Relief mission to Eroudac|relief mission]] to the world with the [[Republic Defense Coalition]]. During the mission, the Jedi became separated from his unit and was injured.<ref name="Heart" />

Churo later traveled to Eroudac in his starship, the [[Glowworm (ship)|''Glowworm'']], to search for the flower. There, he encountered a weakened Rune, who approached Churo for help. After treating the Jedi with a [[Medpac|medkit]], the Hutt told him about the versions of the legend he had learned of, which had prompted him to visit Eroudac to try to find the quiraawa flower. Rune reflected that he had heard of the healing flower's tale, leading the two to discuss the different meanings of "quiraawa" in the [[Eroudan]] [[language]]. As they approached a mountain, Churo spotted a glowing key high above him. Rune [[Telekinesis|moved]] a rock [[Force power|with]] [[the Force]] to use as a key, helping the Hutt out of gratitude for the latter treating his injuries.<ref name="Heart" />

===Hidden oasis===
{{Quote|That is not the question. The question is&mdash;can you pay the cost of your miracle?''"<br />"''Cost? My sister ''Dahara'' transferred me some credits.''"<br />"''No, no, no, Churo the Hutt. A price of a different kind. A world for a world. We are the Heart of Eroudac. We beat in synchronicity. We are the lifeblood.|A Heart of Eroudac and Churo|<ref name="Heart" />}}
[[File:ChuroHeartEroudac-THRAAnnualPhaseIII.jpg|thumb|left|320px|Churo, surrounded by several Heart of Eroudac]]
The key's usage opened up a [[cave]]rn entrance before the pair, prompting both to go in as they discussed their reasons for coming to Eroudac. However, a large group of insects began attacking them, leading both Churo and Rune to run down a different tunnel to avoid harming them. As they ran, a hole in the ground opened up and Rune fell in, leaving Churo alone and surrounded by the now-calm insects. He kept moving through the tunnel until it opened into a wider cavern with lush vegetation and a large tree.<ref name="Heart" />

There, he met several Heart of Eroudac&mdash;Eroudac's native [[species]]&mdash;who informed him he was allowed to enter the oasis only because he had sought them out, specifically, with his research of Eroudac legends. The Heart of Eroudac confirmed his query that a quiraawa flower would heal Nal Hutta. However, when Churo was informed by a [[Unidentified Heart of Eroudac|Heart of Eroudac]] that the cost of the miracle for his homeworld would result in negative consequences for Eroudac, he tearfully decided to leave the oasis. After leaving the cavern, Churo met Rune outside, and the pair departed the planet together aboard the ''Glowworm'' to search for the [[biologist]] [[Glenna Kip]], who might have another solution for Nal Hutta's troubles.<ref name="Heart" />

===A new beginning===
{{Quote|Izzy, after your parents&mdash;why did you leave the academy?''"<br />"''At first it was an accident.''"<br />"''Leaving or going in the first place?''"<br />"''I went to appease my father. He said when he was in school it was the best time of his life until he met my mother and then we became his world. We’d finally stopped moving around and Eroudac would do.|Julen Rakab and Izal Garsea|<ref name="ACOF" />}}
While in the [[Khepi Tomb]] in the [[Khepi system]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' {{C|''A Pirate's Fortune'' DLC}}</ref> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[Rokana Raiders|Rokana Raider]] boasted about having beaten a [[drift-skipper]] [[squadron]] on Eroudac's moon.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]]. The novel establishes that [[Izal Garsea]] left [[Batuu]] when she was five [[Standard year|years]] old, and as the novel takes place on the [[Standard day|day]] before and on Izal Garsea's eighteenth [[birthday]], she must have left the planet in [[21 ABY]].</ref> the [[Garsea family]] moved out from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]] and eventually arrived on Eroudac, where they settled at the [[Eroudac Citadel]]. Afterward, the [[Gender|daughter]] of the family&mdash;[[Izal Garsea]]&mdash;began attending a local [[Eroudac academy|academy]] to appease her [[Izal Garsea's father|father]]. Garsea spent most of her [[Standard day|days]] on Eroudac at the [[Eroudac spaceport|spaceport]], where she would board her family's [[light freighter]], the [[Meridian (light freighter)|''Meridian'']], and [[Pilot|fly]] it around the planet. Izal's [[Parent|mother]], [[Ixel Garsea]], had been able to smooth-talk her way out of citations and get landing permits from the [[police officer]]s in the world's local [[law enforcement agency]].<ref name="ACOF" />

[[File:Haneli.png|thumb|right|240px|Izal Garsea was hired by a professor on Eroudac to smuggle haneli flowers from Haneli (pictured on map).]]
Following the [[death]] of her parents, Izal Garsea took a [[Galactic Politics]] course at the academy on Eroudac, during which she met a [[Izal Garsea's boyfriend|fellow student]] and started dating him. The relationship ended after she met her boyfriend's parents, who did not care for an orphaned woman like Garsea; she had no social status on Eroudac and no aspirations of becoming a [[Politics|politician's]] [[Marriage|wife]]. Sometime later, Garsea accepted a [[Smuggler|smuggling]] job from the academy's [[gene]]tic science [[Unidentified genetic science professor|professor]]. She was set to travel to the planet [[Haneli]] aboard her starship, acquire some [[haneli flower]]s, and smuggle them offworld. During the job, however, Garsea was caught with the flowers and decided to drop the cargo and to abandon her home on Eroudac rather than returning to face her [[professor]].<ref name="ACOF" />

===Planning the future===
{{Quote|Meet me on Eroudac.|Izal Garsea inviting Julen Rakab to a date|<ref name="ACOF" />}}
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Garsea returned to Batuu, where she reunited with her old childhood friend [[Julen Rakab]]. Explaining where she had been, she told Rakab everything that had happened at Eroudac. After a [[Skirmish at Kat Saka's farm|skirmish]] at a [[Saka Homestead|local farm]], Garsea learned that Rakab owned a [[Julen Rakab's Avent100-series light freighter|starship]], which motivated her to invite Rakab on a date on Eroudac.<ref name="ACOF" />

Rakab was initially confused about the choice of Eroudac, but Garsea explained that it was the last place where she had lived with her parents, and that it had ancient ruins and a pink moon. Rakab agreed to go on the date once his ship was repaired. Rakab and Garsea started dating, but their plan to go to Eroudac did not come to fruition, and they instead hoped to visit it together one day.<ref name="ACOF" /> That same year,<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]] included Eroudac on a [[map]] in his [[book]] ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|I was at the academy on Eroudac. Small Mid Rim world.|Izal Garsea|<ref name="ACOF" />}}
By the time of the High Republic Era, numerous Heart of Eroudac hailed from Eroudac, and due to the nature of their role as the planet's [[heart]], if one of them were to leave the planet, the world's lifeblood and synchronicity would wane.<ref name="Heart" /> Several Jedi, including the human Jamil Sollis, lived on Eroudac<ref name="BTN" /> by 230 BBY.<ref name="BTNDate" /> Izal Garsea and her family also resided on the world for some time following their departure from Batuu during the [[New Republic Era|time]] of the [[New Republic]], as did others such as her boyfriend and academy professor.<ref name="ACOF" /> Additionally, the Eroudan language originated from the world and was known to at least some individuals.<ref name="Heart" />

==Locations==
{{Quote|I was friendly with some of the guards at the spaceport because I spent most days burning fuel by taking the ''Meridian'' for solo runs to nowhere.|Izal Garsea explaining her days on Eroudac|<ref name="ACOF" />}}
By the High Republic Era, a Jedi temple was built into the side of one of the world's mountains.<ref name="BTN" /> Other mountains, such as the [[Quiraawa Mountains]], were also a feature of the world.<ref name="Heart" /> The Eroudac Citadel was a residence where the Garsea family lived during their stay on Eroudac. Other points of interest included the local [[spaceport]], an academy, and ancient ruins.<ref name="ACOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Actually, Eroudac, the planet that Izzy Garsea went to college ''[sic]'' and the planet that Jamil Solis is from ''[sic]'' the newest youngling in the group, is going to be featured in the annual one shot that I wrote about Churo the Hutt. So we get to see it&mdash;and I remember saying that when ''[sic]'' we were editing the comic, well I have actually never seen this planet because I made it up for ''Crash of Fate''&hellip;|Zoraida Córdova|<ref name="Interview">{{WookYouTube|bJUdOoC6kTw|Wookieepedia Interviews - Zoraida Córdova}}</ref>}}
Eroudac was created for<ref name="Interview" /> the [[2019]] young-adult novel ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]], which introduced it via mention.<ref name="ACOF" /> The planet's name is an anagram for [[Wikipedia:Ecuador|Ecuador]],<ref name="Anagram">{{WebCite|url=https://www.wordunscrambler.net/permutations-of.aspx?word=ecuador&page=3|text=Permutations of ECUADOR|work=[https://www.wordunscrambler.net WordScrambler]|archivedate=20210727074139}}</ref> the country where Córdova was born,<ref name="Ecuador">{{WebCite|url=http://www.diversityinya.com/2013/06/mermaids-in-america|text=Mermaids in America, Or, how does this Ecuadorian girl end up writing fantasy?|author=[[Zoraida Córdova]]|date=2013-06-05|work=[https://www.diversityinya.com Diversity in YA]|archivedate=20191020055018|nolive=1}}</ref> and its topography was based on the country.<ref name="Newsletter">{{Substack|subdomain=zoraida|url=p/writing-baddies-comics-and-other|text=Writing Baddies, Comics, & Other Updates|name=[[Zoraida Córdova]]|username=zoraida|date=2025-02-02}}</ref> Mentions of Eroudac were later included in the junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', also authored by Córdova and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="BTN" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]] on [[August 27]], [[2024]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> Córdova confirmed the reference to Eroudac in ''Beware the Nameless'' was an intentional callback to her previous ''[[Star Wars]]'' work,<ref name="Interview" /> having created the planet for Izal Garsea.<ref name="THR Roundtable">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-author-roundtable|text=The High Republic Author Roundtable: "The End of the Beginning"}}</ref>

[[File:Eroudac-Uncolored.jpg|thumb|right|300px|An uncolored panel of Eroudac from "The Adventures of Churo the Hutt and the Heart of Eroudac"]]
Ahead of its release, on [[December 12]], 2024, the author also revealed that Eroudac's first full appearance and visual depiction would be in the then-upcoming one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] ''[[The High Republic Adventures Phase III Annual]]'' within her story about Churo,<ref name="Interview" /> "[[The Adventures of Churo the Hutt and the Heart of Eroudac]]." The comic story was illustrated by [[Juan Samu]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of ''The High Republic''{{'s}} Phase III<ref name="Heart" /> on [[January 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="AnnualComic">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3015-809/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-Annual|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III Annual}}</ref> Before the interview, uncolored, unlettered comic pages from "The Adventures of Churo the Hutt and the Heart of Eroudac" had been revealed during the [[Lucasfilm]] Publishing Panel at [[Wikipedia:New York Comic Con|New York Comic Con]] on [[October 19]], 2024.<ref name="NYCC">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2024-lucasfilm-publishing-reveals|text=NYCC 2024: All the ''Star Wars'' News and Reveals from New York Comic-Con}}</ref> Thus, that preview marked Eroudac's visual debut.<ref name="Interview" /><ref name="NYCC" />

Córdova was excited for Eroudac to make its visual debut, reflecting that while editing the comic, she had never imagined seeing the world in visual form. Although the author had originally created Eroudac for ''A Crash of Fate'', she enjoyed being able to revisit subjects she had created in more of her ''Star Wars'' work, having remarked that she had been unsure if she would be able to.<ref name="Interview" /> Córdova felt that Churo's mission to Eroudac was because he wanted to "save the galaxy," noting that she was excited to have picked up a copy of the comic upon its release.<ref name="Newsletter" />
