The '''Department of Prisons''' was a department in the [[New Republic]] that was responsible for concerns regarding [[prison]]s under its control. When traveling to [[Substation Grimdock]], [[Taka Jamoreesa]] claimed that they, [[Han Solo]], [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], [[Kaasha Bateen]], [[Chewbacca]] and [[Peekpa]] were sent from the Department to investigate possible technical malfunctions with the prisons [[computer]] system.<ref name="Last Shot" />
