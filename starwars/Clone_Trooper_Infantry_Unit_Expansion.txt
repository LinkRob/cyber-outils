The '''Clone Trooper Infantry Unit Expansion''' ('''SWQ24''') is an expansion to the tabletop game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. An updated version of the earlier [[Phase II Clone Troopers Unit Expansion]], it will be released on [[November 7]], [[2025]] as part of Atomic Mass Games' overhaul of the game.

==Publisher's summary==
''The Galactic Republic’s clone troopers are an elite fighting force. Genetically crafted for combat and training as a team since their creation, clone trooper infantry operate as a cohesive fighting unit that is as effective as they are flexible, adapting strategies in the midst of battle and highly proficient with a wide array of weaponry.''

''This'' Star Wars™: ''Legion Clone Trooper Infantry set is perfect for players looking to expand the Corps options for their Galactic Republic army''

==Contents==
*11 Miniatures
*1 Unit Card
*6 Upgrade Cards
