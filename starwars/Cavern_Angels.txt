The '''Cavern Angels''' were a [[squadron]] of [[X-wing starfighter]]s and [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|U-wings]] that were used by [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The group was initially formed by several [[T-65 X-wing starfighter|T-65 X-wings]] that were stolen by Gerrera and his partisans from an [[Incom Corporation]] [[Unidentified Incom Corporation warehouse|warehouse]] on [[Fresia]]<ref name="DRVG" /> before [[5 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> The group was later enlarged with the addition of several U-wing support craft that Gerrera had modified with [[Explosive charge|bomb]] racks and heavy weaponry, and affectionately named the "Cavern Angels" due to the Partisans' usually hiding in caves and [[mine]]s.<ref name="DRVG" />

==History==
[[File:SawGerrerasBase.jpg|thumb|Two X-wings of Cavern Angels on Segra Milo|left]]
Because of a shortage of spare parts, usually only one or two of the [[starfighter]]s were operational at any given [[time]]. Despite the difficulties, including a limited supply of [[astromech droid]]s,<ref name="SWROUVG" /> the group followed the Partisans in their frequent relocations around the galaxy.<ref name="N5" /> By 5 BBY, they were stationed in an [[Saw Gerrera's Base|abandoned mine]] on the planet [[Segra Milo]] that served as a Partisan starfighter launch base and a command center for Gerrera.<ref name="SWROUVG" /> There, they were visited<ref name="N5" /> by [[Axis network]]<ref name="DRVG" /> leader [[Luthen Rael]] who asked for their air support in an upcoming [[Spellhaus attack|attack]] on an [[Spellhaus Imperial power station|Imperial power station]] in [[Spellhaus]], although Gerrera declined the request.<ref name="N5" /> The group was eventually noticed by nearby trader [[starship]]s, who notified the [[Galactic Empire]] to dispatch [[Department of Imperial Justice]] patrols,<ref name="DRVG" /> including [[Cantwell-class Arrestor Cruiser|''Cantwell''-class Arrestor Cruisers]] above the planet.<ref name="DF">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> 

The unwanted attention proved to be too much for the Partisans, who carried out a costly relocation.<ref name="DRVG" /> By [[3 BBY]], the Partisans were based out of a [[Resistance base|base]] on [[D'Qar]]<ref name="Ever Been">{{Andor|Ever Been to Ghorman?}}</ref> but evacuated after the discovery of a traitor in their ranks.<ref name="FriendsEverywhere">{{Andor|I Have Friends Everywhere}}</ref> During the [[Jedha insurgency]], the Cavern Angels were based in the [[Catacombs of Cadera]] outside [[Jedha City]]. During the insurrection on Jedha by the Partisans, at least one of the X-wings was shot down, where it crashed in the streets of [[Jedha City]].<ref name="SWROUVG" /> Once the [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] launched their major combat deployment during the [[Jedha insurgency]], four of the X-wings were launched to strike back. Three of them were shot down, and the fourth was chased into the wilderness by six [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]].<ref name="Guardians" />

==Behind the scenes==
Several of the Cavern Angels' X-wings appear in the [[2016]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.

The Cavern Angels' colors were one of the multiplayer starfighter cosmetic options in the [[2020]] game ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>
