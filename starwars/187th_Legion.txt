The '''187th Legion''' was an elite [[clone trooper]] [[legion]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] led by [[Jedi General]] [[Mace Windu]]. The legion participated in the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], subsequently serving the Republic's [[Proclamation of the New Order|successor]] government, the [[Galactic Empire]], as members of the [[Imperial Army]] and enforcing the Empire's rule. The legion shared its name with another unit led by Windu during the Clone Wars: the [[187th Battalion]].

==Description==
[[File:187th-Helmet-Sand.png|thumb|left|220px|The 187th used purple and blue markings painted on their helmets.]]
The elite<ref name="Disney Helmet" /> 187th [[Legion]] was a [[clone trooper]] legion of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]<ref name="Hasbro" /> that fell under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Jedi High Council|High Council]]<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> [[Jedi General]] [[Mace Windu]],<ref name="Hasbro reveal" /> and was later organized under the [[Imperial Army]].<ref name="Disney Helmet" /> It shared the same unit designation as the [[187th Battalion]],<ref name="HasbroPulse Livestream">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@hasbropulse|name=[[Hasbro|Hasbro Pulse]]|video=sCJAvCfeIsw|text=Fan First Wednesday &#124; Star Wars May the 4th Be You Livestream|date=2022-05-04|nolive=1}}</ref> another unit led by Windu.<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/unfinished-business-episode-guide|text="Unfinished Business" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>

The [[trooper]]s of the legion customized their [[Color|white]] [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] with purple and light blue accents, which was partly shared with their Jedi General's<ref name="Hasbro" /> [[Mace Windu's lightsaber|unique]] purple-bladed [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Windu's Lightsaber">{{Databank|mace-windus-lightsaber|Mace Windu's Lightsaber}}</ref> The [[helmet]]s of the 187th contained purple on the helmet's central fin and chin, with light blue vertical stripes across the top of the [[Macrobinocular viewplate|T-shaped visor]] and light blue coloring on the helmet's cheeks.<ref name="Hasbro" /> Other versions of the helmet replaced the armor's light blue with light gray.<ref name="187th SWCC Pin">{{WebCite|url=https://pinandpop.com/pins/52040/star-wars-celebration-2022-mystery-helmets-collection-187th-legion|text=52040 - Star Wars Celebration 2022 - Mystery Helmets Collection - 187th Legion - Misc - Disney Licensed|work=[https://pinandpop.com/ Disney Pin Trading & Collecting Database]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/bTwFn}}</ref> Purple accents were painted on the shoulder [[armor]] plates, the chest, knees, and [[boot]]s, while blue stripes were located on the [[Limb|arm]] and leg armor. The lower half of the leg armor was fully painted purple. 187th [[Cloning|clone]] troopers utilized [[utility belt]]s fastened around their hip, also painted in purple.<ref name="Hasbro" /> An alternate armor design for the 187th Legion that replaced the purple accents with turquoise ones also existed.<ref name="Power Force">{{DisneyToyCite|set=Star Wars: Power Force|packtype=Action Figure|pack=187th Legion Clone Trooper|altlink=https://www.disneystore.asia/187th-legion-clone-trooper-star-wars-power-force-talking-action-figure-417137765243.html|nolive=1}}</ref>

The legion's troopers wielded the [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] and the [[DC-15A blaster rifle]].<ref name="Hasbro" /> Other members additionally donned [[pauldron]]s with ammunition pouches.<ref name="Power Force" />

==History==
[[File:187th-trooper-desert.png|thumb|right|240px|A 187th clone trooper in a desert environment.]]
The elite 187th Legion participated in the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="Hasbro" /> and was under the command of Jedi General Mace Windu.<ref name="Hasbro reveal" /> When Phase II clone trooper armor was introduced by the [[Battle of Mon Cala]]<ref name="Water War">{{TCW|Water War}}</ref> in [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the 187th was equipped with the model of [[clone trooper armor]].<ref name="Hasbro" /> Despite the ideals of the [[Jedi Order]],<ref name="ROTS" /> the helmets, combined with their coloration, of Windu's [[troop]]s struck fear into the hearts of their enemies.<ref name="Disney Helmet" /> After Windu's [[Duel in Palpatine's office|death]] and the destruction of the entire Jedi Order with the implementation of [[Order 66]], a secret [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|Executive Order Code]] that marked the [[Jedi]] as [[Treason|traitors]]<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]]. As such, the [[Republic Military]] transitioned into the [[Imperial Military]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref>

With clone troopers forced to obey the will of Palpatine, secretly the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Sidious,<ref name="ROTS" /> and carry out Order 66 by their [[behavioral modification biochip]]s,<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> the 187th continued to exist as a legion within the Imperial Army. In that capacity, it was the task of Windu's former troops to defend the regime<ref name="Disney Helmet" /> that [[Great Jedi Purge|openly hunted]] the Jedi.<ref name="ROTS" /> At some point during their service history, the 187th was present in a [[desert]].<ref name="Power Force" />

The 187th Legion was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] as an elite force depicted through the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] set [[w:c:brickipedia:75342 Republic Fighter Tank|75342 Republic Fighter Tank]] that was revealed by ''[[This Week! in Star Wars]]'' on [[March 31]], [[2022]],<ref name="SWYouTube" /> by show host<ref name="Baver Host">{{SW|url=news/contributor/kbaber|text=Kristin Baver}}</ref> [[Kristin Baver]].<ref name="SWYouTube" /> The 187th then appeared in the [[Happy Celebration 45th Anniversary A New Hope|Happy Celebration 45th Anniversary ''A New Hope'']], a non-canon animated LEGO short created to celebrate 45 years of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']],<ref name="YouTube">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=LEGO STAR WARS: Celebrate the Season|text=Happy Celebration 45th Anniversary A New Hope|video=HANw_h3x4VM}}</ref> the first film of the [[original trilogy]] released in [[1977]].<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> At [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|Celebration Anaheim 2022]], a helmet of the 187th Legion was featured as one of eight collectible pins from the Mystery Helmets Collection.<ref name="187th SWCC Pin" />

The legion's status as a canonical unit was fully confirmed by a [[2023]] Hasbro figure set, "Mace Windu & 187th Legion Clone Trooper," for ''[[Star Wars: The Black Series]]''. The Hasbro Pulse listing gave the name "Mace Windu & Clone Trooper," but the complete name was located on the release's box.<ref name="Hasbro" /> The [[187th Legion/Legends|187th Legion]] initially debuted in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], first appearing in the [[2006]] [[Hasbro]] exclusive set for Target in "Mace Windu's Attack Battalion."<ref name="Hasbro old">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars Unleashed|packtype=Battle Pack|pack=187th Legion Troopers (Ultimate Battles)|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/sw07unlUB-187.asp}}</ref>

A clone unit, the 187th Battalion, led by Mace Windu appeared in the first episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|seventh and final season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', titled "[[The Bad Batch (episode)|The Bad Batch]]"<ref name="The Bad Batch">{{TCW|The Bad Batch (episode)}}</ref> and released on [[February 21]], [[2020]].<ref name="Disney+">{{WebCite|url=https://collider.com/new-on-disney-plus-february-2020|text=What's New on Disney Plus February 2020, Including Toy Story 4|author=Gregory Lawrance|archivedate=20200221195811}}</ref> The episode had originally aired in an unfinished format during [[Celebration Anaheim (2015)|Celebration Anaheim]] on [[April 17]], [[2015]].<ref name="Anaheim Announcement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-bad-batch-4-episode-arc-coming-to-star-wars-celebration|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' "Bad Batch" 4-Episode Arc Coming to ''Star Wars'' Celebration|nolive=1}}</ref> According to [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Leland Chee]], the decision to not use the purple color scheme of the 187th Legion was made in the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of the series,<ref name="Leland" /> which began airing in [[2008]].<ref name="Ambush Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode001.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Ambush}}</ref> The unit was later named the "187th Battalion" on the Hasbro Pulse livestream for [[May 4|May the Fourth]], 2022, as a ''Star Wars: The Black Series'' set.<ref name="HasbroPulse Livestream" />

[[File:Windu_unit_Anaxes.png|thumb|right|230px|The clone unit Mace Windu led in ''The Clone Wars'']]
In "[[Liberty on Ryloth]]"<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref>&mdash;the penultimate episode of the first season that aired on [[March 13]], [[2009]],<ref name="Liberty Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode021.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref> [[Clone Commander]] [[Ponds]] served under Windu and had customized his armor with similar color<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> to Windu's unit in the seventh season.<ref name="The Bad Batch" /> Sources including the [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 76|seventy-sixth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]'' magazine<ref name="Helmet Collection 76">{{HelmetCollectionCite|76|Helmets}}</ref>&ndash;written by [[Iain Lowson]]<ref name="HC-Credits">{{EmailCite|email=File:HCSV-Email-2025-07-26.png|author=[[Iain Lowson]]|date=2025-07-26}}</ref> and originally released on [[January 31]], [[2017]]<ref name="HC France">{{HelmetDatesFR|76}}</ref>&ndash;and the [[July 18]], 2017,<ref name="Amazon">{{TitanBooks|url=8811-star-wars-on-the-front-lines|text=Star Wars - On the Front Lines}}</ref> reference book ''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]'' authored by [[Daniel Wallace]]<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> clarify that Ponds served as the leader of [[Lightning Squadron]]<ref name="Helmet Collection 76" /> of the [[91st Mobile Reconnaissance Corps]].<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> Chee further elaborated that it was a conscious decision to try to keep them as different units rather than the same unit that uses two different color schemes.<ref name="Leland" /> The reveal of the ''Black Series'' Hasbro figure of the 187th Legion accidentally conflated the 187th Legion and 187th Battalion.<ref name="Hasbro reveal" />

===Non-canon history===
The box cover art for the "Republic Fighter Tank" set depicts two [[B1-series battle droid]]s fighting against Mace Windu, two 187th Legion clone troopers, and a [[Unidentified Clone Commander (187th Legion)|187th Clone Commander]] aboard a [[TX-130 Saber-class fighter tank|TX-130 ''Saber''-class fighter tank]] in a mountainous and desert surface. In the set, the clones had markings that included a small, dark pauldron on their right shoulder. Their [[commander]] utilized [[clone paratrooper]] armor with purple markings, a similar pauldron, and a beige [[kama]].<ref name="LEGO">{{LEGOWeb|url=en-us/product/republic-fighter-tank-75342|text=Republic Fighter Tank}}</ref>

[[File:BattleOfARandomTheater-HappyCelebration.png|thumb|left|300px|Two members of the 187th Legion escorted Mace Windu and Ahsoka Tano to a showing of ''A New Hope'' and encountered a B1 battle droid in a non-canon LEGO short film.]]
LEGO's ''A New Hope'' anniversary short sees two members of the 187th escort Windu and<ref name="YouTube" /> Jedi-turned-Republic Military [[advisor]]<ref name="Old Friends">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref> [[Ahsoka Tano]] to a screening of ''A New Hope'', standing guard as both Force-sensitives depart from a TX-130 ''Saber''-class fighter to the applause of all gathered outside the cinema. Unknown to the clones and their charges, a B1 battle droid hid in the crowd and revealed themself with the intention of firing upon the Republic personnel. While both clone troopers readied their [[blaster]]s, either Windu or Tano used their lightsabers to deflect the droid's blaster bolt, destroying the unit and clearing the path to the theater.<ref name="YouTube" />
