The '''Clan Assembly''' governed [[Foundry Four]], where the members voted on decisions on how to run Foundry Four. It was lead by [[Cacique]] [[Kal Coorsa]], the ruling member of the Clan Assembly. There were four families with voting power within the Clan Assembly, the [[Hardin family]], the [[Sargan family]], the [[Vinder family]], and the [[Korrin family]].<ref name="Friends Like These" />
