'''Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo''' was a [[restaurant]] operating out of [[Black Spire Outpost]]'s [[Docking Bay 7]]. [[Foodstuff|Food]] and cargo delivery [[shuttle]]s landed atop the building to supply the restaurant. Rotating guest [[chef]]s, such as [[Strono Tuggs|Strono "Cookie" Tuggs]], worked out of the location while visiting [[Batuu]].

==Description==
[[File:Docking_Bay_7_Food_and_Cargo_interior-D23.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Food and cargo in the restaurant]]
Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo was located within [[Docking Bay 7]], an old hangar bay and landing platform<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> on [[Merchant Row (Batuu)|Merchant Row]]<ref name="GEStaff" /> in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. [[Foodstuff|Food]] and cargo delivery [[shuttle]]s landed atop the building and lowered supplies to the businesses below. Rather than having a permanent [[restaurant]], guest [[chef]]s visiting the planet would set up temporary kitchens to cook and serve out of the location. Local [[Batuuan]]s worked as serving staff and assistant cooks for the rotating roster of restaurants. Visitors could eat within the hangar or outdoors.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==History==
[[File:Docking_Bay_7_Food_and_Cargo.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Tuggs' transport docked above the restaurant]]
Following the [[Battle of Takodana|destruction]] of [[Takodana Castle]]<ref name="Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs|Strono "Cookie" Tuggs]] docked his [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|modified]] [[Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport]] there and began operating his traveling diner, [[Tuggs' Grub]], out of the building.<ref name="Cookbook" />

When Tuggs' Grub was docked there in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GE Date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Tuggs served a variety of dishes.<ref name="Docking Bay 7">{{GEAttr|Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo}}</ref>

On unusually cold days, they served [[Spiced Surabat Cider]].<ref name="Docking Bay 7" />

==Behind the scenes==
Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo is one of the restaurants in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], a themed attraction that opened at [[Disneyland]] Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="Foodie Guide">{{DPB|url=2019/05/foodie-guide-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Foodie Guide to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> It has menus for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, along with children's menu items.<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> The [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] location serves [[alcohol]]ic beverages,<ref name="WDW Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=dining/hollywood-studios/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/dinner|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Dinner Menu}}</ref> while the Disneyland location does not.<ref name="DL Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch And Dinner Menu}}</ref>
