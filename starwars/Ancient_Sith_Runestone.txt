The '''Ancient Sith Runestone''' was an old relic of the [[Sith]] uncovered by the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. [[Jedi Master]] [[Sylwin]] and [[Padawan]] [[Ady Sun'Zee]] took the artifact to the [[Batuu Jedi temple|Jedi temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. There the Sith Runestone corrupted and turned the [[Jedi]] against one another with the only survivor of the [[Batuu Jedi temple incident|incident]] being Sun'Zee.<ref name="Tales" />
