'''Delequia''' was an [[Ithorian/Legends|Ithorian]] [[Gender/Legends|Female]] [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]]. During the [[Galactic War]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|reconstituted Sith Empire]] she was stationed on [[Ilum/Legends|Ilum]].<ref name="TOR" />
