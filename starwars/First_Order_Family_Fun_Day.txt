The '''First Order Family Fun Day''' was a [[Standard day|day]] for having fun hosted by the [[First Order]]. It was intended for families, and hosted events such as visiting [[Kylo Ren's command shuttle]], or face [[painting]] to look like [[Maul]]. A poster for it was collected by [[Poe Dameron]] in a [[Unidentified book|vehicle guidebook]].<ref name="Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
The First Order Family Fun Day was mentioned in the [[2022]] non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Awesome Vehicles]]''.<ref name="Vehicles" />
