'''Civian Bain''' was a [[Rodian]] criminal who operated during the [[New Republic Era|era]] of the [[New Republic]]. Bain was one of numerous criminals invited to the [[Kijimi summit]] by [[Zeva Bliss]] of the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]], who intended to [[Death|kill]] the guests present. Bliss was stopped by former Spice Runner [[Poe Dameron]], and the criminals were allowed to escape.<ref name="Free Fall" />
