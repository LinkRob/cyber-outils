'''Blowback Town''' was a settlement on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. Around thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[Blarina]] [[merchant]] [[Ohn Gos]] operated in the town, and when the Blarina [[scavenger]] [[Naka Iit]] found the [[Resistance]] [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] lost in the deserts he agreed to take the [[pilot]] to Blowback Town in his [[speeder]] so he might arrange transport offworld with Gos. En route, the pair were attacked by the criminal [[Strus clan]], but Dameron was able to escape them and earn Iit's gratitude, causing the Blarina to promise to intercede with Gos in Blowback and ensure Dameron found passage offworld. The pilot successfully left the planet soon after.<ref name="TFAn" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blowback Town was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|novelization]] of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], which was written by [[Alan Dean Foster]] and released in conjunction with the movie in [[2015]]. It was not mentioned in the film itself, but as the scene containing his mention in the novel in no way contradicts the film, this article assumes it is [[canon]].
