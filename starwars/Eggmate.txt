In [[Tognath]] culture, '''eggmates''' were individuals who shared a special bond due to their unhatched [[egg]]s grafting together while being nurtured in suspension jelly,<ref name="SW">{{SW|url=news/much-to-learn-you-still-have-talking-rogue-one-aliens-with-pablo-hidalgo|text=Much to Learn You Still Have: Talking ''Rogue One'' Aliens with Pablo Hidalgo}}</ref> although the [[Tognath (language)|true word]] was untranslatable. Eggs could fuse in pairs or trios and, once gestated, the larvae would go on to share a close bond in life. If fused early enough, some egg pairs developed a telepathic or empathic bond. These egg mates could always sense one another's location and emotional state of mind. The most successful [[float farmer]]s, a dangerous but highly romanticized Tognath profession, were eggmates, able to detect one another's location and safety.<ref name="DoR">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref>
