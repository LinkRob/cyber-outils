'''Andoan Fruit with Muja Sauce''' was a [[Diet|plant-based]] snack featuring [[fruit]] from [[Ando]] topped with [[muja sauce]].<ref name="Ronto Roasters" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GE date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> it was available at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]], where [[Ronto Roasters]] served it throughout the day. It was suitable for consumption by [[human]]s.<ref name="Ronto Roasters" />

==Behind the scenes==
This snack is found throughout the day in the [[In-universe|real-world]] Ronto Roasters at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]]. It was first served in [[July]] of [[2021]].<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=2021/07/disneys-hollywood-studios-foodie-news-menu-offerings-at-star-wars-galaxys-edge-fairfax-fare-and-more|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios Foodie News: Menu Offerings at ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge, Fairfax Fare, and More!}}</ref> The menu's description reads, "Sticks of Jicama, Cucumber, Pineapple, Melon and Dragon Fruit doused in a Tamarind Sauce and dusted with Peppercorn Ash (Plant-Based)."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ronto-roasters/menus/breakfast|text=Disneyland: Ronto Roasters: Breakfast Menu}}</ref>
