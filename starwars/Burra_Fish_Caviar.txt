'''Burra Fish Caviar''' was a dish consisting of the [[egg]]s of the [[Burra fish]] from the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]]. It was suitable for [[human]] consumption and was served on the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] known as the ''[[Halcyon]]'' alongside [[Poached Lobster Tail]],<ref name="GS" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GSDate">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Burra Fish Caviar was a meal that is available for purchase by guests staying on the ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser]]'' hotel upon its opening in [[2022]]. It is [[Wikipedia:Caviar|caviar]] sold alongside [[Wikipedia:Lobster#As food|lobster]].<ref name="GS" />
