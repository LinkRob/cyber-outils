'''Frederick Ried Roos''' ([[May 22]], [[1930s#1934|1934]] &ndash; [[May 18]], [[2024]]) was an American producer and casting director most famous for his work on [[Wikipedia:The Godfather Part II|''The Godfather Part II'']], for which he won an Oscar. Before this, he worked in the casting department for {{Film|IV}}, recommending actors [[Harrison Ford]], [[Carrie Fisher]], and [[James Earl Jones]], who would go on to portray [[Han Solo]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] voice respectively, to [[George Lucas]]. He also discovered actor [[Alden Ehrenreich]], who portrayed Han Solo in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Obituary" />
