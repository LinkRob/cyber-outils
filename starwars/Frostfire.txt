'''Frostfire''' was from the [[Water|icy]] [[planet]] [[Kijimi]] and its essence could be used to imbue small shards of the [[crystal]] [[nunsteyn]] in order to create [[frostfire crystal shard]] charms, which were believed to grant the wearer resilience.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[smuggler]] [[Neva Yaranu]] had a frostfire crystal shard available to purchase for one thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] in [[Kijimi City]] on Kijimi.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Frostfire was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
