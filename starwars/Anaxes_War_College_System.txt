The '''Anaxes War College System''' was a system used to classify various types of [[starship]]s in [[the galaxy]], and was used by the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Galactic Empire]], and the [[First Order]]. First instituted by the [[Republic Navy War College|Anaxes War College]] during the [[Clone Wars]], the Anaxes System served to expand the existing nomenclature used to classify [[capital ship]]s by adding [[battlecruiser]]s, [[destroyer]]s, and [[dreadnought]]s. According to this system, ships were divided into seven main classifications; these were [[corvette]]s (100-200 [[meter]]s), [[frigate]]s (200-400 meters), [[cruiser]]s (400-600 meters), [[heavy cruiser]]s (600-1,200 meters), destroyers (1,000-2,000 meters), battlecruisers (2,000-5,000 meters), and dreadnoughts (+5,000 meters). These classifications were flexible, however, and some ships could be moved to a higher or lower class depending on their total firepower and function.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Anaxes War College classification system was first developed by author [[Jason Fry]] in the [[Star Wars Legends]] continuity, as part of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Incredible Vehicles]]'' reference book, and was later included in ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]].''<ref name="TFNJF" /><ref name="TFNJF">{{WebCite|url=http://boards.theforce.net/threads/the-clone-wars-incredible-vehicles-spoilers-inbound.31855959/page-2|text=''The Clone Wars: Incredible Vehicles'' thread where Jason Fry commented|work=the book and its usage of some of his ideas for the later ''Essential Guide''.|archivedate=20250404210244}}</ref> It entered canon as part of ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'', also written by Fry.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" />
