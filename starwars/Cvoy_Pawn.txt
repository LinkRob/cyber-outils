'''Cvoy Pawn''' was a pawn shop in the [[Coruscant Underworld]] at the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]]. It was located at [[Terminal 24]]. The building façade possessed a luminous sign that showed the words "Cvoy" and "Cvoy Pawn" in [[Color|yellow]] [[Aurebesh]] letters on a red background.<ref name="To Catch a Jedi" />
