'''Amnesia''' was a mental condition often brought on by a traumatic event that resulted in memory loss of some form or another. The individual affected might remember certain things such as how to perform tasks or speak, but have little or no memory of the personal details of one's life, such as name, occupation, or family. Exposure to sights, sounds or other sensory information containing details from one's past might serve as a trigger to overcome the condition.<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref><ref name="Missing in Action" />

Following his defeat at the hands of [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in [[lightsaber combat]], [[Maul]] suffered partial amnesia regarding exactly what had happened in the incident, but still sought revenge on the [[Jedi]].<ref name="Brothers" />

After the [[Galactic Republic]]'s forces were [[Battle of Sarrish|defeated]] by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] on the [[planet]] [[Sarrish]], the [[clone commando]] [[Gregor]] escaped the battle, but developed amnesia<ref name="Missing in Action" /> after crash-landing on [[Abafar]],<ref name="Gregor DB">{{Databank|gregor|Gregor}}</ref> remembering only his name and forgetting even his status as a clone. He was discovered by [[Borkus]], who took advantage of his condition, giving him a job as a [[dishwasher]] at his [[Power Sliders]] [[Restaurant|diner]] in exchange for room and board. Gregor was eventually able to recover his memory thanks to [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]] reminding him of his life as a [[soldier]].<ref name="Missing in Action" />
