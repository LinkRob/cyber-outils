'''Bandanas''' were a type of [[clothing]]. They could be worn by [[Kupohan]]s in order to protect the frequency filter organs between their primary and basal [[ear]]s. The Kupohan [[Sakhet]], who worked at [[Sakhet's Noodles]], wore a [[Color|red]] bandana while serving [[Foodstuff|food]] to [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Nakari Kelen]].<ref name="HttJ">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref>
