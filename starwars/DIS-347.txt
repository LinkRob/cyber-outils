'''DIS-347''' was a [[droid tri-fighter]] that served as part of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance's]] [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval branch]] during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="X-wing" /> against the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> The [[droid starfighter]] measured to 5.4 [[meter]]s in length and 3.45 meters in width,<ref name="Size" /> and was equipped with three [[engine]] units at its stern and three [[laser cannon]]s. At some point during the war, DIS-347 engaged a Republic [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]] in [[Realspace|space]] [[Starfighter combat|combat]], which led to the Y-wing being critically damaged.<ref name="X-wing" />

==Behind the scenes==
DIS-347 was introduced in the [[Droid Tri-Fighter Expansion Pack]], a [[2020]] expansion pack published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for the ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition]]'' miniatures combat game.<ref name="X-wing" />
