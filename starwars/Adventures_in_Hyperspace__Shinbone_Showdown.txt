'''''Adventures in Hyperspace: Shinbone Showdown''''' is the second volume in the ''[[Star Wars: Adventures in Hyperspace]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] young-reader series, which follows the [[Smuggler/Legends|smugglers]] [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]] in the employ of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba the Hutt]] prior to the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. ''Shinbone Showdown'' was written by [[Ryder Windham]], illustrated by [[Robert Rath]], and published by [[Scholastic]] in [[June]] [[2010]].

==Publisher's summary==
''After transporting rude aliens and unusual plants for Jabba Desilijic Tiure the Hutt, Han and Chewbacca are in the mood for an easy job. But after Jabba sends them to the mining planet Shinbone for delivery and pick up, Han and Chewie learn that working for Jabba is never easy.''

==Plot summary==
[[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]] visited [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba the Hutt]] in his [[Mos Eisley/Legends|Mos Eisley]] compound. Jabba then suggested Solo take a [[Smuggler/Legends|smuggling job]] for him, on the condition that he take a translator with him. Solo reluctantly agreed and took off for [[Gamorr/Legends|Gamorr]] with his [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] partner and the droid [[TC-72]]. In [[Hyperspace/Legends|hyperspace]], Solo could not stand TC-72's overly-talkative mannerism and locked the droid in the cargo bay. Once on Gamorr, Solo insulted a [[Unidentified Gamorrean farmer|Gamorrean farmer]], and she would have killed the smuggler if TC-72 hadn't been there to translate. Afterwards, Solo attempted to barter with the farmer, but she would not budge.

Back on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]], Solo reported to Jabba. Jabba blackmailed Solo into doing another job, as Solo had violated part of his contract, which said he must pay Jabba if he does business on the side. When Solo asked how Jabba knew, TC-72 shows up. Knowing he must pay back Jabba, Solo reluctantly agrees to go to the [[Mining/Legends|mining]] planet [[Shinbone]] to pick up gemstones. Carrying a load of plants, Solo and Chewbacca land on Shinbone and meet Jabba's contact, [[Torkil Mux/Legends|Torkil Mux]]. The miner was using an [[unidentified walking plant species]] to mine gems for him. Mux gives the two smugglers less gems than had been agreed on, saying it was a difficult season and accepts the delivery of the plants, which as it turns out, are the walking plants. They are grown in soil. Later, Chewbacca starts following one of the walking plants, who leads them to [[Torkil Mux's HWK-290 light freighter|Mux's ship]]. Solo and Chewbacca realize Mux has been skimming gems from Jabba, and hatch a plan to take their revenge.

Solo distracts Mux, saying the plants he brought were diseased. Chewbacca rounds up all the walking plants and places them on Mux's ship. He then takes off after receiving Solo's signal. Mux, angry at seeing his ship disappear, demands Solo bring it back. Solo denies all knowledge of the incident and suggests he and Mux's security droid draw their weapons against each other. Mux starts counting up to three, but Solo cheats and shoots first, then walks away from Mux and heads back to the [[Millennium Falcon/Legends|''Millennium Falcon'']]. In orbit, he meets up with Chewbacca and give the walking plants Mux's ship to take to [[Kashyyyk/Legends|Kashyyyk]]. Returning once again to Tatooine, the two smugglers gave the extra gemstones to Jabba.
