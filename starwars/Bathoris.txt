'''Bathoris''' was a frozen [[planet]]<ref name="Pathfinders 3" /> located in the [[Bathoris system]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories]], situated in grid square N-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> According to legend, it was home to a [[Jedi temple|temple]] of the [[Jedi Order]], where the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Caldoth]] was once defeated by the [[Jedi Master]] [[Bran Ath'Morath]].<ref name="The Gilded Cage" /> Around [[362 BBY]]<ref name="Date">The events of ''The High Republic Adventures – Pathfinders'' 3 take place twenty [[Standard year|years]] after the events of [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|''The High Republic'' Phase II]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> a team of [[Republic Pathfinder]]s visited Bathoris as part of their investigation into the death of a [[Jedi Master]], and discovered that a comms team had also been murdered on the world.<ref name="Pathfinders 3" />
