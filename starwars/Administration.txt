'''Administration''' was an organization within the [[Imperial Army]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] considered to be one of the [[army]]'s countless less glamorous units, yet vital to the army's objectives. The organization was active during the [[Imperial Era]],<ref name="Dawn" /> which lasted from [[19 BBY]] to [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Administration was mentioned in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2018]].<ref name="Dawn" />
