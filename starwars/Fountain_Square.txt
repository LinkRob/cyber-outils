The '''Fountain Square''' was a large square located in the [[Ferrix]] [[Gavalox Communications|business center]]. [[Rix Road]] culminated in this square and the main [[Hotel Rix|city hotel]] was located adjacent to it. As one of the most central locations in the city it was used for important events such as the Stone to the Sky ceremony of upstanding citizens' public [[funeral]]s<ref name="Episode 12" /> as well as [[Death penalty|executions]] which were usually accomplished by [[hanging]].<ref name="Announcement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref> In [[5 BBY]], during former [[Daughters of Ferrix]] [[president]] [[Maarva Carassi Andor]]'s funeral, a massive [[Ferrix Riot|riot]] broke out between the funeral attendants and [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] security personnel when [[Prefect (Galactic Empire)|Prefect]] [[Vanis Tigo]] violently interrupted the ceremony as it reached this square.<ref name="Episode 12" />
