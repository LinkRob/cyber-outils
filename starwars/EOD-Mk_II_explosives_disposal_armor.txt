The '''EOD-Mk II explosives disposal armor''', known simply as the '''EOD-Mk II''', was a model of [[explosives disposal suit]] manufactured by [[Creshaldyne Industries]]. It was a heavy, bulky suit designed to protect [[engineer]]s from blasts, shock, and shrapnel. The [[armor]] was composed of overlapping thick plates covered by reinforced [[polycarbonate]] mesh. Extra integrated hard plates backed by ballistic gel were also included at the groin, chest, and [[Limb|elbow]] areas. A single collar of tall, gel-backed hard plates, covered by polycarb mesh, protected the neck, while a sealed, full-face [[helmet]] covered the head. The EOD-Mk II did not come with [[glove]]s, to maximize an [[explosives ordnance disposal trooper]]'s dexterity and precision while handling [[bomb]]s. Due to its thick padding and clumsy form, the EOD-Mk II armor restricted its user's freedom of movement.<ref name="Fully Operational" />
