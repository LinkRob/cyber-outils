'''''Droid Tales''''' is a [[canon]] young-reader picture-book series from [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]. The series currently comprises two books, ''[[C-3PO Does NOT Like Sand!]]'' ([[2019]]) and ''[[R2-D2 is LOST!]]'' ([[2020]]), both written by [[Caitlin Kennedy]] and illustrated by [[Brian Kesinger]]. A third book, ''[[BB-8 and the Snow Monster]]'' was scheduled for release in [[2024]], but was later cancelled.
