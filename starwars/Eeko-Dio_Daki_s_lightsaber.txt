A [[Color|blue]]-bladed '''lightsaber''' was the personal [[lightsaber]] of the [[Mosyk]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]]. While in hiding on the [[Mid Rim Territories]] [[planet]] of [[Janix]]<ref name="Shadow of Maul 1">[[Shadow of Maul 1|''Shadow of Maul'' 1]]</ref> during the [[Imperial Era]], Daki kept his saber hidden in order to conceal his identity as a [[Jedi]].<ref name="Databank" /> He would however take up arms once more alongside his [[Padawan]] [[Devon Izara]] in order to [[Lightsaber combat|combat]] the former [[Sith]] turned [[crime lord]] [[Maul]] when the [[Zabrak]] reemerged on Janix.<ref name="Chapter4" />
