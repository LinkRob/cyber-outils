"'''Annihilated'''" is a story featuring [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] written by [[Daniel Warren Johnson]]. The story was published in the [[Darth Vader – Black, White & Red 3|third issue]] of the anthology miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Darth Vader – Black, White & Red]]'', that was published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[June 28]], [[2023]].
