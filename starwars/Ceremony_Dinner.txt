'''Ceremony Dinner''' also referred to as '''Investiture Banquets''' was a type of gathering hosted hosted by members of the [[Imperial Senate]]. [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] did not host a Ceremony Dinner until a number of years after she started serving in the senate. Eighty new members of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] senate held dinners for [[Senate Investiture]]<ref name="What a Festive Evening" /> during [[3 BBY]]<ref name="Episode Date">"[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]" established that the events of "[[What a Festive Evening]]" take place during [[3 BBY]].</ref>
