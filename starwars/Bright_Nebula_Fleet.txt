The '''Bright Nebula Fleet''' was a [[fleet]] in the [[Republic Navy|naval forces]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] that participated in a [[battle on Lokori]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Although most of the conflict took place beyond [[Lokori]]'s [[moon]]s, [[Separatist drop ship]] were able to penetrate Republic picket lines and release [[squadron]]s of [[Vulture-class starfighter|Vulture droid starfighters]] in the [[atmosphere]]. Over the course of four local [[Standard month|months]], air raids continued and battle raged in near [[Realspace|space]]. As Republic forces were bolstered by elements of the Bright Nebula Fleet, the Separatists matched the Republic [[Starship|ship]] for ship.<ref name="Catalyst" />

Eventually, Separatist forces broke through Republic defenses at the same time Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] declared himself [[Galactic Emperor]] and [[Proclamation of the New Order|proclaimed]] the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Catalyst" />
