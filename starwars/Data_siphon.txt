A '''data siphon''' was a device that could be used to steal data from data storage devices in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> By [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The flashback scenes of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', which include the robbery on [[Csilla]], take place before the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', which take place in [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Chiss]] nicknamed "[[Pontriss]]" by [[Syndic]] [[Mitth'ras'safis]], [[core name]] "Thrass," used a data siphon in an attempt to rob a [[Unidentified bistro|bistro]] on the Ascendancy [[planet]] [[Csilla]]. After the attempted robbery was thwarted by Thrass and his friend [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn," [[Police officer|patrollers]] arrived and logged the data siphon as evidence. The patrollers returned the [[Currency|payments]] Pontriss had been able to download from the bistro's register using the data siphon to the establishment.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
A data siphon appeared in the flashbacks of the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
