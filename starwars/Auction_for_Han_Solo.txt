In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the '''Auction for Han Solo''',<ref name="Aphra 12" /> also known as the '''Auction on Jekara''',<ref name="Star Wars 18">[[Star Wars (2020) 18|''Star Wars'' (2020) 18]]</ref> or as the '''auction of Han Solo''',<ref name="Crime and Punishment">{{InsiderCite|209|Crime and Punishment}}</ref> was hosted by the criminal syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]] on<ref name="Aphra 12" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Star Wars 14">[[Star Wars (2020) 14|''Star Wars'' (2020) 14]]</ref> [[Jekara]]<ref name="Aphra 12" /> for the [[carbonite]]-encased [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] after it [[Thief|stole]] Solo from the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] on the [[moon]] [[Nar Shaddaa]]. Crimson Dawn's leader, [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]], intended to use the auction to allow the great [[Galaxy|galactic]] powers witness the return of the Dawn,<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 1" /> which many believed had disappeared, though in reality they had been planning something big to announce their return.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 13">[[Bounty Hunters 13|''Bounty Hunters'' 13]]</ref>

==The auction==
Among the factions invited to the auction was [[Black Sun]], who were represented by Lord [[Gyuti]];<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 2" /> the [[Galactic Empire]], represented by [[Sub-Administrator]] [[Sly Moore]], who was also attempting to capture Solo for the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]];<ref name="Darth Vader 14" /> the [[Hutt Clan]], who were represented by the [[Grand Hutt Council]];<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 2" /> and the [[Unbroken Clan]] led by [[General]] [[Vukorah]].<ref name="Aphra 12" /> The Rebel Alliance became aware of the auction thanks to [[Amilyn Holdo]], and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], and [[Chewbacca]] traveled to Jekara to rescue Solo.<ref name="Star Wars 14" />

Also present at the auction was the criminal [[Jaylen Vrax]], who was joined by his [[BX-series droid commando]] enforcer [[ND-5]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> Sent there by [[Domina Tagge]] of [[Tagge Corporation|TaggeCo]], [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] and smuggler [[Sana Starros]] were also at the auction, but Aphra was intending to steal from the rich guests.<ref name="Aphra 12" /> Fett also arrived, intending to take Solo back. Once the auction began, multiple bids were placed for Solo, with [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] ultimately winning possession of the smuggler for one million [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 2" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Jaylen and I went to an auction she hosted. It did not end well.|ND-5|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
[[File:VaderCrashesAuction-2020DoctorAphra12.png|thumb|left|250px|Darth Vader arrived after the end of the auction to claim Solo was his property.]]
As Jabba was intending to leave Jekara, Vader arrived at the auction, claiming that Solo was his.<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 2" /> Although Jabba eventually gave up Solo, Qira demanded the Empire provide the credits instead and after Vader refused, Qi'ra decided to [[Duel on the Vermillion|fight]] the Sith Lord, due to her experience with her former boss Maul. Vader quickly disarmed Qira and prepared to kill her, but&mdash;with Vader sensing that his son, [[Luke Skywalker]], had arrived<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 3">[[War of the Bounty Hunters 3|''War of the Bounty Hunters'' 3]]</ref> after having been busy with the [[Battle of Ab Dalis]]<ref name="SW 15">[[Star Wars (2020) 15|''Star Wars'' (2020) 15]]</ref>&mdash;Qira quickly slipped away. Vader ordered his [[Star Destroyer]] to contact Skywalker, which they did, and then Vader delivered his ultimatum to his son: if the young [[Jedi]] did not face him, he would cut Solo in half. Luke Skywalker calmed his friends and used [[the Force]] to communicate to his father that he would face him in his X-wing. Vader agreed to face his son and ordered Solo be taken to his Star Destroyer.<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 3" /> While an Imperial shuttle took Solo up, the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' trailed them and, with [[Lobot]]'s help, they disabled it, but a [[seismic charge]] from ''[[Slave I]]'' forced them out of the way. But before Fett, who had been joined by cyborg bounty hunter [[Beilert Valance]], could reach the shuttle, the Hutts launched all of the ships to the shuttle, thus revealing why they let Vader take Solo.<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 4">[[War of the Bounty Hunters 4|''War of the Bounty Hunters'' 4]]</ref>

During the chaos, Doctor Aphra recovered a Crimson Dawn spy's necklace with vital underworld information, but she got caught and imprisoned.<ref name="Aphra 13">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 13|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 13]]</ref> However, after escaping prison with her crew, they nearly re-stole the necklace, only for [[Deathstick]] to intervene.<ref name="Aphra 14">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 14|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 14]]</ref> The events of the auction were largely unknown to the wider galaxy.<ref name="OSWM 117">{{GermanFanCite|117|Database}}</ref>
