The '''Dawinia system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Hynestian sector]] of the [[Colonies]]. It was located in grid square J-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Dawinian]] [[species]].<ref name="Lando 4" />
