'''''Ewoks Join the Fight''''' is a young-reader book in the [[World of Reading]] series by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]. It retells events from [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] with a focus on the [[Ewok]]s. Written by [[Michael Siglain]] with illustrations by [[Pilot Studio]], the title is a [[canon]] retelling of the events from the [[1983]] [[The Ewoks Join the Fight (storybook)|children's storybook]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Luke, Han, and Princess Leia make unlikely friends with the furry Ewoks of Endor. This level 1 early reader retells classic scenes from ''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'' as the Ewoks join the rebellion against the evil Galactic Empire. Featuring beautiful full-color illustrations, this World of Reading book is perfect for little ''Star Wars'' fans who are ready to read on their own.''<ref name="amazon" />

==Plot summary==
There is a [[Death Star II|new Death Star]] being built near [[Endor]], and [[Han Solo|Han]], [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]], and [[Chewbacca]] are sent to the forest moon on a mission by the [[Rebel Alliance|rebels]]. The assignment is to find a bunker, in which there is a machine that protects the Death Star, and destroy said machine.

While searching, the rebels find two [[Scout trooper|biker scouts]] guarding the bunker, and Han and Chewie beat them up to stop them from warning the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]]. The scouts escape, so Luke and Leia pursue them on [[Speeder bike|speeder bikes]].

They split up, and both take down a biker scout each, but Leia's speeder breaks down. An Ewok approaches Leia, and she feeds him, then both are attacked by a third biker scout, but they fight him off and make friends.

The Ewok takes Leia to his village, while the rest of them search for her, along with [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]]. The rebels are ensnared in a rope trap by the Ewoks, but R2-D2 cuts them down. The Ewoks tie up the rebels, take them to their village, and do not listen to Leia when she tells them the rebels are her friends.

Luke uses the [[The Force|Force]] to levitate C-3PO, tricking the Ewoks into thinking he has special powers, and they fearfully free the rebels. They learn that the Ewok leader is named [[Chirpa|Chief Chirpa]] and the one who helped Leia is named [[Wicket Wystri Warrick|Wicket]].

C-3PO tells the Ewoks about the rebels, and they agree to help them and take them to the bunker. There are more biker scouts inside the bunker, leading to a [[Battle of Endor|big fight]] between them and the rebels and Ewoks. Eventually, the bunker and the new Death Star are destroyed, making the rebels and Ewoks heroes.
