'''Alyla Cylone''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Nothoiin (species)|Nothoiin]] and an operative of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. She and two other Alliance operatives arrived in the [[Anoat sector]] shortly before the [[Iron Blockade]] went up in response to the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Battle of Endor|defeat]] at [[Endor]]. Despite her claim to have never been to the [[sector]] before, her local knowledge proved otherwise.<ref name="Cylone" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
Months into the blockade, Cylone joined the crew of a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]], assisting them on various missions.<ref name="Cylone" />
