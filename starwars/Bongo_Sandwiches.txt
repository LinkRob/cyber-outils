'''Bongo Sandwiches''' was a commercial establishment on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|First Death Star]]. It offered [[Fatfish/Legends|fatfish]], [[Gorg/Legends|chuba]] chips and [[nyork]] meat to paying customers.
