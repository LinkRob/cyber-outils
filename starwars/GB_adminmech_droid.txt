The '''GB adminmech droid'''<ref name="Every Droid">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Droid in Star Wars|video=UQg6aX7lqTA|nolive=1}}</ref> was a type of [[adminmech droid]]. The GB adminmech [[droid]] [[GB-BD]] was forced to work in the control room of the [[Spice mines of Kessel|spice mines]] of [[Kessel]] for the [[Pyke Syndicate]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> When the droid [[L3-37]] initiated a [[Raid on Kessel|uprising]] by removing the [[restraining bolt]]s of droids in the control room, GB-BD was freed.<ref name="Solo" />
