"'''Fast Friends'''" is the second episode of the [[Return of the Jedi (radio)|''Return of the Jedi'' radio drama]]. It first aired on [[National Public Radio]] on [[November 12]], [[1996]]<ref name="Kushner" />. It adapts events from {{Film|VI}} depicting the [[Rescue of Han Solo/Legends|rescue of Han Solo]] and the [[Battle of the Great Pit of Carkoon]].

==Plot summary==
[[File:BobaFett-RotJRadio.flac|thumb|Boba Fett taunts Han Solo in his cell.]]
A disoriented [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] is thrown into a cell already containing [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]]. Chewie tries to reassure his friend that [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] will arrive soon to complete the rescue plan; Han is skeptical. [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]] comes along to gloat over Han's impending death. Up in the audience chamber, [[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Lando Calrissian]] reveals himself to the captive [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia]]: he is posing as a guard for [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba]] under the alias of [[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Tamtel Skreej]]. Both Leia and Lando acknowledge that they are stuck in their positions until Luke arrives.

Luke indeed arrives soon after. He deftly uses a [[Mind trick/Legends|mind trick]] to coerce [[Bib Fortuna/Legends|Bib Fortuna]] into admitting him into the palace and then into the audience chamber. Jabba berates Fortuna as a weak-minded fool. He refuses to bargain with Luke, resists his efforts at mental manipulation, and laughs off his threats. Jabba then opens the trapdoor that sends Luke into the pit of the savage [[Rancor/Legends|rancor]], together with an unfortunate guard. The guard ignores Luke's attempts to help him and is quickly eaten. Luke evades the rancor as Leia shouts advice and encouragement. She gives Luke the idea to lure the creature under its own gate. Luke tosses a bone to activate the mechanism, crushing the rancor under the door's weight. Jabba, enraged, tells his guards to bring in Han and Chewbacca. With [[C-3PO/Legends|See-Threepio]] translating, Jabba announces that all three will now be fed to the almighty [[Sarlacc/Legends|Sarlacc]] while he and his court watch and feast aboard his [[Khetanna/Legends|sail barge]]. 

Aboard the barge, Threepio is reunited with [[R2-D2/Legends|Artoo-Detoo]], who is serving drinks. Artoo assures his counterpart that this is all part of the plan and that he is about to to get into position&mdash;a statement that Threepio does not understand&mdash;while Threepio returns to duty translating for Jabba. Aboard a [[Skiff/Legends|sand skiff]] with the other condemned prisoners, Luke is led onto a plank. Lando, still disguised as a guard, removes Luke's [[Binders/Legends|binders]], whereupon the plot is sprung. Artoo launches [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber/Legends|Luke's lightsaber]] into the air for Luke to catch. He ignites it, and he and Lando begin to take control of the skiff. Han, still mostly blind, needs Lando to explain what is happening. 

[[File:Han-Skiff-RotJRadio.flac|thumb|Lando explains to Han what is happening at the start of the battle.]]
New threats emerge from the barge's [[Me/7 double laser cannon/Legends|pivot gun]], which fires on the skiff, and Boba Fett, who uses [[Z-6 jetpack/Legends|his jetpack]] to fly over. The gun knocks both Fett and Lando overboard. While Luke jumps over to fight the guards on a second skiff, Han and Chewie try to extend Lando a spear to pull him back up. Then Fett returns to the skiff. Han attacks blindly, striking Fett's jetpack; it flies him directly into the Sarlacc's pit. Han and Chewbacca return to helping Lando; Han has to shoot at the Sarlacc's tentacle in order to save him, and Chewbacca pulls them both back aboard. They steer the skiff away from the barge, unsure exactly what is happening to Luke, Leia, and the others still inside it.

==Continuity==
"Fast Friends" opens with two scenes that are original to the radio drama: Boba Fett's visit to Han and Chewbacca's cell and Lando's conversation with Leia while in disguise. After this, the episode closely follows the events of {{Film|VI}}. The radio scene in the rancor pit has Leia, rather than Luke, think of trapping the rancor under the heavy door.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi">{{Film|VI}}</ref> In this way the audio drama can use Leia's dialogue to explain what is happening.

At the end of the episode, Han pointedly asks Chewbacca and then Lando if they can confirm that Boba Fett is really dead; neither can answer for sure. This connects the episode to the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Empire|Dark Empire]]'' comic series (published in [[1991]]&ndash;[[1992]]), in which Fett is revealed to have survived his fall into the Sarlacc's throat.<ref name="Dark Empire 4">[[Dark Empire 4|''Dark Empire'' 4]]</ref>
