'''Chalacta''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="The Secret Jedi" /> located in the [[Metatessu sector]]'s [[Chalacta system]] within the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="Appendix" /> that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Chalactan]] people.<ref name="HC 9">{{HelmetCollectionCite|9|Databank A-Z}}</ref> The [[human]]<ref name="Join the Battle" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Depa Billaba]] was [[Reproduction|born]] on Chalacta.<ref name="The Secret Jedi" /> In the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] [[Standard year|year]] [[36 BBY|7941]], Chalacta was [[Senator|represented]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] by [[Yudrish Sedran]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files" />
