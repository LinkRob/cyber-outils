'''Brentaal Minor''' was a location that shared its name with<ref name="TRS">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Brentaal IV]].<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> During the [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Valo]]<ref name="TRS" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the manager [[Pall Sleko]] reflected that he was pleased his client, the [[Mon Calamari]] [[opera]] [[Musician|singer]] [[Gender|Madam]] [[Trangess Conserra]], had spent their journey to the world warming up her vocal cords, which he considered the most heavily insured "this side of Brentaal Minor."<ref name="TRS" />
