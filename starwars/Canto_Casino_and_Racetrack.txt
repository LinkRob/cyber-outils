The '''Canto Casino and Racetrack''', also known simply as the '''Canto Casino''', was a complex located in [[Canto Bight]]<ref name="Databank" /> on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> It was made up of a [[casino]] known as the [[Crescent Royale Casino]],<ref name="Aphra 4">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 4|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 4]]</ref> other casinos, a [[racetrack]],<ref name="Databank" /> a luxury [[hotel]], a shopping concourse, and twenty-two [[restaurant]]s.<ref name="TLJ Novel">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:Rose_Finn_Canto_Bight_Empire_Magazine.jpg|thumb|250px|left|Rose and Finn entered to casino to find the Master Codebreaker.]]
In [[34 ABY]], during their search for [[Maz Kanata]]'s associate [[Master Codebreaker]] in their attempt to help the [[Resistance]] to escape from the [[First Order]]'s clutches, [[Finn]], [[Rose Tico]], and [[BB-8]] [[Mission to Canto Bight|traveled]] to the Canto Casino at [[Canto Bight]], which was located near the Canto Bight [[racetrack]], where they witnessed a [[Fathier racing|fathier race]]. Although they were incapacitated with [[stun]] blasts by the [[Canto Bight Police Department]] and hauled off to the [[Canto Bight police headquarters]], Finn and Rose managed to escape from the jail to the racetrack thanks to the help of [[DJ]] and BB-8.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

Once at the racetrack, Finn and Tico went to hide from the police and started to plan how they were going to get out of the [[city]]. They ultimately chose to escape with a [[fathier]]. [[Temiri Blagg]], a stable boy who took care of the fathiers at the racetrack, considered calling the racetrack's security, but Finn and Rose managed to convince him and his friends that they were from the Resistance, giving the boy Tico's [[Rose Tico's Resistance ring|Resistance ring]]. Immediately, Finn and Tico released the fathiers and they escaped from Canto Bight.<ref name="Episode VIII" />
