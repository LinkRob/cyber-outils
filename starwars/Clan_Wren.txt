'''Clan Wren''', also known as the '''Wren clan''', was a [[Mandalorian clan]] that was a vassal of [[House Vizsla]]. Clan Wren was led by [[Countess]] [[Ursa Wren]] and consisted of her husband [[Alrich Wren]], their son [[Tristan Wren|Tristan]], and their daughter [[Sabine Wren|Sabine]], and was composed of [[Mandalorian]] [[warrior]]s that donned [[Mandalorian armor]].

Clan Wren was sided with the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]]-aligned [[Clan Saxon]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. However, in [[2 BBY]], Clan Wren led an insurrection against Clan Saxon and the Empire during the [[Mandalorian Civil War (Imperial Era)|Mandalorian Civil War]]. One notable member was Sabine, who joined [[Alliance to Restore the Republic#the rebellion|the rebellion]].

By the end of the [[Galactic Civil War]], all members of Clan Wren had been killed during the [[Night of a Thousand Tears]] with the exception of Sabine.

==History==
===Clone Wars===
During the [[Clone Wars]], Clan Wren aligned itself with [[Clan Vizsla]] and the [[Death Watch]], which earned the clan the enmity of [[Fenn Rau]] and his [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protectors]].<ref name="The Protector of Concord Dawn" /> Ursa Wren joined the [[Mandalore resistance]] under Lady [[Bo-Katan Kryze]].<ref name="Dangerous Debt">{{TCW|Dangerous Debt}}</ref> She fought alongside the resistance and the Republic's [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army's]] [[332nd Company]] during the [[Siege of Mandalore]].<ref name="OFNF">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref>

===Imperial vassals===
[[File:UrsaWren-WotG.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Clan Wren was led by Countess Ursa Wren during the Imperial Era.]]
During the [[Imperial Era]], Clan Wren was ruled by [[Countess]] [[Ursa Wren]], who resided on the planet [[Krownest]].<ref name="Databank" /> Her daughter Sabine was a [[Imperial cadet|cadet]] at the [[Imperial Academy of Mandalore|Imperial Academy]] on Mandalore. During this time, Sabine's talents were used by the [[Galactic Empire]], without her consent, to subjugate the [[Mandalorian]]s.<ref name="Trials of the Darksaber">{{Rebels|Trials of the Darksaber}}</ref> One of these weapons was the [[Arc Pulse Generator]] known as the "Duchess," which was capable of reacting with the [[beskar]] alloy inside [[Mandalorian armor]]; effectively turning priceless heirlooms against their wearers.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />

When Sabine spoke against the Empire, her family sided with the Empire, branding her a traitor. Sabine abandoned the [[Imperial Academy]] and her home, became a bounty hunter, and eventually joined the [[Spectres]], a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic#the rebellion|rebel cell]].<ref name="Trials of the Darksaber" /> To ensure the family's loyalty, [[Governor]] [[Gar Saxon]] imprisoned Ursa's husband and Sabine’s father [[Alrich Wren]] as a hostage on [[Mandalore]]. [[Tristan Wren]], Sabine's younger brother, served in Saxon's elite platoon of [[Imperial Super Commando]]s in order to prove his family's loyalty to [[Clan Saxon]] and regain some status.<ref name="Legacy of Mandalore" />

===Sabine's return===
[[File:Krownest.png|thumb|right|250px|The ''[[Phantom II]]'' approaching Krownest]]
After years of hiding from her past, Kanan convinced Sabine in [[2 BBY]]<ref name="Trials of the Darksaber date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> to take up the mantle of the [[Darksaber]], an ancient [[lightsaber]] that had been used to unite the Mandalorian clans. After mastering the Darksaber and overcoming her inner fears,<ref name="Trials of the Darksaber" /> Sabine returned to her family home on Krownest. Sabine tried to mend her strained relationship with her mother while [[Kanan Jarrus|Kanan]] and his apprentice [[Ezra Bridger]] tried to convince her to aid the Rebellion. In an attempt to save her daughter, Ursa tried to trade Kanan and Ezra with Governor Saxon for Sabine. However, Saxon reneged on the deal and ordered his Imperial Super Commandos to destroy Clan Wren.<ref name="Legacy of Mandalore" />

Tristan chose to stand by his family. Clan Wren then defeated Saxon's Super Commandos with the help of the rebels and Rau, who had also joined the rebellion. Sabine defeated Saxon in [[lightsaber combat]] but chose to spare his life. Saxon then tried to kill Sabine only to be shot dead by Ursa, who reconciled with her daughter. Saxon's death triggered a [[Mandalorian Civil War (Imperial Era)|civil war]] among the Mandalorian clans. While Ezra and Kanan returned to the Rebellion, Sabine and Rau stayed behind to help Clan Wren and its allies fight against Clan Saxon and the Empire.<ref name="Legacy of Mandalore" />

===New allies and challenges===
By the time of the [[Battle of Atollon]], Clan Wren was keeping ground against Clan Saxon and its Imperial backers. Despite these losses, Sabine managed to obtain permission from her mother to take a detachment of Mandalorian volunteers and ships including Tristan and Rau to break the [[Imperial Navy|Imperial]] Navy’s blockade of [[Phoenix Cell]]'s [[Chopper Base]]. Clan Wren's forces managed to destroy an [[Interdictor-class heavy cruiser|''Interdictor''-class heavy cruiser]], allowing the remnants of Phoenix Cell to escape to [[Yavin 4]]. As a result, Clan Wren gained the favor of Captain [[Hera Syndulla]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

[[File:ClanWrenCommandos-SWZ91_JB_Casacop.png|thumb|left|300px|Commandos of Clan Wren]]
Clan Wren and members of the Spectres later took part in a [[Mission to Mandalore|mission]] to rescue Alrich from a [[Mandalore prison outpost|Imperial prison outpost]]. Gar’s brother and successor as Governor [[Tiber Saxon]] ordered Alrich's public termination in [[Sundari]]. Despite seizing the prison with the help of Lady [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] and her warriors, they discovered Alrich was gone. Using information supplied by Ursa, Clan Wren and its rebel and Clan Kryze allies managed to rescue Alrich from an Imperial convoy. However, this victory was overshadowed by news that Governor Saxon had deployed the "Duchess" against Ursa and Tristan's forces.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />

Ursa and Tristan survived but most of their forces were wiped out by the "Duchess," which reacted with their armor and turned their wearers to ash. With the help of Bo-Katan, the surviving Clan Wren members and rebels managed to escape to Bo-Katan's camp. Due to her role in creating the "Duchess," Sabine's presence drew hostility from several of Bo-Katan's followers. However, Ursa warned that anyone who harmed her daughter would attract the wrath of her clan. To atone for her past actions, Sabine agreed to lead a strike mission to destroy the arc pulse generator and the associated data, which were housed aboard [[Tiber Saxon's Star Destroyer|Saxon's Star Destroyer]] above Sundari.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />

Ursa offered to lead the attack but Rau countered that she could barely hold a blaster due to her injuries. Instead, Tristan accompanied his older sister on the strike team. While Tristan and Rau fought with the Imperial defenders in the Star Destroyer's hangar, Sabine and Bo-Katan sought out the weapon. Despite being ambushed by Tiber Saxon, Sabine managed to turn the weapon against Saxon's [[stormtrooper]]s before destroying the weapon and Saxon's Star Destroyer. After destroying the weapon, Sabine convinced Bo-Katan to assume ownership of the Darksaber and the mantle of leadership over the Mandalorian clans. Clan Wren then joined the other Mandalorian clans in pledging [[Mandalorian resistance|allegiance]] to Bo-Katan.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />

===Great Purge of Mandalore and destruction===
[[File:MandalorianResistanceClanWren-2025RebUnit.png|thumb|300px|right|The skilled warriors of Clan Wren (pictured) did not survive the lifespan of the Galactic Empire.]]
{{Quote|Ahsoka became afraid that Sabine was training to be a Jedi for the wrong reasons after what happened on Mandalore.''"<br />"''Which was?''"<br />"''At the end of the war, the Empire purged the entire surface of the planet, killing hundreds of thousands.''"<br />"''Her family?''"<br />"''Were all lost, sadly. At the time, Ahsoka felt that if Sabine unlocked her potential, she would become dangerous.|[[Huyang]] and Ezra Bridger|<ref name="Part Eight">{{Ahsoka|Part Eight: The Jedi, the Witch, and the Warlord}}</ref>}}
During the [[Great Purge of Mandalore]], all members of Clan Wren along with Sabine's family were wiped out. Sabine was the only known survivor during the aftermath of the Galactic Civil War and formation of the [[New Republic]] and this tragedy was later used by [[Dark Jedi|fallen]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Baylan Skoll]] to persuade Sabine to surrender, promising in return that she would be given the opportunity to [[Quest to find Ezra Bridger|find]] the missing Bridger, who Sabine considered the only family she still had along with Syndulla and her son since Jarrus died years ago.<ref name="Part Four">{{Ahsoka|Part Four: Fallen Jedi}}</ref>
