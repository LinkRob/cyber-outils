An '''apartment''' on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] served as the residence of [[Senator]] [[Christo]].

==Description==
[[Christo]]'s apartment<ref name="Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/pursuit-of-peace-concept-art-gallery|text="Pursuit of Peace" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> featured a large aquarium and a view of the [[Coruscant]] skyline.<ref name="Pursuit of Peace" />

==History==
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Senator]]s [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and [[Onaconda Farr]] visited Senator Christo in his apartment to discuss the [[Republic military enhancement bill|military spending bill]].<ref name="Pursuit of Peace" />

==Behind the scenes==
Christo's apartment first appeared in "[[Pursuit of Peace]]," the eleventh episode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]].<ref name="Pursuit of Peace" /> It was identified as Christo's apartment in concept art<ref name="Concept Art Gallery" /> included in the accompanying [[The Clone Wars Episode Guides|episode guide]] on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/senate-murders-episode-guide|text="Senate Murders" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>
