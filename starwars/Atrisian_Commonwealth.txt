The '''Atrisian Commonwealth''' was a [[sector]] in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It contained the [[Frisal system|Frisal]], [[Kiliea system|Kiliea]], and [[Kitel Phard system]]s, all of which were situated in grid square J-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The latter also contained the celestial body [[Atrisia]],<ref name="R1UVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref><ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-09-15.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-09-15}}</ref> and it sat at the endpoint of the [[hyperspace route]] known as the [[Giju Run]]<ref name="GijuRun" /> that linked it to the [[Deymasoll system]] of the [[Colonies]].<ref name="TSWGalaxy">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoVGDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> the Kitel Phard [[Star system|system]] fell within the borders of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="MandoVG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Atrisian Commonwealth was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Atrisian Commonwealth/Legends|Atrisian Commonwealth]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via a mention in [[Timothy S. O'Brien|Timothy O'Brien's]] article "[[From the Files of Corellia Antilles]]," which was published as part of the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 14|fourteenth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'' in [[August]] [[1997]].<ref name="Files">{{JournalCite|14|From the Files of Corellia Antilles}}</ref> The [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' provided the first visual depiction of the [[Sector/Legends|sector]] on a map created by [[Modi]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
