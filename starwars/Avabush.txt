'''Avabushes''' were a type of [[bush]] that was grown in greenhouses in the [[Barosian Avabush Arboretum]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> By [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' describes the [[Heist in the Barosian Avabush Arboretum|heist]] taking place prior to the events of {{Film|IX}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[35 ABY]].</ref> the criminal [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] had completed a [[Heist in the Barosian Avabush Arboretum|heist]] in the arboretum during which they stole an entire greenhouse.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], avabushes were mentioned in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> The [[Avabush/Legends|bushes]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first mentioned in ''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]'', a [[1989]] supplement written by [[Troy Denning]] for [[West End Games]]'' [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="GG4">''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]''</ref>
