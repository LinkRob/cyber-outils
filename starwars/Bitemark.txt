The '''''Bitemark''''' was a [[starship]] that was purchased by the [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]]<ref name="FACPOV" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of "[[Tooth and Claw]]" take place in parallel to the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Shortly after the purchase, the ship was destroyed during an explosive firefight on the [[Jurzan spaceport]] between the bounty hunter and the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]].<ref name="FACPOV" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Bitemark'' was mentioned in "[[Tooth and Claw]]," a short story written by [[Michael Kogge]] and published in the ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]'' anthology in [[2020]].<ref name="FACPOV" />
