'''Brian Herring''' is an English puppeteer and actor who performed [[BB-8]], the [[porg]]s, [[fathier]]s, and numerous other creatures and droids in the [[sequel trilogy]]. He appeared alongside fellow BB-8 puppeteer [[Dave Chapman]] as a Resistance Officer in [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII: ''The Last Jedi'']]. He also worked as a creature and droid performer (including SE-2) in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' and played [[Hirang Birren]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.
