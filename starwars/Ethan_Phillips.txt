'''Ethan Phillips''' (born [[February 8]], [[1950s|1955]]) provided voices for several ''[[Star Wars]]'' video games. He is probably best known for his portrayal of [[MemoryAlpha:Neelix|Neelix]] in [[MemoryAlpha:Star Trek: Voyager|''Star Trek: Voyager'']].
