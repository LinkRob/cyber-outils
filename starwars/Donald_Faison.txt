'''Donald Adeosun Faison''' (born [[June 22]], [[1974]])<ref name="SWYT 25-06-2020" /> is a voice actor who provides the voice of [[Hype Fazon]] in ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', as well as that of the [[Unidentified super tactical droid (Yerbana)|super tactical droid]] in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Old Friends Not Forgotten]]." He previously appeared in ''[[Robot Chicken: Star Wars Episode II]]'', ''[[Robot Chicken: Star Wars Episode III]]'', and the unreleased ''[[Star Wars Detours]]''.
