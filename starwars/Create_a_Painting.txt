The '''Create a Painting''' contest was a competition held by [[Sony|Sony Online Entertainment]] in [[2008]]. Players could submit their own custom [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]-themed artwork to Sony, who would choose three finalists for both the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and would then allow the player-base to vote for the winner on their forums. The Rebel winner was ''[[The March to Restuss]]'' created by the player known as [[Guillemette]] of the [[Bria]] server, and the Imperial winner was ''[[United in Our Cause]]'' created by the player known as [[Zel (Star Wars Galaxies)|Zel]] of the [[Bloodfin (disambiguation)|Bloodfin]] server.
