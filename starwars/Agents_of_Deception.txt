'''''Agents of Deception''''' is the third expansion of [[Star Wars Galaxies Trading Card Game]], released on [[June 24]], [[2009]]. It incorporates more than 200 new cards, including more than 20 new Loot cards, including the [[Corellian/Legends|Corellian]] [[G9 Rigger-class light freighter/Legends|G9 Rigger freighter]].

This set introduces [[Smuggler/Legends|smugglers]] and [[Spy/Legends|spies]] into the game, each adding new elements to gameplay. Smuggler cards utilize '''item''' cards in a new way by bringing items into play when your opponent least expects it and by adding bonuses to existing items to enhance their powers. Spies rely on stealth to get behind enemy lines and deal covert damage beyond typical unit-to-unit damage, plus can launch surprise attacks on your opponent's '''avatar''' itself.

==Plot summary==
The story mode of this set, represented in '''Scenarios''', revolves around a smuggler and spy story arc that puts players in the service of [[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Lando Calrissian]] as well as the dreaded [[Imperial Security Bureau/Legends|Imperial Security Bureau]] headed by the mysterious figure [[Cronal|Blackhole]], and even allows them to infiltrate [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba the Hutt's]] infamous crime syndicate. As the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]] between the Rebel Alliance and the Empire continues, the growth of the lucrative and dangerous smuggling market has expanded to include goods essential to the successes of both the Empire and Rebellion. A key shipment of [[Ryll/Legends|ryll]] has gone missing on its way to a Rebel medical facility. Rumored to have been stolen and taken to [[Smuggler's Run/Legends|Smuggler's Run]], a hidden collection of asteroids serving as a hideout for hundreds of smugglers, this shipment is essential to the Rebel war efforts. The newly appointed [[Baron/Legends|Baron]] of [[Cloud City/Legends|Cloud City]], Lando Calrissian, leads a Rebel team to infiltrate the Hutt crime cartel to track and locate the lost shipment. But the Empire has plans of its own and has ordered the Imperial Security Bureau, led by Blackhole, to track down and locate the shipment for itself.
