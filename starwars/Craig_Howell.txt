'''Craig Howell''' is an illustrator, designer, and Art Director at the Cheeba Productions studio who has created cover art for three ''[[Star Wars]]'' novels.

He also created illustrations for the [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', and ''Indiana Jones and the Kingdom of the Crystal Skull'' style guides. Howell was also the illustrator of the [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'']] 30th anniversary logo, the [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Empire Strikes Back'']] 30th anniversary logo, the ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' logo, and the ''[[Star Wars Detours]]'' logo. Additionally, Howell was featured at the [[Celebration IV]] and [[Celebration V]] art shows. Howell is also credited with creating packaging illustrations for the ''Star Wars'' "electronic helmets" series released by [[Hasbro]] in [[2012]] and in [[2013]].
