'''Corellian Spike Cold Brew''' was an [[alcohol]]ic [[caf]] beverage served at [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE Date">The events of ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'' and those within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The drink shares its name with the [[sabacc]] variety [[Corellian Spike]]. It was suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="Oga's Cantina" />
