The '''findsmen''' (singular: '''findsman''') were a [[Force-sensitive]]<ref name="DoH">''[[Disciples of Harmony]]''</ref> mystic tradition of the [[Gand]] [[species]] that dated back [[Standard century|centuries]] before the [[Galactic Civil War]] on their fog-shrouded [[homeworld]] of [[Gand (planet)|Gand]]. Findsmen were known for their uncanny tracking skills.<ref name="SWCE:UaE" /> Using [[the Force]]<ref name="DoH" /> as a sixth sense, findsmen worked as expert trackers and [[hunter]]s<ref name="Secrets of the Bounty Hunters">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref> after passing the [[Great Rite]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 32">[[Bounty Hunters 32|''Bounty Hunters'' 32]]</ref> The [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] doubted their skills, as most findsmen remained on Gand itself instead of traveling across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Secrets of the Bounty Hunters" />

When the tradition began to die out on Gand, a Gand known as [[Zuckuss]] became one of the first to venture off-world, using his particular talents to become a renowned and highly sought-after [[bounty hunter]].<ref name="SWCE:UaE" /> The findsman Zuckuss was able to predict the future of [[Boba Fett]].<ref name="WOTBH1">[[War of the Bounty Hunters 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters'' 1]]</ref>

At the time of the [[Iron Blockade]], the findsman [[Vakk]] operated in the [[Anoat sector]]. Unusual even among his mystically minded peers, Vakk was strictly non-violent, using his skills only to track and discover but never to apprehend or kill.<ref name="SW:U Vakk">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Vakk"</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Originally appearing unnamed in the [[1980]] film {{Film|V}}, the findsmen were first identified as such in ''[[Galaxy Guide 3: The Empire Strikes Back]]'', a [[1989]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] supplement written by [[Michael Stern]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="GG3:TESB">''[[Galaxy Guide 3: The Empire Strikes Back]]''</ref> The term became [[canon]] when it was utilized in ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', a reference book co-written by [[Tricia Barr|Patricia Barr]], [[Adam Bray]], [[Daniel Wallace]], and [[Ryder Windham]] that was released in [[2015]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>
