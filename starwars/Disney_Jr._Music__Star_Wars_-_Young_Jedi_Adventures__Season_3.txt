'''''Disney Jr. Music: Star Wars - Young Jedi Adventures: Season 3''''' is a three-track single composed by [[Andy Bean]], for the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Three|third season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''. The soundtrack was released digitally on [[December 8]], 2025, by [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]], concurrently with the season.<ref name="qobuz" />
