The '''Day of Demand''' was a [[Standard day|day]] on which the [[Prince|heir]] to the [[throne]] of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] would claim their right to the throne. It was held on the sixteenth anniversary of the individual's [[Name Day]]. During the ceremony, the individual would name three challenges, one each of the [[Challenge of the Body|body]], [[Challenge of the Mind|mind]], and [[Challenge of the Heart|heart]]. If these challenges were deemed worthy, the heir would be tasked with completing the challenges to prove themselves worthy of being invested as the crown prince or princess of Alderaan.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

==Description==
The ceremony followed an established script, involving the heir, monarch, and [[consort]]. If the heir was adopted, they would add the words "it is known" to their first lines announcing they had come of age. The heir was also required to state their challenges of the [[Challenge of the Body|Body]], [[Challenge of the Mind|Mind]], and [[Challenge of the Heart|Heart]], in that order. A banquet was also traditionally held after and usually only a "handful" of off-worlders were present for the ceremony.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

When Leia Organa completed her own Day of Demand she was able to drink true [[Alderaanian wine]] [[Toniray]] rather than the [[Toniray (diluted)|diluted version]] of the [[wine]] she'd been allowed to drink previously.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

==History==
The ceremony dated back to the founding of the [[Alderaanian monarchy]]. The requirement of the [[Prince|heir]] to prove themselves worthy was seen as a primary reason for the [[Alderaanian]] [[monarchy]]'s longevity. In early Days of Demand, princes and princesses would have to fight their way to the [[throne]] room to make their demand, which was remembered in later ceremonies by the carrying of the [[Rhindon Sword]].<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Leia Organa's Day of Demand|one such Day of Demand]] was held for Princess [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] with several of her parents political allies present including [[Mon Mothma]] of [[Chandrila]].<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Day of Demand first appeared in the [[2017]] young-adult novel ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />
