'''Czerka Arms''', also known as the '''Czerka Corporation''',<ref name="Master & Apprentice" /> was an arms manufacturer that produced the [[Adventurer slugthrower rifle]], which was used by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Aurra Sing]] during the final [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="USW" /> They also manufactured combat gear, such as the [[ACH-14 advanced combat helmet]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> Czerka Arms made heavy use of [[Slavery|slave]] labor and employed extensive mechanisms to both prevent slave escapes and recapture those who did.<ref name="Master & Apprentice" />

[[Princess]] [[Fanry]] of the planet [[Pijal]] described the Czerka Corporation as being nearly as powerful as the [[Galactic Senate]]. Czerka was thousands of years old, and rumored by some to be older than the Republic itself.<ref name="Master & Apprentice" />

==History==
===Ancient history and High Republic Era===
[[File:Wookiee_trench.png|thumb|left|300px|The Wookiees of Kashyyyk (members of the species pictured) rose up against the occupying Czerka Corporation.]]
Four [[Millennium|millennia]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]] was discovered by the Czerka Corporation, who renamed the planet G5-623, and later to Edean.<ref name="BMF 43" /> Using superior technology, the company managed to [[Slavery|enslave]] the [[Wookiee]]s until an [[Rebellion on Kashyyyk|uprising]] drove the invaders away.<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> Over its history, the company made a mess of planets that it exploited for natural resources.<ref name="Into the Light">''[[The High Republic: Into the Light]]''</ref>

Around [[387 BBY]], a plot of land on the [[Eirie|Timekeeper Moon]] was handed over to Czerka by the government of [[Eiram]], after its neighbor and rival [[E'ronoh]] failed to pay tariffs on their lease of the land. This contributed to [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|renewed violence]] between the two planets, as E'ronoh believed that Eiram had stolen their centuries-old colony. The E'roni defended the colony, including chasing Czerka out of the system, a move which [[Monarch of E'ronoh|Monarch]] [[A'lbaran]] later defended by pointing out that Czerka had been polluting Eiram's oceans.<ref name="The High Republic: Convergence">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]''</ref> [[Romila Kaveh]] worked as an [[acquisition negotiator]] for Czerka<ref name="Into the Light" /> by [[228 BBY]]. In that year,<ref name="Into the Light Date">{{IntotheLightDate}}</ref> she traveled to<ref name="Into the Light" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|kenari|Kenari}}</ref> [[Kenari]] and hoped to convincing the native [[Kenari (people)|Kenari]] to allow the company to open up [[gas]] [[mining|mines]] on the world.<ref name="Into the Light">''[[The High Republic: Into the Light]]''</ref>

===Late Republic Era===
{{Quote|Czerka Corporation will own Pijal no more!''"<br />"''Princess Fanry, please, wait! You don't know what you're doing. You're hardly more than a child&mdash;''"<br />"''A child? There are queens my age on Naboo! Princes my age on Toydaria! Alderaan's queen took Princess Breha to help her negotiate a treaty, and she's younger than I am! [[File:O1-PeackMomokoCover.png|thumb|right|250px|Jedi Obi-Wan Kenobi and Qui-Gon Jinn (both pictured) discovered the complex situation on Pijal.]]
In [[40 BBY]], Czerka Corporation sought a permanent legal influence on the planet [[Pijal]] by way of inserting themselves into a [[Governance Treaty|treaty]] that would shift the planet from an absolute monarchy to a democratically elected [[Assembly (Pijal)|Assembly]]. The corporation had been in alliance with Pijal for centuries and had slowly been given more power, including overseeing the prison system. This allowed them to hand out long sentences of slave labor, including claiming any children born into slavery. An organization called the [[Opposition]] decried the new treaty through acts of performance art and public protest. The Opposition believed that citizens of [[Pijal's moon]], and those enslaved by Czerka, would not be fairly represented by the new government.<ref name="Master & Apprentice" />

The [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] were [[Mission to Pijal|dispatched to the planet]] in order to oversee the young [[Fanry|princess Fanry's]] [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation and treaty ceremony]] on behalf of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]], but discovered the hidden complexities. A mysterious group referred to as the [[Blackguard]] was perpetrating violent terrorism and letting The Opposition take the blame. Qui-Gon advised the princess and her regent, Jedi [[Rael Averross|Rael Aveross]], not to sign the treaty, but the ceremony went ahead. Instead of signing the treaty, Fanry seized absolute power for herself in order to eject Czerka Corporation from the system. Her first act would have been to destroy Czerka's ship [[Leverage]], which was full of enslaved sentients, but the Jedi and their allies staved off the potential civil war. Fanry's [[Lamia|successor]] prohibited slavery in the Pijal system before signing the treaty forming a democratic Assembly. Czerka's influence on Pijal was formally ended when their contracts were cancelled by the new leader of Pijal's democratic Assembly, former minister [[Orth]].<ref name="Master & Apprentice" />

In the final decades of the Galactic Republic, the Czerka Corporation sponsored [[Podracing|podraces]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Czerka Arms had a sordid reputation, and was long suspected of laundering [[Currency|money]] into the Outer Rim by the [[Coruscant Security Force]].<ref name="Star Wars: Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>

===Imperial and New Republic Eras===
In [[4 ABY]], during the [[Iron Blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]] by the [[Galactic Empire]], the local branch of Czerka Arms found itself cut off from its corporate masters.<ref name="Uprising CR" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
In exchange for fortifying their [[Czerka facility|facility]], the Czerka employees of the Anoat sector eventually agreed to join the cause of [[the Smuggler]], a young scoundrel who led the [[Uprising|resistance]] against the Empire.<ref name="Uprising CR" />{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Czerka Arms was first created for ''[[The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook]]'', released by [[West End Games]] as a supplement for its [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. It was featured in the Legends video game ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]'' and its sequel, ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]'', appearing as an occasional antagonist faction in league with groups such as the [[Darth Revan's Sith Empire|Sith Empire]].
