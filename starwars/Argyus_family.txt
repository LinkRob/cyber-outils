The '''Argyus family''' was a [[human]] family. Members of the Argyus family served as [[senior officer]]s in the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Senate Guard]] for five generations before [[Faro Argyus]] would join the [[Law enforcement agency|security force]] and serve as a [[captain]] within the [[Senate Commando]]s.<ref name="HC 30">{{HelmetCollectionCite|30|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref>
