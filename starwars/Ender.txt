The '''Ender''' was a massive [[Species|creature]] that lived on [[Cianap]]. She destroyed the civilization of the [[Unidentified Cianap species|native species]], forcing them to live a primitive life underground. After [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] landed on the [[planet]] during a nearby [[Battle above Cianap|battle]], he awoke the Ender, and [[Death|killed]] her after a long fight.<ref name="Dark Visions 1" />
