'''Archaeotech''' was a term used by the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]] to describe ancient technology. According to the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]], archaeotech was almost always protected by traps. Examples of archaeotech were the [[techno-totem]] dating back to the [[Hundred-Year Darkness]],<ref name="Aphra 16">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 16|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 16]]</ref> along with the traps located in a [[Unidentified temple (X3-299-11)|temple]] on [[X3-299-11]].<ref name="Aphra Annual 2">[[Doctor Aphra Annual 2|''Doctor Aphra Annual'' 2]]</ref>
