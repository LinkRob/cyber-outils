The '''Commentary box''' of the [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]] was a small area of the grandstands<ref name="CL" /> that was used by commentators to comment on [[podracing]] events, like the [[Boonta Eve Classic]].<ref name="I">{{Film|I}}</ref> The Commentary box featured a data array.<ref name="CL" /> In [[32 BBY]] the two-headed [[Troig]], [[Fodesinbeed Annodue|Fode and Beed]] commented on the Boonta Eve Classic.<ref name="I" />
