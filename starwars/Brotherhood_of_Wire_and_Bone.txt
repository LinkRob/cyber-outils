The '''Brotherhood of Wire and Bone''' was a cult created by [[Fyzen Gor]] that consisted of his [[Original Dozen]] of [[medical droid]]s that were outfitted and enhanced with [[Species|organic]] [[limb]]s. Several organics joined the cult, including former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Admiral]] [[Seven-Seven Dirgeos|Ruas Fastent]]. They originated on Gor's home planet of [[Utapau]], but had moved to the [[Gravan Monastery|Imperial asylum]] on [[Grava]]<ref name="Last Shot" /> by [[7 ABY]].<ref name="Date" />
