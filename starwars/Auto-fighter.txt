'''Auto-fighters''' were [[Artificial intelligence|automated]] [[TIE series]] [[starfighter]]s that were produced by the [[Visler Korda's faction|forces]] under [[Commodore]] [[Visler Aqorian Korda III]] on [[Rekkana]]. Eventually, Rekkana was [[Liberation of Rekkana|liberated]] by [[New Republic]] [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], [[Rekk]] [[Chancellor]] [[Asera]], and the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki|Jaxxon]]. The resistance was able to take control of the remote fighters and have them fire on the Imperial formation, ruining Korda's presentation.<ref name="Hare-Brained Heist" />

==Behind the scenes==
The auto-fighters appear in "[[Hare-Brained Heist]]," published in [[IDW Publishing]]'s [[Star Wars Adventures Annual 2019|''Star Wars Adventures Annual'' 2019]].

In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the Auto-Fighter first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Empire]]'' comic book as the [[TIE/D automated starfighter]].<ref name="DE">[[Dark Empire 3|''Dark Empire'' 3]]</ref>
