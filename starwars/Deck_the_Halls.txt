"'''Deck the Halls'''" is a story in the [[canon]] one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Life Day 1|''Life Day'' 1]]. The story was written by [[Justina Ireland]], illustrated by [[Georges Jeanty]], [[Victor Olazaba]] and [[Pete Pantazis]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 24]], [[2021]].
