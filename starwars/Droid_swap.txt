A '''droid swap''' was a [[starfighter]] maneuver in which two [[pilots]] swap out their [[astromech droids]] from one ship to another. It was most notably utilized during the [[Mission to the Dassal system]], where [[Resistance]] pilots [[Poe Dameron]] and [[Kazuda Xiono]] swapped out the two [[BB-series astromech droid]]s [[BB-8]] and [[CB-23]] from the ''[[Fireball]]'' to [[Poe Dameron's blue X-wing|Dameron's X-wing]].<ref name="The Core Problem" />
