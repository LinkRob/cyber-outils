'''Disneyland Paris''', also known as '''Disneyland Resort Paris''' or simply '''Disneyland Park''', is a real-world theme park located at the larger [[Wikipedia:Disneyland Paris|Disneyland Paris resort]] in [[Wikipedia:Chessy, Seine-et-Marne|Chessy, France]]. Originally opened under the name '''Euro Disney''' or '''Euro Disneyland Park''' on [[April 12]]th, [[1992]],<ref name="DLP A to Z">{{D23|url=a-to-z/disneyland-paris|text=Disney A to Z: Disneyland Paris}}</ref> it has featured various ''[[Star Wars]]''-themed attractions and events over the years, starting with [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] fourth [[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] attraction that was opened along with the rest of the park.<ref name="SWI 104">{{InsiderCite|104|Star Tours: Taking Off on a Star Tour}}</ref> A second theme park, [[Walt Disney Studios Park]], opened in 2002.<ref name="DLP A to Z" />

[[Lucasfilm]]'s ''[[Indiana Jones (franchise)|Indiana Jones]]'' franchise is also represented at the park by the ''[[Wikipedia:Indiana Jones and the Temple of Peril|Indiana Jones and the Temple of Peril]]'' roller coaster.<ref name="DHS official site">{{WebCite|url=https://www.disneylandparis.com/en-usd/destinations/disneyland-park/|text=Disneyland Park|work=DisneylandParis.com|archivedate=20230206155043}}</ref>
