'''Dru O'Higgins''' was a model used by [[Wikipedia:Illustrator|illustrator]] [[Mike Jackson]] in his drawing of [[Yarr]],<ref name="Yarr">{{DeviantArt|username=steampoweredmikej|url=steampoweredmikej/art/Star-Wars-Yarr-363592117|name=[[Mike Jackson]]|text=Star Wars &ndash; Yarr}}</ref> a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|male]]. The image appeared in the [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' (RPG) supplementary book [[Wanted by Cracken (sourcebook)|''Wanted by Cracken'']].<ref name="Wanted">[[Wanted by Cracken (sourcebook)|''Wanted by Cracken'']]</ref> published on [[June 1]], [[1993]].<ref name="BN">{{BN|url=w/star-wars-the-roleplaying-game-west-end-games-incorporated/1018175930|text=Star Wars the Roleplaying Game: Wanted by Cracken}}</ref>
