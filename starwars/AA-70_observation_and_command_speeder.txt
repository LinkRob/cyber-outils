The '''AA-70 observation and command speeder''' was a [[landspeeder]] utilized by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. The AA-70 was a modified version of the [[A-A5 Speeder Truck]] and was used as a mobile command post that could hold a crew of up to four with ten passengers.<ref name="Lead by Example" />
