The '''Final Order''', also known as the '''Final Order fleet''', the '''Sith Eternal fleet''', the '''Sith fleet''', the '''Sith armada''', the '''New Order''', the '''New Empire''', or the '''Sith Empire''', was a massive [[fleet]] of [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] secretly constructed by [[Sith cultist]]s led by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]]. Alongside an [[Sith Eternal army|army]] of countless [[Sith trooper]]s, the Final Order was the culmination of an [[Grand Plan|agenda]] over a [[Millennium|thousand years]] and a [[Contingency|conspiracy]] [[Standard decade|decades]] in the making to build a new [[Galactic Empire]]. With each [[Star Destroyer]] armed with an [[axial superlaser]] capable of destroying entire worlds, the Final Order's objective was to bring [[the galaxy]] under the perpetual reign of a new [[Sith Empire]] through [[Sith Eternal military|military]] force.

As the [[Resistance]] [[First Order-Resistance War|fought]] against the [[First Order]], adherents to the [[Sith]] [[religion]], the [[Sith Eternal]], labored to create the largest armada in [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]]. The Final Order's existence was revealed to [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] by the [[Cloning|reborn]] [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Sidious, who urged [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[Skywalker family|heir]] to take the fleet and rule all the [[galaxy]] as the new [[Galactic Emperor]].

Although Ren renounced the [[dark side of the Force]], Sidious' plans continued apace with the support of [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]]. The ''[[Derriphan]]'', one of the many Star Destroyers comprising the Final Order, demonstrated its power to the galaxy by [[Destruction of Kijimi|destroying]] the planet [[Kijimi]]. In order to prevent Sidious from realizing his goal of establishing a new Sith Empire, the [[Resistance navy]] deployed to the [[Unknown Regions]] in a last-ditch effort to prevent the launching of the Final Order.

Ultimately, the [[Battle of Exegol]] was a decisive defeat for Sidious and his followers, with the Final Order utterly destroyed by the Resistance and a [[Citizens' Fleet]]. As a result, the [[First Order military]] was openly [[Uprising against the First Order|attacked]] by an uprising of people on numerous worlds across the galaxy, from [[Bespin]], [[Jakku]], and [[Endor]], to [[Naboo]], [[Corellia]], and [[Coruscant]].

==Description==
===Composition===
{{Quote|He's been planning his revenge. His followers have been building something for years. The largest fleet the galaxy has ever known. He calls it the Final Order. In sixteen hours, attacks on all free worlds begin. The Emperor and his fleet have been hiding in the Unknown Regions on a world called Exegol.|Poe Dameron|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:Final-Order-Fleet.png|thumb|left|250px|The Final Order was set to be the largest fleet in the galaxy, composed of Sith Star Destroyers.]]
The Final Order was the extensive world-annihilating [[fleet]] of the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Sith cult|cult]] led by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="Episode IX" /> It was composed of 1,080 [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]],<ref name="VFX-BBC">{{YouTube|video=bDa5h3cj1DU|text=How We brought Carrie Fisher Back|channel=@BBCClick|name=BBC Click}}</ref> with the overall design of [[warship]]s being based upon the that of the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyers]] utilized by the [[Imperial Navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> The Final Order also possessed [[AAL-2100/9.5 atmospheric assault lander]]s and [[TIE/dg starfighter]]s.<ref name="Episode IX" /> However, ''Xyston''-class [[Star Destroyer]]s were larger, had [[Color|red]] accents along the [[hull]] of their [[Starship|vessels]], and were equipped with a large [[axial superlaser]] capable of destroying [[planet]]s, which took the place of a standard ''Imperial''-class Star [[Destroyer]]'s main [[hangar]] space. These Star Destroyers housed TIE/dg starfighters, with triangular wings and red accents, which were capable of ship-to-ship combat, and [[Sith trooper]]s, including their variant [[Sith Jetpack Trooper]]s, which were deployed via AAL-2100/9.5 atmospheric assault landers and divided into traditional [[Sith]] [[legion]]s, each of which being named after [[Death|deceased]] Sith [[Lord]]s, and were comprised of five-thousand [[soldier]]s.<ref name="TROS VD" />

===Organization===
{{Quote|Forgive me, sir, but these allies on Exegol&hellip; they sound like a cult. Conjurers and soothsayers.''"<br />"''They've conjured legions of Star Destroyers. The Sith fleet will increase our resources ten-thousandfold.|Domaric Quinn and Enric Pryde|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
Consisting of various ranks including [[Petty Officer]]s, [[lieutenant]]s, and [[captain]]s, the Final Order was staffed by [[Fleet technician]]s and [[Sith Eternal officer]]s.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==History==
===Emerging from the shadows===
{{Quote|What could you give me?''"<br />"''Everything. A new Empire. The might of the Final Order will soon be ready. It will be yours&hellip; if you do as I ask. Kill the girl! End the Jedi&mdash; and become what your grandfather Vader could not. You will rule all the galaxy&hellip; as the new Emperor.|Kylo Ren and Darth Sidious|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:SidiousandKyloRen-TROS.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Final Order was approaching mass deployment after the ascension of Kylo Ren as Supreme Leader of the First Order.]]
The Final Order was created by [[Sith cultist]]s as the last part of the [[Contingency]] of [[Darth]] Sidious&mdash;[[Dark Lord of the Sith]] and [[Galactic Emperor]]&mdash;who aimed to establish eternal Sith rule across the [[The galaxy|galaxy]].<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> With the [[First Order]] rising to power under [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[Skywalker family|grandson]], [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], the [[Emperor]] [[Palpatine's broadcast|announced]] his threat of revenge throughout the galaxy, prompting Ren to seek out Sidious and kill him<ref name="Episode IX" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="TROS Date" /> Using a [[Sith wayfinder]] that once [[Vader's wayfinder|belonged]] to his grandfather, Ren discovered the legendary world of [[Exegol]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> where Sidious and the Sith Eternal were located. He discovered many secrets within the [[Sith Citadel]], such as the origins of his predecessor, Supreme Leader [[Snoke]], as well as Sidious' rebirth in a [[Cloning|cloned body]] and the [[Palpatine family|secret heritage]] of the [[Padawan|Jedi apprentice]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]].<ref name="TROS EE" /> In addition to these revelations, Ren learned that he was the heir to the new Sith Empire envisioned by the cultists and their master.<ref name="TROS VD" />

Snoke had fulfilled his purpose by dying at his apprentice's hand, the Sith Eternal having created the former Supreme Leader to ensure that Ren became a worthy successor to the Sith legacy.<ref name="TROS VD" /> With Ren's arrival on Exegol, Sidious unveiled the Final Order as a vast armada that would conquer the galaxy under the command of Ren as the new Emperor,<ref name="Episode IX" /> with an army of countless Sith troopers also serving at Sidious's command.<ref name="POV 226">{{InsiderCite|226|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> The Sith Lord had a condition, however. Ren had to put an end to the Jedi Order by killing Rey; only then would Sidious release the Final Order to the Supreme Leader's authority.<ref name="Episode IX" />

===Launch of the Final Order===
{{Quote|The Resistance is dead. The Sith flame will burn. All worlds, surrender or die. The Final Order begins.|[[Beaumont Kin]] translate the Darth Sidious' [[ur-Kittât]]|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:SidiousOrdersPryde-TROS.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Darth Sidious commanded Allegiant General Enric Pryde to oversee the Final Order's deployment from Exegol.]]
Despite Sidious' plans for Ren, the Skywalker heir [[Mission to Kef Bir|abandoned]] the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] and the First Order, returning to the [[Light side of the Force|light side]] as Ben Solo. [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]], from his [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']], pledged his loyalty to the Emperor he once served. With the support of Pryde, Sidious determined that the time had come to launch the Final Order from Exegol.<ref name="Episode IX" /> Although the First Order and the Sith Eternal were both offshoots of the Emperor's Contingency, pragmatic [[First Order officer]]s found it difficult to work alongside the fanatic Sith Eternal cultists but had no other option to avoid feeling Darth Sidious' wrath.<ref name="TROS VD" />

Acting on the Emperor's orders, [[General]] Pryde deployed a ''Xyston''-class Star Destroyer, the ''[[Derriphan]]'', from Exegol to destroy a planet close to the Resistance with its axial [[superlaser]] as a show of force. The target that they chose was [[Kijimi]], and the [[capital ship]]'s superlaser sent down a red laser beam to strike the surface, using red bolts of energy to hit the planet. Massive shockwaves erupted across Kijimi, and eventually, the laser beam broke through the core of the world and ripped it apart. The [[destruction of Kijimi]] was broadcast across the galaxy by the Sith Eternal, who proclaimed that the [[Resistance]] was finished and the Sith flame will burn and demanded for every world to surrender.<ref name="Episode IX" />

===Downfall===
{{Quote|But it was not just the Jedi that brought Palpatine to defeat. Not really. It was us. It was the great mass of people who had been forced to repeatedly stare into the depths of Palpatine's evil intentions and then chose to reject him. ''[&hellip;]'' Because regardless of what the Centrists, or the Imperial remnant, or the First Order had believed&mdash;the galaxy did not long for a return to Empire. While there have been so many mistakes and errors made over the last three decades in how we have talked about the years of Imperial rule, enough of the message had made it through. Enough people knew what the Empire stood for. What it meant. What it promised. And they came out to fight against it.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise and Fall" />}}
[[File:Episode_IX_SD_fleet.png|thumb|250px|The Battle of Exegol marked the Resistance's first and last stand against the forces of the Sith Eternal, including the Final Order.|alt=]]
The fleet, its [[warship]]s too numerous for a simultaneous exit from Exegol without a guiding [[navigational beacon]], relied on a navigation signal to operate their initial launch from the Sith world. A [[navigation tower]] on the planet had been constructed to fulfill that purpose, although other ships were able to fulfill the same purpose in emergencies.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Despite the [[Standard decade|decades]] of effort that had been placed into the construction of this titanic force,<ref name="TROS VD" /> the Final Order's purpose of retribution was never fulfilled. It was confronted by a small force of the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]], led by Generals [[Poe Dameron]] and [[Finn]]. Although this early token force was nearly annihilated, a "[[Citizens' Fleet]]" consisting of thousands of armed civilian vessels led by General [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], emerged from hyperspace above the destroyers. Utilizing a weakness in the design of the ''Xyston''-class wherein their planet-killer cannons served as a weak point, the bolstered Resistance fleet quickly routed the Sith fleet. The [[flagship]] of the armada, the ''Steadfast'', was destroyed as well, preventing any of the destroyers from fleeing the engagement.<ref name="Episode IX" />

As a result, the Final Order was decisively defeated at the [[Battle of Exegol]], its warships reduced to debris on Exegol's surface. With the Sith fleet destroyed, the First Order faced [[Uprising against the First Order|widespread revolts]] across the galaxy.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Final Order first appeared in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], the third and final installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

While the Final Order is part of [[Canon|Star Wars Canon]], an organization known as the [[Dark Empire]] from [[Tom Veitch]]'s [[Star Wars: Dark Empire]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series in [[Star Wars Legends]] bears similarities to the organization. As with the Final Order, the Dark Empire was led by a resurrected Emperor [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] and sought to dominate the galaxy like its predecessor, the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]], and reestablish [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] rule. In addition, there was a group of dark side warriors within its ranks ([[Knights of Ren]]/[[Dark Side Elite]]), both have an alternate name with "Empire" in it (Sith Empire and New Empire for the Final Order, and the Empire Reborn for the Dark Empire), both possessed [[Eclipse-class dreadnought|starships equipped with superlasers]] and a [[Dark Empire|fleet]] tasked with retaking control of the galaxy, from a secret Sith planet ([[Exegol]]/[[Byss/Legends|Byss]]), and both attempted to bring a Jedi over to the dark side ([[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]/[[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]]).
