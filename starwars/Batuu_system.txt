The '''Batuu system''' was a [[star system]] with three [[star]]s that was located in the [[Trilon sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] on the edge of the [[Unknown Regions]] at [[galactic coordinates]] -401.72, -561.84, 004.32. It contained the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] and its two [[moon]]s.<ref name="GEStaff" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Batuu system was created for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at Disneyland Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="GEStaff" /> However, prior to the opening of the park it first appeared in various tie-in material, starting with the non-[[canon]] ''[[Star Tours: The Adventures Continue]]'' in [[2017]].<ref name="ST:AC">''[[Star Tours: The Adventures Continue]]''</ref> The system's first canon appearance was in the [[2018]] novel ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]'', which was written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="T:A">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> None of the sources released prior to the opening of the park identified the Batuu system, but staff at ''Galaxy's Edge'' were informed of the system's name to share with guests.<ref name="GEStaff" />
