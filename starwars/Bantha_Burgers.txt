'''Bantha Burgers''' was an eating establishment that served a variety of [[Foodstuff|foods]].<ref name="Meal" /> The establishment offered a [[bantha burger]] served on a [[blue milk]] bun, which was locally sourced<ref name="Delicious">{{SWCT|set=Galactic Feast|cardname=Try a Delicious Bantha Burger|nolink=1}}</ref> from free range [[Bantha]]s.<ref name="FreeRange">{{SWCT|set=Galactic Feast|cardname=Enjoy Bantha Burgers|nolink=1}}</ref> They sold [[Color|blue]] [[milk]] for two [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Milk">{{SWCT|set=Galactic Feast|cardname=Enjoy Blue Milk|nolink=1}}</ref> They also served [[Bantha Fries]] and blue [[ice cream]]. Bantha Burgers offered a deal, the [[Bantha Meal]], which came with a [[burger]], fries, milk, ice cream, and a [[droid]] toy, for five credits.<ref name="Meal" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bantha Burgers featured in "Galactic Feast," a set from [[Star Wars: Card Trader]], being mentioned on nine cards.<ref name="Meal" />
