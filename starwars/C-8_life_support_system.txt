The '''C-8 life support system''', '''C-8 life-support system''', or simply the '''C-8 unit''', was life support system manufactured by [[Corellstand]] that was located in the [[Number Two Hold]] of [[YT-1300 light freighter]]s.<ref name="BMF23">{{BuildFalconCite|23|Secrets of Spaceflight}}</ref> It could be easily restructured for any pressurized gasses, or deactivated to conserve energy for [[droid]]-only crews. The ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was equipped with one such system, which was standard issue for YT-1300 [[light freighter]]s.<ref name="MF 3D" />
