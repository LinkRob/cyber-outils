The '''Biitu system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Tadrin sector]] within the [[Cassander sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> It was located in grid square K-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Biitu system was mentioned as just "Biitu" on a map of [[the galaxy]]<ref name="CLNE">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref> designed by [[Christopher Ibbitt]]<ref name="Fry email">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-31.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-31}}</ref> and written by [[Jason Fry]]<ref name="TTS">{{WebCite|url=https://tarkinstopshelf.libsyn.com/381-talking-star-wars-complete-locations-with-emily-shkoukani-and-jason-fry|text=381: Talking Star Wars: Complete Locations with Emily Shkoukani and Jason Fry!|date=2025-05-09|work=[https://tarkinstopshelf.libsyn.com Tarkin's Top Shelf]|archivedate=20250511074859}}</ref> in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]'',<ref name="CLNE" /> which was released on [[March 6]], [[2025]].<ref name="Amazon UK">{{DK|url=uk/book/9780241731673-star-wars-complete-locations-new-edition|text=Star Wars Complete Locations New Edition}}</ref> It was then identified as the Biitu system in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were also written by Fry.<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

The [[Biitu system/Legends|system]] orginated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in "[[The Great Heep]]," a television movie released as part of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO]]''<ref name="GreatHeep">{{DroidsCite|The Great Heep}}</ref> on [[June 7]], [[1986]].<ref name="YearByYear">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> It was then identified for the first time in ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', a [[2009]] reference book by Fry and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Atlas">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
