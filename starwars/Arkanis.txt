'''Arkanis''' was a [[Water|rainy]]<ref name="Secret Academy" /> [[planet]] in the [[Arkanis sector]]<ref name="DoR" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Timelines" /> and the [[throneworld]] of [[Leeya]], the Empress of the [[Regency Worlds]].<ref name="DoR" /> The [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Arkanis Academy|officers academy]] was located there.<ref name="Vision of Hope" /> It was also the [[homeworld]] of [[Armitage Hux]], one of the key leading figures in the [[First Order]].<ref name="CEUE">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref>

The planet had at least one ocean and large cliffs on the coastline. The aquatic regions on Arkanis supported the planet's [[fishing]] industry. The [[Scaparus Port]] was a hub for fisherfolk and had shops with supplies that were catered for their business. Downpours happened on Arkanis at least 2-3 [[Standard day|days]] a [[Standard week|week]], with the standard otherwise being a near-continuous drizzle. The appearance of [[Star|sunshine]] was considered a rare event.<ref name="Secret Academy" />

During the Imperial Era, the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Vensenor]]'' was operating in the planet's orbit as the [[Vensenor Flight Academy]], which produced [[Imperial Navy]] [[Imperial officer|officers]] and [[pilot]]s, including a number of decorated soldiers.<ref name="DoR" /> Arkanis was also host to [[Project Harvester]], an Imperial project regarding [[Force-sensitive]]s. [[Zare Leonis]]'s [[Gender|sister]], [[Dhara Leonis|Dhara]], was sent here after she was identified as one by [[the Grand Inquisitor]].<ref name="ranks">''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]''</ref>

In [[3 BBY]], there was a [[speeder]] auction on Arkanis that [[Apprentice Legislator]] [[Chassellon Stevis]] attempted to hire the ship ''[[Mighty Oak Apocalypse]]'' to take him to off [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Leia Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>

Following the [[The galaxy|Galactic]] Empire's losses at the [[Battle of Endor]], Arkanis remained under the Empire's control for a [[time]], but the [[Arkanis system|system]] was [[Siege of Arkanis|targeted]] by the [[New Republic]], freed from the Empire's influence and claimed for the New Republic's side.<ref name="lifedebt" /> Arkanis eventually became a wealthy planet of the New Republic, aligned with the [[Centrists]], and represented in the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] by [[Senator]] [[Carise Sindian]].<ref name="Bloodline" /> When the First Order emerged from the [[Unknown Regions]] in [[29 ABY]],<ref name="BSI note">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that the [[First Order]] seceded from the [[New Republic]] five [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the First Order secession must have happened in [[29 ABY]].</ref> however, Arkanis seceded from the Republic to join the military junta [[First Order secession|alongside]] many other star systems previously aligned with the Centrists.<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> During the [[Cold War|cold war]] that then ensued between the First Order and the New Republic, the [[Resistance]] operative [[Vi Moradi]] considered Arkanis to be a planet that held multiple secrets related to the military junta.<ref name="Phasma">[[Phasma (novel)|''Phasma'']]</ref>
