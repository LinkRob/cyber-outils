'''CSA pilots''' were the [[pilot]]s that flew for the [[Corporate Sector Authority]]. Any CSA pilot that broke [[flight regulations]] with an unauthorized pass in a [[starship]] would eventually find themselves connected to a chain gang in a [[Mining|spice mine]].<ref name="SotS">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
CSA pilots were mentioned in the [[2022]] novel ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' and its [[Shadow of the Sith (audiobook)|audiobook]], both authored by [[Adam Christopher]].<ref name="SotS" />
