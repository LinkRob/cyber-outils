A '''Devaronian victory necklace''' was a [[Color|golden]] [[necklace]] from the [[Devaronian]] culture.<ref name="MIS" /> A very rare artifact, it bore a customary marque.<ref name="DB" /> In the early [[Imperial Era]], [[Luthen Rael]] met with a vendor called [[Grenwig]] in a [[Unidentified hill town|hill town]] to sell one necklace, while he was accompanied by [[Kleya Marki]]. During their bargain, they were interrupted by seeing [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Army|troopers]] pushing a group of prisoners to an execution, leading Rael to quickly sell the necklace to Grenwig for 80 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]]s.<ref name="MIS" />
