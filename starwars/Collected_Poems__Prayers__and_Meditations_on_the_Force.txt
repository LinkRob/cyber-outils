The '''''Collected Poems, Prayers, and Meditations on the Force''''' was a set of writings collected and edited by [[Kozem Pel]], a [[Disciples of the Whills|Disciple of the Whills]]. The collection contained writings by a number of spiritual figures, along with the [[Guardian's Mantra]], the [[Jedi Code]], the [[Code of the Sith]], and the [[Coxixian Prayer for the Departed]].<ref name="Guardians of the Whills">[[Guardians of the Whills (novel)|''Guardians of the Whills'']]</ref>
