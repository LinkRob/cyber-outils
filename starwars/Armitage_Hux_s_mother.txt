A [[Gender|female]] individual was the '''mother''' of [[Armitage Hux]]. She was a kitchen worker who had an affair with [[Commandant]] [[Brendol Hux]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], which resulted in the [[Reproduction|birth]] of Armitage.<ref name="Life Debt" /> As a child, her son Armitage never got the chance to get to know her.<ref name="TROS Junior Novel">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Life Debt Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] was requested by [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]] to rescue the young boy and his [[Parent|father]] from [[Arkanis]] which was under [[Siege of Arkanis|siege]] by the [[New Republic]], Sloane was told not to worry about Hux's mother or Brendol's [[Marriage|wife]] [[Maratelle]].<ref name="Life Debt" /> Armitage went on to be one of the [[First Order High Command|leaders]] of the [[First Order]] in the [[Standard decade|decades]] after the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[34 ABY]]. As [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] carries on immediately from the events of ''The Force Awakens'', it can be assumed to take place in the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> in an attempt to stall for [[time]] and allow the [[Resistance]] to [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuate]] the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar]], [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] informed Armitage that the Resistance's leader, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], had an urgent message for him about his mother, which resulted in Hux being enraged enough that he ordered his ship to open fire on Dameron.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>
