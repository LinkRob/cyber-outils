'''Breehld''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who was the personal [[pilot]] of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Moff]] [[Delian Mors]], who controlled the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]]. During an [[Mission to Ryloth|attempt]] by the rebellious [[Free Ryloth Movement]] to [[assassin]]ate [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], he attempted to pilot Mors in a [[shuttle]] from her [[Mors' command center|command center]] on [[Ryloth's largest moon]] to the planet's surface. Free Ryloth movement members in a stolen Imperial [[escort boat]] attacked the shuttle during the journey, and caused it to crash on Ryloth, [[Death|killing]] Breehld, but not Mors.<ref name="LOTS" />
