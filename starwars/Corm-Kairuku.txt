'''Corm-Kairuku''' was a [[net-maker]] for the [[Visitors]], a group consisting of all of the [[Gender|male]] [[Lanai]]s from the population inhabiting [[Temple Island]] on the [[planet]] [[Ahch-To]]. He and the other Visitors were responsible for providing the [[Foodstuff|food]] for the population, and so traveled on a fleet of hand-crafted [[Boat|vessels]] to other islands while [[hunting]] and [[fish]]ing. After the fleet had gathered supplies, they would return to temple island once per [[Standard month|month]], delivering food to the [[Caretakers|female Lanais]] and then celebrating with them in a multi-[[Standard day|day]] festival. Corm-Kairuku wore a [[Color|blue]] [[byssus-wool]] [[Clothing|smock]] over a brown robe, with a [[hat]] featuring a dried [[crinoid]] [[frondtrailer]]. His [[eye]]s were blue and his skin was a mixture of gray and white.<ref name="TLJVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corm-Kairuku was created to be a part of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']];<ref name="TLJVD" /> however, the scene he appeared in was ultimately cut before the final release.<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> Despite this, the character still appeared in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> and released alongside the film on [[December 15]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/new-star-wars-the-last-jedi-books-and-more-revealed-at-sdcc-2017|text=New ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Books and More Revealed at SDCC 2017}}</ref>
