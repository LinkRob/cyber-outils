'''Dua Ningo''' was an elderly [[Sullustan]] that operated as an [[admiral]] for the [[Commerce Guild]]'s [[Sullustan Home Guard]], in which he was tasked with overseeing defense of the [[Brema sector|Sullust sector]] and interests that belonged to [[SoroSuub Corporation]]. Later on, after the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]], Ningo and the Commerce Guild joined the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] to fight against the [[Galactic Republic]], continuing to serve his previous role as an admiral for the Separatist [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|fleet]]. As the war continued to rage, Ningo was charged with supervising construction of a Separatist [[Bulwark flotilla|flotilla]] of [[Bulwark-class battle cruiser|''Bulwark''-class battle cruisers]], hoping that it would be completed [[20 BBY|two years]] after the war began. He had also hoped that the [[cruiser]]s could be used in an attack on the Republic [[capital]], the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dua Ningo was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Dua Ningo/Legends|Dua Ningo]] was introduced in ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'', a [[2005]] reference book authored by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Kevin J. Anderson]].<ref name="TNEC">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''</ref>
