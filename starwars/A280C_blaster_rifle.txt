The '''A280C blaster rifle''',<ref name="Battlefront" /> also designated as the '''A280 C heavy blaster rifle''',<ref name="Forged in Battle" /> was a modified variant of the popular [[A280 blaster rifle]] and was the preferred weapon of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel Alliance Special Forces|Special Forces]]. It featured a high rate of fire and was known for its excellent firepower and sturdy design.<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Description==
[[File:CarsonTeva-CGSWG.png|thumb|left|200px|[[Carson Teva]] armed with an A280C blaster rifle]]
The A280C blaster file,<ref name="Battlefront" /> also designated as the A280 C heavy blaster rifle<ref name="Forged in Battle">''[[Forged in Battle]]''</ref> served as one of [[BlasTech Industries|BlasTech's]] top-selling combat [[Blaster rifle|blaster]] [[rifle]]s for nearly a [[Standard century|century]] before it was used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Before the [[Clone Wars]], the A280C was the standard-issue [[infantry]] rifle for many [[planetary defense forces]] and private [[militia]]s. Despite its heavy weight, the A280C was accurate and durable and it continued to see service throughout the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Almost every military [[academy]], militia, and military trained with the A280C prior to the Clone Wars.<ref name="GaG" />

When more modular platforms were adopted by the [[Galactic Republic]], the A280C waned in use. With the reformation of the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]], the Empire absorbed or disbanded local defense forces, resulting in many decommissioned A280Cs. These guns flooded the secondhand market and [[black market]]s, and found their way to the Alliance to Restore the Republic, and became standard issue.<ref name="GaG" /> The [[bounty hunter]]s and [[commando]]s that worked for the [[Haxion Brood]] were also equipped with them.<ref name="JFO" />
