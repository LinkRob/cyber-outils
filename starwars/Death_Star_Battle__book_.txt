'''''Death Star Battle''''' is a young readers book by [[Trey King]] with illustrations by [[Pilot Studio]] that adapts a portion of the film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] in which [[Luke Skywalker]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] destroy the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. A level 2 [[World of Reading]] story, the title was published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[February 2]], [[2016]].<ref name="Disney" />

==Publisher's summary==
''Level 2 early reader retelling scenes from the classic ''Star Wars'' trilogy with all-new illustrations.''

''It's a race against time as the rebels fight to destroy the Empire's greatest weapon--the Death Star. This level 2 early reader retells classic scenes from ''Star Wars: A New Hope'' as the Luke Skywalker flies his X-Wing in a battle with Darth Vader. Featuring beautiful full-color illustrations, this World of Reading book is perfect for little ''Star Wars'' fans who are ready to read on their own.''<ref name="Disney" />
