'''Brain slugs'''<ref name="beware" /> or '''brain-slugs''',<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> were a [[species]] of [[Slug (creature)|slugs]] that [[Homeworld|hailed]] from [[Nusa Sept V]]. They had [[mouth]]s and [[tongue]]s, but lacked [[eye]]s.<ref name="beware" /> The commonly used phrase "like stealing slime from a brain-slug" meant that something was very easy.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The brain slugs were mentioned in the [[2015]] young readers novel ''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''&mdash;a retelling of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]&mdash;written by [[Tom Angleberger]].<ref name="beware" />
