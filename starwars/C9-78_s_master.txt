In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], including C9-78's appearance and mention of its master, to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the '''master''' of the [[C-series]] [[astromech droid]] [[C9-78]] allowed the [[droid]] a [[Standard day|day]] to leave [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]], where the droid had dwelt for some [[time]] without ever leaving the outpost.<ref name="GEStaff" /> Hoping to find a companion to travel with, C9-78 called in to the radio show [[Droid Station Frequency 3-7-6-4-3-7]] on station [[BSO 401.72]]<ref name="GEBSO">{{GEAttr|BSO 401.72}}</ref> and mentioned its master<ref name="GEStaff" /> while speaking to the show's host, [[B6-24]].<ref name="GEBSO" />

==Behind the scenes==
C9-78's master was mentioned in the background radio loop of the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="GEBSO" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> He is only mentioned by JB-7 in binary,<ref name="GEBSO" /> with a translation of what it's saying available from staff.<ref name="GEStaff" />
