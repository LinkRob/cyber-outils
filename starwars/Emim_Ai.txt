'''Emim'Ai''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Mirialan]] [[bounty hunter]] who relentlessly pursued a scoundrel named [[Misook]] in order to make a name for herself among her superiors.<ref name="Myths & Fables" /> During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] lied to another bounty hunter, [[Kief Varris]] that Emim'Ai attempted to capture [[Moebin Faltus]] at one point.<ref name="Repair">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=14|story=Repair Stop, Part II}}</ref>
