The '''Bodoria system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Andirma sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]], situated in grid square L-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[Jason Fry]], author of the [[2025]] "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix that introduced the Bodoria system, stated that the system was added as a homeworld for one of the new [[species]] introduced in the [[2023]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-09.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-09}}</ref>
