'''Bogey Four''' was the name given to a light cruiser-sized warship of the [[Grysk Hegemony]] by the crew of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]''. Along with [[Bogey Three]], Bogey Four joined a [[Battle against Grysk forces|battle]] against the ''Chimaera''.<ref name="Alliances" />
