'''Beysh Custom Craft''' was a custom [[starship]] manufacturing company active during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]]. It produced the custom [[light freighter]] ''[[Booster BX]]''.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
Beysh Custom Craft was first mentioned in the [[2018]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] roleplaying sourcebook ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''.<ref name="Dawn" />
