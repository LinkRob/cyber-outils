{{Quote|And you bucketheads are gonna to be sorry when my uncle, the Emperor, finds out you're keeping me here against my will!|Ezra Bridger, attempting to trick two stormtroopers|<ref name="Spark">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref>}}
"'''Buckethead'''" (also spelled "'''bucket-head'''" or "'''bucket head'''") was a derogatory nickname used to refer to [[stormtrooper]]s during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], or others wearing similar [[armor]]. It referred to the [[Stormtrooper armor|helmets]] of the stormtroopers, shaped like buckets,<ref name="Spark" /> which even among members of the Empire were sometimes referred to colloquially as "buckets."<ref name="ranks">''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]''</ref> [[Ezra Bridger]] applied the term to both stormtroopers and [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]],<ref name="Spark" /> and [[Han Solo]] even applied it to [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilots]].<ref name="Star Wars 6">[[Star Wars (2015) 6|''Star Wars'' (2015) 6]]</ref> Well over thirty [[Standard year|years]] later, the term was still in use and used by [[Poe Dameron]] in reference to [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] of the [[First Order]].<ref name="awakensnovel">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> [[Kazuda Xiono]] also used the term during the [[Battle of Castilon]].<ref name="No Escape 2">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 2}}</ref>

The term was used by one [[B1-series battle droid]] to insult another B1 during the [[Battle of Rugosa|Ambush on Rugosa]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref>

"Bucket-head" was also used to refer to [[BB-series astromech droid]]s that possessed dome-shaped heads with a flat top, such as the [[droid]] [[CB-23]],<ref name="Extraordinary Droids">''[[Star Wars: Extraordinary Droids]]''</ref> who was a member of the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Sector Six">{{Resistance|Signal from Sector Six}}</ref>
