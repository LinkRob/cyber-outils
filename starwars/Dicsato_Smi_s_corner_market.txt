During the [[Clone Wars]] and subsequent [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Dicsato Smi]] owned a '''corner market''' located in the [[Tann Province]] on [[Ryloth]].

==History==
[[Dicsato Smi]] operated a corner market in the [[Tann Province]] on [[Ryloth]], which required a stream of steady customers, a high turnover for his goods, and standard suppliers to keep the food fresh on his shelves. During the [[Clone Wars]], Smi struggled to keep his corner market stocked and customers satisfied, and during the conflict, he had a smaller variety of products and suppliers, yet was able to continue to operate his market. To continue to run the market, Smi was forced to collaborate with less legitimate suppliers, though they were reliable.<ref name="Dawn" />

After the Clone Wars, the [[Galactic Empire]] established an Imperial [[Protectorate (government system)|Protectorate]] on Ryloth, and the [[planet]] was subjected to extreme rationing. The promised wealth and security that Smi hoped would come never did, taxes had significantly increased, and Imperial [[troop]]s often freely took products without payment. The increasing debt forced Smi to take part in [[black market]] operations to keep the corner market open.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dicsato Smi's corner market was first mentioned in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[canon]] roleplaying [[2018]] sourcebook that is based on ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' and ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. The book was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]]. It was authored by [[John Dunn]], [[Tom Sorenson]], [[Sterling Hershey]], [[Jonathan Julius]], [[Keith Kappel|Keith Ryan Kappel]], [[Tim Huckelberry]], [[Jason Marker]], and [[James M. Spahn]].<ref name="Dawn" />
