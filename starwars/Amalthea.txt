The '''''Amalthea''''' was an [[MC80 Star Cruiser]] of the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Defense Fleet|Navy]] that was active during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Its most notable participation was during the [[Defense of Kuat|battle]] for the [[Kuat Drive Yards]] where it acted as the [[carrier]] for [[Blade Squadron]].<ref name="Blade Squadron: Kuat" />

During the lead up to the [[Battle of Endor]], the warship was included in a list of vessels confirmed for service, pending the ship's [[hyperdrive]] repair.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
