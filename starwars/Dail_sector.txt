The '''Dail sector''' was a [[sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained the [[Tollello system]], which had a [[Unidentified gas giant (Tollello system)|gas giant]] [[orbit]]ed by the floating [[city]] [[Penumbra (city)|Penumbra.]]<ref name="Missing Pieces" /> By around [[377 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[Tales of Enlightenment: Missing Pieces]]" takes place around five [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Jedha]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> a [[Unidentified pirate group (Rooper Nitani)|pirate group]] intended to illegally purchase a large stash of [[Smuggler|smuggled]] [[weapon]]s from [[Ullan Yite]], the leader of the [[Bright Circle]] criminal organization, in Penumbra. The [[pirate]]s intended to use the weapons in a series of raids on other settlements in the [[sector]]. However, Yite was [[Mission to Penumbra|arrested]] by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Rooper Nitani]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Silandra Sho]], and the [[church droid]] [[P3-7A]].<ref name="Missing Pieces" />
