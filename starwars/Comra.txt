'''Comra''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Bloodline" /> located in the [[Prefsbelt sector]]'s [[Comra system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was [[Senator|represented]] in the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Senate|Senate]] by the [[Centrists|Centrist]] [[Mortan]]<ref name="Bloodline" /> in [[28 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']] to [[28 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Comra was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] novel [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']], written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="Bloodline" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Comra/Legends|Comra]] first appeared in the [[2004]] novel ''[[Survivor's Quest]]'', written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Survivor's Quest">''[[Survivor's Quest]]''</ref>
