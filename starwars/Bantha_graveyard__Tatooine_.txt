The '''bantha graveyard''' was located on the [[desert]] [[planet]] of [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a group of [[Tusken Raider]]s visited the site while it was being used as a [[Scavenger|scavenging]] spot by a [[Unidentified Pantoran merchant|Pantoran merchant]] and a [[Unidentified human miner (bantha graveyard)|human miner]]. The Tuskens later departed the site however<ref name="Outlaws" /> by the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> a group of [[pirate]]s had moved into the graveyard and had tried to jumped the [[Pantoran]] while they were scavenging. After managing to escape, they later recounted the encounter to the [[miner]] while both were in the [[Scavenger's Draw]] area of the Tatooine [[city]] [[Mos Eisley]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bantha Graveyard was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] as part of an ambient conversation players can overhear in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> The [[Bantha graveyard (Tatooine)/Legends|bantha graveyard]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the "Tatooine with Luke Skywalker" toy released as part of the [[1995]] [[Kenner Products]] [[Star Wars: The Power of the Force (1995 toy line)|The Power of the Force]] toy line.<ref name="Complete Galaxy">{{KennerCite|set=Star Wars: The Power of the Force (1995 toy line)|pack=Complete Galaxy - Tatooine with Luke Skywalker|link=POTF2cgluke.asp}}</ref>
