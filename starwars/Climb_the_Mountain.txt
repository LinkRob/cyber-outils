"'''Climb The Mountain'''" was a [[music]] track by [[The Drasa Sisters]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> it could be heard on the [[jukebox]] at the [[The Domak Refectory|Domak Refectory]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Climb The Mountain" was created as an [[in-universe]] music track by [[Cody Matthew Johnson]] for the [[2024]] [[Ubisoft]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" /> It was released in the game's soundtrack ''[[Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)]]''.<ref name="Songs from the Underworld">{{OST|Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)}}</ref>
