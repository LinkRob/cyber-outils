'''Bakur Repulsor Corporation''' was a manufacturing company that produced [[repulsorlift]] [[engine]]s. The company's products did not produce the shimmer produced by some short-range repulsorlifts between a vehicle using the engine and the [[planet]]ary surface it was pushing against.<ref name="BMF 38" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Bakur Repulsor Corporation was mentioned in the article "Repulsorlifts," which was published in the "[[Secrets of Spaceflight]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 38|thirty-eighth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine<ref name="BMF 38" /> around [[September 23]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|38}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Bakur RepulsorCorp]] first appeared in the [[1993]] novel ''[[The Truce at Bakura]]'', which was written by [[Kathy Tyers]].<ref name="TTaB">''[[The Truce at Bakura]]''</ref>
