A '''D4 wrist comlink''' was a type of [[wrist comm]] used for encrypted communication. The [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] wore a D4 wrist [[comlink]] which she used<ref name="Outlaws Visual Journey" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsData">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
D4 wrist comlinks first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> They were first pictured in the game's "World Premiere Trailer" which was released in [[2023]].<ref name="World Premiere Trailer">{{SWYouTube|XF0kMT39GNY|Star Wars Outlaws: World Premiere Trailer|sw_url=video/world-premiere-trailer-star-wars-outlaws}}</ref> It was first identified as a D4 wrist comlink in the ''[[Star Wars Outlaws: A Visual Journey]]'' reference art book released as part of the Ultimate Edition of the game.<ref name="Outlaws Visual Journey" /> [[Wikipedia:Concept_art|Concept art]] for the comlinks were was done by the Concept Artist [[Kamil Haddad]] under Lead Concept Artist [[Marthe Jonkers]] and Art Director [[Benedikt Podlesnigg]]. The wrist comm model for ''Star Wars Outlaws'' was made by [[Jakub Mrówczyński]].<ref name="Haddad ArtStation">{{ArtStation|subdomain=kamilhaddad|url=projects/98EJ2v|name=[[Kamil Haddad]]|text=Star Wars Outlaws - Kay's Gear|date=2024-09-30|quote=Gear directed by: [[Benedikt Podlesnigg]] [[Marthe Jonkers]] Check out some sick models of the gear by: [[Jakub Mrówczyński]]}}</ref>
