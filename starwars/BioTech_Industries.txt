{{Quote|Cybernetics are such a pain. Look at this: the 66K needs a novic patellar to power the joint, but BioTech only sells it in the Core and Colonies.|Riley, in the ''Smuggler's Guide''|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
'''BioTech Industries''' was a company that produced [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] technology, including the [[novic patellar]], which powered the joint of a [[66K]] cybernetic [[Limb|leg]]. They sold novic patellars only in the [[Core Worlds]] and [[Colonies]]. Because of this, although she needed a novic patellar for her cybernetic leg, the [[smuggler]] [[Riley]] did not have access to one. In place of the BioTech novic patellar, she used a [[Lotho Junker omnislug]]. She recorded her frustration over the limited availability of the company's product in the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]]'s ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], a members of the [[Imperial Information Office]] used BioTech technology to become more efficient, although doing so occasionally caused mental damage.<ref name="SWBook">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
BioTech Industries was first mentioned in [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 1|first issue]] of the magazine series ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]'', released in [[2016]].<ref name="HC1-DB" />
