{{Quote|What our mothers and fathers fought for, we will not let die. Not today. Today we make our last stand, for the galaxy. For Leia. For everyone we've lost.|Poe Dameron, to the Resistance|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
The '''Battle of Exegol''', also known as the '''raid on Exegol''' or the '''Sith Eternal uprising''', was the decisive final battle in the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]] which resulted in the Resistance and the [[Citizens' Fleet]] defeating the [[Final Order]], the [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyer]] [[fleet]] built by the [[Sith Eternal]]. The Resistance's victory prevented the [[Star Destroyer]]s from deploying from [[Exegol]] to reinforce [[First Order military|First Order forces]] and began an [[Uprising against the First Order|uprising against it]] across [[the galaxy]]. The battle also marked the conclusion of the ancient, millennia-long war between the [[Jedi]] and the [[Sith]], ending in a decisive victory for the Jedi with the destruction of [[Darth Sidious]] and prevention of the Sith's return once and for all.

The origins of the battle emerged from the [[Exegol Broadcast|revelation]] that [[Darth]] Sidious had returned after his [[death]] at the [[Battle of Endor]] and was hiding on Exegol in the [[Unknown Regions]], where [[Sith cultist]]s had constructed a massive fleet to establish a new [[Sith Empire]]. The [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], having just learned her true identity as the Emperor's [[Palpatine family|granddaughter]], utilized a [[Sith wayfinder]] to locate Sidious' [[Sith Citadel|stronghold]], simultaneously showing the Resistance the path to Exegol.

The battle began when the [[Resistance navy]] attacked Exegol to prevent the launching of the Sith fleet. A [[Exegol landing team|ground team]] led by [[General]] [[Finn]] was tasked with taking out a [[navigation tower]] while an [[Exegol air team|air team]] commanded by General [[Poe Dameron]] was to defend them. However, when a [[Unidentified First Order officer (Exegol)|First Order officer]] realized the Resistance was targeting the navigation tower, [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]] had the source of the navigation signal switched to his [[command ship]], forcing the Resistance's ground team to land on the hull of his [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']]. The Resistance was nearly destroyed by the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]] until General [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]] and [[Chewbacca]] arrived in the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' with a massive makeshift citizens' fleet composed of over fourteen thousand [[starship]]s [[pilot]]ed by volunteer civilians from all across the [[galaxy]].

Meanwhile, Darth Sidious attempted to turn Rey to the [[dark side of the Force]], exhorting her to strike him and rule the galaxy as a Sith. In truth, Sidious planned to have his spirit transferred into Rey, which would allow him to possess her body, with the help of a [[Transference|ritual]] that he learned from his master [[Darth Plagueis]]. However, [[Ben Solo]] arrived to help her; they defeated the [[Knights of Ren]] and Sidious' [[Sovereign Protector]]s, but Sidious used their [[Force dyad|connection]] to heal himself. The [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord]] disposed of Solo, using [[the Force]] to cast him into a nearby abyss, and ascended the [[Throne of the Sith]] himself, from where he unleashed torrents of [[Force lightning]] into the sky, electrifying the Resistance's fleet and the citizens' fleet. However, Rey was restrengthened by the spirits of past Jedi, allowing her to stand and face her grandfather, who refocused his lightning towards her, giving the Resistance an opportunity to finish their mission. Finn and [[Jannah]] destroyed the command ship, disabling the signal and preventing the Sith fleet from leaving Exegol, while the citizens' fleet and [[starfighter]]s destroyed a large number of Sith Star [[Destroyer]]s.

Rey defended herself from her grandfather's Force lightning with the [[lightsaber]]s of her [[Jedi Master|masters]], [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], causing the lightning to deflect back at the [[Sith Lord]] and disintegrate him, killing the Emperor and his disciples and permanently preventing the return of the Sith. Rey died, but Solo reappeared and sacrificed himself to [[Force healing|resurrect]] her, giving his life force to her. With the Sith fleet destroyed, Rey and the rest of the Resistance and citizens' fleet departed from Exegol. During their return to their [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|base]], they learned that people all across the galaxy were rising up against the weakened First Order.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|How did you do it? Defeat an empire with almost nothing?''"''<br />''"''We had each other. That's how we won.|Poe Dameron and Lando Calrissian|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:DarthSidiousExegol-TROS.jpg|left|thumb|300px|Despite his imperfect resurrection, Darth Sidious planned revenge on the Jedi and the Resistance.]]
Following the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the spirit of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] and [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Darth Sidious]] was able to return after his [[death]] and arrive on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]] in the [[Unknown Regions]] in a [[Cloning|clone]] body. However, as a result he needed to be sustained by an [[Ommin harness]] due to his new body being physically impaired. On Exegol, the Emperor was attended to by a [[Sith]] [[Religion|cult]], the [[Sith Eternal]]. During the following [[Standard year|years]], the Sith Eternal constructed a massive [[Fleet|armada]] of [[planet]]-destroying [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] for what Sidious called the [[Final Order]], with the goal of establishing a new [[Sith Empire]] and restoring Sith rule to [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> by which point the [[First Order]], a [[First Order military|military]] junta formed by former [[Imperial officer]]s, [[noble]]s, technologists,<ref name="FO Databank">{{Databank|the-first-order|The First Order}}</ref> and [[warlord]]s<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> who had fled into the Unknown Regions after the [[Battle of Jakku|fall]] of the Empire,<ref name="EE">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> had controlled large swathes of the [[galaxy]] as part of their [[First Order-Resistance War|war of conquest.]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> An unexpected [[Exegol Broadcast|broadcast]] was put out across the galaxy using the voice of Emperor Palpatine, long thought to be deceased, which promised revenge.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Seeking to crush any potential threat to his rule, [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], who had risen to rule the First Order after [[Battle of Oetchi|assassinating]] his master and predecessor, [[Snoke]], sought a path to the Emperor. Ren [[Mission to Mustafar (First Order)|acquired]] his [[Vader's wayfinder|grandfather's wayfinder]] [[Wayfinder|device]] and used it to travel alone to Exegol, where he found Sidious, who did return to the land of the living, and his followers. The [[Sith Lord]] revealed to Ren that he created Snoke, and told Ren that the First Order was "just the beginning." He promised Ren the might of the Final Order if he killed [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], the last [[Jedi]], who he revealed to Ren was his granddaughter.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:Vanik-AjanKloss-TROS.png|thumb|right|261px|The Resistance devised a plan to stop the Sith fleet from leaving Exegol.|alt=]]
After a [[Mission to Pasaana (Resistance)|series]] [[Mission to Kijimi|of]] [[Mission to Kef Bir|missions]] with [[Poe Dameron]], [[Finn]], [[Chewbacca]], [[C-3PO]], [[BB-8]], and [[D-O]], Rey acquired the [[Emperor's wayfinder]]. However, it was destroyed by Ren to stop her from facing Darth Sidious without him, who had also informed her that she was the Emperor's granddaughter, making her a [[Palpatine family|Palpatine]]. Rey defeated Ren in a [[lightsaber duel]] after he was distracted by the death of his mother and Rey's [[Jedi Master]], [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], and took his TIE whisper. Initially giving into fear and exiling herself on [[Ahch-To]], where she destroyed Ren's ship, Rey was motivated by the [[Force spirit|spirit]] of her first Jedi Master, [[Luke Skywalker]], who encouraged her to face her fear to win the war and restore the [[Jedi Order]] to its former glory.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Rey traveled along a safe route to Exegol in Skywalker's [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing]], [[Red Five (T-65B X-wing starfighter)|''Red Five'']], using Ren's wayfinder and transmitted her plotted course to the Resistance, allowing them to travel along her passage. Through information acquired from D-O, a [[droid]] that had been owned by a [[Sith cultist]], [[Ochi]], the Resistance learned that the Sith fleet required a signal from a [[navigation tower]] in order to deploy off of Exegol, and devised a battle strategy to take out the tower. Poe Dameron would lead an [[Exegol air team|air team]] to find the navigation tower and take out the enemy cannons while Finn would lead a [[Exegol landing team|ground team]] to take out it out. [[Rose Tico]] added that they deduced that destroying the fleet's [[axial superlaser]]s would ignite the main reactor, destroying the ships. [[Beaumont Kin]] suggested pulling off [[Holdo maneuver]]s, but Finn thought the move had a one in a million chance of working. Dameron and Finn, the former who had become the leading general of the Resistance after Organa's death, sent General [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and Chewbacca on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to the [[Core Worlds|Core systems]] to try and recruit any of their allies to aid them. Meanwhile, Ren experienced a [[Force vision|vision]] of a memory of his father, [[Han Solo]]. Confessing his love for his father, Ren threw his [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|lightsaber]] into the sea and renounced the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]], redeeming himself and assuming his [[Reproduction|born]] identity as Ben Solo once again.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Darth Sidious contacted the First Order's [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]], who, as an Imperial veteran, had previously served the Emperor, allowed Sidious to formally take charge of the First Order, which gained the Sith fleet in order to increase their resources so they can become strong enough to restore the Empire. General Pryde subsequently pledged his personal allegiance to the Sith Lord. Sidious then declared that the Final Order was beginning; he ordered Pryde to meet him on Exegol, and sent him a [[Derriphan|ship]] from the Sith fleet, which Pryde used to [[Destruction of Kijimi|destroy]] a planet known to the Resistance on the Emperor's orders, [[Kijimi]]. Pryde arrived at Exegol on his [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]], the [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']],<ref name="TROS VD" /> which served as the Sith fleet's [[command ship]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

==The battle==
===Air battle===
{{Quote|Welcome to Exegol.|General Poe Dameron|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:SidiousOrdersPryde-TROS.jpg|thumb|left|286px|The First Order was officially pledged to Darth Sidious by Allegiant General Pryde.]]
The Resistance arrived on Exegol to find a massive armada of ''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers, which comprised the Sith fleet. They realized the fleet had the potential to destroy any planet in the galaxy when they received Darth Sidious' demand for the free world's immediate surrender or else they perish, since every Star Destroyer was equipped with the same cannon as the one that destroyed Kijimi. Finn led the ground assault covered by the air assault led by Dameron in order to disable the navigation tower while at the same time avoiding Sith Eternal tracking. In response to the Resistance's attack, Pryde ordered all Star Destroyers to open fire using their [[ion cannon]]s only, to minimize damage from friendly fire.<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> A [[B-wing Mark III|Resistance B-wing]] was immediately destroyed by the Star Destroyers' fire. The Sith fleet soon deployed swarms of [[TIE/dg starfighter]]s. A [[Unidentified First Order officer (Exegol)|First Order officer]] then spotted the assault force and informed Pryde, who immediately recognized the threat and had the source of the navigation signal transferred to the ''Steadfast'', intending to guide the fleet out themselves.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:Resistance_fleet_Ep_IX.png|thumb|250px|Resistance forces arrived on Exegol to prevent the launch of the Final Order.]]
When the lander realized the signal had been transferred, Finn reached out with the Force to identify the new source of the command signal. Identifying the ''Steadfast'' as the source because it stood out among the Sith Star Destroyers, they went forward with their ground assault and landed on the hull of the Resurgent Star Destroyer instead. Pryde ordered a large group of Sith troopers onto the hull to stop the boarders, along with an electromagnetic pulse to disable their speeders. To his disbelief, another officer told him that they weren't using speeders; instead, they were using [[orbak]] mounts. The strike team managed to disable the tower after a brutal battle. Pryde was not concerned, and simply ordered it to be restarted. As the Resistance strike team prepared to leave, Finn and Jannah stayed behind to stop them from resetting and switching the signal to another Star Destroyer. Poe then ordered the starfighter pilots to launch an attack on the Sith Star Destroyers until General Calrissian and his reinforcements arrived.<ref name="Episode IX" />

===Showdown with the resurrected Sidious===
{{Quote|Long have I waited. For my grandchild to come home. I never wanted you dead. I wanted you here... ''Empress Palpatine''.|Darth Sidious, welcoming Rey|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:TheDyadVsTheEmperor-TROS.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A redeemed Ben Solo stood with Rey against the resurrected Darth Sidious, wielding the Skywalker lightsabers in tandem.]]
While the battle raged above, Rey entered the [[Sith Citadel]] and found Darth Sidious in the [[Amphitheater of the Sith Eternal|Amphitheater]], which contained an arena filled with Sith loyalists. Sidious expressed his satisfaction at finally seeing his granddaughter, while telling her that the Resistance was walking into a trap. Above the throne of the Sith, he opened the ceiling, revealing an outnumbered and outmatched Resistance and its seemingly imminent failure against the [[Sith Eternal military]]. Sidious exploited Rey's feelings for the Resistance, telling her that the only way to save them would be for her to strike him down in anger, calling her "Empress Palpatine" and encouraging her to take her rightful place on the throne of the Sith and no one has her back like how Luke was rescued by his father. This would ensure the Sith Lord's essence's transferal to Rey, which will allow him to possess her body<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>, thus renewing the Sith Eternal for the reign of a new Sith Empire that would control the galaxy. Rey was faced with a confrontation of wills as the desire to save the Resistance as well as her uneasiness of being a Palpatine collided in her mind. With the Sith loyalists chanting, Sidious prepared to be struck by Rey.<ref name="Episode IX" />

However, a glimpse of hope came when Rey sensed the newly redeemed Ben Solo through the Force. Landing his [[TIE scout]] next to ''Red Five'', which Rey had used to reach Exegol from Ahch-To, Solo made his way into the Sith Citadel, shooting down some [[Sovereign Protector]]s. On the way, he encountered his former comrades, the Knights of Ren. He attempted to get past them by [[Force push]]ing [[Cardo]] to the ground. However, [[Kuruk]] and [[Ushar]] responded by Force pushing Solo.<ref name="Episode IX" />

When [[Vicrul]] attempted to attack Solo with his [[Vicrul's scythe|phrik scythe]], Solo caused him to stumble. The other knights, Cardo, Kuruk, [[Trudgen]], and Ushar, attacked the unarmed Solo, scarring his face. He contacted Rey through their [[Force-bond]] and nodded. Pretending to strike down Sidious, Rey instead used their Force bond to transfer the [[Skywalker lightsaber|lightsaber]] of [[Anakin Skywalker]] to Solo as she ignited the [[Leia's lightsaber|lightsaber]] of Leia Organa. Solo then proceeded to use the Skywalker lightsaber to kill his former comrades, starting with a fatal slash to Kuruk and Cardo's chests and grazing [[Ap'lek's Mandalorian executioner vibro-ax]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:Force_drained_by_Sidious.png|thumb|right|250px|The Sith Lord attacked the dyad with the dark side, draining Solo and Rey of their essences to restore his broken body.]]
Blocking swings from Ushar's war club and [[Trudgen's vibrocleaver]], Solo Force pushed Ap'lek to his death, as he restrained and felled Vicrul. Trudgen attempted to attack his former leader but was effortlessly stabbed in the chest. Ushar attempted to attack too but Solo leapt over him and stabbed him from the back. After Ushar dropped dead, Solo then arrived at Sidious' lair and reunited with Rey, who had just finished off Sidious' Sovereign Protectors.<ref name="Episode IX" />

At first, Sidious intended to kill both of them, but realized that the duo together made a [[Force dyad]], a phenomenon that, according to Sidious, was a power like life itself and had not been seen in generations. Realizing that their powers combined would allow him to fully return to life, the Emperor used the Force to drain the life energy of both Rey and Solo to restore himself to the form he had before his first death at the hand of Solo's grandfather, during the [[Battle of Endor]]. Drained of their powers, Rey and Solo collapsed to the floor. Having no further need for Solo, Sidious used the Force to throw him into a nearby abyss, exclaiming revenge for what Darth Vader did to him and declaring the death of the [[Skywalker family]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

===The galaxy fleet arrives===
{{Quote|My friends, I'm sorry, I thought we had a shot, but there's just too many of them.''"''<br />''"''But there are more of us, Poe. There are more of us.|Poe Dameron and Lando Calrissian|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:GalaxyFleet.png|thumb|left|250px|During the battle, the Resistance fleet was augmented by a citizens' fleet.]]
Up above, the Resistance was taking heavy losses, as Finn and Dameron continued to lead the fight. [[Major]] [[Temmin Wexley]] was shot down by Sith TIEs, making Poe realize that they were losing the battle. However, General Calrissian arrived in the ''Millennium Falcon'', bringing with him the [[Citizens' Fleet]],<ref name="Episode IX" /> a massive armada comprised of 14,000+ ships from all points across the galaxy.<ref name="Bucket List" /> Amongst those gathered included the legendary ''[[Tantive IV]]'', the [[Mon Calamari fleet]], the [[Phantom Squadron]], the ''[[Ghost]]'', the [[Anodyne Two|Anodyne two]], the [[Alphabet Two|Alphabet two]], [[Zay Versio]] from the [[Inferno Squad]], the ''[[Fireball]]'' piloted by Resistance pilot [[Kazuda Xiono]], and more.<ref name="TROS EE" /> The citizens of the galaxy had come to aid the Resistance in their hour of need.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Dameron quickly ordered the fleet to target the superlaser cannons, to considerable effect. Pryde was stupefied at the appearance and size of the fleet, arguing that the Resistance had no navy. Admiral [[Frantis Griss|Griss]], on the other hand, realized that it was not a navy but ordinary people that finally had enough of Sidious' tyranny and banded together to bring him down once and for all. Sith Star Destroyers began falling to cruisers and fighters alike, with one being brought down single-handedly by a [[Comeuppance|lone Y-wing]] piloted by [[Zorii Bliss]], who had escaped the destruction of her homeworld with [[Babu Frik]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

===The death of Darth Sidious and the Sith Order's demise===
{{Quote|You are nothing! A scavenger girl is no match for the power in me! I am ''all'' the Sith!"<br />"And I&hellip;am all the Jedi.|Darth Sidious and Rey, shortly before the former's death|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:Force_Storm_Exegol.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Sidious unleashed torrents of Force lightning on the Resistance and the citizens' fleet.]]
Darth Sidious, having been fully restored to his former self, told his faithful loyalists to not fear the attack of the Resistance, as nothing would stop the Sith's return. He then proceeded to ignite a powerful storm of [[Force lightning]] into the air, which disabled all Resistance ships up to the size of Nebulon-B frigates, including those of Poe and the galaxy fleet, and sending them plummeting toward the ground, including the ''Tantive IV''. Much to Poe's shock, he realized he was losing control of his X-wing. Weakened and seemingly defeated, Rey heard the voices of past Jedi, including her first Master Luke Skywalker, Luke's father [[Anakin Skywalker|Anakin]], Anakin's former Padawan [[Ahsoka Tano]], Anakin and Luke's Master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], Obi-Wan's Master [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], Master [[Mace Windu]], Master [[Luminara Unduli]], Master [[Aayla Secura]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Kanan Jarrus]], and Master [[Adi Gallia]] encouraging her and lending her the strength to carry on against the Sith Lord.<ref name="Episode IX" /> She had embraced peace and calm, experiencing a state of [[oneness]].<ref name="TROS EE" />

[[File:The_death_of_Sidious.jpg|left|thumb|250x250px|Aided by the spirits of the Jedi, Rey turned her grandfather's power against him, destroying the Emperor and aborting the return of the Sith.]]
Rey then stood up, with her new courage and strength received from the Jedi before her. Sidious, noticing this, ceased his Force lightning attack on the Resistance fleet and their friends. General Dameron, having realized that his starship's systems were back online, regrouped the allied forces along with his own and resumed their assault on the Sith fleet, stating that they had one chance to take out the armada of Sith Star Destroyers. Telling Rey that her death would be the end of the story of the Rebellion, Sidious launched a barrage of Force lightning at Rey. Rey then attempted to block it with Leia's lightsaber, but the Emperor told Rey that she was just "a scavenger girl" who was "no match for the power in" him and that he is "all the Sith." However, Rey issued an immediate rebuttal by telling Sidious that she is "all the Jedi," calling back Anakin's lightsaber and igniting it, using the two lightsabers to deflect her grandfather's lightning back at him. Sidious was initially successful at resisting the attack, but very soon, he became physically unable to defend against the strain of the lightning being used against him. As his body slowly degraded, the horrified Sidious screamed in agony as he was ultimately disintegrated by his own lightning. In a massive explosion that consumed Sidious, the citadel, and all of the Sith Eternal under him, the evil tyrant known as Darth Sidious - the former senator of Naboo, the final Supreme Chancellor of the old Galactic Republic, Dark Lord of the Sith, the Phantom Menace, self-proclaimed Emperor of the entire galaxy, and the man who had wrought unspeakable evil upon countless worlds for an entire century - was finally and permanently slain both physically and spiritually, thus bringing the Sith Order, as well as the ancient conflict between the Jedi and the Sith that had lasted for many millennia, to its final conclusion once and for all.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Rey made the ultimate sacrifice and gave up her mortal coil providing the sheer force required to defeat Darth Sidious, an event which was felt by Finn, and her limp body crashed to the ground. As she died, she reached out with the Force, attempting to find Solo through their Force bond as her last life force left her body.<ref name="TROS EE" /> However, Ben Solo, having survived the earlier ordeal with Sidious, managed to climb up the cliff face from the abyss, and, finding Rey's body near the throne, ran over to her. Ben saw her lifeless face and tearfully cradled her in his arms, but he noticed a spark of her essence and realized that she was not gone for good.<ref name="TROS EE" /> Rey needed to carry on his master's legacy, and so Ben decided to sacrifice himself so that she could live again. He put his hand on Rey's chest and began to transfer his own life force to her body, as she had done for him before, resurrecting her. The pair shared a longing look and a brief kiss, before Ben Solo, the last Skywalker and redeemed Jedi, became one with the Force and vanished into thin air, having helped bring balance back to the Force, just as his grandfather once had. Simultaneously, back on [[Ajan Kloss]], Maz Kanata watched with a peaceful gaze as Leia Organa became one with the Force, seeming ending the bloodline of the Skywalker family.<ref name="Episode IX" /> Though Rey felt despair as he died, she heard his voice calling to her from the afterlife saying that he would always be with her. Rey was reassured that no one was ever truly gone and left the destroyed Citadel.<ref name="TROS EE" />

===Fall of the Final Order===
{{Quote|Their fleet is stuck here! They're toast! Come on!|General Poe Dameron, to the rest of the fleet|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:SteadfastExegol-TROS.jpg|thumb|250px|With the destruction of the ''Steadfast'', the Sith fleet was trapped on Exegol and met a destructive demise.]]
Meanwhile, above Exegol, the Resistance and citizens' fleet were continuing to press the attack against the remaining Sith forces. Finn and Jannah successfully destroyed the [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] by turning its turbolasers against the ship itself. The ''Steadfast'' burst into flames, killing all on board. It then fell towards the ground and crashed into the surface of Exegol, while Calrissian and Chewbacca rescued Finn and Jannah aboard the ''Millennium Falcon''.<ref name="Episode IX" /> 

With the ''Steadfast'' destroyed before it could transfer the signal to another friendly ship, the entire Sith fleet became trapped on Exegol. The Resistance and the galaxy fleet destroyed the rest of the Sith Star Destroyers in a similar fashion to the ''Steadfast'', by blowing up their [[axial superlaser]]s. With the Final Order fleet destroyed, the First Order defeated, and all the enemy ships falling to the ground in ruins, Rey, the Resistance and the galaxy fleet emerged victorious; and left Exegol to return to Ajan Kloss, where they celebrated the end of the First Order-Resistance War.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Aftermath==
===The spark becomes a fire===
{{Main|Uprising against the First Order}}
{{Quote|People are rising up all over the galaxy. Poe, we did it.''"''<br />''"''We did it.|Finn and Poe Dameron|<ref name="Episode IX" />|audio=PeopleAreRising-TheRiseOfSkywalker.flac}}
[[File:The_Uprising_on_Jakku.jpg|left|thumb|264x264px|An uprising against the First Order began after the Resistance and citizens' victory on Exegol.]]
The Battle of Exegol stood as the final battle of the First Order-Resistance War.<ref name="Origami2">''[[Star Wars Origami 2: 34 More Projects from a Galaxy Far, Far Away....]]''</ref> As the [[Resistance]] and their Citizens' Fleet left Exegol, they learned that across the galaxy people were [[Uprising against the First Order|rising up]] as the weakened First Order began to collapse without their cultist allies to reinforce them or the leadership of their Emperor. Across the galaxy, people rose up and destroyed the ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers, as seen on [[Cloud City]], [[Jakku]]'s [[Starship Graveyard]], and on the [[forest]] [[moon]] of Endor. The Resistance returned to the their [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|base]] on Ajan Kloss, where they celebrated their victory, among them Rey, General Finn, General Calrissian, and General Dameron.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Through Rey and Ben Solo, the ancient Jedi-Sith conflict also ended in an ultimate victory for the Jedi, with Darth Sidious being destroyed and the threat of any further Sith rule being quashed forever. Through Finn, Poe Dameron, Lando Calrissian, and the entirety of the Resistance's and Citizen's Fleets, Sidious's Final Order fleet collapsed on Exegol, with no possible threat of return, ushering in a new sense of hope and courage among the galaxy's residents as they mustered up the will to rise up and destroy the remnants of the First Order. With the fall of the First Order, the galaxy had at long last found the peace and tranquility that it had long desired.<ref name="Episode IX" />

===Rey becomes a Skywalker===
[[File:ReySkywalker.png|thumb|right|300px|Though Ben Solo was the last of the Skywalker bloodline, Rey assumed the name "Skywalker" to honor their memory and in recognition of the family she chose.]]
Sometime after the celebration, Rey, with BB-8 alongside her, traveled to Anakin and Luke's home world of Tatooine. Rey found [[Owen Lars|Owen]] and [[Beru Whitesun Lars|Beru Lars]]' old homestead, which also served as Luke's home, and as she stood in it, she started to reminisce and think back to everything she had accomplished since she started on her journey. She then ventured outside the homestead and wrapped both Luke and Leia's lightsabers in cloth, using the Force to bury them both into the sand. As she rose, she revealed that she had constructed a lightsaber of her own, and ignited its yellow blade. [[Unidentified Tatooine elder|An old woman]] soon passed by and asked who she was, and, with Luke and Leia's Force ghosts watching on, Rey confidently declared that she had inherited the Skywalker name and was now known as "Rey Skywalker". In her quest to restore galactic peace, Rey had become a Jedi Master, just as Luke had done before her.<ref name="The Star Wars Book" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Exegol served as the climactic battle of the [[2019]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], the final installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]]. Despite Solo's death thanks to the battle,<ref name="Episode IX" /> his actor, [[Adam Driver]], believed there was still more ground to cover with the character and pitched the post-''Rise of Skywalker'' film ''[[The Hunt for Ben Solo]]'' to [[Lucasfilm]], the higherups of which loved the idea he and his co-creators had established. However, the project was vetoed on the order of [[The Walt Disney Company]] higherups [[Bob Iger (chief executive officer)|Bob Iger]] and [[Alan Bergman]], who, according to Driver, did not understand how Solo could have survived.<ref name="2025AP">{{WebCite|url=https://apnews.com/article/adam-driver-star-wars-soderbergh-jarmusch-4e08164d0419759f1b5b50d69864975d|text=Adam Driver on Jarmusch, 'Star Wars' and putting filmmakers first|author=Coyle, Jake|date=2025-10-20|work=[[Wikipedia:Associated Press|Associated Press]]|archivedate=20251020222002}}</ref>
