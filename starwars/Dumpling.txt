'''Dumplings''' were a fried<ref name="BWDHE" /> or steamed<ref name="The Missing Kibbin" /> [[Foodstuff|food]] that was stuffed with filling. They were edible by many [[species]] across the [[The galaxy|galaxy]]. According to the [[Devaronian]] [[chef]] [[Torro Sbazzle]], dumplings were liked by everyone in [[the galaxy]]. When [[Baron Administrator]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] unexpectedly asked Sbazzle to prepare dinner for [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on [[Cloud City]], she made dumplings. Her version used [[Color|midnight]]-hued [[Flour|pastry flour]] and [[zaffa oil]] for the dumpling casings which were baked before adding a filling of three finely diced [[jogan fruit]]s and [[cheese]]. They were finished off by frying them, dunking them in sticky [[honey-wine]], and garnishing the tops with candied rind. She considered making them with [[bantha butter]] instead of zaffa oil before deciding she would not make a bland dish for what may have been the last meal she ever cooked.<ref name="BWDHE" />
