'''Ellick''' was a [[human]] [[bartender]] that worked aboard the [[casino]] [[cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Ellick had tan skin, short [[Color|brown]] [[hair]] and brown [[eye]]s. They wore a blue patterned [[clothing|shirt]], matching pants, a black [[scarf]] and a black waist [[sash]] while working the bar that [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During the [[Morenia's Sabacc tournament|''Morenia'''s Sabacc tournament]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> they were approached by a [[gambler]] named [[Kay Vess]] who asked them for the player list for the tournament. Ellick responded by telling her that it was above their pay grade but suggested that if Vess was interested in placing a bet that she could find a freelance [[bookie]] onboard despite the ''[[Morenia]]'' banning such individuals. Later a [[BX-series droid commando]] designated [[ND-5]] asked Ellick if they knew someone named [[Zana Gatta]] to which Ellick replayed by sharing that Gatta was in room D-34 and had run up a huge tab.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}
