The following events took place in or around the year '''241 BBY''', aka '''3036 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]], '''222 BFE''' according to the [[Imperial calendar|Imperial Calendar]], and '''Year 7736''' according to the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.

==Events==
*[[Ty Yorrick|Tylera Yorrick]] leaves the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Timelines" />
*[[Chandrila Star Line]]'s [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]], ''[[Halcyon]]'', is constructed<ref name="Timelines" /> at the [[Santhe Shipyards]] within [[Coronet City]] on [[Corellia]],<ref name="TFN Review">{{WebCite|url=https://www.theforce.net/story/front/Star_Wars_Galactic_Starcruiser_Reviewed_189457.asp|text=Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Reviewed|work=TheForce.net|archivedate=20220226171430}}</ref> under the supervision of [[Shug Drabor]],<ref name="Halcyon 1">[[Halcyon Legacy 1|''Halcyon Legacy'' 1]]</ref> and sails on its maiden voyage.<ref name="Timelines" />

==Births==
*[[Tep Tep]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Tep Tep">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As Tep Tep is twelve years old during the novel's events, she must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[241 BBY]].</ref>

==Deaths==
*[[Klias Teradine]] on [[Mount Bikja]].<ref name="Timelines" />
