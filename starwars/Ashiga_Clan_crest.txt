A '''Ashiga Clan crest''' was a small object that [[Queen]] [[Ashiga]] bestowed on the strongest [[myrmite]]s of her [[Melitto hive|hive]] and trusted allies. The crest prominently featured the [[syndicate]]'s symbol.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] was gifted a crest after she gained an excellent reputation with the criminal organization.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A Ashiga Clan crest appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. When equipped in the game as a minor charm, the crest restores health when the player performs melee takedowns or finishers.<ref name="Outlaws" />
