'''Emmett''' was a [[human]] individual who was leader of a group of thieves on the [[planet]] [[Tenoo]] during the [[High Republic Era]] alongside [[Chipp]] and [[Z-3OR]]. Emmet led his group in an effort to steal [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] from a [[hovertrain]] after stealing a group of [[Dunnel]]s.<ref name="The Search for the Missing Dunnels" /> Later he attempted to take the [[droid]] [[MB-4]] for the locations of mines in the droid's [[memory bank]].<ref name="A Mission to Remember" />
