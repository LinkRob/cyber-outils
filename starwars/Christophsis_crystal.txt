'''Christophsis crystals''' were a type of [[kyber crystal]] found on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[crystal]]line [[planet]] [[Christophsis]], which was an early flash point of the [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. And while the planet's importance to both the Republic and the Confederacy stemmed from several different sources, its importance to the [[Jedi Order]] lay in the pure kyber crystals that formed in the crystalline [[forest]]s of the planet.<ref name="Collapse" />

A [[lightsaber]] blade created by Christophsis crystals amplified kinetic energy in a unique way among lightsabers, which resulted in powerful impacts.<ref name="Collapse" />

==Behind the scenes==
Christophsis crystals were mentioned in ''[[Collapse of the Republic]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Collapse" />
