'''''Casus Belli'' 122''' is an issue of the French role-playing magazine [[Casus Belli (magazine)|''Casus Belli'']] that was published in [[October]] of [[1999]]. It contained an article, "[[Le guide de La menace fantôme]]," explaining lore and giving role-playing stat blocks to characters and creatures from the 1999 film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="CB122" />
