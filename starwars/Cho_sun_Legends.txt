In [[Lightsaber combat/Legends|lightsaber combat]], '''''cho sun''''' was the act of [[Dismemberment/Legends|cutting off]] an opponent's weapon [[Limb/Legends|arm]].

==Notable Uses==
{{Quote|Slash off their fighting arms, Goir! You'll take them alive.|[[Sedriss QL]] to [[Vill Goir]] about [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] and [[Kam Solusar]]|<ref name="Dark Empire II (audio)">[[Dark Empire II (audio drama)|''Dark Empire II'' audio drama]]</ref>}}
[[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] [[Ulic Qel-Droma/Legends|Ulic Qel-Droma]] performed a ''cho sun'' on his [[Cay Qel-Droma|own brother]] during the [[Great Sith War/Legends|Great Sith War]], and then killed him.<ref name="TOTJ">[[Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War 5|''Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War'' 5]]</ref> It should be noted that even though Cay Qel-Droma's arm was mechanical, ''cho sun'' still applies because it was the arm with which he dueled.

[[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Jedi Master/Legends|Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] performed ''cho sun'' at least four times: in bars on both [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]<ref name="SWAOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref> and [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]],<ref name="SWANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> also during the [[Battle of Muunilinst/Legends|Battle of Muunilinst]] while battling the [[Gen'Dai/Legends|Gen'Dai]] [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Durge/Legends|Durge]],<ref name="Star Wars: Clone Wars">''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]''</ref> and during a duel with [[Darth Krayt|A'Sharad Hett]] on Tatooine.<ref name="Leg16">[[Legacy (2006) 16|''Legacy'' (2006) 16]]</ref>

Jedi Knight [[Corwin Shelvay/Legends|Corwin Shelvay]] executed a ''cho sun'' against Imperial [[High Inquisitor]] [[Antinnis Tremayne]].

Jedi Master [[Tresina Lobi]] performed a successful ''cho sun'' strike against [[Dark Lord of the Sith/Legends|Dark Lady of the Sith]] [[Lumiya]], during [[Duel in the Fellowship Plaza|their duel]] in Coruscant's [[Fellowship Plaza]].<ref name="Tempest">''[[Legacy of the Force: Tempest]]''</ref>

[[Jaina Solo Fel|Jaina Solo]] performed a ''cho sun'' strike on her twin brother [[Darth Caedus]] during the [[Second Battle of Roche]], slicing his arm off at the elbow, mirroring his [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|grandfather's]] injury during his [[First Battle of Geonosis/Legends|duel]] with [[Count/Legends|Count]] [[Dooku/Legends|Dooku]].<ref name="Invincible">''[[Legacy of the Force: Invincible]]''</ref>

[[Sith Saber]] [[Gavar Khai]] received a ''cho sun'' strike from [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] in [[44 ABY]].

[[Darth Nihl]] finessed a ''cho sun'' while dueling [[Wolf Sazen]] during the [[Massacre at Ossus]].<ref name="SWL">[[Legacy (2006) 1|''Legacy'' (2006) 1]]</ref> Nihl himself lost an arm while fighting [[Cade Skywalker]] at the [[Temple of the Sith]].<ref name="SWL19">[[Legacy (2006) 19|''Legacy'' (2006) 19]]</ref>

[[Mon Calamari/Legends|Mon Calamari]] [[Tealart]] received a ''cho sun'' to his right arm from [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Drok]] following a failed assassination attempt on [[Admiral/Legends|Admiral]] [[Gar Stazi]].
