'''4-A7''' was a [[Gender|masculine-programmed]] [[RA-7 protocol droid]] and prototype [[spy droid]] that served the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] under [[Dark Acolyte]] [[Asajj Ventress]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. During the [[Battle of Teth]] in [[22 BBY]], 4-A7 was stationed at a [[B'omarr Order Monastery]] where the Confederacy had placed [[Rotta]], the kidnapped [[Reproduction|son]] of the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]. After the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Torrent Company]] defeated the monastery's [[battle droid]] defense, Ventress dispatched 4-A7 to greet their enemies. Masquerading as an old [[caretaker droid]], he claimed they had freed him and informed the [[company]]'s [[Jedi General]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], that Rotta was being kept on the [[Prison|detention level]].

After Skywalker and his [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]], [[Jedi Commander|Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], rescued the infant from his prison, 4-A7 recorded them placing the screaming Rotta into a [[backpack]]. The footage was sent to Separatist [[Head of State|leader]] [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] on [[Tatooine]], who used it to make Jabba believe the Republic was responsible for the kidnapping. 4-A7's Separatist loyalties were later uncovered when Tano found him boarding a [[Twilight|freighter]] with several [[B1-series battle droid|B1 battle droids]]. Dropping the caretaker act, he ordered the droids to [[Death|kill]] Tano to prevent Rotta's rescue, but she quickly managed to destroy the three combat units. Tano turned her weapon against 4-A7, and while the [[protocol droid]] demanded not to be killed, she swiftly decapitated him.

==Biography==
===Assault on Teth===
{{Quote|Who are you?''"<br />"''Merely the humble caretaker, O mighty sir. You have liberated me from those dreadful battle bots. I am most thankful.''"<br />"''Where is the Hutt?''"<br />"''The battle bots kept their prisoners on the detention level. I must warn you it is very dangerous down there, my friend.|Anakin Skywalker meets 4-A7 during the droid's caretaker act|<ref name="The Clone Wars" />}}
Manufactured by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] sympathizers in [[Arakyd Industries]],<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> which itself was secretly supporting the Confederacy, 4-A7 was a [[RA-7 protocol droid]]<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> and prototype [[spy droid]]<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> produced early in the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Birth and death date note" /> a major [[The galaxy|galactic]] conflict fought between the Confederacy and [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Birth and death date note" /> During that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Kidnapping date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the kidnapping of [[Rotta]] to [[22 BBY]].</ref> 4-A7 was given to<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> the Separatist [[Head of State]],<ref name="Rise of the Separatists">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]], as part of a plan to<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> manipulate the [[Hutt Clan]] into joining the Confederacy, which would give the Separatists<ref name="TBotCW">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Clone Wars Begin}}</ref> exclusive access<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> to the [[Hutt]]s' secret [[Hyperspace route|hyperlanes]]<ref name="Dooku biography gallery Image 9">{{Databank|url=count-dooku-biography-gallery|title=Count Dooku Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 9}}</ref> and [[Hutt Space|area of space]]. With the [[droid]] being intended to help the operation,<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> Dooku then gave 4-A7 to<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> his personal [[assassin]] and [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]],<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" /> the [[Dark Acolyte]] [[Asajj Ventress]], who also kidnapped [[Rotta]], the son of Hutt [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>

[[File:Ventress_4A7.png|thumb|left|300px|After the droid defenses were defeated, Asajj Ventress dispatched 4-A7 to meet the Republic forces.]]
Traveling to the [[Wild Space]] [[planet]] of [[Teth]], Ventress quickly brought the kidnapped Huttlet to an abandoned<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> [[B'omarr Order Monastery]]<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> that saw use by<ref name="HC 58">{{HelmetCollectionCite|58|Databank A-Z}}</ref> the crime lord<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure]]'s [[Ziro the Hutt's criminal organization|criminal faction]],<ref name="HC 58" /> with whom Dooku had allied to orchestrate Rotta's kidnapping. With Confederate forces occupying the monastery,<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> Ventress also took 4-A7 to the [[monastery]], where<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> he<ref name="SW:TCW Film DVD Commentary" /> remained stationed with a force of [[battle droid]]s.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> One [[4-A7's battle droid platoon|platoon]] of battle droids was assigned to escort the [[spy]] droid only for 4-A7 to prove himself far superior to his security detail.<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> As the Separatists had planned, the Republic eventually discovered that Rotta was there, leading to an engagement<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> known as the [[Battle of Teth]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> Hoping to rescue the infant to make their own alliance with the Hutts,<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> the Republic's [[Torrent Company]] attacked the Separatist base, quickly taking out the droids' outer defenses.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> However, their assault had been part of the Confederate plan; with the battle droids having played their role, Ventress, standing next to 4-A7 on a balcony overlooking the battlefront, sent the spy droid to meet with the Republic.<ref name="The Clone Wars" />

To trick their enemies into a false sense of security, 4-A7 had been ordered to gain the trust of the Republic forces, especially the Jedi, before Ventress allowed their enemies to venture deeper into the monastery.<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> Pretending to be the monastery's<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> old [[caretaker droid]]<ref name="45 Saga Highlights">{{HelmetCollectionCite|45|Highlights of the Saga}}</ref> and guardian,<ref name="Ahsoka Tano and Other Force Users">{{EncyclopediaCite|Ahsoka Tano and Other Users of the Force}}</ref> he approached the Republic forces, alerting them to his presence with a large noise, and greeted their [[Jedi General]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], claiming that the Republic had liberated him from the [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]] and telling them that Rotta was in the monastery's [[Prison|dungeons]]. After warning the [[Jedi Knight]] that the prison level was dangerous, 4-A7 claimed to think Skywalker's [[Padawan]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], was a servant girl and stated that she should not go down; she responded by activating her [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|lightsaber]]&mdash;which startled 4-A7&mdash;and stating she was a [[Jedi]].<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> As their presence at the monastery carried on, the clones and even the two Jedi largely ignored 4-A7 and remained unaware of his status as a secret [[agent]].<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" />

===Recording the Jedi===
{{Quote|My lord, I have the recording you requested.|Asajj Ventress reports to Dooku after 4-A7 records the Jedi|<ref name="The Clone Wars" />}}
[[File:4-A7recordstheJedi.png|thumb|right|300px|Lured into a false sense of security by 4-A7, Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano remained completely unaware as the droid recorded them place the screaming Rotta into a backpack.]]
Beyond luring the Republic into a fake sense of comfort, 4-A7's vital and main role in the Separatist plot<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> was to frame the Jedi Order in the kidnapping of Rotta<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> and make it seem like they intended to harm him. With the Republic forces not paying the droid much mind,<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> 4-A7 moved back to Ventress<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> and hid away from their enemies' sight<ref name="TBotCW" /> on a balcony.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> Upon being ordered to do so by Ventress,<ref name="Boba Fett and Other Bounty Hunters and Criminals">{{EncyclopediaCite|Boba Fett and Other Characters of the Underworld}}</ref> the droid began to secretly record Skywalker and Tano after they rescued Rotta from the dungeons.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> As the two Jedi struggled to place the uncooperative<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> and screaming infant into a [[backpack]], 4-A7 recorded their actions and discussion,<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> specifically extracting several sentences from Skywalker. Due to his past as a [[Slavery|slave]] to<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> crime lady [[Gardulla Besadii the Elder|Gardulla the Hutt]],<ref name="Gardulla">{{Databank|gardulla-the-hutt|Gardulla the Hutt}}</ref> Skywalker displayed a great deal of anger towards Rotta and Hutts in general.<ref name="The Clone Wars" />

While extracting Skywalker's sentences,<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> 4-A7 also utilized editing software to make it seem like the Jedi were responsible for the kidnapping.<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> He created outright fake images of the Jedi<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> and ultimately ensured his doctored footage showed the Jedi apparently planning to murder the Huttlet.<ref name="45 Saga Highlights" /> He then stood by Ventress when she spoke with Dooku via [[hologram]] and sent the [[count]] the recording,<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> which had also been further edited by her and 4-A7<ref name="Ventress biography gallery Image 8">{{Databank|asajj-ventress-biography-gallery|Asajj Ventress Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 8}}</ref> into a montage<ref name="TBotCW" /> that made it seem like Rotta was being abused.<ref name="Ventress biography gallery Image 8" /> On<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[desert]] world<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Tatooine]], Dooku presented the footage to Jabba and used it to claim the Jedi were the ones behind the kidnapping of Rotta,<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> earning the desired effect<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> as Jabba quickly became angry.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> Due to their confidence in 4-A7's abilities, Ventress and Dooku knew the droid's recording was ready to be shown to Jabba without even double-checking it.<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" />

===Fortunes change===
{{Quote|Hey! You're that caretaker droid. I wondered what happened to you.''"<br />"''Oh, young one. I mean, soon to be Jedi Knight. I had to get away from that terrible&hellip;|Ahsoka Tano reunites with 4-A7|<ref name="The Clone Wars" />}}
Intending to recapture Rotta back under the guise of rescuing the Huttlet, another detachment of the Droid Army arrived at the monastery, kicking off another skirmish against the Republic.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> 4-A7, having completed his mission, was thus ordered to prepare an old<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> [[G9 Rigger-class light freighter|G9 ''Rigger''-class light freighter]]&mdash;a [[starship]] known as the ''[[Twilight]]'' owned by Ziro the [[Hutt]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" />&mdash;for takeoff from a landing pad on a building across from the monastery.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> Accompanied to the docked ship by an escort<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> of three [[B1-series battle droid|B1 battle droids]]<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> from his [[platoon]],<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> the droids began to load the craft and prepare to depart. However, Skywalker, Tano, Rotta, and Skywalker's [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] escaped from battle after spotting the ''Twilight'',<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> which they assumed to be abandoned.<ref name="TBotCW" /> Just as the B1s were finishing loading everything they needed before takeoff, the two Jedi arrived on the landing pad atop a [[can-cell]].<ref name="The Clone Wars" />

[[File:4A-7_uncovered.png|thumb|left|300px|With three battle droids clearly in view, 4-A7's true loyalty was obvious to Ahsoka Tano.]]
As 4-A7 walked beside the [[light freighter]] and inspected a box, Tano spotted the protocol droid, claiming to have wondered what happened to him but continuing to assume he was a caretaker droid. Although 4-A7 had been surprised to hear her voice again, he initially tried to continue his caretaker act, but Tano discovered the spy's true loyalties when the three battle droids entered her gaze, one of which reported that everything was aboard and that they could leave.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> In an attempt to stop Rotta's rescue,<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> 4-A7 ordered the combat droids to shoot Tano and then tried to flee up the ship's [[Entry ramp|ramp]]. However, the three B1s were quickly [[Death|destroyed]] by the Padawan,<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> who glowered at the droid<ref name="Audio Description">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]] {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> as she pointed her [[lightsaber]] directly at him. Despite 4-A7 demanding that she not destroy him, Tano quickly decapitated the protocol unit. Repeating his last words, 4-A7's head fell down the ship's ramp and landed in front of Skywalker. Tano, Skywalker, R2, and Rotta then boarded the [[freighter]], with the astromech knocking 4-A7's head out of his way, causing it to utter a final groan.<ref name="The Clone Wars" />

===Legacy===
Although 4-A7 had been very successful in carrying out his part of Dooku's plot,<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> the Republic ultimately won the battle for the Hutts' loyalties; flying the ''Twilight'' off the platform and eventually to Tatooine, Skywalker and Tano [[Mission to Jabba's Palace (Clone Wars)|returned]] Rotta to his father, and after learning the Jedi were not behind the kidnapping, Jabba allowed the Republic to travel through his territories.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> 4-A7's remains were collected and transported back to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]]<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> on the [[Ecumenopolis|city planet]] [[Coruscant]] in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> to be analyzed, securing the Republic with intelligence that helped it establish the [[Hutt–Republic treaty|finalized treaty]] with the Hutt Clan. The analysis also gave the Jedi new information about other Confederate plots.<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" />

[[File:4D6-J-A7_and_officers.png|thumb|right|300px|Likely inspired by 4-A7's efficiency, the Galactic Empire purchased large quantities of RA-7 protocol droids to spy on its own staff.]]
Nonetheless, due to his successes in his mission on Teth, 4-A7 was remembered as an early success of the RA-7 line, although the droid model never succeeded in making a headway in the protocol droid market. Despite that, Arakyd Industries later managed to secure a close business relationship with the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> the successor state of the Republic that [[Proclamation of the New Order|arose]] with the [[Mission to Mustafar|defeat of the Confederacy]] at the end of the Clone Wars<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Likely inspired by 4-A7's effectiveness during the Battle of Teth, the Empire purchased large quantities of RA-7 units to spy on its own personnel.<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" />

==Characteristics==
===RA-7 traits===
{{Quote|Good guy or bad guy, Master?|Unable to tell based on appearance, Ahsoka Tano asks Anakin Skywalker whether 4-A7 is an ally or foe|<ref name="The Clone Wars" />}}
An RA-7 [[protocol droid]]<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> with [[Gender|masculine]] programming<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> from the early Clone Wars,<ref name="Birth and death date note" /> 4-A7 stood 1.7 [[meter]]s tall<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> and had a [[Color|gray]] body with the plating of a [[3PO-series protocol droid|3PO-series protocol unit]].<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> While he was loyal to the Separatists, 4-A7's appearance did not directly convey his allegiance, making Tano question if he was an adversary or ally when they first met. Upon learning his true loyalties, she compared the droid's plating to [[tin]] as an insult.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> Due to his stealth, intellect, and ability to act independently, 4-A7 proved to be an exceptional spy and was far superior in functionality to the battle droids who escorted him. Having come to hate working with the Confederacy's droid troops, Ventress found 4-A7 to be different; she could be certain the droid was capable of carrying out missions in a reliable manner, which quickly earned him her trust.<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" />

[[File:4-A7-vs-Ahsoka.png|thumb|left|300px|Although he had a full body of 3PO plating, 4-A7 had the distinctive RA-7 series head.]]
Equipped with special components and programming,<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> 4-A7 was also programmed as a spy but retained the RA-7's distinctive,<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> albeit ominous,<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> [[insectoid]] head,<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> which featured a [[vocabulator]] containing a sounding box. As such, he was also able to speak in a number of dialects and languages despite his vocabulator's cheap components. Additionally, possessing large broadband [[photoreceptor]]s, he was able to see across a very wide band and operate in almost complete darkness. These photoreceptors were among 4-A7's more impressive components.<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11 note">{{BuildR2Cite|11|Droid Directory}} &ndash; Based on corresponding data for [[RA-7 protocol droid]]s</ref> Additionally, 4-A7 was equipped with a holographic recording [[camera]] known as a [[holocamera]],<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> which was built into<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> his head.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> 4-A7's large photoreceptors hid the camera and a narrowband communication system for encrypted transmissions.<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> The suite<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> of editing functions included in 4-A7's software gave the droid the ability to manipulate recorded footage whenever he wished<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> to doctor a recording for his masters.<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" />

===Personality===
{{Quote|Don't you dare!|4-A7 demands Ahsoka Tano not kill him|<ref name="The Clone Wars" />}}
A basic yet efficient droid who was a loyal servant of the Separatist Alliance,<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> 4-A7 followed the orders Ventress gave him and had no issue with lying to his enemies by pretending to be an innocent and humble caretaker droid. Speaking in a low voice, he said the Confederate battle droids were dreadful and that he was thankful to be free.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> Trying to appear as a droid that would be willing to reveal where the Huttlet was,<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> 4-A7 showed respect toward Skywalker, calling him mighty and a friend. When the Jedi Knight asked where Rotta was, RA-7 was willing to answer and even warned him that the dungeons were dangerous. 4-A7 also claimed to confuse Tano for a servant girl and, after she ignited her lightsaber, offered to give a thousand apologies.<ref name="The Clone Wars" />

[[File:4-A7andVentress.png|thumb|right|300px|4-A7's role as a caretaker was an act as his true loyalty was with the CIS and his superior, Asajj Ventress.]]
Having information that could incriminate the Republic in Rotta's kidnapping was a vital part of the Separatist plan on Teth, with Dooku even stopping Ventress from killing Skywalker and Tano, as he still needed the data. 4-A7 fulfilled that part of the plan by recording the Jedi<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> with his built-in holocam and using his suite of editing software.<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> He was later surprised to hear Tano's voice again and, after calling her "young one" and correcting himself, attempted to keep up his caretaker masquerade. After his true loyalty was revealed, Tano accused him of [[treason]], but 4-A7 raised his voice and showcased that he was willing to kill his enemies as he told the B1 battle droids to open fire. His show of force was short-lived, however, as he then attempted to flee before being confronted by Tano. He showed defiance in his last moments, ordering Tano to not kill him, which his severed head repeatedly uttered as it fell.<ref name="The Clone Wars" />

[[File:JAT.jpg|thumb|left|250px|4-A7 was voiced by James Arnold Taylor in ''The Clone Wars'' film.]]
In the film's DVD audio commentary, editor [[Jason Tucker]] recalled that the scene involving 4-A7's recording of Rotta had been difficult to finish as a number of the crew had been putting forth ideas on how to set it, prompting Director [[Dave Filoni]] to state that 4-A7 was originally meant to be hiding behind pillars. When executive producer<ref name="SW:TCW Film DVD Commentary" /> and ''Star Wars'' creator<ref name="George Lucas to be honored">{{SW|url=news/george-lucas-to-be-honored-as-a-disney-legend-during-d23-expo-in-anaheim|text=George Lucas to Be Honored as a Disney Legend During D23 EXPO in Anaheim}}</ref> [[George Lucas]] wanted Ventress and 4-A7 to be on a balcony, the crew needed to design one for the scene. Later, Filoni recalled that 4-A7's death scene had been added in later on as they had been disappointed that his fate was not revealed. He described the protocol droid as being slightly snarky in the scene, and Producer [[Catherine Winder]] revealed that Tano's last line to 4-A7, "Why, you tin-plated traitor!," was what they called an "Ahsoka-ism." These were pushed by Lucas and were when Tano, while in the middle of a serious battle and being earnest, would use what Winder described as "pretty funny nicknames" against her enemy. Winder stated that Tano had come up with these nicknames herself.<ref name="SW:TCW Film DVD Commentary" />

Animation models used for the RA-7 series in various ''The Clone Wars'' episodes<ref name="Downfall of a Droid">{{TCW|Downfall of a Droid}}</ref><ref name="Mystery Moons">{{TCW|Mystery of a Thousand Moons}}</ref><ref name="Lethal Trackdown">{{TCW|Lethal Trackdown}}</ref><ref name="Nomad">{{TCW|Nomad Droids}}</ref> look very similar to 4-A7's model.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> In fact, the colorization of a model seen in the<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" /> [[The Clone Wars: Season One|Season One]] episode<ref name="Downfall of a Droid guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/downfall-of-a-droid-episode-guide|text="Downfall of a Droid" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> "[[Downfall of a Droid]]" and [[Unidentified RA-7 protocol droid (Coruscant)|one later seen]] in<ref name="EP">{{TCW|Evil Plans}}</ref> the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|Season Three]] episode<ref name="Evil Plans guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/evil-plans-episode-guide|text="Evil Plans" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> "[[Evil Plans]]" are<ref name="EP" /> also very similar to 4-A7's own.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> For the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|Season Five]] episode "[[The Jedi Who Knew Too Much]]," 4-A7's animation model was updated to better match the RA-7 units seen in<ref name="Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-jedi-who-knew-too-much-concept-art-gallery|text="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" Concept Art Gallery}}</ref> the first ''Star Wars'' film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], from [[1977]].<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> This updated model was used for the droid [[R-A7]] in "The Jedi Who Knew Too Much." R-A7's character illustration was done by [[Chris Glenn]] and is dated to [[November]] [[2011]] under the title "4A7 Droid asset."<ref name="Concept Art Gallery" />

===Errors and contradictions===
[[File:4A7-BYOR2D2-11.png|thumb|right|250px|4-A7 has been the subject of several contradictions despite his limited appearances in ''Star Wars'' material.]]
Even though he did not appear in the episode, 4-A7's name was listed in the credits of the ''The Clone Wars'' Season One episode "[[Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence'']],"<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> which first aired on [[October 3]], 2008.<ref name="Rising Original Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode002.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> The [[2017]] [[Droid Directory]] for the [[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2 11|eleventh issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2]]'' magazine and the [[2022]] reference booklet "[[Assassin, Security, and Other Droids of War]]" later stated that 4-A7's role in the Separatist plan for Rotta was framing the Jedi in the Huttlet's kidnapping and murder.<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /><ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> In the film, 4-A7 only records evidence for the former lie, for the Confederacy intended to "rescue" Rotta from the Republic, and Dooku only planned to kill Rotta after the Jedi escaped Teth, by which point 4-A7 had been killed.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> As such, this article follows the film's version of events. "Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" and the [[2021]] reference booklet "[[Boba Fett and Other Characters of the Underworld]]" also incorrectly refers to 4-A7 by the name "4A-7,"<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /><ref name="Boba Fett and Other Bounty Hunters and Criminals" /> which was the name given to the character in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity.<ref name="TCW novel" />

"Boba Fett and Other Bounty Hunters and Criminals" also claims 4-A7 began to broadcast footage that made it seem like the Jedi were actively kidnapping the Huttlet,<ref name="Boba Fett and Other Bounty Hunters and Criminals" /> when the film instead establishes his footage was used to claim the Jedi had already completed the kidnapping.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> The [[Highlights of the Saga]] department of the ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]'' magazine's [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 45|forty-fifth issue]],<ref name="45 Saga Highlights" /> which was released on [[January 26]], [[2016]],<ref name="France">{{HCdatesFR|45}}</ref> and written by [[Iain Lowson]],<ref name="HC-Credits">{{EmailCite|email=File:HCSV-Email-2025-07-26.png|author=[[Iain Lowson]]|date=2025-07-26}}</ref> stated that 4-A7 recorded and edited footage that made it look like the Jedi were holding and planning to kill Rotta while Ventress launched a droid counterattack.<ref name="45 Saga Highlights" /> However, the film depicts Ventress standing with 4-A7 while he records the video and her launching the counterattack afterward.<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> This article follows the movie's version of events. Later, the 2021 reference booklet "[[The Clone Wars Begin]]" stated that Ventress was alone when she hid from the Republic to film and edit the footage<ref name="TBotCW" /> despite the film and prior sources clearly establishing 4-A7's role in the events.<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /><ref name="The Clone Wars" /><ref name="45 Saga Highlights" /><ref name="Ventress biography gallery Image 8" />

"Assassin, Security, and Other Droids of War" also claims that 4-A7 was one of the first prototypes of the RA-7 protocol droid line.<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> Based on timeline information provided by ''Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2'' 11 and ''Star Wars: Timelines'', 4-A7's creation date can be narrowed down to 22 BBY,<ref name="Birth and death date note" /> suggesting that the RA-7 was still new in that time if "Assassin, Security, and Other Droids of War" is correct.<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> However, the ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (television series)|Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi]]'' episode "[[Justice (episode)|Justice]]"<ref name="Justice">{{TOTJ|Justice (episode)}}</ref>&mdash;which first premiered on [[October 26]], [[2022]]<ref name="Getty">{{Getty|url=thewaltdisneystudios/allaccess/#!asset/s3m8knpft46xsk99r7kxs|text=TALES OF THE JEDI Project Profile 10.17.22|date=2022-10-10}}</ref>&mdash;features an RA-7 unit active<ref name="Justice" /> several [[Standard decade|decades]] before 22 BBY.<ref name="Justice Date">"[[Justice (episode)|Justice]]" takes place after [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] became [[Dooku]]'s [[padawan]]. According to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', Jinn becomes Dooku's Padawan in [[68 BBY]] and becomes a [[Jedi Knight]] around [[58 BBY]]. Therefore, "Justice" must take place between 68 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and 58 BBY, several decades before [[22 BBY]].</ref> Therefore, this article assumes "Assassin, Security, and Other Droids of War" to be incorrect.
