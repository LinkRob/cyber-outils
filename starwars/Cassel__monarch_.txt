'''Cassel''' was a [[Pantoran]] [[Gender|male]] who served as the [[Monarch of E'ronoh|Monarch]] of the [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He was often seen as unintelligent. Along with [[Thandeka]], the [[queen]] consort of E'ronoh's twin planet, [[Eiram]], Cassel was kidnapped by the [[Directorate]] in a [[Eiram–E'ronoh crisis|plot]] to destabilize the governments of the area. When [[Lord]] [[Isamer]], a Directorate leader, became angered at his [[lieutenant]]s after discovering they had kidnapped Thandeka over the true queen of Eiram, [[Dima]], Cassel spoke up, claiming Dima would still wish for her consort's return. Thandeka accused Cassel of attempting to ally with their kidnappers, and Cassel claimed to be arguing against Isamer's view of Thandeka being useless.<ref name="Into the Dark" />

When the Jedi came to save both hostages from their captors, Isamer managed to point his blaster at [[Thandeka]], but before he could shoot and kill her Cassel threw himself into the line of fire, saving Thandeka's life but dying from the blaster wound.<ref name="Into the Dark" />
