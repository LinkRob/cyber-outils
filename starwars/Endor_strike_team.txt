During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the '''Endor strike team''' was a team led by [[General]] [[Han Solo]] that was given the task of going to the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]] to [[Battle of Endor|destroy]] the [[Death Star's shield generator|shield generator]] that was protecting the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]].<ref name="Episode VI" />

==History==
{{Quote|Take the squad ahead. We'll meet at the shield generator at 0300.|General Han Solo|audio=SoloDerlin.flac|<ref name="Episode VI" />}}
[[File:EndorStrikeTeam-SWBC39.png|thumb|left|250px|Han Solo leads the team from their landing site.]]
[[Major]] [[Lokmarcha]] was originally going to lead the team,<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> but he was [[Death|killed]] during [[Operation Yellow Moon]].<ref name="Moving Target">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref> Following the [[Rescue of Han Solo|rescue]] of [[Captain]] [[Han Solo]] from [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] on [[Tatooine]], Solo was given the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[general]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]<ref name="Episode VI" /> and replaced Lokmarcha as the team's leader.<ref name="Lost Stars" /> After a briefing aboard ''[[Home One]]'', [[Chewbacca]], along with [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], and [[Commander]] [[Luke Skywalker]] joined the strike team. Twelve [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|Pathfinder]] [[commando]]s were part of the team, including [[Nik Sant]],<ref name="Episode VI" /> [[Kes Dameron]], and [[Tuck Tyrell]].<ref name="SE 1" /> [[Lieutenant]] [[Caluan Ematt]] and [[Rex]] joined as well, along with [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]].<ref name="Episode VI" />

Using the the stolen [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[shuttle]] ''[[Tydirium]]'', the strike team landed on the forest moon. The team split when Solo was accidentally spotted by some [[scout trooper]]s who fled on [[Speeder]] bikes to call for backup, causing Skywalker and Organa to give chase. Eventually, Organa was separated from Skywalker and found by an [[Ewok]], [[Wicket Wystri Warrick]], who brought her to his tribe, the [[Bright Tree Village]]. Skywalker regrouped with Solo, Chewbacca, and the droids, who were looking for Organa and still split from the rest of the group. The Rebels were soon captured by the same Ewok tribe that found Leia. Believing C-3PO to be a god, the Ewoks tied up Skywalker, Solo, Chewbacca, and R2-D2, taking them to their village for a sacrificial dinner in C-3PO's honor. Eventually, after Skywalker demonstrated his powers with [[The Force]], the Ewoks released the Rebels and agreed to help them in their battle against the Imperials on the planet. Skywalker, deciding to confront his father, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], left the group and allowed himself to be captured and brought to Vader, who took him to his master, [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="Episode VI" />

[[File:RebelsFightAtShieldGenerator.png|thumb|left|250px|The Rebels fighting off Imperials in front of the shield generator bunker.]]
The next day, Solo, Organa, Chewbacca, and the droids regrouped with the rest of the team and made their way to a back entrance of the shield generator bunker. When an Ewok, [[Paploo]], stole a speeder bike, all of the scout troopers guarding the entrance gave chase, but one. The trooper was captured by the Rebels, with Sant taking his armor and resuming the trooper's position in front of the entrance. The team entered the bunker and quickly captured the control room, however, in a trap set by Emperor Palpatine, Imperial reinforcements arrived and quickly captured the Rebels. Suddenly, Ewoks who had been hiding in the nearby brush attacked, resulting in total chaos and giving the Rebels enough time to destroy the shield generator bunker.<ref name="Episode VI" />

The tide of battle began turning in the Rebels' favor. More [[Rebel trooper|Rebel troops]] were sent down to the moon to assist in fighting the remaining Imperials.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> The day after the battle, the surviving team members and some Ewoks, lead by Solo, launched an [[Assault on an Imperial outpost|assault against an Imperial outpost]]. The Rebels were victorious and recovered information about the Empire's plans after Endor.<ref name="SE 1" /> In the wake of that assault, Solo knew it would be himself and his Pathfinders who continued to see action if the Empire could dig in across the Outer Rim.<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref> Indeed, the team continued [[Raid at the Wretch of Tayron|attacking]] Imperial holdouts for the next few weeks.<ref name="SE 1" />
