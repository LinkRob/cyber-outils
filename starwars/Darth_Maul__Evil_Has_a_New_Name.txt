"'''Darth Maul: Evil Has a New Name'''" is an interview with [[Ray Park]], the actor who physically portrayed [[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]] for [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. It was conducted by [[Scott Chernoff]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 42|''Star Wars Insider'' 42]].
