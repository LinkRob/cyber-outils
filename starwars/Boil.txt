'''Boil''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] as a member of [[Ghost Company]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Boil and his fellow [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]] "[[Waxer]]" were considered two of the best [[soldier]]s in Ghost [[Company]].

==Biography==
===Clone Wars===
[[File:BoilKillsB1-TCWBattleTales3.png|thumb|left|135px|Boil decapitates a [[B1-series battle droid|B1 battle droid]] during the siege of Hisseen.]]
The [[clone trooper]] known as Boil served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. He served with the [[212th Attack Battalion]]'s [[Ghost Company]]&mdash;led by [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]]. He developed a close friendship with a fellow [[trooper]], [[Waxer]].<ref name="Innocents" /> Early in the war, Boil was part of [[Waxer's squad|squad]] led by Waxer during a [[Mission on Krystar|mission]] to investigate whether [[Cloning|clone]] POWs were being kept on [[Krystar]]. He also took part in the [[siege of Hisseen]] and helped rescue the [[Hisseen's parliament|Hisseenian parliament]] from [[battle droid]]s.<ref name="Battle Tales 3">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 3|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 3]]</ref>

Later in the war, Ghost Company was sent to the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] to [[Battle of Ryloth|liberate]] the [[Twi'lek]] [[prisoner]]s from the [[Separatist Droid Army]]. While [[scout]]ing the abandoned [[city]] of [[Nabat]], Boil and Waxer came across the young orphaned Twi'lek [[Youngling|child]] [[Numa]], who led them to discover that the rest of the city's citizens were being held prisoner by Separatist [[battle droid]]s in the city's center. Under [[General]] Kenobi's leadership, Boil and the other [[soldier]]s of Ghost [[Company]] succeeded in freeing the Twi'leks while also destroying the Separatists' [[proton cannon]]s.<ref name="Innocents" />

[[File:Boil-SWCTRankFile.png|thumb|right|200px|Boil in his Phase II armor with a blaster]]
Boil and Waxer continued to serve together following the liberation of Ryloth; during the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], the two clones were ordered by [[Commander]] Cody to rescue General Kenobi after his [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|Republic gunship]] was shot down by Separatist forces. They successfully completed their mission, avoiding enemy fire and retrieving the [[Jedi]] General along with another survivor, the clone trooper known as "[[Trapper (clone trooper)|Trapper]]."<ref name="Landing at Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref> Boil also saw action in the [[Battle on Kiros]] while still serving in Kenobi's 212th Attack [[Battalion]].<ref name="Kidnapped">{{TCW|Kidnapped}}</ref>

Near the end of the war, Boil joined clone troopers [[Threepwood]] and [[Tracker]] as they accompanied Kenobi and [[Anakin Skywalker]] in a [[Second battle of Christophsis|search]] for [[Quinlan Vos]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]], and [[Asajj Ventress]] on the planet [[Christophsis]]. After inspecting the crash site of the fugitives' [[Starship|ship]] [[Banshee (starship)|''Banshee'']], the clones and the Jedi determined that the fugitives were still alive but at least one of them was wounded. Shortly after, they attacked the Separatist [[Christophsis Separatist tower|base]] on the planet.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>

===Legacy===
[[Standard year|Years]] later, Numa&mdash;now a resistance fighter in the [[Free Ryloth Movement]]&mdash;wore a piece of [[armor]] on her left [[Limb|arm]] with the word "Boil" written in [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="Homecoming">{{Rebels|Homecoming}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
[[File:Boil-SWL107.png|thumb|left|250px|Boil came to care for and protected Numa.]]
Early in the Battle of Ryloth, Boil called the planet's Twi'leks 'tail-heads.' During the conflict, he befriended fellow clone trooper Waxer. Boil soon helped and cared for the displaced Twi'lek girl Numa, after he was persuaded by Waxer to do likewise. He was easygoing and friendly to the girl as they continued to watch over her during the battle for Nabat. After providing assistance in finding Numa's parents during the Battle of Ryloth,<ref name="Innocents" /> Boil added a drawing of Numa to his helmet, which he kept for the remainder of the war as a reminder of the little girl.<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" /> 

Like all clone troopers, Waxer stood at 1.83 [[meter]]s (or 6 feet) tall.<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" /> He had a goatee, brown eyes and tan skin.<ref name="Innocents" />

==Skills and abilities==
Boil was proficient in both armed and unarmed combat. Alongside fellow clone trooper Waxer whom he befriended on Ryloth, Boil was known for his skills in infiltration and reconnaissance, which proved vital in freeing Ryloth from the Separatists led by [[Wat Tambor]].<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" />

==Equipment==
[[File:BoilARF-SWE.png|thumb|right|250px|Boil in his desert-camouflage ARF trooper armor]]
At the Battle of Ryloth, Boil wore [[Phase I clone trooper armor]], which had custom markings painted in the orange of the 212th Attack Battalion.<ref name="Innocents" /> At the Second Battle of Geonosis, he wore [[Advanced Recon Force trooper|Advanced Recon Force Scout Trooper]] armor that was painted dark brown and orange to help him camouflage in the [[desert]]s of the planet. He also painted a small depiction of Numa on the right side of his helmet.<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" /> By the time of the Battle of Kiros, he wore [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] that retained some of his custom markings.<ref name="Kidnapped" />

Boil made use of the standard issue [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] during the war.<ref name="Innocents" />

==Behind the scenes==
The character of Boil was created for the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series and first appeared in the series' [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] episode "[[Innocents of Ryloth]],"<ref name="Innocents" /> first aired on [[March 6]], [[2009]]. Like all clones in the show, Boil was voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]].<ref name="Innocents Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode020.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Innocents of Ryloth}}</ref>
