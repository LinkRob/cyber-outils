'''Barquet''' was a [[Dressellian]] who served as a [[director]] in the [[Galactic Republic]]. Barquet investigated about the connection of the [[Nihil]] and [[Arathab Fal]]. After [[Keefar Branto]] was killed, Barquet hired [[Jedi Master]] [[Emerick Caphtor]] and private agent [[Sian Holt]] to find an [[Unidentified Galactic Republic agent|undercover agent]] on the planet [[Vorzyd V]] who found Arathab.<ref name="TOS 2" />
