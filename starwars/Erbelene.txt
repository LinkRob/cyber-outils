'''Erbelene'''<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> was an individual who spoke with [[Raz Fellidrone]] while he posed as the [[portmaster]] of [[Carannia Spaceport]]. Erbelene sent a delivery to Fellidrone, but had difficulties with customs, and Fellidrone set Erbelene to the [[spaceport]]'s fast track.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
