A '''family''' of [[Kage]] was [[Homeworld|from]]<ref name="The Blade 3" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Quarzite]].<ref name="Quarzite" /> By [[407 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The present day events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic — The Blade]]'' take place between the [[wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran]] and [[Porter Engle]] [[Siege of Bardotta|earning]] his title the "Blade of Bardotta," both of which are dated to [[382 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As the flashbacks in [[The High Republic — The Blade 1|''The High Republic — The Blade'' 1]] take place twenty-five [[Standard year|years]] before the present day events, they must take place in [[407 BBY]].</ref> the family included the [[Gender|female]] [[Barash Silvain]],<ref name="The Blade 3" /> her<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Barash Silvain's parents|parents]], and her [[Barash Silvain's siblings|siblings]].<ref name="The Blade 3" /> Also by that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> while an [[Youngling|infant]],<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> Silvain was discovered to be [[Force-sensitive]] and taken to be [[Jedi training|trained]] as a [[Jedi]] of the [[Jedi Order]] at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="The Blade 3" /> the [[Core Worlds]] planet<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> [[Coruscant]]. As she became a Jedi, Silvain continued to remember her family due to her<ref name="The Blade 3" /> [[eidetic memory]]<ref name="Lightsaber Collection">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> and because she had been discovered at an older age than usual.<ref name="The Blade 3" />

In [[397 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">The present day events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic — The Blade]]'' take place between the [[wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran]] and [[Porter Engle]] [[Siege of Bardotta|earning]] his title the "Blade of Bardotta," both of which are dated to [[382 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As the flashbacks in [[The High Republic — The Blade 3|''The High Republic — The Blade'' 3]] take place fifteen [[Standard year|years]] before the present day events, they must take place in [[397 BBY]].</ref> Silvain confided in her close friend&mdash;the [[Ikkrukkian]] Jedi [[Porter Engle]]&mdash;about remembering her family. The Kage informed Engle that she recalled how it felt to be with her family, and although she had no regrets about being brought to the [[Jedi temple|temple]], she remembered what she had lost. Silvain then told Engle that with him she had found family again, and asked him if he would become her brother, which Engle accepted.<ref name="The Blade 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|It's complicated. It's not impossible to open that door and explore it, but doing it here in a four-issue comic series was maybe not the place to dig into it. And because Barash is from a species that remembers her life from birth, she remembers what a family is, and she remembers what she had. And she knows that the bond she has with Porter is the same.|Charles Soule, on dropping the idea of Barash Silvain being a biological sibling of Porter Engle|<ref name="Art of The High Republic Volume II">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref>}}
[[File:The-Blade-3-Final-Cover.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Barash Silvain's family was first mentioned in ''The High Republic — The Blade'' 3.]]
Barash Silvain's family was first mentioned in flashbacks in the [[The High Republic — The Blade 3|third issue]] of the [[2022]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic — The Blade]]'' released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="The Blade 3" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Art of The High Republic Volume II" /> The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[March 1]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103906/star_wars_the_high_republic_-_the_blade_2022_3|text=Star Wars: The High Republic - The Blade (2022) #3}}</ref>

During development for ''The Blade'', Barash was originally envisioned as Porter Engle's [[Porter Engle's family|biological sister]] and early concept art of her by artist [[Ario Anindito]] designed her as an Ikkrukkian like Engle. The idea, however, was ultimately dropped as Soule felt a four-issue miniseries was not the place to delve into the idea of the Jedi Order taking two siblings from the same family if the concept had been established within it. Soule then noted that as Silvain was from a [[species]] where she remembers her life from [[Reproduction|birth]], she remembers what family is and what she had, finishing by stating that the Kage knew the bond she had with Engle was the same.<ref name="Art of The High Republic Volume II" />
