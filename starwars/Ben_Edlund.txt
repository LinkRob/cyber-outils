'''Ben Edlund''' (born [[September 20]], [[1960s#1968|1968]]) is a writer of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series episode "[[Grievous Intrigue]]." In addition to creating [[Wikipedia:Tick (comics)|The Tick]], Edlund has also worked on [[W:c:firefly:Firefly|''Firefly'']], [[W:c:buffy:Angel (series)|''Angel'']], [[Wikipedia:Point Pleasant|''Point Pleasant'']], and [[W:c:supernatural:Supernatural|''Supernatural'']].
