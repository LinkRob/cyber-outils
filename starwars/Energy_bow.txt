An '''Energy Bow''' was a type of energy weapon used by multiple cultures throughout the galaxy as an alternative to a [[Blaster]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:Nightsister-SWCT.png|thumb|left|250px|A Nightsister wields an energy bow]]
Consisting of a carbon-alloy [[bow]] with a plasma coupler string - a variant technology to the [[Energy binder]]s that tie [[Podracer]] engines together - the energy bow was a modern analog to its more ancient counterpart. When the drawstring was pulled back, usually by means of a small finger grip, plasma was drawn out of a plasma reservoir in the bow's grip to create an "arrow" of concentrated plasma; drawing the bow required strength, sufficient enough that a child would have difficulty drawing it back.<ref name="G&G"/><ref name="Rampage">{{TBB|Rampage (episode)}}</ref> This drawstring drew power independently of the bow's plasma reserves - when all arrows were spent, the string was often used to cut and electrocute, making the bow a handy backup melee weapon.<ref name="G&G"/>

While slower firing and seemingly less efficient than modern blasters, the energy bow held two distinct advantages over such weapons. The first was its exceptional armor-piercing ability; the concentrated plasma arrows were capable of penetrating all but the thickest armor platings, and was capable of exceeding even a heavy rifle in terms of power.<ref name="G&G"/> The second was its pinpoint accuracy - with virtually no bolt deviation, the accuracy of the energy bow was entirely up to the skill of the wielder.<ref name="G&G"/>

Like a [[lightsaber]], a user who lets the force guide each plasma arrow can be extraordinarily lethal, landing miraculous shots that can bring down even the toughest targets with ease.<ref name="G&G"/>

==History==
[[File:OmegaPracticesWithBow-Decommissioned.png|thumb|right|300px|[[Omega]] uses a [[Zygerrian energy bow]]]]
The [[bounty hunter]] [[Shalla Mondatha]] was known to wield one such weapon.<ref name="Steal">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Steal]]''</ref> [[Nightsister energy bow|Those]] built by the [[Nightsisters]] of [[Dathomir]] were [[plasma]]-based.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> The soldier [[Jannah]] used a [[Jannah's energy bow|variant]] of the energy bow that imparted an energetic charge to a physical arrow during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars NE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Zygerrian]] [[enslaver]]s on [[Ord Mantell]] also utilized [[Zygerrian energy bow|energy bows]], which later [[Omega]] took as her primary [[Omega's Zygerrian energy bow|weapon]].<ref name="Rampage"/>
