The '''BT X-42 heavy flame projector''',<ref name="Rise" /> or known simply as the '''clone flamethrower''',<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode205.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Landing at Point Rain}}</ref> was a model of [[flamethrower]] used by the specially trained [[clone flametrooper]]s during the [[Clone Wars]]. Clone flametroopers were deployed at the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]] to use this weapon against the native [[Geonosian]]s, which were unarmored and therefore particularly susceptible to the flamethrower.<ref name="Rise" /> After the end of the war and the ensuing [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], clone [[stormtrooper]]s continued to use the weapon. One was utilized by "[[Wyler]]" during a [[Mission to Desix|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Desix]], which was also wielded by another clone stormtrooper from [[Team two]].<ref name="The Solitary Clone" /> In addition, one was also wielded as [[ES-04]]'s primary weapon in the [[Elite Squad]], as well as [[Fireball (clone trooper)|Fireball]] of the [[Clone Underground]], in addition to [[TK stormtrooper]]s during the [[Raid on Pabu|mission]] to recover [[Omega]] from [[Pabu]] and get her back to [[Tantiss Base]] for [[Project Necromancer]].<ref name="Point of No Return">{{TBB|Point of No Return (The Bad Batch)}}</ref>
