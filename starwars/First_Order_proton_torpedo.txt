The '''First Order proton torpedo''' was a model of [[proton torpedo]] manufactured for the [[First Order]]. It was fired from a prototype launcher installed on the [[TIE/vn space superiority fighter]].<ref name="TFOWM" />

==Behind the scenes==
First Order proton torpedoes were first identified in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[TIE Fighter Owners' Workshop Manual]]'', written by [[Ryder Windham]].<ref name="TFOWM" />
