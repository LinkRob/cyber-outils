'''Bothawui''' was an [[Fervse'dra asteroid belt|asteroid field]]&ndash;[[Planetary ring|ringed]]<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" /> [[gas giant]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> located in the [[Bothawui system]] in [[Bothan Space]], an autonomous region of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Bothawui was considered to be strategically important during the [[Clone Wars]], leading to it being the site of a [[Battle of Bothawui|battle]] between [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[General]] [[Grievous]] that halted a [[Skytop Station campaign|Separatist advance]].<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" /> Later in the war, around the time the [[Galactic Republic]] suffered a loss on [[Ryloth]], Bothawui hosted another battle, though this engagement ended with many Republic losses.<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>

Several of the individuals who taught the [[human]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] to fly [[starship]]s originated on this world, despite the great distance between the world and the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]] where Tarkin lived. Many [[Standard year|years]] later, [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] resources were redirected from Bothawui by the [[Imperial Navy]] to help deal with Tarkin's [[corvette]], ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', after it was [[Mission to Murkhana|stolen]] by [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|dissidents]].<ref name="Tarkin" />
