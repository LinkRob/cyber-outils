The '''Ansata system''' was located in the [[Core Worlds]] in grid square M-11 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Ansata pattern]]s and [[Ansata hair stick]]s|system=Ansata [[Star system|system]]}}</ref> [[Ansata pattern]]s and [[Ansata hair stick]]s.<ref name="CVDNE">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Ansata [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Ansata system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via a mention in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
