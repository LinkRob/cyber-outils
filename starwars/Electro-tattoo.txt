An '''electro-tattoo''' was a type of [[tattoo]] whose ink was capable of carrying data. The [[outlaw tech]] and tattoo [[artist]] [[Naley Frifa]] was adept at inking both gang symbols and electro-tattoos.<ref name="SoloOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] had electro-tattoos on her right arm. She described her tattoos as "intriguing" and refused to answer whether they were a sign of being a daredevil or a "youthful mistake."<ref name="Doctor Aphra audiobook">''[[Doctor Aphra: An Audiobook Original]]''</ref> She eventually came to question if others still got the tattoos.<ref name="Aphra 14" /> Aphra was able to plant [[databomb]]s in her electro-tattoos.<ref name="Grand Imperial Jubilee">{{StoryCite|story=The Grand Imperial Jubilee|book=A New Legacy 1}}</ref>
