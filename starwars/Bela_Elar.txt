'''Bela Elar''' was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]] [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War|war]] against the [[Galactic Empire]], and flew with [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]].

==Biography==
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Bela Elar flew with [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]] under the [[callsign]] [[Red Four]], and fought in a [[Skirmish in the Kiax Nebula|battle]] in the [[Kiax Nebula]] some time after the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Choose Your Destiny: A Luke & Leia Adventure" />

Shortly after the [[Battle of Hoth|evacuation of Hoth]] in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="RPDate">"[[Rendezvous Point (short story)|Rendezvous Point]]" is set in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Hoth]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]]. As such, the events in "Rendezvous Point" must have taken place around that year.</ref> Elar was flying a [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]] when her [[alluvial damper]]s were blown out and had to go extravehicular to fix them in deep space. [[Commander]] [[Wedge Antilles]] and [[Wes Janson]] recruited Elar back into the newly reformed Red Squadron and given the responsibilities of her own flight. She flew a [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]] as flight leader in an [[Attack on an asteroid mining station|attack]] on a nearby [[pirate]] base, that threatened the safety of the Alliance's rendezvous point. Her wingmen during the attack were [[Grizz Frix]] and [[Penn Zowlie]], and her flight's job was to run interference, covering Janson's flight, who would hit targets on the landing field. Zowlie was killed during the battle, but the new squadron succeeded in eliminating the pirates.<ref name="Rendezvous Point" />

==Personality and traits==
Elar was a respected pilot and was one of the first picks for the new Red Squadron. After Antilles recounted the story of her fixing her alluvial dampers in deep space, Janson was impressed and regarded her as a top pilot as well as their squadron's engineering department.<ref name="Rendezvous Point" />
