An '''everblue''' was a type of [[plant]] with branches that was found on the [[planet]] [[Dalna]].<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> a variety of decorations, including bouquets of everblue branches, were placed in the central [[garden]] courtyard of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult's]] [[Path compound|compound]] on Dalna to honor the [[Reproduction|hatching]] of the [[Kessarine]] [[Ferize]]'s [[Youngling|children]] and to welcome new [[refugee]]s.<ref name="Path of Deceit" />

==Behind the scenes==
Everblue branches appeared in the [[2022]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and [[Tessa Gratton]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase II">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> Within the novel, the branches appeared in a chapter written by Gratton.<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref>
