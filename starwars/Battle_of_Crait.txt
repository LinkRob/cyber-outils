The '''Battle of Crait''', also known as the '''siege of Crait''', or the '''assault on Crait''', was a major battle fought in [[34 ABY]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. Shortly after the Resistance's [[Battle of Starkiller Base|victory]] at [[Starkiller Base]], the Battle of [[Crait]] was the result of the First Order's [[Battle of Oetchi|counterattack]], which had decimated the Resistance forces. Unable to escape the [[First Order Navy]], the Resistance, led by [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], fled to a former [[Crait outpost|Rebel outpost]] on the [[salt]]-covered [[planet]] Crait, hoping to go unnoticed. However, the First Order became aware of the Resistance's plan and deployed their [[First Order military|military forces]] to the planet's surface.

Led by the newly self-appointed [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] and General [[Armitage Hux]], the First Order deployed a [[superlaser siege cannon]], planning to break through the massive door which protected the outpost. The Resistance, limited in numbers, had planned to hold out in the base until more allies arrived, but in response to the First Order [[cannon]], the remaining Resistance [[troop]]s utilized [[V-4X-D ski speeder]]s to attack the weapon. The Resistance [[airspeeder]]s faced many losses against the [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s of the First Order, but when the [[light freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' arrived to help the Resistance, Ren redirected the TIE [[Starfighter|fighters]] to pursue the [[starship]]. Although the Resistance ski [[speeder]]s were no longer targeted by the TIE fighters, their efforts were unsuccessful, and the siege cannon fired upon the outpost.

With the base vulnerable and no reinforcements responding to their call, the Resistance lost hope, until Organa's brother, [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]], appeared before them as a [[Force projection]] through [[the Force]]. Buying [[time]] for the Resistance to escape, Skywalker exited the outpost to face Ren in a [[lightsaber duel]]. As Skywalker confronted Ren, [[Captain]] [[Poe Dameron]] led the Resistance through the [[cave]]s of Crait, hoping to find a natural exit. When Ren realized he was fighting Skywalker's projection, the apparition disappeared, and Ren led his [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] into the base. The First Order stormed the outpost, but with Skywalker having distracted the First Order for an ample amount of time, the Resistance survivors escaped with [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and [[Chewbacca]] aboard the ''Millennium Falcon'', inspiring rebellion across [[the galaxy]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Get all our forces down to that Resistance base. Let's finish this.|Supreme Leader Kylo Ren, to General Hux|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:Long_Live_The_Supreme_Leader.png|thumb|left|255px|Ren proclaimed himself as the new Supreme Leader after the death of Snoke.]]
Following the [[Resistance]]'s [[Battle of Starkiller Base|victory]] in destroying [[Starkiller Base]],<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> the Resistance [[Battle of D'Qar|abandoned]] their [[Resistance base|base]] on [[D'Qar]] after the [[First Order]] invaded the [[Ileenium system]]. While the Resistance was searching [[the galaxy]] for a new base of operations, [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] [[Hyperspace tracker|tracked]] the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]] through [[hyperspace]] and gave [[Battle of Oetchi|chase]]. [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] eventually [[Holdo maneuver|sacrificed herself]] and her [[cruiser]] to allow the remaining Resistance [[Soldier|fighters]] to escape to the [[planet]] [[Crait]] in the [[Crait system]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

After Holdo's suicide run, Hux went to the [[Snoke's throne room|throne room]] to report to and check on [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]]. Upon reaching it, however, he found the place in shambles, littered with the bodies of the [[Praetorian Guard|Elite Praetorian Guard]], who were killed by Kylo Ren and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]; Snoke's dismembered corpse was near the throne and [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], unconscious in the wreckage. Hux tried to take advantage of the situation to kill his rival, but Kylo recovered before he could act. After he expressed disbelief at seeing Snoke's dead body, Kylo told him that Rey killed Snoke, proclaimed himself as the new Supreme Leader and ordered the First Order to move to Crait, to crush the Resistance once and for all.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==The battle==
===Arrival at the base===
{{Quote|They're coming, shut the door.|Leia Organa|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:Leia_on_Crait.png|thumb|right|300px|Organa overlooks the landscape of Crait.]]
The remaining members of the Resistance landed and reactivated the old Rebel base. [[Finn]], [[Rose Tico]], and [[BB-8]] later crash-landed at the base in a [[Xi-class light shuttle|''Xi''-class light shuttle]] after escaping the damaged First Order [[flagship]] ''[[Supremacy]]''. They were initially shot at by the [[Resistance trooper|Resistance soldiers]] in the confusion, but were unharmed. General [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] called upon their allies across the [[Outer Rim Territories]] for reinforcements as the group prepared the base's defenses, including several [[laser turret]]s and [[V-4X-D ski speeder]]s. Meanwhile, the First Order&mdash;now under the command of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]&mdash;deployed their own forces, landing eleven [[All Terrain MegaCaliber Six|AT-M6s]], two [[First Order All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-ATs]], one [[Upsilon-class command shuttle|''Upsilon''-class command shuttle]], several [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] and [[First Order All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-STs]], and a [[superlaser siege cannon]] towed by a pair of [[All Terrain Heavy Hauler|AT-HHs]] to break through the base's massive [[steel]] door.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> The [[walker]]s were deployed in a [[Veers Formation]]<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and the forces mobilized.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===The battle begins===
{{Quote|All right, listen up. I don't like these rust buckets and I don't like our odds, but...just keep it tight and don't get drawn in too close till they roll that cannon out front.|Captain Poe Dameron, to "Reb" Squadron|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:FOForcesBattleofCrait-TLJOCE.png|thumb|left|300px|The First Order launched overwhelming forces against the Resistance.]]
The Resistance launched all of their remaining forces, firing heavy [[artillery]] and dispatching thirteen of the old V-4X-D ski [[speeder]]s to knock out the [[superlaser]], led by Captain [[Poe Dameron]]. The First Order walkers and TIE fighters returned fire and the battle began. The Resistance suffered heavy losses from the combined ground and air assaults.

Having no other option, flew the speeders towards the First Order's battering ram cannon in order to prevent them from entering the base and destroying the last of the Resistance. The majority of the speeders were destroyed by [[All Terrain MegaCaliber Six|AT-M6]] fire. As the battering ram prepared to fire, Dameron ordered the squadron to retreat and fall back to the base, Finn refused to retreat with them, instead deciding to fly his speeder into the cannon in an attempt to destroy it. Rose crashed her speeder into his in order to stop him from doing so and save his life, though she badly injured herself in the process.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Arrival of the ''Millennium Falcon''===
{{Quote|Blow that piece of junk OUT OF THE SKY!!!|Kylo Ren|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:ChewbaccaScavengedYT1300-SWZ.png|thumb|right|300px|The ''Millennium Falcon'' led the TIE fighters into caverns under Crait's surface.]]
After the Resistance failed their counter-attack, the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', [[pilot]]ed by [[Chewbacca]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] arrived unexpectedly mid battle and attacked the TIE fighters overhead, drawing them away from the battlefield and ultimately destroying all of them as the Resistance was taking heavy losses from the First Order's assault. With Rey manning the Falcon's lower [[AG-2G quad laser cannon]], Chewbacca was able to lure the attacking TIE Fighters away from the battle through the planet's crystallized caverns, where the freighter lost its [[Rectenna|sensor dish]] and lower quad laser cannon during the chase.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Despite Rey's successful diversion of the First Order's air support, much to the annoyance of the Supreme Leader, the Resistance was still losing and ultimately being pushed back by the First Order's sheer firepower as the First drew ever closer to the base.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===First Order advances===
{{Quote|I held out hope for so long, but I know my son is gone.''"<br />"'' No one's ever really gone|Leia Organa and Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
With the Resistance's effort nearly silenced, the First Order's cannon opened fire at the base, punching easily through the main doors. Supreme Leader Ren ordered General Hux to advance and to give no quarter to his enemies or take any prisoners. As [[First Order snowtrooper|snowtroopers]] began approaching the breach, the Resistance realized that all of the messages sent to their allies had been received, but none had responded. The [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction of the Hosnian system]] had achieved what the [[destruction of Alderaan]] and the [[Tarkin Doctrine]] had failed to achieve in making the galaxy too scared to fight the First Order. Greatly saddened, Organa gave up all hope and said the spark was out. In a last-ditch effort, the Resistance began preparing for a final stand.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:Luke_and_Leia_one_last_time.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Luke and Leia reunited for the last time.]]
As the Resistance contemplated their next move, [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] unexpectedly appeared to the group, with Skywalker greeting his sister for the first time in years. He then gave her Solo's [[Han Solo's dice|gold dice]] to remember him by. Reconciling his failure to stop Ren and for not being there to stop Solo's death, the last [[Jedi]] then resolved to go out to face the First Order on his own as a distraction and to buy the Resistance time to escape. Before Skywalker went to confront his nephew, the Jedi Master wordlessly greeted his old friend [[C-3PO]] as he proceeded towards his destiny.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Skywalker's intervention and escape===
{{Quote|Did you come back to say you forgive me? To save my soul?''"<br />"''No.|Kylo Ren and Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
As Skywalker emerged from the base, Ren ordered all of the walkers to open fire on the Jedi Master in a futile attempt to destroy him. When the shots failed to affect Skywalker at first, Ren commanded the gunners to intensify their attacks to the maximum possible firepower. However, the [[Jedi]] somehow miraculously emerged unscathed despite the First Order's best efforts, and Ren felt the need to go down to the surface and face his old master himself, despite Hux's advice to the contrary. In response, Kylo Ren hurled Hux against a wall with the Force, temporarily knocking him out.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:Family_Reunion.png|thumb|right|300px|Jedi Master Luke Skywalker confronted his former student, Kylo Ren.]]
Skywalker dodged Ren's lightsaber attacks and after evading Ren for a time, Skywalker stopped and apologized to Ren, stating that he had failed him. He also informed his former student that victory was not so certain as he believed; that the war was only beginning, and that the Jedi would continue through Rey. He then deactivated his lightsaber and offered to let Ren strike him down, but Skywalker warned him that doing so in anger would only cause him pain, just as killing Han had, and stated that in death, he would always be with his nephew. When Skywalker allowed Ren to land a blow, and the blade passed through his body without harming him, Ren realized that he was in fact fighting a [[Force projection]] of the Jedi, who was still on [[Ahch-To]]. With that, Skywalker's projection promptly vanished, and Ren was incensed.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Meanwhile, Dameron and C-3PO noticed that the [[Vulptex|vulptices]], creatures which resided in the [[cave]]s, had gone, leaving through a natural exit. Following a remaining vulptex, Dameron led the surviving Resistance members in search of the exit. With the [[time]] bought by Skywalker's distraction, the Resistance found the end of the base's cave system, but saw that a rockslide had blocked the exit to the outside. From the other side of the blockage, Rey used [[the Force]] to lift the rocks and cleared a path for the surviving Resistance fighters to escape to the ''Falcon'', living to fight another [[Standard day|day]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Aftermath==
===The spark remains===
{{Quote|How do we build a rebellion from this?''"<br />"''We have everything we need.|Rey and Leia Organa|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:Luke_fades_into_the_Force.png|thumb|left|250px|After the battle was over, and the Resistance escaped, Luke became one with the Force.]]
The Battle of Crait was a victory for the First Order; the main force of the Resistance was reduced to barely over a dozen personnel aboard only a single vessel. However, the First Order failed in its main objective of completely destroying the Resistance, as some of its members&mdash;including General Organa&mdash;escaped the battle to continue their struggle. Ren also failed to destroy the Jedi Order by murdering Skywalker, who had been projecting his image across the galaxy.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

After the battle, Skywalker, having heavily exerted himself in the effort to buy time for the Resistance, became one with the Force. His passing was felt by Rey and Organa, who noted that he was content and at peace. Rey worried that the Resistance was all but destroyed, but Organa reassured her, saying they had everything they needed to rebuild.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Downtime on the ''Falcon''===
[[File:MillenniumFalconPostCrait-PoeDameron26.png|thumb|233px|The ''Millennium Falcon'' was used by the Resistance to escape Crait.]]
As Chewbacca and [[Nien Nunb]] flew the ''Millennium Falcon'' through hyperspace, Dameron, Rey, and Finn all sat together by the [[dejarik]] board and reflected on recent events, which Dameron stated he wished recent events had gone differently due to the losses the Resistance had suffered, but Rey stated that she was beginning to learn that one could not dwell on changing the past. In an effort to provide a nice distraction from their recent losses, Dameron decided to tell Rey and Finn the background of his [[Attack on Tuanul|original mission]] to [[Jakku]] and how he managed to get off the desert world after he and Finn had [[Escape from the Finalizer|crashed]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 26">[[Poe Dameron 26|''Poe Dameron'' 26]]</ref> Dameron then reflected on the [[Battle of Takodana]] as Organa, who had been reflecting on her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Han Solo]] while serving as the co-pilot on the ''Falcon'' before allowing Nunb to take over, entered the room. After Organa spoke to them about the Force and went to rest, Finn then went to check on how Tico was doing, and Dameron was introduced to the [[Porg]]s by Rey. When Rey asked how [[Maz Kanata|Maz]] was, Dameron assured her that she was fine, as they had spoken to her earlier.<ref name="Poe Dameron 27">[[Poe Dameron 27|''Poe Dameron'' 27]]</ref>

[[File:PoeFinnReyPostCrait-PoeDameron26.png|thumb|220px|Among the remaining Resistance members on the ''Millennium Falcon'' included Poe Dameron, Rey, Finn, and BB-8.|left]]
Rey realized that she had missed a lot because, by the time Resistance troops had landed on Takodana, she had been captured by Kylo Ren. As Dameron had also been captured and tortured by Ren, he coined the nickname "torture buddies" for him and Rey, which she accepted. Dameron then went over the recon mission to Starkiller Base, telling Rey that [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron's]] recon pilots&mdash;[[Temmin Wexley]], [[Karé Kun]], and [[Jessika Pava]]&mdash;had created a mission plan using a [[Unidentified Resistance recon pod|recon pod]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 27">[[Poe Dameron 27|''Poe Dameron'' 27]]</ref> At this time, [[R2-D2]] and BB-8 were also reflecting on recent events, specifically the Battle of Starkiller Base. C-3PO briefly spoke with the two [[astromech droid]]s before going to speak with Rey. Due to 3PO's extensive knowledge of various languages, Rey asked the [[protocol droid]] to help her translate the [[sacred Jedi texts]]. 3PO began to translate the texts, but he noted that he could only give a rough translation as the [[book]]s' wording was ancient. Finn was currently making sure Tico was safe, and Dameron went to talk to his friend.<ref name="Poe Dameron 28">[[Poe Dameron 28|''Poe Dameron'' 28]]</ref> Upon learning Black Squadron was bogged down in the [[Battle of Ikkrukk]],<ref name="Poe Dameron 30" /> which had been raging concurrent to the Battle of Crait,<ref name="Timelines" /> Dameron rushed to their aid and helped deliver a victory.<ref name="Poe Dameron 31">[[Poe Dameron 31|''Poe Dameron'' 31]]</ref>

===Wider reaction===
{{Quote|Luke Skywalker and Leia Organa defeated them on Crait. Did you not know that? There's chatter everywhere.''"<br />"''Propaganda. The First Order destroyed all but a handful of Resistance fighters. They are meaningless. Decimated.|A Naboo and Kylo Ren|<ref name="Legacy of Vader 5">[[Legacy of Vader 5|''Legacy of Vader'' 5]]</ref>}}
Before his death, Skywalker also acknowledged Rey as the last Jedi, giving birth to another new hope for both the Jedi and the galaxy. The story of how Skywalker single-handedly faced the First Order would spread across the galaxy.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Although Skywalker's role gained the most attention, as he faced the enemy alone, his sister, Leia Organa, was also noted, making rumors about the Skywalker twins' participation in the battle something that spread across several worlds.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 5" /> First Order Supreme Leader Kylo Ren knew that rumors of the Resistance's escape could instigate resistance against the First Order, which led him to suppress them<ref name="Legacy of Vader 2">[[Legacy of Vader 2|''Legacy of Vader'' 2]]</ref> and make it seem like they were exaggerated, Resistance propaganda, claiming that their fighters were virtually decimated.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 5" />

The Resistance would eventually rebuild itself, gaining followers on [[Batuu]]<ref name="Black Spire">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref> and [[Ryloth]].<ref name="Resistance Reborn">''[[Resistance Reborn]]''</ref> The war would continue for another [[35 ABY|year]] until the climatic [[Battle of Exegol]]<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> which ended the First Order-Resistance War.<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Crait made its first appearance in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' as a free level download available on [[December 13]], 2017. The battle later appeared in the 2017 film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="Episode VIII" /> The sequences involving the ''Millennium Falcon'' were inspired by the original [[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] ride. As such, director [[Rian Johnson]] was pleased that material from the Battle of Crait was in turn added as a part of ''[[Star Tours: The Adventures Continue]]''.<ref name="breakdown">{{Film|VIII}} special feature - "Scene Breakdown: Showdown on Crait"</ref>
