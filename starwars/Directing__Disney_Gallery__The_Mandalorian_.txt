"'''Directing'''" is the first episode of ''[[Disney Gallery: The Mandalorian]]'', a docuseries on the production of the [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''. The episode focuses on the episodic directors of the show and their experiences working on ''[[Star Wars]]''. The episode aired on [[May 4]], [[2020]] on Disney+.<ref name="D23" />

==Official description==
''Join Jon Favreau and season one's directors as they pull back the curtain on'' The Mandalorian''.''
