'''2023 Topps ''Star Wars'' Chrome''' is a trading card set produced by [[Topps]].<ref name="Release" /> It is the first standalone set released under the Topps Chrome brand that is not tied to a specific film/series or established card line.<ref name="Release" /> The cards were released in hobby and retail boxes.

The 100-card base set features character portraits of various [[Star Wars|''Star Wars'']] characters in live-action and animation. Parallels for the base set include a base refractor (hobby box exclusive), Black Wave refractor (blaster box exclusive), Purple Wave refractor (blaster box exclusive), Aqua refractor (serial numbered to 199), Blue refactor (serial numbered to 150), Green refractor (serial numbered to 99), Gold refractor (serial numbered to 50), Orange refractor (serial numbered to 25), Red refractor (serial numbered to 5), and SuperFractor (serial numbered to 1).<ref name="Release" />
