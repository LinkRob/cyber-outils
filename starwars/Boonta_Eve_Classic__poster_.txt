'''''Boonta Eve Classic''''' was a decorative [[Painting|poster]]. It depicted a trio of [[podracer]]s competing in the [[Boonta Eve Classic]]<ref name="The High Tower" /> race on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]],<ref name="AB">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> with the [[Binary star system|twin suns]]<ref name="The High Tower" /> [[Tatoo I]] and [[Tatoo II]]<ref name="AB" /> in the background. A caption of the poster was written in the [[Aurebesh]] [[writing]] system and featured the work's name.<ref name="The High Tower" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|1|5}}</ref> the [[ace]] [[pilot]] [[Torra Doza]] kept a copy of ''Boonta Eve Classic'' on a wall in her [[Torra's Quarters|room]] in [[Doza Tower]] on the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]''.<ref name="The High Tower" />

==Behind the scenes==
''Boonta Eve Classic'' first appeared in "[[The High Tower]],"<ref name="The High Tower" /> the fifth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' animated television series that premiered on [[October 28]], [[2018]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> ''Star Wars Resistance''{{'s}} art director<ref name="TheHighTower-BLE">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-high-tower-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 9 Fun Facts from "The High Tower" - ''Star Wars Resistance''|date=2018-10-29}}</ref> [[Amy Beth Christenson]] created a concept illustration of the poster dated to [[March 16]], [[2017]].<ref name="ConceptArt">{{SW|url=star-wars-resistance-the-high-tower-concept-art-gallery|text="The High Tower" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> ''Boonta Eve Classic'' was also included in a concept illustration of Torra's quarters done by [[Lucasfilm Animation]] artist [[Aaron Pham]], attributed to the [[March 17|following day]].<ref name="ConceptArt2">{{SW|url=star-wars-resistance-the-high-tower-concept-art-gallery|text="The High Tower" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 7}}</ref>
