The '''Bylaws of Independent Systems''' were a series of laws that were upheld by the [[Separatist Senate|Senate]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. One such law required a voice vote for the deciding vote on the [[Confederate–Republic peace initiative]].<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" />
