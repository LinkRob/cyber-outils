The '''Ankhural cantina''' was located in a [[Unidentified city (Ankhural)|city]] on the [[planet]] [[Ankhural]]. In [[3 ABY]], some personnel from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] gathered across two tables in the [[cantina]] following the [[Howl's funeral|funeral]] of their [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]]. The rebels spoke primarily about the late captain, toasting to him.

==Description==
The Ankhural cantina was an [[Color|orange]]-lit [[cantina]] with tables and an aisle located within a [[Unidentified city (Ankhural)|city]] on the<ref name="BTC" /> [[Hutt Space]]<ref  name="Systems">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Ankhural system]]</ref> [[planet]] of [[Ankhural]]. The cantina offered a steaming red [[wine]] in mugs, which had both sweet and chemical notes.<ref name="BTC" />

==History==
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref> a number of personnel from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] visited the Ankhuralcantina following the [[Howl's funeral|funeral]] of their late [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]]. The rebels occupied two tables on opposite sides of the aisle, both groups toasting to their fallen captain. On one table were the [[Rebel trooper|soldiers]] [[Hazram Namir]], [[Pol Andrissus|Charmer]], [[Gadren]], [[Brand]], [[Twitch]], and the [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] [[Nemenov]], who shared stories about Evon and discussed who he might have been before the war.<ref name="BTC" />

During the chatter in the cantina Namir believed that Howl would die a legend like anyone who died leading an [[army]]. The rest of the table disagreed, leading Namir to reluctantly concede. Later into the evening, the some mugs and a chair at the second table were damaged, and Namir bribed the [[bartender]] into turning a blind [[eye]]. The personnel began leaving the Ankhural cantina in twos and threes after that, leaving only Namir and Gadren left. The pair spoke about Evon's influence over the 61st's goals and what the [[company]] was to do without him.<ref name="BTC" />
