'''Bev Plar''' was a [[Blarina]] [[merchant]], operating on the [[planet]] [[Burnin Konn]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Plar trafficked in specialized goods—including shipments of materials used for crafting [[armor]] and [[Blaster|weapons]]—which the Blarina sold for a variety of [[Currency|currencies]], such as [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], [[chromium]], or accredited requisition scripts.<ref name="Uprising" />

Following the enactment of the so-called "[[Iron Blockade]]" of the [[Anoat sector]] by Imperial [[Governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]], Plar conducted business with a number of individuals at the [[Konn-Nevos Spaceport]], among them a local, up-and-coming [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]]. Plar's inventory was often changing, and the vendor advised those he did business with to check his wares often for new merchandise.<ref name="Uprising" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bev Plar first appeared in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the multiplayer [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', created by the [[Kabam]] company and released on [[September 10]], [[2015]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms. Within the scope of the game, Plar acts as an item vendor, offering randomized goods in exchange for several in-game currencies.
