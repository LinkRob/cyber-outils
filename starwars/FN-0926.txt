'''FN-0926''' was a [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> who served as a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] for the [[First Order]]'s [[FN Trooper Corps]] during the late [[New Republic Era]]. FN-0926 was present in the [[Finalizer|''Finalizer'']] alongside [[Finn|FN-2187]], both in their barracks and later in the stormtrooper training complex as FN-2187 entered looking for [[FO-7155]]. The two troopers spoke and FN-0926 told FN-2187 about the final available squad leader position before [[Captain]] [[Archex|Cardinal]] arrived to oversee FN-2187's duel with [[FO-7155]]. FN-0926 stood more casually than stormtroopers were required to, but supposedly got away with that because of his likability. <ref name="TLO" />
