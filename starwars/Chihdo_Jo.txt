==Behind the scenes==
Chihdo Jo, originally known only as [[Chihdo/Legends|Chihdo]], was created by [[Mary Jo Duffy]], [[Kerry Gammill]], and [[Tom Palmer]] for the [[1977]] run of the [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book series]]. He first appeared in [[Star Wars (1977) 70|issue seventy]], now part of the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity,<ref name="SW 70">[[Star Wars (1977) 70|''Star Wars'' (1977) 70]]</ref> on [[January 18]], [[1983]].<ref name="SW 70 Release Date">''[[Amazing Heroes]]'' #18, [[Wikipedia:The_Comic_Reader#On_the_Drawing_Board.2FThe_Comic_Reader|''The Comic Reader'']] #208 and [[Wikipedia:The Comics Journal|''The Comics Journal'']] #78</ref> The character later appeared in ''Star Wars'' [[canon]] in "[[Duel of the Reprobates]]," a story included in [[Revelations (2023) 1|''Revelations'' 1]], which was written by [[Marc Guggenheim]] and illustrated by [[Salva Espín]]. The one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] was published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Duel" /> on [[December 20]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/112390/star_wars_revelations_2023_1|text=STAR WARS: REVELATIONS (2023) #1}}</ref> ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'', a [[2026]] reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]], [[Retroactive continuity|retroactively established]] that a bounty encased in [[Carbon-freezing|carbon-freeze]] in the [[Chapter 1: The Mandalorian|first episode]] of [[Star Wars: The Mandalorian|''The Mandalorian'']] was in fact Chihdo Jo.<ref name="MandoVG" /> That episode aired on [[November 12]], [[2019]], making it Jo's first official appearance in canon.<ref name="Mando Ch 1 Airdate">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/heat-vision/star-wars-new-mandalorian-details-footage-unveiled-1201931|text='The Mandalorian': Jon Favreau Teases Disney+ Series at Star Wars Celebration|author=Aaron Couch & Graeme McMillan|date=2018-12-10|archivedate=20191109001233}}</ref>
