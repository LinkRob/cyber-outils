'''Diadem Square''' was a square located in [[Coronet City]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. It was the main location of a [[Coronet City revolution|revolution]] organized by the [[Acolytes of the Beyond]].<ref name="ALD" /> In between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]]<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref>, [[Strono Tuggs]] visited a food stall in Diadem Square and got an order of [[Corellia Chew]]s.<ref name="UC">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref>
