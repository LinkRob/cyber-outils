'''DGS-286''' was an [[HMP droid gunship]] that was classified as a heavy [[repulsorlift]] [[airspeeder]],<ref name="FFGXW2" /> reaching 12.3 [[meter]]s in length, 11 meters in width, and 3.1 meters in height.<ref name="VehiclesNewEdition" /> The [[droid]] [[gunship]] boasted a single [[heavy laser cannon]] and two dual medium [[laser cannon]]s,<ref name="Equipment" /> and was also equipped with [[concussion bomb]]s, [[missile]]s, and a [[deflector shield generator]].<ref name="VehiclesNewEdition" /> A pair of two [[Rangefinder|binocular rangefinders]] and rangefinder preprocessors aided DGS-286's long-range [[Realspace|spatial]] and missile guidance systems. But on the forward [[hull]], it received [[sensor]] capabilities from four long-range sensor ports and two [[Color|red]]-colored [[photoreceptor]]s.<ref name="Equipment" /> DGS-286 was also equipped with [[Afterburner|ion-drive engines]] and [[repulsorlift generator]]s.<ref name="VehiclesNewEdition" />

DGS-286 took part in the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="FFGXW2" /> fought from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> in the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval forces]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] against the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="FFGXW2" /> Constructed late in the conflict,<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|droid-gunship|Droid Gunship}}</ref> it was involved in an engagement with a Republic [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter]]. As it gave pursuit, it fired several of its missiles upon the [[starfighter]].<ref name="FFGXW2" />

==Behind the scenes==
DGS-286 was introduced in the [[2020]] ''[[HMP Droid Gunship Expansion Pack]]'' to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition]]'' miniatures game.<ref name="FFGXW2" />
