The ''good'' news is that they're in ''total'' chaos. The [[Local day|morning]] after the [[Galactic Civil War]]'s [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]], a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[strike team]]&mdash;the [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|Pathfinders]]&mdash;carried out an '''assault''' on an [[Imperial outpost (Endor)|Imperial outpost]] on the [[moon]] [[Endor]]. The strike team's target was a base operated by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] holdouts looking for an opportunity to counterattack after the Empire had suffered a defeat during the previous day's engagement. Not wanting to leave remaining [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] behind on the moon, [[General]] [[Han Solo]] led his Pathfinders and some of the moon's [[Homeworld|native]] [[Ewok]]s in an assault on the outpost.

The Pathfinders arrived on a [[Rebel Alliance Transport|transport]] [[pilot]]ed by [[Lieutenant]] [[Shara Bey]] and took out the base's defenses with the [[transport]]'s [[weapon]]s before capturing the base itself. Once inside, they gathered Imperial intelligence revealing the Empire's future plans, indicating that their war with the Empire was not yet over. Afterward, Solo debriefed about the endeavor with other Alliance generals.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|The Emperor has made a critical error, and the time for our attack has come.|[[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] of the Rebel Alliance, before the Battle of Endor|<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>}}
[[File:DreadnoughtHunter-EBME.png|thumb|left|270px|The Rebel Alliance launched the Battle of Endor to deliver a devastating blow against the Galactic Empire.]]
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] launched a [[Battle of Endor|massive attack]] against its wartime enemy, the [[Galactic Empire]], over the [[Endor|Forest Moon of Endor]]. The rebels' objective was to destroy the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]], an Imperial [[battle station]] capable of destroying entire [[Planet|worlds]], and [[Death|kill]] [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. The [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] assaulted the [[Death Star]] and the [[Imperial Navy|Imperial fleet]] over Endor while rebel [[General]] [[Han Solo]] led a [[strike team]]<ref name="Episode VI" /> of [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|Pathfinders]]<ref name="Shattered Empire 1" /> onto the surface of Endor to destroy the [[Death Star's shield generator|generator]] powering the Death Star's [[Deflector shield|shields]]. The strike team was assisted by the planet's [[Homeworld|native]] [[species]], the primitive [[Ewok]]s, along with whom they were able to overwhelm the Imperial [[troop]]s on the [[moon]] and destroy the [[SLD-26 planetary shield generator|generator]]. The battle in [[orbit]] led to the death of the [[Emperor]] and his chief enforcer, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], as well as the battle station's destruction.<ref name="Episode VI" />

The battle's end saw the retreat of most [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] from Endor,<ref name="Shattered Empire 1" /> but some were left abandoned on the moon. While some of those stranded surrendered to the rebels, others dug in and waged a [[Guerrilla warfare|guerrilla campaign]] against the Alliance, including some of the surviving Imperial troops from the main ground battle, who gathered at hidden bases across the moon.<ref name="Changed">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> The [[Imperial outpost (Endor)|last]] of these outposts<ref name="HC75">{{HelmetCollectionCite|75|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> remained on the far side of the moon from where the rebels were located. The rebels themselves regrouped on the surface to celebrate in [[Bright Tree Village]], the home of the Ewoks who helped them defeat the Empire. Many rebels celebrated their victory, including [[rebel pilot]] [[lieutenant]] [[Shara Bey]] and her [[Marriage|husband]], Pathfinder [[Kes Dameron]].<ref name="Shattered Empire 1" />

==The assault==
{{Quote|The Ewoks did us a good ''turn'', and General Madine feels&mdash;and I agree&mdash;that it would be ''rude'' to leave a company of bucketheads in their backyard. So we're going to ''finish'' this, once and for ''all''.|General Han Solo, to the Pathfinders|<ref name="Shattered Empire 1" />}}
The [[Local day|morning]] after the Battle of Endor, Solo reassembled the Pathfinders and told them that, while the Empire was in complete chaos after its loss, the rebels had discovered the Imperial outpost on the far side of the moon. Knowing that the Imperial holdouts were looking for an opportunity to counterattack against the Rebel Alliance and not wanting to leave Imperials on the moon for the Ewoks to contend with alone, Solo and Alliance General [[Crix Madine]] determined that the Pathfinders would attack the outpost. Solo outlined the plan for the Pathfinders, which was to hit the base as hard as they could with limited air cover, and Bey volunteered to be the [[pilot]] that provided air support.<ref name="Shattered Empire 1" />

[[File:ImperialOutpostAssaultBegins-ShatteredEmpire1.png|thumb|right|280px|The transport fires on the base, beginning the assault against the Imperial holdouts.]]
Bey piloted a [[transport]] carrying [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldiers]] and [[Bright Tree tribe]] Ewok [[Soldier|warriors]] who accompanied them to the base,<ref name="Shattered Empire 1" /> including the [[scout]] [[Wicket Wystri Warrick]],<ref name="HC70" /> bringing the [[Rebel Alliance Transport|transport]] in hot in order to mask their approach. Once the [[Starship|ship]] was above the base, the rebels opened fire on the [[company]] of Imperial troops on the ground, which included [[stormtrooper]]s, an [[All Terrain Scout Transport]], and [[scout trooper]]s riding [[74-Z speeder bike]]s. The Imperial troops were quickly overwhelmed, and the Pathfinders disembarked from the vessel in order to fight the Imperials head-on. They fought their way toward the base while Bey continued firing on the outpost with the ship, taking out its defenses. While Bey kept the ship parked outside, the Pathfinders entered the base and found a great deal of data in the Imperial [[computer]]s. They returned to the transport once they were finished analyzing it, bringing with them information about Imperial communications traffic and even plans that the Empire had for after the Battle of Endor.<ref name="Shattered Empire 1" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|You guys were in there a while.''"<br />"''Yeah, there was a lot of data in their computers. Lot of comms traffic. Lot of plans. It's not over yet.|Shara Bey and Han Solo|<ref name="Shattered Empire 1" />}}
[[File:RaidAtTheWretchOfTayron-ShatteredEmpire3.png|thumb|left|300px|After the outpost assault, the Pathfinders were sent on other missions, such as the raid at the Wretch of Tayron (pictured).]]
After the assault's discoveries, Solo came to believe that the war was not yet over<ref name="Shattered Empire 1" /> and relayed this sentiment to his Pathfinders. He then attended a debriefing with other Alliance generals about the Imperial outpost mission, telling his peers about the orders being sent throughout [[the galaxy]] under Palpatine's name, despite the Emperor's death days before. Not long after the assault's completion, Solo was granted a brief respite from wartime efforts to enjoy his honeymoon aboard the ''[[Halcyon]]'' [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|star cruiser]] with his [[Wedding of Han Solo and Leia Organa|newly wedded]] [[Gender|wife]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="Princess and the Scoundrel" />

The Pathfinders later undertook numerous missions to locate and destroy the other remaining Imperial holdouts on Endor and had dealt with most of them within a few [[Standard week|weeks]], although rumors of surviving Imperials hiding out in the [[forest]]s and refusing to surrender circulated for [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="Changed" /> The war continued for some [[time]] after the rebels left Endor. The Pathfinders engaged in multiple assaults after the battle,<ref name="Shattered Empire 2">[[Shattered Empire 2|''Shattered Empire'' 2]]</ref> including a [[Raid at the Wretch of Tayron|raid]] on an [[Imperial Security Bureau]] black site at the [[Wretch of Tayron]]. This allowed the rebels to gain critical intelligence needed to combat [[Operation: Cinder]],<ref name="Shattered Empire 3">[[Shattered Empire 3|''Shattered Empire'' 3]]</ref> the Empire's post-Endor [[Contingency|operation]] designed to destroy multiple worlds in retaliation for the Emperor's death.<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" />

Not long after the outpost mission, Solo authored a missive to Madine praising Dameron's heroics during the endeavor, recommending him for a compensation package upon retirement. Madine processed the request<ref name="Flight Log">''[[Poe Dameron: Flight Log]]''</ref> following Dameron's own request to be discharged<ref name="Shattered Empire 4">[[Shattered Empire 4|''Shattered Empire'' 4]]</ref> and replied to Solo's message affirmatively. Kes's [[Dameron family|son]], [[Poe Dameron]], later kept a copy of both messages in his [[Poe Dameron's flight log|flight log]] compiled after the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]]<ref name="Flight Log" /> of [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The assault on an Imperial outpost first appeared in the [[Shattered Empire 1|first issue]] of the [[2015]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Shattered Empire]]''. The issue was written by [[Greg Rucka]], illustrated by [[Marco Checchetto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Shattered Empire 1" /> on [[September 9]], 2015.<ref name="ComicDate">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/52678/journey_to_star_wars_the_force_awakens_-_shattered_empire_2015_1|text=Journey to Star Wars: The Force Awakens - Shattered Empire (2015) #1}}</ref> The original pitch for ''Shattered Empire'' did not include a role for Han Solo. However, Rucka was later given permission to introduce Solo to the story,<ref name="WookInterview">{{WookYouTube|0CcDDZBZZnc|Wookieepedia Interviews - Greg Rucka}}</ref> which ultimately included his role in leading the assault.<ref name="Shattered Empire 3" />

The event was later mentioned in the [[2022]] novel ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'', written by [[Beth Revis]].<ref name="Princess and the Scoundrel" /> In an interview, Revis later affirmed that she had specifically referenced ''Shattered Empire'' 1<ref name="RevisInterview">{{YouTube|video=_0Qhjn8Rz_4|text=Interviewing Beth Revis, the author of "The Princess & The Scoundrel"|channel=@PrincessandScoundrel|name=Princess and Scoundrel|date=2022-09-06}}</ref>&mdash;which featured the assault<ref name="Shattered Empire 1" />&mdash;in order to tie in the work with other ''[[Star Wars]]'' media and better establish ''The Princess and the Scoundrel''{{'s}} chronology.<ref name="RevisInterview" />
