A '''claw''' was a sharp, curved nail found on the [[limb]]s of some [[species]]. The [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Wookiee]]s of the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]] had extendable claws on their digits, and an [[Wookiee honor code|honor code]] forbade them from using their claws for anything other than climbing.<ref name="TFA junior">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>
