'''Chelidae''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|indigneous]] to the [[planet]] [[Castilon]]. While [[Amphibian|amphibious]], they preferred to live above the [[water]]. They were enclosed in a natural protective shell, and the [[Gender|males]] sported facial [[hair]] while the females did not.<ref name="The Children from Tehar Fun Facts" /> Chelidae were able to slow down their life systems. A group of chelidae served as engineers on the space station ''[[Colossus]]''.<ref name="The Children from Tehar" />
