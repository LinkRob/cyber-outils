By around [[247 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[High Republic Era]], a '''bombing''' of the [[Chamber of the Unknown Spirits]] was carried out by the [[Weequay]] [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Kassav Milliko]] using a [[proton bomb]]. It occurred when the [[pirate]] was in the chamber threatening to destroy the [[Reliquary]], a [[Religion|religious]] object considered sacred by a number of individuals including a [[Unidentified steward|steward]]. The individuals were all on their knees begging the [[Kassav's Tempest|Tempest]] leader to not destroy it, as the chamber was holy ground. Ultimately, Milliko destroyed the object with a proton bomb in the middle of the chamber.<ref name="Tempest Runner script" />

Around 247 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Milliko told [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Asgar Ro]] about when he bombed the Chamber of the Unknown Spirits. After Ro asked what he had done after being begged to not destroy the Reliquary, the Weequay stated he was left with no choice to destroy it, adding the object was left with a lot of holes in it. The two then laughed.<ref name="Tempest Runner script" />
