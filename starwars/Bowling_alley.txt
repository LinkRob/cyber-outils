'''Bowling alley''' was a facility where the sport of [[bowling]] was played.<ref name="PnF">''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]''</ref> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{PnFDate|1}}</ref> several of such facilities were present at the ''[[Death Star]]'' space station.  Prior to its destruction in the [[Battle of Yavin]], their employees were evacuated from the space station by the [[Firestar Girls]] squadron.<ref name="PnF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bowling alley was first mentioned in the non-canonical television special ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]'', which aired in 2014.<ref name="PnF"/>
