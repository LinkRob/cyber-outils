The '''Blue Gown of the Sullen Moon''' was a primarily [[Color|blue]], floor-length [[Clothing|gown]] with long, broad sleeves that had a yellow inner lining and a panel of yellow fabric layered under two smaller blue panels of fabric hanging from the front of the waist.<ref name="ROUVG" />

The [[robe]] was worn together with the [[Lorrdian Cowl of Quiescence]], which matched the gown's colors,<ref name="ROUVG" /> by the [[Lorrdian]] [[Gender|male]] known as [[the High Priest]] while he led a [[Brotherhood of the Beatific Countenance]] [[pilgrim]]age to [[Jedha City]] on the sacred [[moon]] [[Jedha]] shortly before<ref name="Geektionary" /> the [[city]]'s [[Destruction of Jedha City|destruction]] by the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]] in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Members of the Brotherhood concealed their individuality as part of their faith, with others wearing orange robes,<ref name="ROUVG" /> meaning that the High Priest could be identified by the blue cowl and gown.<ref name="Geektionary" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Blue Gown of the Sullen Moon appeared in the [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="RO">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> which was released on [[December 16]], [[2016]].<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/rebel-reading-rogue-one-books|text=Rebel Reading: A Guide to ''Rogue One'' Books|author=[[James Floyd|Floyd, James]]|date=2016-12-14}}</ref> Prior to the film's release, the gown was revealed as part of a reel of promotional footage for ''Rogue One'' shown at [[Celebration Europe (2016)|Celebration Europe]] on [[July 15]], 2016.<ref name="Celebration">{{SWYouTube|HUb_zpdyDpU|Rogue One: A Star Wars Story - Celebration Reel|sw_url=video/rogue-one-a-star-wars-story-celebration-reel}}</ref>

The gown's name was then provided in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="ROUVG" /> and released on the same day as the film.<ref name="SW.com" /> The name of the vestment is a reference to<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/816058739249643520|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=p. 73. There's a They Might Be Giants reference on this page.|archivefile=File:TwitterCap_pablohidalgo_RogueOneUVGdump.png|nolive=1}}</ref> the [[1989]] song "Nightgown of the Sullen Moon" by alternative rock band [[Wikipedia:They Might Be Giants|They Might Be Giants]].<ref name="AllMusic">{{WebCite|url=https://www.allmusic.com/song/nightgown-of-the-sullen-moon-mt0012233649|text=''Nightgown of the Sullen Moon'' by They Might Be Giants|work=[[Wikipedia:AllMusic|AllMusic]]|archivedate=20240210163115}}</ref>
