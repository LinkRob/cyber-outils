'''Ady Sun'Zee''' was a [[Mirialan]] [[Gender|female]] [[Jedi Knight]]<ref name="Last Call" /> of the [[Jedi Order]]. After Sun'Zee sent a [[Distress signal|distress call]] to the [[Jedi Council]], it was answered by [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]]. Ady Sun'Zee wielded a standard blue [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Tales" />

==Biography==
During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Jedi Order]] established several outposts on [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] worlds, including a [[Batuu Jedi temple|research facility]] for rare artifacts on [[Batuu]].<ref name="Meet Ady" />

At one point, Ady Sun'Zee and her [[Jedi Master]] [[Sylwin]] brought the [[Ancient Sith Runestone]] to the Jedi outpost. There, the artifact inadvertently activated and released a powerful dark side corruption that resulted in the deaths of the outpost's personnel, including her master, [[Sylwin]]. Days later, when Master [[Yoda]] arrived in response to her distress call, she had forgotten everything. He told her to wait while he looked around, but Sun'Zee was still confused, entering a tunnel with corrupted [[vine]]s which emanated from the relic. On the way, she was attacked by [[demlin]]s, and upon entering the room where the relic was located, she heard herself and her master taunting her, claiming that she had killed her and the other Jedi in the outpost. Sun'Zee experienced dark [[Force vision|visions]] upon handling the relic again, but was able to overpower them after hearing Yoda calling to her. Upon finding Yoda again, Sun'Zee was able to overcome the relic with [[the Force]] and seal it away permanently. Believing herself to have killed her master, she was shocked to hear Master Yoda tell her she would some day make a great knight; he then revealed to her that she had not killed her master, and that it was a falsehood fed to her by the artifact and her own fear and doubts.<ref name="Tales" />

[[File:Ady-SunZee-lightsaber-TFTGE.png|thumb|left|200px|right|Ady Sun'Zee's lightsaber]]
In the [[Standard year|years]] that followed, Sun'Zee was unable to find a new master due to the sudden outbreak of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] and the [[Nihil conflict|invasion]] of the [[Nihil]]. Although attachments were usually forbidden, Sun'Zee sought solace by utilizing her fallen master's kyber crystal to power her own blade. Eventually she was promoted to the rank of [[Jedi Knight]], and took on the [[Urodel]] [[Padawan]] [[Nooa]] as her apprentice. The two went on a training exercise to [[Valron]], where [[Sylwin]] had taken Ady during her own apprenticeship.<ref name="Last Call" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> [[Seezelslak]] told her story to a [[Unidentified droid repair technician|droid repair technician]] in his cantina, stating that she had hidden the key to the temple somewhere on [[Batuu]], and wondered if it still existed.<ref name="Tales" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a Jedi Knight Ady was trained in the use of the Force and could [[Force push]] away several [[Nihil]] raiders.<ref name="HRA23 4">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4]]</ref> She was trained in the use of a lightsaber for close quarters combat.<ref name="HRA23 5">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 5]]</ref>
