The '''Darc X landspeeder''' was a model of [[landspeeder]] developed by [[Kor Sarun]].<ref name="Announcement" /> This [[repulsorcraft]] was described as being stunning both on and off the track.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|username=SWGalacticRacer|url=status/2053101542148800661|name=''[[Star Wars: Galactic Racer]]''|date=2026-05-09|quote=The Kor Sarun: Darc X landspeeder is stunning on and off the track. This deluxe vehicle is included in-game and as a finely crafted metal model in the Collector's Edition.|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/girar}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:GalacticRacerCollectorsEdition-MetalLandspeederModel.jpg|right|200px|thumb|The metal model of this landspeeder included in the ''Star Wars: Galactic Racer'' Collector's Edition.]]
This landspeeder is set to appear in the upcoming [[2026]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Racer]]'', developed by [[Fuse Games]].<ref name="Insider 236">{{InsiderCite|236|Launchpad}}</ref> The game's Collector's Edition ultimate bundle included a real-life "finely crafted metal model" of the Darc X landspeeder.<ref name="Collector's Edition">{{RacerWeb|url=news/official-gameplay-trailer|text=Pre-Order STAR WARS: Galactic Racer™ Today|archivedate=20260430165012}}</ref>
