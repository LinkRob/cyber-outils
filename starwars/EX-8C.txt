'''EX-8C''', or '''Atesee''', was an [[EX communication droid|EX droid]] assigned to [[Outer Rim Expedition Two-Five-Two]], a team of [[Republic Pathfinder]]s active<ref name="QFTHC" /> in [[382 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Quest for the Hidden City]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> The team was betrayed during an [[Expedition to Gloam|expedition]] to the [[planet]] [[Gloam]], with its leader, [[Jedi Master]] [[Rok Buran]], the sole survivor. Buran was able to retrieve EX-8C from the ruins of the [[Foundling (starship)|''Foundling'']], the expedition's [[starship]], and sent the damaged [[droid]] away with a message asking for help. On its journey, EX-8C was further damaged by an [[asteroid storm]] before being found by [[Amos Tillian]] and [[Kam]], [[Galactic Republic]] [[communications engineer]]s who worked with Master [[Silandra Sho]]'s [[Master Sho's Pathfinder team|Pathfinder team]].<ref name="QFTHC" />
