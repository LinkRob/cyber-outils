'''Fire of the Rising Moons''' was a nighttime festival celebrated in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GEDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the festival was conducted by the modified [[R1-series astromech droid]], [[Bard]], who played recordings of his deceased owner [[Tohan Lege]], known as the Lore-weaver. The voice of Lege introduced the event where colorful [[fireworks]] lit the sky, and observers raised their lights and voices in celebration of their freedom and to honor the heroes and legends of galactic history.<ref name="Disneyland">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=entertainment/disneyland/fire-of-the-rising-moons-fireworks|text=Disneyland: Fire of the Rising Moons}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|During Fire of the Rising Moons, villagers and visitors alike come together to celebrate their freedom and to honor the heroes and legends who came before them. As the skies over Batuu light up in bursts of color, iconic music associated with tales from across the galaxy ignites our imagination. It's a powerful moment you won't want to miss!|Disneyland website|<ref name="Disneyland" />}}
[[Fire of the Rising Moons (show)|Fire of the Rising Moons]] was a guest experience at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in Anaheim, California. The nighttime spectacular provided guests with an alternate view of the park's fireworks display set to iconic ''[[Star Wars]]'' music by [[John Williams]].<ref name="Disneyland" /> As part of the [[2024]] [[Season of the Force]], the show was first set to debut on [[April 5]], 2024, but due to weather, the official premiere was pushed back to [[April 6]].<ref name="MouseInfo">{{WebCite|url=https://mouseinfo.com/2024/04/guide-how-to-enjoy-fire-of-the-rising-moons-fireworks-in-star-wars-galaxys-edge-at-disneyland|text=GUIDE: How to enjoy 'Fire of the Rising Moons' fireworks at Disneyland in Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|archivedate=20240525212314}}</ref> The show was introduced by two new characters, Tohan Lege and Bard. Event audio was played in viewing areas across the land.<ref name="Disneyland" /> Beginning on [[May 24]], 2024, Bard could be seen overlooking [[Docking Bay 5]].<ref name="WDWNT">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2024/05/new-bard-droid-arrives-at-star-wars-galaxys-edge-in-disneyland-for-fire-of-the-rising-moons-fireworks|text=New Bard Droid Arrives at Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge in Disneyland for 'Fire of the Rising Moons' Fireworks|work=WDW News Today|archivedate=20240524210828}}</ref>
