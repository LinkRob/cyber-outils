'''Charles Lippincott''' was the advertising publicity supervisor on [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. He first presented [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'']] to the public in [[July 2]], [[1976]], at a slideshow held at the Westercon Hyatt House Hotel, in Los Angeles.<ref name="San Diego Comic-Con Exclusives">{{ThisWeek|-BZX-WxjhFE|San Diego Comic-Con Exclusives, Brand New LEGO Art, and More!}}</ref>
