The '''Battle of Starkiller Base''', also known as the '''attack on Starkiller Base''', the '''raid on Starkiller Base''', the '''assault on Starkiller Base''', or the '''destruction of Starkiller Base''', was the first major battle fought between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the two factions.

The battle was fought in [[34 ABY]] with [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|First Order TIE fighters]] and Resistance [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]s. The goal of the Resistance fighters was to disable and destroy the [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]] in order to prevent the First Order, which was charging its main weapon, from destroying the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar]] and continuing a reign of terror.

The Resistance forces would achieve that goal decisively, despite a fierce defense from the First Order. Starkiller Base was destroyed, and the First Order was significantly weakened, despite both [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] and General [[Armitage Hux]] surviving the battle.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|If we can destroy that oscillator it might destabilize the core and cripple the weapon.|Ushos O. Statura|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
During their search for [[Luke Skywalker]] in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> the [[First Order]] sought to use [[Starkiller Base]] to destroy the [[Resistance]] and cripple the [[New Republic]]. They launched a [[Hosnian Cataclysm|preemptive strike]] on the [[capital]] of the New Republic, [[Hosnian Prime]], and four other [[planet]]s in the [[Hosnian system]] as demonstration of power, destroying the planets and a [[New Republic Defense Fleet|New Republic fleet]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

On [[Takodana]], [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] realized that the [[scavenger]] and Resistance ally [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] was [[Force-sensitive]]. During the [[Battle of Takodana]], he captured her and took her to Starkiller Base for [[Torture|interrogation]]. However, the interrogation backfired when Rey ended up probing Kylo's mind and discovering his fear of never becoming as great as his grandfather, the infamous [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] enforcer [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], forcing him to leave to seek out his master Snoke's guidance. He then returned to attempt to continue the interrogation, but Rey had already escaped via [[Mind trick|manipulating]] [[FN-1824|the stormtrooper tasked with guarding her]] into releasing her. This angered Kylo enough that he destroyed the interrogation room in a rage-fueled tantrum, causing two patrolling [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] to back away when they noticed the commotion.<ref name="Episode VII" />

By tracking the path of the weapon's energy beam, the Resistance traced it to its origin. [[Jessika Pava]] designed a [[Unidentified Resistance recon pod|recon pod]] that was piloted by [[Temmin Wexley]] to scout the superweapon. The pod was towed to and from Starkiller Base by Wexley's wife, [[Karé Kun]], in her X-wing.<ref name="Poe 27">[[Poe Dameron 27|''Poe Dameron'' 27]]</ref> The First Order were able to track the X-wing back to D'Qar.<ref name="Episode VII" />

At the same time, members of the Resistance's [[J-Squadron]] were tracking their captured member [[Jothan Tiaan Jerjerrod|Jo Jerjerrod]]. By tracking the coordinates of a captured [[TIE/vn space superiority fighter]], they arrived at Starkiller Base and landed, sneaking into the base.<ref name="JTR 3" />

[[Finn]] had given Starkiller Base's blueprints to the Resistance, which revealed that the [[superweapon]] drained the energy of the [[Star|sun]] in order to charge its main weapon, and that it required a [[thermal oscillator]] to sustain the energy on the planet. If this thermal oscillator was destroyed, it would destabilize the planet's core and implode the base. However, they would need to do it before Starkiller Base fully charged its main weapon. Finn, [[Han Solo]], and [[Chewbacca]] volunteered to disable Starkiller's [[Deflector shield generator|defensive shield]] from the inside.<ref name="Episode VII" />

Han Solo [[pilot]]ed the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to Starkiller Base accompanied by Chewbacca and Finn. Their objective was to disable the shields and allow the [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing squadron]] to strike, although Finn's main objective was to rescue Rey. Flying through the shields at [[lightspeed]], the ''Falcon'' made a rough landing on Starkiller Base at the edge of a cliff.<ref name="Episode VII" />

==The battle==
===The ground assault===
{{Quote|People are counting on us! The galaxy is counting on us!|Han Solo, to Finn|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:Han_Solo_and_Finn_on_Starkiller_Base.png|thumb|left|250px|Han, Finn, and Chewie sneak into Starkiller Base to disable the shields.]]
Han, Chewbacca, and Finn snuck into the main base and captured [[Captain]] [[Phasma]], whom they forced to disable the shields before disposing of her in a [[Garbage compactor|garbage chute]]. They reunited with Rey, who had already escaped on her own. Upon leaving the base and seeing the aerial assault, Han, Chewbacca, Finn, and Rey realized the Resistance [[squadron]]s needed assistance and decided to create an opening by planting [[thermal detonator]]s inside the base.<ref name="Episode VII" />

When Han pointed out that there was no way to enter the oscillator in order to plant the bombs, Finn offered to find a way to open a doorway via a conventional [[Junction station|junction]] as his previous experience working on [[Star Destroyer]]s had given him knowledge in this area. He and Rey then approached a parking area where they commandeered a lone snowspeeder and set off towards the junction as Han and Chewie headed for the nearest structure. A single stormtrooper in the area spotted their snowspeeder and attempted to shoot them down. After his shot went awry, he then reported in the incident.<ref name="TFA novel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref>

As Finn and Rey raced to the junction, they were pursued by three other snowspeeders. After briefly exchanging fire with the enemy, Rey ordered Finn to head towards the trees. There, Rey asked Finn to switch places, which led to Finn taking command of the blaster while Rey flew the speeder. With one shot Finn was able to eliminate one of their pursuers from the fight. A short while later, a second shot from Finn was able to take out the second of their pursuers. As they approached a cliff, Finn's engagement with the last of their pursuing party led to him dropping the blaster into the snow, leaving them almost defenseless. Thanks to Rey's expert manuevering however, they were able to jump between cliffs and leave the last of their pursuers overturned in the snow. Finn and Rey then continued onwards to their target.<ref name="Perilous Pursuit">{{FOD|Perilous Pursuit}}</ref>

Upon arriving at the junction, Rey quickly went to work. Within a very short span of time she managed to rig the junction properly, leading to a service hatch opening upon the complex Han and Chewie were sneaking within. Han and Chewie then planned a proper strategy to make maximum use of the explosives at their disposal before getting to work.<ref name="TFA novel" /> Han then confronted Kylo Ren on a bridge over a large pit, pleading with his son to reject [[Snoke]] and return to the [[light side of the Force]]. However, Ren refused and impaled his father with [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|his]] [[lightsaber]]. Han lovingly stroked his son's face before he plummeted down a shaft as Finn, Rey, and Chewbacca looked on. Instead of empowering him,<ref name="Episode VII" /> Kylo Ren felt weakened and horrified by his actions.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> An enraged Chewbacca injured Ren with [[Chewbacca's bowcaster|his]] [[bowcaster]] and proceeded to shoot down several stormtroopers. Chewbacca then detonated the explosives, which created an opening for the base's thermal oscillators.<ref name="Episode VII" />

===The aerial assault===
{{Quote|All teams. I'm going in! Pull up and cover me!|Poe Dameron|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:StarkillerBaseBattle-Fathead.png|thumb|right|220px|First Order TIEs engage Black One.]]
With the shields down, [[Blue Squadron (Resistance)|Blue Squadron]] and [[Red Squadron (Resistance)|Red Squadron]], led by [[Poe Dameron]] as [[Black Leader]], commenced their assault. All starfighter squadrons locked their [[S-foils]] in attack position to initiate the strike on Starkiller Base's thermal oscillator. [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]], seeing the fleet from outside, ordered to dispatch all [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighter]] squadrons. The X-wing starfighter squadrons successfully executed one bombing run of the thermal oscillator, hitting it directly but causing no damage towards it, but then the TIE fighters were upon them. During the battle that ensued between the opposing fighters, a heavy [[cannon]] emplacement launched a missile that destroyed an X-wing fighter. With ten [[Standard minute|minutes]] left until the weapon became fully charged, half of the Resistance [[Resistance navy|fleet]] had already been destroyed. The thermal detonators, triggered by Chewbacca, activated in the base, and a new hole to the oscillator was visible to the fighter squadrons. [[Red Four]] and [[Red Six]] covered Poe while the rest of the squadron focused on firing on the target. As the squadrons soared through the trenches of the base with the First Order TIE fighters and turbolasers trying to block the assault, [[Ello Asty]]'s X-wing was shot down.<ref name="Episode VII" />

===J-Squadron and Jerjerrod===
J-Squadron was able to locate Jerjerrod during the infiltration. They fought a number of stormtroopers, and stole a [[Griffin-class light shuttle|''Griffin''-class light shuttle]] to escape. The [[officer]] [[Wanten]], who had been tracking them, tried to intercede but was killed by the shuttle. After rendezvousing with the X-wing fleet, Commander Dameron let the shuttle take the lead back into hyperspace.<ref name="JTR 3" />

===Duel with the Knight===
====Kylo Ren vs Finn====
{{Quote|Traitor! That lightsaber...belongs to ''me''."<br /> "Come and get it.|Kylo Ren and Finn before their fight|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:Kylo_Ren_confronts_Rey_and_Finn.png|thumb|left|250px|Kylo Ren confronts Finn and Rey outside Starkiller Base.]]
Finn and Rey exited the base, but were followed by Ren, who caught up with them in the forest despite being weakened by the injury he suffered from Chewbacca's bowcaster. Rey angrily called Ren a "monster" for murdering his own father and tried to shoot him with her [[Rey's blaster|blaster]], but Ren knocked her out by [[Force push]]ing her into a [[tree]]. Finn, horrified, rushed over as Ren furiously called him a "[[Treason|traitor]]." Failing to wake her, Finn ignited Anakin Skywalker's [[Skywalker lightsaber|second lightsaber]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

Ren claimed that the lightsaber rightfully belonged to him, but Finn challenged him to "come and get it" before attacking him. Despite Finn's attempts to hold his own against Ren, he was easily overpowered. Ren stabbed him in the shoulder with his lightsaber's [[Crossguard lightsaber|crossguard blades]] and slashed him across the back, incapacitating him, but not before receiving a shoulder wound. Believing that the duel had ended, he reached out for Anakin Skywalker's saber and tried to seize it with a [[Telekinesis|Force pull]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

====Kylo Ren vs Rey====
{{Quote|You need a teacher! ''I'' can show you the ways to the Force!''"<br />"''The Force...?|Kylo Ren and Rey as their blades clash|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
All of a sudden, and without prior warning, Skywalker's weapon flew out from the snow, and not into Ren's hand, but into that of Rey's. Ren was initially taken aback, evincing a degree of surprise and stepped rearward as the saber flew past him. Rey used [[telekinesis]] to pull Skywalker's lightsaber into her [[Limb|hand]] and proceeded to attack the Knight with a brutal barrage.<ref name="Episode VII" /> While the emotional pain of killing his father severely impacted his performance against the much less experienced Rey during their duel,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Ren initially held the advantage, drawing on his rage to ignore the pain. Ren became so impressed with Rey that he tried to tempt her, complimenting her strength in [[the Force]] and offering to complete her training if she would join him.<ref name="Episode VII" /> 

[[File:Anticipate_SWDA.png|thumb|right|300px|Rey draws on the Force to duel Kylo Ren.]]
This backfired, however, as it only served to reassure Rey of her own power: she began to draw on the Force to hone her attacks, and the tide of the [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] shifted in her favor. Ultimately, the emboldened Rey overpowered<ref name="Episode VII" /> the emotionally<ref name="Episode VIII" /> and physically injured Ren and dealt him a severe wound to his face. By that point, Kylo had taken too many wounds to continue standing, but he still struggled in vain to rise again.<ref name="Episode VII" /> While an unidentifiable voice in her mind tempted her to the [[dark side of the Force]] by arguing it would be easy to kill Ren there and then, she rejected the temptation before<ref name="TFA novel" /> the planet ruptured between where they were standing and they were separated from finishing their fight.<ref name="Episode VII" />

===Final actions===
{{Quote|Our job's done here. Let's go home!|Poe Dameron|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:IntegratedSFoilsClosed-SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|Poe Dameron piloted ''Black One'' into the thermal oscillator]]
Poe ordered his squadron to cover him as he entered the hole of the oscillator. A pursuing TIE fighter did not clear the gap, leaving Poe to circle in the interior of the unguarded structure, firing volley after volley of [[Proton torpedo|torpedoes]] into the oscillator, destroying it thirty [[second]]s before it finished draining the sun to charge the weapon. This caused a chain reaction which destroyed the base and leveled the entire planet's terrain. Inside the main base, chaos reigned as General Hux departed the command center and First Order officers realized all hope was lost and evacuated. Hux, meanwhile, reported to [[Supreme Leader]] Snoke, who ordered him to rescue Ren and flee the base, as he wished to complete Ren's training. Rey returned to Finn, despairing that he would not wake up and that they would be trapped on the rupturing planet to meet their doom. However, Chewbacca promptly arrived with the ''Millennium Falcon'' to rescue them.<ref name="Episode VII" />

[[File:Oscillator_Go_Boom_TFA.png|thumb|250px|The destruction of Starkiller Base's thermal oscillator]]
Phasma was able to escape from the trash compactor, and soon made her way back to the computer terminal to erase its history, preventing anyone from knowing that it was her that lowered the shields. She soon discovered that [[Lieutenant]] [[Sol Rivas]] had accessed the terminal, and in doing so learned about Phasma's treason. Phasma, wanting to eliminate any loose ends, pursued Rivas to a TIE fighter facility, where he stole a TIE fighter. Phasma approached pilot [[TN-3465]], who was unable to join the ongoing dogfight due to her droid, [[BB-K8]], performing a data update on her fighter. Phasma approached TN-3465 and ordered her to [[Hunt for Rivas|follow]] Rivas in a two-seat [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]],<ref name="Captain Phasma 1" /> eventually following Rivas to the nearby [[Luprora]].<ref name="Captain Phasma 2">[[Captain Phasma 2|''Captain Phasma'' 2]]</ref> The ''Millennium Falcon'' and the remainder of the Resistance fleet managed to escape Starkiller Base as it erupted into a star. With the mission complete, they entered [[hyperspace]] to return to [[D'Qar]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|They can't get away, Supreme Leader. We have them tied on the end of a string.|General Armitage Hux, to Supreme Leader Snoke|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
Following the battle, Organa sent a coded transmission to all Resistance squadrons stating that the battle of Starkiller Base was won. Organa also told the squadrons to report in immediately and await further instructions.<ref name="FF II">{{SWYouTube|Y0DuA0ERkTA|Force Friday II: A Message from General Leia Organa}}</ref> Around the same time, R2-D2 rebooted and proceeded to merge his part of the galactic map with the piece found by BB-8. Chewbacca and Rey left for Skywalker's location on [[Ahch-To]], while Finn was put into a medically induced coma to heal his injuries from Ren.<ref name="Episode VII" />

After Phasma located Rivas, she killed him along with TN-3465 and BB-K8 to ensure no witnesses knew of what she did before returning to First Order space, where First Order vessels were regrouping.<ref name="Captain Phasma 4">[[Captain Phasma 4|''Captain Phasma'' 4]]</ref>

Surviving First Order defense force pilots, such as [[Poldin LeHuse]], were re-integrated into the fleet. They expressed a desire for vengeance and to prove themselves after failing to save the superweapon.<ref name="TLJ VD" />

Whilst [[Skirmish on the Retribution|aboard]] the Star Destroyer ''[[Retribution]]'', [[Zay Versio]] and [[Dio]] watched Starkiller's explosion, before reporting to [[Shriv Suurgav]] that her mother [[Iden Versio|Iden]] was dead but she made it count. Zay ordered Suurgav to contact the Resistance and tell them that the war was just beginning.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

The Resistance victory was ultimately pyrrhic, as destroying Starkiller Base was not close enough to undo the complete annihilation of the Hosnian system and consequentially the New Republic by the First Order prior, thus serving as a pathway for the latter to claim dominant control over the galaxy. Also in the aftermath, its members had to [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuate]] from D'Qar as a result of the First Order knowing their location, and ultimately was [[Battle of Oetchi|pursued and largely decimated]] before ultimately [[Battle of Crait|escaping]] the First Order with the [[Force projection|help]] of Skywalker.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

According to the Resistance [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]], even four months after the base's destruction, the majority of the galaxy wasn't aware that Starkiller Base had been destroyed.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The first footage of the battle was shown in the first ''The Force Awakens'' teaser trailer on [[November 28]], [[2014]].<ref name="TFA teaser">{{SWYouTube|erLk59H86ww|Star Wars: The Force Awakens Official Teaser|sw_url=video/star-wars-the-force-awakens-official-teaser}}</ref>

Part of the initial bombing run was re-filmed at [[Bad Robot Productions]] in order to show more cockpit scenes. Dameron's run into the breach in the oscillator was done as a deliberate homage to the Death Star trench run, but without a Force-sensitive character.<ref name="Commentary">Director [[J.J. Abrams]] [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] commentary</ref>

The [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]'' states that the Battle of Starkiller Base takes place 30 years after the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="OTFL" /> ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the battle in 34 ABY, thirty four years after the Battle of Yavin.<ref name="Galactic Atlas" />

===''Battlefront II''===
The Battle of Starkiller Base appears in the 2017 videogame, ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''. In the game's Galactic Assault mode, a potential game scenario consists of players fighting for the Resistance landing on the surface during the battle, at [[Precinct 47]], with the overall goal of freeing prisoners inside the base's detention block within the [[Command center (Starkiller Base)|command center]]. They are presented with three sets of objectives, with each set needing to be completed to move on. Meanwhile, First Order players are given only one objective: kill 100 Resistance players before they can complete a set of their objectives, otherwise the kill quota will raise by 50, maxing out again at 100. For the first set of objectives, the Resistance players are tasked with planting bombs at a fuel station and turbolaser nearby the base's main gate. If successful, the Resistance must then capture a command post at the main gate of the base. Finally, if completed, they must override both the base's cell door and gate controls. If the Resistance wins, they are shown boarding [[Resistance transport|transports]] and escaping. If the First Order wins, Starkiller Base fires.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
