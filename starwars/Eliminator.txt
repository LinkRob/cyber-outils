The '''''Eliminator''''' was a [[Star Destroyer]] in service to the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="LS" /> In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">In ''[[Lost Stars]]'', the ''Eliminator'' is mentioned around one [[Standard week|week]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' identifies the [[Standard year|year]] following the battle as [[0 ABY]], the ''Eliminator''{{'s}} appearance must take place in that year.</ref> around one [[Standard week|week]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]], in which the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]] was destroyed, the ''Eliminator'' was one of around a dozen Star Destroyers that formed the new core of the Imperial Starfleet.<ref name="LS" />

At that [[time]], the Star Destroyers were assembled in rough formation along with countless [[transport]]s and smaller [[Starship|ships]]. As their [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|transport]] was docking with the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Devastator]]'', which was also a part the [[fleet]], the Imperial personnel designated [[NO-718]] and [[NY-112]] were ordered by their [[Unidentified Imperial commander (Thane Kyrell)|commander]] to report to [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Cherik]] on the ''Eliminator''.<ref name="LS" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Eliminator'' was first mentioned in ''[[Lost Stars]]'', a [[2015]] young-adult novel by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="LS" />
