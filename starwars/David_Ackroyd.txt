David Ackroyd is an actor who  voiced [[Raymus Antilles/Legends|Captain Antilles]] in the second and third episodes of the [[Star Wars (radio)|''Star Wars'' radio drama]].<ref name="Radio">[[Star Wars (radio)|''Star Wars'' radio drama]]</ref>

Ackroyd's notable TV appearances include roles in ''The Secret Storm'', ''Another World'', ''Dallas'', ''Murder, She Wrote,'' and ''Xena: Warrior Princess''. In [[2004]] he co-founded the Alpine Theatre Project, a performing company in Whitefish, Montana. The company's board includes Ackroyd's fellow ''Star Wars'' radio performer, [[John Lithgow]].<ref name="ATP">{{WebCite|url=https://atpwhitefish.org/about|text=About|work= Alpine Theatre Project|archivedate=20231207154727}}</ref>
