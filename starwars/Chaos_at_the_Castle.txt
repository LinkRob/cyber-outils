'''''Chaos at the Castle''''' is a young readers book in the [[World of Reading]] series that adapts scenes from [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. The book, a level one title, was published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[August 2]], [[2016]]. The book is comprised of 32 pages and is intended for children ages six through eight.

==Publisher's summary==
''This level one early reader retells a favorite scene from ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''. Featuring beautiful stylized illustrations, this World of Reading book is perfect for young fans ready to read on their own.''
