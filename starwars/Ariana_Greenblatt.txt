'''Ariana Greenblatt''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] [[Wikipedia:Actor|actress]]<ref name="Hola" /> who portrays young [[Ahsoka Tano]] in the ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]'' episode "[[Part Five: Shadow Warrior]]."<ref name="Part V">{{Ahsoka|Part Five: Shadow Warrior}}</ref> Having begun her career as a child actress, she appeared in multiple successful [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] series and movies. Ariana Greenblatt is known for her roles in two billion dollar films, portraying young [[w:c:mcu:Gamora|Gamora]] in the [[2018]] superhero film ''[[Wikipedia:Avengers: Infinity War|Avengers: Infinity War]]'' and the role of Sasha in the [[2023]] film ''[[Wikipedia:Barbie (film)|Barbie]]''. Greenblatt also co-starred as Koa in the 2023 science fiction film ''[[Wikipedia:65 (film)|65]]'', where she acts opposite fellow ''Star Wars'' actor [[Adam Driver]].<ref name="Hola" /><ref name="Hollywood Reporter">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-features/65-star-ariana-greenblatt-talks-getting-adam-driver-1235346208|text='65' Star Ariana Greenblatt Talks Getting Adam Driver to Break, 'Barbie' and Prank Calls with Josh Brolin|author=Davids, Brian|date=2023-03-08|work=''[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|The Hollywood Reporter]]''|archivedate=20230723005332}}</ref>
