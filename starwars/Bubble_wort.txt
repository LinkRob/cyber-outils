'''Bubble wort''' was a substance found on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. A [[Bubble wort extract|catalyst]] was extracted from bubble wort, which was them mixed with [[plasma]]. The reaction between the two substances created energy that was used to power the bubbles that made up [[Gungan]] [[City|cities]] such as [[Otoh Gunga]].<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
