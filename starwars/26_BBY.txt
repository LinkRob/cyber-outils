The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''26 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3251 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7951''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Lyra Erso]] meets [[Galen Walton Erso]] on [[Espinar]].<ref name="UVG" />
*[[Pitina Mar-Mas Voor]] [[Marriage|marries]] her [[Pitina Mar-Mas Voor's husband|husband]].<ref name="Pitina Date">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 36|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 36]] states that [[Pitina Mar-Mas Voor]] was [[Reproduction|born]] twenty-five [[Standard year|years]] before [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]], which is dated to [[19 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore it can be deduced that Voor was born in [[44 BBY]]. As Voor was [[Marriage|married]] off on her eighteenth [[birthday]], it can also be deduced she was married in [[26 BBY]].</ref>
*After spending five years in a medical center on [[Gorse]] suffering from [[Shilmer's syndrome]], Lemuel Tharsa reinvents himself as [[Denetrius Vidian]].<ref name="Vidian">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]'' states that [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Hera Syndulla]] met on [[Gorse]], as depicted in the novel [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']], six [[Standard year|years]] prior to the events of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' states that ''Star Wars Rebels'' begins in the [[Standard year|year]] [[5 BBY]], thereby dating the events of ''A New Dawn'' to [[11 BBY]]. In the novel, [[Denetrius Vidian]] reminisces that he reinvented himself fifteen years before, thus setting the date of said reinvention to 26 BBY.</ref>
*As a test for [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] pretends to be kidnapped, forcing the master and apprentice to [[Rescue of Yoda|rescue]] him.<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin" /><ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Births==
*[[Numa]]<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/innocents-of-ryloth-episode-guide|text="Innocents of Ryloth" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}} is five [[Standard year|years]] old by the time of "[[Innocents of Ryloth]]." As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of "Innocents of Ryloth" in [[21 BBY]], Numa must've been born in [[26 BBY]].</ref> on [[Ryloth]].<ref name="SWE Numa">{{SWE|characters|numa|Numa}}</ref>
*[[TK-462]] on [[Eriadu]].<ref name="Birth">As stated in [[TK-462 (short story)|''TK-462'']], the protagonist was twelve in the year that [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] was promoted to [[Grand Moff]]. According to the novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], this occurred 14 [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. Therefore, the birth of the protagonist must have taken place around 26 years before that battle.</ref>
*[[Shaeeah Lawquane]] on [[Saleucami]].<ref name="Shaeeah">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode210.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: The Deserter}} states that [[Shaeeah Lawquane]] was five [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of "[[The Deserter]]." ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Landing at Point Rain]]" to [[21 BBY]], and {{HelmetCollectionCite|76|Highlights of the Saga}} dates the episode "[[Lethal Trackdown]]," to the same year. Since {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}} places "The Deserter" between "Landing at Point Rain" and "Lethal Trackdown," its events must take place in the same year. Because Shaeeah was five years old in 21 BBY, she must have been born in 26 BBY.</ref>
