The '''thirteenth episode''' of the animated web series ''[[Grogu Cutest In The Galaxy]]'' was released on [[September 30]], [[2025]]. It was posted by ''[[Star Wars]]'' Japan on [[Wikipedia:Instagram Reels|Instagram Reels]].<ref name="Instagram" />

{{Media-stub}}

==Plot summary==
[[Din Grogu|Grogu]] is repairing cables in a small compartment of the ''[[Razor Crest]]''. [[Din Djarin]] tells the child to connect the red wire with the blue one. Grogu is confused, and tries to plug the wires together, causing them to short circuit.<ref name="Instagram" />
