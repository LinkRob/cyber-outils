"'''Ewoks & Droids: Read All About 'Em'''" was an article published in [[Bantha Tracks 31|''Bantha Tracks'' 31]]. The article discussed the previously published and future ''Ewoks'' and ''Droids'' children's books published by [[Random House]] from [[1984]] to [[1986]].

The article is notable for including images of the covers of three never-produced books: ''Ewoks ABC Fun'', ''Ewoks Learn-to-Read Activity Book'', and ''Ewoks School Days''.<ref name="BT 31" />

The ''School Days'' title was once listed in [[1994]]'s ''Tomart's Price Guide to Worldwide Star Wars Collectibles'' book, but an [[Wasaka Berries and Monster Ships: Star Wars Storybook 101|article]] in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' later revealed that title (along with ''The Droid Dilemma'') was included to foil other publishers who might have copied Tomart's list for their own works.<ref name="SWI 74">{{InsiderCite|74|Wasaka Berries and Monster Ships: Star Wars Storybook 101|Wasaka Berries and Monster Ships: ''Star Wars'' Storybook 101}}</ref>
