The '''Andoan Wars''' were a series of wars around the [[planet]] [[Ando]], fought primarily between the [[Spiverelda]], the government of the [[Lambda sector]], and the [[Andoan Free Colonies]]. The [[ruling nests]], which governed the [[Harch]] [[species]], sided with the Spiverelda. The [[Galactic Republic]] later sided with the Andoan Free Colonies, which contributed to the Free Colonies being spared.<ref name="CotR" />

The Harch felt insulted by the Republic's interference, which ultimately led to their siding with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]], more than twenty [[Standard year|years]] later.<ref name="CotR" />
