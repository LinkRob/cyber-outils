The '''Felucian pin-up'''<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/deception-trivia-gallery|text="Deception" Trivia Gallery|quote=Among the posters seen in the bar's back room where Hardeen sleeps is one for DJ Rangthang, one for Sy Snootles, a Felucian pin-up, and a travel poster for Pantora. Illustrations by Randy Bantog.}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref> was a decorative [[Painting|poster]] available in [[the galaxy]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. It depicted a [[Gender|feminine]]-looking [[Felucian]] with [[Color|yellow]] skin, posing seductively in blue and green [[Clothing|underwear]]. The poster graced the walls of the back room of the [[Trueping's]] [[cantina]],<ref name="Deception" /> a [[Unidentified Turtle Tanker|Turtle Tanker]] stolen by [[Savage Opress]],<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref> and a building in [[Pons Ora]].<ref name="Missing in Action">{{TCW|Missing in Action}}</ref>
