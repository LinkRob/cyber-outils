The '''''Deliverance''''' was a [[Star Destroyer]] utilized by the [[New Republic]] under [[Twi'lek]] [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]], and served as the [[flagship]] of the Republic [[New Republic Defense Fleet|navy's]] [[Barma Battle Group]] commanded by Syndulla. The ''Deliverance'' was present during the [[Hunt for Shadow Wing|hunt]] for the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> alongside [[Flare Squadron|Flare]], [[Hail Squadron|Hail]], and [[Wild Squadron]]s. Despite the ''Deliverance'' and New Republic [[squadron]]s pursuing the Imperial forces, the 204th's [[bulk freighter]] ''[[Yadeez]]'' and its escorts managed to escape into [[hyperspace]] multiple times. The ''Deliverance'' was also present at the [[Attack on Chadawa]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

Later in that same year,<ref name="Date" /> the ''Deliverance'' participated in the [[Battle of Jakku]],<ref name="Victory's Price" /> which marked the end the [[Galactic Civil War]] with the subsequent signing of the [[Galactic Concordance]].<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> However, the Star Destroyer was destroyed amid the fighting, but not before its crew managed to escape the [[Starship|vessel]] aboard [[escape pod]]s.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
