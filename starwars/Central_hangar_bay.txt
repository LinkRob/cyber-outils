The '''central hangar bay''' was the main [[hangar]] bay of the [[Fortress Inquisitorius]] on the [[planet]] [[Nur]]. It was located on the exterior of the Fortress, and equipped with [[sensor]]s that scanned departing and arriving [[starship]]s to ensure the safety of the facility. As the only access point of the Fortress above the [[water]], it was equipped with its own [[squadron]] of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s as well as plenty of [[stormtrooper]] guards to defend it.<ref name="DRVG" />
