During the [[Nihil conflict]], the [[planet]] [[Eiram]] endured a massive '''cyclone disaster''' that covered nearly an entire continent<ref name="Fallen Star" /> and laid waste to the world. In response, the [[Galactic Republic]] dispatched [[Starlight Beacon]], as the [[Dalnan evacuation]] had proved the [[space station]] could be effective at disaster relief upon arriving over the site of a crisis.<ref name="High Republic 12" /> It remained over Eiram for some [[time]], leaving it to the site where the space station was [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destroyed]].<ref name="Fallen Star" />
