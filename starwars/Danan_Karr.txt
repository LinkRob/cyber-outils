'''Danan Karr''' was a location on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Carajam]], containing a [[spaceport]]. While being pursued by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] on Carajam, [[Zingo Gabnit]] contacted the [[Xan sisters]], who refused to rescue him unless he met them at Danan Karr.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />
