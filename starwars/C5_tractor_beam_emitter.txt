The '''C5 tractor beam emitter''' was a [[tractor beam projector]] used on the [[YT-1300 light freighter]]. A pair of them were installed in the mandibles and used to move cargo and assist in docking.<ref name="Owner" />

==Description==
The C5 was a [[Tractor beam projector|tractor beam emitter]] manufactured by [[Phylon Transport]] for the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]; they were standard equipment on board the company's [[YT-1300 light freighter|YT-1300]] model of [[light freighter]]. A pair of them were installed in opposing positions in the [[Starship|ship's]] forward mandibles and could be remotely controlled from the [[cockpit]],[[engineering]] stations or a dedicated [[Computer|console]] in the ship's freight-loading room. They had a limited range of 30 [[meter]]s and a limited [[targeting computer]] which made them unsuitable for military purposes such as the capture or damage of hostile ships, but they could easily lift up to 900 [[kilogram]]s of cargo making them perfect for hauling and ferrying [[cargo container]]s.<ref name="Owner" />

Despite its name, the C5 actually included a built-in [[tractor beam]] [[Power generator|generator]] as well as emitter which could work independently from the ship's [[Quadex power core]]. The generator contained two counter-rotating [[Force-field generator|force-field assemblies]], each lined with matter coils and field dispersion inhibitors which controlled the spread of the [[force field]]. The field generated in the assemblies was then focused through a pair of [[Focusing lens|focusing rings]] and projected from the main beam emitter. A number of smaller projectors next to the beam emitter projected a control pulse to further regulate the force field. The assembly included a control module between the generator and the emitter, with a maintenance override switch and removable panels for easy access to the system's inner circuits.<ref name="Owner" />
