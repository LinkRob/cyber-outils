[[Lady/Legends|Lady]] '''Felsing''' was a [[Wroonian/Legends|Wroonian]] noble, or so she claimed. In reality, she was a [[Con artist/Legends|con artist]]. Felsing hired a group of [[Spacer/Legends|spacers]] to rescue her son who had been captured by [[Omze'kehr Kahr]] aboard ''[[Omze's Incredible Traveling Starport/Legends|Omze's Incredible Traveling Starport]]''. However, there was no son, and she conned the spacers into breaking into Omze's secure vaults.
