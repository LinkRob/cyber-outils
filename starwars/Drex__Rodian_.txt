'''Drex''' was a [[Rodian]] [[pilot]] who worked for [[Crimson Dawn]]. 

==Biography==
[[File:QiraMostWanted-transparent.png|thumb|left|250px|Drex came into conflict with Qi'ra (pictured).]]
Drex was paired with [[Qorsha]] during the mission to steal and sabotage [[The Hammers (Syndicate)|the Hammer]]'s fleet in order to force them out of competition with Crimson Dawn. He returned to the ''[[First Light]]'' with the rest of the [[Thorum]] crew and continued to receiving training, including to learn how to fly shuttles. He co-piloted the ''[[Crimson Reach]]'' with [[Noyio]] during the mission to retrieve a [[Jedi]] artifact. 

He betrayed his fellow team members to help [[Cerveteri Slane]] get revenge on [[Qi'ra]], eliminating the [[Zabrak]] [[Hilst]]. While trying to [[Death|kill]] Qi'ra, he was stabbed by the [[Twi'lek]] [[Eleera Soi|Eleera]] while on the mission.<ref name="CC" />
