The '''DC-15A blaster carbine''', also referred to as the '''DC-15S blaster''', was a [[blaster carbine]] model used by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. It was part of the [[DC-15 family]]. While smaller than the larger [[DC-15A blaster rifle]], both could be referred to as [[blaster rifle]]s. It was one of the most common weapons issued to [[clone trooper]]s during the [[Clone Wars]] and were used during many battles. Clone [[stormtrooper]]s and other Imperial troops, such as [[TK stormtrooper]]s, continued to make use of the blaster throughout the early years of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Eventually, both variants of the DC-15A blaster rifles were phased out of Imperial military use, and were succeeded by the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]] and the [[DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle]].

==Description==
[[File:Jesse-TCWs4BR2.png|thumb|left|250px|DC-15A-blaster carbines fired blue energy bolts.]]
Part of the [[DC-15 family]],<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> the DC-15A [[blaster]] carbine had the same shot capacity as the [[DC-15A blaster rifle]], but a shorter range. One of the standard-issue weapons for the [[clone trooper]]s during the [[Clone Wars]], the DC-15A was a reliable blaster capable of both sustained fire and slower, long-range accuracy. The variable power output could be controlled by the [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]] and included a low-powered [[stun]] setting.<ref name="SW:AEYNtK" /> The blaster was fitted with a folding [[stock]] that could be extended for extra stability.<ref name="Monster">{{TCW|Monster}}</ref> The weapon could also be mounted on a tripod. DC-15A carbines could be outfitted with [[sniper]] [[scope]]s and also worked in conjunction with the [[Hologram|holographic]] data readouts inside a [[Clone trooper armor|clone's helmet]].<ref name="SW:AEYNtK" /> Clone troopers could also attach [[ascension cable]]s to their carbines to scale high walls.<ref name="TCW film" /> The carbine was able to shoot yellow bolts for training situations.<ref name="Brothers of the Broken Horn" />

==History==
Produced by BlasTech Industries,<ref name="Ultimate" /> the DC-15A blaster carbine saw heavy use during the Clone Wars as one of the main infantry weapons of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] as its clone troopers waged war with the [[Separatist Droid Army]] across the galaxy<ref name="TCW film" /> and defended Republic positions.<ref name="HS 5">[[Hyperspace Stories 5|''Hyperspace Stories'' 5]]</ref> It was also supplied to the [[Senate Guard]], seeing use by the elite [[Senate Commando]]s<ref name="Cloak of Darkness">{{TCW|Cloak of Darkness}}</ref> on the Republic [[capital]] world of [[Coruscant]]<ref name="Hostage Crisis" /> and during their offworld missions.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

After the rise of the Galactic Empire, the DC-15A saw extensive usage by Imperial troops.<ref name="Dark Lord 7" /> Clone troopers continued to use the blaster during the early years of the Empire,<ref name="Common Ground">{{TBB|Common Ground}}</ref> as well as their [[TK stormtrooper]] successors.<ref name="War-Mantle">{{TBB|War-Mantle}}</ref> In time, however, the [[E-10 blaster rifle]] and [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]] would emerge as the main Imperial infantry weaponry.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> The [[Partisans]] and [[Kashyyyk resistance]] made use of DC-15s during the [[liberation on Kashyyyk]].<ref name="JFO" /> Despite it being outdated, Captain [[Rex]] still continued to use one when he trained [[Ezra Bridger]] in blaster training.<ref name="Brothers of the Broken Horn" />
