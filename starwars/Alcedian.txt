'''Alcedians'''<ref name="Escape from Valo" /> were an [[Bird|avian]]<ref name="New Heroes" /> [[Sentience|sentient]]<ref name="Aphra 17" /> [[species]] of [[humanoid]]s<ref name="Escape from Valo" /> with [[Color|light gray]] skin<ref name="Aphra 17" /> that [[Homeworld|originated]] from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]. At one point the species had wings though by the [[time]] of the [[High Republic Era]], the wings had been lost but some kept some of their feathers on their shoulders and [[Limb|arms]]. During this time, two [[Jedi Initiate]]s&mdash;[[Kildo]] and [[Tep Tep]]&mdash;were members of the species.<ref name="Escape from Valo" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[archaeologist]] [[Kho Phon Farrus]] was a member of the species.<ref name="Aphra 17" />
