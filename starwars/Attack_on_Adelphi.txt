During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] '''attacked'''<ref name="Snackfrog of Doom" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Adelphi]]. The Nihil struck without warning and the [[Jedi Order]] was unable to arrive in time to stop them. Three [[Standard hour|hours]] after the attack [[Jedi Master]] [[Torban Buck]] asked a group of [[Jedi Initiate]]s to watch over a group of [[Youngling|children]] who had been separated from their caregivers. The Initiates decided to take the children to a [[Unidentified theater|theater]] where they watched the [[Holodrama|holofilm]] ''[[Socorro Jayme and the Starcave of Sharu]]''.<ref name="Snackfrog of Doom" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on Adelphi was first mentioned in "[[Three Younglings & the Snackfrog of Doom]]," a story included in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] ''[[The High Republic Adventures Phase III Annual]]'', which was published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Snackfrog of Doom" /> on [[January 22]], [[2025]],<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Blog/4192/cover-revealed-star-wars-the-high-republic-adventu|text=Cover Revealed: Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III Annual one-shot}}</ref> as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]. The story was written by [[Charles Soule]] and [[Rosemary Soule]].<ref name="Snackfrog of Doom" />
