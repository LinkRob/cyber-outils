{{Quote|Let's tell our stories about Andara and the ''Allegiance''. If this is going to be a funeral, we'll do it right.|Soran Keize, to the crew of the ''Allegiance'', ''Aerie'' and ''Edict''|<ref name="Shadow Fall" />}}
The '''''Allegiance''''' was a [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier]] which served in the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. In [[4 ABY]], it began serving the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] along with two other [[cruiser]]-[[carrier]]s. However, after the [[New Republic]] carried out a devastating [[Battle of Pandem Nai|attack]] on the 204th at its stronghold in the [[Pandem Nai system]], the ''Allegiance'' and fellow ''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser carrier ''[[Aerie]]'' escaped with much of the [[fighter wing]]'s personnel.

In [[5 ABY]], the ''Allegiance'' assisted in the 204th's [[Operation at Jarbanov|mission]] at the [[planet]] [[Jarbanov]], picking up the 204th's [[Squadron Four (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Four]] on the far side of [[Jarbanov's moon]] and escaping into [[hyperspace]]. Shortly after, the 204th [[Capture of the Edict|captured]] the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Edict]]'' from the [[New Republic]] in the [[Pormthulis system]]. To make the [[Star Destroyer]] hyperspace capable again, the ''Allegiance'' was sacrificed for parts, ultimately being stripped apart before the fighter wing left the [[Star system|system]].

==Description==
The ''Allegiance'' was a [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier]],<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> a [[cruiser]] and light [[carrier]] manufactured by [[SoroSuub Corporation]]<ref name="Encyclopedia Starfighters">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref> which could carry [[starfighter]]s.<ref name="Quasar DB">{{Databank|quasar-fire-class-cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-Class Cruiser-Carrier}}</ref> The cruiser-carrier was equipped with a [[hyperdrive]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==History==
===Service===
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the ''Allegiance'' served in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy|navy]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date 2">{{AlphabetSquadronDate}}</ref> it was one of three ''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carriers acquired by [[Colonel]] [[Shakara Nuress]] after her unit, the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], occupied the [[planet]] [[Pandem Nai]] to use as its base of operations. The cruiser-carriers were used to transport select [[TIE series|TIE]] [[squadron]]s from the 204th to carry out missions to stop the [[New Republic]]'s reconnaissance efforts. However, the 204th's base soon came under [[Battle of Pandem Nai|attack]] from a New Republic [[Barma Battle Group|battle group]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> The ''Allegiance''<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> and the ''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier ''[[Aerie]]'' were forced to flee the system with many of the 204th's personnel after the [[fighter wing]] suffered a number of losses, including Nuress.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

After Pandem Nai, the 204th fell under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of special advisor [[Major]] [[Soran Keize]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the fighter wing traveled to the barren world [[Jarbanov]], home to a [[Jarbanov colony|colony]] which processed junk from the planet's [[orbit]]ting junk rings. The 204th's [[Squadron Four (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Four]] [[Operation at Jarbanov|attacked]] the colony while the ''Aerie'' salvaged wreckage from the junk rings. Meanwhile, the ''Allegiance'' awaited at the far side of [[Jarbanov's moon]]. As planned, Squadron Four rendezvoused with the cruiser-carrier, which escaped into [[hyperspace]] immediately after. Following the operation, the ''Allegiance''{{'s}} [[captain]], [[Rogart Styll]], attended a meeting with Keize, the 204th's six [[squadron commander]]s and the acting commander of the ''Aerie'' to discuss their mission outcome and the fighter wing's next course of action.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

A short while later, the 204th [[Capture of the Edict|captured]] the<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="New Villain">{{SW|url=news/shadow-fall-novel-first-look-at-new-villain-and-more|text=''Shadow Fall'' Novel: First Look at New Villain and More}}</ref> ''[[Edict]]'' from [[New Republic]] occupation in the [[Pormthulis system]]. The ''Edict'' lacked a working hyperdrive or the resources to repair it, so Keize decided that the ''Allegiance'' would be sacrificed to fulfill this need. The decision came without question, and the cruiser-carrier's engine was disabled and disassembled to repair the ''Edict'', the parts being guided down the zero-[[gravity]] corridors and out of the vessel through the hangar to reach the [[Star Destroyer]]'s maw. The repairs took within six [[Standard hour|hours]], and Keize had the 204th's [[Squadron Three (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Three]] strip apart the ''Allegiance'' after. Styll objected to the obliteration of his cruiser-carrier, but his sentiment was overridden.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

===Legacy===
The ''Edict'' and ''Aerie'' exited into hyperspace from the Pormthulis [[Star system|system]] following the ''Allegiance''{{'s}} destruction. Styll, having lost his cruiser-carrier, was given command of the ''Edict''. Keize held a celebration to welcome the remaining crew of the ''Edict'' into the 204th, which ended up with him and the crew of the ''Allegiance'', ''Edict'' and ''Aerie''. The major advised the crewmembers to tell stories of the ''Allegiance'' and the<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> astronomical object<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[Andara]], which had reportedly fallen from Imperial control to New Republic hands. One of the late ''Allegiance''{{'s}} crew told a story of repairing a power conduit during the attack on Pandem Nai.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

Keize soon proposed a new mission to the squadron commanders and senior staff of the ''Aerie'' and ''Edict'': an intervention with the New Republic's [[Cerberon system campaign|invasion]] of the [[Cerberon system]]. The mission was approved, and the 204th [[Attack on Cerberon|attacked]] the New Republic [[New Republic Defense Force|forces]] there, where the ''Edict'' and ''Aerie'' were lost.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
