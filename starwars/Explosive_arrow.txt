An '''explosive arrow''' was an [[arrow]] that could explode. [[Jannah]] and her fellow members of [[Company 77]] made explosive arrows from [[sturm dowel]]s while on [[Kef Bir]]. They were then marked with pigment and used only in times of extreme need, when they would be fired from [[Jannah's energy bow]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
