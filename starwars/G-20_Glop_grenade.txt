The '''G-20 Glop grenade''' was a reusable, non-[[Death|lethal]] [[grenade]] that produced by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]] The grenades cost 100 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] per unit and were often used by [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement agencies]]. The grenades released a foam that hardened on contact with [[oxygen]], thereby trapping anyone caught in the blast.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
