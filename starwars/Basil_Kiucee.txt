[[Admiral]] '''Basil Kiucee''' was a [[Mon Calamari]] member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Between [[2 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]], when the [[Galactic Empire]] landed on the [[planet]] [[Joetz]], the location of a [[Unidentified rebel base (Crane)|Rebel base]], Kiucee contacted [[Princess]] [[Crane (princess)|Crane]] while she [[Reconnaissance mission on Joetz|investigated]] the Empire's movements. The admiral advised her to slow down her [[Crane's speeder|speeder]] and stop her [[music]]. When Crane's [[astromech droid]] [[R9-TR2]] detected signs indicating there were survivors in a [[Unidentified village (Joetz)|village]] destroyed by the Empire, Kiucee ordered Crane to return, but she refused, ending their communication and rescuing the [[Gigoran]] [[Youngling|child]] [[Woopas]]. Crane contacted Kiucee again to inform them she had discovered the Empire was [[mining]] [[Coaxium]] on Joetz, after which she destroyed the Empire's [[Dual excavator drill-equipped mining crawler|mining crawler]]. When they spoke again, Kiucee worried about Woopas and praised Crane for denying the Empire the coaxium despite the loss of the rebel base.

==Biography==
{{Quote|There could be survivors. I'm going in.''"<br />"''It's too dangerous. And you can't trust that piece of junk's old sensors. I must insist you return&hellip;|Crane and Basil Kiucee|<ref name="Four Wings" />}}
[[File:CraneKiuceeTorTu-TheSongOfFourWings.png|thumb|right|300px|Kiucee speaks with Crane via hologram.]]
Basil Kiucee was a [[Mon Calamari]] who served as an [[admiral]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Four Wings" /> Between [[2 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VisionsDate|302}}</ref> Kiucee was part of a group of rebels based on the [[Water|snowy]] [[planet]] [[Joetz]]. When the [[Galactic Empire]]'s forces landed near the planet's pole, the rebels began to evacuate their [[Unidentified rebel base (Crane)|base]] while [[Princess]] [[Crane (princess)|Crane]] set out on [[Reconnaissance mission on Joetz|reconnaissance]] to investigate Imperial movements. As Crane arrived near a coastline on her [[Crane's speeder|speeder]], Kiucee contacted her via a [[hologram]] projected by her [[astromech droid]], [[R9-TR2]]. Kiucee told Crane not to listen to [[music]] and warned her to slow down due to the loud noise of her [[engine]]s. She acknowledged Kicuee's orders, and the admiral then reminded Crane to retreat at the first sign of danger, addressing her as "Princess." In response, Crane told Kiucee not to use her title, as her [[Kingdom of Biwaji|kingdom]] had long been [[Destruction of the Kingdom of Biwaji|destroyed]] and she wanted to leave the past behind. Kiucee responded by suggesting she upgrade her [[droid]], causing Crane to promise she would not, explaining he was the only one who could play her music.<ref name="Four Wings" />

Crane soon reported to Kiucee that she had found a [[Unidentified village (Joetz)|village]] that the Empire had destroyed, and they informed her they would make plans to retreat. Kiucee ordered Crane to return to their base, but when R9-TR2 detected signs indicating there were survivors in the village, she insisted on investigating. Although Kiucee attempted to warn Crane of the danger, adding that the droid's old [[sensor]]s could not be trusted, Crane ignored them and turned off the hologram. After she rescued the [[Gigoran]] [[Youngling|child]] [[Woopas]], Crane contacted Kiucee again and reported that she had learned the Empire had come to Joetz to [[Mining|mine]] a vein of [[coaxium]] and had no knowledge of the rebel base. Their conversation was interrupted when Crane was attacked by [[AT-ATQ]] [[walker]]s, which she destroyed alongside the Empire's [[Dual excavator drill-equipped mining crawler|mining crawler]] with the help of R9-TR2 and Woopas.<ref name="Four Wings" />

After, Kiucee contacted Crane via hologram again while she was aboard her [[Crane's YT-2400 light freighter|YT-2400 light freighter]]. Noticing she was humming, Kiucee asked her about it, and she responded that it was an old song from her kingdom. The admiral noted she had rescued Woopas and was concerned he was alone, but Crane assured Kiucee they did not need to worry. They then lamented the loss of their base, but praised Crane for depriving the Empire of a hyperfuel source. Kiucee then looked toward Woopas as he [[Telekinesis|levitated]] a [[paper crane]] with [[the Force]].<ref name="Four Wings" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|There you are. Are you all right? Tell me you didn't do anything reckless.|Basil Kiucee, to Crane|<ref name="Four Wings" />}}
Basil Kiucee was a Mon Calamari with [[Color|orange]] skin and yellow [[eye]]s.<ref name="Concept4" /> They were brusque<ref name="Getty">{{Getty|subdomain=assets.dam|url=s75j56ckkw879rfqfnxr49s/v/1291165528/original/VISIONS%20SEASON%203%20Final%20Production%20Brief.pdf?Expires=1761418361&KeyName=gcs-bfldr-prod&Signature=yVNCOpLKX0CT4Ufu0FjQLOANhQs=|text=VISIONS SEASON 3 Final Production Brief|date=2025-10-22}}</ref> and showed concern for for Crane's safety. They also criticized R9-TR2 due to its age and did not want Crane to risk searching the destroyed village for survivors.<ref name="Four Wings" />

==Equipment==
Kiucee wore a uniform consisting of light blue [[clothing]] worn under a white top with black around the neck. They wore white [[boot]]s and a white [[belt]] around their waist.<ref name="Concept4" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BasilKiucee-KobayashiMisunoConcept.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Concept art by by Hiroyasu Kobayashi and Shinya Mizuno]]
Basil Kiucee first appeared in a hologram in "[[The Song of Four Wings]]," a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume ]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Project Studio Q]], released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Four Wings" /> on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Cast">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-3-cast-announce|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 3 Cast Announced with New Trailer}}</ref> Before the release of the short, they were first mentioned in a [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]] [[StarWars.com]] article released on [[September 26]], 2025.<ref name="JPN Cast">{{Disney|subdomain=starwars|domain=co.jp|url=news/20250926_01|text=日本語オリジナル版＜本予告＞が全世界解禁！『スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ』Volume3|date=2026-09-26}}</ref> The English spelling of Kiucee was revealed in a production brief released on [[October 23]] of that year,<ref name="Getty" /> and the spelling of their full name was confirmed in the episode's credits. Kiucee was voiced by [[Hiroki Yasumoto]] in the original Japanese and by [[Trevor Devall]] in the [[Wikipedia:English language|English]] dub.<ref name="Four Wings" /> Concept art of the character was created by [[Hiroyasu Kobayashi]] and [[Shinya Mizuno]].<ref name="Concept4" />
