A list of events in '''1977''' related to ''Star Wars''.

==January==
*[[January 6|06]] &ndash; [[Genevieve O'Reilly]] is born.
*[[January 11|11]] &ndash; [[Mark Hamill]] is injured in a car accident, requiring facial reconstructive surgery.
*[[January 29|29]] &ndash; [[Anna Acton]] is born.
*[[January 31|31]] &ndash; [[Bobby Moynihan]] is born.

==March==
*[[March 16|16]] &ndash; [[Ayesha Dharker]] is born.

==April==
*[[April 12|12]] &ndash; [[Star Wars (1977) 1|''Star Wars'' (1977) 1]] published. The cover date is July.
*[[April 14|14]] &ndash; [[Sarah Michelle Gellar]] is born.
*[[April 20|20]] &ndash; [[Justin Ponsor]] is born.
*[[April 22|22]] &ndash; [[Industrial Light & Magic|ILM]] concludes filming for [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] with a [[Star Destroyer/Legends|Star Destroyer]] shot (shot 110P).
*[[April 23|23]] &ndash; [[Neil Hellegers]] is born.

==May==
*[[May 1|01]] &ndash; The first public preview screening of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|Star Wars]] takes place in San Francisco.
*[[May 10|10]] &ndash; [[Star Wars (1977) 2|''Star Wars'' (1977) 2]] published. The cover date is August.
*[[May 12|12]] &ndash; [[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|''Star Wars: From the Adventures of Luke Skywalker'']] re-released.
*[[May 15|15]] &ndash; [[Mel Zwyer]] is born.
*15 &ndash; [[Wikipedia:The New York Times|''The New York Times'']] published the first advertisements for [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'']], only ten days before its premiere.
*[[May 23|23]] &ndash; [[Richard Ayoade]] is born.
*[[May 25|25]] &ndash; [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|'''''Star Wars''''': '''Episode IV''' '''''A New Hope''''']] released under the title '''''Star Wars'''''.

==June==
*[[June 7|07]] &ndash; [[Star Wars (1977) 3|''Star Wars'' (1977) 3]] published. The cover date is September.

==July==
*[[July 1|01]] &ndash; [[Steve Argyle]] is born.
*[[July 6|06]] &ndash; [[Bradford Young]] is born.
*[[July 9|9]] &ndash; [[Ian Doescher]] is born.
*[[July 10|10]] &ndash; [[Gwendoline Yeo]] is born.
*[[July 12|12]] &ndash; [[Star Wars (1977) 4|''Star Wars'' (1977) 4]] published. The cover date is October.
*[[July 13|13]] &ndash; [[Kari Wahlgren]] is born.
*[[July 31|31]] &ndash; [[Leinil Francis Yu]] is born.
*?? &ndash; [[Marvel Special Edition Featuring Star Wars 1|''Marvel Special Edition Featuring Star Wars'' 1]] published.

==August==
*[[August 10|10]] &ndash; [[Star Wars (1977) 5|''Star Wars'' (1977) 5]] published. The cover date is November.
*[[August 13|13]] &ndash; [[Euan Morton]] is born.
*[[August 26|26]] &ndash; [[Patrick Kennedy]] is born.
*[[August 30|30]] &ndash; [[Nalini Krishan]] is born.

==September==
*[[September 1|01]] &ndash; [[Adrienne Wilkinson]] is born.
*[[September 6|06]] &ndash; [[Bertie Carvel]] is born.
*[[September 13|13]] &ndash; [[Star Wars (1977) 6|''Star Wars'' (1977) 6]] published. The cover date is December.
*[[September 15|15]] &ndash; [[Tom Hardy]] is born.
*[[September 16|16]] &ndash; ''[[The Making of Star Wars]]'' aired.
*[[September 22|22]] &ndash; [[Pizzazz 1|''Pizzazz'' 1]] published.

==October==
*[[October 8|8]] &ndash; [[Duncan Pow]] is born.
*[[October 11|11]] &ndash; [[Star Wars (1977) 7|''Star Wars'' (1977) 7]] published. The cover date is January.
*[[October 19|19]] &ndash; [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'']] released as ''La Guerre des Etoiles'' in France.
*[[October 20|20]] &ndash; [[Sam Witwer]] is born.
*20 &ndash; [[Pizzazz 2|''Pizzazz'' 2]] published.
*[[October 21|21]] &ndash; [[Delilah S. Dawson]] is born.
*21 &ndash; ''[[The Star Wars Sketchbook]]'' published.
*[[October 27|27]] &ndash; [[Mat Lucas]] is born.
*?? &ndash; ''[[Star Wars Official Poster Monthly 1]]'' published.
*?? &ndash; [[Marvel Special Edition Featuring Star Wars 2|''Marvel Special Edition Featuring Star Wars'' 2]] published.

==November==
*[[November 8|8]] &ndash; [[Star Wars (1977) 8|''Star Wars'' (1977) 8]] published. The cover date is February.
*[[November 17|17]] &ndash; [[Pizzazz 3|''Pizzazz'' 3]] published.
*[[November 22|22]] &ndash; [[Torstein Nordstrand]] is born.
*[[November 23|23]] &ndash; [[Lateef Crowder]] is born.
*?? &ndash; ''[[Star Wars Blueprints]]'' published.
*?? &ndash; [[Star Wars Official Poster Monthly 2|''Star Wars Official Poster Monthly'' 2]] published.
*?? &ndash; ''[[The Marvel Comics Illustrated Version of Star Wars]]'' published.
*?? &ndash; ''[[The Star Wars Album]]'' published.

==December==
*[[December 13|13]] &ndash; [[Star Wars (1977) 9|''Star Wars'' (1977) 9]] published. The cover date is March.
*[[December 21|21]] &ndash; [[Jacopo Camagni]] is born.
*[[December 22|22]] &ndash; [[Jarrett J. Krosoczka]] is born.
*[[December 25|25]] &ndash; [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'']] released as ''La Guerra de las Galaxias'' in [[Wikipedia:Mexico|Mexico]].
*?? &ndash; [[Pizzazz 4|''Pizzazz'' 4]] published.
*?? &ndash; [[Star Wars Official Poster Monthly 3|''Star Wars Official Poster Monthly'' 3]] published.

==Unknown==
*[[Beau Willimon]] is born.
*[[Del Rey]] is founded.
*[[Klutz]] is founded.
*[[Famous Monsters Star Wars Spectacular|''Famous Monsters: Star Wars Spectacular'']] published.
