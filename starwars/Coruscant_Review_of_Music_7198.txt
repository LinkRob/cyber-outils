'''Coruscant Review of Music #7198''' was an issue of the periodical publication ''[[Coruscant]] review of Music''. The issue discussed [[Grand Admiral]] [[Balanhai Savit]]'s family of musicians as well as his own notable composition of the grand [[opera]] ''[[To the Stars]]'' which brought him to [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]'s attention. The issue was later used by [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] as a source for his [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', being mentioned in the chapter "The New Order".<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Coruscant Review of Music 7198 was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]], which was published by [[Dorling Kindersley]] in [[July 9]] [[2024]].<ref name="Rise" />
