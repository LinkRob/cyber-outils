'''Cabirium''' was one of the rarest [[Ore/Legends|ores]] in [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]]. It had electromagnetic properties, and was extremely hard. However, it was very sensitive to [[Blaster/Legends|blaster]] bolts, and would be consumed by the shots.<ref name="CB66">{{CasusBelliCite|66|Le trésor oublié}}</ref>

A gigantic block was hid by the [[Kanduh]] on an unidentified [[Planet/Legends|planet]] in the [[Halawee system]] millennia before the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]. The [[Imperial Intelligence/Legends|Imperial Intelligence]] manipulated the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in order to discover the ore for the benefit of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. Unfortunately for the Imperials, the Rebels managed to destroy the treasure.<ref name="CB66" />
