The '''Collector Set''' was the name given to a set of two weapons, an [[Arc Caster]] and a [[Pulse Blaster]], utilized by the team of [[Jawa]] brothers known collectively as the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Utooni]] that they could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to their [[Suspicious Set|standard set]]. These [[weapon]]s featured a metallic [[Color|purple]], orange, gold, white, and black color scheme, with additional dangling silver tubes, as well as glowing orange cores.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Collector Set was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Utooni's Suspicious Set, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Gonk Collectors]].<ref name="Hunters" />
