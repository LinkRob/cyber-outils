'''''Corellian Grafitti''''' was a narrative media that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> Around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Beyond Victory Date">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> a small [[viewscreen]] in the quarters of the [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Volo Bolus]] featured a synopsis of the [[Holodrama|holo drama]] ''[[Space Wars]]'' in [[Aurebesh]] with an image of [[Kane (Space Wars)|Kane]] and [[Taryn]]&mdash;the two fictional characters from the drama&mdash;along with quoted reviews by the entities [[Space Wars Explained]] and [[Holopedia]]. In Holopedia's quote, ''Corellian Grafitti'' was used as a comparison to ''Space Wars'' for its deep and detailed worldbuilding.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />

==Behind the scenes==
''Corellian Grafitti'' was first mentioned in the [[2025]] [[Wikipedia:Virtual reality|virtual reality]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]], developed by [[ILM Immersive]] and published by [[Disney Electronic Content]]. While the holo drama was mentioned in Aurebesh via an interactable viewscreen, its capitalization was not given.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />
