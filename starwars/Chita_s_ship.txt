'''Chita's ship'''<ref name="Focus">{{SWYouTube|series=Star Wars: Visions Filmmaker Focus|text=THE SMUGGLER|video=DeV1njld32k}}</ref> was a [[YV-865 Aurore-class freighter]] owned by the [[smuggler]] [[Chita]]. When the [[planet]] [[Zenarita]] was [[Occupation of Zenarita|occupied]] by the [[Galactic Empire]], Chita was hired to bring the planet's [[prince]], [[Arluu]], to safety. Along with the former [[Jedi]] [[Gleenu]], they escaped to the [[freighter]] and used it to travel to<ref name="Smuggler" /> the [[moon]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Yavin 4]].<ref name="Smuggler" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chita's ship appeared in "[[The Smuggler (short film)|The Smuggler]]," a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Trigger (animation studio)|Trigger]], was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Smuggler" /> on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="ReleaseDate">{{SW|url=news/why-we-love-star-wars-visions|text=Why We Love the Unique Stories of ''Star Wars: Visions''}}</ref>
