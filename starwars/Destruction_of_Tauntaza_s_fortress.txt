Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and his allies launched an effort to '''destroy''' a [[Tauntaza's fortress|mobile fortress]] created by [[Governor]] [[Tauntaza]]. Tauntaza had retreated within into the fortress to begin sapping all life from the [[planet]] [[Gabredor III]], using the gathered energy to power a [[Tauntaza's armor|suit of power armor]] designed to stand against Vader.<ref name="Vader 27" /> Although Vader assumed Tauntaza was a rogue agent acting on behalf of the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate,<ref name="Vader 25">[[Darth Vader (2020) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 25]]</ref> she was actually following orders directly from Vader's [[Sith Master]], [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Vader 27" />
