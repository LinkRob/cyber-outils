An '''Anaxes ruffian jacket''' was a type of [[jacket]] worn by common outlaws<ref name="Outlaws" />  on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|fort-anaxes|Fort Anaxes}}</ref> [[Anaxes]].<ref name="Outlaws" />  The [[Color|tan]] jacket had a tall collar, large pockets on its lower-right side, and a grey rectangle on its upper-left chest. The jacket also included a pair of protective grey [[glove]]s and a grey [[Clothing|shirt]] that was worn underneath it.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime [[syndicate]] had at least one jacket stored at their [[Crimson Dawn Hideout (Grazing Fields)|hideout]] in the [[Grazing Fields]] region of [[Toshara]], a [[moon]] also located in the Outer Rim.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> part of a manifest that mentioned the jacket had been stolen from the hideout and ended up in the [[black market]] shop of [[Lalini Ledeno]], a [[Twi'lek]] [[merchant]] associated with the syndicate.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] had learned the "I'm a friend of Arlo" password that Ledeno used to restrict access to his shop and purchased the manifest for 250 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. She eventually traveled to the Crimson Dawn hideout where she stole the jacket after it had been loaded aboard a [[Sentinel-class landing craft]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
An Anaxes ruffian jacket appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. When worn, the jacket increased the ammo capacity of weapons picked up by the players.<ref name="Outlaws" />
