'''E-5s sniper rifles''' were a model of [[sniper rifle]] and [[longblaster]] used by [[Sniper|sharpshooters]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="Battlefront II" /> and the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="GD" /> They saw action under the [[Separatist Droid Army]] during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Battlefront II" /> on the [[planet]] [[Felucia]].<ref name="CL2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> To target opponents attempting to take cover, a [[B1-series battle droid]] took up position and used the [[rifle]] from a distance.<ref name="FFGweb">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/10/30/new-models|text=New Models}}</ref> A tubular device was used to connected the rifle's targeting scope to a [[battle droid]]'s [[photoreceptor]]s, which saw through [[Color|red]] [[electrobinoculars]].<ref name="Legion">{{Legion|set=B1 Battle Droids Upgrade Expansion|cardname=E-5s B1 Trooper}}</ref>

The rifle was also used by the Empire during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="GD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The E-5s sniper rifle first appeared in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2014]] mobile [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''.<ref name="GD" /> The rifle originally appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in "[[Chapter 3 (Star Wars: Clone Wars)|Chapter 3]]" of the ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' television series,<ref name="CW">{{CW|3}}</ref> which was released on [[November 11]], [[2003]].<ref name="TV.com">{{TVcom|url=shows/star-wars-clone-wars/chapter-iii-276609|text=Star Wars: Clone Wars - Season 1, Episode 3: Chapter III}}</ref> The rifle was first identified in Legends in the [[2004]] ''[[Star Wars: Battlefront]]'' strategy guide, ''[[Star Wars Battlefront: Prima Official Game Guide]]''.<ref name="Prima">[[Star Wars Battlefront: Prima Official Game Guide|''Star Wars Battlefront'': Prima Official Game Guide]]</ref>
