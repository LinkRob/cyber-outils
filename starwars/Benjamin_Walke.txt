'''Benjamin Walke''' is the Global Community Lead who serves for Star Wars [[Star Wars video games|video game]]s developed by [[Electronic Arts|EA]] and [[DICE]]. Walke was appointed because the former community manager, Mat Everett, left after deciding to move out of the video games industry.
