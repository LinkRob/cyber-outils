The '''clone DNA room''' was a chamber in [[Tipoca City]] [[Tipoca City Military Complex|Military Complex]] where [[DNA]] samples of [[Jango Fett]] used in [[cloning]] the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] were stored. During the [[Battle of Kamino]], [[Asajj Ventress]] was sent to the room to steal a sample of the [[clone template]], but was thwarted by [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="ARC Troopers" />
