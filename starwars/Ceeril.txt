'''Ceeril''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Skembo]] who served as the [[Ambassador]] of [[Rion]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. While being treated for injuries at the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]], Ceeril attempted to frame some members of the [[Hassarian]] [[species]], who were rivals of the Skembo, in an [[assassin]]ation attempt on him, utilizing his [[Ceeril's bodyguard droid|personal]] [[bodyguard droid]]. However, the Ambassador's deception was uncovered by [[Administrator]] [[Velko Jahen]], and Ceeril was placed under arrest.<ref name="First Duty" />
