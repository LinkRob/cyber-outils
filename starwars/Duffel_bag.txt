A '''duffel bag''' was a device that could used to store items. While disguised as a tourist on [[Sesid]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[commando]] [[Lokmarcha]] was forced to hide his weapons and gear inside a duffel bag.<ref name="MT">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref> [[Col Takbright]] left his [[homeworld]] of [[Uquine]] with some [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and a duffel bag of possessions, eventually joining the Rebellion and becoming a [[pilot]].<ref name="Duty Roster">{{StoryCite|story=Duty Roster|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>
