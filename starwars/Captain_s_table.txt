The '''captain's table''' was an exclusive table found in the [[Crown of Corellia Dining Room]] onboard the ''[[Halcyon]]''. Guests allowed to dine at this table in the center of the [[Restaurant|supper club]] had access to extra courses, which included the [[Riyola Keevan|captain's]] favorite dishes.<ref name="Dining">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/dining|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Dining}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Crown of Corellia Dining Room was revealed to be the main dining area for guests of [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel. For an extra fee, guests could eat at the Captain's table.
