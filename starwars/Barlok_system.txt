The '''Barlok system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Marcol sector]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It fell within grid square L-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained [[Barlok]], a terrestrial [[planet]] known for its advanced horticultural techniques. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] visited Barlok during a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he made<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> buying bushels of the [[Homeworld|native]] [[prisht-fruit]] while he was there.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Barlok system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Barlok system/Legends|Barlok system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it appeared in the [[2006]] novel [[Outbound Flight (novel)|''Outbound Flight'']], which was written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Outbound">[[Outbound Flight (novel)|''Outbound Flight'']]</ref>
