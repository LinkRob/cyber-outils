The '''Anx''' [[language]] was the native tongue of the [[Anx]] [[species]] and was impossible for any other species to produce, often causing outsiders intense discomfort when spoken. Because of this, the language was rarely spoken off of the Anx [[homeworld]] of [[Gravlex Med]], a [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Savage" /> although, in [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date Part 3">{{LRMDate|3}}</ref> the criminal [[Jaylen Vrax]] passed an individual speaking it in [[Kristom]], the second largest urban district on the [[Colonies]] planet [[Giju]].<ref name="LRM">''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon]]''</ref> In the right conditions, the low, rumbling voices of the Anx could carry for [[kilometer]]s, allowing them to be heard across vast cave networks or the open air of their homeworlds savannahs.<ref name="Savage" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Anx language was first mentioned in ''[[Savage Spirits]]'', a [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement for ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Savage" /> It then appeared in the [[2026]] novel ''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon]]'', which was written by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]].<ref name="LRM" /> The [[Anx (language)|language]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in "[[The University of Sanbra Guide to Intelligent Life: The Anx]]," a ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' article written by [[Craig Robert Carey]], [[Jason Fry]], and [[Daniel Wallace]] and published by [[Wizards of the Coast]] in the magazine [[Star Wars Gamer 7|''Star Wars Gamer'' 7]]<ref name="SWG7">{{GamerCite|7|The University of Sanbra Guide to Intelligent Life: The Anx}}</ref> on [[November 6]], [[2001]]<ref name="SW.com">{{SWArchive|url=gaming/other/rpg/news20011018.html|text=Live on the Fringe: ''Gamer'' # 7}}</ref>
