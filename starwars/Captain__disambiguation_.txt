'''Captain''' was a commonly used [[military rank]] and title.

==Canon==
===Ranks and titles===
*'''[[Captain]]'''&mdash; a [[military rank]] used in [[Army|armies]] and [[Navy|navies]] in various militaries, as well as a civilian position for the main [[pilot]] or owner of a [[starship]].
*'''[[Captain of the Guard]]'''&mdash; a rank, position, or title of the leader of a group of [[bodyguard]]s.
*'''[[Captain of the Line]]'''&mdash; a type of naval rank.
*'''[[Captain-Inspector]]'''&mdash; a rank that was used by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s investigative division, the [[Inspectorate]].
*'''[[Clone Captain]]'''&mdash; a rank used by [[clone trooper officer]]s of the [[Republic Military]]. It was placed above [[Clone Lieutenant]] and below [[Clone Commander]].
*'''[[Flag Captain]]'''&mdash; a senior [[Imperial officer|officer]] rank that was used by the individual who captained an [[admiral]]'s [[flagship]].
*'''[[Fleet Captain]]'''&mdash; a naval rank used by the [[Mon Calamari]] captains of the [[Mon Cala Mercantile Fleet]].
*'''[[Gunnery captain]]'''&mdash; a naval rank used by [[gunnery officer]]s.
*'''[[Junior Captain]]'''&mdash; a naval rank used by the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]]. They commanded small Chiss [[starship]]s, and were below the rank of Mid Captain.
*'''[[Mid Captain]]'''&mdash; a naval rank used by the [[Chiss]] Expansionary Defense [[Fleet]]. They served as the [[executive officer]] of a Senior Captain.
*'''[[Senior Captain]]'''&mdash; a naval rank used by the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet. They served as the first officer of an admiral, or commanded their own [[heavy cruiser]]s.

===Models===
*'''[[Commando droid captain]]'''&mdash; a superior variant of the [[BX-series droid commando]] used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].
