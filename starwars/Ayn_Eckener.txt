'''Ayn Eckener''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Naboo (people)/Legends|citizen]] of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]] who lived during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], sometime following the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]] in [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]].<ref name="SWG" /> She shared the same surname as [[Karl Eckener|Karl]] and [[Hugo Eckener/Legends|Hugo Eckener]], prominent members of Naboo society.

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ayn Eckener appears in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Roleplaying game|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']]. However, she never actually appeared on the [[Star Wars video games|video game]]'s live servers as a proper [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]]. According to these files, Ayn Eckener would have been on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]]. Additionally, an emulated depiction pulled from this character's Interchange File Format reveals what she would have looked like if she were added to the game.
