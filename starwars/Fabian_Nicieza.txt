'''Fabian Nicieza''' (born on [[December 31]], [[1960s|1961]])<ref name="Birthdate" /> is an [[Wikipedia:Argentinian|Argentinian]]-[[Wikipedia:American|American]] best known as co-creator of [[Marvel Comics|Marvel's]] [[Wikipedia:Deadpool|Deadpool]] character.<ref name="Nationality" /> He wrote the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story "[[The Secret of Tet-Ami]]" for [[Dark Horse Comics]], which was featured in the [[Star Wars Tales 13|thirteenth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Tales]]'' comic series,<ref name="Secret" /> published on [[September 25]], [[2002]].<ref name="Tales13date">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/11-898/Star-Wars-Tales-13|text=Star Wars Tales #13}}</ref>
