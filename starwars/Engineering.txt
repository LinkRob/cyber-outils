'''Engineering''' was one of eight major departments that made up the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Battle Station Operations]] on board the [[planet]]-[[Death|killing]] [[superweapon]], the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Dawn" /> The [[Death Star]] was later destroyed during the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Engineering was first mentioned in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2018]].<ref name="Dawn" />
