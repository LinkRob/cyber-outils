'''Escarte''' was a [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Rise" /> in the [[Escarte system]] within the [[Keldrath sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It was situated in grid square T-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Escarte was a strategic world and stronghold for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]],<ref name="Separatist Ships">{{EncyclopediaCite|Republic and Separatist Ships (1)}}</ref> situated near<ref name="Appendix" /> the [[Perlemian Trade Route]] where the [[Commerce Guild]] maintained their major interests. Accordingly, the elite [[Escarte Guard]] served as [[bodyguard]]s for high-ranking Guild personnel. Around of the time of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and Separatist Alliance<ref name="Rise" /> that took place between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> where the Guild sided with the Confederacy, Escarte was placed under the protection of several [[Diamond-class cruiser|''Diamond''-class cruisers]] and [[Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class light destroyers]], which were also supported by Guild [[Commerce Guild corvette|corvettes]] and [[Commerce Guild bomber|bombers]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Escarte was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Escarte/Legends|Escarte]] first appeared as an [[Asteroid/Legends|asteroid]] colony in ''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]'', a [[2005]] novel authored by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Labyrinth">''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]''</ref> While the [[2021]] reference booklet "[[Republic and Separatist Ships (1)]]" place Escarte on the Perlemian Trade Route,<ref name="Separatist Ships" /> the [[2025]] "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix instead placed the Escarte system in grid square T-6,<ref name="Appendix" /> matching its Legends placement.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> As grid square T-6 is a significant distance from the Perlemian according to the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]], this article assumes "Republic and Separatist Ships (1)" to be in error.<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref>
