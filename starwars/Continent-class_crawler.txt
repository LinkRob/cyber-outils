The '''''Continent''-class crawler''' was a [[starship]] class used by the [[Kingdom of Shu-Torun|Kingdom]] of [[Shu-Torun]]. The [[Leviathan (Continent-class crawler)|''Leviathan'']] was [[Battle for Jedha|deployed]] against [[Jedha]] in [[1 ABY]].

==Details==
{{Quote|The ''Leviathan'' is armed like a fortress and has the population of a small city. We'd need the power of a Star Destroyer to even ''dent'' it.|Leia Organa|<ref name="Star Wars 42" />}}
The ''Continent''-class crawler was a colossal [[starship]]<ref name="Star Wars 40" /> and [[orbital drill]] used to strip-mine on a massive scale. The size of a [[city]],<ref name="Star Wars 41" /> the vessel also had the population of a small city, and was staffed by a great number of [[droid]]s including [[Loader droid|loadlifter droids]].<ref name="Star Wars 42" /> It was equipped with numerous [[drill]]s and other mining equipment capable of peeling the mantle of a [[moon]], and was towed into orbit over low-gravity moons by [[Spacetug|tugs]]. Its exterior hull was fitted with cannon emplacements,<ref name="Star Wars 40" /> [[ion torpedo]]s,<ref name="Star Wars 43" /> and anti-air weapons, arming the vessel like a fortress,<ref name="Star Wars 42" /> and its [[armor]] could withstand [[mine]]s. A series of treads beneath the vessel allowed it to cross planetary surfaces while a front-mounted drill strip-mined the terrain ahead.<ref name="Star Wars 41" /> It was protected by a core security system, and featured cargo bays and top-mounted landing platforms.<ref name="Star Wars 42" />

==History==
{{Quote|It's like...a huge meteor. But it's too slow. What ''is'' it?|Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Star Wars 41" />}}
''Continent''-class crawlers were used by [[Kingdom of Shu-Torun|Kingdom]] of [[Shu-Torun]] and their [[miner]]s for offworld excavations of low-[[gravity]] [[moon]]s. [[Queen]] [[Trios]] claimed it could peel a [[planet]]'s mantle.<ref name="Star Wars 40" />

After [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebels]] overran the [[drill citadel]] on the moon of [[Jedha]], Trios brought in the ''Continent''-class crawler [[Leviathan (Continent-class crawler)|''Leviathan'']] to continue strip-[[mining]] the moon for [[kyber crystal]]s. Tugged into orbit over Jedha, the vessel served as a symbol of Shu-Torun's commitment to the [[Galactic Empire]], as the behemoth would not be able to achieve orbit again once deployed.<ref name="Star Wars 40" /> The [[orbital drill]] entered Jedha's [[atmosphere]] and began strip-mining on a massive scale while the [[Partisans]] watched. [[Ubin Des]] attempted to plant [[mine]]s in its tracks, but her attack had no effect.<ref name="Star Wars 41" />

[[Chewbacca]] supplied the Rebels with schematics of the ''Leviathan'', allowing [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] to plan a counterattack. Her team infiltrated the vessel by hiding in cargo crates,<ref name="Star Wars 42" /> and took control of the bridge with Trios's help after the Queen revealed she would sacrifice her machine to safeguard her people. With the ''Leviathan'' sabotaged, the Rebels, Trios, and [[Commander]] [[Kanchar]] escaped as it toppled into the blast crater made by the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Star Wars 43" />
