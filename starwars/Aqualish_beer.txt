'''Aqualish beer''' was a type of [[Ale|beer]] in [[the galaxy]]. When [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]] posed as [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] looking to hire [[Nokk]] and [[Winloss]] to destroy the [[Triple-Zero protocol personality matrix]], and [[Ponda Baba]] had been disguised as a [[rebel trooper]], Nokk rejected the job, adding that Organa's guard reeked of Aqualish beer and that there was something shady going on.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 27" />
