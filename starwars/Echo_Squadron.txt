'''Echo Squadron''' was a [[squadron]] that was operational during the [[Galactic Civil War]], where it was part of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] in a division of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]], and then later on as part of the [[New Republic]]. Shortly after the [[destruction of Alderaan]], the Squadron participated in a [[battle at Fostar Haven]].

==Description==
Echo [[Squadron]] was part of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and was made up [[starfighter]] [[Rebel pilot|pilots]] and [[astromech droid]]s, and was under the command of an individual with the [[callsign]] [[Echo Leader]]. The squadron received their orders from [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]].<ref name="Squadrons" />

[[File:AllianceFleetMidRimPriorFostarHaven-Squadrons.png|thumb|left|200px|Echo Squadron with the Alliance fleet in the Mid Rim]]
Echo Squadron was attached to a [[fleet]] that consisted of the [[MC80 Liberty Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80 ''Liberty'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]] [[Aurora (MC80 Star Cruiser)|''Aurora'']], seven [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]], nine [[CR90 corvette]]s, and five [[GR-75 medium transport]]s. The squadron utilized [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s, and had support from [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|BTL-A4 Y-wing starfighters]], [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]s and [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]]. Their starfighters were painted [[Color|blue]] with light gray markings/stripes.<ref name="Squadrons" />

==History==
{{Quote|Echo Squadron, move in and give them cover!|Admiral Ackbar, to Echo Squadron at Fostar Haven|<ref name="Squadrons" />}}
[[File:ConvoyCutOffByVigilanceAtFostarHaven-Squadrons.png|thumb|right|200px|Alderaanian refugee convoy cutoff fleeing Fostar Haven]]
Some time after the [[destruction of Alderaan]], the Alliance received a distress call originating from an [[Alderaanian refugee convoy]] under [[Battle at Fostar Haven|attack]] from [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces at [[Fostar Haven]]. [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] gave the order for Echo Squadron to deploy to Fostar Haven to assist. Upon arrival, they confronted the [[Star Destroyer]] [[Vigilance (Imperial-class)|''Vigilance'']], which was attempting to destroy the [[Alderaanian refugee convoy]]. Echo Squadron was contacted by [[Lindon Javes]], an Imperial defector, and worked with him to protect the convoy from harm. Echo Squadron encountered heavy resistance from the [[Star Destroyer]] as it attempted to halt the convoy's escape, until [[Lindon Javes|Javes]] came up with a plan to disable the [[Vigilance (Imperial-class)|''Vigilance'''s]] targeting system, requiring the help of [[Echo Four (Echo Squadron)|Echo Four]]. With the success of the plan, both the convoy and the Alliance fleet were able to jump to [[lightspeed]].<ref name="Squadrons" />

During the fighting, Echo Squadron lost two members, [[Echo Two (Echo Squadron)|Echo Two]] and [[Echo Five (Echo Squadron)|Echo Five]], but destroyed a number of TIEs along with a [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvette]].<ref name="Squadrons" />

==Squadron livery==
At the time of the Battle at Fostar Haven, ships of Echo Squadron were painted a base cobalt blue with metal highlights. This color scheme appeared on all ships flown under the Squadron's banner.<ref name="Squadrons" />

==Behind the scenes==
Echo Squadron first appeared in the prologue mission of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''.<ref name="Squadrons" />

Echo Squadron shares its name with a [[Echo|clone trooper]] from the [[501st Legion]], and [[Echo Base]], the rebel sanctuary on [[Hoth]].

In [[Star Wars: Squadrons]], the player flies with Echo Squadron under the [[Echo Four (Echo Squadron)|Echo Four]] callsign in the first mission, later transferring to [[Vanguard Squadron]].<ref name="Squadrons" />
