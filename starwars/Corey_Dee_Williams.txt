'''Corey Dee Williams''' (born [[August 14]], [[1960s#1960|1960]]) is the son of [[Billy Dee Williams]], who played [[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Lando Calrissian]] in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]]. Corey played the character [[Klaatu]] during the scenes on [[Khetanna/Legends|Jabba's sail barge]] and also doubled for his father as Calrissian in some scenes of the [[1983]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="Survived">{{InsiderCite|143|I Survived the Sarlacc!}}</ref>
