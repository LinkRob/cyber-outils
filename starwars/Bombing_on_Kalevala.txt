In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Death Watch]], a [[Mandalorian]] splinter group that opposed the pacifistic [[New Mandalorians]], engineered a '''bombing''' on [[Kalevala]],<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore" /> a [[planet]] located in the [[Mandalore system]],<ref name="Kalevala">{{Databank|kalevala|Kalevala}}</ref> that [[Death|killed]] [[Jerec]], the New Mandalorian [[Deputy Minister]]. Later on that [[Standard day|day]], the attack was addressed in the [[Galactic Senate]] by [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore" />

==Behind the scenes==
The bombing on Kalevala was first mentioned in "[[Duchess of Mandalore]],"<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore" /> the fourteenth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' which aired on [[February 12]], [[2010]].<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode214.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Duchess of Mandalore}}</ref>
