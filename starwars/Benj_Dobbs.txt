[[Captain]] '''Benj Dobbs''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Starfighter Corps|Starfighter Corps]] as a [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter|TIE Defender]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]]. After the [[Commanding officer|commander]] of the Imperial [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|7th Fleet's]] [[Defender Squadron One]], [[Vult Skerris]], [[Death|died]] in [[1 BBY]], Dobbs replaced Skerris, gaining command of the [[starfighter]] [[squadron]]. In that [[Standard year|year]], the [[fleet]]'s commander, [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], tasked Dobbs with capturing a dead [[gralloc]] creature while in a [[Kurost transfer point|transfer system]] in the [[Kurost sector]].

The pilot brought the creature's carcass to Thrawn's [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]'' and later escorted a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|shuttle]] carrying two [[Grysk]] [[prisoner]]s to the [[Star Destroyer]]. Later on, when the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[Chiss heavy cruiser|heavy cruiser]] [[Steadfast (Chiss heavy cruiser)|''Steadfast'']] [[Skirmish with Grysk warships|confronted]] two [[Grysk Hegemony]] [[warship]]s, the captain and his squadron attacked the two [[starship]]s, which [[Self-destruct mechanism|self-destructed]].

==Biography==
===Chasing grallocs===

[[Captain]] Benj Dobbs<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="USW" /> who served in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Starfighter Corps|Starfighter Corps]] as a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]] in [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|7th Fleet]]. Before leaving the Empire and Thrawn's [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]'' to serve the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nation<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> in [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Commander]] [[Eli N. Vanto]] knew Dobbs.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Skerris">The events of "[[Rebel Assault]]," including [[Vult Skerris]]' [[death]] in the [[Lothal system]], take place in [[1 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:RebelsDate|here]].</ref> Commander [[Vult Skerris]], the commander of Thrawn's [[Defender Squadron One]], [[Death|perished]] in an [[Attack on Lothal|attack]] on the<ref name="Rebel">{{Rebels|Rebel Assault}}</ref> [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Lothal]],<ref name="Lothal">{{Databank|lothal|Lothal}}</ref> and he was replaced by Dobbs.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> In that same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the ''Chimaera'' traveled to the [[Kurost transfer point]] in the [[Kurost sector]] to deal with the [[gralloc]] creatures that had been attacking the [[starship]]s there, and Thrawn tasked Dobbs and his [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]] [[squadron]] with capturing one of the creatures for study. As the ''Chimaera''{{'s}} [[sensor officer]] fed him a gralloc target's coordinates, the captain chased the creature, failing to capture it after two [[Standard hour|hours]] of attempts.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

As a result, Thrawn changed his strategy, installing a bypass to feed [[Lieutenant]] [[Fentaugh]]'s Defender's sixth [[laser cannon]] to lure the grallocs to the starfighter. After a group of grallocs chased after the Imperial fighter, Dobbs came up behind them and killed the group's third member, maneuvering its carcass between the forward points of one of his Defender's angled wings, bringing it to the ''Chimaera''. Shortly after Thrawn captured two [[Grysk]]s during a [[Attack on a Grysk warship|battle]] against a [[Grysk Hegemony]] [[Grysk warship|warship]], an Imperial [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|shuttle]] brought the [[prisoner]]s to the ''Chimaera'', and Dobbs ran a full-wrap escort. While escorting the shuttle to the Imperial [[Star Destroyer]], a [[Cloaking device|cloaked]] Grysk starship attacked the Defender escort; however, as Dobbs moved to find the vessel, the Grysks escaped to [[hyperspace]]. The captain then jumped to hyperspace under Thrawn's directive, deactivating a nearby Grysk [[gravity well projector]]'s cloaking device and revealing the projector's position.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

===Delta attack===
{{Dialogue|Captain Benj Dobbs|I think we can skip Gamma. Let's move to Delta.|Eli Vanto|Defenders want to move to Delta, Admiral.|Admiral Ar'alani|Do they. Very well, let's split the difference. Half of them to Gamma, half to Delta.|attr=Captain Benj Dobbs, Lieutenant Eli Vanto, and Admiral Ar'alani|src=<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />}}
[[File:Eli_Vanto.png|thumb|left|300px|Captain Benj Dobbs trusted Eli Vanto (pictured).]]
After Thrawn located a [[Grysk forward base]] consisting of two warships conjoined by eight umbilical cylinders, the ''Chimaera'' traveled to the base's location. Once there, Thrawn's [[Executive officer|first officer]], [[Commodore]] [[Karyn Faro]], remotely destroyed the cylinders, causing the ships to separate and creating an explosion. Dobbs reported that the explosion caused only minor damage to the conjoined ships' surface. Thrawn then plotted to destroy a [[Unidentified Grysk task force|Grysk task force]] that was [[Imperial–Grysk conflict|attacking]] Imperial [[Realspace|space]], using Dobbs's Defender squadron and fellow Grand Admiral [[Balanhai Savit]]'s [[Third Fleet]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

Preparing for a battle with the [[task force]], Thrawn asked Dobbs if he trusted Vanto, who had become a lieutenant in the Ascendancy's [[Chiss Defense Force]] and served aboard [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]]'s [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] [[Steadfast (Chiss heavy cruiser)|''Steadfast'']], and Dobbs told the Grand Admiral that he trusted the lieutenant. While Thrawn [[Skirmish against the Third Fleet|arrested]] Savit on charges of [[treason]], the ''Steadfast'' [[Skirmish with Grysk warships|engaged]] the Grysk force's two warships. As the Grysks dug into the [[heavy cruiser]]'s hull, Vanto summoned twelve Defenders under Dobbs's command, which fired at the rear warship.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

Dobbs told Vanto that he wanted to skip [[Gamma attack]], an attack on the rear warship, and move to [[Delta attack]], which involved the Defenders charging from the rear warship to the lead warship. In response, Ar'alani had Vanto bring six of the Imperial fighters back to hyperspace and send the other six to converge on the lead warship. Meanwhile, the ''Steadfast''{{'s}} [[tractor beam]] pulled a [[gravity]] well projector into the lead Grysk warship's [[hangar]] while the Defenders attacked the fighters leaving the hangar. The warship tried to destroy the fighters with its [[cannon]]s; however, the shots missed, and the Grysks [[Self-destruct mechanism|destroyed]] their own vessels. After the battle, Ar'alani instructed Vanto to arrange accommodations and refreshments for the Defenders' pilots.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />
