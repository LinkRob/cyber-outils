'''Fong''' was a [[Color/Legends|light]]-skinned, dark-[[Eye/Legends|eyed]], [[Human/Legends|Human]] who flew as a [[Pilot/Legends|pilot]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in their [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|war]] against the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. During the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]], Fong served as the [[Red Leader/Legends|leader]] of [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)/Legends|Red Squadron]] and piloted a [[T-65 X-wing starfighter/Legends|T-65 X-wing starfighter]],<ref name="Yavin" /> and during the [[Battle of Hoth/Legends|Battle of Hoth]] in [[3 ABY/Legends|3 ABY]],<ref name="Chronology">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''</ref> Fong piloted a [[Snowspeeder/Legends|snowspeeder]] under the [[call sign]] "Speeder 3." As a [[Rebel pilot/Legends|Rebel pilot]], Fong wore an orange [[Rebel flight suit|flight suit]] and a gray, yellow-visored [[Flight helmet/Legends|flight helmet]].<ref name="Hoth">{{Galoob|set=Micro Machines Star Wars Action Fleet|text=''Star Wars Action Fleet''|pack=Ice Planet Hoth|link=mmafsets.asp}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Fong was created as a [[tuckerization]] of [[Jim Fong]], the design director at [[Galoob]], the company which produced the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:Micro_Machines|Micro Machines]] toy line from [[1994]] to [[2002]].<ref name="History">{{SW|url=news/think-big-play-small-the-history-of-star-wars-micro-machines-part-1|text=Think Big, Play Small: The History of ''Star Wars'' Micro Machines, Part 1|nolive=1}}</ref> The character appears in the [[Micro Machines Star Wars Action Fleet|Micro Machines ''Action Fleet'']] playsets "Yavin Rebel Base"<ref name="Yavin" /> and "Ice Planet Hoth"<ref name="Hoth" /> which, respectively, picture Fong on monitors within the [[Great Temple/Legends|Great Temple]]<ref name="Yavin" /> and [[Echo Base/Legends|Echo Base]].<ref name="Hoth" /> While "Yavin Rebel Base" labels Fong as Red Leader during the Battle of Yavin,<ref name="Yavin" /> the title was held by [[Garven Dreis/Legends|Garven Dreis]];<ref name="IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> as such, this article treats Fong as a non-[[canon]] subject.
