'''Blue Convor''' was a [[nightclub]] on the [[spice]]&ndash;[[mining]] [[planet]] [[Kerev Doi]] that was known for its many [[Hologram|holo]] programs. During his stay on the planet, [[Thane Kyrell]] enjoyed the establishment.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BlueConvorSpeeder-Outlaws.jpg|thumb|right|200px|The Blue Convor paint scheme on a speeder bike]]
Blue Convor was first appeared in the [[2015]] young&ndash;adult novel ''[[Lost Stars]]'' by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />

The name Blue Convor was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' as the name of a paint scheme that could be used to customize the appearance the player's speeder bike.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
