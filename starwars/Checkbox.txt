'''Checkbox''' was a [[clone trooper]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] who served on the [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]] ''[[Stellar Rise]]'' during the [[Clone Wars]]. During the war, [[General]] [[Grievous]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Infiltration of the Stellar Rise|infiltrated]] the [[cruiser]], [[Death|killing]] Checkbox as soon as he revealed his presence.

==Biography==
[[File:Grievous-SaberTruth.jpg|thumb|right|270px|Checkbox was slain by General Grievous.]]
Checkbox was the nickname of a<ref name="Saber Truth" /> [[human]]<ref name="ClonesData" /> [[clone trooper]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Saber Truth" /> who was [[Reproduction|born]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]], [[Cloning|cloned]] from the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]].<ref name="ClonesData" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="Saber Truth" /> between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Checkbox was stationed aboard the [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]] ''[[Stellar Rise]]''.<ref name="Saber Truth" />

While taking inventory of crates that had been picked up during a stop near the [[Kazlin system]], he noticed something off about the boxes he was checking&mdash;a [[stone mite]]&ndash;like noise was coming from the one nearest him. As the clone looked closer, listening to the noise, a [[Color|blue]] [[lightsaber]] belonging to Separatist [[General]] [[Grievous]] sliced through, [[Death|hacking]] Checkbox vertically in two. Grievous had [[Infiltration of the Stellar Rise|infiltrated]] the ''Stellar Rise'' in search of [[High Republic Era|High Republic–era]] artifacts and had brought several [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s with him for the job. Two of Checkbox's fellow clones later came to investigate why he was missing from his post only to be killed by the MagnaGuards.<ref name="Saber Truth" />

==Personality and traits==
Checkbox had a meticulous mind, suited for administrative work rather than combat. As a Galactic Republic clone trooper,<ref name="Saber Truth" /> he stood 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|ft]])<ref name="ClonesData" /> and weighed eighty [[kilogram]]s.<ref name="Weight" />

==Equipment==
At birth, like the other clone [[trooper]]s, Checkbox was implanted by the [[Kaminoan]] [[scientist]]s with a [[behavioral modification biochip]] to ensure that the trooper would not resist certain [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|orders]].<ref name="ClonesData" /> Checkbox wore a [[helmet]].<ref name="Saber Truth" />

==Behind the scenes==
Checkbox was created for "[[Galactic Tales: Saber Truth]]," the second installment of the ''[[Galactic Tales]]'' series of short stories. The story was written by [[Richard Dinnick]] and was published in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]''{{'s}} [[Star Wars Insider 210|210th issue]]<ref name="Saber Truth" /> on [[May 3]], [[2022]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=m/101-star-wars-insider-210|text=Star Wars Insider #210}}</ref>
