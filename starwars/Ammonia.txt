'''Ammonia''' was a [[gas]] compound made up of [[nitrogen]] and [[hydrogen]]. A constituent of the [[atmosphere]] of many [[gas giant]]s, it was caustic and slightly [[Poison|toxic]] to most [[species]].<ref name="BMF 43">{{BuildFalconCite|43|Secrets of Spaceflight}}</ref> One type of ammonia was [[Vanoorian ammonia]], which was found on [[Vanooria]].<ref name="PL1" /> The [[planet]] [[Gand (planet)|Gand]], [[homeworld]] of the species of the [[Gand|same name]], had an [[atmosphere]] composed primarily of ammonia rather than [[oxygen]], causing it to be poisonous to most off-worlders.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>
