'''Aldorus VI''' was a green [[Gas giant/Legends|gas giant]] in the [[Aldorus system]].

==Description==
From [[Realspace/Legends|space]], the [[Gas giant/Legends|gas giant]] Aldorus VI seemed to have a greenish tint. Several small [[Asteroid/Legends|asteroids]] could be seen drifting close to the boundary limit of the planet's atmosphere. As it had no landmasses or [[Water/Legends|water]], Aldorus VI couldn't sustain life, and was thus uninhabited.<ref name="CasusBelli" />

==Behind the scenes==
The planet Aldorus VI has only appeared in the French RPG magazine [[Casus Belli 44|''Casus Belli'' 44]].<ref name="CasusBelli" />
