'''Allan R. Banks''' is an [[Wikipedia:United States of America|American]] painter trained in classic-art. Banks was part of the group who made artworks for the [[2010]] artbook ''[[Star Wars Art: Visions]]''. His artwork was named '''''Celebration of Naboo Youth on Freedom Day'''''.
