The '''''Bellicose''-class Heavy Lifter''' was a [[Clone Wars]]-era model of [[heavy duty lifter]] manufactured by [[Hoersch-Kessel Drive, Inc.]] It was originally designed to deploy [[Battle droid|assault droids]] from [[orbit]]. The rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] flew a heavily modified ''Bellicose''-class craft dubbed the [[Ark Angel II|''Ark Angel'']]. Over time, the ''Angel'' was repaired and modified so often that it became unrecognizable.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />
