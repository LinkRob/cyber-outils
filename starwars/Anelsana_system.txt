The '''Anelsana system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Farstey sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]. It was situated in grid square O-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the terrestrial [[Planet|world]] [[Anelsana]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], Anelsana was under the control of the [[Galactic Empire]], which enforced a trade embargo that the world suffered from.<ref name="LPOA" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]].</ref> the [[Alderaanian]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] reflected on the past effects of the embargo on Anelsana while reviewing locations to deliver humanitarian aid, ultimately choosing to travel elsewhere.<ref name="LPOA" />

==Behind the scenes==
