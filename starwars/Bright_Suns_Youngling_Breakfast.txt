'''Bright Suns Youngling Breakfast''' was a breakfast dish for [[youngling]]s which was served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Batuu]]'s [[Black Spire Outpost]]. It included [[egg]]s, potatoes, and [[fruit]]. A larger meal called [[Bright Suns Morning]] was also available.<ref name="DB7" />

==Behind the scenes==
This dish is found in the real world Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]. The menu's description reads, "Scrambled eggs, purple potatoes, and seasonal fresh fruit served with a choice of small lowfat milk or small DASANI® water."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/breakfast|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Breakfast Menu}}</ref>
