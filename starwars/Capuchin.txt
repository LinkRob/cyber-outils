A '''Capuchin''' was present at [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina/Legends|Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] around [[0 BBY/Legends|the time]] [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] were there. The Capuchin wore a red vest and was fond of [[date]]s.

==Behind the scenes==
The Capuchin's appearance in ''[[Star Wars Galactic Spy]]'' is one of the video game's several references to the ''[[Indiana Jones]]'' franchise.
