An '''abandoned munitions warehouse''' was located on [[Coruscant]]'s [[Level 1315]]. It was there [[Barriss Offee]] supplied [[Letta Turmond]] with the [[NM-K reconstitutor|nano-droids]] to [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|bomb]] the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Catch a Jedi" />

When [[Ahsoka Tano]] fled after being framed for Turmond's murder, which caused the [[Jedi Order]] to believe she was responsible for the bombings, Offee directed her to the warehouse, claiming she had found a clue. Then, Offee, disguised as [[Asajj Ventress]], attacked Tano, causing an explosion that alerted the [[Coruscant underworld police]]. [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]] then cornered and arrested Tano after stunning her. They also found the nano-droids in storage boxes nearby, which seemed to confirm her guilt.<ref name="Catch a Jedi" />
