The '''Aqualish crime family'''<ref name="Worker's District">{{Databank|mos-espa-worker-s-district|Mos Espa Worker's District}}</ref> was part of [[Jabba's criminal empire]] when it was led by [[Lord]] [[Bib Fortuna]] following [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba's]] [[death]]. Control over [[Mos Espa]] was divided into three crime families, the [[Strassi family|Trandoshans]] at the [[Mos Espa city center|city center]], the [[Aqualish]] in the [[Worker's District]], and the [[Tatooine Klatooinian family|Klatooinians]] in the [[Mos Espa spaceport]] and [[upper sprawl]].<ref name="Chapter 3" />
