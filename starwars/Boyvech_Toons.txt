'''Boyvech Toons''' was a veteran [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] who flew in the [[New Republic]]'s [[Hail Squadron]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Toons was a scarred veteran of a hundred battles, and his [[BTL Y-wing starfighter|Y-wing]] squadron supported [[Alphabet Squadron]] in the [[Hunt for Shadow Wing|hunt]] for the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], which included the [[Battle of Pandem Nai|attack on Pandem Nai]] and later the [[Cerberon system campaign]]. In [[5 ABY]], when [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]]'s New Republic forces [[Battle in the Red Yars system|caught up]] to the 204th in the [[Red Yars system]], Toons and [[Chass na Chadic]] were the only New Republic pilots to loose shots, but neither was in range when the 204th jumped to lightspeed. Later, Toons fought at the [[Attack on Chadawa|battle]] over the [[planet]] [[Chadawa]], where he and his fellow bomber pilots were tasked with destroying two [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvettes]], in a stealthy mission to prevent the 204th from destroying planet that resulted in a victory. However, Toons was the highest-ranking survivor of only four Hail Squadron pilots to survive the battle. Toons and his fellow pilots were later present at a meeting, held shortly before the [[Battle of Jakku]], in which their wing's commander, [[Wyl Lark]] informed the unit of his decision not to fight in the coming battle.

==Biography==
===Pandem Nai===
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Boyvech Toons served as a [[New Republic|pilot]] in the [[New Republic]]. [[Gender|He]] flew a [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]] for [[Hail Squadron]], one of the [[squadron]]s serving in a [[Barma Battle Group|battle group]] led by [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> Hail Squadron was one of four squadrons based on Syndulla's [[flagship]], the [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class transgalactic military assault ship]] ''[[Lodestar]]'', the other three being [[Meteor Squadron]], [[Vanguard Squadron]] and [[Alphabet Squadron]]. Some [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Hail Squadron, Toons included, took part in an [[Battle of Pandem Nai|attack]] on the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] at the [[tibanna]] [[gas]] [[mining]] [[planet]] [[Pandem Nai]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

During the attack, Hail Squadron helped provide a [[blockade]] behind a [[mine]]field protecting the inner [[Pandem Nai system]] along with Meteor Squadron, Vanguard Squadron and the ''Lodestar'' and its escorts while Alphabet Squadron attacked the 204th's base of operations at Pandem Nai itself. When the planet's gas clouds began burning, threatening civilization on the world, Syndulla ordered for the blockade to push through the minefield to assist in preventing the [[fire]]storm. The firestorm was ultimately stopped, although the chaos allowed a large portion of the 204th to escape.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

Around [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ShadowFallDate}}</ref> Syndulla's battle group [[Cerberon system campaign|invaded]] the [[Cerberon system]] in the [[Deep Core]].<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> Toons<ref name="Victory's Price" /> and Hail Squadron took part in the campaign, where the squadron notably participated in an assault on the planet [[Troithe]]'s [[capital]] [[city]], which resulted in the [[death]] of the Imperial [[Governor]] [[Hastemoor]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

===Chasing the 204th===
In 5 ABY,<ref name="Date 2">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> Syndulla's battle group, with the New Republic refitted [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]'' serving as the flagship, travelled to the [[Croynar sector]] based on information that the 204th had been presence in that [[sector]]. As part of the ''Deliverances''{{'s}} [[Deliverance's starfighter wing|starfighter wing]], alongside the New Republic's [[Flare Squadron]] and [[Wild Squadron]], Hail Squadron traveled to the planet [[Midgor]] and found that the Imperial [[fighter wing]] had attacked the [[freighter]] ''[[Diamond Tor]]'' and its escorts. The ''Deliverance'' and its wing later found the 204th in the [[Ciaox Verith system]] by following a communications signal burst, but the Imperial group escaped.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

Another comm burst led the ''Deliverance'' and its wing to find the 204th in the [[Red Yars system]]. Toons and the other New Republic fighters exited [[hyperspace]] readily deployed, but only he and the [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter]] pilot [[Chass na Chadic]] were able to fire shots at the enemy fighter wing before its forces escaped, both pilots being out of range regardless. The New Republic forces continued to pursue the 204th through a number of other systems, Hail Squadron taking part in the skirmishes. The pursuit arrived at the ancient manmade [[ocean]] world [[Chadawa]], where the Imperial fighter wing became enclosed under the world's mechanical orbital rings, which channeled particle tides of [[radiation]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

Hail Squadron and the rest of the ''Deliverance''{{'s}} wing skirmished with the 204th when the particle tides left openings for starfighters to pass through. It was soon discovered by Syndulla and her colleagues that the 204th's [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvettes]] were sabotaging satellites to culminate high amounts of radiation before plummeting down to the surface of Chadawa. In response, a plan was formulated to prevent Chadawa's devastation, which involved Hail Squadron, led by Alphabet Squadron's [[Captain]] [[Nath Tensent]], carrying out an attack on the Raiders. The pilots of the ''Deliverance''{{'s}} wing prepared to deploy and rushed to their fighters. As they went to their fighters, [[Starfighter Commander]] [[Wyl Lark]] approached Toons and told him that there would not be another [[Alderaan]], a planet destroyed by the Empire.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
