'''Channelfish kibble''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] made from [[channelfish]] that was [[Diet|edible]] by [[tooka]] [[cat]]s.<ref name="Bounty Hunter and the Thief" /> By around [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|222B}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], a [[Unidentified Talpiddian|Talpiddian]] who lived on<ref name="Bounty Hunter and the Thief" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Tenoo]] with their pet [[Unidentified tooka|tooka]] had channelfish kibble at their home.<ref name="Bounty Hunter and the Thief" />

Around that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Feline|tooka]] was rescued by three [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]]&mdash;[[Kai Brightstar]], [[Nubs]], and [[Lys Solay]]&mdash;and the [[Dowutin]] [[Ansen Strung]] after she became stuck on a [[Tenoo tree]]. Once the cat was returned to her owner, the [[Talpiddian]] stated they would go home and get the tooka some channelfish kibble and a bowl of [[Blue milk|bantha milk]], with the cat meowing in response.<ref name="Bounty Hunter and the Thief" />

==Behind the scenes==
Channelfish kibble was mentioned in "[[The Bounty Hunter and the Thief]]," the second part of the [[A Mission to Remember / The Bounty Hunter and the Thief|twenty-second episode]] in the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|second season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'',<ref name="Bounty Hunter and the Thief" /> which aired on [[March 19]], [[2025]].<ref name="Season 2 Resume">{{SW|url=news/young-jedi-adventures-season-2-and-nubs-shorts|text=''Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures'' Returns for Season 2}}</ref>
