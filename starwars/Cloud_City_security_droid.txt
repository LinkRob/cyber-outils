A '''model''' of [[security droid]] was utilized in [[Cloud City]] on [[Bespin]] during its [[time]] under the [[Baron Administrator]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]. The [[droid]]s were shaped like a [[humanoid]] torso with two three-clawed [[Limb|arms]]. They were also equipped with a [[holoprojector]], allowing the Baron Administrator to project his face and voice through the droids. When the [[Youngling|children]] [[Moxxin]], [[Traff]], and [[Vanraya]] [[Thief|stole]] the [[Cantonarive crown coin]] from him, Calrissian used several of the droids to chase after them, speaking to the children through a droid to convince them to return the [[coin]] to him.<ref name="The Coin" />
