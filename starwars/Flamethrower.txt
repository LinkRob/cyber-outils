'''Flamethrowers''' were weapons that fired streams or bursts of [[fire]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Hahaha! You are on fire and also dead.|translated=1|[[0-0-0]], to a group of [[droid worker]]s that [[BT-1]] destroyed with his flamethrower in [[Geonosian hive-mind]]|<ref name="Darth Vader (2015) 4">[[Darth Vader (2015) 4|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 4]]</ref>}}
A [[Millennium|millennia]]-old [[infantry]] mainstay, flamethrowers were modernized by [[Mandalorian super commando]]s and [[Galactic Republic|Republic]]-era [[clone trooper]]s during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> Although they were often used as an anti-personnel weapon, as in the case of the wrist-mounted flamethrower owned by [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]],<ref name="SWE2" /> some individuals, like the [[Dug]] [[podracer]] [[pilot]] [[Sebulba]], mounted flamethrowers onto vehicles. Sebulba used his flamethrower as a weapon against other [[Podracer (profession)|podracers]] to help him illegally win the [[Boonta Eve Classic]].<ref name="Databank Sebulba's Podracer">{{Databank|sebulbas-podracer|Sebulba's Podracer}}</ref>

[[Mandalorian]] warriors often integrated flamethrowers<ref name="Legacy of Mandalore">{{Rebels|Legacy of Mandalore}}</ref> into their [[Mandalorian vambrace|vambraces]] as one of their many anti-[[Jedi Knight|Jedi]] tools,<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> as the flames could not be blocked by the single beam of a [[lightsaber]], forcing the defender to either retreat.<ref name="SWE2" /><ref name="Hunt for Ziro">{{TCW|Hunt for Ziro}}</ref><ref name="Shades of Reason">{{TCW|Shades of Reason}}</ref> A [[Force-sensitive|Force wielder]] with sufficient skill, strength in [[the Force]] and time to react could [[Force barrier|shield]] themselves from the flames with the Force, though this required considerable effort.<ref name="Jedi Night">{{Rebels|Jedi Night}}</ref> Mandalorian [[Personal Combat Shield|plasma shields]] could also be used to defend against flamethrower attacks.<ref name="Phantom Apprentice">{{TCW|The Phantom Apprentice}}</ref>

==History==
===Under the Republic===
{{Quote|We're almost through. Bring in the flamethrowers.|[[Jedi General]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]], during the Second Battle of Geonosis|<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" />}}
[[File:JangoFlamethrower-AOTC.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Jango Fett using his [[ZX miniature flame projector]]]]
At the [[Boonta Eve Classic]],<ref name="Ep. I">{{Film|I}}</ref> [[Sebulba]] equipped his [[Sebulba's Podracer|podracer]] with flamethrowers. He use them as weapon against other podracers to help him win the the podrace.<ref name="Databank Sebulba's Podracer" /> Bounty hunters such as [[Jango Fett]]<ref name="SWE2" /> and Cad Bane used flamethrowers against the Jedi.<ref name="Hunt for Ziro" />

During the Clone Wars, the [[clone flametrooper]]s under Jedi General [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Jet]] used flamethrowers against the native [[Geonosian]]s at the [[Second Battle of Geonosis|Second battle]] of [[Geonosis]].<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" /> The Mandalorians, such as members of the [[Death Watch]],<ref name="A Friend in Need" /> the [[Mandalorian Guard]]s,<ref name="Corruption" /> and the [[Mandalorian super commando]]s used flamethrowers against their enemies.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 1">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 1|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 1]]</ref> [[Gar Saxon]] of the super commandos had a ZX flame projector on his arm.<ref name="ZX Flame Projector">{{Legion|set=Gar Saxon Commander Expansion|cardname=Saxon's ZX Flame Projector}}</ref>

===The Empire and beyond===
{{Quote|Burn them out.|Moff Gideon summons an incinerator trooper|<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref>}}
[[File:MandoFlamethrowerSnowtroopers-TheMandalorianAndGrogu.png|thumb|right|250px|Din Djarin using his flamethrower against two Imperial Remnant snowtroopers]]
The [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Elite Squad Trooper]]s could be armed with flamethrowers.<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|YB9DIxdB198|Sizzle &#124; The Bad Batch &#124; Disney+|sw_url=video/sizzle-the-bad-batch}}</ref> [[C-21 Highsinger]] had a flamethrower built in his right arm, which he used during a [[Heist at Cassaro shipyard]]; first cutting a cable under an [[Unidentified Imperial crawler|Imperial crawler]], causing it to stop and later when he attempted to fight [[Asajj Ventress]], but lost his right arm and legs.<ref name="Friends">{{TOTU|Friends}}</ref> During the [[Galactic Civil War]] by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[flametrooper]]s and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Special-weapons flamethrower|flame troopers]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Imperial [[dewback trooper]]s made use of [[CR-24 Flame Rifle]]s during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="SWL">{{Legion|set=Dewback Rider Unit Expansion|cardname=CR-24 Flame Rifle}}</ref>

[[Gideon's Imperial remnant]] had at least one [[Incinerator stormtrooper|incinerator trooper]].<ref name="Chapter 8" /> The [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] utilized his wrist mounted flamethrower against [[Neo-Imperial forces|Imperial Remnant]] [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtroopers]].<ref name="Official Trailer">{{SWYouTube|IHWlvwu8t1w|The Mandalorian and Grogu &#124; Official Trailer &#124; In Theaters May 22}}</ref> 

During the [[Cold War]] and subsequent [[First Order-Resistance War|conflict]] against the [[Resistance]], the [[First Order]] employed [[First Order flametrooper|flametroopers]], including during the [[Attack on Tuanul]].<ref name="TFA" />
