'''Ella Poworoznik''' (born on [[January 3]], [[2001]] in [[Wikipedia:Alberta|Alberta]], [[Wikipedia:Canada|Canada]]), credited as '''Cherriielle'''  or '''Ella Cherriielle''' professionally, is a [[Wikipedia:Canadians|Canadian]] [[Wikipedia:Artist|artist]]. She created two [[:File:TheHighRepublicAdventures2023-3-coverB.jpg|variant]] [[:File:TheHighRepublicAdventures2023-8-finalvariant-Cherriielle.jpg|covers]] for the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 3|third]] and [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 8|eighth issues]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']]. The comics were written by [[Daniel José Older]] released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's Phase III on  [[February 14]] and [[August 7]] of [[2024]], respectively.

Poworoznik also created the cover artwork for the one-shot comic ''[[The High Republic Adventures Phase III - The Wedding Spectacular]]'', which released on [[December 18]], 2024. The cover artwork for her ''The High Republic Adventures'' 8 variant cover was later reprinted in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Women of the Galaxy: Updated and Expanded]]'' by [[Amy Ratcliffe]]. She is a longtime ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan, having been brought to the attention of [[Lucasfilm]] via her artwork on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]]. The artist also often draws ''Star Wars'' artwork for fun, friends, or other commission work, including one [[:File:Ruu-commission.jpg|piece]] done for the design of the [[Jedi]] [[Ruu]] for ''Star Wars'' author [[Alyssa Wong]].

==Biography==
===Cover milestones===
{{Quote|&hellip;didn't hear anything from April until October, then I finally get an email from Dark Horse; they say we need cover artists for ''Adventures''. Daniel likes your work and wants to work with you.|Ella Poworoznik|<ref name="Interview" />}}
[[File:TheHighRepublicAdventuresTheWeddingSpectacular-virgincover.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Textless version of Cherriielle's cover for ''The High Republic Adventures Phase III - The Wedding Spectacular'']]
Ella Poworoznik,<ref name="About" /> credited as Cherriielle<ref name="Cherriielle">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-795/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-3-Cherriielle-Variant-Cover|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #3 (Cherrielle Variant Cover)}}</ref> and Ella Cherriielle professionally,<ref name="Artbook">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref> is a [[Wikipedia:Canadians|Canadian]]<ref name="Alberta" /><ref name="About" /> [[Wikipedia:Artist|artist]].<ref name="Cherriielle" /> who was born on [[January 3]], [[2001]]<ref name="Birth" /> in [[Wikipedia:Alberta|Alberta]],<ref name="Alberta" /> [[Wikipedia:Canada|Canada]].<ref name="About" /> She<ref name="Pronouns" /> created a [[:File:TheHighRepublicAdventures2023-3-coverB.jpg|variant cover]] for the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 3|third issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']]. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], penciled by [[Harvey Tolibao]], and released by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="THRA3">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 3|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 3]]</ref> Phase III on [[February 14]], [[2024]].<ref name="Cherriielle" /> Poworoznik later created a [[:File:TheHighRepublicAdventures2023-8-finalvariant-Cherriielle.jpg|variant cover]] for the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 8|eighth issue]] of the same series. The issue was written by Older, illustrated by Tolibao, and released on<ref name="THRA8">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 8|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 8]]</ref> [[August 7]] of the same year.<ref name="Cherriielle-8">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-859/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-8-Cherriielle-Variant-Cover|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #8 (Cherriielle Variant Cover)}}</ref> She was first announced to be working on the cover on [[March 22]], 2024,<ref name="THRA8 Tweet">{{Twitter|cherriielle|status/1771196238735487081|Cherriielle|date=2024-03-22|quote=Ella cover 2.0???|archiveurl=https://archive.is/L6Lnh}}</ref> and the artwork for the issue was later released on [[April 16]],<ref name="THRA8 Tweet2">{{Twitter|cherriielle|status/1780322183430193234|Cherriielle|date=2024-04-16|quote=oh Ella cover 2 is out!!!|archiveurl=https://archive.is/A4G7D}}</ref> after which she posted [[:File:TheHighRepublicAdventures2023-8-virginvariant-Cherriielle.jpg|textless version]] of the artwork on Twitter.<ref name="THRA8Textless">{{Twitter|cherriielle|status/1780336686582386909|Cherriielle|date=2024-04-16|quote=oh wait i forgot i can post the textless version!! my bbs ✨|archivedate=20240416211628}}</ref>

Poworoznik created the primary cover artwork for ''[[The High Republic Adventures Phase III - The Wedding Spectacular]]'', a one-shot ''The High Republic Adventures'' comic-book. The issue was written by Older and published by Dark Horse Comics on [[December 18]], 2024.<ref name="WeddingSpectacularDH">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-812/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III--The-Wedding-Spectacular|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III--The Wedding Spectacular}}</ref> She released the [[:File:TheHighRepublicAdventuresTheWeddingSpectacular-virgincover.jpg|textless version]] of the cover on Twitter.<ref name="WeddingSpectacular">{{Twitter|cherriielle|status/1826728212740878465|Cherriielle|date=2024-08-22|quote=textless version! 👀💐✨|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/37WPI}}</ref> Upon its release, Poworoznik expressed her pleasure at having been involved in the issue's creation.<ref name="WeddingComments">{{Twitter|cherriielle|status/1869410931916357792|Cherriielle|date=2024-12-18|quote=out today!! what a joy to have worked on a small part of this :)|archiveurl=https://archive.is/ZgMQt|archivefile=File:Cherriielle-WeddingSpectacular-Twitter.png}}</ref> The artist's illustration for ''The High Republic Adventures'' 8's variant cover was later reprinted in reference book ''[[Star Wars: Women of the Galaxy: Updated and Expanded]]'', authored by [[Amy Ratcliffe]]<ref name="Women-Updated">''[[Star Wars: Women of the Galaxy: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> and released on [[April 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="ChronicleWOMEN">{{Chronicle|url=products/star-wars-women-of-the-galaxy-updated-and-expanded|text=Star Wars Women of the Galaxy Updated and Expanded}}</ref>

===Development, hiring, and other work===
{{Quote|Had the pleasure of commissioning the one and only @cherriielle for my sweet nightmare boy, Ruu, from my High Republic story 'Rogue Element'! Love a former Jedi turned vigilante|Alyssa Wong, about fanwork they commissioned|<ref name="Twitter3">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1735017051301822848|[[Alyssa Wong]]|date=2023-12-13|quote=Had the pleasure of commissioning the one and only @cherriielle for my sweet nightmare boy, Ruu, from my High Republic story 'Rogue Element'! Love a former Jedi turned vigilante|archivedate=20231213215038}}</ref>}}
[[File:CherrielleBTS.png|thumb|left|210px|Fan artwork of Cohmac Vitus and Reath Silas by Cherriielle]]
Poworoznik is a longtime ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan, especially having enjoyed the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' multimedia project,<ref name="Interview" /> having enjoyed the latter project's inclusive nature of making ''Star Wars'' for everyone.<ref name="GhostSquad">{{YouTube|video=AyNzNLg62Cg|text=Ghost Squad Interview Pod - Ella - Illustrator For The High Republic Adventures|channel=@TheGhostSquadAStarWarsPodcast|name=The Ghost Squad - A Star Wars Podcast}}</ref> She posted on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] about her wish to create a ''The High Republic'' cover on [[June 28]], [[2022]],<ref name="Tweet">{{Twitter|cherriielle|status/1541873511970377733|Cherriielle|date=2022-06-28|quote=please @starwars hire me to make high republic cover art it's my dream job|archiveurl=https://archive.is/E2XEn}}</ref> initially in jest. When multiple ''Star Wars'' creators supported her and linked the post&mdash;featuring an image of the [[Jedi]] [[Reath Silas]] and [[Cohmac Vitus]]&mdash;to others, the artist was contacted by [[Lucasfilm Story Group]]'s [[Matt Martin]], who sent her work to those working in ''Star Wars'' publishing. She then met with [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]'s [[Michael Siglain]] at [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]]<ref name="Interview" /> hosted from [[April 7]]&ndash;[[April 10|10]]th 2023.<ref name="Twitter Announcement">{{Twitter|starwars|status/1531047358141587456|[[Star Wars]]|date=2022-05-29|quote=Let's do it again next year. #StarWarsCelebration returns to Europe in 2023.|archivedate=20220530055619}}</ref>

The artist was later hired to do a cover for ''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'' in [[October]] of the same year, as Older wanted to work with her for a variant cover.<ref name="Interview" /> Poworoznik was given a short list of sentence-long ideas to create sketches off of, and once one of those had been selected, she created the issue's artwork.<ref name="FOTF">{{Spotify|url=episode/1dugtIKZxqw4NWFyqE1MTS|text=Illustrating the "High Republic Adventures" with Ella (@cherriielle)}}</ref> She was particularly excited to illustrate the [[Mikkian]] [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]], as she was one of her favorite characters.<ref name="GhostSquad" /> For the cover of ''The High Republic Adventures Phase III - The Wedding Spectacular'', Poworoznik expressed her gratitude to Older for allowing her to design the cover featuring Mrala and the [[human]] [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]]'s [[Marriage|wedding]] [[Clothing|dresses]].<ref name="WeddingDresses">{{Twitter|cherriielle|status/1826647094553444598|Cherriielle|date=2024-08-22|quote=thank you to @djolder for allowing me the opportunity to design the dresses & cover for this one shot!! Star Wars is for everyone 🫶🏳️‍🌈|archiveurl=https://archive.is/GdfV6}}</ref> Nearing the series' end, Older indicated that he was proud that ''The High Republic'' initiative allowed for giving several fan artists an official ''Star Wars'' debut, naming Cherriielle amongst others.<ref name="Substack-DJO">{{Substack|subdomain=danieljosolder|url=p/my-first-comic-book-cover|text=My First Comic Book Cover!|name=[[Daniel José Older]]|username=Daniel José Older|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

Poworoznik also often draws ''Star Wars'' artwork for fun, friends, or other commission work<ref name="Interview" /> and cosplays as franchise characters at conventions.<ref name="GhostSquad" /> The artist takes particular inspiration from the work of ''Star Wars'' artists [[Grant Griffin]] and [[Corey Brickley]].<ref name="GhostSquad" /> ''Star Wars'' author [[Alyssa Wong]] commissioned Poworoznik to create [[:File:Ruu-commission.jpg|artwork]] of the Jedi-turned-vigilante [[Ruu]]'s design<ref name="Twitter3" /> and thanked Poworoznik for a piece inspired by ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'',<ref name="Valo Fanart">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1753477412443361763|[[Alyssa Wong]]|date=2024-02-02|quote=Siri, play "the grudge" by Olivia Rodrigo Thank you @cherriielle for the beautiful ESCAPE FROM VALO fanart! 🩵🪶|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/axPos}}</ref> a middle grade novel Wong had written with Older,<ref name="Escape from Valo">''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]''</ref> which was published on [[January 30]], 2024.<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> Poworoznik's ''Escape from Valo'' piece depicts [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Gavi Takitaken|Gavi]] in battle with the young [[Nihil]] [[Driggit Parse]],<ref name="Valo Fanart" /> Gavi's former best friend.<ref name="Escape from Valo" /> Under Siglain's direction, Poworoznik created concept art of the [[Luminous Nine]] Jedi that traveled to the [[planet]] [[Sophros]] to combat the [[Blight]] threat. The art was later released in the reference book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]'' by [[Kristin Baver]],<ref name="Artbook" /> published on [[August 19]], 2025,<ref name="AbramsArtbook">{{Abrams|url=product/art-of-star-wars-the-high-republic-volume-ii_9781419771422|text=The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic (Volume II)}}</ref> which also included Poworznik's concept artwork of Mrala and Talisola's wedding dresses.<ref name="Artbook" />
