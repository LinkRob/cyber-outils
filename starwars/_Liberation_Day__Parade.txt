The '''"Liberation Day" Parade'''<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> was a celebration held in the [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Theed]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] following the defeat of the [[Trade Federation]] during the [[Battle of Naboo]]<ref name="TPM" /> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> The celebration was attended by the native [[Gungan]]s and [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] people, who had worked together to defeat the invaders with the aid of the [[Jedi Order]], and so the [[Jedi High Council]] also attended. During the parade, [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala]] of the Naboo gave the [[Globe of Peace]] to [[Boss]] [[Rugor Nass]] of the Gungans.<ref name="TPM" />

==History==
Following the [[Battle of Naboo]], a parade, led by [[Augara Jowil|Augie's]] [[Great Municipal Band]], was held in [[Theed]], celebrating the defeat of the [[Trade Federation]] and peace between [[Naboo (people)|the Naboo]] and [[Gungan|the Gungans]]. Ships flew above and a crowd of Naboo on either side waved coloured ribbons and threw petals as the Municipal Band played "Symponik Nabooalla" on instruments including trumpets and drums, proceeded by Gungan flag-bearers. The Band marched towards the [[Theed Royal Palace|palace]], surrounding [[Bombad General]] [[Jar Jar Binks]], [[Captain]] [[Roos Tarpals]] and [[Boss]] [[Rugor Nass|Nass]]. Waiting for the Gungan procession at the end of the road were [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala]], her [[Royal Naboo Security Forces|security force]] and its [[Captain]] [[Quarsh Panaka]], [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and [[Sio Bibble]], holding the [[Globe of Peace]]. Accompanying them were members of the [[Jedi High Council]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], along with his [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and the droid [[R2D2]]. 

Binks, Tarpals and Nass dismounted from their steeds and ascended the stairs. Nass and Amidala met in the middle and the Queen was handed the Globe of Peace by Bibble. Amidala gifted the Globe to Nass, who raised it above his head and exclaimed "Peace!". Nass' proclamation was met with rapturous applause.
