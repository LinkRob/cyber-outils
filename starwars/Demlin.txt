'''Demlins''' were ferocious animals native to the [[planet]] [[Batuu]].<ref name="Tales" />

==Biology and appearance==
Demlins were medium sized creatures with red-brown scaly skin and large [[Demlin scale|blue and purple scales]] on their backs and upper arms. They had four blue eyes and sharp teeth. Their long arms ended with four sharp claws on each hand. They had long tails with striped black and white markings. They stood on two legs ending in feet with three sharp claws.<ref name="Tales" />

==Behavior==
Demlins were aggressive creatures known to bite and scratch. They favored dark places like caves, and were known to attack in groups. Demlins generally stayed back in fights, taking turns to attack, then jumping up and slashing enemies with their claws.<ref name="Tales" />

During the [[High Republic Era]] some Demlins on [[Batuu]] were corrupted by an [[Ancient Sith Runestone]], making them even more aggressive and ferocious than normal.<ref name="Tales" />

==Demlins in the Galaxy==
During the [[High Republic Era]] (circa [[232 BBY]] to [[233 BBY]]), [[Jedi Master]] [[Sylwin]] found an [[Ancient Sith Runestone]] and brought it back to the [[Batuu Jedi temple|Jedi temple on Batuu]] to study with the help of Master [[Cohmac Vitus]].<ref name="Last Call">''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge – Last Call]]''</ref> The artefact caused the Jedi to turn against each other, leaving [[Mirialan]] [[Padawan]] [[Ady Sun'Zee]] as the only survivor. It also corrupted multiple Demlins in the surrounding area. These Demlins attacked Ady at the entrance to the temple where she fought off and killed many of them. Just as one final Demlin was about to attack, Jedi master [[Yoda]] appeared and threw the Demlin out of a window easily with [[The Force]]. Yoda then entered the temple through the main entrance, while Ady entered through the caverns. More Demlins had found their way into the temple and attacked Ady in the Main Hall, entering through the balcony doors. Ady and multiple [[Jedi Temple Security Droid]]s fought them off, Ady destroying the entrances to where the Demlins were coming from and killed the remaining creatures. Ady later experienced visions of Demlins attacking her and Yoda until they managed to contain the power of the [[Ancient Sith Runestone|relic]], pushing back the darkness together.<ref name="Tales" />

In the year [[34 ABY]], a [[Unidentified droid repair technician|droid repair technician]] encountered and fought many Demlins in the caverns in the [[Hissiq Springs]] while fighting the [[Guavian Death Gang]], after being sent to retrieve two crates by the [[Utai]] droid technician [[Mubo]]. They then brought the [[Demlin scale]]s back to [[Seezelslak's Cantina]] and gave them to the [[Azumel]] bartender [[Seezelslak]] along with [[acid-spitter tail]]s and [[terateak pod]]s for the Azumel to make a drink out of them. Seezelslak then told the technician the story of [[Ady Sun'Zee]] and the [[Batuu Jedi temple incident|dark temple]].<ref name="Tales" /> The technician later encountered a Demlin in the caverns below the [[Batuu Jedi temple|ancient Jedi temple]] while retrieving an [[Ancient Sith Runestone| ancient Sith relic]] for [[Ithorian]] collector [[Dok-Ondar]].<ref name="Last Call" />
