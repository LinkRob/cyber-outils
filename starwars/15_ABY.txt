'''15 ABY''', or '''15<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>  After the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="Timelines" /> 

==Events==
*[[Luke Skywalker]] begins [[Jedi training|training]] his nephew [[Ben Solo]] in the ways of the [[Jedi]].<ref name="Timelines" />
*The [[Jedi Order]] is restored.<ref name="Timelines" />
*[[Kadara Calrissian]] is abducted by the [[First Order]].<ref name="Timelines" />

==Births==
*[[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] on [[Hyperkarn]]<ref name="Timelines" />
*[[Julen Rakab]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Rakab">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]]. As ''A Crash of Fate'' establishes that [[Julen Rakab]] is nineteen [[Standard year|years]] old, it can be deduced that he was [[Reproduction|born]] in 15 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref>
*[[Karr Nuq Sin]] on [[Merokia]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Nuq Sin">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Force Collector]]'' to [[32 ABY]], and also establishes that [[Karr Nuq Sin]] is seventeen [[Standard year|years]] old. Therefore, Nuq Sin was [[Reproduction|born]] on [[Merokia]] around 15 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref>
