The '''Battle of Foerost'''<ref name="Battlefront" /> occurred by around [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> sharing its name with the terrestrial astronomical object [[Foerost]].<ref name="ROnovel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref> [[Captain]] [[Tabor Seitaron]], a [[teacher]] of military history at the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Carida Academy]], participated in the battle and considered his part to be among his career's triumphs. When giving the captain a tour around<ref name="Battlefront" /> the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> ''[[Herald]]''<ref name="Battlefront" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="3 ABY">{{BTCdate}}</ref> an [[Unidentified Imperial ensign (Herald)|Imperial ensign]] commented to Seitaron on how the ''Herald''{{'s}} ten percent increase in efficiency could have been useful at the Battle of Foerost.<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Foerost was mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
