The '''Cron Cluster''' was a [[star cluster]]. According to the [[Qel-Droma Epics]], [[star]]s in the cluster were triggered to go [[supernova]] by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Naga Sadow]], resulting in the [[Cron Supernova]] which devastated the nearby [[Jedi]] [[planet]] [[Ossus]].<ref name="NOP" />

{{Star-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Cron Cluster was first [[canon]]ically mentioned in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Nexus of Power]]''.<ref name="NOP" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Cron Cluster/Legends|Cron Cluster]] first appeared in [[Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War 4]], where it was a cluster of ten [[Star/Legends|stars]] in the [[Auril sector/Legends|Auril sector's]] [[Cron system]], which were triggered to go [[Cron Supernova/Legends|supernova]] by the [[Krath/Legends|Krath]] sorceress [[Aleema Keto]].<ref name="TOTJ TSW 4">[[Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War 4|''Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War'' 4]]</ref>
