He portrayed a [[N5-Z2T|security droid]] in "[[Chapter 15: The Believer]]," the fifteenth episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''<ref name="Chapter 15" /> and was a stunt performer and performance capture artist in the film ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''.<ref name="TMAG"></ref> He was the suit actor for [[Embo]] in the film ''The Mandalorian and Grogu''.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://rebelscumconventions.com/pages/guests-1|text=Guests (2026)|work=[https://rebelscumconventions.com Rebel Scum Con]|archivedate=20260527080825}}</ref>
