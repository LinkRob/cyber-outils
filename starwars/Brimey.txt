'''Brimey''' was a pet [[gotis]] who once belonged to a [[Brimey's owner|senator]], until he was stolen by [[Sellaccc Orryak]], leader of the [[Ganguls]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Despite gotis being rare animals, she was not interested in selling it, but rather enjoy the clout that it entailed among the pirates of the [[Yarrum Tower]].<ref name="A Pirate's Pet" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brimey first appeared in "[[A Pirate's Pet]]," the first part of the [[A Pirate's Pet / The Secret Ship|third episode]] of the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|second season]] of the [[Disney Junior]] and [[Disney+]] animated television series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'',<ref name="A Pirate's Pet" /> which aired on [[August 14]], [[2024]].<ref name="YJA S2 Release">{{SW|url=news/young-jedi-adventures-season-2-and-nubs-shorts|text=''Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures'' Returns for Season 2}}</ref>
