The criminal organization [[Black Sun]] maintained a '''stronghold''' in [[Ord Mantell City]] on the [[planet]] [[Ord Mantell]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Later during the war, after Black Sun had joined the [[Shadow Collective]], the Collective's forces as well as its leader, [[Maul]], used the stronghold as a staging ground for the inevitable [[Battle on Ord Mantell|Battle of Ord Mantell]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]].<ref name="SoD 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:MantellCommandHall_concept.png|thumb|left|150px|Concept art of the stronghold's command post]]
The Black Sun stronghold appeared in the [[2014]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] [[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 2|''Darth Maul—Son of Dathomir'' 2]], written by [[Jeremy Barlow]] and drawn by [[Juan Frigeri]].<ref name="SoD 2" /> The comic was adapted from scripts for an unfinished story arc of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated series, written by [[Matt Michnovetz]] and [[Aïda Mashaka Croal]].<ref name="SoD TPB">''[[Star Wars: Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir]]''</ref> Concept art of the stronghold's command post was made by [[Will Nichols]] for episode production code 6.22 of the original arc.<ref name="Concept">{{Blogspot|subdomain=willnicholsportfolio|url=2018/04/backgrounds.html|name=WILL NICHOLS PORTFOLIO|text=BACKGROUNDS|date=2018-04-02}}</ref>
