'''Cyclic insulation''' was a type of material that was used on [[starship]]s. Installing interlocking layers of cyclic insulation on a starship would improve the durability of [[deflector shield]]s when in heavily ionized areas, such as the [[Sayma Nebula]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an amount of the insulation had been stored in an encryption sealed container in the [[Khepi system]] by [[Rokana Raiders]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> [[Kay Vess]] recovered the stash of cyclic insulation from the container in the [[Mynock Lair]] in the Khepi system.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}
