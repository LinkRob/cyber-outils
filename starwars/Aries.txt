'''Aries''' was a [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor|A-wing]] [[pilot]] in [[Riot Squadron]], a [[squadron]] that served both the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and the [[New Republic]]. At some point during Aries' service to Riot Squadron, the pilot was [[Death|killed]]. Several [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> during the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]'s [[Attack on the Hellion's Dare|attack]] on the [[frigate]] ''[[Hellion's Dare]]'', Riot Squadron pilot [[Wyl Lark]] thought of the names of deceased squadron personnel, including Aries.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aries was first mentioned in the [[2019]] novel [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']], written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
