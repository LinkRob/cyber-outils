'''Electromag-pulse disruptors''' were a type of ion melee weapon and tool that was designed to short-out electronic equipment. Consisting of a slim stylus and costing 180 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], the disruptor was used as a tool in the daily work of some [[technician]]s, who kept it handy in order to deal with [[computer]]s or [[droid]]s that they lost control of.<ref name="SM" />

The disruptors could also be used successfully as an improvised weapon in combat situations and could disorientate foes. In the [[Core Worlds]] this was rarely necessary, but in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] the weapon could mean the difference between life and [[death]] for technicians set upon by [[pirate]]s or marauders.<ref name="SM" />
