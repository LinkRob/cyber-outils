'''Darth VaGhal''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]]<ref name="Instagram" /> who was active during the reign of [[Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire]], between [[130 ABY]] and [[138 ABY]].<ref name="Dating note">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2014/04/09/art-from-the-darth-maul-game-you-39-ll-never-get-to-play.aspx|text=Art From The Darth Maul Game You'll Never Get To Play|author=Andrew Reiner|date=2014-04-09|work=[[Wikipedia:Game Informer|''Game Informer'']]|archivedate=20240223064541|nolive=1}} &mdash; ''[[Battle of the Sith Lords]]'' would have taken place during the reign of [[Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire]], which was founded in [[130 ABY]] according to [[Legacy (2006) 1|''Legacy'' (2006) 1]] and dissolved in [[138 ABY]] according to [[Legacy—War 6|''Legacy—War'' 6]]. Therefore, [[Darth/Legends|Darth]] VaGhal must have been active between those two dates.</ref> VaGhal had [[Color/Legends|yellow]] [[Eye/Legends|eyes]]; black, braided [[Hair/Legends|hair]]; light skin; and black markings on their face and forehead.<ref name="ArtStation" />

VaGhal wore a black [[Clothing/Legends|outfit]] that was comprised of both cloth and [[Armor/Legends|armor]]. Portions of VaGhal's torso and [[Limb/Legends|arms]] were exposed while the armor primarily covered their neck. Thin tendrils extended out of the outfit near VaGhal's elbows. In addition to the upper body armor, VaGhal wore a long black skirt made up of the same material as the tendrils extending from their forearms. VaGhal also wielded a purple-bladed [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] with a black [[Lightsaber hilt/Legends|hilt]].<ref name="ArtStation" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth VaGhal is a character created for [[Red Fly Studio]]'s canceled [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Battle of the Sith Lords]]''. VaGhal's design was created by concept artist [[Thomas A. Szakolczay]]<ref name="ArtStation" /> at some point prior to the game's cancellation in [[2011]].<ref name="GI article">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2014/04/09/art-from-the-darth-maul-game-you-39-ll-never-get-to-play.aspx|text=Art From The Darth Maul Game You'll Never Get To Play|author=Andrew Reiner|date=2014-04-09|work=[[Wikipedia:Game Informer|''Game Informer'']]|archivedate=20240223064541|nolive=1}}</ref>
