'''"Muse"''' was the [[callsign]] of a<ref name="Muse" /> [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> [[pilot]] who flew a [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]] for the [[First Order]].<ref name="Muse" /> She<ref name="Evil Resurgent" /> led [[Epsilon Squadron]], a [[squadron]] that served in the [[First Order Navy|naval forces]] of the [[First Order]] [[First Order military|military]].<ref name="Muse" /> At some point by [[35 ABY]],<ref name="35 ABY">''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' establishes that the [[First Order]] fell thirty-five [[Standard year|years]] after the events of {{Film|IV}}, which corresponds to [[35 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> she participated in a [[Starfighter combat|battle]] above a celestial body alongside two other First Order [[TIE series|TIE fighters]].<ref name="Muse" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|It was a real privilege getting the chance to paint some new cards for y'all and I hope y'all enjoy this one!|Anthony Devine|<ref name="Deviant">{{DeviantArt|username=anthonydevine|url=anthonydevine/art/STAR-WARS-The-Force-Awakens-Epsilon-Leader-559603640|name=[[Anthony Devine]]|text=STAR WARS The Force Awakens Epsilon Leader|date=2015-09-11}}</ref>}}
"Muse" was first pictured in [[The Force Awakens Core Set|''The Force Awakens'' Core Set]] for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Miniatures Game]]'',<ref name="Epsilon Leader">{{FFGXW|set=The Force Awakens Core Set|cardname="Epsilon Leader"}}</ref> which released on [[September 4]], [[2015]].<ref name="Man Your Ships!">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2015/9/4/man-your-ships|text=Man Your Ships!|date=2015-09-04}}</ref> Within the Core Set she was depicted on the card "Epsilon Leader,"<ref name="Epsilon Leader" /> and her callsign of "Muse" was later revealed in the article "Evil Resurgent," published [[August 3]], [[2018]].<ref name="Evil Resurgent" /> The illustration depicting "Muse" piloting a TIE fighter was done by [[Anthony Devine]], who described painting "Muse" as amazing, humbling, and a real privilege.<ref name="Deviant"/>
