'''1999 Topps ''Star Wars: Episode I'' Widevision Series 1''' is a trading card set covering the events of {{Film|I}}. Each card had a frame from the movie and a summary of that scene on the back. Also on the backs were other photos and more details. There was a 40 card expansion set inserted into hobby sets. The set was released by [[Topps]] in [[1999]].<ref name="TCDB" />
