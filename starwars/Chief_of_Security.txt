'''Chief of Security''', or '''Security Chief'''<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> was a title given to individuals responsible for the security and defense of locations that were of importance. [[Battlemaster]] [[Cin Drallig]] was the Chief of Security for the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Cin Drallig" /> By [[3 BBY]],<ref name="3 BBY">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'' states that [[Phoenix Cell]] relocated to [[Chopper Base]] three years before the [[Battle of Yavin]] This means "[[The Mystery of Chopper Base]]," which depicts parts of the relocation, takes place in [[3 BBY]]. It also states that [[Zeb's Hideout]], seen in "The Mystery of Chopper Base," is the place where [[Garazeb Orrelios]] relaxes from his duties as Chief of Secuirty, meaning he was Chief of Security by 3 BBY.</ref> [[Captain]] [[Garazeb Orrelios]] served as [[Chief]] of Security for [[Phoenix Cell]]'s [[Chopper Base]] on the [[planet]] [[Atollon]].<ref name="Warhead">{{Rebels|Warhead (episode)}}</ref> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Occ">According to ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'', the occupation of Mon Cala took place a year into the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor's]] rule. Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the beginning of Imperial rule to [[19 BBY]], the occupation of Mon Cala took place in [[18 BBY]].</ref> [[Gial Ackbar]] was the Chief of Security for [[King]] [[Lee-Char]].<ref name="Dark Lord 13">[[Darth Vader (2017) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 13]]</ref>

The [[Mon Calamari]] [[Ghal Tarpfen]] served as Chief of Security of the [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] during the [[High Republic Era|High Republic era]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" />
