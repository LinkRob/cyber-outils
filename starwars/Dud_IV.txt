A '''Dud IV''' was a piece used in the game [[Canto Pass]] which resembled a [[shock grenade]]. A single Dud was worth a negative point with each additional [[Dud piece]] doubling the negative score.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a Dud IV piece had been stored at a [[Hutt Supply Camp|supply camp]] operated by the criminal [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] in the [[Hunter's Canopy]] region of [[Akiva]], a [[jungle]] [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] found the container and stole the game piece.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A Dud IV Canto Pass piece appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It was one of several Canto Pass pieces that could be collected in the game and used as trinkets to customize the appearance of the player's [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]] and the interior of the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', the player's [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
