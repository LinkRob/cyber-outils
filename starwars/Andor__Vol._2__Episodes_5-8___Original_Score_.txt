'''''Andor'': Vol.2 (Episodes 5-8) [Original Score]''' is the soundtrack for the fifth to eighth episodes of [[Andor Season 1|Andor]]: "[[The Axe Forgets]]," "[[The Eye]]," "[[Announcement]]" and "[[Narkina 5 (episode)|Narkina 5]]." It was composed by [[Nicholas Britell]] and released for digital streaming on [[November 4]], [[2022]].<ref name="Vol2 Announcement" />
