'''Caw-crabs''' were a type of [[crab]] that could be found in the [[Sarini Island]] [[Sarini Island zoo|zoo]] on<ref name="ALD" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]. During a visit to the [[zoo]]<ref name="ALD" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> [[Brentin Lore Wexley|Brentin]], [[Temmin Wexley|Temmin]], and [[Norra Wexley]] saw several caw-crabs scuttling and splashing around in their enclosures.<ref name="ALD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Caw-crabs were mentioned in ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', a [[2016]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the second installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="ALD" />
