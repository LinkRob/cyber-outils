The '''Anzellan Droidsmiths'''<ref name="Instagram">{{Instagram|username=starwars|url=p/CpdB53ZJzKN|name=[[Star Wars]]|date=2023-02-21|quote=#TheArmorer and #TheAnzellanDroidsmiths[&hellip;]|archivedate=20230306195209}}</ref> were a legendary<ref name="Graphic Novel">[[The Mandalorian – The Graphic Novel of Season 3|''The Mandalorian'' – The Graphic Novel of Season 3]]</ref> crew of three [[Anzellan]] [[droidsmith]]s living on the [[planet]] [[Nevarro]]. By the time of the [[New Republic]], they found great commercial success when Nevarro was thriving, as they had precise and affordable expertise to keep automated excavation equipment used for belt mining running under extreme conditions. [[High Magistrate]] [[Greef Karga]] tasked them to keep his [[administrative droid]]s and [[foot droid]]s operational.<ref name="TMVD">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> They were requested by [[Din Djarin]] to fix [[IG-11]], who previously [[Self-destruct mechanism|self-destructed]]. The Anzellans could not fix the droid, because his [[memory circuit]] was broken, a component that was very hard to find at that time as they were not being made anymore.<ref name="Chapter 17" /> The Anzellans continued to tinker with IG-11's remains, however, and eventually rebuild him into [[IG-12]], a [[Mech]] that could be piloted by Anzellans or anyone of similar stature, such as [[Din Grogu|Grogu]].<ref name="Chapter 23">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 23: The Spies}}</ref>

The crew initially consisted of a trio named [[Minch]], [[Keeto]] and [[Bai]].<ref name="Chapter 17" /> Minch served as the group's spokesbeing, often taking the lead when dealing with clients, since he had the strongest grasp of [[Basic]].<ref name="TMVD" /> They were later joined by a fourth member and newcomer named [[Clang]].<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-mandalorian-and-grogu-trivia|text=''Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu'' Behind-the-Scenes Facts and Trivia}}</ref> The four helped Djarin make modifications on his new ship, the ''[[Razor Crest 2.0]]''. Although one night [[Embo]] and his [[anooba]] [[Keibu]] came to Nevarro and kidnapped Djarin and by accident Minch. The other three left with Grogu in their [[Anzellan Starship]] to [[Nal Hutta]], where they rescued both but Djarin was too big to fit in their starship and so he and Grogu stayed behind but the droidsmiths came back with [[Adelphi Rangers]] lead by [[Colonel]] [[Ward]] to rescue Djarin, Grogu, and [[Rotta]].<ref name="The Mandalorian and Grogu">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref>
