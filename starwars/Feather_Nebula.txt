The '''Feather Nebula''' was a [[nebula]] located in the [[Irnaj sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western" /> It was located in grid square K-17 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Feather Nebula was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Feather Nebula/Legends|nebula]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in "[[The Guns of Kelrodo-Ai (short story)|The Guns of Kelrodo-Ai]]," a short story written by Fry and published in [[Star Wars Insider 132|issue 132]] of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine<ref name="Guns">{{InsiderCite|132|The Guns of Kelrodo-Ai (short story)}}</ref> on [[March 13]], [[2012]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanMagazines|url=t/star-wars-insider/us/132|text=Star Wars Insider Issue #132}}</ref>
