The '''Begamor two-headed dog''' was a type of [[dog]] found on the [[planet]] [[Begamor]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="TVoSD" /> In [[200 BBY]],<ref name="TVoSD date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Sean]] [[Mission to Begamor|inspected]] the [[Presidential manor|estate]] of [[President]] [[Jamie Brasson]] on Begamor. While visiting the kitchen of the [[Latero]] [[Chef|head chef]] [[Marino Pomana]], Sean noticed that around half of Pomana's branded [[Foodstuff|food]] products were counterfeit goods produced by the local company [[Opera Trade]]. Pomana acknowledged Sean's abilities, but warned that they could not afford to let their guard down. He further threatened to feed Sean to the president's Begamor two-headed dog if he made even the slightest mistake in the kitchen.<ref name="TVoSD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Begamor two-headed dog was mentioned in the [[Wikipedia:Written Chinese|Chinese]] web novel ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''<ref name="TVoSD" /> written by "[[His Majesty the King]]," within its fifth chapter, which was released on [[December 18]], [[2020]]. The dog's name was written in [[Wikipedia:Simplified Chinese characters|simplified Chinese]] as "贝加摩尔双头犬" (bèi jiā mó ěr shuāng tóu quǎn).<ref name="Chapter 5">{{WebCite|url=https://www.qidian.com/chapter/1015950219/623147707|text=第5章 独门秘方 _《星球大战：白银誓约》小说在线阅读|author=[[His Majesty the King]]|date=2020-12-18|language=[[Wikipedia:Simplified Chinese characters|Simplified Chinese]]|archivedate=20260514141239}}</ref>
