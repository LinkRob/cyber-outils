'''BG-RT's Tavern and Spirits''', also known simply as '''BG-RT's''', was a [[cantina]] located within [[Level 1313]] on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. In [[1 BBY]], a [[Shootout at BG-RT's Tavern and Spirits|shootout]] took place in the building between the [[cyborg]] [[Beilert Valance]] and several [[bounty hunter]]s who had arrived to [[Death|kill]] him.

==Description==
BG-RT's Tavern and Spirits was located on<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="UltimateSW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] within [[Level 1313]], which consisted of at least two floors. A [[droid]] was stationed at the entrance, inspecting the firearms of guests who entered the [[cantina]], while another droid served as the cantina's [[bartender]].<ref name="Target Vader 1" />

==History==
{{Quote|You're not the only one who's come to BG-RT's to settle a score with me tonight.|Beilert Valance, to Xonr|<ref name="Target Vader 1" />}}
[[File:XonrValance-TargetVader1.jpg|thumb|left|245px|At the cantina, Xonr planned to kill Beilert Valance for violating the Bounty Hunter Code.]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Target Vader 1|''Target Vader'' 1]] to [[1 BBY]].</ref> the [[cyborg]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]] visited the cantina to drink. The bounty hunter [[Xonr]] later entered the cantina and found Valance drunk at a table. Xonr approached the cyborg and sat in front of him. After downing Valance's drink, Xonr threatened to [[Death|kill]] him for breaking the [[Bounty Hunter Code]].<ref name="Target Vader 1" />

As Xonr aimed a [[blaster]] at the cyborg from underneath the table, other bounty hunters around the cantina began preparing their weapons. A [[Shootout at BG-RT's Tavern and Spirits|shootout]] ensued when Valance killed Xonr using his [[palm blaster]]. Valance, who in reality had allowed the hunters to follow him to the cantina, survived the encounter and eliminated all of them. Following the shootout, he [[Galactic Credit Standard|paid]] the bartender droid for his next round and raised his glass, with the cantina patrons raising theirs in response. He then left the cantina and made his way to [[Docking Bay 21]], where his [[starship]], the ''[[Broken Wing]]'', was parked.<ref name="Target Vader 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
BG-RT's Tavern and Spirits appeared in the [[Target Vader 1|first issue]] of the [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Target Vader]]''. It was written by [[Robbie Thompson]], illustrated by [[Marc Laming]] and [[Cris Bolson]], and released by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> on [[July 3]], [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/77664/star_wars_target_vader_2019_1|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #1}}</ref>
