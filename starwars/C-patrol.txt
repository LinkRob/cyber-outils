'''C-patrol''' was a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] patrol during the [[Imperial Era]]. It consisted of two [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilots]] patrolling the [[planet]] [[Agaris]]. One [[Unidentified TIE pilot (Gall)|pilot]] was disciplined by an [[Unidentified Imperial officer (C-patrol)|officer]] on the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|ISD]] ''[[Executrix]]'' for an inappropriate comment, and the pair later pursued the rebels [[Lina Graf|Lina]] and [[Milo Graf]] on Agaris' surface.<ref name="The Rescue" />
