The '''''Forerunner II'''''<ref name="Bounty Hunters 19" /> was a [[light cruiser]] in the service of the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 18" /> It was commanded by [[Admiral]] [[Tarl Sokoli]],<ref name="Bounty Hunters 19" /> an Imperial war hero.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 18" /> The [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], displeased by Sokoli's public dissatisfaction with the Empire, falsely claimed that Sokoli was secretly working for the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate. He tasked [[Beilert Valance]], [[cyborg]] [[bounty hunter]] who had been captured and forced to serve Vader as the leader of [[Dark Squadron]], with [[Assassination of Tarl Sokoli|assassinating]] Sokoli; Valance had formerly served the Empire after being inspired by Sokoli, and the [[Sith Lord]] wished to test the cyborg.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 19" />

The ''Forerunner II'' was directed by Vader to a location outside the [[Eriadu system]], where the [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter|TIE Defenders]] of Dark Squadron attacked it, engaging with the [[cruiser]]'s own [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] escort. Though two members of Valance's squadron were shot down, [[Dark Three (pilot)|one]] by the [[cannon]]s of the ''Forerunner II'' itself, the cyborg crashed his own [[TIE series|TIE]] into the light cruiser's [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], confronting and killing Sokoli before leaping into [[Realspace|space]] to be picked up by one of his [[squadron]]'s [[starfighter]]s.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 19" />
