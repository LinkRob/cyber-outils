The '''Daclavian system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Perkell sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square Q-7 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Daclavian 3]].<ref name="Origin" />

==Behind the scenes==
