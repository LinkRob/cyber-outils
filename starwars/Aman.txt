'''Aman''' was a [[Sith apprentice]]<ref name="Focus">{{SWYouTube|series=Star Wars: Visions Filmmaker Focus|text=THE BIRD OF PARADISE|video=fSaJe2qO0J4}}</ref> who wielded two [[Color|red]] [[lightsaber]]s and at some point engaged in a [[lightsaber duel]] with the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Nakime]] and [[Nakime's Jedi Master|her master]]. During the duel she struck Nakime's eyes, blinding her and causing her to fall down a cliff.<ref name="Paradise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aman appeared in "[[The Bird of Paradise]]," a [[Canon|non-canon]] short film directed by [[Tadahiro Yoshihira]] and released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume ]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Polygon Pictures]], released on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]]. The character was first pictured in a trailer released on the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' Youtube channel on [[September 25]], 2025.<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|1lpOORwkbdE|Star Wars: Visions Volume 3 &#124; English Dub Trailer &#124; October 29 on Disney+}}</ref>

Aman was based on a [[Wikipedia:Japanese mythology|Japanese mythology]] goddess known as [[Wikipedia:Amanozako|Amanosagume]], the origin of the folkloric mischievous demon [[Wikipedia:Amanojaku|Amanojaku]]. Aman's design was a blend of [[Wikipedia:Oni|oni]], horned demons in Japanese folktales, [[Sith tattoo]]s with [[Wikipedia:Kabuki|kabuki]] makeup and [[Wikipedia:Kumadori|kumadori]] patterns. Also included were motifs of the [[Wikipedia:Joker (character)|Joker]] and skulls symbolizing death, as well as aspects drawn from Japanese [[Wikipedia:Vajrayana|esoteric Buddhism]].<ref name="PolygonInterview">{{WebCite|url=https://www.polygon.com/star-wars-visions-bird-of-paradise-interview|text='Japanese folktales and mythology' inspired the most stunning Star Wars story in years|date=2025-11-10|work=[[Wikipedia:Polygon (website)|Polygon]]|archivedate=20251110152916}}</ref>
