'''At Arissia''' was a [[planet]] within [[the galaxy]] that was one of the [[Jewels of the Old Republic]]. The [[Fallen Sanctum]] on [[At Achrann]], another of the Jewels, had a pillar with the [[hyperspace]] coordinates to At Arissia, along with one for each of the other Jewels. By the time of the [[New Republic]], At Arissia and seven of the other Jewels were believed to have been destroyed by the wider galaxy.<ref name="Can't Say">{{SkeletonCrew|Can't Say I Remember No At Attin}}</ref>
