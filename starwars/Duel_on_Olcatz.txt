A '''duel''' occurred on the [[planet]] [[Olcatz]] between the former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] and [[Aneé-san]], the [[bodyguard]] of the [[Anzellan]] [[Que-Dama]], after the Ronin's [[astromech droid]] companion [[R5-D56]] caused a disruption in Que-Dama's [[To-Ba|gambling hall]] aboard an [[AK-BK]] [[walker]]. During the fighting, the walker fell off the side of a [[mountain]], and the Ronin and [[Aneé-san]] continued fighting on the upside-down walker's feet. [[The Grand Master]], leader of the [[Crusaders (The Grand Master)|Crusaders]] [[Jedi]] sect, interrupted the duel and injured Aneę-san, but the Ronin [[Force push|pushed]] her away before the Grand Master could [[Death|kill]] her. The Grand Master defeated the Ronin as well, but R5-D56 sacrificed himself to allow him to escape.<ref name="Payback" />
