'''Bo Keevil''' was a [[Kel Dor]] [[Gender|male]] who served as a [[pilot]] in [[Ace Squadron]], a [[squadron]] charged with defending the ''[[Colossus]]'' fueling station on the planet [[Castilon]]. Keevil and his fellow pilots also doubled as racers and escorted shipments. Following the [[First Order]]'s [[Occupation of the Colossus|occupation]] of the ''Colossus'', Keevil helped [[Team Colossus|Team ''Colossus'']] in helping the ''Colossus'' [[Battle of Castilon|escape]] into [[hyperspace]].

==Biography==
===Ace Squadron===
During the [[New Republic era]], Bo Keevil was a member of [[Ace Squadron]], a squadron of starfighter pilots who defended the ''[[Colossus]]'' refueling platform on the planet [[Castilon]]. Keevil's fellow pilots included squadron leader [[Hype Fazon]], [[Torra Doza]], [[Freya Fenris]], and [[Griff Halloran]]. Keevil and his fellow pilots also took part in the ''Colossus''{{'}} many races.<ref name="The Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref> He owned a racer called the ''[[Yellow Ace]]''.<ref name="Bo Keevil" />

Keevil and his fellow Aces resided in the luxurious [[Doza Tower]].<ref name="The High Tower">{{Resistance|The High Tower}}</ref> When the undercover [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] accidentally challenged Ace Squadron to a [[Race between Kazuda Xiono and Torra Doza|race]], Keevil's face appeared on a [[hologram]] panel.<ref name="The Recruit" />

===Gorr's pirate raid===
Later, Keevil and his fellow Ace pilots responded to a [[Attack on the Colossus|raid]] by [[Kragan Gorr]]'s [[Warbird gang]]. The pirates had the upper hand until Xiono managed to jam their communications. This jamming allowed the Ace pilots to gain the upper hand and drive the pirates away. Keevil also shot down a pirate [[starfighter]] piloted by an individual wearing a [[stormtrooper]] helmet.<ref name="The Triple Dark">{{Resistance|The Triple Dark}}</ref>

===Kaz's first visit===
[[File:AcesLounge-TheHighTower.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Keevil at the Aces' Lounge.]]
Keevil and his fellow Ace pilots were in the [[Aces' Lounge]] when the mechanics Xiono and [[Tamara Ryvora]] visited Doza Tower as guests of Fazon.<ref name="The High Tower" />

===Gorr's second pirate raid===
While Keevil and his fellow Aces were escorting a shipment, Gorr, at the behest of the [[First Order]], launched a [[Raid on the Colossus|second raid]] on the ''Colossus''. Though the pirates were rebuffed, this raid convinced [[Captain]] [[Imanuel Doza]], the owner of the ''Colossus'', to reconsider talks with the [[First Order]].<ref name="Synara's Score">{{Resistance|Synara's Score}}</ref>

===Xiono's second visit===
Keevil and the other Aces were socializing at the Aces' Lounge when Xiono visited Doza Tower as a guest of Captain Doza's daughter, Torra. Xiono was on a [[Second mission to Doza Tower|secret mission]] to spy on a meeting between Captain Doza and [[Commander]] [[Pyre]] of the First Order.<ref name="Secrets and Holograms">{{Resistance|Secrets and Holograms}}</ref>

===Casual adventures===
Keevil took part in a race with his fellow Aces. Torra initially took the lead but consumed up her booster fuel too early. This allowed Hype to take the lead and maintain his title as the top racer in the Aces.<ref name="The Need for Speed">{{Resistance|The Need for Speed}}</ref>

Later, Keevil and his fellow Aces took part in a mission to escort an incoming freighter. When the freighter had trouble landing due to a pirate attack, the Aces had to work together to slow down its descent.<ref name="Sixty Seconds" />

===Bibo incident===
During the [[Bibo incident]], Bo and his fellow Aces defended the ''Colossus'' against [[Bibo's mother]], a [[rokkna]] sea monster. Despite their efforts and the assistance of Kaz, they were unable to repel the rokkna. One of the rokkna's tentacles collided with the steering vanes of Bo's racer ''[[Yellow Ace]]'', causing it to crash. In the end, Kaz's friend [[Neeku Vozo]] managed to end the rokkna's rampage by returning [[Bibo]] to his mother.<ref name="Bibo">{{Resistance|Bibo (episode)}}</ref>

===Kidnapping of Torra Doza===
Following the [[Torra Doza's abduction|kidnapping]] of their fellow Ace [[Torra Doza]], Bo Keevil and his fellow pilots were dispatched in pursuit of her kidnappers. Torra was rescued by [[Major]] [[Elrik Vonreg]], who convinced Captain Pyre to accept First Order protection.<ref name="Doza Dilemma">{{Resistance|The Doza Dilemma}}</ref>

===First Order occupation===
During the First Order's [[Occupation of the Colossus|military occupation]] of the Colossus, Commander Pyre cancelled all races and banned Ace Squadron from flying. When Captain Doza broke the news to them and also explains that First Order aerial squadrons will provide all aerial security. Bo Keevil asks what they are supposed to do when they can't race and can't fly.<ref name="The Disappeared">{{Resistance|The Disappeared}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
[[File:TeamColossus-NoEscapeP2.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Team Colossus]]
Keevil was a male [[Kel Dor]] with [[Color|yellow]] skin. He was described as a soft-spoken, superb, and confident pilot. He preferred to let his racer, ''[[Yellow Ace]]'', do the talking for him.<ref name="Bo Keevil" />

==Equipment==
Bo Keevil flew a customized [["Changeling" Mark 71NB]], the ''[[Yellow Ace]]'', and owned the droid [[SC-X2]].<ref name="USW NE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> Due to his [[Kel Dor]] anatomy, he wore an [[antiox breath mask]].<ref name="The Recruit" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bo Keevil is a character created for the animated series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''. He speaks so infrequently that the "Meet the Aces" featurette does not list his voice actor, unlike the other Aces.<ref name="Meet the Aces">{{SW|url=news/meet-the-aces-in-a-new-star-wars-resistance-featurette|text=Meet the Aces in a New ''Star Wars Resistance'' Featurette}}</ref> His first speaking role was in the short "[[Sixty Seconds to Destruction]],"<ref name="SStD">{{Resistance|Sixty Seconds to Destruction}}</ref> voiced by [[Dave Filoni]].<ref name="Doza Dilemma" />
