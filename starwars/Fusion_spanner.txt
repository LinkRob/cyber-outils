A '''fusion spanner''' was a type of [[spanner]]. While aboard the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]''<ref name="Into the Unknown" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 ABY">{{ResistanceDate|2|1}}</ref> the [[racer]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Torra Doza]] carried one of the spanners with<ref name="Into the Unknown" /> her<ref name="USWNE" /> in a toolbox. As the [[Resistance]] [[Spy|operative]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] attempted to fix the ''Colossus''{{'s}} [[artificial gravity]] systems, he requested a fusion spanner from Doza, mistaking her for their former colleague [[Tamara Ryvora]].<ref name="Into the Unknown" />

==Behind the scenes==
