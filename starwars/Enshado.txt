The '''''Enshado''''' was a [[First Order Light Cruiser]] in service to the [[First Order]] during its [[Cold War|conflict]] with the [[Resistance]].

==History==
The [[First Order Light Cruiser]]<ref name="Poe Dameron 19" /> ''Enshado''<ref name="Poe Dameron 16" /> was commanded by [[Malarus]] during the [[time]] of the [[Mission to Spalex|operation]] to [[Spalex]]<ref name="Poe Dameron 19" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines" />

[[Gender|She]] was in command of the [[cruiser]] during its [[Mission to obtain fuel|theft]] of [[fuel]] aboard the [[freighter]] ''[[Romary]]'', although the [[Resistance]] was able to recover the fuel.<ref name="Poe Dameron 16" />

Once the [[First Order]] captured [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] using the captured [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Oddy Muva]], they went to Spalex. [[Agent]] [[Terex]] released Muva during his interrogation, allowing him to hotwire the [[Starship|ship's]] system as a distraction, allowing the [[Resistance pilot]]s to escape. Although Malarus was acting under orders not to initiate open conflict with the Resistance, she ordered the ''Enshado'' and its complement of [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] to attack. Muva had also stolen a [[TIE series|TIE]], and during the battle, steered his damaged craft into the [[hangar]], leading to the destruction of the cruiser and his [[death]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 19" />
