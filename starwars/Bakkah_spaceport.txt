'''Bakkah spaceport''' was a small [[Spaceport/Legends|spaceport]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Bakkah]]. It included a [[Cantina/Legends|cantina]] and a [[Medcenter/Legends|medical facility]]. [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] landed at the spaceport on a mission to recover a [[Sith talisman/Legends|Sith amulet]].
