{{Quote|Everything from the YT-450 to the YT-2400 can be had for a price, crews included. And the list of weapon configutations runs longer than the list carried by a Brentaali sommelier.|Lando Calrissian, to Admiral Gial Ackbar|<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>}}
The '''Brentaali''' were a social group. [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] compared the list of possible weapon configurations of the [[YT-series]] to a list carried by a Brentaali [[Wine|sommelier]] in a report on [[Starship|vessels]] that might suit the Alliance's needs that he sent to [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] prior to the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Brentaali were first mentioned in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
