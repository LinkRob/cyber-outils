'''Flix's mother''' was a [[Gender|female]] who had a [[Flix's mother's home|home]] on the celestial body [[Talivar]].<ref name="Ace Squadron" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 ABY">{{ResistanceDate|1|13}}</ref> the [[Gozzo]] Flix, his partner the [[Chadra-Fan]] [[Orka]], and their [[DUM-series pit droid|pit droid]] [[G-LN]]<ref name="Ace Squadron" /> visited Flix's [[Flix's flock|mother]] for a [[Standard day|day]], though Flix expressed disdain about going. Later that [[Time|day]], after the trio returned to<ref name="Dangerous Business" /> the [[planet]] [[Castilon]],<ref name="Ace Squadron" /> Flix refuted claims he looked like his [[Parent|mother]] while returning to [[Office of Acquisitions|Acquisitions]],<ref name="Dangerous Business" /> Flix's and Orka's place of business aboard the ''[[Colossus]]'' [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot||station]].<ref name="Ace Squadron" /> Later, after the [[First Order]] [[Occupation of the Colossus|occupied]] the ''Colossus'', Flix and Orka attempted to escape the occupation by hiding for several days in a [[Cargo container|cargo crate]], within Acquisitions, along with<ref name="No Escape: Part 1">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-no-escape-part-1-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 8 Fun Facts from "No Escape: Part 1" - ''Star Wars Resistance''|date=2019-03-11}}</ref> their pet [[gorg]] [[Bitey]]<ref name="Dangerous Business" /> and G-LN, intending to have the First Order ship them to Flix's [[Reproduction|mother]] on Talivar.<ref name="Ace Squadron" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flix's mother was first mentioned in "[[Dangerous Business]],"<ref name="Dangerous Business" /> the thirteenth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'',<ref name="DVD">''[[Star Wars Resistance: Complete Season One]]''</ref> which aired on [[January 20]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> The scene featuring the mother's mention was previewed four [[January 16|days]] before in a preview for the episode, which was published on the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel.<ref name="YouTube">{{SWYouTube|R5NyB_bAZI8|Pay Attention - "Dangerous Business" Preview &#124; Star Wars Resistance|date=2019-01-16}}</ref> 

They were identified in the corresponding episode guide as "Flix's mother"<ref name="Episode Guide DB">{{SW|url=star-wars-resistance/dangerous-business-episode-gallery|text="Dangerous Business" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> as well as the accompanying article "Bucket's List Extra: 6 Fun Facts from 'Dangerous Business' &ndash; ''Star Wars Resistance''," which were posted on [[January 21]], 2019. Before their role in "Dangerous Business" was finalized, the script originally had Flix and Orka leaving to handle a shipment problem. Another draft changed the reason for their departure to be securing a major deal. It was the second draft of the script that had the group visiting Flix's mother,<ref name="Bucket's List">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-6-fun-facts-from-dangerous-business-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 6 Fun Facts from "Dangerous Business" - ''Star Wars Resistance''|date=2019-01-21}}</ref> an idea from [[Brandon Auman]],<ref name="Eugene">{{WookYouTube|YGQ--m-MBIg|Wookieepedia Interviews - Eugene Son|date=2025-04-03}}</ref> the show's executive producer.<ref name="Brandon">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-resistance-set-for-fall-debut|text=''Star Wars Resistance'', Anime-Inspired Series, Set for Fall Debut|date=2018-04-26}}</ref>
