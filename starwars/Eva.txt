{{Quote|My name's Eva. What's yours?|Eva, to Emerie Karr|<ref name="Identity Crisis" />}}
'''Eva''' was a [[Youngling|young]]<ref name="Identity Crisis" /> [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Identity Crisis AD" /> [[Iktotchi]] who had a high [[Midi-chlorian|M-count]]. Eva was taken from<ref name="Identity Crisis /> her<ref name="Identity Crisis AD" /> home and kept as a [[prisoner]] of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] base at their [[Tantiss Base|base]] on [[Mount Tantiss]], living inside a [[Tantiss vault|vault]] along with several other M-count children, including [[Jax (Mirialan)|Jax]] and [[Sami (Pantoran)|Sami]], although the Empire insisted they were only specimens. Eva was designated '''SP-54''' and had her blood drawn daily as part of the Empire's [[Project Necromancer]].<ref name="Identity Crisis" />

Eva had a relationship with [[Nala Se]] until the [[Kaminoan]] was imprisoned by [[Doctor]] [[Royce Hemlock]], the head of Project Necromancer. When new lead scientist [[Emerie Karr]] began performing [[Nala Se]]'s duties, including interacting with Eva and the other children, Eva was friendly towards Karr, complementing her [[glasses]] and trying to talk with her.<ref name="Identity Crisis" /> She and the other children later escaped with the help of Doctor Karr and the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]].<ref name="Cavalry Has Arrived">{{TBB|The Cavalry Has Arrived}}</ref>
