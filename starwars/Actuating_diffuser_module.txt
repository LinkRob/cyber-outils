An '''actuating diffuser module''' was an [[blaster]] component that destabilized energy particles, causing blaster bolts to explode on impact.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> it was illegal to own a diffuser module in the [[Galactic Empire]]. However, [[Rodian]] [[scavenger]] [[Namda Eaakee]] came into possession of one of the modules.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] traded with Eaakee for the module in exchange for ten [[direct-energy circuit]]s. She later used it to the modify her [[VM-19 heavy blaster pistol]]'s plasma module.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Actuating diffuser modules appeared as an upgrade item in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The player must obtain the trigger in order upgrade their blaser's heavy plasma module to level four.<ref name="Outlaws" />
