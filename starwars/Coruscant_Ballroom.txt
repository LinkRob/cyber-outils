The '''Coruscant Ballroom''' was a location on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], where an event known as [[Coruscant Nights]] took place. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Painting|posters]] advertising Coruscant Nights in the Ballroom were put up in the [[Coruscant Underworld]], in order to attract potential customers. It showed the silhouette of two people against a [[Color|blue]] background and said "Coruscant Nights" at the top<ref name="Trackdown" /> and "Coruscant Ballroom" at the bottom.<ref name="Lethal Trackdown">In "[[Lethal Trackdown]]," the [[Aurebesh]] text "Coruscant Ballroom" can be read at the bottom of a [[Painting|poster]] glimpsed over the entrance to [[Moshi Bar]] as [[Plo Koon]] says, "You may be surprised what people reveal when they have been drinking." If you compare the design at bottom of this poster to the full poster seen ''inside'' the [[Cantina|bar]] (where only the "[[Coruscant Nights]]" parts can be deciphered), you can see that it is the same poster, meaning that it reads "Coruscant Nights" at the top and "Coruscant Ballroom" at the bottom. The full version of the poster ''over the cantina entrance'' can also be seen in the wide shot of the [[Jedi]] leaving the bar, but only from a distance.</ref>
