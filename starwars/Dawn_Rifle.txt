The '''Dawn Rifle''' was a modified [[blaster rifle]] utilized by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Firestorm Rifle|standard rifle]]. This [[blaster]] [[rifle]] featured a black frame with golden stripes, resembling a common color scheme worn by many members of the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime [[syndicate]], such as the [[Hylobon Enforcers]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dawn Rifle was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Imara Vex's Firestorm Rifle, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 4.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Crimson Dawn Mercenary]].<ref name="Hunters" />
