The '''Balm of the Luminous''' was a type of bandage with some words woven into it.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> 

Around [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date2">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}} ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]'' establishes the events of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'' (2023)]] occur concurrently to the events of ''Temptation of the Force'', therefore meaning they must take place in the same year.</ref> [[Tey Sirrek]] applied the Balm of the Luminous to [[Sskeer]] to help him in dealing with his [[Magrak Syndrome]] and his guilt over [[Goonral Monshi]]'s death.<ref name="HR 2023 7">[[The High Republic (2023) 7|''The High Republic'' (2023) 7]]</ref>

Around [[82 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]'' gives the age of [[Dooku]] at the [[time]] of the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] as eighty [[Standard year|years]] old. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the Battle of [[Geonosis]] to [[22 BBY]], placing Dooku's [[Reproduction|birth]] year in [[102 BBY]]. The [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script]] of ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'' states that Dooku is twenty years old when he utilized the Balm of the Luminous, placing the event around [[82 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]s [[Dooku]] and [[Sifo-Dyas]], along with [[Jedi Master]] [[Lene Kostana]], used the Balm of the Luminous to get rid of the [[dark side of the Force]] they had contracted during a [[Ritual of the Three|ritual]] and to bind again to the [[light side of the Force]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Balm of the Luminous was mentioned in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin-Download">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-cavan-scott/9780593102817|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> As ''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' was released as an audio-only story, the spelling of Balm of the Luminous remained unconfirmed<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> until it was given in the [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script release]] of the audio drama<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> on [[October 1]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin-Download" />
