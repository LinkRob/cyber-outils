'''Daisuke Namikawa'''<ref name="ENG Cast" /> ([[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]]: 浪川大輔) is a voice actor who voiced [[the Grand Master]] in the Japanese version of "[[The Duel: Payback]],"<ref name="JPN Cast">{{Disney|subdomain=starwars|domain=co.jp|url=news/20250926_01|text=日本語オリジナル版＜本予告＞が全世界解禁！『スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ』Volume3|date=2026-09-26}}</ref> a short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the non-[[canon]] ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="ENG Cast" /> Namikawa has been the Japanese voice actor for [[Anakin Skywalker]] since the film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]<ref name="Site" /> in [[2002]].<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> Namikawa appeared as a guest at [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan 2025]].<ref name="Day2">{{SWYouTube|q0trVP56Cy0|Star Wars Celebration LIVE! 2025 - DAY 2}}</ref>
