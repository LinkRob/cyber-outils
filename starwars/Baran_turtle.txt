'''Baran turtles''' were a [[species]] of [[turtle]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[swamp]]s of [[Tenoo]]. They were renowned for their durable shells, which were said to be stronger than [[beskar]] [[steel]].<ref name="Scavenger Hunt" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "Scavenger Hunt" take place during [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|season two]] of ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'' but before the events of "[[Yoda Rescue]]," which corresponds to [[232 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:YJADate|here]].</ref> the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]], and [[Nubs]] encountered a group of baran turtles in a swamp on Tenoo.<ref name="Scavenger Hunt" />
