The lighting of [[lantern]]s was a '''ceremony''' on the [[Mid Rim Territories]] [[planet]] of [[Kijimi]] that evolved from an act of basic survival. It was practiced by the [[Dai Bendu]] monks during the cold nights, and was held to express gratitude. The ceremony was forgotten by the time monks disappeared, though the city dwellers were grateful for the lanterns.<ref name="TROS VD" />
