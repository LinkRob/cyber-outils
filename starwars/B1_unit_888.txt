'''B1 Unit 888''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] that served on the planet [[Akiva]], the site of a [[Akiva production plant|droid factory]] and [[Battle of Akiva|battle]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. 888 was destroyed on Akiva.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[ND-5]] was shot by an [[Imperial Military|Imperial]], causing his memory to malfunction and to call B1 unit 888 to report on their status upon encountering the droid's remains.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
B1 Unit 888 appeared in "The Legacy" quest of the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
