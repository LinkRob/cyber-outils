As part of a series of [[Adelhard's Orchestrated Attacks|orchestrated attacks]] on the [[Galactic Empire]] in the lead-up to the [[Battle of Jakku]], the [[Adelhard coalition]] '''attacked''' the [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]] of [[General]] [[Stansel Malgreen]] while it was en-route to [[Jakku]]. Upon the command cruiser entering range of [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]]'s [[flagship]] ''[[Scepter (Imperial-class)|Scepter]]'', Adelhard had the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] open fire on the command cruiser with [[turbolaser]]s before eventually [[ramming]] the [[Star Destroyer]] into the command [[cruiser]], making the cruiser to explode and [[death|kill]] all of its crew members.<ref name="False Histories" />
