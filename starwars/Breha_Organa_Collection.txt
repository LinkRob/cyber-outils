The '''Breha Organa Collection''' was a collection of documents pertaining to Her Majesty [[Breha Organa]], [[Queen]] of [[Alderaan]]. It was part of the archives housed in the [[Alderaan Memorial]] on [[Coruscant]], and notably included a [[Letter from Bail Organa to Breha Organa|letter]] that [[Viceroy]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] wrote to his queen and [[Marriage|wife]] following the [[Death|passing]] of their friend [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="Backstories" />
