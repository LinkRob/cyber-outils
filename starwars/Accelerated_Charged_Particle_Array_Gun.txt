The '''ACP Array Gun''', or '''Accelerated Charged Particle Array Gun''', was a weapon best suited to close range combat.<ref name="CGSWG" /> The gun was favored by some [[Trandoshan]]s during the [[Clone Wars]] and [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="SG" /> The [[pirate]] [[Kragan Gorr]] used a customized ACP Array Gun<ref name="CGSWG" /> during the [[New Republic Era]] when he and his [[Warbird gang|gang]] worked for the [[First Order]].<ref name="Triple Dark">{{Resistance|The Triple Dark}}</ref> Although he lost this weapon after his [[Mutiny on the Colossus|mutiny]] on the ''[[Colossus]]''.<ref name="The Mutiny">{{Resistance|The Mutiny}}</ref>
