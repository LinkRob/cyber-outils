Located on the [[planet]] [[Murkhana]]<ref name="Rise" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> were the '''headquarters''' of the [[Corporate Alliance]]. Around the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], in which the Corporate Alliance sided with the Separatists, the corporation's headquarters were defended by several [[Fantail-class destroyer|''Fantail''-class destroyers]], which were supported by [[Sikurdian privateers]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Corporate Alliance headquarters were mentioned in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" />
