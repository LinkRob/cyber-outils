'''Bail Bonds''' was a bail bonds office located in the [[Coruscant Underworld]], run by a [[Devaronian bail bondsman]]. There, he offered jobs to [[bounty hunter]]s and paid out upon their completion. [[Asajj Ventress]] came there to collect a [[bounty]] on a [[Kalleran]]. She then proceeded to accept a bounty posting on the fugitive [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]],<ref name="Sisters" /> who was framed for the [[bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar]],<ref name="Catch Jedi">{{TCW|To Catch a Jedi}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The name of the business is written in [[Aurebesh]]. The full name can only be seen in a single panel, where the [[Hologram|holographic]] letters are mirror-inverted because they are seen from behind, with the top of the last two characters being off-screen.
