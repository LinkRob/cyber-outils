The '''"Surefire" Enhancement System''' was a model of [[droid targeting system]] manufactured by [[GoCorp]]. It cost 3,200 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and improved its user's ranged accuracy by using [[sensor]]s and miniature gyromotors.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Surefire" Enhancement System was first mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Fully Operational Is Online">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/3/29/fully-operational-is-online|text=Fully Operational Is Online}}</ref>
