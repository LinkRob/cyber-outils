'''Chall granules''',<ref name="TCVDNE">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> also called '''crystalized chall''',<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> were a [[Color|yellow]] powdered form of [[poison]]. [[Bib Fortuna]], [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]]'s [[majordomo]] and chief of staff, was known to carry chall granules and other poisons with his [[Twi'lek]] [[dagger]].<ref name="TCVDNE" /> It was considered more toxic than [[krayt dragon venom]].<ref name="TVE" />

==Behind the scenes==
In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], chall granules were first pictured in ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book for the [[original trilogy]] written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]] and published by [[Dorling Kindersley]] in [[1998]].<ref name="TVD">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The poison became [[canon]] when it was pictured in ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'', which was written by [[Adam Bray]], [[Tricia Barr]] and [[Cole Horton]]<ref name="TVE" /> and released on [[April 4]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/new-galaxy-guides-get-a-first-look-at-dks-star-wars-visual-encyclopedia-and-the-amazing-book-of-lego-star-wars|text=New Galaxy Guides: Get a First Look at DK's ''Star Wars Visual Encyclopedia'' and ''The Amazing Book of LEGO Star Wars''}}</ref> Chall granules were later given their Legends name when they were shown in ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'', compiled all of the previous ''Visual Dictionary'' reference books.<ref name="TCVDNE" />
