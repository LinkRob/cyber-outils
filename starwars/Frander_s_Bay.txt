'''Frander's Bay''' was a [[space station]] orbiting the [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]. It was often frequented by [[pilot]]s, who could be hired by visitors to the station. The [[Frander's Bay Shuttle]] transported passengers from [[Hanna City]] to Frander's Bay. When searching for an unregistered pilot, [[Han Solo]] and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] visited Frander's Bay, eventually hiring [[Taka Jamoreesa]] and their [[ZV-9 freighter]] [[Vermillion (ZV-9 freighter)|''Vermillion'']].<ref name="Last Shot" />
