The '''AC-series pilot droid''' was a model of [[commuter ship]] [[pilot droid]] that predated the better-known [[RX-series pilot droid|RX-series]]. One [[Standard day|day]], one such [[droid]] designated [[AC-38]] allowed its [[Starspeeder 1000|StarSpeeder]] to be hijacked by a rogue [[protocol droid]] and taken to a dangerous trip across [[the galaxy]]. Because of that incident, all AC-series units were retired from service and replaced by RX-series droids, which proved to be only slightly more reliable.<ref name="Droidography" />

==Behind the scenes==
The AC-series droids originated in ''[[Star Tours: The Adventures Continue]]'', a [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] [[Wikipedia:Simulator ride|simulator ride]] [[Wikipedia:Amusement park|theme park]] attraction.<ref name="STAC">''[[Star Tours: The Adventures Continue]]''</ref> They were then introduced into [[canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]'', written by [[Marc Sumerak]]. The book describes an event involving a unit named AC-38 and "a rogue protocol droid," directly referencing<ref name="Droidography" /> ''The Adventures Continue'', where [[C-3PO]] is forced to pilot the [[StarSpeeder 1000]] in the pilot's absence.<ref name="STAC" />
