'''Bantha Chai''' was a [[tea]] beverage created on [[Batuu]] by the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]]. [[Gender|He]] found it more relaxing and calming to drink than the bitter black [[caf]] that he usually drank on the job. His beverage combined hot tea, local spices, and fresh [[blue milk]] from [[Bubo Wamba]]'s [[Milk Stand]]. The resulting beverage was [[Color|blue]] in hue and rich in texture, with mellow flavors and a pleasant aroma. Tuggs included his recipe for the beverage in his ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'',<ref name="Cookbook" /> which was published in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> He presumed that the reader would not have a [[bantha]] available and suggested [[plant]]-based [[milk]] as a possible substitution.<ref name="Cookbook" />

It gives this really gorgeous blue color without the need for any additional food coloring.|Chelsea Monroe-Cassel|<ref name="Interview">{{WebCite|url=https://thathashtagshow.com/2019/09/10/black-spire-outpost-cookbook-author-interview-with-chelsea-monroe-cassel|text=Black Spire Outpost Cookbook Author Interview with Chelsea Monroe-Cassel! - That Hashtag Show|author=[https://thathashtagshow.com/author/lyle-russell/ Lyle Russell]|date=2019-09-10|work=[https://thathashtagshow.com/ That Hashtag Show]|archivedate=20230928204742}}</ref>}}
Bantha Chai was introduced in the recipe book authored by [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]], ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which was released in [[2019]]. The out-of-universe recipe for Bantha Chai uses [[Wikipedia:Rice milk|rice milk]] or the reader's choice of milk, arrowroot powder, blue butterfly pea tea, ginger root, cardamom, mace, and sugar.<ref name="Cookbook" /> Cassel found that the blue butterfly pea tea used in the recipe was the most unique of the ingredients called for in the cookbook.<ref name="Interview2">{{WebCite|url=https://thathashtagshow.com/2019/09/10/black-spire-outpost-cookbook-author-interview-with-chelsea-monroe-cassel|text=Black Spire Outpost Cookbook Author Interview with Chelsea Monroe-Cassel!|author=Lyle Russel|date=2019-09-10|archivedate=20230928204742}}</ref>
