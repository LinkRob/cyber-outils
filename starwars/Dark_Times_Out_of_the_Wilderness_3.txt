'''''Dark Times—Out of the Wilderness'' 3''' also titled with the legacy numbering '''''Republic'' 103''', is the third issue of the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Dark Times—Out of the Wilderness]]''. It was written by [[Randy Stradley]], illustrated by [[Douglas Wheatley]] and [[Dan Jackson]], and first published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] on [[November 30]], [[2011]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Caged fury! Desert raiders have captured Ember Chankeli, only to discover they have a tiger who can't be tamed! Worse for them: they have a Jedi on their trail!''

''But Jedi Master Dass Jennir had better watch his back, because a mysterious assassin ''and'' Darth Vader both want his head!''

*''Darth Vader is hunting Jedi!''

*''War in the wasteland!''

==Opening crawl==
{{OpeningCrawl
|name=PART THREE (of FIVE)
|text=Darth Vader's prisoner&mdash;a crew member of the outlaw
vessel Uhumele&mdash;was murdered in his cell, and the killer
searched the Imperial databanks for information on
Jedi Dass Jennir. Believing the assassin my lead him
to Jennir, Darth Vader is determined to find the
mystery man.

Following clues of their own as to Jennir's location, the
crew of the Uhumele and their new Jedi companion,
Beyghor Sahdett, arrived on Vondarc, where they
infiltrated an Imperial guard post while searching for
additional information.

Meanwhile, having crash-landed on Prine and spent
weeks crossing the hostile wasteland, Jennir and his
droid H2 have a new challenge: rescue their companion,
Ember Chankeli&mdash;who has been captured by desert
pirates&hellip;}}
