'''Ai''' was a sign in the [[Astrology|astrological]] system of the [[planet]] [[Shili]]. It was marked by the formation of [[blue dwarf]]s nearby, and individuals [[Reproduction|born]] under it were supposed to have wisdom, charisma, and exceptional virility. In these respects, it was similar to the sign [[Genry]] from [[Coruscant]]i astrology. During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Apprentice Legislator]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] studied astrology and learned about the sign.<ref name="LPOA" />

In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]].</ref> an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Unidentified Imperial captain (Amilyn Holdo)|captain]] questioned Holdo and her colleague, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], as they departed a [[star system]] under investigation&mdash;the [[Paucris system]]. Holdo dodged the [[captain]]'s line of questioning on their recent travels by noting that they had instead been leaving the [[Shili system]]&mdash;a system with similar ionization properties to the Paucris system&mdash;and flustering the official by implying he was a Genry.<ref name="LPOA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ai was mentioned in the [[2017]] young-adult novel ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="LPOA" />
