'''''Ewoks'' 10''' is the tenth issue of [[Star Wars: Ewoks (1985)|Marvel's ''Ewoks'']] series of comic books. It was published under the [[Marvel Comics]] [[Star Comics]] imprint on [[July 29]], [[1986]] with a cover date of [[November]] 1986.

This book is the second part of a story that began in [[Droids (1986) 4|''Droids'' (1986) 4]]. It contains the chapters "The Demons of Endor" and "A Race in Time."

{{Plot|comic}}

==Development==
Surrounding the main cover image&mdash;a portrait of [[Wicket Wystri Warrick/Legends|Wicket]]&mdash;the cover of this issue features a frame of several Marvel Comics characters including Spider-Man, the Avengers, the X-Men, X-Factor, the Fantastic Four and more as they existed at that time. This cover frame celebrated the 25th anniversary of the creation of the modern Marvel Universe with ''Fantastic Four'' #1. It appeared on almost every Marvel and Star Comic published with a November 1986 cover date.
