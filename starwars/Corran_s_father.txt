The '''father''' of [[Corran (farmer)|Corran]] was a hydroponic [[Farmer/Legends|farmer]] from [[Séléann]]. He owned one of the largest [[Farm/Legends|farms]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]], employing many inhabitants. He remained a supporter of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]], even when the [[Governor/Legends|governor]] of Séléann dissolved the [[Administrative Council]] and declared the planet's allegiance to the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. His wealth and influence, however, provided him with influence in his province.<ref name="BS">{{BackstabCite|27|Le Projet T-65}} {{C|In French}}</ref>
