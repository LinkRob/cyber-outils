A '''compressor''' was a technical device. After he stole the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' from the [[Irving Boys]], [[Crolute]] [[junk boss]] [[Unkar Plutt]] installed a compressor on the ignition line. However, by the [[time]] the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] recovered the ''Falcon'', the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] bypassed a faulty compressor at the [[cockpit]], ceasing the [[Starship|ship's]] malfunction.<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> Chewbacca later removed the compressor, though Rey wondered if this was the case when observing Chewbacca with a mess of cables after the vessel landed on the [[planet]] [[Ahch-To]]. He told her in [[Shyriiwook]] that there was a pest aboard the ship and she suspected a [[mynock]], but the damage was in fact caused by [[porg]]s, [[Bird|avian]] creatures native to the planet.<ref name="Junior Novel">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>
