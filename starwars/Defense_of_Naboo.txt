"'''Defense of Naboo'''" was an epic poem written by the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] [[poet]] [[Omar Berenko]] [[Standard century|centuries]] before the [[Separatist Crisis]]. Like other well-known works of Berenko's, it was composed in the [[Room of Morning Mists]] at [[Varykino]], a villa located in [[Naboo]]'s [[Lake Country]]. "Defense of Naboo" was regarded as a visionary and disturbing text.<ref name="Complete Locations" />
