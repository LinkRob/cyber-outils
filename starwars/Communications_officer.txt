'''Communications officer''', also known as '''comm officer''',<ref name="Part One">{{Ahsoka|Part One: Master and Apprentice}}</ref> '''comms officer''',<ref name="Part Five">{{Ahsoka|Part Five: Shadow Warrior}}</ref> '''Com officer''', '''Comms Technician'''<ref name="Legion">{{Legion|set=Imperial Specialists Personnel Expansion|cardname=Imperial Comms Technician}}</ref> or '''COMMO''' for short, was a [[Assignment|title]] held by beings who operated and controlled communications, be it aboard a [[starship]]<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> or at a ground base.<ref name="DB Brance" /> During a [[Realspace|space]] battle, the COMMO's main job was to monitor transmissions between ships of the [[fleet]].<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> [[Clone communications officer]]s served in the [[Republic Military]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Queen's Hope" />

The [[human]] [[Masil Veit]] served as a communications officer aboard the [[Super Star Destroyer]] ''[[Executor]]'',<ref name="Tooth and Claw">{{StoryCite|story=Tooth and Claw|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> the personal [[flagship]] of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and [[command ship]] of the [[Death Squadron]] naval force.<ref name="2024 Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Ives]] served as an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] communications [[officer]] on board the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Vigilance (Imperial-class)|''Vigilance'']] during the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="TLOP" /> [[Major]] [[Taslin Brance]] served as a communications officer at the [[Resistance]] [[Resistance base|base]] on [[D'Qar]] during the [[Cold War]].<ref name="DB Brance" /> [[Elia Kane]] served as a communications officer in an [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] led by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]] aboard his [[Gideon's light cruiser|light cruiser]] during his [[Hunt for Grogu|attempts]] to capture [[Din Grogu|Grogu]]<ref name="Chapter 12" /> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|12}}</ref>
