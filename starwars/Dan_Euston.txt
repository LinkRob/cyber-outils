'''Dan Euston''' is a British stuntman and actor who played a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] on [[Jakku]]<ref>{{Instagram|username=daneustonstunts|url=p/B_FAOVhgmpm|name=Dan Euston|date=2020-04-17|quote=fro back to wearing the white suit in a desert with @flostunts #starwars #stunts #action #forceawakens #allstuntsinc #stuntperformer #stormtrooper #stormtrooperlife|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/WgwKr}}</ref> and a [[Guavian Death Gang]] member<ref name="Facebook" /> in {{Film|7}}, [[Farsin Kappehl]] in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', and an [[Elite Praetorian Guard]]<ref name="Facebook">{{Facebook|username=LondonFilmandComicCon|url=LondonFilmandComicCon/photos/877358262448719|name=London Film and Comic Con|date=2018-07-19|quote=We have more interesting Star Wars guests attending! DANIEL EUSTON will be appearing at London Film & Comic Con on Sunday 29th July at Olympia London. Daniel was one of the Elite Praetorian guards in Star Wars: The Last Jedi. Daniel also played a First Order and an Empire Stormtrooper, a Rebel Alliance Resistance Bombardier, and was the stunt double for Poe Dameron in both The Force Awakens and The Last Jedi. Daniel was also part of the Guavian Death Gang, a Rebel Alliance Pilot, and Private Farsin Kappehl in Jyn Erso's rebel marine squad|archivedate=20220518185541}}</ref> and [[Edon Kappehl]]<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|8}} credits</ref> in {{Film|8}}. He also serves as [[Oscar Isaac]]'s stunt double in ''The Force Awakens''<ref name="TFA">{{Film|7}}</ref> and ''The Last Jedi''.<ref name="TLJ" />
