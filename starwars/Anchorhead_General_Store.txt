The '''Anchorhead General Store'''<ref name="Databank" /> was a general store located in [[Anchorhead]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The store was owned by a [[Unidentified store clerk|clerk]]<ref name="Part VI" /> who sold various technologies and other supplies. Settlers would often venture into the area to make regular stops at the store which was viewed as reliable. A [[Youngling|young]] [[Luke Skywalker]] would often visit the store with his uncle, [[Owen Lars]], eager to look at any of the new technology that had arrived since their previous visit.<ref name="Databank" /> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Kenobi Date">{{KenobiDate|VI}}</ref> Skywalker and Lars visited the store to get a new belt for their [[landspeeder]] when they were stopped by [[Dardin Shull]], who spoke to Lars on an urgent matter.<ref name="Part VI" />
