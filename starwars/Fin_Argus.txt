'''Fin Argus''' is an actor who provided the voice and motion capture for [[Volo Bolus]], the main character of [[Industrial Light & Magic]]'s [[2025]] virtual reality [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]].<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> They are best known for their performances in the television shows [[Wikipedia:The Other Two|''The Other Two'']] and [[Wikipedia:Queer as Folk (2022 TV series)|''Queer as Folk'']], as well as the film [[Wikipedia:Clouds (2020 film)|''Clouds'']].<ref name="UCB" />
