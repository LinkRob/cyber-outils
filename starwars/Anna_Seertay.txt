'''Anna Seertay''' was a [[Gender|female]] individual who served as a [[colonel]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|Pathfinders]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. She was [[Death|killed]] during [[Operation Cobalt]] prior to the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|Seertay literally wrote the book on this, and she was the soul of the Pathfinders.|General Crix Madine|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
Seertay was an expert at wilderness reconnaissance. She wrote a manual that was widely used by Alliance soldiers. It included a section that described how to operate in a [[jungle]] environment. [[General]] [[Crix Madine]] attested to Seertay's talents as the "soul" of the Pathfinders.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
