The '''Chadra-Fan''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] of [[meter]]-tall, [[rodent]]-like [[humanoid]]s from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Chad]]. They had large [[ear]]s, and flat [[nose]]s with four nostrils.<ref name="SWU" /> The Chadra-Fans [[Troo-tril-tek]]<ref name="Darth Maul II">[[Darth Maul (2017) 2|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 2]]</ref> and [[Tiver]] were [[bounty hunter]]s during the [[Republic Era]]<ref name="Age of Republic" /> while [[Piikow]]<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> and another [[Unidentified Chadra-Fan rebel|Chadra-Fan]] were rebels that opposed the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> The Chadra-Fans [[Shortpaw]]<ref name="SWU" /> and [[Orka]] were junk sellers.<ref name="Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref> Many other sentient beings looked down on Chadra-Fans and saw them as pets or lesser beings.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" />

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|I wanted adventure, but no one seems to take me seriously.''"<br />"''It is probably because you are diminutive and furry, and you have a high voice. These traits combine to trick the human mind into thinking you are a child or a pet because humans are of lesser intellect.|Kriki and Pook|<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" />}}
[[File:Shortpaw_Render.png|thumb|left|180px|Shortpaw, a Chadra-Fan crime lord in the Anoat sector.]]
Chadra-Fans were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[rodent]]- or bat-like [[humanoid]] [[species]] standing roughly a [[meter]] tall, and originating from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Chad]]. They were distinguished by their large [[ear]]s, flat [[nose]]s, and four nostrils.<ref name="SWU" /> Chadra-Fans could have black,<ref name="HS2" /> brown,<ref name="ANH" /> gray,<ref name="SWU" /> or white [[color]]ed hair<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> and had gray,<ref name="Dark Lord 18" /> tan,<ref name="Card Trader" /> or lightly colored skin. Possessing their oversized ears since childhood,<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> Chadra-Fans also had sensitive hearing.<ref name="Dark Lord 18" /> Chadra-Fans had two [[heart]]s and only needed to [[sleep]] about three [[Standard hour|hours]] a [[Standard day|day]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> Chadra-Fan were capable of producing many offspring that were called ''[[fanling]]s''.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />

==Chadra-Fans in the galaxy==
{{Quote|My two Chadra-Fan trackers, Hardhear and his grandson Sissian, could detect Vader at a distance.|Wilhuff Tarkin|<ref name="Dark Lord 18" />}}
Prior to the [[Crisis on Naboo]], the Chadra-Fan [[bounty hunter]] [[Troo-tril-tek]] was hired by [[Maul|Darth Maul]] to assist him in infiltrating the [[Auction of Eldra Kaitis|auction]] of the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Eldra Kaitis]].<ref name="Darth Maul II" /> Sometime before the [[plot to assassinate Senator Amidala]], another Chadra-Fan [[bounty]] hunter named [[Tiver]] was hired by the famed bounty hunter [[Jango Fett]] and [[Death|killed]] shortly after in an attempt to betray him.<ref name="Age of Republic" />

[[File:OfficeOfAcquisitions-TheRecruitPart2.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Orka was the Chadra-Fan co-owner of the Office of Acquisitions]]
Sometime after the [[Invasion of Mon Cala|occupation]] of [[Mon Cala]], the Chadra-Fan trackers [[Hardhear]] and his grandson [[Sissian]] were hired, for their sensitive hearing, to assist [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] in [[Hunt on Chandar's Folly|hunting]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on [[Chandar's Folly]].<ref name="Dark Lord 18" /> The Chadra-Fan [[Kabe]] was a patron of [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] on [[Tatooine]]<ref name="Card Trader" /> and was present when [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] went there looking for transport to [[Alderaan]].<ref name="ANH" /> [[Piikow]] was a Chadra-Fan member of the [[Dreamers]] rebel cell in [[0 ABY]].<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> Another [[Unidentified Chadra-Fan rebel|Chadra-Fan rebel]] was a part of the [[Sullustan resistance]].<ref name="Battlefront" /> Chadra-Fans including [[Meymik]], [[Shortpaw]], [[Tel'kap]], [[Tiquip]],<ref name="SWU" /> and [[Fbinti Fith]]<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Fbinti Fith"</ref> were in the [[Anoat sector]] during the [[Iron Blockade]].<ref name="SWU" /> The Chadra-Fan [[Orka]] was the co-owner of the [[Office of Acquisitions]] on the [[Fuel|refueling]] station ''[[Colossus]]''.<ref name="Recruit" /> The Chadra-Fan [[Kriki]] lived at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]] and was recruited into the [[Resistance]] by [[Vi Moradi]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> [[Kisma Uttersond]] was a Chandra-Fan who joined the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] organization, and cared for [[Mari San Tekka]], a [[hyperspace]] [[prospector]] who was kept aboard the [[flagship]] ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' with [[Marchion Ro]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>
