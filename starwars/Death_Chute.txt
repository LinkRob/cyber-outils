The '''Death Chute''' was a bottleneck located at the end of a [[Unidentified crestracing course|crestracing course]] on<ref name="Star Wars 12" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Han Chewie 1">[[Han Solo & Chewbacca 1|''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' 1]]</ref> [[Galator III]]. It was only big enough to let one [[racer]] through at a [[time]] and resulted in a victory for the individual who made it to the tunnel first. During a race she took part in<ref name="Star Wars 12" /> around [[4 BBY]] and [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Kes Dameron]]'s flashbacks in [[Star Wars (2020) 12|''Star Wars'' (2020) 12]] to around [[4 BBY]].</ref> [[Shara Bey]] was neck and neck with another racer as they approached the Death Chute. At the last [[second]] the other racer lost their nerve and backed down, which allowed Bey to make it through the Death Chute first and win the race.<ref name="Star Wars 12" />
