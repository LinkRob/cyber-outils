'''Alchemy''' was a [[Force power|Force technique]] practiced by [[alchemist]]s. It concentrated the Force into a physical form. Unlike the [[Jedi]], the [[Nightsisters]] used alchemy, along with [[magick]], to consolidate and collect power. The Nightsisters use an ancient creature known as the [[Sleeper]] to create the [[Water of Life]], a form of [[Force healing|healing]] alchemy.<ref name="Collapse" /> Among the [[Sith]], there were [[Sith alchemist]]s which utilized [[Sith alchemy]] to create of amazing feats of metallurgy and biology.<ref name="TROSVD" />
