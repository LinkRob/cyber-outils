"'''Ambushed!'''" is a [[canon]] short story that appears in ''[[The Prequel Trilogy Stories]]''. It was written by [[Andy Schmidt]].<ref name="TPTS" /> It adapts the events of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] from [[Padmé Amidala]]'s arrival to [[Coruscant]] to her departure for [[Naboo]] with [[Anakin Skywalker]].
