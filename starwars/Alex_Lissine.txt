'''Alex Lissine''' is an [[Wikipedia:Australian|Australian]] actor who played [[Imperial officer]] [[Lieutenant Colonel]] [[Ejai]] in the [[2005]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']],<ref name="StarNow" /> although he was uncredited in the role.<ref name="Ep3 Credits">Alex Lissine does not feature in the credits of {{Film|III}}.</ref> Lissine's scene was filmed on [[September 17]], [[2003]] at [[Fox Studios, Sydney]] in [[Wikipedia:Australia|Australia]].<ref name="Making">''[[The Making of Star Wars Revenge of the Sith]]''</ref>
