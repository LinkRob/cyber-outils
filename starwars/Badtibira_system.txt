The '''Badtibira system''' was a [[star system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained the location [[Badtibira]].<ref name="MA" /> It was located in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]],<ref name="Interior" /> situated within grid square K-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> In [[67 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]] spent [[time]] negotiating with individuals from Badtibira in conferences. During the meetings, Dooku's former [[Padawan]], the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Rael Averross]], visited his Master's current student [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] in the former's [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] [[Dooku's quarters|quarters]] and was suprised to see the latter was helping his Master with the Badtibira dealings.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Badtibira system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].
