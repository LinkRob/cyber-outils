'''Brantis Mo Fresk''' was a [[pilot]] who lived during the [[New Republic Era]] and owned a [[Starship|ship]] known as the ''[[Vorantis]]''.<ref name="Last Shot" /> In [[7 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the present day events of ''[[Last Shot]]'' to [[7 ABY]]. As ''Last Shot'' establishes that the [[Karee Blockade Clutch]] took place in the same [[Standard year|year]] as the novel's present day events, it must have also taken place in 7 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> he participated in the [[Karee Blockade Clutch]], where he beat the starship ''[[Brightfox]]''. Later that [[Standard year|year]], the pilot was present on [[Frander's Bay]]&mdash;a [[space station]] that [[orbit]]ed the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]&mdash;where he offered his services to individuals looking for an unaffiliated pilot. While on Frander's Bay, Mo Fresk was represented by a [[Brantis Mo Fresk's barker|barker]] who extolled his exploits and skills.<ref name="Last Shot" />

When the [[New Republic]] operative [[Han Solo]] and his friend [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] visited Frander's Bay in search of a pilot, Solo passed Mo Fresk's barker, who hollered the pilot's name into one of his [[ear]]s. The barker then stated that the pilot's skill was unmatched and then recalled his victory in the Karee Blockade Clutch. Ultimately, Solo and Calrissian hired the pilot [[Taka Jamoreesa]].<ref name="Last Shot" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brantis Mo Fresk was mentioned in the [[2018]] novel ''[[Last Shot]]'', written by [[Daniel José Older]].<ref name="Last Shot" />
