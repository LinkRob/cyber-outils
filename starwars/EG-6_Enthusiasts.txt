The '''EG-6 Enthusiasts''' was an outfit owned by the team of [[Jawa]] brothers known collectively as the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Utooni]] that they could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to their [[Suspicious Salvagers|standard costume]]. This garb featured weathered [[Color|dark blue]] [[robe]]s with yellow markings and a golden spiral trinket secured to the front of the upper Jawa over a pair of crisscrossed [[bandolier]]s, while a seemingly power orange and blue [[EG-6 power droid]] hung from their back.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
EG-6 Enthusiasts was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Utooni, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''.
