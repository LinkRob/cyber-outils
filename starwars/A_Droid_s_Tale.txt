'''''A Droid's Tale''''' was a children's book released by [[Golden Books]] in November [[1997]], written by [[John Whitman]]. The book was part of Golden's "SmartPages" series, that played audio clips when a button was pushed and featured the voice of [[Anthony Daniels]]. The cover price was $22.95.

==Publisher's summary==
''Young'' Star Wars ''fans will delight in this interactive tale of two of the most famous droids in the galaxy! Featuring the authentic voice of C-3PO and still photos from the movies, kids can read, press buttons, and listen as our favorite droid describes each and every page!''
