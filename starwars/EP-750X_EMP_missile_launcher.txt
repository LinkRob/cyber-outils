The '''EP-750X EMP missile launcher''' was a type of [[missile launcher]] produced by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]], where they were used by the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. As an alternative to the standard dorsal-mounted twin [[mass-driver missile launcher]]s, the [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] [[gunship]] could be equipped with an EP-750X. They fired guided [[missile]]s that were tipped with EMP warheads that delivered a powerful ion blast upon detonation, and were, as a result, effective against massed [[droid]] formations, as well as effective anti-[[Starship|ship]] and anti-vehicle weapons.<ref name="Rise" />
