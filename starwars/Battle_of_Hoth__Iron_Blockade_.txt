Just over a [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Battle of Hoth]], in which the [[Galactic Empire]] routed the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] from their [[Echo Base|headquarters]], another '''battle''' was fought on the icy [[planet]] of [[Hoth]], this time between the Empire and the [[Anoat sector]]'s faction of resistance known as the [[Uprising]]. The Imperials aimed to prevent the Uprising from getting ahold of the valuable salvage still lying on Hoth.<ref name="SWU SB Hoth" />

==Behind the scenes==
This battle is a "Sector Battle,"<ref name="SWU SB Hoth" /> a game-wide event that earns the players powerful rewards and dictates the expansion of in-game content. The [[canon]]ical outcome of a Sector Battle is not known, since it is determined by the players' involvement.<ref name="SWUweb">{{WebCite|url=https://playstarwarsuprising.com/game-guide/gameplay/#sector-battles|text=Sector Battles|work=[https://playstarwarsuprising.com Star Wars: Uprising Official Website]|archivedate=20160809151309}}</ref> However, the approximate date and the motives of the battle were addressed in the in-game description.<ref name="SWU SB Hoth" />
