The '''frilled newt''' was a type of [[salamander]] found on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]]. they were very skittish and very evasive. They were mostly found in rock pools and [[water]]&mdash;including Koboh's [[Dredger Gorge]], where they rested and hydrated.<ref name="Survivor" />
