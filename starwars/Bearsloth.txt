'''Bearsloths''' were fierce arboreal [[Diet|predators]] found in the [[forest]]s of the celestial body [[Arbooine]]. They had long [[Hair|furry]] bodies with six [[Limb|legs]] and four [[eye]]s. The [[species]] possessed a slow-acting [[Bearsloth venom|venom]] and would bite prey and then hide while the venom weakened the target to a point where it could not defend itself.

==Biology and appearance==
The bearsloth had a long body with shaggy camouflage-patterned [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]] covering their backs and pale fur on their undersides and face. The face had four horns, two larger horns level with their four blue [[eye]]s and two smaller protruding down beside the [[mouth]]ful of sharp [[Tooth|teeth]]. The [[species]] had six [[limb]]s each ending in sharp black talons.<ref name="A&A" />

[[Bearsloth venom]] did not instantly [[Death|kill]] prey, but slowly sapped their health as they exerted themselves, leading victims to often hasten their own demise by attempting to flee. This made it especially dangerous to those bitten far from civilization. The venom retained its potency long after a bearsloth's death and being cut with a weapon coated in bearsloth venom was just as deadly as being bitten by the creature itself.<ref name="A&A" />

==Behavior==
Bearsloths were arboreal ambush and pack hunters, living amongst the [[forest]]s of the<ref name="A&A" /> [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Arbooine system]]</ref> celestial body [[Arbooine]] and using their fearsome claws to traverse the canopy effortlessly. The species were one of the world's most dangerous [[Diet|predators]] due to their strength, venom, natural ferocity, and fearlessness, posing a constant hazard to anyone visiting Arbooine. Of these attributes, the species was known primarily for its [[Poison|venomous]] bite, but also its ferocity.<ref name="A&A" />

Naturally stealthy and aided by their camouflaged fur, the creatures struck unwary prey from the [[tree]]s before hiding and letting their venom finish off the target. Once the prey was unable to defend itself, the bearsloth would ambush it again and move in for the kill.<ref name="A&A" />

==History==
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[glitterstim]] [[Spice dealer|dealer]] [[Savas|Savas the Dreaded]] sold bearsloth venom that he had acquired on Arbooine to the [[Hutt]] [[Mahon]].<ref name="ND">[[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']]</ref> A [[Unidentified crime lord (bearsloths)|crime lord]] captured several bearsloths and placed them in the forests surrounding their [[Unidentified manor|manor]], where the creatures acted as guards for the criminal and executioners for those that were exiled from the manor into the forest. [[Sathari]] hunters local to Arbooine would use bearsloth venom to poison their [[diiro]]s. The venom became a hot commodity for a local [[Unidentified arms dealer|arms dealer]].<ref name="A&A" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], bearsloths were first mentioned in the [[2017]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] sourcebook [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']], which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' system.<ref name="ND" /> Bearsloths were then first depicted in the [[2019]] Fantasy Flight Games roleplaying sourcebook ''[[Allies and Adversaries]]''.<ref name="A&A" /> The [[Bearsloth/Legends|species]] originated in the [[2015]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] roleplaying supplement ''[[Chronicles of the Gatekeeper]]'', which was also released by Fantasy Flight Games but as apart of the ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' system.<ref name="CotG">''[[Chronicles of the Gatekeeper]]''</ref>
