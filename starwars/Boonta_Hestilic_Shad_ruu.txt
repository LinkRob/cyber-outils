'''Boonta Hestilic Shad'ruu''' was a [[Hutt]] who was said to have ascended to [[Deity|godhood]]. To celebrate the deity's ascension, the celebration of [[Boonta Eve]] was created.<ref name="EYNTK" /> The infamous [[Boonta Blaster]]s were also named after the Hutt, who allegedly commissioned the first one.<ref name="G&G">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref>
