The [[planet]] [[Bromlarch]] was host to a '''planetary aqueduct system''', which provided [[Foodstuff|food]] and [[water]] to its host world. [[Galactic Republic]] [[Senator]]s [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of [[Naboo]] and [[Rush Clovis]] of [[Scipio]] had three nights to prepare a [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] hearing in order to save Bromlarch's aqueduct system. Their hearing saved Bromlarch's aqueduct system, and thus saved Bromlarch's inhabitants from experiencing drought and famine.<ref name="TRoC" />
