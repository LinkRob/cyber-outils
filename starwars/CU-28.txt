'''CU-28''',<ref name="Droid Factory"/> nicknamed '''Copper''',<ref name="MandoVG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> was a was a [[protocol droid]] that served as the assistant to [[High Magistrate]] [[Greef Karga]] on the [[planet]] [[Nevarro]] during the early [[New Republic Era]],<ref name="Ch17" /> helping Karga with daily tasks and administrative work<ref name="Droid Factory" /> and informing him of events in the city.<ref name="Ch17" /> CU-28 was considered fastidious.<ref name="MandoVG" /> He had green [[photoreceptor]]s and his plating<ref name="Ch17" /> had a brilliant copper finish, leading Karga and his staff to usually only call him Copper.<ref name="MandoVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
CU-28 first appeared, albeit unidentified, in the [[The Mandalorian Season Three|third season]] [[Chapter 17: The Apostate|premiere episode]] of the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'',<ref name="Ch17" /> released on [[March 1]], [[2023]].<ref name="Tweet">{{Twitter|starwars|status/1598416900689743873|[[Star Wars]]|date=2022-12-1|quote=The Mandalorian and Grogu return March 1 only on @DisneyPlus.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/ytQHE}}</ref> The droid was designed and sculpted by [[Timothy Reed Martin]]. Martin had originally designed the model for<ref name="IG">{{Instagram|username=monsterman03gmail|name=[[Timothy Reed Martin]]|url=p/DS-_Z3nkk7b|date=2026-01-01|quote=Cool news out of Lucasfilm! The droid I designed and sculpted for Mandalorian formerly and unceremoniously referred to as simply "Copper" has now been renamed "CW-24" after the late great Carl Weathers RIP. Originally this droid was designed by me and debuted in my fan-film short "Birth of a Monster"|archivefile=File:TimothyReedMartin-CU28-Instagram.png}}</ref> the rusty droid 0-TK in the [[2019]] [[Fanon|fan-film]] "Birth of a Monster: A Star Wars Story," where it was portrayed by<ref name="Birth of a Monster">{{YouTube|channel=escapevelocitycontent3342|name=Escape Velocity Content|video=u0OfGeDkSlM|text=Birth of a Monster &#124; A Star Wars Story|date=2019-02-27|archivedate=20251125072029}}</ref> ''The Mandalorian'' droid performer<ref name="Chapter 22">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 22: Guns for Hire}}</ref> [[Daniel Bohman]].<ref name="Birth of a Monster" /> In ''The Mandalorian'', the character was voiced by [[Parvesh Cheena]] and performed by [[Chris Bartlett]] and was simply credited as "Nevarro Copper Droid".<ref name="Ch17" />

The droid was later identified as CW-24 through a preview for upcoming ''[[Star Wars: Droid Factory (toy line)|Star Wars: Droid Factory]]'' figures to be released on [[February 6]], [[2026]].<ref name="WDWNT Announcement">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2025/12/disney-store-parks-announce-droid-factory-friday-featuring-new-monthly-releases|text=Disney Store & Parks Announce Droid Factory Friday Featuring New Monthly Releases|author=Ace, Shannen|date=2025-12-29|archivedate=20260101203823}}</ref> Martin noted that CW-24 was named in tribute to<ref name="IG" /> Greef Karga's actor [[Carl Weathers]] who died in [[2024]].<ref name="death">{{WebCite|url=https://deadline.com/2024/02/carl-weathers-dead-1235812684|text=Carl Weathers Dies: 'Rocky' & 'Predator' Star Who Appeared In 'Happy Gilmor', 'The Mandalorian' & More Was 76|author=Erik Pedersen|date=2024-02-02|work=[[Wikipedia:Deadline Hollywood|''Deadline'']]|archivedate=20240202193817}}</ref> However, the name was then changed to CU-28 prior to the product's release.<ref name="JediNews">{{WebCite|url=https://www.jedinews.com/events/articles/disney-store-revises-names-of-upcoming-star-wars-droid-factory-releases/|text=Disney Store Revises Names of Upcoming Monthly Star Wars Droid Factory Releases|author=Burns, James|date=2026-01-09|work=[https://www.jedinews.com/ JediNews]|archivedate=20260111115008}}</ref>
