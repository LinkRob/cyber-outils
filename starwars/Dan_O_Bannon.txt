'''Daniel Thomas "Dan" O'Bannon''' ([[September 30]], [[1940s|1946]] – [[December 17]], [[2009]]) was a a motion-picture screenwriter and director. [[George Lucas]] met O'Bannon while both were in the USC School of Cinema-Television, and later brought him to work on computer animation and graphic displays for [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>
