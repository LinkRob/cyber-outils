'''Door girls''' were [[Gender|female]]<ref name="SC 102 AD">{{SkeletonCrew|Way, Way Out Past the Barrier}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> individuals who occupied themselves with enticing passers-by to enter an establishment. During the [[New Republic Era]], this career path was followed by the [[Theelin]] [[Melna]] and an [[alien]] [[Unidentified door girl|colleague]] of<ref name="SC 102" /> hers,<ref name="SC 102 AD" /> who worked as door girls for a [[Unidentified dance club|dance club]] located in<ref name="SC 102" /> [[Borgo Prime|Starport Borgo]].<ref name="Starport Borgo">{{Databank|starport-borgo|Starport Borgo}}</ref> In addition to being a door girl, Melna also worked as a dancer.<ref name="Melna">{{Databank|melna|Melna}}</ref>

Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SCDate" /> as Melna and her companion enticed passers-by to enter the dance club, they were noticed by [[Fern]], [[KB]], [[Neel]], and [[Wim]], a group of [[youngling]]s who sought a way back to their [[Homeworld|home planet]] of [[At Attin]]. Fern rebuffed Wim's suggestion to enter the establishment, and so the children walked away. Melna, however, had noticed the youngsters and later attempted to help them, despite finding it difficult to believe the strangers' claim of hailing from At Attin.<ref name="SC 102" />

==Behind the scenes==
Door [[Gender|girls]] first appeared in the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'', making their [[canon]]ical debut in the second episode, titled "[[Way, Way Out Past the Barrier]],"<ref name="SC 102" /> which premiered on [[December 2]], [[2024]].<ref name="Premiere">{{Twitter|StarWars|status/1861409832349172011|[[Star Wars]]|quote=Mark your calendars. 🗓️ On December 2, stream the two-episode series premiere of #SkeletonCrew, only on @DisneyPlus.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/QwMYe}}</ref> The name of the profession was provided in the [[Disney+]] audio description of the episode, narrated by [[Jedediah Barton]].<ref name="SC 102 AD" />
