'''Dhania''' was an astronomical object in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], situated in grid square K-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]] along the [[Corellian Trade Spine]]. It was the [[homeworld]] of [[Gorian Shard]], the self-proclaimed [[Pirate King]] who terrorized the southern Outer Rim during the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="MandoVG" />
