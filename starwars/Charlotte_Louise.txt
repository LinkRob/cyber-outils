'''Charlotte Louise Baker''' (born on [[October 27]], [[1991]] in [[Wikipedia:Surrey|Surrey]], [[Wikipedia:England|England]]), known professionally as '''Charlotte Louise''', is a [[Wikipedia:British people|British]] [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]] and [[Wikipedia:Puppeteer|puppeteer]]. She has worked on several ''[[Star Wars]]'' projects, including minor roles in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Anthology Series'']] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' and the [[sequel trilogy]] films [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], released in [[2017]] and [[2019]], respectively. Louise portrayed the concierge [[Margo]] in the [[2018]] ''Anthology Series'' film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', along with portraying a few background characters.

In [[2022]], she portrayed a dancing [[hologram]] in "[[Kassa (episode)|Kassa]]," an episode of the [[Andor Season 1|first season]] of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', and also did puppeteer work for several other episodes. Louise also puppeteered for the episodes "[[Lost / Found]]" and "[[Day (episode)|Day]]" of the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', as well as episodes in ''Andor''{{'s}} [[Andor Season 2|second season]].

==Biography==
===Film work===

Margo does not have long, flowing locks of hair, nor does she have smooth, blemish free skin, but she is beautiful and powerful regardless.|Charlotte Louise|<ref name="The Cloud Riders">{{WebCite|url=https://thecloudriders.net/2018/06/29/charlotte-louise-solo-a-star-wars-story-interview-margo|text=Charlotte Louise SOLO: A Star Wars Story Interview &ndash; Margo|date=2018-06-29|work=[https://web.archive.org/web/20181128140248/https://thecloudriders.net The Cloud Riders]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/iZEdR|nolive=1}}</ref>}}
[[File:CharlotteLouiseTLJ.jpg|thumb|left|255px|Charlotte Louise in ''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']]
Charlotte Louise Baker was born on [[October 27]], [[1991]],<ref name="Birth" /> in [[Wikipedia:Surrey|Surrey]], [[Wikipedia:England|England]]. The [[Wikipedia:British people|British]] [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]]<ref name="TIP" /> did [[Wikipedia:Puppeteer|puppeteer]] work for the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Anthology Series'']] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Spotlight">{{WebCite|url=https://www.spotlight.com/interactive/cv/5816-4533-0157|text=Charlotte Louise|work=[[Wikipedia:Spotlight (company)|Spotlight]]|archivedate=20180623235706|nolive=1}}</ref> though was uncredited.<ref name="RO">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> The [[2017|next year]], Louise was credited as a creature and [[droid]] puppeteer in the [[sequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> She auditioned for the film without knowing what it was due to the high levels of security involved. Creature effects supervisor [[Neal Scanlan]] was at the audition, and Louise became excited, then realizing it was a ''[[Star Wars]]'' film. After a costume fitting and plaster cast for her costume, Louise was cast as an [[Color|orange]]-skinned [[Namara|alien]], which appeared in [[Canto Bight]]<ref name="TIP" /> on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> Louise was thrilled to have gotten the part, having been a longtime ''Star Wars'' fan.<ref name="The Cloud Riders" /> In [[2018]], Louise portrayed the concierge [[Margo]] in the ''Anthology Series'' film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> She was cast for the role after the completion of ''The Last Jedi''. While the actor was originally hesitant about portraying Margo because of the plaster costume, she found it easy to adapt once the process started. Louise enjoyed working with the CFX team on the film's set, finding them like a second family.<ref name="The Cloud Riders" />

[[File:Charlotte_Louise_Margo.jpg|thumb|right|260px|Louise as Margo in ''Solo: a Star Wars Story'']]
Louise received dialect training under the coach [[Andrew Jack]] for the role because Margo had an [[alien]] dialect. Louise also participated in movement rehearsals for the film to master Margo's graceful movements. The prosthetic costume for the role took around five and a half hours to put on.<ref name="The Cloud Riders" /> Louise also portrayed four other minor characters in the film, including two<ref name="TIP" /> [[Twi'lek]]s.<ref name=Twi'lek">{{Twitter|RealCharLouise|status/1002263213776437249|Charlotte Louise|date=2018-05-31|quote=My Twi'lek character from Solo: A Star Wars Story on the Star Wars Website ☺️👽|archiveurl=https://archive.is/8q7cA|archivefile=File:CharlotteLouise-Twilek-Twitter.png}}</ref> For the Twi'lek she portrayed on a [[Numidian Prime|jungle world]], she participated in cast [[sabacc]] lessons.<ref name="TIP" /> In [[2019]], she was cast as<ref name="TROS">{{Instagram|username=charlotte_louise_baker|url=p/B6SzDkYFLyS|name=Charlotte Louise Baker|date=2019-12-20|quote=So proud to share one of my creatures (LAZU LIRONA) from 🅁🄸🅂🄴 🄾🄵 🅂🄺🅈🅆🄰🄻🄺🄴🅁|archiveurl=https://archive.is/1lzeW|archivefile=File:CharlotteLouise-LazuLirona-Instagram.png}}</ref> the [[smuggler]]<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary ]]''</ref> [[Lazu Lirona]]<ref name="TROS" /> and the dance troupe member<ref name="TROSVD" /> [[Charlu Sisi]] in the sequel trilogy film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. The name Charlu Sisi was [[Tuckerization|inspired by]] Louise's own name.<ref name="Charlu Sisi tweet">{{Twitter|RealCharLouise|status/1210146150570967040|Charlotte Louise|date=2019-12-26|quote=My CHARLU SISI creature in Rise of Skywalker Visual Dictionary|archivedate=20191226205658}}</ref>

[[File:CockroachCreature.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Charlotte Louise portrayed a creature she referred to as a "Cockroach" creature in "Announcement" of ''Andor''.]]
Louise portrayed a dancing [[hologram]] [[Gender|woman]] in "[[Kassa (episode)|Kassa]]"<ref name="AndorPost">{{Instagram|username=charlotte_louise_baker|url=p/CpOFkxajh1Q|name=Charlotte Louise Baker|date=2023-02-28|quote=My dancing hologram (Horned Woman) from Andor S1 E1|archiveurl=https://archive.is/FG0bz|archivefile=File:CharlotteLouise-AndorHologram-Instagram.png}}</ref> but was credited as a puppeteer.<ref name="Kassa">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> The installment was the first episode of the [[Andor Season 1|first season]] of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', which aired on [[September 21]], [[2022]].<ref name="Andor Companion">{{InsiderCite|217|Star Wars: Andor Season One Companion|''Star Wars: Andor'' Season One Companion}}</ref>

She was credited as a puppeteer in several other episodes of ''Andor'', including "[[Reckoning (episode)|Reckoning]],"<ref name="Reckoning">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref> "[[Aldhani (episode)|Aldhani]],"<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> "[[The Eye]],"<ref name="The Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref> "[[Announcement]],"<ref name="Announcement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref> "[[Narkina 5 (episode)|Narkina 5]],"<ref name="Narkina 5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref> "[[Nobody's Listening!]],"<ref name="Nobody's Listening">{{Andor|Nobody's Listening!}}</ref> "[[One Way Out]],"<ref name="One Way Out">{{Andor|One Way Out}}</ref> and "[[Rix Road (episode)|Rix Road]]."<ref name="Rix Road">{{Andor|Rix Road (episode)}}</ref> The episodes released on September 21, [[September 28]], [[October 12]], [[October 19]], [[October 26]], [[November 2]], [[November 9]], and [[November 23]] of 2022, respectively.<ref name="Andor Companion" /> For "Announcement," the puppeteer portrayed an alien she referred to as a "Cockroach" creature, which was sculpted and designed by [[Louis Wiltshire]]. She felt that it was fun to portray a character with a more robust and manly physique, reflecting that embodying the alien was vastly different than her natural body type and movement style. While in costume, she had limited visibility. Louise enjoyed that her character's demeanor was grumpy and confrontational.<ref name="Cockroach" />

===''Andor'' Season 2===
{{Quote|My 'Squid' creature from Andor Season 2. Featured in the 'Day on Andor Season 2' promo. This is what I call a 'blind/deaf head' with zero visibility and almost completely sound proof. Once the animatronics are on inside the head, you are unable to hear anything but the trusty Puppeteer in your headphone. I was lucky enough to have my CFX sister @claireroiharvey in my ear. What few people know about this job, is that you put your safety and performance in the hands of that puppeteer; they watch out for hazards such as camera cranes, stairs, people and sometimes fire/explosives and are literally your eyes and ears!|Charlotte Louise, about her work on ''Andor'' Season 2|<ref name="WeddingPuppet">{{Instagram|username=charlotte_louise_baker|url=p/DKebYBhM717|name=Charlotte Louise Baker|date=2025-06-04|quote=My "Squid" creature from Andor Season 2. Featured in the "Day on Andor Season 2" promo. This is what I call a “blind/deaf head” with zero visibility and almost completely sound proof. What few people know about this job, is that you put your safety and performance in the hands of that puppeteer; they watch out for hazards such as camera cranes, stairs, people and sometimes fire/explosives and are literally your eyes and ears! I also had the amazing Claire Bannister looking after me, making sure everything is as it should be (batteries are attached, head is on/off when it should be, I have water/air, I can get out of the head quickly if needed). [[File:WeddingAlien-Instagram.jpg|thumb|left|270px|One of Charlotte Louise's aliens in ''Andor'']]
Louise also contributed to ''Andor''{{'s}} [[Andor Season 2|second season]] and was credited as a puppeteer in "[[One Year Later]]," "[[Sagrona Teema]]," and "[[Harvest]]," the first three episodes of the season.<ref name="OneYearLater">{{Andor|One Year Later}}</ref><ref name="Sagrona Teema">{{Andor|Sagrona Teema}}</ref><ref name="Harvest">{{Andor|Harvest}}</ref> During at least one of the scenes from those episodes, Louise portrayed an alien who attended the [[Wedding of Stekan Sculdun and Leida Mothma|wedding]]<ref name="AndorStory" /> of the young [[Chandrilan]] couple [[Stekan Sculdun]] and [[Leida Mothma]].<ref name="Sagrona Teema /> Her alien's animatronics were created by [[Nicolas Ferrard]], and she worked with fellow puppeteer [[Claire Roi Harvey]] for the shoot,<ref name="AndorStory">{{Instagram|username=charlotte_louise_baker|url=stories/charlotte_louise_baker/3631795965573255200|name=Charlotte Louise Baker|date=2025-05-13|quote=That time @claireroiharvey and I got to "creature" together&hellip; it was so beautiful. 11 years in the making|archivefile=File:CharlotteLouise-Andor-Instagram.png}}</ref> who helped Louise via headphone.<ref name="WeddingPuppet" />

Louise described the alien as a "Squid" creature, noting that the animatronic head was a "blind/deaf head," which meant it offered no visibility and was soundproof. During the shoot, [[Claire Bannister]] assisted the puppeteer with ensuring the animatronic head had batteries, as well as ensuring Louise's well-being while stuck in the costume. Louise praised the all-female team involved with bringing her alien in the wedding scene to life, expressing her love for their efforts and noting the trust involved with wearing a costume with limitations.<ref name="WeddingPuppet" /> The three episiodes aired on [[April 22]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref>

Released [[April 29|one week later]],<ref name="Trailer News" /> she also was credited for the same work in "[[I Have Friends Everywhere]]," the fifth episode of the season.<ref name="IHaveFriends">{{Andor|I Have Friends Everywhere}}</ref> Louise also contributed in the same capacity for the seventh, eighth, and ninth episodes "[[Messenger]]," "[[Who Are You?]]," and "[[Welcome to the Rebellion]],"<ref name="Messenger">{{Andor|Messenger}}</ref><ref name="WhoAreYou">{{Andor|Who Are You?}}</ref><ref name="WelcomeToTheRebellion">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref> which debuted on [[May 6]] of the same year,<ref name="Trailer News" /> as well the tenth, eleventh, and twelfth episodes, "[[Make It Stop]],"<ref name="MakeItStop">{{Andor|Make It Stop}}</ref> "[[Who Else Knows?]],"<ref name="WhoElseKnows">{{Andor|Who Else Knows?}}</ref> "[[Jedha, Kyber, Erso]],"<ref name="JedhaKyberErso">{{Andor|Jedha, Kyber, Erso}}</ref> released the [[May 13|week following]].<ref name="Trailer News" />
