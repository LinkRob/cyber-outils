'''Frankie Ryan Manriquez''' (born [[March 14]], [[1994]]) voiced a young [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] in ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' [[Clone Wars Chapter 21|Chapter 21]].

==Biography==
Manriquez started working when he was three in print ads. In [[2001]], he made his acting debut in one episode of the TV series [[Wikipedia:The King of Queens|''The King of Queens'']]. He appeared in several other TV shows, such as [[Wikipedia:Life with Bonnie|''Life with Bonnie'']], [[Wikipedia:Blue Collar TV|''Blue Collar TV'']], [[Wikipedia:Life on a Stick|'' Life on a Stick'']] and [[Wikipedia:That's So Raven|''That's So Raven'']].

He also voiced several other characters, including Skippy in [[Wikipedia: Megas XLR|'' Megas XLR'']], Young Ruffshodd in [[Wikipedia:Racing Stripes|''Racing Stripes'']], Jimmy and Ernie in [[Wikipedia:What's New, Scooby-Doo?|''What's New, Scooby-Doo?'']] and Wayne in [[Wikipedia:Higglytown Heroes|''Higglytown Heroes'']].

In [[2005]], Manriquez was nominated for the Best Performance in a TV Series (Comedy or Drama) - Young Actor Age Ten or Younger at the [[Wikipedia:26th Young Artist Awards|26th Young Artist Awards]] for his role in ''Life with Bonnie''.

In [[2009]], Manriquez stopped acting after appearing as Albert in the film [[Wikipedia:The Secrets of Jonathan Sperry|''The Secrets of Jonathan Sperry'']].<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www2.ocregister.com/articles/frankie-says-kid-2573162-little-life?orderby=TimeStampAscending&oncommentsPage=1&showRecommendedOnly=0|text=Frankie went to Hollywood, twice|nobackup=1}}</ref>
