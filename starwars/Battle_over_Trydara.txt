During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] fought a '''battle''' against Alliance forces above [[Trydara]]. The Alliance forces scrambled to flee, but the 204th's own forces picked off the rebels one by one.<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> [[Yrica Quell]], a former member of the 204th who had fought with the [[fighter wing]] at Trydara, was subjected to mental visions of the battle from the perspective of a rebel [[X-wing starfighter]] cockpit by [[The Force|force]] energies from a [[Unidentified observatory|tower]] in the [[Cerberon system]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle was mentioned in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the second volume in [[Alexander Freed]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
