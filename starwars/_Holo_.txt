"'''Holo'''" was the nickname of a<ref name="A Legend Reborn" /> [[Gender|non-binary]]<ref name="Tweet" /> [[human]]<ref name="Starfighter Missions" /> who served as an [[ace]] [[First Order Special Forces TIE fighter pilot|pilot]]<ref name="A Legend Reborn" /> in the [[First Order Special Forces]].<ref name="Special" /> "Holo" [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|piloted]] a [[Color|red]] [[TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor|TIE/ba interceptor]] and wore a matching [[First Order]] TIE [[First Order TIE pilot flight suit|flight suit]].<ref name="Starfighter Missions" /> At some point,<ref name="X-wing">{{FFGXW2|set=Major Vonreg's TIE Expansion Pack|cardname="Holo"}}</ref> they<ref name="A Legend Reborn" /> participated in a skirmish within the [[Dassal system]] that involved other [[starfighter]]s of the [[TIE series|TIE fighter series]].<ref name="X-wing" />

"Holo" had light skin, brown [[eye]]s, and brown [[hair]] pulled back into a ponytail.<ref name="Starfighter Missions" /> Although skillful, they were less skilled than fellow [[pilot]] [[Major]] [[Elrik Vonreg]],<ref name="A Legend Reborn" /> one of the First Order's most skilled pilots.<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
"Holo" was first mentioned in "A Legend Reborn," an article on [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' official website detailing a then-upcoming expansion for the ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition]]'' miniatures game on [[January 8]], [[2020]].<ref name="A Legend Reborn" /> The expansion, named ''[[Major Vonreg's TIE Expansion Pack]]'', was released on [[January 31]] that year<ref name="Major Vonreg Release">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2020/1/31/available-now-january-24-1|text=Available Now &ndash; January 31|date=2020-01-31}}</ref> and included a card representing "Holo."<ref name="X-wing" />

[[Brooks Flugaur-Leavitt]], who was in charge of the design and development of ''Major Vonreg's TIE Expansion Pack'',<ref name="MVEx">{{FFGXW2|set=Major Vonreg's TIE Expansion Pack}}</ref> established on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] that "Holo" was intended to be non-binary.<ref name="Tweet" /> Their first appearance was in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Starfighter Missions]]'',<ref name="Starfighter Missions" /> which was released on [[November 19]], 2020.<ref name="Release">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gamerbraves.com/star-wars-starfighter-missions-launches-november-for-japan-korea-and-sea|text=''Star Wars: Starfighter Missions'' launches November for Japan, Korea and SEA|author=Kit|date=2020-11-11|archivedate=20210617074707}}</ref> In ''Starfighter Missions'', "Holo's" TIE/ba [[interceptor]] was available in two additional color schemes: black with yellow accents, and a red-and-yellow scheme.<ref name="Starfighter Missions" />
