'''Ayalayli thornwood''' was a type of [[Color|brown]] [[Tree|wood]].<ref name="CVD" /> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}} to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] started the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]] in the [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]] in the [[city]] of [[Mos Espa]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] by spitting the head of a [[gorg]] at an antique [[gong]] that was held in an ayalayli thornwood frame.<ref name="CVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ayalayli thornwood first appeared in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], it was identified in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''.<ref name="CVD" /> The name for the [[Ayalayli thornwood/Legends|wood]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used in the [[2012]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Phantom Menace: The Expanded Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]] and [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Expanded">''[[Star Wars: The Phantom Menace: The Expanded Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
