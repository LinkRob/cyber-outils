A '''platoon''' of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] participated in the [[Liberation of New Valis|liberation]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[mining]] colony of [[New Valis]]. The [[infantry]] [[platoon]] was from the [[Marine Corps]], and was led by [[Captain]] [[Ette Shi'lou]], who served with her [[trooper]]s for several [[Standard year|years]]. During the liberation, the platoon fought against a [[Fhaen's company|company]] of [[stormtrooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]], and were eventually victorious.<ref name="Lead" />
