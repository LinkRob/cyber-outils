The '''Cassian Andor, Jyn Erso & K-2SO Expansion''' ('''SWQ39''') is an expansion to the tabletop game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. An updated version of the earlier [[Jyn Erso Commander Expansion]] and [[Cassian Andor and K-2SO Commander Expansion]]s, it was released on [[January 16]], [[2026]] as part of Atomic Mass Games' overhaul of the game.

==Publisher's summary==
''This expansion contains Cassian Andor, Jyn Erso, and K-2SO miniatures, enough for 3 Operative units, as well as Unit, Command, and Upgrade Cards to support gameplay. This pack is a reissue of the previously released SWL59 Cassian Andor & K-2SO and SWL31 Jyn Erso for Star WarsTM: Legion. This pack includes the same miniatures as SWL59 and SWL31 but updates the packaging and cards to the new SWQ multilingual format.''

*''LEAD THE REBELLION: Strengthen your Rebel Alliance forces with Cassian Andor, Jyn Erso, and K-2SO, three iconic heroes ready to take on covert missions in Star Wars: Legion.''
*''THREE OPERATIVE UNITS IN ONE BOX: Includes three detailed miniatures representing these legendary operatives, plus all cards and tokens needed to bring them into your battles.''
*''UPDATED MULTILINGUAL REISSUE: A reissue of SWL59 (Cassian & K-2SO) and SWL31 (Jyn Erso), featuring the same miniatures and rules—now updated with SWQ multilingual packaging and cards.''
*''COMPLETE EXPANSION SET: Contains 3 miniatures with bases, 3 Unit Cards, 2 Upgrade Cards, 6 Command Cards, 1 Punch Sheet, and 1 Insert—everything you need to add tactical depth to your Rebel army.''
*''OFFICIAL STAR WARS PRODUCT: Produced by Atomic Mass Games, this expansion is fully compatible with all Star Wars: Legion Core Sets, expansions, and Rebel units for seamless integration.''

==Contents==
*3 Miniatures
*3 Unit Cards
*2 Upgrade Cards
*6 Command Cards
