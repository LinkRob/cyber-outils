'''Blackbark-wood''' was a type of rigid [[wood]] that came from [[blackbark tree]]s and could be used to make doors.<ref name="MA" /> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> when the [[Pijali]] [[Royal guard (Pijal)|royal guard]] [[captain]] [[Deren]] refused to give his opinion on the cause of an apparent [[assassin]]ation attempt on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]]'s [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] to the world's [[lord]] [[regent]], [[Rael Averross]], the latter thought of the refusal as a blackbark-wood door being slammed shut in his face. In the same conversation, Averross mentally compared Deren's facial expression to blackbark-wood's rigidity.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blackbark-wood was mentioned in the [[2019]] novel ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
