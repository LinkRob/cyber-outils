The '''Director Orson Krennic Commander Expansion''' ('''SWL33''') is an expansion to ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'', a two-player infantry-based wargame published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]]. It was first announced on [[December 3]], [[2018]] and released in March of [[2019]].

==Publisher's summary==
===Online summary===
''Every great achievement requires just as much ambition, and there are few as ambitious as Director [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]]. As the head of the Empire's Advanced Weapons Research division, he oversaw the creation of the dreaded [[Death Star]], using it wipe out any trace of the Empire's activities on [[Jedha]]''.

''Director Krennic brings the same cunning that helped him build one of the most powerful superweapons the galaxy has ever known to the battlefields of Star Wars: Legion. In these fights, he'll do whatever it takes to push his own agenda and ensure that his command cards win priority. Even better, Krennic's position and skills entitle him to his own entourage of [[Death trooper|Imperial Death Troopers]], giving you plenty of room for other special forces units in your army.''

===Back blurb summary===
''This expansion for STAR WARS: LEGION contains 1 finely sculpted Director Orson Krennic miniature, enough for one commander unit, along with new unit, upgrade, and command cards to field in battle.''<ref name="Legion Expansion" />

==Contents==
*1 Miniature
*1 Unit Card
*3 Upgrade Cards
*3 Command Cards
