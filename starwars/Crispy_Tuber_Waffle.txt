'''Crispy Tuber Waffle''' was a dish consisting of [[bacon]], [[cheese]], [[egg]], [[potato]], [[apple]], and [[sauce]] that was suitable for [[human]] consumption. The dish was served on the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''<ref name="GS" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GSDate">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Crispy Tuber Waffles were available for purchase as breakfast by guests staying at the ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser]]'' hotel upon its opening in [[March]] [[2022]].<ref name="GS" />
