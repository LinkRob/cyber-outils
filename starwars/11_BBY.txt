The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''11 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3266 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7966''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Jelucan]] is [[Annexation of Jelucan|annexed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />
*The [[Galactic Empire]] introduced [[ridgecrawler]]s on [[Jelucan]], which became the most common mode of [[transport]], replacing the native [[Muunyak]]s.<ref name="Lost Stars" />
*[[Gorse Conflict]]; [[Hera Syndulla]] and a group of dissidents including [[Kanan Jarrus]] foil [[Count]] [[Denetrius Vidian]]'s plot to destroy [[Cynda]].<ref name="AND" />
*[[Baron]] [[Lero Danthe]] gains control of mining operations in the [[Gorse system]].<ref name="AND" />
*[[Rae Sloane]] promoted to [[captain]] of the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] ''[[Ultimatum]]''.<ref name="AND" />
*[[Lonni Jung]] joins the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] and begins working his way up.<ref name="One Way Out">{{Andor|One Way Out}}</ref><ref name="11BBY">Jung is stated to have been working his way up the ISB for six years in "[[One Way Out]]." The episode takes places days after "[[Narkina 5 (episode)|Narkina 5]]," which {{SW|url=andor-s1-e8-trivia-gallery|text="Narkina 5" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 6}} dates to [[5 BBY]]. Therefore Jung began working in the ISB in 11 BBY.</ref>
*[[Lieutenant]] [[Gorn]] witnesses the [[Eye of Aldhani]].<ref name="The Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref>
*[[Arihnda Pryce]] finishes her period as [[Acting Governor]], becoming full [[Governor]] of [[Lothal]].
