The '''Blythe homestead''' was a home and mining claim on the [[planet]] [[Elphrona]], inhabited by the [[Blythe family]]. It was located thirty [[kilometer]]s southwest of the town of [[Ogden's Hope]], an [[Standard hour|hour's]] [[speeder]] ride in good weather, in a region where air travel was rendered impossible by Elphrona's unique magnetism. The homestead had been built on two neighboring mining claims, both owned by the Blythes. During the [[High Republic Era]], the homestead was [[Raid on Elphrona|attacked]] by [[Nihil]] raiders who sought to take the Blythes hostage and ransom them to their wealthy relatives in the [[Core Worlds]]. After the raid, the surviving Blythes chose to move in with their relatives on [[Alderaan]] rather than return to the homestead.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
