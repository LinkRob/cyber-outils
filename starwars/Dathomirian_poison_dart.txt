The '''Dathomirian poison dart''' was a type of [[poison dart]] used by the [[Nightsisters]] of [[Dathomir]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], former [[Sith acolyte]] [[Asajj Ventress]] utilized the [[dart]] to dull the senses of [[Count]] [[Dooku]] in an [[Mission to Serenno (Nightsisters)|attempt]] to exact revenge for his betrayal in the [[Battle of Sullust]].<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref>
