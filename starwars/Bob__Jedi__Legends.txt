A [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] whose name may have been "'''Bob'''" once [[Flight group/Legends|flew]] on a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry/Legends|LAAT/i gunship]] during the [[First Battle of Geonosis/Legends|First Battle of Geonosis]] which marked the beginning of the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]]. At the [[Time/Legends|time]], [[Gender/Legends|his]] [[Jedi apparel/Legends|Jedi attire]] consisted of a hooded [[Color/Legends|brown]] [[Cloak/Legends|cloak]] over a gray tunic. The [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] had black [[Eye/Legends|eyes]] and wielded a green-bladed [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]].<ref name="VisD" />

All [[Jedi Archives/Legends|records]] of the [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight's]] activities were lost at the end of the Clone Wars when the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] was attacked as a part of [[Operation: Knightfall/Legends|Operation: Knightfall]] during the [[Great Jedi Purge/Legends|Great Jedi Purge]].<ref name="VisD" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Bob" appeared as an unnamed Jedi Knight LEGO minifigure in the first [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry/Legends|Republic Gunship]] set, which was released in [[2002]].<ref name="LAAT" /> Subsequently, many LEGO fans dubbed the unidentified [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] as "Bob" or "Jedi Bob." "Bob," along with some information on the character and a reference to his commonly used fan-given name, was later mentioned in the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Visual Dictionary]]'' in [[2009]].<ref name="VisD" />
