The '''DarkNet''', or '''darknet''', was a banking and communications system that could host encrypted [[bank]] accounts and be used to send files. In [[232 BBY]], the [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Kassav Milliko]] extorted the [[Eriadu system]]'s [[Governor]] [[Mural Veen]] for fifty million [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], which she transferred into his encrypted darknet account. In [[0 ABY]], shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] used the DarkNet to spread footage of the [[Space warfare|space battle]] that had resulted in the destruction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], which the [[smuggler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and his friend [[Lobot]] watched from a [[Okkul's cantina|cantina]].

==Description==
{{Quote|The funds are being transferred now.|Mural Veen, after depositing fifty million credits into Kassav Milliko's darknet account|<ref name="LOTJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>}}
The DarkNet,<ref name="The Angle" /> also known as the darknet,<ref name="LOTJ" /> was a communications<ref name="The Angle" /> and banking system. The system could be used to host encryption-protected [[bank]] accounts that allowed for untraceable deposits,<ref name="LOTJ" /> and files from the DarkNet could be accessed for viewing through [[Viewscreen|holo-screens]].<ref name="The Angle" />

==History==
{{Quote|Governor, it's easy. If you give me fifty million credits, no one has to die. I can stop the Emergences, and you'll save your people's lives. I can make it real simple for you, too&hellip;|Kassav Milliko, just before giving his darnket account's information to Mural Veen|<ref name="LOTJ" />}}
[[File:KassavMilliko-THREOTS1.png|thumb|right|200px|Tempest Runner Kassav Milliko controlled a bank account on the darnket.]]
The darknet was in operation by the [[time]] of the [[High Republic Era]], and the [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Kassav Milliko]] used the system to host a bank account under his control.<ref name="LOTJ" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="LOTJ Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> Milliko attempted to extort [[Governor]] [[Mural Veen]] for fifty million [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] by offering to destroy [[Eriadu system Emergence|incoming]] [[hyperspace]] debris&mdash;[[Emergences]]&mdash;which would be impacting her [[Star system|system]], the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Eriadu system]], in the near future. After Tempest Runner had his associate, the Nihil [[slicer]] [[Wet Bub]], send over his darknet account's encrypted banking information, Veen reluctantly had the necessary funds transferred to the darknet account. However, partially due to the governor's brief hesitation in sending the credits, Milliko's [[flagship]]&mdash;the ''[[New Elite]]''&mdash;failed to destroy the Emergence due to a [[weapon]]s targeting miscalculation, which resulted in the [[death]]s of 1.2 billion individuals on [[Eriadu's inhabited moon]].<ref name="LOTJ" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] used the system to air anti-Imperial [[propaganda]] clips.<ref name="The Angle" /> In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="AngleDate">"[[The Angle]]" establishes that its events occur in the recent aftermath of the [[Battle of Yavin]], which equates to [[0 ABY]] per the [['ABY-BBY' dating system|dating system]] established by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], the Rebel Alliance leaked footage of the [[Space warfare|space battle]]<ref name="The Angle" /> in the [[Yavin system]]<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> on the DarkNet, which displayed the destruction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]<ref name="The Angle" />&mdash;the Empire's [[moon]]-sized [[planet]]-killing [[battle station]].<ref name="Episode IV" /> As the leaked footage spread quickly, the [[smuggler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] watched it in a [[Okkul's cantina|cantina]] with several patrons, including his close friend [[Lobot]]. The [[cantina]] owner, [[Okkul]], had already watched the footage multiple times but did not mind playing it again for Calrissian, who was surprised to see his old [[starship]]&mdash;the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''&mdash;participating in the battle.<ref name="The Angle" />

==Behind the scenes==
The DarkNet was first mentioned in the short story "[[The Angle]]," authored by [[Charles Soule]] as part of the [[2017]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''.<ref name="The Angle" /> The system was first capitalized as darknet in [[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi|''Light of the Jedi'']], a [[2021]] novel written by Charles Soule as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="LOTJ" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> The real-world [[Wikipedia:Darknet|Darknet]] is a part of the [[Wikipedia:Internet|Internet]] that is not indexed by regular [[Wikipedia:Web search engine|web search engines]] and where anonymously run [[Wikipedia:Website|websites]] exist.<ref name="Investopedia">{{WebCite|url=https://www.investopedia.com/insights/what-dark-net|text=What Is the Dark Net?|author=Reiff, Nathan|date=2020-01-11|work=[[Wikipedia:Investopedia|Investopedia]]|archivedate=20200206150004}}</ref>
