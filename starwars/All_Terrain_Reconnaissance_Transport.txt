The '''All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport '''('''AT-RT'''),<ref name="Collapse" /> also known as the '''All Terrain Recon Transport''',<ref name="SWE AT-RT" /> or the '''Republic scout walker''',<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|at-rt-walker|AT-RT walker}}</ref> was a model of recon [[walker]]<ref name="SWE AT-RT" /> manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]]<ref name="DS Incredibuilds" /> which saw extensive use during the [[Clone Wars]] by [[Advanced Recon Force trooper|Advanced Recon Force Scout Troopers]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. It was the precursor to the [[All Terrain Scout Transport]].<ref name="Databank" /> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], these walkers were adopted by both the [[Galactic Empire]], who used [[Scout trooper]]s to man the vehicles,<ref name="Vader 8" /> and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], who upgraded them to compete with the advancements of the Empire.<ref name="Commander" />

The AT-RTs were usually used for a variety of missions including ground support, [[Law enforcement agency|police]] support, civil defense, and post-battle cleanup efforts. They occasionally took up position on the front lines of ground battles when difficult terrain prevented the use of larger vehicles.<ref name="SWE AT-RT" />

==Description==
[[File:ATRTdriver-TROSPromoYT.png|thumb|left|200px|AT-RTs were driven by ARF scout troopers.]]
Manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]] for usage by the [[Galactic Republic]], the All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport was a small, lightweight, one-seat,<ref name="Collapse" /> 2.9 [[meter]] long<ref name="DS Incredibuilds" /> and 3.45 meter tall<ref name="SWE AT-RT" /> [[walker]] of the [[AT-series|All-terrain vehicle]] line used by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] for reconnaissance, forward control, escort, and light strike duties. [[Pilot]]ed by a single [[clone trooper]], the AT-RT granted both high mobility and tactical flexibility to scout [[platoon]]s. It featured a powerful encrypted [[Comm unit|communications system]], a small, but sophisticated [[sensor]] package, and a sound baffling system to assist in stealth operations.<ref name="Collapse" /> The AT-RT cost between 8,000<ref name="Starships" /> to 20,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Collapse" />

For combat, the AT-RT mounted a chin-mounted [[Maad-38 heavy laser cannon|Maad 38 laser cannon]] in its forward [[hull]],<ref name="Incredibuilds" /> which gave the vehicle anti-personnel and light-[[armor]] capabilities and provided the pilot with enough firepower for nearly any mission.<ref name="Collapse" /> However, the forward-mounted [[blaster]] could be switched out for a retrofitted chin-mounted [[flamethrower]] or a retrofitted [[Z-6 rotary blaster cannon]],<ref name="AT-RT Legion" /> while also wielding a [[mortar]] launcher.<ref name="DS Incredibuilds" /> The walker also boasted a maximum speed of 75 [[Kilometer per hour|kilometers per hour]] and could carry up to 100 [[kilogram]]s.<ref name="SWE AT-RT" />

==History==
===The Clone Wars===
<div style="float:right">[[File:ATRTRepublic-SWL2024update.png|thumb|left|300px|AT-RTs attacking an Armored Assault Tank]]</div>
AT-RT walkers were used by the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] throughout the war. During the [[Battle of Christophsis]], clones rode AT-RTs to fight off [[Whorm Loathsom]]'s [[Whorm Loathsom's army|droid army]].<ref name="TCWfilm">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> During the [[Battle of Ryloth]], [[Lightning Squadron]] rode AT-RTs alongside the [[Twi'lek Resistance]], who rode their own [[blurrg]] mounts, to liberate the capital city of [[Lessu City|Lessu]].<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref>

During the [[Battle of Umbara]], [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|Republic gunships]] deployed the walkers, which were used by the [[501st Legion]] to combat the [[Umbaran Militia]] throughout the lengthy campaign.<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" /> During the [[battle on Kiros]], 501st and [[212th Attack Battalion]] clones rode throughout the city to attack the droids, and [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] rode them to chase after the [[Zygerrian]] [[enslaver]] [[Darts D'Nar]] when he fled the [[Artisan colony|city]].<ref name="Kidnapped">{{TCW|Kidnapped}}</ref> Near the end of the Clone Wars, [[41st Elite Corps]] clone troopers used them at the [[Battle of Kashyyyk]].<ref name="SWE AT-RT" />

===Imperial Era===
[[File:ATRT-SWL-2025UnitCardUpdate.png|thumb|left|250px|Rebel soldiers used AT-RTs during the Galactic Civil War.]] 
AT-RTs continued to be fielded by the [[Galactic Empire]], being driven by [[scout trooper]]s. At one point they were deployed alongside an [[All Terrain Anti Aircraft]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> A group of scout troopers utilized at least two AT-RTs on [[Mygeeto]], where they battled a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] group known as the [[Plasma Devils]].<ref name="Vader 8" />

The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], particularly the [[Tatooine rebel cell]], used modified AT-RTs.<ref name="Commander" />

The Alliance to Restore the Republic also modified some AT-RT's to have a chin-mounted [[flamethrower]], as well as modifying others to have a chin-mounted [[Z-6 rotary blaster cannon]].<ref name="AT-RT Legion">{{Legion|set=AT-RT Unit Expansion}}</ref>

===War renewed===
After the [[Battle of Jakku|collapse]] of the Empire in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="GANew">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> the scattered [[Imperial Remnants|remnants of the Imperial state]] fled to the desolate corners of the galaxy, establishing their own factions<ref name="RemnantSnowtrooper">{{HotToysCite|set=Movie Masterpiece Series|packtype=Sixth Scale Figure|pack=#838: Imperial Remnant Snowtrooper|link=collectibles/star-wars-imperial-remnant-snowtrooper-hot-toys-915297}}</ref> under assorted [[warlord]]s who tried to continue the Imperial project. Limited in what resources were at their disposal, however,<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref> the remnants clung onto what power they could claim<ref name="RemnantStormtrooper">{{HotToysCite|set=Movie Masterpiece Series|packtype=Sixth Scale Figure|pack=#837: Imperial Remnant Stormtrooper|link=collectibles/star-wars-imperial-remnant-stormtrooper-hot-toys-915225}} {{c|Collector Edition}}</ref> and enforced that power<ref name="Sideshow2027">{{HotToysCite|set=Movie Masterpiece Series|packtype=Sixth Scale Figure|pack=#874: AT-RT|link=collectibles/star-wars-imperial-remnant-at-rt-driver-hot-toys-915300?var=915550}}</ref> as they saw fit with whatever surviving resources they could muster<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" /> and salvage,<ref name="Sideshow2Set">{{HotToysCite|set=Movie Masterpiece Series|packtype=Sixth Scale Figure|pack=#875: Imperial Remnant AT-RT Driver and AT-RT|link=collectibles/star-wars-imperial-remnant-at-rt-driver-hot-toys-915300?var=9153002}}</ref> such as surviving AT-RTs.<ref name="Sideshow2027" /> While decades-old by that point,<ref name="Episode III" /> the AT-RT proved to be an effective weapon in the hands of the galaxy's scattered [[Neo-Imperial forces]], which most commonly used the quick machines<ref name="Sideshow2027" /> in icy environments<ref name="Sideshow2Set" /> for scouting operations, patrolling, and quick ground attacks.<ref name="Sideshow2027" />

Across Imperial Remnants, AT-RTs were operated by [[Imperial Remnant AT-RT Driver]]s, who were conditioned for service in the brutal climates<ref name="Sideshow2Set" /> many Imperials had fled to in order to escape Republic notice.<ref name="RemnantSnowtrooper" /> The [[Barro's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] led by Commander [[Barro]] used at least one AT-RT piloted by an Imperial AT-RT Driver. The AT-RT was captured and used by the [[Mandalorian]]s [[Din Djarin]] and [[Din Grogu]] during their [[Attack on Barro's Imperial remnant|shutdown]] of Barro's remnant, being destoryed by Walker 3, an [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)|AT-AT]].<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" /> Later in [[34 ABY]], the [[First Order]] also made use of the walkers. They were part of the deployment on [[Vodran]] when [[J-Squadron]] was captured.<ref name="JTR2" />

==Behind the scenes==
The All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport first appeared in the [[2005]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']],<ref name="Episode III" /> and was later identified in ''[[Collapse of the Republic]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Collapse" />
