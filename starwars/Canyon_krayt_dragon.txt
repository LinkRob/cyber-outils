The '''canyon krayt dragon''' was the smaller and more common [[species]] of [[krayt dragon]], a predatory [[reptile]] from [[Tatooine]]. As its name suggested, it dwelled in rock caves and canyons.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Canyon krayt dragons had [[Color|brown]] skin and yellow [[eye]]s, and their head was covered with a multitude of horns.<ref name="Commander" /> They were far more common than their relatives, the [[leviathan krayt dragon]]s.<ref name="NYCC 2025">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2025-lucasfilm-publishing-reveals|text=NYCC 2025: All the News and Reveals from the Lucasfilm Publishing Panel}}</ref> One such beast had taken up residence in the [[Laguna Caves]] by the time of the [[Invasion of Naboo]].<ref name="AbEverything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> As a young boy, [[Luke Skywalker]] once fell into the lair of a canyon krayt dragon in one of his daring outings.<ref name="Ashcan">[[Star Wars Adventures Ashcan|''Star Wars Adventures'' Ashcan]]</ref> A canyon krayt dragon was slain by the [[First Order]]'s [[Supreme Leader]], [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], after escaping from the prison it had been placed in by [[Gardulla Besadii the Elder]]. Ren used the beast's remains to create a suit and weapons, which he used to return to Gardulla's fortress and kill several of her guards.<ref name="Legacy3">[[Legacy of Vader 3|''Legacy of Vader'' 3]]</ref>
