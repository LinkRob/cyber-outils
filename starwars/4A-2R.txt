'''4A-2R''' was a [[RA-7 protocol droid]] [[droid]] that was a member of [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s [[Ohnaka Gang|pirate gang]] by the [[Clone Wars]]. 4A-2R was a valued part of Ohnaka's operation due to the surveillance equipment in his head.<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
4A-2R first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Dooku Captured]]."<ref name="Dooku Captured" /> He was later identified in [[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2 11|''Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2'' 11]],<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> while [[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]] mistakenly identified him as 4A-R2.<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Droids Series 1|cardname=4A-R2}}</ref>
