An '''annunciator''' was a sound amplification device. They were known to ring if badly damaged.<ref name="RSN">[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (novelization)|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'' novelization]]</ref> Annunciators were fitted in [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]] helmets.<ref name="RSVD">''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
