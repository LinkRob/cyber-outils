The '''''Beast of Burden''''' was a [[Hardcell-class interstellar transport|''Hardcell''-class interstellar transport]] used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|CIS Navy]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. At one point, the ''Beast of Burden'' traveled near a Separatist [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigate]]. It was equipped with six [[sublight engine]]s and three forward [[laser cannon]]s.<ref name="FFG" />
