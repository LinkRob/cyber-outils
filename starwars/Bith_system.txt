The '''Bith system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> near the [[Abregado system]], and contained a [[Color|blue]] astronomical object.<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> It shared its name with [[Bith (planet)|Bith]],<ref name="Bith">{{Databank|bith|Bith}}</ref> a [[planet]]<ref name="FO">[[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']]</ref> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Clak'dor">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Clak'dor system]]</ref> that was home to the [[Bith]] [[species]].<ref name="Bith" /> The system was less than thirty-two hours from [[Batuu]].<ref name="SWTftGE">''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge]]''</ref> [[Jedi General]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] positioned their [[task force]]s there around the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Abregado]], an early battle of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />
