The '''assassination attempt on Patriarch Thooraki''' was an unsuccessful [[assassin]]ation attempt against the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Mitth family]] [[Patriarch]] [[Mitth'oor'akiord]], [[core name]] "Thooraki," that took place before [[39 BBY]]. The Patriarch was traveling incognito when the event occurred, causing a [[Mitth'ras'safis's father|man]] from a [[Unidentified minor Chiss family|minor family]] to step in after seeing Thooraki in distress. The [[Gender|man]] was [[Death|killed]], but his actions saved the Patriarch. Thooraki invited the man's [[Reproduction|pregnant]] [[Mitth'ras'safis's mother|wife]] to the [[Mitth family homestead]] on the [[planet]] [[Csilla]] and invited her [[Rematching|into]] the Mitth, which she accepted.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Even more impressive is that the Patriarch was traveling incognito and my father had no idea whose life he'd saved.|translated=1|Thrass, to Thrawn|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
Before [[39 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Patriarch]] [[Mitth'oor'akiord]], [[core name]] "Thooraki," of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Mitth family]] was traveling incognito.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==The assassination attempt==
{{Quote|He simply saw a desperate need and stepped in.|translated=1|Thrass, to Thrawn|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
An attempt on the Patriarch's life took place, but it was thwarted by a [[Mitth'ras'safis's father|man]] that was a member of a [[Unidentified minor Chiss family|minor family]], who intervened after seeing Thooraki in desperate need. Despite being unaware of the Patriarch's identity, the [[Gender|man]] stepped in, giving his life to save Thooraki.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|So the Patriarch decided to fudge the date of the rematching to after my father's death but before my birth. Since my mother was now officially a ranking distant when I was born&hellip;''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''You were born a cousin.|translated=1|Thrass and Thrawn|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
[[File:Thrass-LE.jpg|thumb|right|200px|The father of Thrass (pictured) saved Thooraki's life]]
As a token of his gratitude, Thooraki invited the man's [[Reproduction|pregnant]] [[Mitth'ras'safis's mother|wife]] to the [[Mitth family homestead]] on the Ascendancy's [[planet]] of [[Csilla]], where he offered her to [[Rematching|join]] the Mitth family as a [[ranking distant]], the highest position in the family he could offer. As the woman's son, [[Mitth'ras'safis]] "Thrass," was born before his mother joined the Mitth, he should have been born a ranking distant; however, as the children of ranking distants were born as [[cousin]]s, the second highest position in a family, Thooraki altered the date of the woman's rematching prior to Thrass' birth, resulting in him being born a cousin.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

By [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date3">The flashback sequence in which [[Mitth'ras'safis|Thrass]] recounts the story of his [[Mitth'ras'safis's father|father's]] [[death]] to [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] takes place prior to the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', which take place in [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> now-[[Syndic]] Thrass recounted the incident to his friend, [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], "Thrawn." Thrawn considered Thrass' father to be an honorable man.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
The assassination attempt on Patriarch Thooraki was mentioned in the flashbacks of the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
