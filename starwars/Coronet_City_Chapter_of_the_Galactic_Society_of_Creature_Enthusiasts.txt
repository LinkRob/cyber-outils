The '''Coronet City Chapter of the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts''' was a division of the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]], a research and conservation organization that studied creatures from across [[the galaxy]]. By the [[High Republic Era]], they were led by [[Twi'lek]] [[Chief Inspector]] [[Deemus Abrus]]. They worked with local authorities in an attempt to stop certain creatures from being stolen on the [[black market]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="MidnightHorizonDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> they stopped the [[Shani]] [[Crukibolt Nart Adeen|Crufeela]] from [[Diet|eating]] the [[Bonbrak]] [[Teetak]]. They took Teetak and arrested Crufeela. Abrus suggested sending Teetak to the [[Blue Peaks]] of [[Corellia]] in attempt to repopulate the area.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
