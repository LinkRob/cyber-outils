'''Bana Rhuus''' was a [[Koorivar]] [[Gender|male]] who was a member of [[Separatist Intelligence]] for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Once the war ended, the usage of a body double made it appear as if the Koorivar had switched sides almost immediately after [[General]] [[Grievous]]'s [[death]] while the real Rhuus went into hiding alongside other Separatist forces that continued to [[Fighting on Olkrastrus IV|fight]] on [[Olkrastrus IV]]. [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]] paid Rhuus a visit on Olkrastrus IV in an [[Investigation into the framing of the Jedi|investigation]] conducted by the senator, along with being accompanied by an [[aide]], the [[Heptooinian]] [[Haki Zeophrine]]. Zeophrine, who despised Rhuus, later killed the Koorivar in the [[Spiral Forest]].<ref name="MOF" />
