An '''automated sidewalk''', also known as a '''mobile sidewalk''' or a '''moving sidewalk''', was a mobile walkway that was used to transport pedestrians across a horizontal plane. Because the [[New Republic senatorial complex]] was a vast building, it contained such walkways to help people travel from one part of the complex to another more easily.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>
