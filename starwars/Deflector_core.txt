A '''deflector core''' was the core inner-workings of a [[deflector shield generator]]. In [[2 BBY]], the [[Spectres]] [[Sabine Wren]] and [[Garazeb Orrelios]] extracted a deflector from a [[Deflector shield generator]] on the desert world of [[Geonosis]]. They used the deflector core to fend off several [[droideka]]s. Later, [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]] praised the Spectres for obtaining a new deflector core for [[Chopper Base]].<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis">{{Rebels|Ghosts of Geonosis}}</ref>
