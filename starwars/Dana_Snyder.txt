'''Dana Snyder''' (born [[November 14]], [[1973]]) is an American actor, stand-up comedian, and writer who voiced [[Graballa/LEGO|Graballa]] the [[Hutt]] in ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]''.<ref name="The Star Wars Show June 15 2017">{{StarWarsShow|HSBls7UXOPo|Porg Poetry, Preacher's Julie Ann Emery Talks Leia & Star Wars Books, & More!}}</ref> His other projects have included [[Wikipedia:Aqua Teen Hunger Force|''Aqua Teen Hunger Force'']], the [[Wikipedia:Aqua Teen Hunger Force Colon Movie Film for Theaters|''Aqua Teen Hunger Force Colon Movie Film for Theaters'']], and the animated TV series [[Wikipedia:Chowder (TV series)|''Chowder'']]. During an interview with [[The Star Wars Show]] host [[Anthony Carboni]], Snyder likened his character Graballa to a "Brooklyn mobster guy whose brother is the head of the family." He likened his rendition of Graballa to a Hutt speaking a "Southern accent."<ref name="The Star Wars Show August 23 2017">{{StarWarsShow|vTNJNRXfSIg|New The Last Jedi Vehicles Revealed, Exploring Lucasfilm's Vaults, and More!}}</ref>
