'''Floor manager''' was an authority [[Assignment|position]] for a work area.<ref name="Narkina 5" /> The [[Ferrixian]] [[Timm Karlo]] was a floor manager at the [[Caleen Salyard]].<ref name="Guide" />

The position was also granted to select prisoners in the [[Narkina 5]] [[Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex|Imperial Prison Complex]] by the [[Imperial factory facility prison guard|Imperial prison staff]]. They were responsible for a work room's particular shift (day or night), acting as the de facto leaders of all the prisoners in the unit and supervising their work. Floor managers were visually distinguished from other prisoners by their scrubs, which had the orange striping continue from the left arm to the right shoulder, whereas it terminated on the left shoulder for regular inmates.<ref name="Narkina 5" /> The guards rarely interacted with prisoners and did not even bother eavesdropping on them, leaving routine discipline to the floor managers.<ref name="Narkina 5" /><ref name="Nobody's Listening">{{Andor|Nobody's Listening!}}</ref>
[[Kino Loy]] led [[room five-two]]'s day shift as the floor manager, while [[Zinska]] led the room's night shift.<ref name="Narkina 5" />
