'''CORT-327''' was a [[freighter]] used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. The ship was [[pilot]]ed by [[Burin Kassal]] and copiloted by [[Calle Tharandon]] during a mission to [[Ord Mantell]] to extract rebel operatives who's location had been compromised.<ref name="Legends" />

==Behind the scenes==
CORT-327 appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Assault]]'' companion app ''[[Legends of the Alliance]]'', in the "Flight of the Freedom Fighter" campaign.<ref name="Legends" />
