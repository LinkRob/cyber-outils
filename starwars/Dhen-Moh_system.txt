The '''Dhen-Moh system''' was located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]] at the coordinates K-14 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> It contained the terrestrial astronomical object [[Dhen-Moh]],<ref name="Rise" /> where the [[Galactic Empire]] ordered [[Dhen-Moh genocides|genocides]] during its [[Imperial Era|reign]].<ref name="Alphabet">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> The [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Death|killed]] masses with his [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|lightsaber]] during the atrocity.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dhen-Moh [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published on [[StarWars.com]] alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]<ref name="Systems" />&mdash;both works having been authored by [[Jason Fry]] and released on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
