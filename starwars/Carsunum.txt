{{Quote|There's spice, of course, but stick with andris, carsunum, or sansanna and never mess around with ryll or the prime Kessel strains.|Platt Okeefe|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
'''Carsunum''' was a [[spice]] variant that the [[smuggler]] [[Platt Okeefe]] recommended as a profitable cargo in the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]]'s ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''. Okeefe warned against trafficking variants such a [[ryll]] or the strains of spice mined on [[Kessel]], and suggested that smugglers stick to variants like carsunum, [[andris]], and [[sansanna spice]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Carsunum was first mentioned in the [[2018]] [[canon]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, carsunum was first introduced in the [[1997]] novel ''[[The Paradise Snare]]'', written by [[A. C. Crispin]].<ref name="The Paradise Snare">''[[The Paradise Snare]]''</ref>
