The '''Carida Grand Prix''' was a racing event that shared its name with <ref name="Outlaws" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> [[Carida]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> and took place in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> Racers from twenty-nine worlds participated, with [[Teph Unu]] as the presumptive favorite; however, [[quad racer]] [[Onjo Rhask]] of<ref name="Outlaws" /> the planet [[Tepasi]]<ref name="Travellers">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> instead claimed first place in a [[Holocamera|holo]]-finish that secured him the second win of his career. The outcome of the race was covered by [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Dorna Parros]] on a broadcast of the [[HoloNet News]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Carida Grand Prix was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
