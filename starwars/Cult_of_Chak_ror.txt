The '''Cult of Chak'ror''' was a [[Nikto]] sect whose members were known as the Chak'ror Warriors. At some point before the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Iron Blockade|blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]], the cultists faced an ultimatum from [[Governor]] [[Uhika]]: they had to serve as her shock troops or be wiped out. The cult chose the former for a time, but ended up on their own again after Uhika [[Death|died]]. At least one Chak'ror Warrior joined the crew of [[the Smuggler]], an individual from [[Burnin Konn]] who led an [[Uprising]] against the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Chak'ror" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cult of Chak'ror and its Warriors were created for the mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', which was developed by [[Kabam]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms and released in [[2015]].<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref>
