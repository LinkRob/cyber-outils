The '''Arrival Area''' was a location in the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire's]] [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|DS-1 Orbital Battle Station]], better known as the [[Death Star/Legends|First Death Star]], where packages were delivered to. Residents were requested to get to the Arrival Area to sign for packages sent to them.
