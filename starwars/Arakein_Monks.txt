The '''Arakein Monks''' were a [[Religion|religious]] order that existed almost two [[Millennium|millennia]] before the [[Galactic Civil War]]. They built a [[stone]] [[Chronometer|clock]] at the top of [[Mount Arakeirkos]] and kept the names of deceased in their [[Book of the Dead]].

==History==
{{Quote|What have you heard about Mount Arakeirkos?''"<br />"''I'm not familiar with it.''"<br />"''Neither am I. But at the top, there's a great clock set in stone, built by the Arakein Monks almost two thousand standard years ago. According to legend, whoever watches each swing of its pendulum for a day will have the lifespan of the universe revealed before his eyes.|Micha Evon and Hazram Namir|audio=Arakein-BTC.flac|<ref name="Twilight" />}}
The [[Religion|religious]] order known as the Arakein Monks was active almost two [[Millennium|millennia]] before the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="Twilight" /> originating in the<ref name="Twilight" /><ref name="Planetnamia" /> [[Inner Rim Territories]]' [[Arakein system]].<ref name="Systems">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> The [[monk]]s built a giant [[Chronometer|clock]] out of [[stone]] at the top of a [[mountain]] named [[Mount Arakeirkos]].<ref name="Twilight" /> They were also rumored to put a deceased individual's names into their [[Book of the Dead]] so that they would remember the individual forever.<ref name="Victory's Price" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref> [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] told the rebel [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] about Mount Arakeirkos and the clock built by the Arakein Monks.<ref name="Twilight" />

During the [[Battle of Jakku]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[New Republic pilot]] [[Vitale]] played the game ''[[Who? What? Where?]]''&mdash;where the players listed who first heard about their [[death]] and how and where they died&mdash;with enemy [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilots]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] over a comm channel. One Imperial [[pilot]] wished for the Arakein Monks to put their name in the alleged Book of the Dead, mentioning the books significance.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Arakein Monks were first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Twilight" />
