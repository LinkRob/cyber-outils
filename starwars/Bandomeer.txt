'''Bandomeer''' was an agricultural [[planet]] located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="HC 4" /> at the junction of the [[Hydian Way]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and the [[Braxant Run]].<ref name="Rogue One planet locations" /> The planet remained focused on agriculture until it was discovered to be rich in [[mineral]] resources, which were in turn exploited over the course of [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]]. Over the course of Bandomeer's history, the world's [[miner]]s sometimes had disputes with the companies that ran the [[mining]] operations. After the [[Galactic Empire]] came to power, it eventually set its sights on Bandomeer to exploit its resources. When the miners tried to resist, the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] arrived and duly punished them.<ref name="HC 4" />
