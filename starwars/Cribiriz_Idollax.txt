'''Cribiriz Idollax''' was a [[Culisetto]] [[Gender|male]] [[warlord]] and [[droid]] [[Technician|mechanic]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. When [[Deva Lompop]] sent [[Zuckuss]], [[4-LOM]] and [[bounty hunter]]s to kill him, they instead killed his brother, [[Sribiriz]]. Idollax spent the following [[Standard year|years]] hiding, planning to avenge his brother by [[Torture|torturing]] the people who killed him. At one point he obtained 4-LOM's head and used it to create a spider-like droid, drastically changing the droid's personality; he then sent 4-LOM against his ex-partner. Zuckuss, after he discovered that the Culisetto was still alive and brainwashed his best friend, killed Idollax.

==Biography==
===Early life===
The [[Culisetto]] male [[Doctor]] Cribiriz Idollax was a [[warlord]] and [[droid]] [[Technician|mechanic]] who held a [[Cribiriz Idollax's compound|compound]] on an unidentified [[planet]] serving as his headquarters. He had a brother named [[Sribiriz]], who was known to be good-hearted and selfless. Sribiriz was strongly against his brother's criminal and violent activities, despite loving him.<ref name="WOTBH 4&Z" />

Idollax crafted his own droids that served him as guards and henchmen.<ref name="WOTBH 4&Z" />

===Feud with Zuckuss and 4-LOM===
One day, before [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">The events of [[War of the Bounty Hunters – 4-LOM & Zuckuss 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – 4-LOM & Zuckuss'' 1's]] flashback take place before Zuckuss and 4-LOM tried to take a bounty on [[Han Solo]] placed by [[Rekias Nodo]], event dated to [[9 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection 78]]'', as Zuckuss and 4-LOM had never worked together before the hunt for Cribiriz Idollax.</ref> Sribiriz told his brother he was going to report his crimes to the authorities. Therefore, Cribiriz decided to change his ways and to stop being a criminal. On that same day, [[Deva Lompop]] hired a group of [[bounty hunter]]s, including the infamous [[Zuckuss]] and [[4-LOM]], to kill the Culisetto warlord, who, in her opinion, was becoming a threat. After breaking into Idollax's facility, Zuckuss and 4-LOM found Sribiriz and, mistaking him for his brother, shot him, killing him, while Cribiriz, hidden, watched. Doctor Idollax then swore revenge against the murderers of his brother and went into hiding from the rest of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="WOTBH 4&Z" />

While hiding, Idollax tried to learn more about the bounty-hunter duo, and he came into contact with the deserter [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]] [[Sardis Ramsin]], who,<ref name="WOTBH 4&Z" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> brought him 4-LOM's severed head, which had been found by the [[Jawa]]s on [[Tatooine]], after it was separated from the original body by the bounty hunter [[Boba Fett]]. Idollax then created a new giant spider-like droid, reprogramming 4-LOM's head, and sent it to pursue Zuckuss.<ref name="WOTBH 4&Z" />

On [[Nar Shaddaa]], Zuckuss fought his ex-partner along with other bounty hunters, and won. During the battle, as the [[Gand]] [[findsman]]'s [[Jega's Squad|allies]] [[Kaj]], [[Trot]], [[Mareesee]], and [[Hormf]] battled 4-LOM, Zuckuss discovered the presence of his old enemy, Doctor Idollax, who explained his story and plan. Zuckuss, after the Culisetto mechanic finished talking, shot him with his rifle, killing him.<ref name="WOTBH 4&Z" />
