'''Anesthetics''' were substances that could put an injured person to [[sleep]], thereby relieving them of their pain. [[Vi Moradi]] used an anesthetic to put the [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Archex]] to sleep after he was stabbed.<ref name="Phasma" /> [[61st Mobile Infantry|Twilight Company's]] [[medical droid]]s also made use of anesthetics during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="BTC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> Medical kits could include injectible vials of anesthetics and other substances.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>
