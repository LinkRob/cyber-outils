'''Direct Transaction''' was a type of [[Shift Token]] that was used in the card game [[Kessel Sabacc]]. When played the player trades hands with another player of their choose.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] found a Direct Transaction token in [[Jet Kordo]]'s [[Jet Kordo's vault|vault]] in [[Kijimi City]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Shift tokens were created for Kessel Sabacc which first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. Direct Transaction was one of several Shift Tokens that could be collected and used in Kessel Sabacc, an optional activity in the game.<ref name="Outlaws" />
