'''Chrome Citadel''' was a run-down [[space station]] located in deep [[Realspace|space]]<ref name="Bloodline" /> along the [[Ootmian Pabol]]<ref name="Ootmian" /> in the [[Harron sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Once inhabited by [[asteroid]] [[Mining|miners]], Chrome Citadel was abandoned [[Standard decade|decades]] before the [[Galactic Civil War]] and eventually became home to underworld types such as [[smuggler]]s and [[Slavery|slavers]].<ref name="Bloodline" /> In [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']] to [[28 ABY]].</ref> Chrome Citadel was still inhabited but the piecemeal repairs done to the station's original [[hull]] gave the place the appearance that it could fall apart at any moment.<ref name="Bloodline" />
