A [[Gender|female]] individual was the '''great-grandmother''' of the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]]<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /> [[human]] female<ref name="Dormé">{{Databank|dorme|Dormé}}</ref> [[Dormé|Dorra]].<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /> By [[28 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Queen's Shadow]]'' to [[28 BBY]].</ref> she had taught her great-granddaughter how to carve.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Dorra began serving as a [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] to [[Galactic Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and took on the name Dormé. During a holiday, the [[handmaiden]]s and Amidala visited the senator's [[Naberrie family|parents]] at the [[Naberrie family house|Naberrie's home]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. While at the house, Dormé began carving with Amidala's [[Parent|father]] [[Ruwee Naberrie]], and he complimented her carving skills. In response, Dormé thanked Ruwee and explained that she had been taught how to carve by her great-grandmother, and noted that as her great-grandmother was now older, it was easier for her to carve, stating that sewing made her joints ache. Dormé then mentioned that she was looking forward to visiting her great-grandmother a couple of [[Standard day|days]] later when she swapped places with fellow handmaiden [[Cordé]].<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dormé's great-grandmother was mentioned in ''[[Queen's Shadow]]'', a [[2019]] young-adult novel written by [[E. K. Johnston]] as the first installment in the ''[[Star Wars: Queen's Series]]''.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />
