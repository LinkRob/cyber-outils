The '''Dance of the Worm''' was a test performed by [[Salamander Warriors]] as a way of defending their honor. Each opponent was mounted on a [[Lava worm (Brazadann)|lava worm]] and attached to the saddle by a [[Rope/Legends|rope]]. Then each [[Worm/Legends|worm]] was made to inhale the juices from a [[Gender/Legends|female]] worm, which made them uncontrollable. The worms would then rear up to eject the riders, and the opponents would strike at each other with bladed [[Weapon/Legends|weapons]] in an attempt to make the other opponent fall. Opponents had the right to use any bladed weapon of their choice, which included [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsabers]] and [[Vibro-weapon/Legends|vibro-weapons]]. All moves were permitted, except cutting the rope that prevented the riders from falling into the [[Magma/Legends|lava]]. That last opponent still on the saddle was deemed the winner, and the other opponents were disgraced.<ref name="Cristaux">{{CasusBelliCite|76|La légende des cristaux}}</ref>
