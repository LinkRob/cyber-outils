'''Brigitte Kali Canales''', known professionally as '''Brigitte Kali''', is an Latin-American actress from [[Wikipedia:Miami|Miami]], [[Wikipedia:Florida|Florida]]. She voiced [[Trace Martez]] in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|revival]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="GWaT Credits">{{TCW|Gone with a Trace}} credits</ref><ref name="DND Credits">{{TCW|Deal No Deal}} credits</ref>
