{{Quote|This wreckage is from a battle between ''Separatists'' and the ''Republic'' during the ''Clone Wars''&mdash;|Beilert Valance, to T'onga|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" />}}
Between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''battle''' between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] as part of the [[Clone Wars]] took place in<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim's]]<ref name="BMF 98">{{BuildFalconCite|98|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> [[Ruusan system]]. The [[Republic Military|Republic forces]] consisted of at least one [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] and [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class assault ship]], and at least two [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]]s, while the Separatists fielded at least one [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Bounty Hunters 4|''Bounty Hunters'' 4]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> wreckage from the battle was used by the [[Nautolan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Nakano Lash]] to conceal her [[Nakano Lash's 720 light freighter|ship]] as she waited for her protégé&mdash;the [[cyborg]] [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]]&mdash;to find her. As Valance made his way to Lash's [[720 light freighter|ship]] aboard his [[Broken Wing|own]], he pointed out the wreckage to fellow bounty hunter [[T'onga]], noting it was from the battle during the Clone Wars.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle in the Ruusan system was mentioned in the [[Bounty Hunters 4|fourth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue&mdash;which was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], with the wreckage from the battle illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]]&mdash;was published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" /> on [[August 19]], 2020.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/80123/star_wars_bounty_hunters_2020_4|text=Star Wars: Bounty Hunters (2020) #4}}</ref>
