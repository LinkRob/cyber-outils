'''David Vallone''' is an artist who worked on the [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]]s. 

He and [[Richard Green]] were chosen by [[Collette Michaud]] to join the creative team of ''[[Star Wars: Rebel Assault]]'' in order to replace two artists who left the project. Vallone joined halfway into the project doing the cutscenes of level #8: "Imperial Walkers". He also had creative freedom for level #11: "Yavin Training" which he "designed as [he] went along".<ref>''[[Star Wars: Rebel Assault: The Official Insider's Guide]]''</ref>

He also did some 3D artwork for ''[[Star Wars: Dark Forces]]''.<ref>{{MobyGames|url=person/1687/david-vallone/|text=David Vallone}}</ref>
