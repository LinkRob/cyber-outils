A '''Dedicated Energy Receptor''' ('''DER''') was a type of [[sensor]] found almost exclusively on military [[starship]]s<ref name="YT1300" /> including the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter]]<ref name="Complete">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> and the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[IGV-55 surveillance vessel]]s<ref name="DRVG">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> where it served as the primary passive sensing device for their [[sensor array]]s. DERs could detect any [[electromagnet]]ic emission within their range, including [[comlink]] transmissions, [[infrared]], and [[laser]] light. Because of their military nature, mounting a DER on a civilian ship was generally illegal.<ref name="YT1300" />
