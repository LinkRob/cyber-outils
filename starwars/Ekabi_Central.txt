'''Ekabi Central''' was the vibrant electric town of [[Coruscant]], an [[ecumenopolis]] entirely covered<ref name="MOE" /> by [[Imperial City]].<ref name="Rebellion">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref> Shortly after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="MOE" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Colonel]] [[Halland Goth]] of the [[Emperor's Royal Guard]] and his [[TC-series protocol droid]] [[TC-99]] acquired [[Oolon's Star Almanac]], a collection of [[data-tape]]s in an obscure and obsolete format. Intrigued, the pair trekked all the way from their [[Halland Goth's quarters|accommodations]] in the [[Imperial Inspectorate HQ|Naval Intelligence Agency building]] in the [[Federal District]] to Ekabi Central to look for a compatible reader. After sifting through numerous stalls of unrecognizable devices and parts, Goth found the reader they needed in perfect condition and acquired it, although he felt that he had [[Currency|paid]] three times more for the device than its actual worth.<ref name="MOE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ekabi Central was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Master of Evil]]'', which was written by [[Adam Christopher]].<ref name="MOE" />
