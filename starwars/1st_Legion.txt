The '''1st Legion''', or '''First Legion''', was an elite [[stormtrooper]] [[legion]] in the [[Imperial Military|military forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was the legion that served under the command of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and was known as such across [[the galaxy]] due to their affiliation with [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] [[Sith apprentice|right hand]]. Its [[Soldier|members]] were recruited from the ranks of the equally renowned [[501st Legion]], another stormtrooper legion that directly served under Vader's command. Those in the 1st Legion who failed to meet Vader's expectations were either transferred back to the 501st or [[Death penalty|summarily executed]] for failure. [[Kimmund]], an [[officer]] of the First Legion, was the fourth [[commander]] in [[Kimmund's unit|his particular unit]], his predecessors having been either demoted or put to [[death]] by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]].

Prior to the [[Galactic Civil War]], the First Legion deployed to the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] alongside the [[Imperial Navy|forces]] of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]. Their joint mission brought them into conflict with the [[Grysk]]s, a warlike [[Species|people]] native to the [[Unknown Regions]]. The First Legion fought the Grysks on several occasions during their [[time]] with Thrawn, the first of which was at Batuu where Kimmund's [[trooper]]s [[Mission to Batuu (Galactic Empire)|infiltrated]] a [[Grysk long-range heavy freighter]] and captured several [[Grysk warrior]]s. However, the [[prisoner]]s that they brought aboard the ''[[Chimaera]]''&mdash;an [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] and Thrawn's [[flagship]]&mdash;attempted to steal the First Legion's [[transport]], the ''[[Darkhawk]]'', resulting in a [[Skirmish aboard the Chimaera|battle]] that Kimmund survived with the help of Thrawn's personal [[bodyguard]], the [[Noghri]] [[assassin]] [[Rukh]].

Following the failed [[prison]]break, the First Legion fought the Grysks in the [[Battle of Mokivj]] with [[Darth]] Vader leading the ground assault in person. During the battle, Kimmund's [[troop]]s rescued most of the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Chiss]] [[Gender|girls]] that had been abducted by the Grysks. Thrawn and Vader sought to retrieve all of the captives; therefore, they tracked the Grysks that escaped from [[Mokivj]] via [[hyperspace]], leading to a [[Battle against Grysk forces|another confrontation]] in which the First Legion succeeded in rescuing the remaining Chiss [[Youngling|children]]. The First Legion supported the 501st Legion's [[Blizzard Force]] during the [[Battle of Hoth]], during which 1st and 501st Legion [[Cold weather assault trooper|cold weather assault stormtroopers]] broke into Echo Base under Vader's leadership.

==Description==
{{Quote|We're the First Legion, Lord Vader's personal stormtroopers.|Commander Kimmund|<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />}}
[[File:1stLegionAttack-ThrawnAlliances3.png|thumb|left|250px|The 1st Legion (1st Legion stormtroopers pictured) was an elite military division of the Galactic Empire's Stormtrooper Corps.]]
The [[Gender|men and women]] of the First [[Legion]] were [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] personal [[stormtrooper]]s during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Renowned for their affiliation with the [[Sith apprentice|right hand]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], the legion's [[trooper]]s were proud of their status in the [[Stormtrooper Corps]]. When [[Rukh]] dismissed the First Legion as "unknowns," [[Commander]] [[Kimmund]] rebutted the [[Noghri]] [[assassin]]'s statement, citing his legion's reputation as proof of their fame. As [[soldier]]s they were held in high esteem by [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], who considered their actions against the [[Grysk]]s exemplary.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

The First Legion recruited its members from the ranks of the [[501st Legion]], another renowned [[Imperial Military]] unit<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> that also directly served under Vader,<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> ableit with a history that stretched back to before the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation of the Empire]]<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> as a [[clone trooper]] legion under [[Jedi General]] Anakin Skywalker during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> First Legion [[officer]]s also came from within the 501st, yet those who failed to meet Vader's expectations, such as Commander Kimmund's predecessors, were either demoted and transferred back to the 501st or [[Death penalty|executed]] by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]. Due to Vader's command style, Kimmund concluded that his only hope for survival was to keep the [[troop]]s in line and compliant with orders.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

Beyond Vader and Kimmund, other officers in the 1st Legion included [[Sergeant]]s [[Aksind]], [[Drav]], and [[Viq]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> As a legion in the [[Imperial Army]], the 1st Legion could be split into smaller units. Kimmund commanded [[Kimmund's unit|his own unit]] of the 1st Legion, which included a [[squad]].<ref name="Alliances 3">[[Thrawn: Alliances 3|''Thrawn: Alliances'' 3]]</ref> The 1st Legion also made use of a modified freighter ''[[Darkhawk]]'', while Vader used a [[Lord Vader's Lambda|personal Lambda shuttle]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> During the [[Battle of Hoth]], 1st Legion stormtroopers served as [[Cold weather assault trooper|cold weather assault stormtroopers]].<ref name="Commanding First Legion" />

==History==
===Vader's legion===
{{Quote|We're hardly unknowns.|Commander Kimmund|<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />}}
[[File:Choke_on_your_aspirations.png|thumb|right|250px|During the Age of the Empire, the First Legion served under the Sith Lord Darth Vader.]]
The 1st Legion<ref name="Hoth & DS2">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Battle of Hoth and the Second Death Star}}</ref> was levied by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Vader before the [[Battle of Scarif|outbreak]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Following the [[Battle of Atollon]], the Emperor sensed a [[Disturbance in the Force|disturbance]] in [[the Force]] originating on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Vader and Grand [[Admiral]] Thrawn were both sent to investigate; Commander Kimmund accompanied Vader along with a [[Kimmund's unit|contingent]] of the First Legion. They traveled to Batuu via Thrawn's [[flagship]], the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]''. The [[Star Destroyer]] was complemented by the [[Sith]]'s private [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle]] as well as the First Legion's [[transport]], the ''[[Darkhawk]]''&mdash;a repurposed [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars [[Republic Era|era]] [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[starship]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

===Mission to Batuu===
{{Quote|By the time we got to the cockpit the entire computer system had been wiped clean, and every bit of electronics had been fried. In fact, there were places where it looked like they'd taken literal flame to the walls and equipment.|Commander Kimmund|<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />}}
[[File:Chimaera_Exp_Pack_box_art_crop.png|thumb|left|250px|The First Legion was stationed on the ''Chimaera'' during the Grysk conflict.]]
During the voyage to Batuu, there was a degree of tension between Vader's legion and the crew of the ''Chimaera'', and particularly between Kimmund's unit and Thrawn's [[bodyguard]] Rukh. The Noghri warrior made a habit out of boarding the First Legion's vessel unbidden; therefore, trooper [[Sampa]] rigged the ship's hatches with intrusion alarms to keep Rukh out. Despite their precautions, Rukh still managed to infiltrate the ''Darkhawk'', this [[time]] making it as far as the electronics shop. Using trooper [[Jid]]'s [[helmet]] to trigger the alarm, he alerted the First Legion and was promptly escorted off of the ship by [[Sergeant]] [[Drav]] and trooper [[Morrtic]]. Annoyed by Rukh's actions, Kimmund feared that the next security breach would result in either the Noghri's [[death]] or his own at the hands of the Sith [[Lord]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

Upon reaching Batuu, Vader and Thrawn traveled to the surface while their subordinates remained onboard the ''Chimaera''. Their arrival was followed soon after by a group of Grysk [[freighter]]s, the [[Grysk long-range heavy freighter|largest]] of which was [[Mission to Batuu (Galactic Empire)|attacked]] by Kimmund's forces after Thrawn refused to reveal the ''Chimaera''{{'s}} location to the enemy. The Grysks, a warlike [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that came from the [[Unknown Regions]], threatened the interests of both the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] and the Galactic Empire according to Thrawn. While Vader and Thrawn dealt with the Grysks' [[Darshi]] [[Mercenary|mercenaries]], the First Legion deployed to the freighter via the ''Darkhawk''. After the Grysk vessel's [[hyperdrive]] was disabled by a [[squadron]] of [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]]s led by [[Vult Skerris]], the First Legion boarded the freighter and attempted to secure it along with its holdings for the Empire. Kimmund personally oversaw their efforts to capture the cargo hold, in accordance with Vader's instructions to keep the cargo intact, but the Grysks used explosives to destroy their cargo along with themselves.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

===Defense of the ''Darkhawk''===
{{Quote|Commander Kimmund cannot be blamed for his actions. On the contrary, his actions and those of his stormtroopers were exemplary.|Grand Admiral Thrawn|<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />}}
[[File:Thrawn_Rebels_S3.png|thumb|right|250px|Grand Admiral Thrawn regarded the First Legion as an exemplary unit.]]
Despite failing to secure the cargo hold, Kimmund's men were able to [[stun]] other Grysks before they could complete their [[Death|suicide]] mission. The First Legion then transferred the [[prisoner]]s to the ''Chimaera'' by order of Thrawn, who wanted to interrogate the Grysks. Thrawn had them confined to personal quarters typically reserved for [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilots]]; in response, Kimmund and Sergeant [[Aksind]] decided to remain aboard the ''Darkhawk'' along with its [[pilot]] [[Tephan]], having felt uncomfortable leaving their ship unguarded with the prisoners nearby. When the Grysk escaped from their makeshift [[prison]], they attempted to escape from the ''Chimaera'' by stealing the First Legion's transport. A [[Skirmish aboard the Chimaera|brief but intense skirmish]] ensued between the Grysks and Kimmund's companions, resulting in Aksind's death despite Rukh's timely assistance. Most of the [[alien]]s were killed in the fighting, although Kimmund and Rukh were able to recapture four of them.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

Vader was far from pleased; he and Thrawn allowed the Grysks to escape from their [[prison]] so that they could steal one of the ships, all of which had been outfitted with [[homing beacon]]s that would have led the ''Chimaera'' to the enemy's secret headquarters. Kimmund, who had not been informed of his superiors' intentions, inadvertently ruined the plan by preventing the aliens' escape. If the Grand Admiral had not spoken in the Commander's defense, Kimmund believed he might have become the second officer of his unit to be put to death for failure. As a result of the Grysks' decision to steal a Clone Wars era vessel like the ''Darkhawk'', Thrawn concluded that their base was an old [[Mokivj droid factory|Separatist droid factory]] located on the planet [[Mokivj]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

===Battle of Mokivj===
{{Quote|Commander Kimmund just reported in, sir. They have the prisoners and have cleared the enemy from the courtyard. They're bringing the ''Darkhawk'' in now for a pickup, and expect to be back at the ''Chimaera'' within the hour.|Lieutenant Lomar, reporting the First Legion's success to Grand Admiral Thrawn|<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />}}
[[File:DarthVader-BHUSC.png|thumb|left|250px|Darth Vader led the First Legion against the Grysks during the Battle of Mokivj.]]
Upon the ''Chimaera''{{'s}} arrival at Mokivj, Thrawn requested use of Vader's stormtroopers, intending to lead the [[Battle of Mokivj|ground assault]] in person. However, the Dark Lord rejected Thrawn's plan and insisted that he remain with the [[command ship]] while Vader himself led the First Legion to the planet's surface. They successfully infiltrated the factory where Vader created a ruse to distract the Grysks while Kimmund, Sergeant [[Viq]], and trooper [[Elebe]] searched for the prisoners they had been ordered to rescue. The Grysks who guarded the prisoners were killed by Kimmund's stormtroopers, although the captives themselves surprised the Commander for they were young [[Force-sensitive]] [[Gender|girls]]&mdash;three of whom were kept in [[hibernation chamber]]s&mdash;of Thrawn's species, the [[Chiss]]. Taking the [[Youngling|children]] into their custody, Kimmund and his troopers headed to the factory's courtyard and cleared it of enemy units in order to rendezvous with the ''Darkhawk''. Aboard the ''Chimaera'', [[Senior Lieutenant]] [[Lomar]] informed the Grand Admiral that the First Legion succeeded in their task and would return to the flagship with the rescued prisoners within the [[Standard hour|hour]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

When Kimmund's unit returned to the ''Chimaera'', the Commander was informed by Vader that a small number of prisoners remained with the Grysks, who escaped into hyperspace before Thrawn's Defenders could intercept them. At Thrawn's request, Vader used [[the Force]] to track the Grysks through hyperspace, believing the Sith Lord's [[Force power|powers]] would lead them to the location of the Grysks' main forces, and by extension, the remaining prisoners.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

===Rescue operation===
{{Quote|Commander Kimmund and the First Legion are more than capable of handling a breach and rescue mission on their own.|Darth Vader|<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />}}
[[File:SWR_Sinrich_optical_dephaser.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The First Legion utilized Rukh's stealth and tracking skills to complete a rescue mission.]]
Having tracked the enemy's lair to a [[star system]], the First Legion planned to ambush the Grysk ship using the ''Darkhawk'' while the ''Chimaera'' [[Battle against Grysk forces|attacked]] the main [[warship]]s. Vader opted to support the First Legion by [[pilot]]ing one of the [[Twin ion engine|TIE]] Defenders, believing his presence was not required for Commander Kimmund and the stormtroopers to conduct a breach and rescue operation. The trap was a success; with the [[engine]]s disabled by Vader, the First Legion boarded the Grysk vessel. Aided by Rukh's tracking abilities, [[Kimmund's unit]] began their search for the remaining Chiss captives while two troopers remained with the ''Darkhawk'' to guard it. A few troopers perished during the mission and Jid was left behind after sustaining a direct hit from a Grysk [[lightning gun]], but ultimately the First Legion was successful in its task, rescuing more Chiss from the Grysks.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

===Battle of Hoth===
[[File:FirstLegionSnowtrooper.png|thumb|left|250px|During the Imperial attack, both the 1st and 501st Legions fought against the Rebel Alliance (a 1st Legion snowtrooper on Hoth, pictured).]]
With the [[Galactic Civil War]] raging between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], Darth Vader's [[Death Squadron]] was tasked with locating [[Alliance High Command]]'s base. Using [[Project Swarm]], the Empire located [[Echo Base]] on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Hoth]], and deployed its ground forces after arriving and the rebels activated their [[deflector shield generator]].<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref> Darth Vader deployed the 501st's elite [[Blizzard Force]], under the command of [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]].<ref name="HC 29">{{HelmetCollectionCite|29|Helmets}}</ref> Blizzard Force's units were supported by the First Legion in the critical [[Battle of Hoth]],<ref name="Hoth & DS2" /> which resulted in a major Imperial victory.<ref name="Episode V" /> Vader led 501st<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> and 1st Legion snowtroopers into Echo Base.<ref name="Commanding First Legion" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 1st Legion was created by [[Timothy Zahn]] for the [[2018]] novel ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> The 1st Legion was identified as such in the English copy of "[[The Battle of Hoth and the Second Death Star]]" by [[De Agostini]], which previously wrote it out as First Legion when translated.<ref name="Hoth & DS2" /> In an interview, Zahn said that he originally wanted to include the 501st Legion as Vader's personal force of stormtroopers, as it was in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]]. However, the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] vetoed the idea, saying that Vader would not have wanted to be closely associated to a unit that had been commanded by Anakin Skywalker during the Clone Wars. However, due to the 501st's expert troops, Vader would still select his stormtroopers from within their ranks when recruiting for the First Legion.<ref name="Zahn Interview with SWE">{{YouTube|channel=@StarWarsExplained|name=Star Wars Explained|video=GYUk_LmQKz4|text=Timothy Zahn Full Interview &ndash; Dragon Con 2018|date=2018-09-08}}</ref>

The First Legion being Vader's personal legion during the time of the Galactic Empire contradicted the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', where the 501st were referred to as Vader's personal legion by [[Everi Chalis]].<ref name="BF">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> When asked about the matter, Story Group member [[Matt Martin]] mused that perhaps either Chalis was wrong or that the things could have changed by the time of ''Battlefront''.<ref name="Matt's Declaration">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1063948568686297088|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=She could just be wrong. Or things could have changed by the time of ESB. And Alliances does specifically say the First are pulled from members of the 501st so it's not even completely incorrect anyway. (It was a conscious decision, not an error).|archiveurl=https://archive.is/oCKoC}}</ref> While the "Vader's Fist" nickname was attributed to the 501st in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]],<ref name="Star Wars: Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars: Battlefront II]]''</ref> ''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]'' calls the First Legion "Vader's Fist."<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> However, ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]''<ref name="HC 16">{{HelmetCollectionCite|16|Helmets}}</ref> and ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'' applied it to the 501st in canon as well.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> While ''Helmet Collection'' was released before<ref name="HC 16" /> ''The Odyssey of Star Wars'',<ref name="Abrams">{{Abrams|url=product/untitled-star-wars-novel_9781419756283|text=The Odyssey of Star Wars An Epic Poem}}</ref> ''Battles that Changed the Galaxy'' was released after it.<ref name="DK">{{DK|url=uk/book/9780241418703-star-wars-battles-that-changed-the-galaxy|text=Star Wars Battles That Changed the Galaxy}}</ref>
