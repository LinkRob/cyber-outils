Prior to the onset of the [[Great Sith War/Legends|Great Sith War]], a '''lightsaber duel''' occurred on the planet Vigil, between the [[Sith apprentice/Legends|Sith apprentices]] Xash and Sindra, and the [[Dark Jedi/Legends|Dark Jedi]] Lian Dray. Dray won the duel, after he [[Death/Legends|slew]] the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] siblings while defending his charge, a young [[Gender/Legends|girl]] named Nova.

==Behind the scenes==
The duel on Vigil appeared in author [[Paul Danner]]'s short story titled ''[[Light and Shadow]]'', which was set during the time of the Great Sith War. According to Lostworlds, ''Light and Shadow'' was originally slated to feature in one of [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]s'' but was never published therein. Thus, the characters, places, and events&mdash;the duel on Vigil included&mdash;of ''Light and Shadow'' are not considered part of [[canon]].<ref name="LW">''[https://web.archive.org/web/20050830160143/home.earthlink.net/~kcfurman/rpg.html#lightandshadow Light and shadow on Lostworlds]''</ref>

The story can now be found on the Internet at the [http://www.myuselessknowledge.com/swfa/ Star Wars Fanboy Association].<ref name="SWFBA">''[http://www.myuselessknowledge.com/swfa/lightdark.html Light and Shadow on www.myuselessknowledge.com]''</ref>
