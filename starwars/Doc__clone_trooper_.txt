{{Quote|I'm not a medic, but they call me Doc for a reason. I have an instinct. About medicine, bodies.|Doc, to Asajj Ventress|<ref name="Worthless" />}}
"'''Doc'''" was a [[clone trooper]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Doc fell into a trap within a [[Unidentified fortress (jungle planet)|fortress]] along with [[mercenary]] [[Asajj Ventress]].<ref name="Worthless" />

==Biography==
{{Quote|Let me guess, you fell through the trapdoor, and you've been down here long enough to know there's no exit. You've tried climbing up and failed.|Asajj Ventress, to Doc|<ref name="Worthless" />}}
The [[Gender|male]] [[Cloning|clone]] was grown on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] from the base of [[human]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] to serve in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="Databank" /> He was given the nickname "Doc" due to his instincts concerning medicine and bodies.<ref name="Worthless" /> Sometime between [[22 BBY]] and [[20 BBY]], during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="22 BBY - 20 BBY">"[[Worthless]]" depicts [[Asajj Ventress]] in the service of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the [[First Battle of Geonosis|beginning]] of the Clone Wars to [[22 BBY]] and the [[Battle of Sullust]], during which Ventress was forced out of Separatist service, to [[20 BBY]].</ref> Doc fell through a trapdoor into a [[Unidentified fortress (jungle planet)|fortress]] during a [[Battle on a jungle planet|battle]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Droid Army|army]] on a [[jungle]] [[Unidentified jungle planet (fortress)|planet]].<ref name="Worthless" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Funny, that you have no freedom, yet you fight for the freedom of strangers.''"<br />"''I guess that's what sacrifice is, isn't it? Giving up something valuable in the hopes that others can have something even better.|Asajj Ventress and Doc|<ref name="Worthless" />}}
As a clone of [[Jango Fett]], Doc stood at 1.83 [[meter]]s.<ref name="Databank" /> He believed the sacrifice of his freedom was important for others in the [[galaxy]] to have their own.<ref name="Worthless" />

==Equipment==
Doc wore a [[Color|black]] bodysuit under his [[clone trooper armor]]. He had a [[holster]] that he used to carry Ventress's [[Asajj Ventress's lightsabers|lightsabers]] and a small [[lantern]]. He had a makeshift bag made of fabric to carry the plates of his [[armor]] and Ventress's [[lightsaber]]s during their escape from the fortress.<ref name="Worthless" />
