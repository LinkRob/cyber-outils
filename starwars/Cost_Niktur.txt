'''Cost Niktur''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who grew up on [[Genhu]]. In [[34 ABY]], [[Ymmoss]] and Niktur were captured by the [[First Order]] and [[Harra]] who sent them to [[Harra the Hutt's stronghold|her stronghold]] on [[Vodran]]. When [[J-Squadron]] was [[Mission to Vodran|captured]] on Vodran, Ymmoss and Niktur helped J-[[Squadron]] escape the stronghold.<ref name="Escape from Vodran" />
