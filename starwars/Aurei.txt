An '''aurei''' (plural '''''aurei''''') was a form of [[currency]] used within [[the galaxy]]. Aurei, alongside [[peggat]]s and [[zemid]]s, were among several of the blood money that was looted from thousands of worlds by the [[First Order]]'s campaigns. Because these coins could be melted down, they had a universal value.<ref name="TLJ:TVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
