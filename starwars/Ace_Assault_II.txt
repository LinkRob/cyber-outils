'''Ace Assault II''' is an online game found on [[The LEGO Group|LEGO]].com and successor to ''[[Ace Assault]]''. In the game, the player plays as a LEGO [[Starship/Legends|starship]] and fights against another LEGO starship. A "special edition" version of the game has been released, featuring additional levels.
