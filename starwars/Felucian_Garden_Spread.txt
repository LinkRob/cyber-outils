{{Quote|The Felucians were masters at turnin' their harvest into plentiful and well-balanced meals, a skill I tried to reflect in this tasty vegetarian spread that I named in their honor.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
'''Felucian Garden Spread''', also known as '''Felucian Kefta & Hummus Garden Spread''', was a [[Diet|vegetarian]] [[Foodstuff|dish]] created by the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]]. Inspired by his experiences learning about [[Felucian]] [[Farmer|farming]] at a [[farm]] on the [[planet]] [[Felucia]], it consisted of [[Plant|herb]] hummus spread and [[vegetable]] relish served with [[flatbread]] or [[ronto wrapper]]s and plant-based meatballs or [[Parwan Nutricake]]s.

In [[34 ABY]], Tuggs served the spread at his mobile [[restaurant]] [[Tuggs' Grub]] when it was stationed at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]]. He also published the recipe for another version in his [[Book|cookbook]] ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''.

==Description==
{{Quote|You could scoop it all up with a piece of flatbread, but no one's gonna blame you if you just lick it right off the plate.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
Felucian Garden Spread was a [[Diet|vegetarian]] [[Foodstuff|dish]] with two variations, one being a [[Book|cookbook]] recipe<ref name="Cookbook" /> and the other prepared at a [[restaurant]]. At its base was a relish [[salad]] made with [[Color|red]] and green [[vegetable]]s and a puréed [[Plant|herby]] hummus spread. The restaurant version topped it with pieces of [[flatbread]] and three vegetarian "meatballs" made by shaping plant matter into balls.<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> The recipe could use [[ronto wrapper]]s as the flatbread and [[protein]]-packed patties called [[Parwan Nutricake]]s in place of the plant-based meatballs. The spread was suitable for [[Felucian]]s, [[Artiodac]]s,<ref name="Cookbook" /> and [[human]]s to consume.<ref name="Docking Bay 7" />

==History==
{{Quote|While the Felucian Kefta & Hummus Garden Spread and Ithorian Garden Loaf lack meat, they aren't lacking in flavor.|Eloc Throno, ''Traveler's Guide to Batuu''|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
[[File:Felucian_Garden_Spread_Cookbook.jpg|thumb|left|250px|''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook'' depicts the dish prepared according to Strono Tuggs' recipe.]]
Before the Artiodac [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] worked for [[Maz Kanata]],<ref name="Cookbook" /> who ran a [[Cantina|tavern]] within her [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]],<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> Tuggs spent [[time]] at a [[farm]] on the [[jungle]] planet [[Felucia]]. He learned the Felucian [[Farmer|farming]] and harvesting methods as well as the importance of using fresh and local ingredients in all of his food. Inspired by the Felucian people and their teachings, he created the Felucian Garden Spread dish and named it after them.<ref name="Cookbook" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GE date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> after Kanata's castle was [[Battle of Takodana|destroyed]], Tuggs served the dish at his mobile restaurant, [[Tuggs' Grub]], when it was stationed at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]].<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> In that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Cookbook date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> he also published his recipes for the dish and its components in his cookbook ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''.<ref name="Cookbook" /> Also in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> the [[author]] and explorer [[Eloc Throno]] mentioned the spread by the name Felucian Kefta & Hummus Garden Spread in his guide book ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', noting that it was flavorful despite lacking meat.<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>  Around the same time, Tuggs included his cookbook's Felucian Garden Spread recipe in a small pamphlet of recipes meant to help a Batuu visitor learn more about the planet.<ref name="Treasures">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Treasures from Batuu]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
===''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge===
[[File:DB7_menu.jpg|thumb|right|200px|A Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo menu from summer 2019]]
Felucian Garden Spread is found in the [[In-universe|real world]] quick-service restaurant Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened at [[Disneyland]] Park on [[May 31]] and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] on [[August 29]], [[2019]].<ref name="Foodie Guide">{{DPB|url=2019/05/foodie-guide-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Foodie Guide to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> It was officially announced to the public on [[February 27]] of that year via articles on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="From Blue Milk to Ronto Wraps">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galaxys-edge-food-beverages|text=From Blue Milk to Ronto Wraps: The Food and Beverages of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> and [[Wikipedia:D23 (Disney)|D23: The Official Disney Fan Club]].<ref name="D23 Everything You Need to Know About SWGE">{{D23|url=galaxys-edge-announcements|text=Everything You Need to Know About ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref>

The dish was inspired by the plant-covered planet Felucia,<ref name="From Blue Milk to Ronto Wraps" /> which was described by Brian Piasecki, culinary director for concept development at [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World|Walt Disney World Resort]], as a "completely green planet" suitable to vegetarian or plant-based diets. According to Piasecki, the kefta plant-based meatballs are made with an [[Wikipedia:Impossible Foods|Impossible Foods]] vegan product that has a meat-like taste.<ref name="Food & Wine">{{WebCite|url=https://www.foodandwine.com/travel/disney-star-wars-galaxys-edge-menu|text=How Disney's Chefs Created the Out-of-This-World Menus for Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|author=Wang, Andy|date=2019-04-11|work=[[Wikipedia:Food & Wine|Food & Wine]]|archivedate=20200813174013}}</ref>

Felucian Garden Spread was initially only served during lunch hours,<ref name="Foodie Guide" /> but it has since become available at both lunch and dinner and has been renamed to "Felucian Kefta & Hummus Garden Spread." The menu's description reads, "Impossible&trade; [[Wikipedia:Kofta|Meatballs]], [[Wikipedia:Herb#Culinary|Herb]] [[Wikipedia:Hummus|Hummus]], [[Wikipedia:Tomato|Tomato]]-[[Wikipedia:Cucumber|Cucumber]] [[Wikipedia:Relish|Relish]], [[Wikipedia:Pita|Pita]]."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch And Dinner Menu}}</ref>

The reference book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', written by [[Cole Horton]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> and published on [[July 21]], [[2020]],<ref name="Quarto">{{Quarto|url=books/9780760366745/Star-Wars-Galaxys-Edge-Travelers-Guide-to-Batuu.html|text=Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu}}</ref> mentioned Felucian Kefta & Hummus Garden Spread as a vegetarian meal at Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> Since this matches the renaming of the dish in the theme park locations,<ref name="Menu" /> it appears to be another name for the in-universe dish.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Adaptations==
[[File:Felucian_Garden_Spread_Sims_4.png|thumb|left|200px|A party-sized portion of Felucian Garden Spread from ''The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu'']]
A recipe for the dish was included in the 2019 recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. It incorporates Ronto Wrappers and Parwan Nutricakes, which also have recipes included in the book, but they can be substituted with pita bread and [[Wikipedia:Falafel|falafel]], respectively. The ingredients for the hummus are [[Wikipedia:Black turtle bean|black beans]], [[Wikipedia:Tahini|tahini]], minced [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic]], [[Wikipedia:Olive oil|olive oil]], chopped [[Wikipedia:Parsley|parsley]], [[Wikipedia:Lemon#Juice|lemon juice]], [[Wikipedia:Oregano|oregano]], [[Wikipedia:Cumin|cumin]], [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|pepper]], and [[Wikipedia:Cilantro|cilantro]]. The salad is made with diced tomato and cucumber, chopped fresh [[Wikipedia:Spearmint|mint]], olive oil, salt, and pepper.<ref name="Cookbook" /> Sumerak considered the spread one of his favorites from the recipe collection, recommending that it be consumed with Parwan Nutricakes.<ref name="ForceNet">{{WebCite|url=https://forcecast.net/story/home/The_ForceCast_Author_Series_With_Chelsea_MonroeCassel_And_Marc_Sumerak_185657.asp|text=The ForceCast Author Series With Chelsea Monroe-Cassel And Marc Sumerak|author=Steve|date=2019-11-15|format=[[Wikipedia:Podcast|Podcast]]|work=[https://forcecast.net/ ForceCast.net]|archivedate=20240327213404}}</ref> 

The 2020 [[Canon|non-canon]] video game expansion ''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]'' includes Felucian Garden Spreads as a meal sold to the player's [[Wikipedia:The Sims|Sims]] for eight [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] per serving at the Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo vendor in the Black Spire Outpost neighborhood. Upon eating the meal for the first time, the Sim automatically learns the recipe and can prepare it with stoves in their home neighborhood.<ref name="Sims">''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]''</ref>
