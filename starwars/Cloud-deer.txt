'''Cloud-deer''' were a type of [[deer]] found on the [[planet]] [[Rei'izu]] that had [[Color|white]]-flecked purple-gray [[Hair|fur]] and branching horns. Hundreds of cloud-deer lived on the grounds of a [[Unidentified shrine|shrine]] in the [[pilgrims' ward]] of the [[city]] [[Yojou]] on Rei'izu. While a group including the Rei'izu [[Homeworld|native]] [[Ekiya]], the [[bounty hunter]] [[Chie]], and the former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] [[Mission to Rei'izu|traveled]] through Yojou, many cloud-deer watched them and crossed their path. Later, when the Ronin and Chie encountered a group of citizens trying to shoo away a flock of cloud-deer that were intruding on a grocer's, they slowed with the intention of assisting the citizens, though ultimately did not do so.

==Biology and appearance==
Cloud-deer were a type of [[deer]] with long [[Limb|legs]] and necks, variegated [[Color|purple-gray]] [[Hair|fur]] with white flecks, [[ear]]s, and wet black [[eye]]s. The deer had horns that branched in filigree patterns over their heads.<ref name="Ronin" />

==History==
Cloud-deer were found on the [[planet]] [[Rei'izu]], with hundreds of them living on the grounds of a [[Unidentified shrine|shrine]] in the northern stretch of the [[city]] [[Yojou]]'s [[pilgrims' ward]] that was one of the oldest and largest on the planet. At the end of the [[Sith rebellion]] against the [[Jedi clan]]s, a [[Unidentified witch|Sith witch]] caused Rei'izu to vanish, leaving empty [[Realspace|space]] where it had been. The planet's people became barely present remnants known as [[Shade (Rei'izu)|shades]], but the cloud-deer and other creatures living on Rei'izu were unaffected.<ref name="Ronin" />

[[File:Ronin2-PeachMomoko.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Ronin (pictured) was inclined to help citizens shoo away cloud-deer.]]
Twenty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Sith]] rebellion, the Rei'izu [[Homeworld|native]] [[Ekiya]] [[Mission to Rei'izu|returned]] to the planet as part of a group that was [[Hunt for the Sith witch|searching]] for the [[witch]]. While traveling through Yojou toward the pilgrims' ward, the group saw a cloud-deer standing in the ward and staring at them from across the [[Moga River]]. The group stared back, and Ekiya mentally noted the deer's horn patters were beautiful. Another cloud-deer trotted up behind the first, twitching its ears with curiosity, and Ekiya recalled the deer that lived at the shrine. As the party continued passing farther through the ward toward [[Shinsui Temple]], more and more cloud-deer crossed their path. Eventually, only a frosted park separated them from the chasm around the temple, and pairs of cloud-deer watched the group advance from between barren [[fruit]] [[tree]]s.<ref name="Ronin" />

As a result of the group's actions, Rei'izu was returned and its people were restored. Later, while two members of the party&mdash;the [[bounty hunter]] [[Chie]] and the former Sith known as [[the Ronin]]&mdash;traveled down a street in the pilgims' ward, they encountered a small flock of rogue cloud-deer attempting to intrude on a grocer's and a group of citizens trying to shoo them away. The Ronin and Chie slowed to offer assistance, but the Ronin's [[astromech droid]]<ref name="Ronin" /> [[R5-D56]]<ref name="Payback">{{Visions|The Duel: Payback}}</ref> urged them to continue on. The two ultimately did not help shoo away the cloud-deer, as Ekiya arrived in a [[speeder]] to drive them the rest of the way to their destination.<ref name="Ronin" />
