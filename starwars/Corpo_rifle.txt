'''Corpo rifles''' were a type of long-range [[blaster rifle]] produced by [[BlasTech Industries]] but branded for the [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation]], who supplied them to their [[Corporate Tactical Forces]] as a form of heavier firepower. [[Tac-Corpo]]s used the weapons during special operations including a mission to the [[planet]] [[Ferrix]] in [[5 BBY]].<ref name="CGttSWG" /> Corpo rifles physically resembled a carbine variant of the [[SE-14C blaster pistol]].<ref name="Episode 3">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref>
