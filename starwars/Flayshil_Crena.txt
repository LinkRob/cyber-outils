'''Flayshil Crena''' was a renowned [[philosopher]] who surmised that there existed a finite amount of luck in [[the galaxy]], and that it should be rationed.<ref name="Most Wanted" /> In [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Most Wanted]]'' to [[13 BBY]].</ref> after experiencing some luck, the young [[Rodian]] [[Tsuulo]] contemplated Crena's words and explained the philosopher's teachings to the [[human]]s [[Han Solo|Han]] and [[Qi'ra]], who were disinterested. Tsuulo later recounted another of Crena's sayings, when he told Han and Qi'ra that Creana had also said that which you seek hardest to find remains hidden the longest, before explaining he had no idea what it meant.<ref name="Most Wanted" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flayshil Crena was mentioned in the [[canon]] young adult novel ''[[Most Wanted]]'', which was written by [[Rae Carson]] and published in [[2018]] alongside the film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Most Wanted" />
