{{Quote|The crown of Alderaan is not merely inherited. It must be earned. The heir must prove herself worthy in body, heart, and mind. Are you prepared to do so?|Queen Breha Organa, to her daughter, during her Day of Demand|<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan" />}}
The '''crown of Alderaan''' was the official [[crown]] of the [[Alderaanian]] [[Alderaanian monarchy|monarch]]. It was normally kept in a [[museum]], but was occasionally displayed in the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan|royal palace's]] [[throne]] room on special occasions, such as the [[Prince|heir's]] [[Day of Demand]].<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan" />

In [[27 BBY]],<ref name="QS Date">The main story of ''[[Queen's Shadow]]'' takes place 4 [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Invasion of Naboo]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places in [[32 BBY]].</ref> when [[Alderaan]]'s queen [[Breha Organa]] greeted [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and her entourage in the palace throne room she had the crown woven into the [[braid]] that was twisted up into the top of her head.<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref>

In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] birth in [[19 BBY]]. The events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' commence just after Organa's sixteenth [[birthday]], which places the events of the novel in [[3 BBY]].</ref> the crown was brought back from the museum to the palace throne room for [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia's]] [[Leia Organa's Day of Demand|Day of Demand]]. The crown was placed upon a [[marble]] pillar and illuminated by its own [[candledroid]].<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan" />
