'''Fori's ship''' was a [[Starship|ship]] that belonged to the [[Twi'lek]] [[Fori Nagor]]<ref name="Closed Fist" /> by around [[381 BBY]]. Around that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="DantooineDate">"[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" establishes that [[Marda Ro]] visited [[Dantooine]] around a [[Standard year|year]] after taking possession of the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', an event that ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]]. Therefore, Ro's trip to Dantooine must have occurred around [[381 BBY]]. As [[Fori Nagor]] already owned the [[starship]] at the time of their meeting, she must have had it in her posession by that year.</ref> Nagor visited the [[Unidentified settlement (Dantooine)|oldest settlement]] on the [[planet]] [[Dantooine]], where she met [[Marda Ro]]&mdash;the [[Evereni]]<ref name="Closed Fist" /> founder of a group of [[pirate]]s who would eventually become the [[Nihil]].<ref name="GrattonInterview">{{YouTube|video=llnvoEG0CjE|text=Interview with Tessa Gratton: "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" (TALES OF LIGHT & LIFE)|channel=@FriendsOfTheForce|name=[[Friends of the Force]]|date=2023-10-06}}</ref> She<ref name="Temptation">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> asked Nagor whether she had a ship and offered Nagor a position in her group once Nagor mentioned the ship. Nagor then worked alongside the [[human]] [[Milan Stor]], and they used the ship to search for opportunities for [[Thief|theft]] and [[Smuggler|smuggling]].<ref name="Closed Fist" /> At some point no earlier than [[379 BBY]],<ref name="FaerDate">The segment of "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" depicting a [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi's]] [[death]] onboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' takes place around [[379 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:ClosedFistJediDeathDate|here]]. [[Marda Ro]] met the [[Faers]] after the [[Jedi]]'s death; therefore, she must have met them no earlier than 379 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the vessel was among the three ships Ro sent to intercept the Evereni [[Faers|siblings]] [[Vika Faer|Vika]] and [[Velya Faer]]'s [[Vika and Velya Faer's ship|ship]] in order to send the two Faers an invitation to [[Marda Ro's hunt|meet]] her.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

Later, Ro mentioned the ship in a [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]] she recorded for her [[Ro family|descendants]].<ref name="Closed Fist" /> By [[252 BBY]],<ref name="ShallaDate">"The Lie" chapter in [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]], which includes [[Shalla Ro]]'s [[death]], features [[Asgar Ro]]'s ascension to [[Eye of the Nihil]], an event dated to [[252 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'' establishes she introduced [[Marchion Ro]] to [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]], he must have listened to it by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> during his youth, Ro's [[Gender|great-great-grandson]] [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to her recordings, including the messages where the ship was mentioned.<ref name="Temptation" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ship was mentioned in the short story "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]," written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Closed Fist" />
