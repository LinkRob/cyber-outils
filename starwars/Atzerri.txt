'''Atzerri''' was a [[Gotal]] colony world<ref name="Tarkin" /> in the [[Atzerri system]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]], located in grid square M-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> along the [[Shipwrights' Trace]].<ref name="Shipwrights" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> tens of millions of Gotal [[refugee]]s fled to Atzerri when<ref name="Tarkin" /> their [[homeworld]]<ref name="Gotal">{{Databank|gotal|Gotal}}</ref> of [[Antar 4]] fell to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] shortly after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], the first battle of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Tarkin" />
