The '''Death Star trooper officer corps''' was an [[officer corps]] consisting of [[Death Star Trooper]] [[officer]]s. Members of the [[corps]] had been given additional lengthy training in leadership and administration along with the standard combat and technical training given to non-officer Death Star Troopers. Members of the officer corps were usually in [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[squad]]s or sections aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]], and oversaw operations and made sure standards were kept.<ref name="Dawn" />
