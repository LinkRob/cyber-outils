'''Derrek Raythe''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Moff]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]]. He [[Commanding officer|commanded]] the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Dauntless (Imperial-class)|''Dauntless'']] when it was [[Hangar|docked]] at [[Fondor Station]] above the [[planet]] to extract an [[Climate disruption array|experimental weather satellite]] before being [[Attack on Fondor|attacked]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].

Raythe eventually traveled to [[Vardos]], where [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]] [[Skirmish on Vardos|deployed]] the arrays.

==Biography==
===Stationed at Fondor===
[[File:Fondor_shipyard_attack.png|thumb|left|250px|Fondor is attacked by the Rebel Alliance.]]
A [[human]] [[Moff]] of the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Battlefront II" /> by [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> after the [[Battle of Endor]] which saw the [[death]]s of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]],<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> Derrek Raythe was stationed at the [[Fondor Station]] in [[orbit]] over the [[planet]] of [[Fondor]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

At the [[time]], [[Operation: Cinder]] was instigated by [[Sentinel (Contingency)|sentinel]] [[droid]]s which delivered the posthumous [[Emperor]]'s final Imperial prerogative to selected individuals, including [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]]. Within the plan brought to the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[officer]] was approaching Moff Raythe for [[Climate disruption array|experimental satellites]] needed for the operation's fruition. Raythe's purpose was to retrieve the [[satellite]]s at the [[space station]] for the plan's further advancement.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

This was interrupted, however, by an [[Attack on Fondor|assault]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] which forced him to [[Commanding officer|take charge]] of the [[Realspace|space]] battle aboard his [[Hangar|docked]] [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Dauntless (Imperial-class)|''Dauntless'']], where he gave out commands to the [[Imperial pilot]] of [[Inferno Squad]], [[Commander]] [[Iden Versio]], [[Parent|daughter]] of the Security Bureau admiral, where the [[pilot]] was informed of the cruciality of the plan.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

===Above Vardos===
Raythe would later travel to [[Vardos]] with Admiral Versio, who ordered the satellites to be [[Skirmish on Vardos|deployed]], unleashing the true cruel nature of the [[Superweapon|weapons]] upon the Versios' own [[homeworld]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />
