'''Core ships''', also known as '''Command Spheres''', were modified [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class battleship]] central spheres that were used by the [[Trade Federation]] for [[planet]]ary landings during the [[Clone Wars]] and often hosted [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] leaders. Equipped with both land and [[Atmosphere|air]] defenses, those autonomous spheres were 696 [[meter]]s in diameter and could carry numerous [[B1-series battle droid]]s.

==Characteristics==
[[File:Lucrehulk-class_Core_Ships.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Core ships on Geonosis in the Trade Federation landing zones prior to the Battle of Geonosis.]]
Core ships were huge<ref name="TCVD" /> autonomous<ref name="DS Incredibuilds" /> [[Trade Federation]] [[Command ship|command vessels]] modified from<ref name="TCVD" /> the central spheres of<ref name="DS Incredibuilds" /> [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleships]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> Core ships could detach and re-attach to the battleship ring.<ref name="AoTC" /> A core [[Starship|ship's]] central diameter came to 696 [[meter]]s, or 2,283 [[Foot|feet]], and the vessel possessed landing [[Limb|legs]] which retracted into the [[hull]] during travel in [[Realspace|space]]. Towards the top of the ship was a [[Conning tower|command tower]] and a [[sensor]] [[antenna]].<ref name="TCVD" />

Core ships could carry vast numbers of [[B1-series battle droid]]s.<ref name="TCVD" /> A single core ship could have enough resources to conquer a planet.<ref name="Jedi: Survivor" /> The ships had [[Atmosphere|air]] and land defenses. The Federation would use core ships for [[planet]]ary landings during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="DS Incredibuilds" /> and the craft were often used to host leaders of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="TCVD" /> Circular [[repulsorlift]] pits were used as service areas for core ships.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|We must get the starships back into space!|Rune Haako, during the Battle of Geonosis|<ref name="AoTC" />}}
<div style="float:right">[[File:FriendsLikeThese_FC_BG_art.png|thumb|200px|The wreckage of core ships on [[Xorrn]]]]</div>
Several core ships were present on [[Geonosis]]<ref name="AoTC" /> within the [[Trade Federation landing zones]]<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> prior to and during the [[First Battle of Geonosis]].<ref name="AoTC" /> [[Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel]]s, a [[Hoersch-Kessel Drive, Inc.|Hoersch-Kessel Drive Inc.]] [[supply ship]] used by the Confederacy, were built from salvaged core ships.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> [[Skytop Station]] was a core ship converted to serve as a [[listening post]]. It was later destroyed by a Republic [[Raid on Skytop Station|infiltration]].<ref name="Duel of the Droids Episode">{{TCW|Duel of the Droids}}</ref> In [[19 BBY]], a [[Unidentified core ship|modified core ship]] served as the venue for a meeting between the [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] and [[General]] [[Grievous]] on [[Utapau]], shortly before Grievous' [[Battle of Utapau|final duel]] with [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="RotS">{{Film|III}}</ref>

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">''Tarkin'' states that "Five [[standard year]]s have passed since [[Darth Sidious]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|proclaimed]] himself [[galactic Emperor]]," an event shown in [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']]. Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''Revenge of the Sith'' to [[19 BBY]], it can be deduced that the events of ''Tarkin'' occur in [[14 BBY]].</ref> the fifth [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Imperial Era]], [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] and [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] led an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[mission to Murkhana]]. While Tarkin was looking through a [[viewport]] on the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', it was impossible to tell the core ship repulsorlift pits from [[bomb]] craters.<ref name="Tarkin" /> The core ship of the ''[[Primacy]]'' abandoned on the planet [[Koboh]] was used as a base by the [[Bedlam Raiders]], a group of marauders who used the [[battle droid]]s present on the ship to increase their power.<ref name="Survivor">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@IGN|name=IGN|video=i05L-FaDhko|text=Star Wars Jedi: Survivor &ndash; 9 Minutes of Gameplay &#124; IGN First|date=2023-02-07}}</ref><ref name="Jedi: Survivor" /> Another [[Unidentified Separatist core ship|Separatist core ship]] was left crashed on a large [[Kiza's world|asteroid]] outside of the [[Red Honeycomb Zone]] and later became home to the former [[Acolytes of the Beyond|Acolyte of the Beyond]] [[Kiza]].<ref name="SotS" /> In [[21 ABY]],<ref name="21 ABY date">The events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' take place in [[21 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the ship's forge overloaded during her [[Duel on Kiza's world|duel]] with [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]], melting down much of [[Kiza's droid army]] and leading to her own death. The exterior of the core ship, however, remained standing and was left abandoned once more.<ref name="SotS" />
