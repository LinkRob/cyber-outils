'''Captain Third Class''' was a rank within the [[Mon Calamari Guard]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Ghal Tarpfen]] held the rank before she was abruptly dismissed from the Guard by her former lover [[Shenrick]].<ref name="Shadows Remain" /> Around [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]'' to [[230 BBY]]. "[[Starlight: Shadows Remain]]" is set between ''Mission to Disaster'' and ''The Fallen Star'', meaning it also occurs around that year.</ref> [[Standard year|years]] after she had departed [[Mon Cala]], Tarpfen had a [[dream]] of the end of her [[time]] as a [[Captain]] Third Class while serving as the [[Head of Security]] on the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]].<ref name="Shadows Remain" />
