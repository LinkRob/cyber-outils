'''Felucian ferns''' were a type of [[Fern (plant)|fern]]that could be found on<ref name="MA" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. The fern shared its name with<ref name="MA" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> [[Felucia]]. Felucian ferns had [[Color|blue-green]] fronds with a mild scent.<ref name="MA" /> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] visited the [[Jedi temple arboretum|arboretum]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on Coruscant and found some of the [[Plant|ferns]] within the gardens. The Felucian ferns that Jinn stroked had healthy fronds that sprung back from the touch of his [[Limb|hand]], and after he used [[the Force]] to reach out to the plant, he felt that it emanated peace.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Felucian ferns appeared in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
