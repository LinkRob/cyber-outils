'''Cuhoon'''<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> was a [[Gender|male]] [[Fluggrian]] [[youngling]] who was in [[Tabor Hospital]] on [[Protobranch]]. He was unable to walk, and had to lie on a diagnostic [[hovercot]]. When Protobranch was hit by a [[Protobranch solar storm|solar storm]] that sent its [[Antigrav platform|floating]] [[City|cities]] crashing to the ground, Cuhoon and several other younglings were rescued by [[Doctor]] [[Pira]], with the help of the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]s [[Dooku]] and [[Sifo-Dyas]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
