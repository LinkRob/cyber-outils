'''Eskil Abon Santhe''' was the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[governor]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Garel]]. Santhe was widely known to accept bribes from several criminal organizations, including the [[Black Sun]] and the [[Broken Horn Syndicate]], allowing them to operate on Garel with impunity. This criminal element attracted [[bounty hunter]]s, [[smuggler]]s, and other criminals.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eskil Abon Santhe was first mentioned in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[canon]] roleplaying [[2018]] sourcebook that is based on ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' and ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. The book was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] and was authored by [[John Dunn]], [[Tom Sorenson]], [[Sterling Hershey]], [[Jonathan Julius]], [[Keith Kappel|Keith Ryan Kappel]], [[Tim Huckelberry]], [[Jason Marker]], and [[James M. Spahn]].<ref name="Dawn" />
