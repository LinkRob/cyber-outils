'''Ammon IV''' was a [[Temperature|hot]], [[Desert|barren]] [[planet]]<ref name="TLTC" /> located in the [[Ammon system]] in the [[Ciutric sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> 

== Description ==
Ammon IV was a hot, [[desert]] [[planet]] with little [[water]] that required the use of [[Moisture vaporator|moisture vaporators]] to extract. There were large rock outcroppings that spring from the otherwise relatively flat surface. The planet was close to its sun, as the sun loomed extremely large in the sky.<ref name="TLTC" />

== History ==
Around [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Date">According to ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', in the [[Standard year|year]] 7973 [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]], [[Ketsu Onyo]] resigned from the [[Black Sun]]. It also dates the [[Standard year|year]] that the [[carbon-freezing]] of [[Han Solo]] to 7980 C.R.C., which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]]. Therefore, Onyo's resign must have been around [[4 BBY]]. Since Onyo still works for the Black Sun during "[[Too Late to Change]]", the comic must occur by around 4 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. Additionally, Onyo refers to the events of "[[Blood Sisters]]" during "Too Late to Change," which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to 4 BBY, so it can be concluded that this comic took place around 4 BBY.</ref> the former [[Black Sun]] hitman [[Jarret]] lived there on a [[Jarret's farm|farm]] with his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Loris]] and their [[Jarret's son|son]].<ref name="TLTC" /> The criminal [[Kruno]] and his [[Kruno's gang|gang]] were trying to drive him off his land before they were killed by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Ketsu Onyo]] who had come to the planet to apprehend Jarret. After Loris and Jarret's son convinced her that Jarret was a changed man, she faked his [[death]] to allow him to live out his live in peace.<ref name="TLTC" />
