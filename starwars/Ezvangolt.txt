'''Ezvangolt''' was a [[Grindalid]] who lived on [[Corellia]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> Ezvangolt was a member of the [[Garavult Clan]]. When [[Alys Ongwa|Crash]] and her [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection|team]] where crossing through the tunnels of Coronet City, Ezvangolt, along with [[Vanizma]], [[Zazima]] and [[Proxima]], let them pass through the tunnels.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
