The '''Forces of Cularin trilogy''' was the first trilogy of adventures for the third year of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. All three adventures were written by [[Matthew Domville]] (with [[Jae Walker]] editing) and the third was co-written by Jae Walker.<ref name="FC">{{LivingForce|Forces of Cularin: Force Concession}}</ref>
