'''Baobab EH8 Industrial Airbrush''',<ref name="Name">{{ArtStation|subdomain=gfraguasb|url=projects/1xZmPq|name=[[Gonzalo Bringas]]|text=Star Wars: Path of the Jedi - Concept Art - Props|date=2024-02-01|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/j1dTf|archivefile=File:GonzaloBringas-PotJPropConcept-ArtStation.png}}</ref> also known as a '''Pattern Tool''', was a type of [[airbrush]] used during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="PotJ" />

{{Tech-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Pattern Tool first appeared in the [[Wikipedia:Downloadable content|downloadable content]] ''[[Minecraft Star Wars: Path of the Jedi]]'' within the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Minecraft]]'',<ref name="PotJ" /> which was developed by [[Spark Universe]] and published by [[Mojang Studios]] on [[November 7]], [[2023]], to the game's community marketplace.<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/minecraft-star-wars-path-of-the-jedi|text=''Minecraft Star Wars: Path of the Jedi'' DLC Coming November 7}}</ref> In the game, the airbrush functioned as a way for players to select their astromech droid's plate pattern.<ref name="PotJ" /> A technical identification was given for the device as "Baobab EH8 Industrial Airbrush" through concept art by [[Gonzalo Bringas]], though the capitalization is unclear.<ref name="Name" />
