The '''100th Clone Company''' was a [[company]] of [[clone trooper]]s that served the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Its [[trooper]]s wore [[Color|white]] [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] with black markings and made use of [[DC-15A blaster rifle]]s with a unique coloration.<ref name="Every Move" />
