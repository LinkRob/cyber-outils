'''Elo''' was a [[Accountant|bookkeeper]] and member of the [[Pyke Syndicate]] crime syndicate during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> he worked on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]], where he handled the [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]] transactions between Akiva and the [[Pyke]] [[homeworld]] [[Oba Diah]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> Elo was entrusted with one of at least three [[keycard]]s used to open the [[Pyke Syndicate vault (Myrra)|Pyke's vault]] in the [[Pyke Syndicate District (Myrra)|Pyke Syndicate District]] of the [[city]] [[Myrra]] on Akiva. During that time, he kept his keycard at the [[Pyke Signal Station]] in the [[Stormhollow]] region of Akiva.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
The [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] learned of Elo's keycard and its approximate location from a [[Computer|data terminal]] near the sealed vault<ref name="Outlaws" /> in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> She later stole Elo's keycard from the signal station and used it along with the two other keycards to deactivate the vault's [[ray shield]] and steal 5000 credits and the materials necessary to apply an "Imperial" paint scheme to an [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Elo was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, he is mentioned as part of the "Pyke Syndicate Vault" intel quest.<ref name="Outlaws" />
