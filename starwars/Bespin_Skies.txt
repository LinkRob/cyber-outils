The '''Bespin Skies''' was a costume owned by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sprocket]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Prodigal Programmer|standard costume]]. This [[Clothing|costume]] consisted of a [[Color|gray]], blue, and orange, uniform, as well as a gray and blue [[helmet]], brown [[belt]], and a blue [[Sprocket's datapad|datapad]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bespin Skies was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Sprocket, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Bespin Chair]]. Unlike most Costumes in the game, Bespin Skies did not share the same rarity as its cosmetic set counterpart, with Bespin Chair instead being an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap."<ref name="Hunters" /> It was initially planned to be called "Azure Skies."<ref name="Hunters Panel">{{SW|url=news/swca-2022-star-wars-hunters-panel|text=SWCA 2022: 7 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: Hunters'' Panel}}</ref>
