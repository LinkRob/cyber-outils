Around [[27 BBY]],<ref name="Date1" /> the [[Chiss]] [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," severely beat the [[Vagaari pirates]] during his last '''confrontation with the Vagaari''', ending his<ref name="GG" /> [[Vagaari pirate operations]].<ref name="CR" /> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' in [[18 BBY]]..</ref> the Chiss [[Mid Captain]] [[Ufsa'mak'ro]] "Samakro" read the records of the confrontation and noticed that they were incomplete, speculating that the documents had been deliberately edited.<ref name="GG" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the confrontation with the [[Vagaari]] was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'', the second volume of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|''Ascendancy Trilogy'']].<ref name="GG" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Battle for Outbound Flight|Devastation]]<ref name="SQ">''[[Survivor's Quest]]''</ref> was first mentioned in Zahn's [[1991]] novel ''[[Heir to the Empire]]'', the first installment of Zahn's [[Star Wars: The Thrawn Trilogy|''Thrawn Trilogy'']].<ref name="HTTE">''[[Heir to the Empire]]''</ref> It was identified as the Devastation in the author's [[2004]] novel ''[[Survivor's Quest]]''.<ref name="SQ" /> The Devastation appeared in Zahn's [[2006]] novel [[Outbound Flight (novel)|''Outbound Flight'']].<ref name="OBF">[[Outbound Flight (novel)|''Outbound Flight'']]</ref>
