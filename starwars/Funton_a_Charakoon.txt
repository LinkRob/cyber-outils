The '''''Funton a Charakoon''''', or the '''Festival of the Thousand Suns''', was a [[Winter|midwinter]] celebration observed by the [[Hutt]]s, during which all [[Kajidic|clan]] rivalries and blood feuds were set aside for one night, and revelry lasted until the dawn of the next [[Standard day|day]].<ref name="LDT" />
