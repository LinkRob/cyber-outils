"'''Father's Day'''" is a [[Canon|non-canon]] animated [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short released on [[June 17]], [[2023]] on the LEGO YouTube and [[StarWarsKids.com]] channels. It is part of the ''[[LEGO STAR WARS: Celebrate the Season]]'' series.<ref name="SWKids" />

==Official description==
''A gift from Luke causes Vader to envision lost bonding opportunities with his son.''<ref name="Father's Day" />

==Plot summary==
[[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] is shuffling his chair in his [[Emperor's Throne Room|throne room]] aboard the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] stands beside him. [[Luke Skywalker]] exits the [[turbolift]] and brings a gift wrapped inside a red box. Palpatine uses [[the Force]] to hurl away the box and laughs menacingly. 

As he motions, Darth Vader descends down the stairs and ignites [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|his red lightsaber]]. Skywalker ignites [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber/Legends|his green lightsaber]] in response. The two engage in a lightsaber duel. Vader gains the upper hand with his mastery of the Force and hurls Skywalker aside. He then levitates and proceeds to [[Force choke]] his son. Skywalker chokes but manages to use the Force to summon the red gift package, and removes it cover with the Force. 

Skywalker shows his father a mug in the shape of the [[Death Star]] with the message "#1 DAD." Vader is touched by his son's gift and releases Skywalker. Skywalker experiences a flashback of him and his father assembling [[C-3PO]] at the [[Lars homestead/Legends|Lars homestead]]. Vader then gives Skywalker a lightsaber lesson with a [[Marksman-H training remote]], which fires laser bolts at Skywalker. Vader also gives Skywalker a driving lesson in the [[Lars family]]'s [[X-34 landspeeder]]. They accidentally topple a [[moisture vaporator]], which angers Uncle [[Owen Lars]]. The two escape. 

Back in the present, Vader extends a hand to his son and the two reconcile. However, an enraged Palpatine attacks Skywalker with [[Force lightning]]. Unwilling to lose his son, Vader grabs Palpatine and throws him down the Death Star's shaft. Skywalker hugs Vader. Father and son then share a carton of [[blue milk]].
