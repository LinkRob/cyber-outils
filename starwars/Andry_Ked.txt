'''Andry Ked''' was a Alderaanian human [[soldier]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Ked fought and was wounded during the [[Battle of Hoth]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="FACPOV" />

==Biography==
By the [[Battle of Hoth]], Andry Ked was in his mid-forties and was therefore considered a "senior citizen" among the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[infantry]]men. His weapon's [[scope]] was extremely misaligned. During the [[Battle of Hoth]], Ked took orders from [[Sergeant]] [[Trey Callum]] and fought alongside [[Private]] [[Emon Kref]]. As the troops began to engage approaching [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtroopers]], orders came in to retreat. Ked and the others fell back to the [[South Ridge]], but on the way there, Ked was shot in the back and wounded. As Kref came to help him, an [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] "[[Blizzard 1]]" approached them, but was taken out by a [[T-47 airspeeder]], flown by [[Derek Klivian]], that crashed into the walker and destroyed it. Ked was then assisted by Kref to a transport, where a [[medical droid]] assessed that Ked would make a full recovery.<ref name="FACPOV" />
