The '''Chardis system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Trilon sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square H-16 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the [[planet]] [[Chardis]].<ref name="Origin" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Chardis system was first mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].
