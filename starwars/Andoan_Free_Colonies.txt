The '''Andoan Free Colonies''' were a collection of worlds within the [[Lambda sector]]. Most of the worlds remained loyal to the [[Galactic Republic]] when the rest of the [[sector]] defected to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Andoan Free Colonies were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''Star Wars'' roleplaying products.<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" /> They were first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity's [[HoloNet News (original website)|HoloNet News]] article ''Andoan Free Colonies Criticize Aqualish'' published within [[HoloNet News Vol. 531 49|''HoloNet News Vol. 531'' 49]] in [[2002]].<ref name="HNN">{{HoloNetNewsWeb|49|regional/13328_1.html}}</ref>
