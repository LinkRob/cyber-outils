The '''Deysum system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]],<ref name="The Slice" /> within the [[Trax sector]]. Situated in grid square Q-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], the Deysum system was connected via<ref name="TFABG" /> the [[Trax Tube]]<ref name="Trax" /> [[hyperspace route]] to<ref name="TFABG" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="TLF">''[[The Living Force]]''</ref> [[Randon]] and the [[Uogo'cor system]].<ref name="TFABG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Deysum system was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] boxed set for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> The system was later identified as the "Deysum system" in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]],"<ref name="Appendix" /> an appendix accompanying the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]], an article on [[StarWars.com]] featuring a map of the galaxy.<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]] and posted on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Fry">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map...|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Deysum system/Legends|system]] was introduced in "[[Changing the Balance]]," a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure authored by [[Bill Smith]] and [[Ed Stark]], and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Kit]]'' by [[West End Games]] in [[1991]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Changing">{{WEGCite|story=Changing the Balance|book=Star Wars Gamemaster Kit}}</ref> It was first identified as the Deysum system in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
