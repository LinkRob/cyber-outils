The '''104th Battalion''' was a mobile armored unit in the [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Grand Army of the Republic]] that was led by [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Plo Koon/Legends|Plo Koon]] and [[Clone Commander/Legends|Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe/Legends|Wolffe]], during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]]. It included the [[Wolfpack/Legends|Wolfpack]], a squad named after their commander.

==Vehicles==
The 104th Battalion utilized [[All Terrain Recon Transport/Legends|AT-RT]], [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer/Legends|AT-TE]] [[Walker/Legends|walkers]], [[TX-130 Saber-class fighter tank/Legends|TX-130 ''Saber''-class fighter tanks]], [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut/Legends|HAVw A6 Juggernauts]] and [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry/Legends|LAAT/i gunships]].
