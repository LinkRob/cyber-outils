'''Avedot Cakes''' were a type of [[Diet|vegetarian]] [[cake]] created by the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] after he visited the [[Moon of Avedot]] in the [[Colonies]] during a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> Inspired by the [[moon]]'s glowing [[Color|orange]] [[Atmosphere|skies]], Tuggs baked the small [[Foodstuff|cakes]] in his [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|food freighter]] after his brief visit, using orange root [[vegetable]]s he had previously picked while on the [[planet]] [[Hynestia Prime]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

The moist brown cakes were equal parts spicy and sweet, with white frosting on top and a green garnish. They were served in a brown casing and were brightly colored when bitten into. Tuggs included the recipe for the cakes in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Avedot Cakes were created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the cake makes use of [[Wikipedia:Flour|all-purpose flour]], [[Wikipedia:Brown sugar|brown sugar]], [[Wikipedia:White sugar|granulated sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Baking powder|baking powder]], [[Wikipedia:Sodium bicarbonate|baking soda]], [[Wikipedia:Cinnamon|cinnamon]], ground [[Wikipedia:Ginger|ginger]], [[Wikipedia:Nutmeg|nutmeg]], [[Wikipedia:Kosher salt|kosher salt]], large [[Wikipedia:Eggs as food|eggs]], [[Wikipedia:Vanilla extract|vanilla extract]], and grated [[Wikipedia:Carrot|carrots]]. For the frosting it uses [[Wikipedia:Cream cheese|cream cheese]], softened, unsalted [[Wikipedia:Butter|butter]], [[Wikipedia:Powdered sugar|confectioners' sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Maple syrup|maple syrup]], more vanilla extract, more cinnamon, more kosher salt, [[Wikipedia:Whipped cream|heavy whipping cream]], chopped [[Wikipedia:Pecan|pecans]], and [[Wikipedia:Chives|chives]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
