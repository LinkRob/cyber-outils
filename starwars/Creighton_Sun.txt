'''Creighton Sun''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Jedi Master]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. A virtuous and traditional [[Jedi]], Sun had a reputation of being unsociable.

==Biography==
[[Reproduction|Born]] by [[422 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Creighton Sun’s first mission for the [[Jedi Order]] involved a tumultuous peace negotiation between two warring [[Twi'lek]] clans on [[Ryloth]].<ref name="Convergence" /> A later mission in Sun’s early Jedi career had him and [[Porter Engle]] fly a [[shuttle]] to [[Daedus]] which was shot down by pirates. He managed to crash-land the ship and fought the marauders who had downed them.<ref name="Eye">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]''</ref>

In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> he along with fellow master [[Char-Ryl-Roy]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]] and [[Padawan]] [[Enya Keen]] traveled about the ''[[Valiant]]'' on a relief mission to the [[planet]] [[Eiram]], which was at [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|war]] with [[E'ronoh]]. After helping to negotiate peace between the two planets,<ref name="Convergence" /> Sun traveled to the sacred [[moon]] [[Jedha]] with Jedi Knight [[Aida Forte]] to help oversee a [[Summit on Jedha|summit]] where the the planets were to sign a [[Eiram–E'ronoh peace treaty|treaty]]. However the summit failed and erupted into the [[Battle of Jedha]], with Sun leading the [[Guardians of the Whills]] and the Jedi in the battle. Sun called in Jedi reinforcements and single-handedly convinced the [[Eirami]] and [[E'roni]] troops to stop fighting.<ref name="Battle of Jedha">''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]''</ref> 

After the battle, Sun traveled to the planet [[Dalna]] with Forte to investigate the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[religion|cult]], which was believed to have had a hand in inciting the Battle of Jedha. Arriving in the [[Dalnan sector]], the two Jedi met up with Jedi Master [[Yaddle]] and [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Cippa Tarko]]. They landed on Dalna and walked to the [[Path compound]], where they stole Path robes in order to discreetly investigate. They split up, with Sun and Forte heading into the underground tunnels, witnessing a [[Unidentified Teevan man|Teevan man]] being accused of using the Force. Sun and Forte experienced debilitating fear and hallucinations and then saw the man crumble into dust. They also found a chamber with a stockpile of [[klytobacter]]. The two Jedi were discovered by Path guards and were forced to defend themselves.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

The fighting escalated as the Path attacked Sun, Forte, Yaddle, and many other Jedi on Dalna, erupting into the [[Night of Sorrow]], a horrific and devastating battle. In the battle, Sun encountered [[Shrikarai|creatures]] that preyed upon [[Force-sensitive]]s, causing many to be weakened and experience awful hallucinations, and turning many Jedi to dust. Sun was wounded while saving an old woman from an oncoming speeder, and he witnessed Forte receiving a mortal wound.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

After the battle concluded, Sun woke up on a medical frigate and learned of Forte's death. In the [[Imperial Palace|Coruscant Jedi Temple]], he and [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] decided to keep information about the creatures out of the [[Jedi Archives|Jedi Archive]] so that they could not be used against the Order. Sun took Enya Keen as his Padawan after her master, Char-Ryl-Roy, perished in the Night of Sorrow.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

In the Jedi Temple, Sun and Master [[Rinn (Duros)|Rinn]] listened to a report from [[Arkoff]] about the death of Arkoff's master, [[Ravna Abronsa|Ravna]]. After Arkoff left, Sun asked Rinn about the rumors of monsters on Dalna. Rinn replied that they were just rumors, and Sun agreed.<ref name="Precedent">''[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance: Precedent]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Creighton Sun was a human male with dark-brown eyes<ref name="Convergence" /> and dark hair that eventually began to become gray. Sun was a virtuous Jedi Master. He was fairly traditional, which earned him a reputation of being unsociable and it took some effort to get to know him. Though when he opened up, he had quite the sense of humor.<ref name="Creighton Sun" /> He was often cynical and saw the worst in things though he tried not to.<ref name="Battle of Jedha" />

==Equipment==
Sun carried a [[Color|blue]]-bladed [[Creighton Sun's lightsaber|crossguard lightsaber]]<ref name="Convergence" /> and used a [[comlink]].<ref name="Cataclysm" />
