The '''Bevin system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Rolion sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square Q-6 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Bevin Twelve]].<ref name="Origin" />
