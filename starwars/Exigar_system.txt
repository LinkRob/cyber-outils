The '''Exigar system''' was located in the [[Weneen sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] in [[the galaxy]]. The [[Star system|system]] was located in grid square N-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Exigar system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published on the [[StarWars.com]] website on [[June 24]], [[2025]],<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> as part of the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]].<ref name="Galaxy">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Exigar]] is a planet first mentioned in the [[2011]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''.<ref name="TOR">''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''</ref>
