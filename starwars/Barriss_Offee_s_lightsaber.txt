'''Barriss Offee's lightsaber''' was the personal [[Color|blue]]-bladed [[lightsaber]] carried by [[Barriss Offee]] during the [[Clone Wars]].

==Description==
[[File:BarrissOffeeTCW-SWE.png|thumb|left|250px|Barriss Offee wielding her lightsaber]]
[[Barriss Offee]]'s [[lightsaber]]<ref name="Mini book">''[[Star Wars: The Mini Book of Lightsabers]]''</ref> consisted of a metallic hilt with a cap on the pommel end and a ringed emitter from which the [[Color|blue]] [[plasma]] blade would emerge once the weapon was ignited. It also had a number of prominent dials and buttons on it that allowed Offee to control the blade's activation and intensity but without interfering with her combat grip.<ref name="TLC">''[[Star Wars: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> After Offee began to give into the [[dark side of the Force]], she would instead find herself fighting with [[Asajj Ventress's lightsabers|Asajj Ventress's red lightsabers]], deciding that they suited her.<ref name="Wrong Jedi">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref>

==History==
Offee used her lightsaber during the [[First Battle of Geonosis]].<ref name="Episode II" /> While she tried to stay away from the [[Clone Wars]] for a [[time]], Offee found herself returning to the conflict with the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]],<ref name="A Jedi's Duty">{{StoryCite|story=A Jedi's Duty|book=Stories of Jedi and Sith}}</ref> during which she wielded her lightsaber.<ref name="Weapons Factory">{{TCW|Weapons Factory}}</ref> She also wielded her lightsaber in the subsequent [[Skirmish aboard TB-73|skirmish aboard ''TB-73'']], where she killed [[clone trooper]] [[Trap]] by igniting it through his stomach to prevent him from infecting her with a [[brain worm]].<ref name="BI">{{TCW|Brain Invaders}}</ref>

[[File:BarrissVsAnakin-TWJ.png|thumb|300px|right|Skywalker used Offee's own lightsaber to push her into revealing her betrayal.]]
Offee continued to carry her blue lightsaber until her fall to the dark side of the Force in [[19 BBY]], where she bombed the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] and framed her close friend [[Ahsoka Tano]] for the deed. When Tano's master, [[Anakin Skywalker]], deduced her involvement, he went to Offee's quarters in the Temple and confronted her, "casually" picking up her lightsaber and examining it whilst asking questions before igniting it and lunging at her with her own weapon. This caused Barriss to instinctively summon [[Asajj Ventress's lightsabers]] to her hand to defend herself, exposing herself as the traitor. Skywalker then proceeded to duel her using her saber [[Jar'Kai|alongside]] [[Skywalker lightsaber|his own]] throughout the Temple, the fight carrying on outside to a ledge above the training grounds where Offee knocked Skywalker down towards the ground before leaping down to attack him, knocking her former Jedi lightsaber from his grasp.<ref name="Wrong Jedi" />
