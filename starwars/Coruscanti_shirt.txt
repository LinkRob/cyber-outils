'''Coruscanti shirt''' was a type of [[Clothing|shirt]] worn on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="What a Festive Evening">{{Andor|What a Festive Evening}}</ref>

In [[3 BBY]]<ref name="GhormanDate">"[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]" dates the events of "[[I Have Friends Everywhere]]" to [[3 BBY]].</ref> [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] wore a coruscanti shirt while posing as a [[Coruscanti]] [[fashion designer]] named Varian Skye on a trip to [[Ghorman]].<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" /> After his mission, he told [[Bix Caleen]] about his disguise, including the coruscanti shirt while in [[Axis network safehouse|their safehouse]] on Coruscant.<ref name="What a Festive Evening" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[Diego Luna]] wore a Coruscanti shirt as part of his Varian Skye costume in "[[I Have Friends Everywhere]]," the fifth episode of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" /> on [[April 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref> The shirt was identified as a coruscanti shirt in the following episode, "[[What a Festive Evening]]"<ref name="What a Festive Evening" /> which was released the same day.<ref name="Trailer News" />
