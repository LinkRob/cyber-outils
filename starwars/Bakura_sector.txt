The '''Bakura sector''' was a [[sector]]<ref name="Flight Log" /> on the outer edges of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> that contained the [[Cattamascar system|Cattamascar]], [[Gymelo system|Gymelo]], [[Timora system|Timora]] and [[Cermau system]]s.<ref name="Appendix" /> While the [[Bakura Trace]]<ref name="Bakura" /> [[hyperspace route]] passed through the sector, linking [[Cermau]] with [[Bakura]],<ref name="Map" /> the [[Bakura system]] itself lay in [[Wild Space]] and was not considered part of the sector.<ref name="Appendix" /> 

{{Sec-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Bakura sector was first mentioned in several [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] literature, including [[Karen Traviss]]'s [[2006]] novel ''[[Republic Commando: Triple Zero]]'' and [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|''The Essential Atlas Online Companion'']]. It later appeared in the non-[[canon]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Resistance Rises]]'' episode "[[Poe to the Rescue]]," which first debuted on [[February 15]], [[2016]].

When asked about the [[Bakura system]]'s exclusion from the Bakura sector, author [[Jason Fry]] stated that its absence was intentional, as Wild Space does not have sectors and [[Bakura]] likely chose to remain independent of the official sector.<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-09-03.png|author=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-09-03}}</ref>

===Non-canon content===
[[C-3PO]] informed [[Poe Dameron]] that the [[Resistance]] [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]] was being detained aboard a [[First Order]] [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] in that sector. Using a stolen [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport]], the Resistance managed to [[Rescue of Admiral Ackbar|rescue]] Ackbar from captivity.<ref name="Poe to the Rescue">{{ResistanceRises|Poe to the Rescue}}</ref>

In ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'', it is mistakenly called the "Pakora sector."<ref name="LEGO TFA">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref>
