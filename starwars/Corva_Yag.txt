'''Corva Yag''' was an astronomical object<ref name="THRA 6" /> situated in the [[Corva Yag system]] in the [[Rseik sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], Corva Yag was part of the [[Galactic Frontier]], a region considered to be remote by the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Chronicles" /> When the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Deployment of the stormseeds|established]] their [[Occlusion Zone]] territory in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Corva Yag and the rest of the Rseik sector became located behind its [[Stormwall]] barrier.<ref name="THRA 1">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 1]] &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Rseik sector]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
