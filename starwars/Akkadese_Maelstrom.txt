The '''Akkadese Maelstrom''', also known simply as the '''Maelstrom''', was a cluster of matter in the [[Kessel sector]] containing interstellar gas, [[carbonberg]]s, ice chunks, and other large debris that surrounded the [[planet]] [[Kessel]]. Also within the Maelstrom was the [[Maw Cluster]], as well as massive creatures known as [[summa-verminoth]]. The [[Kessel Run]] was a charted route through the Maelstrom, which was in close proximity to the planet [[Oba Diah]], the base of operations for the [[Pyke Syndicate]].<ref name="TOG" />

==Behind the scenes==
''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' screenwriter [[Jon Kasdan]] has stated that the idea of the Akkadese Maelstrom was present since the film's first script draft. In Kasdan's words, the film's crew wanted a [[Wikipedia:Jules Verne|Jules Verne]] flavor and that they loved the idea of what might look like a storm in space. [[Industrial Light & Magic]] did tests in which they blew up canisters of different colored powder and photographed the explosion in slow-motion.<ref name="Jon's Twitter">{{Twitter|JonKasdan|status/1040529505670946817|[[Jon Kasdan]]|quote=continued|archivefile=File:Jon-Kasdan-SoloFacts-September-14-2018.png}}</ref>
