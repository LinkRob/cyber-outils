The '''ARC-8 starfighter''' was a model of eight-winged [[starfighter]] from the [[High Republic Era]]. [[Standard century|Centuries]] later, an [[Fraxa's ARC-8 starfighter|ARC-8]] was owned by [[Fraxa]], executive of the [[Kwikhaul Livery Corporation]] on the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]], who attempted to restore the antique. [[Jedi Master]] [[Saesee Tiin]] flew the starfighter during the [[Jedi]]'s [[Defense of Kwenn|defense]] of the planet.<ref name="The Living Force" />
