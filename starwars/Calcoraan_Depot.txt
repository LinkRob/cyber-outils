'''Calcoraan Depot''' was a storage facility in [[orbit]] of the [[planet]] [[Calcoraan]]. It belonged to the [[thorilide]] mogul [[Denetrius Vidian]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[noble]]. In that depot, Vidian maintained a large stock of [[Baradium-357]], with which he planned to blow up the [[moon]] [[Cynda]] so he could harvest its thorilide-rich core. However, [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Hera Syndulla]] [[Gorse Conflict|infiltrated]] Vidian's private room in the Depot, eventually uncovering most of the Imperial's plans.<ref name="A New Dawn" />
