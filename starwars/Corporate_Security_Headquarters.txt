The '''Corporate Security Headquarters''' were the headquarters of [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]] and its [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces|Security]] branch on the [[planet]] [[Morlana One]].<ref name="Andor 1" />

==Description==
===Composition===
The Corporate Security Headquarters was a multi-building complex on the [[Morlana One]] seaside which functioned as the headquarters and command center of the [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces|Pre-Mor Enforcement]], the [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]] security branch. It was surrounded by a tall fence with observation towers and comprised of multiple [[glass]] rhombohedrons connected with each other via [[skybridge]]s. On the yard surrounding the towers were paths leading from one building to another and underground passages leading to deeper sublevels.<ref name="Andor 1" />

===Features===
[[File:PMASFcommandcenter-Kassa.png|thumb|left|290px|The Corporate Security Headquarters main command center]]
Inside the main building there was the [[Pre-Mor Enforcement Command center]], a room filled with [[computer]]s and [[Viewscreen|holoscreens]] used to monitor the entirety of the [[Free Trade sector|Preox-Morlana sector]]. A team of analysts worked there in shifts and were responsible for collecting the day's information to deliver it to superior [[officer]]s. They could also put out bulletins issued in the name of the Pre-Mor enforcement, requiring information about criminal activities from the citizens.<ref name="twbm">{{Andor|That Would Be Me}}</ref>

Pre-Mor security officers also maintained offices in the same building. These included the [[Chief Inspector's Office]] which was used for high level meetings and interviews as well as a number of offices for junior officers such as [[Deputy Inspector]]s.<ref name="Andor 1" />
