'''Corona Three''' was the [[callsign]] of the third member of [[Corona Squadron]].

==History==
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Smikes]] flew as Corona Three. Smikes lost his life [[Dogfight in the Hudalla system|in]] the [[Hudalla system]] and was not replaced until after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />
