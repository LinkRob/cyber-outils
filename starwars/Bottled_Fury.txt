"'''Bottled Fury'''" is a short story written by [[Jay Watamaniuk]] and released on the [https://www.swtor.com official site] for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' on [[November 17]], [[2022]].

Immediately following the Spirit of Vengence flashpoint, Shae Vizla - once ruthless bounty hunter, now Mandalore the Avenger, hunts fellow Mandalorian, Heta Kol, to seek justice for the attack of her ship... in this case with the aid of Bottled Fury! This story is concluded in the follow-up tale, 'Snare'; both delving into Mandalorian politics and an exploration of how Shae Vizla finally caught up with Heta Kol on Ruhnuk.
