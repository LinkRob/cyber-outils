'''Dandelions''' were a type of puffy [[plant]].<ref name="EFV" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> the [[Alcedian]] [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Kildo]] ruffled his feathers in shock upon being splashed with a [[Temperature|cold]] vial of [[Juvan|rejuve]] medicine, making his colleague&mdash;the [[Jedi]] [[youngling]] [[Gavi Takitaken|Gavi]]&mdash;think that the former looked like a [[Color|black]] dandelion.<ref name="EFV" /> The plant shared its name with "Dandelion Ewok" and "Dandelion AT-ET," a costume and [[weapon]] respectively, which were used by the [[Grand Arena]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Pilbush]]<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date2">{{HuntersDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dandelions were mentioned in [[The High Republic: Escape from Valo|''Escape from Valo'']], a [[2024]] junior novel written by [[Daniel José Older]] and [[Alyssa Wong]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="EFV" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref> In the real world, dandelions&mdash;also known by their scientific name, [[Wikipedia:Taraxacum|''Taraxacum'']]&mdash;are a type of flowering plant.<ref name="Real">{{WebCite|url=https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/dandelion-taraxacum-officinale|text=Dandelion, ''Taraxacum officinale''|author=Susan Mahr|archivedate=20230929171508}}</ref>
