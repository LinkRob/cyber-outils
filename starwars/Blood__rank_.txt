{{Quote|Being blood of the Irizi was very good for my career, and the family's done the same for many others.|Ar'alani|<ref name="Chaos Rising" />}}
The rank of '''blood''' was the highest family rank a member of a [[Chiss family]] could possess. [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] was [[Reproduction|born]] a full-blooded daughter of the [[Irizi family]], until her promotion to a [[flag officer]]. Members of the [[Chiss Defense Force]] promoted to flag rank or higher (starting at [[commodore]]) were stripped of their family status.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

With the rank of blood came esteem and privileges within [[Chiss]] families. In addition to being granted preferential treatment in family affairs,<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> being blood boosted a Chiss's career prospects as a civilian official of their family.<ref name="Greater Good" />
