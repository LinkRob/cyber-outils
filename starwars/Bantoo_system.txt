The '''Bantoo system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Samix sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square Q-19 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the planet [[Bantoo]].<ref name="EoD" />
