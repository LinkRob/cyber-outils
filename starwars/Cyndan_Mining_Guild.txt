The '''Cyndan Mining Guild''' was a [[mining]] company based on the [[moon]] [[Cynda]], which [[orbit]]ed the [[planet]] [[Gorse]]. During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], the [[Neimoidian]] [[Palfa]] served as the company's [[director]] before [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Count]] [[Denetrius Vidian]] [[Death|murdered]] him for opposing the Count's decision to impose an age cap on the company's workers.<ref name="AND" />
