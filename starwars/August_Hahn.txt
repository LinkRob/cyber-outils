'''August Hahn''' wrote a number of articles and adventures for the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'' campaign]] by [[Wizards of the Coast]]. He played [[Thurm Loogg]] at [[Gen Con Game Fair 2001]].

In 2002, he took over the campaign as its Main Co-ordinator and led Living Force until the campaign's conclusion in 2004.
