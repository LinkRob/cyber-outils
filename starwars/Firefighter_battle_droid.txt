'''Firefighter battle droids''',<ref name="Droids" /> also known as '''firefighting battle droids''',<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/destroy-malevolence-episode-guide|text="Destroy Malevolence" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> or '''B1 firefighter droids''',<ref name="R2-31">{{BuildR2Cite|31|Droid Directory}}</ref> were specialized [[B1-series battle droid]]s equipped with enhanced heat shielding<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> that were programmed and equipped to fight [[fire]]s. Notable for their distinctive paint and heavy [[backpack]]s,<ref name="Droids" /> which featured the numbers 72066 running down either side,<ref name="Destroy Malevolence" /> they carried [[fire hose]]s that sprayed [[fire-repellent foam]].<ref name="Droids" /> They also had small, built-in [[fire extinguisher]]s.<ref name="BMF 57" /> They could also be used for security.<ref name="Official Magazine 118">{{GermanFanCite|118|Database}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Firefighter battle droids were regular [[B1-series battle droid|B1]]s that were remodeled to be effective at fighting fires within [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] warships and installations.<ref name="Destroy Malevolence" /> They were developed by [[Baktoid Combat Automata]] for cost reasons, being easier to remodel B1 droids for firefighting rather than develop a specialized firefighter droid.<ref name="Official Magazine 118" /> They had [[Gender|masculine programming]], [[Color|black]] [[sensor]]s and dark gray plating.<ref name="Destroy Malevolence" /> Their plating was adorned with red highlights and reflective yellow stripes,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> which was fireproof,<ref name="Official Magazine 118" /> and made them more visible in smoky conditions. They did have combat training,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> albeit limited, they could spray [[fire-repellent foam]] on the ground so their opponents would slip.<ref name="Official Magazine 118" /> The firefighter battle droids received specialized programming that helped them respond to emergencies, while altered backpacks held their firefighting gear.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> The firefighter droids were equipped with a [[fire hose]]<ref name="Droids" /> and a [[fire extinguisher]].<ref name="BMF 57" /> Their hoses could emit a pressurized foam,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> and their backpacks had a supply of fire-resistant chemicals. As B1s were considered to not be particularly intelligent, they would sometimes be knocked over by the recoil of their own hose.<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />

==History==
During the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Destroy Malevolence" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|104}}</ref> several firefighter [[battle droid]]s served aboard the ''[[Malevolence]]'', a [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]] that served as the [[flagship]] of [[General]] [[Grievous]], where they attempted to extinguish the [[fire]]s caused by damage the [[Starship|ship]] had sustained in an earlier battle. They were also deployed in one of the [[hangar]]s when [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s captured [[Padmé Amidala's H-type Nubian yacht (Malevolence)|yacht]] self-destructed, though their attempts to put out the flaming wreck proved less than stellar.<ref name="Destroy Malevolence" /> Later, an entry on firefighter battle droids written by [[Captain]] [[Jikesh Valia]] was included in "[[Neyo's Almanac of Clankers]]," a survival compendium sent out by [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Neyo]] that listed notable battle droids.<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />

During or after [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Year">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]'' discusses [[F3-ZK]]'s adventure with [[BB-8]] as presented in [[BB-8 on the Run]], a book concurrent with [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'' in [[34 ABY]]. This means the book must have been published during or after 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> [[R0-GR]], the self-proclaimed last of the [[B1-series battle droid|B1 battle droids]], included the firefighter units in his book ''[[droidography]]''. In the book, R0-GR claimed the firefighters were brave and that it was a result of them being from the B1-series.<ref name="Droidography" />

==Behind the scenes==
Firefighter battle droids first appeared in the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]] [[The Clone Wars: Season One|season one]] episode, "[[Destroy Malevolence|Destroy ''Malevolence'']]," which aired on [[October 17]], [[2008]].<ref name="Destroy Malevolence Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode004.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Destroy ''Malevolence''}}</ref>

Multiple sources state that the droids were 1.91 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know" /><ref name="BMF 57" /> However, ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'' states that the droids were 1.93 meters,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> as does [[Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin 118|''Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin'' 118]].<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />

The firefighters droids' reflective paint stripes are meant to resemble a [[Wikipedia:Firefighter|firefighter's]] uniform.<ref name="Episode Guide" />
