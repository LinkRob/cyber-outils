A [[human]] [[Gender|male]] was the '''father''' of [[Din Djarin]]. When their home came under attack from [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] forces during the [[Clone Wars]], Djarin's [[Parent|father]] and [[Din Djarin's mother|mother]] attempted to hide their son from the enemy [[battle droid]]s. Djarin's parents were [[Death|killed]], and Djarin himself was found by a [[B2-series super battle droid]], but he was rescued by a [[Mandalorian]] [[Unidentified Mandalorian warrior|warrior]], who took him in as a [[foundling]].<ref name="Chapter 8" /> Djarin later adopted his own foundling, [[Din Grogu]], as his son.<ref name="Chapter 24">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 24: The Return}}</ref>
