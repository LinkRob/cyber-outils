'''Akira'''<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> was a village on [[Felucia]] that grew and harvested [[nysillin]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. [[Casiss Midagatis]] served as the village's elder. He hired four expensive [[bounty hunter]]s; [[Sugi (bounty hunter)|Sugi]], [[Embo]], [[Rumi Paramita]] and [[Seripas]] to [[Skirmish on Felucia|defend]] the village from the [[pirate]]s of the [[Ohnaka Gang]]. After their [[T-6 shuttle]] crashed on Felucia's surface, Midagatis convinced the [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Ahsoka Tano]] to join their cause. The Felucian villagers, alongside the Jedi and the bounty hunters worked togehter to drive Ohnaka's pirates off of Felucia.<ref name="BH" />

==Behind the scenes==
Akira first appeared in the seventeenth episode of [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|Season Two]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' titled "[[Bounty Hunters (episode)|Bounty Hunters]]", that first aired in early [[2010]]. It shares it's name with<ref name="BH" /> [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] director [[Wikipedia:Akira Kurosawa|Akira Kurosawa]] to which the episode is dedicated.<ref name="Bounty Hunters EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/bounty-hunters-episode-guide|text="Bounty Hunters" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>
