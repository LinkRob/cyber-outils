'''Fonik Yik''' was an individual that was aboard [[Achra Station]] in the [[Akiva system]] with their [[Kowakian monkey-lizard]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> During their time aboard their kowakian escaped its pen. Later station personal broadcast a message in [[Huttese]] for Yik using the station's speaker system to notify them of the kowakian's escape.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fonik Yik was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They were mentioned in an ambient announcement that players can overhear while on Achra Station.<ref name="Outlaws" />
