'''Doda Bodonawieedo''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Rodian]] musician. He played at [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]], but ended up getting fired and replaced with the band [[Figrin D'an and the Modal Nodes]], protesting as he left the establishment.<ref name="Not For Nothing">{{StoryCite|story=Not for Nothing|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> A few years later he was seen as a member of the [[Max Rebo Band]] at [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]].<ref name="Music" /> This was four [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]], where he and the other members of the band played [[Jedi Rocks]] for the Hutt, who ordered it played a second time but then dropped his [[Slavery|slave]] [[Oola]] into his [[rancor]] pit during the repeat performance when she refused his advances. Bodonawieedo and the band stopped playing as Oola was [[Death|eaten]] by the rancor, and then witnessed the arrival of [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] disguised as the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boushh]] with the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] as a prisoner.<ref name="ROTJ" />

Doda played a [[Unidentified horn|horn]]<ref name="ROTJ" /> similar to fellow [[Bith]] bandmate [[Barquin D'an]]'s [[kloo horn]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Doda Bodonawieedo first appeared in the [[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition|Special Edition]] re-release of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] in [[1997]]. He was first named in the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' ''[[Star Wars: The Power of the Force (1995 toy line)|Power of the Force]]'' set of [[Hasbro]]'s action figure collection which was released in [[1998]]. The name was confirmed to be [[canon]] by ''[[Music in the Star Wars Galaxy: The Modal Nodes vs. The Max Rebo Band]]'', a [[StarWars.com]] article written by [[Amy Ratcliffe]] and published in [[2015]].
