{{Quote|If you live long enough, you see the same eyes in different people. I'm looking at the eyes of a man who wants to run.|[[Maz Kanata]], to [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] deserter [[Finn]]|<ref name="Episode 7">{{Film|VII}}</ref>}}
[[File:SixEyes-SoloUSC.png|thumb|280px|[[Azumel]]s, such as [[Argus Panox]], possessed six eyes.]]
'''Eyes''' were the primary visual organ of many [[Sentience|sentient]] and animal [[species]].

==Description==
Most species possessed a single pair of eyes located on their head, although some species, such as [[Abyssin]], had only one eye,<ref name="A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref> while [[Gran]] had three, and others, like the [[Harch]] and the [[Dyplotid]], possessed several pairs.<ref name="Cat and Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref> Yet other species, like the [[Melitto]] from [[Li-Toran]], or [[Miraluka]] like [[Tenth Brother|Prosset Dibs]], had no eyes at all, relying on other organs to navigate.<ref name="AotG">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Human]]s had white sclera encircling irises that could be of a variety of [[color]]s, whereas an [[Ikkrukkian]] such as [[Porter Engle]] had black sclera around blue irises.<ref name="Blade 1">[[The High Republic — The Blade 1|''The High Republic — The Blade'' 1]]</ref>

The [[Chiss]] had red eyes which could see more [[infrared]] than humans,<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> while [[Kaminoan]]s had large black eyes that could see [[ultraviolet]].<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> [[Jawa]]s had sickly glowing yellow eyes that they used to peer through the dark folds of the [[Robe|cowls]] they [[Clothing|wore]].<ref name="Jawa DB">{{Databank|jawa|Jawa}}</ref> [[Rodian]]s had large, round pupil-less eyes.<ref name="Rodian DB">{{Databank|rodian|Rodian}}</ref> The droid equivalent to an eye was a [[photoreceptor]].<ref name="databank">{{Databank|wac-47|WAC-47}}</ref>

[[File:EyeExam-MIA.png|thumb|left|300px|[[Meebur Gascon]] looks into the eye of [[Gregor]], a human clone]]
All [[clone trooper]]s had brown irises<ref name="Spoils">{{TBB|Spoils of War}}</ref> as they originated from the same [[clone template]] of the human [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]], who had brown irises.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones">{{Film|II}}</ref> This also applied to the mutated [[Clone Force 99]], as their mutation did not affect the iris pigmentation.<ref name="Spoils" /> A human [[parent]] could pass on their eye color to their [[Reproduction|offspring]]&mdash;[[Ben Solo]] had the same piercing brown eyes as his mother [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who was sure that this feature was inherited from her own birth mother, [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="Family at War">''[[Skywalker: A Family at War]]''</ref>

[[File:Vader_yelloweyes.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Fueled by the dark side, Darth Vader's eyes reflected his hatred.]]
Eyes acted as windows to the [[soul]], and so reflected the emotions that the [[Sith]] harnessed. A [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] user's eyes would sometimes change color and burn yellow with a fiery red rim when rage and anger seethed inside them. [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s eyes changed color in this way as he [[Mission to Mustafar|massacred]] the [[Executive Separatist Council]] on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]]<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> radiating anger and hate.<ref name="Shadow of Vader">''[[Star Wars Adventures: Shadow of Vader's Castle]]''</ref> Nevertheless, Skywalker's eyes returned to their normal shade upon the arrival of his [[Marriage|wife]], Padmé Amidala.<ref name="VE" />

==History==
===Blindness and implants===
[[Cybernetics|Cybernetic]] replacements for damaged eyes did exist within the galaxy. [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]] lost his right eye at the hands of [[Asajj Ventress]] and replaced it with a cybernetic.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|clone-commander-wolffe|Clone Commander Wolffe}}</ref> However, other individuals in the galaxy were unable to secure or chose against receiving cybernetic replacements. [[Matese]] lost his right eye and waited for [[Veteran Services]] to cover the cost of a replacement, only to eventually look elsewhere and secure an implant through other means.<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>

In [[3 BBY]]<ref name="3Timelines">{{RebelsDate|221}}</ref> during the [[mission to Malachor]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Kanan Jarrus]]' eyes were slashed by [[Maul]] with his [[Maul's lightsaber|lightsaber]], rendering him blind.<ref name="Twilight">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref> He went onto cover his damaged eyes with a mask and learned to use [[the Force]] to make up for his blindness.<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref> As Jarrus sacrificed himself to [[Rescue of Hera Syndulla (Lothal)|save]] his lover [[Hera Syndulla]] and found family members [[Sabine Wren]] and [[Ezra Bridger]]<ref name="Jedi Night">{{Rebels|Jedi Night}}</ref> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="JediNightdate">{{RebelsDate|410}}</ref> his sight suddenly returned to him, permitting him to look onto his comrades one last time.<ref name="Jedi Night" />

===Eyes in the Skywalker family===
As a child, Leia Organa saw kindness in eyes of her adoptive father [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]]. Despite knowing she was adopted, she hoped she saw Bail's same kindness in her own eyes.<ref name="Leia">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>

When tutoring [[Luke Skywalker]] in the use of [[the Force]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] warned him that his eyes could deceive him and suggested not to trust them. To prove his point, he blindfolded Skywalker, after which Skywalker was successful in deflecting shots from a [[Marksman-H training remote|training remote]] with his [[Skywalker lightsaber|lightsaber]] by drawing upon the power of the Force.<ref name="A New Hope" />

When the redeemed Anakin Skywalker was mortally wounded after killing the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor]] to save his son, his last request was for Luke to remove his helmet so that he could look on his son with his own eyes instead of through the filters of his mask.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

===Eyes in culture===
[[File:RexPhase2close-SWE.png|thumb|left|300px|The helmet of Clone Captain Rex was adorned with Jaig Eyes.]]
[[Jaig Eyes]]&mdash;a name that was short for "''jai'galaara'la sur'haii'se''," itself a [[Mando'a]] phrase for "[[shriek-hawk]] eyes"&mdash;were a [[Mandalorian]] combat honor. [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] was permitted to adorn his helmet with Jaig Eyes<ref name="Updated and Expanded">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> after being bestowed with the honor by his instructors.<ref name="Rex Roll">{{InsiderCite|227|Rex & Roll}}</ref> [[Clone Commander]] [[Blackout]] also had Jaig Eyes on his helmet.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> After his blinding at the hands of Maul,<ref name="Twilight" /> Jarrus would choose to cover his eyes with a mask, which was itself adorned with Jaig Eyes.<ref name="Steps Into Shadow" />

Through her long life, [[Maz Kanata]] came to believe that she could find "the same eyes in different people."<ref name="Episode 7" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Episode7Date">{{FilmDate|VII}}</ref> she noticed [[Finn]]'s eyes and accurately described them as showing he wanted to run away.<ref name="Episode 7" />
