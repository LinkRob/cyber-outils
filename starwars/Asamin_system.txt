The '''Asamin system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Strabin sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]] that fell within grid square M-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Asamin system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Asamin system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[Alliance Intelligence Reports]]'', a [[1995]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="AIR">''[[Alliance Intelligence Reports]]''</ref>
