'''Bormus''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Starfighters" /> in the [[Bormus system]] in the [[Dustig sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was under the jurisdiction of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. [[Koensayr Manufacturing]] was in the process of conducting the first tests on the [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]] under the [[Y-wing project]] helmed by Republic [[Senator]] [[Ask Aak]] at a facility in orbit,<ref name="Starfighters" /> despite the Republic already having a number of BTL-B Y-wings in its arsenal before the outbreak of the war as power projection.<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref>

[[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] asked Koensayr for a [[squadron]] of the BTL-B [[bomber]]s, but because Senator Aak resented Skywalker for failing to stop the [[assassin]]ation of Aak's predecessor,<ref name="Starfighters" /> Senator [[Aks Moe]],<ref name="HC 1">{{HelmetCollectionCite|1|Databank A-Z}}</ref> the request was refused. The Y-wing project was considered essential for a successful military campaign by the [[Republic Military]], and [[General]] Skywalker decided to seize all the ships he required directly from the workshops in orbit, rather than denounce Senator Aak's decision to higher officials.<ref name="Starfighters" />
