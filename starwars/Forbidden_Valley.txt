The '''Forbidden Valley''' was a rocky valley located on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Pasaana]] not far from the [[Lurch Canyon]]. The [[Aki-Aki]] [[Festival of the Ancestors]] took place in the valley.<ref name="Episode IX" /> Thrill-seekers were drawn to the rugged terrain of the Forbidden Valley, while collectors of art and local crafts hunted for wares they could sell as exotic rarities offworld.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

After a [[mission to Pasaana]] in [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Pasaana Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that Landonis Balthazar Calrissian and [[Luke Skywalker]] [[Mission to Pasaana|traveled]] to [[Pasaana]] thirteen [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the mission took place in [[21 ABY]].</ref> [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] was taken with the humble nature of the native [[Aki-Aki]] people, and remained on the planet living as a [[hermit]] in the Forbidden Valley.<ref name="TROS VD" />
