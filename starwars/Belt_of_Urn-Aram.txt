The '''belt of Urn-Aram''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Quest">''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]''</ref> located at<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-31.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-31}}. Fry has stated that the intention behind locations noted on the [[The galaxy|galactic]] map included in ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' that share a name with previously established locations, such as the dot labeled "Amloch" with respect to the [[ocean moon of Amloch]], was that the latter are located at the former.</ref> the [[Urn-Aram system]] within the [[Wazta sector]], which was situated in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western Reaches" /> It was situated within grid square I-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> on the [[Koda Spur]] [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="KodaSpur" /> The belt had rainbow-[[color]]ed anomalies near it. By the [[time]] he was twelve,<ref name="Quest" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[prospector]] [[Dass Leffbruk]] had seen the anomalies, which he considered brilliant.<ref name="Quest" />
