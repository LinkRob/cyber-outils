The '''Battle of Crombach Nebula''' took place<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> sometime between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> in the [[Crombach Nebula]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] forces. During the battle, [[Jedi Master]] [[Wom-Nii Gnaden]] participated in the engagements and utilized a Republic [[NTB-630 naval bomber]] against the Separatist [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigate]] ''[[Tide of Progress XII]]''. However, Gnaden was [[Death|killed]] after his [[bomber]] collided with the Separatist [[Munificent-class star frigate|frigate]] and left a mark within the latter's starboard [[hull]] as a result.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Crombach Nebula was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']].<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Battle of Crombach Nebula/Legends|Battle of Crombach Nebula]] was introduced in ''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', a [[2005]] reference book authored by [[Curtis Saxton]].<ref name="Incredible Cross-Sections">''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
