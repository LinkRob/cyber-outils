'''Carnivore Battalion''' was a [[Republic Military|military]] [[infantry]] [[battalion]] within the [[501st Legion]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] that fought [[Clone Wars]], and was part of a group of three [[brigade]]s. During the [[Battle of Devaron]], the [[clone trooper]]s of Carnivore Battalion manned the [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]] [[Walker]] ''[[773]]'' and boarded a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigate]] under the command of [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]].<ref name="Cargo" />
