'''Crackling pudding''' was a cooked dessert made from [[crackling pod]]s and canned [[Blue milk|bantha milk]]. [[Mattis Banz]] made one for [[Temmin Wexley]]'s and [[Karé Kun]]'s [[Snap and Karé's wedding|wedding]], which he unfortunately flavored with flatulence-inducing [[anilam]] powder.<ref name="Escape from Vodran">''[[Join the Resistance: Escape from Vodran]]''</ref>
