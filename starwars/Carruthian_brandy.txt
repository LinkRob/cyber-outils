'''Carruthian brandy''' was a rare type of [[brandy]]<ref name="Resistance Reborn" /> originating from the [[Carruthia system]]<ref name="Resistance Reborn" /><ref name="Planetnamia" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> While residing in the [[capital]] [[city]] [[Myrra]] on the Outer Rim [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Resistance Reborn" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Resistance Reborn Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Resistance Reborn]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> retired [[pilot]]s [[Wedge Antilles]] and [[Norra Wexley]] kept a case of Carruthian brandy in their [[Wedge Antilles and Norra Wexley's house|home's]] study room. It was displayed in a shelf next to a framed box in which Antilles displayed the [[medal]]s he had earned for his [[Standard day|days]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]].<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />

==Behind the scenes==
