'''Ben Diskin''' is [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice artist]] who performed several characters in animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', including [[Krismo Sodi]],<ref name="Bounty" />, [[WAC-47]]<ref name="Secret Weapons">{{TCW|Secret Weapons}}</ref> and [[AZI-345211896246498721347|AZI-3]].<ref name="Conspiracy">{{TCW|Conspiracy}}</ref> He later worked reprised his role as AZI-3 in ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' animated series.<ref name="Aftermath">{{TCW|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref>
