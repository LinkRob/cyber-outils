A '''dwarf planet''' was a type of celestial body smaller than a [[planet]], but still a world in its own right. The arctic planet [[Ilum]] was said to be even smaller than a dwarf planet, yet was still called a planet despite being only 660 [[kilometer]]s in diameter.<ref name="Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Like actual planets, dwarf planets could have one or more [[moon]]s orbiting them.<ref name="Nexus of Power">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref>
