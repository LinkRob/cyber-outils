'''''Dawn of the Jedi: Force War'' 5''' is the fifth issue in ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: Force War]]'', the third story arc miniseries of the ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series by [[John Ostrander]] and [[Jan Duursema]]. ''Force War 5'' was published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] on [[March 19]], [[2014]]. It is the final issue not only in the ''Force War'' arc, but also for the ''Dawn of the Jedi'' storyline as a whole.<ref name="The Brilliance of Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi">{{SW|url=news/the-brilliance-of-star-wars-dawn-of-the-jedi|text=The Brilliance of ''Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi''}}</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
''The alien Rakata are on the brink of opening the Infinity Gate, ready to unleash their Empire on an unsuspecting galaxy! The Je'daii must win this battle&mdash;even if it means the end of their order. But how can they attain victory when their greatest hero has joined the enemy?''
