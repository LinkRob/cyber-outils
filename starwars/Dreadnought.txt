'''Dreadnoughts''' were massive, powerful [[capital ship]]s. Under the [[Anaxes War College System]], they were classified as ships with a length of 5,000 meters or larger.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> Dreadnoughts included the [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class]]<ref name="SepDreadnought">{{SWE|technology|separatistdreadnaught|Separatist dreadnought}}</ref> and the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class]] used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Ion Cannon">{{Databank|ion-cannon|Ion Cannon}}</ref> as well as the various ships of the [[Mandator line|''Mandator'' line]] used by the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Galactic Empire]], and later the [[First Order]].<ref name="E8 ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> The [[Bellator-class dreadnought|''Bellator-class'']] was another type of dreadnought used by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref>''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref> The [[Super Star Destroyer]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]], such as the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class]], were also referred to as [[Star Dreadnought]]s.<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref><ref name="UltimateSW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> The ''[[Technocrat]]'' was a dreadnought utilized by the [[Nihil]] criminal group.<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref>
