The '''Ahakista system''' was a [[star system]] which contained [[Ahakista]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Dug]] [[gambler]] [[Gronson Takkaro]] fell into debt on Ahakista but then skipped bail and fled the system to the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. The [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] chased Takkaro down to Lothal, where he had to explain about Takkaro's past in the Ahakista system.<ref name="EG" />
