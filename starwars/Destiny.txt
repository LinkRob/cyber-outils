'''Destiny''' or '''fate''' was said to be a predetermined course of events. It could be conceived as a predetermined or likely future, whether in general or of an individual. Although often used interchangeably, the words ''fate'' and ''destiny'' had distinct connotations.

==History==
{{Quote|Remember... a Jedi's strength flows from the Force. But beware...Anger, fear... aggression... the dark side are they. Once you start down the dark path... forever will it dominate your destiny. (...) Luke... do not... do not underestimate the powers of the emperor... or suffer your father's fate you will.|[[Yoda]]|<ref name="Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi">{{Film|VI}}</ref>}}
While stuck on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] questions [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] about trusting their fate to [[Anakin Skywalker]], a boy who they hardly knew. [[Watto]] bargained with Qui-Gon when betting on the [[Boonta Eve Classic (32 BBY)/Legends|Boonta Eve podrace]], suggesting they let fate decide whether to free either Anakin or his mother [[Shmi Skywalker Lars|Shmi]] from [[slavery]], only for Qui-Gon to use [[the Force]] on Watto's [[chance cube]]. When [[Senator]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] tried dissuading [[Queen]] Amidala from returning to [[Naboo]], the Queen said her fate will be no different than that of her people. Queen Amidala negotiated with [[Boss]] [[Rugor Nass]] into joining their two societies to help fight against the [[Trade Federation]], saying their fate is in his hands. Meanwhile, in discovering Anakin Skywalker, Qui-Gon tried convincing the [[Jedi Council]] to [[Jedi training|train]] Anakin as a [[Jedi]], only for the Council to consider young Skywalker's fate to be decided later due to the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] voting for a new [[Chancellor]] and Queen Amidala's ongoing confrontation on Naboo which would draw out the Queen's attacker, the [[Sith]] [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] known as [[Darth]] [[Maul]]. Despite the disapproval of the Council and his [[Padawan|Jedi apprentice]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], Qui-Gon was certain that Anakin's fate was uncertain.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace">{{Film|I}}</ref>

Ten years later, when forced into hiding during the Jedi's investigation of the [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|assassination attempts]] against her, Senator Padmé Amidala expressed to [[Padawan]] Anakin Skywalker how she hadn't worked for a year to defeat the Senate's [[Military Creation Act]] only to not be there when its fate is decide.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones">{{Film|II}}</ref>

[[File:Superconductingloop.png|thumb|left|250px|In a showdown between Mace Windu and Darth Sidious, Anakin Skywalker faces his destiny to turn to the dark side of the Force.]]
Near the end of the [[Clone Wars]] in [[19 BBY]], during the [[battle of Coruscant]], Chancellor Palpatine told [[Jedi Knight]] Anakin Skywalker that they should leave his master Obi-Wan Kenobi or they'll never make it out of [[General]] [[Grievous]]'s ship, the ''[[Invisible Hand]]'', only for Anakin to say Obi-Wan's fate would be the same as theirs. Later, after the [[death]] of Grievous, [[Mace Windu]] took a group of Jedi to [[Duel in Palpatine's office|arrest]] Palpatine, revealed to be the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] Darth Sidious, with Windu saying that the Senate would decide the [[Sith Lord]]'s fate. However, due to the intervention of Anakin Skywalker, Palpatine blasted Windu with [[Force lightning]] and sent him through the window—to his death. While Anakin was ashamed of his actions, Palpatine said Skywalker was fulfilling his destiny, with Sidious dubbing the fallen Jedi as his new Sith apprentice, Darth Vader.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith">{{Film|III}}</ref>

Five years following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], fate was still at work throughout the [[Great Jedi Purge]], which saw the remaining Jedi Knights being hunted down and destroyed by the Empire's [[Jedi hunter]]s and [[Inquisitorius]]. [[Cal Kestis]], a former Jedi Padawan in hiding, befriended a [[Scrapper Guild|scrapper]] named [[Prauf]], who was unaware of his friend's true nature but believed that Kestis deserved better than a life as a scrapper. After [[Escape from Bracca|escaping]] from the Inquisitors with newfound allies aboard the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]'', Cal dreamed of what Prauf told him about moving on with his life and finding his destiny before joining [[Cere Junda]]'s [[Secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order|mission to rebuild the Jedi Order]].<ref name="Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

Years later, following the death of [[Kanan Jarrus]], his Padawan [[Ezra Bridger]] entered the mystical realm connecting all of [[time]] and [[Realspace|space]], known as the [[World Between Worlds]], where he saved [[Ahsoka Tano]] from a duel with Darth Vader in the past by pulling her into the realm. Bridger then became adamant in also changing the fate of his now-deceased master, [[Kanan Jarrus]], only to be dissuaded after Tano reasoned with the Padawan that they would all be dead without Jarrus' selfless sacrifice.<ref name="World">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref>

Nineteen years after the end of the Clone Wars and the rise of the Empire, at a time when the Jedi were all but extinct, [[Han Solo]] claimed to have flown from one side of [[the galaxy]] to the other and never seen anything to make him believe in the Force as a mystical energy field controlling his destiny, saying it was a lot of simple tricks and nonsense. During the [[Rescue of Princess Leia|rescue]] of [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], Obi-Wan told [[Luke Skywalker]] to watch over the [[droid]]s [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]] and deliver them safely to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] or other [[star system]]s would suffer the same fate as [[Alderaan]]. Obi-Wan added that Luke's destiny lied along a different path from his, saying the Force would be with him, prior to his death in the Jedi Master's final duel with Darth Vader.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope" />

The [[Ezaraa]] [[species]], who controlled the [[Dominion of the Ezaraa]] government on two mere [[Ezaraa continents|continents]] of their [[homeworld]] [[Ezaraa (planet)|Ezaraa]],<ref name="Aphra 10">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 10|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 10]]</ref> dreamed of overthrowing the Empire to replace it with the Dominion,<ref name="Screaming Citadel">[[The Screaming Citadel 1|''The Screaming Citadel'' 1]]</ref> ultimately seeing control over the entire [[universe]] as their destiny. To that end, several Ezaraa wished to buy the [[Eternal Rur]] [[crystal]] from [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]], who believed the Ezaraa were fools for trying to expand across the stars when they currently ruled over so little.<ref name="Aphra 10" />

While Luke trained with [[Yoda]] on [[Dagobah]], the Jedi Master warns the young Jedi to beware the [[dark side of the Force]] or it would dominate his destiny and consume him like Darth Vader. Although Luke tried to leave Dagobah, due to having a vision of Han and Leia in danger, Yoda tried to convince Luke to stay and complete his training, with the [[Force spirit]] of Obi-Wan saying even Yoda cannot see their fate. In their [[Duel on Cloud City|duel]] on [[Cloud City]], Vader revealed to Luke that he was his father, claimed that his destiny lied with him, and tried to tempt him to join him to rule the galaxy together as father and son. Later, during the [[Millennium Falcon]]'s escape, Vader contacted Luke, telling him to come with him and that it is his destiny.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back">{{Film|V}}</ref>

About one year later, Luke Skywalker returned to Dagobah, where Yoda revealed Darth Vader's revelation as his father to be true, and then warned the young Jedi to not underestimate the powers of [[Emperor]] Palpatine or Luke would suffer his father's fate. Shortly afterwards, Obi-Wan told Luke he couldn't escape his destiny to face Darth Vader again, saying that if he chose not to kill his father, the Emperor had already won. While on the planet [[Endor]] and the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]], Luke was tempted by Vader and the Emperor to fulfill his destiny or be killed. Although he defeated Vader in a duel, Luke told the Emperor he would never turn to the dark side and that he was a Jedi like his father.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi" />

During the [[First Order-Resistance War]] over 30 years later, after toying with the young Jedi [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] over her association with Jedi Master Luke Skywalker and the [[Resistance]], [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] ordered his apprentice [[Ben Solo]], known as Kylo Ren, to fulfill his destiny by killing Rey as a means to complete his training. However, Ben killed Snoke and became the new Supreme Leader of the [[First Order]].<ref name="Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> After the revelation that Rey was Emperor Palpatine's granddaughter, Luke's Force spirit told Rey that confronting fear was the destiny of a Jedi, and that if she didn't face Palpatine on [[Exegol]] it will mean the end of the Jedi and the war would be lost.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|What these films deal with is the fact that we all have good and evil inside of us and that we can choose which way we want the balance to go. ''Star Wars'' was made up of many themes. It's not just a single theme. One is our relationship to machines, which are fearful but as — also benign and they're — they're an extension of the human, not mean in themselves. The — the issues of friendship and your obligation to your fellow man and to other people that are around you, that you have control over your destiny, that you — you have a destiny, that you have many paths to walk down and — and you may have a great destiny. If you decide not to walk down that path, your life might not be as satisfying as if you wake up and listen to your inner feelings and realize what it is that you have a particular talent for and what contributions you can make to society.|[[George Lucas]]|<ref name="BM">{{WebCite|url=https://billmoyers.com/content/mythology-of-star-wars-george-lucas|text=The Mythology of 'Star Wars' with George Lucas|date=1999-06-18|archivedate=20230321075932}}</ref>}}
According to [[George Lucas]], destiny is a central theme in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga.
