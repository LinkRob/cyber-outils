The '''E-10 blaster rifle''' was a model of [[blaster rifle]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]] used by [[Imperial Army trooper]]s and Imperial Stormtroopers of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was equipped with a [[Glowrod|glow rod]] for low-light vision.<ref name="Official Guide" />

==History==
[[File:Mudtrooper-SWBC51.png|thumb|left|200px|An Imperial Army Mudtrooper wields an E-10]]
The E-10 [[blaster rifle]] served as the forerunner of the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]].<ref name="E10-DB">{{Databank|e-10-blaster-rifle|E-10 Blaster Rifle}}</ref>

[[Imperial Army]] [[Imperial swamp trooper]]s were armed with E-10 [[blaster]] [[rifle]]s during their deployment on [[Mimban]]. The weapon proved itself unreliable during the subsequent [[Mimban Campaign|campaign]]. Many [[soldier]]s on Mimban were eager for the E-10 to be phased out, since the weapon had a habit of jamming at the worst possible moment.<ref name="Official Guide" /><ref name="E10-DB" /> The harsh environment of the [[planet]] also affected other [[E-22 reciprocating double-barreled blaster rifle|weapons]].<ref name="E22-DB">{{Databank|e-22-blaster-rifle|E-22 blaster rifle}}</ref>

The E-10 was later replaced by the E-11 blaster rifle.<ref name="Official Guide" />

==Variants==
On some worlds with harsh environments like [[Vandor]], [[range trooper]]s were equipped with a more robust variant of the E-10 called the [[E-10R blaster rifle]].<ref name="E10-DB" /><ref name="Official Guide" /> This variant remained in service long after the standard E-10 was phased out and continued to be used by range troopers on [[Banas]]<ref name="HS 2">[[Hyperspace Stories 2|''Hyperspace Stories'' 2]]</ref> as late as the [[Galactic Civil War]], between [[1 ABY]] and [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''Hyperspace Stories'' 2 takes place after [[Luke Skywalker]] is promoted to [[commander]]. [[Star Wars (2015) 55|''Star Wars'' (2015) 55]] establishes that he was promoted after the [[Battle of Mako-Ta]], which ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'' states took place twenty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, the assault must have taken place in [[1 ABY]]. ''Hyperspace Stories'' 2 also takes place before [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], which ''Timelines'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="5 BBY">{{AndorDate|106}}</ref> Imperial Army Troopers [[Aldhani Garrison|stationed]] on [[Aldhani]] and [[Mina-Rau]] in [[4 BBY]]<ref name="Harvest">{{Andor|Harvest}}</ref> used a variant of the E-10, which featured a scope,<ref name="Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref> as well as several attachments seen on E-11 blasters. The blasters, therefore, were effectively [[E-10.5 rifle]]s.<ref name="Eye Trivia 3">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e6-trivia-gallery|text="The Eye" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref>
