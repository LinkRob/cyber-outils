==Behind the scenes==
Devash Odiin appeared in the [[2021]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Castaways]]''. During one of the side stories, Devash Odiin tells the player to complete the Endor HoloSim, however, the actual objective given to the player is to complete the Echo Base HoloSim. Multiple descriptions for the side stories misspell Odiin's name as "Odin."<ref name="Castaways" />
