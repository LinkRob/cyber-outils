'''Dieroul''' was an individual. At one occasion, Dieroul was invited to a [[Vector Day]] gala organized at the [[Boulderdash]] by the [[Quenk jazz/Legends|Quenk jazz]] artist [[Mrp-Mrp Poo]]. According to [[Dyslogia Twang]], Dieroul was a "braggart".<ref name="MAGL">''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dieroul was mentioned in a two-page segment titled "[[Sightings by Twang]]," written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that collects concept designs and photographs from various [[George Lucas]] projects.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]].<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
