"'''Danger in the Desert'''"<ref name="Storybook Library" /> ([[Wikipedia:French language|French]]: "'''Danger Dans le Désert'''," [[Wikipedia:Spanish language|Spanish]]: "'''Peligro en el desierto'''") is a short story by [[Dan Brooks]] published in [[5-Minute Star Wars Stories (second edition)|''5-Minute Star Wars Stories'']] which adapts [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="5-Minute Star Wars Stories" />

==Plot summary==
{{Plot|story}}

==Continuity==
"Danger in the Desert" states that [[C-3PO]] spoke over six million forms of communication, however he actually spoke over seven million by this point.<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> It also states in the Spanish version that [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] sensed [[Chewbacca]]'s survival on [[Pasaana]], however [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], which this story partially adapts, established that she didn't learn this until the [[Mission to Kijimi]].<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> This was fixed in the English release.
