'''Doge''' was a title held by the leader of the [[Dug Council]] on the [[planet]] [[Malastare]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Dug]] [[Nakha Urus]] held this title. With the backing of the council, Urus' position allowed him to sign a treaty with the [[Galactic Republic]] regarding the Republic's use of Malastare's [[fuel]] supply.<ref name="ZB" />

==Behind the scenes==
The title of Doge first appeared in "[[The Zillo Beast]]," an episode of the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars television series'']].<ref name="ZB" />
