"'''Feat of Clay: Inside Kotobukiya'''" is an article appearing in [[Star Wars Insider 99|''Star Wars Insider'' 99]], with interviews and translations by [[Shingo Nakagawa]] and [[Mariko Kawamura]]. The piece details the creation of [[Kotobukiya]]'s newest line of clay models, the Bounty Hunter Series, including models for those [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunters]] seen in the film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]&mdash;[[4-LOM/Legends|4-LOM]], [[Zuckuss/Legends|Zuckuss]], [[Bossk/Legends|Bossk]], [[Dengar/Legends|Dengar]], [[IG-88B|IG-88]], and [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]].
