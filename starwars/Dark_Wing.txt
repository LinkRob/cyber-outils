'''Dark Wing''' were a group active in [[Borgo Prime|Port Borgo]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> a lawless [[asteroid]] [[spaceport]],<ref name="Borgo">{{Databank|starport-borgo|Starport Borgo}}</ref> during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. A [[Gender|woman]] tried to join the group there, but later told a sibling in the [[Rokana Raiders]] [[pirate]] group that it was basically impossible. The sibling then shared this information with a comrade in the Raiders while guarding the [[Khepi Tomb]] in the [[Khepi system]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Dark Wing was mentioned in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune|text=Steal a Look at ''Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune'', Available May 15}}</ref> The [[Shadow Wing|group]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[Pirates & Privateers]]'', a [[1997]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[Timothy S. O'Brien]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="P&P">''[[Pirates & Privateers]]''</ref>
