A '''spy''' operated as part of the [[droid]] [[5A-G3]]'s [[Eriadu droid intelligence squad]] during the [[Nihil conflict]]. They were stationed on the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]] while it was trapped behind the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders']] [[Stormwall]]&mdash;a barrier preventing [[hyperspace]] travel, effectively cutting off some worlds from the wider [[galaxy]].<ref name="DFZ 1" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|DFTOZ1}}</ref> the [[spy]] worked alongside several others in the [[squad]] to gather intelligence when the Nihil [[Invasion of Eriadu|invaded]] Eriadu.<ref name="DFZ 1" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> on Eriadu, the spy found an [[EX communication droid|EX droid]], which had been sent through the Stormwall by [[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]] in an attempt to contact [[Jedi]] behind the barrier. They gave the [[droid]] to 5A-G3, who then passed it on to [[Jedi Knight]] [[Qort]].<ref name="DFZ 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
The spy was first mentioned in the [[The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone 1|first issue]] of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone]]''. The comic was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Paris Alleyne]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]<ref name="DFZ 1" /> on [[October 9]], 2024.<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3016-086/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III--Dispatches-from-the-Occlusion-Zone-1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III--Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone #1}}</ref>
