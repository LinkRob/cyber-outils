'''Barchibar''' was a [[Weequay]] member of [[Alys Ongwa]]'s [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection|security crew]] on [[Corellia]]. When the crew was tasked with the protection of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] during a visit to Corellia, Barchibar accompanied [[Ovarto Bitolo-Bash]] in [[Ground Team Six]] among the crowd that was watching Soh.<ref name="THRA Annual 2021" /> During the [[Battle of the Corellian Shipyards]], Barchbar was killed by [[Nihil]] blaster fire as the defending forces regrouped near the third [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] on the [[Galactic Republic]] lot of the [[Santhe Shipyards]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref>
