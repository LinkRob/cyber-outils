"'''Bloodhawk Down'''" was a mini-adventure published at [[Wizards.com]] on [[October 10]], [[2002]] for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'', to display the new content of ''[[Arms & Equipment Guide]]''.

{{Game-stub}}

==Plot summary==
[[Min Erethen]] is a highly valued military [[Scientist/Legends|scientist]] for [[Arakyd Industries/Legends|Arakyd Industries]]. When her mobile base of operations, codenamed "Bloodhawk," crash-lands on an uncivilized planet, the players must rush to rescue her.
