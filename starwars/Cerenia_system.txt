The '''Cerenia system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Mbandamonte sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing" /> It was located in grid square N-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Cerenia system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Cerenia system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in "[[Operation: Pet Show]]," a ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' adventure written by [[Ed Stark]] and published by [[West End Games]] in the [[1993]] supplement ''[[Twin Stars of Kira]]''.<ref name="Twin">{{WEGCite|story=Operation: Pet Show|book=Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)}}</ref>
