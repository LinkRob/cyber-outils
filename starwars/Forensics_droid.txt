A '''forensics droid''' was a [[droid]] tasked with data collection and analysis. One [[SP-4 ISC crime scene analysis droid|SP-4 ISC]] [[analysis droid]], [[Russo-ISC]] worked as a forensics droid for the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="SabotageEpGallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/sabotage-episode-gallery|text="Sabotage" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 10}}</ref> Another SP-4 ISC droid, [[ZED-6-7]], acted as a forensics droid for the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2020) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 1]]</ref>
