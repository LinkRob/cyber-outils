The '''cattorobe''' was a type of [[music]]al wind instrument. It consisted of a cylindrical body with ribbing along much of its length and a tubular mouthpiece extending from the thinner end.<ref name="SWTVE" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], which includes the appearance of the cattorobe, to [[32 BBY]].</ref> a [[Bith]] [[musician]] playing as part of a band in the stands of the<ref name="TPM" /> [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]]<ref name="SWTVE" /> on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] used a cattorobe during the<ref name="TPM" /> [[Boonta Eve Classic]]<ref name="SWTVE" /> [[Podracing|podrace]].<ref name="TPM" />

==Behind the scenes==
A cattorobe first appeared as a prop in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. The prop was not named in the film,<ref name="TPM" /> but received identification in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'', which was written by [[Adam Bray]], [[Tricia Barr]] and [[Cole Horton]].<ref name="SWTVE" />
