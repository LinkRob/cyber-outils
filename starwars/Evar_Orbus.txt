'''Evar Orbus''' was an individual whose [[death]] was ironically caused by the [[musician]] [[Figrin D'an]] of the [[Figrin D'an and the Modal Nodes|Modal Nodes]], at some point prior to Figrin's younger [[Gender|brother]] [[Barquin D'an]] joining the [[Max Rebo Band]],<ref name="Lando">{{EncyclopediaCite|Lando Calrissian and Other Characters From the Galaxy}}</ref> which had occurred by [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Date" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Evar Orbus was mentioned in ''[[Lando Calrissian and Other Characters From the Galaxy]]'', a part of the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Enecyclopedia'']] series published by [[De Agostini]]<ref name="Lando" /> on [[July 22]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|lang=fr|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Official Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref> The [[Evar Orbus/Legends|character]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where Orbus was created for "[[And the Band Played On: The Band's Tale]]," a story written by [[John Gregory Betancourt]] and published in the [[1995]] anthology ''[[Tales from Jabba's Palace]]''.<ref name="Band">{{StoryCite|story=And the Band Played On: The Band's Tale|book=Tales from Jabba's Palace}}</ref>
