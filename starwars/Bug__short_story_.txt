"'''Bug'''" is a short story that was published in the anthology ''[[The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark]]'', based on the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Massacre]]." It was written by [[E. Anne Convery]], and published on [[August 25]], [[2020]] by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]. It is the only original story in the anthology and focuses on [[Bug (Sídi)|Bug]].
