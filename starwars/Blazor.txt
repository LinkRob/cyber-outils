During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Duke]] '''Blazor''' of the [[Black Sun]] criminal syndicate was owed a large sum of [[Currency|money]] by the [[Gatto Gang]] trio of criminals. The [[Gender|duke]] thus hired the [[bounty hunter]] [[IG-88B|IG-88]] to capture them, and the [[Hunting|hunter]] successfully did so after tracking down the gang on a [[Unidentified astronomical object (IG-88)|rocky astronomical object]].<ref name="IG-88 vs Gatto Gang" /> At some point between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The main story of "[[Tales from Wild Space: IG-88 vs. the Gatto Gang]]" takes place between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]] per the reasoning [[Template:WildSpaceDate|here]].</ref> the [[monster droid]] [[CR-8R]] told the [[cartographer]] [[Emil Graf]] of IG-88's hunt.<ref name="IG-88 vs Gatto Gang" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blazor was mentioned in the story "[[Tales from Wild Space: IG-88 vs. the Gatto Gang]]," which was featured in [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 9|issue 9]] of the anthology [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series [[Star Wars Adventures (2017)|''Star Wars Adventures'']]. The story was written by [[Nick Brokenshire]]<ref name="IG-88 vs Gatto Gang" /> and published by [[IDW Publishing]] on [[April 18]], [[2018]].<ref name="IDW">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2017-2020-9/digital-comic/635306|text=Star Wars Adventures (2017-2020) #9|nolive=1}}</ref>
