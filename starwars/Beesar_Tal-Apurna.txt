'''Beesar Tal-Apurna''' was a [[Chadra-Fan]] [[Gender|female]] who [[Marriage|married]] the Chadra-Fan [[Teemank]]. After her wife was [[Death|killed]] by [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] during the [[Battle of Kur]] in [[232 BBY]], she grieved her spouse deeply. Fueled by resentment, Tal-Apurna agreed to [[spy]] on a Nihil collaborator and [[doctor]] [[Kisma Uttersond]] for [[Jedi Master]] [[Emerick Caphtor]] to avenge Teemank's death. While on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Chad]] in [[230 BBY]], the Chadra-Fan followed the doctor and watched him interact with his [[Uttersonds|family]] in their [[Kisma Uttersond's house|home]]. When Tal-Apurna's attempts to attach a [[Homing beacon|tracking device]] to a [[Unidentified Nihil freighter|Nihil freighter]] were unsuccessful, she instead opted to board the [[Freighter|vessel]] and relayed her location to Caphtor's [[droid]], [[Q-2]].

While aboard the ship, she wielded the Nihil [[Storm (rank)|Storm]] [[Zagyar]]'s [[Zagyar's blaster|blaster]] against Uttersond and the criminal [[Arathab Fal]] to protect Caphtor&mdash;who became incapacitated upon encountering the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] [[Living Force|Force]]-[[Diet|eating]] creature aboard. However, when Fal attempted to kill her with his [[Arathab Fal's electrostaff|electrostaff]], [[Private investigator|investigator]] [[Sian Holt]] saved Tal-Apurna, and the group fled the Nihil [[Starship|freighter]] and traveled to the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]]. After seeing the space station was [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|under attack]], Tal-Apurna threatened Uttersond until he agreed to share some details about the Nameless with them. Afterward, she boarded Starlight Beacon with her newfound colleagues. While there, she monitored the doctor for Caphtor and eventually left the doomed station with several other escaping [[Jedi]] aboard [[Sian Holt's ship|Holt's ship]].

==Biography==
===A loving wife===
{{Quote|I'll do whatever it takes!|Beesar Tal-Apurna, about avenging her wife|<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />}}
[[File:EmerickCaphtor-THRTrailOfShadows1.jpg|thumb|right|240px|Beesar Tal-Apurna agreed to spy for Emerick Caphtor to avenge Teemank's death at the hands of the Nihil.]]
The [[Chadra-Fan]] [[Gender|female]] Beesar Tal-Apurna was [[Marriage|married]] to the Chadra-Fan [[Teemank]] for many [[Standard year|years]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="THR Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> following the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], Teemank opted to join the fight against the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] organization [[Nihil conflict|plaguing]] [[the galaxy]] and signed up for the [[Republic Defense Coalition]]. The Chadra-Fan begged her not to go but ultimately failed to change<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> her<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> wife's mind. At some point after joining, Teemank learned how to fly a [[starship]] and taught Tal-Apurna some [[pilot]]ing techniques, though the latter remained relatively inexperienced. Tal-Apurna also learned of her wife's struggles while serving the [[Galactic Republic|Republic's]] coalition, including the coalition's opinion that she was too old to be of use and that Chadra-Fan did not need the Republic.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />

Teemank [[Death|died]]<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> later that year in the [[Kur Nebula]]'s<ref name="THR Encyclopedia" /> [[Battle of Kur]] against the Nihil when the marauders shot down her ship. Tal-Apurna was furious at the Nihil when she passed away, grieving her wife's death deeply.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> The Chadra-Fan became miserable in her grief for some [[time]] afterward,<ref name="Trail of Shadows 5" /> reflecting on her failure to convince her wife not to serve in the coalition and also on the years they had lost together. Not long after, the [[Jedi Master]] [[Emerick Caphtor]] asked Tal-Apurna to [[spy]] on [[Kisma Uttersond]]&mdash;a Chadra-Fan Nihil collaborator and [[doctor]]&mdash;on<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Chadra-Fan">{{Databank|chadra-fan|Chadra-Fan}}</ref> [[Chad]]. Tal-Apurna readily agreed, willing to do anything necessary in Teemank's memory. Eventually, the Chadra-Fan&mdash;strengthened by the legacy of her wife's memory&mdash;came to empathize with Teemank's decision to join the fight to protect her family. Over time, Tal-Apurna witnessed Uttersond's interactions with<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> his<ref name="Trail of Shadows 5" /> [[Uttersonds|family]], realizing he cared about them deeply.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />

===Boarding a Nihil freighter===
{{Quote|Dang&hellip; thing&hellip; won't&hellip; stay on! How do these kriffing trackers work anyway?! No! It's&mdash; it's&hellip; taking off! Only one way this'll happen but&hellip; ''For Teemank!|Beesar Tal-Apurna infiltrating a Nihil freighter|<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />}}
[[File:BeesarTrackingDevice-THRTrailOfShadows4.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Beesar Tal-Apurna attempted to attach a tracking device to a Kisma Uttersond's freighter.]]
In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="IssuesDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic: Trail of Shadows 4|''The High Republic: Trail of Shadows'' 4]] to [[230 BBY]].</ref> Tal-Apurna followed Uttersond to his [[Kisma Uttersond's house|home]] on Chad, hiding from view as he entered his residence. As she watched the doctor interact with his family by peering through a [[Viewport|window]], the Chadra-Fan reflected on Teemank's death and how that had led to her spying for Caphtor and the Republic's investigation. When Uttersond left his house and headed toward a [[Unidentified Chad shipyard|shipyard]], Tal-Apurna followed him there and attempted to attach a [[Homing beacon|tracking device]] on a [[Unidentified Nihil freighter|Nihil freighter]], becoming frustrated with it due to not understanding how it functioned. However, the [[Freighter|vessel]] began to take off before she could firmly secure the technology into place.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />

While considering that she was acting in memory of Teemank's bravery, Tal-Apurna instead boarded the vessel via its landing gear and reported the ship's coordinates to Caphtor's [[droid]], [[Q-2]], via [[Comm unit|comm]]. The Chadra-Fan then continued monitoring Uttersond, overhearing the Nihil<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> [[Storm (rank)|Storm]]<ref name="THR Encyclopedia" /> [[Zagyar]] berating the doctor for messing up their last [[transport]] and overhearing information about their reinforcements. Not long after, Caphtor arrived at the location [[Commanding officer|leading]] a group of [[starfighter]]s, engaging in a [[Attack on a Nihil convoy|skirmish]] with the Nihil [[convoy]] present. The [[Jedi]] told his colleagues that their Republic contact was on one of the vessels and that he intended to board the ship.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />

===Confronting the enemy===
{{Quote|Should've set it to kill, little rat.''"<br />"''I&hellip; I&hellip; How do you change this setting?!''"<br />"''Too late! Blasters don't scare me anyway.|Beesar Tal-Apurna and Arathab Fal|<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />}}
[[File:BeesarUttersondFal-THRTrailOfShadows4.jpg|thumb|right|280px|Beesar Tal-Apurna confronted Kisma Uttersond and Arathab Fal with a blaster.]]
Meanwhile, Tal-Apurna watched Zagyar confront Uttersond, believing the doctor had betrayed them to the Republic. As the vessel lurched when hit by a Republic starfighter, [[Zagyar's blaster]] clattered to the floor, and Tal-Apurna reached for it. However, after seeing the doctor doing the same, she slowly backed into her hiding spot again. Uttersond used the [[blaster]] to kill Zagyar, ranting about how sick he was of encountering both Jedi and Nihil. After the doctor had finished shooting at the Nihil Storm's body several times, the spy was horrified by the burning husk of a corpse sitting before them both. Moments after, when Caphtor boarded the vessel and ignited his [[Emerick Caphtor's lightsaber|lightsaber]] near Uttersond, the doctor opened the peephole of the freighter's containment area for [[Unidentified Nameless (Emerick Caphtor)|one]] of the Nihil's [[Living Force|Force]]-draining [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />

[[File:BeesarFal-THRTrailOfShadows4.jpg|thumb|left|220px|Arathab Fal knocked away Beesar Tal-Apurna's weapon, intending to kill her.]]
The peephole's opening left the Jedi incoherent, causing his colleague&mdash;the [[Private investigator|investigator]] [[Sian Holt]]&mdash;to worry about him when he did not respond. As Uttersond moved open the door between him and the Nameless, intending to kill the Jedi, Tal-Apurna grabbed Zagyar's [[weapon]] and held the doctor at blaster-point. Tal-Apurna hesitated to harm the doctor&mdash;not wanting to harm a fellow Chadra-Fan&mdash;and kept the weapon set to [[stun]] as she confronted him. Uttersond asked her to be reasonable while indicating that the criminal [[Arathab Fal]]&mdash;with whom the doctor had secretly been working to sell a Nameless with which to leave the Nihil&mdash;would not share his same stance of not wanting to hurt her.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />

As Fal approached behind Uttersond, Tal-Apurna used the blaster to stun the doctor, though Fal was left unaffected by the blast. When the spy struggled to change the setting on the weapon to kill to fire again, Fal teased it was too late to do so and relished in her panicked state. Fal knocked the item out of Tal-Apurna's [[Limb|hands]] and moved to kill her with an [[Arathab Fal's electrostaff|electrostaff]] of his own. However, Holt&mdash;who had followed Caphtor onto the ship&mdash;[[Dismemberment|sliced off]] the Nihil's arm with the Jedi's [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />

===Fleeing the freighter===
{{Quote|What's your name?''"<br />"''Beesar Tal-Apurna! You saved me! Thank you!''"<br />"''We're not out of this yet. I'll come back for Uttersond.|Sian Holt and Beesar Tal-Apurna|<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />}}
[[File:BeesarTalApurna-THRTrailOfShadows5.png|thumb|right|200px|Beesar Tal-Apurna coerced Kisma Uttersond into answering their questions by threatening the doctor's family.]]
Q-2, who had arrived with Holt, insisted they needed to evacuate Caphtor from the Nihil freighter as Tal-Apurna closed the nearby peephole to the Nameless's crate, knowing that Uttersond had said it was part of what affected the Jedi. The spy thanked Holt for saving her, telling the [[Detective|investigator]] her name. Though initially hesitant&mdash;having only received minimal piloting instruction from Teemank&mdash;Tal-Apurna agreed to fly [[Sian Holt's ship|Holt's ship]], insisting she would do whatever was necessary to complete their objective. The group then fled the freighter, and Holt took Uttersond [[Prisoner|captive]]. With Tal-Apurna piloting, they made their way to [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]] so that Caphtor could receive medical care.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />

As they approached Starlight Beacon's location, Tal-Apurna was updated on the Jedi's condition and indicated they should arrive at their destination soon. However, the spy and Holt noticed shortly afterward that Starlight was [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|under attack]].<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> The group resolved to question Uttersond for answers, though the doctor claimed he had no moral code and only wanted to acquire lots of [[Currency|money]]. Tal-Apurna became furious, telling those present about Teemank's death to the Nihil and indicating the lives of Uttersond's family could become similarly miserable if he did not cooperate.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 5" />

===Falling stars===
{{Quote|Beesar, keep a sharp eye on Uttersond.''"<br />"''Always, Master Emerick.|Emerick Caphtor to Beesar Tal-Apurna, as their group prepared to confront a Nameless on Starlight Beacon|<ref name="Trail of Shadows 5" />}}
[[File:CaphtorGroupStarlightBeacon-THRTrailOfShadows5.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Beesar Tal-Apurna helped Emerick Caphtor's group after boarding Starlight Beacon.]]
Uttersond relented and agreed to tell them what he knew, giving them a small piece of [[Azlin's journal|parchment]] with [[Writing|handwritten]] notes and telling them a few details about the Nameless and their Force-[[Diet|eating]] capabilities. As the ship's passengers watched the space station before them, the doctor indicated that Starlight Beacon was doomed, as his convoy had been due to be part of the second attack wave. Despite Uttersond's protests as to its futility, Caphtor resolved to board the station, and Tal-Apurna joined her newfound colleagues. After crashing through the ceiling of the station's [[assembly room]], the group encountered Jedi Master [[Torban Buck]] and a group of [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]], who boarded Holt's ship to escape Starlight Beacon.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 5" />

Before they departed, Caphtor led a group to investigate a [[Unidentified Nameless (Starlight Beacon)|wounded Nameless]] Buck had encountered to learn more about them, instructing Tal-Apurna to monitor Uttersond. Caphtor and Holt moved to the front of the group and neared the creature, but when it readied to attack, Uttersond pushed Tal-Apurna aside and threw one of her [[thermal detonator]]s at it. The Jedi and Chadra-Fan were ambushed by a group of Nihil aboard, forcing them to flee back toward their ship. Caphtor bid farewell to Buck, fleeing the station with several [[youngling]]s, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Qort]], Holt, and Tal-Apurna.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 5" /> In the [[229 BBY|year afterward]],<ref name="Date">The chapter of ''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'' that establishes [[Emerick Caphtor]] was still investigating the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] takes place a [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]] but before [[Pra-Tre Veter's execution]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Caphtor continued his investigations regarding the Nihil in the aftermath of Starlight Beacon's destruction.<ref name="EOD">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Stop right there, doc! I&mdash;I don't want to hurt you! This is set to stun! Just&hellip;just&hellip;stay back! And stop what you're doing!''"<br />"''Now, now, a fellow Chadra-Fan, ey? Let's be reasonable, good sister.|Beesar Tal-Apurna and Kisma Uttersond|<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />}}
[[File:BeesarSpying-THRTrailOfShadows4.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Beesar Tal-Apurna agreed to spy on Kisma Uttersond due to her hatred of the Nihil following Teemank's death.]]
Beesar Tal-Apurna had white [[Hair|fur]], black [[eye]]s, and pale skin. She was one [[meter]] (three [[Foot|feet]] three inches) tall<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> and spoke [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]]. By the time she served as a spy on Chad, the Chadra-Fan had an elderly appearance. The woman considered herself soft-spoken and felt she did not operate well under pressure. For many years, she and her wife maintained a deeply loving relationship.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> Upon Teemank's death to the Nihil, Tal-Apurna became extremely depressed while mourning her spouse's passing<ref name="Trail of Shadows 5" /> and her inability to prevent it. Despite not having previously cared about the Nihil threat, her grief fueled her newfound strong resentment for the marauders. Though Tal-Apurna agreed to spy for the Republic in her wife's memory, she felt she was not adequately trained for the role and considered herself only a "kindly old lady."<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />

The Chadra-Fan abhorred the violent way Uttersond killed Zagyar and watched the Nihil's corpse tearfully as it fell. While she did fire on Uttersond and Fal with a stun blast, she did not wish to hurt either and pleaded for them to stop what they were doing so she could assist Caphtor. Whenever unsure of her capabilities, Tal-Apurna reminded herself of her wife's courage in fighting the Nihil and resolved to do whatever was necessary to end their presence in the galaxy.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> After seeing the Nihil's attack on Starlight Beacon, the Chadra-Fan was willing to threaten Uttersond's family and wielded a blaster against the doctor until he agreed to tell their group more about the Nameless threat.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 5" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|Can you pilot a ship?''"<br />"''I&hellip;Teemank taught me a few things, yes.|Sian Holt and Beesar Tal-Apurna|<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />}}
The Chadra-Fan was able to operate as a spy and remain undetected by Uttersond on Chad while surveying the doctor's home. Despite her best efforts, she often struggled to use technology efficiently.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> By 230 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="IssuesDate" /> Tal-Apurna had learned enough piloting knowledge to fly Holt's ship.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> 

==Equipment==
{{Quote|Uttersond's ship! All I have to do is place the tracking device and I'll be done. I can go home.|Beesar Tal-Apurna, after following Kisma Uttersond to a shipyard|<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />}}
[[File:BeesarUttersond-THRTrailOfShadows4.jpg|thumb|left|270px|Beesar Tal-Apurna wielded Zagyar's blaster against her foes.]]
While operating as a spy, Tal-Apurna wore a long-sleeved pale green [[Clothing|shirt]] paired with gray pants and a lime green hood that had holes for her [[ear]]s. She completed her outfit with a brown [[belt]] featuring several pouches and brown [[boot]]s. At the Chad [[shipyard]], Tal-Apurna attempted to attach a tracking device to Uttersond's ship.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" />

Despite the weapon being much larger than her, the Chadra-Fan wielded Zagyar's blaster against Uttersond and Fal aboard a Nihil freighter.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> She later threatened Uttersond aboard Holt's ship with a smaller [[blaster pistol]] that fit in her hand. While aboard Starlight Beacon, Tal-Apurna was armed with several [[Grenade|thermal detonators]] in a silver-colored sling attached to her body across her shoulder.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 5" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|We get a lot of Uttersond who I think is a fascinating character, and I wanted to build up his family life and his backstory some. But I also wanted a counterpoint to him and to get someone who's really invested in stopping the Nihil and helping the Republic for her own personal family reasons because her wife was killed in the Battle of Kur.|Daniel José Older, on Beesar Tal-Apurna's backstory|audio=Teemank-DJO-THRShow.flac|<ref name="THR Show">{{HighRepublicShow|olRlGewiQAw|The Fallen Star, the Nameless First Look, and More!|sw_url=video/the-fallen-star-the-nameless-first-look-and-more}}</ref>}}
[[File:THRTrailOfShadows4.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Beesar Tal-Apurna was introduced in ''Star Wars: The High Republic: Trail of Shadows''.]]
Beesar Tal-Apurna debuted and was first identified in the [[The High Republic: Trail of Shadows 4|fourth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Trail of Shadows]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] miniseries. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], published by [[Marvel Comics]],<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4" /> and released on [[January 26]], [[2022]],<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-the-high-republic-trail-of-shadows-4-exclusive-preview|text=The Jedi Rush to Face the Nihil in Marvel's ''Star Wars: The High Republic: Trail of Shadows'' #4 &ndash; Exclusive Preview}}</ref> as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia series' Phase I.<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> Prior to the issue's release, a page depicting her was included in a [[StarWars.com]] preview for the issue on [[January 19]] of that year.<ref name="Preview" /> Older had initially alluded to Chadra-Fans appearing in the comic on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] on [[December 28]], [[2021]].<ref name="Tweet">{{Twitter|djolder|status/1475917736249380869|[[Daniel José Older]]|date=2021-12-28|quote=Speaking of which, good news for Chadra-Fan fans next month&hellip;|archivedate=20211228195815}}</ref>

While being interviewed on ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Show]]'', Older indicated that Tal-Apurna was his favorite new character from ''Trail of Shadows'' and that he had enjoyed writing her. He was pleased he had gotten the opportunity to write more Chadra-Fan as the author considered them an interesting [[species]]. Older explained that Teemank's death fueled Tal-Apurna's strong motivations for defeating the Nihil to avenge her wife, serving as a contrast to Uttersond's character arc of being a Nihil collaborator in the series. In the same interview, [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]'s [[Michael Siglain]] said he felt ''Trail of Shadows''{{'s}} fourth issue was one of the best created for the ''The High Republic'' initiative, especially given the installment's opening pages&mdash;which featured Uttersond and Tal-Apurna&mdash;were not centered around the miniseries' main characters of Caphtor and Holt.<ref name="THR Show" />
