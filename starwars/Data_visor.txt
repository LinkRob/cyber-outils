The '''data visor''',<ref name="CompSWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> also known as '''Tech's Visor''' in the context of its usage by the [[clone commando]] [[Tech]], was a piece of electronic eyewear that could be attached to a [[helmet]].<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref>

==Description==
[[File:TechVisor-GoH.png|thumb|left|Tech's helmet was completed by a data visor]]
A data visor displayed readouts in [[Aurebesh]] and could be used as an [[electronic translator]], upon which "{{uc:Translation mode initiated}}" was displayed at the bottom of the visor. The device would then display the [[language]] of a target speaker and translate it into [[Galactic Basic Standard]] Aurebesh text. [[Clone commando]] [[Tech]] used the visor to understand and relate the meaning of [[Poletec]]an speech to his [[Clone Force 99]] teammates and vice versa.<ref name="Echo">{{TCW|A Distant Echo}}</ref> The visor could also be used to scan surroundings. For example, when the [[Clone trooper|clone]] used it to scan [[Cid's mine]], it resulted in "[[Ipsium|IPSIUM]] DETECTED", followed by a list of [[Alternative Aurebesh numerals|alternative numerals]] consisting of dots and lines.<ref name="Crossing">{{TBB|The Crossing}}</ref>
