'''''Age of Resistance - Poe Dameron'' 1''' is the fifth one-shot in the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] anthology series ''[[Star Wars: Age of Resistance]]''. It was written by [[Tom Taylor]], illustrated by [[Ramon Rosanas]], and published on [[August 28]], [[2019]] by [[Marvel Comics]]. The issue features the story "'''Fight or Flight'''," and it also includes an essay written by [[Bria LaVorgna]].

==Publisher's summary==
''FIGHT AND FLIGHT! POE DAMERON is the greatest pilot in THE RESISTANCE. But before the Resistance, he commanded another crew. He flew for the NEW REPUBLIC. But was Poe flying for the right cause?''
