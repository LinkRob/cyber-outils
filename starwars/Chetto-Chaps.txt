'''Chetto-Chaps''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|dish]] that was available on the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> An announcement was made by a [[droid]] over the [[cruiser]]'s [[speaker]] system informing passengers a free [[Yerdua Poison Spitter Drop]] was offered with every order of Chetto-Chaps during happy hour.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
