'''Basher''' was a [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtrooper]] [[Sergeant/Legends|sergeant]] serving the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] before the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]]. Basher was a [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]]<ref name="LaunchPad" /> created on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Kamino/Legends|Kamino]] from the [[Clone template/Legends|genetic template]] of [[Jango Fett/Legends|Jango Fett]] to serve in the [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="DB" /> Having survived the war, the now veteran Basher continued their service and stayed with the Empire after its transition from the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]]. Basher expressed disapproval towards "modern" stormtroopers.<ref name="LaunchPad" />

==Behind the scenes==
Sergeant Basher was set to be voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]] in the canceled ''[[Star Wars Detours]]'' series.<ref name="LaunchPad" /> In the trailer for ''Star Wars Detours'', an individual wearing [[Clone trooper armor/Legends|clone trooper armor]] with green and red markings can be seen bobbing their head during a dance party, though it is never specified if this is Basher or a separate character.<ref name="Trailer">{{YouTube|channel=@holonet|name=Star Wars HoloNet|text=Star Wars: Detours - Trailer|video=oMVrMP8us6Q}}</ref> The character can also be seen in another video where they make an appearance on a news channel for being trapped inside of a [[Meditation chamber/Legends|meditation chamber]] for forty-one [[Standard hour/Legends|hours]].<ref name="Extrait1">{{YouTube|channel=@holonet|name=Star Wars HoloNet|text=Star Wars: Detours - Extrait #1|video=wYRHngrc4o0|date=2012-10-17}}</ref>
