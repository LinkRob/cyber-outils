'''FwittsinPru''' was a [[Troig]] spokesperson for the [[Obroan Oddsmakers]]. In a report made by the news organization [[Cynabar's InfoNet]], FwittsinPru spoke on the behalf of the Obroan Oddsmakers and criticized the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], blaming the Alliance for a [[Rescue of Luke Skywalker|mission]] which instigated a fight between the Rebels, the [[Galactic Empire]], and the forces of [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus]] the [[Hutt]].<ref name="TRF" />
