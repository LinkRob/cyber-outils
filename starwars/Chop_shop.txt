The '''chop shop''' was a shop found within the [[Coruscant Underworld (battlefield)|Coruscant Underworld]] battlefield in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. It had an both an interior and outer bay area, servicing various [[Speeder bike|vehicles]], including a number of [[BARC speeder]]s, which were both tightly packed in the garage as well as chained above the bay. Various signs dotted the outside of the establishment, with the largest being one directly on top of the building that read "[[speeder]]" in bold [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="Kay" />

==Behind the scenes==
The chop shop was first designed for the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' by concept artist [[Anna Kay]]. It was proposed as one of four main points of interest on the Coruscant Underworld map, then still referred to as the "Locklands,"<ref name="Kay" /> though it did not make it into the final version of the game.<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>
