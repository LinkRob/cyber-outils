'''CZ-94''' was a [[droid]] that served in the [[portmaster]]'s [[Portmaster's office|office]] at the [[Carannia Spaceport|Serenno Spaceport]]. It was destroyed by [[Asajj Ventress]], [[Sith apprentice]] to [[Dooku]], who visited the spaceport in [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|search]] of her [[Sith Master|master's]] sister [[Jenza]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
