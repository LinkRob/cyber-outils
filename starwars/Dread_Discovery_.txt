"'''Dread Discovery!'''" was the third installment of [[The Keeper's World]], published in [[Pizzazz 3]] in December [[1977]]. The entire story arc was reprinted in the UK in ''[[Star Wars Weekly 47]]&ndash;[[Star Wars Weekly 50|50]]'' in [[1978]]&ndash;[[1979|79]], and in the [[1999]] limited-release comic ''[[Star Wars 0]]''.

==Plot summary==
[[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]], [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]], and [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] have crash-landed on an [[Keeper's World|unknown planet]] while escaping an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Imperial Navy/Legends|fleet]]. C-3PO discovers evidence of large creatures dwelling on the planet. Meanwhile, the Imperials discover their crash site and send a landing craft to re-capture them.
