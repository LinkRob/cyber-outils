'''Durang fruit''' was a [[fruit]] once eaten half-rancid by [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. According to her, when it was half-rancid, it tasted how [[death]] smelled.<ref name="ALD" /> [[Standard decade|Decades]] later, the fruit was used for [[fruit jelly]]<ref name="SWTVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> served in [[Takodana Castle]].<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
A durang fruit first appeared as a background prop in [[Takodana Castle]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], a film released in [[December]] [[2015]]. It was not identified in the film, but was named in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'', released in [[April]] [[2017]].
