'''Computer spikes''', also known as '''data spikes''', were electronic devices that, upon insertion into a [[computer]], allowed the user to access classified data and programs. Spikes could be set to initiate a program or be operated manually, and plugged into the same kind of sockets as [[scomp link]]s. They were often used in infiltrations and thefts.

==Description==
Computer spikes<ref name="Trivia Gallery">
{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/call-to-action-trivia-gallery|text=Call to Action Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref> were handheld, vaguely cylindrical devices designed for hijacking computer systems. They could be inserted into a target computer by an [[astromech droid]],<ref name="Call to Action" /> or be inserted and operated manually.<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref> They could be used in infiltrations<ref name="Call to Action" /> or to open locked doors.<ref name="Shattered" /> Spikes were also a common tool in [[starship]] thefts, used to overload the target ship's defences.<ref name="Pirate's Price">''[[Pirate's Price]]''</ref> Spikes could also be used as [[data rod]]s.<ref name="Decommissioned">{{TBB|Decommissioned}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:DataRod-Decommissioned.png|thumb|right|200px|Tech using a computer spike as a data rod.]]
Computer spikes were in use by the end of the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="Shattered" /> In [[19 BBY]], during the [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|issuing]] of [[Order 66]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> mind-controlled [[clone trooper]]s onboard<ref name="Shattered" /> the ''[[Tribunal]]''<ref name="TCW Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars The Clone Wars Character Encyclopedia: Join the battle!]]''</ref> used a data spike to manually open the door of the [[medical bay]] former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] had sealed herself inside with three astromech [[droid]]s in order to perform emergency surgery on [[Clone Commander]] [[Rex]] to remove his [[behavioral modification biochip]] and free him from the brainwashing. The clones succeeded in getting the door open, however, the efforts of droids [[RG-G1]] and [[CH-33P]] were able to reseal the door, and Tano was able to fight off the attackers with some last-minute assistance from Rex.<ref name="Shattered" /> The attacking clones eventually cut the door out of the frame with [[blowtorch]]es, but by that time Tano and her allies had finished what they needed to do inside the room, and successfully fought their way out.<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref>
Shortly after the rise of the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]], the [[Clone X trooper]] [[CX-2]] got intel from [[Ciddarin Scaleback]], who told her [[Phee Genoa]] had contact with [[Omega]]. After tracking Phee down, CX-2 snuck onto [[pirate]]'s [[starship]], the ''[[Providence]]'', and planted a computer spike into the ship's [[navigation computer]] and discover that she frequently visited [[Pabu]], which he discerned was where Omega was hiding.<ref name="No Return">{{TBB|Point of No Return (The Bad Batch)}}</ref>

[[Maul]]'s droid [[Spybot]] had a [[Slicer|slicing]] device, as one of his tools, that acted like a computer spike while participating in a [[raid on a bank tower]] in [[Janix]]. Spybot launched the device though the open vault doors onto the vault's [[dataport]], giving the [[Shadow Collective]] control of the building. As the vault doors closed, Spybot reopened them, allowing the personnel to enter the tower. One of the [[bank guard droid]]s noticed the device and manage to tear if off and set off the vault's alarm before being destroyed by one of [[Icarus]]'s [[Knife|knives]].<ref name="Dark Revenge">{{MSL|Chapter 1: The Dark Revenge}}</ref>

During the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]], members of the [[Spectres]] rebel cell [[Raid on the communications tower|hijacked]] the [[Imperial Communications Center]] on [[Lothal]] so they could send an anti-[[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Ezra's broadcast|broadcast]]. During the raid, [[Sabine Wren]] and [[C1-10P]] "Chopper" plugged a [[Q-bazik computer spike|Q-bazik data spike]] into the communication tower's computers in order to carry out the hijacking.<ref name="Call to Action" />

[[File:Computer_spike.png|thumb|right|320px|The Specters using a Q-bazik computer spike.]]
Later, during the [[Galactic Civil War]], Chopper was himself hijacked by Imperial [[controller]] [[Boon|LT-319]] during a [[Mission to recover Imperial codes|mission]] to the [[moon]] [[Killun 71]]. After the crew of the ''[[Ghost]]'' had subdued the rogue droid, [[Captain]] [[Hera Syndulla]] used a data spike while hooking Chopper up to her ship in a successful ploy to both undo the hacking and destroy LT-319 and his ship remotely.<ref name="DAD">{{Rebels|Double Agent Droid}}</ref> [[Kay Vess]] also used one extensively to open doors and containers. When she did not use the spike, it was used as a makeshift hairpin.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

After the Galactic Civil War, [[Axe Woves]], a [[Mandalorian]] resistance fighter, used a computer spike to open a door to get inside of an Imperial [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]].<ref name="Heiress">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}}</ref>

The [[slicer]] [[DJ]] handcrafted his own computer spikes that could temporarily befuddle [[bio-hexacrypt]]ion.<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The first [[canon]]ical appearance of a computer spike was in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|season 1]] episode "[[Call to Action]]."<ref name="Call to Action" /> They were first identified as computer spikes in the accompanying episode guide on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Trivia Gallery" />
