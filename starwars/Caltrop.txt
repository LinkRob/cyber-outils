A '''caltrop''' was a type of [[Limb|handheld]] antipersonnel weapon made of sharp spikes. Used defensively, caltrops were dropped to form a field which would slow enemies and cause them to [[Blood|bleed]]. During the [[Iron Blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]], a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] used caltrops as a means of self defense.<ref name="Uprising" />

==Behind the scenes==
Caltrops first appeared in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', which was released in [[2015]] by [[Kabam]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms. In ''Uprising'', caltrops are a defensive ability which can be purchased from a trainer.<ref name="Uprising" />
