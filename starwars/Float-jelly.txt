A '''float-jelly''' was a type of massive floating living [[gas]]bag that resided on<ref name="Dawn" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]] &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Yar Togna system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Yar Togna]]. These gasbags ate ash held in the planet's thick [[atmosphere]] from erupting [[volcano]]es. Float-jellies traveled in lumbering herds that ate the ash, preventing the planet from plunging into a deep freeze. The gasbags had back secretions that made for a rich soil.<ref name="Dawn" />

[[Float farmer]]s used the secretions to grow delicacies such as [[mushroom]]s, [[moss]]es, and [[mold]]s that were prized throughout [[the galaxy]] directly on float-jellies' backs. Farming on the float-jellies was dangerous, successful float farmers were often Tognath [[eggmate]]s. [[Pilot]]s dodged volcanic updrafts and the gasbags colliding while leapers harvested the produce from the backs of the float-jellies.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
Float jellies were mentioned in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[2018]] roleplaying sourcebook published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' game.<ref name="Dawn" />
