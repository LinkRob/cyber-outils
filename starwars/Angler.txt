'''Anglers''' were a bulbous, [[Diet|carnivorous]] [[species]] of organism native to the [[swamp]]s of the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]]. They would sit on the buttress roots of [[tree]]s and pull [[lizard crab]]s from the swamps, which they would then absorb and eat.<ref name="USW" />

==Behind the scenes==
Anglers first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book ''[[The Illustrated Star Wars Universe]]'', which was released in [[1995]] and showcased concept artwork by [[Ralph McQuarrie]] with text written by [[Kevin J. Anderson]]. The species were confirmed to be [[canon]] by the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', which was released in [[2015]].
