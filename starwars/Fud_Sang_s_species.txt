This '''species'''<ref name="TPM" /> was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="Insider" /> that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TPM" /> [[Fud Sang]] was a member of this species.<ref name="Insider" />

==Biology and appearance==
This bipedal<ref name="TPM" /> [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="Insider" /> was distinguished by their hunched, pudgy appearances, with large heads featuring bulging [[eye]]s, flat [[nose]]s, and wide [[mouth]]s.<ref name="TPM" />

==Species in the galaxy==
A young [[Anakin Skywalker]] owned a statue of a member of this species,<ref name="TPM" /> [[Fud Sang]],<ref name="Insider" /> in his home on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="TPM" />

==Behind the scenes==
This species could first be seen briefly via a statue in the background of {{Film|I}}.<ref name="TPM" />
