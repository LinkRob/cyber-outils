'''Cavrolo Sys''' was a [[Togruta]] [[Gender|male]] hired by the [[Scout|explorers]] [[Fionn Tucat]] and [[Romina Foss]] for an [[Mission to Lupal|expedition]] to the long-abandoned [[moon]] [[Lupal]]. The crews' minds were influenced by the solar flares of Lupal's dying [[Unidentified star (Lupal)|star]], particularly the [[Shistavanen]] [[Kordus Vrak]], who confided the primal anger that the light had given him to Sys. Despite Vrak's demands that he be restrained if the solar flares continued, the crew continued their excavations unperturbed. Vrak was driven feral by the light of the star, and proceeded to attack the crew. Sys was the second of the crew to be [[Death|killed]], saddened with the realization that he should have trusted Vrak's instincts.<ref name="Blood Moon" />
