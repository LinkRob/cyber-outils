The '''double-bladed spinning lightsaber''', also known as the '''double-bladed''' '''Inquisitorius lightsaber''', or simply as the '''Inquisitor lightsaber''', was a specially-designed [[lightsaber]] model used by the [[Inquisitorius]], a group of [[dark side adept]]s who enforced the will of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was capable of both single and double blades, and was capable of spinning in a circular motion when both blades were emitted. Known wielders of this model included [[the Grand Inquisitor]], [[Barriss Offee]], the [[Second Sister]], the [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister]], the [[Fourth Sister]], the [[Fifth Brother]], the [[Sixth Brother]], the [[Seventh Sister]], the [[Eighth Brother]], the [[Ninth Sister]], the [[Eleventh Brother]], the [[Thirteenth Sister]], [[Tualon Yaluna]], [[Marrok]], [[Jerserra]] and [[Jerserra's master|her Imperial master]].

==Mechanics and specifications==
===Overview===
{{Quote|Some double-bladed lightsabers could be detached to create two individual weapons, while others, like those used by Imperial Inquisitors, had blades that were designed to rotate freely around their hilt.|[[Luke Skywalker]]|<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Grand_Inquisitor_on_Daiyu.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Grand Inquisitor wielding his double-bladed spinning lightsaber]]
The [[lightsaber]]'s overall design hardly made it any bigger than a single-emitter [[lightsaber]],<ref name="Twilight">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref> a stark contrast to the design of [[Double-bladed lightsaber|dual-bladed]] [[weapon]]s previously used by warriors such as [[Darth]] [[Maul]].<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> Another unique feature of this weapon was that the blade emitters would spin along tracks in the disc while the [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]] would remain stationary in relation, allowing for more emphasis on the attack whilst maintaining a better degree of control over the weapon.<ref name="Twilight" />

The lightsabers of the [[Reva's lightsaber|Third Sister]]<ref name="Part V">{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref> and the [[Sixth Brother's lightsaber|Sixth Brother]] were [[split saber]]s that could separate into two single-bladed lightsabers.<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref> Some versions of the lightsaber also contained a rotating wheel of sharp razor blades which could intimidate an enemy or be used as an alternative way of slicing through opponents. [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]]'s [[Limb|hand]] was almost [[Dismemberment|sliced off]] when the [[Eighth Brother]] used this feature. The buzz-saw mechanism appeared in place of the hinges on the emitter rails of other Inquisitors' lightsabers, disallowing the Eighth Brother from folding the guard into a crescent shape.<ref name="Twilight" />

It could also be used to fly,<ref name="Lightsaber Collection">''[[Star Wars: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> but only by [[Force-sensitive]]s,<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> combining micro repulsorlifts with the blades' spinning in a propeller-like motion to lift the wielder off the ground.<ref name="Lightsaber Collection" /> This was often used as a method of escape, particularly from combat.<ref name="Twilight" />

===Aesthetics===
As was custom with the Jedi and [[Sith]], the lightsabers used by the [[Inquisitorius]] shown variation in the weapon's design while maintaining core features.<ref name="Always">{{Rebels|Always Two There Are}}</ref> The rings of the blades featured some variation in their shape between the different [[Inquisitor]]s. Some also featured [[Aurebesh]] writing on their rings.<ref name="Lightsaber Collection" />

[[Tenth Brother]] wielded two red [[Shoto lightsaber|shoto]] [[Tenth Brother's shoto lightsabers|lightsabers]] that shared heavy design elements with the double-bladed spinning lightsaber.<ref name="Dark Lord 13">[[Darth Vader (2017) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 13]]</ref>

===Weakness===
The weapon suffered from a number of design flaws: the hilt was a large and obvious target. As it wasn't made of lightsaber resistant material if given an opening, an opponent could cut through the disc from the inside or outside of the ring, causing the weapon to fly apart if spinning.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref> Kanan Jarrus defeated the grand inquisitor this way, using both his own and Ezra's lightsabers to tear apart the inquisitor's weapon. The ringed emitter also presented a larger target for a disarming move, and should the emitter be damaged it affected the lightsaber's ability to function properly. This was demonstrated by the Fifth and Eighth Brothers when facing [[Ahsoka Tano]], [[Kanan Jarrus]], and Maul in a confrontation on [[Malachor]]; both times, their opponents targeted the emitter and damaged it, which led to both their [[death]]s shortly afterwards.<ref name="Twilight" />

==Behind the scenes==
The double-blade spinning lightsaber first appeared in ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]'', an animated television movie released in 2014.<ref name="Spark of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref>

The spinning lightsaber was an unused concept for the video game [[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed|''The Force Unleashed'']].<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.newsarama.com/21681-sdcc-2014-star-wars-rebels-panel-live.html|text=SDCC 2014: STAR WARS REBELS Panel|date=2014-07-24|work=Newsarama|archivedate=20190814185550}}</ref> It was identified as "gyroscopic lightsaber" in the [[2017]] non-canon reference book ''[[Ultimate LEGO Star Wars]]''.<ref name="LEGOGyro">[[Ultimate LEGO Star Wars|''Ultimate'' LEGO ''Star Wars'']]</ref>
