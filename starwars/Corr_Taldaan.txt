'''Corr Taldaan''' was a [[Palliduvan]] [[pirate]] who led the [[Comet Runners]].

==Biography==
Between [[233 BBY]] and [[232 BBY]],<ref name="YJA Date">{{YJADate|122B}}</ref> Corr Taldaan&mdash;leader of the [[pirate]] crew [[Comet Runners]]&mdash;and the rest of the crew members were present at [[Yarrum Tower]] they came across the pirate [[Cyrus Vuundir|Taborr Val Dorn]] who was showing off [[Jedi]] artifacts he stole from the droid [[0G-LC]]. Taldaan and his gang stole the artifacts from Val Dorn and planned to sell them on [[Crescent Island]], although they were thwarted by Val Dorn along with [[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]], and [[Nubs]].<ref name="Team Up" />
