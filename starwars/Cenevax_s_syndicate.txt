A '''syndicate''' was led by the [[Jenet]] [[crime lord]] [[Cenevax]] during the [[Republic Era]]. An unlicensed [[gambling]] ring, Cenevax's operation was based in her [[Cenevax's den|den]] within the [[Hythan Quarter]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. After Cenevax [[Attack at the Dragonfire Air Rally|sabotaged]] the [[Serennian]] [[racer]] [[Ramil]] during the [[forty-eighth annual Dragonfire Air Rally]], her crimes were exposed by [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]], Ramil's [[House Serenno|brother]]. Cenevax was apprehended by the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Prison|imprisoned]] in the [[Citadel]], and her operation was dismantled.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
