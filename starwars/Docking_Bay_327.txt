'''Docking Bay 327''', also known as '''Hangar Bay 327''' or simply as '''Bay 327''' was a [[Hangar|docking bay]] aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. The docking bay was overseen by a control room, along with a [[tractor beam projector]]. It served as a cargo loading assembly area. An [[atmosphere containment projector]] kept the station's internal [[atmosphere]] in if the bay doors were opened. It was located directly above a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] staging area and 400 [[meter]]s above [[Detention Block AA-23]].<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

==History==
When it was [[Rescue of Princess Leia|captured]] by the Death Star, the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was pulled into Bay 327 through a [[tractor beam]].<ref name="Episode IV" /> Evading detection, [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Han Solo]], and [[Chewbacca]] launched a covert effort to rescue [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] from her captivity, with [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] coordinating their efforts from Bay 327's control center. At the same time, [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] disabled the tractor beam surrounding Bay 327, before being confronted by his former apprentice, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who ultimately struck him down in a duel in the vicinity of the ''Millennium Falcon''. Despite the intervention of Imperial stormtroopers, Skywalker and his allies ultimately escaped.

It, along with the Death Star, was completely destroyed after the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Episode IV" />
