A [[Hidden Hand|raider group]] '''attacked''' a [[Chorin]] [[Unidentified village (Chorin)|village]]<ref name="TV5" /> at some point prior to [[1 BBY]].<ref name="TV5 Date">The aftermath of the '''attack''' on Valance's village is shown in a flashback in [[Target Vader 5|''Target Vader'' 5]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]].</ref> The attack ended with many of the [[City|village]]'s inhabitants being [[Death|killed]] and buried. After the attack, [[Beilert Valance]] returned to the village and was outraged before leaving to attempt finding the raiders.<ref name="TV5" />

The attack on Valance's village was mentioned in the [[Target Vader 5|fifth issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Target Vader]]''. It was written by [[Robbie Thompson]], illustrated by [[Cris Bolson]], [[Roberto Di Salvo]], and [[Marco Failla]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="TV5" /> on [[November 13]], [[2019]].<ref name="TV5 Release">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/82616/star_wars_target_vader_2019_5|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #5}}</ref>
