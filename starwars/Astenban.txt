'''Astenban''' was a [[moon]]<ref name="Edge of Balance 3" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Seswenna sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was near the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]], as well as near the [[Banchii's main hyperspace route|main hyperspace route]] of<ref name="Edge of Balance 3" /> the Outer Rim [[Inugg system]]<ref name="Edge of Balance 1">[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 1|''The High Republic: The Edge of Balance'', Vol. 1]]</ref> planet [[Banchii]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s planned to use the moon as a base in order to launch attacks on Banchii and other planets in the region from once it fell within its [[Occlusion Zone]]. To prevent the Nihil from doing so, [[Jedi Master]] [[Savina Besatrix Malagán|Sav Malagán]], the [[droid]] [[ZZ-10]], and the [[Banchiian]] [[Shosho]] went on a [[Mission to Astenban|mission]] to Astenban. While the three were on the moon, Astenban was swallowed up by the Occlusion Zone and Nihil forces led by the [[scientist]] [[Niv Drendow Apruk]] quickly invaded the moon. After encountering the pirates, Shosho befriended a [[Homeworld|native]] [[Force-sensitive]] creature known as [[Zimtok]]. After Malagán was forced to destroy the [[Astenban camp|Nihil base]], the Nihil captured Zimtok, though Shosho followed her onto the Nihil's [[Starship|ship]] so he could save her. Malagán and ZZ-10 then left the moon and returned to Banchii.<ref name="Edge of Balance 3" />
