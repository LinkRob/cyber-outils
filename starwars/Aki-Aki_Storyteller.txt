'''Aki-Aki Storyteller''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Aki-Aki]] found at the [[Festival of the Ancestors]]. He told the story "[[The Rise and Fall of Panakin Slytalker]]" which was about a [[Jedi]] [[Knight]] named [[Qui-Gon Jinn|Bye-Gon Jim]] who met a young [[Anakin Skywalker|Panakin Slytalker]].<ref name="LEGO" />

==Behind the scenes==
