The '''Barrier''', or '''Great Barrier''',<ref name="Good Guys">{{SkeletonCrew|The Real Good Guys}}</ref>  or '''Barrier of At Attin'''<ref name="Tower">{{Databank|the-supervisor-s-tower|The Supervisor's Tower}}</ref> was a [[nebula]]-like gaseous orbital defense field that surrounded the [[planet]] [[At Attin]].<ref name="Real Adventure">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}}</ref> [[Starship]]s were not permitted to pass through the Barrier without authorization from [[At Attin's government]].<ref name="Way Out" />

Inside the Barrier were electric [[mine]]s that would send out bolts of energy at any unauthorized ship, destroying it. [[Onyx Cinder|Ships]] transporting [[Republic Emissary|emissaries]] to At Attin to collect newly-minted [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] transmitted a signal that disarmed the mines until they passed them.<ref name="We're Gonna Be In So Much Trouble">{{SkeletonCrew|We're Gonna Be In So Much Trouble}}</ref>

In [[9 ABY]], [[Wendle]] and [[Fara]], while struggling with [[Jod Na Nawood]], managed to throw the [[Barrier override|Barrier's killswitch]], destroying it to allow the [[New Republic Starfighter Corps]] to drive off [[Silvo's pirates|Na Nawood's pirates]].<ref name="Good Guys" />
