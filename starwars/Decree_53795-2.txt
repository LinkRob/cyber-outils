'''Decree 53795-2''' was a order issued by [[Queen]] [[Ashiga]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> during [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> It directed members of the [[Ashiga Clan]] to put the [[Kijimi City]] [[spaceport]]s and as well as the city's [[Thieves' Quarter]] under surveillance in order to locate where the [[Crimson Dawn]] was storing the [[Bann Pu'dira]], a stolen Ashiga .<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Decree 53795-2 was mentioned as part of "The Safecracker" quest in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
