A '''guard''' of two [[death trooper]]s accompanied [[ISB Supervisor]] [[Dedra Meero]] to the [[planet]] [[Ferrix]] to help in her [[Hunt for Cassian Andor|effort]] to capture [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] during [[Maarva's funeral procession]]. However, the funeral exploded into a [[Ferrix Riot|full-scale riot]]. During the riot, Andor [[Death|killed]] one of the death [[trooper]]s with his [[Clem's blaster pistol|blaster]].<ref name="Rix Road" />
