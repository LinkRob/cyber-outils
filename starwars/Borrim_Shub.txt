'''Borrim Shub''' was an [[Borrim Shub's species|alien]] that found itself<ref name="Topps" /> on the streets of the [[planet]] [[Daiyu]]<ref name="Kenobi II">{{Kenobi|Part II}}</ref> in [[9 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Kenobi|Part II}} to [[9 BBY]].</ref> While just trying to get by like everyone else, Shub encountered the [[human]] [[Youngling|child]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who nearly ran into the alien. Shub growled and looked down at Organa, who had frozen in her tracks, then moved on down the street.<ref name="Kenobi II" />

Borrim Shub was a big, [[hair]]y [[alien]] with wide, expressive [[Color|yellow]] [[eye]]s, black and pink skin, and black and gray fur. While some would classify it as horrifying, others would consider it adorable. While on Daiyu, Shub wore a brown [[helmet]], brown wrist guards, a brown [[belt]], a dirty tan apron, and a red [[Clothing|shirt]].<ref name="Topps" />

==Behind the scenes==
Borrim Shub first appeared in the [[Part II|second episode]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' television series,<ref name="Kenobi II" /> which premiered on [[May 26]], [[2022]].<ref name="Companion">{{InsiderCite|214|Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi Companion|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi'' Companion}}</ref> The character was not identified in the series<ref name="Kenobi II" /> but received a name on a card in the [[2023 Topps Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi|2023 Topps Disney ''Star Wars Obi-Wan Kenobi'']] set released by [[Topps|The Topps Company, Inc.]] in [[2023]].<ref name="Topps" />
