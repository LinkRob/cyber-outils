The '''dark purple traveling dress'''<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> was a [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] [[Clothing|outfit]] worn by [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Amidala]]. It had purple [[cerlin]] sleeves<ref name="TCVD NE" /> and was ornamented with shoulder decorations on the darker purple [[velvet]] overdress.<ref name="TVE" /> It was paired with an antique [[Crown|tiara]] embossed with the [[Symbol of the Royal House of Naboo|Naboo royal emblem]] and a mauve [[chersilk]] hair veil with two hanging sections in front criss-crossed by drapa bindings.<ref name="TCVD NE" />

During the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo]], Amidala traveled to the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[capital]] on [[Coruscant]] to plead for aid from the [[Galactic Senate]]. Despite all her efforts, Amidala was unable to secure help from the Republic quickly enough to save her people. On the journey home to [[Naboo]] in her [[J-type 327 Nubian royal starship|royal starship]],<ref name="Episode I" /> her [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] [[Sabé]] assisted her in dressing while the other handmaidens packed the [[Padmé Amidala's wardrobe|queen's wardrobe]].<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> Amidala wore the somber traveling gown in recognition of her people's suffering,<ref name="TCVD NE" /> and she wore it again at the [[Funeral of Qui-Gon Jinn|funeral]] of the [[Jedi]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], who died in the fight for Naboo.<ref name="Episode I" />

==Behind the scenes==
The dark purple traveling dress was created for the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="Episode I" /> The finished costume was created by costume designer [[Trisha Biggar]] and her department. The paneled [[Wikipedia:Velvet|velvet]] overdress was covered with a [[Wikipedia:Screen printing#Garment printing inks|discharge-printed]] Naboo pattern and fastened at the shoulders and neck with matching velvet buttons. It parted over a pleated [[Wikipedia:Silk|silk]] underdress, which was worn over layers of stiffened [[Wikipedia:Petticoat|petticoats]]. Under the overdress were the petal-shaped undersleeves, which were made of a double layer of [[Wikipedia:Shot silk|shot-silk]] [[Wikipedia:Chiffon (fabric)|chiffon]] [[Wikipedia:Satin|satin]], and fitted finger-length corded inner sleeves. The veils were made from chiffon, and the headdress was [[Wikipedia:Gold|gold]], embossed with the Naboo emblem.<ref name="Dressing a Galaxy">''[[Dressing a Galaxy: The Costumes of Star Wars]]''</ref>
