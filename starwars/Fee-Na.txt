'''Fee-Na''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Nautolan]] who lived on [[Miekos]] with her [[Marriage|husband]], [[Noparo Yar]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. She was secretly a member of the [[Confederation of Haulers]]. She was caught by [[Vernestra Rwoh]] and her mother-in-law [[Tanabi Yar]] when she tried to sabotage their plan to fix the [[comm tower]].<ref name="TFP" />
