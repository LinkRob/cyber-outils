'''Cenwick''' was a [[Plant|herb]] used in cooking. The [[Volpai]] sous-[[chef]] [[Robbs Ely]] used a touch of cenwick while making a sauce to served with [[bantha]] meat at the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]] shortly before his [[death]] at the hands of the [[BD-3000 luxury droid]]s [[J'Nell]] and [[Carly]]. While investigating Ely's murder, the castle's head chef [[Strono Tuggs]] recognized the touch of cenwick while sniffing a portion of the sauce that had been left as fingerprints by one of the [[droid]]s on the keypad to Ely's quarters. The chef [[Jom Jarusch]] then used [[Robbs Ely's recipe book]] to create the same sauce with cenwick during a cooking competition that Tuggs set up to catch Ely's killer, leading Tuggs to believe Jarusch was the killer when in fact the chef had simply stolen the book.<ref name="ARFD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cenwick first appeared in the short story "[[A Recipe for Death]]," which was written by [[Landry Q. Walker]] and published as part of the ''[[Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I]]'' anthology book released on [[April 5]], [[2016]].
