The '''Ash Moon 1 system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Jubilar sector]], which was situated in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and was in the [[Standard Galactic Grid|grid square]] T-7.<ref name="GMA" /> [[Ash Moon 1]] was an orbiting [[moon]]<ref name="Aphra 37" /> in the star system.<ref name="GMA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ash Moon 1 system first appeared in the [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 37|37th issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016)|Star Wars: Doctor Aphra]]''<ref name="Aphra 37" /> on [[October 2]], [[2019]],<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/76029/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2016_37|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016) #37}}</ref> but remained unnamed until the "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix was posted on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="GMA" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="GMA Date">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map...|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
