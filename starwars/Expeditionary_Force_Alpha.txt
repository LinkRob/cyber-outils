'''Expeditionary Force Alpha''' was a [[fleet]] used by the [[Erighal family]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. It consisted of several [[patrol ship]]s. Expeditionary Force Alpha was brought into service when a [[family emergency]] was declared, gathering Erighal [[Chiss officer|officers]] and [[Chiss warrior|warriors]] from the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] above [[Copero]]. Expeditionary Force Alpha was present for the [[Hoxim incident]], the culmination of a [[Jixtus's plan|plot to destroy the Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="Greater Good" />
