'''Dust bunnies''' were a type of creature that the [[Force-sensitive]] [[youngling]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] encountered at some point. The same group of dust bunnies that encountered him disturbed his [[meditation]] before chasing him, eventually gifting Grogu a [[flower]].

==Biology and appearance==
Dust bunnies were [[Color|gray]] [[Hair|fuzzy]] creatures with large white [[eye]]s. They had [[Limb|arms]] and legs, which were often concealed beneath their fuzz. They were also capable of swinging from a sort of string and could balance on top of one another.<ref name="Grogu" />

==History==
[[File:DustBunniesGrogu.png|thumb|left|250px|The dust bunnies offer a flower to Grogu.]]
At some point no earlier than [[41 BBY]],<ref name="Date Note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Chapter 1: The Mandalorian]]" to [[9 ABY]]. Since [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] is fifty [[Standard year|years]] old at the time of "Chapter 1: The Mandalorian," he must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[41 BBY]], and "[[Zen – Grogu and Dust Bunnies]]," which features Grogu, must be set at some point no earlier than that year.</ref> a group of dust bunnies discovered<ref name="Grogu" /> the [[Force-sensitive]] [[youngling]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]],<ref name="Chapter8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> who was in [[meditation]]. They began to bounce and swing around him until one finally landed on his head, disturbing his meditation before plummeting into a puddle of [[water]] below. Grogu&mdash;himself falling into the puddle after sneezing&mdash;turned to see the dust bunnies observing him before he began to walk away.<ref name="Grogu" />

The dust bunnies followed, prompting him to begin running from them until he turned, with one dust bunny colliding with his face and knocking him over. Now with Grogu's full attention, the creatures began to dance in front of him before presenting him with a [[flower]], which he accepted, bowing to them in thanks. The dust bunnies then piled up around the child, who proceeded to draw a circular symbol in the air with his finger.<ref name="Grogu" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dust bunnies appeared in the [[2022]] [[Disney+]] animated short "[[Zen – Grogu and Dust Bunnies]]," created by [[Studio Ghibli]].<ref name="Grogu" /> The creatures, known as [[Wikipedia:Susuwatari|susuwatari]] (ススワタリ) in [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]] and alternately translated as "soot sprites," "soot gremlins," or "black soots," originated from outside of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise in the [[1988]] Studio Ghibli film [[Wikipedia:My Neighbor Totoro|''My Neighbor Totoro'']].<ref name="TotoroDate">{{WebCite|url=https://www.studioghibli.com.au/myneighbortotoro|text=My Neighbor Totoro|work=[[Wikipedia:Studio Ghibli|Studio Ghibli]]|archivedate=20221028002344}}</ref>
