A '''DSS-02 regional shield''' was a model of [[deflector shield generator]] used on [[Batonn]] by the [[Batonn insurgents]]. The insurgents quickly turned on and off the shield, allowing them to fire [[Ion cannon]]s at [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Military|forces]] during the [[Siege of the Creekpath Mining and Refining complex|battle]] there. After [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]] rescued her [[parent]]s from their home which was in the shield, she detonated explosives set by [[Agent]] [[Gudry]] prematurely after he discovered Pryce's true intentions and [[Death|died]] when he was shot. The explosion was contained by the shield, resulting in the annihilation of the area, resulting in high civilian casualties as well as the destruction of the insurgents.<ref name="Thrawn" />
