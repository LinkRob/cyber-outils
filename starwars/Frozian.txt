'''Frozians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="Rising Storm" /> from the [[Froz system]].<ref name="Planetnamia" /> The [[High Republic Era]] [[Jedi]] [[Nooranbakarakana]] was a member of the species.<ref name="Rising Storm" />

{{Species-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Frozians were originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[1995]] novel ''[[Ambush at Corellia]]'', written by [[Roger MacBride Allen]] as the first installment of ''[[Star Wars: The Corellian Trilogy]]'',<ref name="Ambush at Corellia">''[[Ambush at Corellia]]''</ref> and were first illustrated by artist [[Storn Cook]] in ''[[Cracken's Threat Dossier]]'', a [[1997]] supplement to ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Cracken's Dossier">''[[Cracken's Threat Dossier]]''</ref> In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Frozians first appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="Rising Storm" /> and were first pictured in the [[The High Republic (2021) 9|ninth issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic (2021)|Star Wars: The High Republic]]'', which was written by Scott, illustrated by [[Ario Anindito]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="THR 9" /> on [[September 1]], 2021.<ref name="THR 9 release">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/94642/star_wars_the_high_republic_2021_9|text=Star Wars: The High Republic (2021) #9}}</ref>
