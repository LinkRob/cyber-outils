'''Commando droid captains''' were [[BX-series droid commando]]s at the rank of [[captain]] tasked with commanding their fellow BX-series [[droid]]s. Other commando droids with high [[Military rank|ranks]] also appeared the same. The standard design of these droid captains possessed [[Color|white]] head and chest markings. In practice, however, not all commando droid officers donned additional markings. Commando droid captains sometimes received edited programming in order to improve their leadership and improvisation.

==Characteristics==
[[File:RishiBXcommander.png|thumb|left|180px|The commando droid captain on the Rishi Moon.]]
Commando droid captains,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> like other [[BX-series droid commando|commando droids]], were programmed with improved combat tactics and [[Ground warfare|battlefield]] awareness.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Some received augmented programming that gave them an advantage in improvisation as well as leading other commando droids. These improvements made commando droid squads even more dangerous to clone troopers defending [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Planet|worlds]].<ref name="Join the Battle" />

The droids had glowing [[Color|white]] [[photoreceptor]]s, like normal commando droids, however, [[captain]]s and other high-[[Military rank|ranking]] commando droids bore white markings on their heads and chests,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and backs.<ref name="Rookies concept art gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/rookies-concept-art-gallery|text="Rookies" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 9}}</ref> However, not all captains<ref name="Citadel">{{TCW|The Citadel (episode)}}</ref> nor commando droid officers ended up donning those markings in practice.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> Just like other commando droids, the captains' midsection armor offered more flexibility over [[B1-series battle droid|B1 battle droids]], enhancing the droids' agility<ref name="Join the Battle" /> and acrobatics.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

==History==
[[Sparky|A command droid captain]] nicknamed "Sparky" by [[clone trooper]] [[Hevy]] led the [[Defense of Rishi Station|attack]] on [[Rishi Station]], which was tasked with transmitting warnings if the Confederacy ever tried to invade the [[Cloning|clone]] homeworld of [[Kamino]]. As per [[General]] [[Grievous]]'s orders, the droids needed to capture the outpost and maintain an all clear signal so as to keep Grievous's coming invasion of Kamino a secret. After landing in [[Droch-class boarding ship|boarding ships]] on Rishi moon, the droid killed clone trooper [[Droidbait]]. As he entered the listening post, he shot and killed clone trooper Sergeant [[O'Niner]], and another clone, [[CT-327]]. The remaining four clones escaped, and the captain thought they were inferior though one of the commando droids reported there were four missing clones. The droids took control of the base and replaced the alarm with an "all clear" signal. They contacted [[General]] [[Grievous]], who instructed the captain to continue transmitting the signal. When clone troopers [[Cody]] and [[Rex]] arrived, the droids destroyed their [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class attack shuttle]]. Later, the clones were able to take control of their station and destroy all the droid commandos, including their capitan.<ref name="TCWRookies" />

[[File:Commando_droids_with_blasters.png|thumb|left|300px|Three BX-series droid commando captains on Naboo.]]
One [[Unidentified BX-series droid commando captain (Citadel)|commando droid captain]] served at the [[Citadel]] and was present during the [[Battle of Lola Sayu]].<ref name="Citadel" /> The droids were also used by the Separatist [[Gungan]] [[Rish Loo]] on [[Naboo]] against [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]],<ref name="Shadow">{{TCW|Shadow Warrior}}</ref> and parts from commando droid captains were turned into [[monster droid]]s. One monster droid and another were used with other monster droids as target practice for [[Death Watch]], along with a commando droid captain that was still in one piece and retained its original parts.<ref name="Friend">{{TCW|A Friend in Need}}</ref>

The droids were also used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederacy]] during the [[Battle of Dathomir]],<ref name="Massacre">{{TCW|Massacre}}</ref> the [[invasion of Florrum]],<ref name="Bond">{{TCW|A Necessary Bond}}</ref> and the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]].<ref name="Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref> [[Standard year|Years]] after the end of the Clone Wars, surviving commando droid [[ND-5]] and his companion [[Kay Vess]] encountered the heavily-damaged but still active commando droid designated [[MT-7]], who believed the war was still ongoing. Claiming to be a commander, ND-9 ordered MT-7 to stand down, finally allowing his fellow BX to rest.<ref name="Outlaws" />
