A celestial body was the '''homeworld''' of the [[human]] [[Gender|woman]] [[Aris Ade]]. One diminutive hailing from there was "-chi."<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> By [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Ade had left her [[homeworld]] and become an [[Elder (Path of the Open Hand)|Elder]] in the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dalna]].<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> while speaking with the [[Evereni]] Path member [[Marda Ro]], Ade told Ro that the latter was becoming strong and capable, calling her "Marda-chi." Afterward, the Evereni [[Brain|mentally]] noted the use of the diminutive from the human's homeworld.<ref name="Path of Deceit" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aris Ade's homeworld was mentioned in the [[2022]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and [[Tessa Gratton]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase II">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> Within the novel, the homeworld was mentioned in a chapter written by Gratton.<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref>
