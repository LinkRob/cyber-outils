'''Boosters''' was a type of component that could provide a sudden burst of speed to a [[starship]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a set of boosters had been added to the [[EML-850 light freighter]], ''[[Trailblazer]]''. Later that same year while searching for the treasure of the [[Khepi]] in the [[Sayma Nebula]] an ion discharge struck the ''Trailblazer'' causing the boosters to go offline. The [[BX-series droid commando]] [[ND-5]] managed to divert power to the boosters allowing the ships [[pilot]] [[Kay Vess]] to boost through the ion clouds of the [[nebula]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Boosters appeared in the [[canon]] continuity in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They were identified as boosters in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion ''Star Wars Outlaws'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> 

Boosters were originally introduced in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity in ''[[Star Wars Galaxies: Jump to Lightspeed]]'' the first expansion of video game ''[[Star Wars Galaxies]]''<ref name="Jump to Lightspeed">''[[Star Wars Galaxies: Jump to Lightspeed]]''</ref> released on [[October 27]], [[2004]]<ref name="SWGMB">[[Star Wars Galaxies Memory Book|''Star Wars Galaxies'' Memory Book]]</ref>
