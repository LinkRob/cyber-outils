'''''Chewbacca Challenge''''' is a [[canon]] trivia board game produced by [[Paladone]]. The game requires players to answer ''[[Star Wars]]'' trivia questions to punish their opponents and "let the [[Wookiee]] win."<ref name="Amazon" />

==Publisher's summary==
''Join the struggle to remain on the good side of your furry friend by answering ''Star Wars'' trivia, rolling the dice and pitting your competitors against a fired-up Chewbacca. At all costs - let the Wookiee win!''<ref name="Amazon" />
