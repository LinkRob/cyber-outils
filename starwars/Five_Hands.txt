'''Five Hands''' was a childish<ref name="Explained">{{SW|url=news/andor-explained-season-2-highlights-episodes-1-2-3|text=''Andor'' Explained: Episodes 1-3 - "One Year Later", "Sagrona Teema", and "Harvest"}}</ref> hand-based game traditionally employed to settle disagreements between two parties. It featured fifteen distinct animal gestures&mdash;such as [[rancor]], [[snork]], and [[snoozbird]]&mdash;which the players showcased over the course of three rounds, under the supervision of a referee. A shortened version of the game followed the so-called [[Roski rules|Roski Rules]].<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=news/andor-trivia-guide-week-1|text=Secrets from the Set of ''Andor'' Season 2: Week 1 Trivia}}</ref>

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Five Hands first appeared in "[[Sagrona Teema]]," the second episode of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the web-TV series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''. It was crafted by actor [[Sam Gilroy]], who portrayed [[Gerdis]] in the episode, as the ''Star Wars'' equivalent of the real-life playground game [[Wikipedia:Rock paper scissors|Rock Paper Scissors]].<ref name="Trivia" />
