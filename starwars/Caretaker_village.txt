The '''Caretaker village''' was a settlement on a northern inlet of [[Ahch-To]]'s [[Temple Island]] inhabited by the [[Caretakers]]. Each [[Standard month|month]], the [[Festival of Return]] was held in this village, in which the [[Visitors]] would return from the [[Ocean|sea]] to deliver [[fish]] and be reunited with the Caretakers.<ref name="TLJVD" />
