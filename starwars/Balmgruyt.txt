'''Balmgruyt''' was a beverage that could be found on<ref name="ALD" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Chandrila]].<ref name="ALD" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> two [[New Republic pilot]]s that the former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[loyalty officer]] [[Sinjir Rath Velus]] dubbed "[[Mister Browscar]]" and "[[Miss Scowlface]]" entered a [[Junari Point]] [[Unidentified bar (Junari Point)|bar]] on Chandrila and briskly ordered balmgruyts from the [[Unidentified bartender (Junari Point)|bartender]] of the establishment, who left her post to fetch the drinks for them.<ref name="ALD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Balmgruyt was mentioned in ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', a [[2016]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the second installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="ALD" />
