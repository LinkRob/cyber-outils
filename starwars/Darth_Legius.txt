'''Darth Legius''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Sith (species)/Legends|Sith Pureblood]] who served the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|reconstituted Sith Empire]].

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Legius was intended to appear in Patch 1.3.0 ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', but was ultimately cut. The character exists within the game files with a unique model.
