'''''Ewoks Monthly''''' was a comic series published by [[Marvel Comics|Marvel UK]] featuring reprints of the US [[Star Wars: Ewoks (1985)|''Star Wars: Ewoks'' (1985)]] Marvel/[[Star Comics]] series.<ref name="Rebelscum" /> The stories were shown in a different order than the original comics, and only lasted ten issues versus the original fourteen.<ref name="Exploring">''A Galaxy Far, Far Away: Exploring Star Wars Comics''</ref>

*6: [[Ewoks (1985) 8|''Ewoks'' (1985) 8]]

*8: [[Ewoks (1985) 14|''Ewoks'' (1985) 14]]
