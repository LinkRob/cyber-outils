The '''Boonta Blaster''' was a [[Huttese]] weapon named for the infamous and [[Deity|deified]] [[Hutt]] who allegedly commissioned the first of its kind: [[Boonta Hestilic Shad'ruu]]. They were bejeweled weapons that cost 1,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and were often custom designed as ornamental scepters for a specific Hutt or passed down as family heirlooms.<ref name="Gadgets" />

Its [[blaster]] components were concealed beneath small fortunes in gems and other rare metals, and the energy beam was emitted from a massive [[gemstone]]. The Hutts relied on the Boonta Blaster as their last line of defense in case their [[bodyguard]]s were defeated, but were far more often used to [[Death penalty|execute]] those who displeased them.<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Boonta Blaster was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Gadgets" /> The blaster was originally mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[Lords of Nal Hutta]]'', a [[2015]] regional supplement to the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[roleplaying game]] series published by Fantasy Flight Games.<ref name="LoNH">''[[Lords of Nal Hutta]]''</ref>
