'''Devan''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[June 19]], [[2003]] and took the form of a biography of [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Devan For'deschel]], including her new appointment as [[Mistress of Lightsabers]] at the [[Almas Academy]]. This biography was accompanied by a number of quotations from For'deschel on issues relating to her role and the ongoing [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].
