In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> forces of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Defense Coalition]]<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> and [[Jedi Order]] [[Commanding officer|led]] by<ref name="Past Mistakes" /> [[Jedi Master]]<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> and [[Marshal]] [[Avar Kriss]]<ref name="Past Mistakes" /> '''engaged''' the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] organization on [[Magaveene]], an astronomical object<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> located in the [[Magaveene system]]<ref name="Past Mistakes" /> within the [[Prefsbelt sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> The battle was [[Nihil conflict|part]] of the [[Jedi]] and Republic's response&mdash;known as [[Operation: Counterstrike]]&mdash;to the [[Valo Atrocity|atrocity]] carried out by the Nihil on<ref name="Past Mistakes" /> the Outer Rim [[planet]]<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> [[Valo]], where it destroyed the [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] being held there.<ref name="Past Mistakes" />

As the battle was ongoing, the [[Frozian]]<ref name="Past Mistakes" /> [[Jedi Knight]]<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> [[Nooranbakarakana]] was sent to assist Kriss with a [[drift]] of [[Jedi Vector]] [[starfighter]]s from the Republic [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]].<ref name="Past Mistakes" /> The engagement ultimately resulted in victory for the Jedi and Republic forces.<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Kriss mentioned the Magaveene engagement while updating Jedi and defense coalition members aboard Starlight Beacon of the status of Operation Counterstrike shortly before the [[Battle of Galov]].<ref name="Tempest Runner script" />

==Behind the scenes==
The engagement on Magaveene was first mentioned in flashbacks in ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', a [[2021]] audio drama written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref>
