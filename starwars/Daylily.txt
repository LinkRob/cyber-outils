'''Daylilies''' were a type of [[lily]]. When [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Tep Tep]], [[Kildo]], and [[Gavi Takitaken|Gavi]] introduced themselves to [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]<ref name="EFV" /> in [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> the Padawan noticed that the former two gravitated toward the latter as their leader, similar to daylilies turning toward the [[Star|sun]].<ref name="EFV" />

==Behind the scenes==
Daylilies were mentioned in [[The High Republic: Escape from Valo|''Escape from Valo'']], a [[2024]] junior novel written by [[Daniel José Older]] and [[Alyssa Wong]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="EFV" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref>
