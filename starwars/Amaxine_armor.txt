'''Amaxine armor''' was a type of [[armor]] that was extremely valuable, as it never rusted or wore out. Created by the legendary [[Amaxine warriors]], some sets of the armor were discovered aboard the [[Amaxine Space Station|Amaxine space station]] in the wake of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]].<ref name="ITD" />
