'''Dermos''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Belsmuth sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. A [[Unidentified insurgent group (Dermos)|group]] of insurgents planning a raid on Dermos were apprehended by the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] in [[3 ABY]]. When [[New Republic pilot]] [[Wedge Antilles]] went [[Mission to Akiva|missing]] in [[4 ABY]], [[Ensign]] [[Ardin Deltura]] suggested Antilles might be at Dermos.

==Description==
Dermos was a [[planet]]<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> in the [[Dermos system]] of the [[Belsmuth sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> It fell within grid square O-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==History==
{{Quote|The five closest worlds to Raydonia offer a glimpse of where Captain Antilles could have been heading next.|Ardin Deltura|audio=Deltura-FiveWorlds-Aftermath.flac|<ref name="Aftermath" />}}
[[File:ISB_cog.svg|thumb|right|300px|alt=The white Imperial cog logo of the Imperial Security Bureau.|The Imperial Security Bureau stopped a raid planned for Dermos.]]
A [[Unidentified insurgent group (Dermos)|group]] of insurgents responsible for attacks on shipping [[Starship|vessels]] in the Outer Rim planned a raid on Dermos but were apprehended by the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The arrests were then covered in a [[HoloNet News]] broadcast by [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Dorna Parros]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the [[New Republic]] [[pilot]] [[Wedge Antilles]] went [[Mission to Akiva|missing]] while [[scout]]ing for [[Imperial Remnants|remnants]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] in the Outer Rim. As Raydonia was Antilles' last known location, [[Ensign]] [[Ardin Deltura]] suggested to [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] that the missing [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] may have jumped to one of the five worlds nearest to Raydonia, listing Dermos, [[Akiva]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in the [[Meerian sector]],<ref name="Akiva">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Akiva system]]</ref> [[Mustafar]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in the [[Atravis sector]],<ref name="CGttSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> and [[Tatooine]] and [[Geonosis]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in the [[Arkanis sector]].<ref name="CGttSWG" /> Ackbar believed Antilles visiting Dermos could make sense and had scouts sent to all five worlds to search for the pilot, who had in fact been captured on Akiva.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dermos was first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], which was written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
