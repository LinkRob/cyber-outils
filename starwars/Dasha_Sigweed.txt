'''Dasha Sigweed''' was an individual who advertised themselves on [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]], via a [[Dasha Sigweed (poster)|series of posters]].<ref name="Lethal">{{TCW|Lethal Trackdown}} et al.</ref> Fifteen [[Standard year|years]] after the Clone Wars ended,<ref name="Day">{{Rebels|Empire Day (episode)}}</ref> the same poster was used to decorate the walls of the ''[[Ghost]]''.<ref name="Spark">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dasha Sigweed's name appears in [[Aurebesh]] on posters seen in several episodes of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' TV series.<ref name="Lethal" /> The name is a play on the name of the European DJ duo [[Wikipedia:Sasha & John Digweed|Sasha & John Digweed]].<ref name="SWArchive">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode211.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Lightsaber Lost|date=2010-01-22|quote=Among the Aurebesh signs spotted around Coruscant are Star Tours posters suggesting trips to Glee Anselm, a distinctive "SODA" advertisement, a poster for a musical act called Stargazer and a performer named "Dasha Sigweed" (a name with a familiar ring to it)}}.</ref>
