The '''sister''' of [[Colli]] was a [[Tholothian]] infant who lived on the [[Al'doleem|river moon of Al'doleem]].

==Biography==
This infant was [[Reproduction|born]] to [[Jogg]] and [[Mareena]] prior to the [[Imperial Era]]. In [[19 BBY]], her [[Parent|father]] was babysitting them infant when he heard noises and came out with the infant. He was shocked to hear that [[Jedi Master]] [[Kirak Infil'a]] had come back for them to mend [[Kirak Infil'a's Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|his ship]], as it had been [[Standard year|years]] since he saw him last and most of the [[Jedi]] were [[Death|exterminated]] during [[Order 66]]. While they were mending his [[Starship|ship]], the infant was amazed when Infil'a [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to lift things up into the air. Then, Infil'a sensed [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] coming and dropped all the things he was carrying using the Force. Colli protected the infant and ran before they both got hurt. As they all witnessed Vader [[Mission to Al'doleem|challenging]] Infil'a, they all ran back into the [[Jogg and Mareena's garage|garage]] to fix Infil'a's ship.<ref name="Darth Vader 4" /> The infant and her brother managed to escape, though her parents were killed when Vader flooded the [[Am'balaar City|city]] by destroying a [[Am'balaar city dam|dam]].<ref name="Star Wars 19" />

==Personality and traits==
The infant had light skin and was amazed when Infil'a used the Force.<ref name="Darth Vader 4" />
