The '''Battle Deck Card Pack''' ('''SWQ125''') is an expansion for ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'', an asymmetrical two-player infantry-based wargame published by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. It was released on [[September 19]], [[2025]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Open war has spread across the galaxy. This card pack updates games of ''Star WarsTM: Legion'', giving players new objectives to achieve and battlefield layouts to discover. The Battle Card Pack 2025 includes 6 new Objective Cards, 6 new Map Cards, 6 new Secondary Objective Cards, and 6 new Advantage Cards.''

==Contents==
*24 Battle Cards
**6 Primary Objective Cards
**6 Map Cards
**6 Secondary Objective Cards
**6 Advantage Cards
