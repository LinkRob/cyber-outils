The '''BioTech Implant''' was a type of [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] implant manufactured by [[BioTech Industries]]. It was similar in appearance to an [[eye]].<ref name="GoH" /> The [[Bionic Eye]] was a cybernetic eye visually similar to a BioTech Implant.<ref name="LL Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/lightsaber-lost-episode-guide|text="Lightsaber Lost" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The BioTech Implant first appeared in the current [[canon]] [[2015]] mobile [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'', developed by [[EA Capital Games]]. In the game they are used as "Gear" to upgrade characters. There exist nine tiers of the BioTech Implant, designated Mk 1 to Mk 9, as well as "Salvage", "Prototype" and "Component" versions.<ref name="GoH" /> Like the other gear items, it was modeled by [[Brittany Hermann]],<ref name="HermannArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=naastika|name=[[Brittany Hermann]]|url=artwork/L3BvNv|text=Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes - Gear Icons|date=2021-01-13}}</ref> with additional help by<ref name="HermannForums1">{{EA|subdomain=forums|url=discussions/swgoh-strategy-and-tips-en/everything-you-never-needed-to-know-about-gear/4039897/replies/4039908|text=Reply|author=[[Brittany Hermann]]|date=2017-03-07}}</ref> [[Paul Martin]]<ref name="Martin1">{{ArtStation|username=morepaul|name=[[Paul Martin]]|url=artwork/8wwOxx|text=Star Wars Galaxy of Heroes - Gear Icons|date=2021-12-06}}</ref> and another artist.<ref name="HermannForums1" /> The [[Biotech implant]] originated in the [[2003]] [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] video game ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]'', developed by [[BioWare]],<ref name="KotOR">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''</ref> which was later bought by [[Electronic Arts|EA]] in [[2007]].<ref name="EA acquisition">{{WebCite|url=http://www.bioware.com/bioware_info/press_releases/2007_10_11_EA_to_acquire_bioware_pandemic/EA_to_acquire_bioware_pandemic.pdf|text=EA TO ACQUIRE BIOWARE CORP. AND PANDEMIC STUDIOS|date=2007-10-11|format=[[Wikipedia:PDF|PDF]]|work=[https://www.bioware.com bioware.com]|archivedate=20080512083113}}</ref> Hermann also said that she used the Bionic Eye of [[Lauli Wahlo]] from the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' as a reference.<ref name="HermannForums1" />
