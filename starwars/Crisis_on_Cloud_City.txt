The '''Crisis on Cloud City'''<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=articles/comics/star-wars-the-high-republic-the-finale-and-more-revealed-star-wars-celebration-japan|text='Star Wars: The High Republic - The Finale' and More Marvel 'Star Wars' Titles Revealed At Star Wars Celebration}}</ref> was an incident during the [[New Republic Era]] that saw the [[Nagai]] [[Garlev]] crash onto [[Cloud City]] on the planet [[Bespin]], where he demanded to speak to his old comrades [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]] and [[Han Solo]], former [[smuggler]]s with major ties to the [[New Republic]],<ref name="SW 3" /> which both had served as [[general]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> Joined by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]], Solo arrived on Bespin and met with Calrissian, but, by that point,<ref name="SW 3" /> a mysterious<ref name="Marvel" /> [[assassin]]<ref name="SW 3" />&mdash;secretly the [[Anzati]] [[Reyna Oskure]]<ref name="SW4" />&mdash;had already killed Garlev. The assassin then managed to escape aboard<ref name="SW 3" /> a new model of [[cloud car]].<ref name="Marvel" />
