'''Daiyu''' was a temperate [[planet]]<ref name="Part II" /> located in the [[Hocatar sector]]<ref name="SWApp" /> of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="CLNE" /> It was under the control of the [[Galactic Empire]] during its [[Imperial Era|reign]].<ref name="Part II" />

==Description==
Daiyu was a mostly lawless world that was largely covered in urban development. Criminals sold illicit goods openly, and [[bounty hunter]]s would visit the world's markets to see if they could find tips for profitable targets.<ref name="STDORVG">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> All signals in and out of Daiyu were blocked in order to protect the secrets of the locals.<ref name="Part II" />

[[Spice]] was dealt on the streets of [[Daiyu City]], bartered for credits and other valuables. Ornate spice canisters made of [[glass]] and [[copper]]-plated numinal were status symbols among local spice users. Spice dealers commonly gave away free samples to visitors, hoping the drug's addictive properties would create future customers.<ref>[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]</ref>

[[Qui-Gon Jinn]] considered Daiyu to be comparable to [[Nar Shaddaa]] in the number of cutthroats and killers that called the planet home.<ref>[[The Moon of Death]]</ref>

==History==
Around [[33 BBY]]<ref name="33 BBY">{{JangoFettSeriesDate}}</ref>, [[Vigor Struk]] attacked three [[Houk]] brothers outside the [[Two Blasters Bar]] in order to claim the [[Tracking fob|tracking fob]] for the bounty on the stolen [[Hope of Glee Anselm]].<ref>[[Jango Fett 1]]</ref> Later, all of the bar's patrons simultaneously noticed that an impressive bounty had been placed on [[Jango Fett]] and [[Aurra Sing]].<ref>[[Jango Fett 3]]</ref>

In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="KenobiDate">{{KenobiDate|I}}</ref> [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister]] of the [[Inquisitorius]] devised a plan to lure [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] out of hiding by learning of his relationship with [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]]. The Third Sister had [[Vect Nokru]] and his [[Black Mask|gang]] [[Ambush on Alderaan|kidnap]] Organa's daughter [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]].<ref name="Part I">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref> Kenobi tracked the gang to Daiyu, where [[Con artist|con artist]] [[Haja Estree]] helped him locate Leia in a cell in the back of a spice laboratory. Despite a hefty bounty placed on his head by the Third Sister and the intervention of the [[Fifth Brother]], [[Fourth Sister]], and [[Grand Inquisitor]], Kenobi managed to escape the planet with Leia.<ref name="Part II" />

Sometime after [[5 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{Marvel|url=articles/comics/star-wars-doctor-aphra-chaos-agent-1-cover-the-new-republic|text=Star Wars' Doctor Aphra Navigates the New Republic in New Ongoing Comic Series}} establishes the events of ''[[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent]]'' take place after the [[Battle of Jakku]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[5 ABY]].</ref>, agents of the [[New Republic|New Republic's]] [[Department of Artifacts and Archival Goods Retrieval]] tracked artifact dealer [[Baff Baksheen]] - and the [[Luovian Gauntlet]] he had been attempting to sell - to Daiyu. After two teams of D.A.G.G.R. agents went missing while investigating Baksheen, the Department sent [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] and [[Luke Skywalker]] to retrieve the gauntlet. The two confronted Baksheen in a cantina in Daiyu City. During the ensuing scuffle, the gauntlet bonded itself to Skywalker's mechanical hand and began to emit dangerous energies. However, Aphra was able to remove the gauntlet, which the two of them returned safely to the D.A.G.G.R.'s custody.

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|&hellip;sort of Hong Kong feel to it. It's got a graffiti-ridden nightlife, and is kind of edgy. It's just got a different lane and a different feeling.|[[Joby Harold]], to ''Entertainment Weekly'' about Daiyu|<ref name="Reveal">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/tv/obi-wan-kenobi-first-look-photos|text=See exclusive first look photos of ''Obi-Wan Kenobi''|author=Ross, Dalton|date=2022-03-09|archivedate=20220309140756}}</ref>}}
[[File:DaiyuConceptArt.jpg|thumb|right|210px|Concept art of the [[Daiyu marketplace]]]]
Daiyu is a planet created by writer [[Joby Harold]] for the ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' television series. It is based in part on the real-life cities [[Wikipedia:Hong Kong|Hong Kong]]<ref name="Reveal" /> and [[Wikipedia:Bangkok|Bangkok]].<ref name="Designing the Galaxy">{{HomeVideoCite|set=Obi-Wan Kenobi: The Complete Series|featurette=Designing the Galaxy}}</ref> It was first pictured in the form of concept art in the sizzle reel for the series released on [[Disney+]] in [[November]], [[2021]].<ref name="Sizzle reel">{{WebCite|url=https://www.starwarsnewsnet.com/2021/11/new-obi-wan-kenobi-sizzle-reel-arrives-ahead-of-disney-plus-day.html|text=UPDATE! First 'Obi-Wan Kenobi' Sizzle Reel Arrives for Disney Plus Day|author=Davis, Grant|date=2021-11-12|archivedate=20220526101004}}</ref> Originally, there were going to be different, more expensive versions of the scene of Kenobi in the [[spice]] lab, but it was decided that a [[Wikipedia:Methamphetamine|meth]] lab was more in reach of something writer [[Joby Harold]] and the crew could do production-wise. Harold thought it was a nice way for Kenobi to be undercover in a world foreign to him compared to his [[Ben Kenobi's cave|cave]] on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Leia Secret">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/obi-wan-kenobi-leia-disney-1235160356|text='Obi-Wan Kenobi' Writer Joby Harold Relieved Leia Was Kept Secret|author=Davids, Brian|date=2022-06-07|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20220607180506}}</ref>
