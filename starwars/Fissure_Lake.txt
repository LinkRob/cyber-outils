'''Fissure Lake''' was a [[Water|lake]] located on the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[planet]] of [[Celwis]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

In [[18 BBY]], [[Patriel]] [[Xodlak'oo'nifis]] was planning a trip to Fissure Lake. Instead, she traveled to [[Redhill Hall]] to meet with [[Councilor (Chiss Ascendancy)|Councilor]] [[Xodlak'uvi'vil]].<ref name="Greater Good" />
