'''Don Francks''' ([[February 28]], [[1930s#1932|1932]] &ndash; [[April 3]], [[2016]])<ref name="Hollywood Reporter" /> was an actor who voiced [[Jann Tosh]] and [[Gir Kybo Ren-Cha]] on ''Droids'' and  [[Unidentified Dulok (X)|an X-marked Dulok]], [[Umwak]] and [[Unidentified Dulok (O)|an O-marked Dulok]] on [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'']].

Francks was the father of [[Cree Summer]], who voiced [[Kneesaa a Jari Kintaka/Legends|Princess Kneesaa]] in ''Ewoks'' and other animated Star Wars characters.
