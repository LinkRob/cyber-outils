The '''Froswythe system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colonies]]. It was situated at the coordinates M-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Froswythe system was mentioned in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends]] continuity, it was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
