'''Flutes''' were a type of woodwind instrument. One type of flute was the [[reed flute]], which were used by members of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Ewok]] [[species]] native to the [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. The [[Growdi Harmonique]], another type of instrument, consisted of a combination of a flute and a [[water organ]].<ref name="AEYNTK" />
