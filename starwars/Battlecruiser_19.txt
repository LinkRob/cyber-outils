'''Battlecruiser 19''' was the [[callsign]] of a [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigate]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval forces]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. In [[21 BBY]], Battlecruiser 19 was one of many [[frigate]]s that served in the Confederacy's [[Ryloth blockade|blockade]] of the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] during the [[Battle of Ryloth]]. Placed under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Neimoidian]] [[Captain]] [[Mar Tuuk]], the frigate was positioned elsewhere by the Neimoidian, who was expecting an attack by [[Galactic Republic]] forces. Following the [[Fall of the Ryloth blockade|arrival]] of a Republic [[Skywalker's fleet|task force]] led by [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], Tuuk ordered Battlecruiser 19 and three other Munificents, including [[Battlecruiser 17]], to the [[Ryloth system]], forcing the Republic to retreat.

Sometime after their retreat, Skywalker's forces returned to the [[Star system|system]] for a second attack on the [[blockade]]. In their return, the Republic forces sent the [[starfighter]]s of [[Blue Squadron (Galactic Republic)|Blue Squadron]] commanded by Skywalker's [[Padawan]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], against the Separatist fleet following the destruction of Tuuk's [[Unidentified Lucrehulk-class battleship (Ryloth)|command ship]]. As a result, Battlecruiser 19 and the other Munificents were bombarded and destroyed by several [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]]s, thereby allowing the Republic to further its campaign to liberate Ryloth.

==Description==
The [[callsign]] of a [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigate]], Battlecruiser 19<ref name="Storm" /> was 825 [[meter]]s (2,707 [[Foot|feet]]) in length, 426 meters in width, and 243 meters in height<ref name="Size note" /> and was equipped with seven [[Ion engine|ion drive]] [[Engine|thrusters]] at its stern,<ref name="Engine note" /> a [[hyperdrive]] generator<ref name="Storm" /> and a class 1 hyperdrive,<ref name="Speed" /> and two long-range [[hyperwave transceiver]] masts.<ref name="Comms note" /> In terms of armament, Battlecruiser 19 possessed two heavy [[turbolaser]] [[cannon]]s mounted at its prow and two long-range heavy [[ion cannon]]s,<ref name="Weapons note" /> as well as a supplemental thirty-four light turbolaser cannons, seventeen on the port side and seventeen on the starboard side.<ref name="Rise weapons" /> Both the port and starboard sides of the [[frigate]]'s [[hull]] were emblazoned with the [[Separatist hex icon|emblem]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Storm" /> It could travel seventy [[Megalight per hour|megalights per hour]].<ref name="Speed" />

==History==
===Ensnaring the Republic===
{{Quote|Now is the time to bring in our reinforcements.|Mar Tuuk, in response to a Republic attack run on his command ship, orders reinforcements to the system|audio=Bring_in_our_reinforcements.flac|<ref name="Storm" />}}
[[File:Separatist-frigates-Ryloth.png|thumb|left|270px|Battlecruiser 19 and three other Munificents reinforce the Separatist blockade in the Ryloth system.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and Confederacy of Independent Systems,<ref name="Storm" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="22 BBY">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Supply Lines]]" to [[22 BBY]].</ref> the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] was [[Battle of Ryloth|invaded]] by the Confederate [[Separatist Droid Army|droid armies]] and an [[orbit]]al [[Ryloth blockade|blockade]] was established. [[Flagship|Led]] by a sole [[Unidentified Lucrehulk-class battleship (Ryloth)|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship]] and supplemented by six ''Munificent''-class star frigates, including Battlecruiser 19, the [[blockade]] was [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by the [[Neimoidian]] Separatist [[Captain]] [[Mar Tuuk]].<ref name="Storm" />

Sometime after the Confederacy's occupation and blockade of Ryloth,<ref name="Storm" /> in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="21 BBY" /> Tuuk's [[fleet]] was challenged by a Republic [[Skywalker's fleet|task force]] consisting of three [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] led by [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]. During the battle, [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s of the Republic's [[Blue Squadron (Galactic Republic)|Blue Squadron]], led by Skywalker's [[Padawan]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], began their [[Fall of the Ryloth blockade|attack run]] on Tuuk's [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Lucrehulk]] after a period of [[Starfighter combat|dogfighting]] with [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class droid starfighters]]. In response, the Neimoidian ordered Battlecruiser 19, [[Battlecruiser 17]], and two other Munificents, which had been positioned elsewhere in expectation of a Republic attack, to join their fight in the [[Ryloth system]].<ref name="Storm" />

As Battlecruiser 19 and the other frigates exited from [[hyperspace]] and assumed their designated attack positions, the Republic forces were caught off guard as Skywalker's Venators suffered extensive damage. Amid their forced withdrawal, the ''Venator''-class [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Redeemer]]'' was destroyed by the reinforced blockade and Battlecruiser 19.<ref name="Storm" />

===Destruction of the blockade===
{{Quote|Where is the Captain? What is going on?''"<br />"''He's in an escape pod.''"<br />"''Idiots! The blockade has been broken.|Wat Tambor, on the status of the blockade to one of the OOM command battle droids|audio=WhereIsTheCaptain.flac|<ref name="Storm" />}}
[[File:Breaking_Ryloth_blockade.png|thumb|right|270px|Battlecruiser 19 and the five other Munificents bombard the ''Resolute''{{'s}} hull.]]
Despite the Republic's retreat, Battlecruiser 19 and the other three frigates remained in the [[Star system|system]] alongside the blockade, rather than returning to their ambushing position. However, the Republic [[task force]] engaged the blockade once more, resulting in the loss of the Separatist flagship at the hands of the heavily damaged ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer ''[[Defender]]'', which was plummeted into the [[command ship]] by Skywalker. As the Lucrehulk was destroyed, the remaining Venator, the ''[[Resolute]]'', arrived in the system and proceeded to angle its ventral hull towards the Separatist frigates as part of Tano's<ref name="Storm" /> [[Marg Sabl]] tactic.<ref name="Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>

With the ''Resolute'' in position, Tano proceeded to direct a second starfighter squadron against the blockade as the [[OOM command battle droid]]s on the six frigates inquired as to what their current orders were following the command ship's destruction. In response, one of the droid commanders ordered the blockade to commence fire on the ''Resolute''. However, during their barrage of turbolaser fire, the Republic squadron's [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]]s assaulted and destroyed the six Munificents, including Battlecruiser 19. Amid their destruction, [[Techno Union]] [[Foreman]] [[Wat Tambor]] proclaimed the blockade's failure after being informed of the situation by [[Unidentified OOM command battle droid 2 (Ryloth blockade)|one]] of the droid commanders. With the blockade destroyed, a Republic [[Ryloth invasion fleet|invasion fleet]] led by Jedi Generals [[Mace Windu]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] arrived in the system and proceeded to land on the planet's surface, furthering the Republic campaign to liberate Ryloth from Separatist control.<ref name="Storm" />

==Commanders and crew==
{{Quote|I think the Captain left.''"<br />"''Who's in charge?''"<br />"''Not me.''"<br />"''Not me.|OOM command battle droids of the Ryloth blockade|audio=WhosInChargeNotMe.flac|<ref name="Storm" />}}
During the attack on the Confederacy's blockade over Ryloth, Battlecruiser 19 was commanded by an OOM command [[battle droid]]. After the loss of their command ship in the Republic's second assault, the droid commander convened with the OOM command battle droids of the five other Munificents as to which of them was in command of the battle. Uncertain as to who, all six droid commanders instead agreed to commence attack on the ''Resolute'' after it arrived.<ref name="Storm" />
