In [[4 ABY]], [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] launched an '''assault''' near [[Alzoc III]]. Reviewing [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] repair schedules, [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]] discovered that an entire [[Imperial Navy|Imperial fleet]] was converging at a repair deport near Alzoc III. Based on this intelligence, the Alliance's [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]] and the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]]' [[starship]] [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']] attacked the Imperial fleet, causing considerable damage to Imperial assets.

==Prelude==
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] embarked on a [[Quest to build the Arrowhead|quest]] to build a powerful starship called the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']] that could change the tide of the war.<ref name="TTOAN">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Tower of Alistan Nor}}</ref> After building the ship, the Freemakers used the ''Arrowhead'' to save the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] from destruction<ref name="FOTA">{{FreemakerAdventures|Flight of the Arrowhead}}</ref> and later inflicted a [[Skirmish on Kashyyyk (Freemakers)|defeat]] on [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces on [[Kashyyyk]].<ref name="APR" />

Due to her service, [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]] was promoted to the Alliance's Strategy Council. Though the Rebel Alliance planned to raid the planet [[Dubrillion]], Kordi learned that an Imperial fleet was converging on the Imperial repair depot above [[Alzoc III]] after researching Imperial repair schedules. She convinced [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] to authorize a raid on that station and the Imperial ships. Ackbar assigned Valeria with leading the assault and tasked Kordi with briefing the lieutenant.<ref name="APR" />

==The raid==
At least eleven [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyers]] including the [[Imperial officer]] [[Estoc]]'s flagship [[Desolator (Imperial-class)|''Desolator'']] docked at the Imperial repair facility near [[Alzoc III]]. Estoc was accompanied by the [[Unidentified AT-AT commander (Kashyyyk)|AT-AT commander]] whose forces had been [[Skirmish on Kashyyyk (Freemakers)|defeated]] by the Freemakers on Kashyyyk. Since the Imperial bureaucracy had overbook the repair facility, it was tightly packed with Star Destroyers.<ref name="APR" />

Shortly later, Lieutenant Valeria's [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]] exited hyperspace. Underestimating the threat of the rebels, Estoc ordered that the alarms be switched off and for the Star Destroyers to open fire on the [[X-wing starfighter]]s. Shortly later, Kordi's brother [[Zander Freemaker]] exited [[hyperspace]], flying the ''Arrowhead''.<ref name="APR" />

Zander uses the ''Arrowhead''{{'s}} [[embersteel]] blade to slice of the bridge of a Star Destroyer. Aware of the devastation that the ''Arrowhead'' wrought on Imperial forces on Kashyyyk, the AT-AT commander tried to convince Estoc to order a retreat. However, an over-confident Estoc instead ordered the Star Destroyers to open fire on the ''Arrowhead''.<ref name="APR" />

Zander sliced a second Star Destroyer in half before ploughing through several more Destroyers, destroying the capital ships. Estoc managed to trap the ''Arrowhead'' with her Star Destroyer's [[tractor beam projector]]. However, Valeria disabled the tractor beam projector, allowing Zander to slice through the Star Destroyer. The ''Arrowhead'' and Blue Squadron then departed, leaving the wrecked Star Destroyers behind.<ref name="APR" />

==Aftermath==
Estoc and the AT-AT commander survived the rebel assault with the latter telling the former that the ''Arrowhead'' was the fight he had encountered on Kashyyyk. A jubilant Zander returned home to the rebel [[MC80 Star Cruiser]] ''[[Home One]]'' where he was greeted by a triumphant Kordi. However, their victory was cut short when their [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]] arrived with news that [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] had captured their younger brother [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]].<ref name="APR" />

Despite the objections of the [[Alliance High Command]], the Freemakers embarked on a [[Rescue of Rowan Freemaker|mission]] to rescue Rowan from the [[Imperial Palace]] on [[Coruscant]], bringing them into conflict with the Imperial [[Hunter droid/LEGO|hunter droid]] [[M-OC/LEGO|M-OC]].<ref name="EFC" />
