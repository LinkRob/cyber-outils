The'''Broadberry Meadow''' was an [[alcohol]]ic beverage served at [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> The [[Color|yellow]]-colored [[cocktail]] was suitable for [[human]] consumption and garnished with edible [[flower]]s. It shared its name with a location in [[Naboo]]'s capital [[city]] [[Theed]].<ref name="Oga's Cantina" />
