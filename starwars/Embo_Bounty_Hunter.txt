The '''Embo Bounty Hunter''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Bounty Hunter (Imara Vex)|standard costume]]<ref name="Hunters" /> as of the [[Standard year|year]] [[9 ABY]].<ref name="HuntersDate">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> The [[Clothing|outfit]] and [[armor]] resembling the aesthetic of the outfit commonly worn by the [[Kyuzo]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Embo]], featuring various shades of [[Color|brown]], gray, red and yellow, with with ornate patterns on the coat and pants, a glowing yellow visor on the [[helmet]], and an angular [[shoulder-mounted armament]].<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Embo retired on Felucia and lived the life of a farmer after his bounty hunting stint - I wonder if I'll do something similar when it's all over?|Imara Vex|<ref name="Hunters" />}}
Embo Bounty Hunter was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Imara Vex, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Embo Rifle]]. In the description for the item, Vex identifies Embo as being retired as of the events of the game<ref name="Hunters" /> in 9 ABY,<ref name="HuntersDate" /> which was later contradicted by Embo serving as an active bounty hunter<ref name="Mandalorian and Grogu">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref> during or after [[11 ABY]]<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoDate|MandalorianAndGrogu}}</ref> in the [[2026]] film ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''.<ref name="Mandalorian and Grogu" /> Being the more recent source, this article follows the version of events put forward by ''The Mandalorian and Grogu'' and disregards the mention of Embo being retired.
