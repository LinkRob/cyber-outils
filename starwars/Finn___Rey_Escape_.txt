'''''Finn & Rey Escape!''''' is a young readers storybook written by [[Michael Siglain]] and published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] that adapts scenes from [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. It was published on [[December 18]], [[2015]], comprises 32 pages, and includes stickers.<ref name="Hachette" /><ref name="New Books">{{SW|url=news/a-galaxy-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens-books-coming-december-18-first-look|text=A Galaxy of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Books Coming December 18 - UPDATED!}}</ref> An [[Finn & Rey Escape! (audiobook)|audio version]] of the book was released on [[Audible]] on [[September 19]], [[2024]]<ref name="Audiobook">[[Finn & Rey Escape! (audiobook)|''Finn & Rey Escape!'' audiobook]]</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
''When Finn and Rey find themselves on the run, they must work together to escape from the evil First Order''.<ref name="New Books" />
