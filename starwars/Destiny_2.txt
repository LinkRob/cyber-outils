'''''Destiny 2''''' is a free-to-play [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online game|MMO]] [[Wikipedia:First-person shooter|FPS]], created<ref name="Bungie Website" /> and currently published by video game development company, [[Bungie]],<ref name="Publisher" /> which launched on [[February 6]], [[2017]].<ref name="Destiny 2 Announcement" /> The first ''Destiny 2 x [[Star Wars]]'' collaboration was announced on [[January 28]], [[2025]], which would begin following week alongside the launch of ''Destiny 2's'' [[Wikipedia:Destiny 2: The Final Shape|''"Episode III: Heresy – 'Act I'"'']] on [[February 4]], 2025.<ref name="Livestream Reveal" />

==Development==
{{Quote|Now it's time to take a first look at the newest collaboration that's going to be coming to ''Destiny 2,'' and this one is joining us from a galaxy far, far away. ''[&hellip;]'' Our next collaboration is inspired by a universe that's loved by so many of us at the studio, ''Star Wars.''|Bungie senior social media manager, Andy Salisbury, announcing the ''Destiny 2 x Star Wars'' collaboration during the ''Destiny 2: Heresy Act I Developer Livestream''|<ref name="Livestream Reveal">{{YouTube|video=hMKjQaJdZvg|text=Destiny 2: Heresy Act I Developer Livestream|channel=@destinythegame|name=Destiny 2}}</ref>}}
[[File:Destiny-2-X-Star-Wars-Collab.jpg|left|250px|thumb|All 3 ornament sets for ''Destiny 2''.]]
This initial collaboration between Bungie and [[Lucasfilm Games]] brought with it a number of "Imperial-themed" cosmetics, including three armor ornament sets, one for each in-game character class, those being the "Shock Enforcer Armor," a [[Stormtrooper]]-inspired set for "Titans," the "Royal Protector Armor," an [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Imperial Royal Guard]]-inspired set for "Warlocks," and the "Covert Ranger Armor," a [[Death trooper]]-inspired set for "Hunters."<ref name="In-Game Info">''[[Destiny 2]]''</ref>

The promotion also features the "DS-2 Shell," a [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]]-inspired "Ghost Shell," as well as the "Advanced Prototype Jumpship," a [[TIE Advanced x1]]-inspired "Ship," the "Repulsorlift Jumpspeeder," a [[74-Z speeder bike]]-inspired "Sparrow," the "Unlimited Power," "Paracausal Pitch," and "Riot Control" Finishers, and the "Remote Training Session," "Infantry Practice," and "Fated Duel" Emotes, all available for purchase via the game's "Silver" digital currency.<ref name="Livestream Reveal" />

[[File:DS-2-Ghost-Shell.jpg|right|250px|thumb|The first "Equipment" cosmetic shown off for the collab, the "DS-2 Shell."]]
Each of these items could be purchased individually, however, the armor ornaments were also listed together in the in-game store as the "Galactic Imperial Set," the equipment could be purchased as a bundle referred to as the "Galactic Imperial Set," and the finishers and emotes were distributed as the "Interstellar Warfare Finishers Bundle" and "Interstellar Warfare Emotes Bundle" respectively.<ref name="In-Game Info" />

The key art used to reveal the ''Destiny 2 x Star Wars'' collaboration was created by the animator "[[Drexis Animations]]."<ref name="Drexis Animations">{{Twitter|Drexis_Anim|status/1884333859250303210|[[Drexis Animations]]|quote=I never would have expected to get to work on something like this but&hellip; BRUH. I got to make this key art for Destiny 2 and Star Wars.|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/f286a}}</ref> Video editor [[Zackery Brown]] also revealed that working on the aforementioned collab was his final project as Bungie Capture Team Leader.<ref name="Zackery Brown">{{Twitter|Zxckery|status/1884325180279079334|[[Zackery Brown]]|quote=Alongside everything else Heresy, this was my final project on the Capture Team & I cannot wait for the world to see it! Exciting things happening, & I am just glad to be apart of it|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/nnupu}}</ref> Both [[Anthony Huls]], a freelance gameplay capture artist,<ref name="Anthony Huls">{{Twitter|Ansonikage|status/1884319227794055208|[[Anthony Huls]]|quote=By far the most exciting thing to work on in secret and it feels so good to see it shared with you all. Can't wait for you to see what we cooked up...|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/DWRkU}}</ref> and Bungie social media manager [[Mitch Alpiner]],<ref name="Mitch Alpiner">{{Twitter|MoltenSlowa|status/1884401199082054075|[[Mitch Alpiner]]|quote=life goal achieved: write social copy that appears on a Lucasfilm channel|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/Mj8hN}}</ref> also contributed to the collaboration in some capacity, the former helping in the capture and creation of promotional material,<ref name="Anthony Huls" /> and the latter devising the official announcement posts for both Bungie and Lucasfilm Games.<ref name="Mitch Alpiner" />

[[File:Dark-Side-Legends-Bundle.jpg|left|250px|thumb|The "Dark Side Legends Bundle" as part of the pre-order bonus for the "''Destiny 2'': Year of Prophecy Ultimate Edition"]]
On [[May 6]], 2025, a ''Destiny 2'' developer livestream was held to promote the upcoming "Year of Prophecy," the first chapter in the game's "The Fate Saga," where the pre-order bonuses<ref name="Edge of Fate">{{YouTube|video=hMKjQaJdZvg|text=Destiny 2: The Edge of Fate Reveal Event|channel=@destinythegame|name=Destiny 2}}</ref> for the game's "Destiny 2: Year of Prophecy Ultimate Edition" were revealed, showcasing three new sets of in-game armor ornaments designated the "Dark Side Legends Bundle" inspired by iconic Star Wars villains. This included the "Obsidian Durasteel Set" inspired by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the "Cyborg Nemesis Set" inspired by [[General]] [[Grievous]], and the "Marauder Progeny Set" inspired by [[Ben Skywalker|Kylo Ren]].<ref name="Dark Side Legends Bundle">{{Twitter|DestinyTheGame|status/1919798852753690988|''Destiny 2''|quote=Power is the only code you follow. Don the armor of legends with Star Wars–inspired Dark Side armor ornament sets for Hunter, Titan, and Warlock. Included in the Destiny 2: Year of Prophecy Ultimate Edition. http://bung.ie/teof|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/ektaX}}</ref>

[[File:Destiny-2-Renegades-Reveal.jpg|right|250px|thumb|Key art for the ''Destiny 2'' expansion "Renegades"]]
These cosmetics were made available the day of the stream, making them some of the final in-game items to be introduced in Destiny's final piece of "Year 10" content, ''"Episode III: Heresy &ndash; 'Act III'"''.<ref name="Edge of Fate" /> The moment the pre-order bonuses became available, a bundle of in-game cosmetics titled the "Clandestine Cargo Set" was also made available for purchase with "Silver." This set included the "Inferno Shell," a [[Dio]] the [[Inferno Squad]] [[ID10 seeker droid]]-inspired "Ghost Shell," the "Silent Vendetta," a [[TIE/vn space superiority fighter|TIE vendetta space superiority fighter]] aka [[Kylo Ren's TIE silencer|TIE silencer]]-inspired "Ship," the "Crescent Infiltrator," a [[FC-20 speeder bike]] ''[[Bloodfin]]''-inspired "Sparrow."<ref name="In-Game Info" />

{{Quote|It is an essential 'Fate Saga' story, and it's an homage to a universe that has inspired Bungie from the start. It's a universe that all of us on the couch grew up with as well as everyone else at the studio and millions of you back at home too. ''[&hellip;]'' It is a peanut butter and chocolate combination inside of what we're making that could only be possible in the kind of world that we build here. And so I am absolutely thrilled to be able to give our first sneak peak at '''Destiny 2'': Renegades.'|Destiny narrative director, Alison Lührs, announcing the ''Star Wars''-inspired expansion ''Destiny 2'': Renegades" during the ''Destiny 2: The Edge of Fate Reveal Event''|<ref name="Edge of Fate" />}}
During the May 6 reveal livestream, Destiny narrative director, Alison Lührs, showcased a teaser for the second expansion of the "The Fate Saga," previously titled "Codename: Behemoth" but now confirmed to be entitled "''Destiny 2'': Renegades." "Renegades" was stated to be an entirely Star Wars-themed expansion, taking many cues from the galaxy far, far away, including OST, sound effects, story, and setting, taking place within locations colloquially referred to as a "lawless frontier," with a narrative described as being very "space western," but still with a distinctly Destiny-centric story.<ref name="Edge of Fate" />

[[File:RenegadeLeader-Destiny2PreOrder.jpg|left|250px|thumb|The "Destiny 2: Renegades Pre-Order Pack" included an exclusive emblem and ship]]
Pre-orders specifically for "Renegades" also included the "Starside Introduction" in-game emblem<ref name="Renegades Pre-Order">{{WebCite|url=https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/destiny-2-renegades-pre-order-pack/9NWVX3B7VN8H|text=Destiny 2: Renegades Pre-Order Pack|work=[xbox.com Xbox]|archivedate=20251202193744}}</ref> and an additional ship, the "Renegade Leader," inspired by the iconic [[X-wing starfighter]] and including a dynamic robotic element resembling an [[astromech droid]].<ref name="X-Wing">{{Twitter|username=DestinyTheGame|url=status/1965520644256252079|name=Destiny 2|date=2025-09-09|quote=Send shadows into retreat. Pre-purchase Destiny 2: Renegades and claim a Renegade Leader Exotic Ship of your own today. Destiny 2: Renegades // December 2, 2025. http://bung.ie/renegades|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/h3ZyA}}</ref> A bug accompanied the release of this pre-order ship, causing another ship added as a reward within the "Ash & Iron" season Reward Pass, the "Autognosis," to instead be erroneously share the name and bio of the "Renegade Leader," though this was eventually resolved.<ref name="In-Game Info" />

A second ''Destiny 2'' Developed Livestream took place on [[November 25]], [[2025]], showcasing a number of aspects of the upcoming expansion, though focusing on a few key elements such as the effort taken to strike a balance between the ''Destiny'' and ''Star Wars'' franchises, the character designs and voice acting, the expansion's OST, and the new weapons coming to the expansion, primarily the [[Lightsaber]]-inspired "Praxic Blade." The rest of the stream featured gameplay footage showing off said new weapons, as well we abilities, activities, enemies, and menus, before ending with the official "Renegades" story trailer.<ref name="Final Livestream">{{YouTube|video=nrsypZ-P06o|text=Destiny 2: Renegades Developer Livestream|channel=@destinythegame|name=Destiny 2|archivedate=20251126175103}}</ref>

===''Destiny 2: Renegades===
[[File:MandalorianArmor-Destiny2.jpg|right|250px|thumb|Mandalorian-themed armor ornaments added alongside the launch of ''Destiny 2'': Renegades]]
"''Destiny 2'': Renegades" launched on [[December 2]], [[2025]]. Accompanying the expansion was a slew of new in-game items inspired by ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian|The Mandalorian]]'', including a Finisher where the user fires a flurry of [[whistling bird]] constructs from their wrist, as well as the "Galactic Tech Set" featuring the "Cargo Runner," a ''[[Razor Crest]]''-inspired "Ship," the "Marshal's Colt," a "Sparrow" based on [[Cobb Vanth's speeder]], and the "Mechanical Assassin Shell," a "Ghost Shell" resembling the disembodied head of an [[IG-series]] like [[IG-11]]. Also included within this promotional tie-in was a trio of [[Mandalorian armor]] ornament sets, the "Singular Mercenary Set" inspired by the [[Boba Fett's armor|armor]] by [[Boba Fett]], specifically within ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'', the "Devoted Warrior Set" inspired by the [[Color|silver]] [[Din Djarin's armor|armor]] worn by [[Din Djarin]], and the "Noble Partisan" inspired by the [[Nite Owls]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze's Mandalorian armor|armor]] worn by [[Bo-Katan Kryze]].<ref name="In-Game Info" />

Additional obtainables inspired by ''Star Wars'' but not specifically ''The Mandalorian'' include the "Winning Beat" Emote, where the emoter drums on a trio of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[helmet]]s [[Ewok]]-style, in addition to three "Praxic Blade" customization sets. These included the "Praxic Embellishment Set," featuring both the "Golden Age Hilt" and "Dark Matter Crystal: Gradient," the "Jewel of Power Set," featuring both the "Kingsblade" and "Dark Matter Crystal: White," and the "Reefblade Set," featuring both the "Royal Guard Hilt" and "Dark Matter Crystal: Purple." All of these new ''Star Wars''-inspired items, as well as every cosmetic from both of the previous collaborations and pre-orders, could be found under a brand new section within the game's "Eververse Store" dubbed the "Legends" tab, all available via the game's "Silver" digital currency.<ref name="In-Game Info" />

[[File:LegendaryWeapons-Destiny2.jpg|left|250px|thumb|The roster of ''Star Wars''-inspired Legendary-rarity weapons added alongside ''Destiny 2'': Renegades]]
Six Legendary-rarity ''Star Wars''-inspired weapons were also added within "Renegades." These in included the "All or Nothing" Pulse Rifle, resembling [[Boba Fett]]'s [[EE-3 carbine rifle]], the "Modified B-7 Pistol" Hand Cannon, resembling [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor's]] [[MW-20 Bryar pistol]], the "Refurbished A499" Sniper Rifle, resembling [[Din Djarin]]'s [[Amban phase-pulse blaster]], the "Uncivil Discourse" Hand Cannon, resembling [[Han Solo]]'s [[Han's heavy blaster pistol|modified]] [[DL-44 heavy blaster pistol]], the "Compact Defender" Sidearm, resembling [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Princess Leia's]] [[Satine's Lament|personal]] [[Defender sporting blaster pistol]], and the "M-17 'Fast Talker'" Submachine Gun, resembling the [[DC-17m Interchangeable Weapon System|DC-17m Repeating Blaster Rifles]] commonly utilized by [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[clone commando]]s.<ref name="November TWID">{{WebCite|url=https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/Article/twid_11_26_2025|text=This Week in Destiny - 11/26/2025|work=[bungie.net Bungie]|archivedate=20251205011704}}</ref>

One of the key elements of the collaboration was the "Praxic Blade," Destiny's very own version of the [[lightsaber]]. This Exotic-rarity weapon could be obtained as one of the steps within the "Renegades" campaign, specifically as a reward for completing the Exotic Mission "Fire and Ice," which sees the player enter on a temple on the icy moon of Europa and face a gauntlet of trials to earn their very own blade, similar to the process [[Jedi Initiate]]s underwent on the [[planet]] [[Ilum]] to obtain their first [[Kyber crystal]]. After engaging with a mix of parkour, puzzles, and combats, players would reach a chest which would grant them the Praxic Blade, before being allowed to test its capabilities on one final wave of enemies.<ref name="In-Game Info" />

[[File:PraxicBlade-Destiny2.jpg|right|250px|thumb|The lightsaber-like "Praxic Blade" alongside a couple of its interchangeable "Dark Matter Crystals"]]
Once acquired, the Praxic Blade features four major forms of customization upon examination, including swapping out its "Dark Matter Crystal," its "Focus," its "Form," and its "Core." Nearly all of these mods will be locked until players complete certain objectives which make them available to equip. "Cores" and "Dark Matter Crystals" specifically offer a unique challenge to find, with the former changing the entire playstyle of the weapon, while the latter changes the color of the blade. One of these crystals, either "Blue" or "Green" will be unlocked by default but at random upon initially obtaining the weapon, with the other earnable within the "Praxic's Path Quest." "Red," "Orange," "Cyan," "Indigo," and "Magenta" crystals could be dropped randomly from "Renegades"- specific multiplayer modes. "White," "Gradient," and "Purple" were available to purchase in individual bundles within the "Eververse Store." "Yellow" was the reward for completing the "Praxic Professional Exotic Order." And the [[Darksaber]]-like "Taken" crystal is obtainable exclusively within the "Equilibrium" Dungeon.<ref name="In-Game Info" />

===Equilibrium===
[[File:EquilibriumKeyArt-Destiny2.jpg|left|250px|thumb|Key art used to reveal the entrance to the "Equilibrium" Dungeon]]
The endgame "Dungeon" activity associated with "Renegades"<ref name="November TWID" /> named "Equilibrium" became available to play on [[December 13]], 2025,<ref name="Launch Blog">{{WebCite|url=https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/Article/renegades_launch_blog|text=Destiny 2: Renegades Launch Blog|work=[bungie.net Bungie]|archivedate=20251205010108}}</ref> first open only to qualified three-man fireteams during the activity's "Contest Mode,"<ref name="In-Game Info" /> before becoming available to all players once after 48 hours or when the special mode has been completed by at least one team, whichever comes first. The primary rewards for completing the dungeon were Exotic-rarity Combat Bow named "Heirloom,"<ref name="December TWID">{{WebCite|url=https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/Article/twid_12_04_2025|text=This Week in Destiny - 12/04/2025|work=[bungie.net Bungie]|archivedate=20251205012416}}</ref> inspired by [[Chewbacca's bowcaster]], as well as the "Dark Matter Crystal: Taken" mod, which changed the Praxic Blade's appearance to resemble the Darksaber,<ref name="In-Game Info" /> though acquiring either award is not guaranteed upon completion of "Equilibrium" outside of the now concluded "Contest Mode" race.<ref name="December TWID" />

Six other Legendary-rarity weapons were also available to earn for completing encounters within "Equilibrium," including the "Conspiracy Honed" Sniper Rifle, resembling the [[DLT-19x targeting blaster]], the "Zealous Ideal" Auto Rifle, resembling the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]], the "High Tyrant" Pulse Rifle, resembling the [[A280C blaster rifle]], the "Voltaic Shade" Scout Rifle, resembling the [[EL-16HFE blaster rifle]], the "Bitter End" Machine Gun, resembling the [[Z-6 rotary blaster cannon]], and the "Sullen Claw" Sword, resembling the [[vibrosword]]s commonly utilized by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|CIS]] [[BX-series droid commando]]s. Additional ''Star Wars''-inspired dungeon rewards included the "Sage Protector" Legendary armor sets available for each class, all resembling the [[Jedi apparel|clothing]] worn by members of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. There were also two pieces of earnable equipment, the "Static Data Shell" Ghost Shell resembling a [[Jedi holocron]], and the "Arid Rambler" Sparrow, resembling [[Luke Skywalker]]'s [[X-34 landspeeder]], as well as two themed "Shaders," the "Praxic Drape" and the "Imperium Parade," inspired by the Jedi Order and Galactic Empire aesthetics respectively.<ref name="In-Game Info" />
