'''''Ahsoka'' 3''' is the third issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries [[Star Wars: Ahsoka (comic series)|''Star Wars: Ahsoka'' comic adaptation]]. It is an adaptation of the [[Part Three: Time to Fly|third episode]] of the television series [[Star Wars: Ahsoka|''Ahsoka'']]. The issue was written by [[Rodney Barnes]], illustrated by [[Steven Cummings]], [[Wayne Faucher]], and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[September 25]], 2024.

==Publisher's summary==
''TIME TO FLY! Hera tangles with New Republic politics while Ahsoka and Sabine Wren voyage to a distant planet!''
