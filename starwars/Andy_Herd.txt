'''Andy Herd''' is an actor. He appeared in [[1983]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|Return of the Jedi]]'',<ref name="Dusts Off" /> where he was credited as '''Andrew Herd'''.<ref name="Return of the Jedi">[[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]''</ref> Herd played both an [[Ewok]] and a [[Jawa]] named [[Kalit]].<ref name="Dusts Off" /><ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=Jabba's Palace Limited|parent=1}}</ref>

Herd was living in [[Wikipedia:Hertfordshire|Hertfordshire]] when he saw a newspaper ad recruiting dwarfs for the film, and the next day, he found himself at [[Wikipedia:Elstree Studios|Elstree Studios]].<ref name="Perfectly Performed">{{WebCite|url=https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/opinion/latest/1939263.small-perfectly-performed/|text=Small, but perfectly performed|work=The Northern Echo|date=2008-01-03|archivedate=20260323081809}}</ref> Herd and fellow Jawa actor [[Brian Wheeler]] feature in a deleted scene in [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]], where they use rubber [[Blaster rifle|rifles]] knock on [[Luke Skywalker]]'s hands as he grabs onto the bottom of the grate covering the [[Pateesa|rancor]] pit.<ref name="YouTube">{{YouTube|video=YmFNpw1aVfE|text=Deleted Jawa Scene Secrets - Andy Herd reveals all - Star Wars 100 Interviews|channel=@StarWars100Interviews|name=Star Wars 100 Interviews|archivedate=20260323132529}}</ref>

Herd has also appeared in movies such as ''[[Wikipedia:Labyrinth (1986 film)|Labyrinth]]'' and ''[[Willow (film)|Willow]]''.<ref name="Perfectly Performed" />
