'''Darth Malted Milkshake''' was a [[Color|pink]] beverage that shared part of its name with the [[Darth]] title<ref name="3rdMath" /> used by members of the [[Sith]].<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Malted Milkshake was introduced in ''[[Star Wars Workbook: 3rd Grade Math]]'', written by [[Claire Piddock]] and [[Megan Butler]] and published in [[2017]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars Workbooks]]'' series. The beverage was featured as part of a hypothetical math problem in which ten [[Liter/Legends|liters]] of Darth Malted Milkshake were made in [[Mos Eisley]],<ref name="3rdMath" /> a [[spaceport]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]],<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> and split between three containers.<ref name="3rdMath" />
