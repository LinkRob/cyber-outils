'''Bralem Mopak''' was a [[Nimbanel]] [[Gender|male]] criminal and [[Merchant|trader]]. Mopak ran an operation on [[Arbra]], holding the resident [[Hoojib]]s in cages and selling them to other criminal groups and individuals so that the beings&mdash;who most assumed to be simple animals&mdash;could be forced to [[spy]] on his targets and send intelligence back to Mopak on Arbra. Mopak's operation was brought to an end when the Hoojibs, led by [[Plif]] and assisted by the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]], escaped and fought back against the Nimbanel. He was taken [[prisoner]] by the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]], who had lost out on a deal with [[Zikk Keradd]] because of Mopak.<ref name="The Hoojib Menace" />
