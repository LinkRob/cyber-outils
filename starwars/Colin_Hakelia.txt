'''Colin Hakelia''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] [[lieutenant]]<ref name="UVG" /> of the [[Galactic Empire]] stationed at the [[Imperial Center of Military Research|Imperial security complex]] on [[Scarif]]. He was impersonated by [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] when [[Rogue One]] landed on the [[planet]], knocked Hakelia and fellow Imperial [[Kent Deezling]] unconscious and stole their uniforms to infiltrate the complex.<ref name="Rogue One" />
