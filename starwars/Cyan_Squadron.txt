'''Cyan Squadron''' was a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[airspeeder]] [[squadron]] stationed on the [[planet]] [[Vrogas Vas]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The squadron operated [[T-47 airspeeder]]s and operated out of the [[Rebel Refueling Base]] there.<ref name="Vader Down 2" />

==Description==
Cyan Squadron consisted of [[T-47 airspeeder]]s and were stationed at the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Rebel Refueling Base|refueling base]] on the [[planet]] [[Vrogas Vas]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Vader Down 2" />

==History==
{{Quote|I can see him.''"<br />"''All units converge. Form a perimeter. Begin attack run, Cyan Three''"<br />"''Affirmative, commander.|Cyan Three and Leia Organa |<ref name="Vader Down 2" />}}
In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' places the Battle of Vrogas Vas in the [[Standard year|year]] immediately following the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' states concluded in [[0 ABY]]. Therefore, the Battle of [[Vrogas Vas]] must have taken place in 0 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] initiated a surprise [[Battle of Vrogas Vas|attack]] on the Rebel Alliance at their base on Vrogas Vas, that started with Vader's complete destruction of [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue]] and [[Yellow Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Yellow Squadrons]]. While Vader was locked in a space battle with [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]], [[Luke Skywalker]] ([[Red Five]]) intentionally rammed Vader's [[TIE Advanced x1]] starfighter with his own, causing both to crash-land on the planet below. Cyan Squadron's T-47 airspeeders were dispatched to locate the two crash sites and [[Death|kill]] Vader if possible. [[Cyan Leader]] and [[Cyan Two]] were unable to locate the [[Sith Lord]], but [[Cyan Three]] encountered Vader with fatal results &mdash; Vader had stripped his downed TIE of its weapons array and used it to destroy Cyan Three.<ref name="Vader Down 2" />

In the aftermath, [[Commander]] [[Arhul Narra]] of Red Squadron, described the event as one of the worst cullings of starfighter pilots he'd ever seen, with the total destruction of Blue, [[Gray Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gray]] and Yellow Squadrons, as well as abandoning their starfighter outpost following the arrival of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] fleet. Darth Vader escaped capture, however all [[Alliance High Command]] staff members were evacuated and all sensitive information stored at the outpost was recovered or intentially destroyed. The Alliance were able to capture Dr. [[Chelli Lona Aphra]], a high-value prisoner, who was remanded to a [[Sunspot Prison|max security penitentiary]] at [[Sunspot]].<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>
