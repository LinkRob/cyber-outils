In the aftermath of the [[Battle of Naboo|battle]] to end the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]], the '''funeral of Qui-Gon Jinn''' was held in [[Theed]] for [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], who was impaled by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]] during the battle. The [[Jedi funeral]] was attended by a number of dignitaries, including [[Yoda]], [[Mace Windu]], and several members of the [[Jedi High Council]], as well as [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]]; [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], [[Boss]] [[Rugor Nass|Nass]], and [[Jar Jar Binks]] of Naboo; and newly-elected [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], secretly the Sith Lord Darth Sidious.<ref name="Episode I" /> Jinn's former master, [[Count]] [[Dooku]], had been considered to attend the funeral but had declined the invitation, claiming he would not be "pleasant company".

At the funeral, Jinn's body was [[Cremation|cremated]] on a funeral pyre; despite having trained to retain an [[Force spirit|eternal consciousness]], his body did not disappear upon his death, as his training was incomplete. Kenobi told Skywalker that he would be Skywalker's master, as the Jedi Council had agreed to allow Skywalker join the [[Jedi Order]]. Yoda and Windu also discussed the return of the [[Sith]], and wondered whether Darth Maul was the master or the apprentice&mdash;as they knew there could only be [[Rule of Two|two]] Sith.<ref name="Episode I" />
