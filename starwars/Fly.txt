They lived at least in the [[Red Isles]] on [[Thakwaa]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> [[Count]] [[Dooku]] used "fly" as a derogatory term whilst [[Second battle of Christophsis|dueling]] [[Quinlan Vos]].<ref name="DD" />
