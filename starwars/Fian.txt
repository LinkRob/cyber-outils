The '''Fian''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that lived in [[the galaxy]] that possessed a higher resistance to [[radiation]] than that of [[human]]s. During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Ar Prace]], a [[Gender|female]] Fian, served as a maintenance [[supervisor]] aboard the [[Galactic Republic]] [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] until its [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]]<ref name="The Fallen Star" /> around [[230 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Fian were first [[canon]]ically mentioned in the [[2022]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]'', written by [[Claudia Gray]] for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="The Fallen Star" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Fian/Legends|Fian]] [[Species/Legends|species]] (plural Fia)<ref name="Essential Guide">[[The Essential Guide to Alien Species (real-world book)|''The Essential Guide to Alien Species'']]</ref> originated in the [[1996]] novel ''[[Before the Storm]]'', the first book in the [[The Black Fleet Crisis|''Black Fleet Crisis'']] trilogy, written by [[Michael P. Kube-McDowell]].<ref name="BTS">''[[Before the Storm]]''</ref>
