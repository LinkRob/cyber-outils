'''Ben Price''' is a [[Wikipedia:Storyboard|storyboard]] and [[Wikipedia:Previsualization|previsualization]] artist for ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> a [[Wikipedia:Computer-generated imagery|CGI]]-based television series that debuted [[October 3]], [[2008]] on [[Cartoon Network]] as a continuation of the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|film with the same name]], released two months prior. Ben Price also did work in [[1993]] on the [[Wikipedia:Beavis and Butt-head|''Beavis and Butt-head'']] television series as a character and animated prop designer.
