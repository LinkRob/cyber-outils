The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''68 BBY''',<ref name="Date note">''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' establishes [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] as being 17 [[Standard year|years]] old. According to {{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Obi-Wan Kenobi - Padawan}}, Kenobi was [[Reproduction|born]] in [[57 BBY]], therefore placing the events of the novel in [[40 BBY]]. ''Master & Apprentice'' also establishes that [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] is forty years old. From this it can be deduced that Jinn was born in [[80 BBY]]. As Jinn became the [[Padawan]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]] when he was twelve, it can be deduced that Jinn became Dooku's Padawan in 68 BBY.</ref> approximately '''3209 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''49 BFE''' according to the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7909''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Qui-Gon Jinn]] becomes the [[Padawan]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]] at age twelve.<ref name="Master & Apprentice" />
*The [[Chiss Syndicure|Syndicure]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] removes the [[Xodlak family]] from the [[Ruling Families|Ten Ruling Families]], demoting them to a [[Great Families|Great Family]] and leaving Nine Ruling Families.<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref><ref name="Xodlak demotion note">''Greater Good'' takes place around 18 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. [[Senior Captain]] [[Irizi'in'daro]] reflected that the Xodlak had been stripped of their Ruling Family status 50 years ago, dating their removal to around 68 BBY.</ref>
*[[Kedpin Shoklop]] was hired on as a salesbeing by [[VaporTech]].<ref name="Rules of the Game">{{StoryCite|story=Rules of the Game|book=Canto Bight (novella collection)}}</ref>
