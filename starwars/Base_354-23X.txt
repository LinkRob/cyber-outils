'''Base 354-23X'''<ref name="Allegiance 2" /> was a secret [[New Republic]] impound facility located on the [[moon of Avedot]]. By the time of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], the impound was decommissioned. While on a mission to acquire weapons for the Resistance, [[Poe Dameron]] learned from a contact on the [[Wayward Comet]] that the impound still contained a cache of weapons confiscated from [[pirate]]s, and he and [[Finn]] planned to visit the impound and take the weapons.<ref name="Allegiance 1" />
