The '''duskblade''' was a short [[sword]] wielded by members of the [[Nightsisters]]. Used both in battle and for ceremonial purposes, the duskblade had a curved single-edged blade and a one-handed hilt. Being lightweight, flexible and easy to handle, these swords were lethal in the hands of a trained user.<ref name="COTR" />
