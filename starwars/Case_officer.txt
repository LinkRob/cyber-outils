A '''case officer''' was an intelligence [[officer]] who recruited operatives and managed their activities. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Dodd Rancit]] was a case officer with the [[Republic Intelligence|intelligence agency]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. As such, he notably supplied [[Commander]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] with counter-intelligence operatives. After the Republic was [[Proclamation of the New Order|reorganized]] into the first [[Galactic Empire]] by [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], the new [[Imperial Security Bureau]] (or ISB) also made use of case officers. One of them, [[Stellan]], recruited the [[Koorivar]] [[spy]] codenamed "[[Bracchia]]."<ref name="Tarkin" />
