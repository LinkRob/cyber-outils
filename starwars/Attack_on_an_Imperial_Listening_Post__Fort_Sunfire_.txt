During the [[Operations on Tatooine]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] listening outpost was '''attacked''' by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] forces as a prelude to their later assault against [[Fort Sunfire]]. Under the guidance of [[Colonel]] [[Jennica Pierce]] and the support of [[Sergeant]] [[Oto Vans]], the rebels emerged victorious, continuing with their [[Attack on Fort Sunfire|assault]] on Fort Sunfire directly.<ref name="Commander" />
