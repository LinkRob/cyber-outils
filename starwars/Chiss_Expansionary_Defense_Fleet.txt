{{Quote|Certainly the Chiss have no territorial ambitions beyond our own borders.''"<br />"''Yet you identify yourself as an ''expansionary'' fleet.''"<br />"''The title also includes the word ''defense''.|Thrawn and [[Fsir]]|<ref name="Greater Good" />}}
The '''Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet''', also known as the '''Chiss Expansionary Fleet''', '''Chiss Defense Fleet''', and the '''Ascendancy Defense Fleet''', was the external defense [[fleet]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Ascendancy military|military]] concerned with protecting [[Chiss]] interests beyond its borders and investigating threats from outside of Chiss territory. The Fleet was bound by a set of [[Non-intervention policy|laws prohibiting pre-emptive strikes]].

The [[fleet]] operated in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Unknown Regions]], and included [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]], a Chiss [[Gender|female]] [[officer]], as well as [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] before he joined the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[human]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]], a [[commander]] in the [[Imperial Military]] and a personal friend of Thrawn, would also join after Thrawn sent him as an Imperial liaison to the Chiss Ascendancy. The fleet also had specialized [[Chiss uniform|military uniforms]].

==History==
===Creation===
{{Quote|An enemy bold enough to stage a deliberate attack on a Chiss warship needs to be identified. That's the primary mission of the Expansionary Defense Fleet, after all.|Thrawn|<ref name="Greater Good" />}}
For years prior to the [[Nikardun conquests]], the [[Chiss Defense Force]] had pled with the ruling [[Chiss Syndicure]] to expand its capabilities and mandate. Shortly before the young Chiss [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Kivu'raw'nuru]] was recruited from his humble [[Kivu family|family]] to [[Rematching|become a member]] of the [[Mitth family]], the Syndicure finally agreed; establishing the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet with the [[Command Articles|mission]] to "watch over Chiss interests in the parts of [[Unknown Regions|the Chaos]] extending beyond the [[Chiss Ascendancy|Ascendancy's]] borders, to learn who was out there and assess their level of threat." At that [[time]], the [[Aristocra]] provided funding for many new [[Starship|ships]], bases, and support facilities, and the young Expansionary Fleet needed many competent [[Chiss officer|officers]] and [[Chiss warrior|warriors]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

===Nikardun conquests===
{{Quote|I am Commander Mitth'raw'nuruodo, officer of the Expansionary Defense Fleet, servant of the Chiss Ascendancy.|Mitth'raw'nuruodo, introducing himself to Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />}}
By the time of the [[Clone Wars]], the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet was the active external branch of the [[Chiss Defense Force]]. During the war,<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> Chiss Defense Fleet [[officer]] [[Senior Captain]]<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] was near the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] world of [[Batuu]] on a mission when he encountered [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Thrawn assisted Skywalker in [[Mission to Batuu|destroying]] a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Mokivj droid factory|droid factory]] on the remote world of [[Mokivj]] and returned to Chiss space with a [[deflector shield generator]] he termed a "Republic energy shield"<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> which was much more powerful than the [[electrostatic barrier]]s employed by the peoples of the Unknown Regions at the time. Thrawn proceeded to use the "Republic energy shield" aboard his ''[[Springhawk]]'' to help defeat the [[Nikardun]] [[General]] [[Yiv]] the Benevolent.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> The Expansionary Defense Fleet was later mentioned in a [[Clone Intelligence]] report describing the mission to Mokivj, which was included in the survival compendium "[[Neyo's Almanac of Clankers]]."<ref name="Official Magazine 118">{{GermanFanCite|118|Database}}</ref>

After defeating Yiv, the Ascendancy sent the Fleet on the [[Nikardun campaigns]] to eliminate remnants of the [[Nikardun Destiny]]. The Syndicure saw the [[Yiv's battle forces|Nikardun forces]] as enough of a threat to bend the [[Non-intervention policy|non-aggression protocols]] to allow the campaigns.<ref name="Greater Good" />

===Imperial–Grysk conflict===
The Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet remained active during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. After the [[Batonn insurgency]], [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] was promoted to the rank of [[Imperial officer|Imperial]] [[Grand Admiral]]. He dispatched his friend and protégé [[Commander]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] to rendevous with Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] and join the Chiss Ascendancy. During the [[Imperial–Grysk conflict]], Chiss Defense Fleet forces monitored the [[battle against Grysk forces]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

==Organization==
{{Quote|The Syndicure doesn't control the Expansionary Defense Fleet. All they can do is advise, encourage, and make trouble.''"<br />"''Especially the latter.|Thrawn and [[Ufsa'mak'ro|Samakro]]|<ref name="Greater Good" />}}
The Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet was the external naval branch of the Chiss Ascendancy's armed forces; collectively the [[Ascendancy military]]. The Chiss Ascendancy was ruled by the Syndicure and the [[Aristocra]],<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> yet [[Chiss family]] politics officially had no impact on the Defense Fleet's descisions. In practice, there were indeed "unofficial" moments where they played a role, however.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> It was an acknowledged fact that the decisions of the Syndicure, while not orders, carried weight.<ref name="Greater Good" />

There were multiple [[military rank]]s used in the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet, including the ranks of Admiral and Commander. Part of the role of the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet was defending Chiss colonies, as well as monitoring battles between other factions and governments.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> The Expansionary Defense Fleet was also not supposed to interfere with events occurring within Chiss borders, with that role being handled by the Chiss Defense Force.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Personnel==
{{Quote|I am Admiral Ar'alani of the Chiss Defense Fleet.|Admiral Ar'alani|<ref name="Thrawn" />}}
[[File:Aralani_and_Vanto.png|thumb|right|150px|Admiral Ar'alani and Lieutenant Eli Vanto of the Chiss Defense Fleet.]]
[[Chiss officer|Officers]] of the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet were outfitted with a type of [[Chiss uniform|uniform]] which was able to absorb energy, protecting the wearer from [[blaster]] [[Laser|fire]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> Chiss uniforms were typically black in [[color]]&mdash;with the exception of ozyly-esehembo's uniforms, which were white&mdash;and featured the emblem of the Chiss Ascendancy on the upper arm.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> Rank was indicated by silver [[insignia pins]] placed on the collar of the uniform.<ref name="Thrawn" /> The rank bars were absent on the uniforms of [[cadet]]s. Fleet Officers and cadets who belonged to one of the [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]] had a shoulder patch with that family's insignia.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

There were some exceptions to the basic Chiss uniform, as Admiral Ar'alani wore a pure white uniform in stark contrast to the black uniforms of her crew.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

==Starships of the Chiss Defense Fleet==
The Chiss Defense Fleet used several types of [[starship]]s, including [[Chiss heavy cruiser]]s<ref name="Thrawn" /> like the [[Steadfast (Chiss heavy cruiser)|''Steadfast'']],<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> and the Chiss vessel [[Whirlwind (Chiss)|''Whirlwind'']] was also part of the fleet.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> [[Chiss shuttle]]s, also referred to as Chiss courier ships, were also deployed.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> [[Chiss defense platform]]s were also utilized by the Chiss Defense Fleet. Another type of warship used by the Chiss was the [[Nightdragon man-of-war]]. Other ships such as [[missile boat]]s, [[patrol cruiser]]s, [[Chiss patrol ship|patrol ships]], [[freighter]]s, [[Chiss heavy cruiser|heavy cruisers]], and [[Chiss destroyer|destroyers]] were used.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
