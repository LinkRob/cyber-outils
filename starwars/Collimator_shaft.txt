'''Collimator shafts''' were components in [[superlaser]] weapons. The [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station]] included a collimator shaft, as did several of its previous smaller-scale prototypes in [[Hypori]] and [[Malpaz]]. Exfoliating [[doonium]] and [[dolovite]] as found in the [[Legacy world]]s [[Wadi Raffa]] and [[Samovar]] were primarily employed to insulate the cores of large [[hypermatter reactor]]s and to dissipate heat in collimator shafts.<ref name="Catalyst" />
