'''Dee''' was a [[droid]] who assisted [[Jedi Master]] [[Zia Zaldor Zanna]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="The Young Jedi" />  They were often tasked with taking care of the [[Tenoo Jedi Temple]] while Master Zanna was away. Dee was also in charge of escorting the [[youngling|younglings]] on field trips, such as to the [[Lava fields (Tiss'ell)|lava fields]] of [[Tiss'ell]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>
