'''Brown dwarf gravy''' was a type of [[Gravy/Legends|gravy]] named after [[Brown dwarf/Legends|brown dwarf]] [[Star/Legends|stars]]. It was served with [[proteinloaf]] at [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]], but it could also be used as a makeshift industrial-grade lubricant according to the restaurant's [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|eponymous proprietor]].
