'''2016 Topps ''Star Wars: Rogue One'' Mission Briefing Monday''' was a trading card set released by [[Topps]] based on the [[2016]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. It was released via the Topps website in sets of five cards every Monday, starting on [[November 7]] and ending with the movie's release on [[December 19]].
