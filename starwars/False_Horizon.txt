"'''False Horizon'''" is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[December 11]], [[2003]] and took the form of multiple broadcasts. The first was from [[Osten Dal'Nay]] and [[Broof Yurdel]] announcing simultaneous strikes by the [[Cularin Militia]] against bases of the [[Thaereian military|Thaereian Navy]] in the [[Cularin system/Legends|Cularin System]]. Following some commentary by [[Yara Grugara]], [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Lanius Qel-Bertuk]] broadcast the support of the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]] for the Militia's efforts. This in turn, was followed by a speech by Cularin's senator [[Lavina Durada-Vashne Wren|Lavina Wren]], who further endorsed the Militia's work. The final footage shown was of Militia starfighters engaging Thaereian forces. Although they appeared outnumbered by the Thaereians, the Cularin forces were quickly joined by fighters from [[Nirama]]'s [[Organization]] and repelled the Navy forces.
