'''Bio-cubes''',<ref name="Mandalore">{{SWE|locations|mandalore|Mandalore}}</ref> also known as '''bio-domes''',<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> were massive, insulated cube or [[Domed city|dome]]-shaped [[City|cities]] inhabited by the [[New Mandalorians]] on their [[homeworld]] of [[Mandalore]] after [[Standard century|centuries]] of war had reduced its surface to inhospitable [[desert]].<ref name="Mandalore" /> The [[planet]]'s [[capital]] city of [[Sundari]] was a bio-dome.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />
