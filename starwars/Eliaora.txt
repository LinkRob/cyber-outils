'''Eliaora''' was a [[Gender|female]] individual who served as [[empress]] of the [[Planet|world]] [[Forrn]].<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> By [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'' to [[4 ABY]].</ref> [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] had visited Forrn and established a connection with Eliaora.<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Organa used her connection with Eliaora to speak at a personal level to the empress when she sent a [[holomessage]] to them after the [[Battle of Endor]] to persuade Eliaora to side with the forming [[New Republic]]. The princess included specific aid or personal favors that she knew the empress would desire and want.<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />
