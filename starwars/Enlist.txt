'''''Enlist''''' was the name of several Imperial [[propaganda]] [[Painting|murals]] in the [[Capital City]] of [[Lothal]]. It showed an Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] accompanied by the [[Aurebesh]] text "ENLIST" and "[[Galactic Empire|EMPIRE]]".<ref name="Entanglement" /> Between that, "THE EMPIRE IS THE FUTURE" was written in smaller characters.<ref name="Legacy">{{Rebels|legacy (episode)|Legacy}}</ref> The murals came in at least two different [[color]] schemes.<ref name="Entanglement" />

The mural was also used as a background motif on the cover of a pocket map of the city, which [[Ezra Bridger]] included in his personal journal.<ref name="Journal">''[[Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger]]''</ref>
