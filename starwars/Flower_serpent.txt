'''Flower serpents''' were a type of [[Snake|serpent]] found on<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="A Test of Courage">''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]''</ref> [[Haileap]]. The [[scientist]] [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]]&mdash;who lived for a [[time]] on Haileap&mdash;considered them to be pretty but [[Death|deadly]], and that they had no care for anything beyond themselves.<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|DFS}}</ref> after Avon's [[Starros Clan|mother]], [[Ghirra Starros]], told her that [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] would be disappointed if she did not attend a dinner at the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[Nihil main base|compound]] on<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> the Outer Rim planet [[Hetzal Prime]],<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> Avon [[Brain|mentally]] noted that Ro reminded her of a [[flower]] serpent.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flower serpents were mentioned in the [[2024]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Defy the Storm]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and [[Justina Ireland]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> Within the novel, flower serpents were mentioned within a chapter written by Ireland.<ref name="ChapterAuthor">{{Bluesky|tessagratton.bsky.social|post/3lzypgmy75s2y|[[Tessa Gratton]]]|date=2025-09-29|quote=Hi!
