"'''Broken Orbits: Something Uffel'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was Episode II of the [[Broken Orbits trilogy]] and was written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]].
