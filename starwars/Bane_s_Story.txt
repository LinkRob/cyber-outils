"'''Bane's Story'''" is a short story that was published in the anthology ''[[The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark]]'', adapting the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episodes "[[Deception]]," "[[Friends and Enemies]]," "[[The Box]]," and "[[Crisis on Naboo]]." It was written by [[Tom Angleberger]], and published on [[August 25]], [[2020]] by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]].
