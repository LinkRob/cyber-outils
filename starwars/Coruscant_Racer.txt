The '''Coruscant Racer''' was a costume owned by [[Dizzy (Ugnaught)|Dizzy]], the [[Ugnaught]] member of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]], that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Wild Racer|standard costume]]. It featured a [[Color|blue]], purple, green, gray, and bronze color scheme, including a [[helmet]].

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Coruscant Racer was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 6.0.0 alongside Season 6 on [[April 15]], [[2025]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Coruscant Paintwork]]. It was made immediately free-to-use at the start of Season 6, unlocked alongside the majority of in-game cosmetics. This costume's flavor text quote is specifically attribute to Team Slingshot in the game, unlock the rest of their cosmetics whose quotes just come from Dizzy.<ref name="Hunters" />
