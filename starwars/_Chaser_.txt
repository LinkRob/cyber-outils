"'''Chaser'''" was the nickname of a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] who served within the [[Galactic Empire]]. As a member of the [[Imperial Starfighter Corps]], Chaser flew a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] during combat in the [[Battle of Yavin]], where they were fired upon by a [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]].<ref name="X-wing" />
