The '''Cylo Directive''' was a subdivision within the [[Tarkin Initiative]], a secret think tank within the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Darth Vader 20" /> It was headed by [[Doctor]] [[Cylo]], who specialized with [[cybernetics]]. Cylo envisioned a society with advanced [[cyborg]]s and without [[the Force]].<ref name="Darth Vader 5" />
