'''Blue Milk Chia Pudding''' was a [[pudding]] served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE Date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The [[sugar]]y [[Foodstuff|dessert]] was consumable by [[human]]s.<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> It was served in honor of the [[Blutopian]] Batuu denizen [[Oga Garra]]'s [[birthday]].<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=2022/07/whats-cooking-at-disneyland-resort-this-july|text=What's Cooking at Disneyland Resort In July|author=Nicole Cantore|date=2022-07-11}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Blue Milk Chia Pudding was a [[In-universe|real-world]] dessert that was first made available to guests in the restaurant Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo in the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]] from [[July 18]] to [[July 20]], [[2022]] as part of the park's celebration of Oga Garra's birthday, along with several other limited-time offerings across the land. The menu described it as "[[Wikipedia:Coconut milk|coconut milk]] and [[Wikipedia:Chia seed|chia seed]] blend, fresh [[Berry|berries]], [[Wikipedia:Pumpkin seed|pepitas]], and shredded [[Wikipedia:Coconut|coconut]]."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch And Dinner Menu}}</ref>
