'''''Doctor Aphra'' 39''' is the thirty-ninth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 20]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''ABOVE ALL &ndash; A ''DARK DROIDS'' TIE-IN! The SCOURGE sets its sights on TAGGE CORPORATION&hellip;and DOMINA TAGGE! As the ''ACQUISITOR'' falls from the sky, DOCTOR APHRA fights for survival&hellip;against an entity that threatens to consume everything she loves! (Including herself!)''

Magna Tolvan was also corrupted by the Scourge.

Then Aphra, Lucky, and Sana traveled to the scourge-affected
world of Epikonia, where Tolvan and other cyborgs infected
by the Scourge are continuing experiments to transition into
organic minds.
