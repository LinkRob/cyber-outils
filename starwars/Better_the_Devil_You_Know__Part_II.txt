"'''Better the Devil You Know, Part II'''" focuses on [[Rey Skywalker|Rey's]] adventure on [[Jakku]], in which [[Unkar Plutt]] was kidnapped. The second part of the story, it was published as the lead story of [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 2|''Star Wars Adventures'' (2017) 2]]<ref name="IDW" /> on [[September 20]], [[2017]].<ref name="CBR" />

==Plot summary==
===Saving Unkar Plutt===
On the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Jakku]], the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] had discovered the [[Kidnapping of Unkar Plutt|kidnapped]] [[Unkar Plutt]] lying in [[desert]] in [[stuncuffs]], surrounded by [[Zool Zendiat]] and his [[Zendiat Collective|collective]]. Rey did not like Unkar Plutt at all but did not think it was right for him to be fed to the [[Ripper-raptor]]s. She thought it was her fault that Plutt was kidnapped because she sold him the [[J9 worker drone]] that the Zendiat Collective was after. She slid down a sand dune and while hiding behind some containers, she took out a bag of [[comlink]]s and placed them in many different areas around her. The raptors started to fly over head as Zendiat asked Plutt one last time where the droid head was. Then from her own comlink, Rey made a squawking noise of a [[bloggin]] that played through all the other comlinks. When they were caught off guard, she leaped down and confronted Zool and his group. Rey knocked out [[Tryki]] with [[Rey's quarterstaff|her quarterstaff]] and used it to push Tryki into [[Kanna]]. Armed with three [[blaster pistol]]s the [[Talz]], [[Ramakak]] shot at Rey but she evaded the shots and knocked him out.

Unkar Plutt questioned what Rey was doing to which she replied that she was rescuing him. Unkar Plutt told her to hurry up but as Rey was deactivating the stuncuffs, she was electrocuted by Zool's [[Vibroknife|vibro-knife]]. Tryki told Zool that RWy had found the comlinks around their [[starship]] and was impressed by her trick. Zendiat looked back at Rey and asked her what should stop them from shooting her. Rey took out the head of the J9 droid and Zendiat allowed her to speak for herself. Rey would give Zendiat the head if he allowed her and Unkar Plutt to go. Zool Zendiat admired her spirit but decided to blast Rey and then take the head. Rey had anticipated this and threw the droid head high in the air, running over to Plutt and taking off his stuncuffs. Unkar Plutt was angry at Rey for the way she talked to him so he pushed her into Ramakak, sending both flying back. Plutt then caught the droid head and told Zool Zendiat that it's head must be worth a lot of credits. He then went over to Rey's pack of [[corn-cluster]]s and threw them up. The Ripper-raptors flew in and started to jab at the Zendiat Collective. He Rey and they both escaped but before they could, Rey took the droid head and launched it back at the group with her quarterstaff. They managed to recover it and leave Jakku.

As they rode back to [[Niima Outpost]], Rey told Plutt that the droid head was actually worthless and that [[Krynodd]] had taken over his business. The angried Unkar Plutt marched into Niima Outpost to take his business back.

===The following day===
The next [[Standard day|day]], Rey climbed out of her [[Hellhound Two|home]] and discovered a crate filled with [[ration pack]]s and a [[holocube]]. A message from Unkar Plutt played and he told her that the rations were payment for getting him back to Niima Outpost and to not try to sell him the holocube. Rey acknowledged this as a thank you from Unkar Plutt, realizing that some days are better than others.
