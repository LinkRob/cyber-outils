The '''''Executor II''-class Star Dreadnought''' was a type of [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] used by the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. At one point, one such [[Star Dreadnought]] flew over a cloudy celestial body with two [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]]. The model was propelled by [[Color|red]]-hued [[engine]]s<ref name="Armada" /> and it succeeded the [[Executor I-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor I''-class Star Dreadnought]].<ref name="Executor II">{{Armada|set=Super Star Destroyer Expansion Pack|cardname=''Executor I''-class Star Dreadnought}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Executor II''-class Star Dreadnought was first mentioned on a card of the same name in the [[2019]] ''[[Super Star Destroyer Expansion Pack]]'' by [[Fantasy Flight Games]], for the miniatures game [[Star Wars: Armada|''Star Wars'': Armada]].<ref name="Armada" /> The image of the [[Starship|ship]] was illustrated by [[Matt Bradbury]].<ref name="Instagram">{{Instagram|username=fantasyflightgames|url=p/BmESlQclX3M|name=[[Fantasy Flight Games]]|date=August 4, 2018|quote=#starwars #armada #superstardestroyer #miniatures #mini #tabletopgame [Art by Matt Bradbury]|archivefile=File:Executor_II_Insta.png}}</ref>
