'''David Pentland''' is an illustrator specializing in military and aviation art.

Pentland was part of the group who made artworks for the [[2010]] artbook ''[[Star Wars Art: Visions]]''. He contributed two pictures titled ''Clash on Kothlis'' and ''Raid on Kothlis''.
