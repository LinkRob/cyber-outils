'''Baik Raaorn''' held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[general]] within the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Confederacy military|military]] during the [[Clone Wars]] with the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Battles" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Raaorn commanded Separatist forces in a [[Battle of Cato Neimoidia|battle]] on the [[planet]] [[Cato Neimoidia]] against the Republic's [[442nd Siege Battalion]], led by [[Jedi General]] [[Plo Koon]], amid the [[Outer Rim Sieges]] offensive launched by the Confederacy. Raaorn and his [[battle droid]] forces suffered from aerial support in the form of [[clone trooper pilot]]s from [[Clone Flight Squad Seven]], while his [[Tarko-se arena|arena]] [[Separatist base (Tarko-se)|base]] in [[Tarko-se]] [[city]] was besieged and claimed by the Republic, effectively allowing the Republic to conquer Cato Neimoidia.<ref name="Battles" />
