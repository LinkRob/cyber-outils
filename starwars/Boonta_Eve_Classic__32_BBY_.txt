A '''Boonta Eve Classic ''' [[Podracing|podrace]] in [[Tatooine]]'s [[Mos Espa podrace circuit]] occurred on [[Boonta Eve]]<ref name="Episode I" /> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> The [[Youngling|young]] [[human]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] won the podrace with [[gender|his]] [[Jedi]]-like reflexes, becoming the first human to win the [[Boonta Eve Classic]]. As a result of a [[Gamble|bet]] between [[Jedi Knight]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and the [[Toydarian]] [[Junk dealer]] [[Watto]], Skywalker was freed from [[Slavery]] upon his victory in the race.<ref name="Episode I" />
