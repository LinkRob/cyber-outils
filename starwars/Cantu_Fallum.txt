'''Cantu Fallum''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Dokora]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> who served as a [[Jedi]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> he was among the Jedi assembled by [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] for a covert [[Duel on Brendok|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Brendok]] to locate the missing Jedi Master [[Sol]]. Upon arrival, Fallum joined the search at a fortress where Sol’s body was discovered, later pursuing the Force-sensitive twins [[Verosha Aniseya|Osha]] and [[Mae-ho Aniseya|Mae Aniseya]].<ref name="TA" />

==Biography==
A member of the [[Amphibian|amphibious]] Dokora species, Cantu Fallum served as a Jedi during the final years of the High Republic Era. During or shortly before 132 BBY, Fallum undertook a peacekeeping mission to the ocean-covered world of [[Ando]], home to the [[Aqualish]] species. Known for his terse and straightforward demeanor, he successfully mediated a truce between warring Aqualish factions, an accomplishment that earned him their respect.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

Later in 132 BBY, Fallum was present at the [[Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] when Master Vernestra Rwoh received confirmation from [[Padawan]] [[Mog Adana]] that Master Sol’s [[transponder]] beacon had been reactivated on Brendok. Seeking to investigate without drawing attention, Rwoh instructed Adana to quietly gather several [[Jedi Knights]], with Fallum among those selected. Departing aboard Rwoh’s [[Cantaros|vessel]], the Jedi arrived on Brendok and landed in a forest clearing near a [[Brendok fortress|fortress]].

Following Rwoh into the structure, Fallum and the others reached its courtyard, where they discovered the lifeless body of Master Sol. Rwoh quickly issued orders, sending Fallum and several Jedi to track the Aniseya twins while others established a perimeter. Guided by the [[Tynnan]] tracker [[Bazil]], Fallum’s group navigated through the wooded terrain until they reached a massive [[Bunta tree|bunta tree]], where they encountered Mae Aniseya. Finding her without memory of recent events, the Jedi took her into custody.

Later, at the Jedi Temple, Fallum was among those escorting Mae into Rwoh’s control room. He assisted in removing her restraints before departing with the other guards, leaving Rwoh to question Mae about her identity and the events on Brendok.<ref name="TA" />

==Personality and traits==
Cantu Fallum was an amphibious Dokora distinguished by the dark, angular spotting that marked most of his body, leaving his face unpatterned. Like others of his species, he was fully capable of functioning both [[Water|underwater]] and on land. Fallum was known for his blunt approach to diplomacy, a style that proved effective during tense negotiations between warring Aqualish factions on Ando.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a Jedi, Fallum was trained in the use of [[the Force]] and adept with a [[lightsaber]].<ref name="TA" />

==Equipment==
During his time on Coruscant and throughout the Brendok mission, Fallum wore traditional [[Jedi apparel|Jedi attire]], including layered robes and a [[color|brown]] cloak. His belt held his lightsaber and featured a belt buckle equipped with a transponder.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
