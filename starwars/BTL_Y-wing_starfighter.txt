The '''BTL Y-wing starfighter''', also known to as the '''Y-wing starfighter/bomber''', '''Y-wing Starfighter''' or more simply as the '''BTL-series''', '''Y-wing fighter''', or '''Y-wing''' and nicknamed '''the wallowing pigs''', was a series of multi-role [[starfighter]]-[[bomber]]s manufactured by [[Koensayr Manufacturing]]. Commissioned by the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]], many surviving Y-wings would go on to serve with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Notable models included the original two-seat [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]]; its successor, the [[BTL-S3 Y-wing starfighter|BTL-S3 Y-wing Starfighter]]; and the single-seat [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:Y-wing_blueprints.png|thumb|left|200px|Schematics of a stripped-down BTL-A4 Y-wing.]]
During the early part of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Republic]] commissioned [[Koensayr]] to develop a combination [[starfighter]] and long-range [[bomber]]. Their result, the original BTL-B model, proved so successful that additional variants were created, among them the BTL-S3 and BTL-A4 variants. However, except for differences in their [[cockpit]] and [[gunner]] layouts, these all shared the same characteristics.<ref name="RSOWM" />

The BTL-B Y-wing consisted of a cockpit section which connected via reinforced central spar and cross-wing to two large Koensayr [[ion engine]]s. The pressurized cockpit seated a [[pilot]] (and in some models a gunner) protected by a [[transparisteel]] canopy. Some cockpit sections were modified so that they were fully contained, repulsor-powered escape vehicles, though most Y-wings incorporated traditional [[ejector seat]]s.<ref name="RSOWM" />

Each [[engine]] was fitted with four strong pylons that extended behind to support a long engine nacelle, at the end of which were disk-ventrals that redirected thrust for maneuverability. A pair of thrust control jets were set in the aft central spar to give the Y-wing greater agility. [[Repulsorlift]]s were fitted in the neck and wings to provide [[Atmosphere|atmospheric]] flight. A [[fuel]] recycling system gave the Y-wing extreme efficiency during long-range missions.<ref name="RSOWM" /> In the event that the craft would have to make a make an emergency landing, the pilot could dump its fuel from the engines.<ref name="In the Name of the Rebellion">{{Rebels|In the Name of the Rebellion}}</ref>

For weaponry, the Y-wing was fitted with two forward firing [[laser cannon]]s, either the [[KX5 laser cannon|KX5]] or [[IX4 laser cannon|IX4]] models.<ref name="RSOWM" /> These weapons, designed to penetrate [[Battlecruiser|battle cruiser]] [[hull]]s, could have devastating effect on lesser targets, collapsing a five-story stone building in just a few shots.<ref name="SF">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> Backing up these were twin [[SW-4 ion cannon|SW-4]] or [[SW-5 ion cannon]]s, mounted on a rotating [[turret]] controlled either by a dedicated gunner or by the pilot depending on the variant. Lastly were a pair of [[MG7 proton torpedo launcher]]s, each with a six-round magazine. Besides [[proton torpedo]]es, these launchers could launch other weapons including [[concussion missile]]s, [[proton bomb]]s, and [[cluster missile]]s.<ref name="RSOWM" /> The starfighter was capable of unleashing a tremendous amount of firepower, especially when attacking in groups: a [[squadron]] of Y-wings could carry enough ordnance to glass an entire [[city]], bathing the battlefield in blinding white detonations which scrambled [[sensor]]s.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> The Y-wing could also carry external ordnance mounted directly on its hull, such as an [[electro-proton bomb]].<ref name="The Zillo Beast" />

The Y-wing was heavily [[armor]]ed with a [[titanium]]-reinforced [[alusteel]] hull and a [[Chempat]] [[deflector shield generator]]. The generator, powered by the ion engines, was linked to shield projectors located across the Y-wing's hull. This allowed the pilot to angle the shields of maximum protection or cover damaged sections of the ship.<ref name="RSOWM" /> The Y-wing could also use its cannons to shoot down enemy [[missile]]s, so long as the [[targeting computer]] remained functional.<ref name="SF" />

For flight control, the Y-wing featured a [[Sublights Products Corporation]] [[NH-7 flight control avionics package]] and photonics communication system, as well as [[ANc 2.7 tracking computer]] linked to an [[SI 5g7 "Quickscan" vector imaging system]], an [[ANs-5d "lock track" full-spectrum transceiver]], [[PA-9r long-range phased tachyon detection array]] and [[PG-7u short-range primary threat analysis grid]]. A composite sensor dome was fitted to the forward end of each engine, while a rear-facing sensor was positioned on the cross-wing.<ref name="RSOWM" /> The Y-wing's sensors were sensitive enough to locate an [[escape pod]]-sized target via it's metallurgical properties within a five-[[kilometer]] radius when operating at low altitudes.<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> A [[R2-series astromech droid]], fitted in a dedicated socket behind the cockpit, carried [[hyperspace]] data for the [[R300-H hyperdrive motivator]] and could manage all other systems by itself.<ref name="RSOWM" /> Y-wings heavily depended on the [[Astromech droid|astromech]] being operational, due to the fact that hyperspace was impossible without one.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

The original Y-wing featured a plethora of additional equipment geared towards its mission of long-range strikes. This included backup [[Battery|batteries]], a secondary [[life support]] system, fresh [[water]] tanks, and an emergency flotation system. Because the Y-wing tended to run [[Temperature|hot]], a complex cooling system extended throughout the entire starfighter.<ref name="RSOWM" />

The Rebel Alliance sought to buy or steal as many Y-wings as they could to form their nascent rebellion. However, they quickly realized that the cooling system required extensive maintenance after every flight, necessitating the removal of the armor plates just to get at it. In order to ease this burden it was decided to remove the armor plating, which was used to refit other ships (such as the [[T-47 airspeeder]]). Additional shield power was added to compensate for the loss.<ref name="RSOWM" />

Other changes included the removal or reconfiguration of various components to lighten weight. This included removing the fuel reclamation system for all but some BTL-S3 models (which were utilized as couriers) and replacing the [[torpedo]] launchers' six-round magazine with a four-round model. The engine-supported pylons were also shortened to improve maneuverability during close [[Starfighter combat|combat]]. While the resulting fighter looked spindly, overall integrity on the Y-wing was not compromised, and it resulted in a faster and more lethal starfighter than the original.<ref name="RSOWM" /> The speed and maneuverability of certain models, in particular the two-seat BTL-S3, was still considered subpar, but they were regarded as reliable and well-armed nonetheless.<ref name="Poe Dameron 17" />

==Models==
For [[Standard decade|decades]] after the Clone Wars, various Y-wing models saw combat in military conflicts, through the Galactic Civil War and up to the [[First Order-Resistance War|First Order-Resistance conflict]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> The Alliance modified their Y-wings by removing the hull and nacelle plating in order to increase ease of maintenance on the aging craft,<ref name="Y-wing Starfighter" /> which would enter the popular memory. Except for the cockpit variations, all ships in the BTL series were similar in design.<ref name="RSOWM" />

===BTL-B Y-wing===
{{Main|BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber}}
[[File:BTL-BYwing-SWE.png|thumb|right|180px|BTL-B Y-wing]]
The [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]] was the original model commissioned by the [[Republic Navy]] in the early stages of the Clone Wars, designed as a combination starfighter and long-range bomber that could replace the [[V-19 Torrent starfighter|V-19 Torrent]] and [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Jedi interceptors]].<ref name="RSOWM">''[[Rebel Starfighters Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> The model's distinguishing feature was its bubble-turret, which swiveled 360 degrees, providing a clear view of surrounding space for gunners.<ref name="Y-wing Starfighter">{{Databank|y-wing-starfighter|Y-wing Starfighter}}</ref> A mainstay of the Republic Navy, it was present at many of the major battles of the war, though most BTL-Bs were scrapped by the Empire after the conflict.<ref name="RSOWM" />

===BTL-A4 LP===
{{Main|BTL-A4 LP}}
The [[BTL-A4 LP]] variant served as a long-distance reconnaissance vessel.<ref name="Starfighters" />

===BTL-A4 Y-wing===
[[File:Ywing.jpg|thumb|right|180px|BTL-A4 Y-wing]]
{{Main|BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber}}
Designed after the success of the BTL-B, the [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]] variant served as a single-seat tactical strike spacecraft which lacked the gunner's position of other variants.<ref name="RSOWM" /> After the Empire phased out the BLT-A4,<ref name="FFG Gray Squadron">{{FFGXW2|set=BTL-A4 Y-Wing Expansion Pack|cardname=Gray Squadron Bomber}}</ref> this variant was extensively utilized by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and [[New Republic]] during the Galactic Civil War.<ref name="Y-wing Starfighter" /><ref name="RSG">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]''</ref> Its durability, dependability, and heavy armament made it a staple in the rebel fleet,<ref name="FFG Gray Squadron" /> seeing action at major battles such as [[Battle of Scarif|Scarif]], [[Battle of Yavin|Yavin]], [[Battle of Endor|Endor]], and [[Battle of Jakku|Jakku]].<ref name="Y-wing Starfighter" /><ref name="RSG" />

===BTL-S3 Y-wing===
[[File:BTLS3-PoeDameron17.png|thumb|right|180px|BTL-S3 Y-wing]]
{{Main|BTL-S3 Y-wing starfighter}}
The [[BTL-S3 Y-wing starfighter|BTL-S3 Y-wing Starfighter]] retained the tailgunner position from the BTL-B, though it eliminated the elevated bubble turret by seating the pilot and gunner back-to-back in the cockpit.<ref name="RSOWM" /> Like the BTL-A4, it would go on to serve with the Rebel Alliance,<ref name="Weapon of a Jedi">''[[The Weapon of a Jedi: A Luke Skywalker Adventure]]''</ref> and by the time of the [[Cold War]] three decades later, it was still valued for its toughness despite being considered an antique.<ref name="Poe Dameron 17" />

===BTA-NR2 Y-wing===
{{Main|BTA-NR2 Y-wing starfighter}}
[[File:BTA-NR2_Y-wing.png|thumb|right|180px|BTA-NR2 Y-wing]]
The [[BTA-NR2 Y-wing starfighter]] restarted the Y-wing line after the [[Galactic Civil War]] re-popularized the design as "the starfighter that broke the Empire's back." It was largely based on the BTL-A4 single-seat variant, and emulated the customizability and lack of armor plating that customers associated with the "rebel Y-wing." Despite this, it featured optional plating and improved [[sensor]]s, [[deflector shield]]s, controls, and [[R750 ion jet engine|engines]]. This variant saw service as a planetary defense craft with the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]], as well as with the Resistance in their fight against the [[First Order]].<ref name="RSOWM" />

==History==
===The Clone Wars===
====Overview====
{{Quote|The Republic commissioned the Y-wing for clone pilots to take on the Confederacy's droid starfighters, and last I checked, the droids didn't win.|Jon Vander|<ref name="RSOWM" />}}
In the Clone War, Y-wings developed under the [[Y-wing project]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Republic, Rebel, and Resistance Starfighters}}</ref> proved their worth to the Republic, taking part in several key battles against the Separatists. Despite their size, the two-seat starfighters were quite maneuverable, with clone trooper pilots routinely performing quick rolls while staying in formation. Their turrets and fixed guns allowed them to engage and destroy multiple targets simultaneously, making them effective in combat missions against enemy starfighters.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" /> This versatility made them suitable for a number of mission profiles, whether it involved attacking enemy capital ships or bombing ground targets. They were, however, considered "buggy" in atmospheric operations.<ref name="The Forgotten Droid" />

====Corvair and the Kaliida Nebula====
[[File:Btlbywing.png|thumb|left|250px|Shadow Squadron departs for its attack on the ''Malevolence''.]]
Y-wings saw use in the [[Battle of Corvair]] alongside [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s under [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="AOR">[[Age of Republic - Anakin Skywalker 1|''Age of Republic - Anakin Skywalker'' 1]]</ref> [[Shadow Squadron]], a unit of [[clone trooper pilot]]s, flew Y-wing bombers in the [[Battle of the Kaliida Nebula]] under the command of Skywalker.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" /> At that time, Y-wings were still being tested.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] flew as the gunner in Skywalker's Y-wing, and [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Plo Koon]] served as a fighter escort. The Y-wings successfully destroyed ''[[Malevolence]]''{{'s}} [[ion cannon]] in the engagement. Out of an initial force of 12 Y-wing bombers, six were lost in the assault.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" />

During their [[investigation on Rodia|investigation]] into the actions of some [[Unidenified rogue Nightsisters|rogue Nightsisters]] on [[Rodia]], Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker crashed their Y-wing into a shield protecting a [[Equator City|city]] near the [[Senatorial Palace]], and when the shield suddenly activated, the starfighter was completely destroyed, while the dome remained unharmed.<ref name ="Mustafar and Other">{{EncyclopediaCite|Mustafar and Other Planets in the Outer Rim}}</ref>

====Ryloth====
{{Quote|That Y-wing crashed here back in the Clone War, and my father left it as a memorial. It also happens to be the ship I pulled Chopper out of, so he still has issues with it.|Hera Syndulla|<ref name="Hera's Heroes">{{Rebels|Hera's Heroes}}</ref>}}

Y-wings participated in the [[Battle of Ryloth]], in which they broke the [[planet]]ary [[blockade]] imposed by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Storm Over Ryloth">{{TCW|Storm Over Ryloth}}</ref> They also participated in atmospheric operations, and at least one crashed outside the [[Syndulla residence]]. [[Hera Syndulla]] rescued [[C1-10P|"Chopper"]] from the wrecked Y-wing, and [[Cham Syndulla]] decided to preserve the crash site as a memorial.<ref name="The Forgotten Droid">{{Rebels|The Forgotten Droid}}</ref><ref name="Hera's Heroes" /> Several of Skywalker's Y-wings destroyed Separatist [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class Droid Bombers]] that were tasked with destroying [[Lessu City|Lessu]] after the Separatist withdrawal from the [[capital]].<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref>

====Malastare====
{{Quote|Approaching drop zone. Everyone stay tight.''"<br />"''Remember, you've only got one shot at this.''"<br />"''Bombs away.|Goji and Anakin Skywalker, as the former releases the electro-proton bomb|<ref name="The Zillo Beast">{{TCW|The Zillo Beast}}</ref>}}
[[File:GojiPayloadSpecialist-SWZ.png|thumb|left|225px|Republic Y-wings prepare to drop an electro-proton bomb at Malastare.]]
In 21 BBY, Y-wings participated in the [[Battle of Malastare]], notably dropping an [[electro-proton bomb]] on the [[Separatist Droid Army]], inadvertently awakening the last surviving [[Zillo Beast]]. The battle ended with a joint Republic/[[Dug]] victory, with the Separatists being driven off the planet and the Dugs signing a [[Treaty of Malastare|treaty]], granting the Republic access to its considerable [[fuel]] reserves.<ref name="The Zillo Beast" />

====Geonosis====
[[File:BTL-B_Y-wing_rear.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Republic Y-wings depart to attack Geonosian gun emplacements at the Second Battle of Geonosis.]]
In [[21 BBY]], Republic Y-wings [[bomb]]ed Separatist anti-aircraft [[cannon]]s in the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], allowing Jedi Generals [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], Anakin Skywalker, and [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] to land at [[Point Rain]]. Y-wings also defended [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]]'s [[command ship]] from attack by [[Geonosian]] [[Nantex-class territorial defense starfighter|''Nantex''-class territorial defense starfighters]] and assisted the defense of Kenobi's position.<ref name="Landing at Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref>

====Kamino====
[[File:Y-wings_kamino.png|thumb|left|250px|Y-wings attack Separatist transports at Kamino.]]
Republic Y-wings joined [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter]]s and V-19 Torrent starfighters in the [[Battle of Kamino]], making bombing runs on Separatist [[transport]]s in [[Realspace|space]]. These transports, unknown to the [[Cloning|clone]] [[pilot]]s, carried [[Trident-class assault ship|''Trident''-class assault ships]], which landed in the [[ocean]] below and deployed [[AQ-series battle droid|aqua droids]] to [[Tipoca City]]. [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]] were eventually forced to withdraw after the clone [[trooper]]s&mdash;including the [[501st Legion]]&mdash;on Kamino mounted a counteroffensive against the [[battle droid]]s.<ref name="ARC Troopers">{{TCW|ARC Troopers (episode)}}</ref>

====Sullust====
Republic Y-wings saw action at the [[Battle of Sullust]], engaging Separatist starfighters and conducting bombing runs on their [[capital ship]]s. Darth Sidious ordered [[Count]] [[Dooku]] to kill [[Asajj Ventress]] in the battle, leading to a Separatist withdrawal and a Republic victory.<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref>

====Patitite Pattuna====
In [[20 BBY]], Republic Y-wings mounted a failed defense of Jedi General [[Adi Gallia]]'s [[flagship]] at the [[Battle of Patitite Pattuna]]. Astromech droid [[R2-D2]] and [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] escaped the doomed [[Star Destroyer]] aboard a Y-wing, and crashed on [[Patitite Pattuna]]'s surface. After repairing their [[Starship|ship]], the duo made a quick side-trip to [[Balnab]] in search of power, but were forced to abandon the ship. Not long after the Battle of Patitite Pattuna, the Republic mounted a [[rescue of Adi Gallia]] and recovered the droids, who had been picked up by [[Weequay]] [[pirate]]s and taken [[prisoner]] by the Separatists.<ref name="Nomad Droids">{{TCW|Nomad Droids}}</ref>

====Umbara====
[[File:OddballCC2237-SWZ.png|thumb|right|250px|Y-wings about to make an explosive delivery to Umbaran troops]]
Y-wings participated in the [[Battle of Umbara]], destroying Separatist [[frigate]]s guarding the planet and dropping ordnance on [[Umbaran Militia]] personnel who had ambushed the 501st [[Legion]].<ref name="Darkness on Umbara">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref> They also took part in atmospheric combat operations, engaging [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class starfighters]] and [[droid tri-fighter]]s in air-to-air combat.<ref name="Plan of Dissent">{{TCW|Plan of Dissent}}</ref> The Battle of [[Umbara]] ended with a Republic victory and the execution of the [[Treason|traitorous]] Jedi General [[Pong Krell]].<ref name="Carnage of Krell">{{TCW|Carnage of Krell}}</ref>

====The ''Negotiator''====
Republic Y-wings mounted a failed defense of Kenobi's flagship, the ''[[Negotiator]]'', after it fell under [[Attack on Obi-Wan Kenobi's fleet|attack]] by Separatist [[warship]]s. The Separatist victory prevented the Republic's involvement in the [[Rescue on Florrum|rescue]] of Ahsoka Tano, who had been [[Raid on the Crucible|abducted]] by Weequay [[Ohnaka Gang|pirates]] in the [[Florrum system]]. Her rescue fell to a group of six [[Jedi Initiate|younglings]] instead.<ref name="Bound for Rescue">{{TCW|Bound for Rescue}}</ref>

====Outer Rim Sieges====
[[File:Gold_Squadron_Anaxes.png|thumb|left|250px|Gold Squadron operated Y-wings at Anaxes.]]
In [[19 BBY]], [[Gold Squadron (Galactic Republic)|Gold Squadron]] used Y-wing bombers to attack Separatist anti-aircraft cannons stationed at the [[Anaxes assembly complex]] during the [[Battle of Anaxes]]<ref name="Unfinished Business">{{TCW|Unfinished Business (episode)}}</ref> of the [[Outer Rim Sieges]].<ref name="A Distant Echo">{{TCW|A Distant Echo}}</ref> The campaign ended with a Republic victory, the death of Admiral [[Trench]], and the defeat of all Separatist forces that were deployed on [[Anaxes]].<ref name="Unfinished Business" /> A BTL was under repair aboard the ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer ''[[Tribunal]]'' after the [[Siege of Mandalore]]. The Y-wing was used by Captain Rex and Ahsoka Tano to escape [[Order 66]]<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> and visit [[Padmé Amidala's funeral]].<ref name="Resolve">{{TOTJ|Resolve (episode)}}</ref> Rex would go onto use the Y-wing in the early [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Battle Scars">{{TBB|Battle Scars}}</ref>

===Age of the Empire===
====Overview====
Soon after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] in 19 BBY,<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> an [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] against Imperial rule began to take shape. Secretly supported by influential [[Senator]]s like [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]]<ref name="A Princess on Lothal">{{Rebels|A Princess on Lothal}}</ref> and [[Mon Mothma]],<ref name="Announcement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref> the various rebel cells operated whatever kinds of vehicles they could acquire. Y-wings, having been relegated to planetary defense duties, were popular craft for the rebels because of its built-in [[hyperdrive]] and durability. Sold to military surplus sellers or scrapyards, the Rebels sought to buy or steal as many as they could, with the Y-wing being the most common Rebel starfighter during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. They proved themselves to have superior firepower, durability, and dependability compared to the Empire's [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] and were used for a variety of missions, including escort, courier service, reconnaissance and surgical strikes. The BTL-A4 was the most common model, with most BTL-Bs having their bubble turrets removed in place of the remotely-controlled turret found on other variants.<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref><ref name="Secret Cargo">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref><ref name="RSOWM" />

====Skuhl====
In [[4 BBY]], at least five Y-wings engaged Imperial TIE fighters while [[Jyn Erso]] mounted an [[Escape from Skuhl|escape]] attempt from [[Imperial Military]] forces on [[Skuhl]]. Crossfire from the dogfight destroyed the [[SC3000 freighter]] ''[[Ponta One]]'', killing [[Hadder Ponta|both]] [[Akshaya Ponta|civilians]] on board.<ref name="Rebel Rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref>

====Yarma====
{{Quote|Y-wings? Kinda old. Can they fly?''"<br />"''Those bombers may be old, but they were solid in combat operations.''"<br />"''If we could steal a squadron's worth&hellip;''"<br />"''They would be key to building a strike fleet.|Hera Syndulla, Captain Rex, and Jun Sato|<ref name="Steps Into Shadow" />}}

In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Phoenix Cell]] launched a [[Mission to Reklam Station|mission]] to recover decommissioned Republic Y-wings from [[Reklam Station]], an aerial [[Salvage yard|scrapyard]] floating in the [[atmosphere]] of the planet [[Yarma]]. The mission ended in a rebel victory, with five Y-wings successfully stolen and Reklam station destroyed. Two of the initial force of seven were lost to Imperial fire, both piloted by Chopper under remote control. Although [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]] originally intended for the Y-wings to enter service with [[Phoenix Squadron]], the ships were instead to transferred to [[Massassi Group|Jan Dodonna's unit]] on [[Yavin 4]].<ref name="Steps Into Shadow" />

====Backbone of the Rebellion====
[[Imperial cadet]]s were trained to destroy Y-wing starfighters at [[Skystrike Academy]] on [[Montross]]. [[Sabine Wren]] and [[Wedge Antilles]] participated in one such training prior to their first attempt to [[escape from Skystrike Academy]] alongside [[Derek Klivian]] and [[Rake Gahree]].<ref name="The Antilles Extraction" />

====Archeon Nebula====
[[File:Archeon_Nebula.png|thumb|left|250px|BTL-A4 Y-wings escort the ''Ghost'' into the Archeon Nebula.]]
The Y-wings of [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]] escorted the ''[[Chandrila Mistress]]'', with former [[Imperial Senate|Imperial Senator]] Mon Mothma aboard, to a secret rendezvous at the with the [[Spectres]] aboard the ''[[Ghost]]''. From there, the ''Ghost'' would transport Mothma to [[Dantooine]] for a meeting of high-level leaders of the rebellion.<ref name="Secret Cargo" />

The ''Chandrila Mistress'' had been tracked by Imperial forces, and shortly after arrival it was attacked by an [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]], a [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]], and several [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s. Although Gold Squadron was able to destroy the TIEs and [[cruiser]]s, Mothma and her crew were forced to evacuate the ''Chandrila Mistress'' and transfer to the ''Ghost''. The Rebels escaped into hyperspace and traveled to the [[Archeon Nebula]], which the Spectres had hoped would help conceal their movements from the Empire. [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] predicted that they would do this, and dispatched a force of [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] and TIEs to destroy them, including a [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]] prototype piloted by [[Vult Skerris]]. ==Behind the scenes==
The Y-wing starfighter first appeared in the now [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] promotional novel [[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|''Star Wars: From the Adventures of Luke Skywalker'']], the very first ''[[Star Wars]]'' product ever released. It was a novelization of the later [[canon]] film known at the time of its release as [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'']].

In the novelization, the Y-wings that flew in the Battle of Yavin operated as part of Red Squadron, with the X-wings being part of Blue Squadron. Because of the limits of blue screen technology at the time, the Y-wing squadron was changed to Gold Squadron and the X-wings to Red Squadron.

Y-wings appeared in numerous other narrative media released between [[1977]] and [[2014]], most notably ''[[The Star Wars Holiday Special]]'', the other two films of the [[original trilogy]], and [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|'' The Clone Wars'']].

In the original release of ''Star Wars'', the Y-wing starfighters were plastic models shot against blue screens with computer-controlled cameras. In [[List of changes in Star Wars re-releases|later versions]] of the film released to theaters and home media, some shots have been removed and replaced with computer-animated ones.

A transparent turret was originally included in the design, but was removed when it was realized that blue screen technology was not yet advanced enough to composite correctly with the turret. The turrets, along with a lot of body paneling and the gunner's position, were added back in to the Y-wing's design on ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars''.

The look of the [[Clone Wars]]-era Y-wing [[bomber]]s stems directly from the design of the original Y-wing and speculation from ''A New Hope'' Visual Effects Art Director [[Joe Johnston]] that the Y-wing used by the Rebel Alliance was a "chopped" version of an older craft. The appearance of the Y-wings seen in the episode "[[Shadow of Malevolence]]" is intended to bridge the design aesthetic of the [[Prequel trilogy|prequel]] and original trilogies.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/shadow-of-malevolence-episode-guide|text="Shadow of Malevolence" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>

On [[April 25]], 2014, [[Lucasfilm]] announced that the former Expanded Universe would be discontinued and rebranded as "Legends," and that it would be replaced with a new single-level canonicity system in which new releases were declared either canon or non-canon from the outset. Since the rebranding, Y-wings have appeared in numerous narrative, promotional, and reference ''Star Wars'' products.

In "[[THE TWINS]]," an episode of ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'', Y-wings are used by [[TRIGGER Empire|Am's Galactic Empire]].<ref name="The Twins">{{Visions|THE TWINS}}</ref>
