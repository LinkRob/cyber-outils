'''Fixer''', also identified by the number '''RC-1140''', was an elite [[clone commando]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] who served as the second-in-command of [[Delta Squad]], a [[special forces]] unit within the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] that was active during the [[Clone Wars]]. Due to his extreme focus on military regulations, he would use the [[operating number]]s of his comrades instead of their chosen names during missions. Fixer served as the [[squad]]'s second-in-command following [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Boss (clone commando)|Boss]]. Delta Squad participated in some of the most dangerous missions of the war, including retrieving the bodies of [[Jedi General]] [[Halsey]] and [[Padawan]] [[Knox]].

==Biography==
[[Clone commando]] RC-1140, who became known by the name Fixer,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> was [[Cloning|clone]] [[Reproduction|grown]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] from the [[DNA]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]].<ref name="Clone Troopers">{{Databank|clone-troopers|Clone Troopers}}</ref> Fixer served as a [[commando]] within the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] and earned his name because of his skills as a [[slicer]].<ref name="Join the Battle" /> [[Gender|He]] participated in the pan-[[The galaxy|galactic]] [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Witches of the Mist">{{TCW|Witches of the Mist}}</ref> serving as the second-in-command of the [[special forces]] unit [[Delta Squad]].<ref name="Join the Battle" />

[[File:DeltaSquad-WOTM.png|thumb|left|250px|Delta Squad returns from Devaron]]
Delta Squad participated in some of the war's most dangerous missions.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> At one point, Fixer and his squad-mates were tasked with securing territory and deployed directly from a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] [[gunship]] to enter the [[Ground warfare|battle]].<ref name="Seizing Territory">{{Destiny|set=Spark of Hope|cardname=Seizing Territory|link=https://swdestinydb.com/card/11129}}</ref> In another instance, Fixer and the rest of Delta Squad were dispatched to the [[planet]] [[Devaron]] in the aftermath of the [[Devaron massacre|massacre]] that took place there and recovered the bodies of [[Jedi General]] [[Halsey]] and [[Padawan]] [[Knox]], and returned them to [[Coruscant]]. The squad was met on the landing platform by [[Jedi High Council]] members [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Plo Koon]], [[Saesee Tiin]], and [[Adi Gallia]], who noted that the massacre was of similar caliber of the damage done by [[Asajj Ventress]] who was presumed dead. Security footage of the battle was recovered and included a hologram of Halsey's death at the hands of [[Savage Opress]], who was unknown to the Jedi and the Republic at that [[time]].<ref name="Witches of the Mist" />

==Personality and traits==
As a [[human]] clone<ref name="Join the Battle" /> of Jango Fett,<ref name="Clone Troopers" /> Fixer was 1.83 [[meter]]s in height, or six [[Foot|ft]], and was born on the planet [[Kamino]]. He was considered to be quiet, grim, and focused on following regulations to the point that he used his comrades' [[operating number]]s during missions.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Skills and abilities==
As a clone commando, Fixer was an elite soldier and had many abilities and training. These abilities included slicer skills, armed and unarmed combat, military tactics, infiltration, reconnaissance, mechanical repairs, and [[jetpack]] training. He was known as Fixer because of his skills as a slicer and a tinkerer.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Equipment==
While on duty, Fixer was equipped with unique [[Katarn-class commando armor|''Katarn''-class Commando Armor]] that was painted in [[Color|green]] and white, and was outfitted with a [[backpack]]. During combat, RC-1140 utilized a [[DC-17m Interchangeable Weapon System]].<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fixer first appeared in the new [[canon]] in [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|season three]] episode "[[Witches of the Mist]]" of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' that was released on [[January 21]], [[2011]].<ref name="Witches of the Mist" /> He first appeared in the [[2005]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Republic Commando]]''. Fixer was identified in the episode guide for "Witches of the Mist,"<ref name="Episode Guide old">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode314.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Witches of the Mist}}</ref> while his designation number, RC-1140, was established in the [[2021]] canon reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'', authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Join the Battle" />
