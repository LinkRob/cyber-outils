An '''exo-glove''' was a device meant to give [[starship]] crewmembers a longer reach when working in tight spaces. Consisting of a large metal glove with [[servomotor]]s for a strengthened grip, it could extend forward to grab out-of-reach objects. [[Spaceport]] dockworkers commonly used exo-gloves for [[Unarmed combat|fighting]].<ref name="TMCAFOF">''[[The Mighty Chewbacca in the Forest of Fear!]]''</ref>
