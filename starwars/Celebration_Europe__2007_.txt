'''Celebration Europe''' is a fan convention which was held at the [[Wikipedia:ExCeL Exhibition Centre|ExCeL Exhibition Centre]] in [[Wikipedia:London|London]] from [[July 13]]<ref name="The Bad Batch Returns" /> to [[July 15]] [[2007]].<ref name="Billy Dee" /> It was the first Celebration event to be held outside the United States. Its purpose was to primarily celebrate the 30th anniversary of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''A New Hope'']], the original ''Star Wars'' film which debuted on [[May 25]], [[1977]].

[[Warwick Davis]] was the Celebrity Master of Ceremonies.

Celebration Europe shared many of the features of [[Celebration IV]], which was held in [[Wikipedia:Los Angeles, California|Los Angeles, California]] earlier in the year. The convention was presented by [[Lucasfilm]] and managed by [[The Cards Inc. Group]].

It was also notable for bringing in a replica of the [[Intergalactic Passport (real-life artifact)|Intergalactic Passports]] that were originally issued as backstage passes for the filming of ''Star Wars Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back''. They included 10 collectable stamps, each acquired from specific events in the showing.

==Development==
On [[September 25]], [[2006]], StarWars.com posted [http://www.starwars.com/community/event/celebration/news20060925.html an official announcement] regarding Celebration Europe.

===Celebration Europe Opening Ceremonies and A New Hope screening===
During the informal opening ceremonies, hosted by Steve Sansweet, kicked of the celebration and introduced special guests Including Mark Hamill and Ian McDiarmid, who made a rare public appearance and talked about his experience as [[Palpatine/Legends|the Emperor of the Galaxy]]. McDiarmid also announced [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].

===Celebration Europe Art Show===
At artists' Alley in the ExCeL Exhibition Centre, visitors could browse though the works of more than 20 artists. Each artist offered a limited edition print inspired by the thirtieth anniversary that was only available at Celebration Europe. They also offered a number of other prints. ===The One Man Star Wars Trilogy===
Fans of the Star Wars movies and fans of live theater could enjoy an exceptional treat when [[The One Man Star Wars Trilogy|Charles Ross]] performed ''[[The One Man Star Wars Trilogy]]'' in the specially constructed 2000 seat theater in the main halls at Celebration Europe. The Canadian actor delighted audiences daily at London's ExCel Exhibition Center from Friday July 13th through Sunday, July 15th.

===Lawrence Noble sculpting===
[[Lawrence Noble]], the artist who sculpted the statue of [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] on display at [[Lucasfilm]]'s [[One Letterman|Letterman Digital Arts Center]], created a life-size bust of [[Grand Moff/Legends|Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin/Legends|Tarkin]]. He sculpted all three days of the event, but took regular breaks.<ref name="CEshowguide">''Celebration Europe Show Guide''</ref>
