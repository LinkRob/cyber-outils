The '''Crimson Dawn Hideout''' was a base for the criminal [[syndicate]] [[Crimson Dawn]] in the eastern edge of the [[Grazing Fields]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the Dawn used the hideout for [[Smuggler|smuggling]], monitoring communications and producing equipment and [[Forger (profession)|counterfeit]] [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|canon}}
During 3 ABY,<ref name="Date" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] stole a number of items from the hideout including an [[Anaxes]] ruffian [[jacket]], [[Eleera Soi]]'s vault [[keycard]] and a copy of a [[Imperial Security Bureau]] report on [[Bram Shano]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Crimson Dawn Hideout appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The Anaxes ruffian jacket that is retrieved from the hideout increases the ammo capacity for weapons that the player are picked up.<ref name="Outlaws" />
