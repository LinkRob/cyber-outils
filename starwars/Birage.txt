'''Birage''' was a small [[city]] located on the [[Arli's moon|moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Arli]]. The settlement was composed of buildings constructed from [[sandcrete]] that were clustered in seven neighborhoods around a central square, also including a [[spaceport]]. [[Jordanna Sparkburn|Jordanna]] and [[Harth Sparkburn]], who resided in a [[San Tekka family manor|manor]] of the [[San Tekka clan|San Tekka family]] near Birage, attended school in the city and often visited it in their childhood. After Arli's [[moon]] fell into the [[Occlusion Zone]] territory of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]], Jordanna visited Birage with [[Cair San Tekka]], another relative, and the pair rescued the city's inhabitants from a [[blight]] that was slowly overtaking the entire moon.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />
