'''Alor Keto''' was a [[Gender|male]] member of the [[Keto family]] who ruled as the [[Emperor]] of the [[planet]] [[Empress Teta]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. Keto's rule lasted several [[Standard decade|decades]], but was weak and ineffectual, and Keto himself was a puppet of the [[Mining Guild]]. During Keto's rule, his health began to decline. His niece, [[Eara Lota]], was the rightful heir of Empress Teta.<ref name="Nexus of Power" />
