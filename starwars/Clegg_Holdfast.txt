'''Clegg Holdfast''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Nosaurian]] [[journalist]] for ''[[Podracing Quarterly]]'' and [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[pilot]] who participated in the [[Boonta Eve Classic]], which took place during the [[Invasion of Naboo]]. He proudly sported medals he had earned while [[podracing]], despite his fellow racers perceiving him as a better journalist rather than racer.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Holdfast piloted a [[Keizar-Volvec|Voltec]] [[9T9-B Wasp|KT9 ''Wasp'']] [[podracer]] and started the race from the front row.<ref name="ThePhantomMenace" /> While the mechanics attended his [[podracer]], being a [[journalist]] himself, Holdfast searched for dirty news in the [[cantina]] of [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]]'s [[hangar]].<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> Holdfast would not complete the Boonta Eve Classic however, crashing in the second lap after his [[Podracer engine|podracer's engines]] were destroyed by a blaze from [[Sebulba]]'s flame-jets.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /><ref name="ThePhantomMenace" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clegg Holdfast was created for the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], although he first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends]] [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|novelization of the film]]. In the theatrical release of the film, Holdfast was only seen fleetingly in the cockpit of his podracer. For the DVD release, new footage was created showcasing Holdfast on the starting grid and having his podracer destroyed by the rival pilot Sebulba. In early concept art by [[Terryl Whitlatch]], Holdfast was depicted as being covered in fur, a trait he did not possess in the final film. An action figure of Holdfast was later released by [[Hasbro]] in [[2009]]. The figure came with removable goggles and a [[blaster pistol]]. He was later rereleased in a multi-pack which contained several other podracer pilots. Along with his blaster and goggles being included in the pack, Holdfast was also given a flag, a base on which to stand on, and a trading card.

In the revised [[Star Wars: Episode I The Beginning|rough draft]] of the script for ''The Phantom Menace'', Clegg Holdfast is said to be from [[Sullust]].<ref name="DB2">{{DB|organization|boontapodrace|Boonta Eve Classic Podracers}}</ref>
