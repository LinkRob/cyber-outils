In a military organization, an '''assignment''' (or '''position''') was a post of duty to which one was assigned. It was distinct from [[Military rank|rank]], which referred to a hierarchical level. For example, after gaining the rank of [[captain]] in the [[Imperial Navy]], [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] was assigned as [[Executive officer|first officer]] aboard the [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]] ''[[Thunder Wasp]]''.<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> The term was also used for a particular mission or task that an individual, a group, or a [[Starship|ship]], was given to do.<ref name="Battlefront: Twilight Company">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref>
