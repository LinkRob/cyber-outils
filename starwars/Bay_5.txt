'''Bay 5''' was a [[hangar]] within the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Sovereign]]''. It housed multiple [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], as well as [[The Inquisitor's TIE Advanced|the Grand Inquisitor's TIE Advanced]].<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Spectres]] rebel cell landed their [[Rebels TIE fighter|TIE fighter]] in the bay and detonated an [[electromagnetic pulse]], which disabled the ''Sovereign''. Following subsequent damage to its power core, the [[Star Destroyer]] was destroyed.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bay 5 first appeared in "[[Fire Across the Galaxy]],"<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> the fifteenth and final episode of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. The episode first aired on [[March 2]], [[2015]].<ref name="Insider 156">{{InsiderCite|156|Star Wars Rebels Recounted|''Star Wars Rebels'' Recounted}}</ref>
