'''Boring IV''' was a [[Planet/Legends|planet]] that occupied the fourth [[Orbit/Legends|orbital]] position in its [[Star system/Legends|system]].<ref name="LCL" />

{{RPGseed}}
At some point after [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[Les Couteaux lumineux]]" takes place after the [[Destruction of Alderaan/Legends|Destruction of Alderaan]], which [[The New Essential Chronology]] dates to [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]].</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] installed an advanced post at the planet. One group of Rebels participated in the installation and, due to their success, were entrusted with another mission to look into the [[Death/Legends|death]] of [[Arkiin Smir]], a rebel extremist and the leader of the [[Luminous Knives]] [[Terrorist/Legends|terrorist]] group, in order to learn the whereabouts of his accomplices.<ref name="LCL" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Boring IV was mentioned in the [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure "[[Les Couteaux lumineux]]" written by [[Michael Moret]] for use with [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. The adventure was published in the [[Casus Belli 70|seventieth issue]] of the French [[Casus Belli (magazine)|''Casus Belli'']] magazine, released through [[July]] and [[August]] of [[1992]].<ref name="LCL" /> As the adventure was released outside of the [[Lucas Licensing]] process, it is considered non-[[canon]]ical with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="UnlicensedStatus">{{UnlicensedStatus}}</ref>
