The '''Brema sector''',<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> also known as the '''Sullust sector''',<ref name="Trkn" /> was a [[sector]] located in the [[galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Leia 2" /> It contained the [[Sullust system]], which was used as a staging point for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s strike on the second [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="ROJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>
