'''Cerakote''' was a [[Color|dark silver-gray]] material that could be used in [[lightsaber]] construction.<ref name="Fallen Order" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Te'Ami's lightsaber|lightsaber]] of [[Duros]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Te'Ami]] was made of cerakote and had a [[copper]] crosspiece.<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> when [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] traveled to the [[planet]] [[Zeffo]], he discovered some cerakote in the [[Ice Caves]] and kept it for making modifications to his [[Jaro Tapal|former master's]] [[Jaro Tapal's lightsaber|lightsaber]], which he received from his [[Jedi Master]] Jaro Tapal shortly before his [[death]] during [[Order 66]]<ref name="Fallen Order" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Order66">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cerakote first appeared in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]]. In the game, it is one of the twenty-four materials players can collect in order to customize the appearance of Cal Kestis' lightsaber.<ref name="Fallen Order" />
