The '''Cusverians'''<ref name="TAVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="The Acolyte" /> [[Levgo Mar]] was a member of this species.<ref name="TAVG" />

==Biology and appearance==
The Cusverians<ref name="TAVG" /> were a bipedal [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that were distinguished by their pointed [[ear]]s, upturned [[nose]]s, and [[Color|dark]] symmertical facial stripes, three straight ones from the tops of their [[eye]]s and around the sides of their heads, as well as one squiggly one extending down from the bottom of their eyes to their necks.<ref name="The Acolyte" />

==Species in the galaxy==
By [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> a member of this, [[Levgo Mar]],<ref name="TAVG" /> species served as a [[Jedi]] of the [[Jedi Order]], operating out of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] just prior to the [[Mission to Khofar]]<ref name="The Acolyte" /> and assisting with the investigation as they specialized in forensic analysis.<ref name="TAVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The species made their debut in the [[Day (episode)|fourth episode]] of the [[2024]] television series, ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', directed by [[Alex García López]].<ref name="The Acolyte" /> Their name was revealed within the ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''<ref name="TAVG" /> which released in select countries on [[June 5]], [[2025]].<ref name="DK UK">{{DK|url=uk/book/9780241710425-star-wars-the-acolyte-visual-guide|text=Star Wars The Acolyte Visual Guide}}</ref>
