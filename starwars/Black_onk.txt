'''Black onk''' was a substance that could be used to inlay [[lightsaber hilt]]s. During the [[Clone Wars]], a series of conflicts fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the breakaway [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the [[architect droid]] [[Huyang]] told a group of [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] aboard the ''[[Crucible]]'' that a [[Color|black]] onk-infused hilts was an option in the process of building their [[lightsaber]].<ref name="A Test of Strength" />

==Behind the scenes==
Black onk was first mentioned in "[[A Test of Strength]]," the seventh episode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]],<ref name="A Test of Strength" /> which originally aired on [[November 10]], [[2012]].<ref name="A Test of Strength air date">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep507|text="A Test of Strength" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide|nolive=1}}</ref>
