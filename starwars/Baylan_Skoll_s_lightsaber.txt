'''Baylan Skoll's lightsaber''' was the personal [[lightsaber]] wielded by [[Jedi]]-turned-[[mercenary]] [[Baylan Skoll]].

==History==
[[File:Gist-Ahsoka4Cover.png|thumb|left|250px|Baylan Skoll wields his blade against Ahsoka Tano]]
Baylan Skoll's lightsaber<ref name="SW">{{SW|url=news/sdcc-2023-ahsoka-props|text=SDCC 2023: See ''Ahsoka'' Props and Costumes from the Lucasfilm Pavilion}}</ref> was constructed by the [[human]] [[Baylan Skoll]] during his days as a student within the [[Jedi Order]]<ref name="Part One" /> during the late [[Republic Era]]. As a [[Jedi]], Skoll fought in the [[Clone Wars]] as a [[Jedi General]]<ref name="Part Six" /> and managed to survive the [[Great Jedi Purge]], taking up the life of a [[mercenary]] instead. At least during his days as a mercenary, Skoll's blade had a [[Color|orange-red]] blade.<ref name="Part Four">{{Ahsoka|Part Four: Fallen Jedi}}</ref> Skoll also took on the human [[Shin Hati]] as his apprentice, training her to be more than the Jedi of old, at least in his opinion. In her capacity as his apprentice, she also wielded an orange-red blade.<ref name="Part Six" />

Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{AhsokaSeriesDate}}</ref> Skoll wielded his lightsaber throughout the [[Quest to find Ezra Bridger|search]] for the missing [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn,"<ref name="Part Four" /> who was lost on the [[extragalactic]] [[planet]] [[Peridea]], where Skoll believed a great power was hiding.<ref name="Part Six" /> First, in the [[Attack on the Vesper|attack]] on the [[New Republic]] [[starship]] ''[[Vesper]]'' he and Hati embarked upon to rescue<ref name="Part One" /> his employer and Thrawn's rescuer-to-be<ref name="Part Six" /> [[Morgan Elsbeth]], he used it to [[Death|kill]] New Republic [[Captain]] [[Hayle]] and many [[New Republic soldier|Republic soldiers]]. [[Huyang]], the [[architect droid]] who originally helped Skoll build his lightsaber, was able to identify the owner by a hologram of the hilt.<ref name="Part One" /> Skoll later used his lightsaber to [[Incident on Seatos|duel]] [[Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="Part Four" /> On Peridea, Skoll briefly wielded his lightsaber against Tano again before continuing on his own quest.<ref name="Part Seven">{{Ahsoka|Part Seven: Dreams and Madness}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Baylan Skoll's lightsaber first appeared in the teaser trailer of the ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]'' television series, which debuted in [[August]] [[2023]].<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|HnzNZ0Mdx4I|Ahsoka &#124; Teaser Trailer &#124; Disney+|sw_url=video/ahsoka-teaser-trailer}}</ref>  

According to showrunner [[Dave Filoni]], Skoll's lightsaber being orange-red instead of the traditional [[Sith]] red blade was intentional because he always felt as a kid that [[Darth Vader's lightsaber]] seemed to fluctuate from visual effects to being a little bit more orange, so he wished to not make a straight-up orange lightsaber, but a double-colored lightsaber so kids who watch the show may not come up with quick conclusions about Baylan Skoll's real allegiances.<ref name="Screen Rant">{{WebCite|url=https://screenrant.com/ahsoka-dave-filoni-orange-lightsabers-response|text=''Dave Filoni Confirms Orange Lightsabers in Ahsoka''|author=Caitlin Tyrrel|date=2023-04-09|work=[[Wikipedia:Screen Rant|''Screen Rant'']]|archivedate=20230410162929}}</ref>
