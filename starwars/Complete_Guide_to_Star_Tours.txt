The '''''Complete Guide to Star Tours''''' is a 12-page document that pertains to the [[Tokyo Disneyland]] version of the original [[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] ride. It was published by the Planning Office for Tokyo Disneyland Relations and [[Wikipedia:Panasonic|Panasonic]], then known as Matsushita Electric Industrial Co. The guide features pictures and brief descriptions of the many [[Droid/Legends|droids]] found in ''Star Tours'', and a detailed description of the attraction itself. The guide also contains information on the Tomorrowland section of Tokyo Disneyland.<ref name="Complete Guide" />

Though the book includes no publishing date, the ride opened [[July 12]], [[1989]] in Tokyo Disneyland, and advertised attractions in the guide include the film ''American Journeys'', which was shown at the park until [[August 31]], [[1992]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=a-to-z/american-journeys-film|text=American Journeys (film)}}</ref>
