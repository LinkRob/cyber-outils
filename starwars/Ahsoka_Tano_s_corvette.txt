In the [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], also known as "[[Fulcrum]]," had a '''corvette''' that was used in discreet meetings.<ref name="Gathering Forces" /> This [[CR90 corvette]]<ref name="CR90Facebook" /> was primarily [[Color|white]], but had some blue markings on it. On the [[4 BBY|fifteenth]] [[Empire Day|anniversary]] of the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the ''[[Ghost]]'' docked with the [[corvette]] so [[Hera Syndulla]] could hand over [[Tseebo]], a [[Rodian]] who knew vital Imperial secrets, to Tano to learn more about what he knew.<ref name="Gathering Forces" />

The corvette, alongside the [[Liberator (CR90 corvette)|''Liberator'']] and another CR90 later participated in the [[Action at Mustafar]] over [[Mustafar]], having been summoned as reinforcements by [[C1-10P]] "Chopper" to rescue his friends. The corvettes blasted their way through the [[squadron]] of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] chasing the [[Spectres]] before retreating back into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref> It was also present for [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[Attack on Phoenix Squadron (4 BBY)|attack]] on [[Phoenix Squadron]], which saw their [[flagship]] ''[[Phoenix Home]]'' destroyed. The CR90s, including this one, were able to escape the battle.<ref name="Siege of Lothal" />
