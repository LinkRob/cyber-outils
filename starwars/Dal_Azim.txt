'''Dal Azim''' was a [[human]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]], who during the [[High Republic Era]] was apprenticed to the [[Thisspiasian]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Oppo Rancisis]]. Around [[255 BBY]], he assisted Rancisis on a mission to the [[planet]] [[Zygerria]], where they [[Attack on the Zygerrian camp|attacked]] a [[Zygerrian Alliance]] [[Unidentified Zygerrian camp|camp]]. They rescued and freed many [[Slavery|enslaved]] people, including the [[Twi'lek]] [[Lourna Dee]], whom they saved from being attacked by the [[Zygerrian]] [[Keeper]] [[Vardem Kroleyic]]. Using [[the Force]], Azim also freed Dee from the [[shock collar]] placed on her by the Zygerrians.

==Biography==
===Rescue mission===
{{Quote|Everyone is from somewhere.|Dal Azim|audio=Dal-EveryoneIsFromSomewhere.flac|<ref name="Tempest Runner" />}}
[[File:OppoRancisis-THRSOS1.png|thumb|175px|Dal Azim's master, Oppo Rancisis]]
Dal Azim was a [[human]]<ref name="Script" /> who was [[Reproduction|born]] during the [[High Republic Era]]<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> around [[270 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> He<ref name="Script" /> eventually became the [[Padawan]] of the [[Thisspiasian]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Oppo Rancisis]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> Around [[255 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> when he was fifteen [[Standard year|years]] old, Azim joined Rancisis on a mission to the [[planet]] [[Zygerria]]<ref name="Script" /> with the [[Galactic Republic]]. Azim and Rancisis [[Attack on the Zygerrian camp|attacked]] a [[Zygerrian Alliance]] [[Unidentified Zygerrian camp|camp]] on the planet in order to free the [[Slavery|enslaved]] people held there.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

Azim and Rancisis eventually broke into pens where enslaved people were held<ref name="Script" /> and saved the enslaved [[Twi'lek]] [[Lourna Dee]] from the [[Zygerrian]] [[Keeper]] [[Vardem Kroleyic]], who was attacking her after she had attempted to use the [[Jedi]]'s attack as an opportunity to revolt. After Kroleyic was incapacitated by Rancisis using [[the Force]], Azim checked on Dee, before introducing himself and his master and telling her that they were there to help. Dee told Azim that Kroleyic had shot her [[Unidentified human (Lourna Dee)|friend]], to which Azim offered condolences and an apology. He then comforted her and assured her of her safety.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

===Aftermath===
{{Quote|This way, Master. Here she is.|Azim leading Rancisis to Lourna Dee|audio=Dal-HereSheIs.flac|<ref name="Tempest Runner" />}}
Later, after the fighting was done, Azim led Rancisis to meet Dee outside the pens they rescued her from. Rancisis had heard that she had led a revolt and wanted to speak with her. When asked of her origin by Rancisis, Dee stated that she was from nowhere, but Azim refuted this, insisting that everyone was from somewhere, which Rancisis agreed with.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

[[File:LournaDeeMaskless-2021THR10.png|thumb|left|180px|Lourna Dee, whom Azim and Rancisis saved from a camp on Zygerria]]
After Dee expressed her anger at being captured, Rancisis offered to help Dee by removing the [[shock collar]] that was placed around her neck, assigning the task to Azim as he believed it would be an interesting challenge for him. Dee was hesitant to allow him to, as she believed that it would shock her if they tried to remove it without a key, but Rancisis assured her that Azim could do it using the Force. At Rancisis's prompt,<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> Azim used the Force to deactivate the electronic mechanism to safely unlock the collar, saying that the Force was with them as he did so.<ref name="Script" /> Dee thanked Azim, who then removed the collar off her neck for her.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

Afterward, Rancisis convinced Dee to attend the [[Carida Academy|Carida Military Academy]]. He checked in on her frequently, and during one such check-in, she expressed that she felt like she could not thrive there because she was unlike Azim or Rancisis.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I'm so sorry. But it's going to be all right now. You're safe.|Dal Azim comforting Lourna Dee|audio=Dal-YoureSafe.flac|<ref name="Tempest Runner" />}}
Dal Azim stood 1.68 [[meter]]s (5 [[Foot|ft]], 6 in) tall.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> A kind and compassionate person, Azim made sure to check on Lourna Dee and her health to make sure that she was alright after rescuing her from her enslavers. He then offered condolences when she revealed to him that her friend had been killed. He also assured her that she was safe.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

==Powers and abilities==
{{Quote|The Force is with us. Always.|Dal Azim, while using the Force|audio=Dal-TheForceIsWithUs.flac|<ref name="Tempest Runner" />}}
Having been trained as a Jedi, Dal Azim knew how to use the Force and was able to move objects [[Telekinesis|telekinetically]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> Azim used the Force to deactivate and unlock a shock collar that had been placed on Lourna Dee.<ref name="Script" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dal Azim appeared in flashbacks in the [[2021]] audio drama ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> The character's last name was first revealed in the drama's [[2022]] [[The High Republic: Tempest Runner (script)|script release]].<ref name="Script" />
