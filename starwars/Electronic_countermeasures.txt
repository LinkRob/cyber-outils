'''Electronic countermeasures''' was an electronic system designed to fool [[sensor]]s into reading wrong or no information.<ref name="TLJ: IC-S">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> The [[SecuriCase]] [[Regal Voyager case|carrying cases]] utilized by the [[Regal Voyager]] passenger line were equipped with strong electronic countermeasures. When [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], brought several items recovered from the [[Jedi Order]] [[outpost on Ord Jannak|outpost]] on [[Ord Jannak]] to Jedi Master [[Eeth Koth]] in a Regal Voyager case, Koth remarked on the electronic countermeasures.<ref name="TLF" />
