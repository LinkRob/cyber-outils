The '''Federal District''', also known as the '''Senate District''', '''Executive District''', '''Legislative District''', '''Military District''', and colloquially referred to as the '''Core of Coruscant''' or '''Core Square''', was the central [[Politics|government]] district on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. It was the location of the [[Imperial Senate Building]], the [[Avenue of the Core Founders]], the [[Imperial Executive Building]], and the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] which, after [[proclamation of the New Order]], was rechristened as the Imperial Palace. Other notable locations included ''[[500 Republica]]'', the [[Imperial Inspectorate HQ|headquarters]] of the [[Naval Intelligence Agency]], and the [[COMPNOR arcology]], which contained the [[ISB Central Office|central offices]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]].

During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Bombing of Coruscant's central power distribution grid|disabled]] the [[central power distribution grid]] of Coruscant, causing a power outage in the Federal District. The district was also [[Zillo Beast incident|damaged]] by a [[Zillo Beast]] that had been [[Battle of Malastare|captured]] on [[Malastare]] and taken to the [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[capital]]. After the [[Galactic Republic]] became the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Jedi temple|temple]] became the palace of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. Following [[Emperor]] Palpatine's [[death]] in the [[Battle of Endor]], the Empire fought to remain in control of the Federal District during a [[Coruscant civil war|civil war]] between the [[Coruscanti government|Imperial government]] and the [[Coruscanti]] that supported the [[New Republic]].

==Description==
[[File:Galactic_Senate.png|thumb|left|250px|The Avenue of the Core Founders and the Galactic Senate Building were located in the Federal District of Coruscant.]]
Under the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]'s central [[Politics|government]] district was located<ref name="SWCL2016" /> in the northern hemisphere<ref name="Cataclysm" /> and generally referred to as the Federal District,<ref name="SWCL2016" /> Senate District, or Legislative District.<ref name="Ultimate">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> It would also be known as the Military District during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="BMF 67">{{BuildFalconCite|67|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> Informally, however, the Federal District was referred to by [[Coruscanti]] as the "Core of Coruscant" or "Core Square."<ref name="Thrawn" /> The Federal District was the focal point of the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]] that served as the [[Galactic Senate Chamber|meeting place]] of the [[Galactic Senate]]. In front of the Senate Building was the [[Imperial Plaza|Senate Plaza]], whose [[Avenue of the Core Founders]] bore statues dedicated to the founding members of the Republic. Located nearby was the [[Imperial Executive Building|Senate Office Building]], where the [[Supreme Chancellor]] maintained a [[Emperor's office|suite]] of offices, ''[[500 Republica]]'', a prestigious and exclusive residential tower where [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] maintained an [[Palpatine's apartment|apartment]] during his service as [[senator]] of [[Naboo]], and the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]], which served as the home of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="SWCL2016" />

[[File:Imperial_Temple.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Imperial Palace, formerly known as the Jedi Temple, was the focal point of the Federal District under the Galactic Empire.]]
The [[Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center]], the Republic's primary [[Prison|incarceration facility]] on the planet, was also one of many buildings located in the district.<ref name="BMF 67" /> With the [[Republic Era|fall of the Republic]] and the [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishment]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Jedi temple|Jedi Temple]] was converted into the Imperial Palace.<ref name="SWCL2016" /> The area surrounding the former [[Jedi]] Temple became a focal point of the so-called "supreme triangle" of the newly-christened Imperial Palace, together with the [[Naval Intelligence Agency]]'s [[Imperial Inspectorate HQ|headquarters]] and the [[COMPNOR arcology]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> that housed the [[ISB Central Office|Central Office]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]].<ref name="KnowEverything" /> It was also within the Federal District that the [[Imperial Military]] constructed a [[Combined headquarters of the Imperial Army and Navy|combined headquarters]] for the [[Imperial Army]] and the [[Imperial Navy]].<ref name="Thrawn" /> The district was covered with towering [[skyscraper]]s and its skies were kept clear via weather control.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==History==
[[File:BombingEffects-HOBS.png|thumb|left|250px|The bombing of Coruscant's central power distribution grid resulted in blackouts across the Federal District.]]
In the [[Standard day|days]] of the Republic, the term "Senate District" was synonymous with the business and power of the [[Galaxy|galactic]] government.<ref name="Tarkin" /> The [[Clone Wars]] brought about great changes to the district, as it saw a massive build-up of new structures to house the increase in government agencies that were created in support of the Republic's war effort.<ref name="Ultimate" /> In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="310 date">{{TCWcanondates|310}}</ref><ref name="219 date">{{TCWcanondates|219}}</ref> the Senate District was the site of a [[terrorist]] [[Bombing of Coruscant's central power distribution grid|bombing]]<ref name="HoBS">{{TCW|Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref> and a [[Zillo Beast incident|rampage]] by a [[Zillo Beast]] that the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] [[Battle of Malastare|captured]] on the planet [[Malastare]].<ref name="The Zillo Beast Strikes Back">{{TCW|The Zillo Beast Strikes Back}}</ref>

[[General]] [[Grievous]] and his strike team landed in the Federal District during the [[Battle of Coruscant]].<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> During the execution of [[Order 66]], the [[501st Legion]] [[Operation: Knightfall|secured]] the Jedi Temple under the command of the newly-christened [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> The [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]], publicly known as [[Galactic Emperor]] Sheev Palpatine, subsequently confiscated the Temple and refurbished the structure as the Imperial Palace, the seat of the [[The galaxy|Galactic]] Empire.<ref name="SWCL2016" /> Along with other government structures that were constructed around it, the palace became the focal point of the Federal District as part of the "supreme triangle."<ref name="Tarkin" />

[[File:ImperialCenter-ROTJ.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The death of Emperor Palpatine was celebrated across the Federal District by the people of Coruscant.]]
After the death of Emperor Palpatine at the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the turning point of the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], the [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] and the Imperial Security Bureau seized control of Coruscant in an effort to put down the [[Coruscant civil war|revolution]] that had erupted on the planet.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> As the Empire was driven back across the galaxy by the [[New Republic]], Amedda was placed under house arrest by order of [[Gallius Rax]], the self-styled [[Counselor to the Empire]], while Imperial forces continued to lose ground and struggled to hold onto the upper levels of the Federal District. With the defeat of the [[Jakku Imperial remnant|Imperial remnants]] at the [[Battle of Jakku]], Amedda signed the [[Galactic Concordance]], formally marking the [[Imperial Instruments of Surrender|surrender]] of the Galactic Empire and replacing the [[Coruscanti government|Imperial government]] on Coruscant with a [[Coruscant's provisional government|provisional government]] that was overseen by the New Republic.<ref name="EmpiresEnd" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 67|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 67]] identifies the [[Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center]] as sitting within the Military District of Coruscant.<ref name="BMF 67" /> ''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]'' later stated that the Central Detention Center was part of the Federal District.<ref name="Cataclysm" /> Therefore, this article assumes "Military District" to be an alternative name for the Federal District.
