The '''B2 Super Battle Droids Unit Expansion''' is an expansion pack for the miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'', announced on [[August 1]], [[2019]],<ref name="Manufactured Might" /> and was released on [[February 28]], [[2020]].<ref name="Out Now" /> In [[2025]], [[Atomic Mass Games]], the new owners of ''Legion'', announced that the expansion would be [[B2 Super Battle Droids Unit Expansion (new edition)|reissued]] with hard plastic miniatures and updated cards in the second quarter of [[2025]].

==Publisher's summary==
''B2 Super Battle Droids are the hulking, brutal cousins of the wiry B1-series battle droids of the Separatist army. Heavily armored and devastatingly powerful with their integrated wrist blasters, the sight of a squad of these droids on the battlefield can chill even the most seasoned of clone troopers''.<ref name="Manufactured Might" />

''The B2 Super Battle Droids Unit Expansion provides your army with six B2 Super Battle Droid miniatures to add to your Star Wars: Legion collection. Four of these miniatures feature the standard wrist cannons seen on most B2 battle droids and three of them can be posed in a variety of positions. Meanwhile, separate B2-HA and B2-ACM miniatures menace opposing armies with heavy weapons.''
*''Deploy a unit of hulking B2 Super Battle Droids to your Star Wars: Legion battles with six new miniatures!''
*''Four unpainted, finely detailed B2 Super Battle Droids blast their opponents with their arm cannons.''
*''Add a B2-ACM Trooper or a B2-HA Trooper for even more ways to attack!''<ref name="FFG Order Page">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/products/star-wars-legion/products/b2-super-battle-droids-unit-expansion|text=B2 Super Battle Droids Unit Expansion|nolive=1}}</ref>

==Contents==
*9 Miniatures
*1 Unit Card
*5 Upgrade Cards
