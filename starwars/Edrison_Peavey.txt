'''Edrison Peavey''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[captain]] in the [[First Order Navy]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[Resistance]]. A [[Imperial officer|veteran]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Peavey went on to serve as a [[First Order officer|military officer]] in the [[First Order military|armed forces]] of the [[First Order]] approximately three [[Standard decade|decades]] after the Empire's [[Battle of Jakku|ultimate defeat]] in the [[Galactic Civil War]]. As a member of the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Finalizer]]''{{'s}} senior staff, Peavey served under the command of [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]]&mdash;the illegitimate son of his contemporary, General [[Brendol Hux]]. Although he regarded his [[commanding officer]] as an example of nepotism and incompetence, the [[Imperial Navy|Imperial veteran]] was a professional [[officer]] who kept his opinions to himself.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Sir, the Resistance cruiser is preparing to jump to lightspeed.''"<br />"''It's empty. They're just trying to pull our attention away. Pathetic. Keep your fire on those transports.|Edrison Peavey and Armitage Hux|<ref name="Expanded Edition" />}}
Belonging to the generation of [[Imperial officer|Imperial veterans]] who founded the [[First Order]], Edrison Peavey survived the [[Battle of Jakku|fall]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="TLJ:TVD" /> which, in turn, led to the [[New Republic Era|rise]] of the [[New Republic]] at the [[Galactic Concordance|conclusion]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> A contemporary of [[General]] [[Brendol Hux]],<ref name="TLJ:TVD" /> the founder of the [[First Order stormtrooper]] [[Project Resurrection|training program]],<ref name="Phasma">[[Phasma (novel)|''Phasma'']]</ref> Peavey served with Hux's<ref name="TLJ:TVD" /> illegitimate<ref name="LD">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> son, General [[Armitage Hux]] aboard the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Finalizer]]''.<ref name="TLJ:TVD" /> Despite the role of both as the leading officers on the ship's [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], however, the vessel was [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren's]] [[flagship]].<ref name="POV 220">{{InsiderCite|220|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> After the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], Peavey was at the general's side when the [[First Order Navy]] deployed a [[fleet]] of [[Star Destroyer]]s to the [[planet]] [[D'Qar]], where Peavey noted that their [[First Order military|forces]] had caught the [[Resistance]] in the middle of an [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation]].<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Peavey's comment irritated Hux, who regarded the older [[officer]] as a relic of the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

[[File:Hux_on_the_bridge.png|thumb|left|300px|Captain Edrison Peavey was an Imperial veteran who privately regarded young First Order officers, such as General Armitage Hux, with disdain.]]
Under normal circumstances, Peavey commanded the ''Finalizer'' in Hux's absence due to his [[Military rank|status]] as the [[command ship]]'s [[captain]]. This [[time]], however, he was transferred along with his general to the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]''&mdash;[[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]]'s [[flagship]]&mdash;following the Resistance's escape from D'Qar.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> Utilizing [[Hyperspace tracker|cutting edge technology]] to track the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]] through [[hyperspace]], the First Order [[Battle of Oetchi|pursued their prey]] to the [[Crait system]] but was unable to eliminate them outright,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> much to Hux's aggravation.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> Peavey informed<ref name="Episode VIII" /> his younger [[commanding officer]]<ref name="TLJ:TVD" /> that the ''Supremacy'' was too slow due to her size; while the Resistance could not escape the [[Star Dreadnought]], they could at least keep their smaller [[starship]]s out of the [[turbolaser]]s' range. The First Order maintained their pursuit of the Resistance, forcing their vessels to [[Fire|burn]] through their fuel reserves. Meanwhile, Hux ordered Peavey to have the ''Supremacy''{{'s}} [[gunner]]s continue the barrage, wanting the Resistance to know they were still being followed.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:Finalizer_Over_Jakku.png|thumb|right|300px|Peavey served as captain of the ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer ''Finalizer'', General Hux's command ship.]]
The Resistance's support ships were destroyed one by one, leaving the [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] vulnerable and dangerously low on fuel. Upon learning that the ''Raddus'' was merely a distraction, Hux ordered Peavey to ignore the Resistance flagship and concentrate the ''Supremacy''{{'s}} turbolasers on the group of [[transport]]s ferrying the ''Raddus''{{'s}} crew to the planet [[Crait]]. Peavey noted the ''Raddus'' began changing course but Hux ordered him to ignore the [[cruiser]], believing it was only attempting to draw the First Order's attention away from the transports. By the time they realized that the ''Raddus'' was preparing to enter hyperspace while facing in the ''Supremacy''{{'s}} direction, it was too late. Despite their efforts to save the Supreme Leader's flagship, the ''Raddus'' crashed through the ''Supremacy'' at [[lightspeed]], tearing the [[dreadnought]] in half and destroying several [[Star Destroyer]]s in the process. However, both Peavey and Hux survived the attack and deployed the [[First Order Army]] to Crait where the Resistance took refuge in an [[Crait outpost|outpost]] that once belonged to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Peavey and Hux oversaw the [[Battle of Crait]] from the [[Kylo Ren's command shuttle|command shuttle]] of [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], the [[Dark side of the Force|dark]] [[Knights of Ren|warrior]] who succeeded Snoke as the new Supreme Leader of the First Order. Although their forces decimated the Resistance and captured the base, the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] for [[the galaxy]] would continue as a small group of survivors escaped from the battle aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> With [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious]]' return to the galaxy, Peavey, like other many other former Imperial officers among the First Order ranks, quickly rushed to join the [[Sith Lord]].<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Your gunnery crews have done excellent work, Captain. I commend them.|Edrison Peavey, to Yago|<ref name="Expanded Edition" />}}
[[File:PeaveyDatabank.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Although he despised General Hux, Captain Peavey felt duty-bound to obey his commanding officer.]]
As a veteran of the [[Galaxy|Galactic]] Empire, Edrison Peavey upheld the principles he gained through his service in the [[Imperial Navy]] and therefore regarded the chain of command with the utmost respect.<ref name="Databank" /> Retaining his sense of professionalism as an [[First Order officer|officer of the First Order]],<ref name="TLJ:TVD" /> Peavey typically maintained a rigid, unimpeachable posture while on duty.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> He also kept his personal opinions of General Armitage Hux a secret due to the latter's status as his commanding officer. Having watched Hux's rise to power through unscrupulous methods and nepotism,<ref name="TLJ:TVD" /> the Imperial veteran harbored contempt for the young general, whom he saw as an arrogant schemer with a poor grasp of tactics.<ref name="Databank" /> Despite his dislike of Hux, Peavey was careful to keep his face expressionless while [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] made a fool of the general.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

Although Peavey believed Hux was capable of defeating the Resistance, he felt that it was only a matter of time until the general proved himself unfit to wield the authority that came with his rank. In Peavey's view, Hux was an incompetent officer and an intemperate leader&mdash;a revolutionary, filled with fire and fervor, yet one whose glory was fleeting. While he also had a low opinion of Hux's late father, Brendol, whom he regarded as a madman, Peavey believed that the elder Hux had a better grasp of military etiquette than his son.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

[[File:ShayReportsToPeavey-TLJ.png|thumb|300px|right|Peavey listens to Petty Officer Shay's report that the ''Raddus'' was powering its hyperdrive.]]
Whereas Peavey held little respect for Hux, he was impressed by Captain [[Yago (First Order)|Yago]], whose gunnery crews displayed excellent marksmanship during their assault on the Resistance transports. Not wanting resentment to fester in the [[Navy]]'s upper echelon, he believed that the ''Supremacy''{{'s}} gunners deserved recognition for their skills and contribution to the destruction of the Resistance. He also gave little consideration to the ''Raddus'' after learning that its crew had abandoned ship. Even as [[Petty Officer]] [[Rumitar Shay]] reported the cruiser was seemingly attempting to flee the battle, Peavey believed that the [[Amilyn Holdo|ship's captain]] was merely trying to lure the First Order away from the transports. He reacted with mild surprise when Hux ordered him to ignore the Resistance flagship, having secretly hoped that the general would not make a rash decision. However, Peavey was stricken with a horrified realization that the ''Raddus'' was about to perform an hyperspace [[ramming]] [[Holdo maneuver|attack]] on the ''Supremacy''.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

Peavey was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]];<ref name="Databank" /> by the time of his service in the First Order, he had [[Color|graying]] blond [[hair]], blue [[eye]]s, and light skin.<ref name="Episode VIII" />
