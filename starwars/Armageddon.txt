The '''''Armageddon''''' was a [[StoneCrusher-class WarMaster|''StoneCrusher''-class WarMaster]] that served in the [[Jixtus's fleet|fleet]] of the [[Grysk]] "[[Jixtus]]." The ''Armageddon'' was destroyed during a [[Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand|battle over Sunrise]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Description==
The ''Armageddon'' was a [[StoneCrusher-class WarMaster|''StoneCrusher''-class WarMasters]] and was equipped with an [[engine]] and a [[hyperdrive]]. It was armed with [[spectrum laser]]s and [[dibber]] [[missile]]s, and shielded with [[electrostatic barrier]]s.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Role==
The ''Armageddon'' was part of "[[Jixtus]]"'s [[Jixtus's fleet|fleet]] to conquer and destroy the Chiss Ascendancy on behalf of the Grysk Hegemony. It was one of three ''StoneCrusher''-class WarMasters in the fleet.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==History==
The ''Armageddon'' took part in the [[Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand|final battle]] of [[Jixtus's plan]] over the [[planet]] [[Sunrise]]. The warship was attacked by [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]''{{'s}} laser fire and a small [[gunboat]] known as [[Paccian Four]] crewed by the [[Paccosh]] [[species]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

The ''Armageddon'' intended to overwhelm the gunboat with a salvo of dibber missiles to be able to focus on the larger Chiss cruiser; however, [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], who was in command of the entire Chiss battle line, had anticipated this, and so the ''Springhawk'' was able to save the gunboat with a [[plasma sphere]] they had fired alongside their earlier spectrum laser attack. The ''Armageddon''{{'s}} lead dibber missiles slammed directly into the plasma sphere, causing the whole cluster of missiles to explode too far away from Paccian Four to damage it, and close enough to the ''Armageddon'' to damage its [[targeting sensor]]s. The paccian gunboat then flew straight through the debris cloud of the dibbers and devastated the whole flank of the ''Armageddon'' with laser fire, effectively blinding the [[Grysk]] ship on that side.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

The ''Armageddon'' continued its fight with the ''Springhawk'', attacking it with laser fire and dibbers until the [[Chiss]] ship turned and tried to flee the confrontation. Since it was on its route to attack two already damaged Grysk [[Prism-class BattleChief|''Prism''-class BattleChiefs]] warships, the ''Armageddon'' pursued it in order to destroy it, falling right into a trap. The [[Light cruiser|Clarr light cruiser]] ''[[Orisson]]'' and the Chiss heavy cruiser ''[[Grayshrike]]'' were ready to ambush the ''Armageddon'', facing each other bow-to-bow in a five kilometer distance. The ''Springhawk'' was about to pass right between them, as would the ''Armageddon'' as it followed the Chiss ship.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

Both the ''Orisson'' and the ''Grayshrike'' fired two [[breacher]] missiles at each other. As the commander of the ''Armageddon'' realized that the breachers would harmlessly pass in front of the ship, the ''Armageddon'' continued its pursuit of the ''Springhawk'' without slowing down. However, the breachers slammed into each other directly in front of the ''Armageddon'', which slammed into a cloud of [[acid]] produced by the breachers' destruction. Damaged and unable to fight back, the ''Armageddon'' was then destroyed by both the ''Springhawk'' and the ''Orisson''.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
