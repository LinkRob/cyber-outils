The '''''Ataraxia''''',<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> known for some time as the '''''Lourna Dee''''',<ref name="THR-23 1" /> was a [[Star Cruiser|starcruiser]]<ref name="THR-21 9" /> in the service of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> First commissioned by [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date note" /> the ''Ataraxia'' served under the command of [[Jedi Master]] [[Jora Malli]] at the [[Battle of Kur]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> During the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], the [[Starship|ship]] was taken by [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]] after she wounded [[OrbaLin]],<ref name="THR-21 15" /> and she renamed it the ''Lourna Dee''.<ref name="THR-23 1" /> The ''Ataraxia'' was eventually reclaimed by Jedi Master [[Keeve Trennis]].<ref name="THR-23 3">[[The High Republic (2023) 3|''The High Republic'' (2023) 3]]</ref>

In [[90 BBY]],<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Dooku]]'s visit to [[Serenno]] during the [[Celebration on Serenno]] as depicted in ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'' to [[90 BBY]].</ref> the ''Ataraxia'' was used to transport a number of [[Jedi]] to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Serenno]], in order for [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi initiates]] to attend a [[Celebration on Serenno|celebration]] of the planets and cultures of the [[The galaxy|galactic]] region. When one initiate, [[Dooku]], was insulted by fellow initiate [[Arath Tarrex]], he suggested fighting back, but his friend [[Sifo-Dyas]] told him that they would be punished as a result, and forced to remain on the ''Ataraxia'' for the duration of the event.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Ataraxia'' was first mentioned in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="DJL Release">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-cavan-scott/9780593102817|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> The spelling of the starship's name was confirmed by the [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script release]] of the audiobook<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> on [[October 1]] of that year.<ref name="DJL Release" />

The vessel subsequently featured in the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' publishing initiative. It was first pictured, albeit not identified as the ''Ataraxia'', in a preview released on [[StarWars.com]] on [[December 1]], [[2020]] for the [[The High Republic (2021) 2|second issue]] of the [[Marvel Comics]] series of the [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2021)|same name]],<ref name="THR2 Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-the-high-republic-2-exclusive-preview|text=Jedi Master Sskeer Returns in Marvel's ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' #2 – Exclusive Preview}}</ref> written by Scott and illustrated by [[Ario Anindito]].<ref name="THR-21 2" /> The ''Ataraxia'' first appeared in the novel ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'', written by [[Charles Soule]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> and published on [[January 5]], [[2021]],<ref name="LOTJ Release">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/622991/star-wars-light-of-the-jedi-the-high-republic-by-charles-soule/9780593157718|text=Star Wars: Light of the Jedi (The High Republic)}}</ref> while the release of ''The High Republic'' 2 on [[February 3]], 2021<ref name="THR 2 Release">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/90347/star_wars_the_high_republic_2021_2|text=Star Wars: The High Republic (2021) #2}}</ref> connected the name to the ship depicted in the preview.<ref name="THR-21 2" />
