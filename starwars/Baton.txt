A '''baton''', also called a '''shock baton''', '''stun baton''', '''security baton''', or '''tactical baton''' was a melee weapon utilized primarily by [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement officers]] as a personal defense and crowd control tool.

==Description==
Batons were melee weapons, but could be equipped with a [[Stun]] to immobilize organic targets by producing an electrical charge that shimmered on the shaft.<ref name="The Dark">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Dark]]''</ref> 

==History==
Batons were carried by [[police officer]]s and [[GU-series Guardian police droid]]s of the [[Coruscant Security Force]]<ref name="Senate Murders" /> and of the [[Mandalorian Guard]].<ref name="TA">{{TCW|The Academy}}</ref> [[Riot clone trooper]] of the [[Coruscant Guard]] were also armed with [[TZ-97 "Zapper" Shock Baton]]s for duty on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="DoMEpGuide" /> Both [[stormtrooper]]s and [[Imperial cadet|Imperial "Riot" Cadets]] wielded them during [[Governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]]'s [[Iron Blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]].<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> The [[Captain]] of the [[Mon Calamari Guard]] [[Gial Ackbar]] used a [[Battle baton|baton]] that also fires blaster bolts during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="WaterWar">{{TCW|Water War}}</ref>

[[Electrobaton]]s and [[electro riot baton]]s were used by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Purge Trooper]]s and [[scout trooper]]s and [[KX-series security droid]]s.<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> The [[Imperial factory facility prison guard|prison guards]] at the [[Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex]] utilized [[zap rod]]s to deal with prisoners.<ref name="Narkina 5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref> During the [[Narkina 5 prison escape]], escaping [[prisoner]]s used zap rods to attack prison guards.<ref name="One Way Out">{{Andor|One Way Out}}</ref>

[[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] owned a [[Thrawn's combat baton|combat baton]].<ref name="Thrawn Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> [[Jyn Erso]] was trained by Saw Gerrera to use [[truncheon]] at his base on [[Wrea]]. She later purchased a collapsible pair of truncheons at [[Five Points]]<ref name="Rebel Rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref> which she used on [[Jedha]] against [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> 

The [[bounty hunter]] [[Mercurial Swift]] had a pair of batons where both ends were concussive while the other end was electric, allowing him to set the baton to kill or [[stun]]. One of Swift's batons was modified with a [[Hypodermic injector|hypoinjector]] for poison.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

The [[First Order]]'s [[First Order riot control stormtrooper|riot control stormtroopers]] used the [[Z6 riot control baton]], which had collapsible vanes and could withstand a [[lightsaber]]'s blows.<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> The [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]] owned a specialized tactical [[Vi Moradi's baton|baton]] that had saved her rump on more than one occasion.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire3">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref>
