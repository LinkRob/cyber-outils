'''Coolsap shrubs''' were a [[species]] of [[Bush|shrub]] [[Homeworld|native]] to<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> [[Gamorr]] that were [[Diet|edible]] by [[Gamorrean]]s.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> By [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> the [[Hutt]] [[Churo]] grew coolsap shrubs and [[snoruuk mushroom]]s within a tank in his [[Churo's greenhouse laboratory|greenhouse laboratory]], which was located in his [[Devirsivik Clan|family's]] [[Devirsivik Clan's palace|palace]] on<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> the Outer Rim<ref name="The Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> planet [[Nal Hutta]], to see if the species could grow in Nal Hutta's [[swamp]]s and thrive there.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> an individual ate the [[Plant|shrubs]] and [[mushroom]]s he had been growing. After discovering the fate of his [[science]] experiments, Churo suspected a [[Gamorrean]] [[Unidentified Gamorrean palace guard|palace guard]] of having eaten them.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coolsap shrubs were mentioned in the [[2024]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=SWCE 2023: 9 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[coolsap tree]]s appeared in ''[[The New Jedi Order: Destiny's Way]]'', a [[2002]] novel written by [[Walter Jon Williams]] as the fourteenth installment of the ''[[Star Wars: The New Jedi Order]]'' series.<ref name="Destiny's Way">''[[The New Jedi Order: Destiny's Way]]''</ref>
