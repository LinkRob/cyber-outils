'''Adjutant Captain''' was a rank held by [[Aila Gris]] of the [[Gagic Alliance]]<ref name="Hidden Danger" /> by [[231 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Starlight: Hidden Danger]]" to [[231 BBY]].</ref> At this [[time]], Gris served in the [[Soikan conflict]], participating in the [[assault on Qunatos]] where she commanded a [[Velko Jahen's platoon|platoon]] before being [[Death|killed]].<ref name="Hidden Danger" />

==Behind the scenes==
Adjutant Captain debuted in "[[Starlight: Hidden Danger]]," a short story written by [[Justina Ireland]]. The first part was released on [[June 22]], [[2021]]<ref name="TitanComics">{{TitanComics|url=m/76-star-wars-insider-203|text=Star Wars Insider #203}}</ref> within [[Star Wars Insider 203|''Star Wars Insider'' 203]],<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-high-republic-next-wave|text=First Details of the Next Wave of ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Stories Revealed - Exclusive}}</ref> while the second part released on [[August 10]] within [[Star Wars Insider 204|''Star Wars Insider'' 204]].<ref name="Launch Event">{{SWYouTube|uILPgvzj708|Star Wars: The High Republic &#124; Live Stream Launch Event}}</ref>
