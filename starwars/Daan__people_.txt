The '''Daan''' were a group that inhabited the [[Cadavine sector]]'s [[Melida/Daan]] alongside the [[Melida]]. After the Melida took the [[Unidentified Daan leader|leader]] of the Daan captive, the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[senator]] [[Lexxir Greylark]] and his eighteen [[Standard year|year]] old son, [[Axel Greylark]], embarked on a [[Diplomatic mission to Melida/Daan|diplomatic mission]] to negotiate with the Melida for the leader's release. During the extraction of the leader however, there was an explosion, as the mission was a trap to show all of the Daan's allies what would happen to those that helped them. With the trap triggered and the Melida on their way, the [[senator]] was buried under rubble in an explosion, while a [[Unidentified Jedi Knight (Axel Greylark)|Jedi Knight]] pulled an injured Greylark away, forcing him to leave his [[Parent|father]] to [[Death|die]].<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Greylark told the [[Jedi]] [[Gella Nattai]] about the incident.<ref name="Convergence" />
