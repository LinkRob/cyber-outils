'''Dagio Belcoze''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Iktotchi]] who worked as a [[computer]] [[engineer]] for the [[Galactic Republic]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], Belcoze worked in [[Doctor]] [[Gubacher]]'s [[artificial intelligence]] team that designed [[Overseer droid (Death Star)|enhanced overseer droids]] to supervise the [[Project Stardust|battle station]] labor force. At a [[Republic Futures Program]] reunion held in an apartment in [[500 Republica]], he ranted that the Republic should bomb the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[capital]] [[Raxus Secundus]]. After demanding details on [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso's]] imprisonment on [[Vallt]], he confronted the [[Crystallography|crystallographer]] about his possible Separatist sympathies and started a brawl, in which he lost due to slower reflexes. After the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Belcoze was part of the research team stationed in the [[Project Celestial Power facility]] on [[Malpaz]], where he witnessed the [[Malpaz incident|destruction]] of the [[Malpaz capital city|city]] and pled with [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] that he tried understanding Erso's research as best he could.<ref name="Catalyst" />
