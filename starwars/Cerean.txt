The '''Cereans''' were a [[species]] of [[Sentience|sentient]] [[mammal]]s hailing from the [[planet]] [[Cerea]]. They were distinguished by their enlarged conical cranium that contained a binary [[brain]]. The [[Jedi Master]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]], who sat on the [[Jedi High Council]] during the waning [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], was a Cerean [[Gender|male]].

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Hey, Soo-Tath! You're looking good! Love the hat! Is it new?|Chelli Lona Aphra, to the Cerean Soo-Tath|<ref name="Aphra 1">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 1|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 1]]</ref>}}
[[File:OmerRun-SWE.png|thumb|left|180px|O-Mer, a Cerean youth.]]
[[Humanoid]] [[mammal]]s distinguished by their massive coned craniums, the Cereans kept complex,<ref name="Databank" /> binary [[brain]]s within their towering skulls<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> which allowed the garden [[species]] to see both sides of any issue.<ref name="VE" /> These organs relied on [[blood]] pumped from their two [[heart]]s.<ref name="Absolutely Everything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> Cereans could grow hair and some sported eyebrows, [[Color|white]] beards,<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> dark stubble,<ref name="Aphra 1" /> and even topknots and braids.<ref name="Padawan Lost" /> The Cerean [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] served as a [[Jedi]] a century before<ref name="Day">{{Acolyte|Day (episode)}}</ref> serving on the [[Jedi High Council]] during the [[Invasion of Naboo]].<ref name="USW" />

==Cereans in the galaxy==
{{Quote|Ki-Adi-Mundi? Was that the one with the pointy head?|A [[First Order]] [[Unidentified First Order stormtrooper sergeant|stormtrooper sergeant]]|<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2">[[Galaxy's Edge 2|''Galaxy's Edge'' 2]]</ref>}}
[[File:KiAdiMundi-SmugglersOutpost2025.png|thumb|230px|right|Ki-Adi-Mundi served the Jedi Order for over a century.]]
The Cerean [[species]] inhabited the beautiful [[planet]] of [[Cerea]], which remained largely unspoiled by technology.<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> In [[132 BBY]], Ki-Adi-Mundi was part of a small council investigating the actions of the [[assassin]] [[Mae-ho Aniseya]].<ref name="Day" /> As a member of the Jedi High Council a century later, Ki-Adi-Mundi's white beard and wise features attested to [[Standard year|years]] spent in service to the [[Jedi Order]]. He fought for the [[Galactic Republic]] throughout the [[Clone Wars]] and was [[Death|killed]] by the [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Galactic Marines|21st Nova Corps]] when [[Darth Sidious]] issued [[Order 66]].<ref name="USW" />

[[O-Mer]] was a Cerean who [[Jedi training|trained]] as a [[Padawan]] during the war and faced [[Trandoshan]]s who hunted him and fellow apprentices for sport on [[Wasskah]].<ref name="Padawan Lost" />

[[Soo-Tath]] was a Cerean who operated as a debt collector during the [[Galactic Civil War]] and threatened [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] after she failed to pay the organization he represented. However, the [[protocol droid]] [[0-0-0]] attacked Soo-Tath with a [[neurotoxin]] that resulted in the Cerean's death.<ref name="Aphra 1" />

[[Kidi Aleri]] was a Cerean female communications specialist who served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] in the Galactic Civil War. She was one of several individuals that were part of a team headed by [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] for a mission known as [[Operation Yellow Moon]].<ref name="Moving Target">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref>
