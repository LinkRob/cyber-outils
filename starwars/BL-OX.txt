'''BL-OX''' was a [[droid]] who lived on the junk [[planet]] of [[Zoh]]. On Zoh, BL-OX and other droids was led by [[Jek-14]]. Shorty after the [[Freemakers]] visited Zoh seeking Jek-14's help, a ungrateful droid named [[N-3RO]] led a droid uprising against Jek-14 and the Freemakers. N-3RO succeeded to convince his fellow droids, including BL-OX that humans can not be trusted. They failed and N-3RO was still on Zoh and had been fitted for a [[restraining bolt]].<ref name="Droidography" />
