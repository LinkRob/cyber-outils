'''Corbin''' was a [[miner]] at an [[Unidentified underground mining colony|underground mining colony]] on an [[Unidentified airless moon (Outer Rim)|airless moon]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. [[Gender|He]] was jealous of several of his coworkers, particularly [[Fiona]] and [[Ryland]]. In [[2 BBY]], after the [[Galactic Empire]] established its authority on the colony, Corbin framed Fiona and Ryland for being [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Spy|spies]], resulting in Fiona's [[death]].<ref name="Laina" />
