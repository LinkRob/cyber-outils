An '''alluvial damper''' regulated the thrust output from a [[hyperdrive]] generator, blocking the emission of ion particles by moving a servo-controlled plate.<ref name="OWM">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> The ''[[Carrion Spike]]'' was equipped with alluvial dampers.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (The Star Wars)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>

Soon after the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] [[Evaan Verlaine]] and the [[Astromech droid|astromech]] [[droid]] [[R2-D2]] outsmarted their pursuers, [[Wedge Antilles]] and [[Luke Skywalker]], making them believe that their alluvial damper had been damaged, when in fact, it had been useless decoy. Thanks to the maneuver, Verlaine and the droid managed to transport [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] out of [[Yavin 4]] despite [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]]'s restriction, to accomplish their self-imposed [[Mission to rescue the survivors of Alderaan|mission]] to rescue the survivors of [[Alderaan]] from the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="PL1" />

In the immediate aftermath of the [[Battle of Hoth]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''{{'s}} faulty hyperdrive prompted its owner [[Han Solo]] to check the system, including its [[horizontal booster]]s and the alluvial damper, while Imperial forces relentlessly attacked them.<ref name="Episode V" />
