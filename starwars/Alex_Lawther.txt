'''Alex Lawther''' is an actor who plays [[Karis Nemik]] in the television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.<ref name="Aldhani" /> He is best known for playing [[W:c:teotfw:James|James]], the leading role in the comedy-drama series ''[[Wikipedia:The End of the F***ing World|The End of the F***ing World]]'', and [[W:c:black-mirror:Kenny|Kenny]] in the ''[[Wikipedia:Black Mirror|Black Mirror]]'' episode "[[Wikipedia:Shut Up and Dance (Black Mirror)|Shut Up and Dance]]."
