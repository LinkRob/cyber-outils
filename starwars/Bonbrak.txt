'''Bonbraks''', also known as the '''barat-karabak''', were a diminutive [[hair]]y [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] native to the [[planet]] [[Bartokan V]]. They were classified as a protected species by the [[Galactic Republic]]. Several Bonbraks worked in the garage of the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] during the [[High Republic Era]].

{{Species-stub}}

==Biology and appearance==
Bonbraks were a diminutive [[hair]]y [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="STET" /> originating from the [[planet]] [[Bartokan V]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> Possessing large ears and beady [[Color|brown]] eyes,<ref name="Livestream" /> Bonbraks were typically small enough to fit on a [[human]]'s shoulder,<ref name="Race to Crashpoint Tower">''[[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower]]''</ref> being similar in size to an [[Anzellan]]. Bonbraks loved to drink [[caf]]. The Bonbrak's own [[language]], [[Bonbreez]], was similar enough to [[Anzellan (language)|Anzellan]] that Bonbraks could translate for Anzellans.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

According to the [[musician]] [[Crukibolt Nart Adeen|Crufeela]], Bonbraks tasted best when eaten alive.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

==Bonbraks in the galaxy==
On their [[homeworld]], Bartokan V, the Bonbrak were worshipped as gods by the [[Svor Clan]], being referred to as the "barat-karabak," meaning "fierce teeth." Because of this, the Clan demanded a special designation for the species, leading to the [[Galactic Republic]] classifying the Bonbrak as a protected species.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

Bonbrak had gone on to inhabit several worlds,<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> including the planets [[Valo]]<ref name="Race to Crashpoint Tower" /> and [[Corellia]], which other beings referred to as "infestations."<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> Despite being a sentient species,<ref name="STET" /> Bonbraks were valuable in the [[black market]], being prized for their taste. Beings would attempt to eat Bonbraks by roasting or deep-frying them.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

Several Bonbraks worked in the garage of the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] on Valo during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Race to Crashpoint Tower" /> They later accompanied Jomaram when he traveled to the [[Starlight Beacon]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> A cluster of Bonbraks were present on [[Freerago's Satellite Diner and Motel]] when the [[bounty hunter]]s [[Zuckuss]] and [[4-LOM]] were interviewed by [[Parazeen Parapa]] for the ''[[Galactic Digest]]''.<ref name="STET" />
