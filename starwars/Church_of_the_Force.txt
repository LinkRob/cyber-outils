The '''Church of the Force''' was a [[Religion|religious]] institution composed of loosely-affiliated worshippers of [[the Force]] who chose to follow the [[Jedi Code|teachings]] of the [[Jedi Order]], yet lacked [[Force-sensitive|Force-sensitivity]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the Church of the Force was represented on the [[Convocation of the Force]].

==Description==
{{Quote|The temple was home to the Church of the Force&mdash;a group whose followers had no Force-sensitivity yet still chose to follow the teachings of the Jedi Order.|Luke Skywalker|<ref name="SotJ" />}}
<div style="float:right">[[File:DuelonCoruscant.jpg|thumb|250px|The Jedi Order's ways were preserved by the Church of the Force, a religious group of non-Force-sensitives.]]</div>
The Church of [[the Force]] was a [[Religion|faith]] whose followers believed in the ideals of the [[Jedi Order]]. In simplest terms, the Church was essentially a non-[[Force-sensitive]] equivalent of the Jedi Order.<ref name="SotJ" /> It existed underground during the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], when such spiritual beliefs were strictly forbidden. Despite the threat of Imperial [[Imperial Era|rule]], and the [[Order 66|destruction]] of the [[Jedi]] Order at the end of the [[Clone Wars]], the Church of the Force believed that the [[Light side of the Force|light]] of the Jedi would one [[Standard day|day]] return to [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TFA VD" />

They eschewed modern technology and instead created their own in order to bring themselves more in touch with nature. A common practice among them was to perform pilgrimages to return items, such as [[kyber crystal]]s, to where they belonged throughout the [[galaxy]]. One such pilgrimage was performed by [[Addar]] and his fellow members to [[Christophsis]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

The [[Temple of the Kyber]], a temple located in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] of [[Jedha]], served as the home of the Church of the Force.<ref name="SotJ" />

==History==
===High Republic Era===
In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="PAU Date">{{PeaceandUnityDate}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], the Church of the Force was [[Representative|represented]] on the [[Convocation of the Force]] on [[Jedha]] by [[Kilan T'Dara]].<ref name="The High Republic 4">[[The High Republic (2022) 4|''The High Republic'' (2022) 4]]</ref> At the time, the Church operated out of a sector of the [[Temple of the Kyber]] that was assigned to them. [[Resamond Saous]] was [[prefect]] of the Church's operation there and [[adjunct]] [[Keth Cerepath]] lived in the temple.<ref name="Battle of Jedha" />

In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> the [[San Tekka clan]] was active in the Church of the Force on [[Naboo]].<ref name="Out of the Shadows" />

===Imperial Era===
{{Quote|The Church of the Force started as an underground movement during a time when the Empire strictly forbade the group's form of worship, but their flock only grew stronger, despite continued oppression over the years.|Luke Skywalker|<ref name="SotJ" />}}
The Church of the Force existed as an underground movement during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], when the [[Jedi Knight]]s were hunted to [[Great Jedi Purge|near-extinction]] and worship of the supernatural was prohibited by Imperial law. One member of the faith was [[Lor San Tekka]], an explorer who did not believe the Imperial description of the so-called Jedi betrayal of the [[Galactic Republic]]. He spent [[Standard year|years]] searching and preserving the [[Timeline of galactic history|history]] of the Jedi that the Empire had tried to suppress,<ref name="TFA VD" /> and eventually helped [[Luke Skywalker]], the last Jedi, in his own search for Jedi lore.<ref name="San Tekka DB">{{Databank|lor-san-tekka|Lor San Tekka}}</ref>

===New Republic Era===
[[File:Kylo_and_Lor_San_Tekka.png|thumb|left|250px|Lor San Tekka, a member of the Church of the Force, was executed on Jakku by the First Order warlord Kylo Ren.]]
During the [[New Republic Era]], the Church was much more free. After Skywalker vanished following the [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destruction]] of his new generation of Jedi, San Tekka held a fragment of a [[Map to Skywalker|map]] that showed where Skywalker had [[Ahch-To|gone]] into exile. Believing that the Jedi had to return in order for there to be balance in the Force, San Tekka gave the map fragment to [[Resistance]] [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] before San Tekka was [[Attack on Tuanul|killed]] by [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]&mdash;Skywalker's nephew, and the man who was believed to have [[Death|killed]] the new Jedi. However, following San Tekka's death, Skywalker was located by a young Force-sensitive [[scavenger]] named [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

==Behind the scenes==
Through the appearance of Lor San Tekka and the Tuanul villagers, the Church of the Force first appeared in the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. It was identified as the Church of the Force in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which provided some history for San Tekka.<ref name="TFA VD" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee and author of the Visual Dictionary [[Pablo Hidalgo]] revealed in an interview that the Church was originally conceived by [[George Lucas]] for ''[[Star Wars: Underworld]]'', a canned live-action ''Star Wars'' TV series. The Church's story was put on hold before appearing in ''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'', directed by [[J.J. Abrams]].<ref name="SWBW 53">{{WebCite|url=http://www.starwarsreport.com/2016/02/19/pablo-hidalgo-interview-swbw-53|text=Pablo Hidalgo Interview &#8211; SWBW #53 &#8211; The Star Wars Report|author=Aaron Goins|date=19-02-2016|archivedate=20201108122550}}</ref>
