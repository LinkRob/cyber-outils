'''Cassel''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[officer]] serving in [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy]], holding the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[commander]].

==Biography==
[[Commander]] Cassel served as [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s [[executive officer]] at [[Sentinel Base]] during the [[Project Stardust|construction]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. [[Gender|He]] was present during the [[Attack on Sentinel Base|attack]] against the installation by a cobbled-together [[Berch Teller's warship|warship]] commanded by [[Berch Teller]] and his [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]]. When Tarkin was summoned to [[Coruscant]] to meet with [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] shortly thereafter, he left Cassel in command during his absence. Cassel's tenure as temporary [[commanding officer]] of Sentinel while Tarkin was gone ended up being extended following Palpatine's command that Tarkin accompany [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] from Coruscant to [[Murkhana]] to [[Mission to Murkhana|investigate]] a cache of [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] communications devices left over from the [[Clone Wars]], and then for several [[Standard week|weeks]] longer following the theft of the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'' by Teller and his crew, as Tarkin's return to Sentinel continued to be delayed by his and Vader's efforts to recover the [[Starship|ship]] and capture Teller's crew and their Imperial co-conspirator [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]].<ref name="Tarkin" />
