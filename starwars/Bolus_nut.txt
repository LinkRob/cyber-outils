'''Bolus nuts''' were a type of [[nut]]<ref name="Reflection" /> that were sticky, crunchy, and sweet. During the [[Reflection Day]] festival in [[Jedha City]] on<ref name="LDCookbook" /> the [[moon]]<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Jedha]], they were sold as portable snacks on [[wood]]en sticks from food stalls<ref name="Reflection" /> that were set up throughout the [[city]] on [[Reflection Eve]].<ref name="LDCookbook" /> Bolus nuts were available during the Reflection Day festivities in the [[Standard year|year]] that the traveler [[Zallo Blaak]] visited Jedha City, at some point prior<ref name="Reflection" /> to the city's [[Destruction of Jedha City|destruction]]<ref name="RO">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

After publishing his first [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]]<ref name="LDCookbook" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for bolus nuts in his second [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]''. Tuggs described the nuts as the most delightful of the [[Foodstuff|foods]] available on Jedha during Reflection Day but stated that he preferred to serve them in a bowl than on sticks.<ref name="LDCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bolus nuts first appeared in "[[Reflection Day (short story)|Reflection Day]]," a short story published<ref name="Reflection" /> on [[September 7]], [[2021]]<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/life-day-treasury-exclusive-reveal|text=Celebrate the ''Star Wars'' Galaxy's Greatest Holiday with ''Life Day Treasury'' - Exclusive Reveal}}</ref> in the ''[[Life Day Treasury]]'', a collection of short stories written by [[George Mann]] and [[Cavan Scott]].<ref name="Reflection" /> The recipe book ''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]'', authored by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]<ref name="LDCookbook" /> and published on [[November 9]] of that same year,<ref name="Feast">{{SW|url=news/the-life-day-cookbook-exclusive-preview|text=Prepare a Feast Fit for a Wookiee With ''The Life Day Cookbook'' - Exclusive Preview}}</ref> provided an [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the nuts. The ingredients are an [[Wikipedia:Egg white|egg white]], [[Wikipedia:Almond|almonds]], [[Wikipedia:Vanilla extract|vanilla extract]], [[Wikipedia:Brown sugar|packed light brown sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Cinnamon|ground cinnamon]], [[Wikipedia:Nutmeg|ground nutmeg]], and [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]].<ref name="LDCookbook" />
