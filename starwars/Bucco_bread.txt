'''Bucco bread''' was a type of [[Color|golden]] [[bread]] [[Diet|eaten]] by [[Colonist|members]] of a [[Sedri Minor colony|colony]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Sedri Minor]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. In [[232 BBY]], the [[human]] [[Youngling|child]] [[Bartol]] and a [[Unidentified Gran child|Gran child]] took a basket of freshly baked bucco bread to a group of [[Jedi]] that had [[Mission to Sedri Minor|visited]] the colony and presented it to the human [[Jedi Knight]] [[Keeve Trennis]].

Trennis was appreciative of the two children's idea but informed them that they had not needed to as the Jedi had [[Foodstuff|food]] of their own. The Jedi Knight did take the basket from Bartol and held it as she spoke with other members of the colony. She was no longer holding the basket when she went to search for the missing Jedi Knight [[Ceret]].

==Description==
Bucco bread was a type of [[Color|golden]] [[bread]] in the form of small round loaves.<ref name="The High Republic 3" />

==History==
{{Quote|You ''do'' eat, don't you?''"<br />"''Heh. Yeah, we eat. That's really kind of you, but you don't have to give us your bread. We have food of our own.|The Gran child and Keeve Trennis|<ref name="The High Republic 3" />}}
By [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic (2021) 3|''The High Republic'' (2021) 3]] to [[232 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], bucco bread was [[Diet|consumed]] by [[Colonist|residents]] of a [[Sedri Minor colony|colony]] on<ref name="The High Republic 3" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="The High Republic 3 preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-the-high-republic-3-exclusive-preview|text=Something Lurks Beneath in Marvel's ''The High Republic'' #3 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref> [[planet]] [[Sedri Minor]].<ref name="The High Republic 3" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> two [[Youngling|children]] that were part of the colony&mdash;the [[human]] [[Gender|boy]] [[Bartol]] and a [[Unidentified Gran child|Gran]]&mdash;took a basket of bucco bread that had been freshly baked that [[Local day|day]] to a group of [[Jedi]] that were [[Mission to Sedri Minor|visiting]] the colony as part of an investigation after discovering a derelict [[Hutt Clan|Hutt]] [[starship|ship]] that had come from the colony. After arriving at the Jedi<ref name="The High Republic 3" /> [[flagship]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> ''[[Ataraxia]]'', Bartol and the [[Gran]] encountered the human [[Jedi Knight]] [[Keeve Trennis]], who was standing outside the ''Ataraxia''.<ref name="The High Republic 3" />

[[File:BartolAndGranOfferKeeveBuccoBread-2021THR3.png|thumb|right|350px|Bartol (center) and a Gran (right) offered bucco bread to Jedi Knight Keeve Trennis (left).]]
Upon greeting Trennis, Bartol informed<ref name="The High Republic 3" /> her<ref name="The High Republic 3 preview" /> that because they thought the Jedi would be hungry, they had brought them the bread, and told her the name of the bread and that it had been baked that [[Standard day|day]]. The Gran then asked the Jedi Knight if the Jedi ate, which she responded to by stating they did. Trennis then told the children that it was really kind of them to offer them the bread, but they did not need to as the Jedi had their own [[Foodstuff|food]]. The Jedi Knight did accept the basket of bucco bread, and was then approached by a [[Rodian]] couple who lived in the colony&mdash;[[Reeta]] and<ref name="The High Republic 3" /> [[Neeso]]<ref name="The High Republic 5">[[The High Republic (2021) 5|''The High Republic'' (2021) 5]]</ref>&mdash;who had brought the Jedi a bowl of [[samana spiced loaf]]. Neeso spotted the bucco bread and noted that the children had had the same idea as them, and Trennis asked if they had brought more bread.<ref name="The High Republic 3" />

As she spoke with the couple, as well as<ref name="The High Republic 3" /> the [[Artiodac]] [[Speaker (Sedri Minor)|speaker]] of the colony [[Kalo Sulman]],<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> the Jedi Knight held the basket of bucco bread in her [[Limb|hand]]. After the colonists were all taken away by Sulman, Trennis went to search for her missing friend,<ref name="The High Republic 3" /> Jedi Knight<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> [[Ceret]], and was no longer holding the basket.<ref name="The High Republic 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bucco bread appeared in the [[The High Republic (2021) 3|third issue]] of the [[2021]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2021)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']], released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="The High Republic 3" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]], illustrated by [[Ario Anindito]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[March 3]], 2021.<ref name="The High Republic 3 preview" />
