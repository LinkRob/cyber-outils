The '''Director of Investigation''' was implemented into the [[Galactic Republic]], activated by<ref name="Rise" /> the [[Standard year|year]] [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> when the [[Clone Wars]] had begun between the Republic and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]]. Held during the war by [[Foga Brill]], the [[director]] was charged with leading the Republic [[Judicial Department]]'s [[Republic Office of Criminal Investigations|Criminal Investigations Office]], as well as the [[Republic Enforcement DataCore|Enforcement DataCore]] and other [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement agencies]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Director of Investigation was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Director of Investigation/Legends|Director of Investigation]] was originally introduced in "[[Byss and the Deep Core]], Part 3: Prakith," a [[2005]] online supplement written by [[Rodney Thompson]] for the ''[[Byss and the Deep Core]]'' article series produced by [[Wizards of the Coast]].<ref name="BatDC">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=starwars/article/sw20050421planet3|series=Byss and the Deep Core|text=Part 3: Prakith}}</ref>
