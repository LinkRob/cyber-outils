'''Dave Boat''' is an American voice actor who provided the voice for the [[Republic Infantry]] in the ''[[Star Wars: Battlefront II]]'' video game. He also provided additional voices for ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', and ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>
