The '''Chas''' were a family of [[bounty hunter]]s who operated during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. They consisted of [[Ramat Cha]], [[Bhada Cha]], and [[Chanath Cha]]. They were [[Assassination attempts on Darth Vader|hired]] to [[Death|assassinate]] the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], though they were [[Mission to Cabarria|unsuccessful]].<ref name="Dark Lord 11" /> Vader killed Ramat and Bhada. Chanath was forced by her [[parent]]s to swear that she would not seek revenge against Vader. Chanath, however, saw the deaths of her parents as the death of her [[heart]], and therefore the end of her oath, and decided to become a pet operative of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] in order to get close to Vader and take her revenge, but she never saw him again.<ref name="Crimson Reign 1">[[Crimson Reign 1|''Crimson Reign'' 1]]</ref>
