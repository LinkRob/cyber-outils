The '''Dewback Set''' was the name given to a set of two weapons, an [[Arc Caster]] and a [[Pulse Blaster]], utilized by the team of [[Jawa]] brothers known collectively as the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Utooni]] that they could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to their [[Suspicious Set|standard set]]. These sandy weathered [[weapon]]s featured a kitbashed appearance with a [[Color|black]], gray, and orange color scheme, as well as tan cloth wrappings. The Arc Caster featured a two-pronged caster component and cylindrical container attached to the rear, while the Pulse Blaster possessed a drum barrel and large ribbed tube-like barrel.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dewback Set was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Utooni's Suspicious Set, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Dewback Deceivers]].<ref name="Hunters" />
