'''Flip''' was an [[Ugnaught]] [[thief|bandit]] active during the [[High Republic Era]]. Working with fellow Ugnaughts [[Claro]] and [[Daro (Ugnaught)|Daro]], Flip was attentive but nonverbal. During a spree of robberies in which Claro stole the [[power converter]] off a [[Jedi shuttle (High Republic Era)|Jedi shuttle]], Flip and his associates were pursued by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Wes Vinik]] and [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Nubs]], who eventually apprehending the thieves, forcing them to return their plunder and clean up the messes they made along the way.<ref name="Nubs and the Bumbling Bandits" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flip is a character that appeared in the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Three|season three]] episode of ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'' television series titled "[[Nubs and the Bumbling Bandits]]". Flip was nonverbal in the episode, but his various vocalizations were provided by [[Dee Bradley Baker]].<ref name="Nubs and the Bumbling Bandits" />
