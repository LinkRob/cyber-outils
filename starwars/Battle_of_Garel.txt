The '''Battle of Garel''' was a battle fought during the [[Galactic Civil War]] in [[3 BBY]] when the [[Imperial Military|armed forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] launched an attack against [[Phoenix Cell]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Garel]]. The Empire learned of the rebellion's location in the [[Garel City Spaceport]] after [[Ezra Bridger]], a [[Padawan]] in the rebellion, was overheard talking about the rebels' location. [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] and [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]] of the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] ''[[Relentless]]'' led the assault against the rebels, while the [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]] known as the [[Seventh Sister]] and the [[Fifth Brother]] sought out Bridger and his master, [[Kanan Jarrus]]. At least one rebel [[transport]] was downed and destroyed during the rebellion's retreat from Garel, but, due to the [[pilot]]ing of [[Captain]] [[Hera Syndulla]] of the ''[[Ghost]]'', the Empire's [[tractor beam]] was disabled and the Phoenix Cell was able to escape from the planet and into [[hyperspace]].

==Prelude==
Following the [[Duel in Hammertown]], the two [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]] known as the [[Seventh Sister]] and the [[Fifth Brother]] discovered that the rebellion had a presence on the [[planet]] [[Garel]]. The Inquisitors shared this information with the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] and [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]]. Kallus, who was a perennial opponent of the [[Spectres]], urged the different [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] officials to work together to crush the rebels. He then ordered Admiral Konstantine to redeploy the [[Imperial Navy|Imperial fleet]] from [[Lothal]] to Garel. Prior to the planned assault, the Imperial authorities on Garel withdrew their [[stormtrooper]] patrols in [[Garel City]] in order to lull the rebels into a false sense of security.<ref name="Legacy" />

The planned Imperial attack on Garel was preceded by an [[Revolt in an Imperial prison|uprising]] in an Imperial prison. [[Ephraim Bridger|Ephraim]] and [[Mira Bridger]], the [[parent]]s of the [[Jedi]] rebel [[Ezra Bridger]], had been inspired by their son's broadcast earlier to launch a [[prison]] breakout. The prison uprising succeeded in freeing many [[prisoner]]s, including former Lothalian [[Governor]] [[Ryder Azadi]], but the Bridgers were [[Death|killed]] during the escape. Meanwhile, on Garel, Ezra experienced a [[Force vision]] of his parents and the impending Imperial attack.<ref name="Legacy" />

Ezra informed the rebel leaders [[Hera Syndulla]] and [[Kanan Jarrus]] about his vision. Hera and Kanan, who had just received news about the prison uprising on Lothal, decided to tell Ezra what [[Tseebo]] had shared with Hera earlier. Tseebo had told Hera that Ezra's parents were imprisoned within a network of Imperial prisons, but they were unsure which one it was. Hera later informed Ezra that [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]]'s contact in the [[Core Worlds|Core]] had sent him news of a mass breakout from an Imperial prison the night before, and Ezra realized that due to his vision, his parents were likely involved. After scanning a list of prisoners involved in the escape with the Force, Ezra picked out "Prisoner X-10." The Empire had deleted the prisoners' names and faces, so they did not know that this prisoner was Governor Azadi.<ref name="Legacy" />

Believing that the prison uprising and his Force vision were related, Ezra decided to travel to Lothal. After [[Sabine Wren]] received intelligence that the Imperial fleet above Lothal had departed, the Spectres made preparations to leave Garel. While shopping for supplies, [[Garazeb Orrelios]] and [[C1-10P|Chopper]] reported that they had not encountered any patrols. However, Sabine, as a former [[Imperial cadet]], knew that the Empire always withdrew their patrols prior to a military operation, and rightfully suspected that an Imperial assault on Garel was imminent.<ref name="Legacy" />

==Assault on Garel==
[[File:Taylander_shuttle.jpg|thumb|250px|Rebel ships take off as Imperial forces arrive]]
Zeb and Chopper shortly found themselves pursued by a stormtrooper patrol. Meanwhile, a fleet of [[Star Destroyer]]s arrived over Garel City. While waiting for Zeb and Chopper to return, Hera ordered Sabine to slice their jamming system in order to hamper the Imperials' efforts to locate the rebel ships. Zeb and Chopper managed to reunite with Kanan and Ezra in their hangar bay but were forced to fight off several stormtroopers led by Agent Kallus himself. Giving into his rage, Ezra used [[the Force]] to [[Force push|hurl]] Kallus against a wall.<ref name="Legacy" />

Kallus was followed by the Fifth Brother and the Seventh Sister, the latter of whom attempted to goad Ezra into a [[lightsaber duel]]. Before Ezra could respond, Kanan used the Force to shoot out the blast door controls, closing it in front of the Inquisitors. Reunited, the Spectres boarded the ''Ghost'' and managed to fight off a group of stormtroopers. Once airborne, Hera gave orders for [[Phoenix Squadron]] to abandon Garel. As the rebel fleet fled into the skies of Garel, Admiral Konstantine ordered his crew to prepare a [[tractor beam]] to intercept the rebel ships. At least one rebel ship was also hit by an Imperial [[laser]] blast and crashed into the streets of Garel.<ref name="Legacy" />

Phoenix Squadron Commander Jun Sato and [[Rex]]'s [[CR90 corvette]] [[Liberator (CR90 corvette)|''Liberator'']] was intercepted by Konstantine's Star Destroyer ''[[Relentless]]'' and trapped by the [[warship]]'s tractor beam. Meanwhile, Hera instructed Kanan, Ezra, and Chopper to leave for Lothal on the ''[[Phantom]]''. The ''Phantom'' then took advantage of a momentary gap in the Imperial formation to flee into [[hyperspace]]. Meanwhile, Hera responded to Sato and Rex's distress call by flying the ''Ghost'' towards the ''Relentless'' in an attempt to knock out the [[tractor beam projector]].<ref name="Legacy" />

Just as Sabine had acquired a [[Proton torpedo|torpedo]] lock on the tractor beam projector, a TIE blast knocked the ship out of alignment before she could fire. Undaunted, Hera then undertook a dangerous maneuver which involved ramming the ''Ghost'' against the projector, knocking it off the ''Relentless''{{'}} hull. The projector then collided with a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]]. With the ''Liberator'' free, the ''Ghost'' and the Phoenix rebel fleet was able to jump into hyperspace.<ref name="Legacy" />

==Aftermath==
Despite their efforts, the Galactic Empire failed to capture most of the rebel fleet hiding on Garel. Most of the Phoenix rebel fleet managed to escape into hyperspace.<ref name="Legacy" /> However, the rebels also suffered serious losses including at least one ship. Upon learning about the Phoenix rebels' losses, [[Alderaanian]] [[Senator]] and rebel leader [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] sent his adopted daughter [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] to deliver three [[Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette|''Sphyrna''-class Hammerhead corvette]] to reinforce the Phoenix forces. In order to hide [[Alderaan]]'s links to the rebels, Leia arranged for the Spectres to steal the ships on Lothal.<ref name="A Princess on Lothal" />

Meanwhile Ezra, Kanan, and Chopper managed to find Prisoner X-10 on Lothal and learned that he was Governor Azadi, a friend of Ezra's parents. From Azadi, Ezra learned about the deaths of his parents and was able to make peace with his past.<ref name="Legacy" /> Reuniting on Lothal, the Spectres then joined forces with Princess Leia and Azadi to [[Mission on Lothal|steal]] the Hammerhead corvettes from an [[Lothal Depot|Imperial depot]] on Lothal.<ref name="A Princess on Lothal" />

In [[2 BBY]], before [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] [[Battle of Atollon|attacked]] [[Atollon]], Ezra mentioned the assault on Garel as they received the warning about the Imperial fleet leaving Lothal.<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The battle on Garel first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Legacy (episode)|Legacy]]," which premiered on [[Disney XD]] on [[December 9]], [[2015]].
