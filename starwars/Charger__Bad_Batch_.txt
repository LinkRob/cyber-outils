'''Charger''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] before going [[Desertion|rogue]] with three other [[clone trooper]]s, [[Cole]], [[Hank]], and [[Rice (clone trooper)|Rice]]. Charger wore customized [[phase I clone trooper armor]] with [[Color|light blue]] markings. The [[Cole's squad|squad]] of rogue [[Cloning|clones]], led by [[Clone Sergeant]] Cole, became [[bounty hunter]]s and took on a [[bounty]] for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] to locate the [[scientist]] [[Ergin Lazlo]].<ref name="RogueAgents1" />
