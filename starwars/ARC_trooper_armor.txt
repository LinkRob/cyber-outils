'''ARC trooper armor''' was the kit issued to the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s elite [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC troopers]]. ARC trooper armor consisted of at least two sets, one being the [[Phase I ARC Trooper Armor]] used during the initial [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Clone Wars]] as a template of [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] with additional equipment and characteristics. After the implementation of Phase II, [[Phase II ARC Trooper Armor]] was introduced.

==Characteristics==
[[File:ARCcmdrColt-TCW.png|thumb|left|250px|ARC [[Commander]] [[Colt]] in his Phase I ARC Trooper Armor]]
ARC trooper [[armor]] was a type of [[clone trooper armor]] used by [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s, also known as ARC troopers, during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="ARC">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/clone-cadets-episode-guide|text="Clone Cadets" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> For some [[time]], ARC trooper armor consisted of the [[Phase I ARC Trooper Armor]],<ref name="Rise" /> one version of which had a [[helmet]] identical to the standard [[phase I clone trooper armor]].<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> Another version<ref name="ARC" /> of the phase I kit,<ref name="Rise" /> however, featured a unique helmet design; that helmet retained the sides, back, and fin of the standard phase I helmet, but the front resembled<ref name="ARC" /> the face of the eventual [[phase II clone trooper armor]] helmet,<ref name="DoU">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref> albeit but with a more triangular structure.<ref name="ARC" /> Both versions of the Phase I set featured additional [[Color|gray]] armor plating compared to the average [[Cloning|clone]], a double [[pauldron]], additional equipment, a [[kama]], a [[rangefinder]], ammunition pouches, a [[backpack]], and two holsters.<ref name="ARC" /><ref name="Battlefront II" />

ARC trooper armor was the basis for the phase II clone trooper armor<ref name="Collapse" /> that became standard issue to all [[clone trooper]]s in the later days of the Clone Wars. As standard clone troopers shifted into their new armor, ARC troopers also transitioned into a new set of gear,<ref name="DoU" /> the [[Phase II ARC Trooper Armor]],<ref name="Collapse" /> which closely resembled the baseline clone armor.<ref name="DoU" />

==History==
[[File:SWBF2_ARC_Troopers.png|thumb|right|250px|An ARC trooper wears the Phase I variant that came with a standard issue helmet.]]
Several [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s from [[Rancor Battalion]] used ARC trooper armor, including [[Clone Commander]] [[Colt]], [[Cloning|Clone]] [[Commander]] [[Havoc (ARC trooper)|Havoc]], Clone Commander [[Blitz]], and Clone Commander [[Hammer (ARC trooper)|Hammer]].<ref name="ARC Troopers">{{TCW|ARC Troopers (episode)}}</ref> ARC troopers [[Fives]] and [[Echo]] wore Phase I ARC armor after their promotions,<ref name="The Citadel">{{TCW|The Citadel (episode)}}</ref> with Fives later transitioning to Phase II.<ref name="Dark">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref> As an ARC trooper, [[Jesse]] used ARC armor by the time of [[Battle of Anaxes]].<ref name="The Bad Batch">{{TCW|The Bad Batch (episode)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Before an update, the video game [[Star Wars Battlefront II]] incorrectly depicted/labeled Phase I ARC trooper armor as Phase II.{{Clothes-stub}}
