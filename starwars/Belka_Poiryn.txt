'''Belka Poiryn''' was a [[Pau'an]] [[Jedi Master]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. She was known for having a deep connection to [[the Force]] and was particularly skilled at [[meditation]]. According to [[Jedi Knight]] [[Reath Silas]], some [[Jedi]] believed she had spent so much time in meditation that it slowed her aging; while Silas did not believe this, he noted she was much more long-lived than most Pau'ans.<ref name="Into the Light" />

During the [[Mission to Kashyyyk (blight)|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]] in [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Into the Light]]'', including Poiryn's time on Kashyyyk, take place in [[228 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:IntotheLightDate|here]].</ref> Poiryn was part of the [[meditation team]] working to hold back the [[blight]]. She, along with the rest of the meditation team, traveled to Kashyyyk ahead of the [[Kashyyyk team]] in order to slow the blight as much as possible. When Silas and his team arrived, she identified him as the team's leader and warned him about a [[Thornseed|source]] of [[Dark side of the Force|darkness]] on the planet, though she could not identify it. After the Thornseed was destroyed, Poiryn described to Silas how she felt a celebration in the Force, caused by a grove of [[white wroshyr tree]]s, which completely healed the damage caused by the blight.<ref name="Into the Light" />
