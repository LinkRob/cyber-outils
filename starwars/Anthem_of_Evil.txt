"'''Anthem of Evil'''" was written by [[John Williams]] for the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. A concert suite and film track, "Anthem of Evil" begins with a choir before drastically increasing in tempo and volume with brass instruments, and is largely developed from [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''The Last Jedi''{{'s}}]] [[desperation motif]], which was in turn developed from [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''The Force Awakens''{{'s}}]] [[tension motif]]. The tension motif itself shares similarities with the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s motif featured in the [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Attack of the Clones'']] track "[[The Arena (musical theme)|The Arena]]."

The piece also uses the [[prequel trilogy]]'s [[Mystery motif|mystery]] and [[Palpatine's machinations motif]], the [[original trilogy]]'s "[[The Emperor's Theme]]" and [[Wikipedia:Siegfried (opera)|Siegfried's motif]] in "[[The Force Theme]]," as well as the [[sequel trilogy]]'s [[Rey's Theme]] and [[Ben Solo's theme|Kylo Ren's theme]].

The melody of the suite is used as a [[Wikipedia:Leitmotif|leitmotif]] for the [[Sith Eternal]] in the film ''The Rise of Skywalker''. In the [[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker (soundtrack)#Official commercial release|official commercial release soundtrack]] of the film, the motif is played in multiple tracks, including "Journey to Exegol," "Destiny of a Jedi," "Join Me," "Approaching the Throne," and "Anthem of Evil," as well as the "Finale" track that occupies the film's end credits.
