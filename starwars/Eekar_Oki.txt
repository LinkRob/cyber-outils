'''Eekar Oki''' was a [[Mon Calamari]] [[Gender|male]] who served the [[Galactic Republic]] as a [[Jedi]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. In [[21 BBY]], Oki was present in a meeting of members of the [[Jedi Order]] after they were contacted by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederate]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] during his imprisonment of [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeth Koth]], and was present later in the year in the [[Imperial Senate Building|Galactic Senate Building]]. In [[20 BBY]], Oki welcomed back lost Jedi trainees after they were returned from [[Wasskah]], and in the [[19 BBY|following year]] he attended the [[Funeral of the Jedi Temple bombing victims|funeral]] of the victims of a [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|bombing]] at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple's]] [[hangar]].

==Biography==
Eekar Oki,<ref name="GI Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode209.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Grievous Intrigue}}</ref> a [[Mon Calamari]] [[Gender|male]], served as a [[Jedi]] during the waning years of the [[Galactic Republic]], during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Grievous Intrigue Date">{{TCWcanondates|209}}</ref> Oki was present among other Jedi, standing to the right<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> of [[Finn Ertay]],<ref name="GI Ep Guide" /> in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple's]] [[Jedi Temple communication center|communication center]] when the [[Jedi Order]] received a message from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[General]] [[Grievous]]. Grievous had [[Capture of Eeth Koth|captured]] the [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeth Koth]] and [[torture]]d Koth in the [[Hologram|holographic]] message. Oki left shortly after [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] told the [[Jedi Initiate]]s to leave.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

[[File:Eekar_Oki.png|thumb|left|250px|Eekar Oki and Finn Ertay in the Jedi Temple's communications center.]]

Later in the same year,<ref name="Heroes Date">{{TCWcanondates|310}}</ref> Oki spoke with fellow Jedi [[Anakin Skywalker]] in a hallway of the [[Imperial Senate Building|Galactic Senate Building]] in the midst of a lockdown made in response to a [[Bombing of Coruscant's central power distribution grid|bombing]] which occurred at [[Coruscant]]'s [[central power distribution grid]]. Oki and Skywalker's conversation was cut short by the arrival of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and Skywalker's [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], after which Oki walked away.<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides">{{TCW|Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref> In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Wookiee Hunt Date">{{TCWcanondates|322}}</ref> the Jedi was also present when Padawan Tano, as well as the [[Jedi Initiate]]s [[O-Mer]] and [[Jinx]], arrived at a [[Hangar|landing pad]] on the Jedi Temple's exterior aboard the ''[[Halo]]'', having returned from being [[Battle on Wasskah|rescued]] by [[Wookiee]]s on the [[moon]] [[Wasskah]]. There, Oki stood next to Ertay and several Wookiees.<ref name="Wookiee Hunt">{{TCW|Wookiee Hunt}}</ref>

After the [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|bombing]] of the Jedi Temple's hangar,<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="The Jedi Date">{{TCWcanondates|518}}</ref> Oki walked through the halls of the Jedi Temple, first passing Masters [[Mace Windu]] and [[Yoda]], who were speaking with Skywalker and Tano, before also passing by [[Sora Bulq]] and [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]].<ref name="Sabotage" /> He also attended the [[Funeral of the Jedi Temple bombing victims|funeral]] in honor of the bombing's victims, standing with his head down between Bulq and Senator [[Mon Mothma]].<ref name="TJWKTM">{{TCW|The Jedi Who Knew Too Much}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Oki had mottled [[Color|blue]] and orange skin, as well as yellow [[eye]]s. He had a single [[barbel]] on his chin,<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> which indicated youth.<ref name="LoG Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/lair-of-grievous-episode-guide|text="Lair of Grievous" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>

==Equipment==
While in the Jedi Temple and the Senate Building, Oki wore brown [[Jedi apparel|Jedi robes]] and carried a [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eekar Oki first appeared, albeit unnamed, in "[[Grievous Intrigue]]," the ninth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> which first aired on [[January 1]], [[2010]].<ref name="Episode Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref> Oki was identified by name in the episode's accompanying episode guide on [[StarWars.com]], in its "Trivia & Details" section.<ref name="GI Ep Guide" />
