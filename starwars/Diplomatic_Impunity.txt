"'''Diplomatic Impunity'''" is a story featuring [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] was written by [[Marc Bernardin]] and illustrated by [[Stefano Raffaele]] and [[Andres Mossa]]. The story was published in the [[Darth Vader – Black, White & Red 3|third issue]] of the anthology miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Darth Vader – Black, White & Red]]'', that was published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[June 28]], [[2023]].
