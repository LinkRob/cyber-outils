'''Emergency repair patches''' were [[durasteel]] disks that could be used applied to damaged machinery to keep it operational until repairs could be made. The patches could be used by [[droid]]s in the same way that organic beings could use [[stimpack]]s. The repair patches cost 25 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] per unit.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
