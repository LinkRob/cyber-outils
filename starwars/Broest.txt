'''Broest''' was a location<ref name="L2" /> in the [[Broest system]] of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]]<ref name="Interior" /> that fell in grid square L-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> [[Ruica]] [[Plant|seeds]] from Broest were transported across [[the galaxy]] to the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] for [[farmer]]s to grow. When, during her childhood, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] would not eat ruica, her<ref name="L2" /> adoptive<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> [[Parent|father]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] told her about the seeds being brought to Alderaan from Broest in order to convince her.<ref name="L2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Broest was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] [[Princess Leia 2|''Princess Leia'' 2]], which was written by [[Mark Waid]]<ref name="L2" /> and released by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[March 18]], [[2015]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/52371/princess_leia_2015_2|text=Star Wars: Princess Leia (2015) #2}}</ref> The first mention of [[Broest/Legends|Broest]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity was in "[[Recon & Report: The Journey to Coruscant]]," an article written by [[Peter M. Schweighofer]] and published in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 2|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 2]] in [[May]] [[1994]].<ref name="Adventure Journal 2">{{JournalCite|2|Recon & Report: The Journey to Coruscant}}</ref>
