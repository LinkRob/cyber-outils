{{Quote|I knew Vader's lightsaber mattered to him&mdash;it was the only object toward which I had ever seen him demonstrate emotion of any kind. In meetings with Palpatine, on occasion, he would touch it, clearly unaware of his actions.|[[Wilhuff Tarkin]]|<ref name="Dark Lord 18">[[Darth Vader (2017) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 18]]</ref>}}
'''Darth Vader's lightsaber''' was the third and final [[lightsaber]] personally built by [[Anakin Skywalker]] after he became Darth Vader, a [[Sith Lord]] who served as [[Darth Sidious]]' last [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] and [[Commander-in-Chief]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|military]]. He built the weapon after he lost [[Vader's lightsaber|his previous lightsaber]] during a [[Assassination attempts on Darth Vader|skirmish]] on [[Cabarria]].

The lightsaber was used to [[Death|kill]] many [[Jedi]] in the [[Great Jedi Purge]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. [[Darth]] Vader most notably wielded the lightsaber in [[Battle on Mapuzo|several]] [[Fight on a barren moon|duels]] with [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] until the latter's [[Rescue of Princess Leia|death]], and later with his son, [[Luke Skywalker]], where he would [[Duel on Cloud City|cut off the young Jedi's right hand]]. During their [[Battle of Endor|final confrontation]] on the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]], Luke returned the favor, cutting off Vader's right hand, which then fell into the reactor shaft, still clutching the weapon.

==Description==
[[File:WeStand-Transformations.png|thumb|left|250px|Darth Vader used his lightsaber until his death on the second Death Star.]]
The lightsaber was equipped with a [[Dual-phase lightsaber|dual-phase function]], allowing the blade's length to be manually controlled and altered in combat.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> The weapon featured a [[Color|black]]-ridged handgrip, black [[power cell]] chamber, beveled emitter shroud, two dual-phase focusing [[crystal]]s,<ref name="GaG" /> high-output [[diatium power cell]], and customary power and length adjustment knobs.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars NE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> Darth Vader was constantly fine-tuning and upgrading his lightsaber for maximum blade power and stability.<ref name="GaG" /> As observed by [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], the lightsaber was something that Vader actually cared about. That left it the only object Tarkin had ever seen the [[Sith Lord]] display any emotion towards.<ref name="Dark Lord 18" />

<div style="float:right">[[File:AnakinSkywalkerFirstLightsaber-SWCT.png|thumb|250px|right|Vader's lightsaber shared a similar design with his first Jedi lightsaber.]]</div>
Because of the modifications Vader made to his lightsaber over the [[Standard year|years]], its exterior also changed, somewhat, in appearance though its design was mostly based on [[Anakin Skywalker's first lightsaber|his first Jedi lightsaber]] which was destroyed on [[Geonosis]] in [[22 BBY]]. Vader appeared to have experimented with a rounded emitter shroud similar to [[Skywalker lightsaber|his second Jedi weapon]].<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> As a [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], Vader would often utilize his lightsaber during confrontations with [[Jedi]], but also against other opponents, even though he sometimes preferred to use [[the Force]] to dispatch lesser victims.<ref name="Darth Vader's lightsaber">{{Databank|darth-vaders-lightsaber|Darth Vader's lightsaber}}</ref> Like his Jedi lightsaber,<ref name="Water War">{{TCW|Water War}}</ref> Vader's lightsaber could operate underwater.<ref name="Vader 3">[[Darth Vader (2020) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 3]]</ref>

==History==
===Creation===
{{Quote|Darth Vader lost his Jedi lightsaber in the same duel that left his organic form ravaged. Soon after his body was rebuilt, his quest to find a new weapon commenced. Vader first claimed the lightsaber of Jedi Master Kirak Infil'a, making the once-pure crystal within sing a hymn of darkness. When that weapon was destroyed, Vader salvaged its crystal and constructed his own lightsaber, simple in design but devastating in function.|[[Darth Sidious]]|<ref name="Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref>}}
A short [[time]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] was tasked by his [[Sith Master|master]], [[Darth Sidious]], to [[Death|kill]] a [[Jedi]] and retrieve their [[kyber crystal]] in order to power a new [[Sith]] [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Dark Lord 1">[[Darth Vader (2017) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 1]]</ref> Vader went to the [[Jedi outpost]] ''[[Brighthome]]'' where he temporarily used a [[Color|green]] lightsaber from the wall to kill most of the [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="Dark Lord 2">[[Darth Vader (2017) 2|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 2]]</ref> He tracked [[Jedi Master]] [[Kirak Infil'a]] to the [[Al'doleem|river moon of Al'doleem]] and [[Mission to Al'doleem|confronted]] him. Vader eventually killed the Jedi Master and retrieved his [[curved-hilt lightsaber]].<ref name="Dark Lord 4">[[Darth Vader (2017) 4|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 4]]</ref> He headed to [[Mustafar]], where he [[Bleeding|bled]] the lightsaber's [[crystal]], turning it from green to red.<ref name="Dark Lord 5">[[Darth Vader (2017) 5|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 5]]</ref> Vader used [[Vader's lightsaber|Kirak Infil'a's lightsaber]] in conjunction with his corrupted, red kyber crystal in multiple conflicts, such as when he briefly [[Lightsaber duel|fought]] [[the Grand Inquisitor]]<ref name="Dark Lord 6">[[Darth Vader (2017) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 6]]</ref> and [[Jocasta Nu]] in the former [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]].<ref name="Dark Lord 9">[[Darth Vader (2017) 9|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 9]]</ref>

[[File:VadersSaber-2017DarthVader12.png|thumb|right|300px|Vader's Sith lightsaber was powered by the corrupted kyber crystal of the Jedi Kirak Infil'a, with Vader designing it a new hilt after the old one was destroyed.]]
He also wielded the hilt during a lesson with Darth Sidious in [[the Works]], where Sidious used [[Darth Sidious' lightsabers|his remaining lightsaber]] to demonstrate that the lightsaber was nothing more than a symbol for the true power that anyone who stood against the dark side would face.<ref name="The Lesson">{{StoryCite|story=The Lesson (Darth Vader)|book=Star Wars (2020) 25|sformatted=The Lesson (Darth Vader)}}</ref> Infil'a's hilt was destroyed on [[Cabarria]] by a [[Chas|family of mercenaries]] hired to [[Assassination attempts on Darth Vader|assassinate Vader]], during which the hilt was shattered by the combined strain of a [[tractor rifle]] and Vader's pulling it back with the Force.<ref name="Dark Lord 11">[[Darth Vader (2017) 11|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 11]]</ref> Vader then used the [[Ninth Sister's lightsaber]] to deal with the [[bounty hunter]]s. The destruction of Infil'a's hilt provoked Vader to build a hilt of his own design. During his journey back to [[Coruscant]], Vader constructed a new hilt for his crystal while aboard his [[Vader's starship|personal]] [[starship]]. Vader later presented the weapon to Sidious, who was impressed with his apprentice's new weapon.<ref name="Dark Lord 12" />

===Mon Cala Campaign===
[[File:Battlefront_Vader.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Vader stands with his lightsaber in hand]]
Around [[18 BBY|one]] year after the rise of the Empire, Vader was sent to hunt down and execute a Jedi hiding on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[ocean]] [[planet]] of [[Mon Cala]].<ref name="Dark Lord 13">[[Darth Vader (2017) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 13]]</ref> Upon making it to the planet, Vader and the [[Inquisitorius]] encountered [[Battle of Dac City|great resistance]] from the native [[Mon Calamari]] and [[Quarren]] in [[Dac City]]. Eventually [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] were able to secure the outpost, however, the natives flooded the [[city]] in a massive tidal wave which plunged Vader into the depths of the vast ocean.<ref name="Dark Lord 14">[[Darth Vader (2017) 14|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 14]]</ref> Sinking deep into the [[Great Ungeness Trench]], Vader activated his lightsaber as a light source in the pitch black.<ref name="Dark Lord 15">[[Darth Vader (2017) 15|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 15]]</ref>

Eventually Vader escaped the trench and returned to his Imperial forces.<ref name="Dark Lord 15" /> The [[Ninth Sister]] of the Inquisitorius informed Vader that they had discovered the location of the Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Ferren Barr]] and [[Ferren Barr's disciples|his disciples]]. While en route to the Jedi's location, Vader was contacted by [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], who wanted Vader to capture the [[King of Mon Cala]].<ref name="Dark Lord 16">[[Darth Vader (2017) 16|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 16]]</ref> Vader entered the [[King]]'s [[Royal Command Bunker]], where he was met by several native [[soldier]]s. Vader used his lightsaber to cut a path to the King, whom he captured. Barr, who had [[Skirmish in Bel City|escaped]] the Inquisitorius, [[Duel in the Royal Command Bunker|confronted]] Vader in the Command Bunker. A [[Lightsaber combat|duel]] broke out between the two. Eventually Vader cut down the Padawan, ending the [[invasion of Mon Cala]].<ref name="Dark Lord 17">[[Darth Vader (2017) 17|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 17]]</ref>

Over the course of their careers, Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin came to understand that Vader's lightsaber was something he cared about, even observing Vader touching it without thinking during some meetings with the Emperor. When Vader asked Tarkin to [[Hunt on Chandar's Folly|hunt him]], which Tarkin believed to be Vader looking for some kind of challenge, Tarkin made sure to target and steal Vader's lightsaber early into the hunt. The effort cost Tarkin six of his hunters, which he deemed a bargain for the victory; [[BX-series droid commando]] hunter [[Eighty-One]] managed to snatch the lightsaber with a cable. Tarkin wore Vader's lightsaber on his belt until Vader reclaimed it in the [[stormlands]]. Upon Vader reclaiming the blade, Tarkin compared Vader to someone returning to life.<ref name="Dark Lord 18" />

[[File:Acme-EvilLord.png|thumb|right|250px|Darth Vader wielding his lightsaber]]
As construction of his [[Fortress Vader|personal castle]] on [[Mustafar]] concluded, Vader later used his lightsaber in the [[Battle of Fortress Vader]], leading [[Fortress Vader Imperial garrison|Imperial forces]] against attacking [[Mustafarian]]s. Afterward, Vader dueled the ressurected Sith Lord [[Darth Momin]], whose spirit had helped design the fortress with the secret aim of restoring himself through a locus in the dark side. Although Momin severed one of Vader's arms, causing the mechanical appendage that held his lightsaber to fall, Vader used the Force to crush Momin before entering the portal Momin had created<ref name="Dark Lord 24">[[Darth Vader (2017) 24|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 24]]</ref> to venture into a metaphysical plain, intending to resurrect his wife [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. Although he failed in that mission, he saw a glimpse of his future [[Mission to Malachor|lightsaber duel]] against former apprentice [[Ahsoka Tano]] on [[Malachor]]. After being hit by a blast of Force energy in his vision that awoke him back in the real world, Vader used the Force to retrieve his lightsaber, which he then plunged into the mound to seal off the portal<ref name="Dark Lord 25" /> that had failed to resurrect Amidala,<ref name="Encyclopedia Maul">{{EncyclopediaCite|Darth Maul and Other Followers of the Dark Side}}</ref> triggering a blast of dark side lighting that raged across the landscape.<ref name="Dark Lord 25">[[Darth Vader (2017) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 25]]</ref>

===Hunting Obi-Wan Kenobi===
====Battle on Mapuzo====
[[File:Scariest-Servant-Vader.png|thumb|left|300px|Darth Vader wields his lightsaber on Mapuzo]]
Vader used his lightsaber in a brief [[Battle on Mapuzo|duel]] between his former master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], which ended with Vader quickly overpowering Kenobi, who had grown inept after years of not using the Force. As the Sith Lord forced Kenobi to run away, Vader knocked down a storage container filled with flammable rocks while he seized Obi-Wan with the Force. After igniting the flammable rocks with his lightsaber, Vader forces Kenobi into the [[Fire|flames]], severely burning him while claiming that his pain had only just begun. The Sith Lord then ordered his [[stormtrooper]]s to seize Kenobi, but the [[Hidden Path]] member [[Tala Durith]] arrived and re-ignited the rocks with a [[blaster]] bolt, cutting Kenobi off from Vader and his [[troop]]s while she rescued the Jedi Master.<ref name="Part III">{{Kenobi|Part III}}</ref>

====Fighting the Third Sister====
When the [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister]] led the [[Attack on Jabiim|attack]] on [[Jabiim]], Kenobi uncovered the Third Sister's true plan to [[assassin]]ate [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. As Kenobi escaped, Vader became distracted, allowing the Third Sister to use [[Reva's lightsaber|her lightsaber]] to strike at Vader. However, the [[Sith Lord]] used the Force to stop the blade. Although Vader didn't use his lightsaber, he pulled the weapon from her hands, splitting it in two and [[Lightsaber duel|dueling]] her with one half. Vader quickly defeated the lone Inquisitor, stabbing her with her own lightsaber and soon revealing that the Grand Inquisitor had survived her betrayal.<ref name="Part V">{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref>

====Fight on a barren moon====
After Vader tracked Kenobi to a [[Unidentified barren moon|barren moon]], he ignited his lightsaber and started [[Fight on a barren moon|dueling]] his former master. Although Vader initially was able to overwhelm his former master, Kenobi managed to regain his strength and re-engaged Vader, heavily damaging the Sith Lord's [[Darth Vader's armor|armor]] and cutting open his [[helmet]], revealing his heavily scarred face.<ref name="Part VI">{{Kenobi|Part VI}}</ref>

===Hunting the rebellion===
====Duel on Lothal====
[[File:Kanan_and_Ezra_face_Darth_Vader.png|thumb|right|250px|250px|Vader duels Kanan and Ezra on Lothal.]]
As a group of rebels known as the [[Spectres]] were escaping on an Imperial [[shuttle]], Vader confronted them with his lightsaber ignited. [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]s [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Ezra Bridger]] activated their own lightsabers and engaged in a lightsaber duel, but they were easily overpowered. The rebels [[Sabine Wren]] and [[Garazeb Orrelios]] then threw [[thermal detonator]]s onto some nearby [[All Terrain Defense Pod]]s, scattering the stormtroopers and distracting Vader. Seizing their chance, Bridger and Jarrus used the Force to knock the Dark Lord of the Sith off-balance as the walkers crashed down on him. The rebels believed that Vader was dead, but he used the Force to lift the burning walkers into the air.<ref name="Siege of Lothal">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''</ref>

====Duel on Malachor====
Under the surface of [[Malachor]], outside the [[Sith]] [[Malachor Sith Temple|Temple]] located there, Vader [[Mission to Malachor|confronted]] Bridger as he was leaving the temple. After questioning Bridger on how he had opened the [[Sith holocron]] he was in possession of, he engaged Bridger in combat; though the young Jedi briefly held his own, he was no match for the Sith Lord, who sliced his blade through Bridger's lightsaber, destroying the weapon. As Vader prepared to finish him off, Ahsoka Tano intervened,<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref> beginning the event he had foreseen in Momin's portal.<ref name="Dark Lord 25" />

[[File:TwilightoftheApprenticethumb.png|thumb|left|250px|250px|Vader locks blades with his former apprentice.]]
Vader told Tano that the Emperor would show her mercy if she agreed to join the Sith in hunting down the remaining Jedi. She refused and told him that his Inquisitors had killed all of the Jedi already. Tano let Vader know that her former master could never be as vile as he is. Vader informed her that Anakin Skywalker was weak, and that he destroyed him, to which Tano vowed to avenge Skywalker's death. When Vader stated that revenge was not the Jedi way, Tano told him that she is no Jedi.<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice" />

The two battled, with Tano pushing Vader back in an initial strike. During this duel, Bridger removed the Sith holocron from the obelisk in the temple with the help of Jarrus, who had just arrived in their ship, causing the temple to start to collapse in on itself. Tano and Vader continued their duel until Vader pushed Tano over the edge into a chasm below.<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice" />

Vader attempted to pull the holocron back from Bridger with the Force, but Tano climbed back up and attacked him, knocking his lightsaber away and damaging his helmet. With the right side of his helmet destroyed, Vader called out Tano's name, which shocked her. Underneath the torn hole in his helmet, she saw the [[eye]] of her former master, and vowed not to leave him again. Vader hesitated before reigniting his lightsaber and telling her that she will die. Meanwhile, around them, the temple continued to close in. The two continued their lightsaber duel as the temple began to collapse. Vader did escape the Sith temple to Malachor's surface, albeit injured.<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice" />

===Galactic Civil War===
====Battle of Scarif====
[[File:VaderProfundity.png|thumb|right|250px|Vader about to attack the rebels on the ''Profundity''.]]
During the [[Battle of Scarif]], Vader boarded the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[command ship]] ''[[Profundity]]'' to retrieve the [[Death Star plans]] from the fleeing rebels. After boarding into a pitch-black corridor, Vader ignited the blade as he confronted a group of [[Alderaanian consular security|Rebel soldiers]] attempting to escape with the plans to the ''[[Tantive IV]]''. Vader cut his way through them with terrifying ease, but his efforts ultimately proved unsuccessful, with the ''Tantive IV'' jumping to [[hyperspace]] before he could take back the plans.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

====Duel on the Death Star====
[[File:KarlFitzgerald-DuelonDeathStar.png|thumb|left|250px|Darth Vader duels his former master for the last time.]]
Vader's lightsaber was later used when Vader's old master Obi-Wan Kenobi infiltrated the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] along with a [[Han Solo|smuggler]] and, unbeknownst to Vader, his son, [[Luke Skywalker]]. After seeing Kenobi, Vader ignited his lightsaber, and the two dueled while his son and the smuggler escaped along with [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. During their duel, Kenobi looked at Luke, lowered his defenses and allowed himself to be cut down by Vader's blade.<ref name="Episode IV" />

====Duel on Cloud City====
[[File:ESB-BespinDuel.png|thumb|right|250px|Darth Vader dueling Luke Skywalker in Cloud City.]]
After failing to capture Skywalker during the [[Battle of Hoth]], Vader set a trap for his son on the [[Cloud City]] of [[Bespin]]. He captured Han Solo and Organa to [[torture]] them, hoping to bring Skywalker to him. After Skywalker saw a vision of his friends' suffering, he rushed to Cloud City to help them, where he encountered Vader. After a long, grueling [[Duel on Cloud City|duel]], the two ended up in Cloud City's center [[reactor shaft]], where Vader used his lightsaber to [[Dismemberment|cut off]] Skywalker's hand. Vader then revealed to Skywalker that he was his [[Parent|father]].<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref>

====Investigation on his son====
After the duel on Cloud City, Vader recruited the [[SP-4 ISC crime scene analysis droid|SP-4 ISC]] [[forensics droid]] [[ZED-6-7]]. While in the [[Lars homestead|Lars Homestead]] on [[Tatooine]] with ZED-6-7, Vader used his lightsaber to fight off some pirates. Later in [[Senator]] [[Padmé's Apartment|Padmé Amidala's apartment]] on Coruscant, Vader used his lightsaber to fight off the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Vendaxan land squid]] that attacked the complex and killed a number of [[Sabé]]'s crew who were investigating [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Amidala's]] death.<ref name="Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2020) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 1]]</ref>

====Battle of Endor====
[[File:FineArtPrints-A-Sons-Destiny.png|thumb|left|250px|Darth Vader dueling his son on the second Death Star.]]
During the [[Battle of Endor]], Vader ignited his lightsaber to defend Darth Sidious when his son attempted to strike him down. Later in the battle, Vader ignited it again in a duel against his son. After Skywalker hid in the shadows of the [[Emperor's Throne Room|throne room]], Vader taunted him, uncovering from Skywalker's mind that he had a daughter and taunting Skywalker by proclaiming that he would turn his sister&mdash;unaware that it was none-other-than Leia Organa&mdash;to the [[dark side of the Force]]. Skywalker attacked Vader, knocking him down and cutting off his hand. Vader's lightsaber, clutched in the severed hand, then fell into the reactor shaft of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]].<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

===Acolytes of the Beyond===
After the fall of the Sith at the Battle of Endor, Vader's lightsaber became an object of worship for [[Sith cultist]]s called the [[Acolytes of the Beyond]], and, on [[Taris]], the [[Religion|cult]] obtained what they believed was the weapon from a [[junk dealer]] named [[Ooblamon]], so they could destroy it and return it to Vader in death.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:VadersLightsaberConceptJohnMollo2.jpg|thumb|right|100px|Concept art of Darth Vader's lightsaber.|none]]Technically, the first Sith lightsaber designed was Darth Vader's. However, there were no early plans to make Vader's weapon distinct from the other lightsabers depicted, beyond the black styling on the hilt itself. The concept art of Vader's lightsaber hilt was based upon [[George Lucas]]'s descriptions of the weapons as jewel-studded cylinders. Ultimately, the hilt carried by [[David Prowse]] in ''A New Hope'' and ''The Empire Strikes Back'' was made from the flash attachment of a British 3-cell MPP Microflash, a flash tube attachment used on forties-era press cameras, with the ridged grip made by gluing portions of hard plastic 'T' track used for the sliding doors of display cabinets (often mistaken for wiper blades) onto the bare cylinder, and the switch plate fashioned from the LED display of a 1974 calculator.<ref name="TCV">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Vader]]''</ref>

In ''Return Of The Jedi'', however the original prop was misplaced and a similar 'lookalike' was created by modifying a 3-cell Graflex Flash tube like the one used for Luke Skywalker's first lightsaber, it is believed by some to be a stunt saber used in the first two movies as the MPP Microflash was much harder to find than the Graflex or possibly even one of the original Skywalker sabers heavily modified. The original lightsaber effect was provided by the reflective, spinning dowel that made up the blade, which appeared to glow to the camera.<ref name="TCV" />

However, Lucas was unsatisfied with these early effects, and had the glows further augmented by rotoscoped animation. During this phase, it was decided to make the lightsabers have different color blades to further differentiate them, and Vader was given a red blade. While the methods used to create the lightsabers would change over the course of the next two movies, the presentation of Vader's lightsaber itself would remain consistent.<ref name="TCV" />
