A '''fengla''' was a type of small, [[hair]]less vermin with high [[Limb|haunches]], [[Color|green]] [[eye]]s, and crooked [[Tooth|incisors]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Sinjir Rath Velus]] encountered a fengala in the subterranean [[Catacombs of Akiva|catacombs]] under the [[city]] [[Myrra]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. At first, Velus saw only the fengla's eyes, which glowed like [[crystal]]s in the darkness. However, once the light of several [[illumi-droid]]s accompanying Velus and his companions reached the creature, he realised what it was as it hissed and spit before scurrying away with its claws clicking.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
A fengla appeared in the [[2015]] novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], which was written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
