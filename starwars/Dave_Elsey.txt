'''Dave Elsey''' worked on [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] as the creature shop Makeup FX designer, and received an Oscar Nomination for his work. ''Revenge of the Sith'' is the only ''[[Star Wars]]'' movie ever to be nominated for an Academy Award in this category. Elsey has since won an Oscar with Original Star Wars trilogy makeup artist [[Rick Baker]] for their work on the [[Wikipedia:The Wolfman (2010 film)|Wolfman]].
