{{Quote|When I was eighteen, I accompanied my father to the Cadavine sector on a diplomatic mission to Melida/Daan. My mother was on her campaign for the Senate. My father was a senator on Coruscant, and he was working with the Jedi to negotiate the release of the Daan's leader held captive by the Melida.|Axel Greylark, to [[Gella Nattai]]|audio=IAccompaniedMyFatherToTheCadavineSector-Convergence.flac|<ref name="Convergence" />}}
The '''Cadavine sector''' was a sector that contained [[Melida/Daan]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Axel Greylark]] and [[Lexxir Greylark|his father]] undertook a [[Diplomatic mission to Melida/Daan|mission]] to Melida/Daan, in order to free the [[Unidentified Daan leader|leader]] of the [[Daan (people)|Daan]], who had been taken captive by the [[Melida]]. The mission turned out to be a trap, and Greylark's father [[Death|died]], and a [[Unidentified Jedi Knight (Axel Greylark)|Jedi Knight]] saved Greylark.<ref name="Convergence" />
