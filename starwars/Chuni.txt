'''Chuni''' was an individual who had earned a notable score in the [[arcade game]] [[Raven 6]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] beat Chuni's high score while playing Raven 6.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Chuni was mentioned in the high scores of the optional arcade game activity in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Prior to the games release on [[August 30]], [[2024]],<ref name="Story trailer">{{SWYouTube|DUyk6D5rgcE|Star Wars Outlaws: Official Story Trailer}}</ref> Chuni's name appeared in the game's "Official Gameplay Showcase" trailer released [[June 10]] of the same year.<ref name="Official Gameplay">{{SWYouTube|R_O1eqWWbe4|Star Wars Outlaws: Official Gameplay Showcase &#124; Ubisoft Forward}}</ref> Each playthrough, Chuni has a chance to be listed in the third, fourth and fifth high score positions on any of the game's Raven 6 [[arcade machine]]s with a random score. As such, this article does not assume any one score or location Chuni played to be [[canon]]ical.<ref name="Outlaws" />
