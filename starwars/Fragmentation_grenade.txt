The '''fragmentation grenade''' was an explosive device that caused damage through its explosion, and the grapeshots contained within it. Its explosive power was higher than normal explosives to further propel the non-explosive material within it.<ref name="EHWaUE">{{EncyclopediaCite|Explosives, Heavy Weapons, and Power Sources}}</ref> One was used by [[Roja]], a [[Rebel trooper|soldier]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]], against the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] during the [[Battle of Hoth]]<ref name="Twilight" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> The [[grenade]]s were also employed by the Empire, specifically by [[death trooper]]s and [[coastal defender stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Destiny" />
