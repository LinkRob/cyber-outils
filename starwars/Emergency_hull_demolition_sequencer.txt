The '''emergency hull demolition sequencer''' was a feature of the [[starship]] ''[[Onyx Cinder]]''.<ref name="PastTheBarrier" /> Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SCDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> when the [[droid]] [[SM-33]] was teaching the [[youngling]]s [[Fern]], [[KB]], [[Neel]], and [[Wim]] how to fly the ship, Wim reached for a covered button which activated the sequence but SM-33 stopped him from pressing it and told him never to touch it.<ref name="PastTheBarrier" /> It would regardless be activated during their [[Mission to Skull Ridge Mountain|escape]] from [[Lanupa]], shedding the outer hull of the ''Onyx Cinder'', including its foremost engines, into a sleeker design.<ref name="ZeroFriendsAgain">{{SkeletonCrew|Zero Friends Again}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The emergency hull demolition sequencer was first mentioned in "[[Way, Way Out Past the Barrier]]," the second episode of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'' which aired on [[December 2]] [[2024]].<ref name="PastTheBarrier" />
