'''Arkanians''' were a [[near-human]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] native to the [[planet]] [[Arkania]], characterized by the skin tone ranging from [[Color|tan]] to pale, typically white hair and pale white eyes. [[Urma Athantras]],<ref name="DoH" /> [[Reeshee Irway]],<ref name="Most Wanted" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Theca]]<ref name="Inquisitor">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''</ref> and [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Cippa Tarko]] were members of the species.<ref name="Cataclysm">''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]''</ref>

==Biology and appearance==
Arkanians were native to the [[planet]] [[Arkania]].<ref name="DoH" /> Arkanians had infrared vision as a result of evolving on a frigid world.<ref name="Cataclysm" /> Arkanian [[scientist]]s had been experts in [[Bioengineering|genetic engineering]] for [[Millennium|thousands]] of years. After countless [[Standard century|centuries]] of medical experimentation, the [[species]] had developed [[Arkanian Offshoot|genetic offshoots]], whom they regard as lesser shadows of themselves or even [[Slavery|slaves]]. Most Arkanians resembled [[human]]s in many respects, save for pale white eyes and clawed digits on each hand. They were capable of seeing into the [[infrared]] [[spectrum]], which led to extreme visual sensitivity<ref name="DoH" /> but also allowed them to spy enemies via their body heat.<ref name="Insider231">{{InsiderCite|231|Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Out with a Bang}}</ref> This was helpful on their dark and cold [[homeworld]], but in certain environments, many were forced to wear polarized lenses or blinders.<ref name="DoH" />

==Society and culture==
{{Quote|What do you miss most about Arkania? I've never been.''"<br />"''I don't understand the question.|The [[human]] [[Joss Adren]] and the Arkanian [[Visma Tarko]]|<ref name="Survivors">{{InsiderCite|224|Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Survivors}}</ref>}}
Arkanian society was based around a rigid caste system, that expected pure-blood and a sharp, logical mind and looked down upon any signs of weakness or flaws.<ref name="DoH" /> One of the most illustrious Arkanian families was the [[Tarkos family]]. When asked what she missed most about Arkania, the Arkanian [[Visma Tarko]] said she did not understand the question.<ref name="Survivors" /> The most defining trait of the Arkanian species was their arrogance which stemmed from their belief in their genetic purity. This led to them often appearing arrogant and prideful to others as they saw themselves as being superior to all other species. Due to this natural arrogance, outsiders often faced Arkanians that expected subservience and respect.<ref name="DoH" />

Scholarly pursuits were highly valued within Arkanian society which meant that many of the species were highly skilled scientists as well as academics. They were not above using their bioengineering advancements to gain profit by selling of biological weapons to the highest bidder. Though a significant minority of Arkanians do hold a great moral objection to their advancements being used to commit such horrible crimes. These Arkanians hold to the belief that once physiological and technological perfection has been achieved, one must also achieve moral perfection to become a perfect being.<ref name="DoH" />

Arkanian scientists were also famous for their skills in genetic manipulation with this avenue of research leading to the creation of the [[Arkanian Offshoot]]s that were engineered to serve their pure-blooded masters. They were bred in great variety as living experiments, and were not regarded as "true Arkanians." They typically lived a brief life, with no hope of being regarded anything other than a slave or pet.<ref name="DoH" />

==History==
Over the years, as the Arkanians evolved on Arkania, they developed infrared vision.<ref name="Cataclysm" /> The ancient Arkanian scientists created the [[Quermian]] species from the [[Xexto]] and settled them on the [[planet]] [[Quermia]] after which they were named. Eventually, the Arkanians moved on, allowing Quermians to develop their own society.<ref name="SS">''[[Savage Spirits]]''</ref>
