'''Command Sector South''' was an area in the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. 

==Description==
The command sector was located on the southern hemisphere towards the bottom of the battlestation. Its northern counterpart was [[Command Sector (North)|Command Sector North]].<ref name="SWCT" />

==History==
Command Sector South was built between [[0 ABY|0]] and [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Complete Locations" /> It was destroyed in 4 ABY, along with the rest of the Death Star during the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>
