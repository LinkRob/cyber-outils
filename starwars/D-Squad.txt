'''D-Squad''' was a [[Republic Military|military]] [[squad]] that served the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The squad consisted of four [[astromech droid]]s, [[R2-D2]], [[QT-KT]], [[U9-C4]], and [[M5-BZ]], one [[DUM-series pit droid]] designated [[WAC-47]], and was placed under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]]. [[Standard year|Years]] later, amid the [[Galactic Civil War]], R2-D2 reassembled the squad with a number of new members in the name of opposing the [[Scourge]], a malevolent [[droid]].

==History==
===Clone Wars===
{{Quote|I would be proud to serve with you droids anytime, anywhere.|Meebur Gascon|<ref name="Point" />}}
[[File:DevisingAGamePlan-MIA.png|thumb|left|250px|D-Squad enlisted the help of the clone commando, Gregor]]
D-Squad was a [[Galactic Republic]] [[droid]] squad created to [[Mission to steal a Separatist encryption module|steal]] an [[encryption module]] from a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Aut-O's flagship|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]]. The squad consisted of consisted of four [[astromech droid]]s, [[R2-D2]], [[QT-KT]], [[U9-C4]], and [[M5-BZ]], one [[DUM-series pit droid]] designated [[WAC-47]], and was placed under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]].<ref name="SW" /> Despite the success of their mission, the squad crashed [[D-Squad's Maxillipede shuttle|their]] [[shuttle]] on the [[planet]] [[Abafar]].<ref name="Sunny">{{TCW|A Sunny Day in the Void}}</ref> While stranded on the [[desert]] world, the droids encountered an [[amnesia]]c [[clone commando]] designated [[Gregor|CC-5576-39]] "Gregor." With the [[Cloning|clone's]] help, Gascon and his squad managed to [[Escape from Abafar|leave]] Abafar.<ref name="MIA" />

Following their escape from Abafar, D-Squad encountered a lone [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], the ''[[Renown]]'', above the planet. Initially, Gascon believed that the Galactic Republic had come to rescue them, however, once they arrived to the [[Star Destroyer]]'s [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], they discovered that Separatist forces had hijacked the vessel. After escaping the [[battle droid]]s, D-Squad found a [[detonator]], and that the docking bays of the cruiser were filled with [[rhydonium]], which was known to be extremely explosive.<ref name="Point" />

Further on the ship, D-Squad ran into a small group of Republic droids including servant droid [[BNI-393]], who explained that the Separatists had taken over the starship and planned to destroy the [[space station]] [[Valor (space station)|''Valor'']], where the [[Republic strategy conference]] was taking place.<ref name="Point" />

Eventually, one of the [[Pistoeka sabotage droid|buzz droids]] discovered D-Squad's [[D-Squad's shuttle|location]], and as the buzz droids were closing in, BZ decided to open the air lock which would force the buzz droids out into [[hyperspace]]. The other droids magnetized themselves to the metallic surface, but BZ failed to magnetize in time, and was sucked out of the Star Destroyer along with all the other buzz droids. Following this loss, Gascon made a brief speech to honor BZ's sacrifice.<ref name="Point" />

When the cruiser came out of hyperspace above [[Carida]], which had ''Valor'' and over a dozen other Star Destroyers in orbit, D-Squad managed to evacuate while R2 detonated the explosives early, preventing the Separatists from destroying the space station. Initially, D-Squad assumed that R2 was gone, but several salvage teams were sent by General [[Anakin Skywalker]] to locate and rebuild R2. After the [[Carida incident|incident]], D-Squad and Colonel Gascon were assigned to the same [[platoon]].<ref name="Point" />

===Combating the Scourge===
[[File:DSquadMeetsAjax-DSquad4.png|thumb|right|300px|D-Squad meets with Ajax Sigma]]
Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]]<ref name="3 or 4 ABY Date" /> and during the [[Galactic Civil War]], R2-D2 briefly reunited with WAC-47, before reassembling the squad to combat the [[Scourge]], which had began to corrupt droids throughout the galaxy. The [[bounty hunter]]s [[4-LOM]] and [[IG-88B|IG-88]], and the [[assassin droid]]s [[BT-1]] and [[0-0-0]] all joined the new incarnation of the team.<ref name="Dark Droids: D-Squad 3" /> QT-KT also later reunited with R2-D2, and joined the squad as they traveled to [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Dark Droids: D-Squad 4">[[Dark Droids: D-Squad 4|''Dark Droids: D-Squad'' 4]]</ref> Joined by [[warrior-priest droid]] [[Ajax Sigma]], D-Squad [[Second attack on Scourge 01|attacked]] the Scourge's headquarters, the converted [[Star Destroyer]] [[Scourge 01|''Scourge'' 01]], where Sigma destroyed the Scourge's primary body, ending its threat and releasing anyone it had corrupted from its control. Sigma offered the members of D-Squad a place amongst his [[Second Revelation]] church, but they declined. 0-0-0 further warned him to not become a threat they would need to form D-Squad again to fight against.<ref name="Dark Droids 5">[[Dark Droids 5|''Dark Droids'' 5]]</ref>
