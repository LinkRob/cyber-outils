'''Cataract Base''', also known as '''Dawnfall Base''',<ref name="Hidden Empire 5">[[Hidden Empire 5|''Hidden Empire'' 5]]</ref> was a safe house used by the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Hidden Empire 2" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Hidden Empire 2|''Hidden Empire'' 2]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]], Crimson Dawn's [[Crime lord|leader]], had [[Cadeliah]] moved to the base<ref name="Hidden Empire 2" /> in order to keep her safe.<ref name="Crimson Reign 5">[[Crimson Reign 5|''Crimson Reign'' 5]]</ref> After the [[Battle against Crimson Dawn|destruction]] of the [[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|''Vermillion'']], it became the secret headquarters of Crimson Dawn,<ref name="Hidden Empire 3">[[Hidden Empire 3|''Hidden Empire'' 3]]</ref> and Qi'ra traveled to the base with the [[Orphans]], where she revealed to them the [[Dawn Fleet|fleet]] she had been building and told of her plans to unleash it against the [[Sith]].<ref name="Hidden Empire 2" />
