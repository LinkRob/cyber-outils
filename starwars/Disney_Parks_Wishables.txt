'''''Disney Parks Wishables''''' is a series of toys sold exclusively at [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] ShopDisney.com and the Disney Store, as well as various Disney theme parks, featuring small plush toys based on theme park attractions. Most are sold in "blind packs," including one of four different toys; which one is unknown until the bag is opened.<ref name="Shop Wishables">{{ShopDisney|url=uniquely-disney/parks-inspired/disney-parks-wishables|text=Disney Parks Wishables}}</ref>

In [[May]] of [[2021]], the ''[[Star Tours/Legends|Star Tours]]'' Attraction Series of Wishables was released.<ref name="DFB">{{WebCite|url=https://www.disneyfoodblog.com/2021/05/04/star-wars-wishables-arrive-in-disney-world|text=Star Wars Wishables Arrive in Disney World!|work=The Disney Food Blog|archivedate=20210517015210}}</ref> The blind pack included either [[C-3PO]], a [[stormtrooper]], a [[StarSpeeder 1000]], or the original ''Star Tours'' version of [[RX-24/Legends|RX-24]]. The [[StarSpeeder 3000]] was available as a limited "chase" variant. A single, separate [[R2-D2]] Wishable was also released.
