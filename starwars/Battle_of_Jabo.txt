The '''Battle of Jabo''' was an engagement fought at some point by<ref name="Revenge" /> approximately [[180 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> In the battle, the [[Twi'lek]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Skia Ro]] of the [[Jedi Order]] famously defeated<ref name="Revenge" /> the [[Sith]]<ref name="Sith" /> [[Darth Kozyr]], a fact that was known to Ro's [[Padawan|apprentice]] [[Christina Starspeeder]].<ref name="Revenge" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Jabo was mentioned in [[Jedi Academy: Revenge of the Sis|''Revenge of the Sis'']], the [[2019]] seventh entry in the ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Academy]]'' [[Canon|non-canon]] young-reader book series. The book was written by [[Jarrett J. Krosoczka]] and [[Amy Ignatow]].<ref name="Revenge" />
