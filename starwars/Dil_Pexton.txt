'''Dil Pexton''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Sullustan]]<ref name="The Snare" /> who served as an [[agent]] for the [[Graf family|Grafs]], a family of [[cartographer]]s in [[Wild Space]]. He helped the family establish deals for the maps and data they collected, and he assisted them in locating a remote, [[Graf-World|uncharted swampworld]] to explore.<ref name="The Escape" /> Dil Pexton owned a [[blaster pistol]] manufactured by [[SoroSuub Corporation]]. Compared to an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] weapon, [[Dil Pexton's blaster pistol|Pexton's blaster pistol]] was cheap, primitive, and short-ranged.<ref name="The Snare" />

Later, after the [[parent]]s were abducted by [[Captain]] [[Visler Aqorian Korda III]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the children [[Lina Graf]] and [[Milo Graf]] traveled to [[Thune]] to get his assistance. Once there, Pexton invited them to his store, where Lina joined them in order to give files which had been transmitted to their [[droid]] [[CR-8R|Crater]]. She was then captured by Korda, who had forced Pexton to assist the Empire or he would be arrested. They convinced Milo to join them at [[Merchant's Bridge]]. Pexton tried to subdue Crater, but he was [[stun]]ned and arrested by the Empire.<ref name="The Snare" />
