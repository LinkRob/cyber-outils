'''Farsigo Bay''' was a [[salt]]y bay of calm [[water]]s located south of [[Myrra]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], Akiva's residents&mdash;including the [[Wexley family]]&mdash;visited the bay on vacations to go [[fishing]], gather [[korlappii shell]]s, and ride in [[boat]]s.<ref name="Aftermath" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the [[junk dealer]] [[Temmin Wexley]] thought about the [[time]]s he and his [[parent]]s&mdash;the Akivans [[Norra Wexley|Norra]] and [[Brentin Lore Wexley|Brentin Wexley]]&mdash;had traveled to the bay for vacations and how peaceful the trips had been.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Farsigo Bay was mentioned in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
