The '''Detective Jedi''' was a bodily configuration equipped by the [[droid]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[J-3DI]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Noble Jedi|standard configuration]]. It was made to resemble [[SP-4 analysis droid]]s such as [[Russo-ISC]].<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Detective Jedi was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter J-3DI, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Detective Saber Design]].<ref name="Hunters" />
