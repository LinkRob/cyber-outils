The '''Darkknell system'''<ref name="TR" /> was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Visual Guide" /> and [[Western Reaches]] regions of [[the galaxy]],<ref name="WesternReaches" /> within the [[Grumani sector]].<ref name="THRA6" /> The [[star system]] was situated in grid square M-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]] and was connected the [[Hydian Way]] [[super-hyperroute]], with [[Darkknell–New Cov hyperlane|another]] [[hyperspace route]] linking it to the celestial body [[Sanrafsix system|Sanrafsix]].<ref name="TFABG" /> The [[star system]] contained the astronomical object [[Darkknell]].<ref name="Visual Guide" />

In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> former [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Pan Eyta]] [[Defense of the Restitution|attacked]] the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Prison ship|correctional vessel]] [[Lourna Dee (prison ship)|''Restitution'']] after discovering that his nemesis, Tempest Runner [[Lourna Dee]], was [[Prison|imprisoned]] onboard. During the attack, Dee launched fellow [[Prisoner|inmate]] [[Sestin Blin]], whom she had befriended, off of the ''Restitution'' in an [[escape pod]]. The battle ended with Dee [[Death|killing]] Eyta and reforming her [[Lourna Dee's Tempest|Tempest]], claiming the ''Restitution'' as her new [[flagship]]. Afterward, the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[patrol ship]] ''[[Moonseeker]]'' found the pod in the Darkknell system upon tracing its [[distress beacon]].<ref name="TR" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:The_High_Republic_Tempest_Runner_final_cover.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The audio drama ''Tempest Runner'' contained the first canonical appearance of the Darkknell system.]]
The Darkknell system was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map included in the [[January 29]], [[2016]] [[StarWars.com]] article "[[Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?|Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?]]" by [[Mark Newbold]].<ref name="TFA">{{SW|url=news/where-in-the-galaxy-are-the-worlds-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?|int=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?}}</ref> It made its first proper appearance in the [[2021]] audio drama ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="TR" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's Phase I.<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> The work was also the first to confirm that the location was in fact a [[star]] system.<ref name="TR" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Knel'char system|Darkknell system]] first appeared in the novella "[[Interlude at Darkknell]]," which was co-authored by [[Timothy Zahn]] and [[Michael A. Stackpole]] and published in the [[1999]] anthology ''[[Tales from the New Republic]]''.<ref name="NR">{{StoryCite|story=Interlude at Darkknell|book=Tales from the New Republic}}</ref> It was subsequently named the Knel'char [[Star system/Legends|system]] by [[The Knight Errant Gazetteer|''Essential Atlas'' Extra: The ''Knight Errant'' Gazetteer]], a [[2010]] StarWars.com article co-authored by [[Daniel Wallace]], [[Jason Fry]], and [[John Jackson Miller]] that served as a supplement to<ref name="GazetteerOriginal">{{SW|url=news/essential-atlas-extra-the-knight-errant-gazetter|text=Essential Atlas Extra: The Knight Errant Gazetteer|int=The Knight Errant Gazetteer}}</ref> the [[2009]] reference book<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> ''[[The Essential Atlas]]''.<ref name="GazetteerOriginal" /> The system was then given its first visual depiction in the [[Knight Errant: Deluge 3|third issue]] of the [[Star Wars: Knight Errant: Deluge|''Deluge'']] story arc of the [[Star Wars: Knight Errant (comic series)|''Star Wars: Knight Errant'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series, which was written by Miller, penciled by [[Iban Coello]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Deluge3">[[Knight Errant: Deluge 3|''Knight Errant: Deluge'' 3]]</ref> on [[October 19]], [[2011]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54312/star_wars_knight_errant_-_deluge_2011_3|text=Star Wars: Knight Errant - Deluge (2011) #3}}</ref>
