The '''DH-17 blaster rifle''' was a type of [[blaster rifle]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]] that was originally employed by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], being used by Rebel operatives such as [[Bistan]].<ref name="Rogue One Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

Following the [[Battle of Takodana]], several [[Resistance trooper|Resistance troopers]] carried refurbished DH-17 blaster rifles when surveying the site of the destroyed [[Takodana Castle|castle]].<ref name=":0">Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens</ref>  Shortly after, some of the surviving [[Resistance Army|ground forces]] of the [[Resistance]] used refurbished DH-17 blaster rifles while fighting in the trenches against the [[First Order]] during the [[Battle of Crait]].<ref name="TLJ" /> The refurbished rifles had hand-calibrated [[macroscope]]s and were [[Color|black]] and silver in color.<ref name="TLJVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
DH-17 blaster rifles first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens]]<ref name=":0" />, which was released on [[December 18]], [[2015]]. The weapons were not named in the film, but received identification in ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="Rogue One Guide" /> and released in conjunction with the film. A weapon named [[DH-17 blaster rifle/Legends|DH-17 blaster rifle]] with a noticeably different appearance previously appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] ''[[Star Wars: Battlefront]]'' series.<ref name="Battlefront guide">[[Star Wars Battlefront: Prima Official Game Guide|''Star Wars Battlefront'': Prima Official Game Guide]]</ref><ref name="Battlefront II guide">[[Star Wars Battlefront II: Prima Official Game Guide|''Star Wars Battlefront II'': Prima Official Game Guide]]</ref>
