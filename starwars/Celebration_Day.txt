'''Celebration Day''' was an event organized<ref name="LivingForce" /> in [[33 BBY]]<ref name="LivingForceDate" /> by the [[Jedi High Council]] of the [[Jedi Order]]&mdash;with assistance from [[Gala Key Arena]] employee [[Lyal Lunn]] and ''[[Remember Kwenn]]'' [[holofeed]] [[engineer]] [[Morna]]&mdash;on the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]]. The event was organized to celebrate the bicentennial of Kwenn's [[Grand Renewal]] project, which had been carried out by the Order<ref name="LivingForce" /> in [[233 BBY]].<ref name="LivingForceDate" />

In addition to carrying out the bicentennial celebration of Kwenn's [[Grand Renewal]] project, Celebration Day was meant to restore trust and hope in the [[Jedi]] who have been caught up in [[The galaxy|galactic]] affairs rather than helping individual planets and their people, including Kwenn. Kwenn, like many other planets in the [[The Slice]], had continuously become more affected by piracy after the Jedi visited the planet less and less over the [[Standard year|years]] after the Grand Renewal project, which caused many people to think poorly of the Jedi.<ref name="LivingForce" />
