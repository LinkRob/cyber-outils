'''Cedraa's palace''' was a palace owned by the [[Hutt]] [[Cedraa]] of the [[Hutt Clan]] on<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Tarnoonga system]]</ref> terrestrial astronomical object [[Tarnoonga]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> At one point by [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> a [[birthday]] celebration was held for Cedraa at his palace, which his cousin [[Churo]] the Hutt attended. During the event, individuals gathered round [[hologame]] tables and Churo noted it was particularly loud.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> while at a [[Nihil]] [[Unidentified factory (Mynos III)|factory]] on the Outer Rim [[planet]] [[Mynos III]], Churo heard Nihil [[pirate]]s break out in raucous laughter and gather round hologame tables. The Hutt then mentally compared it to his cousin's party, noting that the Nihil had been louder.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cedraa's palace was mentioned in the [[2024]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=SWCE 2023: 9 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref>
