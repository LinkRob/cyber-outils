The '''Engai system''' was a [[star system]] that [[Jedi master (feudal)|Jedi master]] [[Numoda]] was sent to by [[Jedi lord (feudal)|Jedi lord]] [[Hanrai]] to root out [[smuggler]]s. The [[Jedi apprentice (feudal)|Jedi apprentice]] [[Yuehiro]] and other apprentices in [[Hanrai's clan|Hanrai's Jedi clan]] later heard there was a complication, after which the [[Jedi clan|clan's]] masters refused to speak about the situation. Yuehiro came to believe that Numoda was [[Death|dead]], although his [[bone]]s were not returned to Hanrai's shrine as they should have been.<ref name="Ronin" />

[[Standard week|Weeks]] after Numoda was sent to the Engai system, Yuehiro snuck into [[Hanrai's library]] in his [[Hanrai's home estate|home estate]] on the astronomical object [[Watoru]] to read records about a [[Unidentified witch|Sith witch]] rumored to have been [[Sith demon|resurrecting]] the dead, causing battlefields to leave no bodies. After Yuehiro was caught, he explained to Hanrai his belief that Numoda had died after being sent to the Engai system and said he hoped to learn about the missing bodies of Numoda and other [[Jedi]] by researching the [[witch]].<ref name="Ronin" />
