The '''Delta Wing trilogy''' was a trilogy of [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] adventures for the [[Wizards of the Coast]] role-playing game, to be released through the [[RPGA]]. The adventures were to be named ''On a Wing and a Prayer'', ''A Bird in the Hand'', and ''The Pirates' Nest''. While the adventures were written and play-tested, they were never released.
