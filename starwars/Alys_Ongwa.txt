'''Alys "Crash" Ongwa''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Corellian]]<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> [[Reproduction|born]] around [[247 BBY]]<ref name="Birth date" /> who lived on [[Corellia]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. She grew up in the [[bodyguard]]ing business, eventually coming to lead the [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection]] agency after inheriting the business from her mother, [[Baynoo Ongwa]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> Following the [[Valo Atrocity]],<ref name="THR Show Ep6">{{HighRepublicShow|-ejhPPnMQMc|Trail of Shadows, a Tale From Last Call, and More!|sw_url=video/trail-of-shadows-a-tale-from-last-call-and-more}}</ref> Ongwa and her crew were tasked with protecting [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during a visit to Corellia.<ref name="THRA Annual 2021" /> At the time, Ongwa was sixteen [[Standard year|years]] old, and was accompanied by the [[droid]]<ref name="THR Show Ep6" /> [[10-K8]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> In the year following the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|Fall of]] [[Starlight Beacon]], Ongwa and her crew traveled the [[galaxy]] to hunt down anyone involved in the [[Nihil]]'s [[infiltration on Corellia]].<ref name="Crash Landing">''[[The High Republic Adventures: Crash Landing]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Alys Ongwa was created by [[Daniel José Older]] for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' publishing initiative.<ref name="THR Show Ep6" /> Older first hinted at the character through her nickname of Crash in an episode of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Show]]'' on [[July 28]], [[2021]],<ref name="THR Show EP4">{{HighRepublicShow|xqBuS4EZ0qE|High Republic Jedi Council Deep Dive, a Lesson on Wayseekers, and More!|sw_url=video/high-republic-jedi-council-deep-dive-a-lesson-on-wayseekers-and-more}}</ref> while Ongwa was formally revealed in a later episode of the show on [[November 17]].<ref name="THR Show Ep6" /> Ongwa debuted in "[[Crash and the Crew Do What They Do]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story written by Older, illustrated by [[Jesse Lonergan]], and published in [[The High Republic Adventures Annual 2021|''The High Republic Adventures Annual'' 2021]] by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="THRA Annual 2021" /> on [[December 15]], 2021.<ref name="Comixology">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Annual-2021/digital-comic/969157|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Annual 2021}}</ref>

Older created the character as a tribute to writer [[Alyssa Wong]], [[Tuckerization|tuckerizing]] Wong's name.<ref name="Twitter Tribute">{{Twitter|djolder|status/1461084533806276612|[[Daniel José Older]]|quote=My tribute to @crashwong is ALIVE AND IN THE WORLD! amazing concept art by @jesselonergans|archivedate=20211126032741}}</ref> Having asked them what role they would like to play within the ''[[Star Wars]]'' universe, Older based Ongwa and her occupation on Wong's answer.<ref name="Twitter Role">{{Twitter|djolder|status/1461392098016509956|[[Daniel José Older]]|quote=in a round about sort of way I asked @crashwong if [they were] in SW what role would [they] play and [they] said [they]'d be the stressed out chaotic manager of an assassin squad (or something like that). Switched assassin to bodyguard and thus: Crash. Art by @jesselonergans|archivedate=20211126032741}}</ref> Ongwa's pink hair was a request from Wong.<ref name="Twitter Pink">{{Twitter|djolder|status/1461392201347444748|[[Daniel José Older]]|quote=[they] requested the pink hair &lt;3|archivedate=20211126033007}}</ref>
