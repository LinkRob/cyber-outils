The '''First Temple''',<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> also known as the '''Jedi Praxeum''',<ref name="Nexus" /> was located on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Ossus]], operated by the [[Jedi Order]]. The [[Jedi temple|temple]] had mosaic floors that were salvaged and used within the Order's newer [[Imperial Palace|headquarters]] on the [[Galaxy|galactic]] [[capital]] world of [[Coruscant]].<ref name="CL" /> The Ossus temple was once believed to be the first Jedi Temple where the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Great Tree (Coruscant)|Great Tree]] originally stood.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==History==
According to the quasi-historical [[Qel-Droma Epics]], the praxeum was built to house [[Jedi pilgrims]] seeking out the [[Eye of Ashlanae]]. It became a source of knowledge and assistance for those Jedi defending the oppressed throughout the Outer Rim. For a time, the praxeum contained the largest single collection of Jedi knowledge in the form of scrolls, [[holocron]]s, and other artifacts. [[Jedi Master]] [[Odan-Urr]] received his training there under Jedi Master [[Ooroo]], who managed the facility until his death during the [[Great Hyperspace War]]. The training of [[Padawan|Jedi Padawans]] continued at the praxeum for another thousand years, until the [[Cron Supernova]] destroyed the [[Knossa]] city.<ref name="Nexus" /> At some point, the temple's mosaic floors were salvaged and used within the Order's newer [[Imperial Palace|headquarters]] on the [[Galaxy|galactic]] [[capital]] world of [[Coruscant]].<ref name="CL" />

==Layout==
According to the Qel-Droma Epics, the praxeum was a five-tiered ziggurat, each tier dedicated to a [[Jedi Code|value of the Jedi]]. The top tier, Knowledge, was where the most treasured artifacts and tomes were stored. Beneath it was the tier of Harmony, which contained the Masters' council chamber, diplomatic offices, and meditation rooms. The Serenity tier was reserved for dormitories, supplemented by meditative balconies, pools, and gardens. The Peace level was where Jedi trained for combat. Finally, the ground level represented [[the Force]], and was reserved exclusively for training students to manipulate the mystical energy field.<ref name="Nexus" />
