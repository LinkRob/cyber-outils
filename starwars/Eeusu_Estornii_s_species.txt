This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="ROTS" /> [[Senator]] [[Eeusu Estornii]] was a member of this species.<ref name="SWVE" />

==Biology and appearance==
This [[humanoid]] [[species]] was distinguished primarily by their [[Color|pale]] skin and large black [[eye]]s.<ref name="SWVE" />

==Species in the galaxy==
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], including Estornii's appearance, to [[19 BBY]].</ref> a member of this species, [[Eeusu Estornii]],<ref name="SWVE" /> was among the [[senator]]s within the procession at [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s [[Padmé Amidala's funeral|funeral]] on Amidala's [[homeworld]], the [[planet]] [[Naboo]].<ref name="ROTS" />

==Behind the scenes==
This species first appeared in the [[2005]] film {{Film|III}}, directed by [[George Lucas]].<ref name="ROTS" /> They were identified as the [[Zeuol]] within the [[Star Wars Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Zeuol">{{DB|character|eeusuestornii|Estornii, Eesu}}</ref>
