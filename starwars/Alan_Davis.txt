'''Alan Davis''' (born [[June 18]], [[1950s#1956|1956]]) is a British comic book illustrator who has worked on numerous titles both in the U.K. and in the U.S., most notably [[Marvel Comics]]' ''Uncanny X-Men'' with [[Wikipedia:Chris Claremont|Chris Claremont]], as well as ''Excalibur'', ''Captain Britain'' and ''Clan Destine''. His ''Star Wars'' work includes the comics ''[[Dark Knight's Devilry]]'' and ''[[Rust Never Sleeps]]''.{{Mediacat|imagecat=images by Alan Davis}}
