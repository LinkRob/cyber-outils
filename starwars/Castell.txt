'''Castell''',<ref name="Lando 2" /> also known as '''Gossam''',<ref name="Email">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-08.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-08}}</ref> was a [[planet]] located in the [[Castell system]]<ref name="Lando 2" /> of the [[Colonies]],<ref name="BMF" /> situated along the [[Perlemian Trade Route]].<ref name="TFABG" /> The [[Commerce Guild]] had interests on Castell and their [[Presidente]] [[Shu Mai]] hailed from the planet. Castell sided with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] during which it was defended by [[Diamond-class cruiser|''Diamond''-class cruisers]], [[Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class Commerce Guild destroyers]], as well as [[Commerce Guild bomber|Commerce Guild Bombers]] and [[Commerce Guild corvette]]s.<ref name="Rots" /> By the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Felucia (Outer Rim Sieges)|Battle of Felucia]] during the [[Clone Wars]] final [[Standard year|year]], Castell had been [[Capture of Castell|captured]] by [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Republic Military|forces]].<ref name="Complete Locations" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="LPOA Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]].</ref> the [[Alderaanian]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] worked with several of her peers in the Imperial [[Apprentice Legislature]] to levy sanctions against the planet [[Lolet]]. Organa then considered the fates of other planets already suffering from Imperial subjugation, including Gossam, and felt guilty that Lolet's fate would likely be the same.<ref name="LPOA">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], it was a colony world of the [[Galactic Empire]] under [[Occupation of Castell|Imperial occupation]]. It was ruled by its [[Castell's sector|sector's]] [[Governor|Imperial governor]], [[Moff]] [[Ssaria]].<ref name="Lando 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Castell was first mentioned in "[[Galactic Power Brokers]]," a ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' article written by [[J.D. Wiker]] and published in the [[Star Wars Gamer 10|tenth issue]] of ''[[Star Wars Gamer]]'' magazine in [[2002]].<ref name="Galactic Power Brokers">{{GamerCite|10|Galactic Power Brokers}}</ref> The [[Planet|world]] was confirmed to be [[canon]] by the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'',<ref name="Ultimate" /> which was published in [[2015]], and makes its first canonical appearance in the [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Lando (comic series)|''Star Wars: Lando'']].<ref name="Lando 1" />
