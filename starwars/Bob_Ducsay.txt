'''Bob Ducsay''' is a [[Wikipedia:Film editor|film editor]] who worked with the production team for the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].

The Last Jedi character [[Dobbu Scay]]'s name is an anagram for Ducsay's.<ref>{{StarWarsShow|p09c0MXDxto|Star Wars: The Last Jedi Secrets Explained}}</ref>
