'''''Classic Podracer''''' was a sports magazine about [[podracing]]. [[Ezra Bridger]] used the front page of one issue of ''Classic Podracer'' as a [[Painting|poster]] in his room aboard the ''[[Ghost]]''. It featured a picture of [[Ben Quadinaros]], set against a backdrop of a flying [[podracer]]. The all-[[Aurebesh]] text on the front page included the words "INSIDE" and "QUADINAROS".<ref name="Steps" /> The cover looked similar to the poster ''[[The Sport's Greatest Rivalry]]''.<ref name="RoC">{{TCW|The Rise of Clovis}}</ref>
