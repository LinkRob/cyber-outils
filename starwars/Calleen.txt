'''Calleen''' was the [[Altiri]] [[Deity|goddess]] of wisdom. The Altiri [[Jedi Master]] [[Lene Kostana]] named her second [[convor]], [[Calleen (convor)|Calleen]], after the goddess.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> Between around [[70 BBY]] and [[68 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Lene Kostana]]'s request to [[Dooku]] happened after [[Rael Averross]] was [[Knighting Ceremony|knighted]] and before [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] became Dooku's [[Padawan]], both dated to around [[70 BBY]] and [[68 BBY]] respectively by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Kostana asked fellow Jedi Master [[Dooku]] to look after her convor and told him the convor's name. Dooku replied that Calleen was also the name of the goddess, impressing Kostana with his knowledge.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

Dooku later told his [[House Serenno|sister]], [[Jenza]], about the events in a [[holo-letter]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="DJL-JournalsDate">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Dooku gave several [[Datacard|data disks]] containing his messages to Jenza to his recently recruited [[Sith assassin]]&mdash;[[Asajj Ventress]]&mdash;for her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|investigation]] into his [[Gender|sister's]] location, and she listened to the holo-disk that mentioned Calleen.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
Calleen was mentioned in a flashback sequence in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin-Download">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-cavan-scott/9780593102817|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> The spelling of Calleen was confirmed by the audio drama's [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script release]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> on [[October 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Penguin-Download" />
