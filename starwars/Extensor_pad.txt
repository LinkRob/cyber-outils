An '''extensor pad''' was a type of technology that could recall a weapon to a user's hand.<ref name="EMPIRE_END">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

==Characteristics==
Built into the fingertips of a user's gloves, extensor pads buzzed when active, and could recall a weapon tens of meters away back to a user's hand. The [[bounty hunter]] [[Mercurial Swift]] utilized extensor pads built into his gloves to recall his [[Concussive baton]]s either back to his hands or into his enemies.<ref name="EMPIRE_END" />
