In [[19 BBY]], a '''bombing''' took place on the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] of the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] during a mass mourning procession for the [[Jedi Order]] after its [[Great Jedi Purge|destruction]] by the [[Galactic Empire]].

==History==
[[File:KilliToshdor-RO.png|thumb|250px|Pilgrims in the Holy City in [[1 BBY]]]]
In the wake of the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Great Jedi Purge|eradication]] and the [[Declaration of a New Order]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="RotS">{{Film|III}}</ref> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a procession of mourners gathered in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] of the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] to grieve the fallen [[Jedi]]. Cries mourning Jedi [[Saesee Tiin]], [[Stass Allie]], [[O'ra've]] and [[Caladastorous]] were heard. Among the attendees was [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]], traveling incognito in [[Ztenortha pilgrim]] clothing, and [[Unidentified Tarsunt admiral|a Tarsunt admiral]] acquainted with Organa.<ref name="MoF" />

Amidst the somber vigil, in which mourners pushed the massive rotating disk known as the [[Stone of First Tears]] in ritual mourning, a bomb detonated within the city, triggering chaos and widespread panic. The detonation caused a stampede, in which several individuals were crushed as they attempted to flee through narrow streets. Later, news feeds described the perpetrator as aligned ideologically with the Imperial regime, wishing to eliminate the Jedi and their supporters.<ref name="MoF" />

==Behind the scenes==
The bombing in the Holy City was depicted in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" />
