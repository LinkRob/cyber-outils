'''Abrion Bridge''' was a bridge located on the [[planet]] [[Abrion Major]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" /> Around [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The [[Obi-Wan 3|''Obi-Wan'' 3]] flashbacks take place shortly after the [[Defense of Rishi Station]], which appears in "[[Rookies (episode)|Rookies]]." {{TCWcanondates|105}} Therefore ''Obi-Wan'' 3, including the [[Battle of Abrion Bridge]], also takes place around 22 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> a [[Battle of Abrion Bridge|battle]] of the [[Clone Wars]] took place between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] on the bridge, which resulted in a costly Republic victory.<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />
