The '''Chief Inspector's Office''' was the workplace of the [[Chief Inspector]] in charge of the [[Pre-Mor Security Inspection team]]. By [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|101}}</ref> [[Hyne]] was the Chief Inspector in charge. Around this time, Hyne met with [[Deputy Inspector]] [[Syril Karn]] as the former was preparing to attend an [[Imperial Regional Command]] review to report on crime rates. During their conversation, they discussed the [[Death|murder]] of two [[Tac-Corpo|Corpos]], [[Kravas Drezzer]] and [[Verlo Skiff]].<ref name="Kassa (episode)">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> After the [[Ambush on Ferrix]], the [[Corporate Security Headquarters]], including the Chief Inspector's office were taken over by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Aldhani (episode)">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref>
