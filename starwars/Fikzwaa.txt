'''Fikzwaa''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in the [[Fikzwaa system]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing" /> Situated at the coordinates N-14 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> it was [[Homeworld|home]] to a [[Unidentified Fikzwaa species|diminutive species]] whose population numbered multiple billion. It had been under the control of the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], but the natives drove the Empire out following the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>

In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VictorysPriceDate}} Furthermore, since the mention of the Fikzwaa in ''Victory's Price'' occurs prior to the Battle of Jakku, it must also have taken place that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] carried out a string of [[Death|genocides]] against worlds that had not remained loyal to that government. Shortly after the [[Destruction of Fedovoi End|devastation]] of the [[planet]] [[Fedovoi End]], the 204th [[engineer]] [[Agias Rikton]] heard from other personnel that the next target was possibly Fikzwaa. When he discussed the possibility with the [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Yrica Quell]], he speculated that a base might have been established on the celestial body after the Empire's presence there had ended.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fikzwaa was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''. Written by [[Alexander Freed]], the novel was the third volume of the ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
