'''Beke Mon'z''' was an [[Ishi Tib]] [[Gender|male]] operative of the [[New Republic Security Bureau]]. Working for [[Officer]] [[Sela Trune]], Mon'z infiltrated the [[smuggler]] gang known as the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] as a [[pilot]]. After the Spice Runners undertook a [[Heist on Kellgar Seven|heist]], they discovered that Mon'z had been siphoning part of the reward. Having learned that Mon'z was a traitor&mdash;but unaware of his allegiance to the New Republic&mdash;the Spice Runners [[Death|killed]] him on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]], subsequently becoming stranded until they recruited the resident [[Poe Dameron]] to be their pilot.

==Biography==
{{Quote|He was feeding us intel. Helping me get a better picture of who the Spice Runners are. But he was sloppy. My only hope is they think he was just a greedy mercenary instead of a double agent.|Sela Trune|<ref name="Free Fall" />}}
During the [[New Republic Era|time]] of the [[New Republic]], Beke Mon'z served as an operative in the [[New Republic Security Bureau]]. [[Gender|He]] worked for [[Officer]] [[Sela Trune]], who had a vendetta against the [[smuggler]] gang known as the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]]. Trune sent Mon'z to infiltrate the Spice Runners, and the New Republic agent did so successfully, acting as the [[pilot]] of a Spice Runner group led by [[Vigilch]], flying the [[XS stock light freighter]] ''[[Ragged Claw]]''.<ref name="Free Fall" />

While part of the Spice Runners, Mon'z fed intel on the gang to Trune.<ref name="Free Fall" /> In [[18 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> after the Spice Runners pulled off a [[Heist on Kellgar Seven|heist]] on the astronomical object [[Kellgar Seven]], gaining a large reward as a result, Mon'z skimmed from the score, passing portions of it to both the New Republic and the Spice Runners' competitors. His Spice Runner accomplices discovered that Mon'z had stolen from them; however, they were unaware that he was an agent of the New Republic. Following the heist, the group escaped to the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]], where they [[Death|killed]] Mon'z for his treachery.<ref name="Free Fall" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|Reports from the dock. They've found a body.|An officer reports Mon'z's death to Sela Trune|<ref name="Free Fall" />}}
After Trune was summoned to Yavin 4 due to reports of potential Spice Runner activity on the moon, the body of Mon'z was discovered at the [[Yavin 4 docks|docks]] of the [[Wetyin's Colony]] settlement. Without a pilot for the ''Ragged Claw'', the Spice Runners were left stranded on Yavin 4, though they managed to escape with the help of the young resident and aspiring pilot [[Poe Dameron]]. Some [[time]] after the death of her operative, Trune remained haunted by visions of Mon'z's body, though was still unsure whether the Spice Runners had deduced his true purpose.<ref name="Free Fall" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I made my decision, I stand by it. He was taking from us. That sneaky little Ishi Tib&mdash;''"<br />"''That sneaky little Ishi Tib was our pilot.|Spice Runners Vigilch and [[Marinda Gan]]|<ref name="Free Fall" />}}
An [[Ishi Tib]] male, Beke Mon'z had [[Color|green]] skin and a small figure. Following his death, his work was identified as sloppy by Trune, while Vigilch described the Ishi Tib as being sneaky.<ref name="Free Fall" />

==Equipment==
While infiltrating the Spice Runners, Mon'z piloted the XS stock light freighter ''Ragged Claw''.<ref name="Free Fall" />
