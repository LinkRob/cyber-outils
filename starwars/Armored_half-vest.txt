The '''armored half-vest''' was an armored vest produced by [[Koromondain]] and sold for 500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. The vest was designed to protect the wearer from ambushes, and was known to its critics as "[[bounty hunter]] insurance." Although the armor's durability decreased as it absorbed damage, its low cost meant that is was used regularly by bounty hunters, paramilitary organizations, and security forces.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
The armored half-vest mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
