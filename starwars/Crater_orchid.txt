A '''crater orchid''' was a type of [[orchid]] with petals.<ref name="Battle Scars" /> Between [[12 BBY]] and [[11 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{BattleScarsDate}}</ref> the [[Dathomirian]] [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Merrin]] removed the [[helmet]] of the [[Keshiri]] [[Chellwinark Frethylrin]], who had disguised herself as an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]] while asking Merrin for help. Merrin was surprised to see that Frethylrin was not [[human]], and while mentally noting her features, compared the freckles across her nose to crater orchid petals.<ref name="Battle Scars" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crater orchids were mentioned in the [[2023]] novel ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Battle Scars]]'', written by [[Sam Maggs]].<ref name="Battle Scars" />
