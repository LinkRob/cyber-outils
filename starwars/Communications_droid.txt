'''Communication droids''' were a type of [[droid]] primarily involved in communications. They were a major aspect of communication during the era of the [[High Republic Era|High Republic]], being used by the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] as well as many other groups. They would project images transmitted to them onto walls dedicated to their use known as [[commwall]]s. A comms droid was used by [[Admiral]] [[Pevel Kronara]] to communicate with [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] during the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]].<ref name="LotJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Imperial Intelligence]] communications hub [[Sector Plexus]] used a vast network of communications droids.<ref name="DoR" /> [[PZ-4CO]], a [[Serv-O-Droid, Inc.]] [[protocol droid]] who served the [[Resistance]] during their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] with the [[First Order]] was a type of communications droid.<ref name="CEUE" /> The [[Color|black]], spherical, hovering [[droid]] [[Iris (droid)|Iris]], who served the First Order [[Captain]] [[Archex|Cardinal]] was a communications droid. [[Gender|She]] was capable of controlling video [[camera]]s and internal [[sensor]]s on board the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Absolution]]'' as well as giving basic medical information about nearby individuals.<ref name="Phasma" />
