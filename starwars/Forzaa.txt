'''Forzaa''' was a [[Zeltron]] [[Gender|female]] who worked as a [[fortune teller]] in the [[Coruscant Underworld|lower levels]] of [[Coruscant]]. When searching for a [[Lost Museum|lost museum]] on [[Level 4]], [[Amilyn Holdo]] visited Forzaa with [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], and the fortune teller guided them in the right direction. She seemed to have abilities that can be found in force-sensitives, such as knowing things about people without speaking to them. She was also in possession of what she called the "[[Liga crystal]]," an object which assisted greatly in Leia and Holdo's expedition.<ref name="The Right Wrong Turn" />
