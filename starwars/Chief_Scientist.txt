'''Chief Scientist'''<ref name="Collapse of the Republic">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> was a title held by the [[Kaminoan]] [[scientist]] [[Ko Sai]] during the production of [[Cloning|clones]] on [[Kamino]] for the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Grand Army Encyclopedia" /> During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|214}}</ref> the title was also used by [[Doctor]] [[Royce Hemlock]] as he led the [[Advanced Science Division]] in pursuing the [[Imperial cloning program|cloning program]] under the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Tipping Point" /> [[Nala Se]] also served as a Chief Scientist under Hemlock, and was later supplanted by Doctor [[Emerie Karr]].<ref name="Identity Crisis">{{TBB|Identity Crisis}}</ref>
