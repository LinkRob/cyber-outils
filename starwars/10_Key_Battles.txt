"'''10 Key Battles'''" is an article featured in [[Star Wars Insider 133|''Star Wars Insider'' 133]] by [[Titan Magazines]]. It was written by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Paul R. Urquhart]] to go along with ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''. The article lists the top ten key battles to take place in [[Star Wars Legends]], including the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]], the [[Battle of Endor/Legends|Battle of Endor]], the [[Battle of Ebaq]], the [[Battle of Uquine]], the [[First Battle of Geonosis/Legends|Battle of Geonosis]], the [[Battle of Coruscant/Legends|Battle of Coruscant]], the [[Battle of Malachor V]], the [[Ruusan campaign|Battle of Ruusan]], the [[Battle of Bilbringi (9 ABY)|Battle of Bilbringi]], and the [[Second Battle of Fondor]].
