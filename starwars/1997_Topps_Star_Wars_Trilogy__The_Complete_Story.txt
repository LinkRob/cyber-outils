'''1997 Topps ''Star Wars'' Trilogy: The Complete Story''' is a trading card set covering the events of the [[original trilogy]]. The set was released by [[Topps]] in [[February]] [[1997]] to coincide with ''[[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition]]'', and was released simultaneously with the ''[[1997 Topps Star Wars Special Edition Widevision|Star Wars Special Edition]]'' card set.<ref name="SWGM 10" /> The cards included the "Widevision" logo as they were in the same format as earlier Widevision sets.<ref name="Widevision book" />
