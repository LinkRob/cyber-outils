'''B8G-H0R''' was a [[B8G labor droid]] that was [[Slavery|forced to work]] in the [[Spice mines of Kessel|spice mines]] owned by the criminal [[Pyke Syndicate]] on the [[planet]] [[Kessel]]. In the [[Standard year|year]] [[10 BBY]], he was involved in a [[Raid on Kessel|slave uprising]] on Kessel caused by a [[Beckett's gang|group]] of outlaws trying to [[Thief|steal]] [[coaxium]] [[fuel]] from the [[Pyke]]s. During the uprising, B8G-H0R was part of a mob that charged out of the [[Mining|mine]] tunnels and onto a landing pad, where the rioters fought with their captors.

==Biography==
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="SoloOG" /> the [[B8G labor droid]]<ref name="Kids" /> B8G-H0R was [[Slavery|forced to work]] in the criminal [[Pyke Syndicate]]'s [[Spice mines of Kessel|spice mines]] on the [[planet]] [[Kessel]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> He shared his work in the mine with other [[labor droid]]s including [[CC-4M]] and [[J3-55]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events on [[Kessel]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[10 BBY]].</ref> a [[Beckett's gang|group]] of outlaws affiliated with the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate arrived on Kessel in order to [[Raid on Kessel|try]] and [[Thief|steal]] [[coaxium]] from the [[Pyke]]s. While in disguise, several members of the group reached the [[Mining|mine's]] control room, where they inadvertently caused the beginning of a slave revolt by freeing the [[droid]] [[DD-BD]] from his [[restraining bolt]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

[[File:Kessel_Slave_Charge.png|thumb|left|250px|B8G-H0R took part in a slave uprising on Kessel.]]
The freed droid began removing the bolts of other droid slaves, who in turn did the same or freed organic slaves from their shackles. As both droid and organic slaves rose up against the Pykes, B8G-H0R hurried through one of the mine's corridors past [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]], two members of the group of outlaws who were headed to the vault containing the coaxium.<ref name="Solo" />

B8G-H0R later was part of a mob of slaves that charged out of the mine tunnels and onto a landing pad on the planet surface, where the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', a [[YT-1300 light freighter]] being used by the outlaws, was parked. Heavy fighting broke out in the area, with both the slaves and the outlaws attacking the Pykes. During the conflict, B8G-H0R initially stood near one of the area's pools of [[kessoline]], grappling with a [[Pyke sentinel]] over an [[Electrostaff|electrostave]]. The droid later moved closer to the mine's entrance again when the outlaw droid [[L3-37]] was [[Death|destroyed]] by the Pykes.<ref name="Solo" />

==Characteristics==
B8G-H0R was a bipedal, [[Color|tan-colored]] labor droid with a large [[gas]]-burning exhaust vent in his torso.<ref name="SoloOG" /> Seemingly [[fuel]]ed by gas consumption, the droid was [[humanoid]] in shape other then a few unusual features like its torso [[radiator]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> It had a head with two black [[sensor]]s, one larger than the other, and an [[antenna]] that protruded from its back,<ref name="SoloOG" /> which the droid used to communicate with its environment by transmitting and receiving messages.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
B8G-H0R first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Solo" /> and was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SoloOG" />
