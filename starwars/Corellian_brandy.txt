'''Corellian brandy''' was a [[liquor]] which was deep [[Color|golden-brown]] in color. It was served in the [[Zero Angle]], a [[Cantina|bar]] in the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Bright Jewel Oversector]] [[Bright Jewel Oversector Flight Base|Flight Base]] on the [[moon]] [[Axxila III]]. Shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Commander]] [[Weller]] entered the bar and ordered the brandy, asking for the good stuff. The barkeeper [[Florn]] poured one glass for Weller and one for himself, and the pair then drank the liquor down in one go, finishing simultaneously.<ref name="LCATZA" /> The [[gangster]] [[Dapp|"Happy" Dapp]] was known to drink [[Corellia]]n [[brandy]] at the [[time]] of the [[Iron Blockade]].<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref>

Corellian brandy was also used in the making of mixed [[alcohol]]ic beverages. The brandy could be mixed with [[Ipellria firewater]] in a centrifuge to create a drink known as a [[Nova Blaster]]. This beverage was served at [[Chalmun]]'s [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina|Cantina]] in [[Tatooine]]'s [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] and at one point asking the [[bartender]] for a Nova Blaster with the Corellian brandy substituted with [[Savareen]] [[Savareen brandy|brandy]] was used to gain access to the [[cantina]]'s stock of [[black market]] weaponry.<ref name="Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the liquor's first appearance was in "[[Last Call at the Zero Angle]]," a short story written by [[Jason Fry]] and published in the [[Star Wars Insider 156|156th issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine,<ref name="LCATZA" /> which released on [[March 10]], [[2015]].<ref name="Titan Magazines">{{TitanMagazines|url=t/star-wars-insider/us/156|text=Star Wars Insider Issue #156 @ Titan Magazines}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, Corellian brandy was first introduced in the [[1992]] novel ''[[Dark Force Rising]]'' by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="DFR">''[[Dark Force Rising]]''</ref>
