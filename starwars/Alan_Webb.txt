'''Alan Norton Fletcher Webb''' was an [[Wikipedia:English|English]] stage and film actor. He was best known for his roles in [[Wikipedia:Chimes at Midnight|''Chimes at Midnight'']], [[Wikipedia:King Lear (1971 British film)|''King Lear'']] and [[Wikipedia:The First Great Train Robbery|''The First Great Train Robbery'']]. Webb was originally cast as [[Darth Sidious|Emperor Palpatine]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. However, Webb bowed out the role after getting the [[Wikipedia:Flu|flu]], leading director [[Richard Marquand]] to cast [[Ian McDiarmid]] instead, who had no problems with the costumes and heavy makeup unlike Webb.<ref name="ROTJ Behind the scenes">''[[The Making of Return of the Jedi]]''</ref>
