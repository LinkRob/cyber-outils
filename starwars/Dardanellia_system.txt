The '''Dardanellia system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square N-14 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Dardanellian locust]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />
