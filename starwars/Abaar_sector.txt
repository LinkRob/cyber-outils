The '''Abaar sector''' was a [[sector]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. Situated iIn grid square K-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it contained the [[Abaarian system]] and the [[Paklarn system]]. The Paklarn [[Star system|system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> contained the [[planet]] [[Paklarn]], where the [[smuggler]] [[Nevil Cygni]] set up an [[Iridium (element)|iridium]] smuggling operation using [[grist mollusk]] shells during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> In [[11 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of chapter 12 of [[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']], in which Paklarn is mentioned, to [[11 BBY]].</ref> [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] uncovered the operation and Cygni's [[Gender|men]] were arrested.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Abaar sector was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Abaar sector/Legends|sector]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the StarWars.com [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Fry.<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> In [[2011]], the sector was depicted on a map designed by [[Modi]] and published in the Online Companion feature "[https://web.archive.org/web/20120117150257/https://www.starwars.com/atlas/midrim/index.html Essential Atlas Extra: Sectors of the Mid Rim]."<ref name="MidRimOriginal">{{SWArchive|url=atlas/midrim/index.html|text=Essential Atlas Extra: Sectors of the Mid Rim}}</ref>
