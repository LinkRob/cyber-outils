The '''Clan Wren Mandalorians Unit Expansion''' ('''SWQ56''') is an expansion to the miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]].'' An updated version of the earlier [[Clan Wren Unit Expansion]], it will be released on [[June 19]], [[2026]].

==Publisher summary==
''An ancient Mandalorian clan with ties to House Vizsla, Clan Wren's warriors are legendary for their battlefield prowess. Wielding deadly weapons with expert precision, Clan Wren unites with the Rebellion, fighting for freedom and the liberation of Mandalore. This expansion for Star Wars: Legion contains 7 miniatures, enough for 1 Special Forces unit as well as cards to support gameplay.''

*''MANDALORIAN SPECIAL FORCES UNIT: Add Clan Wren warriors to your Rebel Alliance army, legendary Mandalorian fighters known for precision combat and aerial mobility.
*''7 HIGHLY DETAILED MINIATURES: Includes seven Mandalorian miniatures with dynamic poses to create a powerful Special Forces unit for Star Wars: Legion.
*''FLIGHT STANDS FOR AERIAL ACTION: Three flight stands allow Clan Wren Mandalorians to take to the battlefield skies with jetpack-powered mobility.
*''EXPANDED TACTICAL OPTIONS: Unit Cards and Upgrade Cards introduce new strategies and customization options for Mandalorian Rebel forces.
*''COMPLETE EXPANSION SET: Includes 7 miniatures, 7 bases, 3 flight stands, 2 unit cards, 4 upgrade cards, 1 punch sheet, and a rules insert.
''

==Contents==
*7 miniatures
*2 Unit Cards
*4 Upgrade Cards
