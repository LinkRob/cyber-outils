'''''Bounty Hunters'' 10''' is the tenth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. It was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[March 17]], [[2021]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE "TERMINUS GAUNTLET" CONCLUDES! VALANCE finds himself alone and outgunned against the dreaded NEW OHNAKA PIRATE GANG! The lives of the crew of a REBEL transport hang in the balance as he battles against grave odds. And what secret is DENGAR carrying that will upend Valance's life?''

==Opening crawl==
{{OpeningCrawl
|name=THE TERMINUS GAUNTLET<br />PART 3<br />A DESPERATE GAMBIT
|text=The Rebellion needs the cyborg bounty hunter Valance to rescue a
transport stranded in deep space. But the ship is under attack by the
fearsome new Ohnaka Gang!

The pirates all but destroyed Valance's freighter in the ensuing battle.
Escaping onto the transport, Valance found their commander ready to give
up. Valance isn't the surrendering type, so he stole and X-wing to fight back.

Now the rebels and the pirates believe Valance's quick exit to hyperspace
means the battle's all but over&mdash;or has Valance's fight only just begun?!}}
