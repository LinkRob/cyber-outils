'''Doshar fields''' were [[desert]] fields located on the [[planet]] [[Florrum]]. In [[22 BBY]], [[Turk Falso]], a member of [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s [[Ohnaka Gang]], shot down a Republic [[Kharrus's Nu-class attack shuttle|shuttle]] carrying [[Senator]] [[Kharrus]], [[Junior Representative|Representative]] [[Jar Jar Binks]], and [[Stone (clone trooper)|CC-5869]]. Kharrus and both clone pilots, including [[Mack]], were killed. The ship crashed in the Doshar fields.<ref name="The Gungan General" />
