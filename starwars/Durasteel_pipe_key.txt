'''Durasteel pipe keys''' were half-meter-long [[durasteel]] rods designed for opening [[Coruscant]]'s sewer grates. When [[4040s]] member [[Thalvus Agyomenidus]] broke into the [[Chandrilan Embassy]] to kill [[Mon Mothma]] and [[Perrin Fertha|her husband]], Agyomenidus bore a durasteel pipe key.<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Durasteel pipe keys appeared in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" />
