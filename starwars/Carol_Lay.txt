'''Carol Lay''' is a comic artist who lettered the [[Star Wars (1977) 6|sixth issue]] of [[Star Wars (1977)|Marvel's ''Star Wars'']] series. That issue, the climax of the comic adaptation of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|the original ''Star Wars'' film]], was reprinted in numerous collections.
