'''2934''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] that served the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] as part of a [[Geonosian droid army|droid army]] on the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]]. In [[22 BBY]], the [[B-series battle droid]] was among those deployed to the [[Petranaki Arena]] to [[First Battle of Geonosis|combat]] a [[Jedi assault team]] that had infiltrated the arena to rescue three [[prisoner]]s due for [[Death|execution]]: [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="Episode II" />
