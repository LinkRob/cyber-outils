The '''Atterra system''' was a [[binary]] [[star system]]<ref name="Bomber" /> located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" /> It contained at least three planets, [[Atterra Alpha]], [[Atterra Bravo]], and a [[gas giant]] [[Atterra Primo]].<ref name="Bomber" />

The system was controlled by the [[First Order]] just before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Destruction of the Hosnian system]] and the [[Resistance]] sent several [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|bombers]] acting as supply ships to Atterra to [[Atterra campaign|run supplies]].<ref name="Bomber" />
