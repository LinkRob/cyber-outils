'''Depatar'''<ref name="Bounty Hunters 33" /> was a discreet [[smuggler]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 32" /> and [[Black market|black-market]] [[spaceport]] located on a [[Unidentified planet (Depatar)|planet]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 33" /> near the [[Lost Souls]] [[asteroid field]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. According to [[Inferno Squad]] [[commander]] [[Iden Versio]], it was the only spaceport close enough to the [[asteroid]] field for [[starship]]s to travel to for emergency repairs after they had traveled through the field.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 32" />

Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date note">The events of [[Bounty Hunters 32|''Bounty Hunters'' 32]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> Inferno Squad were [[Hunt for Valance|hunting]] [[Beilert Valance]] as he had observed secret [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] information about the construction of a [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|new]] [[Death Star]]. After the [[Edgehawk (starship)|''Edgehawk'']], the ship of the [[T'onga's crew|crew]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]], who had [[Mission to Bestine IV|rescued]] Valance from [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], went into the Lost Souls at the suggestion of [[Zuckuss]], Inferno Squad were unable to maneuver their ship, the [[Corvus (Raider II-class)|''Corvus'']], through the field. Versio suggested that they let them go as she knew about the spaceport, and suggested they travel to it, knowing the ''Edgehawk'' would need to go there for repairs after being in the asteroid field. After Zuckuss successfully [[Navigator|navigated]] the ''Edgehawk'' through the Lost Souls, T'onga suggested they go to the spaceport for repairs, unaware that Inferno Squad were already there [[Ambush at Depatar|waiting]] for them.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 32" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Depatar/Legends|Depatar]] was a floating city above the [[gas giant]] [[Genarius]] in the [[Cularin system]].<ref name="cp">{{LivingForce|Clouds of Genarius: Clouded Paths}}</ref> The [[2025]] "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix of [[star system]]s placed the Depatar system in the Cademimu sector of the Outer Rim to be near the Lost Souls asteroid field,<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> conflicting with the Legends placement of Genarius in the [[Expansion Region]],<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> but the appendix's author [[Jason Fry]] has stated that Depatar's Outer Rim placement will be kept.<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-08-31.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-08-31}}</ref>
