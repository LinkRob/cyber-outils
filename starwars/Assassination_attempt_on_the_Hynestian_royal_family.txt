Some [[time]] prior to the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], a banquet was held at the [[Hynestian royal palace]] celebrating a treaty brokered between the [[Hynestian]]s and the [[Hutt Clan]] by the [[Jedi]] stationed on [[Hynestia Prime|Hynestia]]. During the banquet, [[Padawan]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] noted that the banquet was serving [[Povo Punch]] which was poisonous to Hutts. After [[Stellan Gios]] prevented the [[Hutt]]s from drinking the punch, the Hutts began their attack, targeting the [[Hynestian royal family]]. Once the royal family was secured, the Hutts were cornered by the Jedi and the Hynestians.<ref name="First Mission" />

==History==
===Background===
{{Quote|Nice to meet you, I'm Vernestra Rwoh''"<br />"''Stellan Gios. I am so excited to be your new master.|Vernestra Rwoh meeting Stellan Gios for the first time|<ref name="First Mission" />}}
By about [[233 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Padawan Vernestra Rwoh reported to the [[Temple on Hynestia]] to meet her new master Stellan Gios. When she arrived, she was greeted by the sight of Gios and Lynela Kabe-Oyu sparring. After everone was introduced to each other, Stellan Gios invited Vernestra to spar with him, resulting in Gios being toppled by Rwoh. Impressed, the two Jedi agreed that Vernestra should join them to the Banquet.<ref name="First Mission" />

===The Assassination Attempt===
{{Quote|Master Stellan, do you end up fighting Hutts often?''"<br />"''Not usually, but you'll learn as a Jedi there is a first time for everything.|Vernestra and Stellan conversing during the attack|<ref name="First Mission" />}}
While attending the banquet, Vernestra noticed that the guests were being served Porvo punch which is poisonous to Hutts as they are unable to eat [[Povo berry|Povo berries]]. Moving quickly to prevent a diplomatic faux pas, Stellan Gios leaped over the other party-goers and smacked the drink tray carrying the punch out of a server droid's hands.<ref name="First Mission" />

After witnessing that they no longer had an excuse to not sign their treaty with the Hynestians, the Hutts opened fire in the banquet hall, and attempted to attack the Hynestian royal family. After instructing Lynela to protect the royal family, Stellan Gios defended the other civilians at the banquet alongside his new Padawan. After noting that the royal family were under the protection of Lynela, Gios told his padawan that it was time to end the attack.<ref name="First Mission" />

Thinking of a creative solution to trap the attackers, Vernestra used her master's shoulder to vault up into the air and cut down a curtain using [[Vernestra Rwoh's purple-bladed lightsaber|her lightsaber]], causing the curtain to fall on and trap the attackers. Vernestra and her master then subsequently cornered the escaping Hutts who were then confronted by [[Queen]] [[Nyla]] who stated that she will either [[ransom]] the Hutts back to the [[Grand Hutt Council]] or do to them what they normally do to assassins.<ref name="First Mission" />

===Aftermath===
{{Quote|Why didn't you sense that the Hutts weren't going to sign the peace treaty?''"<br />"''You cannot always rely solely on the Force. Sometimes there are surprises.''"<br />"''I don't like surprises all that much.''"<br />"''Oh, Padawan, we are going to work so well together.|Vernestra Rwoh and Stellan Gios reflecting on their mission|<ref name="First Mission" />}}
After the assassination attempt, Vernestra asked her master why he couldn't sense the Hutts treachary, and he taught her about how she can't alwas rely just on the Force to expect things, sometimes she just needs to accept the surprises in life. After this lesson, Stellan Gios requested a lesson of his own, asking Vernestra to teach him how to do the leap she executed during the assassination attempt.<ref name="First Mission" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Assassination attempt on the Hynestain royal family appeared as the main conflict of the comic short story ''[[First Mission]]'', which was released on [[December 15]], [[2021]] as a part of [[The High Republic Adventures Annual 2021]].
