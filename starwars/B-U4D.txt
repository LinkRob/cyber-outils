'''B-U4D''', nicknamed "'''Buford''',"<ref name="B-U4D" /> was a [[heavy loader droid]]<ref name="By the numbers" /> active approximately thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], serving as part of the [[Resistance]]. B-U4D was a burly loading droid clad in [[Color|yellow]] [[ceramic]] [[armor]] plate. B-U4D served on [[D'Qar]] as part of the [[Resistance ground crew|ground crew]] that maintained the Resistance's starfighters. Tough and hard-working, Buford was one of many Resistance droids granted independence and seen as partners in the cause rather than mere machines.<ref name="B-U4D" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The character of B-U4D was first revealed in the [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] section of [[StarWars.com]] as part of the build up to the release of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[2015]]. The character then appeared as a minor background character in the film itself. Concept art of B-U4D under the title "Resistance Base Droid H012" was created during pre-production of the film in [[September]] [[2013]] by creature concept designer [[Jake Lunt Davies]].
