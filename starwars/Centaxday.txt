'''Centaxday''' was the second of the five [[Standard day|days]] in the [[galactic standard calendar]]'s [[Standard week|weeks]]. The day was named for [[Centax-1]], the primary [[moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. It was followed by [[Taungsday]] and preceded by [[Primeday]].<ref name="Falcon61">{{BuildFalconCite|61|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>
