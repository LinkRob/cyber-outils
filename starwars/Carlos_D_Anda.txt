'''Carlos D'Anda''' (born [[May 20]]) is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] artist who pencilled several issues of [[Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse's]] [[Star Wars (Dark Horse Comics 2013)|''Star Wars'']] comic series. He is most famous for his work on [[Wikipedia:Deathblow|''Deathblow'']] and [[Wikipedia:Batman: Arkham City (comic book)|''Batman: Arkham City'']].
