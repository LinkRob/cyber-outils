'''Belin's ship''' was a [[transport]] [[starship]] [[pilot]]ed by the [[Ugnaught]] [[Belin]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. After Belin was trapped behind the [[Stormwall]] in the [[Occlusion Zone]], a region of [[Realspace|space]] captured by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]], he unwillingly used his ship to transport cargo for the Nihil. The [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] and the [[astromech droid]] [[KC-78]], hiding out within the Occlusion Zone, boarded Belin's ship and dispatched the Nihil guards in order to [[Mission to Prandril|deliver]] its cargo of [[grain]] to the [[Rodian]] colony of [[Prandril]]. Belin was reluctant to cross the Nihil, but eventually chose to assist Kriss, who later used the ship to visit [[Hetzal Prime|Hetzal]] to [[Ambush on Hetzal Prime|rescue]] the [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Rhil Dairo]], also meeting fellow Jedi Master [[Porter Engle]]. The Jedi then utilized Belin's ship to [[Skirmish above Seswenna|board]] the Nihil vessel ''[[Cacophony]]'' in order to escape the Occlusion Zone; however, Belin and Dairo chose to remain within Nihil territory to assist the people trapped there.<ref name="The Eye of Darkness" />
