Shortly after [[Order 66]], a '''celebration marking the eradication of the Jedi Order''' was held on [[Coruscant]]. The event was to celebrate the [[Great Jedi Purge|destruction]] of the [[Jedi Order]]. The celebrations were led by [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]], who presented the speech to a massive audience on the steps of the old [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]]. The main event of the celebration was the incineration of the dead [[Jedi]]'s [[lightsaber]]s, including [[Yoda's lightsaber|that]] of [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], whose lightsaber was personally thrown into the flames by Amedda. Subjected to the searing heat, the kyber crystals within the lightsabers were unable to withstand it and exploded angrily in a great burst of blue light, forcing those closest to cower away in shock. The event was overseen by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and his newly-christened [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. While the event occurred, they discussed the history and creation of [[Sith lightsaber]]s and Vader was given the task of constructing his own by Palpatine.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|In order to ensure the security and continuing stability, the Republic will be reorganized into the First Galactic Empire, for a safe and secure society!|Emperor Palpatine proclaiming the creation of the Galactic Empire|<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>|audio = Palpatine-Proclamation.flac}}
[[File:Proclamation-SWBC66.png|thumb|left|225px|Supreme Chancellor Palpatine transforms the Galactic Republic into the First Galactic Empire.]]
As the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] entered its conclusion by [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]],<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the Republic, and secretly the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Sidious,<ref name="Episode III" /> had continued to gain emergency power from the [[Galactic Senate]]. By the final stages of the war, a group of [[senator]]s formed the [[Delegation of 2,000]] to counter Palpatine's growing power and restore authority back to the Senate.<ref name="Meena DB">{{Databank|meena-tills|Meena Tills}}</ref> Soon, Sidious declared the [[Jedi]], keepers of peace and justice in [[the galaxy]] for [[Millennium|thousands]] of [[Standard year|years]], as [[Treason|traitors]], following a supposed [[Duel in Palpatine's office|assassination attempt]]. Sidious acted quickly, and [[Great Jedi Purge|subdued]] the alleged [[Order 66|Jedi insurrection]] with the help of his newly designated [[Sith apprentice]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who was previously the [[Jedi Knight]] Anakin [[Skywalker family]] and the [[Chosen One]] who would bring balance to [[the Force]].<ref name="Episode III" />

Sidious also sent Vader to destroy the [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] on [[Mustafar]], and shut down the [[Separatist Droid Army]], bringing the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars to an end. As the Separatists were defeated and the [[Jedi Order]] was destroyed, Sidious [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] the Galactic Republic into the [[Galactic Empire|First Galactic Empire]] and established himself as its [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]]. In his speech, he further turned the public against the Jedi by espousing the story of the Jedi rebellion&mdash;using his scarred face as proof<ref name="Episode III" />&mdash;and directly blaming the Jedi for the galaxy's troubles. Relieved to see the end of the Clone Wars,<ref name="Launchpad229">{{InsiderCite|229|Launchpad}}</ref> the formation of the Empire was met by strong public and political support.<ref name="Palpatine Databank">{{Databank|emperor-palpatine-darth-sidious|Emperor Palpatine/Darth Sidious}}</ref> Reactions to Palpatine's speech in the [[Galactic Senate Chamber]] were that of mass applause, although a select number of senators watched in shock and disappointment as the Republic was washed away.<ref name="Episode III" />

==The celebration==
{{Quote|Through the grace of our Emperor Palpatine, we were saved from their treachery. The Jedi are ''gone''. The Emperor saw through their Separatist plots, their lies. He survived their attempt to assassinate him.|Mas Amedda speaking at the event|<ref name="Dark Lord 1" />}}
[[File:SidiousVaderCelebrationRally-2017DarthVader1.png|thumb|right|250px|Sidious and Vader overseeing the celebration.]]
Shortly after the proclamation of the [[New Order]], Sidious met with Vader, who was defeated in a [[duel on Mustafar]] against his former [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and had suffered extensive injuries, injuries that required Vader to wear a [[Darth Vader's armor|life support suit]].<ref name="Episode III" /> After leaving the [[Grand Republic Medical Facility|Grand Medical Facility]] Sidious and Vader made their way to the celebratory event outside of the former [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Dark Lord 1" />

As Sidious and Vader watched on from the Jedi Temple, [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] conducted a speech on the supposed treasonous acts of the Jedi, including claiming they had been behind the Separatist Alliance, and that society was now safe from their evil. Amedda continued, flanked by [[stormtrooper]]s and [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guardsmen]], and threw [[Yoda's lightsaber]] into an [[Lightsaber incinerator|incinerator]] with the [[lightsaber]]s of other lost Jedi. During Amedda's speech, Sidious lectured Vader on the newly formed Empire and that no one knew the true intentions of them. Sidious continued and spoke of [[kyber crystal]]s, and bleeding them to turn the color of the lightsaber's blade [[Color|crimson]]. Around that [[time]], Amedda had the incinerator turned on, and the heat of the [[fire]] caused the crystals within the many lightsabers to explode. The explosion caused Amedda and several [[Clone]] [[trooper]]s to be knocked down.<ref name="Dark Lord 1" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Do you understand Lord Vader?''"<br />"''Yes. You could have given me any one of those lightsabers to corrupt. Even Yoda's. But you did not. The saber of a Sith is not given. It is ''taken''.|Sidious and Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 1" />}}
[[File:SidiousLeavesVader-2017DarthVader1.png|thumb|left|200px|Sidious drops Vader off]]
After the event, Sidious transported Vader to the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] with his [[Theta-class T-2c shuttle|''Theta''-class T-2c shuttle]] and while traveling, Sidious asked how Vader felt in his suit, to which Vader replied that it was acceptable. When they landed, Vader asked why they had come to the Mid Rim, and Sidious replied that he had high hopes for what Vader and himself could accomplish, but informed Vader that he first needed a lightsaber, calling back to their conversation at the celebration event.<ref name="Dark Lord 1" />

Sidious gave Vader the quest of finding a living Jedi so Vader had someone to take the crystal from, and noted that it was a daunting task given how they had eliminated the Order. Still, Sidious proclaimed that he had great faith in Vader's resourcefulness and capability. As Vader disembarked, Sidious stated that he had left a ship for Vader's use, but it appeared that it was stolen by a band of outlaws, and stated that the galaxy cried out for order. Vader left and sought to [[Recapture of Vader's starship|recapture]] his [[Vader's starship|starship]].<ref name="Dark Lord 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
The celebration for the eradication of the Jedi Order was first depicted in [[Darth Vader (2017) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 1]], a [[canon]] comic released in [[2017]], written by [[Charles Soule]], and pencilled by [[Giuseppe Camuncoli]].<ref name="Dark Lord 1" />
