'''Accipipteros''' were a type of large, [[bird]]like [[reptavian]]s native to the [[planet]] of [[Dagobah]]. Flying [[Diet|predators]], they were the uncontested rulers of the [[swamp]] world's [[jungle]] canopies, where they spent their entire lives in the branches of [[gnarltree]]s. Their prey consisted of [[bogwing]]s and any other creature that climbed or flew to the top of the treetop canopy or beyond.<ref name="NoP" />

[[Color|Brown]] in color, accipipteros had a long tail, two legs ending in clawed feet, and two wings with three claw-like fingers each.<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> They used these small clawed hands to maneuver prey into their [[mouth]]s after snaring the creatures with their hooked lower jaw. The prey would then be consumed alive.<ref name="NoP" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], accipipteros were first mentioned in ''[[Nexus of Power]]'', a ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement released by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for the ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[roleplaying game]]<ref name="NoP" /> on [[March 17]], [[2016]].<ref name="Places of Power">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2016/3/17/places-of-power|text=Places of Power}}</ref>

The [[Accipiptero/Legends|creatures]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they first appeared in the [[Star Wars (1977) 41|forty-first issue]] of [[Marvel Comics]] original [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'']] comic book series, which was written by [[Archie Goodwin]], illustrated by [[Al Williamson]] and [[Carlos Garzón]].<ref name="SW41">[[Star Wars (1977) 41|''Star Wars'' (1977) 41]]</ref>
