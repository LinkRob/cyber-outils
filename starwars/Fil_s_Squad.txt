'''Fil's Squad''' was the name sometimes used to identify a tight-knit [[squad]] of [[clone trooper]]s under the command of [[Clone Commander]] [[Fil]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Also sometimes known by the name of '''Fil's Clones''', the squad took part in a [[Mission to Grievous's castle|skirmish]] at [[Grievous's fortress]] alongside [[Jedi]] [[Kit Fisto]] and [[Nahdar Vebb]] against [[Grievous]].

==Description==
Fil's Squad, as the group was sometimes known, was a [[squad]] of [[clone trooper]]s active as part of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. Also sometimes identified as Fil's Clones, the squad was led by [[Clone Commander]] [[Fil]], who enjoyed a close bond with the [[soldier]]s under his command.<ref name="Databank Download" /> The squad was composed of five [[Cloning|clones]] other than Fil himself, with the [[trooper]]s equipped with [[DC-15A blaster rifle]]s with [[ascension cable]]s<ref name="Lair" /> or [[DC-15A blaster carbine]]s.<ref name="Databank Download" /> Fil himself used two [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster]]s. The squad utilized a [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class attack/transport shuttle]] for transportation and sported [[Color|rusty red]]-colored markings on their [[Phase I clone trooper armor]],<ref name="Lair" /> which made the squad recognizable. The tight-knit squad was battle-ready and worked well with each other to the extent it could seem as though they shared the same thoughts. For the [[Mission to Grievous's castle|mission to Vassek 3]], Fil's squad was assigned to [[Jedi General]]s [[Kit Fisto]] and [[Nahdar Vebb]].<ref name="Databank Download" />

==History==
After a [[Rescue on the Tranquility|skirmish]] on the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Tranquility]]'' by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] forces that managed to rescue [[Viceroy of the Trade Federation]] [[Nute Gunray]],<ref name="Cloak of Darkness">{{TCW|Cloak of Darkness}}</ref> [[Jedi]] [[General]] Kit Fisto and his former [[Padawan]], Jedi General [[Nahdar Vebb]], were dispatched to track down Gunray. Meeting on [[Vassek 3]], Fisto and Vebb, along with Clone Commander Fil and his squad, moved into a [[Grievous's fortress|fortress]] where they believed a [[homing beacon]] was. Two troopers, [[Niner]] and [[Bel]], remained by the ''Nu''-class transport and [[Kit Fisto's Delta-7B starfighter|Fisto's starfighter]].<ref name="Lair" /> They were subsequently killed by a MagnaGuard, who fired a missle at the transport, destroying it and killing the clones.

Fil and his troopers were able to defeat the battle droids guarding the Viceroy, but realized that it was only a hologram. After Dooku offered a "alternative prize" to the jedi, the troopers went deeper into the fortress. They discovered that the fortress belonged to General Grievous.<ref name="Lair" />As the squad made their way through the fortress, the three other clones perished, with one falling to a trap in the floor. Soon, Fil was the last surviving member of his unit, only to meet his end during the fight with the droid general's pet [[Gor]].
