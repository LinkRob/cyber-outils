An '''autodoc''' was an automated medical apparatus. It was unable to do certain types of complicated surgery and one of these was installed on the ''[[Harvester]]'', a ship [[Nakari Kelen]] and [[Luke Skywalker]] found on the moon [[Fex]] with one injured survivor.<ref name="HttJ">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref>
