'''''Créatures et peuples de la galaxie''''' is a [[Wikipedia:French language|French]] young-readers book written by [[Sabine Boccador]] and published on [[October 13]], [[2017]] by [[Fleurus]] as part of the series ''[[La grande imagerie Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Amazon" />

==Publisher's summary==
''Over 50 creatures from the Star Wars universe are featured in this large imagery set. Each one stranger than the next, these creatures are illustrated and accompanied by a short text explaining their role in Star Wars.<ref name="Amazon" />''
