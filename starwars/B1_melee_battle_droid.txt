'''B1 melee battle droids''' were reprogrammed [[B1-series battle droid]]s from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. When reprogrammed, the droids served the [[Bedlam Raiders]] on [[Koboh]] and had reconfigured tactical programming.<ref name="Survivor" />

Before [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> a [[Primacy|Lucrehulk]] crashed into Koboh's [[swamp]]. Soon after, the Bedlam Raiders found the droids in the [[Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter|''Lucrehulk'' cargo freighter]] and reprogrammed some B1 droids to specialize in melee.<ref name="Survivor" />
