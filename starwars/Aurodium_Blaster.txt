The '''Aurodium Blaster''' was a modified [[heavy blaster pistol]] utilized by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Zaina]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Veteran Blaster|standard blaster]]. It featured a large [[scope]], with [[Color|golden]] plating, and a white and black grip.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Aurodium Blaster was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Zaina's Veteran Blaster, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 2.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[July 30]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Aurodium Bruiser]]. This wrap could be unlocked by reaching the rank Beskar I within ''Hunter's'' "Ranked" mode during the game's second season.<ref name="Hunters" />
