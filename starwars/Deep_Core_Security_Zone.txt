The '''Deep Core Security Zone''' was an area of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Deep Core]] which was controlled by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Trkn" /> It was considered a sensitive region and access to the zone was restricted by the [[15th Deep Core Reserve Fleet]], operating out of nearby [[Empress Teta]].<ref name="NOP">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref> A number of [[Interdictor]] vessels were developed for the zone, but [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] requisitioned them to use in an attempt to capture his stolen [[corvette]], the ''[[Carrion Spike]]''. The [[Emperor's adviser|Imperial Advisor]] [[Kren Blista-Vanee]] informed [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] of Tarkin's plan to use the [[cruiser]]s.<ref name="Trkn" /> Among the few who had clearance to fly through the zone was the [[tramp freighter]] [[captain]] [[Dana Galvason]], a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] sympathizer who flew the ''[[Bakuran Blade]]''.<ref name="BMF 42">{{BuildFalconCite|42|Starship Fact File}}</ref>
