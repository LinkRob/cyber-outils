{{Quote|Did you just ''shapeshift'' to resemble that ''Twi'lek'' to start a bar fight?!?''"<br />"''Don't insult me. I shape-shifted to look ''exactly'' like that Twi'lek.|T'onga and Catak, on the latter's shape-shifting ability|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" />}}
'''Catak''' was a [[gender]]fluid [[Clawdite]] who served as the [[Aide|assistant]] of the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Khel Tanna]]. Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], Catak and Tanna were present at the [[Bounty Hunters Guild Social Club]] on the [[planet]] [[D'Assem]], where Tanna had set up a meeting with the [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]], so she could rent the ''[[Enigma Catalyst]]'' for a job. Whilst at the club, Catak [[Changeling|shape-shifted]] into a [[Twi'lek]] and caused a brawl in the bar. As the brawl ensued, Catak shape-shifted back into a Clawdite and went to sit with Tanna, who introduced him to T'onga. Tanna informed T'onga that Catak had helped them round out the [[T'onga's crew|team]] for their [[Skirmish on the Rishi Moon|job]]. After the fight, the [[assassin]] [[Deathstick]] and bounty hunter [[Durge]] joined the crew.

Catak later aided the crew to [[Skirmish on the Rishi Moon|ambush]] [[Boba Fett]] on the [[Rishi Moon]]. The crew worked out a deal with Fett, which then he drove them with his [[starship]] ''[[Slave I]]'' to a [[mission to Quantxi|mission]] on the junk [[moon]] [[Quantxi]]. However, Catak was ordered to stay behind as their getaway [[pilot]] in the ''Enigma Catalyst'', to their dismay. He accompanied the crew to the [[droid]] [[Space station|station]] known as the [[Haven (droid outpost)|Haven]] to get [[Beilert Valance]]'s [[cerebral processor]] fixed, but perished at the hands of Valance, who had been [[Memory wipe|mind-wiped]] by the [[cyborg]] fixer [[Tarr Kligson]].

==Biography==
===Joining a crew===
{{Quote|Hmmm&hellip; This one is the love of your life? Kind of generic-looking if you ask me.|Catak, on the appearance of Yuralla Vega|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" />}}
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]];<ref name="Date" /> the [[Clawdite]] Catak worked with the [[human]] [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Khel Tanna]] as her [[Aide|assistant]]. Later, the pair were at the [[Bounty Hunters Guild Social Club]] in the [[Black-Market District]] of the [[planet]] [[D'Assem]], though separated among the crowd. Tanna met up with the [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]], who had set up the meeting. T'onga explained that she was in need of a [[starship]] since she sold hers the [[Edgehawk (starship)|''Edgehawk'']]. The bounty hunter further explained that she was doing it to save her friend the human [[cyborg]] [[Beilert Valance]] from losing his memory<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" /> after their [[Ambush at Depatar|run-in]] with<ref name="Bounty Hunters 34">[[Bounty Hunters 34|''Bounty Hunters'' 34]]</ref> the [[Imperial Special Forces]]<ref name="Inferno Squad">''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]''</ref> [[Inferno Squad]] at the [[black market]] [[Depatar]],<ref name="Bounty Hunters 34" /> having lost many people in her life. Tanna remarked that it sounded like a big job and that she needed more people in her [[T'onga's crew|crew]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" />

[[File:CatakPunchesHouk-BountyHunters35.png|290px|thumb|left|A disguised Catak punches a Houk.]]
Somewhere else within the club, Catak [[Changeling|shape-shifted]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" /> himself<ref name="Bounty Hunters 39" /> as a [[Twi'lek]], punching a [[Houk]] while on their way to Tanna. He avoided detection from the Houk, though their action stirred up a bar fight. Catak then quickly caught up, meeting Tanna and T'onga. Tanna introduced Catak to T'onga and explained that the bar fight was intentional to help them get more capable people to join their efforts in saving Valance. With the brawl coming to an end, the [[Gen'Dai]] bounty hunter [[Durge]] and [[assassin]] [[Deathstick]] were the two left standing amidst the chaos. Tanna pointed at the two and hired them for the job, calling them the most competent. When asked, T'onga clarified that she was after the [[Cloning|clone]] bounty hunter [[Boba Fett]], whose head was put on a big bounty by the criminal syndicate [[Black Sun]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" />

Catak and the rest then went onboard Tanna's [[light freighter]] the ''[[Enigma Catalyst]]'', along with other members of the crew, Valance, the [[Trandoshan]] bounty hunter [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk]], the [[Gand]] [[Findsman]] [[Zuckuss]], and his [[droid]] friend [[4-LOM]]. On their way to hunt Fett, Valance tried to preserve his memory by drawing the people he cared for. Catak teased the cyborg by shape-shifting into his former partner [[Yuralla Vega]]. Angered by his action, Bossk choked and threatened the Clawdite. The two were then knocked out by Durge from causing any further commotions. Soon after, the [[freighter]] reached [[Rishi Moon]], where Fett was last tracked. The crew left the ship, though Catak stayed behind. The rest of the crew then approached Fett, who went hostile after being warned by T'onga. The two opposing parties fought, though T'onga eventually managed to hold Fett at [[knife]]point and worked out a deal to benefit them both, sparing him alive.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" />

===Getaway pilot===
{{Quote|Sure&hellip; Leave poor ''Catak'' behind to watch the ship. While everyone ''else'' gets to have all the fun.|Catak|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36" />}}
[[File:CatakConfronted-BountyHunters36.png|250px|right|thumb|Catak startled by Boba Fett, who was confronting them.]]
Catak was later ordered to stay behind and prepared the ''Enigma Catalyst'' for extraction, while the rest of the crew was on Fett's ship ''[[Slave I]]''. Lounging in the cockpit, Catak shape-shifted into Tanna, T'onga, and Valance while bad-mouthing them, though he struggled to shapeshift into the cyborg. The Clawdite was about to talk about Fett but was startled when the bounty hunter interrupted, not realizing that the comm was open the entire time. Tanna reminded Catak to have the ''Catalyst'' ready to go, which he acknowledged and signed off. The rest of the crew onboard the ''Slave I'' then went to the junk [[moon]] [[Quantxi]] for a [[Mission to Quantxi|mission]], doing their part of the bargain with Fett.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36" />

The crew was sent out to assassinate the [[Falleen]] [[Lord]] [[Xanak Grunseit]], who was wanted by the criminal empire [[Hutt Clan]], and as a result put up the bounty against Fett, fearing for their own safety. Despite being outnumbered by the Black Sun forces, the crew managed to defeat them and completed their end of the deal. Fett then fulfilled his and handed T'onga the [[coordinate]]s to the cyborg fixer [[Tarr Kligson]]. He swiftly left with the ''Slave I'', leaving the crew behind. With their only lead in hand, Tanna called Catak for extraction for extraction.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36" />

===A botched job===
{{Quote|You know I always got your back, boss&hellip; But this is starting to feel like a ''wild bantha'' chase.''"<br />"''I just hope Zuckuss and For-Elloem are doing better than we are.|Catak and Khel Tanna|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 38">[[Bounty Hunters 38|''Bounty Hunters'' 38]]</ref>}}
Later, the crew followed Fett's lead, which led them to the [[Haven (droid outpost)|Haven]], an old [[space station]]. Upon their arrival, the crew was greeted by the [[droid]] [[Telemak]] and two other droids, who claimed as the assistant to the guardian of the Haven. When asked by Telemak how the crew arrived at the space station, T'onga told the droid that they learned about Kligson and the space station from Fett. Telemak mistook the name for the human bounty hunter [[Jango Fett]] and became very eager to help Valance, sending the Cyborg to meet Kligson while Catak and the rest of the crew stayed behind in the docking bay.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 37">[[Bounty Hunters 37|''Bounty Hunters'' 37]]</ref>

A few hours went by and the crew started to become impatient. Catak shape-shifted into T'onga and proceeded to criticize the bounty hunter, to which T'onga threatened the Clawdite with a murder plot, startling him. Tanna suggested that the crew needed to be proactive by taking one of the droids to her ship for questioning, though T'onga reminded her that she was the [[captain]]. T'onga then ordered Zuckuss and 4-LOM to inspect the station further.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 37" /> Unbeknownst to them, the space station was taken over by the entity known as the [[Scourge]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 39" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Oh, ''I'll'' do some criticizing. The ''only'' thing missing to make this place more ''obviously'' a death trap is ominous music playing over the comm speakers.|Catak, on their situation by impersonating T'onga|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 37" />}}
[[File:CatakShapeshifts-BountyHunters36.png|250px|thumb|right|Catak attempts to shapeshift into Beilert Valance.]]
Catak was a<ref name="Bounty Hunters 38" /> [[gender]]fluid<ref name="Ethan Sacks" /> Clawdite with [[Color|green]] skin and brown [[eye]]s. He had a laid-back attitude, often remarking on a dire situation in jest.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" /> He often bad-mouthed other people, though became cowardly when confronted. Despite his negative outlook and attitude toward other people,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36" /> Catak showed a strong sense of loyalty and trust toward Tanna.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 38" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|&ndash;&ndash;''Unn''&hellip;Not easy impersonating a cyborg&ndash;&ndash;|Catak|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36" />}}
Similar to other members of their [[species]], Catak was able to shapeshift;<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" /> however, their shape-shifting ability was limited as he struggled to transform into a cyborg such as Valance. The Clawdite was also able to pilot starships, such as the ''Enigma Catalyst''.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36" />

==Equipment==
Catak wore a [[leather]] [[hat]] with [[goggles]], a sleeveless gray body [[armor]], and a pair of red shoulder pads. He also sported a brown shawl, draped on their back.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" />

==Behind the scenes==
Catak first appeared in the [[Bounty Hunters 35|thirty-fifth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" /> on [[June 21]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103055/star_wars_bounty_hunters_2020_35|text=Star Wars: Bounty Hunters (2020) #35}}</ref> When asked about Catak's use of two pronoun sets in ''Bounty Hunters'', Sacks stated via [[Wikipedia:Bluesky|Bluesky]] that the character was [[Wikipedia:Gender fluidity|genderfluid]]. He indicated that while the same principle did not necessarily apply [[canon]]ically to all Clawdite, he felt at least some members of the species who changed genders physically would also change their self perception of their identity in the process.<ref name="Ethan Sacks" />
