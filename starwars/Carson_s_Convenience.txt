"'''Carson's Convenience'''" is an article written by [[John Kirk]]<ref name="SWI" /> and published in [[Star Wars Insider 205|''Star Wars Insider'' 205]]<ref name="SWI" /> on [[September 21]], [[2021]].<ref name="Titan" /> The article featured an interview of [[Paul Sun-Hyung Lee]], the actor who portrays [[Carson Teva]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'' and [[Star Wars: Ahsoka|''Ahsoka'']].<ref name="SWI" />
