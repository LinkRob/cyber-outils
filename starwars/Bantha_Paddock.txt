The '''Bantha Paddock''' was a small [[Bantha]] corral found along the edge of the [[Mos Espa Waystation]] battlefield of the [[Grand Arena]], utilized by the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. This pen could contain at least two Banthas, and was identified by its wooden signage which displayed "Bantha Paddock" in large text at the top and "Do Not [[Diet|Feed]]" painted much smaller at the bottom right, all in [[Aurebesh]] and [[Color|white]], alongside an illustrated Bantha head to the left and a pair of lights illuminating the upper words.<ref name="Hunters" />
