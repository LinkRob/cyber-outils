'''Dasa''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who was the owner of the [[Skuhl refinery|refinery]] on the [[planet]] [[Skuhl]]. When the [[Galactic Empire]] arrived and attempted to purchase her refinery, she refused. However, Dasa later disappeared with her partners shortly after the occupation, and the refinery came under the oversight of Imperial technicians.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
