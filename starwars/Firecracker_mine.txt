A '''firecracker mine''' was a type of [[space mine]] used to disorient enemy [[starship]]s. Deployed in groups, a firecracker [[mine]] emitted an intense burst of light, noise, and [[radiation]] when it exploded. Starships caught in the blast had their [[sensor]]s and [[comm unit]]s suppressed, and their crews could be disoriented. Firecracker mines were sold for 400 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />
