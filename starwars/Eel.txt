'''Eels''' were long-bodied [[fish]] that were found on [[planet]]s throughout [[the galaxy]]. Types of eel included the [[Rishi eel]] of the [[Rishi Moon]],<ref name="Rookies Episode Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/rookies-episode-gallery|text="Rookies" Episode Gallery}}</ref> the [[Mon Cala eel|Mon Calamari eel]] of [[Mon Cala]],<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> the [[Armored eel]] of [[Yavin 4]],<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> the [[Omereth giant eel]] of [[Omereth]],<ref name="HttJ">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref> the [[Porthomer eel]] of [[Lew'el]],<ref name="Legends of Luke Skywalker">''[[The Legends of Luke Skywalker]]''</ref> the [[Purcassian river eel]]<ref name="Weapon of a Jedi">''[[The Weapon of a Jedi: A Luke Skywalker Adventure]]''</ref> and a [[Unidentified eel species|species]] on [[Quila]].<ref name="Ocean Rescue">{{RebelsMagCite|12|Ocean Rescue}}</ref>
