'''Blba trees''' were a type of short, thorny [[tree]] found scattered across the [[grass]]lands of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dantooine]]. They featured [[Color|light brown]] trunks, green leaves, and were [[Diet|edible]] to herds of herbivores such as Dantooine's [[piket]]s. [[Fabool]], balloon-like creatures found on the world, were sometimes skewered by the blba trees' thorns and then consumed by [[carnivorous snail]]s.<ref name="BMF" /> A number of blba trees grew near a [[Resistance]] [[Unidentified outpost (Dantooine)|outpost]] on Dantooine<ref name="Rebuilding the Resistance">{{Resistance|Rebuilding the Resistance}}</ref> by [[34 ABY]].<ref name="ResistanceDate">{{ResistanceDate|2|17}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Blba trees were first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "Visiting Dantooine," an article<ref name="BMF" /> written by [[Tim Veekhoven]]<ref name="SM">{{DMCite|dm=File:Credits-TimVeekhoven-Twitter.png|creator=[[Tim Veekhoven]]}}</ref> and published by [[De Agostini]] in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 37|thirty-seventh]] issue of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine<ref name="BMF" /> around [[September 16]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|37}}</ref> The trees then made their first appearance in "[[Rebuilding the Resistance]],"<ref name="Rebuilding the Resistance" /> the seventeenth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season Two|second season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[January 19]], [[2020]].<ref name="Disney release">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm Animation]] artist [[Jim Moore]] created concept artwork of Dantooine's surface that depicted the trees, which was dated to [[September 12]], [[2018]].<ref name="Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=rebuilding-the-resistance-concept-art-gallery|text="Rebuilding the Resistance" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 7}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Blba tree/Legends|blba trees]] first appeared in [[Dark Apprentice (novel)|''Dark Apprentice'']] a [[1994]] novel written by [[Kevin J. Anderson]] as the second installment of the ''[[Star Wars: The Jedi Academy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="DA">[[Dark Apprentice (novel)|''Dark Apprentice'']]</ref> It later was first visually depicted in ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]'', a [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[BioWare]].<ref name="KOTOR">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''</ref>
