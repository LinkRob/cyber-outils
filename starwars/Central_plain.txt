The '''central plain''' was located on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> conditions across the plain remained stable despite persistently strong and steady winds occurring over several [[Standard day|days]]. During a broadcast of the [[Holonet News Toshara]]'s wind report, [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Botto Fna]] covered the plain's condition, but warned listeners that an [[ion front]] descending toward the [[city]] of [[Mirogana]] on the moon might cause the windflow on the plains to reverse.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The central plain was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
