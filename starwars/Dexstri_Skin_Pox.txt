'''Dexstri Skin Pox''' was a type of [[Disease|pox]] that could be contracted by encountering meat. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Char-Ryl-Roy]] encountered cases of the illness that had resulted in lumps being left behind on the afflicted's skin. While on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]]<ref name="Convergence" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> he and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]] investigated the [[Eirami poison|poisoning]] of the [[Kage]] [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] member [[Abda]]. Char-Ryl-Roy indicated he had never witnessed anything similar to the blisters littering Abda's skin but noted he had witnessed the Dexstri Skin Pox leave behind skin lumps in the past. When Nattai inquired about the pox, the Jedi Master confirmed it was transmissible via meat.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Dexstri Skin Pox was mentioned in [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], a [[2022]] novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Convergence" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Dexstri Skin Pox/Legends|Dexstri Skin Pox]] was a meat [[Parasite/Legends|parasite]] first introduced in ''[[The Truce at Bakura Sourcebook]]''. The [[1996]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] guide was authored by [[Kathy Tyers]] and [[Eric S. Trautmann]] and released as a supplement for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Bakura">''[[The Truce at Bakura Sourcebook]]''</ref>
