'''Dorgat''' was a [[Human/Legends|human]] [[Mercenary/Legends|mercenary]] who operated during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].

==Biography==
Dorgat was [[Major/Legends|major]] in the [[Dwitzel's Rats]] mercenary unit, and the second of [[Dwitzel]].<ref name="CB67" />

He could have been a [[Pirate/Legends|pirate]] before his recruitment.<ref name="CB67" />

==Personality and traits==
Dorgat was brutal and ferocious but effective.<ref name="CB67" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dorgat was created by [[Jean Balczesak]] for an ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] Gamemaster help which appeared in the French magazine ''[[Casus Belli 67]]''.<ref name="CB67" />
