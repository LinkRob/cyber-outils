'''Fermented kelp-ichor''' was a [[Foodstuff|culinary preparation]] favored by the [[Crolute]]s, a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Crul]]. Prepared by an [[Alpha (Crolute)|alpha]] Crolute's concubines and junior [[Marriage|spouses]], fermented [[kelp]]-ichor was consumed by Crolute clan leaders at an alpha's mansion, as the leaders engaged in diplomatic and trade discussions while floating on their backs in the mansion's immersion tank.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Fermented kelp-ichor was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />
