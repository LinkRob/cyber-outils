'''''Darth Vader'' 7''' is the seventh issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. It was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 11]], [[2020]].

The issue features the story '''"Into the Fire Part II: The Assassin,"''' in which [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] struggles to survive the embers of [[Mustafar]], as he faces [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]], [[Sith assassin|assassin]] of the [[Sith]].

==Publisher's summary==
''PAIN LEADS TO ANGER AND ANGER LEADS TO AN ELEMENT FROM EPISODE IX! As DARTH VADER battered and broken by the EMPEROR as punishment for his rebellion, drags himself across the blasted surface of MUSTAFAR with vengeance on his mind! But can he survive the onslaught of a certain mysterious SITH ASSASSIN? And how will his world change when he learns his enemy's GREATEST SECRET?!!''
