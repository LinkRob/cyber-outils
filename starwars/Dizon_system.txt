The '''Dizon system''',<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-31.png|author=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-31}}</ref> known also as the '''Dizon Fray system''',<ref name="Systems" /> was a [[star system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that included the [[moon]] of [[Dizon Fray]], the [[Homeworld|home]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Amphibian|semi-amphibious]] [[Dizonite]]s.<ref name="Rise" /> It was situated at the coordinates U-7 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" />
