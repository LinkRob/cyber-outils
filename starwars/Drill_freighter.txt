'''Drill freighters''' were a type of [[freighter]] used by the [[Galactic Republic]] in the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="RTCT" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> an [[Trymant IV disaster|Emergence]]&mdash;a fragment of the [[Class A modular freight transport|freighter]] ''[[Legacy Run]]''&mdash;of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] directly struck the [[Trymant system]] [[moon]] [[Praknat 3]], throwing off the [[Star system|system's]] [[Gravity|gravitational]] balance and tilting the [[planet]] [[Trymant IV]] toward the [[Trymant system's second sun|system's second sun]]. As a result, Trymant IV's aboveground [[water]] sources were instantly vaporized.<ref name="RTCT" />

By [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> the Republic sent a [[fleet]] of [[drill]] freighters to look for rumored underground water sources beneath the world's lower valley area. While visiting the planet<ref name="RTCT" /> that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Kantam Sy]] informed [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]] of how Trymant IV's residents were attempting to continue to live there despite the devastation, saying they had heard of the freighters sent to help find sources of water.<ref name="RTCT" />

==Behind the scenes==
