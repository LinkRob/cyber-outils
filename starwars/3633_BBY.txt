The [[Standard year/Legends|year]] '''3633 [[Galactic Standard Calendar|BBY]]''' was a period of galactic history. That year, the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Choza Raabat]] was found on a [[Planet/Legends|planet]] in [[Wild Space/Legends|Wild Space]] by a [[Zakuul]] scouting party. Raabat had been stranded for a [[Standard decade/Legends|decade]], and was forced to commandeer the ship after the Zakuulans tried to kill him.
