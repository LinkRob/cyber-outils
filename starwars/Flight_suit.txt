'''Flight suits''', also spelled as '''flightsuits''' or otherwise known as '''jumpsuits''', were a type of [[Clothing|outfit]] worn by [[pilot]]s. They were worn in junction with [[flight helmet]]s.<ref name="ANH">''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]''</ref> Due to the dangerous nature of some piloting occupations, some [[starfighter]] pilots wore [[Environment suit|EVA suits]].<ref name="CE">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> They came in different colors such as blue,<ref name="R1">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> green, and red.

==Galactic Empire==
[[File:TIEPilot-MF33.png|thumb|250px|left|An Imperial Starfighter Pilot]]
[[Imperial Starfighter Pilot]]s wore specialized [[Color|black]] [[TIE pilot flight suit|flight suits]] that were pressurized and made out of energy-shielded fabric.<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> They also wore a vacuum-sealed, reinforced [[helmet]] with lenses that were polarized with displays linked to the ship readouts, as well and communication equipment.<ref name="VG">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> On the outside of the suit was chest [[armor]], a life support pack and an emergency atmospheric unit, while the feet and hands were covered by positive-[[gravity]] pressure [[boot]]s and insulated [[gauntlet]]s.<ref name="RO Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

==Rebel Alliance==
Pilots for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] mostly wore orange flight suits, although other colors did exist. The suits were pressurized and equipped with a [[Flak vest]] and [[Novaldex]] [[Diagnostech life support unit|life support unit]]. The suits had multiple pockets for storing tools and emergency equipment. A pocket on the sleeve allowed a pilot to carry several [[Code cylinder|data cylinders]]. A [[datapad]] was stored on the wrist of the wearer. The suits were worn with insulated [[glove]]s and [[boot]]s.<ref name="RSOWM">''[[Rebel Starfighters Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>

==New Republic==
[[File:Beyta-Ahsoka.jpg|thumb|right|200px|A [[Mon Calamari]] in a blue New Republic flight suit]]
Pilots of the [[New Republic Defense Fleet Starfighter Corps]] wore flight suits similar<ref name="Passenger">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 10: The Passenger}}</ref> to those of the Rebellion.<ref name="R1" /> They came in colors like orange<ref name="Passenger" /> and blue.<ref name="The Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref>

==First Order==
Although [[First Order]] [[TIE series|TIE fighters]] were equipped with life support, the [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|pilots]] still wore insulated and pressurized flight suits that were colored black.<ref name="TFOWM">''[[TIE Fighter Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>[[First Order Special Forces TIE fighter pilot|Special Forces pilots]] wore variants of the flight suits that had a red stripe on them<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>; while [[TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor|TIE/ba Baron]] pilots such as [[Elrik Vonreg]] or [["Holo"]]<ref name="Starfighter Missions">''[[Star Wars: Starfighter Missions]]''</ref> wore custom, crimson-colored flight suits.

==Resistance==
[[Resistance]] pilots wore updated flight suits that often bore the [[Rebel Alliance starbird]]. The suits were colored "interstellar orange." They were equipped with a [[FreiTek Incorporated|FreiTek]] life support system. The flak vest was inflatable and more protective than the older models. Like the older models, the Resistance flight suit was outfitted with multiple pockets.<ref name="RSOWM" />
