'''Ee-Seventeen''' was an [[astromech droid]] working at the [[Yarvellian Institute]] [[medcenter]] on [[Protobranch]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. After the [[Nihil]] [[Minister of Advancement]] [[Baron]] [[Boolan]] arrived at the medcenter delivering a supposed [[Boolan's virus|cure]] for the [[Blight]], a [[computer virus]] began to corrupt internal systems throughout the facility. Ee-Seventeen was connected to the internal systems via [[scomp link]], and was encouraged to disconnect by a [[Unidentified reception droid|reception droid]] to keep from being infected. He, however, became increasingly concerned as a [[speeder bike]] operated by [[Twi'lek]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Lourna Dee]] rapidly sped towards the institute's entrance. Although the droid attempted to lock the doors, he was too slow, and Dee crashed her bike through the glass.<ref name="Script" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ee-Seventeen first appeared in flashbacks in ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]'', a [[2024]] audio drama written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Tempest Breaker">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]''</ref> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The spelling of his name was later confirmed with the release of the audio drama's [[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker (script)|script]] in [[2025]].<ref name="Script" />
