The '''Brackna system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Doldur sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square P-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Brackna]].<ref name="Wayseeker" /> By around [[160 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{WayseekerDate}}</ref> [[soup]] contests were held on Brackna, which was known to the [[human]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Indara]].<ref name="Wayseeker" />
