'''Core Classic''' was a term used to describe the architectural design and structure of the [[Core Worlds/Legends|Core Worlds]]. [[Wilhuff Tarkin/Legends|Wilhuff Tarkin's]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin's mansion|home]] on [[Eriadu/Legends|Eriadu]] was said to be a blend of "Core Classic and [[Mid Rim Ornate]], which declared itself with huge, domed enclosures, gilded columns, and stone floors polished to a liquid sheen." Core Classic architecture had one or more of these qualities.
