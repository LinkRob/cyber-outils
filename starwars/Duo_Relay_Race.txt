The '''Duo Relay Race''' was a racing event hosted at the [[Taqual racing pit]] on the [[planet]] [[Utapau]]. The race involved [[dactillion]] riders guiding their beasts from the top of the [[sinkhole]] to the bottom, where they would hand over to [[varactyl]] riders for a race back to the surface. The Taqual racing pit's instability added to the danger and appeal of the race.<ref name="COTR" />
