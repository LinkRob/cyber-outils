"'''Eyes on the Prize'''" is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] published in the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 6|sixth]] issue of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels Magazine]]''. The story was written by [[Jeremy Barlow]], illustrated by [[Bob Molesworth]], and edited by [[Gunther Nickel]]. It was published on [[June 17]], [[2015]].<ref name="EotP" />

Barlow imagined the story taking place in between the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episodes "[[Gathering Forces]]" and "[[Path of the Jedi]]."<ref name="Twit">{{Twitter|Jeremy_Barlow|status/694055279688679424|[[Jeremy Barlow]]|quote=@lelalmekha I imagined it going between "Gathering Forces" and "Path of the Jedi" in Season 1. Not sure if that's official, though!|nobackup=1}}</ref> However, this information is not officially established as [[canon]].

==Plot summary==
Sometime between [[5 BBY]] and [[4 BBY]], two [[bounty hunter]]s, [[Fissol]] and [[Gowski]], travel to [[Lothal]] after hearing from a [[Duros]] bounty hunter of the presence of a [[Jedi]] on the [[planet]]. On Lothal, they encounter [[Ezra Bridger]] in the caves practicing his [[Jedi training]]. After a short chase, they overcome Bridger and kidnap him. Later, [[Kanan Jarrus]] is curious as to Bridger's whereabouts, and [[Hera Syndulla]] tells him that he is completing an errand for him. Jarrus, having sent his [[Padawan]] on no such errand, realizes Bridger is in trouble and goes after him. The bounty hunters knock out Bridger and gag him, remarking that they expect a handsome reward from the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] for the Jedi's capture. Jarrus reveals his presence and rescues Bridger. Once Bridger comments that the bounty hunters now know of the Jedi presence on Lothal, Jarrus tricks them into thinking there are no Jedi, sending the hunters away.
