'''Eliimy''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Hutt]] who was allegedly the wife of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]].<ref name="Storms-of-Crait" />

During a standoff against [[Trusk Berinato]], [[Han Solo]] claimed to have stolen the wife of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]], Eliimy the Hutt, from him, referring to her as "a beautiful woman." It was unclear whether Eliimy had been real or merely a fictional fabrication created by Solo.<ref name="Storms-of-Crait" />
