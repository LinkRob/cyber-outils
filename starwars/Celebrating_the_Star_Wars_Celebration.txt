"'''Celebrating the ''Star Wars'' Celebration'''" is an interview of [[Dan Madsen]], who was the publisher of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' from [[1987]] through [[2001]]; [[Jon Bradley Snyder]], former editor of ''Star Wars Insider''; [[Stephen J. Sansweet]], head of [[Lucasfilm]] fan relations; and [[Anthony Daniels]], who portrayed [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]] in all six movies.<ref name="SW118" />

The article covered how the first [[Celebration]] came to be, and how its success led to the other Celebrations being held. The interview was conducted by [[Scott Chernoff]], appearing in [[Star Wars Insider 118|''Star Wars Insider'' 118]].<ref name="SW118" />
