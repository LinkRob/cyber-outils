The '''''Aurora III''''', nicknamed "'''Aurie''',"<ref name="No Big Deal">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-insider-222-excerpt|text=From the Pages of ''Star Wars Insider'': A New Mission Begins in an Original ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Tale}}</ref> was a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] in use during the [[High Republic Era]]. During a combined Republic and [[Jedi Order]] effort to save the [[Hetzal system]] from [[hyperspace]] debris, it was [[pilot]]ed by [[Joss Adren|Joss]] and [[Pikka Adren]]. The Adrens continued to fly the ship in the further efforts to deal with the [[Emergences]] of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
