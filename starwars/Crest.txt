'''Crest''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served as a [[stormtrooper]] [[sergeant]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Cloning|Cloned]] from the [[Clone template|template]] of [[Jango Fett]], a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[bounty hunter]] whose [[DNA|genetic material]] spawned the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], Crest fought in the [[Clone Wars]] as a [[soldier]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Although he managed to remain in [[Imperial Military|military]] service under the [[New Order]], his accelerated aging led his [[Commanding officer|superior]], the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], to doubt the clone's reliability.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Sergeant Crest, are you in the crime lord's warehouse?''"<br />"''Yes, Lord Vader. Ready to bring the entire place down at your command.''"<br />"''Then you have redeemed yourself, sergeant.|Darth Vader and "Crest"|<ref name="Tarkin" />}}
The [[clone trooper]] known as Crest was part of an [[army]] of [[DNA|genetically]] identical [[soldier]]s, [[Cloning|bred]] from the [[Clone template|template]] of the [[human]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] to fight for the [[Galactic Republic]]. After serving in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]], Crest became a [[stormtrooper]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

[[File:Aftermath-Mission_to_Murkhana.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Crest (left) served under Tarkin and Vader.]]
Crest served with a contingent of stormtroopers accompanying the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] on a [[Mission to Murkhana|mission]] to [[Murkhana]]. At the [[time]], he held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[sergeant]]. Crest accompanied Vader and Tarkin as they investigated a building which held former [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] contraband. However, as they did so, their [[Starship|vessel]], the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', was hijacked. The guards and two crewmen aboard were killed, and Vader was angry at Crest for allowing such a thing to happen. To get a new vessel, Vader and Tarkin intimidated a [[Faazah|local]] [[Sugi]] crime boss to give up his ship to them by threatening to destroy his warehouse with his family inside. The sergeant and his troopers took control of the warehouse, and later secured the crime lord's ship when he gave into the pressure and gave it up to the Imperials.<ref name="Tarkin" />

When they pursued the ''Carrion Spike'' aboard the crime lord's ship, Crest and his troopers served as gunners. Despite their efforts, the unit failed to halt the ''Spike's'' multiple attacks on Imperial installations, and eventually stayed aboard the [[Liberator (Venator-class)|''Liberator'']], a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]]. By that point, only Crest and six troopers remained of Vader's original contingent that was on Murkhana. Later, they met up with a task force led by the ''[[Executrix]]'', an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]], which Tarkin appropriated as his [[flagship]]. Crest, having been promoted to [[lieutenant]] by Vader, was present with the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord]] aboard a picket ship. Their planned ambush for the ''Spike'' failed, however. He later reported to Vader and Tarkin on the capture of a freighter during the ambush, which he presumed had links with the hijackers of the ''Spike''.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Later, Lieutenant Crest accompanied Vader aboard the [[Conquest (Secutor-class)|''Conquest'']], a [[Secutor-class Star Destroyer|''Secutor''-class Star Destroyer]] which served as the command vessel of [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]]. Vader told Rancit he knew that it was he who was the traitor that provided aid to those who stole the ''Spike'', and provided them with information to carry out attacks on Imperial targets. Vader ordered Crest and his men to lock Rancit in an [[escape pod]] and launch it out to a safe distance; whereon the treacherous admiral was forced to give the fleet a fire order that destroyed him for his disloyalty.<ref name="Tarkin" />
