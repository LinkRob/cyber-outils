{{Quote|That is the ''FateSpinner'', a Grysk ''Shatter''-class WarMaster. It could destroy the ''Whetstone'' in ten minutes. It could turn your entire Kilhorde battle fleet to scrap in two hours.|Jixtus, to Nakirre|audio=Fatespinner.flac|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />|translated=!}}
The '''''FateSpinner''''' was a massive and pristine [[Shatter-class WarMaster|''Shatter''-class WarMaster]] [[warship]] that served as the [[flagship]] of the [[Grysk]] operative "[[Jixtus]]"'s [[Jixtus's fleet|fleet]] and was [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by a Grysk [[Unidentified Grysk captain (FateSpinner)|captain]]. Equipped with a [[nyix]] [[hull]], [[missile]]s shielded by smaller projectiles known as [[dibber]]s, and [[spectrum laser]], the [[starship]] was capable of destroying the large [[Illumine Kilhorde]] battle [[fleet]] of the [[Kilji Illumine]] in two [[Standard hour|hours]].

In [[18 BBY]], the ''FateSpinner'' assisted its master in intimidating his ally, [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]], and [[Sneak attack by the FateSpinner|attempted]] to destroy the [[Chiss]] [[Light cruiser|Clarr light cruiser]] ''[[Orisson]]'' in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nations's [[Ornfra system]] to prevent its [[captain]], Captain [[Clarr'os'culry]], from leaking to the public that the Ascendancy's [[Clarr family]] was housing the ''FateSpinner'' and other Grysk warships in the [[Rhigal system|Rhigar system]]. However, the ''Shatter''-class WarMaster did not succeed, as the ''Orisson'' was able to flee into [[hyperspace]].

[[Standard day|Days]] after the attack at Ornfra, the ''FateSpinner'' and the rest of Jixtus's fleet engaged a [[Thrawn's fleet|fleet]] of Chiss warships in the [[Sunrise system]] during [[Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand]]. The ships initially had the upper hand but with the assistance of the [[Garwian Unity]] and [[Vak Combine]] nations' respective warships and a [[Vagaari]] [[gravity well projector]], the Chiss were able to destroy most of the Grysks. After [[Self-destruct mechanism|destroying]] his own fleet, Jixtus warned the Chiss that the Grysks would be [[Imperial–Grysk conflict|back]] before destroying the ''FateSpinner''.

==Description==
{{Quote|The largest ship, the one now coming up along the starboard side of this pathetic little Kilji war cruiser, is my flagship, the ''FateSpinner''.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Impressive.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''It is indeed. ''[&hellip;]'' It's a ''Shatter''-class WarMaster, the greatest class of warship in the Grysk armada.|Jixtus and Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />|translated=!}}
A massive [[Shatter-class WarMaster|''Shatter''-class WarMaster]], the ''FateSpinner'' was a [[warship]] part of the warlike [[Grysk]] [[species]]' [[Grysk armada|armada]] and the [[flagship]] of a Grysk [[Jixtus's fleet|fleet]] [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by the Grysk "[[Jixtus]]." The ship had a bow filled with clusters of spectrum lasers located in large [[weapon]]s clusters, along with more spectrum lasers and [[Missile launcher|missile tube]]s on the warships weapons shoulders. Lights marked the warships flanks and dorsal spine, which accented the ships length. It used [[dibber]]s to shield its [[missile]]s in combat and was able to [[Hangar|hold]] [[shuttle]]craft and [[Kilji escape pod|escape pods]] from [[Kilji war cruiser]]s, ships three or four times smaller than the [[WarMaster]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==History==
In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the ship's master, Jixtus, allied himself with the ruler of the Illumine, [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]]. The [[Kilji]] would ferry the Grysk across the [[Unknown Regions]] aboard the [[Kilji war cruiser]] ''[[Whetstone]]'', preventing the peoples of the region from knowing the size of their true enemies&mdash;the Grysks and Jixtus's fleet. Jixtus and Nakirre visited the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Mitth family]] [[Patriel]] of [[Avidich]] named [[Thistrian]], and the Grysk did not allow the generalirius to speak of the [[Kilji way of order and enlightenment|Kilji religion]] to the patriel before being ordered away by the [[Chiss]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

As a result, a confrontation between the allies took place, and Jixtus took Nakirre to a [[Unidentified star system (FateSpinner)|star system]] where all of the Kilji's questions would be answered. When they arrived, however, instead of Nakirre receiving his answers, the ''FateSpinner'' pointed all its [[weapon]]ry at the ''Whetstone''. Intimidating Nakirre with the destruction of the Kilhorde, the ''FateSpinner''{{'s}} owner continued using the Kilji as a tool, visiting several worlds in the Chiss Ascendancy, including the [[planet]] of [[Rhigar]], where the Grysk&ndash;aligned Chiss [[Clarr family]] resided.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

The Clarr [[Patriarch]], [[Clarr'ivl'exow]] [[Core name|"Rivlex"]], had accepted Grysk warships from Jixtus, housing them, including the ''FateSpinner'', in the outer [[Rhigal system|Rhigar system]]. The [[Light cruiser|Clarr light cruiser]] ''[[Orisson]]'' saw the starships, and the ''FateSpinner''{{'s}} commanders reported that fact to Rivlex and Jixtus. To prevent Roscu from leaking the Clarr&ndash;Grysk alliance, Jixtus planned to travel with the ''Whetstone'' to the ''Orisson''{{'s}} destination, the [[Ornfra system]]; however, the operative had another task to take care of and instead sent his ''Shatter''-class WarMaster. At [[Ornfra]], the WarMaster [[Sneak attack by the FateSpinner|attacked]] the ''Orisson'' with little effect before the [[cruiser]] escaped to [[hyperspace]]. Shortly after, Jixtus learnt that a [[Thrawn's fleet|fleet]] of Chiss warships had gathered at [[Schesa]] and traveled to the planet known to the Chiss as [[Sunrise]], prompting him to send his fleet to deal with the opposing Chiss formation. At Sunrise, the ''FateSpinner'' and the other ships of Jixtus's fleet engaged the fleet but were defeated by the Chiss, with the ''Shatter''-class WarMaster [[Self-destruct mechanism|self-destructing]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
