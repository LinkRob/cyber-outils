Twenty-seven adults dead and several children wounded in an ''Unbroken Clan&ndash;controlled district''. Doesn't take a Jedi Master to figure out who did it&hellip;''the Mourner's Wail''&mdash;revenge for an attack on a spice run, I heard.|T'onga, to Beilert Valance|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" />}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''bombing on Caviness IV''' was undertaken by the [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate]] in a [[Unbroken Clan district|district]] controlled by its rival, the [[Unbroken Clan]] criminal syndicate, on the astronomical object [[Caviness IV]]. The [[bomb]]ing resulted in the [[death]]s of twenty-seven adults and wounded several [[Youngling|children]]. Sometime after the event, [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s were present at the site of the bombing.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" />

Not long after the bombing, the [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]] mentioned it to fellow [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]], claiming<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" /> he<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-bounty-hunters-4-exclusive-preview|text=A Common Enemy Unites Old Friends in Marvel's ''Bounty Hunters'' #4 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref> would not see it on Imperial feeds. T'onga told Valance it was easy to work out who had carried out the bombing and that she had heard it was revenge for the Unbroken Clan [[Attack on a spice run|attacking]] a Mourner's Wail [[spice]] run. She then noted that the bombing's immediate cause did not really matter as the syndicates had been at [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate–Unbroken Clan war|war]] for [[Standard year|years]] after the bounty hunter [[Nakano Lash]] was believed to have [[Mission to Corellia (Nakano Lash's crew)|assassinated]] [[Krynthia|both]] syndicates' [[Khamus|heirs]], and pinned the deaths in the war on Lash's head.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
The bombing on Caviness IV was mentioned in the [[Bounty Hunters 4|fourth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" /> on [[August 19]], 2020. Prior to the release of the issue, the bombing was mentioned in a [https://web.archive.org/web/20210418230920/https://www.starwars.com/news/marvel-bounty-hunters-4-exclusive-preview preview article] of the issue that was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[August 12]] of the same year.<ref name="Preview" />
