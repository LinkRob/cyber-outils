'''Antigrav platforms''', also known as '''repulsor platforms''' or '''sky-disks''', were [[city]]-sized [[repulsorlift]]-powered platforms used to hold the population of the [[planet]] [[Protobranch]]. When a [[Protobranch solar storm|solar storm]] hit Protobranch, the repulsor technology failed, and the cities fell to the ground.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
