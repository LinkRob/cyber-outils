'''Arathab Fal''' was a [[Tarnab]] [[Gender|male]] who was a wanted murderer during the [[High Republic Era]].

==Biography==
Fal acted as the agent of an individual who claimed to be the upper ranks of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization, wishing to sell a weapon.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 2" /> Fal did this in collaboration with [[Chadra-Fan]] doctor [[Kisma Uttersond]].<ref name="Trail of Shadows 4">[[The High Republic: Trail of Shadows 4|''The High Republic: Trail of Shadows'' 4]]</ref> On the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], Fal met with the criminal [[Urk Panga]] and [[Keefar Branto]], a reformed criminal<ref name="Trail of Shadows 1" /> and undercover [[Galactic Republic]] operative.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 2" /> When the [[detective]] [[Sian Holt]] followed Branto to investigate why he was associating with criminals, Fal emerged from the shadows and attacked them, shooting Panga [[Death|dead]]. Branto and Holt attempted to flee, but the Tarnab also killed the former. Holt was knocked down, and though she managed to reach for her [[blaster]] and fire back at Fal before falling unconscious,<ref name="Trail of Shadows 1" /> the Tarnab escaped, moving on to [[Vorzyd V]], where he once again attempted to sell the item.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 2" /> Sometime later, Fal became a member of the Nihil, and served as one of [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]]'s [[She'ar]].<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> While a member of the She'ar, Fal was killed during an [[Assassination attempt on Marchion Ro|unsuccessful assassination attempt]] on Ro.<ref name="Temptation of the Force">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref>

{{Char-stub}}

==Skills and abilities==
Tharnab was a proficient warrior, he was skilled with a blaster but his true talent layed in close quarters combat and his weapon of choice was the [[Electrostaff]].<ref name="Trail of Shadows 1" /> Despite his large size he was a master of stealth and was known to attack his victims from the shadows.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 2" /><ref name="Trail of Shadows 3">[[The High Republic: Trail of Shadows 3|''The High Republic: Trail of Shadows'' 3]]</ref>
