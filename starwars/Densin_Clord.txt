'''Densin Clord''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[deck officer]] who worked at a [[spaceport]] on the [[planet]] [[Daiyu]]. In [[9 BBY]], he dismissed a traveler seeking help about a delayed [[transport]] when he was approached by the fugitive [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who explained that he was tracking a [[starship]]. Clord clarified to Kenobi that all signatures at Daiyu were blocked and walked away.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Excuse me. I'm looking for a ship. I was tracking its signature.''"<br />"''You're in Daiyu now. All signals in or out are blocked. People like their secrets out here.|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Clord|<ref name="Part II" />}}
[[File:BenKenobiDensinClord-OWKpII.png|thumb|left|300px|Obi-Wan Kenobi seeks assistance from Clord.]]
During the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Densin Clord worked as a [[deck officer]] and attendant of a [[spaceport]] on the [[planet]] [[Daiyu]].<ref name="Part II" /> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{KenobiDate|II}}</ref> he was approached by a traveler who had learned that their [[transport]] had been delayed due to weather on another world. The traveler sought help from Clord, but the deck [[officer]] denied that he could be of any assistance, clarifying that his [[datapad]] would only show the same case and that he could not change weather.<ref name="Part II" />

Clord waved off the traveler, only to be approached by another, the fugitive [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who had arrived [[Search for Princess Leia|in search]] of the [[Ambush on Alderaan|kidnapped]] [[Alderaanian]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. Kenobi explained to Clord that he was looking for a [[starship]] that's signature had been tracked to Daiyu. Clord, though, replied that all signatures going in or out of Daiyu were blocked as people liked to keep there secrets there. After his explanation, the deck officer walked away from the Jedi.<ref name="Part II" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I really&mdash; I don't have time for this. Just be going, okay?|Clord to a traveler|<ref name="Part II" />}}
Densin Clord was a [[human]] male with [[Color|light]] skin and brown [[hair]]. He was dismissive of any travelers he was unable to help, restarting his sentences as he explained the matter to them and considering their cases unworthy of his time.<ref name="Part II" />

==Equipment==
Clord wore a gray shirt and trousers and a brown tunic, hat and [[boot]]s. The deck officer also used an earpiece and a datapad.<ref name="Part II" />
