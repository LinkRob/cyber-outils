A '''Step 2 leveled reader''' that adapts the television series ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]'' will be published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press|Random House/Star Wars]] on [[January 5]], [[2027]] as part of their [[Step Into Reading]] series.<ref name="Edelweiss" />

==Publisher summary==
''An exciting Step 2 Step into Reading leveled reader based on the'' Star Wars: Ahsoka ''series streaming on Disney+!''

''Follow former Jedi Ahsoka Tano across the galaxy as she and her apprentice Sabine Wren defend the New Republic and continue their quest to find Grand Admiral Thrawn. Younglings will thrill to this Step 2 Step into Reading leveled reader that introduces the heroes and villains of the Disney+ series Star Wars: Ahsoka.''

''Step 2 readers use basic vocabulary and short sentences to tell simple stories. They are for children who recognize familiar words and can sound out new words with help.''
