The '''Carida incident''', also known as the '''Cardia job''', was a [[Mission (disambiguation)|mission]] taken by the [[Stinger Mantis crew]]<ref name="Survivor" /> sometime no later than [[9 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> The incident occurred when two members of the crew&mdash;the [[Klatooinian]] [[twin]]s [[Koob]] and [[Lizz]]&mdash;got ravenous and hungry. At some point during the incident [[Gabs]]&mdash;who was also a member of the crew&mdash; saved [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]]'s life and believed that he owed her a [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="Survivor" />

In [[9 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> during the [[Mission on Coruscant|mission]] on [[Coruscant]], Gabs urged Kestis to hurry up to avoid having another Carida incident after the twins began to look ravenous. Later during the same mission, the newer member of the group [[Bode Akuna]] mentioned how hungry the Klatooinian twins looked, to which Kestis replied telling Akuna to remind him to tell him about the Carida job sometime.<ref name="Survivor" />
