'''Agamar''' was a barren, rocky [[planet]] located in the [[Agamar system]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]] in [[Standard Galactic Grid|grid]] coordinates M-5. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[General]] [[Kalani]] was ordered by [[Count]] [[Dooku]] to retreat from [[Onderon]] to Agamar following the Separatist [[Onderonian Civil War|defeat]] on Onderon. Later during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Rebel cell/Canon|rebel cell]] known as the [[Spectres]] [[Mission to Agamar (2 BBY)|confronted]] Kalani on Agamar, eventually teaming up to defeat the Empire, a mutual opponent.

==Description==
Agamar was a [[planet]]<ref name="Last Battle Guide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/the-last-battle-episode-guide|text="The Last Battle" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars Rebels}}</ref> located in the [[Agamar system]]<ref name="The Last Battle" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="TFABG" /> otherwise known as the Mirogoshir system.<ref name="HC 1">{{HelmetCollectionCite|1|Databank A-Z}}</ref> The planet was located in grid square M-5 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="TFABG" /> Agamar had a surface covered in rocky terrain, with a cold climate.<ref name="Databank Agamar" /> However, [[farmer]]s were able to work on Agamar.<ref name="Star Wars 21" /> It had an [[atmosphere]] breathable to [[human]]s and [[Lasat]]s.<ref name="The Last Battle" />

==History==
===Clone Wars===
In [[20 BBY]], the third [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[planet]]<ref name="Last Battle Guide" /> was under the control of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. [[Count]] [[Dooku]] ordered [[General]] [[Kalani]] to retreat to Agamar following his defeat at the [[Onderonian Civil War]] to the [[Onderon rebels]].<ref name="Tipping Points" /> Because of Kalani's override of the [[droid]] shutdown signal, an isolated [[Agamar garrison|Separatist holdout]] remained on the planet until well into the [[Imperial Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="The Last Battle" />

===Reign of the Empire===
One ranger on Agamar, [[Izak Anzio]], left the farming life to enlist in the [[Imperial Military]] by joining an [[Imperial Academy]].<ref name="Star Wars 21" />

[[File:Rebels_and_droids_vs_Imperials.png|thumb|left|200px|The Spectres and Kalani team up to defeat Imperial forces.]]
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the former [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] and the [[Spectres]] traveled to Agamar to [[Mission to Agamar (2 BBY)|salvage]] [[proton bomb]]s for [[Phoenix Cell]]. Rex along with [[Kanan Jarrus]], [[Ezra Bridger]], and [[Garazeb Orrelios]] were quickly captured by Kalani and his [[B1-series battle droid]]s. Kalani forced the rebels to take part in a combat simulation against his [[Separatist holdouts|Separatist holdout]] in order to end the Clone Wars as a Separatist victory. The rebels managed to survive the battle droids and Ezra managed to convince Kalani and Rex to make peace. The rebels and Separatist droids then joined forces to escape an Imperial assault force with the help of [[C1-10P|Chopper]]. After escaping the planet in separate [[Sheathipede-class transport shuttle|''Sheathipede''-class transport shuttles]] both Rex and Kalani agreed it to be the true end to the war. The droid calculated the rebels had less than a one percent chance to stage a successful rebellion against the Empire, and parted ways from them.<ref name="The Last Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
Agamar first appeared in ''[[The Farlander Papers]]'', a companion book written by [[Rusel DeMaria]]<ref name="The Farlander Papers">{{StoryCite|story=The Farlander Papers|book=Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)}}</ref> that was included in the limited edition version of the [[1993]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] video game [[Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']].<ref name="X-Wing">[[Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']]</ref> It became [[canon]] when it was mentioned in "[[Tipping Points]]," the fifth episode of the canon animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]],<ref name="Tipping Points" /> which originally aired on [[October 27]], [[2012]].<ref name="Tipping Points air date">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep505|text="Tipping Points" – ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide|nolive=1}}</ref>
