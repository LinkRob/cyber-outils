The '''Alnarian Collective''' was a group<ref name="Vengeance" /> that originated from<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=Alnarian Collective|system=the [[Alnaria system]]}}</ref> the [[Colonies]] [[Alnaria system]].<ref name="AVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> By [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> a delegation from the Collective was set to meet [[Jedi Master]] [[Fitan]] and her [[Padawan]], [[Oliviah Zeveron]]. When the two parties greeted, Fitan farted vociferously, [[Telekinesis|using]] [[the Force]] to simulate flatulence-caused air flow in her [[Jedi apparel|Jedi robes]]. Fitan and Zeveron then giggled like [[youngling]]s in front of the Collective.<ref name="Vengeance" /> In that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Zeveron recounted the toot to fellow Padawan [[Matthea Cathley]].<ref name="Vengeance" />
