'''Evor Haris''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who served as a peacekeeper on the [[Planet|world]] [[Enujut]], welcoming all visitors. After Haris allowed a group of [[Zemerin]] onto Enujut, they began to create negativity among the world's residents in order to feed off of it. After a [[Unidentified Cybot Galactica protocol droid|protocol droid]] discovered the truth and informed Haris, the peacekeeper tricked the Zemerin into boarding their [[starship]], and the [[protocol droid]] took off to return the Zemerin to their [[homeworld]].<ref name="JTC-E2" />
