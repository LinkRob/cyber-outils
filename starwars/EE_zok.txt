{{Quote|My name is EE'zok. I've been sent personally, by Lady Qi'ra herself, on an important recruitment mission.|EE'zok|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
'''EE'zok''' was a [[human]] member of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate who worked as a [[contract broker]] in the [[city]] of [[Bestine (city)|Bestine]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> they were tasked by the syndicate's leader, [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]], to recruit [[Ays Varga]], a [[superweapon]]s [[engineer]] who defected from the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
When Varga stopped responding to communications, EE'zok hired the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] to find her. Varga had intended to make a deal with [[pirate]]s, who subsequently sold her out to the Empire, but Vess was able to rescue the engineer and bring her to the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
EE'zok appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. In the syndicate quest "Defector," after the player rescues Varga, they can convince her to either join Crimson Dawn or the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]], which is also interested in the engineer. If the player sides with Crimson Dawn, EE'zok has them take Varga to [[Eleera Soi]]; if the Hutts were chosen, EE'zok contacts them after the quest's conclusion, displeased.<ref name="Outlaws" />
