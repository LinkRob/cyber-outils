'''Flight gloves''' were a type of [[glove]] worn by [[pilot]]s. During the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="HC26">{{HelmetCollectionCite|26|Helmets}}</ref> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[rebel pilot]]s of [[Gray Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gray Squadron]] wore flight gloves as part of their [[flight suit]].<ref name="HC26" /> [[Jessika Pava]] and other [[Resistance pilot|pilots]] of the [[Resistance]], a successor to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], wore padded flight gloves during the [[First Order-Resistance War]].<ref name="HC50">{{HelmetCollectionCite|50|Helmets}}</ref>
