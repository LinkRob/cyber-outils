In [[1 ABY/Legends|1 ABY]], an '''Ambush''' occurred on the planet [[Shumari]] by a [[Tusken Raider/Legends|Tusken Raider]] on [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]], intending to avenge his Tusken group that was wiped out by Vader, then known as Anakin Skywalker, on 22 BBY.

==Prelude==
In [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]], [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]], then a [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]], had left for [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] after he received [[Dream/Legends|nightmares]] of his mother, [[Shmi Skywalker Lars/Legends|Shmi Skywalker]], dying. After tracking his mother down to a Tusken Raider village when he learned that Shmi, then taking the name of Shmi Skywalker Lars after [[Marriage/Legends|marrying]] [[Cliegg Lars/Legends|Cliegg Lars]], had been abducted by the Tuskens after they mistook her for a [[Witch/Legends|witch]], he attempted to rescue her. When she died in his arms, Skywalker took vengeance on the village and wiped out all but one of its inhabitants. The sole survivor would hunt Vader down for some time.

==The Ambush==
When Vader arrived at Sundari, he was ambushed by the Raider, who also utilized a modified [[Gaderffii/Legends|gaderffii]] stick as his [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]]. Although the Raider had managed to severely damage Vader and nearly killed him, the Raider ultimately ended up killed.

==Behind the scenes==
The ambush first appeared in the manga ''[[Perfect Evil]]'', where Vader took his mortally wounded apprentice, [[Tao (Sith Apprentice)|Tao]], to his homeworld to be buried. Although the events of the manga were declared non-canon, the duel itself was briefly alluded to in the [[August 26]], [[2013]] blog series [[Barely Tolerable: Alien Henchmen of the Empire]], under the entry for [[KkH'Oar'Rrhr]].
