'''Case file 127456''' was part of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s official records and was related to the disappearance of [[Jedi Master]] [[Sifo-Dyas|Sifo-Dias]]. It had been sealed by the [[Office of the Chancellor]], so when [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] was looking for information on Sifo-Dias after his [[lightsaber]] was found on the [[Oba Diah moon]], Yoda went to [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] to ask about the file. Palpatine claimed that the records had been sealed during the tenure of his predecessor, [[Finis Valorum]].<ref name="Lost One">{{TCW|The Lost One}}</ref>

[[Case file 127456]], specifically translated, says:

"Jedi master [[Sifo-Dyas|Sifo-Dias]] died while trying to negotiate a peace between tribes on [[Felucia]]. He was aboard [[775519|Jedi shuttle T-6 77519]] when it crashed. Much of the information surrounding his death remains confidential under the Office of the [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]]."
