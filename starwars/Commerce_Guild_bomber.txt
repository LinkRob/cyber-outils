The '''Commerce Guild bomber''' was a type of [[bomber]] that saw use by the [[Commerce Guild]] and later the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval forces]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. During the war, which saw the corporation side with the Confederacy, Guild bombers were used alongside [[Commerce Guild corvette|Guild corvettes]], [[Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class light destroyers]], and [[Diamond-class cruiser|''Diamond''-class cruisers]] to defend [[planet]]s off the end of the [[Perlemian Trade Route]] such as [[Castell]], [[Escarte]], [[Felucia]], [[Mooga]], and [[Minntooine]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Commerce Guild bombers were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Commerce Guild bomber/Legends|Commerce Guild bombers]] were introduced in the [[2002]] [[Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds: Clone Campaigns|''Clone Campaigns'']] expansion pack for the<ref name="Clone Campaigns">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds: Clone Campaigns]]''</ref> [[2001]]<ref name="Galactic Battlegrounds">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds]]''</ref> [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds]]''.<ref name="Clone Campaigns" />
