'''Bantamweight Freestyle Class''' was a classification of [[gladiator]].<ref name="Smuggler">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> At some point by [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The [[Grakkus Arena]] was shut down and [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi]] was arrested in [[Star Wars (2015) 12|''Star Wars'' 12]] which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[0 ABY]]. Therefore, the Wookiee warriors must have been delivered by that date.</ref> the [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[Wookiee]] gladiator "[[Snarrl the Feral]]" of [[Kashyyyk Group B9]] was a member of the classification. A receipt for the shipment of the Wookiee by the [[Xonti Brothers]] to [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus]] the [[Hutt]]'s [[Grakkus Arena|arena]] on the [[moon]] of [[Nar Shaddaa]] included Snarrl the Feral's class alongside describing them as a "feeder specimen."<ref name="Smuggler" />
