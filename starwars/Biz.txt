'''Biz''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Lurmen]] who was the [[chief]] of [[Unidentified Lurmen village|a Lurmen village]] on the [[planet]] [[Qalydon]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. He was the [[Marriage|husband]] of [[Elan (Lurmen)|Elan]] and the [[Parent|father]] of [[Maynar]], who aided the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] in liberating their village from the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

==Biography==
Biz was the Chief of a [[Lurmen]] [[Unidentified Lurmen village|village]] on the planet [[Qalydon]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. His people had lived on the planet for generations worshiping [[the Force]] with [[kyber crystal]]s. Biz had a wife named [[Elan (Lurmen)|Elan]] and the two had a daughter named [[Maynar]], who did not share her father's appreciation for the Force and kyber crystals<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

When the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] traveled to Qalydon during their [[Quest to build the Arrowhead|quest]] to build the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']] starship, Biz spotted their ship in the distance and remarked that it did not look [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]]. The Lurmen villages initially thought the Freemakers were hostile when they explained that they had come seeking kyber crystals. Following a brief confrontation, [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]] managed to convince the Lurmen that they were not Imperials.<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

Later that night, Chief Biz and his wife Elan hosted the Freemakers and their droid [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]], educating them about the history of their community and the kyber crystals. Biz said that his people could not give up their heirlooms but was unable to offer information on where they could find other crystals.<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

Shortly later, the village was raided by Imperial forces led by [[Imperial officer]]s [[Durpin]] and [[Plumestriker]]. The Imperials seized their kyber crystals for the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. However, the youngest Freemaker [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan]] used the Freemakers' ship ''[[StarScavenger]]''{{'s}} [[laser cannon]]s to drive the Imperials way. The Imperials fled aboard their [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] which crashed into a nearby mountain.<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

While the Freemakers embarked on a [[Mission to Qalydon|mission]] to recover the kyber crystals, Biz stayed behind with Roger and Elan to help rebuild the village. Roger tried to demonstrate his carpentry skills by repairing a wall, which kept falling. Elan called for their daughter Maynar but she had accompanied the Freemakers on their quest to recover the crystals from the Imperials.<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

With the help of Maynar, the Freemakers managed to recover the crystals from Durpin and Plumestriker despite being attacked by the Imperial [[Hunter droid/LEGO|hunter droid]] [[M-OC/LEGO|M-OC]] and the Imperials escaping a second time on their ship. Following the rescue mission, Biz thanked the Freemakers for recovering his people's crystals and offered to give them to the Freemakers. Unwilling to deprive the Lurmen of their crystals, Rowan politely declined their offer.<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

==Personality and traits==
Biz was a [[Gender|male]] Lurmen with grey fur, brown eyes, and white beard. He supported the Lurmen villagers' traditional way of life. He believed in the Force and revered his people's traditional kyber crystal necklaces, which were passed down from generation to generation. Biz did not have a high opinion of the battle droid Roger's carpentry skills.<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

Biz gained the respect of the Freemakers after they recovered his people's crystals. He offered them the crystals but Rowan declined, not wanting to deprive the Lurmen of their heirlooms.<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />
