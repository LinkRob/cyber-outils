'''Arca Yroca''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[jockey]] who rode [[fathier]]s at the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|racetrack]] in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. In around [[34 ABY]], he rode in a [[Fathier racing|race]] on the [[crime lord]] [[Sturg Ganna]]'s fathier [[Kessel Runner]] and in another on the fathier [[Hoth Stuff]].

==Biography==
The [[human]] [[Gender|male]] Arca Yroca worked as a [[jockey]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. In around [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[34 ABY]]. As [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] carries on immediately from the events of ''The Force Awakens'', it can be assumed to take place in the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> he participated in several [[fathier]] races at the [[Canto Bight]] [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|racetrack]] in the same [[Standard day|night]].<ref name="TLJ" /><ref name="Rose" />

In the fifth race of the night, Yroca rode at first post on a fathier named [[Kessel Runner]] that belonged to the [[crime lord]] [[Sturg Ganna|Big Sturg Ganna]].<ref name="Rose" /> In another race, the jockey rode the fathier [[Hoth Stuff]] and whipped it with an [[electrocrop]]. Shortly after the race, all of the fathiers kept in the racetrack's stables were released by the [[Resistance]] agents [[Finn]] and [[Rose Tico]].<ref name="TLJ" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:Hoth_Stuff.png|thumb|right|Yroca rode Hoth Stuff on Cantonica|250px]]
Yroca had fair skin.<ref name="TLJ" />

==Equipment==
The jockey wore a [[Color|orange]] [[lycresh]] racing [[Clothing|outfit]] designed to be comfortable but also sleek and lightweight to reduce air drag. He also wore a orange [[Hat|helmet]] with his sponsor's logo emblazoned upon it. The jockey used an electrocrop to whip his fathiers during races.<ref name="TLJVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arca Yroca first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="TLJ" /> which was released on [[December 15]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-viii-to-open-december-15-2017|text=''Star Wars: Episode VIII'' to Open December 15, 2017}}</ref> The character was not named in the film,<ref name="TLJ" /> but received identification in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> and released in conjunction with the film.<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/new-star-wars-the-last-jedi-books-and-more-revealed-at-sdcc-2017|text=New ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Books and More Revealed at SDCC 2017}}</ref>
