{{Quote|A mouth full of food ain't gonna put Alderaan back together again, but these pies may be just enough to send us back there, if even for a few savory seconds.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
'''Aldera Meatpies''' were a type of savory [[meatpie]] from the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. A [[Unidentified chef (Tepasi)|student]] of the [[University of Alderaan]] in the planet's [[capital]] [[city]], [[Aldera]], learned to make the [[pie]]s while studying and later served them while working as a [[chef]] on<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> the planet<ref name="Batuu">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Tepasi]] after Alderaan was [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> The [[Artiodac]] chef [[Strono Tuggs]] ordered an [[Foodstuff|Aldera Meatpie]] from the former student on Tepasi during the culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he made<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> 

Having not realized that the pie originated on Alderaan when he ordered it, Tuggs learned its origins from the chef and later included it as an [[Alderaanian]] recipe in his third cookbook, ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> which was published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> In the book, he described the pies as magnificent and claimed that although they could not bring Alderaan back, the pies could send the eater back there for a few [[second]]s.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aldera Meatpies were created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the pies makes use of [[Wikipedia:Vegetable oil|vegetable oil]], diced [[Wikipedia:Yellow onion|yellow onions]], a minced [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic clove]], [[Wikipedia:Tomato paste|tomato paste]], [[Wikipedia:Ground beef|ground beef]], [[Wikipedia:Chorizo|pork chorizo]], [[Wikipedia:Chili powder|chili powder]], [[Wikipedia:Cumin|cumin]], [[Wikipedia:Paprika|paprika]], [[Wikipedia:Garlic powder|garlic powder]], [[Wikipedia:Onion powder|onion powder]], [[Wikipedia:Kosher salt|kosher salt]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]], [[Wikipedia:Shortcrust pastry|pre-made pie dough]], and a large [[Wikipedia:Eggs as food|egg]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
