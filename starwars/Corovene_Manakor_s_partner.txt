They then visited [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]] and asked him to help them to get to the planet [[Lysatra]]. Calrissian organized the transport, but the [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] occupied [[Cloud City]]. Eventually, [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]] helped them escape from the planet [[Bespin]].<ref name="FACPOV ESB" />
