A '''climate disruption array''' was a technological device that could be deployed in the [[orbit]] of a [[planet]] to provoke storms and [[hurricane]]s on its surface from [[Realspace|space]]. [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] could deploy these arrays.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Description==
These arrays were [[satellite]]s modified to provoke storms once the beam reached through the [[planet]]. Once it hit the surface, powerful storms were created and caused devastation on a planetwide scale.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==History==
{{Quote|Operation: Cinder is to begin at once. Resistance. Rebellion. Defiance. These are concepts that cannot be allowed to persist. You are but one of many tools by which these ideas shall be burned away.|A posthumous message from Emperor Palpatine|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
The earliest known [[Fondor Research and Development division|development]] for the disruption arrays could be traced back to [[Fondor]], a critical [[Imperial Navy|naval]] complex for the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Fondor Databank">{{Databank|fondor|Fondor}}</ref>

Following the [[Battle of Endor]], which saw the [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]],<ref name="Return of the Jedi">{{Film|VI}}</ref> [[Operation: Cinder]] was activated. Palpatine's [[Sentinel (Contingency)|Sentinel]] [[droid]]s were sent to "selected" [[Imperial officer]]s including [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]]<ref name="Battlefront II" /> and [[Captain]] [[Lerr Duvat]].<ref name="SE2" /> As part of his contingency plan, Palpatine ordered the chosen officers to unleash the power of the climate disruption arrays on the targeted worlds, seeking to cause destruction across the galaxy. In the aftermath of the Battle of Endor, [[Research Station 9]], a [[Tarkin Initiative]] facility on [[Endor]], contained a [[hologram]] of the disruption array.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

[[File:OperationCinder.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Climate disruption arrays being deployed at Vardos.]]
Admiral Versio deployed the arrays above his [[Homeworld|home planet]], [[Vardos]]. This saw the defection of two [[Inferno Squad]] members, his daughter [[Commander]] [[Iden Versio]] and [[Agent]] [[Del Meeko]], who would not stand to serve a regime that destroyed worlds that they had fought to protect. Following the [[Skirmish on Vardos|skirmish]] on the planet, the satellites had laid waste to the once prosperous planet and Iden and Del had escaped to join the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

[[File:ClimateDisruptionArray-ShatteredEmpire2.png|thumb|right|200px|The arrays deployed above Naboo]]
Captain Duvat was sent to the Emperor's home planet of [[Naboo]], were he was to [[Battle of Theed|scour]] the planet with electrical storms and massive hurricanes.<ref name="SE2" /> [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] scrambled the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Rebel fleet]] to destroy the arrays, and a force lead by General [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] eventually succeeded.<ref name="Shattered Empire 3">[[Shattered Empire 3|''Shattered Empire'' 3]]</ref>

The planet [[Abednedo (planet)|Abednedo]] was also [[Attack on Abednedo|targeted]] by climate disruption arrays.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> This led the [[Abednedo]] people to support the [[New Republic]] and the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Abednedo Databank">{{Databank|abednedo|Abednedo}}</ref>

While other numerous other planets were targeted by Operation: Cinder, ones with unique ecologies were subjected to different climate control weapons; [[Nacronis]], for example, was hit with atmospheric [[vortex detonator]]s that amplified its natural siltstorms to apocalyptic levels.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref>

===Legacy===
In [[34 ABY]], Iden returned to Vardos with [[Shriv Suurgav]] and her daughter, [[Zay Versio|Zay]]. Suurgav noticed what he thought was snow, but Iden revealed it was actually atmospheric ash, leftover from the storms and fires caused by the climate disruption arrays thirty years prior.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
