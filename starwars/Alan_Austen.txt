'''Alan Austen''' was an actor who portrayed a number of characters in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. His characters include a [[Hoth/Legends|Hoth]] [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]], a [[Rebel pilot/Legends|Rebel pilot]] (later identified as [[Kulbart Zamoon]]), an Imperial [[Stormtrooper/Legends|Stormtrooper]] in the [[Carbon-freezing chamber/Legends|Carbon-freezing chamber]] scene and a [[Bespin Wing Guard/Legends|Bespin Wing Guard]] (later identified as [[Alec Mard]]). Austen has also been in various other films.
