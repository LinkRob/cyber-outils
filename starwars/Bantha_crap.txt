'''Bantha crap''' was a phrase used to describe nonsense or false statements. For instance, during [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra's]] escape from [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on [[Tython]], she mentioned "the hopes of forgiveness and a happy ending and all the other bantha crap someone like me was never gonna get."<ref name="Aphra 2016 40">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 40|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 40]]</ref> Later, when recounting the event to [[Sana Starros]], Starros trusted her account, because Aphra's tone revealed when she was "spinning bantha crap" and attempting to make herself seem impressive.<ref name="Aphra 2020 13">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 13|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 13]]</ref>

The term could also used as part of a longer phrase, such as:

*'''Everything one touches turns to bantha crap''': Everything a person does, goes wrong or turns into disaster.<ref name="High Republic 2021 12">[[The High Republic (2021) 12|''The High Republic'' (2021) 12]]</ref>
*'''Feel like bantha crap''': To feel very unwell.<ref name="Beyond the Clouds">{{StoryCite|story=Beyond the Clouds|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>
*'''Full of bantha crap''': Used to describe someone who is not telling the truth.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The word "bantha crap" is similar to the real-world vulgar [[Wikipedia:American English|American English]] noun "bullcrap," which refers to something untrue or worthless,<ref name="Bullcrap">{{WebCite|url= https://dictionary.reverso.net/english-definition/bullcrap|text=bullcrap|quote=something worthless or untrue <small>US</small>|work=[[Wikipedia:Reverso (language tools)|''Reverso English Dictionary'']]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/dvKZC|archivedate=2025-07-10}}</ref> and is a synonym for "bullshit."<ref>{{WebCite|url= https://synonyms.reverso.net/synonym/en/bullcrap|text=Synonyms for bullcrap in English|quote=bullshit|work=[[Wikipedia:Reverso (language tools)|''Reverso English Dictionary'']]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/NOx6d|archivedate=2025-07-10}}</ref> Much like "bantha crap,"<ref name="Solo" /> "bullcrap" is a compound formed from the name of an animal, combined with the word "crap."<ref>{{WebCite|url= https://rabbitique.com/profile/en/bullcrap|text=bullcrap|quote= Compound from English bull + English crap.|work=Rabbitique|archiveurl= https://archive.ph/Yv7Op|archivedate=2025-07-10}}</ref>
