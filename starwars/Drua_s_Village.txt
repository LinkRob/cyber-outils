'''Drua's Village'''<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> was one of numerous remote settlements on the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]].<ref name="Lords of the Sith" /> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> it was where the [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|girl]] [[Drua]] welcomed [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], and the [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guardsman]] [[Erstin Deez]]&mdash;completely unaware of their identities as [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]]&mdash;during their [[mission to Ryloth]].<ref name="Lords of the Sith" />

Vader speculated that Drua's Village, as well as other remote settlements on Ryloth, were formed by either displaced [[refugee]]s of the [[Clone Wars]] or by escaped [[mining]] [[Slavery|slaves]]. Drua informed the Imperial trio that her village had 38 residents, which included [[Naria]] and her [[Reproduction|unborn child]]. Some two [[Standard year|years]] prior, [[Drua's mother]] was also a member of the village before her [[Death|passing]].<ref name="Lords of the Sith" />

After the village witnessed a battle between the [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] and the [[Free Ryloth Movement]], the Emperor commanded Vader to kill every last villager&mdash;which included Drua, [[Drua's grandfather|her grandfather]], [[Mala (Twi'lek)|Mala]], the pregnant Naria, and [[Narmn]]&mdash;to which Vader obliged.<ref name="Lords of the Sith" />
