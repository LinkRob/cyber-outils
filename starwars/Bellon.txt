'''Bellon''' was a [[Devaronian]] [[chef]] serving at [[Presidential manor|Jamie Brasson's estate]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He had a fiery temper, and committed over ten [[Death|murders]] in the [[Begamor underworld]]. However, due to his proficiency at meat cookery, the [[Color|red]]-skinned Devaronian was sheltered by the [[President]] [[Jamie Brasson]] of [[Begamor]] and given the responsibility of preparing meat at Brasson's estate.<ref name="TVoSD" />
