The '''Elite AT-TE''' was an upgraded model of the [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]] that sported tougher armor and more powerful weaponry. After the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] operated these vehicles and supplied them to the [[Tatooine rebel cell]]. [[Colonel]] [[Jennica Pierce]] had a great deal of faith in the Elite AT-TE's capabilities, believing that they could represent a turning point in the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Commander" />
