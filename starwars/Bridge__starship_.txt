[[File:ISD_bridge.jpg|thumb|right|321px|The bridge of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Star Destroyer]].]]
{{Quote|Clear the bridge. Escort the officers down to the hangar.|[[Poe Dameron]]|<ref name="Episode 8">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>}}
The '''bridge''' was a structure on nearly all [[warship]]s and larger [[starship]]s. A bridge served as the central hub for control and direction of the ship and ship activities. It housed crucial navigation and astrogation [[computer]]s, and served as the [[officer]]s' platform by which all orders were conveyed; communications and ship sterrage as well as for issuing strategy and battle maneuvers.<ref name="LOTJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>
