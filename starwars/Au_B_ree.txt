'''Au B'ree''' was a [[Devaronian]] worker at the [[Fekunda Outpost]] when it came under the control of [[Commander]] [[Zhou]] and his [[stormtrooper]] [[squad]]. When the [[Galactic Empire]] arrived at the outpost and demanded the residents provide shipments of [[phareen]] for Imperial supply lines,<ref name="Forces of Destiny—Hera" /> she<ref name="Raising a Rebellion" /> was initially hesitant to engage in acts of [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|rebellion]] against them. While [[Burl]] was invigorated at the thought of fighting back, Au B'ree attempted to remind her coworker that the Empire would retaliate against the entire community&mdash;she preferred to believe the Imperial troops would leave if they complied.<ref name="Forces of Destiny—Hera" />

At Burl's request, however, [[Hera Syndulla]] [[Mission to Fekunda Outpost|arrived]] and encouraged Au B'ree to embrace her natural leadership qualities, praising the Devaronian's levelheadedness and ability to set realistic goals. Syndulla shocked her by suggesting that, should a resistance effort emerge on Fekunda, Au B'ree was the most suitable leader for the cause. She assisted in Syndulla's plan to free the outpost of Imperial control by frustrating the stormtroopers' efforts in the phareen processing. Following their success, she looked forward to working with the Rebellion in the future.<ref name="Forces of Destiny—Hera" />
