The '''Bimms''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] characterized by their [[Hair|fur]], feather-like whiskers and long, floppy [[ear]]s. They spoke a [[language]] called [[Bimmini]]. A Bimm named [[Uumay Hawlatha]] served as a [[Jedi Master]] in the late [[Republic Era]],<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> while [[Voh]] was an elderly Bimm [[Gender|male]] who served as the [[seneschal]] of the [[Jedi outpost]] [[Sanctuary Mount]] on the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]].<ref name="The Living Force" /> A different species, similar to diminutive [[human]]s, was also called [[Bimm]]s.<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
