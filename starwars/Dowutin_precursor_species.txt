The [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Dowutin]] [[species]] evolved from a '''species''' of non-sentient, [[Diet|omnivorous]] herd animals native to [[Dowut]],<ref name="AotG" /> a [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]. The animals were migratory and moved across their [[homeworld]]'s [[Temperature|frozen]] plains in search of the substantial amounts of [[Foodstuff|sustenance]] needed to sustain their large bodies.<ref name="Knights of Fate">''[[Knights of Fate]]''</ref> This included [[tuber]]s, [[insect]] nests and dens of [[Sleep|hibernating]] [[feek]]s, all of which they used their spiked [[Limb|fingers]] and chin tusks to dig for. These digging elements were retained in the morphology of the Dowutin during their evolution.<ref name="AotG" /> Following the evolution of the Dowutin, large herd animals not dissimilar to the precursor species still roamed the frozen plains of Dowut.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />

==Behind the scenes==
The precursor species to the Dowutin were first mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="AotG" />
