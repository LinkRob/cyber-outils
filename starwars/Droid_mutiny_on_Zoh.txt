Sometime during the [[Imperial Era]], the [[EV-series supervisor droid]] [[N-3RO]] led a '''mutiny''' against his master, [[Jek-14]], and Jek's newfound allies, the [[Freemakers]], on the [[planet]] [[Zoh]]. The uprising was foiled by the [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR]], who later recorded the event in his "[[droidography]]."<ref name="Droidography" />
