'''Chris Cassidy''' is the author of four short stories, three co-authored with [[Tish Eggleston Pahl|Tish Pahl]]&mdash;"[[A Credit for Your Thoughts]]," "[[Hutt and Seek]]," and "[[Simple Tricks]]"&mdash;and one solo tale, "[[Precipice]]."

She [[Tuckerization|tuckerized]] fan [[Tracy Duncan]] as [[Dunc T'racen]] in "Hutt and Seek," and helped make a custom action figure of [[Kettch]] for [[Aaron Allston]].
