The '''Exterior''' was a far-flung [[sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. In the aftermath of the [[Rebellion on Akiva]], it was the only true Outer Rim sector that remained under the control of the [[Galactic Empire]], in the person of [[Grand Moff]] [[Randd]],<ref name="Life Debt" /> despite the efforts made to break his control, which the [[New Republic]] donated some of its finest [[squadron]]s to helping accomplish. These endeavours&mdash;along with the [[Liberation of Sullust|liberating]] the [[planet]] [[Sullust]] and defending [[Naboo]] from [[Invasions of Naboo|Imperial invasion]]&mdash;left [[New Republic Defense Force|Republic forces]] so stretched that [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] feared that if the [[Ubrik Adelhard's Imperial remnant|rogue Imperial remnant]] gathered beneath Grand [[Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] began expanding their [[Anoat sector|territories]], the New Republic would lack the [[New Republic Defense Fleet|naval strength]] to defeat the main Empire, which was regrouping on [[Jakku]] for a [[Battle of Jakku|final offensive]].<ref name="Republic Under Siege 4">[[Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege 4|''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 4]]</ref>
