The '''Ancients''' were a group which predated the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Galactic Republic]]. The group was based on<ref name="Entombed" /> [[Skara Nal]]<ref name="Skara Nal">{{Databank|skara-nal|Skara Nal}}</ref> in the [[Kaldar Trinary system]]. They created an ancient four-legged [[droid]] [[Skara Nal Mech|walker]] armed with a [[superlaser]], that was eventually buried on the planet where they were based on previously. The Ancients kept Skara Nal inert by using the [[Heart of the Mountain]], an ancient [[crystal]]. The group also created a [[Omega's Compass|compass]] to locate Skara Nal and navigate through the various booby traps in the underground tunnels leading to Skara Nal.<ref name="Entombed" /> Between [[19 BBY]] and [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|205}}</ref> the group was shrouded in mystery with one of their creations, Skara Nal, becoming a [[pirate]] legend.<ref name="Entombed" />
