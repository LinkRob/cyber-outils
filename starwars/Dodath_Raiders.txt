The '''Dodath Raiders''' were a group who ran [[slave-hauler]]s during the [[Standard month|months]] following the [[Battle of Endor]]. [[Han Solo]] ran foul of the Dodath Raiders during his and [[Chewbacca]]'s attempt to liberate [[Kashyyyk]] from the [[Galactic Empire]]. The Raiders' slave-hauler came up on Solo, firing on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' and taking out its comm array.<ref name="ALD">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', Chapter 17</ref>
