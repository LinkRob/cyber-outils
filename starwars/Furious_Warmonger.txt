The '''Furious Warmonger''' was a costume sometimes worn by the [[Wookiee]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Grozz]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Mighty Champion|standard costume]]. This costume resembled real ceremonial [[armor]] worn by Wookiee [[Warmonger]]s, said to be leaders among the [[Wookiee warrior]]s on [[Kashyyyk]]. [[Cerulean gemstone]]s were embedded within the center of the chest plate and [[helmet]] of this costume.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Furious Warmonger was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Grozz, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Super Battle Clubs]].<ref name="Hunters" />
