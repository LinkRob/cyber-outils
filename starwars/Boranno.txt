'''Boranno''' was a beverage that was purchasable by the tank as a direct import in [[Mirogana Market]] in the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an announcer speaking [[Huttese]] while advertising various beverages over the market's announcement system advertised that there were fine vintages of Boranno and [[Yfranti]] available.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Boranno was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
