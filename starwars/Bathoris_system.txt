The '''Bathoris system''' was an [[Inner Rim Territories]] [[star system]] that lay in grid square N-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Bathoris]]. According to legend, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Bran Ath'Morath]] defeated the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Caldoth]] when Caldoth launched a [[Attack on Bathoris|surprise attack]] on Bathoris's [[Jedi temple (Bathoris)|Jedi temple]].<ref name="The Gilded Cage" /> Around [[362 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Pathfinders]]'' take place twenty [[Standard year|years]] after the events of [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|''The High Republic'' Phase II]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[Galactic Republic]] maintained a [[Bathoris listening station|listening station]]<ref name="Pathfinders 2" /> on Bathoris.<ref name="Pathfinders 3">[[The High Republic Adventures – Pathfinders 3|''The High Republic Adventures – Pathfinders'' 3]]</ref>
