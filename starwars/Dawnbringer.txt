The '''''Dawnbringer''''' was a [[Defender-class cruiser|''Defender''-class cruiser]] that was the sister [[starship]] of the ''[[Vesper]]'', both serving in the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]]. The ''Dawnbringer'' was built at the start of the [[New Republic]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|the-dawnbringer|The Dawnbringer}}</ref> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> By around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Ahsoka series date">{{AhsokaSeriesDate}}</ref> the ''Dawnbringer'' was under the command of [[Captain]] [[Girard]], and was tasked by [[Fleet Command (New Republic)|Fleet Command]] to end [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]]'s [[Incident on Seatos|unauthorized mission]] on [[Seatos]] in the [[Denab system]]. To complete this task, the ''Dawnbringer'' was accompanied by two other [[cruiser]]s.<ref name="Part Five" />
