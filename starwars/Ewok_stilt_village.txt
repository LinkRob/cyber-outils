Several '''stilt villages''' built and inhabited by [[Ewok]]s existed on the [[Endor]] Moon, some of which were located on [[Lake Sui]].<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> They consisted of cone-shaped buildings built upon stilt-assemblies, and were connected to land via a series of bridges. The Ewoks who inhabited such villages also navigated the lake on small [[Boat|sailboats]].<ref name="SWBF">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Ewok stilt villages made their [[canon]]ical debut in the [[2015]] video game ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''.<ref name="SWBF" /> Their appearance is directly based on a piece of concept art by [[Ralph McQuarrie]].<ref name="ISWU">''[[The Illustrated Star Wars Universe]]''</ref>
