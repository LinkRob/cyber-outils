The '''Dimean sector''' was a [[sector]] situated in the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It contained the [[Dimea system|Dimea]], [[Gregonia system|Gregonia]], [[Kessur system|Kessur]], [[Rigoron system|Rigoron]], and [[Veros system]]s, all of which were located in grid square P-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Kessurian]] [[species]]<ref name="Kessurian" /> was [[Homeworld|native]] to the Kessur [[Star system|system]].<ref name="Planetnamia" />

The Veros system featured the [[planet]] [[Veros]], which was the homeworld of the sentient [[Verosian]] species and was ruled by wealthy familial alliances, including the [[House of Basan]], for [[Standard century|centuries]]. Their wealth was taken from them [[Standard decade|decades]]<ref name="Angle" /> prior to [[0 ABY]]<ref name="AngleDate">"[[The Angle]]" establishes that its events occur in the recent aftermath of the [[Battle of Yavin]], which equates to [[0 ABY]] per the [['ABY-BBY' dating system|dating system]] established by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> and found its way into the [[Galactic Empire]]'s possession<ref name="Angle" /> by that [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="AngleDate" /> Veros was the homeworld of the Verosian [[Jaff Basan]] of the House of Basan.<ref name="Angle" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dimean sector was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Dimean sector/Legends|sector]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned, albeit unidentified, in ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]'', a [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[BioWare]].<ref name="KOTOR">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''</ref> The name was then introduced in the [[StarWars.com]] [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]''.<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
