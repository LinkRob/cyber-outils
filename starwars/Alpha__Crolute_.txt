'''Alpha''' was a role within the society of the [[Crolute]]s, an [[Amphibian|amphibious]] [[species]] native to the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Crul]]. Alpha Crolutes' junior [[Marriage|spouses]] and concubines would prepare [[gastropod]]s and [[fermented kelp-ichor]] for consumption by other Crolute clan leaders as they floated on their backs in the immersion tank of an alpha's mansion and negotiated trade deals and diplomacy.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Alphas were mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />
