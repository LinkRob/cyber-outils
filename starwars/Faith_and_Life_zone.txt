The '''Faith and Life zone''' was one of the four areas<ref name="Rising Storm" /> of the [[Carnival District]]<ref name="Race to Crashpoint Tower">''[[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower]]''</ref> in [[Lonisa City]] on [[Valo]]. As part of the grounds of the [[Valo Republic Fair]], the Faith and Life zone contained the [[Starlight Pavilion]], which represented the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]] and the [[Jedi Order]]. The ''[[United in Song]]'' exhibit was also situated within the zone.<ref name="Rising Storm" />
