'''Alpinn''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] where [[Lyra Erso]] searched for [[kyber crystal]]s. Alpinn reflected light in [[realspace]] due to the remnants of ancient mineral seas. When these seas dried up eons before the [[Imperial Era]], they left extensive areas of sedimentary rock and crystallized elements, which covered much of the planet's primary landmass in white.<ref name="Catalyst" />

==History==
At some point much earlier than the formation of the [[Galactic Empire]], a group of [[spacer]]s discovered the ruins of an ancient civilization, including large structures such as statues, temples, and obelisks that had been worn down by wind and seasonal rain. [[Archaeology|Archaeological]] excavations had gone on for nearly a [[standard century]]<ref name="Catalyst" /> by [[21 BBY]] to [[17 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'' begins eleven [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Invasion of Naboo]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[32 BBY]]. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the end of ''Catalyst'' to [[17 BBY]]. Therefore, ''Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel'' must take place from [[21 BBY]] to [[17 BBY]].</ref> Before the election of [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] as [[Supreme Chancellor]], the [[Galactic Republic]] funded the establishment of a [[Alpinn archaeology camp|permanent camp]] consisting of prefab dwellings, [[wind turbine]]s, a [[desalination plant]], and a research center. In the Imperial Era, a team of scientists petitioned for Alpinn to be designated as a [[Legacy world]], which would restrict development, visitation, and resource extraction, and the surveying and mapping conducted by [[Lyra Erso]] and [[Nari]] were part of the resource estimation process for the petition. [[Archaeologist]]s surmised that the discovery of [[kyber crystal]]s on the planet would guarantee the planet's protection from corporate exploitation. Erso and Nari's expedition failed to find kybers, however, instead uncovering [[ranite]], a similar mineral, but one that never occurred together with kyber crystals.<ref name="Catalyst" />
