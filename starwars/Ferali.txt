'''Ferali''' was a [[Klatooinian]] who was a member of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]].<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Ferali was a child and lived with the Path of the Open Hand on the [[planet]] [[Dalna]] where he and the rest of the Path's [[Littles]], including his younger sibling [[Eff]], were looked after by Path member [[Marda Ro]]. That year, a blue [[honey glider]] had attached itself to fellow Little [[Simi]], causing Ferali to fight for its affections alongside Little [[Hallisara]]. When Ro and the Littles went to a stand in the Dalnan [[city]] [[Ferdan]]'s [[Ferdan market|market]] to give out flowers, the glider landed on Little [[Jezra'lin]]'s head, and so Ferali told Simi it was a gift freely given.<ref name="Path of Deceit" />

[[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Kevmo Zink]] visited the stand at the same time, and after he used [[The Force]] to lift a flower, Ro then took him and the rest of the littles to the site where the Path was building their flagship, the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', and used it as an example of clarity and freedom which she then warned Ferali and the others were traits the Jedi did not have and did not allow the Force to have since they viewed themselves as above it, which made Ferali realize why the Jedi shouldn't have lifted the flower.<ref name="Path of Deceit" />
