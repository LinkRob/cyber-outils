'''Council Five Hundred''' was a [[farm]]ing [[city|settlement]] on the agricultural world [[Mina-Rau]]. In [[4 BBY]], the [[Galactic Empire]] began an [[Imperial Supply Census]] on Mina-Rau. During which Council Five Hundred was one of locations audited by the Empire.<ref name="One Year Later" /> During the audit a [[Kellen's friend|friend]] of the [[merchant]] [[Kellen]] at [[Council 436]] resided at Five Hundred and informed him of the Imperial's numbers and activity, including that the Imperials caught an individual running from the audit.<ref name="Sagrona Teema">{{Andor|Sagrona Teema}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
