The [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Anelsana system]]</ref> [[Planet|world]] [[Anelsana]] had a '''continent''' that was located to the north of several [[City|cities]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], Anelsana&mdash;and by extension its continent&mdash;was placed under a trade embargo by the [[Galactic Empire]] while under its control. At some point, the [[Viceroy]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] came to feel that the continent was sparsely populated and a relatively safe place to travel. After this, he included Anelsana's continent on a list of places suitable for his [[Gender|daughter]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], to render humanitarian aid.<ref name="LPOA" />

In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]].</ref> the list was given to Leia, who briefly considered visiting the continent. However, she ultimately decided to travel to the world [[Chasmeene]] instead after surmising that evidence of Anelsana's past trade embargo could not be found within the continent.<ref name="LPOA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Anelsana's continent was mentioned in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', a [[2017]] young-adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="LPOA" />
