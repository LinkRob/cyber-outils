'''Chancellor Soh's home''' was the personal residence of [[Galactic Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. It was located on<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] and Soh lived there with her pet [[targon]]s,<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> [[Matari (targon)|Matari]] and [[Voru (targon)|Voru]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> By [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> when the [[human]] [[Gender|girl]] [[Zenny Greylark]] was little, she went to a party at Soh's home for the first time and was terrified of her [[Cat|targons]].<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> while [[Mission on Palagosal|encountering]] [[Living Force|Force]]-[[Diet|consuming]] [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Palagosal]], Greylark mentally noted that the creatures reminded her of when she had gone to the party at Soh's home when she was little and been terrified of her pets.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chancellor Soh's home was mentioned in the [[2024]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=SWCE 2023: 9 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref>
