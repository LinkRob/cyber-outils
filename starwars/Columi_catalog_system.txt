The '''Columi catalog system''' was a system used to designate [[star system]]s.<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> It shared its name with the [[Columi]] [[species]].<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] star system<ref name="Visual Guide" /> that would eventually be known as the [[Bal'demnic system]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref><ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-10-24.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-10-24}}</ref> had the designation of XA-1958JHR in the Columi catalog system.<ref name="Visual Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
