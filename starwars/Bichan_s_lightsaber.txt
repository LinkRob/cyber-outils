'''Bichan's lightsaber''' was wielded by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Bichan]] during a [[Unidentified Jedi–Sith war|war]] between the [[Sith]] and the [[Jedi Order]]. He used the [[weapon]], including the whip attached to its [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]], to [[Death|kill]] at least eight [[Jedi]] during a [[Duel between Bichan and Jedi|duel]], and he scarred the Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Toul]]'s face with it. Later, Bichan [[Mission to Dolgarak|dueled]] Toul with the [[Sith lightsaber|lightsaber]] at the [[statues of prophecy]] on the [[planet]] [[Dolgarak]]. There, the Sith once again used his whip to immobilize the Jedi. However, Toul ultimately slew him.

==Description==
Bichan's lightsaber<ref name="ConceptGallery9">{{SW|url=journey-to-the-dark-head-concept-art-gallery|text="Journey to the Dark Head" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 9}}</ref> was a [[Sith lightsaber]] used by<ref name="JDH" /> the [[Sith Lord]] [[Bichan]].<ref name="StoryGallery3">{{SW|url=journey-to-the-dark-story-gallery|text="Journey to the Dark Head" Story Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> It had a [[Color|red]]-colored blade and a dark [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]] with brown and tan accents&mdash;including a two-pronged guard that extended for roughly a third of the [[hilt]]'s length.<ref name="JDH" /> Toward the blade end of the hilt was a red [[Activator (lightsaber component)|activation button]].<ref name="ConceptGallery2">{{SW|url=journey-to-the-dark-head-concept-art-gallery|text="Journey to the Dark Head" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> At the pommel of the [[lightsaber]]'s hilt was attached a long whip composed of dark segments that could be telescopically extended and collapsed. The whip's rigidity could be varied, and its end featured a sharp tan blade with three glowing-red accents. In [[Lightsaber combat|combat]], Bichan used the lightsaber in both a single-[[Limb|handed]] and two-handed grip.<ref name="JDH" />

==History==
===Defeating Jedi===
[[File:BichanFlingsToulAround-JourneyToTheDarkSide.png|thumb|right|330px|Bichan using the whip of his lightsaber to fling the disarmed Toul around in the air]]
Bichan wielded his lightsaber during a [[Unidentified Jedi–Sith war|war]] waged between the [[Sith]] tradition of [[Force-sensitive|Force-users]] and the [[Jedi Order]], their ideological antithesis,<ref name="JDH" /> at some point by [[1032 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{VisionsDate|205}}</ref> At one point, he was engaged in a [[Duel between Bichan and Jedi|duel]] with a group of at least nine [[Jedi]] and used the [[weapon]] to [[Death|kill]] at least seven of them. Bichan then attempted to use an overhead strike with the lightsaber to kill the young Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Toul]], but the latter's [[Unidentified human Jedi (Toul)|Jedi teacher]] intervened, parrying the Sith Lord's attack. Shortly thereafter, however, Bichan killed the older Jedi by impaling them in the back with the whip attached to his lightsaber.<ref name="JDH" />

After the Sith dropped the Jedi's body to the ground, Toul launched his own attack, but Bichan again used his whip, this time to disarm the Jedi apprentice and bind together his wrists. The Sith then immobilized Toul by briefly flinging him around in the [[Atmosphere|air]] before lifting him up again. Intrigued by the rage displayed by Toul, Bichan invited the Jedi to search for him when he was ready to embrace the [[dark side of the Force]] and, further taunting him, used the tip of his lightsaber's blade to burn a scar at the right corner of Toul's [[mouth]]. Bichan then dropped the Padawan to the ground and, with his lightsaber still ignited, left the scene of the [[Lightsaber duel|duel]].<ref name="JDH" />

===Final duel===
Some [[time]] later, after Bichan had encountered Toul again<ref name="JDH" /> at a [[Unidentified marketplace asteroid|marketplace asteroid]],<ref name="StoryGallery5">{{SW|url=journey-to-the-dark-story-gallery|text="Journey to the Dark Head" Story Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> he followed the Jedi while he traveled on a [[Mission to Dolgarak|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Dolgarak]]. Bichan shot down the [[Ara's starship|starship]] used by Toul and<ref name="JDH" /> the [[Technician|mechanic]]<ref name="Highlights">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-highlights-journey-to-the-dark-head|text=''Visions'' Revisited: 5 Highlights from "Journey to the Dark Head"}}</ref> [[Ara]], but the pair exited the wreckage of their crashed [[Starship|craft]] aboard a [[Ara's speeder|speeder]] and caused [[Bichan's starship|Bichan's vessel]] to crash-land as well. Bichan, however, survived and [[Force jump|leaped]] onto the top of the [[speeder]], lightsaber ignited, as it began to climb a spiraling pathway leading up one of the planet's two [[statues of prophecy]]. With Toul also climbing atop the speeding vehicle, the pair engaged in a lightsaber duel that continued as Ara drove the speeder off the statue's hand and above the [[cloud]] cover obscuring the heads of the statues.<ref name="JDH" />

[[File:BichanDefeated-JourneyToTheDarkHead.png|thumb|left|330px|Bichan's lightsaber in the immediate aftermath of its owner's death at the hands of Toul (center)]]
Bichan and Toul both leaped to the large [[stone]] ring connecting the heads and resumed their confrontation. At one point, the pair crashed into the [[cave]]rn-like interior of the ring, where Bichan plunged his lightsaber's blade into a [[crystal]] pillar in order to slow his descent. The Sith and the Jedi continued their fight inside the ring, with their lightsaber strikes occasionally generating purple-colored energy discharges and Bichan at one point using the whip of his weapon to lash out at Toul.<ref name="JDH" />

Ultimately, the two combatants re-emerged atop the ring, where Bichan managed to disarm his opponent. He then wrapped his whip around Toul's neck and held him above the edge of the ring's surface, giving him the ultimatum of either joining him or perishing. At that point, however, Ara caused a distraction that allowed Toul to regain his own weapon and kill Bichan. The Sith Lord's body and lightsaber then both remained atop the stone ring while Toul fell toward the ground at the statues' base, followed by the [[parachute]]-wearing Ara with the aim of catching him mid-air.<ref name="JDH" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Debut===
Bichan's lightsaber was created for "[[Journey to the Dark Head]],"<ref name="JDH" /> the fifth episode of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|second volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' non-[[canon]] animated anthology television series, which was produced by<ref name="SWCE 2023">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-visions|text=SWCE 2023: ''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Trailer, Key Art, and Cast Revealed}}</ref> the South Korean<ref name="Studios">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-2|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Release Date, Studios Revealed}}</ref> [[Studio Mir]] and premiered on [[Disney+]] on [[May 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="SWCE 2023" /> Prior to the episode's release, the weapon was first depicted in the [[April 10]], 2023 official trailer for ''Visions''{{'}} second volume,<ref name="Celebration">{{SWYouTube|vf7D4MWJofM|Star Wars Celebration LIVE! 2023 - DAY 4}}</ref> and it was named by the installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions Filmmaker Focus]]'' series of featurettes accompanying the episode.<ref name="Focus" />

===Design===
{{Quote|Bichan's weapon is a key prop that reflects his character. At first, we thought of weapons like a tonfa or a trident lightsaber. But based on Bichan's lines and characteristics, we thought of a snake, so the final design became a snake-like weapon.|Kang You Tae|<ref name="Focus">{{VisionsFocus|Journey to the Dark Head}}</ref>}}
[[File:BichansLightsaberAlternateMode-SWEpisodeGuide.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Concept art of Bichan's lightsaber depicting an alternate configuration]]
Concept art of Bichan's lightsaber was made by the artists [[Kim Hyun Min]] and [[Choi Ji Hyeon]].<ref name="ConceptGallery">{{SW|url=journey-to-the-dark-head-concept-art-gallery|text="Journey to the Dark Head" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slides 2, 4, 9}}</ref> The concept art pieces depicted the blade emitter of the lightsaber as being able to detach from its hilt while attached to an extendable, flexible tubular component similar to<ref name="ConceptGallery29">{{SW|url=journey-to-the-dark-head-concept-art-gallery|text="Journey to the Dark Head" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slides 2, 9}}</ref> the whip attachment of the lightsaber seen in the episode; however, the finished work did not showcase this feature.<ref name="JDH" />

According to Studio Mir's Art Director [[Kang You Tae]], while designing Bichan, giving him weapons such as a [[Wikipedia:Tonfa|tonfa]] or a "[[Wikipedia:Trident|trident]] lightsaber" was initially considered.<ref name="Focus" /> The latter design, illustrated in a [[2022]] concept art piece by [[Kim Il Kwang]], was a black-and-gray, long-handled weapon with three blade emitters that were mounted in parallel at the end of its handle. The emitters could be spread laterally away from each other, and the weapon had a number of embedded glowing elements. The illustration also depicted Bichan pointing the unignited weapon at the face of a captured individual.<ref name="KimIlKwang">{{Twitter|username=@gimigam2|url=status/1658105539736248322|name=[[Kim Il Kwang]]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/ttBoz}}</ref> Another early design for the lightsaber depicted it with two blade emitters attached to cords that ran along the length of Bichan's arms, potentially hidden by the long [[Clothing|sleeves]] of his attire.<ref name="StudioMirRadioTalk1">{{YouTube|video=N8ZGEgeqZbU|text=(ENG) Studio Mir Radio Talk With Star Wars Visions Volume 2 Crew Part. 1|channel=@StudioMIR2010|name=Studio Mir|date=2023-05-19}}</ref>

Ultimately, however, the lines and characteristics of Bichan reminded the creative team of a snake, and consequently the design of his weapon morphed into the "snake-like"<ref name="Focus" /> whip attached to the lightsaber<ref name="JDH" />&mdash;a weapon that, according to Kang, reflected Bichan's character. Attempts by sound supervisor [[Kim Suk Won]]&mdash;who used [[Wikipedia:Traditional Korean musical instruments|Korean musical instruments]] to design new lightsaber sound effects in "Journey to the Dark Head"&mdash;to enable the audience to distinguish between the good and evil characters in the episode by sound alone led to a human voice sample being used to create a "ghostly" sound for Bichan's weapon.<ref name="Focus" />
