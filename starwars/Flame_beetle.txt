'''Flame beetles''' were a species of [[beetle]] that lived on the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]]. The creatures consumed local plants and synthesized their remains into a highly flammable derivative, giving them their signature bioluminescent abdomen. They were then able to make these chemicals spontaneously combust when in proximity to an enemy or upon death.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order timeline">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> during [[Cal Kestis]]' [[Liberation on Kashyyyk|mission to Kashyyyk]], he encountered many flame beetles that assaulted him. He killed many to continue his mission.<ref name="Fallen Order" />
