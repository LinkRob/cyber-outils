The '''Brentaal Trading Houses''', also known as '''Brentaal trading houses''', were a location<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> [[Brentaal IV]]. Taking a break from ciphers to earn [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], [[Master Codebreaker]] paid a visit to the trading houses on Brentaal, one of the few [[Planet|worlds]] that still allowed the Codebreaker to play the games of odds which he recorded in the logbook known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''. The [[smuggler]] [[Platt Okeefe]] mentioned the Trading Houses as a location where one could acquire [[aurodium]] to smuggle in the ''Smuggler's Guide''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
