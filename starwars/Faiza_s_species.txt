This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Announcement" /> [[Faiza]] was a member of this species.<ref name="MakeItStop">{{Andor|Make It Stop}}</ref>

==Biology and appearance==
This [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] was distinguished by their hunched postures and spindly [[limb]]s, with [[Color|dark]] wrinkled skin, as well as elongated [[Bone|craniums]], squinted [[eye]]s, and pointed chins.<ref name="Announcement" />

==Species in the galaxy==
In [[5 BBY]], a trio of member of this species were enjoying the [[Niamos Beach|beaches]] of the [[planet]] [[Niamos]] when [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] was wrongly arrested by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Announcement" /> In [[1 BBY]], a member of the species named [[Faiza]] was a patient at [[Lina Soh Hospital]].<ref name="MakeItStop" />
