A type of '''defense platform''' was utilized by the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nation.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date-CR">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' in [[19 BBY]].</ref> several [[Space station|defense platforms]] [[orbit]]ed the Ascendancy's [[capital]] [[planet]], [[Csilla]], when it was [[Attack on Csilla|attacked]] by three [[warship]]s of the [[Paataatus Hiveborn]] nation. The platforms destroyed the attackers' [[missile]]s while the [[Chiss Defense Force]] [[starship]]s orbiting Csilla obliterated the [[Paataatus]] vessels, ending the attack in fifteen [[Standard minute|minutes]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date-GG">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> the Ascendancy's [[Boadil family|Boadil]] and [[Irizi family|Irizi]] [[Ruling Families|ruling families]] worked on a new defense platform at the Chiss world of [[Rentor]].<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Chiss defense platforms were mentioned in the novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> published on [[September 1]], [[2020]].<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/622990/star-wars-thrawn-ascendancy-book-i-chaos-rising-by-timothy-zahn/9780593157688|text=Star Wars: Thrawn Ascendancy (Book I: Chaos Rising)}}</ref> An excerpt of the novel published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[July 8]] of that year included the first mention of the defense platforms.<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/thrawn-ascendancy-chaos-rising-exclusive-excerpt|text=War Begins in ''Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising'' - Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref>
