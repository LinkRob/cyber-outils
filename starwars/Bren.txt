'''Bren''' was a [[human]] [[Jedi Initiate]] who was part of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Brought to the [[planet]] [[Tenoo]] as one of the new [[youngling]]s from the [[Coruscant]] [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]], Bren once fled off-world in hopes of returning to Coruscant, only to end up at [[Yarrum Tower]], a [[pirate]] base. He briefly befriended the pirate [[Clave Rollek]] before helping fellow Jedi initiate [[Kai Brightstar]] stop Rollek from the stealing the ''[[Crimson Firehawk]]'' [[shuttle]].<ref name="YJA 201B" />
