'''Delta 3''' was a location controlled by the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] by [[10 ABY/Legends|10 ABY]]. At that time it was a site of the New Republic long-range sensors. During the [[Operation Shadow Hand]], those sensors detected an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Fleet/Legends|fleet]] approaching the New Republic base on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Altyr V]].
