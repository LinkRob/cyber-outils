==Behind the scenes==
The breakout appeared in the [[Boba Fett — Black, White & Red 1|first issue]] of the [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Boba Fett — Black, White & Red]]''. The issue was written by [[Benjamin Percy]], illustrated by [[Chris Allen]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Boba Fett BWR 1" /> on [[September 17]], 2025.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/123259/star_wars_boba_fett_-_black_white_red_2025_1|text=Boba Fett — Black, White & Red 1 (2025) #1}}</ref>
