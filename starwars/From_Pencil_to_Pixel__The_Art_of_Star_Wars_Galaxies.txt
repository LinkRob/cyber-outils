'''''From Pencil to Pixel: The Art of Star Wars Galaxies''''' is a [[2003]] reference book that features concept art drawings and computer game art from [[Sony]] Online Entertainment's [[Star Wars video games|MMORPG]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']]. It contains artwork for each of the original playable species, for the first ten planets and their plants and beasts, and for the game's general architecture and starships.

The book was included with the Collector's Edition of ''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'' and was not for sale elsewhere. However, the [[Star Wars Galaxies Bonus DVD|Bonus DVD]] of the ''[[Star Wars Galaxies: The Complete Online Adventures]]'' later included excerpts from the book.<ref>{{Rebelscum|url=story/front/Star_Wars_Galaxies_The_Complete_Online_Adventures_109136.asp|text=Star Wars Galaxies: The Complete Online Adventures|author=Curto, Dan|date=2007-09-28}}</ref>
