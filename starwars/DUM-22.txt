'''DUM-22''' was a [[DUM-series pit droid]]<ref name="Book" /> that was manufactured by [[Serv-O-Droid, Inc.|Serv-O-Droid]]. The [[Maintenance droid|repair droid]]<ref name="Corr" /> could fold itself up like all pit [[droid]]s and was skilled in maintaining [[airspeeder]]s.<ref name="Book" /> The droid stood at 1.19 [[meter]]s, or 3 [[Foot|feet]] and 11 inches, tall.<ref name="Corr" /> DUM-22 had a [[Color|yellow]] [[sensor]] and copper plating. It carried a [[wrench]].<ref name="Book" />

==Behind the scenes==
DUM-22 was introduced in the [[2021]] search-and-find book ''[[Star Wars: These Are the Droids You're Looking For]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and illustrated by [[Art Mawhinney]].<ref name="Book" />
