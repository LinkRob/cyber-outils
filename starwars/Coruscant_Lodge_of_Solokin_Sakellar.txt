The '''Coruscant Lodge of Solokin Sakellar''' was a lodge owned by [[Grand Warden]] [[Solokin Sakellar]] of the [[University of Yabol Opa]], absolute ruler of<ref name="CL" /> the [[Colonies]]<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[Planet|world]] [[Yabol Opa]]'s planet-wide [[university]]<ref name="CL" /> in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}}, as depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[22 BBY]].</ref> The lodge was located in a [[skyscraper]] near a [[Novaplex]] [[hotel]] in [[Imperial City|Galactic City]], the [[ecumenopolis]] that covered the [[Galactic Republic]] [[capital]] [[Coruscant]] in the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Coruscant Lodge of Solokin Sakellar was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="CL" /> from the [[2003]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]'', which was written by [[Simon Beecroft]] and [[Curtis Saxton]], illustrated by [[Richard Chasemore]] and [[Hans Jenssen]], and marked the first mention of the [[Coruscant Lodge of Solokin Sakellar/Legends|lodge]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]''</ref>
