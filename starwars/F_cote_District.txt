The '''F'cote District''' was a district located on<ref name="Boba Fett BWR 3" /> the [[Hutt Space]] [[moon]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Nar Shaddaa]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Shawda Ubb]] [[information broker]] [[Sheraxx Quorsha]] operated in the district and was [[Skirmish on Nar Shaddaa|tracked down]] by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] for information on the [[Kajain'sa'Nikto]] [[Corm Vargon]].<ref name="Boba Fett BWR 3" />

The F'cote District first appeared in the [[Boba Fett — Black, White & Red 3|third issue]] of the [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]]  miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Boba Fett — Black, White & Red]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Juan José Ryp]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Boba Fett BWR 3" /> on [[November 26]], 2025.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/123261/star_wars_boba_fett_-_black_white_red_2025_3|text=Star Wars: Boba Fett - Black, White & Red (2025) #3}}</ref> Sacks revealed on [[Wikipedia:Bluesky|Bluesky]] that the district was named after his friend and ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan Francois Cote, who passed away a week before the issue's release.<ref name="Sacks" />
