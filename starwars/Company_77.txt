'''Company 77''' was a [[company]] of former [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] that mutinied and defected from the [[First Order]] after being ordered to fire on innocent civilians during the [[Battle of Ansett Island]]. Following their defiance of the First Order, Company 77 settled on [[Kef Bir]], an [[ocean]]ic [[moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Endor (planet)|Endor]] and the site of the wreckage of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second]] [[Death Star]]. On Kef Bir, the group tamed a wild [[orbak]] herd as mounts and used both their stormtrooper equipment and old [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] weapons from the Death Star that washed up on the shore to arm themselves and survive on the wild moon.

After living peaceful [[Standard year|years]] on Kef Bir, the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' arrived on the moon in [[35 ABY]] carrying news of the First Order's expanding power. Inspired in the example of [[Finn]], himself a former stormtrooper once known as FN-2187 who now fought against them with [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] [[Resistance]], Company 77 saw the opportunity to fight back. They allied with the Resistance, bringing along their [[orbak]] mounts and participated in a key ground assault in the [[Battle of Exegol]].

==History==
[[File:Company77-TheRiseOfSkywalker3.png|thumb|left|300px|The stormtroopers of Company 77 rebel against their masters at the Battle of Ansett Island.]]
Company 77 was a training unit of [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] that served the [[First Order]] and was made up mainly of young teenagers,<ref name="TROS VD" /> having all been conscripted into the military junta as children.<ref name="Episode IX" /> During their training, the [[company]] took part in the [[Battle of Ansett Island]], where they were ordered by [[Commanding officer|command]] to fire their weapons on innocent civilians. Refusing to carry out the massacre, the entire company mutinied,<ref name="TROS VD" /> laid down their weapons, and defected from the First Order. They commandeered the First Order Cruiser they were assigned to, and landed it on the moon of Kef Bir, where the Company stayed at for some time. Over time they began stripping the cruiser of parts.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Organization==
[[File:JannahsBow-CGSWG.png|thumb|200px|right|Jannah fashioned energy bows for the leaders of Company 77.]]
Within the First Order, Company 77 was subject to the command of leaders who ordered them to fire on civilians. The company had [[Squad leader|squad]] and [[platoon leader]]s, all of whom rallied together to the defy the First Order. After the mutiny, the stormtrooper TZ-1719, who renamed herself Jannah, rose through the ranks to lead the company due to her talent as a warrior and leader. Under Jannah were [[lieutenant]]s and [[sergeant major]]s for whom she crafted [[energy bow]]s to denote their leadership role. [[Forten]], formerly designated JL-4410, was the lead groom responsible for tending to the group's [[orbak]] contingent.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Company 77 first appeared in the [[2019]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="Episode IX" /> They were first mentioned, albeit unidentified, in the 2019 books [[Journey to Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Resistance Heroes|''Resistance Heroes'']] by [[Michael Siglain]],<ref name="Resistance Heroes">''[[Journey to Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Resistance Heroes]]''</ref> ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker Book and Magnetic Playset]]''<ref name="Magnetic Playset">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker Book and Magnetic Playset]]''</ref> by [[Sally Little]],<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/journey-to-star-wars-the-rise-of-skywalker-publishing-program|text=Lucasfilm Unveils Complete 'Journey to <i>Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker</i>' Publishing Program - Exclusive}}</ref> ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Amazing Sticker Adventures]]'' by [[David Fentiman]],<ref name="Amazing Sticker Adventure">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Amazing Sticker Adventures]]''</ref> and ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> They were first identified as Company 77 in the 2019 reference books ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> and ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Galactic Guide]]'' by [[Matt Jones]].<ref name="Galactic Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Galactic Guide]]''</ref>

According to co-writer [[Chris Terrio]], the name Company 77 was a reference to [[Order 66]] and the year [[1977]]. According to [[Glyn Dillon]], Company 77 surviving by scavenging Death Star parts was a thematic reference to [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] surviving as a [[scavenger]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="Art of TROS">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker]]''</ref>
