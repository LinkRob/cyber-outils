'''Ashton Vol''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[mercenary]] active during the [[High Republic Era]] who worked alongside the [[Battle droid|combat droid]] [[L-77]]. Vol and L-77 were drafted into a [[Mission to Angoth|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Angoth]] by the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Republic Defense Coalition]] in order to capture a [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creature from a base of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]. The pair accompanied [[Jedi Master]] [[Mirro Lox]], his [[Padawan]] [[Amadeo Azzazzo]], RDC [[medic]] [[Dorian Innes]], and a team of RDC personnel, with Vol leading the military side of the operation.<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" />
