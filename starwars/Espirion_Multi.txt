{{Quote|To all of our Alderaanian sisters and brothers, this is the captain of the ''Espirion Multi'', saying&hellip; stay back. We've got this.|The captain of the ''Espirion Multi'' tells the Alderaanian fleet to let the her ship handle the Star Destroyer|<ref name="PLV" />}}
The '''''Espirion Multi''''' was a [[Color|red-colored]] [[starship]] used by [[Espirion (species)|natives]] of the [[planet]] [[Espirion]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]]. When a fleet of [[Alderaanian]] ships gathered in Espirion's orbit came under attack from an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]], the Alderaanian-Espirion hybrid [[Beon Beonel]] had the ship aid the Alderaanians after hearing a speech by [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] broadcast from her [[shuttle]] ''[[Lord Junn]]''. The ship's captain contacted the Alderaanians upon entering the combat and told them to stay back while they handled the Imperials. They then attacked the [[Star Destroyer]] and destroyed it.<ref name="PLV" />

The crew of ''Espirion Multi'' included individuals of multiple species, like [[Ithorian]] and [[Gand]].<ref name="PLV" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Espirion Multi'' first appeared in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] [[Princess Leia 5|''Princess Leia'' 5]], which was written by [[Mark Waid]], illustrated by [[Terry Dodson]] and released in [[2015]].
