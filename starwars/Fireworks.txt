'''Fireworks''' were [[color]]ful pyrotechnic displays used for celebrations or special occasions, such as [[Empire Day]] on [[Lothal]],<ref name="Empire Day" /> the [[death]] of [[Galactic Empire|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]],<ref name="VI" /> and [[Boonta Eve]] on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Chapter 18" /> A type of spinning firework, called a firecracker pinwheel, was used during the [[Republic Day]] celebrations of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> The Ewoks of [[Bright Tree tribe]] on the [[Forest Moon of Endor]] used fireworks to celebrate the defeat of the [[First Order]] after the [[Battle of Exegol]].
