'''Barritz''' was a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and [[61st Mobile Infantry|Twilight Company]] and was a combatant in the [[Battle of Hoth|Battle of]] [[Hoth]]. Barritz fought alongside fellow [[trooper]]s [[Parko]] and [[Chanda Bethari]], and ultimately survived the battle. Two [[Standard day|days]] later, Barritz and Parko visited a recuperating Bethari, who was healing in a [[bacta]] [[Bacta tank|tank]] after losing a leg.<ref name="On the Front Lines" />
