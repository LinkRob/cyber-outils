'''CB-3DU''' was a [[protocol droid]] who served as a [[teacher]] on the [[planet]] [[Nevarro]] following its [[Clash on Nevarro|liberation]] from the forces of the [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] led by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]]. As the protocol droid was teaching a class on astrography, the [[Youngling|infant]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], who was under the protection of the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]], was left in the room by [[Magistrate]] [[Greef Karga]] to keep him safe while Djarin assisted Karga in destroying the remaining [[Nevarro Imperial base|Imperial base]] on Nevarro. Grogu was later returned to Djarin upon the latter's arrival at the class.<ref name="Chapter 12" />

==Biography==
{{Quote|The Capital of the Old Republic was within the Core on a planet called Coruscant. The capital of the New Republic...is currently on the planet Chandrila. So, back to the start.|CB-3DU tells her students about the capitals of the Old and New Republics|<ref name="Chapter 12" />}}
[[File:Nevarro_classroom.png|thumb|right|250px|CB-3DU teaches her students.]]
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate}}</ref> CB-3DU<ref name="TMVG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> served as a [[teacher]] on the [[planet]] [[Nevarro]] following its [[Clash on Nevarro|liberation]] from [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant forces]] led by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]]. When [[youngling]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] was introduced to the class by [[Greef Karga]], the droid was teaching<ref name="Chapter 12" /> her<ref name="Chapter 17" /> students about the history of the [[Galactic Republic]] and about the different regions of the galaxy. As the class focused on the newcomer, she ordered the class to remain silent and calm, and then continued by telling her students about the capitals of the old and [[New Republic]].<ref name="Chapter 12" />

After Grogu was introduced to the class, Karga, [[Din Djarin]]&mdash;Grogu's tutor&mdash;and [[Carasynthia Dune]] carried out an attack on the [[Nevarro Imperial base|last Imperial base]]. Grogu was later returned to Djarin upon the latter's return.<ref name="Chapter 12" />

===Pirate encounter===
{{Quote|Stand aside, droid, don't you know who we are?|Vane, to CB-3DU|<ref name="Chapter 17" />}}
Djarin and Grogu later arrived on Nevarro to greet the new [[High Magistrate]] Greef Karga and ask for help to rebuild [[IG-11]] to help him on his journey to [[Mandalore]]. As the two talked, [[CU-28]] alerted Karga and Djarin that the school was being attacked by [[pirate]]s. The two rushed over to the school, only to find [[Vane]], a pirate, harassing the droid, asking her to let him and his companions in. Karga and Djarin shot Vane's companions dead, and Vane fled the scene not long after.<ref name="Chapter 17" />

==Behind the scenes==
CB-3DU first appeared in "[[Chapter 12: The Siege]]," the fourth episode of the [[The Mandalorian Season Two|second season]] of the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', which aired on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Chapter 12" /> on [[November 20]], [[2020]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-november-2020|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in November 2020}}</ref> Credited as "Teacher Droid," the character was voiced by [[Kathryn Elise Drexler]] and physically portrayed by [[Chris Bartlett]].<ref name="Chapter 12" />
