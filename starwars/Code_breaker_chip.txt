A '''code breaker chip''' was a [[computer]] chip designed to bypass algorithmic security processes.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> it was illegal to own the chip in the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] learned that the [[Starfallers]] [[pirate]] gang had stored one of the chips at their [[Shipjacker Workshop|workshop]] in the [[Southern Falls]] region of [[Toshara]], a [[moon]] of [[Toshaal]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. She later visited the workshop and stole the chip and used it to upgrade her [[slicing unit]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Code breaker chip appeared as an upgrade item in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The item was used to unlock "the slicer" expert upgrade which increased the amount of energy of the slicer kit.<ref name="Outlaws" />
