'''10 Ways the Corruption Will RUIN Your Life!''' was an article written by the [[human]] [[Zarra Fingau]] about the [[Corruption (virus)|corruption]], a virus that affected the [[Observatory (The Island)|Observatory]] on [[The Island]] of a [[Unidentified planet (The Island)|planet]]. The [[Weequay]] and self-proclaimed biggest fan of Fingau, [[Dessim Peidur]], read the article and considered it [[eye]]-opening.<ref name="Castaways" />
