'''Baako''' was a [[Gender|male]] who was the brother of the [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]].<ref name="Phasma" /> At one point, he worked as an intern for the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Senate|Senate]] on [[Hosnian Prime]].<ref name="Black Spire" /> By the [[time]] Vi was captured by the [[First Order]] aboard the ''[[Absolution]]'', Baako was Vi's sole living relative and was a dignitary who had recently been stationed on [[Pantora]],<ref name="Phasma" /> with his [[Baako's husband|husband]].<ref name="Black Spire" />
