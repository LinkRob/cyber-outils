The '''CR20 troop carrier''' was a type of troop [[transport]] [[Starship|ship]]. The [[smuggler]] [[Saponza]] used a modified CR-20, christened the [[Strix (ship)|''Strix'']], as his personal craft.<ref name="Strix model" />

{{Ship-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The CR20 troop carrier originated in ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'', a [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] animated television series produced by [[Cartoon Network]]. In the series, it was used by the [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].<ref name="CW11">{{CW|11}}</ref> The ship was [[canon]]ized through its inclusion in [[Disney Interactive]]'s mobile strategy [[Star Wars video games|game]] ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''.<ref name="Commander" />
