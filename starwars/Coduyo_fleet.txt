A '''fleet''' was used by the [[Coduyo family]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. When the family was part of the [[Ruling Families]], it had the right to possess a small [[fleet]] of [[warship]]s, as all other families. After the Coduyo family was demoted to the [[Great Families]] [[Standard century|centuries]] before the [[Nikardun conquests]], they lost their right to the fleet, and only possessed several centuries-old [[cruiser]]s located in their holdings at [[Massoss]].<ref name="Greater Good" />
