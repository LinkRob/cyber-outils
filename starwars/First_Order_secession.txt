In [[29 ABY]], a majority of [[Centrists|Centrist]]-aligned [[star system]]s underwent a '''secession''' from the [[New Republic]] to formally establish the [[First Order]], which had been a unrecognized [[First Order military|military]] junta in the [[Unknown Regions]]. The secession began a period of resurgence of [[Neo-Imperialism|Imperial-style militarism]] and [[fascism]] across the known [[galaxy]].

The First Order had formed from [[Unknown Regions Imperial remnant|remnants]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] that [[Contingency|fled]] into the Unknown Regions after the [[Galactic Concordance|end]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. In the [[Standard year|years]] preceding the secession, the First Order had been purportedly secretly influencing and manipulating the New Republic using proxies and sympathizers.

When the First Order was formally established, it immediately became locked in a [[Cold War]] with the New Republic, ultimately leading to a [[First Order-Resistance War|galactic war]] in [[34 ABY]] that resulted in the First Order succeeding in conquering much of [[the galaxy]] by [[35 ABY]].

==History==
====Background====
{{Quote|The conflict between the parties gets worse every day. Most Centrists and Populists are still polite to each other, but barely. Every debate on the Senate floor turns into an endless argument over 'tone' or 'form' and never about issues of substance&mdash;|Leia Organa|<ref name="Bloodline" />}}
[[File:First_Senator_Leia_poster.png|thumb|right|250px|The position of First Senator was proposed by the Centrists who wanted a strong central government for the New Republic.]]
By [[28 ABY]], [[Inner systems' Imperial remnant|remnants]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] inside the [[Core Worlds|Core]], [[Colonies]], and [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] regions of [[the galaxy]] had largely joined the [[New Republic]], where former Imperials reformed themselves into a [[Politics|political]] block known as the [[Centrists]]. This led to tensions in the [[New Republic Senate]] as Centrist [[senator]]s wanted the New Republic to be more like the centralized [[Galaxy|Galactic]] Empire with a strong central government and military, while the [[Populists|Populist]] block composed of senators from the founding [[Star system|systems]] of the Republic wanted decentralization and demilitarization more in-line with the [[Galactic Republic]] before the [[Clone Wars]]. In the buildup to the event, the [[Amaxine warrior crisis]] occurred, where it would be revealed that influential New Republic political leader and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] hero [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] was the [[Skywalker family|daughter]] of the much-despised [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Influential Centrist Senator Lady [[Carise Sindian]] of [[Arkanis]] secretly worked for the First Order, and had Populist leader [[Tai-Lin Garr]] [[Tai-Lin Garr's assassination|assassinated]] by her ally and fellow First Order supporter [[Arliz Hadrassian]]. The crisis led to the postponement of the creation of a "[[First Senator]]" position, a position that would have had greater powers than the [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Supreme Chancellor]] of the New Republic, a position proposed by the Centrists to end the political gridlock in the senate following years of stagnation.<ref name="Bloodline" />

====The withdrawal====
[[File:HuxAndStormtroopersAtCommandCenter-TFAOfficialTeaser.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Centrists became the political branch of the First Order, the successor state of the Galactic Empire, after seceding from the New Republic.]]
Following the scandal involving Senator Organa, she resigned from the New Republic Senate to form the [[Resistance]] in an effort to address the resurgence of [[Neo-Imperialism|Imperial-style militarism]] exemplified by an emerging power like the First Order. Carise Sindian planned to submit a motion in the Senate for the Centrist worlds to secede from the New Republic. Although Organa managed to destroy Sindian's political career,<ref name="Bloodline" /> a majority of the Centrists formally left the New Republic<ref name="TROS VD" /> as she had planned.<ref name="Bloodline" /> Among the seceding planets that withdrew their memberships in the New Republic were [[Orinda]], [[Kuat]] and Arkanis.<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

A majority of senators welcomed this departure, with the Senate allowing their withdrawal,<ref name="Propaganda" /> a marked departure from the [[Separatist Crisis]] that precipitated the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> The former Centrists then remolded into the First Order, marking the official formation of the regime and beginning the [[Cold War]] between the First Order and the New Republic. The First Order, built from the ashes of the Empire's [[Imperial Military|war machine]], precipitated a return of [[fascism]] and militarism<ref name="TROS VD" /> with [[Snoke]],<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Force-sensitive|adept]] who rose to become [[Supreme Leader]] of the faction,<ref name="Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> at the head of politics and commander of the military.<ref name="TSWB" />

====Aftermath====
[[File:GOH-ResistancevFirstOrder.png|thumb|right|250px|Following the collapse of the New Republic, the First Order embarked on a campaign to conquer the galaxy.]]
The First Order then tightened its grip over territories within its sphere of influence, and invaded several other nearby systems. These included the [[Otomok system]] (which was [[Blockade of the Otomok system|blockaded]]),<ref name="Bomber Command">''[[The Last Jedi: Bomber Command]]''</ref> [[Tehar]],<ref name="The Children from Tehar">{{Resistance|The Children from Tehar}}</ref> and [[planet]]s such as [[Castilon]]<ref name="The Occupation">{{Resistance|The First Order Occupation}}</ref> and [[Jakku]].<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> A growing number of planets faced a First Order presence as tensions escalated in the Cold War. The majority of First Order incursions came in the [[Trans-Hydian Borderlands]] to the north.<ref name="Before the Awakening">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref>

Even with the First Order blatantly invading and occupying worlds in the Mid and Outer Rim, the New Republic refused to directly take action against them outside of official condemnations, leading to the Resistance to take military action against the regime.<ref name="Station Theta Black">{{Resistance|Station Theta Black (episode)}}</ref> Eventually the First Order would [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroy the New Republic Capital]],<ref name="Episode VII" /> leading both to full-scale [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] and the First Order's measured, if brief success in its quest for galactic domination.<ref name="TROS VD" />
