The '''Blazemaker Mark II''' was a [[Ugly|Z-wing]] that [[Zander Freemaker]] assembled at [[Freemaker Salvage and Repair]] on [[the Wheel]]. He later flew it while escaping [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="ROTKS" /> After joining the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], Zander and [[Freemakers/LEGO|his family]] refitted the Blazemaker II with [[laser cannon]]s and a [[LR1K sonic cannon|magma cannon]]. Zander deployed his Blazemaker during a [[Rebel-Imperial dogfight]] but the ship blew up when the magma cannon malfunctioned.<ref name="ZFSPG" /> It had dual [[Chromium|chromium]] plate engines and a [[Durasteel|durasteel]] [[Hull|hull]].<ref name="Home One">{{FreemakerAdventures|Home One (episode)}}</ref>
