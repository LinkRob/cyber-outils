'''''Dark Legends''''' is a [[canon]] unabridged audiobook version of the [[Dark Legends|short story collection of the same name]] written by [[George Mann]]. It was narrated by [[Stuart Blinder]] and released exclusively on [https://www.audible.com/ Audible] as part of their ''[[Star Wars Audio Adventures]]'' on [[October 16]], [[2025]].<ref name="Audible" />

==Publisher's summary==
''Listen on if you dare! This unique in&ndash;world collection hallows the spooky tales and ghost stories that would have kept young Luke and Leia up at night. Created by George Mann and Grant Griffin&mdash;the same team behind the stunning Star Wars: Myths & Fables&mdash;these seven frightful fables have been carefully woven from the expansive fabric that is the Star Wars galaxy.''
