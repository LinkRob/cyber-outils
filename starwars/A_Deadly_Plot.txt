"'''A Deadly Plot'''" is a [[canon]] short story that appears in ''[[The Prequel Trilogy Stories]]''. It was written by [[Meredith Rusu]].<ref name="TPTS" /> It adapts the events of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] from the [[Dogfight over Geonosis]] to the vote to form the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].
