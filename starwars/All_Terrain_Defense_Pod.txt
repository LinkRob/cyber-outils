The '''All Terrain Defense Pod''' ('''AT-DP''') was a large, bipedal [[walker]] utilized by the [[Galactic Empire]] and were manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]] prior to and during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. They were driven by [[Imperial combat driver]]s and served as the mainstay of backwater garrisons.

==Description==
[[File:Atdpint.jpg|thumb|left|180px|AT-DP interior view.]]
The AT-DP walker, a successor to the [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport|AT-RT]] used by the Empire's predecessor state, the [[Galactic Republic]], it served as the mainstay of backwater garrisons and support for larger war machines, including the [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT]]. Unlike the bulky [[All Terrain Armored Cannon|AT-AC]] which was almost as tall, and the smaller [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]], that boasts a host of armaments, the AT-DP was armed only with a single [[Maad-38 heavy laser cannon]]. The weapon was powerful enough to destroy another walker of the same type.<ref name="Hera's Heroes">{{Rebels|Hera's Heroes}}</ref> The [[cockpit]] had seating for a [[Imperial combat driver|driver]] and a [[gunner]], with the gunner situated behind the driver, though both crewmen could move the walker, fire the cannon and had access to the gyroscopic controls used to keep the walker stable. Each of these walkers had a hatch at the top to access the cockpit; a main [[viewport]] at the front, with bulbs on the side and slits to improve visibility; and holographic projectors.<ref name="BR">{{Rebels|Breaking Ranks}}</ref>

[[File:Kanan_shoots_an_AT-DP.png|thumb|right|250px|Kanan Jarrus disabled an AT-DP with a disruptor rifle.]]
To protect the crewmembers and equipment, each walker possessed [[armor]] strong enough to protect from blaster fire. This armor could not protect against missile launchers, however, and in one instance, an AT-DP was disabled when shot with a [[T-7 ion disruptor rifle]].<ref name="Droids in Distress">{{Rebels|Droids in Distress}}</ref> [[Thermal detonator]]s exploded near the legs and feet of a walker could also cause damage.<ref name="Hera's Heroes" /> Although the walkers were sturdy, their heads were prone to exploding under sustained duress, such as being pressed by an [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)|AT-AT]].<ref name="An Inside Man">{{Rebels|An Inside Man}}</ref>

AT-DPs were used throughout the Empire to keep the populations of occupied worlds in line and to combat insurgent forces. Many of these walkers were used in Imperial facilities such as [[Imperial Academy|Imperial Academies]] for both defensive purposes and training [[cadet]]s.<ref name="BR" /> In situations where an Imperial presence needed to be established quickly and powerfully, two walkers could be attached to the underside docking clamps of a [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] for landing in almost any suitable environment.<ref name="Droids in Distress" />

==History==
[[File:ImperialHeist.png|thumb|left|250px|A rebel-controlled AT-DP assaulting Imperial forces.]]
The AT-DP was originally intended to be used to protect Imperial assets on occupied [[Planet|worlds]].<ref name="SWC" /> The Imperial [[291st Legion]] used the newly released AT-DP walkers to clear out a nest of [[Thalassian]] slavers on [[Galpos II]]. They subsequently used the AT-DP walkers as the vanguard in urban fighting on [[Mendavi]].<ref name="edge" /> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="18 BBY" /> AT-DPs operating as part of [[Rantu's battalion]] were deployed to [[Mon Cala]] and fought in the [[Battle of Dac City]] under [[Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]] to combat the forces of [[King]] [[Lee-Char]] on [[Mon Cala]].<ref name="Dark Lord 13">[[Darth Vader (2017) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 13]]</ref>

Some time later, while on a mission to steal unknown Imperial weapons, the [[Spectres]] located T-7 Ion disruptor rifles and used them to disable attacking AT-DPs.<ref name="Droids in Distress" /> Many of these walkers saw action against the rebel cell located on [[Lothal]]<ref name="SWC" /> as part of the [[Lothal garrison]].<ref name="Ghost Raid">{{SW|url=games-apps/star-wars-rebels-ghost-raid|text=''Star Wars Rebels: Ghost Raid''|int=Star Wars Rebels: Ghost Raid|nolive=1}}</ref> These walkers were also prominent in many [[Empire Day]] festivals, serving in a similar capacity to [[stormtrooper]]s and [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyers]] as omnipresent symbols of the Empire's strength.<ref name="Empire Day">{{Rebels|Empire Day (episode)}}</ref> Between [[4 BBY]] and [[3 BBY]],<ref name="4-3 Date">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Imperial Heist]]'' includes [[Sabine Wren]] with [[Color|blue-green]] [[hair]], which first appears in "[[The Lost Commanders]]," however it takes place before [[Kanan Jarrus]] was blinded in "[[Twilight of the Apprentice]]." ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates these episodes to [[4 BBY]] and [[3 BBY]] respectively. Therefore, ''Star Wars Rebels: Imperial Heist'' must take place between these [[Standard year|years]].</ref> the Spectres undertook a [[Unidentified heist|heist]] within an [[Unidentified Imperial base (heist)|Imperial base]]. In order to escape, the rebel cell then needed to steal AT-DPs and fight their way out.<ref name="Imperial Heist">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Imperial Heist]]''</ref>

[[File:LightAttackAtollon.png|thumb|right|250px|AT-DP's were deployed during in the Battle of Atollon.]]
During the [[Mandalorian Civil War (Imperial Era)|Mandalorian Civil War]] in [[1 BBY]], Imperial [[Governor]] [[Tiber Saxon]] deployed two AT-DP's to defend a [[Mandalore prison outpost|Mandalorian prison]] during a [[Mission to Mandalore|mission]] to [[Mandalore]], and used a [[AT-DP Arc Cannon Prototype|special variant]] of the AT-DP to carry the [[Arc Pulse Generator]] nicknamed the ''Duchess''. The weapon and the walker were both destroyed, along with Tiber Saxon, when [[Tiber Saxon's Star Destroyer|his Star Destroyer]] was destroyed.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Heroes of Mandalore]]''</ref> [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn" sent AT-DPs operating under his [[Unidentified armored division|armored division]] into the [[Battle of Atollon]].<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="IV Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> AT-DPs were used by the Empire alongside [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-STs]] on [[Tatooine]] during the [[Secret mission to Tatooine|hunt]] for the [[Death Star]] plans.<ref name="New Edition">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref>

The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] stole a number of these walkers and provided them to [[Tatooine rebel cell|Saponza's Gang]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]]. Meanwhile, the [[68th Legion]] opposing Saponza's gang also made use of AT-DPs.<ref name="SWC" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|There's clearly some influence from concept artist Joe Johnston as well, in our walker. Ours is actually one that Joe had designed for the Episode V scout walker, an abandoned design.|''Star Wars Rebels'' executive producer [[Dave Filoni]]|<ref name="Rebels: The Visual Guide" />}}
The All Terrain Defense Pod was a vehicle created for the [[2014]] animated series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. However, it was first mentioned on a [[StarWars.com]] article, "NYCC 2013: Meet the Inquisitor, the New Face of Evil in ''Star Wars Rebels''."<ref name="NYCC 2013">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2013-meet-the-inquisitor-the-new-face-of-evil-in-star-wars-rebels|text=NYCC 2013: Meet the Inquisitor, the New Face of Evil in ''Star Wars Rebels''|nobackup=1}}</ref> The vehicle would then made its debut through the promotional tie-in web browser [[Star Wars video games|game]] ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Ghost Raid]]''.<ref name="Ghost Raid" /> The design of the All Terrain Defense Pod was developed from an early illustration by concept artist [[Joe Johnston]] for<ref name="Rebels: The Visual Guide" /> the [[1980]] theatrical film<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="Rebels: The Visual Guide" /> Concept artist [[Pat Presley]] would then refined and finalized the design of the vehicle for the animated series.<ref name="Pat Presley">{{WebCite|url=https://phattro.com/star-wars-rebels|text=Star Wars: Rebels|work=[[Pat Presley]]'s [https://phattro.com official website]|archivedate=20250804114500}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[All Terrain Defense Pod/Legends|All Terrain Defense Pod]] appeared in the guidebook ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Handbook: A Commander's Guide]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]], illustrated by [[Joe Corroney]],<ref name="Imperial Handbook">''[[Star Wars: Imperial Handbook: A Commander's Guide]]''</ref> and published by [[becker&mayer!]] on [[October 14]], [[2014]].<ref name="Amazon">{{Amazon|url=Star-Wars-Imperial-Handbook-Edition/dp/1603803408|text=Star Wars: Imperial Handbook Deluxe Edition|nolive=1}}</ref> Despite releasing past the [[canon]] and Legends continuity split in [[April]] 2014, becker&mayer! has confirmed that the guidebook takes place on the latter continuity.<ref name="Facebook">{{Facebook|username=beckermayerbooks|url=beckermayerbooks/posts/865867973431390|name=[[becker&mayer!|Becker&mayer! Books]]|date=2014-09-12|quote=Hi Mark! Imperial Handbook is set in Legends.|archivefile=File:BeckerMayer-ImperialHandbookLegends-Facebook.png|nolive=1}} {{C|In response to: "Does anyone know if this book is set in Legends? Or Canon?"}}</ref>
