The '''Correctional Officer''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Zaina]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Rebel Veteran|standard costume]].<ref name="Hunters" /> It strongly resembled [[Clothing|uniforms]] worn by the [[corrections officer]] of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Mando">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 6: The Prisoner}}</ref>

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Correctional Officer was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Zaina, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.17.0  on [[July 18]], [[2023]] during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Officer's A280-CFE]].<ref name="Hunters" />
