Between [[19 BBY]] and [[18 BBY]], [[Clone Force 99]] and the [[pirate]] [[Phee Genoa]] '''discovered''' an ancient [[superweapon]] [[Skara Nal Mech|walker]]. The subject of legend, the walker had long been believed to be a [[city]] built by the [[Ancients (Skara Nal)|Ancients]] containing a valuable [[crystal]] called the [[Heart of the Mountain]]. Bad Batch member [[Omega]] found a [[Omega's Compass|compass]] with the coordinates of the [[Kaldar Trinary system]], and Genoa persuaded the team to accompany her on a treasure hunt. Upon reaching [[Skara Nal]] in the [[Star system|system]], the compass led them inside of a [[mountain]]. Contending with various booby traps, the group eventually made its way to a chamber containing the Heart of the Mountain. When Genoa removed the crystal, however, the truth of Skara Nal's nature was revealed as being a massive [[walker]] with a [[superlaser]] which began to destroy the surrounding environment. [[Hunter]] persuaded Genoa to put the crystal back as the Batch's [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|starship]], which they had used to travel there, would be destroyed if she did not. The crystal melted when it was returned to its slot inside the walker, and the war machine itself shorted out and collapsed, destroyed for good. All of the [[human]]s survived, but Genoa's [[droid]] [[MEL]]-221 was destroyed, although the pirate was not perturbed as she had backed up Mel's memory on her ship, the ''[[Providence]]''.

==Prelude==
[[Phee Genoa]] was a [[pirate]] and self-described treasure hunter active during the early [[Imperial Era]]<ref name="Entombed" /> between [[19 BBY]] and [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> She was an associate of the [[information broker]]<ref name="Spoils of War">{{TBB|Spoils of War}}</ref> [[Ciddarin Scaleback]],<ref name="Faster">{{TBB|Faster}}</ref> who considered Genoa to be the most trustworthy pirate she knew.<ref name="Spoils of War" /> In that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Genoa hired Scaleback to [[Crab Heist|retrieve]] a crate from [[Aynaboni]]. To carry out the mission, Scaleback sent her "top team": the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]],<ref name="Spoils of War" /> a group of former [[Galactic Republic]] [[clone commando]]s who had [[Escape from Kamino|deserted]] the [[Galactic Empire]] shortly after its [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]].<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> The [[squad]] had met Scaleback after coming to her for help identifying a [[Fennec Shand|bounty hunter]]<ref name="Rampage">{{TBB|Rampage (episode)}}</ref> who had attempted to [[Chase on Pantora|abduct]] the Batch's newest member, [[Omega]],<ref name="Cornered">{{TBB|Cornered}}</ref> a unique female [[Cloning|clone]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> who had been created as a special project of [[Chief Medical Scientist]] [[Nala Se]].<ref name="Return to Kamino">{{TBB|Return to Kamino}}</ref> Afterwards, the Batch had begun working for Scaleback as [[Mercenary|mercenaries]].<ref name="Decommissioned">{{TBB|Decommissioned}}</ref>

Scaleback entrusted Genoa with the knowledge of the Batch's identities and history with the Empire. The team first met the pirate when they returned late to [[Cid's Parlor]] on [[Ord Mantell]] after retrieving the crate, and Batch leader [[Hunter]] was perturbed when he found out Genoa knew who they were, as the Batch had successfully kept out of the Empire's notice<ref name="Spoils of War" /> since being presumed [[Death|dead]] during the [[Attack on Kamino|fall of Tipoca City]]<ref name="Ruins of War">{{TBB|Ruins of War}}</ref> several [[Standard month|months]] earlier.<ref name="Disney">{{Disney|subdomain=press|domain=co.uk|url=press-kit/star-wars:-the-bad-batch-season-2|text=Star Wars: The Bad Batch, Season 2 Press Kit}}</ref> Scaleback insisted that Genoa was trustworthy. After Genoa's departure with her crate, Scaleback gave the Bad Batch their [[Mission to Castle Serenno|next mission]], based on intel from the pirate: a heist at [[Castle Serenno]] to steal part of the [[Dooku's war chest|war chest]] belonging to the late [[Count]] [[Dooku]], leader of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. All of the adult members of the team were persuaded to go due to the usefulness of having some secure wealth in case they had to flee if the Empire arrived on Ord Mantell, so they could secure a future for themselves.<ref name="Spoils of War" /> Ultimately, the job was unsuccessful due to stiff Imperial resistance, and the Bad Batch escaped [[Serenno]] empty-handed.<ref name="Ruins of War" />

Genoa became a familiar face at Cid's Parlor, becoming acquainted with the Bad Batch. Despite her habit of telling constantly-changing tales of her exploits, which irritated Hunter, [[Echo]], and [[Tech]], Omega grew fascinated with Genoa and the idea of securing treasure and riches. The girl began collecting items of potential value to show Genoa whenever she had a chance. Eventually, Omega and [[Wrecker]] went on an errand to a [[Unidentified junkyard|junkyard]] to acquire a new [[compressor]], Wrecker collecting the part while Omega scooped up several knick-knacks to show Genoa. When they returned to Cid's, Tech identified one of Omega's trinkets as worthless, but Genoa took note of another. Inspecting the [[Omega's Compass|device]], she saw coordinates engraved on it and identified it as a [[compass]]. Genoa's [[droid]], [[MEL]]-221, scanned the compass and revealed coordinates Genoa recognized as being in the [[Kaldar Trinary system]]. Tech had no record of the [[Star system|system]], but Genoa countered that the best treasures were not normally located on [[map]]s.<ref name="Entombed" />

Omega was intrigued by the possibility of treasure, and Genoa was confident that was what the compass led to. She invited the Bad Batch to come with her.<ref name="Entombed" />

==The discovery==
The ''Marauder'' followed the coordinates on the compass to<ref name="Entombed" /> [[Skara Nal]]<ref name="Skara Nal">{{Databank|skara-nal|Skara Nal}}</ref> in the system.<ref name="Entombed" />

==Aftermath==
As the Bad Batch and Genoa returned to the ''Marauder'', Hunter said it was a good thing that at least the walker could no longer be activated. Omega sympathized to Genoa over Mel's destruction, but the pirate was unconcerned, stating that she kept the droid's memory backed up on her [[Providence|own starship]] because she was destroyed frequently. Genoa said she would have Mel rebuilt before their journey to [[Vadnay]]. Omega asked what was located there, and Genoa explained she had a lead on an [[ancient chalice]] that had once belonged to the [[Kingdom of Elweys]], explaining more to a fascinated Omega. Hunter was skeptical about another trip with the pirate, but was open to it given Omega's excitement at the prospect.<ref name="Entombed" />
