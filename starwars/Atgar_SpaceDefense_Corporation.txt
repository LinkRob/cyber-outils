'''Atgar SpaceDefense Corporation''' were a weapons manufacturing company who made the [[1.4 FD P-Tower]], a fixed emplacement anti-vehicle [[laser cannon]]. The tower was quick and easy to produce and was used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]] during the [[Battle of Hoth]] in the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="USW" />

==Behind the scenes==
Atgar SpaceDefense Corporation was first mentioned in the ''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]'' by [[Greg Gorden]] which was released in [[1989]] as a supplement for the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' produced by [[West End Games]]. The company was confirmed to be [[canon]] by the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', which was released in [[2015]].
