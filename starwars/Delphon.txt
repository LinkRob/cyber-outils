'''Delphon''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Anoat sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. During the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Iron Blockade]], the criminal [[Trade Spine League]] wanted to quietly move a shipment of [[crystal]]s from the planet [[Burnin Konn]] to Delphon, but had to first deal with the [[Black Hole Gang]].<ref name="CC" /> At another point during the [[blockade]], an anonymous party hired a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] to break into Delphon's [[senator]]ial compound and convince one of the [[aide]]s to secretly work against the Empire.<ref name="Inside" /> One notable settlement on Delphon was [[Delphon City]], which housed at least [[Unidentified cantina (Delphon City)|one]] [[cantina]], at least [[Docking Bay 18 (Delphon City)|one]] [[Hangar|docking bay]], and [[Cage's Tech Emporium]].<ref name="ASM" /> The planet was also described as having more than one moon.

==Behind the scenes==
Had Delphon actually appeared in ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', it would have been depicted as a planet covered in windswept grasslands and picturesque plateaus, with a large human-centric main city and many outlying settlements of various sizes. Because of its location away of the sector's main trade routes, it would have been depicted as a mostly self-sufficient world frequently used as a base of operations for pirates. Conceptual drawings of Delphon were made by [[Cullen Brown]]. The Delphon in-game map would have featured buildings inspired by the [[Niima Bazaar]] from [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="The Art of Star Wars: Uprising">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref>
