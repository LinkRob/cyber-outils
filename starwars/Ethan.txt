'''Ethan''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Jedi]] who had no master.<ref name="Ninth Jedi" /> When he was contacted by [[Margrave]] [[Juro]], who was seeking to revive the [[Jedi Order]], Ethan traveled to [[Hy Izlan]], where he met six other Jedi who were waiting to receive [[lightsaber]]s crafted by the legendary [[sabersmith]] [[Lah Zhima]]. However, when Lah Zhima's daughter, [[Lah Kara]], delivered the lightsabers, the others revealed themselves to be [[Sith]] and prepared to take down Juro. Ethan drew his new lightsaber alongside Kara and Juro, defeating the others, save for [[Homen]], who joined them in their mission to restore the Jedi, and save Lah Zhima.<ref name="Ninth Jedi" />
