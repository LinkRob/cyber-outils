Prior to its release, the family was included in a [[StarWars.com]] preview for the novel on [[October 4]] of that year.<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-convergence-excerpt|text=A Mysterious Mission Begins in ''The High Republic: Convergence'' – Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref>
