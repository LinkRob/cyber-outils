'''Clerion''' was an uninhabited [[gas giant]] located in the [[Lower Tion]].<ref name="SG" /> It was situated in the [[Clerion system]] in the [[Palamut sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The former [[pirate]] [[Queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] marked it as a point of interest on a map she included in the [[book]] that would become known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''. [[Gallandro]] and his crew visited Clerion to steal an [[Codeleach|item]] from the [[Varcinius Repository]], located in geostationary [[orbit]] of the [[planet]].<ref name="SG" />
