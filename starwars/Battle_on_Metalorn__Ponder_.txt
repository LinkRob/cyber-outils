During the [[Clone Wars]] of [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''battle''' took place on [[Metalorn]] involving the [[Galactic Republic]]. Due to a [[Sohi|leak]] in the [[Feenix office|office]] of [[Senator]] [[Tanner Cadaman]], a member of the [[Galactic Senate]]'s [[Intelligence and Armed Services Committee]], details on Republic troop movements regarding Metalorn made it back to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. As a result, the Republic took losses at the battle, with the [[clone trooper]] being the sole survivor of his [[Ponder's squad|squad]] when their [[transport]] went down. A [[Unidentified Jedi general|Jedi general]] there called a medical evacuation for Ponder, who was shuttled to an [[orbital clinic]].<ref name="Sanctuary" />
