'''Electromagnetic radiation''' was a form of [[Radiation/Legends|radiation]]. The [[Gotal/Legends|Gotal]] were particularly sensitive to electromagnetic radiation. As a result, [[Vreshann Electronics, Incorporated]] manufactured a handheld [[EMS Scanner]].

A kind of radiation including visible light, radio waves, gamma rays, and X-rays, in which electric and magnetic fields vary simultaneously. [https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hassat-durr Hassat Durr] users could draw this into their bodies and manipulate it to a certain extent before it became dangerous.
