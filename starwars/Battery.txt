A '''battery''' was a device used to store energy. [[Naamite]] batteries were a type of battery.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> [[Conduit worm]]s absorbed battery cells while crowing around the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]'s power cables, turning them into organs.<ref name="New Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|What's the matter? Someone forget to change your battery?|Anakin Skywalker, to General Grievous|<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>}}
