'''Anzati''' were a long-lived<ref name="Insurgency Rising 1" /> [[humanoid]] [[species]] that hailed from the [[Mid Rim Territories]] [[planet]] of [[Anzat]]. They were feared and hated as they fed on the life force of other [[Sentience|sentient]] species.<ref name="HC3" />

==Anzati in the galaxy==
[[File:AnzatiAaylaSecura-HelmetCollection3.png|thumb|left|300px|An Anzati corners Jedi Aayla Secura]]
After [[Aurra Sing]] left the [[Jedi Order]], Anzati agents trained her into a killing machine.<ref name="JvS">{{Topps|set=2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives|subset=Jedi Temple Archives|cardname=Aurra Sing|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/114595/cid/8022643/2015-Topps-Chrome-Star-Wars-Perspectives-Jedi-vs.-Sith-39-J-Aurra-Sing}}</ref> An Anzati in robes,<ref name="HC3" /> similar to those of a [[Jedi]] or [[Sith]],<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> once got hold of the Jedi [[Aayla Secura]],<ref name="HC3" /> but she survived.<ref name="ROTS" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], an Anzati named [[Reyna Oskure]] was associated with the [[Acolytes of the Beyond]], who always quickly answered a summons from the group and was held in high regard by its members. Oskure was also known to [[Governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]], who requested Oskure to join him shortly into the [[Iron Blockade]], thereby playing into the Acolytes' plans.<ref name="Insurgency Rising 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:AaylaKaarko-SWREP34.png|thumb|right|250px|The full image of an Anzati&mdash;Volfe Karkko&mdash;and Aayla Secura from ''Star Wars'' Legends.]]
The Anzati were first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the Jedi Temple Archives set of [[Topps]] trading cards [[2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives|''2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives: Jedi vs. Sith'']], published in [[2015]].<ref name="JvS" /> They were first identified as a species and pictured in the [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 3|third issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]'' magazine series, published in [[2016]]. The issue used a comic image featuring an Anzati attacking Aayla Secura<ref name="HC3" /> originally printed in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] story [[Star Wars (1998) 34|''Star Wars'' (1998) 34]]<ref name="SW 34">[[Star Wars (1998) 34|''Star Wars'' (1998) 34]]</ref> to depict the canon Anzati.<ref name="HC3" /> In the comic, the Anzati in question was identified as the former [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Volfe Karkko]],<ref name="SW 34" /> but this is not confirmed to also be the case in canon. The Anzati went onto have their first full canonical appearance in the comic [[Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising 1|''Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising'' 1]].<ref name="Insurgency Rising 1" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Dannik Jerriko]] was identified as an [[Anzat (species)|Anzat]] of the Anzati in "[[Soup's On: The Pipe Smoker's Tale]]," a short story written by [[Jennifer Roberson]] that was included in ''[[Tales from the Mos Eisley Cantina]]'', which was released by [[Bantam Spectra]] in [[July]] [[1995]].<ref name="Soup's On: The Pipe Smoker's Tale">{{StoryCite|story=Soup's On: The Pipe Smoker's Tale|book=Tales from the Mos Eisley Cantina}}</ref> Unlike ''Star Wars'' Legends, the canon version of [[Jerriko]] was identified as a [[Mutant/Canon|mutant]] [[human]] in the 2016 reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']].<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
