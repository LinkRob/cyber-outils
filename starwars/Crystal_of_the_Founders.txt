The '''Crystal of the Founders''' was a fictional object that allowed an individual on [[At Attin]] to bypass the communication jamming effects of the [[Barrier]] surrounding the planet.<ref name="Good Guys" /> Around [[9 ABY]]<ref name="SCDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> the [[youngling]] [[Wim]] created a story about the crystal to distract the [[pirate]] [[Jod Na Nawood]] so his [[gender|father]], [[Wendle]] could sneak past Na Nawood and restore power in the [[Unidentified city (At Attin)|city]] and release the [[docking clamp]]s preventing the ''[[Onyx Cinder]]'' from taking off. In the story Wim claimed that his [[Skeleton Crew|group]] of friends found the crystal guarded by an ancient warrior in the northern mountains. Not believing the story Na Nawood, started to turn away, but before he could see Wendle, a youngling named [[Fern]] speaks up and continues the distraction, stating that Wim found the crystal awhile ago. The distraction worked and Wendle was able to restore power.<ref name="Good Guys" />
