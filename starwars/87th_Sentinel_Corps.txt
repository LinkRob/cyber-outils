The '''87th Sentinel Corps''' was an [[infantry]] [[corps]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> As a corps in the [[Republic Military]], the 87th Sentinel Corps consisted of 36,864 [[trooper]]s divided into sixteen [[regiment]]s.<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] establishes that a [[corps]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] contains 36,864 [[clone trooper]]s divided into sixteen [[regiment]]s. Since the 87th Sentinel Corps is a corps within the Grand Army of the Republic, it must have the same composition.</ref>

Its [[soldier]]s were distinguished from other units by the various combinations of [[Color|maroon]] markings on their white [[Phase II clone trooper armor]]. The 87th Sentinel Corps was experienced in urban defense tactics, and were often deployed to Republic [[City|cities]] that were at risk of falling under Separatist control. The unit was made up of standard [[clone trooper]]s, [[clone sharpshooter]]s, [[clone trooper officer]]s, and [[heavy weapons clone trooper]]s. Respectively, the soldiers of the unit were armed with [[DC-15A blaster carbine]]s, [[Valken-38 blaster rifle|Valken-38xs]], [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|DC-17 hand blasters]], [[DC-15A blaster rifle]]s, and [[DC-15LE]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 87th Sentinel Corps first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' [[Star Wars video games|video game]] published by [[Electronic Arts]] in [[2017]] and were the default clone trooper skin for [[Naboo]]<ref name="Battlefront II" /> but were not identified until an update for the game in [[February 27]], [[2019]].<ref name="Battlefront Forum">{{EA|subdomain=battlefront-forums|url=discussion/130124/community-transmission-clone-troopers|text=Community Transmission &ndash; Clone Troopers}}</ref> With the update, the appearance of the 87th clone troopers was also slightly modified in that the red coloring was made slightly darker and different patterns were added, along with equipment changes.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
