"'''Banner Days'''" is an article written by [[August Hahn]] and [[Cynthia Hahn]] as a tie-in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] [[Wizards.com|website]] on [[June 9]], [[2005]] and took the form of a short story about a [[Cularin Militia]] parade.
