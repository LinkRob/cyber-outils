'''Dwint Parsopalton''' was a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]] and was a member of the ''[[Star Hopper]]'' crew.<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 2" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="High Republic Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> when the [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] was [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destroyed]] by the [[Nihil]], Parsopalton was presumed [[Death|dead]] along with several others. A [[229 BBY|year]] later, the [[Galactic Republic]] file on Parsopalton was closed as the Padawan had been presumed dead for over a [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 2" /> Parsopalton had in fact survived and was present on [[Eriadu]] after the end of the [[Nihil conflict]].<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 20">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 20|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 20]]</ref>
