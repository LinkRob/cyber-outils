The '''Espinar system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Core Worlds]]. It was located in grid square K-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the [[planet]] [[Espinar]].<ref name="Origin" />
