The '''Amanin''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] yet primitive [[species]] from the [[planet]] of [[Maridun]]. They were tall, flat-bodied [[alien]]s with long, gangly [[Limb|arms]], and wrinkly hides covered in [[slime]]. At the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]], there were Amani living on [[Utapau]]. Those were considered dangerous by the [[Pau'an]] authorities, and their technology level was low.

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:Amani_DB.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Amani had a distinctive appearance.]]
The Amani were tall, thin [[Sentience|sentients]] with short [[Limb|legs]], gangly arms long enough to reach the ground, big hands, and [[Color|yellow-and-green]] skin<ref name="DB" /> coated in slime. They were capable of curling themselves into a ball and rolling as a means of locomotion.<ref name="DoU" /> The [[species]] had an average height of two [[meter]]s and a life expectancy of 90 [[standard year]]s.<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know" /> Their natural habitat was plains and [[grass]]land.<ref name="VE" />

==Society and culture==
Compared to many other species, the Amani were considered primitives. They had a tribal organization and dwelled in simple [[tent]]s. They were also regarded as dangerous, and Amani head hunters were once highly sought after.<ref name="DoU" /> Amani were often seen carrying staffs on which were strung several [[Bone|skulls]].<ref name="DB" />

==History==
The Amani [[Homeworld|hailed]] from [[Maridun]],<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know" /> an [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Ultimate SW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> covered in [[grass]] plains.<ref name="JC">{{TCW|Jedi Crash}}</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], a conflict that pitted the [[Galactic Republic]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], they came to share their homeworld with a group of [[Lurmen]] [[refugee]]s.<ref name="Lurmen">{{Databank|lurmen|Lurmen}}</ref>

==Amani in the galaxy==
During the [[High Republic Era]], an Amani named [[Prot Xan]] worked as a [[scientist]] for the [[Galactic Agricultural Alliance]].<ref name="Hidden Danger">{{InsiderCite|issue1=203|issue2=204|Starlight: Hidden Danger}}</ref>

By the [[time]] the Clone Wars was drawing to a close, the Amani had recently established settlements on [[Utapau]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> where they co-existed with the native [[Pau'an]]s and [[Utai]],<ref name="DoU" /> living as immigrant hunter-gatherers.<ref name="VE" /> They set up their encampments on the plains or underground, but rarely ventured into the [[City|cities]]. At that time, [[General]] [[Grievous]] used a rogue Amani as a [[bodyguard]] to facilitate a major arm deal with the [[Sugi]]. The [[Jedi Master]] [[Tu-Anh]] was [[Death|killed]] while investigating the matter, which prompted [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his master, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], to embark on a [[mission to Utapau]]. Once there, the two [[Jedi]] visited the [[Amani village|Amani quarter]] in [[Pau City]], where they learned from the local chief that an Amani outcast from the plains had been consorting with some Separatist [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s.<ref name="DoU" />

[[File:Amani_camp.jpg|thumb|250px|An Amani camp on the plains of Utapau]]
Using this information, Kenobi and Skywalker traveled on winged [[dactillion]] mounts to the plains of Utapau. They sighted an Amani encampment that was home to several Amani outcasts, who attacked them with [[spear]]s, [[arrow]]s, and [[Vilmarh's Revenge|precision laser darts]]. The two Jedi overpowered their attackers, but the Amani fled into the wilderness. While tracking the Amani outcasts, the two Jedi were captured by a party of Sugi [[Mercenary|mercenaries]], who had found a large [[kyber crystal]] that they were planning to sell to the Separatist [[Head of State|leader]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. These Sugi were accompanied by at least one Amani outcast. Later, the two Jedi fought their Sugi captors and stole the [[crystal]].<ref name="ISotC">{{TCW|In Search of the Crystal}}</ref>

While looking for a beast of burden to help them transport the kyber crystal, Kenobi encountered a plains Amani villager but used a Jedi [[mind trick]] to convince him that he had not seen the Jedi. Meanwhile, the Sugi leader [[Endente]] tried to cover up the theft of the kyber crystal by claiming that they could no longer rely on the plains Amani to guard it. To cover up the theft, Endente instructed Grievous to meet them at Pau City. However, Grievous saw through the deception and executed Endente.<ref name="Crystal Crisis">{{TCW|Crystal Crisis}}</ref>

Many years later, shortly before the [[Battle of Endor]], an Amani [[bounty hunter]] known as [[Amanaman]] was present in [[Boba Fett's Palace|the palace]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

The [[The Twins|Hutt Twins]] of [[Nal Hutta]] were served by a small army of Amani that provided both security and entertainment, like [[gladiator]] fights. During the [[New Republic Era]], four Amani warriors fought the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] in a pit under their [[Hutt Palace]], initially only for their Hutt Lords' amusement before unleashing a large [[Dragonsnake]] after he manage to kill the warriors, despite their advantage in swimming the depths. Djarin manage to fight [[Hutt's dragonsnake|their dragonsnake]] off with a spear from one of the Amani he killed. After Djarin escaped, the Amani gave chase, swimming in the swamp waters while the [[Droid Gotra]]'s [[sentry droid]]s clashed with him above in the Nal Hutta swamps. When [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] hid Djarin under a makeshift mud hut and got the dragonsnake [[poison]] [[antidote]] from a [[Unidentified species (Grogu)|local alien]] [[fisher]] named [[Gatori]], he encountered a crashed [[TR-RL3 Terrapin-class bulk tanker|TR-RL3 ''Terrapin''-class bulk tanker]] in a swamp which several Amani surrounded the ship in the waters. When Djarin recovered he recognized the tanker as the ship of [[Unidentified gunrunner droid|the]] [[Smuggler|gunrunner]] [[droid]] he hired to get [[Rotta]] away from the Twins, he went into the damaged tanker and fought six Amani warriors, but he manage to kill them with his [[IB-94 blaster]] and [[vibroblade]] before using the tanker to fight the Twins.<ref name="TMAG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref>

During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], an Amani named [[Bina]] operated a [[Creature Stall]] in the [[Black Spire Outpost Market]] on [[Batuu]].<ref name="Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]</ref>
