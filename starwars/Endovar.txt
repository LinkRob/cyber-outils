'''Endovar''' was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Lightsaber Collection">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> located in the [[Endovar system]] within the [[Terrabe sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="The Interior" /> It was situated in grid square M-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

During the [[High Republic Era]], a [[Jedi Temple (Endovar)|Jedi Temple]] of the [[Jedi Order]] was situated on Endovar.<ref name="THRA 12" /> The [[Jedi Master]]s [[Torban Buck]] and [[Kantam Sy]] both spent time on Endovar as [[Padawan]]s. On Endovar, the two participated in an event known as the [[Great Jedi Rumble Race]], though the tradition of hosting the race eventually stopped.<ref name="THRA 11" /> [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] watched after a group of Padawans on the object for some time.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> At some point, Sy's future Padawan, [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]], was present alongside them and Yoda at the [[Jedi temple]] on Endovar.<ref name="THRA 12" /> The [[Jedi]] also had a pen where they looked after [[Happabore]]s on Endovar, including one named [[Monsalmo]]. Endovar was also known to have very frequent [[season]]al festivals.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:HighRepublicAdventures11.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Endovar was first mentioned in ''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 11.]]
Endovar was first mentioned in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 11|eleventh issue]] of the [[2021]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2021)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']], published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="THRA 11" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]] and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="THRA 11" /> on [[December 1]], 2021.<ref name="Comixology THRA11">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-11/digital-comic/969152|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures #11}}</ref> The planet then made its first appearance in a flashback in the series' [[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 12|twelfth issue]], also written by Older, illustrated by [[Harvey Tolibao]] and [[Pow Rodrix]],<ref name="THRA 12" /> and published on [[January 12]], [[2022]].<ref name="Comixology THRA12">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-12/digital-comic/973695|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures #12}}</ref>

Endovar later appeared in flashbacks in ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'', a young-adult novel written by Older<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> that was published as part of Phase I<ref name="Phase" /> on [[February 1]], 2022.<ref name="Disney">{{Disney|url=book/midnight-horizon|text=Midnight Horizon|subdomain=books}}</ref> At one point, the novel mistakenly refers to Endovar as "Andovar,"<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> a spelling that was also repeated in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 7|seventh issue]] of the [[2023]] comic series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']]. The issue was written by Older and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 7">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 7|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 7]]</ref> on [[July 10]], [[2024]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-800/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-7|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #7}}</ref> Endovar was also incorrectly referred to as Endor in ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]'', a reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]]<ref name="High Republic Lightsaber Collection">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> that was published on [[November 12]] of the same year.<ref name="Phase" />
