The '''Dyne 2577 radial sublight engine''' was a large model of [[sublight engine]] manufactured by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]. The [[AA-9 Freighter-Liner]] produced by [[Botajef Shipyards]] was equipped with two Dyne 2577 [[engine]]s.<ref name="BMF50">{{BuildFalconCite|50|Starship Fact File}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Dyne 2577 radial sublight engine was first mentioned in the "[[Starship Fact File]]" department of the [[2015]] magazine [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 50|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 50]],<ref name="BMF50" /> which was written by [[Tim Veekhoven]].<ref name="Veekhoven">{{Twitter|sompeetalay|1036289998377680897|[[Tim Veekhoven]]|quote=#BuildtheMillenniumFalcon: "These officials, led by Constable Marcel Shradeer, received the authority to intervene whenever problems arose between the passengers."
