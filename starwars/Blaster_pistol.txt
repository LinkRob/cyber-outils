The '''blaster pistol''' was a common weapon used by both military personnel and civilians. [[Pistol]]s came in a variety of shapes, sizes, and capabilities.

==Description==
Some were augmented for other functions, such as [[ascension gun]]s that fired [[grappling hook]]s and [[ascension cable]]s. Many blaster [[pistol]]s contained [[stun]] settings which were used to incapacitate a target instead of dealing lethal damage. Like all [[blaster]] technology, the blaster pistol fired cohesive bursts of [[plasma]] or [[Particle beam|particle-based energy]] called "bolts." Blaster bolts could be repelled by [[magnetic seal]]s and [[deflector shield]]s.<ref name="Databank blaster pistol" /> They could also be deflected by a [[lightsaber]] and even redirected back to the shooter or some other target.<ref name="Databank lightsaber">{{Databank|lightsaber|Lightsaber}}</ref> A [[lightsaber-blaster hybrid]] is a lightsaber with a blaster pistol built in the hilt. So far it only fires stun bolts.<ref name="EV">''[[Endless Vigil]]''</ref> Types of blaster pistols included [[Heavy blaster pistol]]s,<ref name="Supply Lines">{{TCW|Supply Lines}}</ref> [[Blaster musket]]s,<ref name="So Much Trouble">{{SkeletonCrew|We're Gonna Be In So Much Trouble}}</ref> [[Hold-out blaster]]s,<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> [[Snub-blaster]]s,<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Bryar pistol]]s,<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars: Battlefront]]''</ref> [[Defender sporting blaster pistol]]s, and [[BH-4 Double Blaster]]s.<ref name="Eminence">{{TCW|Eminence (episode)}}</ref>

Two-barreled blaster pistol designs included the [[Blurrg-1120 holdout blaster]],<ref name="Rebels: The Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[RSKF-44 heavy blaster]], [[S-195 blaster pistol]], and the [["Snubble special" pistol]].<ref name="Solo Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> The [[A-180 blaster|A-180]] and [[A280-CFE convertible heavy blaster pistol|A280-CFE]] were blaster pistols that could be [[Reconfigurable blaster|reconfigured]] into an [[Assault rifle|assault]] [[blaster rifle]] and a [[sniper rifle]].<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> Blaster pistols could also have unique loading mechanisms as the S-195 had as hinged assembly that allowed for faster loading of reactant gas capsules or power plugs, the [[DG-29 heavy blaster pistol]] could be loaded from the side,<ref name="Solo Guide" /> and the [[MW-20 Bryar pistol]] featured a double ammo cartridge that rotated when the current cartridge overheated, readying a fresh one for firing.<ref name="Kassa">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref>

==History==
The [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]] primary wielded [[S-5 Security-grade blaster/ascension gun|S-5 blaster pistols]] and [[CR-2 heavy blaster pistol]]s.<ref name="Episode I" /> [[Bounty hunter]] [[Aurra Sing]] carried a pair of [[DX-13 blaster pistol]]s.<ref name="Complete Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> Bounty Hunter [[Zam Wesell]] carried a [[KYD-21 blaster pistol]].<ref name="Attack of the Clones">{{Film|II}}</ref> The bounty hunter [[Jango Fett]] was proficient at wielding twin [[WESTAR-34 blaster pistol]]s, at the same time.<ref name="Jango Fett Databank">{{Databank|jango-fett|Jango Fett}}</ref><ref name="Attack of the Clones" /> Members of the [[Black Sun]] wielded [[BH-4 Double Blaster]]<ref name="Eminence" /> while the [[Pyke Syndicate]] wielded [[CC-420 pistol]].<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref>  

Individuals and groups within the [[Coruscant Security Force]] like the [[Coruscant underworld police]] carried a heavy blaster pistol<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{SWE|groups|coruscant-underworld-police|Coruscant underworld police}}</ref> and [[Lieutenant]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo]] carried a [[A43 Hushabye]] pistol.<ref name="Blasters">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-blasters-so-uncivilized-but-so-awesome|text=''Star Wars'' Blasters: So Uncivilized, But So Awesome|nolive=1}}</ref> Various [[Police droid]]s also carried a blaster pistol model; with the [[GU-series Guardian police droid]]s carried a [[SS-410 Police Special blaster pistol]]<ref name="BuildR2">{{BuildR2Cite|65|Droid Directory}}</ref> and the [[Unidentified police droid model|Duro police droids]] and the [[Spaceport police droid]]s carried a [[RG-4D]] blaster pistol.<ref name="Tweet">{{Twitter|phatseejay|status/876778865540124673|Christian Johannesén|quote=The same ones. The E-5C is slightly modified with an added stock though.|archivefile=File:RG-4D_and_E-5C.jpg}}</ref> The [[Tactical Defense Force]] on [[Janix]] and carried a blaster pistol model, that was standard among the [[Tactical Defense Droid]]s and officers, like [[Brander Lawson]] and [[Two-Boots]].<ref name="SOM1">[[Shadow of Maul 1|''Shadow of Maul'' 1]]</ref>

[[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] typically fought with two [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|DC-17 hand blasters]],<ref name="usw">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> which were standard issue weapons in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Duros]] bounty hunter [[Cad Bane]] wielded a pair of [[LL-30 blaster pistol]]s.<ref name="Galactic Defense">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''</ref> [[General]] [[Grievous]] carried a custom [[DT-57 "Annihilator" blaster]] called the [[Grievance Striker]], which his nemesis Obi-Wan used to finally kill him.<ref name="SWVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

[[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] used a [[BR-14 blaster]] while [[Rescue of Princess Leia (9 BBY)|rescuing]] young [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="The Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Hidden Path]] member [[Tala Durith]] carried a [[WE-11 blaster pistol]] on [[Jabiim]],<ref name="Part V">{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref> while Path leader [[Kawlan Roken]] carried a [[ZP08 blaster pistol]].<ref name="The Visual Guide" />

[[Smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] made frequent use of [[Han's heavy blaster pistol|his DL-44 heavy blaster pistol]], which was previously owned by his mentor [[Tobias Beckett]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> [[Cal Kestis]] obtained an [[LW-896 Blaster Pistol|LW-896 blaster pistol]] from his former friend [[Bode Akuna]] and [[Duel on Tanalorr|killed]] him with it on [[Tanalorr]].<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] carried a [[Kanan's blaster|modified DL-18 blaster pistol]].<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> The [[Mandalorian]] [[Spectres|rebel]] [[Sabine Wren]] used twin [[WESTAR-35 blaster pistol]]s.<ref name="FF68">{{FactFile2014|68|SAB 1-4|Sabine's equipment}}</ref>

[[Saw Gerrera]] carried a [[DH-16 Blaster Pistol]] during the [[Liberation on Kashyyyk]].<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> [[Luthen Rael]] carried a blaster pistol which he used to engage [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces|Pre-Mor corpos]] on [[Ferrix]]<ref name="Reckoning">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref> and later kill his [[spy]] [[Lonni Jung]] upon learning the truth of the [[Death Star|energy project]]. His adoptive daughter [[Kleya Marki]] used a small blaster pistol when she [[Infiltration of Lina Soh Hospital|infiltrated]] [[Lina Soh Hospital]], killing several [[ISB tactical agent]]s and a few [[Stormtroopers]].<ref name="Make It Stop">{{Andor|Make It Stop}}</ref>

Many [[Galactic Empire]] personnel carried various blaster pistol models, with the [[SE-14r light repeating blaster]], [[Scout trooper]]s wielded an [[EC-17 hold-out blaster]],<ref name="SWVE" /> the [[coastal defender stormtrooper]]s sometimes carried an [[E-11P blaster]],<ref name="The Visual Guide" /> Some Imperial officers carried a [[Power 5 blaster]],<ref name="SWVE" /> [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] carried a [[RK-3 blaster]], though he rarely used the weapon in combat.<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref> [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] carried a [[Krennic's DT-29 heavy blaster pistol|DT-29 heavy blaster pistol]], which was non-regulated by Imperial protocol.<ref name="UVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[soldier]]s commonly used [[DH-17 blaster pistol]]s.<ref name="A New Hope" />

The [[Vilmarh's Revenge]] was a popular type of blaster pistol during the time of the [[Galactic Empire]], and the gangster [[Cikatro Vizago]] was an individual who carried such a pistol.<ref name="rebelsvisual">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] made use of an [[Lando Calrissian's X-8 Night Sniper|X-8 Night Sniper]].<ref name="Idiot's Array">{{Rebels|Idiot's Array (episode)}}</ref> [[Cornelius Evazan]] carried a [[SE-14C blaster pistol]], while [[Ponda Baba]] carried a modified [[DL-21 blaster pistol]].<ref name="A New Hope" /> [[Greedo]] attempted to kill Han Solo in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] with his [[DT-12 heavy blaster pistol]].<ref name="A New Hope" /> [[Dengar]] often carried a [[DL-21 blaster pistol]].<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref> [[Kay Vess]] carried a vintage [[VM-19 heavy blaster pistol]]. [[Crime lord]] [[Jaylen Vrax]] carried a [[Relby K-25 blaster]] pistol, while [[ND-5]] carried a DL-18 pistol.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

Mandalorian foundling [[Din Djarin]] primary used an [[IB-94 blaster]].<ref name="Rubies II">{{WebCite|url=https://www.rubies.com/the-mandalorian-blaster-small-the-mandalorian.html|text=The Mandalorian Blastech IB-94 Blaster (Small Size) &ndash; The Mandalorian|work=Rubies II, LLC|archivedate=20201221124556}}</ref> [[Greef Karga]] sometimes wielded at least two [[KYD-44 blaster pistol]]s or a [[PL-42 blaster pistol]] in combat. Former [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[shock trooper]] [[Carasynthia Dune|Cara Dune]] carried a [[MT2 blaster pistol]] with side [[scope]].<ref name="Mandalorian VisualGuide" /> [[Migs Mayfeld]] carried at least five blaster pistol models, two in his hip [[holster]]s;<ref name="Chapter 6">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 6: The Prisoner}}</ref> a [[W-SP73 pistol]] in his right holster and another model in his left,<ref name="Mandalorian VisualGuide">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> a trio of DT-12s; two were in two chest holsters, and a third in an [[automated weapon mounting]] attached to his [[backpack]].<ref name="Chapter 6" /> [[Moff]] [[Gideon]] carried a [[D-16 blaster pistol]] while operating on [[Nevarro]].<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> [[Marshal]] [[Cobb Vanth]] primary used a [[HF-94 heavy blaster pistol]] when defending [[Freetown]].<ref name="Mando Handbook">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Handbook]]''</ref> After reclaiming [[Boba Fett's armor|his]] [[armor]], [[Boba Fett]] used a [[DB-41 sporting blaster pistol]] against the [[Stormtrooper]]s on [[Tython]]<ref name="Chapter15">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 15: The Believer}}</ref> and later against the Pyke Syndicate on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Chapter7">{{BOBF|Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor}}</ref>

[[Pirate]] [[captain]] [[Brutus]] carried five different blaster pistol models—three on a [[bandolier]] and one holstered at each hip, one of them being a [[blaster musket]] which was in his right holster.<ref name="A Real Adventure">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}}</ref>

[[First Order officer]]s like [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] carried a [[SE-44C blaster pistol]].<ref name="TFA VD [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] carried a customized SE-44C with a [[chromium]] finish.<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> Many [[Resistance]] members used the [[Glie-44 blaster pistol]] as their standard-issue sidearm.<ref name="TFA VD" /> [[Zorii Bliss]] notably [[Zorii Bliss's blasters|wielded two]] [[E-851 blaster pistol]]s on [[Kijimi]].<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
