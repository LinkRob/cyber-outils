A '''crystal forest''' was a natural formation of [[Crystal/Legends|crystals]]. They appeared on a number of [[Planet/Legends|planets]], such as [[Calipsa]], [[Endor/Legends|Endor]], [[Goratak III]], and [[Samaria/Legends|Samaria]].
