'''Balanhai Savit''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]] as one of the twelve [[Grand Admiral]]s in the [[Imperial Navy]]. A member of a highly prominent [[music]]al family on [[Coruscant]] with wide-reaching [[Politics|political]] influence, Savit's prestigious background and artistic talents enabled his advancement. By [[1 BBY]], he was the [[commanding officer]] of the [[navy]]'s [[Third Fleet]], which he led from his [[flagship]], the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Firedrake]]''.

Savit was secretly opposed to [[Project Stardust]] and the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]], and undertook several efforts to undermine its construction. After Savit was exposed by Grand Admiral [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], a [[Skirmish against the Third Fleet|skirmish]] ensued between Thrawn's flagship ''[[Chimaera]]'' and several vessels of the Third [[Fleet]]. Ultimately, Savit was defeated, arrested, and eventually executed.

==Biography==
===Early life===
Balanhai Savit was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] whose family was prominent on [[Coruscant]] due to their [[music]] programs. As such, they had a strong cultural position that forced others to be politically aware of him. Savit himself was a gifted composer, conductor, and performer; during the [[Republic Era]], he performed his composition ''[[To the Stars]]'' at one of [[Balanhai Savit's mother|his mother's]] cultural galas&mdash;a work which he later transformed into a full [[opera]]. His performances attracted the attention of prominent figures such as [[Governor]] [[Grazlos]] and [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], leading certain figures to credit his naval [[Officer|commission]] to his contacts and favors among the [[Coruscanti]] elite. Nevertheless, he was frustrated by the politics which he considered endemic to the inner workings of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

At some point prior to [[13 BBY]], he composed a grand [[Glory of the Empire|Imperial Fanfare]] that would widely used within the [[Imperial Navy]].<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

After the Republic's [[Proclamation of the New Order|transition]] into the [[Galactic Empire]], Savit served in the [[Imperial Navy]] and eventually attained the highly decorated rank of [[Grand Admiral]], gaining [[Commander|command]] of the [[Third Fleet]]. His [[Flagship|flag]] was aboard the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Firedrake]]'', under the immediate command of his subordinate, [[Captain]] [[Boulag]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

===Project Stardust===
====Initial sabotage====
{{Quote|What could you do with the weapons and matériel—with the sheer number of credits—that are going into Stardust? The TIE Defenders, certainly; but how many other vital projects have been scrapped to feed Krennic's insatiable appetite?|Grand Admiral Savit, on his rationale for sabotaging the Death Star project|<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />}}
Savit eventually came to know of an Imperial construction project known as [[Project Stardust|Stardust]], the codename for the construction of a massive [[superweapon]] with the capability to destroy entire planets; he even learned the top-secret name of the [[battle station]]: the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. Savit felt that the construction of the Death Star was an extraordinary waste of resources that could be otherwise used to strengthen the Imperial Navy, and further predicted that the station was vulnerable to attack and destruction.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> Among those few Imperials aware of the project, he was not alone in these concerns; both [[General]]s [[Jylia Shale]] and [[Cassio Tagge]] were outspoken opponents of "Tarkin's folly," though Shale's career was marginalized as a result.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref><ref name="Darth Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2015) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 1]]</ref> Savit did not openly voice his concerns with the project, but his resentment for the project grew and eventually manifested itself in a desire to save the resources of the Empire from being squandered.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

====Working with pirates====
{{Quote|The ''Imperials'' aren't the problem, fancy man. The ''Imperials'' take their money and scamper off someplace to spend it. It's Grand Admiral Spit-Face Savit who's the problem.|[[Skulk]] to [[Brierly Ronan]], [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]], [[Pik]], and [[Waffle]]|<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />}}
Savit devised a scheme to work with [[pirate]]s in the [[Esaga sector]], whereby he would hire Imperial contractors to steal [[turbolaser]] components and other supplies for the Stardust project. Using the cover of [[gralloc]] attacks on their [[freighter]]s, Savit's pirates would feign [[hyperdrive]] malfunctions and disappear from their [[Kurost transfer point|transfer point]] in the [[Kurost sector]]. With the Empire assuming the disappearances were unfortunate accidents, the freighters would rendezvous in relatively close [[star system]]s and offload their stolen cargo. In the process, Savit's Third Fleet would undertake [[Anti-piracy campaign in the Esaga sector|anti-piracy operations in the Esaga]], both as a cover for Savit's actual dealings with the pirates and as a means to clear out competition against his criminal allies. In an effort to further mask his dealings, Savit communicated with the pirates via the [[Encryption G77|G77 encryption]], a code accessible only to the Empire's twelve Grand Admirals. By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Savit's thefts had proved a significant delay to Stardust's imminent completion.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

====Working with Thrawn====
{{Quote|Allow me to introduce Grand Admiral Savit, commander of the ''Firedrake'' and the Third Fleet. I don't believe you two have met before.|[[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] introduces Savit to Thrawn|<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />}}
The delays to Stardust prompted [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] to request a conference with [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] Palpatine and several high-ranking officials on Coruscant to discuss the matter. To Savit's dismay, however, the Emperor would abruptly change the meeting point to the ''Firedrake''. The location of Savit's flagship in the [[Sev Tok system]] was considered closer for the participants&mdash;especially Grand [[Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], who was preoccupied dealing with the [[Lothal campaign]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]&mdash;though both Savit and Thrawn suspected the move was political in nature.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

[[File:Thrawn-ToppsFinest2019.png|thumb|right|200px|Grand Admiral Thrawn was tasked with resolving Stardust's supply issues]]
Though Savit was familiar Thrawn's exploits, this was his first meeting with the [[Chiss]]. In addition to Savit, Thrawn, and Tarkin, the meeting was attended by [[Orson Callan Krennic]], the [[director]] of the Death Star project, to discuss Krennic's [[Project Stardust]], as well as the Emperor via [[hologram]] projection. The meetings addressed the concerns with Stardust&mdash;the very same supply-chain problems that Savit had secretly created. Though Thrawn pointed out that Savit was more capable in dealing with the issue given his familiarity with Governor Haveland's gralloc troubles, Tarkin expressed that Savit had duties elsewhere and recommended Thrawn handle the logistical issues. A wager was proposed where Thrawn was tasked with handling the gralloc issue; if he did so in a [[standard week]], his [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter|TIE Defender]] program would gain funding previously earmarked for Stardust.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

After the meeting, Savit escorted Thrawn back to his shuttle. He reflected on the [[alien]] Grand Admiral's physical impressiveness but also his political incompetence, evidenced by his cluelessness regarding the infighting between Krennic and Tarkin. Though Savit appeared to help Thrawn's mission by providing him information on the grallocs, he actually hoped that the Chiss's investigation would "take the heat" off his own operations while being unable to solve the problem.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

===Unmasking and arrest===
{{Quote|I steal from Stardust so that not everything Krennic has poured money into goes up in smoke and flame or scrap metal. I want ''something'' from Stardust to end up being useful to the navy.''"<br />"''So ultimately you did this out of loyalty to the Empire.''"<br />"''Is that how you see this? No. What he did was treason, pure and simple.|Savit, Thrawn, and Brierly Ronan|<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />}}
Savit's treason was exposed during the [[skirmish against the Third Fleet]], during which Thrawn offered a chance at a possible redemption by helping them [[Skirmish with Grysk warships|against]] [[Grysk|another threat]], but Savit refused. [[Imperial Security Bureau|ISB]] [[Major]] [[Dayja Collerand]] boarded the bridge and arrested Savit.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> He was eventually executed for treason.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|But most important of all, no matter how your expertise and ambition grew, you were always there in the midst of it. You conducted, you arranged, and you continued to perform your creations in tandem with your other performers. That combination, that total immersion in your craft, is what has defined you.|Thrawn, on Balanhai Savit|<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />}}
As a son of one of Coruscant's most prominent musical families, Savit was aware of his family's status and reflected it in conversations; he considered veiled political threats against them unacceptable. As a musician, he was a skilled composer, conductor, and player, immersing himself into his craft in a way that mirrored his command style in the Imperial Navy.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

Despite Savit's professed disdain for the political infighting in the Empire (which he considered "ridiculous nonsense" and likened to the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]]), he also considered himself adept at playing others' politics against each other, such as with Krennic and Tarkin. He was proud of his status as a Grand Admiral and considered himself superior to civilian officials such as Governor Haveland; he generally accorded other high-ranking [[Imperial officer|military officers]] similar professional respect, though his respect for Thrawn was colored by [[Xenophobia#Humanocentrism|humanocentrism]]. Savit was regarded as efficient and highly capable as an officer, though Tarkin believed that Savit lacked Thrawn's tactical and problem-solving proficiency.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

Savit's personal criticism of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star's]] construction was rooted in his belief in supremacy of the [[Imperial Navy]], favoring the expansion and strengthening of the fleet through programs such as Thrawn's TIE Defender. This antipathy drove him to treason against his Empire, working against the battlestation's construction, and his eventual downfall. Nevertheless, he held that someone would eventually find out how to destroy it.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />
