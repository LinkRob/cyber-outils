'''Docking bay 34''' was a [[hangar]] bay in [[Mirogana Spaceport]] in the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in the [[Cadma sector]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Toshaal system]]</ref> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[coordinator]] at the [[spaceport]] announced over the port's [[speaker]] system that the bay needed to be cleared for an incoming [[freighter]] from<ref name="Outlaws" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="GE2">{{EncyclopediaCite|General Equipment (2)}}</ref> [[Thyferra]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
