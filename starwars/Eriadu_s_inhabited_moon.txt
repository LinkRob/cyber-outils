'''Eriadu's inhabited moon''' was a [[moon]] that [[orbit]]ed the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Eriadu]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the moon had an estimated population of 1.2 billion. One of the [[Emergences]] of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] saw three fragments of the [[freighter]] ''[[Legacy Run]]'' enter the [[Eriadu system]]. [[Tempest Runner]] [[Kassav Milliko]] of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] group, having received the locations of future Emergences from [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]], intended to use the knowledge of the imminent [[Eriadu system Emergence|Emergence]] at Eriadu to extort the planet's [[governor]], [[Mural Veen]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

After two fragments entered the [[Star system|system]], one destroying a group of [[lommite]] [[transport]]s, Veen eventually agreed to Milliko's demands in return for the prevention of the Emergence. A third fragment headed toward the moon, but Milliko's [[corvette]] ''[[New Elite]]'' failed to [[microjump]] to the correct location in order to shoot it down, causing the fragment to strike the surface of the moon. Following the impact, a debris cloud rose from moon, and shock waves rolled across its surface.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
