{{Quote|If you sign yourself away to the Empire, I can't come with you. Times are tough now, but we can keep saving without taking their credits. When we have enough money, we can catch a ship and start over somewhere new. We'll leave together.|Caya, to Hoyel|<ref name="Wolf Trap" />}}
'''Caya''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who lived on the [[planet]] [[Kavith III]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. One [[Standard day|day]], Caya and his best friend [[Hoyel]] found a [[Kavith wolf]] with its [[Limb|leg]] stuck in a trap. After the boys argued over what to do, Caya convinced Hoyel to [[Death|kill]] the creature, but the [[wolf]] gnawed its leg [[Dismemberment|off]] and escaped before they could. Some [[time]] later, Caya discovered that Hoyel was planning to leave the planet to join the [[Galactic Empire]] and begged Hoyel to stay. The two had developed romantic feelings for each other, but Hoyel ignored Caya's pleas and left the next [[Local day|morning]]. Later, in [[4 ABY]], when Hoyel was captured by members of the [[Ewok]] [[species]] on the [[moon]] [[Endor]], he recalled his history with Caya and [[Hallucination|hallucinated]] Caya and the wolf encouraging him to set himself free.

==History==
===The trapped wolf===
{{Quote|Don't go. Please, Hoyel. Isn't this enough for you?|Caya, to Hoyel|<ref name="Wolf Trap" />}}
During the [[Imperial Era]], Caya lived on the [[planet]] [[Kavith III]]. One [[Local day|night]]<ref name="Wolf Trap" /> many [[Standard year|years]] before [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The main events of "[[Wolf Trap]]," which the story establishes take place many [[Standard year|years]] after its flashbacks, are concurrent to the [[Battle of Endor]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref> Caya and his best friend, fellow Kavith III [[Homeworld|native]] [[Hoyel]], traversed the planet's surface to [[Hunting|hunt]] following a blizzard, with both [[Gender|boys]] marveling at the [[color]]ful [[aurora]]s in the sky caused by the weather. After spending a while searching for game hidden beneath the [[Water|snow]], each boy found two frozen [[hare]]s.<ref name="Wolf Trap" />

[[File:Imperial_Emblem.svg|thumb|left|230px|Hoyel left Caya behind to join the Galactic Empire (insignia depicted).]]
Eventually, Caya spotted a [[Kavith wolf]] with a trap clamped on its [[Limb|leg]]. Seeing that the [[wolf]] was [[Diet|starving]] and attempting to gnaw its leg [[Dismemberment|off]], Hoyel refused to abandon it. The two held hands to comfort each other, and Caya convinced Hoyel to [[Death|kill]] the creature instead of freeing it because of the potential that the wolf would attack once freed. As the two fearfully approached the wolf with [[Knife|knives]] drawn, Hoyel noticed the wolf's [[eye]]s were the same color as Caya's. Before they could kill it, the wolf bit its trapped leg off and ran away.<ref name="Wolf Trap" />

Later, when Hoyel was nineteen, Caya discovered him looking at a [[Galactic Empire]] recruitment [[Hologram disc|holodisc]]. Realizing Hoyel planned to enlist, Caya felt betrayed, as Hoyel had promised not to and the two had developed romantic feelings for each other. Hoyel tried to justify his decision to join the Empire and convince Caya to come with him, but Caya refused, insisting they could eventually leave the planet by saving up [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] instead. Caya also shot down Hoyel's suggestion that they ultimately [[Desertion|defect]] from the Empire to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]], warning that doing so would leave them in perpetual danger. Although Hoyel promised to be careful and come home one [[Standard day|day]], Caya held out hope that Hoyel would decide their life together was enough. All night long, Hoyel held Caya against him in bed, but Hoyel left to join the Empire the next morning.<ref name="Wolf Trap" />

===A freed man===
{{Quote|I'm sorry. I promised I'd come home, but I couldn't.|Hoyel, to a hallucination of Caya|<ref name="Wolf Trap" />}}
[[File:Hoyel-PeekpasTale.png|thumb|right|250px|Hoyel, Caya's best friend who became an Imperial scout trooper]]
[[Time|Sometime]] after becoming an Imperial [[scout trooper]], Hoyel sent [[Currency|money]] to Caya for a year and a half.<ref name="Wolf Trap" /> In 4 ABY,<ref name="Date" /> Hoyel was stationed on the [[forest]]<ref name="Wolf Trap" /> [[moon]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Endor]] and was captured by members of the native [[Ewok]] [[species]]. While in [[Prisoner|captivity]], Hoyel imagined Caya's voice taunting him and reflected on the captured wolf he and Caya had encountered. One night, as Hoyel [[Sleep|slept]], he [[dream]]t of the day Caya discovered that he was joining the Empire. Although Hoyel feared that he and Caya would be on the run from the Empire if he escaped captivity and returned to Caya, he also feared that if he never returned home, his friend would not remember him fondly.<ref name="Wolf Trap" />

Eventually, Hoyel was approached by a horde of Ewoks and [[Hallucination|hallucinated]] Caya riding the three-legged wolf among the crowd. Fearing the Ewoks were coming to kill him, Hoyel sawed at his bindings with a makeshift knife as he apologized to the apparition of Caya. Just then, the Empire's [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]] exploded above Endor, signaling to Hoyel that the Empire had lost its [[Battle of Endor|battle]] against the Rebellion. Reminded of [[meteor]]s he and Caya used to watch together by the explosion, Hoyel turned to the hallucination of Caya, which nodded. Hoyel then used the makeshift knife to cut off his finger and slip out of his bindings, stripping his [[Stormtrooper armor|armor]] as he ran after the specter of Caya into Endor's woods.<ref name="Wolf Trap" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You think it's that easy to escape the Empire? When they catch you, they'll kill you&mdash;if not now, then someday. We'll never be able to stop running.|Caya, to Hoyel|<ref name="Wolf Trap" />}}
Caya was a male with long blond [[hair]] and brown eyes that shone amber in light. Hoyel considered him beautiful. Caya had romantic feelings toward and cared deeply for Hoyel, leaving him deeply hurt when Hoyel left him behind for the Empire. Caya was terrified of the Empire and believed it was impossible to run from. He wished to help the Rebellion and leave Kavith III but refused to join the Empire to achieve either goal. Caya was empathetic toward a trapped wolf and wanted to kill it to relieve it of its pain, but was terrified to do so and approached the wolf near tears.<ref name="Wolf Trap" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|We have to kill it. For its own good. We ''have'' to, Hoyel.|Caya, on the wolf|<ref name="Wolf Trap" />}}
Caya was a capable hunter and could utilize a [[hunting knife]].<ref name="Wolf Trap" />

==Equipment==
While hunting in the snow, Caya carried a hunting knife and wore wide [[wood]]en [[Clothing|slippers]] with a hooded pelt [[jacket]]. He also held a [[lantern]] when he spoke with Hoyel in the dark.<ref name="Wolf Trap" />

==Behind the scenes==
Caya appeared in flashbacks in the short story "[[Wolf Trap]]," written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published in the [[2023]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="Wolf Trap" />
