{{Quote|As you can see, my young apprentice, your friends have failed. Now witness the firepower of this fully armed and operational battle station.|Emperor Palpatine to Luke Skywalker, on the second Death Star|<ref name="RotJ" />}}
The '''DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station''', codenamed '''''Second Sun''''' and '''DS-2''', also known as the '''Death Star MK. 2''', the '''Death Star Mark 2''', '''Battle Station DS-II''', or simply the '''second Death Star''', was a partially completed [[moon]]-sized [[battle station]] constructed by the [[Galactic Empire]] as the successor to the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first Death Star]]. A larger and more powerful [[Death Star]], the second Death Star's long-term purpose was similar to the first: to terrorize [[planet]]s and [[star system]]s in league with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] through the use of its [[Superweapon|planet-destroying]] [[superlaser]]. However, the Death Star II also served another purpose: luring the Rebels into a [[Battle of Endor|battle]] that would, the Empire hoped, destroy them once and for all. Though designed in the likeness of its predecessor's appearance and function, the DS-2 Death Star saw a technical improvement through the incorporation of millions of [[millimeter]]-sized [[heat-dispersion tube]]s in place of the two-[[meter]]-wide [[thermal exhaust port]] exploited by the Rebel Alliance at the [[Battle of Yavin]].

Secretly constructed in [[orbit]] around the remote [[forest]] moon of [[Endor]], the Death Star II was larger than its 160-[[kilometer]]-wide predecessor and was constructed using more efficient methods. Positioned in synchronous orbit above Endor, the battle station was protected by a [[deflector shield]] projected by a ground-based [[SLD-26 planetary shield generator|shield generator]], which used so much power that it was slowly destroying Endor. With the superlaser being the main priority, the weapon was completed by [[4 ABY]] while the rest of the superstructure was still under construction. After the Empire secretly leaked the location of the "incomplete" second Death Star to [[Bothan]] [[Spy|spies]] working for the Rebellion, the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] arrived at Endor and were shocked to find the superlaser fully operational. In the ensuing battle, rebel [[Endor strike team|ground forces]] managed to destroy the [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] protecting the Death Star, allowing rebel [[starship]]s to infiltrate the battle station's [[reactor]] core and destroy the [[Space station|station]]. Combined with the [[death]]s of [[Darth Sidious]] and his [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the destruction of the second Death Star was a significant defeat for the Empire. Within a [[5 ABY|year]], the fractured Empire formally [[Galactic Concordance|surrendered]] to the [[New Republic]], the restored [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] by the Rebellion. Some of what little [[Death Star ruins|remained]] of the battle station fell on the waters of the [[ocean]] moon of [[Kef Bir]].

==Description==
===Design===
{{Quote|Through sheer stupid luck, the rebels managed to destroy the first Death Star. By rebuilding the Death Star, and using it ''as many times as necessary'' to restore order, we prove that their luck only goes so far. We prove that we are the only galactic authority and always will be.|[[Lieutenant]] [[Nash Windrider]]|<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Death_star_2_blueprints_sw_card_trader.png|thumb|left|200px|Schematics of the Death Star II]]
Upon completion, the DS-2 [[Death Star]]<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> would have been an immense [[battle station]] 200 [[kilometer]]s in diameter<ref name="RotJ novel">[[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (paperback novel)|''Star Wars: Return of the Jedi'' junior novelization]]</ref> that featured 560 internal levels which could house 2,471,647 passengers and crew.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> The [[space station]] was divided into two hemispheres at the [[equatorial trench]],<ref name="Card Trader" /> which itself featured a nested hierarchy of lesser trenches,<ref name="CL" /> along with 768 [[tractor beam]] emplacements,<ref name="AEYNTK" /> a multitude of [[Hangar|docking bays]],<ref name="RotJ" /> and an incomplete [[ion engine]] array<ref name="Card Trader" /> used to rotate the massive installation. The outer "shell" of the battle station was made of [[quadanium steel]], with the areas immediately above and below the equatorial trench being heavily armored. On the outer surface were the station's surface decks ("city sprawl"), which consisted of a dense conglomerate of towers.<ref name="CL" />

Inside were the station's 560 inner decks,<ref name="AEYNTK" /> which were positioned planarly, parallel to the equator. [[Artificial gravity generator|Artificial gravity]] was localized throughout the Death Star II. In the stacked inner decks, [[gravity]] was directed north to south while on the surface decks, gravity was directed towards the center of the station, allowing [[Gunner|gun crews]] to stand on the surface of the globe. The Death Star II's two polar columns, which ran from the reactor to each pole, helped stabilize the station's rotational capabilities. The station's two command sectors were located at the north and south poles, with the south also serving as temporary quarters for outer shell construction crews and as space for emergency replenishment stations for [[labor droid]]s.<ref name="CL" />

Above the Death Star II's main weapon, the [[superlaser]] dish installed in the station's northern hemisphere, was the [[Overbridge (Death Star II)|Overbridge]]. Patterned after the [[Bridge  (starship)|command bridge]] of the original Death Star,<ref name="CL" /> the Overbridge was where the superweapon's systems were monitored and controlled. A [["Tulip" style Imperial work station]] was part of the Overbridge.<ref name="RotJ" /> Located on the station's north pole was [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] [[Emperor's Tower|personal isolation tower]], a one-hundred-story tower which served as the [[Sith Lord]]'s command center and personal abode while on the battle station. The tower, designed from the outside to be intimidating and menacing, was patterned after Palpatine's personal headquarters on [[Coruscant]], the [[Imperial Palace]], formerly known as the Jedi Temple.<ref name="CL" />

Before its [[Battle of Endor|destruction]], the Death Star II's superstructure was still incomplete. Large sections of the outer hull were still missing, exposing the inner decks still under construction.<ref name="RotJ" />

===Armaments===
{{Quote|Our cruisers can't repel firepower of that magnitude!|Gial Ackbar|<ref name="RotJ" />}}
<div style="float:right">[[File:Dsiipown.png|thumb|250px|The Death Star II firing its superlaser]]</div>
The most prominent weapon on the Death Star II was its immense superlaser, which was located above the equatorial trench in the station's northern hemisphere<ref name="Card Trader" /> and was visually identical to that of the first Death Star.<ref name="RotJ" /> Powered directly by the station's hypermatter reactor,<ref name="CL" /> energy was passed through an array of massive [[kyber crystal]]s, forming a series of powerful laser beams.<ref name="UltimateSW" /> The eight<ref name="RotJ" /> tributary lasers were then converged by the superlaser's exposed concave focusing dish<ref name="CL" /> into a single destructive beam. Unlike the previous Death Star's eight equal sized tributary beams surrounding the dish, the Second Death Star's laser consisted of seven smaller equal sized beams circling the dish and one larger beam located in the center.<ref name="RotJ" /> An improvement on that of the first Death Star, the redesigned superlaser required mere [[Standard minute|minutes]]&mdash;as opposed to [[Standard hour|hours]]&mdash;to recharge, and could be focused more finely, allowing it to fire at moving targets such as [[capital ship]]s.<ref name="RotJ novel" /> Like the original superlaser, the power of the Death Star II's superlaser could be controlled.<ref name="RotJ" /> Though its main purpose was the destruction of [[planet]]s,<ref name="CL" /> the laser could be tuned to a much lower power setting, suitable for destroying individual [[Starship|spacecraft]].<ref name="RotJ" />

The Death Star II had extensive point-defense capabilities, featuring 15,000 heavy and 15,000 standard [[turbolaser]] batteries along with 7,500 [[laser cannon]]s and 5,000 [[ion cannon]]s<ref name="UltimateSW" /> spread across its outer surface.<ref name="CL" /> Some areas of the station were equipped with [[Super Blaster 920 laser cannon]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> With the battle station housing 637,835 [[Imperial Navy|Naval]] and [[Imperial Army|Army]] personnel,<ref name="AEYNTK" /> the installation boasted a large complement of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s and their variants, stored for [[Realspace|space]]-based encounters, and a plethora of [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]s and [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]s for ground surface operations.<ref name="UltimateSW" />

Along with its own laser cannon emplacements, the Emperor's isolation tower was surrounded by the largest concentration of anti-[[starfighter]] gun emplacements on the station. Placed at intervals of as little as a few hundred [[meter]]s, the guns also had improved fire-control compared to all other cannons on the station.<ref name="CL" />

===Power===
[[File:DeathStar2Core.png|thumb|left|250px|The hypermatter reactor core of the second Death Star]]
At the center of the station<ref name="CL" /> was its [[hypermatter reactor]] core.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> Approximately one-tenth the diameter of the station itself,<ref name="CL" /> the cavernous [[reactor]] housed a main assembly consisting of a north and south tower, which were stabilized by power regulators.<ref name="RotJ" /> The reactor core was encased in [[capacitor panel]]s and supplied power to the entire station through two cylindrical polar columns, which ran from the reactor to each pole. Subsidiary shafts ran from the polar column as well as from four large reactor shafts&mdash;bent fifteen degrees in five places&mdash;that extended from the reactor to the outside of the station, two of which exited at the equatorial trench. In response to the original Death Star's destruction<ref name="CL" /> at the hands of [[Luke Skywalker]] during the [[Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> the second Death Star's design incorporated millions of [[millimeter]]-sized [[heat-dispersion tube]]s in place of the two-meter-wide [[thermal exhaust port]] exploited by the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="CL" />

A pressurized explosion to the core was capable of detonating the reactor, an explosion of which would be,<ref name="RotJ" /> like the first Death Star,<ref name="ANH" /> catastrophic to the station.<ref name="RotJ" />

==History==
===Construction===
{{Dialogue|Massa|It can't be. Commander Skywalker blew that thing to bits with a proton torpedo. It's space dust.|Madine|An old holo, then.|Ackbar|This footage was obtained by Bothan operatives less than a week ago. It's from Endor&mdash;on the edge of civilized space. The Empire has begun construction on a second Death Star.|attr=Admiral Massa, General Madine, and Admiral Ackbar regarding the second Death Star in a conference on [[Zastiga]]|src=<ref name="Moving Target">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref>}}
With the destruction of the first Death Star at the Battle of [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]], the Empire was left in a vertiginous state as the [[Imperial Military]] reeled at such a sizable loss of its martial forces. With the dissolution of the [[Imperial Senate]] and increasing [[pirate]] and rebel attacks, the Emperor [[0 ABY|immediately]] began construction on a new and improved battle station, one more powerful and without the fatal flaw of its predecessor. However, despite its main purpose as a terror weapon for use against star systems, Emperor Palpatine also planned another use for the weapon: upon completion of the battle station's superlaser, the Emperor intended to leak the location of the apparently incomplete and inoperable second Death Star to the Rebel Alliance, luring their forces into a devastating final battle. After crushing the Rebellion, the Emperor planned to send invasion [[fleet]]s larger than any seen in [[Standard century|centuries]] to [[blockade]] the rebellious worlds of [[Mon Cala]] and [[Chandrila]] until the Death Star II's completion. The Emperor hoped the destruction of these rebel-sympathetic worlds would effectively end any hope [[the galaxy]] might have had of defeating the Empire and its tyrannical regime.<ref name="CL" />

To build this even larger station several years faster than its predecessor, newly appointed [[Moff]] [[Tiaan Jerjerrod]] was given oversight of the project under the cover title "[[Director of Imperial Energy Systems]],"<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> who the Emperor trusted due to his previous work in constructing the first Death Star.<ref name="Moving Target" /> Deemed to be perfect for the secret project<ref name="Stewards of the Forest Moon">{{InsiderCite|212|Stewards of the Forest Moon}}</ref> under the codename [[Project Second Sun|''Second Sun'']].<ref name="Star Wars 26" /> Because of how remote it was,<ref name="Stewards of the Forest Moon" /> the [[Endor system]] was chosen to be the site where the Death Star would be constructed. The moon of Kef Bir was chosen as the initial construction site, but it was later moved to the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]] in the same system.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> A [[Sanctuary Pipeline|secret hyperspace lane]] was carved to reach the system from the factory world of [[Sullust]].<ref name="Moving Target" /> A dozen planets were [[Mining|stripped bare]] just to acquire the raw materials to make this second Death Star.<ref name="beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> The metallic moon of [[Alprezar]], for example, was extensively mined for that purpose.<ref name="TROS VD" />

[[File:DeathStarIIHangars-TCSBRBD2.png|thumb|250px|The equatorial regions of the Death Star II under construction]]
Materials from the [[Karthon Chop Fields]] were used in the construction.<ref name="Mando Handbook">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Handbook]]''</ref> The construction of the Death Star II was conducted in four main stages. The primary stage focused on assembling the components necessary for construction of the reactor core as well as the two massive cylindrical polar columns used to distribute power around the station. With the main power systems in place, four reactor shafts were then fixed to the reactor. Next, a polar cap was fixed to the free end of each polar column, and subsidiary shafts were added to the columns and the four reactor shafts. The equatorial, or "waistband," regions were then constructed, as they contained important docking facilities and the ion thrusters that rotated the station. The third stage prioritized construction of the superlaser. After the main weapon's completion, construction efforts were then focused on the station's outer shell and inner levels, including the Emperor's tower, which was anchored to the station long before his arrival.<ref name="RotJ" />

As was confirmed during development of the first Death Star, construction advanced most efficiently when the working surface allowed for sufficient space for the greatest possible number of self-replicating [[construction droid]]s. This was Moff Jerjerrod's justification for filling the inner structures in a piecemeal manner.<ref name="RotJ" /> During initial construction, the reactor also required temporary thermal exhaust ports, which the Moff ensured were heavily armored.<ref name="beware" /> Arrays of temporary [[repulsorlift generator]]s were installed in engineering sectors to assist construction, and the countless [[welding droid]]s in operation meant that large areas of the station were constantly illuminated, allowing [[human]] crews to supervise construction operations. Convoys of spacecraft were in constant transit to and from construction areas, supplying materials to active crews. During development, outer-shell-construction crews lived in the far south of the station, sharing the space with energy-replenishment stations for labor droids.<ref name="CL" />

While initial progress was steady, budget cuts, supply chain breakdowns and a lack of laborers soon conflicted with Jerjerrod's schedule.<ref name="beware" /> Unbeknownst to him, the Emperor, in keeping with his plan to fool the Rebels into believing the battle station was incomplete, secretly ordered his agents to sabotage supply convoys traveling to Endor.<ref name="CL" /> Hoping to show [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]] detailed reports on the station's construction and thus sway his opinion to realize the impossibility of his task, Jerjerrod was swiftly brushed aside by the [[Sith]] Lord, who professed the Emperor's lack of contentedness with the project, warning him to double his efforts. Despite increasing the pace of construction,<ref name="beware" /> much of the station's inner structures were still exposed and many areas covered in scaffolding and girders upon the Emperor's arrival<ref name="RotJ" /> several [[Standard week|weeks]] later.<ref name="beware" />

The Death Star II was not positioned in a naturally synchronous [[orbit]] around Endor and so required considerable uplift force to counteract the moon's gravity. Held in place by a repulsorlift field projected from an [[Death Star's shield generator|installation]] on Endor's surface,<ref name="Command Center North">{{EA|url=games/starwars/battlefront/star-wars-battlefront-2/news/tips-and-tricks-exploring-death-star-ii-in-battlefront-ii|text=Exploring the Locations of Star Wars™ Battlefront™ II: Death Star II}}</ref> the immense force from this interaction caused disturbances in the moon's crust, including [[groundquake]]s and lakes spilling out from their natural basins. During the final stages of the Death Star II's construction, a [[deflector shield]] was [[SLD-26 planetary shield generator|projected]] around the station from the same ground installation,<ref name="CL" /> protected by a large gathering of elite [[stormtrooper]]s stationed there in preparation for the Rebellion's arrival.<ref name="RotJ" /> The power required for the shield itself further disturbed Endor. In fact, if the relationship between the Death Star and the moon had continued for longer than it ultimately did, the moon would have broken apart from the pressure.<ref name="Masters">{{StoryCite|story=Masters|book=Stories of Jedi and Sith}}</ref>

While the Empire was counting on the moon one day crumbling under that pressure,<ref name="Masters" /> the remote moon was considered the perfect place for the shield generator<ref name="Stewards of the Forest Moon" /> per the Emperor's plan to draw the Rebels into a trap.<ref name="RotJ" /> The Empire also appeared to face no resistance from the native [[Ewok]]s,<ref name="Stewards of the Forest Moon" /> but the small [[alien]]s did eventually start to harass their occupiers.<ref name="Ewok Escape">{{FOD|Ewok Escape}}</ref> An entire Ewok village had stood where the shield generator base was chosen to be built, and that village had been destroyed by the Empire to make room for the installation.<ref name="beware" /> At some point before the Battle of Endor, the second Death Star's software was infiltrated by [[IG-88B|IG-88]]. However, the [[droid]]'s plans were thwarted by the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-Q5]].<ref name="R2-Q5's Card">{{ForceCollection|R2-Q5|star=3}}</ref> At another point, the Empire's [[Alpha Squadron (TIE/IN interceptor)|Alpha Squadron]] flew near the station.<ref name="Alpha Squadron">{{FFGXW2|set=Skystrike Academy Squadron Pack|cardname=Alpha Squadron Pilot}}</ref>

===Rebel discovery===
{{Quote|Luke&hellip;what is it? What do you need to tell us?''"<br />"''The Empire's building another Death Star.''"<br />"''If we could do it once&hellip;we can do it again. Let's get to work.|Mon Mothma and Luke Skywalker|<ref name="SW 28">[[Star Wars (2020) 28|''Star Wars'' (2020) 28]]</ref>}}
After [[Jon Melton]] and [[Bevelyn]], two [[Crimson Dawn]] spies, [[Mission to reveal the Death Star II|escaped]] from the Death Star with valuable information, they contacted the Rebels and asked for help in exchange for valuable information. After the Rebellion dismissed their claims, the family eventually made their way to Coruscant. [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]] later [[Mission to Coruscant|traveled]] to rescue them, and they told Skywalker about the existence of a second Death Star. Back at the Rebel fleet, Skywalker informed [[Mon Mothma]], [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] and [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] about the Death Star, and Mothma decided they should get to work on how to destroy it like they did with the original one.<ref name="SW 28" />

[[File:DS2plans-ROTJ.png|thumb|left|250px|A [[Hologram|holographic]] representation of the Death Star II based on information relayed by Bothan spies]]
With the Death Star II's superlaser fully armed and operational, the Emperor's plan to draw the Alliance into a doomed attack was coming to fruition.<ref name="CL" /> By Palpatine's order, large portions of the Imperial Starfleet began massing in the [[Hudalla system]] in preparations for the upcoming engagement. After members of the [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps]] under [[Corona Squadron]] arrived in the system, the Empire immediately deployed several TIE/ln starfighters to destroy the vessels. Admiral [[Firmus Piett]] gave Commander [[Ciena Ree]], in charge of the TIE squadron, secret orders to allow at least one of the Rebel fighters to escape. Ultimately, all but one of the Rebel ships managed to escape and bring their intelligence back to [[Alliance High Command]], who received further information given to them from the [[Bothan Spynet]]<ref name="Lost Stars" /> regarding the Death Star's location and presumed vulnerability, which had been purposely leaked by Sidious's agents.<ref name="CL" /> In an effort to allow its [[Rebel Alliance Navy|scattered fleet]] time to assemble over the Outer Rim world of Sullust, the Alliance embarked on [[Operation Yellow Moon]], which aimed to distract the Imperials from the buildup until it was too late.<ref name="Moving Target" />

With the station's superstructure still under construction over Endor and the Rebels falsely believing its superlaser was non-operational, along with learning that the Emperor himself would be overseeing the final stages of its completion, the Alliance could not resist the opportunity to destroy not only the Empire's newest superweapon, but also its head of state.<ref name="RotJ" /> While waiting for the Rebels to attack, Palpatine felt the death of [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] from across the galaxy, only for the [[Force spirit]] of the dead Jedi to appear before him and proclaim he could not win the war. When Palpatine declared that he already had won, he found that Yoda had vanished.<ref name="Masters" />

Shortly before the Battle of Endor, the-now Admiral [[Gallius Rax]] was summoned to Palpatine's throne room on board the second Death Star. Rax's visit coincided<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> with the landing of a rebel strike force that included Skywalker on the Sanctuary Moon.<ref name="RotJ" /> Sensing a shatterpoint approaching, Sidious told Rax to be ready to carry out the [[Contingency]]. He commanded Rax to move the [[Super Star Destroyer]] ''[[Ravager]]'' to the [[Vulpinus Nebula]] until the events of the shatterpoint had been resolved.<ref name="Empire's End" />

===Destruction===
{{Main|Battle of Endor}}
{{Quote|That blast came from the Death Star. That thing's operational!|Lando Calrissian|<ref name="RotJ" />}}
[[File:LukeParryingVaderOnDSII-SWROTJVA.png|thumb|right|250px|Luke Skywalker duels his father once again within the Emperor's Tower.]]
With the fleet assembled over Sullust,<ref name="Moving Target" /> the Alliance began planning their attack. Knowing the Death Star was protected by a ground-based planetary shield generator, a [[Endor strike team|strike team]] led by General [[Han Solo]] was dispatched to the surface of Endor in order to destroy the shield generator and allow the fleet to enter the battle station and destroy the reactor core. However, during the operation, the Rebels were captured by the stormtroopers stationed there, as the Emperor had planned. Upon arrival, the Rebel fleet conducted a scan of the station, indicating the shield was down; however, they soon found out their [[Sensor|scanners]] were being jammed. Realizing they had fallen into a trap, General [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] ordered the vessels to pull back from their attack. However, upon turning back, the Rebels discovered the fleet<ref name="RotJ" /> from the Hudalla system<ref name="Lost Stars" /> had amassed in the distance and were deploying their starfighters. Having ordered the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyers]] to stay back, Palpatine revealed his surprise by commanding Jerjerrod to fire on the Rebel fleet. To the Rebels' astonishment, the Death Star's superlaser fired on<ref name="RotJ" /> the ''[[Liberty]]'',<ref name="Lost Stars" /> obliterating it instantly.<ref name="RotJ" />
[[File:FalconEscapesDSII-ROTJHD.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The destruction of the second Death Star represented the beginning of the end for the Empire.]]
While Ackbar argued for retreat, Calrissian assured him that Solo's team would take the shield down and ordered the fleet to move towards the Star Destroyers as [[Nautilian|another]] [[MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser|Mon Calamari Cruiser]] was destroyed by the Death Star, hoping the [[Star Destroyer|Imperial vessels]] would provide them with cover. After several more minutes of [[Starfighter combat|dogfighting]], the Rebels' sensors detected that the Death Star II's deflector shield had finally been deactivated. In response, Calrissian led the starfighters of [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red]] and [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadrons]] into one of the Death Star's reactor shafts. As the ships made their way to the reactor,<ref name="RotJ" /> [[Arvel Crynyd]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> of [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]] managed to crash his damaged [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor|A-wing]] into the bridge of the<ref name="RotJ" /> nineteen-kilometer-long<ref name="SWE Super Star Destroyer">{{SWE|technology|superstardestroyer|Super Star Destroyer}}</ref> [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|Super Star Destroyer]], ''[[Executor]]'',<ref name="Executor DB">{{Databank|executor|''Executor''}}</ref> causing it to plummet towards the surface of the Death Star, where it pierced the surface and erupted in flames.<ref name="RotJ" />

[[File:InfernoSquadEndor.png|thumb|right|250px|[[Inferno Squad]] witness the destruction of Death Star II from Endor.]]
As the ships made their way through the tight reactor shaft, Calrissian ordered all [[pilot]]s bar himself and [[Red Leader]] [[Wedge Antilles]] to split off and return to the surface in an attempt to draw most of the pursuing [[TIE series|TIE fighters]] away. With the Emperor now having been [[Death|killed]] at the hands of [[Darth]] Vader, some of the battle station's personnel began evacuating as the battle raged outside. Entering the cavernous reactor core, Calrissian told Antilles to destroy the power regulator on the reactor's north tower, while he used the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to destroy the bottommost component of the tower. As Antilles and Calrissian escaped back into the shaft, the crippled reactor collapsed and detonated. Making their way back through the shaft, Antilles exited the station as Calrissian folllowed close behind. While the final pursuing [[TIE/IN interceptor|TIE interceptor]] was engulfed by the explosion, Calrissian narrowly escaped the collapsing shaft and directed the ''Falcon'' towards the Rebel fleet as the Death Star II exploded.<ref name="RotJ" />

===Legacy===
====Downfall of the Empire====
{{Quote|This is Leia Organa, last princess of Alderaan, former member of the Galactic Senate, and a leader in the Alliance to Restore the Republic. I have a message for the galaxy. The grip of the Galactic Empire on our galaxy and its citizens is relinquished. The Death Star outside the forest moon of Endor is gone, and with it the Imperial leadership.|Leia Organa|<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>}}
[[File:KefBir-SWcom.png|thumb|right|250px|The wreckage of the second Death Star lay in Kef Bir's sea.]]
Following the destruction of the second Death Star, Imperial records were sent into a state of disarray, with numerous missing persons and unconfirmed dead or wounded.<ref name="Lost Stars" /> In addition, a vast field of detritus orbited Endor. With several thousand death notices to write and records to update, the task was pushed to the side of Imperial priorities in the days after Endor as the Rebel Alliance capitalized on its stunning victory by pushing the shattered Imperial forces off of many worlds across the galaxy, including the Emperor's [[homeworld]] of [[Naboo]], one of the first to fall. [[Holovid]]s of the station's destruction were disseminated across star systems, while the Empire worked to root out and punish those responsible in spreading what it deemed [[treason]]ous lies.<ref name="Aftermath" />

Nonetheless, the truth of the station's destruction eventually became known to the general citizenry, and both the first and second Death Star continued to serve as symbols of fear and oppression for years to come, permanently leaving their marks on citizens across the galaxy.<ref name="Aftermath" /> Following the proven effectiveness of the [[Starhawk]], prototype of the [[Starhawk-class battleship]] line, against Imperial ships, the idea of focusing on building more powerful weapons, despite the difficulties in their construction, was taken seriously by the New Republic. Immediately after the Starhawk's [[Ambush at the Zavian Abyss|success]] on the [[Zavian Abyss]], a brief conversation among New Republic pilots involved the idea of the Republic building a Death Star to end the war, although the destructive nature of the Death Star fueled opposition as it did not align with New Republic principles.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> With the Empire falling apart, many believed that total victory for the recently formed [[New Republic]] was imminent. Nonetheless, a [[Standard year|year]] and four [[Standard day|days]] later, Imperial remnants were still fighting the Republic for control of the galaxy, culminating in the Empire's last stand at the [[Battle of Jakku]]. The [[Galactic Concordance]] was ultimately signed mid-battle, formally ending the [[Galactic Civil War]] and transforming the Empire into a mere rump state.<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Habitation_spheres.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Early concept art for Had Abbadon with two orbiting Death Stars by Ralph McQuarrie]]
The idea of a second Death Star dates back from the early drafts of the [[1983]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. In those versions, two Death Stars orbited the Imperial planet [[Had Abbadon]], which ultimately evolved into Coruscant, as seen in concept art by [[Ralph McQuarrie]].<ref name="TAS">''[[Star Wars: The Annotated Screenplays]]''</ref> [[Original trilogy]] producer [[Gary Kurtz]], on the other hand, stated that the earliest outline for Episode VI didn't include another Death Star, and its inclusion was a decision which displeased him.<ref name="Gary's Displeasure">{{WebCite|url=http://movies.ign.com/articles/376/376873p4.html|text=An Interview with Gary Kurtz|author=P., Ken|date=2002-11-11|work=[[Wikipedia:IGN|IGN]]|archivedate=20120426114524}}</ref>

In the early versions of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], the second Death Star was to be built in [[orbit]] around [[Kashyyyk]]. The construction would be done by [[Wookiee]] [[Slavery|slave]] labor. [[George Lucas]] then decided to switch the Death Stars's location to the [[Endor|forest moon of endor]] and the Wookiees were replaced with [[Ewok]]s.<ref name="Much to Learn">{{SW|url=news/much-to-learn-you-still-have-8-things-you-might-not-know-about-wookiees|text=Much to Learn You Still Have: 8 Things You Might Not Know About Wookiees}}</ref>

[[File:WatchYourTime.jpg|thumb|left|300px|[[Nilo Rodis-Jamero]] and [[Phil Tippett]] working on a Death Star II model for ''Return of the Jedi''.]]
The design of the Death Star II's was a challenge for the crew of ''Return of the Jedi'' because it was to suggest a weapon that was incomplete yet still formidable, leading the artists to experiment with different stages of completion. The final Death Star model measured 137 centimeters in diameter, and only included the station's "front" side. Constructed of Lucite, the station also featured photo-etched thin metal sheets. Micro holes were scored into the Death Star's surface, illuminating the station with hundreds of tiny lights. The second Death Star model is actually the reverse of what appears on film, with most of its left-hand superstructure incomplete. The image of the battle station was flipped horizontally when used in the final film.<ref name="DSII Databank">{{DB|location|deathstarii|Death Star II}}</ref>

Other sections of the station were created through sectional models and matte paintings. One painting of an equatorial hangar bay features elements of the [[Wikipedia:San Francisco|San Francisco]] skyline, including the [[Wikipedia:Transamerica Pyramid|Transamerica Pyramid]].<ref name="DSII Databank" />

One early idea for the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] had Rey living on a junk planet where part of the Death Star II was submerged underwater. Concept art in ''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'' shows her diving, swimming through the Death Star's trench to scavenge goods only to eventually discover pivotal information about the remaining Jedi and Luke Skywalker. Later concept art shows Rey taking the ''Millennium Falcon'' subaqueous to find the Emperor's chamber submerged deep underwater.<ref name="Art TFA">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref> However, the idea was later recycled for the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="Episode IX" />
