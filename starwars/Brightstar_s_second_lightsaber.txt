'''Brightstar's second lightsaber'''<ref name="Lightsaber Collection">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> was a [[Color|green]]-bladed [[Training lightsaber|training]] [[lightsaber]] formerly wielded by [[Grand Master|Jedi Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] and later gifted to [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Kai Brightstar]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Yoda's Mission" /> The blade had a silvery finish with a compact profile and a prominent activation button. The lower grip of the blade had a woven cloth wrapping. The blade, being scaled for Yoda's use, was useful in the hands of the human trainee.<ref name="Collection">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> Brightstar originally lost [[Kai's first lightsaber|his lightsaber]] to save his friend and fellow youngling [[Nubs]] from falling off the ''[[Crimson Firehawk]]''.<ref name="Yoda's Mission" /> Kai would use his lightsaber during a training session against a training droid.<ref name="Meet the Young Jedi" /> He later developed [[Jar'Kai]] with his green and blue training lightsabers to fight off sentient droid [[Apexx]] before he repented. Kai later returned the green training lightsaber to Yoda.<ref name="Making Friends" />
