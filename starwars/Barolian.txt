==Behind the scenes==
Barolians first [[canon]]ically appeared in the [[2021]] audio drama ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> The species was only identified in the audio play's [[The High Republic: Tempest Runner (script)|script book]], which was published in [[2022]].<ref name="Script" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Barolian/Legends|Barolians]] were a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Subspecies/Legends|race]] from the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Baroli/Legends|Baroli]] who were distinguishable by their [[Color/Legends|blue]] [[Hair/Legends|hair]].<ref name="EA Prey">''[[Evasive Action: Prey]]''</ref>
