The '''Anaxes asteroid belt''', also known as the '''Anaxes asteroid ring''', was an [[asteroid field]] located in the [[Azure sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was created when a [[Destruction of Anaxes|cataclysmic event]] destroyed the [[planet]] [[Anaxes]] after the end of the [[Clone Wars]]. One of its [[asteroid]]s, the [[planetoid]] [[PM-1203]], housed [[Fort Anaxes]], an abandoned [[Galactic Republic]] military installation that had been located on Anaxes' surface during the Clone Wars.

==Description==
The Anaxes asteroid belt,<ref name="Dawn" /> otherwise known as the Anaxes asteroid ring,<ref name="SW:TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> was an [[asteroid field]] located in the [[Azure system]]<ref name="USW" /> of the [[Azure sector]]<ref name="Dawn" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Situated along the [[Perlemian Trade Route]],<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> the [[asteroid]] field was formed from the remains of the [[Destruction of Anaxes|destroyed]] [[planet]] [[Anaxes]] and was home to colonies of [[fyrnock]]s.<ref name="Fort Anaxes" />

Despite being an asteroid field, several asteroids within maintained trace [[atmosphere]]s,<ref name="Out of Darkness" /> including [[PM-1203]], a [[planetoid]] that contained [[Fort Anaxes]], a former [[Galactic Republic]] [[Republic Military|military]] base. The field was also home to another planetoid known as [[PM-6986]], which housed a former [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Separatist stronghold (PM-6986)|stronghold]].<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Anaxes asteroid belt first appeared in "[[Out of Darkness]],"<ref name="Out of Darkness" /> the seventh episode<ref name="SW.com Rebels">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels|text=''Star Wars Rebels''}}</ref> of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' television series,<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/out-of-darkness-episode-guide|text="Out of Darkness" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars Rebels}}</ref> which originally aired on [[November 3]], [[2014]].<ref name="Insider 156">{{InsiderCite|156|Star Wars Rebels Recounted|''Star Wars Rebels'' Recounted}}</ref> The asteroid belt was later identified in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[2018]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Dawn" /> It was alternatively identified in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'' as the Anaxes asteroid ring.<ref name="SW:TVE" /> The [[2022]] attraction ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser]]'', set between [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''The Last Jedi'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''The Rise of Skywalker'']], placed Anaxes in the Core.<ref name="GalacticStarcruiser">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref>
