'''Eim'ir's Bakery''' was a bakery located on<ref name="Aphra 7" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Princess and the Scoundrel">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Corellia]] on the same street as a [[Sana Starros' crash pad|crash pad]] used by the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]]. One item it served was [[sweet roll]]s.<ref name="Aphra 7" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 7|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 7]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] bribed an [[Unidentified urchin|urchin]] to nab her sweet rolls from Eim'ir's Bakery after he attempted to [[Thief|steal]] her [[belt]].<ref name="Aphra 7" />

When Starros encountered Aphra the following [[Standard day|day]] outside her crash pad, Aphra presented her with a sweet roll. Starros asked if the roll was from the bakery, which Aphra confirmed. After the smuggler questioned if the archaeologist had bought them for her, Aphra informed Starros of her bribery of the urchin to get the sweet rolls from the bakery.<ref name="Aphra 7" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eim'ir's Bakery was mentioned in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 7|seventh issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 7" /> on [[January 20]], [[2021]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/82210/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2020_7|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020) #7}}</ref>
