'''Boatman droids''' were [[Color|red]]-plated [[droid]]s that would ferry individuals from one location to another. At some point during or after [[35 ABY]], multiple boatman droids were stationed on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Hy Izlan]]. However, only [[Unidentified boatman droid|one]] was functional.<ref name="The Ninth Jedi" />
