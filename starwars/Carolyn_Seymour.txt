'''Carolyn Seymour''' is a voice actress who provided the voices for several Star Wars video games.

==Works==
===Gameography===
*[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (video game)|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'' video game]] &ndash; [[Shmi Skywalker Lars/Legends|Shmi Skywalker]], [[Unidentified Theed woman 1|Concerned Mother]], [[TC-14/Legends|TC-14]]
*''[[Star Wars: Force Commander]]'' &ndash; AT-AT Barge Pilot, ATC-Frigate, Heavy Lifter Pilot
*''[[Star Wars: Galactic Battlegrounds]]'' &ndash; Empire destroyer captain, Gungan medic, Starbomber pilot
*''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight II: Jedi Outcast]]'' &ndash; [[Mon Mothma/Legends|Mon Mothma]]
*''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]'' &ndash; [[Elora]], [[Helena Shan]], [[Forn Dodonna]], additional voices
*''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]'' &ndash; [[Terena Adare]], additional voices
*''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]'' &ndash; Mon Mothma
