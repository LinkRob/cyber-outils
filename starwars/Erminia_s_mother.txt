A [[Gender|female]] individual was the '''mother''' of [[Erminia]], a [[human]] employee of [[De'Rruyet Industries]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 10|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 10]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> they were present outside the [[De'Rruyet Center]] on the<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra 8">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 8|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 8]]</ref> [[Midarr]] where a replicated [[Nihil]] [[Path engine]] [[hyperdrive]] was planned to be unveiled at a<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" /> [[De'Rruyet Industries confluence|confluence]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra 9">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 9|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 9]]</ref> by [[Beol De'Rruyet]], the leader of De'Rruyet Industries. Before the unveiling could happen, the [[Unbroken Clan]] crashed the event and took De'Rruyet and his employees hostage. As the hostage situation played out, Erminia's [[Parent|mother]] remained outside the center and Erminia was eventually safely taken out of the building by the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]]. As they saw their child was safe, Erminia's mother shouted over to Erminia, and Erminia responded.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" />
