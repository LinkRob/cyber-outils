'''Dash Rendar''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[smuggler]]<ref name="TFV" /> and [[mercenary]]<ref name="Rebel Alliance Conversion Kit" /> who lived during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. During his travels, he encountered the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] and fellow smuggler [[Han Solo]].<ref name="TFV" /> A hotshot<ref name="Rebel Alliance Conversion Kit" /> who claimed to be better than Solo, Rendar later claimed that it was Chewbacca who was the real [[brain]]s behind their operation. Rendar also marked a map of the [[Kessel Run]] that was included in [[Tivoche Bilure|Midnight's]] [[Midnight's book|journal]].<ref name="TFV" /> He piloted a [[YT-2400 light freighter]] called the ''[[Outrider]]'' and worked for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Rebel Alliance Conversion Kit" />

Dash Rendar was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] journal book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="TFV" /> Fry later revealed on Twitter that Rendar's inclusion in the book was a meant as a humorous nod to the character's reputation for being extremely similar to Han Solo.<ref name="Fry Tweet" />

[[Dash Rendar/Legends|Dash Rendar]] was originally developed for the [[1996]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] multimedia project [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire|''Shadows of the Empire'']] and first appeared in its [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (novel)|novel]].<ref name="SotE-N">[[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (novel)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'' novel]]</ref>
