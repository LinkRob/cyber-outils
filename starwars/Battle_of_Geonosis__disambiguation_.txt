There were a number of battles on [[Geonosis/Legends|Geonosis]] that were given the name the '''Battle of Geonosis''':

==Canon==
===Clone Wars===
*'''[[First Battle of Geonosis]]''', the opening battle of the [[Clone Wars]].
*'''[[Second Battle of Geonosis]]''', the second battle that took place on [[Geonosis]].
*'''[[Uprising on Geonosis]]''', [[Poggle the Lesser]]'s escape from the [[Galactic Republic]] in [[19 BBY]].

===Imperial Era===
*'''[[Skirmish in the Korakanni Mound]]''', a skirmish between [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], [[death trooper]]s and a group of [[saboteur]]s led by [[Unidentified Geonosian queen|a Geonosian queen]].
*'''[[Mission to investigate Geonosis]]''', a mission undertook by [[Saw Gerrera]].
*'''[[Secret mission to Geonosis]]''', a mission undertook by [[Darth]] Vader.
*'''[[Operation on Geonosis]]''', an operation carried out by [[Jom Barell]].
