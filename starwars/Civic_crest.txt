The '''civic crest''' was a type of decoration worn by [[Mon Calamari]] who had served in [[Mon Cala]]'s [[Mon Cala planetary defense services|planetary defense services]] to commemorate their past role. During his service as [[Admiral]] of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy]], [[Raddus]] had the civic crest pinned in his fleet [[flagship]] service uniform as a tribute to his past in Mon Cala.<ref name="ROVD">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
