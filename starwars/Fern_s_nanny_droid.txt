'''Fern's nanny droid''' was a [[nanny droid]] that belonged to [[Fara]] and [[Fern]]. Fern was able to override the droids memory to convince it of false events.<ref name="Real Adventure" /> Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SCDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> Fern used an memory override to plant the false idea that she was upstairs studying, despite getting home after her mom got home early.<ref name="Real Adventure" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fern's nanny droid first appeared in "[[This Could Be a Real Adventure]]"<ref name="Real Adventure" /> the first episode of the live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'', which aired on [[December 2]], [[2024]].<ref name="Early">{{SW|url=news/skeleton-crew-premiere-date|text=''Star Wars: Skeleton Crew'' Will Debut Early|date=2024-11-26}}</ref>
