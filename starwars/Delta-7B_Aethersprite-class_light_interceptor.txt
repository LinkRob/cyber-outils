The '''Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor''', commonly known as the '''Jedi starfighter''' due to its widespread use by the [[Jedi]], was a [[starfighter]] model employed during the [[Clone Wars]]. It was a variant design of the earlier [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7 ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]], with the Delta-7B featuring a relocated [[astromech droid]] slot and a slightly enlarged [[hull]].<ref name="USW" /> The Delta-7B also had a set of pop-out [[S-foils]] that could be extended for certain flight maneuvers.<ref name="ROC">{{TCW|The Rise of Clovis}}</ref>

{{Ship-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
In ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], there was initially some confusion as to the difference between the Delta-7B and the Delta-7, which had remained separate in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity. The canon [[Memory bank/Legends|Databank]] stated that both models were the same, designating it as the Delta-7.<ref name="Databank: Delta-7">{{Databank|delta-7-jedi-starfighter|Delta-7 Jedi Starfighter}}</ref> However, ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'' made the distinction between the Delta-7 and the Delta-7B, stating that the latter was the model seen throughout the majority of events in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series,<ref name="USW" /> in accordance with Legends source material. In the non-canon [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] animated TV series [[LEGO Star Wars: Droid Tales|''Droid Tales'']] and [[LEGO Star Wars: The Resistance Rises|''The Resistance Rises'']], [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]] owns a blue Delta-7B interceptor called the ''[[Daisy Mae]]''.
