A '''nest''' was located on [[Carrandar Secundus]], where it was destroyed by the [[Galactic Empire]] around five [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. The [[human]] girl [[Merei Spanjaf]] read about the nest's destruction on a newsfeed on her [[datapad]].<ref name="RitR" />
