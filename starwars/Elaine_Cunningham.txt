'''Elaine Cunningham''' is a fantasy and science fiction author, who contributed primarily to [[Star Wars: The New Jedi Order|''The New Jedi Order'']], with a novel called ''[[The New Jedi Order: Dark Journey]]'' as well as short stories called "[[The Crystal]]" and "[[The Apprentice (short story)|The Apprentice]]". She also wrote another short story, "[[Red Sky, Blue Flame]]." Known for her almost poetic literary style, she is known for her contributions to the [[Wikipedia:Forgotten_Realms|Forgotten Realms]] campaign world, including the realms of [[Wikipedia:Evermeet|Evermeet]], [[Wikipedia:Halruaa|Halruaa]], Ruathym and [[Wikipedia:Waterdeep_(city)|Waterdeep]]. She lives in New England with her husband, their two sons, and two eccentric Siamese cats.

Cunningham wrote the long-running ''Songs & Swords'' series of ''Forgotten Realms'' stories. The intended finale to this, entitled ''Reclamation'', was due out in [[November]] [[2008]], but was cancelled because Cunningham was unable to meet deadlines.
