During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], alongside a [[Vader's crew|crew]] that he had assembled, '''attacked''' the [[Crimson Dawn Sub-Headquarters|Sub-Headquarters]] of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate on [[Laecor]]. The attack was part of Vader's quest to eliminate Crimson Dawn, which had operatives throughout [[the galaxy]], including within the bureacuracy of the [[Galactic Empire]]. After locating and entering the base, Vader examined records of Crimson Dawn agents, learning how far they had infiltrated the Empire.<ref name="Vader 19" />
