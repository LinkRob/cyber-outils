'''BG01-1 farming droids''' were a model of [[agrirobot|farming droid]] used on the [[planet]] [[Habo]] to monitor the fields. [[BG01-1]] was one such droid.<ref name="Art" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
===Development===
BG01-1 farming droids were created for the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' short film "[[The Elder (short film)|The Elder]],"<ref name="Art" /> they however did not appear in the final episode.<ref name="The Elder">{{Visions|The Elder (short film)}}</ref> Concept art for the model appeared in [[Zack Davisson]]'s [[2022]] book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: Visions]]''. The design was based on traditional [[Wikipedia:Asian conical hat|Asian conical hats]].<ref name="Art" />
