The '''A-Wing Expansion Pack''' is a supplement to the ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Miniatures Game]]'' from [[Fantasy Flight Games]]. The expansion features a detailed miniature of the [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor/Legends|RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]. It was released on [[February 28]], [[2013]] as part of the game's second wave of expansions.

==Publisher's summary==
''Conceived by General Dodonna, the RZ-1 A-wing interceptor proved its worth by crippling Star Destroyers during the Battle of Endor. Now the A-Wing Expansion Pack adds another exciting dimension to Rebel squadrons in the Star Wars: X-Wing Miniatures Game. The A-Wing Expansion Pack introduces one A-wing miniature with four pilots and five upgrades. Only the most talented pilots belong in an A-wing cockpit as they push the starfighter's limits in the galaxy's deadliest dogfights! (Not a Complete Game. ''X-Wing Core Set'' Required to Play.)''<ref name="Summary">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/products/x-wing/products/a-wing-expansion-pack|text=A-Wing Expansion Pack}}</ref>
