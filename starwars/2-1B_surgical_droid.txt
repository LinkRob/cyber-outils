The '''2-1B surgical droid''' also known as the '''2-1B-series medical droid'''<ref name="Join the Battle" /> or the '''2-1B medical droid''', were a model of [[medical droid]] that was common throughout [[the galaxy]].

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|Can I be of assistance?''"<br />"''No thanks. Just get in the back and take care of the clones.''"<br />"''That is my programming, sir.|TB-2 and Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />}}
[[File:2-1BseriesSurgicalDroid-TCWCEJtB.png|thumb|left|180px|2-1B surgical droids had modular limbs for all situations.]]
The nomenclature of the 2-1B-series&mdash;the digit two, followed by another digit, and then a letter<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=2-1B - Medical Droid}}</ref>&mdash;was also seen on the [[2-0A medical droid]] active in the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Cataclysm">''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]''</ref> [[Humanoid]] in appearance, all 2-1B series droids were equipped with encyclopedic memory banks. Their memory banks and modular [[limb]]s helped ensure that the droids gave the best course of medical treatment in any given medical situation. The droids also had stabilizing feet, hydraulic legs, vocabulators, hydraulics covered by a transparent shell, and some even had hypodermic injectors.<ref name="charencupdated" />

2-1B droids boasted remarkable joint articulation coupled with their precision-crafted servogrip pincers, which could be replaced with multiple attachments to suit various requirements. The droids could be upgraded to specialize in neurosurgery, alien biology, [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] limb replacement, pediatrics, and podiatry.<ref name="SWCT" /> The droids served as [[doctor]]s throughout [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Although they were designed to treat [[human]]s,<ref name="Geek">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> they were able to treat the diseases and injuries of millions of different [[species]]<ref name="2-1B-DB">{{Databank|2-1b-droid|2-1B Droid}}</ref> using stored data.<ref name="Factivity">''[[Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars]]''</ref> In the process, they would perform diagnostics and surgeries.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> They often worked alongside [[FX-7 medical assistant droid|FX-7 medical droids]]. Due to the need to care for patients, 2-1B droids spoke numerous [[language]]s.<ref name="SW:DF">[[Star Wars: Droid Factory (book)|''Star Wars: Droid Factory'']]</ref> 2-1B droids were also designed to interface with [[Starship|shipboard]] medical [[Scanner (disambiguation)|scanners]], which enabled them to diagnose injuries remotely, and they were programmed to give patients calm reassurance.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==History==
===Republic Era===
{{Quote|The senator will be fine. It was only a flesh wound.|A medical droid describes [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s injury to [[Ahsoka Tano]]|<ref name="Assassin">{{TCW|Assassin (episode)}}</ref>}}
2-1B droids were popular since the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="USW" /> Several 2-1B droids were part of [[Ajax Sigma's army]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Revelations 1" /> One droid became a bartender working at [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref> Other notable droids were also stationed on the ''Resolute''<ref name="Downfall of a Droid">{{TCW|Downfall of a Droid}}</ref> and the [[Kaliida Shoals Medical Center]].<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence">{{TCW|Shadow of Malevolence|Shadow of ''Malevolence''}}</ref> The [[BU-series bartender droid]]<ref name="BuildR2">{{BuildR2Cite|5|Droid Directory}}</ref> and the [[DG-1B catering droid]]<ref name="Evil Plans">{{TCW|Evil Plans}}</ref> shared the same frame design as the 2-1B droid.<ref name="BuildR2" />

The droid [[TB-2]] was a 2-1B droid stationed on the [[G9 Rigger-class light freighter|G9 ''Rigger''-class light freighter]] ''[[Twilight]]'' during the [[Clone Wars]]. He was originally a [[hologram]],<ref name="TCWfilm">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> but was eventually uploaded into a body and stationed on both the ''Twilight'' and the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Resolute]]''<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> because of how valuable he had proven himself to be.<ref name="Helmet Collection 58">{{HelmetCollectionCite|58|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref>

Other notable 2-1Bs were used on worlds, such as [[Naboo]],<ref name="Mystery Moons">{{TCW|Mystery of a Thousand Moons}}</ref> [[Alderaan]],<ref name="Assassin" /> and [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Deception">{{TCW|Deception}}</ref> A 2-1B droid helped the group of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], [[Gregar Typho]], [[Queen]] [[Neeyutnee]], [[Jar Jar Binks]], and [[C-3PO]] to unlock what a [[tactical droid]] named [[TR-350]] knew about the droid's presence on Naboo. While they did not get the information out of the droid, Jar Jar happened to stumble across the solution.<ref name="Blue Shadow Virus">{{TCW|Blue Shadow Virus (episode)}}</ref>

A 2-1B droid was present on [[Cato Neimoidia]] where [[Baron]] [[Rush Clovis]] had one check on Amidala after she drank [[Manax root]] [[poison]] that [[Senator]] [[Lott Dod]] gave her to blackmail Clovis.<ref name="Senate Spy">{{TCW|Senate Spy}}</ref>

A 2-1B droid was on [[Alderaan]]. Senator Amidala traveled to Alderaan to attend a meeting concerning the refugees displaced by the Clone Wars. However, bounty hunter [[Aurra Sing]] attempted to assassinate her while she was giving her speech. Although [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] prevented Sing from scoring a direct hit, she managed to shoot the Naboo senator in the arm, knocking her unconscious. The droid treated her and later informed a worried Tano that the senator would be fine as it was only a flesh wound.<ref name="Assassin" />

A 2-1B droid was stationed inside the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. The droid patched up [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] after his staged "assassination" death and loaded the [[facial transformation program]] to morph his physical features to exactly match that of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Rako Hardeen]].<ref name="Deception" />

A 2-1B droid served the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] serving Count [[Dooku]]. The droid tended [[Rush Clovis]]' injuries before playing a message from Dooku with an [[imagecaster]], which also had detail of the [[Muun]]s' secret accounts.<ref name="Rise of Clovis" />

A 2-1B unit was treating [[Grand Minister]] [[Stin]] of [[Clabron]]. The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] sent the Separatist [[assassin]] [[Lis Mohles]] to kill him because he decided to side with the Galactic Republic in the Clone Wars. Stin ordered the medical droid to attend to Amidala's [[aide]] [[Moteé]], who was shot in the shoulder.<ref name="AoR PA">[[Age of Republic - Padmé Amidala 1|''Age of Republic - Padmé Amidala'' 1]]</ref>

[[File:VaderOperation-ROTS.png|thumb|right|300px|A 2-1B unit repairing the newly Sith Lord Darth Vader.]]
At the end of the Clone Wars, a 2-1B surgical droid was present at the [[Grand Republic Medical Facility]] with a [[DD-13 medical assistant droid]], [[FX-6]],<ref name="EP III" /> and another [[FX-9 surgical assistant]].<ref name="Journeys Beginnings">''[[Star Wars Journeys: Beginnings]]''</ref> The droids helped rebuild the body of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] following his [[Duel on Mustafar|defeat]] at the hands of [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] on [[Mustafar]]<ref name="EP III" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> The three droids were later moved to [[Fortress Vader]] on [[Mustafar]].<ref name="The Dark Side">''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side]]''</ref>

===Imperial Era===
[[File:RebuildTheSith-2020DarthVader12.png|thumb|right|300px|A 2-1B unit works on the Sith Lord Darth Vader after the [[mission to Exegol]].]]
During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Galactic Empire]] used millions of 2-1B droids throughout its territory. One such unit later broke free and came to be known as [[2-1B Delta]].<ref name="Delta">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "2-1B Delta"</ref>

[[Phoenix Squadron]] had a 2-1B droid in their [[medical bay]] aboard the [[Liberator (CR90 corvette)|''Liberator'']]. The droid was used to heal [[Hera Syndulla]] after she sustain injuries from a [[Dogfight over Concord Dawn|dogfight]] with the leader of the [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protectors]] [[Fenn Rau]].<ref name="The Protector of Concord Dawn">{{Rebels|The Protector of Concord Dawn}}</ref>

A 2-1B medical droid was among the medics aboard the ''[[Ghost]]'', tending the wounded, which included former [[Imperial Security Bureau|ISB]] [[agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]].<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

The 2-1B droid [[G2-1B7]] served the [[Partisans]] during the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion against the Empire]]. G2-1B7 kept the Partisans' leader, [[Saw Gerrera]], alive due to his ailing health.<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

[[2-1B]] was another notable surgical droid. He served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], and operated on the Alliance [[Commander]] [[Luke Skywalker]]. The droid treated Skywalker with [[bacta]] after he was attacked by a [[Wampa]],<ref name="ESB" /> and was later personally requested by Skywalker<ref name="charencupdated" /> to treat him again after Skywalker [[Duel on Cloud City|lost]] his hand to [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="ESB" /> He went on to be a member of the crew of the ''[[Home One]]'' during the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="EpVI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> [[MHD-19]] was another 2-1B in the Alliance.<ref name="Legends">''[[Legends of the Alliance]]''</ref>

===New Republic Era===
During the [[New Republic Era]], the 2-1B droid [[2MED2]] served the [[Resistance]] during the [[Cold War]] and was assigned to help a team of Resistance fighters transport the [[First Order]] [[RA-7 protocol droid]] [[0-MR1|O-MR1]], who was believed to know the location of [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]], who had been captured by the First Order. On the way back to the Resistance base, the team's ship crashed on an unknown planet and the only survivors were 2MED2, O-MR1, the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]], the [[construction droid]] [[CO-34]], and the two [[security droid]]s [[PZ-99]] and [[VL-44]]. The group of droids worked together to get home, but along the way every droid except C-3PO and O-MR1 were destroyed by creatures native to the planet. 2MED2 was destroyed in an attack from a [[can-cell]].<ref name="The Phantom Limb" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 2-1B surgical droid first appeared in the [[1980]] [[original trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], directed by [[Irvin Kershner]].<ref name="ESB" /> They were first identified in [[canon]] in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', which was written by [[Ryder Windham]], [[Tricia Barr]], [[Adam Bray]], and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="USW" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], they first appeared in the [[Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back (novelization)|novelization]] of ''Empire Strikes Back'' by [[Donald F. Glut]], which was released prior to the film.<ref name="ESB" />
