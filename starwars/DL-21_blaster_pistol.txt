The '''DL-21 blaster pistol''' was a model of [[blaster pistol]]<ref name="SWCT" /> produced by [[BlasTech Industries]].<ref name="TCVDNE" /> The blaster featured a [[scope]] on the top and a finned muzzle that is removable. 

==History==
The [[bounty hunter]] [[Dengar]] had a holstered DL-21 when aboard the ''[[Executor]]'' shortly after the [[Battle of Hoth]].<ref name="Episode V" />  The [[Tempest Force]] garrison officers [[Colonel]] [[Dyer]] and [[Commander]] [[Renz]] used theses models but lacked the finned muzzle.<ref name="Episode VI" /> [[Din Djarin]] kept a DL-21 aboard the ''[[Razor Crest]]''.<ref name="The Mandalorian">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> The DL-21 was also used by [[Aqualish crime family]] who ambush the [[Mods]]. Several residents of [[Freetown]] [[Freetown garrison|garrison]] also used DL-21s to fight off the [[Pyke Syndicate]] forces.<ref name="Honor" />
