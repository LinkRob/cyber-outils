'''Andara''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> located in the [[Andara system]] in the [[Core Worlds]], in grid square L-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Around [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY">{{ShadowFallDate}}</ref> the [[New Republic]] captured Andara from the [[Galactic Empire]]. Reports reached the Empire's [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], and the personnel among the [[fighter wing]] were unhappy, some seeking to take revenge. The 204th attacked and destroyed a New Republic outpost on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet of [[Mon Gazza]] the next day in retaliation to Andara's capture.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
