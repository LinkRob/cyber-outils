The '''7G sector''' was a [[sector]] of [[Realspace|space]] located in the [[Unknown Regions]]. It contained the [[Ilum system]].<ref name="Ultimate SW" />

{{Sec-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The 7G sector originated from the [[2015]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] sourcebook [[Star Wars: Force and Destiny Core Rulebook|''Star Wars: Force and Destiny'' Core Rulebook]], and has been repeated in some subsequent [[canon]] sources.<ref name="FD">[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny Core Rulebook|''Star Wars: Force and Destiny'' Core Rulebook]]</ref> However, [[Jason Fry]], author of the "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix of [[star system]]s, has stated it is excluded from the appendix due to it being an in-joke about Ilum's coordinates on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Email">{{EmailCite|email=File:EAOC discussion 2023 4.jpg|creator=Jason Fry|date=2023-10-22}}</ref>
