'''Ecological Law''' was a philosophy that ruled the planet [[Tibrin]] home world of the [[Ishi Tib]]. It emphasized the health of the environment over the citizens of Tibrin. Any new inventions must first go through rigorous testing to be compliant with Ecological Law. Many off-world corporations chose not to conduct business with [[Tibrin]] for this reason.<ref name="Lead By Example FFG">[[Lead by Example]]</ref>
