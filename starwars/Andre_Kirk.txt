'''Andre Kirk''' is a designer, concept artist and art director who worked on the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''<ref name="FR BTS gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/front-runners-concept-art-gallery|text=Front Runners Concept Art Gallery}}</ref> and served with the concept art team of the animated series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''.<ref name="SIS BTS gallery">{{SW|url=series/episodes/steps-into-shadow-behind-the-scenes-gallery|text=Steps Into Shadow Behind-The-Scenes Gallery}}</ref> He would go onto to be the Art Director for ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' where he lead the art department. For "[[Cornered]]," he provided concept art for the [[Rawlings TK5 speeder bike]].<ref name="Cornered EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/cornered-episode-guide|text="Cornered" Episode Guide}}</ref>
