The role of '''caretaker droid''' was a position held by a [[droid]] tasked with looking after a location. During the [[Battle of Teth]] in [[22 BBY]], the [[RA-7 protocol droid]] [[4-A7]] claimed to be the caretaker of a [[B'omarr Order Monastery]] on the [[Wild Space]] [[Planet|world]], though he was truly a [[spy droid]] for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. While [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Torrent Company]] initially believed the unit's lies, the droid's true loyalties were exposed when [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] saw him with several [[B1-series battle droid|B1 battle droids]]. She then turned her [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|lightsaber]] on the Separatist droids, quickly taking out the [[Battle droid|combat droids]] and 4-A7 himself.

==Description==
{{Quote|Who are you?''"<br />"''Merely the humble caretaker, O mighty sir. You have liberated me from those dreadful battle bots. I am most thankful.|Jedi [[General]] Anakin Skywalker meets 4-A7 during the droid's caretaker act|<ref name="The Clone Wars" />}}
A caretaker droid was a [[droid]] that had been tasked with looking over a location, such as the<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> monasteries of the [[B'omarr Order]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Caretakers could be humble individuals that respected those who helped them. The role could be given to [[protocol droid]]s like the [[RA-7 protocol droid|RA-7 series]].<ref name="The Clone Wars" />

==History==
{{Quote|The droids have done their part. Now it's your turn.|Dark acolyte Asajj Ventress dispatches 4-A7 to meet the Republic|<ref name="The Clone Wars" />}}
In [[22 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of the [[Battle of Christophsis]], which was depicted in the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]], and the early events of the [[Battle of Ryloth]], as depicted in [[Supply Lines]], to [[22 BBY]]. As the film this event takes place within takes place between the events of the pieces of media the other events are attached to according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, this event must also take place in 22 BBY.</ref> during the [[Clone Wars]], a [[The galaxy|galactic]] conflict fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> the prototype [[spy droid]] and RA-7 unit named [[4-A7]] was tasked with framing the [[Jedi Order]] in the kidnapping<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11">{{BuildR2Cite|11|Droid Directory}}</ref> of the [[Reproduction|son]] of [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], [[Rotta]], who the Confederacy had captured and taken to the [[Wild Space]] [[planet]] of [[Teth]] as part of a plot<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> to gain access to [[Hutt Clan]] [[Hyperspace route|hyperlanes]]<ref name="Dooku biography gallery Image 9">{{Databank|url=count-dooku-biography-gallery|title=Count Dooku Biography Gallery}}</ref> and [[Hutt Space|territory]].<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> Thus, during the [[Battle of Teth]], after the Republic's<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> [[Torrent Company]]<ref name="Cody Gallery image 3">{{Databank|clone-commander-cody-biography-gallery|Clone Commander Cody Biography Gallery}}</ref> fought past the [[battle droid]]s guarding an occupied<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> [[B'omarr Order Monastery]], [[Dark Acolyte]]<ref name="USW" /> [[Asajj Ventress]] dispatched 4-A7 to greet their enemies. Pretending to be the monastery's humble caretaker droid, 4-A7 approached the company's [[Jedi General]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], claiming that the Republic had liberated him from the battle droids.<ref name="The Clone Wars" />

[[File:4A-7_uncovered.png|thumb|right|320x252px|Actually a servant of the CIS, 4-A7 claimed to be a caretaker during the Battle of Teth.]]
When Skywalker asked him where Rotta was being held, 4-A7 pointed the [[Jedi Knight]] to the [[Prison|detention level]], warning him that it was dangerous and not a place for [[Ahsoka Tano]], Skywalker's [[Padawan]] learner whom he claimed to have confused for a servant girl. After 4-A7 offered a thousand apologies for this, Skywalker and Tano rescued Rotta from the dungeons and brought him to the courtyard. 4-A7 recorded them place the screaming infant into a [[backpack]],<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> fulfilling his role to frame the [[Jedi Order]] in the kidnapping, as the edited<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> footage was sent to [[Count]] [[Dooku]] to use as evidence against the Republic for Jabba. A short time later and unfortunately for the [[protocol droid]], Tano, Skywalker, Rotta, and the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] arrived at the platform containing the [[G9 Rigger-class light freighter|G9 Rigger freighter]] ''[[Twilight]]'', which 4-A7 and a group of [[B1-series battle droid|B1 battle droids]] hoped to escape on.<ref name="The Clone Wars" />

Upon spotting him, Tano, who had been wondering what had happened to 4-A7, continued to assume he was a caretaker droid and greeted him as such. Though 4-A7 initially tried to keep up his caretaker act, the B1 units walked into view, exposing his true loyalties to the [[Jedi Commander]]. Tano accused the fake caretaker of [[treason]], and 4-A7 dropped his act, ordering the battle droids to [[Death|kill]] her<ref name="The Clone Wars" /> to prevent Rotta's rescue.<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> However, Tano made short work of the B1s with her [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|lightsaber]]. The young [[Jedi]] then turned her blade on 4-A7, swiftly decapitating the droid she had thought was an ally.<ref name="The Clone Wars" />
