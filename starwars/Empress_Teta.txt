'''Empress Teta''' was an [[ecumenopolis]] [[planet]] located in the [[Empress Teta system]], in the [[Koros sector]] of the [[Deep Core]]. Known as '''Koros''' or '''Koros Major''' during the lifetime of its [[Teta|namesake]], it was home to one of [[the galaxy]]'s most ancient and affluent [[human]] civilizations, and was a focal point of the [[Great Hyperspace War]]. A supplier of refined [[carbonite]] and [[andris]] [[spice]] for much of galactic history, Empress Teta was a major hub of operations for the [[Mining Guild]], which exerted control over the [[Tetan monarchy|planetary government]]. By the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the world was alternatively known as '''Cinnagar''', the name of its [[capital]] [[city]].

==Description==
Empress Teta was a [[Ecumenopolis|city-world]] that was considered the gateway to the [[Deep Core]]. Orbited by the [[moon]] [[Atale]],<ref name="NOP" /> it was [[Hyperspace route|connected]] to [[Coruscant]] via the [[Koros Trunk Line]] and to [[Prakith]] via the [[Byss Run]]. Additionally, Empress Teta was situated on the [[Carbonite Run]].<ref name="TFABG" /><ref name="NOP" /> When the [[Galactic Empire]] came to power, Empress Teta became a key checkpoint for the Imperial [[Deep Core Security Zone]], causing [[Imperial Intelligence]] to restrict non-essential traffic by intentionally making the [[Empress Teta system|system's]] [[astrogation]] charts confusing and inconsistent.<ref name="NOP" />

Empress Teta's [[city]]scape was known as [[Cinnagar]], the planet's [[capital]] which grew over millennia to cover the whole surface. The most upscale part of Cinnagar was the [[Core District]], while the [[Royal District]] was the home of the [[Keto family|royal family]]. Under the city-scape existed [[carbonite]] and [[andris]] [[spice]] mines operated by the [[Mining Guild]]. Though most of the system's andris production occurred off-world by the age of the Empire, the guild retained much of its administrative infrastructure in Cinnagar.<ref name="NOP" />

==History==
Believed to be the home to one of the galaxy's oldest [[human]] civilizations, some buildings in Cinnagar were dated to circa [[27,000 BBY]]. In antiquity, Empress Teta was home to extensive mining operations of andris and carbonite, which allowed the world to become an early stronghold of the Mining Guild. Historians linked the [[carbonite]] mining operations on Empress Teta to [[sleeper ship]]s that were used to spread humanity throughout the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="NOP" />

The planet's ancient history was recounted in a series of epic poems known as the [[Qel-Droma Epics]], which detailed the [[Unification Wars (Koros)|conquest of the Koros system]] by the [[Teta]], the [[empress]] of Koros Major, as well as her resistance to [[Sith Lord]] [[Naga Sadow]] during the [[Great Hyperspace War]]; Koros Major would later be renamed in Teta's honor. The poems also recounted the adventures of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Ulic Qel-Droma]], who was believed to have infiltrated a mysterious [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] cult known as the [[Krath]] which slaughtered [[Jedi]] and conquered Empress Teta. While the Jedi taught the Epics as historical fact, the efforts of [[archaeologist]]s to verify the truth of the myths were inconclusive.<ref name="NOP" />

Though the andris mines on Empress Teta were abandoned once they ran dry, the Mining Guild continued to assert control over the world and eventually usurped the power of the ruling [[Keto family]] in all but name. The guild's affiliation with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] caused Empress Teta to side against the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]], while loyalist nobles fled to Coruscant. After the war ended with a Separatist defeat, the newly-formed [[Galactic Empire]] installed these nobles in positions of power, such as planetary bureaucrats or Mining Guild administrators.<ref name="NOP" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the planet was ruled by Emperor [[Alor Keto]], though he was largely an ineffectual puppet of the Mining Guild. Already a major supplier for the [[Imperial Military]], Empress Teta assumed increased strategic importance for the Empire as its location restricted access to the [[Deep Core Security Zone]],<ref name="NOP" /> where the Empire tested experimental technologies such as [[Interdictor]]s.<ref name="Trkn">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> The world was protected by [[Admiral]] [[Yzu]]'s [[15th Deep Core Reserve Fleet]].<ref name="NOP" />

==Behind the scenes==
Empress Teta was first created for [[Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith 1|''Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith'' 1]], the first issue of the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (comic series)|''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi'']], which was released on [[October 1]], [[1994]] and written by [[Tom Veitch]] and [[Kevin J. Anderson]]. The world was confirmed to exist in [[canon]] when it appeared on a galactic map included with ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 36]]'', a magazine released by [[De Agostini]] in [[2015]].
