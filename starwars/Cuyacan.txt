'''Cuyacan''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Cuyacan system]] within the [[Sepan sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of [[the Slice]]<ref name="Location" /> and [[Expansion Region]],<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> in grid square P-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> that had a [[Unidentified Cuyacan princess|princess]] some [[time]] after the [[Battle of Jakku|fall]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was said that the [[smuggler]] [[Ana Tolla]] had [[Thief|stolen]] a [[diamond]] [[Clothing|choker]] from the [[princess]] while she was still wearing it.<ref name="A Crash of Fate">''[[A Crash of Fate]]''</ref>

At some point after the [[Garsea family]] left the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="A Crash of Fate" /> in [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]]. The novel establishes that [[Izal Garsea]] left [[Batuu]] when she was five [[Standard year|years]] old, and as the novel takes place on the [[Standard day|day]] before and day of Izal Garsea's eighteenth [[birthday]], she must have left the [[planet]] in [[21 ABY]].</ref> the [[Frinn Mak Traveling Circus]] was heading to Cuyacan when they rescued the [[smuggler]] [[Izal Garsea]] aboard her [[starship]], the [[Meridian (light freighter)|''Meridian'']], after it ran out of [[fuel]] and became stranded in [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" /> The [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]] included the planet on a map in his [[book]], ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'',<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> which was published in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Cuyacan was first mentioned in the [[2019]] novel ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />
