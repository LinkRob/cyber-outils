'''Chussido''' was a [[Rodian]] [[Gender|female]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Rodia]] who served as a member of [[Enfys Nest]]'s [[Cloud-Riders]] gang. In [[10 BBY]], Chussido was amongst the Cloud-Riders who traveled to the planet [[Savareen]] with Nest in order to try and acquire [[coaxium]] [[fuel]] from a [[Beckett's gang|crew]] of criminals working for the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate. Nest successfully convinced all of the crew bar its leader, [[Tobias Beckett]], to aid her and the others in stealing the coaxium from Crimson Dawn and [[Death|killing]] the syndicate's leader, [[Dryden Vos]].

Chussido and the other Cloud-Riders then laid a trap, while the outlaw [[Han Solo]] and his allies took the coaxium to Vos on his [[yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]''. Believing the coaxium Solo had brought him to be fake, Vos sent his [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] out to capture Nest and her allies, who he believed had the real coaxium. Chussido and the other Cloud-Riders then ambushed the mercenaries and defeated them, allowing Solo and his allies to kill Vos. Solo then returned the coaxium to Nest, Chussido and the other Cloud-Riders.

Chussido eventually joined the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]] sometime after serving the Cloud-Riders, where she resided in a [[Nevarro school|cantina]] on [[Nevarro]]. She was killed while attempting to [[Incident on Nevarro|reclaim]] [[Din Grogu|The Child]] from [[Din Djarin|The Mandalorian]].

==Biography==
===The Cloud-Riders===
{{Quote|We're not marauders. We're allies. And the war's just begun.|Enfys Nests describes Chussido and the other Cloud-Riders|audio=Enfys-Allies.flac|<ref name="Solo" />}}
[[File:Enfys_Nest_and_company.png|thumb|left|250px|Chussido was a member of the Cloud-Riders.]]
Chussido<ref name="Card Trader">{{SWCT|set=The Cloud-Riders|cardname=Chussido}}</ref> was a [[Rodian]] [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Designs" /> who joined up with the rebellious [[Cloud-Riders]] gang under the leadership of [[Enfys Nest]] after her [[homeworld]]<ref name="Solo" /> of [[Rodia]]<ref name="Insider 224" /> was brutalized by one of the [[Five Crime Syndicates]]. In [[10 BBY]], Nest led a small group of the Cloud Riders in an attempt to steal a shipment of [[coaxium]] [[fuel]] from the [[Galactic Empire]] on the [[planet]] [[Vandor]]; however, their plans were thwarted by a [[Beckett's gang|crew]] of criminals led by [[Tobias Beckett]] and associated with the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate, who also attempted to steal the coaxium and destroyed it in the process.<ref name="Solo" />

Determined to obtain coaxium for use against the Empire, Nest then monitored the surviving members of Beckett's crew and learned that they had stolen raw coaxium from a mine on the planet [[Kessel]] and were headed to the planet [[Savareen]] to refine it. Nest, Chussido and the other Cloud-Riders then headed to Savareen to try and claim the coaxium.<ref name="Solo" />

===Savareen standoff===
{{Quote|I need a drink. Bring them inside.|Nest orders Chussido and the other Cloud-Riders to bring Beckett's crew into the cantina|audio=Enfys-Drink.flac|<ref name="Solo" />}}
[[File:Chussido_Tubes_and_Waydurk.png|thumb|right|250px|The Cloud-Riders confronted Beckett's crew on Savareen.]]
Once the refinement process had begun at the [[Bis Refinery]], Chussido stood with Nest and the others as they confronted the criminals in a [[Pnakotic Coast village|village]] near the refinery. The Cloud-Riders initially kept their [[blaster]]s aimed at the criminals, but after Nest removed her helmet the two groups moved inside a local bar to talk. Nest explained what Crimson Dawn and the syndicates had done to Chussido and her companions' homeworlds, as well as their crimes against the local population of the village, and tried to convince Beckett and his followers to hand over the coaxium to them rather than let it be used by the syndicates and their ally, the Empire.<ref name="Solo" />

The speech failed to convince Beckett, but did persuade the outlaw [[Han Solo]] to help them. He worked out a plan with the Cloud-Riders and the rest of Becket's crew which would allow them to [[Death|kill]] Crimson Dawn's leader, [[Dryden Vos]], who was headed to Savareen to collect the coaxium. The plan involved Chussido and the other Cloud-Riders giving their helmets to the residents of the village, who could then act as decoys for them. Solo then told Beckett, who he suspected would betray them, that they were going to hand fake coaxium over to Vos.<ref name="Solo" />

===Vengeance on Vos===
{{Quote|It's empty. The case is empty!|Aemon Gremm realises he has fallen into the Cloud-Riders trap|audio=Aemon-Empty.flac|<ref name="Solo" />}}
[[File:Chussido_kick.png|thumb|left|200px|Chussido took down several of Crimson Dawn's enforcers.]]
Solo and his allies then brought the real coaxium with them to Vos's [[yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]'', while Chussido and the other Cloud-Riders set up an ambush in the village using their local doppelgangers and empty coaxium containers as bait. As expected, Beckett betrayed his companions and informed Vos that the coaxium was fake, causing the [[crime lord]] to send out his force of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] led by [[Aemon Gremm]] to capture the Cloud-Riders, whom he assumed had the real coaxium. The mercenaries surrounded the disguised villagers, but quickly discovered that they did not possess the coaxium.<ref name="Solo" />

Nest then launched an ambush on the unsuspecting mercenaries, and Chussido rushed forward from hiding and kicked a mercenary to the ground. She then knocked another mercenary to the ground with [[stock]] of her blaster, and aimed it at him before he could get up. The remaining mercenaries were quickly subdued or killed, leaving Vos with no back up on the ''First Light''. Solo and his allies then killed Vos and Beckett, before returning the real coaxium to Nest, Chussido and the other Cloud-Riders, who loaded the coaxium onto their [[speeder bike]]s.<ref name="Solo" /> Chussido and the others then mounted their vehicles, waiting for Nest to launch first before taking off behind her and leaving for a rendezvous they had to keep with the [[Onderonian]] rebel [[Saw Gerrera]], to whom they gave the coaxium.<ref name="SoloEE">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

===New Republic Era===
{{Quote|They all hate you, Mando. Because you're a legend!''"<br />"''How many had tracking fobs?''"<br />"''All of them. All of them! But not one of them closed the deal. Only you Mando, only you.|Greef Karga and the Mandalorian discuss Chussido and the other bounty hunters|<ref name="Chapter 3" />}}
[[File:Chussido_cantina.png|thumb|right|250px|Chussido spent time in a cantina with other Guild members.]]
Chussido eventually joined the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]].<ref name="Chapter 3" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> she spent [[time]] in a [[cantina]] on [[Nevarro]] filled with members of the guild, including its agent, [[Greef Karga]]. While at the cantina, the [[bounty hunter]] known as [[Din Djarin|the Mandalorian]] arrived to meet with Karga and deliver four [[Bounty|bounties]] he had collected. Chussido was at the bar when the [[Mandalorian]] entered, and began speaking with a [[human]] there. She then sat at one of the tables with a different human.<ref name="Chapter 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref>

Chussido and all the other hunters accepted a [[Hunt for Grogu|bounty]] from a man known as [[the Client]], who was associated with an [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] and wished for a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Din Grogu|infant]] to be brought to him. Chussido was unable to capture the bounty, as the Mandalorian managed to successfully deliver the Child first, earning much resentment from her and other members of the Guild.<ref name="Chapter 3" />

===Dusted===
{{Quote|That's one impressive weapon.|Karga comments on the Amban sniper rifle after watching it disintegrate Chussido|<ref name="Chapter 3" />}}
[[File:Chussido-TheMandalorian.png|thumb|left|250px|Chussido attempted to kill the Mandalorian]]
When the Mandalorian had a change of [[heart]] and [[Incident on Nevarro|rescued]] [[Din Grogu|the Child]] from the Imperials, Chussido and every other hunter's [[Tracking fob|fob]] reactivated. Karga then led her and the other hunters in ambushing the Mandalorian as he attempted to return to his [[starship]], the ''[[Razor Crest]]''. Chussido was waiting on a street corner as the Mandalorian passed and followed him, slowly surrounding him with other hunters. She and the others kept their [[blaster]]s trained on the Mandalorian as he initially appeared to surrender and follow Karga's command to place the Child on a nearby [[speeder]]; however, upon reaching the speeder, the Mandalorian opened fire and took cover in the speeder.<ref name="Chapter 3" />

The gathered hunters opened fire on the Mandalorian as he began to make a getaway in the speeder while returning fire. Karga then managed to shoot the [[astromech droid]] [[pilot]]ing the speeder and halted the Mandalorian's progress, allowing the other hunters to begin closing in. Chussido remained in cover behind a stack of crates with a [[blaster pistol]] aimed at the speeder, but was [[Death|killed]] when the Mandalorian shot her with his [[Amban phase-pulse blaster]], completely disintegrating the former Cloud-Rider. The Mandalorian ultimately escaped with the Child with aid from his [[The Tribe|Tribe]] of Mandalorians.<ref name="Chapter 3" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:Chussido.png|thumb|right|200px|Chussido wore a necklace of fang-like objects and carried a blaster rifle.]]
Chussido had [[Color|red]] [[eye]]s and yellow skin.<ref name="Solo" /> Chussido was shocked along with all the other Cloud-Riders other than Nest when Solo returned with the coaxium following the death of Beckett and Vos.<ref name="SoloEE" /> 

==Equipment==
Chussido wore a brown and red [[Clothing|jerkin]], with gray pants and [[boot]]s and a brown [[cape]]. The Rodian's mask was a brown-bag like headpiece. She hung a necklace of [[tooth]]-like objects around her neck and carried a [[blaster rifle]].<ref name="Solo" /> The Rodian also wore red and white warpaint across her face.<ref name="Designs" /> While attempting to reclaim the Child under the Bounty Hunters' Guild, she used a [[DT-12 heavy blaster pistol]] with a long thin barrel.<ref name="Chapter 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Chussido_Concept_Art.png|thumb|left|150px|Concept art of Chussido by Adam Brockbank]]
{{Quote|I was really pleased to get a Rodian in — that came up very, very late.|Jake Lunt Davies|<ref name="Designs" />}}
Chussido first appeared in the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="Solo" /> and was later identified in the ''[[Star Wars: Card Trader]]'' app.<ref name="Card Trader" />

The idea of a Rodian Cloud-Rider was a late addition to the film, and concept artist [[Jake Lunt Davies]] drew several initial sketches of Chussido where he focused on making her visually distinct from the Rodian [[Greedo]]. Davies also took inspiration from the Rodians seen in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]'' when designing the character.<ref name="Designs" /> Concept art including Chussido titled "Enfys Gang Group Version 5A" was also produced by costume concept designer [[Adam Brockbank]].<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> The final mask used in ''Solo'' had some sculptural differences from the original and also had war paint applies to further distinguish it from Greedo.<ref name="Designs" />

Chussido's costume was later reused in the [[2019]] television show ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.<ref name="Jake Lunt Davies Mandalorian">{{Instagram|username=jakeluntdavies|url=p/B40rdRZDQpg|name=jakeluntdavies|nobackup=1}}</ref> While [[Star Wars Insider 205|''Star Wars Insider'' 205]] states the Rodian shown in ''The Mandalorian'' has yet to be confirmed as Chussido,<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|205|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> the [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Disney Magic Kingdoms]]'' already verified the Rodian to be the same character.<ref name="Kingdoms">''[[Disney Magic Kingdoms]]''</ref>
