A '''''Battle Dreadnought'' class''', or simply a '''Battle Dreadnought''', was a type of [[warship]] classification used by the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] that was given to [[alien]] [[starship]]s that were slightly smaller than a [[Chiss]] [[Nightdragon man-of-war]] or half as large.<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref> The [[Nikardun Battle Dreadnought]] was a class of warship of the designation.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> Another Battle Dreadnought, the ''[[HopeBreaker]]'', was used against the Chiss by the [[Grysk]] warlike [[species]].<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref>
