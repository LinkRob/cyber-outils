'''Andrew Woolfson''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Scout/Legends|explorer]] who discovered [[Sylvia IV]] and the [[Sylphe]] [[Species/Legends|species]]. It was he who gave them this name.

==History==
Andrew Woolfson led a team sent by the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and explored the [[Sylenia V system]] some decades before the [[Ruusan Reformations/Legends|Ruusan Reformation]]. He encountered a native species on [[Sylvia IV]] and named them [[Sylphe]]s.

The [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] named him the head of the [[Azul brigade]], formed from both [[Human/Legends|Human]] and Sylphe soldiers. He then married a [[Gender/Legends|female]] Sylphe of the unit and had a child, whom they named [[Layahna]].

In [[1125 BBY]], Woolfson was sent with the [[Azul brigade]] to [[Arrakad]]. The [[Planet/Legends|planet]] was a raider's lair from which attacks were launched against Republic commerce convoys in the [[Rigel-Hurib sector]].

The battle was a quick victory for the Republic, but the unit's repatriation was delayed by the lack of fuel. The brigade took five months to arrive on Arrakad, and the metabolism of the Sylphes wasn't adapted to the arid climate of Arrakad. All of them died before the ships arrived. The Human survivors of the brigade deserted or vowed to take revenge, without success. Andrew Woolfson committed [[Death/Legends|suicide]] on Arrakad to follow his wife, deceased because of the climate.

==Behind the scenes==
Andrew Woolfson was briefly mentioned by [[Franck Vidal]] for an ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] scenario which appeared in the French magazine [[Avalon 2|''Avalon'' 2]].

His name is misspelled "Woolfsar" twice in the [[Azul brigade]] entry of the article.
