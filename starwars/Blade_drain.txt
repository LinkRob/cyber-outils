A '''blade drain''' was a [[lightsaber]] attachment that allowed a lightsaber to absorb some of the energy held within the blade of an opponent's saber. This made the opponent's blade less powerful, but came with a risk of overloading the saber that was absorbing the energy.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blade drains were mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
