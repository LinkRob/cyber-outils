'''Endure''' was a [[Jedi]] [[Force power]] used to endure physical pain. During the [[Battle of Nar Shaddaa]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Crucitorn]] used the technique to mitigate the pain of being [[Fire|immolated]]. Jedi Master [[Lene Kostana]] taught the technique to the Jedi [[Dooku]] at some point between [[102 BBY]] and [[68 BBY]], and he later passed on this knowledge to his two [[Padawan]]s: first [[Rael Averross]], and later [[Qui-Gon Jinn]].

==Description==
Endure<ref name="KoF">''[[Knights of Fate]]''</ref> was a [[Force power]] used by several [[Jedi]] to endure physical pain.<ref name="D:JL" /> Masters of the ability could resist otherwise-[[Death|deadly]] injuries and pain. It was also possible to use Endure to make others resist pain.<ref name="KoF" />

==History==
{{Quote|This technique is not one I learned from Master Yoda. And yet I will share it with you, as I shared it with Rael. Who knows? Perhaps you will share it with your Padawan one day.|Dooku to Qui-Gon Jinn about Endure|<ref name="D:JL" />}}
[[File:Justice.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Dooku (left) taught Endure to Qui-Gon Jinn (right) while the latter was his Padawan.]]
[[Jedi Master]] [[Crucitorn]] endured the pain of being [[Fire|immolated]] during the [[Battle of Nar Shaddaa]] between the [[Jedi Order]] and the [[Flame-Wielders]] through [[the Force]].<ref name="D:JL" /> At some point between [[102 BBY]] and [[68 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' establishes that [[Dooku]] was [[Reproduction|born]] in [[102 BBY]] and also dates [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] becoming Dooku's [[Padawan]] to [[68 BBY]]. As ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'' establishes that Dooku had learned Endure prior to Jinn becoming his Padawan, he must have been taught it between those [[Standard year|years]].</ref> Jedi Master [[Lene Kostana]] taught the technique to fellow Jedi [[Dooku]]. Dooku taught it to his two [[Padawan]]s: [[Rael Averross]],<ref name="D:JL" /> whom he trained from around [[80 BBY]] to around [[70 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> and, after taking him on<ref name="D:JL" /> in 68 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Qui-Gon Jinn]]. To show the technique to Jinn, Dooku placed a [[metal]] band that the [[Heltraxi]] used as a [[torture]] device on his own chest.<ref name="D:JL" />

Dooku later told his [[House Serenno|sister]], [[Jenza]], about his conversation with Jinn in a [[holo-letter]].<ref name="D:JL" /> Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="DJL-JournalsDate">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Dooku gave several [[Datacard|data disks]] containing his messages to Jenza to his recently recruited [[Sith assassin]]&mdash;[[Asajj Ventress]]&mdash;for her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|investigation]] into his [[Gender|sister's]] location, and she listened to the holo-disk that mentioned Endure.<ref name="D:JL" />

==Behind the scenes==
Endure was first mentioned in<ref name="KoF" /> the [[2018]]<ref name="Way of the Warrior">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/6/21/way-of-the-warrior|text=Way of the Warrior}}</ref> sourcebook ''[[Knights of Fate]]'', published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for the ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[roleplaying game]].<ref name="KoF" /> It first appeared in a flashback in the [[2019]] audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', authored by [[Cavan Scott]].<ref name="D:JL" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, a similar [[Force power/Legends|Force power]] named [[Crucitorn (technique)|Crucitorn]] was first mentioned in the [[2002]] reference book ''[[The New Essential Guide to Characters]]'', by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TNEGTC">''[[The New Essential Guide to Characters]]''</ref>
