'''Bad Fran''' was a [[humanoid]] who worked as a [[bounty hunter]] for the [[Haxion Brood]] crime syndicate.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

In [[14 BBY]], Bad Fran was sent from [[Ordo Eris]] by the Haxion Brood to eliminate the [[Jedi]] [[Cal Kestis]], but failed and got themselves killed.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />{{Char-stub}}
