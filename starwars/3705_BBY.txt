'''3705 BBY''', or '''3705<ref name="BMF 29" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]].<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> That year, [[Freia Kallea]] established the [[hyperspace route]] known as the [[Hydian Way]], combining multiple smaller routes to create a [[super-hyperroute]], thereby providing [[Merchant|traders]] and [[privateer]]s with a path across the entire [[The galaxy|galaxy]].<ref name="BMF 29" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], 3705 BBY was mentioned in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" section of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 29|twenty-ninth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 29" /> published around [[July 22]], [[2015]]<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|29}}</ref> and written by [[Mark Newbold]].<ref name="SM">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> [[3705 BBY/Legends|3705 BBY]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was mentioned in [[Jason Fry]] and [[Daniel Wallace]]'s reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', published in [[2009]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
