The '''Droideka Oppressor''',<ref name="Commander" /> also known as the '''"Oppressor" model''',<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> was a model of [[droideka]] that was developed so late it never saw use in the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Commander" /> The Oppressor was a heavy [[artillery]] droid,<ref name="CVD" /> and during the [[Galactic Civil War]], a number of [[smuggler]]s re-purposed them to protect their contraband. Similar to its its [[Droideka Sentinel|predecessor]], the Oppressor was fitted with two twin [[blaster]] [[cannon]]s, but its artillery could level anything that was not protected by a [[deflector shield]].<ref name="Commander" />
