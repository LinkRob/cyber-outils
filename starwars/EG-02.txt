'''EG-02''' was a [[GNK-series power droid]]<ref name="GEStaff">{{GEStaff|Service Yard|area}}</ref> manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]]<ref name="Stats" /> with [[Color|white]] plating. During the [[First Order]] [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="GESY" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> EG-02 spent [[time]] on the world at the [[Droid Depot]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]]. The [[droid]] was held upside down while being repaired at the depot's exterior droid repair station in the [[Service Yard]]. While being repaired the [[power droid]] made happy beeping sounds.<ref name="GESY" />
