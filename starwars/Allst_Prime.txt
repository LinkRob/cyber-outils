'''Allst Prime''' was a backwater [[planet]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]] where [[Governor]] [[Magé]] rule over an impoverished population during the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]]. Between [[2 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] was deployed into Allst Prime to [[Battle of Allst Prime|destroy]] the [[Allst Prime Imperial outpost|Imperial outpost]] there. During the battle, the [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldier]], secretly a [[bounty hunter]], [[Brand]] rescued one of her comrades and then joined [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]] to incapacitate Magé at her [[Imperial headquarters (Allst Prime)|headquarters]]. Brand then intended to collect the [[bounty]] on Evon, but the captain convinced her to stay with the 61st.

==Description==
Allst Prime was a backwater [[planet]]<ref name="Inbrief" /> in the [[Taldot sector]]'s [[Allst Prime system]], within an intersecting portion of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Slice]] regions.<ref name="Slice" /> Situated at the coordinates Q-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> it featured [[forest]]s, at least one [[city]], an [[Allst Prime Imperial outpost|Imperial outpost]], and an [[Imperial headquarters (Allst Prime)|Imperial headquarters]].<ref name="Inbrief" /> The world's Type I [[atmosphere]]<ref name="Atmosphere" /> was breathable to at least [[human]]s and [[mirialan]]s.<ref name="Inbrief" />

==History==
{{Quote|All I want is unswerving loyalty&mdash;not to the Alliance, and not even to me, but to the people out there, the ones you saved today.|Evon convinces Brand to stay with the 61st|<ref name="Inbrief" />}}
[[File:Brand_full.png|thumb|left|250px|Brand raises her blaster at Micha Evon while at the Allst Prime Imperial headquarters]]
During the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]], Allst Prime was [[Governor|governed]] by the [[administrator]] [[Magé]], who lived in luxury in the planet's Imperial headquarters while the residents in the nearby city lived in poverty, half-naked children digging through trash heaps for junk to sell for [[Foodstuff|food]].<ref name="Inbrief" /> Between [[2 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{InbriefDate}}</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] was deployed to the planet to destroy the Imperial outpost. The [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldiers]] of the 61st engaged Imperial [[Imperial Military|forces]] on Allst Prime.<ref name="Inbrief" />

The locals of Allst Prime did not put up much resistance to the 61st's arrival. During the fighting, the rebel [[soldier]] [[Brand]]&mdash;secretly a [[bounty hunter]] sent to [[Death|kill]] the 61st's [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]]&mdash;rescued her [[Mirialan]] comrade [[Lylee Anaraku]] from an [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST walker]]. After the pair exchanged, Brand aimed her [[blaster]] at a distant Evon, who was filling in for the fallen [[Forward Air Controller]] [[Cait]]. Anaraku was then injured by an Imperial who Brand killed before she tended to her Mirialan friend.<ref name="Inbrief" />

Later, Brand and Evon entered the Imperial headquarters and incapacitated Governor Magé and her [[Magé's aide|aide]] at the governor's officer. At Magé's office, Brand drew her blaster on Evon to take the bounty on him. However, Evon had already learned of the bounty hunter's background and motives and discussed it to her, convincing her to stay with the 61st for the soldiers she had become close to.<ref name="Inbrief" />
