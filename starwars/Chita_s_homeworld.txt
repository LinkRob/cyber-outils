{{Quote|To tell the truth, the Empire also made a mess of my planet and my family right along with it. It was awful. I guess you all aren't the only ones holding a grudge.|Chita|audio=ChitasPast-Smuggler.mp3|<ref name="Smuggler">{{Visions|The Smuggler (short film)}}</ref>}}
A '''planet''' was the [[homeworld]] of the [[human]] [[smuggler]] [[Chita]].<ref name="Smuggler" /> At some point between [[19 BBY]] and [[0 ABY]],<ref name="EmpireDate">"[[The Smuggler (short film)|The Smuggler]]" establishes the [[Galactic Empire]] "made a mess" of [[Chita]]'s [[planet]] and [[Chita's family|family]] before the events of the short. The short features the [[Great Temple]] inhabited by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the Galactic Empire and the [[evacuation of Yavin 4]] to [[19 BBY]] and [[0 ABY]], respectively, the short's events must take place between those two [[Standard year|years]].</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]] arrived to the [[Planet|world]] and, according to Chita, "made a mess" of the planet and her [[Chita's family|family]], viewing the event as awful. Later,<ref name="Smuggler" /> at some point between [[2 BBY]] and 0 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VisionsDate|307}}</ref> Chita told the former [[Jedi]] [[Gleenu]] of her homeworld when talking of the planet [[Zenarita]]'s struggle under Imperial rule, mentioning they were not the only ones that held a grudge against the Empire.<ref name="Smuggler" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chita's homeworld was mentioned in "[[The Smuggler (short film)|The Smuggler]]," a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Trigger (animation studio)|Trigger]], was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Smuggler" /> on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="ReleaseDate">{{SW|url=news/why-we-love-star-wars-visions|text=Why We Love the Unique Stories of ''Star Wars: Visions''}}</ref>
