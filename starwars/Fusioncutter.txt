A '''fusioncutter''',<ref name="The Snare">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Snare]]''</ref> sometimes spelled '''fusion cutter''',<ref name="AEYNK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> was a type of handheld cutting and welding torch with an energy blade that was commonly found in toolkits.<ref name="The Snare" /> After the battles with the [[Galactic Republic]], [[EV-A4-D]], [[General]] [[Grievous]]'s personal [[medical droid]],<ref name="Lair of Grievous">{{TCW|Lair of Grievous}}</ref> frequently repaired the [[cyborg]]'s body.<ref name="AEYNK" /> When Grievous's face [[Helmet|mask]] needed to be replaced,<ref name="Lair of Grievous" /> it must be cruelly removed from his face with a fusion cutter.<ref name="AEYNK" />
