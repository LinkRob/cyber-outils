'''Ed Begley, Jr.''' (born [[September 16]], [[1940s#1949|1949]] in [[Wikipedia:Los Angeles|Los Angeles]], [[Wikipedia:California|California]], [[Wikipedia:United States of America|United States]]) is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] actor and environmental activist, known for his role as Doctor Victor Ehrlich in the television series [[Wikipedia:St. Elsewhere|''St. Elsewhere'']].<ref name="Interview" /> He was the voice of [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]] in the [[1996]] [[Return of the Jedi (radio)|radio adaptation]]<ref name="Radio">[[Return of the Jedi (radio)|''Return of the Jedi'' radio drama]]</ref> of the [[1983]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>
