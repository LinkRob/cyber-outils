'''''Black One''''',<ref name="Last Order">''[[The Last Order]]''</ref> also known as '''Poe Dameron's X-wing fighter''', or '''Poe's X-wing''', was a [[Resistance]] [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] that was utilized by [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the Resistance.

==Characteristics==
Unlike his beloved ''[[Black One]]''<ref name="TROS VD" /> that had a combination of black and orange, Poe's new X-wing was light gray and orange with a bit of blue.<ref name="Amazing Sticker Adventure" /> Occasionally Dameron temporarily repainted his new X-wing's [[hull]] with a tribute to his old livery.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==History==
[[File:PoeDameronsXWingFighter-Databank.jpg|thumb|left|270px|Poe's X-wing at the Ajan Kloss Resistance base]]
Following the destruction of ''Black One'', [[Poe Dameron]] later used another X-wing to replace it, but it was colored orange. The fighter saw action during the [[Battle of Exegol]], where he used it to lead the Resistance to stop the [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] of the [[Sith Eternal]]'s fleet, the [[Final Order]], from taking off to conquer the galaxy and establish a new [[Sith Empire]]. Once the battle began, Poe Dameron used the fighter to engage several [[TIE/dg starfighter]]s that took off from the Sith Star Destroyers in order to clear the way for the [[Exegol landing team]] to land on the ground so they could destroy the [[navigation tower]]. However, the navigation signal was switched on to the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] so [[Enric Pryde]] could guide the fleet out themselves, forcing Finn to call off the ground invasion but deciding to launch an assault on the [[command ship]]. Poe Dameron rallied his wingmen including [[Temmin Wexley]] to provide cover for the landing ship to allow Finn and the rest of the fighters to land safely. As Finn and Jannah engaged the [[Sith trooper]]s so they could try to destroy the navigation signal, Poe's X-wing destroyed a few Sith TIE fighters to provide support for them. Poe then ordered the rest of the pilots to hit the Sith Star Destroyers themselves as they believed that help wasn't coming.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:HeraldsOfHopeBoxArt.png|thumb|right|250px|Poe's X-wing in flight]]
The Resistance began to take heavy casualties as swarms of Sith TIEs shot several fighters down, leaving Poe distraught without hope. However, Poe then heard [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] on the comlink saying that there were more of them, allowing him to fly the X-wing to a safe position, realizing that Lando brought in a [[Citizens' Fleet|huge fleet]] to aid the Resistance. Poe then rallied all of the pilots to hit the underbelly cannons of the Sith Star Destroyers, and it turned out that the battle was turning the tide. Their arrival wasn't too long as [[Darth Sidious]], back to his normal self, used [[Force lightning]] to disable every ship, including Poe Dameron's X-wing. As Poe Dameron's X-wing was electrocuted by Force lightning, he began losing control of the ship as he tried to contact for help. The X-wing's control systems were then brought back online when Poe told the others that they had one last chance to take down the Sith Star Destroyers' cannons, just as [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] harnessed powers from the spirits of past Jedi to take Sidious down in one last stand. Following the destruction of the ''Steadfast'', Poe used the X-wing to take down the remaining defenseless Sith fleet and tried to rescue Finn and Jannah, but Lando arrived at the rescue. The Resistance began to return to base to celebrate their victory.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Behind the scenes==
Poe's X-wing first appeared in the [[2019]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="Episode IX" /> The ship was first mentioned in the [[2019]] sticker book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Amazing Sticker Adventures]]''.<ref name="Amazing Sticker Adventure" />
