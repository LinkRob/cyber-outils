A '''chest binder''', or simply a '''binder''', was a type of [[clothing]] that fastened one's chest. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[human]] [[pilot]] [[Sky Graf]] used a chest binder to ease their anxiety.

==Description==
Chest binders, also known as binders, were a type of medical [[clothing]]. They were used during the [[High Republic Era]] to bind a person's chest if they were distressed by the shape of their body.<ref name="QFPX" />

==History==
[[File:SkyGraf-QFPX.png|thumb|right|195px|Sky Graf, whose father gave them a binder]]
Around [[385 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'' establishes that [[Sky Graf]] was fifteen [[Standard year|years]] old in between the [[Battle of Jedha]] and the [[Night of Sorrow]], both of which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]]. Therefore, Sky Graf was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[397 BBY]] and was twelve years old around [[385 BBY]].</ref> when [[Sky Graf]], a [[human]] member of the [[Graf family]], was twelve [[Standard year|years]] old, their [[Sky Graf's father|father]] gave them their first chest binder after a panic attack while attempting to put on their brother [[Helis Graf]]'s old [[pilot vest]]. Graf had told their [[Parent|father]] that the panic attack was caused by their fear of not being able to become a [[pilot]] if they could not fit into a pilot vest. When telling them that vests come in different shapes and sizes for all kinds of bodies, Graf's father found out that it was not the fear of not being able to be a pilot that troubled Graf and that they were actually distressed by how their body was different from their brother's. The chest binder thus helped them ease the anxiety.<ref name="QFPX" />

Three years later, in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Graf joined the [[Hyperspace Chase]] with [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] and [[prospector]] [[Dass Leffbruk]] to reach the mysterious [[Sophros|Planet X]] that Graf's father had been obsessed with finding before he disappeared a [[383 BBY|year earlier]]. The chase was a contest sponsored by the [[San Tekka clan]] and the Graf family to map [[hyperspace route]]s. Graf was certain they would find their father if they found Planet X, and while attempting to make their way to the [[planet]], Graf remembered how their father had given them their first binder when they were twelve.<ref name="QFPX" />

Chest binders were mentioned in [[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X|''Quest for Planet X'']], a junior novel written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in [[2023]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia campaign's<ref name="QFPX" /> Phase II.<ref name="Phase">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2022-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=NYCC 2022: 10 Things We Learned From the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref> After Gratton reposted a fan art of Sky Graf on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]], they were asked if Graf is the first [[canon]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' character to use a binder. In response, Gratton said they did not know if that was the case but acknowledged that several readers thought it was.<ref name="Twitter" /> At the [[Lucasfilm]] Publishing Panel at [[Wikipedia:San Diego Comic-Con|San Diego Comic-Con]] on [[July 25]], [[2025]],<ref name="Panel">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-san-diego-comic-con-lucasfilm-publishing-panel-2025|text=SDCC 2025: 9 Highlights from the Lucasfilm Publishing Panel}}</ref> Gratton discussed their creation of Graf. The author indicated that they had never received any pushback for including a non-binary character who wears a binder; instead, they were actively encouraged to be more inclusive and push boundaries.<ref name="SDCC">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@PenguinRandomHouse|name=Penguin Random House|link=Random House Worlds|video=8YEjHFZJ2g8|text=Star Wars Books Panel - with editors, author, and audiobook narrator &#124; San Diego Comic-Con 2025|date=2025-07-29|quote=One of the main characters of my middle-grade is a non-binary kid who wears a binder, and not only did I never get any pushback at any level, but I was encouraged by everyone.}}</ref> In the real-world, binders are used to flatten the chest.<ref name="Binding">{{WebCite|url=https://www.lgbtqnation.com/2022/12/bind-chest-safely|text=How to bind your chest safely|author=[https://www.lgbtqnation.com/author/emily-bashforth Emily Bashforth]|date=2022-12-30|work=[[Wikipedia:LGBTQ Nation|''LGBTQ Nation'']]|archivedate=20230420010845}}</ref>
