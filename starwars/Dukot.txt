'''Dukot''' was an individual who left the materials to apply an equilibrium paint job to a [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]] in a container on a balcony in [[Mos Eisley]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] climbed the buildings in the [[South Quarter (Cloud City)|South Quarter]] of Mos Eisley and used her grapple to swing onto the balcony and steal the materials.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dukot was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. Their stash contains one of the collectible paint schemes that can be used to customize the player's speeder in the game.<ref name="Outlaws" />
