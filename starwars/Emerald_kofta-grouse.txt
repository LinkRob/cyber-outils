'''Emerald kofta-grouses''' were a type of [[bird]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> that partially shared their name with [[kofta-bun]]s, a kind of [[bread]].<ref name="ALD">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> While on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jas Emari]] watched the [[Emperor's adviser|Imperial Advisor]] [[Yupe Tashu]] exit a [[shuttle]], mentally considering that his [[hat]] looked silly, as if someone had [[Death|killed]] an emerald kofta-grouse and placed it on his head.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Emerald kofta-grouses were mentioned in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
