The '''Cartel Hunter''' was a costume owned by the [[Trandoshan]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Charr]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Cunning Trapper|standard costume]].<ref name="Hunters" /> It resembled the general aesthetic of clothing worn by members of the [[White Worms]] operating out of the [[planet]] [[Corellia]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cartel Hunter was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Charr, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Cartel Scatter Gun]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing it individually within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
