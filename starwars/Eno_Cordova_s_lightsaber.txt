==Behind the scenes==
Eno Cordova's lightsaber first appeared in the [[2019]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Jedi Fallen Order - Dark Temple 1|''Jedi Fallen Order - Dark Temple'' 1]] written by [[Matthew Rosenberg]].<ref name="Dark Temple 1" /> In the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', the player can collect Eno Cordova lightsaber components throughout the game to customize [[Cal Kestis]]' lightsaber, similar to the default [[Jaro Tapal]] lightsaber parts. However, unlike [[Jaro Tapal's lightsaber|Tapal's lightsaber]], the Eno Cordova lightsaber parts do not resemble<ref name="Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> the weapon used by Cordova in the ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Fallen Order - Dark Temple]]'' comic book mini-series.<ref name="Dark Temple 1" />
