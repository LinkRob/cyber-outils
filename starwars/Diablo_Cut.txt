'''Diablo Cut''' was a treacherous ravine within [[Beggar's Canyon]] on [[Tatooine]]. By narrowly making it through both this and the [[Stone Needle]] in his [[T-16 skyhopper]], [[Luke Skywalker]] proved [[Gender|himself]] to be an excellent [[pilot]].<ref name="Complete Visual Dictionary" />
