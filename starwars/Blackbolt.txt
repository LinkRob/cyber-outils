'''Blackbolt''' was a [[droid]] [[pirate]] [[captain]] who operated on the [[starship]] ''[[Terror of Tenoo]]'' during the [[High Republic Era]], plundering the [[planet]] [[Tenoo]], including [[Kaliah Kublop]]'s [[Kaliah Kublop's staff|staff]]. However, he eventually vanished; the ''Terror'' crashed on an [[asteroid]] near the planet and both Blackbolt and his vessel were left deactivated. The [[pilot]] [[Nash Durango]], unaware Blackbolt was a droid, accidentally reactivated him when she discovered his lost starship. After being sent through [[hyperspace]] to the far reaches of [[the galaxy]],<ref name="The Terror of Tenoo" /> Blackbolt would later return to Tenoo with his [[Blackbolt's crew|crew]] in search for [[Tanglevine Base]].<ref name="The Lost Treasure of Tenoo" />

Blackbolt wielded a [[Color|red]]<ref name="The Terror of Tenoo" /> [[Electroblade training sword|electrosword]].<ref name="The Terror of Tenoo AD">{{YJA|The Terror of Tenoo}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> that made his body spark with red electricity when in use.<ref name="The Terror of Tenoo" /> The captain had a fear of [[tanglevine snake]]s.<ref name="The Lost Treasure of Tenoo" /> Whenever he sparked red he would get a boost in strength, he also fought Jedi in the past including Jedi Master Staas. Rage and death would come to anyone who called him "rusty."

==Behind the scenes==
Captain Blackbolt is a character created for ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''.
