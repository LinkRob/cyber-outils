'''Denner''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who served as an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[technician]]. Fourteen [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], Denner was stationed on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] [[Ryloth]] and served under [[Moff]] [[Delian Mors]].<ref name="Mercy Mission" />

==Behind the scenes==
Denner first appeared in the [[2015]] [[canon]] short story "[[Mercy Mission (short story)|Mercy Mission]]," written by [[Melissa Scott]].<ref name="Mercy Mission" />
