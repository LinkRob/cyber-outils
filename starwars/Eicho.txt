'''Eicho''' was a [[Valon]] [[Jedi Master]] who served the [[Jedi Order]], serving in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]'s [[Valo Temple|Jedi temple]] by [[Jedi training|training]] the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Tep Tep]], [[Kildo]], and [[Gavi Takitaken|Gavi]]. After Eicho refused to allow Tep Tep to keep a [[rathtar]] named [[Bubbles]] in the [[Jedi temple|temple]], Kildo placed a jar of [[spotted spider]]s in his bag in retaliation. The Jedi Master [[Death|died]] on Valo during a [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] [[Attack on Valo|attack]] in [[230 BBY]], and the [[youngling]]s later recounted the rathtar and spotted [[spider]] incidents to [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] the [[229 BBY|following year]].

==Biography==

Eicho was a [[Valon]] [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]] and operated out of the [[Valo Temple|Jedi temple]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]. He [[Jedi training|trained]] [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Tep Tep]], [[Kildo]], and [[Gavi Takitaken|Gavi]] in [[meditation]], which the [[Youngling|children]] found boring. One [[time]], Tep Tep brought a [[rathtar]] named [[Bubbles]]&mdash;who had broken out of the [[Lonisa City Zoo]]&mdash;into the [[Jedi temple|temple]] to heal its injuries. Eicho passed out upon seeing the creature, and Bubbles attempted to [[Diet|eat]] him.<ref name="EFV" />

Ultimately, Eicho did not allow Tep Tep to keep the creature and spoke harshly to her about bringing it in. Afterward, the Jedi Master canceled his meditation sessions with the younglings for two [[Standard week|weeks]] while recovering from the incident's shock and left the Valo temple. Upon his return, Kildo snuck a jar of [[spotted spider]]s into Eicho's bag as retaliation for the Master reprimanding Tep Tep. Eicho was shocked and departed the temple again shortly after his arrival until he had recovered from the incident. Following the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]] of the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]]<ref name="EFV" /> in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="THRCE">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Valo's Jedi Masters prepared to leave and head to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="EFV" /> the [[Core Worlds]] planet<ref name="THRCE" /> [[Coruscant]]. However, their efforts were stalled when Valo was [[Attack on Valo|attacked]] by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] not long after<ref name="EFV" /> in the same [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="Date" />

All of the Valo Jedi temple's Masters<ref name="EFV" />&mdash;including Eicho<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|djolder|status/1752785438899593614|[[Daniel José Older]]|date=2024-01-31|quote=yes all dead|archivefile=File:ValoMasters-OlderTweet.png}} {{C|In response to: "There's some Valo Jedi Masters from ''Race to Crashpoint Tower'' (Lege, Shonnatrucks, Devo) then also a smattering of Valo Jedi Masters mentioned throughout ''Escape from Valo''. The younglings in the novel and Driggit see many of them perish in the attack on Valo &mdash; is it safe to say all of them passed away save for Kunpar, or were there ones that survived and the younglings only assumed they all got dusted?"}}</ref>&mdash;hurriedly attempted to flee into the world's [[mountain]]s to escape [[Lonisa City]]. However, the Nihil caught the [[Jedi]] and [[Death|killed]] all of them despite their attempts to fight back, with several being fed to a [[Living Force|Force]]-eating [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creature. The ordeal left only Gavi, Kildo, and Tep Tep surviving,<ref name="EFV" /> albeit deeply scarred by the experience.<ref name="BTN">''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''</ref> After living for a [[229 BBY|year]] on their own in the wilderness, Tep Tep, Kildo, and Gavi returned to the temple and met with [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]], one of the only other Jedi on the world, and told him about the deaths of Valo's Jedi Masters. While introducing themselves, the younglings recounted the stories of the rathtar and spotted [[spider]] incidents to Jomaram, as well as Eicho's reactions to both. While Tep Tep insisted she would have never let the rathtar eat Eicho, Kildo claimed that Gavi could not prove he was responsible for placing the spiders in the Master's bag.<ref name="EFV" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date2">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> Tep Tep and Kildo reminisced on seeing Valo's Jedi Masters fed to Nameless during the Nihil's occupation of their planet, somberly remembering how their teachers had turned to husks.<ref name="BTN" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Master Eicho had to leave ''again'' to get over it.''"<br />"''He shouldn't have been rude to Tep Tep. And for the record, you can't prove anything.|Gavi and Kildo, to Ram Jomaram, about Eicho departing the temple following an incident with spotted spiders|<ref name="EFV" />}}
While training younglings on Valo, Eicho became frightened when Tep Tep brought a rathtar to the temple and passed out. The incident flustered him, resulting in him departing the temple for two weeks and canceling scheduled meditation sessions, much to the delight of the three younglings he was [[Teacher|teaching]]. While reflecting on the incident, Kildo did not regret placing spiders in the Jedi Master's bag, having considered the way he had spoken to Tep Tep in the past rude. Eicho attempted to help evacuate the younglings from the Nihil during a Nihil attack, fighting against the occupiers to give his students more time to escape.<ref name="EFV" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a member of the Jedi Order, the Jedi Master was a [[Force-sensitive]] individual trained to [[Force power|wield]] [[the Force]].<ref name="EFV" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eicho was mentioned in [[The High Republic: Escape from Valo|''Escape from Valo'']], a [[2024]] junior novel written by [[Daniel José Older]] and [[Alyssa Wong]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="EFV" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref> On [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]], Older confirmed that all of the Valo Temple's Jedi Masters&mdash;including Eicho&mdash;perished in the Nihil attack.<ref name="Twitter" />
