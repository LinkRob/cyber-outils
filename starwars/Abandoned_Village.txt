The '''Abandoned Village''' was a [[City|village]] located on the [[Temperature|cold]] [[planet]] [[Zeffo]]. The settlement was near the [[Derelict Hangar]], the [[Turbine Facility]], and the [[Weathered Monument]]. It was founded by [[Galactic Republic]] [[archaeologist]]s and [[Scout|pioneers]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Galactic Empire]] occupied the village as part of [[Project Auger]] excavation work.

Before being abandoned by [[14 BBY]], it housed several inhabitants, including [[Mirienna]] and [[Sienna]]&mdash;all displaced because of the occupation. In the aftermath, Mirienna and Sienna survived, and the village was abandoned. The settlement was later inhabited by fauna, such as the [[scazz]], leading to constant attacks on the Imperial [[garrison]] stationed. In 14 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], fugitive [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] [[Mission to Zeffo|came across]] the village in his [[Secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order|quest]] to restore the [[Jedi Order]].

==Description==
===Layout===
[[File:Abandoned_Village_Map.png|thumb|right|280px|The layout of the Abandoned Village]]
The Abandoned Village consisted of ten buildings located on the [[Temperature|cold]] [[planet]] [[Zeffo]]. The [[City|village]] had a cliff on one side, a large ravine encompassing two sides, and a [[mountain]] wall on the fourth. It was close to the [[Derelict Hangar]], where [[starship]]s could land and trade with the villagers, and the [[Turbine Facility]], a run-down facility that operated several [[turbine]]s. The [[Weathered Monument]], a statue created by the ancient technologically advanced [[Zeffonian]]s, could be seen from the village. The village was built in a multilevel area between two mountain peaks. There were at least three entrances to the settlement: two from the Derelict [[Hangar]] and one from the Turbine Facility.<ref name="JFO" />

It could be entered from the Turbine Facility by traveling through a [[cave]] that included a bridge across a ravine. The village was also accessible from the Derelict Hangar through a long winding pathway along the mountain's edge. The path to the village also contained a [[metal]] bridge that had been destroyed by<ref name="JFO" /> the [[Standard year|year]] [[14 BBY]].<ref name="JFO Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> The bridge led into a pathway dotted with wide [[stone]] slabs near the outskirts of the settlement, which ended within a rooftop structure in the village. Many buildings were separated from the main settlement by a large turbine. A shortcut could be accessed through a [[blast door]] in the Derelict Hangar that led into a tunnel and later to the rooftop of a house.<ref name="JFO" />

===Architecture===
[[File:AbandonedVillageArchitecture.png|thumb|left|315px|The village by 14 BBY]]
Much of the village's architecture utilized stone in round, curved shapes,<ref name="Art JFO">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> and many buildings were decorated with banners filled with [[color]]ful imagery. Metal bridges on the rooftops connected these buildings, one of which connected a home close to a [[water]]fall to the rest of the village in between a deep ravine. The ravine contained a river flowing into another waterfall beside the village, which ran into a body of water below the mountain.<ref name="JFO" />

By 14 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="JFO Date" /> several [[GX-8 water vaporator|GX-8 Moisture Vaporators]] surrounded the village, some of which were located on the rooftops of the buildings. Many of the metal bridges were destroyed and were inhabited by local fauna, such as the subterranean, verminous [[scazz]], and at least one nearby [[jotaz]]&mdash;large and highly aggressive predators native to Zeffo. By then, many buildings were covered in snow and [[moss]], with old pots lining the village streets, overgrown with [[grass]].<ref name="JFO" />

==History==
===Republic Era and Imperial Occupation===
{{Quote|Civilians must immediately report for relocation. Looters will be shot. By Imperial Code 94364: No Selling or Trading without proper authorization and explicit consent, this property and its belongings are now under the protection of the Galactic Empire. General Markon 201st Squadron.|An Eviction Notice|<ref name="JFO" />}}
The village was established by a small community of [[Galactic Republic]] [[archaeologist]]s and [[Scout|pioneers]].<ref name="Art JFO" /> After [[Order 66]]<ref name="JFO" /> in [[19 BBY]], an [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|order]] programmed in the [[behavioral modification biochip]]s in [[clone trooper]]s to execute the [[Jedi]] for [[treason]] against the Galactic Republic,<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the villagers found the [[Crash Site]] of a Republic [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Zeffo)|'' Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] that had crashed after a [[Battle against a Separatist dreadnought|battle]] against a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[dreadnought]]. They built a memorial for the fallen [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s on the crashed [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|ship]].<ref name="JFO" />

[[File:Zeffo.png|thumb|right|320px|The Empire occupied the village and forced its inhabitants out.]]
By 14 BBY,<ref name="JFO Date" /> the [[Galactic Empire]] arrived on Zeffo and initiated [[Project Auger]], an Imperial project to excavate Zeffonian artifacts of value for the [[Darth Sidious|Galactic Emperor]], with excavations beginning almost immediately. As part of the project's goals, the Empire occupied the village and redistributed its inhabitants by forcing them from their homes and shipping them to [[labor camp]]s through [[Imperial Code 94364]]. The code forbade selling or trading without proper authorization and also stated that the villagers' property and belongings were to be protected by the Empire's [[201st Squadron]], which [[General]] [[Markon]] led.<ref name="JFO" />

===Escape and discovery===
{{Quote|A mother used this fabric to keep her child warm. They were on the run from something.|Cal Kestis|<ref name="JFO" />}}
While the inhabitants were being relocated, the villager [[Ostar]] distracted several Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s to allow his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Mirienna]], and his [[Gender|daughter]], [[Sienna]], to escape. Mirienna and Sienna survived and were free from the Empire's forced relocation efforts on the planet; however, the village became abandoned and swept by the strong gusty winds of Zeffo.<ref name="JFO" /> In 14 BBY,<ref name="JFO Date" /> two [[scout trooper]]s attacked and [[Death|killed]] a scazz on the edge of the village.<ref name="JFO" /> That year,<ref name="JFO Date" /> fugitive Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] [[Mission to Zeffo|explored]] the village and fought against stormtroopers, standard scout troopers, and [[Stormtrooper Commander|Scout Trooper Commanders]] to reach the [[Tomb of Eilram]] and [[Second mission to Zeffo|later]] the [[Tomb of Miktrull]].<ref name="JFO" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
While there, Kestis used a [[Force power|Force ability]] called [[psychometry]] to learn what happened before he arrived. During his exploration, he opened one chest by a stone wall on the edge of the settlement that contained enough [[durite]] to construct a [[lightsaber]] handle. He also opened two chests positioned on rooftops, one containing a sleeve for the [[Duty and Resolve]] lightsaber and one containing the material for the [[Guardian (outfit)|Guardian]] [[Clothing|outfit]].<ref name="JFO" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Abandoned_Village_Concept_Art.png|thumb|right|300px|Jean-Francois Rey's concept art of the village]]
The Abandoned Village first appeared and was identified in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]] and published by [[Electronic Arts]]<ref name="JFO" /> on [[November 15]], [[2019]].<ref name="YBY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> The location was first revealed in the game's [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1wWkQXUMQYI Xbox E3 Briefing] on [[June 10]], 2019.<ref name="JFO Xbox E3 Briefing 2019">{{EASWYouTube|1wWkQXUMQYI|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order Official Trailer &ndash; Xbox E3 Briefing 2019|sw_url=video/star-wars-jedi-fallen-order-e3-trailer}}</ref> Artist [[Jean-Francois Rey]] created concept art of the village.<ref name="Art JFO" />

Like the rest of the game, the location was heavily influenced by [[Wikipedia:Metroidvania|3D Metroidvania type design]]&mdash;an [[Wikipedia:Action-adventure game|action-adventure]] subgenre defined by large, interconnected maps containing paths that can be unlocked as the player progresses through the game.<ref name="GameSpot">{{GameSpot|url=articles/jedi-fallen-order-didnt-start-out-as-a-star-wars-g/1100-6467511|text=Jedi Fallen Order Didn't Start Out As A Star Wars Game - E3 2019}}</ref> It was the first environment designed and was given simple shapes to evoke familiarity and strong readability. In one set of concept art, it was depicted as being situated at the edge of a mountain, and the buildings there were pictured as being more triangular.<ref name="Art JFO" />
