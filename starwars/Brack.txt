'''Brack''' was the manager of several warehouses located in the southeast section of [[Gelgelar Free Port/Legends|Gelgelar Free Port]]. Brack bought [[vohis mold]] from the local [[Farmer/Legends|farmers]] and sold it to [[Starship/Legends|starship]] [[Captain/Legends|captains]] who then transported it to food companies like [[Nebula Consumables]] and [[Falvin Foods]].
