A '''farrier rasp''' was a tool used for the smithy. It was generally used by blacksmiths to coarsely shaping wood or other material.<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

By the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]], the [[Force-sensitive]] stable hand [[Temiri Blagg]] wore a farrier rasp tied to his [[belt]] frequently during his [[Standard day|days]] of forced labor at the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|Canto Bight racetrack]] of [[Canto Bight]], [[Cantonica]].<ref name="TLJ VD" />
