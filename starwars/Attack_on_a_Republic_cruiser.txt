In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Clone Wars]], a [[Unidentified Death Watch saboteur|Mandalorian saboteur]], who was a member of the [[Mandalorian]] [[Death Watch]] group and acting with support from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], launched an '''attack''' on a [[Galactic Republic]] [[cruiser]]<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" /> located in a [[shipyard]]. Upon being discovered,<ref name="EP 212 Relationships" /> the [[saboteur]] exchanged fire with [[Republic Military|Republic forces]] before [[Death|choosing]] to [[Death|take his own life]]. While that robbed the Republic of a chance to question the Mandalore, a [[Hologram|holographic]] recording of the assault was made.<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" />

In response to the attack, the [[Jedi High Council]] dispatched [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] to the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]] to speak with [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]], the leader of the pacifistic [[New Mandalorians]]. While on Mandalore, Kenobi led an investigation to discover if Kryze, the planet, or the New Mandalorians had joined the Confederacy. Upon his arrival, Kenobi showed the recording of the assault to the Duchess.<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on a Republic cruiser first appeared in a holographic recording in "[[The Mandalore Plot]],"<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" /> the twelfth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which aired on [[January 29]], [[2010]]<ref name="EpGuide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode212.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: The Mandalore Plot}}</ref>
