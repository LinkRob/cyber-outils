'''Crystalline chandelier''' was a type of [[chandelier]] made of [[Crystalline vertex|crystalline]] that was embedded with various [[gemstone]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a crystalline chandelier could be sold for 180 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crystalline chandeliers appeared as inventory items in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
