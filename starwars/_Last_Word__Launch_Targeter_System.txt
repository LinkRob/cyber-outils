The '''"Last Word" Launch/Targeter System''' was a model of [[Micro-Rocket Armor-Mounting]], a type of [[armor]] attachment that could fire [[Wrist rocket|micro-rockets]], manufactured by [[Goseia Heavy Industries Concern]] and sold for 800 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Last Word" Launch/Targeter System was first mentioned in [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']],<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> a [[2017]] supplement for the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="A Fistful of Credits">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/2/2/a-fistful-of-credits|text=A Fistful of Credits}}</ref>
