A '''flux compressor''' was a device that was used to charge smoke payloads with electromagnetic impulses, which could disable [[droid]]s and other electronics. Flux compressors were a key component in constructing [[Ion smoke bomb|ion smoke grenades]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a flux compressor had been stored in a [[smuggler]] hideout on a cliff overlooking [[Lake Amari]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] stole the flux compressor from the smuggler hideout on Akiva and, with the help of [[Temmin Wexley]], was able to start using it to construct ion [[smoke grenade]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Flux compressors appeared as an upgrade item in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The item was used to unlock the "ion smoke bomb" expert upgrade which allowed smoke bombs to effect droids.<ref name="Outlaws" />
