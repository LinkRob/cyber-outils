'''Arctic horny whelmers'''<ref name="Complete Vehicles NE">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> were ten-[[Limb|legged]] creatures found in the arctic regions of<ref name="Republic Vehicles 2" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Galaxy Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]] &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Rothana system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Rothana]]. Being both bulky and fairly slow, they [[Hunting|hunted]] in packs, wearing their prey out with their persistence. Thanks to their low [[gravity]] center, they were capable of sustaining strong gusts of [[winter]] wind, even when climbing over cliffs.<ref name="Republic Vehicles 2" /> The horny whelmers' sturdy form and peculiar squatting stance inspired the design of the [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]] [[walker]],<ref name="Complete Vehicles NE" /> which was built with multiple qualities from the creature.<ref name="Republic Vehicles 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
The arctic horny whelmer was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']]. The portion of text in which the [[species]] was mentioned<ref name="Complete Vehicles">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> was carried over from the [[2002]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', written by [[Curtis Saxton]], which was the first mention of the [[Arctic horny whelmer/Legends|arctic horny whelmer]] [[Species/Legends|species]] in that continuity.<ref name="EP2CC">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
