The '''Eleera case file''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] report that included evidence and records surrounding about [[Eleera Soi]], a [[Twi'lek]] [[contract broker]] and member of the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime [[syndicate]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a copy of it was stored at the [[Imperial Comm Station's East Turbolaser]] in the [[Boulder Forest]] region of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] found the case file in the ground based [[turbolaser]] and stole it.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Eleera case file appeared as a syndicate data item in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, the file allowed players to increase their reputation with a syndicate by giving the data to a associated syndicate merchant.<ref name="Outlaws" />
