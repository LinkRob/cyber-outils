'''Dave Hearn''' is an actor who portrays [[FN-0606]] in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].

Hearn is a founding member of the British theater company [[Wikipedia:Mischief Theatre|Mischief Theatre]]. His stage credits include plays such as ''[[Wikipedia:The Play That Goes Wrong|The Play That Goes Wrong]]'', ''[[Wikipedia:Peter Pan Goes Wrong|Peter Pan Goes Wrong]]'', ''[[Wikipedia:The Comedy About a Bank Robbery|The Comedy About a Bank Robbery]]'' and ''[[Wikipedia:Magic Goes Wrong|Magic Goes Wrong]]''.<ref name="Magic">{{WebCite|url=https://www.magicgoeswrong.com/cast-creatives/dave-hearn|text=Dave Hearn|archiveurl=https://archive.is/gq8ad}}</ref> He also appears in the BBC comedy series ''[[Wikipedia:The Goes Wrong Show|The Goes Wrong Show]]''. ''Rise of Skywalker'' director [[J.J. Abrams]] is a collaborator of Mischief, serving as producer since the Broadway production of ''The Play That Goes Wrong''.
