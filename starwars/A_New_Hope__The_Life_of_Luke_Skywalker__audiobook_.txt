'''''A New Hope: The Life of Luke Skywalker''''' is unabridged audiobook version of the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] young adult novel [[A New Hope: The Life of Luke Skywalker|of the same name]]. It was first released by [[Universo dos Livros]] in [[July 3]], [[2024]] as a [[Wikipedia:Portuguese language|Portuguese language]] audiobook narrated by [[Leonardo Raoni]].<ref name="Amazon PT" />

An [[Wikipedia:English language|English language]] edition of this audiobook was not been released.

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''A new hope. A new hero. A new Jedi.''

''During his childhood, Luke Skywalker lived on a simple farm on Tatooine and considered himself an orphan. Dreaming of the day when he would find his place in a wider world, he sees his friends leave in search of a better life. As his origin and fate have always been uncertain, he enlists the help of Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi&mdash;for him a mysterious hermit&mdash;to finally discover the truth about his father, his twin sister, and his future.''

''After joining the Rebels fighting the Empire, Luke finally finds the way to overcome his greatest challenge: facing the powerful Darth Vader. And by encountering the dark side of the Force, he would be able to bring new hope to a galaxy far, far away...''<ref name="Amazon PT" />
