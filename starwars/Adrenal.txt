'''Adrenal''' was a liquid stimulant utilized by the [[Rodian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Skora]] that could boost her natural speed during the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition hosted within [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. This stimulant was [[Color|yellow]] in coloration, stored in cylindrical vials, and had to be ingested for its effects to kick in.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Substance-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Adrenal were first mentioned in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' via Skora's "Adrenal Boost" Active Ability.<ref name="Hunters" /> In [[Star Wars Legends]], [[Adrenal/Legends|adrenals]] were drugs used to heighten reflexes and aid in concentration.<ref name="KOTOR">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''</ref>
