The '''EV-series''',<ref name="Droidography" /> also known as the '''EV-class''',<ref name="Free Fall">''[[Poe Dameron: Free Fall]]''</ref> was a series of [[droid]]s<ref name="Droidography" /> manufactured by [[MerenData]]<ref name="Updated and Expanded" /> that included the [[EV-series medical droid]]<ref name="EV-AD-4" /> and [[EV-series supervisor droid]].<ref name="Droidography" /> Many EV units had a programming defect that caused them to exhibit cruel behavior, forcing MerenData to issue a mass recall, from which few escaped.<ref name="Updated and Expanded" /> Some surviving EV-series [[droid]]s were repurposed as [[Interrogation droid|interrogator droids]] by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="The Citadel" /> and [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Vader" />
