'''Corellian rum''' was a type of [[rum]] that was [[Diet|consumable]] by [[human]]s and shared its name with the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. In [[4 ABY]], the [[smuggler]] [[Owerto Naiucho]] claimed he had acquired several facial scars by falling on a camping stove while drunk on Corellian rum. The [[5 ABY|following year]], while on the planet [[Chandrila]], the former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[loyalty officer]] [[Sinjir Rath Velus]] considered asking the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] leader [[Han Solo]] if he had a bottle of Corellian rum in his [[Han and Leia's apartment|apartment]] for him to drink.

==Description==
Corellian rum was a type of [[rum]] that was [[Diet|potable]] to [[human]]s.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> The rum had an intoxicating effect when consumed<ref name="Aftermath" /> and could be stored in bottles. Corellian rum shared its name with<ref name="Empire's End" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Corellia]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

==History==
At some point during his career, the [[smuggler]] [[Owerto Naiucho]] acquired several facial scars and began telling conflicting stories to others about how he had earned them.<ref name="Aftermath" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> Naiucho told the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[pilot]] [[Norra Wexley]] that he had gotten drunk on Corellian rum and fell on a [[Temperature|hot]] camping stove, which had scarred his face. She was skeptical of his tale, having heard several of his stories by this point.<ref name="Aftermath" />

While on the Core Worlds planet [[Chandrila]]<ref name="Empire's End" /> the [[5 ABY|following year]],<ref name="Date2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> the former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[loyalty officer]] [[Sinjir Rath Velus]] visited the [[Han and Leia's apartment|apartment]] of the rebel leaders [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Han Solo]] while the latter was home. As Velus and his companions discussed a plan to rescue two of their [[Norra Wexley's team|team members]] stranded on<ref name="Empire's End" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="USWNE" /> planet [[Jakku]], Velus considered asking Solo if he had any Corellian rum hidden in the apartment for him to drink. However, when Organa entered the room, he opted against it.<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corellian rum was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment of [[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy|''The Aftermath Trilogy'']].<ref name="Aftermath" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Corellian rum/Legends|Corellian rum]] was first introduced in the [[1994]] novel ''[[The Courtship of Princess Leia]]'' by [[Dave Wolverton]].<ref name="Courtship">''[[The Courtship of Princess Leia]]''</ref>
