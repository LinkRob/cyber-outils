The '''Badlands of N'g'zi''' were a region of the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]] located near the [[Im'g'twe Hills]]. During the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Republic Military|forces]] routed the [[Separatist Droid Army|Separatist army]] to the Badlands, leading to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists']] retreat.<ref name="GD">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''</ref>
