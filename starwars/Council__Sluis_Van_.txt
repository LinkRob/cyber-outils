The '''Sluissi Council''' was the ruling body of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Sluis Van/Legends|Sluis Van]]. By [[5 ABY/Legends|5 ABY]], it consisted of 100 members elected by universal suffrage and representing the five major parties of Sluis Van proportionally to their last electoral results. Those five parties were the [[Unitarian Party]], the [[Democratic Party]], the [[Union Party]], the [[Liberal Party]] and the [[Sluisvanian Movement]].
