The '''Battle on Quantxi''',<ref name="Timelines" /> also known as the '''Battle of Quantxi''',<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> took place during the [[High Republic Era]] on the junk [[moon]] [[Quantxi]] between members of the [[Jedi Order]] and their allies, and the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s. [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]], a [[Force-sensitive]] girl taken in by the [[Jedi]], had come to the moon in search of her former friend [[Krix Kamerat]], who had been taken by the pirates during their [[attack on Trymant IV]]. When [[Padawan]]s [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]], [[Farzala Tarabal]] and [[Qort]] followed Mrala to Quantxi, they found the Nihil attempting to massacre the local [[Junk Mavens]], and Tarabal and Qort stopped to help them while Talisola followed Mrala. [[Jedi Master]]s [[Yoda]], [[Torban Buck]] and [[Kantam Sy]], who had just fought a [[Battle in the Bright Jewel system|space battle]] against more Nihil forces, followed the Padawans to the moon and assisted in the fight, although Yoda disappeared on another mission partway through. With the pirates antagonizing the Junk Mavens defeated by the Mavens, the Padawans, and a [[Mantellian Savrip]] allied with them, Talisola, Mrala, Sy and Buck attempted to prevent Kamerat from escaping with [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]]. However, the [[Spider Cruiser]] ''[[Squall Spider]]'' was able to assist in the pirates' escape.<ref name="HRA 5" />
