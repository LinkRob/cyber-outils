'''D6-L''', or '''Dee-six''', was an [[R-series]] [[astromech droid]] that served the [[New Republic]]. [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] defector [[Yrica Quell]] chose the [[droid]] for her [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]] in [[Alphabet Squadron]]. After the failed [[Mission to Abednedo]], D6-L offered to resign, blaming itself when Quell's starfighter failed to perform an impossible maneuver to protect a freighter they were escorting with Imperial prisoners onboard. D6-L was destroyed during Alphabet Squadron's [[Battle of Pandem Nai|attack]] on [[Pandem Nai]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
