An '''Elphronan firestork''' was a tall creature that<ref name="Masters">{{StoryCite|story=Masters|book=Stories of Jedi and Sith}}</ref> shared its name with the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Elphrona]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> As the [[Jedi]]-in-[[Jedi training|training]] [[Luke Skywalker]] was in the [[Yoda's Hut|hut]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]]<ref name="Masters" /> on the Outer Rim planet [[Dagobah]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[Masters]]" take place concurrently with the events of {{Film|VI}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref> he crouched like an Elphronan firestork in a [[Thief|stolen]] nest.<ref name="Masters" />
