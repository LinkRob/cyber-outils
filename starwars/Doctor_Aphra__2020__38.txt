'''''Doctor Aphra'' 38''' is the thirty-eighth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 15]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''A TENSE RESCUE! &ndash; A ''DARK DROIDS'' TIE-IN! MAGNA TOLVAN has fallen into the hands of the SCOURGE! In order to save her, DOCTOR APHRA turns to SANA STARROS&hellip;to save the woman they BOTH love!''
