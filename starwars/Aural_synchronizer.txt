An '''aural synchronizer''' was a portable [[Electronic translator/Legends|electronic translator]]. It could translate from [[Galactic Basic Standard/Legends|Galactic Basic Standard]] to [[Durese/Legends|Durese]].<ref name="MAGL" />

[[Etro Edthatt|Etro]] and [[Droza Edthatt]] used Aural synchronizers when they were abducted to [[Earth/Legends|Urthha]].<ref name="MAGL" />

==Behind the scenes==
The aural synchronizer was mentioned in a [[Wikipedia:Supermarket tabloid|supermarket tabloid]] parody attributed to [[Trebor Uarrac]] of the ''[[Galactic Gossip]]'' in ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a book published in [[1993]] that combined photos and concept art from the [[Original trilogy]] and other [[Lucasfilm]] productions with a variety of short fiction pieces. In the story, two Duros newlyweds named Etro and Droza Edthatt are "[[Wikipedia:Alien abduction|abducted]] by Human beings" from the planet "Urthha". After over four solar periods of bizarre experiences, they somehow use a "matter catalyst", which the Humans call a "[[Wikipedia:Blender (device)|blender]]," to leave the planet.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]].<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
