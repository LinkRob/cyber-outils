{{Quote|That's the ''Fireball''? That thing looks like an explosion with wings.|Kazuda Xiono, assessing the ''Fireball''|<ref name="The Recruit" />}}
The '''''Fireball''''' was an old [[Racing starfighter|racer]] that [[Team Fireball|Team ''Fireball'']] tried to restore in their [[Jarek Yeager's Repair Station|repair shop]] on the ''[[Colossus]]'', a refueling station on the [[planet]] [[Castilon]]. It was given its name because of its tendency to burst into [[Fire|flames]] and/or explode.<ref name="The Recruit" />

==Description==
The racer was equipped with armament in the form of wingtip-mounted [[laser cannon]]s and a pair of [[missile launcher]]s.<ref name="Bibo" /> At one point, [[Tamara Ryvora]] was working on installing a cannon on it.<ref name="Hunt">{{Resistance|Hunt on Celsor 3}}</ref>

At one point, the team painted a [[gorg]] on the starboard side.<ref name="Hunt" />

Rumor had it that [[Jarek Yeager]] cobbled it together from an old [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]] and a [[Z-75 Headhunter]] that he inherited from family.<ref name="USWNE" />

==History==
===Xiono's race===
[[File:KazudaXiona-SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|Xiono piloted the ''Fireball'' against Torra Doza's ''Blue Ace''.]]
At the time of [[Kazuda Xiono]]'s arrival at the station as part of a [[Mission on the Colossus platform|spy mission]], it had been promised to Ryvora, if she could get it to fly. Yeager offered it to Xiono in order to compete in a [[Race between Kazuda Xiono and Torra Doza|race]] against [[Torra Doza]]. Yeager's team was able to repair it in time, but Xiono pushed it too fast during the race, causing the engines to catch flame and crash into the ocean. It was later recovered, along with Xiono.<ref name="The Recruit" />

===The broken compensator===
Some time later, Team ''Fireball'' was repairing the [[acceleration compensator]]. Xiono accidentally broke the repair job, causing Ryvora to become angry and demand that he repair it. After becoming involved in the hunt for two children on the station, his new friends the [[Chelidae]] [[engineer]]s helped him repair the compensator, which Xiono presented to Ryvora.<ref name="The Children from Tehar">{{Resistance|The Children from Tehar}}</ref>

===Rendezvous with Poe===
The ''Fireball'' was next flown when Xiono needed to go to an offworld rendezvous with the [[Resistance]]. Yeager arranged for Xiono and [[BB-8]] to leave the ''Colossus'' early enough that Ryvora, who was possessive of the ship, would only learn of their departure after they were gone. However, unbeknownst to Yeager and Xiono, Ryvora had decided to replace the ship's [[stabilizer]]s, and had not yet completed the job. When she revealed this fact to Yeager after discovering Xiono had left, Yeager was concerned. Above Castilon, Xiono and BB-8 ran into mechanical trouble due to the missing stabilizer, and Xiono thought that the ship was going to explode before several Resistance ships arrived just in time. [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]], shocked that Xiono was flying such an old ship, promised to have mechanics look at it.<ref name="Station Theta Black" /> [[File:R1J5-Bucket-Fireball-SWZ.png|thumb|right|250px|The ''Fireball'' comes in for a landing with engines smoking.]]

===The Bibo incident===
Some time later, it was ready for launch. During the [[Bibo incident]], Kaz jumped into the cockpit and launched to assist the [[Ace Squadron|Aces]] in fighting off a [[rokkna]] which was assaulting the station. Kaz used it to strafe the sea monster, and at one point gave a wingtip assist to [[Griff Halloran]]. Eventually, Team ''Fireball'' realized that the rokkna was there to find her baby, which Neeku Vozo had adopted and named "[[Bibo]]." The mechanic returned the baby, defusing the situation.<ref name="Bibo" />

===Torra's kidnapping===
When Torra Doza was [[Torra Doza's abduction|kidnapped]] by the [[Warbird gang]], Kaz and BB-8 sortied in the ''Fireball'' in pursuit. While chasing the [[pirate]]s' skiff, the ''Fireball'' received damage to its transponder, preventing Kaz from relaying Torra's coordinates immediately. By the time they caught up to [[Kragan Gorr]]'s [[sail barge]], the ''[[Galleon]]'', the [[First Order]], led by [[Major]] [[Elrik Vonreg]], faked a rescue of Torra. During the chaos, BB-8 managed to fix the transponder, enabling them to alert [[Captain]] [[Imanuel Doza]] and Ace Squadron to their location, but by then, the First Order had already made off with Torra. The ''Fireball'' then returned to the ''Colossus''.<ref name="Doza Dilemma">{{Resistance|The Doza Dilemma}}</ref>

===Mission to the Dassal system===
[[File:ColossusStationMechanic-SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|The ''Fireball'' takes off from Yeager's repair bay.]]
When Poe infiltrated the ''Colossus'' to pickup BB-8 for their [[Attack on Tuanul|mission]] to [[Jakku]], Kaz informed him of First Order activity in the [[Dassal system]], forcing them to take a detour. To get them off the ''Colossus'' undetected, they used the ''Fireball'' to perform a ''[[Widowmaker (maneuver)|Widowmaker]]'', enabling them to reach Poe's X-Wing with [[CB-23]] onboard. Upon arriving in the Dassal system, they discover its [[Dassal system's sun|star]] had vanished and its planetoids cored out. As they traveled through one of the planetoids, they got caught in a [[gravity well]], which they managed to survive.<ref name="The Core Problem">{{Resistance|The Core Problem}}</ref>

They soon landed on a habitable [[moon]] and determined that its inhabitants were massacred, shortly before they were attacked by a probe droid. After disabling the droid, they determined that it was First Order as some [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] flew overhead. Kaz and Poe managed to make it back to their ships and fled their pursuers. At Poe's direction, Kaz piloted the ''Fireball'' back through the gravity well, knowing the First Order wouldn't be able to quickly adapt as they did. Once they were out of danger, Kaz and Poe did a droid swap before parting ways. With CB-23's aid, Kaz was able to land the ''Fireball'' back on the ''Colossus'' without being detected.<ref name="The Core Problem" />

===War with the First Order===
Kaz took the ''Fireball'' out into battle to escort the ''Colossus'' as it [[Battle of Castilon|fled]] the planet and [[Occupation of the Colossus|First Order occupation]]. He flew around to shoot down Major Vonreg.<ref name="No Escape 2" />

Shortly afterward, as the station launched into [[hyperspace]], Xiono and Yeager were assigned by Captain Doza to Ace Squadron.<ref name="Live Fire" />

===Re-supplying===
With the ''Colossus'' critically low on supplies, the crew was forced to [[Mission to Celsor 3|stop]] at [[Celsor 3]]. The initial attempt to harvest the [[jakoosk]] by the [[Warbird gang]] failed; Xiono and Torra Doza took their ships down to the moon themselves. The ''Fireball'' and ''[[Blue Ace]]'' were stolen by surviving pirates, however the two pilots were able to kill the creature and return to the station.<ref name="Hunt" />

Later, while at a rendezvous point, they were ambushed by the ''[[Thunderer]]''. The Aces fought off First Order TIEs. One almost shot down the ''Fireball'', but Torra Doza was able to destroy it. They then escaped.<ref name="Rendezvous Point">{{Resistance|Rendezvous Point (episode)}}</ref>

===Battle of Exegol===
{{Quote|Fireball'', here. Hi, everyone!''|italics=0|Kazuda Xiono calling in at the Battle of Exegol|<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>}}
In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="TROS Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] to one [[Standard year|year]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], the events of ''The Rise of Skywalker'' must occur in [[35 ABY]].</ref> the ''Fireball''&mdash;along with [[Jarek Yeager's racer]], the ''Blue Ace'',<ref name="Bucket List The Escape Part 2">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-escape-part-2-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 5 Fun Facts from "The Escape - Part 2" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref> the ''[[Green Ace]]'', the ''[[Yellow Ace]]'', and the ''[[Red Ace]]''<ref name="TROS Graphic Novel">[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker Graphic Novel Adaptation|''Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker'' Graphic Novel Adaptation]]</ref>&mdash;were among a [[Citizens' Fleet|fleet]] of 14,000 starships<ref name="Bucket List The Escape Part 2" /> assembled by [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Chewbacca]] to help assist the Resistance during the [[Battle of Exegol|battle]] against the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]] over the [[planet]] [[Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX" /> Kaz flew the ''Fireball'' during the battle and reported in at its start.<ref name="TROS EE" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Fireball-CGSWG.png|thumb|300px|The ''Fireball'' from ''Star Wars Resistance''.]]
The ''Fireball'' is [[Kazuda Xiono]]'s craft in the [[2018]] animated series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''. It was designed by veteran ''Star Wars'' modelmaker [[Bill George]],<ref name="Slash film">{{WebCite|url=https://www.slashfilm.com/bill-george-star-wars-resistance|text=Exclusive: ILM Legend Bill George Returns to 'Star Wars' With 'Resistance'|author=Young, Bryan|date=2018-10-05|work=[[Wikipedia:/Film|Slashfilm]]|archivedate=20200310203137}}</ref> developed as a cross between a [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]] and a [[Wikipedia:Vought F4U Corsair|Chance-Vought F4U Corsair]], an American naval fighter from World War II.<ref name="Aces">{{WebCite|url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/tv/2018/09/14/star-wars-ace-pilots-not-jedi-take-center-stage-animated-resistance-series/1295303002|text='Star Wars': Ace pilots, not Jedi, take center stage in animated 'Resistance' series|author=Brian Truitt|date=2018-09-14|archiveurl=https://www.webcitation.org/72R8wffIB}}</ref>

A full-scale model of the ship was built for the 2018 [[Wikipedia:New York Comic Con|New York Comic Con]].<ref name="NYCC 2018">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2018-the-fireball-lands-at-the-javits-center|text=NYCC 2018: The ''Fireball'' Lands at the Javits Center}}</ref>

The ship also appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], as confirmed by [[Neal Scanlan]] in an interview with Yahoo.<ref name="TROS">{{WebCite|url=https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/star-wars-the-rise-of-skywalker-visual-effects-secrets-revealed-200000081.html|text=Billie Lourd played Leia Organa and other visual effects secrets from 'Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker'|author=Alter, Ethan|date=2020-01-07|work=Yahoo Entertainment|archivedate=20200318224824}}</ref> The ''Fireball'' was among the ships modeled by [[Industrial Light & Magic|ILM]] and included the end battle of film.<ref name="Bucket List The Escape Part 2" />
