'''Cell five-two-five-seven-seven''' was a [[Prison|cell]] in the detention level of the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Tarkin's Will]]''.<ref name="Star Wars 23" /> After she was captured and interrogated by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Commander]] [[Ellian Zahra]],<ref name="Star Wars 22">[[Star Wars (2020) 22|''Star Wars'' (2020) 22]]</ref> [[rebel pilot]] [[Shara Bey]] was held [[prisoner]] in the cell until she was rescued by her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Sergeant]] [[Kes Dameron]] during the [[Battle of Panisia]].<ref name="Star Wars 23" />
