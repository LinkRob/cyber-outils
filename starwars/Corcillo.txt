'''Corcillo''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Human]] who served as an [[sergeant]] in [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="A Perilous Rescue" />

==Biography==
Corcillo was a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] prior to the [[Battle of Endor]]. After [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]] convinced the [[Alliance High Command]] to [[Assault near Alzoc III|raid]] an [[Imperial Navy|Imperial fleet]] near [[Alzoc III]], [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]] ordered Corcillo to hail [[Lieutenant]] [[Colvett Valeria|Valeria]]. Ackbar then tasked Valeria's [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]] and [[Zander Freemaker]] with raiding the Imperial fleet.<ref name="A Perilous Rescue" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corcillo first appeared in the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' [[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures Season Two|Season Two]] episode "[[A Perilous Rescue]]," which premiered on [[Disney XD]] on [[August 10]], [[2017]]. He is voiced by [[James Urbaniak]] who also voiced [[M-OC/LEGO|M-OC]].<ref name="A Perilous Rescue" />
