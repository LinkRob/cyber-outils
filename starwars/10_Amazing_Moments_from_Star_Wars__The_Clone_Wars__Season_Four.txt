"'''10 Amazing Moments from ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'': Season Four'''" is a magazine article in [[Star Wars Insider 134|''Star Wars Insider'' 134]]. It recaps moments from ''[[The Clone Wars: Season Four]]'':

*[[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO's]] adventure on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Patitite Pattuna/Legends|Patitite Pattuna]] from the episode "[[Nomad Droids]]"
*The return of [[Death Watch/Legends|Death Watch]] in "[[A Friend in Need]]"
*The [[Nightsisters/Legends|Nightsisters]]' [[Army of the Dead/Legends|Army of the Dead]] in "[[Massacre]]"
*[[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Pong Krell/Legends|Pong Krell]] showing his true face in "[[Carnage of Krell]]"
*[[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] fighting the [[Blixus/Legends|blixus]] creature in "[[Kidnapped]]"
*The first appearance of [[C-21 Highsinger/Legends|C-21 Highsinger]] in "[[Bounty (episode)|Bounty]]"
*The [[Impeding Assault Tank/Legends|Impeding Assault Tank]] in "[[The General (episode)|The General]]"
*[[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Rex/Legends|Rex]] killing [[Keeper/Legends|Keeper]] [[Agruss/Legends|Agruss]] in "[[Escape from Kadavo]]"
*[[Savage Opress/Legends|Savage Opress]] finding his brother [[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]] on the planet of [[Lotho Minor/Legends|Lotho Minor]] in "[[Brothers (episode)|Brothers]]"
*Darth Maul and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi's]] confrontation in "[[Revenge (episode)|Revenge]]"
