'''Andrew Jack''' ([[January 28]], [[1940s#1944|1944]]<ref name="This Week! Ep1" /> &ndash; [[March 31]], [[2020]])<ref name="death" /> was an [[Wikipedia:Dialect coach|dialect coach]] who worked on more than 80 films since the 1980s. He worked on the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] ([[2015]]), where he provided dialogue and accent coaching for actors [[Daisy Ridley]] and [[John Boyega]], as well as several supporting actors with alien languages.<ref name="Official">{{WebCite|url=http://andrewjack.com/career|text=Career|work=Andrew Jack's [http://andrewjack.com official website]|archivedate=20251212221727}}</ref> He also had a brief acting role in the film as [[Caluan Ematt]].<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.blastr.com/2015-12-22/what-do-all-celeb-cameos-force-awakens-have-common|text=What do all the celeb cameos in The Force Awakens have in common?|author=Denny Watkins|date=2015-12-22|work=[[Wikipedia:Syfy#Syfy Wire|Blastr]]|archivedate=20151222215811}}</ref> He has worked with such actors as [[Wikipedia:Robert Downey Jr.|Robert Downey Jr.]] in the film [[Wikipedia:Chaplin (film)|''Chaplin'']] ([[1992]]), and [[Wikipedia:Pierce Brosnan|Pierce Brosnan]] in [[Wikipedia:GoldenEye|''GoldenEye'']] (1995) and other [[Wikipedia:James Bond in film|James Bond films]]. Jack also created the [[Wikipedia:Middle-earth|Middle-earth]] accents for the [[Wikipedia:The Lord of the Rings (film series)|''Lord of the Rings'' film trilogy]] and taught them to the cast, and designed and taught the accents for the [[Wikipedia:Greeks|Greeks]] and [[Wikipedia:Trojan|Trojans]] for the film [[Wikipedia:Troy (film)|''Troy'']] ([[2004]]).<ref name="Official" /> On the the 31st of March 2020, Andrew Jack passed away from [[Wikipedia:Coronavirus disease 2019|COVID-19]].<ref name="death" />
