'''''Blue Milk: At Markets Everywhere''''' was a [[Foodstuff|food]] advertisement found at [[Nixus Hub 218]]<ref name="Lasat" /> and a [[hangar]] bay on [[Garel]].<ref name="Blood">{{Rebels|Blood Sisters}}</ref> It was written in [[Aurebesh]] and featured a bottle of "[[Blue milk|BLUE MILK]]" accompanied by the slogan "AT MARKETS EVERYWHERE". The ad showed the [[milk]] splashing against a [[Color|yellow]] background.<ref name="Lasat" />
