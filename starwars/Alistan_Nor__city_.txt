'''Alistan Nor''' was a city that had been built in ancient times by the [[Force Builders]], an offshoot of the [[Jedi]] who were devoted to creating fine style, architecture, and [[starship]]s. By the time leading up to the [[Battle of Endor]], the city had long been abandoned and overtaken by the jungle. During the [[Quest to build the Arrowhead|Quest]] to build the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']], [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] visited this city and was [[Mission to Alistan Nor|ambushed]] by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Hunter droid/LEGO|hunter droid]] [[M-OC/LEGO|M-OC]].<ref name="Tower" />
