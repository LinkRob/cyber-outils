'''Culroon III''',<ref name="SYWtBaJ" /> also known as '''Culroon''',<ref name="GEG">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref> was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="SYWtBaJ" /> located the [[Culroon system]] of [[Wild Space]], situated in grid square P-20 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was inhabited by [[Culroonian]]s.<ref name="SYWtBaJ" />

Shortly before the [[Battle of Hoth]]<ref name="Ships">{{ShipsandVehiclesCite|5|Pilots and Crew Members}}</ref> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> the Culroonians [[Culroon III campaign|fought]] the Empire<ref name="SYWtBaJ" /> in a campaign<ref name="GEG">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref> on Culroon III<ref name="SYWtBaJ" /> after a revolt there.<ref name="Helmet">{{HelmetCollectionCite|65|Databank A-Z}}</ref> The [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Maximilian Veers]] fought on Culroon III as an Imperial combatant,<ref name="SYWtBaJ" /> winning the campaign for the Empire.<ref name="Helmet" /> The courage, cunning,<ref name="SYWtBaJ" /> and leadership<ref name="Ships" /> Veers demonstrated on Culroon III led to his promotion to the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[general]] in the [[Imperial Army]],<ref name="SYWtBaJ" /> and assignment to the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[Death Squadron]].<ref name="Helmet" /> Culroon III was mentioned in a profile on Veers in the ''[[Galactic Explorer's Guide]]'' [[author]]ed by the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] and his [[RA-7 protocol droid|protocol droid]] [[DK-RA-43]] after the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]]<ref name="GEG" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Atlas" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Want_to_be_a_Jedi_Cover.jpg|thumb|right|180px|Culroon III was introduced in '' So You Want to Be a Jedi?'' in the current canon.]]
Culroon III was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Adam Gidwitz]]'s [[2015]] children's book ''[[The Empire Strikes Back: So You Want to Be a Jedi?]]'', a retelling of the<ref name="SYWtBaJ" /> [[1980]]<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="SYWtBaJ" /> It was identified as Culroon in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]'' by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="GEG" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity [[Culroon III/Legends|Culroon III]] was a [[Planet/Legends|planet]] that first appeared in the short story "[[The Promotion of Lieutenant Veers]]," published in ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'' in [[1987]]. In the story, Culroon III was the site of an [[Insurrection on Culroon III|ambush]] of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces committed by the native [[Culroon]], which resulted in [[Maximilian Veers/Legends|Maximilian Veers]]' promotion from [[Lieutenant/Legends|lieutenant]] to [[Major/Legends|major]].<ref name="Promotion">{{WEGCite|story=The Promotion of Lieutenant Veers|book=The Star Wars Sourcebook}}</ref>
