"'''Amara Kel's Rules for TIE Pilot Survival'''" is a short story that was published in the [[2020]] anthology book ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''. It was written by [[Django Wexler]] and focuses on the [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]] [[Amara Kel]].
