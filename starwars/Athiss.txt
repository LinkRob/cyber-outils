'''Athiss''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="TRS" /> located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> and the [[New Territories]]'<ref name="NewTerritories" /> [[Athiss system]], which was in the [[Sith Worlds]] portion of the [[Esstran sector]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was situated in grid square R-4 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> Following the [[Battle of Kur]]<ref name="TRS" /> in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[Reekrider Cartel]] sent a shipment of the recreational [[reedug]] drug to Athiss, though it was seized before its arrival by a [[Nihil]] [[Strike]] of [[Tempest Runner]] [[Pan Eyta]]'s [[Pan Eyta's Tempest|Tempest]].<ref name="TRS" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> Tempest Runner [[Lourna Dee]] thought about Eyta's Tempest's seizure of the Reekrider Cartel's shipment to the celestial body.<ref name="TRS" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Athiss was first mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="TRS" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Athiss/Legends|Athiss]] was first mentioned in the ''[[Tales of the Jedi Companion]]'', a [[1996]] [[West End Games]] sourcebook authored by [[George R. Strayton]] for use with ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Tales of the Jedi Companion">''[[Tales of the Jedi Companion]]''</ref> The [[Planet/Legends|planet]] subsequently made its first full appearance in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', a [[2011]] [[BioWare]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]].<ref name="TOR">{{TORcite|type=flashpoint|Athiss}}</ref>
