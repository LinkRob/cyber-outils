'''Dinni Dusko''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] resident of [[Theed/Legends|Theed]] who lived during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="AED" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dialogue pertaining to Dinni Dusko appeared in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online roleplaying game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']]. However, the quest he was mentioned in never actually appeared on the [[Star Wars video games|video game]]'s live servers.<ref name="AED" />
