'''Arbooine''' was an [[Outer Rim Territories]] celestial body located in the [[Arboo sector]]. It was [[Homeworld|home]] to the [[Bearsloth venom|venomous]] [[Tree|arboreal]] [[forest]] [[Diet|predator]] known as the [[bearsloth]], which posed a danger to any visitors to Arbooine. Nevertheless, the creatures and their [[Poison|venom]] led to several individuals traveling to the celestial body.

==Description==
Arbooine was a terrestrial celestial body<ref name="AA" /> located in the [[Arbooine system]], a part of the [[Arboo sector]] in the [[freestanding subsectors]] portion of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> The [[bearsloth]], a large, [[Bearsloth venom|venomous]] [[Tree|arboreal]] [[forest]] ambush [[Diet|predator]], was one of Arbooine's most dangerous carnivorous creatures. Bearsloths posed a constant hazard to anyone visiting Arbooine, and the local [[Sathari]] [[Hunting|hunters]] used their [[Poison|venom]] in their bladed [[diiro]] [[weapon]]s.<ref name="AA" />

==History==
{{RPGseed}}
[[File:Bearsloth-CotG.png|thumb|right|250px|Arbooine was the homeworld of the bearsloth.]]
By the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]], a local [[Unidentified arms dealer|arms dealer]] at some point [[Merchant|traded]] in Arbooine's bearsloth venom and recruited a group of individuals to retrieve some to sell to an eager client. In another instance, a [[Unidentified crime lord (bearsloths)|crime lord]] captured several bearsloths and placed them in the forests surrounding their [[Unidentified manor|manor]], where they acted as both guards and executioners.<ref name="AA" />

At another point, the individual known as [[Savas|Savas the Dreaded]] sold [[glitterstim]] [[spice]] on Arbooine and in turn obtained an amount of bearsloth venom that he sold to the [[Hutt]] [[Mahon's criminal empire|crime lord]] [[Mahon]]. A group of [[bounty hunter]]s subsequently learned of Savas' actions on Arbooine and, in order to track him down, attempted to find a way to travel to the celestial body.<ref name="ND">[[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']]</ref>
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Arbooine was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']], a [[2017]] sourcebook published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for use with the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] system. In that book<ref name="ND" /> and in the [[2019]] Fantasy Flight Games' [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] sourcebook ''[[Allies and Adversaries]]'',<ref name="AA" /> the celestial body is respectively mentioned in a pair of adventure seeds<ref name="ND" /> and a hypothetical gameplay example. This article assumes all three of those scenarios play out as described.<ref name="AA" />
