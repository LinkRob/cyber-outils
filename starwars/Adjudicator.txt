The '''''Adjudicator''''' was an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] that served in the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Captain]] [[Kronn]], it was positioned in [[orbit]] over [[Cymoon 1]] along with two other [[Star Destroyer]]s. After the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[Assault on Cymoon|destroyed]] the [[moon]]'s [[Weapons Factory Alpha]] facility, the ''Adjudicator''{{'s}} [[blockade]] failed to prevent the rebels from fleeing the moon in the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', causing the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to [[Force choke]] Kronn.

==Description==
An [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]], the ''Adjudicator''<ref name="SW3" /> measured 1,600 [[meter]]s long.<ref name="Length" /> Additionally, the [[Star Destroyer]] was equipped with [[sublight engine]]s, a [[hangar]] bay, communications systems,<ref name="SW3" /> and two [[deflector shield generator dome]]s.<ref name="Card Trader" />

==History==
{{Quote|Chewie, we're out of time! Those ''Star Destroyers'' are about to blast us out of the sky!|Princess Leia, warning of the ''Millennium Falcon'' approaching the ''Adjudicator''{{'s}} blockade|<ref name="SW3" />}}
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the ''Adjudicator'' was part of the [[Imperial Navy]] and under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Captain]] [[Kronn]]. Shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Star Destroyer]] was stationed in [[orbit]] above the [[moon]] [[Cymoon 1]], which featured the [[Galactic Empire]]'s expansive [[Weapons Factory Alpha]].<ref name="SW3" /> In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[Weapons factory|factory]] was [[Assault on Cymoon|attacked]] and destroyed by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], which hoped to overthrow the Empire. After destroying the factory, the [[Heroes of Yavin|rebel group]], including [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], and [[Han Solo]], were pursued by the Imperial enforcer and [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="SW3" />

Fleeing in their [[YT-1300 light freighter|freighter]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', the rebels were intercepted over Cymoon 1 by a [[blockade]] formed by the ''Adjudicator'' and two other ''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers. However, the Star Destroyers were unable to stop the rebel [[Light freighter|ship]] from escaping into [[hyperspace]]. Kronn then contacted Vader on the moon below to report the failure, which led the [[Sith Lord]] to [[Force choke|telekinetically choke]] the [[Imperial officer]].<ref name="SW3" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Adjudicator'' appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Star Wars (2015) 3|''Star Wars'' 3]], released under [[Marvel Comics]]' [[2015]] [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'' series]]. The issue was written by [[Jason Aaron]], illustrated by [[John Cassaday]],<ref name="SW3" /> and published on [[March 11]], 2015.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/52652/star_wars_2015_3|text=Star Wars (2015) #3}}</ref>

[[File:Adjudicator_ISD.png|thumb|right|270px|The ''Adjudicator'', as it appeared in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity]]
In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the ''Adjudicator'', later renamed the [[Liberator (Imperial I-class)|''Liberator'']], was an [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer/Legends|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]].<ref name="Dark Empire Sourcebook">''[[Dark Empire Sourcebook]]''</ref> It was first mentioned in the [[Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse]] comic-book [[Dark Empire 1|''Dark Empire'' 1]], written by [[Tom Veitch]]<ref name="Dark Empire 1">[[Dark Empire 1|''Dark Empire'' 1]]</ref> and released on [[December 12]], [[1991]],<ref name="DE1 date">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54057/star_wars_dark_empire_1991_1|text=Star Wars: Dark Empire (1991) #1}}</ref> and first appeared in the [[1993]] vignette "[[Down And Out On Coruscant]]," which was written by [[Michael Allen Horne]] for the ''[[Dark Empire Sourcebook]]''.<ref name="Down And Out On Coruscant">{{WEGCite|story=Down And Out On Coruscant|book=Dark Empire Sourcebook}}</ref> The [[1998]]<ref name="Date">{{LucasArts|url=products/btm/default.htm|text=''Star Wars: Behind the Magic'' Product Spotlight}} {{C|"How to Buy"}}</ref> interactive [[Wikipedia:Personal computer|PC]] CD-ROM game ''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]'' identified the ''Adjudicator'' as one of the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer/Legends|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] in the [[original trilogy]] films {{Film|V}} and {{Film|VI}}. Thus, the [[1980]] film ''The Empire Strikes Back'' [[Retroactive continuity|retroactively]] became the first appearance of the ''Adjudicator'' in Legends.<ref name="BTM">''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]''</ref>
