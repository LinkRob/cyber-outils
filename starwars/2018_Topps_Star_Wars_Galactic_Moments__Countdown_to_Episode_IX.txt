'''2018 Topps ''Star Wars'' Galactic Moments: Countdown to Episode IX''' is a trading card set published by [[Topps]] starting [[December 20]], [[2018]]. Every week thereafter, until the release of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], Topps released 3 new cards in the set. It features cards from all films in the [[Star Wars saga|''Star Wars'' saga]].<ref name="Twitter" />
