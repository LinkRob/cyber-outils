'''Bletel''' was a [[Pyke]] [[gambler]] resided the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> During which they played [[Kessel Sabacc]] with a [[Sullustan]] named [[Massobia]] and a [[Trandoshan]] named [[Khossk]] at a [[Pyke Cardroom (Myrra)|cardroom]] in the [[Pyke Syndicate District (Myrra)|Pyke Syndicate district]] of [[Myrra]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of Akiva. Bletel was a Pyke with [[Color|blue]] skin and black [[eye]]s. While playing [[Sabacc]] at the cardroom they wore a teal color [[clothing|vest]] over a brown shirt along with brown [[glove]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> a [[human]] gambler named [[Kay Vess]] played at least one game of Kessel [[Sabacc]] against Bletel, Massobia and Khossk while at the cardroom. During which she won three hundred [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], and three [[Shift Token]]s for Kessel Sabacc: [[Free Draw]], [[Cook The Books]], and [[General Tariff]]. Her victory also increased the Pyke [[Syndicate]]'s opinion of her.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bletel appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They were one of several Ssabacc players that could be played against by [[Kay Vess]] in Kessel Sabacc, an optional activity within the game. However wining a game was required to unlock the three shift tokens which were also in game collectibles.<ref name="Outlaws" />
