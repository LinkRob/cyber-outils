'''Arno Xan''' was an [[Abednedo]] criminal who lived in the [[Anoat sector]] at the [[time]] of the [[Iron Blockade]]. Xan built a collection of stolen [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[armor]]s, which notably included a set of [[stormtrooper armor]].<ref name="Xan" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
Xan joined the crew of [[the Smuggler]], who dispatched the Abednedo on a variety of missions throughout the Anoat sector.<ref name="Xan" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
In the [[2015]] mobile video game ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', Arno Xan is one of the many crew members that can be sent on Crew Runs for helpful items.<ref name="Xan" />
