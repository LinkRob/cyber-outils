'''Blorp''' was a [[droid]] [[bounty hunter]] of the [[Haxion Brood]] criminal syndicate during the [[Imperial Era]]. At some point, he encountered the [[Stinger Mantis crew|crew]] of the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]'' [[luxury yacht]]. Sometime after the encounter, the ''Stinger Mantis''{{'}} [[pilot]] [[Greez Dritus]] told the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Merrin]], another member of the crew, about Blorp.<ref name="Battle Scars" /> Sometime between [[12 BBY]] and [[11 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{BattleScarsDate}}</ref> when the ''Stinger Mantis'' crew [[Infiltration of a Haxion Brood base|infiltrated]] one of the Haxion Brood's [[asteroid]] [[Haxion Brood base|bases]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], Merrin was reminded of Blorp and thought of the other members of the Brood as "Blorps."<ref name="Battle Scars" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blorp was created for the [[2023]] novel ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Battle Scars]]'', which was written by [[Sam Maggs]].<ref name="Battle Scars" />
