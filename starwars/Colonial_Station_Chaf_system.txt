The '''Colonial Station Chaf system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], which was situated in the [[Unknown Regions]], and was in the [[Standard Galactic Grid|grid square]] F-9.<ref name="GMA" /> An orbiting [[planet]] in the system, [[Colonial Station Chaf]], was a part of a [[Chiss]] [[communications triad]].<ref name="Greater good" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Colonial Station Chaf system was first mentioned in [[Canon]] in the "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix posted on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="GMA" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Jason">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map...|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the Colonial Station Chaf system was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', authored by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
