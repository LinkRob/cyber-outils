The '''21D2-AN excavation droid''', also referred to as the '''21D2-AN excavator unit''', was a boxy model of [[excavation droid]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] requisitioned two of the [[droid]]s for a [[Expedition to Diso|mission]] to the [[Expansion Region]] [[planet]] [[Diso]]. The count used the two excavator units to dig up the [[Temple of Diso]], which was buried beneath hundreds of tons of [[salt]] under a salt flat. The work was completed in a matter of [[Standard hour|hours]], but the temple heavily damaged the two 21D2-AN excavation droids when it reacted to its exposure, as the structure was [[vergence]] in the [[Dark side of the Force]].

The two droids were heavily damaged but still mobile enough to be moved away from the temple by [[B1-series battle droid]]s. Their parts were later collected by the local [[shaman]] [[Ensoor-Koda-Za]] and one of the excavator droid's claws was converted into an altar in his [[Shaman's cave|cave]].

==Description==
The 21D2-AN excavation droid, or 21D2-AN excavator unit, was a model of huge and boxy [[excavation droid]] with massive scoop-claws. It was slow, but capable of moving hundreds of tons within a few [[Standard hour|hours]].<ref name="MOE" />

==History==
===Salt shovelers===
{{Quote|You have the coordinates. Commence excavation.''"<br />"''Roger, roger!|Dooku gives orders to a battle droid commander|<ref name="MOE" />}}
[[File:Dooku-ChromeBlack2022.png|thumb|right|250px|alt=Count Dooku stands upright|Count Dooku requisitioned two 21D2-AN excavation droids for a mission to Diso.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] ordered his [[Sith apprentice]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]], leader of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], to retrieve an artifact from the [[Temple of Diso]] on the [[Expansion Region]] [[planet]] [[Diso]]. As Sidious informed Dooku that he would need to dig during the mission, the count requisitions two 21D2-AN excavation droids and transported them to a [[salt]] flat outside a [[shaman's cave]] on Diso, which he had learned about from the locals.<ref name="MOE" />

Once the [[shaman]], [[Ensoor-Koda-Za]], revealed under [[Torture|duress]] that the temple was beneath the salt flat, Dooku ordered the excavation to begin, and the two [[droid]]s emerged from the transport at a slow pace that Dooku found infuriating. Once [[B1-series battle droid]]s had marked out the dig positions on the salt, the 21D2-ANs unfolded their claws and began digging. The droids uncovered the temple in a matter of hours, excavating hundreds of tons of salt and piling it mounds around the dig site and raising huge, jagged slabs to reveal the stepped pyramid structure underneath. Based on the results, Dooku was impressed by the excavators' capability and speed but when he realized that the artifact that he was to recover was the temple itself he decided the excavators lacked the meticulous attention to detail that would be required to dismantle and transport the entire temple for Sidious.<ref name="MOE" />

===A building fights back===
{{Quote|Move your squad clear. And try to get the excavator droids moved.|Dooku gives orders to a battle droid commander|<ref name="MOE" />}}
[[File:Battle_Droids_Geonosis.png|thumb|left|300px|B1-series battle droids fight alongside a B2-series supper battle droid in the dust of Geonosis.|Dooku ordered his B1-series battle droids to clear the damaged excavator droids away from the temple on Diso.]]
Its exposure having awoken something in the temple, which was a [[vergence]] in the [[Dark side of the Force]], the structure began to react and one of the 21D2-AN excavation droids was lost when the north side of the temple collapsed. When the south side began to buckle, the remaining excavator tried to use its scoop-claws to stabilize the structure. The droid was then caught in an avalanche when the temple's outer wall began to slide and the heavy metal of the machine was wrenched apart, producing an earsplitting groan. Dooku managed to quickly calm the vergence, leaving the shattered remains of the two excavator droids scattered around the salt flat.<ref name="MOE" />

Despite their heavy damage, the two 21D2-AN excavation droids remained mobile and the [[battle droid]]s accompanying Dooku were able to shift them off the salt flat when the [[Sith]] ordered the area around the temple cleared. The temple was then extracted using a combination of Dooku's [[The Force|Force powers]] and the [[tractor beam]] of an [[Unidentified experimental Techno Union transporter|experimental transporter vessel]] produced by the [[Techno Union]]. Once the structure was safely in the transporter's hold, Dooku's battle droids began a rudimentary clean up around the excavation site, but the count was tempted to order them to only take what could be salvaged and leave the rest, as he doubted anyone else would ever visit the now empty site.<ref name="MOE" />

===Parts repurposed===
The Separatists did leave behind droid parts, including both pieces of B1-series battle droids and 21D2-AN excavation droids. Ensoor-Koda-Za gathered up these pieces and used them to create a shrine in his cave, using a fractured fragment of one of the excavator droid's digging claws as an altar. When the Sith Lord [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and a [[squad]] of [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guards]], all also in service to Sidious, arrived at the cave<ref name="MOE" /> in [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Date">The events of ''Master of Evil'' begin during the events of [[Darth Vader (2017) 5|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 5]] and then spans to before the events of [[Darth Vader (2017) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 6]], which corresponds to [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> in search of the shaman, the Royal Guard [[commander]], [[Colonel]] [[Halland Goth]], recognized the altar as a droid part, initially having thought it was [[stone]].<ref name="MOE" />

When Vader questioned Ensoor-Koda-Za about the source of his power, the droid parts around the cave, most of which were mounted on straw panels, began to shudder and then launch themselves at Vader. Despite being struck by many of them, Vader used the Force to choke Ensoor-Koda-Za, causing the remaining droid parts to freeze in mid-air. A straw effigy on the floor of the cave then caught alight, with the [[Fire|flames]] quickly spreading to the rest of the cave and forcing the guards to flee as Vader and the shaman were engulfed, although both escaped alive.<ref name="MOE" />
