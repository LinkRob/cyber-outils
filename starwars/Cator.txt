'''Cator''' was a [[GC-8]] [[droid]] who served as [[Security droid|guardian-protector]] of a [[Jedi Archive vault|secret vault]] in the [[Jedi Archives]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]]. After the [[Great Jedi Purge|fall]] of the [[Jedi Order]], the droid continued to keep watch and was present when [[Jocasta Nu]] returned to collect one of the items in the vault. Shortly afterward, Cator was destroyed by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] during his hunt for Nu.

==Biography==
{{Quote|All remains safe. I know my duty. Even during the battle in the Temple&hellip; I stayed here, guarding these treasures.|Cator, to Jocasta Nu|<ref name="Dying Light II" />}}
Cator was a [[GC-8]] [[librarian]] and [[Security droid|guardian-protector]] [[droid]] manufactured by [[ARO]].<ref name="CGttSWG" /> He was programmed to guard a secret vault&mdash;containing numerous artifacts and treasures&mdash;in the [[Jedi Archives|Archives]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] of [[Coruscant]]. Cator took this duty so seriously that it chose to remain hidden, rather than risk helping the [[Jedi]] during the [[Operation: Knightfall|siege of the Jedi Temple]].<ref name="Dying Light II" />

After the [[Great Jedi Purge|fall]] of the [[Jedi Order]] and the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Cator still remained in the Temple standing guard alone until, eventually [[Jocasta Nu]] [[Hunt for Jocasta Nu|returned]] to the Temple. Cator was reunited with Nu in the secret vault, as she retrieved the list of every known [[Force-sensitive]] [[Youngling|child]] in [[the galaxy]]. After retrieving the list, Nu quickly left, leaving the droid on guard once more.<ref name="Dying Light II" />

Not long after, Cator attempted to deny access to [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who had chased Nu back into the vault. Cator was soon destroyed immediately following this, but not before identifying the [[Sith]] to Nu, as the former Jedi Anakin Skywalker, with a [[bioscan]]. Cator's destruction was not in vain though, as this gave Nu [[time]] to find a [[Lightsaber rifle|powerful weapon]] with which to fight Vader.<ref name="Dying Light III" />

==Behind the scenes==
