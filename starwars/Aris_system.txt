The '''Aris system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Albarrio sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square K-5 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the [[planet]] [[Aris]].<ref name="Origin" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
[[Aris/Legends|Aris]] originated in the [[1989]] roleplaying book ''[[Riders of the Maelstrom]]'',<ref name="Riders">''[[Riders of the Maelstrom]]''</ref> and was recanonized by its mention in the [[2019]] ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Aris was located in the [[Albarrio system/Legends|Albarrio system]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity,<ref name="Riders" /> but the [[2025]] "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix introduced both an [[Albarrio system]] and an Aris system, both located in the Albarrio sector.<ref name="Appendix" /> The concept of the Aris and Albarrio system being distinct systems was first suggested in the unlicensed article "[[Twinkle, Twinkle Little Star, How I Wonder Where We Are]]" in 1990.<ref name="Twinkle">{{VoyagesCite|13|Twinkle, Twinkle Little Star, How I Wonder Where We Are}}</ref>
