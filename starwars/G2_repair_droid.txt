The '''G2 repair droid''' was a model of [[maintenance droid]] that was inclined to chatter. The [[Resistance]] used G2 repair [[droid]]s such as [[G2-PI]] to perform [[memory wipe]]s and [[G2-RX]] who performed repairs in the droid repair bay aboard the MC85 Star Cruiser Raddus.<ref name="Join the Resistance" />
