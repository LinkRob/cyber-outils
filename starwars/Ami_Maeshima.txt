'''Ami Maeshima''' ([[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]]: 前島 亜美) is a Japanese voice actress and musician<ref name="JP" /> who voiced the [[smuggler]] [[Chita]] in the Japanese version of "[[The Smuggler (short film)|The Smuggler]]," a short film from the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 1|third season]] of ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]''.<ref name="SW">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-3-cast-announce|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 3 Cast Announced with New Trailer}}</ref> Maeshima is known in Japan for her role as Aya Maruyama in the [[Wikipedia:BanG Dream!|BanG Dream!]] franchise.<ref name="JP" />
