The '''artillery stormtrooper''', also known as the '''Mortar Stormtrooper''', '''mortar troopers''', or '''shell trooper''', was a [[stormtrooper]] variant within the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|military]] which were skilled with indirect fire weapons. Equipped with explosive loaded [[backpack]]s, some used [[Model 201 mortar system|Model 201 mortars]], including one trooper who [[Battle of Tython (9 ABY)|fought]] on the [[planet]] [[Tython]] following the Empire's [[Battle of Jakku|fall]] as part of an [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]].

==Description==
[[File:ArtilleryStormtrooper-CGSWG.png|thumb|left|220px|An mortar trooper setting up a Model 201 mortar system.]]
Artillery stormtroopers,<ref name="SWCEUAE">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> also referred to as [[Mortar]] Stormtroopers or shell troopers,<ref name="Mando Handbook">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Handbook]]''</ref> were a variant of [[stormtrooper]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] that specialized in indirect fire weapons such as [[mortar]]s while in the battlefield. They launched explosives of different types at a high angle to fall at precisely calculated coordinates from cover.<ref name="Databank" />

The stormtrooper was equipped with standard [[stormtrooper armor]], albeit with [[Color|yellow]] markings,<ref name="SWCEUAE" /> identically arranged to the red markings of the [[Incinerator stormtrooper|incinerator troopers]],<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> which denoted their specialization. Additional equipment which differentiated their kind included a yellow [[pauldron]] and a military [[backpack]] which carried ammunition<ref name="SWCEUAE" /> like [[C-25 fragmentation grenade]]s.<ref name="RGVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> One weapon used by the artillery stormtrooper was a collapsible [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.|Merr-Sonn Munitions]] [[Model 201 mortar system|Model 201 mortar]].<ref name="SWCEUAE" />

==History==
[[File:LivingSet-ArtilleryTrooper.png|thumb|right|250px|An artillery stormtrooper loads their mortar on Tython in 9 ABY.]]
Artillery stormtroopers served in the Empire's [[Imperial Military|military]] as part of the [[Stormtrooper Corps]].<ref name="Databank" /> During the [[New Republic Era|era]] of the [[New Republic]], following the [[Battle of Jakku|demise]] of the larger Empire,<ref name="Chapter 3">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 3: The Sin}}</ref> at least one of the troopers remained in one of its [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|remnants]], which was led by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]].<ref name="Chapter 14" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="9 ABY">{{MandoDate|14}}</ref> the mortar trooper was [[Battle of Tython (9 ABY)|deployed]] on the [[planet]] [[Tython]] as part of an [[Hunt for Grogu|effort]] to capture the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Youngling|infant]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] from the hands of the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]]. Part of a [[Tython detachment|unit of stormtroopers]], the trooper set up their Model 201 mortar system while the other troops advanced up a hillside, under attack from the [[assassin]] [[Fennec Shand]] and the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]].<ref name="Chapter 14" />

The artillery trooper then fired on Shand's position, soon backed by an [[E-HOB heavy repeating laser cannon|E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]]. One of the mortar's shells partially dislodged a rock near Shand. Taking advantage of it, the assassin pushed the rock down the hill, which trampled several stormtroopers and the [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon|E-Web]] emplacement. The stormtrooper forces were ultimately forced to retreat, and their transports were down by a rocket fired by Fett.<ref name="Chapter 14" /> Around the same time,<ref name="Hunters Date">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]], who had abandoned the Empire but adopted a pro-Imperial persona on stage at the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]],<ref name="BftA">''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]''</ref> had a suit of artillery stormtrooper armor he could wear in matches.<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The artillery stormtrooper was first mentioned on its official product page as "Mortar Stromtrooper." This article assumes that the title was a misspelling of "Mortar Stormtrooper."<ref name="Advanced Graphics">{{WebCite|url=https://advancedgraphics.com/mortar-stromtrooper-3444|text=Mortar Stromtrooper 3444|archivedate=20201005210416}}</ref> The trooper variant first appeared in "[[Chapter 14: The Tragedy]]" of the live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', which was directed by [[Robert Rodriguez]]<ref name="Chapter 14" /> and aired on [[December 4]], [[2020]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-december-2020|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in December 2020}}</ref> The variant's name was identified in its [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry.<ref name="Databank" />
