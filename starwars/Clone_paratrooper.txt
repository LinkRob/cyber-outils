'''Clone paratroopers''', also known as '''airborne clones''' or '''Airborne Troopers''', were specialized [[clone trooper]]s trained for airborne operations in the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]]. In the waning [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Clone Wars]], the paratroopers of the [[212th Attack Battalion]]'s [[2nd Airborne Company]] served as front line assault units in the [[Battle of Utapau]]. Following a failed attempt to execute [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] as a result of [[Order 66]], [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]] ordered a group of paratroopers to search for the missing body of their former [[Jedi General|general]]. The [[trooper]]s failed as Kenobi survived and escaped from the [[planet]].

==History==
In [[19 BBY]], approximately three [[Standard year|years]] after the [[First Battle of Geonosis|start]] of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Jedi Order]] launched a [[Republic Military|military]] [[Battle of Utapau|invasion]] of [[Utapau]]. [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was chosen by his colleagues on the [[Jedi High Council]] to lead the campaign. As a result, the [[212th Attack Battalion]] was deployed to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]]-occupied [[planet]].<ref name="Episode III" /> Among the 212th's [[infantry]] was a force of elite [[Cloning|clone]] paratroopers<ref name="Sideshow">{{HotToysCite|set=Movie Masterpiece Series|packtype=Sixth Scale Figure|pack=#794: Airborne Trooper (212th Attack Battalion)|link=collectibles/star-wars-airborne-trooper-hot-toys-914284}}</ref> from the [[2nd Airborne Company]].<ref name="Clone Paratrooper">{{ForceCollection|Clone Paratrooper|star=4}} {{C|Light side card}}</ref> The fight between [[Jedi General]] Kenobi's [[battalion]] and [[General]] [[Grievous]]' [[Separatist Droid Army|droid army]] commenced with a direct assault by standard [[trooper]]s<ref name="Episode III" /> while the paratroopers descended from the [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i]] [[gunship]]s to the front lines of the battle.<ref name="databank armor">{{Databank|clone-trooper-armor-history-gallery|Clone Trooper Armor History Gallery}}</ref>

[[File:212th-control-Utapau.png|thumb|left|250px|A clone paratrooper reports to Commander Cody in the aftermath of the Battle of Utapau.]]
The fighting continued after the destruction of Grievous as both regular troopers and paratroopers fought the [[battle droid]]s for control of [[Pau City]]. When Kenobi attempted to lead the battalion in clearing the [[city]]'s upper levels of the remaining [[droid]] forces, [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] issued [[Order 66]] to the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], causing the clone [[soldier]]s to become hostile towards their [[Jedi]] officers. Following an attempt to eliminate Kenobi, the paratroopers were tasked by their [[Clone Commander|commander]], [[Cody]], to search for the general's body as<ref name="Episode III" /> the clones began an [[occupation of Utapau]].<ref name="SWE Tion Medon">{{SWE|characters|tionmedon|Tion Medon}}</ref> Their search failed to produce any evidence of Kenobi's [[death]], although at least one [[Unidentified clone paratrooper|paratrooper]] was convinced that the Jedi Master perished when he fell into the sinkhole of Pau City. The trooper was wrong, however, as Kenobi survived the fall and escaped from Utapau using the late Grievous' starfighter, ''[[Soulless One]]''.<ref name="Episode III" />

At some point, a 212th paratrooper took part in an engagement within a sandy environment alongside a [[501st Legion]] clone trooper, a clone trooper wearing [[Phase I clone trooper armor]], and an AT-TE that featured the [[Imperial crest]].<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> When [[Phase I Purge Trooper armor]] was used by the Empire after Order 66,<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> [[Purge Trooper]]s who served under [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and the [[Inquisitorius]] to help [[Great Jedi Purge|combat]] Jedi wore helmets and armor similar to that of paratroopers.<ref name="Art of Fallen Order">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

==Equipment==
[[File:Clone_sharpshooter.png|thumb|176x176px|Some clone paratroopers served as snipers.]]
Clone paratroopers wore a modified version of the [[Phase II clone trooper armor]], that featured a [[kama]] and a unique, "beehive" helmet.<ref name="databank armor" /> They were equipped with [[DC-15A blaster carbine]]s and [[DC-15A blaster rifle]]s.<ref name="Episode III" />

Some paratroopers served as [[sniper]]s. These [[troop]]s worked with [[clone medic]]s and [[Heavy weapons clone trooper|Heavy Gunners]].<ref name="Star Wars Journeys: Beginnings">''[[Star Wars Journeys: Beginnings]]''</ref>
