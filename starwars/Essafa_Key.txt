'''Essafa Key''' was an island of the [[Gem Cities of Kwenn]] on the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]], situated in the [[Middle Chain]]. On Essafa, many guilds and corporations promoted bold initiatives as part of the commitment to enriching minds included in the [[Grand Renewal]] that transformed the islands of Kwenn. The island contained an educational district, and bridges linked it to the [[Parva Key|Parva]] and [[Gala Key]]s, as well as elsewhere on the planet.<ref name="The Living Force" />
