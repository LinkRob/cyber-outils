A '''dune-rat''' was a type of [[rat]] that lived in warrens in [[desert]]s.<ref name="Survivor" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Survivor}}
In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="9BBY">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> two members of the [[Narkis Anchorite]]s&mdash;a group assisting with the restoration of the [[Jedi Archives]]&mdash;within [[the Archive]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] discussed [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] patrols keeping [[Unidentified Narkis Anchorite 1|one]] of the [[anchorite]]s awake at [[Local day|night]]. The other [[Unidentified Narkis Anchorite 2|anchorite]] remarked that the Narkis Anchorites were dune-rats to the Empire, hidden and safe.<ref name="Survivor" />
{{EndGame}}

In [[33 ABY]],<ref name="33ABY">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Kidnapping of Unkar Plutt|events]] of "[[Better the Devil You Know, Part I]]" to [[33 ABY]].</ref> the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] engaged in combat with two [[Unkar's thugs|thugs]] attempting to take<ref name="Devil">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=1|story=Better the Devil You Know, Part I}}</ref> her<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> scavenged materials on<ref name="Devil" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="SWE" /> [[Jakku]]. After she knocked one [[Unkar Plutt's thug 1|thug]] away with her [[Rey's quarterstaff|quarterstaff]], their [[Unkar Plutt's thug 2|accomplice]] threatened Rey and referred to her as a "dune-rat."<ref name="Devil" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dune-rats were first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Better the Devil You Know, Part I]]," the lead story of the [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 1|first issue]] of the [[2017]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic series]] [[Star Wars Adventures (2017)|''Star Wars Adventures'']]. The story was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="Devil" /> on [[September 6]], [[2017]].<ref name="CBR">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cbr.com/star-wars-adventures-storylines-creative-teams-reveal-exclusive|text=Star Wars Adventures: First Storylines, Creative Teams Revealed|author=Ching, Albert|date=2017-06-13|archivedate=20190110214816}}</ref> In the [[Wikipedia:French language|French]] version of "Better the Devil You Know, Part I,"<ref name="DevilFrench">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=1|story=Better the Devil You Know, Part I}} {{C|French edition; original text "sale rat"}}</ref> which was published by [[Delcourt]] on [[May 2]], [[2018]],<ref name="Delcourt">{{WebCite|url=https://www.coleka.com/en/comics-graphic-novels/s/star-wars-comics/star-wars-delcourt/star-wars-aventures-tome-1_i222795|text=Star Wars - Aventures : Tome 1|work=[https://www.coleka.com/en Coleka]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/d9yuC}}</ref> Rey is referred to as a "sale rat,"<ref name="DevilFrench" /> which translates to "dirty<ref name="Sale">{{WebCite|url=https://www.larousse.fr/dictionnaires/synonymes/sale/18796|text=Synonymes : sale - Dictionnaire des synonymes|language=French|work=[[Wikipedia:Éditions Larousse|Éditions Larousse]]|archivedate=20260411051552}}</ref> rat."<ref name="rat">{{WebCite|url=https://www.larousse.fr/dictionnaires/synonymes/rat/17561|text=Synonymes : rat - Dictionnaire des synonymes|language=French|work=[[Wikipedia:Éditions Larousse|Éditions Larousse]]|archivedate=20260411051820}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[dune rat]]s were mentioned in [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (novel)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'']], a [[1996]] novel written by [[Steve Perry]].<ref name="Shadows">[[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (novel)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'' novel]]</ref>
