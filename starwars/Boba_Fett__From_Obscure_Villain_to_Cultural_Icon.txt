"'''Boba Fett: From Obscure Villain to Cultural Icon'''" is an article written by [[Jon Bradley Snyder]] for [[Star Wars Insider 30|''Star Wars Insider'' 30]] that examines the immense popularity of the character [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]] in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan community. The article offers perspectives from several ''Star Wars'' VIPs: [[Dave Dorman]], [[Jeremy Bulloch]], [[Stephen J. Sansweet]], [[Steve Perry]], [[Anthony Daniels]], [[Tom Veitch]], [[Kathy Tyers]], [[Kevin J. Anderson]], and [[Irvin Kershner]]. It was reprinted in [[Star Wars: Rogues, Scoundrels, and Bounty Hunters|''Star Wars Insider: Rogues, Scoundrels, and Bounty Hunters'']].
