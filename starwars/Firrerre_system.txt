The '''Firrerre system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Sugai sector]], a sub-[[sector]] of the [[Zuma sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It fell within grid square I-20 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the [[Planet|world]] [[Firrerre]], which was considered local to the [[moon]] [[Endor]],<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|38|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> which was located in the same grid square in the [[Moddell sector]], another subsector of the Zuma sector.<ref name="Endor">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Endor system]]</ref>

By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'' includes an addendum at the end describing some of the events of {{Film|VII}}, written several [[Standard day|days]] after they take place. The addendum is the final entry in the guide and is included chronologically after the entry mentioning Firrerre. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the film to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Firrerre was the site of a [[New Republic Space Ministry Firrerre Office|consular office]] for the [[New Republic]]'s [[New Republic Space Ministry|Space Ministry]].<ref name="RSG">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]''</ref> In 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Starcruiserdate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the Firrerre system's position was marked on several [[Viewscreen|screens]] aboard [[Chandrila Star Line]]'s [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|Star Cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' that displayed a partial [[Star map|map]] of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="GS">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Firrerre system was mentioned as just "Firrerre" on a map of [[the galaxy]] included with [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' box set published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> It then first appeared on several maps viewable in [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]], an immersive two-day roleplaying experience at [[W:c:disney:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]] in Florida<ref name="GS" /> that opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]]<ref name="31 Things">{{SW|url=news/31-things-we-learned-about-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=31 Things We Learned in an Exclusive Preview of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser|nolive=1}}</ref> and was launched for the final time on [[September 28]], [[2023]].<ref name="GalacticStarcruiserClosure">{{WebCite|url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/travel/2023/05/18/star-wars-hotel-closing|text=Disney to close its $5,000 Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser hotel|author=Hannah Sampson|date=2023-05-18|work=[[Wikipedia:The Washington Post|''The Washington Post'']]|archivedate=20230519064024}}</ref> The system was then identified fully as the Firrerre system in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

The [[Firrerre system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in ''[[The Crystal Star]]'', a [[1994]] novel written by [[Vonda N. McIntyre]].<ref name="Vonda">''[[The Crystal Star]]''</ref> It was then identified and depicted for the first time in ''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]'', a [[1998]] reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and illustrated by [[Brandon McKinney]] and [[Scott Kolins]].<ref name="TEGtPaM">''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]''</ref>
