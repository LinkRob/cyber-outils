[[File:PadawanBraid-CM.png|thumb|right|200px|A padawan braid]]
A '''braid''' was a kind of [[hair]]style involving multiple strands of hair twined around each other. They were worn by many cultures and [[species]] across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref> [[Padawan braid]]s were worn by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]s to signify their status as apprentices, and were ritually cut off when their apprenticeships ended.<ref name="Padawan">[[Padawan (novel)|''Padawan'']]</ref> Braids were also significant to the culture of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. Many [[Alderaanian]]s wore braids, and the planet's [[Alderaanian monarchy|monarch]] traditionally always wore them.<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan" /> The [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] had braids made from [[Wookiee]]s on his [[Boba Fett's armor|armor]]. Whilst fighting the Wookiee [[Chewbacca]] on the [[planet]] [[Jekara]], he threatened to make a new braid from Chewbacca.<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 3" />
