'''Ana Tolla''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[smuggler]] who led a [[Ana Tolla's crew|crew]] by the [[time]] of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. Tolla made a name for herself in [[the galaxy]] by successfully completing jobs no one else would take. The upheaval caused by the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of the [[Hosnian system]] in [[34 ABY]] resulted in the loss of half of her crew, prompting Tolla to invite the smuggler [[Izal Garsea]] and her boyfriend, [[Damar Olin]], to join her team on an unofficial basis.

[[Standard month|Months]] later, Tolla and her crew traveled to a [[Unidentified cantina (Actlyon)|cantina]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Actlyon]] following a mission. After receiving refreshments, Tolla hastily departed Actlyon with her crew, choosing to leave her colleague Izal Garsea behind. Some [[time]] later, she and her crew encountered Garsea in the [[Black Spire Outpost]] settlement on the Outer Rim planet [[Batuu]], and threatened the smuggler to stay far away from their current mission, which was to [[Skirmish at Kat Saka's farm|destroy]] the [[farmer]] [[Kat Saka]]'s [[Saka Homestead|farming settlement]]. Tolla hired the [[pilot]] [[Delta Jeet]] to find the farmer [[Julen Rakab]], who she and her associates later abducted from the [[Black Spire Ruins|ancient ruins]] at the edge of Black Spire Outpost.

By forcing Rakab to comply, Tolla was able to access restricted areas of Saka's farm with her comrades. She attempted to use [[sodium detonator]]s to blow up the settlement, which would the possibility of future [[plant]] growth there. Garsea, Rakab, and several residents of Batuu, incapacitated the smuggler's crew, leaving her initial plan was unsuccessful. As a last ditch effort, Tolla attempted to detonate the [[bomb]]s she had planted around the farm while she and her crew were still present. However, her crew member [[Oksan]] stopped her, and was arrested after the event.

==Biography==
===Smuggling adventures===
The [[Gender|female]] individual Ana Tolla was [[Reproduction|born]]<ref name="ACOF" /> around [[11 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> At some point, she took up the [[Smuggler|smuggling]] profession, acquired a [[Ana Tolla's ship|ship]], and formed a [[Ana Tolla's crew|crew]] including the [[human]]-[[Twi'lek]] [[Safwan]], the [[Ketzalian]] [[Lita]], the [[Zygerrian]] [[Oksan]], and several others. To establish a name for herself in [[the galaxy]], Tolla began to take dangerous missions that others were reluctant to attempt. Her efforts were successful in bolstering her reputation, and she continued to make profit off endeavors such as abducting a [[senator]] and [[Death|kill]]ing the competitors of an [[oil]] [[baron]]. Over [[time]], elaborated stories of her endeavors became known, including Tolla's alleged [[Thief|theft]] of a [[diamond]] [[necklace]] from a [[Unidentified Cuyacan princess|princess]] on<ref name="ACOF" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Cuyacan]], and her destruction of a [[vegetable]] [[farmer]]'s property.<ref name="ACOF" />

During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], the turmoil engulfing the galaxy after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of the [[Hosnian system]]<ref name="ACOF" /> in [[34 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> resulted in Tolla losing half of her crew. At some point, she traveled to a [[Abelor port|port]] on<ref name="ACOF" /> the planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> [[Abelor]] where she met the smugglers [[Izal Garsea]] and [[Damar Olin]], inviting them to join her crew on a temporary basis. Tolla, pleased that Garsea owned the [[light freighter]], the ''[[Meridian (light freighter)|Meridian]]'', allowed Garsea and Olin to take part in several missions. Though Garsea and Olin were not yet an official members of the crew, Tolla began ordering them to run errands on behalf of the team, and suggested that the younger smuggler sell her [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="ACOF" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Tolla went on a mission with her crew and traveled with them to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Actlyon]] afterward. She accepted an offer to complete a mission on the Outer Rim planet [[Batuu]] as her team's next endeavor, and assigned tasks to the team to prepare them for their travels. Tolla directed Garsea to attend to a list of menial shopping tasks and repairs, much to the smuggler's dismay. In the evening, Tolla traveled with the crew to a [[Unidentified cantina (Actlyon)|cantina]] in [[Actlyon City]] on Actlyon. The crew enjoyed orders of refreshments at the [[cantina]], taking the respite to have a reminiscing session about their previous adventures. However, when [[bounty hunter]]s raided the cantina, she rushed to escape alongside her crewmates. Tolla led her colleagues back to [[Ana Tolla's ship|Tolla's ship]], leaving their former associate Garsea behind after explaining she would not be part of their next mission.<ref name="ACOF" />

==Personality and traits==
Ana Tolla was a female individual who had [[Color|firey-red]] [[hair]] that she preferred to style in a thin [[braid]]. She had pale blue [[eye]]s, and a scratchy, deep voice. Tolla kept her eyebrows finely drawn, and her [[Limb|hands]] were roughly calloused by the time of her trip to Actlyon.<ref name="ACOF" />

==Skills and abilities==
Consistent with her reputation, Tolla was a skilled smuggler that made a name for herself by doing hard missions.<ref name="ACOF" />

==Equipment==
Tolla possessed a [[blaster]], and owned a [[Ana Tolla's ship|ship]]. During the operation at Kat Saka's farm, she had access to sodium detonators.<ref name="ACOF" />
