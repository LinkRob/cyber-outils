'''Chopper''' was a [[clone trooper]] that served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. A [[soldier]] in [[Clone Sergeant]] "[[Slick]]'s" [[Slick's squad|squad]], Chopper and his fellow [[trooper]]s were part of the [[Jedi]]-led [[army]] sent by the [[Galactic Republic]] to [[Battle of Christophsis|defend]] the [[planet]] [[Christophsis]] from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. By then, Chopper had secretly begun collecting the fingers of [[B1-series battle droid]]s as war trophies, which he fashioned into a necklace. To the surprise and disgust of his comrades, Chopper's breach of protocol was discovered by [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] during their hunt for a [[Cloning|clone]] [[spy]] in the ranks, although Chopper was adamant that he was not a [[Treason|traitor]].

Slick, who was in fact the spy they were searching for, tried to pin his treachery on Chopper. When Chopper pushed back, the [[sergeant]] inadvertently revealed his knowledge of restricted information and attempted to escape from the [[Christophsis Republic base|Republic base]]. Ultimately, the clone turncoat was [[Prisoner|captured]] and placed under arrest by his own squad, but not before he succeeded in [[Saboteur|sabotaging]] much of the Republic's [[Republic Military|military]] supplies.

Like all [[clone trooper]]s,<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" /> the [[trooper]] who took on the nickname Chopper<ref name="The Hidden Enemy" /> was [[Cloning|cloned]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] from the [[DNA|genetic material]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]], who served as the [[clone template]] for the entire [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Republic]] deployed a [[Jedi]]-led [[army]] to the [[planet]] [[Christophsis]] in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> with orders to [[Battle of Christophsis|defend]] the world from the [[Separatist Droid Army|droid army]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="TCWfilm">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> Attached to the [[Slick's squad|squad]] led by [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Slick]], Chopper was among the [[troop]]s that the Republic sent to the [[Christophsian]] [[homeworld]]. At that point, Chopper's contempt for [[battle droid]]s led him to collect the fingers of [[B1-series battle droid|B1s]] as war trophies from the battlefield, which he then fashioned into a necklace. Chopper kept his actions secret, however, as his conduct was a breach of protocol in the [[Republic Military]].<ref name="The Hidden Enemy" />

Chopper's squad was with [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]] when they attempted to ambush the [[Separatist Droid Army]]. To their surprise, the [[droid]]s ambushed them instead, forcing the clones to retreat to their [[Christophsis Republic base|base on Christophsis]]. As they withdrew from the battle, one of Chopper's squadmates, trooper "[[Gus]]" managed to tear off the head of a [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|T-series tactical droid]]. An analysis of the [[droid]]'s memory files revealed that the Separatists were warned about the Republic's strategy, leading the [[Clone trooper officer|clone officers]] to suspect that a clone was, in fact, [[spy]]ing for the enemy. While the [[general]]s Kenobi and [[Anakin Skywalker]] investigated behind enemy lines, [[Commander]] Cody searched for the spy at the base with the aid of [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]].<ref name="The Hidden Enemy" />

Their investigation led them to the barracks that housed [[Sergeant]] Slick's squad. Rex and Cody interrogated the clones individually, each one providing an alibi to confirm their allegiance to the Republic. When they came to Chopper, the clone hid his droid finger necklace and claimed he immediately went to the mess hall after returning to the base. His alibi was flawed, however, as [[Sketch]] revealed that Chopper had not joined them in the mess hall until much later. As a result, he was forced to reveal his war trophy to the clone [[officer]]s. Slick quickly tried to use the violation as proof Chopper was the spy, only for Slick's role as the traitor to be revealed,<ref name="The Hidden Enemy" /> while Chopper was proven innocent.<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:Chopper-holds-necklace.png|thumb|right|300px|Chopper assembled a necklace of battle droid fingers out of his rage at the robotic infantry.]]
Chopper was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] clone of Jango Fett, who was 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]]) tall. He had [[Color|black]] [[hair]],<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" /> which he kept shaved. Chopper's left [[eye]] was brown, while his right eye was pale yellow and he had tan skin.<ref name="The Hidden Enemy" />

Chopper was a clone with an odd personality<ref name="The Hidden Enemy Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode016.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: The Hidden Enemy}}</ref> and admitted to being a little weird.<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" /> He held a grudge against the battle droids of the Separatist Army, believing that the machines owed him something. To fuel his contempt for his mechanical adversaries, Chopper stole fingers from droid wreckage on the battlefield<ref name="The Hidden Enemy" /> as trophies.<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" /> He additionally attempted to hide his crime by both sequestering himself when making a droid finger necklace and using a false alibi to mask his whereabouts during Rex and Cody's interrogation. He was even willing to lie to his commanding officers to hide his violation of protocol, though he was not successful in covering up the truth. In the opinion of fellow trooper Gus, Chopper's actions represented a form of deficiency.<ref name="The Hidden Enemy" />

When his trustworthiness and character were questioned by Sergeant Slick, Chopper appealed to Rex and Cody, his commanding officers, to attempt to make his voice heard over his sergeant's accusations. When Slick continued to put him on the spot, however, the cornered Chopper rounded on his own sergeant and was quick to put the spotlight on him, aggressively questioning the officer's own whereabouts. The trooper's pursuits played a key role in revealing Slick's role as a Separatist spy.<ref name="The Hidden Enemy" />

==Skills and abilities==
As a clone trooper, Chopper was skilled in armed and unarmed combat as had a knowledge of military tactics.<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" />

==Equipment==
Chopper wore the standard [[plastoid]]<ref name="SWECG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Phase I clone trooper armor]],<ref name="The Hidden Enemy" /> which was sealed to a temperature-controlled black [[body glove]] by [[magnatomic grip panel]]s.<ref name="SWECG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> Like most clone troopers, Chopper had an [[behavioral modification biochip|inhibitor chip]] that was implanted into his [[brain]] by [[Kaminoan]] [[scientist]]s, ensuring obedience.<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clone trooper Chopper was first introduced in [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (webcomics)|''The Clone Wars'' webcomic]] [[The Clone Wars: Shadowed|''Shadowed'']], which released on [[February 6]], [[2009]], and is now part of the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' continuity.<ref name="Shadowed">''[[The Clone Wars: Shadowed]]''</ref> That same day, he also made his first [[canon]] appearance in "[[The Hidden Enemy]]," the sixteenth episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season One|season one]]. As with other clones, Chopper was voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]].<ref name="The Hidden Enemy Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode016.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: The Hidden Enemy}}</ref>
