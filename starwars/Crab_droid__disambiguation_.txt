'''Crab droid''' was a term for [[droid]]s that resembled [[crab]]s.

==[[Canon]]==
*'''[[44-CRB crab droid]]''', a model of droid used by the [[Pyke Syndicate]] in the [[spice mines of Kessel]]
*'''[[DTS-series Dismantler Droid]]''', a model of [[demolition droid]] with crablike [[Limb|legs]] manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]] and used by the [[Galactic Empire]]
*'''[[LM-432 crab droid]]''', a model of [[droid tank]] manufactured by the [[Techno Union]] and used by the [[Separatist Droid Army]]
*'''[[Scav droid]]''', a type of crablike droid designed by [[Zeetar]] and used by the [[Nihil]] [[pirate|marauders]]

==[[Star Wars Legends|Legends]]==
*A model of small, '''[[crablike maintenance droid]]''' used aboard the ''[[Star of Empire]]'' [[Luxury liner/Legends|pleasure liner]] and referred to as a '''crab droid''' or '''crab-droid'''
*[[Fastlatch-class defense droid|'''''Fastlatch''-class defense droid''']], a model of small, [[Crab/Legends|crablike]] [[Security droid/Legends|security droid]] developed by the [[Trade Federation/Legends|Trade Federation]]
*'''[[Hermit crab droid]]''', a model of small, disk-shaped [[Droid/Legends|droid]]
*'''[[KM1 mining droid]]''', a model of crablike [[Mining/Legends|mining]] droid manufactured by [[Duwani Mechanical Products/Legends|Duwani Mechanical Products]] and referred to as a '''crab droid'''
*'''[[LM-432 crab droid/Legends|LM-432 crab droid]]''', a model of [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droid]] manufactured by the [[Techno Union/Legends|Techno Union]] and used by the [[Separatist Droid Army/Legends|Separatist Droid Army]] that was part of the [[Spider droid family|same droid family]] as the [[DSD1 dwarf spider droid/Legends|DSD1 dwarf spider droid]] and [[OG-9 homing spider droid/Legends|OG-9 homing spider droid]]
*'''[[Protodeka]]''', a model of giant, crablike droid designed by a [[Unidentified Colicoid engineer|Colicoid engineer]] and manufactured by [[Geonosian/Legends|Geonosians]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Raxus Prime/Legends|Raxus Prime]] that was used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]] as a siege weapon during the [[Dark Reaper crisis]]
*'''[[Viper probe droid/Legends|Viper probe droid]]''', a model of crab-legged [[Probe droid/Legends|probe droid]] manufactured by [[Arakyd Industries/Legends|Arakyd Industries]] as part of the [[Arakyd Probot Series]]
