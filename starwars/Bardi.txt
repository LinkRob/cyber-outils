'''Bardi''' was a [[human]] who was a part of the [[Maya Pei Brigade]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="OYL" /> In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Bardi and several members of the brigade were left on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]] after [[Bardi's brother]] departed without them. After the human [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] brought a [[TIE Avenger prototype|TIE Avenger]] to the moon in relation to a [[Theft of the TIE Avenger prototype|mission]] for the [[Axis network]], Bardi and the member [[Gerdis]] fought amongst themselves.<ref name="OYL" /> Splitting up into factions, the two decided once and for all to play a game of [[Five Hands]] with [[Roski rules]] to decide who would depart and who would stay on the moon. However, during their match, the two heard a loud rumbling that sounded like thunder. A moment later, Bardi and Gerdis were [[Death|killed]] after a wild [[Yavinian doodar]] engulfed the two in its mouth.<ref name="Sagrona Teema">{{Andor|Sagrona Teema}}</ref>
