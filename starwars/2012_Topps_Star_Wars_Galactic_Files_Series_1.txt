'''2012 Topps ''Star Wars'' Galactic Files Series 1''' is a trading-card set released by [[Topps]]. The 350-card base set features four categories: Characters, Vehicles, Creatures, and Factions. Another set of cards, [[2013 Topps Star Wars Galactic Files Series 2|2013 Topps ''Star Wars'' Galactic Files Series 2]], was released on [[August 9]], [[2013]].

==Continuity==
Mistakes can be found on some cards:
*Shu Mai's and Passel Argente's titles have been swapped. Mai is the Commerce Guild Presidente, whereas Argente is the Corporate Alliance Magistrate.
*Droopy McCool played a chidinkalu, not a chindinkalu.
*Deliah Blue is not from the [[Old Republic era]], but the [[Legacy era]].
*Wullf Yularen is not a Republic Captain, but a Republic Admiral.
