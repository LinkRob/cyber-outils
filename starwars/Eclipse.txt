An '''eclipse''' was an astronomical event that occurred when a celestial body was temporarily obscured by another. Having witnessed one such event once on [[Coruscant]], the [[Alderaanian monarchy|Queen of Alderaan]] [[Breha Organa]] initially thought she was witnessing another eclipse when the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] obscured [[Alderaan]]'s [[Alderaan's sun|sun]],<ref name="Eclipse">{{StoryCite|story=Eclipse (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> before [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroying]] the entire planet as [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] tells [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa Skywalker]] to find the hidden [[Great Temple|rebel base]].<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> The Death Star had previously created the same scenario when it obscured [[Jedha]]'s [[Jedha's sun|sun]] before [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroying]] [[Jedha City]] in a test.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
