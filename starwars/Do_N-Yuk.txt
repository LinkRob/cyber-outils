'''Do N-Yuk''' was a [[Rodian]] [[bounty hunter]]. While in a [[To-Ba|gambling hall]] on the [[planet]] [[Olcatz]], they saw the former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] and reported his presence to the [[Crusaders (The Grand Master)|Crusaders]].<ref name="Payback" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Do N-Yuk appeared in "[[The Duel: Payback]]", a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Kamikaze Douga]] and [[ANIMA (company)|ANIMA]], was released on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]]. The character was named in the film's credits.<ref name="Payback" />
