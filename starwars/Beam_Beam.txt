'''Beam Beam''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[fathier]] who was a competitor in the [[Canto Cup]], a [[Fathier racing|fathier race]] held in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> They were [[Reproduction|born]] around [[1 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> and had [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]] and black [[eye]]s. Beam Beam was 299 [[centimeter]]s tall and weighed 915 [[kilogram]]s by the [[time]] of the Canto Cup<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date" />
{{GameMechanics}}
In that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] watched a [[hologram]] of a race that featured Beam Beam, and she [[Gambling|bet]] on the competition.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Beam Beam appeared in ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', a [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[Massive Entertainment]]. They are one of the fathiers that can be randomly assigned to the Canto Fathier Racing activity, which players can optionally partake in.<ref name="Outlaws" />
