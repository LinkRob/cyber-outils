The '''Field Secured Container Vessel (FSCV)''' was a class of [[fuel]] [[tanker]] [[spacetug]] that was used to transport [[starship]] fuel cells by the [[Galactic Empire]]. Two FSCVs could generate one or more [[force field]] bubbles or spheres using [[Force-field generator/Canon|force-field generators]]. Additional force fields could be linked, forming a [[train]]. An FSCV contained a [[Bridge (starship)|command capsule]], a force field generator, [[fuel cell]]s, an auxiliary command capsule, hexagonal-shaped [[engine]]s. One train of FSCVs was ''[[Black Ice]]'', which consisted of nine fuel bubbles and was assigned to the [[Imperial Replenishment Fleet DK-209]] before it was captured by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and destroyed in a collision.<ref name="BMF 34" />
