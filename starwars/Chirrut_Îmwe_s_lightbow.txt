'''Chirrut Îmwe's lightbow''' was a [[lightbow]] handmade and used by [[Chirrut Îmwe]]. The lightbow was made out of an [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]].<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> It had enough firepower to take down a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]]. It was powered by a [[diatium]] power cell, and with a standard [[blaster]] [[power cell]], could fire up to 50 rounds.<ref name="100objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref> It was air cooled.<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> Îmwe showcased his continuing faith in the [[Guardians of the Whills]] by continuing to use this traditional weapon, while his colleague [[Baze Malbus]] used a [[MWC-35c "Staccato Lightning" repeating cannon|less-traditional firearm]]. Îmwe used this weapon during the [[Mission to Eadu]] and later used the weapon during the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="Rogue One" />
