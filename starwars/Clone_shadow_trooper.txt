The '''clone shadow trooper''' was a variant of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[clone trooper]]s,<ref name="FC 4S" /> and served during the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="clonetroopers">{{Databank|clone-troopers|Clone Troopers}} states that the [[clone trooper]]s fought during the [[Clone Wars]]. Since the [[Cloning|clone]] shadow [[trooper]]s are a variant of those [[Soldier|troopers]], as established by {{ForceCollection|Shadow Trooper|star=4}}, they must have fought during the war as well.</ref> At some point at a [[Fire|fiery]] location, a [[trooper]] armed with a [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] joined forces with a [[cloak]]ed individual, who wielded a [[Color|red]] [[lightsaber]], out of necessity.<ref name="TCG">{{FFGTCG|set=Swayed by the Dark Side|cardname=''Allies of Necessity''|link=alliances-cycle/swayed-by-the-dark-side/allies-of-necessity-r2054}}</ref>

The clone shadow trooper was equipped with an [[Clone trooper armor|armor]] that was black in color with white stripes and a [[helmet]] with a red visor.<ref name="TCG" /> The troopers functioned as the predecessors to the [[shadow trooper]]s, a group of elite [[stormtrooper]]s equipped with black experimental stealth armor serving the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="FC 4S" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clone shadow troopers were first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'',<ref name="FC 4S" /> a [[2013]] digital card game developed by [[Konami]] Digital Entertainment. Even though the launch of ''Force Collection'' predates<ref name="Force Collection release">{{SW|url=news/announcing-star-wars-force-collection|text=Announcing Star Wars: Force Collection|nolive=1}}</ref> the ''Star Wars'' canon reset of [[2014]],<ref name="Reset">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Leland Chee]] confirmed that the game was kept updated to fit with canon.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|HolocronKeeper|status/846807700482666496|[[Leland Chee]]|date=2017-03-28|quote=I'm not gonna go through the trouble of changing them and I don't have any reason to believe currently that anyone would. [&hellip;] On the off-chance that we DID have a current need to change any of these, we would have for Force Collection.|archivefile=File:Leland_Chee_Force_Collection.png|archivdate=20190727180426}} &mdash; [[Leland Chee]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] has confirmed that [[in-universe]] information from ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'' conforms with ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]].</ref> The troopers were first pictured on a card in the [[2017]] ''[[Star Wars: The Card Game]]'' set ''[[Swayed by the Dark Side]]''.<ref name="TCG" />

[[Clone shadow trooper/Legends|Clone shadow troopers]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were created for a [[Wikipedia:Target Corporation|Target]]-exclusive [[Hasbro]] action figure from the [[2005]] [[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (toy line)|''Revenge of the Sith'' toy line]]<ref name="Legends">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (toy line)|pack=Utapau Shadow Trooper (Super Articulation!)|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/rots05shadowtrooper.asp}}</ref> and later appeared in ''[[Echoes of the Jedi]]'', the fourth adventure in the ''[[Dawn of Defiance]]'' campaign for [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game Saga Edition Core Rulebook|''Star Wars Roleplaying Game Saga Edition'']], written by [[Abel G. Peña]] and [[Jean-François Boivin]] and released in [[2008]].<ref name="DoD">{{DoD|Echoes of the Jedi}}</ref> The image used to depict a clone shadow trooper in canon<ref name="TCG" /> is mirrored from its original appearance in Legends, where it was used in the 2008 [[Star Wars Galaxies Trading Card Game|''Star Wars Galaxies'' Trading Card Game]] set "[[Squadrons Over Corellia]]."<ref name="Force Corruption">{{SWGTCG|set=Squadrons Over Corellia|cardname=Force Corruption}}</ref> While the canon usage depicts an uneasy alliance,<ref name="TCG" /> "Squadrons Over Corellia" depicted the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side]] user controlling the clone through [[Mind trick/Legends|Force Corruption]].<ref name="Force Corruption" /> At [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|''Star Wars'' Celebration Anaheim 2022]], a helmet of a clone shadow trooper was featured as one of eight collectible pins from the Mystery Helmets Collection.<ref name="Shadow SWCC Pin">{{WebCite|url=https://pinandpop.com/pins/52037/star-wars-celebration-2022-mystery-helmets-collection-shadow-trooper|text=52037 - Star Wars Celebration 2022 - Mystery Helmets Collection - Shadow Trooper - Misc - Disney Licensed|work=[https://pinandpop.com Pin and Pop]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/ivpce}}</ref>
