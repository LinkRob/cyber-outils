A '''listening post''' of the [[Galactic Empire]] was concealed within the [[Water|icy]] [[Planetary ring|rings]] of [[Esseles]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Maps" /> Following the [[Battle of Endor]] and the transformation of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] into the [[New Republic]], [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] forces targeted the listening post in a [[strike on Esseles]] in an attempt to draw out the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|ISD]] [[Overseer (Imperial-class)|''Overseer'']]. The strike succeeded, destroying the listening post and baiting the ''Overseer'' in an [[ambush at the Zavian Abyss]].<ref name="Squadrons" />
