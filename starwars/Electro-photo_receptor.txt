The '''Electro-photo receptor''' was a type of simple [[sensor]] which combined normal, ultraviolet and infrared light to create a picture of the target, whether two-dimensional or [[Hologram|holographic]]. The sensor's range was determined in part by the size of its receptor, with larger dishes allowing for detection at greater range. EPRs also worked in conjunction with [[targeting sensor]]s in order to form an image of the targeted object on the gunner's display.<ref name="YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>

Examples included a [[Tana Ire]] Electro-Photo Receptor which was combined along with a [[Melihat]] [["Multi Imager" dedicated energy receptor]] and a [[Fabritech]] [[ANs-5d "lock track" full-spectrum transceiver]] in what was known as a [[Carbanti]] transceiver package.<ref name="SWCT X-wing" />
