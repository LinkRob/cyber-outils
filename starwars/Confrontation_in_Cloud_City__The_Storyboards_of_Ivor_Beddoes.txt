"'''Confrontation in Cloud City! The Storyboards of Ivor Beddoes'''" is an article that celebrates the thirtieth anniversary of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], showing concept art by artist [[Ivor Beddoes]] for the iconic [[Duel on Cloud City/Legends|duel]] on [[Cloud City/Legends|Cloud City]] between [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] and [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]]. This article was written by [[Jonathan W. Rinzler]], appearing across [[Star Wars Insider 116|''Star Wars Insider'' 116]] and [[Star Wars Insider 117|''Star Wars Insider'' 117]].
