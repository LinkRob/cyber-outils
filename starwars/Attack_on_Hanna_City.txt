An '''attack''' was staged in [[Hanna City]], in [[Chandrila]], by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] in the early stages of the [[Clone Wars]]. The Separatists caught planetary defenses off guard, and jammed all transmissions as Chandrila attempted to send urgent calls to [[Coruscant]]. [[Dreadnought]]s positioned themselves over the capital city, and commenced the bombardment. The Chandrilan forces, practically nonexistent, had been unable to launch a counter-attack, but the [[deflector shield]] over the city was able to keep its inhabitants from harm's way.<ref name="MoF" />

Officials, including [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]], sought refuge in a bunker. The people of the city after a while left their homes and emerged into streets and plazas, shouting defiantly and cheering at the sky as the bombs fell, treating the detonations and the electric iridescence of the shields as a pyrotechnic display. The officials in the bunker also joined the celebrations in the streets.<ref name="MoF" />

After seven hours and seventeen minutes, the Separatists ceased the attack and left Chandrila.<ref name="MoF" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|20}}</ref> when recounting this incident to [[Baron]] [[Shkan-Shkan Yew]], Mothma revealed she never learned the purpose of the aggression, and speculated that it may have been intended as a show of force to incite fear.<ref name="MoF" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on Hanna City was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" />
