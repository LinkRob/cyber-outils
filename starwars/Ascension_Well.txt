The '''Ascension Well''' was a hole in the rocky terrain of the [[planet]] [[Ridlay]]. During the planet's total [[eclipse]]s every fifty [[Standard year|years]], beings that Ridlay's [[Unidentified Ridlay species|inhabitants]] believed to be [[Ghost (folklore)|spirits]] rose from the Ascension Well. As such, the eclipse was an occasion celebrated as combining the lands of the living and the [[Death|dead]], with the Ascension Well decorated with memorials to the deceased. In an eclipse during the [[Clone Wars]], however, the supposed ghosts attacked Ridlay's inhabitants, driving them from their homes. After [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] arrived on Ridlay to investigate the lack of communications, he visited the Ascension Well with several [[clone trooper]]s and a local guide, [[Agon]]. Windu determined that the beings were alive and had conflicted minds, and the group was subsequently confronted by [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], who rose from the Ascension Well on the top of a [[Multi-Troop Transport]].<ref name="Separation Anxiety Part One" />
