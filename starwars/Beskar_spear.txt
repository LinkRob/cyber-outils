A '''beskar spear'''<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|207|"Show Yourself, Jedi."|Show Yourself, Jedi.}}</ref> was wielded by the [[Magistrate]] [[Morgan Elsbeth]] on [[Corvus]] during the [[New Republic Era]]. The [[staff]] was made of pure [[beskar]], a metal alloy tied with [[Mandalorian]] culture.

==History==
[[File:MorganElsbeth-CGSWG.png|thumb|250px|left|Morgan Elsbeth was adept in fighting with the beskar spear.]]
Between [[9 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]], Morgan used the spear to defend herself and fight the [[Noghri]] [[assassin]] [[Rukh]], defeating him.<ref name="Anger" />

After the fall of the [[Galactic Empire]], between [[5 ABY]] and [[9 ABY]], the [[New Republic]] [[Ambassador]] [[Nadura]] traveled to Corvus in hopes for the planet to join the Senate. After a heated argument in which Morgan refused to join the Senate and turn herself in, she ordered her guards to kill Nadura and her delegation, using the spear to kill one of them.<ref name="Hate">{{TOTE|The Path of Hate}}</ref>

By [[9 ABY]], Morgan used the spear to fight the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], but was defeated by the former [[Jedi]]. Tano gifted the staff to the Mandalorian [[Din Djarin]]. The staff was durable enough to be able to block and deflect [[lightsaber]] blows<ref name="Chapter 13" /> and survived the [[Battle of Tython (9 ABY)|destruction]] of the ''[[Razor Crest]]'' intact.<ref name="Chapter 14" /> After the ''Razor Crest''{{'s}} destruction, Din Djarin wielded it during the [[second rescue of Grogu]], where he used it to destroy one [[Phase-III Dark Trooper|dark trooper]] and engage [[Moff]] [[Gideon]] in [[lightsaber combat]], which led to Gideon's defeat and capture.<ref name="Chapter 16">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 16: The Rescue}}</ref>

When Djarin reunited with the surviving members of [[the Tribe]] on [[Glavis Ringworld]], [[the Armorer]] informed him that beskar was only for [[armor]], not weapons, as the spear was capable of piercing [[Mandalorian armor]]. Djarin had her melt the spear and reforge it into a [[Grogu's chainmail shirt|garment]] for the [[foundling]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]].<ref name="Return of the Mandalorian" />
