'''Aquamarine Squad''' was a three-member [[squad]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Alliance Military|military]]. They went on a [[Aquamarine Squad's recruitment drive|recruitment mission]] to the [[spaceport]] [[Mos Eisley]] in [[3 ABY]], where they drew the attention of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s. They eventually escaped and returned to the Rebel Alliance's [[Echo Base]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]].

==Description==
Aquamarine Squad was a [[squad]] of [[infantry]] [[Rebel trooper|troopers]] within the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Alliance Military|military]] that was comprised of three members. The [[rebel pilot]] [[Zev Senesca]], when speaking to an individual, admitted that the use of "aquamarine" to name the squad sounded silly, but the [[color]]s they considered more suitable were already taken by other squads by the time the squad was formed. They wore the [[Temperature|cold]] weather gear common to those stationed<ref name="LEGOTSS" /> on the [[planet]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Hoth]], even while they were on the much hotter<ref name="LEGOTSS" /> planet<ref name="USWNE" /> [[Tatooine]].<ref name="LEGOTSS" />

==History==
{{Quote|I thought I was toast! Y'know, when people were telling me how great it is to be a Rebel, I'm PRETTY sure they forgot to say how super dangerous it can be!|An Aquamarine Squad member|<ref name="LEGOTSS" />}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="3 ABY mission">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'' establishes that the [[Aquamarine Squad's recruitment drive|mission]] occurred while the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] was stationed at [[Echo Base]] on [[Hoth]]. According to the ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'', Echo Base was constructed in [[3 ABY]], and {{Film|V}} establishes that it was destroyed in the same year. Therefore, Aquamarine Squad's mission must occur in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> while the Rebel Alliance was stationed at [[Echo Base]] on Hoth, Aquamarine Squad went on a [[Aquamarine Squad's recruitment drive|mission]] to the [[spaceport]] of [[Mos Eisley]] on Tatooine. While there, they went door to door, finding new recruits for the Alliance. They had some success but were discovered by an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Unidentified stormtrooper (Tatooine)|stormtrooper]] because they tried to recruit his [[Unidentified stormtrooper's wife|wife]]. They split up and escaped, but as a result, they were late to check in with Senesca, who was concerned because they were a new squad.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
{{GameMechanics|LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga}}
Senesca asked an individual to travel to Mos Eisley to retrieve Aquamarine Squad; the individual found two of the members safe but had to rescue the third from stormtroopers on [[Kerner Plaza]]. After speaking with the individual, all three returned to Echo Base.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
{{EndGame}}
