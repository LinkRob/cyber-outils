'''Connossium''' was a type of edible substance found in the chilies used for the [[Foodstuff|beverage]] [[Terullian chili shake]]. When consumed, the substance could improve the user's fast twitch responses.<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> Around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> during a live interview for the [[Subspace transceiver|radio]] show [[In the Pits]], the [[Neidorian]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Paghee'Dhe Dahglyn]] opened up about their usage of connossium by drinking the Terullian chili shake.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />
