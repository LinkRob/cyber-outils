'''Flux capacitor connectors''' were used on [[starship]]s to collect and redirect thermal energy to ship mounted [[laser cannon]]s in order to increase their effective damage. Flux capacitor connectors were adapted from [[astromech droid]] [[heat exchanger]]s and were sold by [[Miyuki Trade League]] affiliated [[merchant]]s for 3600 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] purchased a system of flux capacitor connectors for use in the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Flux capacitor connectors first appeared the [[canon]] continuity in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> In the game, the connectors were an upgrade the player could purchase and equip to their ship which caused their [[laser cannon]]s to generate more heat when firing but the cannons also progressively dealt more damage.<ref name="Outlaws" />

Flux capacitor connectors originally appeared in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity in "[[The Package (adventure)|The Package]]," a mini adventure published in [[West End Games]]' [[Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)|''Twin Stars of Kira'']] supplement for their ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', published in [[1993]]. However in that adventure the connectors were components of a ship's [[Hyperdrive/Legends|hyperdrive]] and not related to weapon systems.<ref name="Kira">{{WEGCite|story=The Package (adventure)|book=Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)}}</ref>

The device shares its name with the [[w:c:futurepedia:Flux capacitor|flux capacitor]], a time machine component from the [[Wikipedia:Back to the Future (franchise)|''Back to the Future'' franchise]].<ref name="BTTF">{{VisualMediaCite|title=[[Wikipedia:Back to the Future (film)|''Back to the Future'']]|director=[[Wikipedia:Robert Zemeckis|Robert Zemeckis]]|prod=[[Wikipedia:Amblin Entertainment|Amblin Entertainment]]|year=[[1985]]}}</ref>
