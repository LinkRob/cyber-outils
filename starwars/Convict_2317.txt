"'''Convict 2317'''" was the [[Operating number|designation]] of an [[alien]] [[prisoner]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] held aboard the [[wreckage-prison]] [[Accresker Jail]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Serving as part of the [[prison]]'s [[Accresker Penal Legion]], the inmate and their [[Chelli Lona Aphra's squad|squad]] was [[Attack on an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate|deployed]] aboard a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]]. During the battle onboard, Convict 2317 lagged behind their [[squad]] due to an issue with their [[Limb|foot]]. Since they passed ten [[meter]]s from their [[Unidentified hubdroid|hubdroid]], their implanted [[proximity bomb]] detonated, exploding the inmate.

==Biography==
{{Quote|I-it's my ''foot,'' hubdroid! Just&mdash;just gimme a second! I'll catch up!''"<br />"''Negative! We advance! ''Advaaance!''<nowiki/><nowiki/>|Convict 2317 and their hubdroid|<ref name="Aphra 20" />}}
[[File:DidNotHavePlotArmour-2016DoctorAphra20.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Convict 2317 stuggles to keep up with their squad aboard the rebel frigate.]]
During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], the [[Color|gray]]-skinned, green-[[eye]]ed [[alien]] was held by the Empire aboard the [[wreckage-prison]] [[Accresker Jail]]. [[Operating number|Designated]] "Convict 2317," they served in the [[prison]]'s convict army, the [[Accresker Penal Legion]], as part of a [[Chelli Lona Aphra's squad|squad]] and had a [[proximity bomb]] implanted in their throat so that they could not stray ten [[meter]]s away from the [[squad]]'s [[Unidentified hubdroid|hubdroid]].<ref name="Aphra 20" /> Around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> 2317 and their [[squad]] were [[Attack on an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate|deployed]] aboard a rebel [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] that had been crippled during a [[fleet]] engagement, Accresker's convict [[legion]] being tasked with clearing out the inside.<ref name="Aphra 20" />

After boarding the rebel [[frigate]], Convict 2317's squad began gunning down the defending [[rebel trooper]]s. However, 2317 had an issue with their [[Limb|foot]] which led them to lag behind from the squad, going outside of ten meters from the [[hubdroid]]. As the [[droid]] detected the issue and warned the inmate, they explained that it was because of their foot and promised to catch up. The hubdroid, though, insisted that they only advanced, and Convict 2317's proximity [[bomb]] began flashing. The inmate cried out as they detonated into a fireball, the rest of their squad advancing beyond the explosion.<ref name="Aphra 20" />

==Equipment==
Convict 2317 wore dark brown leather [[clothing]] with a large hood and compartments around the waist, an outfit that as worn by all of the prisoners at Accresker Jail. The inmate also carried a large [[blaster]].<ref name="Aphra 20" />
