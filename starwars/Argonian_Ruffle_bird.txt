An '''Argonian Ruffle bird''' was a type of [[bird]].<ref name="YM" /> Around [[233 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|101B}}</ref> while on a [[Mission to retrieve the sparkle-fire seeds|mission]] from [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]], [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Lys Solay]] speculated that she and fellow [[Jedi]] [[youngling]]s [[Kai Brightstar]] and [[Nubs]] may have been sent to retrieve a cute Argonian Ruffle bird. The goal of the mission instead ended up being to acquire [[sparkle-fire seed]]s.<ref name="YM" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Argonian Ruffle bird was mentioned in "[[Yoda's Mission]]," the second part of the [[The Young Jedi / Yoda's Mission|first episode]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''{{'s}} [[Young Jedi Adventures Season One|first season]].<ref name="YM" /> The episode was first screened at [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]] on [[April 8]], [[2023]].<ref name="Celebration">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-panel-schedule|text=Plan Ahead with the ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Panel Schedule}}</ref>
