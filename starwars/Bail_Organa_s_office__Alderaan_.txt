An '''office''' belonging to [[Viceroy]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] was located at the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan|royal palace]].<ref name="Ahsoka" /> It was destroyed by the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]'s test firing during [[Destruction of Alderaan|the Disaster]].

The room, like many other rooms in the palace, had an old fashioned door that opened and closed by hand<ref name="PoA">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref> and was roomy, with more than enough space for a desk, chairs, and an [[aquarium]] full of brightly colored sea creatures that Organa had installed in his office to keep his daughter [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]] amused when she was a toddler.<ref name="Ahsoka" /> The corridor leading to the office was the same one that lead to the royal library and was illuminated by [[candledroid]]s.<ref name="PoA" />
