'''Barium''' was a [[poison]]ous [[Color|green]] substance that could be used as a [[fuel]] for vehicles and explosives. It could also repel the [[gnaw-jaw]] [[insect]]s of the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. The [[Spectres|rebel]] [[Sabine Wren]] mentioned barium as a potential explosive fuel in her [[Sabine Wren's journal|journal]], and the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] likewise noted the substance in the [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]] she [[Author|wrote]], documenting it as a hazard for scavengers on Jakku and its use for warding off gnaw-jaws.

==Description==
{{Quote|You know what barium is, don't you? Excellent. Yes, it's starship fuel, and yes, it's incredibly poisonous. In the right quantity, barium will kill you stone-dead, but in certain circumstances, it might just save your life.|Leesa Chaffni, to [[Naldri Zuvio]]|<ref name="JOJ" />}}
Barium was a highly [[poison]]ous substance<ref name="JOJ" /> that could take the form of either [[Color|green]]-colored lumps of solid matter or a powder.<ref name="Rey" /> It shared its name with the [[barium reed]] [[plant]]<ref name="TRS">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> as well as the product known as [[Barium Fizz]].<ref name="Maul2">[[Darth Maul – Black, White & Red 2|''Darth Maul – Black, White & Red'' 2]]</ref> Barium could glow<ref name="JOJ" /> and be used as [[fuel]] for vehicles<ref name="Rey" /> or explosives, with the use of the substance in the latter leading to explosions with a green hue.<ref name="Sabine" /> Similarly, a fuel laden with barium could lead to a green-colored [[engine]] exhaust trail in an [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> Powdered barium also acted as a repellent against [[gnaw-jaw]]s,<ref name="Rey" /> [[Diet|carnivorous]]<ref name="BtA">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref> [[insect]]s living on<ref name="Rey" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]<ref name="TFA BG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]].<ref name="Rey" />

[[File:Fuels-SabineMyRebelSketchbook.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Sabine Wren mentioned barium as a possible explosive fuel in her journal.]]

===The sands of Jakku===
{{Quote|I just needed those glowrods! I needed those glowrods more than anything I'd ever needed before! Actually, that wasn't quite true. I really needed something else. Something I knew I didn't have and had never even seen. Not glowrods. Barium rods.|Leesa Chaffni|<ref name="JOJ" />}}
[[File:Starship_Graveyard_Jakku_SWDA.png|thumb|right|300px|Barium leaked into the sands of Jakku.]]
After<ref name="Rey" /> the decisive [[Galactic Civil War]] [[Battle of Jakku|battle]] on Jakku in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the planet's surface became strewn with the wreckage of starships and vehicles, leading to dangerous fuels such as barium leaking in its sands. Prior to<ref name="Rey" /> her departure from Jakku in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[human]] [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] mentioned barium in the [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]] she was [[Author|writing]]. She included in it a note with a visual depiction of a sample of the substance and wrote that barium&mdash;which she recommended handling with care&mdash;colored sand green, a fact that could inform scavengers to proceed with caution. In the [[book]], Rey also suggested using powdered barium, laid out in a circle around oneself, in order to ward off gnaw-jaws in case of camping in Jakku's desert.<ref name="Rey" />

At one point, the [[Tallimarian]] scavenger [[Velorie Sarcon]] boasted of barium's effectiveness as a gnaw-[[Mouth|jaw]] repellent, claims that were overheard by the [[Kessurian]] [[Leesa Chaffni]], who worked at [[Dai's Cafe]] on Jakku. Chaffni did not know how Sarcon came to possess such knowledge but did not doubt the scavenger's expertise.<ref name="JOJ" />

[[File:Rey-TROSOCE.png|thumb|left|220px|Leesa Chaffni found the glow of the lightsaber wielded by Rey (pictured) to be brighter than that of barium.]]
The Kessurian later [[Mission to the Explorator|ventured]] into the tunnels of an abandoned Jakku [[kesium gas]] [[Unidentified kesium mine|mine]]<ref name="JOJ" /> at one point between 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] and [[35 ABY]]<ref name="JOJDate">The events of ''[[The Jaws of Jakku]]'' take place between the events of {{Film|VIII}} and {{Film|IX}}, which corresponds to between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> in the hopes of discovering the wreckage of the crashed [[Dreadnought-class heavy cruiser|''Dreadnought''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Explorator]]''. When Chaffni encountered a swarm of gnaw-jaws in the tunnels, she, remembering Sarcon's words, frantically but fruitlessly searched her [[backpack]] for barium rods despite knowing it did not contain any and having never even seen one before. The Kessurian eventually retrieved a [[glowrod]] and fled the advancing insects deeper into the mine and into what was indeed the wreck of the ''Explorator''. There, Chaffni and [[Finn]], a member of the [[Resistance]], were both ultimately rescued from the gnaw-jaws by Rey, whom Finn had accompanied back to Jakku. Rey, who had since become a [[Jedi]] [[Jedi training|apprentice]], warded off the gnaw-jaws while [[Force power|using]] [[telekinesis]] and wielding her [[Skywalker lightsaber|lightsaber]], the glowing ignited blade of which Chaffni found to be brighter than any amount of barium.<ref name="JOJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The Imperial TIE bomber's bomb bay held a variable load of ordnance. Payloads included ArmaTek SJ-62/68 orbital mines, ArmaTek VL-61/79 proton bombs, and Merr-Sonn Munitions thermal detonators, which were powered by volatile barium and had a blast radius of 20 meters.|A passage from ''TIE Fighter Owners' Workshop Manual'' misidentifying baradium as barium|<ref name="TIE">''[[TIE Fighter Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>}}
Barium was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]]<ref name="Sabine" /> and published on [[February 3]], [[2015]].<ref name="SabineReleaseDate">{{Amazon|url=Star-Wars-Rebels-Sabine-Sketchbook/dp/0794432891|text=Star Wars Rebels: Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook}}</ref> ''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'', authored by [[Jason Fry]], illustrated by [[Andrew Barthelmes]],<ref name="Rey" /> and released on [[December 18]] of the same year,<ref name="Studio">{{StudioFun|url=book-display.php?isbn13=9780794435691|text=Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Rey's Survival Guide}}</ref> provided the first visual depiction of the substance.<ref name="Rey" /> The [[2019]] reference book ''[[TIE Fighter Owners' Workshop Manual]]'' by [[Ryder Windham]]<ref name="TIE" /> misidentified<ref name="Sabine" /> the explosive compound [[baradium]]<ref name="FullyOperational">[[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']]</ref> as barium.<ref name="TIE" />

[[File:BariumRealWorld.jpg|thumb|right|250px|A sample of pure real-world barium]]
The name "barium" was originally introduced to the ''Star Wars'' franchise in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity. It was featured in the form of a mention of [[barium liquid]] in<ref name="Monsters">''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''</ref> the non-canonical<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}} {{C|In response to: "And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon?"}}</ref> [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'' by [[Bob Carrau]]<ref name="Monsters" /> and then in [[Gregory Walker]]'s [[2009]] [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]]-exclusive [[StarWars.com]] article "[[Dining at Dex's]]."<ref name="Dex">{{Hyperspace|url=fans/hyperspace/source/dexdiner/index.html|text=Dining at Dex's|int=Dining at Dex's}}</ref> Additionally, the [[Barium Frizz]] [[Foodstuff/Legends|beverage]] appeared in "[[Be Still My Heart: The Bartender's Tale]]," a short story written by [[David Bischoff]] and published in the [[1995]] anthology ''[[Tales from the Mos Eisley Cantina]]''.<ref name="Tales">{{StoryCite|story=Be Still My Heart: The Bartender's Tale|book=Tales from the Mos Eisley Cantina}}</ref> In the real world, [[Wikipedia:Barium|barium]] is a [[Wikipedia:Chemical element|chemical element]] and [[Wikipedia:Metal|metal]] with a silvery-white [[Wikipedia:Lustre (mineralogy)|luster]] that, in nature, is always found in the form of [[Wikipedia:Chemical compound|chemical compounds]]. All [[Wikipedia:Solubility|soluble]] barium compounds are toxic to [[Wikipedia:Mammal|mammals]]. Barium [[Wikipedia:Salt (chemistry)|salts]] are used to obtain green color in [[Wikipedia:Flare|flares]] and [[Wikipedia:Fireworks|fireworks]].<ref name="BariumBritannica">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/science/barium|text=Barium &#124; Uses, Compounds, & Facts|author=The Editors of Encyclopædia Britannica|date=2024-06-14|work=[[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica|''Encyclopædia Britannica'']]|archivedate=20240226155412}}</ref>
