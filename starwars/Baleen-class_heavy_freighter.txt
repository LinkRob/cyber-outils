The '''''Baleen''-class heavy freighter''' was a [[bulk freighter]] model used to move huge amounts of cargo.

==Description==
[[File:Eravana-MF98.png|thumb|250px|left|View from the top of a ''Baleen''-class freighter]]
''Baleen''-class [[freighter]]s were about 400 [[meter]]s long and were quite clumsy, but were capable of moving large amounts of cargo across [[the galaxy]] every [[Standard day|day]], and consequently, were essential to commerce. Their forward compartment could be opened in transit and used to salvage items found drifting in [[Realspace|space]], even entire [[starship]]s. Built in [[orbit]]al [[shipyard]]s, ships of the class were designed to never enter a [[planet]]'s atmosphere, instead they were docked at [[space station]]s and transfer yards to load and unload cargo.<ref name="ICS" />

The giant ship's front bay could also be used as a docking bay, and was used for cargo that needed to be specifically handled. Everything else was attached to the transport grid between the engines and the bow section. This setup created a labyrinth of cargo and goods.<ref name="CVNE">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref>

==History==
One such freighter was the ''[[Eravana]]'', [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]]'s ship after the loss of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="ICS" /> The original blueprint for the ''Baleen''-class was kept at the [[Corellian Command Base]] in [[Coronet City]].<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />

<div style="float:right">[[File:EravanaSwallowsFalcon-TFA.png|thumb|300px|right|The ''Eravana'' pulls the ''Millennium Falcon'' into its hangar bay.]]</div>
They successfully escaped the gangs and jumped into [[hyperspace]] in the ''Falcon'',<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> leaving the ''Eravana'' in the hands of the Guavians.<ref name="SW Adventures" /> Immediately, [[Bala-Tik]] ordered his men to inform the First Order that Solo and the droid had escaped from the [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="TROS Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] to one [[Standard year|year]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], the events of ''The Rise of Skywalker'' must occur in [[35 ABY]].</ref> at least twenty-five ''Baleen''-class heavy freighters were part of a [[Citizens' Fleet|fleet]] assembled by [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] to assist the Resistance during the [[Battle of Exegol|battle]] against the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]] over the [[planet]] [[Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX" />
