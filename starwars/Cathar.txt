The '''Cathar''' were a [[cat]]like, [[feline|felinoid]] [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]. Members of the species possessed [[Hair|fur]] and manes, slitted [[Eye|eyes]] and [[nose]]s, pointed [[ear]]s, a [[mouth]] with [[tooth|teeth]], and [[claw]]ed [[Limb|hands]]. They were powerful, swift, and fierce [[soldier|fighters]].

==Biology and appearance==
The Cathar<ref name="Script" /> were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> of [[Feline|felinoid]],<ref name ="A Bad Batch Novel">''[[Sanctuary: A Bad Batch Novel]]''</ref> [[cat]]like [[humanoid]]<ref name="Script" /> with [[Hair|fur]] and manes,<ref name="Victory's Price" /> slitted [[Eye|eye]] and [[nose]]s, pointed [[ear]]s, a [[mouth]]<ref name="Tall Tales" /> with [[tooth|teeth]], and [[claw]]ed [[Limb|hands]].<ref name="A Bad Batch Novel" /> Cathars have very sensitive hearing, even more so than [[Twi'lek]]s or [[Human|human]]s.<ref name="Victory's Price" /> 

==Society and culture==
The species were powerful, quick, and fierce [[soldier|fighters]].<ref name="A Bad Batch Novel" />

==Cathars in the galaxy==
During the [[High Republic Era]], the Cathar [[Tasia]] served as a [[Storm (rank)|Storm]] in [[Lourna Dee]]'s [[Lourna Dee's Tempest|Tempest]] of the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] organization,<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> and [[Far Linghe]] served as a [[Jedi Knight]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "Tall Tales" take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> both the [[Unidentified Cathar twin 1|Cathar]] criminal [[twin]]s were the subject of a bounty taken by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Raslin Grace]].<ref name="Tall Tales" />

In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="VictorysPriceDate">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> the Cathar [[Gender|female]] [[Dhina]] served as an [[ensign]] aboard the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]'', the [[flagship]] of [[New Republic]] [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
