'''Creatinine''' was a substance that was found in the [[blood]] sample analysis of the [[Trandoshan]] [[Nack Movers]]'s blood by [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[SP-4 ISC crime scene analysis droid|crime scene analysis droid]] [[Russo-ISC]] after Movers was [[Death|murdered]] with [[sennari]] [[poison]] in [[21 BBY|7958]] [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] Movers's blood had a concentration of 130 creatinine, which was not considered a contaminant by the [[droid]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Creatinine was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />
