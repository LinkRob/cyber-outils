'''''AT-AT AT-Attack''''' is a ''[[LEGO Star Wars]]''&ndash;theme short video that was released on [[May 21]], [[2026]] on the ''[[StarWarsKids.com|Star Wars Kids]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel.<ref name="AT-AT AT-Attack" />

==Official description==
''[[Din Djarin|Mando]] and [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] sneak aboard an [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT]] to take out a squad of [[Imperial Remnants|Imperial Remnant]] Troopers.''<ref name="AT-AT AT-Attack" />

==Plot summary==
The video opens with [[Din Djarin]] and [[Grogu]] following a convoy of three [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] (AT-AT) walkers on a stolen [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]] (AT-RT) transport. They scurry under the towering AT-AT walkers. After parking their vehicle, they ring a door bell on [[Commander]] [[Barro]]'s walker. When the [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtrooper]]s inside the passenger compartment open a sliding door, Grogu uses the Force to hit them with snowballs while Djarin carries him. 

The duo force their way aboard the AT-AT walker. They engage in a snow fight with the snowtroopers and Grogu fights his way to the cockpit, using the Force to cause a snowtrooper to spill his glass of [[blue milk]]  and slip on the floor. Djarin dodges their snowballs and uses a lasso to throw two snowtroopers out of the vehicle. Djarin confronts a snowtrooper with his flamethrower. The snowtrooper builds a giant snowman but Djarin melts the snowman. The snowtrooper flees but slips on the ice and water, falling out of the AT-At walker. 

Grogu escapes two snowtroopers and enters the cockpit where he is confronted by Commander Barro and two [[AT-AT pilot]]s. He uses the Force to open the cockpit hatch and throws Barro out. He then Force pushes the pilots against the control console, causing one to accidentally kick a lever that causes the walker to advance forward. Realizing what he has done, the youngling tries to regain control of the walker but is unable to stop it from walker towards a clifface.

Djarin appears and escapes with Grogu using his jetpack. The walker slides down the clifface and lands in a valley. Barro and the AT-AT pilots survive the crash because they land in a heap of snow. An airborne Grogu and Djarin approach. Before Barro and his men can shoot them, Grogu uses the Force to pummel them with snowballs.
