'''Althea''' was a [[Gender|female]] member of the [[Isobe royal family|royal family]] of [[Isobe]]. She and her brother [[Thelan]] were the children of [[Lord]] [[Hallux]]. She developed a brief relationship with [[Jun Sato]], a [[human]] from [[Mykapo]] who had joined the renowned [[crystal]] processing school on Isobe. However, it turned out that Sato was part of a [[Thelan's smuggling ring|smuggling ring]], which led him to be banished from Isobe for life, much to Althea's dismay.<ref name="Never Far Behind" />
