The '''battle for Moonbender''' was an attack on [[Moonbender Colony]], a remote colony on an [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Unidentified moon (Moonbender Colony)|moon]], by the [[Sith apprentice]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]]. Having been tasked by his [[Sith Master|master]], [[Darth Sidious]], to [[Death|kill]] the [[Remainders]]&mdash;a group of former [[bounty hunter]]s who ran the colony&mdash;Maul landed on the [[moon]], initially sending the Remainders an order to vacate supposedly from the [[Galactic Republic]]. After the order was revealed to be false, Maul began his attack on the colony, breaching the [[The Tunnel|entrance]] despite [[General]] [[Zandloss]]'s efforts. Maul defeated each of his foes as he fought his way to the colony's [[Moonbender Colony medcenter|medcenter]], where he killed [[Doctor]] [[Korvik]], the last Remainder. Maul reported his success to Sidious, learning that the only reason he had been ordered to capture Moonbender was to test his abilities against the Remainders. Although the Remainders were defeated, at least one resident, a [[Unidentified Twi'lek (Moonbender Colony)|Twi'lek]], survived by hiding in a vent in the [[medcenter]].<ref name="DM-BW&R 2" />
