'''Cordé''', formerly known as '''Cordyn''', was a [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] who served [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] in the [[Standard day|days]] leading up to the [[Clone Wars]]. She was acting as a decoy when she was [[Death|killed]] in an [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|attempt]] on Amidala's life by [[Clawdite]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Zam Wesell]].

==Biography==
Cordyn was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Reproduction|born]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. She had a [[Cordé's sister|sister]] who worked as a jeweler.<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="QP date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Queen's Peril]]'' to [[32 BBY]].</ref> Cordyn was one of several [[Gender|girls]] considered by [[Captain]] [[Quarsh Panaka]] of the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]] while he was looking for additional [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaidens]] for newly-elected [[Queen]] of [[Naboo]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], along with [[Rabé Tonsort|Rabene Tonsort]]. Cordyn strongly resembled Amidala, but Panaka removed her from consideration because her poor hand-eye coordination meant she lacked skill with a [[blaster]] or other forms of combat.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /><ref name="Queen's Peril" />

Four [[Standard year|years]] later, after Amidala's two terms as [[Monarch of Naboo|monarch]] ended, she was asked by Queen [[Réillata]] to serve as the [[Chommell sector]]'s [[Senator]] in the [[Galactic Senate]]. She came to the conclusion that she would need additional handmaidens to act as decoys. Rabé suggested Cordyn, pointing out that her resemblance to the senator would make her an excellent decoy despite her poor fighting skills. Cordyn was tracked down by [[Saché]] on behalf of Amidala and eventually agreed to take on the role, taking on the name Cordé. Amidala also took on two other handmaidens, [[Versé]] and [[Dormé]].<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />

In the [[Standard day|days]] leading up to the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]], Amidala returned to [[The galaxy|the galactic]] [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]] to take part in a [[Military Creation Act|vote]] on the creation of a [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Grand Army of the Republic|army]] in the wake of the [[Separatist Crisis]].<ref name="Episode II" />

[[File:Corde.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Cordé moments before her death]]
On the orders of Amidala's [[Chief of Security|chief of security]], [[Captain]] [[Gregar Typho|Typho]], Cordé served as her decoy, traveling on the senator's [[J-type diplomatic barge|ship]] posing as Amidala while the senator [[pilot]]ed a [[N-1 starfighter|Naboo starfighter]] in disguise. Upon landing, Cordé descended the [[Entry ramp|ramp]] with her retinue, but was soon caught up in an [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|explosion]] that destroyed the [[Starship|ship]] and [[Death|killed]] both her and six other people including Versé. The explosion was set up by [[bounty hunter]] [[Zam Wesell]], who had been hired by [[Jango Fett]] to [[assassin]]ate the senator.<ref name="Episode II" />

While Cordé lay dying, Amidala ran to her handmaiden's side. With her dying words, Cordé apologized for failing her mistress, though Amidala insisted that she had not.<ref name="Episode II" />

===Legacy===
Not long after Cordé's death her fellow handmaiden [[Sabé]] stood in for Amidala in the senate while Amidala was on a mission. When Sabé attended a soirée at [[Cantham House]] hosted by [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] the bodice of the dress she was wearing was held in place by pins designed by Cordé: invisible and entirely secure. Sabé then commented that the pins were 'very clever' as although she had just seen Dormé put the pins in she couldn't find them. Dormé then said that Cordé's whole family was very talented before her voice caught.<ref name="Queen's Hope">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref> Sometime later, Amidala shared private tears for Cordé with [[Duja]], another of her handmaidens.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Cordé had [[Color|brown]] [[hair]], brown [[eye]]s and light skin.<ref name="Episode II" /> Despite being slightly taller than Padmé Amidala, she was considered the closest physical match to her.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />

==Skills and abilities==
Cordé was able to perfect Amidala's voice to the point that even a voice identifier couldn't tell the difference. However, she lacked combat ability, especially with a blaster,<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /> due to poor hand-eye coordination.<ref name="Queen's Peril" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cordé was played by [[Natalie Portman]] inside the cruiser and walking down the entry ramp, a stunt actress during the explosion that took her life, and [[Veronica Segura]] during her death.<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Star Wars Chronicles: The Prequels]]''</ref>
