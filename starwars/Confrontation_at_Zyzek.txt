{{Quote|Senior Captain Thrawn, you may keep your prisoners. I will see you again when I arrive among your people to change the ancient paths of the Chiss to the fuller enlightenment of the Kiljis.|Nakirre to Thrawn|audio=Nakirrezyzek.flac|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />|translated=1}}
In [[18 BBY]], [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]], the ruler of the [[Kilji Illumine]] nation, and the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," engaged in a '''confrontation''' over the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] [[Zyzek]]. After Thrawn's [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]'' arrived at the [[Zyzek system]] and the senior captain broadcast a message, claiming that he possessed [[Watith]] [[prisoner]]s, Nakirre moved his [[Kilji war cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]'' to speak with the [[Chiss]].

He offered to take the Watith to their home, as his [[Kilji way of order and enlightenment|religion]] required he helped others; however, Thrawn offended the generalirius, refusing his offer, prompting Nakirre to prepare his [[war cruiser]] for [[Space warfare|combat]]. However, [[Zyzek System Defense]], which defended the Zyzek [[Star system|system]], intervened, sending two pairs of [[patrol ship]]s to engage the two [[cruiser]]s while requesting for them to stand down. Nakirre complied, and the two [[starship]]s left Zyzek.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|&mdash;to all assembled merchants and traders. I am Senior Captain Thrawn of the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet warship ''Springhawk''. I have news for any Watith who may be present. Are there any of that species to whom I may speak?|Senior Captain Thrawn announces his arrival|audio=Zyzekprelude.flac|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />|translated=1}}
In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a [[Grysk]] operative known as "[[Jixtus]]" allied himself with [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]], the ruler of the [[Kilji Illumine]] nation, promising to teach the Illumine the concept of conquests and guide a series of [[conquests of enlightenment]] to spread the Illumine's [[Kilji way of order and enlightenment]] [[religion]]. In exchange, Nakirre allowed him to travel aboard his [[flagship]], the [[Kilji war cruiser]] ''[[Whetstone]]''. With the approval of the Illumine's [[Kilji Overlords]], the pair traveled to the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] [[Zyzek]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

In that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Watith]] [[captain]] [[Fsir]] [[Skirmish against the Watith|attacked]] the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] [[Chiss heavy cruiser|heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]'' after Jixtus hired him to do so;<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> however, Thrawn defeated the captain and captured his [[freighter]], the ''[[Saltbarrel]]'',<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref> studying it. After studying the [[starship]], Thrawn was convinced that Fsir had been hired to attack the ''Saltbarrel'' at Zyzek, and he brought his [[heavy cruiser]] there.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

As Nakirre and Jixtus prepared to speak with [[merchant]]s of four nations the Kiljis sought to conquer, the ''Springhawk'' arrived. Thrawn broadcast a message that claimed that he held several Watith [[prisoner]]s, prompting the Kilji ruler to ask his ally if any Watith were present and after the Grysk claimed that the Watith who had attacked the ''Springhawk'' had [[Death|died]] during their attack, Nakirre moved his warship to [[Unidentified maneuver (Nakirre)|face]] the Chiss [[heavy cruiser]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==The confrontation==
{{Dialogue|Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo|In my experience, superior wisdom can stand on its own merits. It does not require a warship to force acceptance.|Generalirius Nakirre|You also bring a warship to this place.|Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo|But I do not claim to offer others superior wisdom. Nor do I intend to impose my wisdom upon others.|"Jixtus"|He's trying to goad you into attacking him. Don't let him.|attr=Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo, Generalirius Nakirre, and "Jixtus"|src=<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
The Kilji offered to take the Watith to their [[Unidentified star system (Watith)|home]] although he did not know where that home was located. Nakirre remarked that the Kilji religious beliefs he followed required that he served others; however, Thrawn refused the offer. Angered, the generalirius had his crew prepare his [[war cruiser]] for [[Space warfare|combat]], vowing to enlighten the Chiss species to the Kilji way soon. Thrawn declared that the Chiss were not interested in the Kilji way, remarking that it was not superior to the Chiss religion in an attempt to goad the generalirius into attacking him so that the senior captain could obtain data on the ''Whetstone''{{'s}} capabilities and Nakirre's abilities as the vessel's commander.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

[[Zyzek System Defense]], an organization that protected the Zyzek [[Star system|system]], sent two pairs of [[patrol ship]]s to confront each cruiser while requesting they stand down. As destroying the newcomers would kill their crews, preventing their enlightenment to the Kilji religion, Nakirre complied with the organization's request, resulting in both cruisers' departure from Zyzek.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|You're a fool, Generalirius. The Chiss won't accept your path. Certainly not willingly. Weren't you listening to Thrawn at Zyzek, when he said that the Chiss had their own ancient paths? They won't be interested in yours.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Our path is superior.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''No. Your path is simplistic, self-contradictory, and self-defeating. The only ones who accept it are those the Kiljis are able to force it upon.|Jixtus and Nakirre|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />|audio=Zyzekaftermath.flac|translated=1}}
As Thrawn had not accepted Nakirre's offer and denigrated the Kilji religion, Nakirre sought to enlighten Thrawn to the Kilji way himself. The generalirius and Jixtus visited several of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nation's worlds as they instigated a [[Grysk plot to engineer a Chiss civil war|civil war]] in the Ascendancy, and Nakirre was confident that his religion was superior; however, his Grysk ally reminded him that Thrawn had told him that the Chiss were not interested in the Kilji beliefs at Zyzek.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

After the ''Whetstone'' traveled to the Unknown Regions' [[Sunrise system|"Sunrise" system]], encountering a dead [[Thrawn's fleet|Chiss fleet]] under Thrawn's command, the senior captain told Nakirre that he had arrived too late to bring him the enlightenment the generalirius had promised him at Zyzek.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
The confrontation at Zyzek appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final installment in the [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]] written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
