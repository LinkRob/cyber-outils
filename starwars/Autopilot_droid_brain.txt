'''Autopilot droid brains''' were a type of [[droid brain]] [[autopilot]] used on [[starship]]s. They could automatically [[pilot]] starships and made excellent co-pilots. They typically cost 6,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and notable models included the [[Arakyd Industries]] [[F-9 Integrated Pilot]] and the [[Industrial Automaton]] [[Overwatch Co-pilot]].<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
Autopilot droid brains were first mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Fully Operational Is Online">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/3/29/fully-operational-is-online|text=Fully Operational Is Online}}</ref>
