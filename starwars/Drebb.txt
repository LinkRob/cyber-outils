'''Drebb''' was a member of the [[Pyke Syndicate]] who was stationed on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]] in [[3 ABY]]. That [[Standard year|year]], they were tasked with using the sale of a [[Bev Kranti clam]] to [[Thief|rob]] members of the rival [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate.

==Biography==
{{Quote|You are expandable, the clam is not.|A message to Drebb|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
[[File:BevKrantiClam-Outlaws.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Bev Kranti clam stored at the Pyke Syndicate workshop]]
Drebb was an individual who,<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> had become a member of the [[Pyke Syndicate]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Mon Calamari]] [[information broker]] [[Danka Orsato]] brokered the sale of a valuable [[Bev Kranti clam]] from the [[Pyke]]s to the [[Ashiga Clan]]. However, the Pykes instead planned to use the [[clam]] to scam members of the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate by tricking them into purchasing the clam with no intention of delivering it. To that end, Drebb was tasked with meeting the Crimson Dawn representatives in the [[Mirage (Toshara)|Mirage]] region of Toshara as part of the sale. Prior to the meeting, Drebb was sent a message from their superiors that reminded them not to bring the clam to the meeting; going on to state that Drebb should immediately blast the representatives if they demanded to see the clam prior to the sale and collect any [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] they had on their person. A copy of the message was stored on a [[datapad]], which Drebb brought with them to the meeting.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
Having learned of the Pyke's planned meeting with Crimson Dawn, Orsato hired the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] and her companion [[ND-5]], a [[BX-series droid commando]], to retrieve the clam from the syndicates. The pair arrived at the meeting as it began, and Vess informed the group that she was there to collect the clam on behalf of Orsato. The resulting firefight led to the [[death]] of Drebb, along with all the members of the Pykes and Crimson Dawn present at the meeting. Having survived the exchange, Vess searched the site and found the datapad with the message to Drebb on a nearby [[speeder bike]]. The aftermarket parts used on the Pyke's [[speeder]]s led her to the [[Pyke Syndicate workshop]] on Toshara, where she found the clam and ultimately delivered it to Orsato.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Equipment==
During the meeting with Crimson Dawn, Drebb wore a [[Color|teal]], grey, and brown [[Clothing|outfit]]. They used a speeder bike modified with aftermarket parts to travel to the meeting.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Drebb appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They are one of several Pyke Syndicate members that have to be killed as part of "The Broker" quest, during which the character is identified by the "Instructions for Drebb" datapad. The datapad does not specify which of the Pyke Syndicate enemies specifically is Drebb, making the character's visual appearance unclear.<ref name="Outlaws" />
