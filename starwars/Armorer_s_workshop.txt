The '''Armorer's workshop''' was a workshop used by [[the Armorer]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|armorer-s-workshop|Armorer's workshop}}</ref> around [[9 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate}}</ref> within the [[Nevarro Mandalorian covert|Mandalorian covert]]<ref name="Databank" /> on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Nevarro]]. Using the [[cryo-furnace]] and her many tools within the workshop, such as a [[gravity hammer]] and [[magnetic tongs]], the Armorer constructed [[Mandalorian]] [[Mandalorian armor|armor]]<ref name="Chapter 1" /> and [[whistling bird]]s.<ref name="Chapter 3">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 3: The Sin}}</ref>

The covert, including the workshop within, was left abandoned following the [[Attack on the Tribe|massacre]] of [[the Tribe]] members.<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> Some time later, what remained of the workshop was taken over by [[Aqualish]] [[Unidentified Aqualish gang|thieves]]. The thieves were promptly vanquished when they were attacked by [[Carasynthia Dune|Cara Dune]], the acting [[marshal]] of Nevarro, leaving the workshop vacant once more.<ref name="Chapter 12">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 12: The Siege}}</ref>

Sometime later, the Armorer set a new workshop in a [[Mandalorian covert|covert]] on the [[Glavis Ringworld]], where she reforged a [[beskar spear]] into [[Grogu's chainmail shirt]],<ref name="Return of the Mandalorian">{{BOBF|Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian}}</ref> and another as part of a [[Mandalorian secret covert|covert]] by a body of water on another [[Armorer's planet|planet]].<ref name="The Apostate">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 17: The Apostate}}</ref> Following the Tribe's return to Nevarro to fight off a [[Pirate siege on Nevarro|pirate siege]], the Armorer visited her old workshop.<ref name="The Pirate">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 21: The Pirate}}</ref>
