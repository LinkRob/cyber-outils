'''Bridge guards''' were [[Resistance trooper|soldiers]] of the [[Resistance]] who during the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation]] of [[D'Qar]] in [[34 ABY]], were assigned to protect the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of the Resistance's [[flagship]] the [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] under the command of [[Sergeant]] [[Derham Boyce]]. In front of the situation of that moment, many ground troops from the Resistance were redeployed as extra [[Starship|shipboard]] security. Their task was to be present like vigilantes in case there was any breakdown of order in face that tensions began to rise as events turned increasingly dire.<ref name="TLJ: VD" /> The assigned bridge guards, however, were forced to abandon their positions when the surviving members of the Resistance lead by [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] resolved to escape to [[Crait]] while [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] sacrificed [[Gender|herself]] to destroy the [[First Order]]'s flagship ''[[Supremacy]]'' with the ''Raddus''.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>
