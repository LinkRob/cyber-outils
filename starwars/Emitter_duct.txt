An '''emitter duct''' was a [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] component used on [[starship]]s. When carrying out repairs on the shield generators of<ref name="Lost Found" /> the ''[[Fallon]]'', a [[Trade Federation]] [[WD-2550 Whydah-class Yield Conveyer|WD-2550 ''Whydah''-class yield conveyer]]<ref name="Fallon">{{Databank|fallon-freighter|Fallon freighter}}</ref> in [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> the [[meknek]] [[Fillik]] discovered that the ship's emitter duct was not connected to the [[field projector]]. on the ship's exterior. [[Verosha Aniseya]], another meknek, then used the [[PIP droid]] [[Pip]] to begin welding; however this caused the pressure gauge to spike and led to a small explosion occuring on the ship's exterior nearby.<ref name="Lost Found" />
