In [[18 BBY]], the [[Illumine Kilhorde]], the battle [[fleet]] of the [[Kilji Illumine]] nation, carried out '''conquests of enlightenment''' near the territory of the [[Unknown Regions]] [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nation to spread the Illumine's [[Kilji way of order and enlightenment|religion]]. The Kilhorde conquered several nations, [[planet]]s, and peoples under the directive of the [[Grysk]] operative "[[Jixtus]]," who had allied himself with the Illumine's ruler, [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]].

However, the Chiss [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] had [[Sunrise incident|retrieved]] data on the Kilhorde's weaknesses from the [[Kilji picket cruiser]] [[Hammer (Kilji picket cruiser)|''Hammer'']] and shared it with the [[Paataatus Hiveborn]] nation. The Kilji conquests were taking place to the south and southeast of the Ascendancy, which positioned them north and northwest of the Hiveborn, which the nation deemed unacceptable. As a result, a [[Paataatus attack fleet]] [[Attack on the Illumine Kilhorde|destroyed]] the Kilji fleet.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Then the Kilji Illumine has meant nothing to you?''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''It has meant exactly what it was always intended to mean. Your Kilhorde has blocked any surviving Chiss refugees from fleeing to those worlds and regrouping. [[File:Nine_Ruling_Families_symbols.png|300px|thumb|left|The Illumine Kilhorde attacked several small nations while the Ascendancy's families (emblems pictured) fought each other.]]
The [[Kilji Illumine]], the nation of the [[Unknown Regions]] [[Kilji]] [[species]], sought to enlighten the peoples of the [[universe]] to the Kilji [[religion]], the [[Kilji way of order and enlightenment]]. To achieve this, the Illumine tasked its battle [[fleet]], the [[Illumine Kilhorde]], with visiting the [[Planet|worlds]] of the Unknown Regions and showing them the Kilji religion to spread the Kilji enlightenment.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]], the Illumine's ruler, allied himself with the coordinator of the [[Grysk]] species, an operative known as "[[Jixtus]]," who promised to teach the Illumine the concept of conquest and guide a series of conquests of enlightenment in the Unknown Regions. However, the Chiss [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," [[Sunrise incident|boarded]] the [[Kilji picket cruiser]] [[Hammer (Kilji picket cruiser)|''Hammer'']] while in the Unknown Regions' [[Sunrise system|"Sunrise" system]] and [[Thief|stole]] a [[Kilji data rectangle]] containing data on the Kilhorde's weaknesses in design and combat doctrine, sharing the data with the [[Paataatus Hiveborn]] nation.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

In exchange, Nakirre allowed the Grysk to travel aboard the [[Kilji war cruiser]] ''[[Whetstone]]'', the generalirius's [[flagship]], while the pair instigated a [[Grysk plot to engineer a Chiss civil war|civil war]] in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nation. While the Ascendancy's [[Chiss family|families]] fought each other, the Grysks sent the Kilhorde to the south and southeast of the Ascendancy. They tasked the battle fleet with conquering several small nations consisting of several worlds, with the aim of preventing any Chiss refugees from fleeing to the targeted nations in the process.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==The conquests==
{{Quote|You sent your Kilhorde against several of the small nations to the south and southeast of the Ascendancy, unfortunately for them, that also put them to the north and northwest of the Paataatus Hiveborn. Having a large military force operating that close to their borders was deemed unacceptable.|Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo, to Generalirius Nakirre|audio=Thrawn-PaataatusRevenge.flac|translated=1|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
The battle fleet attacked after the Grysks gave the Kilhorde permission to move against the nations to the south and southeast of the Ascendancy. The worlds and nations targeted by the Illumine's fleet were poor and desperate and willing to accept the Kiljis' enlightenment in return for peace and comfort, and, as a result, the Kilhorde was able to conquer them. The Kilji fleet's [[Field Commander|field commanders]] gave Nakirre details of their victories and mapped out the progress of the conquests in glowing reports of victory.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

As the Kilji conquests took place east and southeast of the Ascendancy, they were positioned north and northwest of the Hiveborn's territory. The Paataatus deemed the military force's operations near their borders unacceptable, and they sent a multipronged [[Paataatus attack fleet]] to destroy it as a favor to Thrawn, who had [[Skirmish above Nettehi|liberated]] the Paataatus from the control of the [[Nikardun Destiny]] hegemony. The attack fleet [[Attack on the Illumine Kilhorde|attacked]] the Kilji fleet, obliterating it and liberating the worlds and peoples it had conquered.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|You did this! Your arrogance&mdash;your refusal to accept the Kilji Illumine as true allies&mdash;your failure to provide adequate support for our conquests of enlightenment!|Nakirre, to "Jixtus"|audio=Nakirredeath.flac|translated=1|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
[[File:Colonial_Station_Chaf.jpg|300px|thumb|right|Generalirius Nakirre feared that his generals had extended their conquests to Colonial Station Chaf.]]
After the destruction of the Kilhorde, Nakirre did not receive any reports from his field commanders, prompting him to speculate that his [[general]]s had "foolishly" extended their conquests to [[Colonial Station Chaf]], a Chiss world. He feared that that was the reason for his commanders' silence, as the conquest of Colonial Station Chaf would have disrupted the operation of the [[long-range triad transmitter]] there, which was the only link between the generalirius and his forces.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

Shortly after, the ''Whetstone'' traveled to the Sunrise [[Star system|system]], where it encountered a [[Thrawn's fleet|fleet]] of Chiss [[warship]]s under Thrawn's command. The senior captain told Nakirre that the Illumine's fleet had been destroyed by the Hiveborn, prompting the generalirius to snap at Jixtus, telling the Grysk that he had failed to provide adequate support for the conquests of enlightenment before the Grysk [[Death|mortally shot]] him, as the Kilhorde's destruction rendered the generalirius useless to Jixtus.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
The conquests of enlightenment were mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final volume of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
