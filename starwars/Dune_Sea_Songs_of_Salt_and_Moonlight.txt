"'''Dune Sea Songs of Salt and Moonlight'''" is a short story featuring [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] that was written by [[Thea Guanzon]] as part of the anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]''. The story was published on [[August 29]], [[2023]].
