'''Corlac''', also known as '''TK-605''', was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as a [[stormtrooper]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] in the [[Battle of Jakku]]. After gunning down their [[corporal]], Corlac and the stormtrooper [[Terex]] buried their [[Stormtrooper|armor]] and spent a year building an "[[Ugly]]" [[starship]] to escape [[Jakku]]. While Terex was still determined to rebuild the Empire, Corlac wanted to form a criminal empire with the help of the [[Gender|female]] criminals [[Wenda]] and [[Bett]]. After gaining access to the [[Rothana Imperial Shipyards]], Corlac and the ladies conspired to assassinate Terex. However, Terex discovered their plot and blasted them to death with the ''[[Carrion Spike]]''{{'s}} [[laser cannon]]s.

==Biography==
===Battle of Jakku===
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Corlac signed up to serve the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[stormtrooper]]. He was given the designation "TK-605." TK-605, along with fellow [[trooper]] [[Terex|TK-603]] and their [[corporal]] [[Battle of Jakku|fought]] the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Defense Force|forces]] at [[Jakku]]. As the battle ended in favor of the New Republic, TK-605 realized that the Empire had lost. When the corporal ordered them to fight to the last man under the threat of death, TK-605 shot him dead. When TK-603 expressed shock, TK-605 responded that he did not feel like fighting to the last man and asked TK-603 if he agreed.<ref name="Poe 8" />

[[File:StormtroopersCorlacTerex-PoeDameron8.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Corlac and Terex as stormtroopers.]]
When TK-603 expressed confusion and asked what were their orders, TK-605 pointed to a stricken [[Star Destroyer]] and said that the Empire was dead and there was nobody to order them around anymore. Taking off his helmet, TK-605 said that he did not work for them anymore. When TK-603 asked why he was taking off his [[stormtrooper armor]], Corlac responded that the New Republic would be hunting down Imperials and they had a better chance of surviving without their armor. TK-603 realized that TK-605 was right. As the two buried their armor, Corlac asked TK-603 what was his name. TK-603 reluctantly introduced himself as Terex.<ref name="Poe 8" />

The two men then hid until nightfall before heading to a settlement to find work. When Terex expressed unease about abandoning his armor, Corlac agreed that it was little more than "trash." Corlac and Terec spent about a [[6 ABY|year]] building an "[[Ugly]]" out of salvaged parts including the head of an [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT walker]] and [[TIE/IN interceptor|TIE Interceptor]] wings. The two men came to regard each other as squad mates. While Terex was still determined to rejoin the Imperial remnants and help put down the Rebellion, Corlac was not interested and wanted to travel to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet of [[Kaddak]] where had some contacts. Terex agreed to come provided he could bring his armor.<ref name="Poe 8" />

===Joining the pirates===
On Kaddak, Corlac re-established contacts with the female crime bosses [[Wenda]] and [[Bett]], who ran a large ship repair and salvage operation. While Corlac had abandoned the Empire, Terex was still determined to rebuild the Empire. knowing that Terex had once worked at the [[Rothana Imperial Shipyards]], Corlac manipulated Terex into helping him and his criminal associates Wenda and Bett gain access to the shipyards under the pretext of bringing back the Empire.<ref name="Poe 9">[[Poe Dameron 9|''Poe Dameron'' 9]]</ref> In truth, Corlac and his associates planned to use the shipyard to obtain ships for their [[pirate]] fleet.<ref name="Poe 10">[[Poe Dameron 10|''Poe Dameron'' 10]]</ref>

[[File:PlotToKillTerex-PoeDameron10.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Corlac, Bett and Wenda planned to get rid of Terex]]
After some attempts, Terex managed to gain Corlac, Wenda, and Bett access to the shipyard's repair bay. While the shipyard had been abandoned, it was still being maintained on auto-security mode by [[droid]]s and other automated systems.<ref name="Poe 9" /> By [[7 ABY]], Corlac and his associates had taken control of the former Rothana Imperial shipyards. While Terex was repairing [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s former starship ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', Corlac informed Wenda and Bett that their plan to rebuild the Empire was a ruse to get Terex's help. Since the ''Carrion Spike'' had cloaking capabilities, Corlac planned to use it for piracy operations. Once Terex had fixed the ship, Corlac planned to kill Terex, regarding him as little more than a tool.<ref name="Poe 10" />

Unknown to Corlac and his criminal associates, Terex was eavesdropping on them via [[comlink]]. He activated the ''Carrion Spike''{{'s}} weapons systems and obliterated the shipyard's control station, killing Corlac, Wenda, and Bett. Realizing that the Empire was finally gone, Terex took control of Corlac, Wenda, and Bett's gang. Terex became the leader of the [[Ranc Gang]] until his pirates intercepted a shipment bound for an Imperial remnant known as the [[First Order]]. Terex subsequently left the Ranc Gang and joined the First Order's [[First Order Security Bureau|Security Bureau]] as a [[spy]].<ref name="Poe 10" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Honestly, I can't wait. If I hear him talk about the noble Imperial spirit one more time...''"<br />"''Isn't he your partner? Didn't you two serve together in Palpatine's army?''"<br />"''I never served anyone, Bett, I'm out for me. Terex, though... yeah. He served. [[File:RancGangRothanaShipyards-PoeDameron9.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Corlac, Terex, and the rest of the Ranc Gang]]
Corlac was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] with blond hair and dark eyes. While Corlac served the Empire as a stormtrooper and claimed that Terex was his partner, he was only concerned with looking out for himself.<ref name="Poe 10" /> Following the Battle of Jakku, Corlac was pragmatic enough to realize that the Empire was a lost cause and cast aside his stormtrooper armor. Corlac also convinced his friend Terex to do the same. Corlac and Terex used their mechanical and scavenging skills to cobble together an "Ugly" out of discarded ship and vehicle parts.<ref name="Poe 8" /> Corlac was also a manipulator who played on Terex's desire to build the Empire in order to achieve his own goal of building a criminal empire. Corlac was even willing to kill his former comrade in arms to achieve these goals. However, Corlac in his pride underestimated Terex's vindictiveness.<ref name="Poe 10" />
