'''Fribulation tanks''' were used by [[dust farmer]]s on the moon [[Raspar-6]]. After a [[Atomic blast on Raspar-6|nuclear blast]] on Raspar-6, the farmers were advised to use [[Hydroponics/Legends|hydroponics]] in fribulation tanks for some time, which could last until mid-[[Aint]] or early [[Nnters]].<ref name="MAGL">''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
