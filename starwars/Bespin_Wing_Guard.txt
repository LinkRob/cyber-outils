The '''Bespin Wing Guard''' was the formal [[law enforcement agency]] of [[Cloud City]], a [[mining]] colony floating in the [[atmosphere]] of the [[planet]] [[Bespin]]. Its members were known as '''cloud officers''' and [[Clothing|dressed]] in dark [[Color|blue]] uniforms with red and gold trim, featuring dark blue headgear and wielded [[Relby K-23 blaster pistol]]s.

==History==
[[File:GratitudeToLando-2020StarWars4.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Members of the Wing Guard thank Lando Calrissian]]
During the [[Iron Blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]], the [[Ivax Syndicate]] sought to procure one of the Wing Guards' [[cloud car]]s, and a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] assisted them in doing so.<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Run: "Cloud Car Procurement"</ref> [[Nikka Xizen]] worked as part of the Wing Guard but eventually retired from it, instead setting up her own [[Nikka Xizen's private security service|private security service]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref> [[Zaina]] owned a cloud officer outfit she could wear within the [[Grand Arena]] as a [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|hunter]].<ref name="SWHunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>
