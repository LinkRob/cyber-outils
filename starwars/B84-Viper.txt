The '''B84-Viper''' was a [[cruiser]] used by criminal factions during the [[Imperial Era]] including the [[Ashiga Clan]], the [[Pyke Syndicate]], and [[pirate]]s. One [[Pyke Syndicate]] B84-Viper and Pyke [[JLF-19 Junkros Fighter]]s battled a pair of [[Mining Guild Freighter]]s from the [[Hutt Clan]] and a group of [[ARC-200 Wing Cutter Fighter|ARC-200 Wing Cutter Fighters]] in [[Tatooine]]'s orbit<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] and [[ND-5]] stumbled on the battle as they arrived from [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

The Viper was armed with six [[laser cannon]] [[turret]]s and [[missile launcher]]s and had six [[sublight engine]]s along with a [[hyperdrive]] system. The B84 was fitted with a [[hangar]] bay that had port and starboard openings and could hold at least four [[starfighter]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The B84-Viper first appeared in ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', a [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Concept design for the cruiser was done by Concept Artist [[Arnaud Kleindienst]] under Lead Concept Artist [[Samuel De Vos]], Associate Art Directors [[Marthe Jonkers]] and [[Gerard Miley]], and Art Director [[Benedikt Podlesnigg]]. Work on the final assets was done by [[Fredrik Andersson]] and 3D Environment Artist [[Pablo Gallego]],<ref name="Arnaud ArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=fredrik_a|url=artwork/OvG4nJ|name=Fredrik Andersson}}</ref> who was part of Ubisoft's vehicle team. The capitalization of the B84-Viper was confirmed on Gallego's ArtStation upload.<ref name="Gallego ArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=pablogallego3d|url=artwork/nJEVVO|name=Pablo Gallego}}</ref>
