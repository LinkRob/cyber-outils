"'''Dug Out'''" is a comic story published in [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic 6.19|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic'' 6.19]]. The story was continued in "[[Outgunned]]" in the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic 6.20|following issue]] and later published in [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Magazine 6|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Magazine 6]].
