The '''40th Emergence''',<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> or the '''fortieth Emergence''', was the fortieth of the [[Emergences]], fallout of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] in which fragments of the [[freighter]] ''[[Legacy Run]]'' shot out of [[hyperspace]] seemingly at random. The fortieth Emergence was a portion of the ''Legacy Run''{{'s}} bridge that contained its [[Black box|flight recorder]]. As a result, a skirmish ensued as members of the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Galactic Republic]] personnel, who were hoping to retrieve the device, fought members of the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s who were attempting to destroy it.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
