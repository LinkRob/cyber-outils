'''''Darth Maul – Black, White & Red'' 2''' is the second issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] anthology miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Darth Maul – Black, White & Red]]''. The issue was written by [[Mark Russell]], illustrated by [[Carlos Nieto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[May 29]], 2024.

==Publisher's summary==
''MAYHEM AT THE MOONBENDER COLONY! Who are THE REMAINDERS, and what threat are they to LORD PALPATINE? DARTH MAUL is sent to conquer a mining colony single-handed, but the unexpected opposition becomes an intense battle of life and death! One of ''STAR WARS''' most famous villains learns one of his most valuable lessons!''

==Plot summary==
In the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]], on the [[Moonbender Colony]], a group of former [[bounty hunter]]s called the [[Remainders]] live among one another. One named [[Korvik]] is a [[doctor]], and he treats a younger [[Twi'lek]] girl. Korvik narrates that most of them were in the [[blaster]] business before, and adjusting to regular life was difficult, so they were sent to the remote Moonbender Colony in the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]]. After he finishes his examination on the girl, his [[medical droid|assistant droid]] [[Bennie]] gives her some [[candy]] on her way out.

Korvik continues narrating that they were sent to the colony to find solitude and peace, but also to be whatever the people needed from them. He runs the only [[medcenter]] in the colony, and the Remainders also serve as the legal system; munitions expert [[Barva-Zul]] serves as the [[sheriff]]. The Remainders defend their colony from occasional [[pirate|raiders]], even though there is not much to [[thief|steal]] since there are only about one hundred residents. This day, however, Bennie informs [[General]] [[Zandloss]] of an approaching [[starship|ship]]. 

The ''[[Scimitar]]'' touches down, and the [[Sith]] [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] [[Darth]] [[Maul]] approaches the colony, causing the [[Council (Remainders)|Council]] of the Remainders to gather and discuss their next move. Zandloss presents a message ordering the Remainders to leave the colony immediately by order of the Republic, but they are hesitant and do not believe its authenticity. To test this, they decide to dispatch a [[Courier droid|messenger droid]] to [[Coruscant]], but Maul's [[DRK-1 Dark Eye probe droid|probe droid]] shoots it down, giving the Remainders their answer. Realizing that the orders were fake, the Remainders prepare to defend the colony and dispatch two [[soldier|sentries]] to take their visitor into custody. 

Outside at the main gate, Maul is approached by the sentries but swiftly [[death|kills]] them both using [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. With this, the council receives a second message from Maul reading "leave or die". The council seriously considers this, but know that it is their job to defend the few remaining people of the colony, so they arm themselves and prepare for a fight. As Maul enters the [[Moonbender Colony tower|tower]], which is the [[Administrator|administrative]] center of the colony, he has to go through "[[The Tunnel]]", which is [[magnetic seal|magnetically sealed]], and the Remainders fire upon him from behind sealed doors. With the shots bouncing all over the walls at many different angles, Maul nearly becomes overwhelmed but is able to use [[the Force]] to get to the doors and cut through them in time. Realizing that the tunnel was lost, Zandloss stays behind and sacrifices himself in order to allow the others to fall back, but Maul continues to push forward and manages to kill the [[Rodian]] [[Zheemo]]. 

Meanwhile, Korvik is delivered a new patient that has just had his leg cut off, and as he gets to work, Korvik narrates that he's only the doctor because there's no one else, and he actually has very little medical knowledge. Down multiple levels, the oldest Remainder, [[Jeq]], who is rumored to have been an [[acrobat]] and is very skilled in close combat, engages Darth Maul with the sheriff Barva-Zul. Overwhelmed, Maul briefly flees and hides in the ventilation system, but he uses his stealth to quietly take out Jeq. Barva-Zul believes to have caught Maul when she comes up behind a man in black robes, but to her horror she discovers that underneath is the [[blood]]y body of Jeq. With this distraction, Maul leaps down from the ceiling and stabs the sheriff, and while she is left to die on the ground, Korvik narrates that every Remainder brought their own history and way of fighting, as well as their own weaknesses. As Maul prepares to move on, Barva-Zul uses a remote to set off [[detonator|explosives]], killing herself but catching the Sith in the explosion. 

Up in the medcenter, Korvik is unable to save the patient he was working on, and knows that the battle will soon be over judging by the amount of wounded coming in to see him. Outside, Maul savagely makes his way through a hallway of defending Remainders, cutting them all down until he reaches the door to the medcenter. He stabs Korvik through the chest, and Maul leaves the colony while the man dies out, believing that his job is finished. Contacting [[Darth Sidious]], Maul informs his [[Sith Master|master]] that all of the colony's defenders have been killed, and he learns that this mission was simply training for him; a test to see how he would fare against different combat styles. 

As Maul leaves aboard the ''Scimitar'', Korvik uses his final moments to free the young Twi'lek from her hiding spot in the medcenter. He then narrates that while they were called the Remainders, none of them remain and maybe the term applied for those that they fought for; those which they'd hoped to survive. In a [[Battle for Moonbender|battle]] where darkness always wins, victory is measured in whatever light escapes.
