'''Felne 6''', also referred to as '''Felne Six''' or the '''sixth moon of Felne''', was a [[moon]] in a [[Felne system|star system]].<ref name="TOTF" />

==Description==
Felne 6 had a breathable atmosphere and was covered in a rain forest. It had no valuable things for exportation, but the atmosphere made it good for certain kinds of long-term scientific experimentation.<ref name="TOTF" />

==History==
In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|TOTF}}</ref> the [[Unidentified Duros outpost commander|project leader]] of a [[science outpost]] contacted the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] and the [[Jedi]] to report a [[Drengir]] infection on the moon.<ref name="TOTF" />
