The '''Aakaash system''' was located in the [[Oplovis sector]]. According to [[Mostima]], the population of the system was almost completely [[human]] with a population of 174.2 billion. It was largely independent from the [[Galactic Republic]] despite being a member world and was ruled by the [[Land & Sky Corporation]]. The population was seen as rather xenophobic compared to most worlds of the Republic. The star system had its own [[Land & Sky currency|currency]]. The system also had its own [[Land & Sky language|language]] that was considered to be very difficult to learn.

==History==
The original colonization of the Aakaash system was led by the [[Ma family]] millennia<ref name="TVoSD" /> by [[200 BBY]].<ref name="Dates">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. The novel states that the disappearance of the [[Gotal]] happened about a hundred [[Standard year|years]] before, thus the event took place around [[300 BBY]].</ref> The original pioneers included not only humans, but also various [[alien]]s including [[Twi'lek]], [[Ithorian]]s, and [[Weequay]]. However, by the time the system had developed [[City|cities]] these groups had disappeared. The Ma family ruled the system for some time before the [[Nan family|Nan]], [[Xia family|Xia]], and [[Chen family|Chen]] families also rose to prominence from a former position of vassalage. It was the Nan family which planned and led the colonization and development of the [[planet]] [[Wind]] after a meeting on [[Lake 101]].<ref name="TVoSD" />

Around [[300 BBY]],<ref name="Dates" /> a suspect disappearance of a group of [[Gotal]] occurred without a proper explanation from the ruling corporation. This caused many Gotal to be wary of the sector to the extent that it was extremely rare for one to find a Gotal in the system.<ref name="TVoSD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Aakaash system first mentioned in the [[Wikipedia:Written Chinese|Chinese]] web novel ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''<ref name="TVoSD" /> written by "[[His Majesty the King]]," within its eleven chapter, which was released on [[December 22]], [[2020]].<ref name="Chapter 11">{{WebCite|url=https://www.qidian.com/chapter/1015950219/624183412|text=第11章 新任务、新星系 _《星球大战：白银誓约》小说在线阅读|author=[[His Majesty the King]]|date=2020-12-22|language=[[Wikipedia:Simplified Chinese characters|Simplified Chinese]]|archivedate=20260604153330}}</ref> It later appeared in the thirteenth chapter of the web novel. The star system's name was written in [[Wikipedia:Simplified Chinese characters|simplified Chinese]] as "乾星系" (gān xīng xì).<ref name="Chapter 13">{{WebCite|url=https://www.qidian.com/chapter/1015950219/624331727|text=第13章 我有种不祥的预感 _《星球大战：白银誓约》小说在线阅读|author=[[His Majesty the King]]|date=2020-12-23|language=[[Wikipedia:Simplified Chinese characters|Simplified Chinese]]|archivedate=20260607155054}}</ref> In the context of Chinese philosophy, it means "Heaven system," but also identifies the system as "乾坤星系" (gān kūn xīng xì) in chapter twenty. Akasha is a Sanskrit word referring to the "ether" or "space," a similar concept in Indian and Buddhist philosophy, and is a probable origin of the official English naming of this system as Aakaash. Notably, the major celestial and orbiting bodies of the system (Heaven, Land, Mountain, Thunder, Water, Fire, Wind, and Lake) correspond to the eight trigrams of Chinese philosophy.
