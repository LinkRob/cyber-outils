The Danjar sector, Mayagil sector, and parts of the Seswenna sector are now hereby granted official Nihil status, and we welcome their citizens into our fold.|Rhil Dairo's broadcast on the expansion of the Stormwall|<ref name="EOD" />}}
The '''Danjar sector''' was a [[sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that bordered the [[Mayagil sector|Mayagil]], [[Tamarin sector|Tamarin]], [[Tarabba sector|Tarabba]], and [[Sluis sector]]s. It contained the [[Chagaria system|Chagaria]], [[Engebo system|Engebo]], [[Entooine system|Entooine]], [[Hurrim system|Hurrim]], [[Lonjair Prime system|Lonjair Prime]], [[Polordio system|Polordio]], [[Qexis system|Qexis]], [[Queyta system|Queyta]], [[Rhamsis Callo system|Rhamsis Callo]], and [[Syboona system]]s.

During the [[High Republic Era]], it was part of [[Galactic Republic]] [[Realspace|space]]. In [[228 BBY]], the Danjar sector became part of the [[Occlusion Zone]]&mdash;an area of space sealed behind a [[Stormwall|barrier]] preventing [[hyperspace]] travel&mdash;following its [[Expansion of the Stormwall|expansion]], which the captured [[journalist]] [[Rhil Dairo]] announced via live broadcast. Later the same [[Standard year|year]], the [[Mission to Planet X (Jedi Order)|Stormwall's fall]] dissolved the Occlusion Zone.

==Description==
The Danjar sector was a [[Realspace|space]] [[sector]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Outer Rim" /> and the [[Western Reaches]]<ref name="WesternReaches" /> that bordered the [[Mayagil sector|Mayagil]], [[Tamarin sector|Tamarin]], [[Tarabba sector|Tarabba]], and [[Sluis sector]]s.<ref name="THRA6">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 6|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 6]]</ref> It contained the [[Chagaria system|Chagaria]], [[Engebo system|Engebo]], [[Entooine system|Entooine]], [[Hurrim system|Hurrim]], [[Lonjair Prime system|Lonjair Prime]], [[Polordio system|Polordio]], [[Qexis system|Qexis]], [[Queyta system|Queyta]], [[Rhamsis Callo system|Rhamsis Callo]], and [[Syboona system]]s, which were situated in grid squares M-19, N-18, and N-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==History==
[[File:RhilDairo.png|thumb|left|240px|Rhil Dairo announced the Stormwall's expansion&mdash;which enveloped the Danjar sector behind its barrier&mdash;via live broadcast.]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], the Danjar sector was part of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="THRA1" /> In [[230 BBY]], the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization [[Deployment of the stormseeds|formed]] the [[Occlusion Zone]], an area of space that was situated behind the [[Stormwall]] barrier preventing [[hyperspace]] travel.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Afterward, the Danjar sector was one of the sectors forming the border of the territory controlled by the Republic, with several of its neighboring sectors cut off behind the Stormwall.<ref name="THRA1" />

In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Stormwall Date">{{StarlightDate|[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4]]|one [[Standard year|year]] and two weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}} Since the events of [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 6|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 6]] are set immediately following the fourth issue's events&mdash;and depict the [[expansion of the Stormwall]]&mdash;the latter event must take place in the same year.</ref> the Stormwall [[Expansion of the Stormwall|expanded]] further into Republic space as retribution for an unsuccessful [[Assault on the Stormwall|assault]] on the barrier by the Republic and [[Jedi Order]]. [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]]'s expansion of the Stormwall was announced via live broadcast by the captured [[journalist]] [[Rhil Dairo]], who had been forced to work for the Nihil. In her message, she indicated that the Occlusion Zone now enveloped the Danjar sector, the Mayagil sector, and parts of the [[Seswenna sector]]. The [[pilot]] [[Belin]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]], and the [[droid]] [[KC-78]] listened to the broadcast from the pilot's [[Belin's ship|starship]], saddened by the news.<ref name="EOD" />

Later the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="TrialsDate">{{TrialsoftheJediDate|23|the [[Nihil]]'s surrender}}</ref> the Nihil surrendered to the Republic following the [[Mission to Planet X (Jedi Order)|Stormwall barrier's fall]], which dissolved the Occlusion Zone as a territory. The worlds of the Occlusion Zone,<ref name="TOTJ">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref> which included those in the Danjar sector,<ref name="EOD" /> were welcomed back into the Republic if they wished. However, some opted to retain their independence, while others remained under the control of individual Nihil [[warlord]]s.<ref name="TOTJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:TheHighRepublic-TheEyeofDarkness-cover.jpg|thumb|right|250px|In the current canon, the Danjar sector was introduced via mention in ''The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness''.]]
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Danjar sector was first mentioned in the novel [[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness|''The Eye of Darkness'']], written by [[George Mann]]<ref name="EOD" /> and published on [[November 14]], [[2023]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's Phase III.<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The sector was later given its first visual depiction via a map in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 1|first issue]] of the 2023 [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']]. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Harvey Tolibao]] and [[Nick Brokenshire]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="THRA1" /> as part of Phase III on [[December 6]] of the same year.<ref name="Phase" />

The [[Danjar sector/Legends|Danjar sector]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in "Sluis Sector Secedes; Seswenna Restates Loyalty," an article<ref name="HNN">{{HoloNetNewsWeb|49|news/13328_4.html|Sluis Sector Secedes; Seswenna Restates Loyalty}}</ref> written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[Paul Ens]]<ref name="credits">{{HoloNetNewsWeb||credits.html|Credits}}</ref> and published in [[HoloNet News Vol. 531 49|''HoloNet News Vol. 531'' #49]] on the [[HoloNet News (original website)|HoloNet News website]]<ref name="HNN" /> on [[March 28]], [[2002]].<ref name="HNN49 date">{{JCFCite|url=posts/6217551|board=Literature|thread=The Official HoloNetNews Discussion Thread|user=Dev Sibwarra|date=2002-03-28|time=4:24 PM|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20200305125429/https://boards.theforce.net/threads/the-official-holonetnews-discussion-thread.5990764/page-13}}</ref> The [[StarWars.com]] [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Jason Fry]], placed the [[Rhamsis Callo system/Legends|Rhamsis Callo system]]<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>&mdash;and therefore the [[planet]] [[Rhamsis Callo]]<ref name="TRG1">{{WEGCite|story=The Silent Witness|book=Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game (first edition)}}</ref>&mdash;in the Danjar sector.<ref name="EAOC" /> Thus, the [[roleplaying game]] adventure idea "[[The Silent Witness]]," which was authored by [[Ken Rolston]] and published in the [[1987]] [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game (first edition)|rulebook]] for the first edition of [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', retroactively became the sector's first appearance.<ref name="TRG1" />

The Online Companion also placed both the [[Entoo system]] and [[Hurrim Base]] within the Danjar sector.<ref name="EAOC" /> The Entoo system&mdash;and therefore the sector's&mdash;first visual appearance came in "[[Bantha Cannon]]," an adventure for ''Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game'' that was released in the [[Challenge 70|seventieth issue]] of the [[Challenge (magazine)|''Challenge'']] magazine in [[June]] [[1993]].  Since that adventure was released outside of the [[Lucas Licensing]] process,<ref name="Bantha Cannon">{{ChallengeCite|70|Bantha Cannon}}</ref> it is considered non-canon with respect to the Legends continuity.<ref name="Unlicensed">{{UnlicensedStatus}}</ref> Meanwhile, Hurrim Base debuted in the [[1999]] [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Alliance]]'', developed by [[Totally Games]], thus retroactively marking the Danjar sector's first unambiguously canonical visual appearance.<ref name="XWING">''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Alliance]]''</ref>
