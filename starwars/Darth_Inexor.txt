'''Darth Inexor''' was a [[Humanoid/Legends|humanoid]] [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] who was active during the reign of [[Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Game Informer" /> between [[130 ABY]] and [[138 ABY]].<ref name="Dating note">{{KraytDates}}</ref> Inexor had [[Color/Legends|red]] [[Eye/Legends|eyes]], gray-green skin, and a completely [[Cybernetics/Legends|cybernetic]] body save for their head. Inexor's body was comprised of red and black [[Armor/Legends|armor]] with green glowing accents near the torso and face. Inexor sported four cybernetic [[Limb/Legends|arms]] and four additional smaller hook-like appendages. They wielded two red-bladed [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsabers]] with black [[Lightsaber hilt/Legends|hilts]].<ref name="Game Informer" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Inexor was a character created for [[Red Fly Studio]]'s [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Battle of the Sith Lords]]''. Inexor's design was created by concept artist [[Thomas A. Szakolczay]] at some point prior to the game's cancellation in [[2011]].<ref name="Game Informer" />
