'''Druckenwell''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Outlaws" /> located in the [[Druckenwell system]] in the [[Doldur sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was situated in grid square P-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]] along the [[Corellian Run]], at the head of [[Bothawui–Druckenwell hyperlane|another hyperlane]] that linked it to [[Bothawui]].<ref name="AVG" /> Around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Beyond Victory Date">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> a job listing for a [[blaster]] [[engineer]] was put out by the [[BlasTech Industries]], which would require a relocation to Druckenwell.<ref name="Beyond Victory">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref> A pair of [[Thief|thieves]] who stole [[skiff]]s on Druckenwell had joined the [[Rokana Raiders]] [[pirate]] group<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> While the pair guarded the [[Khepi Tomb]] in the [[Khepi system]] together for the pirates, one recalled the skiff thefts on Druckenwell as better [[time]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />
