In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s undertook an '''attack''' at an [[Unidentified outpost (Nihil)|outpost]] near<ref name="Mission to Disaster" /> the [[Galactic Frontier]] [[planet]]<ref name="Out of the Shadows">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref> [[Tiikae]]. Within [[Standard hour|hours]] of the Nihil's attack, it was reported to the [[Galactic Republic]] [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]]. [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] was told about the attack when she contacted [[Jedi Master]] [[Estala Maru]] on Starlight Beacon. Rwoh then asked Maru if anyone had gone missing as a result of the attack, to which he stated that none had.<ref name="Mission to Disaster" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack at an outpost near Tiikae was mentioned in the [[2022]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Mission to Disaster" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
