During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Twi'lek Resistance]] leader [[General]] [[Cham Syndulla]] operated out of a '''command outpost'''. Situated in a [[cave]] hidden in one of [[Ryloth]]'s extensive canyon networks, the outpost was well-hidden and difficult to find, keeping it secret from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] who had [[Battle of Ryloth|occupied]] the [[planet]]. After the end of the war and the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Cham's daughter [[Hera Syndulla|Hera]] hid at the outpost with her [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]] after her [[parent]]s were [[Rescue of Hera Syndulla (Ryloth)|arrested]] by the Empire. Calling on her new friend [[Omega]] for help mounting a [[Rescue on Ryloth|rescue mission]], Hera had Omega and [[Clone Force 99]] meet her at the outpost.<ref name="Rescue on Ryloth" />
