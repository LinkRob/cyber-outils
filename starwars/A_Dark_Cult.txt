'''A Dark Cult''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie-in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[April 11]], [[2002]] and took the form of a transmission purported to be intercepted from the [[Almas Academy]] describing the activities of Sith cultists in the [[Cularin system/Legends|Cularin system]].
