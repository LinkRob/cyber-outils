The '''Ferrix Entry Ferry Service''' was a ferrying service that was active on the [[planet]] [[Ferrix]]. The service utilized multiple [[HFY-3920 shuttle ferry|shuttle ferries]] to transport individuals between [[Ferrix shuttle port|stations]] scattered around the planet and the main [[Ferrix City|settlement's]] [[Ferrix Terminal|Terminal]]. The name of the organization was written on the side of the ferries in [[Bazeese script]].<ref name="Reckoning" />
