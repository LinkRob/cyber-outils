The '''Buried Vault''' was a [[Miyuki Trade League]] vault in the [[Tusken March]] area of the [[Western Dune Sea]] on [[Tatooine]], a [[desert]] [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[Jet Kordo]] had left a message for the [[droid]] [[H1-0N]] detailing that they moved to the [[Perched Vault|other vault]] on Tatooine after [[Raatha]] claimed that the vault had been compromised.

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|Canon=1}}
During 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> [[Kay Vess]] located the vault using [[Jet Kordo's Holotracker]] and a [[hologram disc]] she recovered in the [[Pillar Forest]] region of Tatooine. While in the vault Vess took a [[jacket]], a [[X-FP-C28 ship computer]] and the message for H1-0N.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Buried Vault appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It was one of several vaults that the player can find in the game as part of the "Jet Kordo's Legacy" quest.<ref name="Outlaws" />
