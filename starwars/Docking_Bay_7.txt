'''Docking Bay 7''' was a docking bay located along [[Merchant Row (Batuu)|Merchant Row]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]], located in close proximity to [[Black Spire]]. It was the primary landing space for food freighters, which delivered a lot of "tasty fare" sold below in the building's food sale area, which was known as [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>

The docking bay served as a roof for the entire structure and could service ships roughly the size of a [[Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport]]. A mechanical arm was installed and could move cargo from the landing pad and down into the cargo area.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
The docking bay was first unveiled during the D23 Expo for [[2017]] via a scale model of the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] park area. The exterior of the building also appeared briefly in a scene added to the ''[[Star Tours: The Adventures Continue]]'' attraction on [[November 17]], [[2017]]. The docking bay, alongside Black Spires, was named on trading cards available at [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] [[December 16]], [[2017]] "Galactic Nights" session, which covered locations for the upcoming attraction. It shared a card with Black Spires.<ref name="WDWNewsToday">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2018/01/photos-star-wars-galaxys-edge-trading-cards-provide-new-details-guests-will-find-batuu-2019|text=PHOTOS: Star Wars – Galaxy's Edge Trading Cards Provide New Details of What Guests Will Find on Batuu in 2019|work=WDW News Today|archivedate=20180510032057}}</ref>

On the landing pad, three boxes can be seen in the unloading process. These boxes are numbered for each of the [[Original trilogy]] movies' release year.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />
