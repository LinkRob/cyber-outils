'''A2-D6''' was a [[pilot droid]] that served the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Defense Coalition]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. When the Republic and the [[Jedi Order]] attempted to [[Assault on the Stormwall|breach]] the [[Stormwall]] [[hyperspace]] barrier that surrounded the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders']] [[Occlusion Zone]] territory, A2-D6 served as a sacrificial [[droid]] [[pilot]]. Its memory backed up and transferred into a replacement housing, A2-D6 piloted the [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] ''[[Outpath]]'' to test the breach created in the Stormwall. Although the ''Outpath'' successfully made it across the barrier, the Stormwall repaired itself shortly afterward, and several Republic and [[Jedi]] [[Starship|vessels]] that attempted to follow were destroyed.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Can they hear me?''"<br />"''Yes.''"<br />"''This is Master Elzar Mann. We have a breach. Go. Go now!|Elzar Mann and Aliss, on the ''Outpath''{{'s}} success at crossing the Stormwall|<ref name="The Eye of Darkness" />}}
The [[pilot droid]] designated A2-D6 served the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Defense Coalition]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="The Eye of Darkness" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]] to [[230 BBY]]. As ''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'' is set in the following [[Standard year|year]], its events must take place in [[229 BBY]].</ref> the Republic and the [[Jedi Order]] [[Assault on the Stormwall|attempted]] to breach the [[Stormwall]], the [[hyperspace]] barrier that surrounded the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization's [[Occlusion Zone]] territory in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]], by exploiting a glitch in one of the [[stormseed]] buoys that powered the barrier. The plan, formulated by [[technician]] [[Keven Tarr]] and [[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]], involved destroying the stormseeds that surrounded the glitching buoy with the hope that the malfunction might slow down the Stormwall's self-repair systems long enough to send a [[fleet]] through the hole in the barrier. As part of the plan, A2-D6 was to serve as a sacrificial [[droid]] [[pilot]], with its memory being backed up, replicated, and transferred into a replacement housing.<ref name="The Eye of Darkness" />

A2-D6 was stationed aboard the [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] ''[[Outpath]]'', which was to be the first [[starship]] to attempt to enter the breach point once the buoys were destroyed, with the remainder of the Republic and [[Jedi]] fleet to follow if it was successful. Once the fleet&mdash;composed of twenty Defense Coalition vessels, eight [[Jedi Vector]]s, and the [[Pacifier-class sector patrol cruiser|''Pacifier''-class patrol ship]] ''[[Tractate]]''&mdash;had gathered, the Longbeam ''[[Shattersong]]'' and five other vessels destroyed the designated buoys. The ''Shattersong''{{'s}} [[chief communications officer]], [[Aliss]], gave the command to the ''Outpath''. A2-D6 responded in affirmation and the ''Outpath'' entered hyperspace, successfully arriving on the other side. After receiving a transmission from the ''Outpath'', Mann, aboard the ''Shattersong'', ordered the rest of the fleet to begin making the jump to hyperspace; however, the Stormwall repaired itself sooner than expected, and a number of the vessels, unable to disengage their [[hyperdrive]]s in time, were destroyed when they attempted to pass through the barrier.<ref name="The Eye of Darkness" />

==Characteristics==
A droid pilot, A2-D6 communicated in a muted sequence of beeps to indicate its understanding when it was ordered to jump to hyperspace in the ''Outpath''.<ref name="The Eye of Darkness" />
