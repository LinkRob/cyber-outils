'''Florx Biggles''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Ugnaught]] [[Technician|mechanic]] that loved [[droid]]s. After [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] was attacked by his own [[protocol droid]] [[DRX-7]], Calrissian recruited Biggles to travel with him and [[Kaasha Bateen]] onboard the ''[[Lady Luck]]'' to find the [[Phylanx Redux Transmitter]]. While they traveled to [[Chandrila]] to recruit [[Han Solo]], Biggles stayed on the ship to fix DRX-7, who was still trying to harm Calrissian due to being tampered with by [[Fyzen Gor]].<ref name="Last Shot" />
