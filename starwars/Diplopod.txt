A '''dipolopod''' was an [[insect]]-like creature that could be used as a beast of burden. Dipolopods had hard [[Color|green]] plates, [[eye]]less heads, clawed legs and wicked-looking stingers. They could be ridden with the use of a [[saddle]], but often needed prodding using an [[Electro-shock prod|electro-prod]]. They enjoyed eating rotten meat. The [[Arkanis Academy]] kept a number of these creatures.<ref name="secret" />

==Behind the scenes==
Diplopods were created by author [[Jason Fry]] as placeholder creatures for the novel ''[[The Weapon of a Jedi: A Luke Skywalker Adventure]]'', destined to eventually be replaced by [[happabore]]s as a tie-in to [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. Having grown attached to the creatures, Fry chose to reuse the idea for ''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''.<ref name="dorkery">{{Tumblr|subdomain=jasonfry|url=post/156091722656/notes-weapon-of-a-jedi-pt-2|name=Jason Fry's Dorkery|text=Notes: Weapon of a Jedi, Pt. 2}}</ref>
