The '''Carlac belt''' was an [[asteroid field]] near the [[planet]] [[Carlac]] in the [[Carlac system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Prefsbelt sector]]. In [[132 BBY]], the [[DTE-CSA-17 Calaboose prison ship]] ''[[Palwick]]'' exited [[hyperspace]] in the belt and collided with several [[asteroid]]s, spinning out of control. Most of the [[prisoner]]s on board jettisoned in [[escape pod]]s in the Carlac belt before the [[prison ship]] crashed on Carlac.

==Description==
The Carlac belt was<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> an [[asteroid field]]<ref name="LostFoundAD">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> in the [[Carlac system]] of the [[Prefsbelt sector]], which was part of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> and [[New Territories]],<ref name="NewTerritories" /> falling within grid square K-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Color|gray]] [[asteroid]]s of the belt were located close to<ref name="LostFound">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref> the [[planet]] [[Carlac]]. The [[star system]], and therefore the belt, were a part of the [[Entralla Route]] [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==History==
[[File:Palwick-DB.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=The ''Palwick'' flies through the Carlac belt of asteroids with the yellow light of its two K-DT17c sublight engines visible.|The ''Palwick'' collided with asteroids in the Carlac belt.]]
In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], the [[DTE-CSA-17 Calaboose prison ship]] ''[[Palwick]]'' exited [[hyperspace]] from the Entralla Route in the Carlac belt after a [[Dunn's crew|group]] of [[prisoner]]s on board [[Prisoner mutiny on the Palwick|deactivated]] the [[Starship|vessel's]] [[droid]] crew. Upon entering the belt, the [[prison ship]] collided with<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> several<ref name="LostFound" /> asteroids, causing it to spin out of control.<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> The mutinying prisoners then abandoned the ship in an [[escape pod]], leaving behind only the prisoners [[Verosha Aniseya]] and<ref name="LostFound" /> [[Melo Tolicus]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> As the ''Palwick'' collided with more asteroids while drifting toward Carlac, Tolicus took the final escape pod and jettisoned into the belt, leaving Aniseya to brace herself on board the ''Palwick'' as it fell from the belt into Carlac's [[atmosphere]], crashing on the surface.<ref name="LostFound" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Carlac belt first appeared in "[[Lost / Found]],"<ref name="LostFound" /> the first episode of the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', which aired [[June 4]], [[2024]], on [[Disney+]].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref> It was first identified in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
