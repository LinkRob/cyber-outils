'''Coruscant Sector Securitys''', abbreviated to '''CSS''', was a security company based on [[Coruscant]],<ref name="Survivor" /> a [[planet]] in the [[Coruscant subsector|Coruscant sector]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> the company had a [[Color|yellow]] and blue sign with the slogan "always watching so you don't have to" posted on a walkway above an area of rooftops on [[Level 2046]] on Coruscant.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coruscant Sector Securitys was mentioned on a sign in the [[2023]] [[Respawn Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Survivor" />
