'''Chell'''<ref name="SWECG" /> lived inside the [[Skara Nal Mech]] on<ref name="Entombed" /> [[Skara Nal]].<ref name="Skara Nal">{{Databank|skara-nal|Skara Nal}}</ref> It possessed [[Color|gray]] skin, red [[eye]]s, sharp teeth, claws, and a long tail.<ref name="Entombed" />

{{Creature-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Chell, albeit unidentified, first appeared in the [[Entombed|fifth episode]] of the [[The Bad Batch Season 2|second season]] of the [[Disney+]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'',<ref name="Entombed" /> which aired on [[January 25]], [[2023]].<ref name="BB S2">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-bad-batch-season-2-trailer-key-art|text=Clone Force 99 Is Back in New ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 2 Trailer}}</ref> The creature was later identified in the [[2024]] [[Dorling Kindersley]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'', which was authored by [[Dan Brooks]], [[Megan Crouse]], [[Amy Richau]], [[Amy Ratcliffe]], [[Brandon Wainerdi]], [[Dan Zehr]], and [[Kelly Knox]].<ref name="SWECG" />
