'''Bosvarga''' was an [[Unidentified snouted species|alien]] [[mercenary]] who worked alongside his identical [[twin]], [[Cerensco]]. They accompanied the [[assassin]] [[Ochi]] of [[Bestoon]] when he reclaimed his [[starship]], ''[[Bestoon Legacy]]'', after it was hijacked by [[Dathan]] and [[Miramir]] to lead Ochi away from their [[Parent|daughter]], [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]. The twins aimed their [[blaster]]s at the couple as Ochi [[Death|killed]] them with [[Blade of Ochi of Bestoon|his dagger]]. With his bloodlust unsatisfied, the assassin then repeatedly stabbed Bosvarga and Cerensco to death.<ref name="SOTS" />
