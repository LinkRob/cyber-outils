'''Alistar Sinclar''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|man]] who lived on the planet [[Coruscant]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], he served as the manager of the [[Proam Avenue Citizen Assistance]] Office. During Ascension Week, [[Arihnda Pryce]], who had been fired from Senator [[Domus Renking]]'s office, visited Sinclar and asked him to hire her as a staff member. Sinclar hired Pryce for free during Ascension Week as a probationary period, after which he made her a full-time employee.<ref name="Thrawn" />
