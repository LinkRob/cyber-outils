The '''Arantara system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Daimar sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square Q-16 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Arantara]].<ref name="Origin" />

==Behind the scenes==
The first iteration of the "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix of [[star system]]s, released on [[June 24]], [[2025]],<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> listed the Arantara system's sector as "Dalmar" rather than Daimar.<ref name="Appendix" /> The second version of the appendix, released on [[October 13]], fixed this.<ref name="Substack 2">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref>
