'''Faust''' were an [[alien]] [[species]] of tall [[humanoid]]s with [[Color|pale blue]] skin, a double set of eyelids, and light-colored eyes. Their [[homeworld]] was the [[planet]] [[Adana]]. One notable member of this species was the [[scientist]] [[Doctor]] [[Nuvo Vindi]], who recreated [[Blue Shadow Virus]] for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Blue Shadow Virus" /> During the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], at least one Faust individual was arrested on [[Lothal]] as part of an Imperial crackdown following the [[Spectres]]' [[Bombing at the Empire Day parade|attack]] on the [[Empire Day]] celebrations.<ref name="Imperial Justice">''[[Servants of the Empire: Imperial Justice]]''</ref>
