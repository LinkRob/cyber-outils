'''Antibacterial nanofoam''' was a hygiene product used to combat [[bacteria]] that could be used while [[shower]]ing.<ref name="Of MSE-6 and Men" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]] [[TK-421]]&mdash;who was stationed on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]]&mdash;accidentally [[Holorecorder|recorded]] himself via the [[MSE-6 series repair droid]] [[MSE-6-G735Y]] complaining about how constantly wearing a [[Stormtrooper armor|helmet]] was ruining his skin and had given him a large pimple. After [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] saw the [[Hologram|holorecording]] when the [[MSE series|droid]] collided with his ankle, he sent the [[Maintenance droid|droid]] back to TK-421 with a message and a delivery of antibacterial nanofoam. The stormtrooper recorded a response saying he would shower with the product.<ref name="Of MSE-6 and Men" />

==Behind the scenes==
Antibacterial nanofoam appeared in "[[Of MSE-6 and Men]]," a short story written by [[Glen Weldon]] that was published in the [[2017]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''.<ref name="Of MSE-6 and Men" />
