'''Embargo''' was a type of [[Shift Token]] that was used in the card game [[Kessel Sabacc]]. When played the next player will be forced to stand.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] won a Embargo token from a [[Hutt Cardroom|cardroom]] in the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] [[Hutt Cartel District (Mos Eisley)|district]] of [[Mos Eisley]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. Kess also purchased a copy from the [[Sol D'lorak]]'s ship in [[Kijimi City]] on the planet [[Kijimi]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Shift tokens were created for Kessel Sabacc which first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. Embargo was one of several types of Shift Tokens that could be collected and used in Kessel Sabacc, an optional activity in the game.<ref name="Outlaws" />
