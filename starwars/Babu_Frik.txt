'''Babu Frik''' was an [[Anzellan]] who lived during the [[time]] of the [[New Republic Era]] and the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. He maintained a [[Babu Frik's workshop|workshop]] where he worked as a [[black market]] [[droidsmith]] among the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi|Spice Runners]] of [[Kijimi]]. He could reprogram or modify almost any [[droid]], regardless of its security measures. He spoke a fractured, heavily accented form of [[Galactic Basic Standard|Basic]] known as [[Anzellan (language)|Anzellan]].

==Biography==
===Spice running===
[[Reproduction|Born]] around [[50 BBY]],<ref name="BirthDate" /> Babu Frik worked for the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] on the [[planet]] of [[Kijimi]] for some time before the [[Cold War]].<ref name="TROSVD" /> When [[Zorii Bliss]] and [[Poe Dameron]] and members of the gang went to [[Quintil]] to rescue [[Tomasso]], Bliss asked after Frik. Later, after Bliss and Dameron were inducted as Spice Runners, Bliss brought Dameron and their damaged droid companion [[EV-6B6]] to Babu, who fixed the droid for them. When Dameron abandoned the group to join the [[New Republic]], Babu allowed him to take a [[holographic disguise matrix]] so he could sneak offworld.<ref name="Free Fall" /> Sometime after the [[Battle of Crait]], Kijimi fell under [[First Order]] occupation, which made life more difficult for Babu and the band of [[Spicerunner|spice runners]].<ref name="TROSVD" />

===First Order&ndash;Resistance war===
{{Quote|We're trying to find Babu Frik.''"<br />"''Babu? Babu only works for the crew. That isn't you anymore.|Poe Dameron and Zorii Bliss|<ref name="Episode 9">{{Film|IX}}</ref>}}
During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], a group of [[Resistance]] members consisting of [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], [[Finn]], [[Chewbacca]], [[BB-8]], [[C-3PO]], and the now [[Commander|Resistance Commander]] Poe Dameron recovered a [[Blade of Ochi of Bestoon|Sith dagger]]. While C-3PO was able to translate the [[Sith]] inscriptions on the dagger, his programming prevented him from revealing the translation to anyone. When Chewbacca was captured along with the dagger, the group went [[Mission to Kijimi|to Kijimi]] to find Babu Frik in order to retrieve the information in C-3PO's memory banks following the recommendations of Poe.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Babu was able to [[Complete redacted memory bypass|bypass]] C-3PO's programming restrictions to allow C-3PO to speak the translation. Aware that the procedure would erase his memory, C-3PO decided to undergo it as he knew there was no other way to have a chance at defeating [[Darth Sidious]] and the [[Sith Eternal]]. The protocol droid later regained his memory from a backup carried by [[R2-D2]]. Babu tinkered with C-3PO's main processing unit in order to locate the text of the inscription and override the programming preventing C-3PO from speaking the translation. During the procedure, C-3PO's photoreceptors became red as he spoke the words. After completing the translation C-3PO was rebooted with no memory, leaving him only with his base factory programming.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Upon the departure of the Resistance fighters, Babu Frik contacted [[Jannah]] and the rest of [[Company 77]] on [[Kef Bir]] in the hope that they would be able to aid the fighters on their quest to find the [[Emperor's wayfinder]] which would lead them to the Sith world of [[Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX" /> While Frik was busy working on a [[Unidentified battle droid|battle droid]],<ref name="TROS Expanded">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> Sidious gave the order to use [[Derriphan|one]] of his [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] to [[Destruction of Kijimi|destroy]] Kijimi.<ref name="Episode IX" /> While Frik escaped with Bliss, leaving his [[battle droid]] behind,<ref name="TROS Expanded" /> the snow-covered planet was obliterated by the Sith Star Destroyer's [[axial superlaser]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

Although Dameron feared his old friends had been killed, both Babu Frik and Zorii Bliss joined with [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and the [[Citizens' Fleet]]. The two flew a [[Comeuppance|BTA-NR2 Y-wing starfighter]] during the [[Battle of Exegol]] against the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]], including the [[Final Order]]. Zorii and Babu destroyed one of the ''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers during the battle and later celebrated the defeat of the Emperor and the Sith Eternal on [[Ajan Kloss]] with the rest of the freedom fighters. Later,<ref name="Episode IX" /> Frik joined the Resistance itself.<ref name="The Final Order and the Battle of Exegol" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|It could be a rumour, but I believe J.J. Abrams screened the movie for Steven Spielberg, and at the end Spielberg said, 'What happened to Babu?' Everyone thought, "Oh God, what ''did'' happen to Babu?'|Neal Scanlan|<ref name="Empire Spielberg">{{WebCite|url=https://www.empireonline.com/movies/news/star-wars-babu-frik-nearly-died-steven-spielberg-saved-him|text=Star Wars: Babu Frik Nearly Died In The Rise Of Skywalker – But Spielberg (Possibly) Saved Him|author=Travis, Ben|date=2020-04-14|work=''[[Wikipedia:Empire (magazine)|Empire]]''|archivedate=20200531073846}}</ref>}}
Babu Frik was a character created for the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], the third and final installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]]. He was voiced by British actress [[Shirley Henderson]].<ref name="Episode IX" /> He was first revealed during the 2019 [[Force Friday]] merchandise reveal.<ref name="Force Friday livestream">{{SWYouTube|Bj4t7vIuRDY|Triple Force Friday Global Reveal Livestream|nolive=1}}</ref> Babu Frik originated in a [[January]] [[2018]] version of the story. Then called "Babu Zazi," he was going to give the heroes an almost-finished device that could have shut down all First Order technology when completed.<ref name="Art of TROS">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker]]''</ref>

According to sound editors [[David Acord]] and [[Matthew Wood]], Henderson learned to puppeteer for the role so she could "improv and actually move the character's mouth. She was puppeteering while she was doing the improv for the character. She came up with that language and performed it that way. We preserved all of her production sounds, and that was entirely shot on set, live, with her own movement."<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/behind-screen/star-wars-sound-editors-using-elephant-screams-battles-creating-baby-yodas-voice-1273986|text='Star Wars' Sound Editors on Using Elephant Screams for Battles and Creating Baby Yoda's Voice|author=Giardina, Carolyn|date=2020-01-29|work=''[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|The Hollywood Reporter]]''|archivedate=20200624020708}}</ref>

During Episode IX's production, Babu Frik's fate may have differed from that seen in the final version of the film. Concept artist [[Ivan Manzella]] suggested to [[Wikipedia:Empire (magazine)|Empire Magazine]] in [[2020]] that at one point a scene may have been shot in which the Anzellan perished during the destruction of Kijimi. Artists at [[Industrial Light & Magic]] later used deleted footage from one of "several other sequences" of Babu to insert him into the Battle of Exegol, a development which surprised Henderson. Creature supervisor [[Neal Scanlan]] alleged that this came about after director [[J.J. Abrams]] screened a cut of the film that did not address the character's fate to [[Steven Spielberg]], who wanted to know what had happened to Frik.<ref name="Empire Spielberg" />
