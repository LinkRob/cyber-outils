A '''leitmotif''' is introduced in the [[The Mandalorian Season Two|second season]] of the TV series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'' representing [[Boba Fett]]. The [[Wikipedia:Leitmotif|motif]] first appeared in "[[Chapter 14: The Tragedy]]," an episode of ''The Mandalorian'' directed by [[Robert Rodriguez]],<ref name="Tragedy" /> a filmmaker known for taking the lead in his movies' [[musical score]]s.<ref name="Insider" /> While Fett already has a [[Boba Fett's motif (Star Wars saga)|leitmotif]] in the [[Star Wars saga|''Star Wars'' saga]] composed by [[John Williams]],<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> Rodriguez had established the tone of Boba Fett's new leitmotif by the time composer [[Ludwig Göransson]] began working on the episode, and the two collaborated in playing with raw sound effects as their guide for the motif over [[Wikipedia:Zoom (software)|Zoom]] sessions. For the final outcome of the motif, Göransson added sounds of didgeridoo and human breath to distort "some kind of war horn" that the episode director featured.<ref name="Insider" />

Boba Fett's motif is used in "Chapter 14: The Tragedy"<ref name="Tragedy" /> as well as the post-credits scene of "[[Chapter 16: The Rescue]]," the second season finale of ''The Mandalorian''. The post-credits scene teases an upcoming production titled ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]''.<ref name="Rescue">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 16: The Rescue}}</ref>
