The '''Demiloch system''' was located in [[Lannik Space]]<ref name="Systems" /> within [[the Slice]] portion<ref name="Slice" /> of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. Situated in grid square R-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it was linked by a [[Bothawui–Daalang hyperlane|hyperspace route]] to the [[Lannik system|Lannik]] and [[Daalang system]]s.<ref name="Galaxy" /> The [[star system]] contained the terrestrial astronomical object [[Demiloch]], where the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] held a recruitment drive<ref name="BTC" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate|[[Demiloch]]'s mention}}</ref> The recruiting was cut short when an [[Unidentified Imperial spy (Demiloch)|Imperial spy]] posing as a recruit shot the 61st's [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]], leaving the [[Alliance officer|officer]] unconscious for two [[Standard day|days]].<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Demiloch system was first marked on a [[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|galactic map]]<ref name="Galaxy map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> designed by [[Christopher Ibbitt]]<ref name="Fry email">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-31.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-31}}</ref> for the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. The map was first released as a promotional poster at [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|Celebration Anaheim]]<ref name="TimelinesSWcom">{{SW|url=news/swca-2022-lucasfilm-publishing-behind-the-page-panel|text=SWCA 2022: 7 Things We Learned from the Lucasfilm Publishing Behind the Page Panel}}</ref> on [[May 27]], [[2022]]<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|dkpublishing|status/1530292772598534145|[[Dorling Kindersley|DK Books US]]|date=2022-05-27|quote=Just announced at #StarWarsCelebration! Get your exclusive map - only at booth #2525.|archivedate=20220527210041}}</ref> with ''Star Wars: Timelines'' making its debut during [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]]<ref name="Celebration">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-europe-2023-exclusives|text=Check Out ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Exclusives from Hasbro, the LEGO Group, and More}}</ref> on [[April 7]] through [[April 10|10]], [[2023]].<ref name="CelebrationSchedule">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-panel-schedule|text=Plan Ahead with the ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Panel Schedule}}</ref> The system was identified in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] article on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Systems" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
