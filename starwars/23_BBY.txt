The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''23 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3254 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7954''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Orson Callan Krennic]] helps [[engineer]] the [[construction and overhaul facility]].<ref name="Date note">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'' gives the [[time]] in which the [[construction and overhaul facility]] was partially [[engineer]]ed by [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] as five [[Standard year|years]] prior to his visit to [[Kartoosh]] to discuss [[mining]] and research in the [[Western Reaches]], which occurs in [[18 BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore, Krennic's work on the shipyards occured in 23 BBY.</ref>
*The [[Military Creation Act]] existed by this [[time]], and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] began to work against it.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>
*[[Asajj Ventress]] becomes [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[assassin]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Births==
*[[Dalven Kyrell]] on [[Jelucan]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="Dalven">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the prologue of ''[[Lost Stars]]'' to [[11 BBY]]. [[Dalven Kyrell]] is said to be twelve years old during the prologue, setting his birth around 23 BBY.</ref>
*[[Ketsu Onyo]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Ketsu OnyoBirth Date">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'' establishes that [[Ketsu Onyo]] was 18 [[standard year]]s old four years before the [[Battle of Scarif]]. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates this event to [[1 BBY]]. It can then be calculated that Onyo was 18 years old in [[5 BBY]] and was born around 23 BBY.</ref>

==Deaths==
*[[Jenza]] on [[Serenno]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Jenza">The present day events of ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', including [[Jenza]]'s [[death]] on [[Serenno]], take place shortly after [[Asajj Ventress]] is recruited by [[Dooku]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to 23 BBY.</ref>
