'''Devi''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[scavenger]] on [[Jakku]] in [[33 ABY]]. She and her partner [[Strunk]] helped [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] scavenge for a long-buried [[690 light freighter]]. The two then absconded with it to escape the [[planet]] after Rey [[pilot]]ed it to [[Niima Outpost]].

==Biography==
===Life on Jakku===
{{Quote|We all mind our own business. I get it.|Devi|<ref name="BTA" />}}
Born roughly a [[Standard decade|decade]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]<!-- Rey estimates that Devi is about her age (nineteen or twenty) in "Before the Awakening").-->, Devi spent most of her life scavenging on [[Jakku]]. She worked with [[Strunk]], a [[scavenger]] known for his strength but not his intelligence. Both of them wanted to leave Jakku someday.<ref name="BTA" />

According to Devi, she and Strunk once saw a [[nightwatcher worm]] in the [[Sinking Fields]], one so large it could have eaten [[Niima Outpost]] in one bite. They told this to fellow scavanger [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], but she did not believe them, partially because the duo had made other dubious claims in the past.<ref name="Survival Guide">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]''</ref>

====Salvaging the Ghtroc====
{{Quote|I figured it had to be something I knew you were working on ''something'' big, but, like, no way I ever imagined it was something like this.|Devi|<ref name="BTA" />}}
In [[33 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Rey Skywalker|Rey's]] story in ''[[Before the Awakening]]'' to [[33 ABY]].</ref> Devi noticed that Rey was holding back valuable salvage for herself and spending [[time]] repairing relatively worthless parts. Assuming that their fellow scavenger was building something and curious what, Devi and Strunk confronted Rey at the cleaning stations in Niima Outpost. When Rey declined to share her plans, Devi seemingly backed off, but warned Rey if she was noticing the strange behavior, others would notice too.<ref name="BTA" />

[[File:Devi_Strunk_and_Rey.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Devi and Strunk approach Rey about her discovery.]]
Devi and Strunk managed to trail Rey and discovered what the project was ten [[Standard day|days]] later: Rey had found an almost completely intact [[690 light freighter|Ghtroc 690]] [[freighter]]. With the right parts, the [[Starship|ship]] would be able to fly again. Amazed at Rey's good fortune and aware of other useful parts, Devi and Strunk offered to help repair the Ghtroc in exchange for coming with when Rey used it to leave Jakku. When Rey explained that she was going to sell it to [[Unkar Plutt]], Devi prevented Strunk from responding and brokered a deal. In exchange for helping repair the ship, she and Strunk would get half the profits from the sale, which was estimated to be at least five thousand shares.<ref name="BTA" />

The new-business partners began fixing the Ghtroc, with Devi and Strunk being able to quickly supply parts that Rey could not have found on her own. As work progressed, Devi and Strunk began secretly camping out near the ship to guard their investment.<ref name="BTA" />

Two [[Standard week|weeks]] later, when the Ghtroc was repaired enough to fly (but still missing a [[hyperdrive]]), Devi was a passenger on the test flight. Much to her exuberation, the ship worked. That night, while guarding the ship, Devi and Strunk found Rey, who had returned to the site, fending off [[Teedo]] interlopers. The two joined in the defense of it, driving the Teedos away. Devi admitted that she and Strunk had been secretly guarding the wreck for two weeks.<ref name="BTA" />

Devi also told Rey that she had she knew where to find a hyperdrive to complete the Ghtroc. Rey suggested that Devi guard the ship, while she and Strunk retrieve the hyperdrive. When Rey and Strunk returned with the drive the following evening, Rey had enough trust in Devi and Strunk to offer to let them [[sleep]] inside the Ghtroc, where it would be warmer.<ref name="BTA" />

[[File:Rey_690_Light_Freighter_GASC.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Devi and Strunk flying their stolen freighter away from Jakku]]
Two days after collecting and installing the drive (and nearly a [[Standard year|year]] after Rey had found the wreck in the first place), the trio flew their ship into Niima Outpost, making a show of it. Rey left Devi and Strunk onboard while she went off to sell the ship to Unkar. Once their partner was gone, Devi and Strunk stole the ship, using it to leave Jakku, the only reason they had helped in the first place, leaving Rey with nothing.<ref name="BTA" />

Some time later, Rey wrote about Devi in her [[Rey's survival guide|journal]], expressing her distrust for her fellow scavenger and calling her a "swindler" as well as alluding to the Ghtroc incident.<ref name="Survival Guide" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|But I don't believe that. Because those two swindlers have told me a lot of things.|Rey, about Devi and her friend Strunk|<ref name="Survival Guide" />}}
About Rey's age and built small, Devi kept her [[hair]] cut short. The smarter one between her and her partner, she acted as the leader. She brokered the decision to split Rey's salvage of the freighter, taking advantage of the situation to make a deal that Rey felt was unfair but could not see a safe way to decline.<ref name="BTA" />

Despite this, Devi presented a cheerful disposition during the salvage and managed to integrate herself with Rey to the extent of gaining her trust, all while planning to betray her. This was successful partially since Rey could not comprehend anyone wanting to leave Jakku and partially since Rey ''wanted'' to trust Devi.<ref name="BTA" />

Devi had a reputation for making unbelievable claims.<ref name="Survival Guide" /> She had a tendency to talk a lot, which annoyed others, including Rey.<ref name="BTA" />

In a fight, Devi was quick and, in Rey's estimation, more dangerous than Strunk. Devi carried a [[Vibroknife|vibro-knife]] and a shortened [[Electrostaff|shock stick]]. She also had a small [[blaster]] with an indeterminate number of shots.<ref name="BTA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Devi first appeared in the [[2015]] [[canon]] novel ''[[Before the Awakening]]'', written by [[Greg Rucka]] as a prelude to [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="BTA" />
