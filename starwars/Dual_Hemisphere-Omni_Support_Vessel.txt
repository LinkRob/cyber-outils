The '''Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel''', otherwise identified as the '''DH-Omni Support Vessel''', '''DH-O Support Vessel''', or simply as the '''DH-O''' and dubbed as the '''Separatist supply ship'''<ref name="Separatists" /> or '''Separatist support ship''',<ref name="Plan Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/plan-of-dissent-episode-guide|text="Plan of Dissent" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> or '''Separatist transport''',<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> was a model of massive [[transport]] and [[supply ship]] manufactured by [[Hoersch-Kessel Drive, Inc.]] and utilized by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Starfighters and Other Vehicles" />

==Description==
Like the [[Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter|''Lucrehulk''-class]], it was originally a corporate transport [[Starship|vessel]] before being converted for wartime use, serving in a support role for both [[Separatist Droid Army|droids]] and organic [[soldier]]s.<ref name="Starfighters and Other Vehicles" />

==History==
One DH-Omni Support Vessel was present at the [[Battle of Umbara]] and was key to supplying Confederate forces on the surface. It was destroyed by [[Hardcase]], [[Jesse]], and [[Fives]] who were piloting [[Zenuas 33 Umbaran starfighter]]s on a rogue infiltration mission.<ref name="Plan" />

Later in the war, more were seen blocking [[Dooku]]'s home planet of [[Serenno]] until some of them went with [[General]] [[Grievous]] and a Separatist battalion to [[Dathomir]] to [[Battle of Dathomir|massacre]] the Nightsisters for Ventress's betrayal.<ref name="Massacre">{{TCW|Massacre}}</ref> DH-Omni Support Vessels were later present at the [[Invasion of Scipio|Battle of Scipio]] where they blockaded the planet before Dooku and the [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|super tactical droid]] Commander [[Kraken]] withdrew, allowing the Republic to win the battle and nationalize most of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] after [[Rush Clovis]]'s death.<ref name="CH">{{TCW|Crisis at the Heart}}</ref>

One DH-Omni Support Vessel was used to transport a [[kyber crystal]] from [[Utapau]], even being mistaken for a mound by [[Anakin Skywalker]], but was destroyed along with most of the droids on the ship.<ref name="A Death on Utapau">{{TCW|A Death on Utapau}}</ref>

DH-Omni Support Vessels also engaged [[Maul]]'s [[Shadow Collective]] at the [[Battle on Ord Mantell|Battle of Ord Mantell]].<ref name="SoD 2">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 2|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 2]]</ref> More were later present in [[Trench]]'s fleet during the [[Battle of Anaxes]] until the rescue of [[Echo]] by the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]]. The vessels were later blown up by Wrecker alongside the rest of the fleet.<ref name="The Bad Batch">{{TCW|The Bad Batch (episode)}}</ref><ref name="SoD 1">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 1|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 1]]</ref>

[[Unidentified DH-Omni Support Vessel (Agamar)|A wreckage supply ship]] on the planet [[Agamar]] during the [[Battle of Agamar (Clone Wars)|battle]] between Republic and Separatist forces. After the Separatist [[Confederacy military|forces]] were defeated during the [[Onderonian Civil War|civil war]] on the [[planet]] [[Onderon]], the [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|super tactical droid]] [[General]] [[Kalani]] was ordered by Confederate [[Head of State]] Count Dooku to withdraw what forces remained and await further orders above<ref name="Tipping Points">{{TCW|Tipping Points}}</ref> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> planet [[Agamar]].<ref name="Tipping Points" /> However, the [[supply ship]], carrying Kalani and his forces, was [[Battle of Agamar (Clone Wars)|engaged]] by [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Republic Military|forces]] and crashed on the planet's surface during the battle,<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 51">{{BuildR2Cite|51|Droid Directory}}</ref> and remained abandoned as the [[Clone Wars]] moved toward its [[Mission to Mustafar|conclusion]] in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' states that the events of "[[The Last Battle]]" occur seventeen [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the [[Clone Wars]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, the events of "The Last Battle" must take place in [[2 BBY]].</ref> While on Agamar, Kalani later received the order to deactivate himself and his [[droid]] forces, but assumed it to be a Republic trick and refused to follow his orders even to leave the battle droids online while they remained on Agamar's surface.<ref name="The Last Battle" /> 

As a result, for seventeen [[Standard year|years]],<ref name="Date" /> Kalani and his [[Agamar garrison|garrison]]<ref name="USNWE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> of battle droids waited for the chance to end the Clone Wars in favor of the Separatist Alliance.<ref name="The Last Battle" /> 

Later during the [[Galactic Civil War]] in [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the supply ship was [[Mission to Agamar (2 BBY)|visited]] by several [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] operatives of [[Phoenix Cell]] who traveled to the planet in search of [[proton bomb]]s for their fight against the [[Galactic Empire]], but were instead forced by Kalani to take part in his battle otherwise they would be [[Death|terminated]]. During the battle, however, the Empire, alerted by a [[distress signal]] sent out by the [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P]], dispatched an [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] and two [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruisers]] carrying [[All Terrain Defense Pod]]s and [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)|All Terrain Armored Transports]] to the [[Agamar system]] to destroy the rebels, which resulted in both the [[garrison]] and [[Spectres|rebels]] joining forces to fight off the Imperial forces and make their escape from the planet in three ''Sheathipede''-class transport shuttles, [[Phantom II|one]] of which was kept by the rebels as their replacement<ref name="The Last Battle" /> for the [[Mission to Reklam Station|destroyed]] ''[[Phantom]]''<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref> and another which was destroyed amid the escape.<ref name="The Last Battle" />
