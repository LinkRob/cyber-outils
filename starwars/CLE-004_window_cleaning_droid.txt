'''CLE-004 window cleaning droids'''<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> were a model of floating [[cleaning droid]]<ref name="BuildR2 38" /> designed to clean the windows of buildings. They were used on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] to clean the windows of buildings many levels above the planet's surface. The droids would have their designations printed on their bodies, as seen on the unit [[Buffy]].<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref>
