Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]] undertook an '''assault on Jabba's Palace''' to trick the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] into unleashing his [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure's Megadroid|Megadroid]], planning to destroy it in order for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] to undertake their own [[Rescue of Han Solo|rescue]] of the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]], who Jabba had [[Hansicle|frozen]] in [[carbonite]] inside his palace. The mission was ultimately a success after Valance was knocked out by [[Boba Fett]], who was secretly helping Valance in the name of protecting his reputation from the [[Megadroid]]. After defeating Valance, it was he who convinced Jabba to have Valance battle the [[droid]]. In an ensuing [[Death Match on Tatooine|death match]], Valance destroyed the [[Megadroid]] with the assistance of his [[T'onga's crew|crew]] led by the [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] [[T'onga]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 42" />
