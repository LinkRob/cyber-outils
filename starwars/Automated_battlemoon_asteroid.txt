The '''automated battlemoon asteroid''' was a proposed design developed by the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Special Weapons Group|Special Weapons Group]] in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Naboo|Naboo Crisis]] of [[32 BBY]]. However, because of the Republic's pacifistic leanings and the consequent lack of funding, the project never made it past the planning stage.<ref name="Catalyst" />

During the [[Clone Wars]], the battlemoon was compared to the proposed [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], although [[Vice Chair]] [[Mas Amedda]] considered it to be a mere toy in regards to the colossal scale of [[Project Stardust]].<ref name="Catalyst" />

==Behind the scenes==
The automated battlemoon asteroid first appeared in ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'' released in [[2016]] and authored by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Catalyst" />
