'''Cling-boots''' were a type of [[boot]] used in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an announcement was made over the [[Hauler's Keep]]'s speaker system informing [[Felt Ardack]] that they were needed in central grav control and that they should bring their cling-boots.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cling-boots were first mentioned in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> The boots are mentioned in an ambient announcement that players can overhear while on Hauler's Keep.<ref name="Outlaws" />
