'''Beast-yeast''' was an ingredient used at [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. It in some way capable of independent movement and the menu included a warning to all customers stating the diner assumed no responsibility for any that escaped the kitchen during food preparation.

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Beast-yeast is presumably a more dangerous version of real world [[Wikipedia:yeast|yeast]].
