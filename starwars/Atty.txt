'''Atty''' was a [[Stormtrooper Commander]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]] during its [[Imperial Era|reign]] of [[the galaxy]]. At one point, Atty served as the [[Field Commander|field commander]] on [[Ontotho]] during a [[Skirmish on Ontotho|pacification mission]]. During the conflict, one of Atty's [[trooper]]s reported seeing a hostile with a [[lightsaber]], a fact which eventually made its way to the [[Second Sister]], who along with a contingent of [[Purge Trooper]]s, arrived to investigate.<ref name="Dark Temple 1" />
