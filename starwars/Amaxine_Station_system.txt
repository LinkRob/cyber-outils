The '''Amaxine Station system''' was an [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[star system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was centered around a singular [[Unidentified star (Amaxine station)|star]] contained the [[Amaxine Space Station|Amaxine space station]], a [[space station]] constructed by the [[Amaxine warriors]]. The station was eventually taken by the [[Drengir]], who were subsequently [[Binding statues|sealed]] aboard the station by the [[Sith]]. By the [[High Republic Era]], the [[star]] of the [[Star system|system]] was in its final [[Millennium|millennia]] before going [[supernova]], and was prone to intense [[solar flare]]s.<ref name="Into the Dark" />
