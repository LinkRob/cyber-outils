'''Amuncie Tidian''' was a [[Boosodian]] [[Forger (profession)|forger]] from the celestial body [[Boosodia]] who was assocaited with the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]], He was active on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]] shortly before its [[Destruction of Kijimi|destruction]] in [[35 ABY]]. During the [[First Order]]'s [[Occupation of Kijimi|occupation]] of the planet, he exited a building in [[Kijimi City]] but then immediately retreated back inside after spotting a nearby First Order patrol.

Tidian's twenty [[eye]]s could be focused to see different portions of the electromagnetic spectrum, and he believed that he had the ability to see the future with a non-existent twenty-first eye.

==Biography==
Amuncie Tidian was a [[Boosodian]] from<ref name="CGttSWG" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]<ref name="GalaxyMap">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]] &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Boosodia system]]</ref> celestial body [[Boosodia]].<ref name="CGttSWG" /> During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], he who worked as a [[Forger (profession)|forger]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> in the [[Thieves' Quarter]] of [[Kijimi City]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]] and was associated with the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] based there.<ref name="CGttSWG" />

[[File:Amuncie_Tidian_TROS.png|thumb|left|300px|Tidian was caught in a spotlight in Kijimi City.]]
During the First Order's [[Occupation of Kijimi|occupation]] of Kijimi<ref name="TROS VD" /> in [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}} to [[35 ABY]].</ref> Tidian tried to avoid attracting attention to himself due to the illegal nature of his work. He went into hiding whenever the new authorities were nearby,<ref name="Encyclopaedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Din Djarin and Other Characters of the Underworld}}</ref> but exited a building<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> within the Thieves' Quarter<ref name="TROS VD" /> while a [[Floodlight|spotlight]] was being shone upon the building's door. Tidian called out and then looked around, not seeing the [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]], who was hidden nearby, but spotting a First Order patrol attempting to enter a neighboring building. Seeing the First Order caused him to call out in alarm and retreat back to within the building, closing the door behind him.<ref name="TROS" /> Shortly after Tidian retreated into the building, the First Order [[Protocol 13|evacuated]] its personnel from Kijimi<ref name="TROS VD" /> before the planet was [[Destruction of Kijimi|destroyed]] by the [[Sith Eternal]].<ref name="TROS" /> There was good reason to believe that Tidian perished in the blast.<ref name="Encyclopaedia" />

==Personality and traits==
As a Boosodian, Amuncie Tidian's twenty [[eye]]s provided a major advantage in his activities<ref name="Encyclopaedia" /> and meant he was well equipped to work as a forger. With concentration he could focus clusters of his eyes on specific portions of the electromagnetic spectrum, including [[X-ray]]s and, with a large amount of effort, [[radio wave]]s. Not long before Kijimi was destroyed, he<ref name="TROS VD" /> and some others<ref name="Encyclopaedia" /> had become convinced that he was able to see the future, crediting his supposed ability to a non-existent extra eye. Tidian had [[Color|gray]] skin and white eyes.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Equipment==
Tidian wore a black [[Environment suit|insulated environment suit]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> and black [[boot]]s.<ref name="TROS" />

===Creation===
{{Quote|The idea was, 'Has he really seen that many or is it just that he has ''way'' too many eyes?'<nowiki></nowiki>|Martin Rezard|<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Haunted_Alien.png|thumb|right|200px|"Haunted Alien" by Martin Rezard]]
Amuncie Tidian was portrayed by [[Nick Kellington]]<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|NickKellington|status/1209220846843240448|[[Nick Kellington]]|quote=As #AmuncieTidian On a #TheRiseOfSkywalker #Topps card So happy to have this addition to the characters I have played in #StarWars films that have been featured by @Topps 😁 Species: #Boosodian Location: #Kijimi #NickKellington #CreaturePerformer #EpisodeIX #Alien #ILoveMyJob|archivedate=20191225223651}}</ref>
in the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="TROS" /> He was first identified simultaneously in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]],<ref name="TROS VD" /> and a card in the ''[[2019 Topps Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker Series 1]]'' trading card set by [[Topps|The Topps Company, Inc.]]<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2019 Topps Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker Series 1|cardname=Amuncie Tidian|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/211130/cid/13996012/2019-Topps-Star-Wars-The-Rise-of-Skywalker-31-Amuncie-Tidian}}</ref>

Tidian was originally designed to be an alien that had seen the ten thousand [[Star Destroyer]]s of the Sith Eternal's fleet, the Final Order, and reported it back to the Resistance. Looking haunted was an integral part of the original brief for the character and creature concept designer and senior sculptor [[Martin Rezard]] gave the character many eyes so that viewers would question if he had really seen that many ships or whether his number of eyes had caused him to see more.<ref name="Art" />

===Design===
Rezard's concept art was titled "Haunted Alien,"<ref name="Art" /> with [[Calum Alexander Watt]] also creating a costume concept.<ref name="Watt">{{Twitter|CalumAWatt|status/1278348650842927105|[[Calum Alexander Watt]]|date=2020-07-01|quote=Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker, costume concept art for Kijimi crowd, Haunted Alien/Amuncie Tidian, 2018. Creature designed by Creature Department's Martin Rezard. This one doesn't appear in the book, so thanks to @PhilSzostak for permission to share here.|archivedate=20211212104524}}</ref> [[Adrian Parish]] and [[Claire Roi Harvey]],<ref name="Parish">{{Instagram|username=adyfparish|url=p/CAjS3Uzj7hd|name=[[Adrian Parish]]|date=2020-05-24|quote=Only a fleeting glimpse of this guy in The Rise of Skywalker but a joy to work on with Martin Rezard, Nick Kellington, Henrik Svenson and Clare Roy Harvey.|archivefile=File:AdrianParish-AmuncieTidian-Instagram.png}}</ref> who were both credited as creature performers and creature puppeteers, and CFX paint finish design supervisor<ref name="TROS" /> [[Henrik Svensson]] also worked on the character. The Tidian mask included twenty eyes, each with four way movement and skin around them that was mechanised to drag and create a ripple effect.<ref name="Parish" /> The script of the film evolved to not include the character as originally imagined, with the character [[Boolio]] instead delivering similar information to the Resistance. However, director [[J.J. Abrams]] took a liking to Rezard's design and promised to use it elsewhere,<ref name="Art" /> using the character as an extra on Kijimi in the final version.<ref name="TROS" />

[[File:TonyTran-OutlawsKijimiStreetsCloseUp-ArtStation.jpg|thumb|left|300px|alt=Amuncie Tidian is pictured talking to Rothgar Deng in the streets of Kijimi City in concept art for ''Star Wars Outlaws''.|Amuncie Tidian was used in concept art of Kijimi for ''Star Wars Outlaws'' but did not appear in the game itself.]]
A photo of Tidian was among those used for reference by the artists of [[Aardman Animations]] while they worked on "[[I Am Your Mother]],"<ref name="Focus">{{VisionsFocus|I Am Your Mother}}</ref> the fourth episode of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|second series]] of the [[Disney+]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'',<ref name="Mother">{{Visions|I Am Your Mother}}</ref> which aired on [[May 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="VisionsRelease">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-2|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Release Date, Studios Revealed}}</ref> A Boosodian character named [[Glon]] was designed for the episode<ref name="Focus" /> but not ultimately used.<ref name="Mother" /> Concept art of the streets of Kijimi City produced by senior concept artist [[Tony Tran]] for the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' included Tidian with other characters<ref name="ArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=tonytran|url=artwork/RKKZBm|name=[[Tony Tran]]|text=''Star Wars Outlaws'' - Kijimi City Streets|date=2024-10-11}}</ref> that appeared on Kijimi in Episode IX.<ref name="TROS" /> None of these characters were used in the final release of the game.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
