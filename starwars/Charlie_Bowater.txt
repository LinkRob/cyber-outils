'''Charlie Bowater''' (born [[May 14]], [[1988]]<ref name="DeviantArt" /> in [[Wikipedia:Woodville, Derbyshire|Woodville, Derbyshire]], [[Wikipedia:United Kingdom|United Kingdom]])<ref name="Facebook" /> is a [[Wikipedia:British people|British]]<ref name="DeviantArt" /> illustrator and concept artist.<ref name="ArtStation" /> For the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, Bowater illustrated the cover of [[Kiersten White]]'s young-adult novel [[Padawan (novel)|''Padawan'']]<ref name="Padawan Cover Reveal">{{SW|url=news/kiersten-white-star-wars-padawan|text=Kiersten White on Telling a Tale of Young Obi-Wan in ''Star Wars: Padawan''}}</ref> and its [[Padawan (audiobook)|audiobook adaptation]],<ref name="Audiobook">{{Downpour|url=star-wars-padawan?sp=700551|text=Star Wars: Padawan}}</ref> which were released on [[July 26]], [[2022]].<ref name="Padawan Cover Reveal" /> Her<ref name="DeviantArt" /> cover artwork and involvement in the project were first revealed in a [[StarWars.com]] article published on [[February 25]] of the same year.<ref name="Padawan Cover Reveal" />
