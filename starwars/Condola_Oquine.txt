'''Condola Oquine''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] individual who served as a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] in the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. In [[228 BBY]], Oquine sought out the [[Wookiee]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Burryaga Agaburry]] at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] after the [[blight]] affliction was found on his [[homeworld]], the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet [[Kashyyyk]].

After finding Burryaga with a group of other Jedi, the Padawan asked if the news was true. The group thought Oquine referred to a rumor that [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] had been located, and told the Padawan the [[Jedi High Council]] was assessing intel about Ro. Oquine then stated they were not asking about Ro, before telling Burryaga about the discovery of the blight on Kashyyyk.

Condola Oquine was an individual who served as a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] in the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Into the Light" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{IntotheLightDate}}</ref> Oquine was present at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Into the Light" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] when the Jedi Order and [[Galactic Republic]] became aware of a rumor that [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]]&mdash;the leader of the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] organization the Jedi and Republic were in [[Nihil conflict|conflict]] with&mdash;had been spotted by a [[probe droid]] outside of the Nihil's [[Occlusion Zone]] region of [[Realspace|space]]. At the same time, the affliction known as the [[blight]] was discovered on<ref name="Into the Light" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> planet [[Kashyyyk]].<ref name="Into the Light" />

Oquine rushed to a terrace at the [[Jedi temple|Jedi Temple]] to find the [[Wookiee]]<ref name="Into the Light" /> [[Jedi Knight]]<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 3">[[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 3|''The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight'' 3]]</ref> [[Burryaga Agaburry|Burryaga]] to ask him if the news about the blight was true. The Padawan located Burryaga with a group of others consisting of<ref name="Into the Light" /> Jedi Knights<ref name="Into the Light" /><ref name="Shadows of Starlight 3" /> [[Dez Rydan]], [[Reath Silas]], and [[Bell Zettifar]], as well as the former<ref name="Into the Light" /> [[Jedi Master]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Cohmac Vitus]]. After arriving at the terrace's doorway, Oquine asked the group if the news was true, to which Vitus responded that they did not know, but that the [[Jedi High Council]] was evaluating the intel about Ro. In response, the Padawan stated that they were not asking about Ro but about the blight. Upon realizing that the group was not aware of the discovery of the blight on Kashyyyk, Oquine turned and spoke with Burryaga, informing him that the blight had been found on his [[homeworld]].<ref name="Into the Light" />

==Personality and traits==
By 228 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Condola Oquine had a youthful appearance. After arriving at the terrace to speak with Burryaga, the Padawan looked pale and was panting.<ref name="Into the Light" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a member of the Jedi Order, Oquine was [[Force-sensitive]] and [[Jedi training|trained]] to [[Force power|wield]] [[the Force]].<ref name="Into the Light" />
