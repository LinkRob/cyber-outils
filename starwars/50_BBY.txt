The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''50 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3227 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''31 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7927''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*The [[Coduyo family]] declares a [[family emergency]] to protect the planet [[Massoss]] from a "breakaway alien sect."<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref><ref name="Coduyo emergency note">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' takes place in [[18 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. After [[Coduyo'po'nekri|Yoponek]] described the events in which the [[Coduyo family]] invoked the [[family emergency]] to protect their holdings on Massoss as "around thirty years ago," Senior Aide [[Xodlak'ji'iprip|Lakjip]] identified the events as "thirty-two [[Standard year|years]] ago." Therefore, they must have happened in 50 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref>
*[[Asajj Ventress]] is sold to [[Hal'Sted]]<ref name="Asajj Ventress" />

==Births==
*[[Babu Frik]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Babu Frik">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' gives [[Babu Frik]]'s age during the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] as 85 [[Standard year|years]] old. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the ''The Rise of Skywalker'' event in [[35 ABY]], so Babu Frik have been [[Reproduction|born]] around 50 BBY.</ref>
*Asajj Ventress<ref name="Asajj Ventress">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'' places the birth of Asajj Ventress, and her subsequent selling to [[Hal'Sted]], to 50 [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|IV}}, which corresponds to 50 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. Therefore, Ventress' birth must have occurred in 50 BBY.</ref>
