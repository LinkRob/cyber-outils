'''Emir''' was a title of rulership or authority over others.<ref name="Emir Wat Tambor" /> Early in the [[Clone Wars]], [[Wat Tambor]]<ref name="Battle Tales 2">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 2|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 2]]</ref>&mdash;who served as the [[Foreman]] of the [[Techno Union]],<ref name="Emir Wat Tambor" /> a member of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]'s [[Executive Separatist Council]],<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> and a [[general]] of the [[Confederacy military]]<ref name="AtlasNew">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>&mdash;acted as an emir during his occupation of the [[Nexus]] trading outpost.<ref name="Battle Tales 2">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 2|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 2]]</ref> Later, he was appointed [[Emir of Ryloth]] during the [[Battle of Ryloth|occupation]] of that [[planet]].<ref name="Emir Wat Tambor" />
