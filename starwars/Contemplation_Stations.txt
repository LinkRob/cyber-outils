'''Contemplation Stations''' were three enlarged sections of a balcony on the [[High Council Tower]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. The balcony surrounded the exterior of the [[High Council holomap room]], where high-ranking [[Jedi]] were briefed on missions. The stations were encouraged to be used for contemplation and reflection and were often waiting areas for Jedi anticipating an audience with the [[Jedi Council]].<ref name="Complete Locations" />
