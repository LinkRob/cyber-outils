The '''Dark Vortex Club''' was a seedy nightclub on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]], that was also the site of a lot of criminal activity. It was owned by the [[Ubese/Legends|Ubese]] [[Mah-Luu]] and entry into it could only be gained through a [[Darth Vader figurine]]. [[Senator/Legends|Senator]] [[Luralon Odaay]] was a regular patron at it and he liked to engage in the services of the prostitute [[Kandi]] there. Following his death, two [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] agents visited the club as part of their investigations.
