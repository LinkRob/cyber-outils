The '''Benathy''' were a large [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that resided on the [[planet]] [[Benath]] in [[Wild Space]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] led a [[Battle against the Benathy|battle]] against the Benathy, which ultimately failed, though the Benathy were subdued. [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] of the [[First Order]] visited the Benathy for [[Battle against the Benathy (First Order)|negotiations]], accompanied by [[Captain]] [[Ruthford]].<ref name="Age of Resistance" />

The Benathy were massive compared to the average species, with [[Color|brown]] hair covering their bodies. They also had two tusks protruding out of their face.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />
