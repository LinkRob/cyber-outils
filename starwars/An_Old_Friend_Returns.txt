"'''An Old Friend Returns'''" is a one-minute [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] [[LEGO Star Wars Movie Short|Mini Movie]] that is connected to the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Yoda Chronicles]]'' animated TV series. It is the ninth video and was released on YouTube on [[August 6]], [[2014]].<ref name="An Old Friend Returns" />

==Plot summary==
On [[Dagobah/Legends|Dagobah]], [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] and the [[Force ghost/Legends|Force ghost]] of [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] contact the former [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] [[Jek-14/Legends|Jek-14]] by [[Hologram/Legends|hologram]]. Jek, who is on [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]], warns them that [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] has recovered the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Holocron/Legends|holocrons]]. Kenobi fears the terrible things that the [[Palpatine/Legends|Emperor]] can do with them.

Jek then witnesses [[Jar Jar Binks/Legends|Jar Jar Binks]], [[Boss/Legends|Boss]] [[Rugor Nass/Legends|Rugor Nass]], and several [[Gungan/Legends|Gungans]] being pursued by several [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[All Terrain Armored Transport/Legends|AT-AT]] and [[All Terrain Scout Transport/Legends|AT-ST]] walkers. Jek tells Yoda and Kenobi that the Empire is using the holocrons to identify and attack planets sympathetic to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|rebellion]]. Jek then uses his [[Droid/Legends|droid]] arm to destroy two AT-ST walkers.

He flies his [[Jedi Scout Fighter]] into space and hurls a bolt of [[Force lightning/Legends|Force lightning]] through Vader's [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer/Legends|Imperial Star Destroyer]], startling the [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]]. Jek then travels to Dagobah, and his invisible ship startles Master Kenobi. Jek himself is frightened by Kenobi's ghostly apparition. Yoda sighs that Jek is getting older than him.
