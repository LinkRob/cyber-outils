"'''Escaping the Scrap Pile'''" is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] published in the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 19|nineteenth issue]] of the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]] series by [[Egmont]] UK Ltd. It was written by [[Martin Fisher]], illustrated by [[Ingo Römling]], and edited by [[Gunther Nickel]]. The comic was released on [[June 29]], [[2016]].

According to Fisher, the story takes place between the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episodes "[[The Call]]" and "[[Homecoming]]."<ref name="Twit">{{Twitter|tronprogram2|status/751843791565381632|[[Martin Fisher]]|quote=@LelalMekha it takes place between The Call and Homecoming|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/wg5Mq}}</ref>

==Plot summary==
[[C1-10P|Chopper]] wakes up on a [[Unidentified planetoid (scrap pile)|planetoid]], where he is about to be scrapped for parts by [[Davil]] and [[Bidvel]]. Meanwhile on the ''[[Ghost]]'', [[Hera Syndulla]], [[Garazeb Orrelios]], and [[Sabine Wren]] attempt to find the lost [[droid]], but are attacked by an [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]].

At first, Davil and Bidvel do not think Chopper will be worth much, but after realizing that the droid has rebel intel, they plot to sell the information to the [[Galactic Empire]]. Chopper is briefly able to escape, during which time he steals activation codes to reactivate the various droids around the [[Bidvel's scrap pile|scrap pile]]. Chopper is recaptured and Davil copies the information into their computers. As Chopper is about to be dismantled, the droids activate and begin to attack the two [[Xot]]s. A [[B2-series super battle droid]] holds Bidvel at [[blaster]]point, but Davil is able to shoot one of the [[astromech droid]]s. The super droid then shoots Davil with a stun blast and a [[droideka]] stops Chopper's destruction.

Soon after, the ''Ghost'' arrives, having escaped the Imperial [[Starship|ship]], and lands. Syndulla promises to replace any parts Chopper lost, saying she will erase the data on Davil's [[computer]]s that he stole. Chopper gives the rescued droids the idea to form a [[Unidentified droid group|group]] to help droids, and they wish for Chopper to stay with them to lead them. Chopper refuses, preferring to stay with his friends, and the group of droids understands, saying that they hope they will meet him again.
