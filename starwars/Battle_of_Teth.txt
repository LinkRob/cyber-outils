The '''Battle of Teth''', also known as the '''battle on Teth''', the '''assault at the B'omarr Monastery''', the '''battle at the Teth Monastery''', or the '''rescue of Rotta''', took place on the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt]]-[[Hutt Space|controlled]] [[planet]] of [[Teth]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The battle, one of the earliest of the [[Clone Wars]], was waged as the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] attempted to save [[Rotta]], the infant son of [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]], from the Confederacy; the Separatists planned to [[Death|kill]] the [[Youngling|Huttlet]] and pin the blame on the [[Jedi Order]], thus bringing Jabba and the [[Hutt Clan]] onto the side of the Confederacy. [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], were able to rescue the Huttlet from Teth and managed to [[Mission to Jabba's Palace (Clone Wars)|bring]] him to [[Tatooine]], the planet upon which Jabba based his operations.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Soon, the Jedi will not only be at war with you, Count, but with the Hutt clan as well.|Darth Sidious to Dooku and [[Asajj Ventress]]|audio=WithTheHuttClanAsWell-TCWf.flac|<ref name="TCW film" />}}
[[File:Octopus_ship_capture.png|thumb|left|240px|The [[Trident (starship)|''Trident'']] captures the [[Khetanna|sail barge]] carrying Rotta.]]
Following the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[Separatist Droid Army]] seized control of the major [[Hyperspace route|hyperspace lanes]], separating the [[Galactic Republic]] from the majority of its newly commissioned [[Grand Army of the Republic|clone army]].<ref name="TCW film" /> To secure routes in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], Dooku, at the behest of [[Darth Sidious]], made a deal with [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro the Hutt]], uncle of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]], to kidnap Jabba's son [[Rotta]] in order for him to become [[Master of the Hutt Clan|head]] of the [[Hutt Clan]]s. Thus, Dooku sent [[Admiral]] [[Trench]],<ref name="Cat & Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref> [[General]] [[Whorm Loathsom]], and [[Sith assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]] to delay [[Jedi General]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] on the [[planet]] [[Christophsis]]. After Ventress [[Lightsaber combat|fought]] with Skywalker and Kenobi, Loathsom and a [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|T-series tactical droid]] brought in [[Whorm Loathsom's army|reinforcements]] in order to continue delaying the [[Jedi]] while she moved on to the next part of Dooku's plan.<ref name="Hidden Enemy">{{TCW|The Hidden Enemy}}</ref>

Ventress then went to the planet [[Tatooine]], where she attacked the ''[[Khetanna]]'', Jabba's personal [[sail barge]], and kidnapped Rotta. She took him to a [[B'omarr Order Monastery|monastery]] on [[Teth]]. Jabba's guards climbed onto one of the [[skiff]]s and then went to [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] to inform him of this. Jabba sent out a call for help to the Republic, the Confederacy, and his [[bounty hunter]]s. [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] decided that the Jedi must help Jabba in order to gain access to the [[Hutt]]'s [[Realspace|space]] lanes in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] to move their [[Republic Military|troops]]. Despite [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]]'s reservations in dealing with the Hutts, Windu believed there was more to the kidnapping. Windu, knowing that General [[Grievous]] had their forces stretched thin, could only spare Kenobi and Skywalker.<ref name="TCW film" />

[[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] sent [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] to [[Christophsis]] with orders for Kenobi and Skywalker to make contact with them. Yoda and Windu were then informed that two [[scout]]s had found Jabba's [[bounty]] hunters at an abandoned monastery on the planet [[Teth]]<ref name="TCW film" /> under the guard of two [[battle droid]] [[battalion]]s.<ref name="Ahsoka Tano and Other Force Users">{{EncyclopediaCite|Ahsoka Tano and Other Users of the Force}}</ref> Yoda then arrived with reinforcements, ending the [[Battle of Christophsis|battle on Christophsis]]. He sent Kenobi to negotiate a [[Hutt–Republic treaty|treaty]] with Jabba while Skywalker and his new apprentice, Ahsoka Tano, rescued his son. Despite Skywalker's reservations about Jabba, Skywalker, Tano, and [[Captain]] [[Rex]] organized their [[501st Legion|troops]] and traveled to Teth.<ref name="TCW film" />

Jabba's bounty hunters were [[Dismemberment|beheaded]] and had their heads returned to him at his palace. Kenobi went to Tatooine where he assured Jabba that Skywalker would bring him back his son. However, Jabba said if they did not, Dooku and his [[Separatist Droid Army|droid army]] would. He told Kenobi that he had one [[Standard day|planetary rotation]] to bring back his son alive. After Kenobi left, Dooku spoke with Jabba, telling him that he was being deceived by the Jedi, who were really behind his son's kidnapping. At first, Jabba did not believe Dooku and demanded that he bring evidence of his claims. Dooku assured Jabba that he would, instructing Ventress and the [[RA-7 protocol droid]] [[4-A7]] to get the data he needed.<ref name="TCW film" />

==The battle==
===Scaling the mountain===
{{Quote|Too many droids here for them to be renegades. I sense Count Dooku's hand in this. Let's find Jabba's son and get outta here.''"<br />"''No problem. The hard part's over.''"<br />"''I wish you wouldn't say that.|Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano, reflecting on the early battle|<ref name="TCW film" />}}
[[File:Skywalker_Teth_cliff.png|thumb|left|250px|Skywalker and Tano fighting their way on a cliff towards the monastery]]
Skywalker traveled to Teth, where he was informed by Windu's scouts that at least two battle [[droid]] battalions were holed up at the monastery. Skywalker, Tano, Rex, and the rest of [[Torrent Company]], a military [[Company|unit]] of [[clone trooper]]s, took the [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i]] [[gunship]]s and [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer|AT-TEs]] to land on Teth. They were forced to land below the monastery in order to avoid the [[DSD1 dwarf spider droid|dwarf spider droids]]' [[artillery]] [[fire]]. Skywalker and his forces used their [[ascension cable]]s to climb the side of the cliff. As they climbed, Skywalker and his men were able to defeat the battle droids' outer defenses.<ref name="TCW film" />

Upon breaching the monastery, they found a supposed [[caretaker droid]], 4-A7, who told them where the battle droids kept their [[prisoner]]. Skywalker and Tano made their way through the Separatist defenses and found Rotta, who was [[Disease|sick]]. Ventress and 4-A7 managed to record Skywalker and Tano stuffing Rotta into a [[backpack]], with Skywalker saying he hated Hutts. Ventress sent the [[Holorecorder|recording]] to Dooku, who ordered her to rescue the Huttlet. Dooku then showed Jabba the recording, convincing him that the Jedi had kidnapped his son. Dooku told him that his droid [[army]] was planning to rescue his son. When Jabba asked Dooku what he wanted in return, Dooku asked if he would consider allying the Hutt Clans with the Confederacy.<ref name="TCW film" />

===Storming the temple===
[[File:IAmFear-SWL108.png|thumb|250px|right|Asajj Ventress Force chokes Captain Rex before attempting a mind trick on the clone officer.]]
A [[Unidentified clone sergeant|clone sergeant]] reported to Rex that all remaining battle droids had been destroyed. Skywalker then contacted Master Kenobi about Rotta. He informed Kenobi of Rotta's medical condition and the Separatist involvement. However, Skywalker, his [[R2-series astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]], and his forces came under attack by Ventress' reinforcements. Kenobi told them that he would bring Republic reinforcements to the planet. Skywalker, Tano, Rex and their forces retreated into the monastery, sealing the doors. Tano convinced her master that they had to get Rotta back to his [[Parent|father]] breathing by finding a way out. Skywalker left with Ahsoka and Rotta, while Rex and his men stayed behind to hold back the enemy forces.<ref name="TCW film" />

After Ventress cut the locks, the droids stormed the monastery, [[Death|subduing]] the [[Cloning|clones]]. As Ventress ordered her troops to lock down the monastery, Rex attempted to shoot her but was stopped. Ventress then [[Force choke]]d Rex and demanded that he reveal where Skywalker went. When Rex refused, Ventress used a [[mind trick]] to make him contact the [[Jedi Knight]].<ref name="TCW film" /> However, Rex resisted<ref name="Grand Army Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Grand Army of the Republic}}</ref> and alerted Skywalker by using his first name during the call. Skywalker was then convinced that Ventress was there to kill Rotta.<ref name="TCW film" />

===Final battle===
[[File:Obi-Wan_vs_Ventress_Teth.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Obi-Wan Kenobi dueled Asajj Ventress]]
As Skywalker and Tano moved to rejoin Rex, Rotta spotted a [[Twilight|ship]] at another landing platform. Skywalker decided they would go for it, using a [[can-cell]] to get there. Tano briefly [[Lightsaber duel|dueled]] with Ventress before her master came back for her. Once there, they found 4-A7, who exposed himself as a Separatist [[spy]]. Skywalker and Tano took the [[Starship|ship]] and left. Following this, Kenobi, [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Cody]] and their [[212th Attack Battalion|reinforcements]] arrived and rescued Captain Rex and Torrent Company from Ventress' battle droids. Kenobi confronted Ventress, engaging her in a lightsaber duel, fighting through the monastery. During that [[time]], Skywalker attempted to land on Admiral [[Wullf Yularen]]'s [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Teth)|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]]. However, Separatist [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]] destroyed the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer's]] [[hangar]]s, forcing Skywalker to [[Mission to Jabba's Palace (Clone Wars)|take]] the ship to Tatooine. Kenobi and Ventress [[Force sense|sensed]] that Skywalker was gone. Kenobi offered Ventress a chance to surrender, but instead, she escaped onto a vulture droid. Following this, Kenobi rejoined Rex and his forces in finishing the battle.<ref name="TCW film" />

==Aftermath==
===Bringing Rotta home===
{{Main|Mission to Jabba's Palace (Clone Wars)}}
{{Quote|The Republic had too many clone troopers, my lord. By the time we were able to find Jabba's son, Skywalker had already killed him.''"<br />[Jabba yells]<br />"''Most unfortunate. This is a very unexpected turn of events. I take it you at least defeated the Jedi?''"<br />"''No, Master. The Jedi escaped and are headed to Tatooine.''"<br />"''I'm sure you did the best you could. We will discuss your failure later.''"<br />"''Yes, my master.|Asajj Ventress and Count Dooku, detailing a false report of the battle to deceive Jabba the Hutt.|<ref name="TCW film" />}}
After escaping, Ventress contacted Dooku and Jabba, telling them that the battle was lost and lying that Skywalker had killed Rotta. Despite this, Dooku convinced Jabba to let him take of care of Skywalker. Due to Dooku's lies, Jabba refused to accept any communications from [[Chancellor]] Palpatine and the Jedi. All the while, thanks to the sudden eruption of the Battle of Teth and several other engagements in the Outer Rim, Palpatine implemented a series of new security measures on<ref name="TCW film" /> his homeworld, the planet<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Naboo]]. Naboo [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]&mdash;who had originally wanted to discuss those security measures but was distracted by the news that Skywalker, secretly her husband, had been blamed for the kidnapping&mdash;tried to broker a treaty with the Hutts through Jabba's uncle, Ziro. However, Amidala learned that Ziro was conspiring with Dooku in order to take control of the Hutt clans. Ziro took her prisoner, but she was [[Raid on Ziro's Palace|saved]] by her [[protocol droid]], [[C-3PO]], who alerted [[Commander]] [[Fox]] and his [[Coruscant Guard]]. As Skywalker and Tano prepared to land on Tatooine, they came under attack by [[IG-100 MagnaGuard|MagnaGuards]] in [[Rogue-class Porax-38 starfighter|''Rogue''-class Porax-38 starfighters]]. Despite destroying them, Skywalker and Tano were forced to crash-land in the [[Dune Sea]].<ref name="TCW film" />

[[File:Count_Dooku_speaks_with_Darth_Sidious.png|thumb|300px|Darth Sidious and Count Dooku speak after the Mission to Jabba's Palace]]
Skywalker sensed that Dooku was coming to finish the job, so he sent Ahsoka and R2 to take Rotta to Jabba's Palace. Skywalker was found by Dooku and dueled him in order to distract the [[Sith Lord]]. However, Dooku anticipated this and had prepared his MagnaGuards for it. Skywalker was able to subdue Dooku and leave him in order to rescue his Padawan before she was killed by Jabba. As Skywalker entered the palace, Jabba told him that his son was not there. Skywalker threatened Jabba, despite telling him that he would die. Fortunately, Tano arrived with his son, alive. Still furious, Jabba ordered them to be [[Death penalty|executed]]. As his henchmen pulled out their [[blaster]]s, Jabba was contacted by his uncle Ziro, but it was actually Amidala who revealed Ziro's part in Dooku's scheme. Jabba, seeing he had been deceived, then allowed the Republic access to to his space lanes and asked that Dooku be brought to justice. Despite the setback, [[Darth]] Sidious assured Dooku that the engines of war had turned in their favor.<ref name="TCW film" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|This was one of our first missions. Why are we here?''"<br />"''You tell me.|Ahsoka Tano and Anakin Skywalker, reliving the Battle of Teth|<ref name="Part Five">{{Ahsoka|Part Five: Shadow Warrior}}</ref>}}
The Battle of Teth<ref name="DB:Ahsoka">{{Databank|ahsoka-tano|Ahsoka Tano}}</ref> had ended as one of the first of many missions Skywalker and Tano undertook together.<ref name="Part Five">{{Ahsoka|Part Five: Shadow Warrior}}</ref> Together, the two formed a formidable team.<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> however, Tano would end up leaving the Jedi Order after she was falsely accused of a [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|terrorist bombing]].<ref name="TWJ">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref> She embarked on the path of a [[Force-Sensitive Outcast]], while Skywalker later fell to the [[dark side of the Force]] as the Sith Lord [[Darth]] Vader.<ref name="Part Five" />

[[File:Acme-TheBattlesNotOverYet.png|thumb|left|300px|Ahsoka Tano relived the Battle of Teth later in her life]]
During the Battle of Endor of [[4 ABY]], Skywalker would turn his back on the Sith and return to the [[light side of the Force]] to save his son, [[Luke Skywalker]], from Sidious. Skywalker sacrificed himself in the effort and became a [[Force spirit]].<ref name="Timelines" /> After her duel with former Jedi [[Baylan Skoll]]<ref name="Part Five" /> in [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Ahsoka series date">{{AhsokaSeriesDate}}</ref> Tano found herself in the [[World Between Worlds]] with Skywalker, who promised to finish her training. As part of her final lesson, she relived<ref name="Part Five" /> the Battle of Teth as a teenager again.<ref name="DB:Ahsoka" /> She was surprised to be back in the Clone Wars before rushing into combat, soon<ref name="Part Five" /> reliving the [[Battle of Ryloth]] and the [[Siege of Mandalore]] as well.<ref name="DB:Ahsoka" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Development===
The Battle of Teth appeared in the [[2008]] [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]].<ref name="TCW film" /> The events of Rotta the Huttlet's capture were originally a [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)#Production|three-episode story arc]] in the 2008 [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' television series']] [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]],<ref name="pablo">{{JCFCite|url=posts/29437817|board=Live Action, Clone Wars & Classics|thread=The Clone Wars- Episode 106: Downfall of a Droid Discussion Thread (Spoilers Allowed)|user=[[Pablo Hidalgo|Pabawan]]|date=2008-11-11|time=6:23 PM|archivedate=20160427093053}}</ref> but after producer [[George Lucas]] decided to debut the series with a theatrical release, the episodes were combined with another first-season episode entitled "The New Padawan" to form ''The Clone Wars'' film.<ref name="DB Christophsis">{{DB|location|christophsis|Christophsis}}</ref>

[[File:Asajj_vs_Anakin_Teth.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Anakin Skywalker dueled Asajj Ventress on Teth in a deleted scene from the Clone Wars film.]]
Concept art of the Battle of Teth was done by supervising director [[Dave Filoni]] and [[Thang Le]] and storyboards of the duel between Ventress and Kenobi in the pillar room were done by [[Steward Lee]].<ref name="Art of TCW">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''</ref> Three notable parts of the battle were cut from the final movie, despite being shown in the promotional material for the film: the duel in the monastery [[rancor]] pit, Skywalker and Tano's fight with the Vulture droid on the monastery's outer platform, and Tano's emptying of the ''Twilight''<nowiki/>'s cargo hold. Filoni later commented that he removed the rancor sequence as he had wanted to tell new stories that "feel" like ''[[Star Wars]]'' without repeating the original ''Star Wars'' itself.<ref name="TheForce message boards">{{JCFCite|url=threads/29628723|board=Live Action, Clone Wars & Classics|thread=TheForce.Net interviews Henry Gilroy... and Dave Filoni !!! (Your questions answered!)|archivedate=20170207114253}}</ref> The rancor was originally designed as a "cartoonified" version of the creature from ''[[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|Return of the Jedi]]'', but was changed to the new [[jungle rancor]]. Lightning concept art of the rancor scene was done by Le.<ref name="Art of TCW" />

===Appearance in the ''Ahsoka'' television series===
The identity of what battle Ahsoka Tano first relived in "[[Part Five: Shadow Warrior]]" was left unconfirmed in the episode.<ref name="Part Five" /> When the [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] was updated with information from ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]'' in late [[2023]], it was confirmed the engagement was the Battle of Teth.<ref name="DB:Ahsoka" />
