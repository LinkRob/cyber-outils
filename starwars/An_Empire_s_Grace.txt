'''''An Empire's Grace''''' was a sculpture created by the [[sculptor]] [[Covik]], composed of [[beskar]]. The sculpture was acquired by [[Ronen Tagge]], who brought Covik in to debate the quality of his work compared to a [[painting]] made by [[Falber]]. Tagge destroyed both ''An Empire's Grace'' and Falber's painting as both [[artist]]s watched.<ref name="Aphra 1" />
