'''Chromium''', also known as '''chrome''', was a rare and valuable elemental [[:Category:Metals|metal]] that could be used as [[currency]]<ref name="Uprising" /> as well as plating for a [[starship]]. In the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] culture, a ship plated with chromium signified the presence of [[Monarch of Naboo|royalty]].<ref name="SOTG">''[[Star Wars: Ships of the Galaxy]]''</ref> Even after stepping down as [[Queen]] of [[Naboo]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] continued to travel in ships fully clad in chromium.<ref name="Complete Visual New">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> Chromium was mined from one of Naboo's [[moon]]s.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> The lightsaber hilt of the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Charlin Plaka]] was made of chromium.<ref name="Inquisitor">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''</ref>

To resist interdimensional shift stress, [[hyperdrive]] systems typically required a specific alloy of chromium and [[titanium]].<ref name="TL">{{StoryCite|story=True Love|book=Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I}}</ref> Pure chromium had the ability to block communications.<ref name="Foundry Raid">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;The Forgotten Foundry: "The Motherlode"</ref>

The [[Phasma's armor|armor]] worn by [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] of the [[First Order]] was coated in chromium salvaged from a Naboo [[yacht]] once [[Palpatine's yacht|owned]] by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. The polished finish of that armor was capable of reflecting certain harmful [[radiation]]s<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and blaster fire-proof.<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> [[Phasma's blaster rifle]] was also coated in chromium.<ref name="TFAVD" /> [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] had a [[Lando Calrissian's SE-14r light repeating blasters|pair]] of [[SE-14r light repeating blaster]]s plated in brushed chromium.<ref name="Solo Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>

In the [[Anoat sector]], the [[Trandoshan]] [[Brask]], who operated a requisitions depot on [[Burnin Konn]] during the [[Iron Blockade]], offered select inventory in exchange for chromium.<ref name="Uprising" />

Despite its [[gold]]-like shine, [[C-3PO]]'s hull actually had a chromium finish.<ref name="rebelsvisual">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>

The ''[[Libertine]]'' had a chromium-detailed streamlined [[hull]].<ref name="TLJ: ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>

Chrome shared its name with various [[Clothing|costumes]] and [[weapon]]s used by [[Grand Arena]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Slingshot]]<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date2">{{HuntersDate}}</ref>
