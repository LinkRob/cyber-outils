{{Quote|You must go to the moon of the planet Dawk and find a Devaronian named ''Coir Cion.''|Darth Sidious, to Darth Maul|<ref name="DM-BW&R 3" />}}
'''Dawk''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that was [[orbit]]ed by a [[Moon of Dawk|moon]]. During the [[Republic Era]],<ref name="DM-BW&R 3" /> between around [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Darth Maul – Black, White & Red 3|''Darth Maul – Black, White & Red'' 3]] take place during [[Maul|Darth Maul's]] [[time]] as the [[Sith apprentice]] of [[Darth Sidious]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to between around [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]].</ref> the [[Devaronian]] criminal [[Coir Cion]] operated on the [[moon]] of Dawk. The [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] [[Mission to the moon of Dawk|dispatched]] his [[Sith apprentice]], [[Maul|Darth Maul]], to [[Death|kill]] Cion and his associates after the Devaronian threatened to blackmail [[Senator]] Sheev Palpatine&mdash;Sidious's public persona&mdash;with information on his underworld dealings.<ref name="DM-BW&R 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dawk was mentioned in the [[Darth Maul – Black, White & Red 3|third issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Darth Maul – Black, White & Red]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries, written by [[Erica Schultz]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="DM-BW&R 3" /> on [[June 26]], [[2024]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/110340/star_wars_darth_maul_-_black_white_red_2024_3|text=Star Wars: Darth Maul &ndash; Black, White & Red (2024) #3}}</ref>
