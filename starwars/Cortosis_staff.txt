'''Cortosis staffs''' were staffs made of [[cortosis]], a material that was able to block [[lightsaber]] blades. The staffs cost 2,500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cortosis staffs were first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
