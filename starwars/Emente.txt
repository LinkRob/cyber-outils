'''Ementes''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] with six [[eye]]s.<ref name="LRM" /> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date Part 3">{{LRMDate|3}}</ref> an [[Unidentified Emente|Emente]] managed [[speeder]] rentals at a busy [[Unidentified Witzer spaceport|spaceport]] on the outskirts of [[Witzer]], the second largest urban area on the [[planet]] [[Sern Prime]]. When the criminals [[Jaylen Vrax]] and [[ND-5]] arrived at the [[spaceport]], she approved their rental, although four of her eyes lingered on ND-5 longer then Vrax. In response, ND-5 informed her that some visible damage to his chestplate was not a risk to anyone, and she offered a weak smile in return. After she explained the terms of the rental contract to Vrax, she carried out a retinal scan on him with a [[datapad]] and nodded when it was complete.<ref name="LRM" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], an Emente appeared in the [[2026]] novel ''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon]]'', which was written by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]].<ref name="LRM" /> The [[Emente|species]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it appeared in the [[2007]] novel ''[[Legacy of the Force: Sacrifice]]'', which was written by [[Karen Traviss]].<ref name="S">''[[Legacy of the Force: Sacrifice]]''</ref>
