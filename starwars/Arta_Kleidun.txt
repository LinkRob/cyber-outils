'''Arta Kleidun''' was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]] who owned a [[clothing]] shop called [[Black Spire Outfitters]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]]. Her handmade work was well known and well worn throughout the Outpost, with many residents sporting her [[Old Republic-era]] referential clothes.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Biography==
Before [[34 ABY]], Kleidun developed her unique style of long, flowing pieces using deep colored dyes. Once she opened a store to sell her clothes, the Twi'lek woman purchased a sewing and mending droid, [[GX-8]], and brought on additional clothiers to mimic her style.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

In 34 ABY, the [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]] met her shortly after arriving in the outpost. Kleidun, who was attired in a [[Color|magenta]] kurta, assisted Moradi in selecting a shawl in the local style.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> Later that year, [[Eloc Throno]] visited the shop and noted Kleidun's impact on Batuuan fashion.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />
[[File:GX-8.png|left|thumb|170x170px|Kleidun's GX-8 droid]]

==Personality and traits==
Kleidun was a fastidious, stylish woman who could discern her clienteles' unsaid needs behind their requests.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> She ignored the latest fashion trends from the core worlds, and instead she maintained her own artistic voice. Her store reflected her same humility and individuality.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />
