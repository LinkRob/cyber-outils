'''Concordia Faceoff''' was a term used to describe a maneuver in which a [[Fang-class Protectorate fighter|Fang Fighter]] [[pilot]] would use their [[Starship|ship]] to perform head-on charges upon their attackers, using the ships' narrow attack profile to their advantage.<ref name="Zealous Recruit">{{FFGXW2|set=Fang Fighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Zealous Recruit}}</ref> Notable practitioners of this maneuver included the [[Mandalorian]] pilots [[Fenn Rau]],<ref name="Fenn Rau">{{FFGXW2|set=Fang Fighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Fenn Rau}}</ref> [[Joy Rekkoff]],<ref name="Joy Rekkoff">{{FFGXW2|set=Fang Fighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Joy Rekkoff}}</ref> [[Kad Solus]],<ref name="Old Teroch">{{FFGXW2|set=Fang Fighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Old Teroch}}</ref> and [[Teroch]].<ref name="Kad Solus">{{FFGXW2|set=Fang Fighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Kad Solus}}</ref>
