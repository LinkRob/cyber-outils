"'''Choices of Zahn!'''" is an article written by [[Jonathan Wilkins]] which appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 127|''Star Wars Insider'' 127]]. In the article, he interviews author [[Timothy Zahn]] about the early days of the [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]] and his new novel, ''[[Choices of One]]''.<ref name="sw127" />

Zahn acknowledges [[Wookieepedia]] as one of the resources he uses to keep up with the Expanded Universe.<ref name="sw127" />
