A '''corps''' was a large military formation used by various factions throughout [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]]. The term could also be used for administrative branches and services, such as an [[officer corps]], [[diplomat]]ic corps, or [[engineer]]ing corps. Operational corps included [[Grand Army of the Republic]] units such as the [[41st Elite Corps]] and the [[327th Star Corps]].

==Description==
===Administrative===
[[File:DarthVaderStormtrooperArmy-2015DarthVader11.png|thumb|left|250px|Darth Vader gathers members of the stormtrooper corps]]
Corps was used to denote specific groups within a military branch. The [[Imperial Army]] used a variety of corps for their specialized army branches, such the [[Imperial Armor Corps]] and its subdivision, the [[Imperial Artillery Corps]].<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> The Imperial Army also maintained a [[Imperial Vehicle Corps|combat driver corps]],<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> an [[Imperial Vehicle Corps]],<ref name="Unwelcome Arrival">"[[Unwelcome Arrival]]"</ref> the [[Army Air Corps]], the [[Army Engineering Corps]], and the [[Stormtrooper Corps]].<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> The Stormtrooper Corps itself used corps to denote several of its [[stormtrooper]] branches, such as the [[range corps]] and the [[scout trooper corps]].<ref name="Imperial Troops">{{EncyclopediaCite|Imperial Troops}}</ref> Previously, the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] similarly used the [[flame trooper corps]]<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> and [[Ranger Corps]].<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>

The [[Imperial Navy]] used corps within their administration as well, such as the [[logistics corps]].<ref name="The Sith of Datawork">{{StoryCite|story=The Sith of Datawork|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> The [[First Order Navy]] maintained a [[Transport Corps]].<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The term was also used in less military organizations such as the [[Imperial Survey Corps]].<ref name="MT">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref> A [[Starfighter Corps]] was an entire sub-branch of military units for [[starfighter]]s, [[bomber]]s, and other light craft.<ref name="DB:Rebel pilots">{{Databank|rebel-pilots|Rebel Pilots}}</ref> [[Officer corps]] were used by various militaries for their [[officer]]s, such as the [[Imperial Officer Corps]]<ref name="Vader 12">[[Darth Vader (2017) 12|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 12]]</ref> and [[Imperial Navy's officer corps]],<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> or the [[rebel officer corps]].<ref name="Lead">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref>

===Operational===
A corps was a large military formation deployed in surface warfare. In the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], a corps consisted of 36,864 [[clone trooper]]s and was led by a [[Clone Marshal Commander]] and [[Jedi General]]. Each corps was divided into sixteen smaller divisions known as [[regiment]]s, each led by a [[Clone Commander]]. During the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], the Republic deployed eight corps of [[trooper]]s to the [[Geonosis|planet]].<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> The Imperial Army continued the use of corps in their forces, such as the [[Galactic Marines|21st Nova Corps]]<ref name="HC 69">{{HelmetCollectionCite|69|Helmets}}</ref> and the [[91st Mobile Reconnaissance Corps]].<ref name="91st Recon FC">{{ForceCollection|Clone Trooper: 91st Reconnaissance Corps|star=3}}</ref> The [[First Order]] also made use of corps in their [[First Order military|military forces]], with an [[First Order Army|army]] corps composed of 36,000 [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]].<ref name="Complete">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref>
