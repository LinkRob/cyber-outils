'''Eiram's western isles''' were a chain of islands west of [[Erasmus Capital City]] on [[Eiram]]. During the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]]<ref name="Convergence" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> they were attacked by [[E'ronoh's military|E'roni forces]] which forced many of its inhabitants to flee to the [[capital]] [[city]] or more precisely to the [[Rayes Canal]].<ref name="Convergence" />
