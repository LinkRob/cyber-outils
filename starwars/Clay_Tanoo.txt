'''Clay Tanoo''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] [[colonist]] on the [[planet]] [[Cyphar]] who had training in plant genetics as well as in organic chemistry. His [[Clay Tanoo's brother|brother]] had taught him how to refine raw [[Scarn (spice)|scarn]] into addictive [[spice]]. Along with [[Brigte Polcery]] and [[Lenora Scath]], Tanoo formed a conspiracy that hired [[Nevil Cygni|Nightswan]] to help them smuggle the spice off Cyphar inside [[shellfish]]. After learning of the smuggling operation [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] burned the entire spice vein and arrested Tanoo redhanded on [[Afe clan]]'s territory, where Tanoo and several others had been digging for the spice.<ref name="Thrawn" />
