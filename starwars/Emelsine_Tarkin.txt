[[Lady]] '''Emelsine Tarkin''',<ref name="Heart to Heart" /> or '''Emie''' for short, was a [[human]]<ref name="Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone 1" /> [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Heart to Heart" /> who was a member of the [[Tarkin family]] who lived on<ref name="Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone 1" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 10">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 10|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 10]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Eriadu]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. When her cousin [[Sevran Tarkin]] took control of the family after the [[Nihil]] [[invasion of Eriadu]] and began icing out the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Galactic Republic]] members on Eriadu, Emelsine remained the only family member who would tell the Republic and [[Jedi]] things about the family while it decided where it stood in the conflict.<ref name="Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone 1" />
