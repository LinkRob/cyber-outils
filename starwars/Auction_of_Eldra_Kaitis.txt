An '''auction''' was held by [[crime lord]] [[Xev Xrexus]] of the [[Xrexus Cartel]] for the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Eldra Kaitis]]<ref name="Darth Maul 2" /> in [[33 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> The auction, hosted at a [[Xev's hideout|space station]] orbiting the [[Moon of Drazkel]], was attended by several major criminal organizations, including the [[Pyke Syndicate]], the [[Moogan]]s, [[Black Sun]],<ref name="Darth Maul 2" /> the [[Droid Gotra]]<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> and the [[Ohnaka Gang]]. Additionally, it drew the attention of the [[Sith apprentice]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]], who wanted to [[Death|kill]] Kaitis and satiate his thirst for Jedi.<ref name="Darth Maul 2" />

The auction was ultimately won by [[Jee Kra]],<ref name="Darth Maul 3">[[Darth Maul (2017) 3|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 3]]</ref> although Kra would be killed by Darth Maul. The [[Sith]] would [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] Kaitis and ultimately kill her.<ref name="Darth Maul 5">[[Darth Maul (2017) 5|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 5]]</ref>
