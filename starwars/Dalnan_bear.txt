'''Dalnan bears''' were a type of [[bear]] from the [[planet]] [[Dalna]] that stole [[farmer]]s' crops and cattle.<ref name="Cataclysm" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Night of Sorrow]] to [[382 BBY]].</ref> when the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[The Force|Force]] [[religion]] on Dalna anticipated an [[Night of Sorrow|attack]] from the [[Jedi]], the [[Mirialan]] [[Binnot Ullo]] told the [[human]] [[Axel Greylark]] that he would be fighting with the unskilled farmers, though he admitted that even the farmers had more experience than Greylark because of their having to deal with Dalnan bears and other beasts.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dalnan bears were mentioned in [[The High Republic: Cataclysm|''Cataclysm'']], a novel written by [[Lydia Kang]] and published in [[2023]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Cataclysm" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase II">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-cataclysm-excerpt|text=The Jedi Arrive at Dalna in ''The High Republic: Cataclysm'' - Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref>
