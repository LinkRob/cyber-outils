'''Daghee''' was a backwater<ref name="Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> in the [[Daghee system]] in the [[Thusa sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]], in grid square O-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Soh family]]&mdash;including<ref name="Rising Storm" /> the [[human]]<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> [[Lina Soh]] and her<ref name="Rising Storm" /> human<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> [[Gender|son]] [[Kitrep Soh]]&mdash;[[Homeworld|hailed]] from Daghee.<ref name="Rising Storm" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], Lina [[Senator|reprensented]] the planet in the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] until she was [[Supreme Chancellor election, 234 BBY|elected]] as [[Supreme Chancellor]] around [[234 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Chronicles date">{{ChroniclesoftheJediDate}}</ref> the [[Vurk]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Harli Cogra]] [[Author|wrote]] a [[book]] titled ''[[Chronicles of the Jedi]]'', which detailed the [[Jedi]] of the High Republic Era, and included a mention of Daghee in the fourth chapter of the book within a section dedicated to Lina.<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi" />
