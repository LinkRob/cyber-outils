'''Adrenaline''' was a substance that naturally occurred in the body of [[human]]s<ref name="Time">{{StoryCite|story=For the Last Time|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> and the [[Zillo Beast]].<ref name="Meta">{{TBB|Metamorphosis (episode)}}</ref> Medical kits could include injectible vials of adrenaline and other substances.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==History==
Shortly after the formation of the [[Galactic Empire]], an [[Aurebesh]] data readout accessed by [[Clone Force 99]] member [[Tech]] aboard the ''[[Marauder (Omicron-class)|Marauder]]'' noted that the creature had a "HIGH ADRENALINE" level.<ref name="Meta" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Imperial officer]] [[Firmus Piett]] felt a surge of adrenaline when he entered [[Darth Vader's meditation chamber]] aboard the ''[[Executor]]'' and realized just what Lord Vader could do, and just what it meant to him.<ref name="Time" /> After [[The Contessa]] assigned [[Wedge Antilles]] to cobble together a makeshift [[squadron]] to neutralize a new [[pirate]] threat, the two got into a discussion about the role of adrenaline in combat.<ref name="Point" /> When attacking an [[Stormtrooper|Imperial stormtrooper]], [[Isabalia]] was propelled by fear and adrenaline as she punched him with all she had.<ref name="Clouds">{{StoryCite|story=Beyond the Clouds|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> When [[Willrow Hood]] attacked a stormtrooper on [[Cloud City]], he whipped a [[camtono]] into their [[rifle]] as desperation mixed with his adrenaline.<ref name="Due">{{StoryCite|story=Due on Batuu|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> When stormtrooper [[Deena Lorn]] defected from the Empire, she thought to herself that "Maybe it was the adrenaline, the fatigue, maybe it was the years of anger and hatred" and also reflected on how she had previously left the [[carbon-freezing chamber]] in Cloud City in an adrenaline-fueled daze.<ref name="Witness">{{StoryCite|story=The Witness|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>
