An '''amphi-mammalian''' were a class of [[species]] that had both [[mammal]]ian and [[amphibian]] traits. One notable species in this class was the extremely rare [[binog]], which was native to [[Bogano]]. At least this species of amphi-mammalian was capable of living for thousands of [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>
