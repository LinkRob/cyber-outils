'''Feethan Ottraw Scalable Assemblies''' was a a design and [[starship]] manufacturing firm based in [[Pau City]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Utapau]] that produced the [[Belbullab-22 heavy starfighter]], the heaviest [[starfighter]] used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|navy]],<ref name="Collapse" /> with a solid combination of firepower, durability and speed.<ref name="Feethan Ottraw Autopilot">{{FFGXW2|set=Servants of Strife Squadron Pack|cardname=Feethan Ottraw Autopilot}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Feethan Ottraw Scalable Assemblies was first mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' reference book ''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', which was written by [[Curtis Saxton]] and released in [[2005]]. The company was confirmed to be [[canon]] by the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', which was released in [[2015]].
