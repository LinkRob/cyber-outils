In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Alexsandr Kallus]] '''captured''' former [[Imperial officer]] [[Jovan]] on [[Lothal]].<ref name="Kallus' Hunt" />

== Background ==
[[Lieutenant]] [[Jovan]] was an [[Imperial officer|officer]] of the [[Imperial Navy]] and had been stationed on [[Garel]]. He was re-assigned to the [[Academy for Young Imperials]] on [[Lothal]] where he was to be arrested by his old friend, [[Imperial Security Bureau]] Agent [[Alexsandr Kallus]], for embezzlement.<ref name="Kallus' Hunt" /> 

== Fugitive ==

==== Academy escape ====
Commandant [[Cumberlayne Aresko]] ordered the Academy to be placed in lockdown as Jovan fled his arrest, with Aresko ordering him to be shot on sight. Jovan fled from [[stormtrooper]] patrol group four that encountered him in one of the hallways, firing at Jovan as he fled toward the hangar bays. They informed command of his location, to which Agent Kallus responded with a pair of stormtroopers, catching up to Jovan in another hallway. However, Jovan managed to get beyond a closing door, cutting off Kallus' pursuit. Jovan then stole a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]] and blasted a hole in the wall, escaping from the facility. Kallus managed to get through the door, but was not able to catch the fleeing Jovan.<ref name="Kallus' Hunt" />

==== Fleeing to Dinar ====
While discussing the situation with Aresko in his office, Kallus was informed that the stolen TIE fighter had been found near the Lothalian settlement [[Dinar]]. Kallus brought a [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] and four stormtroopers to investigate the landing site. Stormtrooper TK-517 investigated the TIE fighter, but found it abandoned. Kallus then ordered for the search to be expanded, heading into Dinar and questioning the local citizenry.<ref name="Kallus' Hunt" />

While searching the settlement by himself, Kallus was stopped by Jovan, who held him at gunpoint and tried to convince the ISB agent to let him go. Kallus refused and as they argued, they were come upon by a pair of stormtroopers. Jovan shot one of the troopers before Kallus could tackle him. The two tussled, with Jovan getting the upper hand and knocking Kallus into the second trooper and fled. Kallus ordered the stormtroopers to remain in Dinar while he went after Jovan.<ref name="Kallus' Hunt" />

==== Chase over Lothal ====
Jovan stole another TIE fighter and flew away from Dinar. Kallus jumped into a second nearby TIE fighter and chased after Jovan. As the pair flew among the rock outcroppings that jotted the surface of Lothal, Jovan struck one of them, but managed to get turned around and targeted Kallus' TIE fighter, causing Kallus to lose his weaponry. Kallus attempted to chase after Jovan, but his craft was too damaged and he accidentally struck one of the outcroppings, forcing him to fly higher. Jovan thought he had escaped, but he too struck another outcropping and crashed.<ref name="Kallus' Hunt" />

== Capture ==
Having destroyed his TIE fighter, Jovan was barely able to crawl out of the hatch. Kallus, who had landed nearby, approached the crash site on foot and despite Jovan's plea for mercy, [[Stun|stunned]] Jovan. Jovan was then brought to the Imperial cruiser in [[Stuncuffs|binds]] and placed in a [[Prison|cell]].<ref name="Kallus' Hunt" />
