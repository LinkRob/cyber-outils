A '''family emergency''' was a heightened state of security for a [[Chiss family]] which could be declared by members of political rank at least [[Patriel]]. During a family emergency, members of that family serving in the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]]&mdash;both [[Chiss officer|officers]] and [[Chiss warrior|warriors]]&mdash;could be recalled to a specific [[planet]] or location to defend their family's interests.<ref name="Greater Good" />

According to the [[Historian|history]] student [[Coduyo'po'nekri]], family emergencies could be declared by member families of the [[Chiss]] [[Great Families]], so long as:<ref name="Greater Good" />
*the family once belonged to the [[Ruling Families]], and
*their family fleet was undercrewed.

A [[Jixtus's plan|conspiratorial scheme]] by "[[Jixtus]]" of the [[Grysk Hegemony]] nearly caused a civil war in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], when three rival Great Families; each allied with a mutually-rivaled Ruling Family; declared family emergencies to attempt to claim a supposedly [[nyix]]-rich [[Hoxim|mining planet]]. The three families sent their family fleets to the world and [[Hoxim incident|nearly attacked one-another]], until [[Senior Captain]]s [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] and [[Irizi'in'daro]] managed to defuse the tensions and bring each family glory through cooperation.<ref name="Greater Good" />

Upon learning of the process of family emergencies from Yoponek, the ambitious [[Xodlak family]] [[Councilor (Chiss Ascendancy)|Councilor]] [[Xodlak'uvi'vil|Lakuviv]] kidnapped and impersonated Xodlak family [[Patriel]] [[Xodlak'oo'nifis|Lakooni]], declaring a Xodlak family emergency by posing as her. Unbeknownst to Lakuviv, the [[Pommrio family]] and [[Erighal family]] had also done the same, calling their Fleet personnel to serve in their family fleets and stake claim to the mining world.<ref name="Greater Good" />

==History==
Family emergencies were declared since at least [[50 BBY]],<ref name="Greater Good date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref><ref name="Coduyo emergency note">When [[Coduyo'po'nekri|Yoponek]] described the events in which the [[Coduyo family]] invoked a family emergency to protect their holdings on [[Massoss]], Senior Aide [[Xodlak'ji'iprip|Lakjip]] identified the events as "thirty-two [[Standard year|years]] ago." Therefore, they must have happened around [[50 BBY]].</ref> when the [[Coduyo family]] declared one to defend the planet [[Massoss]] from a "breakaway alien sect."<ref name="Greater Good" />

The simultaneous declarations of family emergencies by the Xodlak, Pommrio, and Erighal families in [[18 BBY]]<ref name="Greater Good date" /> nearly led to a civil war within the Chiss Ascendancy. The [[Grysk]] "[[Jixtus]]" had a [[Jixtus's plan|plot to destroy the Chiss Ascendancy]] that depended on their family fleets coming into conflict above the planet [[Hoxim]]. However, Senior Captains [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] and [[Irizi'in'daro]] managed to prevent the [[Hoxim incident]] from blossoming into a full-fledged Chiss civil war.<ref name="Greater Good" />
