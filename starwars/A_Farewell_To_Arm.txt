'''A Farewell To Arm''' is a one-minute [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] [[LEGO Star Wars Movie Short|Mini Movie]] that is connected to the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Yoda Chronicles]]'' animated TV series. It is the eleventh video and was released on YouTube on [[October 20]], [[2014]].<ref name="A Farewell To Arm" />

==Plot summary==
[[Jek-14/Legends|Jek-14]] has been attacked by [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] while on his way to retirement. Following a brief pursuit, Jek uses his [[Droid/Legends|droid]] arm to destroy the pursuing [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter/Legends|TIE fighters]]. He then transforms them into a giant baseball bat and whacks Darth Vader's [[TIE Advanced x1/Legends|TIE Advanced fighter]] away.

Jek then travels to meet [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] on the planet [[Dagobah/Legends|Dagobah]]. He tells the venerable [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] that he wants to lead a normal life. Yoda obliges his request and uses [[The Force/Legends|the Force]] to transform Jek's droid arm into a normal [[Human/Legends|Human]] one. Jek thanks Yoda and says that he will be able to lead a more peaceful life now before departing on his [[Jedi Scout Fighter]]. After Jek leaves, Yoda remarks that his life will be quieter now. However, the frogs and trees then explode into fireworks.
