A '''felnox''' was a type of creature that could be found in alleyways in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]].<ref name="Vengeance" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="DateVeng">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'', in which a felnox appears, begins during the [[Battle of Jedha]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[Evereni]] [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Children of the Open Hand|member]] [[Yana Ro]] encountered a family of [[creepmice]] scurrying away from a mangy felnox in an alleyway in the Holy [[City]].<ref name="Vengeance" />

==Behind the scenes==
A felnox first appeared in the [[2023]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]].<ref name="Vengeance" />
