'''Creshaldyne Industries''' was an [[armor]] attachment manufacturing company active around the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The company constructed the [[LV-2 "Bio-Strong" Support Suite]], a [[physiological enhancement system]] manufactured by the industry and made available for purchase around the war's time.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Creshaldyne Industries was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Savage Spirits]]'', a [[2016]] sourcebook for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[roleplaying game]].<ref name="SS">''[[Savage Spirits]]''</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Creshaldyne Industries/Legends|Creshaldyne Industries]] was introduced the ''[[Galaxy Guide 10: Bounty Hunters]]'', a [[1994]] supplement authored by [[Rick D. Stuart]] for the ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="GG10">''[[Galaxy Guide 10: Bounty Hunters]]''</ref>
