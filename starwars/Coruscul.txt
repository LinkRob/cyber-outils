'''Coruscul''' was a [[Planet/Legends|world]] situated in the [[Core Worlds/Legends|Core Worlds]]. It was the site of a hidden [[Fortress/Legends|stronghold]] at one point used by the [[Order of the Silver Jedi]] sect of the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]]. After he was liberated from a dead-in-[[Realspace/Legends|space]] [[Outrider-class cruiser|starship]] at some point during the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic's]] existence, the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side of the Force]]&ndash;affiliated being known as the [[Force vampire]] began travelling to Coruscul in order to destroy the stronghold there.

==Description==
Coruscul was a [[Planet/Legends|world]]<ref name="Polyhedron" /> located in the [[Coruscul system/Legends|Coruscul system]], which was a part of the [[Arrowhead]], or [[The Slice/Legends|the Slice]] portion of the [[Core Worlds/Legends|Core Worlds]].<ref name="TEA" />

==History==
[[File:Jedi_symbol.svg|thumb|right|300px|Coruscul was the site of a stronghold of the Order of the Silver Jedi, a sect of the Jedi Order (emblem depicted).]]
During the reign of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]], Coruscul was the site of a [[Fortress/Legends|stronghold]] of the [[Order of the Silver Jedi]], a sect of the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]].<ref name="Polyhedron" /> At some point between [[86 BBY/Legends|86 BBY]] and [[24 BBY/Legends|24 BBY]],<ref name="Dates">{{PolyhedronCite|104|Dangers from the Dark Side: Villains for the Star Wars: New Republic Campaign, Part Two}} establishes that [[Force vampire|Tel Angor]], who was ninety [[Standard year/Legends|years]] old by the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic's existence]], was transformed into the [[The Force/Legends|Force]] vampire no later than several years before the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]]. ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' dates the [[First Battle of Geonosis/Legends|beginning]] of the Clone Wars to [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]] and the [[Declaration of a New Republic/Legends|establishment]] of the New Republic to [[4 ABY/Legends|4 ABY]], therefore placing Angor's transformation sometime between [[86 BBY/Legends|86 BBY]] and [[24 BBY/Legends|24 BBY]].</ref> an unsuccessful experiment by the former Order resulted in the transformation of the [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] [[Force vampire|Tel Angor]] into the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side of the Force]]&ndash;affiliated being known as the [[The Force/Legends|Force]] vampire. Although the Force vampire proceeded to destroy any records he could find concerning his creation, he never visited the Coruscul stronghold&mdash;the last location where such data was stored&mdash;before he became trapped aboard an [[Outrider-class cruiser|''Outrider''-class cruiser]] that remained dead in [[Realspace/Legends|space]] until after the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] was [[Declaration of a New Republic/Legends|established]]<ref name="Polyhedron" /> in [[4 ABY/Legends|4 ABY]].<ref name="Dates" /> After a New Republic [[Picket ship/Legends|picket ship]] unwillingly liberated the Force vampire, he began travelling to Coruscul aboard the [[Passenger liner/Legends|passenger starliner]] ''[[Dantooine's Pride]]'' with the goal of destroying the stronghold that both served as a last reminder of the Order of the Silver Jedi and contained evidence of the vampire's existence.<ref name="Polyhedron" />

==Locations==
Coruscul was the site of a hidden stronghold that at one point was used by the Order of the Silver Jedi. Records documenting the creation and [[Force power/Legends|abilities]] of the Force vampire were entered into [[Computer/Legends|computer]] logs at the Coruscul stronghold, and a [[Unidentified data journal|data journal]] similarly describing the entity's genesis was also kept there.<ref name="Polyhedron" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coruscul was mentioned in "[[Dangers from the Dark Side: Villains for the Star Wars: New Republic Campaign, Part Two]]," a [[roleplaying game]] source article authored by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and [[Michele Carter]] for use with [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' and published in the [[Polyhedron 104|104th issue]] of the ''[[Polyhedron]]'' magazine in [[February]] [[1995]]. Since that article was released outside of the [[Lucas Licensing]] process,<ref name="Polyhedron" /> it was considered non-[[canon]]ical with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="UnlicensedStatus">{{UnlicensedStatus}}</ref> The [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' placed the Coruscul [[Star system/Legends|system]], and therefore Coruscul itself, in grid square L-10.<ref name="TEA" />
