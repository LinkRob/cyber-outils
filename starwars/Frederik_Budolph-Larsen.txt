'''Frederik Budolph-Larsen''' (born [[December 4]], [[1975]]) is an animator and director who worked on ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Yoda Chronicles]]'' and ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]''. He directed the [[2016]] ''Freemaker Adventures'' episodes "[[Race on Tatooine]]" and "[[Showdown on Hoth]]." Budolph-Larsen also worked on other projects including the [[Wikipedia:Hitman (series)|''Hitman'']] computer games ''Blood Money'', ''Contracts'', and ''Hitman 2: Silent Assassin'' and [[Wikipedia:Lego Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu|''Lego Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu'']].
