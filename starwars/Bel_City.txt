'''Bel City''' was an [[Water|underwater]] city on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Mon Cala]]. In [[18 BBY]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ferren Barr]] and his group of followers were located in an [[Ferren Barr's cave refuge|underwater cave]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]], led by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Barr and his group fled to Bel City to find a way offworld.<ref name="Dark Lord 16" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bel City first appeared in [[Darth Vader (2017) 16|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 16]], a [[2017]] [[canon]] comic written by [[Charles Soule]] and illustrated by [[Giuseppe Camuncoli]].<ref name="Dark Lord 16" />
