'''Chopsticks''',<ref name="Queen's Peril">''[[Queen's Peril]]''</ref> also known as '''eating sticks''',<ref name="Heir to the Jedi">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref> were a type of eating utensil that originated from and commonly used in the [[Aakaash system]] of the [[Oplovis sector]]. Foreigners of the [[star system]] found chopsticks difficult to use.<ref name="Vow" /> On [[Naboo]], [[Captain]] [[Quarsh Panaka]] and his wife [[Mariek Panaka|Mariek]], both of the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]], used chopsticks to eat a late-night dinner shortly after [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] had been elected [[Queen]].<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> They were also used to eat [[Corellian buckwheat noodles]] or [[nerf nugget]]s with [[onion]]s. If one were to order food from the dining establishment on [[Denon]], [[Sakhet's Noodles]], they would receive their food in [[flimsicard]] boxes with disposable eating sticks to eat it. In [[0 ABY]], [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Nakari Kelen]] dined at Sakhet's Noodles, and they used disposable eating sticks to consume their food.<ref name="Heir to the Jedi" />
