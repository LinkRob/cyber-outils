"'''Adversaries: Characters for the Star Wars: New Republic Campaign'''" was a roleplaying game supplement written by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and [[Michele Carter]], published in [[Polyhedron 97|''Polyhedron'' 97]] in [[July]] [[1994]]. Like all supplements printed in ''[[Polyhedron]]'' prior to [[2002]], the article is considered ambiguously [[canon]].
