The '''Ascendancy military''', also known as the '''Chiss military''', was the standing military of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] and was based in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Unknown Regions]], known to the [[Chiss]] as the Chaos. It consisted of the [[Chiss Defense Force]], charged with defending Chiss territory, and the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]], charged with patrolling the Chaos.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> The Defense Force and the Expansionary Defense Fleet were bound by a [[Non-intervention policy|set of laws]] restricting their authority to perform preemptive strikes.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

The mission of the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet was to "watch over Chiss interests in the parts of the Chaos extending beyond the [[Chiss Ascendancy|Ascendancy's]] borders, to learn who was out there and assess their level of threat."<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> In addition to the Expansionary Defense Fleet, many [[Chiss family|Chiss families]] had family fleets that functioned analogously to [[planetary defense forces]] for worlds where that family had a strong presence.<ref name="Greater Good" />

==History==
Long before the [[Clone Wars]], the Chiss Ascendancy participated in the [[Sith Wars]] in "[[The galaxy|Lesser Space]]" before retreating to an isolationist existence within the [[Unknown Regions|Chaos]]. Some time prior to the [[Vagaari pirate operations]], the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet was formed to protect and reconnoiter [[Chiss]] interests beyond the borders of the Ascendancy.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Starships of the Ascendancy military==
[[File:Steadfast-Thrawn6.png|thumb|left|200px|The Ascendancy military employed Chiss heavy cruisers]]
The Ascendancy military utilized a variety of starships. The largest starships class was the [[Nightdragon man-of-war]]. Smaller then the Nightdragon where the two other [[man-of-war]] classification, the [[Groundlion man-of-war]] and [[Firewolf man-of-war]]. Smaller than the man-of-war was the [[Chiss heavy cruiser]]. The fifth largest class of [[capital ship]] was the [[Chiss frigate]]. The ascendancy military also employed [[Patrol cruiser|Chiss patrol cruisers]] and [[Chiss patrol ship]]s.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
