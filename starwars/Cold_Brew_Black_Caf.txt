'''Cold Brew Black Caf''' was a cold [[chocolate]] and [[caf]] beverage available at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] and [[Kat Saka's Kettle]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. The drink was [[Color|brown]] in color and served with cream cheese and chocolate puffs.<ref name="Docking" /> At Kat Saka's Kettle, the beverage had the option of being served with [[alcohol]].<ref name="Kat Saka" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cold Brew Black Caf was added to the menu of Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at [[Disneyland]]'s [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[July]] of [[2021]].<ref name="DPTikTok">{{TikTok|disneyparks|video/6981466366092020997|[[The Walt Disney Company|Disney Parks]]|text=Behold, the greatest cold brew in the galaxy ☕️💫 #Disney #DisneyParks #Disneyland #GalaxysEdge #DockingBay7 #ColdBrew|date=2021-07-05|archivedate=20210710072523}}</ref> It was later introduced at [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]]'s Galaxy's Edge, where it was sold at Kat Saka's Kettle starting on [[May 31]], [[2022]].<ref name="DHS Release">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2022/05/review-delicious-spiked-and-regular-cold-brew-black-caf-coffee-arrives-at-star-wars-galaxys-edge-in-disneys-hollywood-studios|text=REVIEW: Coco Puff-Topped Cold Brew Black Caf & Spiked Alcoholic Coffee Finally Lands in Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge at Disney's Hollywood Studios|author=Shannen Ace|date=2022-05-31|work=[https://www.wdwnt.com WDW News Today]|archivedate=20220531201509}}</ref> At Hollywood Studios, guests can order the beverage with chocolate liqueur, making it an alcoholic beverage.<ref name="Kat Saka" />

[[File:SpikedColdBrewBlackCaf.jpg|left|thumb|240x240px|The alcoholic Spiked Cold Brew Black Caf served at Kat Saka's Kettle]]
The menu description at Disneyland is "Cold Brew Coffee topped with Sweet Cream Cheese and Chocolate Puffs."<ref name="DL Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/breakfast|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Breakfast Menu}}</ref> The description at Disney's Hollywood Studios is similar, reading "Joffrey's Coffee™ Cold Brew topped with Sweet Cream Cheese and Chocolate Puffs." The description of the Spiked Cold Brew Black Caf, as served at Hollywood Studios, is "Joffrey's Coffee™ Cold Brew with Chocolate Liqueur topped with Sweet Cream Cheese and Chocolate Puffs."<ref name="DHS Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=dining/hollywood-studios/kat-sakas-kettle|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios: Kat Saka's Kettle}}</ref>

The chocolate puffs which top the drink have been compared to the popular America cereal [[Wikipedia:Cocoa Puffs|Cocoa Puffs]].<ref name="DHS Release" /> For short while in [[2023]], the chocolate puffs at Hollywood Studios were replaced by a different chocolate cereal compared to the American cereal [[Wikipedia:Cocoa Kripsies|Cocoa Krispies]].<ref name="DFB">{{WebCite|url=https://www.disneyfoodblog.com/2023/08/13/its-a-cocoa-puff-crisis-the-cold-brew-black-caf-has-changed-in-disneys-hollywood-studios|text=It's a Cocoa Puff Crisis! The Cold Brew Black Caf Has CHANGED in Disney's Hollywood Studios|author=Robin Burks|date=2023-08-14|work=[https://www.disneyfoodblog.com Disney Food Blog]|archivedate=20230814142225}}</ref> The original puffs were later reinstated to the drink.<ref name="DFB Restored">{{WebCite|url=https://www.disneyfoodblog.com/2023/09/06/cocoa-puff-crisis-averted-cold-brew-black-caf-is-back-to-normal-in-disneys-hollywood-studios|text=Cocoa Puff Crisis Averted! Cold Brew Black Caf Is BACK to Normal in Disney's Hollywood Studios|author=Robin Burks|date=2023-09-06|work=[https://www.disneyfoodblog.com Disney Food Blog]|archivedate=20240407161741}}</ref>
