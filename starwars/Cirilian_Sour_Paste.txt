'''Cirilian Sour Paste''' was a drink created by the proprietor [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza]] "Aunt Z" that became known as one of the signature offerings of her [[cantina]], [[Aunt Z's Tavern]]. In [[34 ABY]], it was available for order in the tavern. That same [[Standard year|year]], it was mentioned in the [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]]' [[The Life Day Cookbook|cookbook]] in an entry about Aunt Z's [[Cirilian Noodle Salad]] recipe.

==Description==
Cirilian Sour Paste was a beverage created by the proprietor [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza]] "Aunt Z"<ref name="Life Day" /> that could be [[Diet|consumed]] by [[human]]s and [[Kadas'sa'Nikto]]s.<ref name="The High Tower" /> The drink partially shared its name with the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Cirilia system]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> and with Aunt Z's [[Cirilian Noodle Salad]], which was inspired by Cirilian Sour Paste.<ref name="Life Day" />

==History==
[[File:CirilianSourPasteServed-TheHighTower.png|thumb|right|300px|Aunt Z served Cirilian Sour Pastes to her patrons.]]
By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="HT Date">{{ResistanceDate|1|5}}</ref> Aunt Z served Cirilian Sour Paste in her [[Aunt Z's Tavern|tavern]] aboard the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]'', and it became known as one of the [[Cantina|establishment's]] signature offerings.<ref name="Life Day" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="HT Date" /> the [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] ordered three Cirilian Sour Pastes for himself and his [[Team Fireball|colleagues]], [[Tamara Ryvora]] and [[Neeku Vozo]], while in Aunt Z's Tavern. The proprietor accepted their order and brought three of the beverages from behind the bar.<ref name="The High Tower" />

Later that year,<ref name="TD Date">{{ResistanceDate|1|18}}</ref> the mechanics again visited the tavern and ordered three Cirilian Sour Pastes.<ref name="The Disappeared">{{Resistance|The Disappeared}}</ref> At some point after publishing his first [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]]<ref name="Life Day" /> that same year,<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] published a [[Life Day|holiday]] [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]'', and mentioned the beverage when expounding on Aunt Z's Cirilian Noodle Salad recipe.<ref name="Life Day" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I'm excited to share this recipe for Cirilian Noodle Salad. ''[&hellip;]'' This recipe gets its name from the drink Cirilian Sour Paste, served in Aunt Z's tavern on the Colossus in ''Star Wars Resistance''.|Jenn Fujikawa on her work for ''Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook''|<ref name="Life Day Article">{{SW|url=news/the-life-day-cookbook|text=Whip, Whip, Stir: Creating ''The Life Day Cookbook''|author=[[Jenn Fujikawa]]|date=2021-11-01}}</ref>}}
Cirilian Sour Paste first appeared in "[[The High Tower]],"<ref name="The High Tower" /> the fifth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[October 28]], [[2018]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> [[Jenn Fujikawa]], one of the authors of the [[2021]] cookbook, ''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]'', created a recipe for the dish Cirilian Noodle Salad for the work and named it after Cirilian Sour Paste.<ref name="Life Day Article" />
