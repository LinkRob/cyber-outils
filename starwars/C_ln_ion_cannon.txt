The '''C/ln Ion cannon''' was a model of [[ion cannon]] that [[Boba Fett]] had installed on his [[Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft|''Firespray-31''-class patrol and attack craft]] ''[[Slave I]]''.<ref name="BMF65" />

==Behind the scenes==
The C/ln Ion cannon was first mentioned in the non-[[canon]] [[2015]] [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] [[LEGO Ultimate Collector's Series|Ultimate Collector's Series]] set [[w:c:brickipedia:75060 Slave I|75060 ''Slave I'']] in which is was said to be created by [[Brugiss]].<ref name="75060SlaveI">{{LEGOCite|ucs|75060|Slave I}}</ref> It was later introduced into new [[Canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 65|sixty-fifth issue]] of ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' published by [[De Agostini]] on [[March 30]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMF65" /> It was originally introduced into the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity in the [[2007]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Complete Cross-Sections]]''.<ref name="CrossSesctions">''[[Star Wars: Complete Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
