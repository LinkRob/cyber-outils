'''Dillon '''was a famous [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] hero of the [[Galactic Civil War]] whose activities had passed into [[Timeline of galactic history|history]] by the [[time]] of the [[First Order]]. [[Captain]]s in the First Order's [[First Order military|military]] wore an [[Rank insignia of the First Order|armband]] on their [[First Order military uniforms|uniform]] commemorating Dillon.<ref name="TFA VD" /> When [[Resistance]] member [[Finn]] snuck aboard the ''[[Supremacy]]'' with [[Rose Tico]] and [[DJ]] to disable the [[hyperspace tracker]], he wore a stolen uniform with Dillon's name.<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> This led to [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[926]] mistaking Finn's stolen uniform as a sign that his former comrade had been promoted to the officers' ranks. As the two were briefly reunited, 926 said that he never took Finn for captain material.<ref name="Novel">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dillon is most likely named after costume concept artist [[Glyn Dillon]].
