'''CC-1139''' was the designation of a [[clone trooper]] who was created on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] to serve in an [[army]] for the [[Jedi]], [[Grand Army of the Republic|one]] that would soon be used by the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. A [[human]] [[Cloning|clone]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]],<ref name="100Scenes" /> CC-1139 had Fett's [[Color|tan]] skin and height of 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|ft]]),<ref name="Clones" /> and weighed eighty [[kilogram]]s.<ref name="Weight" /> They were also implanted with a [[behavioral modification biochip]] by the [[Kaminoan]] [[scientist]]s to ensure that the [[trooper]] would not resist certain [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|orders]].<ref name="Clones" /> CC-1139 wore white [[Phase I clone trooper armor]].<ref name="100Scenes" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[LEGO Star Wars in 100 Scenes]]'' establishes the scene with CC-1139 takes place during the events of {{Film|II}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]].</ref> CC-1139 carried a [[blaster rifle]] over their shoulder while in formation among fellow clone troopers at a facility on Kamino, and were overseen by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="100Scenes" />

==Behind the scenes==
CC-1139 was mentioned in the [[Canon|non-canon]] [[2015]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars in 100 Scenes]]''.<ref name="100Scenes" />
