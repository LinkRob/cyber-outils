'''Caern Adan''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Balosar (species)|Balosar]] former financial journalist who served as an [[agent]] in both the [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|intelligence branch]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], and its [[New Republic Intelligence|successor]] in the [[New Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Concerned about the remaining [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], Adan recruited Imperial defector [[Yrica Quell]] and several other pilots into a ragtag squadron of pilots codenamed [[Alphabet Squadron]], with their main mission to track down the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]].

==Biography==
===Imprisonment by the Empire===
At some point in his life, Caern Adan served as a financial journalist. He wrote articles about disputes within the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] and the [[Corporate Sector]] solvency when he was arrested by the [[Galactic Empire]]. He remained imprisoned for twenty-seven months without knowing why he was arrested and never being formally charged. Adan became a heavy drinker and developed many fears while he remained in prison, and during his imprisonment became radicalized in his opinions about the Empire. When he was freed he joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> During his time in the prison, he met [[Kairos]] and former Imperial torture droid [[IT-O]]. They escaped while leaving [[Ver Iflan]], who had reprogrammed the droid, behind.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

===Service in New Republic Intelligence===
After [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] death at the [[Battle of Endor]], the absence of a clear successor caused a leadership vacuum at the top of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s hierarchy. Several worlds and systems joined the Rebel Alliance which transitioned into the [[New Republic]]. However, even with this extra support, the New Republic military was spread thin and struggling to transition from a military rebellion to a government that protected its citizens. Adan, along with several cadres of the newly-christened [[New Republic Intelligence]], believed that intelligence was the only thing that could hold the New Republic together. As such, Adan began to push for a greater role of New Republic Intelligence in the war's conduct, specifically identifying threats that the Senate did not consider an immediate priority.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

Primarily among his concerns was the threat posed by the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], an elite unit of TIE fighter pilots commanded by Colonel [[Shakara Nuress]] which went by the name of 'Shadow Wing'. However, the military did not consider Shadow Wing a major priority, busy as it was trying to assert its control over the galaxy's major [[Hyperspace route|hyperlanes]]. To make matters worse for Adan's post-war ambitions for the intelligence service, [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] had made no secret of her plans to demilitarize the New Republic once the war ended.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

As such, Adan decided to form a group of intelligence officers that would track down and disable the 204th Imperial Fighter Wing. He was accompanied by a reprogrammed Imperial [[IT-O]] [[droid]] on Traitor's Remorse, where he recruited former 204th [[pilot]], Quell to join him.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

Adan assembled a [[Alphabet Squadron|squadron]] to help take down the 204th. He hired former Alliance pilot [[Nath Tensent]] and recruited Kairos, Quell, former [[Riot Squadron]] pilot [[Wyl Lark]], and former [[Hound Squadron]] pilot [[Chass na Chadic]]. He then convinced [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] to station his new squadron at her [[Barma Battle Group]], specifically aboard her flagship, the ''[[Lodestar]]''.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

===Seizing Cerberon===
{{Quote|Well, no one can say we didn't try.|Adan to Quell just before his death|<ref name="Shadow Fall" />}}
The ''Lodestar'' battlegroup later conducted the [[Cerberon system campaign]]. The [[Troithe operation]] went on for weeks, and during the fighting, Quell and Adan convinced General Syndulla to lay a trap for Shadow Wing. After the capital was seized, the trap was prepared. Kairos was wounded during the fighting, and when Adan went to visit her, he was subdued and captured, and was then sold to the Empire in an auction.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

Quell and Tensent were able to rescue him, although he had been tortured. They returned to the ''Lodestar''; however, Colonel [[Soran Keize]] unknowingly avoided the trap when he led Shadow Wing on a direct assault on the system, destroying the New Republic assault ship, but getting trapped on Troithe as well. Quell, IT-O, and Adan escaped aboard a [[Unidentified freighter (Yrica Quell)|freighter]]. They crashed on a [[Unidentified planetoid (Cerberon system)|planetoid]] in the system which was unstable and falling into the [[black hole]]. They wandered for days trying to find a way off, but Adan's wounds became infected, leading to his death.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==Personality and traits==
Adan believed that [[New Republic Intelligence]] deserved more resources allocated for them and that they were key to dissolving the remaining Imperial holdouts. He hoped to use [[Alphabet Squadron]] to help convince the [[New Republic Senate]] that the demilitarization was still a good idea and that Intelligence could prevent future threats instead of fleets going to continue to wage wars. Adan was partial to [[Corellian red]] wine.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> He felt guilty about leaving Iflan and the other prisoners behind and kept IT-O around as a reminder. But he was able to manage the guilt by trying to do good.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
