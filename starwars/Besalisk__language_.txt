The '''Besalisk''' [[language]] was the native tongue of the eponymous [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Besalisk]] [[species]]. It consisted of sharp noises that resembled low grunts and a series of barks. As Besalisks were easily able to learn [[Galactic Basic Standard]] and other languages, the Besalisk language was rarely heard off of the Besalisk [[homeworld]] of [[Ojom]], with visitors to the world also rarely learning the language even if they were capable of making all the required sounds. Hearing a [[droid]] speak in Besalisk could be startling and surreal, although most [[translator droid]]s had the language readily available for use.<ref name="SM" />
