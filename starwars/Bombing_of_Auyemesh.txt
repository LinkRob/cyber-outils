The '''Bombing of Auyemesh''' occurred in [[17 ABY/Legends|17 ABY]], when the [[Dark Jedi/Legends|Dark Jedi]] [[Kueller]] had the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] of [[Auyemesh]] bombed as a demonstration of his power.

==Origins==
After [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] had traveled to [[Pydyr]], his [[T-65 X-wing starfighter/Legends|T-65 X-wing starfighter]] had exploded and injured him. [[Kueller]] used his powers to block the [[The Force/Legends|Force]] repercussions of this, so the [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master's]] sister, the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] [[Chief of State (New Republic)/Legends|Chief of State]] [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa Solo]] could only sense that something was wrong, but not know what that something was. Kueller then contacted the New Republic on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]], via a [[Holo-coding|holo-coded]] interstellar link and demanded to speak to Leia.

==The bombing==
Leia answered the call and Kueller claimed that he was behind the earlier [[Bombing of Pydyr|destruction]] that she had felt. As a demonstration that this was caused by him and that he could do it again to any world that he wanted, he had [[Auyemesh]] bombed by [[Droid/Legends|droids]] with detonators concealed inside themselves. He did not reveal to Leia where the destruction had occurred or how it had been caused, but she could feel what had happened through a disturbance in the Force that it caused, which combined with a revelation by Kueller of what had happened to her brother, brought her to the brink of despair.

==Aftermath==
The bombing killed a least one million people and caused a large number of [[Speeder/Legends|speeder]] crashes on Auyemesh. These were soon reported to the New Republic, which allowed the identification of Auyemesh as the site of the bombing. Kueller's message was traced to [[Almania]] and Leia decided to set out there with a taskforce to [[Battle of Almania|stop]] Kueller.
