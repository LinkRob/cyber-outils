"'''Bedtime on Concordia'''" is a short story published in the developer blog for ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' on [[October 28]], [[2014]]. It foreshadows the first appearance of [[Mandalore the Avenger|Shae Vizla]] in the ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Shadow of Revan]]'' expansion, who despite being featured prominently in the promotional material for the original game, was completely absent from the game at the time of [[2011]] release.
