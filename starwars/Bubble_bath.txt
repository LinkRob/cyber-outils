A '''bubble bath''' was a type of [[bath]] that featured white bubbles floating on the surface of the liquid. During the [[New Republic Era]], bubble baths were practiced on the isolated [[planet]] of [[At Attin]], whose population was dedicated to [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]] [[At Attin Mint|production]]. Around [[9 ABY]], the [[Myykian]] [[Gender|boy]] [[Neel]]'s bubble bath was interrupted by his best friend [[Wim]], who climbed onto his [[Neel's home|house]] to knock on the rooftop [[viewport]]. Some [[time]] later, Neel's bubble bath was disrupted again when [[Silvo's pirates]] launched a [[Invasion of At Attin|planetary invasion]], with a large [[Silvo's pirate frigate|pirate frigate]] hovering over the powerless suburbs.

==Overview==
Bubble [[bath]]s<ref name="SC 101 AD">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> were characterized by a mass of white bubbles floating in a liquid-filled bathtub. Bubble baths were taken in the privacy of one's home. An individual taking a bubble bath would have previously removed their upper [[clothing]] and sit in the tub during the bath.<ref name="SC 101" />

==History==
[[File:PeekThroughViewport.png|thumb|left|250px|Wim peeking at Neel as he takes a bath]]
Bubble baths were practiced on the isolated [[planet]] of [[At Attin]],<ref name="SC 101" /> one of the [[Jewels of the Old Republic]] that accommodated [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]] mints.<ref name="SC 103">{{SkeletonCrew|Very Interesting, As an Astrogation Problem}}</ref> The population of the world referred<ref name="SC 101" /> to the credit production<ref name="SC 107">{{SkeletonCrew|We're Gonna Be In So Much Trouble}}</ref> as the "[[Great Work]]," determining its citizens' individual aptitudes at an early age via the so-called [[Career Assessment Test]].<ref name="SC 101" /> Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SCDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> the [[Myykian]]<ref name="Lovitt:2025">{{WebCite|url=https://collider.com/skeleton-crew-season-1-finale-explained-jon-watts-chris-ford|text=From Tak Rennod To Order 66, ’Star Wars: Skeleton Crew’s Co-Creators Answer Your Biggest Season 1 Finale Questions|author=Lovitt, Maggie|date=2025-01-17|work=[[Wikipedia:Valnet#Collider|''Collider'']]|archivedate=20250124182530}}</ref> [[Gender|boy]] [[Neel]] took a bubble bath in<ref name="SC 101" /> his<ref name="SC 101 AD" /> [[Neel's home|house]]'s upstairs [[refresher]] after he took his test.<ref name="SC 101" />

Meanwhile,<ref name="SC 101" /> Neel's best friend<ref name="Neel">{{Databank|neel|Neel}}</ref> [[Wim]] had skipped the Career Assessment Test. On his way to [[At Attin Middle School Gamma|school]],<ref name="SC 101" /> he<ref name="SC 101 AD" /> had happened upon a [[Onyx Cinder|ship]] buried in the [[Forest|woods]] and mistaken it for a lost [[Jedi temple]]. Wim stopped by Neel's place to tell him about his find but did not find him with the rest of the family<ref name="SC 101" /> in the living room<ref name="SC 101 AD" /> downstairs. When asked about Neel, his baby sister [[Tuloo]] pointed upstairs. Therefore, Wim climbed onto the rooftop<ref name="SC 101" /> and looked through the [[Viewport|skylight]].<ref name="SC 101 AD" /> His loud knocking scared Neel, who was sitting topless in the bathtub. The confused boy screamed before asking his friend why he had skipped the test. In response, Wim only asked whether Neel's [[Parent|father]] [[Nol]] still had their tools, which he believed would come in handy when they uncovered what he believed was a [[Jedi]] temple.<ref name="SC 101" />

After Neel and Wim penetrated the starship, they accidentally left the planet alongside the girls [[Fern]] and [[KB]].<ref name="SC 101" /> The [[youngling]]s subsequently had a run-in with a hostile [[Silvo's pirates|pirate crew]]<ref name="SC 102">{{SkeletonCrew|Way, Way Out Past the Barrier}}</ref> but managed to escape from [[prison]] by allying themselves with former [[pirate]] [[Captain]] [[Jod Na Nawood|Silvo]].<ref name="SC 103" /> However, after<ref name="SC 105">{{SkeletonCrew|You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates}}</ref> he<ref name="SC 103" /> obtained the coordinates to At Attin, Silvo turned on the children.<ref name="SC 105" /> When they all arrived on the planet, he posed as a Galactic Republic emissary and demanded an audience with [[the Supervisor]], a [[droid]] tasked with overseeing the Great Work. The younglings, in turn, were sent to their homes, with Fern and<ref name="SC 108">{{SkeletonCrew|The Real Good Guys}}</ref> her<ref name="SC 101 AD" /> mother [[Fara]] keeping Silvo company during the audience.<ref name="SC 108" />

[[File:BubbleBath-SC108.png|thumb|right|250px|A bubble bath disrupted by a power outage]]
Back in his home, Neel took a bubble bath for another time. His bathing, however, was disrupted again when Silvo destroyed the Supervisor, causing a mass power outage in the process. Neel looked up to watch through the skylight as a large [[Silvo's pirate frigate|pirate frigate]] coursed over the suburbs ready to launch an aerial bombardment. The confused boy called his parents before he shortly left the bath to get in touch with the other younglings to repel the [[Invasion of At Attin|invasion]] together.<ref name="SC 108" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|That's the first thing he does when he gets back home!|Jon Watts on Neel's bubble baths|<ref name="Lovitt:2025" />}}
Bubble baths first appeared in ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', a novel by [[Timothy Zahn]] released in [[2020]].<ref name="TACR">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> They were first pictured in the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'',<ref name="SC 101" /> which aired in [[2024]] and [[2025]],<ref name="Premiere">{{Twitter|StarWars|status/1861409832349172011|[[Star Wars]]|quote=Mark your calendars. 🗓️ On December 2, stream the two-episode series premiere of #SkeletonCrew, only on @DisneyPlus.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/QwMYe}}</ref> where Neel—one of the protagonists—took a bubble bath in the first<ref name="SC 101" /> and the final episode of the show.<ref name="SC 108" /> Commenting on the Myykian youngling's hygiene habits, the showrunner [[Jon Watts]] noted that taking a bath was the first thing Neel did after he came back home.<ref name="Lovitt:2025" />

Following the premiere of each of the three [[original trilogy]] instalments, a range of ''[[Star Wars|Star-Wars]]''&ndash;related shampoos and bubble-bath accessories were released on the market in [[1977]], [[1980]], and [[1983]], respectively.<ref name="Newbold:2014">{{SW|url=news/luke-i-am-your-lather-shampoo-soap-and-bubble-bath-from-a-galaxy-far-far-away|text="Luke, I Am Your Lather": ''Star Wars'' Shampoo, Soap, and Bubble Bath}}</ref>
