'''Fasca Tiradé''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Zilkin]]<ref name="Rose" /> who was the [[chief engineer]]<ref name="Helmet Collection">{{HelmetCollectionCite|79|Databank A-Z}}</ref> onboard the ''[[Ninka]]''. He worked with [[Rose Tico]] and other [[engineer]]s<ref name="Rose" /> to learn how to use her [[baffler]] on the [[U-55 orbital loadlifter]]s in order to mask their [[engine]] emissions.<ref name="Helmet Collection" /> This would later prove instrumental in avoiding [[First Order]] detection when attempting to evade them during an [[Battle of Oetchi|attack]] on the [[Resistance]]'s [[Resistance navy|fleet]], but would eventually prove futile when the First Order discovered the Resistance's escape plan.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> He was vulgar and could insult someone in two dozen [[language]]s.<ref name="Rose" />
