[[Time|Sometime]] during the [[Clone Wars]], an intense '''duel''' took place in a collapsing [[Unidentified satellite city|satellite city]] above the [[planet]] [[Duro]] between [[Jedi Knight]] [[Pablo-Jill]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[General]] [[Grievous]]. The [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] left Pablo-Jill injured and Grievous stole [[Pablo-Jill's lightsaber]] from the floating debris.<ref name="Lightsabers" />

==Behind the scenes==
The duel over Duro was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars Lightsabers: A Guide to Weapons of the Force]]'', a [[canon]] [[2018]] reference book authored by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Lightsabers" />
