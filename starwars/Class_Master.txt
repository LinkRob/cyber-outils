'''Class Master''' was a title held by some of the [[Castaways (The Island)|Castaways]] of [[The Island]] on a [[Unidentified planet (The Island)|planet]]. [[Tolar Panro]] was a type of Class Master called a [[Trooper Master]]. Other Class Masters included [[D-0C]], [[Grath (Chiss)|Grath]], [[Ossa Grum]], and "[[The Wise One]]."<ref name="Castaways" />

==Behind the scenes==
Class Masters appeared in the [[2021]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Castaways]]''.<ref name="Castaways" />
