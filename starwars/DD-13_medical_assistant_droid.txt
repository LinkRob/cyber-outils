The '''DD-13 medical assistant droid''',<ref name="JTS" /> also known as the '''DD-13 tripedal medical droid'''<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> or simply the '''DD-13 medical droid''',<ref name="Droid">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Droid in Star Wars|video=UQg6aX7lqTA|nolive=1}}</ref> was a model of [[medical droid]] designed to install [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] implants.<ref name="JTS" /> Its tri-[[Limb|legged]] design granted it stability.<ref name="TVE" /> A DD-13 worked at the [[Grand Republic Medical Facility]],<ref name="Orders" /> where it assisted in fitting the grievously injured [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] with his [[Darth Vader's armor|life support armor]].<ref name="RotS" />

==Behind the scenes==
The script for "[[Blue Shadow Virus (episode)|Blue Shadow Virus]]," the seventeenth episode in the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated television series, which first aired on [[February 13]], [[2009]], originally called for [[TR-350]] to be a tripedal medical droid, rather than a [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|T-series tactical droid]]. This is why [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]] told the [[tactical droid]] "We all want to hear your tale of medical heroics." in the final episode.<ref name="BSVEG">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode017.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Blue Shadow Virus}}</ref> The DD-13 eventually made its series debut in "[[Orders (episode)|Orders]],"<ref name="Orders" /> the fourth episode of ''The Clone Wars''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|sixth season]],<ref name="OEG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/orders-episode-guide|text="Orders" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> which originally aired in [[Wikipedia:Germany|Germany]] on [[February 15]], [[2014]].<ref name="Jedi News">{{WebCite|url=http://www.jedinews.co.uk/misc/articles/star-wars-the-clone-wars-season-six-launching-15th-february-in-germany|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'': Season Six Launching 15th February In Germany|author=Mark Newbold|date=2014-01-16, 11:31 AM, BST|work=[http://www.jedinews.co.uk Jedi News]|archivedate=20170425214828}}</ref><ref name="German TV Schedule">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fernsehserien.de/star-wars-the-clone-wars/folgen/zustand-unbekannt-583331|text=Star Wars: The Clone Wars Staffel 6, Folge 1: Zustand unbekannt|work=[https://www.fernsehserien.de fernsehserian.de]|language=[[Wikipedia:German|German]]|archiveurl=https://www.webcitation.org/6pzs5OjmE}} {{C|[https://translate.google.com/translate?hl=en&sl=de&tl=en&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.fernsehserien.de%2Fstar-wars-the-clone-wars%2Ffolgen%2Fzustand-unbekannt-583331 English translation]}}</ref>
