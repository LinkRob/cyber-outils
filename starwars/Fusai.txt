'''Fusai''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Star Wars 19" /> located in the [[Fusai system]] within the [[Fusai sector]], a [[Sector|subsector]] of the [[Zuma sector]], in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]. It was situated in grid square H-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] operated a [[Alliance Records Office|records office]] on Fusai during its [[Galactic Civil War|war]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Star Wars 19" />

By the time of an [[Prison siege at Sunspot|attack]] at the Alliance's<ref name="Star Wars 19" /> Outer Rim<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Sunspot Prison]]<ref name="Star Wars 19" /> in [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the rebel [[3PO-series protocol droid|protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] had heard from a rebel [[Unidentified R5 series astromech droid|R5 astromech droid unit]] that the office on Fusai was looking for [[protocol droid]] staff. After the siege, C-3PO told the [[R2-series astromech droid|astromech]] [[R2-D2]] that<ref name="Star Wars 19" /> he<ref name="Timelines" /> intended to request a transfer to work at the records office, as he considered the work there more suitable and safer. R2-D2 then asked if<ref name="Star Wars 19" /> he<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> could accompany C-3PO, which the protocol responded to by stating he could not as there were no openings for [[Astromech droid|astromechs]] at the office.<ref name="Star Wars 19" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Star_Wars_19.jpg|thumb|right|200px|''Star Wars'' 19 marked the first mention of Fusai in the current ''Star Wars'' canon.]]
Fusai was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars (2015) 19|nineteenth issue]] of the [[2015]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'']]. The issue was written by [[Jason Aaron]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 19" /> on [[May 25]], [[2016]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/55768/star_wars_2015_19|text=Star Wars (2015) #19}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]]<ref name="Complete Encyclopedia">{{CSWECite|I|299|Fusai}}</ref> [[Fusai/Legends|Fusai]] first appeared, albeit unidentified, as the location where<ref name="Gambler's World">"[[Gambler's World]]"</ref> the [[BRT supercomputer/Legends|BRT supercomputer]]<ref name="The New Essential Guide to Droids">''[[The New Essential Guide to Droids]]''</ref> [[Mistress Mnemos]] operated in the comic story "[[Gambler's World]]." The story was written and illustrated by [[Russ Manning]]<ref name="Gambler's World" /> and published between [[March 12]] and [[September 8]], [[1979]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Classic Newspaper Comics Vol. 1]]''</ref> as part of the daily [[Star Wars (comic strip)|''Star Wars'' comic strip]] distributed by the [[Los Angeles Times Syndicate]] to major American and Canadian newspapers from 1979 to [[1984]].<ref name="Insider2012">{{InsiderCite|Special Edition 2012|The Empire Strips Back}}</ref> The [[2006]] reference book ''[[The New Essential Guide to Droids]]'' written by [[Daniel Wallace]] later identified the location where Mnemos was installed as Fusai.<ref name="The New Essential Guide to Droids" />
