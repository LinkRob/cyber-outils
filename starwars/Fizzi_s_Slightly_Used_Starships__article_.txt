"'''Fizzi's Slightly Used Starships'''" is an article written by [[John J. Richardson III]] which appeared in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 9|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 9]]. The article features illustrations by John J. Richardson III and [[David Pilurs]].

==Contents==
This article serves as a supplementary piece for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', introducing [[Fiz Cor'gril|Fiz Cor'Gril]]'s secondhand starship business based in bustling commerce port of [[Trevi City]]. It includes four characters, Fiz Cor'Gril, [[Garginoolaara]], [[Shanna Kinn]] and [[R5-M1]] as well as seven starships for sale, each with detailed descriptions:

*[[Karbarr's Fortress|''Karbarr's Fortress'']], a modified [[Corellian Engineering Corporation/Legends|Corellian]] [[HT-2200 medium freighter]]
*''[[Twilight Jumper]]'', a [[Ghtroc Industries/Legends|Ghtroc Industries]] [[720 light freighter/Legends|720 light freighter]]
*''[[Cutter's Way]]'', a heavily modified [[Lantillian ShipWrights/Legends|Lantillian]] [[GX1 short hauler/Legends|GX1 short hauler]]
*''[[Storm Killer]]'', a heavily modified Corellian [[YT-1300 light freighter/Legends|YT-1300 light freighter]]
*''[[Rogue Runner]]'', a Corellian [[Gymsnor-3 light freighter/Legends|Gymsnor-3 light freighter]]
*''[[Nautical Star]]'', a 30-meter-long [[Mon Calamari/Legends|Mon Calamari]] [[DeepWater-class light freighter|''DeepWater''-class light freighter]] .
*''[[Wild Star]]'', a modified [[Uulshos Manufacturing/Legends|Uulshos]] [[DPx yacht]]
