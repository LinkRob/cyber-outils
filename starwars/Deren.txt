'''Deren''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as the [[Captain of the Guard|captain]] of the [[Royal guard (Pijal)|royal guard]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]].<ref name="Master & Apprentice" /> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> when [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] planned a revolution to stop the influence of the [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] over Pijal, Deren was obligated to assist her.<ref name="Master & Apprentice" />
