'''Clarr'etu'vilimt''', also known by his [[core name]] '''Retuvili''', was a male [[Chiss]] who served as the [[Clarr family]]'s [[Patriel]] on the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[planet]] [[Rhigar]]. In the aftermath of a [[Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand|battle]] near the planet [[Sunrise]] involving the forces of the Ascendancy and the [[Grysk]]s, Retuvili met with [[Clarr'os'culry]], a member of the [[Clarr family homestead defense force]], and told her about the name by which other Clarr had begun to refer to her: The One They Could Not Kill.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
