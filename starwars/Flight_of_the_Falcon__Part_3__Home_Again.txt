"'''Flight of the Falcon, Part 3: Home Again'''" is the third comic in the ''[[Flight of the Falcon]]'' publishing line. It was published as the secondary story of [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 16|''Star Wars Adventures'' (2017) 16]] on [[December 19]], [[2018]].

==Plot summary==
On the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Felucia]], [[Bazine Netal]] was walking through the [[jungle]]s when the [[Kyuzo war shield|shield]] of the [[bounty hunter]], [[Embo]] went flying into a [[tree]] next to her. Embo pointed his [[bowcaster]] at Netal but she negotiated with him. Since Embo's funding from the [[New Republic]] was cutoff she was willing to pay him if he told her the information he knew on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.

[[Standard decade|Decades]] earlier, [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Nien Nunb]] landed the ''Millennium Falcon'' at a [[Unidentified space station (Lando Calrissian)|space station]] for refueling. Nunb went off to the market to pick up supplies, while Calrissian worked on repairing the [[starship]]. Calrissian was disappointed in the way the current owners, [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] maintained the ship. Calrissian checked over all the systems on the ''Falcon'' and was perplexed to learn that the systems were all fine.

But when he turned around there stood Embo, bowcaster aimed at Calrissian's head. Calrissian did not understand Embo's [[Kyuzo (language)|language]] but when Embo said "[[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star]]," he realized that Embo was after him. But Embo did not know what Calrissian looked like, which he used to his advantage. Pretending to be a cleaner, Calrissian told Embo that he must be looking for Solo but took it too far when saying that they should split the bounty. Embo was about to shoot Calrissian but then a pipe burst knocking Embo back and disarming him.

Calrissian tied Embo up outside the ship and Nunb came back. Calrissian reported that the ''Falcon'' was ready and the two departed the [[space station]].

Back on Felucia, Netal was disappointed that no one could seem to capture the ''Millennium Falcon''. She reluctantly paid Embo but stopped when he told her that the ''Falcon'' had recently resurfaced. Embo provided her with information to where to look next and she went on her way.

==Continuity==
"Flight of the Falcon, Part 3: Home Again" features the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''{{'s}} [[rectenna]], which was destroyed during the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Endor">{{Film|VI}}</ref> However, this comic takes place after the Battle of [[Endor]], as it refers to the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star II]] as having been destroyed. Furthermore, this comic takes place shortly before "[[Hare-Brained Heist]]," which doesn't feature the rectenna.<ref name="Hare">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|annual=2019|story=Hare-Brained Heist}}</ref>
