The '''Desert Isolation''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Bounty Hunter (Imara Vex)|standard costume]]. The [[Clothing|outfit]] was rugged and worn, featuring minimal [[armor]], such as asymmetrical metallic [[Color|dark gray]] kneepads, a red and silver left gauntlet with a flame decal on the side, a orangeish-yellow left shoulder pad, and a larger red and brown right shoulder pad, connected to a red boxy [[shoulder-mounted armament]] housing a golden payload. The rest of the costume consisted of gray pants, tan shin and forearm wrappings, brown boots, belt, and various other bodily straps with silver details, a light gray shirt, tan vest, and short black sleeveless cloak. Unlike most of Imara Vex's costumes, Desert Isolation forwent her signature [[helmet]]s, instead covering her head only in a tan and orange cloth wrapping, with a [[macrobinoculars]] covering her [[eye]]s and her [[hair]] extending out to the side, exposing the remainder of her face.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Desert Isolation was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Imara Vex, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Desert Sandviper]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing the Desert Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" /> It was initially planned to be called "Desert Moratorium."<ref name="Hunters Panel">{{SW|url=news/swca-2022-star-wars-hunters-panel|text=SWCA 2022: 7 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: Hunters'' Panel}}</ref>
