'''Citadel Command''' was a command unit stationed within the [[Citadel]] [[prison]] on the [[planet]] [[Lola Sayu]]. Commanded by the [[Phindian]] [[Commander]] [[Osi Sobeck]], the unit was also operated by several [[OOM-series security droid]]s and the [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]] [[K2-B4]].<ref name="The Citadel" />
