The '''Dennig-Far''', or "False Hope" in [[Galactic Basic Standard]], was an [[Oktarian (language)|Oktarian]] term that the [[Oktarian|people]] of [[Oktaro]] used to describe the three [[Standard year|years]] between the [[Skirmish on Oktaro|liberation]] of their [[planet]] from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] and the arrival of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It came to be because, once the Separatists were gone, the Oktarians thought they could rebuild their civilization for a brighter future, but their efforts were ultimately in vain because of the Imperial occupation.<ref name="TMCAFOF">''[[The Mighty Chewbacca in the Forest of Fear!]]''</ref>
