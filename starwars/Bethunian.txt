'''Bethunians''' lived in the [[City-state]] [[Bethune]] on [[Gansevor]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="The Blade 2" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The present day events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic — The Blade]]'' take place between the [[wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran]] and [[Porter Engle]] [[Siege of Bardotta|earning]] his title the "Blade of Bardotta," both of which are dated to [[382 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> they [[Siege of Firevale|attacked]] the people of [[Firevale]] for kidnapping the [[Sicatra|heir]] to their [[Bethune's royal family|royal family]].<ref name="The Blade 2" />
