The '''biosphere zone''' was a section of the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]] which contained recreations of the natural environments of various worlds of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. As a way to showcase the Outer Rim's diversity of worlds, the biospheres were intended to be periodically rotated. When [[Jedi Master]] [[Loden Greatstorm]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] visited the Beacon during a [[Starlight Beacon conclave|conclave]], available biospheres included the [[jungle]] of [[Dantooine]] and an [[Water|ice]] flat from [[Mygeeto]]. Upon returning to the [[Elphrona Outpost]], the two told [[Indeera Stokes]] and [[Porter Engle]] about the biosphere zone.<ref name="LotJ" />
