'''Candied petals''' were a type of [[candy]] [[Diet|edible]] by [[human]]s and [[Hutt]]s.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> One type of candied petal was a [[candied nightjar petal]].<ref name="Defy the Storm">''[[The High Republic: Defy the Storm]]''</ref> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Storm Date">{{THRContent228BBY|DFS}}</ref> the former [[deputy]] [[Jordanna Sparkburn]] and her girlfriend, [[Sylvestri Yarrow]], kidnapped the [[scientist]] [[Xylan Graf]] while he was attending a party at the [[Starros family homestead|Starros Manor]] on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Hosnian Prime]]. When the two [[Gender|women]] walked on either side of Graf to escort him, he compared them to beautiful candied nightjar petals.<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Nameless Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> while in the [[Greylark family]]'s [[Greylark residence|residence]] on<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> the Core Worlds planet<ref name="Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]], the human [[Zenny Greylark]] and the Hutt [[Churo]] ate [[Alderaanian rose cake]] covered in candied petals to celebrate that Churo's [[Devirsivik Clan|sister]] [[Dahara Devirsivik Norri]] would arrive to take him [[Nal Hutta|home]] that [[Local day|night]]. Norri took longer than excepted to arrive and they continued to eat the [[cake]] while they waited.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

==Behind the scenes==
Candied petals were first mentioned in the young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Defy the Storm]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and [[Justina Ireland]]<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> and published on [[March 5]], [[2024]].<ref name="Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The chapter they were mentioned in was written by Gratton.<ref name="ChapterAuthor">{{Bluesky|tessagratton.bsky.social|post/3lzypgmy75s2y|[[Tessa Gratton]]|date=2025-09-29|quote=Hi! I wrote Jordana and Xylan, and Justina wrote Vernestra and Avon!|archivedate=20251002012604}} {{C|In reply to: "hiya! out of pure curiosity, was curious if you were able to share the breakdown of which point-of-view characters you and Justina each wrote from Defy the Storm? big fan of the book - was hoping to document some of its development!"}}</ref> Candied petals first appeared in the junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]]<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> and published on [[August 27]], 2024. Both novels were published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Guide" /> In the real world, candied flower petals are a type of [[Wikipedia:Candy|candy]] made from [[Wikipedia:Flower|flowers]], [[Wikipedia:Sugar|sugar]], and [[Wikipedia:Egg white|egg whites]].<ref name="Petals">{{WebCite|url=https://frolicandfare.com/candied-flower-petals|text=Candied edible flower petals recipe|work=[https://frolicandfare.com/ Frolic & Fare: A Botanical Blog With Botanical Recipes]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/5WwDZ}}</ref>
