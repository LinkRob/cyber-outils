'''Cantonica''' was a [[desert]] [[planet]] in the [[Cantonica system]] within the [[Corporate Sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The planet was home to the [[casino]] [[city]] [[Canto Bight]], which was a destination for wealthy tourists, gamblers, and war profiteers. During the [[First Order-Resistance War]] in [[34 ABY]], the [[Resistance]] operatives [[Finn]], [[Rose Tico]], and [[BB-8]] traveled to Cantonica on a [[Mission to Canto Bight|mission]] to recruit the [[Master Codebreaker]] to disable the [[First Order]]'s [[hyperspace tracker]].

==Description==
{{Quote|So! Canto Bight! Playground of the most glamorous beings in the galaxy! Big-money card games and high-stakes fathier races! The galaxy's biggest artificial ocean! And the fanciest food this side of Coruscant.|[[Kedpin Shoklop]]|<ref name="Rules of the Game" />}}
[[File:CantoBight-SWBC49.png|thumb|left|250px|The coastal city of Canto Bight]]
Cantonica was a [[desert]] [[planet]]<ref name="Canto Bight DB" /> in the [[Cantonica system]]<ref name="TLJ Junior novel" /> within the [[Corporate Sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> that was home to the [[casino]] [[city]] [[Canto Bight]].<ref name="Canto Bight DB" /> A [[Color|pink]], blue, and green [[nebula]] was visible from the surface of the planet. The city was located on the shores of the [[Sea of Cantonica]], an artificial [[ocean]] considered the largest in the [[galaxy]]<ref name="Rules of the Game" /> that manifested a strange phenomenon of a dark-purple glow on the horizon during sunset. Water evaporated at a rate of several tons per day in the desert climate, making the cost of maintaining the artificial sea astronomical. Further, its creation caused the lower levels of Canto Bight to become damp and dank, with occasional moisture seepage. Sump pumps were utilized full-time nearer the coast in order to keep the water at bay.<ref name="Hear Nothing, See Nothing, Say Nothing">{{StoryCite|story=Hear Nothing, See Nothing, Say Nothing|book=Canto Bight (novella collection)}}</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|I wish I could put my fist through this whole lousy, beautiful town.|Rose Tico, on Canto Bight|<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>}}
Cantonica was part of the [[Corporate Sector]],<ref name="Cantonica DB">{{Databank|cantonica|Cantonica}}</ref> a fiefdom where corporate entities were given free reign to govern their own territory.<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> It was once a quiet, dusty world dotted with ancient cities and ruins from a civilization that few remembered. The deserts became a place for criminals to lie low or ply side hustles, and contained [[smuggler]]s' hideouts and quiet crooks' bars. Eventually, the casino city of Canto Bight was built, transforming the planet into a playground for the wealthy, and the artificial Sea of Cantonica seemingly appeared out of nowhere.<ref name="Rules of the Game" />

Around the [[3 ABY]], [[Kay Vess]] was living in the attic of [[The Broken Hoof]] [[Cantina|bar]] in the city's [[South Stretch Worker's District]]. Upon waking up on one night, she received her data spike from the bar owner [[Bram Shano]], who allowed her to stay there despite some of her many debits and enemies.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

[[File:TLJ-Movie-Storybook-Travel-to-Canto-Bight.png|thumb|left|250px|A Resistance transport pod piloted by Finn and Rose approaches Cantonica.]]
By the outbreak of the [[First Order-Resistance War]] in [[34 ABY]], Canto Bight had become a haven for wealthy tourists, gamblers, and war profiteers who sold weapons to both the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. Traveling via a [[Resistance transport pod|transport pod]], Resistance operatives [[Finn]] and [[Rose Tico]] [[Mission to Canto Bight|visited]] Canto Bight in an attempt to recruit the [[Master Codebreaker]] in order to disable the First Order's [[hyperspace tracker]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

However, Finn and Rose were arrested by the [[Canto Bight Police Department]] for a traffic violation. While in prison, they managed to convince the [[slicer]] [[DJ]] to help them in return for his freedom. The prisoners escaped the cell with the help of BB-8, who tied up the guards. They then managed to flee Canto Bight with the help of the stable hand [[Temiri Blagg]], who released a herd of [[fathier]]s. Finn, Rose, BB-8, and DJ then escaped aboard the stolen [[yacht]] ''[[Libertine]]''.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Behind the scenes==
According to the map in ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', Cantonica is located in an area that [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]] identifies as the grid square R-5,<ref name="Coordinates">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'' places Cantonica in what [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]] identifies as grid square R-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].</ref> however the grid square is far from the Corporate Sector's position on the map.<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> Jason Fry has stated to "disregard that placement."<ref name="JCF2023-06-12">{{JCFCite|url=posts/56701760|board=Literature|thread=The Essential Atlas and Galactic Cartography: Official Discussion|user=[[Jason Fry|jasonfry]]|date=2020-06-12|time=9:38 AM|quote=Yeah, looks like they "made Cantonica fit." I'd disregard that placement.|archivedate=20230822012518}}</ref> [[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] places Cantonica in the Corporate Sector at grid square S-4.<ref name="SWGalaxyMap" />
