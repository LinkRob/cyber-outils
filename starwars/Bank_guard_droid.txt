'''Bank guard droids'''<ref name="Dark Revenge AD">{{MSL|Chapter 1: The Dark Revenge}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> were a type of [[security droid]] employed by the [[Gran]] [[crime lord]] [[Nico Deemis]] at his [[Nico Deemis' bank tower|bank tower]], which was located in the [[banking district]] of [[Janix city]] on the [[planet]] [[Janix]]. The guards carried a [[blaster pistol]], a similar model wielded by their [[Head Boss]], Nico Deemis.<ref name="Dark Revenge" />
