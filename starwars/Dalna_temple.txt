The '''Dalna temple'''<ref name="MtD" /> also known as the '''temple on Dalna'''<ref name="ATOC">''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]''</ref> was a [[Jedi temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Dalna]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. It was run by Jedi Master [[Nyla Quinn]] along with Jedi Knights [[Yacek Sparkburn]] and [[Lyssa Votz]]. The temple was also home to many [[tooka]]s.<ref name="MtD" /> After a [[Wevo crisis|skirmish]] against [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s on [[Wevo]], [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]] sent a [[distress signal]] to the temple, amidst a general broadcast, signals on the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi]] channels, and a message to [[Port Haileap]].<ref name="ATOC" /> [[Vernestra Rwoh]] and [[Imri Cantaros]] visited the temple while investigating disappearances on Dalna.<ref name="MtD" />
