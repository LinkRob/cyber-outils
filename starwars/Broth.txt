'''Broth''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] [[Maz Kanata]] had at her [[Maz Kanata's campsite|campsite]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]].<ref name="Rey Chronicles" /> It was used as an ingredient in [[Purlah Soup]], a type of [[soup]] made by [[Loonoo]] on the [[moon]] of [[Nar Shaddaa]] whose broth was edible to [[rib-cat]]s.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> During the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[time]] on [[Hoth]], [[Hendri Underholt]] left a bowl of broth to sit too long, resulting in an [[Water|ice]] shell being formed.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> [[Bone broth]] was a type of broth made on [[Sorgan]].<ref name="Chapter 4">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 4: Sanctuary}}</ref>
