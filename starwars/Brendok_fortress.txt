'''Brendok fortress''' was a former [[mining]] facility in the [[Northlands]]<ref name="Episode VII">{{Acolyte|Choice}}</ref> of the [[planet]] [[Brendok]], [[Annexation of the Brendok fortress|annexed]] by a [[Brendok coven|coven]] of [[witch]]es led by [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Aniseya]]<ref name="Databank" /> following their [[Exile of Aniseya's coven|exile]]. For many [[Standard year|years]] during the [[High Republic Era]], they operated in peace there.<ref name="Episode III" />

{{Struct-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Introduced in the [[2024]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', the design of Brendok fortress was inspired by the 1947 British film [[Wikipedia:Black Narcissus|''Black Narcissus'']] by [[Wikipedia:Powell and Pressburger|Michael Powell and Emeric Pressburger]], set at a nuns' mountain retreat in the [[Wikipedia:British Raj|Indian]] [[Wikipedia:Himalayas|Himalayas]], coupled with the [[Wikipedia:Hoover Dam|Hoover Dam]] in the United States.<ref name="Crouching Jedi Hidden Sith">{{WebCite|url=https://arstechnica.com/culture/2024/06/star-wars-behind-the-scenes-creating-the-unique-aesthetic-of-the-acolyte|text=Star Wars behind the scenes: Creating the unique aesthetic of The Acolyte|author=Jennifer Ouellette|date=2024-06-26|work=[[Wikipedia:Ars Technica|''Ars Technica'']]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/CuDKv}}</ref>
