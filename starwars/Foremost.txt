The '''''Foremost''''' was an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] in service to the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy|naval forces]]. The ''Foremost'' joined other members of [[Admiral]] [[Carlou Gendling]]'s [[Carlou Gendling's task force|task force]], including the ''[[Thunder Wasp]]'' and two [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvettes]] during the [[battle over Umbara]]. [[Umbaran]] insurgents sent [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]] to attack Gendling's squadron, including the ''Foremost''.<ref name="Thrawn" />
