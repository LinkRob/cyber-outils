The '''3NX-series Protection Droid''' was a model of [[security droid]] available during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Despite their surprising sturdiness, the [[droid]] series was never a top seller, as they had a tendency to complain when shot. At least one 3NX-series Protection Droid joined the crew of a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] at the time of the [[Iron Blockade]].<ref name="Uprising" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 3NX-series Protection Droid first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', a mobile game released in [[2015]]. The droid model is one of the many crew members that can be used on Crew Runs to help collect useful items.<ref name="Uprising" />
