{{Quote|Remote sensors detected an unusual level of Imperial activity in the Hudalla system...Corona Squadron, you will go to Hudalla. Observe the Imperial ships and obtain as much data as possible.|Admiral [[Gial Ackbar]]'s briefing|<ref name="Lost Stars" />}}
The '''dogfight in the Hudalla system''',<ref name="Lost Stars" /> also known as the ''' raid on the Hudalla system''',<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Battle of Hoth and the Second Death Star}}</ref> occurred around [[4 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> shortly before the [[Battle of Endor]] when the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] discovered a meeting of the [[Imperial Navy]]. [[Corona Squadron]] was ordered to survey the [[Star system|system]], and five of their [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wings]] waited for several [[Standard day|days]] to gather intelligence.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

The [[Galactic Empire]] suspected a rebel presence, and sent four [[TIE/IN interceptor]]s to root them out. [[Admiral]] [[Firmus Piett]] gave [[Commander]] [[Ciena Ree]] secret orders to let at least one of the Rebels escape. The Rebel [[pilot]]s noticed the approaching [[starfighter]]s, and began preparations to jump to [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />

The TIEs got into range and fired on the X-wings, starting a short dogfight in which one TIE and one X-wing were destroyed. At one point, Commander Ree had to put herself in front of Commander [[Nash Windrider]] in order to fulfill her orders. They were both congratulated by Piett, as the dogfight allowed the Rebels to be drawn into a trap soon after at [[Endor]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />
