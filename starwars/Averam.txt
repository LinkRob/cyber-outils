'''Averam''' was an astronomical object<ref name="THRA 6" /> in the [[Seswenna sector]]'s [[Averam system]], located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="WesternReaches" /> It was situated in grid square M-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], connected to<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> the [[planet]]s<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref><ref name="Gargantua">{{YJA|The Girl and Her Gargantua}}</ref> [[Eriadu]] and [[Kundu Minor]] via the [[Hydian Way]] [[Super-hyperroute|hyperspace route]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]],  the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Expansion of the Stormwall|expanded]] their [[Occlusion Zone]] territory following an attempted Republic [[Assault on the Stormwall|assault]] on its [[Stormwall]] barrier. As a result, Averam fell within Nihil [[Realspace|space]], cut off from [[hyperspace]] travel with the rest of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="THRA 6" />

Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="TrialsDate">{{TrialsoftheJediDate|23|the [[Nihil]]'s surrender}}</ref> the Nihil surrendered to the Republic following the [[Mission to Planet X (Jedi Order)|Stormwall barrier's fall]], which dissolved the Occlusion Zone as a territory. The worlds of the Occlusion Zone,<ref name="TOTJ">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref> which included Averam,<ref name="THRA 6" /> were welcomed back into the Republic if they wished. However, some opted to retain their independence, while others remained under the control of individual Nihil [[warlord]]s.<ref name="TOTJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current [[canon]] continuity, Averam first appeared on a map in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 6|sixth issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']]. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Harvey Tolibao]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="THRA 6" /> on [[May 29]], [[2024]]<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-799/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-6|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #6}}</ref> as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia initiative's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="THRA 6" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity,<ref name="TLC">''[[The Last Command]]''</ref> the [[Planet/Legends|planet]]<ref name="Rebellion">[[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']]</ref> [[Averam/Legends|Averam]] was first mentioned in the [[1993]] novel ''[[The Last Command]]'', written by [[Timothy Zahn]] and released as the third installment of ''[[Star Wars: The Thrawn Trilogy]]''.<ref name="TLC" /> The planet made its first full appearance in the [[1998]] [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']], developed by [[Coolhand Interactive]].<ref name="Rebellion" />
