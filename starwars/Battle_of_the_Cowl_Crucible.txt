The '''Battle of the Cowl Crucible''' was a surreal skirmish between the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] and a group of [[Mining/Legends|miners]] in the vicinity of the [[Cowl Crucible]]. As the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] navy led by [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] went into the depths of the crucible to retrieve [[Cortosis/Legends|cortosis]], the local Rebels opened fire on the fleet. The crucible was a pathway between the [[The galaxy/Legends|galaxy]] and unknown worlds, possibly another universe. Vader had a strange vision of a [[Soul Calibur|blue sword]], and reached out to it. He ordered his captain [[Mulchive Wermis|Wermis]] to hold the fleet behind after having this vision. Vader [[Telepathy/Legends|called out]] to his apprentice [[Galen Marek]], also in the area, through [[The Force/Legends|the Force]]. Marek was also having a vision, one of a [[Taki (Soulcalibur)|a woman]] clutching the blue sword Vader foresaw. He killed her with [[Force lightning/Legends|Force lightning]] and took the sword. Meanwhile, in the physical world, Marek had passed out, and his ship, the ''[[Rogue Shadow]]'', crash-landed onto an unknown planet. Marek had awoken and mindlessly wandered to the north. Vader followed him to the planet, meeting up with [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Juno Eclipse/Legends|Juno Eclipse]].

This strange imbalance of the two universes was sensed by [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] on [[Dagobah/Legends|Dagobah]], having visions of [[Mitsurugi|a warrior]] killing [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]] and [[Tusken Raider/Legends|Tusken Raiders]]. This warrior suddenly attacked Vader, and Yoda, materializing in this strange world, was attacked by a monstrous being.
