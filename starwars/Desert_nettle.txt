'''Desert nettles''' were a type of [[Plant|nettle]]. The severed heads of the nettles could be used to make [[E'roni tea]], a beverage found on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]] that was [[Diet|potable]] to [[human]]s.<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> while traveling across E'ronoh on the [[sail barge]] ''[[Amaryliss]]'' to [[Aid mission on E'ronoh|deliver]] relief supplies to [[E'roni]] citizens, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]] and E'roni [[Princess]] [[Xiri A'lbaran]] enjoyed servings of E'roni [[tea]] made from the heads of [[desert]] nettles.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
Desert nettles were mentioned in [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], a [[2022]] novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Convergence" />
