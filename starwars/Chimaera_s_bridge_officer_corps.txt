The '''''Chimaera''{{'s}}
 bridge officer corps''' was the [[Officer corps|corps]] of [[Imperial officer]]s stationed aboard the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of the<ref name="Thrawn" /> [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Steps" /> ''[[Chimaera]]''. The [[warship]] came under the command of [[Commodore]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], a [[Chiss]] [[officer]] who arrived with his protégé, [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]]. [[Karyn Faro]] had been promoted to the [[Star Destroyer]]'s [[Executive officer|first officer]] by the [[time]] they arrived. Thrawn would go onto become an [[admiral]], and the ''Chimaera'', Thrawn, and the other officers became part of the [[96th Task Force]].<ref name="Thrawn" />

After Thrawn was promoted to the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[Grand Admiral]] for the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Batonn insurgency|victory]] at [[Batonn]], he was given command of the [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]].<ref name="Steps" /> The ''Chimaera''{{'s}} officers also served in the [[Fleet|armada]], with Faro being promoted to commodore, while Vanto was dispatched by Thrawn to join the [[Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="Thrawn Treason" /> Thrawn and his officers fought against the early [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] in the [[Lothal campaign]]<ref name="Zero Hour" /> and engaged in a [[Imperial–Grysk conflict|conflict]] against the [[Grysk Hegemony]].<ref name="Thrawn Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> Ultimately, however, the Seventh Fleet was defeated by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]], who had fellow rebel [[Mart Mattin]] summon a pod of [[Purrgil]] during the [[Liberation of Lothal]]. These creatures defeated the fleet in [[orbit]] and proceeded to take Bridger, Thrawn, and all others aboard the ''Chimaera'' away into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Family&Farewell">{{Rebels|Family Reunion – and Farewell}}</ref>
