A '''Droid Maintenance Mandatory Psychological Check-In''' was an interview session carried out with a [[droid]] on the receiving end as part of mandatory maintenance to assess the psychological state of the unit. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] was known to have submitted former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] and reprogrammed [[KX-series security droid]] [[K-2SO]] to this check-in, asking the droid questions to ascertain his loyalties, past experience, violence propensity, and secret keeping abilities.<ref name="Zine" />
