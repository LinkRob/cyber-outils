The '''boiler room''' was a section of the [[Dai Bendu Monastery|Ashiga Clan Stronghold]] which was connected to the [[Kijimiko Caverns]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==History==
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[Kay Vess]] made her way through the Kijimiko Caverns as part of a coup attempt by [[Crimson Dawn]] and [[Krisk Ashiga]] in order to replace [[Queen]] [[Ashiga]], ruler of the [[Ashiga Clan]]. Vess's task was to sneak in and deactivate the monastery's outer defenses, which the Clan was using as their stronghold. After making her way through the caverns, Vess used an [[explosive charge]] to blow a hole in the cavern's wall giving her access to monastery's boiler room. From there she proceeded through the boiler room, [[Slicing unit|sliced]] into it, and took down the defenses.<ref name="Outlaws" />

In 35 ABY, the cryochamber was destroyed along with the planet during the [[Destruction of Kijimi]] by the [[Sith Eternal]] with the [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Derriphan]]'' of the [[Final Order]] [[fleet]].<ref name="Destruction" />

==Behind the scenes==
The boiler room first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It is only accessible during the "Hive" main quest.<ref name="Outlaws" />
