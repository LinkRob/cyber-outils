The '''combat speeder''' was a model of [[speeder]] used by [[General]] [[Grievous]], the [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander of the Droid Army]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], during the [[Clone Wars]]. [[Endente]]'s [[Sugi]] [[arms dealer]]s also used the speeders. During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Armor Corps]] also made used of combat speeders.

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|Try the booster on the left.''"<br />"''That's not a booster.''"<br />"''No, I think it's a booster.|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Crisis" />}}
The combat [[speeder]] featured a turbine [[engine]], a pair of [[laser cannon]]s, and recessed [[Missile launcher|rocket launchers]] in the forward prow. The craft was steered by a simple column connected to the directional vanes. [[Endente's arms dealing group]] had access to the model.<ref name="Crisis" />

==History==
[[File:Combat_speeders.png|left|thumb|300px|A variant of the combat speeders used by Endente's group]]
[[General]] [[Grievous]] used [[Grievous's Combat speeder|his speeder]] in his [[Invasion of Florrum|campaign]] to conquer the [[planet]] [[Florrum]],<ref name="SWE entry" /> where he used it to chase [[Hondo Ohnaka]], his remaining [[Ohnaka Gang|pirates]], and a group of [[Jedi]] [[Youngling]]s in order to prevent them from retreating Florrum.<ref name="ANB" /> [[Endente]]'s [[Sugi]] [[arms dealer]]s also used the speeders on [[Utapau]]. They were colored with [[Color|red]] markings and at least two were modified with a [[Cannon|heavy cannon]] at the front. They were used to chase after [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who had stolen their giant [[kyber crystal]].<ref name="Crisis" />

During the [[Imperial Era|rule]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Imperial Army]]'s [[Imperial Armor Corps]] made use of combat speeders in conjunction with other types of vehicles that supported [[infantry]] operations.<ref name="Dawn" />
