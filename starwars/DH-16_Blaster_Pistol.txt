The '''DH-16 Blaster Pistol'''<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> was a type of [[blaster pistol]]. One such model was used by the [[Partisan]] leader [[Saw Gerrera]] during the [[Liberation on Kashyyyk]].<ref name="JFO" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The DH-16 Blaster Pistol, albeit unidentified, first appeared in the [[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'',<ref name="JFO" /> which was developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]] and published by [[Electronic Arts]] on [[November 15]], [[2019]].<ref name="YBY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> The blaster pistol was later identified in the [[2015]] mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'' as Gerrera's relic,<ref name="GoH" /> which was added during the [[May 2]], [[2023]] patch.<ref name="Kit Reveal">{{EA|url=games/starwars/galaxy-of-heroes/news/kit-reveal-saw-gerrera|text=Kit Reveal - Saw Gerrera}}</ref>

On [[Wikipedia:Instagram|Instagram]], concept artist [[Matthew Savage]] revealed that the blaster was originally intended to be used by Gerrera<ref name="Concept">{{Instagram|username=mattsav.concept|url=p/B5F3ATODdj_|name=[[Matthew Savage]]|date=2019-11-20|quote=Original drawn up for Rogue One, this blaster was designed for Saw Gerrera. Very pleased and surprised to see it pop up in @respawn ‘s Jedi: Fallen Order. I designed it over an airsoft pistol so the entire barrel section would recoil when fired.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/6toBH|archivefile=File:MatthewSavage-DH16Concept-IG.png}}</ref> in the [[2016]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'';<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> before getting removed from the final cut of the film. Additionally, Savage also revealed that he designed the blaster on top of a [[Wikipedia:FN Five-seven|FN Five-seven]] [[Wikipedia:Airsoft gun|airsoft pistol]].<ref name="Concept" />
