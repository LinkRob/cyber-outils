'''Coolth''' was a substance used on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="CL" /> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Dates">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events on [[Tatooine]] of {{Film|I}} and {{Film|IV}}, as both depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[32 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]] respectively.</ref> the [[junk dealer]] [[Watto]] had private underground tanks of coolth and [[shisha]] under the [[city]] of [[Mos Espa]] that he conveyed through pipes into his [[Watto's junkshop|junkshop]], where the coolth was used in a coolth grid in the building's wall. He also had a surplus coolth conduit lent against the back of the store when the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his allies visited in search of a [[T-14 hyperdrive generator]].<ref name="CL" />

A coolth inflow pipe that passed through the wall of the [[Skywalker home]] in Mos Espa's [[Slave Quarters Row]] led to the hovel's second level. Coolth cells in the domes of buildings in [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]], including the [[arena citadel]], ventilated the structures.<ref name="CL" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Dates" /> the [[Lars family]] had a [[coolth unit]] installed in the wall of their [[Lars homestead|homestead]] on Tatooine.<ref name="CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], coolth was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']].<ref name="CL" /> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[1999]] reference book [[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary|''Star Wars: Episode I The Visual Dictionary'']], which was written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]].<ref name="TPM VD">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
