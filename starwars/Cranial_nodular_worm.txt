{{Quote|''For the removal and control of:'' bladderworm, crop microserps, multilegged throat croakers, intestinal fangworm colonies, lesser and greater nasal nesting spit-crawlers, cranial nodular worms.|Advertisement leaflet for Happy-Bore Medicated Dewormer included in Rey's survival guide|<ref name="RSG" />}}
'''Cranial nodular worms''' were a type of [[Parasite|parasitic]] [[worm]] that could infest the craniums of the domesticated creatures known as [[happabore]]s, which could be found on [[planet]]s across the [[Western Reaches]]. If a happabore was infested with the parasite, it could be treated with [[Galacti-Stok]] product [[Happy-Bore Medicated Dewormer]], which contained [[Trestibinexocycline 5-Gamma]], [[thlank oil]], [[greel-root acid]], and forage products. Instructions for the slurry formulation of the product suggested use of the dewormer on cranial nodular worms and, while living on the [[desert]] planet [[Jakku]], the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] found a copy of these instructions.<ref name="RSG" /> She<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> then included them alongside information about happabores on Jakku in a [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]] that she compiled<ref name="RSG" /> by [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'' includes an addendum at the end describing some of the events of {{Film|VII}}, written several [[Standard day|days]] after they take place. The addendum is the final entry in the guide and is included chronologically after the entry mentioning cranial nodular worms. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the film to [[34 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Cranial nodular worms were mentioned in ''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'', a [[2015]] replica journal written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="RSG" />
