'''Dornean Braha'ket Fleetworks Conglomerate''',<ref name="Rebel Files" /> or simply '''Braha'ket Fleetworks Conglomerate''',<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> was a [[starship]] [[Factory|manufacturer]] that notably produced the [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunship]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> a [[gunship]] class designed by the [[Dornean]]<ref name="Starships and Speeders">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> [[species]] and used to protect their [[homeworld]] of<ref name="Starfighters">[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles (second edition)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles'', second edition]]</ref> [[Dornea]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> against the [[Galactic Empire]]. The gunships were designed as dedicated anti-[[starfighter]] platforms, armed with eight double [[turbolaser]]s and eight [[concussion missile launcher]]s. A number of Braha'toks also came to serve the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during its [[Galactic Civil War|war]] against the Empire,<ref name="Starfighters" /> with another serving under a [[Unidentified pirate captain|pirate captain]] who used the gunship to raid [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Hyperspace route|shipping lanes]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="4 ABY">In ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', the report mentioning the Dornean Braha'ket Fleetworks Conglomerate was made inbetween the [[Rescue of Han Solo]] and the [[Battle of Endor]], which are both dated to [[4 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''.</ref> rebel [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] made a report to the Alliance's [[Commander-in-Chief]] [[Mon Mothma]] that detailed the main composition of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance fleet]]. The ''Braha'tok''-class gunship was listed, with the Braha'ket Fleetworks Conglomerate being acknowledged as the vessel's manufacturer. The report was soon compiled by [[archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]] in his archive of non-electric documents ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dornean Braha'ket Fleetworks Conglomerate was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2017]] boxset and reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Its shortened name, Braha'ket Fleetworks Conglomerate, was given by the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''.<ref name="USWNE" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, its sole mention was in the [[Databank (original)|Databank]] entry for the [[Braha'tok-class gunship/Legends|''Braha'tok''-class gunship]] on [[StarWars.com]], which was implemented as user [[Ello137]]'s<ref name="Databank">{{DB|starship|dorneangunship|Dornean gunship}}</ref> winning entry for ''[[What's The Story?]]'', a contest on the website's [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] feature, on [[August 31]], [[2006]].<ref name="Round 6">{{SWArchive|url=databank/updates/news20060831.html|text=What's the Story, Round 6}}</ref>
