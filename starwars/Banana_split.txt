A '''banana split''' was a type of [[ice cream]] made with [[Long fruit|bananas]]<ref name="BananaSplitParty" /> that was [[Foodstuff|edible]] by [[human]]s.<ref name="Human" /> The ice cream was popular among [[First Order stormtrooper]]s stationed aboard the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]''. Shortly before the [[Battle of Oetchi]]<ref name="BananaSplitParty" /> of [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> a [[First Order]] [[Unidentified First Order stormtrooper (Supremacy)|stormtrooper]] prepared and served the dessert during a banana split party held for a large gathering of [[Trooper|troops]]. The dessert featured a fifty-[[foot]]-long banana and a [[Color|multi-colored]] arrangement of ice cream topped with various foods, drawing praise from the stormtroopers, with one suggesting they have it every [[Standard day|Sunday]].<ref name="BananaSplitParty" />

When the celebration was abruptly interrupted by [[Resistance]] forces launching a surprise assault on the ''Supremacy'', an [[First Order All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]] [[walker]] [[pilot]]ed by<ref name="BananaSplitParty" /> the [[BB-series astromech droid|BB unit]]<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> [[BB-8]] slipped on the oversized banana peel left from the party, toppling onto the banana split and causing disorder among the First Order ranks. The incident inadvertently aided the Resistance in turning the tide of the battle.<ref name="BananaSplitParty" />

==Behind the scenes==
A banana split appeared in the [[2019]] [[Canon|non-canon]] [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] animated short film "Banana Split Party," which was featured on the official LEGO [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel as part of a fan storytelling initiative.<ref name="BananaSplitParty" /> In the real world, a [[Wikipedia:Banana split|banana split]] is a classic American dessert typically made with a split banana and scoops of [[Wikipedia:Vanilla ice cream|vanilla]], [[Wikipedia:Chocolate ice cream|chocolate]], and [[Wikipedia:Strawberry ice cream|strawberry]] ice cream, topped with whipped cream, nuts, and cherries.<ref name="PABook">{{WebCite|url=https://pabook.libraries.psu.edu/literary-cultural-heritage-map-pa/feature-articles/banana-split-rich-history|text=The Banana Split: A Rich History|author=Corris, Tracy|date=2010-04-27|work=Pennsylvania Center for the Book|archivedate=20250607220850}}</ref>
