'''Clan Bralor''' was a [[Mandalorian]] [[clan]] that existed during the [[New Republic Era]]. One member of the clan, [[Kort Bralor]], was a member of the [[Children of the Watch]], a [[religion]] of Mandalorian followers of the [[Way of the Mandalore]]. Orange was a [[color]] associated with the clan.<ref name="MandoVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clan Bralor made its first appearance in the [[Canon]] continuity in "[[Chapter 1: The Mandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian]]," the first episode of the [[The Mandalorian Season One|first season]] of the [[Disney+]] streaming series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'',<ref name="Chapter 1" /> which was released on [[November 12|November 22]], [[2019]].<ref name="DTCI">{{WebCite|url=https://lumiere-a.akamaihd.net/v1/documents/the_mandalorian_project_one-sheet_10_ec1e005f.pdf|text=The Mandalorian Media Kit|date=2019-09-24|format=[[Wikipedia:PDF|PDF]]|work=[[Wikipedia:Walt Disney Direct-to-Consumer & International|Walt Disney Direct-to-Consumer & International]]|pages=[https://lumiere-a.akamaihd.net/v1/documents/the_mandalorian_project_one-sheet_10_ec1e005f.pdf#page=1 p. 1]|archivedate=20210623202717}}</ref> It was first identified as Clan Bralor in the [[2026]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and published by [[Dorling Kindersley]].<ref name="MandoVG" />

[[Clan Bralor]] first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2004]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]''.<ref name="KotORII">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]''</ref>
