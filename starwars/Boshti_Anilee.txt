The couple then joined the competitive circuit and won trophies in three [[sector]]s. They began following the ''[[First Light]]''&mdash;the [[Nau'ur-class yacht|yacht]] of [[Crimson Dawn]] leader [[Dryden Vos]]&mdash;from [[Spaceport|port]] to port, and Anilee and Gwin were aboard the [[Starship|vessel]] in [[10 BBY]] while it was on the planet [[Vandor]]. ==Biography==
Boshti Anilee was a [[Twi'lek]] dancer who trained for a [[Standard year|year]] at the [[Dette Lawnic Dance Academy]] on the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] with their romantic and professional partner, [[Hado Gwin]]. After completing their training, the couple joined the competitive circuit and became trophy winners in three [[sector]]s. They began following the ''[[First Light]]''&mdash;a [[Nau'ur-class yacht|yacht]] belonging to [[Dryden Vos]], the leader of the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime syndicate&mdash;from [[Spaceport|port]] to port<ref name="SoloOG" /> and in [[10 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events on the ''[[First Light]]'' in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[10 BBY]].</ref> attended a party on the [[Starship|vessel]] while it was docked at [[Fort Ypso]] on the planet [[Vandor]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

[[File:Applause_for_Ventafoli.png|thumb|left|250px|Anilee and Hado Gwin applaud singer Aurodia Ventafoli on the ''First Light''.]]
At the party Anilee and Gwin spent their [[time]] among the other guests on the [[yacht]]'s observation deck, where they danced to [[music]] performed by the [[Musician|singers]] [[Aurodia Ventafoli]] and [[Luleo Primoc]]. After the criminal [[Tobias Beckett]] and his [[Beckett's gang|gang]] entered the deck, the performance came to an end, and Anilee and Gwin applauded the singers along with the rest of the guests. The couple then spoke with a masked [[human]], before moving further back toward the rear of the deck. The yacht later left Vandor, and Vos was [[Showdown on Savareen|killed]] not long after.<ref name="Solo" />

==Personality and traits==
Boshti Anilee had light skin and [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s.<ref name="SoloOG" />

==Equipment==
While aboard the ''First Light'', Anilee wore a black [[Clothing|dress]] with a black headband and black high heels. They hung a silver [[necklace]] around their neck and had their [[Limb|fingernails]] painted black. Anilee and Gwin both had a series of black bands on each of their fingers.<ref name="SoloOG" />
