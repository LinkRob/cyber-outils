It was good enough already, but coulda used some heat to balance out the sweet&mdash;so I swapped their traditional fruit garnish for a candied hot pepper that sparkles like Etai's crystal mines.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
'''Candied hot peppers''' were a type of hot [[Color|green]] [[pepper]] candied via coating in a white powder. Inspired by how [[etaan crystal]]s could be substituted for<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> standard<ref name="EV">''[[Endless Vigil]]''</ref> [[kyber crystal]]s to power a [[lightsaber]], the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] swapped out the traditional [[fruit]] [[Foodstuff|garnish]] that the people of the [[planet]] [[Etai]] consumed with [[jybbuk slush]] topped with [[condensed nerf milk]] and so created [[Spiced Jybbuk Juice]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

The peppers, which Tuggs described as sparkling like the [[crystal]] [[Mining|mines]] on Etai, balanced out the sweetness of the beverage with heat and were used alongside other red and green garnishes. After a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs included the recipe for Spiced Jybbuk Juice in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Candied hot peppers were part of a recipe in the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for Spiced Jybbuk Juice uses [[Wikipedia:Jalapeño|jalapeño]] slices coated in [[Wikipedia:White sugar|granulated sugar]] to represent the candied hot peppers.<ref name="UltimateDate" />
