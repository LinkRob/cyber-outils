The '''Fedalle system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Core Worlds]] that fell within grid square M-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was part of the [[Hydian Way]],<ref name="CLNE" /> a [[super-hyperroute]]<ref name="BMF 29">{{BuildFalconCite|29|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> that connected it to the [[Trellen system]] and the [[Skako system]].<ref name="CLNE" /> The Fedalle system<ref name="Appendix" /> contained the [[planet]] [[Fedalle]] which was the site of the [[Fedallese]] ruins of [[Navosh-Hul]] in the [[Warplands]], which were ancient by the [[time]] the [[human]] [[Soran Keize]] visited them as a curated tourist site while he a was [[Youngling|child]].<ref name="ShadowFall" />

Around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The flashbacks of [[Star Wars (2020) 50|''Star Wars'' (2020) 50]] take place approximately twenty-two [[Standard year|years]] after [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|ascension]] to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]], meaning they must occur around [[3 ABY]]. The flashbacks also take place after the events of [[Star Wars (2020) 43|''Star Wars'' (2020) 43]], which occur after the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Droids]]'' crossover event, an event {{SW|url=news/marvel-dark-droids-interview|text=Charles Soule Unleashes ''Dark Droids''}} places after ''[[Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters]]'', which is dated to 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] by ''Timelines''. As [[Darth Vader (2020) 30|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 30]] takes place between ''War of the Bounty Hunters'' and ''Dark Droids'', its events must take place around 3 ABY.</ref> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and the [[Sith assassin]] [[Ochi]] brought the former [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] [[handmaiden]] [[Dormé]] in a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] as a trap for her comrades [[Eirtaé]], [[Saché]], and [[Rabé Tonsort]]. The trio arrived shortly after the [[shuttle]] in a [[Star Commuter 2000]] and [[Battle on Fedalle|battled]] Vader, but ultimately agreed to work with him to [[Battle against Jul's droid army|save]] their shared ally [[Sabé]] and left Fedalle.<ref name="Vader30">[[Darth Vader (2020) 30|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 30]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Fedalle system was mentioned as just "Fedalle" on a map of [[the galaxy]] included with the [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' set released by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> It first appeared in the [[Darth Vader (2020) 30|thirtieth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']], which was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Luke Ross]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Vader30" /> on [[January 11]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103061/star_wars_darth_vader_2020_30|text=Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020) #30}}</ref>

It was then identified as the Fedalle system in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were also written by Fry.<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Fedalle system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', which was written by Fry and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
