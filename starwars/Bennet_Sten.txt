{{Quote|Yuko, your parents were wonderful people. They brought food and medical aid to those in need across the Outer Rim.|BILY, to Bennet's son Yuko|<ref name="Yuko" />}}
'''Bennet Sten''' was a [[human]] [[smuggler]] and [[thief]] who brought medical supplies and [[foodstuff]] to people in need across the [[Outer Rim Territories]] with his partner [[Myler Sten]]. He eventually had a [[Gender|son]], [[Yuko]], and created multiple [[Holorecorder|holorecordings]] of himself with Myler and Yuko in their hidden [[Sten family home|home]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The [[Sten family|family]] also had two [[droid]]s, a [[bear]]-like [[caretaker droid]] [[BILY]] and a small [[triangle droid]]. At some point, the family stole something valuable to the [[pirate]] [[captain]] [[Fox-Ear]]. In a skirmish between the Stens and some [[starfighter]]s, a [[starship]] crashed into [[Sten family starship|their own]]. After Bennet and Myler's eventual [[death]]s from [[Disease|sickness]], BILY watched after and took care of Yuko.

[[Fox-Ear's pirate gang]] eventually tracked down the Stens' child and attempted to retrieve their stolen treasure. The pirates were eventually defeated by a [[Unidentified leviathan krayt dragon|krayt dragon]] while trying to infiltrate the family's bunker. BILY took Yuko into the old home, where the droid dusted the [[parent]]s' graves and Yuko witnessed the recordings created by Bennet and Myler.

==Biography==
===Smuggling and death===
{{Quote|You don't move, okay?''"<br />"''Look how much you've grown!''"<br />"''Bet you're gonna end up bigger than daddy.|Bennet and Myler Sten measuring Yuko's height|<ref name="Yuko" />}}
[[File:StenFamilyBonding-YukosTreasure.png|thumb|right|300px|Bennet and Myler feeding Yuko on a hologram]]
At some point, Bennet Sten became a [[smuggler]] with the<ref name="Yuko" /> [[Gender|woman]]<ref name="YukoAD" /> [[Myler Sten]]. Together, the pair brought [[foodstuff]] and medical equipment to those in need across the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Bennet and Myler eventually became the [[parent]]s of [[Yuko]] and came into possession of a [[Yuko's triangle droid|triangle droid]] and [[BILY]], a [[bear]]-like [[caretaker droid]]. The [[Sten family|family]] lived in a hidden bunker [[Sten family home|home]] on<ref name="Yuko" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Tatooine]] within<ref name="Yuko" /> the [[Jundland Wastes]].<ref name="YukoAD" /> The bunker had a handprint lock openable by Yuko.<ref name="Yuko" />

The Stens [[Holorecorder|recorded]] several moments with their child on [[hologram]]s, including a point where Myler held Yuko in<ref name="Yuko" /> her<ref name="YukoAD" /> [[Limb|arms]] while Yuko held on to Bennet's finger as the couple laughed. Later, they recorded a moment when Myler fed Yuko with a [[spoon]] and Bennet told him that he did a good job. Another recording featured the parents measuring Yuko's height on a wall, where Bennet placed a pair of [[goggles]] on<ref name="Yuko" /> his<ref name="YukoAD" /> son's head and said he may grow bigger than even himself. A final recording was taken in front of their [[Sten family starship|starship]].<ref name="Yuko" />

[[File:StenBattle-YukosTreasure.png|thumb|left|300px|Bennet and Sten were caught in starfighter combat.]]
The Stens eventually stole something valuable to the [[pirate]] [[captain]] [[Fox-Ear]] that<ref name="Yuko" /> he<ref name="YukoAD" /> regarded as treasure. At some point, Bennet and Myler became surrounded in a [[Starfighter combat|skirmish]] by multiple [[starfighter]]s. As the [[Starship|ship]] shook under [[laser]]fire, BILY took Yuko in his arms and their [[triangle droid]] zipped across the floor, knocking itself upside down. A starship was struck by the fire and crashed into their ship. Bennet and Myler later grew [[Disease|sick]] and [[death|died]], leaving Yuko, BILY, and the triangle [[droid]] alone. On Tatooine, two grave markers were placed in a room of their home for the couple, and BILY took care of Yuko in their absence.<ref name="Yuko" />

===Fox-Ear's revenge===
{{Quote|I have no business with you. Where might your parents be? They took something valuable of mine, and I'm here to get it back. With interest, of course.|Fox-Ear, to a frightened Yuko|<ref name="Yuko" />}}
[[File:FoxEarGangAttacks-YukosTreasure.png|thumb|right|300px|Fox-Ear's pirate gang comes for what was stolen from them.]]
At some point no earlier than [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{VisionsDate|305}}</ref> Fox-Ear [[Fox-Ear's search for the Stens|tracked]] the Stens to Tatooine, where he provided the local child [[Sola (Tatooine)|Sola]] with a hologram of Bennet and Myler with their triangle droid. Sola used the [[holopuck]] to question multiple individuals of the [[city]] [[Mos Eisley]]. The child eventually came across BILY and questioned him about the couple, lying about them being his parents. BILY lied in return, claiming he had no idea who they were. Sola followed the droid back to his home with Yuko and spotted the triangle droid from the hologram, and he brought the information back to [[Fox-Ear's pirate gang]]. The [[Twi'lek]] pirate [[Gura]] informed Sola that they planned to take everything owned by the Stens as payback for what they stole. Learning this, Sola left to warn Yuko.<ref name="Yuko" />

After Sola arrived at [[BILY and Yuko's home]], [[Fox-Ear's starship]] blasted a hole in the front of the building. Stepping inside, Fox-Ear stated he had no business with Yuko or BILY, and instead asked where Bennet and Myler were, admitting he was there to retrieve what was stolen from him with interest. BILY held Yuko and told Fox-Ear that the Stens had gotten sick and died. Not believing the droid, Fox-Ear grabbed Yuko and threatened him if BILY did not reveal the location of the treasure. After Sola saved Yuko from the pirate's clutches, BILY was taken by Fox-Ear to lead the pirates to the treasure. Losing the only family he had left, Yuko traveled with Sola and the triangle droid to get him back.<ref name="Yuko" />

[[File:SadYuko-YukosTreasure.png|thumb|left|300px|Yuko watched the holorecordings made for him by his parents.]]
The pirate gang eventually arrived at the entrance to the Stens' bunker; however, BILY refused to open the door using the handprint. Fox-Ear then ordered his subordinates to retrieve Yuko to open the door for him as he reprogrammed BILY. Yuko and Sola eventually made their way to the pirates and defeated a reprogrammed BILY, returning him to his normal state. A [[Unidentified leviathan krayt dragon|leviathan krayt dragon]] found the large group and chased the pirates off a ledge into a deep chasm. The children, BILY, and the triangle droid then ventured into the bunker of the Sten family, where Yuko watched the recordings his parents made for him. BILY dusted Bennet's grave and told Yuko of Bennet and Myler's good deeds across the Outer Rim. Although the original plan by BILY was to give the family starship to Yuko when he was grown up, the caretaker droid provided it to the children and offered to teach them how to fly it.<ref name="Yuko" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|It spilled. Yuko. Ah&hellip;''"<br />"''Ah&hellip; ''[chuckles]'' Yeah. Good job, Yuko.|Myler and Bennet feeding Yuko|<ref name="Yuko" />}}
Sten was a male human with light skin and spiky [[hair]]. He was considered a wonderful person by his droid BILY. Alongside Myler, Bennet provided help to those in need across the Outer Rim. His son made him smile, and he believed Yuko would eventually grow up to be bigger than himself. He created hologram recordings with Myler for Yuko to see.<ref name="Yuko" />

==Skills and abilities==
Sten was a smuggler capable of [[pilot]]ing a starship and could engage other ships in combat.<ref name="Yuko" />

==Equipment==
Bennet Sten wore a collared [[jacket]] over a shirt with a [[bandolier]] across his chest. He also wore [[boot]]s and a [[belt]] with a silver-[[color]]ed buckle around the waist of his pants. On his head, he wore a pair of goggles, which he later gave to Yuko. He used a pen to mark down his son's height.<ref name="Yuko" />
