'''''Empire of Dreams: The Story of the Star Wars Trilogy''''', or simply '''''Empire of Dreams''''', is a [[Wikipedia:Documentary film|documentary]] by [[Wikipedia:Kevin Burns|Kevin Burns]] about the making of the [[Original trilogy|three original ''Star Wars'' films]]: [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''A New Hope'']], [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''The Empire Strikes Back'']], and [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Return of the Jedi'']]. It is strictly chronological, divided into three parts, one for each film. It is narrated by [[Wikipedia:Robert Clotworthy|Robert Clotworthy]], and features interviews with [[George Lucas]] and major cast and crew. It tries to put ''Star Wars'' into a sociological and political perspective by using interviews with spectators.

==Release and reception==
[[File:EmpireofDreams.png|thumb|right|250px|The original title screen from the DVD release]]
A 90-minute version of ''Empire of Dreams'' was made for [[Wikipedia:A&E Network|A&E]], and a two-and-a-half-hour version was the main feature on the bonus disc of [[Star Wars Trilogy (DVD)|the 2004 DVD release of the original ''Star Wars'' trilogy]].<ref name="Embarrassment of Riches" /> In November 2019, it became available on the [[Disney+]] streaming service.<ref name="Disney+" />
