'''Crane''' was a [[Clone Commander]] who, in a [[Force vision]] experienced by [[Dooku]], served under [[Jedi Master]] [[Jor Aerith]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. During his vision, Dooku witnessed a battle in which Crane executed [[Order 66]] and shot Master Aerith.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Crane first appeared in the audiobook ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in [[2019]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> While the script implies the execution of Order 66 occurs during a different battle than the initial vision of the Clone Wars Dooku experiences,<ref name="Jedi LostScript">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> the audio drama itself treats them as the same singular vision.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
