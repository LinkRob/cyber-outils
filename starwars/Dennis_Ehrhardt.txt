'''Dennis Ehrhardt''' is a [[Wikipedia:German|German]] [[Wikipedia:Author|author]] and [[Wikipedia:Film Director|director]] of audiobooks. Ehrhardt wrote the script for the [[German Clone Wars audio dramas]] which adapted a number of episodes of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' animated television series.<ref name="German Clone Wars" />
