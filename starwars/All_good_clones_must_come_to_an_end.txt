"'''All good clones must come to an end'''" is the eighteenth comic in a series on the official [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] website. It finishes the story arc started in "[[A droid in the hand is worth two in the asteroid belt]]." It was placed on the website on [[November 25]], [[2009]].

==Plot summary==
A unit of clones are seeking [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] when they encounter [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droids]] led by [[Nute Gunray/Legends|Nute Gunray]]. Fighting ensues, excluding [[1139]] who is still tied up from [[Clone troopers are excellent diplomats|events earlier]]. The mobility compromised 1139 is then freed by his [[Tauntaun/Legends|tauntaun]], and together they eliminate the droids, driving Gunray into flight aboard his [[Sheathipede-class transport shuttle/Legends|shuttle]]. The clones having used this shuttle to arrive on [[Endor/Legends|Endor]] are thus stranded.

Fortunately for the clones, they quickly discover [[Jetpack/Legends|jetpacks]] and utilize them to traverse the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] until they find three [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/Legends|Y-wings]]. They abscond with these in an act that leaves the owners of the starfighters&mdash;Plo Koon, Yoda and Mace Windu&mdash;stranded instead. However, as they launch away from Endor, the group is caught in a [[Tractor beam/Legends|tractor beam]] and brought into a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer/Legends|Star Destroyer]]. Here they are reprimanded for their failure by none other than [[Supreme Chancellor/Legends|Supreme Chancellor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] himself. Displeased with them, the Supreme Chancellor dismisses them from the military, at which point the clones take opportunity to excuse themselves for a beach vacation.
