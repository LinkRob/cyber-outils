A '''poster''' was put up on [[Coruscant]] to advertise a series of [[cabaret]]s on [[Coruscant]]. It showed a [[Twi'lek]]<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore" /> [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/lethal-trackdown-trivia-gallery|text="Lethal Trackdown" Trivia Gallery|quote=The nightclub on Coruscant is filled with posters of various female pin-up models}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref> posing in a revealing outfit. The same motif was featured on the facade of the cabarets. While the [[Painting|poster]] put up in the [[Imperial City|city]] streets also featured the same [[color]] palette as the one on the facade,<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore" /> a differently colored version was put up in [[Moshi Bar]], a seedy [[cantina]] in the [[Coruscant Underworld]].<ref name="Lethal">{{TCW|Lethal Trackdown}}</ref>
