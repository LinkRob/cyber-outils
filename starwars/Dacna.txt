'''Dacna''' was a member of a [[Unidentified green-skinned species|green-skinned species]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 1" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 1|''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 1]] are set between the events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone]]'' and [[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 2|''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 2]] which includes the [[Battle of Eriadu]]. ''Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone'' and the Battle of Eriadu are both dated to [[228 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:THRContent228BBY|here]].</ref> Dacna and [[Morka]] were under attack by the [[Nihil]] near the [[Occlusion Zone]] while [[pilot|piloting]] the [[freighter]] [[Marshskimmer|''Marshskimmer'']]. After repeating a [[distress signal]], Dacna and Morka were rescued by [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Master|Masters]] [[Keeve Trennis]] and [[Sskeer]], who destroyed the Nihil [[Cloudship]]s in their [[Jedi Vector]]s.<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 1" />
