The '''Eiram–E'ronoh crisis''' was an incident that took place during the [[High Republic Era]] after members of the [[Directorate]] kidnapped Monarch [[Cassel (monarch)|Cassel]] of [[E'ronoh]] and [[Queen]] Consort [[Thandeka]] of [[Eiram]]. The two [[planet]]s, normally at odds, asked the [[Jedi Order]] for assistance in desperation, and [[Jedi Master]]s [[Laret Soveral]] and [[Simmix]] were dispatched with their [[Padawan]]s, [[Orla Jareni]] and [[Cohmac Vitus]]. [[Lord]] [[Isamer]], the leader of the Directorate, had been hired for the job by the [[Hutt]]s, and he believed that they wanted to work with his syndicate to drive the people of the region into distrusting the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi]]. In truth, the Hutts intended the mission to destroy the Directorate, which would allow them to move their criminal operations into the region whenever they wanted. Nonetheless, the crisis did push Eiram and E'ronoh to ally together and work with the Republic.<ref name="Into the Dark" />
