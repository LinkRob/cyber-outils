A '''cross-converter''' was a component used in [[speeder]]s that an [[actuator]] could be connected.<ref name="Outlaws" /> During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[Teeka's tribe|group]] of [[Jawa]]s sold a refurbished actuator to a [[technician]] that worked in the [[Bestine junkyard]] but when they hooked it up to a standard cross-converter it gave out.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cross-converters were first mentioned in "The Hotfixer" expert quest in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
