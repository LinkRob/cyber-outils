The '''DE-10 blaster pistol''' was a stylish and reliable [[heavy blaster pistol]] that was manufactured by [[Antrech Arms]], a subsidiary of [[BlasTech Industries]]. It was vaunted for its reliability and ease of maintenance, and heavily resembled a [[slugthrower]] revolver.<ref name="Collapse" />

Constructed from high-quality materials, the DE-10 could stand up to immense punishment on the field, and unlike most [[blaster]] weapons, it fired a distinctive bright-[[Color|blue]] bolt, a feature that was designed simply to set it apart from its competitors. The blaster also cost 1,400 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Collapse" />
