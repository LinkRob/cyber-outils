'''Bespin: Action Tidings''' was an online supplement for [[Wizards of the Coast]]'s ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'', published on [[Wizards.com]] in [[September]] and [[October]] [[2004]] as part of the site's ''[[Planet Hoppers]]'' series. It was written by [[Cory J. Herndon]]. The supplement was split into five separate articles chronicling the history of [[Bespin/Legends|Bespin's]] [[Ugnaught/Legends|Ugnaught]] holonews program ''[[Action Tidings]]'', and its host, [[Ars Fivvle]].

==Contents==
===Part 1: Live From the Surface===
''&hellip;we meet [[Ugnaught/Legends|Ugnaught]] newsman [[Ars Fivvle]], host of [[Cloud City/Legends|Cloud City's]] ''[[Action Tidings]]''.''<ref name="Bespin" />

===Part 2: That Den o' Human Iniquity===
''&hellip;Ars Fivvle describes the heroic tale of Cloud City's founding, complete with ancient holofootage.''<ref name="Bespin" />

===Part 3: The High Human o' Cloud City===
''&hellip;[[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Lando Calrissian]] comes to [[Bespin/Legends|Bespin]] and likes it so much he takes over the place.''<ref name="Bespin" />

===Part 4: The Empire's Ugly Nose===
''&hellip;[[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] also comes to Bespin and takes over the place just to impress his children.''<ref name="Bespin" />

===Part 5: An Exclusive!===
''&hellip;Calrissian returns to Bespin and helps daring Ugnaught [[Holojournalist/Legends|holojournalist]] Ars Fivvle free Cloud City's Ugnaughts from the Imperials.''<ref name="Bespin" />
