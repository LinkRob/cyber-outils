The '''coosta mouse''' was a type of [[mouse]] that roamed the [[Temple garden]] at the [[Tenoo Jedi Temple|Jedi Temple]] on the [[forest]] [[planet]] [[Tenoo]].<ref name="Creature Safari" /> Around [[233 BBY]],<ref name="YJA Date">{{YJADate|105B}}</ref> while on a trip to the planet [[Yamradi]], the [[human]] [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Kai Brightstar]] admired a group of small creatures known as [[chylaroo]]s. He then compared them to the coosta mice because of their similar appearance.<ref name="Creature Safari" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coosta mice were mentioned in "[[Creature Safari]]," the second part of the [[The Jellyfruit Pursuit / Creature Safari|fifth episode]] of the [[Disney+]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''{{'s}} [[Young Jedi Adventures Season One|first season]],<ref name="Creature Safari" /> which released on [[May 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="D23 May">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-may-2023|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in May 2023}}</ref>
