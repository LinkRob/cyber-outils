'''Cerlin''' was a type of fabric used in the [[Clothing|wardrobes]] of [[Royal House of Naboo|Naboo's monarchy]]. As [[Monarch of Naboo|queen]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] wore a [[Dark purple traveling dress|traveling gown]] with [[Color|purple]] cerlin sleeves that had a soft sheen to them.<ref name="TCVD" /> [[Apailana]], another queen of the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]], wore an intricately patterned capelet made of metallic silver cerlin when she attended the [[Padmé Amidala's funeral|funeral]] of [[Senator]] Padmé Amidala.<ref name="ECUC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The material called "cerlin" in-universe has different names in the real world. [[Trisha Biggar]], the costume designer for the [[prequel trilogy]], explained in ''[[Dressing a Galaxy: The Costumes of Star Wars]]'' that the sleeves for the dark purple traveling dress were made of a double layer of shot-silk chiffon satin.<ref name="Dressing a Galaxy">''[[Dressing a Galaxy: The Costumes of Star Wars]]''</ref> In the traveling exhibit ''[[Rebel, Jedi, Princess, Queen: Star Wars and the Power of Costume]]'', which ran from [[2015]] to [[2018]],<ref name="Itinerary">{{WebCite|url=http://www.powerofcostume.si.edu/intinerary.html|text=''Itinerary''|author=[https://www.sites.si.edu Smithsonian Institution Traveling Exhibition Service]|partialdate=[[2017]]|archivedate=20201111181401}}</ref> the placard for Apailana's costume indicated that her cloak was made of silk with a pattern burned out using a devoré technique.<ref name="PowerOfCostume">{{WebCite|url=https://blog.hobbydb.com/2017/04/14/star-wars-and-the-power-of-costume|text=Star Wars and the Power of the Costume Art Exhibit|date=2017-04-14|archivedate=20210807160952}}</ref>
