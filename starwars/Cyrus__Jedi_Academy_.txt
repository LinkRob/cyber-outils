'''Cyrus''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Dathomirian/Legends|Dathomirian]] [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] who attended the [[Jedi academy]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] during [[Roan Novachez]]'s time of attendance there. A skilled student, he was nevertheless a bully and troublemaker. He was rarely seen outside the company of fellow Padawan [[Cronah]], and together the two took pleasure in bullying their fellow students at Jedi academy, though they particularly took to bullying Roan. The two were sometimes joined in their bullying by a female student named [[Jo-Ahn]].<ref name="jacademy" />

In [[195 BBY]],<ref name="RoP Date note">The events of ''Jedi Academy: Return of the Padawan'' take place one [[Standard year/Legends|year]] after the events of [[Jedi Academy (novel)|''Jedi Academy'']], in which [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] is 700 years old. Since ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' establishes that Yoda was [[Reproduction/Legends|born]] in [[896 BBY/Legends|896 BBY]], it can be deduced that the events of ''Jedi Academy: Return of the Padawan'' take place around [[195 BBY]].</ref> during Roan's second year, he briefly took to hanging out with Cronah and Cyrus, though he eventually realized that they did not consider him a true friend. Cyrus then was under Roan's command as Blue Two during the final exam for [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighter]] pilot training. Along with Cronah, he ditched Roan during the exam, causing them both to get a C+ for the exam. The two both posted disparaging comments on [[Holobook (HoloNet)|Holobook]], leading [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] to shut down student access to it for a time. Along with Cronah, he tortured the class pet [[Voorpak/Legends|voorpak]], [[Voorpee]], resulting in the two being placed on academic probation for the next semester and being denied access to Holobook even after it was restored to the other students.<ref name="return">''[[Jedi Academy: Return of the Padawan]]''</ref>

In [[194 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Jedi Academy (novel)|''Jedi Academy'']] states that [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]], who was born in [[896 BBY/Legends|896 BBY]] according to ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'', is 700 [[Standard year/Legends|years]] old. Therefore, ''Jedi Academy'' takes place in [[196 BBY]], and ''Jedi Academy: The Phantom Bully'', which takes place two years later, takes place in [[194 BBY]].</ref> during Roan's third year, he seemed resolved to prove himself as a model Jedi and displayed none of his past bad behavior, no longer spending time with Cronah. He trained personally under [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] Yoda. During an incident at a talent show involving numerous [[Voorpee's clones|clones]] of Voorpee, he broke his arm and temporarily had to wear a cast due to the school's [[Bacta tank/Legends|bacta tank]] malfunctioning.<ref name="phantom" />
