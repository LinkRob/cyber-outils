'''Avrac Kefundee''' was a criminal who was serving a 25-year sentence in the [[Wobani Penitentiary]] for aggravated assault of a [[New Republic]] [[officer]]. Kefundee's profile in the New Republic prison registry was one of several that [[Marshal]] [[Carasynthia Dune]] looked at from her office on the [[planet]] [[Nevarro]]<ref name="Mando14" /> in [[9 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|14|the appearance of Avrac Kefundee's profile}}</ref> while searching for the profile of prisoner [[Migs Mayfeld]].<ref name="Mando14" />

==Behind the scenes==
Avrac Kefundee's profile first appeared in "[[Chapter 14: The Tragedy]],"<ref name="Mando14" /> the sixth episode of the [[The Mandalorian Season Two|second season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', which was released on [[December 4]], [[2020]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-december-2020|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in December 2020}}</ref>
