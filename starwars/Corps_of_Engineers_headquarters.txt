The '''Corps of Engineers headquarters''' was the headquarters of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Corps of Engineers|Corps of Engineering]] located on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] asked his friend the [[scientist]] [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]] to meet him in his office located in the headquarters to discuss a project that Galen had come up with for the two.<ref name="Catalyst" />
