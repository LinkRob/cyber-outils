The '''Delcontrian faction''' was a group<ref name="QP" /> that originated from the [[Delcontr system]]<ref name="Planetnamia" /> of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Oriolan sector]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The group served as representatives in the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="QP" /> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Queen's Peril]]'' to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the group voted with the [[Trade Federation]] on a proposed [[Unidentified taxation bill|taxation bill]] until [[Senator]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naboo]]&mdash;secretly the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Sidious, who discreetly controlled the Trade Federation&mdash;insisted on a bypass amendment, which lost the Trade Federation the Delcontrian faction's vote. Sidious, who had intended for the bill to fail from the beginning, was pleased with what he had learned about several groups in the Senate due to the bill's failure by studying their involvement. Sidious promised the Trade Federation's [[Viceroy of the Trade Federation|Viceroy]], [[Nute Gunray]], that the Delcontrian faction would propose a new initiative soon, though the latter remained skeptical of the group doing so.<ref name="QP" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Delcontrian faction was mentioned in ''[[Queen's Peril]]'', a [[2020]] young-adult novel by [[E. K. Johnston]] as the second installment of ''[[Star Wars: Queen's Series]]''.<ref name="QP" />
