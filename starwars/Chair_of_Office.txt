The '''Chair of Office''' was the name for the official seat of the [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. The chair was located in the [[Emperor's office|Chancellor's office]] in the [[Imperial Executive Building|Senate Office Building]] on [[Coruscant]]. It was made of an ultra-dense [[lanthanide]] alloy [[armor]], was equipped with a defensive shield, and also provided direct, secure communication with the Chancellor's [[aide]]s and a summoner for the elite [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Red Guard]].<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
