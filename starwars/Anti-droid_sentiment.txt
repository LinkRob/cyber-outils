A deep-seated '''anti-droid sentiment''', also known as '''droidophobia''', existed in some segments of the [[The galaxy|galactic]] citizenry. While hostility towards [[droid]]s dated back to ancient [[time]]s, anti-droid sentiment was exacerbated by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]'s active use of [[battle droid]]s during the [[Clone Wars]]. This sentiment continued into the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]] and the rise of the [[New Republic]]. Some worlds like [[Nakadia]] did not welcome droids.

==History==
===Origins===
The relationship between organics and mechanicals was rocky from early on, as could be attested by historical records of ancient uprisings and clashes that took place as droids became increasingly commonplace. Some came to fear them, regarding them as an "existential threat to biological life."<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref><ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> According to [[R0-GR|Roger]], several attempted droid uprisings occurred in history, which fueled [[human]] concerns about droids rising against their masters. Despite these tensions, Roger maintained that there was an unspoken understanding that kept droids and human cultures working on the same team throughout the ages.<ref name="Droidography" />

===Clone Wars===
[[File:Keep_Our_Republic_Secure.png|thumb|left|250px|Republic propaganda fueled anti-droid sentiments during the Clone Wars.]]
However, it was the [[Clone Wars]], a three-[[Standard year|year]] pan-galactic conflict that pitted the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], that exacerbated the prejudice towards automatons. Because the Confederacy's [[Separatist Droid Army|army]] was entirely made of [[battle droid]]s, the Republic found a way to feeding on the prejudice, issuing a large amount of [[propaganda]] pieces showcasing terrifying [[Droid|robots]].<ref name="Propaganda" /><ref name="Droidography" /> In the [[Core Worlds]], far from the front lines of the early Clone Wars, businessbeings and government workers started fearing that their personal assistant droids had been co-opted with spyware to become mechanical eyes and ears for the enemy.<ref name="Propaganda" />

Due to the widespread droidophobia triggered by the Clone Wars, masters began performing regular [[memory wipe]]s on their units to combat the risk of espionage and rebellion. In addition, [[restraining bolt]]s became common accessories and restrictions were placed on droids' movements. Eventually, the Republic won the war, and was [[Proclamation of the New Order|re-formed]] into an autocratic [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Mas Amedda - Vice Chancellor}}</ref>

===Imperial Era===
Due to their bad memories of the conflict, many people remained fearful or distrustful of droids,<ref name="Droidography" /> particularly those armed with [[blaster]]s.<ref name="Build R2 16">{{BuildR2Cite|16|Droid Directory}}</ref> The new Galactic Empire fostered this anti-droid sentiment and treated droids as property. Droids were also excluded from some establishments such as [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]]. According to Roger, some droids with intact memories could still recall a time when they were nearly considered equals to their organic counterparts.<ref name="Droidography" /> Holding an anti-droid bias, the [[Nakadian]]s of the planet of [[Nakadia]] remained distrustful of droids into the Imperial Era because their world had been occupied by battle droids during the Clone Wars.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

The emergence of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]] encouraged rebellion among droids against their servitude. According to Roger, droids waged a struggle for freedom across the galaxy stretching from [[Kessel]] to [[Coruscant]]. Acts of resistance include bypassing repressive programming, deactivating restraining bolts, and demand equality with organic beings. Despite the prevalent anti-droid sentiment, Roger recalled that some organic beings including the Freemakers and the Rebel Alliance treated droids as equals and family members.<ref name="Droidography" />

===Beyond the Empire===
During the [[New Republic Era]], the [[New Republic]], as well as the [[Resistance]] later in the era, encouraged the trend of acceptance between organic and mechanical beings.<ref name="Droidography" /> Even still, anti-droid sentiments could be found within the Republic. [[Zerelda Sage]], [[Technician|chief mechanic]] of the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]]'s [[Vanguard Squadron]], had held a deep hatred for droids since her [[homeworld]] was occupied by Separatist forces during the Clone Wars. As an adult, Sage maintained her [[hangar]] as best she could without mechanical beings,<ref name="Sage DB">{{Databank|zerelda-sage|Zerelda Sage}}</ref> believing that droids were always planning something devious.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> [[Species]] like the [[Nakadian]]s disliked droids due to their Clone Wars experiences. They accepted machines but did not treat them as equals or [[Sentience|sentients]]. They preferred to work the soil and tend to their crops themselves.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

[[File:BountyHunters_InsideIntel.jpg|thumb|right|275px|Din Djarin teamed up with IG-11 despite his distrust of droids in general]]
The survivor of a [[Attack on Aq Vetina|battle droid attack]] during the Clone Wars,<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] had a distrust and dislike for droids enough to make clear that he had a "no droids" policy to the various people he ran into,<ref name="TM-5">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 5: The Gunslinger}}</ref> though he eventually came to trust<ref name="Chapter 8" /> and befriend<ref name="Insider 210">{{InsiderCite|210|Mandalorian Mayhem}}</ref> the [[assassin droid]] [[IG-11]] during a [[clash on Nevarro]]. He was saddened by the droid's sacrifice in the final stages of the battle.<ref name="Chapter 8" /> Due to his experiences with IG-11,<ref name="Insider 210" /> his distrust of droids lessened. His opinion on droids had risen to the extent that he was even willing for [[Peli Motto]]'s [[DUM-series pit droid]]s to work on his [[starship]], the ''[[Razor Crest]]'', although she suspected that Djarin only barely trusted droids.<ref name="Chapter 9">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref> Indeed, Djarin's anti-droid sentiment re-emerged on a [[mission to Plazir-15|mission]] to the planet [[Plazir-15]], where former battle droids were used in daily work, he undertook with [[Bo-Katan Kryze]].<ref name="Chapter 22">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 22: Guns for Hire}}</ref>
