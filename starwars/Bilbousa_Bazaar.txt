'''Bilbousa Bazaar''' was the primary marketplace in the [[city]] of [[Bilbousa]] on the [[Hutt]] world of [[Nal Hutta]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], both [[Cad Bane]] and an undercover [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] acquired goods from a [[Pablo's pawnshop|pawnshop]] inside of the Bazaar.<ref name="FAE" />
