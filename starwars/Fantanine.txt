The '''Fantanines''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] with [[insectoid]] features. They had long lifespans and a small population, which led them to strongly avoid conflict within their species. The [[Ben Diffle Fantanines]] were a group of Fantanines who operated a [[Fantanine mining colony|mining colony]].<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />
