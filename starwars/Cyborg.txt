A '''cyborg'''<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> or a '''machine''',<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> sometimes called a '''mod''',<ref name="Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref> was any living, [[Species|organic]] being, [[Sentience|sentient]] or not, whose body was enhanced with robotic parts. [[Cybernetics|Cybernetic]] augmentation was an invasive process that changed a being's [[brain]] functions and physiology in significant ways, so much so that it was usually irreversible.<ref name="TVE" />

Cybernetic technology could be used to help repair a body or sustain a life to varying degrees. [[Luke Skywalker]] received a cybernetic hand after [[Dismemberment|losing]] his original hand in a [[lightsaber]] [[Duel on Cloud City|duel]],<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref> while almost the entirety of [[General]] [[Grievous]]' body was replaced by cybernetic [[limb]]s and other body parts.<ref name="GrievousSWE">{{SWE|characters|generalgrievous|General Grievous}}</ref> After falling to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] and becoming [[Darth]] Vader, [[Anakin Skywalker]] was [[Duel on Mustafar|severely burned]] on [[Mustafar]], requiring him to live in a [[Darth Vader's armor|cybernetic suit]] of [[armor]] for the rest of his life. In addition, all of his limbs were amputated and required mechanical replacements.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> The [[human]] [[Tarr Kligson]] was saved from death with cybernetics but began to think of himself as a droid, intending to ascend into a fully-droid form.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 38">[[Bounty Hunters 38|''Bounty Hunters'' 38]]</ref> He believed his cybernetic form left him ostracized by organics and droids alike.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 39">[[Bounty Hunters 39|''Bounty Hunters'' 39]]</ref>

The [[Scourge]] focused on taking control of cyborgs to learn how to transplant itself into organic life.<ref name="Dark Droids 3">[[Dark Droids 3|''Dark Droids'' 3]]</ref> Searching for cyborgs,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 39" /> the Scourge infected a [[Unidentified cyborg (Gallios)|cyborg]] on [[Gallios]],<ref name="D-Squad 2">[[Dark Droids: D-Squad 2|''Dark Droids: D-Squad'' 2]]</ref> [[Lobot]], [[Magna Tolvan]], and [[Beilert Valance]],<ref name="Dark Droids 3" /> who was offered to the Scourge by a willing Kligson. The Scourge, however, did not assimilate Kligson&mdash;judging him to be too human for itself<ref name="Bounty Hunters 39" /> at that time<ref name="Dark Droids 3" />&mdash;and left him to the mercy of Valance's angered allies.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 39" /> The Scourge's ultimate target was Vader,<ref name="Dark Droids Target Vader">{{Marvel|url=articles/comics/star-wars-dark-droids-checklist-reading-order-scourge|text=The Scourge Reveals Its Ultimate Goal by Targeting Darth Vader in 'Star Wars: Dark Droids'}}</ref> whose [[Force power]]s, knowledge, and memories were of great interested to the Scourge.<ref name="Dark Droids 3" /> During the [[Scourge of Coruscant]], the corpses of four [[death trooper]]s were possessed by the Scourge. With his latest rebellion against his [[Sith Master]] [[Darth Sidious]] thwarted, Vader willingly bonded with the Scourge in an effort to finally overpower Sidious.<ref name="Vader 40">[[Darth Vader (2020) 40|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 40]]</ref> He quickly separated himself from its main consciousness, however, and attempted to take control of it for himself.<ref name="Vader 41">[[Darth Vader (2020) 41|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 41]]</ref>

The [[Zero Angle]] [[cantina]] had a cyborg [[bartender]], [[Florn (individual)|Florn]].<ref name="LCZA">{{InsiderCite|156|Last Call at the Zero Angle}}</ref> [[Guavian security soldier]]s were cybernetically enhanced humans that served as the enforcers of the [[Guavian Death Gang]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> [[Fennec Shand]] had a cybernetic lower abdomen after she was shot and nearly killed by [[Toro Calican]].<ref name="Chapter 14">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 14: The Tragedy}}</ref> [[C-3PO]] often introduced himself using the phrase "I am C-3PO, human-cyborg relations."<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref>
