'''Culisetto''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] of small, tube-mouthed [[insectoid]]s that fed off of [[blood]] and also wore traditional [[Color|brown]] traveling [[Clothing|smocks]]. The [[Dengue sisters]] were members of this species<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> as well as the [[bounty hunter]] [[Vorhdeilo]].<ref name="Darth Maul 2">[[Darth Maul (2017) 2|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 2]]</ref>

{{Species-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Culisetto were created for [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], which was released in [[2015]]. The aliens were not identified in the film,<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> but received a name in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary|''Star Wars'': The Force Awakens: ''The Visual Dictionary'']], a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released the same day as the film's release.<ref name="TFAVD" />
