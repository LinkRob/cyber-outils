'''Bong Dazo''', born '''Angelo Ty Dazo''', was an artist who penciled a number of [[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic (comic series)|''Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic'' comics]], as well as [[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed (graphic novel)|''The Force Unleashed'']] graphic novel. He was born in [[Wikipedia:Meycauayan City|Meycauayan]], [[Wikipedia:Bulacan|Bulacan]] in the [[Wikipedia:Philippines|Philippines]]. Before working on [[Star Wars comic books|''Star Wars'' comics]], he was an illustrator for local Filipino comics and likewise introduced the first manga style in the Philippines in [[1988]] before working for [[Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse]] on ''The Irregulars'', written by [[Michael Reaves]].<ref name="DH">{{DarkHorse|url=Books/12-777/The-Irregulars-TPB|text=The Irregulars TPB}}</ref>

Dazo passed away from liver cancer on [[June 29]], [[2018]], less than two weeks after his fifty-third birthday.<ref name="Campiti's obituary" />
