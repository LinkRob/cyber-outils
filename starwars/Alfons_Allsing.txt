'''Alfons Allsing''' was an individual who, around the time of the [[Mid Rim Retreat]], served as a [[minister]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s civil government. Allsing, however was actually an active operative of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] within the Empire, providing information to the Alliance's [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|Intelligence]] agency.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
