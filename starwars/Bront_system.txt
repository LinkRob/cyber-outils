The '''Bront system''' was located in the [[Svivreni sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="TrailingSectors" /> The [[star system]] was situated in grid square O-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Bront system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Bront system/Legends|Bront system]] was introduced in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
