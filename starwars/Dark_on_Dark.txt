'''Dark on Dark''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] adventure ''[[Below the Belt: Philosophy]]''. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[July 12]] [[2002]] in the form of a monologue from [[Ezil|Old Ezil]] speculating on who is trying to overthrow [[Nirama]].
