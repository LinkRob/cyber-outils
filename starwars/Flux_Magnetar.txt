'''Flux Magnetar''' is a fictional lead character in the [[holodrama]] ''[[The Adventures of Flux Magnetar]]''. The series released at least twenty nine episodes<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> In the series they led a failed uprising against the [[King]] on [[Bolaxia]]. As a result they were sentenced by [[Dornigar]] to trial by combat in the burning pits of [[Mount Vardosh]]. After surviving the trial and defeating Dornigar, Flux regroups with some [[krill]] [[farmer]]s and vowing to not leave until Bolaxia is free. Since all the soldiers had been captured Flux planned to turn the framers into the fiercest warriors in all of [[Wild Space]]. They subsequently captured the fortress and began to release the people of Bolaxia until Dornigar returned holding their love, [[Pema]], hostage.<ref name="Outlaws" />
