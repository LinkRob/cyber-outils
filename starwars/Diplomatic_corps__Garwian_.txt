The '''diplomatic corps''' was the [[diplomat]]ic service of the [[Garwian Unity]], a nation in the [[Unknown Regions]], of which the [[Garwian]] [[envoy]] [[Proslis]] was a member.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="CR Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' in [[19 BBY]].</ref> the [[Chiss]] [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," undertook an unsanctioned [[Primea diplomatic reception|mission]] to the [[Vak Combine]] nation [[planet]] of [[Primea]], during which he traveled to the world aboard [[Proslis' diplomatic ship]]. When word of the senior captain's mission arrived at the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], his [[Commanding officer|superior]], [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]], attempted to defend his actions by stating that he had not inserted himself into Garwian [[politics]]. However, [[Irizi family]] [[Syndic]] [[Irizi'stal'mustro]], core name "Zistalmu," retorted that Thrawn traveled aboard a [[Garwian diplomatic ship]] in the company of a Garwian envoy and sarcastically asked if the Garwians had transformed their diplomatic corps into a [[knitting]] club.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Garwian diplomatic corps appeared in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
