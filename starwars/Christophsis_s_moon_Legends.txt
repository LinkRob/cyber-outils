'''Christophsis's moon''' was a [[Moon/Legends|moon]] that [[Orbit/Legends|orbited]] the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Christophsis/Legends|Christophsis]]. Its light-colored surface was marked by large fissures. During the [[Battle of Christophsis/Legends|Battle of Christophsis]], a [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] [[Republic Navy/Legends|naval]] task force hid behind the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] as [[General/Legends|General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] planned their next move.

==Behind the scenes==
''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', released before [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|''The Clone Wars'': Season Two]] stated that Christophsis didn't have a moon.
