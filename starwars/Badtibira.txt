'''Badtibira''' was a location<ref name="MA" /> in the [[Badtibira system]] of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]],<ref name="Interior" /> situated in grid square K-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> In [[67 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]] spent [[time]] negotiating with individuals from Badtibira in conferences. While Dooku was away, Dooku's former [[Padawan]], the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Rael Averross]], visited his Master's current student [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] in the former's [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] [[Dooku's quarters|quarters]]. Surprised to see Jinn was not assisting his Master with the Badtibira dealings, Averross took the opportunity to introduce the Padawan to the [[holocron of prophecy]].<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Badtibira was mentioned in a flashback in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
