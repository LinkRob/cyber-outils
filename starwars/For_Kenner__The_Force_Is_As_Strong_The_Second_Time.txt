"'''For Kenner, The Force Is As Strong The Second Time'''" was a feature article in [[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 8|''Star Wars Galaxy Magazine'' 8]], written by [[Stephen J. Sansweet]]. It profiled [[Kenner Products|Kenner's]] [[Star Wars: The Power of the Force (1995 toy line)|''Star Wars: The Power of the Force'']] toy line, which debuted in [[1995]].
