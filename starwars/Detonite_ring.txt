{{Quote|<nowiki />''A priceless Lengilese life-fasting bracelet'', huh? How long did it take you to paint those ''detonite rings''?|Sana Starros, to Chelli Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 7" />}}
'''Detonite rings''' were a type of explosive. They had a thin [[ring]] shape, and were small enough to be hand-held. The rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] painted several detonite rings in order to claim they were [[Lengilese]] [[life-fasting bracelet]]s, offering one as tribute to the [[Crime lord|crime boss]] [[Lady]] [[Proxima]] in return for information on the [[Path engine]] that had passed through [[Corellia]]. Aphra used another detonite ring against [[General]] [[Vukorah]] of the [[Unbroken Clan]] to steal the [[datapad]] of the [[smuggler]] [[Remy (smuggler)|Remy]], which contained information on the Path engine's origins. As Aphra escaped with the smuggler [[Sana Starros]], she threw several more detonite rings behind them at the pursuing Unbroken Clan operatives.<ref name="Aphra 7" />
