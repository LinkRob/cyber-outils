'''Faylin''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who was part of the [[Free Ryloth Movement]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. She piloted [[Cham Syndulla]]'s ship when they tracked [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor]] when they were stranded on the surface following the attack and destruction of the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Perilous]]''. She also helped Cham, [[Isval]], [[Kallon]], [[Goll]] and his team escape when they were attacked by [[Gutkurr]]s. After, she tailed behind them as they continued the hunt and when they were surrounded and doomed by a squadron of [[V-wing]]s, Faylin came to rescue Cham and got him out safely as the others were slaughtered.<ref name="LOTS" />
