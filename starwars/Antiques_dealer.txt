'''Antiques dealers''' were individuals who sold antiques for a living. The [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Antron Bach]] worked as an antiques dealer before being asked to guard a horde of [[Jedi]] treasures by the [[Jedi Order]] following [[Order 66]].<ref name="TEOH">{{InsiderCite|154|The End of History}}</ref> The [[Ithorian]] [[collector]] [[Dok-Ondar]] was a antiques dealer who ran a [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities|Den of Antiquities]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]].<ref name="Dok-Ondar">{{GEAttr|Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities}}</ref>

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
An antiques dealer first appeared in the short story "[[The End of History]]," which was written by [[Alexander Freed]] and published in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' [[Star Wars Insider 154|154]] in [[2014]].
