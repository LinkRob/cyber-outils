'''Derham Boyce''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as [[sergeant]] in the [[Resistance Army|ground forces]] of the [[Resistance]] during their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[First Order]]. During the [[Battle of Oetchi|attack]] on the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]] by the First Order following the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation]] from the [[planet]] [[D'Qar]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[34 ABY]]. As [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] carries on immediately from the events of ''The Force Awakens'', it can be assumed to take place in the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> Boyce was redeployed as a [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] [[Bridge guard|guard]] on board the [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] in order to provide extra shipboard security. The sergeant wore a [[Color|brown]] military [[Clothing|uniform]] including a [[Armor|combat helmet]], a weatherproof field jacket, pants, [[boot]]s and an [[RM-45 ammunition and tool pouch]]. He carried a [[EL-16 blaster rifle]].<ref name="TLJVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Derham Boyce first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> which was released on [[December 15]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-viii-to-open-december-15-2017|text=''Star Wars: Episode VIII'' to Open December 15, 2017}}</ref> The character was not named in the film,<ref name="TLJ" /> but received identification in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> and released in conjunction with the film.<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/new-star-wars-the-last-jedi-books-and-more-revealed-at-sdcc-2017|text=New ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Books and More Revealed at SDCC 2017}}</ref>
