The '''Arcane Library''' was a [[library]] and artifact vault located within the [[Sith Citadel]], a [[Sith temple]] used by the [[Sith Eternal]] on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]]. It contained information on a variety of [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lords]] and [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Force-sensitive|users]] including recollections and dictations by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]]. After the [[Battle of Exegol]] in [[35 ABY]], the [[Resistance]] excavated the temple during the [[Exegol Excavation Project]] and uncovered information in the library that [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] cited in his [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''. 

==Description==
{{Quote|The excavations on Exegol have found a few references to the group within what appear to be recollections by Palpatine where he states that they had become 'obsolete.'|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
[[File:SithCitadelSurface-CLNE.png|thumb|left|300px|alt=A cutaway of the Sith Citadel on Exegol shows the Arcane Library on the right side of the building as well as various other rooms and levels. The floor outside the citadel is made up of flat, gray slabs.|The Arcane Library also served as an artifact vault.]]
The Arcane Library was a [[library]]<ref name="Rise" /> and artifact vault located three floors above ground level, directly below a [[gladiator]]ial arena,<ref name="CLNE" /> in the [[Sith Citadel]] on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]].<ref name="Rise" /> The library had [[Color|gray]] floors and walls with a central atrium with at least four statues on display, one of which was a large octahedron. The area on each side of the atrium was split into two floors, with one against an exterior wall and one against an interior wall with doors on each floor.<ref name="CLNE" />

The balcony floors on both sides and the lower floor on the interior side each housed two long brown pieces of furniture along their length, while the exterior side lower floor housed at least three sets of freestanding shelving. The side sections of the library were separated from the atrium by a series of regular arches on both levels.<ref name="CLNE" /> The ancient citadel was<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> a [[Sith temple]]<ref name="Rise" /> that acted as headquarters of the [[Sith Eternal]] and was one of the oldest bastions of the [[Sith]].<ref name="TROSVD" />

[[File:ArcaneLibrary-GalacticAtlasUpdated.png|thumb|right|250px|alt=An aisle of black bookshelves, their shelves filled with books, stands beneath an arched gray ceiling.|The Arcane Library was part of the Sith Citadel on Exegol.]]
The library contained information concerning a number of [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lords]], including [[Darth Plagueis]], [[Maul|Darth Maul]], [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]], and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. The information on Plagueis included scrolls and [[writing]]s covering his obsession with [[immortality]] that had been dictated by his [[Sith apprentice]], [[Darth Sidious]], shortly before the latter's [[death]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> although some of it was defaced. The library also contained material with references to the [[Force artifact]] known as the [[Fermata Cage]] and recollections by Sidious that referenced the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Force-sensitive|Force users]] of the [[Inquisitorius]], in which he mentioned them becoming obsolete.<ref name="Rise" />

==History==
{{Quote|It is only through material recently uncovered on Exegol that we even have an indication that the individual who would become his master was named Darth Plagueis. According to scrolls and writings that appear to have been dictated by Palpatine shortly before his most recent death, Plagueis had spent much of his life as a Sith Lord attempting to discover the secrets of eternal life.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
[[File:Plagueis-TheAcolyte.png|thumb|left|290px|alt=Hidden in shadow, Darth Plagueis peers around a rocky corner.|Material from the Arcane Library first revealed the existence of Darth Plagueis.]]
After the Sith Eternal was defeated in the [[Battle of Exegol]]<ref name="Rise" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Resistance]] began the [[Exegol Excavation Project]], an excavation of the temple led by [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]]. The project recovered information from the library, creating several sections of documentation on what they discovered. This included files on Vader, Maul, Tyranus, and Plagueis in a "[[Sith Lord]]s" section, with an Inquisitorius file stored in the "Warriors of the Dark Side" section and the Fermata Cage file stored under "Force Artifacts."<ref name="Rise" />

The information from the library helped Kin further construct a hierarchy of the Sith Lords, which had been a major part of<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD" /> research prior to the project. Although the hierarchy remained incomplete, the new material revealed the existence of Plagueis for the first [[time]] as well as Tyranus's apprenticeship to Sidious. Kin later cited the files made on the library's material multiple times in his [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise" /> which was also published in 35 ABY.<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>

An updated edition of the ''[[Galactic Atlas]]''&mdash;a book published by the [[Graf Archive]] at some point after Sidious's death&mdash;included a map of Exegol created by the [[artist]] [[Gammit Chond]] that showed an image of the Arcane Library's interior.<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>
