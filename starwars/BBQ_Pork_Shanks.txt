'''BBQ Pork Shanks''' was a dish served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. The meat was served with [[salad]] and a [[muffin]]. It was consumable by [[human]]s.<ref name="Docking Bay 7" />

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
BBQ Pork Shanks was a [[In-universe|real-world]] entrée available for a limited time at [[Disneyland]]'s location of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]. The dish was only set to be available for the weekend of [[May 31]] to [[June 2]], [[2024]], commemorating the fifth anniversary of the land. It was available for purchase at Docking Bay 7.<ref name="Dish announcement">{{Instagram|username=disneyeats|url=disneyeats/p/C7hgKO9xZ6X|name=Disney Eats|date=2024-05-28|quote=May 31 marks five years since Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge opened at @disneyland! Celebrate with the BBQ Pork Shanks with a leek and brussels sprouts salad and blueberry corn muffin, available at Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo May 31 through June 2! 🪐💫 #DisneyEats #DisneyParks#DisneylandResort#StarWarsGalaxysEdge|archiveurl=https://archive.is/ukmt6}}</ref> The menu description of the dish read, "Tangy BBQ Pork Shanks, Leek and Brussels Sprouts Salad, Blueberry Corn Muffin."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch And Dinner Menu}}</ref>
