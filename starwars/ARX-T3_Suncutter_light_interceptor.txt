The '''ARX-T3 Suncutter light interceptor''' was a [[starfighter]] manufactured by [[Koensayr Manufacturing]].<ref name="Knights of Fate" />

==Characteristics==
Mounting considerable armament for an [[interceptor]], the Suncutter boasted exceptional speed and mobility due to an in-line ion turbine engine; combined with heavier armor than most interceptors, the Suncutter was an ideal dogfighter, and often had the edge in stand-up fights.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />

==History==
A departure from [[Koensayr Manufacturing]]s better known [[BTL Y-wing starfighter|Y-wing]], the Suncutter focused on a unique balance of defense, agility, and armament over armor or playload as with previous craft. Produced in the early days of the [[Galactic Empire]], its number became limited when Koensayr lost its contract.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />
