'''Chulco Gi''' was a [[Lorrdian]] [[Gender|male]] who was a member of the [[Brotherhood of the Beatific Countenance]] and aspired to join the [[Disciples of the Whills]]. Gi and [[Ubin Des]] encountered [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Han Solo]] in their [[battle for Jedha]] after the [[Battle of Yavin|destruction]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. Gi [[Death|died]] after being corrupted by the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] of [[the Force]] at the [[Central Isopter]] [[Temple of Central Isopter|shrine]], where he was subsequently impaled by Skywalker.<ref name="SW 38" />
