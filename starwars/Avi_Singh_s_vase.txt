{{Quote|This antique vase was gifted to Senator Singh for his years of service. It is priceless.|GS-8|<ref name="Common Ground" />}}
An ''' antique vase''' was owned by [[Senator]] [[Avi Singh]], gifted to him for his many years of dedicated service. The vase was located at [[Avi Singh's residence|his residence]] and, according to his [[RA-7 protocol droid]] [[GS-8]], it was most prized to him.<ref name="Common Ground" />

During the [[Occupation of Raxus Secundus]] by the [[Galactic Empire]], Singh was detained by the Imperial authorities, and [[Clone Force 99]] and GS-8 [[Extraction on Raxus|broke him out]]. Even though GS-8 brought the vase along with them, the senator ultimately smashed it against a clone [[stormtrooper]]'s head during their escape, destroying it. He commented that he never really cared about the vase anyway.<ref name="Common Ground" />
