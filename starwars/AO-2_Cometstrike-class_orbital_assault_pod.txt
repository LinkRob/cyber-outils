The '''AO-2 ''Cometstrike''-class orbital assault pod''' was a model of [[orbital assault pod]] developed by [[Cygnus Spaceworks]] as the successor to the [[AO-1 orbital assault pod]]. The craft had a single purpose, to quickly and securely deliver its passengers to a [[planet]]'s surface, and as such was designed to hurtle towards its target at a speed high enough to avoid engagement from enemy [[Starfighter|fighters]] as well as ground-defense weapons. The pods were marketed towards [[mercenary]] companies and were used by small [[warlord]]s on the edge of the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="Knights of Fate" />
