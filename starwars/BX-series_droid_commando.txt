'''BX-series droid commandos''', also known as '''BX-series commando droids''', '''BX commando droids''', '''BX droids''', or simply '''commando droids''', were advanced [[commando]] [[battle droid]]s used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Designed for infiltration and action behind enemy lines, they were also used to perform security, [[bodyguard]], and enforcer duties for certain high-ranking Separatists. Ruthless, lethal, and adaptive, these [[droid]]s were the bane of [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[clone trooper]]s and in multiple [[squad]]s could change the tide of the battlefield. They were able to put even a [[Jedi]] on the back foot and were a match for some of the most skilled [[Cloning|clones]]. However, their hefty price tag prevented them from being mass-produced.

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|So, commando droids, right? What exactly does a commando droid do?''"<br />"''We are programmed for combat, clandestine infiltration and assassination.''"<br />"''You're still here, so I guess you must have been pretty successful.|A Bedlam Raider speaks with a commando droid.|<ref name="Survivor" />}}
[[File:CommandoDroid-CGSWG.png|thumb|left|250px|BX-series droid commandos were much more flexible than most other droid models.]]
Part of an advanced [[droid]] series,<ref name="Force Collection commando droid">{{ForceCollection|BX Droid Commando|star=4}}</ref> BX-series were more adaptable, even smarter & much sturdier than their [[B1-series battle droid|B1-series counterpart]]s. They were programmed with improved combat tactics and [[Ground warfare|battlefield]] awareness and equipped with glowing [[Color|white]] [[photoreceptor]]s. [[Commando droid captain]]s and other high-[[Military rank|ranking]] commando droids bore white identifiers on their heads and chests.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> The unit was designed for infiltration and heavy combat, whereas the unit's programming prepared it for combat, assassination missions, and clandestine infiltration tasks.<ref name="Survivor" /> The BX unit [[ND-5]] would later recall that, as a battle droid, he was concerned with following his orders and destroying whomever he was sent against, instead of caring about why he was performing those duties. ND-5 feared even later into his life he would be able to do nothing more than hunt and destroy targets, but his companion [[Kay Vess]] assured ND-5 he was more than just a war machine.<ref name="Outlaws" /> The body of a BX unit was made from a [[titanium]]-reinforced [[steel]]<ref name="DV19">[[Darth Vader (2015) 19|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 19]]</ref> that served as strong blaster resistant armor.<ref name="TCWRookies" /> Even the internal components were shielded.<ref name="Outlaws" /> BX units stationed as security in the [[Spire (Stygeon Prime)|Spire]] [[prison]] were plated with [[cortosis]].<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> [[Commando Droid Diplomat]]s were a specialised variant of commando droids, differantiated by their brown, yellow and blue colored plating.<ref name="AFiN">{{TCW|A Friend in Need}}</ref>

Due to a more compact head than the B1, commando droids could don [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] for infiltration missions.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Their heavy [[armor]] improved durability and allowed them to withstand several direct hits from a [[DC-15A blaster carbine]],<ref name="TCWRookies" /> though a point-blank headshot with an [[ELG-3A blaster pistol]] was capable of destroying one instantly.<ref name="SW" /> Additionally commando droids were extremely agile and acrobatic,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> with their midsection armor offering more flexibility over B1 battle droids.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> They were also able to move and react much faster than a standard battle droid performing several flips in one.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> They were even able to continue fighting after sustaining significant damage such as having their legs sliced off with a [[lightsaber]],<ref name="GI" /> in contrast to standard B1 battle droids that could be defeated simply by knocking them over.<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>

[[File:CommandOverride-SWQ151.png|thumb|right|250px|A group of commando droids ambush a squad of clone troopers.]]
For improved infiltration capabilities, commando droids were able to alter their [[vocabulator]]s in order to imitate clone troopers. However, their awkward movements in the armor and atypical responses to orders (usually "roger, roger," a common battle-droid response) meant that observant individuals could see through the ruse.<ref name="TCWRookies" /> Commando droids were also quite strong, as one was able to lift [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] up by his neck<ref name="TD" />, as well as [[Morgan Elsbeth]] during the genocide of the Nightsisters<ref name="The Path of Fear">{{TOTE|The Path of Fear}}</ref>, and Crosshair during the rescue of Governer Grotton on Desix<ref name="Solitary" />. Commando droids were skilled in melee combat, using [[vibrosword]]s in close quarters<ref name="TCWRookies" /> as well as being very agile, able to leap and contort their bodies to avoid blasterfire and surprise enemies.<ref name="Citadel" /> While they had numerous improvements over rank-and-file B1 battle droids, their production costs proved to be much higher, causing the B1 to continue to remain the standard infantry for the Confederacy.<ref name="CommandoDroidSWE">{{SWE|technology|commandodroid|commando droid}}</ref> Commando droids also had combat programming.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

On Grievous's behalf, the droid [[C-5DS]] contacted the [[Akiva production plant]] with an advance order for twelve thousand BX commandos for use in Grievous's forces.<ref name="Outlaws" /> At least one commando droid took part in the [[Battle of Antar 4]], where the BX unit fought well.<ref name="Survivor" />

In 22 BBY,<ref name="CC Date">{{TCWcanondates|301}}</ref> re-programmed commando droids, as well as B1 battle droids and [[B2-series super battle droid]]s, were utilized in the [[Citadel Challenge]]<ref name="CC">{{TCW|Clone Cadets}}</ref> in the [[Tipoca City Training Facility]]<ref name="Training f DB">{{Databank|tipoca-city-training-facility|Tipoca City Training Facility}}</ref> on the planet [[Kamino]] to train [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[clone cadet]]s in simulated combat scenarios. For the scenario, the droids were colored white with orange target markings on their head and torso.<ref name="CC" /> That year,<ref name="CC Date" /> the clone cadets of [[Domino Squad]] fought commando droids in the challenge.<ref name="CC" />

[[File:DroidCommandos-Rookies.png|thumb|left|300px|Three commando droids about to kill Clone Sergeant O'Niner.]]
Later that year,<ref name="Rookies date" /> at a point in the Clone Wars when commando droids were still considered new, Grievous used commando droids to [[Defense of Rishi Station|infiltrate]] and take control of the Republic's [[Rishi Moon]] [[Rishi Station|Station]]. At first, the droids landed in a [[Droch-class boarding ship|''Droch''-class boarding ship]] and, after ambushing the deck officer, [[CT-327]], they forced their way into the base and killed [[Clone Sergeant]] [[O'Niner]] and all of his men save four clones that escaped through a ventilation shaft. The surviving clones, who had all been part of the Domino Squad, were able to maintain control of the station until [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]] and [[Cloning|Clone]] Captain Rex arrived to inspect the station. Along with the survivors of the [[Rishi Station unit]], Cody and Rex were able to retake the [[listening post]] from the commando droids and destroy them.<ref name="TCWRookies" />

However, droid reinforcements arrived at the station, so one of the station's [[clone trooper]]s, [[Hevy]], sacrificed himself destroy the base. The commando droids had hard-wired the base's all-clear signal, so the only way to alert the Republic of a planned Separatist attack on [[Kamino]] was to destroy the station, but the [[detonator]] did not link up with the [[tibanna]] that was to be used to destroy the station. This forced Hevy to activate it himself, so Hevy sacrificed himself for the closest thing a clone trooper could call home. The base was destroyed, and the Republic stopped receiving the all-clear signal, which alerted them that something was wrong. A Republic [[fleet]] arrived at the [[Rishi]] [[moon]], and Grievous and his fleet, being outgunned, were forced to retreat.<ref name="TCWRookies" />

[[File:BX-seriesDroidCommandosUnitExp-SWL72.png|thumb|right|200px|Four commando droids infiltrate the Rishi Moon base.]]
At least two commando droids were stationed in [[Lessu City]] during the [[Battle of Ryloth]]. There they attempted to stop the [[Advanced Recon Force trooper|ARF troopers]] [[Stak]] and [[Razor]] from activating the [[plasma bridge]], but were defeated. The two ARF [[trooper]]s activated the bridge, and Lessu was ultimately taken by the Republic and the [[Twi'lek Resistance]]. Separatist leader [[Wat Tambor]] was captured, and the Separatist occupation of [[Ryloth]] came to an end.<ref name="LoR">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref>

General Grievous used commando droids when he boarded [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] member [[Eeth Koth]]'s [[flagship]], the [[Steadfast (Venator-class)|''Steadfast'']], when he captured Koth. Later, in the [[Saleucami system]], Grievous and his [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|tactical droid]], [[TV-94]], then [[Battle of Saleucami|used them]] against fellow [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Adi Gallia]], and [[Anakin Skywalker]] as they rescued Koth from Grievous.<ref name="GI" /> A pair of commando droids then acted as [[sniper]]s after Grievous was forced to crash-land on [[Saleucami]]'s surface. One of the droids then shot Captain Rex as he approached them leading a speeder patrol. The shot pierced the clone captain's armor and would have gone straight through his heart had it been two inches to the left. Both droids were promptly run down and shot by Rex's companions.<ref name="TD" />

While Rex was recovering at a [[Lawquane's Farm|farmstead]], he discovered that it belonged to a clone deserter named [[Cut Lawquane]], whose adopted children stumbled across a Separatist escape pod that had crashed in one of their fields. Their exploration of the pod led to them accidentally activating its complement of commando droids, who proceeded to attack the homestead as Rex and Cut defended it. Despite being heavily damaged, the droids gave the clones a considerable fight and caused extensive damage to the homestead before they were all destroyed.<ref name="TD" />

====The war continues====
The [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] used a [[Unidentified BX-series droid commando 1 (Cad Bane)|pair of]] [[Unidentified BX-series droid commando 2 (Cad Bane)|commando droids]] when he [[Senate hostage crisis|held]] several members of the [[Galactic Senate]] hostage. There, they were able to impersonate the [[Senate Commando]]s stationed at the entrance. They later escaped along with Bane, Ziro, and the other bounty hunters. These commando droids were adorned with the [[Black Sun]] symbol and sported a dark blue color.<ref name="HC">{{TCW|Hostage Crisis}}</ref> It was never discovered whether the two droids were gifts that Bane had received, or units he had captured and reprogrammed.<ref name="DD">{{BuildR2Cite|69|Droid Directory}}</ref>

[[File:BXDroidCommando-CntrAtt.png|thumb|left|180px|A commando droid at the Citadel.]]
Commando droids were also used as guards at the [[Citadel]] on [[Lola Sayu]] under [[Warden]] [[Osi Sobeck]], denoted by special purple and gold plating. There they guarded the [[prisoner]]s, served as security,<ref name="Citadel" /> and operated the [[laser cannon]]s.<ref name="CA" />

The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederate]] [[minister]] [[Rish Loo]] used commando droids against [[Jedi General]] Anakin Skywalker and [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] when his plan was [[Skirmish on Naboo|exposed]]. Though the droids were destroyed in fairly short order, they distracted the two long enough for Loo to escape.<ref name="SW">{{TCW|Shadow Warrior}}</ref>

On [[Kiros]], commando droids drove [[Flitknot Speeder Bike]]s and [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|AATs]] when the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Republic forces]] [[Battle on Kiros|arrived]]. The Confederate [[commander]] [[Darts D'Nar]] had two with him as his guards when he negotiated with Jedi General Obi-Wan Kenobi for his surrender. Kenobi fought D'Nar in [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand combat]], and this distracted him while Jedi General Anakin Skywalker and [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] disarmed all of D'Nar's [[bomb]]s in the [[city]]. After being informed of this, Kenobi swiftly destroyed both droids by lifting them into the air and crushing them with [[the Force]]. D'Nar escaped from Kenobi by using a bomb he had hidden in his [[Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Kiros)|T-series tactical droid]], much to the [[tactical droid]]'s horror, but Skywalker and Tano caught up to D'Nar, and he was captured by Tano.<ref name="Kidnapped">{{TCW|Kidnapped}}</ref>

During a [[Confederate–Republic peace conference]] at [[Sundari]] on [[Mandalore]], [[Commando Droid Diplomat]]s served as guards to the Confederate delegation. Several then forcibly removed [[Onderon]]'s Senator, [[Lux Bonteri]], when he spoke out against [[Count]] [[Dooku]] for his part in the [[Attack on Confederate people|murder]] of [[Mina Bonteri|Lux's mother]]. After [[Padawan]] Ahsoka Tano rescued Bonteri from his [[Death|execution]], the commando droids then fought the [[Senate Guard]]s under Captain [[Taggart (Senate Guard)|Taggart]] when the peace conference broke down.<ref name="AFiN" />

[[File:RideEmCowdroids-SWZ.png|thumb|right|250px|A pair of commando droids riding an HMP droid gunship on Onderon.]]
Commando droids were used by Grievous when Dooku ordered the destruction of the [[Nightsisters]] of [[Dathomir]]. During the [[Battle of Dathomir]], commando droids were deployed by Grievous to combat the Nightsisters, and were with Grievous when he and his droids found [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]] and Old [[Daka]] in a secret [[cave]] in the [[Nightsister lair]]. A commando droid used a [[Missile launcher|rocket launcher]] to destroy the cave's wall, allowing Grievous and several droids to get in and eliminate the Nightsisters inside.<ref name="Massacre" /> Around that time, several commando droids fought a group of Nightsisters while Grievous fought [[Selena]] before killing the future Magistrate [[Morgan Elsbeth]]'s mother. Three commando droids later chased Morgan, but she hid in a tree, fooling two of the droids, though the last one stayed back to find and fight Morgan, though the Nightsister triumphed, despite the droid's tough design. Her victory would be a small pyrrhic win however, since the Separatist Droid Army continued the massacre of the people with Grievous's laugh echoing throughout the burning land.<ref name="The Path of Fear">{{TOTE|The Path of Fear}}</ref>

Several commando droids were deployed to the [[Onderonian]] [[capital]] city [[Iziz]].<ref name="Front Runners">{{TCW|Front Runners}}</ref> They led the attack on the [[Onderon rebels]]' [[The nest|base]] in the [[Onderon highlands|highlands]], killing many rebels alongside the new Separatist [[HMP droid gunship]].<ref name="Tipping Points">{{TCW|Tipping Points}}</ref>

====The final year====
Several commando droids took part in Grievous's [[Invasion of Florrum|invasion]] of [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s [[Ohnaka Gang|Gang's]] [[Hondo's complex|base]] on [[Florrum]], which left the [[pirate]] [[captain]]'s base in ruins.<ref name="A Necessary Bond">{{TCW|A Necessary Bond}}</ref>

[[File:Aftermath-of-Zanbar.png|thumb|left|270px|The remains of commando droids in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Zanbar]].]]
[[Admiral]] [[Trench]] used commando droids in his [[Battle of Ringo Vinda|defense]] of [[Ringo Vinda space station]]. Several commando droids were also on [[Kraken's Maxillipede shuttle|the]] [[Maxillipede shuttle]] under the command of Commander [[Kraken]] used to [[transport]] clone trooper [[Tup]] from Ringo Vinda, as the trooper had mysteriously killed Jedi General [[Tiplar]] in what had been an early execution of [[Order 66]]<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref> due to a failure in the clone's [[behavioral modification biochip]].<ref name="Fugitive">{{TCW|Fugitive}}</ref> The droids on the [[shuttle]] were destroyed by Anakin Skywalker, Clone Captain Rex, and [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Fives]], and Tup was rescued,<ref name="The Unknown" /> but Kraken was eventually rebuilt. Commando droids were used by Dooku when he [[Invasion of Scipio|invaded]] [[Scipio]]. There, they fought Commander [[Thorn]]'s detachment of the [[Coruscant Guard]] and left no survivors. A lone commando droid then apprehended Republic Senator Amidala alongside a battle droid and brought her before Count Dooku, with both droids being destroyed when Anakin Skywalker impaled them with his lightsaber.<ref name="Crisis at the Heart">{{TCW|Crisis at the Heart}}</ref>

Several<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> Commando Droid Diplomats<ref name="Legacyvideo">{{SWYouTube|vnH1JKr4o_8|Star Wars: The Clone Wars Legacy|sw_url=video/the-clone-wars-legacy}}</ref> also guarded Grievous and Dooku during Dooku's acceptance speech for the [[Raxian Humanitarian Award]] on a balcony on [[Raxus Secundus]].<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

Commando droids were also involved in the [[Battle of Anaxes]] during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]]. A team of commandos arrived at the remains of a fight between battle droids and [[Clone Force 99]], and [[Unidentified BX-series droid commando (Anaxes)|one unit]] reported the unusual tactics used by this clone squad to Admiral Trench. Commando droids also arrived at a [[Anaxes Separatist Security Outpost|Separatist Security Outpost]] after it was attacked by Clone Force 99, [[Advanced Recon Commando]] [[Jesse]], and Captain Rex.<ref name="The Bad Batch">{{TCW|The Bad Batch (episode)}}</ref> Later during the battle, General Skywalker defeated the two commando droids Trench sent against him while aboard the admiral's flagship, the ''[[Invulnerable]]''<ref name="Unfinished Business">{{TCW|Unfinished Business (episode)}}</ref> Also during the Sieges, an entry on the BX-series droid commando was included in "[[Neyo's Almanac of Clankers]]," a survival compendium sent out by [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Neyo]] that listed notable battle droids.<ref name="Database">{{GermanFanCite|121|Database}}</ref>

As with all the other battle droids, the commando droids [[Mission to Mustafar|were deactivated]] alongside the entire droid army when the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|took control]] of [[the galaxy]]<ref name="Solitary" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="19 BBY date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|III}}, which features the [[Mission to Mustafar|deactivation]] of the [[Separatist Droid Army]], to [[19 BBY]].</ref>

===Age of the Empire===
{{Quote|A working BX unit? I've only seen them in pieces.|Temmin Wexley, upon seeing ND-5|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
In the early days of the [[Galactic Empire]], a platoon of commando droids served under the command of governor [[Tawni Ames]] of [[Desix]].<ref name="Solitary" /> The commando droid [[Eighty-One]] was active during the Imperial Era but was destroyed during the [[hunt on Chandar's Folly]].<ref name="Dark Lord 18">[[Darth Vader (2017) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 18]]</ref> On the planet [[Koboh]], commando droids and other former Separatist forces were reprogrammed and used as soldiers by the [[Bedlam Raiders]].<ref name="Survivor" />

[[File:VadersArmy-AN.png|thumb|right|300px|0-0-0 and BT-1 leading a group of Darth Vader's commando droids]]
After the [[Battle of Yavin|destruction]] of the first [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], [[Darth]] Vader's position became endangered, as his [[Sith Master|master]], [[Darth Sidious]], planned to replace him with the help of a rogue [[cybernetics]] doctor named [[Cylo]]. In order to cement his place and reassure his master, Vader enlisted [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]] and forcefully took a droid factory from [[Karina]], using it to create an [[Darth Vader's secret forces|army]] of commando droids to deal with Cylo, and lost half of them doing so.<ref name="Darth Vader 4" /> Aphra commanded a platoon of Vader's commando droids for the [[assault on Commodex Tahn's villa]].<ref name="AphraBook">''[[Doctor Aphra: An Audiobook Original]]''</ref>

Later, Vader used his commando droids during the [[Battle at Rubix's citadel|final battle]] of the [[Shu-Torun War|War on Shu-Torun]]. One of Vader's [[assassin droid]]s, [[0-0-0]], had also modified the droids to have [[cyber syringe]]s in order to drain the [[blood]] of the enemy troops. Horrified at the commando units suddenly proclaiming that they would bring death to their organic foes, the enemy army surrendered.<ref name="DV19" /> Afterward, Vader sent his droids to help 0-0-0, [[BT-1]], and [[Krrsantan]] [[Capture of Chelli Lona Aphra|capture Aphra]].<ref name="Darth Vader 21">[[Darth Vader (2015) 21|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 21]]</ref> The [[Tech Masters]] used commando droids as guards.<ref name="CYD2" />

[[File:ND5full-OutlawsVCG.png|thumb|left|150px|After being freed from Jaylen Vrax's control by Kay Vess, who removed his restraining bolt, ND-5 killed Vrax and left with her.]]
Later, Aphra was pursued by a [[Unidentified droid bounty hunter|monster droid bounty hunter]], which was made from BX-series and B2 battle droid parts. The bounty hunter was destroyed during the [[skirmish on Arbiflux]].<ref name="Aphra 34">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 34|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 34]]</ref> The Commando droid ND-5, owned by the crime lord Jaylen Vrax, worked with Kay Vess in pulling off a heist on [[Westhill Palace|Sliro's Mansion]]. After his restraining bolt was removed by Vess, he killed Vrax<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> and realized that, for the first time in his entire existence, he had a real ability to choose what to do with himself. While he considered leaving Vess and deduced he would have a higher chance of survival on his own, he realized working with Vess felt right and thus stayed by her side, deciding further that simply surviving was not enough for him.<ref name="OutlawsPirate">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' {{C|''A Pirate's Fortune'' DLC}}</ref>

In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Life Debt Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> the [[Siniteen]] prison builder [[Golas Aram]] used several commando droids to guard his private compound on the planet [[Irudiru]]. The [[New Republic]] operatives [[Sinjir Rath Velus]] and [[Jas Emari]] fought and defeated several of Aram's droids with the help of the [[New Republic Special Forces]] soldier [[Jom Barell]].<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>

===Rise of the First Order===
{{Quote|A commando droid? Okay... I think I might be able to help.|Temmin Wexley|<ref name="Poe Dameron 13">[[Poe Dameron 13|''Poe Dameron'' 13]]</ref>}}
During the [[New Republic Era]] around [[11 ABY]], several commando droids were part of the [[Droid Gotra]], serving a security for the [[Hutt Clan]] on [[Nal Hutta]]. The commando droids were all destroyed when the [[Hutt Palace]] was destroyed by the [[Adelphi Rangers]] lead by [[Colonel]] [[Ward]] to rescue [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]], his adoptive son [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], and [[Rotta]] the [[Hutt]].<ref name="The Mandalorian and Grogu">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref>

[[File:NunzixMeetsPoe-PoeDameron10.jpg|thumb|right|175px|Nunzix was a Resistance spy droid.]]
In [[34 ABY]], the commando droid [[N1-ZX]] (or "Nunzix") was a member of the [[Resistance]]'s [[Resistance spy droid network|spy droid network]] prior to the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]]. Nunzix was programmed with a self-preservative software that made him obsessed with preserving his own life. Nunzix had a grumpy and selfish demeanor and was unwilling to risk his own life. He obtained information about the [[First Order]] and the location of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] while posted on the planet [[Kaddak]]. [[Poe Dameron]], his commander [[C-3PO]], and [[BB-8]] undertook a [[Mission to Kaddak|mission]] to rescue him. Nunzix showed little gratitude to his rescuers and the [[spy droid]] vowed to only release the information once they returned to the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 10">[[Poe Dameron 10|''Poe Dameron'' 10]]</ref>

Poe came to suspect that the information was ruse by the [[First Order Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Terex]] to lure the Resistance into a trap. Terex and the [[Ranc Gang]] pursued Poe and the droids.<ref name="Poe Dameron 11">[[Poe Dameron 11|''Poe Dameron'' 11]]</ref> N1-ZX and his rescuers crash-landed on a [[Unidentified desert planet|desert planet]] and fled into a cave.<ref name="Poe Dameron 12">[[Poe Dameron 12|''Poe Dameron'' 12]]</ref> With N1-ZX unwilling to fight, Poe overrode his programming with a droid personality template supplied by his friend [[Temmin Wexley]]. Nunzix assumed the B1 battle droid [[Mister Bones]]' personality and slaughtered many of Terex's gangsters before being gunned down and disintegrated by Terex himself.<ref name="Poe Dameron 13" />

==Equipment==
[[File:FFG-Commandodroidsattheready.png|thumb|left|300px|Commando droids were armed with weapons like vibroswords and blaster rifles.]]
Droid commandos used a variety of weapons during battle. They commonly used the standard blaster of the Droid Army, the E-5 blaster rifle.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> They were also known to use<ref name="GI" /> [[vibrosword]]s<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> for melee combat.<ref name="GI" /> For ground assault they used [[thermal detonator]]s<ref name="TCWRookies" /> and [[BX-series sniper rifle|BX-series]] [[sniper rifle]]s for sniping.<ref name="TD" /> Commando droids sometimes used special [[Droid commando personal shield|energy shields]].<ref name="CA" />

The droids also often carried [[stun baton]]s.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> During the Battle of Dathomir, one of General Grievous' commando droids wielded a missile launcher.<ref name="Massacre" /> At the Citadel, one droid commando used an [[electrostaff]] for the use of torture. Other droid commandos at the Citadel used the cannons stationed there, but those cannons were normally operated by B1 battle droids.<ref name="CA" /> The droids also had the ability to imitate the voices of others and would impersonate clone troopers<ref name="TCWRookies" /> and Senate Commandos by altering their voices and wearing their armor.<ref name="HC" />

==Specialized BX-series commando droids==
*'''[[Commando droid captain]]'''&mdash;A commando droid variant tasked with commanding other commando droids. They had white head and chest markings.<ref name="Join the Battle" />
*'''[[Commando Droid Diplomat]]'''&mdash;A variant that guarded Separatist [[diplomat]]s.<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> Their plating was blue, brown and yellow.<ref name="AFiN" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|<nowiki></nowiki>''[BX-series droid commandos are]'' obviously more intelligent. They're substantially faster. It raises the question, why don't the Separatists just use hundreds and thousands of these guys? They would win the war rather easily. And, obviously, a more sophisticated droid is more expensive to make. It's harder to manufacture. But, in the episode, if you watch, they're not invulnerable either. They lose the base, and then, actually, a large squad of regular battle droids actually takes the base back over, so it shows you that, while dim-witted, the original battle droids are still a threat.|Supervising Director [[Dave Filoni]] on the relationship between B1 battle droids and BX commando droids|<ref name="Rookies featurette">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season One|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season One]]</ref>}}
BX-series droid commandos were created for the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' and first appeared in the season one episode "[[Rookies (episode)|Rookies]]",<ref name="TCWRookies" /> which aired on [[October 24]], [[2008]].<ref name="This Week Hype">{{ThisWeek|WDz6G4BMe7c|The Mandalorian Season Two Hype, Tales from the Galaxy's Edge Action, and More!|sw_url=video/the-mandalorian-season-two-hype-tales-from-the-galaxy-s-edge-action-and-more}}</ref> The commando droids were conceived out of the need for the series to have battle droids that were more advanced and intimidating than regular B1 droids. They were designed by [[Kilian Plunkett]], with concept sketches dated to "Nov 09". Lightning concepts for "Rookies" were done by [[Matt Gaser]]. Because they had to be able to wear clone trooper armor, Plunkett designed the commando droids with more human-like proportions than the other battle droids.<ref name="Art of">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''</ref>

BX units appeared as enemies in the video game ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'', within which one BX claims to have been made five "cycles" before the Clone Wars began.<ref name="Survivor" /> However, other appearances have suggested that the BX unit came into being during the war itself.<ref name="TCWRookies" /><ref name="Outlaws" />
