'''Ahsoka's Jedi Interceptor''' was an '''[[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor]]''' flown by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]. The ship [[color]] was red similar to her previous [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]]. Her droid [[R7-A7]] continued to serve as her copilot.<ref name="Sabotage" />

==History==
===Defense of Cato Neimoidia===
During the [[19 BBY|last]] [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] utilized the [[Starfighter|fighter]] in the [[Defense of Cato Neimoidia|Battle of Cato Neimoidia]]. The Eta-2 was flown by Tano when she and her Master [[Anakin Skywalker]] engaged [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[warship]]s in [[Cato Neimoidia]]'s [[orbit]]. She then lead a [[squadron]] of [[Z-95 Headhunter]]s down to the surface alongside her master, followed closely by [[Low Altitude Assault Transport|LAATs]] for ground defense.<ref name="Sabotage" />

On their way down to the surface, Tano and Skywalker got involved in a dog fight against a trio of [[droid tri-fighter]]s. The trio fired missiles at the two Jedi, with Tano narrowly avoiding the projectiles. The projectiles dispensed [[Pistoeka sabotage droid|buzz droids]] which collided with Skywalker's ship, causing the droids to infest and severely damage the ship.<ref name="Sabotage" />

[[File:AhsokaEta2.png|left|thumb|Ahsoka flying her Eta-2, preparing to save Anakin.|250x250px]]
Seeing that Skywalker's fighter was going down Tano decided to help him get out of his cockpit. Instructing Skywalker's [[Astromech droid|astromech]] [[R2-D2]] to land the ship on a stretch of land up ahead, Tano left control of the ship in the hands of [[R7-A7]] while she helped him successfully evacuate the fighter.<ref name="Sabotage" />

==Commanders and crew==
The starfighter was piloted by [[Ahsoka Tano]], who also functioned as its captain. Tano's [[astromech droid]] [[R7-A7]] functioned as the ship's repairer should it be damaged in open combat.<ref name="Sabotage" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ahsoka Tano's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor's first and only appearance was "[[Sabotage (episode)|Sabotage]]," the seventeenth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="Sabotage" />
