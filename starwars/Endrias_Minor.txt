'''Endrias Minor''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Han Chewie 9" /> in the [[Endrias Minor system]] in the [[Tammuz sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> the criminal [[Khel Tanna]] and her [[Khel Tanna's crew|crew]] were involved in a firefight on Endrias Minor when Tanna received a message from a [[Unidentified Gran (Khel Tanna)|Gran]] who informed Tanna that he had spotted [[Han Solo]], along with an urn Tanna sought, on the [[Smuggler]]'s [[Moon]] [[Nar Shaddaa]]. Tanna and her crew then left Endrias Minor and headed to Nar Shaddaa to acquire the urn.<ref name="Han Chewie 9" />
