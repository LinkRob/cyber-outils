'''Eric Larson''' was a Senior Art Director at TL Partnership, a promotional advertising agency based in [[Wikipedia:Dallas|Dallas]], [[Wikipedia:Texas|Texas]]. A ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan, Eric Larson handled much of the design efforts when the [[Wikipedia:PepsiCo|PepsiCo]] food corporation created collectible promotions to coincide with the release of the [[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition|Special Edition]] of the [[original trilogy]] in [[1997]]. Larson notably created "[[Unidentified alphabet/Legends|Jabba's alphabet]]," a writing system in which coded messages would be written in pizza boxes for [[Wikipedia:Pizza Hut|Pizza Hut]].<ref name="SWI35" /> That alphabet was not used much in [[canon]], but it did appear in the [[2002]] video game ''[[Star Wars: Racer Revenge]]''.<ref name="SWR">''[[Star Wars: Racer Revenge]]''</ref> The [[Star Wars Insider 35|thirthy-fifth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine featured a four-page interview with Larson.<ref name="SWI35" />
