'''Electrolytic transfer fluid''' was a substance in which [[NM-K reconstitutor]]s were generally kept in a suspension before being applied to a raw material work area with a swab or roller.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Behind the scenes==
Electrolytic transfer fluid was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />
