'''''Dawn of the Jedi: Force War'' 2''' is the twelfth issue in the ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series and the second in the [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: Force War|''Force War'']] arc. It is set in [[25,792 BBY]] during the earliest days of the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]], in a [[Pre-Republic era|time before]] the polarization of the [[Light side of the Force/Legends|light]] and [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark sides]] of [[The Force/Legends|the Force]]. This issue was released on [[December 18]], [[2013]].

==Publisher's summary==
''The Je'daii go on the offensive against the invading Rakatan Empire!''

''Xesh uses his Force Hound training to locate the Rakatan command center, which sparks an ambitious plan: infiltrate the enemy stronghold and kill the supreme commander. But Xesh has not accounted for a traitor in his own ranks!''
