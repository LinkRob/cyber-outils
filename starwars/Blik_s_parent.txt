A [[Duros]] individual<ref name="TOTU 104" /> was the '''parent''' of [[Blik]] and the partner of [[Blik's mother|its mother]].<ref name="TOTU 104 AD" /> Between c. [[62 BBY]] and [[33 BBY]],<ref>{{TalesDate|U4}}</ref> the trio was strolling the streets of a [[Unidentified city (Cad Bane)|bustling city]] when Blik attempted to chat up the Duros<ref name="TOTU 104" /> [[Gender|boys]]<ref name="TOTU 104" /><ref>{{Databank|cad-bane|Cad Bane}}</ref> [[Cad Bane|Colby]] and [[Niro]]. When its [[parent]]s caught up, its mother told it off for talking to "street [[rat]]s," with the other parent shaking their [[head]]. As the trio walked away, Blik turned its head to take one last look at the boys.<ref name="TOTU 104" />

A [[Color|blue]]-skinned Duros with red [[eye]]s, Blik's parent was dressed in long [[clothing]] consisting of a dark-brown robe. Around the neck, the individual wore a wrapped scarf, and on their head, they sported a tall [[Hat|headdress]] that flared outward at the top. In addition, they carried a large shoulder bag while strolling city streets with their [[Youngling|offspring]] and its mother.<ref name="TOTU 104" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blik's parent made their [[canon]]ical debut in "[[The Good Life]]," an episode of the animated television miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Underworld]]'',<ref name="TOTU 104" /> which premiered on [[May 4]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|GkywNANueew|Tales of the Underworld &#124; Official Trailer &#124; Available May 4 on Disney+}}</ref> The character's relation to Blik was revealed in the episode's audio description.<ref name="TOTU 104 AD" />
