The '''Battle of Kashyyyk''', also known as the '''invasion of Kashyyyk''', '''defense of Kashyyyk''', or the '''Kashyyyk campaign''', was a legendary battle that acted as one of the final engagements of the [[Clone Wars]]. The battle was fought in [[19 BBY]] on the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]] as part of an [[Outer Rim Sieges|offensive]] launched by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. At that time, with the [[Galactic Republic]] at its full industrial capabilities by the third [[Standard year|year]] of the war against the Confederacy, the Separatists found themselves being driven back into the Outer Rim by the Republic. With Kashyyyk located at a strategic position along a junction of [[hyperspace route]]s and home to information about secret hyperspace routes, the Confederacy sought to claim control of the planet. However, as its capture would risk the war's prolongment, the Republic vowed to fend off the Confederacy and dispatched [[clone trooper]] forces under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] to the planet.

While the Confederacy fought to prevent the arrival of Republic reinforcements, with a [[blockade]] of [[Trade Federation]] [[warship]]s in [[orbit]], the Republic managed to ward a Separatist invasion [[fleet]] away from Kashyyyk. Despite being driven, the Confederacy had already deployed their [[Separatist Droid Army|droid armies]] to the surface, where they were commanded by the [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]] [[General]] [[Linwodo]] and sent against the [[city]] of [[Kachirho]] from the [[Wartaki Sea]] to engage the Republic's clone troopers. However, amid the engagements, [[Clone Commander]] [[Gree (clone trooper)|Gree]] and the [[Grand Army of the Republic|clone army]] received orders from [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], secretly the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]], to [[Death penalty|execute]] their [[Jedi General]]s in accordance with the [[Order 66]] directive. As a result, the Republic's forces were thrown into disarray as the clones fought to eliminate their Jedi commanders, which allowed the Confederacy's [[army]] to advance and prepare for an assault on Kachirho. Despite their attempts, the [[battle droid]]s were pushed back by the Republic, which had been reorganized under the leadership of Clone Commander [[Faie]], who led the Galactic Republic to their ultimate victory.

==Prelude==
===Kashyyyk's occupation===
Sometime around [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{BuildFalconCite|43|Guide to the Galaxy}} establishes that [[Kashyyyk]] was occupied by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] sometime before to the [[Battle of Kashyyyk]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the Battle of Kashyyyk to [[19 BBY]], the occupation of Kashyyyk must have taken place around 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the final [[Standard year|year]]<ref name="SWGA" /> of the [[Clone Wars]] fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the confederate government coordinated an invasion of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Kashyyyk]]. During their [[Occupation of Kashyyyk (Clone Wars)|occupation]], various permanent [[Slavery|enslavement]] camps were established on the planet by Confederacy-aligned members of the [[Trandoshan]] [[species]] for Kashyyyk's [[Homeworld|native]] [[Wookiee]] race. In the occupation, the planet became subject to a number of incursion and probing attacks carried out by the Confederacy.<ref name="BMF 43" />

In addition, in the war's final year, the Confederacy had launched a massive [[Outer Rim Sieges|offensive]] across the [[Outer Rim Territories]] under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander]] [[General]] [[Grievous]]. Despite the Republic's [[Republic Military|military forces]] being pushed to the brink,<ref name="Old Friends">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref> the offensive had eventually grown attritional for the Confederacy, with an increase in military spending and requisition bills, and the harnessing of the government's peak industrial power for the war, which led to the confederate government's seeming entrapment as the Republic's victory appeared inevitable. As a result, the Republic was able to force the Confederacy from the [[Core Worlds]] and [[Colonies]] into the Mid Rim and Outer Rim regions.<ref name="Battles" />

===Invading Kashyyyk===
====An attractive target====
During the Outer Rim Sieges, Kashyyyk had become an attractive target for the Confederacy as a result of its strategic position along a junction of four well-traveled [[hyperspace]] routes and its location on the doorstep of the [[Foundry of the Confederacy]], a region of Separatist [[factory world]]s that had become targeted by the Republic's [[12th Sector Army]] in the offensive.<ref name="Battles" /> Alongside its housing of an important [[HoloNet]] relay station,<ref name="Complete Locations" /> the confederate government also sought to capture the planet in order to gain access to the [[data vault]]s of the ancient [[Claatuvac Guild]] of Wookiee [[cartographer]]s and [[navigator]]s located in the [[Kachirho]] [[tree]]-[[city]], which contained information regarding secret hyperspace lanes blazed by the guild's [[scout]]s.<ref name="Battles" /> Earlier, the Wookiees had first attempted to remain neutral in the conflict despite being members of the Republic, but due to the importance of the planet, both sides attempted to negotiate with the royal families of Kashyyyk, trying to convince them to join their cause.<ref name="Spanish HC 27">[[Star Wars Helmet Collection 27|''Star Wars Helmet Collection'': Commander Gree]] {{C|Spanish version}}</ref>

The Claatuvac Guild Archives interested [[General]] [[Grievous]] because it contained many unknown space roads.<ref name="The End of the Clone Wars">{{EncyclopediaCite|The End of the Clone Wars}}</ref> Growing impatient, Grievous directed a large-scale attack on the Wookiee homeworld, believing that the threat would persuade the inhabitants into accepting the conditions posed by Separatist [[Head of State|leader]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]], and his allies. The decision, however, proved to a dire mistake for the Confederacy.<ref name="Spanish HC 27" /> The failed attack on Kachirho's refinery began with an invasion of the planet<ref name="The End of the Clone Wars" /> following their occupation<ref name="BMF 43" /> in an effort to reverse their declining fortunes in the war with help from the Claatuvac Guild's information.<ref name="Complete Locations" /> The Confederacy's deployment of a secret force of [[battle droid]]s<ref name="BMF 43" /> and [[warship]]s had strewn Kashyyyk's night sky with a harsh light as they arrived, which became visible to the Wookiee natives on the surface.<ref name="CVD" /> At the same time, [[Tarfful]] was [[Rescue of Tarfful|captured]] by the Trandoshans on an independent mission.<ref name="The End of the Clone Wars" />

====Arrival of Yoda====
{{Quote|Go I will. Good relations with the Wookiees I have.|Yoda, in response to the attack|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:41stelite.png|thumb|left|300px|Jedi Master Luminara Unduli spent months preparing for the Confederacy's attack.]]
However, in the final [[Standard day|days]] of the war,<ref name="SWGA" /> as the Confederacy's capture of Kashyyyk and seizure of the hyperspace routes risked the prolongment of the war,<ref name="Battles" /> the Republic was unable to afford the loss of the [[Kashyyyk system]]<ref name="Episode III" /> and vowed to prevent its capture. Therefore, in response to the invasion, the Republic ordered the reassignment of [[Jedi General]] [[Quinlan Vos]] and his [[clone trooper]]s from their [[Siege of Boz Pity|engagements]] on the planet [[Boz Pity]] to reinforce [[Jedi]] General [[Luminara Unduli]] and her [[41st Elite Corps]] on Kashyyyk.<ref name="Battles" /> Prior to Vos's arrival, Unduli had already spent<ref name="Complete Locations" /> several [[Standard month|months]] on the planet in preparation and consultation with the [[Wookiee warrior]]s, while also establishing a [[staging area]] for Republic [[walker]]s and [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut]].<ref name="Battles" />

Several [[Standard day|days]] following the Republic's [[Battle of Coruscant|decisive victory]] over Grievous's assault on<ref name="Episode III" /> their [[capital]] world<ref name="SWGA" /> of [[Coruscant]], the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Jedi High Council|leading council]] learned of [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]] General [[Linwodo]]'s coming attack on Kachirho. From there, [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] was dispatched to reinforce the Wookiee homeworld due to his friendly relations with the natives,<ref name="Episode III" /> who was titled the "[[Defender of the Home Tree]]" for his prevention of several Separatist [[Attacks on Kachirho|attacks]] on the city<ref name="Battles" /> in his [[defense of Kashyyyk]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> As Yoda arrived with a [[battalion]] of [[clone trooper]]s and a single [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]],<ref name="Episode III" /> the Separatist general studied the battlefield records of Generals Unduli and Vos prior to deploying his droid forces, and had grown confident in his ability to overwhelm the two Jedi as a result.<ref name="Battles" /> Also present on world was [[Tsui Choi]], an [[Aleena]] [[Jedi]].<ref name="Ratts jedi" />

==The battle==
===Confederate assault===
{{Quote|The droids have started up their main power generators.''"<br />"''Then now, the time is, Commander.''"<br />"''Yes, sir.|Commander Gree and Yoda|audio=Linwodo.flac|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:BoK-ROTS.png|thumb|right|250px|The clones defended Kashyyyk against an army of Separatist battle droids.]]
The Separatist Droid forces targeted the [[Kashyyyk oil refinery|planet's oil refinery]].<ref name="SWGA" /> While [[Yoda's Republic taskforce]] drove the Separatist invasion fleet away, droids occupied the [[Wawaatt Archipelago]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> In addition to the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], the [[Wookiee Army]] was present in Kachirho, as Wookiees from across the planet arrived to defend the city.<ref name="SWCT Wookiee Army">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Wookiee Army - Galactic Republic}}</ref> After clone troopers and [[Wookiee warrior]]s set up a perimeter on the [[beach]] to confront the enemy, the Droid Army mobilized and traveled across<ref name="Episode III" /> the [[Wartaki Sea]]<ref name="Battles" /> to reach Kachirho.<ref name="Episode III" />

As clones and Wookiees fired from the beach, other Wookiee [[Soldier|warriors]] swung from the cliffs along the archipelago to place [[mine]]s on the [[NR-N99 Persuader-class droid enforcer|NR-N99 enforcer tanks]]. Meanwhile, [[clone scout trooper]]s fired from trenches and along the branches of [[Tree Kachirho]]. On the vehicle front, Wookiee [[Oevvaor jet catamaran|Oevvaor Jet Catamarans]] were aided by [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut]]s, [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport|AT-RTs]], [[All Terrain Attack Pod|AT-APs]], and other assault vehicles. They faced enormous opposition from Separatist NR-N99 [[droid tank]]s, [[DSD1 dwarf spider droid|Dwarf Spider Droids]], and [[HMP droid gunship]]s. Atop the seawall were two concussion [[turret]]s and multiple clone troopers. [[Red Company]] and [[Unidentified company|another company]] were among the 41st units involved in the battle.<ref name="Episode III" /> The Republic and Wookiee forces ultimately held the beach against the air and sea attacking Separatist forces.<ref name="SWCT Wookiee Army" />

===Order 66===
{{Quote|The time has come. Execute Order 66.''"<br />"''It will be done, my lord.|Darth Sidious, to Gree|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:GreeAndJekAreDead-CGSWG.png|thumb|left|250px|Jedi General Yoda survived the betrayal of his clone troopers, who tried to kill him under Order 66.]]
As the battle raged on, Yoda felt a great [[disturbance in the Force]] as [[Anakin Skywalker]] was converted to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] and became Darth Vader, followed by an even stronger disturbance as [[Order 66]], which was carried out throughout [[the galaxy]], had begun. [[Clone Commander]] [[Gree (clone trooper)|Gree]] received a call on his [[comlink]], in which [[Darth Sidious]] instructed him to execute the order.<ref name="Episode III" /> [[Clone Captain]] [[Jek]] of the [[Ranger Corps]]<ref name="Battles" /> joined Gree in the attempt to [[assassin]]ate Yoda. As the clones prepared to execute him, the Grand Master drew his [[Yoda's lightsaber|lightsaber]], sensing what was about to happen, and in one fell swoop decapitated his would-be assassins. Yoda then proceeded to escape into the nearby [[jungle]] along with the still loyal Wookiee warriors Tarfful and [[Chewbacca]] as the battle raged below.<ref name="Episode III" /> The confusion that followed Order 66 allowed the Separatist military to breach the line, but they were beaten back again.<ref name="SWCT Wookiee Army" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Goodbye, Tarfful. Goodbye, Chewbacca. Miss you, I will.|Yoda|audio=Yoda-MissYouIWill.flac|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:Wookieegoodbye.png|thumb|right|250px|Yoda escaped from Kashyyyk with the help of his Wookiee allies.]]
The Battle of Kashyyyk was remembered as a legendary event.<ref name="HTGree">{{HotToysCite|set=Movie Masterpiece Series|packtype=Sixth Scale Figure|pack=#877: Clone Commander Gree|link=collectibles/star-wars-clone-commander-gree-hot-toys-915591}}</ref> As the [[Galactic Empire]] rose, Yoda and the Wookiees would proceed to escape into the nearby [[mountain]]s. Later that evening, Chewbacca and Tarfful brought Yoda to an [[escape pod]]. After exchanging farewells, Yoda left the planet in the pod. Yoda managed to meet up with [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], having also escaped Order 66, and the two infiltrated the [[Jedi temple]] to reverse the [[Jedi beacon|message]] being sent to [[Jedi]], warning them to stay away. He then [[Duel in the Galactic Senate|attempted]] to kill Palpatine, but failed, and [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] himself to [[Dagobah]].<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:ClonesVsWookiees-TSotW.png|thumb|left|300px|Wookiees clashed with their former clone allies as the dawn and first days of the Imperial Era emerged.]]
Though the clone troopers won the day in the Battle of Kashyyyk, they would soon take the place of the Separatists as an invading force, except they would be successful in conquering the world<ref name="rebelsvisual">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> in the so-called [[pacification of Kashyyyk]]<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> launched by the Galactic Empire,<ref name="Ghosts of Kashyyyk 1">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=27|story=Ghosts of Kashyyyk, Part 1}}</ref> the [[Proclamation of the New Order|successor]] state of the Republic. With the Imperials besieging Kashyyyk,<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref> the Wookiees were ultimately unable to hold back<ref name="rebelsvisual" /> the "pacification" operation that ushered in an [[Occupation of Kashyyyk|Imperial occupation]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> The Empire also legalized slavery, leading many, the Empire itself in particular, to take Wookiee slaves due to their immense strength<ref name="rebelsvisual" /> by suggestion of Trandoshan slavers, who were given command over slaving raids across the planet.<ref name="BMF 43" />

Chewbacca was one of the slaves taken by the [[New Order]] from Kashyyyk, and suffered many [[Standard year|years]] as one, until he was freed by a<ref name="Absolutely Everything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> defecting [[Imperial Army trooper|Imperial soldier]] named [[Han Solo]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> Master Unduli was captured by the Empire and taken to the [[Spire (Stygeon Prime)|Spire]] on [[Stygeon Prime]]. There, she was executed by the [[Grand Inquisitor|Grand]] [[The Grand Inquisitor|Inquisitor]]<ref name="Dave Filoni Luminara">{{WebCite|url=https://www.ign.com/articles/2014/10/28/star-wars-rebels-dave-filoni-on-the-luminara-twist-in-rise-of-the-old-masters|text=''Star Wars Rebels'': Dave Filoni on the Luminara Twist in "Rise of the Old Masters"|archivedate=20200509105550}}</ref> and her remains were kept there to lure other Jedi survivors.<ref name="Rise of the Old Masters">{{Rebels|Rise of the Old Masters}}</ref> Master Vos also survived Order 66.<ref>{{GentleGiantCite|set=Mini Busts|pack=Commander Faie|altlink=http://www.the3dstudio.com/premier-guild/premier-guild-membership/commander-faie-deluxe-mini-bust-2015-pgm-gift}}</ref> He escaped from Kashyyyk after he killed Commander [[Faie]] and escaped with his lover, [[Khaleen Hentz]].<ref name="Card-Topps-Quinlan Vos">{{Topps|set=2019 Topps Star Wars Masterwork|cardname=Quinlan Vos}}</ref>

In regards to the Separatists, the remains of the [[B1-series battle droid]]s who fought in the battle were left behind to rust and were still laying on Kashyyyk by [[14 BBY]].<ref name="Fallen Order" />
