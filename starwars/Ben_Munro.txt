'''Ben Munro''' is an art director who worked within the art department of [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="TLJ" /> He has a small cameo in the film, appearing as a [[Resistance]] member in the [[Crait outpost]]. On-screen for only a few moments, Munro was one of several crew members to appear as an extra in the control room set. Director [[Rian Johnson]] later identified him in the film's audio commentary.<ref name="TLJ commentary">{{Film|VIII}} audio commentary</ref>
