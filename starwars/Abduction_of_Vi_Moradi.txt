In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]] was abducted by [[First Order]] forces who identified her role in the Resistance. Interrogated by the [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Captain]] [[Archex|Cardinal]], his goal soon shifted from gathering intelligence on the Resistance to learning about the past of Captain [[Phasma]] in an attempt to find a scapegoat to depose her. As a result, Moradi recounted the [[mission to Parnassos]] to Cardinal. During the interrogation, Cardinal learned of Phasma's involvement in the [[death]] of [[Brendol Hux]], alongside the latter's son, [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]]. As a result, Cardinal attempted to assassinate Phasma out of revenge for his mentor's death, but was overpowered and nearly killed himself. Moradi then escaped, taking Cardinal with her.<ref name="Phasma" />
