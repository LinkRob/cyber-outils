'''Astaroth''' was a monstrous creature from an unknown [[Planet/Legends|world]].

{{Char-stub}}

==Biography==
Astaroth appeared on a [[Unidentified volcanic planet|volcanic planet]] in one of [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda's]] [[Force vision/Legends|vision]], approaching the sword [[Soul Edge]]. He later faced off against Yoda on a [[Unidentified planet in the Cowl Crucible|mysterious planet]] wielding a giant [[Axe/Legends|axe]].

==Personality and traits==
Astaroth was a monstrous sight to behold; he was a hulking, golem-like figure with purple armor and red cracks spreading across his body from a crimson jewel in the center of his chest.

==Behind the scenes==
Astaroth was pitted against Yoda as an introduction for Yoda's appearance in the fighting game ''[[Soulcalibur IV]]''.
