The '''GC-8''' was a model of [[droid]]<ref name="CGttSWG" /> with relatively [[humanoid]] features. [[Cator]] was a GC-8 droid who served as a [[librarian]] and [[Security droid|guardian-protector]] within the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] [[Jedi Archives|Archives]], protecting the [[Jedi Order]]'s most sensitive information until he was destroyed by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]<ref name="Dying Light II" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Darth Vader (2017) 9|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 9]] including the [[Death|destruction]] of Cator on [[Coruscant]], to [[19 BBY]].</ref>
