'''Batuuan Beef and Crispy Topato Stir-fry''' was a [[Foodstuff|beef]] and [[topato]] dish served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> It was [[Diet|consumable]] by [[human]]s.<ref name="Docking Bay 7" />

==Behind the scenes==
Batuuan Beef and Crispy Topato Stir-fry was an entrée available at Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]].<ref name="DHS Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=dining/hollywood-studios/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch Menu}}</ref> It was not available at the restaurant's [[Disneyland]] location.<ref name="DLP Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch And Dinner Menu}}</ref> The dish was released as part of a [[2021]] menu refresh at Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo.<ref name="FoodBlog">{{WebCite|url=https://www.disneyfoodblog.com/2021/07/06/7-new-eats-are-coming-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge-in-disney-world|text=7 NEW Eats Are Coming to Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge in Disney World|work=[https://www.disneyfoodblog.com Disney Food Blog]|archivedate=20231003143225|author=Kelly Oliveros|date=20221-07-06}}</ref> The menu described the dish as "Smoky [[Wikipedia:Braising|Braised]] [[Wikipedia:Beef|Beef]] glazed in [[Wikipedia:Tamarind|Tamarind]] [[Sauce]]. Served with Stir Fried [[Mushroom]]s and [[Vegetable]]s, Crispy Herb [[Wikipedia:Yucca|Yucca]], [[Wikipedia:Pickling|Pickled]] [[Onion]]s, and [[Wikipedia:Coriander|Cilantro]]."<ref name="DHS Menu" />

On [[May 1]], [[2026]], the dish was removed from the Docking Bay 7 menu, with another beef dish, [[Shaak Pot Roast]] with [[Kublag Mash]] taking its place.<ref name="DHS Menu" /><ref name="DPB26">{{DPB|url=disney-experiences/disney-eats-may-the-4th-foodie-guide|text=Disney Eats: May the 4th Foodie Guide 2026}}</ref>
