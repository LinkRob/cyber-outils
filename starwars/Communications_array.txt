A '''communications array''' was a device that could be found on certain [[starship]]s as part of their communications systems. [[Trade Federation]] [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleships]] had several communications arrays on the aft section of the ship, above the engines.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> [[Kuat Drive Yards]]' [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]]s had a primary communications array located on the top part of the ships, on their port side.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>
