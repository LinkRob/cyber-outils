"'''Deadly Droid'''" is a short comic story published in [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic 6.31|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic'' 6.31]].

==Publisher's summary==
''A crucial meeting of the Galactic Senate draws dignitaries from all over the Republic&mdash;as well as those who would destroy them&hellip; A deadly droid threatens to disrupt the Council and damage the war effort in the new Star Wars: The Clone Wars comic strip adventure.''
