A '''black market''' was an illegal means of dealing goods and services, outside of the eyes of the government. Despite the best efforts of [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement agencies]] and [[Clone trooper|clone]] interdiction forces, black markets prospered throughout the [[Clone Wars]]. To counter the appeal of black markets, the government issued [[propaganda]] pieces such as "[[Black Market (propaganda)|Black Market]]" stating that helping oneself in that way was helping the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|enemy]], and hurting the war effort.<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref>

Toward the end of the [[High Republic Era]], corrupt [[Doctor|surgeons]] in the [[Begamor underworld|underworld]] of [[Begamor]] sought fresh organs to trade in the black market.<ref name="Vow">''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''</ref>

During the Clone Wars, [[Prime Minister]] [[Almec]] [[Mandalorian black market conspiracy|established]] a black market on [[Mandalore]] to provide needed goods after the [[planet]] was cut off from [[Galactic Republic]] aid.<ref name="Corruption">{{TCW|Corruption (episode)}}</ref> However, the creation of the market also granted Almec increased power and wealth, all while the humanitarian supplies never made it to the people.<ref name="TA">{{TCW|The Academy}}</ref>

After her [[Asajj Ventress's lightsabers|previous]] [[lightsaber]]s were stolen by [[Padawan]] [[Barriss Offee]], [[Asajj Ventress]] acquired a [[Asajj Ventress's lightsaber|replacement]] on the black market. She stated that although some thought only [[Jedi]] carried them, they were easy enough to acquire at the black market.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>

[[Ezra Bridger]] once stole several items from the [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]] of [[Baron of the Empire|Baron]] [[Valen Rudor]] to sell on [[Lothal]]'s black market.<ref name="Ezra's Gamble">''[[Ezra's Gamble]]''</ref>

[[Garazeb Orrelios]] accidentally uncovered the criminal [[Skraik's gang]] operating out of the sewers in Garel City, when one of its members, [[Lister]], stole a [[capacitor]] relay that he was installing on the [[starship]] ''[[Ghost]]''. The gang stole ship parts and sold them on Garel's black market.<ref>{{RebelsMagCite|29|The Size of the Fight}}</ref>

[[Bix Caleen]] had connections in the black market where she used a junction box to contact a [[Luthen Rael|buyer]] for [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor's]] stolen [[Imperial N-S9 Starpath Unit]].<ref name="Episode 1">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref>

[[Delphi Kloda]] taught his [[Teacher|pupil]] [[Bazine Netal]] that every [[planet]] had a black market if one knew where to look.<ref name="perfect" />

The [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Norath Kev]] was assigned to track the [[First Order]] moving their shipments and deals through black market channels until [[Lechee]], a First Order collaborator, sold him out to the [[bounty hunter]] [[Ax Tagrin]].<ref name="Missing Agent">{{Resistance|The Missing Agent}}</ref>
