An '''ecumenopolis''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Chapter 19" /> or [[moon]]<ref name="Star Wars 8">[[Star Wars (2015) 8|''Star Wars'' (2015) 8]]</ref> entirely encompassed by a single [[city]].<ref name="Chapter 19" /> Due to their lack of exploitable ground, such world-spanning cities were dependent on imports for [[Foodstuff|food]] and raw materials.<ref name="Coruscant">{{Databank|coruscant|Coruscant}}</ref> The [[bounty hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] found working on an ecumenopolis to be difficult as both the [[hunter]] and their [[bounty]] could easily hide away.<ref name="SWHunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>

There were only a handful of ecumenopolis in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Chapter 19" /> Many of them, such as the galactic [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]],<ref name="Coruscant" /> as well as [[Denon]]<ref name="HttJ">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref> and [[Eufornis Major]],<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks">''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]''</ref> were situated in the [[Core Worlds]]; others were in the [[Deep Core]], such as [[Troithe]],<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> and [[Empress Teta]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> City-worlds in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] included [[Botajef]],<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> [[Nar Shaddaa]],<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> [[Taris]],<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> [[Uchinao]],<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> and [[Wind]].<ref name="Vow">''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''</ref> The ecumenopolis planet [[Eyo-Dajuritz]] was considered to be fossilized by the time of the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The first ecumenopolis seen on-screen within ''Star Wars'' was Coruscant in ''[[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition]]'', released in [[1997]].<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> The term made its first ''Star Wars'' appearance in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] novel ''[[Coruscant Nights I: Jedi Twilight]]'', in [[2008]].<ref name="Jedi Twilight">''[[Coruscant Nights I: Jedi Twilight]]''</ref> In ''Star Wars'' [[canon]], the term was first used in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Heir to the Jedi]]'', written by [[Kevin Hearne]].<ref name="HttJ" />
