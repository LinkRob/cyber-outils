The '''''Foundling''''' was a [[Pathfinder ship]] used by [[Outer Rim Expedition Two-Five-Two]], a [[Republic Pathfinder]] team led by [[Jedi Master]] [[Rok Buran]]. After the team staged an [[Expedition to Gloam|expedition]] to the ravaged [[planet]] [[Gloam]], they were betrayed by the former [[miner]] [[Rillik]], who led the team's members into a trap which only Buran survived, before destroying the [[starship]] to prevent anyone from escaping.<ref name="QFTHC" />
