The '''disputed wilderness zone''' was an area on the [[swamp]] [[planet]] [[Mimban]] where [[Mimban Campaign|combat]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Mimbanese Liberation Army]] was the fiercest. [[Imperial Forward Field Operations]], the temporary Imperial headquarters, was located deep inside the zone.<ref name="Official Guide" />
