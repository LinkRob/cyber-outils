'''Deckard''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served in [[Cardiff Baye's battalion|a battalion]] under [[Clone Commander]] [[Maximus (Clone Commander)|Maximus]] and [[Jedi General]] [[Cardiff Baye]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Deckard participated in the [[battle on Covenanon]] and the [[Invasion of Katou|mission to]] [[Katou]]. Like their team members, Deckard wore [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] adorned with [[Color|light red]] markings.<ref name="HS:Grievous" />

==Behind the scenes==
Deckard first appeared in the [[2026]] graphic novel ''[[Hyperspace Stories: Grievous]]'', written by [[Michael Moreci]] and drawn by [[Caio Filipe]]. Concept art of the clone characters was also drawn by Filipe.<ref name="HS:Grievous" /> The character was first revealed in panels of the comic shown during the [[Wikipedia:San Diego Comic Con|San Diego Comic Con]] in July [[2025]].<ref name="SDCC 2025">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-san-diego-comic-con-lucasfilm-publishing-panel-2025|text=SDCC 2025: Highlights from the Lucasfilm Publishing Panel}}</ref>
