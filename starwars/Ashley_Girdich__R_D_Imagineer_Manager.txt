"'''Ashley Girdich: R&D Imagineer Manager'''" was the forty-third episode of the [[Disney+]] documentary series [[Wikipedia:One Day at Disney Shorts|''One Day at Disney (Shorts)'']], which included behind-the-scenes looks at the jobs of various [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] employees. The episode featured [[Walt Disney Imagineering]] Research and Development Imagineer Manager [[Ashley Girdich]] and the experimental ''[[Star Wars]]'' character [[J4-K3]] (a.k.a. Jake), a [[droid]] designed by Imagineering R&D to be autonomous and who was play-tested at [[Disneyland]] for a short time. 

==Official description==
===Disney+ press release===
''Project manager Ashley Girdich oversees dynamic assignments for Walt Disney Imagineering's Research and Development group. From managing teams to keeping projects and plans on track, Ashley is constantly pushing boundaries to ensure the next generation of Imagineers continue to create industry-leading, interactive experiences for park guests.''<ref name="D+ />
