A '''base''' was established on the [[planet]] [[Christophsis]] during the [[Clone Wars]] by the [[Galactic Republic]], which used it<ref name="Hidden Enemy" /> as its main base of operations<ref name="TBotCW">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Clone Wars Begin}}</ref> for a [[Battle of Christophsis|battle]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The base was large and had several indoor spaces for Republic [[clone trooper]]s, including a mess hall, barracks for individual squads, and other rooms with [[holoprojector]]s and communications equipment. Outside, the base had a large staging area with numerous vehicles, including [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer|AT-TE walkers]] and [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]], several of which were destroyed when the treasonous [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Slick]] planted explosives while attempting to escape capture.<ref name="Hidden Enemy" />
