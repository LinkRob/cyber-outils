'''A Friendly Face''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] adventure ''[[Below the Belt: Philanthropy]]''. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[April 30]] [[2002]] and took the form of an interview by [[Yara Grugara]] with the crimelord [[Nirama]].
