The [[planet]] [[Batonn]]'s '''main continent''' was the location of [[Paeragosto City]] and the [[Creekpath Mining and Refining complex]]. [[Scrim Island]] laid three hundred [[kilometer]]s to the west of Batonn's main continent. During the [[Imperial Era]], Batonn's main continent harbored a [[Batonn insurgents|rebel insurgency]] led by the insurgent leader [[Nevil Cygni|Nightswan]]. The continent was also the site of the [[Siege of the Creekpath Mining and Refining complex]], which ended both the [[Batonn insurgency]] and the [[Batonn sector insurgency]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s favor.<ref name="Thrawn" />
