'''Daring Droid''' was an [[astromech droid]] found in [[Exegol]] space that was aligned with the [[Resistance]]. As the [[First Order]] was pinning down the Resistance, this [[droid]], along with a [[protocol droid]], went out to rescue [[soldier]]s nearby. After the droid saved Resistance [[pilot]]s, the droid disappeared and couldn't be found.<ref name="LEGO" />
