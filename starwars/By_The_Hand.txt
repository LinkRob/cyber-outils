''''By The Hand'''' was the identification of a type of [[tattoo]] worn on an individual's right [[Limb|forearm]]. The tattoo was a stylized drawing of a five-fingered hand. [[Arvel Skeen]], a member of the [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team]], had a 'By The Hand' tattoo, which was correctly identified by [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], who was working with the team as a [[mercenary]] under the name Clem. Andor claimed to have become familiar with the tattoo's meaning while he was at a youth center on [[Sipo]].<ref name="Axe" />
