'''Barry Gingell''' worked as a graphic designer on the television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.<ref name="Andor1">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> In addition, the [[hologram]] of [[Anto Kreegyr]] in the episode "[[Daughter of Ferrix]]"<ref name="Daughter">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> was modeled on him.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|BazatronPrime|status/1593257884309106688|Barry Gingell|quote=For all the fat, bald guys that couldn't find a good star wars cosplay..... You're welcome. Thank you to Luke Hull and Tony Gilroy for making me an actual star wars character.|date=2022-11-17|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/zcN4k}}</ref> Gingell was dressed up by the show's costume designer and wore three layers of coats for the "part", before being scanned. His image was digitally altered in post production to make him look a little more heavyset, and his eyebrows were reduced.<ref name="Twitter2">{{Twitter|BazatronPrime|status/1593281126818357259|Barry Gingell|quote=Yeah buddy. It was a very cool present from my bosses. I was dressed by our fantastic costume designer and scanned by the digital guys. I think they reduced my eyebrows and padded me out a bit to be honest. But I was wearing three coats!|date=2022-11-17|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/d05i0}}</ref>
