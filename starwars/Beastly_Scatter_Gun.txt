The '''Beastly Scatter Gun''' was a [[scatter gun]] owned by the [[Trandoshan]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Charr]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Cunning Scatter Gun|standard slugthrower]]. It was designed to loosely resembled the large [[Species|creatures]] known as [[Reek]]s, including a real Reek [[Bone|horn]] attached to the barrel of the [[Slugthrower|gun]] as a trophy.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Beastly Scatter Gun was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Charr's Cunning Scatter Gun, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.18.0 during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Red Beast]].<ref name="Hunters" />
