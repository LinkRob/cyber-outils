During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]<ref name="HS 5" />&mdash;who was secretly the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Sidious<ref name="Ultimate SW New">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>&mdash;revealed [[Sith assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]]'s mission to the [[Moon of Staggec]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] to a group of [[clone trooper]]s, who then attempted to '''ambush''' the enemy [[commander]]. Ventress was on the [[moon]] [[Hunt for a Jedi artifact|searching]] for a [[Unidentified Jedi artifact|Jedi artifact]] for her master, [[Count]] [[Dooku]], but found it was no longer stored in the abandoned [[Jedi Temple (Moon of Staggec)|Staggec Jedi Temple]]. Outside, the [[Cloning|clones]] ambushed Ventress, but she quickly cut them down. Ventress then contacted Dooku to accuse him of leaking her location, although she dropped the theory quickly when Dooku threatened her. She then [[Mission to Naboo (Asajj Ventress)|followed the trail]] of the artifact to<ref name="HS 5" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Ultimate SW New" /> [[Naboo]].<ref name="HS 5" />
