'''Chan Harra''' was a [[Gender|female]] individual who served as an [[Imperial cadet]] at the [[Arkanis Academy]]. Tall and [[Color|pale]], she had an interest in joining the academy's secretive group of cadets known as the [[Commandant's Cadets]], but had never received an invitation. Harra was one of the first cadets at the academy met by [[Zare Leonis]], who had secretly infiltrated the academy to rescue [[Dhara Leonis|his sister]]. He did not recognize the name of her [[homeworld]]. Leonis later served in a training mission with her, as well as [[Xan Lanier]], [[Tigard Manes]], and [[Anya Razar]]. Razar described Harra as a "rock," someone that Leonis could rely upon.<ref name="TSA" />
