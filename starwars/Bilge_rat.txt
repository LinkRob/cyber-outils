'''Bilge rats'''<ref name="SC2 AD">{{SkeletonCrew|Way, Way Out Past the Barrier}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> were a type of [[rat]]<ref name="SC 105 AD">{{SkeletonCrew|You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> and [[rodent]] that existed in [[the galaxy]]. A bilge rat named [[Snowball]] nested within the [[droid]] [[SM-33]]<ref name="SC2" /> and damaged<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|snowball|Snowball}}</ref> his<ref name="SC2" /> [[memory circuit]]s.<ref name="Databank" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bilge rats first appeared simultaneously in "[[This Could Be a Real Adventure]]"<ref name="SC1">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}}</ref> and "[[Way, Way Out Past the Barrier]],"<ref name="SC2" /> the first and second episodes of the live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'', which aired on [[December 2]], [[2024]].<ref name="Early">{{SW|url=news/skeleton-crew-premiere-date|text=''Star Wars: Skeleton Crew'' Will Debut Early|date=2024-11-26}}</ref> The species was named in the English audio description of the series.<ref name="SC2 AD" />
