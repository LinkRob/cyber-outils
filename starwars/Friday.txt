'''Friday''',<ref name="RJ" /> sometimes shortened to '''Fri''',<ref name="Deception">{{TCW|Deception}}</ref> was a [[Standard day|day]] of the [[Standard week|week]].<ref name="RJ">''[[Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger]]''</ref>

A [[Painting|poster]] in the [[Bilbousa Bazaar]] showed a a silhouette of a [[Gender|feminine]] looking [[Twi'lek]] doing a pole dance, with a [[Huttese (alternative writing system)|Huttese]] caption reading "Dancers every FRIDAY [[Standard day|NIGHT]]!!"<ref name="Friends">{{TCW|Friends and Enemies}}</ref> The Twi'lek poster also appeared on the door leading out of the [[Refresher|latrine]] at the [[79's]] cantina on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Friday was mentioned in the [[2022]] [[Canon|non-canon]] video game ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', in which [[Yarael Poof]] brings up his anxieties about casual Friday should the player refuse to help him switch places with his [[Kaminoan]] counterpart.<ref name="LSTSS">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''</ref>

===Non-canon history===
Friday was observed on [[Kamino]]. Some Fridays were designated as "casual," meaning that individuals were expected to dress in more comfortable [[clothing]]. During a misadventure in [[Tipoca City]] after having been mistaken for the Kaminoan data manager [[Rala De]], [[Jedi Master]] Yarael Poof worried that his [[Jedi apparel|Jedi robes]] would be considered inappropriate attire for an upcoming casual Friday.<ref name="LSTSS" />
