The '''Bear Clan''', also known as the '''Mighty Bear Clan''', was a class of [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] that began their [[Jedi training|training]] in the ways of [[the Force]] under the tutelage of [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]]. Prior to the [[First Battle of Geonosis|start]] of the [[Clone Wars]], Yoda requested the clan's assistance in [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'s search for the lost [[planet]] of [[Kamino]].

==History==
{{Quote|Gravity silhouette remains, but the star and all the planets&hellip;''disappeared'' they have. How can this be? A thought? Anyone.''"<br />"''Master? Because someone erased it from the Archive memory.|Yoda and J. K. Burtola|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
The Bear Clan was a class of the [[Jedi Order]] that consisted of [[Jedi Initiate|younglings]]<ref name="Bear Clan" />&mdash;[[Youngling|children]] between the ages of four and eight who were divided into ten clans of up to twenty students.<ref name="Jedi Youngling">{{Databank|jedi-youngling|Jedi Youngling}}</ref> The [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]], the [[capital]] [[Planet|world]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], served as both their home and school,<ref name="Episode II" /> where the younglings received their first lessons in the [[Jedi training|Jedi arts]] before becoming [[Padawan]]s to the more experienced members of the Order, such as [[Jedi Knight|Knights]] and [[Jedi Master|Masters]].<ref name="Jedi Youngling" /> Like all [[Jedi]]<ref name="Voices">{{TCW|Voices}}</ref> with the exception of [[Anakin Skywalker]],<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> the Bear Clan was initially instructed in the art of [[lightsaber combat]]<ref name="Bear Clan" /> and the ways of [[the Force]] by [[Yoda]],<ref name="Episode II" /> the Jedi [[Grand Master]] and a leading member of the [[Jedi High Council|High Council]].<ref name="Yoda">{{Databank|yoda|Yoda}}</ref>

[[File:JediYoungling.png|thumb|left|250px|The Bear Clan received their first lessons in lightsaber combat from Grand Master Yoda.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Episode II" /> the Jedi Knight [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] interrupted the Bear Clan's [[lightsaber]] training session with Yoda,<ref name="Bear Clan" /> whose wisdom he sought in order to discover the reason behind the lack of information on the planet [[Kamino]] in the [[Jedi Archives]]. The Grand Master discussed the case in front of the younglings, having decided to include the Bear Clan in their conversation as observers and potential participants. After analyzing an incomplete [[star]] map, the younglings were encouraged by Yoda to share their insight in the matter, to which [[J. K. Burtola]] revealed his conclusion&mdash;the location of Kamino had been erased from the Archives' memory banks. Marveling at the imaginative mind of a child, Yoda agreed with the youngling's deduction, although he was also disturbed by it, as only a member of the Jedi Order could tamper with their records.<ref name="Episode II" />

Shortly after the [[Battle of Fortress Vader]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] entered a [[portal]] constructed by [[Darth Momin]] to resurrect his wife [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. While walking through visions, he saw Yoda training the Bear Clan at the same moment Kenobi arrived before as the words of "Focus, young Skywalker" resounded in his mind.<ref name="Dark Lord 25">[[Darth Vader (2017) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 25]]</ref>
