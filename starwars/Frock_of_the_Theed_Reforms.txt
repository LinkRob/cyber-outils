The '''Frock of the Theed Reforms''' was a dress that [[New Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Thadlé Berenko]] wore<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|VII}} to [[34 ABY]].</ref>

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Frock of the Theed Reforms appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']],<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> which was released on [[December 18]], [[2015]].<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> It was first identified in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TFAVD" /> and released the same day as the film.<ref name="New Books">{{SW|url=news/a-galaxy-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens-books-coming-december-18-first-look|text=A Galaxy of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Books Coming December 18 - UPDATED!}}</ref>
