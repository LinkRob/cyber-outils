A '''battle''' was fought on the terrestrial astronomical object [[Ferrok Pax]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]], nicknamed Twilight Company, and a warrior group that had captured fellow rebel [[soldier]]s. Twilight Company lost many [[troop]]s trekking to the enemy. When the battle occurred, the company won, but with only thirty-seven remaining soldiers. One veteran of the battle, the [[Besalisk]] [[Gadren]], recounted the campaign to the tenative leader of Twilight Company [[Hazram Namir]] in [[3 ABY]].

==The battle==
{{Quote|Then came the battle. We won that day, against a laughing menace and her fiendish warriors, and we extracted the rebels we had been sent to save. But only thirty-seven Twilight soldiers survived the clash.|Gadren, to Hazram Namir|<ref name="Twilight" />}}
The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]], better known as Twilight [[Company]], was deployed to the terrestrial astronomical object [[Ferrok Pax]] to rescue rebel captives from a warrior faction,<ref name="Twilight" /> sometime by the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]].<ref name="1 BBY" /> Knowing that attacking the enemy from the air would ruin their chances of a surprise, the [[soldier]]s of Twilight Company, numbering at barely two-hundred, trekked through the ruins on the surface for several [[Standard day|days]] in order to flank the enemy. As supplies dwindled, however, many [[troop]]s were [[Death|lost]] to hunger and weakness. Others vanished to the [[alien]] technology or were killed by beasts that lurked in the wilderness. When Twilight Company met the enemy warriors, they won the ensuing battle and rescued the captured rebels, though only thirty-seven survived the fight.<ref name="Twilight" />

==Aftermath==
After Ferrok Pax, [[Micha Evon]], the Twilight Company's founder and [[commanding officer]], rebuilt the company.<ref name="Twilight" /> [[Mon Mothma]], the leader of the Alliance, considered the battle of Ferrok Pax a very costly battle and published her opinion in the document "Short Notes on the History of the Rebel Alliance Navy" in her personal files. She noted the 61st's losses there as an example of why the logistics of using [[infantry]] would be very costly in lives and as context for the importance of having the [[Rebel Alliance Navy]].<ref name="Rogue One novel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref> The [[Besalisk]] [[Gadren]], a veteran of the battle, had recurring memories of his time on Ferrok Pax and told the story to [[Hazram Namir]], the tenative leader of Twilight Company while on the [[junker]] world [[Heap Nine]]<ref name="Twilight" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="3 ABY">{{BTCdate}}</ref> Gadren used the story as a point to say that Namir feared the sacrifices that his company had made during its ongoing campaign [[Operation Ringbreaker]] as well as the sacrifices that were to come, adding that Evon had used sacrifice as a strength to benefit the company and its goal.<ref name="Twilight" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle of Ferrok Pax was first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Twilight" />
