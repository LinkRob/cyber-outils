'''Firefighter droids''' or '''firefighting droids''' were [[droid]]s that were specifically designed to combat fires. [[Firefighter battle droid]]s and [[LC-24 fire droid]]s were types of firefighter droids. Another model had wheels and were also capable of flight.<ref name="Master and Apprentice" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Rising Storm Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> a group of firefighter droids attempted to extinguish wildfires.<ref name="Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> In [[230 BBY]]<ref name="Mission to Disaster date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> firefighting droids flew in an attempt to put out the flames of [[Port Haileap]].<ref name="Mission to Disaster">''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]''</ref> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Master and Apprentice date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> firefighting droids attempted to extinguish the fires of a warehouse outside the [[Pijal's capital city|capital city]] of [[Pijal]], caused by [[Halin Azucca]].<ref name="Master and Apprentice>''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref>
