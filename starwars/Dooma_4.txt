'''Dooma 4''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Crossing the Line" /> in the [[Dooma system]] in the [[Dentari sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that housed an [[Imperial Metal Factory]]. At some point, the [[Partisans]] led by [[Saw Gerrera]] undertook a [[Assault on Dooma 4|mission]] to the [[Factory|facility]] after learning that it provided components for a secret [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Project Stardust|project]].<ref name="Crossing the Line" />
