'''Flechette micro-rockets''' were a class of [[Wrist rocket|micro-rocket]] that were designed to destroy groups of [[infantry]]. The rockets typically sold for 175 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and were the most popular class of micro-rocket on the market. Flechette micro-rockets released a wave of sharp needles that could easily kill unarmored individuals.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
