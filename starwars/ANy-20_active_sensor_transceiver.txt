The '''ANy-20 active sensor transceiver''', also known as an '''ANy-20 scan-mode transceiver''', was a type of specialized [[starship]] [[sensor]] manufactured by [[Fabritech]]. It sent out long-range pulses to collect data from a wide region of [[Realspace|space]], notably allowing the operator to locate other ships beyond visual range. The [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]'s [[YT-1300 light freighter]] was equipped with one such device.<ref name="MF 3D">''[[Star Wars: Millennium Falcon: A 3D Owner's Guide]]''</ref>
