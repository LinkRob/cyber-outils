'''Ertegas''' was an [[agriworld]]<ref name="Value of Food" /> located in the [[Anoat sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It had at least one [[moon]], where the [[Galactic Empire]] exploited a [[crystal]] [[Mining|mine]].<ref name="Haunted Mine" /> Ertegas never held any interest to the [[Trade Spine League]] until the [[Iron Blockade]],<ref name="Value of Food" /> which was established by the Empire in an attempt to prevent news of their crushing defeat by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] at the [[Battle of Endor]] from reaching the Anoat [[sector]]'s populace,<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> caused the value of [[foodstuff]] to rise,<ref name="Value of Food" /> though the harvests were disrupted when malfunctioning [[droid]]s began threatening Ertegan civilians.<ref name="Droid Function">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Run: "Droid Function"</ref>

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
[[The Smuggler]] sent a member of their crew to negotiate a deal with Ertegan [[Merchant|traders]] on the League's behalf,<ref name="Value of Food" /> while also sending a pair to assist the locals with the droids in return for crystals as payment.<ref name="Droid Function" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ertegas was originally created for the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] web article "[[Galactic Gazetteer: Hoth and the Greater Javin]]," which was written by [[Jason Fry]], [[Daniel Wallace]], and [[Craig Robert Carey]] for [[Wizards of the Coast]] and released in [[2004]].<ref name="Hoth and the Greater Javin">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=starwars/article/sw20040325web|text=Galactic Gazetteer: Hoth and the Greater Javin|int=Galactic Gazetteer: Hoth and the Greater Javin}}</ref> It became [[canon]] when it was mentioned in the mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', which was developed by [[Kabam]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms and released in [[2015]].<ref name="Uprising" />
