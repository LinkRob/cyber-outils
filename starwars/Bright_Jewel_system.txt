The '''Bright Jewel system''',<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> also referred to as the '''Ord Mantell system''',<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> was a [[star system]] located in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim's]] [[Bright Jewel sector]]. It notably contained the [[planet]] of [[Ord Mantell]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and its [[moon]]s,<ref name="Son of Dathomir 2" /> as well as the astronomical object [[Phelbos]].<ref name="Vizsla Keep 09" /> The [[asteroid]] outpost [[Vizsla Keep 09]] was located in Phelbos' [[Planetary ring|rings]].<ref name="Location note" />
