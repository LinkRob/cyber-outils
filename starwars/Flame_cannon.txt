A '''flame cannon''' was a type of [[flamethrower]] used by the [[Trandoshan]] [[Commander]] [[Babwa Venomor]] on [[Kashyyyk]], as the Trandoshan [[hunter]]s and [[clone trooper]]s of his [[Trandoshan-Imperial convoy]] [[Fire|scorched]] the [[planet]]'s [[jungle]]s, while hunting [[Wookiee]]s.

==History==
During the [[Mission to Kashyyyk (Clone Force 99)|Mission to Kashyyyk]] between [[19 BBY]] and [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|206}}</ref> the [[clone trooper]]s of [[Clone Force 99]] and the [[Wookiee]]s of a [[Wookiee sanctuary|local village]] ambushed [[Trandoshan]] [[Commander]] [[Babwa Venomor]]'s [[Trandoshan-Imperial convoy]] of [[clone trooper]]s and Trandoshan [[hunter]]s. After both sides took several [[Death|casualties]], the Wookiee [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Gungi]] disabled an [[incinerator tank]] with [[Gungi's lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. In response, Venomor emerged from his own tank with a flame cannon. Aiming the flame cannon at the Jedi, he inadvertently set one of his own men on [[fire]], while firing the weapon at Gungi, but the Wookiee managed to avoid the flames and retreated into the [[forest]], drawing Venomor in alone after him. Venomor stalked Gungi through the forest with his flame cannon ready. Venomer set the foliage ablaze as he saw the Jedi dart past him. Soon after, the Trandoshan was surrounded by a circle of flames and Gungi leapt from a nearby [[tree]].<ref name="Tribe" /> Dodging the Jedi's [[lightsaber]], he continued to fire the [[flamethrower]], pushing the Wookiee back when Gungi slashed at him again.<ref name="Tribe AD">{{TBB|Tribe}} {{C|[[Disney+]] audio description}}</ref> After Venomor realized the slash had destroyed the flame cannon, he threw it and his satchel on the ground, preparing to fight with his [[Unarmed combat|claws]].<ref name="Tribe" />
