The '''Day of Ascendancy''' was a [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] holiday celebrating the coronation of a new [[Monarch of Naboo]]. It was held a few [[Standard day|days]] after the election, every two to four [[Standard year|years]] depending on whether a new monarch had been elected in the [[planet]]'s biannual elections or not. The [[Coronation of Queen Amidala|coronation]] of [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> and the [[Second coronation of Réillata|second coronation]] of [[Réillata]]<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref> were held on Days of Ascendancy in the years [[32 BBY]] and [[28 BBY]].<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the [[Invasion of Naboo]] to [[32 BBY]]. ''[[Queen's Peril]]'' establishes that [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] became [[Queen]] of [[Naboo]] [[Standard month|months]] before the invasion and in the same year, dating her [[Coronation of Queen Amidala|coronation]] to 32 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. ''[[Queen's Shadow]]'', which depicts [[Réillata]]'s [[Second coronation of Réillata|second coronation]], explicitly takes place four years after the invasion, which can be calculated as [[28 BBY]]</ref>
