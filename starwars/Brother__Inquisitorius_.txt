'''Brother''' was a title of the [[Inquisitorius]] that was given to [[Gender|male]] [[Inquisitor]]s, [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Dark side adept|agents]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]] to hunt down [[Jedi]] who survived the [[Great Jedi Purge|fall]] of the [[Jedi Order]]. The title was earned after an Inquisitor proved their worth to the Inquisitorius. The title did not refer to [[blood]] relation, but instead fostered a sense of unity amongst the Inquisitors.

==Description==
[[File:FifthBrotherDaiyu-SWOWK.png|thumb|left|270px|The Fifth Brother takes command of a force of stormtroopers to hunt Jedi in the name of the Galactic Empire.]]
The individuals who carried the title of Brother were part of the [[Inquisitorius]],<ref name="Rebels Recon ATTA" /> a group of [[The Force|Force]]-[[Force-sensitive|wielding]] warriors, trained in the ways of the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]], who were used by the [[Galactic Empire]] to [[Jedi hunter|hunt]] down and eliminate any [[Jedi]] who survived the [[Great Jedi Purge|fall]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Fifth Brother Databank">{{Databank|the-fifth-brother|The Fifth Brother}}</ref> There were several individuals who carried this title, though the organization itself was relatively small. Although they were not related by [[blood]], the use of the titles of Brother and Sister gave the group a sense of cohesion.<ref name="Rebels Recon ATTA" />

The [[death]] of [[the Grand Inquisitor]] at the [[Action at Mustafar|hands]] of the [[Spectres|rebel]] Jedi [[Kanan Jarrus]]<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref> created a power vacuum within the ranks of the Inquisitors, so the Brothers and Sisters of the Inquisitorius competed against one another<ref name="Rebels Recon ATTA" /> in the hopes of currying favor with the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]<ref name="The Future of the Force" /> so they could become the next Grand Inquisitor.<ref name="Rebels Recon ATTA" /> The Brothers and Sisters were also responsible<ref name="The Future of the Force" /> for carrying on the order's goal to find and either destroy or convert Force-sensitive children, so they could not become a threat to the Empire.<ref name="Spark of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref>

The title of Brother followed a number provided to the individual. Examples of Inquisitors with the Brother title included the [[Marrok|First Brother]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|marrok-inquisitor|Marrok (Inquisitor)}}</ref>, the [[Third Brother]], the [[Fifth Brother]], the [[Sixth Brother]], the [[Eighth Brother]], the [[Tenth Brother]],<ref name="Darth Maul">{{EncyclopediaCite|Darth Maul and Other Followers of the Dark Side}}</ref> and [[Eleventh Brother]].<ref name="Countdown">{{InsiderCite|229|Inquisitor Countdown}}</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|The kill is mine!''"<br />"''You are short-sighted. We will use the boy as bait to draw in the others.|The Fifth Brother clashes with the Seventh Sister|<ref name="Always Two There Are" />}}
The titles of Brother and Sister were used within the Inquisitorius, the dark side group utilized by the Galactic Empire in order to hunt down Jedi survivors during the [[Imperial Era|dark times]] of Imperial rule over [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Rebels Recon ATTA" /> During the early rebellion against the Empire, Darth Vader and the Empire sent the Grand Inquisitor to eliminate the threat posed by the Jedi-led rebellion in the [[Lothal system]].<ref name="Spark of Rebellion" /> After the Grand Inquisitor's death,<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> Vader was ordered by the [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]], [[Darth Sidious]], to dispatch another Inquisitor to hunt the rebels down.<ref name="The Siege of Lothal">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''</ref> Vader dispatched the [[Fifth Brother]] to deal with the rebels.<ref name="Relics of the Old Republic" /> 

Their mission from Vader brought the Seventh Sister and the Fifth Brother into conflict not only with the rebels, but also with themselves as they competed to gain favor with the Sith Lord. One such conflict was during a [[Skirmish aboard a Republic medical station|confrontation]] with the rebel [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]] and his rebel companions, [[Sabine Wren]] and [[Garazeb Orrelios]]. The Fifth Brother intended to kill Bridger, but this was contrary to the Seventh Sister's plan to use Bridger to lure the other rebels, including Tano, to their destruction.<ref name="Always Two There Are" /> Not long after, a [[Duel in Hammertown|joint mission]] also led the Seventh Sister and the Fifth Brother to hunt two Force-sensitive infants, [[Pypey]] and [[Alora]]. This mission further brought out the competition between the two Inquisitors. The Fifth Brother attempted to take charge of the pursuit, but his instincts and orders were questioned by the Seventh Sister. Ultimately, the two [[youngling]]s were rescued by the rebels Kanan Jarrus, [[Ezra Bridger]], and [[Garazeb Orrelios]].<ref name="The Future of the Force" />

During the course of their interactions, the Fifth Brother and the Seventh Sister were looked upon with some trepidation by the Imperial rank-and-file such as [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]], who doubted their ability to produce the results that the Empire expected of them. Nonetheless, they continued to work together in the pursuit of the rebellion.<ref name="Legacy">{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
After the introduction of the Grand Inquisitor in the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'',<ref name="Spark of Rebellion" /> the show's [[Star Wars Rebels Season Two|second season]] further expanded upon the Inquisitorius. The Fifth Brother was first introduced in the episode "[[Relics of the Old Republic]]."<ref name="Relics of the Old Republic" />
