{{Quote|The city's central computer told you? R2-D2, you know better than to trust a strange computer.|[[C-3PO]] to [[R2-D2]], referring to the central computer of [[Cloud City]]|<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref>}}
'''Computers''' were hardware designed for mechanical control, data processing, and information storage. [[Astromech droid]]s<ref name="ESB" /> and [[protocol droid]]s<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> could interface with them, while living beings could access them through computer terminals or a portable [[datapad]]. [[Starship]]s with on-board [[hyperdrive]]s were equipped with [[navigation computer]]s to make the calculations necessary for a safe [[hyperspace]] trip<ref name="A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref>, while armed [[warship]]s and [[starfighter]]s were often equipped with [[targeting computer]]s to aid their [[gunner]]s and [[pilot]]s in aiming their vessel's weapons.<ref name="A New Hope" /><ref name="SWCVNE">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref>

The central processing computer of a [[droid]] was also called a "[[droid brain]]."<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

Those skilled at hacking into computer systems were known as [[slicer]]s.<ref name="tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>
