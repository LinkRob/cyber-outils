The '''DW-3 concussion grenade launcher''' was a model of [[concussion grenade]] [[Concussion grenade launcher|launcher]] manufactured by [[Dymek|Dymex]]. It stored three heavy-duty Dymex [[TriplePounder concussion grenade|TriplePounder grenades]] capable of swiveling a wide swath of infantrymen. The [[All Terrain Scout Transport]] used by the [[Imperial Army]] was equipped with one such launcher on the starboard side of its command module.<ref name="AT-ACT Deluxe Book" />
