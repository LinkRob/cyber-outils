An '''aide''' or '''assistant''' was a worker or individual who assisted a person of high [[Politics|political]] or military standing.<ref name="Convergence">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]''</ref> A number of [[senator]]s in the [[Galactic Senate|Republic Senate]],<ref name="RS">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> and later, [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] leaders were assisted by aides.<ref name="Announcement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref>

When [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] became senator of [[Naboo]] she took on [[handmaiden]]s as her aides.<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref> Her known aides included [[Dormé]], [[Versé]], [[Cordé]], and [[Teckla Minnau]].<ref name="Star Wars Episode II: Attack of the Clones">{{Film|II}}</ref> Later, following the outbreak of the [[Clone Wars]], Amidala hired [[Moteé]] and [[Ellé]]<ref name="Star Wars Episode III: Revenge of the Sith">{{Film|III}}</ref> to replace Versé and Cordé<ref name="Queen's Hope">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref>&mdash;who had died in an [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|assassination attempt]] on the senator's life.<ref name="Star Wars Episode II: Attack of the Clones" />

During the last days of the [[Separatist Crisis]], [[Taun We]], aide to [[Lama Su]], the [[Prime Minister]] of the [[Ruling Council (Kamino)|Ruling Council]] of [[Kamino]], greeted [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] when the [[Jedi Master]] visited the [[planet]].<ref name="Star Wars Episode II: Attack of the Clones" />

Amidala's close friend and ally [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] also had several senatorial aides including [[Sheltay Retrac]]<ref name="Star Wars Episode III: Revenge of the Sith" /> and later Organa's adopted daughter and Amidala's biological daughter [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="Leia Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>

During the Clone Wars, [[Cellheim Anujo]] served as an aide to [[Quarren]] [[Senator]] [[Tundra Dowmeia]].<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

[[Kilji vassal]]s acted as aides to [[Kilji Illumine]] [[commanding officer]]s and [[Kilji way of order and enlightenment|their]] masters, doing everything for them.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>

[[ISB Attendant|Attendants]] in the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] acted as aides to [[ISB Supervisor]]s.<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref>

[[Flag Lieutenant]]s acted as the personal aides to [[admiral]]s in the [[Imperial Military]].<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>
