'''Espinar''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Catalyst" /> located in the [[Espinar system]] of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> where [[Lyra Erso]] led a survey team, discovered and mapped a [[cave]] system that promised a bounty of rare [[crystal]]line deposits.<ref name="The Ultimate Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> Whilst on Espinar, she set [[eye]]s on a man named [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]], who later [[Wedding of Lyra and Galen Erso|became]] her [[Marriage|husband]], for the first [[time]]. Lyra served as his guide as he led a [[Galen Erso's field team|field team]] that studied [[mineral]]s. Lyra and Galen failed to uncover any [[kyber crystal]]s. During her time in [[Prisoner|captivity]] on the planet [[Vallt]], Lyra recalled the first time she and Erso met on Espinar. Erso ran into [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] very shortly after returning from the planet, unexpectedly introducing his new love to Krennic for the first time.<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>
