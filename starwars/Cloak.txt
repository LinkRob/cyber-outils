[[File:EmperorPalpatine-BTMF22.png|thumb|right|200px|Emperor Palpatine wore a hooded cloak of zeyd cloth.]]
A '''cloak''' was a type of loosely fitting garment featuring a hood. [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was known to wear a cloak.<ref name="readalong">[[A New Hope Read-Along Storybook and CD|''A New Hope'' Read-Along Storybook and CD]]</ref> [[Ezra Bridger]] once wished for a [[silk weave dusk cape]], a type of cloak.<ref name="journal">''[[Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger]]''</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] [[Sleep|slept]] in his [[Yoda's Hut|hut]] on [[Dagobah]] under a [[Qui-Gon Jinn's cloak|cloak]] owned by [[Qui-Gon Jinn]].<ref name="There is Another">{{StoryCite|story=There is Another|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> "[[Jixtus]]" of the [[Grysk]] [[species]] used a cloak, [[glove]]s and a veil to hide his appearance. [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]] of the [[Kilji Illumine]] disliked having the cloaked [[alien]] aboard his [[Kilji war cruiser]] ''[[Whetstone]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>
