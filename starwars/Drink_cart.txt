'''Drink carts''' were small ground vehicles used by [[Jat Kaa's Coolers]] to deliver various cold beverages to customers and locations at [[Black Spire Outpost]]. Various [[R-series]] astromech droids, purchased by the [[Lurmen]] [[Jat Kaa]] from [[Droid Depot]], operated the drink carts which carried [[Coca-Cola]], [[Diet Coke]], [[Sprite]], and [[Dasani]].<ref name="SWGE">{{GEAttr|Jat Kaa's Coolers}}</ref><ref name="TGTB">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> Carts were themed to match the colors and designs of the packaging of one of the beverages.<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge]]''</ref>
