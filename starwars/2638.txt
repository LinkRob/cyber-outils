'''2638'''<ref name="CVD numbers">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'' shows what numbers the signs on the [[comlink booster pack]] stand for.</ref> was a [[B1-series battle droid]] in the [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] that was active during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Episode III" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA" /> 2638 was involved in the [[Battle of Utapau]], where it fired near [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], but the [[droid]] was blasted by [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="Episode III" />
