'''Carbonbergs''' were large, drifting masses of solid [[carbon]] that could be encountered in [[realspace]]. Among other hazards, the [[Akkadese Maelstrom]] notably contained [[planet]]-sized carbonbergs that routinely crashed into each other, creating [[Gravity|gravitational]] chaos.<ref name="Solo Expanded" />
