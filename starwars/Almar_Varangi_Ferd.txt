'''Almar Varangi Ferd''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Human/Legends|Human]] criminal who operated as a [[Pirate/Legends|pirate]] captain during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].

==Biography==
Ferd was a colossal man, tall and muscular, who would often wear [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] attire with the [[Mo'ork skull]], his coat of arms, on his handles and chest. He wore a [[Colonel/Legends|colonel's]] cap from the [[Imperial Army/Legends|Imperial Army]] and a [[BlasTech 8000]] heavy blaster.

His two [[Gamorrean/Legends|Gamorrean]] bodyguards never left his side, and he regularly used his position to abuse others, which prevented his numerous crew as well as the citizens of [[Providence (lair)|Providence]] from truly liking him.

Colonel [[Viyenah]] of [[Imperial Intelligence/Legends|Imperial Intelligence]] suspected him to be of [[Corellian/Legends|Corellian]] birth and to have been part of the [[Imperial Navy/Legends|Imperial Navy]], where he would have acquired his leadership qualities. After his retirement, he became a cargo pilot before becoming a [[Pirate/Legends|pirate]], with a crew mainly composed of renegade soldiers, traitors and brutish men.

Ferd was sentenced to death on more than twenty planets, and had bounties on his head all over [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]], since he dedicated his operations to attacks on merchant ships and planetary facilities. He also practiced [[Slavery/Legends|slavery]], and always killed useless prisoners.

He nourished a feud with [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] ever since Solo caused him to lose a cargo.

==Personality and traits==
Ferd was brutal and impulsive, readily tyrannical and dominating. Viyenah compared him to a dictator.

This attitude led his crew to both show him admiration and fear him, as he didn't hesitate to execute those who broke his rules.

Vulgar and repulsive, he was nonetheless smart and capable, able to deploy intelligence and cunning in perilous situations. He was an exceptional [[Pilot/Legends|pilot]].

==Behind the scenes==
Almar Varangi Ferd was created by [[Jean-Michel Ringuet]] for the ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] aid "[[Providence (Avalon)|Providence]]," which appeared in the French magazine [[Avalon 1|''Avalon'' 1]]. He was illustrated by [[Jean-Marc Emy]].
