The '''Aldhani rebel infiltration team''',<ref name="TB DB">{{Databank|taramyn-barcona|Taramyn Barcona}}</ref> or '''Aldhani heist team''',<ref name="2023 Topps">{{Topps|set=2023 Topps Star Wars|cardname=Luthen Rael|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/370777/cid/22170346/2023-Topps-Star-Wars-16-Luthen-Rael}}</ref> was a rebel cell comprised of seven [[human]]s. Six of them were volunteers&mdash;the leader [[Vel Sartha]], [[Taramyn Barcona]], [[Arvel Skeen]], [[Karis Nemik]], [[Cinta Kaz]], and [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] double agent [[Lieutenant]] [[Gorn]]. The seventh member was [[Cassian Jeron Andor]], operating under the name "Clem," who was paid for the job and forcibly installed by [[Luthen Rael]], who demanded Sartha accept his presence if she wanted to continue the operation. Together, they planned a [[Aldhani heist|mission on Aldhani]], with the goal of stealing a quarter's worth of the Imperial payroll for the entire [[sector]] in which [[Aldhani]] was located.<ref name="Aldhani" />

On the day of the phenomenon known as the [[Eye of Aldhani]], Barcona led Skeen, Nemik, and Andor to the [[Aldhani dam]], the main Imperial base, disguised as [[Imperial Army trooper]]s. Meanwhile, Sartha and Kaz donned wetsuits and traveled through the water, climbing up the dam before disabling communications. Gorn allowed the squad of four men to pass by and take the Imperial [[commandant]], [[Jayhold Beehaz]], and his family, hostage. Rendezvousing with the other two members of their group, Gorn watched their backs as Sartha, Barcona, Nemik, Skeen, and Andor went down to the vault to locate the credits. As Kaz watched the hostages, the others forced the Imperial soldiers there to load the [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] onto the [[Max-7 Rono box freighter|Max-7 Rono freighter]] the group would use to escape. However, by the time Gorn arrived in the vault to escape as well, a group of Imperials had discovered them.<ref name="The Eye" />

After a shootout that left Gorn and Barcona dead, Andor was able to launch the ship, although Nemik was grievously injured during takeoff. After escaping from three [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] in the chaos of the Eye of Aldhani, a brilliant meteor shower, Sartha, Skeen, and Andor visited a doctor to help Nemik while Kaz left the Imperial base disguised as an Imperial officer. Nemik was unable to be saved, and when Skeen suggested that he and Andor take the payload and abandon Sartha, Andor shot and [[Death|killed]] him. He would tell Sartha of this before taking his cut and departing.<ref name="The Eye" />

By [[2 BBY]], Sartha would compare her former team to "a bunch of maniacs running around Aldhani" in comparison to the more organized [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Messenger">{{Andor|Messenger}}</ref>
