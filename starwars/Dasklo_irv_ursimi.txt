[[Patriarch]] '''Dasklo'irv'ursimi''' ([[Core name]] "Kloirvursi") was the leader of the [[Chiss]] [[Dasklo family|Dasklo]] [[Ruling Families|Ruling Family]]. During the [[Grysk plot to engineer a Chiss civil war|Chiss Ascendancy crisis]],<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> in [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Lesser Evil Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> [[Clarr family|Clarr]] Patriarch [[Clarr'ivl'exow]] (Core name "Rivlex") stated that Kloirvsi would never accept suggestions or alliances from one of the [[Great Families|Forty families]], and that the alliances with the Dasklo traveled from Kloirvursi outward, as Rivlex had been informed by a [[Grysk]], "[[Jixtus]]" of the Dasklo supposedly forming an alliance with one of the Forty.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

Later, [[Clarr'os'culry]] (Core name "Roscu") asked Rivlex if the [[Defense Hierarchy Council]] had asked the Nine Ruing Families for support in their attempt to prevent a civil war, and Rivlex that stated that not yet, as he speculated that the Council was aware that Kloirvusi would not contribute, and if the Dasklo did not, the Clarr would not either.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> Kloirvursi later participated in a [[Circle of Unity]] in [[19 BBY]].
