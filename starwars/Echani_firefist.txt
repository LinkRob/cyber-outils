'''Echani firefist''' was an [[Echani (martial art)|Echani]] form of [[unarmed combat]]. The energetic fighting style of former [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] and [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Magistrate]] [[Morgan Elsbeth]] combined Echani firefist with [[Dathomiri mistdance]] and [[Coruscant]]i [[Hijkata]].<ref name="MandalorianVG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref>
