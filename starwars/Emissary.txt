'''Emissary''' was a title for a [[diplomat]]. During the era of the [[New Republic]], the [[pirate]] [[Jod Na Nawood]] posed as an emissary of the [[Galactic Republic|old Republic]] to gain access to the hidden [[Planet|world]] of [[At Attin]]. The [[Twi'lek]] [[Yendor Brethen|Yendor]] served as [[Ryloth]]'s emissary to the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]].<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>
