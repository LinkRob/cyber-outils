'''''Bounty Hunters'' 4''' is the fourth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. It was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[August 19]], [[2020]]. It features the story "'''Galaxy's Deadliest Part 4: Hunter's Mutiny'''," in which [[bounty hunter]]s [[Beilert Valance]] and [[T'onga]] find their old mentor, [[Nakano Lash]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE SINS THAT CAN'T BE ABSOLVED! With a blaster aimed at this [''sic''] back, cyborg bounty hunter VALANCE closes in on NAKANO LASH, the mentor who abandoned him many years ago. But what is the secret that Lash has been hiding ever since that fateful, doomed mission&mdash;one that led her to betray her entire crew? Those answers may die with her if a vengeful BOBA FETT gets to her first.''

==Opening crawl==
{{OpeningCrawl
|name=GALAXY'S DEADLIEST<br />PART 4<br />Hunter's Mutiny
|text=Nakano Lash betrayed the bounty hunter crew she led by killing their client&mdash;
the heir of the Mourner's Wail Syndicate&mdash;which incited a war between the
Mourner's Wail and their rival, the Unbroken Clan.

Years later, Lash re-emerged, and the price on her head attracted hordes of hunters&mdash;
including her old crew: the Trandoshan Bossk, Boba Fett, the human T'onga, whose
brother T'ongor died as a result of Lash's actions, and her cyborg protégé Valance.

After Valance's droid acquired the possible coordinates to Lash's location on the
graveyard planet of Galmerah, Bossk attacked! Although Valance narrowly managed
to defeat the Trandoshan, two new threats quickly emerged. The Thisspiasian hunter
Ooris has made off with Valance's droid while T'onga has cornered Valance at gunpoint....}}
