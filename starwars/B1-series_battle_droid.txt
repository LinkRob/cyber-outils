'''B1-series battle droids''', also referred to as '''B1 battle droids''', '''standard battle droids''', '''clankers''', or '''tinnies''' were the predominantly used [[battle droid]]s manufactured by [[Baktoid Combat Automata]] and [[Baktoid Armor Workshop]]. Even though they were extensively produced and deployed, the B1-series battle [[droid]] was flimsily designed and easy to destroy, and was only truly successful in substantial overwhelming numbers. Early versions of the model also required the use of a [[Central Control Computer]], but despite these weaknesses they saw extensive service as the mainlines [[soldier]] of the [[Trade Federation]]'s mechanized [[Trade Federation Droid Army|droid army]]. The droid [[army]] was deployed during the [[Invasion of Naboo]], and after landing B1 battle droids marched into the [[Naboo]] capital of [[Theed]], and using the massive army the Trade Federation [[Capture of Theed|captured]] the [[city]]. The droids continued to maintain the Trade Federation's grip on the [[planet]], throughout the occupation, and the majority of Federation droid [[troop]]s were deployed to combat the [[Gungan Grand Army]] at the [[Great Grass Plains]]. In the [[Battle of Naboo|ensuing battle]] many [[militiagung]]s were [[Death|killed]], with the army initially capitulating to Federation forces, which had the gungan soldiers in retreat. However, the entirety of the droid army was defeated because it shut down due to the control computer being destroyed, along with the Federation's [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Droid Control Ship]] by the young [[Force-sensitive]] [[pilot]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], during the portion of the battle that transpired in space.

During the [[Separatist Crisis]], the Trade Federation leadership met with other corporate leaders on the planet [[Geonosis]], and the Federation had given its droid army, including the B1 battle droid, to the growing [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The droids became the main soldier of the new [[Separatist Droid Army]]. The forces of the army used newer reworked B1s. These B1 models did not have a reliance on a central command signal, and so these newer models did not require a control ship. They were capable of independent thought; however, it was limited. On Geonosis the droid army [[First Battle of Geonosis|clashed]] with the [[Galactic Republic]]'s new [[Grand Army of the Republic|army]] of [[clone trooper]]s for the first time. This battle launched the [[The galaxy|galactic]] conflict known as the [[Clone Wars]]. The B1 battle droids and the other models of combat droids used by the Confederacy fought the forces of the Republic on many worlds for the duration of the long conflict.

Throughout the droids' history, it was evident that the B1 was dim-witted. Programming glitches manifested as the B1 battle droids developing personality quirks, and they were known to make commentary on what occurred around them. The B1s were outmatched by the [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s of the Republic, but massive numbers of B1s could gain victory over the more experienced clone troopers, and even members of the [[Jedi Order]]. The B1 battle droids, along with other Separatist droids by the Republic, were manufactured in massive numbers for the Separatists, but military [[officer]]s, like the [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander of the Droid Army]] [[General]] [[Grievous]], grew angry at the failings of the B1. Following the defeat of the Confederacy, B1 battle droids were deactivated by the [[Galactic Empire]] along with the rest of the droid army. B1 battle droids continued to see use through [[Separatist holdouts]] and the black market during the [[Imperial Era]].

==Description==
===Design===
{{Quote|Interesting design. Small and only moderately armored, but narrow and therefore difficult to target.''"<br />"''They're the workhorses of the Separatist army.|Thrawn and General Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>}}
[[File:1138Binks-TPM.png|thumb|left|226x226px|Early models of B1 battle droids were controlled through a central command signal. This ultimately cost the Trade Federation the Battle of Naboo.]]
B1-series battle droids<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> were [[humanoid]] [[Class four droid|fourth class]] [[battle droid]]s<ref name="VE" /> measuring 1.93 meters tall.<ref name="Battle Droid" /> The [[OOM-series battle droid]]<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> was a mark of the B1-series,<ref name="TROSVD" /> while [[631 model B1 battle droid]]s were a more cheaply made model.<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|56|Starship Fact File}}</ref> Unlike early B1 battle droids, later B1s were able to operate without a central command signal.<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> [[Central Control Computer]] were located in an [[orbit]]ing [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Droid Control Ship]], and Central Control [[Computer]]s were used because the budget-minded [[Trade Federation]] saved the production costs of thousands of individual [[droid]]s.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> By the [[time]] B1s were being used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the B1 battle droid had been reworked to not require a central command signal to operate,<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> and these later B1s that did not require a control ship were capable of limited independent thought,<ref name="CVD" /> and B1s not programmed by central computers saw themselves as "independent thinkers." Despite the new model within the [[Separatist Droid Army]] there were still older B1 models programmed by central computers used, such as [[RB-551]], a B1 battle droid who served as part as a [[Unidentified droid patrol|droid patrol]] during the [[Battle of Ryloth]].<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> Central Control Computers still did see use due to battle droids that required them, as was on the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]], ''[[Malevolence]]'',<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> but the battle droids of the Separatists had been upgraded by the [[Geonosian]]s to possess autonomous thinking.<ref name="Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>

<div style="float:right">[[File:AcceptableLosses-SoH.png|thumb|250px|B1 battle droids were designed to operate standardized weapons]]</div>
The humanoid physiques of the B1 model were specifically commissioned by the Trade Federation for practical reasons. The humanoid form allowed B1s to operate existing machinery, vehicles, and weapons originally designed for organic operators and pilots, saving the Federation unnecessary production costs and retrofitting. B1s could pilot [[Single Trooper Aerial Platform]]s, [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tanks]], [[Multi-Troop Transport]]s, and Federation [[battleship]]s, among other [[Starship|vessels]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> B1 battle droids' limited programming meant they were not always effective at these types of jobs, however.<ref name="Battle Droid" /> During the [[Battle of Rugosa|Ambush on Rugosa]], a B1 battle droid at the controls of the [[laser cannon]]s on a [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigate]] missed every [[Laser|shot]] it fired at [[Republic escape pod]]s. When the B1 battle droid was confronted about this by the [[OOM command battle droid]] in command of the [[frigate]], the droid told the [[commanding officer]] that it had missed due to flawed programming it suffered.<ref name="Ambush" /> The limited programming of the B1 battle droids also made them unfit to serve many of the jobs they were tasked with on the advanced ''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser ''Malevolence'', during the [[Malevolence Campaign|''Malevolence'' Campaign]].<ref name="Battle Droid" />

[[File:B1stages-SWBC13.png|thumb|left|300px|B1 battle droids could be folded into a compact configuration.]]
The body of the B1 battle droid was also designed so the unit could be able to fold into a compact stowed configuration. One of the uses of this configuration was storage for transport, as in this configuration one hundred and twelve B1 battle droids could be stored into a Multi-Troop Transport.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> B1 battle droids could also fold into this configuration when they were low on power and needed to close down to recharge.<ref name="The Deserter" /> When the B1s were activated, the [[limb]]s of the B1s unfolded first, and their long neck unfolded last.<ref name="CVD" /> B1 battle droids were also designed to resemble their Geonosian creators,<ref name="Updated and Expanded" /> and were able to be produced cheaply. The cheap price tag of the B1s often allowed multiple [[battalion]]s of B1 battle droids to be sacrificed for a victory without concern.<ref name="On the Front Lines" />

B1 battle droids had a [[comlink]] just below their head,<ref name="Counterattack">{{TCW|Counterattack}}</ref> and their [[photoreceptor]]s could switch to an infrared mode. B1 battle droids had the ability to pick up electromagnetic fields, such as the small fields generated from [[holoprojector]]s.<ref name="Front Runners">{{TCW|Front Runners}}</ref> B1 battle droids possessed simple [[vocabulator]]s,<ref name="Updated and Expanded" /> which gave the majority of B1s high-pitched voices, but other B1s spoke in lower, more monotone voices. B1 battle droids could exhibit various tones of voice, such as fear,<ref name="Cargo of Doom" /> confusion, and excitement.<ref name="Defenders of Peace">{{TCW|Defenders of Peace}}</ref> These also were examples of the B1 battle droid's "personality quirks," which were the result of programming glitches in the B1 battle droids.<ref name="Battle Droid" />

[[File:AhsokaAtChristophsis-OtFL.jpg|thumb|right|197x197px|B1 battle droids could be destroyed by both blaster bolts and lightsabers.]]
Inexpensive yet durable metal protected the droid's signal-receiver assembly.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> However, this metal did not protect B1 battle droids against blaster fire or [[lightsaber]]s well, and these weapons could easily defeat a B1.<ref name="Landing at Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref> Another weak point in the droids, which could be aimed for by [[sniper]]s, were the droid's [[capacitor]]s, and hitting between the B1's capacitors could take out the unit.<ref name="Beginnings">''[[Star Wars Journeys: Beginnings]]''</ref> Despite these tactics often utilized by the Republic, [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] informed the [[Onderon rebels]] that the only true way to take out a battle droid was to take out their head, and Rex stated this was because the droids didn't even require their bodies to report intel back to command.<ref name="A War on Two Fronts">{{TCW|A War on Two Fronts}}</ref>

===Performance===
{{Dialogue|General Grievous|How are you finding the assistance of the droid battalion? Have they quelled your superiority complex programming?|AD-W4|You jest, Grievous. Their battle prowess is laughable while their 'banter' is&mdash;|B1 battle droid|He is so mean to us! Meaner even than you General. All we want to do is give him a callsign and&mdash;|AD-W4|[destroys the B1 out of annoyance] "''They do, however, make excellent blaster fodder.''"|line4=no|attr=General Grievous, droid mercenary AD-W4, and a B1 battle droid|src=<ref name="Mace Windu 3">[[Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu 3|''Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu'' 3]]</ref>}}
[[File:STAP-TCW.png|thumb|left|246x246px|B1 battle droids could pilot Single Trooper Aerial Platforms.]]
The B1 was designed to be easily controlled, inexpensive to mass-produce, and unquestioningly obedient;<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> however B1s often questioned their orders. This could be observed during the [[Battle of Quell]], in which [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|T-series tactical droid]] [[TF-1726]] gave the order to destroy Republic [[Jedi General]] [[Aayla Secura]]'s [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]]. A B1 battle droid disagreed with this, mentioning that there were still hundreds of droids aboard the [[cruiser]]. These droids had been sent to attack the Republic [[Star Destroyer]] earlier in the battle, but TF-1726 responded that he didn't care, and ignored the B1's protest, so the B1 followed its orders to fire on the enemy ship.<ref name="Jedi Crash">{{TCW|Jedi Crash}}</ref> Another example of this was when a B1 questioned the hired [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]]'s decision not to launch reinforcements to the [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]] engaging Republic forces during the [[Battle of Devaron]]. Later during the battle, two B1s were concerned over their orders to defend the ship's [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]]. The droids knew only the few droids stationed on the bridge would be defending against two incoming [[Jedi]].<ref name="Cargo of Doom">{{TCW|Cargo of Doom}}</ref> During a [[sandstorm]] in the final acts of the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], [[O.M.5]] and [[Unidentified B1 battle droid (O.M.5)|another B1 battle droid]] questioned where [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] member [[Poggle the Lesser]] was leading them, but the pair continued to follow him for as long as they could.<ref name="Legacy of Terror">{{TCW|Legacy of Terror}}</ref>

These examples of B1s questioning and commenting on their orders<ref name="Jedi Crash" /><ref name="Cargo of Doom" /><ref name="Legacy of Terror" /> all additionally served as examples of the "banter" and other comments that the B1 battle droids would often make.<ref name="Mace Windu 2">[[Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu 2|''Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu'' 2]]</ref> Other examples were when two B1 battle droids spoke on the [[battlesphere]] they were stationed on, [[Skytop Station]], as one needed to get its head adjusted due to high amounts of moisture.<ref name="DOTD">{{TCW|Duel of the Droids}}</ref> Another example came when a group of B1 battle droids talked about how they believed they were superior models to the older RB-551.<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" />

[[File:B1battledroid-TGTB.png|thumb|right|180px|B1 battle droids came in various colors, including rust-red color.]]

B1 battle droids also made other types of comments on duty, such as [[Unidentified B1-series battle droid 1 (Hissrich)|one B1 battle droid]] commenting on the beauty of the [[planet]] [[Hissrich]],<ref name="Mace Windu 1">[[Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu 1|''Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu'' 1]]</ref> and another insulting [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[clone trooper]]s during the [[Battle of Teth]], calling the [[Cloning|clones]] of [[Torrent Company]] "Republic dogs."<ref name="tcwfilm">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> One B1 battle droid seemingly saw itself as being above the [[astromech droid]]s of [[D-Squad]], as it called them "stupid astromechs."<ref name="SW">{{TCW|Secret Weapons}}</ref> These "personality quirks" displayed by B1 battle droids were the result of programming glitches the droids suffered. B1 battle droids proved to be extremely dim-witted, but no attempt was made to fix these issues, as the droids were still able to use their swarm tactics, and advanced programming was not required for this.<ref name="Battle Droid" />

Individual B1 battle droids were not effective [[soldier]]s against enemies like clone troopers in combat,<ref name="Battle Droid" /> and so the B1 primarily relied on mass-assault tactics to achieve victory,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> but the cheap cost of the B1 battle droid made this a viable tactic for military use.<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> A singular B1 droid was stronger than the average clone trooper.<ref name="Master of Evil">''[[Master of Evil]]''</ref> Although later models were capable of limited independent thought,<ref name="CVD" /> B1 battle droids had a limited intelligence compared to other soldiers<ref name="tcwfilm" /> and required a superior [[commander]] to lead them effectively in battle. Motion-capture data from highly trained organic droids allowed the B1 to demonstrate an array of combat stances, positions, and maneuvers.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Despite their advantages, B1s were just as vulnerable to [[Electromagnetic pulse|EMP]] weapons as other droids.<ref name="Battle Droid" /> The [[Gungan Grand Army]] used this tactic during the [[Battle of Naboo]] when they used [[booma]]s against the [[Trade Federation Droid Army]],<ref name="Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace" /> and, during the Clone Wars, the Republic also used this advantage. Clone troopers used [[Electromagnetic pulse grenade|Electro Magnetic Pulse grenades]], which were nicknamed "droid poppers" by the clones, against B1s and other battle droids,<ref name="DOTD" /> and the Republic would develop the [[electro-proton bomb]], which could short-circuit an entire droid army.<ref name="Battle Droid" />

[[File:Battle_Droid_SWD_2018_Season_2_OP_Elite_Prize_Card.png|thumb|left|240px|B1 battle droids often had to rely on their numbers to achieve victory.]]
In the [[Ground warfare|field]], B1 droids often utilized [[E-5 blaster rifle]]s and wore [[comlink booster pack]]s.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> The droids were also able to use [[electrobinoculars]]<ref name="Mace Windu 1" /><ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> and [[thermal detonator]]s on the field.<ref name="SL" /> During the Battle of [[Ryloth]], two B1s in [[Nabat]] used [[electropole]]s to force [[Gutkurr]]s to move to attack [[Ghost Company]].<ref name="Innocents of Ryloth" /> B1 battle droids were programmed to never surrender,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> but the droids were also able to focus on self-preservation, and they could attempt to escape their possible destruction.<ref name="Cargo of Doom" /><ref name="The Deserter" /> B1 battle droids outranked the astromech droids used by the Confederacy,<ref name="ANB">{{TCW|A Necessary Bond}}</ref> and B1 battle droids were able to hold [[military rank]]s such as [[sergeant]]<ref name="Innocents of Ryloth" /> and [[lieutenant]].<ref name="Lair">{{TCW|Lair of Grievous}}</ref> B1 battle droids could also serve as [[scout]]s for military operations<ref name="Mace Windu 2" /> and as [[gunner]]s to operate heavy [[artillery]], such as the laser [[cannon]]s on Armored Assault Tanks.<ref name="ANB" /> B1 battle droids were able to operate in [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Cargo of Doom" />

==History==
===Origin===
{{Quote|Some of you gathered here today are old enough to remember when Baktoid Armor Workshop came to Geonosis and struck a deal with us to build foundries and produce battle droids and other automata for which we were handsomely rewarded.|Archduke Poggle the Lesser to the Geonosians |<ref name="Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel" />}}
[[File:Geonosis_primary_droid_foundry.png|thumb|right|300px|B1 battle droids saw production in droid foundries, such as the [[Geonosis primary droid foundry|primary droid foundry]] during Clone Wars's Second Battle of Geonosis, and foundries allowed many B1s to be constructed.]]
B1 battle droids would be manufactured by [[Baktoid Combat Automata]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and [[Baktoid Armor Workshop]],<ref name="Updated and Expanded" /> and the latter would approach the Geonosians to build battle droids and other weapons.<ref name="Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel" /> The Geonosians designed the B1 battle droids,<ref name="Battle Droid" /> and B1s were constructed in their [[Geonosis droid factories|droid foundries]] on [[Geonosis]]. This allowed countless droids to be produced at one time.<ref name="AOTC" /> [[Archduke]] Poggle the Lesser also received backing from the Trade Federation's mysterious benefactor [[Darth Sidious]], and millions of B1 battle droids were produced on Geonosis for the Trade Federation.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

===Invasion of Naboo===
{{Quote|Finally the mackineeks gottin us surround, but mesa and the rest, we just looked at each other smiling. We was going to rush them. We didn't care about their flashers. We just had a feeling that we'd like seeing what we could be doing. And that's when the mackineeks switched off.|The Gungan [[militiagung]] [[Oma Prumba]]'s account on the end of the Battle of Naboo|<ref name="On the Front Lines" />}}
[[File:DroidArmy.png|thumb|left|300px|Thousands of B1-series battle droids were deployed against the Gungan Grand Army.]]
In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Prop 31-814D]] made [[Free Trade Zone]]s eligible for taxation,<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> and in retaliation the Trade Federation initiated a [[blockade]] of the planet Naboo. The Jedi [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] were sent to negotiate with the Federation, but the negotiations were thwarted when [[Darth]] Sidious contacted [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]] and ordered him to [[Death|kill]] the Jedi. Although the two Jedi were able to [[Escape from the Saak'ak|escape]], the Trade Federation sent an invasion force to Naboo in hopes of forcing [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] to sign a treaty which would legitimize the occupation. At the end of the occupation B1s fought against the [[Gungan]] Grand Army at the [[Great Grass Plains]] during the Battle of Naboo, and did gain the upper hand. This was truly a distraction to draw the main droid forces away from Naboo's [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Theed]], which had been occupied since its [[Capture of Theed|capture]] by the Federation. In Theed they fought against the Royal Naboo Security Forces under the command of the Queen of Naboo, Padmé Amidala. The droids were ultimately shut down at the end of the occupation during the Battle of Naboo when the Trade Federation's orbiting Droid Control Ship was destroyed by the young [[Force-sensitive]] [[pilot]] [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace" />

[[File:Battle_droids_attack.png|thumb|right|300px|B1 battle droids and B2 super battle droids fought for the Confederacy of Independent Systems on numerous worlds such as Ryloth.]]
The Trade Federation lost thousands of B1 battle droids due to the Battle of Naboo,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and the defeat showed the faults of the B1. It was clear that the B1 could effectively bully civilians, but the droid was not a match for trained soldiers. The lessons learned from Naboo led the [[Techno Union]] to develop the B2 super battle droid. Super battle droids were a heavy infantry model of droid designed solely for combat, but also would also see other uses.<ref name="super battle droid databank">{{Databank|super-battle-droid|Super Battle Droid}}</ref> The Trade Federation would come to ally itself with the growing Confederacy of Independent Systems.<ref name="AOTC" /> By the [[Clone Wars]], Separatist leaders had come to see networked control centers for B1s as a vulnerability,<ref name="B1 Battle Droid card">''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game Adversary Deck]]'' Republic and Separatist Adversary Deck (Card: B1 Battle Droid)</ref> and the reliance on a central command signal, the tactic that had cost the Federation the Battle of Naboo, eventually was removed from the B1 battle droids.<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> The new model of B1 that did not need a control ship to operate were capable of limited independent thought,<ref name="CVD" /> and the newer B1s regarded themselves as "independent thinkers." Older models controlled by central computers still saw use, despite the newer model of B1,<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> and the computers were used to give commands to droids performing complicated technical duties.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Development===
B1 battle droids first appeared in the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace" />

In the mid-1990s, [[Doug Chiang]]'s earliest designs of the battle droids were a nod to the helmets worn by the stormtroopers of the [[original trilogy]], suggesting the evolution of large-scale combat in the ''Star Wars'' galaxy.<ref name="Early Concepts">{{SW|url=news/5-early-star-wars-character-concepts|text=Yoda Almost Looked Like a Garden Gnome, Plus 4 More Early ''Star Wars'' Character Concepts}}</ref> The finished droid's proportions are reminiscent of African sculpture. Early design had towering droids twice the height of humans, but subsequent iterations brought their height down to human size.<ref name="Databank Battle droid">{{DB|droid|battledroid|battle droid}}</ref>

The mechanical sound of their movements was recorded years earlier by [[Ben Burtt]], during filming {{Film|VI}}; the sound of the servomotors of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba's]] puppet attracted his attention and recorded it on tape, and eventually used it for ''The Phantom Menace''.<ref name="guide">[[Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide (real-life book)|''Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide'']]</ref>

The droids speak with a monotonic voice, performed by different actors. Initially Burtt wanted to experiment with recording words independently, without intonation, which then would be synthesized to form phrases. This effect, similar to automated answering machines, would highlight their low intelligence. This concept was abandoned as it made them too impersonal and dumb, without "sense of purpose and will to survive".<ref name="guide" />

===Contradictions===
The [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'' first identified the OOM-series battle droid and stated they had been created before the B1-series.<ref name="CVD" /> However, [[2015]]'s ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'' and [[2019]]'s ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' both contain battle droid pages that list the model as B1. Therefore, all information on the page applies to the B1 battle droid, but the section also mentions the security, pilot, and command droids<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /><ref name="Ultimate Star Wars New">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> that ''The Complete Visual Dictionary'' treated as predecessors.<ref name="CVD" /> Additionally, both versions of ''Ultimate Star Wars'' state that OOM-9 is an OOM command battle droid and a B1.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /><ref name="Ultimate Star Wars New" /> Another 2019 reference book, ''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition]]'', similarly included a battle droid page that listed the droid type as a B1, but it also included information on pilot battle droids.<ref name="Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition]]''</ref> ''Star Wars Battlefront II'' and ''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]'' both also state B1s served as pilot droids.<ref name="Battlefront II" /><ref name="Droidography" /> The battle droid [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry on [[StarWars.com]] does not mention a difference in model despite including information involving B1s<ref name="Battle Droid" /> and OOMs.<ref name="Battle Droid History Gallery" /> ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' later established that the OOM-series was a mark of the B1 line.<ref name="TROSVD" /> Given that the majority of sources treat the OOM-series as part of the B1-series, this article assumes ''Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition'' is incorrect.
