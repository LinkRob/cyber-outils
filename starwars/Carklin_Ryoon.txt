'''Carklin Ryoon''' was a [[Ssori]] [[gunner]] who was a member of [[Eleodie Maracavanya]]'s [[pirate]] nation [[Sovereign Latitudes of Maracavanya]]. Ryoon was stationed aboard the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|Super Star Destroyer]] ''[[Liberty's Misrule]]'', which had been commandeered by the pirates. He had a tiny [[mouth]], sharp [[Tooth|teeth]], and bug [[eye]]s. While many members of his [[species]] chose to wear mechanical suits to compensate for their small physique, he preferred to remain purely organic. Following the [[Battle of Jakku]], Ryoon informed Eleodie that three [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]] had exited [[hyperspace]] and were hailing them. Ryoon complied with Eleodie's orders to [[Destruction of Imperial Star Destroyers|attack]] the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Starship|ships]] in order to curry favor with the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Empire's End" />
