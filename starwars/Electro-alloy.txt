'''Electro-alloy''' was a type of heavy alloy that could be used to make chains.<ref name="TOTF" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> the [[Nihil]] [[Minister of Protection]] [[Abediah Viess]] took over the [[San Tekka clan|San Tekka family's]] [[San Tekka compound|compound]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] and detained [[Plinka]]&mdash;a [[Grand Theljian snow dog]] owned by the [[physicist]] [[Xylan Graf]], who had [[Desertion|defected]] from the Nihil&mdash;and restrained her in electro-alloy chains. When Graf's [[Marriage|husband]] [[Cair San Tekka]] returned to his family's compound, he was brought before Viess, who kept Plinka tied up between them.<ref name="TOTF" />

When Cair protested her poor treatment of the [[dog]] and purported her to be owned by himself instead, Viess proceeded to slice open part of Plinka's throat. The electro-alloy chains holding the dog shorted out as she [[Blood|bled]] and Cair rushed to comfort her. By later that evening, Plinka had been released from the chains and received limited medical attention from Cair.<ref name="TOTF" />
