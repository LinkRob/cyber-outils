{{Quote|I thought only the Jedi possessed such weapons!|[[Reess Kairn]] to [[Aurra Sing/Legends|Aurra Sing]]|<ref name="The Bounty Hunters: Aurra Sing">''[[The Bounty Hunters: Aurra Sing]]''</ref>}}
The [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Aurra Sing/Legends|Aurra Sing]] had a scarlet-bladed '''lightsaber'''. Sing didn't construct the [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] herself, but took it from one of the Jedi she killed.

Prior to the [[Invasion of Naboo/Legends|Invasion of Naboo]], Sing used it to kill [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] [[Mana Veridi]] on the planet [[Kwannot]] and later wielded it in combat against [[Qui-Gon Jinn/Legends|Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]].

In [[27 BBY/Legends|27 BBY]], Sing carried her lightsaber while on the search for the rogue Jedi [[Reess Kairn]].
