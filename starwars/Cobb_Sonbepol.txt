'''Cobb Sonbepol''' was a [[Gotal]] who was present in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in the [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] in the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]]. When local hermit [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[moisture farmer]] [[Luke Skywalker]] entered the [[cantina]] in search of a [[pilot]], Sonbepol sat at a table conversing with fellow patrons [[Momaw Nadon]] and [[Trinto Duaba]].

==Biography==
In the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">{{FilmDate|IV|1|Cobb Sonbepol's [[time]] in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]]}}</ref> shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Gotal]]<ref name="ANH" /> Cobb Sonbepol<ref name="Tweet">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/853330463041232897|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=Two newly named cantina denizens courtesy of the panel @TomSpinaDesigns and I just wrapped. #SWCO|archivefile=File:PabloHidalgo-CobbSonbepol-Twitter.png|nolive=1}}</ref> sat in a booth right next to the door in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in the [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], conversing with the [[Ithorian]] [[Momaw Nadon]] and the [[Stennes Shifter]] [[Trinto Duaba]].<ref name="Complete locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> While they spoke, local hermit [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and the young [[moisture farmer]] [[Luke Skywalker]] entered the [[cantina]] with their [[droid]]s, [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]], to look for a [[pilot]].<ref name="ANH" />

The [[bartender]] [[Wuher]]<ref name="Absolutely Everything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> turned the droids away, leaving Kenobi and Skywalker to approach the bar alone. At the counter, Skywalker caught the attention of criminals<ref name="ANH" /> [[Ponda Baba]] and [[Cornelius Evazan]].<ref name="Absolutely Everything" /> While Nadon discussed putting down roots on Tatooine with Duaba in the booth with Sonbepol,<ref name="Complete locations" /> the confrontation at the bar quickly escalated and drew interest from the rest of the cantina, ending when the criminal duo tried to attack Skywalker but were incapacitated by Kenobi with his [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber|lightsaber]].<ref name="ANH" />

==Personality and traits==
Sonbepol had [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]] and skin, and orange-colored [[eye]]s.<ref name="ANH" /> The Gotal spoke in [[Antarian]] while conversing with Nadon and Duaba.<ref name="Language">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Language in Star Wars Movies|video=CZp8Civk2co|nolive=1}}</ref>

==Equipment==
While in Chalmun's Cantina, Sonbepol wore a brown [[Clothing|jacket]] and blue pants.<ref name="databank">{{Databank|mos-eisley-cantina|Mos Eisley Cantina}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:CobbTrintoMomaw-DB.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Cobb Sonbepol sat with Trinto Duaba and Momaw Nadon in the cantina sequence.]]
Cobb Sonbepol was portrayed by [[Rob Bottin]]<ref name="Archeology">{{YouTube|video=ElyMaaAFLKU|text=The Star Wars Cantina PT. 2 &#124; Full Panel #indianacomicconvention #tomspinadesigns #starwarshistory|channel=@FanXComicConventions|name=FanX Comic Conventions|date=2025-05-03}}</ref> in the [[1977]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="ANH" /> The character's costume in the film used the same mask and prop claws as [[Feltipern Trevagg]] and [[Hennet Kayn]], the two other Gotal characters visible in the cantina.<ref name="Cantina BTS">{{SW|url=news/swca-secrets-of-the-mos-eisley-cantina-panel-liveblog|text=SWCA: Secrets of the Mos Eisley Cantina Panel Liveblog}} shows that the same mask was used for the three [[Gotal]]s seen in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. {{SW|url=news/swcc-2019-6-surprises-from-inside-the-mos-eisley-cantina|text=SWCC 2019: 6 Surprises from Inside the Mos Eisley Cantina|nolive=1}} provides [[Hennet Kayn]]'s name, and [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] provides the name of [[Feltipern Trevagg]].</ref> The Gotals were added to the film as part of supplemental footage for the cantina sequence shot during post production<ref name="Beastmaster">{{InsiderCite|126|The Beastmaster!}}</ref> on a sound stage in Dovington's Hollywood studio between [[January 24]] and [[January 25|25]], 1977.<ref name="MakingOf">''[[The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film]]''</ref>

The claws had originally been crafted by costume designer [[Rick Baker]] as props from the film [[Wikipedia:It's Alive (1974 film)|''It's Alive'']], which Baker had also previously worked on.<ref name="Claws">{{SW|url=news/swca-secrets-of-the-mos-eisley-cantina-panel-liveblog|text=SWCA: Secrets of the Mos Eisley Cantina Panel Liveblog}} shows that the claws used in the Cobb Sonbepol costume were originally from a set of props all made by [[Rick Baker]] for the film [[Wikipedia:It's Alive (1974 film)|''It's Alive'']], identifiable by the distinctive monster baby prop as seen in the following image: {{IMDb|url=title/tt0071675/mediaviewer/rm2118398464|text=Rick Baker in It's Alive (1974)|archivedate=20190522205651}}</ref> They were modified with fur for ''[[Star Wars]]''<ref name="Cantina BTS" /> by make-up artist [[Laine Liska]], who also created the character's mask<ref name="Rolecall">{{SWArchive|url=episode-iv/bts/article/f20030502/index.html|text=Cantina Roll-Call: Shedding Light on Some Alien Aliases|int=Cantina Roll-Call: Shedding Light on Some Alien Aliases}}</ref> after it was designed by conceptual artist [[Ron Cobb]].<ref name="Chronicle">''[[Star Wars Chronicles]]''</ref> The cantina character [[Myo]] also used a separate pair of the same claw props but without the fur.<ref name="MakingOf" />

Having gone unnamed for forty years after first appearing in ''A New Hope'', the Sonbepol character was first identified in [[2017]]. During the "Behind the Scenes of the Cantina" panel at [[Celebration Orlando|''Star Wars'' Celebration Orlando]] that year, [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Pablo Hidalgo]] used elements of attendee [[Sonja Beyer-Paulsen]]'s name to create the name "Cobb Sonbepol."<ref name="Tweet" />
