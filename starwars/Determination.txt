The '''''Determination''''' was a [[Star Destroyer]] in service to the [[Galactic Empire]] during its [[Imperial Era|reign]] of [[the galaxy]]. Soon after the [[Battle of Yavin]], the Star Destroyer was stationed in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]], where it engaged and destroyed a small [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] force.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
