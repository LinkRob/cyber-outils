An '''artificial sun''' was a device that mimicked the effect of a [[Star|sun]]. The devices could operate standalone<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> or as part of an [[artificial weather system]] meant to support [[plant]] life.<ref name="Trials" /> By [[228 BBY]],<ref name="TrialsDate">{{TrialsoftheJediDate|1}}</ref> the the [[garden]] in the [[Room of a Thousand Fountains]] within the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] had an artificial weather system that provided artificial sunlight.<ref name="Trials" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="TrialsDate" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]] visited the garden, noticing when a piece of [[Tolisian ore|ore]] resting on a pedestal glinted in the room's artificial sunlight.<ref name="Trials" />

One such device was also installed in the underground [[cave]]rn of the [[Mitth family]]'s [[Mitth family homestead|homestead]] on the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[capital]] [[planet]] [[Csilla]]. The artificial sun rose and set like [[Csilla's sun]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> At some point, the [[Moten estate]] on the world [[Dallow]] used artificial sun technology in the form of high-resolution ceiling tiles mimicking the same position as the [[Dallow system]]'s [[Dallow system's sun|actual sun]].<ref name="Sanctuary" />

==Behind the scenes==
An artificial sun first appeared in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
