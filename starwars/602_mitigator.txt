The '''602 mitigator''' was a device that was utilized by the [[New Republic]] [[Re-Integration Institute]]. It was a type of [[Mind Flayer]], but been repurposed for experimental treatment, and used lower voltages, being designed to be a non-invasive treatment that causes relief.

==Description==
The 602 mitigator<ref name="Mando Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> was a therapeutic device used for the non-invasive treatment of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[propaganda]] victims in the [[New Republic]] [[Re-Integration Institute]]; treated patients were approved to join the [[New Republic Amnesty Program]] but would be sent back in case they relapsed.<ref name="C19" /> The device was used in attunement sessions for cases of aggressive transgression, and used alpha waves to calm anxious minds.<ref name="Mando Visual Guide" /> The electricity used to zap a patient's [[brain]] was intended to mentally re-calibrate it to get rid of unwanted compulsions. The Re-Integration Institute used low-voltage zaps that induced hallucinations of pleasant [[color]]s and a light buzzing to the patient, bringing forth a great sense of relief. In these low voltages, the treatment was considered safe and patients remained healthy; it was, however, possible to increase the voltage dramatically, which would fry a patient's mind causing intense pain and discomfort.<ref name="C19" /> It was a type of [[Mind Flayer]],<ref name="Insider" /> though the New Republic claimed it was merely a similar device.<ref name="C19" /> The device featured a system control programming interface.<ref name="Mando Visual Guide" />

==History==
{{Quote|This isn't the Empire, son.|The Mon Calamari technician, to Penn Pershing|<ref name="C19" />}}
[[File:Mind_Flayer_MandoS3.png|left|250px|thumb|The New Republic repurposed the Mind Flayer as a rehabilitation device]]
[[Doctor]] [[Penn Pershing]] was forced to go through a mental recalibration using the lower voltages of a 602 mitigator, an experimental process thought to help soothe traumatic memories to help one overcome Imperial indoctrination. However, this process was sabotaged by [[Elia Kane]], who increased the voltage to its maximum.<ref name="C19" />
