'''Before the Rise''' was a web-exclusive article on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website written by [[J.D. Wiker]] and published on [[April 16]], [[2002]],<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://theforce.net/rpg/story/Updates_at_Wizards_of_the_Coast_67719.asp|text=Updates at Wizards of the Coast|author=JF|date=2002-04-16|work=[[TheForce.net]]|archivedate=20200930074612}}</ref> intented to be used as a scenario supplement for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]''.

The article covered four political factions: the [[Galactic Senate/Legends|Galactic Senate]], the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]], the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutts]] and the [[Vena Isolationists]] in the period shortly before the [[Rise of the Empire era]].
