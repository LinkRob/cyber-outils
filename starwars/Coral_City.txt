'''Coral City''',<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Rebel Alliance}}</ref> also known as '''Mon Cala city''',<ref name="Water War EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/water-war-episode-gallery|text="Water War" Episode Gallery}}</ref> was the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]], that was built over a coral reef and ruled by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[King of Mon Cala|monarch]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], it was the site of the [[Battle of Mon Cala]].<ref name="Water War" />

{{City-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The city's [[skyscraper]]s were intentionally designed to resemble [[MC-series star cruiser|Mon Calamari Star Cruisers]].<ref name="Water War CAG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/water-war-concept-art-gallery|text="Water War" Concept Art Gallery}}</ref> [[Dave Filoni]] explained while answer fan questions that Mon Cala city was going to be referred to as "Dac city" at some point in the script but this reference was changed to Mon Cala city.<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=video/questions-answered-mon-cala-tills-and-sharks-the-clone-wars|text=Questions Answered: Mon Cala, Tills and Sharks}}</ref> [[Dac City]] would appear in the [[Darth Vader (2017) 13|thirteenth issue]] of the [[2017]] [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2017)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series.<ref name="DV13">[[Darth Vader (2017) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 13]]</ref>
