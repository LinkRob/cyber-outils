'''Cyril Ikechukwu Nri''' is a [[Wikipedia:British Nigerian|Nigerian British]] actor, writer and director who portrays a [[First Order officer]] in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. He plays a [[Supreme Council]] member attending the meeting in which [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] presents the head of [[Boolio]] and declares that there is a [[spy]] within the [[First Order]].<ref name="TROS">{{Film|9}}</ref>

Nri is best known for playing [[W:c:TheBill:Adam Okaro|Superintendent Adam Okaro]] in the police TV series [[Wikipedia:The Bill|''The Bill'']].
