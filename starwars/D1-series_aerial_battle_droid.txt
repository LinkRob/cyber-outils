The '''D1-series aerial battle droid''', also known as the '''D-wing air support droid''', the '''D-wing air security droid''', '''D-wing security droid''' or simply the '''D-wing droid''', was a D1-series aerial [[battle droid]] and a variant of the [[B-series battle droid|B-series]] [[droid]] line. The droids had wings that they used to pursue enemies to areas where normal battle droids could not. They were developed by the [[Techno Union]], who operated out of a facility on the [[planet]] [[Skako Minor]]. They were active throughout the Clone Wars.

==Description==
{{Quote|That wasn't part of the plan&mdash;those things can fly!''"<br />"''What?!|[[Tech]] and [[Hunter]] on the D-wing droids, during their escape from [[Purkoll]]|<ref name="On the Wings" />}}
[[File:D-wing_droid.png|thumb|left|250px|A D-wing droid with its wings spread]]
D1-series aerial battle droids<ref name="Rise" /> were [[security droid]]s<ref name="Skako Databank" /> that were developed as an experiment of the [[Techno Union]]<ref name="Rise" /> by the [[Standard year|year]] [[26 BBY]].<ref name="26 date note" /> That year was when the [[Galactic Republic]] first became aware of them.<ref name="Official Magazine 118">{{GermanFanCite|118|Database}}</ref> Additionally, the Techno Union would become allies of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="Rise" /> and use the droids in Separatist capacity.<ref name="Battle Tales 2" /> The droids were used as security at the Techno Union facilities,<ref name="Official Magazine 118" /> such as the city of [[Purkoll]] on [[Skako Minor]].<ref name="On the Wings" />

D-wing droids were variant battle droids that were part of the [[B-series battle droid|B-series]] [[droid]] line.<ref name="Rise" /> They were armed with built-in [[blaster]]s and could use fold-out wings to enter flight mode. They had programming similar to the common Separatist [[B1-series battle droid]], and, although they had a different shaped head, otherwise appeared similar.<ref name="On the Wings" /> The D-wing droids were also proficient in security maneuvers.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==History==
During the [[Clone Wars]], in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Quarmendy Date">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 2|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 2]] places the mission to [[Quarmendy]] during the [[Clone Wars]] and prior to the [[siege of Hisseen]], which [[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 1|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 1]] establishes is prior to the [[Battle of Christophsis]]. Since the [[First Battle of Geonosis|beginning]] of the Clone Wars and the Battle of [[Christophsis]] are dated to [[22 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'', the mission to Quarmendy must take place in that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> D-wing air support droids were used by [[Minister]] [[Wat Tambor]] at the floating city [[Nexus]] to defend the city from infiltration. However, the [[clone trooper]] [[squad]] called the [[Wolfpack]], consisting of [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]], [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Sinker]], [[Clone Corporal]] [[Comet]], and clone trooper [[Boost]] were able to penetrate the defenses of the Nexus and rescue the city's [[Custodian of the Nexus|custodian]], [[Orkle]].<ref name="Battle Tales 2" /> Later, an entry on D1 battle droids written by [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]] was included in "[[Neyo's Almanac of Clankers]]," a survival compendium sent out by Commander [[Neyo]] that listed notable battle droids.<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />

Towards the end of the Clone War during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]], the [[clone commando]]s of [[Clone Force 99]] led by [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] of the [[501st Legion]] infiltrated the Techno Union's base to rescue the captured and thought to be killed in action [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Echo]]. D-wing droids were deployed to [[Death|kill]] the intruders, but ultimately failed as Echo was successfully rescued. After the rescue squad fled to the local [[Poletec]] village, more D-wing droids&mdash;along with two [[Octuptarra tri-droid|octuptarra magna tri-droids]]&mdash;engaged the Republic forces in combat. When the Republic was aided by the local Poletecs, they defeated the Separatist forces.<ref name="On the Wings" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|They're flying battle droids. I thought 'why not?'. I wanted to make them fly. They are cool and they are kinda like little dinosaurs.|Dave Filoni|<ref name="Celeb">{{SWYouTube|HrulKkPjFms|Bad Batch Star Wars: The Clone Wars Panel &#124; Star Wars Celebration Anaheim|sw_url=video/bad-batch-star-wars-the-clone-wars-panel-star-wars-celebration-anaheim}}</ref>}}
D-wing air support droids first appeared in the unfinished version of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[A Distant Echo]]." The droids again made an appearance in the unfinished version of "[[On the Wings of Keeradaks]]." [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|Finished versions]] of these episodes began release in [[February]] of [[2020]] on [[Disney+]], in which the D-wings made their first appearance in a completed render.

The droids were conceived by series supervising director [[Dave Filoni]] who did a first sketch of them before he gave the task over to [[Kilian Plunkett]] and his team who tried multiple designs for them<ref name="Celeb" /> with an illustration of their final design done by [[Carlos Sanchez]].<ref name="Concept Art" />
