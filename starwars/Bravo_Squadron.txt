{{Quote|Bravo Squadron were tasked with protecting Naboo. When the Trade Federation attacked the planet, Bravo Squadron put up a valiant defense of the planet!|[[Zaina]]|<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>}}
'''Bravo Squadron''',<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> also referred to as '''Bravo Flight''',<ref name="Naboo royal guards SWE">{{SWE|groups|nabooroyalguards|Naboo royal guards}}</ref> was the [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] that took part in the [[Battle of Naboo]] during the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]] of the [[planet]].<ref name="Episode I" />

[[Ric Olié]] flew as [[Bravo Leader]] during the battle, leading the squadron against the Federation's [[Vuutun Palaa|Droid Control Ship]] and [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]]. [[Porro Dolphe]] flew as [[Bravo Two]], [[Arven Wendik]] flew as [[Bravo 3]],<ref name="Episode I" /> [[Dineé Ellberger]] flew as [[Bravo Five]],<ref name="X-Wing">{{FFGXW2|set=Naboo Royal N-1 Starfighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Dineé Ellberger}}</ref> and [[Gavyn Sykes]] flew as [[Bravo Six]].<ref name="KOTOBUKIYA">[[ARTFX +]] {{C|set: [https://www.kotobukiya.co.jp/product/product-0000001554 R2-C4]}}</ref>
