'''Anteevy''' was an terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="TRK" /> located in the [[Anteevy system]] in the [[Esuain sector]],<ref name="Appendix" /> on the edge of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the Empire maintained a facility known as [[Ice Station Beta]] on Anteevy. When a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|group]] of dissidents [[Mission to Murkhana|stole]] the Imperial [[corvette]] ''[[Carrion Spike]]'' from [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] and used it to [[Attack on Galidraan Station|attack]] [[Galidraan Station]], Anteevy and its Imperial facility were considered as potential targets for the [[Starship|ship]]-jackers. They, along with [[Lantillies]] and a [[Cartao Imperial facility|facility]] on [[Cartao]], were listed as theoretical targets by [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]] of [[Naval Intelligence Agency|Naval Intelligence]] when he was questioned on the situation by [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="TRK" />
