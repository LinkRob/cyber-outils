{{Quote|&mdash;the ''Sword of Khashyun''. Legend has it that it was forged by a sect of Dowutin Sith warriors who believed lightsabers were for the weak. They believed that only a truly worthy warrior would have the strength to wield it.|Chelli Lona Aphra|<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 4" />}}
A '''sect''' of the [[Sith]] order consisted of [[Dowutin]] [[Soldier|warriors]] who believed that [[lightsaber]]s were for the weak. Therefore, they created the [[Sword of Khashyun]], which they thought could only be wielded by the strongest and worthiest [[Sith warrior|warriors]]. Legend had it that the [[sword]] bore a curse that forced the cultists turn on each other. Eventually, the cultists built a [[Sith temple (Sword of Khashyun)|temple]] on [[Moraband]] where the sword was put to rest between the feet of a [[Mummy|mummified]] [[Unidentified Dowutin Sith|Dowutin Sith]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 4" />
