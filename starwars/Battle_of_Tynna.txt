A '''battle''' took place over the [[Expansion Region/Legends|Expansion Region]] [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Tynna/Legends|Tynna]] during the [[Yuuzhan Vong War]] in [[25 ABY/Legends|25 ABY]]. The battle was not fought for a specific strategic target; rather, the [[Extragalactic/Legends|extragalactic]] [[Yuuzhan Vong]] launched the attack in an attempt to ensure that the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] believed their captured intelligence that the next Yuuzhan Vong attack would be either [[Corellia/Legends|Corellia]] or [[Bothawui/Legends|Bothawui]]. The Yuuzhan Vong hoped that the New Republic would deplete their forces protecting both planets, when they in fact planned to attack [[Fondor/Legends|Fondor]] next. While a New Republic [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighter]] contingent was posted over Tynna, it was quickly defeated by the invaders.<ref name="Jedi Eclipse" />

Having done away with its defenders, the Yuuzhan Vong made planetfall on Tynna and landed at the [[Tanallay Surge complex]].<ref name="Jedi Eclipse" /> Tricked by the collaborationist [[Peace Brigade]] into surrendering without mounting any resistance,<ref name="NJOSource">''[[The New Jedi Order Sourcebook]]''</ref> a group of several hundred unarmed [[Tynna/Legends|Tynnans]] awaited the Yuuzhan Vong at the Surge complex. However, commanders [[Nas Choka]] and [[Malik Carr]] instead oversaw the release of a [[Yuuzhan Vong chemical|chemical]] into the local [[Water/Legends|waters]] with a view to [[Terraforming/Legends|transforming]] Tynna into a biological production facility. Designed to augment the water for the production of [[dovin basal]]s and [[villip]]s, the chemical was deployed at a lagoon near the Surge complex and quickly spread across Tynna, turning the planet's blue seas yellow. The events were broadcast to every [[City/Legends|city]] on Tynna, and were also witnessed by New Republic intelligence officers [[Belindi Kalenda]] and [[Showolter]], who observed the conquest from a [[LightStealth-18 Reconnaissance Ship]] nearby.<ref name="Jedi Eclipse" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Tynna first appeared in the novel ''[[The New Jedi Order: Agents of Chaos II: Jedi Eclipse]]'', which was written by [[James Luceno]] and released in [[2000]].<ref name="Jedi Eclipse" />
