A '''Datarec''' was a hand-held device used in the [[Unknown Regions]] to display information. The [[Agbui]] [[Haplif]] owned a datarec while conducting a [[Jixtus's plan|plot to destroy the Chiss Ascendancy]] on behalf of the [[Grysk Hegemony]]. Haplif used his datarec to display information about [[planet]]s of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

When Haplif met the [[Grysk]] agent "[[Jixtus]]" on the planet [[Shihon]] during the [[Grand Migration]] of that planet's [[bird]]s, both men carried datarecs.<ref name="Greater Good" />
