'''Arkie''' was an individual who ran a [[beach]]front [[Arkie's|store]]<ref name="Andor7" /> near several resorts on<ref name="Andor7Audio">{{Andor|Announcement}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> the [[planet]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|niamos|Niamos}}</ref> [[Niamos]].<ref name="Andor7" /> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|107}}</ref> [[Windi]], a [[human]] who was staying in a nearby [[Niamos hotel|hotel]] with fellow human [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], asked Andor to visit Arkie's when he went out for a walk. When Andor said that he planned to go in the opposite direction, she informed him that Arkie had new flavors and requested that he also purchase [[Peezo]]s and [[Color|green]] [[Revnog]], as they were out of both. Andor agreed but was arrested by the [[Galactic Empire]] outside of Arkie's before he could enter.<ref name="Andor7" />
