'''CryonCorp''' was a company that manufactured various weapon attachments. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], available CryonCorp products included the [["Heads-Up" Scanner Suite]] and [[PL-1 "Second Sight" Scope]], both models of weapon attachment,<ref name="SM" /> as well as the [[SE-90 Structural Engineering Scanner]], [[EnhanceScan general purpose scanner]],<ref name="GaG" /> [[EnhanceScan 3.1]] [[portable scanner]]s.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
CryonCorp was mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] sourcebook ''[[Lead by Example]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Lead">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref> The company originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[Cracken's Rebel Field Guide: Scanners]]'', an article published in ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal 4]]'' in [[1994]].<ref name="AJ4">{{JournalCite|4|Cracken's Rebel Field Guide: Scanners}}</ref>
