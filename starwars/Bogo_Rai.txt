'''Bogo Rai''' was an astronomical object located within the territory of the [[Unknown Regions]]' [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nation.<ref name="Lesser Evil map" /> It was situated in grid square E-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], within the [[Bogo Rai system]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Bogo Rai was depicted on a [[:File:Chiss_Ascendancy_map.jpg|map]] provided by publisher [[Del Rey]] as a bonus for preordering<ref name="Lesser Evil map" /> the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final volume in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|''Ascendancy Trilogy'']].<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Bogo Rai system]] was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
