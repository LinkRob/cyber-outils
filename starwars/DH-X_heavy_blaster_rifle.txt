The '''DH-X heavy blaster rifle''' was a cumbersome model of long-range [[heavy blaster rifle]] that was designed to punch a hole the size of a [[smashball]] through any target within sight. The weapon had a short and squat [[barrel]] with a [[durasteel]] finish and a long, rectangular emitter nozzle. This was unusual for a heavy [[blaster]], as most had designs with long, thin barrels. DH-Xs came standard with a [[Stock|buttstock]] made of solid composite alloy and a [[monocular targeting optics suite]].<ref name="G&G" />

The DH-X heavy blaster rifle was manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]] and was one of the company's heaviest [[Blaster rifle|rifles]], costing 1,900 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. It was popular with [[Mercenary|mercenaries]], especially with larger [[species]] such as [[Wookiee]]s and [[Barabel]]s who could wield the large weapon with ease. Due to its size, smaller or weaker users would attach miniature [[repulsor pod]]s onto the DH-X to aid them in using it. Others mounted the weapon on a vehicle such as a [[speeder bike]] sidecar.<ref name="G&G" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the DH-X heavy blaster rifle was mentioned in the [[2019]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="G&G" /> The [[DH-X heavy blaster rifle/Legends|weapon]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was mentioned inthe [[2014]] ''Star Wars Roleplaying'' supplement ''[[Dangerous Covenants]]'', also published by Fantasy Flight Games.<ref name="DC">''[[Dangerous Covenants]]''</ref>
