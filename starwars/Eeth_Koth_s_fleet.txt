During the [[Clone Wars]], a '''fleet''' served within the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Navy|naval forces]] under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Eeth Koth]], who led the [[fleet]] aboard his [[flagship]], the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Venator-class)|''Steadfast'']].<ref name="Clone Wars" /> In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Grievous Intrigue]]" to [[21 BBY]].</ref> following an [[Second Battle of Geonosis|engagement]] on the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]], Koth and his fleet were ordered to locate those responsible for [[Attacks on hyperspace routes|attacks]] near [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[hyperspace route]]s.<ref name="Clone Wars" />

However, while moving through the [[Saleucami system]], the ''Steadfast'' was [[Capture of Eeth Koth|attacked]] by the [[Grievous's Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class light destroyer]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance's]] [[General]] [[Grievous]]. Thus, Grievous boarded the [[Star Destroyer]], leading to Koth's capture after a brief [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] aboard the ''Steadfast''.<ref name="Grievous">{{TCW|Grievous Intrigue}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Eeth Koth's fleet was introduced in "[[Grievous Intrigue]],"<ref name="Grievous" /> the ninth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which aired on [[January 1]], [[2010]].<ref name="EpGuide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode209.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Grievous Intrigue}}</ref> It was later described as a fleet in "[[The Clone Wars Begin]]," an issue of [[De Agostini]]'s [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] line<ref name="Clone Wars" /> published on [[May 18]], [[2021]].<ref name="De Agostini">{{DeAgostini|lang=es|url=libros-comics/enciclopedia-star-wars|text=Official Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref>
