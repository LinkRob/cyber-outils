'''Flikflak''' was a board game played with pieces.<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[Villarandi]] owner of the [[Cantina|tapbar]] [[Enlightenment]] [[Kradon Minst]] offered the [[Evereni]] [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] [[Children of the Open Hand|member]] [[Yana Ro]] a game of flikflak. After pointing out his lack of all the pieces, Yana Ro offered him a new set in exchange for [[Information broker|information]].<ref name="Path of Vengeance" />
