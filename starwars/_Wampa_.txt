"'''Wampa'''" was the nickname of a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] who served within the [[Galactic Empire]]. As a member of the [[Black Squadron (Galactic Empire)|Black Squadron]], the pilot flew a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]] under the callsign [[Black Eleven]] during the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="X-wing2" /> At some point during the [[Galactic Civil War]], the pilot participated in an attack on an [[MC-series star cruiser]].<ref name="X-wing" />
