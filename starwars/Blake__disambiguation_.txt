'''Blake''' may refer to the following:

==[[Canon]]==
*'''[[Blake]]''', a [[human]] [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Lieutenant]] during the early [[Imperial Era]] who took over [[law enforcement agency|police]] operations on [[Janix]].

==[[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]]==
*'''[[Adran Blake]]''', a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Human/Legends|Human]] who worked as a cook in the [[Space Monkey Café]] on [[Cularin/Legends|Cularin]].
*'''[[Blake (Rebel)]]''', a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] at [[Echo Base/Legends|Echo Base]] during the [[Battle of Hoth/Legends|Battle of Hoth]], who later became a [[General/Legends|general]] in the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]].

==[[In-universe|Out-of-universe]]==
*'''[[Alex Blake]]''', an actor who portrayed [[Flob]] in ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.
*'''[[Alyssa Blake]]''', a visual effects production coordinator for [[Industrial Light & Magic]] on [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].
*'''[[Jerome Blake]]''', an actor who portrayed a number [[alien]] characters in the [[prequel trilogy]], including [[Mas Amedda]], [[Orn Free Taa]], [[Rune Haako]], [[Graxol Kelvyyn]], [[Oppo Rancisis]], [[Mik Regrap]], and [[Horox Ryyder]].
*'''[[Nick Blake]]''', a model maker in the Minature Construction and Photography Unit on [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].
*'''[[Paul Blake]]''', an [[Wikipedia:English people|English]] actor who portrayed the [[bounty hunter]] [[Greedo]] in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].
*'''[[Tom Blake]]''', character designer who worked on creature concept design for the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of [[Star Wars: Andor|''Andor'']].
*'''[[Blake Cooper]]''', a musician on the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]''.
*'''[[Blake Henriksen]]''', an artist who provided illustrations for several cards in ''[[Star Wars: The Card Game]]''.
*'''[[Blake Lee]]''', a lighting and compositing artist on ''[[LEGO Star Wars Summer Vacation]]''.
*'''[[Blake Sweeney]]''', a digital effects artist for [[Industrial Light & Magic]] on [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].
*'''[[Blake Tucker]]''', an actor who voiced a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] in ''[[Star Wars: Rebel Assault II: The Hidden Empire]]''.
*'''Alexene Farol Fallmuth''', who is known by the pen name '''[[Olivie Blake]]''', and who authored the short story titled "[[Then Fall, Sidious]]"  published in the anthology book ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]''.
