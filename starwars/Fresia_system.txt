The '''Fresia system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Torranix sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It fell within grid square K-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the [[planet]] [[Fresia]], [[homeworld]] of the [[human]] [[Rebel pilot]] [[Jaldine Gerams]]<ref name="Geektionary" /> and the fierce creature known as the [[Coromon Headhunter]].<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|59|Starship Fact File}}</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], Fresia was represented in the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] by [[Senator]] [[Seti Ashgad]] until Ashgad vanished amidst an increase in [[death]] threats and [[assassin]]ation attempts on all senators.<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> At some point between [[19 BBY]] and [[5 BBY]],<ref name="RaidDate">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'' establishes that the [[raid on an Incom warehouse]] took place between the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and the events of "[[Narkina 5 (episode)|Narkina 5]]." The book dates the Empire's formation and the events of "Narkina 5" to [[19 BBY]] and [[5 BBY]], respectively.</ref> the [[partisans]] of resistance fighter [[Saw Gerrera]] carried out a violent [[Raid on an Incom warehouse|raid]] on an [[Incom Corporation]] [[Unidentified Incom Corporation warehouse|warehouse]] on Fresia and stole eight [[X-wing starfighter]]s.<ref name="DRVG">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Fresia system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Fresia/Legends|Fresia]] was located in the [[Fre'ji system]], which was first mentioned in the [[2003]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' supplement ''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]'', which was published by [[Wizards of the Coast]].<ref name="CatCW">''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]''</ref> It first appeared in the [[2006]] [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]''.<ref name="EaW">''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]''</ref>
