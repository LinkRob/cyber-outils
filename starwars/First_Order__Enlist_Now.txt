'''First Order: Enlist Now''' was a [[First Order]] [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] used for recruitment. In [[34 ABY]], [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] placed a [[hologram]] of the poster on the bar of [[Aunt Z's Tavern]] on the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]'', despite the owner [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza]]'s objections. She was later taken [[prisoner]] by the First Order after refusing to display the propaganda.

==Description==
First Order: Enlist Now was the title of a [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]]<ref name="Bucket's List">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-disappeared-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 6 Fun Facts from "The Disappeared" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref> used for recruitment by the [[First Order]]. It depicted a line of three [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] in [[First Order stormtrooper armor|armor]] beneath the words "First Order" and the organization's [[First Order insignia|emblem]] and above the words "Enlist Now," with all text [[Writing|written]] in [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="The Disappeared" />

==History==
{{Quote|Remember, each of you could have a future in the First Order if you want it.''"<br />"''Don't ya get it? We're not interested. Go find your recruits somewhere else!|A stormtrooper and Z'Vk'Thkrkza as she rejects the poster|audio=TroopersAuntZRecruits-TheDisappeared.flac|<ref name="The Disappeared" />}}
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|1|18}}</ref> a [[Unidentified First Order stormtrooper (Aunt Z's Tavern)|pair]] of [[Unidentified First Order stormtrooper (First Order: Enlist Now)|stormtroopers]] placed a [[datapad]] emitting a [[Color|red]] [[hologram]] of the poster on the bar of [[Aunt Z's Tavern]] on the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]'' on the [[Planet|world]] [[Castilon]]. The [[Cantina|tavern's]] owner,<ref name="The Disappeared" /> [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza]],<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Ace Squadron}}</ref> who was also known as Aunt Z, turned off the poster and claimed it did not fit in with the decor, but the [[trooper]]s turned it back on and explained that its display was not optional.<ref name="The Disappeared" />

[[File:TheDisappeared-EpisodeGuide.jpg|thumb|right|300px|alt=Two First Order stormtroopers slid a holoprojector across the bar at Aunt Z's to an unimpressed looking Aunt Z. The holoprojector displays a red poster with the First Order emblem and three First Order stormtroopers on it.|Z'Vk'Thkrkza rejected showing First Order: Enlist Now at her tavern.]]
The stormtroopers later returned to the tavern after learning that a discussion with anti&ndash;First Order sentiments was taking place there. After dismissing the concerns of patrons [[Kazuda Xiono]], [[Tamara Ryvora]], [[Torra Doza]], and [[Neeku Vozo]] about residents of the ''Colossus'' being "disappeared" by the First Order, the troopers tried to recruit some of those present. Aunt Z then angrily informed the stormtroopers that they were not interested and turned off the hologram of the poster, throwing it back to the stormtroopers.<ref name="The Disappeared" />

The troopers did not respond and left with the datapad,<ref name="The Disappeared" /> but the action left Aunt Z firmly in the crosshairs of the First Order<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Ace Squadron}}</ref> and they took her [[prisoner]] soon after.<ref name="The Disappeared" /> The [[hospitality droid]] [[G1-7CH]] was left to manage the tavern alone and accepted all propaganda required,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> with three holograms of the poster left along the bar. When Xiono and Doza came back in search of Aunt Z, G1-7CH gestured to the posters in explanation of where she had gone.<ref name="The Disappeared" />

The First Order: Enlist Now poster first appeared in "[[The Disappeared]],"<ref name="The Disappeared" /> the eighteenth episode of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''{{'s}} [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]],<ref name="Number">{{InsiderCite|190|Resistance Revisited|''Resistance'' Revisited}}</ref> on [[February 24]], [[2019]].<ref name="Air date">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> Prior to the release of the episode, the poster was first shown in a preview clip released on the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel on [[February 20]], 2019.<ref name="YouTube">{{SWYouTube|WZU2xVibTrw|Conspiracy Theory - "The Disappeared" Preview &#124; Star Wars Resistance}}</ref>
