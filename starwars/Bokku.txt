'''Bokku''' was a [[Hutt]] who served on the [[Grand Hutt Council]] of the [[Hutt Clan]] during the [[Imperial Era]],<ref name="WOTBH 1" /> operating from a [[Lair of Bokku the Hutt|lair]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. He employed [[Gamorrean]]s, [[Bith]], a [[Nikto]], and a [[Klatooinian]]. He also had regular contacts in the [[Galactic Empire]]. When the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] undertook a search for<ref name="Vader 12" /> the [[carbonite]]-frozen body of the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]], which had been lost by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] before he could deliver it to [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the Hutt,<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 1">[[War of the Bounty Hunters Alpha 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters Alpha'' 1]]</ref> Vader and the [[Sith assassin]] [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]] visited Bokku. Under Vader's command, Ochi ordered that Bokku recover Solo, promising the Hutt an increase of power. Bokku was dubious about the plan and questioned Ochi's legitimacy, leading the [[Sith Lord]] to reveal his presence as Ochi insisted that Bokku would serve Vader.<ref name="Vader 12" /> After Bokku refused to serve Vader, the Sith Lord slaughtered many of the Hutt's guards, eventually leading Bokku to the decision to help him,<ref name="Vader 13">[[Darth Vader (2020) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 13]]</ref> though he had already been working with [[Crimson Dawn]] in order to betray Jabba.<ref name="Darth Vader 15" /> In the aftermath of the [[Auction for Han Solo]] and the Hutt's [[Attack on the Executor|attack]] on the Imperial fleet, Vader slew Bokku above [[Jekara]].<ref name="Darth Vader 17" />
