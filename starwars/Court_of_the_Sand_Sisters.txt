The '''Court of the Sand Sisters''' was a courtyard located near the cliffside [[Sand Sisters' convent|convent]] of the [[Sand Sisters]] in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. Adjacent to the [[South Stretch Worker's District|Worker's District]], the courtyard was a popular spot for trysts<ref name="CLNE" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events covered on the [[Fathier]] Chase pages of ''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
