'''Felzonis''' was a [[human]] [[ISB Attendant|attendant]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Security Bureau]] (ISB). He worked within the ISB's [[Investigations]] department at their [[Imperial Security Bureau Central Office|central office]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. In [[5 BBY]], [[ISB Supervisor]] [[Dedra Meero]] found him [[sleep]]ing at his desk and, after waking him, ordered that he carry out a [[Multi-Sector Data Blend]] surveying Imperial equipment that had gone missing over the past two [[Standard year|years]].

In [[2 BBY]], Felzonis was posted at the [[Imperial Security Bureau control center|ISB control center]] in the central office, tasked with monitoring a select group of [[senator]]s following the controversial [[Ghorman Massacre]]. After noticing suspicious activity from Senator [[Mon Mothma]], the attendant raised it to the attention of Supervisor [[Lagret]]. After Lagret checked in on an [[Exmar Kloris|ISB field agent]] with Mothma, Felzonis updated the supervisor on information regarding suspected resistance from senators in the upcoming [[Imperial Senate]] session. When the session ended with Mothma speaking out against the Empire, Lagret passed orders for her arrest through Felzonis. The attendant helped keep Lagret updated with the search for the rogue senator, informing the supervisor when [[Colonel]] [[Wullf Yularen]], the ISB director, was calling in.

==Biography==
===Theft investigations===
{{Quote|Hmm. I need a Multi-Sector Data Blend going back two years. They survey pool is Imperial naval bases and repair facilities. I want a record of all missing avionics, comm, navigation, and targeting equipment.''"<br />"''For which star system?''"<br />"''All star systems. Is that a problem?|Deedra Meero and Felzonis|<ref name="Announcement" />}}
During the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Felzonis served as an [[ISB Attendant|attendant]] in<ref name="Announcement" /> the [[Investigations]] branch<ref name="Dawn Guide">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> of the Empire's [[Imperial Security Bureau]] (ISB).<ref name="Announcement" /> He<ref name="Databank" /> was stationed in the [[ISB datavault]] within the [[Imperial Security Bureau Central Office|ISB Central Office]] on the Imperial [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Announcement" /> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="5 BBY">{{AndorDate|107}}</ref> in the aftermath of a [[Aldhani heist|rebel heist]], [[ISB Supervisor]] [[Dedra Meero]] visited the datavault to further her investigations into stolen Imperial equipment. Felzonis was [[Sleep|asleep]] in a seat behind a reception desk at his<ref name="Announcement AD">{{Andor|Announcement}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> station when Meero entered the room. He awoke with a start when she addressed him and corrected his [[Clothing|tunic]] when she pointed out that it hung open.<ref name="Announcement" />

[[File:Isb-datavault.png|thumb|left|300px|Felzonis was ordered by Dedra Meero to run a Multi-Sector Data Blend.]]
After taking his name, Meero ordered the attendant to run a [[Multi-Sector Data Blend]] surveying avionics, comms, navigation, and targeting equipment that had gone missing from [[Imperial Navy|Imperial naval]] bases and repair facilities across all [[star system]]s over the past two [[Standard year|years]]. Upon his compliance to this order, the supervisor further informed him that the report would also have to be confidential, with a copy sent only to her office. Felzonis promised that that he could be relied upon before Meero left.<ref name="Announcement" />

After receiving the report, Meero put together a document detailing what she believed to be a organized [[Axis network|rebel effort]] to [[Thief|steal]] highly-restricted Imperial equipment across [[the galaxy]]; however, despite Felzonis's promise, ISB Supervisor [[Blevin]] became aware of the Data Blend she had requested. He reported this as potentially breaching the [[Sector Protocols]] outlined in the [[ISB Code of Conduct]] during a meeting of the [[ISB Board]], pointing out that she had previously been reprimanded for overreach into other supervisor's sectors. Meero used the recently enacted [[Imperial Emergency Act]] to justify her accessing of other sector's data without filing a request or official sanction, ultimately earning the approval of her and Blevin's superior [[Major]] [[Lio Partagaz]] by explaining her theory.<ref name="Announcement" />

===Senate surveillance===
====Suspicious activity====
{{Quote|The technicians said there's no malfunction or communication issues on our end. Right here, these are all Senators the Select Council have approved for increased surveillance. It's always a bit quiet this early, but&hellip; that's Senator Mothma. It's gone dead. Plus the driver, it seemed worth mentioning.|Felzonis briefs Lagret|<ref name="Welcome" />}}
In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="2 BBY">{{AndorDate|209}}</ref> Felzonis was stationed in the [[Imperial Security Bureau control center|ISB control center]] within the central office. Following the highly controversial [[Ghorman Massacre]]<ref name="Welcome" />&mdash;an Imperial-engineered event where the Empire [[Death|killed]] many [[Ghorman (people)|Ghorman]] civilians<ref name="Who">{{Andor|Who Are You?}}</ref>&mdash;Felzonis and other control room staff were told to pay particular attention to the [[Imperial Senate]] over the coming [[Standard day|days]], with the [[Select Committee|Select Council]],<ref name="Welcome" /> a committee that the ISB took orders from,<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> approving the increased surveillance of a number of [[senator]]s. With [[listening device]]s planted in various senators' offices, Felzonis monitored them from the control center. Senators [[Ton]] and [[Mon Mothma]] notably discovered these devices, with the latter choosing to destroy it.<ref name="Welcome" />

[[File:Felzonis-tech-Andor209.png|thumb|right|300px|Felzonis has a technician investigate the bug in Mon Mothma's office.]]
Felzonis immediately noticed the lack of response from the bug in Mothma's office, calling over a [[Unidentified console tech|technician]] to investigate the matter, establishing that the problem was not on the ISB's end. Additionally, [[Exmar Kloris]], an ISB field [[agent]] undercover as Mothma's driver, reported that the senator had not left the Senate that night. As a result, Felzonis called for an ISB supervisor's attention. Supervisor [[Lagret]] soon arrived, the attendant briefing him on the suspicious activity around Mothma and the increased attention on the Senate. After Lagret asked to see a console, Felzonis showed on the screen how Mothma's bug had gone dead.<ref name="Welcome" />

====Clash of agents====
{{Quote|They're at Gate 9. They've killed Jung's agent.|Felzonis reports Beska's death to Lagret|<ref name="Welcome" />}}
[[File:Felzonis-Lagret-Andor209.png|thumb|left|300px|Felzonis brought Mothma to Lagret's attention.]]
As the morning began, Felzonis stood by Lagret as the latter contacted Kloris for a report on Mothma, learning that she was likely still in the Senate. Later that morning, as an emergency Senate session on Ghorman was set to take place, the attendant picked up rumors that prominent senators may try to challenge the Empire's narrative on the massacre. Felzonis informed Lagret as such, but was assured by the supervisor that the [[Chair of the Senate]] would not allow any of them to speak. Indeed these suspicions were true, as Mothma planned to speak out on the reality of the Ghorman massacre. [[Beska]], one of ISB Supervisor [[Lonni Jung]]'s agents, infiltrated a small rebel extraction team that was there for to escort Mothma from the Senate after the planned speech.<ref name="Welcome" />

As the resulting Senate session progressed, Beska called in to the control room from the Senate building with urgent news. Lagret attempted to speak with her, but she was interrupted when one of the rebel team, [[Charval]], caught her.<ref name="Welcome" /> Felzonis watched the supervisor flog the radio after it went dead.<ref name="Welcome Script">{{Disney|subdomain=assets.debut|url=documents/Andor%20209%20--%20POST%20FINAL%20--%203.3.24%5B1%5D.pdf|text=Episode #209 "Welcome To The Rebellion" Script|format=PDF}}</ref> After killing Charval, Beska contacted Felzonis again, asking for instructions. The attendant put her back on with Lagret as a result. After Beska updated Lagret on the situation, the supervisor ordered the Senate feed to be shut down. However, two rebel sympathising [[technician]]s in the Senate sabotaged these efforts, allowing Mothma's speech to go ahead despite Lagret's flush of orders.<ref name="Welcome" />

[[File:Mothma-doesnt-leave-Andor209.png|thumb|right|300px|Lagret gives orders for Mothma's arrest to Felzonis.]]
After Mothma berated the Empire's actions on Ghorman, Felzonis went to Lagret, who ordered that the senator be arrested before she can leave the building. As security searched the Senate building, Mothma was helped by the rebel agent [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], who killed Beska when she attempted to arrest the senator. Felzonis soon received news of the dead agent and reported such to Lagret,<ref name="Welcome" /> who was just turning to speak to him.<ref name="Welcome Script" /> Mothma and Andor continued to elude security and got away in Mothma's [[JPP-192 limospeeder]] after killing Kloris. [[Colonel]] [[Wullf Yularen]],<ref name="Welcome" /> director of the ISB,<ref name="Dawn Guide" /> soon called in to the control room, Felzonis notifying Lagret.<ref name="Welcome" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I'll need this report kept confidential and a copy sent exclusively to my office. Can I rely on you for that level of discretion?''"<br />"''Absolutely. Yes. Can do.''"<br />"''Will do.''"<br />"''Right. Get on with it.|Dedra Meero and Felzonis|<ref name="Announcement" />}}
[[File:Felzonis-informs-Andor209.png|thumb|left|300px|Felzonis was sure to keep Lagret updated at the ISB control center.]]
Felzonis was flustered when Meero woke him and did not think to correct his uniform until she pointed it out. He awoke with a start when the supervisor caught him sleeping and hastily corrected his tunic when it was pointed out. The attendant also corrected his words repeatedly, alternating uncertainly between referring to his superior as sir and ma'am.<ref name="Announcement" /> Felzonis nervously pursed his [[Mouth|lips]]<ref name="Announcement AD" /> after Meero corrected his "can do" to "will do." Despite his promises of discretion, he failed to prevent Blevin from learning of his work.<ref name="Announcement" />

At the control center, Felzonis was diligent and was sure to note any suspicious activity following the Ghorman Massacre<ref name="Databank" /> to Lagret when it came about. A [[human]], Felzonis had dark skin, [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s, and black [[hair]].<ref name="Welcome" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|At ease, Attendant.''"<br />"''Yes, sir. Ma'am. Sir.''"<br />"''Your tunic.''"<br />"''Sorry, sir. Ma'am. Sorry.|Dedra Meero catches Felzonis sleeping|<ref name="Announcement" />}}
Felzonis wore a light gray [[Imperial officer's uniform]] with two blue pips on his [[rank insignia plaque]], typical of ISB Attendants.<ref name="Announcement" /> At the control center he additionally wore a black [[Imperial kepi]], donned a [[code cylinder]] on his chest under each shoulder, and used a console equipped with a head set.<ref name="Welcome" />
