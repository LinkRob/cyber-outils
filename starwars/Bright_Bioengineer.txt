The '''Bright Bioengineer''' was the standard outfit owned by the [[Twi'lek]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Tuya]] that she wore for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bright Bioengineer was a "Base" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Tuya, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 6.0.0 alongside Season 6 on [[April 15]], [[2025]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Prototype Launcher]]. This costume was unlocked by default when Tuya became playable.<ref name="Hunters" />
