Christine Galey is an actor with several uncredited roles in ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]''. Most notably, she performed inside [[R2-D2]] in ''The Book of Boba Fett,'' the first woman to play the role.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.chameleonmediaproductions.com/christine-galey|text=Christine Galey|work=Chameleon Media|archivedate=20231005033635}}</ref>
