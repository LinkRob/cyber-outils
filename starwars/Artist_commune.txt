An anti-violence '''artist commune''' was founded by the [[Gender|woman]] [[Celena]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Nooroyo]]. During the [[Republic Era]], five [[Torada Collective]] [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] [[artist]]s settled in the commune, including [[Antraya]]&mdash;the sister of the planet [[Naboo]]'s [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Jamillia]]&mdash;and the man [[Kharl]].

After the [[First Battle of Geonosis|onset]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] in [[22 BBY]], Jamillia [[Mission to Nooroyo|sent]] Naboo's [[senator]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], to retrieve the artists and bring them back to Naboo. In the commune, Amidala and her secret paramour, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], met with Celena, who brought them to speak with Kharl. Though the other artists ultimately refused to leave, Antraya reluctantly departed the commune to reunite with Jamillia on Naboo.

==Description==
{{Quote|Residents can't be unreasonably messy in their quarters, and they must help maintain the gardens and such while they stay here.|Celena, about the artist commune|<ref name="QH" />}}
An artist commune was located north of [[Nooro-City]] on<ref name="QH" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Nooroyo system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Nooroyo]]. The commune featured several [[Color|bright blue]] houses near the edge of a river. The residential complex featured copious amounts of natural [[Plant|flora]] surrounding its walkways and [[garden]]s. The artist commune was surrounded by a sculpted [[metal]] fence and had at least one landing pad.<ref name="QH" />

==History==
{{Quote|Our task is to bring you all back to Naboo. I can understand that you are reluctant, but if the Separatists are here for spice, it's only a matter of time before this planet is swamped with fighting. It won't just be the droids and the Republic, either. Criminals will get involved. We can't force you to do anything, but I would appreciate it if you would make our case to your friends.''"<br />"''It'll be a hard sell. Most of us left Naboo for a reason, even if the reasons aren’t all the same.|Padmé Amidala and Kharl|<ref name="QH" />}}
During the [[Republic Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Gender|woman]] [[Celena]] established an anti-violence artist commune on Nooroyo. While living in its houses, [[artist]]s adopted a peaceful lifestyle as they practiced their crafts. Residents were also expected to maintain the artist commune's gardens and abide by its other rules, such as those surrounding the [[Foodstuff|food]] they [[Diet|consumed]], with any disputes being handled by Celena. At some point, five [[Torada Collective]] [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] artists&mdash;including the man [[Kharl]] and the woman [[Antraya]], who was the sister of the planet [[Naboo]]'s incumbent [[Monarch of Naboo|queen]], [[Jamillia]]&mdash;left their [[homeworld]] to join the commune. On Nooroyo, the Naboo artists integrated into the commune under Celena's leadership and formed an attachment to their newfound home, with Kharl helping teach [[Youngling|children]] about paint colors and preserving plants.<ref name="QH" />

[[File:Jamillia-large.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Jamillia (pictured) sent Padmé Amidala to bring five Naboo Torada Collective artists back to Naboo.]]
Following the [[First Battle of Geonosis|start]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="QH" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Jamillia and the [[parent]]s of the five Naboo artists living on the planet worried for their safety due to the onset of the conflict, prompting the [[queen]] to send Naboo's [[senator]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], to bring them back to their homeworld. Amidala and her secret paramour, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Mission to Nooroyo|traveled]] to Nooroyo, and after informing the [[Nooroyo primary landing control]] of their destination, they were permitted to land at the residential complex. Celena welcomed the pair, though her demeanor stiffened upon learning the reason for their visit. The woman led them through the complex and explained the rules its residents followed before eventually entering a house to find the Torada Collective artists they sought.<ref name="QH" />

At Celena's behest, Kharl met with the pair and insisted that Nooroyo was now their home, expounding on his own personal reason for living on the world&mdash;to acquire medicinal [[spice]] to deal with chronic pain. After the man spoke with Amidala further, he panicked upon realizing who she was and informed her of the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Viceroy of the Trade Federation|Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]]'s presence on Nooroyo for spice negotiations, which put her in danger due to the [[Neimoidian]]'s several [[Death|attempts]] on her life over the past [[Standard decade|decade]]. As Amidala and Skywalker fled back to their [[Padmé Amidala's H-type Nubian yacht (Malevolence)|Nubian yacht]], Kharl left and then returned with a reluctant Antraya, with the latter having agreed to flee the artist commune while the other artists chose to remain. After departing the commune, the pair successfully reunited Jamillia with Antraya on Naboo.<ref name="QH" />
