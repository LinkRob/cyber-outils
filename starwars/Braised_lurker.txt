'''Braised lurker''' was a [[Foodstuff|dish]] that was made the meat of a [[lurker]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> a type of [[amphibian]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Judakann]].<ref name="Freefall">''[[Poe Dameron: Free Fall]]''</ref> It was used to make [[Lurker Stew]] by the [[Rokana Raiders]] [[pirate]] group while they were based at the [[Khepi Tomb]] in the [[Wild Space]] [[Sayma Nebula]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{GameMechanics}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] received a portion of the [[stew]] while she was disguised as a Rokana Raider.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}
