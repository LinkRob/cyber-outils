'''2017 Topps ''Star Wars'' Galactic Files Reborn''' is a trading card set released by [[Topps]] on [[July 19]], [[2017]]. It is the sequel to Topps' [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[2012 Topps Star Wars Galactic Files Series 1|2012 Topps ''Star Wars'' Galactic Files Series 1]] and [[2013 Topps Star Wars Galactic Files Series 2|2013 Topps ''Star Wars'' Galactic Files Series 2]] and comes with 200 new base cards. Each base card has lore text on the reverse side.
