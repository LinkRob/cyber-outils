'''Florinda Jackard''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[scientist]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. After the man [[Nilsson Summach]] came up with a prototype for the [[47 lightsaber nullifier|four-seven lightsaber nullifier]], Jackard [[Thief|stole]] the device, leading to a search for her between [[Nilsson's crew]] and the [[Jedi]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] and [[Indara]].<ref name="Wayseeker" />
