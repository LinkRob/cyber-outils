'''Belson Dool''', called '''Blinkers''' by [[Gender/Legends|his]] friends, was a male [[Sullustan/Legends|Sullustan]] [[Technician/Legends|technician]] who lived during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="AED" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:TemplateCommoner.png|250px|thumb|Every possible depiction of Belson Dool]]
Dialogue pertaining to Belson Dool appeared in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online roleplaying game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']]. However, he never actually appeared on the [[Star Wars video games|video game]]'s live servers as a proper [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]].<ref name="AED" />
