The '''Fourth Division''', also known as the '''Fourth Fleet''',<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> was a cell of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] operational after the [[Battle of Hoth]]. The Fourth Division attempted to group at [[Rendezvous Point Delta-Three]], but was tracked by the [[Galactic Empire]] when it attempted to contact the [[Eighth Division]], and was [[blockade]]d against a [[star]], preventing its escape and resulting in the loss of one ship, the ''[[Tyvarex]]'', to heavy fire. The ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' arrived at the rendezvous point and broke the blockade enough for the surviving ships of the Fourth Division to escape.<ref name="Star Wars 1" />

The Fourth Division was composed of at least three [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]]s, including the ''[[Redemption]]'' and the ''Tyvarex'', at least four [[CR90 corvette]]s, at least two [[GR-75 medium transport]]s<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> that included the ''[[Bright Hope]]'',<ref name="Fourth Division">[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 20|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 20]] establishes that the ''Bright Hope'' was present at the [[Backup Rendezvous Point Gamma-Nine]] in {{Film|V}}. [[Star Wars (2020) 1|''Star Wars'' (2020) 1]] establishes that the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[starship]]s present at Backup Rendezvous Point Gamma-Nine were part of the Fourth Division. Therefore, the ''Bright Hope'' must have been part of the Fourth Division.</ref> multiple [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing]], [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wing]], and [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor|A-wing]] [[starfighter]]s, and several other ships of indeterminate design.<ref name="Star Wars 1" />
