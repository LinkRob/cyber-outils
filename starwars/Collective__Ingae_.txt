The '''Collective''' was a group of academics and [[scientist]]s founded in the aftermath of the [[Nihil conflict]] by individuals including [[Avon Sunvale]]. The group fled the most populous parts of [[the galaxy]] to the [[Wild Space]] [[planet]] [[Ingae]] so they could pursue scientific experiments in isolation from those who would profit off their breakthroughs<ref name="Wayseeker" />
