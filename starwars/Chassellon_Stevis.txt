'''Chassellon Stevis''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] from the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] and whose mother was head of the [[Coruscant diplomatic legation]].<ref name="Leia" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] birth in [[19 BBY]]. The events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' commence just after Organa's sixteenth [[birthday]], which places the events of the novel in [[3 BBY]].</ref> Stevis was a part of the [[Apprentice Legislature]] and was in the same [[pathfinding]] class as [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. During the first pathfinding class Stevis' fellow [[Apprentice Legislator]] [[Harp Allor]] hurt her ankle and Stevis decided to leave her behind so that he wouldn't get kicked out of the class.<ref name="Leia" />

However when Stevis returned to the [[Pathfinding class chalet|pathfinding chalet]] he saw that Harp was already there drinking [[mocoa]] with Organa and the other students who stayed behind to help her. Stevis was then reprimanded by by [[Chief]] [[Pangie]] for abandoning Harp.<ref name="Leia" />

Stevis continued to serve alongside Organa in the Apprentice Legislature, including a visit to the [[Emperor's Museum]], and agreed to remain silent when he saw Organa suspiciously trying to gain passage off Coruscant on the ship ''[[Mighty Oak Apocalypse]]'' which previously Stevis had been attempting to hire to take him to a [[speeder]] auction on [[Arkanis]]. Stevis later attended the ceremony which saw Organa officially invested as [[crown]] princess of [[Alderaan]] and winked at her as she passed. Also in attendance was another Apprentice Legislator [[Amilyn Holdo]] of [[Gatalenta]].<ref name="Leia" />

He was rather arrogant, which Organa and her [[Bail Prestor Organa|father]] noted was a common [[Coruscanti]] trait. He also had an interest in buying and refurbishing antique speeders which caused Organa to realize that he had more patience than she thought.<ref name="Leia" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chassellon Stevis first appeared in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', a [[2017]] young adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]] that was released as part of [[Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi|Journey to ''Star Wars'': ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="Leia" /> While Stevis is described as dark-skinned in the novel, the colored pages of the [[Leia Organa: Ordeal of the Princess|manga adaptation]] show him as clearly light-skinned, while the black and white pages show him as light dark-skinned.<ref name="Leia Manga">''[[Leia Organa: Ordeal of the Princess]]''</ref>
