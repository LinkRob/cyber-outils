'''Cleat''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served as a [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and flew under the [[callsign]] [[Phoenix Four]] during the [[Attack on Lothal|Attack on]] [[Lothal]]. They were [[Death|killed]] when [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] [[Commander]] [[Vult Skerris]] shot them down with his [[TIE/D Defender Elite]] as they was attempting to break through the [[blockade]] of the [[planet]].

==Biography==
Cleat was a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[starfighter]] [[pilot]] who answered to the [[callsign]] [[Phoenix Four]] in [[1 BBY]]. That year, they flew as part of a strike team of [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s led by [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] that tried to [[Attack on Lothal|attack]] the [[Imperial Armory Complex]] and [[Lothal City fuel depot|nearby fuel depot]] on [[Lothal]]. After the rebel strike team exited [[hyperspace]], Cleat remarked that while the Imperials had numbers the rebels had talent. Cleat's sentiments were echoed by fellow rebel pilot [[Mart Mattin]] and Hera's [[C1-series astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]]. They and their fellow pilots complied with Hera's orders to stay with their wingmates.<ref name="Rebel Assault" />

The rebels were met by [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]], which deployed [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] to intercept them. Under Hera's orders, Cleat and [[Phoenix Five]] pilot [[Duke (pilot)|Duke]], went to thin out the TIE fighters. Cleat and their wingmate complied with Hera's orders and shot down a TIE fighter each. Cleat told Duke to keep up. Amidst the dogfight, the Imperial ace pilot [[Vult Skerris]] appeared in a [[TIE/D Defender Elite]] with two TIE fighters behind Duke.<ref name="Rebel Assault" />

Cleat tried to warn Duke but he was swiftly shot down by Skerris. Skerris then turned his attention to Cleat and fired on their X-wing, taking out one of their [[engine]]s. Over the [[comlink]], Cleat reported that they were losing control. Hera told Cleat to scissor right so that she could cut them off. Before Cleat could do so, Skerris fired a second laser shot at the X-wing's engines. This caused Cleat's starfighter to explode, killing them in the process. Ultimately, Hera's strike team was wiped out by Thrawn's forces and Hera herself was captured after she crash-landed in [[Capital City]].<ref name="Rebel Assault" /> Cleat's sacrifice however was not in vain since Hera and her fellow [[Spectres]] managed to destroy the Lothal City fuel depot, which also damaged the Imperial factory and knocked out TIE/D Defender production on Lothal.<ref name="DUME">{{Rebels|DUME}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Cleat was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] with light skin. They were a skilled T-65B X-wing starfighter pilot who believed that the Rebel Alliance's starfighter pilots were superior to the Empire's [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilots]] despite the asymmetry in numbers. Cleat's confidence was misplaced when they were ambushed by the Imperial ace pilot Vult Skerris' TIE/D Defender Elite.<ref name="Rebel Assault" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cleat first appeared in "[[Rebel Assault]]," the ninth episode of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Four|fourth season]] of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. They were voiced by [[Anna Graves]], who voiced [[Satine Kryze]] and [[Sugi (bounty hunter)|Sugi]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="Rebel Assault" />
