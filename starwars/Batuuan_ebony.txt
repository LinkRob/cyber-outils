'''Batuuan ebony''' was a type of ebony that could be found in [[the galaxy]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Nihil]] [[captain]] [[Melis Shryke]] stood in the reception chamber of the [[Nihil main base]] on [[Hetzal Prime]] and admired the desk of [[General]] [[Abediah Viess]] which was made from Batuuan ebony.<ref name="Eye of Darkness">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]''</ref>
