An '''abacus''' was a device with components that swung and clacked together. The [[Kupohan]] [[Azzur Nessin]] wore his [[Hair|beard]] in braided strands with [[gold]]en beads that swung against each other and clacked like an abacus when he spoke.<ref name="HttJ">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref>

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Abacuses were first established to exist in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' universe in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] sourcebook ''[[Pirates & Privateers]]'', which was written by [[Timothy S. O'Brien]] and released in [[1997]] by [[West End Games]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. They then appeared in [[canon]] in the novel ''[[Heir to the Jedi]]'', which was written by [[Kevin Hearne]] and released in [[2015]].
