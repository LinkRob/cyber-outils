'''Denise Gough''' is an Irish actress who plays [[Dedra Meero]] in the [[2022]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.

==Biography==
===Entering a galaxy far far away===
[[File:SWcom-DedraMeero.png|thumb|left|300px|Denise Gough was cast as Dedra Meero, the main antagonist of ''Andor'' (Gough as Meero, pictured).]]
When Denise Gough was a child, she saw ''[[Star Wars]]'' and had a toy that she thought was the [[DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Building Dedra">{{WebCite|url=https://www.empireonline.com/tv/features/andor-denise-gough-interview-building-star-wars-villain-dedra-meero|text=Andor's Denise Gough On Building Dedra Meero &ndash; Star Wars' Terrifying New Villain|author=Travis, Ben|work=[[Wikipedia:Empire (magazine)|Empire]]|date=2022-11-29|archivedate=20221130235403}}</ref> [[Tony Gilroy]], the writer and executive producer of the ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' television series,<ref name="Kassa">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> was wowed by Gough's performance in the play [[Wikipedia:People, Places and Things|''People, Places and Things'']], and he knew she was right for the role. Gough's agent later told her that ''[[Star Wars]]'' called, and the actress was then approached by Gilroy for a role. Gough was concerned due to not knowing who Gilroy was and knowing almost nothing about the franchise, but Gilroy told her he also knew little about ''Star Wars''. He then gave Gough a rundown of ''Andor'' and sent her the scripts for the first three episodes. She thought the scripts were magical, not caring that her character, [[Dedra Meero]], does not appear until<ref name="Building Dedra" /> the episode "[[Aldhani (episode)|Aldhani]]."<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> Meero served as the show's main antagonist.<ref name="217 Andor Companion">{{InsiderCite|217|Star Wars: Andor Season One Companion|''Star Wars: Andor'' Season One Companion}}</ref>

After an hour-long debrief discussing Meero with Gilroy, Gough decided she wanted the role.<ref name="Building Dedra" /> Gough was drawn to the role because of the attention to the characters's humanity.<ref name="Potent Moment" /> Gilroy described Meero as "fascist" and "badass" to Gough.<ref name="Building Dedra" /> When Gough asked Gilroy about Meero's backstory, he explained that she was an outsider trying to be in a position of power to feel in control.<ref name="New kind">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/andor-denise-gough-and-kyle-soller-star-wars-1235225592|text=‘Andor' Stars Denise Gough and Kyle Soller Discuss Playing a New Kind of Imperial Character|author=Davids, Brian|date=2022-09-22|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20220922195502}}</ref> The actress only watched ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' for preparation to avoid having too much information and to not play a version of something. Gough instead tried to play her character and flesh out Meero's main characteristics.<ref name="Building Dedra" /> Gough liked the look and style of actress [[Wikipedia:Jennifer Connelly|Jennifer Connelly]] in the [[Wikipedia:Snowpiercer (TV series)|''Snowpiercer'']] television series, so she stole her steely elegance. Gough was also influenced by the villain [[Wikipedia:Gus Fring|Gustavo Fring]] from the [[Wikipedia:Breaking Bad (franchise)|''Breaking Bad'']] television series.<ref name="Crossovers">{{WebCite|url=https://thedirect.com/article/andor-season-2-cast-crossovers-exclusive|text=Andor Season 2's Important Crossovers Teased by Cast (Exclusive)|author=Hargrave, Sam|date=2022-10-12|work=[https://thedirect.com The Direct]|archivedate=20221202235830}}</ref>

===Season 1 filming===
[[File:Andor_Dedra_Meero_poster.png|thumb|right|130px|Denise Gough as Dedra Meero in her character poster]]
The actress loved getting to play a female character climbing up in a male-dominated setting, as well as showing how the pursuit of power and control poses danger on anyone regardless of gender.<ref name="Potent Moment" /> Gough thought that Meero believes she deserves better than the position she is in.<ref name="New kind" /> She also thought that Meero had a zealous belief in the [[Galactic Empire]] and believed she was doing the right things.<ref name="ProductionBrief" /> She found it terrifying how Meero wants control of everyone and everything, making her view the character as the antithesis of [[Luthen Rael]].<ref name="Building Dedra" />

Gough loved playing Meero and rooted for her because she was surrounded by men improperly doing their jobs in the [[ISB conference room|room]] she works at. The actress made sure to make Meero as ambitious as the most villainous of men and "color in all the shades" in order to make audiences conflicted. Gough believed her character's need to be seen and achieve came from a lack of psychological validation.<ref name="Journeys">{{SW|url=news/denise-gough-kyle-soller-andor-interview|text="Heroes of Their Own Journeys": Denise Gough and Kyle Soller Bring Complexity to ''Andor'''s Empire}}</ref> Gough at first thought that Meero's uniform looked too much like [[Mon Mothma]].<ref name="Building Dedra" /> During her first fitting in Meero's uniform, Gough told the designer the uniform had to be tailored and nipped in a certain spot. He told her that [[Kyle Soller]] had all of his [[Syril Karn]] costumes tailored. Gough then realized that she and Soller would be perfect together due to both having [[Wikipedia:Obsessive–compulsive disorder|OCD]].<ref name="New kind" />

Scenes on [[Ferrix]] were filmed in an intricate set in [[Wikipedia:Marlow, Buckinghamshire|Little Marlow]], [[Wikipedia:Buckinghamshire|Buckinghamshire]].<ref name="ProductionBrief" /> According to Tony Gilroy, everything on Ferrix was filmed first.<ref name="Good day">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/andor-season-2-post-credits-scene-tony-gilroy-1235268962|text=‘Andor' Creator Tony Gilroy Talks Luthen's Good Day, That Post-Credit Scene and Season Two |author=Davids, Brian|date=2022-11-23|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20221123185515}}</ref> On Gough's first day on set, she was in the Ferrix set<ref name="New kind" /> for [[Maarva's funeral procession|Maarva Andor's funeral]],<ref name="Building Dedra" /> where Gough and two [[Death Trooper]]s walked through a large group of extras. When Gough stated humming the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Main Title|theme]], all of the extras joined in, and Gough became super aware that she was in ''Star Wars''.<ref name="New kind" /> The actors then heard the voice of [[Fiona Shaw]], Maarva's actress, delivering a monologue. Gough was excited about Shaw swearing at the end of the speech.<ref name="Building Dedra" /> She was amazed by the high level of detail that she thought was just for the actors.<ref name="Real People">{{WebCite|url=https://variety.com/2022/tv/news/star-wars-andor-tony-gilroy-diego-luna-1235348148|text=How ‘Andor' Became the First ‘Star Wars' TV Series for Grown-Ups: ‘I Wanted to Do It About Real People'|author=Vary, Adam B|work=[[Wikipedia:Variety (magazine)|''Variety'']]|archivedate=20220824140042}}</ref> Early in the shoot<ref name="Building Dedra" /> for the episode "[[Announcement]],"<ref name="Announcement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref> Gough had to say she needed a [[Multi-Sector Data Blend]] going back two years, but she could not properly enunciate the words. She then discussed this with director [[Benjamin Caron]], and she managed to say the line after realizing she needed muscles she usually only used on stage.<ref name="Building Dedra" />

When Gough and Kyle Soller first met and filmed their scenes together, Gough realized Karn was just as intense as Meero. They tried to match each others' intensity and ambition.<ref name="Journeys" /> During the filming of Meero's rejection of [[Syril Karn]] outside the [[ISB Central Office|ISB Building]]<ref name="Building Dedra" /> in the episode "[[Nobody's Listening!]]",<ref name="Nobody's Listening">{{Andor|Nobody's Listening!}}</ref> Gough tried to be furious to show how Meero did not like to be touched by Karn. Gough later realized the scene showed that Meero had never been touched before. The actress loved the scene in which Meero [[torture]]d [[Bix Caleen]]. She said her "Twitchy-face Gough" emerged organically, possibly from her body language being under control. Caron told Gough to not have any swiss and to have her hands behind her back. Gough initially had reservations about Meero getting saved by Karn in the [[Rix Road (episode)|finale]]. When Karn brought Meero into a little room, Gough found a way so that the first thing Meero did was reach for something to hit Karn with.<ref name="Building Dedra" />

===Season 2===
{{Expandsect}}
Gough received the scripts for the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of ''Andor''. She wanted the season to show Meero out of uniform.<ref name="Building Dedra" />
