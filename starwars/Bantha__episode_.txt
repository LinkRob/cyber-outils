"'''Bantha'''" is the second episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Creatures]]'', a webseries produced for [[StarWarsKids.com]]. The episode sees [[Safari droid]] [[SF-R3]] travel to [[Tatooine]] to learn more about the [[Homeworld|native]] [[bantha]]s. It was released alongside the first episode, "[[Porgs (Galaxy of Creatures)|''Porgs'']]," on [[October 14]], [[2021]].<ref name="Announcement" />

==Publisher's summary==
[[File:BlueEyedBantha.png|thumb|left|235px|A bantha after a bath, revealing its face.]]
''Aree joins a Tusken Raider in this hilarious bath tutorial for a dusty Bantha. Turns out droids and water don't mix well!''

==Plot summary==
[[SF-R3]] ("Aree") introduces himself to the audience, telling them that they will be visiting [[Tatooine]] to find out how the [[Tusken Raider]]s keep [[bantha]]s clean. Aree and [[Camera|Cam]] find a Tusken Raider leading his [[bantha]] into a pool of water in a [[moisture farm]]. The Tusken Raider washes the bantha with soap and brush. Following the Tusken Raider's example, SF-R3 pushes a bantha inside a pool but almost short circuits.

Aree uses several tools including an electric comb to clean the bantha's fur. While combing his fur, he releases a [[scurrier]] which flees into the desert. Despite the hard work, Aree and Cam eventually managed to complete their task. In the end, Aree surmises that all one needs to clean bantha is hole, water, and some soap and tools. He concludes the video with some fun facts about the bantha's biological information.
