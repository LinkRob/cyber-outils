{{Quote|They're the most beautiful creatures in the universe. They live on the moons of Iego, I think.|Anakin Skywalker, describing the "Angels" of Iego|<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref>}}
The '''Diathim''',<ref name="HC14">{{HelmetCollectionCite|14|Databank A-Z}}</ref> also known as the '''Angels'''<ref name="Moon" /> as a result of [[spacer]]s mistaking them for such beings,<ref name="HC14" /> were a mysterious [[species]] of winged [[Sentience|sentients]]<ref name="Moon" /> who inhabited the [[Moons of Iego|moons]]<ref name="Angel">{{Databank|angel|Angel}}</ref> of the [[planet]] [[Iego]], specifically living on the<ref name="Moon" /> largest moon,<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Millius Prime]].<ref name="Moon" /> Tall and beautiful, the beings called Angels had [[Color|blonde]] [[hair]], blue [[eye]]s, shining [[Color|white]] skin and had a yellow aura.<ref name="Angel" /> The Diathim stood at 2.35 [[meter]]s, or 7 [[Foot|feet]], 9 inches tall, and were capable of [[flight]], due to their six [[Limb|wings]].<ref name="TCWE" />

A species who were cautious around others, legends were developed around the Diathim,<ref name="HC14" /> with stories of their reputed beauty being spread from the [[Outer Rim Territories]] to the [[Core Worlds]] by deep space [[spacer]]s. They were regarded as potentially being the most beautiful beings in the [[Universe|known universe]].<ref name="Angel" /> When [[Anakin Skywalker]] first met his [[Wedding of Anakin Skywalker and Padmé Amidala|future]] [[Marriage|wife]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], he compared her appearance to that of an Angel.<ref name="Episode I" /> By [[21 BBY]]<ref name="Date 21 BBY">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Mystery of a Thousand Moons]]" to [[21 BBY]].</ref> during the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] had conquered Millius Prime, forcing the peaceful Diathim from their homes. Skywalker and [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] would later meet an [[Diathim refugee|Angel]] in the midst of their [[mission to Iego]], during which they liberated Iego and its moons by destroying the [[LaserWeb Defense Station]] left by the Separatists.<ref name="Moon" />
