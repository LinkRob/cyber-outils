'''Bachani''' was a type of [[flower]]ing [[plant]] that was considered helpful by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]]. Shortly after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]<ref name="Inquisitor" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> there were bachani growing in the flower beds of a small walled [[garden]] accessible from the [[Jedi Archives]] in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. When she first discovered the garden, Akaris recognized the bachani and remembered its name from readings assigned to her by her [[Jedi Master]], [[Sember Vey]].<ref name="Inquisitor" />
