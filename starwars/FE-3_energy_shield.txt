The '''FE-3 energy shield''' was a type of [[deflector shield]] that could be equipped to [[R93 refractor]] [[starfighter]]s. The shield was versatile and could vary its capacity based on a [[starship]]'s flight mode.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Rokana Raiders]] used R93 refractors including a ship called the ''[[Cutlass (R93 refractor)|Cutlass]]'' which was equipped FE-3 energy shields.<ref name="Outlaws" />
