The '''Dasoor Challenge''' was a [[podracing]] [[Racetrack|course]] that shared its name<ref name="Beyond Victory">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref> with the [[planet]] [[Dasoor]]<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> in the [[Dasoor system]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim's]] [[Agarix sector]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Dasoor system]]</ref> The [[Dobatan]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Weebahn]] had won a race on the course by the [[time]] he disappeared while taking part in the [[Hydian-Hutta Podracing Circuit]]<ref name="Beyond Victory">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref> around [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> The win was recorded on his downloadable [[Racer|racing]] profile.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Dasoor Challenge was mentioned in the [[2025]] [[Industrial Light & Magic]] [[Star Wars video games|virtual reality game]] [[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]].<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where the [[Dasoor Challenge/Legends|course]] was first mentioned in [[HoloNet News Vol. 531 55|''HoloNet News Vol. 531 #55'']],<ref name="HNN">{{HoloNetNewsWeb|55|regional/1359_1.html|Campaigning Reports from the Mid Rim}}</ref> an article written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[Paul Ens]]<ref name="Credits">{{HoloNetNewsWeb||credits.html|Credits}}</ref> and published on the [[HoloNet News (original website)|HoloNet News]] website on [[May 10]], [[2002]].<ref name="HNN55 date">{{JCFCite|url=posts/6774521|board=Literature|thread=The Official HoloNetNews Discussion Thread|user=IAmTheDarkSide|date=2002-05-10|time=12:55 AM|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20220611192020/https://boards.theforce.net/threads/the-official-holonetnews-discussion-thread.5990764/page-14}}</ref>
