'''Brotwu''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual and the [[Parent|father]] of [[Brotwu]]. Along with their family whom all shared the same name, Brotwu ended up stranded on [[The Island]] of a [[Unidentified planet (The Island)|planet]], and became some of the many inhabitants known as [[Castaways (The Island)|Castaways]] who called the island home. On The Island, Brotwu ran a [[cantina]] named [[Brotwu's]]. Over the [[Standard year|years]], the Brotwu family had been good to their fellow residents of The Island, leaving a lasting impact of admiration among the Castaways. Brotwu eventually passed down ownership of the cantina to their progeny, Brotwu.<ref name="Castaway" />
