'''''Cynda Dreaming''''' was a [[freighter]] that operated between the [[planet]] [[Gorse]] and its [[moon]] [[Cynda]] during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]]. It was destroyed by the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] ''[[Ultimatum]]'' in [[11 BBY]].

==History==
''Cynda Dreaming'' was a [[freighter]] that operated during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]].<ref name="AND" /> In [[11 BBY]],<ref name="Visual Guide Date" /> it was in operation between the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Gorse]] and its [[moon]] [[Cynda]]. It collided with the command bridge of the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] ''[[Ultimatum]]'' after coming out of [[hyperspace]] above the [[planet]] [[Gorse]]. The ship recovered with serious damage; a nonhuman aboard estimated that repairs would take up to two months. Realizing that it would only take three weeks to replace the freighter, [[Count]] [[Denetrius Vidian]] ordered the destruction of the ''Cynda Dreaming''. The ''Ultimatum''&#39;s captain, [[Rae Sloane]], concurred, and the Star Destroyer opened fire on the freighter, killing all those on board.<ref name="AND" />
