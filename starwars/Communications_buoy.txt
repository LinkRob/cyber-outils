'''Communications buoys''' were devices used for communication between [[planet]]s. [[Elecia Zeveron]] planned to intercept and corrupt messages sent via the communications buoy near [[Pipyyr]] in order to free [[Axel Greylark]] from [[Prison|imprisonment]] there.<ref name="Cataclysm" /> [[Elecia Zeveron|The Mother]] gave [[Fel Ix]] a set of [[Graffian]] codes that, when inserted in the buoy's programming, would allow her to take control or kill the buoy remotely.<ref name="QfPX" />

Around [[9 ABY]]<ref name="SCDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> a group of parents built a communications buoy on the [[planet]] [[At Attin]] that could make it through the [[Barrier]] around their planet in order to contact their [[Skeleton Crew|children]].<ref name="So Much Trouble">{{SkeletonCrew|We're Gonna Be In So Much Trouble}}</ref>
