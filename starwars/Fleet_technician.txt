'''Fleet technicians''' were a type of [[technician]] who operated and maintained the [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] of the [[Final Order]], serving the same role as the [[First Order fleet gunner|First Order fleet gunners]]. They were fanatically loyal to the [[Sith Eternal]] and the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]].

==Description==
[[File:BustCollection-SithFleetTech.png|thumb|left|250px|Donning a suit of black and red gear, Sith fleet technicians were prepared to operate the massive ''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers.]]
The technicians of the Sith fleet were raised on [[Exegol]] by the cultists of the Sith Eternal, and so revered the [[dark side of the Force]] and upheld the ideology of the [[Sith]] with true fanaticism.<ref name="TROS VD" />

The Sith technicians wore [[Color|black]] flame-resistant [[uniform]]s with flared trousers, allowing the storage of various tools. The uniforms were designed to be sealed, keeping the technician safe from a majority of the toxic environments found in [[Starship|ships]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

The uniform came with a red bicep-mounted [[comlink]] and a black [[utility belt]]. The belt was equipped with a [[radiation]] tracker and anti-rad inoculation kit.<ref name="TROS VD" />

The [[helmet]] worn by the technicians was based off of the helmets used by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy Trooper|navy troopers]] and [[Imperial weapons technician|weapons technicians]]. It was red with a black visor and had a particle-scattering corrugated surface. The helmet had air-filtration systems and could block dangerous electromagnetic radiation. System data was sent wirelessly to the helmet's receivers and projected on the interior of the visor.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Role==
Fleet technicians were tasked with smoothly operating the ''Xyston''-class [[Star Destroyer]]s of the Sith fleet. Mandates could vary from tending to the radioactive [[Axial superlaser|superlaser]], to operating on the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of the Sith Star Destroyer, the latter of which was considered to be a much more favorable position to be assigned to.<ref name="TROS VD" />
