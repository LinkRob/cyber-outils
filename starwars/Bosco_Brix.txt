'''Bosco Brix''', also known as '''The Mad Bomber''', was a racer who participated in [[Riot Racing]] on the [[planet]] [[Serolonis]].<ref name="Faster" /><ref name="Episode Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bosco Brix first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' episode "[[Faster]]."<ref name="Faster" /> The character was named after ''The Bad Batch'' Senior Storyboard Artist [[Bosco Ng]].<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/faster-episode-guide|text="Faster" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}} {{C|Slide 9}}</ref>
