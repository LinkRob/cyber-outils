'''Bab''', commonly known as "'''Old Bab'''", was a [[Sullustan]] [[pirate]] who served as the [[Quartermaster]] for the [[Rokana Raiders]] pirate group in [[3 ABY]].

==Biography==
===Raiding with the Raiders===
Old Bab was a [[Sullustan]] who had joined the [[Rokana Raiders]] [[pirate]] group<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> During that [[Standard year|year]], she served as the [[quartermaster]] of the pirate group while it was based at the [[Khepi Tomb]] in the [[Sayma Nebula]] and their leader, [[Stinger Tash]] searched for the rumored treasure of the [[Khepi]] within the tomb. After the crew had spent a significant time at based at the tomb with no treasure to be seen many of the crew were on verge of mutiny. A fact that Bab mentioned to [[Reess Passik]], a former member of the pirate group, over an encrypted channel. Also during the conversation Passik, who was working as a [[contract broker]] for the [[Miyuki Trade League]], advised that Bab find out if the treasure was even real and if it wasn't that she should walk away from the crew. Bab replied that she was too old for that. Before they could continue their conversation Passik received guests and Bab deactivated the communication.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

===No Vote sentence===
Sometime later Tash threw Bab in the brig after catching Bab in her room and did so without a vote as required by the raider's [[Rokana Code]]. Tash's actions convinced Bab who had known her since Tash was knee high to an [[Astromech droid|astromech]] that she was hiding sometime. After spending her third night in the brig Bab wrote in her diary that during the nights in the cell she could hear sounds coming from underneath the rock. She wondered in the diary entry if it could be the [[nebula]]'s [[gravity]] pulling the tomb apart or if some of the other members of the crew were right about the tomb having [[Khepi guardian droid|guardian droids]]. Bab later saw [[Kay Vess]] outside her cell. Vess who was disguised as a Rokana raider so she and her companion [[Hondo Ohnaka]] could steal the treasure for themselves. After Vess opened the cell door Bab shared her suspicions with Vess, who suggested they tell the other raiders. But Bab suggested that Tash was too crafty and that Vess would need to get to know the crew better to be able to convince them to turn on Tash.<ref name="Outlaws" />

===Decision for leadership===
Shortly after, Tash successfully broke into the tomb's burial chamber. When Old Bab and several other raiders joined Tash at the chamber, Ohnaka was also there with a piece of the [[Architect's Scepter]], an artifact required to access the treasure. After initially threatening to smash the section to pieces, Ohnaka proceeded to challenged Tash for leadership of the raiders. Vess, still disguise as a raider spoke for Ohnaka before the group put it up for a vote.<ref name="Outlaws" />  
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
While speaking for Ohnaka, Vess had the opportunity to warn of the dangers of the [[droid]]s and win Old Bab's vote for Hondo. After the vote, disaster struck which resulted in activation of the tomb's [[sentry droid]]s killing many of the raiders and causing the rest to flee the base.<ref name="Outlaws" />  
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Old Bab appeared in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', which released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirates Fortune">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune|text=Steal a Look at ''Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune'', Available May 15}}</ref> During the vote for leadership there are three possible outcomes depending on the players action: Tash wins and activates the sentry droids, Ohnaka wins and activates the sentry droids, or Ohnaka wins and Tash overloads the base's generator causing nebula gasses to flood the base. With each of the outcomes, Old Bab is not shown to have died making their fate currently unknown.<ref name="Outlaws" />
