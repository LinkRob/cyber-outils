"'''Big Quince'''" was a short story by [[Peter M. Schweighofer]] with illustrations by [[John Paul Lona]]. It was published in the ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'' [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 2|issue 2]] by [[West End Games]] in [[May]] [[1994]]. Like the four other Schweighofer/Lona collaborations for the ''Adventure Journal'', "Big Quince" alternates between narrative prose and wordless comics. And like the others, it begins and ends with a frame story in which the [[Black Curs]] mercenaries share stories on the planet [[Wroona/Legends|Wroona]]. The middle is a flashback from [[Tru'eb Cholakk]]'s point of view, his story of how he met [[Platt Okeefe/Legends|Platt Okeefe]] and became a [[Smuggler/Legends|smuggler]].

==Plot summary==
Immediately after the events of "[[Chessa's Doom]]," Tru'eb Cholakk, Platt Okeefe, [[Dirk Harkness]], [[Jai Raventhorn]], and [[Starter]] leave the bar [[Gorkin's Rest]]. Starter, stumbling a little, comments on the story that Harkness just finished telling. He cannot believe that the dour man could have changed so much from his younger days. Tru'eb counters that crisis has also changed him. He begins to tell the story of how he escaped [[Slavery/Legends|slavery]] to become who he is today.

The young [[Twi'lek/Legends|Twi'lek]] is sold into slavery by his scheming cousin [[Ku'amar]] in a dispute over inheritance within their [[clan]]. The youth finds himself working as the personal servant of the notorious [[Sludir]] crime lord, [[Big Quince]]. One day, aboard his starship ''[[Quincey's Girl]]'', Quince orders Tru'eb to go select a slave woman to give as a bribe to the Imperial [[Moff/Legends|Moff]] [[Antoll Jellrek|Jellrek]]. In the ship's slave pen, Tru'eb sees mostly fellow Twi'leks, whom Quince has told him to avoid because they might "scratch Jellrek's eyes out." He finds a single [[Human/Legends|human]]: the captive [[Spacer/Legends|spacer]] Platt Okeefe. She introduces herself as Oakie.

Tru'eb takes Oakie to Quince's luxurious quarters, removes the cloth binding her wrists, and instructs her to bathe. The two talk a little, and Oakie learns that Tru'eb is a fellow slave. She immediately suggests that they escape together. She begins to form a plan centered on the two crates of [[Ryll/Legends|ryll]] spice that, alongside her, will comprise Moff Jellrek's bribe. Taking a sticky wad of [[Sludir soap]] from the bath, she affixes it inside the lid of one of the crates. She then uncorks a bottle of [[Gruvian Tovash]] and presses it into the soap, positioning it so that the liquor will spill out when the crate is opened. Tru'eb reattaches her binding but makes sure the knot is easy to undo.

''Quincey's Girl'' lands in a [[Star Destroyer/Legends|Star Destroyer's]] docking bay. Quince and his entourage disembark while the Moff and his contingent of [[Stormtrooper/Legends|Stormtroopers]] are stepping out of their shuttle. Quince presents Moff Jellrek with his gifts. The Moff orders the crates of ryll to be opened. When the alcohol spills into the spice, it creates a huge cloud of smoke that disorients the criminals and troopers alike. Oakie breaks her bonds, and she and Tru'eb make their escape, fighting their captors however they can before running onto the Imperial shuttle. Oakie maneuvers the craft away from the destroyer and into [[Hyperspace/Legends|hyperspace]].

After Tru'eb finishes his story, Platt explains that ryll reacts with some alcohols to produce a billowy cloud of blue smoke. The smoke might appear scary but is harmless; Platt has seen people make it inside their mouths as a party trick. Tru'eb concludes by saying that after their escape, Platt taught him everything he knows about starships.
