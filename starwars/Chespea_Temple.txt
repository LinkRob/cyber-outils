The '''Chespea Temple''' was a [[Jedi temple|Jedi Temple]] on the [[forest]] [[planet]] [[Chespea]]. It was a very old [[Jedi Order|Jedi]] Temple that had housed a small group of [[Jedi]] for many generations. It had [[gold]]en roofs and was surrounded by forest. The practices and customs of the [[Jedi Knight]]s who operated from the Chespea Temple differed in some ways, most notably in their independence.<ref name="The Fallen Star" />

In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> after the organization of [[Pirate|marauders]] known as the [[Nihil]] attacked the planets [[Aleen]], [[Yeksom]], [[Japeal]], and [[Tais Brabbo]] and thus caused a refugee crisis, the Chespea Temple put up makeshift shelters around the temple to help and house those that had evacuated. However this made the temple a target, as a Nihil [[Cloud (rank)|Cloud]] then fired on and destroyed the temple with the hope of sending a message to the Jedi that the recent and upcoming Nihil attacks were about them. All who were inside were killed, though the [[Imgree's Padawan|Padawan]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Imgree]] was able to send a message to [[Coruscant]] to tell them that the temple was gone.<ref name="The Fallen Star" />
