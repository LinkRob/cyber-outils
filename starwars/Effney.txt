'''Effney''' was a [[Imperial Military|military]] [[Imperial officer|officer]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Prior to the [[Battle of Jakku]], Effney managed a [[kesium gas]] rig in the [[desert]]s of [[Jakku]]. The rig was manned by several [[Slavery|slaves]], including the [[New Republic]] operative [[Norra Wexley]]. He later disintegrated the [[B1-series battle droid]] [[Mister Bones]] in order to humiliate Norra. However, Bones rebooted and repaired himself. After [[Death|killing]] the [[stormtrooper]] sentries, Bones proceeded to rip Effney from [[limb]] to limb as revenge.<ref name="Empire's End" />
