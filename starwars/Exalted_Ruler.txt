'''Exalted Ruler''' was a title that was used by [[Supreme Chairman]] of the [[Pantoran Assembly]] [[Chi Cho]] during the [[Clone Wars]], shortly prior to his [[death]] during the [[One-Day War]]. Prior to the battle, Supreme Chairman Cho stated his position and title to [[Chief]]tain [[Thi-Sen]] during tense diplomacy to assert his claim that the [[planet]] [[Orto Plutonia]] belonged to [[Pantora]].<ref name="Trespass" />
