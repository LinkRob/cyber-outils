==Behind the scenes==
Daschorb first appeared in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> The droid was later added to the mobile video game ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'' where it is used to represent a Haxion Brood Bounty Droid as well as the<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> different looking droid<ref name="VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> [[HURID-327]].<ref name="GoH" />

Within ''Fallen Order'', the bounty hunter who captures Cal Kestis on [[Zeffo]] is randomized.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> In the [[2026]] [[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order, Vol. 2|manga adaptation]], the hunter who captures Kestis is [[Atticus Rex]].<ref name="Manga">[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order, Vol. 2|''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'', Vol. 2]]</ref>
