The '''G2''' was a model of [[grenade]] manufactured by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]] that was available during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. It caused damage using over-pressure shockwaves rather than shrapnel, [[baradium]] or incendiary chemicals. Its closest competitor was [[Baktoid Personal Ordnance]]'s [[BKX-4 "Shockwave" Grenade]].<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
The G2 was first mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']], a [[2018]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] game.<ref name="Fully Operational" /> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was introduced as the [[G2 concussion grenade]] in the [[2014]] ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' supplement ''[[Dangerous Covenants]]''.<ref name="Dangerous Covenants">''[[Dangerous Covenants]]''</ref>
