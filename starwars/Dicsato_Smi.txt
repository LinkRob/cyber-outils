'''Dicsato Smi''' was a [[Gender|male]] who operated a [[Dicsato Smi's corner market|market]] in the [[Tann Province]] on [[Ryloth]] during the [[Clone Wars]] and then during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. During the Clone Wars, Smi struggled to keep his corner market stocked and customers satisfied. After the Clone Wars, the Empire established an Imperial [[Protectorate (government system)|protectorate]] on Ryloth, and the [[planet]] was subjected to extreme rationing.<ref name="Dawn" />

Taxes had significantly increased, and Imperial [[troop]]s often freely took products without payment. The increasing debt forced Smi to take part in [[black market]] operations. Since [[Twi'lek]]s were constantly taken as [[Slavery|slaves]] for the Empire and criminal organizations, Smi worried that if he upset the Imperials, he would be subjected to the same fate.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dicsato Smi was first mentioned in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[canon]] roleplaying [[2018]] sourcebook that is based on ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' and ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. The book was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]]. It was authored by [[John Dunn]], [[Tom Sorenson]], [[Sterling Hershey]], [[Jonathan Julius]], [[Keith Kappel|Keith Ryan Kappel]], [[Tim Huckelberry]], [[Jason Marker]], and [[James M. Spahn]].<ref name="Dawn" />
