'''Butterflies''' were flying [[insect]]s that sported two pairs of large, brilliantly [[color]]ed wings. The [[planet]] [[Maridun]] was notably home to the [[Carrier butterfly|carrier butterflies]], who were often used as messengers.<ref name="Everything to Know" /> A [[species]] of [[Endor blue butterfly|blue-winged]] butterflies could be found on the [[Endor|Forest Moon]] of the planet [[Endor (planet)|Endor]].<ref name="Battlefront" /> The planet [[Sriluur]] also hosted [[Sriluurian butterfly|another]] species of butterfly.<ref name="The Steal" /> [[Konkiv]] was also home to many butterflies.<ref name="Metal Heart Be True" />
