'''Calior''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Vader 18" /> situated in the [[Seitia sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Inhabited by [[human]]s, Calior was controlled by a [[Calior regional government|regional government]]. During the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Imperial [[Chief Regional Auditor]] [[Cygnus Anton]] visited Calior with a group of [[stormtrooper]]s and forces of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate in order to claim its [[southern water works]] as property of the Empire. Anton's forces were attacked by a group known as the [[Revengers]] that opposed Crimson Dawn, as well as the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and several [[assassin]]s who were serving him on a quest to destroy the syndicate.<ref name="Vader 18" />

Calior's landscape featured lakes as [[Color|purple]] [[grass]] plains.<ref name="Vader 18" />
