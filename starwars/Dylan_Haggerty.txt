'''Dylan Haggerty''' is an actor whose ''[[Star Wars]]'' work includes the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]'', where he played a [[bounty hunter]] in the game's live action cutscenes, credited as "Bounty Hunter 2." Haggerty has also appeared in films such as ''Con-Air'' and in television series such as ''The X-Files'' and ''24''.
