A '''fire team''' was a grouping of [[soldier]]s that was part of an overall [[squad]].

==History==
Fire teams existed within the [[Imperial Army]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps]]<ref name="FACPOV">{{StoryCite|story=The Witness|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Alliance Army]]. In at least the case of the [[61st Mobile Infantry]], fire teams consisted of two [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldiers]], who would then form four-person [[squad]]s. As noticed by [[Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]], who commanded a [[Namir's squad|squad of his own]], even [[soldier]]s who fought with another as part of a fire team felt like they were alone because of outnumbered the rebels proved to be in battle. That outlook affected how Namir trained former [[stormtrooper]] [[cadet]]s who defected and joined the 61st, as Namir found himself needing to teach them how to fight one-person [[Guerrilla warfare|guerrilla wars]] instead of more conventional combat tactics.<ref name="TwilightCompany">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> In the experience of [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]] official [[Greef Karga]], the Imperial [[warlord]] known as "[[the Client]]" only traveled with a fire team of stormtroopers at most, only for the Client to be backed up by<ref name="Chapter 7" /> a [[The Client's squad|full squad]]<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> during the [[Clash on Nevarro]].<ref name="Chapter 7" />

Fire teams also existed within the ranks of the [[First Order Army]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|Stormtrooper Corps]]. [[First Order stormtrooper|Stormtrooper]] [[Finn|FN-2187]] was part of a fire team that sustained losses during the [[attack on Tuanul]]<ref name="FinnStory">''[[The Force Awakens: Finn's Story]]''</ref> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> After victory over the villagers was secured, FN-2187 gathered the surviving members of his fire team, only for all gathered to be ordered by<ref name="FinnStory" /> the warlord<ref name="Kylo Ren">{{Databank|kylo-ren|Supreme Leader Kylo Ren}}</ref> [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] to slaughter the surviving villagers. FN-2187 found himself unable to follow the command and simply pretended to shoot.<ref name="FinnStory" /> In the [[Sith Eternal army]], fire teams were known as [[Triad (unit)|triads]] and consisted of three [[Sith trooper]]s. One of those three, the team leader held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[lance corporal]] and [[Commanding officer|commanded]] two [[corporal]]s. Three fire teams would then unite to create a squad, which secured an additional member via the squad's commanding [[sergeant]].<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A fire team would first be mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] through the novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''.<ref name="TwilightCompany" /> A fire team then appeared through [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> However, the unit was not outright identified as such until ''[[The Force Awakens: Finn's Story]]''.<ref name="FinnStory" />
