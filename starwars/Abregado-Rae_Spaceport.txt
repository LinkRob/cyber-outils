The '''Abregado-Rae Spaceport''' was a [[spaceport]] located on the<ref name="BH12" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Abregado-rae|Abregado-Rae]].<ref name="BH12" /> Around [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the flashbacks of [[Bounty Hunters 12|''Bounty Hunters'' 12]] to around [[5 BBY]].</ref> the spaceport was controlled by the [[Pyke Syndicate]], and the [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]], to prove his skill to [[Caviness Thorn]] and his [[Caviness Thorn crew|crew]], needed to fight his way to the spaceport [[Death|killing]] several Pykes. Valance joined Thorn's mission to eliminate [[Han Solo]], taking up positions around the spaceport. However, Valance betrayed Thorn and his men once he realized who their target was, not wanting to kill his former [[Galactic Empire]] comrade-in-arms.<ref name="BH12" />
