{{Quote|The Empire sent out powerful Force-users, dark side adepts, to hunt down any Jedi survivors. They were called the ''Inquisitorius''.|[[Verla]], to [[Luke Skywalker]]|<ref name="Star Wars (2020) 5">[[Star Wars (2020) 5|''Star Wars'' (2020) 5]]</ref>}}
A '''dark side adept''', or '''darksider''', was someone with the power to use the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] of [[the Force]]. They were often steeped in the lore of the dark side and opposed to those who used the [[Light side of the Force|light side]], such as [[Jedi]].<ref name="tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> While all [[Sith]] were dark side adepts, the term was typically used in reference to non-Sith individuals, such as the [[Inquisitorius]],<ref name="Star Wars (2020) 5" /> [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]],<ref name="The Force Databank">{{Databank|the-force|The Force}}</ref> and the former [[Sith acolyte]] [[Asajj Ventress]] who were all considered dark side adepts as well.<ref name="Hidden Empire 1">[[Hidden Empire 1|''Hidden Empire'' 1]]</ref>

During the [[Rule of Two|Rule of the Two]] times, [[Sith assassin|Sith Assassins]] and [[Sith acolyte|Sith Acolyte]] were ranks held by the Dark Side adept.

==Behind the scenes==
In the continuity of [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], a [[Dark side adept/Legends|Dark Side Adept]] was anyone using the dark side of the Force outside of the traditions of the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] or the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]]. Dark side adepts were referenced in passing in [[James Luceno]]'s [[2014]] [[canon]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']].<ref name="tarkin" /> [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Matt Martin]] later clarified on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] that the book, which had spelled the term as "Dark Side Adept," had made a mistake,<ref name="Matt tweet 1">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1169276174511398912|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=@WookOfficial and @EvanSchultheis I think you may have found an error in that book!|archivefile=File:Matt_dark_side_adept_1.png}}</ref> and that it should not be capitalized.<ref name="Matt tweet 2">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1169119612341895168|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=@WookOfficial I say this because I know you guys will appreciate it, not because I want to be a pedantic jerk (at this moment): "dark side" should not be capitalized.|archivefile=File:Matt_dark_side_adept_2.png}}</ref><ref name="Matt tweet 3">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1169122781948071936|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=@WookOfficial I guess technically "adept" probably wouldn't be capitalized either. Or well, as an official title it could be but in the case of the Inquisitors it's just a description so...|archivefile=File:Matt_dark_side_adept_3.png}}</ref>
