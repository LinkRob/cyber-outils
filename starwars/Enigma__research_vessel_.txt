The '''''Enigma''''' was a [[research vessel]] once belonged to [[Darth Nul]] at some point<ref name="Codex" /> before [[3622 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{TOR_updates|7.8.1}}</ref> The ''Enigma'' was a self-sustaining vessel designed to be a mobile research facility capable of traveling to the farthest reaches of [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]]. After Darth Nul's defeat at the hands of the [[Tenebrae|Sith Emperor]], her loyal followers scrambled to conceal her holdings from the Emperor's forces. A group tasked with maintaining the ''Enigma'' fled to the [[Wild Space/Legends|Wild Space]]. Over the [[Standard century/Legends|centuries]], the ship became overrun with wildlife and vegetation.<ref name="Codex" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Enigma'' first appeared in Game Update 7.8.1: ''Master's Enigma'', released on [[March 10]], [[2026]], as part of ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Legacy of the Sith]]'',<ref name="Master's Enigma">{{TORweb|url=info/news/%5Bnews-category%5D/20260310-0|text=Game Update 7.8.1 "Master's Enigma is live!"}}</ref> the eighth digital expansion to the [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''.<ref name="Announcement">{{TORweb|url=info/news/article/20210701|text=Announcing Legacy of the Sith, the next expansion for Star Wars: The Old Republic}}</ref>
