'''Durga''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Wayseeker" /> located in the [[Durga system]] within the [[Marzoon sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square I-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], Durga was ruled by a [[Durga royal family|royal family]].<ref name="Wayseeker" />

Between around [[170 BBY]] and around [[160 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The Acolyte: Wayseeker]]'' take place around [[160 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:WayseekerDate|here]]. ''Wayseeker'' states that [[Vernestra Rwoh]] visited Durga during her travels as a [[Wayseeker]] which began a [[Standard decade|decade]] earlier, therefore meaning she traveled to Durga between around [[170 BBY]] and around 160 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the [[Mirialan]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] visited Durga during her travels as a [[Jedi]] [[Wayseeker]], meeting the [[Monarchy|royal]] family and mediating a [[Marriage|wedding]] contract.<ref name="Wayseeker" /> Around 160 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Rwoh returned to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Wayseeker" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] and recounted her travels as a Wayseeker to other members of the [[Jedi Order]], including her time on Durga. As she spoke, the Jedi Master did impressions of the individuals involved with the wedding contract, which [[Jedi Knight]] [[Indara]] mentally noted had a good number of the Jedi present laughing.<ref name="Wayseeker" />
