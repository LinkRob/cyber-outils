The '''badlands''' were an area breaking up a [[forest]] on the [[planet]] [[Siline]]. It was an arid landscape devoid of any life, which emerged around [[228 BBY]]<ref name="Blight" /> when the [[Blight]] appeared on various worlds throughout [[the galaxy]], consuming the life [[the Force|force]] of anything it touched, before it was stopped by the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Trials">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref> Despite the abrupt end of the Blight's expansion on Siline, the afflicted area remained devoid of life for nearly a [[standard century|century]], gaining its new name of the badlands. Around 80 [[standard year|years]] after its creation, the surrounding forest began to slowly reclaim a foothold in the blighted land, but after nearly two decades, only a few meters had been repopulated, though a few patches of foliage had also emerged.<ref name="TCC" />

The devastation wrought by the Blight collapsed some of the planet's crust, revealing undiscovered layers of its strata. This exposed the valuable mineral [[iflisalt]]. The [[Lionine Graf's great-grandmere|great-grandmere]] of [[Lionine Graf]] took advantage of this opportunity, establishing the [[Graf-Siline Industries Consortium]] and developing proprietary technology to detect and extract iflisalt. Over generations, this gave the [[Graf family]] an important role in Siline's economy. Furthermore, the Grafs would place their base of operations near the badlands, their [[mining|mines]] extending beneath the blighted land.<ref name="TCC" /> 

By [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> the badlands still were unsettling to [[Force-sensitive]]s. During the [[Crowning Convocation]] [[Crowning Convocation (Jecki and Yord)|held that year]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]s [[Jecki Lon]] and [[Yord Fandar]] visited the badlands with three [[Silinese]] during the [[Survival level|survival portion]] of the competition. The Padawans were deeply disturbed by the lifelessness of the place, despite purposefully not embracing the Force during the competition. As a prank, Yord touched the calcified ground of the badlands and pretended to be injured, much to Jecki's consternation.<ref name="TCC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Siline badlands appeared in ''[[The Acolyte: The Crystal Crown]]'', a [[2025]] young-adult novel by [[Tessa Gratton]]. Though the badlands were not explicitly linked to the Blight in the novel, the acknowledgments section of the book indicates that the Blight was indeed referenced. Additionally, the description of the badlands, including its idiopathic appearance around a century before the events of the novel and suddenly ceasing its expansion, is consistent with the Blight.<ref name="TCC" />
