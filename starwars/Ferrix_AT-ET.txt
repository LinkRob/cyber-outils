The '''Ferrix AT-ET''' was an external [[All Terrain Ewok Transport]] [[walker]] configuration utilized by the [[Ewok]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Pilbush]] that she could operate for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Original AT-ET|standard walker]].<ref name="SWH" /> The walker's body resembled the [[RK-392 mobile tac-pod]]s utilized by [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces]]' [[Ambush on Ferrix]].<ref name="Reckoning">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref>

{{Veh-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ferrix AT-ET was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Pilbush's Original AT-ET, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 4.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Ferrix Rising]].<ref name="SWH" />
