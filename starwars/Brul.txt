'''Brul''' was a [[Fruit/Legends|fruit]] found on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Xilxash]]. Brul was a highly sought after commodity, and was often found in the best restaurants on the [[The galaxy/Legends|galactic]] [[Capital/Legends|capital]] of [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. [[The Nook]], a breakfast establishment near Coruscant's [[Senate Building/Legends|Senate Building]], served fresh [[brul juice]].
