'''Elee''' was the nickname given by the [[human]] [[Salju]] to a pet that her [[Harta|father]] brought back for her from his travels when she was [[youngling]]. Elee was a small and [[hair]]y quadruped, and the gentlest creature Salju had ever met.<ref name="Datapad">{{DatapadCite|A Lifelong Companion|ALifelongCompanion1-GEDatapad.png|ALifelongCompanion2-GEDatapad.png|ALifelongCompanion3-GEDatapad.png|}}</ref> She kept it as a pet on [[Batuu]] until Elee grew too big and was moved to live with a family of her own kind in the [[Surabat River Valley]]. When Salju ran [[Black Spire Station]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], she often visited Elee and they played as they did when they were young.<ref name="TGTB" />

==Behind the scenes==
Elee was first mentioned in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] on [[May 31]]. She was conceived for an unbuilt third attraction for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] often informally referred to as "the [[Bantha]] ride", where guests could ride a full-sized animatronic Elee around Black Spire Outpost, a concept which was eventually dropped for budgetary reasons.<ref name="Blog Mickey">{{WebCite|url=https://blogmickey.com/2021/04/scrapped-plans-for-kalikori-club-restaurant-bounty-hunter-ride-and-more-revealed-for-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Scrapped Plans for "Kalikori Club" Restaurant, Bounty Hunter Ride, and More Revealed for Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|archivedate=20210423152621}}</ref>
