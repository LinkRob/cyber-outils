'''Diggles''' was a [[Mardorsh]] that was encountered by [[Lys Solay]] and [[SF-R3]] on an [[Aludia|astronomical object]] around [[233 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|114B}}</ref><ref name="Creature Comforts" /> Diggles was the first Mardosh to ever be identified by the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]].<ref name="Creature Comforts" />

==Biography==
Around [[233 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Jedi]] [[youngling]] [[Lys Solay]], [[SF-R3]], and [[CAM]] traveled to an [[Aludia|astronomical object]] in search of new species to document. During their search they came across a creature from a then undocumented species known as the [[Mardorsh|Mardosh]]. Lys Solay named the creature Diggles. After trying to find Diggle's home, Lys Solay, SF-R3, and CAM ran into another large unidentified creature, which turned out to be an adult Mardosh and [[Unidentified Mardosh|Diggle's mother]]. Lys Solay said her goodbyes to Diggles and left him on the planet with his mother and siblings. After the expedition, the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] named Diggle's species the Mardosh.<ref name="Creature Comforts" />
