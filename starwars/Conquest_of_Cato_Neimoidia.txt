During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[planet]] [[Cato Neimoidia]] was '''conquered''' by the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="HC 9" /> at some point after it had fallen under the control of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Build 44" /> The changing-of-hands over Cato Neimoidia had also involved the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]], which was granted control over Cato Neimoidia for a [[time]] by the [[Neimoidian]]s as a collateral payment on a loan<ref name="HC 9" /> so the [[Trade Federation]] could have a stake in [[Poggle the Lesser]]'s [[Geonosis primary droid foundry|primary droid foundry]].<ref name="Senate Spy">{{TCW|Senate Spy}}</ref>

The base-of-operations for the Trade Federation, the [[Cato Neimoidian government]] had previously tried to take a neutral stance in the war,<ref name="Brotherhood">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']]</ref> but the planet instead became a hotly contested battleground for both the Republic and CIS. The Federation's importance to the war made the Republic seek control over the planet;<ref name="Build 44" /> in addition to the [[assault on Cato Neimoidia]], the [[Republic Military]] continued to make ground and air attack runs to keep control over the planet.<ref name="BMF 31">{{BuildFalconCite|31|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> After the Republic's conquest, the Separatists sought to take Cato Neimoidia back<ref name="Build 44" /> and did so through an [[Defense of Cato Neimoidia|invasion]]. In response, the Republic needed to launch another [[Battle of Cato Neimoidia|assault]] towards the end of the conflict.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The conquest of Cato Neimoidia was first mentioned in the [[Databank A-Z]] section of [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 9|''Star Wars Helmet Collection'' 9]],<ref name="HC 9" /> which was written by [[Iain Lowson]] and edited by [[Andrew Kemp]]<ref name="HC-Credits">{{EmailCite|email=File:HCSV-Email-2025-07-26.png|author=[[Iain Lowson]]|date=2025-07-26}}</ref> for a [[December 9]], [[2014]], release.<ref name="France">{{HCdatesFR|9}}</ref> Further detail, such as the planet having been under Separatist control before the Republic attack, was established by the [[Droid Directory]] segment of [[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2 44|''Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2'' 44]]<ref name="Build 44" /> in [[November]] [[2017]].<ref name="DateR2">{{R2Dates|44}}</ref> The [[2022]] novel ''[[Brotherhood (novel)|Brotherhood]]'' by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]] would go onto feature Cato Neimoidia in a heavy capacity, establishing it as a neutral world during the early Clone Wars.<ref name="Brotherhood" /> As such, how Cato Neimoidia fell to the Separatists before the conquest&mdash;such was whether the world willing joined the Confederacy at some point after ''Brotherhood''{{'s}} events or if it had been annexed by the Separatists via a conquest or invasion of their own&mdash;has not yet been confirmed as of the present.
