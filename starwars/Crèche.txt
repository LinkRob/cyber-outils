The '''Crèche''' were a [[religion]] on the [[planet]] [[Ovanis]] in [[Outer Rim Territories]], upon which they protected a giant [[Crèche egg|egg]] that they believed would one [[Standard day|day]] hatch a savior and lead to the salvation of [[the galaxy]]. The [[Galaxy|galactic]] explorer [[Lor San Tekka]] lived with them for two [[Standard year|years]] to understand their ways. Many years later, [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] of the [[Resistance]] found the Crèche and attempted to gain their trust as part of his [[Hunt for Lor San Tekka|search]] for the old explorer. The Crèche were later caught up in a conflict between the Resistance and the [[First Order]], a successor state of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The Crèche later adopted a [[Unidentified Crèche creature|black-skinned wing creature]] as their savior and left with it to find a new home.

==Practices and beliefs==
{{Quote|We are the Crèche. This egg is our charge, our purpose, and our salvation.|The Crèche elder to Poe Dameron|<ref name="Poe Dameron 1" />}}
[[File:CrecheEgg-PoeDameron1.png|thumb|right|200px|A Crèche elder, admiring the Crèche egg.]]
The Crèche were a [[religion]] on the planet [[Ovanis]] who worshiped, protected, and nourished a [[Crèche egg|large glowing blue egg]] in the [[Cave of the Crèche]]. They regarded the creature inside as their savior unborn. Members of the Crèche dedicated their entire lives to protecting the egg for generations. They believed that the egg held their salvation. When the egg hatched, they believed that they would receive their reward. The Crèche was led by a [[Unidentified Crèche elder|female elder]] who was a [[humanoid]] with black eyes. The religion included several [[human]]s, an [[Ottegan]], and a [[Trandoshan]]. Members of the Crèche wore blue markings on their foreheads. Several members of the Crèche wielded blasters.<ref name="Poe Dameron 1" />

==History==
{{Quote|The savior unborn! It comes at last! Our long vigil is ended&mdash;we will be redeemed!|The Crèche elder during the hatching of the Crèche egg|<ref name="Poe Dameron 3" />}}
[[File:CrecheCreature-PoeDameron3.png|thumb|left|150px|The Crèche creature, hatched]]
The Crèche were a [[religion]] on the remote planet of Ovanis. For generations, followers the Crèche spent their time protecting and worshiping the Crèche egg, which they kep in the Cave of the Crèche. Several years before the [[Cold War]], the explorer [[Lor San Tekka]] visited the Crèche and spent two seasons learning their ways and communicating with the "savior unborn." He became their brother and learned their secrets. Following [[Operation: Sabre Strike]], the [[Resistance]] leader [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] dispatched [[Captain]] [[Poe Dameron]] and his [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] to the planet Ovanis to ask the Crèche about the whereabouts of Lor San Tekka. Leia believed that Tekka could help find her missing brother [[Luke Skywalker]],<ref name="Poe Dameron 1" /> who disappeared after a failed attempt to restart the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref>

Poe and his droid [[BB-8]] landed their [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] in the Cave of the Crèche. Shortly after landing, armed Crèche members confronted the two strangers. One shot Poe's [[comlink]] out of his hand. Poe managed to calm the Crèche and told them about the purpose of his visit. The Crèche elder was familiar with Tekka but told him he had left a long time ago. She told Poe about the cult's devotion to the Crèche egg and offered to share their secrets if he would join them. BB-8 discovered a [[Homing beacon|tracker]] on his starfighter and alerted Poe, who confronted them.<ref name="Poe Dameron 1" />

Mistaking the tracker for a [[detonator]], the elder thought that Poe was threatening the egg and ordered her followers to subdue him. Poe managed to calm the situation down and told them that he was being tracked by people who would endanger the egg. Poe convinced the Crèche to let him contact his fellow pilots so that they could protect the group. Poe soon discovered that a [[First Order landing platform]] had arrived above the Cave of the Crèche and was deploying several [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]], who were equipped with [[jetpack]]s.<ref name="Poe Dameron 1" />

Poe managed to convince the Crèche to hide him and BB-8. They tried to seal the door to the cave but the First Order [[Agent]] [[Terex]] blasted through the door with his [[First Order speeder bike]]. The Crèche elder and her followers confronted Terex with blasters and ordered him to leave, claiming that his presence polluted the sacred energies in the chamber. However, Terex's stormtroopers swarmed the cave. Terex offered to fix their door and informed the Crèche that he had come to find Poe Dameron. The Crèche elder feigned ignorance but a stormtrooper manage to pick up Poe's tracker.<ref name="Poe Dameron 2">[[Poe Dameron 2|''Poe Dameron'' 2]]</ref>

When Terex threatened to torch the Crèche egg, Poe surrendered himself and offered to talk with Terex. When Poe informed Terex that his Black Squadron comrades were attacking Terex's First Order forces, Terex was unperturbed and ordered his [[First Order flametrooper|flametroopers]] to begin torching the egg. He also revealed that he had brought reinforcements in the form of the [[Maxima-A-class heavy cruiser|Maxima-A class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Ravenous]]'' and more [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s.<ref name="Poe Dameron 2" /> While Terex and Poe argued, the flametrooper's [[flamethrower]]s caused the Crèche egg to hatch, releasing a [[Unidentified Crèche creature|large blue-skinned creature]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 3">[[Poe Dameron 3|''Poe Dameron'' 3]]</ref>

Believing the creature to be their savior, the Crèche elder asked the creature how to save this sinner's world. The creature instead attacked the Crèche and swiped the cultists with its massive wings. The Crèche creature then proceeded to attack Terex's stormtroopers. When the Crèche elder confronted the creature and asked how they had angered it, the creature tried to devour her but was attacked by a large black winged creature. The two creatures fought and broke a hole through the cave's ceiling, interrupting an aerial dogfight between Black Squadron and Terex's TIE fighters. Ultimately, the black winged creature triumphed over the blue winged creature and fired a red beam through the latter, killing it.<ref name="Poe Dameron 3" />

Following the [[Mission to Ovanis|battle]], the Crèche worshipers disarmed Terex and his surviving stormtroopers. To avoid war between the First Order and the [[New Republic]], Poe decided to release Terex and his men. The Crèche cultists climbed onto the back of the Crèche creature and departed to a new home. Before leaving, Poe asked the Crèche elder if she knew what was inside the egg. The lady admitted they did not know but remarked that was never the point. The Crèche then left Black Squadron and Terex's men in the cave.<ref name="Poe Dameron 3" />
