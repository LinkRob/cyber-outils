The '''Crimson Dawn Cardroom''' was a [[gambling]] parlor that operated<ref name="Outlaws" /> during [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> in the [[Crimson Dawn District (Kijimi)|Crimson Dawn District]] of [[Kijimi City]] on [[Kijimi]], a [[planet]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. During which the [[gambler]]s [[Avrar]], [[Ju'syl]], and [[Torai]] frequently played [[Kessel Sabacc]] in the cardroom.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==History==
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|Canon=1}}
During 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] won a game of Kessel Sabacc in the cardroom where three [[Shift Token]]s, [[Major Fraud]], [[Immunity]] and [[Exhaustion]], were included in the pot.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}

In 35 ABY, the cardroom was destroyed along with the planet during the [[Destruction of Kijimi]] by the [[Sith Eternal]] with the [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Derriphan]]'' of the [[Final Order]] [[fleet]].<ref name="Destruction" />

==Behind the scenes==
The cardroom first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They were one of several locations in the game where the player can play Kessel Sabacc, an optional activity.<ref name="Outlaws" />
