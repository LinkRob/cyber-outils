'''Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities''' was located in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the planet [[Batuu]]. Its proprietor was the [[Ithorian]] collector [[Dok-Ondar]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Description==
[[File:DokOndarsInterior-TGTB.png|thumb|left|250px|The interior of the shop]]
Dok-Ondar was an avid collector of antiques and any other oddities that would interest him,<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> including [[weapon]]s, [[armor]], [[Species|animals]], [[plant]]s, [[droid]]s, and even [[Death|remains]] of [[Sentience|sentient]] beings.<ref name="Merchant Row">{{GEAttr|Merchant Row (Batuu)}}</ref> He amassed his vast collection in his travels and with the aid of his employees, [[scavenger]]s, [[Mercenary|mercenaries]], [[bounty hunter]]s, and any others who were willing to take on his quests.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1">[[Galaxy's Edge 1|''Galaxy's Edge'' 1]]</ref> His expansive collection of antiquities and valuables made his store resemble a small museum with buyable treasures.<ref name="Merchant Row" />

Outside of the store, Dok-Ondar displayed a bust of [[Jedi]], a statue of a [[Unidentified Jedi priestess|Jedi priestess]]<ref name="A Crash of Fate">''[[A Crash of Fate]]''</ref> resembling one formerly located at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]],<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> and a large stone foot.<ref name="Merchant Row" /> He also had an Ithorian gravestone dedicated to his [[Dok-Ondar's father|father]] and [[Dok-Ondar's mother|mother]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 4">[[Galaxy's Edge 4|''Galaxy's Edge'' 4]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Den_of_Antiquities.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Concept art of the shop]]
Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities is one of the gift shops at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], found at both [[Disneyland]] and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]]. It specializes in high-end collectibles related to Jedi and Sith lore such as statues, historic lightsabers, and holocrons. The popular series of replica lightsaber hilts of well-known characters, known as Legacy Lightsabers, can be purchased at Dok-Ondar's. An animatronic of Ondar can also be found in the shop, with cast-member attendants interacting with him.<ref name="Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities">{{GEAttr|Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities}}</ref>

By the end of [[May]], [[2024]], the five-year anniversary of Galaxy's Edge, 32 Legacy Lightsaber hilts had been released and sold at Dok-Ondar's.<ref name="Ki-Adi Mundi DPB">{{DPB|url=2024/05/disney-cast-member-celebrates-star-wars-galaxys-edge-anniversary-with-new-legacy-lightsaber-hilt|text= Disney Cast Member Celebrates Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge Anniversary with New Legacy LIGHTSABER Hilt}}</ref>
