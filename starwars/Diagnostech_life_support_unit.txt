The '''Diagnostech life support unit''' was a [[life support]] system manufactured by [[Novaldex]]. Many [[Rebel pilot|pilots]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps|Starfighter Corps]] wore one over their flak vests. It notably featured a G-Force indicator with an alarm [[klaxon]], and an [[atmosphere]] exchange hose with an integrated controls conduit.<ref name="ROUVG" />
