A '''squadron'''<ref name="Landing AD" /> of [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]]s served under [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] during the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], transporting them and [[clone trooper]]s from the [[501st Legion]] to the surface of [[Geonosis]] from their [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class assault ship]]. The [[squadron]] consisted of at least five [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]] and at least one [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/carrier|LAAT/c carriers]] that was transporting an [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer|AT-TE walker]]. Skywalker's gunships were shot down, and Skywalker lost all his tank support.<ref name="Landing" />
