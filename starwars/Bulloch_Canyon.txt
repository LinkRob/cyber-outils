The '''Bulloch Canyon''' was located on [[Nevarro]]. [[High Magistrate]] [[Greef Karga]] ceded all land from the western [[magma|lava]] flats to Bulloch Canyon to the [[Children of the Watch]], who [[Pirate siege on Nevarro|liberated]] the [[planet]] from [[Gorian Shard's pirate crew|Gorian Shard's pirate gang]].<ref name="Chapter 21" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bulloch Canyon is most likely named after actor [[Jeremy Bulloch]], who played [[Boba Fett]] in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].
