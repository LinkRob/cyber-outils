'''Dac City''' was a [[city]] located above the [[ocean]]s on the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]],<ref name="Dark Lord 13" /> which itself was also sometimes known as Dac.<ref name="Mon Cala">{{Databank|mon-cala|Mon Cala}}</ref> Sometime after the [[Proclamation of the New Order]], [[King of Mon Cala|King]] [[Lee-Char]] met with [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[ambassador]] [[Telvar]] to discuss trade proposals between the Empire and Mon Cala here.<ref name="Dark Lord 13" /> After the [[death]] of Ambassador Telvar, Imperial forces landed in the city, culminating in the [[Battle of Dac City]].<ref name="Dark Lord 14">[[Darth Vader (2017) 14|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 14]]</ref>
