'''Coleet''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served as a [[spy]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. She was a Rebel mole inside the [[Arrth-Eno Prison Complex]] and joined fellow spy [[Eneb Ray]] in a [[Arrth-Eno Mission|plot]] to [[Death|kill]] [[Darth Sidious]], the [[Galactic Emperor|ruler]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The plot failed and Coleet, along with every Rebel spy on Coruscant except for Ray, was killed when the Empire destroyed Arrth-Eno to kill the spies along with a group of [[prisoner|imprisoned]] opposing [[senator]]s and, at the same time, score a [[propaganda]] victory against the Rebellion by pinning the explosion on them.<ref name="Annual 1" />
