"'''A Salvager's Run'''" is a short narrative written by [[Greg Gorden]] for the ''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]'', originally published in [[October]] [[1989]] for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. The story is a companion piece to the following narrative, "[[The Full Might of the Empire]]."
