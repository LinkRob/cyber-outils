'''Battlecruiser 17''' was a [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigate]] that was active in the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval forces]]. In [[21 BBY]], when the Separatists [[Ryloth blockade|blockaded]] the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] during the [[Clone Wars]], the [[frigate]] was assigned to the command of [[Captain]] [[Mar Tuuk]]. Aboard his [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship]] [[Command ship|command vessel]], Tuuk expected the [[Galactic Republic]] to retaliate and positioned Battlecruiser 17 away from the battlezone. 

When a [[Skywalker's fleet|Republic fleet]] led by [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] arrived, Battlecruiser 17 and three other Munificents were called in for reinforcements. Although the Republic made a brief withdrawal, they later returned with a new plan of action. In the second assault, Skywalker's [[Padawan]]&mdash;[[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]&mdash;was able to [[Fall of the Ryloth blockade|break through the blockade]] after destroying the six frigates, including Battlecruiser 17.

==Description==
Battlecruiser 17 was a [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigate]]<ref name="Storm" /> that reached 825 [[meter]]s in length, 426 meters in width, and 243 meters in height,<ref name="Size" /> equipped with seven [[ion engine]] [[Engine|thrusters]] at<ref name="Equipment" /> its stern and a [[hyperdrive]] generator<ref name="Storm" /> and two [[hyperwave]] supralight [[Hyperwave transceiver|transceivers]].<ref name="Equipment" /> Emblazoned with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Separatist hex icon|hex icon]],<ref name="Storm" /> the [[frigate]] was armed with two heavy [[turbolaser]] [[cannon]]s and two heavy [[ion cannon]]s at its prow, supported by thirty-four twin light turbolaser cannons on its port and starboard sides.<ref name="Equipment" /> As customary with ''Munificent''-class [[Starship|vessels]], Battlecruiser 17 was stockpiled with four [[squadron]]s of forty-eight [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class droid starfighters]] and 150,000 [[battle droid]]s, of which handled [[Space warfare|ship-to-ship combat]] situations, repelled boarding parties and fought [[Ground warfare|surface engagements]].<ref name="Complete" />

==History==
===Defense of the blockade===
{{Quote|Now is the time to bring in our reinforcements.|Mar Tuuk orders Battlecruiser 17 to the system|audio=Bring_in_our_reinforcements.flac|<ref name="Storm" />}}
[[File:Separatist-frigates-Ryloth.png|thumb|left|270px|Battlecruiser 17 enters the Ryloth system alongside the rest of the Separatist blockade.]]
During the [[The galaxy|galactic]] conquest of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Storm" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] was [[Invasion of Ryloth|invaded]] by the Separatist Alliance's [[Separatist Droid Army|droid armies]].<ref name="Supply">{{TCW|Supply Lines}}</ref> After the invasion succeeded, a [[Ryloth blockade|blockade]] was stationed in [[orbit]] under the [[Commanding officer|supervision]] of [[Captain]] [[Mar Tuuk]], from the helm of a [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship]] that was supported by six ''Munificent''-class star frigates.<ref name="Storm" /> In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> implementing Battlecruiser 17 into their ranks, the [[cruiser]] and three other frigates were placed outside the [[Ryloth system]], as Tuuk expected the [[Galactic Republic]] to launch an attack.<ref name="Storm" />

As Tuuk predicted, the Republic [[Fall of the Ryloth blockade|deployed]] the [[Skywalker's fleet|fleet]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], composed of three [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]]&mdash;''[[Resolute]]'', ''[[Defender]]'' and ''[[Redeemer]]''. Sending vulture droids into battle, the Republic starfighters of [[Blue Squadron (Galactic Republic)|Blue Squadron]] led by Skywalker's [[Padawan]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], broke through the Separatist lines and pushed for the [[Command ship|command vessel]]. Noticing the Republic's direct approach on the [[Unidentified Lucrehulk-class battleship (Ryloth)|battleship]], Battlecruiser 17 and the other three frigates, including [[Battlecruiser 19]], were called upon for reinforcements and overwhelmed the Republic [[fleet]]. Caught in their trap, the Republic retreated from the battlezone but lost the ''Redeemer'' in the escape.<ref name="Storm" />

===Blockade's destruction===
{{Quote|What should we do?''"<br />"''Begin attack.|An OOM command battle droid, ordering the frigates to fire on the ''Resolute''|<ref name="Storm" />}}
[[File:Breaking_Ryloth_blockade.png|thumb|right|270px|All six ''Munificent''-class star frigates fire upon the ''Resolute''.]]
After the Republic left to recuperate, Tuuk ordered the fleet to prepare their weapons and the crews to their stations, learning the Republic commander was the famed Anakin Skywalker. With a new plan of action to break the Separatist formation, Skywalker returned aboard the ''Defender'' and offered Tuuk his false surrender, in exchange for [[Foodstuff|food]] and medical supplies being safely provided to the starved population. Though Tuuk discovered the deception, the ''Defender'' crashed into the Lucrehulk and effectively called the ''Resolute'' into battle. The [[Star Destroyer]] [[Marg Sabl|angled]] its [[hull]] and pulled the confused frigates out of position, with their [[OOM command battle droid]]s ordering a bombardment on the ''Resolute''.<ref name="Storm" />

While the ''Resolute'' was bombarded, [[Commander]] Tano took another starfighter squadron into battle. Using [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]]s, the [[Jedi]] Commander attacked each of the Munificents and destroyed them in quick succession, including Battlecruiser 17. After the blockade was completely dispatched, Jedi [[General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Mace Windu]] arrived with the Republic [[Ryloth invasion fleet|invasion fleet]] of [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class assault ships]] to land on the surface and successfully liberate Ryloth from the Separatists.<ref name="Storm" />
