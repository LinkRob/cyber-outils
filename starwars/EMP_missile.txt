'''EMP missiles''' were a type of [[missile]] that utilized electromagnetic technology. The [[EP-750X EMP missile launcher]] fired guided EMP missiles that delivered a powerful ion blast, which were effective against large [[droid]] formations, as well as effective against [[starship]]s and vehicles.<ref name="Rise" />
