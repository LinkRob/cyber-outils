'''Arcanaut Studios''' is a [[Star Wars video games|video game]] company founded in [[July]] [[2025]] by [[Casey Hudson]]. On [[December 11]], it was announced at [[Wikipedia:The Game Awards 2025|The Game Awards 2025]] that Arcanaut Studios would develop ''[[Star Wars: Fate of the Old Republic]]'', a singleplayer [[Wikipedia:Action role-playing game|action role-playing game]] in collaboration with [[Lucasfilm Games]].<ref name="Press Release" />

On [[March 8]], [[2026]], [[Pascal Blanché (art director)|Pascal Blanché]] announced on social media that he was joining the studio as its Art Director, to work on ''Fate of the Old Republic''.<ref name="Blanché">{{Instagram|username=pascalblanche|url=p/DVoRQc7jUqH|name=Pascal Blanché|date=2026-03-08|quote=Super excited to announce that I started a new position as Studio Art Director at Arcanaut Studios, where I joined forces with Casey Hudson and his amazing team to work on the next epic Star Wars: Fate of the Old Republic!<br />#swfotor|archivefile=File:PascalBlanche-FOTOR-Instagram.png|archive_blocked=1}}</ref>
