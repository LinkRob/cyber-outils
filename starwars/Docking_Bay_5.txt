'''Docking Bay 5''' was a [[Hangar|docking bay]] at the [[Spaceport (Batuu)|spaceport]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. During the [[First Order]] [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the planet, Docking Bay 5 hosted [[Ohnaka Transport Solutions]], where the [[pirate]]-turned-legitimate [[Hondo Ohnaka]] hired flight crews to pilot the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' on [[Heist on a First Order train|runs]] to [[Corellia]].<ref name="MFSR">{{GEAttr|Millennium Falcon: Smuggler's Run}}</ref>
{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Docking Bay 5 first appeared as the setting for the theme park attraction ''[[Millennium Falcon: Smuggler's Run]]'',<ref name="MFSR" /> which opened along with the rest of the themed land, [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] on [[May 31]], [[2019]] at [[Disneyland]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref>
