'''Berk''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]]. He worked under the [[Zabrak]] [[Arane Oreida]] as one of her [[Arane's partisans|partisans]] during a [[Mission to Inusagi|mission]] on the [[planet]] [[Inusagi]]. As instructed, he approached [[Jyn Erso]] and uttered the code phrase "cloud" to receive his forged credentials, which allowed him to enter the [[Chieftess's Palace|chieftess's palace]]. There, he wielded an [[FC-1 flechette launcher]] alongside the other partisans and laid waste to the crowd inside the palace ballroom, killing both Inusagians and [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] alike. Later he was hired by [[Saw Gerrera]] to find Erso in order to make sure she was alright following an [[Mission to Tamsye Prime|incident]] on [[Tamsye Prime]]. Berk was instructed to not be seen and eventually found Erso on [[Skuhl]], although she recognized him and confronted the man. Berk then told her why he was following her.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

[[Standard year|Years]] after, Erso again met him on [[Wobani]] where he, like her, had become a [[prisoner]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. An Imperial [[admiral]] there shot him to death in order to intimidate the other prisoners.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
