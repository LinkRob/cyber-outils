'''Fowlitz''' was a [[human]] [[gender|male]] who was a member of the [[4040s]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] activist group active during the first [[Standard year|year]] of the Empire's reign. He was a [[shuttle]] [[pilot]] by trade, and [[musician|played]] [[Kloo horn]] for a [[Fowlitz's band|band]] in various clubs and bars.<ref name="MoF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fowlitz appeared in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" />
