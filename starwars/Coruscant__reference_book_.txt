"'''Coruscant'''" is the fifteenth booklet the [[canon]] reference book series titled [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']]. It was published on [[February 9]], [[2021]] by [[De Agostini]] and on [[February 19]] of that same year by Altaya.

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''Ever since written chronicles exist, Coruscant, also known as "the Core," was the governmental, cultural, judicial and power center, benign or evil. Every seasoned traveler knew that the coordinates of the planet were 0-0-0.''
