'''Corn-clusters''' were a type of powder-based [[Foodstuff|food]] that required the addition of [[water]].<ref name="Devil1" /> The food came packaged in small flat containers that contained one long section of [[Color|brown]] substance and three small sections of dark turquoise substance.<ref name="Devil2" /> At some point between [[28 ABY]] and [[34 ABY]], [[Bobbajo]] the crittermonger gave the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] three corn-clusters for helping to capture a [[bloggin]] that had escaped from his cages in [[Niima Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]].<ref name="Devil1" />

Rey warned him not to let [[Unkar Plutt]], the [[junk boss]] of the outpost, catch him sharing food, but Bobbajo was unconcerned as Plutt had recently been [[Kidnapping of Unkar Plutt|kidnapped]].<ref name="Devil1" /> While Rey was trying to save Plutt from the [[Zendiat Collective]] in the nearby badlands, she dropped the corn-clusters after being pushed by Plutt. The junk boss grabbed up the packets and, after reprimanding Rey for having a secret food stash, tossed their contents into the air, attracting [[ripper-raptor]]s that attacked the Collective and aided Rey and Plutt in escaping.<ref name="Devil2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corn-clusters first appeared in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story "[[Better the Devil You Know, Part I]],"<ref name="Devil1" /> which was released on [[September 6]], [[2017]].<ref name="CBR">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cbr.com/star-wars-adventures-storylines-creative-teams-reveal-exclusive|text=Star Wars Adventures: First Storylines, Creative Teams Revealed|author=Albert Ching|date=2017-06-14|archivedate=20190110214816}}</ref> The story was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and illustrated by [[Derek Charm]].<ref name="Devil1" />
