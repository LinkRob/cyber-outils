'''Drift''', designated '''CT-1020''', was a [[clone trooper pilot]] who served in the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="X-wing" /> [[Reproduction|Grown]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] from the [[DNA]] of [[Jango Fett]], a [[bounty hunter]] who became the [[Clone template|template]] of the [[clone trooper]] [[army]],<ref name="Clone Troopers" /> CT-1020 participated in the pan-[[The galaxy|galactic]] [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], serving as the [[pilot]] of a [[Clone Z-95 starfighter|Clone Z-95 Headhunter]] and at one point participating in [[starfighter combat]] alongside another Z-95 in an [[atmosphere]].<ref name="X-wing" />
