'''Discord missiles''', also known as '''buzz droid missiles''' were jet-powered [[missile]]s carried by [[droid tri-fighter]]s, [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and [[HMP droid gunship|droid gunships]]<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref> that contained up to seven [[Pistoeka sabotage droid]]s. Once fired, they dodged their way through enemy defenses with maneuvering thrusters until they reached their target and hatched open, releasing a swarm of [[Pistoeka sabotage droid|buzz]] [[droid]]s that used their mechanical arms and cutting tools to dismantle enemy [[Starship|ships]] and [[droid]]s and inflict as much damage as possible.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

==Description==
[[File:TrifighterDiscord-BYOR2D2-44.png|thumb|250px|left|A droid tri-fighter with a discord missile loaded on its underside.]]
Discord missiles were a type of [[guided missile]] ordnance that was used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. They could be launched by [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> [[HMP droid gunship]]s<ref name="The Unknown" /> as well as [[droid tri-fighter]]s<ref name="Complete">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> and instead of detonating on impact they released a swarm of [[Pistoeka sabotage droid]]s to directly attack and dismantle enemy [[starfighter]]s and [[droid]]s.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> The missiles were powered by an on-board [[reactor]] with enough [[fuel]] for two [[standard minute|minutes]] of flight and could accelerate beyond 10,000 G, while their [[engine|thruster]] could tilt to allow for extremely tight turns assisted by a multitude of [[stabilizer]] fins. These features made the weapons able to outrun and outturn almost every manned [[starship]]<ref name="Complete" /> as well as dodge anti-missile defenses and reliably deliver their payload of up to seven sabotage droids.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> A system of homing and [[targeting computer|guidance]] instruments mounted on the missile's nose further augmented their accuracy and lethality in battle.<ref name="Complete" />

==Behind the scenes==
Discord missiles first appeared in the [[2005]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> They were later first identified in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', published by [[Dorling Kindersley]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />
