A small and ingrate [[Planet/Legends|planet]], '''Alzaris''' had a very low population, concentrated around a very small open field which served as a [[Spaceport/Legends|spaceport]] and some massive greenhouses. The planet is constantly bathed in the reddish light of its dying star.<ref name="CB62" />

Although the atmosphere is extremely wet but breathable, there is arid portions on the planet, which are used for crops.<ref name="CB62" />

A big part of the production is sold to the [[Rama Imperial space station|Imperial artificial satellite]] in orbit around [[Rama (star)|Rama]].<ref name="CB62" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alzaris was created by [[Jean Balczesak]] for the ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] scenario "Hold-up galactique" which appeared in the French magazine [[Casus Belli 62|''Casus Belli'' 62]].<ref name="CB62" />

The planet's soil is described in two different ways in the scenario : arid and soggy. It is possible that the arid portions are used for the greenhouse, as the muddy area are impractical for farming.<ref name="CB62" />
