'''Bamayar''' was a [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] home to the [[Ducal Palace]] and [[Chenodra]] [[Spaceport|port]]. Between around [[3 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]], the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] cargo [[pilot]] [[Bodhi Rook]] met and fell in love with a [[Gender|woman]] on Bamayar, who rejected him. During the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Mid Rim Retreat|Mid Rim offensive]], which began around [[1 ABY]], the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] [[Battle of Bamayar|captured]] the Ducal Palace and Chenodra. Following the latter's capture, [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]] spoke about the port's buildings to the [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldier]] [[Twitch]].

==Description==
Bamayar was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Heroes of Rogue One">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Heroes of Rogue One}}</ref> located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Bamayar system]],<ref name="Systems" /> alongside the [[gas giant]] [[Bamayar IX]].<ref name="Rogue One novel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue On: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref> It was situated in the [[Maldrood sector]] at the coordinates R-7 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> The planet was home to the [[Ducal Palace]] and the stench-ridden [[Spaceport|port]] [[Chenodra]].<ref name="Battlefront" />

[[File:Bodhi_Rook_Fathead.png|thumb|left|180px|Bodhi Rook (pictured) fell in love with a woman on Bamayar, but was rejected.]]
Between around [[3 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]],<ref name="BodhiDate">The [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] issue "[[The Heroes of Rogue One]]" establishes that [[Bodhi Rook]] met a [[Gender|woman]] on Bamayar during his service as a cargo [[pilot]]. The issue also states that Rook's cargo pilot service began two [[Standard year|years]] and twenty-four [[Standard month|months]] after Rook entered the [[Terrabe Sector Service Academy]], which "The Heroes of Rogue One" dates to [[7 BBY]]. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates Rook's death to [[1 BBY]]. ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]'' establishes that a standard year was 365 [[Standard day|days]] long, while the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 61|sixty-first]] issue of ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' sets the length of a standard month at thirty-five days, with twenty-four months therefore being approximately 2.3 standard years. Therefore, Rook's service as a cargo pilot, including his [[time]] on Bamayar, took place between around [[3 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]].</ref> [[Bodhi Rook]], a cargo [[pilot]] in the [[Galactic Empire]], met and developed feelings for a [[Gender|woman]] on Bamayar. However, the woman rejected his advances,<ref name="Heroes of Rogue One" /> and Rook's friends went [[Alcohol|drinking]] with him after.<ref name="Rogue One novel" />

During the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Mid Rim Retreat|Mid Rim offensive]],<ref name="Battlefront" /> which began around [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] [[Battle of Bamayar|occupied]] the Ducal Palace and Chenodra. During the cleanup at Chenodra, [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]], better known as Howl, approached the rebel [[soldier]] [[Twitch]]&mdash;who was convinced she and another soldier named [[Maximian Ajax]] had made a big mistake during their [[time]] on Bamayar&mdash;and began talking to her about the buildings there and its arches and columns.<ref name="Battlefront" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> following Howl's [[funeral]], Twitch brought up a story about the captain at Chenodra.<ref name="Battlefront" />
