'''Fondor''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Colonies]] region. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Fondor served as a massive manufacturing hub for the Imperials, featuring dozens of [[orbit]]al [[shipyard]]s known as the [[Fondor Shipyards]]. Late in the [[Galactic Civil War]], Fondor served as the site of [[Attack on Fondor|two]] [[Battle of Fondor Shipyards|attacks]] from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] against the Empire, the latter of which resulted in an Imperial defeat. During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the shipyards were seized by the [[First Order]].

==Description==
Fondor was a [[planet]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|fondor|Fondor}}</ref> which was situated in the [[Colonies]] region of [[the galaxy]], within [[Standard Galactic Grid|grid square]] L-13.<ref name="TFABG" /> It featured a [[Color|purple]]<ref name="Databank" /> surface, covered by interconnected bright lines and circles. Fondor also had at least one [[moon]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==History==
===Clone Wars===
[[File:CountDooku-SWL-2025UnitCardUpdate.png|thumb|left|250px|Count Dooku, leader of the Confederacy of Independent Systems, planned to capture Fondor.]]
In the [[Republic Era|last years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], an important part of the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]]'s economy was the shipping of [[lommite]] to Fondor via [[Malastare]]. During the [[Separatist Crisis]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]], the [[Head of State|leader]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], argued that Fondor would join the Confederacy instead of the Republic in an attempt to convince Eriadu's [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] to side with the Confederacy. Tarkin doubted that Fondor would join willingly, suspecting the Confederacy might capture the world by force. He ultimately rejected the offer, siding with the Republic, which later emerged victorious in the ensuing [[Clone Wars]] and was [[Proclamation of the New Order|reformed]] into the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

===Imperial Era===
With the formation of the Empire, Fondor became a manufacturing center, with its [[Fondor Shipyards|large]] [[shipyard]]s becoming on par with those at [[Scarif]] and [[Kuat]].<ref name="Propaganda" /> Fondor became home to many loyal Imperial citizens, [[scientist]]s, and [[Imperial officer|officers]]. Its shipyards were used for Imperial research and development of classified technologies. The [[climate disruption array]]s, among other projects, could trace parts of their early development to Fondor.<ref name="Databank" />

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Sovereign]]'' was stationed at Fondor with [[Commander]] [[Abel LaSal]] stationed onboard.<ref name="Tarkin" />

[[Luthen Rael]], who led the [[Axis network]] during the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]], was from Fondor.<ref name="2023 Topps" />

===Galactic Civil War===
{{Quote|Attention, attention. Rebel ships have entered Fondor space.|An Imperial over the ''Dauntless''{{'s}} loudspeaker|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
[[File:FondorStation-BFII.png|thumb|right|250px|One of the many orbital stations over Fondor]]
Sometime after the [[Battle of Endor]], Commander [[Iden Versio]] of [[Inferno Squad]] was recalled to a [[Fondor Station|shipyard]] above the planet and received orders for [[Operation: Cinder]] from her [[Garrick Versio|father]] and a [[Sentinel (Contingency)|sentinel]] [[droid]]. After securing the climate disruption arrays to the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|ISD]] [[Dauntless (Imperial-class)|''Dauntless'']], the [[Fondor Shipyards|shipyards]] were [[Attack on Fondor|attacked]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Shortly before the [[Battle of Jakku]], [[Battle of Fondor Shipyards|another battle]] took place between Imperial and [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Defense Force|forces]], which resulted in the defeat of the Galactic Empire.<ref name="The Ghost Ship">{{StoryCite|story=The Ghost Ship|book=Star Wars Adventures: Destroyer Down}}</ref>

===First Order-Resistance war===
The [[Resistance]] came to Fondor looking for help but were refused it. Sometime after, the [[First Order]] came to the planet seeking any traces of the Resistance. Despite Fondor's past loyalty to the Empire, history of independence and denial of the Resistance's plea for help, [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] ordered the shipyards seized under charges of treason, and killed a [[Unidentified human male (Fondor)|Fondor official]] who protested these actions.<ref name="Allegiance 2">[[Allegiance 2|''Allegiance'' 2]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Fondor was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], written by [[James Luceno]] and released in [[2014]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> It made its first full appearance in the campaign of [[DICE]]'s [[2017]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Fondor had previously been created for the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity's novel ''[[Han Solo at Stars' End]]'', written by [[Brian Daley]] and released in [[1979]].<ref name="Stars' End">''[[Han Solo at Stars' End]]''</ref>
