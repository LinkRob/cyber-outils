"'''A Trip to the Light Side: Joel Aron'''" is an interview with [[Joel Aron]], the CG effects supervisor of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' and ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. It was conducted by [[Jonathan Wilkins]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 152|''Star Wars Insider'' 152]]. The article was later reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2016|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2016]].

Aron discusses working in a variety of capacities with people including [[David Meyers]], [[Sean Wells]], [[Liz Cummings]], [[Athena Yvette Portillo]], [[Dave Filoni]], [[Roger Christian]], [[Jonathan W. Rinzler]], [[Kilian Plunkett]], [[Ralph McQuarrie]], [[Ben Burtt]], and, of course, [[George Lucas]].
