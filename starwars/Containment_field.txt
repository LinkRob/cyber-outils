A '''containment field''', also known as an '''energy field''',<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 4">[[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 4|''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 4]]</ref> was a device used to hold [[prisoner]]s whose origins were on the [[planet]] on [[Geonosis]], where its prototype was manufactured long before the [[Clone Wars]]. The field would be created and maintained by means of disk-like generators bolted to the floor and to the ceiling directly above in the room.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>
Such devices were designed for permanent installation and averaged 7,500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. [[Gordarl Weaponsmiths]]' [[A1 Containment Field]] was one such model.<ref name="No Disintegrations">[[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']]</ref>

A detainee would hover over a [[meter]], held inside the [[Color|blue]]-colored field, and be required to wear magnetic cuffs that not only anchored the being in place but also monitored life signs, since a too-powerful field could stop one's heart or cause irreversible [[brain]] damage. In addition, the cuffs could be used as [[torture]] devices, capable of unleashing powerful electrical charges, and rotate the prisoner.<ref name="Tarkin" /> Against [[Force-sensitive]]s, the field served to disrupt the captive's connection to [[the Force]], preventing their escape.<ref name="Episode II" /><ref name="Bound for Rescue">{{TCW|Bound for Rescue}}</ref>

[[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Head of State|leader]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] used a [[Geonosian]] containment field to hold [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] captive prior to the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]. Dooku attempted to sway the incapacitated Jedi over to the [[dark side of the Force]], but Kenobi resisted Dooku's words and was sentenced to [[Death|execution]] in the [[Petranaki]] [[Petranaki Arena|arena]].<ref name="Episode II" />

The [[pirate]] king [[Hondo Ohnaka]] used containment fields to restrain his hostages, mostly notably doing so to Dooku and later [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], when holding them for ransom. Tano tried to convince Ohnaka to release her, but Ohnaka responded by taunting her and refusing to give her anything to drink.<ref name="Bound for Rescue" />

[[File:Luminara_Restrained.png|thumb|Luminara Unduli being held in a containment field by Karina the Great]]
By [[14 BBY]], the ''[[Executrix]]'' was the only [[capital ship]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy|Navy]] to have such a device.<ref name="Tarkin" />
