The '''Bridger family''' was a [[human]]<ref name="Gathering Forces" /> family [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]<ref name="Ephraim DB" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Lothal">{{Databank|lothal|Lothal}}</ref> [[Ezra Bridger]], the son of [[Ephraim Bridger|Ephraim]] and [[Mira Bridger]], became part of the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. [[Force-sensitive|Strong]] with [[the Force]], Ezra [[Jedi training|trained]] to become a [[Jedi]] during his apprenticeship under former [[Padawan]] [[Kanan Jarrus]], one of the few [[Great Jedi Purge|surviving]] members of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Spark">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:EzraBridger-Platinum2024.png|thumb|200px|left|Ezra Bridger, in 9 ABY]]
[[Ephraim Bridger]] and his wife, [[Mira Bridger|Mira]], lived on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]] with their son, [[Ezra Bridger|Ezra]]. Ezra's [[parent]]s spoke out against the rule of the [[Galactic Empire]] by broadcasting revolutionary messages. Their actions were supported by the planet's [[governor]], [[Ryder Azadi]]. As a result of their broadcasts, the Bridgers and Azadi were arrested and imprisoned by the Empire. [[Tseebo]], a [[Rodian]] who lived on Lothal and was a close friend of Ephraim and Mira, promised to look after Ezra, but he was too afraid and failed to do so, leaving Ezra to live on his own for several [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="Empire Day" />

Ezra, who for years was unknowingly [[Force-sensitive]], believing his parents to be [[Death|dead]], grew up as an orphan and con artist on Lothal during the reign of the Galactic Empire. He lived in the abandoned [[LothalNet comm tower E-272]], where he kept a collection of [[Stormtrooper armor|stormtrooper helmets]]. After getting caught up with the [[Spectres]] in [[Lothal Calendar|3272 LY]] during a [[Mission in Capital City|weapons heist]] in [[Capital City]] and [[Mission to free Wookiee prisoners|freeing]] [[Wookiee]] [[Slavery|slaves]], Bridger joined the rebel crew and began [[Jedi training|training]] as a [[Jedi]] under [[Kanan Jarrus]].<ref name="Spark" /> Bridger and the crew undertook many missions together in an effort to defeat the Empire.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

He eventually broadcasted his own [[Ezra's broadcast|message of rebellion]] to the citizens of Lothal. This broadcast reached the Imperial prison where Ephraim and Mira were being held, inspiring them to lead a [[Revolt in an Imperial prison|prison revolt]]. Dozens of [[prisoner]]s including Ryder Azadi, escaped with the Bridgers's help, but Ephraim and Mira were killed during the escape. Ezra later learned of his parent's fate from Azadi. He saw his opposition to the Empire as a way to honor his parents' sacrifice. Ezra continued training to be a Jedi under Jarrus.<ref name="Call to Action">{{Rebels|Call to Action}}</ref>
