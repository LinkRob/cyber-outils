'''Bantha Surprise''' was a nourishing [[stew]] traditionally made for [[Life Day]]. Inspired by the [[chef]] [[Gormaanda]]'s [[holovid]] recipe for the dish, the [[Artiodac]] chef [[Strono Tuggs]] published a recipe for it in his holiday-themed [[The Life Day Cookbook|cookbook]].

==Description==
{{Quote|So instead, I tried to capture the spirit of that holiday classic in my own variation, usin' veg and spices native to Kashyyyk to create a hearty and nourishin' Life Day feast.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Life Day Cookbook''|<ref name="Life Day" />}}
Bantha Surprise was a hearty [[stew]] traditionally eaten on [[Life Day]] made from [[bantha]] meat.<ref name="Life Day" /> The dish could be [[Diet|consumed]] by [[Wookiee]]s and was considered a delicacy in Kashyyykian culture among the [[species]].<ref name="Secrets" /> The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] had his own variation on the dish that included [[vegetable]]s and spices from the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]].<ref name="Life Day" /> In the Wookiee [[language]] of [[Shyriiwook]], "''grrooh muggawaa ruhggha''" meant "more bantha surprise, please."<ref name="Secrets" />

==History==
{{Quote|Bantha is pretty versatile, so you can always make this recipe your own, just like I did, by addin' ingredients native to your world.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Life Day Cookbook''|<ref name="Life Day" />}}
[[File:Strono_Tuggs_VD.png|thumb|right|250px|alt=A disfigured Artiodac with a brown apron and green shirt stands with a cleaver in his left hand.|Strono Tuggs (pictured) had his own variation on Bantha Surprise.]]
At some point after the [[Battle of Mos Espa]]<ref name="BH">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref> of [[9 ABY]],<ref name="BOBF Date">{{BoBFDate}}</ref> the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] [[author]]ed ''[[The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]'', where he recommended that the reader enjoy a hearty bowl of Bantha Surprise while still able, for as long as the Wookiee [[bounty hunter]] [[Krrsantan]] was active, one could not count out becoming the hunter's next target.<ref name="BH" /> During Strono Tuggs's [[Youngling|youth]], he watched a [[holovid]] of his favorite chef, [[Gormaanda]], in which she tried to prepare Bantha Surprise. Tuggs viewed the video many times, but his own Bantha Surprise never came out as he expected&mdash;though in his estimation, neither did Gormaanda's.<ref name="Life Day" />

Therefore, instead of continuing to use the same recipe, Tuggs created his own version in order to concoct a hearty, nourishing dish to contribute to a Life Day celebration. At some point after publishing his first [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]]<ref name="Life Day" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs published a recipe for his Bantha Surprise variation in his holiday [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]'', where he shared his story of watching Gormaanda's holovids. He also suggested that the reader modify the recipe as needed by adding local ingredients to the dish.<ref name="Life Day" />

The Wookiee [[Chewbacca]] would salivate by just thinking of his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Mallatobuck]]'s Bantha Surprise, her specialty that she made for Life Day. The [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] recorded this fact in ''[[The Secrets of the Wookiees]]'' at some point after [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Secrets" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bantha Surprise was introduced to the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]'', a recipe book authored by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]<ref name="Life Day" /> and published on [[November 9]], [[2021]].<ref name="Whip Whip Stir">{{SW|url=news/the-life-day-cookbook|text=Whip, Whip, Stir: Creating ''The Life Day Cookbook''}}</ref> It was first unveiled in a [[StarWars.com]] preview for the book on [[July 27]] of that year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/the-life-day-cookbook-exclusive-preview|text=Prepare a Feast Fit for a Wookiee With ''The Life Day Cookbook'' - Exclusive Preview}}</ref> The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the meat includes [[Wikipedia:Flour|flour]], [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]], and [[Wikipedia:Chuck steak|beef chuck]]. The [[Wikipedia:Stew|stew]] calls for [[Wikipedia:Olive oil|olive oil]], an [[Wikipedia:Onion|onion]], [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic]], [[Wikipedia:Beef broth|beef broth]], [[Wikipedia:Red wine|red wine]], [[Wikipedia:Worcestershire sauce|worcestershire sauce]], [[Wikipedia:Tomato paste|tomato paste]], dried [[Wikipedia:Rosemary|rosemary]], dried [[Wikipedia:Thyme|thyme]], [[Wikipedia:Paprika|paprika]], salt, black pepper, a [[Wikipedia:Bay leaf|bay leaf]], [[Wikipedia:Carrot|carrots]], [[Wikipedia:Celery|celery]], [[Wikipedia:Red potatoes|red potatoes]], [[Wikipedia:Cornstarch|cornstarch]], and [[Wikipedia:Parsley|parsley]] for [[Wikipedia:Garnish (food)|garnishing]].<ref name="Life Day" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Bantha rump|Bantha Surprise]] debuted in the [[1978]] television film ''[[The Star Wars Holiday Special]]''.<ref name="ths">''[[The Star Wars Holiday Special]]''</ref> Its name was fully capitalized in the [[2003]] [[Sony]] Online Entertainment [[Star Wars video games|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies]]''.<ref name="SWG">{{SWGcite|exp=AED|type=item|Bantha Surprise}}</ref>
