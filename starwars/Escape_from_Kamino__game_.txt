'''''Escape from Kamino''''' is a [[Wikipedia:Facebook|Facebook]] Flash-based [[Star Wars video games|game]] which was released on [[October 27]], [[2010]] as a tie-in to ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II]]''. It recreates [[Subject 1138|Starkiller's]] [[Escape from Kamino (1 BBY)|escape]] from [[Kamino/Legends|Kamino]],<ref name="FB: Escape" /> which was in [[1 BBY/Legends|1 BBY]].<ref name="TFUIIComic">The [[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II (graphic novel)|''Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II'']] comic places the project in [[1 BBY/Legends|1 BBY]].</ref>
