The '''''Enigma''''' was a [[Dreadnought-class heavy cruiser|''Dreadnought''-class heavy cruiser]] in the [[Rebel Alliance Navy]]. Having first served during the [[Republic Era]], the [[heavy cruiser]] was eventually appropriated by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. During its service with the Alliance, the [[starship]] was allegedly haunted; it suffered power fluctations, new parts on board mysteriously broke or disappeared, and its crew members went missing, leading to speculation of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[saboteur]] or a curse.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Enigma'' was mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Starships and Speeders]]'', published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2020]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />
