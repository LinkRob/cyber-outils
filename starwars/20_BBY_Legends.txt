'''20 before the Battle of Yavin (BBY)''' was a [[Standard year/Legends|year]]. During that period of [[Time/Legends|time]], the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] raged on between the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Around 20 [[Galactic Standard Calendar|BBY]], the CIS began to construct their [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|superweapon]] over the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Geonosis/Legends|Geonosis]]. This year was known as '''Year 980''' after the [[Ruusan Reformations/Legends|Ruusan Reformation]] and '''Year 3,633''' after the signing of the [[Treaty of Coruscant]].

==Events==
*[[Mammon Hoole]] became a [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] [[Scientist/Legends|scientist]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia">{{CSWECite|II|56|Hoole, Mammon}}</ref>
*[[Orman Tagge/Legends|Orman Tagge]] became [[Baron/Legends|Baron]] of the [[House of Tagge/Legends|House of Tagge]].
*[[Reflex Amendment/Legends|Reflex Amendment]] was passed by the [[Galactic Senate/Legends|Galactic Senate]].
*[[Seti Ashgad/Legends|Seti Ashgad]] was [[Exile (punishment)/Legends|exiled]] to [[Nam Chorios/Legends|Nam Chorios]].
*After being abandoned [[Time/Legends|millennia]] before, [[Betha II]] was resettled by [[Smuggler/Legends|smugglers]].
*[[Supreme Chancellor/Legends|Supreme Chancellor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine's]] [[Centax-2/Legends|Centax-2]] [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] army's first batch of clones were born.
*[[Dewlannamapia]] and [[Isshaddik]] left [[Kashyyyk/Legends|Kashyyyk]] and joined the crew of [[Garris Shrike]]'s [[Liberator (Liberator-class)|''Trader's Luck'']].<ref name="TPS">''[[The Paradise Snare]]''</ref>
*[[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] was exiled on an uninhabited [[Unidentified planet (Thrawn's exile)/Legends|world]] far in the outer fringes of [[Chiss/Legends|Chiss]] space.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
*[[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]] returns to [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]] after [[32 BBY/Legends|twelve years]] and formed the [[Shadow Collective/Legends|Shadow Collective]] alongside his brother, [[Savage Opress/Legends|Savage Opress]].
*The [[Death Watch/Legends|Death Watch]] [[Takeover of Mandalore/Legends|take control]] of [[Sundari/Legends|Sundari]], [[Capital/Legends|capital]] [[City/Legends|city]] of the [[New Mandalorians/Legends|New Mandalorians]].
*The [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]] is [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar/Legends|attacked]].
*A planet-wide [[jailbreak]] results in government breakdown on [[Ord Vaxal]].<ref name="Polyhedron165">''{{PolyhedronCite|165|Ord Vaxal: Prison Planet of the Empire}}''</ref>
*15:6<ref name="chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=196444&start=285#10095419|thread=EU Leadup to Revenge of the Sith|user=[[Leland Chee|Tasty Taste]]|date=2005-03-29|time=10:49 AM}}</ref><ref name="NEC" />
*15:6<ref name="GrS">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' places this event two years and one month after the [[First Battle of Geonosis/Legends|First Battle of Geonosis]], which is dated to 13:5 by ''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>&ndash;On approach to [[Westport]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]], the [[Stone mite/Legends|stone mite]]-infested [[Freighter/Legends|freighter]] ''[[Spinner]]'' crashes into [[Kishi]], which is quickly overrun by the mites in an outbreak that results in the total collapse of the area's [[Pillar Zone]]. [[Jedi healer/Legends|Jedi healers]] eventually destroy the mites, and [[Senate Bureau of Intelligence/Legends|Senate Bureau of Intelligence]] head [[Armand Isard/Legends|Armand Isard]] labels the incident as an act of terrorism.<ref name="NEC" />
*15:6<ref name="GrS" />&ndash;Agents of Armand Isard trace the ''Spinner'' to a previous stop on [[Orleon]], and Isard, wrongly suspecting the planet of hosting Separatist bioweapon facilities, authorizes the release of [[mutant]] stone mites there. [[Carbonite/Legends|Carbonite]] canisters are launched onto Orleon from high [[Orbit/Legends|orbit]] and thaw upon impact, releasing thousands of mites which proceed to begin consuming the planet's crust, and Orleon's entire population of 800 million evacuates offworld.<ref name="NEC" />
