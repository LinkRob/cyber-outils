Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Imperial Special Forces]] unit [[Inferno Squad]] '''ambushed''' the [[T'onga's crew|crew]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]] at the [[Black market|black-market]] [[spaceport]] [[Depatar]] during their [[Hunt for Valance|hunt]] for the rogue [[cyborg]] [[Beilert Valance]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 33" /> After Inferno [[Squad]]'s leader [[Iden Versio]] knocked out T'onga and impersonated her before attacking the crew at their [[starship]], the ''[[Edgehawk (starship)|Edgehawk]]'', and, with the help of [[Del Meeko]] and [[Gideon Hask]], succeeded in defeating the hunters, subduing Valance, and wiping his memory.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 34" />
