The '''Battle of the Corellian Shipyards''',<ref name="EFV">''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]''</ref> also known as the '''battle on Corellia''' was a battle fought on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Corellia]] concurrent with the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]] as part of the [[Nihil conflict]] in<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> [[230 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Alys Ongwa|Alys "Crash" Ongwa]] and members of [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection|her company]] fought alongside Jedi Masters [[Kantam Sy]], [[Cohmac Vitus]], and [[Yoda]], Padawans [[Reath Silas]] and [[Ram Jomaram]], and [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]] against Nihil forces led by [[Sabata Krill]], who attempted to steal ships from [[Coronet City]]'s shipyards.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle on Corellia first appeared in the [[2022]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'', written by [[Daniel José Older]] and released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' publishing initiative.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
