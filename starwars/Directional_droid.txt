The '''directional droid''',<ref name="Every Droid" /> or '''orbital tech-support droid'''<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> was a [[droid]] model used to assist [[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptors]] while docking with [[hyperspace transport ring]]s. The droids were fitted with yellow lights that turned green when the transport ring was correctly oriented, allowing the [[pilot]] to depart. When [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] departed [[Coruscant]] for [[Utapau]] towards the end of the [[Clone Wars]], two directional droids helped orient his transport ring for his [[hyperspace]] journey.<ref name="ROTS" />
