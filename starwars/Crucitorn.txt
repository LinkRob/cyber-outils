'''Crucitorn'''<ref name="Script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> was a [[Jedi Master]] who served in the [[Jedi Order]]. During the [[Battle of Nar Shaddaa]],<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> that took place before [[68 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> between the Order and the [[Flame-Wielders]], Crucitorn became immolated by the latter, but managed to [[endure]] the pain and fight back.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> Around 68 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Jedi Master [[Dooku]] cited Crucitorn's battle as an example when demonstrating pain endurance to his [[Padawan]], [[Qui-Gon Jinn]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

Dooku later told his [[House Serenno|sister]], [[Jenza]], about his conversation with Jinn in a [[holo-letter]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="DJL-JournalsDate">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Dooku gave several [[Datacard|data disks]] containing his messages to Jenza to his recently recruited [[Sith assassin]]&mdash;[[Asajj Ventress]]&mdash;for her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|investigation]] into his [[Gender|sister's]] location, and she listened to the holo-disk that mentioned Crucitorn.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crucitorn was mentioned in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-cavan-scott/9780593102817|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> The spelling of Crucitorn's name was confirmed by the [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script]] of the audio drama,<ref name="Script" /> which was released on [[October 1]], 2019.<ref name="Penguin" />
