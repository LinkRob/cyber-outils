'''Bosco Ng''' is a [[Wikipedia:Storyboard|storyboard]] and [[Wikipedia:Previsualization|previsualization]] artist and television director who has worked on the animated television shows ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' and ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''.

Beginning in [[2012]], Ng directed several episodes of ''The Clone Wars''. He directed the episode "Friends and Enemies" in [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|season four]]. In [[2015]], he directed the ''Star Wars Rebels'' television movie ''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]'',<ref name="Siege">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''</ref> and episodes such as "[[Relics of the Old Republic]]," "[[Blood Sisters]]," and "[[A Princess on Lothal]]."
