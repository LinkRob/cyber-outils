'''Cartoon Network''' ('''CN''') is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] cable television network and brand owned by [[Wikipedia:The Cartoon Network, Inc.|The Cartoon Network, Inc.]], a part of [[Wikipedia:Warner Bros. Discovery|Warner Bros. Discovery]]. The network's animation production arm '''Cartoon Network Studios''' co-produced (with [[Lucasfilm]]) and broadcasted the ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' 2D animated series, which aired from [[2003]] to [[2005]]. They would also broadcast new episodes of the  ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' 3D animated series (produced by [[Lucasfilm Animation]]) from [[The Clone Wars: Season One|Season One]] in [[2008]] to [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|Season Five]] in [[2013]]. Additionally, Cartoon Network broadcasted the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short films ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Revenge of the Brick]]'', [[LEGO Star Wars: The Quest for R2-D2 (film)|''LEGO Star Wars: The Quest for R2-D2'']], ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Padawan Menace]]'', and ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Out]]'', as well as the ''[[Robot Chicken: Star Wars]]'', ''[[Robot Chicken: Star Wars Episode II]]'', and ''[[Robot Chicken: Star Wars Episode III]]'' sketch comedy specials (the latter three via the network's late-night [[Wikipedia:Adult Swim|Adult Swim]] programming block). Many ''Star Wars'' games were also found on Cartoon Network's website, including ''[[Republic Ace]]'', ''[[Fierce Twilight]]'', and ''[[Path of the Jedi (video game)|Path of the Jedi]]''.

As part of Warner Bros. Discovery, Cartoon Network is part of the same conglomerate that encompasses [[Turner Network Television]], which broadcasted the 3D animation television series ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' after Cartoon Network, and [[Warner Bros.|Warner Bros. Pictures]], which distributed the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' feature film]].

[[File:ArtooNetwork.png|thumb|right|200px|The Cartoon Network logo, R2-D2 style]]
A commercial ident bumper for the network (primarily shown before the ''Clone Wars'' series) has [[R2-D2]] using one of his tools to turn "Cartoon Network" into "Artoo Network."

From [[September 6]] to [[September 12]], [[2021]], ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', {{Film|IV}}, {{Film|V}}, {{Film|VI}}, {{Film|I}}, {{Film|II}}, and {{Film|III}} all aired on Cartoon Network for a special event named "A Week of Star Wars Movies."
