The '''battlesphere''' was a variant of the [[core ship]] manufactured by the [[Trade Federation]] and later used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Powered by several [[reactor]]s, battlespheres were equipped with a [[deflector shield generator]], and also sported several [[hangar]] bays, which could carry up to at least three [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class droid starfighters]] and at least three [[Rogue-class Porax-38 starfighter|''Rogue''-class Porax-38 starfighters]]. [[Skytop Station]] was a modified battlesphere that acted as a Separatist [[listening post]] and was situated in the upper [[atmosphere]] of [[Ruusan 2]], where it was later [[Raid on Skytop Station|destroyed]] by a squad of [[Galactic Republic]] forces.<ref name="Duel of the Droids" />

==Behind the scenes==
Battlespheres first appeared in "[[Ambush]],"<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> the first episode of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]], which aired on [[October 3]], [[2008]],<ref name="Ambush Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode001.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Ambush}}</ref> albeit in the episode's opening newsreel.<ref name="Ambush" /> They later made their full appearance and received their name in the first season's seventh episode "[[Duel of the Droids]],"<ref name="Duel of the Droids" /> which aired on [[November 14]] of that same year.<ref name="Duel of the Droids Ep Guide" />
