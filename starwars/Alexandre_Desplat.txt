'''Alexandre Michel Gerard Desplat''' is a French film composer. His music has garnered several Academy Award nominations, including one win for [[Wikipedia:The Grand Budapest Hotel|''The Grand Budapest Hotel'']].

On [[March 15]], [[2015]], Desplat revealed he would once again be working with director [[Gareth Edwards]],<ref name="Rogue One">{{WebCite|url=http://filmmusicreporter.com/2015/03/15/alexandre-desplat-to-score-star-wars-spinoff-rogue-one|text=Alexandre Desplat to Score 'Star Wars' Spinoff 'Rogue One'|date=2015-03-15|archivedate=20171023152439}}</ref> with whom he collaborated on [[Wikipedia:Godzilla (2014 film)|''Godzilla'']], by composing the score for the stand-alone ''[[Star Wars]]'' film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Rogue One" /> However, due to scheduling conflicts, Desplat was replaced by composer [[Michael Giacchino]] during the film's post-production.<ref name="Giacchino">{{WebCite|url=https://heroichollywood.com/michael-giacchino-replaces-alexandre-desplat-for-rogue-one-score|text=Michael Giacchino Replaces Alexandre Desplat For 'Rogue One' Score|date=2016-09-15|archivedate=20161024112228}}</ref> Desplat and Giacchino's involvement marks the first time that the music for a live-action theatrical ''Star Wars'' film has not been composed by [[John Williams]], who scored the first seven live-action theatrical releases beginning with [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] in [[1977]].
