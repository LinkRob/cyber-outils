'''Emoch Akagarti''' was the [[ephor]] of the continent of [[Pammur]] on [[Affadar]]. He and his government struck an alliance with the [[Galactic Empire]] to fund a [[mining]] operation on the [[planet]], despite it having a detrimental effect on the quality of its [[water]]. In [[0 ABY]], members of a remnant [[Partisans|Partisan]] cell called the [[Dreamers]] [[Mission to Affadar|broke]] into [[Akagarti's residence]] and held his family hostage. He was brought to the [[Affadar water purification plant]] where [[Iden Versio]], secretly a member of [[Inferno Squad]] [[Dreamers Infiltration|infiltrating]] the Dreamers, recorded footage of Akagarti admitting the water was unsafe for consumption, with the plant not actually operating. He was left by the Dreamers, locked in a tank of the toxic water.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
