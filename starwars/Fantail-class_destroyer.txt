'''''Fantail''-class destroyers''' were a class of [[destroyer]] that saw use by the [[Corporate Alliance]] and later the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval forces]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. During the war, in which the Corporate Alliance sided with the Confederacy, ''Fantail''-class destroyers were used to defend the corporation's [[Corporate Alliance headquarters|headquarters]] on the [[planet]] [[Murkhana]] and were supported by [[Sikurdian privateers]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
''Fantail''-class destroyers were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Fantail-class destroyer/Legends|''Fantail''-class destroyer]] was introduced in ''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]'', a [[2005]] novel authored by [[James Luceno]], in which it was simply referred to as "Fantail."<ref name="Labyrinth">''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]''</ref> It later received its full name in ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'', a [[2012]] reference book authored by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Paul R. Urquhart]].<ref name="TEGtW">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref>
