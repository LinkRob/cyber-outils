'''388''' was a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[B2-HA super battle droid]] who crashed on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]] aboard the [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class battleship]] ''[[Primacy]]'' before being found and reprogrammed by the [[Bedlam Raiders]] along with the rest of the [[Core ship|core ship's]] complement.<ref name="Survivor" /> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> 388 argued with fellow B2-HA [[droid]] [[459]] in the ''Primacy''<nowiki/>'s wreckage about which of them was superior.<ref name="Survivor" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Survivor}}
388 was [[Death|destroyed]] by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] alongside 459.<ref name="Survivor" />
