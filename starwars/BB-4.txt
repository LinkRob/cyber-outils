'''BB-4''' was a [[Color|khaki]]-plated<ref name="TCG" /> [[BB-series astromech droid|BB-series]] [[astromech droid]] in the service of the [[Resistance]]. Similarly to [[2BB-2]], another BB unit in the service of the Resistance,<ref name="Droids App">''[[Star Wars Droids App by Sphero]]'' {{C|[[:File:BB-unit_Data_Sphero.jpg|Screenshot]]}}</ref> the [[droid]]'s [[sensor]] was not fully encased in<ref name="DroidFactory" /> his<ref name="TCG" /> head.<ref name="DroidFactory" /> BB-4 also had a Resistance [[Rebel Alliance starbird|crest]] on his body.<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

He rolled about the Resistance's [[hangar]] bays of outposts and [[capital ship]]s and was also used in the [[Astromech socket|sockets]] of [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]s for navigation and maintenance.<ref name="Droids App" />
