'''Etta''' was the mother of [[Hue Tico]], who in turn was the father of [[Paige Tico|Paige]] and [[Rose Tico]].

Etta was one of the first settlers on [[Hays Minor]] along with her [[Marriage|husband]], [[Storm]]. She had a [[tooka]] as a pet before relocating to Hays Minor.<ref name="Bomber Command" />
