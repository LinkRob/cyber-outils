'''Dopheld Mitaka''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[First Order officer|officer]] who served the [[First Order]] as a [[lieutenant]] on board the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Finalizer]]'', the [[flagship]] of the [[Force-sensitive|Force-user]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]. He took part in an operation above the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] to prevent the [[Resistance]] from obtaining [[Map to Skywalker|information]] about the [[Ahch-To|location]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]].

==Biography==
[[File:ForcePull-Convergence.png|thumb|left|250px|Kylo Ren pulls Dopheld Mitaka across the room with the Force]]
Dopheld Mitaka was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who was a member of the [[First Order]]. He graduated at the top of his [[First Order Academy|Academy]] class and earned a prestigious placement aboard the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Finalizer]]'' about thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> Mitaka took part in an operation to [[Attack on Tuanul|capture]] a [[BB-series astromech droid]] called [[BB-8]], who was carrying the fragment of a [[Map to Skywalker|map]] containing the location of the [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]]. After BB-8 [[Escape from Jakku|escaped]] [[Jakku]] with the former [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Finn|FN-2187]] and the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', Mitaka informed [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] about FN-2187's role in the [[droid]]'s escape.<ref name="TFA" />

In a fit of rage, Kylo Ren destroyed a nearby computer terminal with his [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|lightsaber]], with Mitaka bracing himself beforehand, initially thinking Ren was going to lash out at him. When he had calmed down enough to inquire if there was anything else in the report, Mitaka then informed him about Rey's involvement. Ren then used [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|pull]] Mitaka towards him and grabbed him around the neck. He then demanded to know more information about the girl.<ref name="TFA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Mitaka first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], which was released in North America on [[December 18]], [[2015]]. Mitaka was portrayed by actor [[Sebastian Armesto]]. He was not identified in the film, but received a name in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released in conjunction to the film. The cuff-title on his left sleeve reads "[[Clyss Power|Power]]" in [[Aurebesh]], to commemorate [[Clyss Power]], a notable Admiral in the Galactic Empire's Navy who fought in the Galactic Civil War.
