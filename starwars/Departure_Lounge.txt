The '''Departure Lounge''' was a highly dense area in [[Mos Eisley/Legends|Mos Eisley]], [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]]. It contained [[Docking Bay 94/Legends|Docking Bay 94]].

The Departure Lounge usually attracted many tourists and scavengers, being considered as a good place to find bargains and spare parts.
