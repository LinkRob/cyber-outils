The '''''Aquilifer''''' was an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] that was built at the [[Kuat Drive Yards]] for service in the [[Imperial Navy]] and later served as the [[flagship]] of [[Admiral]] [[Tyrus Meori]]. The [[Star Destroyer]] was heavily damaged during the [[Mutiny on Mon Cala]].<ref name="Battles" />
