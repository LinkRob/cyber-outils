The '''Edis system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Farana (region)|Farana region]] of [[Wild Space]]. It was located in grid square S-3 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the [[planet]] [[Edis VII]].<ref name="Origin" />
