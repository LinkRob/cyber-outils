The '''Fifth Fleet''' was a [[fleet]] within the [[Open Circle Fleet]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] that was active during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] by the onset of the [[Clone Wars]]. [[Starship]]s that were of the Open Circle's Fifth Fleet were identified by five [[Color|red]] markings on each side of the vessels.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> During the war, various [[flagship]]s of [[Jedi General]]s and their [[task force]]s in the armada, along with other capital ships, were from the Fifth Fleet, including the ''[[Endurance]]'', [[Guarlara (Venator-class)|''Guarlara'']], ''[[Negotiator]]'', ''[[Resolute]]'', ''[[Triumphant]]'', and [[Vigilance (Venator-class)|''Vigilance'']].<ref name="Note" />

==Description==
===Organization===
The Fifth Fleet was a [[fleet]] within the [[Republic Navy]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] which was established by the early moments of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> As part of the [[Open Circle Fleet]], vessels of the Fifth Fleet were identified by the Open Circle emblem on the [[hull]] as well as five [[Color|red]] stripes on both sides of the vessels.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> A number of starships in the Republic Navy that were not part of the Open Circle Armada also utilized five red stripes on both sides of the hull.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> Several [[flagship]]s of [[task force]]s of the Open Circle Armada were vessels from the Fifth Fleet.<ref name="Cat and Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref> [[Admiral]] [[Shoan Kilian]] commanded forces of the Fifth Fleet, as did [[Jedi General]] [[Mace Windu]].<ref name="Death Trap" />

===Fleet composition===
As a fleet in the Open Circle Armada, itself a major naval force in the Republic Navy, the Fifth Fleet was equipped with many [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] including the ''[[Endurance]]'', the [[Guarlara (Venator-class)|''Guarlara'']], the ''[[Negotiator]]'', ''[[Resolute]]'', ''[[Triumphant]]'', the [[Vigilance (Venator-class)|''Vigilance'']], and the [[Even Piell's cruiser|cruiser]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Even Piell]].<ref name="Note" /> Additionally, the Fifth Fleet was composed of a large number of [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class assault ships]], [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruisers]], and [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|''Consular''-class cruisers]].<ref name="Bound for Rescue">{{TCW|Bound for Rescue}}</ref> Vessels of the Fifth Fleet were distributed throughout the Open Circle Armada's forces to make up the armada's task forces and smaller fleets.<ref name="Crisis at the Heart">{{TCW|Crisis at the Heart}}</ref>

An [[Ryloth invasion fleet|invasion fleet]] that participated in the [[Battle of Ryloth]] and consisted of three ''Acclamator''-class [[assault ship]]s was part of the Fifth Fleet,<ref name="Innocents of Ryloth" /> as was the [[Obi-Wan's fleet|fleet]] of [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] during the [[Battle of Utapau]] that included at least two Venators.<ref name="Episode III" /> The [[Even Piell's fleet group|fleet group]] led by Jedi General Even Piell and [[Captain]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] was also of the Fifth Fleet.<ref name="The Citadel" />

==History==
{{Quote|Not to worry, our fleet can be there within the hour.|Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Old Friends">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref>}}
[[File:BlockadeChristophsis.png|thumb|left|250px|Venators of the Fifth Fleet, namely the ''Resolute'', at the Battle of Christophsis]]
Following the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] and the outbreak of the pan-[[Galaxy|galactic]] [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], the [[Republic Navy]] assembled on the Republic capital [[Planet|world]] of [[Coruscant]] at a staging area to distribute the collected [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> During this [[time]], at least four Acclamators of the Fifth Fleet were launched.<ref name="Rise" /> On [[Geonosis]] at some point during the war, the Separatist [[pilot]] [[Chertek]] attacked a Fifth Fleet ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer from a [[Nantex-class territorial defense starfighter|''Nantex''-class territorial defense starfighter]].<ref name="Chertek">{{FFGXW2|set=Nantex-class Starfighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Chertek}}</ref>

By the [[Battle of Corvair]], the ''[[Resolute]]'' from [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s [[Skywalker's fleet|task force]] was from the Fifth Fleet,<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> and by the [[Battle of Christophsis]], three Venators and four Acclamators belonging to the [[Wurtz's fleet|fleet]] of [[Admiral]] [[Wurtz]] were from the Fifth Fleet, along with an ''Acclamator''-class assault ship that participated earlier in the battle.<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> The ''[[Negotiator]]'' from [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's taskforce|the task force]] of [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] also participated in the Battle of [[Christophsis]], and by that time was also part of the Fifth Fleet.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />

[[File:Naval_style.png|thumb|right|220px|The Fifth Fleet's ''Guarlara'' exchanges fire with the ''Invisible Hand'']]
With its [[Vanguard of the Fifth Fleet|vanguard]] arriving to the [[Coruscant system]] under Kenobi and Skywalker,<ref name="Starfighters">{{EncyclopediaCite|Republic, Rebel, and Resistance Starfighters}}</ref> the Fifth Fleet was one of the fleets to participate in the [[Battle of Coruscant]]<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> along with the [[Coruscant Home Defense Fleet]]<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> and other Republic Navy units. Near the end of the battle, the Fifth Fleet Venator ''[[Guarlara (Venator-class)|Guarlara]]'' landed the decisive blow to the Separatist command ship, the ''[[Invisible Hand]]''.<ref name="Episode III" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fifth Fleet was first mentioned in the [[2018]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']].<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> It was previously mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith Incredible Cross-Sections]]'' that was released in [[2005]].<ref name="ROTS ICS">''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
