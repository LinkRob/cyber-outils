'''Artemesium''' was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Battle Scars" /> located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]]<ref name="TheSlice" /> regions of [[the galaxy]], within the [[Lifh sector]]'s [[Artemesium system]]. It was situated in grid square R-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Artemesium had a a breathable [[atmosphere]]. A future [[Inquisitor]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Inquisitorius]] known as the [[Fifth Brother]] lived on the planet with his [[Fifth Brother's family|family]] until he was taken in by the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Battle Scars" />

When the Fifth Brother was [[Youngling|young]], the [[Galactic Senate]] decided that his [[homeworld]] would be stripped of its resources in order to save lives, and the resulting exploitation, which itself also cost lives, left Artemesium ruined.<ref name="Battle Scars" /> Sometime between [[12 BBY]] and [[11 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{BattleScarsDate}}</ref> as the Fifth Brother received a [[Mission to Murkhana (Stinger Mantis)|mission]] from [[The Grand Inquisitor]] to travel to [[Murkhana City]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] planet [[Murkhana]] and retrieve the schematics for a [[cloaking device]] known as the [[Shroud (device)|Shroud]], he reflected on his past on Artemesium and the planet's fate.<ref name="Battle Scars" />

==Behind the scenes==
Artemesium was first mentioned in the [[2023]] novel ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Battle Scars]]'', written by [[Sam Maggs]].<ref name="Battle Scars" />
