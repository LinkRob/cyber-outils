'''Amphibians''' were [[species]] that could breathe both [[Atmosphere|air]] and [[water]], and were equally at home in either.<ref name="AotG">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref> Examples of [[Sentience|sentient]] amphibious [[species]] included the [[Mon Calamari]],<ref name="Star Wars (2015) 49" /> the [[Chagrian]]s,<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> the [[Gungan]]s,<ref name="db Gun">{{Databank|gungan|Gungan}}</ref> the [[Nautolan]]s,<ref name="db Nau">{{Databank|nautolan|Nautolan}}</ref> the [[Aqualish]],<ref name="Epic Battles">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Visual Guide: Epic Battles]]''</ref> the [[Ishi Tib]],<ref name="EG">''[[Ezra's Gamble]]''</ref> and the [[Karkarodon]].<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> The [[planet]] of [[Dagobah]] was home to a number of quite common amphibian creatures.<ref name="Dbdag">{{Databank|dagobah|Dagobah}}</ref>

Some [[Sentience|non-sentient]] amphibians such as the [[gorg]] were used as [[Foodstuff|food]], other, like the [[fambaa]], were used to transport big loads.<ref name="TVE" />
