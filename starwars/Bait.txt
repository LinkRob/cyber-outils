"'''Bait'''" is a short story by [[Alan Dean Foster]]. Published in [[Star Wars Insider 162|''Star Wars Insider'' 162]], it focused on the character [[Grummgar]], who made an appearance in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="Panel" />

The story was later included in the special eBook edition of the [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|novelization]] of ''The Force Awakens'', also by Alan Dean Foster, as well as its paperback edition, released on [[September 27]], [[2016]].<ref name="amazonawakens" />

==Plot summary==
The [[Dowutin]] [[big game hunter]] [[Grummgar]] is on [[Ithor]] [[Poaching|illegally hunting]] a [[molsume]] for [[Grummgar's client|a pelt collector]]. He wants to wait for his prey next to a glade in a [[jungle]] [[forest]], but sees that it is already occupied by [[Nysorly|a small]] [[human]], which he assumes to be a young girl. He considers warning her, but instead decides to use her as bait for the molsume. After some time, Grummgar spots a molsume in the foliage of the [[tree]]s preparing to attack the girl. He decides to wait and shoot it with his [[NT-242|242 hunting rifle]] when it jumps at the girl, ideally before it kills her. Instead the girl shoots dead the molsume herself with a [[shortblaster]] she had concealed. Grummgar approaches the "girl"&mdash; who is named Nysorly and is actually an adult&mdash;and the two trade banter and argue about the prize while holding each other at gunpoint. Grummgar says he as an alternative idea and suddenly fires his rifle, prompting Nysorly to shoot with her shortblaster at his [[hunting speeder]], sending it and Grummgar crashing in a [[johinuu tree]]. As she turns to reload her weapon she sees that he actually shot at a second molsume that had approached them and the two reconcile and skin the two beasts together.
