'''Aphra's crew'''<ref name="Aphra Takes Flight">{{InsiderCite|172|Aphra Takes Flight}}</ref> was the grouping of various [[bounty hunter]]s, [[assassin]]s, and other allies who worked with the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] at any given [[time]]. For a brief time, even the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] worked with her: Vader [[Theft of the Triple-Zero protocol personality matrix|hired]] Aphra to build [[Darth Vader's secret forces|secret forces]] without the knowledge of Vader's [[Sith Master]] [[Darth Sidious]] in [[0 ABY]]. Aphra, however, eventually [[Cylo Hijacking|fell]] out of Vader's favor, and she went back to searching for lost artifacts.

After, she obtained the [[Kyber crystal]] with the conscience of an ancient Jedi called [[Rur]] with her team including [[Krrsantan]], [[0-0-0]] and [[BT-1]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" /> She attempted to [[Auction of Rur|auction]] it to a handful of criminal syndicates<ref name="Doctor Aphra 9">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 9|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 9]]</ref> but failed when 0-0-0 wanted her to free him from his programmed leash and released Rur on the criminals.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 11">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 11|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 11]]</ref> Rur killed most of the criminals and 0-0-0 was free of his leash by Aphra, but not before contacting [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 12|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 12]]</ref> Vader arrived to find Rur and defeated him after a short duel, obtaining the crystal. Aphra, Krrsantan, 0-0-0 and BT-1 escaped in separate ships and 0-0-0 and BT-1 took control of the [[Rodian]] gang known as the [[Son-tuul Pride]].<ref name="Dr.Aphra 13">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 13|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 13]]</ref>

==Members==
{{Dialogue|Aphra|Who's gonna stop us?|Tolvan|"''Us''"?|Starros|Chelli Lona Aphra, are you inviting us to join you in a lifetime of crime?|Aphra|Yeah. I ''am''.|attr=Chelli Lona Aphra invites Magna Tolvan and Sana Starros to join her|src=<ref name="2020 Aphra 40">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 40|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 40]]</ref>}}
Members of the crew included [[0-0-0]], a protocol droid reactivated by Aphra,<ref name="Darth Vader 3" /> Chelli's estranged father, [[Korin Aphra]], who agreed to help Aphra in discovering the secrets of [[Ordu Aspectu]],<ref name="Doctor Aphra 2" /> and the bounty hunter [[Krrsantan|Black Krrsantan]], who took part in a raid on the [[Son-tuul Pride]] with Aphra<ref name="Darth Vader 7" /> before he later rescued and healed Aphra after Vader attempted to kill her.<ref name="Darth Vader 25">[[Darth Vader (2015) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 25]]</ref> He continue to help Aphra in endeavors.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" /> Other members included [[BT-1]], the assassin droid companion of 0-0-0, the bounty hunter [[Beebox]], who worked with Aphra on a [[Son-tuul Pride robbery|heist]] on Anthan Prime,<ref name="Darth Vader 7" /> [[Vulaada Klam]], Aphra's ward,<ref name="Dr. Aphra 32">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 32|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 32]]</ref> and Klam's pet [[qaberworm]], [[Gurtyl]].<ref name="Dr. Aphra 27">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 27|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 27]]</ref>

[[File:LuckyAphraStarros-2020DoctorAphra38.png|thumb|left|300px|From left to right: Just Lucky, Chelli Aphra, and Sana Starros on a [[Rescue of Magna Tolvan|mission to rescue Magna Tolvan]]]]
The mercenary [[Just Lucky]] also worked with Aphra on several occasions. [[Eustacia Okka]] had worked with Aphra at the [[University of Bar'leth]], and discovered the [[Rings of Vaale|Rings]] of [[Vaale]]. Doctor Okka's assistant droid was [[TA-418]], and [[Detta Yao]] was an archeology student who worked with Aphra and Dr. Okka during their expedition to find the Rings of Vaale.<ref name="Dr. Aphra 2020 1">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 1|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 1]]</ref> [[Tam Posla]] was a former lawman who worked as a mercenary for Aphra. The assassin [[Sister Six]] worked under Aprha and 0-0-0.<ref name="Dr. Aphra 15">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 15|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 15]]</ref> [[Sana Starros]], a former classmate and girlfriend from the [[University of Bar'leth]],<ref name="Dr. Aphra Audio">''[[Doctor Aphra: An Audiobook Original]]''</ref> also worked with her on occasion. After the [[Scourge crisis|scourge of the droids]], Aphra would be joined on her travels by Starros and [[Magna Tolvan]], the two women she loved.<ref name="2020 Aphra 40" />
