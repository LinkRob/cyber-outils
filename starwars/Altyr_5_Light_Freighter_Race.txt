The '''Altyr 5 Light Freighter Race''' was a [[starship]] race for [[light freighter]]s. Despite the name, the race took place in [[Realspace|space]]. Starting sometime between [[10 BBY]] and [[9 BBY]],<ref name=" Imperial Date Note">{{ChopperChaseDate}} The game states that 5 years prior to when the game takes place, Imperial-endorsed winners began to continually win. Thus, this streak must have started sometime between [[10 BBY]] and [[9 BBY]].</ref> [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]]-endorsed [[racer]]s began to win, until sometime between [[5 BBY]] and [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Date Note" /> when the [[Spectres]] won. [[Imperial Patrol]] hated when racers who were not endorsed by the Empire were in the lead, and would to anything in their power to stop them from from winning.<ref name="RCC" />
