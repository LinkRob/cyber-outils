'''Chocolate Portion Bread''' was a rich, [[Diet|vegetarian]] [[Foodstuff|dessert]] [[bread]] made from [[Polystarch portion bread|portion bread]] with additional ingredients added. The [[Color|brown]] bread filling was served in a bowl with a darker brown topping over it and a green garnish sprinkled on top. The dish was created by the inhabitants of the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]] after they began making variants of portion bread by mixing in extra ingredients to create a variety of different flavors.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] tried many of the portion bread variants on Jakku during a culinary tour he took of [[the galaxy]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]]<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> and selected Chocolate Portion Bread as his favorite. He then included a recipe for the dish in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chocolate Portion Bread was created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the bread makes use of [[Wikipedia:Vegetable oil|vegetable oil]], [[Wikipedia:Wheat flour|cake flour]], [[Wikipedia:White sugar|granulated sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Cocoa solids|cocoa powder]], [[Wikipedia:Baking powder|baking powder]], [[Wikipedia:Cinnamon|cinnamon]], [[Wikipedia:Kosher salt|kosher salt]], [[Wikipedia:Coconut milk|coconut milk]], [[Wikipedia:Vanilla extract|vanilla extract]], mini [[Wikipedia:Chocolate chip|chocolate chips]], and a dusting of [[Wikipedia:Matcha|matcha powder]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
