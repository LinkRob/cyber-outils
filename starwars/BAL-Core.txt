The '''BAL-Core''' was a model of [[Labor droid|worker droid]] with two-tone [[Color|blue]] plating and two white [[photoreceptor]]s. The model was a type of [[monster droid]] and had the head and two [[Limb|legs]] of an [[BDX droid]] attatched to the spherical body of a [[BB-series astromech droid]],<ref name="Fortnite" /> a product of [[Industrial Automaton]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> A group of [[Jawa]]s sold blueprints for BAL-Cores out of the [[Sandcrawler Shop]] near the [[Junkers Cantina]] on<ref name="Fortnite" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Tatooine]]. They priced the default design at 43,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], a golden design at 172,000 credits, a [[diamond]]-colored version at 344,000 credits, and a rainbow-colored design at 668,000 credits.<ref name="Fortnite" />
