An '''ascension gun'''<ref name="DB">{{Databank|blaster-pistol|Blaster Pistol}}</ref> was a [[blaster]] weapon that was also able to fire a [[grappling hook]] attached to a tether, allowing the wielder to cross a horizontal gap or make a vertical climb.<ref name="Episode I" /> The [[S-5 Security-grade blaster/ascension gun]]<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> was a model of [[heavy blaster pistol]]<ref name="Blasters and Firearms">{{EncyclopediaCite|Blasters and Ranged Weapons}}</ref> and ascension gun.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> Firing the [[Ascension cable|ascension hook]] could also be used as a weapon, such as when the [[clone trooper]] [[Hardcase]] used it to shoot a noisy [[Banshee (species)|banshee]] during a stealthy [[Battle of Umbara|mission]] on [[Umbara]].<ref name="The General" />
