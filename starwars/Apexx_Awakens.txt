"'''Apexx Awakens'''" is the first part of the [[Apexx Awakens / Harvester Madness|sixth episode]] of the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Three|third season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''. It was released on [[Disney+]] on [[December 8]], [[2025]].

==Official description==
''[[Rek Minuu|Rek]] builds a new droid.''

==Plot summary==
===The stolen power cell===
[[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Nubs]], [[Lys Solay]], and [[Kai Brightstar]] help their [[Chadra-Fan]] friend [[Marlaa Jinara]] organize piles of new scrap in [[Marlaa Jinara's junkyard|her junkyard]]. Nubs tosses some scraps to [[JG-1]] to sort out. Unknown to the group, they are being watched by one of rogue [[droidsmith]] [[Rek Minuu]]'s [[Rek Minuu's spider droid|spider droids]], its sensors focusing on a panel in JG-1's back. Brightstar enlists Nub's help in lifting a heavy cylindrical machine part. Nubs is unable to lift the machine despite his physical strength. Brightstar calls JG-1 for help, and the [[droid]] slowly lumbers over. Jinara checks on the droid's rear hatch and discovers that one of her [[power cell]]s is missing. She explains that JG-1 relies on rare, special power cells. She assumes the missing one must have fallen out. 

Solay offers to help Jinara find the power cells. She spots a power cell but finds that it is being carried by one of Rek's spider droids. The droid and two of its companions attempt to flee. The Jedi younglings, Jinara, and her other droid [[PT-1]] give chase. The spider droids pass the power cell among one another. One of the spider droids then climbs a pile of scrap metal. The Jedi start to climb after, but one of the other droids pushes them back down on a sheet of metal. The Jedi younglings pursue the spider droids while Jinara stays behind to look after JG-1. 

===Rek's trap===
Meanwhile, Rek plots in his workshop. He connects with his spider droids over [[holoprojector|holo]] and is pleased that they have obtained the power cell, which he needs for a special project. Rek orders two of the spider droids to return with the power cell and tasks the third with leading the Jedi younglings to [[Rek's ship|his ship]]. With the help of his smaller spider droid [[Minmo]], Rek finishes assembling an armored [[color|blue]] droid. The spider droids arrive with the power cell, and he prepares to power up the blue droid. He orders the left-behind spider droids to start luring the Jedi.

While searching through the streets of [[Kublop Springs]] for Rek's spider droids, the third spider droid suddenly appears in front of the Jedi. It leads them through a forest to Rek's starship. As the spider droid climbs the ramp leading to Rek's starship, Brightstar suspects that the droid is leading them into a trap. Just then, a [[hologram]] of Rek appears. When Brightstar says that they are here to reclaim the power cell, Rek invites them aboard his starship to do just that. Brightstar tells his friends to stay together and keep alert. 

Inside Rek's workshop, Rek introduces them to his new droid "friend" [[Apexx]], a towering blue humanoid droid. Apexx walks around the Jedi and remarks that they are "small." When Solay requests the return of the power cell and offers to help him find another one for Apexx, Rek reveals that he lured them inside his workshop in order to test his new droid on them. Apexx attacks the Jedi with two [[electrobaton]]s, prompting them to draw their [[training lightsaber]]s. Apexx, stronger and taller than the younglings, quickly gains the upper hand and corners the Jedi.

===Outwitting Apexx===
Brightstar [[Force jump]]s onto a platform to escape Apexx. While Apexx is distracted, Solay jumps on his back in an attempt to remove the power cell. However, he throws her off, and Solay narrowly dodges being slashed by one his electrobatons. While Apexx's back is turned fighting his friends, Brightstar attempts a leaping lightsaber attack, but the droid easily parries before using the boy's momentum to throw him into Nubs. As the Jedi regroup, they face Minuu's taunts. Brightstar resolves not to give up despite how strong and fast their opponent is. 


The Jedi fight a second round, but they are still unable to compete with Apexx's strength and speed. Solay takes a moment to strategize. Noticing a reduced power level indicator on Apexx's power cell, she convinces Brightstar that the three of them should force the droid to chase them until its power cell is out of charge. Apexx, while in pursuit of Nubs, gets his electrobatons stuck in a crate. He tries to slam at the diminutive [[Pooba]] with the crate, but Nubs slashes it with [[Nubs's lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. Brightstar fills Nubs in on the plan, and the trio rush out of the workshop. 

The Jedi lure Apexx off the ship and run circles around him in the forest, each popping up one after the other to keep the droid on the move. These exertions cause Apexx's power cell to run out of charge, shutting him down. Watching the skirmish, Rek congratulates his Jedi opponents and allows them to reclaim the power cell. Brightstar tells Rek that it is wrong to steal and offers to help him achieve his goals without stealing. Rek turns down the Jedi's offer. Alone with his droids, Minuu instead tells his droids that they will work on creating a bespoke, better power cell. He sends one of his [[security droid]]s to retrieve the powerless Apexx.

===Homecoming===
Back at Jinara's junkyard, Jinara recharges JG-1's depleted power cell and fits it back to the droid. JG-1 hugs the Jedi younglings while Jinara thanks the Jedi younglings for their help. The Jedi are tired but cheerful.
