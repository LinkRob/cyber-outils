The '''first Emergence''' was the first of the [[Emergences]], fallout of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] in which fragments of the [[freighter]] ''[[Legacy Run]]'' shot out of [[hyperspace]] seemingly at random. Seven pieces of the [[starship]] emerged in the [[Ab Dalis system]]. While six of them passed through the [[star system]] harmlessly, the seventh struck a glancing blow to [[Ab Dalis]], destroying a [[city]] on the [[planet]] and [[Death|killing]] approximately twenty million people. The devastation led [[Larence Garello]] of [[Garello Technologies]] to evacuate his employees and research from the planet; however, the [[convoy]] was [[Attack on the Garello convoy|attacked]] by the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s before they could make it out of the system.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
