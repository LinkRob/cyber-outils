The '''''Banshee''''' was a [[Lancer-class pursuit craft|''Lancer''-class pursuit craft]] which served as the personal [[starship]] of [[bounty hunter]] [[Asajj Ventress]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The ''Banshee'' featured two cabins, a small galley, and a cargo hold, used for holding [[Bounty|bounties]] [[prisoner]].<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

==History==
{{Quote|And you're certain the ship's cloaking device is active? I don't want to be hurtling out of hyperspace unprotected into the path of a dozen Separatist cruisers....|Anakin Skywalker to Asajj Ventress, on the ''Banshee''|<ref name="Dark Disciple" />}}
[[Asajj Ventress]] came into possession of the ''Banshee'' during the last [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Clone Wars]], and used it as a [[transport]] and home as she sought out her [[Bounty|bounties]]. It<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> shared its name with a [[Banshee (starfighter)|''Ginivex''-class fanblade starfighter]] previously used by Ventress.<ref name="Power of the Dark Side">''[[Star Wars Villainous: Power of the Dark Side]]''</ref>

During the [[Second battle of Christophsis]], the ''Banshee'' was shot down by the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|Republic Star Destroyer]] [[Vigilance (Venator-class)|''Vigilance'']], on [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s orders as part of their plan to inflantrate the ship to rescue [[Quinlan Vos]] and the ship crash landed in [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]].<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

By the [[Imperial Era]], she started using a [[Ventress' ship|different ship]] when she visited [[Clone Force 99]] on [[Pabu]].<ref name="Harbinger">{{TBB|The Harbinger}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Banshee'' was first revealed at [[Celebration Anaheim (2015)|Celebration Anaheim]] during "The Untold ''Clone Wars'' Panel." Originally set to appear in unfinished episodes of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', the episodes were adapted into a novel written by [[Christie Golden]] entitled ''[[Dark Disciple]]'' after the show's cancellation. Although the ship has not been depicted in [[canon]], concept art of the ''Banshee'', based off [[Doug Chiang]]'s work on the [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|Republic Gunship]], was shown during the panel.<ref name="Untold Clone Wars Panel">{{SWYouTube|7yKM6RExLlI|The Untold Clone Wars Panel &#124; Star Wars Celebration Anaheim}}</ref> The final model was revamped into the ''[[Shadow Caster]]'', featured in the episode "[[Blood Sisters]]" of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. The production asset for the Banshee was released in the episode's trivia section on Starwars.com.<ref name="SW-Blood Sisters Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/blood-sisters-trivia-gallery|text="Blood Sisters" Trivia Gallery}}</ref>
