'''Bantha dung''' was an insult, used to refer to something<ref name="Reirin">{{StoryCite|story=Reirin (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> or someone worthy of contempt.<ref name="Aphra 2016 15" /> It literally referred to a [[bantha]]'s feces,<ref name="Doctor Aphra (2016) 38">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 38|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 38]]</ref> which was also known as '''bantha droppings''', with "pile of bantha droppings" being employed metaphorically with the same implications, such as "you steaming pile of bantha droppings"<ref name="The Glass Abyss">''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]''</ref> or "this is a big pile of bantha droppings."<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

The phrase "piece/smear of bantha dung" was a way of showing contempt toward someone.<ref name="Aphra 2016 15">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 15|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 15]]</ref><ref name="Han Chewie 10">[[Han Solo & Chewbacca 10|''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' 10]]</ref> The word could also mean worthless, e.g. "[[Tern Koyatta|Your]] words ain't ''bantha dung'' right now."<ref name="Ewoks 4">[[Ewoks (2024) 4|''Ewoks'' (2024) 4]]</ref> The saying "a burr [[seed]] in bantha dung" was used to describe something that was hard to find.<ref name="Aphra 2016 22">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 22|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 22]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
When used about something worthy of contempt, "[[bantha]] dung" is similar to the real-world swear word "bullshit." When used about ''someone'' worthy of contempt, the term is similar to the real-world swear word "shit." 

The phrase "pile of bantha droppings" is similar to the real-world vulgar slang phrase "pile of shit."<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://idioms.thefreedictionary.com/pile+of+shit|text=pile of shit|quote=1. ''vulgar slang'' A lot of malicious or ignorant lies. (…) 2. ''vulgar slang'' Someone or something that is totally or utterly worthless, contemptible, or inferior.|work=[[Wikipedia:The Free Dictionary|''The Free Dictionary'']]|archivedate=20250710225907}}</ref>

"Piece/smear of bantha dung"<ref name="Aphra 2016 15" /><ref name="Han Chewie 10" /> is similar to the rude real-world idiom "piece of shit."<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://idioms.thefreedictionary.com/piece+of+shit|text=piece of shit|quote=1. ''rude slang'' A derisive term for something that one considers utterly worthless or inferior. (...) 2. ''rude slang'' A derisive term for someone whom one considers contemptible or deplorable.|work=[[Wikipedia:The Free Dictionary|''The Free Dictionary'']]|archivedate=20250711002749}}</ref>

The saying "a burr [[seed]] in bantha dung"<ref name="Aphra 2016 22" /> is similar to the real-world idiom "a needle in a haystack."<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/needle-in-a-haystack|text=a needle in a haystack|quote=something that is impossible or extremely difficult to find, especially because the area you have to search is too large|work=[[Wikipedia:Cambridge University Press and Assessment|''Cambridge Dictionary'']]|archivedate=20250710231559}}</ref>
