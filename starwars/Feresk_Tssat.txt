'''Feresk Tssat''', nicknamed "'''Frisk'''," was a [[Trandoshan]] [[Gender|male]] [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] who flew for the [[New Republic]]'s [[Vanguard Squadron]]. Frisk predominantly flew a [[BTL Y-wing starfighter]]. The [[squadron]] served in the [[Bormea sector]] around [[5 ABY]], but soon traveled to the [[planet]] [[Troithe]] to [[Battle of Troithe|assist]] [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]]'s [[Barma Battle Group]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. When the [[fighter wing]] launched a [[bulk freighter]] in an attempt to escape, Tssat, one of Syndulla's [[pilot]]s, asked [[Wyl Lark]] where the [[squadron commander]] wanted Vanguard Squadron. The 204th ultimately escaped the battle despite the efforts of the New Republic's forces.

==Biography==
{{Quote|This is General Syndulla to all New Republic forces. Vanguard Squadron is here.|Hera Syndulla|<ref name="Shadow Fall" />}}
At some point during Feresk's life, he worked as a [[smuggler]] who acquired valuable objects for paying clients. When Feresk, by his own accounts, accidentally procured a counterfeit [[painting]] for an Imperial [[governor]], who in turn showed it to [[Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], a [[death mark]] was placed on Feresk's head, forcing him to find sanctuary with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Squadrons" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Feresk Tssat<ref name="Squadrons" /> served as a [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] in the [[New Republic]]'s [[Vanguard Squadron]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> Around [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY">{{SW|url=news/shadow-fall-author-interview|text=In ''Shadow Fall'', the Dark Secrets of ''Alphabet Squadron'' Revealed}} establishes that ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'' takes place approximately half a [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], while ''[[Before the Awakening]]'' states [[Kes Dameron]] and [[Shara Bey]] moved to [[Yavin 4]] around six [[Standard month|months]] after the Battle of [[Endor]]. Given that ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates their move to [[5 ABY]], the events of ''Shadow Fall'' must also take place around that [[time]].</ref> the [[squadron]] was on a special assignment in the [[Bormea sector]] to help improve the New Republic's [[starship]] supply. [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] brought some forces to assist Vanguard Squadron, but soon received news that her own [[Barma Battle Group|battle group]] had been [[Attack on Cerberon|devastated]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] during its [[Cerberon system campaign|campaign]] in the [[Cerberon system]]. Syndulla subsequently took Vanguard Squadron, Tssat included, and<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> the squadron's headquarters,<ref name="Databank Temperance">{{Databank|the-temperance|The Temperance}}</ref> the [[MC75 Star Cruiser]] ''[[Temperance]]'', to the Cerberon [[Star system|system]], where some of her [[battle group]]'s remaining forces were [[Battle of Troithe|fighting]] the 204th at the [[planet]] [[Troithe]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

The squadron engaged the [[fighter wing]], which soon launched a [[bulk freighter]] from the surface of Troithe in an attempt to escape. As one Vanguard [[pilot]] reported a 204th squadron seemingly docking into the [[freighter]], Tssat asked [[Wyl Lark]],a senior pilot from Syndulla's remaining forces, where the [[squadron commander]] wanted Vanguard Squadron. Lark attempted to make the fighter wing surrender, but chose to have the New Republic forces pursue the bulk freighter when the enemy did not comply. Ultimately, the 204th launched missiles to distract the New Republic, and the fighter wing escaped aboard the freighter while under pursuit from Vanguard pilots.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Keo, I ain't racing you. I learned my lesson the first twelve times.|Frisk, to Keo Venzee|<ref name="Squadrons" />}}
[[File:FriskPiloting-SWU.jpg|250px|thumb|right|Tssat served the New Republic as part of the Vanguard Squadron]]
Feresk was a Transdoshan male with pale pink scales and orange eyes. The coloration of his pale pink scales were the result of his [[Albinism]].<ref name="Frisk Databank">{{Databank|frisk|Frisk}}</ref> He wore a blue flight suit with a standard New Republic helmet. His helmet had blue trim and orange Alliance logos on the front half of each side. Feresk wasn't shy about his loyalties and desires to make credits, but in a dogfight he was always dedicated to his fellow squadron pilots.<ref name="Squadrons" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I like Trandoshans, probably from writing Qyzen Fess for SWTOR, so I wanted to have trandoshan scoundrel in Squadrons.|Joanna Berry|<ref name="Insider 206">{{InsiderCite|206|The Squadrons Scribe}}</ref>}}
Feresk Tssat was created for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]'',<ref name="Squadrons" /> released on [[October 2]], [[2020]].<ref name="SW.com trailer">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-squadrons|text=''Star Wars: Squadrons'', a New Space Combat Game, Coming October 2}}</ref> Tssat was voiced by [[James Arnold Taylor]].<ref name="Squadrons" /> The character was first pictured in the reveal trailer for ''Star Wars: Squadrons'' on [[June 15]], 2020<ref name="Squadrons Trailer">{{EASWYouTube|w0eRkhR1z6A|Star Wars: Squadrons &ndash; Official Reveal Trailer|sw_url=video/reveal-trailer-star-wars-squadrons}}</ref> and first appeared, albeit only through voice, in the novel ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]]<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> and published on [[June 23]], 2020.<ref name="Penguin-Hardcover">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/609592/shadow-fall-star-wars-by-alexander-freed/9781984820044|text=Shadow Fall (Star Wars)}}</ref> While the novel only provided the name Tssat,<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> ''Star Wars: Squadrons'' identified his forename and nickname of "Frisk."<ref name="Squadrons" />

''Squadrons'' writer [[Joanna Berry]], who was responsible for conceiving the concept and characters, wanted to have a Trandoshan scoundrel character in the game based on a fondness for the species that she attributed to having written the Trandoshan [[Qyzen Fess]] in the online role-playing game ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''. Berry stated that the nature of ''Squadrons'' required the characters to be distinct while also maintaining a balance of character types within the squadron.<ref name="Insider 206" />
