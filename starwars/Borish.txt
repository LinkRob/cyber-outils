'''Borish''' was a spoken [[language]] that was used in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="SWKids">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Language in Star Wars Movies|video=CZp8Civk2co|nolive=1}}</ref> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Borish was spoken by a [[Limb|tentacled]] individual in the [[Prison|dungeon]] of [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="SWKids" />

==Behind the scenes==
Borish first appeared in the [[1983]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="Return of the Jedi">{{Film|VI}}</ref> It was first identified in the ''[[Star Wars By the Numbers]]'' video "Every Language in Star Wars Movies," which was posted on the [[StarWarsKids.com|Star Wars Kids]] YouTube channel in [[2019]].<ref name="SWKids" />
