A '''computer core''' was a type of [[computer]] system that could be installed in [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] and store data. The ISD [[Vigilance (Imperial-class)|''Vigilance'']] had a damaged computer core which stored information regarding the [[ambush of a rebel convoy at Derra]]. The information was archived in a file named "Derra IV" in the Military Operations section of the [[Imperial Archives]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] and [[Resistance]] paramilitary member [[Beaumont Kin]] cited the information from the ''Vigilance''{{'s}} computer core in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise" />
