'''Edwina Pellikka''' worked in the costume department on production of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. Pellikka experimented with a variety of color variations before creating the final camouflage design for the [[Endor]] [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] commandos.
