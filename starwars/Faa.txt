'''Faa''', also known as '''faa fish''' or '''big-mouth faa''',<ref name="Survivor" /> were a [[species]] of [[scalefish]] native to the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. They lived alongside six other scalefish species in the [[water]]s around the [[city]] [[Otoh Gunga]] in [[Lake Paonga]], and could also be found in the [[Naboo Abyss]] where they were preyed upon indiscriminately by the large [[colo claw fish]] and [[opee sea killer]]s that dwelt there.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> 

==Biology and appearance==
Unlike other scalefish species, which were mostly harmless, the faa was a vicious [[Diet|carnivore]]<ref name="VE" /> They had a wide, flat head with a narrow midsection that ended in a large paddle-like tail. Small pectoral fins were located on the side of the head. Their skin was a graduated orange color with blue stripes.<ref name="Bestiary" />

==History==
Faa were present in the waters of the planet Naboo. Jedi Master [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], his [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], and the Gungan [[Jar Jar Binks]] encountered some faa as they swam to Otoh Gunga to meet with its representatives during the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo]].<ref name="Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace"/>

By [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Survivor Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> there were only a few faa left on the planet [[Koboh]] due to them being harvested for their succulent glands.<ref name="Survivor" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Survivor}}
[[Skoova Stev]], a [[fisher]]man who resided on Koboh, caught a big-mouth faa<ref name="Survivor" /> in 9 BBY<ref name="Survivor Date" /> and put it in a fish tank at [[Pyloon's Saloon]].<ref name="Survivor" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Faa_scalefish.png|250px|thumb|right|The faa fish]]
Faa first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace">{{Film|I}}</ref> They were first identified in the Star Wars Legends video game [[Star Wars Episode I: The Gungan Frontier|Star Wars: Episode I The Gungan Frontier]].<ref name="Star Wars Episode I: The Gungan Frontier">[[Star Wars Episode I: The Gungan Frontier|''Star Wars'' Episode I: ''The Gungan Frontier'']]</ref> They were confirmed to be canon in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]].''<ref name="AEYNTK" /> The species was first identified as big-mouth faa in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'', which was developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]] and published by [[Electronic Arts]] on [[April 28]], [[2023]].<ref name="Survivor Release">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-jedi-survivor-is-here|text=''Star Wars Jedi: Survivor ''Is Here!|date=2023-04-28|quote=''Star Wars Jedi: Survivor'', the sequel to 2019's modern classic ''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'', has arrived for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X and S, and PC.}}</ref>

Faa, along with fellow scalefish [[doo]], [[ray]], [[mee]], [[see]], [[laa]], [[tee]], appear to be a reference to the real-world [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solf%C3%A8ge musical scale], i.e, "Do, Re, Me, Fa, So, La, Ti, Do."
