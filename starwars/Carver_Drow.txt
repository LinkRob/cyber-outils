'''Carver Drow''' was a [[human]] [[Jedi Master]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He was the master of [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Celesta Kami]] and trained on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]].<ref name="The Tale of Short Spire" />

==Biography==
At some point during the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]], and [[Nubs]] traveled to the [[Batuu Jedi temple|Jedi temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] and trained with [[Jedi Master|Master]] Drow and his [[Padawan]] [[Celesta Kami]]. During their time on Batuu, Drow sent the other [[Jedi]] to [[Black Spire Outpost]] to get tools for a project he was working on involving the natural rock spires on the planet. At the [[Black Spire Outpost Market]], they ran into [[Ozon Nimbee]], who was trying to convince the people of Black Spire to cut down the spires to build a futuristic [[spaceport]]. After revealing his scheme to the locals, Nimbee released [[Pistoeka sabotage droid|buzz droids]] to cut down the spires. Drow would show up to help destroy the buzz droids and oust Nimbee from the planet.<ref name="The Tale of Short Spire" />

During the youngling's stay on Batuu, Drow demonstrated a new [[lightsaber]] technique he was working on. Kai struggled to perform the technique and became discouraged. Drow, sensing the youngling's frustration, brought him to a [[Unidentified cave|cave]] outside of Black Spire Outpost where Kai experienced [[Force vision]]s of other individuals including [[Bell Zettifar]], [[Ember]], [[Djovi Resmia]], [[Cyrus Vuundir|Taborr Val Dorn]], and Master [[Yoda]]. Kai left the cave understanding that Jedi cannot be perfect and found Drow outside [[Meditation|meditating]] on a rock while waiting.<ref name="The Tale of Short Spire" />

During the [[Battle of Tenoo]], after Brightstar sent out a distress message, Drow arrived at the [[Tenoo Jedi Temple|Tenoo Temple]] along with Master [[Zia Zaldor Zanna]]. The two Masters used [[telekinesis]] to throw the pirate [[Sellaccc Orryak]] into her lieutenants [[Jooro Jarrot]] and [[Toda-Joh]].<ref name="The Battle of Tenoo" />

As an adult, Drow was assigned to a Jedi Temple on [[Batuu]] where he discovered a [[Unidentified Jedi weapon|powerful Jedi weapon]]. After discussing it with [[Yoda]], it was decided to bury it deep within the planet as it was too dangerous if it fell into the wrong hands.<ref name="GE-EotE1">[[Galaxy's Edge – Echoes of the Empire 1|''Galaxy's Edge – Echoes of the Empire'' 1]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Carver Drow is a character who was first seen in the [[2023]] [[Disney Junior]] and [[Disney+]] animated television series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'', specifically in the episodes "[[The Tale of Short Spire]]", "[[The Caves of Batuu]]", and "[[The Prince and the Pirate]]". He was voiced by [[Keston John]].<ref name="The Tale of Short Spire" />
