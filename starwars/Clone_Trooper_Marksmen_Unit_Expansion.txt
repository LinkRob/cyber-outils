The '''Clone Trooper Marksmen Unit Expansion''' ('''SWQ23''') is an expansion to the tabletop game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. An updated version of the earlier [[Phase II Clone Troopers Unit Expansion]], it will be released on [[November 7]], [[2025]] as part of Atomic Mass Games' overhaul of the game.

==Publisher's summary==
''Clone troopers are skilled with a wide variety of weapons, however, the DC-15 has become the mainstay of the clone army. Clone Marksmen use the DC-15 blaster rifle alongside heavy weapons to create a withering hail of accurate firepower. This'' Star Wars: ''Legion Clone Trooper Marksmen box adds 11 Clone Trooper Marksmen miniatures, enough for 1 Corps unit. This is an excellent next-step expansion for players looking to build their Galactic Republic larger than a Recon-level army.''

==Contents==
*11 Miniatures
*1 Unit Card
*4 Upgrade Cards
