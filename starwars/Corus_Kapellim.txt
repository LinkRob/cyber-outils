'''Corus Kapellim''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[smuggler]] from [[Brix]]. A self-styled "gentleman flyer," Kapellim specialized in stolen gems and priceless art. As he found bulk smuggling distasteful, he was more concerned about the challenge rather than the riches he stole. During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], Kapellim often stole objects left behind by those fleeing from First Order occupation, returning them to their owners for a large fee.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Equipment==
Kapellim wore a flight suit with [[Dactillion-leather]] [[glove]]s, a [[Clone Wars]]-era [[Valahari flight harness]] and an antique phonendoscope that monitored his vital signs.<ref name="TROS VD" />
