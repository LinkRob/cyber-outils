'''Boss''', designated '''RC-1138''', was an elite [[clone commando]] [[Clone Sergeant|sergeant]] who led the elite [[special forces]] [[Delta Squad]] during the [[Clone Wars]], serving with [[commando]]s [[Fixer (clone commando)|Fixer]], [[Sev]], and [[Scorch]]. Delta Squad, under Boss's leadership, participated in some of the war's most dangerous missions.

==Biography==
[[File:MasterHalseyReturned-WOTM.png|thumb|left|300px|Boss and Delta Squad return Master Halsey's body to the Jedi Temple.]]
RC-1138,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> who took on the name Boss, was a [[human]] [[Cloning|clone]]<ref name="Episode Guide" /> who was [[Reproduction|grown]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] from the [[DNA]] of [[Jango Fett]], a [[bounty hunter]] who became the [[Clone template|template]] of the [[clone trooper]] [[army]].<ref name="Clone Troopers">{{Databank|clone-troopers|Clone Troopers}} states that [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]] were based on the [[clone template]] of [[Jango Fett]], a [[human]] [[Gender|male]], and were created on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]]. As the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Witches of the Mist]]" establishes that RC-1138 was a clone [[trooper]], they must therefore be a [[Cloning|clone]] of Jango Fett.</ref> RC-1138<ref name="Join the Battle" /> served as a [[clone commando]] within the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], and he participated in the pan-[[The galaxy|galactic]] [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Witches of the Mist" /> serving as the leader of the [[special forces]] unit [[Delta Squad]] with the [[military rank]] of [[Clone Sergeant]].<ref name="Join the Battle" />

Boss and Delta [[Squad]], which included second-in-command [[Fixer (clone commando)|Fixer]], [[Sev]], and [[Scorch]], participated in some of the war's most dangerous missions.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> At one point, he and his squad were fielded into a battle from a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i]] with the mission of taking control of a location.<ref name="Seizing Territory">{{Destiny|set=Spark of Hope|cardname=Seizing Territory|link=https://swdestinydb.com/card/11129}}</ref> Boss and his squad were also dispatched to the [[planet]] [[Devaron]] in the aftermath of the [[Devaron massacre|massacre]] and recovered the bodies of [[Jedi Master]] [[Halsey]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Knox]], and returned them to [[Coruscant]]. The [[squad]] was met on the landing platform by [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] members [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Plo Koon]], [[Saesee Tiin]], and [[Adi Gallia]], who noted that the massacre was of similar caliber of the damage done by [[Asajj Ventress]] who was presumed [[Death|dead]]. Security footage of the battle was recovered and included a [[hologram]] of Halsey's death at the hands of an [[Savage Opress|as-yet unknown]] [[assassin]].<ref name="Witches of the Mist" />

==Personality and traits==
As a [[human]] clone<ref name="Join the Battle" /> of Jango Fett,<ref name="Clone Troopers" /> RC-1138 was 1.83 [[meter]]s in height, or six [[Foot|feet]], and was born on the planet [[Kamino]].<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Skills and abilities==
As a clone commando and as a sergeant, RC-1138 was an elite soldier and had many abilities and training. These abilities included leadership skills, armed and unarmed combat, military tactics, infiltration, reconnaissance, sniper skills, and [[jetpack]] training.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Equipment==
While on duty, RC-1138 was equipped with unique [[Katarn-class commando armor|''Katarn''-class Commando Armor]] that was painted in [[Color|orange]] and white, and was outfitted with a [[backpack]]. During combat, RC-1138 utilized two [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|DC-17 hand blasters]].<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
RC-1138 "Boss" first appeared in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|season three]] episode "[[Witches of the Mist]]" of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' that released on [[January 21]], [[2011]]. Supervising Director [[Dave Filoni]] requested that the team incorporate Delta Squad into the plot during the episode's development. RC-1138 first appeared in the [[2005]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Republic Commando]]'', in which he was voiced throughout by [[Temuera Morrison]]. RC-1138's nickname, "Boss," was established in the accompanying episode guide for "Witches of the Mist,"<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/witches-of-the-mist-episode-guide|text="Witches of the Mist" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> while the designation number, RC-1138, was established in the [[2021]] canon reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'' by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Join the Battle" /> RC-1138's designation is another instance of [[1138 (easter egg)|1138]] being used in ''[[Star Wars]]''.
