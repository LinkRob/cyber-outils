During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Galactic Empire]] '''ambushed''' [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Commander]] [[Arhul Narra]] of [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]]. Narra and members of Red Squadron were [[Death|killed]] during the ambush.<ref name="HC3" />

{{Battle-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The ambush on Red Squadron was mentioned in [[canon]] through [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 3|''Star Wars Helmet Collection'' 3]].<ref name="HC3" /> The battle originated in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] as an [[Battle of Derra IV|engagement]] in the [[Derra system/Legends|Derra system]] before the [[Battle of Hoth/Legends|Battle of Hoth]].<ref name="XWRS25">[[X-Wing Rogue Squadron 25|''X-Wing Rogue Squadron'' 25]]</ref> The concept of a pre-[[Hoth]] [[Ambush of a rebel convoy at Derra|ambush at Derra]] would be introduced into canon in ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> No source has yet to link the battles mentioned in ''Star Wars Helmet Collection'' 3 and ''Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy'', as they were in Legends.
