'''Asharl panthers''' were large, aggressive [[feline]]s native to the frigid [[planet]] [[Ilum]] in the [[Unknown Regions]]. They lived in Ilum's Northern and Southern hemispheres.<ref name="Nexus of Power" />

==Biology and appearance==
Asharl panthers had broad, earless heads, a pronounced brow ridge, expressive faces, and a short, blunt snout. They had a pair of [[Limb|tentacle]]-like sensory organs on their shoulders. They lived in family groups of two to four. They also had smooth, dense [[Hair|fur]], [[color]]ed in shades of white, blue, and gray, that held their body warmth in. They were two to three meters long, around one meter tall at the shoulders, and had two-toed feet with non-retractable claws. Their [[eye]]s were golden in color.<ref name="Nexus of Power" />

==Behind the scenes==
Asharl panthers were first mentioned in ''[[Nexus of Power]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]].<ref name="Nexus of Power" />
