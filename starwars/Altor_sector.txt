The '''Altor sector''' was a [[Sector/Legends|sector]] of [[Realspace/Legends|space]]<ref name="Twinkle">{{VoyagesCite|13|Twinkle, Twinkle Little Star, How I Wonder Where We Are}}</ref> that shared its name with [[Altor 14]], a [[Planet/Legends|planet]] in the [[Roti-Ow system]]<ref name="CSWE RotiOw">{{CSWECite|III|111|Roti-Ow system}}</ref> of the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories']] [[Zoraster sector/Legends|Zoraster sector]].<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Altor sector was mentioned in "[[Twinkle, Twinkle Little Star, How I Wonder Where We Are]]," a [[1990]] [[roleplaying game]] source article written by [[Glen Allison]] as a supplement for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' and published in the [[Voyages SF 13|thirteenth issue]] of the ''[[Voyages SF]]'' magazine. The article placed the [[Adoris system/Legends|Adoris]], Altor, [[Kinyen system/Legends|Kinyen]], [[Paulking system/Legends|Paulking]], [[Peluchia system/Legends|Peluchia]], and [[Thila system/Legends|Thila]], in the Altor sector. "Togor" [[Star system/Legends|system]] is also mentioned in the article but is attributed to ''[[Strike Force: Shantipole]]'' of which appearances, [[Pothor system/Legends|Pothor system]] is the closest match. The article initially placed the [[Thanos system]] (in which the planet [[Togoria system|Togoria]] is canonically placed) in the erroneously spelled, "[[Tau Sekar sector]]", prior to the ''[[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion]]'' placing Thanos system in the [[Taldot sector]].<ref name="EAOC" /> 

Since the article was released outside of the [[Lucas Licensing]] process, it is considered non-canon,<ref name="Unlicensed">{{UnlicensedStatus}}</ref> and the [[StarWars.com]] [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' has since instead placed all of the abovementioned systems in other sectors.<ref name="EAOC" />
