"'''A Bith Saved Is A Bith Learned'''" is an adventure in ''[[Galaxy Guide 7: Mos Eisley]]''.

==Plot summary==
A [[Sleeft Docking Bay Maintenance]] [[Freighter/Legends|freighter]] was checked out by a [[Bith/Legends|Bith]] named [[Angor Farn]]. He accidentally got the wrong ship: the cargo is a load of high-quality [[Droid/Legends|droid]] parts, stolen from of [[Armanda Durkin]]'s warehouses by Sleeft.
