'''Armen Sim''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who formerly served as a [[commander]] during the [[Clone Wars]] within the [[Humbarine system fleet]] and later the [[Imperial Navy]]. During the Clone Wars he had worked together in [[Republic Intelligence]] with the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Urbra Procura]] and also a [[Unidentified Balosar Jedi General|Balosar Jedi General]], of whom he later kept a [[hologram]] as his only memorabilia. He was considered a decorated war hero and [[Sim family|had three estranged children]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], Sim began living in an [[Armen Sim's apartment|apartment]] in the [[Imperial Elderblock 26-0640]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] paid for by his eldest son [[Wavell Sim|Wavell]] and was a neighbor of Procura. Sim was generally distant and had no visitors. At some point, when Sim was over the age of 73, the [[Nurse|orderly]] [[Strick]] thought that he wanted to betray Procura to the [[Galactic Empire]] and murdered Sim by giving him an overdose of medication to protect her friend, leading to him dying peacefully in his sleep, with the [[Unidentified staff doctor|staff doctor]] ruling it a death from natural causes. The following day, Procura and [[Inspector]] [[Noh (Imperial Security Bureau)|Noh]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] both [[Investigation of the murder of Armen Sim|investigated]] Sim's death. Both search Sim's room. Procura found an Imperial [[comlink]] and the hologram of the Jedi inside his room, but was caught by his arriving family. Later at the evening, Strick confessed to Procura, but was killed almost immediately afterwards by Noh. The Inspector however didn't suspect the Procura as the Jedi.<ref name="Elderblock" />
