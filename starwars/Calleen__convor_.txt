'''Calleen'''<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> was a [[convor]] owned by the [[Jedi Master]] [[Lene Kostana]], replacing her previous convor, [[Ferana]]. Kostana named the convor after [[Calleen]], the [[Altiri]] [[Deity|goddess]] of wisdom. [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]] later used Calleen to follow the corrupt [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[inspector]] [[Sartori]] to the lair of the [[crime lord]] [[Cenevax]]. When Cenevax captured Dooku and his [[Padawan]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], Calleen attacked the crime lord, but Cenevax [[Death|killed]] the convor.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
