'''Circusci Cus''' was a building in the [[city]] of [[Cliffhold]] on [[Iego]]. Like so may other buildings in the city, it was shaped like a round tower. Large, [[Color|purple]] marquees written in [[Aurebesh]] ran down the sides of the building, and spelled out the name of the establishment.<ref name="Mystery of a Thousand Moons" />
