'''Bergruutfa,''' also shortened to "'gruutfa," were large docile pachyderms. 

==Biology and appearance==
Bergruutfa had tusked mouths that produce a large amount of drool that required cleaning. The animals were very skittish and easily startled by the small scuttling lizards. There was a market for their dung.<ref name="Vengeance" />

==History==
[[Okut Dand]] was hired to haul a consignment of bergruutfa to the [[Marat system]]. [[Yana Ro]] and [[Werth Plouth]] joined the ship's crew in exchange for passage to [[Dalna]].<ref name="Vengeance" />
