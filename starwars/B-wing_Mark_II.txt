The '''B-wing Mark II''' was a [[starfighter]] model manufactured by [[Slayn & Korpil]]<ref name="RSOWM" /> and was in action before the [[Cold War]]. Some of its parts were used by [[Resistance]] [[technician]]s to assemble [[Resistance transport|troop transports]] and [[Resistance transport pod|transport pods]].<ref name="ICS" />

==Characteristics==
[[File:ResistanceTransportPod-RiseoftheResistance.png|thumb|250px|left|Resistance transport pods were created from the Mark II cockpit module.]]
The B-wing Mark II had a rounded cockpit with space for a single [[pilot]]. It was armed with at least an [[R-9X heavy laser cannon]], and was equipped with a precision [[targeting laser]]. These systems, as well as the cockpit and life-support, were used by [[Resistance]] technicians in the construction of their [[Resistance transport|troop transport]].<ref name="ICS" />
