'''Dwitzel''' was a [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Twi'lek/Legends|Twi'lek]] [[Mercenary/Legends|mercenary]] who operated during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|On the way children, we now know who has to die...|Dwitzel|<ref name="Casus Belli 67">{{CasusBelliCite|67|Les Rats de Dwitzel}}</ref>}}
At a very young age, Dwitzel was sold into slavery. She spent several years in a [[Brothel/Legends|brothel]] on [[Waltar]], before killing four people with a broken bottle and escaping. She then enlisted herself in the [[Republic Army]] and quickly became the first [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Officer/Legends|officer]] of the spaceborne [[Commando/Legends|commandos]].<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

After [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] [[Declaration of a New Order|became Emperor]], Dwitzel was expelled from the army because of the new [[Humanocentrism|humanocentric]] policy. She came to [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]], and lived from agricultural material reparations.<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

However, she did not forget her military past and, at the same time, founded a [[Mercenary/Legends|mercenary]] unit known as the [[Dwitzel's Rats]]. Her operatives showed her a fanatical devotion, as she was an excellent soldier, competent with both ranged and melee weapons as well as a fantastic leader.<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

==Personality and traits==
A [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Twi'lek/Legends|Twi'lek]], Dwitzel acted as a cold professional in all circumstances. She never smiled and, when extremely concentrated, she sported a deep melancholy.<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

She was smart and cunning, and disliked the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dwitzel was created by [[Jean Balczesak]] for an ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] Gamemaster help which appeared in the French magazine ''[[Casus Belli 67]]''. She was illustrated by [[Fred Blanchard]].<ref name="Casus Belli 67" />
