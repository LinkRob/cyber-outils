'''Defancio Storsilt''' was an [[alien]] that was in the [[Canto Bight]] [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|casino]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[34 ABY]]. As [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] carries on immediately from the events of ''The Force Awakens'', it can be assumed to take place in the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> Defancio had [[Color|brown]] skin and gray [[hair]].<ref name="TLJVD" /> Defancio was the owner of the racing [[fathier]] [[Hy-Stepping]].<ref name="RTRF">''[[The Last Jedi: Rose Tico: Resistance Fighter]]''</ref> According to [[Bargwill Tomder]], Storsilt owned almost all of the fathiers in Canto Bight.<ref name="HNSNSN">{{StoryCite|story=Hear Nothing, See Nothing, Say Nothing|book=Canto Bight (novella collection)}}</ref>
