A '''cooler valve''' was an item used in [[starship]] repairing. At some point by<ref name="FITYMI">{{StoryCite|story=Fake It Till You Make It|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">"[[Fake It Till You Make It]]" takes place concurrently with {{Film|V}}, the events of which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> while at the [[shipyard]]s of [[Musca]], the [[Unidentified maintenance droid (ML-08)|droid]] [[ML-08]] went to look for a cooler valve for the ''[[Rabbit's Foot]]'', the [[WUD-500 star yacht|ship]] of his master, the [[Lepi]] [[Gender|businessman]] [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]]. However, due to his poor memory, the [[Maintenance droid|droid]] got lost, forgetting where the ''Rabbit'' was docked and why they had come to Musca in the first place.<ref name="FITYMI" />

==Behind the scenes==
A cooler valve was mentioned in "[[Fake It Till You Make It]]," a short story written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in the [[2020]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''.<ref name="FITYMI" />
