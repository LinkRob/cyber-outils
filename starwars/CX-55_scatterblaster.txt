The '''CX-55 scatterblaster''' was a model of [[Scattergun|scatterblaster]] used by the [[Alazmec of Winsit]], a group of [[Alazmec]] cultists that was drawn to the [[Corvax Fen]] on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]]. The [[blaster]] had a [[Color|brown]] [[stock]] and a long black [[barrel]], with sections of silver-colored paneling around the muzzle and just in front of the trigger.<ref name="TROS VD" /> The weapon fired yellow blaster bolts.<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}} to [[35 ABY]].</ref> [[First Order military|forces]] of the [[First Order]] led by [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] [[Mission to Mustafar (First Order)|fought]] the Alazmec in the fen in order to obtain a [[Vader's wayfinder|Sith wayfinder]]. During the battle, at least two cultists used CX-55 scatterblasters while fighting Ren,<ref name="TROS" /> but the weapons were no match for his [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|lightsaber]],<ref name="Junior">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> and he deflected their shots with his [[Lightsaber|blade]] and then used it to [[Death|kill]] the two cultists.<ref name="TROS" />

==Behind the scenes==
The CX-55 scatterblaster first appeared in the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="TROS" /> The weapons were not identified in the film but received a name in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
