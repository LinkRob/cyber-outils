'''Coruscanti Society #4125''' was an issue of the periodical publication ''[[Coruscanti Society]]'' which contained a report of [[Penn Pershing]]'s speech on the [[Galaxies Opera House]] as part of his [[New Republic Amnesty Program]] duties. A photograph showing Pershing and three Coruscanti socialites was later used by [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] on his [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' for the chapter "Truth and Reconciliation".<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Coruscanti Society 4125 was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]], which was published by [[Dorling Kindersley]] in [[July 9]] [[2024]].<ref name="Rise" /> The image used for the issue's report is a still from ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'' [[The Mandalorian Season Three|Season Three]] [[Chapter 19: The Convert]].<ref name="Convert">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 19: The Convert}}</ref>
