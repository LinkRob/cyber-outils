'''2013 Topps ''Star Wars'' Jedi Legacy''' is a trading card set released by [[Topps]] on [[May 22]], [[2013]]. This collection of 135 cards includes 90 Base Cards that tell the story of [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke]] and [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]], and 45 Insert Cards with three different themes:
*''Connections'' (15 cards depicting characters who played a role in both Luke and Anakin/Vader's journeys)
*''Influences'' (9 characters who influenced Luke and 9 characters who influenced Anakin/Vader)
*''The Circle Is Now Complete'' (12 cards illustrating 6 encounters between Luke and Vader).

==Release and reception==
Two sets of two exclusive promo cards were offered with [[Star Wars Insider 142|''Star Wars Insider'' 142]]. The two sets, belonging to "Challenge of a Falling Jedi" and "Battle through Blood," depicted the [[Battle of Coruscant/Legends#The Duel on the Invisible Hand|duel]] between Anakin Skywalker and Darth Tyranus, the [[Duel on Cloud City/Legends|duel]] between Darth Vader and Luke Skywalker, Luke Skywalker, and Darth Vader.
