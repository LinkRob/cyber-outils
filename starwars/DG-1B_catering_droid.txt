The '''DG-1B catering droid''',<ref name="HC 58">{{HelmetCollectionCite|58|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> also known as the '''baker droid''', was a model of [[service droid]], programmed to cater during social events. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] made use of [[Unidentified baker droid|a baker droid]] to aid in the preparation of a [[jogan fruitcake]] for a gathering she would be holding in honor of Senator [[Aang]]. As the [[cake]] still lacked its [[jogan fruit]] garnish, Amidala dispatched her droids to purchase some. As the gathering began before their arrival with the jogans, the baker droid questioned as to what they were going to do.<ref name="Evil Plans" />

The [[droid]]s had [[Color|blue]] sensors, [[Gender|masculine programming]], and wore a white apron.<ref name="Evil Plans" />
