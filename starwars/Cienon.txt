'''Cienon''' was a [[Human]] [[Gender|male]] renowned mathematician and brother of [[Saska Teft]]. He was wheelchair bound and rode on his custom-built [[Repulsorlift chair|hovering wheelchair]] his sister made for him. In an effort to escape the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]], he joined a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[convoy]] transporting civilians to a hidden rebel [[Planet|world]]. The Empire ambushed the convoy, and the [[transport]] he was on managed to jump to [[hyperspace]] and escape the battle. Eventually, the [[hyperdrive]] gave out near the planet [[Tatooine]], where the damaged ship crashed. Cienon, along with the other survivors, set up a camp where they stayed before being attacked by [[Captain]] [[Terro]] and his forces.<ref name="Legends" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cienon first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Assault]]'' companion app ''[[Legends of the Alliance]]'', in the "Jabba's Realm" campaign. Cienon has a chance of being one of the eight crash survivors present during the first mission, or guaranteed if the player chooses Saska Teft to be in the [[squad]]. Depending upon the actions and success of the player during the missions, Cienon's fate can vary. Cienon can end up being [[Slavery|enslaved]] by [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]], eaten by a [[rancor]], left behind at Terro's camp, or successfully have escaped Tatooine with the squad.<ref name="Legends" />
