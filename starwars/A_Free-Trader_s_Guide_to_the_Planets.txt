"'''A Free-Trader's Guide to the Planets'''" is an article written by [[Timothy S. O'Brien|Timothy O'Brien]] with illustrations by [[Steve Bryant]]. It was published in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 10|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' ''10'']] in [[May]] [[1996]] by [[West End Games]].

==Contents==
This article serves as a supplementary piece for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', introducing the crew of the [[Smuggler/Legends|smuggling]] vessel ''[[Seventy-Seven Stars]]'' as they look through the [[Free-Trader's Guide]] for planets where they can hide from [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Moff/Legends|Moff]] [[Roff]]. It includes description of weather, sentient species, culture and creatures of the planets [[Chad/Legends|Chad]], [[Gamorr/Legends|Gamorr]], [[Kubindi/Legends|Kubindi]] and [[Toola/Legends|Toola]].
