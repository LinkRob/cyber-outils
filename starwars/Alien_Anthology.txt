'''''Alien Anthology''''' is a paperback supplement to the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]''. It was written by [[Steve Miller]] and [[Owen K.C. Stephens|Owen Stephens]] and published by [[Wizards of the Coast]] in October [[2001]]. A web enhancement named "Even More Aliens!", detailing three more species, was released on Wizards of the Coast's website.

A revised and expanded hardcover volume called the ''[[Ultimate Alien Anthology]]'' was released in [[2003]].
