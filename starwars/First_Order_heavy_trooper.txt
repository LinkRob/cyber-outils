The '''First Order heavy trooper''', also called the '''megablaster heavy assault trooper'''&mdash;so named for the [[FWMB-10 repeating blaster]] or "megablaster" that they carried&mdash;was a variant of the standard [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] used by the [[First Order]].<ref name="Visual" /> Also known in short as '''heavy troopers''', they carried web gear loaded with extra ammunition in order to power the megablaster they carry.<ref name="TLJ Visual">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

In [[34 ABY]], megablaster heavy assault troopers were deployed during the [[Battle of Kestro City|Battle of Kestro]] and the subsequent [[Skirmish on the Retribution]], where they fought against [[Iden Versio]], [[Zay Versio]], and [[Shriv Suurgav]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />
