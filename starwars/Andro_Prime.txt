'''Andro Prime''' was a terrestrial astronomical object where the [[pirate]] [[Phee Genoa]] at one point repaired the circuits of her [[power droid]], [[MEL]].<ref name="PoNR" /> During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|311}}</ref> Genoa reminded the [[droid]] of the incident and a similar event on<ref name="PoNR" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Entombed">{{TBB|Entombed}}</ref> [[Skara Nal]] while the pair stopped at a<ref name="PoNR" /> [[Unidentified lunar base|lunar base]]<ref name="PoNR AD">{{TBB|Point of No Return (The Bad Batch)}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> to get their ship,<ref name="PoNR" /> the ''[[Providence]]'',<ref name="Providence">{{Databank|providence|Providence}}</ref> [[Fuel|refueled]].<ref name="PoNR" />
