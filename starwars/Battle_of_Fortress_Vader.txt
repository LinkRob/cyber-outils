The '''Battle of Fortress Vader''' was fought on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]] at some point during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] when an army of [[Mustafarian]]s, led by [[Father (title)|Father]] [[Kkkt]], rose up to save what was left of their [[homeworld]] after [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] caused the planet to scream as he built his castle, [[Fortress Vader]]. During the battle, Vader proved a challenge to the Mustafarians, but Kkkt and the other chiefs used [[the Force]] to flood the battlefield with lava.

The [[Fortress Vader Imperial garrison]] was wiped out, with only Vader surviving. He retreated to the fortress as the remaining Mustafarians tried to destroy it and used the energies of the dark side locus under it to wipe the army out. With the Mustafarians dealt with, the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Momin]] confronted Vader in the focusing chamber to kill him. The two fought and Momin badly injured Vader, but was then crushed by a stone. Now alone in the fortress, Vader opened the door to the [[dark side of the Force]] and entered it.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Even the beasts themselves conspire to thwart my vision. Fascinating. Utterly fascinating.|Momin|<ref name="Dark Lord 23" />}}
[[File:FortressVaderFailure-2017DarthVader23.png|thumb|left|180px|With each failed attempt Vader and Momin continued to cause havoc for the Mustafarians]]
After [[Death|killing]] two rogue [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]] during a [[Coruscant Incident|chase]] on [[Coruscant]], [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] gave [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] the planet [[Mustafar]] to build himself a personal [[Fortress Vader|stronghold]].<ref name="Dark Lord 20">[[Darth Vader (2017) 20|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 20]]</ref> The [[fortress]] was designed by the disembodied spirit of an ancient [[Sith Lord]] named [[Darth Momin]]. The construction of the castle unleashed a great deal of [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] energy, which was sensed by a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Kkkt|elder]] of a nearby [[Kkkt's clan|tribe]] of [[Mustafarian]]s. Through several different iterations of construction [[Darth]] Vader and Momin attempted to open a dark side [[portal]]. Each [[time]] the portal started to open, there were cataclysmic effects on the surrounding landscape, which was extremely dangerous for the native tribes and fauna. There were small uprisings of Mustafarians over the castle's construction. Sometimes, large herds of [[lava flea]]s attacked the castle, which caused even Momin himself to marvel. None of these small uprisings proved to be more than the [[Fortress Vader Imperial garrison|Imperial garrison]] could handle.<ref name="Dark Lord 23" />

==The battle==
===Mustafarian resistance===
{{Quote|There will be no evacuation. There will be no retreat. You will destroy the attackers. You will teach them the meaning of Empire. Here. I will show you.|Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 24" />}}
When the construction was complete on the ninth iteration of Vader's castle, the [[Sith]] once again attempted to open the portal, but they were stopped by reports of another, unprecedented attack by the native population, led by [[Father (title)|Father]] Kkkt. Frustrated, Vader was forced to leave in order to aid his [[troop]]s in fending off the attack, lest the castle be overrun and destroyed. In Vader's absence, Momin once again activated the portal, despite his claims that he could not [[Force power|use]] [[the Force]] in his disembodied state. Momin's original body stepped through the portal, and the ancient Sith was fully resurrected, thousands of [[Standard year|years]] after his death.<ref name="Dark Lord 23" />

<div style="float:right">[[File:KkktAttacksFortressVader-2017DarthVader23.png|thumb|right|250px|Father Kkkt and his army of Mustafarians]]</div>
The [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[garrison]] found themselves quickly overwhelmed by the attackers, and attempted to retreat, but Vader was able to turn the tide of the battle and rally his troops against them. Seeing an opportunity to destroy Vader, Kkkt rallied more mystics of his tribe to call on the "[[Blood]] of Mustafar." Kkkt and his mystics were able to use the Force to summon a great wave of [[Magma|lava]] that washed over all of the [[Imperial Military|Empire's forces]], leaving no survivors except for Vader himself, who used the Force to [[Telekinesis|lift]] himself to safety, but not without serious damage to his [[Darth Vader's armor|suit]] and [[cybernetics]]. Vader reentered the [[Sith cave|cave]] beneath his castle in order to open the portal, which once again released cataclysmic levels of dark side energy, burning what was left of the Mustafarian resistance.<ref name="Dark Lord 24" />

===Duel in the Sith cave===
{{Quote|Momin. I warned you of the consequences for betrayal.|Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 24" />}}
[[File:LordMominsDeath-2017DarthVader24.png|thumb|left|200px|Momin's death at the hands of Vader]]
When Vader returned to the [[cave]], Momin attacked him and the two Sith Lords engaged in a [[lightsaber duel]]. During the fight, Momin taunted Vader, claiming that he would never be able to open the dark side door, as it would have rejected him. Momin lamented the apparent decline of the Sith's power in the thousands of years since his death, calling Vader and Sidious "[[Jedi]]-obsessed weaklings." Vader was gravely wounded during the duel, but as the ancient Sith Lord claimed the dark side loved him, Vader was able to reach out with the Force and crush Momin beneath an enormous [[stone]]. A disbelieving Momin died for the second time.<ref name="Dark Lord 24" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|From the looks of you, your search for knowledge was&hellip;challenging. That is good. Perhaps it means you learned something of value.|Palpatine, to Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 25">[[Darth Vader (2017) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 25]]</ref>}}
[[File:SithCavePortal-2017DarthVader25.jpg|thumb|right|180px|Vader enters the portal]]
With Momin dealt with, Vader opened the door to the dark side himself<ref name="Dark Lord 24" /> and his spirit left his body to enter it. On the other side, Vader saw his life pass before him through visions before reaching an illusion of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]]. There, he was faced by the visions of Jedi he had encountered in the past and he massacred them all. After killing an illusion of Palpatine, Vader then found his beloved, [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. He intended to resurrect her, but she instead jumped off the balcony where she stood and was destroyed. Heartbroken, Vader was soon distracted by a beam of light. The [[Luke Skywalker|individual]] within it overwhelmed him, and Vader's spirit was returned to his body. Having failed to resurrect his wife, Vader destroyed the portal and contacted Palpatine.<ref name="Dark Lord 25" /> Later, he returned Momin's mask,<ref name="Encyclopedia Maul">{{EncyclopediaCite|Darth Maul and Other Followers of the Dark Side}}</ref> which still contained the essence of the rogue Sith Lord,<ref name="Lando 3">[[Lando 3|''Lando'' 3]]</ref> to Palpatine.<ref name="Encyclopedia Maul" />
