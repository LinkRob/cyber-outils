The '''Almak sector''' was a [[sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western" /> Situated in grid square J-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it contained the [[Jiaan system]] and the [[Almak system]],<ref name="Appendix" /> the latter of which contained [[Almak]], [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Leffingite]] [[species]]. Many Leffingites were forced to flee Almak after they came under attack from the government during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Alien Archive" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Almak sector was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Almak sector/Legends|sector]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the StarWars.com [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Fry.<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> The Almak sector was depicted on a map designed by [[Modi]] and published in the [[2011]] StarWars.com article [https://web.archive.org/web/20170713024921/http://www.starwars.com/news/essential-atlas-extra-sectors-of-the-mid-rim The Essential Atlas Extra: Sectors of the Mid Rim].<ref name="Mid Rim">{{SW|url=news/essential-atlas-extra-sectors-of-the-mid-rim|text=Essential Atlas Extra: Sectors of the Mid Rim}}</ref>
