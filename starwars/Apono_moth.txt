'''Apono moths''', or simply '''aponos''', were a type of [[moth]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]. Due to the world's strong winds, the [[insect]]s developed strong [[Limb|winds]] that allowed them to stay airborne, although their fat bodies and tendency to stay at low altitudes made them a primary target as prey for the terrestrial [[gushiro]]. Apono moths' wings were also covered in vibrant and changing [[color]]s, which the creatures used to communicate between themselves.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Apono moths appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
