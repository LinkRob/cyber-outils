'''Cradossk''' was a [[Trandoshan]] [[Gender|male]] and the [[Parent|father]] of [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]]. A seasoned warrior and respected [[bounty hunter]], Cradossk shared a rivalry with the [[Wookiee]] [[chief]]tain [[Tarfful]], and, by the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]], Cradossk's son gained a reputation for [[hunting]] Wookiees. Despite adopting Cradossk's trade, Bossk was reluctant to talk about his father with young accomplice [[Ezra Bridger]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], Cradossk was a notable member of the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]], and he made a profit by providing services to the [[Galactic Empire]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Our agent in the Bounty Hunters Guild reports that sentiment is trending negative toward the Rebellion as the Empire increases its use of guild services. In other words, Cradossk is making a killing.|Excerpt of Captain Cassian Andor's report to General Davits Draven|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
[[File:Bossk-TCW.png|thumb|right|350px|Cradossk was the father of the bounty hunter Bossk (pictured).]]
A fearsome [[Trandoshan]] [[Gender|male]], Cradossk was an experienced warrior and respected [[bounty hunter]].<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> Like other Trandoshans,<ref name="Wookiee">{{SW|url=wookiee-history-gallery|text=Wookiee History Gallery}} {{C|Image 2}}</ref> Cradossk had a fierce rivalry with the [[Wookiee]]s of [[Kashyyyk]], including [[Tarfful]],<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> a Wookiee [[chief]]tain who fought against Trandoshan [[Hunting|hunters]].<ref name="Wookiee Hunt">{{TCW|Wookiee Hunt}}</ref>

Several [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Death Trap">{{TCW|Death Trap}}</ref> Cradossk's son, [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], and his daughter, [[Doshanalawook]]<ref name="FACPOV" /> were [[Reproduction|born]] on [[Trandosha]],<ref name="Battlefront II" /> the [[species]]' [[homeworld]].<ref name="Trandosha">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/padawan-lost-trivia-gallery|text="Padawan Lost" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Image 4}}</ref> After hatching, Bossk ate his unborn siblings' eggs, including Doshanalawook's. Despite being told otherwise, Bossk failed to kill his sister and her cracked egg was given to the Wookiee [[Liswarr'arindoo]] in exchange for a bottle of [[Kowakian rum]].<ref name="FACPOV" /> Cradossk eventually taught Bossk how to [[Thief|steal]] [[landspeeder]]s. Like his [[Parent|father]],<ref name="Ezra's Gamble">''[[Ezra's Gamble]]''</ref> Bossk became a [[bounty]] hunter and a renowned Wookiee hunter.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> 

At some point, Cradossk expressed his disappointment with his son over his frequent failures in capturing the Wookiee [[Chewbacca]] and his companion [[Han Solo]], stating that he had doubts whether Bossk really was the right candidate to have devoured the nest eggs of his siblings and emerge as its only candidate.<ref name="FACPOV" /> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Chronology Note">In ''[[Ezra's Gamble]]'', [[Ezra Bridger]] is fourteen [[Standard year|years]] old. Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' establishes that Bridger was [[Reproduction|born]] in [[19 BBY]], ''Ezra's Gamble'' must therefore take place in [[5 BBY]].</ref> during the [[Imperial Era]], Bossk took a mission to the [[planet]] [[Lothal]], where he hired the street urchin [[Ezra Bridger]] as a guide. When the young [[human]] asked questions about Bossk's father, the Trandoshan became annoyed, stating that fathers were "overrated."<ref name="Ezra's Gamble" />

Prior to the start of the [[Galactic Civil War]], Cradossk was a notable member of the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> an institution designed to regulate the bounty-hunting profession.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> The [[Galactic Empire]] made use of the Guild's services, and, according to [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor]], an [[intelligence officer]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], Cradossk was "making a killing." Andor's statement was sent to [[General]] [[Davits Draven]], and the transmission was recorded in ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Personality and traits==
Cradossk had [[Color|orange]] [[eye]]s and green skin.<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> He had a hatred of Wookiees, which he passed on to his son, Bossk.<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> Cradossk was a untrustworthy individual who lied often, sometimes purposefully misleading his son Bossk while on a hunt so as to claim a bounty for himself.<ref name="FACPOV" />

==Equipment==
As a bounty hunter, Cradossk wore a red [[flight suit]] and armed himself with a [[Relby-v10 micro grenade launcher]], a weapon also used by Bossk.<ref name="Galactic Defense" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Join Tarfful and Cradossk to lead your forces into battle!|"Fierce Enemies" caption for ''Star Wars: Galactic Defense''|<ref name="Galactic Defense" />}}
Bossk's father was first referenced in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], without name, in ''[[Ezra's Gamble]]'', a junior novel written by [[Ryder Windham]] and published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] in [[2014]] as a prequel to the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''.<ref name="Ezra's Gamble" /> The bounty hunter Cradossk first appeared in the 2014 [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''. Although ''Galactic Defense'' did not state Cradossk's identity as Bossk's father,<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> a relationship originally established by author [[K. W. Jeter]] in the [[1998]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel ''[[The Mandalorian Armor]]'',<ref name="Mandalorian Armor">''[[The Mandalorian Armor]]''</ref> dialogue in the [[2017]] video game ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' confirmed as much.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

In ''Galactic Defense'', Cradossk and Tarrful were unlockable Champions during the limited-time event "Fierce Enemies." Cradossk could gain Champion Synergy with other bounty hunter Champions, namely Bossk, [[Cad Bane]], [[Dengar]], [[Boba Fett]], [[Jango Fett]], and [[Aurra Sing]].<ref name="Galactic Defense" />
