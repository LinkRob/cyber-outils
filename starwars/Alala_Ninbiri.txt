'''Alala Ninbiri''' was a<ref name="QfPX" /> [[Gender|non-binary]]<ref name="Email" /> [[prospector]] who captained a [[transport]]. They were known to [[Spence Leffbruk]] who avoided dealing with them due to a bad reputation. They often engaged in [[Smuggler|smuggling]] activities.<ref name="QfPX" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> they were present above [[Dalna]].<ref name="QfPX" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alala Ninbiri was mentioned in the [[2023]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="QfPX" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> When asked about the usage of singular "they/them" pronouns for Ninbiri, the author stated that they had intended the usage to indicate a [[Wikipedia:Non-binary gender|non-binary]] gender for the character.<ref name="Email" />
