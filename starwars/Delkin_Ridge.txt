'''Delkin Ridge''' was a location on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] where the [[bartender]] [[Ackmena]] and her [[Marriage|wife]] [[Sorschi]] lived<ref name="WDSTKH" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Delkin Ridge was first mentioned in the short story "[[We Don't Serve Their Kind Here]]," which was written by [[Chuck Wendig]] and released as part of the [[2017]] anthology book ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''.<ref name="WDSTKH" />
