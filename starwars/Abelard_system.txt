The '''Abelard system''' contained the planet [[Abelard]], on which there was an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] base at one point. Sometime after the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[Battle of Abelard|attacked]] the base and managed to steal a stabilised [[ytterbium]] cargo. The ''[[Solemnis]]'' was tasked to convey it to the [[Echo Base/Legends|Alliance base]] on [[Hoth/Legends|Hoth]] but disappeared before reaching [[Ristal]], one of its step.<ref name="CasusBelli" />

The system was at two days of the [[Aldorus system]] with a Class 2 [[Hyperdrive/Legends|hyperdrive]].<ref name="CasusBelli" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Abelard system was created by [[Jean Balczesak]] for the ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] scenario ''[[L'écume de l'espace]]'' which appeared in the French magazine [[Casus Belli 44|''Casus Belli'' 44]].<ref name="CasusBelli" />
