The '''electro-bisento''' was a shaft with a blade placed on the top. It was wielded by the [[First Guard]], one of eight [[Praetorian Guard|Elite Praetorian Guard]]s who served as the [[Bodyguard|protectors]] of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] of the [[First Order]].

==Description==
[[File:OnGuard-CM.png|thumb|left|270px|Two of the Elite Praetorian Guard wielded electro-bisentos.]]
Like all the other high-tech [[Melee weapon|weapons]] used by the [[Praetorian Guard|Elite Praetorian Guard]] to defend [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] from any threat, the tempered metal blade of the electro-bisento was connected to a compact [[ultrasonic generator]] that created a high-frequency vibration across the cutting edge, which increased its lethality in the process. Parallel to this, there was an [[plasma]] filament that created a highly energized blade capable of parrying a [[lightsaber]].<ref name="TLJVD" />

==History==
One of the three [[Praetorian Guard|Imperial Praetorian Guards]], assigned to Moff Gideon to aid in the [[Conflict on Mandalore|battle for Mandalore]], stabbed the [[Heavy Infantry Mandalorian]] [[Paz Vizsla]] with a Electro-bisento in the back of his leg before the [[Mandalorian warrior]] succumb to his death.<ref name="Chapter 23" />

[[File:FirstGuard-TLJVD.png|thumb|right|200px|The First Guard, wielder of an electro-bisento]]
In [[34 ABY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[34 ABY]]. As [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] carries on immediately from the events of ''The Force Awakens'', it can be assumed to take place in the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the [[First Guard]] and another member of the Elite Praetorian [[Bodyguard|Guard]] carried their electro-bisentos<ref name="TLJVD" /> aboard Supreme Leader Snoke's [[flagship]], the ''[[Supremacy]]'', during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. After Snoke was [[Battle of Oetchi|assassinated]] by his [[Dark side of the Force|dark]] apprentice, [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], the guards utilized their electro-bisentos along with other weapons against Ren and the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan|learner]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], both of whom wielded lightsabers. Despite their efforts to avenge Snoke, all eight Praetorian Guards were killed by the [[Force-sensitive]] pair. Kylo Ren fought both electro-bisento users. During the fight, Ren snatched an electro-bisento from one guard, briefly using it along with [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] against another before killing its owner. The First Guard overpowered Ren and almost killed him until Rey threw the [[Skywalker lightsaber]] to Ren.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>
