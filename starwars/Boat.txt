A '''boat''',<ref name="MT">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref> also known as a '''surface ship''',<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> or simply a '''ship''', was a vehicle designed to navigate or "sail," on [[water]]. Ships equipped for naval warfare were referred to as '''warships'''.<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> Examples of these types of vehicles were [[aircraft carrier]]s, [[submarine]]s, and [[waveskimmer]]s.<ref name="Dawn" /> By analogy, crafts designed to "navigate" in interstellar [[Realspace|space]] were referred to as [[starship]]s.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

Most [[Pamarthen]]s of the [[ocean]]-dominated [[planet]] [[Pamarthe]] had to learn how to sail a boat before they were taught how to fly a starship.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> The [[crime lord]] known as [[Big String]] once used a boat to try and escape the [[bounty hunter]] [[Chanath Cha]].<ref name="Lando 2">[[Lando 2|''Lando'' 2]]</ref> On the [[Endor]] Moon, the [[Ewok]]s who lived in [[Ewok stilt village|stilt villages]] built over lakes used small sailboats.<ref name="SWBF">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref>
