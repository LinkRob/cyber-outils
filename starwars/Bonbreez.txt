'''Bonbreez''' was a [[language]] spoken by the [[Bonbrak]]s, a [[Hair|furry]] diminutive [[species]] whose members acted as [[Technician|mechanics]].<ref name="Databank" /> Bonbreez was similar enough to [[Anzellan (language)|Anzellan]] that Bonbraks could act as translators for [[Anzellan]]s. In Bonbreez, "Vakateebakbak" meant "false statement," and "Ata" meant "can call," as in "Ata [[Teetak]]," meaning "my name is Teetak."<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
