'''Chocolate Chip and Dale''' was an [[ice cream]] flavor made with [[chocolate]], available for sale at the [[31 Hundred Flavors]] ice cream stand on [[Disney Playground Planet]]. The ice cream's name was mentioned on the Featured Flavors menu of the stand, with the shop operated by a [[Merchant|vendor]] serving a wide variety of ice cream flavors, including "[[Obi-Rum Kenobi]]," "[[I am Groot Beer]]," and "[[Wookies and Cream]]."<ref name="May12th" />

==Behind the scenes==
