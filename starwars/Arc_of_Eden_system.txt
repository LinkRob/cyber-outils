The '''Arc of Eden system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Immalia sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="The Interior" /> It was situated in grid square K-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the location known as the [[Arc of Eden]]. According to the [[human]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]],<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> by [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> the Arc of Eden served the best [[hot chocolate]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Arc of Eden system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
