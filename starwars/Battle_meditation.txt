'''Battle meditation''' was a [[The Force|Force]] [[Force power|ability]],<ref name="X-Wing" /> rare among [[Jedi]], that could be used to form connections to those around them.<ref name="Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>

During the [[Valo Atrocity]], [[Porter Engle]] used a form of battle meditation to unite the minds of other Jedi in order to fly their [[starfighter]]s in formation with him.<ref name="THR Char Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Adi Gallia]] used battle meditation whilst in an engagement [[pilot]]ing her [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]] alongside a [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]] over a [[planet]].<ref name="X-Wing" /> [[Jedi Master]]s [[Mace Windu]], [[Oppo Rancisis]]<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> and [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] were skilled in battle meditation.<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'' </ref> During his [[Jedi training|training]] on [[Dagobah]], [[Luke Skywalker]] used battle meditation while lifting several rocks [[Telekinesis|telekinetically]] using the Force.<ref name="Legion">{{Legion|set=Core Set|cardname=Battle Meditation}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Battle meditation first appeared in the current [[canon]] in the mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'' as part of the move set of Grand Master Yoda,<ref name="GoH" /> who was added on [[February 9]], [[2016]].<ref name="Yoda trailer">{{EASWYouTube|DlJm0KByKpA|Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes - Grand Master Yoda trailer}}</ref> It later appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' as performed by Porter Engle,<ref name="Rising Storm" /> but Engle's actions were not outright confirmed to be a form of Battle Meditation until ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.<ref name="THR Char Encyclopedia" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity, [[Battle meditation/Legends|battle meditation]] was first mentioned in the [[1983]] novelization of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="RotJ n">[[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (novelization)|''Star Wars: Return of the Jedi'' novelization]]</ref>
