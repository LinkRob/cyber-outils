'''Aaron Neil''' is a British actor who portrays [[Aarton Chireen]] in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. He is known for co-starring as [[W:c:DoctorWho:Varun Singh|Varun Singh]] in the ''[[Wikipedia:Doctor Who|Doctor Who]]'' spin-off series ''[[Wikipedia:Class (2016 TV series)|Class]]''<ref name="Class">{{WebCite|url=https://www.tvmaze.com/characters/339645/class-varun-singh|text=Varun Singh|archiveurl=https://archive.is/fAg2B}}</ref> and Raj in the British comedy-drama series ''[[Wikipedia:Home (British TV series)|Home]]''.<ref name="Home">{{WebCite|url=https://www.sueterryvoices.com/news/aaron-neil-and-laurence-howarth-feature-in-comedy-series-home|text=Aaron Neil and Laurence Howarth feature in comedy series 'Home'|archiveurl=https://archive.is/nLjRq}}</ref>
