'''Dianoga''' were large [[Diet|omnivorous]] [[cephalopod]]s that [[Homeworld|hailed]] from the [[planet]] [[Vodran]] in the [[Si'Klaata Cluster]], located in [[Hutt Space]] region of [[the galaxy]]. Although primitive, they were actually [[Sentience|sentient]], and some dianoga were [[Force-sensitive|sensitive]] to [[the Force]].

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:Dianoga_SWGE-D23_video.jpg|thumb|left|250px|[[Toothy]], a juvenile dianoga]]
Dianoga were [[Sentience|sentient]]<ref name="The Baptist" /> [[cephalopod]]s characterized by seven suckered [[Limb|tentacles]], an [[eye]]stalk, a [[mouth]] of sharp [[Tooth|teeth]],<ref name="Join the Resistance" /> and several [[heart]]s.<ref name="The Baptist" /> They could grow to a length of 7<ref name="USW" /> to 10 [[meter]]s.<ref name="Databank" /> Their [[blood]] had a [[Color|blue]] tint.<ref name="The Baptist" /> Dianoga were [[Diet|omnivores]],<ref name="ANH paperback novel" /> feeding on smaller animals like [[fish]] and [[crab]]s, but also feed on [[bone]]s and aquatic [[plant]]s.<ref name="The Baptist" /> The [[trash squid]] was part of the Dianoga family.<ref name="Creatures Part 2" />

Although they could survive in the open air for short periods of time, dianoga were entirely dependent on [[water]], lest they completely dried out. While physically [[Gender|hermaphroditic]], dianoga could choose to identify as female, diangous (the most common gender), or male. The [[Reproduction|mating]] process involved partners exchanging [[egg]]s with one another. The normal skin color of a mature individual was a deep purple, but dianoga could change their color and patterns for active [[camouflage]]. They could notably turn black, gray, and even transparent. They also had the ability to [[Regeneration|regenerate]] lost limbs.<ref name="The Baptist" /> They had excellent hearing.<ref name="Journey to the Bracca Badlands">{{YJA|Journey to the Bracca Badlands}}</ref>

==Society and culture==
Dianoga had a primitive tribal culture. When they were not feeding, they often [[Language|spoke]] a deep, complex humming [[Dianoga language|language]]. Because its reverberations carried so completely in the water, that language scared away all nearby prey. Their people venerated water, which they called "the Great Cleanser When It Was Time to Be Cleansed," and believed in [[Transfer essence|reincarnation]].<ref name="The Baptist" />

==History==
Dianoga hailed from [[Vodran]], a [[swamp]]y [[planet]] located in the [[Si'Klaata Cluster]].<ref name="Join the Resistance" /> They shared their world with the sentient [[Vodran (species)|Vodrans]], who usually stayed well away from the deeper wetlands.<ref name="The Baptist" />

==Dianoga in the galaxy==
{{Quote|There's something alive in here.|Luke Skywalker referring to the dianoga Omi|<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>}}
[[File:Dianoga-BF.png|thumb|right|200px|A dianoga in the water tanks of the [[SoroSuub Corporation|SoroSuub]] [[SoroSuub Refinery|refinery]] on Sullust.]]
A family of dianoga inhabited a pool in the [[Bracca Badlands|Badlands]] of the junkyard world [[Bracca]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. When, in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="YJADate">{{YJADate|219A}}</ref> a pair of [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s attempted to conscript four [[Pikpik]] [[scrapper]]s, the captives' friend [[Eren Kitt]] and a trio of [[Jedi Initiate]]s accidentally disturbed the family while crossing the pool on a [[skiff]]. In confronting the Nihil, the [[Jedi]] upset a [[Giant dianoga/Canon|giant dianoga]], using its attacks as a distraction to rescue the Pikpik from [[slavery|enslavement]].<ref name="Journey to the Bracca Badlands">{{YJA|Journey to the Bracca Badlands}}</ref>

[[Standard century|Centuries]] later, [[Unidentified Dianoga (Bracca)|another dianoga]] inhabited Bracca during the [[Imperial Era|early days]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The being made a home within the waters that flooded the remains of a derelict [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Clone Force 99)|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], one of many [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|such vessels]] left for scrap on the planet.<ref name="Battle Scars" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of [[The Bad Batch Season 1|''The Bad Batch'' Season 1]] in [[19 BBY]].</ref> members of the rogue [[clone commando]] [[squad]] [[Clone Force 99]] entered the wrecked [[cruiser]], searching for a [[medical bay]] within the [[Starship|ship]]. One of their number, the group's demolitions expert [[Wrecker]], briefly wrestled with the dianoga when he found himself dangling from a cable over the being's watery home. [[Blaster]]fire from the other [[Cloning|clones]], however, drove the dianoga off and ended the encounter.<ref name="Battle Scars" />

During the [[trade wars]], the [[collector]] [[Dok-Ondar]] offered to [[Currency|pay]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]] double if he threw in [[Ki-Adi-Mundi's lightsaber]] with the dianoga pup he was selling to him.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2">[[Galaxy's Edge 2|''Galaxy's Edge'' 2]]</ref> At some point before the [[Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="The Baptist" /> a female dianoga named [[Omi]] was captured by Vodrans, who handed her to members of the Empire en route to their new [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]]. She was dumped into the Death Star's [[garbage masher 3263827]], where she would feed on the organic materials that occasionally fell in.<ref name="The Baptist" /> During the [[Rescue of Princess Leia|rescue]] of [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[Luke Skywalker]] first realized something was alive in the garbage compactor, but shortly after his realization, Omi dragged Skywalker under the mucky water. [[Han Solo]], [[Chewbacca]], and Organa tried to help the struggling Skywalker, only for the dianoga to release Luke just before the walls began to close.<ref name="Episode IV" />

During the [[Skirmish on the Ghost Moon|skirmish]] on the [[Ghost Moon]], [[Sergeant]] [[Kreel]] of the [[Task Force 99]] also known as the [[Special Commando Advanced Recon Trooper|SCAR Troopers]] faced a dianoga in the sewers.<ref name="SW21">[[Star Wars (2015) 21|''Star Wars'' (2015) 21]]</ref>

Experienced [[chef]]s could make [[grilled dianoga]] into a tasty breakfast dish, but overcooking it would activate the [[blood parasite]]s in the fatty tissue, destroying the flavor.<ref name="Recipe">{{StoryCite|story=A Recipe for Death|book=Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I}}</ref>

Like the original station, the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star II]] also utilized captive dianoga in its waste facilities. Following the station's destruction at the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Complete Locations New Edition">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> debris from the structure crashed on the moon [[Kef Bir]]. Dianoga that survived the impact established a colony in the moon's oceans, invasively joining Kef Bir's ecosystem.<ref name="Complete Locations New Edition" />
