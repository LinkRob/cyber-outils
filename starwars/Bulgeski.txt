'''Bulgeski''' was a [[Sallichen]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> [[senator]] who represented [[Salliche]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] during the [[Republic Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Biography==
[[File:Dooku-TotJCompanion.png|thumb|200px|right|Dooku (pictured) disagreed with Bulgeski over the affect that cuts to the trade route defense program would have on the Outer Rim.]]
A [[Sallichen]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> that lived in the twilight [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Republic Era|era]], Bulgeski served as [[senator]] for the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Salliche]] inside the [[Galactic Senate]]. An [[agriworld]] that cultivated the [[Plant|crops]] exported to [[planet]]s like the [[capital]] [[Coruscant]], as their spokesman, Bulgeski claimed that Salliche represented the breadbasket of the Core Worlds and called for the [[Republic Security Force|Republic Guard]] to intervene when his [[homeworld]] fell beneath assault from [[Norkronian]] raiders.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

In [[42 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'' places [[Dooku]] and Bulgeski's exchange shortly before Dooku leaves the [[Jedi Order]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[42 BBY]].</ref> not long after his request for aid, Bulgeski was present at the [[Grand Convocation Chamber|Senate Chamber]] when [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]] addressed him and his fellow senators to voice protest to cuts that the congress was intending to make to their [[trade route defense program]]. Though Dooku sought to intervene on behalf of the populations of worlds in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that would be affected, Bulgeski objected to his arguments and engaged in a verbal sparring match where the senator accused the [[Jedi]] of attempting to influence the Senate. Dooku tried to use the recent request that Bulgeski had made for assistance to support his argument that the Outer Rim should receive the same benefits, but the Sallichen responded that the importance of his homeworld necessitated this intervention, as Salliche represented a vital [[Foodstuff|food]] source for Coruscant and the other Core Worlds. When his statement prompted his opponent to theorise that Bulgeski and his constituents were afraid that the agricultural worlds of the Outer Rim would become their competitors, the senator denied this as a scandalous accusation, while [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Skor Kalpana|Kalpana]] dismissed Dooku's advice.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
