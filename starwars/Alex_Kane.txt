'''Alex Kane''' is a freelance writer who was published on [[StarWars.com]] between [[2017]] and [[2019]].<ref name="Profile" /> He is also the author of ''Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic'', an unlicensed book dedicated to the making of the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''.<ref name="Making of">{{BookCite|author=Kane, Alex|title=Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic|publisher=[[Wikipedia:Boss Fight Books|Boss Fight Books]]|date=2019|isbn=1940535212}}</ref>
