The '''Akiva Summit'''<ref name="Under Siege 1">[[Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege 1|''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 1]]</ref> was an emergency summit held on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Akiva]] between some of the most powerful figures remaining in the [[Galactic Empire]] a few [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]. The meeting was ostensibly arranged by [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] and attended by surviving Imperial elite such as self-proclaimed [[Grand Moff]] [[Valco Pandion]], [[General]] [[Jylia Shale]], [[advisor]] and [[Dark side of the Force|Dark Side]] adherent [[Yupe Tashu]], and financier [[Arsin Crassus]], and it operated under the temporary title of the [[Imperial Future Council]]. The purpose of the gathering was to collectively determine a path of action for the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Aftermath" /> [[Governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] was also asked to attend the Summit, but when he was confronted for his absence, the Grand [[Moff]] told [[Admiral]] Sloane that the future of the Empire was within the [[Anoat sector]] and suggested she instead dispatch reinforcements to support his [[Ubrik Adelhard's Imperial remnant|Imperial faction]] in their own efforts.<ref name="Consolidation">{{StoryCite|story=Consolidation|book=Battle of Jakku &mdash; Republic Under Siege 1}}</ref>

The summit was bogged down by infighting between the delegates, particularly the rivalry between Admiral Sloane and Grand Moff Pandion. A [[Norra Wexley's team|group of insurgents]] under the [[New Republic]] starfighter pilot [[Norra Wexley]] learned about the conference and inspired an [[Rebellion on Akiva|uprising]] in [[Myrra]]. [[New Republic Defense Fleet|New Republic Navy]] forces under [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]] then intervened on Akiva, bringing the emergency summit to a close. Sloane managed to escape but Pandion and Crassus were killed during the escape attempt. General Shale and advisor Tashu were captured by New Republic forces.<ref name="Aftermath" /> In truth, the summit had been staged by [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]] with the objective of eliminating rivals to his succession over the Empire.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>
