'''Free Radical Design''' was a UK-based [[Star Wars video games|video game]] development company founded in [[1999]], known for the [[Wikipedia:TimeSplitters|''TimeSplitters'']] series. Between [[2006]] and [[2008]], the company was developing ''[[Star Wars: Battlefront III]]'', but the game ended up cancelled by [[LucasArts]].

Late-2008, cornered by ''Battlefront III'' cancellation, and [[Wikipedia:Haze (video game)|''Haze'']] poor release, the company was in bankruptcy.<ref name="Eurogamer" /> It was then purchased by [[Wikipedia:Crytek|Crytek]] in February [[2009]], becoming '''Crytek UK'''.<ref name="Crytek" /> In [[2014]], the studio closed as Crytek sold its assets to [[Wikipedia:Koch Media|Koch Media]], and the staff was transferred to [[Wikipedia:Dambuster Studios|Dambuster Studios]].<ref name="Defunct" />
