The '''CloakEye sensor jammer''' was a type of [[sensor jammer]] used on the [[RZ-2 A-wing interceptor]].<ref name="TLJ ICS" /> The advanced sensor jammer was housed in front of the cockpit canopy, and worked in conjunction with an [[Irilliad]] [["Blinder" 4B2 jamming array]] that broadcast data bursts to disrupt enemy sensors and impede detection.<ref name="RSOWM">''[[Rebel Starfighters Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>
