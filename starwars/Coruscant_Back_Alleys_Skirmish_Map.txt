The '''Coruscant Back Alleys Skirmish Map''' is a skirmish map for the tactical combat board game ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Assault]]'' from [[Fantasy Flight Games]]. The map is a 26 inch by 26 inch, slip-resistant mat.<ref name="Back Alley">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/10/12/battle-in-the-back-alley|text=Battle in the Back Alley}}</ref>

{{Game-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''While Coruscant is the center of the Star Wars™ galaxy, it is far from the safest place. In the undercity, far away from the eyes of the Emperor, skirmish battles rage on!''

''The Coruscant Back Alleys Skirmish Map is a 26" x 26" slip-resistant mat for use in your Imperial Assault skirmish games, making setup quick and allowing you to jump right into the action. This skirmish map takes your battles to the neon-soaked back alleys of Coruscant, where dark corners and narrow passageways make for tight-quarters combat.''
