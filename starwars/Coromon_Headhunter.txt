The '''Coromon Headhunter''' was a fierce creature [[Homeworld|native]] to<ref name="BMF59" /> the terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> [[Fresia]]<ref name="BMF59" /> in the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Fresia system]]</ref> The [[Z-95 Headhunter]] produced by [[Incom Corporation]] and [[Sublights Products Corporation]] borrowed its popular name from the Coromon Headhunter.<ref name="BMF59" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Coromon Headhunter was mentioned in the "Incom/SubPro Z-95 Headhunter" article<ref name="BMF59" /> written by [[Mark Newbold]]<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-Credits.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> and published in the "[[Starship Fact File]]" department of the [[De Agostini]] magazine [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 29|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 29]],<ref name="BMF59" /> which was released around [[February 17]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|59}}</ref> The [[Coromon Headhunter/Legends|creature]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was created for the [[2003]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' supplement ''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]'', which was published by [[Wizards of the Coast]].<ref name="CatCW">''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]''</ref>
