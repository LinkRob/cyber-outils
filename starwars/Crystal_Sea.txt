The '''Crystal Sea''' was an inland [[Ocean|sea]] located in the southern hemisphere of the [[planet]] [[Auratera]], in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The [[city]] of [[Dewbright]] was located in a large cove on the Crystal Sea's eastern edge. The city's [[Ithorian]] residents used [[boat]]s and [[skimmer]]s to sail across the Crystal Sea and down its connected rivers in order to reach sites that needed to be restored to their undamaged, pre&ndash;[[Sith Wars]] state.<ref name="Nexus" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Crystal Sea was mentioned in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Nexus of Power]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[roleplaying game]].<ref name="Nexus" />
