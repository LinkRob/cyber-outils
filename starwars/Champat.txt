'''Champat''' a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Timelines" /> located in the [[Champat system]] within the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Interior]] regions.<ref name="Interior" /> Situated at the coordinates M-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> it was an industrial location and the birthplace of [[human]] [[Caluan Ematt]] in [[20 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Champat later became a major resource and production hub for the [[Galactic Empire]]. During the [[Operation Ringbreaker]] campaign, conducted by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[General]] [[Crix Madine]], inspired by the campaign's early success, listed the industrial Core World on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[map]] of Imperial [[shipyard]]s, drydocks, and [[mining]] centers he believed should subsequently be targeted.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

Madine labeled Champat as having a medium production output level and a high security level and included the map in an official document to the Alliance [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|leader]] [[Mon Mothma]]. Operation Ringbreaker was unsuccessful, and Madine's document was eventually included in the non-electric document archive ''[[The Rebel Files]]'' by [[archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
Champat was first mentioned in the [[2017]] reference book and boxed set ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
