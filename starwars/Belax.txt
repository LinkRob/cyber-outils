'''Belax''' was a [[city]] on the [[planet]] [[Hintis IV]]. The population of the city dwindled as the [[Hintis]] star started to expand around 429 BBY until it became completely deserted around 279 BBY when the planet was evacuated at full scale. As the city was abandoned it became a ruin and the buildings were consumed by [[Creeping vine]]s. During the [[High Republic Era]] the Republic established a scientific outpost in the city to monitor and gather data on the dying [[Hintis]] star as an early warning system to ensure that the fallout of the dying star was minimal. During the [[Nihil conflict]] the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] used this outpost as a temporary base.<ref name="EoD" />
