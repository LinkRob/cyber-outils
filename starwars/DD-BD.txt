'''DD-BD''' was a [[WDD adminmech droid]]<ref name="SoloOG" /> that operated within the control hub of [[mining]] operations on [[Kessel]].<ref name="lego">{{LEGOWeb|url=en-us/themes/star-wars/characters/dd-bd-3a3f83a658124a0b82789965c72c90b9|text=DD-BD}}</ref> Before being sold into [[slavery]], DD-BD served on a [[Morseerian]] [[pirate]] [[Starship|ship]]. However, when the ship was impounded by the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]], he was found there and sold at auction to the [[Pyke Syndicate]] for just a fraction of his worth. There, he labored as a slave in the mining operation center on the [[planet]] Kessel.<ref name="DD databank" /> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on [[Kessel]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to nine years after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the formation of the Empire to [[19 BBY]]; therefore, the events on Kessel in ''Solo'' must take place in [[10 BBY]].</ref> DD-BD was freed from slavery when his [[restraining bolt]] was removed during a [[Raid on Kessel|slave revolt]] in the [[Mining|mines]] started by the [[droid]] [[L3-37]]. As the first of many droids freed, DD-BD helped spark a rebellion on Kessel.<ref name="Solo" />

[[Standard decade|Decade]]s later, DD-BD was mentioned in the ''[[Galactic Explorer's Guide]]'', a survival guide written by [[Hondo Ohnaka]] and [[DK-RA-43]].<ref name="Galactic Explorer's Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Like other [[Adminmech droid|adminmech]] [[droid]]s, DD-BD was programmed to be driven by efficiency and quotas and to never question orders. He possessed a very overeager personality.<ref name="SoloOG" /> Being freed by [[L3-37]] awoke a new sense of rebellion and independence within DD-BD,<ref name="DD databank" /> unusual for droids of his class.<ref name="SoloOG" />

==Equipment==
DD-BD was encased in a [[Color|white]] [[plastoid]] shell with heavy weighted feet for enhanced stability. He was equipped with a hybrid radar [[eye]] and [[photoreceptor]] and an [[Electro-shock prod|electroprobe]] with variable voltage settings. During his time working on [[Kessel]] he was fitted with a powerful [[restraining bolt]] that was welded to his body and capable of shutting him down or shocking him for disobedience.<ref name="SoloOG" />

==Behind the scenes==
DD-BD first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' and was one of several characters played by ''[[Star Wars]]'' veteran [[Warwick Davis]].<ref name="Five More Along Weazel">{{WebCite|url=https://screenrant.com/warwick-davis-interview-solo-star-wars-story|text=Warwick Davis Interview: Solo: A Star Wars Story|author=Leadbeater, Alex|date=2018-09-25|archivedate=20181006054346}}</ref>
