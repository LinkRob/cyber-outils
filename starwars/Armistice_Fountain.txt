The '''Armistice Fountain''', also known as the '''Armistice Statue''', was a fountain located in [[Janix city]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories]] [[planet|world]] of [[Janix]] by sometime between [[17 BBY]] and around [[13 BBY]]. It memorialized a conflict from the planet's history that had long past by the time of the early [[Imperial Era]], and it was situated within an enclosed town square. The statue, which stood atop a fountain basin, featured a stylized woman who was holding a shield above her head in her right [[limb|hand]] and a [[spear]] in her left. 

In the early Imperial Era, the renegade [[Sith Lord]]-turned-[[crime lord]] [[Maul]], seeking to flee [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces [[Hunt for Maul|hunting]] him on the Janix, planted a dream of the statue in the [[mind]] of the young [[Great Jedi Purge|fugitive]] [[Twi'lek]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Devon Izara]]. She shared the dream with the group she was on the run with, and though her [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]] cautioned that the [[Dark side of the Force|darksider]] may be setting a trap, the group resolved to meet with Maul at the location local [[Tactical Defense Force]] [[Captain]] [[Brander Lawson]] identified as the Armistice Fountain.

When they arrived, Maul and his remaining [[Shadow Collective]] forces met them, with the [[Zabrak]] offering the group passage off world with them if Lawson could help them reach their rendezvous with a scheduled [[Crimson Dawn]] extraction in the [[jungle]] outside the city. After a short discussion, the group resolved to ally with Maul, and their combined forces left. The [[Marrok|First]] and [[Eleventh Brother]] [[Inquisitor]]s, on the trail of the now allied groups, arrived at the Armistice Statue soon after, with the latter commenting on their quarry's recent presence before continuing their hunt.

==Description==
The Armistice Fountain,<ref name="MSL 109" /> also known as the Armistice Statue,<ref name="MSL Trivia" /> was a fountain located in [[Janix city]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Shadow of Maul 1">[[Shadow of Maul 1|''Shadow of Maul'' 1]]</ref> [[planet|world]] of [[Janix]], and which memorialized a conflict from the planet's history that was long past by the time of the early [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="MSL Trivia" /> The fountain, which was situated within an enclosed town square, featured a stylized woman who was holding a shield above her head in her right [[limb|hand]] and a [[spear]] in her left, standing atop a basin set on a plinth. The interior of the shield was [[gold]]-[[color]]ed, and decaying [[Tree|leaves]] were strewn in and around the fountain.<ref name="MSL 109" /> 

==History==
{{Quote|They were here.|The Eleventh Brother to the First Brother, on their quarry having been at the Armistice Fountain|<ref name="MSL 109" />}}
The Armistice Fountain was built by sometime between [[17 BBY]] and around [[13 BBY]],<ref name="ShadowLordDate">{{ShadowLordDate}}</ref> to memorialize a conflict from the Janix's history that had long past by the early Imperial Era. At one point, local [[Tactical Defense Force]] [[Captain]] [[Brander Lawson]] became familiar with the fountain's appearance and location. When the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces [[Operations on Janix|hunting]] for them both closed in, the renegade former [[Sith]] [[crime lord]] [[Maul]] implanted a dream of the fountain in the [[mind]] of the [[Twi'lek]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Devon Izara]], prompting her to wake with a start. When queried by her [[Jedi Master]], [[Eeko-Dio Daki]], as well as their fellow fugitive allies, including Lawson, his son [[Rylee Lawson|Rylee]], and his [[Tactical Defense Droid|droid]] partner [[2B0T]] "Two-Boots," Izara shared the details of the dream, prompting the [[police officer]] to identify the location she described as the Armistice Fountain not far from their current location. Lawson and Daki shared their concerns that the [[Dark side of the Force|darksider]] who they had previously fought<ref name="MSL 104">{{MSL|Chapter 4: Pride and Vengeance}}</ref> was laying a trap, but Izara countered that she felt rendezvousing at the fountain could be their way off the planet, and the group resolved to go just as the [[Marrok|First]] and [[Eleventh Brother]] [[Inquisitor]]s arrived in search of them.<ref name="MSL 109" /> 

When the group arrived at the fountain, Maul was indeed there, and Daki told the [[Zabrak]] his message had been received as he faced the statue. Turning around to face the newly arrived group as the surviving members of his [[Shadow Collective]] emerged from the shadows, Maul shared his alleged intentions to get them all off the planet. He claimed to only want to help them evade the Empire's grasp, and his [[spy droid]] [[Spybot]] projected a [[hologram]] of the city and the planned rendezvous with [[Crimson Dawn]] for extraction outside of it. Maul proposed an alliance with the [[Light side of the Force|light side]] grouping, as Lawson knew a way out of the city, and after quickly discussing the matter a short way away from the statue, they agreed. Whilst the unlikely alliance fought Imperial forces at a power station to access a fissure leading out of the city, the Inquisitors on their trail arrived at the Armistice Fountain. Once there the First Brother used [[psychometry]] as the Eleventh [[Brother]] commented that their quarry had been there, then both turned their heads and looked to the smoke rising from Maul's destruction of an [[All Terrain Armored Cannon]] [[walker]]. The First Brother stated that their prey was making their escape, before the Inquisitors left the fountain and continued their pursuit.<ref name="MSL 109" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|In the mix on the back end, David W. Collins is always adding weird stuff. When you hear a little turn of Crow's head, Collin's [sic] put in this creaking, weird sound. It's great. We call it 'bone leather.'|''Maul - Shadow Lord'' Supervising Director Brad Rau on David W. The Armistice Fountain first appeared in the ninth episode of ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'' [[Maul - Shadow Lord Season 1|Season 1]], entitled "[[Chapter 9: Strange Allies]]",<ref name="MSL 109" />  which first aired on [[Disney+]] on [[May 4]], [[2026]].<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|starwars|status/2033951658485682588|''[[Star Wars]]''|date=2026-03-17|archivefile=File:MaulShadowLord-EpisodeTitles-Twitter.jpg}}</ref> It was alternately identified as the "Armistice Statue" in an update to the ''Maul - Shadow Lord'' trivia article on [[StarWars.com]] the same day, with further details about how the fountain memorialized a conflict from long ago in Janix's past also being shared. The eerie creaking sounds made by the Eleventh Brother's neck in the show, including when he looks up at the fountain, were created by Supervising Sound Editor, Sound Designer, and Re-recording Mixer [[David W. Collins]].<ref name="MSL Trivia" />
