The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''132 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3145 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''113 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7845''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> It was during the final days of the [[High Republic Era]], the peace of which was fading by that time.<ref name="Launchpad 231">{{InsiderCite|231|Launchpad}}</ref>  

==Events==
*The [[assassin]] [[Mae-ho Aniseya]] carries out a [[Mae's quest|quest]] to kill [[Jedi]] connected to her past.<ref name="Revenge / Justice">{{Acolyte|Revenge / Justice}}</ref>
**[[Jedi Master]] [[Indara]] is [[Death|killed]] by Aniseya.<ref name="Lost / Found" />
**Mae's sister [[Verosha Aniseya]] is [[Mission to arrest Osha Aniseya|wrongly arrested]] by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Yord Fandar]] for [[Indara's murder]], she is soon exonerated.<ref name="Lost / Found" /> She reunites with her former master, [[Jedi Master]] [[Sol]], in the name of stopping her sister.<ref name="Revenge / Justice" />
**Jedi Master [[Torbin]] takes his own life upon being confronted by Mae.<ref name="Revenge / Justice" />
**After discovering that Mae answers to an [[The Stranger|unseen master]], Sol and Mae engage in a duel, and Mae learns her sister is alive. Osha allows her sister to escape upon coming face-to-face with her for the first time in [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="Revenge / Justice" />
