"'''Freedom Fighters From Lothal and Other Characters'''" is a volume in the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series of reference books published by [[De Agostini]]. It was published on [[July 6]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya" />

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''The Lothal rebels, a group of individuals from very different backgrounds, were united by a common ideal: the fervent defense of freedom. They eventually formed Phoenix Squadron, one of the largest cells in the Rebel Alliance. With the starship Ghost as a mobile base of operations and two Force users among them, they played a vital role in the early stages of the Galactic Civil War. They enlisted the help of many contacts, and their fight against injustice would inspire many across the galaxy.''<ref name="Altaya" />
