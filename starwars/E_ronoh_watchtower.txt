By the [[time]] of the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]], a '''watchtower''' was established on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]]. Families of individuals serving in [[E'ronoh's fleet]] waited in the structure for their relatives to return home in their [[Starship|ships]].<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> while flying on a [[Skirmish in the Eiram system|mission]], the [[Thylefire Squadron]] [[pilot]] [[Bly Tevin]] thought about his [[Bly Tevin's mother|mother]] climbing onto the [[Sentry tower|watchtower]] to wait for his return home. Moments after, however, his [[Saboteur|sabotaged]] [[Bly Tevin's devilfighter|devilfighter]] exploded and [[Death|killed]] him.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
The E'ronoh watchtower was mentioned in [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], a novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's Phase II<ref name="Convergence" /> that was published on [[November 22]], [[2022]].<ref name="ReleaseDates">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-convergence-excerpt|text=A Mysterious Mission Begins in ''The High Republic: Convergence'' &ndash; Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref> Prior to its release, the watchtower's mention was included in a preview for the novel released on the official [[Wikipedia:Penguin Random House|Penguin Random House]] [https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com website]<ref name="Excerpt">{{WebCite|url=https://sites.prh.com/convergence|text=STAR WARS: CONVERGENCE EXTENDED EXCERPT|work=[[Wikipedia:Penguin Random House|Penguin Random House]]|archivedate=20221004162329}}</ref> on [[October 4]] of that year.<ref name="ReleaseDates" />
