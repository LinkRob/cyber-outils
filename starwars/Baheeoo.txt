'''Baheeoo''' was a [[Gigoran]] [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] who was part of the crew of the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]]'s [[flagship]]. In [[232 BBY]], they and the Nihil [[Garank]] and [[Ordo (Talpini)|Ordo]] raced across the ''Gaze Electric'' to relay Ro's command to change course to the [[planet]] [[Trymant IV]]. Wanting to reach the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] first, Garank threw Ordo onto Baheeoo and fled as the pair tumbled to the ground. To make up time, Baheeoo and Ordo mounted a [[Bogaranth]] creature and rode it to their destination. Upon their arrival at the bridge, Garank was [[Death|killed]] when they were [[Diet|eaten]] by the Bogaranth, which caused the other two Nihil to lament over their loss.

Afterward, Baheeoo traveled to Trymant IV with other Nihil aboard the [[Spider Cruiser]] ''[[Squall Spider]]'' to rescue [[Elder (Elders of the Path)|Elder]] [[Tromak]]. While there, the Gigoran followed Ro's directive and helped [[Attack on Trymant IV|attack]] the [[Jedi]] on the world, who were trying to rescue the planet's civilians.

==Biography==
===Race to the bridge===
{{Dialogue|Ordo|Sheesh! What a grump!|Garank|Snrff! Karnff! Horf horf.|Baheeoo|Yeah, but fellas&hellip;at this rate we're still never gonna make it!|attr=Ordo, Garank, and Baheeoo as they raced to the ''Gaze Electric''{{'s}} bridge|src=<ref name="SWA6" />}}
[[File:BaheeooRiding-SWA2020-6.png|thumb|right|300px|Baheeoo and Ordo rode a Bogaranth to reach the ''Gaze Electric''{{'s}} bridge faster.]]
The [[Gigoran]] [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] Baheeoo was part of the crew of the ''[[Gaze Electric]]''<ref name="SWA6" />&mdash;the [[flagship]]<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> of [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]]&mdash;during the [[High Republic Era]], as one of a small group of Nihil permitted aboard.<ref name="SWA6" /> In [[232 BBY]], Ro orchestrated a collision between the [[Byne Guild]] [[freighter]] ''[[Legacy Run]]'' and an [[Stormship|obstacle]] that resulted in the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], which spurred [[Emergences]]&mdash;pieces of [[hyperspace]] debris&mdash;emerging from [[Realspace|space]] and impacting celestial bodies throughout [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> After being informed one such [[Trymant IV disaster|Emergence]] would threaten several locations in the [[Trymant system]], Ro decided to travel to the [[planet]] [[Trymant IV]].<ref name="SWA6" /> The [[Storm (rank)|Storm]]<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> [[Zagyar]] tried to relay the command to the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], but after finding that the [[Comm unit|comms]] on the ''Gaze Electric'' were inoperable, instead sent the Nihil Baheeoo, [[Garank]], and [[Ordo (Talpini)|Ordo]] to immediately alert the [[Starship|ship's]] [[Unidentified Gaze Electric captain|captain]] to the change in plans.<ref name="SWA6" />

Motivated by Zagyar's threat to throw the last Nihil who arrived out an [[airlock]], the three Nihil rushed from the back of the ''Gaze Electric'' toward the bridge, unsure if they would arrive in the short amount of time Ro expected. Garank pointed to the side and huffed in [[Gamorrean (language)|Gamorrean]], confusing Baheeoo as the Gigoran did not speak the [[language]]. When Baheeoo looked to see what Garank was gesturing to, the Gamorrean picked up Ordo and threw the [[Talpini]]'s body against the Gigoran, knocking both over. Garank then fled and left the pair behind, intent on reaching the ''Gaze Electric''{{'s}} bridge first. Baheeoo and Ordo decided to take a risky shortcut through the [[Bogaranth pen|pen]] holding the ship's [[Bogaranth]]s&mdash;bloodthirsty creatures that [[Diet|ate]] anything in their path. While Garank continued running toward the bridge, the other Nihil pair mounted a Bogaranth and rode it to the same destination, causing a crowd of Nihil to dive out of their way in fear as they passed.<ref name="SWA6" />

===Bridge and firefights===
{{Quote|There's the bridge!''"<br />"''And there's Garank!''"<br />"''There ''was'' Garank, anyway&hellip;''"<br />"''Oof! That hadta hurt!|Ordo and Baheeoo as they approached the bridge and Garank was eaten by a Bogaranth|<ref name="SWA6" />}}
Upon arrival, Baheeoo called out after spotting Garank, though their Bogaranth then ate the Gamorrean. The Talpini restated Baheeoo's comment to refer to the now-[[Death|deceased]] Garank in the past tense, while Baheeoo lamented that being eaten must have hurt. The pair rushed in to inform the [[captain]] to set a new course. The [[Pantoran]] was surprised at their arrival and ordered the crew to enter Trymant IV's coordinates, and Baheeoo and Ordo were informed shortly afterward by [[Unidentified Gaze Electric Nihil|another Nihil]] that the Nihil [[Grik]] had just fixed the comms. As the ''Gaze Electric'' jumped to hyperspace, the Nihil on the bridge listened to an incoming [[Hologram|holographic]] message from Ro, who promised the Nihil would gain incredible powers over the [[Galactic Republic]] and their [[destiny]] awaited at their next destination.<ref name="SWA6" />

[[File:Baheeoo-2021THRA2.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Under Ro's orders, Baheeoo attacked several Jedi on Trymant IV.]]
Later, Baheeoo joined the Nihil traveling on the [[Spider Cruiser]] ''[[Squall Spider]]'' to Trymant IV to evacuate [[Elder (Elders of the Path)|Elder]] [[Tromak]],<ref name="THRA 2">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 2|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 2]]</ref> due to his knowledge of the location of a [[Nameless control rods|relic]] that Ro sought.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> Under Ro's directive, the Nihil [[Attack on Trymant IV|attacked]] the [[Jedi Master]]s [[Torban Buck]] and [[Yoda]], as well as the [[Padawan]]s with them, who were on the world trying to evacuate civilians and save them from the impending disaster. At one point, Baheeoo launched themselves over a ledge and used their [[weapon]] to fire off an explosive, heated projectile. However, before it could land, Yoda jumped above his fellows, cutting the projectile in two. Eventually, Ro and what remained of his Nihil fled Trymant IV aboard the ''Squall Spider'', escaping the [[Jedi Vector]] [[blockade]] in the planet's [[Atmosphere|skies]].<ref name="THRA 2" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Wee! Wee! Snorf!''"<br />"''What is it, Garank? I don't speak Gamorrean.|Garank and Baheeoo, with the former tricking the latter to gain a headstart to the bridge|<ref name="SWA6" />}}
[[File:BaheeooBonk-SWA2020-6.png|thumb|right|300px|Garank threw Ordo into Baheeoo, knocking both over.]]
When informed only the first Nihil to reach the ''Gaze Electric''{{'s}} bridge would not be thrown out an airlock, Baheeoo was extremely motivated to make it there in time. Though Garank left the Gigoran and Ordo after pushing them to the ground, the latter pair stayed together as they tried to reach their destination. Though Baheeoo was nervous about riding a Bogaranth due to how dangerous they were, they nevertheless mounted one with Ordo. After seeing Garank get eaten by the Bogaranth they were riding, Baheeoo expressed their sympathies, even though the Gamorrean had been willing to leave them behind in order to beat them to the bridge.<ref name="SWA6" />

When Ro ordered his Nihil to kill all the Jedi present on Trymant IV, Baheeoo loyally followed his commands.<ref name="THRA 2" /> The Gigoran had [[Color|white]] [[hair]] and blue [[eye]]s.<ref name="SWA6" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|Wahooooo!!!''"<br />"''Ayeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee!!!|Baheeoo and Ordo riding a Bogaranth|<ref name="SWA6" />}}
Baheeoo was physically fit, able to run for long distances. The Gigoran could also ride a Bogaranth, despite the creatures being dangerous and hard to handle.<ref name="SWA6" /> Baheeoo could wield projectile weaponry and attacked Jedi with it on Trymant IV.<ref name="THRA 2" /> While they spoke and understood [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]], they did not understand the Gamorrean language.<ref name="SWA6" />

==Equipment==
While on the ''Gaze Electric'', Baheeoo wore a light blue [[Breath mask|breathing mask]] attached to a grey [[backpack]] via tubes.<ref name="SWA6" /> Later, on Trymant IV, the Gigoran wore a brown harness and wielded a long, [[staff]]-like weapon bearing an orange projectile on the end.<ref name="THRA 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:TheHighRepublicAdventures-2.jpg|thumb|right|210px|Baheeoo debuted in the second issue of the 2021 ''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'' comic series.]]
Baheeoo first appeared, albeit unidentified, in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 2|second issue]] of the [[2021]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2021)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']], released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="THRA 2" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Harvey Tolibao]] and [[Pow Rodrix]], and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="THRA 2" /> on [[March 3]], 2021.<ref name="Comixology">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-2/digital-comic/912530|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures #2}}</ref> The Gigoran was then identified by name in "[[Tales of Villainy: The Gaze Electric|Tales of Villainy: The ''Gaze Electric'']]," a secondary comic story published in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2020) 6|sixth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars Adventures (2020)|''Star Wars Adventures'']] anthology comic-book series. The story was written by Older, illustrated by [[Nick Brokenshire]], and published by IDW Publishing<ref name="SWA6" /> on [[July 7]] of the same year.<ref name="Previews World">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2020-6/digital-comic/905537|text=Star Wars Adventures (2020-) #6|nolive=1}}</ref>

When designing the color scheme for the comic story, Brokenshire aimed to reference the style of ''[[Star Wars]]'' comics from the [[1970]]s and [[1980]]s, when there was limited color technology and panels still needed to visually pop. As such, the ''Gaze Electric'' and its crew&mdash;including Baheeoo&mdash;were colored in this style, though the artist noted that the colors may not necessarily be meant to reflect reality.<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume I]]''</ref>
