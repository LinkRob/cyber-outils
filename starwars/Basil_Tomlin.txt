'''Frances Alfred Basil Tomlin''' was an actor who played two uncredited roles in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], portraying the character [[BoShek]] as well as an unnamed [[stormtrooper]] (alongside his brother, George), according to investigative journalist Billy Jensen.<ref name="SWViral">{{WebCite|url=http://starwarsviral.com/news/finding-boshek-or-the-man-who-could-have-been-han-solo-new-details-revealed|text=Finding BoShek, or the Man Who Could Have Been Han Solo – New Details Revealed!|author=Billy Jensen|date=2016-02-23|work=StarWarsViral.com|archivedate=20160227151426|nolive=1}}</ref> Jensen published his story on Tomlin in [[February]] [[2016]], over 11 years after Tomlin's death.<ref name="Finding BoShek" />
