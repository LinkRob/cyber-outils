'''Choi'olan''' was a member of the wealthy [[Olan Clan]] on [[Ryloth/Legends|Ryloth]]. Choi'olan offered to pay 25,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]] for a sheet of [[Transparisteel/Legends|transparisteel]] measuring four [[Meter/Legends|meters]] high, six meters long, and one meter thick.
