{{Quote|You're a sensible man, Lenz. Are you sure you're from Chandrila?|[[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]], to [[Winmey Lenz]]|<ref name="Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>}}
The '''Chandrilans''' were a cultural group of [[human]]s who called the [[planet]] of [[Chandrila]] [[Homeworld|home]]. They were regarded as peaceful but slightly haughty, pedantic, and interested in [[politics]].<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>

Despite spending the majority of her life on [[Coruscant]], [[Leida Mothma]], who was born on Chandrila, enjoyed the simplicity of Chandrilan customs, unlike her [[Reproduction|mother]], Mon Mothma, who regarded many of the Chandrilan traditions to be outdated in modern times.<ref name="Leida Databank">{{Databank|leida-mothma|Leida Mothma}}</ref>

Following the [[Imperial Era]], some Chandrilans disliked former [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]].<ref name="Life Debt" /> Chandrilans included [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]]<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> and [[Ben Solo]], the son of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebellion]] war heroes [[Han Solo]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

==Society and culture==
Chandrilan [[Gender|women]] like [[Mon Mothma]] were known to favor simple [[hair]]styles.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> Chandrilans had many customs such as the [[Day of Days]].<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> In Chandrila, the age of majority for marriage is 14 years old,<ref name="Daughter of Ferrix">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> and getting married at this approximate age is a tradition in Chandrilan culture, which Mon Mothma and [[Perrin Fertha]] did.<ref name="Narkina 5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref>
