'''Fried Tip Yip Chicken Chunks''' was an [[Foodstuff|entrée]] served to [[youngling]]s during dinner in the [[Crown of Corellia Dining Room]] on the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''<ref name="Cruiser" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The [[tip-yip]] meal was on the Younglings' Menu and was safe for [[human]]s to consume.<ref name="Cruiser" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fried Tip Yip Chicken Chunks were available in the [[In-universe|real world]] at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]], Florida,<ref name="Cruiser" /> which opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref> It was last served at the hotel during the [[September 28]][[September 29|&ndash;]][[September 30|30]] voyage of [[2023]], the final voyage before the closure of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser.<ref name="Official website">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/overview|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Overview}}</ref> It was offered at both dinners on the kids' menu. It has been compared to the real-world food [[Wikipedia:Chicken nugget|chicken nuggets]].<ref name="DFB dinner">{{WebCite|url=https://www.disneyfoodblog.com/2022/02/25/photos-and-review-we-ate-everything-on-the-dinner-night-1-menu-at-the-star-wars-hotel-in-disney-world|text=PHOTOS and REVIEW: We Ate EVERYTHING on the Dinner Night 1 Menu at the Star Wars Hotel in Disney World|author=Kelly Oliveros|date=2022-02-25|work=[https://www.disneyfoodblog.com Disney Food Blog]|archivedate=20240328133507}}</ref>
