The '''assault on Naboo''',<ref name="Legacy of Vader 4" /> also known as the '''assault of Naboo''' was an attack carried out in [[34 ABY]] by the [[First Order]], led by [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], on the planet [[Naboo]] during Ren's quest to find more about [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]'s past.<ref name="Solicit">{{WebCite|url=https://aiptcomics.com/2025/03/20/full-marvel-comics-june-2025-solicitations-godzilla-and-imperial-lead-the-charge|text=Take Pride in Marvel Comics Full Solicits For June 2025|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/MMZYq}}</ref>

==Prelude==
===Vader's Legacy===
{{Quote|I am alone. There is power in that. Purity.''"<br />"''I am so pleased. our journeys...  will continue? There is much I would still show you about your grandfather's life and legacy. Tatooine was only the beginning.|Kylo Ren and Vaneé|<ref name="Legacy of Vader 3" />}}
[[File:RenSeesHologramOfVader-LegacyOfVader1.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Meeting Vader's former servant, Vaneé, Ren wanted to learn how his grandfather turned his past into power.]]
In the aftermath of the battles of [[Battle of Oetchi|Oetchi]] and [[Battle of Crait|Crait]], [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] became the new [[Supreme Leader]] of the [[First Order]], but the junta also suffered many losses after their recent engagements with the [[Resistance]], which costed them the [[Supremacy]] and a large portion of their fleet despite still having many [[legion]]s<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> and [[battalion]]s across the galaxy.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 1">[[Legacy of Vader 1|''Legacy of Vader'' 1]]</ref>

Kylo Ren's personal defeat against Skywalker, who managed to distract him for long enough so that the Resistance could escape his gasp, led the Supreme Leader to a private mission to destroy everything from his past. Traveling the the world of [[Mustafar]] to visit his [[Anakin Skywalker|grandfather]]'s [[Fortress Vader|fortress]], Ren slaughtered a [[Sith cult]] known as the [[Alazmec of Winsit]] and met [[Vaneé]], his grandfather's old servant, who convinced the young tyrant to follow Vader's example and transform the pain of his past into a source of power, like his grandfather before him.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 1" />

===The remains of Naboo===
{{Quote|These fools are obsessed with their past. Maintining it. Lettting it control them. "Lineage matters&hellip;" Pathetic. But I can help them.|Kylo Ren, to Vaneé|<ref name="Legacy of Vader 4" />}}
[[File:KyloAndVaneeInNaboo-LegacyOfVader4.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Kylo Ren and Vaneé visited Naboo to learn more about Vader's past life]]
Following a harrowing [[Mission to Tatooine (Kylo Ren)|mission]] on [[Tatooine]], Kylo Ren met Vaneé on [[Naboo]], at the ruined [[Varykino Villa]].<ref name="Legacy of Vader 3" /> Wanting the Supreme Leader to comprehend the planet's importance, Vaneé told Ren about Naboo's former glory as a powerful cultural hub with a democratic monarchy and how it didn't suffer as much compared to other worlds when the monarchy was supplanted by the Empire, in part because, before his take over of the galaxy, [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] used to represent Naboo as a member of the Galactic Senate. The servant explained to Ren how Naboo fell after the [[Galactic Civil War]] as the galaxy felt the planet's citizens had profited from their association with Palpatine.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 4" />

While Ren didn't care about the Emperor's past with Naboo, Vaneé was quick to point out that it was also the planet where Vader fell in love with [[Padmé Amidala]], his grandmother and [[Queen of Naboo]]. Though Ren dismissed the idea of falling in love, the servant further explained that it was Vader's attachment to Padmé that granted him his dark power and allowed him to push his limits. When the young tyrant claimed to have no limits, Vaneé question if he really had everything he needed and desired, causing Ren to reflect on Rey. Their conversation was interrupted by a man and woman in a boat that told the two that the villa was cursed, explaining that fifty years ago a queen lived at the villa and returned dead under mysterious circumstances, with many believing that a "spirit of death" then came to the villa and destroyed all memory of the queen. While Ren dismissed their superstition, the two pointed out that Naboo's elite believed in the story and left the place, leading to worse people forcefully taking over.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 4" />
[[File:KyloVsVerunaRaiders-LegacyOfVader4.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Kylo Ren massacred Storg Veruna and his raiders]]
Before the two people could finish, [[Storg Veruna]] and his enforcers arrived, Veruna told Ren and the others that they were trespassing his property, but Ren quickly dispatched them with hand-to-hand combat. As one of the enforcers managed to run away, the two visitors reacted in dismay, believing that the other Verunas would return to retaliate. Instead of letting them leave, Ren used the Force kill them after telling Vaneé that he was wrong, that Vader recognized that Naboo was an unworthy memory and it needed to be destroyed. Believing the people of Naboo's obsession with the past to be pathetic, Ren cryptically told his servant that he could help them.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 4" />

==The Assault==
===Naboo's new King===
{{Quote|Are they fighting back?"<br />"The&hellip; Naboo? No. Not in any significant way. The population seems well and truly cowed-- as they should be, when faced with the might of the First Order.|Kylo Ren and Armitage Hux|<ref name="Legacy of Vader 5" />}}
[[File:KyloNabooKing-LegacyOfVader4.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Determined to eliminate Naboo's supposed weakness, Ren proclaimed himself the planet's new king.]]
Two days after his initial visit to [[Naboo]] [[Supreme Leader]] [[Kylo Ren]] met with [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] aboard his flagship the ''[[Finalizer]]''. The Supreme Leader asked Hux if, taking into account that both [[Starkiller Base]] and the ''[[Supremacy]]'' were recently lost, the [[First Order]] could still destroy a planet. Hux said that it depended on what he meant by destroying, as fully destroying a planet would be difficult, but the First Order could still scour an inhabited planet clear of all structures and life with enough time, since their present military forces were more than enough to get said job done.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 5" />

Ren was pleased to hear this and hinted at the possibly that he had a planet in mind to destroy. When questioned about his intentions by Hux, the Supreme Leader dismissed it by asking the General about the status of his throne and leaving the bridge before a frustrated Hux could finish answering the question. Having commandeered Naboo's communications network, Ren opened communications with the planet to announce that their obsessions with lineage and superstition had caused their society to fester, announcing himself as [[Monarch of Naboo|King of Naboo]] to help them embrace a future where they let go of the past.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 5" />
[[File:Spinnaker-LegacyOfVader5.png|thumb|left|250px|The city of Spinnaker was invaded by the First Order]]
After deploying his forces to locations such as the city of [[Spinnaker]], indicating to the oldest people living there that an invasion was coming, Ren oversaw the assault with Hux, who asked Ren about his reasons for invading Naboo and declaring himself king of the planet. Ren simply stated that, as the Supreme Leader, he was already the king of Naboo. The General explained that there were other targets that would make better use of the First Order's military resources, such as [[Corellia]], which could serve as a much needed shipyard and for which Hux already had a battle plan prepared. The Supreme Leader completely ignored Hux's requests and instead asked him if the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] were fighting back against the invasion, with the general confirming that there was no retaliation in any significant way, as the population remained cowed, something that drew Ren's interest.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 5" />
