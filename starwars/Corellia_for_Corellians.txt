"'''Corellia for Corellians'''" was a satirical [[Music|song]] by an [[Unidentified Alderaanian songwriter|Alderaanian writer]] released by around [[270 BBY]], written as a joke about the isolationistic tendencies of the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. Forty [[Standard year|years]] after its release, the [[Corellian]] [[Musician|singer]] [[Crukibolt Nart Adeen|Crufeela]] attempted to use the song in a comeback performance, only for it to be discovered that she was lying about writing it and had lip-synched to a recording of a [[Unidentified Naboo teenager|Naboo teenager]].

==Description==
"Corellia for Corellians" was written in a jingoistic style as it was intended as a joke at the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]'s isolationistic tendencies. However, the [[Music|song's]] satirical nature was easy to miss, leading some to mistake it for a straightforward patriotic song. A performance of the song by a [[Unidentified Naboo teenager|Naboo teenager]] was very high-pitched.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

==History==
"Corellia for Corellians" was written by an [[Unidentified Alderaanian songwriter|Alderaanian]] as a joke and released<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> by around [[270 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> Forty [[Standard year|years]] afterwards, the [[Corellian]] [[Musician|singer]] [[Crukibolt Nart Adeen|Crufeela]], a [[Shani]] already in trouble due to her habit of [[Diet|eating]] endangered [[Species|creatures]], used the song in a [[Unidentified galactic talent show|galactic talent show]] as part of an attempted comeback after an endangered [[papdoo]] scandal. Not understanding that the song was satirical, Crufeela claimed to have written "Corellia for Corellians" herself in memory of the victims of a recent natural disaster on Corellia. However, the comeback attempt failed, as not only was the true authorship discovered, Crufeela was also revealed to not have performed the song, having lip-synched to a voice recording scammed out of a [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] teenager who had been trying to make it big on the [[HoloNet|holos]], which had subsequently been delivered to Crufeela for her use in the competition.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

Although he was not interested in pop culture, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Reath Silas]] witnessed Crufeela's lip-synched performance of "Corellia for Corellians" on the talent show, having also overheard the news about the singer from other Padawans who kept abreast of such things. He did not think the song was very good.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> Around [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[bodyguard]] [[Alys Ongwa]], head of [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection]], heard that Crufeela had been hired as the new performer for the [[Finance Ball]], and quipped to her rival [[Dizcaro]], whom Crufeela had hired for protection, that the singer would have to learn how to lip-synch on the fly if someone tried to make a song request in the middle of her performance. Silas, who was working with Ongwa's agency to uncover [[Nihil]] influence on Corellia, thought about the incident involving "Corellia for Corellians" while helping to execute a sting operation on Crufeela intended to get her arrested so they could replace her with a new performer, [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Mz. Z]]. When Crufeela was arrested for trying to eat the [[Bonbrak]] [[Teetak]], [[Chief Inspector]] [[Deemus Abrus]] described it as her third fall from grace, after "Corellia for Corellians" and the papdoo scandal.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
