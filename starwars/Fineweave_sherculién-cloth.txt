'''Fineweave sherculién-cloth''' was a type of fabric used in [[clothing]], such as the shirt worn by [[Lobot]].<ref name="SWCE">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> The material was also used to make the [[robe]] worn by [[holovid]] [[artist]] [[Blysto Noxton]].<ref name="Solo: A Star Wars Story">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
