The '''Army infantry corps''' was the [[infantry]] [[corps]] of the [[Separatist Droid Army]].<ref name="Force Collection B1 battle droid" /> [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] infantry forces were mainly made up of their [[battle droid]]s,<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> particularly the [[B1-series battle droid]]s. The B1 was the bulk of the infantry corps<ref name="Force Collection B1 battle droid" /> as the Separatists' main [[soldier]].<ref name="Battle Droids - Separatist Droid Army">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Battle Droids - Separatist Droid Army}}</ref> Other combat [[droid]]s, such as the [[B2-series super battle droid]] and [[BX-series droid commando]], were deployed.<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref>
