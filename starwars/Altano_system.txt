The '''Altano system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Piryn Shar sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]. It was located in grid square K-16 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and shared its name with the [[Altano Trade Route]].<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Altano system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].
