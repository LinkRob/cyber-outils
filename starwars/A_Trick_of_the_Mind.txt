"'''A Trick of the Mind'''" is a story in the [[canon]] one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Revelations (2023) 1|''Revelations'' (2023) 1]]. The story was written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Andrea Di Vito]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 20]] [[2023]].

==Plot summary==
{{Quote|The '''Jedi Mind Trick?''' How did you think that would ever work?|Dengar to Advocate Salli Georgio|<ref name="Trick">{{StoryCite|story=A Trick of the Mind|book=Revelations (2023) 1}}</ref>}}
On the planet [[Samaria]], in the [[Imperial Court of Criminal Justice]], [[Advocate]] [[Salli Georgio]] questions whether the [[Magistrate]] is familiar with the [[Jedi Mind Trick]]. Georgio explains the trick to allow the [[Jedi]] to command a sentient being to do what the Jedi wishes. The [[Unidentified judge (Dengar)|Magistrate]] questions the relevance of the old religion, which Georgio swiftly explains. Defending the [[Bounty hunter|bounty hunter]], [[Dengar]], Georgio explains that he experienced the effects of a Jedi Mind Trick while in the [[Black-Market District]] on [[D'Assem]]. While allegedly under the influence of the Mind Trick, Dengar was forced to take control of a [[Speeder|speeder]] and cause significant vehicular damage to distract the law enforcements of D'Assem for the Jedi's benefit. The Magistrate is not fully convinced of the alleged claims of Georgio explaining that the official legal position of the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] states that the Jedi no longer exist, and that such an event could not possibly have occurred. In response to this, the Magistrate sentences Dengar to a fine of fifty thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and sixty days of hard labor in the [[Orgonium mines]]. Before being transferred to the mines, Dengar discusses the ridiculous choice of using the Jedi Mind Trick to get him out of hard labor. Georgio defends her decision as the best choice from a situation where Dengar was incredibly guilty. Angry with this, Dengar attempts to attack Georgio, but is stopped by the [[Bailiff|bailiff]] and removed from the room. Georgio begins to receive a communication from [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] asking for her out-of-the-box thinking that she specialises in.
