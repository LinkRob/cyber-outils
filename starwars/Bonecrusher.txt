The '''''Bonecrusher''''' was a sleek [[cruiser]] owned by the [[Barabel]] [[Galamal]].<ref name="POV" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The [[Mission to Planet X (Path of the Open Hand)|mission to Planet X]] takes place between the [[Battle of Jedha]] and the [[Night of Sorrow]], both of which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> it was part of a mission conducted by the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[religion|cult]] to retrieve [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] eggs from [[Sophros|Planet X]], during which it was [[captain]]ed by Galamal and carried Path members [[Geth (Path of the Closed Fist)|Geth]], [[Shalish]], [[Wole]], and [[Tareen]]. The ''Bonecrusher'' and ''[[Scupper]]'' made it to the surface of Planet X, surviving the journey through the [[planet]]'s treacherous [[The Veil (Sophros)|Veil]], which had vaporized the third ship ''[[Moon of Sarkhai]]''. After Galamal's [[death]], the ship was piloted by Tareen on the return journey. To escape the expanding Veil, the ''Bonecrusher'' and ''Scupper'' attempted to jump to [[hyperspace]] from within the Veil. The ''Scupper'' was successful, but the ''Bonecrusher'' disappeared in an explosion and flash of light. The ''Scupper''{{'s}} sensors were unable to detect whether the ''Bonecrusher'' had disintegrated or jumped to hyperspace, but several members of its crew later encountered the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] vessel ''[[Witherbloom]]''.<ref name="The High Republic Adventures – The Nameless Terror 1">[[The High Republic Adventures – The Nameless Terror 1|''The High Republic Adventures – The Nameless Terror'' 1]]</ref>
