The '''Battle of Arantara''' was an engagement fought in [[22 BBY]] on the astronomical object [[Arantara]] during the ongoing [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Galactic Republic]]. [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] participated in the battle, with the latter becoming injured in the fighting before being rescued by Skywalker.

==The battle==
{{Quote|You&hellip;came for me.''"<br />"''It should have been ''me'' who went in the first place.|Captain Rex and General Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="501 Plus One" />}}
[[File:RexInjured-501PlusOne.png|thumb|left|250px|Although he was injured, Rex fought with excellence in the Battle of Arantara.]]
The battle for<ref name="501 Plus One" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Arantara system]]</ref> astronomical object [[Arantara]] was fought<ref name="501 Plus One" /> in [[22 BBY]] during the opening assaults of the [[The galaxy|galaxy]]-wide [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Dispatched to Arantara to respond to a troubling Separatist presence,<ref name="EncyclopediaComp" /> members of<ref name="501 Plus One" /> the elite<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> [[501st Legion]]&mdash;including [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]]<ref name="501 Plus One" /> and his newly assigned<ref name="Rex Roll">{{InsiderCite|227|Rex & Roll}}</ref> [[Jedi General]], [[Anakin Skywalker]]&mdash;took part in the battle.<ref name="501 Plus One" />

During the battle,<ref name="501 Plus One" /> Rex's skills were put to the test. Leading from the front,<ref name="Rex Roll" /> he fought with merit<ref name="SW Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> and bravely led his fellow [[clone trooper]]s forward<ref name="Rex Roll" /> without Skywalker, but he was wounded and knocked unconscious. Rex's [[Phase I clone trooper armor|armor]] was damaged in the battle as well,<ref name="501 Plus One" /> with even his [[helmet]] faceplate shattering. Rescuing Rex,<ref name="Rex Roll" /> Skywalker arrived and saved the life of his [[captain]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Hidden behind cover in a rocky terrain, the [[Cloning|clone]] awoke to Skywalker standing over him. The [[Jedi]] apologized for not leading his [[soldier]]s into the fray; Skywalker told Rex that a real leader always led at the front,<ref name="501 Plus One" /> a message that reinforced lessons Rex already knew and became the basis of trust, and a growing friendship, between the captain and the Jedi.<ref name="Rex Roll" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|<nowiki/>'A true leader always leads from the front.'<nowiki/>''"<br />"''Whosa told you dat?''"<br />"''A friend.|Captain Rex and Junior Representative Jar Jar Binks during the Battle of Mimban|<ref name="501 Plus One" />}}
[[File:RexSkywalker-Umbara.jpg|thumb|right|300px|As the Clone Wars continued, Anakin Skywalker and Rex continued to work together as trusted friends and leaders of the 501st Legion.]]
Beyond being one of several post-[[First Battle of Geonosis]] engagements that saw the true level of Rex's skills put to the test,<ref name="Rex Roll" /> the Battle of Arantara became one of the many battles in which Rex fought with excellence.<ref name="SW Book" /> Skywalker and Rex continued to work together as leaders of the 501st<ref name="Darkness">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref> and soon developed a mutual respect, even forming a friendship, despite their differences: while Rex acted by-the-book, Skywalker was far more brash.<ref name="Timelines" /> Becoming far less rigid over time,<ref name="Darkness" /> Rex would come to admire and emulate some of Skywalker's mannerisms.<ref name="Rex">{{Databank|clone-captain-rex|Clone Captain Rex}}</ref>

During a [[Battle of Mimban|battle]] on<ref name="501 Plus One" /> the [[Expansion Region]] [[planet]]<ref name="EncyclopediaComp" /> [[Mimban]],<ref name="501 Plus One" /> another Separatist trouble spot Rex was dispatched to during the early days of the war,<ref name="EncyclopediaComp" /> Rex remembered Skywalker's lesson about leading from the front, using the example to fight [[battle droid]]s alone while his clone troopers evacuated. After he was saved from [[B1-series battle droid|B1-series units]] by<ref name="501 Plus One" /> [[Junior Representative]]<ref name="SW Book" /> [[Jar Jar Binks]], Rex explained his philosophy, noting that a friend had given him the lesson.<ref name="501 Plus One" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Arantara first appeared in "[[501 Plus One]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic story]] printed in [[Age of Republic Special 1|''Age of Republic Special'' 1]],<ref name="501 Plus One" /> which was published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 16]], [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/73017/star_wars_age_of_republic_special_2019_1|text=Star Wars: Age of Republic Special (2019) #1}}</ref> "501 Plus One" was written by [[Marc Guggenheim]] and illustrated by [[Caspar Wijngaard]].<ref name="501 Plus One" /> The capitalization of the battle's name was later confirmed in ''[[The Star Wars Book]]'', a [[2020]] reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]], [[Cole Horton]], and [[Dan Zehr]].<ref name="SW Book" />
