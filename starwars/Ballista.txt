A '''ballista''' was a primitive but effective weapon that fired heavy bolts with ropes at distant targets.<ref name="DB:Tusken Ballista" /> The weapon was used by the [[Tusken Raider]]s of [[Tatooine]]. Members of a Tusken tribe used the ballista in an effort with the citizens of [[Freetown]], [[Marshal]] [[Cobb Vanth]], and [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] to [[Mission to Tatooine (Din Djarin)|kill]] a [[greater krayt dragon]]. The bolts were able to pierce the krayt dragon's hide, but the Tuskens and citizens of Freetown were unable to hold the creature down with the ropes. However, Djarin was able to [[Death|kill]] it.<ref name="Ch.9" />
