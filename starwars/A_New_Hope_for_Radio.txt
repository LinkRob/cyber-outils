"'''''A New Hope'' for Radio'''" is the first part of an article to commemorate the 30th anniversary of the [[Star Wars (radio)|''Star Wars'' radio drama]]. It discusses how the project originated, and how hiring ''[[Star Wars]]'' author [[Brian Daley]] to work on the script made it a success. The article was written by [[Michael Kogge]] and appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 127|''Star Wars Insider'' 127]]. It was later reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2014|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2014]].<ref name="SW127" />
