'''''Canto Bight''''' is the unabridged audio version of the [[canon]] [[Canto Bight (novella collection)|novella collection of the same name]], written by [[Saladin Ahmed]], [[Rae Carson]], [[Mira Grant]], and [[John Jackson Miller]]. The audiobook was performed by [[Sean Kenin Elias-Reyes|Sean Kenin]], [[Saskia Maarleveld]], [[Marc Thompson]], and [[Jonathan Davis]], and was released by [[Random House Audio]] on [[December 5]], [[2017]], as part of [[Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi|Journey to ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'']].

==Publisher's summary==
'''''In Canto Bight, one is free to revel in excess, untouched from the problems of a galaxy once again descending into chaos and war. Dreams can become reality, but the stakes have never been higher—for there is a darkness obscured by all the glamour and luxury.'''''<ref name="Penguin-Download" />

•An honest salesman meets a career criminal as a dream vacation turns into the worst nightmare imaginable, in a story by Saladin Ahmed.<ref name="Penguin-Download" />

•Dreams and schemes collide when a deal over a priceless bottle of wine becomes a struggle for survival, as told by Mira Grant.<ref name="Penguin-Download" />

•Old habits die hard when a servant is forced into a mad struggle for power among Canto Bight's elite, in a tale by Rae Carson.<ref name="Penguin-Download" />

•A deadbeat gambler has one last chance to turn his luck around; all he has to do is survive one wild night, as told by John Jackson Miller.<ref name="Penguin-Download" />
