'''''Bounty Hunters'' 29''' is the twenty-ninth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]] and [[Arif Prianto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 14]], [[2022]].

==Publisher's summary==
''MISSION TO BESTINE IV! BOSSK VS. TANKA! Forced to take on a job for Crimson Dawn, the bounty hunters take on the Empire! Valance has a mission of his own, one that will put everyone he cares about in danger! What is the dark secret that threatens them all?''

==Plot summary==

=== Crimson Dawn's Mission ===
[[T'onga]] and her [[T'onga's crew|crew]], including [[Zuckuss]] and [[Tasu Leech]], accept a mission from [[Crimson Dawn]]: to sabotage an Imperial supply shipment heading to the Imperial naval base on [[Jahnae]], a planet in the [[Bestine system]]. This shipment consists of munitions intended to crush a local insurgency, potentially leading to the deaths of many innocents. The crew travels to Bestine in the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]], where the Imperial transport is expected to land in a remote, water-heavy region near a small village of support staff. T'onga emphasizes the unknown dangers ahead, while Tasu Leech, eager for action, boasts about his combat skills learned in "the fighting pits."

=== Imperial Counter-Operation ===
Unbeknownst to T'onga, the Imperial cargo transport is heavily guarded by a squad led by her former associate, [[Beilert Valance|Valance]]. Valance's team, which includes [[S'ira]] and [[Ankala Sahm|Ankala]], is part of a galaxy-wide Imperial campaign against Crimson Dawn, with bounties flooding the underworld for all syndicate associates. Valance reveals his true motivations to his squad, stating:<blockquote>"WE DON'T SERVE ANYBODY. WE ARE STANDING UP FOR THESE INNOCENT PEOPLE WHO ARE CAUGHT BETWEEN THE EMPIRE AND CRIMSON DAWN." - Valance.</blockquote>He explains that the transport carries medicines and supplies for these innocents, which the Crimson Dawn-backed guerrillas would prevent them from receiving. Based on his past experience, Valance accurately predicts the rocky outcropping where Crimson Dawn's operatives will strike, as it offers the best cover from sensors before the transport reaches the base's full protection.

=== Showdown on Bestine ===
As a massive storm rages on Bestine, the Imperial transport makes landfall. T'onga's crew, including the [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], who T'onga brought along for muscle, prepares to attack. Valance instructs his [[stormtroopers]] to patrol the perimeter and uses [[speeders]] to cover the extensive terrain. T'onga's team moves in, engaging Valance's forces. Tasu Leech, frustrated by the slow pace, clashes with another Trandoshan from Valance's squad. The two engage in a brutal hand-to-hand fight amidst the downpour, with Tasu Leech sustaining heavy blows. Bossk intervenes, engaging the larger Trandoshan to protect his ally. Inside the transport, T'onga and her team push towards the bridge, but the element of surprise is lost as Imperial forces engage them. T'onga realizes they must reach the bridge to stop the ship before reinforcements arrive. As T'onga battles her way through the transport, the lights flicker and die. In the darkness, she hears a familiar voice and recognizes her target.

=== IG-88's Hunt ===
Simultaneously, on the planet [[Son-tuul]], the [[assassin droid]] [[IG-88B|IG-88]] confronts the [[Son-tuul Pride]] Syndicate. He accuses them of violating Imperial law by aiding Crimson Dawn and issues a twenty-five-second ultimatum to surrender their leaders. The [[Rodian]] syndicate members, believing their fortress is impenetrable and unwilling to yield, refuse to comply. As the countdown expires, IG-88 brutally executes the Rodian leaders and their guards, efficiently terminating them with his [[Blaster|blasters]] and bare hands, logging the bounties as per Imperial request.

=== Reunion ===
Back on Bestine, T'onga and Valance find themselves face-to-face in the darkened transport. Both are stunned to recognize their former associate, setting the stage for a dramatic confrontation.
