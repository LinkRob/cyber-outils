'''Eternity Enterprises''' was a company that offered tours of the [[Seikara Caverns]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dekien]]. The former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] once hid a shard of the [[Kyber mirror of Shinsui Temple]] in the [[cave]]rns. When he went to [[Mission to Dekien|retrieve]] it, he accessed the caverns by taking a tour on a [[Eternity Enterprises barge|pleasure barge]] operated by Eternity Enterprises.<ref name="Ronin" />
