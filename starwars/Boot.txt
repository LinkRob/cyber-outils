{{Quote|What are you doing? I was going to study that!''"<br />"''Study the bottom of my boot!|[[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], after Skywalker used his boot to crush a [[brain worm]]|<ref name="Legacy of Terror">{{TCW|Legacy of Terror}}</ref>}}
'''Boots''' were a type of footwear, and could be made of various materials to increase their durability. Boots could come in multiple [[color]]s, and were widely worn across the [[galaxy]] by civilians, military personnel and officials alike. Members of the [[Jedi Order]] often wore boots, along with [[Imperial officer]]s and the various [[stormtrooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] pilots wore insulated boots with their [[flight suit]]s.<ref name="RSOWM">''[[Rebel Starfighters Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>
