The '''Eriaduan cruiser''' was a class of [[cruiser]] used by the [[Eriaduan]]s of the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]] that was active during the [[High Republic Era]]. The cruiser was long, thin, and blade-like, resembling a [[sword]], and had boarding capabilities as well as armament.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
