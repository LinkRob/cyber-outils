By [[37 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the '''conflict on Bri'n''' had arisen between the [[Metal Clan]] and the people of Mistress [[Th'er]], the [[priestess]] of wood. The [[Jedi Order]] sent [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] to the planet<ref name="Age" /> in 37 BBY.<ref name="Date" /> The [[Metal Clan leader|leader]] of the Metal Clan attempted to capture Th'er so that either her word or her [[blood]] could be used to destroy her [[tree]]s. Surrounded by forces of the Metal Clan, Jinn chose to escape with Mistress Th'er in an [[Eta-class shuttle|''Eta''-class shuttle]] and return to [[Coruscant]]. After seeking guidance from [[the Force]], Jinn met with Th'er, suggesting that she recruit allies to her cause to help fight against the Metal Clan.<ref name="Age" />
