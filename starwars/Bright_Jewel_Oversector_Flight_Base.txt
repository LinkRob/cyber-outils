The '''Bright Jewel Oversector Flight Base''' was a flight base maintained by the [[Galactic Empire]] on the [[moon]] [[Axxila III]], in the [[Bright Jewel Oversector]]. Unlike others, this Imperial flight base had only a single [[Cantina|bar]], the [[Zero Angle]], for both groundhogs ([[Atmosphere|atmospheric]] [[pilot]]s) and vac-heads ([[Realspace|space]] pilots).<ref name="LCZA" />
