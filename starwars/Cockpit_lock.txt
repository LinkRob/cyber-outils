A '''Cockpit lock''' was a device used to secure a ship's cockpit, preventing potential thieves from gaining access. One such device was used during the [[High Republic Era]] by the [[pilot]] [[Luggs Neelo]] on his [[XD-7 speedster]]. The locks were not infallible, however, as the [[thief]] [[Draiven Bosh]] was able to break the one on Neelo's [[Starship|ship]] and steal it.<ref name="Starship Show">{{YJA|The Starship Show}}</ref>
