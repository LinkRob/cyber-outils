The '''Deco Straits''' were a location that housed a number of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] factions. After the [[Battle of Jakku]]<ref name="Chaos Agent 5">[[Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent 5|''Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent'' 5]]</ref> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Unidentified Imperial faction|one]] of the remaining factions attempted to continually recruit the freelance [[slicer]] [[Dellen Aphra]], but she refused each time.<ref name="Chaos Agent 5" />

Eventually, the faction sent three individuals to her [[Dellen Aphra's safe house|safe house]] on the astronomical object [[Provina]] to forcibly recruit her. After their arrival into the safe house, Dellen's [[Aphra family|half-sister]]&mdash;the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]]&mdash;asked what was happening, to which Dellen informed her of the faction's attempts to recruit her. After defeating the three individuals, Dellen and Chelli left Provina.<ref name="Chaos Agent 5" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Deco Straits were mentioned in the [[Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent 5|fifth issue]] of the [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent]]''. The issue was written by [[Cherish Chen]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Chaos Agent 5" /> on [[October 29]], 2025.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/122236/star_wars_doctor_aphra_-_chaos_agent_2025_5|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra - Chaos Agent (2025) #5}}</ref>
