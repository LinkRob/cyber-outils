During the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|war]] between the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]s [[E'ronoh]] and [[Eiram]], the former launched an '''attack''' on a [[southern rain forest]] on the latter. The [[E'ronoh's military|E'ronoh forces]] had been unaware that the land, previously believed to be uninhabited, actually contained an outpost. The E'roni dropped [[proton torpedo]]es on the [[Water|rain]] [[forest]], destroying the outpost and leaving no survivors.<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Odelia]], the [[queen]] [[consort]] of Eiram provided a [[datacard]] that contained a recording of the attack to be played during a [[Summit aboard the Paxion|peace summit]] aboard the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Alif-class Longbeam|''Alif''-class Longbeam]] ''[[Paxion]]''.<ref name="Convergence" />
