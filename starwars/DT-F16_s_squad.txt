A [[death trooper]] '''squad''' was tasked with the safe transportation of a large [[kyber crystal]] from the [[Jedha system]] for delivery to the [[Tonnis sector]] in a [[freighter]] to rendezvous with an [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] under the command of [[Captain]] [[Slavin]]. The freighter was [[Mission to Faos Station|infiltrated]] by the rebels [[Saw Gerrera]], [[Sabine Wren]], [[Ezra Bridger]], and the [[droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]]. They took control of hold six, where the crystal was being kept, forcing [[Commander]] [[DT-F16]] to order her squad to retreat. Only DT-F16 could successfully retreat, as the rebel infiltrators subdued the rest of the squad. Saw Gerrera later sabotaged the crystal causing it to destabilize. The rebels escaped as the crystal exploded, destroying the freighter and [[Slavin's Star Destroyer]], killing Slavin and ending DT-F16 and her squad in the process.<ref name="INR" />

Other members of the squad included [[3-6]] and [[Unidentified death trooper|another death trooper]].<ref name="INR" />
