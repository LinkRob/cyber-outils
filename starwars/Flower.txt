'''Flowers''' were [[Reproduction|reproductive]] structures found in certain types of [[plant]]s<ref name="SWTVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> that existed in a wide variety of [[color]]s. Some flowers blossomed from [[tree]]s, such as [[Color|pale-blue]] flower of the [[blueblossom tree]]<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> and [[uneti blossom]] of the [[uneti tree]].<ref name="TRS">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> One type was the [[hai-ka flower]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

Some species like the [[Marina Rung flower]] were [[Foodstuff|edible]], others, such as the [[Queen's Heart]], were used as ornamental plants.<ref name="SWTVE" /> [[Greez Dritus]], the [[captain]] of the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]'', collected flowering plants in a [[terrarium]] onboard the ship.<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>
