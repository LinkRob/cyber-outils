A '''ferryman''',<ref name="SC 102" /> or a '''dinghy driver''',<ref name="Dinghy driver">{{Databank|dinghy-driver|Dinghy driver}}</ref> was an occupation that involved ferrying people from their [[starship]]s to ports. During the [[New Republic Era]], [[Borgo Prime]] utilized [[space dinghy]]s [[pilot]]ed by ferrymen to transport [[pirate]]s from their ships to the port.<ref name="SC 102" />

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ferrymen were first mentioned in the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'' episode "[[Way, Way Out Past the Barrier]]," which aired on [[December 2]], [[2024]]<ref name="SC 102" />
