'''Erisher''' was an individual who served the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[commander]] aboard the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Inflictor]]'' following the [[Battle of Endor]]. Erisher was present when [[Grand Moff]] [[Randd]] promoted [[Ciena Ree]] to [[Captain]] of the ''Inflictor''.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

==Behind the scenes==
Erisher was first mentioned in the novel ''[[Lost Stars]]'', which was written by [[Claudia Gray]] and published in [[2015]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />
