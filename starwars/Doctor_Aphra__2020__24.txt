'''''Doctor Aphra'' 24''' is the twenty-fourth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[September 28]], [[2022]].

==Publisher's summary==
''"DESCENT" Desperate to rescue DOCTOR APHRA at any cost, SANA STARROS and her CREW'S search leads them to an OLD HAUNT&hellip;and two FAMILIAR FACES they might not survive! Meanwhile, Aphra delves deeper into the secrets of THE ASCENDANT&hellip;and the true origin of the SPARK ETERNAL!''

==Opening crawl==
{{OpeningCrawl
|name=DESCENT
|text=As a Spark-possessed Doctor Aphra made her way to the ''Vermillion'', flag ship of Crimson Dawn, to gather more
information from the Archivist, Sana Starros has gathered her team of Aphra associates and convinced Magna

The team ended up on the planet Birukay, but there were no prized possessions waiting for them. Instead,
Aphra had left the two murderous droids BT-1 and 0-0-0 there to greet the search party&hellip;.}}
