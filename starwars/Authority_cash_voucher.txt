An '''Authority cash voucher''' was a form of [[currency]] in the [[Corporate Sector Authority]]. The criminal [[Big Bunji]] once worked with the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] on a scam that involved running counterfeit Authority cash vouchers to the poor people on the far side of the [[Cron Drift]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>
