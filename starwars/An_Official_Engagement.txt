"'''An Official Engagement'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year two of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was a stand-alone adventure and was written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]].

{{Game-stub}}

==Opening crawl==
''A private courtship ends in a very public, very controversial, wedding announcement. Even in Cularin, love and politics mix in the most curious ways, but assassination threats? Hardly an ideal bridal shower present. An intensive roleplay-intensive (note: NOT combat-free!) adventure for Living Force heroes levels 1-9.''
