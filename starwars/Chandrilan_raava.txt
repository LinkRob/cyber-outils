'''Chandrilan raava''' was an [[alcohol]]ic beverage from the [[planet]] [[Chandrila]] that was consumable by [[human]]s.<ref name="AEE" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[loyalty officer]] [[Sinjir Rath Velus]] drank it the [[Standard day|night]] before he intended to rescue [[bounty hunter]] [[Jas Emari]] from the planet [[Jakku]]. He recounted to his [[Norra Wexley's team|colleague]] [[Temmin Wexley]] that he had not [[Sleep|slept]] that night, but instead read a [[book]] and drank [[Chandrilan]] raava, since he was out of [[rum]].<ref name="AEE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chandrilan raava was mentioned in ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', a [[2017]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the third installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="AEE" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Liquor/Legends|liquor]] [[raava]] was first introduced in "[[Out of the Cradle]]," a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure written by [[Patricia A. Jackson]], which was published in the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 2|second issue]] of ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'' in [[May]] [[1994]].<ref name="AJ">{{JournalCite|2|Out of the Cradle}}</ref>
