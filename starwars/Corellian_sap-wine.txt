'''Corellian sap-wine''' was a type of [[wine]] from<ref name="ALD" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. It was [[Diet|potable]] by [[human]]s and possessed [[alcohol]]ic properties. Several [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="ALD" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[loyalty officer]] [[Sinjir Rath Velus]] consumed Corellian sap-wine and became drunk. His crewmate [[Temmin Wexley]] tried to ask him for advice concerning self-identity, but upon attempting to respond, Velus burped and passed out. [[Standard week|Weeks]] later,<ref name="ALD" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> Wexley reflected on Velus's consumption of the beverage and how his drunkenness had prevented him from finishing their prior conversation.<ref name="ALD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corellian sap-wine was mentioned in ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', a [[2016]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the second installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="ALD" />
