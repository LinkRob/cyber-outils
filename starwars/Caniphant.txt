'''Caniphants''' were a crafty type of creature that covered their own tracks, making them extremely difficult to find. A feral caniphant at one point terrorized a [[Unidentified ranch|ranch]] on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]] where the [[Hunting|hunter]] [[Mosey Cimmaron]] and her [[Mosey Cimmaron's mother|mother]] lived, destroying the barn on two occasions. In order to locate the hard-to-track creature, Cimmaron and her [[Parent|mother]] reversed the ranch's [[moisture vaporator]]s and filled the air with [[Water|moisture]]. This made the caniphant's scent far more pungent, allowing them to track it.<ref name="Survivor" />

{{GameMechanics}}
After the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] [[Death|slew]] the unpleasant smelling [[mogu]] known as the [[Mire Terror]]<ref name="Survivor" /> in [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> Cimmaron told him about the caniphant that terrorized the ranch as an example of how important smell could be when hunting .<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], a caniphant was mentioned in an optional conversation available after completing the "The Mire Terror" rumor in the [[2023]] [[Respawn Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Survivor" /> The [[Caniphant/Legends|creature]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it appeared in ''[[The Game Chambers of Questal]]'', a [[1990]] ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' adventure written by [[Paul Murphy]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="Questal">''[[The Game Chambers of Questal]]''</ref>
