The '''Bombardment of Xorrn''' took place on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Xorrn]] in [[19 BBY]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Following numerous unsuccessful [[Battle of Xorrn (Clone Wars)|assaults]] on the planet after the war's [[First Battle of Geonosis|opening battle]], to destroy illegally constructed [[Geonosian]] [[Geonosian hive spire|hive spires]] and [[battle droid]] [[Factory|factories]] buried beneath the surface, the Republic's capture of Xorrn became worthless to further casualties. To that end, the government dispatched the [[9th Engineering Battalion]] and its [[Ferra sector fleet|fleet]] operating in the [[Ferra sector]] to eradicate the factories, ordering a [[Orbital bombardment|planetary bombardment]] on Xorrn.

In the bombardment, countless [[core ship]]s belonging to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Trade Federation]], which were retrieving battle [[droid]] shipments for the Confederacy, were blasted and destroyed by the Republic. As a result of the planetary besiegement, Xorrn's surface became riddled in a graveyard of Republic and Separatist [[warship]]s, which were left long after the war's [[Mission to Mustafar|end]].

==Behind the scenes==
The Bombardment of Xorrn was mentioned in the [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] adventure book ''[[Friends Like These]]'' as part of the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]].<ref name="Friends" />
