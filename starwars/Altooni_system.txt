The '''Altooni system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Corellian sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]]. It was located in grid square M-11 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Altoonian ale]]|system=Altooni [[Star system|system]]}}</ref> [[Altoonian ale]].<ref name="Double or Nothing 2">[[Lando - Double or Nothing 2|''Lando - Double or Nothing'' 2]]</ref>
