'''Felix Tagge''' was a [[human]] member of the [[House of Tagge]] who served as one of the [[Tagge Corporation]]'s two [[Head of Finance|Heads of Finance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The other Head of Finance was [[Adelaide Tagge]], whom Felix referred to as "dear." Felix and Adelaide served on the Board of the Tagge Corporation, led by Adelaide's cousin [[Domina Tagge]].<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" /> Felix and Adelaide were the [[parent]]s of [[Ronen Tagge]].<ref name="Aphra 28" />
