'''Argin''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who resided in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. He and [[Bishta]] planned to [[Thief|steal]] from the [[collector]] [[Dok-Ondar]]'s [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities|antiquities store]], but after hearing of the recent [[death]] of [[Kase Finto]], who had been killed after attempting to scam Dok-Ondar, Argin warned Bishta against doing the job.<ref name="Datapad" />
