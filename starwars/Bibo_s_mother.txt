The '''mother''' of [[Bibo]] was an adult [[Gender|female]] [[rokkna]]. As an adult, she was large enough to be capable of destroying the ''[[Colossus]]'' platform by herself. She [[Bibo incident|attacked]] the platform trying to retrieve her child after he had accidentally been brought on board inside of a wrecked [[Z-95 Headhunter]] and adopted as a pet by [[Neeku Vozo]]. Her attack had been foreseen in dreams by [[Eila]], who told Vozo that the only way to stop the attack would be to return Bibo. Vozo was initially reluctant, as he and [[Tamara Ryvora]] mistakenly assumed that the mother intended to ''eat'' Bibo. Eventually, Vozo realized that Bibo and his mother were the same species and returned him, after which the mother departed peacefully.<ref name="Bibo" />
