'''''Andor'': A Disney+ Day Special Look''' was a special documentary that detailed a behind the scenes look at the then-upcoming ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' television series. It was released on [[Disney+]] on [[September 8]], [[2022]].<ref name="Special Look" /> It has since been removed.

==Official description==
''Celebrate Disney+ Day with a special look at the Star Wars original series Andor coming to Disney+ on September 21.<ref name="Look on Disney">{{DisneyPlus|url=movies/andor-a-disney-day-special-look/67DzOGaaVwBJ|text=Watch Andor: A Disney+ Day Special Look|nolive=1}}</ref>''
