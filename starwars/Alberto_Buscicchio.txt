'''Alberto Buscicchio''' is a reference book colorist who provided additional colors for ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> He also provided illustrations for ''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Wookiees]]''<ref name="Celebration" /> and ''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Clone Troopers]]''.<ref name="Edelweiss">{{Insight|url=products/star-wars-the-secrets-of-the-clone-troopers|text=Star Wars: The Secrets of the Clone Troopers}}</ref>
