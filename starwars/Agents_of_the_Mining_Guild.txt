During the [[Imperial Era]] the [[Mining Guild|'''Mining Guild''']] sent '''agents''' to patrol and represent its interests in the [[Spice mines of Kessel|spice mines]] on the [[planet]] [[Kessel]] in order to make sure that it got its cut of the profits. The local [[Pyke Syndicate]] agreed to the guild employees being there, but often disagreed with them on tactics for running the mine, as the criminals preferred less bureaucratic and more brutal methods of security.<ref name="SoloOG" /> 

==Organization==
The agents of the Mining Guild present on Kessel wore [[Color|white]] [[Clothing|coveralls]] along with white [[glove]]s, a brown [[bandolier]], and a white mask and a datafeed [[headset]] and red [[goggles]]. The coveralls were marked with red sigils denoting one of the houses of the Mining Guild. Some of the agents also beige helmets while others didn't.''<ref name="Solo" />''

==History==
[[File:Tolsite-at-Kessel.jpg|thumb|left|Yorsh Manted and another guild employee on Kessel in 10 BBY.|200px]]
In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Yorsh Manted]] and another agent of the Mining Guild accompanied the Pyke [[capo]] [[Quay Tolsite]] when he traveled to one of the mine's landing pads to meet the crew of the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] [[Millennium Falcon]]. The [[Beckett's gang|crew]] was led by the [[human]] [[Qi'ra]] who claimed to be a [[Slavery|slaver]] and offered her captives [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] as a sample of her product. Manted and the other guild employee checked the two slaves and upon signaling that they were satisfied, Tolsite had Solo and Chewbacca taken away while he took Qi'ra to discuss business. Tolsite led Qi'ra and her partners [[Tobias Beckett]] and [[L3-37]] to the control room of the mine where other agents were working.

The Falcon's crew however were actually on Kessel to steal the Pyke's [[coaxium]]. Beckett and Qi'ra killed Tolsite, his guards, and the agents stationed in the room, and L3 started a [[Raid on Kessel|revolt]] in which the slaves overthrew their Pyke overseers.''<ref name="Solo" />''

==Behind the scenes==
The agents first appeared as a minor role in the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="Solo" /> and were first described in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]], but remained unnamed.<ref name="SoloOG" />
