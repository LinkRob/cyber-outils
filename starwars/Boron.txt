'''Boron''' was a substance that was present in the soil of the [[planet]] [[Lah'mu]]<ref name="R1VG" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Raioballo sector]]<ref name="RO World Locations">{{SW|url=news/where-in-the-galaxy-are-the-worlds-of-rogue-one|text=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Rogue One''?|int=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Rogue One?}}</ref> and in the [[atmosphere]] of the planet [[Toshaal]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> of the Outer Rim's [[Cadma sector]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Toshaal system]]</ref> The boron in Lahmu's soil contributed to the [[Water|groundwater]] there having an unpleasant taste, leading the [[Erso family]] that had [[Erso homestead|settled]] on the planet to use [[GX-8 water vaporator]]s to instead distill water [[Gas|vapor]] from the atmosphere.<ref name="R1VG" />

Boron could be used to manufacture [[Diamond-Boron Missile]]s.<ref name="Hyena">{{FFGXW2|set=Hyena-class Droid Bomber Expansion Pack|cardname=Diamond-Boron Missiles}}</ref> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="3 ABY">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[governor]] [[Thorden]] of Toshaal's [[moon]] [[Toshara]] announced plans to restore the aging [[Toshaal Atmospheric Mining Station]] that was situated in Toshaal's [[orbit]]. In a [[Holonet News Toshara]] news report, the [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Kotera Braal]] noted that the refurbishment of the [[Space station|station]] would facilitate a four-fold increase in the extraction of boron from Toshaal's atmosphere.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Boron was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''.<ref name="R1VG" /> The name "[[Boron/Legends|boron]]" was originally introduced to the ''Star Wars'' franchise in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via a mention of a [[boron power generator]] in ''[[Strike Force: Shantipole]]'', a [[1988]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure authored by [[Ken Rolston]] and [[Steve Gilbert]] and published by [[West End Games]] for use with ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Shantipole">''[[Strike Force: Shantipole]]''</ref> [[Steve Perry]]'s [[1996]] novel [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (novel)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'']] subsequently provided the first identification of boron as a distinct material.<ref name="SotE">[[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (novel)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'' novel]]</ref>

[[File:BoronRealWorld.jpg|thumb|right|250px|A sample of real-world crystalline boron]]
In the real world, [[Wikipedia:Boron|boron]] is a [[Wikipedia:Chemical element|chemical element]] and [[Wikipedia:Metalloid|metalloid]], that, in its pure [[Wikipedia:Allotropes of boron|crystalline form]], has a black [[Wikipedia:Lustre (mineralogy)|luster]] and is hard but brittle. In nature, boron has low abundance and is predominantly found in the form of [[Wikipedia:Chemical compound|chemical compounds]]. It is essential to [[Wikipedia:Plant|plant]] growth and also has many industrial applications. Boron is used to increase hardness in [[Wikipedia:Steel|steel]], and it combines with various [[Wikipedia:Metal|metals]] to form a class of compounds called [[Wikipedia:Boride|borides]], with some of the latter being among the hardest and the most heat-resistant of all known substances.<ref name="BoronBritannica">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/science/boron-chemical-element|text=Boron &#124; Properties, Uses, & Facts|author=The Editors of Encyclopædia Britannica|date=2024-11-11|work=[[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica|''Encyclopædia Britannica'']]|archivedate=20241224193343}}</ref> [[Wikipedia:Boron carbide|Boron carbide]] and [[Wikipedia:Boron nitride|boron nitride]] are the hardest known materials after [[Wikipedia:Diamond|diamond]], with boron carbide being used in the manufacture of military armor.<ref name="BoronCarbideBritannica">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/science/boron-carbide|text=Boron carbide &#124; Hardness, Abrasion Resistance, Refractory|author=The Editors of Encyclopædia Britannica|date=2010-04-14|work=[[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica|''Encyclopædia Britannica'']]|archivedate=20240619042932}}</ref>
