The '''Antar Atrocity''' was the name given by the media to an operation undertaken by the [[Galactic Empire]] in which Imperial forces, led by [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], carried out arrests, executions, and massacres on [[Antar 4]], as punishment for Antar 4's allegiance to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]].

==Origins==
[[File:CountDooku-SWBC61.png|thumb|left|300px|The Antar Atrocity could be traced back to the Separatist Crisis spurred on by Count Dooku (pictured) during the last years of the Galactic Republic]]
The origins of the Antar Atrocity were found in the [[Separatist Crisis]] in which thousands of [[star system]]s left the [[Galactic Republic]] and pledged loyalty to [[Count]] [[Dooku]], the leader of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. One world to leave the Republic was [[Antar 4]], which had been a member of the Republic since its inception. The secession began with a [[Antar 4 schism|schism]] among the inhabitants of Antar 4, in which Separatist-allied terrorist groups began to gain power. Republic loyalists from the [[Gotal]] species were able to retain their power until after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], but Antar 4 fell to the Separatists. Millions of Gotal fled to [[Atzerri]], a colony world, and were replaced by species loyal to the Separatists, such as [[Koorivar]], [[Gossam]]s, and other species. Some Gotal and Koorivar loyalists formed a resistance group, but Antar 4 remained in the hands of the Separatists.<ref name="Tarkin" />

The end of the Clone Wars saw the death of Dooku<ref name="Tarkin" /> at the [[Battle of Coruscant]], the destruction of [[General]] [[Grievous]] during the [[Battle of Utapau]], and the execution of the [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] in the [[mission to Mustafar]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> With the collapse of the Confederate state,<ref name="Visual">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> the Republic claimed victory over the Separatists. With the war at an end, [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] declared himself [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] and [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]], promising a new era of peace, prosperity, and security for all of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

==The Atrocity==
{{Quote|When Imperial forces under Wilhuff Tarkin punished the world for siding with the Separatists in the Clone Wars, aggression didn't stop with arrests by secret police. Next came executions, and finally massacres.|Airen Cracken|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
[[File:Tarkin-profile.png|thumb|right|200px|Imperial forces in the Antar Atrocity were led by Moff Wilhuff Tarkin]]
In the early days of the Empire, Imperial forces targeted former Separatist worlds, including Antar 4, in order to pacify any remnants of Separatist rebellion.<ref name="Tarkin" /> Someone who was involved in many post-Clone Wars mop-up operations,<ref name="FFG 90">{{FactFile2014|90|TAR 9-12|Wilhuff Tarkin}}</ref> [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]<ref name="Tarkin" />&mdash;a skilled [[commanding officer]] whose talents could crush remaining Separatist opposition present within [[the galaxy]]<ref name="FFG 90" />&mdash;was assigned to make an example of the formerly rebellious world<ref name="Tarkin" /> and punish it for siding with the CIS.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> shortly after Tarkin held a [[Imperial summit on Eriadu|high-level summit on Eriadu]] designed to find ways to unite the galaxy under the Imperial banner,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> Imperial forces under his command<ref name="Rebel Files" /> invaded Antar 4<ref name="Tarkin" /> and suppressed its people.<ref name="FFG 90" /> Tarkin made no attempt to sort the Separatists from Imperial loyalists when he carried out mass arrests, executions, and massacres.<ref name="Tarkin" /> The operation began with those arrests, which were carried out by [[Law enforcement agency|secret police]], before moving into the executions and massacres.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Tarkin carried out the operation with brutal efficiency, as was typical of how he served the Imperial regime and which would come to characterize his career.<ref name="FFG 90" />

==Aftermath==
===Public outcry===
[[File:DeathStar_SWII.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The then-under construction Death Star promised to make campaigns like the Antar Atrocity obsolete]]
Although the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]] attempted to hide the fact that many loyalists had been killed, the news media learned of what happened and dubbed it the Antar Atrocity. The event attracted controversy and attention from members of the [[Core Worlds]], with many citizens clamoring for answers as to what had transpired.<ref name="Tarkin" /> The incident was one of several atrocities carried out by Tarkin during the early Imperial Era that saw public outcry.<ref name="Ultimate">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> Tarkin's [[Tarkin Massacre|actions on Ghorman]] had inspired a public reaction as well. In response to these controversial events<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> but particularly the Antar Atrocity, the Emperor removed Moff Tarkin, his ally and friend, from public sight and assigned him to [[Pacification operations in the Western Reaches|pacification operations]] in the [[Western Reaches]], hoping to distance him from the Antar 4 controversy. Later, Palpatine assigned him to the [[Outer Rim Territories]] to oversee the construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], the Emperor's Deep Space Orbital Battle Station. The creation of the battle station would make invasions like the Antar Atrocity obsolete, with it instead hoped the fear of the station alone would be enough to bring entire star systems to their knees.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Overall, the Antar Atrocity&mdash;much like his actions at<ref name="207 POV">{{InsiderCite|207|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> the Outer Rim planet [[Mon Cala]], where Tarkin had commanded an [[Invasion of Mon Cala|invasion]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> shortly after his actions on Antar<ref name="Timelines" />&mdash;helped establish Tarkin's merciless reputation.<ref name="207 POV" /> One of the most vocal reporters of the Antar Atrocity was [[Anora Fair]], who worked alongside [[Hask Taff]] to document the arrests and massacres that were taking place. They were assisted by [[Berch Teller]], a disaffected former Republic intelligence operative who provided them with the information they needed to report on the Atrocity. COMPNOR realized this and targeted them for assassination, but Teller was able to bring himself and the others to safety before the Empire could eliminate them.<ref name="Tarkin" /> In the opinion of later [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[General]] [[Airen Cracken]], the Antar Atrocity would never have become publicly known as the massacre it was if had not been leaked to the media.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|Teller's example ''[in exposing the Antar Atrocity]'' proves it's possible to sway public opinion against the Empire's version of events. Atrocities are happening every day. Let's put resources into counterpropaganda.|Airen Cracken, on the lessons of the Antar Atrocity|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
[[File:CarrionSpike-2020StarWars7.png|thumb|right|300px|Berch Teller and other resistance fighters stole the ''Carrion Spike'' (pictured) and launched a campaign against Imperial targets]]
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Teller led a small [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|cell]] of resistance fighters [[Berch Teller campaign|against]] the Empire. The cell included Fair and Taff. They [[Mission to Murkhana|stole]] the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', Moff Tarkin's personal vessel, and used it to cause damage and sow chaos in different corners of the galaxy. The Empire eventually captured Teller's compatriots and quietly executed them, while Teller himself escaped and later tracked Tarkin to the Moff's homeworld of [[Eriadu]], where Teller attempted to assassinate the now promoted [[Grand Moff]] Tarkin but was thwarted by Tarkin and his uncle [[Jova Tarkin|Jova]]. Teller was left in the [[Carrion Plateau|wilderness]] to survive.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Meanwhile, Tarkin had re-appeared to the public in a grand fashion with his new title, all without interviewers mentioning the Antar Atrocity or asking about where he had been.<ref name="Tarkin" /> In a later classified letter to Rebel Alliance [[Commander-in-Chief]] [[Mon Mothma]], with the text designed RI 336-IS and able to be read only by personnel with the rank of general or above, General Cracken reflected on the Antar Atrocity and included what he considered a belated thank you to Teller, whose efforts in leaking the event to the media he applauded. To Cracken, Teller's actions were proof that counterpropaganda could be effective and overrule the Imperial narrative for any of the countless atrocities carried out under Palpatine's rule.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
The reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' recounts the Antar Atrocity after the [[invasion of Mon Cala]].<ref name="Ultimate" /> However, the later reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' pushed the invasion to be after the Antar Atrocity,<ref name="Timelines" /> which was repeated in ''Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition''{{'s}} updated version ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />
