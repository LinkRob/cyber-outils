'''Defense Drones''', also referred to as '''Targeting Drones''' or '''Healing Droids''', were a [[drone]] model designed by the rookie [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[medic]], [[programmer]], and tinkerer, [[Sprocket]], for use in the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition hosted on [[Vespaara]]. These small [[Repulsorlift|hovering]] drones were developed for a twofold purpose, to both seek out Sprocket's enemies within the [[Grand Arena]] and highlight their position, allowing others to see them through obstacles, as well as reinforce allies by projecting an [[Deflector shield|overshield]] that lessoned the damage dealt by incoming [[blaster]] fire. Their healing capacity could alternatively distribute a [[bacta]] solution, similar to his [[Medical Droid (Sprocket)|Medical Droids]]. Quick and nimble, these droids would encircle their targets from above to perform their assigned task, and would usually emit a pulsing [[Color|blue]] glow that turned red when targeting an opponent.<ref name="Hunters" /> They were nearly identical to the [[Senate hovercam droid]],<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> albeit much smaller.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:SprocketDroidConcepts-AnnaKay.jpg|right|250px|thumb|Concept art by Anna Kay]]
This [[droid]] was designed for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', which was developed by [[NaturalMotion]]'s [[BossAlien]]<ref name="Hunters" /> and initially published by [[Zynga]] on [[November 16]], [[2021]] as a [[Wikipedia:Soft launch|soft launch]]<ref name="Soft">{{Twitter|ZyngaStarWars|status/1460656552734605317|[[Zynga]] Star Wars|date=2021-11-16|quote=We are excited to announce Star Wars: Hunters is now entering soft launch and is available on Android for players in India, Philippines, Malaysia & Indonesia.|archivedate=20220625084625|archivefile=File:ZyngaStarWars-SoftLaunchDate-Twitter.png}}</ref> before the global release on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Global">{{SWYouTube|4dZcq6m3y40|Star Wars: Hunters Launch Date Reveal Trailer}}</ref> They were designed by concept artist [[Anna Kay]],<ref name="Kay">{{WebCite|url=https://www.annakayart.net/professionalwork|text=Professional Work|work=[[Anna Kay]]'s [https://www.annakayart.net official website]|archivedate=20250308190224}}</ref> with the final look being inspired by the Senate hovercam droid which first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="TPM" /> At one point, Sprocket had separate droids for seeking and defense, though at some point they were combined into this one unit.<ref name="Kay" /> Other unused concepts for this droid included one that resembled a [[Hologlide J57 cam droid]], and others that looked like the head of a [[DUM-series pit droid]].<ref name="TPM" />
