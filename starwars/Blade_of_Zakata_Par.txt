The '''Blade of Zakata Par''' was an artifact [[Phee Genoa]] claimed to have found. On a [[Discovery on Skara Nal|journey]] to the [[Kaldar Trinary system]]<ref name="Entombed" /> between [[19 BBY]] and [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Genoa offered to tell the young [[Cloning|clone]] [[Omega]] the story of how she had found the blade, which Omega was interested in. As she began, Genoa described the tale as "exciting" and advised Omega to strap in.<ref name="Entombed" />
