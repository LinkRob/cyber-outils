This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Solo" /> [[Freedi Pamular]] was a member of this species.<ref name="Andor" />

==Biology and appearance==
This bipedal [[species]]<ref name="Solo" /> was distinguished by their large builds and [[Color|beige]] skin. They could speak the language [[Narkinian]].<ref name="Andor" />

==Species in the galaxy==
[[File:Freedis-Species-Solo.png|thumb|left|200px|A member of this species fights back against the Pykes on Kessel]]
In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Timeline">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> a member of this species was enslaved by the [[Pyke Syndicate]] on the [[planet]] [[Kessel]], being freed and engaging in a revolt during a [[Coaxium]] heist performed by the crew of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' gone wrong.<ref name="Solo" /> Later, in [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|111}}</ref> a member of this species named [[Freedi Pamular]] and his [[Keredian]] [[brother]] [[Dewi Pamular|Dewi]] were [[fishing]] by a quarry [[Water|lake]] on the small [[moon]] [[Narkina 5]] when they captured the fugitives [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] and [[Ruescott Melshi]], who had been trying to [[Thief|steal]] the pairs' [[TUG-b13|quadjumper]].<ref name="Andor" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Freedi-Pamular-Sculpt.jpg|thumb|right|200px|The sculpt for this species on the set of Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]
The species briefly made their onscreen debut in the [[2018]] Anthology Series film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Solo" /> before being fully showcased in the [[Daughter of Ferrix|eleventh episode]] of the [[Andor Season 1|first season]] of the television show ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', which was written by [[Tony Gilroy]], directed by [[Benjamin Caron]], and released on [[November 16]], [[2022]].<ref name="Release dates">{{Twitter|starwars|status/1557442379975573504|[[Star Wars]]|date=2022-8-10|quote=This is what revolution looks like. Watch #Andor, a Star Wars Original Series, streaming only on @DisneyPlus September 21.|archivedate=20220810191942}}</ref> The design was originally created for<ref name="Trivia2">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e11-trivia-gallery|text="Daughter of Ferrix" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film,<ref name="RO">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', but did not make it into the final cut of the film. Plans were then made to show off the design during the [[Kessel]] sequence of<ref name="Trivia2" /> ''Solo: A Star Wars Story'',<ref name="Solo" /> ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', with the Andor Season 1 trivia erroneously stating they were entirely cut from the film,<ref name="Trivia2" /> however they do make a blink-and-you'll-miss-it appearance.<ref name="Solo" />
