The '''''Fellfire''''' was one of three [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers]] of the [[First Order]] that partook in the [[Battle of D'Qar]] alongside the [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|dreadnought]] ''[[Fulminatrix]]''. [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] had planned to destroy the [[Resistance]] in a show of might using the [[dreadnought]]'s [[Orbital autocannon|autocannons]] in [[orbit]] of the [[planet]] [[D'Qar]], but [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] and the [[Resistance Starfighter Corps]]' bravado in taking on the ''Fulminatrix'' by themselves succeeded in eliminating the [[warship]]. While [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] launched from the ''Fellfire'' and its fellow [[capital ship]]s crippled the Resistance's force of fighters and [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|bombers]], the [[First Order Navy|First Order flotilla]] failed to vanquish the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]], which fled unscathed.

==Description==
{{Quote|I'll be a tiny target for those big cannons of theirs. They'll never hit me.|Poe Dameron, regarding the ''Fellfire'' and the other First Order ships' weaponry|<ref name="Poe28" />}}
As a [[warship]] of the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] model,<ref name="TLJjn" /> the ''Fellfire'' measured 2915.84 [[meter]] (9566.29 [[Foot|feet]]) in length,<ref name="Length" /> possessed three [[Destroyer Ion Engine]]s and eight [[Gemon-8 ion engine]]s,<ref name="CT" /> and was equipped with a [[hyperdrive]].<ref name="TLJn" /> It also had a [[III-a1a primary hypermatter-annihilation reactor]], a [[deflector shield generator]], [[multi-spectrum sensor tower]]s, [[sensor tower]]s, a [[hyperwave comm scanner]],<ref name="Cross" /> and [[hangar]] bays.<ref name="Poe28" />

Resistance [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] was confident that the ''Fellfire''{{'s}} large [[laser cannon]]s would fail to hit his [[Black One|X-wing starfighter]] if they engaged in combat.<ref name="Poe28" /> Nevertheless, the [[capital ship]] was able to launch its complement of [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s to counter Resistance [[Starfighter|fighters]].<ref name="TLJjn" />

==History==
===Fiery ambitions===
{{Quote|<nowiki />''[&hellip;]'' they'll all be competing with each other, doing anything they can to make sure it's ''their'' ship that picks me off.|Poe Dameron, voicing his plans for distracting the ''Fellfire'' and other Star Destroyers|<ref name="Poe28" />}}
[[File:ResistanceNavyOverDqar-TLJ.png|thumb|left|360px|The Resistance fleet evacuating D'Qar]]
The ''Fellfire''<ref name="TLJjn" /> was a [[First Order]] ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer that accompanied two other [[Starship|vessels]] of its class&mdash;including [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]]'s [[flagship]], the ''[[Finalizer]]''&mdash;to<ref name="TLJn" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Ileenium system]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|VIII}} to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Following a carefully orchestrated demonstration under Hux's [[Commanding officer|command]], the [[First Order Navy|First Order flotilla]] aimed to destroy the [[Resistance]] at its [[Resistance base|base]] on the [[planet]] [[D'Qar]] and thereby end the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between them and the Resistance in a spectacle that would demonstrate the might of the First Order to the rest of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TLJn" /> En route to D'Qar, the [[Star Destroyer]]s' movements were detected by [[Resistance High Command]], who immediately ordered an evacuation of their base.<ref name="Poe28" />

Commander Dameron proposed that he could buy [[time]] for the escape by manning a single [[T-70 X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] and, taking advantage of the First Order commanders' egos, taunt them into attempting to down his fighter, which he would then [[Pilot|fly]] close to the Star Destroyers in order to encourage friendly fire among them. Dameron's [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] wanted to join their commander in protecting the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]] and taking on the three Star Destroyers&mdash;a plan that [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] [[Jessika Pava]] described as "crazy" if done solo&mdash;but General [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] diverted them to another [[Mission to the Outer Rim|mission]].<ref name="Poe28" />

===As plans fall through===
{{Quote|How many ships?''"<br />"''Reading three ''Resurgent''-class Destroyers&hellip;and something else. Something&hellip;big.|General Leia Organa and a [[Unidentified Resistance tactical officer|Resistance officer]]|<ref name="TLJA">[[The Last Jedi Adaptation 1|''The Last Jedi Adaptation'' 1]]</ref>}}
The ''Fellfire'' and another Resurgent were spotted by Resistance member [[Kaydel Ko Connix]] from the base upon their exit from [[hyperspace]] into D'Qar's [[orbit]], and the two were soon joined by the ''Finalizer'', from which Hux led the siege of D'Qar. General Hux delegated the obliteration of both the Resistance's base and [[fleet]] to the final arrival: the [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought]] ''[[Fulminatrix]]''.<ref name="TLJ" /> Aboard the Resistance [[MC85 Star Cruiser|Star Cruiser]], the ''[[Raddus]]'', the makeup of the [[First Order military|First Order forces]] was promptly gathered by the Resistance<ref name="TLJA" /> via [[Comm/scan processor|comm/scan]]<ref name="TLJn" /> and a corresponding [[Hologram|holographic]] display of them was set up in the [[Bridge (starship)|command bridge]].<ref name="TLJA" />

[[File:Mandator_IV-class_Siege_Dreadnought.jpg|thumb|right|360px|The ''Fellfire'' was one of four First Order warships that caught the Resistance amid their evacuation.]]
With the Resurgents flanking the ''Fulminatrix''<ref name="TLJ" /> as they moved into positions closer to the Resistance fleet, Dameron attempted to stall the First Order capital ships by hurtling toward them in his lone [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] and humiliating "General Hugs" over [[comm unit]]. While he failed to involve the ''Fellfire'' and the other Star Destroyers in combat<ref name="TLJn" /> as the pilot had initially planned,<ref name="Poe28" /> he instead took out all the dorsal [[Point-defense laser cannon|cannons]] on the [[dreadnought]], which destroyed the Resistance's base with its [[Orbital autocannon|autocannons]] only after the last of their transports scurried from D'Qar to join the ''Raddus''.<ref name="TLJn" />

As the ''Fulminatrix''{{'s}} [[Cannon|autocannons]] readied to fire upon that [[MC-series star cruiser|ship]], the dreadnought, which was protected by only its own [[TIE series|TIE fighters]], was [[Battle of D'Qar|approached]] by Resistance [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|bombers]] and their starfighter escort.<ref name="TLJ" /> With [[Captain]] [[Moden Canady]] of the ''Fulminatrix'' having told General Hux that fighters needed to be scrambled earlier when Dameron targeted the dreadnought's [[Weapon|guns]],<ref name="TLJn" /> the ''Fellfire'' and its fellow Destroyers eventually launched more TIEs to counter the threat. Although the onslaught of an overwhelming number of TIEs crippled the [[Resistance Starfighter Corps]], the ''Fulminatrix'' was destroyed.<ref name="TLJjn" />

The TIEs pursued the remaining fighters toward the ''Raddus'' and the ''Fellfire'', and the other Destroyers fired at the escaping crafts, but the fleet fled the Ileenium [[Star system|system]] with its [[Star Cruiser|cruiser]] unharmed.<ref name="TLJn" /> The ''Finalizer''<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and another Resurgent from the Battle of D'Qar later joined the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]'' and its [[Supremacy task force|task force]] of Star Destroyers in [[Battle of Oetchi|hounding]] the Resistance fleet<ref name="TLJn" /> through the [[Oetchi system]].<ref name="TLJjn" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Fellfire'' was created for the [[2017]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="TLJ" /> and it was named in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: A Junior Novel|junior novelization]] of the film by [[Michael Kogge]]. The novel does not specify which Star Destroyer in the film is the ''Fellfire''.<ref name="TLJjn" />
