A '''calling glove''',<ref name="Aphra 21">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 21|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 21]]</ref> also known as an '''Ascendant glove''', were a rare [[Ascendant]] artifact wielded by the [[archaeologist]] [[Kho Phon Farrus]]. They allowed the wearer to manipulate objects via magnets in a way resembling Force [[telekinesis]].<ref name="Aphra 17" />
