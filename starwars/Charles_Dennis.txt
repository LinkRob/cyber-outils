'''Charles Dennis''' is a actor who voiced [[Ulic Qel-Droma/Legends|Ulic Qel-Droma]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (video game)]]''. He also voiced [[Davik Kang]] in ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]'' and [[Dol Grenn]] in ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]''.
