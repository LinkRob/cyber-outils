'''Bal-Cobor Sampurn''' was a [[human]] politician who served the [[Sern sector]] as a [[senator]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Sampurn favored the supervision of [[Jedi Order]] activity by the Senate.<ref name="SWTATVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>

Sampurn was known to wear [[Ghorman]] [[Ghorman twill|twill]] hoods and robes woven on an antique [[Beldoni loom]], believed to be fifteen thousand years old.<ref name="SWTATVG" />
