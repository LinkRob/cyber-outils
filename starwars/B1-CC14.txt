'''B1-CC14''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] with [[Gender|masculine programming]] who served the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]].

==Biography==
Toward the end of the [[Clone Wars]], he served on board the ''[[Obrexta III]]'', a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[cruiser]] tasked with carrying the captured [[clone trooper]] [[Kix|CT-6116]] from the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] to the planet [[Serenno]]. En route, the cruiser was [[Attack in an unidentified sector|attacked]] by [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Grand Army of the Republic|forces]] and was heavily damaged. Knowing that the [[Starship|ship]] was in no shape to reach Serenno, B1-CC14 activated the emergency beacon before jumping the [[Starship|ship]] to a random point through [[hyperspace]]. The jump took the cruiser to the [[desert]] planet [[Ponemah Terminal]], where it crashed into the planet's surface and B1-CC14 lost power. Nearly fifty [[Standard year|years]] later, an [[ion storm]] struck the vessel and repowered some of its systems, including the emergency broadcast. A number of [[pirate]] groups were attracted by the broadcast, believing the ''Obrexta III'' to contain the [[Hunt for Dooku's Treasure|legendary lost treasure]] of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. After [[Sidon Ithano's pirate crew|one group]] of pirates managed to get aboard the vessel, B1-CC14 briefly powered back up and activated a failsafe that would mobilize all [[B2-series super battle droid]]s on board to stop the intruders. The [[battle droid]] then deactivated again and was destroyed soon after, when the ''Obrexta III'' exploded.<ref name="TCC" />
