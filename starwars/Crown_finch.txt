A '''crown finch''' was a type of [[bird]]. [[Jedi Knight]] [[Ky Narec]] [[Foodstuff|fed]] crown finches and [[song sparrow]]s, another type of bird, that lived in the [[Jedi Temple training ground|training ground]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="Dooku Assassin Date">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Narec's voice told his former [[Padawan]], [[Asajj Ventress]], that what he missed the most of the Jedi Temple on Coruscant was feeding the birds, which fluttered with their various [[color]]s around the [[Great Tree (Coruscant)|Great Tree]] within the temple.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crown finches were mentioned in a flashback sequence in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin-Download">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-cavan-scott/9780593102817|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> The spelling of crown finches was confirmed by the audio drama's [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script release]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> on [[October 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Penguin-Download" />
