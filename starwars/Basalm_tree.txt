'''Basalm trees''' were a type of spiky [[tree]] featuring [[Color|multihued]] fronds that could be found on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Aricho]].<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|DFS}}</ref> after the [[Shani]] [[Deva Lompop]] and [[Mirialan]] [[Jedi]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] landed on Aricho, Rwoh thought about how the trees reminded her of Lompop's rainbow-colored feathers.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

==Behind the scenes==
Basalm trees appeared in the [[2024]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Defy the Storm]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and [[Justina Ireland]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase III">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> Within the novel, basalm trees' appearance was within a chapter written by Ireland.<ref name="ChapterAuthor">{{Bluesky|tessagratton.bsky.social|post/3lzypgmy75s2y|[[Tessa Gratton]]]|date=2025-09-29|quote=Hi!
