The '''Darvannis system''' was located in the [[Calaron sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] in [[the galaxy]]. The [[Star system|system]] was located in grid square T-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Darvannis system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the article "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Darvannis]] is a planet first introduced in the [[2013]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Rise of the Hutt Cartel]]'', the first digital expansion to ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''.<ref name="RotHC">''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Rise of the Hutt Cartel]]''</ref>
