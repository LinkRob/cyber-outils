'''Convoy's Courage''' was a [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] of the [[New Republic Defense Fleet Starfighter Corps]] that served during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[New Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[New Republic pilot|pilots]] of the squadron were renowned for their flawless flight record in medical and escort operations during the war. The squadron was outfitted with [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s, [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]s, and [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]]s that were painted in the squadron's livery; [[Color|purple]] and gray with yellow stripes.<ref name="Squadrons" />

==Behind the scenes==
Convoy's Courage was one of the multiplayer starfighter cosmetic options in the [[2020]] game ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''.<ref name="Squadrons" /> In an earlier pre-release version of the game, the cosmetic was named Caravan's Courage, a possible nod to [[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]].<ref name="SWExplained">{{YouTube|video=-uoDpbwvecE|text=Star Wars: Squadrons Gameplay Impreesions - Fleet Battles, Dogfights, Customization, and More!|channel=@StarWarsExplained|name=Star Wars Explained}}</ref>
