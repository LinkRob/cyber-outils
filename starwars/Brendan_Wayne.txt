'''Brendan Wayne''' is an actor who served as a body double for [[Din Djarin]] in some of the episodes of the [[The Mandalorian Season One|first]], [[The Mandalorian Season Two|second]], and [[The Mandalorian Season Three|third seasons]] of the [[Disney+]] live-action TV series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''. He is the grandson of famous actor [[John Wayne]].<ref name="Vulture">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vulture.com/2019/12/the-mandalorian-double-brendan-wayne-interview.html|text=Meet the Man Beneath the Mandalorian's Armor|author=Liz Shannon Miller|date=2019-12-10|work=[[Wikipedia:Vulture (website)|''Vulture'']]|archivedate=20260321015634}}</ref> According to fellow ''Mandalorian'' actor [[Emily Swallow]] on [[February 16]], [[2024]], Wayne is the main suit actor for Djarin.<ref name="Swallow interview">{{YouTube|video=F2jmWi-f144|text=THE MANDALORIAN Star Emily Swallow On Her Journey Into The STAR WARS Universe! &#124; Ep 52|channel=BrotherlyLovePod|name=Brotherly Love Pod|date=2024-02-16|archivedate=20240404031633}}</ref>
