The '''Cauldron''' was the wreckage of the [[engine]] of the [[cruiser]] [[Kinro's Blessing]] that had crashed down in [[Lake Amari]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the wreckage formed an island with a small ecosystem and a number of [[scavenger]]s had searched the wreckage, including [[Namda Eaakee]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During 3 ABY,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] visited the island and bought a [[Moldour]] [[clothing|belt]] and a [[Actuating Diffuser Module]] from Namda Eaakee. Vess also took the materials required to apply a "Clear Detail Finish" to an [[EML-850 light freighter]] from a locked room near the wreckage.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Cauldron first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The location contained a paint scheme that players could use to customize the appearance of the [[Trailblazer]]. The location also included the Actuating Diffuser Module which was a keypart to upgrade the player's heavy plasma pistol.<ref name="Outlaws" />
