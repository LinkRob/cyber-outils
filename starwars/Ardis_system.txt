The '''Ardis system''' was located in the [[Unknown Regions]] in grid square G-3 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ardis [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Ardis system/Legends|Ardis system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', a [[2011]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[BioWare]].<ref name="TOR">{{TORcite|type=space|Ardis Outpost Fortification}}</ref> The [[Star system/Legends|system]] was subsequently identified by name in the StarWars.com [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
