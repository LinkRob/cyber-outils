'''Aika Lars''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who lived prior to the [[Clone Wars]]. She was the first [[Marriage|wife]] of the human [[Cliegg Lars]], a [[moisture farmer]] from [[Tatooine]]. The two met on a [[Core Worlds|Core World]], where they fell in love, married, and [[parent]]ed a son, [[Owen Lars|Owen]]. While Owen was still [[Youngling|young]], Aika [[Death|died]], so Cliegg and his son returned to his [[Lars homestead|family's farm]] on Tatooine.<ref name="SWCE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aika Lars was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] companion book to [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]. The book was written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]] and published by [[Dorling Kindersley|DK Publishing]] in [[2002]].<ref name="VD">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> In [[April]] [[2016]], Aika was [[canon]]ized by the ''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]'', a DK reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SWCE" />
