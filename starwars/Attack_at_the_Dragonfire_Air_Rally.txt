An '''attack''' took place during the [[forty-eighth annual Dragonfire Air Rally]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> in around [[58 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> The [[crime lord]] [[Cenevax]], who ran an illegal [[gambling]] ring, made a deal with the [[Serennian]] [[racer]] [[Ramil]] wherein he would purposefully lose so that Cenevax's syndicate would profit from the bets placed on him. When it seemed Ramil was about to go back on the deal and win the race, Cenevax had his [[airspeeder]] sabotaged, and Ramil was severely injured in the crash. The corrupt [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[inspector]] [[Sartori]], who worked for Cenevax, covered up the crime. Ramil's brother, the [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]], who had been watching the race, investigated with his [[Padawan]], [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], tracking Sartori to Cenevax's [[Cenevax's den|den]]. While interrogating Cenevax, Dooku learned that she had a contact on the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] and returned to confront its members, learning Master [[Yula Braylon]] had been blackmailed by the crime lord.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
