The '''attack on Calderos'''<ref name="Leia" /> was carried out in [[3 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> by the rebellion on [[Calderos Station]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[space station]] and deep-space waypoint. It was carried out to conceal rebel shipping to an [[Crait outpost|outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Crait]]. While returning from [[Wobani]] to [[Alderaan]], the ''[[Tantive IV]]'' came across the aftermath of the attack.<ref name="Leia" />
