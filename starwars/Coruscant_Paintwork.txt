The '''Coruscant Paintwork''' was a costume owned by [[Dizzy (Ugnaught)|Dizzy]], the [[Ugnaught]] member of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]], that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Wild Racer|standard costume]]. It featured [[Color|blue]] and silver plating, with a dark gray frame, having glowing purple [[sensor]]s extending from its head, as well as glowing red interiors in the [[barrel]]s of the [[Limb|arm]] [[cannon]]s. The front of the head featured angular blue designs, with similarly shaped slits on the back of the head plating.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Droid-stub}}

Coruscant Paintwork was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 6.0.0 alongside Season 6 on [[April 15]], [[2025]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Coruscant Racer]]. It was made immediately free-to-use at the start of Season 6, unlocked alongside the majority of in-game cosmetics.<ref name="Hunters" />
