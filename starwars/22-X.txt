'''22-X''' was a [[protocol droid]] that negotiated with the [[Trade Federation]], a [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[megacorporation]], and was eventually salvaged by [[3 ABY]] by a [[Teeka's tribe|group]] of [[Jawa]]s on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|She said she'll put a turret anywhere, if you give her a tooth. Forgive the intrusion, but I sensed you needed an interpreter.|22-X to Kay Vess|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
22-X was a [[protocol droid]] that negotiated with the [[Trade Federation]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> a [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[megacorporation]],<ref name="FACPOV">{{InsiderCite|200|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> sometime before [[3 ABY]]. By 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> 22-X's still-functional head had been removed from their body and they had been salvaged by a [[Teeka's tribe|group]] of [[Jawa]]s on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The group included [[Teeka]] and [[Entip]], who traded with locals and offworlders from their [[sandcrawler]] as it moved between various settlements.<ref name="Outlaws" />

[[File:22-X-Body-Outlaws.jpg|thumb|right|250px|22-X's head in a bin next to their new body]]
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] visited the sandcrawer while looking for someone to install a [[turret]] on the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her [[EML-850 light freighter]]. However, negotiations quickly halted due to Vess's inability to speak the [[language]] [[Jawaese]] until 22-X interrupted to [[Translator|translate]], informing her that Teeka would install a [[TU-B4 laser turret|laser turret]] in exchange for a [[tooth]]. After apologizing for interrupting, the [[droid]] went on to clarify that Teeka wanted a tooth from a [[sarlacc]] creature's second [[mouth]]. After Vess agreed to get the tooth, Teeka deactivated 22-X while they were stating how the exchange reminded them of their previous negotiations with the Trade Federation.<ref name="Outlaws" />

By the time Vess returned with the tooth, 22-X had been reactivated and a replacement body had been placed next to them. Before concluding their exchange, Vess complimented the droid's new body, and 22-X thanked her for it and expressed a desire to be reattached. Afterward, Vess gave Teeka the tooth she had acquired and was shocked to see Teeka throw it into a large pile of sarlacc teeth.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|I love watching the wheels of commerce in motion.|22-X|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
22-X had [[color|rose]]-colored plating and yellow [[photoreceptor]]s. The body they were set to be attached to<ref name="Outlaws" /> in 3 ABY<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> had a matching rose color. 22-X loved to watch commerce in action. They spoke [[Galactic Basic Standard]] and could understand Jawaese.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
22-X appeared in the expert quest "The Hotfixer" in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The quest is not required to complete the game's main story nor its expansion but does unlock both the "Hotfixer Expert" upgrade tree and the ability for the player to use and upgrade a ship-mounted turret.<ref name="Outlaws" />
