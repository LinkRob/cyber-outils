The '''Cloud Officer''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Zaina]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Rebel Veteran|standard costume]].<ref name="Hunters" /> It strongly resembled [[Clothing|uniforms]] worn by the [[Bespin Wing Guard]] of [[Cloud City]].<ref name="TESB">{{Film|V}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Cloud Officer was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Zaina, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Bespin Blaster]].<ref name="Hunters" />
