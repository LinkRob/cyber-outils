'''Barrel-shrouds''' were a type of perforated weapon component that attached to the barrel of a weapon and provided air-cooling. The [[Britarro]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Nik Hepho]] carried an [[A310 rifle]] with a [[Color|white]] barrel-shroud while he was on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] in the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A barrel-shroud first appeared as part of a prop in the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which was released in North America on [[December 16]], [[2016]]. It was not identified in the film, but received a name in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released on the same day.
