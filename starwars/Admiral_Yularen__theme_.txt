The track "'''Admiral Yularen'''" was written by [[Kevin Kiner]] for the [[2008]] film [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'']]. The track serves as the [[Wikipedia:Leitmotif|leitmotif]] for [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]]. A part of the theme shares similarities with [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian's motif|Lando Calrissian's motif]] in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. The motif is used in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Holocron Heist]]," as well as the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Through Imperial Eyes]]."
