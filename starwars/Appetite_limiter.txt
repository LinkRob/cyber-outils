'''Appetite limiters''' were surgical implants that presumably kept one from being overly hungry and needing more food. The residents of the poor parts of [[Troithe]], were noted to have these implants by [[Nath Tensent]]'s observation.<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>
