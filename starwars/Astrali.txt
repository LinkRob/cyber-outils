'''Astrali''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Wayseeker" /> located in the [[Astrali system]] within the [[Var Hagen sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square M-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], Astrali housed a [[Temple on Astrali|Jedi temple]] of the [[Jedi Order]], with the [[human]] [[Jedi]] [[Indara]] becoming familiar with its interior<ref name="Wayseeker" /> by around [[160 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{WayseekerDate}}</ref>

Around that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> while Indara&mdash;by then a [[Jedi Knight]]&mdash;was visiting the [[Temple on Hynestia|Jedi temple]] on<ref name="Wayseeker" /> the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Hynestia Prime|Hynestia]] with [[Jedi Master]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]], she looked over the [[Jedi temple|temple's]] interior, which was made of appealing [[Color|pale]] brick and had [[Viewport|windows]] located high on the walls that let in a small amount of light. When Rwoh asked Indara if it was her first visit to Hynestia, the Jedi Knight responded in the affirmative and added that the temple's workmanship reminded her of the temple on Astrali.<ref name="Wayseeker" />

==Behind the scenes==
