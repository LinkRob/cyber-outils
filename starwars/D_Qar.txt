'''D'Qar''' was a verdant [[planet]] located in the [[Ileenium system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. By the [[34 ABY|thirtieth]] [[Standard year|year]] after the formation of the [[New Republic]], the planet was the location of the [[Resistance base|headquarters]] of the [[Resistance]], a private paramilitary force formed by [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] to combat the growing threat of the [[First Order]], a self-proclaimed continuation of the [[Galactic Empire]].

After [[Battle of Starkiller Base|destroying]] the First Order's [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]], the Resistance [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuated]] their base, but were caught by a First Order [[fleet]]. The Resistance escaped into [[hyperspace]] from the resulting battle, but not before taking heavy losses and destroying the First Order [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|dreadnought]] ''[[Fulminatrix]]''. Some [[time]] later, the ''[[Colossus]]'' [[space station]] arrived above D'Qar, hoping to meet with the Resistance to [[Fuel|refuel]], but found only the wreckage from the Resistance's battle with the First Order. While [[Scavenger|salvaging]] the wreckage of the destroyed [[dreadnought]], the First Order tracked the station to D'Qar, and attempted to [[Skirmish over D'Qar|attack]] and destroy it. The ''Colossus'' was refueled using salvaged [[coaxium]] and managed to escape from the system.

==Description==
[[File:DQar_TFABG.png|thumb|left|250px|The planetary ring around D'Qar]]
D'Qar was a verdant<ref name="VE" /> [[planet]] located in the [[Ileenium system]] on the edge of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] and [[Outer Rim Territories]], relatively close to [[Naboo]] and [[Crait]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> An obscure world far from the main space lanes,<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the remote<ref name="SWCL" /> D'Qar was surrounded by a wide [[planetary ring]] that cast a shadow on its [[forest]]ed surface.<ref name="TFA" /> The lush, [[jungle]]-covered terrain did not host any [[Sentience|intelligent]] [[Species|life]], though creatures such as [[bird]]s and [[insect]]s called the huge [[tree]]s and fast-growing roots of the jungle home, as did the [[carnivorous plant]] known as the [[creepervine]].<ref name="TFA Junior Novel" /> Aside from the foliage, D'Qar's surface featured plains and mountains.<ref name="SWCL" /> The world had a comfortable, [[Temperature|temperate]] climate despite its challenging ecosystem and dangerous [[Diet|predators]].<ref name="VE" />

==History==
===Galactic Civil War===
{{Quote|Corona Two, do you also read negative?"<br />"Confirmed. Complete negative on Imperial activity. Unless the Empire's drafting small woodland creatures all of a sudden.|Corona Four and Corona Two|<ref name="Lost Stars" />}}
At one [[time]], D'Qar was home to a [[Unidentified civilization (D'Qar)|civilization]], but the civilization was long gone, leaving behind immense ruins, by later [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]].<ref name="P21">[[Poe Dameron 21|''Poe Dameron'' 21]]</ref>

In [[3 BBY]], the planet served as a base for [[Saw Gerrera]] and his [[Partisans]], who abandoned [[Resistance base|their base]] after discovering their member [[Pluti]] was an [[Information broker|informant]] for the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Ever Been" /> 

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], D'Qar served as a shadow planet and deep space cache for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. D'Qar was included in a map of the [[galaxy]] that listed [[safe world]]s, [[starfighter]] hubs at level five or higher, regional headquarters and operations sectors, and deep space caches. The map was then incorporated into ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

Following the [[Battle of Hoth]], pilots [[Yendor Brethen|Yendor]], [[Thane Kyrell]], and [[Kendy Idele]] of [[Corona Squadron]] were sent to D'Qar to investigate possible signs of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] outposts and activity. This occurred after reports from deep-cover [[Spy|spies]] on [[Coruscant]] revealed that the Empire had been processing massive amounts of material for the [[Imperial Navy|Imperial Starfleet]], as well as circulating rumors of a new large-ship construction facility. Nonetheless, after exhaustive scans in the planet's hemispheres, planetary and solar [[orbit]]s, the [[squadron]] came up empty and returned to its [[MC80 Star Cruiser]] ''[[Liberty]]''.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

The Rebel Alliance then established a small outpost on the planet in the base<ref name="CLNew">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref> once used by the Partisans.<ref name="Ever Been" /> However, after as the war shifted in focus to chasing the retreating [[Imperial Military]]<ref name="TFA VD" /> and as the Galactic Civil War ended with the signing of the [[Galactic Concordance]], a D'Qar base was no longer necessary.<ref name="DB" /> The base was, therefore, only used for a brief time by the Alliance.<ref name="TFA VD" />

===Conflict with the First Order===
[[File:Resistance_group_shot_R2-KT.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The [[Resistance base]] on D'Qar.]]
After ''The Rebel Files'' were [[Excavation on Durkteel|rediscovered]] on [[Durkteel]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> in 34 ABY<ref name="Date">Since ''[[The Rebel Files]]'' were buried on the same [[Standard day|day]] as the [[Battle of Endor]], which according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' takes place in [[4 ABY]], and because ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' establishes that ''The Rebel Files'' were rediscovered 30 [[Standard year|years]] after the battle, it can be determined that ''The Rebel Files'' were uncovered in [[34 ABY]].</ref> and handed to the [[Resistance]], [[Major]] [[Caluan Ematt]] and [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] wrote annotations. Organa wrote a question, asking if the Alliance built up D'Qar during the early rebellion, or if it was later. Ematt rebutted that it was later along with [[Mirrin Prime]], after the [[Battle of Yavin]], and he would know as it was his [[Shrikes]] that scouted both locations.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

In the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="GA" /> the outpost was used as the base of operations for the Resistance, a small military force funded by like-minded [[New Republic]] [[senator]]s who feared the growing power of the [[First Order]], a military junta that aimed to succeed the Galactic Empire.<ref name="TFA" /> Most of the base was hidden underground, with the planet's thick foliage helping to conceal the facility from air and ground [[sensor]]s.<ref name="SWCL" />

After the First Order's [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of the [[Hosnian system]], D'Qar was to be the next target of the First Order's [[superweapon]], [[Starkiller Base]]. However, the Resistance managed to launch an [[Battle of Starkiller Base|attack]] against the weapon, destroying it and its threat to the destruction of the system.<ref name="TFA" /> Shortly afterward, the Resistance [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuated]] their base, but were interrupted when a First Order fleet arrived above D'Qar. Almost immediately after the last [[U-55 orbital loadlifter]] had taken off, the base was annihilated by four blasts from the [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|First Order]] [[Dreadnought]] ''[[Fulminatrix]]''{{'s}} [[orbital autocannon]]s. The ''Fulminatrix'' was later destroyed by the [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|Resistance bomber]] ''[[Cobalt Hammer]]'', and the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]] escaped from the system, leaving the First Order behind.<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

[[File:AQuickSalvageRun-EpisodeGuide.jpg|thumb|left|260px|The ''Colossus'' arrives above D'Qar.]]
Shortly after the end of the battle over the planet, the [[supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]'' arrived at D'Qar hoping to refuel and resupply at the Resistance base, unaware of the base's recent destruction. Locating the abandoned and badly damaged ''Fulminatrix'', [[Kazuda Xiono]] suggesting looting the ship for its [[coaxium]]. Xiono and the [[pirate]] crew known as the [[Warbird gang]] departed for the [[dreadnought]]. The ''Colossus'' was soon [[Skirmish over D'Qar|discovered]] by the First Order, but Xiono and the pirates managed to retrieve the coaxium in time to escape.<ref name="A Quick Salvage Run">{{Resistance|A Quick Salvage Run}}</ref>

==Inhabitants==
At one point, D'Qar had been home to a culture advanced enough to build cities.<ref name="P21" /> By the time of the Galactic Civil War, D'Qar lacked intelligent life, but the planet teemed with a wide variety of species. Large trees with fast-growing roots covered the planet's surface, inhabited by many insects and nocturnal creatures, while flocks of avians dominated the skies,<ref name="SWCL" /> and the [[Diet|carnivorous]] creepervine ensnared creatures to eat among the jungles.<ref name="TFA Junior Novel" /> It was the [[homeworld]] of [[Jacque Porte]].<ref name="Dying Light 10">[[Darth Vader (2017) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 10]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The planet D'Qar was first revealed by the magazine ''Empire'' on [[November 26]], [[2015]].<ref name="Empire Magazine">{{WebCite|url=https://www.empireonline.com/movies/news/exclusive-empire-reveals-new-star-wars-planets|text=Exclusive: Empire reveals names of new Star Wars planets|author=De Semlyen, Phil|date=2015-11-26|work=Empire Magazine|archivedate=20191010031905}}</ref> Its location in the galaxy was revealed in its print issue, using a map created for ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''.<ref name="TFA VD" /> The planet went on to appear in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].
