'''Begamor''' was a [[planet]] of [[the galaxy]] with an irregular orbit around a binary star. The light of the planet's suns appeared to have a green tint upon the surface.<ref name="Silver Dawn" /> The world was once led by a corrupt [[president]], [[Jamie Brasson]]. In [[200 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Mostima]] and her [[Padawan]] [[Sean]] went to [[Mission to Begamor|deal]] with Jamie Brasson. In orbit of the planet were tens of thousands of artificial [[satellite]]s and more than thirty [[space station]]s.<ref name="Silver Dawn" />
