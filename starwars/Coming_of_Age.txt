"'''Coming of Age'''" is an article written by [[Jonathan Wilkins]] that appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 123|''Star Wars Insider'' 123]]. The article is an interview with voice actress [[Ashley Eckstein]] about the changes in [[Ahsoka Tano/Legends|Ahsoka Tano]] in [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|season three]] of [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''The Clone Wars'']], specifically in regards to the [[Mortis/Legends|Mortis]] story arc.
