'''D0E24 electrobinoculars''' were a model of [[electrobinoculars]] manufactured by [[Lydree]]. They were gray in [[color]] and semi-circular in shape, with ribbed hand grips and two lenses. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Sol]] used D0E24 electrobinoculars and kept them in a carry satchel.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
D0E24 electrobinoculars were mentioned in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
