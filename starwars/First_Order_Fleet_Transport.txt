The '''First Order Fleet Transport''' was a vehicle used by [[First Order]] [[troop]]s, [[officer]]s, and personnel to travel quickly to different places aboard massive [[starship]]s. They were [[pilot]]ed by [[astromech droid]]s and programmed with ship schematics and security access codes.<ref name="Inside">{{WebCite|url=https://insidethemagic.net/2017/07/d23-expo-2017-first-order-ride-vehicle-revealed-star-wars-land-attraction-walt-disney-world-disneyland|text=D23 EXPO 2017: First Order ride vehicle revealed for Star Wars Land attraction at Walt Disney World, Disneyland|author=Brigante, Ricky|date=2017-07-13|work=[http://www.insidethemagic.net InsideTheMagic]|archivedate=20201109042906}}</ref>

A [[Resistance]] unit hijacked four Fleet Transports to rescue captured Resistance recruits on board the ''[[Finalizer]]'' by reprogramming the [[R5 series astromech droid|R5 units]] controlling the vehicles. The rescue became complicated when they unexpectedly encountered a pair of [[First Order All Terrain Armored Transport|All Terrain Armored Transports]] and other obstacles, including [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:RiseOfTheResistanceWalkers.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Recruits fleeing past AT-ATs]]
The Fleet Transport serves as the ride vehicle for the ''[[Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance|Rise of the Resistance]]'' at the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] land at [[Disneyland]] and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]]. It was revealed in the form of a model at the 2017 D23 Expo's "A Galaxy of Stories" exhibit,<ref name="Inside" /> but was first seen in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' on the [[Unknown Regions]] map.<ref name="BF2 Unknown Regions">{{YouTube|video=A_MIwt4UDGQ|text=UNKNOWN REGIONS Secrets EXPLORED - Star Wars Battlefront 2 Out of Map|channel=@A7ZATRU|name=AZZATRU}}</ref>

It also appeared on promotional trading cards handed out at the [[December 16]], 2017 "Galactic Nights" event, specifically on the card titled "The rescue doesn't go as planned". The card revealed that one of the rides at the upcoming attraction entailed Resistance members hijacking a Fleet Transport to rescue a captured individual, but ended up caught and surrounded by AT-ATs.<ref name="Card">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge trading cards|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge trading cards]] {{C|Card: [https://www.cinemablend.com/news/1753060/awesome-star-wars-trading-cards-reveal-big-secrets-about-disneys-new-theme-park The rescue doesn't go as planned]}} {{C|[https://web.archive.org/web/20210121001042/https://www.cinemablend.com/news/1753060/awesome-star-wars-trading-cards-reveal-big-secrets-about-disneys-new-theme-park backup link]}}</ref> A full-sized vehicle displayed at Star Wars Celebration 2019 featured safety instructions in [[Aurebesh]] in addition to the standard English script.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|laughing_place|status/1116412215362502656|LaughingPlace.com|quote=The Rise of the Resitance Ride Vehicle #GalaxysEdge|date=2019-04-11|archivedate=20210310084833}}</ref>
