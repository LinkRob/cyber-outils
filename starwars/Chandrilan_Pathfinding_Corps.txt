The '''Chandrilan Pathfinding Corps''' was a [[pathfinding]] organization from the [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]. Members included [[Chief]] [[Pangie]], who taught a pathfinding class for members of the [[Apprentice Legislature]] in [[3 BBY]] which had been organized by [[Queen]] [[Breha Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] so that her daughter, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]], could meet her fellow [[Apprentice Legislator]]s.<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan" />
