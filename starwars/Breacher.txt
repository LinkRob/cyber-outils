The '''breacher''' was a [[missile]] weapon that was in use by the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. Upon impact they exploded into sticky [[acid]] globs that ate through the metals of the [[hull]]s of spacecraft, making them more vulnerable to [[laser]] fire.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref> This made breachers effective even when the missiles were destroyed en route to their targets, because the acid would keep going for a certain range where it could still damage enemy [[starship]]s.<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref>

The use of breachers to weaken an enemy's hull and systems, followed by [[spectrum laser]]s targeting the weakened area of the enemy ship, was called a [[follow-up spread]], and formed the basic "one-two punch" of [[Chiss]] battle tactics.<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref>
