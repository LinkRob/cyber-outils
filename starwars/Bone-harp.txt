A '''bone-harp''' was a type of [[harp]].<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TrialsoftheJediDate|3}}</ref> [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] told [[Nihil]] [[Minister of Information (Nihil)|Minister of Information]] [[Ghirra Starros]] to meet him in the [[Throne hall|throne room]] of his [[flagship]], the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', only to have his [[She'ar]] [[Bodyguard|guard]] [[Faksir]] tell her to instead meet him in the [[starship]]'s cargo decks. Starros considered returning to her [[Starros's chamber|chambers]], but realized she would not, as Ro had answers to her questions, and reflected that he could play her like a bone-harp.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />
