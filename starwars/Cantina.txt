'''Cantinas''', also called '''bars''',<ref name="79s">{{Databank|79-s|79's}}</ref> '''canteens''',<ref name="IT">{{EncyclopediaCite|Imperial Troops}}</ref> '''saloons''',<ref name="Jho">{{Databank|old-jho|Old Jho}}</ref> '''lounges''',<ref name="Mando Chapter 21">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 21: The Pirate}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> '''inns''',<ref name="The Ganguls">{{YJA|The Ganguls}}</ref> or '''taverns''',<ref name="EG">''[[Ezra's Gamble]]''</ref> were [[Alcohol|drinking]] establishments that were frequented by the unsavory elements of society. The [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] contained a [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina|cantina]] which was visited by [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] when they were seeking passage off-world.<ref name="A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref>
