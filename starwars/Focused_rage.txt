'''Focused rage''', also known as '''Physical Force''',<ref name="TSotS" /> was a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[The Force|Force]] [[Force power|ability]] that temporarily increased durability in battle, as well as overall aggression and potency of one's attacks. It was utilized to great effect by [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War]].<ref name="SWFA" /> Ren's grandfather, the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], also made use of focused rage in battle. Deriving power from their inner conflict and anger, both Vader and Ren could draw power from the Force, focusing their rage to magnify their hate and maximize their fighting prowess, bolstering their aggression and durability.<ref name="SWFA" /><ref name="BF2">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Focused rage first was named in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Force Arena]]'', in which the ability takes the form of a card used during gameplay to boost Kylo Ren's stats. In-game, focused rage appears visually as a [[Color|red]] haze surrounding Ren when in use.<ref name="SWFA" />
