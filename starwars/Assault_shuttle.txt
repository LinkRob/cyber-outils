An '''assault shuttle''' was a type of [[shuttle]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> and the [[First Order]]. As [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] observed the [[hangar]] of the ''[[Supremacy]]'', he saw [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[platoon]]s board assault shuttles.<ref name="TLJ Junior novel">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>
