"'''Explosive Developments'''" was a short story written by [[Peter M. Schweighofer]] with illustrations by [[John Paul Lona]]. It was published in the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 3|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 3]] by [[West End Games]] in [[August]] [[1994]]. It is the third in a series of five collaborations by Schweighofer and Lona, and like the others it tells the story of one member of the [[Black Curs]] mercenaries through a combination of written narrative and short, wordless comics. In "Explosive Developments," [[Jai Raventhorn]] tells a story to her teammates, which is presented via flashback.

==Plot summary==
Immediately following the events of "[[Big Quince (short story)|Big Quince]]," the five companions - Jai Raventhorn, [[Tru'eb Cholakk]], [[Platt Okeefe/Legends|Platt Okeefe]], [[Dirk Harkness]], and [[Starter]] - walk from the cantina to an unmarked door at the end of an alley in [[Wroona]]'s starport district. Jai mentions that she, like Tru'eb, once had a run-in with [[Moff/Legends|Moff]] [[Antoll Jellrek|Jellrek]]; in fact, she tried to kill him more than once. They enter the back room of a [[Starship Outfitters|shadowy business]] whose owner, [[Tulagn]], shows them a large crate full of [[Thermal detonator/Legends|thermal detonators]]. Starter takes one and throws it to Jai as a joke. It is deactivated, but Jai nonetheless grabs Starter by the throat and warns him never to do it again. While Dirk haggles over the price of five detonators, Starter and Tru'eb ask Jai why she overreacted. She begins to tell her story.

Some years earlier, Jai, armed to the teeth, sneaks into the [[Repulsorlift/Legends|repulsorlift]] bay of Moff Jellrek's mansion. She is on a personal mission to kill him. She plants two detonators in his [[LAVr QH-7 Chariot/Legends|Chariot command speeder]] and sets their timers for five minutes, the time when the Moff is scheduled to leave for the starport. She exits the speeder, but a trooper has already spotted her. She drops him with her blaster, hops on a [[Speeder bike/Legends|speeder bike]], and zooms out of the bay. Other troopers pursue her. Some board bikes, but others take the command speeder. Jai leads them on a chase through the rocky crags and spires of the surrounding badlands. She keeps the chase going until the timer runs out. The bombs she set take out all of her pursuers but one, and she loses him when he crashes his bike against a rock.

Jai returns, frustrated, to her freighter, ''[[Doomsayer]]''. She has survived, but again she has failed to kill Moff Jellrek. She notices footprints in the dust leading to the hatch, then dusty footprints on the ramp. She draws her blaster and boards. The ship is empty, but Jai sees one more detonator in the cockpit. She rushes outside, where a masked figure, the [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Beylyssa]], leaps from the top of the ship and attacks. Beylyssa knocks Jai's blaster away, then draws her own weapon and destroys it. She immobilizes Jai with a shot to the back and pins her down with a knife to her throat. Jai manages to activate another detonator on Beylyssa's belt and shove her away with a kick to the gut. Jai's face gets cut badly, but she gets away. Back inside the ship, she sees that the detonator in the cockpit had never been armed: it was only a trick. As the detonator outside explodes, Jai takes off, shooting up the canyon with her ship's guns for good measure.

Jai finishes her story, which explains where she got her scar and why she is jumpy around thermal detonators. Platt remarks that she thinks Beylyssa is still alive, or else someone else is copying her disarmed detonator trick. Dirk completes the purchase, and Platt invites all the companions aboard her ship for one more drink.
