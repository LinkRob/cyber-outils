{{Quote|The food on Dandoran is just as fresh and clean as the planet it hails from, as evidenced by this chopped veg salad that's bound to brighten your palate. It's a bit too healthy for the Hutts, but I'll be more than glad to have their portions.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" />}}
'''Dandoran Salad''' was a type of [[Diet|vegetarian]] [[salad]] made up of [[Color|red]] and green chopped [[vegetable]]s. It was made on the [[planet]] [[Dandoran]] in [[Hutt Space]], a region controlled by the [[Hutt]] [[species]]; however, the dish was too healthy for the Hutts to find palateable. The [[Artiodac]] [[Strono Tuggs]] ate a Dandoran Salad while on Dandoran during a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]], including a poriton in Hutt Space booked by the Hutts,<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs included a recipe for the salad in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> He described the [[Foodstuff|dish]] as fresh and clean and claimed it would brighten his readers' palates, stating he would happily eat any portions of the salad that his Hutt hosts did not want.<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dandoran Salad was created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the dish makes use of diced [[Wikipedia:Cucumber|cucumber]], [[Wikipedia:Roma tomato|roma tomatoes]], [[Wikipedia:Scallion|green onions]], chopped fresh [[Wikipedia:Mentha|mint]], chopped [[Wikipedia:Coriander|cilantro]], chopped fresh [[Wikipedia:Parsley|parsley]], [[Wikipedia:Lime (fruit)#Uses|lime juice]], extra virgin [[Wikipedia:Olive oil|olive oil]], [[Wikipedia:Kosher salt|kosher salt]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]], and [[Wikipedia:Sumac|sumac]].<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" />
