A '''Flood droid''' was a large droid with four arms built around a central unit. Following the [[Battle of Hoth]], the [[Aqualish]] landlord [[Furlac]] used one of these droids to block the entrance of the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]]' [[Freemaker Salvage and Repair|garage]] on [[the Wheel]] [[space station]] after they defaulted on their rent payment. He withdrew the droid after [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]] offered to pay up the rent within a day.
