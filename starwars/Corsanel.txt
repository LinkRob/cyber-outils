'''Corsanel''' was a fictious [[planet]] that was listed in [[Oolon's Star Almanac]], a record of thousands of made-up [[star system]]s and their inhabitants written by the [[author]] [[Oolon]] and stored on [[data-tape]]s. Shortly after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="MOE" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Colonel]] [[Halland Goth]] of the [[Emperor's Royal Guard]] and his [[TC-series protocol droid]] [[TC-99]] came to possess the Almanac and read through the entries as an amusing diversion.<ref name="MOE" /> Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''Master of Evil'' begin during the events of [[Darth Vader (2017) 5|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 5]] and then spans to before the events of [[Darth Vader (2017) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 6]], which corresponds to [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the pair found the entry about Corsanel within the first one-thousand-and-one entries, which only covered the first two tapes. A [[Standard week|week]] later, TC-99 told Goth about the entry for the planet [[Senalcor]], but the colonel mistakenly confused the new planet with Corsanel, causing the [[protocol droid]] to chide the [[Imperial officer]].<ref name="MOE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corsanel was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Master of Evil]]'', which was written by [[Adam Christopher]].<ref name="MOE" />
