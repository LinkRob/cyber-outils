A '''Chandi Merle''' was a temple figure of great significance in [[Chandrilan]] culture. The statues were originally a set of twelve that stood in a temple on the [[planet]] [[Chandrila]] around 25,000 [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Imperial Era]], but were [[Sacking of Chandrila|stolen]] by [[Rakatan]] invaders.<ref name="Harvest" /> By [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[One Year Later]]" dates the first three episodes of ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' [[Andor Season 2|Season 2]] to [[4 BBY]].</ref> only eight of the twelve had been recovered and returned to Chandrila.<ref name="Harvest" />

In [[5 BBY]], the curator [[Luthen Rael]] notified the Chandrilan oligarch [[Davo Sculdun]] of a rumor that a Chandi Merle was discoverable, and eventually secured the piece.<ref name="Harvest" /> The following year,<ref name="Date" /> Rael helped Sculdun bring what was the ninth figure back to Chandrila for the [[Wedding of Stekan Sculdun and Leida Mothma|wedding]] of his son [[Stekan Sculdun|Stekan]] and [[Leida Mothma]], intending for it to be a gift to the new couple. Rael helped Sculdun discreetly bring the large figure to the event, keeping it a secret from [[Mon Mothma]], the mother of the bride-to-be. On the third and final day of the wedding, Sculdun unveiled the Chandi Merle.<ref name="Harvest" />

==Behind the scenes==
A Chandi Merle first appeared in the episodes "[[Sagrona Teema]],"<ref name="Sagrona">{{Andor|Sagrona Teema}}</ref> and "[[Harvest]]" of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.<ref name="Harvest" />
