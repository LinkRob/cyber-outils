'''5A-G3''', or '''Fiveay-Jeethree''', was a [[droid]] active during the [[High Republic Era]]. Following the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]]'s [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|fall]] in [[230 BBY]], they worked with the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Qort]] and the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Mikkian]] [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]] to search for the pair's missing friend, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]]. 5A-G3 developed a [[spy]] network&mdash;the [[Eriadu droid intelligence squad]]&mdash;alongside the [[pilot]] [[Argomon]] and [[B-333]] to track [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] movements to find leads in their search. In [[229 BBY]], the droid accompanied Mrala and Qort to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Eriadu]] and later followed them to the Outer Rim [[moon]] [[Bracront]] to [[Mission to Bracront|pursue]] a lead on Talisola's whereabouts.

While on Bracront, the droid enjoyed fighting alongside Qort in a skirmish to storm the [[Tartak Vil's fortress|Nihil fortress]]. 5A-G3 fled with the group&mdash;and Talisola, who had been operating on the world as a [[warlord]] named Tartak Vil. Following the droid's return to Eriadu, she met the former Padawan [[Ruu]], wishing to flirt with him. Afterward, the droid introduced a group of Jedi to B-333 and Argomon before the latter pair left to place a [[Homing beacon|tracking device]] on the Nihil prison ship ''[[Korvix Vorn]]''. When the technology pinpointed the ''Korvix Vorn''{{'s}} location, 5A-G3 participated in an [[Raid on the Korvix Vorn|attack]] on the vessel to rescue any Jedi [[prisoner]]s aboard, while also intending to [[Saboteur|sabotage]] the vessel and destroy it. After helping a boarding party secure the prison ship's topside [[cannon]]s, the droid and Argomon rigged the ''Korvix Vorn'' to explode, fleeing from the vessel with B-333 after joining the rest of their colleagues aboard their ship.

On Eriadu, 5A-G3 helped their colleagues attempt to reclaim the [[Padawan Academic Cruiser]] ''[[Star Hopper]]'' from the Nihil, though the attempt ultimately failed, and also participated in a game with their allies. Afterward, with Eriadu now in the Occlusion Zone, 5A-G3 accompanied their associate [[Sevran Tarkin]] and several of their other colleagues into [[Seswenna Hall]] to address the [[Quintad|Ruling Council]]. When Sevran later distanced herself from the droid and her other colleagues following the Nihil's [[Invasion of Eriadu|invasion]] of Eriadu, 5A-G3 sent spies after the former and assisted with luring Sevran's pet [[veermok]]&mdash;[[Balinta]]&mdash;away from her owner. 5A-G3 and her allies released Balinta and followed her to Sevran's location, detaining several Nihil in the process. Later, 5A-G3 attended Mrala and Talisola's [[Mrala-Talisola Wedding|wedding]] and helped [[Battle of Eriadu|defend]] Eriadu from the invading Nihil as part of the [[Eriaduan resistance]]. The droid fought the Nihil by combating Nihil [[gunboat]]s aboard a [[rescue speeder]], and also by clashing with the marauders while operating a heavily-[[Weapon|armed]] [[maintenance wagon]] alongside the [[cantina]] worker [[Jackaby Snell]]. The Nihil were ultimately defeated on Eriadu and collectively surrendered not long afterward, with 5A-G3 celebrating their victory amongst friends.

==Biography==
===Combating the Nihil===
====Searching for a friend====
{{Quote|A warlord on the remote moon of ''Bracront'' has been transmitting that message for weeks.''[&hellip;]'' The Padawans assigned to monitor chatter while you're on attack runs didn't think it had much promise.|5A-G3 to Zeen Mrala, about one of many open cases they hoped would locate Lula Talisola|<ref name="THRA 1" />}}
[[File:MralaQort5AG3-2023THRA1.png|thumb|left|300px|5A-G3 helped Zeen Mrala and Qort search for Lula Talisola in the aftermath of Starlight Beacon's fall.]]
5A-G3, also known Fiveay-Jeethree, was a [[droid]] active by the [[time]] of the [[High Republic Era]]. Following the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]] of the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]] by [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]<ref name="THRA 1" /> in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date-THRA1">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 1]] dates itself to a [[Standard day|day]] after the [[Standard year|year]] anniversary of the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[space station]]'s fall to [[230 BBY]], the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] must take place in [[229 BBY]].</ref> they<ref name="Twitter1" /> began working with the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Qort]] and the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Mikkian]] [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]].<ref name="FirstDetails">{{SW|url=news/dark-horse-star-wars-the-high-republic-adventures-phase-iii|text=First Details on Dark Horse's ''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'' Phase III}}</ref> 5A-G3 began running [[Standard week|weekly]] reports compiling all currently open cases that might contain clues to the whereabouts of Mrala and Qort's friend&mdash;[[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]]&mdash;who had been declared missing and was presumed [[Death|dead]] in the aftermath of the station's fall.<ref name="THRA 1" /> The droid also made the acquaintance of the pilot [[Argomon]] and the droid [[B-333]], working with the pair to monitor Nihil activity<ref name="THRA 3">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 3|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 3]]</ref> by leading a [[spy]] network<ref name="THRA 4">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4]]</ref> called the [[Eriadu droid intelligence squad]]<ref name="THRA 5" /> to find the Nihil [[prison]] [[Starship|ship]] ''[[Korvix Vorn]]'' in the hopes of some of the [[prisoner]]s aboard being captured Jedi.<ref name="THRA 3">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 3|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 3]]</ref>

As their group worked through and discarded cases that came up without new information, 5A-G3 continued the search for Talisola, occasionally alongside Padawans, who helped monitor incoming intel. The droid continued aggregating relevant cases while Mrala was away on attack runs against the Nihil, assisting in the [[Galactic Republic]]'s fight against the marauders on the borders of the [[Occlusion Zone]], a region of [[Realspace|space]] sealed behind a seemingly impenetrable barrier called the [[Stormwall]] that only Nihil ships could pass through. In one instance, 5A-G3 became aware of a Nihil [[warlord]] operating on<ref name="THRA 1" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Tarabba sector]]</ref> [[moon]] [[Bracront]], who began broadcasting that they had "what the Mikkian wants." However, when the Padawans monitoring Mrala's chatter regarded the intel as useless, the droid similarly put the case aside.<ref name="THRA 1" />

====Arkorayal Cantina====
{{Quote|Did you?! ''Fiveay-Jeethree'', I swear on every single star if you&mdash;''"<br />"''The new faces in the holos tonight?? Zeen, I would ''never.|Zeen Mrala and 5A-G3, about Lula Talisola's figure appearing amongst the presumed dead and confirmed deceased holograms|<ref name="THRA 1" />}}
[[File:Mrala5AG3-2023THRA1.png|thumb|right|340px|Zeen Mrala conversed with 5A-G3 about Lula Talisola's hologram outside the Arkorayal Cabaret Cantina.]]
After one of Mrala's missions<ref name="THRA 1" /> in [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date-THRA1" /> the droid accompanied the pair to<ref name="THRA 1" /> the Outer Rim [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Eriadu]]'s [[Bri-Phrang City]] and waited outside<ref name="THRA 1" /> the [[Arkorayal Cabaret Cantina]], a [[Republic Defense Coalition]] outpost<ref name="THRA 3" /> established to intercept Nihil leaving the Occlusion Zone. While there, Mrala sang a [[Unidentified Corellian funeral shanty|Corellian funeral shanty]] inside the establishment to honor the lives lost during the Starlight Beacon's fall the [[Standard year|year]] before. As Mrala sang, surrounded by the [[hologram]]s of several Jedi killed or flagged as missing during the event. Mrala was stunned upon seeing Talisola's likeness appear as one of the figures for the first time, assuming 5A-G3 had altered the holos.<ref name="THRA 1" />

[[File:MralaQort5AG32-2023THRA1.png|thumb|left|250px|5A-G3 activated their holoprojector, allowing Zeen Mrala to search through open cases concerning Lula Talisola's whereabouts.]]
The Mikkian rushed outside to confront 5A-G3, who was singing to themselves outside. The droid informed Mrala that the hologram's addition to the usual lineup of Jedi figures had not been of their doing, continuing to ramble as their companion's distress increased. However, the [[cantina]] employee<ref name="THRA 1" /> [[Jackaby Snell]]<ref name="THRA 15">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 15|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 15]]</ref> informed Mrala that the addition had been done automatically, as Republic protocol would declare individuals dead if missing and presumed to have passed away for over a year. Outside the cantina, 5A-G3 announced<ref name="THRA 1" /> her<ref name="Twitter1" /> arrival to Mrala and Qort as they conversed, and when they began to speak in the [[Aloxian (language)|Aloxian]] [[language]] to evade the droid, she primly informed them that she also spoke the language.<ref name="THRA 1" />

The droid activated their [[holoprojector]], displaying open cases that might lead to the location of Talisola and the other Jedi missing. Mrala thanked the droid but instructed all the cases to be recalled&mdash;even the previously discarded&mdash;to ensure nothing had been missed. 5A-G3 obliged, and Mrala found numerous scams and trap cases before eventually stumbling upon a file detailing a Nihil warlord from Bracront who had been broadcasting that they had "what the Mikkian wants" for some weeks. When the droid revealed that the Padawans monitoring Mrala's chatter had discarded the intel, the Mikkian was frustrated that she had not already gone and killed the marauder.<ref name="THRA 1" />

====Fortress on Bracront====
{{Dialogue|A Mikkian civilians|If we have your help, we could storm the fortress! Should we do it?|Qort|N&mdash;|Zeen Mrala|Absolutely!|5A-G3|Yes. 100% yes.|attr=A Mikkian, Qort, Zeen Mrala, and 5A-G3, on Bracront|src=<ref name="THRA 1" />}}
[[File:5AG3Bracront-2023THRA1.png|thumb|right|300px|5A-G3 enjoyed engaging in a fortress skirmish on Bracront.]]
5A-G3 briefed Mrala on the Bracront situation and why the Nihil was not the Republic's top priority&mdash;due to the warlord not causing much trouble and being protected by the moon's local populace. Mrala decided to [[Mission to Bracront|travel]] there aboard a ship with Qort and the droid. After arrival, 5A-G3 followed the pair onto Bracront's surface and were met by a crowd<ref name="THRA 1" /> of impoverished and destitute<ref name="THRA 2 PublisherSummary">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-792/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-2|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #2}}</ref> Mikkians, all drawn there by the same broadcast. Mrala was annoyed that all of them had come for the same reason, though the droid pointed out that their group had done the same.<ref name="THRA 1" />

5A-G3 supported Mrala's wish to storm the [[Tartak Vil's fortress|fortress]] where the Nihil warlord was taking shelter despite Qort's protests. The droid eagerly jumped into the fight and gleefully insulted the [[Pau'an]]s they were attacking while wielding their circular blade. Mrala stormed her way to the top of the compound, discovering&mdash;to her shock&mdash;that the moon's Nihil warlord was Talisola, dressed in full marauder [[Clothing|attire]].<ref name="THRA 1" /> Meanwhile, 5A-G3 and Qort prevented the enraged Mikkians from following Mrala, resulting in the droid consuming three [[Limb|fingers]] of one of the crowd members. Afterward, the group fled the world on their ship with Talisola, narrowly escaping the angered civilians chasing them.<ref name="THRA 2">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 2|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 2]]</ref>

====Plans on Eriadu====
{{Quote|This is Bee-Triple-Three. He's been working on a black market hauler for the Nihil, which allows him to get in and out of the Occlusion Zone and keep tabs on things for me.|5A-G3 introducing B-333 to their colleagues|<ref name="THRA 3" />}}
[[File:B333And5AG3-2023THRA3.jpg|thumb|left|200px|5A-G3 introduced B-333 to their colleagues.]]
Following their return to Eriadu's Arkorayal Cabaret Cantina, 5A-G3 met with Talisola and Mrala to inform them that they were due on stage to perform soon with a guest performer.<ref name="THRA 3" /> The droid then conversed with the visiting operative [[Alys Ongwa|Crash Ongwa]], interrupting her conversation with Talisola to inform the operative that her [[bodyguard]], the former Padawan [[Ruu]], was unnerving the establishment's patrons. When 5A-G3 inquired about flirting with Ruu, Ongwa paused and indicated they would have to ask him directly.<ref name="THRA CL">''[[The High Republic Adventures: Crash Landing]]''</ref>

Eventually, 5A-G3 left the cantina and spectated a [[lightsaber duel]] between Talisola and her Master, [[Kantam Sy]]. Afterward, 5A-G3 introduced the Jedi present to B-333 and Argomon, detailing how they could help the Jedi and the Republic gain intel on the ''Korvix Vorn'' when it made a supply run outside the Occlusion Zone by using their [[black market]] [[Argomon's hauler|hauler]] to gain access. The pair then left the Jedi<ref name="THRA 3" /> and followed 5A-G3's instructions to plant a [[Homing beacon|tracking device]] on the Nihil vessel. Argomon then departed the ''Korvix Vorn''<ref name="THRA 4" /> and left B-333 behind as part of 5A-G3's eventual plan to [[Saboteur|sabotage]] the prison ship,<ref name="THRA 5">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 5]]</ref> returning to Eriadu. When the tracking device succeeded in pinpointing the ''Korvix Vorn''{{'s}} location not long afterward<ref name="THRA 4" /> in [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date-THRA4">{{StarlightDate|[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4]]|one [[Standard year|year]] and two weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> Argomon and B-333 informed 5A-G3 of the development.<ref name="THRA 4" />

====''Korvix Vorn'' raid====
{{Quote|Five-ay&hellip;did you?!''"<br />"''I had to go retrieve my asset! I figured I'd leave some gifts behind courtesy of the Eriadu droid intelligence squad, is all.|Zeen Mrala and 5A-G3, after the latter and Argomon exploded the ''Korvix Vorn''|<ref name="THRA 5" />}}
[[File:ArgomonZeen-2023THRA4.jpg|thumb|right|340px|5A-G3 and their colleagues participated in a raid on the ''Korvix Vorn''.]]
Afterward, 5A-G3 attended a meeting hosted by the Republic Defense Coalition consisting of several Jedi and Republic operatives in Eriadu's outpost to discuss how to infiltrate the ship and rescue any Jedi trapped aboard. Once assigned roles for the endeavor, the group disbanded and prepared for the [[Raid on the Korvix Vorn|raid]].<ref name="THRA 4" /> Having assumed the mission objective included destroying the ''Korvix Vorn'' after rescuing its prisoners, 5A-G3 and Argomon worked together to formulate a plan to explode the vessel and reunite with B-333.<ref name="THRA 5" />

The droid, Mrala, Talisola, and Argomon then traveled in a ship to the ''Korvix Vorn''{{'s}} location. After weaving through the surrounding [[Starfighter combat|fighting]] vessels, they landed in the prison ship's [[hangar]] using Argomon's access card. Meanwhile, the operator of the ''Korvix Vorn'', a disgraced Nihil enforcer known as "[[The Warden]]," jettisoned the vessel's prisoners into space and left them to die.<ref name="THRA 4" /> The group joined other Jedi as part of a boarding party and headed toward the ''Korvix Vorn''{{'s}} lower [[cannon]]s but quickly redirected to deactivate the vessel's topside blister bomb armaments to assist the efforts of the Jedi and Republic Defense Coalition forces fighting in space outside. 5A-G3 used their sawblades to slice off the limbs of Nihil in the cannon station, succeeding in securing the area with the rest of their boarding party.<ref name="THRA 5" />

[[File:5AG3ArgomonB333-2023THRA5.png|thumb|left|300px|5A-G3, Argomon, and B-333 after exploding the ''Korvix Vorn'']]
When several Jedi and Mrala began acquiring [[oxygen]] tanks to rescue the prisoners floating in space outside, 5A-G3 and Argomon broke off from the group and promised to meet them back on their ship. 5A-G3 and Argomon collaborated to sabotage the ''Korvix Vorn'' and rig it to detonate, reuniting with B-333 during their efforts. Afterward, they joined Mrala, Talisola, and several others aboard the group's ship. As they flew away, the vessel exploded in [[Fire|flames]], much to the shock of the Jedi and rescued prisoners aboard. 5A-G3 informed those present that they had always intended to destroy the ''Korvix Vorn'', leading everyone to sigh in relief and hug each other. The droid returned to Eriadu with their colleagues and rescued prisoners, grateful to be headed home.<ref name="THRA 5" />

[[File:5AG3SpeederDriving-2023THRA6.jpg|thumb|right|310px|5A-G3 piloted a speeder for their friends as they sought to reclaim the ''Star Hopper''.]]
After returning to Eriadu, 5A-G3 joined several Jedi and Republic colleagues for a game involving scoring pucks into illuminated goals. However,<ref name="THRA 6" /> Jedi Master<ref name="Escape From Valo">''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]''</ref> [[Yoda]] contacted the group via hologram and informed them they needed to evacuate because the Stormwall had [[Expansion of the Stormwall|expanded]] and left Eriadu at the border of Republic space. However, the [[Padawan Academic Cruiser]] ''[[Star Hopper]]''&mdash;which The Warden had [[Commanding officer|commandeered]]&mdash;arrived in Eriadu's [[atmosphere]] for an attack to scare those at the Republic outpost. When Talisola rushed to reclaim the ship, as it had been her and her colleagues' former home, 5A-G3 insisted on not being left behind.<ref name="THRA 6" />

The droid and several others flew toward the ''Star Hopper'' in a flying [[Unidentified maintenance wagon|maintenance wagon]], with 5A-G3 interrupting Talisola and Mrala's flirting to point the most optimal time for them to strike the Academic Cruiser. 5A-G3 continued flying the [[Maintenance wagon|wagon]] underneath the vessel, assuming that her colleagues may need a backup plan if something went awry. When the ''Star Hopper'' fled to hyperspace shortly afterward, 5A-G3 maneuvered the maintenance wagon to catch their colleagues from the sky, as they had fallen off the ''Star Hopper''{{'s}} [[hull]] when it pulled away. Though most of the group was disappointed with their failed attempt to reclaim the vessel, Talisola pointed out that they had succeeded in carrying out psychological warfare by scaring the Nihil away. As 5A-G3 listened, they steered the wagon down to Eriadu's surface to allow its passengers to disembark and begin another round of their game.<ref name="THRA 6" /> At some point afterward, 5A-G3 added considerable weaponry to the maintenance wagon to ready it for its next foray into combat.<ref name="THRA 18">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 18|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 18]]</ref>

In [[Eriadu City]], the group went to [[Seswenna Hall]] with their associate [[Sevran Tarkin]] to address the [[Quintad|Ruling Council]] to speak in favor of the Republic's interests prior to an impending Nihil invasion. 5A-G3 followed Mrala and Tarkin deeper into the building, side-stepping the [[Unidentified security guard (Eriadu)|security guard]] that attempted to stop her and chopping off the end of the guard's blaster. Upon the arrival of Sevran's brother, [[Navaj Tarkin]], 5A-G3 expressed their appreciation of his entrance to Seswenna Hall. The group eventually fled the venue following a group of Nihil's arrival and the marauder organization's subsequent [[Invasion of Eriadu|invasion]] of Eriadu.<ref name="THRA 9" />

Though Ongwa indicated they would need to attack another convoy soon, the droid felt it would be difficult given the strength of the blockade around the city. Not long after, Sevran usurped Tragkul as head of the Tarkin family<ref name="THRA 10">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 10|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 10]]</ref> and distanced herself from her Republic colleagues.<ref name="DOZ 1">[[The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone 1|''The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone'' 1]]</ref>

Ongwa attended the meeting via a hologram displayed from 5A-G3, while the others moved to face each other. While Ongwa unsuccessfully attempted to have Sevran spied on, 5A-G3 continued working with numerous of her own spies throughout Bri-Phrang to gain intel on the situation.<ref name="DOZ 1" />

The droid ultimately indicated that their efforts had not resulted in much usable information, despite the efforts of several spies, and Ongwa reported the same. The group realized that one predictable aspect about Sevran was her attachment to her pet [[veermok]], [[Balinta]]. They then subsequently lured Balinta away to [[Lady]] [[Krevlin Tarkin]]'s [[Krevlin Tarkin's greenhouse|greenhouse]] with [[thrumfrog]]s so that they could later release her, since the creature would always return to its owner's side, thus leading them to Sevran.<ref name="DOZ 1" />

When Mrala inquired how Sevran could have misplaced such a large creature, the droid quipped that she should ask<ref name="DOZ 1" /> the Jedi Knight<ref name="TOTF">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> [[Bell Zettifar]]<ref name="DOZ 1" />&mdash;referring to when he had been separated from his close friend, the [[Wookiee]] Jedi Knight [[Burryaga Agaburry]]&mdash;<ref name="TOTF">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> though the rest of the group glared at 5A-G3 for the comment. ====Catwalk attack====
{{Quote|Fiveay, stay here and keep an eye on these thugs.''"<br />"''Yay!''"<br />"''Do ''not'' dump them in the lava.''"<br />"''Boo.|Qort and 5A-G3, after capturing some Nihil|<ref name="DOZ 1" />}}
[[File:5ARodian-THRADOZ1.jpg|thumb|right|300px|5A-G3 attacked the Nihil she and her colleagues encountered under the Arkorayal Cabaret Cantina.]]
Not long after, 5A-G3 had Sevran followed, though the latter eventually shook off those following her. The droid then called Qort via comms to set Balinta free, which he and the Sevran's cousin [[Emelsine Tarkin]] then did. 5A-G3 followed the pair as they chased the veermok through the streets of Bri-Phrang City, eventually ending up a turbolift leading under the Arkorayal Cabaret Cantina. Several Nihil ambushed the trio in the tunnel they entered, prompting the droid and Qort to attack the marauders and tie them up on a catwalk. 5A-G3 remained with the captured Nihil to watch them, saddened that Qort instructed her not to dump them into some nearby lava.<ref name="DOZ 1" />

[[File:5AG3Qort-THRADOZ1.jpg|thumb|left|300px|5A-G3 and Qort watched Elzar Mann's recorded message via an EX droid.]]
Around this time, the [[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]] sent numerous [[EX communication droid|EX droids]] through the Stormwall, having learned from the Jedi Master [[Porter Engle]] that the [[Standard century|centuries]]-old technology could make it across the barrier. The droids were sent to worlds that Jedi had been known to be operating on from within Nihil territory, one of which was Eriadu. A [[5A-G3's spy|spy]] working for 5A-G3 found an EX droid after seeing it fall from the sky, giving it to the latter. 5A-G3 brought it to Qort and the pair watched the droid play a holographic message of Mann's, asking them to record a status update on their situation and send the EX droid back across the Stormwall. Qort pressed record, explaining the incident with the veermok, the current political state on Eriadu, and 5A-G3's involvement in recent events.<ref name="DOZ 1" />

====Nuptials and Eriadu invaders====
{{Quote|I'll let Sevran know. She won't be happy.''"<br />"''Neither will Farzala. Didn't sound like he has any extra Jedi laying around.|Emelsine Tarkin and 5A-G3 in a strategy meeting about Bri-Phrang's lava reactor core instability|<ref name="THRA 15" />}}
[[File:EriaduDefensesGather-2023THRA14.jpg|thumb|right|300px|5A-G3 was amongst those who gathered to defend Eriadu and listened to Qort's rallying message before they continued their fight.]]
Not long afterward, 5A-G3 attended the [[Mrala-Talisola Wedding|wedding]] of Mrala and Talisola on Eriadu. Prior to its start, the droid was present in the Arkorayal Cantina alongside many of the other guests. During the brief ceremony, as the pair were pronounced spouses, 5A-G3 held their claws up in excitement and activated flames from their [[rocket booster]]. The attendees&mdash;including 5A-G3&mdash;celebrated the couple's nuptials briefly before leaving the cantina to [[Battle of Eriadu|fend off]] the Nihil invading the planet.<ref name="THRA WS">''[[The High Republic Adventures Phase III - The Wedding Spectacular]]''</ref>

In the streets of Bri-Phrang City, 5A-G3 joined their colleagues as part of the [[Eriaduan resistance]] as they engaged in combat between the buildings lining the city's streets. Later, by the seas near the city, the forces defending Eriadu&mdash;which included the droid&mdash;boarded [[rescue speeder]]s and again clashed with the Nihil under the leadership of Tarabal and Qort. Several Nihil operating [[gunboat]]s under "The Warden"'s command were eventually driven back by the group, with 5A-G3 boarding a Nihil gunboat after its capture. After taking over at least one of the gunboats, the Jedi regrouped under Tarabal's command and rallying cry after Qort was injured, intending to continue rebuffing the remaining Nihil on Eriadu.<ref name="THRA 14" />

====Riding into battle====
{{Quote|&hellip;''Jackaby Snell. What in the sweet simmering void are you doing here? This whole place is about to be covered in lava!''"<br />"''Fiveay, have you ever done something so truly horrible and unforgiveable that it changes the very fabric of your reality?''"<br />"''I've been busy recently with the war and all. But I try to make it a regular practice. The tooth-covered scales of the void do demand their own chaotic balance, though, so I try to offset those acts by doing something truly wondrous as well. It feels a bit icky, but we must all make sacrifices.|5A-G3 and Jackaby Snell|<ref name="THRA 18" />}}
Later, 5A-G3 joined several of her colleagues&mdash;including Argomon, Emelsine Tarkin, B-333, and a [[Unidentified Tarkin family staffer|staffer]]&mdash;in a building in Bri-Phrang City. As the fighting raged on, they strategized their next move after being informed that the Jedi Master [[Yaddle]] needed more Jedi assistance to stabilize the [[lava reactor core]] under the city's streets. While Emelsine noted that Sevran would be displeased with the development, 5A-G3 echoed that Tarabal would have a similar reaction.<ref name="THRA 15" />

[[File:5AG3Jackaby-2023THRA18.jpg|thumb|left|300px|5A-G3 and Jackaby Snell destroyed Nihil fighters in a maintenance wagon.]]
Afterward, with attempts to stabilize the core proving unsuccessful, 5A-G3 helped Yaddle evacuate Eriadu's citizens. 5A-G3 finished the task and prepared to leave, but encountered Snell and grabbed<ref name="THRA 18" /> him<ref name="THRA 15" /> by the back of his cloak. Snell admitted to 5A-G3 that he had done something awful, prompting the droid to expound on her own chaotic past choices. As 5A-G3 spoke, Snell slipped away, intending to sacrifice his life in an attempt to atone for having spied for the Nihil. 5A-G3 caught up with Snell and boarded his maintenance wagon, berating him for his intent to end his life and noting that she had noticed he was likely to do something reckless. 5A-G3 then activated the maintenance wagon's newly installed weaponry, shocking Snell.<ref name="THRA 18" />

In the wagon, 5A-G3 and Snell destroyed three Nihil fighters, angering the Warden, with the latter thanking the droid for saving his life. As they flew through the sky, Jackaby Snell's grandmother&mdash;Grandma [[Snell]]&mdash;landed on their maintenance wagon, having fought the Warden's underlings and escaped after jumping off the ''Star Hopper''.<ref name="THRA 18" /> Not long after, the Eriaduan resistance destroyed the ''Star Hopper'' with the Warden aboard,<ref name="THRA 19">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 19|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 19]]</ref> defeating the Nihil on Eriadu. The Nihil collectively surrendered to the Republic not long afterward.<ref name="Trials">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref> On Eriadu, 5A-G3 and their colleagues celebrated their victory, while also taking time to reminisce on the allies who had lost their lives during the Nihil conflict.<ref name="THRA 20">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 20|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 20]]</ref>

==Characteristics==
===Personality===
{{Quote|Your creepy bodyguard is unnerving the customers with his perplexing eyes and chaotic smile.''"<br />"''Okay, and?''"<br />"''Permission to flirt with him?|5A-G3 and Crash Ongwa|<ref name="THRA CL" />}}
Though 5A-G3 cared about Mrala and Qort, she considered all organic beings to be temporary "organ flesh sacks" who would someday meet their end in the void.<ref name="THRA 1" /> They enjoyed the company of many of their other colleagues,<ref name="THRA 3" /> including B-333, whom the droid took care to rescue from the ''Korvix Vorn'' with Argomon.<ref name="THRA 5" /> By the time of the [[Nihil conflict]], the droid was loyal to the Jedi and the Republic and worked actively with them against the Nihil.<ref name="THRA 4" /> However, upon inquiry, 5A-G3 affiliated herself more strongly with "chaos" than the Republic.<ref name="THRA 9" /> Despite challenging conditions she often operated under, the droid took pride in maintaining a competent network of spies that provided useful information.<ref name="DOZ 1" />

[[File:5AG3ZeenLula-2023THRA3.png|thumb|right|300px|5A-G3 enjoyed teasing Zeen Mrala about her connection to Lula Talisola.]]
She enjoyed thinking about the concept of death, considering it an "infinite finale" and a "caress of the beyond." When given the option, the droid enjoyed engaging in<ref name="THRA 1" /> and threatening violence to others.<ref name="DOZ 1" /> 5A-G3 felt that while she sometimes did acts that should be deemed unforgiveable actions to be committed on occasion, she made an effort to do good as well to even out her debt to "the void."<ref name="THRA 18" /> The droid became known among their colleagues as a "multi-hyphenate chaotic droid."<ref name="THRA 4" /> 5A-G3 liked Ruu when he visited Eriadu and wished to flirt with him, finding the former Padawan's swirling black [[eye]]s mesmerizing.<ref name="THRA CL" /> Navaj Tarkin also caught 5A-G3's attention, commenting on him to her colleagues.<ref name="THRA 9" /> She enjoyed teasing Mrala and Talisola about their feelings for one another<ref name="THRA 3" /> and also expressed pride in the work they did to help others, often pointing out the impact their assistance had.<ref name="THRA 4" />

The droid was unwilling to let those she cared about go into danger alone, both to protect them and to not miss out on the opportunity for potential chaos and violence which they greatly enjoyed.<ref name="THRA 6" /> She held particular distaste for the Nihil and did not care if they died in painful ways, such as pushing the marauders into lava.<ref name="DOZ 1" /> 5A-G3 was willing to operate outside of official mission parameters, opting to strike blows against the Nihil and rationalize them as assuming that their endeavors had been the endeavor's intent.<ref name="THRA 5" /> Despite Snell having spied for the Nihil, 5A-G3 still cared about his wellbeing, willing to save him and aid his attempt to atone for his actions.<ref name="THRA 18" />

===Physical traits===
{{Quote|I speak Aloxian, I'll have you know. And yes, my timing is impeccable, thank you. Always. Anyway, I thought it might cheer Master Zeen up to go through the list of open cases I aggregate weekly that may hold some clue as to the whereabouts of Padawan Talisola and the others.''"<br />"''Thank you! But don't stop there. Pull up ''all'' the cases we've discarded since we began the search. We might've missed something.|5A-G3 and Zeen Mrala|<ref name="THRA 1" />}}
[[File:5AG3-2023THRA5.png|thumb|left|290px|5A-G3 attacking Nihil with her sawblades]]
5A-G3 had orange [[sensor]]s, silver-and-blue plating, and a rectangular base. The droid also possessed two jointed limbs ending in claws and one curved smaller limb. She was equipped with a holoprojector that could project both maps<ref name="THRA 1" /> and holograms of individuals,<ref name="DOZ 1" /> as well as a rocket booster.<ref name="THRA WS" /> She had two silver-colored circular, spinning sawblades as [[weapon]]s that extended from her clawed limbs, each bearing several sharp ridges. These limbs were sharp enough to slice through both organic limbs<ref name="THRA 5" /> and [[metal]] weapon barrels.<ref name="THRA 9" /> 5A-G3's head was extendable to at least double her normal height<ref name="THRA 6" /> and the droid's programming allowed them to speak at least two languages, Aloxian and [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]].<ref name="THRA 1" />

They were capable of aggregating inputs of data case files<ref name="THRA 1" /> and piloting a maintenance wagon.<ref name="THRA 6" /> 5A-G3 successfully operated a network to track Nihil activity,<ref name="THRA 4" /> which consisted of numerous spies carrying out high-level duties.<ref name="DOZ 1" /> As part of their work, they proved capable of sabotaging vessels such as the ''Korvix Vorn'', resulting in its destruction, and engaging in close-quarters combat.<ref name="THRA 5" /> On one occasion, 5A-G3 installed extensive weaponry onto a maintenance wagon, which she later used to combat Nihil invaders on Eriadu.<ref name="THRA 18" /> Aside from their technical skills, 5A-G3 considered themselves an entertainment and gastronomical droid.<ref name="THRA CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Milestones===
{{Quote|5A-G3, the chaotic she/they droid who ponders their place in the vast cosmos, is joining my best girl zeen mrala in phase iii of THRA|Sage Groves, whom 5A-G3's creation was inspired by|<ref name="Twitter1" />}}
[[File:TheHighRepublicAdventures2023-1-cover.jpg|thumb|right|210px|5A-G3 debuted in the first issue of the 2023 ''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'' comic series.]]
5A-G3 was a character created for the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']], released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's Phase III.<ref name="FirstDetails" /> They first appeared in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 1|first issue]] of the series, which was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Harvey Tolibao]] and [[Nick Brokenshire]], and released by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="THRA 1" /> on [[December 6]], 2023.<ref name="FirstDetails" /> 5A-G3 was first pictured in a [[StarWars.com]] [https://web.archive.org/web/20230721210609/https://www.starwars.com/news/sdcc-2023-lucasfilm-publishing-panel post] documenting the [[Lucasfilm]] panel's [[Wikipedia:San Diego Comic-Con|San Diego Comic-Con]] reveal of the issue's cover artwork on [[July 21]] of the same year.<ref name="Publishing Panel">{{SW|url=news/sdcc-2023-lucasfilm-publishing-panel|text=SDCC 2023: 10 Reveals from the Lucasfilm Publishing Panel}}</ref> The publisher's summary for the issue mistakenly referred to the droid as "5AG3."<ref name="THRA 1 PublisherSummary">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-790/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #1}}</ref>

5A-G3 was first identified by name in a StarWars.com post discussing details about the then-upcoming ''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'' series on [[September 18]], 2023.<ref name="FirstDetails" /> The droid also appeared in the [[The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone 1|first issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone]]'' comic miniseries, which was written by Older, illustrated by [[Paris Alleyne]], and released by Dark Horse<ref name="DOZ 1" /> on [[October 9]], [[2024]] as part of ''The High Republic''{{'s}} Phase III.<ref name="DOZ 1 DH">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3016-086/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III--Dispatches-from-the-Occlusion-Zone-1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III--Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone #1}}</ref> The day before the issue's release, Alleyne released several uncolored pages of the comic on social media, including several sketched panels featuring 5A-G3.<ref name="ParisAlleyne">{{Instagram|username=parisalleyne|url=p/DA3fq15P_h-|name=[[Paris Alleyne]]|date=2024-10-12|quote=STAR WARS: The High Republic (Phase III) Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone #1 is out tomorrow! I never thought I'd be drawing Star Wars and I'm having an amazing time diving into this world!|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/BkktW|archivefile=File:ParisAlleyneDOZ-Instagram.png}}</ref>

The [[August 19]], [[2025]]<ref name="Abrams">{{Abrams|url=product/art-of-star-wars-the-high-republic-volume-ii_9781419771422|text=The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic (Volume II)}}</ref> reference book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]'', written by [[Kristin Baver]], included two of Tolibao's unfinished panels from<ref name="Artbook">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref> the one-shot comic ''[[The High Republic Adventures Phase III - The Wedding Spectacular]]''<ref name="THRA WS" /> that included 5A-G3.<ref name="Artbook" /> ''The Wedding Spectacular'' was written by Older and released by Dark Horse<ref name="THRA WS" /> on [[December 18]], 2024.<ref name="Dark Horse-Wedding">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-812/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III--The-Wedding-Spectacular|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III--The Wedding Spectacular}}</ref> The art book also included three of Tolibao's concept sketches of 5A-G3, one of which was in sketch form. The other two pieces of artwork showed the droid with a green and a red sensor, respectively, and showed 5A-G3 with different hinges and overall body shape<ref name="Artbook" /> than her final design.<ref name="THRA 1" /> The first piece of sketched concept artwork featured in ''The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume I'' was revealed prior to the book's release via social media on [[August 15]] of the same year.<ref name="WeddingPuppet">{{Instagram|username=kristinbaver|url=stories/kristinbaver/3698578522957753629|name=Kristin Baver|date=2025-08-15|quote=look my little angel 5A-G3 is featured in the new high republic art book by @kristinbaver|archivefile=File:5AG3GrovesBaver-Instagram.png}} {{C|Story originally by [[Sage Groves]] and reposted by Kristin Baver; story now inaccessible}}</ref> In the reference book, Older reflected on 5A-G3's tendency to sit and wonder what it would be like to become one with the galaxy, while also operating a spy network and working at a cabaret.<ref name="Artbook" />
