The '''Csap family''' was a [[Chiss family]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. It received little respect in the [[Chiss Syndicure|Syndicure]] being "mostly ignored or used as bargaining levers" and used as the "butt of a lot of jokes,".<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>

During the [[Nikardun campaigns]], the Csap family was negotiating with the [[Ruling Families|Ruling]] [[Mitth family]] to work together on a construction project in the [[Dioya system]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

The Dioya system was the site of an [[Attack on the edge of the Dioya system|attack]] which ultimately led the Chiss to discover the threat posed by the [[Nikardun Destiny]]. Mitth family [[Syndic Prime]] [[Mitth'urf'ianico]], who was waging a campaign to sabotage the career of [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], hoped to convince the Csap of Thrawn's "reckless adventurism" during his negotiations with the Csap.<ref name="Greater Good" />

[[Csap'ro'strob]] was a member of the Csap family who served as [[Mid Captain]] of [[Irizi'in'daro]]'s ''[[Grayshrike]]''.<ref name="Greater Good" />
