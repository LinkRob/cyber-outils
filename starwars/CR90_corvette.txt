The '''CR90 Corvette''', also identified as the '''CR90 class''' and colloquially known as the '''Corellian corvette''' or '''blockade runner''', were consular [[Corvette|vessels]] manufactured by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]. While the CR90 would see initial use within the late [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Imperial Senate]], many vessels would be appropriated by the early rebellion and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] against the [[Galactic Empire|First Galactic Empire]], despite not being designed as a combat-oriented vessel. They were later used by the navies of both the [[New Republic]] and the [[Resistance]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:Tantive_IV_blueprints.png|thumb|left|250px|Blueprints of the ''Tantive IV'', a CR90 corvette]]
Designed by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> as a diplomatic ship, it would see use within the annals of the late Galactic Republic, Imperial Senate, and later as a combat craft in both the early rebellion and Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Databank" /> Measuring 150 [[meter]]s in length,<ref name="Rebel Files" /> it featured two dual and four single [[turbolaser]] turrets, and had the ability to reach a maximum speed of 950 [[Kilometer per hour|kph]]. According to [[Luke Skywalker]], some combat-outfitted CR90 ships could be equipped with six dual turbolasers. Capable of carrying an entire diplomatic escort, it had a passenger capacity in the hundreds.<ref name="Heir to the Jedi" /> Although the ships were not built as [[carrier]]s, three [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]s could be attached to the [[docking ring]]s, allowing for a small escort.<ref name="Wings of the Master" /> They would serve as the backbone of the early rebellion and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Alliance]].<ref name="Ships of the Galaxy" /> Though not particularly heavily armed, CR90s possessed enough forward firepower to destroy a [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] quite easily.<ref name="Iron Squadron" />

==History==
===Clone Wars===
The CR90 line was in service during the [[Clone Wars]]. [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] owned a CR90 corvette called the ''[[Tantive IV]]'', which he used to travel to [[Toydaria]] to negotiate a relief effort for the [[Battle of Ryloth|blockade]] on [[Ryloth]].<ref name="Supply Lines">{{TCW|Supply Lines}}</ref>

===Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire===
After the [[Proclamation of the New Order|fall]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] and the rise of the [[Galactic Empire]], the CR90 continued to see service within the Empire, with several such cruisers parked at the [[R/M Facility Four deepdock]] at [[Belderone]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> Additionally, the corvette would continue to be used by Bail Organa during his tenure in the [[Imperial Senate]].<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

Two CR90 Corvettes were part of the [[Botajef Defense Force]], which was mobilized against the Empire when [[Governor]] [[Quesl]] declared the secession of [[Botajef]] from the Empire. [[Commodore]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] was able to put a stop to them with minimal casualties, capturing Quesl aboard his corvette using a [[tractor beam]].<ref name="Thrawn" />

Bail Organa traveled in the ''Tantive IV'' when he [[Mission to steal Imperial weapons|recovered]] [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]] from the [[Spectres|rebels]] who operated on [[Lothal]].<ref name="Droids in Distress">{{Rebels|Droids in Distress}}</ref> Organa's rebel movement also used a number of CR90s. [[Ahsoka Tano]], under the codename "Fulcrum," traveled in [[Ahsoka Tano's corvette|one]] when the rebels handed the [[Rodian]] informant [[Tseebo]] over to her.<ref name="Gathering Forces">{{Rebels|Gathering Forces}}</ref>

===Unifying the Rebellion===
[[File:Ibaar.png|thumb|left|250px|The ''Liberator'' and Transport One approach the blockade over Ibaar]]
[[File:Corvette negvv.png|thumb]]
Five CR90 corvettes served as part of the [[Phoenix Cell]] under the command of [[Jun Sato]], in addition to the flagship, a ''Pelta''-class frigate called the ''[[Phoenix Home]]''.<ref name="Siege of Lothal" /> Three of them were used to assist the [[Action at Mustafar|rescue]] of Lothal rebel leader [[Kanan Jarrus]].<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref> After the loss of the flagship in the [[Siege of Lothal]], Commander Sato took one of the corvettes, ''[[Liberator (CR90 corvette)|Liberator]]'', as his new flagship.<ref name="The Lost Commanders">{{Rebels|The Lost Commanders}}</ref> The ''Liberator'' and another corvette, under the callsign [[Transport One]], were used in an [[Blockade of Ibaar|attempt]] to bring relief supplies to the starving people of [[Ibaar]]. Transport One was destroyed in the first to run the Imperial blockade. It was later replaced by one of the other corvettes for the second attempt, which was successful thanks to the [[Prototype B6]].<ref name="Wings of the Master" /> When Phoenix Cell was discovered to have been hiding on [[Garel]], [[Lothal sector fleet|forces]] led by [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]] led an [[Battle of Garel|assault]] on them. As the ''Liberator'' tried to escape, it was captured by Konstantine's [[tractor beam]]. However, the tractor beam was disabled by the ''[[Ghost]]'', which freed the ''Liberator'' and escaped.<ref name="Legacy">{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref>

After [[Mon Mothma]] [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|declared]] the formation of the Rebel Alliance, several CR90s traveled with many more rebel ships to her location at [[Dantooine]].<ref name="Secret Cargo">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref> Shortly after, [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]] took three CR90s with his fleet to meet Phoenix Cell, and their three CR90s, at their [[Chopper Base|base]] on [[Atollon]], in preparation to attack the [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter|TIE Defender]] project on Lothal. However, [[Grand Admiral]] Thrawn's [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|forces]] [[Battle of Atollon|ambushed]] the fleet of rebel ships. In the ensuing battle, three of the six CR90s were destroyed. The final three fled with the remaining rebels back to Phoenix Cell's base. When Thrawn's ground forces overran the base, the ''Liberator'' and one other CR90 escaped and attempted to run Thrawn's blockade. The ''Liberator'' escaped, but the other corvette was destroyed.<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

===Galactic Civil War===
{{Quote|We're still flying under a diplomatic flag. The Empire would not dare board us.''"<br />"''This just came in. The Empire has issued a priority red directive. Any ships matching the description of a CR90 corvette are to be stopped and held. Priority red means all previous orders and duties are immediately superseded for all Imperial ships galaxy-wide. I've never seen this much comm traffic&mdash;the Empire's flooding every frequency with it. Whatever Rogue One beamed us from Scarif, they really want it back.|[[Corla Metonae]] and a [[Unidentified Tantive IV bosun|bosun]] aboard the ''Tantive IV''|<ref name="Raymus">{{StoryCite|story=Raymus|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>}}
[[File:CandorEscapes-TheBridge.png|thumb|right|300px|The CR90 corvette ''Candor'' escapes an Imperial Star Destroyer.]]
Between circa [[2 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the CR90 corvette ''[[Candor]]'' was active within the rebel fleet as an arms smuggling vessel under the command of Captain [[Jardar]], who would be killed in an [[Attack on the Candor|Imperial attack]]. Suddenly the highest ranking officer onboard, [[Minister]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] assumed command of the ''Candor'' and ensured it escaped, earning her a formal promotion into the CR90's new captain.<ref name="The Bridge">{{StoryCite|story=The Bridge|book=Age of Resistance Special 1}}</ref> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas Updated">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> after the Alliance to Restore the Republic [[Battle of Scarif|stole]] the [[Death Star plans|plans]] to the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] traveled aboard the ''Tantive IV'' to bring the plans to Alderaan. However, her ship was [[Secret mission to Tatooine|intercepted]] by the ''[[Devastator]]'' over [[Tatooine]]. The ''Tantive IV'' attempted to escape, but a shot from the [[Star Destroyer]] crippled its [[engine]]s, leaving it vulnerable to boarding.<ref name="Episode IV" />

During the [[Mid Rim Retreat]], the Rebel's [[61st Mobile Infantry|Twilight Company]] utilized the CR90 ''[[Thunderstrike]]'' as a transport before its [[Siege of Inyusu Tor|demise]] on [[Sullust]].<ref name="Twilight" /> At one point during the war, the Rebels used the corvette ''[[Jaina's Light]]''.<ref name="SWZ">{{FFGXW2|set=Huge Ship Conversion Kit|cardname=''Jaina's Light''}}</ref> Numerous CR90s joined the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] near Sullust in preparation for an assault on the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. The fleet arrived at the [[Endor system]] to find it was a trap.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> In the ensuing [[Battle of Endor|battle]], several CR90s were destroyed.<ref name="Levers of Power">{{StoryCite|story=The Levers of Power|book=The Rise of the Empire}}</ref> Many CR90 corvettes participated in the [[Battle of Jakku]]. Captain [[Lindsey]]'s [[Lindsey's CR90 corvette|corvette]] was heavily damaged and crashed onto the surface of [[Jakku]].<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> Several other corvettes joined it in what would become known as the [[Starship Graveyard]].<ref name="Battlefront" />

===New Republic and beyond===
[[File:New_Republic_corvette.png|thumb|left|250px|A CR90 in service to the New Republic]]
The CR90 corvette ''[[Nechrim's Wake]]'' served as part of the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] under [[Captain]] [[Gauvis]].<ref name="SW 2025 3">[[Star Wars (2025) 3|''Star Wars'' (2025) 3]]</ref> Around [[6 ABY]],<ref name="6ABYDate">{{StarWars2025Date|3}}<</ref> the vessel crashed on [[Loradil]] on the edge of the [[Fenril sector]], where the surviving crew encountered a group of [[B1-series battle droid]]s.<ref name="SW 2025 3" /> Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> a CR90 corvette was a part of a squadron of [[New Republic]] [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] and [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing fighters]] called in by [[Kh'ymm]] to help the [[Skeleton Crew]] defeat [[Silvo's pirates]] during the [[Invasion of At Attin]].<ref name="The Real Good Guys">{{SkeletonCrew|The Real Good Guys}}</ref>

One [[Unidentified CR90 corvette (Resistance)|CR90]] that had served in the Galactic Civil War from the Battle of Scarif to the Battle of Jakku was used by the Resistance.<ref name="The Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref> Many years after the end of the [[Galactic Civil War]], a former Imperial [[senator]] discovered the remains of the ''Tantive IV'' lying decrepit in a hangar in the [[Yarma system]]. Sympathetic to Leia's cause, the senator repaired the former consular ship and returned it to her as a gift. Leia used the vessel as her personal flagship from the [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|Resistance base]] on [[Ajan Kloss]]. Given the lack of barracks on the moon, most of the Resistance support crew lived and slept aboard the ''Tantive IV'' when not on duty; it was also used as a generator for the Resistance base.<ref name="Episode IX Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

[[File:CR90Corvette-TheRecruitPart1.jpg|thumb|right|250px|A CR90 in service to the Resistance]]
In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="TROS Date">{{FilmDate|IX}}</ref> multiple CR90 corvettes including the ''Tantive IV'' were part of a [[Citizens' Fleet|fleet]] assembled by [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] to assist the Resistance during the [[Battle of Exegol|battle]] against the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]] over the [[planet]] [[Exegol]]. A few corvettes, including the ''Tantive IV'', were destroyed in the battle. Nonetheless, the engagement ultimately ended in triumph for the Resistance and the Citizens' Fleet; the Sith Eternal and Sidious himself were destroyed, which itself led to a [[uprising against the First Order]] across the galaxy.<ref name="Episode IX" />
