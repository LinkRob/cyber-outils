'''Caldra Tertius''' was a location<ref name="FACPOV" /> in the [[Caldra Tertius system]] in the [[Ado sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was situated near [[Caldra Prime]] and a number of trade [[Planet|worlds]]. A group of [[pirate]]s blazed a [[Hyperspace route|hyperspace lane]] from the [[Vosch Cluster]] to the trade worlds around the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Hoth]]<ref name="FACPOV" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Seventh Division|Part]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] grouped at a [[Unidentified rendezvous point|rendezvous point]] that laid between the Vosch Cluster and the trade worlds around Caldra Prime and Caldra Tertius was threatened by the presence of the pirates.<ref name="FACPOV" />
