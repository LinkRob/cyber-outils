'''Bedat''' was an individual known to the group of [[Dark side of the Force|dark-side]] [[Force-sensitive|practitioners]] known as the [[Knights of Ren]]. After the group was abandoned by their [[Ren|leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]<ref name="Legacy of Vader 8" /> at some point no earlier than [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the Knights came into contact with Bedat.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 8" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Bedat had locked the Knights in a cell, but was [[Death|killed]] by the [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Tava Ren]], who became the Knights new leader.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 9" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> after the Knights ditched Tava during a [[Raid on Vandor|raid]], she asked them why they had become the type to run in hot situations. After [[Ap'lek]] told her it was because of Kylo Ren, Tava questioned who that was. In response, [[Cardo]] told her that he was their former leader before her time and also before Bedat, leading Tava to assume Bedat had killed Kylo before she then killed Bedat himself. Cardo noted to Tava that was not exactly the case, before informing her of their time under Kylo and how it ended.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 8" />
