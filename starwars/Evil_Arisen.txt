"'''Evil Arisen'''" is a [[roleplaying game]] [[adventure]] seed for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition]]'' from [[West End Games]]. The scenario was written by [[Peter M. Schweighofer]] for the book ''[[Tales of the Jedi Companion]]'', published in [[1996]].

The scenario sends a group of [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knights]] to investigate the activities of a group of offworlders who recently arrived on the planet [[Muzara]] and questioned the inhabitants&mdash;native [[Muza]] and [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Brentaal League]] settlers&mdash; about the ruins of [[Kabus-Dabeh]]. The Jedi follow the strangers into the ruins, where they track them into a [[Kabus-Dabeh well|well]] and the [[Kabus-Dabeh underground cavern|cavern]] into which it feeds. They discover that the strangers are [[Dark Jedi/Legends|Dark Jedi]] and followers of the ways of the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] and stop them before they can retrieve a [[Unidentified Sith artifact|Sith artifact]] and bring evil to the [[Muzara system/Legends|Muzara system]].
