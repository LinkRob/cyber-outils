The '''''Cosinga''-class Heavy Corvette''' was a class of [[corvette]] that was used as a [[Yacht|Luxury Star Yacht]] for [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. The ''[[Imperialis]]''<ref name="BMF 63" /> and its [[Imperialis (Jakku)|replica]] at the [[Jakku Observatory]] were ships of this class.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

{{Ship-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
''Cosinga''-class Heavy Corvette was first identified in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 63|sixty-third issue]] of the [[De Agostini]] magazine ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'', published in [[2015]].<ref name="BMF 63" /> Part of the corvette's class name is shared with the first name of [[Cosinga Palpatine]], [[Parent/Legends|father]] of [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] introduced in the [[2012]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel [[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']], written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Darth Plagueis">[[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']]</ref>
