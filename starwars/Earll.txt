'''Earll''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served in the [[Imperial Military|military]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. 

== Biography ==
At some point, Earll joined the [[Galactic Empire]]. By the fifteenth [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], she held the rank of [[commander]] and was in charge of a [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] and its [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] escort. Around that time, Earll responded to a distress call sent by [[Nadea Tural]] of the [[Imperial Senate]], but it transpired that the [[senator]] had already been rescued by a group of anti-Empire [[Spectres|insurgents]] from [[Lothal]]. The ''Gozanti'' cruiser engaged the rebels' ship, the ''[[Ghost]]'', and after disabling it, Earll boarded the ship accompanied by a squad of [[stormtrooper]]s. They captured the crew of the ''Ghost'' and rescued Tural. Earll ordered the immediate execution of its crew to which Senator Tural, who had demanded a fair trial for the rebels. Tural then interposed herself when Earll's troopers were about to fire, allowing the [[Spectres]] to get the upper hand. The Imperial commander herself was shot and [[stun]]ned by [[Hera Syndulla]], the [[captain]] of the ''Ghost''. The Spectres and Tural then escaped aboard the ''Ghost''.<ref name="SP" /> 

==Personality and traits==
Earll was a tall, thin woman with pale sallow skin, ice blue eyes, and short black hair. She was ruthless and unsympathetic, willing to execute the ''Ghost'' crew despite the protests of Senator Tural. She appeared more competent than most Imperials encountered by the ''Ghost'' crew, personally subduing Sabine Wren and Ezra Bridger while her squad successfully captured the others. Despite succeeding in capturing them, she failed to execute them nonetheless.<ref name="SP" />
