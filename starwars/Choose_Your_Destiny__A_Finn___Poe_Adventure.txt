'''''Choose Your Destiny: A Finn & Poe Adventure''''' is a [[canon]] adventure book written by [[Cavan Scott]] and illustrated by [[Elsa Charretier]]. It is the fourth novel in the ''[[Star Wars: Choose Your Destiny]]'' anthology series, and it is part of the [[Journey to Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker|Journey to ''Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker'']] publishing program. The book was published on [[October 4]], [[2019]] by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]].

==Publisher's summary==
''The Resistance needs your help!''

''Join Finn, Poe, and BB-8 as they infiltrate a First Order&ndash;occupied bacta plant for General Leia Organa''.

''With over twenty possible outcomes, you'll have to choose carefully to keep Finn, Poe, and BB-8 safe from the First Order.''

''Choose your destiny!''
