'''Dodd''' was a [[Clone Commander]] who served as a [[Clone navigation officer|navigation officer]] for the [[Republic Navy]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Complete S2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dodd was slated to appear in "[[Grievous Intrigue]]," the ninth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the animated ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, but was ultimately cut from the episode. The [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Two|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season Two]] featured an unfinished animatic from the production of the "Grievous Intrigue" episode which featured the clone commander.<ref name="Complete S2" />
