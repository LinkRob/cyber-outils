'''''Count Dooku: Words of Truth''''' was a [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] that [[Ansibella Dellu]] worked on during her studies at [[Reena University]]. It was meant to advertise a series of lectures that [[Count]] [[Dooku]], then leader of the [[Independent Movement for Self-Determination]], was supposed to give at the [[university]]'s [[Panos Lecture Hall]] at a yet-to-be determined date and [[time]]. Following the end of the [[Clone Wars]], the newly-created [[Imperial Security Bureau|Security Bureau]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] conducted loyalty purges, during which much propaganda material featuring Dooku was destroyed. However, rare specimens of Dellu's work-in-progress poster survived those purges.<ref name="Star Wars Propaganda" />
