"'''Back from the Drawing Board'''" is an article series published under various titles written by [[Kevin Beentjes]] and published through the [[Star Wars Blog|''Star Wars'' Blog]]. It shows ''[[Star Wars]]'' creatures and [[Alien/Legends|aliens]] which were created using rejected designs.

==Media==
===Issues===
*[[June 6]], [[2013]]: Drawing from the Past: How Prehistoric Creatures Influence Star Wars Designs
*[[August 15]], 2013: Drawing from the Present: Familiar Creatures in a Galaxy Far, Far Away
*[[October 17]], 2013: Drawing from the Imagination: Mythological Creatures in ''Star Wars'', Part 1
*[[November 7]], 2013: Drawing from the Imagination: Mythological Creatures in ''Star Wars'', Part 2
*[[October 1]], [[2014]]: Back from the Drawing Board: Recycled ''Star Wars'' Aliens
