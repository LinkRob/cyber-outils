{{Quote|You know how to fly?''"<br />"''I stole my first escape pod when I was six, while with my mom on a diplomatic trip to Mon Cala. Trust me, I know how to fly.|Imri Cantaros and Avon Starros|<ref name="A Test of Courage" />}}
A '''diplomatic trip to Mon Cala''' was undertaken by the [[Galactic Republic]] [[senator]] [[Ghirra Starros]]<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> in [[238 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> For the trip to<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Mon Cala]], Ghirra's six [[Standard year|year]] old [[Starros Clan|daughter]] [[Avon Sunvale|Avon]] accompanied her. During her [[time]] on the planet, Avon [[Thief|stole]] an [[escape pod]] and [[Pilot|flew]] it.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> during the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[Attack on the Steady Wing|attack]] on<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> the [[Star Cruiser]]<ref name="Mission to Disaster">''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]''</ref> ''[[Steady Wing]]'', a group of individuals including Avon made their way to a [[hangar]] where they came across a [[Shuttle|maintenance shuttle]] they could use to escape. Before the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] could sit in the pilot's seat, she discovered Avon was already seated there, and the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Imri Cantaros]] skeptically asked the girl if she knew how to fly. In response, Avon informed the group of the time she had stolen the escape pod during the trip to Mon Cala, and told them to trust her.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

==Behind the scenes==
The diplomatic trip to Mon Cala was mentioned in the [[2021]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published as part of<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
