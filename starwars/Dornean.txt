The '''Dorneans''' were a [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]]<ref name="Starfighters">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref> to the [[planet]] [[Dornea]].<ref name="Homeworld" /> The Dorneans designed the [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunship]], a [[gunship]] class manufactured by the [[Dornean Braha'ket Fleetworks Conglomerate]]<ref name="Starships and Speeders">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> to protect their homeworld from the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Starfighters" /> The [[starship]] became the representative ship of the Dornean race.<ref name="Starfighter Missions">''[[Star Wars: Starfighter Missions]]''</ref> A number of these gunships came to aid the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]],<ref name="Starfighters" /> notably after Dornea joined the faction following the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dornean species was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]'', written by [[Landry Q. Walker]].<ref name="Starfighters" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the species first appeared in the [[1996]] novel ''[[Before the Storm]]'', written by [[Michael P. Kube-McDowell]].<ref name="Before the Storm">''[[Before the Storm]]''</ref>
