The '''D9 Demolitions Droid''' was a model of [[droid brain detonator]] manufactured by [[CryonCorp]]. It used a [[droid brain]] to detonate explosive devices.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
The D9 Demolitions Droid was first mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Fully Operational Is Online">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/3/29/fully-operational-is-online|text=Fully Operational Is Online}}</ref>
