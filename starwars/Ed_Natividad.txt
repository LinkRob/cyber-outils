'''Ed Natividad''' is a storyboard and conceptual artist who worked on the [[1999]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. As conceptual artist, he helped shape the look of the movie's architectural elements, costumes and weapons.<ref name="Bio" />

==Biography==
In [[1991]], Ed Natividad graduated from the [[Wikipedia:College for Creative Studies|Center for Creative Studies College of Art Design]] in [[Wikipedia:Detroit|Detroit]].<ref name="About">{{WebCite|url=http://www.collegeforcreativestudies.edu/Alumni/2862/Natividad|text=Alumni: Ed Natividad|work=[http://www.collegeforcreativestudies.edu College for Creative Studies]|archivedate=20170908213427}}</ref> While at the Center for Creative Studies, he majored in transportation design and illustration, and took a minor in anatomy. In [[1994]], Natividad started working at [[Industrial Light & Magic]].<ref name="Bio" />
