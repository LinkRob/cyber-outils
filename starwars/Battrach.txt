The '''Battrach''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] in the [[galaxy]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Keeve Trennis]] claimed to have talked to a [[Gender|male]] Battrach [[Smuggler|gunrunner]] at [[Port Lennax]] when [[Infiltration of the Nihil Tempest|infiltrating]] the [[Nihil]].<ref name="The High Republic 10" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Battrach.jpg|thumb|right|200px|The Battrach in ''Star Wars'' Legends]]
The Battrach were first [[canon]]ically mentioned in the [[The High Republic (2021) 10|tenth issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic series]] [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2021)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']], written by [[Cavan Scott]] and illustrated by [[Georges Jeanty]] as part of the [[Star Wars: The High Republic|multimedia project of the same name]]<ref name="The High Republic 10" /> and published on [[October 20]], [[2021]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/94643/star_wars_the_high_republic_2021_10|text=Star Wars: The High Republic (2021) #10}}</ref> The [[Battrach/Legends|Battrach]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via the unlicensed roleplaying adventure "[[Flight of the Jedi]]," which was published in the [[Valkyrie 19|nineteenth]] and [[Valkyrie 20|twentieth]] issues of ''[[Valkyrie]]'' magazine. In the adventure, they were a [[Species/Legends|species]] of bioengineered amphibians.<ref name="Valkyrie 19>{{Valkyrie|issue1=19|issue2=20|story=Flight of the Jedi}}</ref>
