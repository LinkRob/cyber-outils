'''Chris Glenn''' is a [[Lucasfilm]] concept artist whose credits include the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' and ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' animated television series.

The character [[Fenn Rau]] was originally to be named "Glenn Rau" after Chris Glenn and [[Brad Rau]], though the name was changed during the script phase.<ref name="BTSname">{{WebCite|url=http://fullofsith.com/archives/2100|text=Episode CCXXVI: Brad Rau and Forces of Destiny|date=2017-08-30|work=Full of Sith|archivedate=20191231234719}}</ref>
