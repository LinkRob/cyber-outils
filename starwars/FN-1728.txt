'''FN-1728''' was a [[human]] who served the [[First Order]] as a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]]. The [[trooper]] briefly chatted with [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] while she was [[Hunt for Rivas|hunting]] for the alleged [[Treason|traitor]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Sol Rivas]] on [[Starkiller Base]].

==Biography==
FN-1728 was a [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] who was stationed at [[Starkiller Base]] during the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]]. FN-1728 and two other [[trooper]]s came across [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] as she attempted to [[Hunt for Rivas|hunt]] down [[Lieutenant]] [[Sol Rivas]], FN-1728 reported to Phasma that word was that [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] was being evacuated from the base before being told by Phasma that they should man their post until ordered otherwise.<ref name="Captain Phasma 1" />

==Personality and traits==
FN-1728<ref name="Captain Phasma 1" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> who served as a First Order stormtrooper. They were dutiful and complied with Phasma's orders to remain at their post despite the impending destruction of Starkiller Base. They wore [[Color|white]] [[First Order stormtrooper armor|stormtrooper armor]].<ref name="Captain Phasma 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
FN-1728 first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: Captain Phasma]]'', a [[2017]] four-issue comic published by [[Marvel Comics]] as part of the ''[[Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]'' promotional material. They were created by [[Kelly Thompson]], illustrated by [[Marco Checchetto]], and colored by [[Andres Mossa]].
