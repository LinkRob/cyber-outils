'''Acceleration compensators''',<ref name="Cross">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> also known as '''inertial compensators'''<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> or '''inertial dampeners''',<ref name="TLJ Expanded">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> were devices that manipulated [[gravity]] to compensate for the intense accelerations that [[starship]]s underwent during high-speed maneuvers, thus preventing their occupants from being [[Death|killed]] by excessive g-forces.<ref name="Cross" />

Inertial dampeners helped for a time to protect the remains of the ''[[Supremacy]]'', the [[flagship]] of the [[First Order]], following the [[Resistance]] vessel [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] ramming it while entering [[hyperspace]]. Ultimately, however, the vessel was catastrophically damaged, forcing its abandonment.<ref name="TLJ Expanded" />
