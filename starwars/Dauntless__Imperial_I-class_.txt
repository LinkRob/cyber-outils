The '''''Dauntless'''''<ref name="UVG" /> was an [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] that was in service of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[Star Destroyer]] was stationed above [[Jedha City]]<ref name="Rogue One" /> in [[1 BBY]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Battle on Jedha]] to [[1 BBY]].</ref> after [[Imperial Intelligence]] claimed a single [[destroyer]] would shut down any resistance to the Empire's [[Jedha's kyber mines|kyber crystal mining]] operation on [[Jedha]].<ref name="197 Probe">{{InsiderCite|197|Probing the Galaxy: Imperial Reconnaissance Report}}</ref> Despite the deployment of the ship's considerable resources, including [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] escorts for [[Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle|Imperial cargo shuttles]] ferrying kyber crystals to and from the city, Rebel attacks on Imperial forces [[Campaigns of Saw Gerrera's Partisans|continued]], led by the rogue [[Saw Gerrera]] and his [[partisans]]. 

The ship's role as a deterrent was ended following a [[Battle of Jedha|final skirmish]] between rebel elements, including Jyn Erso and Cassian Andor, and the city's garrison. The ''Dauntless'' enacted [[Protocol 13]], withdrawing its troops from the city shortly before its [[Destruction of Jedha City|destruction]] by the [[Death Star]], an Imperial [[superweapon]] whose [[superlaser]] was fueled by kyber crystals harvested from the very world it decimated.<ref name="Rogue One" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Dauntless'' first appeared in [[canon]] in the [[2016]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', although it was not named.<ref name="Rogue One" /> It was later identified as the ''Dauntless'' in ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', a book written to accompany the film, written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="UVG" /> It first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in [[Riders of the Maelstrom (adventure)|''Riders of Maelstrom'']], a [[roleplaying game]] book written by [[Ray Winninger]] and published by [[West End Games]] in [[1989]].<ref name="Riders of the Maelstrom">[[Riders of the Maelstrom (adventure)|''Riders of Maelstrom'']]</ref>
