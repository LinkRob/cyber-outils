The '''end-of-year family race''' was a special racing event held at the [[Hanna City Flight Academy]] hosted by [[Wedge Antilles]].<ref name="I Am Your Mother">{{Visions|I Am Your Mother}}</ref>

{{Event-stub}}

==Behind the Scenes==
The end-of-year family race first appeared in the [[Aardman Animations|Aardman]] short "[[I Am Your Mother]]". Starting from the [[Hanna City Flight Academy]] through the city and before the final stretch across the desert, [[Aurelien Predal]] created 3D mock-ups of the ships, schools and city to incredibly map out the circuit of the race, ending up to be a great, useful tool of comminutation while Predal quickly provide compositions and images with the storyboard department at Aardman, lead by head of story [[Adrian Magnza]], to provide impact changes from the rough layout of [[Hanna City]] when necessary from the art and CG departments.<ref name="aurelien-predal">{{WebCite|url=http://www.aurelien-predal.com/star-wars-visions-volume-2|text=“I Am Your Mother” - Star Wars: Visions|archivedate=20240627093317}}</ref>
