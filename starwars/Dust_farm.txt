'''Dust farms''' were used to carry out [[dustgrain farming]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Pasaana]]<ref name="Atlas" /> located in the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Aki-Aki]]s  often manned the [[farm]]s and had one holiday break every 42 [[Standard year|years]] for the [[Festival of the Ancestors]]. A sign in [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]]'s [[Yoda's Guide to the Galaxy|book]] asserted that gathering dust on Pasaana was better than touring [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Atlas" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dust farms were mentioned in the [[2021]] non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Yoda's Galaxy Atlas]]'' written by [[Simon Hugo]].<ref name="Atlas" />
