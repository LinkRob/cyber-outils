A '''battle''' took place on<ref name="Battlefront" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Heroes of Rogue One">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Heroes of Rogue One}}</ref> [[Bamayar]] during the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] failed [[Mid Rim Retreat|Mid Rim Offensive]] in the [[Galactic Civil War]]. During the battle,<ref name="Battlefront" /> which took place between circa [[1 ABY]] and [[2 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] took control of the [[Ducal Palace]] on Bamayar, as well as the stench-ridden [[Spaceport|port]] [[Chenodra]].<ref name="Battlefront" />

After the capture of Chenodra, the 61st conducted a cleanup, where the [[company]]'s [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]] approached the [[Rebel trooper|soldier]] [[Twitch]]. Twitch was convinced she and the fellow [[soldier]] [[Maximian Ajax]] had made a big mistake on Bamayar, but instead of bringing up the mishap, Evon began talking to her about the buildings of the port. [[Hazram Namir]], a [[First Sergeant]] in the 61st, briefly remembered the fighting on Bamayar while on the [[planet]] [[Haidoral Prime]]<ref name="Battlefront" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref> Twitch also recounted her experience with Evon at Chenodra<ref name="Battlefront" /> later that [[Standard year|year]]<ref name="Date" /> at a gathering after Evon's [[funeral]].<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle of Bamayar was mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
