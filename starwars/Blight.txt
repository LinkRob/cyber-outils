The '''Blight''', first known as the '''blight''', and also known as the '''Gray''', was the term used to refer to an affliction that arose during the [[High Republic Era]]. Speculatively referred to as a [[disease]], the blight affected matter, living or otherwise, and drained it of life, leaving it a calcified husk, similar to the effect that the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures had on [[Force-sensitive]] individuals. The blight spread through contact, slowly consuming whatever it touched. Being affected by the Blight did not cause pain but instead simply made the affected area feel empty, as if life was not present.

The Blight was actually an ancient '''hunger''' emerging within a void between the [[Light side of the Force|light]] and [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]]s of [[the Force]] in a [[Vergence|Force nexus]] on the [[planet]] [[Sophros]]. From the nexus evolved the Shrikarai, whose [[egg]]s were produced by the vergence itself. The Shrikarai consumed equal amounts of light and dark, the oppositional Force energy working together to keep the hunger contained. When the Shrikarai began being forcibly removed from their homeworld, the balance began to shift, eventually reaching a breaking point in which the remaining Shrikarai could not hold back the hunger. This resulted in the Blight erupting on various worlds. With each Shrikarai that died, the Blight grew worse. 

Although a coming blight was foreseen in [[382 BBY]], the affliction had begun to spread across [[Planet|worlds]] of [[the galaxy]] by [[228 BBY]]. In that [[Standard year|year]], it emerged on the [[Arli's moon|moon]] of [[Arli]], whose residents were trapped on the [[moon]] by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]. [[San Tekka clan|San Tekka family]] members [[Jordanna Sparkburn]] and [[Cair San Tekka]] [[Mission to Arli's moon|traveled]] to the moon to find the former's relatives, but the latter was inadvertently infected with the blight, which slowly calcified his [[Limb|hand]]. The two were able to [[Dismemberment|cut]] San Tekka's hand off and isolate it within a [[Beam-generated containment field|containment field]], preventing the spread of the blight. Sparkburn delivered the blighted hand to [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]], who brought it to the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]]. The hand was later given to Knight [[Reath Silas]], who presented it to former [[Jedi]] [[Azlin Rell]] during their investigations into the Nameless.

==Description==
===Cause===
The affliction known as the blight<ref name="Defy the Storm" />&mdash;known eventually as the Blight, with a capital B<ref name="Trials of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref>&mdash;was speculatively referred to as a desiccating [[disease]].<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> It had a deep connection to the [[Shrikarai]],<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> who were known in [[the galaxy|the greater galaxy]] as the Nameless.<ref name="Edge 3">[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 3|''The High Republic: The Edge of Balance'', Vol. 3]]</ref> According to the Shrikarai, the blight, which they called the hunger, was an ancient power that emerged within a void between the [[Light side of the Force|light]] and [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]]s of [[the Force]] in the [[Sophros]] [[Vergence|Force nexus]]. Also from the nexus evolved the Shrikarai, whose [[egg]]s were produced by the vergence itself. The Shrikarai consumed equal amounts of light and dark, the oppositional Force energy working together to keep the hunger contained.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> 

The spread of the Blight began when the Shrikarai were forcefully removed from their homeworld to be used as weapons against the [[Jedi Order]]. With each Shrikarai who was killed, the Blight grew worse, eventually reaching a breaking point in which the remaining Shrikarai could not hold back the hunger. It was hypothetically possible for the Blight to spread across the entire [[galaxy]], infecting and [[Death|killing]] everyone within. As more Shrikarai were taken from their homeworld and died, this resulted in the Blight erupting on various worlds.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />

===Effect and appearance===
{{Quote|But it&hellip;ate&hellip;a moon?''"<br />"''Everything. It was like&mdash;total desiccation of everything.|[[Xylan Graf]] and Cair San Tekka|<ref name="Defy the Storm" />}}
[[File:LodenGreatstormDead-THRTrailOfShadows1.jpg|thumb|right|275px|The blight's effect was similar to that which the Nameless creatures had on Force-sensitives (the end result of a Nameless feasting upon one such Force user, pictured).]]
The blight affected all types of matter: living beings, including people, fauna, and [[Plant|flora]]; and non-living matter, such as natural terrain or structures. Matter that was consumed by the blight had the color and life drained from it until it was left a calcified husk, resembling a [[carbon]] [[mineral]] substance, rock, [[pumice]], or ash. The effect of the blight was similar in nature to that had by the Nameless on [[Force-sensitive]] individuals. To Force-sensitives, the blight felt dead, as if [[the Force]] unnaturally eschewed it.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

The blight appeared a chalky gray [[color]], streaked with lines of white as if diseased. When present on skin, the blight appeared as a scab-like growth, featuring rough edges with hooks or tendrils as if to pull itself larger. Land consumed by the blight retained its shape, keeping the silhouettes of terrain features and flora, but with all color and life drained. The edge of the blight was uneven, with the effect causing it to seem like a slowly-spilling liquid. In regions, the blight appeared to crumble or disintegrate. From [[Realspace|space]], blighted land appeared shadowed and swathed in white and gray.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

The blight would eventually [[Death|kill]] living beings it infected, but death was slow, taking numerous [[Standard hour|hours]]. Medical substances such as [[Juvan|rejuve]] and [[hydro-gel]] were ineffective at healing those affected&mdash;the blight could only be stopped by the removal of the afflicted body part. To an individual infected by a small patch of blight, the affliction did not cause any pain or sensation,<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> instead simply feeling empty, as if life was absent.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />

===Transmission===
{{Quote|The containers were destroyed, and Tiliqana&hellip; It happened to her at the same pace it's eating the valley.|Harth Sparkburn|<ref name="Defy the Storm" />}}
Emerging randomly across [[Planet|worlds]],<ref name="THR Phase III">{{SW|url=the-high-republic|text=Star Wars: The High Republic}}</ref> the blight slowly spread through contact with afflicted matter. It was not transmissible via air, and unlike a [[mold]], the blight did not travel like spores.<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> Biologist [[Elping Tsoo]] further noted that the blight did not resemble a fungus nor ameboid parasite, with its aftereffects instead appearing closer to some kind of sprayed weapon. Even then, that sprayed weapon comparison was made by a perplexed Tsoo, who confessed he had no idea what he was dealing with. Upon being faced by blight victims, he was unsure if the victims were consumed by the blight or transformed into it.<ref name="Pathogen">{{InsiderCite|225|Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Pathogen}}</ref> It was possible to prevent the spread of the blight by removing an afflicted part and trapping it in stasis within a [[containment field]], although its effect could still spread to matter within the field.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

The transformation rate varied between various types of matter, including [[crystal]]line and natural [[Volcano|volcanic]] structures versus [[metal]]lic alloys. The blight could consume living beings at a similar rate to land. The spread of the blight was too slow to see over short periods, although its nature made it appear as if it was moving. The blight did not only consume the surface of terrain, but also spread underground. Terrain past the boundary of a blighted region remained living and healthy until the affliction spread; similarly, any body parts of an infected individual that had not yet been reached by the blight were unharmed.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

==History==
===A day to come===
[[File:Nameless-SWU.png|thumb|right|250px|The Nameless (pictured) were connected to the blight.]]
In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="382 Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> while on a [[Mission to Planet X (Path of the Open Hand)|mission]] to the world of [[Sophros|Planet X]] to collect Nameless eggs for use against the [[Jedi Order]], the [[Path of the Open Hand]] encountered the [[Under-Dweller]]s. One member of the team, [[Bokana Koss]], also experienced a maddening and horrifying [[Force vision]] about a coming "blight" that nothing would be able to stop, warning [[Guide of the Closed Fist|Guide of the Open Hand]] [[Marda Ro]] that the Force itself would be "chained" and "no more" after what was to come. He further remarked how the Under-Dwellers wanted to spread and would do so as a "blight upon [[the galaxy]]'s lands." According to Koss, the Under-Dwellers wanted their mission to succeed so as to begin their blight, although one of the creatures attacked and knocked Koss out of the vision.<ref name="Path of Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref>

===A blight upon the galaxy===
====Appearance on Arli's moon====
{{Quote|At first we thought it was some kind of mold, but it doesn't travel like spores, and it affects everything. But we haven't figured out the pattern of infection or speed of takeover.''"<br />"''Now we have even less idea what it is or what's causing it. People who could leave did so after it became clear it affects fauna, too, and&mdash;and people.|Essren and Harth Sparkburn|<ref name="Defy the Storm" />}}
[[File:NihilSymbol-2020StarWars30.svg|thumb|left|240px|The Nihil marauders (symbol pictured) prevented escape from Arli's moon after the blight emerged there.]]
By [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|DFS}}</ref> during the time of the [[Galactic Republic]] and Jedi Order's [[Nihil conflict|conflict]] against the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]],<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> the blight had begun to randomly spread across worlds within the [[galaxy]].<ref name="THR Phase III" /> In that year,<ref name="Date" /> the blight appeared on the [[Arli's moon|moon]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Arli]], within the Nihil's [[Occlusion Zone]] territory. The blight spread to consume part of a valley near the [[city]] of [[Birage]], including the ridge known as the [[Iliri Spine]] and the land hosting the [[San Tekka family manor|family manor]] of the [[San Tekka clan|San Tekka family]] members who lived on the moon; it also desiccated the [[cave]] systems underneath the valley, causing the [[water]]ways and rivers to dry up. In a matter of [[Standard week|weeks]], the blight had spread to cover around half of the moon, with resident [[Harth Sparkburn]] expecting that the entire moon would be consumed within three more weeks.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

The residents of Birage attempted to perform experiments on the blight, throwing various items into the affliction as a test. However, without proper equipment, they were unable to determine the pattern or speed of infection. [[Tiliqana]], Birage's head [[medic]], attempted to collect samples of the blight using medical waste procedures, but her containers were destroyed and she was infected by the affliction, which eventually killed her. After realizing it could affect fauna and people, some of the moon's residents were able to leave, but the Nihil eventually prevented any further escape, leaving three [[starship]]s in [[orbit]]. The residents suspected that the Nihil were possibly involved in the blight, but even the pirates themselves were wary of the affliction.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

====Discovery of desiccation====
{{Quote|This whole moon will probably be consumed in another three weeks, if we can't stop it.''"<br />"''Harth, you have to go with us. You can't stay. You just said you have no idea ''how'' to stop it, so we'd be abandoning you to&mdash;to desiccate like this!|Harth and Jordanna Sparkburn|<ref name="Defy the Storm" />}}
[[File:JordannaSparkburn-THRS.png|thumb|right|240px|Jordanna Sparkburn (pictured) visited Arli's moon, where she encountered the blight for the first time.]]
[[Jordanna Sparkburn]] and [[Cair San Tekka]], members of the San Tekka family, [[Mission to Arli's moon|traveled]] to Arli's moon in the [[cruiser]] ''[[Brightbird]]'' in order to locate the former's relatives. After evading the Nihil vessels&mdash;who did not follow them into the [[atmosphere]]&mdash;the pair flew over the blighted landscape, unable to make sense of the affliction, before landing at Birage. There, they were met by Harth and the other residents of the city, who at first did not wish to speak of the blight but eventually explained what they knew of the affliction.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

When Jordanna wished to see the affliction for herself, Harth led her and San Tekka to the edge of the blighted land. As they inspected the blight, the terrain collapsed beneath them&mdash;a result of the caves below having been desiccated&mdash;taking Jordanna and Harth with it. San Tekka assisted in rescuing the pair before they fell, but in the process unknowingly touched the blight, which spread onto the knuckle of his [[Limb|hand]]. After avoiding the landslide, the trio realized the cave system also spread under Birage, rushing back to the city to rescue the residents. The remaining population of Birage boarded the ''Brightbird'' and escaped the moon, evading the Nihil in orbit.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

====Lending a hand====
{{Quote|There's some kind of blight attacking a moon on the frontier. It devoured my hometown, and when my cousin Cair touched it, this is what it did to his hand. We had to amputate it, but&hellip; He survived, but nothing that touches this does.|Jordanna Sparkburn, to Vernestra Rwoh|<ref name="Defy the Storm" />}}
[[File:CairSanTekka-THR-ConceptArt.png|thumb|left|240px|After Cair San Tekka (pictured) was infected by the blight, his hand was amputated and delivered as evidence to the Jedi Order.]]
In [[hyperspace]], however, Harth's daughter [[Peach (human)|Peach]] noticed the spot of blight on San Tekka's hand. As the ''Brightbird''{{'s}} passengers panicked, San Tekka recalled everything he had come into contact with, including Peach herself and parts of the ship. Although San Tekka suggested that he would isolate in an [[escape pod]] and launch after exiting hyperspace, Jordanna proposed [[Dismemberment|amputating]] the hand, noting that as nothing else that he had touched was changing, the rest of his body might be safe. San Tekka retrieved a [[beam-generated containment field]], and with Harth and his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Essren Sparkburn|Essren]], assisting the process, Jordanna used her [[Merry Maker]] [[laser]] blade [[weapon]] to cut off the hand. Harth activated the containment field, trapping the blight&mdash;which had had nearly consumed the forefinger by that point&mdash;in stasis.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

Although San Tekka was concerned their attempt was ineffective, he survived after the amputation of his blighted hand, which had been completely consumed within two [[Standard hour|hours]]. Once they had escaped to the planet [[Seswenna]], Jordanna was determined to deliver the hand to the Republic and the [[Jedi]], sickened by the sight of the blight and unsure whether it was a Nihil [[superweapon]] or unknown entity from [[Wild Space]]. Storing the containment field in a [[plastic]] alloy case, Jordanna handed the sample of the blight to [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] after escaping the Occlusion Zone, informing her about the affliction.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

Rwoh brought the blight sample to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> the planet<ref name="Temptation of the Force">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]], delivering it to the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]]. It was eventually passed on to Knight [[Reath Silas]], who was investigating the Nameless with the assistance of the former Jedi [[Azlin Rell]]. He presented the blighted hand to Rell, who at first assumed it was from a Jedi killed by the Nameless; Silas informed him that not only was the hand not a Jedi's, but no Nameless were present. When Silas described the blight, Rell declared that something was coming for "them." Silas asked whether Rell referred to the Nihil or the Nameless, but the former Jedi shook his head and uttered a name, merely laughing when the Knight demanded to know.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

===Fear of the Drengir===
In time, Silas and Rell discovered a link between the Nameless and the blight. While [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] did not doubt their lead, reflecting that every recent event might indeed be connected, he ordered the two to put aside their investigation for a time in order to head to the planet [[Banchii]], where [[Azlin's journal|Rell's old journal]] was located. On world, the [[Drengir]] species, which possessed a natural connection to the [[dark side of the Force]]<ref name="Edge 3" /> but had seemingly been forced back into slumber with the [[battle on Mulita]],<ref name="THR 8">[[The High Republic (2021) 8|''The High Republic'' (2021) 8]]</ref> had re-emerged. In the time since, the Drengir<ref name="Edge 3" />&mdash;which shared a connective consciousness known as the [[root-mind]]<ref name="Character E">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref>&mdash;became aware of the blight, which the Drengir identified as a series of diseased roots spreading across the galaxy. Realizing the blight was close to Banchii, the Drengir re-emerged on world and possessed the bodies of local corpses, which the Drengir used to warn the people of Banchii to run.<ref name="Edge 3" />

When Rell finally arrived on Banchii with Silas and Yoda, he sensed fear coming from everyone on world, including from the Drengir, whom Rell deduced were fearful of the Nameless or perhaps something worse. Finally, a Drengir proper would emerge with the possessed bodies, one of which was used to warn the Jedi of the blight's approach and urge all gathered to escape. However, their discussion was interrupted by the arrival of Nihil forces under<ref name="Edge 3" /> [[Ministry of Advancement]]<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 5">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 5]]</ref> [[scientist]] [[Niv Drendow Apruk]], who brought with him Nameless.<ref name="Edge 3" />

===Preventing the end===
During their [[Mission to Planet X (Jedi Order)|mission to Planet X]], [[Jedi Master]]s [[Elzar Mann]] and [[Avar Kriss]] met with the Shrikarai, who explained to them the truth behind their existence and the Blight, which they referred to as the hunger. <ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />

Following a massacre of Shrikarai at the hand of [[Marchion Ro]], few Shrikarai remained. This accelerated the Blight beyond levels previously seen. Even the assembled Jedi holding back the patch of Blight deep within the Jedi Temple were beginning to fail. An elder Shrikarai, who grew despondent, came to oversee an abundance of Shrikarai eggs provided by the planet. Unfortunately, the incubation period and maturation time would exceed the amount of time remaining for the galaxy to survive the hunger. Mann and Kriss, however, developed a plan: they would hold back the hunger themselves. Avar Kriss remained in the dark void, acting as a source of light in the darkest place in the vergence. Elzar, on the other hand, would be guided to a place of light, serving as a steward of the dark side. There they would maintain for the rest of their lives, waiting for the next generation of Shrikarai to mature and reclaim their duty. Taking on this mantle, Elzar Mann and Avar Kriss stopped the spread of the Blight.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />

===A legacy of emptiness===
Nearly a [[Standard century|century]] after the Blight was contained by Masters Mann and Kriss, the effects of the Blight persisted on some planets. On the world of [[Siline]], the Blight had calcified an area of [[forest]], leaving it devoid of life and cracking the planet's mantle. It came to be known as the [[Badlands (Siline)|badlands]]. A [[Lionine Graf's great-grandmere|member]] of the [[Graf family]] took advantage of the destruction on Siline after realizing the cracked mantle had revealed new layers of strata containing veins of the valuable mineral [[iflisalt]]. She used this discovery to establish the [[Graf-Siline Industries Consortium]], which [[mining|mined]] iflisalt there for generations, contributing greatly to the planet's economy and drawing interest from the Republic to make Siline a member world.<ref name="Crown">''[[The Acolyte: The Crystal Crown]]''</ref> 

By [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Crystal Crown Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> nature had reclaimed only a few [[meter]]s of the badlands from the perimeter, as well as a few patches within. When Jedi [[Padawan]]s [[Jecki Lon]] and [[Yord Fandar]] were shown the area by [[Silinese]] locals, both were deeply disturbed by the badland's emptiness in the Force, which lingered all those years later.<ref name="Crown" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:TheHighRepublic-PathofVengeance-cover.jpg|thumb|right|200px|The Blight was first mentioned in ''The High Republic: Path of Vengeance''.]]
The blight was first mentioned in the young-adult [[2023]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> It later appeared in the novel ''[[The High Republic: Defy the Storm]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and [[Justina Ireland]]<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> and published on [[March 5]], [[2024]] as part of the initiative's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase" /> The blight was first visually depicted in the short story "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Pathogen]]," written by [[Lydia Kang]], illustrated by [[Jake Lunt Davies]], and published in [[Star Wars Insider 225|''Star Wars Insider'' 225]]<ref name="TftOZ Pathogen">{{InsiderCite|225|Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Pathogen}}</ref> on [[April 30]] of that year.<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=m/156-star-wars-insider-225|text=Star Wars Insider #225}}</ref> Ahead of release of the [[2025]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]'', [[Random House Worlds]] editor [[Tom Hoeler]] revealed that within the novel, the Blight, which had previously been referred to with a lowercase "b," would be referred to with a capitalized "B" due to the going concern it had caused [[in-universe]].<ref name="Tom Twitter">{{Twitter|DarthInternous|status/1928169270539661654|[[Tom Hoeler]]|quote=The phrase "the blight" which had previously been lowercase is now - in Trials of the Jedi only - capitalized to "the Blight." It's become such a going concern in the GFFA that it has "earned" capitalization.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/pnyq4}}</ref>
