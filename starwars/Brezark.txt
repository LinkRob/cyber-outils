'''Brezark''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Quarren]] [[Gender|male]] who served as a [[mercenary]] and master trapper during the [[High Republic Era]].

==Biography==
At some point, he [[Strike on the royal family of Burundang|wiped out]] the [[royal family of Burundang]]. Eventually, Brezark was recruited into the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization by [[Marchion Ro]]&mdash;the [[Eye of the Nihil]]&mdash;and was spared from the experiments of the Nihil [[scientist]] [[Baron]] [[Boolan]] by Ro.<ref name="Premonition" />

In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Brezark and a group of Nihil were aboard a [[Unidentified Nihil satellite|Nihil satellite]] with a kidnapped [[Unidentified Twi'lek family|Twi'lek family]] in order to lure a [[Jedi]] onto the [[Space station|station]] to be [[Death|killed]] by the [[Great Leveler]], a [[Living Force|Force]]-[[Diet|consuming]] [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creature. When one [[Mission to a Nihil satellite|arrived]], the Quarren learned the Jedi was in fact [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], and attempted to kill Yoda with [[Poison|toxic]] [[Slythiant solvent]], but failed. Despite, the Grand Master's desire to save Brezark, the Quarren was killed by the effects of the Great Leveler, being turned into dust.<ref name="Premonition" />

==Powers and abilities==
Brezark was an experienced mercenary specialized in traps and capable of using firearms. He was also [[Force-sensitive]] and was capable of using [[Telekinesis]].<ref name="Premonition" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brezark appeared in ''[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance: Premonition]]'', a [[Star Wars comic books|volume]] of the [[Wikipedia:Manga|manga]] series ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Edge of Balance]]''.
