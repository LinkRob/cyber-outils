'''Café Raduli''',<ref name="Rules of the Game" /> also known as the '''Raduli café and patisserie''', was a shop located on [[Cabranga Street]] in the coastal [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. The café was a popular location in the [[Old Town (Canto Bight)|Old Town]] area.<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
