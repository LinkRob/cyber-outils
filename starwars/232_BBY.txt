The following events took place in or around the [[Standard year|year]] '''232 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3045 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''213 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7745''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*The [[Kublop Springs festival]] is held by the local residents and invited the [[Jedi]] to celebrate.<ref name="Yoda's Mission">{{YJA|Yoda's Mission}}</ref>
*[[Team Crimson Bolt|Team ''Crimson Bolt'']] wins the [[Kublop Classic]] on [[Tenoo]].<ref name="Nash's Race Day">{{YJA|Nash's Race Day}}</ref>
*The residents in [[Kublop Springs]] hold the [[Jellyfruit Festival]].<ref name="The Jellyfruit Pursuit">{{YJA|The Jellyfruit Pursuit}}</ref>
*[[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]], [[Nubs]], [[SF-R3]], and [[CAM]] discover a new [[bird]] species, the [[burrowberry bird]] on [[Yamradi]].<ref name="Creature Safari">{{YJA|Creature Safari}}</ref>
*To commemorate the launch of the new [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]], the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Galactic Republic]] host a [[Starlight Beacon conclave|conclave]] at the station.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
*Lys Solay, SR-R3 and CAM discover a new [[reptile]] species, the [[Mardorsh]].<ref name="Creature Comforts">{{YJA|Creature Comforts}}</ref>
*In the midst of the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] occurs, causing many [[starship]]s across [[the galaxy]] to be abruptly knocked out of [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /><ref name="Into the Dark">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref>
*The Nihil [[Raid on Elphrona|raid]] the [[Blythe family]] [[Blythe homestead|homestead]], with the secret goal of capturing, for [[Marchion Ro]], a Jedi who would come to the rescue.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
*The ''[[Vessel]]'', carrying a group of Jedi, was stranded at the [[Amaxine Space Station|Amaxine space station]] in the aftermath of the Great Hyperspace Disaster along with many other galactic citizens. There, they encounter and unknowingly [[Drengir crisis|unleash]] the [[Drengir]] across the galaxy.<ref name="Into the Dark" />
*The [[Nihil]] [[Attack on Trymant IV|attack]] [[Trymant IV]].<ref name="HRA 1">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 1]]</ref>
*[[Keeve Trennis]] is [[Knighting Ceremony|knighted]] and begins serving on the Jedi front-lines fighting the Drengir.<ref name="HR 2">[[The High Republic (2021) 2|''The High Republic'' (2021) 2]]</ref>
*The [[padawan]]s of the [[Star Hopper|''Star Hopper'']] welcome [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]], a refugee from Trymant IV, into their ranks, but not as a padawan.<ref name="HRA 2">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 2|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 2]]</ref><ref name="HRA 3">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 3|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 3]]</ref>
*The Galactic Republic starship ''[[Steady Wing]]'' is [[bomb]]ed by undercover Nihil [[saboteur]]s, causing the party onboard to be stranded on the [[jungle]] [[moon]] [[Wevo]].<ref name="AToC" />
*[[Krix Kamerat]], another Trymant IV refugee, joins the Nihil and is taken on as the protege of Marchion Ro as the [[Evereni]] [[Eye of the Nihil|Eye]] searches for the [[Great Leveler]].<ref name="HRA 5">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 5]]</ref>
*The Nihil [[Kassav's Tempest|Tempest]] of [[Kassav Milliko]] battles the Galactic Republic and Jedi Order at the [[Kur Nebula]], the Tempest being decimated.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
*A Drengir arrives on [[Starlight Beacon]] in the [[Death|corpse]] of a [[Unidentified Hutt trader|Hutt trader]], and [[Assault on Starlight Beacon|attacks]] the station when awakened.<ref name="HR 4">[[The High Republic (2021) 4|''The High Republic'' (2021) 4]]</ref><ref name="HR 5">[[The High Republic (2021) 5|''The High Republic'' (2021) 5]]</ref>
