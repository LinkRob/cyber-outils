The '''A1 Containment Field''' was a model of [[containment field]] manufactured by [[Gordarl Weaponsmiths]] and sold for 7,500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. It could be installed on some large [[starship]]s, and could restrain a single individual in its [[stasis field]].<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />

==Behind the scenes==
The A1 Containment Field was first mentioned in [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']],<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> a [[2017]] supplement for the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="A Fistful of Credits">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/2/2/a-fistful-of-credits|text=A Fistful of Credits}}</ref>
