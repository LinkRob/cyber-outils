{{Quote|He is one of the most respected Jedi in the Order, his name mentioned in the same breath as Masters Brisbane, Maota, and Yoda.|Stellan Gios, about Porter Engle|<ref name="TRS" />}}
'''Brisbane''' was a [[Jedi Master]] who was part of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. By that [[time]], they had become widely respected among [[Jedi]] ranks. During the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]'s [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]]<ref name="TRS" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Jedi Master [[Stellan Gios]] remarked on Jedi Master [[Porter Engle]]'s high regard within the Jedi Order, indicating that he was sometimes mentioned in the same breath as other respected Jedi such as Brisbane, Master [[Cherff Maota]], and Master [[Yoda]].<ref name="TRS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brisbane was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="TRS" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
