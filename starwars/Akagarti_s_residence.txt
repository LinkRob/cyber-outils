'''Akagarti's residence''' was the personal residence of [[Ephor]] [[Emoch Akagarti]] and his family on the [[planet]] [[Affadar]]. During the [[mission to Affadar]], the [[Dreamers]] infiltrated Akagarti's residence in order to take Emoch to the local [[Affadar water purification plant|water purification plant]] where they could ruin his reputation by exposing his true nature to the people of Affadar.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
