"'''Bell and the Band'''" is the second part of the [[Scrapping for a Song / Bell and the Band|fourth episode]] of the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Three|third season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''. It was released on [[Disney+]] on [[December 8]], [[2025]].

==Official description==
''[[Kai Brightstar|Kai]] and [[Bell Zettifar|Bell]] must rescue [[Ember]].''

==Plot summary==
===Bell's vioddle===
[[Jedi Initiate]] [[Kai Brightstar]] and [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] disembark from a [[Jedi Order|Jedi]] [[shuttle]] outside a [[Unidentified town (Bogachi)|town]] on [[Bogachi]]. After tasking the [[astromech droid]] [[Beepers]] and Zettifar's pet [[charhound]] [[Ember]] guard the [[starship|ship]], Zettifar reveals to Brightstar that the case he has been carrying contains his damaged [[vioddle]], which he is taking to a [[music]] shop to be repaired. Brightstar is surprised his mentor is a [[musician]], and asks him if learning an instrument is part of [[Jedi training]]. Zettifar tells the younger [[human]] that he simply plays for fun and the enjoyment of music. Zettifar is greeted by the shop's [[Nautolan]] owner, [[Jasa Plynn]], whom he introduces to Brightstar. 

Plynn examines at the cracked vioddle and says it will be easy to repair. Brightstar also befriends Plynn's two-tailed [[feline]] pet [[Flim (creature)|Flim]]. As Brightstar pets the creature, his eyes suddenly turn [[color|blue]] and he marches out of the store, to the shock and dismay of Plynn. Brightstar and Zettifar follow Flim outside and discover that all of the other domesticated animals of the town are all marching in a trance-like state through the streets.

===The rogue musician===
Beepers contacts the Jedi via [[holoprojector|holo]] and tells them that Ember is leaving the ship. When they return to the starship, they find that Ember is also trapped in a trance. The Jedi and Beepers follow the entranced Ember. Plynn, finished with the repairs, sees the strange behavior and decides to follow the Jedi and Beepers. The four follow the animals into a clearing, where they find a starship. Nearby, an [[alien]] plays a [[kloo horn]] atop a large speaker, the creatures marching around him. Zettifar and Brightstar ask the musician to what is going on, he reveals that the animals are his biggest fans. With his [[sound wave modulator]], he has made them follow his beck and call and do whatever he wants. 

The kloo horn player introduces himself as [[Newf Grayson]] and says that he wants the creatures, his adoring fans, to be his "captive audience". Grayson tells them to leave so he can continue his concert, but Zettifar proclaims they are not leaving until Grayson frees the creatures from their trance. This upsets Grayson, and he turns the creatures to stare down Zettifar and Brightstar, blocking their path. The Jedi task themselves with shutting down the sound wave modulator. As Zettifar assesses the scene, however, he makes eye contact with, Ember. The two approach one another. His charhound growls at Zettifar, who is dismayed that his pet does not seem to recognize him, despite his pleas. The Padawan turns around, rejoining Brightstar to strategize. 

===Rescuing the animals===
Brightstar comes up with an idea for Zettifar to challenge Grayson to a musical duel with his vioddle. Meanwhile, Brightstar will flank the creatures and disable the sound wave modulator. Grayson accepts the challenge. Zettifar has Plynn lay down a beat on Beeper's dome while he jams on his vioddle, fully distracting Grayson. In response, Grayson plays a groovy countermelody. Zettifar plays another tune to counter Grayson's music, impressing the alien musician. Brightstar nearly reaches the sound wave modulator, but accidentally bumps into a baby [[bantha]], drawing Grayson's attention. 

Angry at their deception, Grayson uses his kloo horn to summon the animals to encircle Brighstar and Ember to snatch Zettifar's vioddle. Grayson prepares to order the creatures onto his ship. However, Brightstar uses [[the Force]] to [[telekinesis|twist]] the sound wave modulator's volume setting to its highest level. When Grayson plays a note with his kloo horn, the machine overloads, causing it to explode, freeing the animals from their trance and launching Grayson into a nearby [[bush]]. 

===Grayson turns a new leaf===
Ember and Flim reunite with their owners. The creatures growl at Grayson. Zettifar returns Grayson's kloo horn to him and tells him that he has to rely on building an audience organically instead of using tricks to gain a captive audience. A penitent Grayson apologizes for his deceptive ways and hopes to jam with Zettifar again. Zettifar says they will have to return the animals to their owners first. Grayson apologizes to Plynn for trying to steal Flim. Plynn accepts and offers to tune Grayson's kloo horn back at the shop. When Zettifar asks Brightstar if he is interested in learning a musical instrument, Brightstar tells the older Jedi he may prefer a less exciting hobby, to which Zettifar proposes [[fishing]].
