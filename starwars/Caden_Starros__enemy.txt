A [[Gender|male]] individual was, according to a story of the [[Starros Clan]], the '''enemy''' of [[Caden Starros]], the [[Parent|great-grandfather]] of the [[human]] female [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]]. According to the story, the man [[Thief|stole]] Caden's [[Starship|ship]] and left him stranded on a [[Unidentified minor moon|minor moon]]. Caden eventually tracked the enemy to<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> the [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Cad Bane">{{Databank|cad-bane-biography-gallery|Cad Bane Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 18}}</ref> [[Orondia]] to take his revenge.<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> By [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> Avon had been told the story.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the girl asked the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Imri Cantaros]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] if they did not want to [[Gwishi|find]] [[Klinith Da|out]] who had caused the [[Attack on the Steady Wing|death]] of the former's [[Jedi Master]], [[Douglas Sunvale]]. Avon mentally noted she did not believe the Jedi would not want revenge, though if someone she loved had been hurt, the only thing she would want was revenge. The girl then recalled the story about Caden and his enemy, noting that whether or not the story was real, it sounded more sensible to her than forgetting about being wronged.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

==Behind the scenes==
The enemy of Caden Starros was mentioned in the [[2021]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published as part of<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
