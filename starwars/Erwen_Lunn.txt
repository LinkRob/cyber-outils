'''Erwen Lunn''' was a [[Sullustan]] [[Gender|male]] and the father of [[Gala Key Arena]] worker [[Lyal Lunn]]. The Lunn family had lived on [[Kwenn]] at least since Erwen's great-grandparents were children, when they had watched the [[Grand Renewal]] of Kwenn. His grandparents had built a house on [[Zyboh Key]] with a view of the brazier in the [[Kwenn Jedi outpost|Jedi outpost]] on [[Sanctuary Mount]], and even when Erwen's wife had the opportunity to leave Kwenn for her job, she quit in order to stay and raise Lyal there.<ref name="The Living Force" />

Lyal was his father's caretaker in his later years, when Erwen became ill and disabled. He modified their flat on [[Addoa Key]] to accommodate his father's reduced mobility, and attempted to earn money for a [[medical droid]] by selling drugs for the [[Filthy Credits]] [[pirate]] gang. After an attempt on Lyal's life, he asked [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] make sure his father was taken care of. Yoda spent most of a day with Erwen, who recognized the Master from the [[hologram]]s his great-grandparents had made during the Grand Renewal. He asked Yoda many questions about the past, and then spoke about his family history and his love for Lyal. Erwen was growing weaker, and Yoda realized he was dying. In his last moments, Yoda helped him sit up so he could see the [[Undying Flame]] in the Sanctuary Mount brazier.<ref name="The Living Force" />

After Erwen's death, the medical droid that Lyal had finally been able to purchase carried Erwen's body out of the flat. Lyal planned to hold a memorial service at the [[Paths of Harmony]] once he healed from his injuries. Later, Yoda introduced Lyal to the journalist and technical engineer [[Morna]], who gave her the Lunns' holograms. Morna planned to use the holos, which had historical imagery never seen before, in a [[holofeed]] program that would help the people of Kwenn tell their stories.<ref name="The Living Force" />
