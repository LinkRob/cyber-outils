'''Baklek Base''' was a large [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Military|military]] base<ref name="Thrawn" /> located in the [[Baklek system]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories]],<ref name="Appendix" /> near the [[space station]] [[Lansend Twenty-Six]] in order to provide quick assistance to the station, due to it being used as a holding location for [[Slavery|slaves]] en route to the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. During the battle of [[Lansend Twenty-Six|Lansed Twenty-Six]] Commodore [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] requested assistance from this military base.<ref name="Thrawn" />
