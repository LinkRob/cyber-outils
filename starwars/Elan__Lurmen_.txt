'''Elan''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Lurmen]] who lived on the [[planet]] [[Qalydon]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. She was the [[Marriage|wife]] of [[Unidentified Lurmen village|her village's]] [[chief]], [[Biz]], and the [[Parent|mother]] of [[Maynar]], who helped the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] in protecting their home from the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

==Biography==
Elan lived with her husband [[Chief]] [[Biz]] and their daughter [[Maynar]] in a [[Lurmen]] [[Unidentified Lurmen village|village]] on the [[planet]] [[Qalydon]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. Her family had inhabited the village for generations where they worshiped [[the Force]] using [[kyber crystal]]s.<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

Prior to the [[Battle of Endor]], the Freemakers visited the planet seeking [[kyber crystal]]s for their [[Quest to build the Arrowhead|quest]] to build the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']] starship in order to turn the tide of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Elan initially thought the offworlders' starship ''[[StarScavenger]]'' was lost and disagreed with [[Ymojin]]'s suggestion that they had come for a marriage.<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

When the Freemakers revealed that they were seeking kyber crystals, the Lurmen including Elan reacted with hostility, believing that the offworlders were [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]]. Elan and some of the villagers attacked them with catapults. When the [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]] remarked that he thought that Lurmen were not violent, Elan countered that some of them were and fired a second salvo at [[Zander Freemaker]]. Zander's sister [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]] managed to bring an end to the hostilities by convincing the Lurmen that they were not from the Empire.<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

Later that night, Elan and her husband Biz hosted the offworlders and told them about their history and customs. Elan explained that much of their tribe had given up their crystals and thought their way of life was dying out. Biz and Elan were unwilling to give up their crystals but were unable to offer information on where the Freemakers could find other crystals. She was also curious about Roger, asking if he was indeed a metal [[droid]].<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

Shortly later, the village was raided by the [[Imperial officer]]s [[Durpin]], [[Plumestriker]], and a contingent of [[stormtrooper]]s. The Imperials confiscated their kyber crystal necklaces for the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. During the occupation, Elan defied the thin-skinned Durpin by calling him a "scum weasel" and "[[nerf herder]]." Before the Imperials could do further damage, Kordi and Zander's [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] and Maynar used the ''StarScavenger''{{'s}} [[laser cannon]]s to drive away the Imperials. The Imperials escaped on their [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] but Rowan shot it down over the mountains.<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

While the Freemakers undertook a [[Mission to Qalydon|mission]] to recover the stolen crystals, Roger helped Biz to repair a damaged wall. Elan called her daughter Maynar for help. However, Maynar had stowed away with the Freemakers to recover the crystals. Despite being held hostage by the Imperial [[Hunter droid/LEGO|hunter droid]] [[M-OC/LEGO|M-OC]], the Freemakers managed to rescue Maynar and recover the crystals. Elan and her husband welcomed the Freemakers home. Though Biz offered the Freemakers the crystals, Rowan was unwilling to deprive them of their relics and left peacefully.<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

==Personality and traits==
Elan was a [[Gender|female]] [[Lurmen]] with brown eyes and grey skin. She was a fighter who knew how to use catapults. Elan also had a feisty spirit and defied Durpin and his Imperial troops when they occupied their village. Elan shared her husband Chief Biz's appreciation for her people's kyber crystals, which they used to commune with the Force. She admired Rowan when he refused to take her people's crystals and remarked that he had a feisty spirit.<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />

==Behind the scenes==
Elan first appeared in the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' [[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures Season Two|Season Two]] episode "[[The Lost Crystals of Qalydon]]," which premiered on [[Disney XD]] on [[August 7]], [[2017]]. She was voiced by [[Pamela Adlon]], who also voiced [[Ymojin]].<ref name="The Lost Crystals of Qalydon" />
