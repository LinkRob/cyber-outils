'''Fort Tusken''' was a settlement on [[Tatooine]] that was founded some eight [[Standard decade|decades]] prior to the [[Proclamation of the New Order|proclamation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="HC11">{{HelmetCollectionCite|11|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Situated on the edge of the Badlands of the [[Jundland Wastes]], it almost immediately faced attacks from the local [[Tusken Raider|Sand People]]. The settlers experienced considerable loss of life and goods, until they ultimately decided to abandon Fort Tusken. From that point on, the settlers of Tatooine referred to the Sand People as "Tusken Raiders."<ref name="HC11" />
