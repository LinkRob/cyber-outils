"'''Endgame'''" is an adventure in ''[[Endgame]]'', a supplement for [[The DarkStryder Campaign (series)|''The DarkStryder Campaign'']]. It is set in [[8 ABY/Legends|8 ABY]].

{{Game-stub}}

==Plot summary==
The ''[[FarStar]]''{{'s}} mission to track down [[Moff/Legends|Moff]] [[Kentor Sarne]] draws to a close. The crew and their allies face the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Warlord/Legends|warlord]] and the mysterious [[DarkStryder]] in [[Battle of Kathol System|battle]] over the remote [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Kathol (planet)|Kathol]].
