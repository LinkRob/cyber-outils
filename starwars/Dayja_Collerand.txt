'''Dayja Collerand''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[major]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Security Bureau]]. As a favor for [[Grand Admiral]] [[Balanhai Savit]], Collerand went undercover, using the alias "'''Mole'''," in the [[City|town]] of [[Tiquwe]] on [[Aloxor]] in order to learn about [[pirate]] gangs. During their [[mission to Aloxor]], he encountered [[Lieutenant]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]], [[Assistant Director]] [[Brierly Ronan]] and two [[death trooper]]s, [[Pik]] and [[Waffle]], who had been captured by criminals while attempting to look into a conspiracy involving stolen components intended for [[Project Stardust]]. Collerand eventually rescued them from the criminals and helped them with their mission. A short time later, during a [[skirmish against the Third Fleet]], he helped [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] arrest [[Balanhai Savit]], a [[treason]]ous Grand [[Admiral]] who had been stealing the components from Stardust.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />
