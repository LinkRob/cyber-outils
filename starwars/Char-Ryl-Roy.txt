'''Char-Ryl-Roy''' was a [[Cerean]] [[Gender|male]] [[Jedi Master]] who served on the [[Jedi High Council]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Roy, along with fellow [[Jedi]] Master [[Creighton Sun]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]], and Roy's [[Padawan]] [[Enya Keen]] traveled to [[Eiram]] aboard the [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] ''[[Valiant]]'' on a mission to deliver medical supplies. Char-Ryl-Roy wielded a [[Char-Ryl-Roy's lightsaber|green-bladed lightsaber]].<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:OrlenMolloCereanJedi-TheChancellors.png|thumb|left|200px|Orlen Mollo with Char-Ryl-Roy]]
Char-Ryl-Roy appeared in the [[2022]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Convergence" /> In the ''[[Characters of Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' episode "The Chancellors," the [[Cerean]] [[Jedi]] is pictured speaking with [[Orlen Mollo]] while [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]] can be seen in the background.<ref name="CotHR">{{SWYouTube|series=Characters of Star Wars: The High Republic|text=The Chancellors|video=w54C6NHFasc|sw_url=video/the-chancellors-characters-of-the-high-republic}}</ref> A picture of Char-Ryl-Roy in ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]'' later confirmed that the Jedi from ''[[Characters of Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' was indeed Char-Ryl-Roy.<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi" />

He was originally planned to be named Charl Roy, however it was changed to Char-Ryl-Roy to match similar naming structure with other Cereans.<ref name="Art of THR">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref>
