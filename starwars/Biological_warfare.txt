'''Biological warfare''' was the use of infectious agents<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> known as [[bioweapon]]s, such as neurotoxins and defoliants,<ref name="BTC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> to kill living organisms as an act of war.<ref name="Catalyst" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Doctor]] [[Nuvo Vindi]] recreated and modified the long-eradicated [[Blue Shadow Virus]] [[disease]] as part of the research effort on [[Jedi]]-proof biological weapons.<ref name="Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> Later during the same conflict, [[Morseerian]] [[scientist]]s worked to develop an infectious agent against the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="Catalyst" />

After [[Cloning|clone]] trooper [[Tup]] [[Death|killed]] [[Jedi General]] [[Tiplar]] during the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]] for no reason that the [[Jedi]] and other clones could discern, Tiplar's sister [[Tiplee]] and [[General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] wondered if his behavior could have been caused by an [[anti-clone virus]] that the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] had been rumored to be developing.<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Galactic Empire]] used the [[planet]] [[Coyerti]] as a testing and development ground for biological weapons.<ref name="BTC" />
