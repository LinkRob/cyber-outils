The '''Battle of Oetchi''', also known as the '''retreat to Crait''' or the '''attack on the Resistance fleet''', took place in [[34 ABY]] when the [[First Order]] attacked the [[Resistance]] within the [[Oetchi system]], intent on destroying the insurgents that opposed their [[First Order-Resistance War|conquest]] of [[the galaxy]]. Owing to [[Active tracker|technological]] innovations pioneered by [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] under [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] direction, the [[First Order military]] had the ability to [[Hyperspace tracker|track]] the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]] through [[hyperspace]]. Possessing enough [[fuel]] for only one more jump to [[lightspeed]], the Resistance searched for another way to escape from the First Order.

[[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] took charge of the Resistance after an [[Kylo Ren's personal squadron|attack]] on the [[command ship]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] incapacitated [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. While Holdo planned to sacrifice the Resistance's [[starship]]s in order to hide the survivors on [[Crait]], [[Captain]] [[Poe Dameron]] took matters into his own hands by devising a plan to save the [[fleet]]. [[Finn]] and [[Rose Tico]] therefore [[Mission to Canto Bight|traveled]] to [[Canto Bight]] on [[Cantonica]], intent on recruiting the [[Master Codebreaker]] so that they could disable the First Order's [[active tracker]]. They were instead joined by [[DJ]], a [[slicer]] who agreed to work for the Resistance in exchange for compensation.

Meanwhile, [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] boarded the ''[[Supremacy]]'', [[flagship]] of the [[First Order Navy]], and was taken [[prisoner]] by her rival [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]. Following her short and unconventional [[Jedi training|training]] under [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]], Rey left the [[planet]] [[Ahch-To]] intent on confronting Ren in person. The [[Force-bond|bond]] between them in [[the Force]] had shown Rey that there was still [[Light side of the Force|light]] within the former Ben Solo, leading her to believe that the [[Knights of Ren|dark warrior]] was in fact the key to defeating the First Order.

Finn and Tico also made it to the [[dreadnought]] with DJ's assistance. However, they were caught and apprehended by Captain [[Phasma]]'s [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]], leading the slicer to betray his allies in order to save himself. Dameron, whose conflict with Holdo came to a head, staged a [[Treason|mutiny]] and took control of ''Raddus''. Although he secured control of the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], Organa regained consciousness and subdued the [[Resistance pilot]], thus ending the insurrection.

By then, Kylo Ren had taken Rey directly to his [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] master, Snoke, who [[torture]]d and [[Mind probe (Force power)|interrogated]] Rey in order to learn the location of Skywalker's [[First Jedi Temple|hermitage]]. Having gained the knowledge he sought, Snoke ordered Ren to [[Death penalty|execute]] Rey, but Ren instead saved her by [[Death|killing]] his master. They stood together as allies against the late Snoke's [[Praetorian Guard|Elite Praetorian Guards]], all of whom fell in battle against both the [[Jedi]] and [[Ren|Master of the Knights of Ren]]. When Rey beseeched Ren to call off the First Order's attack on the Resistance, Ren revealed he had no intention of saving his [[Parent|mother's]] insurgents and invited Rey to rule the [[galaxy]] at his side. Disillusioned, Rey rejected Ren's entreaty and decided to save the Resistance on her own.

After the crew of the ''Raddus'' embarked to the [[Crait outpost]] via several [[transport]]s, Holdo sacrificed her life by piloting the [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] directly through the ''Supremacy'' at lightspeed, cutting the [[Star Dreadnought]] in half. In the ensuing chaos, Kylo Ren stepped forth to claim the mysterious and enigmatic Snoke empty [[throne]], although he had to [[Force choke|intimidate]] General [[Armitage Hux]] into recognizing his ascension as the new Supreme Leader. After Ren secured Hux's allegiance, they personally oversaw the [[First Order Army]]'s deployment to Crait. Vowing to forge a new galactic order, Ren [[Battle of Crait|invaded Crait]] with the intention of ending the war once and for all. As such, he instructed his [[army]] to give no quarter nor take any prisoners.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|They can't get away, Supreme Leader. We have them tied on the end of a string.|Armitage Hux, to Snoke|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
In the aftermath of the [[Battle of Starkiller Base|destruction]] of [[Starkiller Base]], the [[First Order military|forces]] of the [[First Order]] attacked the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar]]. Having failed to annihilate the [[planet]] before the [[superweapon]] was destroyed, the [[First Order Navy]] deployed a [[fleet]] of [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers]] along with the [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought]] ''[[Fulminatrix]]''. While the [[Resistance]] [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuated]] their headquarters, [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] engaged the [[dreadnought]] alone, using his [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] to clear the surface [[turret]]s before ordering a [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|bombing]] run in defiance of orders from [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who wished to focus on retreating from the battle. Though his plan succeeded in destroying the [[warship]], the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]] sustained heavy losses before escaping into [[hyperspace]]. Dameron was consequently demoted to the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[Captain]] by Organa, who reprimanded the [[pilot]] for his actions and insubordination.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:ScorchZetaLeader-SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|The First Order deployed a fleet against the Resistance, determined to wipe out Leia Organa's insurgency once and for all.]]
General [[Armitage Hux]] was likewise admonished by his [[Commanding officer|superior]], [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]], who [[Telekinesis|telekinetically]] assaulted the [[First Order officer]] by way of [[the Force]]. In an effort to assuage the Supreme Leader's anger, Hux revealed that the First Order had developed the [[hyperspace tracker]], a technological breakthrough that would allow their forces to [[Active tracker|track]] the Resistance's movement through hyperspace and determine their likeliest destination. Snoke was pleased with his general's plan, though less so with his apprentice, the [[Knights of Ren|dark warrior]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], who returned to his master shortly after the loss of Starkiller Base. Where he once considered Ren the natural heir of his legendary [[Skywalker family|grandfather]], the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], Snoke berated Ren for failing to defeat [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], a [[Gender|girl]] with no prior experience in [[lightsaber combat]]. In losing his master's favor, Ren flew into a rage and destroyed his [[Kylo Ren's helmet|combat helmet]], and then instructed his subordinates to prepare his [[Kylo Ren's TIE silencer|personal starfighter]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==The battle==
===Chasing the Resistance fleet===
{{Quote|They tracked us through lightspeed.''"<br />"''That's impossible.''"<br />"''Yes. And they've done it.|Leia Organa and Finn|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:TIE_attack_on_the_Resistance_fleet.png|thumb|right|250px|Though the Resistance escaped the attack on D'Qar, the First Order successfully tracked their ships through hyperspace, bombarding the fleet with ships and starfighters.]]
Upon entering the [[Oetchi system]]<ref name="TLJ Junior Novel" /> via hyperspace, the Resistance immediately set to work on locating a suitable new base when they were set upon by a fleet of First Order [[Star Destroyer]]s,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> which began the Battle of Oetchi.<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> Comprising thirty ''Resurgent''-class [[Starship|vessels]] and the Supreme Leader's [[flagship]], the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]'',<ref name="TLJ VD" /> the First Order fleet destroyed the [[Vakbeor-class cargo frigate|''Vakbeor''-class cargo frigate]]<ref name="ICS" /> ''[[Vigil]]'',<ref name="TLJ VD" /> before it could activate its [[deflector shield]]s. Though the means were unknown to them at the [[time]], the Resistance deduced that the First Order had discovered a way to track ships through hyperspace, making another jump to [[lightspeed]] futile as well as a waste of [[fuel]]. Despite demotion from [[Wing Commander]], Dameron volunteered to lead a counterattack in order to defend the Resistance [[command ship]], the [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']]. At the same time, Organa instructed the remaining ships in her fleet to strengthen their rear shields and move out of range of the Star Destroyers.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:Predictive_Shot_SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|Kylo Ren piloted his personal TIE silencer during the attack on the Resistance flagship, but was unable to open fire on his mother.]]
Before the [[Resistance pilot]]s could scramble their [[starfighter]]s, the ''Raddus''<nowiki/>' [[hangar]] was destroyed by a [[torpedo]] fired from Kylo Ren's [[TIE/vn space superiority fighter]]. Several pilots died in the attack, including [[Tallissan Lintra]], although Dameron narrowly survived. Following his successful assault on the hangar, Ren had an opportunity to destroy the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of the command ship but found himself unable to open fire on his [[Parent|mother]], General Organa.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> However, a member of [[Kylo Ren's personal squadron]], [[Lieutenant]] [[Poldin LeHuse]],<ref name="TLJ VD" /> held nothing back and landed a direct hit on the bridge,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> desiring to make the Resistance suffer for the destruction of Starkiller Base. The attack obliterated the [[Resistance High Command]], killing [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] and other [[Resistance officer]]s with the exception of Organa, who called upon the Force to survive the vacuum of [[Realspace|space]] and return to the ''Raddus''. General Hux insisted that Ren return to the ''Supremacy'', which could no longer protect his squadron due to the distance between the First Order and the Resistance ships. Although Ren complied, his squadmates were shot down by the [[Free Virgillia-class Bunkerbuster|''Free Virgillia''-class Bunkerbuster]]<ref name="TLJ VD" /> ''[[Ninka]]''.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:Amilyn_Holdo_Ellen.png|thumb|right|250px|Vice Admiral Amilyn Holdo took charge of the Resistance while General Leia Organa recovered from her injuries.]]
With Organa incapacitated and the other high-ranking [[officer]]s killed in the attack, command of the Resistance defaulted to [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]],<ref name="Episode VIII" /> whose brusque manner and insistence on secrecy led her into conflict with Dameron.<ref name="Holdo DB">{{Databank|amilyn-holdo|Amilyn Holdo}}</ref> Shut out from the Admiral's plans, Dameron devised his own plan to save the fleet with the help of [[Resistance technician]] [[Rose Tico]] and the renegade [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Finn]]. Using the information that Finn acquired from his past service aboard the ''Supremacy'', the trio intended to disable the hyperspace tracker so that the Resistance could jump to lightspeed without fear of being tracked. Their plan required the service of a [[Master Codebreaker]], and as such, they contacted Maz Kanata, who pointed them to the [[casino]] [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on [[Cantonica]]. Time was of the essence, however, as the Resistance fleet was running dangerously low on their fuel supply.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:Poe_meets_Holdo.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Unsatisfied with Holdo's approach as a leader, Captain Poe Dameron devised a plot that undermined the Vice Admiral's authority.]]
The three remaining Resistance ships continued to outrun the larger First Order vessels, their speed and strong shields able to keep them just out of range enough for the ''Supremacy''{{'s}} turbolasers to be ineffective. However, their fuel reserves were dangerously low, and one by one, the other ships ran out of fuel and were immediately destroyed once they had drifted into range of the enemy. The [[Nebulon-C escort frigate]], [[Anodyne (Nebulon-C)|''Anodyne'']] which was also their medical frigate, was the first to run dry and was shortly followed by Holdo's former ship, the ''Ninka''.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Even though, both their crews successfully managed to evacuate their doomed craft before their destruction. This left the ''Raddus'' as the sole cruiser left in the fleet, carrying what now amounted to the entire Resistance. Vice Admiral Holdo revealed her plan to evacuate the Resistance on to [[U-55 orbital loadlifter|small transports]]. Believing the action to be too risky and foolish, Dameron incited a mutiny and had the leadership held at gunpoint by at least [[Nodin Chavdri|three]] [[Pammich Nerro Goode|Resistance]] [[C'ai Threnalli|members]] as he, [[Kaydel Ko Connix]], and two other resistance members went to contact Finn and Tico. The two had found a codebreaker named [[DJ]] who successfully got them onto the ''Supremacy''.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Infiltrating the ''Supremacy''===
{{Quote|FN-2187. So good to have you back.|Phasma, to Finn|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:UndercoverFO.png|thumb|right|250px|DJ helped Finn and Rose Tico in their efforts to infiltrate the ''Supremacy'', and subsequently sold them out to the First Order.]]
Meanwhile, Finn, Tico, DJ, and [[BB-8]] infiltrated the ''Supremacy'' and got into the room where they could disable the tracker. However, a First Order astromech droid, [[BB-9E]], had found the group out earlier and called in reinforcements, causing them to be confronted by Captain [[Phasma]] and her [[First Order stormtrooper|troops]]. However, after being caught by Phasma's troops, DJ told the First Order the Resistance's plan, which he had overheard Finn and Tico mention, in exchange for money.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Finn and Tico were about to be executed by the First Order when the sudden destruction of the ''Supremacy'' sent everyone into chaos. The duo freed themselves and attempted to make for a nearby shuttle, only for Phasma and her stormtroopers to emerge from the flames and begin firing on them. Suddenly, an [[First Order All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]] started firing on Phasma and her troops, soon revealed to be piloted by BB-8. In the chaos, Finn engaged Phasma with a [[Z6 riot control baton]], and was initially overpowered, but he eventually managed to hit Phasma in a surprise attack before she fell into a fiery pit to her death. Finn, Tico and BB-8 then hijacked a shuttle and escaped the remains of the ''Supremacy'', heading toward the Resistance base on Crait.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Mutiny on the ''Raddus''===
{{Quote|Vice Admiral Holdo, I am relieving you of your command for the survival of this ship, its crew and the Resistance.|Poe Dameron, to Amilyn Holdo|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:Amilyn_Holdo_TLJ.png|thumb|left|180px|Holdo lost control of the ''Raddus'' when Dameron staged a mutiny to seize the ship.]]
Aboard the ''Raddus'', Holdo fought back against Dameron's mutiny as she and her allies stunned his allies. As Dameron heard about the capture, General Organa broke into the room he locked himself in and stunned him, returning him to Holdo as the Resistance fighters prepared to evacuate the ''Raddus''. Holdo decided to stay onboard to confront the First Order in an act of self-sacrifice, and when Dameron came to, Organa revealed that they were nearing an old rebel base on the planet [[Crait]] and that Holdo had devised the plan because she knew that the First Order would be less likely to spot their smaller escape ships.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> The retreat to Crait was regarded as a desperate gamble even by former rebel officers who had lived through the [[Battle of Crait]], [[Battle of Hoth|flight from Hoth]], and [[reinforcement of Thila]].<ref name="NewComplete">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref>

[[File:HoldoManeuver-TheLastJedi5.png|thumb|right|250px|The ''Supremacy'' meets its end during the Battle of Oetchi]]
Having learned of the Resistance's plan from DJ, the First Order fired the ''Supremacy''{{'s}} [[turbolaser]]s batteries at the unprotected escape vessels, destroying all but six of them. They saw that the ''Raddus'' was preparing to jump to hyperspace, but believed the cruiser was deserting the Resistance. Once he realized the ship was turning towards the ''Supremacy'', a frantic General Hux ordered to fire all cannons on the ''Raddus'', but it was too late. Holdo jumped into hyperspace, shearing the massive ship in half and completely destroying<ref name="Episode VIII" /> twenty<ref name="Poe 31" /> other Star Destroyers on escort duty or being repaired on one of the lower hangars.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Duel aboard the ''Supremacy''===
{{Quote|Ben. When we touched hands, I saw your future. Just the shape of it, but solid and clear. You will not bow before Snoke. You'll turn. I'll help you.|Rey, to Ben Solo|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:Force-bond.png|thumb|right|250px|Their destinies entangled, Kylo Ren and Rey shared a Force-bond that made it possible for them to touch across the light-years.]]
While the First Order pursued the Resistance fleet, Rey and Kylo Ren discovered that they shared a powerful [[Force-bond]] with each other.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Their connection was raw and unprecedented; it not only made it easier to sense the other's thoughts and emotions, but also allowed them to see and hear each other across a distance spanning [[light-year]]s.<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> The bond, which both Ren and Rey became aware of to an extent upon their [[Battle of Takodana|first encounter]],<ref name="Kylo Ren">{{Databank|kylo-ren|Supreme Leader Kylo Ren}}</ref><ref name="Rey">{{Databank|rey|Rey}}</ref> was manipulated by Snoke, who bridged the minds of his apprentice and the [[scavenger]] from [[Jakku]], knowing that Rey would be drawn to the conflict within Ren. At the time neither Ren nor Rey were aware of Snoke's machinations,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> and the animosity Rey felt towards the dark apprentice gradually gave way to a new understanding as their bond granted her insights into Ren that not even her reluctant mentor, [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]], could see.<ref name="TLJ VD" /> Sensing that their destinies were somehow entangled, Rey confided her fears and sadness to Ren, having sensed that he also suffered from feelings of solitude and abandonment.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> But they assured each other that neither of them was alone, and through their bond, discovered that they could physically touch hands across the [[star]]s.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> In that moment, Rey was convinced that good still existed within the former Ben Solo, and as such she regarded him as the galaxy's last hope.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

[[File:Rey_and_Ben_Solo.png|thumb|left|250px|Rey confronted Kylo Ren in person, intent on turning the former Ben Solo back to the light.]]
When Skywalker refused to end his [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] on [[Ahch-To]], Rey sought out Ren on her own, hoping he would renounce the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] and embrace the [[Light side of the Force|light]] with her help.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Although Ren exulted at the way that Rey left Skywalker to join him aboard the ''Supremacy'',<ref name="Kylo Ren" /> he brought her to the Supreme Leader who wished to interrogate the [[prisoner]] himself. As they made their way to [[Snoke's throne room]], Rey implored Ren to abandon his journey down the dark path, having sensed Ren's conflict and how it caused him so much pain.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> Her confidence was further bolstered by the [[Force vision]] she experienced during their last connection, explaining that when they touched hands she saw a future where Ren no longer bowed before Snoke. However, Ren also had a vision of his own, one where he saw Rey standing by his side.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:ActOfBetrayal-SoH.png|thumb|right|200px|Supreme Leader Snoke was killed by Kylo Ren, who saved Rey by turning against his dark master.]]
Rey's confrontation with Snoke led to her<ref name="Episode VIII" /> brutal<ref name="Rey" /> interrogation by way of the Supreme Leader's [[Force power]]s, and as a result the location of Luke Skywalker's [[First Jedi Temple|hermitage]] was successfully [[Mind probe (Force power)|probed]] from her mind. Snoke, who intended to kill the last [[Jedi]] after dealing with the more immediate threat of the Resistance, held Rey in stasis and commanded Ren to kill her, exclaiming that this act would be the fulfillment of his dark destiny.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Ren's emotions were even further churned up by the bond between himself and Rey,<ref name="Kylo Ren" /> but his newfound resolve and clarity reassured Snoke that his oft-conflicted apprentice would not give into sentiment again.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> In the end, Snoke's visions and Force senses failed him for he did not see his end at the hands of his own disciple.<ref name="Snoke">{{Databank|supreme-leader-snoke|Supreme Leader Snoke}}</ref> Through guile and subterfuge,<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> Ren cleaved Snoke in half with [[Skywalker lightsaber|Rey's lightsaber]], ending his master's life<ref name="Snoke" /> in an act worthy of the [[Sith]] [[Rule of Two]].<ref name="The Dark Side">''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side]]''</ref>

[[File:ReyKyloVsGuards.png|thumb|left|250px|Rey and Kylo Ren stood together, united against the fallen Supreme Leader's Praetorian Guards.]]
With the death of their Supreme Leader, the [[Praetorian Guard|Elite Praetorian Guard]] encircled and engaged the two [[Force-sensitive]]s,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> attempting to avenge their slain master.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> Kylo Ren and Rey drew their [[lightsaber]]s and stood together as allies<ref name="Kylo Ren" /><ref name="Rey" /> against the [[Color|crimson]] [[armor]]ed [[Bodyguard|sentinels]]. Despite the Guard's efforts, all eight were killed in battle.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> But while Ren had indeed turned against Snoke,<ref name="Rey" /> killing the Supreme Leader in order to save Rey,<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> the now former apprentice had no intention of turning from the darkness.<ref name="Kylo Ren" /> As Rey beseeched him to call off the First Order's attack on the remaining Resistance [[transport]]s,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Ren saw a chance to fulfill<ref name="Kylo Ren" /> what he believed was his birthright: ruling over the weaker [[Species|beings]] of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

[[File:Mine_mine_mine.png|thumb|right|250px|Neither Kylo Ren nor Rey would renounce their allegiance to the dark side and the light, causing the two Force prodigies to become enemies once again.]]
Once more, Rey implored him to resist the temptations of the dark side, hoping to reach the side of Kylo Ren that was still Ben Solo. Believing that Rey was still clinging to the past, Ren revealed to her the full truth of the vision he saw when they touched hands, that Rey was in fact the daughter of insignificant [[junk dealer]]s who sold her into [[Youngling|child]] labor before dying in the [[desert]] of Jakku. He suspected that in her [[heart]] Rey already knew the truth but had suppressed the memory of her abandonment.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Rey desperately tried to block out Ren's words, but she could sense that there was no deception in his intentions, and that he really was telling her the truth, causing tears to fall from her [[eye]]s as she slowly came to terms with her origins. Yet even though Rey came from a [[Palpatine family|family of no background]], she was not irrelevant to the [[Solo family|son]] of [[Han Solo]] and [[Princess]] Leia Organa,<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> for Ren now envisioned them ruling together over a new [[Galaxy|galactic]] order.<ref name="Kylo Ren" /> However, Rey chose to turn her back on Ren, disillusioned by his choice to remain in darkness. As a result, they struggled for control over the fabled Skywalker lightsaber that Rey had claimed as her own, causing it to split in two which rendered Ren unconscious.<ref name="Rey" />

Rey was initially tempted to end Ren's life while he was unconscious and defenseless, just as Skywalker had been on the [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|night]] his [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker|temple]] [[Fire|burned]] down. She ultimately rejected the idea, having found her faith in the Force and now believing herself and Ren to be instruments of its will. Though still disappointed that her vision had not come to pass as she saw it, Rey did not believe it was her place to decide Ren's fate. For better or worse, it was the will of the Force that he continued to live, and therefore Rey left him alive and escaped from the ''Supremacy'' using Snoke's [[Snoke's escape shuttle|private shuttle]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

==Aftermath==
===All roads lead to Crait===
{{Main|Battle of Crait}}
{{Quote|Get all our forces down to that Resistance base. Let's finish this.|Kylo Ren, to Armitage Hux|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:Long_Live_The_Supreme_Leader.png|thumb|left|250px|After Snoke's death, General Armitage Hux was forced to recognize Kylo Ren as the new Supreme Leader of the First Order.]]
General Hux hurried to Snoke's [[throne]] room following Holdo's attack on the ''Supremacy'', only to find the Supreme Leader's bisected remains along with the corpses of his Praetorian Guards.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> He also found Kylo Ren alive but unconscious, and considered ridding himself of his rival once and for all. But Ren had awakened before the general could carry out the deed,<ref name="General Hux">{{Databank|general-hux|General Hux}}</ref> forcing Hux to conceal his [[Blaster pistol|sidearm]]. When asked about what had transpired in the throne room, Ren lied to Hux by telling him that Rey was the one who killed Snoke. Hux likewise informed Ren that the girl had already left the ''Supremacy'', yet Ren was unconcerned with her whereabouts, knowing she would travel to the [[Crait outpost]] in an attempt to save the Resistance. He ordered Hux to deploy their military forces to the planet, determined to bring a decisive end to the war,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> to which Hux scoffed at Ren,<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> neither willing to recognize Ren's authority nor surrender command of the [[First Order Army|army]] to him.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Hux's indignation towards Ren gave way to fear when he suddenly found himself struggling to breathe.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> The dark warrior had begun [[Force choke|choking]] the general by way of the Force,<ref name="General Hux" /> threatening to asphyxiate Hux unless he accepted his place in the new order of things. As a result, Ren successfully claimed the mantle of Supreme Leader, having compelled Hux to recognize his claim as Snoke's successor. With that, the general complied with the new Supreme Leader's orders,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> although Ren decided to keep Hux at his side in order to discourage any treacherous actions during their [[Battle of Crait|assault on Crait]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

===Beyond the battle===
The ''Supremacy'' was abandoned by the First Order after the damage it sustained,<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> with the ''Finalizer'' instead taking over as the First Order flagship.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 1">[[Legacy of Vader 1|''Legacy of Vader'' 1]]</ref> Members of the First Order not present of the battle were not immediately aware of what had happened, believing that the Resistance had been completely destroyed during the evacuation of D'Qar and that Snoke remained the Supreme Leader. Such was the case of [[Colonel]] [[Barrut]], who was informed by Dameron of the Resistance's survival and the change of Supreme Leader during the [[Battle of Ikkrukk|Battle of Grail City]], yet Barrut insisted to her men that Dameron was lying.<ref name="Poe 31" />

Rumors of Organa's exploit during the attack made their way to the planet [[Batuu]] through an offworlder known to [[Savi]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref>
