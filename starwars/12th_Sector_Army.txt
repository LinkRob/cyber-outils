The '''12th Sector Army''', simply the '''12th''', and alternatively as '''Twelfth Army''', was a [[Sector Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] that was active during the [[Clone Wars]], serving under the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] in the northern quadrant of [[the galaxy]]. It later served under the [[Imperial Army]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], and at least one of its [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]] participated in a [[mission to Murkhana]] that was undertaken by [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]. Its insignia depicted a [[Color|cerulean]]-skinned [[Gender|female]] [[Twi'lek]] carrying a [[spear]].

==Description==
The 12th Sector Army,<ref name="Battles" /> simply the 12th,<ref name="BMF 18" /> and alternatively known as the Twelfth Army,<ref name="Insider" /> was a [[Sector Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Sector Armies [[7th Sector Army|7th]] through 12th of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] were assigned to the northern quadrant of [[the galaxy]],<ref name="BMF 18" /> and as such, was present on [[Felucia]].<ref name="BMF 21" /> As a Sector Army, the 12th<ref name="BMF 18" /> consisted of four [[corps]] for a total of 147,456 [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> As one of the twenty Sector Armies, the Twelfth Army was assigned a [[Haven-class medical station|''Haven''-class medical station]], [[Felucia Medical Station HCTFF2]], that was placed on Felucia's orbit.<ref name="Note" />

The Twelfth Army's insignia was a [[Color|cerulean]]-skinned [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]] that carried a [[spear]] within a dark circle with a yellow background.<ref name="Tarkin" /> The Twelfth Army utilized [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]] adorned with its insignia during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Insider" />

==History==
===Clone Wars===
The 12th Sector Army was active during the [[Cloning|Clone Wars]],<ref name="BMF 18" /> which lasted from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During the war, Felucia's proximity to key [[hyperspace route]]s made it an important target for both sides, and as such it became part of the Twelfth Army's campaign for the Republic during the war.<ref name="BMF 18" /> Felucia became a battleground in multiple instances throughout the war, including the [[First Battle of Felucia]] that involved the Republic's [[7th Sky Corps]], [[104th Battalion]], [[501st Legion]], and the [[Open Circle Fleet]].<ref name="Holocron Heist">{{TCW|Holocron Heist}}</ref>

Subsequently, the Republic placed [[Felucia Medical Station HCTFF2]],<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> assigned to the Twelfth Army,<ref name="Note" /> in orbit above Felucia, only for it to be [[Destruction of the Felucia medical station|destroyed]] by the Confederacy.<ref name="Collapse" /> The [[Second Battle of Felucia]] again involved the 104th and 501st, along with several clone [[tank]] [[division]]s.<ref name="Padawan Lost">{{TCW|Padawan Lost}}</ref> Republic forces drove the Separatists from the [[Core Worlds]] and [[Colonies]], chasing them into the [[Mid Rim Territories]] and the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. As such, the 12th Sector Army targeted the [[Foundry of the Confederacy]], containing [[factory]] worlds including [[Boz Pity]], [[Metalorn]], [[Saleucami]], and Felucia once again during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]]. [[Jedi General]] [[Quinlan Vos]] and his [[Quinlan Vos's unit|unit]] fought against Separatist droid General [[Linwodo]] during the [[Siege of Boz Pity]] following a [[battle on Metalorn]]. Vos and his unit then participated in the [[Siege of Saleucami]] alongside other Republic forces,<ref name="Battles" /> while the [[327th Star Corps]] participated in the [[Battle of Felucia (Outer Rim Sieges)|Battle of Felucia]].<ref name="BMF 21" />

===Imperial Era===
The Twelfth Army continued to serve the [[Galactic Empire]], the Republic's successor state, into the Imperial Era. Serving under the [[Imperial Army]], elements of the Twelfth Army took part in a [[mission to Murkhana]] under the direction of [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> A 12th Sector Army gunship, complete with the detachment's Twi'lek logo, was used by Vader and Tarkin to travel.<ref name="Insider" />
