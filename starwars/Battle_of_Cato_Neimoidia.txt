{{Quote|The Trade Federation was central to those great events&mdash;in fact, one of the final battles of the Clone Wars was fought right here on Cato Neimoidia.|A [[Neimoidian]]|<ref name="Poe Dameron 20">[[Poe Dameron 20|''Poe Dameron'' 20]]</ref>}}
The '''Battle of Cato Neimoidia''', also known as the '''capture of Cato Neimoidia''', was one of the final battles of the [[Clone Wars]], in which [[Jedi General]] [[Plo Koon]] led the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s [[442nd Siege Battalion]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] for control of the [[Neimoidian]] [[Purse world|Purse World]] of [[Cato Neimoidia]], headquarters of the [[Trade Federation]]. The [[Galactic Republic]] captured the [[planet]] in the final [[Standard hour|hours]] of the conflict. While conducting a post-battle patrol flight over a [[bridge city]], Koon was [[Death|killed]] by his [[Clone trooper|clone]] [[Wingmate|wingmen]] when [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] [[Great Jedi Purge|enacted]] [[Order 66]].

==Prelude==
[[File:AhsokaEta2.png|thumb|left|220px|Tano and Skywalker defend Cato Neimoidia from Separatist attack]]
During the [[19 BBY|final year]] of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the Neimoidian [[Purse world|Purse World]] of [[Cato Neimoidia]], [[homeworld]] of [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] member [[Nute Gunray]]<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> and headquarters of the [[Trade Federation]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> was [[Defense of Cato Neimoidia|invaded]] by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Though [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Jedi Commander|Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] led the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s [[501st Legion]] into battle against the Separatist, they were recalled to [[Coruscant]] after the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] was [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|bombed]],<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> allowing the battle to end in a Confederate victory. Having captured the planet,<ref name="Battles" /> Cato Neimoidia fell under Separatist control.<ref name="Complete Locations" /> While Skywalker and his fellow [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] would lead an [[assault on Cato Neimoidia]] in the time before the [[Battle of Anaxes]],<ref name="Grand Army Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Grand Army of the Republic}}</ref> the planet remained in Separatist hands.<ref name="Battles" />

==The battle==
{{Quote|And on Cato Neimoidia and the other Neimoidian purse worlds, our brothers are making progress every day!|[[Commander]] [[Neyo]], "[[Neyo's Almanac of Clankers]]"|<ref name="OSWM 121" />}}
[[File:OddBall-CC2237-SWZ.png|thumb|left|220px|Odd Ball flies beside Plo Koon during the battle.]]
During the [[Outer Rim Sieges]] in the final [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> the capitulation of Cato Neimoidia was identified as a prime strategic goal that would help to end the conflict.<ref name="Complete Locations" /> The [[Jedi Council]] dispatched [[Jedi Master]] [[Plo Koon]] to Cato Neimoidia<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> to lead the [[442nd Siege Battalion]],<ref name="TCW encyclopedia" /> proficient in besieging enemy fortifications,<ref name="Hasbro">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|pack=Clone Troopers|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/Clone-Trooper-Four-Pack-Black-Series-Entertainment-Earth.asp}}</ref> against the Trade Federation stronghold.<ref name="EE web" /> The [[Unstable Terrain Artillery Transport]] was deployed during the campaign and earned notoriety for its effectiveness on Cato Neimoidia.<ref name="History 58" />

A series of aerial campaigns fought over the world<ref name="SWE ARC-170" /> and in its atmosphere<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> also made up part of the battle, with [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter]]s<ref name="SWE ARC-170" /> and least one [[Clone Z-95 starfighter|Clone Z-95 Headhunter]]<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> flying under Koon's leadership.<ref name="SWE ARC-170" /> At one point, [[Davijaan]] "Odd Ball" flew alongside Koon on Cato Neimoidia.<ref name="Odd Ball card">{{FFGXW2|set=ARC-170 Starfighter Expansion Pack|cardname="Odd Ball"}}</ref> During the battle, Koon led [[starfighter]]s against a trio of Separatist [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnoughts]] and [[droid tri-fighter]]s.<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> Additionally, [[Clone Flight Squad Seven]] participated in the battle.<ref name="Battles" /> The Republic eventually captured Cato Neimoidia<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> in the final [[Standard hour|hours]] of the Clone Wars.<ref name="EE web" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Execute Order 66.''"<br />"''It will be done, my Lord.|Darth Sidious and Captain Jag|<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref>}}
[[File:Plokoonjsf.png|thumb|right|250px|Captain Jag prepares to kill Plo Koon in the aftermath of the battle.]]
Not long after the Republic victory,<ref name="CVD" /> while flying near a [[bridge city]]<ref name="ROTS" /> on a patrol flight,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Koon was unaware what was happening in the [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter|ARC-170 starfighter]] behind him, [[pilot]]ed by [[Captain]] [[Jag]]. The [[clone trooper pilot]] was contacted by [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], who ordered him to [[Great Jedi Purge|execute]] [[Order 66]]. Without hesitation, Jag adjusted his fighter and opened fire on Koon. The [[Jedi Master]]'s [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7 ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]] was hit and exploded in mid-air, [[Death|killing]] him immediately. What remained of the fighter crashed into a building on the bridge city.<ref name="ROTS" /> Much as its usage in the [[Fourth Battle of Mygeeto|late stages]] of the [[Mygeeto Campaign]] did, the UT-AT's effectiveness on Cato Neimoidia helped to cement its legacy as a useful weapon.<ref name="History 58" />
