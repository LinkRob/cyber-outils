"'''Blade Squadron: Zero Hour'''" is a short story by [[David J. Williams]] and [[Mark S. Williams]], with art by [[Chris Trevas]]. The story, published in [[Star Wars Insider 160|''Star Wars Insider'' 160]], is a sequel to the short story "[[Blade Squadron (short story)|Blade Squadron]]" and a tie-in prequel to the novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]. It takes place after the [[Battle of Endor]],focusing on the squadron's exploits during the [[Battle of Malastare (Galactic Civil War)|Battle of Malastare]]. Their objective was to destroy the [[planet]]'s [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] and communications hub; however, [[Gina Moonsong]] disobeyed orders and also destroyed [[Moff]] [[Valco Pandion]]'s [[Pandion's vacation palace|vacation palace]].
