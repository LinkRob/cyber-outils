'''Brazadann''' was a planet located in the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim]], and the twin of the planet [[Oriadann]].<ref name="Cristaux" />

{{Planet-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
'''Brazadann''' was created by [[Christophe Debien]] and [[Patrick Bousquet]] for the ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] scenario "[[La légende des cristaux]]" which appeared in the French magazine [[Casus Belli 76|''Casus Belli'' 76]]. It was illustrated by [[Igor Chevalier]] and [[Cyrille Daujean]].<ref name="Cristaux" />
