The '''Alvadorjian Senate bill''' was an unsuccessful bill in the [[Galactic Senate]] during the chancellorship of [[Finis Valorum]] pertaining to the plight of the [[Alvadorjian]]s of [[Ylagia]].<ref name="MoF" />

The Alvadorjians of Ylagia had a biological mutability that made cross-clan breeding difficult, and over time this trait had reduced the species population from 49 clans to seven.<ref name="MoF" />

After what happened to the [[Yehoulta clan]], popular support among youths and academics on [[Coruscant]] and [[Brentaal IV]] coalesced into an activism campaign that propelled the bill forward. The piece of legislation discussed funding the genetic repair of Alvadorjians and setting aside land for the clans on newly colonized worlds.<ref name="MoF" />

[[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] co-sponsored the bill, but due to the [[Selonian worker scandal]] and the [[Invasion of Naboo]], public interest in the matter waned, causing the bill to fail before reaching the [[Galactic Senate]] floor.<ref name="MoF" />

The unsuccessful bill, compounded by centuries of unkept Republic promises to aid the Alvadorjians, prompted five of the seven remaining clans to join the cause of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] when offered a genetic cure by [[Tychon Nulvolio]] that reportedly could take effect in five generations.<ref name="MoF" />
