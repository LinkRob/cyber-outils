'''Buoys''' were a variety of navigational technology that saw a variety of uses throughout [[the galaxy]]. Many buoys were utilized in [[realspace]],<ref name="SoloExpanded" /> though some buoys saw use [[planet]]side.<ref name="Lost Stars" /> Some, such as [[navigation buoy]]s, were utilized to chart courses through [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Catalyst" /> [[Sensor buoy]]s were a variety utilized to monitor [[starship]] activity,<ref name="STB" /> while [[space buoy]]s were utilized in the [[Akkadese Maelstrom]] in order to maintain [[the Channel]], a section of the [[Kessel Run]] [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="SoloExpanded" /> [[Buoy droid]]s were a variety of planetside buoy that also operated independently as a [[droid]]. They were utilized to guide incoming and outgoing ships in [[Coruscant]]'s [[spaceport]]s.<ref name="Lost Stars" />
