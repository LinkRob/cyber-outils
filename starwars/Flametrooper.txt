'''Flametroopers''' were a variant of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]] equipped with [[Flamethrower|incendiary weapons]]. In the [[Imperial Era]], they were deployed to [[Kashyyyk]] and [[Agaris]] to defend Imperial facilities against native fauna.

==Description==
Flametroopers were a variant of [[stormtrooper]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps]], notable for their use of [[flamethrower]]s. Over the [[Imperial Era]], they used several different designs of [[armor]]. One, notably utilized on [[Agaris]],<ref name="Adventures in Wild Space: The Rescue" /> was similar to the armor of [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtroopers]],<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> but with [[Color|maroon]] markings on the [[helmet]] and shoulders. It featured a black [[backpack]] which attached to the flamethrower, and fabric extending from the goggles over the [[mouth]].<ref name="Adventures in Wild Space: The Rescue" /> Another armor variant was used on [[Kashyyyk]], which was similar, but more angular in design with a lower set mouth cover, and purely white with only an orange stripe on the back of their helmets. It had a white backpack with orange stripes, and a white [[kama]].<ref name="Fallen Order" />

==Role==
[[File:WatervinePeachFlamed-2020StarWars46.png|thumb|left|300px|Flametroopers burn through watervine peach field]]
Flametroopers could be used to defend Imperial facilities from attacks from native fauna<ref name="Adventures in Wild Space: The Rescue" /> or enemy combatants.<ref name="Fallen Order" /> Furthermore, they could be used in assaults on enemy positions<ref name="Lost Stars" /> as determined by their superiors, which enabled corrupt superiors to dispatch flametroopers to their own ends.<ref name="SW46">[[Star Wars (2020) 46|''Star Wars'' (2020) 46]]</ref> They were skilled in the use of incendiary weapons. Their [[Fire|flame]] [[rifle]] had a range of roughly ten [[meter]]s. However, it limited their agility. It also utilized a combustible fuel source that required a cool down after use.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

==History==
[[File:Imperial_flametrooper.png|thumb|right|150px|One of the flametroopers on Agaris]]
During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] used flametroopers on the planet Agaris to burn down the native [[Agarian]]s living in the [[forest]]s surrounding the [[Imperial compound (Agaris)|Imperial compound]] there.<ref name="Adventures in Wild Space: The Rescue" /> At some stage of the Imperial Era, a [[Unidentified Imperial officer (watervine peach)|high-ranking Imperial officer]] attempted to claim the entire production of the rare [[watervine peach]] for himself and his followers, to which the local farmers on the [[Unidentified watervine peach homeworld|peach's homeworld]] refused. In response, the [[officer]] dispatched flametroopers to burn down the watervine peach orchards, thereby destroying the crop forever for everyone else in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="SW46" />

During the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]], flametroopers were deployed on the [[planet]] Kashyyyk's [[Imperial Refinery]]. There, they helped defend the facility against the native [[wyyyschokk]] and [[flame beetle]]s with their flamethrowers.<ref name="Fallen Order" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> in the [[Battle of Hoth]], [[Rebel pilot]] [[Thane Kyrell]] speculated that the Empire would deploy flametroopers to combat the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s ground forces.<ref name="Lost Stars" /> During the [[New Republic Era]], the supposed [[Sith Lord]] [[Rieve]] donned pieces of flametrooper gear in her [[Icy Sith]] armor for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]].<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Flametroopers were first mentioned in ''[[Lost Stars]]'', a [[canon]] [[2015]] young adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="Lost Stars" /> They first appeared in the [[2017]] junior novel ''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Rescue]]''.<ref name="Adventures in Wild Space: The Rescue" /> Concept art for the flametroopers in ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' was done by [[Jose Daniel Cabrera Peña]].<ref name="ArtOfJFO">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>
