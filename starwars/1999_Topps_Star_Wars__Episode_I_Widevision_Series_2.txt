'''1999 Topps ''Star Wars: Episode I'' Widevision Series 2''' is the second of two trading card sets released by [[Topps]] in [[1999]], covering the events of {{Film|I}}. Preceded by the [[1999 Topps Star Wars: Episode I Widevision Series 1|1999 Topps ''Star Wars: Episode I'' Widevision Series 1]] set, the set had a total of 80 cards.<ref name="TCDB" />
