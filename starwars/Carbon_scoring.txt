'''Carbon scoring''' was residual damage from burning and gas that could end up on metal objects such as [[droid]]s<ref name="Episode IV" /> or [[starfighter]]s.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref>  It could be removed with a [[carbon chisel]].<ref name="RSG">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]''</ref> Common causes of carbon scoring were the impacts of [[blaster]] bolts<ref name="TPATS">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref> and explosions.<ref name="Of MSE-6 and Men">{{StoryCite|story=Of MSE-6 and Men|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>

After their experience escaping from the ''[[Tantive IV]]'' and venturing through the [[Jundland Wastes]], both [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] had a lot of carbon scoring, something [[Luke Skywalker]] noted when he examined them.<ref name="Episode IV" />
