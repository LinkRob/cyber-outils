'''''Classic Star Wars'' 5''' is the fifth issue of the ''[[Classic Star Wars]]'' series, published by [[Dark Horse Comics]].

This particular ''Classic'' series were reformatted and colored reprints of the [[Star Wars (comic strip)|''Star Wars'' daily newspaper strip]] by [[Archie Goodwin]] and [[Al Williamson]] from the early 1980s.
Williamson supplied new panels and art to flow the stories for traditional comic format.

This issue reprints the middle of the ''[[The Serpent Masters]]'' comic strip.

==Publisher's summary==
''After crash-landing their stolen Imperial ship, Luke and his new-found companion Tanith Shire are captured and enslaved by the Serpent Masters of Tanith's home planet. Artoo and Threepio are out of commission and the ''Millennium Falcon'' is light-years away, leaving Luke to find his own means of escape &ndash; with nothing but his wit, his skill, and his trusty lightsaber at hand!''
