'''Booma Ball Bob''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Weequay]] residing on [[Tatooine]]. He was in charge of the Booma Ball Game and would reward the winner a [[Kyber brick]].<ref name="LEGO" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Booma Ball Bob" first appeared in a side game titled "Shooting Gallery: Booma Ball" in the [[2022]] non-[[canon]]ical [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''.<ref name="LEGO" />
