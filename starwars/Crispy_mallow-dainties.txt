'''Crispy mallow-dainties''' were a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] that could be found in<ref name="ALD" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]'s [[capital]] [[city]], [[Hanna City]].<ref name="ALD" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> food [[Merchant|vendors]] in the city [[Currency|sold]] crispy mallow-dainties on the world's [[Liberation Day (Coruscant)|Liberation Day]] holiday. The [[New Republic]] [[pilot]] [[Wedge Antilles]] noticed the smell of the food wafting throughout Hanna City's streets during the event.<ref name="ALD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crispy mallow-dainties were mentioned in ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', a [[2016]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the second installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="ALD" />
