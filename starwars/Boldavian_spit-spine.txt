A '''Boldavian spit-spine''' was an entity<ref name="Missing Pieces" /> that originated in<ref name="PLanetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=Boldavian spit-spine|system=[[Boldavia system]]}}</ref> the [[Expansion Region]] [[Boldavia system]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> They were considered rare during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Missing Pieces" /> Around [[377 BBY]],<ref name="Dates">The events of "[[Tales of Enlightenment: Missing Pieces]]" takes place around five [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Jedha]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> in the [[Cantina|bar]] [[Enlightenment]] in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] on<ref name="Missing Pieces" /> the [[moon]] [[Jedha]], the [[Sullustan]]<ref name="THRCE">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Piralli]] spoke with his friend,<ref name="Missing Pieces" /> the [[Twi'lek]]<ref name="THRCE" /> [[Moona]], about a [[Sullustan Mark VIII Legacy-class space yacht|Sullustan Mark VIII ''Legacy''-class space yacht]] that he was restoring, which was only missing its [[central processor]]. When Moona asked him why he had not just bought a [[processor]] at a market, Piralli explained that the [[yacht]] needed a specific type that was no longer produced and compared the rarity of the required processor to that of a Boldavian spit-spine.<ref name="Missing Pieces" />

==Behind the scenes==
A Boldavian spit-spine was mentioned in "[[Tales of Enlightenment: Missing Pieces|Missing Pieces]]," a short story written by [[George Mann]] and published in the short story collection ''[[Star Wars Insider: The High Republic: Tales of Enlightenment]]'' as part of the ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Enlightenment]]'' series within the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Missing Pieces" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]] on [[April 2]], [[2024]]. Prior to the story's release, the mention was included in an excerpt of the story posted on [[StarWars.com]] on [[April 1]] of that year.<ref name="Phase">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-insider-tales-of-enlightenment-excerpt|text=From the Pages of ''Star Wars Insider'': ''Star Wars: The High Republic: Tales of Enlightenment'' - Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref>
