'''Black Spire Hot Chocolate''' was a hot, sweet [[chocolate]] beverage available at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] and [[Ronto Roasters]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. The drink was [[Color|brown]] in color and served with a topping of shimmery, [[sugar]]y purple-colored whipped [[cream]].<ref name="SWGE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Black Spire Hot Chocolate was added to the menus of Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo and Ronto Roasters after the initial opening of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]].<ref name="DPB New Food">{{DPB|url=2020/01/discover-new-galactic-food-and-merchandise-in-star-wars-galaxys-edge-at-disneyland-park|text=Discover New Galactic Food and Merchandise in ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge at Disneyland Park}}</ref> The menu description is, "Hot chocolate topped with brown sugar whip cream."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/breakfast|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Breakfast Menu}}</ref>
