'''Fuse Games''' is a [[Star Wars video games|video game]] development company founded in [[2023]] by [[Matt Webster]]<ref name="SWGR News" /> and based in [[Wikipedia:Guildford|Guildford]], [[Wikipedia:England|England]].<ref name="AboutFuse" /> It was founded from prior members of [[Criterion Games]].<ref name="Reveal article">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galactic-racer-reveal-trailer|text=We're Going Back to Jakku in the Trailer for ''Star Wars: Galactic Racer'' - Exclusive Interview}}</ref> The company's debut game is the upcoming [[2026]] [[Wikipedia:Racing game|racing game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Racer]]'' in collaboration with [[Lucasfilm Games]] and [[Secret Mode]].<ref name="SWGR News" />
