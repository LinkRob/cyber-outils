"'''Chapter 2: Sinister Schemes'''" is the second episode of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'' and was released on [[April 6]], [[2026]] alongside "[[Chapter 1: The Dark Revenge]]". Its title was revealed on [[March 17]], 2026 by the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' Twitter account.<ref name="Twitter" />

==Official description==
''Maul seeks to enlighten his new prisoners while Lawson pursues him.''

==Plot summary==
===Fight and flight===
Maul and his [[Zabrak]] henchmen [[Scorn]] and [[Icarus]] kidnap [[Devon Izara]] and [[Looti Vario]]. The group ascend a ladder where they are pinned down by blaster fire from [[Captain]] [[Brander Lawson]] and his [[Tactical Defense Force]] officers. Izara manages to break away from her Zabrak captor and [[Spybot]]. She flees into a tunnel but reaches a dead end. Spybot [[stun]]s her, knocking her unconscious. The Zabraks carry her and Vario into two waiting vehicles, including the stolen [[bank transport]]. Before fleeing, they short-circuit the circular door on the hangar. Lawson, [[2B0T]] ("Two-Boots") and the other police officers break through the door. Two-Boots commandeers an [[airspeeder]] piloted by a droid and the police pursue Maul's airspeeders. 

The bank transport lumbers its way through a highway, attracting the attention of a [[Tactical Defense Droid]] in a police airspeeder. The police airspeeder pursues the bank transport but the Mandalorians aboard destroy the vehicle with a [[Missile launcher|rocket launcher]]. [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]] watches the fleeing vehicles from an overhead bridge. He [[Force jump]]s onto the highway, causing the smaller airspeeder carrying the Zabraks and Vario to flip and crash. The bank transport collides with the smaller vehicle. As the Zabraks climb out of the vehicle, Maul faces off with Master Daki. 

Police forces led by Captain Lawson converge on the crash site. Maul distracts his pursuers by using [[the Force]] to split a nearby pedestrian bridge. While the police rescue the civilians, Daki uses his Force powers to suspend the bridge structure long enough for Lawson and his officers to evacuate the civilians on their commandeered transport.

===A new day===
The following morning, Captain Lawson and his colleagues gather in the police station's command center to discuss the events of the previous night. He is curious as to why Maul is interested in the [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|girl]]. The Tactical Defense Force's Chief [[Klyce]] speaks with Lawson and tells him that the planetary [[Governor]] Is concerned and wants to involve the [[Galactic Empire]]. Lawson is reluctant to get the Empire involved and says that it is a local matter that he and his force will manage. 

Meanwhile, Maul arrives with his [[Shadow Collective]] forces and captives at a secret hideout in the lower levels of [[Janix city]]. Seeing that Izara is wounded, Maul convinces her to let the Mandalorians treat her injuries. When [[Rook Kast]] asks why he has spared Vario, Maul explains that the crime lord is still of use to him for now. Kast asks about the girl. Maul explains that she is a Jedi whom he hopes to forge into a weapon against those who have betrayed him including [[Darth Sidious]]. Kast voices her skepticism of Maul's plan to use a Jedi. Maul is interested to learn about how Izara survived. 

Izara is led to Maul by her Mandalorian captors and asks what he wants with her. Maul locks his two prisoners in two separate cylindrical vaults. Vario protests his incarceration while Izara is silent but defiant. Maul tells Izara that she has been indoctrinated since her childhood and that she must learn to adapt. 

===Duty above family===
Meanwhile, Captain Lawson returns to his home where he is greeted by his teenage son [[Rylee Lawson]], who is settling down for breakfast. Rylee is aware of the events from last night and asks about the mysterious girl and Two Boots. Lawson reassures Rylee that Two Boots is alright. When Rylee tells his father that his mother [[Drea Lawson]] has invited him for a visit. Lawson tells his son to wait for him to come along. Lawson promises his son that he will take time off soon. Shortly later, Two-Boots tells Lawson he has an update. Lawson says that he will be on his way and tells his son that he will catch up later. Rylee stares at a [[hologram]] of his parents. 

Back at the police headquarters, Two-Boots tells Lawson that the police have interrogated members of Vario's syndicate but they have proven uncooperative. Two-Boots has not been able to find information about the Shadow Collective and questions whether Vario was truthful. Lawson tells him to dig deeper and warns against involving the Empire.

===Plans in motion===
Later, Vario is released from his cell. Vario expresses relief but is kicked down a flight of stairs. He comes face to face with Maul who demands that he provide him with the time and location of the [[Pyke Syndicate]]'s smuggling operations in [[Janix's star system]]. His plan is for Vario to then set up a meeting with [[Marg Krim]], the crime lord of the Pyke Syndicate, so that Maul can kill him. Vario expresses reluctance so Maul intimidates him by levitating a sled containing several bars of metal currency above him. At the last moment, Maul drops the sled near Vario. Vario agrees to help Maul. 

Later that evening, Captain Lawson drives his [[landspeeder]] into a large building with purple glowing lights. There, he meets with [[Rheena Sul]], a [[Gender|female]] [[alien]] with purple skin. After exchanging pleasantries over drinks, Lawson seeks information from Sul about the Shadow Collective. She recalls that the Collective was an underworld cartel that originated on [[Mandalore]] and was founded by Maul, who merged the Pykes, [[Crimson Dawn]] and the [[Black Sun]] crime syndicates. When Lawson asks about Maul, Sul says that Maul vanished after the [[Clone Wars]] and that the syndicates disbanded. 

Sul asks if he suspects Maul is on Janix due to the recent death of [[Nico Deemis]] and the disappearance of Looti Vario. She speculates that they made a deal with Maul that went wrong. When Lawson asks if Maul was seeking revenge against the two crime lords, Sul says that she was wise to have never made a deal with Maul. She warns Lawson to be careful since Maul is present on the planet. When Lawson asks if she has anymore information to share, she attempts to invite him to stay for "dinner." 

===A philosophical conversation===
While waiting in her cell, Izara is visited by Maul. When she asks when he will release her, Maul responds that she can choose to leave when she wants. Izara counters that she wants to leave now but says that is not possible. Maul replies that he is concerned by her point of view of the situation. He offers to show her a pathway to power that the [[Jedi]] have not taught her. When Izara declines, Maul says he admires her spirit but counters that times have change. He asks her what it means to be a Jedi in a time of lawlessness. Izara responds that she still believes in the Jedi principles of being guardians of peace and justice in [[the galaxy]]. 

Maul remarks that if there were more Jedi like her, the galaxy would not be in its present state. Izara tells Maul that she does not trust him, having heard the stories, and tells him to stop pretending that he cares. Maul says that Izara is operating under the premise that he is the villain here but counters that life is more complicated than the binary of good and evil. He ponders how he can prove to Izara that they are on the same side. When Izara suggests that he can let her out, Maul responds that she can choose to leave. Their conversation is interrupted by Kast, who informs Maul that Vario's intelligence about the Pyke shipment has proven reliable. Maul walks away from Izara's cell. 

===A warning to the Pykes===
Later, the Pyke Syndicate's ship is about to jump into [[hyperspace]]. However, Spybot sabotages the [[hyperdrive]], preventing them from entering hyperspace. The Pykes lose control of the ship's guidance system and the ship is redirected onto a nearby forested planetoid. The captain sees a squad of Mandalorians led by Rook Kast approach the ship's bridge, and attempts to send a [[distress signal]] but one of the pilots inform him that their comms are [[Sensor jammer|jammed]]. The captain then leads several armed Pykes towards the rear exit. However, a cloaked Maul ascends into the ship through a cargo lift. The Pykes open fire but the former Sith cuts them down with his [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|double-bladed lightsaber]]. While Maul slaughters the crew and enforcers, Spybot lowers the cargo lift, allowing Kast and her followers to seize the blocks of money. 

Maul forces his way into the cockpit and uses the Force to hurl the captain against the ceiling. The captain vows that Marg Krim will take revenge against Maul for this slight and tells him that everything he once controlled now belongs to Krim. Maul disagrees and kills him with his lightsaber. Back on Janix, Captain Lawson is followed by a hooded figure down an alleyway. He turns around but sees nothing. After Lawson drives away in his speeder, the figure is revealed to be the [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]].

===Breaking free===
Later, Maul visits Vario outside his cell. When Vario asks if his information was useful and if he can be set free, Maul responds that Vario will only be released once Krim is dead. Vario sighs but one of Maul's Zabrak growls at him. Maul then turns his attention to Izara, telling her that is time to see the galaxy for what it truly is. He then walks away, leaving her alone in her cell. Reflecting on Maul's words, Izara tries to use her Force powers to unlock her cell door. She fails on the first attempt. Instead, she uses the Force to lift her cylindrical cell from its base, causing it to crash and its back wall to slide open. Izara looks tensely around her surroundings.
