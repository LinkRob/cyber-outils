The '''Chadawan moon''' was a desolate [[moon]], barely larger than an [[asteroid]], which [[orbit]]ed [[Chadawa]], an inhabited [[ocean]] [[Planet|world]] constructed by an ancient [[alien]] [[species]] eons before the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Chadawa and its moon were located in the [[Chadawa system]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in the [[Croynar sector]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" />

Chadawa's moon appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the final volume of [[Alexander Freed]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
