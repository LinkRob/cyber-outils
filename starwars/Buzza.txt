'''Buzza''' was an [[Ewok]] [[Gender|male]] who served as the [[chief]] of the [[Bright Tree tribe|tribe]] that lived in [[Bright Tree Village]] on the [[moon]] [[Endor]] and was the [[Parent|father]] of [[Chirpa]]. The Ewok [[Logray]] heard stories that Buzza told about an Ewok [[deity]] known as the [[Golden One]], and Buzza once scared Chirpa and Logray by creating tracks of the [[Kroolok]], a [[troll]] that supposedly lived on Endor. 

In [[1 BBY]], after [[wokling]]s were taken from their nests, Buzza spoke about the disappearances with the [[shaman]] [[Makrit]], who was secretly behind the incidents. The Ewok [[Ra-Lee]] believed the [[Youngling|woklings]] had been taken by [[Dulok]]s, which Buzza initially agreed with until they were influenced by Makrit using the [[sunstar]] artifact. Logray, Chirpa, and Ra-Lee then decided to rescue the missing woklings and saved them from the [[Great Devourer]], a [[Gorax]] who Makrit was attempting to sacrifice them to. Forty-two [[season]]s before the [[Battle of Endor]] of [[4 ABY]], Chirpa succeeded his father as chief. Others, including Chirpa and Logray, remembered Buzza on multiple occasions. 

==Biography==
===Chief of the Bright Tree tribe===
{{Quote|It has to be ''Duloks,'' up to their old tricks. Who else would sneak into our village in the dead of night to steal our young?''"<br />"''You're right, Ra-Lee. And we will have our revenge. The Duloks have crossed us for the last time.|Ra-Lee and Buzza|<ref name="TfVC 4" />}}
Buzza was an [[Ewok]] [[Gender|male]] who served as the [[chief]]<ref name="TfVC 4" /> of the [[Council of Elders (Ewok)|Council of Elders]] of the [[Bright Tree tribe|tribe]] that lived in [[Bright Tree Village]] on the [[moon]] [[Endor]] and was the [[Parent|father]] of [[Chirpa]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> Buzza told stories around a [[fire]] to [[wokling]]s about the [[Golden One]], an Ewok [[deity]]. In the stories, Buzza described the Golden One's [[eye]]s as giving off a soft and steady glow, like [[star]]s that had been plucked from the sky. Buzza also said that He could only see light and that darkness was defeated by being looked at by the Golden One. The chief's stories were heard by the Ewok [[Logray]].<ref name="DI">{{StoryCite|story=Divine (?) Intervention|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi|sformatted=Divine (?) Intervention}}</ref> On another occasion, when Chirpa and Logray snuck out into the [[Water|snow]], Buzza created tracks of the [[Kroolok]], a type of [[troll]] that supposedly lived on Endor, to scare them.<ref name="Kroolok">{{StoryCite|story=The Kroolok|book=Life Day Treasury}}</ref>

[[File:MakritBuzza-TalesFromVadersCastle4.png|thumb|right|300px|Makrit (left) speaking with Buzza (right)]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="TfVC 4 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the flashbacks of [[Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle 4|''Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle'' 4]] to [[1 BBY]].</ref> Buzza spoke with the [[shaman]] [[Makrit]] about [[Youngling|woklings]] who had been taken from their nests as they [[Sleep|slept]]. They were interrupted by Chirpa and the Ewok [[Ra-Lee]], who had returned from [[hunting]] a [[boar-wolf]]. Chirpa attempted to inform<ref name="TfVC 4" /> his<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> father of the hunt's success, but Buzza said they were discussing grave matters and told them of the missing woklings. Ra-Lee suspected [[Dulok]]s had taken the missing woklings, and Buzza agreed, declaring that they would take revenge on the Duloks. However, Makrit&mdash;who was secretly taking the woklings to be sacrificed to a [[Gorax]] known as the [[Great Devourer]]&mdash;used the [[sunstar]] artifact on Buzza, turning their eyes [[Color|orange]] and influencing them into believing the woklings had likely wandered off by themselves. Ra-Lee was surprised that the chief suddenly agreed with the shaman, as Buzza did not take orders from Makrit, but Chirpa concluded that as the shaman, Makrit knew best. However, Logray convinced Chirpa and Ra-Lee to confront the Duloks that [[Local day|night]].<ref name="TfVC 4" />

When the three Ewoks arrived at the [[Dulok camp]], they found only a single [[Unidentified Dulok (camp)|Dulok]] attempting to sacrifice a wokling. Makrit, who had followed them, stopped the Dulok with the sunstar and then claimed he owed Buzza an apology, as the chief had been correct in suspecting the Duloks. The Ewoks then traveled to the [[Desert of Salma|Salma Sands]] at the base of [[Mount Krana]], where the woklings were being held. There, Makrit betrayed Chirpa, attempting to sacrifice him to the Great Devourer, as he believed the chief's son would be the perfect sacrifice. However, Chirpa and the other Ewoks managed to escape and rescue the woklings, leaving Makrit to be [[Diet|eaten]] by the Gorax.<ref name="TfVC 4" />

===Remembered by others===
{{Quote|I thought the trail tracks were a nice touch.''"<br />"''A little trick my father pulled when we snuck out in the snow. Do you remember?''"<br />"''I still have nightmares.|Logray and Chirpa|<ref name="Kroolok" />}}
[[File:ChiefChirpa-SoH.png|thumb|left|200px|Buzza's son, Chirpa, who succeeded his father as chief]]
Around 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date1">{{TalesVadersCastleDate}}</ref> after a [[Lina Graf's crew|crew]] led by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[commander]] [[Lina Graf]] crashed on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]] during a [[Fact-finding mission to the Outer Rim|fact-finding mission]], the [[bodyguard droid]] [[XM-G3]] included Buzza in a story he told to the [[Peasle]] [[Skritt]] about Makrit's attempt to sacrifice the woklings.<ref name="TfVC 4" /> Forty-two [[season]]s before the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="SW:AEYNtK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> of [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Chirpa succeeded Buzza as chief.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

Between 1 BBY and 4 ABY,<ref name="Kroolok Date">"[[One Normal Day]]" establishes the events of "[[The Kroolok]]" take place before its own events. As the events of "One Normal Day" occur concurrently to the events of {{Film|VI}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]], "The Kroolok" must take place by that [[Standard year|year]]. "The Kroolok" also establishes that its events occur after the flashback events of [[Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle 4|''Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle'' 4]], which ''Timelines'' dates to [[1 BBY]].</ref> when Chirpa's [[Kintaka clan|daughter]] [[Kneesaa a Jari Kintaka]] and her friends [[Wicket Wystri Warrick]] and [[Teebo]] snuck out into the snow during [[winter]], Chirpa and Logray&mdash;by then a shaman&mdash;scared them by making Kroolok footprints and tail tracks with [[snowshoe]]s. After pulling off the trick, Chirpa reminded Logray of when his father had done the same to them, noting that Buzza had used the same method to create the Kroolok's tail tracks. Logray grinned at the memory and admitted he still had [[Dream|nightmares]] from it.<ref name="Kroolok" /> In 4 ABY,<ref name="Date">The events of "[[Divine (?) Intervention]]" are concurrent to the events of {{Film|VI}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref> when Logray saw the [[3PO-series protocol droid|protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]], he noticed that the droid matched the description of the Golden One from Buzza's stories, causing the shaman to briefly believe that C-3PO was the god.<ref name="DI" />
