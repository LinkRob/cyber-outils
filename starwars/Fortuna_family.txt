The '''Fortuna family''' was a family of [[Twi'lek]]s that [[Homeworld|originated]] on [[Ryloth]], in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The family was heavily involved in the criminal underworld, one member, [[Bib Fortuna]],<ref name="Visual Guide" /> became the [[majordomo]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]'s [[Jabba's criminal empire|criminal empire]].<ref name="Episode VI" /> Bib's cousin [[Beezer Fortuna]] resented this criminally-minded side of the family and was more [[Politics|politically]]-minded, occupying an important position in [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans|insurgent cell]].<ref name="Visual Guide" />
