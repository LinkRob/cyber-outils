The '''Arli system'''<ref name="Appendix" /> was situated in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] and named for the [[Unidentified gas planet|gas planet]] nearest to its [[star]] also contained the [[ocean]] [[planet]] [[Arli]] and its [[Arli's moon|moon]]. During the [[Great Hyperspace Rush|Hyperspace Rush]], a [[Hyperspace route|route]] from [[Hetzal Prime|Hetzal]] to the [[star system]] was mapped by the [[San Tekka clan]], who expected that it would become a major hub. However, a more optimal location was soon mapped a system over, and many of the settlers in Arli's star system moved elsewhere. The San Tekkas remained, however, settling on Arli's [[moon]], while a population continued to [[Farm|harvest]] an adaptable type of [[Red moss|moss]] on Arli.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />
