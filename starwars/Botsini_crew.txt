The '''Botsini crew''' was a crew of individuals that operated at some point after the [[Battle of Jakku|fall]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The crew was known for a strategy where the [[captain]] gave each member of the crew a specific job, withholding the full details of their plans, so that if a mistake was made it would be clear who was at fault. The [[Batuuan]] resident [[Julen Rakab]] thought that the strategy could only work if there were backup plans.<ref name="A Crash of Fate">''[[A Crash of Fate]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Botsini crew was first mentioned in the [[2019]] novel ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />
