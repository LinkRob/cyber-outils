A '''duel''' took place at an [[Unidentified Outer Rim spaceport|Outer Rim spaceport]] during the [[Clone Wars]], when two [[Jedi]] clashed with the renegade Sith Lord [[Maul]] and [[Savage Opress]]. It was one in a series of raids committed by the brothers after the [[Massacre on Raydonia|massacre]] they perpetrated on [[Raydonia]]. They concluded the duel by [[Death|killing]] the two Jedi.<ref name="Revival" />
