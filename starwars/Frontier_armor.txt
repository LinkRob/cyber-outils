'''Frontier armor''' was a type of [[Color|gray]] colored [[armor]] available five [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. The street urchin [[Ezra Bridger]] read that the armor was lightweight, flexible, and capable of withstanding a shot from a [[blaster]]. He included the armor on a wishlist he compiled in his [[Rebel Journal|journal]].<ref name="ERJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Frontier armor was first shown in the reference book ''[[Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and published in [[2014]].
