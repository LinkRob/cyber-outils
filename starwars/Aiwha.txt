'''Aiwhas''', also called '''air whales''', were a [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[species]] of winged [[cetacean]]s [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Kamino]]. They were used as mounts by the [[Kaminoan]]s. Kaminoans were derogatorily referred to as "aiwha-bait" by the [[clone trooper]]s.

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|That uniform suits you about as well as a hat on an aiwha.|[[Kan Be]] to [[Terex]]|<ref name="Poe Dameron 5">[[Poe Dameron 5|''Poe Dameron'' 5]]</ref>}}
[[File:Aiwha-TVE.png|thumb|left|250px|An aiwha fitted with a riding saddle]]
Massive, winged [[cetacean]]s,<ref name="Databank" /> aiwhas were a [[species]] of [[whale]]<ref name="TVE" /> [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[ocean]]s and [[Atmosphere|skies]] of the [[planet]] [[Kamino]]. This [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[species]],<ref name="Absolutely" /> one of a wide variety of aquatic lifeforms on Kamino,<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> was also known as the "air whale," and was known to be peaceful and majestic.<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Being both capable swimmers and fliers,<ref name="Databank" /> the aiwha utilized their flippers as wings, and vice versa, to propel them through the sea or sky. Their long, slightly down-turned, beak-like mouths were filled with [[baleen]] used for filtering [[Krill (creature)|krill]] and other tiny prey from mouthfuls of seawater.<ref name="TVE" /> Aiwhas gave out bleating cries when [[Death|dying]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|We've got to stop meeting like this, my friend.|Obi-Wan Kenobi, saved by an Aiwha for the second time|<ref name="ARC Troopers">{{TCW|ARC Troopers (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:Aiwhas-ARCT.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Aiwhas during the [[Clone Wars]]]]
As the climate of Kamino changed drastically, leading to planet-wide flooding as the ice caps melted, the native [[Kaminoan]]s adapted both themselves and some of their fellow creatures—such as the aiwha—to the changing environment by utilizing their advanced [[cloning]] technologies, becoming biological curiosities as mounts to traverse Kamino's<ref name="Monsters of the Clone Wars">{{SW|url=news/monsters-of-the-clone-wars-a-season-six-bestiary|text=Monsters of ''The Clone Wars'': A Season Six Bestiary|nolive=1}}</ref> endless oceans.<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> When harsh weather battered the surface,<ref name="Databank" /> riders traveled beneath the waves; in this way, the versatility of the aiwha enabled riders to avoid the lashing tempests of severe electrical storms, which raged for much of the planet's year.<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

During the [[Battle of Kamino]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] hitched a ride on an aiwha while investigating the planet's [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] attackers,<ref name="Databank" /> and was rescued twice during the battle by the creature.<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /><ref name="ARC Troopers" /> During the search for the rogue [[clone trooper]] [[Fives|CT-5555]] "Fives," teams rode atop aiwhas to track-down the fugitive.<ref name="Fugitive">{{TCW|Fugitive}}</ref><ref name="Monsters of the Clone Wars" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The air whale ("aiwha") was something George Lucas was trying to get into Star Wars as far back as Empire, where they would have flown over Bespin. In ROTJ, they would have flown over Sicemon, a grassland planet. For Episode I, the Gungans would have flown them on Naboo.|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1490520737957888004|Pablo Hidalgo|quote=The air whale ("aiwha") was something George Lucas was trying to get into Star Wars as far back as Empire, where they would have flown over Bespin. In ROTJ, they would have flown over Sicemon, a grassland planet. For Episode I, the Gungans would have flown them on Naboo.|archivedate=20220303202028}}</ref>}}
[[File:Aiwha_concepts.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Concept art for ''The Empire Strikes Back'' and ''Return of the Jedi'']]
The aiwha first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]. The concept of an "air whale" was first created by [[Ralph McQuarrie]], for use on the planet [[Bespin]] in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], although budget limitations saw their use postponed.<ref name="Monsters of the Clone Wars" /> They were later intended to serve as flying whale taxis for the world of [[Sicemon]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], and though they likewise didn't appear in that film, [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] material utilized the concept as the [[Thranta/Legends|thranta]]. [[Terryl Whitlatch]] redesigned the creatures as [[Gungan]] mounts for [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], calling them "aiwha" as a derivation of "air whale." The creatures were again cut from the movie, and ultimately appeared on [[Kamino]] in [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars:'' Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="Concept to Screen">{{SW|url=news/from-concept-to-screen-kamino|text=From Concept to Screen: Kamino|nolive=1}}</ref>
