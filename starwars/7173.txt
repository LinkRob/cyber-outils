'''7173''' was the designation of a [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> who served as a member of the [[Stormtrooper Corps]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="JFO" /> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="JFO Date" /> 7173 was stationed on the [[planet]] [[Zeffo]] as part of a [[squad]] guarding an abandoned village and complained to another [[Soldier|trooper]] about the low temperature.<ref name="JFO" />

The pair also discussed a [[skungus]] that had exploded in their [[commander]]'s quarters, which 7173 believed had been placed there on purpose. When the other soldier revealed that their next assignment was on a covert mission with [[Purge Trooper]]s, 7173 claimed to have turned down the same assignment as they did not want to abandon their post.<ref name="JFO" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 14 BBY, the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] killed 7173 when he was on his way to the [[Tomb of Eilram]] on a mission to restore the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="JFO" />
{{EndGame}}

==Personality and traits==
A member of the [[Stormtrooper Corps]], 7173 claimed they did not want to abandon their post. They also showed dejection at their lack of promotion prospects.<ref name="JFO" />

==Behind the scenes==
7173 first appeared in the [[2019]] video game ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''. The trooper is one of the two participants in several conversations that can be overheard between a [[stormtrooper]] and a [[scout trooper]] on Zeffo, although it is not clear which of the two troopers is 7173. While 7173's death is not required within the game's storyline, this article assumes that the player defeats all enemies within the game.<ref name="JFO" />
