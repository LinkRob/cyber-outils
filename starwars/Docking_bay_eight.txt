'''Docking bay eight''' was a [[Hangar|docking bay]] located within a [[Unidentified port|port]] on the [[planet]] [[Li'eta]], which operated during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="MOF" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate}}</ref> a local [[Unidentified Separatist woman|machine shop owner]] who had previously been an operative for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between it and the [[Galactic Republic]] helped former Separatist [[Assassin|operative]] [[Soujen Vak-Nhalis]] find a way to leave Li'eta. She suggested that he leave for docking bay eight at the port that [[Local day|night]] for a cargo flight to [[transport]] him. However, Vak-Nhalis studied her and surmised that the [[Gender|woman]] was lying and [[Death|killed]] her for exposing his identity. Eventually, he found his way off the planet via other means.<ref name="MOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Docking bay eight was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MOF" />
