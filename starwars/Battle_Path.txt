{{Quote|I am the Eye, and I will give you what you need to defeat our enemies. These are the Battle Paths, my friends, and with them... you cannot lose.|Marchion Ro|<ref name="LOTJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>}}
The '''Battle Paths''' were a variant of the [[Path]] [[hyperspace route]]s used by the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s. Operated through Nihil [[Path engine]]s, the Battle Paths caused Nihil [[starship]]s to make multiple extremely short jumps through [[hyperspace]]. [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] employed the Battle Paths during the [[Battle of Kur]] to destroy [[Kassav Milliko]]'s [[Tempest]], both to inflict severe damage on the [[Galactic Republic]] force the [[Kassav's Tempest|Tempest]] was fighting and to be able to frame their deaths as a heroic sacrifice to the rest of the Nihil.<ref name="LOTJ" />
