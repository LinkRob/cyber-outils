"'''From Trenches to Wrenches: The Roger Story'''" is the first episode of ''[[LEGO Star Wars: All-Stars]]''. It was released on [[Disney XD]] on [[November 10]], [[2018]]. The episode is a collection of the eight short films "[[Lo, I am Manufactured]]," "[[It Gets Ugly]]," "[[The Good Stuff!]]," "[[The Prisoner of Tatooine]]," "[[The Droid Rescue Gambit]]," "[[Yub Nub!]]," "[[What Goes Up... (episode)|What Goes Up...]] and "[[The Power at Jakku]]." In the episode, the [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]] narrates chapters from his autobiography ''[[From Trenches to Wrenches: The Roger Story/LEGO|From Trenches to Wrenches: The Roger Story]]''.
