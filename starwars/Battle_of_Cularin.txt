The '''Battle of Cularin''' was a battle associated with [[Cularin]],<ref name="Lead by Example" /> an astronomical object in the [[Expansion Region]],<ref name="The Force Awakens Beginner Game">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> that occurred sometime between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> The [[Maelstrom-class battle cruiser|''Maelstrom''-class battle cruiser]] [[Maelstrom (Maelstrom-class)|''Maelstrom'']] of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Navy|navy]] served with distinction in the battle, under the leadership of its [[commanding officer]], [[Jedi General]] [[Darrus Jeht]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Lead by Example" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Battle of Cularin was introduced in the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Lead by Example]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]] for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="Lead by Example" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Battle of Cularin/Legends|Battle of Cularin]] appeared in "[[Living Force: Last Stand]]," the final short story written by [[August Hahn]] and [[Cynthia Hahn]] as a supplement to [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']], a [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game|roleplaying]] campaign created by [[Wizards of the Coast]]. The story was published on [[Wizards.com]] on [[February 1]], [[2007]].<ref name="Living Force: Last Stand">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=starwars/livingforce/lf20070201LastStand|text=Living Force: Last Stand|int=Living Force: Last Stand}}</ref>
