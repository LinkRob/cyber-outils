The '''Big Settlement hydro farm''' was a major [[hydro farm]] located in [[Big Settlement]] on [[Atterra Bravo]]. The hydro farm provided much of the town's water until it was seized by the [[First Order]] shortly before the [[Atterra campaign]]. The facility's foreman was [[Darrus Rantarovo]].<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" />
