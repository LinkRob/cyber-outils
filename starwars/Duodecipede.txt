'''Duodecipedes''' were creatures whose [[Limb|legs]] could be roasted for [[Foodstuff|consumption]] as [[roasted duodecipede leg stick]]s.<ref name="CV New">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}}, as depicted in ''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]'', to [[22 BBY]].</ref> the [[human]] [[moisture farmer]] [[Owen Lars]] of the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] had roasted duodecipede leg sticks and [[crimplecress slice]]s in the food storage compartment of his [[Zephyr-G swoop]].<ref name="CV New" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], duodecipedes were first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> from the [[2002]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', which was written by [[Curtis Saxton]] and marked the [[Duodecipede/Legends|creature's]] first mention in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Cross-Sections">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
