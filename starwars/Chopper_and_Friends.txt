"'''Chopper and Friends'''" is the ninth episode of the animated micro-series ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]''{{'}} [[Forces of Destiny: Season Two|second season]]. It was released on [[May 4]], [[2018]].

==Official description==
''Hera is surprised to discover that Chopper has crewed the ''Ghost'' with two Ewoks who prove their mettle and help take down some stray TIEs over Endor''.<ref name="Jedi News" />

==Plot summary==
Following the [[Battle of Endor]], [[C1-10P|Chopper]] is playing spears with two [[Ewok]]s including [[Kneesaa a Jari Kintaka|Kneesaa]] outside the ''[[Ghost]]'' when [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] tells him to stop it because [[Comm/scan processor|com-scan]] has picked up some leftover [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] that are headed for the [[Bright Tree Village|Ewok village]]. She tells them they have to get to the ''Ghost''. Chopper protests but Hera tells him that it is just you and me. The Ewoks speak to Chopper before he and Hera depart aboard the ''Ghost''.

The ''Ghost'' flies in to the skies above [[Endor]] and runs into several TIE fighters. Hera says they are running into Point 0.75. A dogfight ensues and Hera tells Chopper to get to the guns. The droid responds that they have a new crew and introduces Hera to Kneesaa and her companion. Hera is upset because she thinks that the Ewoks are only good at fighting on the ground. Hera reluctantly tells Chopper and the Ewoks to get to it.

As the two TIEs exchange fire with the ''Ghost'', Kneesaa climbs to the top gun turret while Chopper and the other Ewok man the rear gun turrets. Despite being too short, Kneesaa manages to fire several shots. She shoots at one of the TIE fighters, causing it to collide with the second one. Following the dogfight, Hera compliments Chopper's crew for doing a good job and says "let's take them home."
