'''Durs''' was a member of the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Special Forces|Special Forces]] who was deployed to the [[planet]] [[Akiva]] during the [[Rebellion on Akiva]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="AftermathExcerpt" /> Durs was [[Jom Barell's unit|one]] of six SpecForces [[New Republic soldier|soldiers]] commanded by [[Sergeant major]] [[Jom Barell]] brought in by a [[transport]] to drop from Akiva's [[atmosphere]] into the outskirts of the [[city]] of [[Myrra]]. On Barell's orders, the [[soldier]]s each leapt from the transport in order to dive to the planet's surface using [[Parawings|parawaings]], with Durs and then Barell being the last two to exit the transport. As they descended, an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[turbolaser]] opened [[fire]] on the soldiers, obliterating Durs' colleagues [[Corporal]] [[Kason]] and [[Stromm]] in quick succession. Barell then order the survivors to open their parawings, but Durs' comrade [[Gahee'abee]] was immediately [[Death|killed]] upon doing so. Durs, at this point the closest to the ground, was then also hit by the [[laser]] and killed, after which the only other remaining soldier serving under Barell, [[Polnichk]], was broken by the wind. Barell himself managed to reach the ground and went on to take part in claiming a victory for the New Republic. While deploying to Akiva, Durs wore [[carbon]]-laced [[armor]] and [[orbital drop mask]]s along with parawings.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Durs first appeared in the novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], which was written by [[Chuck Wendig]] and released in [[2015]].
