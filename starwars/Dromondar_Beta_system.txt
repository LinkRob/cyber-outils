The '''Dromondar Beta system''' of the [[Unknown Regions]] was located in grid square H-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[star system]] was used by the [[First Order]]<ref name="ICS" /> by [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]'' states that the [[All Terrain Heavy Hauler]] had operated on [[Dromondar Beta]] by the events of {{Film|VII}}, which take place in [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> with the Order's [[All Terrain Heavy Hauler]] being field-tested on [[Dromondar Beta]]. The [[Kuat-Entralla Drive Yards]]&ndash;manufactured [[AT-series|all-terrain vehicle]] completed its mission goals there even though more than forty percent of its [[limb]]s had been made defunct.<ref name="ICS" />
