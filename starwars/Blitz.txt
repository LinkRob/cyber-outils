[[Clone Commander]] '''Blitz''' was an [[Advanced Recon Commando]] in [[Rancor Battalion]], part of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. He, along with fellow ARC [[Commander]]s [[Colt]], [[Hammer (ARC trooper)|Hammer]], and [[Havoc (ARC trooper)|Havoc]] oversaw [[clone trooper]] training during the war.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Attention! Helmets on!|Blitz to the columns of rookie clones after their graduation from clone cadet|<ref name="Clone Cadets" />}}
[[File:Ambush-SWQ133.png|thumb|200px|left|Blitz (back left), with other members of Rancor Battalion ambush a column of B2 super battle droids.]]
Blitz,<ref name="Card Trader">{{SWCT|set=Rank & File|cardname=Clone ARC Commander Blitz}}</ref> like all [[Cloning|clones]], was created on the [[Water|aquatic]] [[planet]] of [[Kamino]] from the DNA of the [[Death|late]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]].<ref name="Clone Troopers" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], he served as an [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] with the [[military rank]] of [[Clone Commander]] and was placed in [[Rancor Battalion]]. During the Clone Wars, he oversaw [[clone trooper]] training with ARC [[Commander]]s [[Colt]], [[Havoc (ARC trooper)|Havoc]], and [[Hammer (ARC trooper)|Hammer]] on Kamino.<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> At some point, Blitz journeyed into a [[forest]] with Colt, Havoc, and another ARC trooper. During that mission, Blitz prepared to take out a group of [[B2-series super battle droid]]s with the others ARC troopers in an ambush.<ref name="BattleCards">{{Asmodee|subdomain=cdn.svc|domain=net|url=production-amgcom/uploads/2025/04/DOC13_BattleCards-1.pdf|text=''Star Wars: Legion'' 2025 Updated Battle Cards (PDF)}}</ref>

In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Blitz joined Colt, Hammer, and Havoc in overseeing the final training stages of several [[clone cadet]] [[squad]]s, including [[Domino Squad]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> Later, Blitz participated in the [[Battle of Kamino]]<ref name="ARC" /> in [[21 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> As [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Trident-class assault ship|Trident drills]] attacked, Blitz led three [[Advanced Recon Force trooper]]s to destroy them, directing the [[trooper]]s to fire a [[Missile|rocket]] at one of the drill ships. Of those ARC troopers who took part in the battle, Colt and Havoc were both killed.<ref name="ARC" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:Blitz_posing.png|thumb|right|180px|ARC trooper Commander Blitz]]
As a [[human]] clone of the bounty hunter Jango Fett, Blitz stood at a height of 1.83 [[meter]]s<ref name="Clone Troopers" /> and weighed eighty [[kilogram]]s.<ref name="Weight" />

==Skills and abilities==
Blitz was a Clone ARC Commander who served as an ARC Trooper in the Rancor Battalion.<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> and led ARF troopers into battle.<ref name="ARC" /> As an ARC Trooper, Blitz was trained in armed and unarmed combat as well as military tactics, survival, reconnaissance and infiltrations.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'' &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s</ref>

==Equipment==
Blitz wore [[Phase I ARC Trooper Armor]] with yellow markings, a white [[kama]] with black diamonds embroidered on its design, and a yellow dual [[pauldron]]. Blitz also had a yellow [[rangefinder]] on his [[helmet]] and a life support backpack. Blitz's helmet had a yellow stripe running downward on the left side, that followed through his breast plate.<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> Blitz also had ammunition cartridges on top of his chest plates.<ref name="JtB Corresponding">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''&mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Havoc (ARC trooper)]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Blitz first appeared in the episode "[[Clone Cadets]]," albeit unidentified.<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> He was first identified in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], on the card Clone ARC Commander Blitz, released as part of the Rank & File set on the ''[[Star Wars: Card Trader]]'' app in [[2018]].<ref name="Card Trader" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity, he was first identified by the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (toy line)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' toy line]] set Defend Kamino.<ref name="Defend Kamino">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Clone Wars (toy line)|packtype=Battle Pack|pack=Defend Kamino|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/defend_kamino.asp}}</ref>
