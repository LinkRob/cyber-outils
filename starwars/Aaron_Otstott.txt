'''Aaron Otstott''' was a client programmer for ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''<ref name="SW:TOR" /> and a client engineer for ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Rise of the Hutt Cartel]]''.<ref name="SW:TOR:ROTHC" /> They were credited with "Engineering" on ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Galactic Starfighter]]'',<ref name="SW:TOR:GS" /> ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Knights of the Fallen Empire]]'', and ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Knights of the Eternal Throne]]''. They were also credited with "Programming" on ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Legacy of the Sith]].<ref name="SW:TOR:LOTS" />
