'''Francis Tsai''' was an American comic-book artist, illustrator, author and conceptual artist who worked in the entertainment industry since 1998. His distinctive and award-winning style of work was used worldwide in a variety of media including commercials, books, comics and movies.

==Biography==
Born in Honolulu, Hawaii and raised in Lubbock, Texas, Francis Tsai's interest in art began at a young age. Francis Tsai's college education was in physical chemistry and quantum mechanics. He went on to receive a graduate degree in architecture, which proved to be a strong foundation for his design work.

===Illustration work===
Francis Tsai has created covers for [[Marvel Comics]] and DC Comics/Wildstorm Publications.

Francis Tsai provided artwork for many of [[Wizards of the Coast]]'s role-playing games, such as ''Dungeons and Dragons'', ''Magic: The Gathering'', and ''Star Wars''.
