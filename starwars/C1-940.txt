'''C1-940''' was a [[C1-series astromech droid]] optimized to function in the permafrost desert of [[Jedha]] and its spiritual nature.<ref name="Toy" />

==Behind the scenes==
This [[droid]] was a toy made available exclusively at [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] D23 convention starting on [[September 9]]th, [[2022]]. It was later made available internationally and on ShopDisney.com's UK website. It's coloring is inspired by [[Mickey Mouse]]'s costume from "The Sorcerer's Apprentice" segment of the animated film ''[[Wikipedia:Fantasia (1940 film)|Fantasia]]''. The droid's name comes from the film's [[1940s|1940]] release date.<ref name="GF.com">{{WebCite|url=https://galacticfigures.com/figureDetails.aspx?id=30536|text=C1-940 (D23 Expo 2022)|work=GalacticFigures.com|archivedate=20230314213856}}</ref>

"The Sorcerer's Apprentice" also inspired other [[Star Wars: Droid Factory (toy line)|Droid Factory]] toys, with Mickey's hat in the film made available for its customizable droid figures, as well as its [[R4-D23]] and [[R5-D23]] figures.
