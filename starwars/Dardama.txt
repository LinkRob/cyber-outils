'''Dardama''' was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]] who was a member of the rebel group led by [[Yendor Brethen]]. In the [[Standard year|year]]<ref name="LD" /> [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> Yendor and his team went to [[Ryloth Insurgency|relief]] the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] but they only found an abandoned Imperial station with an injured [[Chorn|stormtrooper]]. The Twi'leks seemed prepared to show him compassion, but Dardama fired a blaster, ending his life.<ref name="LD" />
