[[File:Dark_Side_symbol.png|thumb|right|200px|Ancient symbol of the dark side of the Force]]
The '''dark side of the Force''', also known as '''Bogan''',<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref> regarded as the '''dark side of the Unity''' by the [[Sorcerers of Tund]],<ref name="The High Republic 2022 7">[[The High Republic (2022) 7|''The High Republic'' (2022) 7]]</ref> and called the '''shadow''' by the [[Knights of Ren]],<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 2|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 2]]</ref> was one of two aspects of [[the Force]].<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref> To tap into the power of the dark side was to indulge in raw emotions such as passion, anger, vengeance, and hatred. The dark side was greed, the fear of change, and the inability to let go. By holding on to things, one became angry and hateful, which in turn led to suffering. Those who were unable to conquer their dark side were devoured by it.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> The [[Sith]] were [[Force-sensitive]]s well known for serving the will of the dark side and being the mortal enemies of the [[Jedi]], who served the will of the [[light side of the Force]].<ref name="Episode I" /><ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref>

The dark side was associated with the night. As with the light side, it was an integral part of all living things, and as the shadow of the [[soul]], the dark side had to be continually overcome by [[Sentience|sentients]] to walk the path of the light; indulging in the dark side led to imbalance.<ref name="Destiny">{{TCW|Destiny (The Clone Wars)}}</ref> The dark side was not more powerful than the light side; it was an easier, albeit destructive, path. Failing to resist the temptation of darkness would allow the dark side to dominate one's destiny,<ref name="Episode V" /> although redemption was always a possibility.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

[[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Chosen One|gifted]] in the Force,<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> succumbed to the seduction of the dark side because of his refusal to let go of his attachments. Skywalker was attached to [[Shmi Skywalker Lars|his mother]] and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|his wife]], and the possibility of their [[death]]s caused him to rebel against the natural course of life and the Force itself, seeking [[Immortality|power]] in his fear of losing his loved ones and becoming a hateful, disfigured [[cyborg]] and an agent of evil.<ref name="Episode III" /><ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>

==Definition==
{{Quote|Strike me down with all of your hatred and your journey towards the dark side will be complete!|[[Darth Sidious]]|<ref name="Episode VI" />}}
The dark side was often given into because it tempted its possible users; even the [[Son]],<ref name="Ghosts">{{TCW|Ghosts of Mortis}}</ref> a [[Force wielder]] who embodied the dark side,<ref name="Overlords2">{{TCW|Overlords}}</ref> was unable to resist it.<ref name="Ghosts" /> [[Farmer]] [[Kaeden Larte]], who had previously seen her friend [[Ahsoka Tano]] use the [[light side of the Force]], felt as though the dark side was unnatural and wrong upon seeing the [[Sixth Brother]] use the [[Force choke]] ability.<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref> Nonetheless, the dark side did exist naturally within every [[Sentience|sentient]] being in some form, as every being had the capacity for emotions such as fear,<ref name="Destiny" /> anger, and hatred.<ref name="Episode I" /> Should an individual try to deny that darkness existed within them, that darkness would only gather strength. The key to resisting the dark side, therefore, was to recognize its existence and yet to still actively choose the light side.<ref name="Destiny" />

It was stated that the Sith referred to the dark side as "dark" because it was secretive. The Sith kept their knowledge and identities secret because they believed it would make it easier for them to impose order.<ref name="Aphra 32">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 32|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 32]]</ref> Nonetheless, the dark side of [[the Force]] was defined as anger, fear, aggression<ref name="Episode V" /> and a lust for power.<ref name="Episode III" /> Living a life defined by selfishness, filled with a grasping for more, and powered by fear, the dark side created a life of horror for those who embraced it.<ref name="Insider228">{{InsiderCite|228|Phantoms of the Space Opera}}</ref>

==The essence of the dark side==
===Overview===
{{Quote|You've told me your existence has been nothing but pain and loss. Never feeling secure. Never having a home. You deserve so much, and I can get it for us. The life we'll be able to have together once&mdash;''"<br />"''What kind of life will that be, Quinlan? The kind where we're slaves to our hatred? Our rage? That's what the dark side made me. That's what it ''does''. Nothing is ever enough. You get more, and more, but you're never happy. It's a trap baited with all the things you want most in life&mdash;and it's not worth living. I already left that behind.|[[Quinlan Vos]] and Asajj Ventress|<ref name="disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>}}
[[File:ComeGetMeBoys-THE.png|thumb|right|300px|Having previously given in to it, Asajj Ventress (pictured during her days as a dark side adept) knew that the dark side never brought true happiness, as those who used it were never satisfied.]]
The dark side was selfishness and possession, interlocked with the fear of loss that gave rise to anger and hate and brought suffering to those on whom its power was unleashed and who embraced it alike. As noted by former [[Dark Acolyte]] [[Asajj Ventress]], those who followed the dark side were never satisfied, always desiring more than what they gained.<ref name="disciple" /> In fact, [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] stated that giving into fear, anger and hate was a path to the dark side. He further declared that suffering was an inevitable next step for the individual who had given into the dark side after they reached hate.<ref name="Episode I" /> Yoda believed that once someone took the dark path, it would dominate their destiny forever due to its addictive nature.<ref name="Episode V" /> Nevertheless, a return to the light side was always possible.<ref name="Episode VI" /><ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> An example of living in line with the dark was hating the Jedi and hoping for them to fail or die.<ref name="The High Republic: Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

[[Dark side adept]]s, such as the [[Sith Master]] [[Darth Sidious]] and his [[Sith apprentice]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], maintained that hatred was the key to their strength.<ref name="DV 2020 33">[[Darth Vader (2020) 33|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 33]]</ref><ref name="DV 2020 40">[[Darth Vader (2020) 40|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 40]]</ref> Despite Yoda's warning that one would find only suffering after they gave into hate,<ref name="Episode I" /> the fallen [[Jedi]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]], known also as the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Tyrannus, declared that he had persevered and moved beyond the stage of suffering in order to secure ultimate power.<ref name="disciple" /> Alternatively, Sidious claimed that one could find power in hatred, with suffering only an alternative path if one failed to forge their hate into power. Vader believed the same<ref name="DV 2020 34">[[Darth Vader (2020) 34|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 34]]</ref> even though, in truth, loneliness,<ref name="There is Another">{{StoryCite|story=There is Another|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> pain, and sadness born from his past existed under the Sith Lord's surface.<ref name="Lords">''[[Lords of the Sith]]''</ref> Vader was someone who tried to overcome that past time<ref name="DV 2015 24">[[Darth Vader (2015) 24|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 24]]</ref> and time again.<ref name="DV 2020 34" /> Vader also once demanded [[Sabé]], who was not [[Force-sensitive]] but had the chance to serve him as his [[lieutenant]], chose between suffering or power.<ref name="DV 2020 33" /> She, however, rejected his question altogether by deciding not to hate.<ref name="DV 2020 34" />

[[File:SWDarthVader24.png|thumb|left|250px|Darth Vader believed that his hatred gave him power, but great suffering existed under the dark lord's surface.]]
Sidious's power in the dark side was so great that he fed off the hatred of others; as he told Vader during the [[Scourge of Coruscant]], in which Vader had tried to test his master with his increased power in the Force granted by his hate, Sidious thrived off the hatred Vader and anyone else was radiating towards him. In effect, Vader's efforts to increase his powers with hate of his master had only made Sidious stronger.<ref name="DV 2020 40" /> Indeed, the [[Scourge]] told Vader that his fear and hate only made him into a stronger weapon for Sidious, instead of allowing him to live on his own terms.<ref name="DV 2020 41">[[Darth Vader (2020) 41|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 41]]</ref> Vader only managed to slay Sidious when he rejected the ways of the dark, reembracing his existence as the Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker. Rather than act out of a desire to overthrow the Sith Master for personal power, Skywalker did so out of love for his son, [[Luke Skywalker]], and returned to the light side he had once rejected.<ref name="Episode VI" />

The [[Jedi]] [[Elan Rast]] once reflected that the dark side presented itself as a release to someone's needs, effectively blinding them to their greater missions by acting as a short term solution. Advising his disciple [[Tensu Run]] to not give into his anger and warning him such a loss of control would only lead to the dark side, Elan advised Run to carry his pain and frustrations with him, which Run took as a sign to cleanse himself of such emotions instead of letting them be released into the world.<ref name="Inquisitors 2">[[Inquisitors 2|''Inquisitors'' 2]]</ref> [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] believed that, as the power of the dark side rose, the light side would naturally grow in strength to meet it. Indeed, he believed that, as his apprentice [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren's]] power in the dark side grew, an equal from the light side would emerge to challenge him.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> After the [[human]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey's]] powers were awakened<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> and her existence was revealed to Snoke, he heralded her as the individual whose rise he had warned Ren of.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Hubris===
{{Quote|The beast is you and you are the beast. To deny it simply gives it power.|[[Serenity]], to Yoda|<ref name="Destiny" />}}
[[File:DarkSide-SecretsOfTheSith.png|thumb|right|250px|The dark side promised power, but it could also be a pathway to suffering.]]
The dark side was selfishness, fear, and aggressive feelings in all living things. However, beings were often tempted to deny and try to escape from this truth, imagining the dark side to be something external, something that is not a part of them, something that they can rid themselves of. Such hubris was easy to fall into&mdash;even Grand Master Yoda, who trained to conquer his darkness for hundreds of years, initially refused to recognize his own dark reflection. However, in denying this truth he only fed the dark side. Once he admitted he had darkness though, he dominated his dark reflection and burnt it up with the Force, banishing it away.<ref name="Destiny" />

In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] confronted his former [[Padawan]], the fallen [[Jedi Knight]] Anakin Skywalker, on the [[Volcano|volcanic]] [[planet]] of [[Mustafar]]. Skywalker had turned to the dark side, creating the persona of the [[Sith Lord]] Darth Vader, and believed himself capable of overthrowing his Sith Master Darth Sidious.<ref name="Episode III" /> The [[Duel on Mustafar|duel]] between Kenobi and Vader resulted in a stalemate,<ref name="Obi-Wan DB">{{Databank|obi-wan-kenobi|Obi-Wan Kenobi}}</ref> although Vader refused to concede despite his former master gaining the high ground.<ref name="Episode III" /> The dark side filled his senses with rage and hubris, causing Vader to ignore Kenobi's warning as he attempted another attack.<ref name="Obi-Wan DB" /> Vader was consequently disarmed by Kenobi's counterattack, losing the [[Skywalker lightsaber]] and his organic [[limb]]s in the process. As a result, he nearly [[Fire|burned]] to [[death]] on the banks of the [[Magma|lava]] river.<ref name="Episode III" />

===Attachment===
{{Quote|Rejoice for those around us who transform into the Force. Mourn them, do not. Miss them, do not. Attachment leads to jealousy, the shadow of greed, that is.|Yoda, to Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:Anakin_Padme_Hug.png|thumb|left|250px|Anakin Skywalker's attachment to his wife, Padmé Amidala, was the catalyst for his conversion to the dark side.]]
Living beings tended to mistake attachment for love, whereas attachment was possession and as such, like everything of the dark side, had selfishness at its core. Since it was possession, it fed into the fear of loss, which was yet another face of greed. When attachment creeped onto love, it became pain just like Anakin Skywalker described it: love was haunted by the fear of loss and the fear of the pain that loss would bring about&mdash;thus, it was not love, but a prison, in which the loved and the lover were both prisoners.<ref name="Overlords">{{TCW|Overlords}}</ref> Attachment led to jealousy and it was always followed by fear, which led to hate.<ref name="Episode III" />

Anakin Skywalker struggled to balance his life as a Jedi with the personal attachments he formed, in particular with [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] whom he [[Marriage|married]] despite the [[Jedi Code]]. Throughout the [[Clone Wars]], Skywalker had faithfully served the [[Jedi Order]] and the [[Galactic Republic]]. Ultimately, however, he was prepared to sacrifice [[the galaxy]] rather than lose Amidala.<ref name="Skywalker: A Family at War">''[[Skywalker: A Family at War]]''</ref> Alternatively, the young Jedi [[Ezra Bridger]] fully embraced the light side by rejecting Maul's offer of apprenticeship and, later, was forced to accept his mentor [[Kanan Jarrus]]'s demise at Ahsoka Tano's urging in the [[World Between Worlds]] despite thinking he had a chance to change Jarrus's fate. Later, Bridger's attachment to his family was exploited by Sidious, who used Bridger's longing and love for his parents to try and convince him into opening a new gateway into the World Between Worlds during the [[Liberation of Lothal]], but he instead destroyed the portal to stop the Sith Lord's plans.<ref name="Born Rebel">{{InsiderCite|227|Born to Rebel}}</ref>

===Greed for power===
{{Quote|Power! Unlimited power!|Darth Sidious|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:Palpatine-hd.png|thumb|right|250px|Darth Sidious, who coveted unlimited power, ruled the galaxy as Emperor of the Galactic Empire.]]
The trap of the dark side was that whatever one selfishly gained through it, it was never enough. The Sith were butchering each other, victims of their own greed, [[Jedi-Sith War|which led to their defeat and near destruction during their wars against the Jedi]].<ref name="Sacrifice">{{TCW|Sacrifice (episode)}}</ref> Darth Sidious' ultimate desire was to use the powers of the dark side to [[Alter Reality|alter]] the [[universe]] itself.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> Anakin Skywalker, who initially only wanted the power to defeat death out of attachment to his wife, ultimately developed the desire to rule the galaxy and make things the way he wanted them to be.<ref name="Episode III" />

Although [[Jedi]] were taught to follow the light side of the Force, the dark side was seductive, and one had to be at calm, at peace, to be passive in order to have a clear mind and thus, a clear judgment, to be mindful of thoughts and feelings.<ref name="Episode V" /> Thus, they could avoid falling to the dark side. Even a Jedi raised in the traditions of the Jedi Order for years could be corrupted by the lure of the dark side in certain situations.<ref name="Episode III" /> Despite this, the Jedi often described the dark side as a "quick and easy path."<ref name="Episode V" />

There were some dark side users who used it for benevolent motives, not just selfishness and greed. [[Darth Plagueis]] used it to immortalize the beings he loved,<ref name="Episode III" /> [[Dooku]] wanted to use it to destroy the corrupt Republic and bring a just new galactic order,<ref name="Episode II" /> the [[Son]] deceived himself saying he wanted the best for the galaxy,<ref name="Altar">{{TCW|Altar of Mortis (episode)}}</ref> and [[Anakin Skywalker]] before becoming Darth Vader wanted to save his wife by studying Plagueis.<ref name="Episode III" />

===Dark side abilities===
====Powers====
{{SeeAlso|Sith sorcery}}
{{Quote|The dark side of the Force is a pathway to many abilities some consider to be&hellip;unnatural.|Darth Sidious, to Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:DarthSidiousLightning-RotJAVA.png|thumb|left|250px|Force lightning was an ability of the dark side that Sith Lords such as Darth Sidious mastered.]]
Relying on anger, fear, hatred, and bitterness, the dark side of the Force granted certain abilities that wielders of the light side had no access to, such as [[possession]]<ref name="Visions and Voices">{{Rebels|Visions and Voices}}</ref><ref name="Son of Dathomir 4">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 4|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 4]]</ref> and the [[Dark illusion|ability to manipulate]] [[Force vision]]s.<ref name="Sacrifice" /> To use the dark side was to let anger and hatred for someone wash them away entirely, allowing the individual to be filled with anger and hate and the desire to cause pain, suffering,<ref name="Witches of the Mist">{{TCW|Witches of the Mist}}</ref> and to dominate others.<ref name="Twin Suns">{{Rebels|Twin Suns (episode)}}</ref> The [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis|Cal Kestis's]] use of the dark side in the [[Nova Garon system]] was a prime example of what the dark side can do. Consumed by anger, Kestis used the dark side to extraordinarily enhance his powers, leaving a trail of destruction through heavily armed enemies. <ref name=":1">[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]</ref>

Most dark side powers were purely offensive in nature, such as [[Force lightning]]. Dark side power could also be used to alter life and could, according to [[Tragedy of Darth Plagueis the Wise|legend]], be used to manipulate [[midi-chlorian]]s to create life or keep someone from dying.<ref name="Episode III" /> The dark-side historian and Sith cultist [[Yupe Tashu]] told [[Wedge Antilles]] on [[Akiva]] that some Sith Lords were able to siphon the Force from their captives in order to extend their own lives for centuries beyond their natural expiration.<ref name="Aftermath" /> Dark side abilities were considered by some to be "unnatural."<ref name="Episode III" />

====Will and usage====
{{Quote|Fear is the path to the dark side. Fear leads to anger. Anger leads to hate. Hate&hellip;leads to suffering.|Yoda|<ref name="Episode I" />}}
[[File:Return_of_the_jedi_4.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The dark side tempted Luke Skywalker to kill his father, Darth Vader.]]
Luke Skywalker was tempted by the dark side when he attacked his father in fear of losing his sister just like Anakin Skywalker pledged loyalty to Darth Sidious in fear of losing his love.<ref name="The Star Wars Archives: Episodes I–III, 1999–2005">''[[The Star Wars Archives: Episodes I–III, 1999–2005]]''</ref> Jedi Padawan [[Ezra Bridger]] drew upon his fear, anger and hate to summon a massive [[fyrnock]] to attack [[the Grand Inquisitor]] for fear of losing his [[Spectres|friends]].<ref name="duel">''[[Ezra's Duel with Danger]]''</ref> In Nova Garon, when Cal Kestis came face to face with [[Lank Denvik]], someone he intensely hated at that moment, after using the dark side to eliminate many enemies with great ferocity, he nearly killed him, but restrained himself when the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Merrin]] convinced him not to, guided by anger. Kestis acknowledged that this use of the dark side of the Force almost caused him to lose himself.<ref name=":1" />

A dark side adept's devotion to pain could have specific reasons for existing. After Anakin Skywalker became the Sith Lord Darth Vader, he was a pained man that tried to use more pain to soothe himself. The attempted remedy came from trying to hurt not only himself, but others, yet it failed to work.<ref name="There is Another">{{StoryCite|story=There is Another|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> In fact, even after spending years hating his former master Obi-Wan Kenobi,<ref name="The Siege of Lothal">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''</ref> his death only increased the loneliness he felt.<ref name="There is Another" />

===Dark presence===
{{Quote|Wait. Do you sense that? It feels&hellip;''"<br />"''Cold.|Ben Solo and Luke Skywalker|<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" />}}
The power of the dark side was penetrating and pernicious. The [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] was built on top of an ancient [[Shrine in the Depths|Sith shrine]] and, unbeknownst to the Jedi, the power of the dark side seeped upwards through the Temple from the shrine, slowly weakening the Jedi over a millennium.<ref name="Tarkin" />

[[File:CaveOfEvil-Voices.png|thumb|left|250px|The Cave of Evil on Dagobah was an example of a place with a high concentration of the dark side.]]
If the presence of the dark side was significantly powerful, nearby Force-sensitives often feel the sensation of cold. This happened on several occasions to Ezra Bridger and Kanan Jarrus in the presence of Darth Vader, and also occurred when Luke sensed the dark energy emanating from the [[Cave of Evil]] on [[Dagobah]].<ref name="Episode V" /><ref name="TSOL">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''</ref> Extensive use of the dark side could leave an entire planet corrupted by its energy. [[Dathomir]] was one place that was heavily steeped in the dark side.<ref name="disciple" /> The [[Tomb of Kujet]], located on Dathomir, was a place on the planet where the shadow of the dark side of the Force weighed particularly heavily.<ref>[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]</ref> This could also resonate through physical objects physically associated with individuals with a strong dark side presence,<ref name="DLOTS 24">[[Darth Vader (2017) 24|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 24]]</ref> such as that of [[Darth Momin]] and his [[Mask of Lord Momin|mask]]. Momin's mask corrupted nearby beings and turned them into mindless killers.<ref name="Lando 3">[[Lando 3|''Lando'' 3]]</ref>

===Perception===
====Jedi====
{{Quote|The dark side consumes. And there is no balance to be found in such a path.|Qui-Gon Jinn|<ref name="Age of Republic - Qui-Gon Jinn 1">[[Age of Republic - Qui-Gon Jinn 1|''Age of Republic - Qui-Gon Jinn'' 1]]</ref>}}
[[File:YodavsHisDarknessf-Destiny.png|thumb|right|250px|Yoda had to overcome his dark side in order to learn the way of immortality.]]
The dark side of the Force was highly corrupting. According to Grand Master Yoda, the destiny of one who embraced the dark side would be forever dominated by it,<ref name="Episode V" /> but redemption was never impossible; choices and actions, both selfless and selfish, were what paved one's path to follow through life. The Jedi knew that the dark side is fear, leading to anger, which in turn leads to hatred, and finally leading to suffering&mdash;mostly for the one who embraced the dark side. The Jedi conquered their dark side through patience, training and discipline, thereby not giving it power over themselves.<ref name="Destiny" />

When the Jedi Order's [[Infiltration of the Nihil Tempest|attempt to infiltrate]] the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] failed in the [[skirmish on Xais]], Jedi Master [[Avar Kriss]],<ref name="The High Republic 12">[[The High Republic (2021) 12|''The High Republic'' (2021) 12]]</ref> even though she had supported the plan,<ref name="The High Republic 10">[[The High Republic (2021) 10|''The High Republic'' (2021) 10]]</ref> took it as proof that the Jedi and others who deemed themselves servants of the light side could not afford to hide within shadows. She deemed that to be the territory of their enemies and something the Jedi needed to rise above. As such, their next attack, the [[Battle for No-Space]], was a direct assault against the Nihil.<ref name="The High Republic 12">[[The High Republic (2021) 12|''The High Republic'' (2021) 12]]</ref>

====Sith====
{{Quote|The dark side is strength. I am that strength.|Darth Vader|<ref name="Darth Vader 20">[[Darth Vader (2015) 20|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 20]]</ref>}}
[[File:Displate-DarthVaderattack.png|thumb|left|300px|The Sith were devotees of the dark side, using anger and hate to harness its power.]]
In contrast to the Jedi who adhered to the ways of the light side,<ref name="Jedi Order DB">{{Databank|jedi-order|Jedi Order}}</ref> the Sith were devotees of the dark side of the Force. Desiring power at any cost, the Sith practiced the ways of hate, deception, and ambition in order to harness the energies of the dark side.<ref name="Sith DB">{{Databank|sith|Sith}}</ref>

Darth Sidious stated his power came from his hatred<ref name="DV 2020 33" /> and the hatred from others that he thrived off of. In effect, the hate one held towards Sidious only made him stronger.<ref name="DV 2020 40" /> However, according to [[Yupe Tashu]], Sidious also believed that the source of his dark side power came from the universe [[Unknown Regions|beyond the edges]] of the known galaxy.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> Drawing on his pain<ref name="Lords" /> and hatred,<ref name="DV 2020 10">[[Darth Vader (2020) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 10]]</ref> Darth Vader saw the dark side as strength and believed he embodied its power.<ref name="Darth Vader 20" />

Lord Momin saw the dark side of the Force as a higher power Specifically, Momin believed in the creation of art that glorified the dark side, which could, in his view, grant the artist eternal life.<ref name="DLOTS 22">[[Darth Vader (2017) 22|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 22]]</ref> Such extremist views led to both Jedi and Sith [[holocron]] [[Timeline of galactic history|chroniclers]]' neglect of Momin.<ref name="DLOTS 21">[[Darth Vader (2017) 21|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 21]]</ref> When Momin's spirit restored his body and dueled Vader, he decried the modern Sith by claiming they had failed the dark side to focus on destroying the Jedi, but Momin himself was then killed by Vader.<ref name="DLOTS 24">[[Darth Vader (2017) 24|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 24]]</ref>

====Knights of Ren====
{{Quote|You said you could sense the shadow in all of us&mdash;that's right. We've all got it. But you know your apprentice does too, right? Powerful. Can feel it. Better be careful, Jedi man.|Ren, to Luke Skywalker|<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" />}}
[[File:The_Rise_of_Kylo_Ren_art_reveal.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Knights of Ren were a new generation of dark-side followers led by Kylo Ren, the grandson of Darth Vader, but their true allegiance was to Darth Sidious.]]
The ability to use the dark side was a requirement to join the Knights of Ren.<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 1">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 1|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 1]]</ref> As a condition, however, a potential recruit had to willingly kill someone as a form of sacrifice.<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 4">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 4|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 4]]</ref>

By [[19 ABY]], The Knights of Ren consisted of seven elite warriors<ref name="KOR DB">{{Databank|knights-of-ren|Knights of Ren}}</ref>&mdash;[[Ap'lek]], [[Cardo]], [[Kuruk]], [[Ren (human)|Ren]], [[Trudgen]], [[Ushar]], and [[Vicrul]]&mdash;who roamed the galaxy as marauders, particularly the [[Unknown Regions]] where they attacked isolated communities on sparsely populated worlds. Although members of the Knights of Ren were Force-sensitive, they did not adhere to any Force-based religion.<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> Nevertheless, the Knights' source of power was the dark side of the Force, which was referred to as the "shadow" by their leader,<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" />

As dark-side wielders who thrived in a lawless environment,<ref name="TSWB" /> the Knights of Ren held beliefs that were diametrically opposed to the traditions of the Jedi Order.<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" /> According to Supreme Leader Snoke of the [[First Order]], the Knights' disregard for rules granted them flexibility and the freedom to act as they pleased, whereas the Jedi, in Snoke's view, were rigid due to their code. Despite Snoke's perception of the Knights' strength,<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 4" /> Jedi Master [[Luke Skywalker]] observed that they lacked discipline and proper training,<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" /> and possessed only the most basic skills of the dark side.<ref name="TSWB" />

[[File:Acme-PulloftheLight.png|thumb|left|250px|Kylo Ren endures the pulls of the light and dark sides]]
Ben Solo, the grandson of Anakin Skywalker and nephew to Luke Skywalker, ultimately joined the Knights of Ren following the [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destruction]] of his uncle's [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker|Jedi Temple]].<ref name="TSWB" /> Solo's connection to the dark side was sensed by Ren during their [[Mission to Elphrona|first encounter]] on [[Elphrona]]; the dark warrior encouraged Skywalker's apprentice to learn about the dark side by joining the Knights of Ren.<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" /> Intrigued by the Knights' philosophy and its emphasis on power,<ref name="TSWB" /> Solo&mdash;overwhelmed by Ren's murder of his friend [[Tai]]&mdash;gave into the dark side and killed Ren during a [[Skirmish on the Minemoon|fight]],<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 4" /> adopting the mantle of Kylo Ren and becoming the [[Ren|Master of the Knights of Ren]].<ref name="TSWB" /> While Kylo was neither a Jedi nor a Sith, both disciplines influenced Kylo's understanding of the Force.<ref name="Kylo Ren DB">{{Databank|kylo-ren|Supreme Leader Kylo Ren}}</ref>

Kylo Ren, however, would find himself troubled by a pull to return to the light side. During the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], he tried to overcome his regrets and fully embrace the dark side by killing his father [[Han Solo]],<ref name="Episode VII" /> but the act only further conflicted Ren. Despite his emotional disturbance, Ren nevertheless again tried to cement himself into the dark side upon killing Snoke and assuming the mantle of Supreme Leader for himself.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> During the [[mission to Kef Bir]], the sacrifice of his mother [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo]] helped push Ren back to the light once and for all. After a discussion with what he believed to be a memory of his father, the redeemed Ben Solo helped overthrow the resurrected Sidious in the [[Battle of Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

====Other groups====
The [[Sorcerers of Tund]] regarded members of their order who embraced the dark side as [[Apostate (religion)|apostates]]. Due to their alternate name for the Force, the Sorcerers called the dark side the "dark side of the Unity."<ref name="The High Republic 2022 7" /> With the [[moon]] of [[Jedha]] being important to many Force religions, the Sorcerers, Jedi and other groups were represented on the [[Convocation of the Force]],<ref name="POD">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> but dark side faiths were not welcome.<ref name="Peace and Unity">{{StoryCite|story=Peace and Unity|book=The High Republic (2022) 1}}</ref> Most members of the [[Dank Graks]]<ref name="Celebration 2022">{{SWYouTube|swclPDFHKNk|Star Wars Celebration LIVE! &ndash; DAY 1|nolive=1}}</ref> criminal faction<ref name="THRA 1">[[The High Republic Adventures (2022) 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2022) 1]]</ref> were former members of dark side-aligned cults.<ref name="Celebration 2022" /> The [[Path of the Open Hand]] cult regarded all who used the Force with distain, believing that any use of the Force could result in an equal amount of harm elsewhere in the universe. As such, the [[Children of the Open Hand|Children]] of the Path worked to liberate [[Force artifact]]s, including those created by followers of the dark side, and lock them away to prevent their use.<ref name="POD" /> The [[Final Occultation]] was a dark side cult that, unlike the "order" advocated for by the Sith, believed in bringing chaos<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/marvel-darth-maul-black-white-and-red-reveal|text=Marvel's ''Darth Maul – Black, White & Red'' Coming April 2024 - Exclusive}}</ref> to the entire galaxy.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/110338/star_wars_darth_maul_-_black_white_red_2024_1|text=Star Wars: Darth Maul &ndash; Black, White & Red (2024) #1}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the [[Adventures of the Starkiller, Episode I: The Star Wars|second]] and [[The Star Wars: From The Adventures of Luke Starkiller|third drafts]] of the script for [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''A New Hope'']], the dark side of the Force is called "the Bogan". [[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow|''Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow'']] [[canon]]ized "the Bogan" as a name for the dark side.

As George Lucas explained numerous times over the years, the dark side was greed, self-centeredness, fear, anger, hate, bitterness, possessiveness, not being able to let go of things&mdash;and thus, it was the opposite of the light side of the Force, which was compassion, caring about others, love, fairness, charity and hope. He stressed that the dark and light sides are not malevolent or benevolent in themselves, but one must stay on the light side to keep the Force in balance and do the right thing.<ref name=":0">{{WebCite|url=https://www.wired.com/1999/05/illusion|text=Wired - Illusion 1999|archivedate=20161220160229}}</ref><ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.sci-fi-online.com/Interview/05-11-01_GeorgeLucas.htm|text=Sci-fi Online interview 2005 - George Lucas|archivedate=20080829195528}}</ref><ref>{{YouTube|video=9nFMBBrliyQ|text=YouTube "George Lucas Expertly Explaining How the Force Works|channel=@StarWarsCoffee|name=StarWarsCoffee}}</ref><ref>{{WebCite|url=https://content.time.com/time/subscriber/article/0,33009,924122-6,00.html|text=Time Magazine - The Empire Strikes Back!|archivedate=20210612015715}}</ref><ref>{{WebCite|url=http://content.time.com/time/magazine/article/0,9171,23298-1,00.html|text=Time Magazine - "Of Myth And Men" 1999|archivedate=20161012173643}}</ref>
