'''Dryden Vos's petars''' were the custom made [[Kyuzo]] [[petar]]s commissioned by [[Dryden Vos]].

==Description==
[[File:DrydenVos_DoRVG.png|thumb|left|250px|An angered Vos holds an activated petar.]]
A double-bladed [[dagger]]-like [[melee weapon]], the petars were designed by the [[Kyuzo]] for [[Dryden Vos]], the leader of [[Crimson Dawn]]. Cast in [[bronzium]], the weapons featured a conductive tempered carbon edge that became energized with a [[monomolecular energy cord|monomolecular laser sheath]] with the flick of a switch.<ref name="100 Objects" /> This vastly increased the cutting power and deadliness of the weapon. Powering this deadly edge were rechargeable [[energy cell]]s sealed within the hilt of the weapon.<ref name="Official Guide" /> The blades were styled to fit Vos's [[near-human]] hands, including a hole for his sharpened thumb [[claw]]s. The petars were weighted to perfectly suit Vos's particular style of combat, which incorporated the [[Unarmed combat|martial art]] known as [[Teräs Käsi]].<ref name="100 Objects" />

==History==
[[10 BBY|Ten years]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]], during a party aboard his personal [[yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]'', Dryden Vos used one of his petars to murder [[Governor]] [[Diles Anevi]] who had angered the [[crime lord]] during negotiations over a deal that would waive travel restrictions on Crimson Dawn [[starship]]s moving along the [[Great Gran Run]] [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="Solo TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>

[[File:DrydenVQira2-SASWS.png|thumb|right|300px|Vos drives his energized petar into Qi'ra's vibrocutlass.]]
When he was later betrayed by his [[lieutenant]], [[Qi'ra]], and [[Han Solo]], Vos attempted to kill the [[smuggler]] with his petars, throwing one which became embedded in the wall. He was then forced to defend himself with the remaining blade when Q'ira attacked him with a [[vibrocutlass]]. Vos used the energized blade to cut into the sword, causing the petar to become stuck. Without a weapon, Vos was then impaled and killed by his former protégé.<ref name="Solo" />
