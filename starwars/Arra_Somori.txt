{{Quote|You see, I found your full name, Captain Arra Somori. The rift between a mother and daughter is not something we can patch with logical argument.|Tione Zrar|<ref name="Disciples of Harmony" />}}
'''Arra Somori''' was a [[Pantoran]] [[Gender|female]] who was the daughter of [[Thissa Somori]], the leader of a small village. Somori became estranged from her [[Parent|mother]], becoming the [[captain]] of a [[pirate]] crew. When her crew fell into debts with the [[Hutt Clan|Hutts]], Somori regularly returned to her mother's village, forcing the inhabitants to pay them in goods and money. Upon one visit to the village, Somori found that it was defended by the consulars [[Urma Athantras]], [[Velai]], and [[Tione Zrar]], who confronted the pirates. After the resulting standoff, Velai suggested that the pirates negotiate with the villagers, and come to a mutual agreement. During the negotiations, Zrar revealed Captain Somori's parentage, and proposed that the pirates protect the village from local [[narglatch]]es, selling the [[Species|creatures']] hides for more profit than they could get from extorting the villagers.<ref name="Disciples of Harmony" />
