'''Encryption G77''', also known as the '''G77 encryption''' or simply '''G77''', was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] encryption code reserved only for the twelve [[Grand Admiral]]s of the [[Imperial Navy]]. Grand Admiral [[Balanhai Savit]] used the encryption to communicate with the [[pirate]]s he had hired to steal components from [[Project Stardust]], and the [[Grysk]]s gained access to it when capturing the [[Unidentified mobile way station|way station]] where the pirates were based. After deducing the nature of Savit's scheme, Grand Admiral [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] used the encryption as a piece of evidence that he was guilty.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />
