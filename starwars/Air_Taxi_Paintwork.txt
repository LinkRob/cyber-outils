The '''Air Taxi Paintwork''' was a bodily configuration owned by the [[droideka]] [[Team Slingshot droideka|member]] of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]] that she could swap for her standard [[Beloved Paintwork|frame]] during the the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. This configuration featured a dark metallic [[Color|gray]] and silver frame, with yellow plating with black and white checkered patterns on both the head and torso. The droideka's [[sensor]]s featured both glowing red and orange components.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Air Taxi Paintwork was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Air Taxi Racer]].<ref name="Hunters" />
