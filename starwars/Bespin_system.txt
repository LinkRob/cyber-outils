The '''Bespin system''' was a [[star system]] situated in the [[Anoat sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="SWU" /> [[Miser]], [[Orin]], [[Velser's Ring]] and [[Bespin]] were part of the system.<ref name="Bespin OR planets" /> Located in the clouds of Bespin, [[Cloud City]] was a small [[Tibanna]] gas mining colony that tried to avoid [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] detection, but was unable to do so following the [[Battle of Hoth]].<ref name="TESB">{{Film|V}}</ref>
