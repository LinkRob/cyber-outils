The '''double-bladed vibrosword''' was a [[melee weapon]] that consisted of two full-length [[vibroblade]]s mounted at the ends of a hilt. Wielding the [[vibrosword]] required the use of two [[Limb|hands]], and was difficult and dangerous for untrained users, but could be a deadly weapon when held by a skilled combatant.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />
