A '''facial transformation program''' altered the facial [[bone]] and muscle structure of an individual to [[Reconstructive surgery|transform]] their physical appearance into that of another person. [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] utilized the program in order to disguise himself as the [[bounty hunter]] [[Rako Hardeen]], under orders from the [[Jedi High Council]]. When disguised as Hardeen, Kenobi was able to thwart [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s plan to [[Plan to kidnap Chancellor Palpatine|kidnap]] the [[Supreme Chancellor]].<ref name="Deception" />
