Early in the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[planet]] [[Yar Togna]] was brutally '''conquered'''<ref name="The Ultimate Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> and then occupied by the Empire.<ref name="Edrio">{{Databank|edrio-two-tubes|Edrio Two Tubes}}</ref> The incursion saw Imperial [[troop]]s use [[rifle]] butts and clubs against civilians and their property. Due to the brutal attacks the population suffered from the Empire, which left the planet in a ruined state,<ref name="Rise Fall" /> and with the Empire moving to [[Slavery|enslave]] the population,<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> a [[Tognath diaspora|mass exodus of its inhabitants]] occurred. The [[eggmate]]s [[Edrio]] and [[Benthic]] escaped.<ref name="The Ultimate Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] remembered the mass retreat of the [[Tognath]] as an act of cultural genocide carried out by the Empire.<ref name="Rise Fall" />
