'''6-RA-7''', also spelled "'''Six-Aray-Seven'''," was an [[RA-7 protocol droid]] in service of the [[Inquisitorius]]. On [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris']] trip to [[Pkori]], she brought 6-RA-7 under the assumption that the [[Pkorian|native people]] did not speak [[Galactic Basic Standard|Basic]].<ref name="Inquisitor" />

==Personality and traits==
6-RA-7 was painted completely [[Color|black]] with a mouthlike [[vocabulator]] that had an acid yellow glow. Being programed for translation, strategy, and torture, 6-RA-7 had an immense dissatisfaction to pleasantries, especially to people who were not members of the Inquisitorius.<ref name="Inquisitor" />
