'''''Darth Vader'' 33''' is the thirty-third issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Adam Gorham]] and [[Federico Blee]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[May 3]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''UNBOUND FORCE &ndash; Part One! When the Dark Lord of the Sith loses control of the Force, nothing is safe &ndash; not even his own ''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought. But in the face of total destruction, Commander Sabé finds an opportunity. Does Vader's untrammeled onslaught expose a vulnerability? Or is it just another doorway to the terrifying tyranny of the Sith?''
