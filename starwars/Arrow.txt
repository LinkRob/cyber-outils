An '''arrow''' was a projectile meant to be shot from a [[bow]].<ref name="DB Endor">{{Databank|endor|Endor}}</ref> Primitive arrows like the ones used by the [[Ewok]]s of the [[Endor|Forest Moon]] of [[Endor (planet)|Endor]] took the form of straight thin shafts with a pointed head at one end.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> The ones fired by the [[Nightsisters]]' [[Hunter (Nightsister)|Hunters]] of [[Dathomir]], however, were [[plasma]]-generated, just like the strings of their [[Nightsister energy bow|energy bows]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> A group of [[Zygerrian]] [[enslaver]]s on [[Ord Mantell]] also utilized similar [[Zygerrian energy bow|energy bows]], which later [[Omega]] took as her primary [[Omega's Zygerrian energy bow|weapon]].<ref name="Rampage">{{TBB|Rampage (episode)}}</ref> The Jedi Master [[Yoda]] used a bow and arrow to hunt a [[Unidentified quadruped (Dagobah)|quadruped]] for its meat while he was in exile on [[Dagobah]], and he successfully managed to kill one of the beasts by shooting it in the neck with an arrow.<ref name="Age of Rebellion">{{StoryCite|story=The Trial of Dagobah|book=Age of Rebellion Special 1}}</ref>
