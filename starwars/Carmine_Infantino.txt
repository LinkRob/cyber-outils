'''Carmine Infantino''' was a comic-book artist and editor who illustrated a number of issues in [[Star Wars (1977)|Marvel's ''Star Wars'']] comic series.

In the late 1970s, Infantino was the regular penciller for [[Marvel Comics|Marvel's]] ''Star Wars'' title, doing the art duties on nearly every issue until the adaptation of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. He and writer [[Archie Goodwin]] were responsible for such creations as [[House of Tagge/Legends|House of Tagge]] and the [[The Wheel/Legends|The Wheel]].
