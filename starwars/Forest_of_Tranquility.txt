The '''Forest of Tranquility''' was a [[forest]] on the remote [[moon]] of [[Treen]]. The [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Vosa (Padawan)|Vosa]] visited the forest to [[Meditation|meditate]], but mistakenly sat on the nest of a [[spikeworm]]. An [[alien]] [[Unidentified four-armed ape|ape]] warned Vosa of the danger, saving him from being attacked by the [[worm]].<ref name="JTC-E6" />
