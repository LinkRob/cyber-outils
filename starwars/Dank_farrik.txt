'''Dank farrik''',<ref name="Bounty Hunters 24" /> or just '''dank''',<ref name="The High Republic 8">[[The High Republic (2022) 8|''The High Republic'' (2022) 8]]</ref> was an expression and possibly an [[List of phrases and slang|expletive]] used throughout [[the galaxy]]<ref name="Mandalorian Season 2 Junior Novel">[[The Mandalorian Season 2 Junior Novel|''The Mandalorian Season 2'' Junior Novel]]</ref> that could be used to express frustration.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 22">[[Bounty Hunters 22|''Bounty Hunters'' 22]]</ref> Though [[Kay Vess]] sometimes used it  to express wonder instead, for example, when finding rare or unique treasures.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
