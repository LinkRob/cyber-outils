"'''A Warlord's Fury'''" was a ''[[Star Wars Miniatures]]'' scenario that was written by [[Sterling Hershey]] and published on [[Wizards.com]] on [[May 25]], [[2006]].

==Plot summary==
During the time of the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]], after the end of [[Operation Shadow Hand]], the galaxy is still in chaos. An [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Warlord]] engages a crime syndicate which controlled an [[X-1 Viper]] war droid. His forces launch an assault against the syndicate's headquarters to eliminate the threat.
