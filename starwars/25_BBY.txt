The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''25 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3252 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7952''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and his [[advisor]], [[Yupe Tashu]], travel to [[Jakku]]. Palpatine meets a boy named [[Gallius Rax|Galli]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref><ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
*[[Lyra Erso|Lyra]] and [[Galen Walton Erso]] are [[Wedding of Lyra and Galen Erso|married]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Timelines" />

==Births==
*[[Sarco Plank]]<ref name="Sarco Plank">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]]. {{GEAttr|Star Wars: Datapad}} states that Sarco Plank was 59 years during the events of ''Star Wars:'' Galaxy's Edge, meaning he was born in around 25 BBY.</ref> on [[Li-Toran]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="Datapad">{{GEAttr|Star Wars: Datapad}}</ref>
*[[Bodhi Rook]] on [[Jedha]].<ref name="Rebel Dossier" />
*[[Yendor Brethen|Yendor]] on [[Ryloth]].<ref name="Bloodline" />
*[[Jek Lawquane]] on [[Saleucami]]<ref name="Jek">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode210.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: The Deserter}} states that [[Jek Lawquane]] was four [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of "[[The Deserter]]." ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Landing at Point Rain]]" to [[21 BBY]], and {{HelmetCollectionCite|76|Highlights of the Saga}} dates the episode "[[Lethal Trackdown]]," to the same year. Since {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}} places "The Deserter" between "Landing at Point Rain" and "Lethal Trackdown," its events must take place in the same year. Because Jek was four years old in 21 BBY, he must have been born in 25 BBY.</ref>
