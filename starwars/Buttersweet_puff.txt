'''Buttersweet puffs''' were a type of [[Foodstuff|pastry]] that [[human]]s could [[Diet|consume]]. In [[232 BBY]], the [[Byne Guild]]'s owner&mdash;[[Scover Byne]]&mdash;offered to acquire some for her adoptive [[Gender|daughter]], the [[pilot]] [[Affie Hollow]], while they visited the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. Hollow accepted, as the pastries were one of her favorites. In [[28 ABY]], the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Senate|Senator]] [[Ransolm Casterfo]] brought fellow [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] a box containing a dozen buttersweet puffs, for which she thanked him.

==Description==
Buttersweet puffs were a type of small,<ref name="Bloodline" /> powdered<ref name="ITL" /> [[Foodstuff|pastry]] that was [[Diet|consumable]] by [[human]]s.<ref name="Bloodline" />

==History==
{{Quote|What's that?''"<br />''"A dozen buttersweet puffs. To heal what ails you.|Leia Organa and Ransolm Casterfo|<ref name="Bloodline" />}}
[[File:AffieHollow.jpg|thumb|right|240px|Buttersweet puffs were one of Affie Hollow's favorite treats.]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], the puffs were a favorite of the [[Byne Guild]] [[pilot]] [[Affie Hollow]].<ref name="ITD" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="ITD Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> her adoptive [[Parent|mother]]&mdash;the Byne Guild's owner, [[Scover Byne]]&mdash;offered to get her some of the puffs while the pair were visiting the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. Byne hoped to cheer her [[Gender|daughter]] up after her traumatic visit to the [[Amaxine Space Station]] while also lying to the latter that the [[space station]] was only used as a mere way station for the Byne Guild. Although the pilot agreed to acquire some of the treats, Hollow felt discomforted by Byne's words&mdash;which she knew to be incorrect&mdash;and forced a smile.<ref name="ITD" /> While assisting with an [[Evacuation of Inad Komesh|evacuation]] on the industrialized [[moon]] [[Inad Komesh]]<ref name="ITL" /> in [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{IntotheLightDate}}</ref> the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Burryaga Agaburry]]'s [[Hair|fur]] became covered with dust. His colleague, [[Jedi]] Knight [[Reath Silas]], felt that the [[Wookiee]] looked like he had been powdered like a buttersweet puff.<ref name="ITL" />

While on<ref name="Bloodline" /> the Core Worlds planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Hosnian Prime]]<ref name="Bloodline" /> in [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Bloodline Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']] to [[28 ABY]].</ref> the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Senate|Senator]] [[Ransolm Casterfo]] acquired a box of a dozen buttersweet puffs for one of his colleagues, [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who was recovering from her injuries received during the [[Napkin Bombing]] attack on a [[New Republic Senate conference building|Senate conference building]]. He brought the puffs to her [[Leia Organa's home|home]], and she thanked him for the gift, commenting that the treats were a nicer present than receiving [[soup]]. Organa enjoyed one of the puffs as the pair discussed the recent incident.<ref name="Bloodline" />
