'''''Ewoks Coloring Book''''' was a coloring book published by [[Kenner Products|Kenner]] in [[1985]].<ref name="SWCA" /> It was based on the [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'']] television series, partially adapting the episode "[[The Haunted Village]]."<ref name="THV">{{Ewoks|The Haunted Village}}</ref>
