'''Bruzo''' was a [[Pantoran]] [[Gender|male]] who lived at the [[Barakat Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]].<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> he challenged [[Princess]] [[Xiri A'lbaran]] with an archaic [[E'roni]] rite, believing that she was not worthy of E'ronoh's [[Monarchy|throne]]. A'lbaran won the challenge, however, when she wasn't looking Bruzo tried to charge at her again, but he was stopped by her [[Marriage|fiancée]], Prince [[Phan-tu Zenn]] of [[Eiram]], who punched Bruzo in the face before he could reach her.<ref name="Convergence" />
