'''Dalna''' was a small [[planet]] located in the [[Dalnan sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Known for its agriculture, Dalna was inhabited by a settler population known as the [[Dalnan]]s, who distrusted outsiders and rarely traveled offworld. During the [[High Republic Era]], it was home to the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[religion|cult]], who believed that using [[the Force]] would cause [[death]] and destruction and therefore hated the [[Jedi Order]]. This eventually boiled over into a devastating large scale conflict with the Jedi on Dalna known as the [[Night of Sorrow]], in which the Path, lead by [[Marda Ro]] of the [[Ro family]], brutally attacked the Jedi at their [[Path compound|compound]] on the planet. Many Dalnans joined the Jedi in the battle, resulting in many Dalnan deaths. After this conflict, the Dalnans adopted a culture focused on training for battle and the Jedi Order developed a small presence there in the form of a [[Dalna temple|temple]]. However, Dalna was not a part of the [[Galactic Republic]], and many Dalnans blamed the Jedi for the Night of Sorrow.

Around a [[Standard century|century]] after the war, following the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], Dalna considered entering into the Republic, with a Dalnan delegation sent aboard the [[cruiser]] ''[[Steady Wing]]'' to attend the [[Dedication of the Starlight Beacon|dedication]] of the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]]. The ''Steady Wing'' was [[Attack on the Steady Wing|sabotaged]] by members of the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] organization who wished to prevent Dalna from joining the Republic, resulting in the [[death]]s of most of the cruiser's passengers and the entire Dalnan delegation aside from [[Honesty Weft]], son of [[Ambassador]] [[Weft]]. A sect of the Nihil led by [[Kara Xoo]] later established a presence on the planet, using it to kidnap [[Youngling|children]] and raise them into the marauder's ideology. Eventually, the planet was completely [[Destruction of Dalna|devastated]] due to a [[Groundquake|seismic]] and [[Volcano|volcanic]] event caused by experimentation done by the Nihil and one of their scientists, [[Zadina Mkampa]]. However, it eventually recovered and its waterfalls were visited by Jedi [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his master [[Dooku]].

==Description==
{{Quote|The entirely of the planet is very unsettled. Dalna is a relatively young planet and up until a few centuries ago was still plagued by frequent groundquakes and volcanic eruptions. The Bensha Fault is the main space between the tectonic plates. It shifts regularly and runs through the Maawat Mountains.''"<br />"''It's why we've discouraged any settlement in that region. The quakes frequently level any dwelling, and there are numerous hot springs that vent volcanic gases. A series of explosions there might set off any number of natural disasters, and that would be terrible.|[[Lyssa Votz]] and [[Vice President]] of Dalna, [[Hackrack Bep]]|<ref name="Mission to Disaster" />}}
Located in the [[Dalnan sector]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> Dalna was a small terrestrial [[planet]] on the very edge of the [[Galactic Frontier]].<ref name="Path of Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref> It [[orbit]]ed [[Binary star system|two]] [[Star|suns]] and possessed temperate zones.<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> The two suns generated a feeling of [[sun sickness]] in those not used to living in such light.<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> The planet also featured [[water]]falls.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> From [[Realspace|space]], Dalna appeared [[Color|blue]] and white.<ref name="THR 12">[[The High Republic (2021) 12|''The High Republic'' (2021) 12]]</ref> It was a young planet that was, especially in the [[Bensha Fault]] region, very unstable.<ref name="Mission to Disaster" />

The rainy season brought great rains and, at times, floods to Dalna. Once every couple of [[Standard month|months]], Dalna faced three full days of massive rainfalls, which set into motion a blooming season that farmers used to harvest crops.<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> A small world that depended on its harvest for its overall economy,<ref name="Mission to Disaster" /> Dalna was known for its agriculture, with crops that included the [[summer melon]]. The [[gnostra bush]] that grew on Dalna was a natural resource that could be turned into [[gnostra fiber]], as well as [[gnostra berry wine]] and [[gnostra berry juice]]. The planet's fauna included the [[flutterbug]] and the [[wisp spider]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> The waterfalls of Dalna were considered magnificent.<ref name="Path of Vengeance" />

==History==
===Consequences of war===
{{Quote|We have had battles on our planet in the last century. The peace of the Republic does not always extend to her planets.|Honesty Weft|<ref name="A Test of Courage" />}}
[[File:Path-of-Deceit-textless.png|thumb|left|250px|The peace of Dalna was disturbed when "the Mother" began moving the Path of the Open hand to steal artifacts, catching the attention of the Jedi Order.]]
Dalna was repeatedly and haphazardly settled over its history, which mostly likely was because of its volcanic activity. The [[Path of the Open Hand]] cult called Dalna home for some time. Amongst its members were [[Marda Ro]] and [[Yana Ro]]. After the [[Children of the Open Hand|Children]] of the Cult [[Theft of the Rod of Seasons|stole]] the [[Rod of Seasons]], the [[Jedi Order]] [[Mission to Dalna (Macri and Zink)|arrived]] on Dalna to investigate the Path. Both [[Jedi]], [[Zallah Macri]] and [[Kevmo Zink]], were [[Death|killed]] when the Cult leader, "[[Elecia Zeveron|the Mother]]," unleashed a [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] known as the [[Great Leveler|Leveler]]. Having fallen in love with Zink despite the Path's different views on [[the Force]], Marda swore to destroy the Jedi, believing that the Order had brainwashed Zink into an ideology that led to his demise. The Path quickly abandoned Dalna aboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' [[starship]]. Afterward, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Azlin Rell]] and his [[droid]], [[C-9]], arrived in the abandoned [[Path compound]] to look for the missing Jedi, only to find their remains.<ref name="Path of Deceit" />

After the Path's operations on Jedha were destroyed with the [[Battle of Jedha]], secretly orchestrated by the Mother herself, the Path returned to Dalna,<ref name="Battle of Jedha">''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]''</ref> where they prepared for a stand against the Jedi that erupted as the [[Night of Sorrow|Battle of Dalna]]. Remembered as the Night of Sorrow,<ref name="Cataclysm">''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]''</ref> the incident ended up a massacre<ref name="Out of the Shadows">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref> as Jedi, Path members, and anti-Path [[Dalnan]]s fell in battle.<ref name="Cataclysm" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Cohmac Vitus]] would later blame the deaths on the Jedi fighting battles for the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Out of the Shadows" /> At the close of the battle,<ref name="Cataclysm" /> Marda, who had come to see the Nameless as monsters, activated a collection of [[thermal detonator]]s to flood the cavern she stood within and wash away the Nameless, which all, with the exception of the Leveler, drowned in the sudden waves or were smashed against the rocks. However, Marda's explosion had the unintended effect<ref name="Path of Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref> of bringing down the entire underground network, collapsing the Path caverns.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

While she had come to see the Nameless as monsters, Marda retained control over the Leveler and used it to strike down the Mother, whom she had learned was a Force-user herself, and escaped with it aboard the ''Gaze Electric'', from which she began a new plan to "free" the Force.<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> Much like the incident on Jedha, "the faithful" amongst the subsequent Ro family generations came to remember the events on Dalna as proof that the Jedi were not leading the galaxy towards balance, but instead to destruction.<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> Path little<ref name="Battle of Jedha" /> [[Tromak]], who later became a leader of the [[Elders of the Path]], crossed paths with the [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] on Dalna.<ref name="THRA 5">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 5]]</ref> The fighting on Dalna caused the resident [[Dalnan]] population to respond by becoming a culture that trained constantly for battle. Republic [[envoy]]s to Dalna were often intimidated by the formidable show of strength that the Dalnans expressed, and the planet remained outside Republic control. Though the following [[Standard century|century]] did not see any further war, battles did occur on the planet.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

===Diplomacy and disaster===
{{Quote|Why didn't you want Dalna to join the Republic?''"<br />"''Because this is ''our'' sector. If we have Republic cruisers patrolling this part of space it will ruin everything.|Honesty Weft and Klinith Da|<ref name="A Test of Courage" />}}
During the [[High Republic Era]], the Jedi Order had a small presence on Dalna,<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> operating a [[Dalna temple|Jedi temple]] on the planet.<ref name="MTD Reveal">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-high-republic-mission-to-disaster-new-jedi|text=Meet 3 New Jedi from ''Star Wars: The High Republic: Mission to Disaster'' – Exclusive Reveal}}</ref> Whilst most [[Dalnan]]s were isolationist, distrustful of outsiders, and not fond of space travel,<ref name="Mission to Disaster" /> Dalna considered the possibility of joining the Republic<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> in [[232 BBY]]<ref name="ATOC Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'' to [[232 BBY]]. </ref> for the economic<ref name="Mission to Disaster" /> and security benefits joining the union would entail. In the [[time]] following the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] and amid the subsequent [[Emergences]], a delegation from the [[Dalnan ambassadorial corps]] intended to travel to the [[Starlight Beacon]], a [[space station]] of both the Republic and the Jedi, for its [[Dedication of the Starlight Beacon|dedication]], aboard the [[cruiser]] ''[[Steady Wing]]''. The delegation, which included [[Ambassador]]s [[Weft]], [[Janex]], and [[Starstriker]], as well as Weft's son, [[Honesty Weft|Honesty]], boarded the ''Steady Wing'' on the nearby planet [[Haileap]] in order to travel to Starlight and then to [[Coruscant]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

[[File:Escaping_the_Steady_Wing.png|thumb|right|300px|The destruction of the ''Steady Wing'' saw the deaths of most of a Dalnan delegation to the Republic.]]
However, the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization, whose area of operations included the Dalnan [[sector]], wished to prevent Dalna's entry into the Republic and the subsequent Republic presence in the sector. As the ''Steady Wing'' departed Haileap carrying the delegation, it was [[Attack on the Steady Wing|sabotaged]] by the Nihil pirates [[Klinith Da]] and [[Gwishi]]. The cruiser was destroyed, and a majority of those aboard [[Death|killed]]. Honesty Weft was the only member of the Dalnan delegation to survive, escaping alongside [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]], [[Padawan]] [[Imri Cantaros]], young inventor [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]], and the droid [[J-6]], to the nearby [[moon]] [[Wevo]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

After the group captured the pirates who had been responsible on Wevo Starros sent several [[distress signal]]s, including one to the [[Dalna temple|temple on Dalna]], while Weft sent a message to the [[Saludad|Dalnan capital]] due to the current [[Unidentified Dalnan governor|governor's]] experience with the Nihil. The group was eventually rescued from Wevo, and Weft intended to address the [[Galactic Senate]] as part of the Republic and Dalna's response to the attack. The Nihil [[Storm (rank)|Storm]] [[Kara Xoo]], in response to the capture of the two [[saboteur]]s, Xoo planned to target schools on Dalna for recruiting.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

===Later history===
{{Dialogue|Ro|Why am I hearing reports of a Nihil loss at Dalna?|Xoo|It's just another ploy by the Republic to undermine our victories.|Dee|If that's the case, why is your fleet down twenty ships?|attr=Marchion Ro and the Tempest Runners discuss the loss at Dalna|src=<ref name="Out of the Shadows" />}}
A group of Nihil traveling with [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] aboard his [[flagship]], the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', intended to visit Dalna in the time after the Great Disaster, along with Jedha and the [[Trymant system]]. However, after learning of a [[Trymant IV disaster|new Emergence]] about to occur in the Trymant [[Star system|system]], Ro had his forces change course to reach Trymant before the disaster struck.<ref name="Adventures 6">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=6|story=Tales of Villainy: The Gaze Electric|stext=Tales of Villainy: The ''Gaze Electric''}}</ref> Around a year after the Great Disaster, Nihil in the [[Kara Xoo's Tempest|Tempest]] of Xoo, a recently promoted [[Tempest Runner]], carried out a run at Dalna. The marauder forces, led by an [[Unidentified Ithorian Nihil|Ithorian Nihil]], found the Republic waiting for them, suffering a loss of twenty [[starship]]s. Ro questioned reports of the events at Dalna while meeting with the Tempest Runners, and while Xoo claimed it was just a Republic ploy to undermine Nihil victories, fellow Tempest Runner [[Lourna Dee]] noted the loss that Xoo's [[fleet]] had sustained. After learning who had been in charge of the run, Ro had the [[Ithorian]] killed.<ref name="Out of the Shadows" />

While investigating the [[Great Leveler|cause]] of the death of [[Jedi Master]] [[Loden Greatstorm]] at the [[Battle of Grizal]], Master [[Stellan Gios]] looked into [[Ruusstha Vidyarvrikt]], a late Jedi nursemaid from Dalna who used to sing a [[Shrii ka rai ka rai|lullaby]] that had been found to be connected to Greatstorm's death. Gios found that some areas of the [[Jedi Archives]] regarding Dalna had been deleted, and his colleague, the [[Jedi investigator]] [[Emerick Caphtor]], contemplated heading to Dalna to investigate further.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 3" />

[[File:TowingTheBeacon-2021THR12.jpg|thumb|left|260px|The Starlight Beacon was towed to Dalna by the ''Halcyon'' and numerous other ships.]]
Rwoh and Cantaros later found themselves on Dalna, where they worked with the local Jedi there, including Jedi Master [[Nyla Quinn]], Jedi Knight and [[Jedi archivist|archivist]] [[Lyssa Votz]], and Jedi Knight [[Yacek Sparkburn]].<ref name="MTD Reveal" /> Following a disaster on Dalna, the Republic and Jedi undertook a [[Mission to Dalna|lifesaving mission]] that involved the Starlight Beacon being towed to the planet by the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' and a number of other vessels, including [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruisers]]. The success at Dalna pushed the Republic, as well as the [[Jedi High Council]], to replicate the response to further disasters; after a [[Eiram cyclone disaster|cyclone]] struck the planet [[Eiram]], the Beacon was later towed back to the [[Eiram system]]. At some point prior to the station's move to Eiram, the Jedi acquired intelligence on Dalna that suggested that the Nihil had recently fortified their base, the [[Great Hall of the Nihil|Great Hall]] within [[No-Space]], against attack.<ref name="THR 12" />

Whilst some had feared the planet would be permanently lost after the disaster, with [[Tracer Lore]] claiming the entire planet would be covered in [[Magma|lava]] in as little as two [[Standard week|weeks]], Honesty Weft believed that scientists would find a way to stabilize the planet so that, after a few years, the Dalnans could return home. Having decided the planet did not feel like home after he lost his father, Weft did not intend to return with them.<ref name="Mission to Disaster" /> At some point after becoming the Padawan of the Jedi Master [[Dooku]], [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] visited the waterfalls of Dalna with his master. Dooku himself recorded the events in his [[Dooku's journal|journal]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|Our Metamorphosis does test the strengths of our children, but only to prepare them for the harsh realities of life on our planet.|Ambassador Janex|<ref name="A Test of Courage" />}}
[[File:Honesty-ATOC.png|thumb|right|190px|Honesty Weft, a young Dalnan]]
No sentient species had evolved on Dalna by the time of the High Republic Era. As such,<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> the inhabitants of Dalna&mdash;known as Dalnans&mdash;were settlers, with resident [[species]] that included [[Aqualish]], [[human]]s, [[Pantoran]]s, [[Trandoshan]]s, and [[Weequay]]s.<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> The planet was sparsely populated, however.<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> The [[Ongree]] Ruusstha Vidyarvrikt also hailed from Dalna.<ref name="Trail of Shadows 3" /> The Dalnans rarely left the temperate zones of their planet and left Dalna entirely even less often, possessing a distrust of outsiders and a dislike for space travel, the latter of which was partially due to the presence of the Nihil pirates. There were not many [[droid]]s on Dalna, with the settlers tending to rely on their own devices.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

Happy there would be few distractions from their faith, the Path of the Open Hand made Dalna their home because of the world's peaceful status and small population. However, the Path would abandon the world in the wake of the Jedi Order's mission to investigate them on world.<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> As a result of the war on Dalna a century before the Great Disaster, the culture of the Dalnans became oriented around training for conflict. When battle did occur, the Dalnans would keep their fights planetside rather than in space.<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> The people of Dalna were suspicious of Jedi despite the fact that several were based on the planet.<ref name="MTD Reveal" /> The small number of Jedi who resided on Dalna often kept to themselves, only stepping in when they were asked to.<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> Dalnans maintained a mostly isolationist approach to the wider galaxy, even continuing to distrust outsiders and space-travel after they joined the Republic.<ref name="Mission to Disaster" />

Young Dalnans would undergo [[Metamorphosis Trials]] to test the strengths that would be required in their pursued field later in life; for example, those joining the [[Dalnan military|military]] would have a Metamorphosis focused on [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand combat]] as well as defending oneself and surviving harsh conditions. Those who had not yet completed their Metamorphosis would not be seen as adults until they did.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

==Locations==
{{Quote|I also sent a message to the Dalnan capital. I figured maybe they could help with the Nihil, since the governor has experience with them.|Honesty Weft|<ref name="A Test of Courage" />}}
Dalna possessed a [[capital]] [[city]], which was overseen by a [[governor]]. A [[military compound]] also existed on the planet, with infantry training in and around the facility. Approximately one hundred schools were present on Dalna.<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> The Jedi also operated a [[Jedi temple]] on Dalna during the High Republic Era.<ref name="MTD Reveal" />
