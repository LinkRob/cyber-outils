'''Donith Garbis''' was a dangerous [[crime lord]] and the patriarch of the [[Garbis family]]. In the few [[Standard year|years]] that led to the [[Battle of Yavin]], he and his family operated on the [[planet]] of [[Thune]].<ref name="Cargo" />

== History ==
In [[3 BBY]], Donith led the Garbis crime family from their [[Garbis family mansion|mansion]] in [[Thune City]] on the planet [[Thune]]. The Garbis family had a rule on the planet that all unwatched cargo supplies immediately became their property. Under that rule, henchmen of the Garbis family took some supplies belonging to a [[rebel cell]], the [[Spectres]]. The Spectres wanted it back and intercepted the shipment as it was being delivered to Garbis hands. Donith's son, [[Tomith Garbis]], was sent to take it back, but [[Mission to Thune City|failed]] and was chastised by his father, who declared a vendetta against the Spectres.<ref name="Cargo" />

== Description and personality ==
Donith was a male [[humanoid]] of an [[Tomith Garbis' species|unknown species]]. He had wrinkly, white skin with black markings around his yellow eyes, chin, neck, and ears, with a flat nose. One of his eyes was missing and had scarring over its socket.<ref name="Cargo" /> He was not forgiving of his son's mistakes and took family losses personally.<ref name="Cargo" />

== Behind the scenes ==
