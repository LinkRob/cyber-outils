'''Clan Keldau''' was a [[Mandalorian clan]] whose heraldic device was the ''[[kyr'bes]]'', the stylized [[Bone|skull]] of a [[mythosaur]]. Though the clan eventually died out, its emblem endured and ultimately became a symbol embraced by all [[Mandalorian]]s, regardless of lineage.<ref name="Mandalorian VisualGuide">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref>
