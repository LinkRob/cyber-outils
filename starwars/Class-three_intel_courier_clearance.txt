'''Class-three intel/courier clearance''' was a special type of clearance given to certain [[officer]]s or [[Imperial cadet|cadets]] within the [[Galactic Empire]]. Due to the sensitive nature of material studied at the [[Arkanis Academy|officers' academy]] on [[Arkanis]], all [[cadet]]s attending it were given this type of clearance.<ref name="secret">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref> [[Zare Leonis]] discussed his obtaining of this clearance with [[Ezra Bridger]], noting that gaining it would give him greater access to information that could be used by the [[Spectres]].<ref name="vision" />
