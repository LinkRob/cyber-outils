The '''''Advent''''' was a cargo [[freighter]]. While carrying [[foodstuff]] to a [[Star system|system]] suffering from famine, it was protected by the [[Jedi]] [[Rael Averross]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Nim Pianna]] due to [[pirate]] activity in the [[sector]]. However, the crew [[Mutiny on the Advent|mutinied]], which led to the [[death]] of Nim Pianna.<ref name="MA" />
