The '''Emperor's Boosters''' was an organization of which [[Moff]] [[Tiaan Jerjerrod]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] was a member of. During a [[Bothan]] intelligence operation, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] was able to obtain a type of data card of Moff Jerjerrod, which demonstrated his [[Military rank|rank]], [[homeworld]], height, weight, age, and memberships. Along with the Emperor's Boosters, listed memberships included [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order|COMPNOR]], [[Keep the Core Secure]], and the [[Taung & Zhell Society]] from the [[University of Coruscant]].<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

The data card would eventually be incorporated into ''[[The Rebel Files]]'', a central data repository that contained physical documents and intelligence of the Alliance.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
