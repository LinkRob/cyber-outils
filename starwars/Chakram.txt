The '''chakram''' was a bladed [[melee weapon]] used by the [[Dressellian]] warriors. It was a flat, open-centered metal discus with a sharpened edge. The warriors in the employ of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] fought with one chakram in each hand.<ref name="FA" />
