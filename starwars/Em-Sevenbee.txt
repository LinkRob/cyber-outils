'''Em-Sevenbee''' was an [[R5 series astromech droid]] that served at a [[Gabredor III colony|colony]] of [[refugee]]s on the [[planet]] [[Gabredor III]]. After the [[Gabredor III colony's protocol droid|colony's protocol droid]] broke down and was not replaced by<ref name="Vader 23" /> [[Tauntaza]],<ref name="Vader 25">[[Darth Vader (2020) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 25]]</ref> the [[governor]] of the planet, the colony began to use Em-Sevenbee to keep track of things for them.<ref name="Vader 23" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Darth Vader (2020) 23|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 23]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] traveled to the colony after he agreed to help the former [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] [[Sabé]] save the colony from the governor, who had begun to use the services of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate to consolidate her power and had neglected the people of her planet. After they arrived at the colony, the [[forensics droid]] [[ZED-6-7]] was directed to Em-Sevenbee, and acquired data that showed that the governor had only delivered thirty-seven per cent of critical supplies that had been promised to the colony, as well as that the governor had forcibly conscripted thirty-three [[colonist]]s, with none ever returning. A short [[time]] later, Em-Sevenbee accompanied Vader and ZED-6-7 to a nearby [[forest]], and after ZED-6-7 plugged into EM-Sevenbee again and discovered that the area was suffering from a drought.<ref name="Vader 23" />
