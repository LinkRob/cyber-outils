'''Bora Vio'''<ref name="Bounty Lost" /> was a [[planet]]<ref name="Bounty Lost AD">{{TBB|Bounty Lost}} {{AD|Disney+}}.</ref> situated in the [[Lido system]]. The planet had a cloudy [[atmosphere]], which hosted a [[Kaminoan]] [[Bora Vio cloning facility|facility]] that was abandoned by the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The Kaminoans utilized the facility on Bora Vio as a rendezvous for the [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] to deliver the [[Cloning|clone]] [[Omega]] to [[Taun We]]. However, fellow [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] [[Fennec Shand]] was sent by the Kaminoan [[Nala Se]] to [[Rescue of Omega|deter]] Bane and prevent Omega from being handed over to Taun We. Omega was able to escape captivity, and as Shand fought Bane, she transmitted a signal from the facility and escaped on a [[Kaminoan flight pod|flight pod]]. The signal reached her squad, the [[clone commando]] unit known as the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]], and they soon rescued her from Bora Vio in their [[starship]], [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|''Marauder'']].<ref name="Bounty Lost" />

After freeing former Imperial [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]] from the [[Imperial labor camp]] on the [[planet]] [[Erebus]],<ref name="Juggs">{{TBB|Juggernaut (episode)}}</ref> the Bad Batch traveled to Bora Vio to rendezvous with [[Echo]], their former squad mate. Echo arrived in a stolen [[Rho-class transport shuttle|''Rho''-class transport shuttle]] that they used to travel to [[Imperial Station 003]] above [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Breach">{{TBB|Into the Breach}}</ref>
