The [[Amaxine warriors (New Republic Era)|Amaxine warriors]] had a '''camp''' on the [[planet]] [[Daxam IV]]<ref name="Bloodline" /> in [[28 ABY]].<ref name="Timeframe">{{Twitter|DelReyStarWars|status/652893780236988420|[[Del Rey]]|quote=So excited to have @claudiagray writing a #StarWars novel with us. SW: New Republic: Bloodline coming 2016. Set 6 years before TFA.|archivefile=File:New Republic Bloodline Twitter Post.jpg}} establishes that the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']] take place six [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|VII}}. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], so ''Bloodline'' must take place in [[28 ABY]].</ref>. The Amaxine warriors were a paramilitary faction that admired the [[Galactic Empire|Old Empire]] and were arming for a confrontation with the [[New Republic]]. The camp was home to at least a thousand Amaxine warriors and included facilities for [[starfighter]]s. At the center of the camp lay a clearing that was used by the warriors to practice with [[Force pike]]s. After visiting [[Arliz Hadrassian]]'s [[Hadrassian's compound|compound]] to collect his [[Emperor's Royal Guard]]'s helmet, [[Ransolm Casterfo]] engaged in a mock duel with an Amaxine warrior at the camp.<ref name="Bloodline" />
