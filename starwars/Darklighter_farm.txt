The '''Darklighter farm''',<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> also known as the '''Darklighter homestead''',<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> was a [[moisture farm]] owned by the [[Darklighter family|family]] of [[Biggs Darklighter]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> It fell into disrepair sometime after the [[Battle of Yavin]], to the point it was abandoned<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> with broken [[Moisture vaporator|vaporators]] and the interior was filled with wild vegetation.<ref name="Outlaws" />

In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]], the [[mercenary]] [[Hoss]] hid out at the farm after escaping from prison in [[Wayfar]]. After which the [[sheriff]] of Wayfar [[Quint Cresadde]] directed a [[bounty hunter]] that was looking for Hoss to the farm. Once there, Hoss killed the hunter in the kitchen of the farm and left the farm, leaving behind the body and a transponder. Later Cresadde also sent the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] to the farm who was looking to hire Hoss for a job. While there Vess was able to restore power to the farm and found the transponder and used it to track down Hoss.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Darklighter homestead was first mentioned in [[canon]] in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']].<ref name="SWCL" /> It was later first depicted in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' that was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
