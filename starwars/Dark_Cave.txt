The '''Dark Cave''' was located in the [[Great Chott salt flat]] region of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Merqaal]] [[Nix]] guided his companion [[Kay Vess]] through the cave to an overlook where he found the [[Horn/Legends|horns]] of an animal. Nix liked to chew on them and sharpen his claws on them.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The dark cave appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The location was one of the areas in the game where the player follows Nix through the environment to find a collectible; in this case, the "Horns" Nix treasure. This along with other Nix treasures found on Tatooine could be traded with [[Berek Keehro]] in [[Mirogana]] on [[Toshara]] to unlock the [[Color|red]] [[hat|headband]] cosmetic for Nix.<ref name="Outlaws" />
