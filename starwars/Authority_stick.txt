An '''authority stick''' was a short wooden staff used by the [[Ewok]] [[Teebo]] of [[Bright Tree Village]] on [[Endor]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> Teebo's authority stick had a stone club head attached to it, which he wielded as an [[axe]].
