A  '''Cholganna survivalist jacket''' was a type of highly resistant [[jacket]] manufactured<ref name="Outlaws" /> on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="Nexu GoC">{{GoC|Nexu}}</ref> [[Cholganna]] to protect its wearer from the world's dangerous creatures.<ref name="Outlaws" /> The [[Clothing|jacket]] was [[color|dark red]] with orange shoulders that featured a cross hatched pattern. It had a black vertical strap next to a silver colored circle with a semicircular symbol and a rectangle at the bottom on upper-right chest of the jacket. The back of the jacket featured a large orange [[Aurek]] symbol from the [[Aurebesh]] alphabet. The jacket included a pair of dark green fingerless [[Color|gloves]] and a tan undershirt with dark green cuffs.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Quarren]] [[merchant]] [[Shuma (merchant)|Shuma]] had at least one Cholganna survivalist jacket and a pair of [[Cholganna survivalist pants]] for sale as they traveled around the [[Mid Rim Territories]] and [[Outer Rim Territories]] in their [[Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] outlaw [[Kay Vess]] purchased a Cholganna survivalist jacket from Shuma for 2500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A Cholganna survivalist jacket appeared in ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', a [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. When equipped by the player, the jacket reduces damage taken from [[blaster]], projectile [[weapon]]s and explosions. If the player is wearing a full set of Cholganna survivalist gear&mdash;the jacket, the [[Cholganna survivalist belt|belt]], and the pants, the player will slowly heal fully depleted health segments over time.<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Iuliia Misiul]] created concept art of the Cholganna survivalist jacket and other outfits used by Vess.<ref name="Art Station">{{ArtStation|username=Iuliia Misiul|url=/artwork/4N9Ve4|name=[[Iuliia Misiul]]|text=Star Wars Outlaws &mdash; Kay's Gearsets}}</ref>
