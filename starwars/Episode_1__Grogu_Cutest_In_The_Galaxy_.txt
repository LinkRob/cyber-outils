==Plot summary==
On a [[planet]] orbited by two other astronomical objects, [[Din Djarin]] walks, with [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] following behind. Din turns back and tells Grogu, "No" twice. Grogu stops and stands behind a rock, confusing Din in the process. As they continue to walk, Grogu sees blue [[Butterfly|butterflies]] and stops to admire them. Walking past a boulder, Grogu is surprised by an aggressive [[tooka]] that leaps out from behind the rock. Grogu runs toward Din for safety, holding onto his leg.
