'''Daxan Beta''' was a [[planet]] situated near a main trade route used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] briefly studied it as a potential location for a new Rebel base after the [[Evacuation of Yavin 4|evacuation]] of the [[Great Temple]] on [[Yavin 4]], but quickly rejected it due to its proximity to the Imperial trade route.<ref name="PL1" />
