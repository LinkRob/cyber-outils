'''Boar-wolves''',<ref name="beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> also known as '''borras''',<ref name="Night of the Gorax">[[Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle 4|''Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle'' 4]]</ref> were [[Sentience|non-sentient]], bristle-backed creatures that lived on [[Endor]],<ref name="Aftermath" /> a [[forest]]ed [[moon]] that [[orbit]]ed the [[planet]] of the [[Endor (planet)|same name]].<ref name="LS">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> Boar-wolves had thick hair, humped backs, and and two tusks.<ref name="BMF 38" />

They were [[Death|killed]] for their meat by the [[Ewok]]s, a [[species]] of diminutive sentients native to Endor. Ewok [[hunting]] parties who managed to bring back boar-wolf meat were considered to have been lucky. Some Ewoks also crafted [[Knife|knives]] from boar-wolf [[Tooth|teeth]].<ref name="beware" /> Although boar-wolves could be dangerous, they were domesticated by the [[Sentience|semi-sentient]] [[Gorax]], who would care for the young after killing their mother.<ref name="BMF 38" />
