An '''electroripper staff''' was a type of energized [[staff]] weapon.

==Characteristics==
An electroripper staff was a partially collapsible staff weapon that was tipped with a high-voltage [[energy ribbon]] on one end, and a [[Kinetite generator|kinetite charge]] on the other.<ref name="Official Guide" /> When activated, the staff extended and its blade and kinetite charge energized; the energy ribbon was capable of shredding metal and incapacitating opponents, while the kinetite charge could create shockwaves of varying intensity.<ref name="Official Guide" /><ref name="Solo" />

A brutal [[melee weapon]], the leader of the [[Cloud-Riders]], [[Enfys Nest]], used an electroripper staff as her signature weapon.<ref name="Official Guide" /><ref name="Solo" />
