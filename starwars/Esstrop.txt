'''Esstrop''' was a [[Kessurian]] member of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] who ventured out into the [[Galactic Frontier]] to spread their religion's teachings. As a result, they were not present on the Path's [[Homeworld|home]] [[planet]], [[Dalna]], when a [[Night of Sorrow|battle]] in [[382 BBY]] culminated in the cult's end. At some point between the event and around [[381 BBY]], Esstrop and their partner [[Kortanio]] were among the few Path members tracked down by the [[Evereni]] [[pirate]] leader and former Path [[Guide of the Closed Fist|member]] [[Marda Ro]] and chose to join her [[Nihil|group]]. Upon joining, Ro introduced them to a [[Shrikarai|beast]] called the [[Great Leveler]] in her [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Gaze Electric]]''.

Esstrop served Ro as a raider, working with Kortanio and occasionally also alongside the [[human]] [[Milan Stor]] and the [[Twi'lek]] [[Fori Nagor]]. At some point no earlier than [[379 BBY]], all four raiders attacked a [[refugee]] camp and kidnapped the refugees; however, Ro ordered Nagor to set the refugees free when presented with them. Later, Esstrop and Kortanio escorted the Evereni [[Faers|siblings]] [[Vika Faer|Vika]] and [[Velya Faer]] into the ''Gaze Electric''{{'s}} [[throne hall]] to [[Marda Ro's hunt|meet]] Ro. At some point, Ro mentioned the Kessurian in a message she recorded for her [[Ro family|descendants]], which her [[Gender|great-great-grandson]] [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to by [[252 BBY]].

==Biography==
===Path of the Open Hand and joining Marda Ro===
{{Quote|I have collected some scattered members of the Path of the Open Hand, those who never knew the Closed Fist.|Marda Ro, in her journal|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
[[File:NightofSorrow.png|thumb|left|300px|Esstrop was unaware that the Path of the Open Hand was dissolved due to being off-world during the Night of Sorrow (pictured).]]
Esstrop was a [[Kessurian]] who became a member of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]]<ref name="Closed Fist" /> at some point no later than [[382 BBY]].<ref name="PathFall">''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'' establishes that the [[Path of the Open Hand]] dissolved in the aftermath of the [[Night of Sorrow]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]]. Therefore, Esstrop must have become a member of the Path at some point no later than 382 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> During their [[time]] in the Path, they ventured out into the [[Galactic Frontier]] in an [[Esstrop and Kortanio's exploratory shuttle|exploratory shuttle]] to spread its teachings accompanied by their partner [[Kortanio]]. Because of this, they were not present when the Path faced its violent end<ref name="Closed Fist" /> during the [[Night of Sorrow]] on its [[Homeworld|home]] [[planet]], [[Dalna]],<ref name="THRCE">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> in 382 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].<ref name="PathFall" /> Unaware that the Path was gone, Esstrop continued with Path activity on the Frontier.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

At some point between the Path's dissolution and around [[381 BBY]],<ref name="RecruitDate">"[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" establishes that [[Marda Ro]] visited [[Dantooine]] around a [[Standard year|year]] after taking possession of the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', an event that ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]]. Therefore, Ro's trip to Dantooine must have occurred around [[381 BBY]]. Esstrop was recruited by [[Marda Ro]] into her [[Nihil|group]] of [[pirate]]s prior to her visit to Dantooine. Therefore, Ro must have recruited them no later than around 381 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]</ref> Esstrop was one of the few scattered Path members tracked down by the [[Evereni]] [[Marda Ro]]&mdash;a former [[Guide of the Closed Fist|member]] of the Path of the Open Hand. Ro told them a lie about the Path that positioned<ref name="Closed Fist" /> her<ref name="Temptation">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> at the center of their faith and desires and offered them to either join her group of [[pirate]]s<ref name="Closed Fist" />&mdash;who would eventually become the [[Nihil]]<ref name="GrattonInterview">{{YouTube|video=llnvoEG0CjE|text=Interview with Tessa Gratton: "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" (TALES OF LIGHT & LIFE)|channel=@FriendsOfTheForce|name=[[Friends of the Force]]|date=2023-10-06}}</ref>&mdash;or cease any Path-related activities. Esstrop and Kortanio were among the few that chose to join Ro.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

After Esstrop joined Ro's band, she took them aboard her [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', where she introduced them to the [[Great Leveler]]&mdash;a [[Shrikarai|monster]] that [[Diet|fed]] on the [[Living Force]]. She explained that the only objective was to survive and take care of the Leveler so that one [[Standard day|day]] it could strike back against [[Jedi Order|abusers]] of [[the Force]]. She also added that the group had no shared religion or a story to tell [[the galaxy]] anymore.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

===Service to Ro and legacy===
{{Quote|Rarely, but when the opportunity arises, all four raiders take two or even all three of the small ships to go on a quick mission of destruction and pillaging. I have encouraged them, told them to take what they like: that is our new way.|Marda Ro, in her journal|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
[[File:MardaRo-PathOfDeceit.png|thumb|right|180px|Esstrop served Marda Ro (pictured).]]
Esstrop and Kortanio quickly turned over their faith to Ro and became her best raiders, taking their [[shuttle]] to find things to [[Thief|steal]] and happily present to Ro back on the ''Gaze Electric''. Esstrop, their partner, and Ro's two other raiders&mdash;the [[human]] [[Milan Stor]] and the [[Twi'lek]] [[Fori Nagor]]&mdash;occasionally went on bigger missions together.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

At some point no earlier than [[379 BBY]],<ref name="RaidDate">The segment of "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" depicting a [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi's]] [[death]] onboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' takes place around [[379 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:ClosedFistJediDeathDate|here]]. The raid in which the [[Unidentified green-skinned man|green-skinned man]] and other [[refugee]]s were captured by [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Pirate|raiders]] occurs after the [[Jedi]]'s death; therefore, it must have taken place no earlier than 379 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the four conducted an attack on a [[refugee]] camp and took the refugees themselves as they had no valuable possessions. Once they returned to the ''Gaze'', Nagor took the refugees to the ship's [[throne hall]] and presented them to Ro. Enraged at what they had done, Ro commanded Nagor to set the refugees free, with the exception of a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Unidentified green-skinned man|green-skinned man]] who Ro led to the Great Leveler.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

Later, Esstrop and Kortanio escorted the Evereni [[Faers|siblings]] [[Vika Faer|Vika]] and [[Velya Faer]] to the ''Gaze''{{'s}} [[throne]] hall after they accepted an invitation from Ro to [[Marda Ro's hunt|meet]] with her. At some point, Ro recorded a [[Marda Ro's journal|message]] for her [[Ro family|descendants]] where she talked about Esstrop and stressed the importance of passing on their name.<ref name="Closed Fist" /> By [[252 BBY]],<ref name="ShallaDate">"The Lie" chapter in [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]], which includes [[Shalla Ro]]'s [[death]], features [[Asgar Ro]]'s ascension to [[Eye of the Nihil]], an event dated to [[252 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'' establishes she introduced [[Marchion Ro]] to [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]], he must have listened to it by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> during his youth, Ro's [[Gender|great-great-grandson]] [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to her memoirs, including the entries where Esstrop was mentioned.<ref name="Temptation" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Esstrop and Kortanio easily turned over their faith to me.|Marda Ro, in her journal|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
Esstrop was in a relationship with Kortanio, a fellow Kessurian. They could not bear to be fixed in place and became devoted to Ro quickly.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|They are my best raiders&mdash;always moving, always seeking, always taking what they can and bringing it back to the belly of the ''Gaze'', happy to display their prizes for me.|Marda Ro, in her journal|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
Ro regarded Esstrop as a great seeker and as one of her best raiders.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|We have three small ships that live in the hold of the ''Gaze'' now. ''[&hellip;]'' One ''[is]'' a retrofitted exploratory shuttle Esstrop and Kortanio had been using to spread the word of the Path.|Marda Ro, in her journal|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
Esstrop and Kortanio had an exploratory shuttle that they used both in service to the Path and Ro.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

==Behind the scenes==
