"'''Clones are famous all across the galaxy'''" is the sixteenth in a series of comics on the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] website. It is a direct follow up to "[[A clone by any other name]]." It was placed on the website [[August 21]], [[2009]].

==Plot summary==
"Clones are famous all across the galaxy"

The comic begins with the group of [[IG-86 sentinel droid/Legends|assassin droids]] that captured [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] being chased toward a [[Hoth/Legends|planet]] by the clones assigned to recover Artoo. The clones fire from their [[Nu-class attack shuttle/Legends|attack shuttle]] but they can't hit the [[Assassin droid/Legends|assassin droid's]] craft. Then the shuttle is hit and crashes into the planet. The group survives but their [[Shuttle/Legends|shuttle]] is lost. However, the team's [[Gunner/Legends|gunner]], Blasty, says he can repair their [[Clone Walker|walker]].

The clones are then shown traveling across the snow covered landscape with the remade [[Walker/Legends|walker]]. Eventually, [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]] [[1137]] notices that [[1139]] has gone missing. Also at that moment, the gunner spots a [[Sheathipede-class transport shuttle/Legends|Neimoidian shuttle]]. Inside the shuttle, [[Viceroy/Legends|Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray/Legends|Nute Gunray]] speaks to an assassin droid to confirm they have R2-D2. A [[Droid/Legends|droid]] tells the Viceroy that he had visitors and Gunray goes to the shuttle's door to see them. Once outside, Gunray is tackled by two of the clones. Just then, 1139 returns riding a [[Tauntaun/Legends|tauntaun]]. The clones board the shuttle and take off, leaving Gunray tied up with his droids. As the shuttle climbs above the planet, someone complains about a stink followed by 1139 getting yelled at for bringing the tauntaun.

"TO BE CONTINUED!"
