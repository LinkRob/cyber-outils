==Description==

The Akiva production plant, known also as the Myrra droid factory due to its location in the [[Catacombs of Akiva|Akivan catacombs]] under the [[city]] of [[Myrra]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> was a [[droid]] [[factory]] was established by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Trade Federation]] on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> The factory contained vast construction systems to build batches of [[B1-series battle droid]]s and construct the [[BX-series droid commando]] model. [[Technician]] [[Gedeek Obaz]] also believed the factory was able to produce the B1's [[OOM command battle droid]] variant when he believed he spotted one such droid in its ruins.<ref name="Outlaws" /> Upon construction, newly-produced droids were raised out of the facility via a telescoping platform, allowing the droids to be picked up by incoming [[starship]]s.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==History==
===Establishment===
After its establishment by the Trade Federation and Confederacy of Independent Systems,<ref name="Aftermath" /> the Akiva production plant constructed [[battle droid]]s for the [[Confederacy military]] during the [[Clone Wars]], a galaxy-wide war between the Confederacy and [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> It was also open for civilian droid purchases.<ref name="Aftermath" /> The CIS purchased droids from the factory<ref name="Outlaws" /> but was also directly involved in building droids within, even generating profits itself from the operation. According to the Akivan [[Norra Wexley]], the factory was established in the later years of the Clone Wars,<ref name="Aftermath" /> but the factory in fact had been active since 22 BBY at least.<ref name="Outlaws note">"[[Confederacy of Independent Systems|CIS]] Transmission 04" in ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' establishes that the Akiva production plant was in operation while the [[BX-series droid commando]] was still undergoing preliminary tests. As the BX was introduced to the frontlines by the events of "[[Rookies (episode)|Rookies]]," which is set in [[22 BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', the Akivan factory must have been in operation by 22 BBY.</ref> The factory was overseen by [[chief supervisor]] [[Poreni]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

===Production and destruction===
{{Quote|General Grievous. This is chief supervisor Poreni at the Akive production plant. I am happy to announce that the latest batch of B1 battle droids is completed ahead of time. As per agreement, we will deliver them to Hakara forthwith, where they shall be ready for deployment immediately.|Poreni|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
[[File:Grievouspoints-ShadowofMalevolence.png|thumb|left|300px|General Grievous himself (pictured) sourced battle droids from the Akiva production plant.]]
During the war, the Akiva production plant built units including the B1-series battle droid and the BX-series droid commando. [[General]] [[Grievous]] received B1 [[Batch 4652-A]] from the factory but was dissatisfied with their performance, with his complaints forwarded to the factory on his behalf by [[C-5DS]]. In response, he was awarded a twenty percent discount on future B1 orders and put in an advance order of twelve thousand BX commandos.<ref name="Outlaws" />

As it did with most droid factories in time, the Republic discovered the factory. After discovering the location, the Republic [[Battle of Akiva|attacked]] with [[Jedi]] and [[clone commando]]s. Nonetheless, the factory remained in operation in some form until the end of the war, at which point it was abandoned abruptly.<ref name="Outlaws" /> The factory was left a wrecked husk of its former self.<ref name="Aftermath" />

===After the war===
{{Quote|You've got to be kidding me."<br />"And this is supposed to be a secret base.|Kay Vess and Asara|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
[[File:Star-Wars-Outlaws-AkivaDroidFactory.png|thumb|250x250px|In 3 ABY, thief Kay Vess saw what was left of the abandoned Separatist factory.]]
After the war, resourceful [[scavenger]]s scoured the factory to build droids of their own.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> The factory was used as a base by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. The rebels at the factory encountered criminal [[Kay Vess]], the [[Rodian]] [[Gedeek Obaz]], and the [[BX-series droid commando|BX commando droid]] [[ND-5]] when they entered the factory to repair ND-5, which was malfunctioning. In exchange for aid to ND-5, Vess helped the rebels free their comrades from the clutches of the [[Hutt Clan]], who intended to sell them as prisoners to the [[Galactic Empire]]. When the Empire discovered the location of the rebel base, the factory was nearly destroyed by the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Revelator]]''. The ''Revelator'' was destroyed before it could destroy the base by an [[Battle of the Revelator|attack]] composed of the Rebel Alliance, [[Kay Vess' crew]], and a crime syndicate with which Vess had a good reputation. With the Empire's discovery of the base, the rebels abandoned the location, allowing [[Pirate|pirates]] to establish themselves there. Vess was hired by the [[Pyke Syndicate]] to recover valuable equipment left behind by the rebels.<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Norra Wexley's team]] later passed through the ruined factory during the [[rebellion on Akiva]]. Wexley recalled the factory as a site made during the late Clone Wars,<ref name="Aftermath" /> but it had actually been in operation since at least [[22 BBY]]<ref name="Outlaws note" /> before the Republic shut it down<ref name="Outlaws" /> late in the war.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Akiva production plant first appeared in the [[2015]] novel, [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] by [[Chuck Wendig]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> It was later shown as part of "The Legacy" main quest in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
