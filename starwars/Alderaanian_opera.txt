'''Alderaanian opera''' was a type of [[opera]] native to the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Queen's Hope">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref>

In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Queen's Hope]]'' is set ten [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Naboo]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[32 BBY]]. Therefore, ''Queen's Hope'' must be set in [[22 BBY]].</ref> the Alderaanian [[senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]] attended the Alderaanian opera. With him was his colleague [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] with two of her [[handmaiden]]s. During this time Amidala was disguised as a handmaiden and her handmaiden [[Sabé]] was disguised as Amidala. At this point Sabé had missed the last three seasons of Alderaanian opera and was excited to catch up.<ref name="Queen's Hope" />

Later at a soirée hosted by Organa one of the performers approached Sabé, again disguised as Amidala, and asked her what she thought of the most recent season interrupting her conversation with [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Queen's Hope" />
