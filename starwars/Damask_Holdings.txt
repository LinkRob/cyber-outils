'''Damask Holdings''' was a company that operated during the [[Republic Era]]. In [[65 BBY]], Damask Holdings and the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] people entered a joint venture to establish a [[Theed power generator|plasma refinery complex]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. After the [[Outer Rim Construction and Assembly]] company completed its construction, Naboo's [[Senator]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] hosted an opening ceremony for the complex at some point after his election to the [[Galactic Senate]] in [[52 BBY]]. Before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|start]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] in [[22 BBY]], Damask Holdings also established a [[Damask Holdings base|base]] on the [[Volcano|volcanic]] and [[mineral]]-rich [[Outer Rim Territories]] planet [[Mustafar]].

==Description==
Damask Holdings was a company that carried out [[plasma]] refining operations.<ref name="NoP" />

==History==
===A joint venture===
Damask Holdings operated during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="NoP" /> At some point, evidence of a vast plasma source below the cliff face of the world's [[capital]] [[city]], [[Theed]], was discovered. The [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] people of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naboo]], who had been sourcing their power primarily from a series of outlying [[Mining|mines]],<ref name="CL">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref> opted to enter a joint venture with Damask Holdings to expand plasma refining on the planet.<ref name="NoP" />

[[File:Theed_Generator_Complex.png|thumb|left|300px|Damask Holdings's plasma refinery complex]]
In [[65 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Damask Holdings&mdash;along with the company [[Outer Rim Construction and Assembly]]&mdash;helped initiate the construction of a [[Theed power generator|plasma refinery complex]] on the [[Cliffs of Theed]],<ref name="NoP" /> located strategically within the [[Theed Royal Palace]]'s [[Theed Hangar]]. As the company's complex featured highly efficient generators, which provided more power than the city could use, the facility also maintained the capacity to export plasma produced in excess. Following the refinery complex's opening ceremonies,<ref name="CL" /> which occurred after [[Senator]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] election to the [[Galactic Senate]] in [[52 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] specifies that [[Senator]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] oversaw the opening ceremonies of Damask Holdings' [[Theed power generator|plasma refinery complex]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates Palpatine's election to the [[Galactic Senate]] to [[52 BBY]], the opening ceremonies must have taken place at some point after his election that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the establishment enriched Naboo and became a popular tourist attraction. The ceremony was hosted by Palpatine, who was secretly a [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] with the title of [[Darth]] Sidious.<ref name="CL" />

===Continued operations===
[[File:Mustafar-TROSGG.png|thumb|right|250px|Damask Holdings opened a base on Mustafar (pictured) before the start of the Clone Wars.]]
Though the factory was a joint venture between Damask Holdings and the Naboo people,<ref name="NoP" /> its [[Standard day|day]]-to-day operations were managed by personnel from the [[Plasma Energy Engineering]] unit of the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]].<ref name="CL" /> However, the plasma refinery's continued operation posed a threat to the profits of another increasingly disgruntled company operating on the world: the [[Trade Federation]]. These tensions eventually led the Trade Federation&mdash;under Palpatine's heavy influence&mdash;to [[Invasion of Naboo|blockade]] Naboo in [[32 BBY]]. However, the [[blockade]] dissolved shortly afterward due to the [[Battle of Naboo|defeat]] of the company's [[Trade Federation Droid Army|forces]] at the [[Limb|hands]] of the combined efforts of the Naboo, the [[Gungan Grand Army]], and their [[Jedi Order|other allies]].<ref name="Timelines" />

Prior to the [[First Battle of Geonosis|start]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="BMF17" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Damask Holdings also established a [[Damask Holdings base|base]] on the [[Volcano|volcanic]] and [[mineral]]-rich [[Outer Rim Territories]] planet [[Mustafar]]. By around that time, they were one of several companies that wanted to exploit the world's mineral resources.<ref name="BMF17" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Damask Holdings was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 17|seventeenth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF17" /> which was released around [[April 29]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|17}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Damask Holdings/Legends|Damask Holdings]] originated in the [[2012]] novel [[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']], authored by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Darth Plagueis">[[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']]</ref>
