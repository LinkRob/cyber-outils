An '''energy crossbow''' was a type of [[crossbow]] that fired energy projectiles.<ref name="Infiltration">{{TBB|Infiltration (episode)}}</ref> Similar to an [[energy bow]], the energy crossbow fired similar bolts of energy but was more compact and could easily fold away for travel.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|omega-s-energy-crossbow|Omega's energy crossbow}}</ref> During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|306}}</ref> [[Echo]] gifted [[Omega]] a [[Omega's energy crossbow|modified energy crossbow]] that he had acquired<ref name="Infiltration" /> as a replacement for her [[Omega's Zygerrian energy bow|energy bow]].<ref name="Databank" />
