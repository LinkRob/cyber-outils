'''Bant Eerin''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Mon Calamari]] [[Jedi Master]] who served in the [[Jedi Order]] during the last [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. She was one of [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'s oldest friends at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. She was involved in a [[Rescue of Yoda|test]] for Kenobi and his [[Padawan]], [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/swca-2022-marvel-yoda-interview|text=SWCA 2022: Marvel's ''Star Wars: Yoda'' Miniseries Revealed}} places ''[[Star Wars: Yoda]]'' concurrent to [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> on [[Dagobah]], Jedi Master [[Yoda]] spoke to the [[Force spirit]] of the now-deceased Obi-Wan Kenobi. When Yoda stated that he needed herbs to make his soup, Kenobi recalled that Yoda used to make him and the other [[Padawan]]s pastries at the [[Jedi crèche|Jedi créche]], with Eerin aiding with the filling of the pastries.<ref name="Yoda">[[Yoda 10|''Yoda'' 10]]</ref>
