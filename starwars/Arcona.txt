'''Arcona''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] of [[desert]]-dwelling [[humanoid]]s that was [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Cona]] that possessed a triangular shaped head. Notable Arcona include [[El-Les]], a [[bounty hunter]] who assisted in the training of new [[Cloning|clone]] [[Clone trooper|troopers]], and [[Hem Dazon]], an Arcona who was stranded on [[Tatooine]].

==Biology and appearance==
Arcona were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] of [[desert]]-dwelling [[humanoid]]s that was [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Cona]]. They were distinguished by their triangular heads with two large [[eye]]s set wide apart,<ref name="Alien Archive" /> and hands with three or four fingers.<ref name="CLONECADETS" /> Arcona could have blue,<ref name="Fuel for the Fire" /> brown,<ref name="ANH" /> gold,<ref name="EYNTK" /> gray,<ref name="Han Chewie 1" /> green,<ref name="CLONECADETS" /> or purple colored skin.<ref name="TFVC 3" />

==Society and culture==
Arcona lived in family-based communities and were notorious for their addiction to common [[salt]].<ref name="Alien Archive" />

==Arcona in the galaxy==
During the [[Clone Wars]], an [[Gender|male]] Arcona [[bounty hunter]] named [[El-Les]] assisted in the training of new [[Cloning|clone]] [[Clone trooper|troopers]] for the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="CLONECADETS" />

A green Arcona was present at an event of Riot Racing on [[Serolonis]] around [[19 BBY]]. They were killed by a stray blaster bolt when one of the racers accidentally fired into the crowd.<ref name="Faster">{{TBB|Faster}}</ref>

Close to 20 [[Standard year|years]] later, shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]], a brown-skinned Arcona named [[Hem Dazon]] lived on [[Tatooine]], where he had become stranded after spending all his [[Currency|money]] on [[salt]] and [[juri juice]].<ref name="EYNTK" /> The Arcona [[Boggs Triff]] led a [[Boggs Triff|mob]] on the moon of [[Nar Shaddaa]] until he was hunted down by [[IG-88B]].<ref name="Tales">''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge – Last Call]]''</ref>

[[Reeg Brosna]] was an Arcona [[pirate]] in the [[Sidon Ithano's pirate crew|crew]] of the Crimson Corsair [[Sidon Ithano]]. While [[Hunt for Dooku's Treasure|searching]] for a lost [[Kix|treasure]], Brosna was swept away by a [[sandstorm]] on [[Ponemah Terminal]].<ref name="TCC">"[[The Crimson Corsair and the Lost Treasure of Count Dooku]]"</ref>

[[Garma]] was an elderly female Arcona who resided on the [[Colossus]].<ref name="Fuel for the Fire" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arcona first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], during the cantina scene.<ref name="ANH" /> Hem Dazon, the Arcona seen in the cantina, was realized as a simple puppet with illuminated eyes. Because of the shape of the creature, its production nickname was "T-head."<ref name="Old DB">{{DB|species|arcona|Arcona}}</ref>
