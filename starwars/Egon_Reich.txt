'''Egon Reich''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Zabrak/Legends|Zabrak]] who was a friend of [[Roan Novachez]], [[Pasha]], and [[Bill]] at the [[Jedi academy]] in the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].<ref name="jacademy" /> Once, during a [[Jedi Combat Acrobatics]] class, he was called upon to do a somersault, but ended up knocking into Roan and another student ([[Shi-Fara]]), an experience Roan described as being like [[bowling]].<ref name="return">''[[Jedi Academy: Return of the Padawan]]''</ref>
