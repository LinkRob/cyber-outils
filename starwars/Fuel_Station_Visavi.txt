'''Fuel Station Visavi''' was a [[Galactic Empire]] [[space station]] located in [[orbit]] of the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]. The Empire initially transported the station to Toshara when it first occupied the moon in order to [[fuel]] [[cruiser]]s and [[shuttle]]s. The corrupt [[Governor]] [[Thorden]] later used the fuel station to hold illicit goods as well as handle transactions with criminal syndicates. The scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] was hired by [[Crimson Dawn]] to board Fuel Station Visavi and erase data to frame the [[Pyke Syndicate]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
