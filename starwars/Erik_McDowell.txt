'''Erik McDowell '''is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] voice actor who voiced the [[Mantellian Separatist Movement|Mantellian Separatist]] [[Jex (Separatist)|Jex]] in the video game ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''.

==Biography==
McDowell was born in New York, and grew up in New York City. At a young age, he acted on stage and in some independent movies in his home state. After starting his professional career in acting being in the award-winning short ''So, What Do You Think?'', McDowell moved to Los Angeles to continue his career path.<ref name="Bio">{{OfficialSite|url=http://www.erikmcdowell.com/bio.htm|archivedate=20200201061256}}</ref>
