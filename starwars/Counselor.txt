'''Counselor''' was an advisory rank used by the [[Hetzalian government]]. [[Daan]] was one individual who served in the role.<ref name="LotJ" /> The term "counselor" did not exist as a title within the [[Galactic Empire]]'s government. Following the [[Battle of Endor|death]] of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and the loss of his puppet [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]], [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]] established the position [[Counselor to the Empire]], claiming himself to be unworthy of the [[Emperor]] title and too modest for something like Grand Admiral. In truth, Rax used the title to rule over the Empire. [[General]] [[Hodnar Borrum]] objected to the title's creation, but Rax managed to secure its creation all the same.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==Behind the scenes==
The counselor title was first mentioned within the novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' and then depicted via the Counselor to the Empire title.<ref name="Life Debt" /> The comic series ''[[Star Wars: Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege]]'' incorrectly features Gallius Rax using the title Counselor before he betrays Rae Sloane<ref name="Under Siege 2">[[Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege 2|''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 2]]</ref> when, as established by ''Aftermath: Life Debt'', he only established the Counselor to the Empire title&mdash;the first counselor title in Imperial records&mdash;after losing Sloane.<ref name="Life Debt" /> Indeed, Sloane only learns Rax has become known as "Counselor Rax" during the events of ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'',<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> only for [[Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege 2|''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 2]] to mistakenly feature Sloane calling Rax as such.<ref name="Under Siege 2" />
