A '''behavioral inhibitor''' was a piece of technology that controlled a [[droid]]'s behavior. They came in various levels of strictness, depending on the extremity of the droid's behavior. During the [[Imperial Era|age]] of the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]], rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] fitted the specialized [[assassin droid]] [[BT-1]] with behavioral inhibitors due to its homicidal tendencies.<ref name="Darth Vader 3">[[Darth Vader (2015) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 3]]</ref>
