The '''209th Stormtrooper Division''' was a [[division]] of [[stormtrooper]]s within the [[Galactic Empire]] who were stationed on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. The unit's proximity to the [[Emperor's Retreat|retreat]] of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] led many to believe that membership in the division was a fast track to joining the [[Emperor's Royal Guard]]. This led to transfer orders to the unit being highly desired and often called "Crimson Orders."

==Description==
The 209th Stormtrooper Division was an [[infantry]] [[division]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps]].<ref name="Rise" />

==History==
{{Quote|Whether that is actually true or not is difficult to ascertain given the absence of reliable records relating to Palpatine's red-robed defenders. Nonetheless, a transfer order to the 209th was often referred to as a 'Crimson Order' and they were, as a result, highly prized.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
[[File:ElectrostaffGuard-SWL.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=A red-robed member of the Emperor's Royal Guard stands in a gray-walled room wielding a electropike crackling with purple electricity.|Membership in the 209th was seen as a fast track to joining the Emperor's Royal Guard.]]
For most of the [[Imperial Era]], the 209th Stormtrooper Division was stationed on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]], [[homeworld]] of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. Their proximity to his [[Emperor's Retreat]] there led many in the [[Imperial Military]] to believe that the [[stormtrooper]]s within the division were on a fast track for recruitment into the [[Emperor's Royal Guard]], a group of elite, [[Color|crimson]]-[[Clothing|robed]] [[bodyguard]]s. As such, the highly-prized transfer orders into the 209th often became known as a "Crimson Order." A file on the division was stored in the "Military Deployments" section of the [[Imperial Military Archives]].<ref name="Rise" />

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned the 209th as an example of Imperial units having their own traditions or approaches in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''. Due to the absence of reliable records on the [[emperor]]'s guards, Kin was unable to determine if it was true that membership of the 209th led to an increased chance of recruitment.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 209th Stormtrooper Division was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
