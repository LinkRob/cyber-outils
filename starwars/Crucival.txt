'''Crucival''' was an Old Tionese [[Colonist|colonial]] backwater [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] and the [[homeworld]] of [[Hazram Namir]]. [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] defector [[Governor]] [[Everi Chalis]] considered settling there to avoid being found by the [[Galaxy|Galactic]] Empire. The world was almost constantly wracked by war between various factions, including the [[Malkhanis]] and their successors, the [[Opaline Creed]].

==Description==
{{Quote|I promise you, we have no designs on Crucival.''"<br />"''Not a lot here worth taking.|Gadren and Namir|<ref name="Twilight" />}}
Crucival was a terrestrial backwater [[planet]]<ref name="Twilight" /> located in the [[Crucival system]] in the [[Tion Hegemony]] [[sector]], within an intersecting portion of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> The world was situated at the coordinates S-6 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> It was covered vastly in [[Color|yellow]] [[grass]] and [[mountain]]s. It was rare to find any [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] other than [[human]]s on Crucival.<ref name="Twilight" />

==History==
===Wars break out===
{{Quote|It happened in my war, too. Even after our enemies won, they still turned on one another.|Namir's father, to his son, on the aftermath of the Clone Wars|<ref name="Twilight" />}}
After the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Twilight" /> a [[Galaxy|galactic]] war between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] which ended in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the victors of the war on Crucival turned on each other. During that time, the residents had little to no contact with the rest of the [[the galaxy]], aside from assets of the [[Galactic Empire]], the Republic's successor, which holed up at a [[Crucival transmitter tower|transmitter tower]]. Off-world goods such as [[blaster]]s were subsequently rare and valuable.<ref name="Twilight" /> Around [[6 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Malkhanis]], a clan led by [[Warlord]] [[Malkhan]], became prominent on Crucival, staking their claim to the world in a series of [[Malkhani Insurrections|insurrections]]. The success of the clan coaxed the young man [[Hazram Namir]] into joining, leaving behind his home and his [[Hazram Namir's father|father]] and going by the name Donin.<ref name="Twilight" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="6 BBY">The section of ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'' with the [[Malkhani Insurrections]] is set thirteen [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Clone Wars]], which [[Mission to Mustafar|ended]] in [[19 BBY]], according to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. The Malkhani Insurrections must have therefore occurred in [[6 BBY]].</ref> after an enemy faction captured a quarry from the Malkhanis, members of the clan, Namir included, gathered at a hilltop to face their adversaries on the forty-seventh [[Standard day|day]] of the Malkhani Insurrections. While the force awaited a battle, Namir was alerted by one of the Empire's [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. An older [[soldier]] warned him about messing with the Empire and spoke with him and advised him to take it one fight at a time. The Malkhanis' enemies eventually arrived and the two sides open fired on each other.<ref name="Twilight" /> The insurrections ended after that day,<ref name="Timelines" /> and Malkhan [[Death|died]]. The warlord's [[lieutenant]]s each laid claims to the their late leader's cache of off-world weapons, resulting in a civil war which saw many deaths and the end of the Malkhanis.<ref name="Twilight" />

===Wars continue===
{{Quote|No trouble. Mostly kind of boring.''"<br />"''Boring can be good. I know you're itching for a shooting war, but &mdash;boring is good. A break can be good.''"<br />"''I'm not&mdash; When the shooting ''does'' start? ''Someone's'' getting stabbed in the back.|Namir and Pira Ten|<ref name="Twilight" />}}
While many former Malkhani clansmen were forced to live out on the streets, their brands marking them as outcasts, Namir found and became a member of the [[Opaline Creed]], a religious cult based at a [[Opaline Creed cloister|cloister]] outside the [[city]] of [[Templemarch]], being given the name [[Umu]] Seven.<ref name="Twilight" /> In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="4 BBY">The section of ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'' involving the [[Tripartite Culture Effrontery]] is set fifteen [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Clone Wars]], which [[Mission to Mustafar|ended]] in [[19 BBY]], according to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. The Tripartite Culture Effrontery must have therefore occurred in [[4 BBY]].</ref> four-hundred [[Standard day|days]] into a period known as the [[Tripartite Culture Effrontery]], Namir ran errands for the Creed at a [[Unidentified bazaar|bazaar]] before making his way back towards Templemarch.<ref name="Twilight" />

On his way back to Templemarch, Namir encountered his father, but tried to keep walking without him. Namir's father attempted to have a conversation with his son and invited the boy back home. Namir, though, refused, making his loyalty to the Creed clear. His father leaving him, he continued to Templemarch and the Creed cloister, where he reunited with fellow Creed member [[Pira Ten]]. The pair spoke about an [[alien]] Namir had made up and lack of conflict among the Creed, which Namir found boring.<ref name="Twilight" />

===War comes from above===
{{Quote|Could you teach them? Could you make them into soldiers you would fight beside?''"<br />"''I wouldn't have much choice. If they were my comrades&hellip;I'd do what I had to in order to make them ready.''"<br />"''Then, perhaps we have a place for you after all.|Gadren accepts Namir into the 61st|<ref name="Twilight" />}}
The Creed eventually fell, along with its cloister, and other factions came to stake claim to Crucival. Namir served a number of them, including one led by the [[Lady of Coins]], a leader of acolytes.<ref name="Twilight" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="1 BBY">The section of ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'' where the Battle of the [[Crucival transmitter tower|Tower]] takes place at least two [[Standard month|months]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]], which corresponds to [[1 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore, the battle in 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] if it was fought at least two months beforehand.</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] targeted Crucival's Imperial transmitter tower. In anticipation of a rebel attack, the Imperials there invited Crucival's residents to fight for them, offering offworld blasters as payment. Namir accepted the invite, along with and a [[Hazram Namir's band|band]] of warriors he had recruited who were ready to stop moving between wars, including Pira. The [[Battle of the Tower]] soon broke out, and nearly all of Namir's band [[Death|died]] in the first wave. The Imperial forces quickly hid behind a large [[deflector shield]] around the tower, leaving their civilian recruits to face the rebel [[Alliance Military|forces]] alone, with the exception of [[TIE series|TIE]] fighters and [[probe droid]]s.<ref name="Twilight" />

On the fourth day of the battle, the 61st and its [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]'' were yet to break the shield. While other Crucival residents remained fighting, Namir chose to abandon his position, injuring himself when trying to avoid a probe [[droid]]. The soldier found a fatally-wounded Pira thereafter, and the pair spoke until the latter died of her injuries. After Namir escaped the battlefield and watched the fighting continue from the ruins of the Creed cloister. The transmitter tower fell the next day, and the 61st began recruiting Crucival residents a [[Standard week|week]] later. Namir, having spent near a week scavenging in the cloister went and enlisted, speaking with the [[Besalisk]] [[rebel trooper]] [[Gadren]] about war, other planets and Namir's experience with wars. Namir subsequently joined the 61st and left Crucival with the rebel [[company]]. After the abortion of [[Operation Ringbreaker]] and wanting to avoid the war to continue her artwork career on a remote world, former [[Governor]] [[Everi Chalis]]&mdash;a defector to both the Empire and Rebellion&mdash;decided to start anew on Crucival, having heard about it from Namir.<ref name="Twilight" />

==Locations==
{{Quote|The Creed's in Templemarch, yes? I'll make sure you're not bothered on the way.|Namir's father, to his son|<ref name="Twilight" />}}
On the Crucival, the Empire operated out of a transmitter tower until it's fall at the Battle of the Tower. Meanwhile, the Malkhanis controlled their own territories for some time, which included a camp and a quarry that was at one point captured by enemies. Crucival also included the city Templemarch, where the Opaline Creed operated from a cloister just outside that was reduced to ruins after the Creed's fall. There was also at least one bazaar that was a walking distance Templemarch, as well as the [[Gulches]].<ref name="Twilight" />
