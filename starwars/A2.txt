{{Quote|Access to A2 is limited to authorized personnel.|A public address announcer|<ref name="Rogue One" />}}
'''A2''' was a location within the [[Eadu Energy Conversion Laboratory]] on [[Eadu]]. During the [[mission to Eadu]], a public address announcer said that only authorized personnel could access A2.<ref name="Rogue One" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|One of the odd jobs I had on this movie was writing the PA chatter. In this shot, there's a voice mentioning that access to A2 is limited to authorized personal ''[''sic'']''. At the time, A2 was the floor at Lucasfilm where animation and story development sat.|Pablo Hidalgo|<ref name="A2">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1727464645307633864|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=One of the odd jobs I had on this movie was writing the PA chatter. A2 was first mentioned in the [[2016]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Rogue One" /> It was written as background chatter by [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Pablo Hidalgo]], as a reference to the floor at [[Lucasfilm]] for animation and story development.<ref name="A2" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[A2 (droid)|A2]] was an Imperial droid that appeared in the novel ''[[X-Wing: Wraith Squadron]]''.<ref name="Wraith Squadron">''[[X-Wing: Wraith Squadron]]''</ref>
