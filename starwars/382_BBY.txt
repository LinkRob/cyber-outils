The following events took place in or around the [[Standard year|year]] '''382 BBY''',<ref name="Year">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> also known as '''2895 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''363 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7595''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*The [[Rod of Seasons]] is [[Theft of the Rod of Seasons|stolen]] from [[Hynestia Prime]].<ref name="POD">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref>
*[[Zallah Macri]] and [[Kevmo Zink]] encounter the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] while [[Mission to Dalna (Macri and Zink)|investigating]] the theft of the rod. A Path member named [[Marda Ro]] falls for Zink, but both he and Macri are killed by the Path's newly-hatched [[Great Leveler|Leveler]], a [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creature that feeds on [[Force-sensitive]]s.<ref name="POD" />
*In the aftermath of the [[death]] of her lover, Marda Ro spurs the Path into expanding its reach by moving to the holy [[moon]] [[Jedha]].<ref name="POD" />
*[[Outer Rim Expedition Two-Five-Two]] is largely [[Expedition to Gloam|wiped out]] on the [[planet]] [[Gloam]] after a betrayal, with only [[Jedi Master]] [[Rok Buran]] surviving to send out a [[distress signal]]. Master [[Silandra Sho]] and her [[Master Sho's Pathfinder team|Pathfinder team]] [[Mission to Gloam|respond]], discovering the true history of Gloam and rescuing Buran, as well as stranded father-and-son [[prospector]]s [[Spence Leffbruk|Spence]] and [[Dass Leffbruk]].<ref name="QFTHC">''[[The High Republic: Quest for the Hidden City]]''</ref>
*The [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Jedi Order]] hold a [[Summit aboard the Paxion|summit]] between the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|warring]] planets [[Eiram]] and [[E'ronoh]] that is resolved with the heirs of both planets, [[Princess]] [[Xiri A'lbaran]] of E'ronoh and Prince [[Phan-tu Zenn]] of Eiram, agreeing to marry in the hopes of uniting the planets and ending the war.<ref name="Convergence" />
*The Path of the Open Hand and [[terrorist]] group the [[Children of E'ronoh]] attempt to assassinate Princess A'lbaran and Prince Zenn in the lead up to and during their [[Wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran|wedding]].<ref name="Convergence" />
*Jedi Masters [[Porter Engle]] and [[Barash Silvain]] perform a [[Mission on Gansevor|mission]] to the planet [[Gansevor]] to help warring factions there.<ref name="The Blade 2">[[The High Republic — The Blade 2|''The High Republic — The Blade'' 2]]</ref> They successfully stop the [[Siege of Firevale|Attack on Firevale]] and Engle defeats [[Abediah Viess]] and her [[Viess's army|mercenary army]]. However, Silvain, believing she made a grave mistake during the mission decides to leave from active participation in the Jedi Order and starts the [[Barash Vow]].<ref name="The Blade 4">[[The High Republic — The Blade 4|''The High Republic — The Blade'' 4]]</ref>
*[[Maz Kanata]] and her [[Maz Kanata's pirate crew|pirate crew]] engage in a [[Turf war with the Dank Graks|turf war]] with the [[Dank Graks]].<ref name="THRA 2">[[The High Republic Adventures (2022) 2|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2022) 2]]</ref>
*A [[Summit on Jedha|summit]] is held in the [[Jedha City|holy city]] of [[Jedha]] to officially end the Eiram and E'ronoh war, but a series of bombings and riots spurred by the Path of the Open Hand leads to the war reigniting on the [[moon]] in the form of the [[Battle of Jedha]].<ref name="Battle of Jedha">''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]''</ref>
*After returning to Dalna, the Path of the Open Hand conducts a [[Mission to Planet X (Path of the Open Hand)|mission]] to the mysterious [[Sophros|Planet X]] to retrieve more Nameless.<ref name="PoV">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref>
*The [[Hyperspace Chase]] is held, which [[Sky Graf]], Dass Leffbruk, and Master Sho's Padawan [[Rooper Nitani]] utilize to try and find Planet X, though they ultimately abandon the mission.<ref name="QfPX">''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]''</ref>
*After receiving an ultimatum from her son Axel Greylark and the Path's [[Mother (title)|Mother]], [[Elecia Zeveron]] who wish to exploit her power, Kyong Greylark steps down as [[Supreme Chancellor]]<ref name="Cataclysm">''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]''</ref>
*Marda Ro reorganizes the militant members of the Path present on Dalna into the [[Path of the Closed Fist]].<ref name="PoV" />
*The Path of the Open Hand attacks several groups of Jedi that arrived on Dalna to investigate them, erupting into a battle that becomes known as the [[Night of Sorrow]] in which many Jedi are killed by the Path's Nameless<ref name="PoV" /><ref name="Cataclysm" />
*In the aftermath of the Night of Sorrow, the Path of the Open Hand is disbanded and Elecia Zeveron is killed by Marda Ro.<ref name="PoV" />
*Marda Ro takes the Path's flagship the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' and the Leveler, leaving Dalna and founding what will become the [[Nihil]].<ref name="PoV" />
*[[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] and Master [[Creighton Sun]] decide to keep the Nameless secret and out of the [[Jedi Archives]], believing it will help protect the Jedi.<ref name="Cataclysm" />
*After being driven to madness by encountering the Nameless three times during the Night of Sorrow, Jedi Knight [[Azlin Rell]] writes a [[Azlin's journal|journal]] about the creatures before abandoning the Jedi Order, falling to the dark side of the Force, and dedicating his life to studying the Nameless.<ref name="Precedent">''[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance: Precedent]]''</ref>
