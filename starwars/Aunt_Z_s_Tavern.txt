'''Aunt Z's Tavern''' was the most popular [[Restaurant|eatery]] on the ''[[Colossus]]''. It was owned by [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza]], whose alias Aunt Z gave the [[Cantina|tavern]] its name. A [[droid]] named [[G1-7CH|Glitch]] assisted her by [[Service droid|serving]] clients and running simulation on the races' odds. The tavern was one of the sponsors of [[Hype Fazon]], and his ship, ''[[Green Ace]]''.<ref name="Aunt Z DB" /><ref name="The Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref><ref name="The High Tower">{{Resistance|The High Tower}}</ref>

==Description==
Newcomers or regulars, like [[Al]] and [[Jooks]], could go there to [[Foodstuff|eat]], bet on the races, receive wise advice from [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza|Aunt Z]], and of course enjoy a drink. The counter was often busy, but there were several other areas for patrons to hang about, including alcoves and a balcony that had a direct view of [[Doza Tower]]. Besides refreshment, foods, bets, and the occasional bar fight, patrons could also entertain themselves with [[music]] from a jukebox, two arcades, and a [[Holo-darts|holo-dart]] target.<ref name="Aunt Z DB" /><ref name="The Recruit" /><ref name="The High Tower" />

The place was decorated with all sort of oddities. Behind the counter was a collection of [[helmet]]s, some of them [[Mandalorian]], [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]], [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]], or even from the old [[clone trooper pilot]]. There was also a reproduction of the decal that decorated the flanks of the ''[[Crumb Bomber]]'', a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|gunship]] from the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="High Tower BL">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-high-tower-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 9 Fun Facts from "The High Tower" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref>
