The '''hyperdrive chamber''' of the ''[[Colossus]]'' was located deep in the station. It contained the [[supertanker fuel depot]]'s [[hyperdrive]], mounted directly above the central control table. The room also contained controls for all of the ''Colossus''<nowiki>'</nowiki> systems, including doors, power systems, and atmospheric controls.<ref name="No Escape 1" /> By the time the ''Colossus'' was [[Occupation of the Colossus|occupied]] by the [[First Order]] in [[34 ABY]], the hyperdrive chamber had been out of use for many years.<ref name="Bucket's List Extra">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-no-escape-part-1-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 8 Fun Facts from "No Escape: Part 1" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Colossus''<nowiki>'</nowiki> hyperdrive chamber first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|season 1]] episode "[[No Escape: Part 1]]."<ref name="No Escape 1" /> It was designed to feel like the lower levels of [[Cloud City]] and the [[carbon-freezing]] chamber, as seen in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. The intent was that as the room had not been used for several years, it needed to feel dark and abandoned.<ref name="Bucket's List Extra" />
