'''Dark Side Chocolate''' was a type of [[chocolate]]. Named after the [[Dark side of the Force]], it featured the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[Darth Vader's armor|helmet]] on the [[Color|gray]] wrapper. The wrapper also had the candy's name [[Writing|written]] in [[High Galactic]] on it.<ref name="Card Trader" />
