The '''Corellia Mining Corporation''' was a [[mining]] company from the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] that produced [[sandcrawler]]s and the [[TR-RL3 Terrapin-class bulk tanker|''Terrapin''-class Bulk Tanker]] "Turtle Tanker". A number of the corporation's sandcrawlers were brought to the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] during a [[mining]] boom, where they were later abandoned and taken over by [[Jawa]] clans<ref name="EoSaOV" /> at some point prior to [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{Film|II}} depicts a [[sandcrawler]] inhabited by a [[Jawa]] clan. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' states that the events of ''Attack of the Clones'', which includes the appearance of the sandcrawler, to [[22 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Corellia Mining Corporation was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]'', which was written by [[Landry Q. Walker]].<ref name="EoSaOV" /> The company originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[1998]] game guide ''[[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron: The Official Nintendo Player's Guide]]''.<ref name="RSGG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron: The Official Nintendo Player's Guide]]''</ref>
