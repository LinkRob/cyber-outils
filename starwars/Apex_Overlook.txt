The '''Apex Overlook''' was a luxurious open plaza located in the heights of [[Cloud City]], a floating structure suspended high among the clouds of [[Bespin]]. At the time of the [[Iron Blockade]], high-ranking members of the [[Anoat sector]]'s [[Noble Court]]-in-exile sometimes met at the Apex Overlook.<ref name="Uprising" />
