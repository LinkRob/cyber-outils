'''Droid remote controls''' were devices used to remotely activate [[droid]]s. The [[Broken Horn Syndicate|crimelord]] [[Cikatro Vizago]] used them to control his [[IG-RM bodyguard and enforcer droid]]s. After the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] stunned him and captured his [[starship]] ''[[Broken Horn]]'', Ohnaka took the remote control. After meeting the pirate, rebel [[Ezra Bridger]] stole the remote control. After freeing Vizago as part of a deal, Vizago was able to regain it and take back his ship.<ref name="BotBH">{{Rebels|Brothers of the Broken Horn}}</ref>
