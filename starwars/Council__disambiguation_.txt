A '''council''' is a group of people, staffed by councilors and usually those possessing some form of governing power.

==Canon==
*'''[[Advisory council]]'''&mdash; a council that operated on the [[planet]] [[Troithe]] alongside remnants of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s forces.
*'''[[Andelm IV council]]'''&mdash; a council that controlled the [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]] on the planet [[Andelm IV]].
*'''[[Archaeological Research Council]]'''&mdash; a committee of [[archaeologist]]s on [[Kallidah]].
*'''[[Bahk-tov Council]]'''&mdash; also known as the '''Bardotta Council''', was the ruling body of [[Bardotta]] and comprised members from the [[Dagoyan Order]].
*'''[[Black Sun ruling council]]'''&mdash; a group of [[Falleen]] [[noble]]s that controlled the [[Black Sun]] until its massacre by [[Lord]] [[Maul]] and the [[Shadow Collective]].
*'''[[Bromlarch Council]]'''&mdash; the planetary government council of [[Bromlarch]].
*'''[[Calamari Council]]'''&mdash; also known as the '''Mon Calamari government council''', was a representative legislative body of [[Mon Cala]].
*'''[[Council (Remainders)]]''', the governing body of the [[Remainders]] of [[Moonbender Colony]]
*'''[[Council of Elders]]'''&mdash; a [[Religion|religious]] body within the [[Elders of the Path]], privy to sacred knowledge.
*'''[[Council of Elders (Ewok)]]'''&mdash; the ruling body of an [[Ewok]] tribe on the [[Endor|Forest Moon of Endor]].
*'''[[Council of Elders (Path of the Open Hand)]]'''&mdash; the governing body that oversaw the [[Path of the Open Hand]].
*'''[[Council of First Knowledge]]'''&mdash; one of four [[Jedi Council]]s within the [[Jedi Order]], and was responsible for procuring advise and ancient wisdom.
*'''[[Council of Labor Abuses]]'''&mdash; a body within the [[Imperial Senate]].
*'''[[Council of Moffs]]'''&mdash; a collective body within the Galactic Empire that was composed of [[moff]]s and created following the betrayal of [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]].
*'''[[Council of Mothers]]'''&mdash; the ruling body on the planet [[Gatalenta]].
*'''[[Council of Neutral Systems]]'''&mdash; a [[Politics|political faction]] within the [[Galactic Senate]] that was led by [[Mandalorian]] [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]], and made up of 1,500 neutral [[star system]]s.
*'''[[Council of Reassignment]]'''&mdash; one of four Jedi Councils within the Jedi Order, it managed the [[Jedi Service Corps]] as well as [[Jedi Initiate]]s who were chosen to not become [[Padawan]]s.
*'''[[Council of Reconciliation]]'''&mdash; one of four Jedi Councils within the Jedi Order, it was charged with settling interplanetary disputes, and worked with the [[Republic Diplomatic Corps]] and the Galactic Senate.
*'''[[Defense Hierarchy Council]]'''&mdash; also known as the '''Hierarchy Defense Council''', a military council of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] which included high-ranking individuals, including [[Supreme Admiral]] [[Ja'fosk]] and [[Supreme General]] [[Ba'kif]].
*'''[[Dug Council]]'''&mdash; also known as the '''Malastarian council''', a government body that represented the [[Dug]] [[species]] on [[Malastare]].
*'''[[Entropian Hive Trading Council]]'''&mdash; a group that controlled the [[Entropian Hive]] [[asteroid]] trading post.
*'''[[Executive Separatist Council]]'''&mdash; the executive government body of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], helmed by [[Head of State]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and comprising the leaders of major corporations.
*'''[[First Order Supreme Council]]'''&mdash; alternatively known as the '''First Order High Council''', a high-ranking military council of [[First Order High Command]] that was created and chaired by [[Supreme Leader]] of the [[First Order]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]].
*'''[[Grand Hutt Council]]'''&mdash; a ruling group of each [[Hutt]] crime family that led the [[Hutt Clan]]. Its leader was [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]].
*'''[[Gungan High Council]]'''&mdash; also identified as the '''Gungan Rep Council''', was the governing body of [[Gungan]] society on the planet [[Naboo]].
*'''[[Iego council]]'''&mdash; a guild of residents and officials on [[Iego]].
*'''[[Imperial Future Council]]'''&mdash; a short-lived group of high ranking Imperial officials, including [[Grand Moff]] [[Valco Pandion]], [[Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]], [[General]] [[Jylia Shale]], [[Arsin Crassus]], and [[Yupe Tashu]] that attempted to take control of the splintering Empire during the [[Galactic Civil War]].
*'''[[Imperial Ruling Council]]'''&mdash; also known as the '''Imperial Advisory Council''', a collective ruling body of bureaucrats that was made up of the closest [[advisor]]s of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. The Imperial Rulign Council ran the affairs of the Empire.
*'''[[High Council (Talz)|High Council]]'''&mdash; the governing group of [[Talz]] on [[Orto Plutonia]] that was led by [[Chief]]tain [[Thi-Sen]].
*'''[[Imperial Penal Council]]'''&mdash; a judicial organization within the Galactic Empire.
*'''[[Jedi Council]]'''&mdash; a governing group within the Jedi Order, including the [[Council of First Knowledge]], the [[Council of Reassignment]], the [[Council of Reconciliation]], and the [[Jedi High Council]].
*'''[[Jedi High Council]]'''&mdash; a body of twelve high-ranking [[Jedi]] that led the Jedi Order. It was led by the [[Master of the Order]], and spearheaded the Jedi Order's operations.
*'''[[Li-Toran planetary council]]'''&mdash; a ruling council created by the largest [[Melitto hive]] following the [[Unending Conflict]] to prevent further catastrophe.
*'''[[Mandalorian Government Council]]'''&mdash; also known as the '''New Mandalorian ministry council''' or simply as the '''Ruling Council''', it was the government body of [[minister]]s charged with maintaining the [[New Mandalorians|New Mandalorian government]].
*'''[[Naboo Royal Advisory Council]]'''&mdash; also known as the '''Royal Consultative Council''', it was composed of ministers that served as advisors for the [[Monarch of Naboo]].
*'''[[Oba Diah high council]]'''&mdash; an organization within the [[Pyke Syndicate]] on [[Oba Diah]].
*'''[[Republic High Council]]'''&mdash; an organization of the [[Galactic Republic]] active during the [[High Republic Era]].
*'''[[Republic Judiciary Council]]'''&mdash; a judicial organization that held responsibility over the [[Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center]] and its 3,200-strong [[Coruscant Guard]] force.
*'''[[Republic Security Council]]'''&mdash; a Galactic Senate committee that was responsible for protecting the Republic and oversaw the Republic's war effort during the [[Clone Wars]]. It advised the [[Supreme Chancellor]] and possessed the ability to activate any of the [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic]].
*'''[[Ruling Council (Kamino)]]'''&mdash; the governing council that oversaw the [[Kaminoan]] government.
*'''[[Ruling Council (Skako Minor)]]'''&mdash; a governing planetary council of [[Skako Minor]], until the massacre of its members by the Empire. It was replaced by the [[Skakoan Council]].
*'''[[Security council]]'''&mdash; a [[New Republic]] committee responsible for security and defense.
*'''[[Shadow Council]]'''&mdash; a secret council of high-ranking Imperials that was established in the latter stages of the Galactic Civil War and survived [[Standard year|years]] into the [[New Republic Era]]. Originally, it comprised [[Counselor to the Empire]] [[Gallius Rax]], [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]], [[Grand Moff]] [[Randd]], [[General]] [[Hodnar Borrum]], [[Commandant]] [[Brendol Hux]], and [[Chief informational officer]] [[Ferric Obdur]]. During the New Republic Era, it was composed of Grand Admiral [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[Moff]] [[Gideon]], Commandant Hux, [[Captain]] [[Gilad Pellaeon]], and multiple [[warlord]]s.
*'''[[Special Senator Council on Galactic De-Escalation Strategies]]'''&mdash; a [[New Republic Senate]] committee.
*'''[[Skakoan Council]]'''&mdash; a ruling regime that governed Skako Minor that was loyal to the Galactic Empire.
*'''[[Togruta security council]]'''&mdash; a security and military council within the [[Togruta government]] that was tasked with advising the [[Togruta]] [[monarch]] on such issues.
*'''[[Toydarian Ministry Council]]'''&mdash; a ministerial body of [[Togruta]] that also advised the ruling monarch.
*'''[[Umbaran ruling council]]'''&mdash; a governing body that ruled the [[Umbaran government|government]] of [[Umbara]].
*'''[[Virujansi council]]'''&mdash; the government that oversaw [[Virujansi]] until its replacement by an Imperial [[governor]]ship.
*'''[[War Council Advisory Panel]]'''&mdash; a Galactic Republic military body that advised the Supreme Chancellor of matters related to the war effort during the Clone Wars.
*'''[[Wookiee Council]]'''&mdash; the government that ruled [[Kashyyyk]] and was a member of the Galactic Republic.
*'''[[Yomo Council]]'''&mdash; a faction that splintered from the Galactic Empire during the final stages of the civil war and based on [[Fedovoi End]] until its destruction.
