A [[Gender|male]] '''individual''' lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. At some point, he became the [[Parent|father]] of [[Cibaba]], a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Azumel]].<ref name="Saber for Hire 4" /> Long before around [[228 BBY]], Cibaba's father had [[Death|passed away]].<ref name="Date" /> By [[241 BBY]],<ref name="Jedi Master date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' states [[Cibaba]] was a [[Jedi Master]] at the time that [[Klias Teradine]] was [[Duel on Mount Bikja|killed]] during the flashback events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak]]'', which are dated to [[241 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Cibaba had been a<ref name="Saber for Hire 4" /> [[Jedi Master]]<ref name="Jedi Master date" /> of the [[Jedi Order]] before later joining [[Nihil]] [[Baron]] [[Boolan]]'s [[Jedi hunter|Jedi hunting]] [[Children of the Storm]]. While serving the Nihil, Cibaba took to calling Boolan his [[Father (Nihil)|Father]] as the [[Ithorian]] had "remade" him.<ref name="Saber for Hire 4" />

Around 228 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Cibaba was [[Skirmish on Abtin|confronted]] by his former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]], [[Ty Yorrick]], on<ref name="Saber for Hire 4" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Saber for Hire 1">[[The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire 1|''The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire'' 1]]</ref> [[Abtin]]. Yorrick looked into the Azumel's thoughts and [[Force vision|saw]] him being operated on by an Ithorian. When she asked who she had seen, Cibaba told Yorrick that it was his Father, which she countered by stating his father was long dead and she was sure he had not been an Ithorian. Cibaba then reaffirmed that Boolan was his Father and that he had become his Child when the baron had "remade" him.<ref name="Saber for Hire 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cibaba's father was mentioned in the [[The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire 4|fourth issue]] of the [[2024]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire]]''. The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]<ref name="Saber for Hire 4" /> on [[October 9]], 2024.<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3014-607/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures--Saber-for-Hire-4|text=Star Wars: The High Republic--Saber for Hire #4}}</ref>
