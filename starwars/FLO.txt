'''FLO''', also known as '''WA-7''',<ref name="CVD, NE">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> or '''Wanda''',<ref name="Inheritance">{{InsiderCite|211|Galactic Tales: Inheritance}}</ref> was the nickname of a [[WA-7 waitress droid]] working at [[Dex's Diner]] on [[Coruscant]]. She once served [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] when he came to the diner asking for help from [[Dexter Jettster]].<ref name="Episode II" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|You've, er, caught me at a bad time. My best waitress, Wanda, has blown a fuse and I can't keep up with all these customers! Think ya could help out an old buddy and take some orders to the right tables? I gotta fix up old Wanda. Soon as we get finished, I'm all yours. Whaddya say?|Dexter Jettster in ''LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga''|<ref name="LEGOTSS">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''</ref>}}
FLO was voiced by Australian actress [[Susie Porter]] for [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], who also portrays [[Hermione Bagwa]] in the film. During shooting, Porter delivered her lines as both FLO and Bagwa, though in the final film FLO was chosen to have the speaking role.<ref name="DB-Bagwa, Hermione">{{DB|character|hermionebagwa|Bagwa, Hermione}}</ref> The name FLO is a nod to the character Florence "Flo" Castleberry from the 1970s sitcom [[Wikipedia:Alice (American TV series)|''Alice'']], itself based on the 1974 film [[Wikipedia:Alice Doesn't Live Here Anymore|''Alice Doesn't Live Here Anymore'']], which was directed by [[George Lucas]]'s filmmaker friend [[Martin Scorsese]].<ref>{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1487499338259435523|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=And yes, Wanda's name tag says FLO, which is a nod to Flo from 'Alice,' which itself is an adaptation / spin-off of a Martin Scorsese film, Alice Doesn't Live Here Anymore, and George and Marty are friends, so it's references within references.|archivedate=20220303201616}}</ref>

In the non-canon video game ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', FLO is referred to as "Wanda," and she blows a fuse just before [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] arrives at the [[Restaurant|diner]] to ask [[Dexter Jettster]] about a [[Kamino saberdart]]. As a result, Jettster has to ask Kenobi to help wait tables while he fixes Wanda.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
