'''Dhanduese Drums''' were a [[Foodstuff|dish]] consisting of roasted, [[Color|brown]] [[tip-yip]] [[Limb|drumsticks]] coated in a [[special spice rub]] made by the [[Bird|avian]] [[Dhanduese]] [[species]] and topped with a green garnish. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] made the dish after visiting the Dhanduese [[homeworld]], the [[Dhandu 3|third moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Dhandu]], during a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref>

Tuggs initially saw the Dhanduese as a potential ingredient before learning that they were [[Sentience|sentient]] and so shared a meal with them instead. His hosts then gave him the [[Spice (seasoning)|spice]] rub that he would use on the drumsticks, and he later included the recipe for Dhanduese Drums in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> In the book, Tuggs called the drumsticks flavorful and said that they would always remind him of the Dhanduese, recommending readers serve the drumsticks on top of [[Pasaana Battercake]]s for a powerful flavor combination.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dhanduese Drums were created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the dish makes use of [[Wikipedia:Chicken as food|drumsticks]], [[Wikipedia:Corn starch|cornstarch]], [[Wikipedia:Chili powder|chili powder]], [[Wikipedia:Powdered sugar|confectioners' sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Cumin|cumin]], [[Wikipedia:Curry powder|curry powder]], [[Wikipedia:Turmeric|turmeric]], [[Wikipedia:Coriander|coriander]], [[Wikipedia:Garlic salt|garlic salt]], [[Wikipedia:Kosher salt|kosher salt]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper#White pepper|white pepper]], minced [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic cloves]], minced [[Wikipedia:Ginger|ginger]], [[Wikipedia:Olive oil|olive oil]], and minced [[Wikipedia:Scallion|green onions]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
