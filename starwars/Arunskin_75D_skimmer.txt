The '''Arunskin 75D skimmer''' was a model of [[skimmer]] produced by [[GoCorp]]. The [[Aki-Aki]] [[farmer]] [[Jo-Dapshi Gorobunn]] owned a 75D skimmer on [[Pasaana]], using it on his [[farm]]. The skimmer was stolen by [[Resistance]] members who were [[Mission to Pasaana (Resistance)|escaping]] [[First Order]] forces.<ref name="TROS VD" />
