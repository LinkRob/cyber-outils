'''Application SUG-171-TI''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] application used to dispatch an operational suggestion. It was used when it was needed to bypass the chain of command and there was no military emergency. The application was created by [[Imperial Navy High Command|Fleet Command]] as a method to improve the initiatives of all [[Imperial officer|officers]].<ref name="TSOD">{{StoryCite|story=The Sith of Datawork|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>
