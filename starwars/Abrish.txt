'''Abrish''' was a [[Muun]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Tweet" /> who worked for a small [[MaDall's empire|enslavement empire]] on the [[planet]] of [[Oon]] during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]].<ref name="RSlavers" /> 

== History ==
Abrish worked for [[MaDall]]'s [[MaDall's empire|small enslavement operation]] on [[Oon]], managing the acquisition of new slaves. At least once in [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Return of the Slavers]]" to [[4 BBY]].</ref> Abrish oversaw a new shipment of slaves collected from all across the [[Lothal sector]], informing the empire's leader, MaDall, of her newest acquisitions to arrive from aboard an [[YV-865 Aurore-class freighter|''Aurore''-class freighter]], which included a [[Mandalorian]] called [[Sabine Wren]].<ref name="RSlavers" />

== Personality and traits ==
A male [[Muun]], Abrish had green skin and gold eyes. He was aloof to [[slavery]] and sought to please his master, MaDall, with those slaves he acquired.<ref name="RSlavers" />

==Behind the scenes==
Abrish first appeared,<ref name="RSlavers" /> albeit unnamed,<ref name="Tweet" /> in "[[Return of the Slavers]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] found in the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 5|fifth]] issue of the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]].<ref name="RSlavers" /> Abrish later received a name in a [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] post by [[Martin Fisher]], who showed a part of the script of "Return of the Slavers" to confirm Abrish's name and that they are a [[Muun]].<ref name="Tweet" />
