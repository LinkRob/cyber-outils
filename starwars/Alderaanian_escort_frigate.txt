The '''[[Alderaanian]] escort frigate''' was a [[warship]] that served in the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]]. One known starship was the ''[[Sunspire]]'', which was gifted by [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="Life Debt" /> Another ship named the [[Sunspire (New Republic)|''Sunspire'']] saw action during the [[rebellion on Akiva]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> and a [[Skirmish near Wild Space|skirmish]] near [[Wild Space]].<ref name="Life Debt" />
