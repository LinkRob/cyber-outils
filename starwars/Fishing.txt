'''Fishing''',<ref name="Vision trivia">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/vision-of-hope-trivia-gallery|text="Vision of Hope" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> also known as '''slinging''',<ref name="Relics Rebels Recon">{{RebelsRecon|VRAcatgArJA|#2.03: Inside "Relics of the Old Republic" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-relics-of-the-old-republic}}</ref> was the act of catching [[fish]]. Long before the arrival of the [[Galactic Empire]], fisheries were a major industry on the [[planet]] of [[Lothal]].<ref name="Vision trivia" /> During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], fishing operations also existed on [[Arkanis]], and [[Scaparus Port]] housed many shops supplying fisherfolk.<ref name="secret">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref> The planet [[Pillio]] was a profitable destination for feuding [[Mon Calamari]] and [[Nautolan]] fishing companies.<ref name="Pillio Databank">{{Databank|pillio|Pillio}}</ref> [[Penn Zarang]] once said that fishing was a dangerous business on Arkanis.<ref name="secret" /> The [[Sifo-Dyas' father|father]] of [[Sifo-Dyas]] was a fisherman in [[Minashee]].<ref name="D:JL">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> The Force-sensitive woman [[Verla]] worked as a fisherwoman on the planet [[Serelia]].<ref name="Star Wars 5">[[Star Wars (2020) 5|''Star Wars'' (2020) 5]]</ref>
