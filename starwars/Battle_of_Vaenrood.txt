During the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], a '''battle''' occurred on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Vaenrood]],<ref name="tdc" /> located in the [[Vaenrood system/Legends|system of the same name]] in the [[Pakuuni sector/Legends|Pakuuni sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="tea-oc">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Vaenrood system/Legends|Vaenrood system]]</ref> Several [[Standard month/Legends|months]] before the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] [[Battle of Kal'Shebbol|removed]] [[Moff/Legends|Moff]] [[Kentor Sarne]] from power on the planet [[Kal'Shebbol/Legends|Kal'Shebbol]], Republic forces engaged the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] on Vaenrood. During the engagement, [[New Republic Army|New Republic ground forces]] prosecuted an action against Imperial units in [[Mountain/Legends|mountainous]] terrain, supported by [[T-65 X-wing starfighter/Legends|X-wing]] [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighters]] flying air support. During the engagement, one of the X-wings&mdash;[[Pilot/Legends|piloted]] by [[Darryn Thyte]]&mdash;had its wing clipped by Imperial [[Artillery/Legends|artillery]] fire. The resulting crash buckled and snapped the canopy, which in turn sheared off most of Thyte's right [[Limb/Legends|arm]] above the elbow.<ref name="tdc" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Vaenrood was mentioned in the short story "[[The Saga Begins]]" and in Darryn Thyte's biography in ''[[The DarkStryder Campaign]]'', a book published by [[West End Games]] in [[1995]].<ref name="tdc" />
