"'''An Extinct Guest'''" is a short story written by [[Trevor J. Wilson]] and [[Craig Robert Carey]]. It forms part of the article "[[Alien Encounters (article series)|Alien Encounters]]" in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 14|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 14]], first published in [[August]] [[1997]]. The story tells of the [[Iyra]] [[sentientologist]] [[Tem Eliss]] receiving a visit from [[S'itl Thirr]], a member of the [[Abinyshi]], a [[Species/Legends|species]] that [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] records listed as extinct.
