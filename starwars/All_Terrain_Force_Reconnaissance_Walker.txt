The '''All Terrain Force Reconnaissance Walker (AT-FRC)''' was a two-person bipedal [[walker]] used for reconnaissance, first by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s near the end of the [[Clone Wars]], and later on by the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was derived from the smaller, one-position [[All Terrain Recon Transport]] (AT-RT).<ref name="LbE" />

The AT-FRC featured a narrow, lightly-[[Armor|armored]] body between two reverse-articulated legs that gave the walker a smaller silhouette, enabling it to effectively utilize cover. The driver would sit in the higher of the two seats, with the lower being occupied by the reconnaissance systems operator in a [[transparisteel]] bubble canopy providing good visibility with few blind spots.<ref name="LbE" />
