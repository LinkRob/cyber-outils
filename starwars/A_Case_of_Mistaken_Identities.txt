"'''A Case of Mistaken Identities'''" is a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure outline in the [[September]] [[1996]] ''[[Shadows of the Empire Planets Guide]]'', published by [[West End Games]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' as part of the ''[[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire]]'' multimedia project. The adventure sends [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] players to the [[Both system]], where they inadvertently become embroiled in a conflict between warring [[Crime lord/Legends|crime lords]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Bothawui/Legends|Bothawui]]. ''A Case of Mistaken Identities'' serves as a prelude to two sequel adventures involving the Rebel players, ''[[An Invitation to Defect]]'' and ''[[A Most Deadly Hunt]]''.
