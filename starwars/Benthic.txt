'''Benthic''' "'''Two Tubes'''", also known simply as "'''Tubes''',"<ref name="TOG" /> was a [[Gender|male]] [[Tognath]] [[mercenary]] [[pilot]] who flew alongside his [[eggmate]] and brother,<ref name="Alien Archive" /> [[Edrio]]. The two shared the nickname derived from the breathing apparatus that allowed their Tognath physiology to process [[oxygen]] [[atmosphere]]s. Benthic's [[homeworld]] [[Yar Togna]] was conquered and occupied by the [[Galactic Empire]], forcing him to flee as a [[refugee]]. Desiring to strike back at the Empire, Benthic allied himself with [[Enfys Nest]]'s [[Cloud-Riders]] and later Edrio and Benthic allied with [[Saw Gerrera]] sometime before the [[Battle of Yavin]].

==Biography==
===Early life===
[[File:Tubes.png|thumb|left|150px|Benthic during his time with the Cloud-Riders]]
Benthic was [[Reproduction|born]] in [[32 BBY]]<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> on the [[planet]] [[Yar Togna]]. Sometime during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], the world was [[Conquest of Yar Togna|conquered]] by the [[Galactic Empire]], forcing Benthic, along with his [[eggmate]]/brother<ref name="Alien Archive" /> [[Edrio]], to flee and become [[refugee]]s.<ref name="Visual Guide" /> While many of his [[Species|people]] were exploited by criminal syndicates like [[Crimson Dawn]] after fleeing their [[homeworld]], Benthic joined [[Enfys Nest]]'s [[Cloud-Riders]], a [[pirate]] group that took revenge on the Empire and [[crime lord]]s who subjugated his people.<ref name="TOG" /> In [[10 BBY]], Benthic accompanied Nest and his fellow Riders to a [[Showdown on Savareen|rendezvous]] with [[Han Solo]] and his [[Beckett's gang|crew]] on [[Savareen]]. After a brief showdown, the group took off their helmets and Nest convinced them to turn against [[Dryden Vos]] from Crimson Dawn, which ended with the Riders taking the [[coaxium]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

Desiring to strike back against the Empire, Benthic and Edrio later joined the [[Partisans]], a rebel militia led by [[Saw Gerrera]],<ref name="Edrio Two Tubes" /> eventually becoming Gerrera's second-in-command.<ref name="Star Wars 39">[[Star Wars (2015) 39|''Star Wars'' (2015) 39]]</ref>

===Actions with the Partisans===
{{Quote|He's lying! I have only allegiance to you!|translated=1|Benthic|<ref name="Daughter of Ferrix">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref>}}
In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Narkina Trivia">{{AndorDate|108}}</ref> Benthic and the Partisans were stationed at a [[Saw Gerrera's Base|base]] on<ref name="Narkina 5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref> the [[planet]]<ref name="Narkina 5 AD">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> [[Segra Milo]]. The Tognath stood outside the base while the rebel [[Luthen Rael]] spoke with Gerrera inside, offering the Partisans a mission to assist rebel [[Anto Kreegyr]]. Saw refused to risk the lives of his forces for the attack, however, dismissing Kreegyr as a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]].<ref name="Narkina 5" /> Later on, however, Gerrera was willing to take on the mission.<ref name="Daughter of Ferrix" />

[[File:Benthic_Segra_Milo.png|thumb|272x272px|Benthic was stationed alongside the Partisans at a base on Segra Milo.]]

When Rael visited again, he approached the base, only to be intercepted by Benthic and a fighters. The Tognath ordered Luthen to stop, then demanded that the rebel be searched. Rael expressed confusion at his orders, but Benthic rebutted that Saw was in a mood for it. The fighter then searched Luthen, and when they pulled out a staff of his, Rael asked that they either put it down or give it back. Irritated, Benthic ushered Luthen deeper into the base, where Saw was waiting for them.<ref name="Daughter of Ferrix" />

Upon their arrival, Gerrera asked Rael to tell Kreegyr that the Partisans would help him attack the [[Spellhaus Imperial power station]], but the rebel advised Saw not to assist in order to safeguard their rebellion against the Empire, as the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] had laid a trap for Kreegyr and his [[Anto Kreegyr's group|men]]. In the ensuing argument, Saw interrogated Luthen about the [[Axis network|people]] providing him with information, and questioned if he had an agent in the Partisans' ranks. He was shocked when Rael claimed that Benthic was his informant, but as the Tognath stepped forward to rebuke the claim, Luthen grabbed Benthic's [[DT-15 blaster pistol]] and held it to Saw, which compelled Benthic to back off. After a short standoff, Rael convinced Saw to let Anto take the fall for their mutual benefit and took his leave from the base.<ref name="Daughter of Ferrix" />

===Jedha insurgency===
[[File:Tognath-SW_Card_Trader.png|thumb|180px|Benthic (left) and Edrio (right) during the Jedha insurgency|left]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the Partisans were engaged in an [[Jedha insurgency|insurgency]] on the [[desert]] [[moon]] [[Jedha]] with [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]]. Sometime later, the Imperial [[pilot]] [[Bodhi Rook]] defected from the Empire and sought out Gerrera with a message from [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]]. Benthic and an assortment of Partisan [[soldier]]s met with the [[Gigoran]] [[mercenary]] [[Moroff]], who brought Rook to them. After Benthic briefly interrogated Rook, he ordered his men to put a bag over his head and bring him to Gerrera for further interrogation. They brought Rook for Gerrera and Benthic gave Gerrera Rook's [[Identichip|identicard]] and [[Data rod|datastick]] with the message from Erso which he said he found in Rook's boots, which Rook denied.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

Shortly thereafter, Benthic led several soldiers to [[Battle on Jedha|ambush]] an Imperial [[Kyber crystal|kyber]] [[transport]]. During the intense firefight in [[Jedha City]], [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], who had traveled to Jedha with [[Jyn Erso]] seeking out Gerrera, killed one of the Partisans in self-defense to protect Erso, which Benthic saw. Soon after, the Imperial transport was defeated. Benthic took the pair for hostiles and ordered his troops to contain them, along with [[Baze Malbus]] and [[Chirrut Îmwe]]. They took them back to the [[Catacombs of Cadera]], and Benthic gave Jyn to Gerrera while the rest were put in cells inside the catacombs. During this time, Imperial [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] ordered the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] to [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroy Jedha City]]. The shock wave from the destruction caused the Partisans' nearby hideout to collapse into rubble. During this time, Benthic rushed outside and quickly evacuated his fellow Partisans into escape [[shuttle]]s before personally escaping from the base.<ref name="Rogue One" /> During the destruction his brother Edrio was killed.<ref name="Edrio Two Tubes" />

===Galactic Civil War===
{{Quote|You asked the Partisans to join you. Do not be surprised when they act like the Partisans.|translated=1|Benthic, to Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Star Wars (2015) 64">[[Star Wars (2015) 64|''Star Wars'' (2015) 64]]</ref>}}
[[File:BenthicTwoTubes-CavernAngelsMarksman-UT-600U-wing.png|thumb|250px|Benthic piloting his U-wing and deploying Partisans into action]]
Following the [[Battle of Yavin|Battle]] of [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]], Benthic led the surviving Partisans in continuing the fight against the Empire on Jedha. Benthic was later [[Battle for Jedha|approached]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] representatives [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[Luke Skywalker]], and [[Captain]] [[Han Solo]]. At first, Benthic thought they should kill them.<ref name="Star Wars 38">[[Star Wars (2015) 38|''Star Wars'' (2015) 38]]</ref> However, Organa offered the Alliance's continued support of the Partisans on Jedha. He was told that the Empire was going to continue taking kyber [[crystal]]s from Jedha. Despite his doubts, Benthic accepted Organa's offer and discussed the arrangement.<ref name="Star Wars 39" />

At [[Ai-jed]], Benthic was joined by Skywalker and saw the Empire's latest [[Drill citadel|defilement]] on Jedha. Benthic told Skywalker and Solo that they would destroy it.<ref name="Star Wars 39" />

Sometime later, Organa recruited the help of Benthic and his Partisans for a [[Mission to Shu-Torun (Rebel Alliance)|mission]] to [[Shu-Torun]]. The mission, led by Organa, was to deal a catastrophic blow to Shu-Torun's economy. Benthic agreed to join based on the fact that he had an opportunity to destroy an Imperial world, not concerning himself with Organa's aspirations. When Benthic and his crew arrived on-world, they quickly made a plan to annihilate the planet&mdash;a destruction greater than Jedha's. Benthic learned that if he destroyed the [[Spike (Shu-Torun)|Spike]], the whole planet and everyone on it would go with it. With the forced help of [[R2-D2]] and Skywalker subdued, the Spike was ready to be blown.

[[File:Salobea-2015StarWars67.jpg|thumb|258px|Benthic and the partisans were dropped off at Salobea.|left]]
As fate would have it, Organa rushed in and persuaded Benthic not to destroy the Spike, saying that wasn't the "dream" of the Partisans&mdash;an ideal originating with Saw Gerrera. Benthic agreed and they escaped Shu-Torun aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. Benthic requested that he and that Partisans be dropped off at [[Salobea]], which he described as "something else for the Partisans." Once there, Solo recounted the time he met Benthic when he was a member of the Cloud Riders and told the leader of the Partisans that he does not have to be anything he does not want to be, especially with the right motivation. Benthic heeded the words and implied that he would change.<ref name="Star Wars 67" />

==Skills and abilities==
Due to his [[Tognath]] physiology, he was resistant to pain and interrogation. He is also a skilled marksman, skilled in ground combat, and a capable leader, serving originally as the Partisan's second-in-command and informal rebel lieutenant despite holding no formal ranking<ref name="Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> before serving as the leader following Gerrera's death.<ref name="Star Wars 39" /> Like other Partisans and Gerrera, Benthic holds no moral qualms in employing extreme methods and tactics.<ref name="Star Wars 67" />

==Equipment==
While he was part of the Cloud-Riders, Benthic wore a reflecting mask with<ref name="Solo" /> breathing hoses made from pressure-tight corrugated metal.<ref name="TOG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Benthic "Two Tubes" first appeared in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', where he was played by [[Aidan Cook]],<ref name="Rogue One" /> while his eggmate Edrio was portrayed by [[Paul Kasey]],<ref name="Kasey">{{ToppsCite|set=2016 Topps Star Wars: Rogue One Series 1|cardname=Paul Kasey as Edrio Two Tubes|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/142661/cid/11209460/2016-Topps-Star-Wars-Rogue-One-Series-1---Autographs-NNO-Paul-Kasey}}</ref> and was voiced by [[David Acord]].<ref name="Rebels Trivia">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/in-the-name-of-the-rebellion-trivia-gallery|text="In the Name of the Rebellion" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref> His name and species were first revealed on the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:Facebook|Facebook]] page on [[July 20]], 2016, accompanying Edrio's reveal during that year's [[Wikipedia:San Diego Comic-Con|San Diego Comic-Con]].<ref name="Edrio Reveal">{{Facebook|username=StarWars|url=StarWars/posts/1212534632131469|name=Star Wars|date=2016-07-20|quote=From the Lucasfilm Pavilion at San Diego Comic-Con (2913), meet Rogue One’s Edrio Two Tubes: Edrio Two Tubes is a mercenary pilot who flies alongside his eggmate, Benthic. The two share the nickname derived from the breathing apparatus that allows their Tognath physiology to process oxygen atmospheres. Edrio’s home world Yar Togna was conquered and occupied by the Empire, forcing him to flee as a refugee. Desiring to strike back at the Empire, Edrio and Benthic have allied with Saw Gerrera.|archivefile=File:Edrio_reveal.png}}</ref> Concept art of one of the two "Tubes" for the film was done by [[Adam Brockbank]] and Fisher with the production codes "Two Tubes" and "Saw's Pilot".<ref name="ArtOfRO">''[[The Art of Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

One of the two Tognaths later appeared in the Anthology film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Solo" /> released on [[May 25]], [[2018]],<ref name="Film Announced">{{SW|url=news/christopher-miller-and-phil-lord-to-helm-han-solo-anthology-film|text=Christopher Miller and Phil Lord to Helm Han Solo Anthology Film|nolive=1}}</ref> again portrayed by Cook<ref name="Dee Tails Tweet">{{Twitter|deetails|status/1018874896699609090|[[Dee Tails]]|date=2018-07-16|quote=Yeah, Regineer Teed, is me. Gelan Yees, Dilu Miah. Auromae Iselo, Derek Arnold. Tubes, Aiden Cook aka Two Tubes. Silvasu Fi, Stephanie Silva. Faddera Rabar, Barbara Fadden. Other than Warrick (Weazel) and Erin (Enfys) the others are stuntmen and women.|archivedate=20181030063907}}</ref> who remained uncredited for the role,<ref name="Solo" /> and was first shown in the teaser trailer released on [[February 5]] of that year.<ref name="Teaser">{{SWYouTube|dNW0B0HsvVs|Solo: A Star Wars Story Official Teaser|sw_url=video/official-teaser-solo-a-star-wars-story}}</ref> However, it was initially unclear which of the two eggmates appeared in the film as the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' identified him only as "Tubes".<ref name="TOG" /> Co-writer [[Jon Kasdan]] initially identified the character as Edrio in a [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Tweet]] on [[June 24]], 2018,<ref name="Kasdan 1">{{Twitter|username=JonKasdan|url=1011071249668296706|name=[[Jon Kasdan]]|date=2018-06-24|quote=#RogueOne fan too. Hence critical appearance by Edrio "Two Tubes" who had dialogue which was ultimately cut. Wanted to establish the connection between Enfys and Saw, and the slowly developing rebellion across the galaxy.|archivedate=20191125041806|nolive=1}}</ref> but later clarified in early July after being asked by a fan that he had requested Edrio, but may have actually gotten Benthic instead.<ref name="Kasdan 2">{{Twitter|username=JonKasdan|url=1017135101048651783|name=[[Jon Kasdan]]|quote=I REQUESTED Edrio but MAY have actually gotten Bethnic. You never can tell with those Tognath eggmates, and since nobody on our crew SPOKE Tognath we couldn’t ask ‘im… He's probably on twitter thought (sic!) and will read this and clarify|nobackup=1|nolive=1}}</ref><ref name="Zirohu">{{Wordpress|subdomain=zirohu|name=Ziro.hu|url=2018/07/12/jon-kasdan-doesnt-know-which-tubes-is-in-the-solo-movie|text=Jon Kasdan doesn't know which Tubes is in the Solo movie|date=2018-07-12|archivedate=20260323193149}}</ref> The film's [[Solo: A Star Wars Story: A Junior Novel|junior novel adaptation]], written by [[Joe Schreiber]]<ref name="Solo Junior">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> and published on [[September 4]], [[2018]],<ref name="Disney">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/solo-a-star-wars-story-junior-novel|text=Solo: A Star Wars Story Junior Novel|nolive=1}}</ref> then later confirmed that it was Benthic who appeared in the film.<ref name="Solo Junior" /> Kasdan stated that he originally wanted the mercenary [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] for the role of Enfys Nest's second in command, who would ultimately abandon her in the end of the film. He said that he fought long and hard for the inclusion, but was overruled.<ref name="Commentary 3">{{Twitter|JonKasdan|status/1040529511513616385|[[Jon Kasdan]]|date=2018-9-14|quote=continued|archivefile=File:Jon-Kasdan-SoloFacts-September-14-2018.png|archivedate=20181002054217|nolive=1}} {{C|post 3/4}}</ref> Kasdan wanted to include Two Tubes in ''Solo'' because he was a fan of ''Rogue One'' and wanted to establish a connection between Nest and Saw Gerrera and show the slowly developing rebellion. Tubes also originally had dialogue in ''Solo'', which was ultimately cut.<ref name="Kasdan 1" />

Benthic later also re-appeared in the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', first appearing in the [[Andor Season 1|season one]] episode "[[Narkina 5 (episode)|Narkina 5]]" in [[2022]], again portrayed by Cook.<ref name="Narkina 5" /> His identity was later confirmed by the episode's guide on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Narkina 5 EG">{{SW|url=series/andor/andor-episode-8-narkina-5|text="Narkina 5" Episode Guide &#124; Andor}}</ref>

He was also pictured in early concept art for the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' done by [[Prog Wang]].<ref name="ArtOfJFO">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>
