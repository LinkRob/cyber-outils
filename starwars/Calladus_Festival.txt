The '''Calladus Festival''' was a celebration that took place during the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]]. During one instance of the festival, [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]] disguised himself as an Imperial guard and swindled many festival-goers, including the bulk of the [[Zakku Rain Cartel]], out of many of their [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Lando" />
