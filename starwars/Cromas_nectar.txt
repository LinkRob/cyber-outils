'''Cromas nectar''' was a [[Foodstuff|beverage]] that was [[Diet|potable]] by the [[Chiss]] and [[Agbui]] [[species]].<ref name="Greater Good" /> By [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' take place in [[18 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. As the appearance of cromas [[nectar]] occurs in a flashback set prior to ''Greater Good''{{'s}} main events, it must have appeared by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> while traveling aboard the [[Haplif's starship|starship]] of the [[Agbui]] agent [[Haplif]], the Chiss [[Coduyo'po'nekri]], [[core name]] "Yoponek," and his [[Marriage|betrothed]] [[Yomie]] drank cromas [[nectar]]. Yoponek nearly choked on his beverage while trying to drink, talk, and laugh all at once. The Agbui [[Shimkif]] also drank the nectar after the two Chiss had gone to [[sleep]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cromas nectar appeared in the flashbacks of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'', the second installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Greater Good" />
