'''C.B. Lee''' (born [[November 7]], [[1987]]) is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] [[Wikipedia:Author|author]] of [[Wikipedia:Chinese people|Chinese]]-[[Wikipedia:Vietnamese people|Vietnamese]] descent. They wrote the short story "[[A Good Kiss]]," which tells the story of the background character [[Chase Wilsorr]], who appears in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. The story was published in the anthology novel ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]'' on [[November 10]], [[2020]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|It was really important to me&mdash;being queer myself and loving ''Star Wars'' since I was a kid. It's so powerful to see yourself in these stories. For a long time growing up I didn't see anybody who was like me, who looked like me, who felt like me portrayed in a positive way.|C.B. Lee, regarding Chase Wilsorr's sexual orientation|<ref name="Tatooine Times" />}}
[[File:CB_Cosplay.jpg|thumb|200px|left|C.B. Lee with a copy of ''From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back'']]
C.B. Lee, an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] [[Wikipedia:Author|author]] of [[Wikipedia:Chinese people|Chinese]]-[[Wikipedia:Vietnamese people|Vietnamese]] heritage,<ref name="Press Kit" /> was born on [[November 7|November 7th]]<ref name="TwitterBirthdate" /> [[1987]].<ref name="TwitterAge" /> They<ref name="Twitter" /> were recruited by the [[Del Rey]] editor [[Tom Hoeler]] via their agent, Thao Le, to write a short story for ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]'', an anthology book based on the events of<ref name="Tatooine Times" /> the [[1980]] film<ref name="SWReveal">{{SW|url=news/empire-at-40-from-a-certain-point-of-view-the-empire-strikes-back-authors-revealed|text=''Empire'' at 40 &#124; ''From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back'' Authors Revealed}}</ref> [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. The author felt very excited, as they had been a ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan since childhood,<ref name="Tatooine Times" /> and later even cosplayed for conventions as the [[Resistance]] member [[Rose Tico]] and the [[pilot]] [[Jessika Pava]].<ref name="TwitterCosplay">{{Twitter|username=cblee_cblee|url=status/1037169451630649345|name=C.B. Lee|archiveurl=https://archive.md/GyyoV}}</ref><ref name="Jessika">{{Twitter|username=cblee_cblee|url=status/1021153976098566144|name=C.B. Lee|archiveurl=https://archive.md/oSuBt}}</ref> They also told the podcast [https://open.spotify.com/show/0WPRWweJ6sBemfFaUmqLGw Everything is Canon] that the ''Star Wars'' universe is the biggest sandbox to work with for a creative mind, and complimented the franchise by describing it as a rich and deep world to dive into. Lee claimed it to be a big part of their life, and the [[Original trilogy|original]] and [[Sequel trilogy|sequel]] trilogies to be their favorites.<ref name="EverythingCanon">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cinelinx.com/off-beat/shows/everything-is-canon-minecraft-the-shipwreck|text=Everything is Canon - interview with C.B. Lee|archiveurl=https://archive.md/ltLob}}</ref>

While Lee's involvement with the project was announced on [[June 15]], [[2020]] via a [[StarWars.com]] article,<ref name="SWReveal" /> they posted about their contribution [[June 11|four days]] prior.<ref name="TwitterReveal">{{Twitter|username=cblee_cblee|url=status/1271249961347190784|name=C.B. Lee|archiveurl=https://archive.md/Z9GW2}}</ref> They, and the other authors who contributed to the book, donated all of their earnings on it to the charity foundation [https://firstbook.org/ First Book].<ref name="SWReveal" /> Lee's story was titled "A Good Kiss" and was published in ''From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back''<ref name="FACPOVESB">{{StoryCite|story=A Good Kiss|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> later that year, on [[November 10]].<ref name="SWReveal" /> 

[[File:Chase_Wilsorr.png|thumb|200px|right|Chase Wilsorr, the main character of "A Good Kiss"]]
The author's first idea for a story involved a background character seen passing<ref name="Tatooine Times" /> [[Captain]] [[Han Solo]] and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="FACPOVESB" /> The author developed the character into the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Chase Wilsorr]] as the story's protagonist. Lee wrote Wilsorr as an "awkward disaster" and used "Be Your Best Self" as the moral of the story. As a [[Wikipedia:Queer|queer]] author, they based Wilsorr's sexual orientation on their own, as they felt it was powerful and validating to see someone like themselves in a story, especially as they had not seen other queer individuals portrayed in a positive way when they grew up.<ref name="Tatooine Times" />

Lee felt Wilsorr was a relatable character because he strove to be a hero but was not very charismatic nor skilled with [[weapon]]s. They remarked that he must have failed his training multiple times, and that pushed him to try and better himself and read self-help [[book]]s. The author added that the motivational quotes Wilsorr is heard reciting in the story are equivalent to a person in the real world trying to find their place in the universe that way.<ref name="AuthorCommentary">{{Spotify|url=show/4tzqEFcW0c2w3TMfSvqpwk|text="A Good Kiss" by C.B. Lee}}</ref> They also said that his story is about the extraordinary in the ordinary, and that Chase was just trying to do his job through it all,<ref name="Book Preview">{{Instagram|username=cblee_cblee|url=p/CGENz-4gs_B|name=C.B. Lee|archiveurl=https://archive.md/S7mNx}}</ref> even though he might not be remembered for it in the long run. Lee said that from their opinion, ''Star Wars''{{'s}} morale is that everyone can be a hero, even in small ways. Every contribution to a larger and good cause is remarkable, and it takes courage to make a small difference, and courage, unlike superpowers, is something that is within all of us.<ref name="EverythingCanon" />
