The '''EGL power droid''' was a model of [[power droid]]<ref name="Kids">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Droid in Star Wars|video=UQg6aX7lqTA|nolive=1}}</ref> with a long cylindrical body atop two short [[Limb|legs]]. The [[Manumission|manumitted]] EGL power [[droid]] [[EGL-21]] dwelt in [[Niima Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]<ref name="TFA" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], which includes EGL-21's appearance, to [[34 ABY]].</ref>
