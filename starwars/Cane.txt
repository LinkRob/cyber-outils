A '''cane''', also known as a '''walking stick''', was a device used to assist an individual in walking.<ref name="Temptation of the Force">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref>

Many individuals in the galaxy owned canes, including [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], who owned a [[Emperor Palpatine's cane|black walking cane]],<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]],<ref name="TESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> who owned a [[gimer stick]] given to him by the [[Wookiee]]s,<ref name="CVD New Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> and [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

Some [[Force-sensitive|Force-users]] incorporated [[Sabercane|detachable lightsabers]] into their canes, such as elderly Jedi Master [[Tera Sinube]] and former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]].<ref name="SW VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]] used a [[Lando's cane|cane]], the head of which was carved in the shape of [[Cloud City]] and included his name as an inscription.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
