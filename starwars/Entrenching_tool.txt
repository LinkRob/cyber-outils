The '''entrenching tool''' was a type of shovel used for digging trenches, the creation of campsites, and the establishment of defensive structures. It was versatile and could be used as a [[melee weapon]], making it extremely valuable in wilderness areas.<ref name="Forged in Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
The entrenching tool was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Forged in Battle]]'',<ref name="Forged in Battle" /> a [[2016]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Soldier On!">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2016/8/17/soldier-on-1|text=Soldier On!}}</ref> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[Galaxy at War]]'', a [[2009]] hardcover supplement for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game Saga Edition Core Rulebook]]'' published by [[Wizards of the Coast]].<ref name="Galaxy at War">''[[Galaxy at War]]''</ref>
