The '''Carz system''' was located in the [[Svivreni sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="TrailingSectors" /> The [[star system]] was situated in grid square O-20 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Carz system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Carz system/Legends|Carz system]] first appeared in "[[Tales of the Smoking Blaster|Tales of the ''Smoking Blaster'']]," a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure written by [[Bill Smith]] and included in ''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Handbook]]'', a [[1993]] supplement published by [[West End Games]] for use with ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. The adventure also featured the first visual depiction of the [[Star system/Legends|system]], drawn by [[Paul Daly]].<ref name="TSB">{{WEGCite|story=Tales of the Smoking Blaster|book=Star Wars Gamemaster Handbook|stext=Tales of the ''Smoking Blaster''}}</ref>
