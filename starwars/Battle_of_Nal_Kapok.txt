The '''Battle of Nal Kapok''' took place on the [[planet]] [[Nal Kapok]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and the [[Galactic Republic]]. [[R0-GR]], a [[B1-series battle droid]] veteran of the Clone Wars, considered this battle the worst battle he fought in.<ref name="Droidography" />
