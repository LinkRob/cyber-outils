'''Ethark''' was a tropical [[Planet/Legends|planet]] in the [[Epsilon 69.02 system]]. The surface consisted of vast steppes of [[Photosynthesis/Legends|photosynthetic]] [[Plant/Legends|plants]], [[Forest/Legends|forests]], and [[Mineral/Legends|mineral]] reliefs. An estimated two-thirds of the surface was covered in forests.<ref name="LHI" />
