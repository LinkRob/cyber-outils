'''Baroo''' was a location with artificially created [[beach]] resorts during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> a [[hologram]] on a [[skyscraper]] on<ref name="Convergence" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] advertised unmissable vacation packages to Baroo's beaches, which was seen by [[Axel Greylark]], the [[Greylark family|son]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Kyong Greylark]].<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
Baroo was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'', a [[2022]] written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Convergence" />
