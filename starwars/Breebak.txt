'''Breebak''' was a [[Bonbrak]] who worked alongside the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] in a [[Jedi garage|garage]] on the [[planet]] [[Valo]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. When Jomaram investigated a fault in the local [[Crashpoint Tower|communications tower]] with his [[droid]], [[V-18]], shortly before the opening of the [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]], Breebak and the fellow Bonbrak [[Tip]] stowed away. Jomaram discovered that [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] had sabotaged the tower, and the two Bonbraks emerged after V-18 was damaged by the raiders, repairing the droid. Before Jomaram and the group could return to [[Lonisa City]] and spread word of the Nihil presence, they were arrested by the [[Valo Security Force]]. Valo was [[Valo Atrocity|attacked]] by the Nihil, and Jomaram and his companions, joined by Padawan [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]] and the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]], set out to repair the communications tower.<ref name="Race to Crashpoint Tower" /> Later, Breebak accompanied Jomaram to [[Corellia]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref>
