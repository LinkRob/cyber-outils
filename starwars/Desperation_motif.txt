A '''desperation motif''' was composed by [[John Williams]] for the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] and its [[Musical score|score]]. Representing the struggles and desperation of the film's protagonists, the [[Wikipedia:Leitmotif|leitmotif]] is rhythmically unstable. It is developed from [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''The Force Awakens''{{'s}}]] [[tension motif]], and in [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''The Rise of Skywalker'']], the motif is in turn developed into the "[[Anthem of Evil]]." The tension motif itself shares similarities with the motif representing the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] featured in the [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Attack of the Clones'']] track "[[The Arena (musical theme)|The Arena]]."
