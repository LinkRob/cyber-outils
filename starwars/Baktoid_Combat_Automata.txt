'''Baktoid Combat Automata''', abbreviated '''BCA''',<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> was a manufacturer of [[battle droid]]s, many of which saw use in the [[Separatist Droid Army]].<ref name="USW" /> It was a sister company to [[Baktoid Armor Workshop]], both of which were owned by the [[Techno Union]].<ref name="Build R2 45" /> Baktoid Combat became an associated<ref name="Blasters/Firearms">{{EncyclopediaCite|Blasters and Ranged Weapons}}</ref> sister company to Baktoid Armor. Baktoid Combat also had its own subsidiary in [[Baktoid Fleet Ordnance]].<ref name="Build R2 45" />

Baktoid's factories on [[Geonosis]] and other [[planet]]s built countless battle [[droid]]s for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="USW" /> After the Empire's takeover of Combat Automata, certain executives and leading [[engineer]]s formed a corporation of their own, [[Baktoid Industrial Systems]], which the Empire later annexed as well.<ref name="Forged in Battle">''[[Forged in Battle]]''</ref>
