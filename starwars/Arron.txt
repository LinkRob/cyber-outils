'''Arron''' was a [[human]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. The [[pilot]] took part in a mission to hunt down the [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] ''[[Hellion's Dare]]'', but was [[Death|killed]] in the [[Oridol Cluster]] by the [[time]] [[fighter wing]]'s forces were able to eliminate their target. After taking charge of the 204th, [[Major]] [[Soran Keize]] wished to have Arron replace another fallen pilot, but instead learned of the former pilot's death.

==Biography==
During the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> the [[human]]<ref name="USW" /> [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] Arron served in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> Some [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Alphabet">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas>''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Arron took part in a mission<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> to hunt down the [[New Republic]] [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] ''[[Hellion's Dare]]'' as a part of an effort coordinated by [[Colonel]] [[Shakara Nuress]], the 204th's [[commanding officer]], to maintain the secrecy of the 204th's new base of operations in the [[Pandem Nai system]].<ref name="Alphabet" />

Part of two [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]] [[squadron]]s aboard the [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser carrier]] ''[[Aerie]]'', numbering at thirty-two, Arron was with the attack force when it [[Skirmish at Jiruus|drove]] the ''Dare'' and it's two escort squadrons out of the [[Jiruus system]] and [[Attack on the Hellion's Dare|chased]] the New Republic [[New Republic Defense Fleet|forces]] through the [[Oridol Cluster]]. The 204th forces were able to almost completely eliminate the enemy,<ref name="Alphabet" /> but not before Arron was [[Death|killed]]. After becoming the fighter wing's new commanding offer, [[Major]] [[Soran Keize]] suggested having Arron replace another fallen [[pilot]] named [[Draige]] as second-in-command of [[Squadron Four (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Four]], but instead learned of Arron's death, which the major partly blamed on his absence from the 204th at the [[time]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
