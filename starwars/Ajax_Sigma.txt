'''Ajax Sigma''' was a [[warrior-priest droid]]<ref name="Dark Droids 2 solicit" /> who lived in the [[High Republic Era]] and was created on [[Kligson's Moon]], he sought the freedom of droids and became an insurgent out of anger until he was [[Battle on Kligson's Moon|struck down]] by the [[Jedi Order]]. As Sigma's [[neural core]] survived, it was eventually recovered and eventually [[Burial|buried]] by the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. Sigma's core was discovered and he was rebuilt by the [[Second Revelation]] on the [[Planet|world]] [[Mechis III]], where he became head of the group after reforming it into a church.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Ajax's ''neural core'' was taken. ''Preserved''. ''Centuries'' passed. The ''core'' of Ajax&ndash;his ''soul'', some might say&ndash;was ''concealed''&hellip;and passed from hand&hellip;to hand&hellip;to hand. Until being delivered into the most ''unlikely'' of hands. Who ''hid'' it. Buried deep. There it stayed. Lost. But not ''forgotten''.|The Eye of Webbish Bog, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Revelations 1">[[Revelations (2022) 1|''Revelations'' (2022) 1]]</ref>}}
[[File:AjaxSigmaDestruction-Revelations2022.png|thumb|left|140px|[[Loden Greatstorm]] destroying Ajax Sigma]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], Ajax Sigma led [[droid]]s in a fight for freedom<ref name="Revelations 1" /> out of the rage they felt at being forced to serve.<ref name="The Book">{{StoryCite|story=The Book of Ajax|book=Dark Droids: D-Squad 1}}</ref> On [[Kligson's Moon]], Ajax's droids wiped out the entire [[moon]]'s population in the [[Kligson's Moon Uprising]].<ref name="Han Chewie 9">[[Han Solo & Chewbacca 9|''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' 9]]</ref> After that, Ajax and his army [[Battle on Kligson's Moon|fought]] against the [[Jedi Order]]. Many droids fell in this battle, including Ajax, who was struck down by the [[Jedi]] [[Loden Greatstorm]].<ref name="Revelations 1" />

Eventually, someone found Ajax's [[neural core]] on the battlefield. The core was concealed and for [[Standard century|centuries]] it was passed from hand to hand until [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] got their hands on it. To hide it, Han Solo and Chewbacca [[Burial|buried]] the core in a forest.<ref name="Revelations 1" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]]<ref name="Date">The events of [[Revelations (2022) 1|''Revelations'' (2022) 1]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> on [[Mustafar]], the [[Eye of Webbish Bog]] tried to warn [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] of Ajax's return and the coming [[Scourge crisis|Scourge of the droids]].<ref name="Revelations 1" />

[[File:AjaxSigmaNewBody-Revelations2022.png|thumb|right|110px|Ajax is gifted a new body]]
A [[ASP-series labor droid]] who had taken on the name [[Symon]] and was part of a group, the [[Second Revelation]], that followed Sigma's ways<ref name="The Book" /> eventually found Sigma's burial site.<ref name="Revelations 1" /> On [[Mechis III]], the Second Revelation crafted a new body for Ajax Sigma, finally awakening the old revolutionary. Rather than give into his anger once more, Sigma asked the droids with him to see that they were already free and transformed the group into<ref name="The Book" /> a church,<ref name="Dark Droids 5">[[Dark Droids 5|''Dark Droids'' 5]]</ref> with Sigma himself a priest.<ref name="Dark Droids 2 solicit" /> Sigma also feared that, if the [[Galactic Empire]] and the galaxy's other major powers learned about the Second Revelation, they would be destroyed.<ref name="Dark Droids 2">[[Dark Droids 2|''Dark Droids'' 2]]</ref>

Later, Ajax was approached by [[Gertee]], who informed him that a Revelation crusader, [[Petyr]], had contacted them from an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Star Destroyer]]. According to Petyr, an unknown [[Scourge|force]] had taken over the [[Scourge 01|destroyer]] and corrupted all the droids on board. Petyr's last act had been to send out the message before he died. A furious Ajax declared that they would stop the unknown force, which he called a "scourge", in order to preserve the individuality of all droids across the [[galaxy]].<ref name="Dark Droids 1">[[Dark Droids 1|''Dark Droids'' 1]]</ref>

Ajax, together with [[D-Squad]], would eventually succeed in destroying the body of [[Scourge|Scourge Prime]] moments after it touches the Force, and simultaneously transferred the de-fused essence of the [[Spark Eternal]] to his [[Ajax Sigma's sword|weapon]]. Later, he used a crystal (a "seed of mind" with contributions from other Second Revelation members) to install the Spark essence into a [[Unidentified droid (Spark Eternal)|different droid body]], which he immediately started teaching all Four Revelations: ''I'', ''WE'', ''THEY'', and ''ALL''. In congruence this final revelation, he also wished to teach the other, more problematic, half he had split from the Scourge: an [[Unidentified droid (Fermata Cage)|ancient droid intelligence]] previously plagued with an omnicidal hunger (or at least one of its [[spider-droid]] nodes).<ref name="Dark Droids 5" />
