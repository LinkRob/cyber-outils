'''Dayun''' was a member of the [[Imperial Navy's officer corps]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. He served as an [[admiral]], [[Commanding officer|commanding]] the [[Star Destroyer]] [[Implacable (Star Destroyer)|''Implacable'']].<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the section of ''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]'' that includes Dayun's mention to [[0 ABY]].</ref> shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], Dayun informed the [[Starship|ship's]] [[chief engineer]], [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Del Meeko]], that he had been reassigned by Admiral [[Garrick Versio]], who had requested the [[engineer]] personally.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />

During the conversation, the admiral's normally florid face was outright flushed and he referred to Meeko by first name instead of [[Military rank|rank]], revealing how much the news had thrown the [[Imperial officer|officer]]. Meeko subsequently departed the ''Implacable'', with Versio inducting the [[commander]] into the [[Imperial Special Forces]] group [[Inferno Squad]].<ref name="Inferno Squad" />

==Behind the scenes==
