'''''DJ Rangthang''''' was a [[Painting|poster]] advertisement<ref name="Deception" /> for the musical [[DJ (occupation)|DJ]] known as [[Rangthang]],<ref name="Hunt for Ziro">{{TCW|Hunt for Ziro}}</ref> available in [[the galaxy]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. It featured a black, beige and purple [[color]] palette and showed Rangthang against a building backdrop, with an [[Aurebesh]] caption at the top. The poster was put up in such diverse places as [[Trueping's]], a [[cantina]] in [[Coruscant]]'s [[Coruscant Underworld|seedy underworld]];<ref name="Deception" /> by the [[airlock]] in a [[TR-RL3 Terrapin-class bulk tanker|Turtle Tanker]]<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref> commandeered by [[Savage Opress]] on [[Stobar]];<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref> in the [[Pons Ora]] settlement on [[Abafar]];<ref name="Missing">{{TCW|Missing in Action}}</ref> by [[Jackar's home|Jackar Bowmani's apartment]] on Coruscant;<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> and onboard a [[Coruscant train]].<ref name="Catch">{{TCW|To Catch a Jedi}}</ref>

Fifteen [[Standard year|years]] after the Clone Wars ended, the poster was used to decorate the walls of the ''[[Ghost]]''.<ref name="Spark">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
