The '''fifth Emergence''' was the fifth of the [[Emergences]], fallout of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] in which fragments of the [[freighter]] ''[[Legacy Run]]'' shot out of [[hyperspace]] seemingly at random. During the Emergence's aftermath, [[Innamin]] and [[Peeples]] worked together to wire a [[subnode]] that would model the event.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
