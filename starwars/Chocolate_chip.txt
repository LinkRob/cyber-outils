'''Chocolate chip''' was a type of [[Chocolate/Legends|chocolate]] [[Cookie/Legends|cookie]] that was [[Foodstuff/Legends|edible]] by [[Human/Legends|humans]].<ref name="JA:ANC" /> In [[182 BBY]],<ref name="Date Note">{{JediAcademyDates|4}}</ref> [[Coruscant Jedi Academy|Jedi Academy]] [[Jedi Initiate/Legends|students]] [[Victor Starspeeder]] and [[Artemis Oophanoe]] read [[Book/Legends|books]] on the [[Endor/Legends|Forest Moon of Endor]] for a project. While reading, Starspeeder took notes by [[Writing system|writing]] down his favorite cookies, including Chocolate chip, the [[Wookiee-ookiee|Wookiee cookie]], [[Bantha Barks]], and [[Choco droids]].<ref name="JA:ANC" />
