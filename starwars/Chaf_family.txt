The '''Chaf family''' was a [[Ruling Families|ruling family]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nation that opposed the [[Mitth family]] but was not enthusiastic about the [[Irizi family]], which sought to ally itself with the Chaf.<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref> A [[Unidentified Chaf task force|Chaf task force]] was determined to capture a massive [[Unidentified Lesser Space starship|starship]] hailing from [[Lesser Space]], the region of [[Realspace|space]] beyond the [[Unknown Regions]]. However, Mitth [[Syndic]] [[Mitth'ras'safis|Thrass]] and a [[Unidentified human female (Lesser Space)|human female]] [[Thief|stole]] the [[starship]] and crashed it. [[Senior Commander]] [[Chaf'pri'uhme|Afpriuh]] was a member of the Chaf.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

Around [[18 BBY]],<ref name="LSD">The main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' take place around [[18 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> after the [[Unidentified Chaf patriarch|Chaf patriarch]] allied his family with the coordinator of the [[Grysk]] warlike [[species]] "[[Jixtus]]," he received several [[Warship|Grysk warships]] while the patriarch supplied [[fuel]], supplies and a safe harbor to Jixtus's vessels. The warships left their harbor for the Chiss [[planet]] [[Schesa]], where all Grysk warships of Jixtus's [[fleet]] prepared to [[Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand|destroy]] [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] [[Thrawn's fleet|fleet]] on the planet [[Sunrise]]. Unfortunately for Jixtus&mdash;and the Chaf&mdash;all Grysk warships were destroyed by Thrawn's forces, leading to an investigation of the Chaf patriarch's dealings with Jixtus which resulted in him stepping down.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Chaf family was first mentioned in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first book of [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|''The Ascendancy Trilogy'']] by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> With the appearance of Senior Commander Afpriuh, the family's members first appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'', the second book of ''The Ascendancy Trilogy''.<ref name="Greater Good" /> The [[Chaf family/Legends|Chaf family]]  was originally introduced in Zahn's [[2004]] novel ''[[Survivor's Quest]]'', now part of the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' continuity.<ref name="SQ">''[[Survivor's Quest]]''</ref>
