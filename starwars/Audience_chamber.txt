The '''audience chamber''' was a location within the headquarters of the [[Gillanium Syndicate]], where [[the Underlord]] met with visitors. During the [[Imperial Era]], a [[Skirmish with the Gillanium Syndicate|skirmish]] broke out in the audience chamber after a group of criminals led by [[Kaluub Akjee]], who had stolen the head of the [[assassin droid]] and [[bounty hunter]] [[IG-88B|IG-88]], attempted to claim several [[Bounty|bounties]] the [[droid]] possessed, which, unknown to the criminals, included the Underlord's son [[Venga Liss]]. During the skirmish, IG-88 called his [[starship]], [[IG-2000]], bringing himself a replacement body. After reattaching his head, IG-88 destroyed the remains of the Gillanium Syndicate.<ref name="The Long Game" />
