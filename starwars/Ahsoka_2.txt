'''''Ahsoka'' 2''' is the second issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries [[Star Wars: Ahsoka (comic series)|''Star Wars: Ahsoka'' comic adaptation]]. It is an adaptation of the [[Part Two: Toil and Trouble|second episode]] of the television series [[Star Wars: Ahsoka|''Ahsoka'']]. The issue was written by [[Rodney Barnes]], illustrated by [[Georges Jeanty]], [[Karl Story]], and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[August 28]], 2024.

==Publisher's summary==
''TOIL AND TROUBLE! AHSOKA and GENERAL HERA SYNDULLA travel to NEW REPUBLIC shipyards and make an unexpected discovery. The adaptation of the hit Disney+ series continues!''

==Opening crawl==
{{OpeningCrawl
|name=PART TWO: TOIL AND TROUBLE
|text=Padawan. Rebel. Ronin. She has been many things, but above all she has defended peace and justice
throughout a galaxy rife with conflict, losing mentors and friends in the process. She is&hellip;.

Former Jedi AHSOKA TANO found a star map leading to the location of lost IMPERIAL GRAND
ADMIRAL THRAWN--and her friend EZRA BRIDGER, a Jedi who disappeared while fighting Thrawn.
Ahsoka sought out her former apprentice SABINE WREN to unlock the map, but SHIN HAITI, pupil to
the fallen Jedi turned mercenary BAYLAN SKOLL, stole the map and attacked Sabine,
leaving Sabine wounded&hellip;.}}
