An '''emerald grape''' was a type of [[grape]] used to make [[emerald wine]].

==History==
During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], [[Captain]] [[Hiram Zataire]] served [[Imperial Security Bureau|ISB]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] [[emerald wine]] aboard the ''[[Lawbringer]]'', commenting that despite [[Lothal]]'s greenness, its [[grape]]s "were as sour as [[Hutt punch]]." Kallus listened with feigned interest, desiring only to engage in small talk in order to keep the measure of the captain.<ref name="rebellion" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[Emerald wine]] appeared in titles such as ''[[The Courtship of Princess Leia]]''<ref name="courtship">''[[The Courtship of Princess Leia]]''</ref> and [[Scourge (novel)|''Scourge'']],<ref name="scourge">[[Scourge (novel)|''Scourge'']]</ref> which are considered part of the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity and not [[canon]],<ref name="End of EU">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> but the grapes used in making it were only mentioned in the canon novel ''[[The Rebellion Begins]]'', a novelization of ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''.<ref name="rebellion" />
