The '''Attack on Tehar''' was carried out in [[34 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> by the [[First Order]] [[First Order military|military]] under the command of [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], where they [[Death|massacred]] a [[Unidentified village (Tehar)|village]] on the [[planet]] [[Tehar]]. Only two [[Youngling|young]] [[Kel and Eila's family|siblings]], [[Kel]] and [[Eila]], survived the massacre, escaping by stowing away on a [[Freighter|cargo ship]].<ref name="The Children from Tehar" />

Eventually, the children reached the ''[[Colossus]]'', a fueling [[Supertanker fuel depot|station]] on the planet [[Castilon]]. The First Order placed a [[bounty]] on the children and sent [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] to the [[Space station|station]] to [[Hunt for Eila and Kel|locate]] them after receiving word they were seen aboard the ''Colossus''. [[Neeku Vozo]], [[Kazuda Xiono]], and several [[Chelidae]]&mdash;residents aboard the ''Colossus''&mdash;helped the children fake their deaths and fool the First Order into believing it had successfully erased all evidence of the attack.<ref name="The Children from Tehar" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack was first mentioned in "[[The Children from Tehar]],"<ref name="The Children from Tehar" /> the sixth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[November 4]], [[2018]].<ref name="Episode Guide">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> The massacre was first identified as the "Attack on Tehar" in the [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.<ref name="Timelines" />
