'''Flashsticks''' were nonlethal weapons used by the [[Drall constabulary]]. The weapons cost 375 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and emitted a bright flash of light that could blind or stun an opponent. [[Species]] that did not rely on eyesight or that could not see at all were not particularly affected by the weapon. Many users wore protective eye gear to prevent the flashes from disorienting themselves, and some used two of the devices at once.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flashsticks were first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
