'''Combat arachnids''' were [[bantha]]-sized [[spider]]s from the [[planet]] [[Carida]], whose bodies were covered in reflective spines. They had pointed [[Limb|legs]], sharp [[Tooth|fangs]], and razor-sharp barbs on their lower abdomens. They produced chittering sounds with their teeth, but were also capable of roaring. [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]]'s menagerie of monsters included at least one such creature.<ref name="Escape" />
