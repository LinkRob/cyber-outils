'''Beryllium''' was a substance that could be used as [[fuel]] for explosives with a [[Color|white]] hue.<ref name="Sabine" /> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="5 BBY">The mention of beryllium in [[Sabine Wren]]'s [[in-universe]] [[Sabine Wren's journal|journal]] represented by ''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]'' is set between the formation of the [[Spectres]] and the events of ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]'', both of which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[5 BBY]].</ref> [[Sabine Wren]], a member of the anti&ndash;[[Galactic Empire]] [[Spectres|rebel crew]] of the [[VCX-100 light freighter|starship]] ''[[Ghost]]'', noted in<ref name="Sabine" /> her<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Sabine Wren's journal|journal]] beryllium as a possible fuel for creating white explosions.<ref name="Sabine" />

==Behind the scenes==
Beryllium was introduced to the current [[canon]] of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise in ''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]]<ref name="Sabine" /> and published on [[February 3]], [[2015]].<ref name="SabineReleaseDate">{{Amazon|url=Star-Wars-Rebels-Sabine-Sketchbook/dp/0794432891|text=Star Wars Rebels: Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Beryllium/Legends|beryllium]] was an [[Ore/Legends|ore]] mentioned in "[[Alien Encounters (article series)|Alien Encounters]]," a feature written by [[Trevor J. Wilson]] and [[Craig Robert Carey]] and published in the [[August]] [[1997]] [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 14|fourteenth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'' magazine.<ref name="Journal14">{{JournalCite|14|Alien Encounters (article series)}}</ref> In the real world, [[Wikipedia:Beryllium|beryllium]] is a [[Wikipedia:Chemical element|chemical element]] and [[Wikipedia:Metal|metal]],<ref name="BerylliumBritannica">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/science/beryllium|text=Beryllium &#124; Properties, Uses, & Facts|author=The Editors of Encyclopædia Britannica|date=2025-01-31|work=[[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica|''Encyclopædia Britannica'']]|archivedate=20250130123635}}</ref> with beryllium in powder form being used to obtain white color in [[Wikipedia:Fireworks|fireworks]].<ref name="BerylliumFireworks">{{WebCite|url=https://www.k-state.edu/media/newsreleases/jun12/fireworks62712.html|text=The beauty behind the bang: A look at how fireworks produce color|author=Stefan Bossmann|date=2012-06-27|work=[[Wikipedia:Kansas State University|Kansas State University]]|archivedate=20240726041303}}</ref>
