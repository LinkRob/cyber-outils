An '''abandoned military relay tower''' existed on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planetoid]] [[Sídi]]. Built long before the [[Clone Wars]], it had been abandoned by its builders some time prior to the conflict. It was located next to an [[Unidentified inn (Sídi)|inn]] run by the [[human]]s [[Rank]] and [[Leera]], and the two charged guests large sums of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to use the tower to check for transmissions. The tower was locked with a code, but Rank and Leera's daughter [[Bug (Sídi)|Bug]] figured out the code by spying on her father, after which she began to sneak into the tower regularly.<ref name="Bug" />
