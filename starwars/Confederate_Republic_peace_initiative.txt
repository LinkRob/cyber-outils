This '''peace initiative''' was a proposal created by the [[Separatist Senate]] in the hope of achieving a diplomatic end to the [[Clone Wars]]. Certain [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Senator]]s, such as [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], were in favor of the proposal, but a number of factors ultimately resulted in the proposal not being passed and the war continuing, mainly the [[Bombing of Coruscant's central power distribution grid|bombing]] of Coruscant's [[central power distribution grid]].<ref name="Pursuit of Peace" />

==History==
===The initiative===
{{Quote|Surely there's room in the galaxy for Confederate and Republic planets to coexist. I move that we immediately open peace negotiations with Chancellor Palpatine on Coruscant.|Bonteri moves to discuss peace with the Republic|<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" />}}
When [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Gume Saam]] of the [[Techno Union]] brought forth the [[Republic financial reform bill]] to negotiate more funds from the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] to allow for the production of more [[clone trooper]]s, the discussion was tabled when Senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] pleaded with the Senate to begin peace talks with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], starting the peace initiative. When her proposal was met with support from multiple senators, Amidala secretly traveled to [[Raxus Secundus]] to meet with her friend and now-Separatist [[Separatist Senate|Senator]] [[Mina Bonteri]]. Meanwhile, Senator Saam and Senators [[Lott Dod]] of the [[Trade Federation]] and [[Nix Card]] of the Banking Clan began to formulate a way to stop the peace talks in order for their companies to continue profiting from the war.<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" />

Amidala was accompanied by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] as she met with Senator Bonteri.<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" /> Introducing the motion alongside her friend and associate [[Tawni Ames]], the [[governor]] of the [[planet]] [[Desix]],<ref name="The Solitary Clone" /> Bonteri's measure was ultimately supported in the [[Separatist Senate]].<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" />

In secret, [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] planned to sabotage the peace talks, tasking [[General]] [[Grievous]] with orchestrating a [[Bombing of Coruscant's central power distribution grid|bombing]] of Coruscant's [[central power distribution grid]]. [[Infiltrator demolition droid|Demolition droids]] infiltrated the power grid as the initiative was being voted on. When the attack was successful, the Republic Senate overwhelmingly agreed to end the initiative, not willing to deal with the Separatists who seemed to backstab them during peace talks.<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" /> The Separatists, meanwhile, were told that Palpatine had rejected their plea for peace.<ref name="The Solitary Clone" /> When the Republic retaliated with a supposed [[attack on Confederate people]], Dooku formally withdrew the treaty.<ref name="Pursuit of Peace" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|Peace? There was a time I believed in that. So much so, my colleague Mina Bonteri and I put forth a treaty with Separatists and Republic senators alike to end the war. Your Supreme Chancellor rejected it. I realized then, peace was never an option.|Tawni Ames|<ref name="The Solitary Clone" />}}
The failure of the initiative for peace convinced Ames that the Republic never actually intended for the war to end, instead only using it to further its power. With the [[Proclamation of the New Order]] and the Republic's transformation into the [[Galactic Empire]] Tawni's belief into the Separatist cause was reinforced and she refused to acknowledge Desix's new Imperial governor [[Grotton]], instead taking him hostage in [[Desix City]] and demanding that her planet and people be free from Imperial rule. Tawni was eventually [[Death|killed]] by [[genetically defective clone]] [[Crosshair|CT-9904]] "Crosshair" who was sent by [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]], along with [[clone stormtrooper]] [[Clone Commander|commander]] [[Cody|CC-2224]] "Cody" to free Grotton and restore Imperial rule to the planet. Before her death, she recalled the Confederate-Republic peace initiative's failure to Cody, eventually inspiring him to desert the Imperial army and go AWOL.<ref name="The Solitary Clone" />
