'''Bob Vitas''' is best known as the author of the now-defunct The Completely Unofficial Star Wars Encyclopedia.

He was responsible for naming the [[Hamadryas]] [[Species/Legends|species]] through the ''[[What's The Story?]]'' feature of [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]], in the [[Cebann Veekan]] [[Databank (original)|Databank]] entry. Bob Vitas is also credited in the "Author's Acknowledgments" section of [[Daniel Wallace]]'s ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' and ''[[The New Essential Guide to Droids]]'', and he contributed to and edited ''[[The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia]]''. He went by the alias of ''Bubba1227'' on [[StarWars.com]] and was known as {{U|Bobvitas}}<ref>[[User:Bobvitas|Bob Vitas's user page]] on Wookieepedia</ref> on [[Wookieepedia]].

The character [[Vob Bitas]] was named after Bob Vitas.
