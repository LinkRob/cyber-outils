The '''Cellar of Penitence''' was an area affiliated with the [[Church of Infinite Perception]] on the [[planet]] [[Florrum]], where the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] hired the [[Forger (profession)|forger]] [[Bar the Rotter]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cellar of Penitence was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and released in [[2018]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
