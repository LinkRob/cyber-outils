The '''Corridor of the Lost''' was located in the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Imperial Palace|Great Temple]] on<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]]. It was where [[Jedi Knight]]s who had departed from the Order were memorialized, like how<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> [[Jedi Master]]s that had departed the Order by their own choice<ref name="Wayseeker">''[[The Acolyte: Wayseeker]]''</ref>&mdash;known as the [[Lost Twenty|Lost]]&mdash;were commemorated with statues in the [[Jedi temple|Temple's]] [[Jedi Archives]].<ref name="The Rising Storm" />

In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> Jedi Master [[Stellan Gios]] encountered a [[Tholothian]] individual wielding a [[Ty Yorrick's purple-bladed lightsaber|purple-bladed lightsaber]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Valo]]. After being notified of her identity as [[Ty Yorrick]], Gios mentally noted he was not familiar with the name, and that he would check the Order's registry when back on Coruscant. He then noted she had been obviously [[Jedi training|trained]] by a [[Jedi]] and considered she had been a [[Padawan]] that had not been [[Knighting Ceremony|Knighted]], or she would have been memorialized in the Corridor of the Lost. Yorrick, in fact, had been a Padawan before she left the Order.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />
