'''''5-Minute Star Wars Villain Stories''''' is a hardcover title collecting five villainous stories from [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]'s previously published ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories]]''. It was first available in [[September]] [[2017]] exclusively from retailer [[Wikipedia:Kohl's|Kohl's]], which donated 100 percent of the book's net profits to its Kohl's Cares charitable initiative. A companion title, ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Hero Stories]]'', was also available.

==Contents==
*"[[Yoda and the Count]]," written by [[Rebecca L. Schmidt]] and illustrated by [[Pilot Studio]]
*"[[Escape from Darth Vader (5-Minute Star Wars Stories)|Escape from Darth Vader]]," written by [[Brooke Vitale|Brooke Dworkin]] and illustrated by [[Stéphane Roux]]
*"[[The Battle of Hoth (short story)|The Battle of Hoth]]," written by [[Calliope Glass]] and illustrated by Pilot Studio
*"[[Rescue from Jabba's Palace (Star Wars Galactic Adventures)|Rescue from Jabba's Palace]]," written by Brooke Dworkin and illustrated by Pilot Studio
*"[[Mission to Maz|Chaos at the Castle]]," written by [[S.T. Bende]] and illustrated by Pilot Studio
