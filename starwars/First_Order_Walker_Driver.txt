'''First Order Walker Drivers''' were personnel that specialized in operating [[walker]]s such as the [[First Order All Terrain Armored Transport]], [[All Terrain MegaCaliber Six]], and the [[All Terrain Heavy Hauler]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. They participated in the [[Battle of Crait]] and the [[Battle of Batuu]].

The Walker Drivers wore varying [[armor]], but all were similar to [[First Order stormtrooper armor|that]] of [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] and were distinguished by a [[Color|gray]] stripe over their [[helmet]]s. While some wore helmets that simply resembled that of a standard stormtrooper, some had helmets with a reinforced midrib protruding over the head while others donned [[First Order snowtrooper|snowtrooper]] gear.

==Overview==
===Role===
[[File:Hasbro_First_Order_snowtrooper_walker_pilot.jpg|thumb|left|200px|A driver with snowtrooper gear.]]
[[First Order]] Walker Drivers<ref name="LEGO walker">{{LEGOCite|num=75189|name=First Order Heavy Assault Walker}}</ref> were specialists who operated complex vehicles,<ref name="Hasbro">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Last Jedi (toy line)|pack=Battle on Crait|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/Hasbro-Star-Wars/Battle-On-Crait-Star-Wars-The-Last-Jedi-four-pack-Hasbro}}</ref> including the [[All Terrain Heavy Hauler]], the [[All Terrain MegaCaliber Six]],<ref name="TLJ ICS" /> and the [[First Order All Terrain Armored Transport]].<ref name="Resistance" />

The MegaCaliber Six was led by an [[Vehicle commander|officer]] who [[Commanding officer|commanded]] one [[pilot]] and one [[gunner]] [[officer]], both of whom were Walker Drivers who sat at their stations in the [[cockpit]]. Two Walker Drivers who served as weapons [[engineer]]s were stationed in a space within the walker's main body that was near the [[MegaCaliber Six turbolaser cannon]] at the top, where one engineer managed a weapons diagnostics interface. The [[AT-series|All Terrain]] Heavy Hauler was manned by four gunners wearing the armor of Walker Drivers, with two operating targeting interfaces at each side of the vehicle. A Walker Driver pilot and a co-pilot sat at the [[Spacetug|tug]] walker's [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], where they were overseen by a vehicle commander.<ref name="TLJ ICS" /> The cockpit of an All Terrain Armored Transport was occupied by two Walker Drivers.<ref name="Resistance" />

===Equipment===
[[File:FO-AT-AT-Driver.png|thumb|right|170px|A First Order Walker Driver in stormtrooper armor]]
Donning a [[Color|gray]] [[Clothing|jumpsuit]], the walker crew at the [[Battle of Crait]] wore a [[helmet]] that resembled [[First Order stormtrooper armor|that]] of standard First Order [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]]. The right of the helmet's front bore a gray stripe.<ref name="TLJ ICS" /> Some walker drivers wore an armor akin to that of [[First Order snowtrooper|snowtroopers]], with the addition of a gray stripe over the right side of the helmet.<ref name="Hasbro" /> All Terrain Armored Transport walker drivers aboard the ''[[Finalizer]]'' wore a set of armor with a helmet similar to that of a typical stormtrooper's, though it had a reinforced midrib that bore a dark line broadening in width as it came down the forehead.<ref name="Resistance" />

The troopers were also armed with a [[blaster]] for combat. Their weapon could either be a [[F-11D blaster rifle]]<ref name="Hasbro" /> or a [[SE-44C blaster pistol]].<ref name="Hasbro Pulsecon">{{SW|url=news/hasbro-pulsecon-2021|text=Hasbro PulseCon 2021: HasLab Rancor, New Mando Figures, and More Revealed}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:First_Order_walker_pilot.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Walker pilots were stationed aboard AT-M6s during the Battle of Crait.]]
In [[34 ABY]], First Order Walker Drivers manned All Terrain MegaCaliber Sixes and All Terrain Heavy Haulers in the Battle of Crait against the [[Resistance]].<ref name="TLJ ICS" /> The First Order forces won the battle on [[Crait]] under the leadership of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], who gave orders from his [[Kylo Ren's command shuttle|command shuttle]] that were relayed by [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] beside him and [[Lieutenant]] [[Jober Tavson]], a [[Transport Corps]] member who was piloting the [[Upsilon-class command shuttle|shuttle]]. The walkers suffered no casualties.<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

Sometime later, Ren's [[flagship]], the ''Finalizer'', [[Battle of Batuu|captured]] a group of Resistance members over [[Batuu]]. As the Resistance members escaped towards the [[starship]]'s [[escape pod]]s, they encountered a vehicle bay containing All Terrain Armored Transports. Two Walker Drivers within one of the transports spotted the escaping rebels and activated the walker's [[laser cannon]]s. However, the Resistance members evaded the shots and escaped to Batuu safely.<ref name="Resistance" />

==Behind the scenes==
The First Order Walker Driver was first mentioned in the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] set "[[W:c:brickipedia:75189 First Order Heavy Assault Walker|75189 First Order Heavy Assault Walker]]," released in [[2017]] as a tie-in to<ref name="LEGO walker" /> the [[sequel trilogy]] film released later in the same year, [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="TLJ" /> The drivers made their first canonical appearance, however, in the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] ride ''[[Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance]]''.<ref name="Resistance" />
