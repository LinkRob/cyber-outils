'''Centax-3''',<ref name="BMF 26">{{BuildFalconCite|26|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> or '''Centax 3''', and nicknamed "'''The Aunt'''," was the third<ref name="Aphra 35" /> of four<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> [[moon]]s [[orbit]]ing the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. The [[Aloo family]] had a residence there.<ref name="TMCAFOF" />

Shortly after the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]], [[Anandra Milon]], an [[Alderaanian]] resident of Coruscant, asked an [[Unidentified old man (Hangra's Meat Shack)|old man]] running the [[Hangra's Meat Shack]] stall about a "Centax 3 delivery." The [[Gender|man]] did not know what she was talking about, and eventually took pity on her, giving her a small amount of [[Foodstuff|food]].<ref name="OTLD">{{InsiderCite|151|One Thousand Levels Down}}</ref>

The [[Coalition for Progress headquarters|headquarters]] of the [[Coalition for Progress]], the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[propaganda]] division, was located on Centax 3.<ref name="Aphra 35" />

==Behind the scenes==
Centax 3 was first mentioned in [[canon]] in "[[One Thousand Levels Down]]," a short story by [[Alexander Freed]] which was published in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' [[Star Wars Insider 151|151]] in [[2014]].<ref name="OTLD" /> It first appeared in [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 35|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 35]], a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] written by [[Simon Spurrier]], penciled by [[Andrea Broccardo]],<ref name="Aphra 35" /> and published on [[August 21]] [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/71454/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2016_35|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016) #35}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Centax-3/Legends|Centax-3]] was first mentioned in ''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]'', a sourcebook for the [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]] published by [[Wizards of the Coast]] on [[January 3]], [[2003]].<ref name="Coruscant and the Core Worlds">''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]''</ref>
