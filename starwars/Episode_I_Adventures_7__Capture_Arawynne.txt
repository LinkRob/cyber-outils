'''''Episode I Adventures 7: Capture Arawynne''''' was the seventh installment of ''[[Star Wars: Episode I Adventures]]''.

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''[[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba]] the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]] is angry. Princess [[Arawynne]] and the rest of the [[Ghostling]] children are missing. They have been hidden, and Jabba wants them found&hellip;at any cost. He sends four of his most fearsome trackers into the pleasure garden of [[Gardulla Besadii the Elder/Legends|Gardulla]] the Hutt's fortress in order to catch the slaves.''

''But Arawynne and her friends aren't going to be captured without a fight!''
