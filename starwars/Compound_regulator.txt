A '''compound regulator''' was a component used in the [[power core]] of [[BX-series droid commando]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> after [[ND-5]], a BX-series droid, suffered damage while [[Attack on the Kassina Outpost|attacking]] the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Kassina Outpost]] on [[Akiva]] and began experiencing a cascading system failure. After an initial examination, a [[Rodian]] [[droidsmith]] named [[Gedeek Obaz]] determined that ND-5 likely needed a Compound Regulator to address the failure. Unwilling to give up on ND, [[Kay Vess]] and Gedeek took ND to [[Temmin Wexley]]'s [[Temmin's|shop]] in the [[city]] of [[Myrra]] on [[Akiva]]. Temmin lead the group to a tunnel lead from the basement of his shop to a [[Clone Wars]] era [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Abandoned droid factory|droid factory]]. The group eventually stumbled upon a group of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] which agreed to help find a Compound Regulator if Vess helped free some of their people from the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]]. After succeeding in freeing the rebels, Vess returned to the [[Kivo-Base|rebel base]] in time to help Gedeek insert a new regulator into ND-5.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Compound regulators first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' that was published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game it was used as an ship upgrade material to improve the ''[[Trailblazer]]'s'' weapons and shields.<ref name="Outlaws" />
