'''By the light''' was an exclamation, referring to the [[light side of the Force]]. It was used by [[Jedi Order|Jedi]] [[Elzar Mann]], [[Loden Greatstorm]],<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> [[Terec]],<ref name="High Republic 2023 1">[[The High Republic (2023) 1|''The High Republic'' (2023) 1]]</ref> [[Torban Buck]]<ref name="Snackfrog of Doom">{{StoryCite|story=Three Younglings & the Snackfrog of Doom|book=The High Republic Adventures Phase III Annual}}</ref> [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]],<ref name="Jedi Knights 2">[[Jedi Knights 2|''Jedi Knights'' 2]]</ref> and [[Anakin Skywalker]];<ref name="Yoda 8">[[Yoda 8|''Yoda'' 8]]</ref> the miner [[Erika Blythe]]; a trader on the [[Rooted Moon]]; [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[captain]] [[Finial Bright]];<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> [[starship]] [[engineer]] [[Zepher]];<ref name="Squadron">{{YJA|Squadron (episode)}}</ref> and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] member [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="Star Wars 49">[[Star Wars (2020) 49|''Star Wars'' (2020) 49]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The expression is similar to the real-world exclamation "by God."<ref name="By God">{{WebCite|url=https://idioms.thefreedictionary.com/by+God|text=by God|quote=4. An exclamation of shock or surprise. ''By God, you startled me! I didn't even hear you come in.'' 5. An exclamation of satisfaction or enjoyment.
