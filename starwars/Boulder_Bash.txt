'''Boulder Bash''' was a [[hologame]] that simulated the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. The game was accessible via the [[Holo-Arcade]] and was created by the [[Mon Calamari]] child prodigy [[Sprocket]], who was one of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunters]] in the competition.<ref name="Announcement" /> Although Boulder Bash included faithful recreations of the various battlegrounds of the [[Grand Arena]], the game diverted from reality in that all eight Hunters were the [[Wookiee]] juggernaut [[Grozz]]. Additionally, the simulated battles allowed each Wookiee to frequently form and throw giant [[Stone|boulders]] at one another.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Boulder Bash is a limited-time game mode in the [[2024]] competitive arena combat [[Star Wars video games|game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' by [[Zynga]] and [[Lucasfilm Games]]. The game mode was first added on [[January 9]], 2024.<ref name="Announcement" /> In the [[In-universe|real-world]] game, all eight players battle as Grozz in a four-versus-four Squad Brawl to thirty points. Their abilities are restricted to basic melee attacks and Grozz's Boulder Bash Ultimate ability; they lose access to his other abilities and map healing points. Additionally, the Boulder Bash ability is rapidly regenerating.<ref name="Hunters" />
