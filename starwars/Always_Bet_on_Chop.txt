"'''Always Bet on Chop'''" ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]] title "'''Setze immer auf Chop'''") is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] written by [[Jeremy Barlow]], illustrated by [[Bob Molesworth]], and edited by [[Gunther Nickel]]. The story was published in the 23rd issue of the [[Wikipedia:Germany|German]] [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]], which was released on [[September 28]], [[2016]].

In a Twitter message, Barlow suggested a placement between "[[Blood Sisters]]" and "[[Stealth Strike]]."<ref name="SG idea">{{Twitter|Jeremy_Barlow|status/774138895344611329|[[Jeremy Barlow]]|quote=Hi, Eddie! I'd put it between "Blood Sisters" and "Stealth Strike" in season 2. Unless that contradicts something. :)|nolive=1|nobackup=1}}</ref>

==Plot summary==
[[Garazeb Orrelios]] recounts a mission with Chopper that had gone awry. One day, the two are tasked to retrieve precious [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] banking codes from a [[Unidentified gladiatorial arena|sporting arena]] where [[gladiator droid]] fights take place. However, Chopper accidentally deletes the codes while trying to download them from a wall-mounted computer interface. In addition, a fight promoter named [[Blik (Tarsunt)|Blik]] walks in on them. Although Blik does not suspect any wrongdoings, he mistakes Chopper for one of the day's droid contestants, and he urges the two [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] down a corridor.

The rebels become separated. Chopper is taken to the [[Verpine]] [[Effor]], who will prepare him for the fight, and Orrelios must follow Blik so he can "finalize the registration." In the arena, Chopper is pitted against a [[droideka]] nicknamed [[The Unwinder]]. Meanwhile, at the bookie station, Orrelios is exposed when his name does not appear in any of the battledome's databases. Suspicious, the [[Twi'lek]] bookie calls security. Meanwhile, in the arena, Chopper manages to avoid The Unwinder's hail of [[blaster]] bolts, much to the surprise of the entire audience and the two announcers. Back at the bookie stand, Orrelios bonks the heads of the Twi'lek and Blik together, knocking them out so he can escape. Heading toward the arena floor, the [[Lasat]] yells to the [[astromech droid]] that it is time to leave. Chopper, however, retorts that he is having too much fun, which irritates his companion. The spectators in the stands are visibly enjoying the show.

A short distance away, however, a group of [[stormtrooper]]s shows Orrelios' [[Hologram|holographic]] image to Effor, who points toward the arena. At first, the Lasat appears ready to abandon Chopper in the battledome, running for an exit. However, he quickly has second thoughts and turns back. Now cornered by stormtroopers holding blasters, Orrelios is determined not to go down without a fight, despite being [[Unarmed combat|unarmed]]. Suddenly, Chopper speeds past Orrelios and the Imperials, taking them all by surprise. The Unwinder comes barreling in Chopper's wake, heading right toward the troopers. Chopper then circles back for his comrade, and tells him to get moving. The two successfully escape as the Unwinder tosses and smashes the Imperial soldiers around.

Later, aboard the ''[[Ghost]]'', Orrelios and Chopper are in the crew area, along with [[Rex]]. Sitting on the bench's edge, the Lasat tells his tale to Rex, who laughs in delight. Smiling at Chopper with genuine affection, Orrelios remarks he only wishes they could have gotten that data they were after. [[Hera Syndulla]] then appears, revealing that Chopper did upload the Imperial codes as soon as the two rebels returned, and in fact, they could have left the battledome before any of their mishaps occurred. Once again, Orrelios growls at Chopper in wide-eyed exasperation.

==Continuity==
"Always Bet on Chop" features gladiator droids, which made their canon debut in "[[Nomad Droids]]," the sixth episode of the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' television series']] [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|fourth season]],<ref name="ND">{{TCW|Nomad Droids}}</ref> but whose origins can be traced to the [[1979]] [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] novel ''[[Han Solo at Stars' End]]''.<ref name="Han Solo at Stars End">''[[Han Solo at Stars' End]]''</ref> The comic also features the first canon depiction of a Verpine, a species originally created for Strike Force: Shantipole and retconed into [[Star Wars (1977) 7|''Star Wars'' (1977) 7]], the seventh issue of [[Marvel Comics]]' earlier Legends [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'']] comic series<ref name="New Planets, New Perils!">[[Star Wars (1977) 7|''Star Wars'' (1977) 7]]</ref> in [[1977]].<ref name="New Planets, New Perils! release date">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/53797/star_wars_1977_7|text=Star Wars (1977) #7}}</ref>
With its arms ending in mechanical claws, The Unwinder resembles the [[grapple droideka]] that appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Episode I Jedi Power Battles]]''.<ref name="SWJPB">[[Star Wars: Episode I Jedi Power Battles|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Jedi Power Battles'']]</ref>
