'''Armorweave''' was a material used on various pieces of [[clothing]], including [[cape]]s,<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> as [[armor]].<ref name="Nexus">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref>

==History==
The [[Jedi Temple Guard armor]] was incorporated with layers of armorweave to provide them with an effective defense against [[blaster]] bolts and [[lightsaber]]s.<ref name="Nexus" /> [[Count]] [[Dooku]], leader of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], wore a traditional [[Serennian cloak]]<ref name="AotC">{{Film|II}}</ref> made of armorweave. His apprentice, [[Asajj Ventress]], also wore clothing made of the same material.<ref name="RotS">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

[[Captain]] [[Phasma]] of the [[First Order]] wore a traditional armorweave cape alongside her [[Phasma's armor|custom armor]] that was representative of her command.<ref name="TFAVD" /> [[Archex|Cardinal]], also of the [[First Order]], wore an armorweave cape alongside his [[Cardinal's armor|own armor]].<ref name="Phasma">[[Phasma (novel)|''Phasma'']]</ref> The [[pirate]] [[Sidon Ithano]] wore a cape lined with armorweave.<ref name="TFAVD" />
