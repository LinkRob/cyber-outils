An individual was the '''second cousin''' of the [[Nihil]] [[Cloud (rank)|Cloud]] [[Dent (Nihil)|Dent]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> By [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> they were [[Marriage|married]] to [[Buggo]], a [[Gender|male]] [[Strike]] who served under Dent's [[Commanding officer|command]], which led her to consider Buggo family of a sort.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> while carrying out a [[Raid on Elphrona|raid]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Elphrona]], Dent [[Brain|mentally]] considered that she should have sent the Strike to ambush the [[Jedi]] on the planet but then recalled that he was the husband of her second cousin, and therefore the closest she had to family in a way.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
