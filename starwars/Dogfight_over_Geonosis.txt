A '''dogfight''' over [[Geonosis]] took place between [[Jango Fett]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] inside the dangerous [[asteroid]] [[Rings of Geonosis|ring]] around the [[planet]] which was then the-[[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[capital]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Dad! I think we're being tracked!''"<br />"''He must have put a homing device on our hull. Hang on, son. We'll move into the asteroid field, and we'll have a couple of surprises for him.|Boba and Jango Fett|<ref name="aotc" />}}
After a [[Mission to Kamino (Separatist Crisis)|duel]] with [[Jango Fett]] on [[Kamino]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] threw a [[homing beacon]] on the departing ''[[Slave I]]'s'' [[hull]]. He tracked the [[Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft|ship]] to the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]]. After [[Boba Fett]] saw they were being tracked, Jango flew ''Slave I'' into the [[Rings of Geonosis|asteroid field]] around the planet.<ref name="aotc" />

==The dogfight==
{{Quote|Get him, Dad! Get him! Fire!|Boba Fett, to Jango|<ref name="aotc" />}}
[[File:SeismicCharge.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A seismic charge detonating.]]
Kenobi followed in close pursuit of Jango, dodging [[asteroid]]s and incoming [[seismic charge]]s. Jango flew through tunnels in asteroids, but Kenobi didn't give up. Once out of Kenobi's view, Jango turned the tide and was now himself in pursuit; who fired until the [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Jedi interceptor]] was hit. After this, Fett fired a homing [[missile]] hoping to finish him off.<ref name="aotc" />

Kenobi however told his [[astromech droid]] [[R4-P17]] to release his [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|interceptor]]'s spare parts so the missile could not dodge the tiny objects and explode, leading Jango to believe he had killed him, however Kenobi landed in an asteroid and powered down until Jango had entered Geonosis's [[atmosphere]]. He followed the him to the surface.<ref name="aotc" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|May I ask why a Jedi Knight is all the way out here on Geonosis?''"<br />"''I'm tracking a bounty hunter named Jango Fett. Do you know him?''"<br />"''There are no bounty hunters here that I'm aware of. The Geonosians don't trust them.''"<br />"''Well, who can blame them? But he is here, I assure you.|Count Dooku and Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="aotc" />}}
After the skirmish, Kenobi landed on the surface of Geonosis after following Fett to a [[Geonosis droid factories|droid foundry]] and observing the high levels of [[Trade Federation]] activity while landing, where he learned more of the [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|assassination attempts]] on [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] as he secretly entered the foundry in search of the [[bounty hunter]]. He overheard the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederacy]] [[Head of State]] and fallen [[Jedi Master]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and the [[Executive Separatist Council|various delegates]] who were discussing it's new [[Separatist Droid Army|droid army]], that would force the [[Galactic Republic]] to bow to any of the Confederacy's demands, which several interstellar conglomerates including the [[Commerce Guild]] and [[Corporate Alliance]] pledged their support to Dooku and the alliance. But the Trade Federation, who [[Viceroy of the Trade Federation|Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]] refused and outlining his conditions to joining it and would only sign the treaty if Amidala was killed but would still pledged his battle droids nevertheless.<ref name="aotc" />

[[File:SecessionistMovement.png|thumb|right|250px|Head of State Dooku meeting with delegates from various interstellar conglomerates during a summit held on Geonosis.]]
During the meeting, Dooku explained that 10,000 more [[star system]]s would join the Separatists. [[Foreman]] [[Wat Tambor]] told Dooku that the [[Techno Union Droid Army|droid army]] of the [[Techno Union]] was at his disposal and [[Chairman]] [[San Hill]] told him that the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] would sign his treaty. He was pleased and told them that when the [[Trade Federation Droid Army]] combined with the others, the [[Jedi]] will be overwhelmed. After the meeting had ended, Kenobi returned to his [[starfighter]] and tried to contact his [[padawan]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], about his discovery. He told Skywalker to redirect his message to [[Coruscant]]. Kenobi told [[Mace Windu]] and [[Yoda]] about how he tracked Fett to a droid foundry on Geonosis, that Dooku and the Council were forming a droid army, that Gunray was behind the assassination attempt on Amidala. Kenobi was then arrested by the [[Geonosian]]s and was held captive until the arrival of Skywalker and Amidala, whom attempted to rescue him, though they were themselves were arrested as well.<ref name="aotc" />
