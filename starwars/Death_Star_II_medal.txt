The '''Death Star II medal''' was a campaign [[medal]] that was awarded to every [[Rebel pilot|Alliance pilot]] who flew against the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Battle of Endor]]. Although it was not as rare as its predecessor, the [[Death Star medal]], it was still considered impressive, and many were awarded posthumously because of the deadly circumstances of the [[Space warfare|naval battle]] above [[Endor]]. It was essentially identical to the previous [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] medal, as it featured a completed [[Death Star]] [[battle station]] with a small [[Aurebesh]] number two at the center.<ref name="Lead" />

Similarly to the Death Star medal, the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star II]] medal was worn on a [[ribbon]] rack on a dress uniform or on the collar of a [[flight suit]]. [[Wedge Antilles]] was the only member of the [[Alliance Military]] who was permitted to wear both the Death Star medal and the Death Star II medal.<ref name="Lead" />
