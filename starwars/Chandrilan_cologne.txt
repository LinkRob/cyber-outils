'''Chandrilan cologne''' was a fragrance owned by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[general]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]. The evening before the climactic [[Battle of Endor]] between the Alliance and the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="FACPOV" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[rebel pilot]] [[Norra Wexley]] spoke with Calrissian aboard the [[MC80 Star Cruiser|cruiser]] ''[[Home One]]'' and, after he took her [[Limb|hands]] and helped her up, Wexley smelled a hint of Chandrilan cologne on Calrissian. The smell caused Wexley's [[nose]] to twitch, which, much to her irritation, led Calrissian to wink at her. The cologne shared its name with<ref name="FACPOV" /> the [[Core Worlds|Core World]] [[Chandrila]].<ref name="Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Chandrilan cologne appeared in "[[The Buy-In]]," a short story written by [[Suzanne Walker]] as part of the [[2023]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="FACPOV" />
