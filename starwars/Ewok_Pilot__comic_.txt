'''''Ewok Pilot''''' was a comic by [[Roan Novachez]] in ''[[The Padawan Observer]]'', a school newspaper of the [[Jedi academy]] at the [[Imperial Palace/Legends|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. It featured the character by the same name, [[Ewok Pilot]].

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The visual style of the comic is the same as that used for the rest of the illustrations by [[Jeffrey Brown]] in the ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Academy]]'' titles.
