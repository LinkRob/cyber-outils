'''Everbloom''' was a mobile [[space station]] which was the headquarters and estate of the wealthy [[Graf family]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Named for the lush [[garden]]s onboard the station, it had a resemblance to the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Starlight Beacon]], which the Grafs had helped design.<ref name="OOTS" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the Grafs, who were secretly conspiring with the [[scientist]] [[Chancey Yarrow]] and the [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]] as part of Chancey's efforts to build a [[gravity well projector]], assisted in the [[Ambush on Everbloom|ambush]] of several [[Jedi]] who arrived on the station in the company of [[Xylan Graf]] while it was in the [[Dalnan sector]]. In the ambush, the [[pirate]]s kidnapped [[Sylvestri Yarrow]], Chancey's daughter who had believed her [[Death|dead]], and [[Padawan]]s [[Reath Silas]] and [[Imri Cantaros]]. Afterwards, [[Jedi Master]] [[Cohmac Vitus]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]], and Sylvestri's girlfriend [[Jordanna Sparkburn]] accepted the offer of a new [[starship]] from Matriarch [[Catriona Graf]], although they suspected she was up to something. Everbloom remained in the Grafs' possession even after the [[Galactic Republic]] froze the family's assets, and Chancey later met Catriona and Xylan onboard Everbloom with the pirate [[Nan Hague|Nan]], after the [[Battle at Gravity's Heart|destruction]] of the [[Gravity's Heart]] station.<ref name="OOTS" />
