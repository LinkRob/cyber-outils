'''Beroq 4''' was a location<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> in the [[Beroq system]] within the [[Expansion Region]]'s [[Baroli sector]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], it housed a [[Unidentified fuel depot|fuel depot]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> By [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Gorma date">''[[Poe Dameron: Flight Log]]'' establishes that the [[Liberation of Gorma]] took place before the [[Battle of Endor]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref> as part of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Galactic Civil War|war]] against the Empire, [[rebel pilot]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Shara Bey]] took part in the [[Liberation of Gorma|liberation]] of<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> the [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Flight Log">''[[Poe Dameron: Flight Log]]''</ref> [[Gorma]]. Less than a [[Standard week|week]] later, the lieutenant carried out a raid at the Beroq 4 [[fuel]] depot during [[Operation: Mynock Bite]], earning Bey her triple [[ace]] status.<ref name="Before the Awakening" />

Long after Bey's [[Death|passing]]<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> in [[10 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> her [[Dameron family|son]] [[Poe Dameron]] began [[New Republic pilot|flying]] for the [[New Republic]]<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> in [[30 ABY]],<ref name="Poe pilot date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that [[Poe Dameron]] began [[New Republic pilot|flying]] for the [[New Republic]] four [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]].</ref> where Dameron learned of his [[Parent|mother's]] heroic acts during her service in the Alliance, including when she took part in the raid at Beroq 4.<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> In [[31 ABY]],<ref name="Poe story date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that [[Poe Dameron]] joined the [[Resistance]] three [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. As Dameron's story in ''[[Before the Awakening]]'' depicts his joining of the Resistance, its events must take place in [[31 ABY]].</ref> while carrying out a patrol in<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]]<ref name="Galaxy Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]] &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Mirrin system]].</ref> [[Mirrin sector]], Dameron mentally reflected on his life and recalled learning about his [[Parent|mother's]] raid of the fuel depot at Beroq 4.<ref name="Before the Awakening" />
