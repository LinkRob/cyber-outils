The '''Clan Tal MT-97''' were a pair of unique [[MT-97]] [[blaster pistol]]s owned by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Aran Tal]]. They were created by [[Mandalore Technologies]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Clan Tal MT-97 was a "Base" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Aran Tal's twin MT-97 blaster pistols, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 1.0.0 alongside Season 1 and the game's global launch on [[June 4]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Clan Tal Warrior]]. This wrap was unlocked by default when Aran Tal became playable.<ref name="Hunters" />
