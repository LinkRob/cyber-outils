The '''Edjian Prince''' was a mythical [[Corellian]] figure who braved the unknown [[forest]]s of the [[Uhl Eharl Khoehng]]. The Edjian Prince was one such legend on [[Corellia]] that predated the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] and helped define the spirit of adventure and exploration that became essential parts of Corellian heritage. The [[Coronet City Spaceport|Coronet Spaceport]] had a relief sculpture depicting the Edijan Prince.<ref name="Official Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
