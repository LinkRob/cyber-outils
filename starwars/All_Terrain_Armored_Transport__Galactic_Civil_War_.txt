This '''model''' of [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] was used during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. It replaced its larger [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)|predecessor]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Not every place can be fully armored. Think about it. The legs are vulnerable exactly where any creature's legs would be.''"<br />"''Gotcha. Targeting the joints.|Thane Kyrell and Yendor flying snowspeeders during the Battle of Hoth|<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>}}
[[File:ATATblueprint-Displate.png|thumb|250px|left|An AT-AT schematic]]
The All Terrain Armored Transport was an imposing, heavily armored, quadrupedal combat [[walker]] of the [[AT-series|All-terrain vehicle]] line that stood over 22 [[meter]]s tall. Its impressive size was used for both psychological effect as well as for tactical reasons, a weapon of terror that could cow the local populace with its mere presence. It's purpose was to use its sheer mass and impressive firepower to break through enemy lines and deploy its cargo of assault troops.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /><ref name="Encyclopedia Starfighters" /><ref name="Ultimate">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref><ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

The walker's [[cockpit]] was located in its heavily armored "head"<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> (also referred to as the "command pod"<ref name="incredibuilds" />), which connected to the main body via a "neck" composed of a ringed electromagnet system to allow greater flexibility. The head also carried a variety of systems such as [[sensor]]s (including rangefinders and terrain scanners), power cells, [[targeting computer]]s,<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> and spotlights.<ref name="incredibuilds" /> The head also contained a top hatch which was secured by cryptographic lock.<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

The command crew consisted of two [[AT-AT pilot]]s and a [[vehicle commander]]. One pilot drove the walker while the other acted as the gunner, although both were capable of filling either roles. Fire control could be surrendered to the vehicle commander via a periscope display, which could also be used to view sensor readouts and tactical data.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> Other standard personnel stationed elsewhere in the walker included an engineer and loadmaster.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> AT-AT pilots and crew wore specialized suits in case the walker's pressurized cockpit was smashed open in hostile environments.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref>

The walker's main armaments were also carried in its head: medium [[blaster]]s located on the sides or "temples" and a pair of Class II heavy [[laser cannon]]s fitted on the "chin."<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /><ref name="incredibuilds" /> The chin-mounted cannons could be fired simultaneously to create a powerful blast or in rapid succession and could vary their power intensity.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /><ref name="Empire">{{Film|V}}</ref> During the [[Battle of Hoth]], AT-AT laser fire was able to penetrate through the ground to hit [[Echo Base]]'s command center.<ref name="Twilight">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref>

The armor on the AT-AT, too heavy to be carried by [[repulsorlift]]s, was impervious to normal [[blaster]] bolts and cause them to glance off or be absorbed and dissipated. Only the heaviest [[turbolaser]] weaponry could actually penetrate an AT-AT's armor plating.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> The neck and the underbelly however were weak points on the AT-AT and vulnerable to vehicular blasters and portable [[missile]] launchers, necessitating [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]]s as escorts to protect its flanks.<ref name="Ultimate" /> The walker was also vulnerable to other AT-AT fire if one happened to be hijacked and turned against the other.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

<div style="float:right">[[File:AT-ATblueprints-RelicsoftheOldRepublic.jpg|thumb|250px|right|All Terrain Armored Transport blueprints seen by Sabine Wren & Ezra Bridger.]]</div>
The AT-AT strode into battle on four tall, reinforced armored legs which (while making it highly visible) helped the vehicle to see over obstructions and provide a clear line of sight for its weapons. Fitted with powerful shock absorbers and piston drives, each leg's foot pad was equipped with terrain scanners and computers to help the pilots navigate rough ground. Although capable of traversing a variety of terrain types, certain features such as steep hillsides or deep swamps proved impassible.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> The legs were also unstable and gave the walker a high center of gravity, leaving it vulnerable to being tripped up.<ref name="Ultimate" />

Connecting the "head" to the "body" was what was dubbed the "tunnel," which led to a troop compartment capable of ferrying up to 40 troopers along with [[heavy weapon]]s. Troops were seated in double-tiered benches which also functioned as backpack chargers.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /><ref name="RSG" /> Extendable boom racks with harnesses and high-speed cable winches allowed troopers to quickly deploy from the AT-AT.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> The main, rectangular troop hatch was located in the middle of the walker's side, while two smaller escape hatches were located both to the main hatch's immediate left and right,<ref name="RSG" /> and in the walker's underside.<ref name="CL" /> Past the main troop compartment was a vehicle bay at the walker's rear that could hold up to five [[speeder bike]]s, typically [[74-Z speeder bike|74-Z models]].<ref name="RSG" /> These also were deployed via harnesses from the AT-AT and typically used for scouting and mop-up operations.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" />

A powerful reactor provided all of the energy the AT-AT needed to function, with fuel cell, energizer rods and fuel pump located in the rear of the walker above the vehicle bay.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> On the exterior of the walker was a steep staircase, that could be used by the walker's personnel to climb from one hatch to the other, or to the walker's roof.<ref name="RSG" /> Special locking points along the exterior of the AT-AT helped to secure it for transport via landing barges.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> Along the top of the walker were filtered atmosphere intakes, while at the bottom was the walker's fuel slug tank, which had a reputation of being dangerous.<ref name="RSG" />

The [[Elite AT-AT]], a more heavily armored variant with a darker color scheme, was based on the body of this version.<ref name="Commander" />

==History==
===Age of the Empire===
{{Quote|I heard of these. Imperial armored transports. Giant four-legged things that were used to keep the peace on conquered worlds.|[[Scoggan]], to [[Davjan Igo]] upon entering the ruins of an AT-AT|<ref name="True Love">{{StoryCite|story=True Love|book=Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I}}</ref>}}
This model succeeded the [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)|model]] used in the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|pre&ndash;]][[Galactic Civil War]] conflict against the rebellion.<ref name="ROTOR Trivia">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/relics-of-the-old-republic-trivia-gallery|text="Relics of the Old Republic" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> Plans for this version existed as early as [[4 BBY]].<ref name="Empire Day">{{Rebels|Empire Day (episode)}}</ref><ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>

===Galactic Civil War===
{{Quote|That armor's too strong for blasters. Rogue group, use your harpoons and tow cables. Go for the legs; it might be our only chance of stopping them.|Luke Skywalker, during the Battle of Hoth|<ref name="ESB" />}}
[[File:StolenATAT-2015StarWars3.png|thumb|left|250px|The stolen AT-AT on Cymoon 1]]
Following the [[Battle of Yavin]], AT-ATs were employed in several skirmishes on [[Tatooine]]. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] also stole a number of AT-ATs and provided them to [[Tatooine rebel cell|Saponza's Gang]].<ref name="Commander" /> At least one AT-AT helped enforce the Imperial occupation of the Rebellion's former base, the [[Great Temple]], on [[Yavin 4]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 2">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 2|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 2]]</ref> During the Rebel [[Assault on Cymoon|assault]] on the Imperial [[weapons factory]] [[moon]] of [[Cymoon 1]], the [[Heroes of Yavin]] group of Rebels led by [[Han Solo]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] stole an AT-AT from [[Weapons Factory Alpha]]. They used the walker to escape from the factory after they sabotaged its main [[Reactor|power core]] to explode. During their escape, Solo fired a shot that nearly killed the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], using the AT-AT's powerful quad [[laser cannon]]s.<ref name="Star Wars 2" /> Soon after, Vader overtook the Rebels in their escape and cut down the massive walker's back left leg. The AT-AT fell tremendously, and its passengers barely escaped the wreckage<ref name="Star Wars 3">[[Star Wars (2015) 3|''Star Wars'' (2015) 3]]</ref> before fleeing Cymoon 1 entirely for the safety of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]].<ref name="Star Wars 4">[[Star Wars (2015) 4|''Star Wars'' (2015) 4]]</ref>

AT-ATs<ref name="V16">[[Darth Vader (2015) 16|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 16]]</ref><ref name="V17">[[Darth Vader (2015) 17|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 17]]</ref> were also used by the Empire in support of the [[Kingdom of Shu-Torun]] during the [[Shu-Torun War|civil war]] on the mining planet [[Shu-Torun]], where rebellious [[ore-duke]] refused to bow to the Empire and its puppet regime. One AT-AT was destroyed in the [[Battle at a delving citadel|assault]] on a [[delving citadel]] at an ore-duke stronghold by a laser shot to its neck, but the delving citadel itself would be destroyed shortly after.<ref name="V16" /> After the Empire fought off an ambush, Imperials forces, including an AT-AT, joined the Shu-Torun troops in the [[Battle at Rubix's citadel|siege]] of [[Rubix's delving citadel]], which ended the war in a victory for the Empire and its regime.<ref name="V17" /> When [[Aphra's crew]] [[Raid at the Great Temple|raided]] the Great Temple on Yavin 4, they spotted an AT-AT<ref name="Doctor Aphra 2" /> amongst the occupying [[platoon]]s and then watched one move through the jungle. [[0-0-0]] and [[BT-1]] passed an AT-AT and the rest of the Imperial force during the raid.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 3">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 3|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 3]]</ref> An AT-AT cut off the crew's escape from the Great Temple until [[Krrsantan]] rescued them in the ''[[Ark Angel II]]''.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 3">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 3|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 3]]</ref>

[[File:BattleOfHoth-2020StarWars18.png|thumb|300px|Imperial AT-ATs near-effortlessly march through Echo Base's defenses]]
During their [[Escape from an unidentified ocean planet|escape from an oceanic planet]], Organa and [[Luke Skywalker]] destroyed an AT-AT.<ref name="Star Wars 33">[[Star Wars (2015) 33|''Star Wars'' (2015) 33]]</ref> During the [[Battle of Hoth]], [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]] led his [[Blizzard Force]] of AT-ATs on the icy fields of [[Hoth]] to knock out the [[Deflector shield|shield]] [[Deflector shield generator|generator]] that protected [[Echo Base]], the Rebel headquarters. Despite facing resistance from the [[Alliance Army]] and [[T-47 airspeeder]]s, Veers' squadron emerged victorious, allowing the Imperials to invade Echo Base and rout the Rebels, striking a major blow in the war. However, the battle also revealed that the AT-ATs could be toppled by a cable wrapped around their legs. Although that weakness had been used effectively by the Alliance on Hoth,<ref name="ESB" /> the Empire continued to use these mechanical behemoths in the later stages of the war.<ref name="ROJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

In the [[Battle of Ab Dalis]], AT-ATs marched against the grounded [[Eleventh Division]] of the Alliance Navy, which would be reinforced by [[Starlight Squadron]]. The AT-ATs and other Imperial forces were unable to prevent a rebel victory after the [[squadron]] destroyed the ''[[Ultima II]]''.<ref name="SW 2020 15">[[Star Wars (2020) 15|''Star Wars'' (2020) 15]]</ref> AT-ATs marched as part of an [[Empire Day|Imperial Unity Day]] parade on [[Bar'leth]], only to be destroyed by a [[Unity Day attack|surprise rebel ambush]].<ref name="SW 2020 26">[[Star Wars (2020) 26|''Star Wars'' (2020) 26]]</ref> At least one AT-AT was part of the [[occupation of Endor]]. The walker was used by [[Commander]] [[Altadan Igar]] to transport Skywalker to the [[Death Star's shield generator]].<ref name="ROJ" /> AT-ATs took part in the [[Battle of Cawa City]].<ref name="Shattered Empire 2">[[Shattered Empire 2|''Shattered Empire'' 2]]</ref> The [[Hellhound attack force]] was part of the Jakku remnant.<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref>

===Rise of the First Order===
[[Standard decade|Decades]] after the war, a young [[scavenger]] named [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] used one of the downed AT-ATs, designated ''[[Hellhound Two]]'', as her home on [[Jakku]].<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> The [[First Order Army]] utilized an [[First Order All Terrain Armored Transport|updated version]] of the AT-AT, which saw deployment during a [[Battle against the Benathy (First Order)|battle]] against the [[Benathy]] [[species]] on [[Benath]]<ref name="AoR Kylo 1">[[Age of Resistance - Kylo Ren 1|''Age of Resistance - Kylo Ren'' 1]]</ref> and the [[Battle of Crait]] against the [[Resistance]].<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>
