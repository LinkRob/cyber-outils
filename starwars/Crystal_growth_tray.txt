A '''crystal growth tray''' was a device used for growing [[crystal]]s that consisted of a central [[Color|bronze]]-colored dish with three gray stabilization struts ending in flat circular heat shields. The [[Jedi]]-in-[[Jedi training|training]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] owned a crystal growth tray which she kept on her workbench in the [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|Resistance base]] on the [[moon]] [[Ajan Kloss]], where she flexed her mechanical skills, studied the [[sacred Jedi texts]], and worked on mending the broken [[kyber crystal]] in the [[Skywalker lightsaber]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}} to [[35 ABY]].</ref> the tray was sat on the workbench,<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> filled with blue crystal seeds in a growth medium<ref name="TROS VD" /> when Rey was informed that the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' [[YT-1300 light freighter]] had returned to base. The tray was in the same position when Rey later retrieved the<ref name="TROS" /> ''[[Rammahgon]]'',<ref name="TROS VD" /> one of the Jedi [[Book|texts]], from a container near the bench.<ref name="TROS" />

==Behind the scenes==
A crystal growth tray first appeared in the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="TROS" /> It was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
