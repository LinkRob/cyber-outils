The '''Durango Shuttle Service'''<ref name="The Princess and the Jedi">{{YJA|The Princess and the Jedi}}</ref> was a [[transport]] company operated on the [[forest]] [[planet]] [[Tenoo]]. It was ran by the [[human]] couple [[Kryys Durango]] and her [[Marriage|wife]], [[Ceeli Durango]]. Their daughter [[Nash Durango]] worked as one of the [[pilot]]s using her [[shuttle]], the ''[[Crimson Firehawk]]''.<ref name="The Young Jedi" /> The company later was hired to transport the [[human]] [[Princess]] [[Inaya]] to her [[homeworld]] [[Hazun]].<ref name="The Princess and the Jedi" />
