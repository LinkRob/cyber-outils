'''Endor-la''' was a "lost city" found on the [[Endor/Legends|forest moon of Endor]]. Though it was a supposedly peaceful place, young [[Ewok/Legends|Ewoks]] [[Wicket Wystri Warrick/Legends|Wicket]] and [[Teebo/Legends|Teebo]] once got into trouble there after fleeing from some [[Dulok/Legends|Duloks]].

==Behind the scenes==
Endor-la was only mentioned in [[Marvel Comics]]' solicitation information for the ultimately canceled fifteenth issue of the [[Star Wars: Ewoks (1985)|''Ewoks'']] comic series.<ref name="Marvel Age">''Marvel Age 53''</ref>
