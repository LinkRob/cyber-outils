'''Anzellan''' was a [[language]] spoken by the [[Anzellan]] [[species]]. [[Zorii Bliss]], leader of the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]], knew Anzellan in order to communicate with the Anzellan [[droidsmith]] [[Babu Frik]].<ref name="TROS Expanded" />

==Known words and phrases==
*'''''a' deg a' ''''' - an Anzellan greeting used a sign of respect.<ref name="SC Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)]]</ref>
*'''''ba ma' shay''''' - an Anzellan blessing that translates to "may you journey boldly".<ref name="SC Datapad" />
*'''''ta' bue e tay''''' - a celebratory blessing that translates to "cherish the moment".<ref name="SC Datapad" />
