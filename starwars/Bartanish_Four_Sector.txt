'''Bartanish Four Sector''', often shortened to '''Bash Four,''' was a [[sector]] located on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. It housed a [[Bartanish Four Sector supplementary citizen assistance office|supplementary citizen assistance office]] opened by [[Senator]] [[Domus Renking]] of [[Lothal]]. It held many apartments and businesses, including a Dojo. It was closer to the [[Federal District]] than the nearby [[Lothalian citizen assistance office]].<ref name="Thrawn" />
