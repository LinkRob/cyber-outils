'''Andui''' was a [[city]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Takodana]]. Using a [[speeder bike]], it took a solid [[Standard day|day]] to get there from the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of [[Maz Kanata]]. After having her face reconstructed by the [[Frigosian]] [[cryptosurgeon]]s [[Thromba]] and [[Laparo]], the criminal [[Ryn Biggleston]] attempted to find offworld transportation in Andui, but was apprehended by a [[deputy]].<ref name="The Face of Evil" /> Andui was the largest city on the planet, albeit still relatively small.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>
