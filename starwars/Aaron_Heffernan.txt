'''Aaron Heffernan''' is an [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]] who played the role of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial swamp trooper|Mudtrooper]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.

==Biography==
Aaron Heffernan was born circa [[1990]] and lives in [[Wikipedia:London|London]], [[Wikipedia:England|England]]. From [[2008]] to [[2013]], he pursued drama studies at [[Wikipedia:Trinity College Dublin|Trinity College Dublin]].<ref name="Spotlight">{{WebCite|url=https://www.spotlight.com/2613-5613-4148|text=Spotlight - Aaron Heffernan|archivedate=20180720203419}}</ref> In [[2018]], he portrayed an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial swamp trooper|mudtrooper]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Instagram" />
