'''Burryaga's lightsaber''' of [[Wookiee]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Burryaga Agaburry]] was a single-bladed [[crossguard lightsaber]] with a [[Color|blue]] blade<ref name="CotJ" /> powered by a [[kyber crystal]].<ref name="DB:Kyber" /> The [[lightsaber]]'s hilt was plated in<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> [[meryx]],<ref name="M&A" /> the amber of the [[white wroshyr tree]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> which had been extinct for millennia,<ref name="M&A" /> wrapped in the leather of a [[tach]], and was finished off with an pure [[electrum]] crosspiece. It was specially crafted to be used by a tall Wookiee, and as such was one of the longest [[lightsaber]]s used by the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="CotJ" />
