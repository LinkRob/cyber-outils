The '''Coalition for Progress (CFP)''', also known as the '''Coalition of Progress''' or simply '''Progress''', was a sub-bureau of the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]]. It was responsible for the cultural progress of the [[Galactic Empire]] and shaping its [[Propaganda|public image]].

==History==
{{Quote|In recent years, this work had fallen under scrutiny by the Imperial Coalition for Progress division of COMPNOR because it had fallen into disrepair and had became an issue of public safety.|Newscaster [[Alton Kastle]]|<ref name="HNN" />}}
The Coalition for Progress was formed as a sub-bureau of the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]], as part of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] massive restructuring of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] hierarchy. The Coalition soon became responsible for monitoring and molding the Imperial image to the [[The galaxy|galactic]] citizenry, and raising the next generation of Imperial leaders.<ref name="Propaganda" />

In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="VG">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> the Coalition for Progress intervened on [[Garel]], ordering the destruction of a controversial statue created by the famous [[artist]] [[Janyor]] of [[Bith (planet)|Bith]]. Officially, the Coalition for Progress maintained that the work of art had been in poor condition due to neglect, and had become a danger to the population. A few citizens staged a [[Garel statue protest|spontaneous protest]], but were promptly quelled by local authorities.<ref name="HNN" />

Around [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{Aphra2016Date|29}}</ref> the Coalition for Progress became aware of a live holofeed broadcasting on [[Milvayne]] which showed [[0-0-0|two]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|fugitives]] on the run from the [[Milvayne Authority]] and other enemies. Their Imperial [[Propaganda]] [[Flagship]], the ''[[Enduring Pride]]'' flew [[Kilometer|klicks]] above the planet as [[Okma]] spoke with the coalition's leader,<ref name="Aphra 29" /> [[Chairman|Chair]]<ref name="Aphra 35" /> [[Pitina Mar-Mas Voor]]<ref name="Aphra 36" /> about the matter. She felt the broadcast showed unacceptable scenes that could inspire mischievous ideas in the citizens of Milvayne. Though Okma pointed out that Milvayne had accepted Imperial rule on the terms that they kept an invisible presence, meaning they were not allowed to intervene, the [[minister]] assured him that it was not necessary to avoid this.<ref name="Aphra 29" />

==Structure==
===Art Group===
{{Main|Art Group}}
An office reporting on the suitability of artistic expression within the Empire and applied inflexible criteria that exemplified its austere tenets.<ref name="Propaganda" />

===Commerce===
{{Main|Commerce (department)}}
The Commerce department of the Coalition was tasked with creating galactic norms in regards to the economy.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

===Education===
{{Main|Education}}
The [[Education]] department made sure schools and other such education facilities followed what the Empire thought should be taught.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

===Justice===
{{Main|Justice (department)}}
The [[Justice (department)|Justice]] branch was focused on bringing legal norms up to a standard that ensured Imperial superiority.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

===Ministry of Information===
{{Main|Ministry of Information}}
Ministry that oversaw public relations between the Empire and its citizenry, and responsible for monitoring the [[HoloNet News]] Network.<ref name="Propaganda" />

===Science===
{{Main|Science}}
Organization that guided research and development into new military applications.<ref name="Propaganda" />

===Sector Monitor===
{{Main|Sector Monitor}}
A battery of galaxy-wide [[Sector Monitor]]s reported to the Coalition for Progress and acted as civilian spy agencies feeding the Coalition information regarding cultural progress within the Empire, using their vast surveillance network to monitor the development of art, science, education, commerce, and law enforcement. Groups within the government would act accordingly when "corrections" were required.<ref name="Propaganda" /> The Sector Monitors would report information of suspicious activities to the [[Imperial Security Bureau]], while data on cultural shifts against the [[New Order]] were reported to the Coalition for Progress.<ref name="Dawn" />

===Sub-Adult Group===
{{Main|Sub-Adult Group}}
Youth-based organization dedicated to molding adolescents into administrative and bureaucratic positions within the Empire. An alternate route to those that chose to forgo military service and traditional [[Imperial Academy|Academy]] enrollment.<ref name="Propaganda" />
