During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Galactic Empire]], under the leadership of [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], sought to mine [[Quadanium steel|quadanium]] on the [[planet]] [[Agaris]], establishing an [[Imperial compound (Agaris)|Imperial compound]] there. However, the compound was '''attacked''' by the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Agarian]]s, led by [[Hffrr]], and persuaded to fight the Empire by the [[Graf family]]. The Agarians launched poisonous gas at the compound, driving the Imperials back to their [[Starship|ship]], the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|ISD]] ''[[Executrix]]''. Knowing Tarkin would launch an [[orbital bombardment]], the Agarians and the Grafs fled the world, leaving it to the Empire.<ref name="The Rescue" />
