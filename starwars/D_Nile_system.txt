The '''D'Nile system''' was located in the [[Hertae sector]], a part of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It was situated in grid square Q-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The D'Nile [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[D'Nile system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via a mention in the StarWars.com [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>

The system's name references a misinterpretation<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|HolocronKeeper|status/22222850016|[[Leland Chee]]|quote=Why not, I just added planet Denile/Denial (sp?) and Skywalker wine as an in universe beverage into the Holocron.|archivedate=20200406095156|nolive=1}}</ref> of a comment made by [[George Lucas]],<ref name="SW">{{SWArchive|url=fans/events/jon_stewart_george_lucas_main_event/index.html|text=''Star Wars'' Celebration Main Event: Play by Play|author=[[Bonnie Burton]]; [[Pete Vilmur]]}}</ref> the creator of the ''Star Wars'' franchise and the [[Original trilogy|original]] and [[Prequel trilogy|prequel trilogies]] of ''Star Wars'' films,<ref name="YbY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> during an interview at the [[Celebration V]] convention on [[August 14]], [[2010]]. The comment, which stated "The core of his sense of loss is on that planet. It's called denial," served to elaborate on why [[Anakin Skywalker]],<ref name="SW" /> the central character of the original and prequel trilogies,<ref name="YbY" /> avoided his [[homeworld]], the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="SW" />

On [[August 26]], 2010, however, [[Leland Chee]], the keeper of [[Lucasfilm]]'s [[Holocron continuity database]], announced that, in response to the interview, a planet named "Denile" or "Denial" had been added to the database.<ref name="Twitter" /> Furthermore, on [[March 26]], [[2011]], Fry noted on the [[Jedi Council Forums]] of [[TheForce.net]] that a group of [[Star system/Legends|star systems]] included in the Online Companion, including the D'Nile system, referenced "established worlds."<ref name="JCF">{{JCFCite|url=posts/31618818|board=Literature|thread=The Essential Atlas and Galactic Cartography: Official Discussion|user=[[Jason Fry|jasonfy]]|date=2011-03-26|time=10:52 PM|quote=Of that list of systems, Gultanna, Manoe, Opitha Tren and Thory Prime were created for the appendix, basically for bookkeeping purposes so every sector would be associated with at least one system. The others are all established worlds.|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20191013233754/https://boards.theforce.net/threads/the-essential-atlas-and-galactic-cartography-official-discussion.21938930/page-163#post-31618818}}</ref>
