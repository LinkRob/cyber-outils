'''5G Sith Jet Trooper Experience''' was an interactive augmented reality (AR) experience created by [[ILM Immersive|ILMxLAB]], [[Wikipedia:Verizon Communications|Verizon's]] RYOT Studios, and [[Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures|Walt Disney Studios]] StudioLAB. The experience took place at two different location for the premiere and afterparty of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="Forbes" /> It featured an in-universe set where guests would stand in front of a large LED screen and interact with two [[Sith Jetpack Trooper]]s in real-time. Two actors, wearing motion capture suits, interacted with guests from a studio in [[Wikipedia:Playa Vista, Los Angeles|Playa Vista]], where their performance would be captured and transferred to a digital character<ref name="EricDay" /> rendered in Unreal Engine.<ref name="Forbes" /> Their interaction would then be projected onto the LED screen using a 5G connection. There was also a camera at the event that would allow the digital aspects of the experience to be composited onto the real life footage, making it appear like the Sith Jet Troopers were physically there.<ref name="Presentation" />

The actors had lines fed to them by the on-site directors, with several pre-set animations for the performers and guests to react to. These animations included lightning in the stormy skies of [[Exegol]], a cadre of [[Sith trooper]]s running through the [[hangar]], and use of the Jetpack Troopers' [[Jetpack]]s to hover or fly in from out of frame. As users finished their interaction, they could access a terminal in which they entered their contact information to receive the re-rendered augmented reality video of their interaction. As guests entered their information, they could watch a smaller screen showing a behind-the-scenes live feed of the actors performing in the motion capture studio.<ref name="Presentation">{{WebCite|url=https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/e/2PACX-1vT3FYMNcqTljq4jT8P4IXg-7UNP6mg6gS0azIRvcOAVNehRkfWLFZ_5QqRNj082UXqVhgZaom_IxZ_X/pub?slide=id.g10974f006208bef_5|text=The Making of Verizon&#39;s 5G Jet Trooper Experiencea|work=Google Docs|archivedate=20240216105006}} via {{WebCite|url=https://ericday.me/creative-director|text=Portfolio|author=Eric Day|work=[https://ericday.me/starwars ericday.me]|archivedate=20231003133725}}</ref>

The experience was rendered live in [[Epic Games]]' Unreal Engine.<ref name="Forbes">{{WebCite|url=https://www.forbes.com/sites/jessedamiani/2019/12/19/star-wars-premiere-features-live-virtual-sith-jet-troopers-through-verizon-5g-and-motion-capture-exclusive|text='Star Wars' Premiere Features Live Virtual Sith Jet Troopers Through Verizon 5G And Motion Capture|author=Jesse Damiani|date=2019-12-19|archiveurl=https://archive.is/frE2v}}</ref> The two Sith jet troopers were performed by [[Hunter Smith]], [[Verona Blue]], and [[Alan Maxson]]. Despite being paired with the premiere of ''[[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker]]'' and working with [[Matt Martin]],<ref name="EricDay" /> the canonicity of this event has not been established.
