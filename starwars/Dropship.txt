'''Dropships''' were any craft designed to transport troops to the surface of a [[planet]]. Models of dropship included the [[Imperial Dropship Transport]], the [[Y-45 armored transport hauler]], the [[Theta-class barge|''Theta''-class barge]],<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> and the [[Y-85 Titan dropship]].<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> The [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruisers]] could also be used as dropships, as they could carry either four [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s,<ref name="Rebels Guide">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> four [[All Terrain Defense Pod]]s, or even two [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]s<ref name="Rebels Princess">{{Rebels|A Princess on Lothal}}</ref> on underside-mounted docking clamps.<ref name="Droids">{{Rebels|Droids in Distress}}</ref>
