'''Bastian''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[lieutenant]] and [[starfighter]] [[pilot]] of the [[Resistance]] during its [[Cold War|conflict]] with the [[First Order]]. He participated in the [[Battle of Starkiller Base|assault]] on [[Starkiller Base]] as a member of [[Red Squadron (Resistance)|Red Squadron]], a [[Squadron|group]] of [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]s. Flying as [[Red Four]] during the battle, Bastian was ordered by [[Poe Dameron]] to protect him and the other pilots. He later cheered along with the other surviving pilots after the assault turned out to be a success, and Starkiller Base was destroyed.<ref name="Episode VII" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:Lt_Bastian.png|thumb|left|250px|Lieutenant Bastian cheers in the cockpit of his T-70 X-wing.]]
Bastian was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] with dark skin, [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s, and black [[hair]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> A well admired and accomplished pilot, Bastian was an experienced professional and an intelligent man. He was brave and dedicated to the cause of freedom, and felt strongly against all that the First Order stood for.<ref name="Topps Galactic Files">{{Topps|set=2017 Topps Star Wars Galactic Files Reborn|cardname=TFA-24 Lieutenant Bastian|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/135181/cid/10118903/2017-Topps-Star-Wars-Galactic-Files:-Reborn-TFA-24-Lieutenant-Bastian}}</ref>

==Equipment==
[[File:LieutenantBastian-SWZ.png|thumb|right|270px|Lieutenant Bastian piloting his T-70 X-wing through some asteroids.]]
As an [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] pilot, he wore a red [[flight suit]] and a white [[flight helmet]] with red markings, alongside the unit insignia for [[Cobalt Squadron]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Star Wars is crazy. Even the audition process was crazy. They had an app for you to get the [scripts] and stuff like that, then they'll drive you to learn your lines there.|Tosin Cole|<ref name="RadioTimes">{{WebCite|url=https://www.radiotimes.com/tv/sci-fi/doctor-who-series-11-new-companion-tosin-cole-exclusive-interview|text=Doctor Who's new companion Tosin Cole talks TV diversity and sci-fi secrecy ahead of series 11|date=2018-07-22|author=Fullerton, Huw|quote='''You were in Star Wars: The Force Awakens in 2015 [playing Lieutenant Bastian] - how does the secrecy compare to Who?''' Oh, Star Wars is crazy. Even the audition process was crazy. They had an app for you to get the [scripts] and stuff like that, then they'll drive you to learn your lines there. '''So is Doctor Who more laid back?''' I don't know - it's kind of similar, but they take the secrecy crazy seriously [on Star Wars]. I've never had to download an app for anything over here. Or have read scripts that you have to leave with them. It's a bit crazy.|archivedate=20220411035231}}</ref>}}
Bastian is portrayed by [[Tosin Cole]] in the [[2015]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], directed by [[J.J. Abrams]] and written Abrams, [[Lawrence Kasdan]] and [[Michael Arndt]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> Cole auditioned for the role. During the shooting, the actor had to download a special app to get access to the script and had to learn his lines then and there, due to the secrecy of the production. He was also not allowed to take any script with him off-set.<ref name="RadioTimes" />

[[Jason Fry]], the author of the [[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition|novelization]] of [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] originally desired to show the pilots who flew with Dameron in ''The Force Awakens'' on a mission to explain their absence from the film, but he realized that the plotline couldn't have a payoff.<ref name="Independent">{{WebCite|url=https://www.independent.co.uk/arts-entertainment/books/features/star-wars-last-jedi-book-expanded-edition-author-jason-fry-interview-luke-skywalker-han-solo-rian-a8245706.html|text=Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition author on creating new scenes, reimagining Luke Skywalker's life, and working with Rian Johnson|author=Sheperd, Jack|date=2018-03-11|work=[[Wikipedia:The Independent|The Independent]]|archivedate=20180309000832}}</ref>
