The '''''Banshee''''' was a [[Ginivex-class fanblade starfighter|''Ginivex''-class fanblade starfighter]] used by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[commander]] and [[Sith assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Ventress used the ''Banshee'' during the [[Battle of Sullust]],<ref name="Power of the Dark Side" /> and survived thanks to its design.<ref name="Build Your Own X-Wing">{{BuildXWingCite|19|Starfighter Aces}}</ref> Later, during her time as a [[bounty hunter]], Ventress used a modified [[Lancer-class pursuit craft|''Lancer''-class pursuit craft]] that was also named the [[Banshee (starship)|''Banshee'']].<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>
