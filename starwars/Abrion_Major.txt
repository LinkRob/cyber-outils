'''Abrion Major''' was a [[planet]] covered in islands and one of the hundreds of [[agriworld]]s located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Abrion sector]]. Shortly after the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]], led by [[Senator]] [[Esu Rotsino]], Abrion Major as well as the rest of the Abrion [[sector]] seceded from the [[Galactic Republic]] to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Rotsino then represented Abrion Major and the other worlds of the Abrion sector in the [[Separatist Senate]]. 

Around [[22 BBY]], [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] led the [[212th Attack Battalion]] on an [[Battle of Abrion Bridge|assault]] at [[Abrion Bridge]], in order to prevent the Separatist from producing a new [[Mega-ion cannon]] at a [[Separatist weapons factory (Abrion Major)|weapons factory]]. The [[Galactic Republic]] was successful in the assault but suffered heavy losses.

==Description==
[[File:KenobiOnAbrionMajor-ObiWan3.png|300px|thumb|left|Abrion Major was a terrestrial planet with many islands.]]
Abrion Major was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Rise" /> in the [[Abrion Major system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> in the [[Abrion sector]], located in [[the Slice]] portion of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. One of two-hundred others within its [[sector]], Abrion Major was an [[agriworld]] that helped formed [[the galaxy]]'s bread-basket that was the Abrion sector. As such, their primary export was [[foodstuff]]s, delivered to the rest of the [[galaxy]].<ref name="Rise" />

Abrion Major was home to an archipelago located in the [[Western sea]]. Located on the central island of the achipelago, was a [[Separatist weapons factory (Abrion Major)|weapons factory]] under control of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The planet had [[Abrion Major's sun|one]] [[Star|sun]] that, in the opinion of [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], created breathtaking sunrises.<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />

==History==
===Seceding from the Republic===
Prior to the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Politics|politician]] [[Esu Rotsino]] was made the [[senator]] for the entire Abrion sector, representing her [[homeworld]] and the sector in the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Galactic Senate|Senate]].<ref name="Rise" /> Around [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'' states that the [[Abrion sector]] seceded shortly after the [[Clone Wars]] [[First Battle of Geonosis|broke out]], dated to [[22 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> shortly after the Republic had initiated conflict with the opposing [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Rotsino recognized the Republic's stifling [[bureaucrat]]ic procedures and inequitable trade. Tendering the formal [[articles of secession]], the Abrion sector seceded from the Republic into the Confederacy under her guidance, offering her a seat in the [[Separatist Senate]] as representative for Abrion Major and the sector as a whole. Abrion Major thus became a crucial point for the regional Separatist war-effort, primarily in their offensives along the [[Corellian Run]] in the [[Trailing Sectors]]. The Separatists had also sought to cause [[Foodstuff|food]] crises in several Republic sectors, due to Abrion Major's and the entire Abrion sector's role as the galactic bread-basket.<ref name="Rise" />

===Battle of Abrion Bridge===
[[File:CutOffByIGs-ObiWan3.png|thumb|right|300px|Republic forces suffered heavy casualties during the battle.]]
Before the [[Defense of Rishi Station|destruction]] of [[Rishi Station]], the [[Galactic Republic]] recovered intelligence regarding the Separatist producing a new [[Mega-ion cannon]] at a [[weapon]]s [[factory]] on Abrion Major. The only way to reach the factory was through the [[Abrion Bridge]]. Sometime following the Rishi Station battle, [[Jedi]] [[General]] Obi-Wan Kenobi and the [[212th Attack Battalion]] volunteered to lead the assault. The [[battalion]] was joined by [[Commander]] [[Mekedrix]], [[Lieutenant]] [[Oron]] and his [[Mekedrix's company|company]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />

The Republic deployed several [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]]s to the bridge but came under heavy fire from the factory defenses. Although suffering heavy losses, the [[clone trooper]]s and Republic forces were able to land on the bridge but met stiff Separatist resistance. Mekedrix's company cut through the defenses and was already reaching the factory. Despite the setbacks, Kenobi's forces were able to reach the factory and steal the plans to the ion cannon. But as the Republic retreated, they were met by [[IG-series assassin droid]] units and [[IG-227 Hailfire-class droid tank]]s who were held back until the Republic was trapped on the bridge. Despite Republic [[bomber]]s inbound to destroy the bridge, Kenobi refused to leave his men behind. Unfortunately he was unable to rescue many of them, leaving behind many casualties on the bridge.<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />
