{{Quote|Duracrete slug. About ten meters long, by the look of him. They burrow into the stone. Best to keep an eye out.|[[Keets Freely]] to [[Trever Flume]]|<ref name="The Last of the Jedi: Underworld">''[[The Last of the Jedi: Underworld]]''</ref>}}
Having been spread throughout [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]] by [[Freighter/Legends|freighters]] and cargo ships, '''duracrete slugs''' were large [[Silica parasite/Legends|silica parasite]] mollusks that dug tunnels into building foundations and fed off the [[Duracrete/Legends|duracrete]] therein, although indigestible concrete was also eaten. This was then excreted on to the skin, forming armor. Duracrete slugs were virtually blind, but had feelers capable of sensing rock layers. While their average length was three to five [[Meter/Legends|meters]], some specimens on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] were known to reach ten meters in length, especially in the [[Coruscant Underworld/Legends|Coruscant underlevels]].<ref name="Ep 2 Dictionary" /> They were very similar in appearance to the [[duracrete worm|duracrete worms]].

==Behind the scenes==
The species was created by concept sculptor [[Robert E. Barnes]] in collaboration with author [[David West Reynolds]] for the reference book [[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]].<ref name="Archive">{{SWArchive|url=eu/lit/ref/f20061031/index.html|text=Building a ''Complete Visual Dictionary''}}</ref>

According to Robert E. Barnes the duracrete slug is a "species-shift" from the [[duracrete worm]] in the [[The Wildlife of Star Wars: A Field Guide]] book by Terryl Whitlatch. With the main difference between the two being that instead of having a soft/slimy skin, the duracrete slug has an armored skin developed from consuming and excreting duracrete and is also bigger.<ref name="Archive" />
