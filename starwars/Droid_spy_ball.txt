'''Droid spy balls''' were small, spherical [[Spy|espionage]] [[droid]]s that could drill through walls and bug neighboring rooms for listening. Although they were highly illegal, some were used in the [[Galactic Senate]] in the waning [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
