'''DRX-7''' or '''DRX-752B''' was a [[protocol droid]] who served [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] on [[Cloud City]], [[Bespin]]. He was reprogrammed by a mysterious man to assassinate Lando. He was defeated by [[Kaasha Bateen]] and Lando.<ref name="Last Shot" />
