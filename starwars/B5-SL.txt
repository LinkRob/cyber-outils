'''B5-SL'''<ref name="Toy">{{DisneyToyCite|set=Droid Depot (toy line)|pack=Droid Factory Mystery Crate|altlink=https://www.endorexpress.net/site-news/2022/01/droid-factory-mystery-crate-series-1}}</ref> was a [[BB-series astromech droid]] with a black [[sensor]] and [[Color|blue]] and brown paneling.<ref name="GEDD" /> Like all BB-series units manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]], it stood 0.67 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="Stats" /> During [[First Order]]'s [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="GEDD" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> B5-SL spent [[time]] in a caged area of the [[Droid Depot]] in Batuu's [[Black Spire Outpost]]<ref name="GEDD" /> while the depot's owner, [[Mubo]], ran a diagnostic scan on the [[droid]].<ref name="GEStaff">{{GEStaff|Droid Depot|store}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
B5-SL appeared as an animatronic within the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="GEDD" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The droid is capable of lighting up and making beeping and booping noises while rolling around within the cage and turning and tilting its head back and forth.<ref name="GEDD" />
