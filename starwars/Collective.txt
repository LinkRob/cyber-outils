The '''Collective''' was an underground organization composed of [[engineer]]s, [[technician]]s, and [[scientist]]s who were determined to stop the spread of [[Fascism|authoritarianism]] via the use of technology. It used a [[Color|white]]-horned [[Snake|serpent]] as its symbol. The Collective operated on [[Corellia]], where they were supported by [[Nifera Shu]], [[Marriage|wife]] of the influential businessman [[Hasadar Shu]]. After the Collective received [[First Order]] data containing a list of targets and [[prisoner]]s from [[Monti Calay]], Nifera Shu put it up for auction.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />

The First Order raided the auction to retrieve that list, however the [[Resistance]] managed to rescue the auctioneer and escape along the other Resistance party with escape prisoners and stolen ships. After the rescue, the Collective let the Resistance pay for the list and then gave them coordinates to a Rendezvous Place to catch with Leia Organa's Resistance cell.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />
