The '''Fluggrians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] with [[Color|yellow]] [[eye]]s and green, purple, white and pink skin that was [[Homeworld|native]] to [[Ploo IV]]. [[Elan Mak]] was a Fluggrian [[podracer]] who was active on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Alien Archive" />

==Biology and appearance==
Fluggrians were a [[Sentience|sentient]]<ref name="AEYNTK" /> [[species]] of beings had round torsos and bulbous heads. Covered in [[Color|green]]-colored skin, Fluggrians possessed long, slender [[Limb|arms]] that ended in hands with three, clawed digits. Their arms also happened to have a row of spikes on the underside of their forearms that stretched from their elbows to their wrists. Similar knobs protruded from their foreheads. In addition to their green skin, Fluggrians had purple marks that stretched around their faces, and had pink, puffy lips.<ref name="TPM" />

==Society and culture==
By the time of the [[Invasion of Naboo]], the Fluggrians had integrated with the larger [[The galaxy|galactic]] community. At least one Fluggrian, [[Elan Mak]], pursued a career of [[podracing]].<ref name="AEYNTK" /><ref name="TPM" />

==Fluggrians in the galaxy==
The Fluggrians originated from a [[homeworld]] and eventually found their way into the galaxy at large.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> 

Elan Mak was a [[podracer]] [[pilot]] who originated from the [[planet]] [[Ploo IV]]. During the Invasion of Naboo, Mak was present on the world of [[Tatooine]] to participate in the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] podrace. Piloting a predominantly white-colored podracer, Mak finished the race in fifth place, beating the event's favored racer, [[Sebulba]].<ref name="TPM" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:ElanMak_Concept.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Terryl Whitlatch conceptual artwork of a Fluggrian]]
Fluggrians were developed for the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. The overall look and design of the species was created by conceptual artist [[Terryl Whitlatch]]. A detailed sculpture was built based on Whitlatch's artwork, the species, represented by the character of Elan Mak, appeared in the film as a fully computer-rendered character who could be fleetingly glimpsed in long range shots of his podracer. For the film's DVD release, a deleted scene was included that contained a more detailed digital Fluggrian.
