A '''droid truck''' was a type of [[truck]] presumably operated by a [[droid]] or droids. When training a group of struggling new [[Imperial cadet]]s at the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Academy for Young Imperials]] on [[Lothal]], [[Lieutenant]] [[Currahee]] shouted at them to test their resolve, telling them that one of these trucks was waiting for them if they wanted to quit. Once, while traveling on [[Merei Spanjaf's jumpspeeder|her jumpspeeder]] to the [[Transportation Ministry]] on Lothal, [[Merei Spanjaf]] passed a slow-moving droid truck.<ref name="ranks" />
