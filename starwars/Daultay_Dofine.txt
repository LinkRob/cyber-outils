'''Daultay Dofine''' was a [[Neimoidian]] [[Captain]] of the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Droid Control Ship]] the ''[[Vuutun Palaa]]'' during the [[Invasion of Naboo]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|This scheme of yours has failed, Lord Sidious. The blockade is finished. We dare not go against the Jedi.|Daultay Dofine, to [[Darth Sidious]]|<ref name="tpm" />}}
A [[Gender|male]] [[Neimoidian]], Daultay Dofine made a career as a [[starship]] [[captain]] in the [[Trade Federation]],<ref name="DB DD" /> one of the wealthiest corporate organizations in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="DB TF">{{Databank|trade-federation|Trade Federation}}</ref>

In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> a mysterious and malevolent [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord]] of [[Sith]] called [[Darth Sidious]] conspired with the Federation, and Dofine became one of his three principal Neimoidian collaborators, along with [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]] and [[Settlement Officer]] [[Rune Haako]].<ref name="SWE Dofine">{{SWE|characters|daultaydofine|Daultay Dofine}}</ref> Sidious arranged for the Federation to blockade and [[Invasion of Naboo|invade]] the peaceful [[planet]] of [[Naboo]] in protest over increased taxation from the Senate.<ref name="tpm" /> According to the plan, Dofine was at the helm of the ''[[Saak'ak]]'', the [[Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter|''Lucrehulk-''class LH-3210 cargo freighter]] that served as the Federation's [[flagship]].<ref name="TPM Journey">''[[Star Wars Journeys: The Phantom Menace]]''</ref> The captain led a [[Standard week|week]]-long<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> blockade that left the fragile planet starved and ill-supplied.<ref name="SWE Dofine" />

Eventually, the [[Democracy|democratic]] union known as the [[Galactic Republic]] dispatched [[ambassador]]s to the ''Saak'ak'' in an attempt to resolve what looked like a mere trade dispute gone awry. However, when said ambassadors were revealed to be [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], Dofine panicked. Suddenly doubting Darth Sidious's plan, the Captain boldly told the Sith Lord that his scheme was finished if the [[Jedi]] were involved. Although he was indirectly verbally reprimanded by Sidious for speaking out of turn when the Viceroy was ordered to not have the Captain in the leader of the Sith's eyesight again, Dofine nevertheless remained in command of the blockade's forces for the remainder of the operation.<ref name="tpm" />

When the blockade was complete, the Neimoidians were ready to initiate the next phase of Sidious' plan: invading and occupying Naboo. In order to do so, Dofine transferred from the ''Saak'ak'' to the [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Droid Control Ship]] ''[[Vuutun Palaa]]'', which contained legions of [[B1-series battle droid|B1]] [[battle droid]]s.<ref name="SWE Dofine" /> It was he who gave the order to activate the ship's droids, unleashing a mechanical army on the surface of Naboo. Dofine later [[Death|perished]] aboard the vessel when it was blown up by [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="tpm" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Daultay_Dofine_Concept_Art.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Concept art of Daultay Dofine]]
The character of Daultay Dofine was conceived by director [[George Lucas]] for the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. In the film, he was played by [[Alan Ruscoe]] and voiced by [[Chris Sanders]].<ref name="tpm" /> In the revised rough draft of the film's script, Dofine was described as fat and short, although by production, the character was thin, much like the other Neimoidian characters.<ref name="db">{{DB|character|daultaydofine|Dofine, Daultay}}</ref>
