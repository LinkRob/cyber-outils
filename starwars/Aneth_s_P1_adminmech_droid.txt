The [[human]] [[Aneth]],<ref name="SagronaTeema" /> co-owner<ref name="Beela">{{Databank|beela|Beela}}</ref> of the [[Mina-Rau General Store]] on the [[planet]] [[Mina-Rau]],<ref name="Store">{{Databank|mina-rau-general-store|Mina-Rau General Store}}</ref> owned a shabby<ref name="HarvestAD">{{Andor|Harvest}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> '''P1 adminmech droid''' with [[Color|red]] and white plating.<ref name="SagronaTeema" /> In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="AndorDate">{{AndorDate|202}}</ref> the [[P1 adminmech droid|droid]] rolled round the store babbling while Aneth,<ref name="SagronaTeema" /> her [[Marriage|spouse]]<ref name="HarvestAD" /> [[Kellen]], and their<ref name="SagronaTeema" /> [[Gender|daughter]]<ref name="Beela" /> [[Beela]] unloaded a delivery of supplies.<ref name="SagronaTeema" />

The droid was later situated behind the store's counter when a [[Krole's contingent|group]] of [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] visited.<ref name="SagronaTeema" /> When the Imperials returned the next [[Local day|day]], the [[adminmech droid]] followed Aneth as far as the store's entryway when she exited to greet them, staying there as two [[stormtrooper]]s posted either side of the door and other Imperials entered.<ref name="Harvest">{{Andor|Harvest}}</ref>
