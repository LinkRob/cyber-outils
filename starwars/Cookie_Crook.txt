"'''Cookie Crook'''" was a [[human]] who sold a [[Wookiee Cookie/LEGO|Wookiee Cookie]] at the [[Festival of the Ancestors]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Pasaana]]. They possessed [[Color|light]] skin, wore brown [[clothing]], and [[Language|spoke]] [[Galactic Basic Standard|Basic]].<ref name="LEGO TSS" />

==Behind the scenes==
