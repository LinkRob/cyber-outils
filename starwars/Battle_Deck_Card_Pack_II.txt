The '''Battle Deck Card Pack II''' is an expansion for the tabletop game [[Star Wars: Legion|''Star Wars'' Legion]] by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. It will be released on [[March 20]], [[2026]], and contains 16 new Battle Cards.

==Publisher's summary==
''Objectives on the battlefield change as quickly as the tide of battle. Commanders need to think on their feet as battles change at a moment’s notice. This Battle Pack adds sixteen Battle Cards consisting of four new Primary Objectives Cards, four new Secondary Objectives Cards, and eight new Advantage cards, two each for Rebel Alliance, Galactic Empire, Separatist Alliance, and Galactic Republic.''
