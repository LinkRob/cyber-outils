In either [[230 BBY]] or [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> in the [[Standard day|days]] after<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] was [[Destruction|destroyed]] by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]], the criminal [[Hutt Clan]] '''annexed''' a number of [[star system]]s belonging to the Republic.<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}} ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]'' establishes the events of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'' (2023)]] occur concurrently to the events of ''Temptation of the Force'', therefore meaning they must take place in the same year.</ref> after learning the Nihil and [[Hutt]] Clan planned to meet, Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]]<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> [[Lina Soh]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> feared the two were about to form an alliance.<ref name="The High Republic 1" />

[[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]] subsequently contacted the crew of the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] ''[[Gios]]'' and informed them about the meeting. After Mann noted the Hutts had been expected to struggle to hold the systems they had annexed, Jedi Master [[Keeve Trennis]] began to consider the implications of the Nihil supporting the Hutt Clan. Before<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> she<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> could finish, however, [[Jedi]] [[Santar]] stated it would take them a long time to send the Hutts back to<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> the [[Hutt Space]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Nal Hutta]].<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> The crew of the ''Gios'' were ultimately able to [[Battle at Ballum|prevent]] an alliance being formed between the two.<ref name="The High Republic 2">[[The High Republic (2023) 2|''The High Republic'' (2023) 2]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Hutt Clan's annexation of Galactic Republic star systems was mentioned in the [[The High Republic (2023) 1|first issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']], released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]. The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 8]], 2023.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/110346/star_wars_the_high_republic_phase_iii_2023_1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic &lsqb;Phase III&rsqb; (2023) #1}}</ref>
