'''Draviil''' was a [[Tof]] who, alongside another member of the [[species]], kidnapped the [[Nagai]] [[Jesrit]] and the [[human]] [[Rynn Zenat]] on the [[planet]] [[Nagi]] in order to bring them to the [[Anzati]] [[Reyna Oskure]] on her request.<ref name="SW 8" /> During the [[Battle of Nagi]], Draviil and the Tof soldiers fought alongside Oskure and the rest of her forces. Draviil struck Jesrit, and threatened to kill her, but she kicked his leg and broke it, as he collapsed to the ground.<ref name="SW 10">[[Star Wars (2025) 10|''Star Wars'' (2025) 10]]</ref>
