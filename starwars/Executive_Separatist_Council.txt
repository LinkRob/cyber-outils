The '''Executive Separatist Council''',<ref name="HC 48" /> also known simply as the '''Separatist Council'''<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> or '''Executive Council''', was an executive governing body<ref name="MaskofFear">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> led by [[Count]] [[Dooku]] within the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. It was made up of a number of Separatist leaders from the various major powers that backed the Separatist cause during the [[Clone Wars]], including the [[Trade Federation]], the [[Techno Union]], the [[Corporate Alliance]], the [[Commerce Guild]], and the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]].<ref name="Episode II" /> The entire Council was betrayed by [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]], who had them [[Mission to Mustafar|slaughtered]] by his new [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on [[Mustafar]] at the end of the war in [[19 BBY]], bringing an end to the Confederacy leadership.<ref name="Episode III" />

==History==
{{Quote|The Banking Clan will sign your treaty.''"''<br />''"''Good, very good.|[[San Hill]] and Count Dooku|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
Before the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] was founded, [[Count]] [[Dooku]] of [[Serenno]] secretly allied with major [[The galaxy|galactic]] corporations, including the [[Techno Union]], [[Commerce Guild]], and [[Trade Federation]]. While joining the [[Count of Serenno|Count]] was a risk, becoming one of the Confederacy's founding corporations offered them special privileges, and the chief privilege was that the corporation would gain a seat on the [[Council]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

[[File:JourneysBeginnings-SepCouncil.png|thumb|left|250px|Dooku and the Council meet on Geonosis prior to the Clone Wars.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> the Council members were present on [[Geonosis]] and planned with Count Dooku to [[Clone Wars|combat]] the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Episode II" /> [[Senator]] [[Rogwa Wodrata]] attended the meeting as well.<ref name="OF84">{{GermanFanCite|84|Database}}</ref> By the [[time]] of their [[Geonosian accord|meeting]], the Trade Federation of [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]] had pledged their [[Trade Federation Droid Army|Droid Army]] to Dooku, and the other corporations decided to do the same.<ref name="Episode II" /> The Council provided funding, [[warship]]s, and droids to the CIS.<ref name="Passel Argente databank">{{Databank|passel-argente|Passel Argente}}</ref> Despite the corporate leaders making a business deal with Dooku, they were also willing to maintain deals with the Republic: to such companies, the coming Clone Wars were a chance to make money off of both sides.<ref name="Umbara Commentary">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Four|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season Four]], "Darkened World of Umbara" Commentary</ref>

During the Clone Wars, the Council acted as the secret group behind the civilian [[Separatist Senate]], which dealt with civil matters, with the Council dealing with military matters and funding. Its members were mostly fueled by self-interest and greed.<ref name="VE" /> The [[Battle of Coruscant]] in 19 BBY<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> saw the death of Count Dooku, which unbeknownst to them, was orchestrated by [[Darth Sidious]] in order to lure [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] to the Dark Side. Following the battle, the Council members were placed under the protection of [[General]] [[Grievous]]. Acting on orders from Lord Sidious, Grievous ordered the Council to relocate to the volcanic planet [[Mustafar]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The Council members left [[Utapau]] just before a [[Battle of Utapau|battle]] broke out on the planet between the Republic and the Confederacy, which resulted in Grievous' death at the hands of Skywalker's mentor [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].

Soon afterward, Sidious deceptively told them through a hologram that his new apprentice, Darth Vader, would take care of them, with them being unaware that he had really [[Mission to Mustafar|sent]] Vader to kill them because he had no further use for any of the Council members. The council was ultimately betrayed by Sidious and slaughtered by Vader. With the Republic transformed into the [[Galactic Empire]] and the Council wiped out, the Confederacy collapsed with the deactivation of its droid armies.<ref name="Episode III" />

==Powers==
The Executive Separatist Council was the advisory<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> and quasi-governing body of the Confederacy overseen by the [[Head of State]]-Count Dooku.<ref name="Episode III" /> Meanwhile, the [[Separatist Senate]] was the official legislative body<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> but was, in practice, mostly ignored by Dooku,<ref name="Crisis at the Heart">{{TCW|Crisis at the Heart}}</ref> who had been granted emergency wartime powers.<ref name="Rise" />

The Confederacy of Independent Systems had been born out of those who wanted a chance at self-determination away from the Republic.<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> One major criticism of the Republic among the Separatists was that it<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides">{{TCW|Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref> and its Supreme Chancellor had become puppets of corporate monopolies.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> While the Separatists prided themselves as being a real democracy who were not under the rule of those companies, even if they did business with them,<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" /> the establishment of the Executive Separatist Council<ref name="Rise" /> and the great power the corporations were awarded over all resulted in those companies ruling over the CIS. Fighters like [[Soujen Vak-Nhalis]] were forced to admit the reality of separatism involved agreeing to what he thought was a horrible deal to serve corporations, which he hoped, at the end of the day, would be worth it once the CIS won the war and its worlds could determine their own destiny.<ref name="MOF" />

==Members==
The council consisted of several Separatist leaders:

*[[Nute Gunray]]<ref name="DB Separatist Council">{{Databank|separatist-council|Separatist Council}}</ref> - [[Viceroy]] of the [[Trade Federation]] and leader of the Council<ref name="HC 56" />
*[[Wat Tambor]]<ref name="DB Separatist Council" /> - [[Foreman]] of the [[Techno Union]] and [[Emir of Ryloth]]
*[[Poggle the Lesser]]<ref name="DB Separatist Council" /> - [[Archduke]] of [[Stalgasin hive]]
*[[San Hill]]<ref name="DB Separatist Council" /> - [[Chairman]] of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]]
*[[Shu Mai]]<ref name="DB Separatist Council" /> - [[Presidente]] of the [[Commerce Guild]]
*[[Passel Argente]]<ref name="DB Separatist Council" /> - [[Magistrate]] of the [[Corporate Alliance]]
*[[Po Nudo]]<ref name="DB Separatist Council" /> - [[Senator]] of [[Ando]] and leader of the [[Hyper-Communications Cartel]]
*[[Rogwa Wodrata]]<ref name="Episode II" /> - a former Republic senator<ref name="OF84" />
*[[Tikkes]]<ref name="DB Separatist Council" /> - Senator of [[Mon Cala]] and the [[Quarren Isolation League]]

These leaders were also helped by various aides, including [[Rune Haako]], [[Cat Miin]], [[Sun Fac]], [[Denaria Kee]],<ref name="Episode II" /> [[Rute Gunnay]],<ref name="Episode III" /> an [[unidentified Purkoll citizen]],<ref name="A Distant Echo">{{TCW|A Distant Echo}}</ref> and [[Nank Tun]].<ref name="Episode III" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Because something that's really come to light in ''The Clone Wars'' series is that George sees it as Separatists, Republic, and then in the middle is big business. The corporations. ''[&hellip;]'' That all needs to come to light with Nute Gunray, Wat Tambor, San Hill, Passel Argente, all those guys and explaining who the Separatist Council is that you see in the films, versus the Separatist Senate, versus the Trade Federation and the guilds. The Commerce Guild, the Trade Federation, the Techno Union. That's why we have all those guys we've invented that have nothing to do with the Council that you see. Because in the EU, its always been seen as the Trade Federation are Separatists, but that's not true. Obi-Wan Kenobi witnessed a business deal going on. And in the business deal, he saw those corporate leaders telling Dooku they're going to sell him arms and sell him droids to build an army. That doesn't mean that those corporations don't also have deals with the Republic to give them arms, but it got blanketed in all the media that it was very black and white. But the Clone Wars is anything but black and white, as we know.|[[Dave Filoni]], on the relationship between the corporate leaders on the Separatist Council and the corporations themselves|<ref name="Umbara Commentary" />}}

Shot as a separate stage element at ILM, no still photography exists of the shoot. Kinda looks like an Aqualish maybe? Maybe I'll pick up that mystery again someday.|archivedate=20220607013621}}</ref>
