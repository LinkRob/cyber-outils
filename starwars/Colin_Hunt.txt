'''Colin Hunt''' is an English actor. In [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], he had uncredited roles that [[Jonathan W. Rinzler]] listed as "Wooof (Rancor Guard)" and "Rebel Ranger" in the [[2013]] reference book ''[[The Making of Return of the Jedi]]''. The book also includes a continuity photograph for scene 17 of Hunt in his [[rancor]] guard costume, along with [[Paul Brooke]] costumed as the [[Malakili|rancor keeper]] and [[Mark Hamill]] as [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="Making of" />
