'''Amberine''' was a type of material found on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[moon]] of [[Toshara]]. The material was used when making jewelry such as [[bracelet]]s, [[necklace]]s and [[ring]]s. It could also made into strands and woven into [[Amberwoven carpet|carpet]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]] had built a [[Imperial Forward Base|forward base]] on top of a structure of amberine on Toshara in order to signal control to the [[Tosharan]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 ABY,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[merqaal]] [[Nix]] led the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] to a carved chunk of amberine in [[Carver's Maze]] on Toshara.
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Amberine was created for the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The exact nature of the material wasn't stated in the game but was depicted in as coming from the ground and used to make jewelry.<ref name="Outlaws" />
