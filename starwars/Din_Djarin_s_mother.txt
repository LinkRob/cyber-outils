A [[human]] [[Gender|female]] was the '''mother''' of [[Din Djarin]]. Djarin's [[Parent|mother]] and [[Din Djarin's father|father]] escaped their home and tried to hide their son from the [[droid]]s. Djarin's parents were [[Death|killed]] after hiding their son, who was found by a [[B2-series super battle droid]]. However, Djarin was saved by a [[Mandalorian]] [[Unidentified Mandalorian warrior|warrior]], who took him in as a [[foundling]].<ref name="Chapter 8" /> Djarin later adopted his own foundling, [[Din Grogu]], as his son.<ref name="Chapter 24">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 24: The Return}}</ref>
