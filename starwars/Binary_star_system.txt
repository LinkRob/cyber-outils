A '''binary star system''' was a double [[Star system|solar system]] comprising two [[star]]s. Binary stars were also occasionally referred to as '''twin suns'''.<ref name="Visions and Voices">{{Rebels|Visions and Voices}}</ref> Such systems included the [[Tatoo system|Tatoo]],<ref name="Journey Through Space (2015)">[[Star Wars: Journey Through Space (2015)|''Star Wars: Journey Through Space'']]</ref> [[Montross system|Montross]],<ref name="TAE">{{Rebels|The Antilles Extraction}}</ref> [[Mon Calamari system|Mon Calamari]] systems,<ref name="DB Dac">{{Databank|mon-cala|Mon Cala}}</ref> [[Dalnan system]],<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> as well as the system that housed the [[planet]] [[Halcyon (planet)|Halcyon]].<ref name="Trkn">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> On one [[Unidentified hospitable planet|hospitable planet]], the presence of two suns ensured the world never turned to night,<ref name="ShadowsComeKran">{{InsiderCite|212|Galactic Tales: From The Shadows Come The Kran}}</ref> but there were other planets in binary systems that still possessed a day to night cycle.<ref name="Twin Suns">{{Rebels|Twin Suns (episode)}}</ref> On [[Dalna]], the two suns created such a bright day that off-worlders not used to it could start to feel [[sun sickness]].<ref name="Path of Deceit" />

In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] [[Skirmish on Dathomir|went]] with the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]] to his [[homeworld]], [[Dathomir]], to perform an ancient ritual in order to join their minds to gathering information from each other. They learned that the [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was hiding on a [[Tatooine|planet]] with twin suns and that he held the key to destroying the [[Sith]].<ref name="Visions and Voices" /> Maul eventually tracked Kenobi to [[Tatooine]] based on this knowledge, but was forced to [[Mission to Tatooine (Imperial Era)|lure]] Bridger there when he was unable to draw Kenobi out by himself. Kenobi easily [[Death|killed]] the weakened Maul in a final duel.<ref name="Twin Suns" />
