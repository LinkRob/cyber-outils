The '''Dromund Kaas system''' was a [[star system]] of the [[Sith Worlds]] within the [[Esstran sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] in [[the galaxy]]. The [[Star system|system]] was located in grid square R-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> It contained the [[planet]] [[Dromund Kaas]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dromund Kaas system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the article "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> The [[Dromund system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in Part 5 of ''[[The Dark Forces Saga]]'', which was released in the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[June 9]], [[2005]].<ref name="Wizards">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=starwars/article/sw20050609a|series=The Dark Forces Saga|text=Part 5: Two Peas in a Pod}}</ref>
