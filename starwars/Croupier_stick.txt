A '''croupier stick''' was a foot-long stick by used a croupier to move around [[coin]]s during games at [[casino]]s, such as in the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|Canto Casino]] of the coastal [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on [[Cantonica]]. The inner edge of the Canto Casino's croupier stick had magnetic strips to better gather the [[cantocoin]]s.<ref name="TLJ:TVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
