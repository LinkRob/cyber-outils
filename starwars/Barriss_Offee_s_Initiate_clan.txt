Not many [[Standard year|years]] before [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[A Jedi's Duty]]" are set immediately before the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[21 BBY]].</ref> the [[Mirialan]] [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Barriss Offee]] was a member of an '''Initiate clan''' within the [[Jedi Order]]. The [[Clan (Jedi)|clan]] was based at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="A Jedi's Duty" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]]. At one point, the clan wandered through the high archways and trimmed courtyards of the [[Jedi temple|Temple's]] [[Great Hall (Jedi Temple)|Great Hall]]. In the [[Local day|evenings]] while part of the clan, the Mirialan lingered on mezzanines so she could stare in wonder at Coruscant's horizon, which was full of [[skyscraper]]s and streams of [[Skylane|air traffic]].<ref name="A Jedi's Duty" />

By [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">"[[A Jedi's Duty]]" establishes that [[Barriss Offee]] had become a [[Padawan]] by the [[time]] of the [[First Battle of Geonosis|Battle of Geonosis]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]].</ref> Offee had become a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]].<ref name="A Jedi's Duty" /> In 21 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> as Offee walked through the Jedi Temple, she mentally recalled when her clan had wondered through the Great Hall, and noted that she had marveled at the giant pillars and grand statues within it.<ref name="A Jedi's Duty" />

==Behind the scenes==
Barriss Offee's Initiate clan was mentioned in "[[A Jedi's Duty]]," a short story written by [[Karen Strong]] and published in the [[2022]] anthology ''[[Stories of Jedi and Sith]]''.<ref name="A Jedi's Duty" />
