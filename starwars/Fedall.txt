'''Fedall''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Quarren]] who was the brother of the boatman [[Queeg]]. He sought revenge on the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]], whom he believed had killed his brother when he went aboard the boatman's since sunken [[boat]] on the [[moon]] [[Trask]]. Fedall confronted Djarin at the port with a number of Quarrens and threatened to [[Death|kill]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]]. However, Queeg's true killers arrived and killed Fedall and his comrades.

==Biography==
{{Quote|He didn't kill your brother. I did.|Bo-Katan Kryze, to Fedall|<ref name="Chapter 11" />}}
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[New Republic Era|reign]] of the [[New Republic]],<ref name="Chapter 11" /> Fedall<ref name="MandoVG fr">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'' {{Otherlang|fr}}</ref> was present on the [[Water|estuary]] [[moon]] of [[Trask]]. One [[Standard day|day]], his brother [[Queeg]] was [[Death|killed]] after taking the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] and his [[foundling]]<ref name="Chapter 11" /> [[Din Grogu|Grogu]]<ref name="Chapter 13">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 13: The Jedi}}</ref> aboard his [[boat]], which sank along with the entire crew. Believing that Djarin had killed his brother, Fedall, holding a [[spear]], confronted the Mandalorian and Grogu that night at the port, backed by a number of armed Quarren.<ref name="Chapter 11" />

Fedall accused Djarin of killing Queeg, but the Mandalorian simply asked for the Quarren to let him pass. Fedall ignored Djarin's request, however, and threatened to kill Grogu in revenge. That was when three other Mandalorians flew down on [[jetpack]]s to assist Djarin. The lead Mandalorian, [[Bo-Katan Kryze]], revealed that she had killed Queeg. She and her two comrades then fired on Fedall and his assailants, killing all of them. The Mandalorians departed the scene after.<ref name="Chapter 11" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Hey! You&hellip; You killed my brother.|Fedall confronts Din Djarin|<ref name="Chapter 11" />}}
Fedall was a Quarren male with [[Color|orange]] skin and blue [[eye]]s. Believing Din Djarin had killed his brother Queeg, he was insistent with the Mandalorian and wished to kill Grogu in revenge.<ref name="Chapter 11" />

==Equipment==
Fedall wore a green [[Jacket|coat]] with a brown [[belt]] and brown trousers and [[boot]]s. He wielded a spear and had white fabric wrapped around his wrists.<ref name="Chapter 11" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fedall appeared in "[[Chapter 11: The Heiress]]" of [[Jon Favreau]]'s [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', which was directed by [[Bryce Dallas Howard]]<ref name="Chapter 11" /> and released on [[November 13]], [[2020]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-november-2020|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in November 2020}}</ref> He was credited as "Quarren" in the subtitles.<ref name="Chapter 11" /> While the English version of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'' left the character unnamed,<ref name="MandoVG" /> the [[Wikipedia:French language|French]] version identifies him as Fedall.<ref name="MandoVG fr" /> The names Fedall and [[Queeg]] likely derive from the characters [[wikipedia:Fedallah|Fedallah]] and [[wikipedia:Queequeg|Queequeg]], harpooneers from [[wikipedia:Herman Melville|Herman Melville]]'s novel [[wikipedia:Moby-Dick|Moby-Dick; or, The Whale]].
