'''Crystallo''' was a game that could be [[Gambling|gambled]] on.<ref name="Shadow">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref> In [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> it was available to play at the tables in [[Sennifer's Beam and Balance]], a drinking and gambling establishment on [[Boxer Point Station]], a [[space station]] in the [[Janx system]].<ref name="Shadow" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crystallo was mentioned in the [[2022]] novel ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'', which was written by [[Adam Christopher]].<ref name="Shadow" />
