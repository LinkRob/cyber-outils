{{Quote|I was a regional administrator when you last saw me at the Berullian Checkpoint.|Prita Sven, to Lando Calrissian|<ref name="Last Shot">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref>}}
The '''Berullian Checkpoint''' was a location<ref name="Last Shot" /> in the [[Berullia system]] in the [[Quence sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> where [[Vice Grand Administrator]] [[Prita Sven]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] claimed she had met the [[smuggler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] while serving as a regional [[administrator]]. Calrissian, however, had not visited the Berullian Checkpoint, and had last seen Sven on<ref name="Last Shot" /> the [[moon]]<ref name="Pantora">{{Databank|pantora|Pantora}}</ref> [[Pantora]].<ref name="Last Shot" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Beckett's gang]] pulled off an operation at the Checkpoint, stealing Imperial supply ships for weeks in the Berullia system and causing three Imperial advisors to complain to the [[Emperor]], prompting [[General]] [[Ormeddon]] to be sent to resolve the issue.<ref name="Vandor">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]''</ref>
