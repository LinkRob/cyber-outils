'''Abediah Viess'''<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 4">[[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 4|''The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight'' 4]]</ref> was a [[Mirialan]]<ref name="The Blade 2" /> [[Gender|female]] who served as the [[general]]<ref name="THR show" /> of a [[mercenary]] [[army]]. 

==Biography==
===Siege of Firevale===
By [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The present day events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic — The Blade]]'' take place between the [[wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran]] and [[Porter Engle]] [[Siege of Bardotta|earning]] his title the "Blade of Bardotta," both of which are dated to [[382 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> Abediah Viess was leading a powerful mercenary force.<ref name="The Blade 2" /> She was contacted by [[Field Marshal]] [[Tozen]] of the [[Bethune army|Bethune military]] to provide backup in [[Bethune]]'s siege against its rival city-state [[Firevale]] on the planet [[Gansevor]].<ref name="The Blade 2" /> The siege was orchestrated by two scions of the Firevale and Bethune royal families, Prince [[Colden]] and Princess [[Sicatra]], who had just got married. They wished to use their marriage to provoke a [[Bethunian]] assault on Firevale in order to force Colden's mother, Queen [[Keraan]], to abdicate the throne in favor of Colden himself.<ref name="The Blade 4">[[The High Republic — The Blade 4|''The High Republic — The Blade'' 4]]</ref> Initially, Viess hid her fleet behind one of Gansevor's moons, but when the [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Porter Engle]] and [[Barash Silvain]] intervened to try and resolve the dispute, Tozen requested that she join his forces to expedite the siege.<ref name="The Blade 2" /> Viess obliged, but was almost immediately confronted by Engle, who had been sent to halt the attacks while Silvain worked to get to the bottom of the issues inside Firevale itself. Viess told Engle that she disliked his kind, but when Tozen agreed to pause his assault while Silvain located the 'abducted' Princess Sicatra she grudgingly ordered her soldiers to stand down alongside the Bethunian troops.<ref name="The Blade 3">[[The High Republic — The Blade 3|''The High Republic — The Blade'' 3]]</ref>

Later, as Viess and Tozen talked in the Bethunian command tent, they both agreed that the Jedi had to be killed in order for the original plan of installing Colden on the throne to work. Tozen promised Viess that he would give her and her soldiers twelve hours to plunder Firevale if she could kill Engle and Silvain for him, terms which the general gladly accepted. Viess immediately attempted to obliterate Engle, who had remained behind Bethunian lines, with a [[missile launcher]], but the expert duelist evaded her shot. She then ordered all batteries to open fire on Firevale's walls, recommencing the assault. As the guns pounded away at the walls, Viess confronted Engle with her [[beskar]] [[spear]], declaring herself to be a [[Jedi hunter|Jedi killer]]. The two clashed blades, but Engle was the superior duelist, disarming her and scarring her across the cheek. In response, Viess threw a [[grenade]] at both the Jedi and the Bethunian soldiers surrounding him, forcing Engle to deflect the blast while she made her escape.<ref name="The Blade 4" />

Angered, Viess returned to the command tent and ordered Tozen to target the buildings inside Firevale rather than just the walls. Tozen argued that such an act would destroy the city, that he needed its infrastructure to remain intact, but Viess declared that her soldiers could pick treasures out from among its ruins. She then had her men shoot and mortally wound Tozen, who managed to get a message to Sicatra in Firevale, warning her of what had happened, before he died. As Viess ordered the rate of fire to be doubled, the news of Tozen's death made Sicatra realize the folly of her and Colden's plan and, having confessed all to Silvain and Queen Keraan, she stood atop Firevale's walls and delivered a speech to the Bethunian soldiers below. Sicatra's words persuaded the Bethunians to turn against Viess and her mercenaries, who found themselves suddenly caught between their former comrades' [[blaster]]s and Porter Engle's [[lightsaber]]. Viess decided the venture was no longer profitable and ordered a general withdrawal, though not before taking one last shot at Engle from the back of her transport. An exhausted Engle was unawares of the incoming blast, but was ultimately saved by Silvain, who had returned to his side.<ref name="The Blade 4" />

===Joining with the Nihil===
After operating as a mercenary-for-hire for a [[Standard century|century]], Viess eagerly joined the [[Nihil]] when their [[Occlusion Zone]] engulfed ten [[sector]]s of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], including her world. She led [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]]'s newly established [[Ministry of Protection]], making use of her expertise in hunting Jedi.<ref name="Darkness" /> Her combat skills and brutality made her a formidable Nihil adversary to those who opposed her. Despite her boasts of being a Jedi killer, her old opponent Engle, continued to elude her<ref name="Databank" /> until they finally met again around [[229 BBY]].<ref name="Darkness" />

==Personality and traits==
Viess preferred the colors black and purple, using them in her armor and throughout her ship, the ''[[Auberge]]''.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />

==Skills and abilities==
Viess was a skilled and quick fighter even in her old age, she was also extremely charismatic and deceitful.<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 4" />

==Equipment==
General Viess' full suit of black armor had lightsaber-resistant [[beskar]] filigreed inlay, including on her [[vambrace]]s.<ref name="Darkness" /> She carried several Jedi lightsabers in her robe<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 4" /> and wielded different weapons.<ref name="Darkness" /> She had a beskar [[spear]],<ref name="The Blade 4" /> an old-fashioned beskar sword, a pair of long beskar daggers, and an ornately engraved blaster.<ref name="Darkness" /> She had several personal ships that she used during her time with the Nihil. The first was the battleship ''[[Foregone Catastrophe]]'' which was destroyed by Engle. The second was the ''[[Precipitate Fire]]'' which was blown up during the [[Assault on Naboo]]. Her third ship was the [[war cruiser]] ''Auberge'' taken from a [[spice dealer]].<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />
