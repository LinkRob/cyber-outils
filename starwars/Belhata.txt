'''Belhata''', addressed as '''Belhata the Hutt''', was a [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]] with a [[Gender/Legends|male]] personality who lived during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]. He employed the [[Quarren/Legends|Quarren]] [[Riljcha Voruste]]. At some point Belhata spent time on [[Bestine (city)/Legends|Bestine]] on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]], being involved with the local corrupt politicians.<ref name="AED" />

==Behind the scenes==
Belhata appears in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online roleplaying game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']]. According to these files, Belhata would have been in some way involved with the politicians of [[W:c:swg:Bestine|Bestine]]. However, Belhata is not known to have appeared on the [[Star Wars video games|video game]]'s live servers as a proper [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]]. Nevertheless, an emulated depiction pulled from this character's Interchange File Format reveals what he would have looked like if he were added to the game.<ref name="AED" />
