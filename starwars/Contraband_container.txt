'''Contraband containers''' were a type of long cylindrical storage container used by [[smuggler]]s to transport contraband.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] found one container aboard the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A contraband container appeared as an exclusive cosmetic item in the Ultimate Edition of the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, the container could be used to customize the appearance of the player's [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
