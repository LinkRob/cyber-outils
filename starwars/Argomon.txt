'''Argomon''' was a [[Gender|non-binary]] [[Nautolan]] [[pilot]] who operated as a [[black market]] [[smuggler]] alongside the [[droid]] [[B-333]]. By [[229 BBY]], they joined the droid [[5A-G3]]'s [[Eriadu droid intelligence squad|spy network]] to work against the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] in conjunction with the efforts of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] and the [[Jedi Order]]. That [[Standard year|year]], they helped [[transport]] the [[Bonbrak]]s [[Breebak]], [[Teetak]], and [[Tip]], as well as the droid [[V-18]], across the [[Stormwall]] [[hyperspace]] barrier to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]. During this time, part of Argomon's work included transporting supplies in their [[Argomon's hauler|hauler]] to the Nihil [[prison]] [[Starship|ship]] ''[[Korvix Vorn]]''. On one such occasion later in 229 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], Argomon and B-333 delivered boxes of the [[mycopram]] regenerative substance to the Nihil [[scientist]] [[Niv Drendow Apruk]], planting a [[Homing beacon|tracking device]] on the ''Korvix Vorn'' while there.

Not long afterward, in [[228 BBY]], the black market pilot participated in a [[Raid on the Korvix Vorn|raid]] on the prison ship after the tracking device pinpointed the vessel's location for the Jedi and the Republic, hoping to rescue the [[prisoner]]s trapped aboard and [[Saboteur|sabotage]] the vessel to destroy it. After helping a boarding party secure the prison ship's topside [[cannon]]s, Argomon and 5A-G3 rigged the ''Korvix Vorn'' to explode, fleeing from the vessel with B-333 after joining the rest of their colleagues and several rescued prisoners aboard their ship. After returning to the Outer Rim planet [[Eriadu]]'s [[Arkorayal Cabaret Cantina|Republic outpost]], Argomon enjoyed playing games with several of their colleagues. Later, Argomon attended the [[Mrala-Talisola Wedding|wedding]] of the [[Marriage|wives]] [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]] and [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]] and helped [[Battle of Eriadu|defend]] Eriadu from the invading Nihil as part of the [[Eriaduan resistance]]. The Nihil were ultimately defeated on Eriadu and collectively surrendered not long afterward, with Argomon joining a victory celebration.

==Biography==
===Black market pilot===
{{Quote|This obnoxious Nihil prison warden on the Korvix Vorn penal ship has been nagging me to come find out which of his prisoners is Jedi. ''[&hellip;]'' And their black market runners might be able to smuggle at least some Mycopram to me.|Niv Drendow Apruk about acquiring mycopram, which Argomon eventually smuggles to him|<ref name="Escape From Valo">''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]''</ref>}}
[[File:ArgomonB333-2023THRA3.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Argomon and B-333 worked on a black market hauler to transport goods to the Nihil.]]
The [[Gender|non-binary]]<ref name="Gender" /> [[Nautolan]] [[pilot]] Argomon lived during the [[High Republic Era]], working as a [[black market]] [[smuggler]] alongside the [[droid]] [[B-333]]. Following the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]] of the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]]<ref name="THRA 3" /> in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="THRCE">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Argomon began working for the droid [[5A-G3]]'s [[spy]] [[Eriadu droid intelligence squad|network]] against the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] following the group's formation of the [[Occlusion Zone]],<ref name="THRA 4" /> a region of [[Realspace|space]] sealed behind a seemingly impenetrable barrier called the [[Stormwall]] that only Nihil [[Starship|ships]] could pass through.<ref name="THRA 3" />

In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> Argomon [[transport]]ed the [[Bonbrak]]s [[Breebak]], [[Teetak]], and [[Tip]], as well as the droid [[V-18]], to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Valo]] to help the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] and his allies expel the Nihil from the planet. To sneak V-18 across the Stormwall successfully, the Bonbraks disassembled V-18 into several shipping boxes to store on the black market pilot's [[Argomon's hauler|hauler]] before eventually reassembling him upon arrival at a [[Unidentified Nihil warehouse|warehouse]]. Not long afterward, the Nihil on Valo were defeated during a [[Raid of the Innovator|raid]] of the research vessel ''[[Innovator]]'' while trying to retrieve the regenerative [[mycopram]] substance from the ship. As he departed the planet, the Nihil [[scientist]] [[Niv Drendow Apruk]] decided to seek mycopram instead from black market runners working for the Nihil [[prison]] ship ''[[Korvix Vorn]]''.<ref name="Escape From Valo" />

===Tracking the ''Korvix Vorn''===
{{Quote|Did you bring my ''mycopram'', hauler?''"<br />"''Sure did, sir! Eighteen boxes worth!''"<br />"''Just eighteen? What was even the point of returning with such a small load?''"<br />"''Prices and demand surged once that celebrity guy said he uses it and looks like a twelve-year-old!|Niv Drendow Apruk and Argomon on the ''Korvix Vorn''|<ref name="THRA 3" />}}
During this time, Argomon and B-333 continued to the Stormwall several times to make supply runs of illicit goods to the Nihil, operating under the guise of tolerating the marauders. One of the Nihil vessels<ref name="THRA 3" /> they<ref name="Pronouns" /> transported goods to was the ''Korvix Vorn'',<ref name="THRA 3" /> for which they were given an access card.<ref name="THRA 4" /> Later in 229 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date-THRA3">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 3|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 3]] takes place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[space station]]'s fall to [[230 BBY]], the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] must take place in [[229 BBY]].</ref> 5A-G3 instructed B-333 and Argomon to place a [[Homing beacon|tracking device]] on the ''Korvix Vorn'' to help forces of the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Jedi Order]] pinpoint the next time it left the Occlusion Zone. B-333 remained anxious about the endeavor, panicking as Argomon gleefully accelerated their hauler's speed. The pilot reassured the droid that the Nihil would always let them across the Stormwall, though the latter remained worried.<ref name="THRA 3" />

[[File:ArgomonKorvixVorn-2023THRA3.png|thumb|left|300px|Argomon delivered mycopram to Niv Drendow Apruk on the ''Korvix Vorn''.]]
After arriving on the ''Korvix Vorn'', Argomon delivered eighteen boxes of mycopram substance to Apruk. Though displeased with the quantity, the scientist accepted Argomon's reasoning that a [[Unidentified celebrity|celebrity's]] usage of mycopram had caused a price and demand surge. As the pilot began preparing to unload their cargo, they commented that Apruk's young [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creature&mdash;[[Deblindrix III]]&mdash;would require more than mycopram to survive, which caused Apruk to dismiss the former derisively.<ref name="THRA 3" /> After the pair successfully planted a tracking device on the vessel,<ref name="THRA 4" /> Argomon left B-333 behind on the ''Korvix Vorn'' as part of 5A-G3's eventual plan to [[Saboteur|sabotage]] the ship<ref name="THRA 5">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 5]]</ref> and returned to<ref name="THRA 4" /> the Outer Rim planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> Eriadu's [[Arkorayal Cabaret Cantina|Republic outpost]].<ref name="THRA 4" />

===Rescue endeavors===
{{Dialogue|Pevel Kronara|Based on info from a source who has been inside the prison&hellip;|Argomon|That's me, eh! I'm the source.|Pevel Kronara|&hellip;and passed along data to their handler here on Eriadu&hellip;|5A-G3|I'm their handler. You're welcome.|Pevel Kronara|May I continue?|5A-G3 and Argomon|Absolutely.|attr=Pevel Kronara, Argomon, and 5A-G3 on Eriadu|src=<ref name="THRA 4" />}}
[[File:KorvixVornBoarding-2023THRA5.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Argomon and their colleagues participated in a raid of the ''Korvix Vorn''.]]
Not long afterward,<ref name="THRA 4" /> in [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date-THRA4">{{StarlightDate|[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4]]|one [[Standard year|year]] and two weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> the tracking device on the ''Korvix Vorn'' revealed the vessel's location, which 5A-G3 forwarded news of from Argomon to the Jedi and the [[Republic Defense Coalition]]. Argomon attended a meeting with several Jedi on Eriadu that was spearheaded by the Republic Defense Coalition [[Admiral]]<ref name="THRA 4" /> [[Pevel Kronara]],<ref name="THRCE" /> who expounded on the black market pilot's contributions to their intel. After Kronara finished the briefing, those present left to prepare for their planned [[Raid on the Korvix Vorn|raid]] on the ''Korvix Vorn''.<ref name="THRA 4" /> Having assumed the mission objective included destroying the ''Korvix Vorn'' after rescuing its [[prisoner]]s, 5A-G3 and Argomon worked together to formulate a plan to explode the vessel and reunite with B-333.<ref name="THRA 5" />

Afterward, Argomon, Jedi Padawan [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]], the [[Mikkian]] operative [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]], and 5A-G3 boarded a ship and traveled to the prison ship's last known location. When the ''Korvix Vorn''{{'s}} [[blister bomb]]s stalled the approach of the Republic and Jedi, Argomon suggested to Talisola and Mrala that their access card would allow them aboard from the prison ship's [[Hangar|loading dock]]. Once aboard, the black market pilot and their colleagues rushed out of the ship and attacked the Nihil in the hangar with their [[weapon]]s.<ref name="THRA 4" /> Meanwhile, the operator of the ''Korvix Vorn'', a disgraced Nihil enforcer known as "[[The Warden]]," jettisoned the vessel's prisoners into space and left them to die. Argomon and their colleagues joined other Jedi as part of a boarding party and traveled toward the ''Korvix Vorn''{{'s}} lower [[cannon]]s. The group quickly redirected to deactivate the vessel's topside blister bomb armaments to assist the efforts of the Jedi and Republic Defense Coalition forces fighting in space outside. Argomon used their handheld weapon to attack the Nihil present, shooting at several enemy combatants.<ref name="THRA 5" />

[[File:5AG3ArgomonB333-2023THRA5.png|thumb|left|300px|5A-G3, Argomon, and B-333 after exploding the ''Korvix Vorn'']]
When several Jedi and Mrala began acquiring [[oxygen]] tanks to rescue the prisoners floating in space outside, Argomon and 5A-G3 separated from the others and promised to meet them back on their ship. The pair collaborated to sabotage the ''Korvix Vorn'' and rig it to detonate, with Argomon changing into a [[Environment suit|spacesuit]] for the job. During their efforts, they reunited B-333 before fleeing the vessel. Afterward, they joined Mrala, Talisola, and several others aboard the group's ship. As they flew away, the vessel exploded in [[Fire|flames]], much to the shock of the Jedi and rescued prisoners aboard. 5A-G3 informed those present that their trio had always intended to destroy the ''Korvix Vorn'', leading everyone to sigh in relief and hug each other. Argomon returned to Eriadu with their colleagues and rescued prisoners, grateful to be headed home.<ref name="THRA 5" />

===Sports and blockades===
{{Dialogue|Alys Ongwa|That shipment we brought is dwindling. We'll need to attack another convoy soon.|5A-G3|Which will be tricky because the blockade on Bri-Phrang City is fully operational.|Qort|And still no communication from the Republic, thanks to the Stormwall tech.|Argomon|And there are both Nihil and some ''Eriadu security ships'' taking part.|attr=Argomon and their colleagues discussing the Nihil's Eriadu invasion|src=<ref name="THRA 10">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 10|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 10]]</ref>}}
[[File:EriaduBaseGames-2023THRA6.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Argomon enjoyed playing games with their Eriadu colleagues.]]
After returning to Eriadu, Argomon joined several Jedi and Republic colleagues for a game involving scoring pucks into illuminated goals, wearing a red [[Clothing|jersey]] for their team. However,<ref name="THRA 6">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 6|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 6]]</ref> [[Jedi Master]]<ref name="Escape From Valo" /> [[Yoda]] contacted the group via hologram and informed them they needed to evacuate because the Stormwall had [[Expansion of the Stormwall|expanded]] and left Eriadu at the border of Republic space. Moments after, the [[Padawan Academic Cruiser]] ''[[Star Hopper]]''&mdash;commandeered by the Warden&mdash;arrived in Eriadu's [[atmosphere]] for an attack to scare those at the Republic outpost. Argomon stared at the ''Star Hopper'' from Eriadu's surface, watching as Talisola led several others in an attempt to reclaim the vessel, which had formerly been in the Jedi Order's service. However, when their attempt was ultimately unsuccessful, Argomon joined the returning Jedi and 5A-G3 for another round of their game.<ref name="THRA 6" />

Following this, the Occlusion Zone expanded again, enveloping Eriadu,<ref name="THRA 9">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 9|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 9]]</ref> and Argomon was present on Eriadu with their colleagues during this time.<ref name="THRA 10" /> The Nihil's subsequent [[Invasion of Eriadu|invasion]] of the world<ref name="THRA 9" /> and blockade around Bri Phrang City caused severe supply shortages, only temporarily alleviated by a shipment of supplies brought to the city by the operative [[Alys Ongwa]]'s [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection|crew]].<ref name="Crash Burn">''[[The High Republic Adventures: Crash and Burn]]''</ref> As their resources dwindled, Argomon attended a meeting with their allies to discuss their group's next move against the occupiers. Though Ongwa indicated they would need to attack another convoy soon, 5A-G3 expressed that it would be difficult given the strength of the blockade around the city. Argomon responded to them by noting that the blockade was held in place by both the Nihil and some Eriadu security ships, putting emphasis on the participation of the latter.<ref name="THRA 10" />

===Nuptials and Eriadu defense===
{{Quote|The people of Eriadu are rising against the Nihil! Their fight is our fight! Their freedom is our freedom! The tide of history sweeps around us. We must move with it as one, and when we rise&hellip; ''We rise together!!|Farzala Tarabal rallying his allies, including 5A-G3, as they repelled the Nihil on Eriadu|<ref name="THRA 14" />}}
[[File:EriaduDefensesStreetFight-2023THRA14.png|thumb|left|300px|Argomon and their allies helped defend Bri-Phrang City from Nihil invaders.]]
Not long afterward, Argomon attended the [[Mrala-Talisola Wedding|wedding]] of Mrala and Talisola on Eriadu. Prior to the ceremony, they helped the soon-to-be-wed pair break into the [[Tarkin family]]'s hotel suite at the Arkorayal and was hit by a blaster shot to the wrist. As Mrala and Talisola spoke to the family's leader&mdash;[[Sevran Tarkin]]&mdash;Argomon stood to the side and conversed with the [[Pirate Queen|pirate queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] and Jedi Master [[Kantam Sy]]. During the ceremony, as the wives were pronounced spouses, Argomon cheered for the pair. The attendees&mdash;including Argomon&mdash;celebrated the couple's nuptials briefly before leaving the Arkorayal Cantina to [[Battle of Eriadu|fend off]] the Nihil invading the planet.<ref name="THRA WS">''[[The High Republic Adventures Phase III - The Wedding Spectacular]]''</ref>

In the streets of Bri-Phrang City, Argomon joined their colleagues as part of the [[Eriaduan resistance]] as they engaged in combat between the buildings lining the city's streets. Qort rallied the group of fighters, giving them instructions on where to move next as part of their plan to repel the Nihil. To cross the nearby [[Water|bay]], Argomon piloted a rescue speeder, which Qort rode on the back of. As Argomon continued steering their vessel, Qort attacked a Nihil [[gunboat]], with their speeder speeding away as the gunboat exploded. Though Qort was eventually injured in the ensuing melee, Argomon safely made it aboard another Nihil gunboat. Tarabal continued to rally the remaining fighters, including Argomon, to continue resisting the Nihil.<ref name="THRA 14" />

Later, Argomon joined several of their colleagues&mdash;including B-333, [[Tarkin family]] member [[Emelsine Tarkin]], 5A-G3, and a [[Unidentified Tarkin family staffer|staffer]]&mdash;in a building in Bri-Phrang City. As the fighting raged on, they strategized their next move after being informed that the Jedi Master [[Yaddle]] needed more Jedi assistance to stabilize the [[lava reactor core]] under the city's streets.<ref name="THRA 15">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 15|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 15]]</ref> Argomon and B-333 eventually boarded a vessel and kept combatting the Nihil, informing Tarabal of their efforts' limited effects. Knowing the Warden would not stop his assault until Eriadu was destroyed, he ordered Argomon to work with Jedi Master [[Tera Sinube]] to clear a path for Mrala and Talisola to attack.<ref name="THRA 18">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 18|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 18]]</ref> Not long after, the Eriaduan resistance defeated the Nihil on Eriadu and the Nihil collectively surrendered to the Republic not long afterward.<ref name="Trials">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref> On Eriadu, Argomon and their colleagues celebrated their victory, while also taking time to reminisce on the allies who had lost their lives during the Nihil conflict.<ref name="THRA 20">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 20|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 20]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Between you and me, I think it's gonna take more than some wrinkle cream to cure whatever's going on with your little dog there.''"<br />"''Stop speaking to me.|Argomon and Niv Drendow Apruk|<ref name="THRA 3" />}}
[[File:ArgomonZeen-2023THRA4.jpg|thumb|right|340px|Argomon and their colleagues]]
By 229 BBY,<ref name="Date-THRA3" /> Argomon was willing to work against the Nihil alongside B-333,<ref name="THRA 3" /> disliking individuals that operated alongside the marauders.<ref name="THRA 10" /> They enjoyed teasing B-333 when the droid worried about their endeavors, assuring the droid that everything would work out. Argomon felt that Apruk's attempts to use mycopram on young Nameless creatures were fruitless, though the scientist did not appreciate their opinion.<ref name="THRA 3" />

During a meeting on Eriadu, Argomon felt inclined to point out to those present that they were the contact who had helped locate the ''Korvix Vorn'', though they quickly allowed Kronara to continue with the briefing.<ref name="THRA 4" /> The pilot and 5A-G3 were proud when they destroyed the Nihil prison ship, having assumed it was part of their original mission objective.<ref name="THRA 5" /> Argomon was competitive, participating alongside several of their colleagues in sport games on Eriadu.<ref name="THRA 6" /> The pilot had spotted [[Color|green]] skin and black [[eye]]s.<ref name="THRA 4" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|Brace for hyperspace, ''babyyy''!|Argomon piloting their hauler|<ref name="THRA 3" />}}
During the [[Nihil conflict]], Argomon was a skilled pilot. They operated successfully as a member of a spy network to aid the Jedi and the Republic against the Nihil, fooling the marauders into believing they were only a black market smuggler.<ref name="THRA 3" /> Argomon was capable of memorizing small details about places they had visited, allowing them to aid Talisola and Mrala by helping them navigate the ''Korvix Vorn''. They were able to wield a handheld weapon<ref name="THRA 4" /> to attack several Nihil and successfully helped detonate the ''Korvix Vorn'' alongside 5A-G3.<ref name="THRA 5" /> Argomon was able to a rescue speeder during the Eriadu invasion and also participate in heated combat against the Nihil on several occasions.<ref name="THRA 14" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|You know&hellip;the loading dock is right there below the laboratory, jutting out of that central tower&hellip;and I do still have my access card to get in.|Argomon, to Zeen Mrala and Tani Briwill as their ship approached the ''Korvix Vorn''|<ref name="THRA 4" />}}
[[File:ArgomonsHauler-2023THRA3.png|thumb|left|230px|Argomon's hauler]]
While operating their hauler as part of 5A-G3's network, Argomon wore a purple [[jumpsuit]] paired with gray [[boot]]s<ref name="THRA 4" /> and silver knee pads.<ref name="THRA 10" /> Over the jumpsuit, they donned a light brown vest, a turquoise sash around their waist, and a decorative turquoise pendant around their neck. Argomon also opted to keep pale cloth wrappings on their [[lekku]] and on both [[Limb|arms]]. At some point, the Nihil gave the black market pilot an access card for the ''Korvix Vorn'' due to their work delivering cargo for the marauders. Argomon also owned at least one handheld [[blaster]], which they used during the Nihil prison ship raid.<ref name="THRA 4" />

While sabotaging the ''Korvix Vorn'' with 5A-G3, Argomon donned a gray spacesuit featuring a transparent [[helmet]].<ref name="THRA 5" /> On Eriadu, they wore a red jersey marking them as a member of the red team during a game of sport with their colleagues.<ref name="THRA 6" /> During Mrala and Talisola's wedding, Argomon donned a collared, orange-colored jacket, a purple shirt, a pair of light tan pants, a turquoise bowtie, a brown belt with a silver-colored buckle, and brown shoes. They paired the outfit with turquoise wrappings around their lekku.<ref name="THRA WS" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Argomon uses any pronouns but mostly they/them.|Daniel José Older|<ref name="Pronouns" />}}
[[File:TheHighRepublicAdventures2023-3-coverA.jpg|thumb|right|210px|Argomon debuted in the third issue of the 2023 ''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'' comic series.]]
Argomon was first indirectly mentioned, albeit unidentified, in [[The High Republic: Escape from Valo|''Escape from Valo'']], a junior novel written by [[Daniel José Older]] and [[Alyssa Wong]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Escape From Valo" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]] that was published on [[January 30]], [[2024]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref> The smuggler then was first identified and made their first appearance in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 3|third issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']]. The issue was written by Older, illustrated by [[Harvey Tolibao]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the ''Star Wars: The High Republic''{{'s}}<ref name="THRA 3" /> Phase III on [[February 14]], 2024.<ref name="DH3">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-794/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-3|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #3}}</ref>

The same day as the third issue's release, Older revealed on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] that Argomon was [[Wikipedia:Non-binary gender|non-binary]] and [[Wikipedia:Pansexuality|pansexual]].<ref name="Gender" /> The author also specified that the pilot uses any pronouns&mdash;though mostly "they/them"<ref name="Pronouns" />&mdash;which was later reflected in ''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures''{{'s}} [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|fourth issue]], which was written by Older and released by Dark Horse<ref name="THRA 4" /> on [[March 13]], 2024.<ref name="DH4">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-796/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-4|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #4}}</ref> The detailing of the top half of Argomon's jumpsuit slightly differs between issues three<ref name="THRA 3" /> and subsequent issues in the series<ref name="THRA 4" /><ref name="THRA 5" /><ref name="THRA 6" /> with the third issue depicting a striped purple and light brown style<ref name="THRA 3" /> and others showing a solid purple jumpsuit with a light brown vest on top. This article reflects the depiction of their clothing in later issues of ''The High Republic Adventures'' to reflect the most recent sources.<ref name="THRA 4" /><ref name="THRA 5" /><ref name="THRA 6" />

[[Kristin Baver]]'s [[2025]] reference book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]'' included one of Tolibao's unfinished panels from<ref name="Artbook">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref> the one-shot comic ''[[The High Republic Adventures Phase III - The Wedding Spectacular]]''<ref name="THRA WS" /> that included Argomon.<ref name="Artbook" /> ''The Wedding Spectacular'' was written by Older and released by Dark Horse<ref name="THRA WS" /> on [[December 18]], 2024.<ref name="Dark Horse-Wedding">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-812/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III--The-Wedding-Spectacular|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III--The Wedding Spectacular}}</ref>
