{{Quote|They speak many dialects but rely upon a common language that evolved on ''[sic]'' Delphidian Cluster to allow for peaceful trade.|An artist's journal entry on the Delphidians|<ref name="AlienArchive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref>}}
The '''Delphidian Cluster''' was a [[stellar nursery]] located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]] near [[Wild Space]].<ref name="AotG" /> It was situated in grid square I-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Timelines" /> The [[star cluster]] was home to the group of related [[species]] known as the [[Delphidian]]s, who individually spoke a large number of dialects but also developed a common [[trade language]],<ref name="AotG" /> [[Delphidian (language)|Delphidian]].<ref name="Language">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Language in Star Wars Movies|video=CZp8Civk2co|nolive=1}}</ref>

[[Standard century|Centuries]] before the Delphidian Cluster was discovered by the [[Galactic Republic]], the Delphidians were a part of a [[Delphidian brawling confederation|brawling confederation]].<ref name="AotG" /> The Delphidian [[pirate]] [[Sidon Ithano]] hailed from the cluster.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> The Delphidian Cluster was eventually mentioned in an entry of an [[Unidentified artist|artist's]] [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]] dedicated to the Delphidians. The journal was eventually restored, expanded, and displayed at the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="AlienArchive" /> at some point no earlier than [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Delphidian Cluster was introduced in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]'' by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="AotG" />
