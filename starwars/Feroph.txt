'''Feroph''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/rp Reaper attack lander]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] who held the rank of [[captain]]. Feroph had been involved at the [[Mission to Eadu]], flying through the [[planet]]'s turbulent storms as the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] attacked the [[Eadu Energy Conversion Laboratory]].<ref name="X-Wing" />

==Behind the scenes==
Captain Feroph is a character in the ''[[TIE Reaper Expansion Pack]]'' of ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Miniatures Game]]'' by [[Fantasy Flight Games]]. According to the artist for his card, [[Anthony Devine]], the image depicting the character originally depicted Feroph simply flying away from the exploding Eadu Energy Conversion Laboratory, but it had been deemed too boring, so he added in various TIEs and X-Wings dogfighting and resubmitted it, citing it changed the overall story slightly.<ref name="Tumblr preview announcement">{{Tumblr|subdomain=anthonydevineart|url=post/173457242969/the-preview-for-the-tie-reaper-expansion-pack-is|name=Anthony Devine Art|text=The preview for the TIE Reaper Expansion Pack is up|quote=Originally, the brief required only the Reaper flying away from the exploding base, but I thought that was quite boring when I was working on it, so I added in the X-Wings & TIE Fighters later on & resubmitted as a suggestion which was approved :) It changed the story slightly, but it looks cooler}}</ref>
