'''Dewflower''' was an [[acklay]] who lived in the [[Lonisa City Zoo]] on the [[planet]] [[Valo]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="EFV" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> the [[Alcedian]] [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Initiate|Youngling]] [[Tep Tep]] befriended Dewflower's [[Puffball's pack|pack]] after she and the other Younglings [[Kildo]] and [[Gavi Takitaken|Gavi]] had to hide in the Zoo from the [[Nihil]]. Tep Tep later introduced the Acklay packmates [[Blueblossom (acklay)|Blueblossom]], Dewflower [[Posypetal]], [[Moonbloom]] and [[Puffball (acklay)|Puffball]] to [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]].<ref name="EFV" />
