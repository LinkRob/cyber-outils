'''Drazkel''' was the [[homeworld]] of [[Lady]] [[Xev Xrexus]], leader of the [[Xrexus Cartel]], a collection of semi-legitimate [[droid]] manufacturing firms from the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> It was [[orbit]]ed by the [[moon of Drazkel]]<ref name="Darth Maul 4" /> and was located in the [[Drazkel system]] in the [[Oriolan sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]], in grid square J-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
