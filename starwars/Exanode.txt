The '''exanode''' was a unit of information. The [[data-tape]]s used by the [[Galactic Empire]] on [[Scarif]] had a 512-million-exanode capacity.<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> After the [[Battle of Jakku|fall]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[New Republic Intelligence]] was given access to trillions of exanodes of Imperial files.<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>
