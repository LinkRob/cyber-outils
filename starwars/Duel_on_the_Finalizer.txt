During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] had a '''duel''' aboard his [[flagship]] the ''[[Finalizer]]'' with the new [[Ren|leader]] of the [[Knights of Ren]], [[Tava Ren]]. While the fight was halted as Tava proposed a truce so they could get to know each other, their meeting ended with the new Ren narrowly escaping the ship.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|So the minute it gets a little hot, you run, leave your leader behind? You're the Knights of Ren. The shadow made flesh. What happened to you?|Tava Ren, to her knights|<ref name="LoV 8" />}}
Following a failed [[Raid on Vandor|mission]] in the cold planet of [[Vandor]], where the [[Knights of Ren]] abandoned their new leader [[Tava Ren]], leaving her to fight an army of [[Monk]]s, Tava managed to escape and reunite with her knights at the ''[[Night Buzzard]]''. Tava chastised them for their cowardice and threatened them with her lightsaber, wondering what happened to them, which prompted [[Ap'lek]] to mention [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], their old leader, and how he [[Assassination of an Inner Rim warlord|abandoned]] them in favor of [[Snoke]] and the [[First Order]].<ref name="LoV 8" />

==The duel==
===Fight of the Rens===
{{Quote|This is more fun than you've had in ages, isn't it?|Tava Ren, to Kylo Ren|<ref name="LoV 9" />}}
[[File:KyloAttacksTava-LegacyOfVader9.jpg|thumb|left|270px|Rejecting Tava's offer of service, Kylo Ren attacks the new leader of his former knights.]]
[[Tava Ren]] ordered the ''[[Night Buzzard]]'' to make his way to [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]'s flagship the ''[[Finalizer]]'', requesting boarding clearance by transmitting a tightband communications code that had been exclusively used by [[Snoke]] in the past. Informed of the incoming arrival by a [[First Order]] officer while speaking with [[Armitage Hux|Hux]], Kylo Ren allowed the ship to land once he realized it was the Night Buzzard, saying some old friends had come to pay him a visit. As the [[Supreme Leader]] went to  personally greet the ship as it landed, he was surprise when, instead of one of his old followers, a new dark-side user came out of the ''Night Buzzard''. After the woman introduced herself as Tava Ren, she knelt down before Kylo and said that, as a [[Knights of Ren|Knight of Ren]], she was at his service.<ref name="LoV 8" /> While this was a lie, as Tava was hoping to find a way into his good grace so she could kill him, it backfired as the Supreme Leader immediately ignited his [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|lightsaber]] and attacked her after getting confirmation that she was a Knight of Ren.<ref name="LoV 9" />

As Tava narrowly dodged the Supreme Leader's initial strike, she was quick to try to disarm the situation by telling Kylo that he didn't have to kill her, but the former leader of the knights continued in his relentless assault, leading Tava to keep dodging her strikes before pulling out her own lightsaber and igniting it, saying that fighting was still a fine way to get to know each other. As the two knights clashed their sabers, Tava kept trying to play with Kylo's curiosity, but the Supreme Leader wasn't interested at all on why she had come to him and just wanted her dead. As a First Order officer ordered a [[legion]] of [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]]s to help their leader by shooting Tava down, Kylo used the force to push his own army away, ordering them to leave the fight to him. Tava was quick to point out to Kylo that he was having fun during this fight, making the Supreme Leader recall [[Battle of Oetchi|fighting]] alongside [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] against Snoke's [[Praetorian Guard|guards]].<ref name="LoV 9" />
[[File:KyloDefeatsTava-LegacyOfVader9.jpg|thumb|right|350px|Following a duel where Tava questioned the Supreme Leader's intentions, Kylo defeated the new Ren.]]
Kylo Ren told Tava that the Knights of Ren meant nothing to him, but she rebutted by questioning why was he still carrying their name if that was the case. She mentioned how the other knights were terrified of him, but she didn't see it. Just as she was about to attack him, Kylo disarmed her in one strike, proving his superiority and grabbing her own saber with the force. The Supreme Leader held both blades in a scissor position around Tava's neck and intended to kill her, but the new Ren offered a change to talk, as there were things only they could discuss as practitioners of the shadow, saying this was a unique opportunity to have a conversation like that. While this convinced Kylo to spare her for the moment, the Supreme Leader told Tava to remember how easy it was for him to disarm and almost kill her.<ref name="LoV 9" />

===Uneasy conversation===
{{Quote|You could have killed me today. But you didn't. Why? Because you miss this. The knights. The freedom. They're waiting for you, you know. You just have to&hellip; take them.|Tava Ren, to Kylo Ren|<ref name="LoV 9" />}}
[[File:MeetingOfRens-LegacyOfVader9.jpg|280px|left|thumb|The Supreme Leader decided to spare Tava and the two had a discussion about the knights as well as their desires.]]
Accepting her offer, the two Rens sat at a table and had a meal inside the ''Finalizer'' while they discussed personal matters. Kylo asked Tava where the knights were, and Tava said that they were too afraid to come with her, something that even the Supreme Leader admitted was smart of them. Before unveiling more information, Tava tried to assert to Kylo the notion that she was a Ren, the rightful leader of the knights, but the Supreme Leader was more interested in how and why she took control of the group, being surprised at the fact that they were even alive in the first place since the last time he saw them.<ref name="LoV 9" />

Tava revealed to Kylo how the knights were shattered and stuck together in an attempt to survive, ultimately needing to take a new job as muscle for the gang leader [[Bedat]], shaking down shopkeepers for protection money. She recounted how one day, after the knights mistakenly killed a person they weren't supposed to during a mission, Bedat locked them up and prepared them for execution, but they were rescued by Tava, who was also one of Bedat's enforcers at the time, becoming the new Ren in the process and leading the knights from then on.<ref name="LoV 9" />
[[File:TavaKissesKylo-LegacyOfVader9.jpg|250px|right|thumb|Believing she knows what the Supreme Leader really wants, Tava finished her conversation with a kiss.]]
The Supreme Leader was still interested in knowing why she came to him in the first place saying she will serve him, to which Tava responded by saying that it was a lie and that she originally planned to kill him, using the offer of servitude as bait, something that amused Kylo. When questioned about how foolish her plan was by the Supreme Leader, Tava simply resorted to saying that he was fascinated by her and that they both understood each other better than anyone through the shadow.  Believing him to not be happy as the Supreme Leader, Tava told Kylo that he missed the Knights of Ren and the freedom he had with them before joining the First Order. She attempted take him back into the Knights, finishing her conversation by giving him a kiss.<ref name="LoV 9" />

===Escaping the flagship===
{{Quote|For the Shadow. For the Ren!|Tava Ren|<ref name="LoV 9" />}}
[[File:TavaPullsNightBuzzard-LegacyOfVader9.jpg|250px|thumb|left|During her escape, Tava used the shadow to crush a legion of stormtroopers with the ''Night Buzzard''.]]
Kylo quickly leaned back after the kiss and proceeded to alert his ship's security forces about Tava, marking her as a target and an intruder on the ''Finalizer'', effectively rejecting her offer. Before escaping, Tava made it clear to the Supreme Leader that she was still right about everything she said, despite Kylo's attempt of masking his desires. In her attempt to reach the hangar, Tava encountered a huge number of stormtroopers blasting her, but Ren quickly deflected and sliced her opposition despite being pinned down by the troopers.<ref name="LoV 9" />

After killing the group, Tava reached the hangar, but saw a legion of stormtroopers waiting for her in front of the ''Night Buzzard''. Before the legion could fire, however, Tava reached deep into the shadow and pulled the ship closer to her, making it crush the stormtrooper legion in the process. Once she entered the ''Night Buzzard'', Tava quickly took off and left the ''Finalizer''. Before the ship could enter hyperspace, General Hux informed Kylo that they had a lock on it and could blast it down, but the Supreme Leader remained silent, allowing Tava to escape his gasp.<ref name="LoV 9" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|The only interesting thing you did was when you let that woman go--the one who inherited the Knights of Ren. Because that was a choice for you--not some foolish attempt to do something Vader would have done. Or Luke, or your mother&hellip; or your father.|Ben Solo, to Kylo Ren|<ref name="LoV 12" />}}
Following his confrontation with [[Tava Ren]], [[Kylo Ren]] returned to [[Mustafar]] after his initial [[Mission to Mustafar (Kylo Ren)|mission]] there in an attempt to discover the secret of his [[Anakin Skywalker|grandfather]]'s power hidden within the vault of his [[Fortress Vader|castle]]. After approaching Darth Vader's vault, Kylo Ren was met with what appeared to be the specter of his grandfather before it revealed itself to be a vision of his [[Jedi]] self, who urged him to make his own choices and see himself for how he truly was. During their confrontation, Solo's specter mentioned how during his journey to learn about Vader's past and power, the only choice Ren had made for himself was letting Tava Ren go, something that was not an attempt to imitate Vader or anyone else.<ref name="LoV 12">[[Legacy of Vader 8|''Legacy of Vader'' 12]]</ref>
