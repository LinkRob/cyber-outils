'''becker&mayer!''' is a book-packaging company that produces specialized book products for adults and children. Their books often incorporate innovative features such as deluxe presentation formats with electronics and audio. Many of the book products they create are released through other publishing companies.

Started in the 1980s, becker&mayer! was once part of the McEvoy Group, which also owns [[Chronicle Books]]. becker&mayer! was acquired in 2016 by [[Quarto Group]]'s US subsidiary, Quarto Publishing Group USA.<ref name="acquisition">{{Quarto|url=news/530/The-Quarto-Group-Acquires-becker-mayer.html|text=The Quarto Group Acquires becker&mayer}}</ref>
