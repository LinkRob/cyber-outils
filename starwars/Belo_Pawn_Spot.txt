'''Belo Pawn Spot''' was a pawn shop owned by the [[Pantoran]] [[Belo Roovin]] in the city of [[Mirogana]] on the [[planet]] [[Toshara]] in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Belo's pawn shop was first mentioned in a behind-the-scenes ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' trailer by [[Ubisoft]]. It was written in [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="BTS">{{UbisoftYouTube|BxyqrPk51KM|Star Wars Outlaws: Behind The Scenes &ndash; Navigating the Underworld}}</ref> The shop's first appearance was in [[August]] of [[2024]] in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''Star Wars Outlaws''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
