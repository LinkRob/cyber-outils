A '''cabaret''' was a type of entertainment establishment on the [[planet]]s [[Coruscant]]<ref name="Lightsaber Lost">{{TCW|Lightsaber Lost}}</ref> and [[Cantonica]], in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]].<ref name="Canto">{{Databank|canto-casino|Canto Casino|quote=A fathier racetrack occupies the center of the complex, surrounded by game rooms, restaurants, cabarets and luxury hotels.}}</ref>

One Coruscant cabaret façade showed a picture of a kneeling [[Twi'lek]]<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore" /> [[Gender|woman]],<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/lethal-trackdown-trivia-gallery|text="Lethal Trackdown" Trivia Gallery|quote=The nightclub on Coruscant is filled with posters of various female pin-up models}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref> dressed in a revealing, [[Color|purple]] [[Clothing|outfit]]. The picture was also put up as a [[Cabaret poster|series of posters]] in public places to promote the establishment.<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore">{{TCW|Duchess of Mandalore}}</ref> A differently [[color]]ed version of the poster was put up in [[Moshi Bar]].<ref name="Lethal Trackdown">{{TCW|Lethal Trackdown}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:LoieStormtroopers-TheSpyDancer.png|thumb|right|300px|Loi'e gave dazzling cabaret performances for Imperials while secretly assisting the Rebellion.]]
In the [[Canon|non-canon]] ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' short "[[The Spy Dancer]]," [[Loi'e]] is a cabaret dancer who entertains the [[Galactic Empire]] occupiers of her world whilst [[spy]]ing for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]].<ref name="Visions">{{Visions|The Spy Dancer}}</ref>
