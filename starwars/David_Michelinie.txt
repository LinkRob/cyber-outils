'''David Michelinie''' is an comic book writer who was the regular writer on the [[Star Wars (1977)|''Marvel Star Wars'']] comic series from [[1981]] to [[1983]].

Michelinie co-created several Star Wars characters including [[Plif/Legends|Plif]], [[Lumiya|Shira Brie]], and [[Fenn Shysa]].
