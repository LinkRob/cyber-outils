The '''Delva Racine original hanging sleeve dress''' was a [[Color|white]] [[Clothing|dress]] created by<ref name="GG" /> [[human]] high-fashion [[Artist|designer]] [[Delva Racine]]<ref name="Complete Visual New">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> for the royal [[Princess]] of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="GG" /> Organa wore the dress during the [[Royal Award Ceremony]] on the<ref name="Episode IV" /> [[jungle]] [[moon]]<ref name="Y4DB">{{Databank|yavin-4|Yavin 4}}</ref> [[Yavin 4]]<ref name="Episode IV" /> in [[0 ABY]],<ref name="IV Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IV}} which take place after the [[Battle of Yavin]], to [[0 ABY]].</ref> which was held to commemorate the heroes of [[Battle of Yavin]] and destruction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star I]] [[battle station]].<ref name="EPIV JN">[[Star Wars: A New Hope (paperback novel)|''Star Wars: A New Hope'' junior novelization]]</ref>

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Delva Racine original hanging sleeve dress first appeared in the [[1977]] [[original trilogy]] film, {{Film|IV}}.<ref name="Episode IV" /> It was first named in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="GG" />

For the ''A New Hope'', the dress was made of [[Wikipedia:Silk|white silk]] and was given a more formal and formfitting look than the white gown which Leia had worn earlier in the film. The turtleneck costume was also removed to allow for a lower-cut necklace in order to make room for her [[chalcedony waves]] necklace. Early concept art of the dress had been drawn by [[John Mullo]] and the dress was made by a company known as Bermans in conjunction with a French dresser.<ref name="Costumes">''[[Star Wars Costumes: The Original Trilogy]]''</ref>
