'''Automaintenance Crew 28-G''' was a [[droid]] maintenance crew on [[Cloud City]], consisting of a number of [[MSE-6 series repair droid]]s, which operated during the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. When former [[Baron Administrator]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] attempted to [[Mission to Cloud City|rescue]] his [[aide]] [[Lobot]] from Cloud City, the [[cyborg]], who was connected to the [[city]]'s [[Central Processing]], transmitted orders to the droids of Automaintenance Crew 28-G, who were undertaking repairs on [[Level 110]]. The droids were instructed to attack the Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s attempting to break into the control center, allowing Calrissian to rescue Lobot.

==Description==
Automaintenance Crew 28-G was an automated [[droid]] maintenance crew that operated on [[Cloud City]],<ref name="Star Wars 4" /> a [[city]] within the [[atmosphere]] of the [[gas giant]] [[planet]] [[Bespin]].<ref name="Cloud City">{{Databank|cloud-city|Cloud City}}</ref> The crew consisted of six [[MSE-6 series repair droid]]s.<ref name="Star Wars 4" />

==History==
{{Quote|Looks like we'll have to get out of this on our own, buddy. You got any bright ideas?|Lando Calrissian, to Lobot|<ref name="Star Wars 4" />}}
[[File:AttackOfTheMouseDroids-2020StarWars4.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Automaintenance Crew 28-G attack the Imperial stormtroopers.]]
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars (2020) 4|''Star Wars'' (2020) 4]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> during the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Automaintenance Crew 28-G was performing repairs on [[Level 110]] of Cloud City. As the droids worked, they were contacted by [[Lobot]],<ref name="Star Wars 4" /> the [[cyborg]] [[computer]]-liaison officer and [[aide]] to former [[Baron Administrator]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]],<ref name="Lobot">{{Databank|lobot|Lobot}}</ref> who had been connected into the Cloud City's [[Central Processing]] following the [[Galactic Empire]]'s occupation of the city.<ref name="Star Wars 4" />

Lobot, intending to protect Calrissian, who had [[Mission to Cloud City|returned]] to Cloud City to rescue him, from a number of Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s, provided the droids of Automaintenance Crew 28-G with new instructions, calling them away from their maintenance and down to [[Level 109]], where the stormtroopers were attempting to force their way into Central Processing. The [[MSE series]] droids attacked and incapacitated the stormtroopers, allowing Calrissian to free Lobot, though several of the droids sustained damage in the attack.<ref name="Star Wars 4" />
