The '''crackdown on Pipada''' was the [[Galactic Empire]]'s response to protests by the [[Drabatan]] [[species]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pipada]] following the Empire's [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishment]], which occurred in [[19 BBY]]. The [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] [[blockade]]d the planet and deployed [[swamptrooper]]s to its surface. By [[1 BBY]], after one of the most vocal protesters&mdash;a Drabatan [[Musician|singer]] named [[Sa'Kalla]]&mdash; was captured and [[torture]]d, a [[Sa'Kalla (movement)|movement]] was formed in her name and opposed the Empire.

The [[Unidentified Imperial governor (Pipada)|Imperial governor]] of Pipada attempted to stop the violence caused by the movement by bringing Sa'Kalla to the [[Pipada Auditorium]], where she was ordered to convince the crowd to submit. She defied the order and was [[Death penalty|executed]] on stage, causing the stadium to erupt in violence, and the Drabatans [[Death|killed]] the [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] before briefly reclaiming their [[homeworld]]. Soon after, the Empire returned with reinforcements and imposed martial law. During the Imperial occupation, Pipada was harvested for its resources and most of the Drabatan [[species]] was forced to abandon the planet.

==Prelude==
At some point after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishment]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Dawn" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="EmpireDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[amphibian|amphibious]] [[Drabatan]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to<ref name="Dawn" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]] &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Pipada system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Pipada]] protested its rule. The most vocal protester was the young and popular [[Musician|singer]] [[Sa'Kalla]], who wrote protest [[Music|songs]] speaking out against Imperial tyranny, with the Empire quickly becoming aware of the songs.<ref name="Dawn" />

==The crackdown==
[[File:Imperial_Emblem.svg|thumb|left|200px|The Empire responded to the protests by blockading Pipada.]]
By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> criticism of the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor]] went too far and the Empire responded by initiating the crackdown on Pipada, [[Blockade|blockading]] the planet and deploying [[garrison]]s of specialized [[swamptrooper]]s to the surface. What began as protests turned into brutal massacres. During the crackdown, Sa'Kalla was [[Prisoner|captured]], and a [[Sa'Kalla (movement)|movement]] was organized in her name that spread across the planet.<ref name="Dawn" />

When the violence caused by the movement began to threaten Imperial control on the planet, the [[Unidentified Imperial governor (Pipada)|Imperial governor]] of Pipada brought Sa'Kalla to the [[Pipada Auditorium]] in front of an audience of half a million Drabatans. Believing that they had [[torture]]d Sa'Kalla into submission, they ordered her to convince the people to submit. Sa'Kalla instead shouted for the people to resist Imperial rule, and she was quickly [[Death penalty|executed]]. The whole stadium erupted in violence, and the enraged crowd tore Imperial security forces to [[Death|pieces]]. The Drabatans briefly reclaimed their planet while many fled offworld. The Empire soon returned with reinforcements and imposed martial law across the planet.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Aftermath==
After the Empire captured Pipada,<ref name="Dawn" /> the planet was harvested for its resources to expand the Imperial Military and satisfy its [[fuel]] needs, causing most of the Drabatan species to abandon the planet.<ref name="Heroes">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Heroes of Rogue One}}</ref> Despite the loss of Pipada, Drabatans continued to resist the Empire in ways big and small. Those who fought the Imperial forces joined resistance groups and used the war cry of "Sa'Kalla!" for the late singer.<ref name="Dawn" />

The occupation and harvesting of resources were mentioned in an [[Unidentified artist|artist's]] [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]] as part of an entry on the Drabatan species.<ref name="AA">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> At some point no earlier than [[34 ABY]],<ref name="AADate">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref> the journal came into the possession of the [[Graf Archive]]&mdash;a vast depository owned by the [[Graf family]] that contained documents that held historical importance.<ref name="AA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The crackdown on Pipada was first indirectly mentioned in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'' released in [[2016]].<ref name="Ultimate">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> The crackdown was later identified in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[2018]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] sourcebook published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' game.<ref name="Dawn" />
