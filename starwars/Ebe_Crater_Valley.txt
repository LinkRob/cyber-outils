'''Ebe Crater Valley''' was a landmark on the [[desert]] [[planet]] of [[Tatooine]]. Pocketed with dips and craters, the valley was part of the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]], and contained a [[Ebe Crater quarry|quarry]], in the last [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="CL 2016" />
