'''Batuuan braga bears''' were a [[species]] of [[bear]] that lived on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. They were [[Color|brown]] and chased after their [[Diet|prey]]. When the [[droid]] [[SF-R3]] visited Batuu, he learned that the growl of a braga bear was a sign to run away.

==Biology and appearance==
Batuuan braga bears were a type of [[bear]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Batuu]].<ref name="Black Spire" /> The bears had [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]], a tail, pointed [[Tooth|teeth]], and black [[claw]]s, and they lived in [[cave]]s in the [[mountain]]s. [[Porg]]s, an [[Bird|avian]] [[species]] that had been brought to Batuu, knew to run when they heard a braga bear's growl.<ref name="Porgs" />

==Behavior==
{{Quote|Don't eat me!|SF-R3, while being chased by a braga bear|<ref name="Porgs" />}}
The bears chased after their [[Diet|prey]] and were known to chase the inhabitants of Batuu, where they were the apex predator.<ref name="Porgs" />

==History==
{{Quote|When you hear the growl of a Batuuan braga bear&mdash;You run!|SF-R3|<ref name="Porgs" />}}
[[File:TimeToRun-GoC.jpg|thumb|right|260px|The droids are chased by a Batuuan braga bear.]]
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date1">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> when the [[Chadra-Fan]] [[Resistance]] member [[Kriki]] noticed that the [[smuggler]] [[Zade Kalliday]] was running towards them, she speculated that Kalliday had either encountered a braga bear or was in trouble; in reality, it was the latter.<ref name="Black Spire" /> During or after that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date2">The [[Creature Stall]] in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] establishes that [[porg]]s were brought to [[Batuu]]. The events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]&mdash;and therefore the Creature Stall&mdash;are set in [[34 ABY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[safari droid]] [[SF-R3]]<ref name="Activity Sheet">[[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts Activity Journal]]</ref> visited Batuu in order to learn about the porgs and attempt to teach them tricks. While hiking up a mountain to get to them, his [[cam droid]], [[CAM]], ran into one of the bears in a cave, but the [[droid]] was able to quickly escape.<ref name="Porgs" />

Later, while trying to train a trio of porgs to play [[Death|dead]], a Batuuan braga bear approached, causing the porgs to fly away in fright and leaving the droids by themselves. They too ran away, with SF-R3 begging the bear to not eat him. When recounting his adventures on Batuu for a [[Holorecorder|holorecording]] for the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]], the droid noted that while he intended to be the [[teacher]] on the planet, he ended up learning a lesson: to run when a braga bear's growl was heard. As he recorded the segment, a bear again chased after him, causing him to flee again.<ref name="Porgs" />

==Behind the scenes==
Batuuan braga bears were first mentioned in the [[2019]] novel ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'', written by [[Delilah S. Dawson]] as part of the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge (media project)|multimedia project]].<ref name="Black Spire" /> They made their first appearance in "[[Porgs (Galaxy of Creatures)|Porgs]]," an episode of the ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Creatures]]'' animated shorts, which was published online on [[October 14]], [[2021]].<ref name="Porgs" />
