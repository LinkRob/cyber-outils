The '''[[chef]]''' who operated in the [[Spectres]]' secret hideout known as the [[Haven]], was a [[Gender|male]] [[Human]] who offered to cook a celebratory meal, if one of the rebels brought him food to use as ingredients.<ref name="Recon Misions" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You have collected food for me! Let's liven up the place! More citizens and buildings will create a bigger celebration.|The chef, to one of the Spectres|<ref name="Recon Misions" />}}
After being brought the ingredients he required to prepare a meal, the chef enthusiastically offered advice to one of the Spectres, on how to enliven the upcoming festivities.<ref name="Recon Misions" />
