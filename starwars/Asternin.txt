'''Asternin''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] located near the [[Nihil]] [[Occlusion Zone]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Nine months after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], the settlement<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 3" /> [[Solunus]]<ref name="Eye of Darkness">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]''</ref> on Asternin was [[Raid at Solunus|raided]] by the Nihil. [[Galactic Republic]] [[Republic Defense Coalition|forces]], alongside [[Jedi Master]] [[Pra-Tre Veter]] and [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Bell Zettifar]] and [[Burryaga Agaburry]], [[Mission to the edge of the Stormwall|came to the aid]] of the settlers on the planet. While providing support, Nihil [[scout]]s identified the [[Grand Master]] and alerted [[Melis Shryke]] aboard the [[Stormship]] ''[[Cacophony]]''. Nihil forces quickly arrived with their [[47 lightsaber nullifier]], rendering the [[Jedi]] weaponless. While Zettifar and Agaburry hurried the townspeople to safety, Veter used his powerful [[Force power|Force abilities]] to fend off and distract the Nihil before he was eventually captured.<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 3" />
