'''Choyyssyk''' was a [[Wookiee]] [[Gender|male]] [[commander]] and member of [[Chief]]tain [[Tarfful]]'s [[Kashyyyk resistance|resistance]] group on [[Kashyyyk]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. He was [[Attack on an Imperial outpost (Choyyssyk)|captured]] and [[Prison|imprisoned]] in the [[Imperial Refinery]], but was [[Liberation on Kashyyyk|freed]] with the help of [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]] and the former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]]. After being freed, Choyyssyk worked with the Partisans, becoming friends with [[Mari Kosan]], Gerrera's [[lieutenant]]. When most of the Partisans fled Kashyyyk and Kestis returned to meet with Tarfful, Choyyssyk was present on [[Cargo Pad 119-Grek]] with [[Mirienna]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==Biography==
===Prisoner of the Empire===
{{Quote|Grooooowwwl.''"<br />"''Hey. We're here to free you. You're not alone.|Choyyssyk and Cal Kestis|<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />}}
[[File:Choyyssyk-TSotW.png|thumb|left|300px|Choyyssyk became an ally of Cal Kestis.]]
Choyyssyk was a [[Wookiee]] who lived on his [[species]]' [[homeworld]] of [[Kashyyyk]] during its [[Occupation of Kashyyyk|occupation]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]. He served as a [[commander]] with the [[Kashyyyk resistance]] led by [[Chief]]tain [[Tarfful]], but was captured during a [[Attack on an Imperial outpost (Choyyssyk)|mission]] to attack an Imperial outpost and [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] in an [[Imperial Refinery]] where [[Slavery|enslaved]] Wookiees were used in the harvesting of [[Wroshyr sap]]. Force to wear a slave collar, Choyyssyk spent [[Standard month|months]] in the refinery, as Tarfful could not risk any [[Wookiee warrior|warriors]] to rescue him.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> the Wookiee saw the fugitive [[Jedi]] [[Cal Kestis]] enter the cell block where he and many other Wookiees were being held and called out to Kestis as the Jedi attempted to open the cells. The Jedi reassured the prisoner that he was to be freed, but before Kestis could get the cell doors open a [[KX-series security droid]] attacked him from behind. The Jedi defeated the [[droid]] and then opened the cell doors, causing Choyyssyk and the other Wookiees present to cheer as they escaped, and Choyyssyk himself to hug Kestis.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

===Ally of the Partisans===
[[File:CalMeetsTarfful.png|thumb|Tarfful, Choyyssyk and Mari Kosan talk to Cal Kestis at the Origin Lake.]]
{{Dialogue|Kestis|You know Tarfful?|Choyyssyk|Raaaraaaa-ra-oooorr.|Kosan|He was part of Tarfful's crew, but got captured on a mission.|Choyyssyk|Rawr&hellip;|Kosan|Tarfful couldn't risk more warriors to save him. He spent months in that refinery.|Choyyssyk|Roaar.|Kosan|Choyyssyk will help you find Tarfful, but they're always on the move. Could take some time.|Kestis|That's all I can ask, thank you.|attr=Choyyssyk vows to help Cal Kestis|src=<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />}}
The Wookiees then joined up with Kestis's ally [[Saw Gerrera]] and his [[Partisans]] as they fought their way through the facility, ultimately making their way to the roof. There, with the [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] defeated, Gerrera gave a speech to those present. Choyyssyk and the Partisan [[Mari Kosan]] then approached Kestis, who asked if Choyyssyk was familiar with Tarfful who he needed to speak about his old friend [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]] for his [[secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order]]. With Kosan translating Choyyssyk's [[Shyriiwook]] for Kestis, the Wookiee vowed to do whatever it took to find Tarfful for Kestis, although he explained that as Tarfful was constantly on the move it might take some time. Kestis thanked him and then left Kashyyyk, confident that Choyyssyk and Kosan would find Tarfful for him.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

The two later managed to find Tarfful and met with him and Kestis at the [[Origin Lake]] near the [[Shadowlands]]. Tarfful pointed the Jedi up the giant [[Origin Tree]] and Kosan gave him a [[rebreather]] so he could dive underneath the [[tree]]s roots.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Dialogue|Kestis|Seems like Saw's willing to do whatever it takes.|Kosan|He has his own way of doing things, yes, but these are dire times. Choyyssyk had [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]] and brown [[eye]]s. He became fast friends with Kosan after the [[prison]] break and the pair would stop at nothing to save Kashyyyk from Imperial occupation.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
