'''Ettel Nuts''' were a snack served at [[Jat Kaa's Coolers]] beverage carts throughout [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="JKC" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE Date">The events of ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'' and those within the original timeline of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> It was suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="JKC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ettel Nuts are available in the [[In-universe|real-world]] at beverage carts throughout [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]]. They debuted on [[May 1]], [[2026]]. The menu's description reads, "Assortment of sweet, spicy, and [[Wikipedia:Queso flameado|queso fundido]] nuts with chocolate candy rocks."<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=disney-experiences/disney-eats-may-the-4th-foodie-guide|text=Disney Eats: May the 4th Foodie Guide 2026}}</ref>. 

Ettel nuts were first introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in an article titled "[[Dining at Dex's]]" on the [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] section of [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="DiningatDex's">{{Hyperspace|url=fans/hyperspace/source/dexdiner/index.html|text=Dining at Dex's|int=Dining at Dex's}}</ref>
