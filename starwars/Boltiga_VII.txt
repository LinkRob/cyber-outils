'''Boltiga VII''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Outlaws" /> located in the [[Boltiga system]] of the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It fell within grid square M-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and served as a [[mining]] colony during the [[Imperial Era]]. A [[Unidentified rebel spy (Ukio Bread Incident)|rebel spy]] convicted of five counts of high [[treason]] in the [[Ukio Bread Incident]] was sentenced to life imprisonment on Boltiga VII<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Shortly after the [[spy]]'s trial concluded, the [[HoloNet News]] [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Dorna Parros]] reported on the sentencing.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Boltiga VII was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
