'''Ferrule''' was an [[orbak]] from [[Kef Bir]], owned by [[Jannah]], the leader of a [[Company 77|tribe]] of former [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] defectors.<ref name="TROS VD" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ferrule first appeared in the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']],<ref name="TROS" /> and was identified in the accompanying reference guide ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> The hero horse portraying Ferrule was called [[Ben (horse)|Ben]].<ref name="Featurette">{{Film|IX}} "Cast of Creatures" featurette</ref>
