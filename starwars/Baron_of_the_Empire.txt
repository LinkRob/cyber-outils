'''Baron of the Empire''',<ref name="Conversion Kit">{{FFGXW2|set=Galactic Empire Conversion Kit|cardname=Baron of the Empire}}</ref> also known as '''Baron of the Imperial Navy''',<ref name="Imperial Officers and Agents">{{EncyclopediaCite|Imperial Officers and Agents}}</ref> '''Flight Baron''',<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> or simply '''baron''', was a title given to decorated [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilots]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|military]]. To signify their status, they had the right to bear [[Color|red]] markings<ref name="TFAVD" /> on their otherwise all-black [[flight suit]]s and [[Flight helmet|helmets]].<ref name="Empire Day" /> Such barons included [[Valen Rudor]],<ref name="Core Set">{{FFGXW2|set=Core Set|cardname=Valen Rudor}}</ref> [[Soontir Fel]],<ref name="Fel Card">{{FFGXW2|set=Skystrike Academy Squadron Pack|cardname=Saber Squadron Ace}}</ref> and [[Titan Three (Flight Baron)|Titan Three]].<ref name="Squadrons" /> The [[First Order]] later drew inspiration from the Imperial barons' flashes of red when designing the helmets of their [[First Order Special Forces]].<ref name="TFAVD" />
