{{Quote|I am not programmed to enjoy terminating you&hellip;and yet my receptors would have to analyze this as a ''positive'' experience.|4-LOM, to Beilert Valance|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 7">[[Bounty Hunters 7|''Bounty Hunters'' 7]]</ref>}}
'''4-LOM "FOR-ELLOEM"''' was an ambitious [[LOM-series protocol droid|LOM-series]] [[protocol droid]] that specialized in millions of languages. He was manufactured for the employment of [[insectoid]] [[species]]. A logic glitch allowed him to overwrite his programming and become a galactic [[thief]] and later a [[bounty hunter]]. He often worked together with [[Gand]] [[findsman]] [[Zuckuss]]. Following the [[Battle of Hoth]], both 4-LOM and Zuckuss were hired by the [[Sith Lord]] known as [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to capture [[Han Solo]], [[captain]] of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' and hero of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. However, the [[bounty]] on the [[smuggler]]'s head was instead claimed by the infamous [[Boba Fett]].

==Biography==
===Early life and career===
====Becoming a bounty hunter====
{{Quote|Although I cannot prove it, it is my belief that the Gotra targeted the ''Princess'' with the intent of freeing 4-LOM to be a droid enforcer in their ranks.|Andressa Divo|<ref name="Scum and Villany" />}}
[[File:4-LOM-GA.png|thumb|left|250px|4-LOM became a bounty hunter after rewriting his programming.]]
4-LOM was a [[LOM-series protocol droid|LOM-series]] [[protocol droid]] manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> who was programmed by a [[4-LOM's programmer|specific individual]]. The programmer told him about a [[Unidentified planet (droid uprising)|planet]] where [[droid]]s rose up.<ref name="Powered Down 2">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=11|story=Powered Down, Part 2}}</ref> 4-LOM was assigned to a [[luxury liner]], but logic glitches and personality software corruption<ref name="Updated and Expanded" /> allowed 4-LOM to override his programming.<ref name="Card Trader">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=4-LOM - Bounty Hunter}}</ref> 4-LOM rewrote his programming,<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> and became a [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[thief]], and later a [[bounty hunter]].<ref name="Card Trader" /> As a criminal 4-LOM was known for specializing in anticipating the moves of its targets.<ref name="CVD" />

The [[Droid Gotra]] used 4-LOM's story as proof that a droids original programmed function did not determine its future. Industrial Automaton however, tried to bury the droid's story, fearing that the public might learn their protocol droids could potentially turn into lethal [[Death|killers]]. However, [[Andressa Divo]] eventually looked into 4-LOM's history. After obtaining Droid Gotra [[databank]]s, she discovered that they had targeted a luxury liner, which was never attacked. Divo also learned that it used LOM-series protocol droids and suspected that the Gotra may have caused 4-LOMs corrupted programming all along to create a symbol for their cause.<ref name="Scum and Villany">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>

4-LOM often worked together with the tracker [[Zuckuss]], one of the first traditional [[Gand]] [[Findsman|findsmen]] to leave his [[homeworld]],<ref name="Card Trader" /> the [[planet]] [[Gand (planet)|Gand]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and claimed several high profile [[Bounty|bounties]] for the [[Hutt Clan]].<ref name="Card Trader" /> Working with his partner, 4-LOM would provide analysis and information to help Zuckuss's mysterious ways.<ref name="CVD" />

====Killing Cribiriz Idollax====
{{Quote|We had worked other jobs together of course... <br /> ...that was when we knew when we team up, we become unstoppable!|Zuckuss, about 4-LOM|<ref name="WOTBH 4-LOM & Zuckuss" />}}
One of the first jobs 4-LOM took with Zuckuss was a hunt for the [[Culisetto]] [[doctor]] [[Cribiriz Idollax]] given by [[Deva Lompop]]. 4-LOM saved Zuckuss as he was cornered by Idollax's [[droid]] [[bodyguard]]s, and Zuckuss would [[Death|kill]] the doctor by cutting him in half with his [[snare rifle]]. Afterwards, Zuckuss and 4-LOM agreed to split the [[bounty]] in half. Unbeknownst to the two bounty hunters, the doctor Cribiriz Zuckuss killed was actually his brother [[Sribiriz]], leading Cribiriz to vow revenge against the bounty hunters.<ref name="WOTBH 4-LOM & Zuckuss" />

====Capturing Beris Ford====
{{Quote|Beris Ford&mdash;A small-time hoodlum with ideas above his station. Still, the reward for his capture will pay for our tractor beam repairs.|4-LOM|<ref name="Hunter vs Hunted">[[Star Wars Adventures Free Comic Book Day 2018|''Star Wars Adventures'' Free Comic Book Day 2018]]</ref>}}
In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{HelmetCollectionCite|78|Highlights of the Saga}}</ref> Zuckuss and 4-LOM captured the small-time criminal [[Beris Ford]], keeping him imprisoned aboard their [[Mist Hunter|ship]] and hoping to use the reward for his capture to pay for [[tractor beam]] repairs. [[Standard week|Weeks]] later, the hunters took up a [[bounty]] placed by [[Rekias Nodo]] on [[Han Solo]], and confronted him at [[Valtos]] [[Valtos space port|space port]]. Zuckuss incapacitated the [[smuggler]] inside a [[stun net]] fired from his [[snare rifle]], and he and 4-LOM loaded Han's cargo into the ''Mist Hunter'', where they [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] him along with Ford. Sensing a [[disturbance in the Force]], Zuckuss checked on the stolen cargo only to be attacked by [[Chewbacca]] bursting from one of the containers, and was hit with a stun net from his own [[rifle]]. Zuckuss was left tied up while the [[Wookiee]] rescued Solo and Ford, and the scoundrels fled with their cargo back to the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. However, the hunters pursued, and after a firefight Zuckuss chased the ''Falcon'' into [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Hunter vs Hunted" />

[[File:Escaping_jungle_planet_-_Adventures_11.jpg|thumb|right|250px|4-LOM, Solo, Chewbacca and Zuckuss escape]]
Following Solo and Chewbacca to a [[jungle]] [[Unidentified planet (droid uprising)|planet]], Zuckuss again sensed something was wrong, but 4-LOM continued the pursuit through a heavy cloud layer until their [[Starship|ship's]] systems&mdash;and 4-LOM himself&mdash;suddenly powered down, forcing them to drop out of the sky and crash-land on a [[beach]]. Zuckuss was trapped in the wreckage, and called out to Solo for help in freeing his legs, though slapped [[stuncuffs]] on Solo when he was in reach. However, the planet's technology-negating effects caused the cuffs to release Solo, who left Zuckuss in his predicament. After Solo and Chewbacca found an [[energy projector]] transmitting an [[immobilizer beam]], Zuckuss attacked them with native [[spear]]s as they climbed the tall mast. Chewbacca dislodged the projection sphere from the top, causing it to crash on the beach below, which caused mechanical drones to erupt from the sand and attacked all three interlopers.<ref name="Powered Down I">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=10|story=Powered Down, Part 1}}</ref>

===Galactic Civil War===
====Kalarassi Five====
Around [[2 BBY]],<ref name="AOR Special 1 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[The Long Game (comic story)|The Long Game]]" to around [[2 BBY]].</ref> 4-LOM was among 5 bounty hunters investigating a jailbreak on [[Kalarassi Five]].<ref name="AoR Special 1">{{StoryCite|story=The Long Game (comic story)|book=Age of Rebellion Special 1}}</ref>

====The hunt for the ''Millennium Falcon''====
[[File:HuntThemDown-TCG.jpg|thumb|left|250px|[[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] addresses 4-LOM and the other assembled bounty hunters.]]
{{Quote|There will be a substantial reward for the one who finds the ''Millennium Falcon''. You are free to use any methods necessary, but I want them alive.|Darth Vader to 4-LOM and several other bounty hunters|<ref name="The Empire Strikes Back" />}}
Shortly after the [[Battle of Hoth]], both 4-LOM and Zuckuss assembled, along with fellow bounty hunters [[Boba Fett]], [[Dengar]], [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], and [[IG-88B|IG-88]], aboard the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|Super Star Destroyer]] ''[[Executor]]'', and were hired by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to locate the ''Millennium Falcon''. However, the bounty was collected by Fett who tracked down the [[light freighter]] to [[Cloud City]] on [[Bespin]].<ref name="The Empire Strikes Back" />

====On Valance's trail====
{{Quote|Valance, turn over your young charge and your death will be swift and ''merciful''. Relatively.|4-LOM|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 7" />}}
4-LOM and Zuckuss soon caught wind of a bounty placed on [[Cadeliah]], who was traveling with the cyborg bounty hunter [[Beilert Valance]]. The two were intent on capturing the girl, and followed Valance to the [[Spur Orbiting Market]], where they briefly clashed. Valance managed to sever 4-LOM's arm and escaped to bring the girl to [[Lowik]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 6">[[Bounty Hunters 6|''Bounty Hunters'' 6]]</ref> The pair of insect-headed hunters followed Valance to the forest world, and once again [[Skirmish on Lowik|clashed]] with the cyborg, but this time against members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] as well. The two used a cache of [[B2-series super battle droid]]s to fight the rebels. Valance was able to buy the two off of his trail by giving them a [[Fire ruby]]. The ''Mist Hunter'' left soon after, Zuckuss vowing the two would resume their hunt in three rotations.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 7" />

Following his winding path,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 10">[[Bounty Hunters 10|''Bounty Hunters'' 10]]</ref> the duo eventually did catch up with Valance and his new partner Dengar in the [[Y'Toub system]], engaging in a short dogfight through an [[Asteroid field]] before the Corellian bounty hunter achieved a truce with 4-LOM and Zuckuss. This peace came in exchange for information regarding the bounty [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]] had recently placed on Boba Fett, and his location on [[Nar Shaddaa]]. The two sets of hunters then departed the system.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 12">[[Bounty Hunters 12|''Bounty Hunters'' 12]]</ref>

====War of the bounty hunters====
{{Quote|Where is ''Han Solo''? We searched your ship--he's not there. You must have stashed him somewhere.|4-LOM to Boba Fett|<ref name="WOTBH 1">[[War of the Bounty Hunters 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters'' 1]]</ref>
}}
[[File:Boba4LOMHead-WOTBH1.jpg|thumb|right|280px|Boba Fett carries 4-LOM's head onboard the ''Slave I'']]
On a landing platform on the smuggler's moon of [[Nar Shaddaa]], 4-LOM and Zuckuss rigged a [[thermal detonator]] onto the ''[[Slave I]]'', catching Fett as he was returning to his ship. Zuckuss approached the armored hunter and engaged him from the front, while 4-LOM came from behind, forcing Fett to drop his weapons at gunpoint. The duo implored the location of Solo, but Fett was angered at the revelation that the two had entered his [[Jango Fett|father's]] ship, and remotely activated its canons to blast them away. Zuckuss was kicked off the platform after the explosion, and 4-LOM had been blown in half. Fett, needing information, decapitated the droid and took his head onboard the ''Slave I''. Placing a restraining bolt on 4-LOM's head, Fett learned that the bounty was placed on him by Jabba himself. Fett took 4-LOM's head with him to [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]].<ref name="WOTBH 1" />

Just as the droid was attempting to convince Fett of his usefulness, Fett disposed of 4-LOM's head by tossing it off of the bridge leading to Jabba's Palace.<ref name="WOTBH 1" /> In the deserts of [[Tatooine]], a [[Jawa]] eventually found 4-LOM's head, trading it to [[Sardis Ramsin]], leading it to eventually fall into the hands of [[Cribiriz Idollax]] back on [[Nar Shaddaa]]. Idollax used the head to create a large, spider-like droid, barely maintaining the personality 4-LOM once had. In an alley on the moon, the monstrous 4-LOM attacked his former partner Zuckuss, who had recently joined a [[Jega's Squad|team]] of hunters, and had sensed 4-LOM's presence. The team fought this [[monster droid]] while Zuckuss tracked down the nearby Cribiriz and killed him. Zuckuss then destroyed the device controlling 4-LOM, freeing him. The rebuilt 4-LOM malfunctioned, fleeing into the alleys of Nar Shaddaa, leaving Zuckuss alone once again.<ref name="WOTBH 4-LOM & Zuckuss" />

4-LOM eventually made his way to an abandoned [[Hutt freighter]] in the [[Wreck Belt]]<ref name="BH 20">[[Bounty Hunters 20|''Bounty Hunters'' 20]]</ref> of the planet [[Lotho Minor]] in the Outer Rim.<ref name="BMF 62">{{BuildFalconCite|62|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> There, the reprogrammed droid launched a trap for Zuckuss to fulfill his programming to murder his former best friend, but Zuckuss, journeying to the ship with [[T'onga's crew]] after joining it, managed to overcome the droid. Afterward, Zuckuss restored his friend's real programming. With his partnership with Zuckuss restored,<ref name="BH 20">[[Bounty Hunters 20|''Bounty Hunters'' 20]]</ref> 4-LOM then joined Zuckuss on [[T'onga]]'s crew.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 21" />

4-LOM and the crew took a [[Mission on Corellia]], where the droid served as the pilot for the ''[[Edgehawk (starship)|Edgehawk]]'' while the rest of the crew went to kidnap [[Vukorah]] of the [[Unbroken Clan]] and enlist her to help save Cadeliah, a young girl and the heir to the Unbroken Clan and Mourner's Wail Syndicate. 4-LOM contacted T'onga, who informed him that the crew ran into complications due to their 360 second open window of jamming the syndicate's comms but the crew was slow to subdue Vukorah. Despite the setback, the droid assured her that he will be at the rendezvous point at the arranged time. Needless to say, [[Tasu Leech]] was annoyed at 4-LOM and vowed to rip the droid apart wire by wire. After the group gave a chase, they arrived at the rendezvous point, but was upset that 4-LOM wasn't there. Leech argued with Zuckuss, who was confident that 4-LOM will come, even under difficult circumstances. As more Unbroken Clan enforcers arrive, 4-LOM arrives with the ship and on T'onga's orders, he opens fire on the enforcers, allowing the group to escape.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 22">[[Bounty Hunters 22|''Bounty Hunters'' 22]]</ref>

====Joining D-Squad====
At some point, 4-LOM arrived on [[Gallios]], where he hunted [[assassin droid]]s [[0-0-0]] and [[BT-1]] , who were starting a [[Massacre on Gallios|massacre]] on the planet. 4-LOM saw his professional rival [[IG-88B]], taking R2-D2 and pieces of 0-0-0 away, and shot a blaster bolt. IG-88 taunts that 4-LOM should o brought his friend Zuckuss along if he was going to snake the bounty the protocol droid has on him. Demanding for the astromech, IG-88 told 4-LOM that he already has the protocol droid and astromech, but 4-LOM corrects him, talking about the silver one before an infect [[Unidentified cyborg (Gallios)|cyborg]] arrives.<ref name="DD DS2">[[Dark Droids: D-Squad 2|''Dark Droids: D-Squad'' 2]]</ref>

===New Republic Era===
{{Quote|Stolak Vandello, the Bounty Hunter's Guild has authorized this unit to capture or terminate you at the behest of your former employers.|4-LOM to Vandello|<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 5" />}}
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Halcyon 5 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Halcyon Legacy'' 5 to [[34 ABY]]. As the flashback events of ''Halcyon Legacy'' 5 take place around twenty-five [[Standard year|years]] earlier, they must take place around [[9 ABY]].</ref> during the [[New Republic Era|time]] of the [[New Republic]]; 4-LOM and the rest of T'onga's crew, which included Bossk, who had rejoined<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 5">[[Halcyon Legacy 5|''Halcyon Legacy'' 5]]</ref> despite his prior betrayal,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 40">[[Bounty Hunters 40|''Bounty Hunters'' 40]]</ref> undertook a job on the ''[[Halcyon]]'' for the [[Black Sun]] to capture or kill the [[Balosar (species)|Balosar]] [[Stolak Vandello]], who was in debt to the Black Sun syndicate. After Bossk failed to capture him, 4-LOM broke into the Balosar's room where the droid was soon taken out by his lover [[R'Tess]], stabbing the droid in the back. Taking advantage of 4-LOM rebooting himself, the two lovers left their room but were cornered by the [[Aqualish]] [[Wooro]], Zuckuss, and T'onga, who eventually took Vandello back to Black Sun.<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 5" />

===Legacy===
[[Exantor Divo]] later recorded 4-LOM's origins and his mother's theory about the Droid Gotra's involvement in ''[[Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''.<ref name="Scum and Villany" />

The [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] learned of 4-LOM's exploits and recounted what he had heard in a book titled ''[[The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]'', where he admitted that 4-LOM was one of the few of his kind to exploit loopholes in his software and upgrade his purpose. Ohnaka also notes of 4-LOM's partnership with a Gand findsman named Zuckuss, who Ohnaka notes, the two even bear a striking
resemblance to each other, as the droid's head was crafted to emulate the appearance of the insertoid species he was built to serve. Ohnaka even points how their partnership began working in tandem early in their bounty-hunting careers, soon finding that their unique skills and mindsets complemented each other quite well, since Zuckuss relied on his arcane abilities as a findsman and 4-LOM balanced things out with his more logic-based approach. In case the two hunters went after him, Ohnaka planned to use Cribiriz's idea to split those cracks in their once unstoppable partnership wide open if the pair are fighting among themselves, they are far less likely to be fighting him.<ref name="Secrets of the Bounty Hunters">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|He may look like a nightmarish version of C-3PO, but this nasty fella is actually a LOM-series protocol droid. These units had weird bug-like heads designed to look like the insectoid alien species they served. They also had a pretty nasty flaw in their programming that allowed their personality software to become corrupted.|R0-GR|<ref name="Star Wars: Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref>}}
[[File:4-LOM_SaV.png|thumb|right|220px|4-LOM firing his DLT-19 rifle]]
A cold, calculating<ref name="DB">{{Databank|4-lom|4-LOM}}</ref> and ambitious mechanical,<ref name="Card Trader" /> 4-LOM suffered from logic glitches and personality software corruption<ref name="Updated and Expanded" /> that allowed him to escape his original programming.

A LOM-series protocol droid,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> 4-LOM stood 1.67 [[meter]]s tall<ref name="Updated and Expanded" /> and was covered in battered rusted [[Color|black]] droid plating.<ref name="The Empire Strikes Back" /> His head was designed to emulate the [[insectoid]] [[species]] he was designed to serve,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> with two large green compound [[photoreceptor]]s.<ref name="The Empire Strikes Back" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|My translation circuit can be very helpful in negotiations, and Zuckuss recently upgraded my targeting software.|4-LOM's severed head, to Boba Fett|<ref name="Date note" />}}
4-LOM utilized a [[4-LOM's DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle|standard]] [[DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle]] that was capable of firing [[electromagnetic pulse]]s as well as standard blaster bolts<ref name="Force Arena" /> as well as a [[BlasTech Industries]] [[DLT-19x targeting blaster]]. The droid's chassis further housed an integrated stun-gas emitter, which allowed him to deploy stun gas against organic enemies.<ref name="Allies and Adversaries" />
