'''Anda''' was a [[Jedi hunter|Jedi Hunter]] and [[bounty hunter]].<ref name="Child of Hope" /> He<ref name="Child of Hope AD" /> led a group of hunters who pursued a [[Unidentified Jedi starship|Jedi starship]], and he later fought the [[Jedi]] [[Lah Kara]] alongside his subordinate [[Unda]] after tracking her to an [[Unidentified abandoned ship|abandoned ship]]. He wielded an [[electrostaff]].<ref name="Child of Hope" />

==Behind the scenes==
Anda appeared in "[[The Ninth Jedi: Child of Hope]]," a short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the non-[[canon]] ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short was produced by [[Production IG]] and released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Child of Hope" /> on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Cast">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-3-cast-announce|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 3 Cast Announced with New Trailer}}</ref> Concept art of Anda was created by [[Naoyoshi Shiotani]].<ref name="Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=the-ninth-jedi-concept-art-gallery|text="The Ninth Jedi" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref>
