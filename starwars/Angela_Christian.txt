'''Angela Christian''' is an American actress and singer who portrays a [[First Order officer]] in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. She plays a [[First Order Supreme Council]] member attending the meeting in which [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] presents the head of [[Boolio]] and declares that there is a [[spy]] within the [[First Order]].<ref name="TROS">{{Film|9}}</ref> She has also appeared in episodes of [[Wikipedia:Law & Order|''Law & Order'']], [[Wikipedia:House of Cards (American TV series)|''House of Cards'']], and [[Wikipedia:Unforgettable (American TV series)|''Unforgettable'']].
