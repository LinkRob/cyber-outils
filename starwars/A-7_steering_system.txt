The '''A-7 steering system''' was a model of [[speeder bike]] steering system. It provided bikes with a larger maximum turn angle over the standard central poise steering system. Upgrading an [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder]] bike previously equipped with the [[CP-0 steering system]] to the A-7 steering system cost 1500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and required a [[H-M engine power core]], fifteen units of [[polycarbonate]], and eight [[helicyclic gear]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics}}
During [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] paid for the services of a speeder [[Technician|mechanic]] to upgrade her speeder bike with the A-7 steering system, providing the necessary parts.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}
