'''Brachtor''' was a [[Geonosian/Legends|Geonosian]] who held the position of hive commander amongst the Geonosians who had settled in the [[Northern Jundland Wastes]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] during the [[Cold War (Galactic Republic–Sith Empire)|Cold War]]. He was eventually [[Death/Legends|killed]] while near the [[Northern Windfarm]].
