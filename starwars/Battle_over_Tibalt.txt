Following the [[Quest for the Kyber Saber Crystals|search]] for the [[Kyber Saber]], a '''battle''' between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] took place on the [[planet]] [[Tibalt/LEGO|Tibalt]]. As a result, the planet was littered with wrecked [[starship]]s. Using the Imperial database, the [[Hunter droid/LEGO|hunter droid]] [[M-OC/LEGO|M-OC]] deduced that the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] would be heading there to salvage spare parts. M-OC [[Mission to Tibalt|ambushed]] the Freemakers there but was unable to capture them.<ref name="TOT" />

==Prelude==
Following the [[Quest for the Kyber Saber Crystals|search]] for the [[Kyber Saber]], the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. After a series of adventures, the Freemakers found work as mechanics aboard the [[MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80A ''Home One'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]] ''[[Home One]]''.<ref name="ANH">{{FreemakerAdventures|A New Home}}</ref> [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] eventually managed to reunite with [[Commander]] [[Luke Skywalker]], who was a [[Jedi]] in the Rebel Alliance. However, their reunion was interrupted when two [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyers]] emerged from [[hyperspace]] and deployed [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]].<ref name="TOT" />

==The escape==
In response, the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] deployed its [[starfighter]] forces including Commander Skywalker, [[Lieutenant]] [[Colvett Valeria|Valeria]] and her [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]], and [[Red Two]]. Valerian planned to create a hole in the Imperial formation so that the Alliance Fleet could jump into [[hyperspace]]. Rowan attempted to help his siblings and [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]] but found that they did not need his help. In space, Valerian and Red Two managed to lose their TIE pursuers by flying straight towards each other and then pulling up. The two rebel pilots survived but their TIE pursuers crashed into each other.<ref name="TOT" />

However, the Alliance Fleet was unable to deploy its [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing starfighters]] because they lacked sufficient [[targeting laser]]s. Roger managed to find a crate which enabled Kordi to load the fighters with ordnance. The B-wings then departed ''Home One''{{'s}} hangar and managed to clear path through the TIE fighters. This enabled the Alliance Fleet to flee into hyperspace.<ref name="TOT" />

==Aftermath==
Despite the escape, the rebel starfighters sustained considerable damage. Due to a shortage of spareparts, the Freemakers decided to depart on an [[Mission to Tibalt|mission]] to [[Tibalt/LEGO|Tibalt]] to gather more wreckage. At the request of [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]], Rowan recruited the [[Mon Calamari]] starship builder [[Quarrie]], who wanted to build a starship that would turn the tide of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Their Tibalt mission brought them into conflict with the Imperial [[Hunter droid/LEGO|hunter droid]] [[M-OC/LEGO|M-OC]], who had been sent by the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor]] to capture Rowan.<ref name="TOT" />

==Behind the scenes==
