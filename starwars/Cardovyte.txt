'''Cardovyte''' was an astronomical object in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that featured the legendary [[Crystal Chaos]], an expanse of [[crystal]]line stalagmites. In [[35 ABY]], during the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] in the [[YT-1300 light freighter|freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' passed through the Crystal Chaos of Cardovyte while using [[hyperspace skipping]] to escape [[First Order]] [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] [[Mission to Sinta Glacier Colony|pursuing]] them from the [[Sinta Glacier Colony]].

==Description==
The astronomical object Cardovyte was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], situated in grid square M-6 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Crystal Chaos]] of Cardovyte<ref name="TROS VD" /> consisted of a massive cavernous structure<ref name="TROS Expanded" /> that featured a colossal forest of translucent,<ref name="TROS Junior">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> sparkling [[crystal]] stalagmites that were capable of destroying [[starship]]s.<ref name="TROS Expanded" /> At least two [[star]]s&mdash;one that shone [[Color|yellow]] and another that was red and appeared smaller&mdash;were visible in the [[Realspace|space]] near Cardovyte, casting a red glow on the celestial body. One of the stars<ref name="TROS" /> was bright, its red [[Temperature|heat]] illuminating the stalagmite forest<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> and reflecting light onto the crystal.<ref name="TROS Expanded" />

==History==
{{Quote|What are you doing?''"<br />"''Lightspeed skipping.|Finn and Poe Dameron, after hyperspace skipping away from Cardovyte|<ref name="TROS" />}}
By the [[time]] of the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the Crystal Chaos of Cardovyte had become legendary<ref name="TROS Junior" /> and was famous across [[the galaxy]],<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> featuring in [[Hologram|holo]]-adventures.<ref name="TROS Junior" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}} to [[35 ABY]].</ref> while being pursued by [[First Order]] [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] following a [[Resistance]] [[Mission to Sinta Glacier Colony|mission]] to [[Sinta Glacier Colony]], the [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] performed [[hyperspace skipping]] in the [[YT-1300 light freighter|freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'',<ref name="TROS VD" /> emerging within the stalagmites of the Crystal Chaos. Fellow Resistance member [[Finn]], who recognized the Crystal Chaos of Cardovyte from holo-adventures,<ref name="TROS Junior" /> called out to Dameron, who maneuvered the ''Millennium Falcon'' around the stalagmites as the [[TIE series|TIEs]] fired at the [[freighter]], striking several of the columns.<ref name="TROS" /> Several of the [[TIE series|TIEs]] failed to avoid the stalagmites and were destroyed, and Dameron soon engaged the [[hyperdrive]] once more, arriving at the [[Mirror-Spires]] of [[Ivexia]].<ref name="TROS Expanded" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cardovyte first appeared in the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="TROS" /> It was identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
