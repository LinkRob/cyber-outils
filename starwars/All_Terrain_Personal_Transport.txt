The '''All Terrain Personal Transport''' ('''AT-PT''') was an [[AT-series|all-terrain]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> light [[infantry]] [[walker]]<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> manufactured by [[Rothana Heavy Engineering]].<ref name="Savage Spirits" /> The AT-PT was distinguished by its pair of triple-jointed [[Limb|legs]] and single-[[pilot]] [[Cockpit|cabin]]. It was armed with twin [[blaster cannon]]s located below the cabin's [[viewport]].<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> 

The walker was developed before the height of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Knights of Fate">''[[Knights of Fate]]''</ref> a conflict fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] from [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> During the war, the three-legged [[All Terrain Attack Pod]] walker was developed based on the design of the AT-PT and the [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]] walker.<ref name="Knights of Fate" /> AT-PTs continued to be used after the war concluded and the Republic was [[Proclamation of the New Order|reformed]] into the [[Galactic Empire]], including by the [[Imperial Army]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], a [[The galaxy|galaxy-wide]] conflict between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="Galactic Defense" />

==Behind the scenes==
AT-PTs first appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] continuity in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]'', developed by [[DeNA]], released in [[2014]] and shut down in [[2016]].<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> In the [[2017]] browser game ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Imperial Heist]]'', the player is able to unlock an AT-PT to use during the [[Unidentified heist|heist]] if they fail after traveling a certain distance.<ref name="Imperial Heist">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Imperial Heist]]''</ref> The walkers were later identified as "All Terrain Personal Transports" in the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] booklet "[[Walkers and Ground Vehicles of the Empire and First Order]],"<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> which was published on [[May 11]], [[2021]].<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|lang=fr|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Official Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[All Terrain Personal Transport/Legends|All Terrain Personal Transports]] first appeared in ''[[Dark Force Rising]]'', the second volume of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Thrawn Trilogy]]'',<ref name="Dark Force Rising">''[[Dark Force Rising]]''</ref> which was published in [[1992]].<ref name="TERC">''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]''</ref> The [[Walker/Legends|walkers]] were named in the accompanying ''[[Dark Force Rising Sourcebook]]'', written by [[Bill Slavicsek]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' and released by [[West End Games]] in [[1993]].<ref name="Sourcebook">''[[Dark Force Rising Sourcebook]]''</ref>
