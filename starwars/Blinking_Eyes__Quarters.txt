"'''Blinking Eyes: Quarters'''" is an adventure from the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign that was written by [[Derrel Weaver]] and [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]]. It was released in [[January]] [[2002]] and was the first adventure in the [[Blinking Eyes trilogy]].
