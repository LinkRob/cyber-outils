The '''Crimson Dawn District''' was an area of [[Kijimi City]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]] that was under the control of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate<ref name="Outlaws" /> during [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The district was located nearby the [[Thermal District]] which contained [[The Wellspring]], the Dawn's main holdout in the city.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==History==
In 35 ABY, the district was destroyed along with the planet during the [[Destruction of Kijimi]] by the [[Sith Eternal]] with the [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Derriphan]]'' of the [[Final Order]] [[fleet]].<ref name="Destruction" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Crimson Dawn District first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The district was one of several areas in the game that was more accessible depending on the players reputation with the owning faction, in this case with Crimson Dawn<ref name="Outlaws" />
