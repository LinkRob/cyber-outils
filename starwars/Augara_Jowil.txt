'''Augara Jowil'''<ref name="OM90" /> was an [[Otolla]] [[Gungan]]<ref name="TPM" /> who was bandleader of the [[Great Municipal Band]] alongside [[Beezar Pert]].<ref name="OM90" /> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}}, which includes Augara Jowil's appearance, to [[32 BBY]].</ref> they were amongst the Gungans who gathered in the [[Gungan Sacred Place]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] after the [[Trade Federation]] [[Invasion of Naboo|invaded]] the world. At the sacred place, Jowil stood witness to [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] asking for the aid of [[Boss]] [[Rugor Nass]] of the Gungans in defeating the Federation. Nass agreed, and the [[Gungan Grand Army]] then worked with the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]] to defeat the Federation during the [[Battle of Naboo]].<ref name="TPM" /> After the battle, Jowil and Beezar and the Great Municipal Band led a parade through the streets of Naboo's [[capital]], [[Theed]], playing songs including the "[[Symponik Nabooalla/Canon|Symponik Nabooalla]]."<ref name="OM90" />

==Behind the scenes==
Augara Jowil was created for the<ref name="TPM" /> [[May 19]], [[1999]]<ref name="YearByYear">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']],<ref name="TPM" /> but was first mentioned prior to the film's release in the title of the track "Augie's Great Municipal March" on the film's [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (soundtrack)|soundtrack]],<ref name="TPM OST">[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (soundtrack)|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'' soundtrack]]</ref> which was released on [[May 4]].<ref name="YearByYear" /> In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the character's full name was used in [[Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin 90|''Star Wars - The Official Magazine'' 90]], which was released in [[2018]].<ref name="OM90" /> Jowil's full name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used in an entry for the character written by [[Chad Finke]] and published as part of the [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] feature "[[What's The Story?]]" on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="db">{{DB|character|augie|Jowil, Augara "Augie"}}</ref>
