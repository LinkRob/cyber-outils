The '''DRK-1 Dark Eye probe droid''', also known as the '''DRK-1 probe droid''', '''Sith probe droid''', '''Sith seeker''', or '''Repair droid''', was a model of [[probe droid]] used by the [[Sith]] and later the [[Galactic Empire]].

==Characteristics==
{{Listen3|DarkEyeProbeDroids-TPM.flac|DRK-1 Dark Eye probe droids}}
[[File:DRK1SithProbeDroids-SWQ08.png|thumb|270px|left|Darth Maul releases his Sith probe droids into a city environment.]]
The DRK-1 probe was a spherical [[probe droid]],<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|sith-probe-droid|Sith Probe Droid}}</ref> [[spy droid]],<ref name="TPM" /><ref name="Darth Maul II" /> and [[Cam droid|camera droid]]<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> capable of floating above the ground,<ref name="Databank" /> equipped with whisper-quiet [[repulsorlift]]s that allowed for stealthy tracking of a target.<ref name="Droidography" /> Sporting a single [[eye]], these [[droid]]s had a [[Color|black]] carapace and were fitted with a<ref name="Databank" /> long-range<ref name="Droidography" /> [[antenna]] rising from their globe-like body.<ref name="Databank" /> [[Maul|Darth Maul's]] DRK-1 probe droids spoke the [[language]] [[Sisrai]].<ref name="YouTube">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Language in Star Wars Movies|video=CZp8Civk2co|nolive=1}}</ref> Small in size and incredibly fast, the probe droids were difficult to spot with unaided senses, and sensor-baffling technology made them virtually undetectable via electronic equipment.<ref name="Rise" />

Some DRK-1 probe droids were equipped with a [[micro-grenade launcher]]. When confronted, the probe droids would attack, destroying its opponent or forcing its own destruction, providing little to no opportunities for recovery.<ref name="Rise" />

==History==
===Usage by Maul===
{{Quote|Show me.|Darth Maul commanding one of his DRK-1 Dark Eye probe droids|<ref name="Darth Maul II" />}}
The [[Jedi Order]] believed that DRK-1 probe droids were designed from specifications found in ancient [[Sith holocron]]s.<ref name="Rise" /> According to the [[B1-series battle droid]] turned [[author]] [[R0-GR]], the droids were said to be favored by [[Sith Lord]]s.<ref name="Droidography" />

[[File:DRK-1ProbeDroids-SWZ.png|thumb|left|270px|Darth Maul launches DRK-1 probes from ''Scimitar''.]]
The Sith Lord [[Darth]] Maul made use of several DRK-1 probes, keeping at least three of the [[spy droid]]s<ref name="TPM" /> aboard his personal [[starship]], the ''[[Scimitar]]''.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> He utilized these droids to track down a [[freighter]] belonging to the [[Haddrex Gang]] on [[Nar Shaddaa]], which led Maul and his [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane's group|compatriots]] to the [[Xev's hideout|hideout]] of [[Xev Xrexus]] in the [[Drazkel system]]. There, Maul followed one of the droids to the location where [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Eldra Kaitis]] was [[Prisoner|imprisoned]].<ref name="Darth Maul II" />

The one of the droids were present to warn Maul that [[Xev Xrexus]] was arriving with two [[Droideka]]s. Xrexus warned Maul he was in a restricted area, and allowed him to go. After Maul and the bounty hunter group took Kaitis from [[Jee Kra]] and the probes informed the group of a countdown, which [[Troo-tril-tek]] didn't understand but [[protocol droid]] [[FE-B3]] suggested he listen but the warning came too late with their stolen [[Shekelesh-class freight gunship|''Shekelesh''-class freight gunship]], crashing on  the [[moon of Drazkel]].<ref name="Darth Maul 3">[[Darth Maul (2017) 3|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 3]]</ref>

Following the [[freighter]]'s crash, Maul and Bane agreed to spit up, with Maul taking Kaitis and Bane taking his crew to find a new ship. The four probe droids followed Maul and Kaitis with one of the probes informing Maul that the hunters were near with the first wave being the [[Pyke Syndicate]]. As Maul fights four [[Pyke]]s, one probe manage to kill a [[Pyke Syndicate Foot Soldier|Pyke guard]] before being destroyed by another Pyke guard.<ref name="Darth Maul 4">[[Darth Maul (2017) 4|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 4]]</ref>

After Maul killed Xrexus, the remaining three probes found Maul and informed him that the bounty hunter are picking him up in a stolen [[Flarestar-class attack shuttle|''Flarestar''-class attack shuttle]], piloted by [[Aurra Sing]].<ref name="Darth Maul 5">[[Darth Maul (2017) 5|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 5]]</ref>

===Naboo and the Clone Wars===
[[File:DRK1SithProbeDroid-SWL76.png|thumb|right|200px|Maul's probe droids move through the streets of Mos Espa.]]
In [[32 BBY]], Maul launched three probe droids while seeking out [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] on [[Tatooine]], and one of them successfully located her Jedi protector, [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], in [[Mos Espa]]. The droid reported back to Maul who promptly set out on his [[FC-20 speeder bike|speeder]],<ref name="TPM" /> the ''[[Bloodfin]]'',<ref name="USW" /> to [[Duel on Tatooine|engage]] Jinn in a [[lightsaber duel]].<ref name="TPM" /> However one of these droids, after rescuing a [[labor droid]] and being saved from a [[sandcrawler]] by the same droid, decided to remain on Tatooine with its new friend.<ref name="Probe Droid Problem">{{StoryCite|story=Probe Droid Problem|book=Darth Maul (2017) 1}}</ref> Jinn relayed a report about the DRK-1 probe droid to the Jedi sometime during the blockade of Naboo,<ref name="Rise" /> shortly before his [[death]].<ref name="TPM" />

Encounters with DRK-1 probe droids were sparse. Further reports arose from isolated sites across the galaxy, but such sightings were often limited or unverifiable. During the [[Clone Wars]], it was not widely known which of Count [[Dooku]]'s agents had the authority to deploy the droids or knowledge of their missions. Although unable to recover the probes, providing limited basis for conjecture, the Jedi and [[Republic Intelligence]] correctly hypothesized the droids' use of sensor-masking technology.<ref name="Rise" />

===Delivery for Umpass-stay===

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], these droids were used as repair units by the [[Imperial Army]] to make field repairs to vehicles. They were unarmed however, leaving them defenseless against enemies.<ref name="Commander" />

[[File:First Order Patrol Droid.png|thumb|left|200px|The First Order patrol droid, the descendant of the DRK-1 Dark Eye probe droid.]]
In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Max Rebo Date">{{SW|url=news/the-illustrious-jabba-the-hutt-bids-you-welcome-to-his-new-marvel-comic-jabbas-palace-exclusive-reveal|text=The Illustrious Jabba the Hutt Bids You Welcome to His New Marvel Comic, ''Jabba's Palace'' - Exclusive Reveal}} establishes that [[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (2023)|''Star Wars: Return of the Jedi'']] takes place in the era of {{Film|VI}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref> on the [[Outer Rim]] world of [[Tatooine]], a DRK-1 probe droid approached [[Ak-rev]] and [[Umpass-stay]], a pair of [[Drum|drummers]] in service of the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]], outside [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]]. Derided by the duo, the droid informed the musicians he bore a message for Umpass-stay from his father, [[Chief]] [[Daladit-Chan]], to be relayed in absolute privacy. Explaining that as adoptive brothers, they owed each other their lives, Umpass-stay convinced the droid to deliver his message in front of Ak-rev, and the probe obliged, finding the message to be rather straightforward: kill Jabba.<ref name="Max Rebo 1" />

In his "[[droidography]]," a written work detailing the droids of [[the galaxy]], R0-GR speculated that DRK-1 probe droids were favorites of the Sith not because of their near-silent repulsorlifts but due to their all-black color scheme, declaring that the dark side warriors were spooky and did not need the probe droids to prove it. R0-GR also described the DRK-1 as an ancestor of the [[First Order]] [[Patrol droid (First Order)|patrol droid]], which he considered to be slightly less grim.<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> The book was published no earlier than [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Droidography Date">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]'' notes [[BB-8]]'s participation in the [[Battle of Oetchi|skirmish]] aboard the ''[[Supremacy]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]], placing the release of [[R0-GR]]'s [[in-universe]] "[[droidography]]" as sometime during or subsequent to that [[Standard year|year]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The DRK-1 Dark Eye probe droids were designed for the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]<ref name="ArtofI">''[[The Art of Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace]]''</ref> and first appeared in the film's Legends [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|novelization]].<ref name="Noveliation">[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'' novelization]]</ref> They were designed by concept artist [[Doug Chiang]]<ref name="ArtofI" /> and were inspired by the [[Viper probe droid]], [[IT-O interrogation droid]] and [[Marksman-H training remote]] from the [[Original trilogy]].<ref name="DB old">{{DB|droid|sithprobedroid|Sith probe droid}}</ref> The sounds of the DRK-1 droids were achieved by [[Ben Burtt]] through the use of [[Wikipedia:Shortwave radio|shortwave radio]] technology and electronically altered words voiced by himself.<ref name="The Sounds of Star Wars">''[[The Sounds of Star Wars]]''</ref>

A deleted scene "[[Content cut from Star Wars#Episode I The Phantom Menace|Farewell to Jira]]" shows Qui-Gon Jinn using [[Qui-Gon Jinn's lightsaber|his]] [[lightsaber]] to destroy one of the probe droids that was following them. Inspecting the droid, Qui-Gon notice it was unusual on Tatooine and both he and Anakin break into a run, explaining why they are running to the ship in the film.
