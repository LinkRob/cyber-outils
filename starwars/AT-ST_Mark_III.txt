The '''AT-ST Mark III''', also known as the '''Veteran AT-ST''', was an [[All Terrain Scout Transport]] variant used by the [[Imperial Army]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Due to its experimental [[armor]], it was both faster and more durable than a standard AT-ST. It was painted in a dark [[color]] scheme, and at times a gray color scheme. It was fitted with twin rotating [[heavy laser cannon]]s and was [[pilot]]ed by a veteran [[AT-ST driver|crew]] and excelled against most targets.<ref name="Commander" />

==Behind the scenes==
The AT-ST Mark III was originally created for ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'',<ref name="Commander" /> a mobile strategy [[Star Wars video games|game]] developed by [[Disney Interactive]] and released on [[August 21]], [[2014]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] devices.<ref name="Star Wars Commander release">{{Disney|subdomain=dcpi|url=star-wars-commander|text=Star Wars™: Commander}}</ref>
