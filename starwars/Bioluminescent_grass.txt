'''Bioluminescent grass''' was a type of [[Bioluminescence|light-emitting]] [[grass]] that grew in the [[Seikara Caverns]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dekien]]. The former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] noticed tall glowing grass and other bioluminescent life-forms while searching the [[cave]]s for a shard of the [[kyber mirror of Shinsui Temple]] shortly before the [[catastrophe in the Seikara Caverns]].<ref name="Ronin" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bioluminescent grass appeared in the [[2021]] non-[[canon]] novel ''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]'', written by [[Emma Mieko Candon]].<ref name="Ronin" />
