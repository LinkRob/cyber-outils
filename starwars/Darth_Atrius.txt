'''Darth Atrius''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Sith Lord]] who lived during the [[time]] the [[Sith]] were plentiful and powerful. He wielded [[Darth Atrius' lightsabers|two]] [[crossguard lightsaber]]s. At some point after the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]] recovered his [[Darth Atrius' lightsabers|pair of crossguard]] [[lightsaber]]s. She took one to a [[Unidentified crime boss (Hradreek)|crime lord]] on [[Hradreek]] whilst selling the other to the [[Galactic Empire]]. Both sabers were later destroyed by [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].

==Biography==
===Life and death===
[[File:DarthAtriusLightsabers-2015StarWarsAnnual4.png|thumb|right|250px|Darth Atrius' lightsabers, both crossguards but differed in appearance.]]
[[Darth]] Atrius lived during a [[time]] when the [[Sith]] were a powerful and dominating force in [[the galaxy]]. During his life, he constructed a [[Darth Atrius' lightsabers|pair]] of [[crossguard lightsaber]]s that he used in battle. Atrius used the [[Lightsaber|sabers]] to strike down many opponents and claim many victories. Over time Atrius' rage infected the [[lightsaber]]s, and had the ability to bring out the inner rage of the bearer, forcing them into a [[Death|killing]] frenzy. Atrius eventually [[Death|died]], but his sabers survived.<ref name="Annual 4" />

===Legacy===
[[Millennium|Millennia]] after his death, Atrius' lightsabers were discovered by the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]], who had been hired to retrieve the weapons by a [[Unidentified crime boss (Hradreek)|crime lord]] on [[Hradreek]]. One of the lightsabers was delivered to the [[crime lord]], while the other saber was sold by Starros to the [[Galactic Empire]], on the orders of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. Attempting to deceive both parties, she gave one saber to each employer, hoping they wouldn't take notice of the missing second saber. The [[Imperial officer]] she gave one of the sabers too was pleased with the saber, unaware that the Emperor was looking for the pair. Starros then gave the second saber to the [[Crime lord|crime boss]], but was called out on her deceit.<ref name="Annual 4" />

The crime boss ignited the blade and began questioning the smuggler on the location of the other saber, and fell into a frenzy, killing all of his men. The Imperial officer presented the saber to [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who planned to hand over the sabers to his [[Sith Master|Master]], [[Darth Sidious]]. When Vader learnt of the missing saber he [[Force choke|executed]] the [[officer]] and set off to recover the other. Meanwhile the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[pilot]] [[Luke Skywalker]] entered the room in which the crime boss had slaughtered his crew and found the lightsaber on the floor. He used the weapon to slaughter a squad of Imperial [[Stormtrooper]]s who discovered him with the corpses. To avoid more [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] Skywalker stole a nearby [[podracer]] and fled in it, but crashed shortly after. Realizing he had evaded the Empire, Skywalker decided that the lightsaber was too dangerous to be kept in one piece, and used his [[Skywalker lightsaber|lightsaber]] to destroy Atrius' blade.<ref name="Annual 4" />

Meanwhile, Vader ran into a group of thugs who Starros had hired to keep the Imperials off her back, he utilized the weapon to kill all of the thugs. He proceeded to track Starros to a warehouse where she had set an ambush for him&mdash;Starros detonated the roof of the warehouse, crushing Vader and allowing her to escape. After escaping the rubble, Vader observed Skywalker leaving, and decided that the saber was too unpredictable, crushing it.<ref name="Annual 4" />
