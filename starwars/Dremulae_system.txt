The '''Dremulae system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Deep Core]]. It was situated at the coordinates M-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Dremulae system was first mentioned in the "The Deep Core," an article in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 36|thirty-sixth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine<ref name="BMF36">{{BuildFalconCite|36|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> that was published around [[September 9]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMF36date">{{BMFdates|36}}</ref> It was identified as a system in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
