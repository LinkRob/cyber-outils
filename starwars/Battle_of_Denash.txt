A '''battle''' occurred in the [[Standard year|years]] leading up to the [[Battle of Yavin]] between the [[Denash insurgents]] and [[Admiral]] [[Kinshara]]'s [[One Twenty-Fifth task force]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The 125th Task Force encountered little opposition since most of the Denash insurgents had evacuated most of their ships and materiel to [[Batonn]].

==Prelude==
During the [[Imperial Era]], the pirate and insurgent leader [[Nevil Cygni|Nightswan]] waged an [[Nightswan's campaign|insurgent campaign]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Later, he relocated his operations to the [[Batonn sector]], leading to the emergence of insurgent groups on [[Denash]], [[Batonn]], and [[Sammun]]. The [[Denash insurgents]] fought against the Imperial [[Governor]] [[Wistran]]. Following the [[Battle of Scrim Island|capture]] of [[Scrim Island]] on Batonn by the [[Batonn insurgents]], [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Jok Donassius]] ordered military operations against insurgent forces in the sector. [[Admiral]] [[Kinshara]] of the [[One Twenty-Fifth task force]] was tasked with pacifying the Denash insurgents.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==The battle==
Admiral Kinshara's 125th Task Force succeeded in pacifying the Denash insurgents, who did not put up much of a fight. However, preliminary interrogation of prisoners suggested that a large portion of their ships and material had already been transferred to Batonn.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==Aftermath==
Following the Battle of Denash, Admiral Kinshara reported his findings to Fleet Admiral Donassius and the other Imperial admirals including Admiral [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] and Admiral [[Durril]]. Thrawn reported that [[Commander]] [[Karyn Faro]] had succeed in defeating the [[Sammun insurgents]] and capturing several starships and a large amount of material. By contrast, Admiral Durril's [[One Oh Third Task Force]] had been repulsed by the Scrim Island insurgents with considerable damage. Admiral Thrawn was tasked with recapturing the Scrim Island and subsequently defeated Nightswan and the Batonn insurgents.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Denash first appeared in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[2017]] novel [[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']], a rebooted [[canon]]ical origins of the titular character.
