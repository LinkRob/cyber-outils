'''Coca-Cola''' was a carbonated, [[Caffeine|caffeinated]] [[soda]] served in [[plastic]] orb bottles and [[paper]] cups at various locations throughout [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] in [[34 ABY]]. The beverage was imported by [[Jat Kaa]], a [[Lurmen]] [[Gender|male]] from the planet [[Mygeeto]], who [[Merchant|sold]] it to travelers and locals via his company, [[Jat Kaa's Coolers]], at pop-up locations around the outpost and in the nearby [[Black Spire Ruins|ancient ruins]].

==Description==
Coca-Cola was a [[Color|brown]] carbonated [[soda]] with [[caffeine]]. It was packaged in red, spherical [[plastic]] bottles with white [[Aurebesh]] [[writing]] that read "Coca-Cola" and "original taste" surrounded by a black, angular border. The beverage was sealed using a metallic, screw-on cap with several rectangular protrusions. It was also served from dispensers with [[Water|ice]] in large or regular-sized [[paper]] cups. The drink was safe for [[human]]s to consume, and an altered version of the beverage called [[Diet Coke]] was available.<ref name="Kettle" />

==History==
During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]<ref name="JKCstaff" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] and [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[Lurmen]] [[Gender|male]] [[Jat Kaa]], [[Homeworld|hailing]] from the [[planet]] [[Mygeeto]], had Coca-Cola bottles imported to [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] world of [[Batuu]]. Workers for his company, [[Jat Kaa's Coolers]], [[Merchant|sold]] them at various pop-up locations throughout the outpost and the nearby [[Black Spire Ruins|ancient ruins]].<ref name="JKCstaff" />

[[File:Coca-Cola_cart.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A Jat Kaa's Coolers vehicle serving Coca-Cola near Black Spire Ruins]]
Kaa also purchased several [[R-series]] [[Astromech droid|droids]] named [[J1-KC]], [[J2-KC]], and [[J3-KC]] from [[Mubo]],<ref name="JKCstaff" /> the owner of the [[Droid Depot]],<ref name="Depot">{{GEAttr|Droid Depot}}</ref> and [[Programmer|reprogrammed]] them to drive [[Drink cart|purpose-built vehicles]] around the outpost selling bottles of Coca-Cola. Kaa had the vehicles painted in the same color scheme as the Coca-Cola bottles, with the beverage's logo on the side. He also had two other vehicles painted in the green-and-yellow scheme of [[Sprite]] bottles and the gray-and-red scheme of Diet Coke bottles. These two vehicles were [[pilot]]ed by the R-series droids [[J4-KC]] and [[J5-KC]], respectively, and were used to deliver the Coca-Cola beverages to the other vehicles.<ref name="JKCstaff" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Timelines" /> cups filled with the soda were available at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]]<ref name="DB7" /> and [[Ronto Roasters]]<ref name="Ronto" /> as well as in the [[Crown of Corellia Dining Room]] on the [[Purrgil-class starcruiser|''Purrgil''-class starcruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''.<ref name="COCDR">{{GSAttr|Crown of Corellia Dining Room}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|When we first approached Coca-Cola and shared the idea of bringing their products into the ''Star Wars'' galaxy in a new and unique way, they immediately saw the opportunity to give our guests the refreshing drinks they love in a way that maintained the authenticity of the story &ndash; which is very important to us &ndash; and that was, frankly, just plain cool.|Scott Trowbridge, portfolio creative executive and studio leader, Walt Disney Imagineering|<ref name="Coke">{{WebCite|url=https://www.coca-colacompany.com/stories/coca-cola-and-disney-designed-these-galactic-bottles-for-star-wars-galaxy-s-edge|text=Coca-Cola and Disney Designed These Galactic Bottles for Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|author=Moye, Jay|date=2019-04-13|work=[https://coca-colacompany.com Coca-Cola Journey]|archivedate=20190620211503}}</ref>}}
[[File:Coca-Cola_crate.jpg|thumb|right|275px|A promotional display of the various specialty Coca-Cola products available at ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]
The soft drink [[Wikipedia:Coca-Cola|Coca-Cola]] is one of the beverage items available in the theme park land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]]. The beverage's ''[[Star Wars]]''&ndash;themed packaging was developed exclusively for the park as part of a collaboration between [[Walt Disney Imagineering]], [[Lucasfilm]], and [[Wikipedia:The Coca-Cola Company|The Coca-Cola Company]],<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/6-things-we-learned-at-the-star-wars-galaxys-edge-panel|text=SWCC 2019: 6 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge Panel}}</ref> which has had a partnership with Disneyland since [[1950s|1955]].<ref name="DPB Coke">{{DPB|url=2019/04/specially-designed-coca-cola-products-coming-exclusively-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Specially Designed Coca-Cola Products Coming Exclusively to Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The specially-packaged drink was revealed at [[Celebration Chicago|''Star Wars'' Celebration Chicago]] in [[April]] 2019, during the "Bringing ''Star Wars'' to Life at Disney Parks" panel,<ref name="SW.com" /> which showcased an advertisement featuring a crate full of Coca-Cola, Diet Coke, Sprite, and [[Dasani]] beverages being [[Thief|stolen]] from two distracted [[First Order stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="YouTube">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@DisneyParks|name=Disney Parks|video=5sAstC56zmE|text=Specially Designed Coca-Cola Products Coming Exclusively to Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge}}</ref>

[[File:Coke_concept.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Bottle concepts throughout the design process]]
The creative process behind the bottles took over three years, with Coca-Cola Design presenting designs in various shapes and sizes before settling on the rustic orb bottles present in ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge. [[Matt Cooper]], an associate industrial designer at Coca-Cola, stated that the goal of the final design was to evoke a sense that the bottles had just been pulled off a [[Starship|ship]] or left behind and repurposed into beverage containers.<ref name="Coke" />

The textures for the soda bottles were captured in [[Wikipedia:West Midtown|West Midtown]] in [[Wikipedia:Atlanta|Atlanta]], [[Wikipedia:Georgia (U.S. state)|Georgia]], home of The Coca-Cola Company, with Cooper creating the concept art. The bottle caps, which were also conceptualized by Cooper, were designed to look like something that belonged in the hands of the Resistance, specifically in juxtaposition to the Dasani bottle, which was made to look like it was possibly brought in by the First Order. The shapes for the orbs were designed by [[James Cha]].<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge]]''</ref> The idea to use Aurebesh for the logo originated when a group of Imagineers toured the Coke Archives in Atlanta and saw the brand's iconic [[Wikipedia:Spencerian script|Spencerian script]] logo in various languages.<ref name="Coke" /> In the trailer for ''[[Star Wars Detours]]'', which was released on [[August 14]], [[2012]], a menu in [[Dex's Diner]] includes items named "COKE" and "SODA."<ref name="Detours">{{SWYouTube|video=-yRNXFhboBI|text=STAR WARS DETOURS<sup>TM</sup> Trailer #1|date=2012-08-24}}</ref>
