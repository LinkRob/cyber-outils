The '''Alliance Light Stealth Armor''' was type of low-grade, light stealth [[armor]] created by the [[Alliance Military]]'s [[Alliance Military Research and Development|Research and Development division]]. It was created by reverse-engineering the technology behind [[Storm Commando armor|Imperial Storm Commando armor]], and implementing it into retrofitted [[phase II clone trooper armor]]. The armor was coated in a specialized material that absorbed light and sound waves, which made moving around undetected by sentries and [[sensor]]s easier.<ref name="Forged in Battle" />

The armor was commonly used by [[rebel trooper]]s and [[Rebel Alliance Special Forces]] on infiltration missions during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Forged in Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
