'''Fleshflies''' were a type of [[fly]] that [[Diet|fed]] on [[Species|animal]] carcasses.<ref name="EFV" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> the [[Nihil]] recruit [[Driggit Parse]] reflected on the way the Nihil [[Pirate|marauders]] had [[Attack on Valo|ravaged]] her [[homeworld]]&mdash;the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]&mdash;thinking that the [[Occupation of Valo|occupiers]] had swarmed over the [[Valon|locals]] like fleshflies to a carcass.<ref name="EFV" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fleshflies were mentioned in [[The High Republic: Escape from Valo|''Escape from Valo'']], a [[2024]] junior novel written by [[Daniel José Older]] and [[Alyssa Wong]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="EFV" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref>
