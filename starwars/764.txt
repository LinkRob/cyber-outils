'''764''' was a [[stormtrooper]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Whilst [[Secret mission to Tatooine|attacking]] the [[Tantive IV]] under the command of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], Fellow Stormtrooper [[184]] asked 764 for a password they needed for their mission, only to offend 764 in the process by mistaking them for [[318 (Stormtrooper)|318]]. To further confuse matters, 764 themselves had confused 184 for 318. The Stormtroopers were then encountered by [[Captain]] [[Raymus Antilles]] and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], having opened the door to the room the Stormtroopers were in by destroying the fuel cells powering the door. Leia and Antilles were able to bypass the Stormtroopers.<ref name="LEGO" />
