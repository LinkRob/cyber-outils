A '''Force dyad''', also known as a '''dyad in the Force''', was a pairing of two [[Force-sensitive]] [[Species|beings]] linked together by a unique [[Force-bond]]—that was unbreakable—that made them one in [[the Force]]. The power of a dyad was as strong as life itself, with the individuals forming the dyad sharing a connection that spanned across [[time]] and [[Realspace|space]].

Members of a dyad were attuned to each other's senses completely, including what they saw, heard, and felt. They possessed unique [[Force power]]s, such as the ability to physically interact across many [[light-year]]s in [[the galaxy]]. A dyad could also utilize [[Force healing]] to such a degree that, should either member of the dyad [[Death|die]], the other would be able to transfer enough of their own Force energy to resurrect them, albeit at the cost of their own life.

A [[Doctrine of the Dyad|prophecy]] foretold that there would be a dyad in the Force, which had been unseen for generations by the time of the [[New Republic Era]]. The prophesied dyad was realized within [[Ben Solo]] and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]. Although they were enemies during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], Solo and Rey shared a unique [[Force-bond|bond]] that featured prominently in the lore of the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Sith cultist|cultists]]. During the [[Battle of Exegol|battle]] on [[Exegol]], the life force of their bond was used to restore the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]].

==Description==
===Overview===
{{Quote|My mother was the daughter of Vader. Your father was the son of the Emperor. What Palpatine doesn't know is we're a dyad in the Force, Rey. Two that are one.|Kylo Ren, to Rey|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:DyadReyKylo-SotS.png|thumb|left|250px|Force dyads were bonded, creating a connection that effectively made two Force-sensitive beings one in the Force.]]
A [[the Force|Force]] dyad was a phenomenon that occurred when two [[Force-sensitive]] [[Species|beings]] shared a unique [[Force-bond]] with each other, connecting their [[Brain|minds]] across [[time]] and [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> They were physically two separate individuals, but they were one in the Force.<ref name="Episode IX" /> This form of connection made it possible for the bonded individuals to communicate with each other across great distances.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

A Force dyad, binding two separate beings together, was not an easily discernible phenomenon. The [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] was unaware that [[Ben Solo]] and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] were a dyad until he inadvertently siphoned a fraction of their bond's life force.<ref name="Episode IX" /> [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]], the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] master of Solo's alter ego, Kylo Ren, was aware that his apprentice had an equal in the [[light side of the Force]]. However, he incorrectly presumed that it was Solo's uncle, [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]], before realizing that it was, in fact, a [[scavenger]] girl from [[Jakku]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Capabilities===
{{Quote|Legends claim that two beings sharing this profound connection gain access to a great number of abilities&mdash;skills beyond the grasp of even the most powerful Force wielder.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="Secrets of the Sith">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref>}}
[[File:BenSoloHealsRey-TROS.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Both halves of the Force dyad possessed the rare ability of Force healing.]]
According to [[Darth]] Sidious, the Force dyad was a power like life itself;<ref name="Episode IX" /> and the members of a dyad possessed many unique [[Force power]]s that were otherwise inaccessible to even the most powerful Force wielder who lacked the dyadic relationship. He believed that if two [[Sith]] were bonded so deeply as to transcend their physical beings, the power they could unlock together would know no limits.<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" />

The beings who formed a dyad in the Force possessed the uncommon power of [[Force healing]]. It was an ability that could be used to heal a wounded person or animal, although this process temporarily weakened the healer who transferred some of their Force energy. This ability was lethal to the user if they utilized it to resurrect a deceased individual for it required them to sacrifice all of their life force.<ref name="TSWB" />

The bond between Force dyads was an exceedingly unique connection<ref name="TSWB" /> that made it possible for them to communicate across [[light-year]]s.<ref name="Kylo Ren Bio Gallery">{{Databank|kylo-ren-biography-gallery|Kylo Ren Biography Gallery}}</ref> The bond gave the dyads the ability to see and feel what the other was experiencing,<ref name="TSWB" /> and also gave them a clear sense of the other's thoughts and emotions.<ref name="TLJ EE">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> Another unique aspect about their relationship as dyads was their ability to pass objects to one another through the Force, regardless of the distance between them,<ref name="TSWB" /> through time and space in an instant,<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /> as demonstrated when Rey passed Anakin Skywalker's lightsaber to Ben Solo during their final conflict against Palpatine and the Knights of Ren.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:ReyKyloVsGuards.png|thumb|left|250px|The individuals in a dyad were able to sense each other's actions.]]
The dyad had an instinctive drive to fight back to back, defending each other when threatened by multiple enemies. Through their bond, both members of a Force dyad could sense each other's next actions.<ref name="TSWB" /> The strength of their bond became increasingly powerful when the dyads fought together&mdash;their Force powers mirroring and amplifying each other.<ref name="TROS VD" />

It was possible to [[Force drain|drain]] the dyad of their combined power, allowing a Force wielder to utilize the dyad's healing ability<ref name="Episode IX" /> while also receiving a significant increase in Force power.<ref name="TSWB" /> This process would leave the dyad in a physically weakened state, to the extent of near or total paralysis.<ref name="TROS JN">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>

==History==
===Origins===
{{Quote|For a millennium, the Sith have adhered to the Rule of Two. But this decree is said to merely be a pale imitation of its predecessor, the Doctrine of the Dyad.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" />}}
[[File:Darth_Vader_2020_Vol_2_textless_cover.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Darth Sidious tried unsuccessfully to create a dyad with Darth Vader.]]
The [[Doctrine of the Dyad]] was a concept centered on the Force dyad.<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /> The Doctrine of the Dyad featured prominently in the lore of the [[Sith Eternal]], a [[Sith cult]] that prophesized the birth of a dyad. The power of the dyad went unseen for generations by the time of the [[New Republic Era]], when the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|fall]] of the [[New Republic]] gave way to a [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

Eons before the time of Darth Sidious, the Doctrine of the Dyad was etched into the walls of the [[Sith Citadel]] on [[Exegol]], serving as a reminder of the dyad's significance to the Sith Order.<ref name="Secrets of the Sith" /> During the [[Republic Era]], [[Darth Plagueis]] sought to create a dyad with Sidious, his [[Sith apprentice]], but his attempt was unsuccessful. Sidious also tried and failed to create a dyad with his own apprentice, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. It would be [[Standard decade|decades]] before he discovered that a dyad had formed naturally between his [[Palpatine family|granddaughter]] and Vader's [[Skywalker family|grandson]], Rey and Kylo Ren.<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

===The dyad at war===
{{Quote|I warned my young apprentice that as he grew stronger, his equal in the light would rise.|Snoke, to Rey|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:ADyadintheForce.png|thumb|left|300px|Kylo Ren and Rey formed a prophesied dyad in the Force, the first in generations.]]
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the First Order [[warlord]] Kylo Ren encountered Rey, a scavenger from Jakku, while leading a [[First Order military|military]] [[Battle of Takodana|raid]] on [[Takodana]].<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> As he [[Telepathy|telepathically]] [[Mind probe (Force power)|probed]] Rey's mind, Ren became puzzled by a strange connection that he felt with her.<ref name="Kylo Ren Bio Gallery" /> Their innate connection that Ren felt with the scavenger intractably bridged their minds, but at the time he was unaware that they formed the prophesied dyad.<ref name="TROS VD" /> Even then, however, a degree of connection had existed between them both already, with a young Rey having felt Ren's fall to the dark side of the Force<ref name="Rise of Kylo Ren 4">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 4|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 4]]</ref> in [[28 ABY]].<ref name="28 Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the main events of [[The Rise of Kylo Ren 4|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 4]] to [[28 ABY]].</ref>

Ren and Rey both possessed raw, untamed strength with the Force,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> by virtue of their respective bloodlines as the grandson of Darth Vader and the granddaughter of Darth Sidious.<ref name="Episode IX" /> While searching through Rey's mind, Ren discovered that the girl could sense his thoughts and feelings as well.<ref name="Kylo Ren Bio Gallery" /> As a side effect of their bond, Rey assimilated some of Ren's Force knowledge and [[Jedi training]] while their minds were connected.<ref name="TLJ EE" /> Her latent Force powers manifested at an exponential rate, allowing her to utilize a few techniques such as [[mind trick]] and [[telekinesis]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> By delving into Rey's innermost thoughts, Ren not only sensed her loneliness and longing for family, but also discovered certain details about her past that Rey had subconsciously suppressed as a [[Youngling|child]].<ref name="TLJ EE" />

[[File:Force-bond.png|thumb|right|250px|Ren and Rey discovered their ability to connect with each other across the galaxy.]]
The bond between Ren and Rey became stronger after their [[Battle of Starkiller Base|duel]] at [[Starkiller Base]]; their connection was so raw and powerful that it reminded Rey of touching a live wire in the wreckage of a [[starship]].<ref name="TLJ EE" /> At this point, they discovered their ability to see, hear and touch each other through the Force.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> The bond allowed Rey to gain new insights into Ren,<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> who no longer attempted to hide his pain and misery from her. Convinced that the light side still existed within the former Ben Solo, Rey chose to confront Ren in person, believing she could help him turn away from the dark side of the Force.<ref name="TLJ EE" />

Ren's master, Supreme Leader Snoke, was aware of his apprentice's connection to a certain entity in the light, knowing that Ren's growing strength with the dark side meant that his equal would rise in the light. He assumed it would be Luke Skywalker, Ren's previous mentor, until he discovered Rey after her awakening in the Force. Snoke, who claimed to be the one who bridged their minds,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> gloated in his success at having gathered Rey and Ren before him, for it proved beyond a doubt that there existed a unique bond between them. Using Snoke's reliance on sensing his every thought, Ren outmaneuvered his mentor and ultimately [[Battle of Oetchi|killed]] him. With Snoke's unexpected death, the [[Praetorian Guard|Elite Praetorian Guards]] did all they could to stop their leader's [[assassin]], causing Ren and Rey to fight alongside each other. Their bond grew even more powerful in combat, amplifying the powers of the two.<ref name="TROS VD" />

===The dyad united===
{{Quote|Stand together, die together.|Darth Sidious, to Ben Solo and Rey|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:DyadofKyloRenandRey-TROS.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Ren was more determined to convert Rey after discovering their nature as a dyad.]]
Despite the sympathy that Rey developed for Ren as a result of their connection and her long isolation, she chose to withdraw from their budding relationship, due to being unable to support Ren's actions against the Resistance and his machinations to rule [[the galaxy]]. Before his death, Snoke knew that Rey and Ren were the prophesied dyad in the Force.<ref name="TROS VD" /> As the new Supreme Leader of the First Order, Ren vowed to destroy Rey for rejecting him after he entreated her to rule the galaxy by his side,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> but later confessed that he had no desire to hurt her. During the final [[Standard day|days]] of the conflict, Ren was able to use their connection as a means of tracking Rey's location, first on [[Pasaana]] and later aboard his own [[flagship]], the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']]. Having discovered that they formed a Force dyad, Ren shared this information with Rey as well as the fact that it remained unknown to Darth Sidious at the time.<ref name="Episode IX" />

With their combined power as a dyad, Ren believed that together they could destroy Sidious and claim the [[Throne of the Sith]],<ref name="Episode IX" /> a proposition which Rey denied. Though stung by her rejection,<ref name="TROS VD" /> Ren continued to pursue Rey at Sidious' behest, leading to their [[Mission to Kef Bir|final confrontation]] on [[Kef Bir]]. As they [[Lightsaber duel|fought]] on the [[Death Star ruins|wreckage]] of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]], Ren's [[Parent|mother]], [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], died reaching out to her son in the Force. His mother's sacrifice, combined with Rey's compassion and the memory of his [[Solo family|father]], [[Han Solo]], led him to cast off the persona of Kylo Ren and reclaim his former identity as Ben Solo. Meanwhile, Rey resolved to confront and destroy her grandfather, Sidious, on the dark [[Planet|world]] of Exegol.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:BenandReyKiss-TROS.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Rey kissed Ben Solo after Darth Sidious' destruction, acknowledging their bond as a dyad for the first time.]]
While the Resistance and its [[Citizens' Fleet|allies]] engaged the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]] in the [[Battle of Exegol]], Solo and Rey confronted Sidious in the heart of his citadel. Having failed to take Rey as his new vessel, Sidious resolved to destroy them both, but in the process he discovered that their bond was as powerful as life itself. Realizing that they were a Force dyad, Sidious decided to remain in his clone body and have it healed instead by nearly draining their combined life force in order to do so. Yet his triumphant return was short-lived; calling on the spirits of all the past Jedi, Rey was able to vanquish her grandfather at the cost of her life. When Solo found Rey's body, he sacrificed himself to bring her back to life. The two embraced and kissed each other&mdash;an acknowledgment of their bond as a dyad, and having truly found each other<ref name="TROS EE" />&mdash;before Solo vanished while still in Rey's arms.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Rey mourned Solo, feeling that she had lost her other half in the dyad. Solo called out to her, however, moments after becoming one with the Force. He assured Rey that he would always be with her, which gave Rey a sense of relief, realizing that Solo was not truly gone.<ref name="TROS EE" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Right from Episode VII, from the scene in which Rey is interrogated by Kylo Ren, it was clear that they have a connection, that they can understand each other, that they can literally read each other's minds. They're made uncomfortable by it and yet they're both drawn to each other. What we wanted to do was complicate that and say actually their connection is deeper than that. we began talking about them as a mythic concept, which is in Joseph Campbell, which is the mythic dyad--that they're two parts of the same whole.|Chris Terrio|<ref name="The Skywalker Legacy">{{HomeVideoCite|film=9|featurette=The Skywalker Legacy}}</ref>}}
A Force dyad first appeared in the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> The concept was first described as a dyad in the Force in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]<ref name="Episode IX" /> and was later identified as a Force dyad in the [[StarWars.com]] [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry for Emperor Palpatine/Darth Sidious.<ref name="Palpatine DB">{{Databank|emperor-palpatine-darth-sidious|Emperor Palpatine/Darth Sidious}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, in the [[2011]] novel [[Knight Errant (novel)|''Knight Errant'']], written by [[John Jackson Miller]], a similar [[Force-bond/Legends|Force bond]] existed between the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] twins [[Quillan (Sith)|Quillan]] and [[Dromika]], who were described as "separated in body, but conjoined through the Force" and "one being, that no power in science or [[Sith alchemy/Legends|Sith alchemy]] could separate."<ref name="Knight Errant">[[Knight Errant (novel)|''Knight Errant'']]</ref>

Episode IX co-writer [[Chris Terrio]] describes Kylo Ren and Rey's Force dyad as being soulmates in the Force, in which despite being enemies on opposite sides of the war, the Force acts as fate by bringing them together, allowing them to understand each other.<ref name="The Skywalker Legacy" />
