'''Electromagnets''' were a type of [[magnet]]. [[Bantha-II cargo skiff]]s were equipped with electromagnets on extendable trunks on their undersides.<ref name="ROTJ" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], which includes the appearance of the electromagnets, to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] allies of the [[droid]]s [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]] used two electromagnets on the underside of a Bantha-II cargo [[skiff]] to pull the two droids out of the sand near the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], where the duo had become partially buried after jumping from the side of the [[sail barge]] ''[[Khetanna]]'' shortly before its [[Rescue of Han Solo|destruction]].<ref name="ROTJ" />
