The '''boiler rifle''' was a model of short-range [[blaster rifle]] that could be modified with an [[Barrel|underbarrel]] spin-sealed bottle of [[tibanna]] gas that illegally doubled the weapon's fire power. In [[1 BBY]], the [[Rebel Alliance Special Forces]] [[commando]] [[Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek]] used a [[black market]] boiler [[rifle]] during the [[Battle of Scarif]].

In [[9 ABY]], a group of [[hunter]]s used one such rifle when ambushing the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The weapon was later used by the [[Kanjiklub]] criminal group, with the group's [[Lieutenant]] [[Razoo Qin-Fee]] maintaining and modifying the weapons. Two members of the gang carried boiler rifles while [[Skirmish aboard the Eravana|boarding]] the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]]'s [[Baleen-class heavy freighter|freighter]], the ''[[Eravana]]'', in [[34 ABY]].

==Description==
[[File:Boiler_rifle.png|thumb|left|300px|Boiler-rifles used spin-sealed bottles of tibanna gas.]]
Boiler [[rifle]]s were a type of short-range [[blaster rifle]]. The body, grip, and trigger were [[Color|black]] in color, as was the weapon's [[stock]], which attached to the body on an extended recoil-absorbing stalk. The [[barrel]] was silver in color and had a spin-sealed bottle<ref name="TFAVD" /> of explosive<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> [[tibanna]] [[gas]] attached to its underside,<ref name="TFAVD" /> a modification that doubled the weapon's firepower but was dangerous and illegal.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> Such a modification quickly burned through<ref name="RogueOneUVG" /> the circuitry of the weapon's<ref name="G&G">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref> [[galven barrel]], meaning that the weapon required constant upkeep.<ref name="RogueOneUVG" /> The rifles also had a [[scope]] attached above the body, although these were mostly for show due to the rifle's short range.<ref name="TFAVD" /> When being prepared to fire, the rifle emitted a whirring sound.<ref name="Chapter10">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 10: The Passenger}}</ref>

==History==
===Battle of Scarif===
[[File:PaoCrouch-RO.png|thumb|right|300px|The commando Pao used a boiler rifle on Scarif.]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="RogueOneDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which includes the appearance of the boiler rifle, to [[1 BBY]].</ref> the [[Drabatan]] [[Rebel Alliance Special Forces|Special Forces]] [[commando]] [[Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek]] used a [[black market]] tibanna-jacked boiler rifle during the [[Battle of Scarif]], in which a [[Rogue One|group]] of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] forces traveled to the [[planet]] [[Scarif]] in order to steal the [[Death Star plans|plans]] for the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]] from the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Citadel Tower]].<ref name="RogueOneUVG" />

During the mission, Pao wore four replacement galven [[barrel]]s on each [[Limb|wrist]].<ref name="RogueOneUVG" /> The Drabatan proved himself with the blaster during the mission, but [[Death|perished]]<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> while fighting [[stormtrooper]]s on the beaches of Scarif in order to create a distraction for rebels who had entered the tower to steal the plans.<ref name="RogueOne">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> An [[Unidentified artist|artist]] compiling a [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]] of notes and sketches included a sketch of Pao carrying his boiler rifle in the [[book]], which was restored, expanded, and displayed at the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="Alien Archives">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> at some point no earlier than [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>

===Tatooine ambush===
[[File:BoilerRifleMandalorian.png|thumb|left|200px|A boiler rifle was used during an ambush of Din Djarin.]]
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoDate|10|the appearance of the boiler [[rifle]]}}</ref> a group of four [[Hunting|hunters]] in possession of a boiler rifle ambushed the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] and the<ref name="Chapter10" /> [[foundling]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]]<ref name="Chapter13">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 13: The Jedi}}</ref> on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The group used a trip wire to destroy the [[speeder bike]] that their targets were traveling on and then attacked them with other [[blaster]]s, leaving the boiler rifle in a large case to the side.<ref name="Chapter10" />

After the Mandalorian managed to defeat [[Biran Redben|one]] of his attackers and disarm [[Unidentified Kajain'sa'Nikto's ally 1|another]], the disarmed hunter ran to the case and picked up the boiler rifle, aiming it at Djarin as the Mandalorian [[Unarmed combat|grappled]] with two other attackers. Before the rifle could be fired, Djarin launched a [[fibercord whip]] from his [[gauntlet]] to pull the boiler rifle from its wielder's [[Limb|hands]] and whip it into the heads of the two grapplers, knocking them out. Djarin then defeated the final attacker and safely left with Grogu.<ref name="Chapter10" />

===Kanjiklub===
[[File:Volzang_with_rifle.png|thumb|right|200px|Volzang Li-Thrull carried a boiler rifle.]]
During the [[Cold War]] between the [[Resistance]] and the [[First Order]], boiler rifles were used by members of the [[Kanjiklub]] criminal organization. [[Lieutenant]] [[Razoo Qin-Fee]] maintained the boiler rifles used by its members, constantly modifying and upgrading them.<ref name="TFAVD" />

In the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|VII}} to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Kanjiklub members [[Tolomar Reez]]<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> and [[Volzang Li-Thrull]] carried tibanna-jacked boiler rifles when they [[Skirmish aboard the Eravana|boarded]] the [[Baleen-class heavy freighter|''Baleen''-class heavy freighter]] ''[[Eravana]]''<ref name="TFAVD" /> to reclaim a debt from the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]]; however, the rifles were unable to penetrate the skin of the [[Death|deadly]] [[rathtar]]s that the gang members encountered onboard.<ref name="TFA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Boiler rifles first appeared in the [[2015]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="TFA" /> They were first identified by name in ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] in conjunction with the film.<ref name="TFAVD" />
