The '''Aruza system''' was located in the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="The Interior" /> It was situated in grid square I-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Star system|system]] was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Aruzan]] [[species]] and contained the celestial body [[Aruza]], which was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Gender|female]] Aruzan [[Manaroo]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Bounty Hunters 14|''Bounty Hunters'' 14]], which depicts [[Manaroo]] as a [[prisoner]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Manaroo was held as a [[prisoner]] in the [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 14">[[Bounty Hunters 14|''Bounty Hunters'' 14]]</ref> of the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] as leverage against her boyfriend [[Dengar]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Aruza system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Aruza system/Legends|Aruza system]]<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> first appeared through the appearance of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Aruza/Legends|Aruza]] in "[[Payback: The Tale of Dengar]]," a short story written by [[Dave Wolverton]] that was published in the [[1996]] anthology ''[[Tales of the Bounty Hunters]]''.<ref name="Payback">{{StoryCite|story=Payback: The Tale of Dengar|book=Tales of the Bounty Hunters}}</ref> The [[Star system/Legends|system]] was then identified in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA" />
