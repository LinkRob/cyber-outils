'''Eban''' was molecular [[Botanist/Legends|botanist]] who belonged to a [[marsupial]] [[Species/Legends|species]]. He once attended a [[Vector Day]] party organized by the [[Quenk jazz/Legends|Quenk jazz]] artist [[Mrp-Mrp Poo]]. At some point during the party, one of the segments of [[Tireis Blackandwhite]]&mdash;a [[Gender/Legends|female]] of a species that could divide herself into separate entities&mdash;wished to leave early in the company of the dashing Eban, which provoked an argument with her other segment. During the event, Eban proudly wore his ceremonial [[Helmet of Fourteen Confusions]], which, according to journalist [[Dyslogia Twang]], although it improved his looks, it caused him to break into impromptu speeches written in the [[41st eon]] as he passed through [[radar detector]]s.<ref name="MAGL">''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''</ref>
