A [[Gender|female]] individual was the '''mother''' of the [[Twi'lek]] girl [[Drua]], a [[Youngling|child]] who lived on the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. The [[Parent|mother]] [[Death|died]] in a [[winter]] [[season]]<ref name="LOTS" /> around [[16 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> which left Drua an orphan, as the girl had never known her [[Drua's father|father]]. Afterward, Drua lived with her [[Drua's grandfather|grandfather]] in a [[Drua's Village|village]] on Ryloth.<ref name="LOTS" /> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Drua spoke to the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lords]] [[Darth Sidious]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]&mdash;unaware of their true identities&mdash;and told them of her mother's death as she led the pair to her [[City|village]].<ref name="LOTS" />
