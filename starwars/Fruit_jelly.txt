{{Quote|I'd use whatever was in season in the castle gardens&mdash;includin' durang fruit, oi-oi berries, and even the occasional chando pepper. I always liked serving the jellies family style, so that folks could take as much as they wanted&mdash;and they always did. '''Fruit jelly''' was a [[jelly]] [[Foodstuff|dish]] that could be made with a variety of different [[fruit]]s. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] served them without charge as a signature dish while he worked at [[Takodana Castle]], using the popular jellies to prepare customers' palates for later courses.

In [[34 ABY]], a platter of the jellies sat on a table at the castle where the castle's owner, [[Maz Kanata]], sat with the guests [[Han Solo]], [[Finn]], [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], and [[BB-8]] shortly before the [[First Order]] [[Battle of Takodana|destroyed]] the establishment. Tuggs moved on to work out of a [[Tuggs' Grub|food freighter]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]], but never served the jellies there, although he did include an updated recipe for the dish in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', in [[35 ABY]].

==Description==
{{Quote|This updated recipe uses my classic Takodanan technique, blendin' shi-shok I picked up on Kashyyyk with brub berries from the Forest Moon of Endor. Scoop yourself up a healthy servin' now, before someone else gets it first!|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
Fruit jellies were a [[Foodstuff|dish]] consisting of pieces of [[fruit]] suspended in<ref name="VE" /> or mixed with wobbly [[jelly]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> A variety of fruits could be used, including [[chando pepper]]s, [[durang fruit]], [[heartweed]], [[honey melon]], [[muja fruit]], [[oi-oi]]s,<ref name="VE" /> [[shi-shok]], or [[Brub berry|brub berries]]. The dish could be served in a [[glass]] with the jelly broken up and mixed with the fruit<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> or on a platter with the jelly in a domed shape with the fruit suspended inside.<ref name="VE" />

==History==
{{Quote|I've always claimed that my food freighter serves up the tastes of Takodana, but there's one taste that's never made its way onto the menu&mdash;my signature fruit jellies. I used to put platters of these wobblin' wonders out on the tables at Maz's castle to prep my customers' palates for the courses to come.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
[[File:Fresh_fruit_jellies.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Fruit jellies were served on platters at Takodana Castle.]]
While working as a [[chef]] in the kitchens of [[Takodana Castle]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> [[planet]] [[Takodana]],<ref name="VD" /> the [[Artiodac]] [[Strono Tuggs]] made fruit jellies as a signature dish. Using whatever produce from the castle's [[garden]]s was fresh as ingredients, Tuggs prefered serving the dishes family style so that everyone could help themselves to as much as they wanted. He laid out platters of the jellies on the various dining tables in the castle and did not charge for the food, using it to prepare the customers palates for what was to come in later courses. The jellies were always popular and had the Artiodac charged for them they would have been his best seller by some margin.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|VII}} to [[34 ABY]].</ref> a platter that included fresh dome-shaped fruit jellies that included different ingredients such as chando [[pepper]]s, durang fruit, heartweed, honey [[melon]], muja fruit, and oi-oi<ref name="VE" /> was sat with other plates of food on a Takodana Castle table that [[Maz Kanata]], the [[alien]] owner of the castle, used while sitting to talk with the [[droid]] [[BB-8]] and the [[human]] guests [[Han Solo]], [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], and [[Finn]]. Shortly after the group left the table, Takodana Castle was [[Battle of Takodana|destroyed]] by the [[First Order]].<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref>

Tuggs survived the castle's destruction and soon began serving food out of a [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|food freighter]] called [[Tuggs' Grub]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the planet [[Batuu]]. Although he claimed that he served a "taste of Takodana" at the [[freighter]], Tuggs never included the fruit jellies on the menu. However, after taking a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> he created an updated fruit jelly recipe using shi-shok he had picked up on the planet [[Kashyyyk]] and brub [[Berry|berries]] from the [[moon]] [[Endor]]. The new version of the dish still used his classic Takodanan technique, but was served with the jelly mixed with the fruit in a glass rather then in a domed form. He included the recipe in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 ABY.<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fruit jelly first appeared in the [[2015]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="TFA" /> The dish was first identified in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''.<ref name="VE" /> The [[2023]] recipe book ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]], includes an [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for fruit jellies which makes use of [[Wikipedia:Strawberry|strawberry]] [[Wikipedia:Gelatin|gelatin]], boiling [[Wikipedia:Water|water]], cold [[Wikipedia:List of lemon-lime drink brands|lemon lime soda]], [[Wikipedia:Lime (fruit)#Uses|lime juice]], balled [[Wikipedia:Pitaya|dragonfruit]], halved [[Wikipedia:Grape|grapes]], and [[Wikipedia:Blueberry|blueberries]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
