The '''Barchetta River''' was on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]], located about 45-minutes west of [[Capital City]]. [[Westhills]] was located just beyond the river. The [[Galactic Empire]] built larger bridges across the river to accommodate their increases in harvesting and production.<ref name="EotG" />
