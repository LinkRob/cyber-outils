The '''Black Forest''' was a [[forest]] on [[Kashyyyk]] that had been [[Death|dead]] millennia before<ref name="Life Debt" /> [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Timeline">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> The native [[Wookiee]]s believed that the area had been poisoned by a darkness, and some believed that the Black Forest was cursed and haunted by bad spirits. The crash site of the prison [[Starship|ship]] ''[[Ashmead's Lock]]'' located there.<ref name="Life Debt" />
