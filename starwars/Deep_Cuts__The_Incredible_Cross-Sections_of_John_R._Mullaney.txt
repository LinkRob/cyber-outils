'''Deep Cuts: The Incredible Cross-Sections of John R. Mullaney''' is an article by [[Christopher Cooper]] published in [[Star Wars Insider 234|''Star Wars Insider'' 234]] on [[October 14]], [[2025]]. It features an interview with the artist [[John Mullaney]] about his work in the ''[[Inside the Worlds of]]'' and ''[[Incredible Cross-Sections]]'' reference books series by [[Dorling Kindersley|DK]].<ref name="Insider" />
