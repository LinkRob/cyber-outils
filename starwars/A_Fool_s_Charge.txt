"'''A Fool's Charge'''" is an adventure in ''[[The Unknown Regions]]'', a [[2010]] supplement to the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game Saga Edition Core Rulebook|Saga Edition]] from [[Wizards of the Coast]], which was written by [[Sterling Hershey]], [[Rodney Thompson]], and [[Daniel Wallace]]. The story sends a group of characters on an errand for [[Lieutenant/Legends|Lieutenant]] [[Amalia]], who asks them to recover a stolen piece of military gear and to do so secretly. The characters travel to the area where the hardware was stolen and come under attack themselves. After combatting [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighters]], the characters track their attackers to the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[O'reen]], in the [[Unknown Regions/Legends|Unknown Regions]]. There, they see a [[Cruiser/Legends|battle cruiser]] being prepped for a military excursion in [[Orbit/Legends|orbit]]. They sneak past the patrols and land on the world, where they break into an [[Unidentified analysis station|analysis center]] and recover their prize, a top-secret [[Tactical computer/Legends|tactical computer]]. The [[O'reenian]]s detect them, though, so they fight their way back outside and to their [[Starship/Legends|starship]] to escape.<ref name="AFC 204">{{WizCite|book=The Unknown Regions|story=A Fool's Charge}} 204</ref>
