'''''From a Certain Point of View''''' is the unabridged audio version of the [[canon]] short-story [[From a Certain Point of View|anthology book of the same name]]. The audiobook features narrations by staple performers like [[Marc Thompson]] and [[January LaVoy]], as well as celebrities [[Jon Hamm]] and [[Neil Patrick Harris]]. It was released by [[Random House Audio]] on [[October 3]], [[2017]].

==Publisher's summary==
'''''Experience Star Wars: A New Hope from a whole new point of view.'''''

''On May 25, 1977, the world was introduced to Han Solo, Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia, C-3PO, R2-D2, Chewbacca, Obi-Wan Kenobi, Darth Vader, and a galaxy full of possibilities. In honor of the fortieth anniversary, more than forty contributors lend their vision to this retelling of Star Wars. Each of the forty short stories reimagines a moment from the original film, but through the eyes of a supporting character.''<ref name="Penguin" />

==Development==
Although described officially as unabridged, the audiobook does not include any form of the comic "[[Far Too Remote]]," by [[Jeffrey Brown]]. This is likely because of the story's comic format, which would not work without visuals. Even in the cover says 40 stories, only contains 39.

It was directed by [[Kevin Thomsen]] with both [[Aaron Blank]] and [[Nick Martorelli]] serving as executive producers.<ref name="Audiobook" />
