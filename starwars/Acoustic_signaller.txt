{{Top}}
'''Acoustic signallers''' were components which could be installed into [[droid]]s. The droid [[HURID-327]] was equipped with a [[binary]] acoustic signaller.<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

{{Tech-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Acoustic signallers were first mentioned in ''[[Galaxy Guide 7: Mos Eisley]]'', a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', written by [[Martin Wixted]] and released in [[1993]]. They were confirmed to be [[canon]] by the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released in [[2015]].
