'''Addoa Key''' was an island of the [[Leeward Chain]] within the [[Gem Cities of Kwenn]] on the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]]. [[Rendili Hyperworks]] operated on the island, which was home to [[Factory|factories]] connected via [[spaceport]] to the [[Kwenn Space Station]] above the planet. Addoa Key was linked via bridges to [[Malbaira Key]] and elsewhere on Kwenn.<ref name="The Living Force" />
