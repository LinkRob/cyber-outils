'''Arzen fields''' were a type of terrain where agriculture was possible. When the weather on a [[Raspar system/Legends|Raspar system]] [[Unidentified planet (West Fissure)|planet]] was warm, especially during the months [[Nnters]] and [[Hinggnih]], [[Mooxx fat]] could accumulate and create clouds over arzen fields, which made irrigation difficult.<ref name="MAGL">''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Arzen fields were mentioned in a one-page weather forecast titled "Region 29: The West Fissure," written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that collects concept designs and photographs from various [[George Lucas]] projects.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]].<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
