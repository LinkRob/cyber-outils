A '''podracing track''' was located at the edge of a [[Unidentified city (Ankhural)|city]] on the [[planet]] [[Ankhural]]. Shortly after the [[Battle of Hoth]], members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] utilized the [[podracing]] track for repairs on their [[Starship|ships]], the ''[[Thunderstrike]]'' and ''[[Apailana's Promise]]''.<ref name="Twilight" />
