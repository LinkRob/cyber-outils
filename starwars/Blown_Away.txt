"'''Blown Away'''" was an interactive associated with the [[Clouds of Genarius trilogy]] for the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It ran one time only, at the [[Gen Con Game Fair 2001]] and was attended by about 60 players. The events were chronicled in-universe in the article [[The Sivulliq Is Out There -- Somewhere|''The ''Sivulliq'' Is Out There -- Somewhere'']].

The 60 players were divided into seven teams, each trying to reach the generator. Along the way, they encountered such obstacles as bandits trying to loot the falling city, dark Jedi, and some bar fights. A number of members of the [[RPGA]] played as NPCs for the characters to interact with. They were [[August Hahn]] as [[Thurm Loogg]], [[Sean Hanlin]] as [[Xav Verivax]], [[Scott Lahna]] as [[Shanahan|"Dewback" Shanahan]], and [[Adam Norman]] as [[Miim Te'Suub]],.

==Plot summary==
The [[SoroSuub Corporation/Legends|SoroSuub Corporation]] was holding their annual celebration to mark the anniversary of their first [[G-59 Cannibalizer|Cannibalizer]] production in the [[Edic Bar]] facility. Held on the resort world of [[Tolea Biqua]] when a [[Radiation/Legends|radiation storm]] swept over the floating city, the city's shields were knocked out and the generators holding the city afloat damaged. A group of heroes from [[Cularin/Legends|Cularin]] rushed to fix the station before it was crashed into the planet below.
