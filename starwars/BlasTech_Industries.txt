'''BlasTech Industries''' was an arms manufacturer operating from the time of the [[Clone Wars]] ([[22 BBY]]) to the [[Cold War]] era ([[29 ABY|29]]-[[34 ABY]]). It notably produced the [[DC-15 family]] of [[blaster rifle]]s and [[Blaster carbine|carbines]] for the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]] used by the [[Stormtrooper Corps]] and the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Army|Army]] as well as the [[DH-17 blaster pistol]], [[A300 blaster rifle]], and the [[DL-18 blaster pistol]] used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Following the signing of the [[Galactic Concordance]] and the replacement of the Galactic Empire with the [[New Republic]], BlasTech spun off a subsidiary with [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]], the [[Sonn-Blas Corporation]], to operate within the borders of the [[First Order]]. The Merr-Sonn corporation continued its operation throughout the Cold War and the [[First Order-Resistance War]] and supplied the [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper corps]] of the First Order with [[F-11D blaster rifle]]s and [[SE-44C blaster pistol]]s.

==History==
===Republic and Imperial Eras===
[[File:USTSearcher.png|thumb|left|250px|BlasTech armed the Galactic Empire with designs, including the infamous E-11 used by the Stormtrooper Corps.]]
BlasTech Industries was a prominent weapons manufacturer in the [[galaxy]] during the time of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Following the [[Clone trooper|creation]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] by the [[Kaminoan]] cloners, BlasTech was selected to manufacture their weaponry, which it did with the [[DC-15 family]] of blaster weapons. This included the [[DC-15A blaster rifle]] and the [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] as well as more specialized weaponry.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> In part due to BlasTech's importance to the Republic war effort, BlasTech's parent company, the [[Techno Union]], was able to maintain its shaky claim of corporate neutrality<ref name="Rise" /> despite favoring the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="HC 59">{{HelmetCollectionCite|59|Databank A-Z}}</ref>

During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], BlasTech also secured numerous contracts with the [[Galactic Empire]], manufacturing the notorious [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]]<ref name="UltimateSW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> and its specialized versions<ref name="ROUVG" /> for the [[Stormtrooper Corps]] as well as the [[Imperial Army]] and [[Imperial Navy|Navy]].<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> The E-11 became the most successful small arm in the company's history, and the increased demand for BlasTech weapons resulted in the company expanding its operation with new factories, which were built on [[Planet|worlds]] the Empire had expanded to, such as [[Lothal]].<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> The company established a weapons laboratory in the [[Easthills]] near Lothal's [[Capital City]].<ref name="Imperial Justice">''[[Servants of the Empire: Imperial Justice]]''</ref>

===New Republic Era===
After the [[New Republic]] victory in the [[Battle of Jakku]] and the surrender or escape of all remaining Imperial factions still active in the galaxy,<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> acting [[Galactic Emperor]] and former [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]], along with [[Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] signed the [[Galactic Concordance]] in [[Chandrila]] to formally end the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> After the fall of the Empire, the Industries continued to be active. The BlasTech-produced [[EL-16HFE blaster rifle]] was used by the [[New Republic]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> The [[IB-94 blaster]] pistol, [[The Mandalorian's IB-94 blaster pistol|one]] of which was used by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]], was produced by BlasTech at some point.<ref name="Rubies II">{{WebCite|url=https://www.rubies.com/the-mandalorian-blaster-small-the-mandalorian.html|text=The Mandalorian Blastech IB-94 Blaster (Small Size) &ndash; The Mandalorian|work=Rubies II, LLC|archivedate=20201221124556}}</ref> Djarin's fellow [[The Tribe|Tribe]] member [[Paz Vizsla]] used the [[M-55 Medium Repeating Blaster]]<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> as a [[Heavy Infantry Mandalorian]].<ref name="Secrets of the Bounty Hunters">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref>

[[File:Hux-blaster-and-stormtroopers.png|thumb|right|300px|Thanks to BlasTech and Merr-Sonn creating the Sonn-Blas Corporation to secretly supply the First Order, its military was armed with a new generation of blasters.]]
The New Republic-Imperial treaty prohibited the deployment of armed stormtrooper forces in the galaxy or the sale of arms to them. To circumvent this prohibition,<ref name="TFAVD" /> BlasTech, working with rival<ref name="TSWB" /> munitions manufacturer [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]], spun off a subsidiary, [[Sonn-Blas Corporation]], to manufacture and supply weapons for the [[First Order]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|Stormtrooper Corps]]. This subsidiary operated from the time of the [[Cold War]] to the [[First Order-Resistance War]],<ref name="TFAVD" /> manufacturing and supplying<ref name="TSWB" /> blasters and other assorted weaponry for the First Order's militias.<ref name="TFAVD" /> Sonn-Blas would prove to be the young regime's primary weapons supplier and helped ensure Merr-Sonn and BlasTech profited off the First Order.<ref name="TSWB" /> The First Order's rival, General [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] [[Resistance]], used by-that-point outdated BlastTech [[EL-16 blaster rifle|EL-16s]] and EL-16HFEs.<ref name="TFAVD" />

==Weapons of BlasTech==
===Pistols===
[[File:DL-44_Blaster_Pistol_DICE.png|thumb|left|250px|The DL-44 heavy blaster pistol was manufactured by BlasTech.]]
Over the lifespan BlasTech Industries, countless models and designs of [[blaster pistol]]s were conceived and produced. From the hard hitting [[DL-44 heavy blaster pistol]] to the "light" [[EC-17 hold-out blaster]] along with countless other models designed for both state-owned and private use. The Automatic [[DH-17 blaster pistol]] proved so successful that [[army]]<ref name="USW" /><ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref> and [[navy]] personnel of both the [[Alliance Military|Alliance]] and [[Imperial Military]] used the model during the Galactic Civil War.<ref name="Episode IV" /> BlasTech also manufactured the DL-44 heavy blaster pistol favored by [[Han Solo]].<ref name="TFAVD" />

===Rifles and carbines===
[[File:DealGoneBad.png|thumb|right|300px|Boba Fett fires an EE-3, a model of blaster carbine produced by BlasTech.]]
BlasTech also produced other blaster types, including [[blaster rifle]]s and [[blaster carbine]]s. One example of a blaster rifle produced by the company was the DC-15A blaster rifle,<ref name="USW" /> which was identified as a [[heavy blaster rifle]] model.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> The E-11 medium blaster rifle<ref name="Rebels Visual">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> was produced by BlasTech, with further "E-11" designs, such as the [[E-11s sniper rifle]]<ref name="GaG">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref> and [[E-11D blaster carbine]], also being manufactured by BlasTech.<ref name="ROUVG" /> Other blaster carbines produced by BlasTech included the [[EE-3 carbine rifle]]<ref name="USW" /> and [[EE-4 carbine rifle]].<ref name="SG" />

===Other blaster models===
BlasTech was also able to produce [[blaster cannon]]s, such as the [[Z-6 rotary blaster cannon]] it produced,<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> with [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]] also involved in its production.<ref name="Blasters and Firearms">{{EncyclopediaCite|Blasters and Ranged Weapons}}</ref> BlasTech also produced [[sniper rifle]]s, such as the E-11s sniper rifle<ref name="GaG" /> and [[DH-447 sniper rifle]].<ref name="ROUVG" />

===Multi-purpose blasters===
{{Main|Reconfigurable blaster}}
BlasTech industries manufactured and designed several blaster models which could be [[Reconfigurable blaster|configured]] for a number of different roles. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] used the [[A-180 blaster|A-180]] and [[A280-CFE convertible heavy blaster pistol|A280-CFE]]. Both blaster pistols could be configured into an [[sniper rifle]] and an [[assault rifle]].<ref name="ROUVG" />

The DL-44 heavy blaster pistol could be turned into a [[blaster rifle]], with the addition of an extended barrel and a stock.<ref name="Solo Official Guide" />

The company also sold reconfigurable blasters such as the [[A300 blaster rifle|A-300 blaster rifle]], which gave users the option to remove the long air-cooled [[barrel-shroud]] and the shoulder [[stock]] to allow for greater movement. A collapsing barrel could be pulled out to allow for greater accuracy without the need for previously mentioned heavy barrel. Note that the rifle could not be reduced to a [[pistol]].<ref name="ROUVG" />

===Vehicle weaponry===
Apart from personal weapons, BlasTech also manufactured heavier weaponry that was equipped on vehicles. [[Ezra Bridger]]'s [[Sabine's speeder bike|speeder bike]] was equipped with twin [[JB-37 blaster cannon|JB-37]] [[blaster cannon]]s manufactured by BlasTech.<ref name="Databank Ezra's Speeder Bike">{{Databank|ezras-speeder-bike|Ezra's Speeder Bike}}</ref>
