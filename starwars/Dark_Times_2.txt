'''''Dark Times'' 2''' is the second issue of the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Times]]'' comic series. It was released on [[January 24]], [[2007]]. It was written by [[Randy Stradley|Mick Harrison]] from a plot by [[Randy Stradley|Welles Hartley]], illustrated by [[Douglas Wheatley]], and colored by [[Ronda Pattison]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Despite his acceptance of the Sith ways, Darth Vader is shocked to find the Empire&mdash;and himself&mdash;complicit in an evil he thought he had put behind him. A Separatist freedom fighter deals with the realization that the sacrifice made by his brothers-in-arms has been for nothing&mdash;and that their families are paying the price for their resistance. The crew of a smuggler's ship is forced to take sides in a conflict they have sought to avoid&mdash;and which has already changed the course of all their lives&hellip;''

''The dawn of the Empire plunges the galaxy into dark times, and every direction leads to danger!''
