'''Darbus Mizz''' was a human [[Gender|male]] [[Bespin Wing Guard]] who worked for [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]]. When the [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] occupied [[Cloud City]] he got an order from Lando to bring [[Yathros Condorius the First]] to a safe place. After they found [[Darbus Mizz's brother|Mizz's brother]] dead, Yathros convinced Mizz to stay and help [[Liberation of Cloud City|liberate]] Bespin.<ref name="The Man Who Built Cloud City" />
