'''Drift''' was a term for a fleet of [[Jedi Vector]] [[starfighter]]s, flown by the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]], flying together in both [[starfighter combat]] and demonstration maneuvers.<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

Using [[the Force]], a group of [[Jedi]] piloting their Vectors could achieve coordination that was beyond normal formation flying. Jedi could bridge their minds and make their ships move as one.<ref name="Chronicle">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref>

During an [[outreach program]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] witnessed a Drift of three hundred Vectors. Zettifar regarded it as the most beautiful things he had seen.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
