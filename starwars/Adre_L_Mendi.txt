'''Adre L'Mendi''' was an [[Imperial officer]] who served in the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. As part of the [[Imperial Navy]], L'Mendi held the [[military rank]] of [[captain]] and commanded the ''[[Tenacious]]'', an [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]]. During the [[Liberation of Erso]], Captain L'Mendi was notified of a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[starship]] over the [[planet]] [[Wobani]]. When the ''Tenacious'' arrived, it destroyed the Alliance [[corvette]] [[Vigilant (CR90 corvette)|''Vigilant'']] and deployed [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] reinforcements.<ref name="Battles" />

[[Red Flight (Rebel Alliance)|Red Flight]] attacked the [[tractor beam targeting array]] of the ''Tenacious'' allowing for the escape of all rebel forces. Captain L'Mendi's failure had devastating consequences for the Empire,<ref name="Battles" /> as [[Jyn Erso]]'s actions led to the [[Battle of Scarif]] which witnessed the theft of the Empire's [[Death Star plans]].<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> The theft allowed for the Rebel Alliance to destroy the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] and eliminate high-ranking members of the [[Imperial Military]] such as [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] and members of the [[Joint Chiefs of the Imperial Military|Joint Chiefs]].<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>
