The '''Bureau of Standards Ghorman field office''' was an office of the [[Imperial Bureau of Standards]] tasked with the standardization of local production on [[Ghorman]] which included [[ghorlectipod]]-related inventories and audits. In [[3 BBY]], the office was a recent addition to an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial office complex|office complex]] near the [[Palmo Plaza]] in the [[planet]]'s [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Palmo]].<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|bureau-of-standards-ghorman-field-office|Bureau of Standards Ghorman field office}}</ref> During this year the [[Ghorman Front]] placed a [[listening device]] in the [[Division Chief]] office where they listened to [[Syril Karn]].<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" /> Later that year the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] searched the office for listening devices during which they found the one planted by the Ghorman Front, however [[Leeza]], an operative for the Front working in the office, was able to warn the group which deactivated the connection preventing the ISB from tracing the device.<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere">{{Andor|I Have Friends Everywhere}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The field office appeared in the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the live-action series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', first appearing in the series's fourth episode, "[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]," which was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" /> on [[April 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref>
