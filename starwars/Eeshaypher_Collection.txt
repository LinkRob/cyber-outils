The '''Eeshaypher Collection''' was a forbidden gallery of artifacts, relics, and antiques that were all related to the [[Dark side of the Force]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref> By [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Shadow of the Sith Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] had come into possession of an ancient book that was a catalog of the Eeshaypher collection. While studying the book on the [[planet]] [[Tython]] that [[Standard year|year]], Skywalker discovered an entry about a [[Sith wayfinder]] within it, the appearance of which he found analogous to that of a [[Sith holocron]]. The book described the Sith wayfinder as being used for the purpose of navigation, specifically to the ancient hidden [[Sith]] world of [[Exegol]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />
