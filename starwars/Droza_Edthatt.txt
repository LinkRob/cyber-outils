'''Droza Edthatt''' was a [[Duros/Legends|Duros]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] known for, along with her husband [[Etro Edthatt]], making headlines for being kidnapped by [[Unidentified Human abductors|members]] of the [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Species/Legends|species]]. Shortly after [[Marriage/Legends|marrying]] Etro and returning from their honeymoon, the couple was abducted by the Humans, who held the newlyweds hostage on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Earth/Legends|Urthha]] for more than four [[Star/Legends|solar]] periods, until the couple managed to escape the planet using a [[matter catalyst]], a common piece of transportation technology on that planet.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Once we adjusted our aural syncronizers, we understood everything our captives had to say.|Droza Edthatt, describing her first moments on Urthha|<ref name="MAGL" />}}
[[File:Blender.png|thumb|left|100px|The Edthatts escaped Urthha using a matter catalyst, known locally as a "blender"]]
[[Gender/Legends|Female]] [[Duros/Legends|Duros]] Droza Edthatt [[Marriage/Legends|married]] fellow Duros [[Etro Edthatt]] and, upon returning from their honeymoon, the newlyweds, while still mid-digestion, were battered with metal sticks by [[Unidentified Human abductors|Human]] [[Human/Legends|beings]], forced out of their [[Transport/Legends|transport]] and loaded into [[Unidentified transport|another transport]] that, according to them, resembled an "upside down" [[re-humidifier]]. The Humans then blinded the Duros and levitated them to their [[Homeworld/Legends|homeworld]] of [[Earth/Legends|Urthha]], where they would stay four over four [[Star/Legends|solar]] periods.<ref name="MAGL" />

Arriving on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]], the Duros couple adjusted their [[aural synchronizer]]s, a simultaneous translation device, since neither one of them spoke [[Galactic Basic Standard/Legends|Galactic Basic Standard]], in order to readily comprehend Human speech. They were then taken to a dinner party, where the couple were forced to sit and consume food. To the Edthatts, Human cuisine resembled food from the [[Vretha system/Legends|Vretha system]]<ref name="MAGL" />&mdash;a location within the [[Mid Rim/Legends|Mid Rim's]] [[Hollan sector/Legends|Hollan sector]]<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>&mdash;, which prompted Etro to start stamping it, angering the hostess in the process. He ate a [[Potato/Legends|potato]], but it made him feel sick and break out in [[collard lesions]], scaring away the all Humans. With the house empty, the Duros couple used the privacy to go to the freezer, seeing as it was their honeymoon.<ref name="MAGL" />

The Edthatts spent their remaining solar periods on Urrtha bouncing on antennas, reading vegetables and choking books and paintings, which, according to them, "put up little resistance," in opposition to what they were used to. They also noted a lack of suitable objects to scratch. On the day a Human youngling tried to throw "folded piles of paper" at the house, he helped the couple leave the planet by means of a [[matter catalyst]], known locally as a "blender", after the child beat them in a "professional-level" game of [[Twister (game)|Twister]].<ref name="MAGL" />

The Edthatts would later recall the experience with joy in a thorough article for the periodical ''[[Galactic Gossip]]'', as part of an interview with staff writer [[Trebor Uarrac]].<ref name="MAGL" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|We were quite frightened by the spontaneous action.|Droza Edhthatt, recalling when Etro broke out in collard lesions|<ref name="MAGL" />}}
Although the Duros were famed for being a species with a strong interest on space travel,<ref name="Galaxy Guide 4" /> Droza Edthatt and her husband, until the abduction, had never seen Humans,<ref name="MAGL" /> the most populous species in [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]],<ref name="NEGAS">''[[The New Essential Guide to Alien Species]]''</ref> and could not speak their language. She struggled with many cultural concepts and objects common to Humans such as "party," "table" or pieces of technology like matter catalysts. She additionally did not speak Galactic Standard Basic, a language that, at first, reminded her of [[Mocking Trees]]. Edthatt required aural synchronizers to communicate with some off-worlders.<ref name="MAGL" />

Initially frightened with the kidnapping and the cultural shock, Edthatt over time developed a positive view of the whole affair, enjoying her time on Urthha. Edthatt only lamented she and Etro could not find suitable objects to scratch. She, like all Duros, possessed [[Color/Legends|red]] [[Eye/Legends|eyes]] and no [[Hair/Legends|hair]].<ref name="MAGL" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Duros.png|thumb|The original image from the set of ''Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope'']]
Droza Edthatt was featured in a two-page story titled "Aliens Kidnapped by Humans!", a [[Wikipedia:Gossip magazine|gossip magazine]] parody attributed to Trebor Uarrac of the ''Galactic Gossip'', originally written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that combines creature designs and photographs from various projects of [[George Lucas]], creator of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, with original text by Carrau.<ref name="MAGL" /> In the story, two Duros newlyweds named Etro and Droza Edthatt are "[[Wikipedia:Alien abduction|abducted]] by Human beings" from the planet "Urthha". After more than four solar periods of unusual experiences, they use a "matter catalyst", which the humans call a "[[Wikipedia:Blender|blender]]," to leave the planet.<ref name="MAGL" /> A picture of the Edthatts, originally a production photo from [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']],<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> is included in the segment, although it is not made clear who is who in the image.<ref name="MAGL" /> The Duros in the original picture were [[canon]]ically identified as [[Chachi De Maal/Legends|Chachi De Maal]]<ref name="GG7">''[[Galaxy Guide 7: Mos Eisley]]''</ref> and [[Ohwun De Maal/Legends|Ohwun De Maal]].<ref name="sanbra">{{GamerCite|2|The University of Sanbra Guide to Intelligent Life: The Duros}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is non-canonical.<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
