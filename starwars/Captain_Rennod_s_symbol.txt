'''Captain Rennod's symbol''', or '''Captain Rennod's sign''' was a symbol used by the infamous [[pirate]] [[captain]] [[Tak Rennod]]. Rennod marked the entrance to his lair in [[Skull Ridge Mountain]] on the [[planet]] [[Lanupa]].<ref name="Learn About Pirates" />

Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SCDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> while searching for a way back to their home [[At Attin]] a [[Skeleton Crew|group]] of [[youngling]]s canvassed the [[Skull Ridge Mountain Hotel & Spa]] in for the symbol. [[Fern]] found the sigil and with the help of an alien named [[Cthallops]], the group entered a tunnel to Rennod's lair. After the Crew found a way into the lair proper, the kids pulled a lever shaped as a roast to reveal a [[dataport]] which also displayed Rennod's sigil.<ref name="Learn About Pirates" />

==Behind the scenes==
Rennod's sigil first appeared in "[[You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates]]," the fifth episode of the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'', which aired on [[December 24]], [[2024]].<ref name="Learn About Pirates" />
