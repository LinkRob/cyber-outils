'''Arlynn''' was an individual that accepted [[gambling|bets]] on [[fathier racing]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a message detailing the upcoming winner of a fixed [[fathier]] races was forwarded to members of the [[Pyke Syndicate]], a copy of which was stored on a [[datapad]] at the [[Pyke]]'s [[Pyke Crystal Mine|crystal mine]] on the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. In the message, the sender specified that the recipients should not bet using any official [[casino]] channels and instead [[Currency|payments]] should be sent directly to Arlynn.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
While investigating fixed races on Akiva<ref name="Outlaws" /> that year,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] found and read the datapad entry at the [[crystal]] mine. She later used that information to win [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] by betting on the fixed winner.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Arlynn was mentioned in a datapad entry in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] an published by [[Ubisoft]]. The datapad, titled "FWD: Next races", is part of the game's "Money drop" optional intel quest.<ref name="Outlaws" />
