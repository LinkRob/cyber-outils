The '''Crown of Verity''' was an [[Jedi artifact|artifact]] that served as a symbol of office for the [[Magistrates of Benetage/Canon|Magistrates]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Benetage]] before the planet was abandoned. Those who wore it could discern whether others were telling the truth, but were simultaneously unable to lie. Through unknown means, the crown ended up at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the planet [[Coruscant]].<ref name="NoP" /> Around [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Order 66 date">[[Tobar Ka-Teen]]'s trip to [[Auratera]] coincided with the execution of [[Order 66]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]].</ref> during the [[Clone Wars]], it was brought to the planet [[Auratera]] by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Tobar Ka-Teen]]. After Ka-Teen was believed to be [[Death|killed]] by the [[Clone trooper]]s that had accompanied him during [[Order 66]], the crown was considered lost.<ref name="NoP" /> However, it had actually been stolen from Ka-Teen. During the [[New Republic Era]], the artifact was in possession of the [[Nagai]] [[smuggler]] [[Ibo Truddispal]].<ref name="SW 2" />
