'''Drem''' was an individual who operated on the moon [[Nar Shaddaa]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. At some point, [[Han Solo]] was sent to negotiate with Drem to acquire some [[hyperdrive]] modules. [[Chewbacca]] was not allowed to attend the negotiations after a stunt he had pulled on Drem the last time they met.<ref name="Legends" />

==Behind the scenes==
Drem was mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Assault]]'' companion app ''[[Legends of the Alliance]]'', in the "Protector" ally encounter.<ref name="Legends" />
