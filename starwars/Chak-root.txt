'''Chak-root''' was a substance transported by [[smuggler]]s, such as [[Tregga]].<ref name="SoloOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> The smuggler [[Alfris Sotin]] contacted the [[spicerunner]] gang known as the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] claiming to possess a large supply of chak-root, but [[Skirmish at Ankot Station|betrayed]] them to the [[Guavian Death Gang]] when one of its cells sent two members to meet Sotin aboard [[Ankot Station]].<ref name="Free Fall">''[[Poe Dameron: Free Fall]]''</ref>
