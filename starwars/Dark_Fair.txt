The '''Dark Fair''' was a [[Standard week|week]] long banquet held by the [[Nihil]] on [[Valo]] to honor the [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]]. It was planned by [[Vark Tarpalin]]. When the Fair was supposed to begin, the [[Ram Jomaram|Scarlet Skull]] [[Attack on the Dark Fair|attacked]] the Fair, destroying multiple Nihil [[starship|ship]]s.<ref name="EFV" />

{{Event-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Dark Fair appeared in [[The High Republic: Escape from Valo|''Escape from Valo'']], a [[2024]] junior novel written by [[Daniel José Older]] and [[Alyssa Wong]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="EFV" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref> The event was included in the novel as a way to add a bit of humor to the book's dark themes, which primarily focused on the Nihil's Valo occupation. Both Wong and Older found the concept of several children ruining Tarpalin's grand event amusing.<ref name="Podcast">{{Spotify|url=episode/4D2UuP7yk8IXzNbvShs78k|text=ESCAPE FROM VALO with Daniel José Older & Alyssa Wong}}</ref>
