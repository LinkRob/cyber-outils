A '''Canto Club Sandwich''' was a [[sandwich]] served by the [[restaurant]] [[Canto Bites]] in [[Canto Bight]], a [[city]] on<ref name="LEGOTSS" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Canto Bight DB">{{Databank|canto-bight|Canto Bight}}</ref> [[Cantonica]]. It was [[Diet|edible]] by [[human]]s and contained at least six layers of [[Color|green]], red, yellow, and brown fillings.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />

{{GameMechanics|LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga}}
At some point by [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 ABY date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of {{Film|VII}} in [[34 ABY]]. {{Film|VIII}} takes place immediately after the end of ''The Force Awakens''. As the player only gets access to [[Canto Bight]], the location of [[Canto Bites]], during the story mode of ''The Last Jedi'', the mission must take place during or after 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> a [[Polite Patron|human customer]] ordered a Canto Club Sandwich at Canto Bites. Due to the infamously poor service, the human asked an individual who was not employed by the restaurant to bring them their sandwich. The individual used a [[protocol droid]] to interface with the [[Protocol Droid Terminal]] next to the service window, which allowed control of the protocol droid [[chef]]s in the kitchen. The protocol droid at the terminal controlled one of the chef protocol droids to place the sandwich on the conveyor belt between the kitchen and the service window, at which point the individual delivered the Canto Club Sandwich to the customer, who thanked them for their help.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Canto Club Sandwich appeared in the non-[[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', which was released in [[2022]]. It featured in the side quest "Canto Bites."<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
