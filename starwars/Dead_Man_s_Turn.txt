'''Dead Man's Turn''' was a landmark within the [[Beggar's Canyon]], located on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The turn held notoriety for the particular danger it posed to people who flew through the canyon, giving it the befitting name.<ref name="SWCL" />
