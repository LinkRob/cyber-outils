The '''''Flamsteed''''' was a large utilitarian [[freighter]] in operation around the time of the [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|formation]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]<ref name="AlHornerPodcast" /> in [[2 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Manned by a small, peculiar crew, the [[starship]] flew the same uneventful route for several dozens of [[Standard year|years]], and on occasion, its crew accepted high-paying jobs to [[transport]] passengers to [[Yavin 4]],<ref name="AlHornerPodcast" /> an [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] that served as an important base for anti-[[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] insurgents of the incipient rebellion.<ref name="DB">{{Databank|yavin-4|Yavin 4}}</ref> While the crew was comfortable with accepting these risky jobs, they needed to bribe key individuals along the way.<ref name="AlHornerPodcast" />

At one point, while [[Axis network]] operative [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] and other passengers rode on the ''Flamsteed'' to return to Yavin, the starship was detained by the Galactic Empire, as the bribery suddenly became ineffective. [[KX-series security droid|KX enforcer droid]] [[K-2SO]] then boarded the freighter and sometime later realized he was trapped, turning the event into a lethal pursuit for Andor.<ref name="AlHornerPodcast" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The name of the ship in ''Alien'' is the ''Nostromo''. Our ship was the ''Flamsteed''. What I loved about ''Alien'' was always the blue-collar quality of it. So I made it a very blue-collar freighter.|Dan Gilroy|<ref name="AlHornerPodcast" />}}
[[File:USCSSNostromo.png|thumb|250px|The UCSS ''Nostromo'' from the film ''Alien'' (1979) inspired the ''Flamsteed''.]]
According to writer [[Dan Gilroy]], the ''Flamsteed'' was slated to appear in the scrapped "K-2SO horror episode" planned<ref name="AlHornerPodcast" /> for the ninth episode&mdash;eventually titled "[[Welcome to the Rebellion]]"<ref name="EW">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/andor-creator-reveals-scrapped-k2so-horror-movie-episode-11728167|text=''Andor'' creator reveals scrapped K-2SO 'horror movie' episode|author=Dalton Ross|date=2025-05-08|work=Entertainment Weekly|quote=They had to bring this huge ugly tanker ship to Yavin, and there was a KX unit that was trapped inside there hunting. It was sort of like a monster movie with K2 on it. It was really cool.|archivedate=20250516013944}}</ref>&mdash;of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the [[Disney+]] live-action series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', which aired on [[May 6]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref> The episode was conceived as a self-contained story inspired by the<ref name="AlHornerPodcast" /> [[1979]]<ref name="BOM">{{WebCite|url=https://www.boxofficemojo.com/title/tt0078748|text=Alien (1979)|work=Box Office Mojo|archivedate=20191025173708}}</ref> film ''[[Wikipedia:Alien (film)|Alien]]'', with the ''Flamsteed'' modeled after the ''[[w:c:alienanthology:USCSS Nostromo|Nostromo]]'', a starship featured in the film, but budget constraints led to the the script being discarded during development.<ref name="AlHornerPodcast" />

While ''Andor'' series creator [[Tony Gilroy]] initially described the ''Flamsteed'' as a "huge ugly [[tanker]] ship,"<ref name="EW" /> script writer Dan Gilroy later characterized it as a "blue-collar," "sleepy" freighter.<ref name="AlHornerPodcast" /> This article assumes Dan Gilroy's description is the most accurate, given his drafting of the script that includes the ''Flamsteed''.
