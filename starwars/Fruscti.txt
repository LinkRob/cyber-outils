'''Fruscti''' was a [[language]] considered dead<ref name="Wayseeker" /> by around [[160 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{WayseekerDate}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]]. The phrase ''sigeo nocht rewnat qas bindt lokicgh fro'' in Fruscti translated to "the son of the brightest king casts the darkest light" in [[Galactic Basic Standard]].<ref name="Wayseeker" />
