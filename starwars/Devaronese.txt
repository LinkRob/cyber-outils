'''Devaronese''' was the [[language]] of the [[Devaronian]]s, developing on the [[planet]] [[Devaron]]. The Devaronians were also capable of speaking [[Galactic Basic Standard|Basic]]. Few Devaronians bothered to learn the language by the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]], preferring to speak Basic in all situations.<ref name="NoP" />
