The '''Brodo Asogi system''' was located in the [[Perinn sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] in [[the galaxy]]. The [[Star system|system]] was situated in grid square J-4 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Asogian]] [[species]]<ref name="JC">{{InsiderCite|161|Jedi Challenge!}}</ref> partly shared its name with both the Brodo Asogi system and the [[Asog system]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Brodo Asogi system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published on the [[StarWars.com]] website on [[June 24]], [[2025]],<ref name="Appendix" /> as part of the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]].<ref name="Galaxy">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> The [[Brodo Asogi system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]''.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
