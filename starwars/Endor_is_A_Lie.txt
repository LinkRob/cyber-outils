'''''Endor is A Lie''''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] created by [[Governor]] [[Ecressys]]<ref name="SWTR&FotGE" /> shortly after the [[Battle of Endor]]. It depicted [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] sitting ominously on his [[throne]] aboard the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] and glaring towards the observer with glowing [[eye]]s, with the [[High Galactic]] font spelling out in [[Color|red]] on top in all capitals "[[Endor|ENDOR]] IS A LIE," and in white at the bottom in all capitals "BELIEVE IT AT YOUR PERIL," with a smaller font below in red that stated "Long live Emperor Palpatine, long live the Galactic Empire" with the [[Imperial crest]] between the two statements.<ref name="Propaganda" /><ref name="SWTR&FotGE" />
