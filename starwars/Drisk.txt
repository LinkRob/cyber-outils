'''Drisk''' was a [[Pau'an]] [[aide]] who served alongside [[Tolteck]] on [[Starlag XIX]].<ref name="Final Round" />

== Biography ==
At some point Drisk joined forces with [[Tolteck]], an individual who owned the prison [[Starlag XIX]].<ref name="Final Round" /> In [[2 BBY]],<ref>"Final Round" features the ''[[Phantom II]]'', which first appeared in "[[The Last Battle]]," and it also mentions [[Jun Sato]], who died in "[[Zero Hour]]." ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' places "The Last Battle" seventeen [[Standard year|years]] after the end of the [[Clone Wars]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]], meaning that "The Last Battle" takes place in [[2 BBY]]. ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'' places the events of "Zero Hour" 17 [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], which occurred in [[19 BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'', meaning that "Zero Hour" takes place in 2 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], and "Final Round" takes place in the same year.</ref> Tolteck had the captured [[bounty hunter]] [[Ketsu Onyo]] brought to the facility. Tolteck then contacted Onyo's [[Spectres|friends]] in the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|rebellion]] and offered to trade Onyo for [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. [[Hera Syndulla]], [[Garazeb Orrelios]], and [[Rex]] traveled to Starlag XIX to make the trade, but Tolteck changed the deal upon their arrival, demanding that Orrelios fight the prison champion, [[Aaston Lok]], for her freedom. The rebels agreed and after Lok saw that Orrelios was a [[Lasat]], he refused to fight the rebel. Drisk offered the opinion that Toltreck should have taken the rebels' credits earlier, but Toltreck instead just activated Lok's [[shock collar]] and electrocuted the prisoner, demanding he fight Orrelios.<ref name="Final Round" />

Lok then began to fight Orrelios, but Syndulla and Rex had enough and sought to leave with their team. Toltreck refused to let them, telling the pair that he had sold them to Drisk for use in his labor colony. However, at that point, the rebels had a second team outside, [[Tonk's crew]], who had attached an explosive to the facility. On Rex's command, [[Tonk]] set off the explosive, causing all of the prisoner's shock collars to be disabled. This caused general mayhem as the prisoners all sought to escape the facility simultaneously. Syndulla took the distraction to release Onyo's bonds while Rex punched out Drisk. The rebels then escaped with the help of Lok.<ref name="Final Round" /> 

== Traits and personality ==
Drisk was a tall, [[Gender|male]] [[Pau'an]] with gray skin and black eyes. He wore a black and purple robe. He had no qualms concerning [[slavery]], attempting to acquire Syndulla, Rex, Orrelios, and Onyo without their consent to work in his labor colony.<ref name="Final Round" />

== Behind the scenes ==
