Eleven [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="VPdate" /> the [[New Republic]] and the [[New Separatist Union]] engaged one another in several '''border skirmishes'''. Republic [[General]] [[Si-Flachitt]] provided a report on the skirmishes at a meeting with [[New Republic High Command]] [[officer]]s.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
The border skirmishes with the New Separatist Union were mentioned in ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', a [[2021]] novel written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
