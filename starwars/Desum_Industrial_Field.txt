The '''Desum Industrial Field''' was located on the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] [[Sposia]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> By [[27 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' establishes that the [[Vagaari pirate operations]] took place around [[27 BBY]]. As [[Mitth'ras'safis]] [[Death|died]] during the operations and [[V-484]] was on its way to the Desum Industrial Field when Mitth'ras'safis was alive, the [[starship]] must have been on its way to the industrial field by [[27 BBY]].</ref> the [[Chiss]] [[Stybla family]]'s [[Vivan cargo ship]] [[V-484]] was on its way to the industrial field when a [[pirate]] [[starship]] of the Ascendancy's [[Clarr family]] disguised as an [[Obbic family]] [[transport]] [[Thief|hijacked]] it and brought it to the Chiss [[blackdock]] [[space station]] [[Glastis Three]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
