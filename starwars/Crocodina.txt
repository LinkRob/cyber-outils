'''Crocodinas''' were a type of [[crocodile]]-like creature with scaley heads that included a pair of [[eye]]s and a long, thin [[Nose|snout]] with a [[tooth]]y [[Mouth|maw]].<ref name="RegalRobot" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]]<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] had the head of a crocodina displayed on the wall near its bar.<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The head of a crocodina first appeared in the [[1977]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="ANH" /> The prop was part of the set filmed on Stage 6 at [[Elstree Studios]] on [[April 13]]&ndash;[[April 21|21]], [[1976]],<ref name="MakingOf">''[[The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film]]''</ref> and was in actuality the taxidermied<ref name="Archeology">{{YouTube|video=J7ygjomkfXQ&t|text=Star Wars: A New Hope - Cantina Archeology Part 1: Behind The Scenes of the Mos Eisley Cantina - UK|channel=@CredibleNerds|name=The Credible Nerds}}</ref> head of a [[In-universe|real-life]] [[Wikipedia:Gharial|gharial]].<ref name="SWCC">{{SW|url=news/swcc-2019-6-surprises-from-inside-the-mos-eisley-cantina|text=SWCC 2019: 6 Surprises from Inside the Mos Eisley Cantina}}</ref>

The gharial head prop was first brought to the attention of fans by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[Tom Spina]] at the "Secrets of the Mos Eisley Cantina" panel at [[Celebration Anaheim (2015)|Celebration Anaheim]] in [[2015]].<ref name="SWCA">{{SW|url=news/swca-secrets-of-the-mos-eisley-cantina-panel-liveblog|text=SWCA: Secrets of the Mos Eisley Cantina Panel Liveblog|nolive=1}}</ref> The creature was then identified for the first time when<ref name="RegalRobot" /> Spina's company,<ref name="Spina">{{SW|url=news/tom-spina-regal-robot-star-wars-interview|text=Tom Spina on Building a Desk That Jabba Would Love and a Throne Fit for an Emperor}}</ref> [[Regal Robot]], produced a magnet version of the prop in [[2024]].<ref name="RegalRobot" />
