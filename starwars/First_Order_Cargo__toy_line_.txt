'''First Order Cargo''' is the toy line and merchandise line sold mostly at the [[First Order Cargo]] retail location at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]]. The store was opened along with the rest of the theme park area in [[2019]]. The packaging for the products feature black and red colors and the [[First Order insignia]].<ref name="WDWNT" />

Some of the items have been sold at other Galaxy's Edge locations, like the [[First Order Short-Range Evacuation Vehicle]] sold at [[Resistance Supply]], which is close to the vehicle's origin place, the ''[[Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance]]'' attraction.
