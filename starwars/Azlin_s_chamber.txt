'''Azlin's chambers''' was a room located<ref name="Into the Light" /> in the [[Jedi Temple Detention Center|detention level]]<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Into the Light" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] that was lived in by the former [[Jedi Knight]] [[Azlin Rell]]. Rell, who had been living in a [[Azlin's quarters|cell]]<ref name="Into the Light" /> in the Detention Center<ref name="Edge of Balance V3">[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 3|''The High Republic: The Edge of Balance'', Vol. 3]]</ref> beforehand, was given the room after he had helped the [[Jedi]] during a [[Mission to Banchii|battle]] against the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures on<ref name="Into the Light" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Banchii]], which Jedi Knight [[Reath Silas]] considered to have been earned. Silas visited the chamber to speak with Rell after returning from an [[Evacuation of Inad Komesh|evacuation]] of the [[moon]] [[Inad Komesh]].<ref name="Into the Light" />
