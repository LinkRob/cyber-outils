The '''AA Targeting Array''' was an anti-air targeting array building located in [[Kestro City|Kestro]] on the [[planet]] [[Vardos]] that was connected to the [[turbolaser]] emplacements throughout the [[city]]. During [[Operation: Cinder]], the Array was [[Skirmish on Vardos|destroyed]] by former [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] [[Commander]] [[Iden Versio]] and [[Agent]] [[Del Meeko]], which neutralized anti-air turbolaser emplacements in the area, allowing Versio, Meeko, and civilians to escape the planet.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Behind the scenes==
The AA Targeting Array first appeared in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''{{'s}} campaign, a video game released in [[2017]] and produced by [[DICE|EA DICE]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />
