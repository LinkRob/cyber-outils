'''Draper''' was a [[human]] who served as a [[lieutenant]] in the [[First Order military|armed forces]] of the [[First Order]] and was also a member of the [[First Order Supreme Council]],<ref name="TROS" /> a cabinet of high-ranking [[First Order officer]]s,<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> during the last days of the [[First Order-Resistance War]]. In [[35 ABY]], they attended a meeting of the council on board the ''[[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|Steadfast]]'' in which [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] presented the head of the [[Resistance]] sympathizer [[Boolio]] and declared that there was a [[spy]] within the First Order. Draper and the other council members then watched as Ren [[Death|executed]] [[Domaric Quinn]] when he questioned Ren on the First Order's allegiance with the [[Sith Eternal]] cult and its [[Final Order]] [[fleet]] on [[Exegol]].<ref name="TROS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Lieutenant Draper was played by [[Alison Rose]] in [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. Her role is confirmed in the film's credits.<ref name="TROS" />
