'''Convenient Daily Departures: The History of ''Star Tours''''' is a [[2013]] reference article written by [[James McFadden]] for the official [[Star Wars Blog]]. The article deals with both the real-life attractions [[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] and ''[[Star Tours: The Adventures Continue]]'', and the [[in-universe]] history of the fictional [[Star Tours/Legends|Star Tours]] company.<ref name="Article" />
