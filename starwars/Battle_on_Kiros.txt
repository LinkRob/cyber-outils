A '''battle''' took place on the [[planet]] [[Kiros]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the joint [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederate]]-[[Zygerrian Slave Empire|Zygerrian]] occupation and a [[Galactic Republic]] intervention force.

==Prelude==
{{Main|Conquest of Kiros}}
{{Quote|Count Dooku, we have chosen to remain neutral in this war. Why you have launched this invasion against us?''"<br />"''Think of our presence as a peaceful occupation for your protection as well as ours.|Governor Roshti speaking with Count Dooku|<ref name="Kidnapped" />}}
In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Togruta]]n [[Artisan colony]] of [[Kiros]] chose to stay neutral during the galaxy-wide [[Clone Wars]]. However, the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Zygerrian Slave Empire]] decided to [[Conquest of Kiros|invade and occupy Kiros]]. Upon seeing the initial invasion, Governor [[Gupat Roshti]] contacted the [[Jedi]] [[Jedi High Council|Council's]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] for aid. Yoda promised to send help and warned him not to trust [[Count]] [[Dooku]], who was present there with his forces.<ref name="Kidnapped" />

[[File:InvasionOfKiros-Kidnapped.png|left|thumb|220x220px|B1 battle droids capture the colonists]]
After Roshti and Yoda ended their communication, the [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]] troops came from their [[C-9979 landing craft|landing crafts]]. Dooku, a [[Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Kiros)|tactical droid]], and [[Zygerrian]] Commander [[Darts D'Nar]] then came out from the [[YV-865 Aurore-class freighter|YV-865 ''Aurore''-class freighter]], ''[[Tecora]]''. Together, they met Governor Roshti. Dooku assured him that this was a peaceful occupation, not an invasion. He then offered Roshti and his people sanctuary away from the War. Roshti, seeing no other choice, agreed to Dooku's offer. In truth, Dooku had made an alliance between the Confederacy and the Zygerrians' Slave Empire to provide them with slaves. Thus, D'Nar then had the Droid Army round up all of Kiros' people. D'Nar then all the Togrutas shipped to [[Zygerria]] where they would sold into slavery at Queen [[Miraj Scintel]]'s [[Royal Slave Auction|slave auction]]. D'Nar was then left in command of Droid Army's occupation force and had his headquarters in the Governor's [[Governor's tower|tower]].<ref name="Kidnapped" />

==The battle==
{{Quote|You are no doubt aware your droid forces have been destroyed, and you are surrounded. Do the honorable thing and give up now, D'Nar. I promise you will receive fair treatment.''"<br />"''You've broken through my defenses. You think you've won? Think again! I have planted bombs all over the city. No one will survive. Now, surrender.|[[Jedi]] [[Jedi General|General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] speaking with Commander [[Darts D'Nar]]|<ref name="Kidnapped" />}}
Ten planetary rotations later, the Republic forces arrived led by Jedi Generals [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], [[Clone Commander]] "[[Cody]]," and Captain "[[Rex]]." The republic forces then landed on surface. As the [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|Republic landing ship]] made its landing, the Republic forces deployed [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer|AT-TEs]], [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport|AT-RTs]], and their troops. The Jedi and their troops then started their ground assault. Kenobi, Skywalker, Tano and Rex then took several of their troops on [[BARC speeder]]s ahead towards the Governor's tower. Together they were able to take out several [[BX-series droid commando|commando droids]] on [[Flitknot Speeder Bike]]s as well the [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|AATs]] and the rest of the battle droids. Captain Rex was able to destroy the last one with his missile launcher.<ref name="Kidnapped" />

[[File:BARCrancorsidecar-Kidnapped.png|left|thumb|235x235px|Republic forces enter the city on speeders]]
However, en route to the tower, Rex and Tano saw that there were no people anywhere.<ref name="Kidnapped" /> They found it suspicious since the Droid Army was not shy about using hostages such as they did during the [[Ryloth]] [[Battle of Ryloth|Campaign]].<ref name="IoR">{{TCW|Innocents of Ryloth}}</ref> As their forces surrounded the tower, Commander Cody then received a message from D'Nar, who wished to discuss terms of surrender with Kenobi. However, Skywalker did not like the fact the D'Nar was Zygerrian slave trader and wanted to deal with him himself. However, Kenobi insisted that he should do as D'Nar wished. Kenobi then had Skywalker contact Admiral Yularen and start a bio-scan to find the colonists. Kenobi then went into the tower and left his com-link on for Skywalker to listen on his discussion with D'Nar.

As Kenobi entered tower's office, he interrupted D'Nar's conference with Dooku and Prime Minister [[Atai Molec]], who were surprised that he had not left yet. After Dooku introduced Kenobi to Molec and D'Nar, he demanded to bring Kenobi to him on his knees. D'Nar then demanded that Kenobi surrender to him. He told Kenobi that he had planted [[thermal annihilator bomb]]s through out the city. He would detonate them and kill anyone nearby. D'Nar then had his tactical droid set one off near the Republic forces.<ref name="Kidnapped" />

Skywalker then canceled the bio-scan and had R2-D2 scan for bombs. Kenobi then distracted D'Nar by pretending to surrender. D'Nar considered killing as revenge for the Jedi's termination of their slave empire, but knew Dooku would rather have him alive. Kenobi then offered D'Nar a chance to fight him in the Zygerrian way without his lightsaber. Thus, the two of them fought each other without their weapons.<ref name="Kidnapped" />

[[File:AnakinAhsokaUnderFire-Kidnapped.jpg|thumb|213x213px|Skywalker and Tano locate the bombs]]
Skywalker and Tano then took a pair of AT-RTs to disarm the bombs with their lightsabers. The battle droids attempted to inform D'Nar of this to no avail due to Kenobi destroying his projector. As Skywalker and Tano were about to disarm the last two bombs in a courtyard, they then came under attack by several [[B2-series super battle droid|super battle droids]]. They were then pinned down by a pair of [[Droideka Sharpshooter]]s.<ref name="Kidnapped" />

Skywalker and Tano were unable break through their shields. However, they saw that the droids were vulnerable on their sides. Together they were able to destroy the droids and disarm the last two bombs. As D'Nar was winning his fight against Kenobi, the tactical droid showed him a message from his battle droids, telling that all the bombs had been disarmed. D'Nar then took the detonator and tried to set the bombs off to no avail. Kenobi then destroyed the commando droids and retrieved his lightsaber. Kenobi then offered him the chance to surrender again. D'Nar refuse and decided to set off the last one that was on the back of the tactical droid. As the bomb was going off, D'Nar made his escape. Kenobi tossed the tactical droid out the tower and the droid was destroyed in the bomb's explosion.<ref name="Kidnapped" />

Kenobi then told Skywalker and Tano that D'Nar was escaping to his ship, the ''Tecora''. As D'Nar started to take off, Skywalker and Tano were able to use the AT-RTs to reach the ship. Skywalker was then able to damage one of the ''Tecora''{{'s}} engines. D'Nar then spotted the two jedi and decided to unleash his pet, a [[Blixus]] upon them. Skywalker then fought the creature while Ahsoka stabilized the ship. Tano then went the cockpit where she found D'Nar. The two them fought until Ahsoka was subdue him. Skywalker was then able to kill the Blixus. Skywalker then went to cockpit and demanded the location of the togrutas. At first, D'Nar refuse until Skywalker held his lightsaber at his neck. D'Nar then revealed that his queen was going to have slave auction on Zygerria.<ref name="Kidnapped" />

==Aftermath==
{{Dialogue|Yoda|Troubling is the return of the Zygerrians.|Koon|It is likely they mean to use Dooku to rebuild their Slaver Empire.|Skywalker|We cannot allow that, Master.|Yoda|Agree I do, but first, find the colonists you must. In great danger they are.|Koon|What is it, Master?|Yoda|Sense I do, a darker hand in this mystery. Slavery, a great tool it is&hellip;for the rise of the Sith.|attr=Yoda, Plo Koon, and Anakin Skywalker, discussing their next course of action after the battle|src=<ref name="Kidnapped" />}}
Following D'Nar's capture and the destruction of Droid Army occupation force, Kiros was liberated. However, all the colonists were still nowhere to be found. Admiral Yularen that they were no where on the planet. Skywalker, Kenobi, Tano, and Yularen informed the Jedi Council of this. Master [[Plo Koon]] believed that the Zygerrians were using the Confederacy to rebuild their Slave Empire.<ref name="Kidnapped" /> Thus, Kenobi, Skywalker, Tano and Rex undertook a [[Mission to Zygerria|mission]] to [[Zygerria]] to find the colonists.<ref name="Slaves of the Republic" /> The Republic then returned the colonist to Kiros after finding them on [[Kadavo]].<ref name="EfK">{{TCW|Escape from Kadavo}}</ref>
