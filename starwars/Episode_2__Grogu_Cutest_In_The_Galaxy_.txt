The '''second episode''' of the animated web series ''[[Grogu Cutest In The Galaxy]]'' released on [[December 10]], [[2024]]. It was posted by ''[[Star Wars]]'' Japan on [[Wikipedia:Instagram Reels|Instagram Reels]].<ref name="Instagram" />

{{Media-stub}}

==Plot summary==
[[Din Grogu|Grogu]]'s pram opens up, revealing the child. Later, in space, while [[Din Djarin]] is piloting his [[Din Djarin's N-1 starfighter|N-1 starfighter]], Grogu pops up from the transparent observation dome in the ship. In the subsequent scene, Grogu is seen eating a struggling [[Arges frog]] behind a rock. Finally, Grogu is seen waving.
