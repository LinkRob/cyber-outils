The '''Battle at Gravity's Heart''',<ref name="Timelines" /> also known as a '''mission in the Berenge Sector''',<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> occurred at the [[space station]] [[Gravity's Heart]], located in the barren [[Berenge sector]], during the [[Nihil conflict]] of the [[High Republic Era]]. The station contained a [[gravity well projector]] developed by the renegade [[scientist]] [[Chancey Yarrow]], and the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]] hoped to destroy it and rescue three abductees, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]s [[Reath Silas]] and [[Imri Cantaros]], and Chancey's daughter [[Sylvestri Yarrow|Sylvestri]], who had been unaware of her mother's plotting with the [[Nihil]]. During the battle, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] encountered the dying [[hyperspace]] [[prospector]] [[Mari San Tekka]], who gave her one final route to a location on the edge of [[the galaxy]] before dying.<ref name="OOTS" />
