'''2015 Topps ''Star Wars'' Chrome Perspectives''' is a trading card set released by [[Topps]] on [[August 21]], [[2015]]. The set's cards are presented from the [[in-universe]] perspectives of both the [[Jedi]] and the [[Sith]] via the [[Jedi Archives|Jedi Temple Archives]] and [[Sith Information Guide]], respectively.
