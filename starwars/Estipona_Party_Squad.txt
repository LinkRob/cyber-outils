The '''Estipona Party Squad''' was a group of cannibals active at the time of the [[Cold War]]. After landing on the ghost [[moon]] [[Kufs]], [[J-Squadron]] members [[Dec Hansen]] and [[Sari Nadle]] wondered if the approaching [[droid]]s were from the group.<ref name="Join the Resistance 2">''[[Join the Resistance: Escape from Vodran]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Estipona Party Squad was first mentioned in the [[2017]] young adult novel ''[[Join the Resistance: Escape from Vodran]]'', written by [[Ben Acker]] and [[Ben Blacker]].<ref name="Join the Resistance 2" />
