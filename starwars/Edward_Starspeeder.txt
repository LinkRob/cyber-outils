'''Edward, or Mervin, Starspeeder''' (depending on the volume), also known as '''Krio Vin''', was a [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] and the father of [[Victor Starspeeder|Victor]] and [[Christina Starspeeder]].<ref name="forceoversleeps" />'''<ref name="Jedi Academy: At Last, Jedi">''[[Jedi Academy: At Last, Jedi]]''</ref>'''

==Personality and traits==
Krio Vin, at his core, seemed to be little (if anything) more than a power-hungry, egomaniacal, narcissistic and bitter lord of the Sith, and his goals were solely to acquire absolute power and share it with his family. Truly arrogant, he believed himself a broader thinker than [[Victor Starspeeder's mother|his ex-wife]], who he claimed never understood what the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side of the Force]] had to offer, and to be far stronger than Victor and his "little pals." He did not, however, appear to be of a particularly sophisticated mind, as he was easily deceived by his own daughter, and his means of turning people to the dark side seemed to be making abrasive demands and reaching for [[Krio Vin's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] if they said no.

==Powers and abilities==
[[File:KrioVinSaberOversleeps.png|thumb|Krio Vin's lightsaber]]
Krio Vin was a competent [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] duelist, capable of easily parrying three Jedi initiates' attacks simultaneously ([[Jedi Academy: A New Class|Coleman Flytrap]], [[Maya Phoenix]], and [[Artemis Oophanoe]]) and intercepting a powerful blow from [[Zefyr|Mr. Zefyr]]. He was adept at telekinesis, using it to restrain and levitate his son. He was also a skilled pilot.

==Behind the scenes==
Krio Vin is the overarching antagonist of the [[Victor Starspeeder]] trilogy (the main one being [[Zachary O'Halleran]]/ZC-04).
