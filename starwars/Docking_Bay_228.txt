'''Docking Bay 228''', abbreviated '''DB-228''', was a [[Hangar|docking bay]] located on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. Shortly before its destruction at the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[MSE-6-G735Y]] carried a [[scanner servo]] to Docking Bay 228, where it was used by [[TK-450]].<ref name="Of MSE-6 and Men" />
