'''Battle Royale''' was one of the many types of games hosted by the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. In this game, the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunters]] would start on a platform around the edge of [[Mini Battle Maps|one of three small circular floating platforms]] themed after a real location such as [[Tatooine]], before engaging in combat for a set period of time on this mini map. When the timer elapsed, Hunters would make their way to a small platform on the sides of the arena to jump onto a [[Bantha-II cargo skiff|skiff]] which would transport them to another of the platforms, where the countdown would reset and combat would resume.<ref name="ArtStation" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:TatooineMiniBattleMap.png|thumb|right|230px|Battle Royale would have take place in the Mini Battle Maps]]
This game mode was designed, in part, by concept artist [[Clint Young]] for the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', though it did not make it into the final version of the game, alongside the battlefields designed for it.<ref name="ArtStation" />
