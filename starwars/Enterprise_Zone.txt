The '''Enterprise Zone''' was a location in the [[Lowlands]] region of the [[planet]] [[Aldhani]]. Displaced [[Dhani]]s from the [[Highlands (Aldhani)|Highlands]] were forced to live in the Enterprise Zone by the [[Galactic Empire]] as factories, new towns, and Imperial housing was built there. Only a small population of Dhanis continued to occupy the area after the Enterprise Zone was constructed, mainly [[ghoat-herder]]s.<ref name="Aldhani" />

The rebel [[spy]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Gorn]] wanted to refocus the [[Aldhani Garrison]]'s attention to the Lowlands as a distraction.<ref name="Aldhani" />
