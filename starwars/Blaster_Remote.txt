The '''Blaster Remote''' was a piece of weaponized equipment developed by the rookie [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[medic]], [[programmer]], and tinkerer, [[Sprocket]], for use in the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition hosted on [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" /> Developed from modified [[Training droid|training]] [[remote]]s, they were not to be confused with actual training [[droid]]s, as, like the [[Defense Drone (Sprocket)|Defense Drones]] and [[Ultra Turret]]s also designed by Sprocket, they were controlled manually from his [[datapad]] and custom [[Repulsorlift chair|repulsorlift]] [[Command Chair]].<ref name="Sprocket">{{Hunters|url=characters/sprocket|text=Sprocket}}</ref> When participating in matches within the [[Grand Arena]], Sprocket would always bring two Blaster Remotes to serve as his primary [[weapon]] of choice. They were also highly customizable.<ref name="Hunters" />
