'''Feez''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Fallanassi]] who was the [[Gretta's family|aunt]] of the Fallanassi [[Gretta]]. During their life, Feez had four children, learned to play the [[valachord]], and studied medicine.<ref name="Star Wars 42" /> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], they, along with Gretta, helped the [[Jedi]]-in-[[Jedi training|training]] [[Luke Skywalker]] as he [[Purification|healed]] a [[Color|red]] [[kyber crystal]].<ref name="Star Wars 43" /> Feez taught the Jedi a number of things about the purification process, including the risks and set up a ritual with [[candle]]s and [[incense]] to help facilitate the [[meditation]].<ref name="Star Wars 42" />
