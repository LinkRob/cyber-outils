'''Columus''' was an astronomical object located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Core Worlds]] which was the homeworld of the [[Columi]].<ref name="TFABG" /> The [[Pirate Queen|pirate queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] hid a portion of the spoils from her reign there in a buried vault equipped with an [[antitamper sub-space alarm]]. As of when she marked Columus on a [[map]] in the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' [[Book|logbook]]<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> at some point prior to or during [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The entry in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'' mentioning Seeratter is placed chronologically prior to an entry written by the criminal [[Tobias Beckett]]. ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' depicts Beckett's [[death]] on the [[planet]] [[Savareen]], and ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on Savareen in the film to nine [[Standard year|years]] after the [[proclamation of the New Order]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the proclamation to [[19 BBY]], Beckett's death must occur in [[10 BBY]], and so his entry in the guide and those chronologically before it must all be set prior to or during 10 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the alarm protecting the cache of treasure on Columus had not sounded.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
