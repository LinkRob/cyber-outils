{{Quote|They're only bounty hunters. They have no allegiance.|Maul|<ref name="Eminence">{{TCW|Eminence (episode)}}</ref>}}
'''Bounty hunters''' were individuals who could be hired to capture or kill a designated target. The target would have a price or [[bounty]] on their heads that would be collected by the bounty hunter upon successful capture or proof of [[death]]. Bounty hunters would sometimes team together as part of a larger mission or to handle dangerous targets.

==Description==
{{Quote|Respected and feared by all those who encounter them, a bounty hunter is part pirate, part mercenary, with just a hint of assassin thrown in for good measure when the job requires. Whereas a pirate usually deals in goods, a bounty hunter deals in people. But not just any people. No, no, no. As their name suggests, bounty hunters are interested only in those who have a high price—or bounty—placed on their heads.|Hondo Ohnaka|<ref name="TSotBH">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Bounty_Hunter_SWU.jpg|thumb|left|300px|A bounty hunter in the [[Anoat sector]]]]
A bounty hunter was an individual who could be hired to capture, hunt, or eliminate a designated target if such target had a price or [[bounty]] on their heads that could be collected by the bounty hunter, either capturing or [[Death|killing]] the target, depending on their clients' wishes.<ref name="Episode V" /> For the right price, most bounty hunters had no morals and did everything for the money. Aside from the highest bidder, bounty hunters were only loyal to themselves.<ref name="SW:TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Bounties could be put out on the [[Hunter Net]]<ref name="Dark Lord 11">[[Darth Vader (2017) 11|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 11]]</ref> or given in-person to a hunter for direct commission with a [[chit]] card.<ref name="Chapter 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref>

"Class one bounty hunters" were known for their exceptional skills, reputation, and ability to take on particularly dangerous or high-profile targets.<ref name="Bad Territory">{{TBB|Bad Territory}}</ref> Bounty hunters also often associated with the underworld.<ref name="Chapter 1" /> The information for killing a target was often confidential.<ref name="Reunion">{{TBB|Reunion}}</ref><ref name="Jar Jar 1">[[Jar Jar 1|''Jar Jar'' 1]]</ref>

Many bounty hunters acted like [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] and were hired to do jobs other than tracking down bounties.<ref name="TCW Film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref><ref name="HfZ">{{TCW|Hunt for Ziro}}</ref><ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> Indeed, [[Greef Karga]] of the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]] outright stated all hunters were effectively mercenaries, explaining that hunters only stayed around a place if profit or work were to be had.<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> Although hunters had no true loyalty to them, only staying around for payment<ref name="Eminence">{{TCW|Eminence (episode)}}</ref> or if a job offering emerged,<ref name="Evil Plans">{{TCW|Evil Plans}}</ref> [[Hutt]]s often kept hunters on retainer so as to have more experienced and capable [[bodyguard]]s protecting them; [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]] was one such Hutt who kept several renowned hunters with him or within Hutt palaces throughout the [[Clone Wars]] and the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="TCW Film" /><ref name="HfZ" /><ref name="Episode VI" /> Several bounty hunters also worked as [[assassin]]s, being hired by employers who would pay a substantial reward for the death of a certain target or targets.<ref name="AotC">{{Film|II}}</ref>

Independent-minded [[droid]]s could also act as bounty hunters, to the dismay of the organic bounty hunting professional known as "[[Din Djarin|the Mandalorian]]," who simply referred to one, the [[assassin droid]] [[IG-11]], a "[[List of phrases and slang#B|bounty droid]]."<ref name="Chapter 1" /> In cases such as the [[Haxion Brood Bounty Droid]], droids could be reprogrammed to assist hunters in their work.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==History==
{{Quote|Bounty hunters! We don't need their scum.|Imperial Admiral [[Firmus Piett]]|audio=WeDontNeedTheirScum.flac|<ref name="Episode V" />}}
The [[Tempest Breakers]] bounty guild was established by former [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]] and former Nihil [[pirate]] [[Quin Carree]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Tempest Breaker">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]''</ref> Bounty hunter [[Shenda Mol]] was a figure active in the late [[Republic Era]], during which time she began a spree of mass-murders in her pursuit of bounties; rather than just take out her intended target, she began to indiscriminately wipe out beings in her path as part of a competition with herself to slay one being of every age from zero [[Standard year|years]] of age to two hundred.<ref name="Master">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> Around [[66 BBY]],<ref name="66 BBY Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]]. The novel also establishes that [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] was forty [[Standard year|years]] old during its main events and fourteen years old in the [[Mission to Numidian Prime]], which dates the endeavor to around [[66 BBY]].</ref> the Jedi Order dispatched a [[Numidian Prime strike team|strike team]] to [[Mission to Numidian Prime|capture]] Mol, who was sent to the planet [[Stygeon Prime]] to answer for her crimes.<ref name="Master" /> [[Jango Fett]] was hired by [[Dooku|Tyranus]] to be the [[DNA|genetic]] [[Clone template|template]] for the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones">{{Film|II}}</ref> During the Clone Wars, [[El-Les]] and [[Bric]] were hired to help train the [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="Clone Cadets">{{TCW|Clone Cadets}}</ref> [[Mandalorian]] [[Fenn Rau]], served as a [[flight instructor]] for the Grand Army of the Republic, before becoming the leader of the [[Journeyman Protector|Journeyman Protectors]].<ref name="Protector of Concord Dawn">{{Rebels|The Protector of Concord Dawn}}</ref>

[[File:AlphDLopez-Textless.png|thumb|right|270px|Boba Fett, a [[human]] male, was considered the best bounty hunter in the galaxy during the Imperial Era.]]
By the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], many bounty hunters were affiliated with the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]], which was authorized by the [[Imperial Office of Criminal Investigations]] to issue [[Imperial Peace-Keeping Certificate]]s,<ref name="Ezra's Journal">''[[Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger]]''</ref> which gave bounty hunters access to the [[Imperial Enforcement DataCore]].<ref name="Ezra's Gamble">''[[Ezra's Gamble]]''</ref>

Following the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[Zabrak]] bounty hunter [[Jas Emari]] attempted to collect [[New Republic]] bounties on the [[Imperial officer]]s [[Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]], [[Grand Moff]] [[Valco Pandion]], [[General]] [[Jylia Shale]], the financier [[Arsin Crassus]], and the [[Emperor's adviser|Imperial adviser]] [[Yupe Tashu]]. Emari subsequently joined [[Norra Wexley]]'s [[Norra Wexley's team|team]] and played a role in the [[Rebellion on Akiva|rebellion]] on [[Akiva]]. In the months after the events of [[Endor]], the bounty hunter [[Dengar]] predicted that the new government would start forcing bounties on other bounty hunters.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>

In [[5 ABY]], [[Grand Admiral]] Sloane later hired the bounty hunter [[Mercurial Swift]] to assassinate [[Vice Admiral]] [[Perwin Gedde]] and to rescue [[Commandant]] [[Brendol Hux]] and his son [[Armitage Hux]] from [[Arkanis]] under orders from [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]]. After the [[Liberation Day (New Republic)|Liberation Day]] [[Attack on Chandrila|attack]] on [[Chandrila]], the New Republic began denying services to bounty hunters in response to mass paranoia following the attack.<ref name="Life Debt" />

Later, Swift decided to collect the [[Black Sun]] [[crime lord]] [[Gyuti]]'s bounty on Jas Emari. He hired Dengar, [[Embo]], and [[Jeeta]] as his crew. However, Emari managed to turn Swift's crew against him by convincing them that the New Republic would reward them for helping to hunt Grand Admiral Sloane. Due to their services during the [[Battle of Jakku]], Dengar, Embo, and Jeeta received full pardons and some money from the New Republic. They then joined [[Jas Emari's crew|Emari's crew]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

[[Pokkit]] took low rent jobs for the Bounty Hunters' Guild, although one of her bounties was stolen by a [[human]] who she knew as [[Jod Na Nawood|Dash Zentin]]. She later began working for a private client, and so no longer needed to take work from the Guild.<ref name="SC5">{{SkeletonCrew|You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates}}</ref>

Around 9 ABY, a [[galactic recession]] led to exceptionally low bounties, making the dangerous profession less attractive. As a result, at the request of the client ordering the bounty or at the discretion of the local Guild boss, high-value targets were assigned to several bounty hunters equipped with identical [[tracking fob]]s.<ref name="100 objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref>

==Stance==
===Work with the Hutt Clan===
{{Quote|They want to know who shall take on this very dangerous task.''"<br />"''That would be me. Cad Bane, at your service. I'll take on any job for the right price.|TC-70 and Cad Bane|<ref name="Evil Plans">{{TCW|Evil Plans}}</ref>}}
From before the Clone Wars, bounty hunters had notably worked for the [[Hutt Clan]]. For example, Cad Bane and [[Cad Bane's group|his team]] [[Senate hostage crisis|freed]] [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]] the Hutt from [[Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center|prison]],<ref name="Hostage Crisis" /> and [[Sugi (bounty hunter)|Sugi]] and Embo protected the [[Grand Hutt Council|Hutt Council]] when the renegade [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] and [[Death Watch]] [[Mission to Nal Hutta|attacked]].<ref name="Eminence">{{TCW|Eminence (episode)}}</ref>

===Work during the Clone Wars===
{{Quote|Make it quick, general. We're charging by the minute, and if my ship gets damaged it will cost you extra.|Sugi, to Tarfful|<ref name="Wookiee Hunt">{{TCW|Wookiee Hunt}}</ref>}}
[[File:Hunter_Instinct_SWDL.png|thumb|left|250px|Bossk, a Trandoshan bounty hunter during the Clone Wars, and beyond]]
Prior to the outbreak of the Clone Wars, the [[Sith]] [[Sith Lord|Lord]] Darth Tyranus hired Jango Fett to be the clone template for Kamino's Clone Army. Tyranus then hired Fett and [[Zam Wesell]] to [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|assassinate]] Senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of [[Naboo]] at the behest of [[Trade Federation]] [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]].<ref name="Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones" /> Tyranus's master, [[Darth Sidious]] hired [[Cad Bane]] to [[Raid on the Jedi Temple|steal]] a [[holocron]] from the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]].<ref name="Holocron Heist" /> He also hired Embo to attack Senator [[Rush Clovis]].<ref name="An Old Friend">{{TCW|An Old Friend}}</ref>

Some bounty hunters, such as Sugi, [[Seripas]], Embo, and [[Rumi Paramita]] were hired by the [[Felucian]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters" /> [[Casiss Midagatis]]<ref name="TCW CE">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> to protect his village from the [[Ohnaka Gang]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters" /> Sugi and Seripas were then hired by [[Wookiee]] General [[Tarfful]] to take him and his fellow soldiers to rescue [[Chewbacca]] from [[Trandoshan]] hunters on [[Wasskah]].<ref name="Wookiee Hunt" /> The [[Kaminoan]]s hired Bric and El-Les to train the [[clone cadet]]s.<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> The Republic also placed bounties on wanted fugitives during the Clone Wars, such as [[Savage Opress]].<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref> Even the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] offered a million [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] a head on any Jedi.<ref name="HfZ" /> [[Sib Canay]] hired [[Greedo]] and his [[Gotal]] [[Unidentified Gotal bounty hunter|partner]] to kidnap [[Pantoran]] [[Chairman]] [[Notluwiski Papanoida|Papanoida's]] two daughters to [[Tatooine]] and the [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Droid Control Ship]] at [[Pantora]].<ref name="Sphere of Influence" />

===Work with the Empire===
{{Quote|There will be a substantial reward for the one who finds the ''Millennium Falcon''. You are free to use any methods necessary, but I want them alive. No disintegrations.|Darth Vader to a group of bounty hunters|<ref name="Episode V" />}}
[[File:DevaronianFemale-ND.png|thumb|right|200px|A female Devaronian bounty hunter during the Imperial Era]]
The Galactic Empire often employed bounty hunters to hunt down [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebels]].<ref name="Episode V" /> Private individuals could also list bounties on people. Jabba placed a very large bounty on [[Han Solo]]<ref name="Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref> who was captured by [[Boba Fett]].<ref name="Episode V" /> 

The [[Advanced Science Division]] hired certain class one bounty hunters, like [[Asajj Ventress]] and Cad Bane, to retrieve [[Midi-chlorian|M-count]] targets for the Empire.<ref name="Bad Territory" /> The class one hunters meet at the [[Imperial Station 003]] where the targets, mostly children, were exchanged with credits in the station's hangars and were then transported to [[Tantiss Base]] for experimentation.<ref name="Identity Crisis">{{TBB|Identity Crisis}}</ref>

===Work with the First Order===
The First Order employed the bounty hunters to hunt [[Resistance]] [[Spy|spies]]. After the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]], [[Hamato Xiono]] warns his son [[Kazuda Xiono]] after the First Order has identified him, his actions have put his whole family in danger and that there may be bounty hunters coming after him.<ref name="AQSR">{{Resistance|A Quick Salvage Run}}</ref> The First Order sent [[Ax Tagrin]] to [[Varkana]] where [[Lechee]], a First Order collaborator who moved their shipments and materials through [[black market]] channels, told him that [[Norath Kev]] was a Resistance spy.<ref name="Missing Agent" />
