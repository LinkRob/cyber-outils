'''A6-G2''', also known as "'''Aces'''," was a cheerful [[astromech droid]] assigned to [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] for his [[mission to Lenahra]]<ref name="Padawan" /> in [[41 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Padawan (novel)|''Padawan'']] to [[41 BBY]].</ref> [[Gender|She]] was adept at flying and eager to follow directions.<ref name="Padawan" />

She helped Kenobi along with a group of teenagers during the fight that occurred between two of the teenager's uncle, [[Loegrib]], and the teenagers. After the skirmish, A6 returned to [[Coruscant]], along with Kenobi.<ref name="Padawan" />
