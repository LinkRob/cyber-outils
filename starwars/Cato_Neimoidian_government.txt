The '''Cato Neimoidian government''' was the [[planet]]ary governing body of [[Cato Neimoidia]]. It had strong ties to the [[Trade Federation]] but remained independent of it. 

==History==
During the [[Clone Wars]], the government had opted to publicly declare neutrality, much like the [[Trade Federation]]. [[Alluv Eyam]] served as the government's [[minister of defense]]. [[Rosbuen Frisk]] also served as the [[Galactic Republic]]'s diplomat to Cato Neimoidia, operating from the [[Republic consulate office]] when on the planet.<ref name="Brotherhood" />

The Cato Neimoidian government had its own military, the [[Trade Federation Home Defense Legions|Neimoidian Defense Legion]], which was utilized by the regime as well as the Trade Federation. The Defense Legion maintained a special forces unit, [[Neimoidian special forces]], of which the [[commando]] [[Ruug Quarnom]] was formerly an operative. She later served in the [[Neimoidian Royal Guard]]&mdash;another branch of the military which also saw deployment within both the Trade Federation and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]&mdash;alongside the guardsman [[Ketar Nor]].<ref name="Brotherhood" />
