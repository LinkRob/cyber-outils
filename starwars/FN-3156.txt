'''FN-3156'''<ref name="Poe Dameron: Flight Log" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> who served as a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] in the [[First Order]]. After [[Attack on Tuanul|capturing]] the [[Resistance]] [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] in [[Tuanul]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]], FN-3156 along with [[FN-2198]], [[HX-1072]], and [[JY-834]] performed a scan of Dameron's [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] and reported that they found nothing of significance.<ref name="Poe Dameron: Flight Log" />
