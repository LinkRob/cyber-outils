'''Detectives''', or '''investigators''', were individuals who investigated criminal activity. Generally they were [[police officer]]s, however, [[Jedi]] such as [[Tera Sinube]] who were able to use [[the Force]] to help piece together the crime, would assist regular police detectives with their investigations, in their capacity as a [[Jedi investigator]].<ref name="Tera Sinube" /> Detectives from the [[Coruscant Security Force]] would sometimes be referred to as [[inspector]]s.<ref name="Senate Murders" /> During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Imperial Security Bureau]] personnel could be [[Assignment|assigned]] as investigators to investigate criminal and rebel activity, as exampled when an [[Unidentified Imperial Security Bureau major|ISB major]] helped [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] investigate a [[Hunt for Jocasta Nu|bombing]] on Coruscant when a [[Jedi]] emblem was recovered from a site in the early days of the Empire.<ref name="Dying Light II" /> In the Imperial Security Bureau, there was also an elevated rank of [[Chief Investigator]].<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> [[Erno]], [[Mursey]], and [[Shreen]] were detectives that worked at the [[Peace and Security Headquarters]] in [[Coronet City]].<ref name="Life Debt" />
