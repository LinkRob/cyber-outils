'''Blosphi extract''' was a [[foodstuff]] which, during the [[Imperial Era]], was usually sold as rations for [[Wookiee]]s. It provided enough nutrition to support them, though most didn't care for it. Despite this, several other [[species]], including [[Dashade]]s, enjoyed it as a delicacy.

A large amount of blosphi extract was aboard [[starship]]s stolen by [[Grand Admiral]] [[Balanhai Savit]] when leaving the [[Kurost transfer point]] with components for [[Project Stardust]]. Savit had hired criminals led by two Dashades, [[Maliss]] and [[Sorath]], to capture the stolen ships and steal vital components from them, and Grand Admiral [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] theorized that he was paying the Dashades with blosphi extract.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />
