An '''attack''' on the [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]]<ref name="Chapter 16" /> that had once been owned by [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> occurred in [[9 ABY]]<ref name="9 ABY date note" /> when [[Boba Fett]] and [[Fennec Shand]] stormed the old palace which was under the rule of<ref name="Chapter 16" /> its new [[crime lord]],<ref name="audio description">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 16: The Rescue}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> Jabba's former [[majordomo]] [[Bib Fortuna]]. During the attack, Shand first stormed in, shooting every person inside the palace except for Fortuna and [[Unidentified Rutian Twi'lek|his]] [[Twi'lek]] [[Slavery|slave]], which she freed. As Fortuna watched in horror, Fett walked in calmly and [[Death|killed]] him, sitting on the throne with Shand by his side.<ref name="Chapter 16" />
