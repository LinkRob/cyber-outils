'''1139''' was a [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]] who served the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].

==Biography==
Once during a mission, 1139 and their fellow [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]], [[1137]] were forgotten on a desert [[Planet/Legends|planet]] after their unit miscounted and departed. Ultimately, this proved fortuitous, for while abandoned the two soldiers learned of a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|CIS]] secret weapon project. As dutiful soldiers, 1139 and their companion resolved to destroy it. To aid in this endeavor, they built a [[TX-130 Saber-class fighter tank/Legends|TX-130]] fighter tank, with which they destroyed the Separatist weapon. Soon thereafter, realizing their absence, a Republic gunship returned to retrieve the duo.<ref name="Always count your clones" />

Having proven their competence, 1139, together with 1137, were then assigned to hunt the missing [[Astromech droid/Legends|astromech]] [[Droid/Legends|droid]] [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]], their squad ordered by [[Supreme Chancellor/Legends|Supreme Chancellor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] to find them before the Jedi. Travelling in a [[Nu-class attack shuttle/Legends|Nu-class attack shuttle]], their first stop was the sector where R2-D2 was last seen, taking directions from a group of [[Rocket battle droid/Legends|Rocket battle droids]] who were floating through space. Landing on an unidentified [[Planet/Legends|planet]] as their scanners picked up various droid signatures.<ref name="A droid in the hand">{{LEGOWebCite|subdomain=starwars|url=en-us/Comics/comick11.aspx|text="A droid in the hand is worth two in the asteroid belt"|int=A droid in the hand is worth two in the asteroid belt|nolive=1|nobackup=1}}</ref>. After struggling to find any droids, the clone squad was ambushed by various [[B1-series battle droid/Legends|B1]] and [[B2-series super battle droid/Legends|B2]] [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droids]], 1139's commander ordered their shuttle to give them cover fire while the other clones attacked the droids head on. The clone squad were able to quickly overwhelm them and steal the droid forces' [[Armored Assault Tank/Legends|Armored Assault Tank]],<ref name="Clones don't surrender">{{LEGOWebCite|subdomain=starwars|url=en-us/Comics/comicl12.aspx|text="Clones don't even know the meaning of the word surrender"|int=Clones don't even know the meaning of the word surrender|nolive=1|nobackup=1}}</ref> only to be bombed from above by the clone forces in the shuttle, who had mistaken them for the enemy.<ref name="Do clones dream">''[[Do Clones dream of Electric Mynocks?]]''</ref>

Waking up the unconcious 1137, 1139 began work on rebuilding their squadron's arsenal, only for their squad to be ambushed by a further wave of Armored Assault Tanks.<ref name="Do clones dream" /> Fighting alongside their squad, 1139 and their comrades were eventually able to commandeer the AATs, regrouping with 1137 after the battle was over.<ref name="Clone trooper obeys orders">{{LEGOWebCite|subdomain=starwars|url=en-US/Comics/265916.aspx|text="A clone trooper always obeys orders"|int=A clone trooper always obeys orders|nolive=1}}</ref>

Their partnership with 1137 became momentarily contentious, however, when they expressed desire for names to supersede their numerical designation&mdash;a sentiment not shared by 1137.<ref name="By any other name">{{LEGOWebCite|subdomain=starwars|url=en-us/Comics/Player/2009-4.aspx|text="A clone by any other name"|int=A clone by any other name|nolive=1}}</ref> Nevertheless, their disagreement was quickly resolved, and their search continued. Eventually, it took them and their pilot to an ice planet, where their ship crashed. As with their previous abandonment, this, too, proved fortuitous, as it beget a chance encounter with none other than the prominent CIS leader, [[Nute Gunray/Legends|Nute Gunray]]. Deciding against direct assault upon the [[Neimoidian/Legends|Neimoidian's]] shuttle, 1139 and their companions settled on a ruse. Feigning that they were selling Clone Scout cookies, 1137 and the pilot distracted Gunray while 1139 and their commander subdued him. This being accomplished, the clone troopers absconded with the shuttle.<ref name="Clones are famous">{{LEGOWebCite|subdomain=starwars|url=en-us/Comics/comico16.aspx|text="Clones are famous all across the galaxy"|int=Clones are famous all across the galaxy|nolive=1|nobackup=1}}</ref>

The next stop on the astromech-hunt was [[Endor/Legends|Endor]]. Mistaking an [[Ewok/Legends|Ewok]] as a hostile [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]], 1137 retreated into the shuttle. After being corrected by the commander, 1139 sought to establish contact with the Ewok. However, their attempt ultimately resulted in the capture of the whole party. Taken to the Ewoks' camp, 1139 awoke to find themselves roasting over a fire and his comrades-in-arms, 1137 among them, being employed as percussion instruments. Their dilemma proved temporary as soon Nute Gunray arrived, escorted by droids and tanks, seeking vengeance for his humiliation.<ref name="Clones are diplomats">{{LEGOWebCite|subdomain=starwars|url=en-us/Comics/comicp17.aspx|text="Clone troopers are excellent diplomats"|int=Clone troopers are excellent diplomats|nolive=1|nobackup=1}}</ref> This intervention inadvertently allowed the troopers to free themselves, after which they quickly dispatched their foes. Dismayed by their ferocity, Gunray retook his shuttle and fled.<ref name="Clones come to an end">{{LEGOWebCite|subdomain=starwars|url=en-us/Comics/Player/2009-7.aspx|text="All good clones must come to an end"|int=All good clones must come to an end|nolive=1}}</ref>

Despite the rather chaotic turn of events, 1139 and their companions retained enough composure to search for another means of transportation: three [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/Legends|BTL-B Y-wing starfighters]]. Leaving Endor, they were intercepted by a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer/Legends|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]]. Aboard was, to 1139's surprise, none other than Supreme Chancellor Palpatine. Extremely displeased with their performance, citing the fact that Anakin and Ahsoka had already recovered the missing R2-D2, the Republic leader discharged them from the [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Grand Army of the Republic]]. Without further ado, 1139 and their comrades took a beach-side vacation.<ref name="Clones come to an end" />

==Behind the scenes==
1137 and [[1139]] are the numbers before and after [[1138 (easter egg)|1138]]. The name of the comic that in which 1137 and 1139 are introduced, "[[Always count your clones before take-off]]," derives from the plot point of the duo having been forgotten behind.
