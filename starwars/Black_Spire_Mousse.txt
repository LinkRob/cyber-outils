'''Black Spire Mousse''' was a [[Foodstuff|dessert]] served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The dish was based around [[Jogan fruit]] mousse with [[Blue milk]] sauce, and was served with a [[chocolate]] coin adorned with the emblem of Black Spire Outpost.

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Black Spire Mousse is a dessert available at the [[In-universe|real-world]] at Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]]<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> starting in [[June]] [[2024]].<ref name="WDWNT">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2024/05/two-new-dishes-docking-bay-7-dhs|text=Two New Dishes Coming to Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at Disney's Hollywood Studios|work=WDW News Today|archivedate=20240531162806}}</ref> The dish is described as "White Chocolate Jogan Fruit Mousse atop a Brownie Pistachio Brittle served with Blue Milk Sauce, Pineapple Cream, and Hibiscus Sponge topped with a Chocolate Black Spire Coin."<ref name="DHS Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=dining/hollywood-studios/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/dinner|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Dinner Menu}}</ref>
