'''Air brakes''' were [[starship]] components designed for use in an [[atmosphere]]. It was considered dangerous to leave them engaged while in [[hyperspace]]; leaving them on would result in a ship rumbling and creaking, with worse to follow should a pilot forget to disable them. In the [[Standard year|year]] [[19 BBY]], fledgling pilot [[Trace Martez]] flew the ''[[Silver Angel]]'' &mdash; a modified [[Nebula-class freighter|''Nebula''-class freighter]] she built from spare parts &mdash; for the first time. En route in hyperspace to the [[planet]] [[Kessel]], Martez forgot to disengage the freighter's air brakes after emerging from the skies of the planet [[Coruscant]] but later remembered to disengage them after noticing her freighter's rumbling and creaking.<ref name="Deal No Deal">{{TCW|Deal No Deal}}</ref>
