'''Cyphar''' was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Thrawn" /> located in the [[Spirva sector]] of<ref name="SWSystems" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="TFABG" /> that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Cyphari]] [[species]].<ref name="Thrawn" /> The planet was mainly covered in grasslands and deserts with canyons, and had ground rich in [[Scarn (spice)|scarn]], a pre-[[spice]] compound. At some point, during the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]], a [[Cyphar Incident|land dispute]] occurred between the Cyphari's [[Afe clan]] and the human colonists from the [[Hollenside Enclave]]. the situation escalated so much, the Empire was forced to send Admiral [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] and Ensign [[Eli N. Vanto]] to ease the tensions.
