'''''Buy Republic War Bonds''''' was a [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] used by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the late portion of the [[Clone Wars]], and created by [[Janyor]] of [[Bith]]. It depicted two [[Phase II clone trooper armor|Phase II]] [[clone trooper]]s, a [[Imperial shock trooper|shock trooper]], as well as [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Bacara]] standing in a [[city]]scape as well as a [[Color|red]] and black banner overhead with the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Republic crest|logo]] and the words in [[High Galactic]] "Buy Republic War Bonds," with the colors of each word being the inverse of the banner. At the bottom was a message in High Galactic stating it was a message from the reader's Republic benefactors.<ref name="Propaganda" />

The [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned the poster in his book ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was released in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>
