The '''''Echo of Hope''''' was a refitted [[Mon Calamari]]<ref name="BTA" /> [[MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80A ''Home One'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]]<ref name="Codebreaker2" /> in service to the [[Resistance]] during their [[Cold War|conflict]] with the [[First Order]]. After leaving the [[New Republic]] to join the Resistance, [[Rapier Squadron]] was redeployed aboard the ''Echo of Hope''.<ref name="BTA" /> Poe Dameron later attended a briefing onboard where he learned about a First Order [[Cheka|code breaking device]] on [[Nar Kaaga]].<ref name="Codebreaker1">[[Hyperspace Stories—Codebreaker 1|''Hyperspace Stories—Codebreaker'' 1]]</ref>
