'''AT-AT 36''' was the designation of an [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)|All Terrain Armored Transport]] that was deployed on the [[city]] of [[Jalath]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. There, along with another [[walker]], it was assigned to provide security for a convoy of three [[Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette|''Sphyrna''-class Hammerhead corvettes]] and prevent them from falling into rebel hands. While participating in a [[Mission on Lothal|battle]] against [[Phoenix Cell]], AT-AT 36 was damaged and toppled by a [[corvette]] that was under rebel control.<ref name="Princess on Lothal" />
