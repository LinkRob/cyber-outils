'''Clone Lieutenant''' was a [[Military rank|rank]] held by a number of [[clone trooper officer]]s throughout the [[Republic Military]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The rank would continue to be used within the early [[Imperial Era]] as [[Cloning|clones]] filled out the ranks of the early [[Stormtrooper Corps]].

==Description==
A Clone Lieutenant was a [[clone trooper]] with the rank of [[lieutenant]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]<ref name="CL" /> or [[Republic Navy|Galactic Republic Navy]], both of which were part of the overall [[Republic Military]].<ref name="TCWfilm" /> In the Grand Army, a Clone Lieutenant commanded a [[platoon]] of thirty-six [[trooper]]s<ref name="CL" /> via the [[Assignment|position]] of [[platoon leader]].<ref name="Lieutenant Waxer note">{{TCW|Carnage of Krell}} exhibits [[Waxer]] as being the [[platoon leader]] of a [[Waxer's platoon|platoon]]. According to [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], [[platoon]]s in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] were led by a Clone Lieutenant. Indeed, ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'' notes that Waxer was promoted to the rank of Clone Lieutenant by the time of "Carnage of Krell."</ref> The four [[lieutenant]]s of a [[company]], in turn, were led by their respective [[Clone Captain]].<ref name="CL" /> However, Clone Lieutenants were also able to hold command in smaller army units, such as a [[squad]],<ref name="Ambush" /> and fell into the command structure of overall formations, such as a [[division]]. Indeed, a Clone Lieutenant could assume command of an overall force should something happen to their superiors.<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> [[Clone trooper pilot]]s active under the Republic Navy could also hold the rank of lieutenant.<ref name="TCWfilm" /> Elite [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s<ref name="Victory and Death" /> and [[clone commando]]s could also hold the rank.<ref name="Hilo Databank" />

[[File:Hasbro-PhaseIboxart.png|thumb|left|300px|Clone Lieutenants held command over formations of their clone siblings and were, at first, identified by blue markings.]]
At the start of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> [[Phase I clone trooper armor|Phase I armored]]<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> lieutenants wore [[clone trooper armor]] featuring four [[Color|blue]] circles on the left breastplate as well as a stripe of blue down the length of each [[Limb|arm]], vertically up the middle of the [[helmet]] and laterally around the visor.<ref name="CL" /> Encouraged by the [[Jedi]] to personalize their [[armor]] and develop their personalities, clone troopers,<ref name="Aftermath Trivia Guide">{{SW|url=aftermath-trivia-gallery|text=Tech's Notes: "Aftermath" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref> including the lieutenants, would quickly<ref name="Ambush" /> come to personalize their gear<ref name="Aftermath Trivia Guide" /> with unit markings and individual flourishes.<ref name="Ambush" /> Such would continue into the days of [[Phase II clone trooper armor]].<ref name="Carnage">{{TCW|Carnage of Krell}}</ref> During the early [[Imperial Era]], the Clone Lieutenant rank persisted into the [[Stormtrooper Corps]].<ref name="Hilo Databank" />

==History==
Lieutenant [[Hawk]] served as a [[pilot]] for the [[501st Legion]]. In that capacity, Hawk flew a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|gunship]] during the [[Battle of Teth]].<ref name="TCWfilm" /> Clone Lieutenant [[Thire]] served in the [[Coruscant Guard]] as a [[Imperial shock trooper|shock trooper]]. He [[Battle of Rugosa|accompanied]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] to [[Rugosa]] in order to persuade [[King]] [[Katuunko]] to ally with the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Ambush" /> Another clone lieutenant was [[Trap]]. Trap led the [[Tango Company]] on a mission to escort the Republic [[Pelta-class frigate|Pelta-class]] [[Pelta-class Medical Frigate|medical frigate]] [[TB-73]] back to the [[Ord Cestus medical station]]. Whilst on route to the station, fellow clone trooper [[Scythe]] was infected by a [[Geonosian]] [[brain worm]], which [[Skirmish aboard TB-73|led]] to the infection of all other clones on board, including Trap.<ref name="Brain Invaders">{{TCW|Brain Invaders}}</ref>

Lieutenant [[Galle]] served under [[Jedi General]] [[Aayla Secura]] as part of the [[327th Star Corps]]. When [[Order 66]] was issued by [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], Galle opened fire on Secura alongside the clones present, [[Death|killing]] her.<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> The Clone Lieutenant rank continued to be used in the early Imperial Era, during which time clones still saw a great degree of use in the [[Imperial Military]]. The [[commando]] [[Hilo]] held the rank of lieutenant.<ref name="Hilo Databank" />
