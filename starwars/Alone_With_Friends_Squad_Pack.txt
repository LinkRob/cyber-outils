The '''Alone With Friends Squad Pack (SWP56)''' is an expansion for the [[Atomic Mass Games]] miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: Shatterpoint]]''. It features figures of [[Finn]], [[Rey Skywalker]], [[BB-8]], [[Han Solo]], and [[Chewbacca]].<ref name="AMG" />

**[[Chewbacca]]
