The '''Dwarf Star''' was a small-scale replica of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] built by [[Lax Torios]] for [[planet]]ary use on [[The Island]] of a [[Unidentified planet (The Island)|planet]]. Prior to the Dwarf Star's creation, Lax Torios expressed that she wanted to build her own tiny [[Death Star]], to which the other [[Castaways (The Island)|Castaways]] laughed at her. The laughing soon stopped, however, after she created it. She sold it at her vehicle shop for forty thousand [[databits]]. She also created two variants of her Dwarf Star, the [[Color|blue]], red, white, and yellow [[Beach Ball]], and the green and red [[Bauble Battlestation]].<ref name="Castaways" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dwarf Star appeared in the [[2021]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Castaways]]''.<ref name="Castaways" />
