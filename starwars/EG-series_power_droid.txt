The '''EG-series power droid''', also known as the '''EG power droid''', was a model of [[power droid]] manufactured by [[Veril Line Systems]]. Like the very similar [[GNK-series power droid]], it emitted low honking sounds, which earned it the nickname of "gonk droid."<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> 

When [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] were captured by [[Jawa]]s on [[Tatooine]], an [[EG-6 power droid]] was present in the [[sandcrawler]] they were stored in. The [[Lars family]] owned an EG-6 power droid.<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> The droid [[WG-22]] was a modified EG-series droid to be a [[gladiator droid]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> The droid [[EG-86]] was a [[courier droid]] of vital information for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]].<ref name="Blood Sisters">{{Rebels|Blood Sisters}}</ref> 

[[EG-4]] was a EG-series power droid owned by the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. While the Alliance was based in [[Echo Base]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]], EG-4 provided power to the [[starship]]s and machinery around the base.<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref>
