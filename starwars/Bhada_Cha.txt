'''Bhada Cha''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[bounty hunter]] who was the [[Marriage|husband]] of [[Ramat Cha]] and the [[Parent|father]] of [[Chanath Cha]].<ref name="Dark Lord 11" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> shortly after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Bhada, Ramat, and Chanath [[Mission to Cabarria|attempted]] to claim a [[bounty]] placed on the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Bhada and his family traveled to the [[planet]] [[Cabarria]], but their mission failed and Bhada and Ramat were [[Death|killed]] by Vader.<ref name="Dark Lord 11" />
