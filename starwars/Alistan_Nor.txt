'''Alistan Nor''' was a remote [[planet]] located in [[the galaxy]] where the ancient [[Force Builders]]' [[city]] of [[Alistan Nor (city)|Alistan Nor]] was located. Alistan Nor historically was covered in [[grass]]y plains, but over [[time]], it became a lush [[jungle]]. Alistan Nor was also surrounded by three [[asteroid]] rings. By the time of the [[Quest to build the Arrowhead|quest to build the]] [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']], Alistan Nor had been abandoned and became a lush jungle.<ref name="TTOAN" />
