The '''Elders''' were a [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[humanoid]] [[species]] that [[Homeworld|inhabited]] ancient [[Naboo]]. Little was known about them other than their history of conflict with the [[Gungan]]s and the monuments they left behind. [[Archaeologist]]s were unsure whether the Elders' giant statues were representations of themselves or massive icons devoted to their [[Deity|deities]]. By the [[time]] of the [[Invasion of Naboo]], the Elders were long gone. Several Gungan and Naboo chroniclers surmised that the Elders failed to live in harmony with the [[planet]]'s delicate balance and were consumed by their own greed.<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> One of their monuments, located north of [[Lianorm Swamp]], became a [[Gungan Sacred Place|sacred]] gathering place for the Gungans.<ref name="Ultimate SW" /> The Elders also left behind a sanctuary on the Naboo moon of [[Rori]].<ref name="Nexus of Power">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref>
