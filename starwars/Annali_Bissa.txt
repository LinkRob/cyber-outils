'''Annali Bissa''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] child from [[Corellia]] whose name was in a [[memory crystal]] after [[Order 66]]. After [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Hunt for Jocasta Nu|captured]] [[Jedi]] [[Chief Librarian]] [[Jocasta Nu]] following a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]], [[Imperial shock trooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]] searched Nu and discovered the memory crystal. After Vader analyzed the memory crystal, he discovered that it was a list of Force-sensitive children, targets of the former [[Jedi Order]], which included Bissa.<ref name="Dying Light 10" />

Jocasta was aware that [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], secretly [[Darth]] Sidious, Vader's [[Sith Master]], was after the list, even seeking the list when he served as [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]], so that he could, as Nu suspected, train more [[Sith warrior]]s. After Vader [[Death|killed]] all shock [[trooper]]s after Nu revealed his former identity, the former [[Jedi Knight]] Anakin Skywalker, he then also killed Nu and informed his master that she had attempted to escape, which resulted in her death. When Palpatine asked Vader if he had recovered any information from Nu, Vader responded that he had learned nothing.<ref name="Dying Light 10" />

==Behind the scenes==
Annali Bissa's name written in [[Aurebesh]] was first mentioned in the [[canon]] [[2018]] comic, [[Darth Vader (2017) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 10]], written by [[Charles Soule]] and pencilled by [[Giuseppe Camuncoli]].<ref name="Dying Light 10" />
