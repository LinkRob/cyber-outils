The '''Argoban''' were a group<ref name="HRA 9" /> from<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=Argoban|system=[[Argoba system]]}}</ref> the [[Argoba system]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> known for being [[Pirate|raiders]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. In particular, they were known for kidnapping [[Aloxian]] [[Youngling|children]] from [[Zav Alox]] to be [[Slavery|enslaved]] as child [[soldier]]s. The Argoban would take away the [[vonduun crab]] [[Bone|skulls]] worn by the children in order to increase their aggression and natural [[Death|killing]] instincts. When an [[Qort|Aloxian infant]] wound up at [[Takodana Castle]], [[Maz Kanata]]'s associate [[Farks]] speculated that the child had been taken by Argoban raiders for that purpose.<ref name="HRA 9" />
