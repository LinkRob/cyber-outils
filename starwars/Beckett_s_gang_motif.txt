{{Top|music}}
A '''motif''' for [[Beckett's gang]] was composed by [[John Powell]] for the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''. Its debut is during the [[Mimban Campaign|battle]] on [[Mimban]], when [[Tobias Beckett]] first appears to a choral rendition of the motif. [[Han Solo]] subsequently discovers that Beckett is leading a crew of outlaws while posing as [[Imperial Army]] personnel.
