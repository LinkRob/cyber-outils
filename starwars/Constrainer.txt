The '''''Constrainer''''' was an [[Interdictor-class heavy cruiser|''Interdictor''-class heavy cruiser]] in service to the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Equipped with [[gravity well projector]]s, it was capable of generating an [[interdiction field]], an artificial [[gravity well]] that allowed the [[Interdictor]] to prevent other [[Starship|vessels]] from entering [[hyperspace]]. During the [[Lothal campaign|campaign]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Phoenix Cell]], the [[heavy cruiser]] was attached to the [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]] under [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]. In [[2 BBY]], the ''Constrainer'' formed part of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Dark Omen's squadron|squadron]] sent to the [[planet]] [[Atollon]], where the Phoenix Cell had set up their [[Chopper Base|base]]. 

During Thrawn's ensuing [[Battle of Atollon|assault]] on the rebels, the ''Constrainer'', as well as another [[Kassius Konstantine's Interdictor|Interdictor cruiser]], prevented enemy ships from bypassing the Imperial [[blockade]]. However, the other Interdictor was destroyed, allowing rebel [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Ezra Bridger]] to escape the fray. Bridger subsequently enlisted several [[Mandalorian warrior]]s to aid the rebels. These warriors, led by Bridger and [[Sabine Wren]], landed on the ''Constrainer''{{'s}} [[hull]] and fired on one of its [[gravity]] well projectors. Although engaged by Imperial [[Jumptrooper|jet troopers]], the [[strike team]] nonetheless managed to rupture the generator. This caused it to explode, obliterating the ship and allowing the rebels to flee.

==Description==
{{Quote|Ready your jetpacks and target those gravity well projectors.|Sabine Wren, during the attack on the ''Constrainer''|audio=Ready-Jetpacks-Zero-Hour.flac|<ref name="Zero Hour" />}}
[[File:Constrainer-Chimaera.png|thumb|right|300px|The ''Constrainer'' above the ''Chimaera'']]
Belonging to the [[Interdictor line|''Interdictor'' line's]]<ref name="Line" /> [[Interdictor-class heavy cruiser|''Interdictor''-class]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the ''Constrainer''<ref name="BTCTG" /> was classed as both a [[Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Timelines" /> and [[heavy cruiser]].<ref name="Starships&Speeders" /> Measuring 1,129 [[meter]]s in length,<ref name="Length" /> the [[Starship|vessel]] was equipped with [[deflector shield]]s, [[laser cannon]]s, five [[sublight engine]]s, and a [[hyperdrive]]. Its dorsal [[hull]] housed an open bay, the aft wall of which contained an otherwise enclosed [[hangar]]. An [[Interdictor]] vessel, the ''Constrainer'' possessed four [[gravity well projector]]s,<ref name="Zero Hour" /> which were capable of generating an [[interdiction field]].<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> The artificial [[gravity well]] generated by this field enabled the [[cruiser]] to pull other starships out of [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Zero Hour" />

==History==
===Arrival at Atollon===
{{Quote|Something's pulled them out of hyperspace.''"<br />"''If that is true, there will be no escape for us.|General Dodonna and Commander Sato, referring to the two Imperial Interdictors|audio=No-Escape-Zero-Hour.flac|<ref name="Zero Hour" />}}
The ''Constrainer'' served in the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="Zero Hour" /> and formed part of the [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] during his [[Lothal campaign|campaign]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Phoenix Cell]].<ref name="Zero Hour" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Thrawn discovered that [[Chopper Base]]&mdash;headquarters of the Phoenix Cell<ref name="Zero Hour" />&mdash;was located on the [[Lothal sector]] [[planet]]<ref name="DawnOfRebellion">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> [[Atollon]]. Subsequently, he ordered [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]] to dispatch<ref name="Zero Hour" /> a [[Dark Omen's squadron|squadron]]<ref name="BTCTG" /> of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] vessels to [[Battle of Atollon|engage]] the rebels at Atollon. The [[squadron]] included a total of five [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]],<ref name="Zero Hour" /> including the ''[[Dark Omen]]''<ref name="BTCTG" /> as the [[Command ship|lead]] [[destroyer]]; two ''Interdictor''-class heavy cruisers that included the ''Constrainer'' and [[Kassius Konstantine's Interdictor|another]] commanded by Admiral Konstantine; and two [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruisers]].<ref name="Zero Hour" />

[[File:Atollon-Interdictors.png|thumb|left|300px|The ''Constrainer'' (far background) was deployed at the Battle of Atollon alongside Konstantine's Interdictor vessel (foreground).]]
The squadron exited hyperspace in the [[Atollon system]] in a line formation, with the ''Constrainer'' positioned to the left side, and the Imperial forces immediately blocked the rebels' long-range transmissions. Rebel [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]], whose [[Massassi Group|forces]] were assisting the Phoenix Cell, ordered all rebel ships to flee, with an [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] subsequently jumping to hyperspace. However, the [[frigate]] was then pulled out of hyperspace by the interdiction field generated by the ''Constrainer'' and the other Interdictor. Upon realizing the Empire had brought Interdictor vessels, rebel [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]] lamented that there would be no escape for the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|rebel fleet]]. Konstantine's heavy cruiser then [[Space warfare|opened fire]] on the Nebulon-B, at which [[time]] the [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] were joined by Grand Admiral Thrawn from his [[flagship]], the<ref name="Zero Hour" /> [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="StarshipsAndSpeeders">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> ''[[Chimaera]]''.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

===Rebel retreat===
{{Quote|Here's the problem, that Interdictor cruiser.''"<br />"''If not for Commander Sato, there would be two.|Sabine Wren and Ezra Bridger, discussing the ''Constrainer''|audio=Interdictor-Problem-Zero-Hour.flac|<ref name="Zero Hour" />}}
The rebels subsequently charged the Imperial [[blockade]], with Commander Sato employing [[Danaan tactics]]. The Imperial vessels held their positions and waited for the enemy vessels to come to them, per Thrawn's orders, leading to the rebels being overrun. At this point, the ''Constrainer'' fired several [[laser]] blasts at the rebels. However, Commander Sato managed to goad Konstantine's heavy cruiser out of formation, then maneuvered the [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier]] ''[[Phoenix Nest]]'' to [[Ramming|ram]] the Interdictor, destroying both ships. This allowed [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Ezra Bridger]] to escape to hyperspace in his [[Kryze Gauntlet ship|Gauntlet]] [[starfighter]], [[Nightbrother (Gauntlet fighter)|''Nightbrother'']].<ref name="Zero Hour" />

[[File:Atollon-blockade-hologram.png|thumb|right|300px|A hologram of the Imperial blockade above Atollon (''Constrainer'' at bottom left)]]
Following the destruction of the other Interdictor and Bridger's escape, the Imperial forces pressed their attack, with the ''Constrainer'' turning so it was perpendicular to the Imperial line and its port side faced the rebels. Ultimately, the rebels were forced to return to their base on Atollon's surface. Thrawn's blockade subsequently formed into a triangle, with a single ''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer leading the group, followed by two more behind, with the final three at the rear. The ''Constrainer'' was then positioned after this sextet, immediately behind the ''Chimaera''. From this position, the blockade commenced an [[orbital bombardment]] on Chopper Base, which was repelled by the rebels' [[deflector shield generator]].<ref name="Zero Hour" />

Meanwhile, Bridger travelled to the<ref name="Zero Hour" /> [[Mandalore sector]] planet<ref name="Comprehensive Guide">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Krownest]] to recruit [[Mandalorian]] rebel [[Sabine Wren]] and her [[Clan Wren|clan]]. At the [[Wren Stronghold]], Bridger presented the family with a [[hologram]] representation of the Imperial blockade, including the ''Constrainer'', with Wren commenting that the Interdictor was the main problem facing the rebels. After agreeing to help, she then requested the [[C1-series astromech droid|astromech droid]] [[C1-10P]] bring up schematics of the ''Constrainer'' to plan their attack.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

===Attack on the ''Constrainer''===
{{Quote|Ezra, we're headed for the blockade. Is the Interdictor disabled yet?''"<br />"''Working on it!|Hera Syndulla and Ezra Bridger, discussing the attack on the ''Constrainer''|audio=Interdictor-disabled-Zero-Hour.flac|<ref name="Zero Hour" />}}
[[File:Constrainer-gravity-well.png|thumb|left|300px|One of the ''Constrainer''{{'s}} gravity well projectors was damaged by the attacking rebels.]]
While Thrawn led a [[Ground warfare|ground assault]] on the rebel base, Bridger returned in the ''Nightbrother'', aided by five [[Fang-class Protectorate fighter|''Fang''-class]] starfighters [[pilot]]ed by [[Mandalorian warrior]]s. While the Fang fighters [[Starfighter combat|engaged]] the Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], the ''Nightbrother'' deployed Bridger, Wren, and four other Mandalorian warriors onto the hull of the ''Constrainer'', ready to attack its [[gravity]] well projectors. Meanwhile, aboard the ''Chimaera'', [[Governor of the Lothal sector|Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]] was alerted to the rebel boarding by an [[Unidentified Imperial technician (Chimaera)|Imperial technician]], to which the [[governor]] ordered that the [[strike team]] be repelled.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

However, the rebels had already begun firing on the dorsal portion of the ship's aft starboard gravity well projectors with their [[blaster]]s, despite the ''Constrainer''{{'s}} deflector shields. At this point, six Imperial [[Jumptrooper|jet troopers]] flew over the generator and engaged the group. The rebels [[Death|killed]] several of the [[trooper]]s and pressed their attack on the heavy cruiser, the hull of which was cracking and emitting lightning. Rebel [[Captain]] [[Hera Syndulla]] then contacted Bridger and reported that the remaining rebel forces were heading for the blockade. Two [[TIE series|TIE fighters]] then fired at the ''Constrainer'', aiming for Bridger, but were quickly shot down by the Fang fighters, with one of the Imperial starfighters careening into the hull of the ''Constrainer'' and exploding. Bridger's group then intensified their attack, with the Mandalorians also using their [[jetpack]]-mounted [[Missile|rockets]] to damage the gravity well projector.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

[[File:Constrainer-explosion.png|thumb|right|300px|The ''Constrainer'' is destroyed.]]
Due to the rebel attack, the ''Constrainer''{{'s}} deflector shields were breached. The damaged gravity well projector exploded, allowing the rebels to return to the ''Nightbrother''. The explosion spread across the Interdictor's hull, spewing smoke and lighting, and the heavy cruiser began to shrink. Then, the ''Constrainer'' was destroyed as the whole ship was engulfed in [[Color|blue]] [[Fire|flame]], resulting in a shockwave. As the still-exploding wreckage began to slowly angle towards Atollon, the remaining rebel forces flew over the enflamed vessel to make the unimpeded jump to hyperspace.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|[Thrawn's] use of Interdictor cruisers to trap the rebel fleet at Atollon was a perfect demonstration of his ability to predict his enemies' decisions and counter them in advance.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>}}
In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] noted in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="Comprehensive Guide" /> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' that Thrawn had recognized the necessity of the Empire forcing the rebels to remain on the battlefield. Kin then stated that Thrawn's trapping of the rebel fleet with Interdictor cruisers at the Battle of Atollon perfectly demonstrated the Grand Admiral's ability to preempt his enemies' strategies and counter them ahead of time.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Appearance in "Zero Hour"===
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the ''Constrainer'' was first pictured in the midseason trailer for the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Three|third season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', which was uploaded to the official ''Star Wars'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel on [[January 4]], [[2017]].<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|upN-7ALj2OU|Star Wars Rebels Season 3 – Mid-Season Trailer (Official)|sw_url=video/star-wars-rebels-season-three-mid-season-trailer-official}}</ref> The vessel then appeared in "[[Zero Hour]],"<ref name="Zero Hour" /> the season's final episode. The episode was released on [[March 25]], 2017, over two parts, the twenty-first and twenty-second episodes of the season overall.<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|174|Star Wars Rebels Season Three Episode Guide|''Star Wars Rebels'' Season Three Episode Guide}}</ref>

In "Zero Hour," the ''Constrainer'' is notably absent from [[Wikipedia:Wide shot|wide shots]] of the Imperial blockade, for example, [[:File:Dark-Omen-and-squadron.png|here]] and [[:File:Battle_of_Atollon.png|here]].<ref name="Zero Hour" /> In the episode's corresponding installment of the ''Star Wars'' YouTube channel's series ''[[Rebels Recon]]'', ''Rebels''{{'s}} animation supervisor, [[Keith Kellogg]], explained that due to technical limitations, not all of the ships could be rendered at the same time.<ref name="RebelsRecon">{{RebelsRecon|MyUY7LnE4ls|#3.21: Inside "Zero Hour" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/disneyxd-rebels-recon-inside-zero-hour}}</ref>

===Naming===
[[File:Constrainer-DFR1.png|thumb|right|250px|The ''Constrainer'', as it appeared in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity]]
The ''Constrainer'' was unnamed in the episode<ref name="Zero Hour" /> but received identification in a preview for the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'', which was released on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 17]], [[2021]].<ref name="BTCTG-Preview">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-battles-that-changed-the-galaxy-announce|text=In ''Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy'', Go Inside the Crucible of War}}</ref> The book itself was released on [[October 28]] of the same year.<ref name="DK">{{DK|url=uk/book/9780241418703-star-wars-battles-that-changed-the-galaxy|text=Star Wars Battles That Changed the Galaxy}}</ref> The book erroneously uses an image of another ''Interdictor''-class heavy cruiser, the [[Interdictor (Interdictor-class)|''Interdictor'']],<ref name="BTCTG" /> from the ''Star Wars Rebels'' episode "[[Stealth Strike]]"<ref name="StealthStrike">{{Rebels|Stealth Strike}}</ref> to depict the ''Constrainer''.<ref name="BTCTG" />

The name ''Constrainer'' originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where the [[Constrainer/Legends|vessel]] was an [[Immobilizer 418 Interdictor-class Heavy Cruiser|Immobilizer 418 cruiser]] used by [[Thrawn's fleet (Galactic Empire)|Thrawn's fleet]].<ref name="DFR1">[[Dark Force Rising 1|''Dark Force Rising'' 1]]</ref> It first appeared in the second novel of ''[[Star Wars: The Thrawn Trilogy]]'', ''[[Dark Force Rising]]'', which was released in [[1992]] and written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="DarkForceRising">''[[Dark Force Rising]]''</ref> The [[Dark Force Rising 1|first issue]] of the novel's [[Star Wars: Dark Force Rising (comic series)|comic adaptation]] then gave the [[Starship/Legends|ship]] its first depiction. The [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] was written by [[Mike Baron]], illustrated by [[Terry Dodson]], and released by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="DFR1" /> on [[May 28]], [[1997]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54069/star_wars_dark_force_rising_1997_1|text=Star Wars: Dark Force Rising (1997) #1}}</ref>
