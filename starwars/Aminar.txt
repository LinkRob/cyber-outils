'''Aminar''' was a [[Gender|female]] nominally elected leader of the [[planet]] [[Desinta]]. Aminar served as the leader of Desinta<ref name="Resolve" /> sometime during the [[Standard year|years]] [[42 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Date">[[Resolve (short story)|"Resolve"]] is set after [[Dooku]]'s departure from the [[Jedi Order]], dated to 42 years before the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] by ''[[The Star Wars Book]]'', which corresponds to [[42 BBY]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system|dating system]] used by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. The story is also set prior to [[Qui-Gon Jinn]]'s [[death]] in the [[Battle of Naboo]], which is dated to [[32 BBY]] by ''Galactic Atlas''.</ref> During that time, Aminar met with [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] after the Jedi had come to Desinta to retrieve [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Lizel Liit]].<ref name="Resolve" />
