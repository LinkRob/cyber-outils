'''Firefeather starcutters''' were rare and expensive [[starship]]s. They could be recognized by their distinctive whistling sound. A member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Alliance Civil Government|council]] flew one such ship to [[Yavin 4]] shortly before the [[Battle of Scarif|beginning]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Rogue One Novel" />
