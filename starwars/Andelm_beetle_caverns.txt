The '''Andelm beetle caverns''' were a series of [[cave]]rns located underground on the [[planet]] [[Andelm IV]]. Indigenous [[Andelm beetle]]s inhabited the caverns. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[crime lord]] [[Jaum]] utilized forced labor to [[Mining|mine]] the caverns for the Andelm beetles and for the [[mineral]] [[dedlanite]].<ref name="Chewbacca 1" />
