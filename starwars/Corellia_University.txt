{{Quote|Hey. I didn't get to congratulate you on graduating yet. Corellia University is our home's best school. I almost went there.|Jaylen Barsha, to Sliro Barsha|<ref name="LRM">''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon]]''</ref>}}
'''Corellia University''' was a [[university]] located on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. [[Scientist]]s practiced their fields of study in the halls of the university,<ref name="Fully" /> and it was considered by some to be the best university on Corellia. During the [[Republic Era]], the [[Corellian]] [[Sliro Barsha]] was a student at Corellia University. Sliro's [[Barsha family|brother]], [[Jaylen Vrax|Jaylen Barsha]], almost attended the same university, as his higher education options were limited by his poor performance in school. However, his [[parent]]s [[Nnytyl Barsha|Nnytyl]] and [[Roisem Barsha]] made donations to the prestigous [[University of Bar'leth]] so that Jaylen could continue his education there instead.<ref name="LRM" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{LRMDate|1a|the mentions of Corellia University}}</ref> Sliro graduated from Corellia University, which Jaylen congratulated him on.<ref name="LRM" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corellia University was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']], a [[2018]] sourcebook for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="Fully" /> The [[Corellia University/Legends|university]] was originally introduced via mention in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[Shintel Downtime]]'', a roleplaying adventure part of ''[[The DarkStryder Campaign]]'', which was a [[1995]] supplement to ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="DarkStryder">{{DarkStryder|adventure=Shintel Downtime|book=The DarkStryder Campaign}}</ref> It later made its first appearance in ''[[Star Wars Galaxies]]'', a [[2005]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[Sony|Sony Online Entertainment]] and published by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="SWG">{{GalaxiesNGE}}</ref>
