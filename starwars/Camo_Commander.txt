The '''Camo Commander''' was a set of [[camo stormtrooper armor]] worn by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Loyalist Trooper|standard costume]]. This [[stormtrooper Commander]] [[armor]] sported a [[Color|green]] and brown camouflaged design, with small sections of the standard white [[stormtrooper armor]] underneath the paint still visible in certain places.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Camo Commander was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Sentinel, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 1.0.0 alongside Season 1 during the game's global launch on [[June 4]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Camo E-Web]]. This costume could be unlocked by reaching Tier 69 on the Season 1 Arena Pass' free track.<ref name="Hunters" />
