'''Atha Prime''', genetics master and ruler of the [[Dark Worlds|dark worlds]], was an arbiter of the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] and a co-conspirator of [[Grand Moff/Legends|Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin/Legends|Tarkin's]]. He was [[Exile (punishment)/Legends|exiled]] to the fringes of [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]] by [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]], although the [[Galactic Emperor/Legends|Emperor's]] [[Battle of Endor/Legends#Demise of the Sith|death]] in [[4 ABY/Legends|4 ABY]] allowed Prime to return to the galaxy and begin a campaign of conquest. With his [[Clone Warrior]]s at his side and the double-decker [[Star Destroyer/Legends|Star Destroyer]] [[Annihilator (starship)|''Annihilator'']] at his command, Prime waged war with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]], doing battle with its heroes, such as [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] and [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]].

==Biography==
===Egotistical mastermind===
Atha Prime, an egotistical<ref name="topt">{{GalaxyCite|2|Galactic Bazaar|Galactic Bazaar: Tales of Phantom Toys}}</ref> genetics master and ruler of the "dark worlds," was one of the architects of the Clone Wars,<ref name="apd" /> a [[The galaxy/Legends|pan-galactic]] conflict that raged from [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]] and ended with the collapse of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and the subsequent rise of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]].<ref name="nec">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''</ref> During those wars, as an affiliate to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Prime attacked the [[Mountain/Legends|mountainous]] [[Planet/Legends|world]] of [[Skye/Legends|Skye]], committing terrorist acts that were genetic in nature. He was defeated there by [[Jedi General/Legends|General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]], along with [[Commander/Legends|Commanders]] [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Halagad Ventor]].<ref name="aite" /><ref name="JC">{{JCFCite|url=posts/27839083|board=Literature|thread=Topic: Aliens of the Empire (Part 2 now up)|user=[[Abel G. Peña|Halagad_Ventor]]|date=2007-12-09|time=2:07 AM|archiveurl=https://archive.is/srYvN}}</ref> For a short while, he was involved with Grand Moff [[Trachta]], who would later move against Palpatine in an attempted [[Anti-Sith conspiracy|Anti-Sith coup]]<ref name="JC2">{{JCFCite|url=posts/26306274|board=Authors and Artists|thread=The Official Abel G. Pena Discussion Thread|user=Halagad_Ventor|date=2007-04-22|time=8:11 PM|archiveurl=https://archive.is/BPVB2}}</ref> with a force of cloned [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]].<ref name="Betrayal 1">[[Empire 1|''Empire'' 1]]</ref>

===Exile and return===
[[File:Concept-luke.jpg|thumb|left|Prime's adversary, Luke Skywalker.]]

Prime was exiled from the Empire and forced to reside in a remote fringe of the galaxy.<ref name="apd" /> The first [[Death/Legends|death]] of Emperor Palpatine in [[4 ABY/Legends|4 ABY]] at the [[Battle of Endor/Legends|Battle of Endor]]<ref name="nec" /> freed Prime from his exile and allowed him to begin his intended [[Blood/Legends|bloody]] conquest of the galaxy. He had been able to harness the power of [[Cloning/Legends|cloning]] technology, and with it, he created an army of [[Clone Warrior]]s that were his to command.<ref name="apca">{{WebCite|url=http://theswca.com/index.php?action=disp_item&item_id=47404|text=Atha Prime Concept Art|work=The Star Wars Collectors Archive|archivedate=20201024141258}}</ref>

With the Empire in disarray, Prime, along with his armies, began his campaign by securing control over the fragments of the Imperial forces. He then began to [[Slavery/Legends|enslave]] worlds that had recently been liberated by the Alliance to Restore the Republic and conquered worlds held by his foes along the galactic frontier. It was his intent to completely wipe out the Alliance and take control of the entire galaxy.<ref name="apd" /> As a result, Prime and his armies were forced to battle the Alliance, specifically the [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] Luke Skywalker and war hero Han Solo.<ref name="apca" /> Meanwhile, Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin, who had narrowly escaped death at the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]] in [[0 ABY/Legends|0 ABY]], emerged from hiding to take control of the shattered remains of the Galactic Empire.<ref name="topt" />

For transport during his campaign, Prime used his personal [[Starship/Legends|ship]], the [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighter]] ''[[Skyriver Invader|Apex Invader]]'',<ref name="apca" /> which was [[Pilot/Legends|piloted]] by the [[Droid/Legends|droid]] [[Blue-Tark/Legends|Blue-Four]]. Blue-Four also served as Prime's personal attendant.<ref name="bfd">{{WebCite|url=http://theswca.com/index.php?action=disp_item&item_id=50967|text=1985-86 Line Extension Presentation Binder Page 12 - Blue-Four Droid|work=The Star Wars Collectors Archive|archivedate=20201018071836}}</ref> Prime's [[Flagship/Legends|flagship]] for the campaign was the double-decker Star Destroyer ''Annihilator'', the [[Bridge (starship)/Legends|bridge]] complex of which the ''Apex Invader'' could connect to.<ref name="ahnca">{{WebCite|url=http://theswca.com/index.php?action=disp_item&item_id=48980|text=The Annihilator -- Concept Artwork|work=The Star Wars Collectors Database|archivedate=20201017033258}}</ref> While connected, Prime would have the ultimate vantage point in a battle, and he would be able to command both ships simultaneously. The ''Annihilator'' was roughly the size of a [[City/Legends|city]], and apart from being the flagship of Prime's navy, it served as a mobile command base.<ref name="topt" />

==Personality and traits==
An egotistical man,<ref name="topt" /> Atha Prime was also patient, biding his time in exile until the galaxy would be vulnerable. As a leader, he was able to rally the remainder of the Imperial forces in the wake of the Battle of Endor, and put them to effective use, enslaving worlds that had only recently experienced freedom from Palpatine's rule.<ref name="apd" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Creation===
{{Quote|A powerful force long kept in exile in a remote fringe of the galaxy has been released by the death of the Emperor. It moves now, like a plague, securing control over the shattered remnants of the empire and re-enslaving newly freed worlds. Atha Prime, genetics master, ruler of the dark worlds and architect of the Clone Wars, is free again. His advanced army of combat clones has already decimated rebel outposts along the galactic frontier. His goal is to crush forever the Rebel Alliance and control the Galaxy.|Original Kenner brief for "Atha Prime"|<ref name="apd" />}}
[[File:GuardPrimeSentinel.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Concept art for a [[Emperor's Royal Guard/Legends|royal guard]] shown next to Atha Prime and an [[Imperial Sentinel]]]]
In [[1984]], a year after the release of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] and the third ''[[Star Wars]]'' toy line, [[Kenner Products|Kenner]] decided to create a new line, dubbed "Power of the Force," in an attempt to renew interest in the brand. At first, most of the figures in the line were ones from ''Return of the Jedi'' that had not been produced in [[1983]]&ndash;84,<ref name="afa">''[[Star Wars: The Action Figure Archive]]''</ref> but eventually the company began to develop small vehicles that were of original design and had not appeared in the films. The original vehicles were a success, prompting Kenner to take their idea further by creating a set of characters that would populate a new storyline taking place after ''Return of the Jedi'', and that would be released in [[1986]].<ref name="topt" />

Hoping to push the ''Star Wars'' saga in a new direction, the Kenner designers created Atha Prime, who would serve as the main villain in the new plot line, which involved him battling redesigned versions of Luke Skywalker and Han Solo. In addition to Prime, they also designed the Clone Warriors, which would serve as Prime's soldiers and replacements to the stormtroopers featured heavily in the films. Prime was given two vehicles, which would be made into playsets, and a droid attendant. In particular, Prime would be used in conjunction with the ''Apex Invader'' toy.<ref name="topt" /> He was also identified as an architect of the Clone Wars,<ref name="apd" /> although in [[2002]], when [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] was released, it was shown that Palpatine and [[Dooku/Legends|Dooku]] were the true masterminds of that conflict.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>

For visual representation of Prime, the Kenner designers made scant adjustments to a piece of concept art from ''Return of the Jedi'' pre-production. The art, done in [[1981]] by [[Nilo Rodis-Jamero]], was originally meant to be a design for the [[Emperor's Royal Guard/Legends|Emperor's Royal Guard]], although the final version of that design differed significantly from Rodis-Jamero's work. In reusing that design, Kenner's adjustments included changing the guard's [[Axe/Legends|axe]]-like weapon into a [[Staff/Legends|staff]] and slight alterations to the figure's headpiece.<ref name="sobar">{{SWArchive|url=episode-i/bts/design/f20000313/index.html|text=Shades of Blue and Red}}</ref>

===Attempts at canonization===
====''Star Wars'' Legends====
[[File:ShadowSpawnDelayedANGRY.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Shadowspawn, the character Abel G. Peña attempted to consolidate with Atha Prime]]
Kenner's ideas were eventually rejected by [[Lucasfilm]], who were apparently not ready to undertake a concerted effort to create an [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]] set after ''Return of the Jedi''.<ref name="topt" /> In 1986 the last issue of [[Marvel Comics]]' [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'']] comic run, [[Star Wars (1977) 107|''Star Wars'' (1977) 107]] would be published, which would turn out to be the start of a hiatus on post-''Return of the Jedi'' content, until [[1991]] when [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Heir to the Empire]]'' was released by [[Bantam Spectra]]. That year, [[Tom Veitch]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Dark Empire]]'' comic mini-series would introduce the [[Imperial Sentinel]]s, which bore great resemblance to Prime.<ref name="afa" /> They were in fact based on the same ''Return of the Jedi'' concept art that Prime had been modeled on.<ref name="sobar" />

The character of Prime was all but forgotten, until [[The Official Star Wars Fact File|''Official Star Wars Fact File'']] author [[Abel G. Peña]] wrote him into the backstory of the ''coup'' attempt featured in the [[Dark Horse Comics]] [[comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Empire: Betrayal]]''. The reference, however, which was to go in the article published in issue [[The Official Star Wars Fact File 122|122]] of the ''Fact File'', was cut from the released product.<ref name="JC2" /> Peña was not deterred and made another, more elaborate connection in an article on Imperial [[Warlord/Legends|Warlords]] that he wrote with [[Daniel Wallace]]. This time, Peña consolidated the character of Prime and the established character of [[Cronal|Shadowspawn]] from the ''[[Dark Empire Sourcebook]]'', making them one and the same. But the subsequent cancellation of ''[[Polyhedron]]'' magazine, which the article had been written for, left it unpublished and the connection non-[[canon]].<ref name="JC2" /> For three years, Peña attempted to resell the article, to no avail. He was eventually able to canonize at least an element of Prime's character with the [[2007]] ''[[Aliens in the Empire]]'' article, co-written by [[Rich Handley]]. In it, there is a reference to a "[[Zeta Magnus|genetics terrorist]]" that Peña intended to be Prime.<ref name="JC" />

A blurb of the novel ''[[Luke Skywalker and the Shadows of Mindor]]'', by [[Matthew Stover]], published by [[StarWars.com]] on [[May 22]], [[2008]], contained information pertaining to Shadowspawn that matched specific elements of Kenner's brief for Atha Prime, particularly the phrase, "The Emperor's death has paved the way for Shadowspawn's return from exile in the Outer Rim&hellip;"<ref name="sw.com">{{SWArchive|url=eu/lit/novel/news20080522.html|text=Book Previews: Obi-Wan & Luke Covered}}</ref> This led to speculation on the [[Jedi Council Forums]] as to whether or not Stover had retained Peña's unpublished backstory of Shadowspawn that consolidated him with Prime<ref name="JC3">{{JCFCite|url=posts/28496792|board=Literature|thread=Luke Skywalker and the Shadows of Mindor|user=TalonCard|PTtime=5/24/08 1:56am [and subsequent posts]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/DCwy4}}</ref> although Peña stated to not expect that as only he and [[Daniel Wallace]] had seen his Shadowspawn backstory.<ref name="JC2" /> Upon its release, however, the book was revealed to have no mention of Prime whatsoever.<ref name="LSatSoM">''[[Luke Skywalker and the Shadows of Mindor]]''</ref>

In March of [[2015]], the "unnamed genetics terrorist" was detailed in ''[[SkyeWalkers: A Clone Wars Story]]'', a novella written by Peña, and given the name "[[Zeta Magnus]]."<ref name="SW:ACWS">''[[SkyeWalkers: A Clone Wars Story]]''</ref>
