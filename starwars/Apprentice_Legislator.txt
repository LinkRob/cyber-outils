'''Apprentice Legislators''' were the members of the [[Apprentice Legislature]], an organization for [[Politics|politically]]-minded youth.<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan" /> The position existed in the governments of both the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and its successors state, the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan" /> [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Naberrie]] joined the Apprentice Legislators on [[Naboo]] at the age of eight. Amidala's daughter,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of [[Alderaan]], later served as an Apprentice Legislator during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the Empire alongside [[Amilyn Holdo]] of [[Gatalenta]].<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan" />
