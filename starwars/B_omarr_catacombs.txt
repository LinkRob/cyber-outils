{{Quote|Zaril's brain languished in the B'omarr catacombs, forgotten and alone&hellip;catacombs that eventually fell into the slimy hands of ''Jabba the Hutt''.|[[Thom Hudd]]|<ref name="Return 4" />}}
The '''B'omarr catacombs''' were a series of tombs located under what would become the [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] that housed the removed [[brain]]s of the [[monk]]s of the [[B'omarr Order]]. After [[Raid on the Jedi vault|raiding]] the [[Jedi vault]] on [[Alaris Prime]], the [[Defel]] [[thief]] [[Zaril]] had his brain entombed within the catacombs on [[Tatooine]], hoping to evade the [[Jedi]]'s pursuit. [[Standard century|centuries]] later, Jabba's cousin [[Crakka]] and her [[beastmaster]] [[Dagra Van]] attempted to steal Zaril's brain and locate his treasure, but the two were thwarted by Jabba.<ref name="Return 4" />
