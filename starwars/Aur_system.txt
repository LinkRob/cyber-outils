The '''Aur system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It fell within grid square J-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Aur system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

The [[Aur system/Legends|Aur system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was originally meant to be mentioned in the [[2012]] source book ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'' by Fry and [[Paul R. Urquhart]], however the sidebar that would have mentioned the system was cut before the book's release. The information regarding the system was then released in [[2013]] as part of ''[[Star Wars: The Essential Guide to Warfare Author's Cut]]'', a series of articles published on the StarWars.com [[Star Wars Blog|Blog]] by Fry and [[Del Rey]] editor [[Erich Schoeneweiss]] containing various cut content from ''The Essential Guide to Warfare''.<ref name="EGtW:AC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-guide-to-warfare-authors-cut-the-celestials|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Guide to Warfare'' Author's Cut &mdash; The Celestials|int=Star Wars: The Essential Guide to Warfare Author's Cut}}</ref>
