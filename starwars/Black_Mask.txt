The '''Black Mask''' was a [[mercenary]] gang<ref name="Dawn" /> of [[bounty hunter]]s<ref name="Darth Vader 33">{{DarthVaderCite|33|The Dark Side}}</ref> that was active during the [[Imperial Era]] consisting of [[Vect Nokru]], [[Vidara Jurness]], and [[Ayo Bons]].<ref name="Dawn" /> The Black Mask was tasked by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Inquisitor]] [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister]] to [[Ambush on Alderaan|kidnap]] the [[Princess]] of [[Alderaan]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] in order to lure the fugitive [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] out of hiding.<ref name="Part I" />

After bringing Organa<ref name="Part I" /> from the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Ultimate SW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> Alderaan to the planet [[Daiyu]], the Black Mask succeeded in capturing Kenobi, however, the [[Jedi]] and Organa managed to escape after distracting the mercenaries with a [[spice]].<ref name="Part II">{{Kenobi|Part II}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Black Mask, albeit unidentified, first appeared in "[[Part I]]," the first episode of the [[Disney+]] live-action series ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]''.<ref name="Part I" /> The episode premiered on [[May 26]], [[2022]].<ref name="Part I Release">{{InsiderCite|214|Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi Companion|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi'' Companion}}</ref> The name Black Mask was later given in the [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'',<ref name="Dawn" /> within a section written by [[Emily Shkoukani]].<ref name="DawnAuthor">{{WookYouTube|-nqSqVOvfM4|Wookieepedia Interviews - Emily Shkoukani & Jason Fry}}</ref>
