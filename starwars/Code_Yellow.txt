A "'''Code Yellow'''" was a [[starship]] distress signal used by the [[New Republic]] as of 4 or 5 ABY.<ref name="squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>

==History==
In around 4 or 5 ABY, the captured ''Imperial II''-Class Star Destroyer ''Victorum'' sent out a Code Yellow after its [[hyperdrive]] failed, leaving it stranded behind [[Galactic Empire|enemy]] lines. This was received by the ''[[Temperance]]'', who deployed [[Vanguard Squadron]] to assist.<ref name="squadrons" />
