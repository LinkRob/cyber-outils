'''Alto Industries''' was a company that produced the [[Variable Combat Kit]], a model of [[all-terrain leg]]s design as a [[starship]] attachment. The kit was available during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="SM" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alto Industries was mentioned in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Special Modifications]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="SM" />
