'''Dixian''', also known as '''Low Ghor''', was a writing system for the [[Ghor (language)|Ghor]] [[language]], spoken by the [[Ghorman (people)|Ghormans]] on the [[Ghorman|planet]] with the same name. Apart from Dixian, Ghor could also be written in [[Ghorelle]], also known as High Ghor.<ref name="Trivia" />

{{Language-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dixian was created for the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the [[Disney+]] televison series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'',<ref name="Trivia" /> first appearing in the episode "[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]", released on [[April 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref> It was named after graphic designer [[Lauren Dix]].<ref name="Trivia" /> In the episode, a collar<ref name="EverBeen" /> on the animatronic creature Elvis features its namesake in Dixian.<ref name="DixianTweet">{{Twitter|starwars|status/1930308573671624737|Star Wars|date=2025-06-05|archiveurl=https://archive.is/tIznQ}}</ref>
