'''Bahryn meteorites''' were [[meteorite]]s often found on [[Geonosis]]'s [[moon]] [[Bahryn]]. Unlike most other meteorites, they emitted [[Color|yellow]] light and a small but considerable amount of heat. [[Garazeb Orrelios]] and [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] found one such rock when they were stranded on the frigid moon. Because of the previous failure of their sole [[Power generator|heat lamp]], the meteorite saved their lives. Kallus later kept it so that he could remember the experience.<ref name="THO">{{Rebels|The Honorable Ones}}</ref>
