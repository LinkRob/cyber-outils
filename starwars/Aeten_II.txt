'''Aeten II''',<ref name="TIE Manual" /> also simply known as '''Aeten''',<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> was a [[Volcano|volcanically]] turbulent [[planet]]<ref name="TIE Manual" /> located in [[Wild Space]] in grid square J-6 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> A terrestrial planet, Aeten II held the second [[orbit]]al position in the [[Aeten system]].<ref name="TIE Manual" /> It was known for its rare [[stygium crystal]]s, a type of [[crystal]] that could power a [[starship]]'s [[Cloaking device|stealth systems]].<ref name="TIE Manual" />

During the [[Republic Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], [[scientist]]s discovered the existence of stygium crystals on Aeten II, which proved previously theoretical designs for a cloaking device as feasible.<ref name="TIE Manual" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Aeten II was first mentioned as "Aeten" in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]].<ref name="TFABG" /> Its entire name was later given in the reference book ''[[TIE Fighter Owners' Workshop Manual]]'', written by [[Ryder Windham]] and released in [[2019]].<ref name="TIE Manual" /> While ''The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game places Aeten II in Wild Space, ''TIE Fighter Owners' Workshop Manual'' and ''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]'' states its location as the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="CVNE">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref><ref name="TIE Manual" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Aeten II/Legends|Aeten II]] was first mentioned in [[Star Wars: Episode I Incredible Cross-Sections|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Incredible Cross-Sections'']], a reference book written by [[David West Reynolds]] and published in [[1999]].<ref name="Episode I Incredible Cross-Sections">''[[Star Wars: Episode I Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
