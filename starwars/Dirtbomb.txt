'''Dirtbombs''' were a type of [[bomb]] filled with with [[metal]] shavings and soil native to [[Meddan]]&mdash;which contained [[mineral]]s effective in scrambling [[sensor array]]s&mdash;packed within shells. During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Zeltron]] criminal [[Samu Mavolak]] used a dirtbomb against the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] in an attempt to flee away.<ref name="Boba Fett BWR 2" />
