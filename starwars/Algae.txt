'''Algae''' grew on a number of [[planet]]s, including an airborne variety on [[Bespin]],<ref name="Ultimate SW" /> and in the waters of [[Ahch-To]],<ref name="Last Jedi Junior" /> [[Sibensko]],<ref name="Bloodline" /> and [[Toydaria]]. Toydaria was home to large mats of algae on its surface.<ref name="TVE" /> The [[gangster]] "Big" [[Sturg Ganna]] had a suite with fountains containing [[flower]]ing [[Lily|lilies]] and [[Bioluminescence|bioluminescent]] algae which cast a bluish glow.<ref name="nothing">{{StoryCite|story=Hear Nothing, See Nothing, Say Nothing|book=Canto Bight (novella collection)}}</ref>

Algae farms were used to cultivate algae produce. When a piece of a projectile from the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] hit the surface of [[Hetzal Prime]], several thousands of square kilometers of algae farms were destroyed.<ref name="LotJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>
