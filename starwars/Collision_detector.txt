A '''collision detector''', or '''collision alarm''',<ref name="Tipping Point">{{TBB|Tipping Point}}</ref> was a safety device built into a [[starship]] or vehicle to alert the [[Pilot|operator]] of an imminent impact.<ref name="SWZ" /> They were banned from being incorporated into [[Podracer]]s in many race series. Frowned upon by veteran racers, they were often times referred to derisively as "electronic nannies."<ref name="EV" />
