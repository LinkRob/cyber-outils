'''''Bounty Hunters'' 1''' is the first issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. It was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]], and published on [[March 11]], [[2020]] by [[Marvel Comics]].

The issue features the story "'''Galaxy's Deadliest Part 1: Ghosts of Corellia'''," in which [[Nakano Lash]], the treacherous mentor of [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]], resurfaces.

==Publisher's summary==
''VALANCE'S FIRST APPEARANCES, ALSO AVAILABLE- NEVER BETRAY A BOUNTY HUNTER &ndash; ESPECIALLY IF IT'S BOBA FETT! Years ago, VALANCE and fellow bounty hunters BOSSK and BOBA FETT took on a mission that went sideways in a bad way after Valance's mentor, NAKANO LASH, violently betrayed them. Valance's team barely escaped with their lives. He never thought he'd face his old mentor ever again-until Lash finally resurfaces under mysterious circumstances. Every bounty hunter in the galaxy wants a piece and Valance is hell-bent on getting to the prize first. He has score to settle-but so does Boba Fett! ETHAN SACKS (''OLD MAN HAWKEYE'' and ''GALAXY'S EDGE'') and PAOLO VILLANELLI (''VADER: DARK VISIONS'' and ''JEDI FALLEN ORDER - DARK TEMPLE'') are teaming up to bring you the bounty hunter adventure you've been waiting for this March!''

Skilled and ruthless bounty hunters&mdash;some of the most dangerous killers in the galaxy&mdash;scour systems to track down wanted criminals for the highest price. Three of these hunters&mdash;Valance, Bossk and Boba Fett&mdash;are considered some of the best. And they have a history with each other&hellip;one that's about to put them on a deadly collision course.}}
