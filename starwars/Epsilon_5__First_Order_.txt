'''[[Epsilon 5 (Callsign)|Epsilon 5]]''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] of the [[First Order]] who [[pilot]]ed a [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]. Epsilon 5 participated in an [[Ottegan Assault|attack]] on the [[Ottegan]]-populated [[Planet|world]] of [[Arthon]], where the [[starfighter]] was one of two that escorted [[Kylo Ren's command shuttle]]. During the battle, a [[missile]] from an Ottegan [[siege engine]] that was directed at Ren's [[Upsilon-class command shuttle|shuttle]] struck Epsilon 5 after the [[command shuttle]] evaded the shot. The Epsilon 5 [[trooper]] called out through a comm channel from his [[TIE series|TIE fighter]] as it crashed, annoying [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] as it marked the eighth recorded First Order casualty of the battle.<ref name="LEGO TFA" />
