The "'''Five Fire Rings of Fornax'''" were the subject of a phrase spoken by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Colvett Valeria]] when she discovered [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi]] and [[Zander Freemaker]] attempting to depart from the ''[[Home One]]'' with the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']].<ref name="Escape from Coruscant" /> It shared its name with the astronomical location [[Fornax]].<ref name="Force Awakens">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Five Fire Rings of Fornax were first mentioned in "[[Escape from Coruscant]]," a [[2017]] episode of the animated television series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]''.<ref name="Escape from Coruscant" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Fire Rings of Fornax]] were first mentioned in the [[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|novelization]] of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="New Hope">[[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|''Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope'' novelization]]</ref>
