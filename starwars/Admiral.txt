'''Admiral''' was a senior command [[Military rank|rank]] in many [[Navy|navies]], including those of the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="TCW Film" /> the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="ANH" /> the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> the [[Chiss Ascendancy]],<ref name="Thrawn" /> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]],<ref name="ROTJ" /> the [[First Order]],<ref name="TFAVD" /> the [[New Republic]],<ref name="Aftermath" /> and the [[Resistance]].<ref name="TFA" />

There were different grades for Admiral, denoting seniority and position in a fleet. In the Galactic Empire, the ranks were as followed (from highest rank to lowest): [[Grand Admiral]], [[Fleet Admiral]],<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> Admiral, [[Vice Admiral]], and [[Rear Admiral]].<ref name="Tark">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> Below the different admiral ranks was [[Commodore]].<ref name="Thrawn" /> The [[Assignment|position]] of [[sector admiral]] also existed.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>
