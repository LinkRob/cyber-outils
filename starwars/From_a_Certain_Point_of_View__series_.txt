'''''From a Certain Point of View''''' is a [[canon]] series of short-story anthologies. Each anthology in the series celebrates the 40th anniversary of one of the films of the [[original trilogy]].<ref name="Announcement" /><ref name="TESB Announcement">{{SW|url=news/empire-at-40-from-a-certain-point-of-view-the-empire-strikes-back-cover|text=''Empire'' at 40 &#124; Check Out the Cover of ''From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back''}}</ref>
