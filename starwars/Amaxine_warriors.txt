The '''Amaxine warriors''' were supposedly a warrior people that dated back to the [[Republic Era|dawn]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Instead of using [[currency]], the Amaxine warriors traded weapons for goods and services. In approximately [[1022 BBY]], the Amaxine warriors defected from their government and disappeared from the known galaxy; it was believed that at some point the warriors left [[the galaxy]], in search of [[Galaxy|another]]. Long after the Amaxines' disappearance, a [[Amaxine warriors (New Republic Era)|paramilitary organization]] active during the [[New Republic Era]] named themselves after the ancient warriors.

==Description==
[[File:AmaxineSpaceStation-TheRiseOfKyloRen1.jpg|thumb|left|250px|An Amaxine space station.]]
Amaxine technology was sophisticated and capable of surviving centuries after first being built. Their [[space station]]s had ports for their uniquely shaped [[starship]]s, and the stations were also equipped with a mechanism for dispensing [[hyperspace]] capable pods. The pods could be launched great distances across the galaxy, and allowed for precise reconnaissance or sabotage of a target. This, in fact, earned the Amaxine scouts a reputation of appearing almost out of thin air and signaling their troops to attack. One such space station was located around an unknown star that, by the time of the [[High Republic Era|High Republic]], was close to dying, though the station itself was intact. However, its pod system was sabotaged by Reath Silas to prevent the [[Drengir]] and the [[Nihil]] from accessing it.<ref name="ITD" />

==History==
===Origins===
According to an old story told by Leia Organa's adoptive [[Parent|mother]] [[Breha Organa]], the Amaxine warriors were purportedly a warrior people whose entire culture was based in warfare. Instead of currency, they traded weapons for goods and services.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> Around [[2500 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the Amaxine warriors encountered the [[Drengir]]. They built a relay point for their hyperspace pods to land on an unknown world occupied by the sentient race and intended to make war on them. However, the Drengir fought back and were able to defeat and then devour them.<ref name="ITD" /> Around [[1022 BBY]], the Amaxine warriors defected from their organized government and vanished from [[the galaxy]] at large.<ref name="Timelines" /> According to legend, their reason for doing so was a refusal to make peace with the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]]. Unable to defeat such a powerful foe, they departed the known galaxy for another one to search for another war to fight.<ref name="Bloodline" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|The tale has it that they refused to make peace with the Old Republic but they knew they could never defeat such an enemy. So instead they pointed their ships at the galaxy beyond ours and left forever.|Leia Organa|<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>}}
During the [[High Republic Era]], an [[Amaxine Space Station|Amaxine space station]] located in a [[Amaxine Station system|star system]] centered by a [[Unidentified star (Amaxine station)|dying star]] was used by the [[Byne Guild]] as a waypoint for its [[Indentured servitude|indentured]] [[pilot]]s. The station was later used as a shelter by multiple [[starship]]s waylaid by the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]].<ref name="ITD" />

During the [[New Republic Era]], a [[Amaxine warriors (New Republic Era)|paramilitary organization]] commanded by [[Arliz Hadrassian]] and allied with the secretive [[First Order]] named itself after the Amaxines.<ref name="Bloodline" />

==Behind the scenes==
