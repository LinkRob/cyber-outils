'''Cehrazad''' was a [[humanoid]] [[bounty hunter]] commando affiliated with the [[Haxion Brood]] crime syndicate. Cehrazad was one of the bounty hunters sent after [[Cal Kestis]]. They used an [[A280C blaster rifle|A280C blaster Rifle]], [[flash grenade]]s, a retractable [[Shield (physical armor)|shield]], and [[Repulsorlift|repulsors]] in their gauntlets in combat and wore a yellow cybernetic arm, a gray shirt, a yellow chest plate, a [[kama]], and a yellow and gray helmet.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
Cehrazad fought Cal in 14 BBY but was killed by the Jedi.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
