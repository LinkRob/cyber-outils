'''Barab ingots''' were a type of [[kyber crystal]] found on the [[Radiation|irradiated]] [[planet]] [[Barab I]]. The crystals were found in clusters in the planet's [[mountain]]s and were hot to the touch. They also glowed and, when used in a [[lightsaber]], created a powerful, fiery blade and made the entire lightsaber slightly radioactive.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
