'''''Darth Vader and the Cry of Shadows'' 2''' is the second issue of five in the ''[[Star Wars: Darth Vader and the Cry of Shadows]]'' comic mini-series written by [[Tim Siedell]]. It was released on [[January 15]], [[2014]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Clone trooper Hock has been through much since being left for dead by his Jedi leaders in the Clone Wars. Now, Hock believes he has found a truly great warrior who deserves loyalty and commands respect&mdash;Darth Vader! If Hock can impress the Dark Lord as a stormtrooper, he will have earned respect of his own from the leader he admires most.''
