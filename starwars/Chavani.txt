[[Doctor]] '''Chavani''' was a [[Gender|female]] therapist who in the<ref name="Aftermath" /> [[Standard year|year]] [[5 ABY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> treated the former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] prisoner [[Brentin Lore Wexley]] after he was freed from [[Ashmead's Lock]] on the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]] by the [[New Republic]] during the [[Liberation of Kashyyyk]]. Chavani spoke to Wexley's son [[Temmin Wexley|Temmin]] and reassured that it would be normal for his [[Parent|father]] to regularly go on long walks, as a lot had likely built up in Brentin Wexley's mind during the years he was imprisoned.<ref name="Aftermath" />
