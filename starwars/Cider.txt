'''Cider''' was a type of beverage found in [[the galaxy]]. On [[Corellia]], [[Jenra]] had fragrant, ice-cold [[fruit cider]] available in her lounge, and the young [[human]] [[Qi'ra]] drank half of the pitcher without pause.<ref name="Most Wanted" /> In [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]], a seasonal hot beverage called [[Spiced Surabat Cider]] was available from [[restaurant]]s such as [[Ronto Roasters]] with and without [[alcohol]].<ref name="Ronto Roasters">{{GEAttr|Ronto Roasters}}</ref> [[Spice Runner Hard Cider]], a foamy and warming local cider,<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> was available on tap at [[Oga's Cantina]].<ref name="Oga's Cantina">{{GEAttr|Oga's Cantina}}</ref> The dark [[Color|red]] drink was named after [[spicerunner]]s who [[Smuggler|smuggled]] [[spice]] throughout Batuu's region in the galaxy.<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>
