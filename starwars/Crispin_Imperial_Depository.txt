The '''Crispin Imperial Depository''' was a depository located in the [[Iridium Mountains]] on the [[planet]] [[Vandor]] that received shipments of cargo, such as the valuable [[Fuel|hyperfuel]] [[coaxium]]. It was deliberately built in a location that was difficult to reach and lacked any airfield or [[hangar]] to receive cargo drops, and instead, incoming shipments were mounted on [[20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport|20-T Railcrawler conveyex]] [[transport]]. The depository and its incoming shipments were defended by [[11-3K viper probe droid]]s and a team of [[range trooper]]s led by [[Captain]] [[Denwade Banevans]]<ref name="TOG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Crispin Imperial Depository first appeared in the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> and was first identified as the Imperial Depository in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TOG" /> It was later identified as the Crispin Imperial Depository in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Expanded Edition]]'', the expanded novel version of the movie written by [[Mur Lafferty]].<ref name="Expanded">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>
