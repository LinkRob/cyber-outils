'''Brink''' was a [[human]] [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] for the [[New Republic]] who flew a [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wing]] against the [[Kenner Loring's faction|forces]] of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Grand General]] [[Kenner Loring]] over the [[planet]] [[Malastare]] in [[5 ABY]]. Despite the efforts of his squadmate [[Urtis]], Brink was corned and [[Death|killed]] by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|&laquo;Coming up behind you, Brink&mdash;hold on&hellip; One down&mdash;wait, no! ''Brink!''&raquo;''<br />"''Dammit&hellip;he's gone.|quotestart=0|Urtis and Darna|<ref name="Republic Under Siege 1" />}}
Brink was a [[human]] [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] in the [[New Republic Defense Fleet Starfighter Corps|Starfighter Corps]] of the [[New Republic]] during its [[Galactic Civil War|war]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Republic Under Siege 1" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> he flew a [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]] against [[Kenner Loring's faction|Imperial forces]] serving [[Grand General]] [[Kenner Loring]] in<ref name="Republic Under Siege 1" /> the [[Malastare system]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> over Loring's base of operations on<ref name="Republic Under Siege 1" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Malastare]]. Near an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]], Brink's [[starfighter]] was swarmed by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s and he called out to his squadmates [[Darna (pilot)|Darna]] and [[Urtis]] over [[Comlink|comms]] for aid.<ref name="Republic Under Siege 1" />

[[File:BrinkDeath-BattleOfJakkuRepublicUnderSiege1.png|thumb|right|300px|Brink was killed by the Imperial forces of Grand General Kenner Loring over Malastare.]]
Urtis responded from their [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter]], first in [[Galactic Basic Standard|Basic]] and then in [[Sullustese]], and positioned their [[starship]] behind the [[TIE series|TIEs]], but Brink was not reassured and, as Urtis shot down one TIE, another scored a hit on Brink's [[engine]] that caused his Y-wing to exploded, [[Death|killing]] him. Darna cursed his death, but Urtis reminded her there was no [[time]] for mourning and continued the fight, although both [[pilot]]s were also killed by the Imperials soon after.<ref name="Republic Under Siege 1" />

==Personality and traits==
Brink was a dark-skinned human.<ref name="Republic Under Siege 1" />

==Equipment==
At the time of his death, Brink wore an [[Color|orange]] [[flight suit]], with a gray [[harness]] and a white [[flight helmet]] with black markings.<ref name="Republic Under Siege 1" />
