The '''E-B load lifter''' was a model of industrial [[loader droid|loader]] [[droid]] used to lift, unload and move heavy cargo during the [[Imperial Era]]. 

==History==
In [[9 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{KenobiDate|III|E-B loader droids being in use on Mapuzo}}</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]] used E-B loader droids in their [[mining]] operations on the [[planet]] [[Mapuzo]]. When [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] smuggled themselves to Mapuzo from [[Daiyu]] aboard [[Transport eight]], E-B loader droids were stationed at the landing pad for unloading the cargo from the [[TR-286 transport]].<ref name="Kenobi III" />

One E-B loader droid on Mapuzo named [[NED-B]] worked with the [[Imperial officer]] [[Tala Durith]] as an agent of the underground movement known as the [[Hidden Path]]. They helped [[Smuggler|smuggle]] surviving [[Jedi]] and other [[Force-sensitive]] individuals to safety. He kept watch over a safehouse hidden within the droid maintenance building of a [[Mapuzo mining village]].<ref name="Kenobi III" />
