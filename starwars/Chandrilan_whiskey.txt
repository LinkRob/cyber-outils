'''Chandrilan whiskey''' was a type of [[whiskey]] that was expensive during the [[High Republic Era]]. [[Axel Greylark]]&mdash;the [[Greylark family|son]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Kyong Greylark]]&mdash;was given a bottle of the beverage by [[Lady]] [[Lu-reen Faraday]], the [[Chandrilan]] [[Gender|heiress]] of the [[Faraday Spirits]] company, from a rare batch of the drink that had only three hundred bottles left due to a shipping accident. Axel later enjoyed the Chandrilan whiskey in a [[Axel Greylark's flask|flask]] while in the [[gambling]] den [[Raik's Parlor]] on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].

==Description==
Chandrilan whiskey was a type of smoky [[whiskey]]<ref name="Convergence" /> that was potable by [[human]]s<ref name="Human" /> and partially shared its name with [[Chandrilan]]s, residents of the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Chandrila]].<ref name="Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref> The beverage featured a burning effect when [[Diet|consumed]], which some individuals found pleasant.<ref name="Convergence" />

==History==
{{Quote|Coruscant's little prince don't trust no one to pour him a drink, isn't that right?''"<br />"''Raik, darling, I mean no offense. But this was a very good year.|Raik and Axel Greylark, as the latter sipped his Chandrilan whiskey|<ref name="Convergence" />}}
[[File:Axel-Greylark-headshot.png|thumb|right|230px|Axel Greylark drank Chandrilan whiskey from his flask in Raik's Parlor.]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], a shipping accident resulted in a batch of Chandrilan whiskey becoming rare in [[the galaxy]]. With only three hundred bottles left in existence, the whiskey became worth one thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] a bottle. At some point, [[Axel Greylark]]&mdash;the [[Greylark family|son]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Kyong Greylark]]&mdash;became romantically involved with [[Lady]] [[Lu-reen Faraday]], the Chandrilan [[Gender|heiress]] of her [[Faraday family|family's]] company, [[Faraday Spirits]]. Faraday gifted Axel an expensive bottle of the whiskey when the pair were visiting each other.<ref name="Convergence" />

However, he ended his relationship with the heiress not long afterward<ref name="Convergence" /> in [[382 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> and visited the [[gambling]] den [[Raik's Parlor]] on<ref name="Convergence" /> the Core Worlds planet<ref name="Cookbook" /> [[Coruscant]] to avoid the [[HoloNet]] tabloids' reporting on his love life. While in Raik's Parlor, Axel enjoyed his gifted Chandrilan whiskey, which he kept in his [[Axel Greylark's flask|flask]]. He played roulette for several [[Standard hour|hours]] into the [[Local day|night]] alongside the establishment's other patrons, occasionally pulling his [[flask]] from a compartment in his [[droid]], [[QN-1]], to take a swig of the beverage.<ref name="Convergence" />

After finishing a round of roulette, a [[Unidentified Mirialan woman|Mirialan woman]] teased him for drinking fine Chandrilan whiskey from his flask as they watched the rest of Raik's Parlor's patrons file out of the room. At the prompting of the establishment's owner&mdash;the [[Utai]] [[Raik]]&mdash;Axel indicated that his whiskey was of a rare variety, thinking about how he had acquired the beverage from Faraday. However, he internally reflected that he was wary of any drink served to him being [[poison]]ed, instead choosing to enjoy his own libations. As the [[Mirialan]] and Raik began discussing Faraday, he hastily put away his flask of the drink.<ref name="Convergence" />
