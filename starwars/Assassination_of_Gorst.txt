In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="WAFEDate">{{AndorDate|206}}</ref> the [[Axis network]] dispatched [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] and [[Bix Caleen]] to '''assassinate''' [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[doctor]] [[Gorst]], whom they had learned was having his [[Torture|interrogation]] methods extended into a full program by [[Imperial Intelligence]], convincing the Emperor he was too valuable to be left as a one-man [[Imperial Security Bureau]] asset.

With Gorst himself on Coruscant for the work, the [[Imperial Navy]] donated a facility on Coruscant to the ISB for the training, and Major [[Lio Partagaz]] assigned the work on the program to [[ISB Supervisor]]s [[Avril Heert]] and [[Lonni Jung]], who was, unknown to all, an undercover agent for the [[Axis network]]. The network subsequently dispatched Caleen and Andor—who were living undercover on Corsucant—to take out Gorst. Gorst entered the interrogation room and was surprised by Bix, who strapped him to the same torture device she was strapped on two years prior. Not long after, Cassian destroyed his office with explosives.<ref name="WAFE" />

==Prelude==
By [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|112}}</ref> the [[Human]] Imperial Security Bureau doctor Gorst had developed a torturous interrogation method of using the [[Death|dying]] screams of [[Massacre of the Dizonites|massacred]] [[Dizonite]]s, which induced severe emotional distress on the victim.<ref name="Nobody">{{Andor|Nobody's Listening!}}</ref> That year, Gorst used the method on the [[Ferrixian]] [[Bix Caleen]] to draw information on her friend, Cassian Andor, from her, inducing lasting trauma in the process. Gorst savored in the torture and would appear in Bix's nightmares over the next two [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="Date" />

[[File:HeertJungGorstOversight-WaFE.png|thumb|left|300px|Partagaz assigned Heert and Jung to oversee the Gorst project.]]
In 3 BBY,<ref name="WAFEDate" /> the ISB's cousin organization, [[Imperial Intelligence]], convinced the Emperor that Gorst's interrogation methods were too valuable to be a single asset, and moved to expand it into a full program. With facilities donated by the Imperial Navy, Gorst himself was present on the Imperial capital [[Planet|world]] of [[Coruscant]] for the work. Knowledge reached ISB [[major]] [[Lio Partagaz]], who assigned the project to [[ISB Supervisor]]s [[Avril Heert]] and [[Lonni Jung]], who was, unknown to all, an undercover agent for the [[Axis network]].<ref name="WAFE" />

The Axis network subsequently dispatched Caleen and Andor-who were living in an [[Axis network safehouse|Axis safehouse]] on Coruscant after [[Ferrix Riot|fleeing]] [[Ferrix]] and [[Skirmish on Mina-Rau|later]] [[Mina-Rau]]-to assassinate Gorst.<ref name="WAFE" />

==The assassination==
On one of the nights of [[Senate Investiture]] Week, the two operatives carried out the operation. A cloaked Bix entered the building Gorst had established his office and torture device in, hiding in the shadows of the room and waiting for Gorst's arrival. On his way back to his office after getting himself a sweet treat, Gorst entered the interrogation room and was surprised by Bix, who strapped him to the same torture device she was strapped on two years prior. Gorst recognized Bix, but was unsure from where, and began struggling in fear as Bix strapped him to the torture chair and fitted the device around his head.<ref name="WAFE" />

[[File:GorstGoesBoom-WaFE.png|thumb|300px|right|Cassian and Bix walk away as Gorst's office explodes.]]
After activating the machine, she told Gorst that the torture would only feel like forever and left the office as the Dizonite screams filled Gorst's ears, causing him to scream and thrash about violently in his restraints. On the way out of the building, Bix was confronted by an [[Unidentified Imperial security guard|Imperial security guard]], who informed her nobody was allowed in the building, but was shot by Bix only a moment later. Outside the building, Bix met up with Cassian, who remotely detonated explosives in Gorst's office, putting him out of his misery and destroying his work as they walked away from the scene.<ref name="WAFE" />
