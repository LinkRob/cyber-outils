The '''double-bladed spinning lightsaber''' was wielded by the [[Inquisitor]] known as the [[Fourth Sister]]. It was similar in appearance to the [[double-bladed spinning lightsaber]]s wielded by other [[Inquisitorius|Imperial Inquisitors]]. She utilized the [[lightsaber]] during her mission to [[Search for Princess Leia|capture]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], on [[Daiyu]]. The lightsaber was abandoned in a cave.

When [[Lina Graf]], a [[commander]] within the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], infiltrated [[Fortress Vader]] on [[Mustafar]] wearing the Fourth Sister's stolen [[armor]], the attendant [[Vaneé]] eventually realized that Graf was, indeed, not the Inquisitor because she lacked her lightsaber.

==Description==
The [[lightsaber]] was a [[double-bladed spinning lightsaber]] owned by the [[Fourth Sister]], a [[Nogratu]] [[Inquisitor|member]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s<ref name="WayOut" /> [[Jedi hunter|Jedi-hunting]]<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Inquisitorius]]. The [[Sword|saber]] possessed a cylindrical [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]] with an emitter ring, forming its [[Hilt|disc-hilt]]. The ring featured a notch with a dull spike in the middle near the ring's [[blade emitter]]s. The notches were opposite to each other.<ref name="WayOut" /> The [[Weapon|lightsaber]] contained two [[kyber crystal]]s<ref name="SecretsJedi" /> that emitted [[Color|red]] blades.<ref name="WayOut" /> The Fourth [[Sister (Inquisitorius)|Sister]] carried the saber on the upper back of<ref name="Realization" /> her<ref name="SWE" /> [[armor]] in both crescent<ref name="Realization" /> and disk mode.<ref name="PartII">{{Kenobi|Part II}}</ref>

==History==
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="19BBY">The events of "[[Devoted]]" take place during and immediately after [[Operation: Knightfall]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]].</ref> the Fourth Sister carried the lightsaber on her back in crescent mode within a partially constructed [[Fortress Inquisitorius]],<ref name="Devoted">{{TOTE|Devoted}}</ref> the headquarters of the Inquisitors.<ref name="SWE" /> There, she walked through through a hallway into the fortress's [[Fortress Inquisitorius audience chamber|audience chamber]] after being welcomed in by [[the Grand Inquisitor]],<ref name="Devoted" /> the leader of the Inquisitors.<ref name="SWE" /> Inside the audience chamber, along with fellow Inquisitors the [[Marrok|First Brother]], [[Eleventh Brother]], and [[Barriss Offee]], she met<ref name="Devoted" /> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]<ref name="SWE" /> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the Inquisitors' new Master.<ref name="Devoted" />

[[File:Realization-Preview.png|thumb|left|275px|Barriss Offee and the Fourth Sister travel to a settlement with their lightsabers on their backs.]]
Sometime between 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Post19">The events of "[[Realization]]" take place after "[[Devoted]]," which takes place during and immediately after [[Operation: Knightfall]], but before [[the Grand Inquisitor]]'s [[death]]. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates Operation: Knightfall to [[19 BBY]] and the [[Grand Inquisitor]]'s death to [[4 BBY]], therefore the events of "Realization" must occur between 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and 4 BBY.</ref> the Fourth Sister and Offee [[Mission to a rocky planet|traveled]] aboard a [[Phi-class shuttle|''Phi''-class shuttle]],<ref name="Realization" /> the ''[[Scythe_(Phi-class)|Scythe]]'', to a rocky [[Unnamed planet|planet]]<ref name="RealizationAD">{{TOTE|Realization}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> to investigate reports of a [[Jedi]]. After landing [[shuttle]] outside a [[Unidentified settlement (rocky planet)|settlement]], the Fourth Sister departed the ''Scythe'' with the weapon on her back, in crescent mode. She journeyed to the settlement along with Offee, and questioned the villagers in a market there on the whereabouts of the Jedi, while Offee spoke with a young [[Unidentified human girl (rocky planet)|child]] to the side. The [[Unidentified human governor|governor]] promised the Fourth Sister that a Jedi was never present in the settlement, while the young child told Offee the Jedi was hiding in the [[mountain]]s.<ref name="Realization" />

When Offee informed the Fourth Sister of the Jedi's location, the Nogratu [[Telekinesis|moved]] containers with [[the Force]] to block the market's exit. The Fourth Sister then removed her lightsaber from her back while confronting the governor for lying to her. After<ref name="Realization" /> he<ref name="RealizationAD" /> retorted that people should be judged by their actions, the Fourth Sister ignited a single blade of her lightsaber, causing the nearby villagers to scream. She swung her blade at the governor,<ref name="Realization" /> [[Death|killing]] a [[Gender|boy]] who stepped in the way.<ref name="RealizationAD" /> She switched her hilt to disk mode<ref name="Realization" /> and ignited the second blade as she slaughtered the villagers,<ref name="RealizationAD" /> occasionally utilizing the spinning function of her [[double-bladed lightsaber]]. During the chaos, the Nogratu impaled a blade through a wall before returning to the center of the market to finish the massacre.<ref name="Realization" />
