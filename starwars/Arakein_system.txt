The '''Arakein system''' was located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]] in grid square N-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> It was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Religion|religious]] group known as the [[Arakein Monks]].<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Arakein [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]<ref name="Systems" />&mdash;both works being written by [[Jason Fry]] and published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
