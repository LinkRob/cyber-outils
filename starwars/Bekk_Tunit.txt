'''Bekk Tunit''' was a famous [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] who crashed at some point prior to the [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Star Wars: Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}} to [[32 BBY]]. [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] depicts Bekk Tunit's [[glove]]s as being present on Watto's trophy shelf during the events of the film.</ref> One of Tunit's [[glove]]s was recovered from the crash site and by 32 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] it had come into the possession of the [[Toydarian]] [[junk dealer]] [[Watto]], who kept it on the trophy shelf of [[podracing]] memorabilia within his [[Watto's junkshop|shop]] in the [[city]] of [[Mos Espa]] on the planet [[Tatooine]].<ref name="CL 2016" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bekk Tunit was first mentioned in [[canon]] in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']].<ref name="CL 2016" /> The mention of Tunit in the book is a reprint of information from Tunit's first mention in the [[2000]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', which was written by [[Kristin Lund]].<ref name="Inside Ep1">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref>
