The '''Dasklo family's private fleet''' was the personal private and family [[fleet]] of the [[Dasklo family]], one of the [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. As [[Patriarch]] of the Dasklo family, [[Dasklo'irv'ursimi]] commanded the fleet, which consisted of a [[Frigate|Dasklo family frigate]] and at least one [[Destroyer|Dasklo destroyer]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
