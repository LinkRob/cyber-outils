'''CT-3278''' was a [[Clone trooper|clone]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[stormtrooper]] in the [[Imperial Army]]. Several months after the conclusion of the [[Clone Wars]], CT-3278 was stationed on the planet of [[Serenno]] as part of [[Wilco's company|company]] under the command of [[Clone Captain|Captain]] [[Wilco]].

==Biography==
While serving on the [[planet]] [[Serenno]], CT-3278 and two other [[Clone trooper|clone stormtroopers]] were sent to the location of a crashed [[Class four container transport|cargo container]] that had been hijacked by the rogue clones of [[Clone Force 99]]. Using [[BARC speeder]]s, the three [[stormtrooper]]s arrived at the container's location and set up a small perimeter. While searching the crash site, they eventually spotted [[Echo]] inside the container and opened fire, but the latter managed to close the container's door, trapping himself and rogue clone [[Omega]] inside.

In the meantime, CT-3278 reported his findings and requested backup while one of his comrades set up a mounted cannon. After a while, another rogue clone of Clone Force 99, [[Tech]], arrived and stunned one of the stormtroopers. CT-3278 and the other remaining stormtrooper proceeded to pin down Tech behind a [[tree]] with heavy blaster fire. CT-3278 ordered his subordinate to hold fire as he approached the tree, but was quickly disarmed by Tech. Engaging in hand-to-hand combat, CT-3278 threw Tech to the ground and attempted to wrestle away control of the commando's [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|DC-17 hand blaster]], but was ultimately overwhelmed by Tech. After shooting down the [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|gunship]] that CT-3278 had called in, Clone Force 99 would thereafter escape in their ship the [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|''Maraude''r]].<ref name="Ruins" />

==Personality and traits==
As a [[Cloning|clone]] of [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]], CT-3278 stood 1.83 [[meter]]s tall, had [[Color|black]] [[hair]], brown [[eye]]s, and tan skin.<ref name="Clone troopers" />

==Equipment==
As a clone stormtrooper, CT-3278 wore standard-issue [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] that was devoid of any color or markings.<ref name="Ruins" />
