The '''Cauldron Nebula''' was a [[nebula]] in the [[Corva sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square P-3 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Cauldron Nebula was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Cauldron Nebula/Legends|Cauldron Nebula]] first appeared in ''[[Jedi Search]]'', a [[1994]] novel written by [[Kevin J. Anderson]] as the first installment of ''[[Star Wars: The Jedi Academy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Search">''[[Jedi Search]]''</ref>
