'''Ando''' was a [[water]]-covered [[planet]] in the [[Ando system]] of the [[Lambda sector]] in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]], located in grid square Q-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Amphibian|amphibious]] [[Aqualish]] [[species]]. At the [[time]] of the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo|Invasion]] of [[Naboo]], it was a member of the [[Galactic Republic]] and was represented in the [[Galactic Senate]] by [[Senator]] [[Po Nudo]]. During the [[Separatist Crisis]], turmoil occurred on Ando that left [[the galaxy]] staggered and it seceded from the Republic, joining the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], which promised it the support of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] and [[Techno Union]]. A type of [[wine]] known as [[Andoan wine]] was produced on Ando. It was the homeworld of Senator Po Nudo, the weapons manufacturer [[Amda Wabo]], and the [[smuggler]] [[Ponda Baba]].

==Description==
Ando was a terrestrial [[planet]] covered in [[water]],<ref name="Force Arena" /> with some [[swamp]] regions.<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" /> Five percent of the planet was comprised of islands often fought over by the [[Aquala]], the [[Quara]], and the [[Ualaq]] [[subspecies]] of the [[Aqualish]].<ref name="Helmet 2">{{HelmetCollectionCite|2|Databank A-Z}}</ref>  It was situated in the [[Ando system]]<ref name="RotS" /> of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim's]]<ref name="TFABG" /> [[Lambda sector]],<ref name="RotS" /> in [[Standard Galactic Grid]] square Q-15.<ref name="TFABG" />

==History==
During the [[Jedi-Sith War]], the [[Sith]] constructed a [[Ando Sith temple|temple]] on Ando which became a stronghold of the [[dark side of the Force]] during the conflict.<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />

By [[32 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> during the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]] of the planet [[Naboo]], Ando was a member of the [[Galactic Republic]], represented in the [[Galactic Senate]] by [[Senator]] [[Po Nudo]].<ref name="Po Nudo" /> Later, during the [[Separatist Crisis]], [[the galaxy]] was left staggered by turmoil occurring on Ando,<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> and the planet seceded from the Republic to join the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Propaganda" /> which promised it the support of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] and [[Techno Union]].<ref name="New Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

Ando was represented in the [[Separatist Senate|Separatist Parliament]] via the [[Ando Commission]].<ref name="AndoComm">{{SWU|set=Secrets of Power|cardname=Ando Commission|link=2642304891}}</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and Confederacy,<ref name="SWGA" /> the terrorist organization [[Death Wind]], led by the former Republic [[commander]] [[Mekedrix]], established a base in the ruins of the Sith temple on Ando. [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] were [[Mission to Ando (Mekedrix)|sent]] to Ando to deal with the group.<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" /> During the war, the [[Hyper-Communications Cartel]] had transmission facilities on the planet. By the time of the [[Outer Rim Sieges]], [[IG-227 Hailfire-class droid tank|IG-227 ''Hailfire''-class droid tanks]] were deployed on Ando and used to guard these facilities.<ref name="OSWM 121">{{GermanFanCite|121|Database}}</ref>

[[Andoan wine]] was produced on Ando.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

==Inhabitants==
Ando was the [[homeworld]] of the Aqualish, a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Amphibian|amphibious]] [[species]].<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> Aqualish from Ando included Po Nudo,<ref name="Po Nudo" /> weapons manufacturer [[Amda Wabo]],<ref name="Head to Head">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Head to Head]]''</ref> and [[smuggler]] [[Ponda Baba]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Ando was first [[canon]]ically mentioned in ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Head to Head]]'', a [[2014]] young readers book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Head to Head" /> It was originally created for ''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]'', a [[1989]] roleplaying supplement by [[West End Games]].<ref name="Galaxy Guide 4">''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]''</ref>
