During the early [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Great Jedi Purge]] survivor [[Padawan]] [[Ferren Barr]] recruited several individuals who became his '''disciples'''. During the [[invasion of Mon Cala]], the majority of the disciples were hunted down and [[Death|destroyed]] by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and members of the [[Inquisitorius]].

==History==
===Recruiting===
{{Quote|You ''will'' find justice for your sister's murder, Nipaltoo. I swear it on the force.''"<br />"''But ''how'', Master Barr?''"<br />"''Follow me.|Ferren Barr recruits Nipaltoo|<ref name="Dark Lord 16" />}}
[[File:YouNeedATeacher-DV17.png|thumb|left|250px|Ferren Barr promised to train Verla]]
During the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Jedi]] were all but wiped out and deemed as enemies of the new government. The former Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Ferren Barr]], managed to survived and was found at a [[cantina]] sometime after. There, he recruited six individuals, whom loathed the new Empire. Each of them had their reasons for joining him against the Empire: [[Nipaltoo]]'s sister, [[Palabee]], was among the many Jedi they had murdered; the Empire had also bulldozed [[Orvek]]'s home and force him to live elsewhere; [[Rebb]] and [[Stell]] had turned against the Empire following the occupation of their city; [[Daren]]'s life had been saved by a Jedi once and was willing to help Barr in any way he could;<ref name="Dark Lord 16" /> and [[Verla]] had been promised by Barr to be trained in her special force abilities.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" /> However, not all of the disciples intentionally joined Barr. Nipaltoo and Orvek ended up falling for Barr's Jedi [[mind trick]]s which fueled their lust to fight against the Empire.<ref name="Dark Lord 16" /> As well as the six disciples, Barr also possessed an [[R1-series astromech droid]] named [[Endee]] in which he installed all of his knowledge about how the Jedi was so easily destroyed.<ref name="Dark Lord 14" />

===Moving to Mon Cala===
During the first [[Standard year|year]] into the Empire's [[Imperial Era|reign]] Barr took his disciples and Endee to [[Mon Cala]], there he intended to have the [[Mon Calamari monarchy]] turn against the Empire and openly fight them. He began by influencing [[King]] [[Lee-Char]]'s trade negotiations with the Imperial [[ambassador]] [[Telvar]], having him dismiss Telvar's every offer as an excuse for the Empire to bankrupt Mon Cala. This caught the attention of the [[Emperor]], [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], who recognized the negotiating tactics as that of a Jedi's format. Thus he sent his [[Sith apprentice]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to investigate this.<ref name="Dark Lord 13">[[Darth Vader (2017) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 13]]</ref> Vader took with him: three Imperial [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]], the [[Sixth Brother]], [[Ninth Sister]] and [[Tenth Brother]]; and a death [[squad]] of fourteen [[Purge Trooper]]s, elite [[Cloning|clones]] whom finished their training after the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Dark Lord 16" />

[[File:BarrsFiles-2017DarthVader14.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Ferren Barr shows his disciples his files on Anakin Skywalker]]
When Vader arrived, the king's [[Chief of Security]] [[Gial Ackbar]] denied that any enemy of the Empire could be on Mon Cala.<ref name="Dark Lord 13" /> However, Barr had been planning the [[assassin]]ation of Telvar prior to this, fearing that the negotiations would benefit Mon Cala instead of causing conflict.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" /> The assassination subsequently took place as Vader was talking to Gial Ackbar.<ref name="Dark Lord 13" /> Vader immediately made haste for Lee-Char's [[Dac City palace|palace]] in [[Dac City]] and Telvar's superiors came to [[Invasion of Mon Cala|invade]] Mon Cala. As the first wave of fighting commenced, Lee-Char notified Barr of this and when he mentioned Vader's arrival, Barr called Vader by his former name, Anakin Skywalker. Barr approached his disciples about the arrival of him and explained to them how Skywalker became Vader and helped destroy the Jedi order he once fought with.<ref name="Dark Lord 14" />
