'''Boku jinken droids''' were a type of [[droid]] that could be used as defense systems against opponents. During the [[lockdown of Olcatz|lockdown]] of the [[planet]] [[Olcatz]], the [[Anzellan]]<ref name="Payback">{{Visions|The Duel: Payback}}</ref> [[Yakuza]] leader<ref name="ProductionBrief" /> [[Que-Dama]] and his crew employed [[B1-Bokujin]] models as traps along [[Tkoku Bridge]] to defend [[Tkoku Island]]. When the [[Crusaders (The Grand Master)|Crusaders]] attempted to cross the bridge, the droids unleashed punches that knocked down some of the Crusaders.<ref name="Payback" />

==Behind the scenes==
Boku jinken droids first appeared in "[[The Duel: Payback]]",<ref name="Payback" /> a non-[[canon]] short film directed by [[Takanobu Mizuno]] released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Kamikaze Douga]] and [[ANIMA (company)|ANIMA]], was released on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="ProductionBrief" /> The droid series was identified by name in Aurebesh in the film.<ref name="Payback" />
