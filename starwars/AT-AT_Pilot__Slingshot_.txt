The '''AT-AT Pilot''' was a costume owned by [[Dizzy (Ugnaught)|Dizzy]], the [[Ugnaught]] member of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]], specifically added within the 3.0.0 update alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It resembled the uniforms worn by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[AT-AT pilot]]s, with varying shades of [[Color|gray]] and white for the majority of the color scheme, as well as black elements and red [[Imperial crest|Imperial cogs]] on the [[helmet]]. The outfit was shrunk down to accommodate Dizzy's size, and the helmet was open underneath the [[goggles]], exposing the Ugnaught's face, though it still included a [[Unidentified life support pack|life support pack]] secured to the chest and attached to the helmet via tubes.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
AT-AT Pilot was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 3.0.0 update alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[AT-AT Racer]].<ref name="Hunters" />
