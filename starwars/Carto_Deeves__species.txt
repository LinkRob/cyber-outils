This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]]. [[Carto Deeves]] was a member of this species.<ref name="Mando14" />

==Biology and appearance==
This [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] was distinguished by their sunken [[eye]]s, with [[Color|light]] speckled skin, as well as prominent brows and other protruding features.<ref name="Mando14" />

==Species in the galaxy==
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate}}</ref> a member of this species was serving a thirty-five [[Standard year|year]] sentence in [[Wobani Penitentiary]] for the assault of a [[New Republic]] [[officer]]<ref name="Mando14" />

==Behind the scenes==
This species first appeared in the [[Chapter 14: The Tragedy|sixth episode]] of the [[The Mandalorian Season Two|second season]] of the television series, [[Star Wars: The Mandalorian|''The Mandalorian]],'' directed by [[Robert Rodriguez]].<ref name="Mando14" />
