The '''Chroman system''' was located in the [[D'Aelgoth sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western Reaches" /> Situated in grid square K-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it was connected by a [[Karfeddion–Kriselist hyperlane|hyperspace route]] to<ref name="Galaxy" /> the [[planet]]s<ref name="Aphra 23">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 23|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 23]]</ref><ref name="TFAVG">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Tiferep Major]]<ref name="Galaxy" /> and [[Ogem]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> It was the location of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Chroman Labor Pits]], labor facilities that were owned by the [[Galactic Empire]]. There, the Imperial [[Captain]] [[Fatulaine]] began to secretly free [[prisoner]]s after working there for just three [[Standard day|days]].<ref name="Uprising" />
