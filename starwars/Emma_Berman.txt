'''Emma Berman''' is an actress who voices [[Nash Durango]] in the children's animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''. Berman was first announced as the voice behind Durango on the ''[[Star Wars]]'' Twitter account on [[September 10]], [[2022]].<ref name="Twitter" />
