"'''"You're Hired" to be Darth Vader's Secret Apprentice!'''" is an interview of [[W. Haden Blackman]] and [[Sam Witwer]] conducted by [[Jonathan Wilkins]] and [[Brian J. Robb]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 97|''Star Wars Insider'' 97]]. Blackman and Witwer discuss the production process of ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]''.
