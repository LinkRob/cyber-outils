The '''Elien system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It was located in grid square J-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Elienise field carrot]]|system=Elien system}}</ref> the [[Elienise field carrot]].<ref name="Temptation">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> By [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|TOTF}}</ref> roasted Elienise field [[carrot]]s had become the favorite [[Foodstuff|treat]] of [[Plinka]], a [[Grand Theljian snow dog]] owned by the [[Graf family]] scion [[Xylan Graf]].<ref name="Temptation" />
