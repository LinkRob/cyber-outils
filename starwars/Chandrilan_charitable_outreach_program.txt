The '''Chandrilan charitable outreach program''' was a charitable foundation created by the [[Imperial Senate|Imperial Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]], who represented the [[planet]] [[Chandrila]],<ref name="Announcement" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[5 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> While the program appeared to be one of the [[Senator]]'s usual well-intended efforts, it was created as a front for Mothma to distribute [[Galactic Credit Standard|funds]] that were supporting the growing [[Axis network|rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Announcement" />

==History==
===Charity begins at home===
{{Quote|I can't pull funds the way I used to. They're watching me now.|Mon Mothma, to Luthen Rael|<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:Aldhani-AltThumb.jpg|thumb|right|260px|Mon Mothma secretly funded a rebel network.]]
During the [[Imperial Era]], as resistance to the [[Galactic Empire]] continued to grow, [[Imperial Senate|Imperial Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] of the [[planet]] [[Chandrila]] partnered with the rebel leader [[Luthen Rael]] and began to support his [[Axis network|secret network]] of rebel operatives. As Mothma continued her duties as a [[senator]] and Rael ran his [[Galactic Antiquities and Objects of Interest|antiquities shop]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], the [[Chandrilan]] used [[Galactic Credit Standard|money]] from her [[Mothma family|family's]] considerable wealth to fund rebel activity.<ref name="Aldhani" />

In [[5 BBY]], as Imperial regulations continued to impact Mothma's ability to manage the family wealth, Mothma met with Luthen Rael to discuss the growing impediments to her activities. Visiting the shop, Mothma alluded to her ideas of a charity foundation, saying that she intended to bring someone into the circle to help support their actions. Although Rael objected, saying that they could not afford to make their secret operations more vulnerable, Mothma reminded him that she was the one with the most at risk.<ref name="Aldhani" />

[[File:TayKolma.png|thumb|left|240px|Mothma recruited Kolma to help her continue to fund rebellion against the Empire.]]
After attending an event with her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Perrin Fertha]], Mothma was asked by her partner about why she had not told him about the new foundation, to which Mothma replied that she did not think that Fertha would be interested in it due to its charitable nature.<ref name="Axe" /> Not long after, Mothma approached her childhood friend and [[banker]] [[Tay Kolma]] at a dinner party that she was hosting at the [[Chandrilan Embassy]]. Mothma, unsure about how to broach the subject of her clandestine and rebellious pursuits, asked Kolma if she could ask for a political favor. However, when Kolma suggested she not ask him and hinted that his opinions were likely to be rather extreme due to his growing discontent with the Empire, Mothma was grateful to learn Kolma's stance on Imperial rule.<ref name="Announcement" />

She then indirectly revealed that, although the general public and the many Imperial [[Spy|spies]] deployed to watch her regarded her as merely an annoying do-gooder, she was secretly raising money for her own means behind the scenes. Mothma explained that she was asking Kolma to use his banking position within the [[Bank of Kolma]] to help her access her funds in spite of the Empire's interreference. Through the Chandrilan charitable outreach program, which Kolma would become the [[chairman]] of, the banker would be required to continually visit [[Coruscant]]. Although Kolma requested more clarity as to what he was getting into, Mothma continued her veiled statements and insisted that he help her.<ref name="Announcement" /> Kolma would agree to help.<ref name="Narkina 5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref>

===Mothma's money moves===
{{Quote|If we plan on making any further donations, we need to be sure that the work you've done so far isn't gonna come back to haunt you.|Tay Kolma, to Mon Mothma|<ref name="Narkina 5" />}}
[[File:Narkina5-AndorS1E8.png|thumb|right|260px|Mon Mothma and Tay Kolma became collaborators for the charity, a front for their funding of the rebellion.]]
Following the discussion, Kolma began making more frequent trips to Coruscant and also staying in the [[ecumenopolis]] at times. When Mothma hosted an event at the embassy for politicians with the goal of garnering support for her latest initiative, Kolma approached her with unfortunate news. As Mothma hinted at upcoming monetary needs, Kolma said that the Empire's new banking regulations, which included staffing up Imperial auditors, were complicating his ability to move money. Mothma suggested immediate action before the auditors were placed in a potentially compromising position, but Kolma warned that such a plan had its own risks. As Mothma's daughter [[Leida Mothma|Leida]] approached and noted that Kolma had been around more often, they ceased their discussion.<ref name="Narkina 5" />

Later, Kolma began to become concerned about a withdrawal of 400,000 credits that appeared on the ledger but had vanished. Attempting to find a solution that would paper over such an expense, he contacted [[Davo Sculdun]], a Chandrilan<ref name="Listen">{{Andor|Nobody's Listening!}}</ref> oligarch<ref name="Episode Highlights">{{SW|url=news/andor-episode-9-nobodys-listening-highlights|text=''Andor'' Analyzed: 5 Highlights from Episode 9, "Nobody's Listening!"}}</ref> with treasury relationships and massive business expenses that could help solve the issue. Telling Kolma told him that Mothma had some concerns about the recent tax laws and financial inflexibility,  Sculdun offered to meet Mothma on Coruscant.<ref name="Listen" />

Kolma met with Mothma to discuss further donations to the charity, Kolma informed Mothma of the discrepancy, saying that a deposit into the account would help smooth things over. Without the funds to do so, Mothma asked Kolma what could be done about it, and Kolma proposed his plan to her, knowing that Mothma did not respect Sculdun. Although she scoffed at the notion, calling Sculdun a thug, Kolma said that they needed to find a solution before the account was scanned. With no available alternatives,<ref name="Listen" /> Mothma agreed to meet with Sculdun.<ref name="One Way Out">{{Andor|One Way Out}}</ref>

===Crippling compromise===
Hosting Sculdun at the embassy, with Kolma in attendance as well, Mothma expressed that she exploring financial situations for her foundation. Sculdun replied that his wealth meant that he could smooth out any transactional issues without outside observation. Elaborating on the Empire's cumbersome restrictions, Sculdun told Mothma that she should feel no shame in wanting to access her family's wealth. Reminding him that it was a charitable foundation they were establishing, Mothma inquired as to what Sculdun's fee would be, and the oligarch replied that he would like to return to Coruscant with his son, [[Stekan Sculdun|Stekan]], and introduce him to Leida. Referring to the customary Chandrilan wedding traditions, Sculdun stated that he wanted the two young people to meet. Mothma, who did not support the old ways, asked if it was his only offer, and when Sculdun confirmed, Mothma immediately declined and told Kolma to escort him out. Sculdun replied that it was a lot to think about, and although the senator replied that she was not thinking about it, she shuddered at the prospect of it.<ref name="One Way Out" />

Unable to find another way, Mothma decided to agree to Sculdun's terms, despite her disgust with the old ways. Leida, who had a certain fervor for the traditional ways despite her mother's opinions, met Stekan<ref name="Rix Road">{{Andor|Rix Road (episode)}}</ref> and eventually became engaged to him. Meanwhile, Sculdun held up his end of the bargain and allowed money to flow through the charity. One year after their meeting, in [[4 BBY]], the [[wedding of Stekan Sculdun and Leida Mothma]] was held at the [[Mothma Estate]] on Chandrila.<ref name="OYL">{{Andor|One Year Later}}</ref>

===Faltering leadership===
Although Kolma had initially been happy to assist with the foundation and its true purpose, by the time of the [[Marriage|wedding]], he had grown displeased with the way that increasing rebel activity in [[the galaxy]] was affecting his own wealth. During the three-day long proceedings, Kolma confronted Mothma about his soured investments and growing discontent. Mothma promised to work on allaying Kolma's concerns after the wedding, scheduling a meeting together on Coruscant for the following week. After their conversation, Mothma told Rael, who was in attendance due to helping Sculdun supply a rare [[Chandi Merle]] artifact as a wedding present, about the new developments. Rael was extremely concerned by the news, and although Mothma insisted that she would find a way to solve the issue,<ref name="Sagrona">{{Andor|Sagrona Teema}}</ref> Rael set his own plans into motion.<ref name="Harvest">{{Andor|Harvest}}</ref>

On the last day of the wedding, Kolma decided to leave the event early, but not before making one more comment about the situation, hinting that Sculdun could be of help to him. Mothma expressed her frustration about Kolma's betrayal to Rael, but continued to assert that she would find a way to make him cooperate. Rael gravely stated that they could not afford to be in debt to him and that she needed to be protected. Meanwhile, Rael's operative [[Cinta Kaz]], replacing [[Otto (Chandrila)|Kolma's driver]],<ref name="Harvest" /> would assassinate him.<ref name="Welcome">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref>

By [[2 BBY]], Mothma's need for secret methods of supporting the rebellion vanished when she publicly denounced [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] in front of the [[Imperial Senate]] and, [[Escape from the Senate|fleeing]] Coruscant, joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] forces on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]].<ref name="Welcome" />
