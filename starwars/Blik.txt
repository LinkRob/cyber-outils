'''Blik''' was a [[Duros]] [[youngling]].<ref name="TOTU 104" /> At some point between c. [[62 BBY]] and [[33 BBY]],<ref>{{TalesDate|U4}}</ref> it<ref name="TOTU 104 AD" /> was strolling the streets of a [[Unidentified city (Cad Bane)|bustling city]] with its [[Blik's mother|mother]] and another [[Blik's parent|parent]] when<ref name="TOTU 104" /> he<ref name="TOTU 104 AD" /> came across the Duros<ref name="TOTU 104" /> [[Gender|boys]]<ref name="TOTU 104 AD" /> [[Cad Bane|Colby]] and [[Niro]]. Seeing as one of them banged on a roadside machine and the other penetrated a trashcan, Blik asked what they were doing, only for its parents to come around and scotch the conversation. Blik's [[Parent|mother]] instructed it to get away from the younglings because he had been told to eschew street [[rat]]s. Soon, the family moved on, leaving Colby and Niro behind. While his parents led him away, Blik turned back to take one last look at his peers, receiving a rushing pat on its shoulder from its mother as it walked.<ref name="TOTU 104" />

A [[Color|red]]-[[eye]]d Duros youngling, Blik had light-blue skin with a dark-blue patch atop its [[hair]]less head. It sported a dark-olive, high-collared [[Clothing|top]] beneath a sleeveless, beige-and-brown shirt with a crisscross pattern on the chest area. In addition, it wore a pair of brown pants, its backside partially covered by a roughly oval pad protruding from the top.<ref name="TOTU 104" />
