'''Draige''' was a<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> [[human]]<ref name="USW" /> [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] who served as second-in-command of [[Squadron Four (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Four]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[New Republic]]'s [[Barma Battle Group]] launched an [[Battle of Pandem Nai|attack]] on the 204th at the [[planet]] [[Pandem Nai]]. Members of<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> Draige's [[squadron]]<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> pursued the [[New Republic pilot]] and Imperial defector [[Yrica Quell]] during the battle,<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> in which Draige [[Death|perished]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> Having taken many losses, the [[fighter wing]] fled the [[Pandem Nai system]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> After taking [[Commanding officer|command]] of the 204th, [[Major]] [[Soran Keize]] suggested replacing the late [[pilot]] with another named [[Arron]], but ultimately chose to transfer and promote the pilot [[Palal Seedia]] to fill Draige's role as Arron had also died by that [[time]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
Draige was mentioned in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the second volume of the ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy which was written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
