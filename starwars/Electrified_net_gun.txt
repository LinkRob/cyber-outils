An '''electrified net gun''',<ref name="Padawan Lost" /> also known as an '''electronet launcher'''<ref name="Friends" /> or '''energy net gun''',<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> was a type of [[net gun]] that could fire a paralyzing, electrically charged [[stun net]] in the form of five [[Color|yellow]] energy balls, between which the webbing of the net formed and expanded as it approached its target. During the [[Second Battle of Felucia]] during the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Trandoshan]] [[Lo-Taren]] utilized an electrified net gun to capture the [[Jedi]] [[padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], who was active in the battle on the behalf of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Tano was knocked unconscious by the weapon and was subsequently kidnapped by the Trandoshan.<ref name="Padawan Lost" />
