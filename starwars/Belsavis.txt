'''Belsavis''' was an astronomical object located in the [[Bozhnee sector]]'s [[Belsavis system]] within an intersecting portion of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western Reaches" /> Situated at the coordinates L-18 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it was connected via [[hyperspace route]] to the astronomical object [[Karfeddion]]<ref name="Galaxy" /> and was located near the [[Thull's Shroud]], a location where the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] group operated during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Galactic Empire]] had a [[Prison|penal]] site at Belsavis.<ref name="Narkina 5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="5 BBY">{{AndorDate|108}}</ref> the Empire sent a group of newly convicted [[prisoner]]s on the<ref name="Narkina 5" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|niamos|Niamos}}</ref> [[Niamos]], including the [[human]] [[Tuck Hybecks]], to Belsavis to where they would carry out their sentences.<ref name="Narkina 5" />

==Behind the scenes==
Belsavis was introduced in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[Galaxy|galactic]] [[Star map|map]] included in the article "[[Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?|Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?]]" by [[Mark Newbold]], published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[January 29]], [[2016]]. It's name was obscured in that article,<ref name="TFA">{{SW|url=news/where-in-the-galaxy-are-the-worlds-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?|int=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?}}</ref> but Belsavis's complete name was introduced in a map included in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]],<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> an [[August]] 2016<ref name="What Will You Do?">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2016/7/26/what-will-you-do|text=What Will You Do?}}</ref> [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' boxed set.<ref name="TFABG" /> Originating in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Belsavis/Legends|Belsavis]] was introduced in the [[1995]] novel [[Children of the Jedi (novel)|''Children of the Jedi'']], written by [[Barbara Hambly]].<ref name="Children of the Jedi">[[Children of the Jedi (novel)|''Children of the Jedi'']]</ref>
