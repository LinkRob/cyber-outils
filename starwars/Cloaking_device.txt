A '''cloaking device''', also known as a '''stealth device''', was a type of technology that could render a [[starship]] invisible.

==Description==
{{Quote|And you're certain the ship's cloaking device is active? I don't want to be hurtling out of hyperspace unprotected into the path of a dozen Separatist cruisers....|Anakin Skywalker to Asajj Ventress, on the ''Banshee''|<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Darth_Maul_Sith_Infiltrator_XW2.png|thumb|left|250px|Darth Maul engages the ''Scimitar''{{'s}} cloaking device over Tatooine]]
A cloaking device rendered an object completely invisible to nearly all forms of detection,<ref name="Stealth Ship">{{Databank|stealth-ship|Stealth Ship}}</ref> by disrupting the electronic signature its various systems and sensors normally emitted.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> The object, however, could still be tracked via its [[magnetic signature]].<ref name="CaM">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref> A form of experimental [[starship]] technology,<ref name="Christophsis">{{Databank|christophsis|Christophsis}}</ref> cloaking devices allowed ships to bypass enemy vessels and fleets undetected,<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> rendering them essentially invisible to ordinary scanners. However, an [[interdiction field]] rendered stealth technology inoperable, and vulnerable to detection. Cloaking devices could be powered by either [[hibridium]] or [[stygium crystal]]s.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>

==History==
===Late Republic Era===
A practical cloaking device had been a dream of [[Chiss]] [[Chiss Defense Force|Defense Force]] [[scientist]]s for generations.<ref name="TA:CR">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref>

[[File:Scimitar_cloaking_SoD4.png|thumb|right|250px|The ''Scimitar'' cloaking]]
[[Maul|Darth Maul's]] ''[[Scimitar]]'' had a cloaking device, which he used to launch a sneak attack on [[Unidentified pirate faction|pirates]] assaulting [[Trade Federation]] forces over [[Kellux]]. Taken by surprise, the [[pirate]]s were destroyed by [[proton torpedo]]es and [[mine]]s deployed by the ''Scimitar''.<ref name="Darth Maul I" />

Prior to the [[Clone Wars]], [[Admiral]] [[Trench]] engaged [[cruiser]]s equipped with cloaking devices. However, he was able to use [[tracking torpedo]]es to hit them whether they were cloaked or not.<ref name="CaM" />

===The Clone Wars===
{{Quote|Jam their communications and cloak this ship from view... they won't know we've arrived until it's much too late.|[[Darth]] Sidious to General Grievous, on the ''Scimitar''|<ref name="SoD4">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 4|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 4]]</ref>}}
[[File:StealthShip-ChristophsisMoon.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The cloaked stealth ship]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[engineer]]s<ref name="Admiral Trench">{{Databank|admiral-trench|Admiral Trench}}</ref> utilized experimental [[starship]] technology<ref name="Christophsis" /> to create the [[IPV-2C Stealth Corvette|stealth ship]], a heavily-armed vessel fitted with a cloaking device.<ref name="Stealth Ship" /> [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] used the craft to [[Battle of Christophsis|deliver]] supplies to [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa's]] camp on [[Christophsis]]. However, Skywalker also used it to attack Trench's command ship, the ''[[Invincible]]''. Despite the failure of his first attack, Skywalker used Trench's [[tracking torpedo]]es against him, leading the missiles back to the ''Invincible'', destroying the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], severely injuring Trench, and briefly breaking the blockade.<ref name="CaM" />

Later in the war, the [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] was given a [[Xanadu Blood|modified]] [[Rogue-class Porax-38 starfighter|''Rogue''-class Porax-38 starfighter]], the ''[[Xanadu Blood]],'' with a cloaking device, as part of his payment from his [[Sith Lord]] client, [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="HH">{{TCW|Holocron Heist}}</ref> [[Asajj Ventress]] used the cloaking device aboard the [[Lancer-class pursuit craft|''Lancer''-class pursuit craft]] [[Banshee (starship)|''Banshee'']] to deliver [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] aboard a [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] to [[Rescue of Quinlan Vos|rescue]] [[Quinlan Vos]].<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> Sidious himself utilized the cloaking device aboard the ''Scimitar'', Darth [[Maul]]'s former vessel, to reach [[Dathomir]] undetected. There, he and [[General]] [[Grievous]] engaged Maul and his [[Shadow Collective]] in [[Second Battle of Dathomir|battle]], a final confrontation with [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]].<ref name="SoD4" />

===Age of the Empire===
[[File:CloakingDevice-SWZ.png|thumb|right|250px|A [[G-1A starfighter]] equipped with a cloaking device.]]
{{Quote|They can't have disappeared. No ship that small has a cloaking device.|[[Lorth Needa]], regarding the vanishing of the ''Millennium Falcon''|<ref name="TESB">{{Film|V}}</ref>}}
[[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s personal [[corvette]], the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', had a cloaking device based on the stealth ship used at Christophsis, and was powered by [[stygium crystal]]s.<ref name="Tarkin" /> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], it was very rare for small ships such as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to have cloaking devices.<ref name="TESB" /> Cloaking devices were used by [[shadow trooper]]s during battles against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref>
