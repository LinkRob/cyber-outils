'''Doonium''' was a heavy [[metal]] that was used primarily for [[starship]] construction. Doonium was found and [[Mining|mined]] on numerous [[planet]]s and [[asteroid field]]s around [[the galaxy]],<ref name="Catalyst" /> a few of these locations being the [[planet]]s [[Batonn]], [[Umbara]],<ref name="Thrawn" /> [[Samovar]],<ref name="Catalyst" /> [[Lothal]], and the [[Socorro]] [[Socorro asteroid belts|asteroid belts]]. 

==History==
During the [[High Republic Era]], doonium was used with [[durasteel]] and [[Tolisian ore]] to create armor to protect Jedi from the effect of the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]].<ref name="Trials">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref>

The [[Pryce Mine|Lothal doonium mine]] was run by [[Pryce Mining]] until [[Arihnda Pryce]] made a deal with [[Senator]] [[Domus Renking]] to have ownership transferred to the [[Galactic Empire]]. Doonium saw use in [[Mark One Pistoeka sabotage droid]]s.<ref name="Thrawn" />

Following the end of the [[Clone Wars]], the Empire extensively mined and stockpiled large amounts of doonium, secretly utilizing it in the [[Project Stardust|construction]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]],<ref name="Thrawn" /> for which, along with [[dolovite]], it was essential in shielding the [[hypermatter]] [[reactor]] core and focusing dish.<ref name="Catalyst" /> Around this [[Imperial Era|time]], three people in particular, [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] and [[Nevil Cygni]] all noticed that doonium was being transferred to an unknown point in the galaxy, though only Cygni, the rebel agent "Nightswan," actively [[Batonn sector insurgency|fought]] to prevent the Empire's shipments of the mineral in fear of whatever the Empire was creating. Thrawn later learned from unguarded dispatches the true nature of the doonium's secret use.<ref name="Thrawn" />
