'''Convorees''' (singular: '''convor'''),<ref name="DB Convor" /> also known as '''Trandoshan birds''',<ref name="MIA">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/missing-in-action-trivia-gallery|text="Missing in Action" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> were a [[species]] of beautiful, regal, and elegant [[owl]].<ref name="Dooku">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref>

The slang phrase "chopped convor liver" was used to denote a person who felt they were getting less attention or consideration than someone else, as in "Do I look like chopped convor liver?"<ref name="Pirate's Price">''[[Pirate's Price]]''</ref>

==Biology and appearance==
Convorees had two [[eye]]s, two wings, a beak,<ref name="DB Convor" /> a prehensile tail, and [[Color|gold]], green, or brown plumage.<ref name="PL" /> Although had short, plump bodies, they were excellent fliers.<ref name="Dawn" /> convorees were very similar in appearance to [[Kiros bird]]s from the planet [[Kiros]], who also possessed grabbing tails. The two species could be differentiated by the fact that Kiros birds had purple and blue feathers instead of gold and brown. Convorees were popular as pets throughout the galaxy.<ref name="AEYNTK" />

Convorees were native to the [[moon]] [[Wasskah]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars New Edition" /> but could be found in several places across [[the galaxy]], including the [[planet]]s [[Atollon]]<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> and [[Takodana]].<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> Visitors who returned from [[Malachor]] reported glimpses of convorees.<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref>

The birds had a strong connection both to [[the Force]] and to [[Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars New Edition" /> The convor [[Morai]] had a spiritual connection with the [[Daughter]],<ref name="Morai">{{Databank|morai|Morai}}</ref> and, after her death, was a known companion of Tano.<ref name="A World Between Worlds">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref>

==Behavior==
{{Quote|Oh just wait until the society hears about this new convor behavioral discovery, CAM.|SF-R3|<ref name="GoC" />}}
Convorees were sneaky birds<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> and in the [[jungle]]s of Wasskah they worked in pairs to fend off predators such as [[momong]]s, lifting those animals into the air together and then dropping them from the [[tree]]tops.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> The convoree themselves [[Diet|fed]] mostly on [[insect]]s and small [[rodent]]s.<ref name="DB Convor" />

==History==
Many cultures were superstitious about convorees, thinking them as harbingers, avatars, or spirit guides. Notably, ancient heroes in [[Togruta]] mythology saw convorees as reincarnations of deceased ancestors that would appear in times of need. Togrutan funerals often culminated with a convor hatchling being presented to the bereaved.<ref name="Dawn" />

During the [[Clone Wars]], a convor was being sold for 60 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] in the town of [[Pons Ora]] on the planet [[Abafar]].<ref name="MIA" />

[[File:CAMandConvor-GoC.png|thumb|left|200px|The disguised [[CAM]] and a convor]]
At some point after [[34 ABY]], the [[Safari droid]] [[SF-R3|Aree]] and the [[cam droid]] [[CAM]] were sent to [[Atollon]] by the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] to study the behavior of convorees. Aree found out that convorees could be attracted by disguising a cam droid as a convor, but making friends with convorees works even better by not disguising and just being oneself.<ref name="GoC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Convorees are named after [[Dave Filoni]]'s wife [[E. Anne Convery]]. When asked about the bird's appearance in "[[The Mystery of Chopper Base]]" and "[[Twilight of the Apprentice]]," he responded there was a deeper meaning behind its presence: "In some ways, I could say that it's a messenger, it's an observer. It is definitely something. And... I would rather have fans debate&mdash;but I would suggest... that whatever that thing is an avatar of has actually appeared in the animated ''Star Wars'' universe before. So decrypt from there."<ref name="Filoni IGN">{{WebCite|url=https://uk.ign.com/articles/2016/03/31/star-wars-rebels-dave-filoni-on-ahsokas-fate-mauls-return-and-much-more?page=1|text=''Star Wars Rebels'': Dave Filoni on Ahsoka's Fate, Maul's Return and Much More|author=Goldman, Eric|date=2016-03-30|work=IGN|archivedate=20200103221540}}</ref>
