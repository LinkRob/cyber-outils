'''Carlo Lo Raso''' is a freelance artist and former [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] Consumer Products character artist. He has done packaging design artwork for [[Hasbro]], notably the character designs for the [[W:c:swmerchandise:Galactic Heroes|Galactic Heroes]] figure sets.
