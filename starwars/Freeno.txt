'''Freeno''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Er'Kit (species)|Er'Kit]] [[miner]] who worked at an [[aurodium]] [[Elphrona aurodium mine|mine]] on the [[planet]] [[Elphrona]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. One [[Standard day|day]], while attempting to unearth a valuable [[kantak crystal]], Freeno caused a collapse in the mine. He would have [[Death|died]] in the cave if not for the intercession of [[Jedi]] [[Bell Zettifar]] and [[Loden Greatstorm]], who urged the Er'Kit to consider less selfish paths in the future.<ref name="Set For Life" />
