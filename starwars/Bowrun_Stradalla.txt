'''Bowrun Stradalla''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Artisan/Legends|artisan]] who lived in the wilderness outside of [[Theed/Legends|Theed]] on [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]] during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]. He was a leading crafter of musical instruments.<ref name="AED" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dialogue pertaining to Bowrun Stradalla appeared in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online roleplaying game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']]. However, he never actually appeared on the [[Star Wars video games|video game]]'s live servers as a proper [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]].<ref name="AED" />
