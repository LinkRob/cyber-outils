The '''Brother Claw Gang''' was a criminal gang led by the [[crime lord]] [[Brother Claw]] that had territory on the [[planet]] [[Burnin Konn]]. he gang consisted primarily of [[human]]s, wielding [[blaster pistol]]s or [[vibrosword]]s. During the [[Iron Blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]] where Burnin Konn was located, the [[Galactic Empire]] shot down the [[Trade Spine League]] [[starship]] ''[[Mother of Pearl]]'' when it tried to leave Burnin Konn, causing the ship to crash in Brother Claw's territory.<ref name="SWU" />

The crime lord [[Dapp|"Happy" Dapp]] then sent a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] into the gang's territory to salvage the cargo of the ''Mother of Pearl''. The [[smuggler]] entered the gang's territory and successfully fought through a large number of Brother Claw Gang members before reaching the ship and recovering some of its cargo for Dapp.<ref name="SWU" />
