'''Czerialans''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Czerialus]]. They resembled [[human]]s with high cranial domes with tufts of [[Color|white]] [[hair]]. [[Jannitha Xoczuko]] was a member of this species. During the [[Imperial Era]], their homeworld was under [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] control and the [[Xoczuko family]] sought to return after being disheartened on [[Corellia]], unaware of the Empire's control on the planet.<ref name="Official Guide" />
