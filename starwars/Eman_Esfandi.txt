'''Eman Esfandi''' is the [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] Persian-Equadorian artist<ref name="DragonWulf" /> who portrayed [[Ezra Bridger]] in the [[2023]] [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]''.<ref name="Teaser Trailer">{{SWYouTube|HnzNZ0Mdx4I|Ahsoka &#124; Teaser Trailer &#124; Disney+}}</ref> The [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]] is also a [[Wikipedia:Musician|musician]] under the name '''DragonWulf''' and also a film [[Wikipedia:Director|director]] and producer.<ref name="DragonWulf" />

Eman Esfandi was born in Laredo, Texas. His mother and father were immigrants from Ecuador and Iran, respectively. He had an Associate's degree in Business Administration and a Bachelor's degree in Economics from the University of Texas, before pursuing a career in acting.<ref name="Background">{{WebCite|url=http://voyagela.com/interview/check-eman-esfandis-artwork|text=Check out Eman Esfandi's Artwork|date=2019-08-06|work=[http://voyagela.com/''VoyageLA'']|archivedate=20220909224147}}</ref>
