'''Darth Kallomena''' was a [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] living during the [[Galactic War]]. Some time after stopping the [[Children of the Emperor]], [[the Barsen'thor]] came into contact with Kallomena while pursuing [[Torwin Vehl]] on [[Bothawui/Legends|Bothawui]].<ref name="SWTOR" />

After a skirmish with the Barsen'thor, Kallomena was defeated and presumed that [[The Force/Legends|the Force]] was teaching her a lesson. She then allied with the Barsen'thor and aided in the search for Vehl, which eventually led Kallomena to the planets [[Sleheyron/Legends|Sleheyron]] and [[Makeb]].<ref name="SWTOR" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Kallomena appears in the game files for ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' under cnv.exp.01.bothawui.class.jedi_consular.darth_kallomena_unnamed.

She was intended to appear as part of a fourth chapter in the Jedi Consular class story, which would have been released in the first expansion for the game.<ref name="SWTOR" /> The content was eventually scrapped, and the remnants of the project were retooled into ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Rise of the Hutt Cartel]]''. Kallomena does not appear in the live version of the game.
