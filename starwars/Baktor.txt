'''Baktor''' was a [[Corellian|resident]] of [[Coronet City]]&mdash;the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Corellia]]&mdash;during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> By [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> they had come to owe a favor to [[Alys Ongwa]]&mdash;a Corellian who ran the [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection]] business&mdash;who therefore had Baktor's contact information saved in a list.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> during a [[Battle of the Corellian Shipyards|battle]] against the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s at the planet's [[Santhe Shipyards|shipyards]], Ongwa sent out a message across the city requesting help for her, her company, and several [[Jedi]]. During the message, she called out Baktor's favor to her, intending to collect on it by asking them to travel to the [[shipyard]]s to help prevent Corellia from falling to the Nihil. Eventually, the Nihil forces were defeated.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
