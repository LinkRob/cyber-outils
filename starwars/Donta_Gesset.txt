'''Donta Gesset''' was the [[Governor of Naboo|Governor]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] late in the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Gesset greeted [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] when she arrived in [[Theed]] to secure Naboo's allegiance to the [[New Republic]], and escorted the princess to [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Sosha Soruna]] to begin the discussions. Gesset was present when the [[Galactic Empire]] launched [[Operation: Cinder]] against Naboo in an attempt to render it inhospitable.<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" />
