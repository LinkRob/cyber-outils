The '''BT-45D U-wing''' was a [[transport]] model manufactured by [[Incom Corporation]], and was the civilian version of the military [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]]. Stripped of all its military applications as well as its [[hyperdrive]], the BT-45D found favor with private security forces<ref name="RSOWM" /> on a few scattered [[Planet|worlds]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], while the UT-60D only had a limited production run prior to [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /><ref name="RO date">{{FilmDate|RO}}</ref> Incom Corp recycled pre-planned options for the BT-45 version.<ref name="RSOWM" />

==Behind the scenes==
The BT-45D U-wing was first mentioned in the U-wing section of the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" />
