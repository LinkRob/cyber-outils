The '''Altier sector''' was a [[sector]] in the [[Expansion Region]] that contained the [[Ombakond sector]].<ref name="Appendix" />

{{Sec-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The entry for the [[Middian system]] in the "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix published in [[June]] [[2025]] lists the [[Ombakond sector]] (misspelled as Ombakand) as a subsector of the Altier sector, though the sector is not listed as such for any of the other systems in the Ombakand sector.
