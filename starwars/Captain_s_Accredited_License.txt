A '''Captain's Accredited License''' was an accreditation registered with the [[Bureau of Ships and Services]] and [[Imperial Space Ministry]]. The [[smuggler]] [[Platt Okeefe]] wrote in the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' that savvy smuggler could slide into legit [[Cargo hauler|cargo hauling]] at the first hint of [[Imperial Office of Customs|customs]] crackdown by acquiring this license.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Captain's Accredited License was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and released in [[2018]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
