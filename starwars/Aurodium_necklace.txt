An '''Aurodium necklace''' was a type of [[necklace]] worn across [[the galaxy]] by many different beings.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an aurodium necklace could be sold for twenty six [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aurodium necklaces appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. In the game, the necklace could only be sold for credits, and did not have another function.<ref name="Outlaws" />
