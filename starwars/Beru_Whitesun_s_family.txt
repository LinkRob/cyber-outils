The [[human]] [[Gender|female]]<ref name="SWCEUAE" /> [[Beru Whitesun Lars|Beru Whitesun]]<ref name="Beru DB">{{Databank|beru-lars|Beru Lars}}</ref> was a member of a '''family''' that, by the [[time]] of the [[Separatist Crisis]], had been made up of three generations of [[moisture farmer]]s.<ref name="SWCEUAE" />

Beru [[Marriage|married]] into the [[Lars family]], becoming the wife of [[Owen Lars]]. They lived at the [[Lars homestead|Lars Homestead]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] where they raised their [[Skywalker family|nephew]], [[Luke Skywalker]], during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Skywalker: A Family at War">''[[Skywalker: A Family at War]]''</ref>
