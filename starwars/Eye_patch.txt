An '''eye patch'''<ref name="Bounty Hunters 31">[[Bounty Hunters 31|''Bounty Hunters'' 31]]</ref> (also spelled as '''eyepatch''')<ref name="Pirate Chemist" /> was an article of [[clothing]] worn over a missing [[eye]]<ref name="AotC">{{Film|II}}</ref> or eyestalk.<ref name="SC 101">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}}</ref> During the [[Republic Era|Fall of the Jedi]]<ref name="AotC" /> and the [[Imperial Era|Reign of the Empire]] eras,<ref name="DV3">[[Darth Vader (2020) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 3]]</ref> the [[human]] [[Captain]] [[Gregar Typho|Typho]] sported a silver eye patch over<ref name="AotC" /> his<ref name="Databank-Captain Typho">{{Databank|captain-typho|Captain Typho}}</ref> missing eye.<ref name="AotC" /> During the [[New Republic era]], the [[Gran]] [[pirate]] [[Pax (pirate)|Pax]] wore an eye patch with a band in place of<ref name="SC 101" /> his<ref name="SC 101 AD">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> rightmost eyestalk.<ref name="SC 101" />
