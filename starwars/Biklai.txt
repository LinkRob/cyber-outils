{{Quote|I was reporting from Biklai during the transition from the Old Republic to the Empire, which was less transition and more bloodbath. I witnessed Kynch order her troops to open fire. There were civilians in the crowd.|Ricta Varsh|<ref name="Tales" />}}
'''Biklai''' was a terrestrial astronomical object in [[the galaxy]]. During the first days of the [[Galactic Empire]], the population of Biklai protested the Empire's regime in what became known as the [[Biklai uprising]]. Imperial commander [[Elix Kynch]] order her troops to open fire into a crowd of civilians unaffiliated with the protests, laughing as they died, and journalist [[Ricta Varsh]]'s eyewitness story of the incident was squashed by management at [[HoloNet News]]. For her actions, Kynch would be dubbed the "Butcher of Biklai."<ref name="Tales" />
