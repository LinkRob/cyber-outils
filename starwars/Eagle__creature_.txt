'''Eagles''' were a type of [[Diet|predatory]] flying creature. During the [[High Republic Era]], while on a [[Mission to Cethis|mission]] to the astronomical object [[Cethis]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Amadeo Azzazzo]] watched three [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[transport]] [[starship]]s landing as their security escorts circled above like watchful eagles.<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" />
