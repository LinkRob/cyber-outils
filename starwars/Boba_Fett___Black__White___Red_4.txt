'''''Boba Fett — Black, White & Red'' 4''' is the fourth and final issue of [[canon]] [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Boba Fett — Black, White & Red]]''. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Sara Pichelli]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 31]], 2025.

==Publisher's summary==
''BOBA FETT VS. THE SHADOW OF BRI-PHRANG! A murder in the TARKIN family puts BOBA FETT on the hunt for a ruthless killer! Things get even more wild as bounty hunter RASLIN GRACE joins the fray! Will Boba get to the bottom of the murder, or will Raslin's violent ways stop him dead in his tracks?''

==Plot summary==
{{Quote|Imperial families. Backwater crime lords, smugglers and thieves - we're all the same. I’m not paid for moral opinions, so I don't offer them. But at least I know what I am. People like the Tarkins don't.|Boba Fett|<ref name="BWR4">[[Boba Fett — Black, White & Red 4|''Boba Fett — Black, White & Red'' 4]]</ref>}}
The story opens at the [[Royal Imperial Academy]] where Cadet [[Sydna Tarkin]] is praised for her strategic brilliance. Shortly after, a masked assassin infiltrates her private quarters and kills her, dismissing her offers of family wealth and stating she will never find peace. [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Grand Moff Tarkin]] is informed of the murder but coldly dismisses it as a "failure" he has no time for. Sydna's mother, seeking "Tarkin justice," hires [[Boba Fett]] to find the killer, offering 20,000 credits for delivery and double if the target is brought in alive.

Fett arrives in [[Eriadu City]] and meets an informant named Livu, who explains that the Tarkins are widely loathed by the local population. Livu identifies the killer as the Shadow of Bri-Phrang, a vigilante many locals consider a hero. During the investigation, Fett is attacked by [[Raslin Grace]], a member of the [[Eriaduan]] royalty who hunts for sport. Though she initially offers to work with Fett, she quickly turns on him, revealing she wants to kill Fett herself to mount his helmet on her wall as a trophy.

Fett uses Raslin as bait to draw out the Shadow of Bri-Phrang. After a skirmish in tight quarters, Fett captures the Shadow and saves them from Raslin’s finishing blow, though only to secure the higher bounty for a living target. Fett delivers the prisoner to Sydna's mother, who prepares to inflict suffering on the assassin. The Shadow remains defiant, claiming their actions have proven the "aristocrats" are not untouchable. Fett departs Eriadu, having collected his payment and remaining indifferent to the planet's social unrest.
