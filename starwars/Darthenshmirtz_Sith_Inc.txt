The '''''Darthenshmirtz Sith Inc''''' was a [[starship]] owned by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[scientist]] [[Heinz Darthenshmirtz]].<ref name="Phineas" />

==History==
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{PnFDate|1}}</ref> the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[scientist]] [[Heinz Darthenshmirtz]] was aboard the ''Darthenshmirtz Sith Inc'' alongside his trusted [[protocol droid]] [[Norm-3PO]] and went to [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. While onboard, the scientist complained that the Empire stole his idea and built the Death Star, although his original invention was intended to be a nutcracker called the [[Doof Star]]. He later landed his [[starship]] on a docking bay and ordered Norm-3PO to bring all of his gear to his [[Abandoned recycling compartment|lair]].<ref name="Phineas" />

==Behind the scenes==
''Darthenshmirtz Sith Inc'' first appeared in ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]'',<ref name="Phineas" /> which premiered on [[July 26]], [[2014]].<ref name="Reporter">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/live-feed/phineas-ferbs-star-wars-special-711433|text='Phineas and Ferb's' 'Star Wars' Special Lands Premiere Date|author=Ng, Philiana|date=2014-06-12|work=The Hollywood Reporter|archivedate=20201205100409}}</ref> The starship was a parody of the building [[W:c:phineasandferb:Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated|Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated]] owned by the [[W:c:phineasandferb:Heinz Doofenshmirtz|counterpart]] of [[Heinz Darthenshmirtz]] in the [[Disney Channel]] animated series ''[[W:c:phineasandferb:Phineas and Ferb|Phineas and Ferb]]''. The design and traits of the starship is very similar to the building it was based on.<ref name="Phineas" />

The name plaque of the starship reads "<small>''[obscured]''</small>MIRTZ SITH INC"; this article assumes the full name is "Darthenshmirtz Sith Inc."<ref name="Phineas" />
