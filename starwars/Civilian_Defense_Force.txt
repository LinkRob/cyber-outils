The '''Civilian Defense Force''', also known as the '''Yavin system's Defense Force''', was a civil defense force tasked with protecting the [[Yavin system]].<ref name="Free Fall" /> After the [[Battle of Endor]], [[Lieutenant]] [[Shara Bey]] retired from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and [[Dameron homestead|settled]] with her [[Dameron family|family]] on [[Yavin 4]]. She flew [[Shara Bey's A-wing|her]] old Alliance [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]] as part of the civilian defense.<ref name="Before the Awakening" />
