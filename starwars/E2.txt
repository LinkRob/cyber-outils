{{Quote|You're always right, E2.|Lola|<ref name="Visions" />}}
'''E2''' was a [[Color|black]] spherical looking [[droid]] belonging to the former [[Sith]] [[Lola]]. After Lola left her old [[Kutu|Sith master]] she became a reclusive [[artist]]. Some time later Lola's old master tracked her down. In the ensuing chase E2 used a [[laser cannon]] mounted inside his head to shoot the pursuers down but the [[Sith Master|Sith master]] used [[the Force]] to rip apart E2's three [[Limb|legs]]. After Lola defeated her old master she repaired E2 and the two departed on her triple-bladed [[starship]] to find a more welcoming world.<ref name="Visions" />
