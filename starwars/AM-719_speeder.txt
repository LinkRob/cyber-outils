The '''AM-719 speeder''', more commonly known as the '''Alderaan royal speeder''' was a model of [[speeder]] used by the [[House of Organa]], the Royal Family of [[Alderaan]]. The speeders were used for transport within [[Aldera]] between the [[Aldera spaceport]] and the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan]]. They were equipped with large [[Viewport|windows]] that allowed the public to view the passengers.<ref name="Databank" />

The family used the speeder<ref name="Part I">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref> in [[9 BBY]]<ref name="Kenobi date">{{KenobiDate|I}}</ref> during a visit made by [[Queen]] [[Breha Organa]]'s sister [[Duchess]] [[Celly Organa]] and her family.<ref name="Part I" />
