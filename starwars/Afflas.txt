'''Afflas''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Zeltron]] [[con artist]] who operated on the [[moon]] of [[Nar Shaddaa]] in the time of the [[Galactic Empire]] and teamed up with [[Lentsch]] and [[Loonoo]] to run a scam.

==Biography==
{{Quote|When she hooks an offworld, she invites him to a nice-looking spice den and convinces him to order an off-menu sampler.|Loonoo|<ref name="Smugglers Guide" />}}
While on the [[moon]] of [[Nar Shaddaa]], Afflas worked with [[Lentsch]] and [[Loonoo]] to operate a [[spice den]] scam at [[Lentsch's spice den]] in [[Megablock 65]]. As part of the scam, Afflas used her charms to convince an offworlder to join her at the [[spice]] den and order an off-menu sampler, after which Lentsch would deliver a bill far higher than what the victim was expecting with threats of violence if they didn't pay. Afflas and Lentsch's operation was so successful that the two decided they did not need Loonoo's help in running it, despite the fact that the original idea for the scam was Loonoo's. After Afflas and Lentsch shut him out of the scheme, Loonoo recorded the experience in [[Maz Kanata]]'s ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smugglers Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Afflas was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smugglers Guide" />
