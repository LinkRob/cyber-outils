'''Banchii survivalist pants''' were resistant and insulated [[Clothing|pants]] that were made on<ref name="Outlaws" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Banchii]]. The pants were white in [[color]] and had a pattern of crossing diagonal lines below the knees. A pair of brown and blue [[boot]]s was included with the pants.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[Wesla's]], a [[tailor]] shop on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet [[Kijimi]] that was operated by the [[Twi'lek]] [[Lani Wesla]], offered a pair of Banchii survivalist pants for sale. Wesla also had a [[Banchii survivalist belt]] and a [[Banchii survivalist jacket]] in stock.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] purchased a pair of Banchii survivalist pants from Wesla for 850 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Banchii survivalist pants appeared in ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', a [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. When equipped by the player, the pants greatly reduce damage taken from explosions while crouched, rolling, or sliding.<ref name="Outlaws" />
