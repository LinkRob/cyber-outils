'''Charlin Plaka's lightsaber''' was a [[lightsaber]] used by the [[Twi'lek]] [[Charlin Plaka]] as she served the [[Jedi Order]] as a [[Padawan]] and [[Jedi Knight|Knight]] during the [[Republic Era]]. The weapon's hilt made of shining [[chromium|chrome]]. After she killed Plaka, the [[Inquisitor]] [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] took the [[Jedi]]'s lightsaber with her as proof that she was worthy of the title Thirteenth Sister. 

==Description==
{{Quote|If we were using real lightsabers, you’d either be grievously injured or dead.|Iskat Akaris, to Charlin Plaka, after sparring with training sabers|<ref name="Inquisitor" />}}
The [[lightsaber]]'s [[hilt]] was constructed by [[Charlin Plaka]] of gleaming [[Chromium|chrome]] and was considered elegant by [[Padawan]] [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] in comparison to her own [[Iskat Akaris' first lightsaber|weapon]]. As unique as all [[Jedi]] lightsabers,<ref name="Inquisitor" /> the weapon contained a kyber crystal.<ref name="Kyber Databank"/> 

==History==
{{Quote|I struck one, although it didn’t die, and it was awful.|Charlin Plaka, referring to a Geonosian|<ref name="Inquisitor" />}}
[[File:RiseofRedBlade-Inquisitor-Iskat.png|left|thumb|The Thirteenth Sister (pictured) earned her title by killing Charlin Plaka and took the Jedi's lightsaber as proof.]]
By [[22 BBY]],<ref name="GeonosisDate"/> Jedi Padawan Charlin Plaka had constructed her lightsaber and carried it with her. Prior to the two engaging in a sparring session with [[Training lightsaber|training sabers]], Charlin's fellow Padawan, Iskat Akaris, wished she could use her real lightsaber against Plaka, noting to herself how hers seemed ugly and brutish compared to Charlin's handsome weapon. Plaka went on to use her lightsaber during the [[Clone Wars]], starting with the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], where she injured a [[Geonosian]] and helped to neutralize an enemy [[LR1K sonic cannon|cannon]] with it.

Sometime between [[19 BBY]] and [[14 BBY]],<ref name="FirrhanaDate">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]'' establishes that [[Charlin Plaka]] and [[Klefan Opus]] were captured and held [[prisoner]] by [[Jakadis]] between [[Order 66]] and the events of [[Darth Vader (2017) 19|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 19]], which equates to between [[19 BBY]] and [[14 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> as the [[Great Jedi Purge]] commenced, Plaka and [[Jedi Master]] [[Klefan Opus]] were captured by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jakadis]] and [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] in his [[Unidentified Sith temple (Firrhana)|fortress]] on the [[planet]] [[Firrhana]]; their lightsabers were kept in a chest with other [[Force artifact|Force artifacts]]. The imprisoned Jedi were eventually discovered by Akaris, now an [[Inquisitor]] [[Mission to Firrhana|hunting Jedi]]. She slew Charlin and [[Unidentified Zeltron Jedi|another Jedi]] in their cells and killed Opus in a lightsaber duel before retrieving their lightsabers from the chest. Akaris took the three [[Weapon|weapons]] with her back to [[Inquisitorius Headquarters]] on the planet [[Coruscant]] where she turned them over to [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and [[the Grand Inquisitor]] as proof that she had earned her new Inquisitorial title of Thirteenth Sister.<ref name="Inquisitor" />
