'''Akaba spice''' was a substance found in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the criminal [[Pyke Syndicate]] had a recipe for the [[Foodstuff|beverage]] [[spotchka]] which included akaba spice as an ingredient. A copy of the recipe was stored using the filename "[[SK01]]" in a [[Computer|terminal]] at the [[Pyke Syndicate stronghold]] in the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] was hired by [[Eleera Soi]], a [[contract broker]] for the rival [[Crimson Dawn]] [[syndicate]], to steal "SK01" from the Pyke stronghold. After Vess made her into the building and [[Slicer|sliced]] the terminal, she was disappointed to learn that the file was simply a spotchka recipe. She eventually delivered the recipe to Soi at [[Makal's Gambling Parlor]] in Mirogana.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Akaba spice was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
