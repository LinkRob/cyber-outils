The '''Dothnia system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Hunnoverrs sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square S-17 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> [[Dothnian slime]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
