'''31174''' was an [[Turbolift|elevator]] located in a tower of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] ''[[Invisible Hand]]''.

==Description==
The [[Turbolift|elevator]] was a large, [[Color|grey]] elevator with a large interior more than capable of accommodating 10 people. There were ten lights on the inside: three on each non-door wall and one large light on the ceiling. The same type of ceiling light was on the top and bottom of the elevator's exterior.<ref name="RotS">{{Film|III}}</ref>

31174 was the middle elevator of its group of five sharing the same shaft. It was secured to the shaft by two railings that ran along the left and right sides of the elevator.<ref name="RotS" />

==History==
During the [[Battle of Coruscant]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] used this elevator while rescuing [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. While the two were heading to the top of the tower, the lift was unexpectedly shut down. Using his [[Hush-98 comlink|comlink]], Obi-Wan told [[R2-D2]] to fix the problem, but Anakin instead impatiently cut a hole in the roof of the lift. Meanwhile, R2-D2 managed to reactivate it, first sending it down and forcing Anakin to hang onto a ledge, but then correcting its direction to up at the urgent request of Kenobi. Two [[Security droid|security]] [[B1-series battle droid|battle droids]] held Anakin at gunpoint, but the [[Jedi]] leaped onto 31174 as it ascended by, and the [[droid]]s were destroyed. The elevator itself was lost when the second half of the ''Invisible Hand'' broke in two above the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="RotS" />
