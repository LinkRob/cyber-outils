{{Quote|I can see in your eyes the toll of the war. You strike us. We strike harder. On and on&hellip;|Queen Adrialla of Eiram, to Captain Xiri A'lbaran of E'ronoh|<ref name="Convergence" />}}
The '''Eiram and E'ronoh War''',<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref> also known as the '''Eiram&ndash;E'ronoh war''',<ref name="Eye of Darkness">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]''</ref> was a conflict fought between the [[planet]]s [[Eiram]] and [[E'ronoh]]. The conflict involved several wars between the two planets, which was why it was also referred to as the '''Forever War'''. The latest incarnation of the war was fought<ref name="Convergence" /> between [[387 BBY]] and [[382 BBY]].<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> The [[Spiral-class Viz-Core Eviscerator|''Spiral''-class Viz-Core Eviscerator]] [[starfighter]] model saw action during the war.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> The peoples of Eiram and E'ronoh would not settle their differences, entering into a [[cold war]], until the aftermath of the [[Eiram–E'ronoh crisis]].<ref name="Into the Dark" />

==The war==
===War of 387 BBY &ndash; 382 BBY===
====Beginning====
The [[Monarch of E'ronoh]] doubled down on the war again after his son [[Niko A'lbaran]] died on a diplomatic mission to [[Eiram]]. The Monarch thought that his son was killed by the [[Adrialla|the queen of Eiram]] which was not true as his son had drowned in a tragic accident. Also the E'roni's believed that Eiram stole their colony on the [[Eirie|Timekeeper Moon]] which further increased the tension between the planets.<ref name="Convergence" /> The [[Battle of Salamento Bridge]] was fought at one point.<ref name="No Such Thing" />

====Effects of the war====
Because important trade routes went through the [[Eiram system]], several worlds dependent on imported food were beginning to starve as the war made the trade routes in the Koradin sector, including the Lipsec Run, unusable. The planets of [[Sump]] and [[Shuraden]] requested aid from the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] for this exact reason. The Republic conducted extensive remapping and cleared the hyperlanes of pirates who tried to collect their spoils of war twice to reestablish a lifeline for the starving systems again.<ref name="Convergence" /> The [[Hutt Clan]] profited greatly from the war.<ref name="Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref>

The war also caused one of E'ronoh's few allies, [[Merokia]], to cut ties with [[E'ronoh]] and not fulfill their promise of relief and decreased the off-planet marble contracts of E'ronoh drastically. The [[Path of the Open Hand]] arrived on E'ronoh at least and secured new followers from young citizens who wanted to avoid the draft.<ref name="Convergence" />

Even though the war never reached Eiram's capital in the form of fighting, surrounding towns were attacked which eroded the infrastructure of [[Erasmus Capital City|Erasmus]]. Eiram's population was hit hard by the effects of the war as food and water had become scarce goods. Additionally the environment suffered heavily during wartime. The many beaches of Eiram were often stained with oil and fuel that leaked from downed ships. Surrounding greenery was burned from explosions and dwellings were in disrepair.<ref name="Convergence" />

Out of desperation Eiram's queen Adrialla made a bargain with The Mother for food, fuel and medical supplies. When she did not uphold her end of the bargain though, The Mother sent one of her Children to threaten her into fulfilling her promise.<ref name="Convergence" />

====Ceasefire effort====
{{Quote|You think that matters? You think the people of Eiram care about what those trumped-up royals have to say about any of this? For years they asked us to fight their battles. To sacrifice our loved ones&mdash;our husbands, wives, partners, and children&mdash;on the altar of their glorious war. And we did so, willingly. We followed them to hell. We pledged our very existence to their cause. And then, one day, just like that, they tell us to lay down our weapons. That the fighting is over. That there will be no more bloodshed. As if everything that had gone before could just be forgotten. As if we could simply accept our losses and move on. Be friends with the people who killed our loved ones. Perhaps even marry them&hellip; No one came and asked us about peace, Creighton.|[[Lian Cerox]], to Jedi Master [[Creighton Sun]]|<ref name="Battle of Jedha" />}}
Five years after the war reignited, a new attempt for a ceasefire was made. Though the [[Elecia Zeveron|The Mother]] tasked one of her [[Children of the Open Hand|Children]], [[Serrena]], to manipulate one of [[Thylefire Squadron]]'s starfighters which was supposed to be flown by [[Xiri A'lbaran]] in hope of breaking the ceasefire and prolonging the war between the planets. Although the sabotage operation was successful and caused a skirmish between Eirami and E'roni forces, the ceasefire ultimately held.<ref name="Convergence" /> Path member [[Shea Ganandra]] suspected that the Hutt Clan was involved in getting peace talks to that point, reasoning that the [[Hutt]]s wanted to take advantage of post-war rebuilding as they had taken advantage of the war itself.<ref name="Vengeance" />

The Path's interference with the [[Summit on Jedha|effort to sign the treaty]] on the neutral moon of [[Jedha]] resulted in the conflict exploding into active warfare again with the [[Battle of Jedha]].<ref name="Battle of Jedha" /> In learning the Path of the Open Hand's role in engineering the war, which it hoped to use to create chaos it would thrive within, the peoples of Eiram and E'ronoh united against their common foe and entered the [[Night of Sorrow|Battle of Dalna]] together.<ref name="Cataclysm">''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project features the Eiram and E'ronoh War, first appearing in [[Zoraida Córdova]]'s novel ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]''. For ''Convergence'', Córdova researched the World Wars and the Cold War about the cyclical nature of human conflict.<ref name="Convergence SWNN">{{WebCite|url=https://www.starwarsnewsnet.com/2022/11/interview-author-zoraida-cordova-talks-about-the-hope-she-brings-to-the-high-republic-in-convergence.html|text=Interview: Author Zoraida Córdova Talks About the Hope She Brings to the High Republic in 'Convergence'|author=Kyle Larson|date=2022-11-25|work=[https://www.starwarsnewsnet.com Star Wars News Net]|archivedate=20221125141304}}</ref>
