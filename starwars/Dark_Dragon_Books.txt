'''Dark Dragon Books''' is an imprint of ''[[Silvester]]'' based in 's-Hertogenbosch that specializes in publishing Art Collections worldwide and republishing comic book content from several different other publishers for the Dutch market. In cooperation with [[Lucasfilm]], [[Marvel Comics]] and [[IDW Publishing]], they republished several stories in softcovers from both the new [[Star Wars comic books|''Star Wars'' comics]] from ''[[Marvel Comics|Marvel]]'' and ''[[IDW Publishing|IDW]]'', but also comics from ''[[Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse]]''.
