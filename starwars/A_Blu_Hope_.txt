"'''A Blu Hope!'''" is an interview with [[Kayleen Walters]], the Senior Director of Marketing at [[Lucasfilm]], about what to expect on the ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]'' Blu-ray. The article also includes interviews with [[Jenny Cresswell]] (a [[Jenny/Legends|woman]] with whom [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] is flirting in a deleted scene of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''A New Hope'']]), [[Garrick Hagon]] ([[Biggs Darklighter/Legends|Biggs Darklighter]]) and [[Anthony Forrest]] ([[Laze Loneozner/Legends|Laze "Fixer" Loneozner]]). It was published in [[Star Wars Insider 128|''Star Wars Insider'' 128]].<ref name="sw128" />
