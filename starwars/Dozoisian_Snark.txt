'''Dozoisian Snark''' was a type of [[poison]]<ref name="Crimson Reign 2" /> that [[Homeworld|originated]] from<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Dozoisia system]].<ref name="Appendix" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsComicDate|Crimson Reign 2|''Crimson Reign'' 2}}</ref> the [[Sith assassin|assassin]] [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]]&mdash;who was aligned with the criminal syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]]<ref name="Crimson Reign 1">[[Crimson Reign 1|''Crimson Reign'' 1]]</ref>&mdash;visited the shop [[Zlarb's]] on<ref name="Crimson Reign 2" /> the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[capital]] [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Star Wars 27">[[Star Wars (2020) 27|''Star Wars'' (2020) 27]]</ref> [[Coruscant]] to acquire poisons for his [[Assassination of the Emperor's Royal Guard|mission]] to [[Death|assassinate]] [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guards]]. The shop's propetior [[Zlarb]] mentioned Dozoisian Snark as one poison available, though the [[assassin]] ultimately did not purchase it.<ref name="Crimson Reign 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dozoisian Snark was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Crimson Reign 2|second issue]] of the [[2021]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Crimson Reign]]''. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Crimson Reign 2" /> on [[February 2]], [[2022]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/97141/star_wars_crimson_reign_2021_2|text=Star Wars: Crimson Reign (2021) #2}}</ref> The [[Poison/Legends|venom]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was known as [[Dozoisian Snark Venom]]. It appeared in ''[[Boba Fett: Pursuit]]'', a [[2004]] young-reader novel written by [[Elizabeth Hand]] as the sixth installment in the [[Star Wars: Boba Fett (novel series)|''Boba Fett'']] novel series.<ref name="Boba Fett: Pursuit">''[[Boba Fett: Pursuit]]''</ref>
