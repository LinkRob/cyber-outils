The '''Full Sabacc''' was one of the best hands in the "[[Corellian Spike]]" variant of [[Sabacc]]. It consisted of five cards together equaling zero, one of which is a [[sylop]] (for example, two tens, two negative tens, and a sylop).<ref name="Solo Expanded">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> Its equivalent in "regular" Sabacc was the [[Pure Sabacc]].<ref name="Anaheim Deck">[[Sabacc (Celebration Anaheim)]]</ref>
