The '''''Enigma Catalyst'''''<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" /> was a [[YT-series]] [[starship]] owned by the criminal [[Khel Tanna]] and was used by her [[Khel Tanna's crew|crew]].<ref name="Han Chewie 5" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> Tanna used the ship to travel to the [[planetoid]] [[Mollo Tanka]] to [[Mission to Mollo Tanka|recover]] [[Han Solo]] so he could lead the crew to a [[Unidentified urn|urn]] that contained the ashes of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt's]] [[Krestrel D'Naran|archrival]]. After they traveled to [[Antillion]], where Solo had last seen the urn, the crew made their way to [[Paqualis III]] to [[Extraction on Paqualis III|extract]] the criminal [[Corbus Tyra]], who had been taken to the [[Benelex Marshal Service Headquarters]] by [[Marshal]] [[Buck Vancto]], as Solo believed he had the urn.<ref name="Han Chewie 5" />
