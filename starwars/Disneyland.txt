'''Disneyland''' (or '''Disneyland Park''') is a real-world theme park located at the [[Wikipedia:Disneyland Resort|Disneyland Resort]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]]. Opened on [[July 17]]th, [[1950s|1955]],<ref name="DL A to Z">{{D23|url=a-to-z/disneyland|text=Disney A to Z: Disneyland}}</ref> it has featured various ''[[Star Wars]]''-themed attractions and events over the years, starting with the opening of the first [[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] attraction in [[1987]].<ref name="SWI 104">{{InsiderCite|104|Star Tours: Taking Off on a Star Tour}}</ref> It eventually became home to the first [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] themed land.<ref name="Now Open">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galaxys-edge-now-open-at-disneyland-resort|text=''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge Is Now Open at Disneyland Resort!}}</ref>

[[Lucasfilm]]'s ''[[Indiana Jones]]'' franchise is also represented at the park by the ''[[Wikipedia:Indiana Jones Adventure|Indiana Jones Adventure]]'' ride.<ref name="Disneyland Park official site">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=destinations/disneyland|text=Disneyland Park}}</ref>
