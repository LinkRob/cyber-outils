'''Caranthanax B'''<ref name="Vader 48" /> was a [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Vader 49">[[Darth Vader (2020) 49|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 49]]</ref> in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]. When<ref name="Vader 47" /> the former [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]]<ref name="Vader 46">[[Darth Vader (2020) 46|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 46]]</ref> [[Sabé]] allied with the [[thief]] [[Warba Calip]] to locate [[Luke Skywalker]]&mdash;[[Jedi]] trainee and the [[Skywalker family|son]] of [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]&mdash;Calip sent Skywalker a [[datacard]] summoning him to<ref name="Vader 47" /> the [[Southern Pole]]<ref name="Vader 48" /> on Caranthanax B. Upon Skywalker's arrival, Sabé intended to test if he had a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]].<ref name="Vader 47" />
