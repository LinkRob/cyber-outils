'''Brian Muir''' is a sculptor who worked on [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] and ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.

He also worked on the earlier [[Lucasfilm]] productions [[Wikipedia:Raiders of the Lost Ark|''Raiders of the Lost Ark'']], [[Wikipedia:Indiana Jones and the Temple of Doom|''Indiana Jones and the Temple of Doom'']] and [[Willow (film)|''Willow'']].
