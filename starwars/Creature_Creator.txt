"'''Creature Creator'''" is an interview with [[Jon Berg]]. It was conducted by [[Thomas G. Smith]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 147|''Star Wars Insider'' 147]]. The interview expands upon the excerpt in the book ''[[Industrial Light & Magic, The Art of Special Effects]]''.

It was re-printed in ''[[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2018]]''.
