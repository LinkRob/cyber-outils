The '''''Amity''''' was a [[Starhawk-class Battleship Mark I|''Starhawk''-class Battleship Mark I]] that served in the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] in [[5 ABY]]. The ''Amity'' took part in the [[Battle of Jakku]] where it was subsequently destroyed.

==Description==
The ''Amity'' was a [[Starhawk-class Battleship Mark I|''Starhawk''-class Battleship Mark I]]<ref name="Empire's End" /> manufactured by [[Nadiri Dockyards]]. It had a hatchet-shaped front and was classed as a [[battleship]]. Starhawks were built from recycled Imperial [[starship]]s.<ref name="Life Debt" /> ''Amity'' had minute yellow and blue markings on its mostly grey hull.<ref name="Nadiri Starhawk Expansion Pack">{{Armada|set=Nadiri Starhawk Expansion Pack}}</ref> As a Starhawk, the ''Amity'' was equipped with a powerful front-facing [[tractor beam]] that relied on [[magnite]] crystals, which allowed the ship to trap and carry ships many times its size.<ref name="Empire's End" />

==History==
[[File:AmityDestroyed-BattleOfJakkuLastStand4.png|thumb|left|250px|The ''Punishment'' collides with the ''Amity'', destroying both vessels.]]
The ''Amity'' was one of the first Mark One Starhawks to be commissioned by the [[New Republic]] for its [[New Republic Defense Fleet|Defense Fleet]] following the [[Battle of Endor]]. The ''Amity'' and its sister Starhawks ''[[Concord]]'' and [[Unity (Starhawk-class)|''Unity'']] served during the pivotal [[Battle of Jakku]]<ref name="Empire's End" /> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Date" />

During the battle, the ''Amity'' flanked the port side of fellow Starhawk ''Concord''. The three Starhawks served as the wedge of the New Republic attack on the dense Imperial [[Star Destroyer]] defensive line above [[Jakku]]. The ''Amity'' was destroyed when [[Captain]] [[Groff]] of the Star Destroyer ''[[Punishment]]'' rammed into her. Both ships were destroyed in the ensuing explosion.<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Behind the scenes==
''Amity'' first appeared in [[Chuck Wendig]]'s [[2017]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', the third installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.
