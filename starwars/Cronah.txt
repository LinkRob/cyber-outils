'''Cronah''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Zabrak/Legends|Zabrak]] [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] who attended the [[Jedi academy]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] during [[Roan Novachez]]'s time of attendance there. A skilled student, he was nevertheless a bully and troublemaker. He was rarely seen outside the company of fellow Padawan [[Cyrus (Jedi Academy)|Cyrus]], and together the two took pleasure in bullying their fellow students at Jedi academy, though they particularly took to bullying Roan. They were sometimes joined in their bullying by a female student named [[Jo-Ahn]].<ref name="jacademy" />

During Roan's second year, Cronah befriended Novachez briefly with Cyrus. Cronah then was under Roan's command as Blue Three during the final exam for [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighter]] pilot training. Along with Cyrus, he ditched Roan during the exam, causing them both to get a C+ for the exam and were happy when [[Roan Novachez]] got a "C-". The two both posted disparaging comments on [[Holobook (HoloNet)|Holobook]], leading [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] to shut down student access to it for a time. Along with Cyrus, he tortured the class pet [[Voorpee]], stuffing the [[Voorpak/Legends|voorpak]] in his locker, resulting in the two being placed on academic probation for the next semester and being denied access to Holobook even after it was restored to the other students.<ref name="jacademy2">''[[Jedi Academy: Return of the Padawan]]''</ref>

In Roan's third year, he got infuriated that Cyrus had changed and was envious of Roan and thus drifted away from both. He teamed with [[Ronald Rinzler]] to secretly prank Roan for the whole year. The two seemed to part on neutral terms.<ref name="jacademy3" />
