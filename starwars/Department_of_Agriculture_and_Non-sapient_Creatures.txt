The '''Department of Agriculture and Non-sapient Creatures''' was subdivision of the [[Galactic Empire]] that was concerned with matters regarding agriculture and [[Sentience|non-intelligent]] creatures.<ref name="MoF" /> During the [[Galactic Republic]], it was known as the '''Department of Agriculture and Nonsentient Creatures'''.<ref name="Knights of Fate" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|55}}</ref> it was affiliated with a data processing center located on [[Coruscant]]'s [[Federal District]].<ref name="MoF" />

{{Imp-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Department of Agriculture and Non-sapient Creatures was first mentioned in ''[[Knights of Fate]]'', a [[2018]] sourcebook for [[Fantasy Flight Games]] ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[roleplaying game]] as "Department of Agriculture and Nonsentient Creatures."<ref name="Knights of Fate" /> It made its first appearance in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" />
