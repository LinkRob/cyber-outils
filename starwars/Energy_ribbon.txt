An '''energy ribbon''' was a type of energized edge that could be applied to a suitably conductive melee weapon.<ref name="Official Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> Similar to a [[monomolecular energy cord]] in that it drastically increased the cutting power of a weapon, an energy ribbon glowed with fluorescent light and was high enough in voltage that it could shred metal or incapacitate opponents with its energy. [[Electroripper staff]]s were tipped with energy ribbons, such as the one notably used by [[Enfys Nest]].<ref name="Official Guide" /> The claws of the [[BL-155 Laser ax]] used by [[First Order stormtrooper executioner|stormtrooper executioners]] of [[First Order]] emitted four monomolecular energy ribbons.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>
