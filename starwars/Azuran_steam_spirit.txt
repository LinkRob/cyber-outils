'''Azuran steam spirit''' was a beverage kept in a bottle<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> that originated from<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Azura system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]].<ref name="Appendix" /> By [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> the [[human]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] had been given a bottle opener for Azuran steam spirit by a [[Unidentified sommelier|sommelier]] on<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Bothsoliman">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Bothsoliman system]]</ref> astronomical object [[Bothsoliman]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> after Calrissian used the bottle opener to [[Skirmish on Torrenoteer Minor|escape capture]] from [[bounty hunter]]s on<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> the Outer Rim<ref name="Torrenoteer Minor">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Torrenoteer system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Torrenoteer Minor]], he showed it to his companion&mdash;[[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]]&mdash;and informed him that the bottle opener was for Azuran steam spirit.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />
