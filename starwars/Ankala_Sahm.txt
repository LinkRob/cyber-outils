'''Ankala Sahm'''<ref name="Vader 19" /> was a [[human]] who led the [[Revengers]], a group that opposed the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate. Having had her [[Unidentified village (Ankala Sahm and Tanka)|village]] destroyed by Crimson Dawn, she joined up with [[S'ira]] and [[Tanka]], who had suffered the same fate, following Crimson Dawn operatives and striking against them. While on [[Calior]], Ankala and the Revengers were [[Skirmish on Calior|assisted]] by servants of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who also intended to eliminate Crimson Dawn, and the group subsequently was [[Vader's crew|recruited]] in Vader's quest.<ref name="Vader 18" />
