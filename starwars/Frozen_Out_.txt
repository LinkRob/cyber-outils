"'''Frozen Out!'''" is a short comic story published in [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic 6.34|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic'' 6.34]].

==Publisher's summary==
''In this issue's all-new ''The Clone Wars'' comic adventure, 'Frozen Out', Obi-Wan and Jar Jar are forced to land on a frrrr-reezing planet! Stuck between a squadron of battle droids and a herd of rampaging wampas, how will our heroes possibly escape? There's only one way to find out&hellip;''
