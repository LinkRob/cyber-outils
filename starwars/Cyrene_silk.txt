'''Cyrene silk''' was a type of [[Color|black]] [[silk]] that was used for [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s [[foreign residence gown]] during her visit to [[Coruscant]]<ref name="TCVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> The darkness of the fabric was meant to represent her sadness of being parted from [[Naboo]].<ref name="TCVD" />
