An '''Adventurer slugthrower rifle''',<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> also known as the '''Czerka Adventurer rifle'''<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> was a [[Slugthrower rifle|projectile rifle]] with great accuracy over long distances, and was manufactured by [[Czerka Arms]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> The weapon could be easily dismantled for ease of transport.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Aurra Sing]] was known to carry one with her. Unlike most other weapons, it shot a solid projectile.<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref>
