'''Bovo's''' was a [[Cantina/Legends|cantina]] near the [[Treasure Ship Row/Legends|Treasure Ship Row]] in [[Coronet City/Legends|Coronet City]] and owned by [[Carn Truuvik]], a successful criminal. [[Gender/Legends|He]] and several other beings loyal to him, including [[Catrinna]], conned targets out of their [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]] through rigged [[Sabacc/Legends|sabacc]] games during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="SoF" />
