'''Anbert Dregil''', also known as "'''Long Arms'''" and "'''Ol' Gundark'''," was a [[Kogini]] who worked as a [[Junk dealer|scrap merchant]] and stevedore in [[Olega City]] on the [[planet]] [[Olega]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. In [[132 BBY]], the street urchin [[Guthan Archi]] stole a rock from a cart Dregil was pulling through the streets of the [[city]].

==Biography==
[[File:AnbertDregil-RevengeJustice.png|thumb|300px|alt=Walking with his knuckles on the ground, Anbert Dregil hauls a cart of rocks through the night-time streets of Olega City, with a shielded door to the Jedi Temple visible behind him.|Anbert Dregil worked as a hauler in Olega City.]]
Anbert Dregil was a [[Kogini]] who worked in [[Olega City]] on the [[planet]] [[Olega]] as a [[Junk dealer|scrap merchant]], hired hauler, stevedore, and [[collector]] of [[camera]] equipment during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> He<ref name="Acolyte 2 AD" /> was known around the [[city]] by nicknames like "Long Arms" and "Ol' Gundark,"<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> the latter referencing a [[Gundark|type]] of burly [[Diet|predator]].<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> he pulled a cart full of rocks behind him<ref name="Acolyte 2 AD" /> past the [[Olega Jedi temple]]<ref name="Revenge Justice">{{Acolyte|Revenge / Justice}}</ref> at dusk<ref name="Acolyte 2 AD" /> while grunting.<ref name="Revenge Justice" />

Near the [[Jedi temple|temple's]] entryway, the street urchin<ref name="Revenge Justice" /> [[Guthan Archi]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> ran up to Dregil's cart and stole one of the rocks to throw at the [[Jedi]]'s<ref name="Revenge Justice" /> [[Security droid|gatekeeper droid]], [[TT-8L-OT]],<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> but the Kogini continued without noticing. The following [[Local day|night]], Dregil walked with a [[humanoid]] down one of the city's streets near the [[Olega Apothecary]]<ref name="Revenge Justice" /> as local [[Merchant|vendors]] shut their shops for the night.<ref name="Acolyte 2 AD" />

==Personality and traits==
A burly individual<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> with a large upper body,<ref name="Acolyte 2 AD" /> Dregil had [[Color|yellow]] [[eye]]s and yellowy-brown skin.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==Equipment==
He wore a purple [[Clothing|cowl]] with a gray [[metal]] headpiece, gray pants, and several brown straps on his [[Limb|arms]] while pulling his cart. A brown object made of three incomplete circles hung from his [[belt]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

Anbert Dregil first appeared in "[[Revenge / Justice]],"<ref name="Revenge Justice" /> the second episode of the live-action [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', which aired on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref> Creature concept designer and sculptor [[Andre Gilbert]] created concept art of Dregil titled "Alien Cart Monger" that showed the character with greener skin and long tentacle-like fingers. Gilbert also created an unused concept for the character that depicted Dregil as a gray-skinned quadruped with horns and [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] arms.<ref name="ArtOf">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''</ref> The alien costume was physically portrayed by [[Nick Kellington]], requiring great strength,<ref name="Quinn">{{Facebook|username=MikeQuinnOfficial|url=photo/?fbid=997027082432427&set=a.120417703426707|name=[[Mike Quinn]]|date=2024-06-09|quote=👽 Other creatures from the first two episodes I performed on were non-speaking roles. Selkath's face (eyes & mouth) in the Jedi Temple, costume performed by Liam Cook. The cart puller (face), costume performed by Nick Kellington, who exhibited superhuman strength 🤣. The third was TT-8L-0T the entry droid, performing the entrance, exit and head up/down on the closeups. All super fun and amazing builds from the entire CFX team. This stuff looks so good and is capable of much more scrutiny that is seen on screen. It's that good! Even for the background creatures. Well done every single person involved! Also a shoutout to the amazing crew who supported and championed us, helped us look beautiful on camera (and encouraged me to say naughty things with my animatronics 😉).|archivedate=2024-06-24|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/7ecW5}}</ref> with puppeteer [[Mike Quinn]] controlling the animatronic face with an RC unit.<ref name="Kellington2">{{Instagram|username=nickkellington|url=p/DKj1BsZqwyz|name=[[Nick Kellington]]|date=06-06-2025|quote=My alien creature I performed in The Acolyte made it in the Visual Guide. Whoop! 😁 Name: Anbert Dregil Species: Kogini A.K.A: "Long Arms" or "Ol' Gundark" Thank you Pablo Hidalgo (I was inside the bodysuit and Nien Nunb himself @mikequinnstudios puppeteered the face with an RC unit) #StarWars#TheAcolyte#DisneyPlus#NickKellington#CreaturePerformer|archiveurl=https://archive.is/aAltO|archivefile=File:NickKellington-AnbertDregil-Instagram.png}}</ref>

Kellington referred to the creature as "Long Arms" and thought it looked similar to a [[rancor]] upon first seeing a maquette of the character in the creature workshop.<ref name="Kellington1" /> The Olega scenes including the vendor were shot at [[Shinfield Studios]],<ref name="Location">{{Lucasfilm|url=news/lucasfilm-on-location-the-acolyte|text=Lucasfilm on Location: ''The Acolyte''}}</ref> [[Wikipedia:England|England]].<ref name="Julian Foddy">{{Lucasfilm|url=news/julian-foddy-acolyte|text=''The Acolyte'' Visual Effects Supervisor Julian Foddy}}</ref> The character was not identified in the episode<ref name="Revenge Justice" /> but was named in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]. Dregil's name is a [[tuckerization]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> of Gilbert's name.<ref name="ArtOf" />
