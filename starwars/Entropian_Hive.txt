The '''Entropian Hive''' was an [[asteroid]]<ref name="Alphabet" /> in the [[Entropian Hive system]] in the [[Elbaran sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]],<ref name="Appendix" /> along the [[Cerean Reach]].<ref name="Cerean Reach" /> The asteroid was used as a [[black market]] trading post, controlled by the [[Entropian Hive Trading Council]]. [[Yrica Quell]] was sent to the Entropian Hive by [[Caern Adan]] to recruit [[Nath Tensent]], former [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[pilot]], into the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Alphabet" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|When writing, say like: Okay, Quell is going to meet up with Nath for the first time in some sort of&hellip;sleazy cantina-y place&mdash;you know&mdash;I wanted to find&mdash;ok&mdash;well, that's&hellip;that's Star Wars. Like sleazy cantina-y place is good. What's the spin on&mdash;you know&mdash;what that place looks like, what's the status of it, what the locals are like that's going to feel uniquely post-Endor.|Alexander Freed|<ref name="Star Wars Explained">{{YouTube|video=0h5Bn3R_Buc|text=Alexander Freed Talks Shadow Fall, the Conclusion of The Alphabet Squadron Trilogy, and More!|channel=@StarWarsExplained|name=Star Wars Explained|quote=I got the chance to speak with Alexander Freed about his Star Wars novels, specifically the recent release, Shadow Fall!}}</ref>}}
The Entropian Hive first appeared in the [[2019]] novel [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']], written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Alphabet" /> When creating the location, Freed wanted to establish a "sleazy cantina"-like place for Yrica Quell to meet Nath Tensent which felt unique to the post-[[Battle of Endor]] period.<ref name="Star Wars Explained" />
