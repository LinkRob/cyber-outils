The '''Aurodium Bullseye''' was a [[slugthrower rifle]] owned by the [[Miraluka]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Diago Velaar]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Frontier Slugthrower|standard slugthrower]]. It was a valuable weapon to Diago who worked hard to obtain it.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Aurodium Bullseye was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Diago's Frontier Slugthrower, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 4.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Aurodium Marksman]]. This wrap could be unlocked by reaching the rank Beskar I within ''Hunter's'' "Ranked" mode during the game's fourth season.<ref name="Hunters" />
