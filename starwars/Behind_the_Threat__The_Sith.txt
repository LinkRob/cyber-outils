'''Behind the Threat: The Sith''' is a five-part web enhancement written by [[Matthew Grau]] and released by [[Wizards of the Coast]] on [[June 18]], [[2008]]. It discussed the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] and their role in the galaxy.

==Continuity==
In the article, Jacen Solo is mistakenly referred to as "Jacen Skywalker".
