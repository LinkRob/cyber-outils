'''Blaster modules''' also referred to as '''blaster cartridges'''<ref name="Outlaws Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Visual Guide]]''</ref> were a type of [[blaster]] component that could be quickly swapped in order to alter the firing mode of a [[blaster pistol]]. The [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] used a light [[plasma module]] for [[Kay's blaster|her]] [[VM-19 heavy blaster pistol]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] <ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Vess constructed additional modules including an [[ion module]] configured for burst firing and a [[power module]] configured for firing a single bolt while preparing for a [[Heist on Canto Bight|heist]] on the [[casino]] [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. Following the heist, the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] gave Vess a [[power regulator]] which she used to construct another ion module that was configured to discharge a powerful shockwave when its ion bolt impacted a target.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Outlaws Date" /> Vess constructed five other blaster modules for her [[heavy blaster pistol]]. These included two more [[plasma]] modules, one configured to fire rapidly and the other which fired heavy bolts at a slower rate, one additional ion module who's ion bolts surged on impact, and two additional power modules, the first caused its bolts to detonate on impact and the second which dealt more damage the longer it was charged.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Several types of blaster modules appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Prior to the game's release, its blaster modules appeared in a number of the game's trailer's; first appearing in the game's "Ubisoft Forward: Official Livestream" which was released on [[June 12]], [[2023]].<ref name="Official Story Trailer">{{SWYouTube|DUyk6D5rgcE|Star Wars Outlaws: Official Story Trailer}}</ref> The components were first identified as both  "blaster modules and blaster cartridges" in the digital art book ''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Visual Guide]]'',<ref name="Outlaws Visual Guide" />  released on on [[July 30]] 2023 as part of a promotional fan kit.<ref name="Fan Kit">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/6xTzeAfu46dyxcRLbXvlGg/the-star-wars-outlaws-fan-kit-is-here|text=The Star Wars Outlaws fan kit is here!}}</ref>

As part of the game's main quests player is required to construct the burst ion module as part of the "New Tricks" mission and later must construct the bolt power module after completing the first of either "The Heavy", "The Safecracker" or "The Droidsmith" quests. Players will unlock the key parts in order to construct the other modules by completing main quests but they are not required to actually construct these to complete the game's story. The player receives the shock ion module as part of the main quest of the "A Pirate's Fortune" story expansion for the game.<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Wikipedia:Concept_art|Concept artist]] for the game's blaster modules was done by the Concept Artist [[Kamil Haddad]] under Art Director [[Benedikt Podlesnigg]] with the in&ndash;game model being created by [[Jakub Mrówczyński]].<ref name="Haddad ArtStation">{{ArtStation|subdomain=kamilhaddad|url=projects/1Nxr8G|name=[[Kamil Haddad]]|text=Star Wars Outlaws - VM-19 Kay's Heavy Blaster Pistol|date=2024-09-30|quote=Art Directed by: Benedikt Podlesnigg. Check out the in-game model made by the amazing : Jakub Mrówczyński|archivedate=20250422221227}}</ref>
