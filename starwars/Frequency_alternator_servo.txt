A '''frequency alternator servo''' was a [[starship]] component. When [[Kazuda Xiono]] was fixing his new [[racing starfighter]], the ''[[Fireball]]'', five frequency alternator servos were among the spare parts that had to be bought from [[Orka]] and [[Flix]]'s [[Office of Acquisitions]].<ref name="Recruit" /> When [[Tamara Ryvora]] was repairing Xiono's racing starfighter later, her [[Aurebesh]] list of parts also included five frequency alternator servos. An Aurebesh list of inventory in the Office of Acquisitions on the ''[[Colossus]]'' included "7 frequency alternator servos".<ref name="Dangerous">{{Resistance|Dangerous Business}}</ref> During a salvage operation on the [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|First Order Dreadnought]] ''[[Fulminatrix]]'', [[Valik]] managed to retrieve a frequency alternator servo.<ref name="Salvage">{{Resistance|A Quick Salvage Run}}</ref>
