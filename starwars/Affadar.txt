'''Affadar''' was a largely undeveloped [[planet]] in [[the galaxy]] that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[T'Laeem]], a sentient but primitive [[amphibian]] [[species]]. Shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Galactic Empire]] set up a [[mining]] operation on the planet.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
