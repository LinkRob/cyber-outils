A '''demesne''' was the playing board of the game [[Shah-tezh]]. [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] used this concept to explain the [[Galactic Empire]] to a young [[Gallius Rax]] twenty five years prior to the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

By the design of the game, all pieces exist to protect the [[Imperator]], and if the Imperator falls a game must begin anew. By analogy, if the Emperor were to meet his demise, the Empire would be dismantled and begin anew via the [[Contingency]], since those serving the Empire failed their utmost role of protecting their leader. Following this design, [[Counselor to the Empire|Counselor]] Gallius Rax, a title perhaps eponymous of the Shah-tezh [[Counselor (Shah-tezh)|piece]], exhorted [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] to travel to the [[Eclipse (Executor-class)|''Eclipse'']], a [[Super Star Destroyer]] forging a path through the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="Empire's End" />
