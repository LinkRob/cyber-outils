'''Elaine Baker''' is the ex-wife of make-up designer [[Rick Baker]]. During the filming of the second ''[[Star Wars]]'' film, [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], Baker was filmed for test shots of [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. However, [[Marjorie Eaton]] would be the one to appear in the final film.

Though Elaine Baker is credited in ''[[The Making of The Empire Strikes Back]]'' as being the actress who appears in the final film,<ref name="Making of">''[[The Making of The Empire Strikes Back]]''</ref> a twitter post by [[Lucasfilm]] [[Lucasfilm Story Group|Story Group's]] [[Pablo Hidalgo]] suggests the information in the book is incorrect,<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|username=pablohidalgo|url=status/791503521925304320|name=[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2016-10-26|quote=@Brandon_Bird Okay here's what I've got. It is not Elaine Baker in the movie. @PhilTippett sculpted the piece and Rick applied it. {{C|In response to:Some controversy about the ESB Emperor this morning: http://earwolf.com/episode/the-empire-strikes-back-with-clive-revill<br />so you don't have to listen: did Elaine Baker or Marjorie Eaton play the Emperor that appears onscreen?<br />My theory: Baker was used as a makeup test, which would explain her having no call sheets, but ppl remembering filming her.<br />So my question is: is there any way to know who *made* the Emperor makeup? [...] My hunch is that Baker was a test Emperor, simply b/c it'd be odd for there to be no call sheets for prod. footage}}|archivedate=20161112061520|archivefile=File:Pablo Emperor actress tweet.png}}</ref> making Eaton (credited in the book as doing the test shots) the one who appears in the film.<ref name="Making of" />
