"'''Class Wars!'''" is an article written by [[Tony A. Rowe]] that appeared on [[Star Wars Insider 114|''Star Wars Insider'' 114]]. The article discussed the new character classes available in the ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' game: The [[Jedi Consular]] and the [[Sith Inquisitor]].<ref name="sw114" />
