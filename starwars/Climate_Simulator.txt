The '''Climate Simulator''' was a courtyard located on the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''. It allowed guests to acclimate to the weather conditions of the ship's ports of call, including [[Batuu]].<ref name="Stowaway">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-play-test-feature|text=Stowaway on a Test Flight for ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser|nolive=1}}</ref> The simulator included various [[plant]]s, including the [[toscwon's tongue alocasia]] from the [[planet]] [[Ithor]].<ref name="Galactic Starcruiser">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref> It was located on Deck 4.<ref name="SWGS Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)|''Star Wars'': Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)]]</ref>
