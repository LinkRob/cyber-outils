"'''EndTimes: The Hand'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year five of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign. It was the second adventure in the [[EndTimes trilogy|''EndTimes'' trilogy]] and was written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] and [[Jae Walker]].

==Plot summary==
The heroes travel to the [[Cularin asteroid belt]] to meet with [[Nirama]], who wants to tell them a way to defeat [[Len Markus]] and the [[Darkstaff]]. However, Markus has gotten there first and, along with a group of [[Mercenary/Legends|mercenaries]], has captured Nirama.
