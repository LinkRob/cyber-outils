'''Fengrine''' was an [[Agriworld|agri]]-industrial [[Planet|world]] that had [[hydroponic shell]]s around it.<ref name="CV New" /> It was located in the [[Fengrine system]] in the [[Senex sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]], in grid square L-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The common image of armchair [[Farmer|farming]] in these shells was far removed from the [[moisture farm]]ing that occurred on the [[Temperature|searing]] [[desert]] planet [[Tatooine]]<ref name="CV New" /> in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}}, as depicted in ''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]'', to [[22 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Fengrine was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> from the [[2002]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', which was written by [[Curtis Saxton]] and marked the [[Fengrine/Legends|world's]] first mention in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Cross-Sections">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
