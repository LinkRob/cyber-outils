'''Cantonican Cactus Bites''' was a [[Foodstuff|dish]] served on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. It was made in part from roasting the native [[Cantonican cactus]]. After making a culinary tour of the [[galaxy]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for the dish in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> which was published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cantonican Cactus Bites were created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the dish makes use of [[Wikipedia:Brussels sprout|Brussels sprouts]], diced [[Wikipedia:Shallot|shallots]], minced [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic]], [[Wikipedia:Pine nut|pine nuts]], [[Wikipedia:Olive oil|olive oil]], [[Wikipedia:Brown sugar|light brown sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Zest (ingredient)|orange zest]], [[Wikipedia:kosher salt|kosher salt]], and [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
