'''Dedra Meero's apartment'''<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|dedra-meero-s-apartment|Dedra Meero's apartment}}</ref> was an apartment owned by [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[ISB Supervisor|Supervisor]] [[Dedra Meero]] and her partner [[Syril Karn]] that was located in [[Imperial City]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Sagrona" /> The apartment had panoramic views of the Coruscant skyline. Meero's apartment was a minimalistic, with primarily white tones on the inside. It was a comfortable refuge from the daily rigidity of Meero's work with the ISB.<ref name="Databank" />

{{Struct-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Meero's apartment building was inspired by the [[Wikipedia:Torres Blancas|Torres Blancas]] building in Madrid, Spain, an example of Spanish [[Wikipedia:Organic architecture|Organic architecture]] architecture. The apartment interior was inspired by a 1970s prototype that set decorator [[Rebecca Alleway]] found in a magazine, which included a fondue table.<ref name="Week1Trivia">{{SW|url=news/andor-trivia-guide-week-1|text=Secrets from the Set of ''Andor'' Season 2: Week 1 Trivia}}</ref>
