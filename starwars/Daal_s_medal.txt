'''Daal's medal''' was an amulet owned by the young [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Daal]]. The [[Sith Mother]] used the medal to speak to Daal.

==Description==
'''Daal's medal'''<ref name="Guide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-visions/screechers-reach-episode-guide|text="Screecher's Reach" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}}</ref> was a [[gold]]en amulet attached to a string, creating a necklace. A [[Sith Lord]] known as the [[Sith Mother]] was able to speak and hear through the amulet.<ref name="Screecher" />

==History==
{{Quote|I follow your path with strength and courage|Daal, speaking to her medal|<ref name="Screecher" />}}
At some point, the young [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Daal]] received the amulet. While working at the [[Factory Workshop]] on a [[Unidentified planet (Screecher's Reach)|planet]], Daal left the workhouse wearing the amulet around her neck. When asked by her friends [[Baython]] and [[Quinn]] where she was going, Daal clutched the medal in her hand and told them she was going to [[Screecher's Reach]] as another one of her friends [[Keena]] approached the group. After stealing some [[speeder]]s from the workhouse, her friends joined her on the journey to the [[mountain]].<ref name="Screecher" />

When they arrived at the mountain, Daal held the amulet in her hand when speaking to her friends as their [[Fire|campfire]] shined on the medal. Before entering the [[cave]] Daal put the medal back around her neck and held it close, saying she follows it's path with strength and courage. After having entered the cave, Daal confronted the [[The Force|Force]]-sensitive [[Screecher (Sith)|Screecher]] and once again held her medal and spoke to it once more, incorrectly assuring herself the Screecher was only in her mind. After being attacked by the Screecher with a crimson-bladed [[lightsaber]], Daal attempted to escape the cave.<ref name="Screecher" />

Climbing up the side of the cave to an exit, the Screecher grabbed her ankle and dragged her down. Begging the amulet to give her strength, Daal used the Force to [[Telekinesis|pull]] a jagged rock down on top of the Screecher, knocking her lightsaber from her hands. Daal then used the lightsaber to [[Death|kill]] the Screecher. After leaving the cave, the Sith Mother spoke to Daal, declaring it was time for them to meet. The Sith Mother then landed on the planet in [[Sith Mother's ship|her starship]].<ref name="Screecher" />

==Behind the scenes==
Daal's medal appeared in "[[Screecher's Reach (short film)|Screecher's Reach]]," a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|second volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series, written by [[Will Collins]] and [[Jason Tammemägi]] and directed by [[Paul Young]]. The animated short, produced by [[Cartoon Saloon]], was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Screecher" /> on [[May 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-2|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Release Date, Studios Revealed}}</ref>
