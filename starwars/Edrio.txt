'''Edrio''' "'''Two Tubes'''" was a [[Gender|male]] [[Tognath]] [[mercenary]] [[pilot]] who flew alongside his [[eggmate]], [[Benthic]]. The two shared the nickname derived from the breathing apparatus that allowed their Tognath physiology to process [[oxygen]] [[atmosphere]]s. Edrio's [[homeworld]] [[Yar Togna]] was [[Conquest of Yar Togna|conquered]] and occupied by the [[Galactic Empire]], forcing him to flee as a [[refugee]]. Desiring to strike back at the Empire, Edrio and Benthic had allied with [[Saw Gerrera]] some time before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. 

In 1 BBY before the destruction of Jedha, he was presented after the battle on Jedha as his brother, Benthic took Jyn, Andor, Baze and Chirrut to their hideout as hostage. During this time, the Imperial Director Orsan Callan Krennic ordered the Death Star to destroy Jedha City. Edrio was killed in the resulting explosion along side with many of Saw's Partisans.  

==Biography==
===Joining Gerrera===
Edrio was [[Reproduction|born]] in [[32 BBY]] on [[Yar Togna]]<ref name="Timelines" /> After the [[Galactic Empire]] had [[Conquest of Yar Togna|conquered]] and occupied his [[homeworld]], where his eggmate [[Benthic]] hailed from too,<ref name="Visual Guide" /> Edrio took up the life of a [[mercenary]] and [[Death|killed]] an [[Unidentified Imperial Moff|Imperial Moff]], which caught the attention of rebel leader [[Saw Gerrera]] of the [[Partisans]], which Edrio subsequently joined.<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> Benthic, meanwhile, joined the Partisans after his time with the [[Cloud-Riders]].<ref name="Star Wars 67">[[Star Wars (2015) 67|''Star Wars'' (2015) 67]]</ref>

===Early rebellion===
[[File:Gerrera_and_Edrio_INR.png|thumb|left|260px|Edrio flying with Saw aboard a U-wing.]]
Edrio flew a [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|U-wing transport]] with Gerrera. Together, they both attacked the [[Marauder (Arquitens-class command cruiser)|''Marauder'']]. They [[Mission to Jalindi|rescued]] the [[Spectres]] [[Ezra Bridger]] and [[Sabine Wren]] from the [[Jalindi Station]] before dropping [[proton bomb]]s that destroyed both the relay and the ''Marauder''.<ref name="In the Name of the Rebellion">{{Rebels|In the Name of the Rebellion}}</ref>

The group then [[Mission to Faos Station|traveled]] to [[Faos Station]]. There, he dropped Gerrera and the others off and was told by Gerrera to follow his signal. Edrio then followed Gerrera's signal to the [[Tonnis sector]], where the [[Galactic Empire]] attempted to transfer a [[kyber crystal]] to [[Captain]] [[Slavin]]'s [[Slavin's Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]]. Edrio then completed his rendezvous with Gerrera after he caused the [[crystal]] to explode. They made their escape before the crystal exploded.<ref name="In the Name of the Rebellion" />

Sometime later, Edrio and the rest of the Partisans [[Assault on Dooma 4|raided]] an [[Imperial Metal Factory]] on [[Dooma 4]] after learning the factory had provided components for a secret Imperial [[Project Stardust|project]].<ref name="Crossing the Line">{{RebelsAnimationCite|4|Crossing the Line}}</ref>

===Death on Jedha===
{{Quote|The ground rolled over. A scramble to a snubfighter. My eggmate left behind. Another body in the ashes of Jedha. Another body to avenge.|translated=1|Benthic, speaking of Edrio|<ref name="Star Wars (2015) 39">[[Star Wars (2015) 39|''Star Wars'' (2015) 39]]</ref>}}
[[File:Partisans_on_Jedha.png|thumb|right|290px|Benthic and Edrio during the Jedha insurgency]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> shortly after the [[Battle on Jedha]], Edrio was present as his brother Benthic took [[Jyn Erso]], [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], [[Baze Malbus]] and [[Chirrut Îmwe]] hostage because Andor had just killed a Partisan in self-defense to protect Erso. They then took them back to the [[Catacombs of Cadera]]. During this time, Imperial [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] ordered the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] to [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroy Jedha City]]. The shock wave from the destruction caused the Partisans' nearby hideout to collapse into rubble,<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> killing Edrio alongside many of his Partisan comrades.<ref name="Edrio Two Tubes" />

==Personality and traits==
Edrio was a male [[Tognath]] who wore a breathing apparatus in order to process oxygen atmospheres. Edrio was close with his eggmate Benthic, who both hated the Empire for invading their homeworld of Yar Togna. Their hatred for the Empire led them to join Saw Gerrera's militant Partisans.<ref name="Edrio Two Tubes" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Edrio_costume.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Edrio "Two Tubes" costume at San Diego Comic-Con International]]
The character Edrio "Two Tubes" first appeared in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Rogue One" /> where he was portrayed by [[Paul Kasey]],<ref name="Kasey">{{ToppsCite|set=2016 Topps Star Wars: Rogue One Series 1|cardname=Paul Kasey as Edrio Two Tubes|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/142661/cid/11209460/2016-Topps-Star-Wars-Rogue-One-Series-1---Autographs-NNO-Paul-Kasey}}</ref> who remained uncredited for the role, while his eggmate Benthic was portrayed by [[Aidan Cook]].<ref name="Rogue One" /> The costume of Edrio "Two Tubes" was first revealed on [[July 20]], [[2016]] at [[Wikipedia:San Diego Comic-Con|San Diego Comic-Con]], along with an official description for the character which was posted on the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:Facebook|Facebook]] page on the same day.<ref name="Edrio Reveal">{{Facebook|username=StarWars|url=StarWars/posts/1212534632131469|name=Star Wars|date=2016-07-20|quote=From the Lucasfilm Pavilion at San Diego Comic-Con (2913), meet Rogue One’s Edrio Two Tubes: Edrio Two Tubes is a mercenary pilot who flies alongside his eggmate, Benthic. The two share the nickname derived from the breathing apparatus that allows their Tognath physiology to process oxygen atmospheres. Edrio’s home world Yar Togna was conquered and occupied by the Empire, forcing him to flee as a refugee. Desiring to strike back at the Empire, Edrio and Benthic have allied with Saw Gerrera.|archivefile=File:Edrio_reveal.png}}</ref> Concept art of one of the two "Tubes" for the film was done by [[Adam Brockbank]] and Fisher with the production codes "Two Tubes" and "Saw's Pilot".<ref name="ArtOfRO">''[[The Art of Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> Edrio was also featured in concept art by [[Jon McCoy]] for the scene in which [[Bodhi Rook]] is brought to the [[Partisans]].<ref name="McCoy">{{WebCite|url=https://jonmccoyart.com/blog/rogue-one-a-star-wars-story-character-intros|text=Rogue One &#124; Character Intros|work=[[Jon McCoy]]'s website|archivedate=20260504183613}}</ref>

Edrio re-appeared in the episode "[[In the Name of the Rebellion]]" of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Four|fourth season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' in [[2017]], where he was voiced by [[David Acord]],<ref name="In the Name of the Rebellion" /> who had previously voiced<ref name="Rebels Trivia">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/in-the-name-of-the-rebellion-trivia-gallery|text="In the Name of the Rebellion" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref> Benthic<ref name="Rogue One" /> in ''Rogue One''. In an earlier version of the episode's script, the position was originally supposed to be filled by a new [[Klatooinian]] character named [[Lorc]], but the role was changed to Edrio to better connect to the film.<ref name="Rebels Trivia" /> Edrio's model for the series was designed by [[Kilian Plunkett]].<ref name="ArtoRebels">''[[The Art of Star Wars Rebels]]''</ref>

One of the two Tognaths later appeared in the Anthology film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> released on [[May 25]], [[2018]],<ref name="Film Announced">{{SW|url=news/christopher-miller-and-phil-lord-to-helm-han-solo-anthology-film|text=Christopher Miller and Phil Lord to Helm Han Solo Anthology Film|nolive=1}}</ref> again portrayed by Cook<ref name="Dee Tails Tweet">{{Twitter|deetails|status/1018874896699609090|[[Dee Tails]]|date=2018-07-16|quote=Yeah, Regineer Teed, is me. Gelan Yees, Dilu Miah. Auromae Iselo, Derek Arnold. Tubes, Aiden Cook aka Two Tubes. Silvasu Fi, Stephanie Silva. Faddera Rabar, Barbara Fadden. Other than Warrick (Weazel) and Erin (Enfys) the others are stuntmen and women.|archivedate=20181030063907}}</ref> who remained uncredited for the role.<ref name="Solo" /> However, it was initially unclear which of the two eggmates appeared in the film as the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' identified him only as "Tubes".<ref name="TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> Co-writer [[Jon Kasdan]] initially identified the character as Edrio in a [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Tweet]] on [[June 24]], 2018,<ref name="Kasdan 1">{{Twitter|username=JonKasdan|url=1011071249668296706|name=[[Jon Kasdan]]|date=2018-06-24|quote=#RogueOne fan too. Hence critical appearance by Edrio "Two Tubes" who had dialogue which was ultimately cut. Wanted to establish the connection between Enfys and Saw, and the slowly developing rebellion across the galaxy.|archivedate=20191125041806|nolive=1}}</ref> but later clarified in early July after being asked by a fan that he had requested Edrio, but may have actually gotten Benthic instead.<ref name="Kasdan 2">{{Twitter|username=JonKasdan|url=1017135101048651783|name=[[Jon Kasdan]]|quote=I REQUESTED Edrio but MAY have actually gotten Bethnic. You never can tell with those Tognath eggmates, and since nobody on our crew SPOKE Tognath we couldn’t ask ‘im… He's probably on twitter thought (sic!) and will read this and clarify|nobackup=1|nolive=1}}</ref><ref name="Zirohu">{{Wordpress|subdomain=zirohu|name=Ziro.hu|url=2018/07/12/jon-kasdan-doesnt-know-which-tubes-is-in-the-solo-movie|text=Jon Kasdan doesn't know which Tubes is in the Solo movie|date=2018-07-12|archivedate=20260323193149}}</ref>  The film's [[Solo: A Star Wars Story: A Junior Novel|junior novel adaptation]], written by [[Joe Schreiber]]<ref name="Solo Junior">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> and published on [[September 4]], [[2018]],<ref name="Disney">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/solo-a-star-wars-story-junior-novel|text=Solo: A Star Wars Story Junior Novel|nolive=1}}</ref> then later confirmed that it was Benthic who appeared in the film.<ref name="Solo Junior" /> Kasdan stated that he originally wanted the mercenary [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] for the role of Enfys Nest's second in command, who would ultimately abandon her in the end of the film. Edrio later also re-appeared in the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' in [[2025]] with his brother,<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman?">{{Andor|Ever Been to Ghorman?}}</ref> after Benthic had previously already appeared in [[Andor Season 1|season one]].<ref name="Narkina 5 EG">{{SW|url=series/andor/andor-episode-8-narkina-5|text="Narkina 5" Episode Guide &#124; Andor}}</ref> Once again, only Cook was credited for the portrayal of "Two Tubes" without any further clarifications which of the two he portrayed.<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman?" /> However, Cook had previously already portrayed Benthic in the first season.<ref name="Narkina 5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref>
