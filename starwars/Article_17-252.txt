'''Article 17-252''', also called '''A-17-252''', was a procedural rule of the [[Imperial Senate|Galactic Senate]] which stated that a [[Senior Senator|senior senator]] may yield the floor to another senior [[senator]] freely in case of emergency. In [[2 BBY]], Senator [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] invoked the rule and gave the senate floor to Senator [[Mon Mothma]].

==Description==
{{Quote|A Senior Senator, in the case of emergency, may yield the floor freely, without interruption, to another Senior Senator.|[[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] invoking A-17 252|<ref name="Welcome to the Rebellion">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref>}}
Article 17-252, shortened to A-17 252, was an article of [[Imperial Senate]] Protocol proposed directly by the [[Imperial Ruling Council]]. It stated that, in the case of an emergency, a [[Senior Senator]] would be allowed to yield the floor to another Senior Senator freely and without interruption. This circumvented the [[Chair of the Senate]], who would usually be the one to recognize [[Senator]]s based on predetermined agendas, and allowed any Senator to take the floor and speak however they wanted.<ref name="Welcome to the Rebellion" />

==History==
Article 17-252 was part of the latest ruling on Senate protocol<ref name="Welcome to the Rebellion" /> as of [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|209}}</ref> proposed directly by the Emperor's Council. In that same year, Bail Organa, the Senior Senator from [[Alderaan]], invoked 17-252 during [[Escape from the Senate|the emergency session]] following the [[Ghorman Massacre]] to allow Senior Senator [[Mon Mothma]] of [[Chandrila]] to take the floor and make her speech despite not being featured in the day's agenda. With the [[Oathkeeper]] being specifically instructed not to let her talk, the Article was the only way for her to bypass Imperial restrictions. Ultimately, Mothma was able to take the floor and condemn [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] as the one responsible for the attack on Ghorman, which she called a genocide.<ref name="Welcome to the Rebellion" />
