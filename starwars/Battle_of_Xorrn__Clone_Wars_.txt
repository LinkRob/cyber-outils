Throughout the duration of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Friends" /> between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> numerous '''battles''' were fought on the [[planet]] [[Xorrn]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Not long after the conflict's [[First Battle of Geonosis|opening battle]], the [[Galactic Republic]] discovered illegally established [[battle droid]] [[Factory|factories]] underneath the planet's surface, constructed years prior by the [[Geonosian]] [[species]]. Due to their discovery, Xorrn was transformed into a war zone for the duration of the conflict.<ref name="Friends" />

However, as the war reached its final [[Standard year|year]], the Republic had suffered countless casualties in their attacks on Xorrn and the factories. Ultimately deciding that the planet was no longer worth further losses, the Republic enacted an [[orbital bombardment]] of the world. As a result, numerous factories were destroyed alongside several [[warship]]s of the Republic and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Friends" />
