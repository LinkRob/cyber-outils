'''Etorasp spaceport''' was a small [[Spaceport/Legends|spaceport]] on the planet [[Etorasp]]. It included a trading post and a medical facility. [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] landed at the spaceport on a mission to rescue [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] [[General/Legends|general]] [[Marutz]] from [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] imprisonment.
