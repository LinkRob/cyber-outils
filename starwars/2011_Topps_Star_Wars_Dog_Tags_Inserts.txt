'''2011 Topps ''Star Wars'' Dog Tags Inserts''' is a collection of cards released by [[Topps]] in [[2011]] and packaged alongside the [[Topps Star Wars Dog Tags|Topps ''Star Wars'' Dog Tags]]. Each card depicts a major character from the [[Original trilogy|Original]] and [[Prequel trilogy|Prequel trilogies]], and the set has a total of 24 cards.<ref name="TCDB" />
