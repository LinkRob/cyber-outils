'''Exastone''' was a type of [[Color|gray]] [[stone]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Sith assassin]] [[Mae-ho Aniseya]] used a set of [[Mae's knives|knives]] with hardened exastone tips.<ref name="Visual Guide" />

{{Substance-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Exastone first appeared simultaneously in "[[Lost / Found]]"<ref name="Lost/Found">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref> and "[[Revenge / Justice]],"<ref name="Revenge/Justice">{{Acolyte|Revenge / Justice}}</ref> the first and second episodes of the [[2024]] [[Disney+]] live-action series [[Star Wars: The Acolyte|''The Acolyte'']],<ref name="Lost/Found" /><ref name="Revenge/Justice" /> which aired on [[June 4]], 2024.<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref> It was first depicted in a trailer shown exclusively at [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]]<ref name="Celebration">{{CelebrationTrailer|''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''|Celebration Europe (2023)}}</ref> during the [[Lucasfilm]] Studio Showcase on [[April 7]], [[2023]].<ref name="Studio Showcase">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-lucasfilm-studio-showcase|text=SWCE 2023: 11 Highlights from Lucasfilm's Studio Showcase}}</ref> Exastone was identified in a [[StarWars.com]] article published on [[April 10]], [[2025]] that showed preview pages of ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', a reference book by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] that was released on [[June 17]], 2025.<ref name="Master of Evil">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-lucasfilm-publishing-panel|text=''Star Wars: Master of Evil'', a New Darth Vader Novel, Turns the Page on the Sith Lord}}</ref>
