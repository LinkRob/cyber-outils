'''Cantina Crush''' was a [[Temperature|cool]] and [[cream]]y beverage served at [[the Labyrinth]] [[cantina]] on the astronomical object [[Kelada]], where the [[bartender]] [[Lorana]] used slices of local [[fruit]] that made the beverage tart and sweet. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] drank the beverage at the Labyrinth during a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] he took between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]]. After Lorana gave him the recipe, he included it in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]''.

==Description==
Cantina Crush was a [[cream]]y beverage served [[Temperature|cool]] and made using slices of [[fruit]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> The [[human]]<ref name="Comic">[[Star Wars Adventures Free Comic Book Day 2019|''Star Wars Adventures'' Free Comic Book Day 2019]]</ref> [[bartender]] [[Lorana]] used a local fruit for the drink that was as sweet and tart as a [[citrom]] when serving the beverage in [[the Labyrinth]], a [[spaceport]] [[cantina]] on the astronomical object [[Kelada]] in the [[Colonies]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==History==
{{Quote|When I got to Kelada, I intended to search the local marketplaces for unusual ingredients and new recipe ideas. But plans don't always unfold exactly how you expect. Truth is, I never made it further than the spaceport's cantina, a joint called the Labyrinth.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
[[File:LabyrinthKelada-SWAFCBD2019.jpg|thumb|right|300px|alt=A Rodian and a stormtrooper stand in the dusty streets of Kelada outside a building with a sign reading Labyrinth in Aurebesh.|Cantina Crush was served in the Labyrinth.]]
During a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] visited Kelada intending to search for unusual ingredients and new recipe ideas in the local marketplaces; however, he never made it farther than the Labyrinth, as after Lorana served him a Cantina Crush, he continued ordering more until the cantina closed for the [[Standard day|day]]. Tuggs gave the bartender a generous tip when settling the bill, and in return she gave him a [[napkin]] with the recipe for Cantina Crush [[Writing|written]] on it. Tuggs, believing he got the better end of the deal despite how much he had spent, then included the recipe in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> which was published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cantina Crush was created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the drink makes use of quartered [[Wikipedia:Lime (fruit)|limes]], [[Wikipedia:Water|water]], [[Wikipedia:White sugar|granulated sugar]], sweetened [[Wikipedia:Condensed milk|condensed milk]], [[Wikipedia:Ice cube|ice]], and additional limes for garnish.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
