'''Copulan shadow-warriors''' were [[Soldier|warriors]]<ref name="Glass" /> who originated from<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[Copul system]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During a [[Mission to Metagos|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Metagos]]<ref name="Glass" /> around [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]'' take place one [[Standard week|week]] after the [[Funeral of Qui-Gon Jinn|funeral]] of [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[32 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] infiltrated the [[Floating Palace (Metagos)|Floating Palace]], the headquarters of the [[Chulok's organization|gangster]] [[Chulok]], and mentally compared himself to a Copulan shadow-warrior as he moved through the darkness.<ref name="Glass" />

==Behind the scenes==
Copulan shadow-warriors were mentioned in the [[2024]] novel ''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]'', written by [[Steven Barnes]].<ref name="Glass" />
