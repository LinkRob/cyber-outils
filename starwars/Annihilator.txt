The '''''Annihilator''''' was a [[Star Destroyer]] in the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. In [[4 ABY]], it served in the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[fleet]] defending the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. When the [[Battle station|station]] was [[Battle of Endor|attacked]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], Imperial [[Commander]] [[Ciena Ree]] flew past the ''Annihilator'' to ensure its [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] was not attacked.

==Description==
A [[Star Destroyer]], the ''Annihilator'' featured a [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]].<ref name="LS" />

==History==
In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the [[Battle of Endor]] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> during the [[Galactic Civil War]], the ''Annihilator'' was part of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[fleet]] defending the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]], an Imperial [[battle station]] situated over the [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. At that [[time]], the [[Space station|station]] and its fleet were [[Battle of Endor|attacked]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], who hoped to destroy the [[Death Star]].<ref name="LS" />

During the battle, Imperial [[Commander]] [[Ciena Ree]] flew her [[TIE/IN interceptor]] around the ''Annihilator''{{'s}} bridge area, aware that a [[rebel pilot]] could crash their [[Starship|ship]] into it. However, she then received the information that the [[Death Star's shield generator]] on Endor had been destroyed, opening up the station for attack from the rebels. Ree then witnessed the rebels destroy the Imperial [[flagship]] ''[[Executor]]'', the next nearest Star Destroyer, and, just as she was about to attack a [[Mon Calamari]] [[Star Cruiser]], all Imperial forces were ordered to retreat, forcing her to swoop away from the ''Annihilator''. Shortly after, the rebels successfully destroyed the Death Star.<ref name="LS" />

==Behind the scenes==
