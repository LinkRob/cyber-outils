'''Crystalline reformatter''' were a type of device consisting of a [[Color|silver]] metallic cylinder with one tapered end with a protrusion. A second curved protrusion extended from one side of the device out in front of the first protrusion. The reformatters also had two switches on their body both marked in blue.<ref name="TROS VD" />

The [[Jedi]]-in-[[Jedi training|training]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] owned a crystalline reformatter and kept it on her workbench at the [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|Resistance base]] on the [[moon]] [[Ajan Kloss]]. The equipment on the workbench was used for flexing her mechanical skills, studying the [[Sacred Jedi texts|jedi texts]] and repairing the [[Skywalker lightsaber]] by healing its [[kyber crystal]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] to one [[Standard year|year]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], the events of ''The Rise of Skywalker'', which includes the appearance of the heating pedestal, must occur in [[35 ABY]].</ref> the reformatter was among the equipment laid out on the workbench when the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' returned to the base.<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A crystalline reformatter first appeared in the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="TROS" /> It was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
