A '''company''' was a ground [[infantry]] unit. In the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], each [[regiment]] consisted of sixteen companies of 144 [[clone trooper]]s led by their respective [[Clone Captain]]s, with each divided into four [[platoon]]s.<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> In the [[Stormtrooper Corps]], a company consisted of four platoons, giving one a total of 200 [[stormtrooper]]s. A [[battalion]] consisted of four Imperial companies.<ref name="SuperGraphic">''[[Star Wars Super Graphic: A Visual Guide to a Galaxy Far, Far Away]]''</ref>

[[Cloning|Clone]] [[Captain]] [[Rex]] commanded the [[501st Legion]]'s [[Torrent Company]] and [[332nd Company]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Other Grand [[Army]] companies included the [[212th Attack Battalion]]'s [[Ghost Company]],<ref name="Cody gallery">{{Databank|clone-commander-cody-biography-gallery|Clone Commander Cody Biography Gallery}}</ref> the [[41st Elite Corps]]' [[Green Company]],<ref name="CoD">{{TCW|Cloak of Darkness}}</ref> and [[Tango Company]].<ref name="Brain Invaders Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/brain-invaders-episode-guide|text="Brain Invaders" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>

The [[Alliance Army]] also used companies as [[infantry]] units, such as [[61st Mobile Infantry|Twilight Company]], though their numbers were less standardized.<ref name="Twilight">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref>
