'''''Darth Vader'' 15''' is the fifteenth issue of the [[2020]] comic book series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']], and is part of the [[Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters]] crossover event the issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]] and [[Jason Keith]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[August 25]], [[2021]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE ASSASSIN'S CHOICE Mere months ago, OCHI OF BESTOON was the assassin who dared to take on DARTH VADER himself in the fiery lava pits of MUSTAFAR. Now Ochi serves Vader as his
most loyal agent during the WAR OF THE BOUNTY HUNTERS. Who is Ochi of Bestoon? What are his true goals? And how have the secrets of EXEGOL shaped him forever? Also featuring the next great reveal about BOKKU and the Hutt Council!''
