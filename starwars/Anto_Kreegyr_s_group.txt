'''Anto Kreegyr's group''' was a [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|rebel cell]] led by [[Anto Kreegyr]],<ref name="Nobody's Listing!" /> a former militant of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Narkina 5" /> as part of the [[neo-Separatist Coalition]].<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> With the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] learning of Kreegyr's plan for the [[Spellhaus attack|raid]] on the [[Spellhaus Imperial power station]] and [[Luthen Rael]] being unwilling to warn Kreegyr to protect the rebel spy [[Lonni Jung]],<ref name="Daughter of Ferrix">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> Kreegyr and his forces were wiped out in the battle,<ref name="Rix Road">{{Andor|Rix Road (episode)}}</ref> although official numbers varied on the number of Kreegyr's associates killed in the battle, with the Rebel Alliance archives suggesting the associates numbered fifty while Saw Gerrera's records suggested thirty.<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>
