'''EG-4''' was a [[EG-series power droid]] owned by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. While the Alliance was based in [[Echo Base]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]], EG-4 provided power to the [[starship]]s and machinery around the base.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> During the [[Battle of Hoth]], all personnel were forced to evacuate Echo Base and flee the planet.<ref name="ESB" />
