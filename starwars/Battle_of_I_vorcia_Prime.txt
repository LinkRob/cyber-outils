The '''Battle of I'vorcia Prime''' was a battle the [[Galactic Empire]] participated in during the [[Imperial Era]] on [[I'vorcia Prime]]. The battle resulted in the destruction of a large part of the [[planet]]'s unique ecosystem, although it was later turned into a reserve where [[Imperial officer]]s hunted exotic animals.<ref name="GE 1" />
