A '''conductive coil''' was a device used in [[starship]]s to stabilize dangerous ion currents.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] paid a starship [[Technician|mecahnic]] one thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], and supplied them with ten [[cargo signature scrambler]]s to install a series of conductive coils in the hold of the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her [[EML-850 light freighter]]. The upgrade allowed her and her companion to more easily [[smuggler|smuggle]] ionized cargo for the [[Miyuki Trade League]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Conductive coils appeared in the [[canon]] continuity in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> In the expansion, players could purchase "Ion cycler" upgrade for their ship to reduce duration of unsustainable ion cargo during smuggling contracts.<ref name="Outlaws" />
