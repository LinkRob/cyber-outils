The '''Akuria system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Oplovis sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It notably contained the<ref name="Appendix" /> astronomical object [[Akuria]].<ref name="Star Wars: Scum and Villainy" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Year Ref">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'' establishes that this report was generated in the [[Standard year|year]] 7980 C.R.C., the same year that [[Han Solo]] was frozen in [[carbonite]]. Given that Solo was frozen in carbonite during the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' established took place in [[3 ABY]], it can be concluded that 7980 C.R.C. is equivalent to 3 ABY.</ref> the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] identified Akuria as a location with potential [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] activity.<ref name="Star Wars: Scum and Villainy" />
