Elder '''Chettkap''' was a [[Jawa]] [[Gender|male]] who served as the [[chief]] of a [[Chettkap's tribe|Jawa tribe]] on the [[planet]] [[Arvala-7]]. Around [[9 ABY]], the tribe stole parts from the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]]'s [[gunship]], the ''[[Razor Crest]]''. When Djarin then went to negotiate with the Jawas, Chettkap demanded a trade for his [[starship]] parts, soon agreeing to accept a [[mudhorn egg]] as a trade. The Jawas delivered the Mandalorian near a [[mudhorn cave]] and had just given up waiting for him when he later returned with the [[egg]] as promised. Chettkap accepted the [[mudhorn]] egg and began feasting on its yolk with some of his tribe members.

==Biography==
{{Quote|There must be something else.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''[After speaking with his tribe members] We will require the egg. Bring us the egg.|translated=1|Kuiil and Chettkap|<ref name="Chapter 2" />}}
During the [[New Republic Era|era]] of the [[New Republic]], Elder Chettkap<ref name="Mando Handbook" /> was the [[chief]] of an [[Jawa|Offworld Jawa]] [[Chettkap's tribe|tribe]]<ref name="Chapter 2" /> on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Arvala-7]], keeping order on the tribe's [[Unidentified sandcrawler|sandcrawler]].<ref name="Mando Handbook" /> Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|2|Chettkap's appearance}}</ref> the Jawa elder oversaw the tribe as it dismantled and stole pieces of the ''[[Razor Crest]]'', a [[gunship]] used by the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]]. When stumbling upon the scrapping of his [[Starship|vessel]], the Mandalorian open fired on the Jawa tribe, disintegrating a few of the Jawas, and pursued the fleeing sandcrawler until he was incapacitated by its crew.<ref name="Chapter 2" />

[[File:Arvala-7-Jawa-Tribe-TheMandalorian.png|thumb|left|300px|Chettkap (center) and his tribe as they negotiate with Din Djarin and Kuiil]]
The next [[Standard day|day]], Chettkap and his tribe set up camp in a valley, where they inspected their machinery parts. Djarin then arrived with the [[Ugnaught]] [[Moisture farmer|vapor farmer]] [[Kuiil]]. After the Jawas had the Mandalorian leave his weapons aboard Kuiil's [[Repulsorcart|hovercart]], Chettkap greeted and sat down with Kuiil, Djarin joining them both as other Jawas gathered behind the elder. When the Ugnaught discussed the Jawas returning the stolen parts from the ''Razor Crest'', Chettkap proposed a trade for the [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter's]] [[beskar]] [[Mandalorian armor|armor]]. Djarin refused to make a trade as the parts had been belonged to him, attempting to reiterate the fact in [[Jawaese]].<ref name="Chapter 2" />

Chettkap mocked the Mandalorian's attempt as the Jawa [[language]], jokingly comparing it to a [[Wookiee]]'s [[Shyriiwook|dialect]], and so the aggravated bounty hunter responded by firing his [[flamethrower]] in the Jawas' direction. Kuiil asked what else the Mandalorian could trade to the Jawas, to which Chettkap pointed over to an [[alien]] [[Din Grogu|child]] on Kuiil's cart, though the Mandalorian refused to trade that either. Kuiil asking again, the chief huddled with his Jawa comrades and they agreed to make Djarin supply the the [[Mudhorn egg|egg]] of a [[mudhorn]] for the parts to his ship.<ref name="Chapter 2" />

[[File:ChettkapEatsEgg-TMCh2.png|thumb|right|270px|Chettkap was the first to taste the mudhorn egg.]]
Djarin agreeing to retrieve the [[egg]], the Jawa tribe [[transport]]ed him near a [[mudhorn cave]], and they awaited his return during the day. When the bounty hunter failed to return, the Jawas chose to leave, preparing the sandcrawler for departure. That was when the Mandalorian arrived with the mudhorn egg, and Chettkap and some of his tribe members exited the vehicle to take it. The chief received the egg from Djarin's hands and held it up before his comrades before using his [[knife]] to cut it open while another Jawa held it. Trying the yolk of the Mudhorn egg, Chettkap then allowed his tribe members to join him, and they feasted on the egg's contents. Djarin and Kuiil soon parted from the tribe with the parts for the ''Razor Crest''.<ref name="Chapter 2" />

==Personality and traits==
A Jawa male, Chettkap had glowing [[Color|red]] [[eye]]s<ref name="Mando Handbook" /> as opposed to the yellow eyes which were typical of Jawas from the planet [[Tatooine]].<ref name="DB Jawa">{{Databank|jawa|Jawa}}</ref> He spoke Jawaese, and mocked Din Djarin's difficulty in speaking the language.<ref name="Chapter 2" />

==Equipment==
Like other Jawas,<ref name="DB Jawa" /> Chettkap [[Clothing|wore]] [[Color|brown]] [[robe]]s which concealed almost all of his body. He also wore a special necklace that the chief wore as a symbol of his leadership, which included a knife.<ref name="Mando Handbook" /> He wore a [[squill-leather]] bandolier and attached to it his recurved kukri machete.<ref name = "Visual">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref>
