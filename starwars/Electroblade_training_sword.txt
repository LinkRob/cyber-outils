'''Electroblade training swords''',<ref name="TCG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>  or '''electroblades''', were a type of [[sword]] whose tapered blades could deliver a nasty shock and were able to deflect [[blaster]] bolts, though only to a limited extent. The [[Jedi Order]] used them as training weapons, along with [[wood]]en [[Bokken saber|staves]].<ref name="Obi-Wan and Anakin" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], the Jedi Order utilized [[DD-1 hover droid]]s equipped with electroblade training swords to train [[Jedi Initiate]]s at the [[Tenoo Jedi Temple]].<ref name="AotTD">{{YJA|Attack of the Training Droids}}</ref>
