'''Administrative Temple Garden 313A''' was a [[garden]] located in [[Imperial City]] on the<ref name="Vader 14" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Coruscant">{{Databank|coruscant|Coruscant}}</ref> [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Vader 14" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Darth Vader (2020) 14|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 14]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> [[Sub-Administrator]] [[Sly Moore]] and the members of the [[Umbaran's Court]] met with the [[bounty hunter]] [[IG-88B|IG-88]] in the garden where the [[droid]] informed them he had failed in his [[Plot to assassinate Darth Vader|attempt]] to [[Death|kill]] the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], and that he intended to terminate his agreement with them. After this, Vader arrived and struck down the droid, which caused the members of the court to flee.<ref name="Vader 14" />
