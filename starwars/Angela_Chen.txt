'''Angela Chen''' is a concept artist who provided conceptual artwork for ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'', where she created concept art for [[Suu Lawquane]], [[Fauja]], a [[chain code]],<ref name="Cut and Run EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/cut-and-run-episode-guide|text="Cut and Run" Episode Guide}}</ref> and [[Bib Fortuna]].<ref name="Rampage EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/rampage-episode-guide|text="Rampage" Episode Guide}}</ref>
