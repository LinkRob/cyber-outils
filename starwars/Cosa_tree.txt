'''Cosa trees''' were a type of [[tree]] found on the [[Deep Core]] [[planet]] [[Cosia]]. They were massive trees that covered the surface, reaching high into the sky and with palm leaves over a hundred [[meter]]s long. The [[Cosian]]s, the native [[species]] of Cosia, built [[City|cities]] within the trees. The buildings were connected by etched [[wood]] walkways.<ref name="DoH" />
