The following events took place in or around the [[Standard year|year]] '''234 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3043 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''215 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7743''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Imri Cantaros]] becomes the [[Padawan]] of [[Douglas Sunvale]].<ref name="Imri" />
*[[Lina Soh]] is [[Supreme Chancellor election, 234 BBY|elected]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Timelines" />
*A [[Blight|catastrophe]] at the [[Badlands (Siline)|Badlands]] on [[Siline]] occurs around this year.<ref name="Catastrophe-Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}} The novel establishes that the [[Blight|catastrophe]] at the [[Badlands (Siline)|Badlands]] occurred a bit more than a hundred years ago, which corresponds to around 234 BBY.</ref>

==Deaths==
*[[Nan's father]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Nan">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[232 BBY]], states that [[Nan's father]] died about two [[Standard year|years]] before the events of the novel; therefore, Nan's father died in 234 BBY.</ref>
