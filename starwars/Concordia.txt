{{Quote|The Concordian moon is a province, with its own governor.|Duchess [[Satine Kryze]]|<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" />}}
'''Concordia''' was a [[moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]]. It was [[Governor|governed]] by [[Pre Vizsla]].<ref name="Databank" /> The [[Death Watch]], which was in fact led by Vizsla, used the [[Concordian mines|abandoned mines]] on Concordia as [[Deathwatch hideout|secret hideouts]].<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" />

==History==
{{Quote|I thought Concordia was an agricultural settlement.''"<br />"''Before the end of our wars, they turned it into a mining base. The sheer number of mining facilities here nearly destroyed our forests. They're finally growing back.|[[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and Satine Kryze|<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" />}}
[[File:Concordia_Orbit.png|thumb|left|200px|Concordia seen from [[Mandalore]]'s orbit]]
Prior to the end of Mandalore's wars, insurgents turned Concordia into a [[mining]] base. The mines almost destroyed the [[moon]]'s [[forest]]. However, when the war ended, the mines were abandoned and the forests began to grow back. After the [[Great Clan Wars|Mandalorian Civil War]], all of Mandalore's warriors were [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] to Concordia by the [[New Mandalorians]] and were presumed to have [[Death|died]] out.<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" />

By the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Concordia had become a province of Mandalore with its own [[governor]]. However, Concordia's governor, [[Pre Vizsla]] of [[House Vizsla]]'s [[Clan Vizsla]], was secretly leading the splinter group, [[Death Watch]] from the [[Concordian mines|mines]].<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" /> [[Din Djarin]], who belonged to the [[Children of the Watch]], grew up on Concordia.<ref name="Chapter 18">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 18: The Mines of Mandalore}}</ref> The Children of the Watch later survived the [[Night of a Thousand Tears]] by being cloistered on Concordia.<ref name="BOBFChapter5" />
