'''''Droid Diary''''' was a personal [[Book|diary]] kept by the [[protocol droid]] [[AD-3]] in which she recorded her personal thoughts. [[Gender|She]] kept the book aboard the [[Pelta-class frigate|''Pelta''-class frigate]] ''[[Athylia]]'' and was chastised by [[Jedi Master]] [[Kelleran Beq]] for speaking of it at inappropriate times.<ref name="JTC 1" />
