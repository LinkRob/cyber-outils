The '''Chambers''' existed in the [[city]] of [[Diurna]] on the [[planet]] [[Aubadas]] for the use of the [[Katikoot]] [[Grand Council]].<ref name="HiddenCity" />

==Description==
It was a large, grand, administrative building with a pillared entrance. The interior was quite dark and shadowy, the floor made with slabs of polished stone. There were grand tapestries on the walls depicting various historical moments for the Kakikoot species. The ceiling was domed and embellished with cultural patterns and swirls. To enter the chambers, there was a large spiral staircase watched by two Kakikoot guards. The chambers themselves were a suite of rooms connected to a primary hallway.<ref name="HiddenCity" />
