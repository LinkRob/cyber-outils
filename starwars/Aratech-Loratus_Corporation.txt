'''Aratech-Loratus Corporation''' was a company which created vehicles for the [[First Order]]. Three [[Standard decade|decades]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], two of their products were the [[Light Infantry Utility Vehicle]]<ref name="Cross" /> and the [[125-Z treadspeeder bike]].<ref name="ROSVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The corporation's name is mentioned in reference books for the [[2015]] saga film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].
