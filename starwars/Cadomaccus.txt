'''Cadomaccus''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Zabrak]] with [[Color|Black]] and red skin and maroon eyes. A failed initiate of the [[Kouhun (order)|Kouhun]] [[assassin]]s, he waited on the desolate [[planet]] [[Mataou]] preparing a fortified position.<ref name="Uprising" /> Between [[4 ABY]] and [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Deathdate" /> [[the Smuggler]], a male [[human]], was building an [[Uprising|alliance]] to fight [[Ubrik Adelhard's Imperial remnant]], a breakaway faction of the [[Galactic Empire]] within the [[Anoat sector]]. The Smuggler sought to obtain favor with the Kouhun and had progressed through the first two of three trials&mdash;the test of meat and the test of steel&mdash;and was required to complete the final test, the test of spirit, having been [[poison]]ed prior to the first test and progressively getting weaker.<ref name="Uprising" />

This final test, given by the Kouhun leader [[Deathstick]], required the Smuggler to either kill or succumb to the poison and die. Deathstick directed the Smuggler to Mataou, noting that Cadomaccus had failed his trials. [[Loathe]], a member of the Kouhun, briefed the Smuggler on Cadomaccus' situation, warning of the failed initiate's defenses and Cadomaccus' belief that to overcome and kill the Smuggler would grant him membership with the assassin group, despite his previous failings. These defenses, consisting of [[Hutt Guard Droid]]s, [[Hutt security droid]]s and ancient [[security droid]]s, were no match for the Smuggler, who progressed to the Zabrak's position on the rooftop of one of the planet's buildings. The Smuggler pleaded with Cadomaccus, requesting the Zabrak trust him and that he had a plan to save both of them, as the Smuggler did not see himself as a murderer despite having killed, considering those kills to be necessary. Refusing the Smuggler's offer and armed with [[grenade]]s and a [[blaster pistol]], Cadomaccus initiated combat, with the two exchanging fire on the rooftop until the failed initiate was killed by their successor. Passing this test, Deathstick noted that the group would not have killed Cadomaccus if he had joined forces with his opponent, noting that the slain individual had no restraint when it came to killing, likening him to a butcher. The Smuggler would go on to receive the [[Antidote]] to the poison and gain the favor of the Kouhun.<ref name="Uprising" />
