'''Carbonite 'n' Cream Pie''' was a type of [[Diet|vegetarian]] [[terrine]] served as a [[Foodstuff|dessert]] by the pastry [[chef]]s of the [[Deep Core]] [[Planet|world]] [[Empress Teta]]. The [[cream]]y, [[cookie]]-filled dish had a [[Color|brown]] base with a white layer and an uneven brown topping. Carbonite 'n' Cream Pie was served [[Temperature|cold]], and the chefs on Empress Teta rapidly blast-chilled it during preparation by dipping it into a miniature [[carbon-freezing chamber]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

The [[Artiodac]] chef [[Strono Tuggs]] stopped on Empress Teta during a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]]<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> and learned of the chef's creative application of [[carbonite]] in the kitchen. He quickly ate up a plateful of Carbonite 'n' Cream Pie that he was served and later included a recipe for the dish in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> which was published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> He assured readers of the book that the dish could also be prepared using a regular freezer should they not have carbonite available.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Carbonite 'n' Cream Pie was created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the dessert makes use of whole and crushed [[Wikipedia:Chocolate|chocolate]] [[Wikipedia:Sandwich cookie|sandwich cookies]], [[Wikipedia:Chocolate ice cream|chocolate ice cream]], melted [[Wikipedia:Dark chocolate|dark chocolate]], and softened [[Wikipedia:Vanilla ice cream|vanilla ice cream]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
