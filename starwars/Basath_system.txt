The '''Basath system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained the [[moon]] [[Basath]].<ref name="The Dark Mirror" /> It was situated at the coordinates J-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />
