During the [[Galactic Civil War]] in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Twi'lek]] [[New Republic]] [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] and her forces aboard the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]'', who had continued their [[Hunt for Shadow Wing|hunt]] for the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], managed to track the [[fighter wing]] and the [[bulk freighter]] ''[[Yadeez]]'' to the [[Red Yars system]], where they engaged one another in a brief '''battle'''. However, the fighter wing managed to escape the [[Star system|system]] shortly thereafter when [[New Republic pilot]]s [[Chass na Chadic]] and [[Boyvech Toons]] loosed shots, but neither were in range when the fighter wing made the jump to [[lightspeed]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
