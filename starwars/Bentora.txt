'''Bentora''' was a terrestrial astronomical object in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Bentora system]]. Upon the [[Deployment of the stormseeds|formation]] of the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s' [[Occlusion Zone]]<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Bentora became located in Nihil [[Realspace|space]] behind its [[Stormwall]] barrier that prevented [[hyperspace]] travel. While stranded in the Occlusion Zone, the [[journalist]] [[Rhil Dairo]] visited Bentora with her [[cam droid]], [[T-9]], where she interviewed victims of recent Nihil raids. During Dairo's [[time]] on Bentora, she was captured by the Nihil and seperated from T-9. Eventually, the journalist escaped the Nihil<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> and at some point after, reunited with her [[droid]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
