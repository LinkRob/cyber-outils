The '''Fondor Shipyards''' above the [[planet]] [[Fondor]] was a [[shipyard]] that consisted of at least nineteen large, wheel-shaped [[Imperial dockyard]]s that featured multiple [[hangar]]s as well as several docking bays for [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==History==
After the [[Proclamation of the New Order|founding]] of the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> in [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[shipyard]]s were converted from civilian shipyards, to the manfacture of [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]].<ref name="ROUVG" /> Prior to the [[Battle of Scarif]], [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] agents were located near the Fondor Shipyards, and reported to [[General]] [[Airen Cracken]] about their findings.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics}}
At some point during the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Fondor Shipyards came under attack by Rebel forces lead by [[Major]] [[Shin]]. While the Rebels attempted to destroy an ''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer docked at the shipyards for repair, [[Colonel]] [[Brenna]] and her fighters mobilized to defend it.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
{{EndGame}}

After the fateful [[Battle of Endor]], which saw the destruction of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] and the [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]],<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> [[Inferno Squad]] was sent to [[Fondor Station|one]] of the [[shipyard]]s to protect [[climate disruption array]]s for [[Operation: Cinder]] by [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]] and one of the late Emperor's [[Sentinel (Contingency)|sentinel]] [[droid]]s. Shortly after, [[Fondor Station|one]] of the [[Space station|stations]] was [[Attack on Fondor|attacked]] by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Starship|vessels]], in which they attempted to hijack the [[Star Destroyer]] [[Dauntless (Imperial-class)|''Dauntless'']], which was carrying the climate disruption arrays. The attack failed, and Alliance forces were defeated.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

Shortly before the [[Battle of Jakku]], the Shipyards were once again [[Battle of Fondor Shipyards|attacked]], this time by the [[New Republic]], the Rebel Alliance's successor state. The battle resulted in a New Republic victory, and the shipyards were taken.<ref name="The Ghost Ship" />

After the [[Battle of Crait]], the [[First Order]] dispatched at least thirteen [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers]] to the Fondor Shipyards after hearing rumors of Resistance activity. After an interrogation, one of the [[Unidentified human male (Fondor)|crewmembers]] of the shipyards admitted that the Resistance had arrived, but he refused to offer them any assistance. [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] reminded him that simply having had contact with the Resistance was an act of [[treason]], and thus this "contact" with the enemy gave Ren an excuse to formally put the shipyards under First Order control, something the "lenient" Empire should have done. The crewmember protested that Fondor had always been independently run, which even the Empire respected, but Ren [[Death|executed]] him.<ref name="Allegiance 2" />
