The '''Chandrilan black-horned net spider''' were [[spider]]s found on [[Chandrila]]. Following the [[Battle of Endor]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] likened the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor]]'s contingency plan to that of the Chandrilan black-horned net spider as they weave many intricate traps.<ref name="TPATS" />
