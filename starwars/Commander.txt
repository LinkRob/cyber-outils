'''Commander''' was the [[Military rank|rank]] or title of an [[officer]] with authority over a body of [[troop]]s or a military operation. In [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]], [[soldier]]s served under commanders in various military organizations.

==History==
The [[Republic Military]] made use of [[Clone Commander]]s<ref name="Wolffe databank">{{Databank|clone-commander-wolffe|Clone Commander Wolffe}}</ref> such as [[Cody]]<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref> and [[Jedi Commander]]s during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Storm over Ryloth" /> The [[Confederacy military]] had commanders within its ranks as well,<ref name="WW" /> with [[General]] [[Grievous]] additionally overseeing the [[Separatist Droid Army]] as [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Martial Commander]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> During the Clone Wars, [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] claimed he served as a commander in the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" /> despite actually being a [[Senior Captain]].<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref>

The [[Imperial Army]],<ref name="ANH" /> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]],<ref name="ESB" /> the [[New Republic]],<ref name="Aftermath" /> the [[First Order military]],<ref name="The Children from Tehar" /> and the [[Resistance]] all had commanders within their ranks.<ref name="Episode VII" /> After failing in the [[Battle of Panisia]], Imperial Commander [[Ellian Zahra]] found her way to a [[Zahra's raider crew|raider crew]], who agreed to serve under her in return for her tactical insight. As leader of the raiders, she continued to use the title of commander, but she longed to return to the Empire.<ref name="SW 2020 49" /> In the case of the First Order military, Commanders also were identified by wearing teal uniforms similar to that of [[Major]]s and [[Colonel]]s.<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
