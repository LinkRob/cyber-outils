The '''Clew''' was a periodic event held by [[exogorth]]s, also known as space slugs. During a Clew, the [[Hum]] through which the exogorths communicated changed and the creatures congregated. Then, they would show off the creatures that they kept within their stomachs, and eventually would [[Reproduction|mate]] with one another.<ref name="No Cave">{{StoryCite|story=This Is No Cave|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>
