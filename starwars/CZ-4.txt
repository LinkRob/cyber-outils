'''CZ-4''' was a [[CZ-series secretary/business communications droid]] that belonged to the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] along with [[CZ-3]].<ref name="Build R2 9" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], which include the appearance of CZ-4, to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the broken body of the [[secretary droid]] was piled in a corner<ref name="Build R2 9" /> of the [[droid]] "assessment" room<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> in the dungeon of [[Boba Fett's Palace|the Hutt's palace]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] when the [[supervisor]] [[EV-9D9]] met the new droids [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] there.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> CZ-4 was missing its [[Limb|legs]] and had its right hand attached to its left arm,<ref name="Build R2 9" /> but it was later repaired and stood among a crowd that watched the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] dragged before Jabba after the [[Jedi]] had [[Death|slain]] [[Pateesa|Jabba's rancor]].<ref name="ROTJ" />
