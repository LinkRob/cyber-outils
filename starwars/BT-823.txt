'''BT-823''' was a [[bounty hunter]] who worked with fellow [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunters]] [[CT-720]] and [[Beilert Valance]]. Valance was later reminded of his work with BT-823, CT-720, and a few other hunters by the former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Cavic]] sometime prior to the [[Hunt for Vader|hunt]] for the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]<ref name="Target Vader 6" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Cavic further called the aforementioned bounty hunters a "solid bunch."<ref name="Target Vader 6" />

==Behind the scenes==
BT-823 was mentioned in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Target Vader 6|''Target Vader'' 6]], which was written by [[Robbie Thompson]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Target Vader 6" /> on [[December 11]], [[2019]]. Beforehand, an excerpt of the comic including BT-823's mention was released on [[StarWars.com]] on [[December 4]] of that year.<ref name="Excerpt">{{SW|url=news/doctor-aphra-40-target-vader-6-exclusive|text=Doctor Aphra Says Goodbye in ''Doctor Aphra'' #40 - Exclusive}}</ref>
