The '''Felucia system''' was an [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[star system]] located in the [[Thanium sector]] that contained the [[planet]] [[Felucia]].<ref name="Collapse" /> The system was also found in the [[Trans-Hydian Borderlands|Trans-Hydian Region]] on the west side of the [[Perlemian Trade Route]].<ref name="BMF 21" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The Felucia system is ours!|[[Asajj Ventress]] in ''Star Wars: Galactic Defense''|<ref name="GD" />}}
The Felucia system first appeared via the planet of Felucia in the [[2005]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> It was first identified by the [[2014]] mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''.<ref name="GD" />
