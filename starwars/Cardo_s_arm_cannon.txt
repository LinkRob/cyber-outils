'''Cardo's arm cannon''' was a modified [[arm cannon]] that was the weapon of choice for the [[Knights of Ren|Knight of Ren]] [[Cardo]].

Cardo's primary weapon was a customized arm [[cannon]], which incorporated three weapons into one: a wide-bore [[flamethrower]] that fired [[Naphthex gel]], a rapid-fire repeating [[laser cannon]], and a vented [[plasma bolt]] launcher capable of firing explosives over 200 meters.<ref name="Episode IX VD" />
