"'''A Flash of Inspiration'''" is an interview with [[Edward Summer]] that was written by [[Jonathan W. Rinzler]] and appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 139|''Star Wars Insider'' 139]]. The first of a three-part series with "[[Summer Blockbuster]]" and "[[Age of Empire]]" in issues 140 and 141, it was originally described as the first of only a two-part series.
