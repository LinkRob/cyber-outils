{{Quote|With regret, I report that my fleet will no longer be able to provide protection for the troops on Ryloth. I repeat my request that help be sent right away.|Admiral Dao, reporting the status of his fleet to the Jedi Council|<ref name="Supply Lines" />}}
[[Admiral]] [[Dao]] operated a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] as his '''flagship''', active as part of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Navy|naval forces]]. Using it to command a [[Dao's fleet|relief supply fleet]], the [[flagship]] was stationed above the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] during the [[Clone Wars]] in [[22 BBY]]. The [[Star Destroyer]] fought to hold off the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance's]] blockade of [[warship]] that had launched an [[Invasion of Ryloth|invasion]] of Ryloth.

In the battle, Dao's flagship had suffered critical damages to its essential systems and was dangerously low on [[fuel]] and ammunition. Left without protective [[Deflector shield|shielding]] and a functioning [[reactor]], the Venator was destroyed alongside its accompanying one and giving the Separatists control over Ryloth's [[orbit]]. After the [[fleet]] was lost, the Republic ran a secret supply operation to Ryloth, launched from the planet [[Toydaria]].

==Description==
{{Quote|The main reactor's been hit, sir. Systems are shutting down. We are dead in space.|A clone navigation officer, to Admiral Dao|<ref name="Supply Lines" />}}
The [[Starship|vessel]] was part of the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class]] of [[Star Destroyer]], active in the [[Republic Navy|Galactic Republic Navy]] as a [[battleship]] and [[carrier]] for [[starfighter]]s.<ref name="Venator" /> Measuring to 1,137 [[meter]]s in length, 548 meters in width and 268 meters in height,<ref name="Size" /> the Star Destroyer had two [[Bridge (starship)|bridges]],<ref name="Supply Lines" /> the port for starfighter flight control and the starboard for overall command,<ref name="Bridges">{{BuildFalconCite|35|Starship Fact File}} states the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer's]] [[Bridge (starship)|bridges]] were for [[starfighter]] flight control and general command.</ref> one of which contained a [[Holotable|holographic table]] and pits for crewmembers. The Star Destroyer was equipped with a [[Reactor|main reactor]] and [[deflector shield generator]], and [[Comm unit|communications equipment]].<ref name="Supply Lines" /> Like all Venators, the vessel was armed with eight [[DBY-827 heavy turbolaser turret]]s, fifty-two [[point-defense laser cannon]]s, four [[proton torpedo launcher]]s, and six [[tractor beam projector]]s.<ref name="Venator" />

==History==
{{Quote|Admiral Dao, this is General Di. We're in trouble down here. We need those supplies immediately.''"<br />"''I understand, General, but I am in no position to help. We are critically low on fuel and ammunition.|Admiral Dao, speaking to Jedi General Ima-Gun-Di on his fleet|<ref name="Supply Lines" />}}
[[File:TCW_Dao_fleet.png|thumb|left|300px|Dao's fleet provided support for the Outer Rim garrison during the Battle of Ryloth.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] was [[Battle of Ryloth|invaded]] by the Confederacy's [[Separatist Droid Army|droid armies]] in an attempt to claim the world<ref name="Supply Lines" /> and its [[hyperspace]] location.<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> In an effort to hinder the invasion force,<ref name="Supply Lines" /> a Republic relief supply [[Dao's fleet|fleet]]<ref name="SL Gallery 2">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/supply-lines-episode-gallery|text="Supply Lines" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Admiral]] [[Dao]] aboard a ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer, and supplemented by a second Venator,<ref name="Supply Lines" /> defended Ryloth<ref name="SL Gallery 3">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/supply-lines-episode-gallery|text="Supply Lines" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> against the Confederacy's [[Ryloth blockade|blockade]] of [[warship]]s<ref name="Supply Lines" /> led by [[Neimoidian]] [[Captain]] [[Mar Tuuk]].<ref name="Storm">{{TCW|Storm Over Ryloth}}</ref> While the [[fleet]]s battled in [[orbit]],<ref name="Supply Lines" /> a small<ref name="Battles" /> [[Outer Rim garrison|garrison]] of Republic [[clone trooper]]s led by [[Jedi General]] [[Ima-Gun Di]] fought against the invading Separatist Droid Army on the surface.<ref name="Supply Lines" />

Amid the orbital engagements, Dao's [[flagship]] was contacted by [[General]] Di on the surface, who requested additional supplies from the fleet for his forces and the [[Twi'lek Resistance|resistance force]] of [[Homeworld|native]] [[Twi'lek]]s led by General [[Cham Syndulla]], which had been depleted of its resources from the battle. However, despite the [[Jedi Master]]'s pleas, the Republic admiral explained&mdash;as one of Dao's Star Destroyers was destroyed&mdash;that his fleet was in no position to provide assistance as a result of his starships being critically low on [[fuel]] and ammunition. In response, Di ordered the admiral to once more contact the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]]<ref name="Supply Lines" /> on the planet<ref name="Battles" /> [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Supply Lines" />

Contacting the [[Jedi High Council]] on Coruscant, Admiral Dao requested that assistance be dispatched to Ryloth as soon as possible. During his meeting with [[Jedi]] Masters [[Mace Windu]] and [[Yoda]], however, the admiral was informed by a [[Unidentified clone navigation officer (Ryloth)|clone navigation officer]] that his warship had been rendered inoperable as their main reactor was destroyed, resulting in the Star Destroyer's subsequent destruction following the deactivation of their shields. Despite the destruction of Dao's fleet, the Republic was able to deliver relief supplies to Ryloth and its starving population following a [[The Matter of Using Toydarian Territory to Aid the Shipment of Relief Supplies to the Citizens of Ryloth|humanitarian dispute]] on<ref name="Supply Lines" /> the planet<ref name="Ghosts">''[[Ghosts of Dathomir]]''</ref> [[Toydaria]].<ref name="Supply Lines" />

==Commanders and crew==
The ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer served as the command ship of Admiral Dao and his relief supply fleet during the Battle of Ryloth. Its bridge was crewed by several [[clone trooper]]s and [[clone navigation officer]]s.<ref name="Supply Lines" />
