'''Candlewick flowers''' were the [[flower]]s of a climbing [[plant]] found on [[Alderaan]]<ref name="Leia" /> and [[Birren]]<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" /> that bloomed during late [[spring]]. They opened during the night, revealing their luminescent petals in pale [[Color|orange]] and gold. They grew in the [[garden]]s at Alderaan's [[Royal Palace of Alderaan|royal palace]], which could be seen from the palace's private [[library]], and [[vine]]s of candlewick flowers grew around the rails on the south terrace.<ref name="Leia" />

When [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] was a small child she used to believe that the glowing from the [[pulmonodes]] in the chest of her mother, [[Queen]] [[Breha Organa]], was a bouquet of blooming candlewick flowers. Even after realizing that the glow was her mother's pulmonodes, even meeting many others with the same mechanical organ replacements, Leia still harbored affection for the flowers as to her they would always symbolize magic and love.<ref name="Leia" />

[[Alderaanian]] settlers to the planet Birren introduced native Alderaanian plants, including the candlewick flower. After the [[destruction of Alderaan]], Birren was one of the few places they could be found growing in the wild.<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" />
