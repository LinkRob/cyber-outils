'''Dry-rock salamanders''' were a type of [[salamander]] found in the [[Triple Moon Caves]] on the [[planet]] [[Siline]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], they were endangered.<ref name="Crown" /> In [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> while visiting the [[cave]]s, the [[Silinese ]] [[Rhos Arthyst]] told [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Yord Fandar]] to watch out for both dry-rock salamander and [[crystal bat]]s and noted the salamanders' endangered status.<ref name="Crown" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dry-rock salamanders were mentioned in ''[[The Acolyte: The Crystal Crown]]'', a [[2025]] young-adult novel by [[Tessa Gratton]].<ref name="Crown" />
