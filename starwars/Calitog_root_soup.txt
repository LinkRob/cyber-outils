'''Calitog root soup''' was a type of [[soup]]. At the [[Tenoo Jedi Temple]], the [[caretaker droid]] Dee was tasked with making chilled Calitog root soup for Jedi Master [[Zia Zaldor Zanna|Zia]]. When [[Bulcha]] the [[Hutt]] stormed the temple and ordered his droids to charge at the Jedi, Dee releases several pots and cauldrons of green Calitog root soup on an attacking droid.<ref name="HomeSweetTemple">{{YJA|Home Sweet Temple}}</ref>
