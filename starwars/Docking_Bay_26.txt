'''Docking Bay 26''' was a [[Hangar|docking bay]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Eiram]]'s [[Erasmus Capital City]]. The hangar was situated near Eiram's south coast and the [[city]]'s [[electrostatic dome]] opened to let incoming [[Starship|ships]] land.<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[Eirami]] [[Prince]] [[Phan-tu Zenn]] waited in the docking bay with his [[Eirami guard|guard]], [[Vigo (Eiram)|Vigo]]. Not long after, the prince met the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[envoy]] [[Axel Greylark]]'s ship, the ''[[Eventide]]''.<ref name="Convergence" />
