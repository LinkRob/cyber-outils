The '''Commercial District''' was a region of [[Imperial City|Galactic City]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] that contained towering buildings.<ref name="ACS" /> Around [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Reveal">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[A Coruscant Solstice]]" to around [[232 BBY]].</ref> the [[thief]] [[Nittel Karkson]] made for the buildings of the Commercial District in an attempt to escape after he was spotted pickpocketing on [[Republic Avenue]] by [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]]. Karkson slipped down an alleyway between [[Coruscant's oldest tailor]] and a luxury [[airspeeder]] showroom before entering a hatch into the lower levels of the [[city]].<ref name="ACS" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Commercial District appeared in "[[A Coruscant Solstice]]," a short story written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in the [[2021]] ''[[Life Day Treasury]]'' anthology.<ref name="ACS" />
