'''Beak''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who served as a [[Rebel trooper|soldier]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[61st Mobile Infantry]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Thought to be an exceptional soldier, he, [[Roja]] and [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] was assigned to escort [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]] and [[Everi Chalis]] to [[Hoth]] for a strategy conference with the [[Alliance High Command]]. Before they could depart Hoth, however, the [[Galactic Empire]] discovered and [[Battle of Hoth|attacked]] the [[Echo Base|rebel base]] there. Forced to retreat from their original posts, Beak and the other two attempted to rescue Evon and Chalis when [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] intervened and cut Beak in half.

==Biography==
===Joining the Alliance===
Beak joined the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]], also known as Twilight Company, sometime before the [[Galactic Civil War]]. He and [[Roja]] were present for the [[Battles at Mygeeto|battles]] on [[Mygeeto]] and served Twilight Company through the [[Mid Rim Retreat]].<ref name="Battlefront" />

===Escort to Hoth===
{{Quote|Tomorrow morning, Howl, Chalis, me, Roja, and Beak. Wish me luck on the shuttle ride.|Hazram Namir, before the journey to Hoth|<ref name="Battlefront" />}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref> Beak was assigned along with [[Roja]] and [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] to escort the defected Imperial governor [[Everi Chalis]] and [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]], nicknamed Howl, to the [[Alliance High Command]] on [[Hoth]]. They were met by Alliance [[General]] [[Philap Bygar]] who commended Twilight Company for their service. Whilst Chalis and Howl attended a strategy conference with the high command, Roja, Beak and Namir were given duties throughout the [[Echo Base|Rebel base]]. Roja quickly bonded with the [[T-47 airspeeder|snow speeder]] technicians, and Namir got into a fight with a [[Rebel Alliance Special Forces]] soldier named [[Kryndal]], causing him to be on load-lifting duty for several days. Roja and Beak eventually found Namir again, and they told stories of past battles and the incompetence of the base troops. Howl and Chalis returned the next day with news of their imminent departure. However, they did not have time to think about this as the Empire had found the rebel base and a mass [[Code K-one-zero|evacuation]] immediately came into preparation.<ref name="Battlefront" />

===Battle of Hoth===
{{Quote|Gozanti Cruisers. They're bringing walkers.''"<br />"''You sure?''"<br />"''Clamped onto the undercarriage. Only thing it could be.''"<br />"''Call it in.|Beak and Hazram Namir|<ref name="Battlefront" />}}
Beak and the other two were deployed to defend against encroaching Imperial scout forces that fanned out from the main attack force. They passed a pair of [[macrobinoculars]] between them, and [[Roja]] spotted ships overhead. Beak identified them as [[Gozanti-class Assault Carrier|Gozanti cruisers]] carrying Imperial vehicles to the ground and Namir told him to report it to Echo Base. The walkers were confirmed by [[Outpost Beta]] shortly before it was destroyed. Beak also answered Roja's continuous questions to Namir. The contingent of light vehicles overwhelmed the defenses, and the turrets were reduced to cinder. Without proper defenses to challenge them, the remaining Imperial scout forces withdrew and the three were forced to retreat. They found a [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut]], and Roja got it back online. Beak then assisted Roja driving the Imperial vehicle, and Namir stayed in the back with a few other survivors.<ref name="Battlefront" />

When they got back to the base, the rebel defenses were in full retreat and the main base had taken severe hits from AT-AT shots. A [[colonel]] claimed that the command had taken a hit, and the trio made for the base. Beak and the other two later navigated the hallways of the base and found Chalis and an injured Howl. Namir then scouted the hallways and came back with a route to the hanger. The group made for the hanger but were cut off by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and his [[501st Legion|troops]]. The trio shot at the group, and Vader jumped in front of Beak, bisecting him and killing him instantly. As a response, Roja threw a grenade at Vader, but it was deflected and knocked out Namir.<ref name="Battlefront" />

===Post death===
Namir awoke shortly after to find Roja and Howl dead and Chalis swept aside by Vader and his troops. He and Chalis escaped Hoth and returned to Twilight Company on [[Ankhural]].<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Beak is a fine soldier, and Roja is...Roja. There are worse comrades to have.|[[Gadren]]|<ref name="Battlefront" />}}
Beak was respected as a fine soldier amongst the Twilight troops. An intelligent individual, he was able to identify Gozanti cruisers from a distance. Beak enjoyed [[Dejarik|holochess]] and played several games with Captain Howl while stationed at Echo Base on Hoth.<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Behind the scenes==
Beak appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
