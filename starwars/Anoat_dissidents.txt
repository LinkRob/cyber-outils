Several [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], a group of anti-[[Galactic Empire]] '''dissidents''' established a [[Unidentified Anoat dissident base|secret base]] on the [[planet]] of [[Anoat]]. Although they were not affiliated with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], [[Tam Bastion]] thought that the dissidents would have joined the Alliance's cause eventually. However, the Imperial forces of the [[Anoat sector]] decided to [[Gassing of Anoat|gas]] the entire planet in an effort to get rid of the dissidents.<ref name="SM: Heading to Anoat" />

Years after the gassing, during the [[Iron Blockade|blockade]], Tam Bastion tasked a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] with collecting unclaimed resources that had been left behind after the disaster.<ref name="SM: Heading to Anoat" />
