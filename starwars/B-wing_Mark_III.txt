In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' to one [[Standard year|year]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], the events of ''The Rise of Skywalker'', which includes the use of the Resistance B-wing must have occurred in [[35 ABY]].</ref> the [[Resistance]] used<ref name="Episode IX" /> the '''B-wing Mark III''',<ref name="Card Trader">{{Topps|set=2019 Topps Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker Series 1|cardname=B-Wing Fighter}}</ref> also known as the '''Resistance B-wing''',<ref name="Every Ship in ST">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Ship in the Star Wars Sequel Trilogy|video=bb3vhWXClTY|nolive=1}}</ref> during the [[First Order-Resistance War]].<ref name="Episode IX" /> The Resistance B-wing was the newest of the [[B-wing heavy assault starfighter|"blade wing" line]], lacking the speed and maneuverability of its contemporaries, but it had the raw firepower needed to take on the [[Sith Eternal]]'s [[fleet]], the [[Final Order]]. All the Resistance B-wings were armed with the fearsome [[Gyrhil]] [[R-9X heavy laser cannon]].<ref name="CGttSWG" />

The starfighters were limited in stock, with barely a half-dozen among the core of the Resistance. [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] [[pilot]]s were also cross-trained on these newer models. The pilot [[Pattros Navesh]] of [[Lantillies]]<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> led assaults against [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers]] in the craft.<ref name="E9 TVD" /> At least six were present at the [[Battle of Exegol]] between the Resistance and the Sith Eternal, including two flown by [[Enanan Supa]] and Pattros Navesh. One was shot down by [[ion cannon]] fire shortly after the Resistance forces exited [[hyperspace]] into the skies of [[Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX" />
