The '''Collus system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It was part of the [[Thuldin's Grant sector]] and lay within grid square J-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Collus Reach]]<ref name="SotJ" /> was located in<ref name="PLanetnamia" /> the Collus system.<ref name="Appendix" /> At some point between around [[389 BBY]] and around [[379 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Rooper Nitani]] is fifteen [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]]. Furthermore, by the [[time]] of the events of "[[Shield of the Jedi]]," Nitani is around eighteen years old, and that story also establishes that she explored the ruins in the Collus Reach no more than ten years earlier.</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] explored the ruins of old [[The Force|Force]] [[Religion|sects]] deep in the Collus Reach.<ref name="SotJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Collus system was first mentioned on a map created by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Christopher Ibbitt]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> and published on [[June 17]], [[2025]].<ref name="Edelweiss">{{DK|url=us/book/9780593958605-star-wars-the-acolyte-visual-guide|text=Star Wars The Acolyte Visual Guide}}</ref> It was identified in an update to "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]],<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[October 13]], 2025. Both works were authored by Jason Fry.<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref>
