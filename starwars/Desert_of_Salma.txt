The '''Desert of Salma''', also known as the '''Salma Sands''',<ref name="TfVC 4" />, was a vast [[desert]] located on the [[Endor|Forest Moon]]. A dusty, barren, and harsh expanse pockmarked with acrid and acidic pools, it was bordered by tall [[mountain]] ranges. The [[Ewok]]s, one of several [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[moon]], typically avoided to traverse the desert, which was often roamed by gigantic [[Gorax]]es.<ref name="BMF 38">{{BuildFalconCite|38|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>
