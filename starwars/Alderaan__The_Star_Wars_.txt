==Behind the scenes==
In the [[1973]] [[The Star Wars story synopsis|treatment]] and the [[1974]] [[The Star Wars rough draft|rough draft]] of the script for [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''A New Hope'']], Alderaan was the name of the capital of the [[Galactic Empire (The Star Wars)|New Galactic Empire]]. In the [[1975]] [[Adventures of the Starkiller, Episode I: The Star Wars|second draft]], Alderaan is where [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] holds [[Deak Starkiller]] prisoner and in the [[1975]] [[Adventures of the Starkiller, Episode I: The Star Wars|third draft]], Alderaan is where Vader holds [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]] prisoner. The treatment described it as a "city-planet" and the rough, second, and third drafts described it as a [[Gas giant/Legends|gas giant]] that resembled [[Bespin/Legends|Bespin]], with a [[Imperial City of Alderaan|city]] that resembled [[Cloud City/Legends|Cloud City]] on it.<ref name="Making of">''[[The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film]]''</ref>

The original name in the second draft of ''A New Hope'' for what would become Alderaan was [[Ogana Major]]. [[Aquilae (The Star Wars)|Aquilae]], [[Utapau (Kessil system)|Utapau]] and [[Ophuchi]] were also considered as names for worlds partially corresponding to Alderaan.<ref name="Making of" />
