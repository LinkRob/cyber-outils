The '''''Aionomica''''' were a collection of [[sacred Jedi texts]] located in the [[tree library]] carved out of an [[uneti tree]] on [[Temple Island]] on the [[planet]] [[Ahch-To]].<ref name="TLJ Novel" /> The text was written in an ancient, lost [[language]]<ref name="Survival Skills">''[[Star Wars: How Not to Get Eaten by Ewoks and Other Galactic Survival Skills]]''</ref> and was preserved in two volumes, the ''Aionomicum I'' and ''Aionomicum II'' by [[Jedi Master]] and [[historian]] [[Ri-Lee Howell]]. The two-volume combination of codex, correspondence, and scrapbook collected many of the earliest accounts of explorations and codifications in [[the Force]]. The physical books contain passages written in the hands of the original sages, however the books' contents would later be stored in [[holocron]]s which were lost prior to [[34 ABY]]. There was rumored to be a third volume of the ''Aionomica'', which was the subject of a [[Aionomica forgery scandal|forgery scandal]] 300 years prior to the [[Great Jedi Purge]].<ref name="RoS VD" />

On the spine of the book, "[[Jedi|JE DI]] DE O" was written in [[Aurebesh]], the letters stacked on top of each other in an haphazard way. Another Aurebesh word, "MARTYN", was also printed on the spine, in a regular, neat way. In 34 ABY,<ref name="Date Note">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[34 ABY]]. ''[[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi]]'' takes place immediately after the end of ''The Force Awakens''.</ref> [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] discovered the [[tree]] library and [[Luke Skywalker]] explained to her what it was. In doing so, he told her the names of the [[book]]s ''Aionomica'' and ''[[Rammahgon]]''. Rey took the sacred Jedi texts, including the ''Aionomica'', onto the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' when she left Ahch-To.<ref name="TLJ Novel" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Aionomica'' first appeared in the [[2017]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="Episode VIII" /> It was first identified in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]'', the [[2018]] novelization of the film written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="TLJ Novel" /> Although initially not written in italics in the novel, the title of the text was later shown to be italicized in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="RoS VD" />

The name on the spine, "Martyn,"<ref name="VD" /><ref name="Twitter" /> is the same as [[Martyn Doust]], who worked as a prop workshop supervisor on {{Film|IX}}.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
