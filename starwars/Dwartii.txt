'''Dwartii''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> located in the [[Dwartii system]] within the [[Nouane sector]] of<ref name="Appendix" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]],<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> in grid square N-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was [[Homeworld|home]] to the eponymous [[Four Sages of Dwartii]]&mdash;[[Sistros Nevet]], [[Faya Rodemos]], [[Yanjon Zelmar]], and [[Braata Danlos]]&mdash;who were controversial [[philosopher]]s and lawgivers from the early [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'', 2016 edition]]</ref> as well as the philosopher [[Cowus Roont]], often called the 'Fifth Sage' of Dwartii.<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

Dwartii also shared its name with a [[Dwartii (group)|social group]] that had a set of glyphs associated with it.<ref name="TLJ Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> A map showing Dwartii's location in [[the galaxy]] was included in ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', a guide [[book]] for the planet [[Batuu]] written by the [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> A [[Unidentified historical book|book]] describing objects relating to [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]] included an entry on the Four Sages of Dwartii's statues, which mentioned their homeworld of Dwartii.<ref name="100 Objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Dwartii was first mentioned in the [[2014]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> The name "Dwartii" originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where the [[Four Sages of Dwartii/Legends|Four Sages of Dwartii]] were mentioned in the [[2005]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], written by [[Kerrie Dougherty]].<ref name="Complete Locations 2005">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'', 2005 edition]]</ref> Based on that mention, the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' also introduced the [[Dwartii system]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
