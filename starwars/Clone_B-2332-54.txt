"'''Clone B-2332-54'''" is a short story written by [[Eric S. Trautmann]] for ''[[The Last Command Sourcebook]]'', published in [[1994]]. It was reprinted in ''[[The Thrawn Trilogy Sourcebook]]'' in [[1996]].

==Plot summary==
In [[3 ABY/Legends|3 ABY]], shortly after the fateful [[Duel on Cloud City/Legends|Duel on Cloud City]], an [[Ugnaught/Legends|Ugnaught]] worker in [[Cloud City/Legends|Cloud City's]] [[Smelting Core D]] named [[Groggin]] discovers the severed hand of [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] along with the [[Anakin Skywalker's second lightsaber/Legends|Jedi's lightsaber]] among a pile of rubbish. As Groggin goes to melt down the [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]], the [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] enters the Smelting Core and demands Groggin to give him the severed hand. Groggin, ignorant as to who he is speaking with, demands 2,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]] in payment for the hand. Vader proceeds to [[Telekinesis/Legends#Force_Choke|Force Choke]] Groggin to [[Death/Legends|death]] and takes the severed hand, still holding Skywalker's lightsaber. As Vader boards his [[Shuttle/Legends|shuttle]] to take the hand and weapon to the [[Galactic Emperor/Legends|Emperor]], he briefly considers [[Cloning/Legends|cloning]] Skywalker from the cells in the severed hand and taking the clone on as his apprentice, but [[Palpatine/Legends|Darth Sidious]] senses Vader's deception and orders him to take the hand and lightsaber to the storehouse at [[Mount Tantiss/Legends|Mount Tantiss]] on [[Wayland/Legends|Wayland]].
