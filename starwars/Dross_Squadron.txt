'''Dross Squadron''' was a [[squadron]] that served with the [[Resistance]] during their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] with the [[First Order]].<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />

==History==
===Mission to Bracca===
{{Quote|Sweet buttered biscuits, Dross Squadron's got starfighters.|Shriv Suurgav at Bracca|<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />}}
Sometime after the [[Battle of Crait]], the Resistance was in desperate need of [[starfighter]]s to aid the war effort against the First Order. [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] tasked [[Shriv Suurgav]] with [[Dross Leader|leading]] a team to the junkyard planet of [[Bracca]] to [[Mission to Bracca|steal]] whatever starfighters they could find. [[Pacer Agoyo]]'s sister, [[Puwanini]], was a member of the [[Scrapper Guild]] working on Bracca. She provided the team with information that enabled them to infiltrate the junkyards unchallenged, by disguising themselves as [[Rigger (occupation)|Riggers]] and [[Hazmat (occupation)|Hazmats]]. After arriving by train to one of the junkyard platforms, a group of [[stormtrooper]]s, recognized [[Raidah Doon]] as a known criminal from [[Gheia Six]] and a firefight ensued. [[Sanrec Stronghammer]] was fatally injured but survived long enough to hold back the First Order forces, which enabled the rest of the team to swing across the gap above the [[Ibdis Maw]] to another platform. This was a whole platform of X-wings that Puwanini identified as being suitable, were waiting to be scrapped. Despite Stronghammer's [[death]], the team was able to escape with one [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|T-65B X-wing]], two [[T-70 X-wing starfighter|T-70 X-wings]], and one [[T-85 X-wing starfighter|T-85 X-wing]], making it a small victory for the Resistance.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />
