'''District Advocate''' was a title held by [[Dar Champion]] on [[Lanz Carpo]], who was elected into the position.<ref name="Star Wars 68">[[Star Wars (2015) 68|''Star Wars'' (2015) 68]]</ref> As District Advocate, Champion was able to make arrests, even against members of [[Boss]] [[Carpo]]'s [[Red Carpo Guard]]. Despite his powers as District Advocate, Boss Carpo often freed corrupt members of his [[Carpo Crime Syndicate|syndicate]] that Champion had arrested.<ref name="Star Wars 69">[[Star Wars (2015) 69|''Star Wars'' (2015) 69]]</ref>
