'''Atale''' was a [[Color|red]]-colored [[moon]] that [[orbit]]ed the [[planet]] [[Empress Teta]] in the [[Empress Teta system]]. Like the rest of its [[star system]], it was situated in the [[Koros sector]], a part of the [[Deep Core]]<ref name="NoP" /> and [[the Interior]] regions,<ref name="Interior" /> and was located in the grid square L-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="TFABG" /> The Empress Teta system, and therefore also Atale, was situated on the [[hyperspace route]]s known as the [[Byss Run]], the [[Carbonite Run]], and the [[Koros Trunk Line]]. Atale was used by the [[Mining Guild]] as the main production site of [[andris]] [[spice]].<ref name="NoP" />

Anderson]], and penciled by [[Chris Gossett]].<ref name="TotJ" />
