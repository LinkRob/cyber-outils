The '''forensic science commander''' was the [[Military rank|rank]] given to [[V2A DAZ]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Coruscant Security Force]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The forensic science commander provided forensic science reports in support of [[Death|homicide]] investigations. After the murder of [[Senator]] [[Onaconda Farr]], [[Inspector]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo]] received a forensic report from the forensic science commander detailing that [[poison]] was found in Farr's body.<ref name="Senate Murders" />
