'''Dool''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Officer/Legends|officer]] of the [[Corellian Security Force]] who lived on [[Corellia/Legends|Corellia]] during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]. Sometimes after the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]], [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] Dool worked in [[Coronet City/Legends|Coronet City]] when [[Sheelya]], the wife of one of his men, reported troubles with [[Greck]] criminals. Dool therefore began to investigate the Greck and tasked a spacer to retrieve a CorSec dossier with information about Greck that was stolen by the [[Chirq Council]]. The CorSec captain later requested the help of his friend [[Lyman Zavala]], a city councilmen in [[Tyrena/Legends|Tyrena]] to continue his investigation.<ref name="SWG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dool appears in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']], a [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online roleplaying game]] developed by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment and published by [[LucasArts]],<ref name="SWG" /> prior to its closure on [[December 15]], [[2011]].<ref name="Shutdown">{{SonyCite|url=playerinfo|text=IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT ''STAR WARS'' GALAXIES™}}</ref> According to these files, introduced with the "[[W:c:swg:Publish 28 Notes for April 26, 2006|Publish 28]]," on [[April 26]], [[2006]],<ref name="SonyForumsCite-What's On Deck! Publish 28 Update Notes, April 26th">{{SonyForumsCite|url=swg/board/message?board.id=Announcements&message.id=3077&jump=true|text=What's On Deck! Publish 28 Update Notes, April 26th}}</ref> Dool would have been present in Coronet and involved with a quest named "Captain Dool." However, he is not known to have appeared on the game's live servers as a proper [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]]. Nevertheless, an emulated depiction pulled from this character's Interchange File Format reveals what he would have looked like if he were added to the game.<ref name="SWG" />
