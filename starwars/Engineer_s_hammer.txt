The '''engineer's hammer''' was an all-purpose [[Hammer (tool)|hammer]] designed for construction and demolitions. It had a [[meter]]-long [[plasteel]] haft at one end and a sledgehammer head and prybar at the other. The engineer's hammer was typically used to breach doorways, destroy walls, pull down fixtures, and destroy structures by [[Limb|hand]]. It cost 350 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and could also be used as a [[melee weapon]]. The [[Rebel Alliance Corps of Engineers|Alliance Corps of Engineers]] was known to use the hammer.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
The engineer's hammer was first mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Fully Operational Is Online">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/3/29/fully-operational-is-online|text=Fully Operational Is Online}}</ref>
