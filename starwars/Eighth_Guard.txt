The '''Eighth Guard''' was one of eight [[Praetorian Guard|Elite Praetorian Guard]]s who served the [[First Order]]. The Eighth Guard carried a [[vibro-voulge]], and was killed during a fight with [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] aboard the [[Supremacy]] subsequent to the death of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]]<ref name="Episode VIII" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date" />
