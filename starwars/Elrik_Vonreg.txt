[[Baron]] '''Elrik Vonreg''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[First Order TIE fighter pilot]]. During the [[New Republic era|era]] of the [[New Republic]], Vonreg held the [[military rank]] of [[major]] in the [[First Order military|armed forces]] of the [[First Order]]. A skilled [[starfighter]] [[pilot]], he [[Mission to rendezvous with the Resistance|fought]] a [[Kazuda Xiono's team|New Republic squadron]] of [[T-85 X-wing starfighter|T-85]] [[X-wing starfighter]]s led by [[Kazuda Xiono]] as well as a [[Resistance]] [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] piloted by [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]].<ref name="Vonreg DB" /><ref name="The Recruit" />

Despite his efforts, however, Major Vonreg was forced to break off his attack and return to [[Starkiller Base]] where he reported to [[Captain]] [[Phasma]]. As a result, the New Republic managed to transfer intelligence to the Resistance, and as such discovered that the First Order was operating in secret on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Castilon]]. The Resistance dispatched a [[spy]], the newly recruited [[Kazuda Xiono]], to [[Mission on the Colossus platform|investigate]].<ref name="The Recruit" /> Vonreg was [[Death|killed]] by Xiono during the [[Battle of Castilon]] when he was shot down.<ref name="No Escape 2">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 2}}</ref>

==Biography==
===Combating New Republic pilots===
{{Quote|I can't believe the kid's still in one piece. This TIE is a real ace.|Poe Dameron, referring to Kazuda Xiono and Elrik Vonreg|<ref name="The Recruit" />}}
[[File:MajorVonregsTIE-SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|Vonreg's TIE Baron engages with a pair of New Republic T-85 X-wings]]
Sharing his last name with [[Galactic Empire]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] [[Havina Vonreg]] and the [[Vonreg family]], an [[Noble|aristocratic]] family,<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> [[Major]] and [[Baron]] Elrik Vonreg was a [[Military rank|ranking]] [[officer]] and [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in the [[First Order military|armed forces]] of the [[First Order]]. Utilizing his own [[Elrik Vonreg's TIE Interceptor|personal]] [[TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor]], Vonreg engaged a [[Kazuda Xiono's team|squadron]] of [[New Republic]] [[T-85 X-wing starfighter|T-85]] [[X-wing starfighter]]s at one point in the waning [[Standard month|months]] of the [[Cold War]]. The [[New Republic pilot]]s had acquired critical information pertaining to the First Order's operations in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]], and had been instructed by [[New Republic High Command|New Republic Command]] to [[Mission to rendezvous with the Resistance|deliver the intelligence]] to the [[Resistance]]. Vonreg, attempting to thwart their mission, drove off two of the [[pilot]]s, [[Mia Gabon]] and [[Hugh Sion]], although [[Kazuda Xiono]] remained behind to await the Resistance's arrival at the rendezvous point.<ref name="Vonreg DB" /><ref name="The Recruit" />

With the arrival of Resistance [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]], Vonreg was forced to break off his attack on Xiono and engage the [[Resistance pilot]] in a dogfight. However, he was ultimately outmaneuvered by the teamwork of Dameron and Xiono, resulting in damage to his [[starfighter]]. As a result, the Major retreated into [[hyperspace]], returning to [[Starkiller Base]] in the [[Unknown Regions]]. Since he failed to prevent Xiono from completing his mission, the Resistance learned of the First Order's interest in the ''[[Colossus]]'', an [[Starship|aircraft]] [[Fuel|refueling]] station located on the [[ocean]] [[planet]] [[Castilon]]. The Resistance, therefore, recruited Xiono to serve as their [[spy]] on the ''Colossus'', hoping to gather more intelligence on the First Order.<ref name="The Recruit" />

===Conspiring with pirates===
[[File:PhasmaAndVonreg-TheTripleDark.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Vonreg and Phasma contacting the pirates.]]
Vonreg and Commander [[Pyre]] presented the idea that [[pirate]]s be hired to attack the ''Colossus'', in order to force Captain [[Imanuel Doza]], the platform's owner, to invite the First Order in as security. However, after an [[Attack on the Colossus|attack]] by [[Kragan Gorr]] and his [[Warbird gang|gang]] failed, Vonreg, along with Captain Phasma, expressed his disappointment in the outcome when the pirates reported in afterwards.<ref name="The Triple Dark">{{Resistance|The Triple Dark}}</ref>

===Meeting with Doza===
[[File:Vonreg-TheHighTower.png|thumb|left|250px|Elrik Vonreg talks to Imanuel Doza]]
Sometime after the attack, Vonreg [[Mission to Doza Tower|visited]] the ''Colossus'' on a freighter delivering a much-needed shipment of fuel to the platform, in order to speak with Captain Doza. Vonreg insisted to Doza that the ''Colossus'' was vulnerable to pirate attack, and made several veiled threats to him. Doza did not take the First Order's offer, noting that they would have to do better than one shipment of fuel to buy him. Shortly after leaving [[Doza's office]], Vonreg became aware that someone had been spying on the meeting using a pair of matched [[comlink]]s, as when the two comlinks were brought too close together and both active they produced feedback. Vonreg hear this and spotted the spy, Xiono,<ref name="The High Tower">{{Resistance|The High Tower}}</ref> but unknown to Vonreg this was the same pilot who he had fought against in [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="The Recruit" /> Vonreg ordered his [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] to capture Xiono, who fled. After Xiono managed to evade the troopers, Vonreg confronted Doza, who was incredulous about the thought of there being a spy on his platform. After Doza's daughter [[Torra Doza|Torra]] claimed the intruder had merely been a friend of hers, who had gotten lost on his way out of [[Doza Tower]], Doza ordered Vonreg to leave with his troopers.<ref name="The High Tower" />

===All Aces Battle Royale===
Sometime later, [[Marnia Un'la'na]], an informant of the First Order, was on the ''Colossus'' prior to the [[All Aces Battle Royale]]. She was contacted by an [[Ugnaught]] associate who stated that they had an urgent message, and met with them in a [[hangar]]. After conversing on Un'la'na transmitting the data, she told the Ugnaught that it would be a covered transmission and that they could tell Vonreg that he'd get his information.<ref name="All Aces Battle Royale">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=16|story=All Aces Battle Royale (comic story)}}</ref>

===Mission to Station Theta Black===
[[File:VonregAndTIEs-StationThetaBlack.png|thumb|right|250px|Vonreg accompanied Captain Phasma to Theta station and almost killed Dameron and Xiono, but was forced to fall back.]]
After an intruder alert at the abandoned First Order [[dedlanite]] mine [[Station Theta Black]], Major Vonreg accompanied a First Order demolition team led by Captain Phasma. The First Order's [[Mission to Station Theta Black|demolition mission]] was complicated by the presence of two Resistance pilots Commander Dameron and Xiono, who were investigating First Order activities in the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="Station Theta Black" />

Poe's substitute [[BB-series astromech droid]] [[CB-23]] managed to hide Poe and Kazuda's [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]s within the asteroid field. Captain Phasma and her troops subsequently encountered the Resistance pilots but failed to capture or eliminate them. CB-23 managed to remotely pilot the two Resistance pilots' X-wings to the station, helping them to escape into space.<ref name="Station Theta Black" />

Flying his TIE Interceptor, Major Vonreg led his TIE squadron in pursuit of the Resistance pilots. Poe managed to shoot one of Vonreg's wingmates but was unable to shake off the pursuing First Order fighters. Before Major Vonreg could eliminate Poe and Xiono, Captain Phasma ordered Vonreg to break off the pursuit. Vonreg protested that he almost had his opponents but complied with Phasma's orders after she warned that he would die when she blew up the station.<ref name="Station Theta Black" />

Vonreg and his surviving pilots accompanied Phasma's shuttle out of the system. Though the destruction of Station Theta Black generated a massive explosive wave, Poe and Xiono survived the explosion and were able to warn [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] that the First Order was building many weapons.<ref name="Station Theta Black" />

===The Doza plot===
{{Dialogue|Vonreg|Good evening, Kragan. Commander Pyre sends his regards.|Kragan|He's going for the girl! Stop him!|Vonreg|<nowiki></nowiki>''[To Torra Doza]'' This way, Torra. I'm taking you home.|Torra|Uh, thanks?|attr=Elrik Vonreg, Kragan Gorr, and Torra Doza|src=<ref name="The Doza Dilemma" />}}
[[File:BetrayingPirates-TheDozaDilemma.jpg|thumb|left|250px|On behalf of the First Order, Vonreg betrayed the Warbird gang.]]
After Commander Pyre hired the Warbirds gang to [[Torra Doza's abduction|kidnap]] [[Torra Doza|Torra]], Major Vonreg was dispatched to meet with the pirates under the pretext of parleying with them. However, he and his stormtroopers instead opened fire on the pirates [[Drell]] and [[Skreek]]. Vonreg then rescued Torra and returned her to her father. He convinced a relieved Captain Doza to accept First Order protection,<ref name="The Doza Dilemma" /> which led to a [[Occupation of the Colossus|military occupation]] of the ''Colossus''.<ref name="TFOO">{{Resistance|The First Order Occupation}}</ref>

===Intruders in the Dassal system===
[[File:TargetKazPoe-TheCoreProblem.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Thanks to a probe droid, Vonreg found Poe and Kaz in the Dassal system.]]
After a [[spider probe droid]] alerted the First Order to the presence of intruders in the [[Dassal system]], Major Vonreg and his squadron pursued the Resistance pilots Poe and Xiono through the cored-out planet [[Najra-Va]]'s core. The Resistance pilots managed to escape by flying through a [[gravity well]], which claimed the lives of two of Vonreg's fellow pilots.<ref name="The Core Problem">{{Resistance|The Core Problem}}</ref> Though Poe and Xiono managed to escape, Vonreg managed to obtain footage of Xiono's starfighter ''[[Fireball]]'', which led the First Order to discover Xiono's identity as a spy for the Resistance.<ref name="The Disappeared">{{Resistance|The Disappeared}}</ref>

===Escape of the ''Colossus'' and death===
After Xiono and his [[Team Colossus|Resistance cell]] submerged the ''Colossus'' to [[Mission to contact the Resistance|contact]] General Organa, Major Vonreg flew his TIE Interceptor with his squadron over the station.<ref name="No Escape 1">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 1}}</ref>

After Xiono's Resistance cell retook control of the ''Colossus'', Pyre and [[First Order Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Tierny]] fled with [[Tamara Ryvora]], who decided to join the First Order due to her bitterness towards Kaz and [[Jarek Yeager]] for keeping secrets. Following the launch of the ''Colossus''{{'s}} engines, Vonreg participated in the [[Battle of Castilon]]. However, multiple TIEs were destroyed, and, as he was about to shoot down Yeager, a surprise maneuver from Xiono led to Vonreg being killed in action.<ref name="No Escape 2" />

===Legacy===
Some time after Vonreg's death on Castilon, Sion, who also joined the Resistance, asked Xiono about their last encounter with Major Vonreg during a [[Mission to rendezvous with the Resistance|mission]] to rendezvous with the Resistance. Xiono claimed that he had taken care of the First Order ace, but fellow Resistance pilot [[Norath Kev]] responded that Commander Dameron had a different account. The trio then turned their conversation to the topic of evacuating Resistance recruits from First Order-[[Blockade of Dantooine|occupied]] [[Dantooine]].<ref name="Rebuilding">{{Resistance|Rebuilding the Resistance}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
[[File:ElrikVonregMajorBaron-CM.png|thumb|right|250px|Baron Vonreg in the cockpit of his personal interceptor.]]
Elrik Vonreg was a [[human]] male.<ref name="Vonreg DB" /> As a military pilot, he was willing to engage multiple adversaries on his own.<ref name="The Recruit" /> While Vonreg was willing to eliminate opponents like Poe Dameron and Kazuda Xiono, he still complied with his superior officer Captain Phasma's orders to break off the pursuit since she had activated the explosives to destroy Station Theta Black.<ref name="Station Theta Black" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|We can't even touch him!|Mia Gabon, on Elrik Vonreg|<ref name="The Recruit" />}}
[[File:ElrikVonregsTIEinterceptor-CM.png|thumb|left|250px|Vonreg was an expert pilot]]
Serving with the [[First Order Navy]], Major Vonreg was a skilled pilot whose abilities were acknowledged by Mia Gabon and Poe Dameron. The major could engage multiple opponents, such as a [[squadron]] of starfighters, without the support of [[wingmate]]s. He was also capable of engaging Dameron in a dogfight for a [[time]].<ref name="The Recruit" />

He proved himself to be a capable shot with a blaster in a melee with pirates whom the First Order had double-crossed. Vonreg additionally possessed some political ability being able to negotiate with Captain Doza of the ''Colossus'', allowing a First Order security force to be stationed on the ''Colossus'' at Commander Pyre's recommendation.<ref name="Doza Dilemma">{{Resistance|The Doza Dilemma}}</ref>

Despite his skills as a starfighter pilot, Major Vonreg was bested by Xiono, who managed to shoot down his fighter, claiming his life.<ref name="No Escape 2" />

==Equipment==
Major Vonreg wore a [[Vonreg's red armour|modified]] [[Color|crimson]] version of the [[Clothing|uniform]] and [[Armor|equipment]] utilized by [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|First Order TIE fighter]] pilots. He also wielded a [[Elrik Vonreg's RK-3 blaster|custom]] [[RK-3 blaster]] that shared the same color as his uniform.<ref name="The Recruit" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Every time we have a villain pilot, we would talk about, well, is this our Red Baron? And finally it's like, let's make one red all out. It seems to fit with the First Order, since they seem to really like that as a color. If the good guys have aces, the villains need them, too.|[[Dave Filoni]]|<ref name="USA Today">{{WebCite|url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/tv/2018/09/14/star-wars-ace-pilots-not-jedi-take-center-stage-animated-resistance-series/1295303002|text='Star Wars': Ace pilots, not Jedi, take center stage in animated 'Resistance' series|work=[https://www.usatoday.com USA Today]|archivedate=20180915094934}}</ref>}}
[[File:LEGOVonreg-75240.png|thumb|right|200px|Elrik Vonreg in LEGO form]]
Elrik Vonreg was created and developed for ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''. He is voiced by [[Lex Lang]].<ref name="The Recruit" /> His unique armor was designed so that he could stand out from other First Order pilots, as an indication of his superior rank and skills. His helmet mix inspiration from First Order TIE Fighters pilots, [[clone trooper pilot]]s and [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Imperial Guards]] helmets. His character was developed with the famous [[Wikipedia:World War I|World War I]] [[Wikipedia:Luftstreitkräfte|German]] ace [[Wikipedia:Manfred von Richthofen|Red Baron]] in mind, from which he inherited the red color and the title of baron.<ref name="High Tower BL">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-high-tower-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 9 Fun Facts from "The High Tower" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref>
