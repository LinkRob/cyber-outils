'''Chthonic worm gods'''<ref name="Aphra 16" /> were guardians<ref name="Aphra 38">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 38|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 38]]</ref> of the [[hellhulk station]]s used during the [[Hundred-Year Darkness]]. A [[Deity|god]] referred to as the [[Hellhulk]] was kept dormant as a trap in the [[Wat Tambor's workshop|workshop]] of the [[Skakoan]] [[Techno Union]] [[chairman]] [[Wat Tambor]] on the [[planet]] [[Skako Minor]]. The god was awakened when a [[techno-totem]] displayed in the workshop was disturbed by an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Delta-class T-3c shuttle|''Delta''-class T-3c shuttle]] that crashed through the wall of the [[laboratory]]. It then attacked the occupants of the [[shuttle]] and a team of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] led by [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]], who had been [[Mission to Skako Minor (Son-tuul Pride)|trying]] to loot the workshop. The god was [[Death|killed]] when the mercenary [[droideka]] [[Dek-Nil]] caused the workshop's ceiling to collapse on the deity.<ref name="Aphra 16" />
