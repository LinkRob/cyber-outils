The '''Bubo Wamba Family Farms Novelty Drink Vessel''' was an illuminated beverage container available for purchase at the [[Milk Stand]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. It displayed the official [[bantha]] logo of [[Bubo Wamba Family Farms]] on one side and on the other, it featured the less common logo featuring the [[thala-siren]].<ref name="Milk Stand">{{GEAttr|Milk Stand}}</ref>
