'''Dantari flu''' was an infectious variant of the [[flu]]. During the [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]<ref name="TRS" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Kitrep Soh]]&mdash;[[Soh family|son]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]]&mdash;and the [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Rhil Dairo]] conversed about the ''[[United in Song]]'' [[music]]al exhibit at the event. When Kitrep expressed he felt the music was catchy, Dairo indicated that the Dantari flu shared the same trait.<ref name="TRS" /> At some point by [[4 ABY]],<ref name="FACPOVDate">The section of "[[The Last Flight]]" mentioning Dantari flu takes place during the [[Imperial Era]] at some point prior to the [[Battle of Endor]], an event that ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[pilot]] [[Sila Kott]] learned about the [[death]] of her former paramour&mdash;the [[Gender|woman]] [[Lante]]. Not wanting to cry in front of her colleagues, Kott faked having Dantari flu for the [[Standard day|day]] and stayed in bed, reminiscing on her and Lante's [[time]] together.<ref name="FACPOV">{{StoryCite|story=The Last Flight|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Dantari flu was first mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="TRS" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Dantari flu/Legends|Dantari flu]] was a sickness first introduced in "[[Kessel: Hell in Space]]," a [[Wizards of the Coast]] ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' article written by [[Cory J. Herndon]] and released on [[Wizards.com]] on [[May 1]], [[2003]] as part of the [[Planet Hoppers]] series.<ref name="Kessel">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=starwars/article/sw20030501kessel|text=Kessel: Hell in Space|int=Kessel: Hell in Space}}</ref>
