"'''Droid Hunt'''" is an [[adventure]] seed for [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition|''Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game,'' 2nd ed.]] It was written by [[Bill Olmesdahl]] and published as part of the book ''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Screen]]'' in [[1992]]. "Droid Hunt" is offered as an example of a "quest" style adventure.<ref name="GS 18">''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Screen]]'', p. 18.</ref>

==Plot summary==
{{RPG}}
The [[Karflo Corporation/Legends|Karflo Corporation]] has hired a group of [[Spacer/Legends|spacers]] to investigate the disappearance of an expensive [[StelProbe V Droid|StelProbe V exploration/contact droid]] sent to the remote [[Planet/Legends|world]] of [[Wizar II]]. Once they arrive there, the spacers discover that the droid has been captured by the planet's native population, a primitive [[Species/Legends|species]] known as the [[Uroth]]s. They seem to believe that the droid is their god of death, and they are willing to protect it with their lives. Nevertheless, the spacers manage to recover the droid and return it to Karflo.<ref name="Star Wars Gamemaster Screen">''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Screen]]'', pp. 18&ndash;19.</ref>
{{Endgame}}

==Gameplay==
Olmesdahl offers variants of the adventure outline. In one, the spacers are [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] operatives instead of independent spacers. Their goal is to recover the StelProbe V for the Alliance to analyze. Another option pits the player characters against rival droid hunters. Finally, the droid's reactor may be compromised, meaning that it will melt down soon and wreak havok on Wizar II.<ref name="GS 19">''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Screen]]'', p. 19.</ref>
