During the [[Galactic Civil War]] as a part of [[Operation Ringbreaker]], 20 [[soldier]]s of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] launched an '''attack''' on the [[Kuliquo belt]]<ref name="Battlefront" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[mining]] [[asteroid field]]. The soldiers were personally selected by [[Captain]] [[Hazram Namir]] to sabotage machinery located in an airless and lightless death trap.<ref name="Battlefront" />
