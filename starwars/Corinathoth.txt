'''Corinathoths''' were a [[species]] native to the [[planet]] [[Maridun]]. They were [[Hunting|hunted]] by the [[Amani]]n, a [[Sentience|sentient]] species from the same planet.<ref name="Mustafar and Other Outer Rim Planets" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Corinathoth.jpg|thumb|right|250px|A ''Star Wars'' Legends depiction of a group of Corinathoths]]
Corinathoths originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, and first appeared in the [[Empire 16|sixteenth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Empire]]'' series of [[Star Wars comic books|comic books]], published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Empire 16">[[Empire 16|''Empire'' 16]]</ref> on [[January 28]], [[2004]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54712/star_wars_empire_2002_16|text=Star Wars: Empire (2002) #16}}</ref> They were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Mustafar and Other Planets in the Outer Rim]],"<ref name="Mustafar and Other Outer Rim Planets" /> a [[2022]] booklet in a series of reference booklets titled the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']].<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|lang=fr|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Official Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref>
