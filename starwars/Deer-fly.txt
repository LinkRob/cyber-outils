'''Deer-flies''' were a type of [[fly]] with [[Color|orange]] [[eye]]s that could be found in the [[mountain]]s on the [[planet]] [[Naalol]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> deer-flies buzzed around a [[Unidentified mountain town|mountain town]] on Naalol where [[valdeer]] [[Farmer|shepherds]] had lived until it was ruined during [[Battle of Naalol|fighting]] in the [[Galactic Civil War]]. When [[New Republic]] [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] visited the [[City|town]], she informed her [[advisor]] [[Hostis Ij]] that she [[Military Disarmament Act|planned]] to cut the [[New Republic Defense Force|Republic's military]] presence by ninety percent once the war was over, causing his [[mouth]] to fall open in shock as though he was trying to catch a meal of deer-flies.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Deer-flies were mentioned in the [[2015]] novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] by [[Chuck Wendig]], written as the first installment of ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" /> [[In-universe|Out-of-universe]], [[Wikipedia:Deer fly|deer flies]] are [[Wikipedia:Fly|flies]] of the taxanomic genus Chrysops.<ref name="Britannica">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/animal/deerfly|text=Deerfly|work=[[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica|Britannica]]|archivedate=20241004212236}}</ref>
