'''Atomically aerated cream''' was a type of [[Cream/Legends|cream]] used as an ingredient in several dessert dishes. These included a dish from the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Bakura/Legends|Bakura]] known as [[namana cream pie]], which also included [[namana custard]] and was served for 3.7 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]] a slice. Both [[Neuvian sundae|double-scoop sundaes]] and [[Tibanna split]]s used the cream as a topping for [[Ice cream/Legends|ice cream]], the former with the addition of a [[zherry]] on top and the latter with [[ettel nut]]s and [[Ecclessis fig]]s as toppings. All three dishes were served at the [[Restaurant/Legends|eatery]] known as [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]],<ref name="DaD" /> until the diner's destruction during the first two [[Standard year/Legends|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire's]] rule.<ref name="DexEra">''[[The Last of the Jedi: Underworld]]'' established that [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] was destroyed by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. ''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]'' later stated that the novel took place around the [[Standard year/Legends|year]] [[18 BBY/Legends|18 BBY]]. ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' states that the Empire was formed in [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]], so Dex's Diner was destroyed either during 19 BBY or 18 BBY.</ref> The [[Besalisk/Legends|Besalisk]] [[Chef/Legends|chef]] and owner of the establishment, [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|Dexter Jettster]], enjoyed the taste of the dishes.<ref name="DaD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Atomically aerated cream was first mentioned in [[2009]] in the article "[[Dining at Dex's]]," which was written by [[Gregory Walker]] and released exclusively on the [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] section of [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="DaD" />
