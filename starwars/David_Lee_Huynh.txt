'''David Lee Huynh''' is an [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]] who narrated the short story "[[The Last Flight]]" in the [[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi (audiobook)|audiobook]] of ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]''<ref name="FaCPoV RotJ">{{Penguin|url=books/714036/from-a-certain-point-of-view-return-of-the-jedi-star-wars-by-olivie-blake-saladin-ahmed-charlie-jane-anders-fran-wilde-mary-kenney-mike-chen-and-more|text=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi (Star Wars)}}</ref> and voiced [[Selik]] in the audio drama ''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]''.<ref name="Battle of Jedha">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-high-republic-the-battle-of-jedha-audiobook-original-first-listen|text=Two Jedi Knights Look to Negotiate a Truce in ''Star Wars: The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha'' &ndash; First Listen}}</ref>
