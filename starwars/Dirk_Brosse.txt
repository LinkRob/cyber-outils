'''Dirk Brossé''' (born [[February 18]], [[1960s|1960]], [[Wikipedia:Heusden, Belgium|Heusden]], [[Wikipedia:Ghent|Ghent]]) is a [[Wikipedia:Flanders|Flemish]] [[Wikipedia:Belgium|Belgian]] composer and conductor. In [[2009]], Brossé conducted the music for [[Star Wars: A Musical Journey (event)|''Star Wars: A Musical Journey'']], a new project by [[Gary Lucas]] and [[John Williams]].<ref name="Yahoo">{{WebCite|url=http://uk.news.yahoo.com/4/20090409/ten-star-wars-the-orchestral-version-ea4616c.html|text=Dirk Brossé, Star Wars, a Musical Journey|nobackup=1}}</ref> In 2010, he conducted ''[[Star Wars: In Concert]]''. Brossé is known for his collaborations with Belgian film director [[Wikipedia:Stijn Coninx|Stijn Coninx]], such as [[Wikipedia:Daens (film)|''Daens'']]. His works include songs, symphonic works, oratorios, chamber music and music for the theatre and film.
