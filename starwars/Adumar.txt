'''Adumar''' was an astronomical object<ref name="TFABG" /> located in the [[Adumar system]]<ref name="Empire's End" /> of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Wild Space]].<ref name="TFABG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Adumar was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Canon]] in the [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], released in [[2016]] by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="What Will You Do?">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2016/7/26/what-will-you-do|text=What Will You Do?}}</ref> Adumar had previously first appeared in the [[1999]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel ''[[X-Wing: Starfighters of Adumar]]'', written by [[Aaron Allston]].<ref name="Starfighters of Adumar">''[[X-Wing: Starfighters of Adumar]]''</ref>
