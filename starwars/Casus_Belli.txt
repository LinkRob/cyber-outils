The '''''Casus Belli''''' was<ref name="Aphra 36" /> a [[battleship]]<ref name="Aphra 37 solicit" /> in the [[Tagge Corporation fleet|fleet]] of the [[Tagge Corporation]].<ref name="Aphra 36" /> It served as a research facility for the company, and [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]]&mdash;the company's [[Executive officer|leader]]&mdash;oversaw experimental [[weapon]] development for the company aboard the [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 37" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Tagge met with the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] aboard the ship after she undertook a job for Tagge.<ref name="Aphra 36" /> After the ship's [[droid]]s were [[Scourge crisis|infected]] by the [[Scourge]], Tagge [[Skirmish aboard the Casus Belli|had]] the ship crashed onto the moon [[Asheris]], destroying it and all of the droids on board.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 37" />
