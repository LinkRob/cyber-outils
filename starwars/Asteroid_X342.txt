{{Quote|Put this ship on a course to Asteroid X342 in the outer ring.|Chainbreaker ordering the ''Liswarr'' toward Asteroid X342|<ref name="Tooth and Claw">{{StoryCite|story=Tooth and Claw|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>}}
'''Asteroid X342''' was an [[asteroid]] located in the [[Rycep asteroid belt]], just one jump away from the [[Anoat system]]. While hunting the criminal known as "[[Doshanalawook|Chainbreaker]]" in the belt, the [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk]] hid his [[starship]], the ''[[Hound's Tooth]]'', on Asteroid X342. After he confronted Chainbreaker on her ship, the ''[[Liswarr]]'', and ultimately decided to let her go, Chainbreaker redirected the ''Liswarr'' to the asteroid, where she knew Bossk's ship was hidden.<ref name="Tooth and Claw" />
