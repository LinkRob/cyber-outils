'''Fortuna Flight''' was a [[foodstuff]] advertised and sold in the [[Grand Arena]] during the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] sporting event. This item was priced at six [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the menu of the [[The Restraining Bolt]], an [[Restaurant|eatery]] found on the bottom level of the [[Mos Espa Waystation]], one of the Grand Arena's battlefields located on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" /> The [[Fortuna family|Fortuna]]s, a prominent [[Twi'lek]] family among the [[galaxy]]'s criminal underworld,<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> served as part of the foodstuff's namesake.<ref name="Hunters" />
