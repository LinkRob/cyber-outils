'''Bahlah''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Hostage" /> in the [[Bahlah system]] in the [[Thesme sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> that was home to the [[Morubas Crime Family]]. [[Maul]] was held hostage in a [[Stronghold of the Morubas crime family|stronghold]]. [[Gender|His]] brother, [[Savage Opress]] led the [[Shadow Collective]] in [[Battle of Bahlah|rescuing]] him.<ref name="Hostage" />
