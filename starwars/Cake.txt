'''Cake''' was a [[Foodstuff|confection]] sometimes eaten by the slice and often for dessert. [[Sic-Six-layer cake]] was offered at [[Dex's Diner]].<ref name="whatwookiee">[[Star Wars: What is a Wookiee? (2015)|''What is a Wookiee?'']]</ref> [[Flatcake]]s were another type of cake,<ref name="newdawn">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref> as was [[jogan fruitcake]].<ref name="EP" />

When [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] was too young to attend her [[parent]]s' formal functions little [[sweetmallow]] cakes used to be sent to [[Leia's bedroom|her room]] in appeasement.<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref>

While traveling together, [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Nakari Kelen]] had a discussion regarding the feared [[Galactic Emperor|Imperial]] enforcer [[Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]], who at the time Skywalker believed had [[Death|killed]] his father. Kelen felt that if Skywalker ever had the chance to meet and talk with Vader, he should ask him if he had ever eaten cake because if he had done so then it would be a sign that he was at one time [[human]] and therefore not beyond the possibility of redemption.<ref name="heir" />

In her 19 [[Standard year|years]] of existence, [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] had never tasted cake and looked forward to holding [[Han Solo]] to his promise of her, Solo, [[Chewbacca]], and [[Finn]] all having a party with cake when they escaped [[Starkiller Base]].<ref name="awakensjunior">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>

==Non-canon history==
After Anakin Skywalker joined with [[Darth Sidious]] and took up the mantle of Darth Vader, he was confronted by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] on [[Mustafar]], who stated that he was supposed to be the [[Chosen One]]. To this, Vader replied that he had chosen the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]], that "they" let him eat all the cake he wants. Later, after being burned and having to don a [[Darth Vader's armor|personal protective armor]], he tried to enjoy a piece of [[chocolate]] cake, only to find that to his surprise he was unable to eat it due to his [[armor]].<ref name="LEGO III">[[LEGO Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (Scholastic)|''LEGO Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith'' picture book]]</ref>
