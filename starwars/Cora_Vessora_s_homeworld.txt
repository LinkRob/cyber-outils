A '''planet''' was the [[homeworld]] of [[Cora Vessora]], a [[witch]] who refused to join the [[Jedi]] or the [[Sith]] and vowed revenge against the two parties when the Sith [[Death|killed]] her loved ones and the Jedi did nothing to stop it. The [[planet]], as well as Vessora, later appeared in the [[opera]] piece ''[[The Cantata of Cora Vessora]]'', which chronicled Vessora's life.<ref name="ALD">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>
