{{Quote|I've got membership in the Corellian Merchants' Guild and the Mandroxan Cartel both.|Tryphon Leo, ''Smuggler's Guide''|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />}}
The '''Corellian Merchants' Guild''' was a guild associated with the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. The freelance [[smuggler]] [[Tryphon Leo]] was a member of both the Corellian Merchants' Guild and the [[Mandroxan Cartel]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], a fact that Leo wrote down in the [[book]] ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> The guild also had a presence in the [[Port District (Coruscant)|Port District]] on the planet [[Coruscant]], as one of the many transportation companies that was always had aspiring [[freighter]] crews looking for work.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

By [[35 ABY]],<ref name="TROS Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}} to [[35 ABY]].</ref> the Corellian Merchants' Guild was one of the most influential guilds in the [[Thieves' Quarter]] of [[Kijimi City]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]] along with the [[Lantillian Spacers' Brotherhood]] and the [[Intracluster Gatherers]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Corellian Merchants' Guild was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> The [[Corellian Merchants' Guild/Legends|Corellian Merchants' Guild]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'', a [[1987]] sourcebook for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and [[Curtis Smith]].<ref name="RPG">''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]''</ref>
