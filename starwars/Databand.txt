A '''databand''' was a device similar to a [[bracelet]] or a [[watch]] that allowed individuals to interface with [[access point]]s in [[starship]]s. The [[passenger liner]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' was known to have sensors that could be interacted with through databands.<ref name="FAQ">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=faq/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/databands|text=What is a Databand?}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
