'''Detention Block AA-23''' was a detention area located within Detention Block AA on the [[Detention Level]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. It was reserved for political [[prisoner]]s. The detention cells for this sector were located 400 [[meter]]s below [[Docking Bay 327]].<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

==History==
After [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] was [[Secret mission to Tatooine|captured]] by [[Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]] and the [[Galactic Empire]], she was sent to [[Cell 2187]] on sub-level five of detention area AA-23 and scheduled to be [[Death|executed]]. In a [[Rescue of Princess Leia|daring rescue]] by [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Han Solo]], and [[Chewbacca]], she was freed from the cell, only to be caught in a close quarters battle with [[stormtrooper]]s. Princess Organa improvised an escape through a chute to [[garbage masher 3263827]].<ref name="SWCL" />
