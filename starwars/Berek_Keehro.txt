'''Berek Keehro''' was a [[human]] [[tailor]] that worked at a [[clothing]] store called [[Sirei's Stall]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]] in [[3 ABY]]. During which they were associated with the [[Pyke Syndicate]] and was willing to pass information along to them.

==Biography==
{{Quote|My, what a beautiful merqaal! Those are so rare!|Berek Keehro to Kay Vess|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
Berek Keehro was a [[human]] who worked as a [[tailor]] and [[merchant]] at [[Sirei's Stall]], a [[clothing]] store in [[Mirogana Market]] on the [[city]] of [[Mirogana]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]] in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> During which the stall's stock included at least one unit of [[ironweave]], an [[Agamar gunslinger shirt]], a [[Nar Shaddaa thief belt]], and an [[Abelor survivalist belt]]. They also offered a [[Begamor ruffian jacket]], a [[Begamor ruffian belt]] and a pair of [[Begamor ruffian pants]] if they were provided with ten units of [[coarseweave]] and five units of [[Robuma leather]]. During the same [[Standard year|year]], they were willing to share the location of a fellow merchant named [[Chrissanta Palus]] who offered rare tailoring supplies. Keehro was affiliated with the [[Pyke Syndicate]] and was willing to pass along information to the [[syndicate]] that they received from customers. They also adjusted the prices that they sold items at depending on if the customer had a good or bad relationship with the [[Pyke]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> Keehro noticed a rare [[Merqaal]] named [[Nix]] with his companion [[Kay Vess]]. Vess later traded a number of trinkets that Nix found with Keehro in exchange for accessories that Nix could wear. These accessories included a [[Tosharan]] [[amberine necklace]], an [[Akivan]] [[Ahia-ko wreath]], and a red [[headband]]. Vess also traded an old [[identichip|identicard]] in exchange for part of an [[Karoka fruit]] from the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]] which fit on Nix's head.<ref name="Outlaws" />

During the same year, Vess traded a total of thirty units of coarseweave and fifteen units of [[Robuma]] [[leather]] in exchange for the [[Begamor]] ruffian jacket, belt and pants. Vess also purchased the [[Agamar]] gunslinger shirt, [[Nar Shaddaa]] [[thief]] [[belt]], the [[Abelor]] survivalist belt from the store as well as a unit of ironweave which she used in an [[armor]]ed undershirt.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{EndGame}}

==Personality and traits==
Berek Keehro was a human with dark skin, [[color|black]] [[hair]], brown [[eye]]s and spoke [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Equipment==
While working at Sirei's Stall, Keehro wore a yellow outfit with a grey [[scarf]] and a grey waist [[sash]] with an attached grey pouch.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Berek Keehro appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They were part of the "Nix Treasure Trader" intel quest where the player can trade the collectible "Nix Treasures" that they find in the world in exchange for accessories for Nix. They were one of the several merchants that players could purchase pieces of clothing and intel from. Plays can start the "Special Tailoring Supplies" intel quest by purchasing the matching intel piece from them. Players could also give syndicate data items to Keehro in order to increase their relationship with the Pyke Syndicate.<ref name="Outlaws" />
