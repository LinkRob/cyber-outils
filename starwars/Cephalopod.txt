'''Cephalopods''' were a class of [[species]] having tentacles attached to the head. Notable cephalopods included the [[dianoga]]s from the [[planet]] of [[Vodran]],<ref name="Join the Resistance 1">''[[Join the Resistance]]''</ref> and the [[Mairan]]s<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> from [[Maires]].<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref>
