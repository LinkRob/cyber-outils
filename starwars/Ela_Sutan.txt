'''Ela Sutan''' was a [[Caamasi]] [[Gender|female]] [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> By [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The present day events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic — The Blade]]'' take place between the [[wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran]] and [[Porter Engle]] [[Siege of Bardotta|earning]] his title the "Blade of Bardotta," both of which are dated to [[382 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> she<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> served on the [[Jedi High Council]].<ref name="The Blade 4">[[The High Republic — The Blade 4|''The High Republic — The Blade'' 4]]</ref> Sometime later, Sutan was present in the [[Jedi Council Chamber]] when the Council received a message of help from the [[Scalvi]].<ref name="Yoda 1" />

==Biography==
====Porter's report====
[[File:ElaSutan-THRBlade4.jpg|thumb|left|150px]]
Around [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Jedi]] was present when [[Porter Engle]] informed the Council that [[Barash Silvain]] took a [[Barash Vow|vow]] to distance herself from the [[Jedi Order]] to find out what [[the Force]] wanted her to do.<ref name="The Blade 4" />

====Night of Sorrow====
Master Sutan would take part in the infamous [[Night of Sorrow|Massacre on Dalna]], after she and fellow high council member [[Gluth Andoi]] felt that [[the Force]] was calling them to [[Dalna]] personally. They decided to go with a contingent of Jedi including master [[Rinn (Duros)|Rinn]] and knight [[Azlin Rell]]. Taking a large [[Starship|ship]] adorned with the Jedi symbol they landed in the Dalnan city [[Ferdan]], thus inadvertently disturbing ongoing negotiations between Jedi [[Oliviah Zeveron]] and [[Matthea Cathley]] with former [[Path of the Open Hand]] leader [[Werth Plouth]] and [[Yana Ro]], the two Path members subsequently fleeing. The two Jedi greeted the council members before Ferdan [[Sheriff]] [[Jinx Pickwick]] angrily approached the ship and urged all the Jedi to leave. After everyone finished introducing themselves, the Council members invited everyone aboard their ship to discuss what had been happening. After they assuaged Pickwick's concerns and all explained why they were there, Zeveron and Cathley notably recognizing the dead Jedi from Rell's story as the ones they had previously had mentioned to them by Path member [[ Xander Cran]], they all decided to head into the Path compound peacefully and diplomatically in the hopes of reaching an understanding.<ref name="Path of Vengeance" />

Master Ela Sutan and her group wandered through the surface of the Path compound, discovering it to be empty and sensing no one with the Force. Sutan yelled to announce who they were and what they wanted, but Path members, who were watching them via [[camera]]s, were ordered by [[Elecia Zeveron|the Mother]], leader of the Path, to activate the trap waiting for the Jedi in the compound: [[Security droid|enforcer droids]]. Though the droids emerged from the ground all around them and surrounded them, the Jedi pressed on, making their way forward to enter the [[cave]]s. Seeing this, Path member [[Marda Ro]] decided to send the [[Great Leveler]] and its [[Shrikarai|brethren]] out into the caves to hunt the Jedi.<ref name="Path of Vengeance" />

Later, freighted Jedi fighting the Path elsewhere on the planet, [[Creighton Sun]], tried to send a message from the damaged and failing [[Lazuli]] out requesting help, only receiving a response from Master Sutan, who had made it into the caves of the compound with her group and was currently fighting the Path there. Sutan said that she could not help them, and Sun sent the message out again in desperation.<ref name="Path of Vengeance" />

====Plea of the Scalvi====
Master Sutan would be present during a Jedi High Council meeting where the members heard a message by the [[Scalvi]] requesting help. She urged the council to help them, emphasizing that all life was luminous and that their cry has been heard and must be responded to.<ref name="Yoda 1" />

====Cibaba’s Memory====
[[File:ElaAndCibaba-THRASaberForHire2.jpg|thumb|right|150px]]
At some point during or prior to [[241 BBY]], <ref name="Date2">The events of the flashback in [[The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire 2|''The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire'' 2]] including the memory-sharing, take place while Ty Yorrick is still a Jedi. Since she left the order in [[241 BBY]], this flashback must take place before or in that year.</ref>  Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Ty Yorrick|Tylera Yorrick]] connected to the memories of her master [[Cibaba]] through the Force, seeing one of his old memories of igniting his saber with Ela Sutan in the midst of [[laser]] fire.<ref name="Saber for Hire 2">[[The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire 2|''The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire'' 2]]</ref>

==Powers and abilities==
Ela Sutan was skilled enough in the use of [[the Force]] to have attained the rank of Jedi Master and acquire a seat on the Jedi High Council, as well as surviving the [[Night of Sorrow]]. Sutan wielded a [[color|blue]] single-bladed [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Saber for Hire 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ela Sutan first appeared in flashbacks in the [[Yoda 1|first issue]] of the [[2022]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Yoda]]''. The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]], illustrated by [[Nico Leon]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Yoda 1" /> on [[November 23]], 2022. Prior to release of the issue, Sutan was included in a preview of the comic posted on [[StarWars.com]] on [[November 16]] of the same year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-yoda-1-exclusive-preview|text=Yoda Begins a New Mission in Marvel's ''Star Wars: Yoda'' #1 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref>

The [[The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire 2|second issue]] of the [[2024]] comic miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire]]'' included a Caamasi Jedi within a [[Force vision]] experienced by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ty Yorrick|Tylera Yorrick]], though it was not confirmed to be Sutan herself.<ref name="Saber for Hire 2" /> The issue's artist, [[Rachael Stott]], later confirmed that the Caamasi depicted in the issue was indeed Sutan on her [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]].<ref name="Stott Twitter">{{Twitter|RachaelAtWork|status/1796433396794892410|[[Rachael Stott]]|quote=Great catch- yes it is! 🥰|archivefile=File:ElaSaberHire-Twitter.png}} {{C|In response to: "Hi, Ms. Stott! I was reading the new issue of Saber For Hire and I was wondering if you could clarify if this character you drew above young Cibaba was meant to be Ela Sutan or not? Thank you for your time!"}}</ref> ''Saber for Hire'' 2 was written by Cavan Scott, and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] on [[May 29]], 2024, as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3014-605/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures--Saber-for-Hire-2|text=Star Wars: The High Republic--Saber for Hire #2}}</ref> Phase III.<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref>
