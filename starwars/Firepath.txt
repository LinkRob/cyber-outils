'''Firepath''' was a type of [[Board game|classical game]].<ref name="Rogue's Gambit">{{StoryCite|story=Rogue's Gambit|book=A New Legacy 1}}</ref> The game could evolve a high risk of [[Fire|incineration]]. The [[J'feh]] [[senator]] [[Nadrian DuQuosenne]] often challenged her visitors to games of Firepath, a pastime which fellow [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] senator [[Ta'am Khlaides]] thought of as her attempting to [[Death|murder]] them.<ref name="Mof" /> The [[protocol droid]] [[0-0-0]] was more familiar with Firepath than the game [[Dejarik]], a fact which came up when the [[Wookiee]] [[Krrsantan]] challenged the [[droid]] to a game of the latter aboard the [[Ark Angel II|''Ark Angel'']]<ref name="Rogue's Gambit" /> in [[0 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{Marvel|url=articles/comics/star-wars-a-new-legacy-1-marvel-comics-history|text=Marvel Comics Celebrates Their History of Star Wars Comic Book Storytelling in 'Star Wars: A New Legacy' #1}} establishes the events of the story take place during the events of [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2015)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'' (2015)]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[0 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Firepath was first mentioned in the current [[canon]] in "[[Rogue's Gambit]]," a story by [[Kieron Gillen]] featured in the one-shot [[A New Legacy 1|''A New Legacy'' 1]],<ref name="Rogue's Gambit" /> published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/123263/star_wars_a_new_legacy_2025_1|text=STAR WARS: A NEW LEGACY (2025) #1}}</ref>

[[Firepath/Legends|Firepath]] originally appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in "[[Dark Lord's Conscience]]," a comic story written by [[Alan Moore]] as part of [[1982]]'s [[The Empire Strikes Back Monthly 155|''The Empire Strikes Back Monthly'' 155]].<ref name="Conscience">{{StoryCite|story=Dark Lord's Conscience|book=The Empire Strikes Back Monthly 155}}</ref>
