'''Batch 4652-A''' was a batch of over twenty-six thousand [[B1-series battle droid]]s manufactured at the [[Akiva production plant]] and deployed to [[Hakara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> around [[22 BBY]] during the early [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Rishi Station note" /> During their service against the [[Republic Military]], the droids displayed demonstrated poor combat performance, with less than five percent having survived through confrontation with [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] forces. As such, the [[droid]] [[C-5DS]] communicated the poor performance to [[Poreni]] the chief supervisor of the production plant on behalf of [[General]] [[Grievous]]. Poreni promised to improve the next batch and offered a twenty percent discount on it, which C-5DS accepted as a discount on all B1 orders going forward.<ref name="Outlaws" />
