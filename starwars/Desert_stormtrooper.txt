'''Desert stormtroopers''', or '''sandtroopers''', were specialized [[stormtrooper]]s who were trained and equipped for [[desert]] operations. Organized into a [[Sandtrooper Division|specialized division]] of the overall [[Stormtrooper Corps]], they served the interests of the [[Galactic Empire]] in deserts throughout its [[Imperial Era|reign]].

==Description==
===Overview===
Established as a specialized [[division]] within the [[Stormtrooper Corps]]<ref name="Born in the Storm">{{StoryCite|story=Born in the Storm|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> known simply as the [[Sandtrooper Division]],<ref name="Imperial Troops">{{EncyclopediaCite|Imperial Troops}}</ref> sandtroopers served as part of the [[Imperial Army]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. They were deployed to [[desert]] locations.<ref name="Born in the Storm" />

===Equipment===
{{Quote|There we were, bunked up in just our underskivvies, which by the way, since we're on the subject, are all well and good when you're freezing your balls off on Faz or Rhen Var or something but in the doubled-sunned deserts of Tatooine serve only to bake you thoroughly to crisp and lodge sand in the most unmentionable and unreachable places. So thanks for that. Also the temp regulators in those helmets you gave us are an absolute sham; like, not even remotely functional. So you know &hellip; you might want to get on that or something.|TD-7556 in an incident report|<ref name="Born in the Storm" />}}
[[File:RT-97Cstormtrooper-SWL52.png|thumb|right|250px|A sandtrooper firing a RT-97C heavy blaster rifle]]
Equipped and trained to operate on desert worlds, the [[stormtrooper armor]] used by sandtroopers was augmented with [[cooling fan]]s and a helmet [[sand filter]]. In addition, they wore an [[SD-48 survival backpack]] stocked with extra [[Ration pack|rations]] and [[water]].<ref name="Databank" /> Sandtroopers utilized weapons such as the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]], the [[T-21 light repeating blaster]], the [[DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle]], and the [[RT-97C heavy blaster rifle]].<ref name="A New Hope" /> The [[Heavy Weapons Stormtrooper]] variant of the sandtrooper was the [[Heavy Sandtrooper]].<ref name="Star Wars: Commander" />

===Specialized desert stormtroopers===
*'''[[Dewback trooper]]s''': Sandtroopers that specialized in utilizing [[dewback]]s as mounts.<ref name="A New Hope" />
*'''[[Heavy Sandtrooper]]s''': Sandtroopers who served with heavy weapons.<ref name="Star Wars: Commander" />
*'''[[Stormtrooper Commander|Sandtrooper Commanders]]''': Stormtrooper Commanders who were also sandtroopers.<ref name="A New Hope" />
*'''[[Sandtrooper Scout]]s''': Sandtroopers who served as [[scout]]s.<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref>

==History==
===Desert specialists of the Empire===
Sandtroopers were deployed to [[Tatooine]], where they served in its [[Tatooine garrison|planetary garrison]].<ref name="Insider 216">{{InsiderCite|216|Launchpad}}</ref> On planet, the sandtroopers had to deal with tribes of [[Tusken Raider]]s. Two sandtroopers stationed on Tatooine once came across a [[Unidentified Tusken Raider youngling|Tusken Raider youngling]], but one of the [[trooper]]s elected to [[Death|kill]] the [[Youngling|child]] instead of touch the young sand person.<ref name="Sand will Provide">{{StoryCite|story=The Sand Will Provide|book=Star Wars (2015) 37}}</ref>

[[File:StormtroopersFindChild-TheSandWillProvide.png|250x300px|thumb|left|Two sandtroopers found a Tusken Raider youngling in Mos Eisley.]]
Following a [[Raid on a Tusken village|raid]] on his [[Tusken village (Jundland Wastes)|village]] in the [[Jundland Wastes]], a lost [[Unidentified Tusken Raider youngling|Tusken Raider youngling]] was separated from his tribe, ending up in the [[city]] of [[Mos Eisley]]. After the child was forced out of [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]], two sandtroopers&mdash;the same two who had come across the other Tusken child&mdash;arrived due to the commotion. As they debated amongst themselves as to who had to pick up the young Tusken Raider, with the [[soldier]] who did not shoot the previous child being unwilling to touch him due to the possibly that the Tusken had [[Sand lice/Canon|sand lice]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] appeared. Using a [[mind trick]] on the troopers, Kenobi ensured he was able to return the child back to his tribe.<ref name="Sand will Provide" />

Sandtroopers also served on the [[Ring of Kafrene]] trading post and the [[moon]] of [[Jedha]]. On Jedha, the Imperial [[garrison]] found itself [[Jedha insurgency|besieged]] by rebel leader [[Saw Gerrera]] and his [[Partisans]].<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

===Search on Tatooine===
{{Quote|She must have hidden the plans in the escape pod. Send a detachment down to retrieve them&hellip; See to it personally, Commander.|Darth Vader|<ref name="A New Hope" />}}
[[File:Doctor_Aphra_5_Laming_Textless.jpg|250px|thumb|right|Sandtroopers served on the desert world of Tatooine.]]
Under the orders of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]<ref name="A New Hope" /> and [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], a [[Unidentified sandtrooper regiment|regiment]]<ref name="Born in the Storm" /> of sandtroopers were dispatched to the surface of Tatooine, where they [[Secret mission to Tatooine|searched]] for the missing [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[Death Star plans|plans]], which were hidden in the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]].<ref name="A New Hope" /> Even though there was already an Imperial presence on Tatooine, with sandtroopers among the forces kept on the planet,<ref name="Sand will Provide" /> the increased [[stormtrooper]] activity was upsetting to many in Mos Eisley.<ref name="You Owe Me a Ride">{{StoryCite|story=You Owe Me a Ride|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> The sandtroopers themselves were not told much about mission, only knowing that the [[droid]]s they needed to recover apparently had some kind of secret plans.<ref name="Born in the Storm" />

[[Dewback trooper|Some troopers]] involved in the search utilized the [[Homeworld|native]] [[dewback]]s as mounts,<ref name="A New Hope" /> having been assigned to serve in mounted [[squad]]s.<ref name="Born in the Storm" /> The sandtrooper unit that [[Davin Felth]] served in uncovered the droids' [[escape pod]].<ref name="A New Hope" /> After one mounted squad under [[Commander]] [[TD-4445]] moved into the [[city]] of [[Mos Eisley]], [[Foot Patrol 7]]&mdash;a unit under the authority of Commander [[Wanten|TD-110]]&mdash;was also sent into the town to look for the droids. After spotting two droids TD-110 suspected to be the units they were after,<ref name="Born in the Storm" /> the stormtroopers of the patrol were mind tricked by Kenobi, preventing them from taking away R2-D2 and [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]].<ref name="A New Hope" />

[[File:DewbackRider-2025EmpUnit.png|thumb|270px|left|Some desert stormtroopers were trained to ride dewbacks as mounts.]]
Soon afterward, Foot Patrol 7<ref name="Born in the Storm" /> arrived in the [[Chalmun]]'s [[cantina]] to investigate a disturbance, which had actually been caused by Kenobi defending [[Luke Skywalker]] from the criminals [[Ponda Baba]] and [[Cornelius Evazan]]. Having realized Kenobi was secretly a [[Jedi]], the [[bartender]], [[Wuher]], chose to give as little information as he could about the situation.<ref name="theirkind">{{StoryCite|story=We Don't Serve Their Kind Here|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> Thus, the sandtroopers did not find any useful information, walking to the corner to only find<ref name="Born in the Storm" /> [[Captain]] [[Han Solo]] and his [[First Mate]], [[Chewbacca]], sitting where Wuher had pointed them.<ref name="A New Hope" /> Additionally, although sandtrooper [[Sardis Ramsin|TD-7556]] heard whispers from the patrons about a [[lightsaber]], he did not believe them. Although Wuher claimed a [[Talz]] patron was telling them that the droids had been spotted heading into the desert,<ref name="Born in the Storm" /> the droids were spotted by the [[Kubaz]] [[spy]] [[Garindan ezz Zavor]], who informed the nearby stormtroopers of his lead.<ref name="A New Hope" />

After TD-4445 contacted Foot Patrol 7 about Zavor's intel,<ref name="Born in the Storm" /> sandtroopers attacked [[Docking Bay 94]], where Kenobi, Skywalker, and the two droids had boarded a [[YT-1300 light freighter]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="A New Hope" /> As Foot Patrol 7 raced to reach the [[hangar]],<ref name="Born in the Storm" /> the ''Falcon'' escaped, enabling the fugitives to escape Tatooine.<ref name="A New Hope" /> Upon seeing the [[starship]] fly away, TD-7556&mdash;who had become disillusioned with the Empire and instead simply wished to ride dewbacks&mdash;deserted from the patrol, leaving only a sarcastic incident report behind.<ref name="Born in the Storm" />

===Galactic Civil War continues===
After the fugitives escaped the sandtroopers on Tatooine, Kenobi was killed by [[Darth]] Vader on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]], and Skywalker later destroyed that same [[space station]] in the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="A New Hope" /> The destruction of the Death Star, combined with the [[Assault on Cymoon|loss]] of [[Weapons Factory Alpha]], threw the Empire's supply line into chaos, resulting in the Empire opening negotiations with the [[Hutt Clan]]. Dewback-mounted sandtroopers were among the [[troop]]s that escorted Vader to [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on Tatooine to begin the talks.<ref name="Star Wars 4">[[Star Wars (2015) 4|''Star Wars'' (2015) 4]]</ref> Sandtroopers later participated in the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="Empire's End" />
