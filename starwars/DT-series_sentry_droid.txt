The '''DT-series sentry droid''', also known simply as the '''Imperial sentry droid''', was a line of [[sentry droid]]s manufactured by [[Baktoid Combat Automata]] and utilized by the [[Galactic Empire]]. Imperial sentry droids were used as guards and combat training units, with one specific training model being stationed in [[Tipoca City]] on the [[extragalactic]] [[Kamino]] after the [[Clone Wars]]. Many of the droids were stationed aboard Imperial [[starship]]s such as [[Class four container transport]]s and [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyers]].

==Description==
{{Quote|The only problem is the sentry droid. It can give us away, but destroying it will also alert the Empire. To distract it, I've been sending it out on false missions, always deeper in the jungle. I think it's gone mad&hellip;|An [[Unidentified Imperial admiral (Konkiv)|Imperial admiral]]|<ref name="TTMH">{{RebelsMagCite|32|To Thy Metal Heart Be True}}</ref>}}
[[File:Imperial_sentry_droids_attack.jpg|thumb|left|244x244px|Imperial sentry droids in action]]
The DT-series sentry droid was an [[Blaster|armed]] model of sentry [[droid]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> that saw use by the [[Ruling Council (Kamino)|Kaminoan government]] and the [[Galactic Empire]], which earned the droids the name of "Imperial sentry droid."<ref name="Imperial Sentry Droid Databank">{{Databank|imperial-sentry-droid|Imperial sentry droid}}</ref> Equipped with [[Color|white-plating]] and [[wrist blaster]]s on both [[Limb|arms]],<ref name="Aftermath" /> one variant of the DT-series<ref name="Imperial Sentry Droid Databank" /> was introduced<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[19 BBY]]<ref name="19 BBY date" /> and saw use as [[training droid]]s, although they could be equipped with live rounds for [[Death|lethal]] firepower.<ref name="Aftermath" /> The variant was stored on racks in lockers when not in use.<ref name="Return to Kamino">{{TBB|Return to Kamino}}</ref> Another variant, with black and grey plating, was in existence<ref name="The Wynkahthu Job" /> by [[9 BBY]].<ref name="9 BBY date" /> It closely resembled<ref name="The Wynkahthu Job" /> the [[Dark Trooper|suits and droids]]<ref name="Chapter 16">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 16: The Rescue}}</ref> produced by the [[Dark Trooper project|dark trooper program]].<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> The DT unit [[D-L1T]] of that variant had white plating.<ref name="Survivor" />

[[File:Sentry_Droid_HotE.png|thumb|225x225px|A sentry droid opens fire.]]
Based on old [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]]s and experimental droid plans that were abandoned,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> that variant of sentry droid<ref name="The Wynkahthu Job" /> was equipped with black-plating and equipped with a built-in [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]] on the droid's right<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> or left hand depending on the unit.<ref name="Heart of the Empire">{{SWIA|set=Heart of the Empire|cardname=Sentry Droid|link=http://cards.boardwars.eu/Expansion-Boxes/Heart-of-the-Empire/Deployment%20Cards/Imperial/Sentry+Droid+elite.jpg}}</ref> They could also posess a built-in [[Smart Rocket]] on their right hand, and wielded [[Melee weapon|melee weapons]] such as [[Electrostaff|electrostaffs]] and [[Electrohammer|electrohammers]].<ref name="Survivor" /> Whilst they could still be used as training units,<ref name="TIE" /> these sentry droids were often used for guard missions and, should one of the activated sentry droids fall under attack during such a deployment, the remaining deactivated droids would power on. They could also [[stun]] hostile targets. The droid was armored and could take several shots from small arms fire.<ref name="The Wynkahthu Job" />

==History==
===Tipoca City training===
[[File:Training_droid_in_droid_locker.png|thumb|250px|One variant of sentry droid was used on the planet Kamino as part of its training program.|left]]
White-plated DT-series units<ref name="Imperial Sentry Droid Databank" /> were introduced in 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]<ref name="19 BBY date" /> as training droids used by the Kaminoan government under [[Prime Minister]] [[Lama Su]]. In that role, many were stationed in the [[Tipoca City Training Facility]] on<ref name="Aftermath" /> the [[extragalactic]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Kamino]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> In the immediate aftermath of the [[Clone Wars]], [[Clone Force 99]] was pitted against a number of the sentry droids to test their effectiveness as the [[Galactic Empire]] began to establish its [[Imperial Military|military]]. On the orders of [[Admiral]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], the droids used live-rounds against the [[clone commando]]s, much to the surprise of the [[squad]], but they nonetheless managed to triumph over the training units. Impressed, Tarkin dispatched the [[commando]]s on a [[Mission to Onderon (Clone Force 99)|mission to Onderon]] that ultimately changed his mind about their usefulness, with the exception of [[Crosshair]].<ref name="Aftermath" />

When Clone Force 99 later returned to Kamino to [[Rescue of Hunter|rescue]] its [[sergeant]], "[[Hunter]]," from Crosshair and his [[Elite Squad]], the young clone [[Omega]] activated many of the sentry droids to save her squadmates from Crosshair's trap, resulting in an overwhelming number of training units swarming the training facility where Crosshair held them captive. As [[Elite Squad Trooper]] [[ES-02]] fled under fire from the training droids, Clone Force 99 united with their lost brother to fight off the droids, with Crosshair and his former squad managing to take out all the units Omega had activated before [[Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart|Rampart]] ordered the destruction of [[Tipoca City]]. Any surviving training droids<ref name="Return to Kamino" /> were left to sink to the bottom of Kamino's seas after Rampart's bombardment destroyed the city.<ref name="Kamino Lost">{{TBB|Kamino Lost}}</ref>

===In the service of the Empire===
{{Quote|This unit can defeat you.|A DT-series sentry droid, to Cal Kestis|<ref name="Survivor" />}}
Imperial sentry droids of the black and grey model<ref name="Survivor" /> were introduced by the year 9 BBY. During that year,<ref name="9 BBY date" /> several were stationed on [[Jedha]]. One such unit, armed with an electrostaff and an E-11 [[blaster rifle]], was present in the [[Pilgrim's Path]] in the [[Moon|moon's]] [[Narkis Highlands]] alongside several [[Stormtrooper|stormtroopers]]. The droid was not responding to comms for a pair of [[Trooper|troopers]], so one asked the other to check on it, who refused as they were afraid of it. The droid was soon after destroyed along with its escort by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]]. Kestis would continue to destroy several more DT-series sentry droids as part of his [[Mission to Pilgrim's Sanctuary|journey]] through the Pilgrim's Path, including one with an electrohammer and a Smart Rocket.<ref name="Survivor" />

Later, a pair of DT-series sentry droids guarded [[Imperial Post 8L-055]] on [[Koboh]]. When Kestis attacked the base, he fought through scores of Imperials in its [[hangar]], until the sentry droids came down on an [[Turbolift|elevator]] supported by several [[electro riot baton]]-wielding [[Scout trooper|scout troopers]] and a pair of [[Jumptrooper|jumptroopers]]. Kestis defeated all forces, including the sentry droids, and headed deeper into the facility on the elevator.<ref name="Survivor" /> Around the same time, DT-series sentry droids formed part of the security of an [[Imperial Security Bureau]] (ISB) [[II-0810 Satellite Station|base]] located in the [[Nova Garon system]]. The base was invaded by Cal Kestis, who used the power of the [[dark side of the Force]] to leave a trail of destruction across the base. Many sentry droids were destroyed by Kestis, who had the help of his ally, the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Merrin]], in combating a large group of these droids during the retreat from the base.<ref name="Survivor" />

A unique DT-series sentry droid designated D-L1T was present on [[Coruscant]] at the same time. This unit possessed black and white plating and wielded both and electrohammer and an electrostaff. It was also destroyed by Cal Kestis.<ref name="Survivor" />

===Conflict with the rebels===
{{Quote|I know the governor made a wise choice in selecting you, A'Shar. You will do great things for the Empire. In spite of your tiny stature and inferior biological makeup.|DT-unit [[DT-JZ]], to [[A'Shar Farless]]|<ref name="Goodbye">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=5|story=Tales of Villainy: The Short Goodbye}}</ref>}}At some point, the DT-series sentry droid [[DT-JZ]] was assigned as guardian to [[Captain]] [[A'Shar Farless]] at the [[Imperial Center of Military Research]] on [[Scarif]]. She was left behind by Farless when he was given a [[A'Shar Farless' squad|squad]] to command at the facility.<ref name="DD3" />[[File:Thrawn_Sparring_TIE.png|thumb|250px|Grand Admiral Thrawn sparred with specially programmed sentry droids]]
In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> four Imperial sentry droids were stationed aboard an Imperial [[class four container transport]] that was trapped in the stormy atmosphere of the planet [[Wynkahthu]]. When the [[Spectres]] and the pirates [[Hondo Ohnaka]] and [[Azmorigan]] [[Mission to loot an Imperial cargo ship|boarded]] the ship, they accidentally reactivated one of the sentry droids. This sentry droid knocked out Azmorigan and the rebel [[Garazeb Orrelios]] and threw them into the ship's brig. Later, Azmorigan managed to shoot the droid and destroy it. However, this activated the other sentry droids who attacked the rebels and pirates. The droids were destroyed when the container transport descended into a vortex.<ref name="The Wynkahthu Job" />

Later, [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] kept two sentry droids aboard his [[Thrawn's Office|office]] in his flagship ''[[Chimaera]]'' to practice hand-to-hand combat. He used the override code "Rukh" to deactivate them when they were not needed. The "[[Fulcrum]]" operative [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] later reprogrammed these droids to attack Thrawn in order to provide a distraction for he and his rebel comrades to escape.<ref name="TIE" />

===Victims of the Scourge===
[[File:ScourgeAttacksVader-DarkDroids3.png|left|thumb|216x216px|A sentry droid controlled by Scourge attacks Darth Vader while he is in a bacta tank.]]
[[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] used sentry droids as part of the security of his fortress on the volcanic planet [[Mustafar]]. When the [[Scourge]], an artificial intelligence that aimed to dominate the minds of organic and artificial beings, [[Scourge attack on Fortress Vader|attacked]] [[Fortress Vader]] around [[3 ABY]] to try to control Vader, who he believed was the key to controlling the power of [[the Force]], some fortress's sentry droids were controlled by the Scourge to attack Vader. Although Vader was attacked at a time when he was very vulnerable, the Scourge were unable to fully focus on the attack due to a [[Attack on Scourge 01|confrontation]] elsewhere, which allowed Vader to recover and win, destroying the droids.<ref name="DD3" />

[[File:ScourgedImperialDroids-DSquad3.png|thumb|216x216px|DT-series sentry droids and KX-series security droids infected by Scourge on Ryloth.]]
A number of DT-series units were active on the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] as part of the [[Ryloth Insurgency|Imperial occupation effort]], in which capacity DT units served alongside KX droids and organic personnel like stormtroopers in maintaining Imperial rule<ref name="D-Squad 3">[[Dark Droids: D-Squad 3|''Dark Droids: D-Squad'' 3]]</ref> despite an insurgency carried out by the [[Free Ryloth Movement]].<ref name="Lords of the Sith">''[[Lords of the Sith]]''</ref> The Scourge infected several DT and KX units on Ryloth in pursuit of [[R2-D2]], who was assembling a new [[D-Squad]] to put down the Scourge once and for all.<ref name="D-Squad 3" />

===New Republic Era===
After the rise of the New Republic and [[Battle of Jakku|fall of the Empire]] in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the former stormtrooper [[Sentinel]],<ref name="SWHunters Page">{{Hunters|url=/|text=Main page}}</ref> who had actually abandoned the Imperial regime,<ref name="BftA">''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]''</ref> took on a career as a [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]]<ref name="SWHunters Page" /> in [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] sporting matches, where his on-stage persona was that<ref name="BftA" /> of a loyalist stormtrooper unwilling to accept defeat.<ref name="SWHunters Page" /> Sentinel owned a suit of armor forged from the remains of a DT-series droid for use in matches, with the supposedly loyal Imperial once remarking how stormtroopers like himself were superior to the robots in every way. The only exception, he claimed, was that DT-series units were disposable while stormtroopers were not,<ref name="Hunter">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> but stormtroopers had indeed been treated as expendable by the Imperial regime.<ref name="TPATS">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref>

===Era of the Resistance===
When [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], Darth Vader's grandson, found his grandfather's former servant, [[Vaneé]], at Fortress Vader, they embarked on a journey to other worlds, such as [[Tatooine]] and [[Naboo]], so Ren could understand his grandfather's past. After returning to the fortress, Vaneé, believing Ren hadn't yet learned what was necessary to be Vader's successor, sent sentry droids to attack him. The droids did so because they identified him as an intruder. Despite being wounded by the droids, Ren destroyed them with a [[lightsaber]] he found there. Vaneé's attack was successful because it made him realize that Ren had learned what Vaneé believed he needed to know: that he was already who he needed to be. However, Vaneé's actions led Ren to kill him.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 11">[[Legacy of Vader 11|''Legacy of Vader'' 11]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The DT-series sentry droid first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[The Wynkahthu Job]]."<ref name="The Wynkahthu Job" /> It was inspired by the [[Phase III Dark Trooper|Phase III]] [[Dark Trooper/Legends|dark troopers]] seen in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] video game ''[[Star Wars: Dark Forces]]''.<ref name="Wynkahthu Recon">{{RebelsRecon|UqxnQZDRF4g|#3.08: Inside "The Wynkahthu Job" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-the-wynkahthu-job}}</ref> First revealed in the sizzle reel for ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'',<ref name="Bad Batch reel">{{SWYouTube|YB9DIxdB198|Sizzle &#124; The Bad Batch &#124; Disney+|sw_url=video/sizzle-the-bad-batch}}</ref> another version of the sentry droid appeared earlier in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' timeline in ''The Bad Batch'' episode "[[Aftermath (episode)|Aftermath]]."<ref name="Aftermath" /> The official [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] on [[StarWars.com]] confirmed this droid to be the same type of sentry as those seen in ''Star Wars Rebels'' by using an image from ''The Bad Batch'' on the entry for the ''Rebels'' droids.<ref name="Imperial Sentry Droid Databank" />
