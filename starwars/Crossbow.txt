A '''crossbow''' was a ranged weapon. The [[Hunting|hunters]] [[Winloss]] and [[Nokk]] carried a crossbow during a [[Hunt on X3-299-11|job]] given to them by [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]]. Nokk threatened to use it to [[Death|kill]] a subdued [[Shadowfang Beast]] after learning Aphra would not pay them for their work, but handed it to Winloss as she could not bring herself to kill the creature. Winloss was unable to kill it either, and threw the weapon behind him, where it was picked up by [[Squoxx]], who shot and killed the beast.<ref name="Aphra Annual 2">[[Doctor Aphra Annual 2|''Doctor Aphra Annual'' 2]]</ref>
