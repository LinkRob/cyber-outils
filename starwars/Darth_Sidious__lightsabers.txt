{{Quote|Before I was confined to this imperfect form, I possessed a pair of exquisite lightsabers, constructed from the rare metals phrik and aurodium.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="Secrets of the Sith">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref>}}
'''Darth Sidious' lightsabers''' were the pair of nearly identical personal [[electrum]]-finish [[lightsaber]]s of [[Darth Sidious]], built during his [[Sith apprentice|apprenticeship]] under [[Darth Plagueis]]. Although a skilled and deadly combatant, Sidious rarely used his weapons—only doing so when absolutely necessary—as he would have been immediately identified as a [[Sith Lord]]. Instead, he preferred to exercise his powers of manipulation and used servants to carry out his hidden agenda.

==Specifications==
[[File:DarthSidious-TLC.png|thumb|left|220px|Sidious was as deadly with one of his lightsabers as he was with both.]]
[[Darth Sidious]] had discerning tastes<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> for the finer enjoyments of life,<ref name="FF 117 Palpatine">{{FactFile2014|117|LIG 19-20|Palpatine's lightsabers}}</ref> which was reflected in his [[lightsaber]]s. They were constructed from nearly indestructible [[phrik]] with a flat [[aurodium]] blade emitter, and all finished with [[electrum]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Both weapons utilized a [[Bleeding|corrupted]] [[kyber crystal]] at their cores.<ref name="USWNE" />

One of the weapons was normally hidden inside a [[neuranium]] sculpture that was displayed on the desk of his [[Emperor's office|office]] within the [[Imperial Executive Building|Senate Office Building]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

==History==
===Creation===
[[Darth]] Sidious or Sheev Palpatine’s lightsabers were constructed by him during his [[Sith apprentice|apprenticeship]] under [[Darth Plagueis]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

===Clone Wars===
====Mandalore====
[[File:Darth_Sidious_Mandalore.png|thumb|right|250px|Darth Sidious wielding both weapons on Mandalore]]
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA" /> Darth Sidious carried his lightsabers when he traveled to [[Mandalore]] to confront his rogue first apprentice [[Maul|Darth Maul]] and his younger brother and apprentice [[Savage Opress]]. He wielded [[Jar'Kai|both]] in the [[Battle of Mandalore|ensuing duel]], separating the pair and effortlessly dispatching and [[Death|killing]] Opress. Maul, using his two [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|two]] [[Darksaber|lightsabers]], proved a much tougher opponent and held his own for a while longer. Sidious disarmed Maul and subdued him with a combination of [[telekinesis]] and [[Force lightning]].<ref name="The Lawless" />

====Second Battle of Dathomir====
[[File:Maul-confronts-Sidious-and-Grievous.png|thumb|left|250px|Sidious using one of his lightsabers on Dathomir]]
Sidious later wielded one of his lightsabers during the [[Second Battle of Dathomir]], and used it to fight Maul again and his and Savage’s [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]] in combat who died with the help of [[General]] [[Grievous]] and Sidious’ current apprentice, [[Dooku|Count Dooku]].<ref name="Son of Dathomir 4">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 4|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 4]]</ref>

===Rise to Power===
====Duel in Palpatine's office====
[[File:VERY_NASTY.png|thumb|right|250px|Palpatine brandishes one of his lightsabers against the Jedi]]
Near the end of the [[Clone Wars]], [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] Palpatine [[Duel in Palpatine's office|fought]] against [[Jedi Master]]s [[Mace Windu]], [[Kit Fisto]], [[Saesee Tiin]], and [[Agen Kolar]] using one of his lightsabers. He slew Kolar and Tiin easily, killed Fisto shortly afterward, but had an extended confrontation with Windu. The [[Jedi]] Master guided the duel toward the bay window of the Sith Lord's office and out onto the wind-blown ledge outside. Windu disarmed the [[Sith Lord]] with a surgically precise kick to the face, throwing Sidious off-balance and causing the Sith Lord to drop his lightsaber as he flailed for purchase which fell out the window into the seemingly bottomless streets of [[Coruscant]] before his friend and final apprentice [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] arrived.<ref name="Episode III" /> Consequently, Sidious was forced to use his remaining lightsaber from there on out even if he was as deadly with one as he was with two.<ref name="Lawless trivia gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-lawless-trivia-gallery|text="The Lawless" Trivia Gallery}}</ref>

====Showdown in the Senate Building====
[[File:Clash_of_Titans.png|thumb|left|250px|Sidious duels Yoda using his remaining lightsaber.]]
After Skywalker’s friend and mentor Master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Yoda]] realized what happened, Yoda [[Duel in the Galactic Senate|went to face]] Sidious personally at the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]], while Kenobi [[Duel on Mustafar|went to face]] Skywalker. Yoda surprised Sidious, who told him that his arrogance blinded him and his new apprentice would be more powerful than both of them but Yoda answered Sidious that his faith in Vader was a mistake along with his faith in the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]]. Yoda then [[Lightsaber duel|dueled]] Sidious, who used his remaining lightsaber to face the Jedi Master. The duel brought them to the [[Galactic Senate Chamber]], the heart of the [[Imperial Senate|Senate]]. Sidious hurled [[Repulsorpod|Senate pods]] at Yoda, and the effort to avoid them exhausted the Jedi Master. Sidious then blasted Yoda with Force lightning, but with the last of his strength, the Jedi Master repelled it enough to send both himself and Sidious over the pod's edge. Sidious was able to hold on to a senate pod, while Yoda fell to the Senate floor, leaving only his cloak. With [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa's]] help, Yoda fled the Senate Building and would go into exile.<ref name="Episode III" />

===Age of the Empire===
====Death threat====
Shortly after his new apprentice was reconstructed as a [[cyborg]], Sidious informed Vader that his wife had died. In a rage, Vader threw the [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] against the wall, but Sidious managed to convince Vader to let him go. After Vader did so, Sidious shocked his apprentice with bolts of [[Force lightning]]. Sidious then ignited his lightsaber and threatened that if Vader touched him with the Force again, he would finish what Obi-Wan could not.<ref name="Darth Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2017) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 1]]</ref>

====Vader's vision====
After Vader tracked down Jedi Master [[Kirak Infil'a]] and took his [[Kirak Infil'a's lightsaber|lightsaber]], claiming the [[kyber crystal]], Palpatine sent him to a [[Sith cave]] which was all that remained of the [[Sith temple]] on [[Mustafar]] so that he could corrupt it with his pain and anger in order to make it "[[Bleeding|bleed]]." Inside the cave, Vader's first attempt resulted in him experiencing an intense, light-side [[Force vision]] that urged him to return to the light side. He experienced a vision in which he reassembled the lightsaber and returned to Emperor Palpatine on Coruscant with its blade still green. Realizing Vader had not embraced the dark side, Palpatine attacked his apprentice; but the cyborg killed him in the vision and reconciled with Kenobi. However, Vader ultimately rejected the vision and completed the bleeding.<ref name="Darth Vader 5">[[Darth Vader (2017) 5|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 5]]</ref>

====Training Darth Vader====
[[File:SidiousLessonLigthsaber-TheLesson.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Sidious used his remaining lightsaber to teach Vader a lesson.]]
Later, after Vader bled his third kyber crystal, Palpatine sought to teach him a lesson with a sparring duel on [[the Works]], at the [[Inquisitorius Headquarters|first headquarters]] of the new [[Inquisitorius]]. Igniting his remaining lightsaber, Palpatine charged at Vader and quickly overwhelming his apprentice. Palpatine told his apprentice that he fought like a Jedi by overly relying on his lightsaber, and that the true weapon of the Sith was the dark side of the Force. After attacking Vader with pieces of wreckage that he lifted with the Force, Palpatine stated that the purpose of the Sith lightsaber was to serve as a symbol for the power of the dark side: a blaze that consumes all who come against it.<ref name="The Lesson DV">''{{StoryCite|story=The Lesson (Darth Vader)|book=Star Wars (2020) 25|sformatted=The Lesson (Darth Vader)}}''</ref>

====Fighting on Ryloth====
Emperor Palpatine once again used his lightsaber and fought alongside Vader against a swarm of [[lylek]]s on the planet [[Ryloth]]. During his fight at Ryloth he used his lightsaber to deflect every single blaster-shot aimed at him.<ref name="Lords of the Sith">''[[Lords of the Sith]]''</ref>

===Destroying Crimson Dawn===
[[File:NoSurvivors-HiddenEmpire4.png|thumb|left|250px|Emperor Palpatine, Darth Vader, and their forces boarded the Amaxine Station to destroy Crimson Dawn once and for all.]]
Soon before the [[Battle at Amaxine Station]], Emperor Palpatine used his lightsaber to once again spar with his apprentice while conversing about [[Qi'ra]] and her [[War against the Sith|plans]] to destroy the Sith. Not long after, Palpatine and Vader arrived at the [[Amaxine Space Station|Amaxine Station]] aboard the latter’s flagship the ''[[Executor]]'' and took his [[ST-45|shuttle]] down to it. Once landed, Palpatine and Vader ignited their lightsabers to use to finally put an end to [[Crimson Dawn]], with Palpatine decreeing there were to be no survivors left in their wake.<ref name="Hidden Empire 4">[[Hidden Empire 4|''Hidden Empire'' 4]]</ref>

Fighting their way into the station with their lightsabers, Palpatine and Vader led the latter's [[Darth Vader's guard|guard]] of [[death trooper]]s and the former's [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guard]]s. To Palpatine, the moment was a rare opportunity to take a direct hand against his enemies; much to his enjoyment. In the end, Crimson Dawn's plans would be crushed with the destruction of the [[Fermata Cage]]. Palpatine's rule over the galaxy would instead be brought down by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] with the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="Hidden Empire 5">[[Hidden Empire 5|''Hidden Empire'' 5]]</ref> where Vader returned to the light side and sacrificed himself to destroy his Master in order to save his son Jedi Knight [[Luke Skywalker]] and the fate of Palpatine’s remaining lightsaber was unknown. Without Emperor Palpatine,<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> the Empire struggled along for the following year until the [[Battle of Jakku]]<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> in [[5 ABY]]. Due to his belief about the lightsaber’s role in the power of the Sith, Palpatine never reclaimed his lightsaber when he [[Cloning|returned]] to life on its hidden world [[Exegol]] and did not use it when his granddaughter was dispatched by Luke to face him and destroy the Sith once and for all to win the [[First Order-Resistance War|last stage]] of the War between the Sith and the Jedi Order and the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|That Darth Sidious fights with two lightsabers is evident in Episode III -- he has two lightsabers in Revenge of the Sith, though he never has an opportunity to wield them simultaneously since he loses one in his fight with Mace Windu before dueling Yoda.|The Lawless Trivia Gallery in StarWars.com|<ref name="Lawless trivia gallery" />}}
Darth Sidious' lightsabers first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]], [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], first used under his public persona of Palpatine, [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Senate]]. As a blooper, Sidious' lightsaber can sometimes be seen interchanged with the [[Skywalker lightsaber]] in the [[Duel in Palpatine's office|duel]] scene in [[Palpatine's office]].<ref name="Episode III" /> It would be revealed that Sidious wields two lightsabers in "[[The Lawless]]", an episode of the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' television series]].<ref name="The Lawless" /> These weapons continued to appear in ''Star Wars'' [[canon]] through other media, including ''[[The Clone Wars Legacy]]'' and a few [[Star Wars comic books|comic books]]. Darth Sidious's lightsabers were first identified in ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''.<ref name="USWNE" />
