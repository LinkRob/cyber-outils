'''Bureaucratosis''' was an entity in [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]] that was represented by the [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Senator/Legends|Senator]] [[Yaun]] in the [[Galactic Senate/Legends|Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]]<ref name="Padawan Menace" /> of [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]]<ref name="NEC">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''</ref> and the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire's]] [[Imperial Senate/Legends|Imperial Senate]]<ref name="Raid on Coruscant">{{TheYodaChronicles|Raid on Coruscant}}</ref> around [[0 ABY/Legends|0 ABY]].<ref name="RoC Date">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Yoda Chronicles]]'' takes place immediately after the [[Battle of Yavin]], which ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' dates to [[0 ABY/Legends|0 ABY]].</ref>
