'''Eric McNine''' is a VFX artist who worked as an effects creator for the "Strategic Combat" in [[2017]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Challenges]]'' augmented/virtual reality experience.<ref name="ArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=ericmcnine|url=artwork/1JBA8|name=[[Eric McNine]]|text=Star Wars: Jedi Challenges|quote=This was a mobile AR game I had the pleasure of working on. I created the effects for the strategic combat mode. Being a mobile game, we were limited on many factors like polycount and rendering features. In addition, it was augmented reality which presented its own unique challenges, such as making things clear and easy to see against a variety of backgrounds and in a number of lighting conditions.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/SI4TL}}</ref> McNine worked on ''Jedi Challenges'' from nearly the moment he was hired by [[Blind Squirrel Games]] in [[June]] 2017 until he left the studio in [[November]] [[2018]]. Since then, he has worked as a technical artist for [[Wikipedia:Activision Blizzard|Activision]] [[Wikipedia:Blizzard Entertainment|Blizzard Entertainment]] and Mountaintop Studios, and now a Lead VFX Artist with [[Wikipedia:Squanch Games|Squanch Games]].<ref name="LinkedIn" />
