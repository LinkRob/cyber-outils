A '''collector''' was any individual who assembled a group of items, primarily antiques or relics, as a hobby or occupation.

==History==
Collectors assembled items such as antiques, relics<ref name="Force Collector" /> or art<ref name="World Between Worlds">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref> as a hobby or occupation. While a collector was not necessarily wealthy, they often were. Truly wealthy and serious collectors were aware that it was a standard to show they had the necessary [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to pay before they were allowed to see the goods by [[merchant]]s.<ref name="Force Collector" /> Some derided those who spent large amounts on items they collected, considering these people to have more [[Currency|money]] than [[brain]]s.<ref name="Thrawn" />Some collectors both bought and sold the object they sought out, such as the [[junk boss]] [[Unkar Plutt]] or [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities]] proprietor [[Dok-Ondar]].<ref name="Force Collector" /> Dok-Ondar, who was galaxy-renowned as a collector, chose the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] as the site of his Den of Antiquities. A collector's paradise, ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'' author [[Eloc Throno]] considered it the finest shop of its kind in its region of [[the galaxy]] and a must-do stop for collectors and other curious individuals.<ref name="Traveler's Guide to Batuu">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>

There were written guides for collecting, such as the ''[[Antique Military Collector's Guide]]'', which gave readers guidance on how to collect and how much their items of interest were worth.<ref name="Force Collector" />

==Collectors in the galaxy==
{{Quote|Kings not only like to collect, they like to boast about their collections.|Han Solo|<ref name="TFA novel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref>}}
[[File:GrakkusCollection-2015StarWars9.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Grakkus' collection of Jedi Order artifacts]]
The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Kaleesh]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] collected [[lightsaber]]s from defeated Jedi as trophies.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> In addition, [[Padawan]]s [[Padawan braid|braid]]<ref name="Lair of Grievous" /> before they are [[Jedi Knight|knighted]]. Grievous takes pride in his collection of war trophies,<ref name="SW Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> which he started as a [[warlord]] on [[Kalee]].<ref name="HC 8">{{HelmetCollectionCite|8|Helmets}}</ref> In his [[Grievous's fortress|hidden castle]] on the [[Vassek 3|third moon]] of [[Vassek]], he kept his lightsaber collection along with the braid of a Padawan on illuminated tables.<ref name="Lair of Grievous">{{TCW|Lair of Grievous}}</ref>

The [[Hutt]] [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus]] collected items related to the fallen [[Jedi Order]],<ref name="Star Wars 9">[[Star Wars (2015) 9|''Star Wars'' (2015) 9]]</ref> the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]] had a vast collection of treasures kept in [[Takodana Castle|her castle]],<ref name="TFA novel" /> and anonymous collectors such as the "[[Avenger of Jakku]]," "[[Emperor's Wrath (collector)|Emperor's Wrath]]," and "[[Interceptor Fire]]" collected artifacts of the [[Galactic Empire]] following the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>

The Imperial [[Chiss]] [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] was an avid collector of art, as both a personal hobby<ref name="World Between Worlds" /> and as a means to understand his military enemies. Through studying the art he collected, Thrawn gained insight into the values and culture of other [[species]] and used those insights to predict how they would act.<ref name="Thrawn" /> [[Ezra Bridger]] collected Imperial [[Helmets (department)|helmets]] and in some cases whole sets of armor.<ref name="Spark of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref> [[Karr Nuq Sin]], a [[Force-sensitive]] teenager with the ability of [[psychometry]], collected any items with a history that he believed could induce a vision, including military antiques and [[Jedi artifact]]s. In time, Nuq Sin narrowed his collecting to any objects relating to [[Jedi]] history, hoping to understand the Jedi and his own connection with [[the Force]].<ref name="Force Collector" /> During the same period, [[Trudgen]], a member of the [[Knights of Ren]], collected grim trophies<ref name="Trudgen DB">{{Databank|trudgen|Trudgen}}</ref> from fallen conquests. He added these trophies to his weapons and [[armor]] as symbols of his triumphs. One such trophy was a fragment of the [[helmet]] of a [[death trooper]] defeated by Trudgen, which he attached to his own helmet.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

[[File:Lost_King_of_Duros.png|thumb|right|200@px|The hand of the Lost King of Duro was in Dryden Vos' collection.]]
The [[Crimson Dawn]] [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]] was a collector with a passion for antiquities. He used his power and resources to build a vast collection that included a near-complete set of [[Mandalorian rally master armor]], a reconstructed [[Sith holocron]], a [[traditional Noghri carver set]] and the [[Mummy|mummified]] [[Limb|hand]] of the [[Lost King of Duro]]. Unlike most [[archaeologist]]s, he disregarded the ethical regulations that prevented the taking of items sacred to other cultures. Vos displayed many rarities in his study on the [[Nau'ur-class yacht|''Nau'ur''-class yacht]] ''[[First Light]]'', and rotated other items to be displayed from his storehouses on [[Cato Neimoidia]], [[Byblos]], and [[Taanab]]. Vos employed relic-hunters and well-paid buyers to risk their lives acquiring new prizes for his collection. Other items were given as gifts by those seeking to curry Vos' favor or forgiveness, although even the most priceless offerings did not guarantee his favor.<ref name="Solo Official Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
