==Behind the scenes==
The Black Vulkars first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] game ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''. They were a faction on [[Taris/Legends|Taris]] that the [[Revan/Legends|player]] had the option to help or oppose.
