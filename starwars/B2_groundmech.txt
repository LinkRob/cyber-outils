The '''B2 groundmech''' was a model of [[salvage assist unit]]<ref name="SDCC" /> manufactured by [[Cybot Galactica]]. It was intended for use in salvage operations and as such was equipped with many tools to assist [[human]] operators, including a storage tray, a powerful [[engine]] capable of tugging many [[kilogram]]s of cargo as well as high-torque mobility [[wheel]]s.<ref name="DRVG" />

By at least [[23 BBY]],<ref name="FlashbackDate">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'' establishes that [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] was twenty-seven [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', which the book dates to [[5 BBY]]. {{Databank|cassian-andor|Cassian Andor}} states that Cassian was nine during the ''Andor'' flashbacks, therefore the flashbacks take place around [[23 BBY]].</ref> the [[Andor family]] owned a B2 unit, named [[B2EMO]].<ref name="Reckoning">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref>
