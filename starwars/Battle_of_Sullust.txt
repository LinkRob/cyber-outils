The '''Battle of Sullust''' was a conflict which took place during the [[Clone Wars]], a conflict between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] for control over the [[planet]] [[Sullust]]. 

During this battle, [[Asajj Ventress]] commanded Separatist forces, but was targeted by [[Count]] [[Dooku]], who wished to prove his allegiance to his [[Sith Master]], [[Sith Lord|Lord]] [[Darth Sidious|Sidious]] by [[Death|killing]] his aspiring [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]]. Ventress was presumed dead in the conflict, but had escaped, while the Galactic Republic succeeded in its objectives with moderate cost.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|There is a disturbance in the Force. Your assassin, she has become very powerful."<br />"Yes, my Lord. She is quite important to me."<br />"Too important.|Dooku and Darth Sidious speaking about Ventress|<ref name="Nightsisters" />}}
Despite her past failures during battles on [[Teth]],<ref name="TCW (f)">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> [[Rugosa]],<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> and [[Kamino]],<ref name="ARC Troopers">{{TCW|ARC Troopers (episode)}}</ref> Ventress remained [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]]'s most trusted and cunning assassin. However, his master, [[Darth Sidious]], feared that she was being trained by Dooku to overthrow him, so that Dooku would become the master and Ventress as his [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]]. Dooku then sent Ventress to [[Sullust]] to battle.<ref name="Nightsisters" />

==The battle==
[[File:BattleOfSullust-VentressAttack.jpg|left|thumb|220x220px|Ventrss flies her starfighter during the battle.]]
In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Nightsisters Timelines" /> Ventress and [[TJ-912]] led the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Confederacy navy]] against the [[Republic Navy]] under [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]]. Ventress, in her [[Ginivex-class fanblade starfighter|''Ginivex''-class fanblade starfighter]] then led [[Trident group one|two]] [[Trident group two|groups]] of fighters in attacking the Republic's flagship, the ''[[Resolute]]''. Trident group one attacked the starboard engines and Trident group two disabled the port shield generators. Using this opportunity created by the [[droid tri-fighter]]s, Ventress was able to destroy the ''Resolute''. Yularen was able to escape the ''Resolute''{{'s}} destruction.<ref name="Nightsisters" />

Ventress and her fighters then pursued Kenobi and Skywalker in their [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|fighters]]. Ventress pursued Kenobi while her fighters went after Skywalker. Ventress was then able to shoot down Kenobi and force him to land on the [[Providence-class Dreadnought|Separatist command ship]]. Ventress was then forced to crash-land on the command ship when Skywalker shot her down.<ref name="Nightsisters" />

===Betrayal of Asajj Ventress===
However, during the battle, Dooku was contacted by Lord Sidious. Darth Sidious told Dooku that he sensed a [[disturbance in the Force]]. Sidious suspected Dooku was training Ventress as his [[Sith apprentice]] to destroy him, and demanded that Dooku eliminate her to prove that the Count's allegiance was to him alone. Despite his objections of killing her, Dooku carried out his master's wish and recalled Ventress' reinforcements. Dooku then contacted Ventress, telling her that she had failed him for the last time and was no longer his apprentice and would die.<ref name="Nightsisters" />

[[File:SkywalkerVentressKenobi-Nightsisters.jpg|left|thumb|220x220px|Ventress duels Kenobi and Skywalker.]]
Ventress then dueled Kenobi and Skywalker in the command ship's hangar. She was able to evade the Jedi's attacks until they force-pushed against her fighter. However, Ventress was able to force-choke them until [[TJ-912]], under Dooku's orders, had her [[TJ-912's Recusant-class light destroyer|ship]] fire on the command ship to kill her. [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena'' bombers]] along with the destroyer's cannons took out the ''Providence''{{'s}} shields, exposing it to enemy fire. One bomber sustained heavy damage and crashed into the hangar. Skywalker and Kenobi then decided to flee the doomed flagship. Ventress barely escaped the cruiser's hangar before the ship was completely destroyed. However, her fighter's life support failed and she was left unconscious in her fighter. TJ-912 reported to Dooku that Ventress was killed. Dooku then ordered the [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|tactical droid]] to withdraw from Sullust with what remained of their fleet.<ref name="Nightsisters" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Dooku. He betrayed me."<br />"I know. I had hoped you would find your way to us sooner."<br />"I will have revenge."<br />"Yes, you shall, Sister. We will see to it.|Ventress and Mother Talzin|<ref name="Nightsisters" />}}
[[File:VentressNightsister-SWE.png|thumb|220x220px|Returning to Dathomir, Ventress reunited with the Nightsisters and sought revenge on Dooku.]]
Dooku then reported Ventress's death to Darth Sidious, who was pleased with Dooku proving his loyalty to him once again.<ref name="Nightsisters" /> Both Dooku and the Jedi Order believed that Ventress was killed during the battle.<ref name="Witches of the Mist" /> In truth, Ventress was found alive by [[Ratch]]'s crew of the [[Raider (salvage freighter)|''Raider'']]. She was then revived by them and demanded that they take her home to [[Dathomir]]. When they refused, Ventress force-choked and killed them. She then took the ''Raider'' to Dathomir. There, she was taken by Mother [[Talzin]] and her [[Nightsisters]] where she recuperated by the [[Water of Life]]. Together, Ventress and her fellow Nightsisters planned revenge on Count Dooku.<ref name="Nightsisters" />

Sidious and Dooku’s betrayal of Ventress at Sullust unknowingly set into motion a series of events that would ultimately lead to the return of Darth [[Maul]], Sidious’ first apprentice, into the galaxy, leading to [[Mandalorian Civil War (19 BBY)|growing]] [[Campaign against the Shadow Collective|challenges]] for the Sith and their plans for the remainder of the war.
