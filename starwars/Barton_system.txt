The '''Barton system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colundra sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] in [[the galaxy]], situated in grid square S-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> The [[Star system|system]] contained the [[planet]] [[Barton IV]].<ref name="TBB" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Barton system first appeared in "[[The Outpost]]," the twelfth episode of the [[The Bad Batch Season 2|second season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'',<ref name="TBB" /> which aired on [[Disney+]] on [[March 8]], [[2023]].<ref name="Airdate">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-bad-batch-season-2-trailer-key-art|text=Clone Force 99 Is Back in New ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 2 Trailer}}</ref> The system was then identified in the appendix [[Star Systems of the Galaxy]], published on the [[StarWars.com]] website on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" />
