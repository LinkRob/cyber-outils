'''Balamak''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> located in the [[Balamak system]] in the [[Taldot sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> along the<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[Great Kashyyyk Branch]] [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="GreatKashyyykBranch" />

Sometime before the [[Battle of Scarif]], a [[Battle of Balamak|battle]] was fought on Balamak, the battle was denoted by [[Color|red]] lining on the garb of [[senator]] [[Vasp Vaspar]].<ref name="Visual Guide" /> The population of Balamak was brutally suppressed by the [[New Republic]] according to the news feeds of the [[First Order]]. The [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Finn|FN-2187]] saw one such broadcast in a morale session during his training shortly before his [[Attack on Tuanul|deployment]] to the [[planet]] [[Jakku]].<ref name="BTA">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref>

{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Balamak was first mentioned in ''[[Tatooine Manhunt]]'', an adventure supplement for the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' made by [[West End Games]]. The book was published in [[1988]] and was written by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and [[Daniel Greenberg]]. The location was confirmed to be [[canon]] when it was mentioned in the "Finn" section of the anthology collection ''[[Before the Awakening]]'', which was written by [[Greg Rucka]] and released in [[2015]].
