A '''Darvian Medallion''' was a type of medallion that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="What a Festive Evening" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Episode Date">"[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]" established that the events of "[[What a Festive Evening]]" take place during [[3 BBY]].</ref> [[Luthen Rael]] acquired a Darvian Medallion for his shop, [[Galactic Antiquities and Objects of Interest]]. When [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] showed up at the shop unannounced, Rael used the medallion to concoct an improvised cover story to explain Andor's presence at the shop. Stating that the medallion had gone missing from a shipment that his assistant [[Kleya Marki]] accepted earlier that day and that Andor had brought it to the shop.<ref name="What a Festive Evening" />
