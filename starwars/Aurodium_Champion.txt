The '''Aurodium Champion''' was a modified [[blaster rifle]] utilized by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Firestorm Rifle|standard rifle]]. This [[blaster]] [[rifle]] featured a [[Color|gold]], silver, and gray frame, with a golden illustration of the ''[[Hypernova]]'' on the stock.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Aurodium Champion was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Imara Vex's Firestorm Rifle, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 1.0.0 alongside Season 1 and the game's global launch on [[June 4]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Aurodium Conqueror]]. This wrap could be unlocked by reaching the rank Beskar I within ''Hunter's'' "Ranked" mode during the game's first season.<ref name="Hunters" />
