'''Dathomirians''', also known as '''Dathomirian Zabraks''' and culturally as '''Dathomiri''' or '''Dathmiri''', were a [[subspecies]] of [[Zabrak]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Peridea]] in a [[Far galaxy|distant galaxy]]. On Peridea, the Dathomiri established a society known as the [[Witch Kingdom of the Dathmiri]] and learned to ride the [[purrgil]], creatures able to survive in the vacuum of [[Realspace|space]] and travel through [[hyperspace]]. Although their kingdom collapsed in the end, the Dathomiri who traveled into the [[The galaxy|known galaxy]] across the [[intergalactic void]] were able to reestablish themselves on a new planet, [[Dathomir]]. Tales of Peridea and its galaxy would go on to become nothing more than fairytales spoken among [[Jedi]], and [[Arcana Star Map|the]] [[map]] containing the [[Pathway to Peridea]] was long hidden in a [[Nightsisters' Temple|stronghold]] on the planet [[Arcana]].

The Dathomiri were renowned for their use of [[magick]], an aspect of [[the Force]] which manipulated [[magical ichor]], which was found on Dathomir. By the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Nightsisters]] of Dathomir&mdash;a clan of magick-wielding Dathomiri witches&mdash;were led by [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]], and her son [[Maul]] was trained as the [[Sith apprentice]] of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]], while the Dathomirian [[Asajj Ventress]] became an [[Dark Acolyte|acolyte]] of the apprentice who replaced Maul, [[Dooku]], leading the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]]. During the war, much of the species was [[Battle of Dathomir|eradicated]] under Talzin's leadership as she earned the ire of the [[Sith]].

By the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]], the last known survivors of the Nightsister clan were the Dathomirians [[Shelish]], [[Yenna]], [[Jerserra]], [[Merrin]], and [[Morgan Elsbeth]]&mdash;although other Dathomiri [[witch]] [[Dathomiri clan|clans]] were also known to have survived. Later, during the [[New Republic Era]], three Dathomirians known as the [[Great Mothers]] were found on Peridea&mdash;living proof of the existence of the old Dathmiri kingdom since its abandonment by the majority of its populace.

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|There was a woman. With bone-white skin.|Jedi Padawan Dooku, after having a Force vision of his eventual apprentice Asajj Ventress|<ref name="ScriptJedi Lost">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref>}}
[[File:Shatterpoint-Dathomirianwallpaper.png|thumb|left|250px|Examples of male and female Dathomiri]]
Dathomirians,<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> also known as Dathomirian Zabraks and culturally as Dathomiri<ref name="Witches of the Mist">{{TCW|Witches of the Mist}}</ref> or Dathmiri,<ref name="Part Six" /> were a subspecies of Zabrak<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> native to the [[planet]] Peridea<ref name="FG Databank" /> in the distant past<ref name="Part Six" /> and the world of Dathomir<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> afterward.<ref name="FG Databank" /> Dathomiri showed considerable [[Gender|sexual]] dimorphism. Female Dathomiri had [[Color|white]], blue, or gray skin with various hair types.<ref name="Collapse" /> Male skin was commonly orange or yellow. [[Maul|Few]] had red skin.<ref name="Monster" /><ref name="Episode I" /> Male Dathomirians had horns, whereas females did not.<ref name="Monster" />

The Dathomiri had [[tattoo]]s on their body that represented their tribal heritage. The male [[Zabrak]]s, particularly those belonging to the [[Nightbrothers]], had natural striping which they embellished with tattoos. The females, particularly those belonging to the [[Nightsisters]], had more subtle tattoos that were in contrast to their pale skin.<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> Tattoos were made using the [[mushling]]. The pods of the mushling were boiled down into a [[Color|yellow]] paste, and then mixed with pigments, like [[clay]] and ash, to create a variety of colors.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

Many Dathomiri were [[Force-sensitive]], including the witches that lived on their native planet.<ref name="Witches of the Mist" /> Some male Dathomiri were capable of rudimentary [[Force power|Force abilities]],<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> while a few, like [[Savage Opress]] and [[Maul]], were exceptionally good at manipulating [[the Force]] due to their training.<ref name="Monster" /><ref name="Episode I" />

==Society and culture==
[[File:Nightsisters-SWE.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Several Nightsisters]]
Dathomirians were a reclusive people and hid dark secrets.<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> Female Dathomiri lived as [[witch]]es. Their society was [[matriarch]]al in nature and the Nightsisters were particularly dominant in society.<ref name="Witches of the Mist" /> Nightsisters titled their leaders [[Mother (title)|Mother]].<ref name="TalzinSWE">{{SWE|characters|mothertalzin|Mother Talzin}}</ref> Nightsisters were particularly skilled in using [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[magick]] and [[voodoo]], which had such powers as [[Army of the Dead|raising the dead]],<ref name="Massacre" /> or making one invisible.<ref name="Nightsisters" />

Some female Dathomirians lived in clans,<ref name="Collapse" /> while others were of no clan.<ref name="Bug" /> There were different witch clans on Dathomir, which included the [[Nightsisters]],<ref name="Nightsisters" /> the [[Nardithi Nightsisters]],<ref name="GoD" /> the [[Blue Coral Divers Clan]], the [[Singing Mountain (clan)|Singing Mountain]], the [[Howling Crag]],<ref name="Bug" /> and the [[Mountain Clan]].<ref name="The Path of Fear">{{TOTE|The Path of Fear}}</ref>

A ritual known as the [[Selection]] happened when females wanted to choose a mate. Males would undergo brutal trials to prove themselves. The winner would become the Nightsister's mate and servant.<ref name="Monster" />

[[File:NightbrotherArcher.png|thumb|left|250px|A Nightbrother archer]]
Males, particularly the Nightbrothers, were non-dominant in society, and lived separately from the females. They were tribal in nature, and lived a violent and brutal life.<ref name="Monster" /> Sometime after the [[Battle of Dathomir]], the Nightsister [[Merrin]] made the Nightbrothers her servants.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

Nightbrothers were accustomed to engaging in rites of passage; one such practice involved covering their faces with thin, dark tattoos. The best and boldest of the Nightbrothers faced another sort of rite of passage in which they battled beasts known as [[chirodactyl]]s. Those that faced these behemoth creatures and survived were deemed worthy.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

Nightbrothers practiced painting [[Dathomirian Glyphs|glyphs]] using acid from [[hydraatis]] on hard rock surfaces. Their usage of it was deliberately careful to preserve accuracy and damage to themselves. Those who were master calligraphers—rare positions within the society—were extremely valued for their talents.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

The Nightbrothers respected power and honor, often fighting in a [[Nightbrother arena|combat arena]] to demonstrate their strength with intent to win. Although hostile to outsiders, one that proved powerful could be given substantial influence over the group, be it through respect or fear, even allowing them to gain leadership over the society and worship.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==History==
===Children of Dathomir===
{{Quote|I see what once was the great Witch Kingdom of the Dathmiri. The existence of the Great Mothers confirms this.|[[Baylan Skoll]] to [[Shin Hati]]|<ref name="Part Six" />}}
On the planet [[Peridea]] in [[Far galaxy|another galaxy]], an ancient civilization known as the great [[Witch Kingdom of the Dathmiri]] eventually fell, and its existence became only rumors and tales among the [[Jedi Order]], a group of Force-sensitive guardians of the peace. Nevertheless, the [[Pathway to Peridea]] across the [[intergalactic void]] had been established from a [[Seatos reflex point|henge]] on the planet [[Seatos]], and the reflex point required the use of [[Arcana Star Map|star map]] that was stored in a [[Nightsisters' Temple|Nightsister stronghold]] on the planet [[Arcana]].<ref name="Part Six" /> The [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] witches who descended from the Dathomiri eventually discovered a world they would dub "Dathomir" and kicked out its [[Fromprath]] inhabitants.<ref name="CompEncyc">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>

With the Fromprath forced into exile, the cultural group who would become known as the Dathomirians,<ref name="LastShot">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref> with the Nightsisters among them, claimed the planet. Having secured a new homeworld for themselves,<ref name="CompEncyc" /> the Nightsisters ruled over the planet<ref name="Nightsisters">{{Databank|nightsisters|Nightsisters}}</ref> as the most dominant clan on Dathomir. They used the Zabrak Nightbrothers as mates and servants.<ref name="Witches of the Mist" /> One mother, [[Talzin]], gave birth to three sons, [[Savage Opress]],<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> [[Feral]],<ref name="Graphics">''[[Star Wars Super Graphic: A Visual Guide to a Galaxy Far, Far Away]]''</ref> and [[Maul]]. Talzin lost Maul to the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]], who took him as his [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]].<ref name="Son of Dathomir 3">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 3|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 3]]</ref> Later, Talzin was forced to give [[Hal'Sted]] a child, [[Asajj Ventress]], in order to protect her clans.<ref name="Nightsisters2">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref>

===Disciples of destruction===
[[File:Mother_Talzin_SWDL.png|thumb|right|200px|Mother Talzin, leader of the Dathomiri Nightsisters.]]
Following Ventress' [[Battle of Sullust#Betrayal of Asajj Ventress|betrayal]] at the hands of [[Count]] [[Dooku]], Talzin and Ventress planned their revenge upon Dooku.<ref name="Nightsisters" /> Talzin allowed her son, Savage, to become Dooku's apprentice<ref name="Monster">{{TCW|Monster}}</ref> with the intention to kill the Count. However, Savage and Ventress failed.<ref name="Witches of the Mist" /> In response, Sidious and Dooku ordered [[General]] Grievous to [[Battle of Dathomir|wipe the Nightsisters out]]. Only Ventress, Talzin,<ref name="Massacre">{{TCW|Massacre}}</ref> [[Shelish]],<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> [[Yenna]],<ref name="Bug">{{StoryCite|story=Bug (short story)|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}}</ref> [[Jerserra]],<ref name="GoD">''[[Ghosts of Dathomir]]''</ref> [[Morgan Elsbeth]], and young [[Merrin]]<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> survived Grievous' onslaught. Savage, discovering his brother Maul was alive,<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref> brought him back to Dathomir. There, Talzin was able to restore Maul's mind, memories, and body, building him a new set of metal legs.<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref> Following the death of Savage,<ref name="The Lawless">{{TCW|The Lawless}}</ref> Maul and his [[Shadow Collective]] joined forces with Brother [[Viscus]] and his Nightbrothers.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 2">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 2|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 2]]</ref> However, the [[Nightbrother village]] came under [[Second Battle of Dathomir|attack]] and only Maul was able to escape.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 4">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 4|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 4]]</ref> The surviving [[Nightbrothers]] retreated to [[Unidentified Nightbrother village|another village]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

===Survivors===
Strong willed and defiant, Dathomirian Zabraks fought against the [[Galactic Empire]]'s escalating attempts to subvert them. Rather than surrendering, many joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], fearlessly fighting their oppressors. A number served in the [[Alliance Military]] as [[Heavy Soldier]]s.<ref name="BF2">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> However, a young [[Jerserra]] was found on the planet by a [[Jerserra's master|female Inquisitor]] of the Empire and taken for secret training.<ref name="GoD" />

During the age of the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]], the Nightsister [[Merrin]], a survivor of the Battle of Dathomir, encountered the [[Jedi Master]] [[Taron Malicos]], who crashed on Dathomir following [[Order 66]]. Malicos, who fell to the dark side of the Force on Dathomir, manipulated Merrin, wishing to learn their magick. When the former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] visited Dathomir in search of the [[Tomb of Kujet]], Merrin realized Malicos was deceiving her. She was reluctant to fight alongside Kestis as all she knew was that an armored-warrior brandishing a [[lightsaber]] had killed her people. Kestis reassured her that he was on her side, and he had shared his own story of a grief-stricken past. After working together to defeat Malicos, Merrin joined Kestis' crew aboard the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]''.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

===Heir to the Empire===
After the fall of the Empire and the founding of the New Republic, the Nightsister [[Morgan Elsbeth]] worked in conjunction with the Dathomiri [[Great Mothers]] on Peridea&mdash;who collaborated with the stranded Imperial [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]&mdash;to reach Peridea with an [[Hyperspace transport ring|intergalactic vessel]], the ''[[Eye of Sion]]'' that could deliver them through the [[intergalactic void]] and re-establish control over the galaxy that the Empire once had.<ref name="Part Six" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dathomirian Zabraks first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="Episode I" /> They appeared in the [[2017]] video game'' [[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' as playable skins for the Heavy classes of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]] and [[Resistance]].<ref name="BF2" />
