The '''Badlands''' was an area<ref name="HC11" /> of [[desert]]<ref name="CL" /> the [[Jundland Wastes]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The settlement known as [[Fort Tusken]], which faced numerous attacks from [[Tusken Raider]]s, was established on the edge of the Badlands around eight [[Standard decade|decades]] before the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="HC11" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Badlands were mentioned in "Inhabitants of Tatooine," an article published in the "[[Weapons & Uniforms]]" department of [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 11|''Star Wars Helmet Collection'' 11]], a magazine published by [[De Agostini]]<ref name="HC11" /> on [[June 1]], [[2016]].<ref name="Date">{{OSVCdates|11}}</ref> They were then depicted as part of a map that showed the location of Fort Tusken in the reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], which was released on [[September 27]], 2016.<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/545478/star-wars-complete-locations-by-dk|text=Star Wars: Complete Locations}}</ref> The [[Badlands (Tatooine)/Legends|Badlands]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they appeared in the [[2002]] [[Rainbow Studios]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Racer Revenge]]''.<ref name="RR">''[[Star Wars: Racer Revenge]]''</ref>
