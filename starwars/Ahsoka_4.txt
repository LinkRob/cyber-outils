'''''Ahsoka'' 4''' is the fourth issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries [[Star Wars: Ahsoka (comic series)|''Star Wars: Ahsoka'' comic adaptation]]. It is an adaptation of the [[Part Four: Fallen Jedi|fourth episode]] of the television series [[Star Wars: Ahsoka|''Ahsoka'']]. The issue was written by [[Rodney Barnes]], illustrated by [[Georges Jeanty]], [[Dexter Vines]], and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 9]], 2024.

==Publisher's summary==
''FALLEN JEDI! Hera risks her career to help her friends, while Ahsoka and Sabine confront enemies!''

AHSOKA TANO tracked MORGAN ELSBETH, an ally of THRAWN, to SEATOS, where the ''EYE OF SION'', a
vessel capable of reaching Thrawn through a hyperspace jump between galaxies, is being readied.
After arriving, Ahsoka's ship was attacked by SHIN HATI and MARROK, two Force users in Morgan
Elsbeth's employ. Ahsoka and her Padawan SABINE WREN were forced to land, and are now being
