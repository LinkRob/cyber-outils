The '''arrack vine''' was a type of vine found on [[Lothal]], though due to the [[mining]] activities of the [[Galactic Empire]] on the [[planet]], it had become rare or extinct. The [[poet]] [[Holshef]] once recalled to [[Merei Spanjaf]] how prior to the arrival of the Empire, he could tell which [[Standard week|week]] of [[spring]] it was by the shade of [[Color|green]] in the [[grass]]lands, as well as the scent of arrack vine and [[jogan fruit]].<ref name="secret" />
