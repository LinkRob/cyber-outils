'''Cassius tea'''<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|mauls-tea-set|Maul's tea set}}</ref> was a type of [[tea]] made from the [[Flower|florets]] of a [[cassius tree]]. [[Mandalorian]] individuals, including members of [[Death Watch]] such as [[Pre Vizsla]], were known to drink the tea.<ref name="Eminence" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="19 Date">{{TCWcanondates|514}}</ref> Vizsla blended the tea during a meeting with the former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] to discuss a takeover of the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]], and told him that the tea brought good health.<ref name="Eminence" /> Between [[17 BBY]] and around [[13 BBY]],<ref name="MSL Date">{{ShadowLordDate}}</ref> Maul blended the tea when conversing with the former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Devon Izara]] in the [[Shadow Collective]] [[Maul's safe house|safe house]] on the [[planet]] [[Janix]].<ref name="MSL Chapter 3">{{MSL|Chapter 3: Whispers in the Unknown}}</ref>

[[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]] imported the tea from Mandalore. In his ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', [[author]] and [[The galaxy|galactic]] explorer [[Eloc Throno]] noted the belief about the tea's health benefits.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
The [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity first depicted a tea made from cassius florets in the young reader novel ''[[The Clone Wars: Darth Maul: Shadow Conspiracy]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]] and published [[January 1]], [[2013]].<ref name="Shadow Conspiracy">''[[The Clone Wars: Darth Maul: Shadow Conspiracy]]''</ref> Only weeks later, Cassius Tea appeared in "[[Eminence (episode)|Eminence]]," the fourteenth episode in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="Eminence" /> It was first identified as Cassius Tea in the new [[canon]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', written by [[Cole Horton]] and first published digitally on [[June 23]], [[2020]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />
