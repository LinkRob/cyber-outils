'''Dee Shambo''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] member of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Ministry of Enlightenment]] who, alongside his colleague [[Nisus Osar]], arranged the [[death]] of an [[Imperial Navy]] [[Unidentified Imperial naval inspector|inspector]] in [[5 ABY]] as part of a [[Anti-Ghorman whispering campaign|story]] to turn [[The galaxy|galactic]] opinion against the [[Ghorman (people)|Ghorman]] people of the [[planet]] [[Ghorman]]. In [[4 BBY]], he and Osar attended a [[Maltheen Divide conference|secret meeting]] at the [[Maltheen Divide]] where [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] spoke about the need to remove the Ghormans from Ghorman so that the Empire could [[mining|mine]] [[kalkite]] there. Krennic then introduced Shambo and Osar, who proudly explained their plan to weaponize [[Galaxy|galactic]] opinions against the Ghorman. Media coverage to this effect then continued in the lead up to the [[Ghorman Massacre]] in [[2 BBY]].

==Biography==
===Making a good story===
{{Quote|Last year, when the shipping lanes had to be shut down, how hard would it have been to simply allow the Imperial inspections that most other planets welcome?''"<br />"''And didn't it seem like the Ghor were doing what they always do? Putting themselves first. Did a dedicated Imperial Naval Inspector really have to die to protect Ghorman pride?''"<br />"''We did that. We made the story. We shaped it. We blew it up. We decided when it was over.|Dee Shambo and Nisus Osar|audio=Shambo-Osar-OYL.flac|<ref name="OYL" />}}
Dee Shambo was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who worked in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Ministry of Enlightenment]]. In [[5 BBY]], when Imperial [[Hyperspace route|shipping lanes]] had to be shut down, the [[Ghorman (people)|Ghorman]] people of the [[planet]] [[Ghorman]] resisted Imperial inspections being carried out on their world. Working with<ref name="OYL" /> his<ref name="OYLAD" /> colleague [[Nisus Osar]], Shambo arranged for the [[death]] of an [[Imperial Navy]] [[Unidentified Imperial naval inspector|inspector]] and then shaped a story for the press that the death had been due to the Ghorman's pride. This was part of a wider [[Anti-Ghorman whispering campaign]] designed to turn the rest of [[the galaxy]] against the Ghorman, as plans were forming for the removal of the Ghorman people from Ghorman so that the Empire could [[Mining|mine]] [[kalkite]] there.<ref name="OYL" />

[[File:MaltheenDivideMeeting-OYL.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Dee Shambo and Nisus Osar sit at a long conference table with other Imperials as Orson Krennic stands at the head of the table with a mountain vista visible through a large window behind him.|Dee Shambo and Nisus Osar attended a secret meeting in the Maltheen Divide.]]
Toward that goal, Shambo and Osar attended a [[Maltheen Divide conference|secret meeting]] in<ref name="OYL" /> a [[Maltheen Divide lodge|lodge]]<ref name="OYLAD" /> at the [[Maltheen Divide]] in [[4 BBY]] to discuss next steps for the removal of the Ghormans. The meeting's chair, [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]], explained that there would be no record of the meeting and that no one present was to take notes, as officially none of them were ever there and any security violation within the group would be brought to the personal attention of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="OYL" />

The diretor then played the dozen other attendees a tourism video for Ghorman, detailing the world's [[Ghorman twill|Ghorman silk]] industry and the [[Spider|arachnid]] [[ghorlectipod]]s that made it. Krennic then revealed the Empire's need for kalkite, claiming it was for the [[Emperor]]'s [[Project Celestial Power|Energy Initiative]],<ref name="OYL" /> which in reality was a [[Project Stardust|project]] to construct the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]].<ref name="WEK">{{Andor|Who Else Knows?}}</ref>

===Weaponized opinions===
{{Quote|With the right ideas planted in the right market, in the right sequence, we can now weaponize this galactic opinion.|Dee Shambo|audio=Shambo-WeaponizedOpinion-OYL.flac|<ref name="OYL" />}}
[[File:ShamboTalking-OYL.png|thumb|left|300px|alt=Dee Shambo and Nisus Osar stand behind their chairs and lean on the backs as they speak to others at a conference table next to a window out onto the mountains of the Maltheen Divide.|Dee Shambo spoke about weaponizing galactic opinion about the Ghorman people.]]
Krennic explained that should the Ghorman resist their relocation, the group gathered would be responsible for dealing with any difficulties in terms of both military action and [[propaganda]] that would be required to counter the world's visibility and [[Politics|political]] power on the [[Galaxy|galactic]] stage. Krennic then introduced Shambo and Osar, who stood and explained the work they had already done with the [[inspector]]'s death to turn public opinion against the Ghorman. Shambo then spoke of how the opinion of Ghorman arrogance that they had created could be weaponized against the Ghor.<ref name="OYL" />

After Osar began talking about how they would compare the personality of the Ghorman people to the spiders they worked with, Krennic cut the pair off<ref name="OYL" /> while giving a tickled smile. During a break for gourmet [[Foodstuff|snacks]],<ref name="OYLAD" /> Shambo and Osar helped themselves from a table in the corner then stood drinking by the conference room's fireplace as Krennic approached [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[ISB Supervisor|Supervisor]] [[Dedra Meero]], having noticed that<ref name="OYL" /> she<ref name="OYLAD" /> was not impressed by Shambo and Osar's campaign. The director asked for whatever plan she had concocted while the duo had been speaking and Meero proposed that the propaganda would only get the Empire so far, claiming that what they really needed was to manipulate rebels on Ghorman into acting in a way that they could retaliate against.<ref name="OYL" />

[[File:Anti-GhormanReporter-WAY.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=A reporter speaks in front of a camera droid in Palmo Plaza on Ghorman with crowds of shouting Ghorman people behind a barricades in the background.|As suggested by Shambo, anti-Ghorman propaganda turned the galaxy against the Ghorman.]]
Meero was then given control of the Ghorman project and, with continuing media coverage to turn the galaxy against the Ghorman, she oversaw a [[Ghorman Massacre|massacre]] of the Ghorman people<ref name="WAY">{{Andor|Who Are You?}}</ref> in [[2 BBY]].<ref name="MassacreDate">{{AndorDate|208}}</ref> Following the massacre, several [[senator]]s in the [[Imperial Senate]] followed anti-Ghorman talking points, but Senator [[Mon Mothma]] spoke out against it and declared that the Senate had lost sight of the truth, denouncing the Emperor before [[Escape from the Senate|fleeing]] to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="WTTR">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Shambo simpered proudly<ref name="OYLAD" /> when telling other Imperials about how he and Osus had arranged the death of the Imperial inspector and confidently stated that they could weaponize opinion against the Ghorman. He had light skin, brown [[eye]]s, and black [[hair]],<ref name="OYL" /> which he kept well-groomed<ref name="OYLAD" /> at the Maltheen Divide meeting.<ref name="OYL" />

==Equipment==
During the meeting at the Maltheen Divide,<ref name="OYL" /> Shambo wore a civilian [[suit]]<ref name="OYLAD" /> consisting of a gray [[jacket]] and [[Clothing|pants]] with a white shirt and black tie. He also bore a plaque on the left [[breast]] of the jacket.<ref name="OYL" />
