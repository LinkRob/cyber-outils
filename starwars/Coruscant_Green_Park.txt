'''Coruscant Green Park''' was a park area located on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], filled with [[tree]]s and elegant buildings. By the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]], the park had become endangered. In an effort to preserve it, [[Protect Coruscant Green Park|posters]] were put up around [[Imperial City|Galactic City]] in order to encourage people to take a stand and help protect the park. These posters were written in [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" />

==Behind the scenes==
A torn poster seen on the [[holobooth]] in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[To Catch a Jedi]]" says:<ref name="Catch">{{TCW|To Catch a Jedi}}</ref>

<poem><big><big><big>PROTE</big></big></big><small>''[ripped off]''</small>
<big>CORU</big><small>''[ripped off]''</small>
<small>''[ripped off]''</small>RE<small>''[ripped off]''</small></poem>

The faded poster seen in [[Asajj Ventress]] neighborhood in the episode "[[The Wrong Jedi]]" says:<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" />

<poem>''<small>[faded]</small>''<big><big><big>OTECT</big></big></big>
''<small>[faded]</small>''<big>RUSCANT</big>
''<small>[faded]</small>''REEN PARK</poem>

This article assumes that the third word is supposed to be "[[Color|green]]."
