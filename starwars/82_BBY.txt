==Behind the scenes==
Although [[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine 1|''Star Wars Jedi Master'' Magazine 1]] establishes 82 BBY as the [[Reproduction|birth]] [[Standard year|year]] of [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]],<ref name="JMM1">[[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine 1|''Star Wars Jedi Master'' Magazine 1]]</ref> later sources such as ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' instead placed his birth in [[84 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> This article assumes that ''Star Wars Jedi Master'' Magazine is incorrect in this regard.
