The '''Department of Naval Procurement''' was a department within the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Defense Procurement Office|defense procurement office]] that was active during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. It was tasked with obtaining [[starship]]s, supplies, and new technology for the [[Republic Navy]]. The office and procurement department were placed under the supervision of the [[Procurement Panel]] of the [[Galactic Senate]]'s [[Naval Subcommittee]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Department of Naval Procurement was mentioned in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Rise" />
