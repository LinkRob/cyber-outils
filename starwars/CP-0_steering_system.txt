The '''CP-0 steering system''' was a model of [[speeder bike]] steering system. It provided bikes with a larger maximum turn angle over the standard central poise steering system. Upgrading an [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder]] bike previously equipped with the central poise steering system to the CP-0 steering system cost five hundred [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and required five units of [[durasteel]], and two [[direct-energy circuit]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics}}
During [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref>, the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] paid for the services of a speeder [[Technician|mechanic]] to upgrade her speeder bike with the CP-0 steering system and eventually with the [[A-7 steering system]], providing the necessary parts.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}
