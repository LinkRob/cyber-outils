'''''Captain Phasma'' 1''' is the first issue in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Captain Phasma]]''. The story follows [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] during the events of the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]]. The issue was written by [[Kelly Thompson]], illustrated by [[Marco Checchetto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[September 6]], [[2017]] under the [[Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi|Journey to ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'']] publishing label.

==Publisher's summary==
''As the excitement ramps up for this December's ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'', come back with us to the final moments of ''The Force Awakens'' and the destruction of Starkiller Base to learn the fate of Captain Phasma! Captured by the Resistance and thrown into a garbage masher, we follow the chrome-adorned warrior from the site of the First Order's biggest defeat to the doorstep of this holiday season's biggest blockbuster!''<ref name="Marvel" />

==Plot summary==
During the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] initiates an audio recording of the final moments of [[Starkiller Base]] after escaping from the [[garbage compactor]] on [[Level Six]]. Seeking to cover up her unwilling role in the [[superweapon]]'s destruction, Phasma proceeds to the nearest computer access point under the pretext of ascertaining how the [[First Order]]'s [[Deflector shield|shield defenses]] had been penetrated. As the [[starfighter]] battle above rages, Phasma navigates through the explosions and carnage.

Upon reaching the computer she has used to lower the deflector shields under duress, Phasma deletes all records indicating that she had disengaged the shield defense under duress. While checking to see that nobody else has accessed the computer systems, Phasma discovers that [[Lieutenant]] [[Sol Rivas]] had accessed the defense shield files. In order to cover her complicity in the station's destruction, Phasma blames Rivas for lowering Starkiller Base's shields and proceeds to hunt him down. She sights Rivas on [[Level Two]] of the [[South Platform]] and tries to shoot him but he escapes.

As Starkiller Base collapses around her due to the ruptured fuel cells, Captain Phasma uses a grappling gun to create an improvised flying fox and escape to the surface. On the surface, she encounters several [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] whose leader [[FN-1728]] informs her that [[General]] [[Armitage Hux|Hux]] is being evacuated. Phasma orders FN-1728 to man his post unless ordered otherwise.

Phasma treks through the snow and battle debris in pursuit of Rivas, who heads towards an auxiliary [[hangar]]. At 15:25 [[Standard hour|hours]], Phasma witnesses [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] dueling with an unknown opponent, who turns out to be [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]. As the planet disintegrates, Phasma narrowly avoids falling down a chasm into the [[magma]] below. Phasma chases Rivas, shooting at him but missing due to a second chasm that emerges at front of her. Phasma leaps over the chasm and reaches the auxiliary hangar, only to discover that Rivas has escaped on a [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]].

Inside the hangar, Phasma meets the [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|First Order TIE Fighter Pilot]] [[TN-3465]] and enlists her services in tracking down Rivas in the remaining TIE/sf fighter. They are accompanied by the [[BB-series astromech droid|BB unit]] [[BB-K8]], who was performing a data update on TN-3465's fighter. After escaping the destruction of the planet, Phasma briefs TN-3465 about their mission to hunt down Rivas, claiming that he allowed Starkiller Base to be destroyed. Phasma then ends her audio report.
