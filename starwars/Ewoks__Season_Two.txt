The '''second season''' of the animated [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'' television series]], marketed as '''''The All New Ewoks''''', premiered on [[September 13]], 1986, with the airing of the episodes "[[The Crystal Cloak]]" and "[[The Wish Plant]]" on [[ABC Television Network|ABC]]. Many of the episodes were shorter in length and were paired together in airings. The opening sequence features a [[Friends Together, Friends Forever|new song]] and, unlike the [[Ewoks: Season One|first season]], does not duplicate content from the episodes.
