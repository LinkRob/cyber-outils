The '''Ansuroer sector''' was a [[Sector|subsector]] of the [[Suolriep sector]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It contained the [[Reibrin system]].<ref name="Appendix" />

{{Sec-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Ansuroer sector first originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, and was later recanonized when it was included in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]] and written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
