The '''Baath brothers''' were the owners of the [[Outlander Club]], a club located on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. The [[crime lord]] [[Hat Lo]] used the brothers' private bar in the club prior to the [[First Battle of Geonosis]].<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
