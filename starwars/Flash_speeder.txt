The '''Flash speeder''' was a lightweight [[repulsorlift]] [[landspeeder]] vehicle used by the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces|Royal Guards]] of [[Naboo]]. They were normally used as quick and agile patrol craft, but could be used as a combat vehicle when the need arose. A [[turret]]-mounted [[blaster cannon]] could be affixed to the rear spoiler.<ref name="SWE Flash speeder" />

==History==
[[File:FlashSpeederTCW-SWE.png|thumb|left|200px|An unarmed civilian model Flash speeder.]]
During the [[Invasion of Naboo]], [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and [[Captain]] [[Quarsh Panaka|Panaka]] used Flash speeders and [[Gian heavy patrol speeder]]s in their mission to liberate the capital city of [[Theed]] from the occupation of the [[Trade Federation]].<ref name="Databank Flash speeder" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], Flash speeders were used by a [[Cad Bane's group|group of bounty hunters]] hired by [[Count]] [[Dooku]] during an attempt to kidnap [[Darth Sidious|Chancellor Palpatine]] at the [[Festival of Light]].<ref name="Crisis on Naboo">{{TCW|Crisis on Naboo}}</ref>

A Flash speeder was owned by the [[Pyke Syndicate]] in their [[Oba Diah city|city]] on [[Oba Diah]]. [[Marg Krim]]'s [[majordomo]] [[Fife]] used one to prevent [[Ahsoka Tano]], [[Trace Martez]], and [[Rafa Martez]] from escaping and the [[laser turret]] destroyed Tano's stolen [[MSP80 Pteropter hover pod]] before taking the three back to prison.<ref name="Dangerous Debt" />

Hearing the [[Inquisitor]]s had a [[Duel in Hammertown|mission on]] [[Takobo]], [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Garazeb Orrelios]] discovered they were hunting [[Force-sensitive]] children, which they had [[Ezra Bridger]] take [[Pypey]] to his mother [[Oora]], while Jarrus and Zeb attempt to keep the [[Fifth Brother]] and [[Seventh Sister]] busy, jumping onto a flash speeder piloted by a [[Ithorian]] driver. After causing the Inquisitor's stolen [[V-35 Courier]] to crash, the Fifth Brother threw his [[Fifth Brother's lightsaber|lightsaber]] at one of the engines, causing the speeder to crash, but both the rebels and the Ithorian survived.<ref name="The Future of the Force">{{Rebels|The Future of the Force}}</ref>

In [[2 BBY]], [[Phoenix Cell]] used Flash speeders at their [[Chopper Base|base]] on [[Atollon]]. The [[Lasat]] rebel [[Garazeb Orrelios|Zeb]] and the [[droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]] drove a Flash speeder to investigate a damaged [[sensor beacon]] in [[Sector 6]], where they discovered a [[E-XD-series infiltrator droid|droid]] that they were unaware was an undercover Imperial [[probe droid]]. Once they discovered its true nature, the pair, along with [[AP-5]], managed to disable it and used the Flash speeder to follow the infiltrator droid back to its [[X3-13]] [[hyperdrive pod]] and disabled its long-range [[transmitter]], forcing the droid to fly back with its [[proton warhead]] still active.<ref name="Warhead" />
