'''Ayy Vida''' was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]] [[dancer]] who was a frequent patron of the [[Outlander Club]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. She was [[Slavery|enslaved]] by the [[crime lord]] [[Hat Lo]], who forced her to entertain his clients, and she was friends with fellow Twi'lek [[Lunae Minx]]. On one particular [[Standard day|night]] in [[22 BBY]], Vida was present in the club when the [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] entered while [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|pursuing]] the [[Clawdite]] [[assassin]] [[Zam Wesell]].

==Biography==
[[Slavery|Enslaved]] by the [[crime lord]] [[Hat Lo]], the [[Twi'lek]]<ref name="Die Wanna Wanga" /> [[Gender|female]] [[dancer]] Ayy Vida was forced to be his concubine<ref name="Coruscant" /> and partner<ref name="Die Wanna Wanga" /> and entertain his clients,<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> while also stalling those who sought business with him.<ref name="CLNE">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref> Vida frequented the [[Outlander Club]] on<ref name="Die Wanna Wanga" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="CLNE" /> [[Coruscant]], where she befriended the fellow Twi'lek [[Lunae Minx]], in the hopes that someone would free her from Lo.<ref name="Die Wanna Wanga" />

In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="GA">{{FilmDate|II}}</ref> Vida and Lo took refuge in the warm and welcoming glow of the Outlander Club during a [[Temperature|cold]] and [[Water|rainy]] [[Standard day|night]].<ref name="Coruscant" /> Vida consorted<ref name="AotC" /> and talked<ref name="Coruscant" /> with patron [[Achk Med-Beq]], a conman dressed as a member of the [[Republic Security Force]],<ref name="HC39">{{HelmetCollectionCite|39|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> and Lunae Minx<ref name="Die Wanna Wanga" /> at a table when the [[Clawdite]] [[assassin]] [[Zam Wesell]] [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|attempted]] to escape the pursuing [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] by hiding in the club. Shortly afterward, Wesell was cut down by Kenobi after attempting to attack him at the club's bar, and with drinks in hand, Minx, Vida, and Med-Beq turned their attention to the incident with the rest of the club's patrons, causing Skywalker to dismiss their interest by declaring the event "Jedi business."<ref name="AotC" /> When the Jedi left with the Clawdite, Vida moved to a table to entertain Lo's clients.<ref name="Complete Locations" /> 

==Personality and traits==
Vida had [[Color|yellow]] skin with mottled red stripes and brown [[eye]]s.<ref name="AotC" /> 

==Equipment==
She wore a red and yellow [[Limb|hand]]-dyed [[Clothing|bodysuit]] and a [[silver]]-colored headpiece.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I've never looked like this before. I have been airbrushed all over.|Karina Wakefield, speaking about her costume|audio=KarinaWakefield-Costume.flac|<ref name="ATBP">{{SW|url=video/a-twinkle-beyond-pluto|series=Making Episode II Webdocs|text=Part 5: A Twinkle Beyond Pluto}}</ref>}}
[[File:Ayy_Vida_concept.jpg|thumb|Concept art of Ayy Vida by Dermot Power]]
Ayy Vida was played by [[Karina Wakefield]] in the [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']],<ref name="DtG">''[[Dressing a Galaxy: The Costumes of Star Wars]]''</ref> released on [[May 16]], [[2002]].<ref name="YBY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> The Outlander Club scenes including Vida were shot at [[Fox Studios, Sydney]] in [[Wikipedia:Australia|Australia]] on [[July 17]], [[2000]].<ref name="Mythmaking">''[[Mythmaking: Behind the Scenes of Attack of the Clones]]''</ref> Prior to the film's release, Vida was pictured in "Part 3 - Creative People Producing Solutions," the third episode of the behind-the-scenes docuseries ''[[on location]]''. The episode was released on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="onlocation">{{SWArchive|url=episode-ii/bts/onloc/3.html|series=On Location|text=Part 3 - Creative People Producing Solutions}}</ref> on [[August 15]], 2000.<ref name="onlocation date">{{SWArchive|url=episode-ii/bts/onloc/archive.html|text=''on location'' archive}}</ref> Wakefield's costume was airbrushed all around for her part as Vida.<ref name="ATBP" /> Concept art of the character<ref name="DB">{{DB|character|ayyvida|Vida, Ayy}}</ref> was done by [[Dermot Power]]. One piece with the production code "dp239" depicted the character with green skin wearing beige clothes with fishnet sleeves and high black boots,<ref name="DPower">{{WebCite|url=https://dermotpower.com/star-wars-episode-2attack-of-the-clones|text=Star Wars Episode 2:Attack of the Clones|work=[[Dermot Power]]'s website|archivedate=20260211111619}}</ref> and one depicted her with blue skin and red clothes with black sleeves.<ref name="ArtofII">''[[The Art of Star Wars Episode II: Attack of the Clones]]''</ref> Vida appeared as an unlockable character titled "Female Coruscant Citizen #2" in the ''Twlight of the Republic'' Play Set of the non-canon [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Disney Infinity 3.0]]''.<ref name="Infinity">''[[Disney Infinity 3.0]]''</ref>

In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Vida's name was revealed by the article "Die Wanna Wanga: Encounters of The Twi'lek Kind", written by [[Tim Veekhoven]] and posted on StarWars.com on [[August 19]], [[2014]].<ref name="Die Wanna Wanga" /> The [[Ayy Vida/Legends|name]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was introduced by the article "Extras, Extras: Ros Breden," posted to StarWars.com on [[January 31]], 2002.<ref name="LegendsName">{{SWArchive|url=episode-ii/bts/profile/f20020131/index.html|text=Extras, Extras: Ros Breden}}</ref> In the article "[[Who's Who in the Outlander Club: It's Ladies' Night]]," released in the [[Star Wars Insider 75|seventy-fifth]] issue of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine<ref name="Who">{{InsiderCite|75|Who's Who in the Outlander Club: It's Ladies' Night}}</ref> on [[April 6]], [[2004]],<ref name="SW.com">{{SWArchive|url=community/fanclub/mag/news20040315.html|text=The New Face of Evil}}</ref> author [[Daniel Wallace]] speculated that Vida's name might be a tuckerization of the name of [[Fay David]]. David served as the art department supervisor for ''Star Wars'' Episode II: ''Attack of the Clones'' alongside the third prequel trilogy film, {{Film|III}},<ref name="Who" /> released in [[2005]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref>
