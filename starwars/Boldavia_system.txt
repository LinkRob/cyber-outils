The '''Boldavia system''' was located in the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="The Interior" /> It was situated in grid square I-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="PLanetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=the [[Boldavian spit-spine]]|system=the Boldavia [[Star system|system]]}}</ref> the entity known as the [[Boldavian spit-spine]].<ref name="Missing Pieces">{{InsiderCite|link=Star Wars Insider: The High Republic: Tales of Enlightenment|''Star Wars Insider: The High Republic: Tales of Enlightenment''|Tales of Enlightenment: Missing Pieces}}</ref>
