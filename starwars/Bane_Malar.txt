==Biography==
===The Hunt for Phaedra===
{{Quote|You try anything, the only '''choice''' I'll give you is where to shoot you '''first'''.|Bane Malar to Phaedra|<ref name="Jedi Knights 4">[[Jedi Knights 4|''Jedi Knights'' 4]]</ref>}}
[[File:ZamWesellAurraSingBaneMalarPhaedraNarShadaa-JediKnights4.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Bane, Zam, and Aurra cornering Phaedra]]
Bane Malar was a [[Gender|male]]<ref name="FC" /> [[bounty hunter]] who frequently associated with the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]'s [[Jabba's criminal empire|criminal empire]].<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1" />

Prior to [[34 BBY]]<ref name="JediKnightsDate">The events of ''Jedi Knights'' 4 take place between [[40 BBY]] and [[34 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:JediKnightsDate|here]].</ref> the criminal mastermind [[Phaedra]] [[Robbery of the Galactic Bank of Coruscant|stole]] 10,000 [[InterGalactic Banking Clan|IBC]] [[Credits]] from [[Senator]] [[Beka]], unaware the funds were being held illegally for Jabba the Hutt. Upon discovering the theft, Jabba placed a large dead-or-alive bounty. Bane, with fellow bounty hunters [[Aurra Sing]] and [[Zam Wesell]], pursued Phaedra to [[Nar Shaddaa]]. Before cornering her, the trio agreed to split the bounty equally.<ref name="Jedi Knights 4" />

After Phaedra threatened to detonate [[Phaedra's shuttle|her shuttle]] with remote-controlled [[Thermal detonator|thermal detonators]], the bounty hunters were interrupted by the arrival of [[Jedi Master|Jedi Masters]] [[Shaak Ti]] and [[Qui-Gon Jinn]]. Seizing the distraction, Phaedra boarded a nearly identical [[Phaedra's decoy shuttle|decoy]] of her shuttle. As her droid [[Arjee-Entoo]] piloted the original shuttle away, the bounty hunters rushed back to their starships. The Jedi, recognizing the deception, abandoned their pursuit.<ref name="Jedi Knights 4" />

After destroying the decoy shuttle, the group believed their mission was complete and traveled to [[Tatooine]] to claim their reward from Jabba, unaware Phaedra had fled to [[Ord Mantell]].<ref name="Jedi Knights 4" />

===Hunting a reprobate===
[[File:BaneMalarCapturesRikDuel-DuelOfTheReprobates.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Bane capturing Rik Duel]]
{{Quote|Reach for that blaster, lose your hand.''"<br />"''If you're gonna kill me, what do I need my '''hand''' for? You... '''are''' here to kill me, right?''"<br />"''Nothing personal. '''Jabba's''' orders...|Bane Malar and Rik Duel|<ref name="Duel">{{StoryCite|story=Duel of the Reprobates|book=Revelations (2023) 1}}</ref>}}
By [[4 ABY]],<ref name="reprobatesDate">"Duel of the Reprobates" takes place prior to the [[death]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], which is dated to [[4 ABY]] in ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Malar tracked down the scoundrel [[Rik Duel]] on Jabba's behalf. Duel convinced Malar that he was worth more alive than dead, resulting in Duel becoming Jabba's "top lieutenant."<ref name="Duel" />

===The Silvan Kaan affair===
{{Quote|As it is, Mr. Malar, I'm unclear as to what you want me to tell you.''"<br />"''You think you've been ''subtle''? You think I haven't seen you having whispered conversations in quiet corners with that Twi'lek?''"<br />"''My purview as Jabba's translator often requires me to converse with Mr. Fortuna&mdash;''"<br />"''Not Fortuna. ''Silvan Kaan''. The guy's so slippery, those head-tails of his might as well be ''serpents''.|Eightyem and Bane Malar|<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1" />}}
Malar was present in the [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] of Jabba the Hutt when the crime lord was visited by [[Twi'lek]] [[criminal]] [[Silvan Kaan]]<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1" /> in 4 ABY.<ref name="Date" /> Malar grew suspicious of Kaan and Jabba's [[translator droid]], [[80-M|Eightyem]], whom Kaan had persuaded into a partnership. The bounty hunter took Eightyem aside and, instructing the [[droid]] not to lie, confronted him about his actions. Though Malar made direct accusations towards the droid, the interpreter maintained a façade of ignorance, causing the bounty hunter to admit his own uncertainty as to the violations committed. Malar then told Eightyem that he would discover the truth of the matter. Eightyem informed Kaan of what had transpired, though the Twi'lek remained unconcerned.<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1" />

Malar later foiled an attempt on Jabba's life by [[Charn Roondha]], an [[Ithorian]] who collected taxes imposed by the Hutt. The bounty hunter held the would-be [[assassin]] at the point of his own weapon, a [[WESTAR-35 blaster pistol]] that Malar noted Roondha was likely not cleared to have on his person, a fact confirmed by Jabba's aide, [[Bib Fortuna]]. The weapon had indeed been smuggled in by Eightyem on Kaan's suggestion.<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1" />

Malar accompanied Jabba's posse aboard the Hutt's [[sail barge]], the ''[[Khetanna]]'', to the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]] out in the [[Dune Sea]], where Roondha was fed to [[Almighty Sarlacc|the]] [[sarlacc]] that nested there. The bounty hunter commented on the Ithorian's demise and made a veiled threat toward Kaan about joining the tax collector in the sarlacc's gut, where he would be digested over the course of a thousand years. Malar then told Kaan to enjoy the party and departed.<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1" />

Sometime later at Jabba's Palace, Malar ambushed Kaan as he removed Eightyem's restraining bolt, training his sniper rifle on the Twi'lek. The bounty hunter suggested that the criminal take a trip back to the Great Pit of Carkoon, presenting him with a holographic recording of Eightyem's transgressions. Kaan was brought before Jabba, who indeed condemned the man to the sarlacc for his deceptions, while Eightyem was sentenced to disintegration on [[supervisor droid]] [[EV-9D9]]'s torture rack for his own treachery.<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:BaneMalar-ROTJJabbasPalace1.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Bane aiming his blaster rifle]]
{{Quote|Fine. But if anyone gets to '''kill''' her, it's '''me'''.|Bane Malar to Zam Wesell|<ref name="Jedi Knights 4" />}}
Bane Malar was very confident in his abilities. While tracking bounties, he often taunted his targets. The protocol droid 80-M described him as preferring to take in bounties dismembered and dead rather than alive.<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|Is there a problem, Mr. Malar?''"<br />"''You tell me, droid. And don't ''lie''. I'm too smart for you to outsmart&mdash;so you best not ''try''.|Bane Malar to Eightyem|<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1" />}}
Bane Malar maintained a career as a bounty hunter<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1" /> and tracker.<ref name="FC" /> A natural telepath, Malar would use [[glitterstim]] to enhance his abilities, which allowed him to invade the minds of those he was hunting down.<ref name="FC" />  Malar was successful at disrupting Roondha's assassination attempt on Jabba the Hutt with his blaster rifle. The bounty hunter was confident in his ability to outsmart the droid Eightyem.<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1" /> He was also able to pilot starships.<ref name="Jedi Knights 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bane Malar was created for the [[1983]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], although no shots featuring him are present in the final cut.<ref name="rotj">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], Malar was first identified and given a name in [[1998]] in the card game [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game|''Star Wars'' Customizable Card Game]],<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=Jabba's Palace Limited|cardname=Bane Malar}}</ref> and was first mentioned in the [[Canon|canon continuity]] in the [[2013]] game [[Star Wars: Force Collection|''Star Wars: Force Collection'']].<ref name="FC" />
