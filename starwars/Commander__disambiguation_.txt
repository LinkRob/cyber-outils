'''Commander''' could refer to several things:

==[[Canon]]==
===Military ranks===
*'''[[Commander]]'''&mdash; A military rank given to a [[commanding officer]], or a position given to an individual of an operation.
**'''[[Clone Commander]]'''&mdash; A [[clone trooper officer]] military rank, higher than [[Clone Captain]].
***'''Clone ARC Commander'''&mdash; A [[clone trooper]] officer military rank used by the elite [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s.
***'''[[Clone Marshal Commander]]'''&mdash; A high-ranking [[Cloning|clone]] officer military rank, in command of [[corps]]-sized units.
**'''[[Jedi Commander]]'''&mdash; A military rank used by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] military officers within the [[Republic Military]] during the [[Clone Wars]].
**'''[[Junior Commander]]'''&mdash; A military rank used by the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]].
**'''[[Lieutenant Commander]]'''&mdash; A military rank used by multiple groups, including navies and [[Starfighter Corps]].
***'''[[Second Lieutenant Commander]]'''&mdash; A military rank used in the [[Imperial Starfighter Corps]], and had the ability to lead a [[squadron]] within a [[fighter wing]].
**'''[[Mid Commander]]'''&mdash; A military rank used by the [[Chiss]] Expansionary Defense Fleet that was placed above Junior Commander.
**'''[[Senior Commander]]'''&mdash; A military rank used by the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet that was placed above Mid Commander.

===Positions/titles===
*'''[[Commander-in-Chief]]'''&mdash; A title used by the individual of a government who had complete control of the government's military and armed forces.
*'''[[Commander of Organic Training]]'''&mdash; A title used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].
*'''[[Field Commander]]'''&mdash; A position used by a commanding officer of ground forces used by the [[Galactic Empire]].
*[[Fleet Commander|'''Fleet Commander''']]&mdash; A position used by a commanding officer of [[Navy|naval]] forces used by the [[New Republic]].
**'''[[Supreme fleet commander]]'''&mdash; A title used by the leader of the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]], previously known as the '''supreme commander of the Alliance Fleet''' under the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Navy]].
*'''[[Flight Commander]]'''&mdash; A position used by a commanding officer of [[starfighter]] forces within the Galactic Empire.
*'''[[Forensic science commander]]'''&mdash; A title used by a leader of [[Coruscant Security Force]]'s forensic analysis group.
*'''[[Port commander]]'''&mdash; A title used by the Galactic Empire of an [[Imperial officer]] who oversaw the administration of a [[spaceport]], and could be held by a [[lieutenant]].
*'''[[Purge Trooper Commander]]'''&mdash; A title used by officers of [[Purge Trooper]]s used by the Galactic Empire.
*'''[[Squadron commander]]'''&mdash; A title used by the commanding officer of a starfighter squadron.
**'''[[Second Squadron Commander]]'''&mdash; A title used by an officer within a starfighter squadron, used by the [[First Order]].
*'''[[Starfighter Commander]]'''&mdash; A title used by a commanding officer within an Imperial [[capital ship]] that was responsible for overseeing and coordinating the ship's starfighter complement.
*'''[[Station commander]]'''&mdash; A title used by officers that were responsible for a station control center.
*'''[[Stormtrooper Commander]]'''&mdash; A title used by the commanding officer of an Imperial capital ship's [[stormtrooper]] contingent. It could also refer to a title used by stormtrooper officers. [[Scout trooper]] officers were referred to as '''Scout Trooper Commander'''.
*'''[[Sub-commander]]'''&mdash; A title used within the Imperial Navy of the Galactic Empire.
*'''[[Supreme Commander (disambiguation)|Supreme Commander]]'''
**[[Supreme Commander (Galactic Empire)|'''Supreme Commander''']] of the Empire&mdash; The title held by the leader of the entire [[Imperial Military]] and its [[Imperial High Command]].
**[[Supreme Commander (Mon Cala)|'''Supreme Commander''']] of Mon Cala&mdash; The title used by the [[King of Mon Cala]] as the leader of the [[Mon Cala planetary defense services]].
**'''[[Supreme Commander of the Alliance Armed Forces]]'''&mdash; The title held by the leader of the entire [[Alliance Military]] and its [[Alliance High Command]].
**'''[[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies]]'''&mdash; The title held by [[General]] [[Grievous]] as leader of the [[Confederacy military|military forces]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].
*'''[[Tactical commander]]'''&mdash; A title used within the [[Nikardun Destiny]]'s [[Yiv's battle forces|armed forces]].
*'''[[Vehicle commander]]'''&mdash; Used to describe Imperial and First Order officers who oversaw operations from military vehicles.
**'''[[Imperial combat assault tank commander]]'''&mdash; Specialized vehicle commanders who served as [[Imperial combat assault tank pilot]]s.
*'''[[Wing Commander]]'''&mdash; A title used by the leader of a starfighter wing.
