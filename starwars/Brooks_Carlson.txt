'''Brooks Carlson'''<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="SWE" /> who was a [[sergeant]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|Pathfinders]] unit during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> By [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the [[Battle of Endor]] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> he was considered a veteran and had trained the [[Communications officer|communications operative]] [[Corporal]] [[Beezer]]. In that year, Carlson was an automatic choice for a [[Endor strike team|strike team]] made up of volunteer [[Rebel Commando|commandos]] that was put together by [[General]] [[Han Solo]] and [[Major]] [[Bren Derlin]] for a [[Battle of Endor|mission]] to the [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. The team, which also included Beezer, were tasked with destroying the Imperial [[Death Star's shield generator|shield generator]] that protected the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star II]] [[superweapon]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

The team arrived on the moon in the stolen Imperial [[shuttle]] ''[[Tydirium]]'' and proceeded to a security bunker at the rear of the generator. At the bunker, they fell into an Imperial trap, but overcame the Imperial forces with aid from the local [[Ewok]] population, allowing them to destroy the [[Deflector shield generator|generator]]. This allowed for the destruction of the [[Death Star]] by the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] above. Carlson had light skin and [[Color|gray]] [[hair]]. He wore a [[Color|green]] [[helmet]], a white [[Clothing|shirt]], and a green-and-brown camouflaged duster.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Brooks Carlson was pictured in an entry for [[Rebel trooper]]s on the [[StarWars.com]] [[Encyclopedia (StarWars.com)|Encyclopedia]].<ref name="SWE" /> The character was then identified in the English release of "[[The Battle of Endor and the Fall of the Empire]]," an entry in [[De Agostini]]'s [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> that was originally released in Spanish on [[June 29]], [[2021]].<ref name="De Agostini">{{DeAgostini|url=libros-comics/enciclopedia-star-wars|text=Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref>

The image used in the Encyclopedia entry was a promotional photo taken for the [[1983]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']],<ref name="BtM">''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]''</ref> although Carlson does not appear in the film itself.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> The photo depicts Carlson,<ref name="BtM" /> portrayed by stuntman [[Alf Joint]],<ref name="Aficionado">{{Blogspot|subdomain=starwarsaficionado|url=2010/09/classic-image-rebel-assembly.html|name=Star Wars Aficionado|text=CLASSIC IMAGE: REBEL ASSEMBLY!}}</ref> and other commandos stood with protagonist [[Luke Skywalker]] as their prisoner in one of the Death Star II's corridors.<ref name="BtM" /> The name Beezer originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used on two cards in the [[1999]] ''[[Endor Limited]]'' expansion set of the [[Decipher]] [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game|''Star Wars'' Customizable Card Game]].<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=Endor Limited|cardname=Sergeant Brooks Carlson}}</ref><ref name="Beezer">{{CCG|set=Endor Limited|cardname=Corporal Beezer}}</ref>
