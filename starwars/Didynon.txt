The '''Didynons''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] in [[the galaxy]]. They had eyes that could retract into the calloused dermal plates on its head. [[Engi Golba]] was a Didynon from [[Didyma V]] who escaped his [[Planet|world's]] annexation by the [[First Order]] to join the [[Resistance]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
