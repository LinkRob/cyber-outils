'''Aruza''' was a celestial body<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> located in the [[Aruza system]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="The Interior" /> It was situated in grid square I-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Aruza was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Aruzan]] [[Gender|female]] [[Manaroo]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Bounty Hunters 14|''Bounty Hunters'' 14]], which depicts [[Manaroo]] as a [[prisoner]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Manaroo had become indebted to the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> and was held as a [[prisoner]] by Jabba<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> within his [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 14">[[Bounty Hunters 14|''Bounty Hunters'' 14]]</ref> on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] as leverage against her boyfriend [[Dengar]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aruza was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Aruza/Legends|Aruza]] first appeared in "[[Payback: The Tale of Dengar]]," a short story written by [[Dave Wolverton]] that was published in the [[1996]] anthology ''[[Tales of the Bounty Hunters]]''.<ref name="Payback">{{StoryCite|story=Payback: The Tale of Dengar|book=Tales of the Bounty Hunters}}</ref>
