"'''A Hero's Journey: A Tale of Two Heroes'''" is an article published in [[Star Wars Insider 174|''Star Wars Insider'' 174]]. Part of [[Tricia Barr]]'s series on the Hero's Journey, it sees Barr and [[B.J. Priester]] discuss [[Finn]]'s journey in {{Film|VII}}.
