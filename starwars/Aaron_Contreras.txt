'''Aaron Contreras''' is the author of the [[2019]] book ''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''.<ref name="Art of Fallen Order">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> Contreras also served as narrative lead for both the 2019 [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> and its [[2023]] sequel ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> Conteras worked as Narrative and Cinematic Director on the upcoming turn-based tactics game ''[[Star Wars Zero Company]]''.<ref name="Zero Comp Update">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-zero-company|text=Join the Ranks of ''Star Wars Zero Company'' - Update}}</ref>
