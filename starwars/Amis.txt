'''Amis''' was a [[Mandalorian]] [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[cadet]] of the [[Imperial Academy of Mandalore|Royal Academy of Government]] on [[Mandalore]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. In the course of his academic career, he attended classes with several friends, including [[Korkie Kryze]]&mdash;nephew to [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] of the [[New Mandalorians]]. Although Amis and his companions managed to expose a [[black market]] [[Mandalorian black market conspiracy|conspiracy]] on their [[homeworld]], they were unable to save the Duchess during the [[Takeover of Mandalore|rise]] of [[Death Watch]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|But I thought you said it was the duty of every citizen to expose corrupt officials.''"<br />"''I'm only saying that you should take this slowly.|Amis and Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="Academy" />}}
During the [[Clone Wars]], Amis was a [[cadet]] of the [[Imperial Academy of Mandalore|Royal Academy of Government]]<ref name="Amis DB" /> in [[Sundari]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]].<ref name="Academy" /> As a student, he was groomed for a [[Politics|political]] career in order to lead the next generation of [[Mandalorian]]s.<ref name="Amis DB" /> In the course of his academic career, he maintained friendships with fellow cadets [[Lagos]], [[Soniee]], and [[Korkie Kryze]]&mdash;the young nephew of [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] of the [[New Mandalorians|New Mandalorian]] government.<ref name="Academy" />

[[File:MandoCadetsInClass-TA.png|thumb|left|250px|During the Clone Wars, Amis studied politics alongside his friends Korkie Kryze, Lagos, and Soniee.]]
As the war progressed across [[the galaxy]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Amis and his classmates received a guest speaker from the [[Jedi Order]]&mdash;[[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], [[Jedi Commander]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] and apprentice of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]. Inspired by Tano's lecture, Korkie convinced his friends to investigate the [[Foodstuff|food]] shortage in the city, an activity which Amis preferred over hunger. With Tano's help, who used [[information retrieval]] on a [[hologram]] to confirm Almec was the hooded figure in the secret meeting with [[Gotal]]s, they discovered an [[Treason|illegal]] [[Mandalorian black market conspiracy|operation]] established by [[Prime Minister]] [[Almec]]. Almec sought to use the [[black market]] trade network as an economic asset for Mandalore. Due to the efforts of Amis and his classmates, Almec was arrested and sent to [[prison]].<ref name="Academy" />

In the aftermath of [[Death Watch]]'s [[Takeover of Mandalore|rise to power]], Amis aided his friends and the [[Nite Owls]] in an attempt to rescue Korkie's deposed aunt. Their plan ultimately failed, however, consequently leading to Satine's [[Death|execution]] as the capital became engulfed in a state of [[Mandalorian Civil War (19 BBY)|civil war]].<ref name="The Lawless" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:AmisFull-TCWCEJtB.png|thumb|right|180px|Amis took up arms to rescue Duchess Satine.]]
{{Quote|Let's go down to the warehouse district and see if there really is a food shortage.''"<br />"''Beats sitting around here listening to my stomach growl.|Korkie Kryze and Amis|<ref name="Academy" />}}
Amis was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]].<ref name="Amis DB" /> As an adolescent, he had [[Color|blond]] [[hair]], blue [[eye]]s, and light skin.<ref name="Academy" /> By the time of the New Mandalorian government's collapse, he had a shaved head<ref name="The Lawless" /> and he was 1.68 [[meter]]s (5 [[Foot|feet]], 6 inches) tall.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

In his [[time]], Amis was one of the most promising cadets of the Royal Academy of Government,<ref name="Amis DB" /> a prestigious institution that trained the best and brightest of Mandalorian youth to pursue careers in politics.<ref name="Lagos DB">{{Databank|lagos|Lagos}}</ref> Amongst his fellow cadets, Amis was noted for his flippant attitude and high-spirited nature. However, he was also highly intelligent<ref name="Amis DB" /> and loyal to his friends,<ref name="Academy" /> having assisted them in revealing the corruption at the highest level of Mandalorian politics.<ref name="Amis DB" /> 

While initially growing up under the pacifist ideology of the [[New Mandalorians]], Amis continued to support his comrades and took up arms in an attempt to rescue the Duchess Satine from [[Death Watch]] custody after their [[takeover of Mandalore]].<ref name="The Lawless" />

==Skills and abilities==
As a idealistic cadet in Mandalore's Royal Academy, Amis was productive in his government studies. Amis was also highly skilled in investigations,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> as alongside his friends, he uncovered a smuggling plot devised by Prime Minister Almec.<ref name="Academy" />

==Equipment==
Amis was equipped with a [[WESTAR-35 blaster pistol]] while he and his friends rescued Satine from the [[royal prison]].<ref name="The Lawless" />

==Behind the scenes==
Amis first appeared in "[[The Academy]]," the sixth episode in [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|Season Three]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series.<ref name="Academy" /> He was portrayed by voice actor [[Omid Abtahi]], who reprised the role in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]] episode "[[The Lawless]]."<ref name="The Lawless" />
