'''Bheriz''' was a remote [[Mining/Legends|mining]] [[Planet/Legends|planet]].

{{Planet-stub}}

==History==
Sometime during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]], it was contacted by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|CIS]] forces and the [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Republic]]. Its native sentient species were the [[Bherizian]], who were led by the [[Viceroy of Bheriz]].
