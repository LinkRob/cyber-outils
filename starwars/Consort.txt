A '''consort''' was the [[Marriage|spouse]] of a reigning [[Monarchy|monarch]]. [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] was the consort of [[Queen]] [[Breha Organa]], who [[Alderaanian monarchy|ruled]] over the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] until its [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="anhillustrated">''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]''</ref> During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Odelia]], as the wife of the reigning queen [[Adrialla]], and [[Thandeka]], as the wife of the reigning queen [[Dima]], both [[Eirami government|served]] as queen consort over the planet [[Eiram]].<ref name="The High Republic: Convergence">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]''</ref><ref name="The High Republic: Into the Dark">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref>
