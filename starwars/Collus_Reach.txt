The '''Collus Reach'''<ref name="shield">{{StoryCite|story=Shield of the Jedi|book=The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life}}</ref> was located in<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Collus system]], which was part of the [[Thuldin's Grant sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]],<ref name="Interior" /> and lay in grid square J-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> At some point between around [[389 BBY]] and around [[379 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Rooper Nitani]] is fifteen [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]]. Furthermore, by the [[time]] of the events of "[[Shield of the Jedi]]," Nitani is around eighteen years old, and that story also establishes that she explored the ruins in the Collus Reach no more than ten years earlier.</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] explored the ruins of old [[The Force|Force]] [[Religion|sects]] deep in the Collus Reach, which she remembered shortly before her [[Rooper Nitani's Jedi Trials|Jedi Trials]]<ref name="shield" /> around 379 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> while reflecting on the challenges she had faced and lessons she had learned in the past few [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="shield" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Collus Reach was mentioned in the short story "[[Shield of the Jedi]]," written by [[George Mann]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="shield" />
