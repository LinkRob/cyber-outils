'''Ekria''' was an individual whose name was carved on the wall of a safehouse on the [[planet]] [[Mapuzo]]<ref name="Part III">{{Kenobi|Part III}}</ref> used by the [[Hidden Path]], and on the wall of another facility they used on the [[planet]] [[Jabiim]]. The Path was a network that smuggled [[Force-sensitive]]s and [[Jedi]] away from the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Part V">{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref> in [[9 BBY]].<ref name="Timeline">{{SW|url=news/future-lucasfilm-projects-revealed|text=Future Lucasfilm Projects Revealed}} states that ''Obi-Wan Kenobi'' is set ten [[Standard year|years]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, ''Obi-Wan Kenobi'' must be set in [[9 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Ekria.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Ekria in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity]]
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Ekria was introduced in "[[Part III]]" of ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'', released on [[June 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="Part III" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Ekria/Legends|Ekria]] was a [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]]<ref name="Fortune">''[[Reversal of Fortune]]''</ref> who survived the [[Great Jedi Purge/Legends|Great Jedi Purge]] and eventually renounced her Jedi legacy, preparing to live out the rest of her life as an ordinary person.<ref name="Game">''[[Evasive Action: End Game]]''</ref> She first appeared in ''[[Reversal of Fortune]]'', a series of daily webstrips published on [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|''Hyperspace'']] October [[2004]] through June [[2005]], written by [[Paul Ens]] and illustrated by [[Tom Hodges|Thomas Hodges]].<ref name="Fortune" />
