==Events==
*[[Silver Knights]] begin to clash with the [[Blue Dragon]]. The two would clash over thirty times over the course of the next ten years.<ref name="Dragon Knights">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the web novel states that the [[Silver Knights]] clashed with the [[Blue Dragon]] over thirty times in the past [[Standard decade|decade]], the clashes must have began in [[210 BBY]].</ref>
*Li Qiong begins working for the [[Nan family]]<ref name="Nan handmaiden">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the webnovel states that [[Li Qiong]] had worked for the [[Nan family]] for ten [[Standard year|years]], Li must have been employed by them around [[210 BBY]].</ref> of the Aakaash system, becoming a loyal handmaiden to Nan Holi's daughter, Nan Moyou.<ref name="Vow">''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''</ref>

Since the webnovel states that [[Marino Pomana]] had worked as [[Jamie Brasson]]'s [[chef]] for ten [[Standard year|years]], Pomana must have been employed in that capacity in [[210 BBY]].</ref>
**President Brasson begins the construction of the [[Begamor barrier]],<ref name="Begamor barrier">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]].
