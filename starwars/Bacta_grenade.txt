'''Bacta grenades''' were [[grenade]]s that released a mist of [[bacta]] upon impact that healed those within range. They were carried by [[clone medic]]s during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Beginnings" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bacta grenades first appeared in [[Star Wars Galaxies]]'', a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Star Wars video games|MMORPG]] developed by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment<ref name="Galaxies">{{GalaxiesAED}}</ref> that was released on [[June 26]], [[2003]]<ref name="SWGofficialrelease">{{LucasArts|url=company/release/news20030617.html|text=LucasArts Announces Star Wars Galaxies™: An Empire Divided™ Will Release June 26, 2003|date=2003-06-17}}</ref> and shut down on [[December 15]], [[2011]],<ref name="Shutdown">{{SonyCite|url=playerinfo|text=IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT ''STAR WARS'' GALAXIES™}}</ref> where they were added to the game as part of the [[2005]] New Game Enhancements as a long-range healing ability for the medic class.<ref name="Galaxies" /> They became [[canon]] when they appeared in ''[[Star Wars Journeys: Beginnings]]'', a mobile game developed by [[Disney Interactive]] that was released on [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] devices in [[2014]].<ref name="Beginnings" />
