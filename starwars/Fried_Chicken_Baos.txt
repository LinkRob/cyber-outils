'''Fried Chicken Baos''' was a dish comprised of [[chicken]], [[vegetable]]s, and a bun. The dish was served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]], where the dish was served with two of the item. It was consumable by [[human]]s.<ref name="Docking Bay 7" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:FriedChickenBaos.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The dish was served to guests of the Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge theme part in Disneyland.]]
Fried Chicken Baos was first made available to guests in the restaurant Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[Disneyland]] during the [[Season of the Force]] event in [[2024]]. It was served as an entrée from [[April 5]] to [[June 2]], 2024.<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=2024/03/disney-eats-season-of-the-force-foodie-guide-for-disneyland-resort-2024|text=Disney Eats: Season of the Force Foodie Guide for Disneyland Resort 2024}}</ref> The meal returned to Disneyland in [[November]], 2024,<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch And Dinner Menu}}</ref> and again for the [[2025]] Season of the Force.<ref name="DPB25">{{DPB|url=dlr/disney-eats-season-of-the-force-foodie-guide-for-disneyland-resort-2025|text=Disney Eats: Season of the Force Foodie Guide for Disneyland Resort 2025}}</ref> Its menu description read, "Two crispy chicken baos, spicy katsu sauce, and creamy slaw."<ref name="Menu" />
