The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] '''captured''' four loaded [[transport]]s in the [[Pyros system]] in [[3 ABY]]. [[Minister of Finance (Alliance)|Minister of Finance]] [[Nower Jebel]] messaged Rebel Alliance [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] following the action, complaining that even that victory would not make up for the loss of the [[ion cannon]] at the [[Battle of Hoth]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

The communication was preserved in the archive ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
The capture is mentioned in the [[2017]] in-universe reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
