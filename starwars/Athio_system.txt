The '''Athio system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Mayagil sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that fell within grid square N-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It contained the terrestrial astronomical object [[Athio III]], where the [[Jedi]] [[Velerie Tide]] disguised herself as a local to hide from the [[Galactic Empire]] following the [[Great Jedi Purge]]<ref name="Dawn" /> that began in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> By [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Kenobi date">{{KenobiDate|IV}} The episode shows [[Velerie Tide]] to already be [[Death|dead]].</ref> Tide had been captured and [[Death|killed]].<ref name="Dawn" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
The Athio system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
