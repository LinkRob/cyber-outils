'''Arda''' was an astronomical object located in the [[Gordian Reach]] of the regions known as the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="RO World Locations" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> The celestial body was situated in grid square Q-6 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="TFABG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arda was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[Galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] boxed set for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="TFABG" />
