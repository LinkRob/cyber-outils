'''Christophsians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] race native to the [[planet]] [[Christophsis]]. They looked similar to [[human]]s in appearance.<ref name="Hostage Crisis TG" /> During the [[Battle of Christophsis]], [[Christophsian rebels]] fought against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], they were represented by a [[senator]] in the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Hostage Crisis TG" />
