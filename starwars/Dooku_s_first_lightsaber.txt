This '''lightsaber''' was built by [[Dooku]], future [[Head of State|leader]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Sith Lord]], during his [[time]] as a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]].<ref name="CVD" /> At some point after becoming a [[Jedi Knight]],<ref name="Updated and Expanded" /> Dooku set aside his first weapon and built a new and [[Dooku's lightsaber|superior lightsaber]] with a personalized design that he used throughout his years as both a [[Jedi Master]] and [[Sith]].<ref name="CVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dooku's first [[lightsaber]] was first mentioned in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''.<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref>

The sources are inconsistent regarding when Dooku set aside this lightsaber in favor of building a new one that he used for the rest of his life. While both ''Absolutely Everything You Need to Know''<ref name="AEYNTK" /> and its [[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded|updated]] edition state that he used his second lightsaber as a Jedi Knight,<ref name="Updated and Expanded" /> ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'' states that he built it as a Jedi Master.<ref name="CVD" />
