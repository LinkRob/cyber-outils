{{Quote|Ah&hellip; yes, wood. Not a common choice. And only wood from a Brylark tree is as strong as metal.|Huyang|<ref name="A Test of Strength" />}}
The '''Brylark tree''' was a type of [[tree]] whose wood was as strong as metal and was sometimes used for [[lightsaber]] hilts. During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[architect droid]] [[Huyang]] asked [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Gungi]] what he envisioned [[Gungi's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] to look like. Gungi, through [[the Force]], envisioned his lightsaber to be made of wood. Huyang, knowing that the only wood strong enough would be from a Brylark, retrieved a Brylark wood hilt, which Gungi used for his lightsaber. Huyang also stated that it was a rare choice among hilts, just as [[Wookiee]]s like Gungi were rare among the [[Jedi]].<ref name="A Test of Strength" />
