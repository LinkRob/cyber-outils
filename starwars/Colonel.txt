'''Colonel''' ('''Col.''') was a [[military rank]] that was common to most militaries throughout [[the galaxy]]. It was generally below the rank of [[general]] and above the ranks of [[major]] and [[commander]]. The rank itself was relatively high in the greater chain of command.

==Description==
===Overview===
Colonel, abbreviated to Col. when addressing an individual of the station,<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> was a [[military rank]] used by [[officer]]s in the armed forces of various factions throughout [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]], as well as [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement and intelligence agencies]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> The rank of colonel was below that of [[Brigadier General|brigadier general]]<ref name="HC 79">{{HelmetCollectionCite|79|Databank A-Z}}</ref> and above a [[Lieutenant Colonel|lieutenant colonel]].<ref name="Lords of the Sith">''[[Lords of the Sith]]''</ref> The title of [[High Colonel]] also existed.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref>

In the [[Galactic Empire]], a colonel served as the [[commanding officer]] of an [[Imperial Army]] [[division]]<ref name="Dark Lord 8">[[Darth Vader (2017) 8|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 8]]</ref> or a [[brigade]].<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> They could also serve as the senior troop [[commander]] of a [[Star Destroyer]]'s army complement, or as the commander of a [[space station]].<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> In the [[Imperial Starfighter Corps]], a colonel served as a [[Wing Commander|wing commander]], and could direct their [[starfighter]] forces from [[carrier]]s such as Star Destroyers.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> Colonels also served as [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|Imperial pilots]] for high-ranking officials<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> or as [[pilot]]s of advanced starfighters.<ref name="Starfighter Missions">''[[Star Wars: Starfighter Missions]]''</ref> The commander of the [[Emperor's Royal Guard]] could hold the rank of colonel,<ref name="Master of Evil">''[[Master of Evil]]''</ref> and the rank also existed in the [[Imperial Military]]'s [[Science and Engineering Division]]<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> as well as [[Imperial Intelligence]]'s clandestine [[Ubiqtorate]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> Members of [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order|COMPNOR]]'s [[Imperial Security Bureau]] could also use the rank of colonel, with its [[director]] holding the rank,<ref name="Dawn" /> as could the [[Assistant Director]] of the [[Imperial Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research]]<ref name="Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref> and the [[Deputy Director]] of the [[Naval Intelligence Agency]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

==History==
===Fall of the Republic Era===
[[File:ColonelGascon-SW.png|thumb|left|220px|Colonel Meebur Gascon, strategist and tactician of the Republic High Command.]]
Between around [[39 BBY]]<ref name="Flashback start note">{{WhenThrassMetThrawnDate}}</ref> and [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Flashback end note">The flashbacks in ''Chaos Rising'' occur prior to that novel's main events, which are set in [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Chiss officer]] [[Wevary]] held the rank of colonel within the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Ascendancy military|armed forces]], commanding [[Taharim Academy]] for the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> During the pan-[[The galaxy|galactic]] [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] that took place between [[22 BBY]] and 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the rank was used by both factions. [[Meebur Gascon]] of [[Republic High Command]], a military strategist, held the rank of colonel before<ref name="Secret Weapons">{{TCW|Secret Weapons}}</ref> being promoted to [[Brigadier General]].<ref name="HC 79">{{HelmetCollectionCite|79|Databank A-Z}}</ref>

[[Republic officer]] [[Strulka]] served as a decorated [[soldier]] and eventually rose to the rank of colonel.<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> [[Halland Goth]], commander of the Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]]'s [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Red Guard]], served with the rank of colonel.<ref name="Master of Evil" /> [[Separatist officer]] [[Coburn Sear]] also held the rank, and served under [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Martial Commander]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] and General [[Kleeve]]. Colonel Sear lead [[Separatist Droid Army]] forces during the [[Third Battle of Mygeeto]].<ref name="Kanan 9">[[Kanan 9|''Kanan'' 9]]</ref>

===Imperial Era===
====Early reign and rebellion====
Following the end of the Clone Wars and the Republic's [[Proclamation of the New Order|transition]] to the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> in 19 BBY,<ref name="Timelines" /> the rank of colonel was used throughout its organizations such as the [[Imperial Army]],<ref name="Vader 13">[[Darth Vader (2017) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 13]]</ref> the [[Imperial Starfighter Corps]],<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> and the [[Imperial Security Bureau]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> As the Red Guard was reorganized into the Emperor's Royal Guard, Goth continued to serve as its commander with the rank of colonel.<ref name="Master of Evil" /> At around this time,<ref name="Barokki date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Darth Vader (2017) 12|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 12]] to [[19 BBY]].</ref> Colonel [[Barokki]] served as the commanding officer of an Imperial Army [[Barokki's division|division]], and personally gave a report to [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. The Emperor was unsatisfied with the division's performance, and Barokki was punished.<ref name="Dark Lord 8">[[Darth Vader (2017) 8|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 8]]</ref> Barokki was also a conspirator to murder the Emperor's [[emissary]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], and was [[Death|killed]] in the when Vader randomly eliminated members of the [[Imperial Officer Corps]].<ref name="Dark Lord 12">[[Darth Vader (2017) 12|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 12]]</ref> Strulka continued to serve the Empire, before ultimately resigning from the [[Imperial Military]].<ref name="Uprising" />

[[File:Bergon-2017DarthVader13.png|thumb|right|250px|Colonel Bergon, army commander who participated in the Invasion of Mon Cala.]]
In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Bergon Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Darth Vader (2017) 15|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 15]] to [[18 BBY]].</ref> Colonel [[Bergon]] served as a commander during the Imperial [[Invasion of Mon Cala]]. Following the [[Battle of Dac City]] and the destruction of Imperial forces deployed to surface [[City|cities]], Bergon deployed Imperial forces from [[aerial landing platform]]s. Bergon was subsequently killed during an [[attack on Bergon's sky-base]],<ref name="Dark Lord 15">[[Darth Vader (2017) 15|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 15]]</ref> although Imperial efforts were eventually successful.<ref name="Dark Lord 17">[[Darth Vader (2017) 17|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 17]]</ref> Also in that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Lesser Evil Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' to [[18 BBY]].</ref> the [[Kilji]] [[Tildnis]] served as a colonel within the [[Kilji Illumine]], a theocratic conquering faction in the [[Unknown Regions]]. As part of their [[conquests of enlightenment]], Colonel Tildnis served in a [[Crofyp's task force|task force]] of [[warship]]s under [[General]] [[Crofyp]], though the force was subsequently destroyed.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> Sometime in the early [[Imperial Era]], Colonel Mosh Barris served as the senior troop commander of the Imperial [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Strikefast]]'', and coordinated Imperial forces as they attempted to capture [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]].<ref name="Thrawn" /> Colonel [[Toln Yurak]] was an Imperial Army officer who was interviewed by ''[[Clone Wars Quarterly]]'', and expressed support for the [[Clone Wars reenactment movement]].<ref name="Rise" /> [[Wullf Yularen]], who had served as a [[Fleet Admiral]] within the [[Republic Navy]], continued to serve in the [[Imperial Navy]]<ref name="BMF 18">{{BuildFalconCite|18|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> before ultimately pursuing a career with the Imperial Security Bureau, holding the rank of colonel and rising to become its [[Deputy Director|deputy director]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> and then [[director]]. Yularen would hold his rank and position within the ISB throughout the Imperial Era.<ref name="Dawn" />

===New Republic Era===
====End of Galactic Civil War, early New Republic====
[[File:ColonelWard-SWGoH.png|thumb|250px|left|Colonel Ward served both the Rebel Alliance and New Republic.]]
Following the Battle of Endor, Imperial forces found themselves scattered, including Colonel Nuress's 204th and Colonel [[Madrighast]] with his [[Madrighast's unit|unit]]. Madrighast commanded the ''[[Unyielding]]'' and attempted to link up with scattered Imperial forces.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> Colonel Nuress died during the [[Battle of Pandem Nai]], while<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> Madrighast and their unit eventually joined the [[Chadawan government]], an Imperial remnant, before ultimately perishing in the [[attack on Chadawa]] by the loyalist 204th Imperial Fighter Wing, now led by Colonel [[Soran Keize]], and the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> Colonel [[Gralm]] served as the station commander for the [[Remitik system munitions depot]], and coordinated Imperial forces as they [[Battle in the Remitik system|defended]] against a [[New Republic]] attack.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> [[Barson Nestroph]], a colonel in the Empire, was the father of [[Heater (pilot)|Heater]], a [[New Republic pilot]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

Meanwhile, the Rebel Alliance began the process of transitioning itself into the [[New Republic]] after the Battle of Endor,<ref name="TPATS">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref> culminating in the formal establishment of the New Republic in 5 ABY.<ref name="NR5 date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that the [[New Republic]] was officially founded twenty-nine [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]], thereby placing the formation of the New Republic from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] in [[5 ABY]].</ref> New Republic [[spymaster]] [[agent]] [[Calder Behrens]] served<ref name="Insurgency Rising 2">[[Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising 2|''Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising'' 2]]</ref> as a colonel.<ref name="Rebel Insignia">[[Calder Behrens]] is depicted with what ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'' denotes as a Colonel's [[rebel command insignia]].</ref> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate 21">{{MandoDate|21}}</ref> Colonel [[Tuttle]] served in the [[New Republic]]'s [[Requisitions]] department.<ref name="Chapter 21" /> Colonel [[Ward]]<ref name="Celeb swcom">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-the-mandalorian-and-grogu|text=''The Mandalorian and Grogu'' Kickstart ''Star Wars'' Celebration Japan 2025}}</ref> served in the [[New Republic Defense Fleet Starfighter Corps]]<ref name="Celeb panel">{{SWYouTube|RsstzKR_I7s|Jon Favreau and Sigourney Weaver &#124; Star Wars Celebration LIVE!}}</ref> and worked with the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] to eliminate [[Imperial Remnant]] [[warlord]]s.<ref name="Mando&Grogu">{{SWYouTube|IHWlvwu8t1w|The Mandalorian and Grogu &#124; Official Trailer &#124; In Theaters May 22}}</ref>

====Rise and reign of the First Order====
[[File:Datoo_-_SW_Card_Trader.png|thumb|right|180px|Erich S. Datoo, a Colonel in the First Order]]
The [[First Order]], a new faction risen from the ashes of the Empire, also used the rank of colonel across its forces.<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Colonel [[Kaplan (colonel)|Kaplan]] served aboard the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Finalizer]]'', and was killed during a [[Escape from the Finalizer|rebel escape]].<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> [[Erich S. Datoo]] served as a colonel and was responsible of the primary fire control room on the First Order's [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]].<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Colonel [[Barrut]] commanded the [[Maxima-A-class heavy cruiser|''Maxima-A''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Fortitude]]'' during the [[Battle of Ikkrukk]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 31">[[Poe Dameron 31|''Poe Dameron'' 31]]</ref> [[Genial]] served as a colonel within the First Order's [[Reform Office]],<ref name="Resistance Reborn">''[[Resistance Reborn]]''</ref> while [[Grav Talis]] also served as a First Order colonel.<ref name="Starcruiser">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref> Colonel [[Ansiv Garmuth]] served within the [[First Order Security Bureau]], and served aboard the ''[[Supremacy]]'', the [[flagship]] of the First Order.<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

In response to the threat of the First Order, [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] established the [[Resistance]],<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> a paramilitary faction that received limited support from sympathizers in the New Republic.<ref name="Resistance DB">{{Databank|the-resistance|The Resistance}}</ref> Following the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|decapitation]] of New Republic leadership,<ref name="Episode VII" /> the Resistance stood as the only notable force opposing the advancing First Order, although it also received its fair share of losses with the [[Battle of Oetchi]] and [[Battle of Crait]]. In any case, the group survived and continued its fight.<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> [[Aftab Ackbar]] served as a colonel within the Resistance as a [[pilot]]<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> and provided the weakened Resistance with critical naval vessels.<ref name="Spark">''[[Spark of the Resistance]]''</ref> Around this time, the [[Pastorian]] Colonel [[Squill]] served as part of the military forces of the [[Pastoria kingdom]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 29">[[Poe Dameron 29|''Poe Dameron'' 29]]</ref>
