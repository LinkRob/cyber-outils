'''Agron cake''' was a type of [[cake]].<ref name="TOTF" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> the [[physicist]] [[Xylan Graf]]&mdash;who had been forced to maintain the [[Stormwall]] [[hyperspace]] barrier for the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]&mdash;left his position and remanded himself to [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] custody. While on the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[cruiser]] ''[[Axiom]]'' being debriefed, Graf expressed his feelings that the Nihil leaders [[Marchion Ro]] and [[Abediah Viess]] were delusional about the technology's limitations, remarking that the Jedi seemed to be slipping through the barrier "as easily as if it's a piece of agron cake."<ref name="TOTF" />
