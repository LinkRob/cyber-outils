In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''conflict over agriworlds''' was fought by the [[New Republic]] three [[sector]]s away from the [[Cerberon system]]. During the battle, the New Republic won [[supply ship]]s which were later sent to supply the [[planet]] [[Troithe]] with resources. The confrontation was fought around the same time as the [[Cerberon system campaign]]. Republic [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] discussed the battle for at least two nights in a [[Hologram|holographic]] conference with [[New Republic High Command]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
