{{Quote|{{'}}When the righteous lose the light, evil once dead shall return.' That's so vague it could refer to anything or anyone!|Obi-Wan Kenobi reciting a prophecy to Qui-Gon Jinn, who wondered if it referred to Darth Wrend|audio=KenobiProphecy.mp3|<ref name="MA" />}}
'''Darth Wrend''' was a legendary [[Sith Lord]] who fought against the [[Jedi Order]] and was believed [[Death|dead]], only to return and fight them once again. Around [[67 BBY]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] wondered whether a prophecy in the [[holocron of prophecy]] about evil returning once "the righteous [lost] the light" referred to [[Darth]] Wrend rather than a potential reincarnation of the [[Sith]] Order as believed by others who had known the prophecy.

==Biography==
[[File:AoR-QuiGonJinn-art.jpg|thumb|right|200px|As a Padawan, Qui-Gon Jinn (pictured) believed that a prophecy of the Sith might have referred to Darth Wrend.]]
Darth Wrend was a [[Sith Lord]]<ref name="Darth note">''[[Star Wars Made Easy]]'' establishes that the title of [[Darth]] translates to "Dark Lord" and was given to the [[Sith Lord]]s. As ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' establishes that Darth Wrend bore the title of Darth, they must have been a Sith Lord.</ref> who fought against the [[Jedi Order]] and was eventually thought [[Death|dead]]. At some point afterward, the [[Sith]] nevertheless returned to wage war against the [[Jedi]]. By the [[time]] Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] began studying the [[holocron of prophecy]]<ref name="MA" /> in [[67 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' establishes that [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] began studying the [[holocron of prophecy]] in [[67 BBY]]. ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' places [[Darth]] Wrend's mention to a few [[Standard month|months]] after Jinn had commenced his research.</ref> [[Darth]] Wrend was considered legendary among the Order.<ref name="MA" /> Around that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Jinn read an ancient prophecy stating that the Sith would disappear yet return again once "the righteous [lost] the light." The Padawan mentally considered that most notes on the prophecy had interpreted it as a potential reincarnation of the Sith Order, though Jinn wondered that it might specifically refer to Wrend and their return from believed death to fight the Order once again.<ref name="MA" />

In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' to [[40 BBY]].</ref> when [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was the Padawan of Jinn, Kenobi mentioned the prophecy to his [[Jedi Master]] and complained about its vagueness.<ref name="MA" /> In [[32 BBY]], the Sith were revealed to still be active with the discovery of [[Maul|Darth Maul]], who killed Jinn in a [[lightsaber duel]] during the [[Battle of Naboo]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
