{{Quote|Ghorman is a single product economy. We import almost everything we need to survive. The blockade didn't have to go very long to remind us how vulnerable we are.|Dasi Oran to Mon Mothma|<ref name="Ever Been" />}}
In an incident related to the alleged [[death]] of a [[Unidentified Imperial naval inspector|naval inspector]] in<ref name="OYL" /> [[5 BBY]],<ref name="DateAldhani" /> the [[Galactic Empire]] '''blockaded''' [[Hyperspace route|shipping lanes]] to the [[Ghorman system]] on orders from the [[Imperial Ruling Council]], including council members [[Ars Dangor]] and [[Sly Moore]].<ref name="Aldhani" /> While the supposed death came after months of [[Ghorman (people)|Ghormans]] refusing to comply with the [[Imperial Navy]]'s inspections, in reality, the death of a naval inspector was arranged by [[Ministry of Enlightenment]] employees [[Dee Shambo]] and [[Nisus Osar]] as part of an [[Anti-Ghorman whispering campaign]] while plans were forming for the [[Suppression of Ghorman|removal]] of the Ghorman people from [[Ghorman]] to allow Imperial [[mining]] of the rare [[mineral]] [[kalkite]] there.<ref name="OYL" /> In the end, the blockade was lifted on the order of the [[Imperial Senate]] once [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] rallied enough support for the Ghormans.<ref name="Ever Been" />

The blockade was a learning moment for the Ghorman people, who were faced with the reality that&mdash;as a single product economy whose export was [[Ghorman twill]]&mdash;they imported the essentials of survival. Therefore, Ghormans, such as [[Senator]] [[Dasi Oran]], were left with the fear that another such blockade or similar Imperial crackdown could spell doom for their people. Alternatively, other anti-Imperial Ghormans, such as those among the [[Ghorman Front]], were not dissuaded.<ref name="Ever Been" />
