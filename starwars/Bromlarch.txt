'''Bromlarch''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /> located in the [[Vish sector]] of [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was represented in the [[Galactic Senate]] by [[Senator]] [[Caelor Gaans]]. Bromlarch had a [[Bromlarch planetary aqueduct system|planetary aqueduct system]] that was damaged during a [[Groundquakes at Bromlarch|series of groundquakes]].<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /> [[Galactic Republic]] [[Senator]]s [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and [[Rush Clovis]] both had three nights to rescue Bromlarch's aqueduct system, thus saving the planet from famine,<ref name="TRoC" /> by creating the [[Mid Rim Cooperation Motion]]. Senator [[Mina Bonteri]] and [[Jedi Master]] [[Depa Billaba]] accompanied them.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />
