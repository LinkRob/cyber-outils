Sometime into the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Rise" /> fought from [[22 BBY]] until [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval force]] had devised plans for a '''flotilla''' of [[Bulwark-class battle cruiser|''Bulwark''-class battle cruisers]]. The [[warship]]s were secretly constructed under the supervision of [[Admiral]] [[Dua Ningo]], a stalwart Separatist from the [[Commerce Guild]]'s [[Sullustan Home Guard]]. Hoping to use them in battle against<ref name="Rise" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]], Ningo had expected the [[flotilla]]'s completion and functionality<ref name="Rise" /> by [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> two [[Standard year|years]] after the [[First Battle of Geonosis|Battle of Geonosis]] kickstarted the conflict.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bulwark flotilla was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Bulwark Fleet]] was introduced in ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'', a [[2005]] reference book authored by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Kevin J. Anderson]].<ref name="TNEC">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''</ref>
