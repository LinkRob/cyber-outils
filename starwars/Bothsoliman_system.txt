The '''Bothsoliman system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Hollan sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> It was located in grid square M-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Bothsoliman]]. According to the [[human]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], good [[tailor]]s could be found on Bothsoliman, and he had spent time there<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> by [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> with a [[Unidentified sommelier|sommelier]] giving him a bottle opener for [[Azuran steam spirit]] there.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bothsoliman system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
