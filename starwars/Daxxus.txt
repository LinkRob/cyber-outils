{{Quote|How did I talk my way out of the Imperial inspection of my cargo? Why, by simply describing the symptoms of my Anoat pox! The berry stains on my face may have helped, too.|Daxxus|<ref name="SW:U">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref>}}
'''Daxxus''' was a [[Blarina]] criminal who operated in the [[Anoat sector]] at the [[time]] of the [[Iron Blockade]]. On one occasion, Daxxus deterred [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] from inspecting their cargo by staining their face with [[berry]] juice and pretending to have the [[Anoat pox]].<ref name="Daxxus" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
Eventually, Daxxus joined the crew of [[the Smuggler]], who dispatched the Blarina on a variety of missions throughout the Anoat sector.<ref name="Daxxus" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Daxxus was created for the [[canon]] mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', which was developed by [[Kabam]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms and released in [[2015]],<ref name="SW:U" /> where they were a unique 5 star crew member.<ref name="Daxxus" />
