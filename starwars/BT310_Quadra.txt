The '''BT310 Quadra''' was a model of [[podracer]] manufactured by [[Balta-Trabaat]]<ref name="Endless Vigil" /> that had four [[engine]]s.<ref name="Ben Quadinaros Databank" />

==History==
In the [[Standard year|year]] [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the inexperienced [[Toong]] [[podracing]] [[pilot]] [[Ben Quadinaros]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> rented<ref name="Star Pilot">[[Star Wars: Star Pilot (2015)|''Star Wars: Star Pilot'']]</ref> a hastily put together<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> BT310 for use in the [[Boonta Eve Classic]],<ref name="Star Pilot" /> an annual podrace hosted on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] by the [[Hutt]]s to commemorate the [[Boonta Eve]] holiday.<ref name="Boonta Eve Classic Podracer Pilots">{{Databank|boonta-eve-classic-podracer-pilots|Boonta Eve Classic Podracer Pilots}}</ref> However, the quadra-Podracer suffered from an engine malfunction<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> that caused it to stall on the starting grid.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> When its engines did ignite, the BT310's [[power coupling]]s shorted out,<ref name="Ben Quadinaros Databank" /> causing them to break loose and blast off in all directions<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> of [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]],<ref name="Ben Quadinaros Databank" /> destroying the podracer.<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know" /> Despite this, Quadinaros continued piloting a BT310 and was depicted using one in ''[[The Sport's Greatest Rivalry]]'', a poster pitting him against his arch-rival, [[Sebulba]].<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Ben Quadinaros, in the "Explode-At-The-Starting-Line-Mobile."|Fodesinbeed Annodue|<ref name="Exit from Endor">{{DroidTales|Exit from Endor}}</ref>}}
The BT310 Quadra was created for the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="The Phantom Menace" /> It was first identified in Ben Quadinaros's entry in the now-discontinued [[Encyclopedia (StarWars.com)|Encyclopedia]] on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Ben Quadinaros SWE" />

It is likely that the BT310's "quadra-Pod" racing engines factored in the naming of Ben Quadinaros. It was realized as a detailed concept model and then rendered as a computer-generated vehicle.<ref name="Ben Quadinaros Podracer">{{DB|vehicle|benquadinarospodracer|Podracer, Ben Quadinaros'}}</ref>

In the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' episode "[[Race on Tatooine]]," Quadinaros piloted a BT310 during the [[29th Annual Bricklayers' Classic]] on Tatooine; as he was about to win the race, its engines broke loose as a podracer piloted by [[Zander Freemaker]] collided with the quadra-Podracer, causing the Toong to lose.<ref name="Race on Tatooine">{{FreemakerAdventures|Race on Tatooine}}</ref>
