'''Code E-9-73A''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] regulation that forbade [[Imperial Military|military]] personnel to donate, barter, or sell [[ration pack]]s to civilians. For each violation of the code, a report was filed to the culprit's [[commanding officer]].<ref name="Survival Guide">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]''</ref>
