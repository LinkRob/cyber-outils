'''Bastons''' were a type of [[melee weapon]] similar to a [[truncheon]]. They were similar in size and shape to [[sword]]s or [[lightsaber]]s, and could be wielded in a similar fashion. When her [[starship]] was captured in a [[tractor beam]] by the ship of [[pilot]]s [[Chardri Tage]] and [[Tamsin]] early in the [[Imperial Era]], former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] armed herself with two bastons from her ship's weapons box, using them to ambush and knockout Tage and Tamsin before they had time to react.<ref name="Ahsoka" />
