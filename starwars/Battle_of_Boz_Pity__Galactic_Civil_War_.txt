The '''Battle of Boz Pity''' was a battle fought during the [[Imperial Era]]. Following the [[Rescue of Admiral Ackbar (Galactic Civil War)|rescue]] of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] en route to [[Kessel]], Ackbar was injured during the Battle of Boz Pity, but recovered after [[bacta]] treatment.<ref name="Rebel Alliance" />
