'''Ashla''' was a [[moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Tython]]. For millennia, it was held as a sacred world to the [[Jedi Order]]. Members of the Order would travel to the moon so they could [[Meditation|meditate]] on [[Disturbance in the Force|disturbances]] and [[Force vision|visions]] they would find troubling. 

==Description==
Ashla was a terrestrial moon that orbited the planet [[Tython]] in the [[Tython system]], located in the [[Deep Core]]. It had a breathable [[atmosphere]] and a surface of saltwater [[ocean]]s and islands with large golden sand beaches. A native [[species]] was the [[Diet|carnivorous]] flying creature named a [[wingmaw]], which populated the skies of the moon.<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> 

==History==
Months after the [[Dedication of the Starlight Beacon|dedication]] of the [[Starlight Beacon]]<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]] journeyed to the [[Isle of Seclusion]] on Ashla aboard his [[Jedi Vector]]. Mann had experienced a terrible [[Force vision]] during the aforementioned dedication, and chose the Isle as a place where he could [[Meditation|meditate]] in solitude on what he had seen. He waded into the ocean surrounding the Isle, and eventually received a vision that directed him to the planet [[Valo]].<ref name="The Rising Storm" />

==Behind the scenes==
The moon Ashla was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm]]'', which was written by [[John Ostrander]], illustrated by [[Jan Duursema]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="DJFS 1">[[Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm 1|''Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm'' 1]]</ref> on [[February 15]], [[2012]].<ref name="DJFS 1 date">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/18-026/Star-Wars-Dawn-of-the-Jedi-1-Jan-Duursema-cover|text=Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi #1 (Jan Duursema Cover)}}</ref> In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Ashla was first mentioned as a name for the [[Light side of the Force]] in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels|Rebels]]'' episode "[[The Holocrons of Fate]]" ([[October 1]], [[2016]]).<ref name="The Holocrons of Fate">{{Rebels|The Holocrons of Fate}}</ref> The moon Ashla itself first appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]].<ref name="The Rising Storm" />
