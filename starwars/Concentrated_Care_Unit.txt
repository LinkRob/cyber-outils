The '''Concentrated Care Unit''' was a room located on level 9 in [[Lina Soh Hospital]].<ref name="MakeItStop" /> In [[1 BBY]], after [[Luthen Rael]] stabbed himself using the [[Nautolan bleeder]], he was kept safe in this room with [[life support]] for his [[Torture|interrogation]] the next morning.<ref name="MakeItStop" /> However, when Rael's assistant, [[Kleya Marki]], infiltrated the [[Medcenter|hospital]] and entered the room, she removed the life support that kept him alive, [[Death|killing]] him within [[Second|seconds]], and she kissed him on his forehead goodbye.<ref name="MakeItStop" />
