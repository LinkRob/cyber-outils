'''Bryan Burk''' is an American film and television producer who has collaborated on many projects with film director [[J.J. Abrams]]. Burk, alongside Abrams and the duo's production company, [[Bad Robot Productions|Bad Robot]], co-produced the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'']].
