'''Diurna''' was a massive subterranean [[city]] on the [[planet]] [[Aubadas]] that was populated by the [[Katikoot]] [[species]]. Its buildings were made of [[soapstone]] and consisted primarily of towers and arches, with [[hoversled]] being the primary mode of transportation for the city's citizens. The city was also largely supported by the planet's [[Life-giving moss]]. It was located inside a cavern, the walls of which were lined with phosphorescent waterfalls that fed into a river-like moat around the city filled with plankton that illuminated the entire cavern.<ref name="QFTHC" />

In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Quest for the Hidden City]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the city was lead by [[Minister of works]] [[Kittik]] who contacted the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Galactic Republic]] for help as they were facing an energy crisis. [[Republic Pathfinder]] team [[Outer Rim Expedition Two-Five-Two]] responded first, before heading to Aubadas's twin planet [[Gloam]] to help find fuel supply for the city. But after that team went missing, [[Jedi Master]] [[Silandra Sho]]'s [[Master Sho's Pathfinder team|pathfinder team]] visited the city in search of team Two-Five-Two.<ref name="QFTHC" />
