'''Citizen officials''' were government workers for the [[Empire (Rei'izu)|Empire]]. They were required to take exams [[Standard year|annually]]. Two [[Standard decade|decades]] after the failed [[Sith rebellion]], posters with the dates for the annual examinations were put up on plastered walls of a communal storehouse in the [[city]] [[Osou]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Genbara]]. Citizen officials were known to, at times, be afraid to speak candidly in the presence of certain figures.<ref name="Ronin" />

When [[Jedi lord (Empire)|Jedi lord]] [[Hanrai]] conversed with the former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] aboard his [[Imperial Dreadnought|Jedi Dreadnought]] ''[[Reverent]]'', the [[Gender|lord]] recounted the Ronin's tale to the man himself as the [[black current of the Force]] brought Hanrai scents, sounds, and images that evoked heavy parchment on which reports to the [[Unidentified Emperor|Emperor]] were customarily written down, the low whisper of a citizen official afraid to speak frankly, and the type of [[soup]] he had with his favorite [[information broker]] when they met.<ref name="Ronin" />

==Behind the scenes==
