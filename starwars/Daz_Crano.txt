'''Daz Crano'''<ref name="Star Wars Adventures 29" /> was a [[Nautolan]] who served as a [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] in the [[Resistance]]. Crano assisted the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' in landing on [[Kashyyyk]] during [[First Order]] [[Blockade of Kashyyyk|occupation]] of the [[planet]].<ref name="Star Wars Adventures 27" />

==Biography==
During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], Daz Crano accompanied the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to [[Kashyyyk]] in her [[T-70 X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] to assist in disrupting the [[First Order]]'s [[Blockade of Kashyyyk|operations]] on the planet. Daz's friend [[Beaumont Kin]], who was on board the ''Falcon'' along with [[Chewbacca]], [[Nien Nunb]], and the [[porg]] [[Terbus]], told her about the history of Kashyyyk as they fought through the First Order's [[blockade]], with Daz interjecting that he ought to focus instead on not getting blasted. Daz and the ''Millennium Falcon'' successfully landed on the planet, although Daz's [[Starship|ship]] took some minor damage.<ref name="Star Wars Adventures 27" />

Shortly after they landed, another Wookiee told Chewbacca something that appeared to upset him, although neither Daz nor Beaumont knew enough [[Shyriiwook]] to understand what it was. Chewbacca then ran off into the [[Black Forest|forest]] with Terbus, leaving Daz and Beaumont behind.<ref name="Star Wars Adventures 27" />

Daz, Beaumont, and Nien realized that with Resistance reinforcements being held back by the First Order's blockade, it was up to them to help Chewbacca, although Beaumont insisted that the Wookiee could take care of himself. Daz got back into her X-wing and flew over the forest to try to spot Chewbacca, while the other Resistance troops joined some Wookiees in searching on foot. Chewbacca spotted Daz's ship flying overhead and tried to get her attention, which unfortunately alerted some nearby [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] to his presence.<ref name="Star Wars Adventures 28">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=28|story=Ghosts of Kashyyyk, Part 2}}</ref>

Daz didn't see Chewbacca, but a porg which had snuck onto her ship did, and managed to communicate to her that it had spotted something. Beaumont expressed doubt that the porg actually saw anything useful, but Daz decided to check again regardless. The Resistance fighters and Wookiees doubled back as well, and successfully aided Chewbacca in freeing some Wookiees who had been captured by the First Order. When talking to Beaumont after they regrouped, Daz said the experience reinforced her belief that everyone in the [[galaxy]] needed to work together to fight the First Order.<ref name="Star Wars Adventures 29" />

==Personality and traits==
A [[Nautolan]], Daz had [[Color|green]] skin and large black eyes. The tendrils on her head were a darker shade of green.<ref name="Star Wars Adventures 27" /> She believed strongly in the goals of the Resistance.<ref name="Star Wars Adventures 29" /> While she had a sense of humor, she disapproved of joking around in serious situations.<ref name="Star Wars Adventures 27" />
