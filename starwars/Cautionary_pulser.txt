A '''cautionary pulser''',<ref name="Solo The Official Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> also refered to as just a '''pulser'''<ref name="Outlaws" /> was a device built into a [[blaster]] handle that buzzed when the firearm was low on ammunition.<ref name="Solo The Official Guide" /> During the [[showdown on Savareen]],<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> [[Han Solo]]'s [[Han's heavy blaster pistol|blaster]] was equipped with a cautionary pulser.<ref name="Solo The Official Guide" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a member of the [[Rokana Raiders]] working guard duty in the [[Khepi Tomb]] in the [[Khepi system]] complained that the pulser in their [[blaster]] had been fried for several [[Standard week|weeks]], meaning that they kept running out of ammunition. As a solution, they suggested the group steal better blasters.<ref name="Outlaws" />
