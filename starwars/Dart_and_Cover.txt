"'''Dart and Cover'''" is an animated short that is part of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''. It was released on the Disney Channel's YouTube channel on [[December 10]], [[2018]], and is the second of twelve shorts.<ref name="Shorts Press Release">{{WebCite|url=https://www.jedinews.com/film-music-tv/articles/new-star-wars-resistance-shorts-debut-on-disney-channel-youtube|text=New Star Wars Resistance Shorts Debut On Disney Channel YouTube|author=LaSalata, Justin|date=2018-12-10|work=Jedi News|archivedate=20200729224922}}</ref>

==Official description==
''Kaz and Bucket's game goes awry as they try and impress their pals.''<ref name="Dart and Cover" />

==Plot summary==
In [[Aunt Z's Tavern]], Kazuda Xiono shows off at [[holo-darts]] as [[Torra Doza]], [[Neeku Vozo]], [[BB-8]], Bucket, [[Bolza Grool]] and [[Al]] watch. Torra isn't impressed by Kaz's pre-throw bragging, but all three darts hit close to the center of the board, with one almost being a bulls-eye. Bucket isn't impressed by this, and after Kaz is done, he takes off his helmet to collect the darts. To Torra's surprise, the [[astromech droid]] decides to go next. Neeku decides to hide behind Torra, but Bucket's first two shots, surprisingly, are bulls-eyes. Kaz laughs when Bucket's third shot goes wide, but the dart ricochets off of several objects, including a tray held up by [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza|Aunt Z]], to strike Kaz in the buttocks. He then chases after Bucket as the droid flees the tavern.
