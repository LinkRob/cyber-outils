The '''Atollon Coral Mesa'''<ref name="TMCB Concept Art">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/the-mystery-of-chopper-base-concept-art-gallery|text="The Mystery of Chopper Base" Concept Art Gallery}}</ref> was a land formation situated on the [[planet]] of [[Atollon]]. A broad, flat-topped elevation, it was covered in huge [[coral]]line formations.<ref name="The Mystery of Chopper Base" /> Many creatures, including [[krykna]] and [[dokma]], lived in the area. The [[Bendu]], a powerful being who represented a balance in [[the Force]], also lived in a [[Bendu's valley|valley]] in the center of the Coral Mesa.<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref>

In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> [[Phoenix Cell]] established a [[Chopper Base|base]] on the Coral Mesa.<ref name="The Mystery of Chopper Base" />
