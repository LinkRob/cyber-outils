A '''baptism''' was a dark ritual undertaken by the [[Nightsisters]] of [[Dathomir]], using the dark [[magick]]s, that would result in an individual being proclaimed a true Nightsister. During the [[Clone Wars]], baptisms by the Nightsister clans would take place in an [[oil]]y subterranean lake beneath the [[Nightsister lair]].

==The ritual==
{{Quote|Do you pledge yourself to the sisterhood, to the magicks, and the old ways?''"<br />"''I do.''"<br />"''Do you abandon your old life for this new one?''"<br /> “‘I do. <br />"''Your loyalty, your life.''"<br />"''My loyalty, my life.|Mother Talzin questions Asajj Ventress during her baptism|<ref name="Massacre" />}}
The ritual would take place beneath the [[Nightsister lair]] of [[Dathomir]], and required the [[Mother (title)|Mother]] of the Nightsisters, during the [[Clone Wars]] that title was held by [[Talzin]], as well as several regular Nightsisters. The Mother would question the would-be Nightsister on their loyalty to their clan, and if the prospective sister was deemed fit, they would be dunked into the [[water]]s of the lake beneath the Nightsister lair. The Nightsister would also be wrapped in [[magical ichor]] by the Mother, and lifted from the lake. For a moment, the individual's [[eye]]s would roll back into their heads, and then return to their natural state, signifying the beginning of their life as a Nightsister.<ref name="Massacre" />

After the baptism, the Nightsisters would organize a feast to celebrate the arrival of their new Sister.<ref name="Massacre" />

==History==
[[File:Baptism-Massacre.png|thumb|right|250px|Ventress's baptism]]
One such baptism took place during the Clone Wars, when Mother Talzin baptized [[Asajj Ventress]], who came to Dathomir in the hopes of being re-initiated into her clan after being [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] from it for a long [[time]], as an apprentice to [[Count]] [[Dooku]].<ref name="Massacre" />
