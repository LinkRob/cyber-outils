The '''First Order delivery service''' was a [[First Order]] mailing service active on the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]''<ref name="No Escape" /> by [[34 ABY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|1|20}}</ref> the business partners [[Flix]] and [[Orka]] hid in a crate<ref name="No Escape" /> labeled "TALIVAR organic material"<ref name="Bucket's List No Escape">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-no-escape-part-1-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 8 Fun Facts from "No Escape: Part 1" - ''Star Wars Resistance''|date=2019-03-11}}</ref> with the [[DUM-series pit droid|pit droid]] [[G-LN]] and<ref name="No Escape" /> their pet<ref name="Bitey">{{Databank|bitey|Bitey}}</ref> [[gorg]] [[Bitey]] while in their business aboard the ''Colossus'', the [[Office of Acquisitions]], intending to flee from the First Order.<ref name="No Escape" />

The pair hoped that the First Order delivery service would ship the crate to [[Flix's flock|Flix's]] [[Flix's mother|mother's]] [[Flix's mother's home|home]] on the celestial body [[Talivar]] and stayed inside the container for [[Standard day|days]]. Not long after, however, the [[Supertanker fuel depot|platform]] was [[Mission to contact the Resistance|submerged]] into<ref name="No Escape" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Number">{{InsiderCite|190|Resistance Revisited|''Resistance'' Revisited}}</ref> [[Castilon]]'s [[Water|oceans]]. The [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] found the pair in the crate and informed them of this, causing Flix and Orka disappointment that their plan to have the First Order delivery service transport them would not work.<ref name="No Escape" />
