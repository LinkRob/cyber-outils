'''Chewie Goes to the Dentist''' is a comic printed in ''[[Star Wars Jedi Quest Kids Club 2]]''.

==Plot summary==
[[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] forces [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]] to get a tooth pulled, and enlists [[Watto/Legends|Watto]], supposedly "the best dentist in the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim]]." Watto, assisted by [[DUM-series pit droid/Legends|pit droids]], attempts to remove Chewbacca's bad tooth, while [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]] reassures Chewbacca that it "won't hurt a bit." Chewbacca cries out in pain, destroying the dental chair and knocking Watto to the ground. Watto asks what happened, and Han explains that sometimes "it's best to let the [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] whine," to which Watto retorts that Chewbacca is an "ungrateful fur ball."
