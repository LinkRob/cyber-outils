"'''Down And Out On Coruscant'''" is a vignette written by [[Michael Allen Horne]] for the ''[[Dark Empire Sourcebook]]'' in [[1993]].

==Plot summary==
[[General/Legends|Generals]] [[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Wedge Antilles/Legends|Wedge Antilles]] lead the [[Star Destroyer/Legends|Star Destroyer]] [[Liberator (Imperial I-class)|''Liberator'']] to [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] after seeing an opportunity to [[Second Battle of Coruscant|strike]] at [[Galactic City/Legends|Imperial City]] during the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Imperial Civil War|infighting]] in [[10 ABY/Legends|10 ABY]]. After dropping out of [[Hyperspace/Legends|hyperspace]] above the [[Planet/Legends|planet]], they come under attack by Imperial forces. [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] is able to safely land the damaged ''Liberator'' on the surface of Coruscant, but the group is forced to defend themselves against Imperial ground forces for five days until [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] and [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa Solo]] arrive to rescue them.
