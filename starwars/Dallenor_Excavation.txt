The '''Dallenor Excavation''' was an [[Archaeology|archaeological]] excavation on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dallenor]]. It was led by the [[archaeologist]] [[Clatriffe]]. The first artifacts uncovered in the excavation included ancient broken [[droid]] parts, but eventually an item thought to be a [[Jedi holocron]] was found. The [[Krypder Riders]], a [[pirate]] gang led by [[Hudso Shaku]], attempted to steal the [[holocron]] from the archaeologists, leading Clatriffe to hire some of the planet's natives to protect the area. The [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] received a message about the holocron, sending [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] on a [[Mission to Dallenor|mission]] to retrieve the artifact.<ref name="Age of Republic" />
