'''Clausito''' was a [[Wookiee warrior]] aligned with the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. On the [[Wookiee]] [[homeworld]] of [[Kashyyyk]], for several [[Standard month|months]] prior to the [[Battle of Kashyyyk|battle]] there<ref name="Battles" /> in [[19 BBY]], the [[capital]] [[city]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> of [[Kachirho]]'s [[Kachirho Wookiee Militia|militia]]&mdash;of which Clausito was a leader&mdash;consulted with [[Jedi Master]] [[Luminara Unduli]]. The [[Jedi]] took note of the [[species]]' proud warrior culture and prepared new defenses for the capital as well as repaired prior damage dealt by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]].<ref name="Battles" />

Named by<ref name="Untold Clone Wars Panel" /> ''[[Star Wars]]'' creator<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[George Lucas]], Clausito was developed for an [[The Clone Wars Legacy|unfinished arc]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated series, set on Kashyyyk<ref name="Untold Clone Wars Panel" /> and written by [[Matt Michnovetz]].<ref name="Kashyyyk writer">{{Twitter|BFree63|status/565709839247347714|[[Brent Friedman]]|quote=@MattMichnovetz wrote that arc, and he writes for Rebels!|archivedate=20200131203505}} {{C|In response to: "Do you know who wrote TCW Yoda/Wookiees arc? BTW, would love if you could write for Rebels!"}}</ref> A clip from the arc's unfinished story reels was shown at [[Celebration Anaheim (2015)|Celebration Anaheim]] in [[2015]], featuring Clausito, who was identified by series supervising director [[Dave Filoni]] following the clip's conclusion.<ref name="Untold Clone Wars Panel" /> The character was subsequently mentioned in the [[2021]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'', which marked the first [[canon]]ical mention of the character and also gave the proper spelling of Clausito's name.<ref name="Battles" />

In the unfinished ''The Clone Wars'' arc, Clausito was a Wookiee [[Gender|male]] [[major]] with gray [[eye]]s and [[Color|black]] fur, which he wore in four [[braid]]s at his beard. He also had [[claw]]-like weapons attached to his forearms. The clip played at Celebration Anaheim showed Clausito and several other Wookiee warriors, accompanied by the Republic's [[Clone Force 99]], traversing Kashyyyk's [[forest]]s when the group was attacked by<ref name="Untold Clone Wars Panel" /> [[Kinrath|netcasters]].<ref name="Tribe episode guide">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/tribe-episode-guide|text="Tribe" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}}</ref> Despite the unexpected encounter, the Wookiees and [[Clone trooper|clones]] managed to eliminate the creatures. While encountering the netcasters, Clausito wore [[armor]], a [[belt]], and wielded a [[bowcaster]].<ref name="Untold Clone Wars Panel" />
