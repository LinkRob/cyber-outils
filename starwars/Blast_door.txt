A '''blast door''',<ref name="ANH" /> or '''shield door''',<ref name="Empire">{{Film|V}}</ref> was a type of secured door<ref name="ANH" /> that was used in numerous types of facilities, including military installations<ref name="Empire" /> and [[starship]]s.<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|Shields are back online. Get out of there! The blast doors will close in seconds.|Commander [[Tacks]]|<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>}}
When the [[Trade Federation]] [[blockade]]d the [[planet]] [[Naboo]], blast doors were used aboard their [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Lucrehulks]].<ref name="TPM" /> These ships also contained external blast doors over entrances to the reactor, that would fail and open if the control tower was damaged; a result of a key structural flaw.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Rishi Station]] on the [[Rishi Moon]] had blast doors that were infiltrated by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] in a short [[Defense of Rishi Station|battle]].<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref>

On a massive [[space station]] known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]&mdash;during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]&mdash;blast doors were used to secure the station's [[hangar]] bays.<ref name="ANH" />

==Behind the scenes==
The sound for the doors opening and closing was created via paper sliding into and out of an envelope.<ref>Nat (2017-06-15). [https://www.tvovermind.com/opening-door-sound-star-wars-somewhat-surprise "The Opening Door Sound in Star Wars is Somewhat of a Surprise"]. ''TVOvermind''. Retrieved 2019-03-19.</ref> The book ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'' confirmed that "shield door" and "blast door" refer to the object by calling<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> the [[Echo Base]] shield doors<ref name="Empire" /> and [[Crait outpost]] shield door<ref name="TLJ EE">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> blast doors.<ref name="Battles" />
