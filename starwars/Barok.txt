'''Barok''' was a [[human]] who served as a [[lieutenant]] in the [[First Order]] and served in the [[Kijimi garrison]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Biography==
Barok joined the First Order at some point prior to [[35 ABY]], and was stationed as a lieutenant in the Kijimi garrison, serving at [[Kijimi City]] on the [[Planet]] [[Kijimi]], under [[Captain]] [[Athewn Ozi]]. In 35 ABY, while [[Resistance]] member [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] was being hunted down by the First Order on Kijimi, Ozi gave Barok orders to lock down the entire city, which he did using sensor data transmitted by a [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] overhead to coordinate his troops. Afterward, he reported that the city was successfully locked down to First Order [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
