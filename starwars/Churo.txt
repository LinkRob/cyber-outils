'''Churo''', or '''Churo the Hutt''', was a [[Hutt]] [[scientist]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. He lived in [[Bilbousa]] [[City]] on the [[planet]] [[Nal Hutta]] alongside his family, the [[Devirsivik Clan]]. During a mission to the planet [[Mynos III]] alongside his sister, [[Dahara Devirsivik Norri]], Churo acquired the [[egg]] of a [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creature as part of a plan to commandeer a powerful [[weapon]] of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]. During his escape off Mynos III, Churo's [[starship]]&mdash;the [[Clawdite (ship)|''Clawdite'']]&mdash;was knocked out of [[hyperspace]] by an explosion, leaving him stranded on the planet [[Palagosal]] in the [[Arkanis sector]]. After sending out a [[distress signal]], Churo met and befriended a group of [[Jedi]]&mdash;[[Jedi Master]] [[Ada-Li Carro]] and the [[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Kildo]], [[Tep Tep]], and [[Jamil Sollis]]&mdash;along with [[Zenny Greylark]] and [[Ensign]] [[Welga]] of the [[Republic Defense Coalition]].<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />
