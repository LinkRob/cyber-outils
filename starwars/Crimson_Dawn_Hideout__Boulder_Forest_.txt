'''Crimson Dawn Hideout''' was a single room hideout for [[Crimson Dawn]] built into the mountains just west of the [[city|town]] of [[Jaunta's Hope]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the hideout contained a number of supplies including [[durafiber]] fabric.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics}}
During the same year,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] stole a number of items including the durafiber from the hideout.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Crimson Dawn Hideout first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The durafiber found in the hideout was a key quest item to unlock the "Scoundrel's Pouch" upgrade in [[Selo Rovak]]'s mechanic expert upgrade tree.<ref name="Outlaws" />
