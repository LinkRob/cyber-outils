'''Clamfruit''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|edible]] [[fruit]] that had a peel, juice, and a briny scent. Four [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]], in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]], the [[Blutopian]] [[Oga Garra]] [[Diet|ate]] clamfruit during her first meeting with the [[human]] [[Vi Moradi]], who found the fruit's smell unpleasant. Garra threw a clamfruit rind at Moradi when the [[Resistance]] [[spy]] would not volunteer information, and she spattered Moradi's face with clamfruit juice while warning the human against getting on Garra's bad side.<ref name="Black Spire" />
