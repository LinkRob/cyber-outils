The '''Arms Factory''', located in the southeastern part of [[Foundry Four]], was dedicated to producing a variety of [[starship]] weaponry. Once produced, large weapons were moved one at a time on specialized skiffs through the residential area of the foundry's northeastern area, which had the nearest landing shaft. One of four [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/carrier|LAAT/c cargo carriers]] lifted the weapons to [[orbit]], where the [[Ferra Sector Shipyard]] refitted civilian [[transport]]s for combat. The [[factory]] also produced both light and medium [[turbolaser]] [[cannon]]s, and had a dozen ready to mount on the ''[[Icarii-7]]''.<ref name="Friends Like These" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Arms Factory was introduced in the [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] adventure book ''[[Friends Like These]]'' as part of the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]].<ref name="Friends Like These" />
