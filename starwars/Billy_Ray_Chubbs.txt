'''Billy Ray Chubbs''' is an American motion capture performer and actor. He began working as a security guard at [[Industrial Light & Magic]]<ref name="One Day Book" /> in [[2003]]<ref name="Fortune">{{WebCite|url=https://fortune.com/longform/facebook-walmart-fortune-500-companies-jobs|text=7 Fortune 500 Jobs That Seem Too Good to Be True|author=Covello, Lauren; Entis, Laura; Marinova, Polina|date=2016-06-09|work=Fortune|archivedate=20190730185534}}</ref> before eventually becoming the receptionist at [[Lucasfilm]]'s San Francisco campus.<ref name="Lucasfilm">{{Lucasfilm|url=news/lucasfilm-highlights-from-one-day-at-disney|text=Lucasfilm Highlights from ''One Day at Disney''}}</ref> By [[2007]],<ref name="Backstage">{{WebCite|url=https://www.backstage.com/u/billy-ray-chubbs|text=Billy Ray Chubbs|work=Backstage|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/}}</ref> he was invited by ILM to serve as a stand-in motion capture performer, filling in gaps needed to complete action sequences.<ref name="YouTube">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@Lucasfilm|name=Lucasfilm|link=Lucasfilm|video=NEA8vuOmtPQ|text=Lucasfilm Extra Credits: Billy Ray Chubbs|date=2019-06-24}}</ref> During his tenure, he has performed various roles for ''[[Star Wars]]'' projects, including a small voice role in ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]''<ref name="FOD">{{FOD|Monster Misunderstanding}}</ref> and as an extra in [[Padmé Amidala's funeral]] sequence in {{Film|III}}.<ref name="YouTube" />
