'''Cameron Riley Monaghan''' (born [[August 16]], [[1993]]<ref name="SWYT 08-20-2020" />) is an [[Wikipedia:America|American]] actor, model and former child actor who plays the role of [[Cal Kestis]] in the ''[[Star Wars Jedi]]'' video games.

He is also known for his role as [https://shameless.fandom.com/wiki/Ian_Gallagher_(US) Ian Gallagher] on the [[Wikipedia:Showtime (TV network)|Showtime]] comedy drama series [[Wikipedia:Shameless (U.S. TV series)|''Shameless'']] and his dual role as twins [https://gotham.fandom.com/wiki/Jerome_Valeska Jerome] and [https://gotham.fandom.com/wiki/Jeremiah_Valeska Jeremiah Valeska] on the [[Wikipedia:Fox Broadcasting Company|Fox]] crime series [[W:c:gotham:Gotham|''Gotham'']].

==Biography==
===Early life===
Cameron Riley Monaghan was born on [[August 16]], [[1993]].<ref name="SWYT 08-20-2020" />

===''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order''===
{{Quote|Oh..it's not a big deal. It's only every kid's freaking dream ever to be a Jedi. It's... I mean.. it's incredible.|Cameron Monaghan on being cast as [[Cal Kestis]]|<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order panel">{{SWYouTube|5H6LQkxwVxc|The Galaxy-Wide Premiere of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order Live Panel}}</ref>}}
In [[2018]], Cameron Monaghan was cast in the lead role of [[Cal Kestis]] for ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', a ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars video games|video game]] in development by [[Respawn Entertainment]] set for release on [[November 15]], [[2019]]. The role of Kestis attracted his attention because he liked the character's optimism in spite of all the difficulties has lived through and he hadn't worked a video game before. During the casting process of ''Fallen Order'', Monaghan was at first unlikely to be cast on the role, as his audition clip was placed in a folder marked "Other" instead of the one marked "Review First". However, when [[Aaron Contreras]] saw his audition after all auditions from the "Review First" folder didn't convince him, he knew that Monaghan was right for the role.<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order panel" />

Upon getting the role, Cameron was restricted from telling anyone about the role. If his family or friends asked him, he would respond "Yeah, you're not going to see me very often. I can't tell you why". Playing Cal Kestis was a huge experience for Monaghan; it was his first time performing a role in [[Wikipedia:Motion capture|motion capture]] and while he had seen videos from actors performing with such process, he believed at first to be voice work until fully coming to understand the concept.<ref name="TSWS Monaghan">{{StarWarsShow|zcp4nBE3BM0|Cameron Monaghan Takes The Stage At SWCC 2019 &#124; The Star Wars Show Live!}}</ref> Cameron enjoyed working on the game as he felt that being able to explore to the limits of imagination in a digital placement was like being a kid playing on a big [[Wikipedia:Sandbox|sandbox]]. Monaghan's casting was eventually confirmed during [[Celebration Chicago]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order panel" />

On [[September 16]], [[2023]], at the end of a "''Star Wars: Jedi'' Game Series" panel at Ocala Comic Con, Cameron Monaghan mentioned that the third ''Jedi'' game has started development.<ref name="Ocala Comic Con">{{YouTube|video=N9t3Vd1VWC0|text=Ocala Comic Con 2023 &ndash; Star Wars: Jedi Game Series Panel &ndash; Cameron Monaghan & Tina Ivlev|channel=@SaixOrg13|name=Saix_XIII|date=2023-09-18|quote=We're working on the third and we're in the process of doing it right now. So you know... that's a big undertaking and there's been some conversations so far. But, you know, hopefully, when all things are said and done, we'll be able to go in and make something really cool for you guys again.|archivedate=20231017084014}}<!--43:00--></ref>

==Personal life==
{{Quote|If I told that kid he was gonna swing a lightsaber one day, I think his brain would've exploded.|Cameron Monaghan|<ref name="SWCC 2019 5 Things We Learned">{{SW|url=news/swcc-2019-5-things-we-learned-from-the-star-wars-jedi-fallen-order-panel|text=SWCC 2019: 5 Things We learned from the ''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'' panel}}</ref>}}
As stated in [[StarWars.com]], Monaghan has been a ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan since childhood. He grew up watching the ''Star Wars'' [[original trilogy]] on [[Wikipedia:VHS|VHS]], often replaying his favorite [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] scenes.<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order panel" /> He recalled wielding a toy [[lightsaber]] for such a long period of time that it became bent and inoperable as well as going to see [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']] with his grandfather at age seven in [[1999]], an experience which was pleasant for both of them. Monaghan also recalled watching ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' during its initial airing and playing ''Star Wars'' [[Star Wars video games|video game]]s.<ref name="Interview" />
