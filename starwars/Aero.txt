"'''Aero'''" was the nickname of a [[Special Commando Advanced Recon Trooper]] in [[Task Force 99]], a [[squad]] within the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps]]. Aero served as the squad's [[engineer]], capable of repairing and hot-wiring vehicles for [[transport]]. Aero participated in Task Force 99's mission to the [[Ghost Moon]] in order to [[Skirmish on the Ghost Moon|kill]] a group of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebels]] hiding there,<ref name="SW21" /> and later traveled to [[Hubin]] to [[Skirmish on Hubin|apprehend]] [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] [[Heroes of Yavin|group]] of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]].<ref name="SW60">[[Star Wars (2015) 60|''Star Wars'' (2015) 60]]</ref>
