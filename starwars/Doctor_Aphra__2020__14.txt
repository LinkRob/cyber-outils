'''''Doctor Aphra'' 14''' is the fourteenth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']], and is part of the ''[[Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters]]'' event. The comic was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Federico Sabbatini]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[September 8]], [[2021]].

==Publisher's summary==
''REPEAT OFFENDERS DOCTOR APHRA and SANA STARROS are imprisoned by a POWERFUL ENEMY! If they want to escape they'll have to work together with former foes JUST LUCKY and ARIOLE&hellip;And venture deeper into the heart of a CRIMSON DAWN FLAGSHIP!''
