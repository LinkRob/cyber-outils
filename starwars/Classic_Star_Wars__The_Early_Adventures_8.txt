'''''Classic Star Wars: The Early Adventures'' 8''' is the eighth issue of the ''[[Classic Star Wars: The Early Adventures]]'' series, published by [[Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse]].

This particular ''Classic'' series were reformatted and colored reprints of the ''Star Wars'' [[Star Wars (comic strip)|daily newspaper strip]] by [[Russ Manning]] from the early 1980s.

==Publisher's summary==
''Crucial components of the Rebel Alliance's X-wing fighters have been compromised by a neutral planet's desire for profits. Some people in Tun Wala would sell the units to the Empire as soon as they'd sell them to the Rebellion! Now Darth Vader is on his way to the planet to destroy the Rebels, and, neutral or not, the planet itself!''
