The '''eastern exurbs''' were a location on the [[planet]] [[Christophsis]].<ref name="Front Lines" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the exurbs served as the site for the [[Bail Organa's Christophsis refugee camp|refugee camp]] of Republic [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]], as well as the [[Minister of Sustenance's compound|compound]] for the [[Minister of Sustenance]].<ref name="Front Lines" /> However, during the Confederacy's [[Battle of Christophsis|invasion]] of the planet, the exurbs and the senator's relief mission within were targeted by the [[Whorm Loathsom's army|droid army]] of [[Kerkoiden]] Separatist [[General]] [[Whorm Loathsom]], as well as the confederate government's planetary [[Christophsis blockade|blockade]] [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by the [[Harch]] [[Admiral]] [[Trench]], who deployed a [[squadron]] of [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class Droid Fighter/Bombers]] to commence a bombing run on the camp.<ref name="Cat">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The eastern exurbs were introduced in "[[Cat and Mouse]],"<ref name="Cat" /> the sixteenth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which aired on [[March 26]], [[2010]].<ref name="Cat and Mouse Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode216.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Cat and Mouse}}</ref> It was later first identified in ''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]'', a [[2017]] reference book authored by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Front Lines" />
