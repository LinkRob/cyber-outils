The '''C290''' was a model of [[blaster rifle]] produced by [[BlasTech Industries]]. It was designed for use in [[Water|wet]], [[Temperature|humid]], or [[swamp]]y environments. BlasTech used the C290 as the basis for the [[DC-12U beam rifle]], used in underwater operations.<ref name="COTR" />
