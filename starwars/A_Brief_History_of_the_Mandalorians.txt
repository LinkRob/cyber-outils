"'''A Brief History of the Mandalorians'''" is an article that appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 115|''Star Wars Insider'' 115]] written by [[Leland Chee|Leland Y. Chee]]. The article traced the appearances of the [[Mandalorian/Legends|Mandalorians]] from its early concept all the way to the [[Legacy era]].<ref name="sw115" />
