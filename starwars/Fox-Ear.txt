{{Quote|I have no business with you. Where might your parents be? They took something valuable of mine, and I'm here to get it back. With interest, of course.|Fox-Ear|audio=NoBusinessWithYou-YukoTreasure.mp3|<ref name="Yuko" />}}
'''Fox-Ear''' was a [[pirate]] [[captain]] that led a [[Fox-Ear's pirate gang|gang]] of [[alien]]s including [[Gura]], [[Riku]], and several members of a [[Unidentified species (Fox-Ear's underlings)|diminutive red species]]. At some point no earlier than [[1 BBY]], Fox-Ear and his gang searched the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] for [[smuggler]]s [[Bennet Sten|Bennet]] and [[Myler Sten]], whom he claimed had stolen something important to him. Using the local [[Youngling|child]] [[Sola (Tatooine)|Sola]], the gang tracked the location of the Sten's [[Sten family|child]] [[Yuko]] and [[droid]] [[BILY]] to their [[BILY and Yuko's home|home]]. [[Pilot]]ing his [[Fox-Ear's starship|starship]], the pirates destroyed the home's entrance and threatened to harm Yuko if BILY did not lead them to Fox-Ear's stolen treasure. BILY complied and guided the pirates to the [[Sten family home]] in a [[cave]] within the [[Jundland Wastes]].

Finding their way to the vault entrance of the home, BILY claimed that the door could not be opened. Fox-Ear responded by firing his [[blaster pistol]] at the droid, [[stun]]ning BILY. Fox-Ear then ordered his underlings to retrieve Yuko from where he was left in the home while he reprogrammed the disabled BILY. Later, Fox-Ear's minions, alongside Yuko and Sola, barreled through a nearby cave wall, being chased by a [[Unidentified leviathan krayt dragon|leviathan krayt dragon]]&mdash;with the large beast crashing into a deep chasm. Yuko demanded Fox-Ear return BILY to him, to which Fox-Ear obeyed, calling for the reprogrammed BILY, now known as Darth Bear. To Fox-Ear's frustration, the droid still refused to open the vault door, but attacked Yuko and Sola.

Fox-Ear eventually coerced Yuko to open the vault, promising to change BILY back to normal. However, the vault was empty, save for a single [[Batuuan Spira]]. The [[krayt dragon]] then returned from the chasm and briefly chased the pirates before directing its attention to the children and BILY. While the pirates ran, Sola threw his [[cane]]&mdash;with [[Yuko's triangle droid]] attached to the end&mdash;at the pirates, pinning Fox-Ear's cape to the ground. The droid released a loud noise that lured the krayt dragon to the pirates, barreling through the small group and sending them all plummeting into the chasm.

==Biography==
===Searching for treasure===
{{Dialogue|Fox-Ear|Very well, Riku, Gura, prepare for attack.|Sola|Huh? Attack? Didn't you tell me the job was retrieving stolen&mdash;|Gura|How naive. You didn't think we'd let them get away with stealing from pirates, did you? We'll be taking everything they own as payback.|attr=The pirates reveal their plans|audio=TakeEverything-YukoTreasure.mp3|src=<ref name="Yuko" />}}
[[File:FoxEarsPirates-YukoTreasure.png|thumb|right|300px|Fox-Ear was the leader of a pirate gang.]]
Fox-Ear was the [[humanoid]] leader of a [[pirate]] [[Fox-Ear's pirate gang|gang]].<ref name="Yuko" /> At some point no earlier than [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{VisionsDate|305}}</ref> the pirates operated on<ref name="Yuko" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Tatooine]] in search of a treasure they claimed was stolen from them by the [[smuggler]]s [[Bennet Sten]] and [[Myler Sten]]. The pirates recruited the local [[human]] [[Youngling|child]] [[Sola (Tatooine)|Sola]] to search for the location of the [[Sten family|Stens]], claiming the job was to only retrieve what was stolen. Sola questioned individuals across the [[city]] [[Mos Eisley]] with a [[holopuck]] featuring the Stens and their [[Yuko's triangle droid|triangle droid]]. Sola eventually followed the [[bear droid]] [[BILY]] back to his [[BILY and Yuko's home|home]] he shared with the son of the Stens, [[Yuko]]. Sola then spotted the seemingly unique [[droid]] seen in the hologram.<ref name="Yuko" />

Sola returned to the pirates with the information, and Fox-Ear questioned Sola on if he had discovered the Sten hideout as well. The child assured Fox-Ear that he had found it and tossed a [[memory chip]] to<ref name="Yuko" /> him.<ref name="YukoAD" /> The pirate caught the chip and laughed as he ordered the pirates [[Gura]] and [[Riku]] to prepare for an attack. Riku whimpered and a confused Sola confronted them on their claim the job was only to retrieve the stolen treasure. Gura interrupted Sola and informed him that the pirates would be taking everything as payback. Fox-Ear rose from his chair and walked to the [[cockpit]] of the ship, stating they were leaving immediately. As the pirates prepared, Sola left the ship to warn BILY and Yuko.<ref name="Yuko" />

===Child of the Stens===
{{Quote|Yuko's parents are no longer with us. They got sick and died.''"<br />"''You think you can trick me? You may be in hiding but I see right through you. You know where it is. Spit it out. Tell me where my treasure is?|BILY and Fox-Ear|audio=TellMeWhere-YukoTreasure.mp3|<ref name="Yuko" />}}
[[File:FoxEarGangAttacks-YukosTreasure.png|thumb|left|300px|Fox-Ear attacked BILY and Yuko's house in search for Bennet and Myler Sten.]]
Following Sola close behind over the<ref name="Yuko" /> [[Jundland Wastes]],<ref name="YukoAD" /> the pirate [[starship]] eventually arrived at the home, raining bursts of [[laser]]s at the property. After BILY pulled Sola inside, the starship landed and Fox-Ear's pirates blew open the door to the kitchen, knocking out the lights and sending the residents across the room. Fox-Ear stepped forward and looked at decorations hanging from the ceiling before focusing his sight on BILY holding Yuko. Not seeing Bennet and Myler, Fox-Ear stared down at Riku and asked if they were at the correct house. Riku affirmed the location was accurate.<ref name="Yuko" />

Yuko yelled at the pirates, questioning what they were doing. Fox-Ear then stated he had no business with BILY and Yuko, instead asking where Yuko's parents were. He claimed they had stolen something valuable from him and he was there to retrieve it with interest. BILY spoke up and claimed the Stens had both gotten sick and [[Death|died]]. The pirate did not believe BILY and grabbed him by his fur, claiming he could see right through the lie despite the droid being in hiding. Fox-Ear demanded BILY reveal the location of his stolen treasure. After once again refusing the pirate's commands, Fox-Ear grabbed Yuko by the collar of his [[Clothing|shirt]] and threatened him.<ref name="Yuko" />

[[File:FoxEarsThreat-YukoTreasure.png|thumb|right|300px|Fox-Ear threatened Yuko to force BILY to assist the pirates.]]
Sola ran from across the room and jumped to snatch Yuko from Fox-Ear's grasp. Riku and Gura fired their [[blaster]]s at the children as the door shut behind them. BILY attempted to run at the pirates and begged them to stop before getting shot by Gura's blaster. Fox-Ear called BILY a nuisance and told the pirates to bring the droid to the ship with the intent to reprogram him. Riku asked Fox-Ear what to do about the children, and was told to [[Fire|burn]] them with the house. Pleading, BILY sat up and told the pirates that he would take them to the treasure if they did not hurt Yuko. Fox-Ear grinned and chuckled at BILY and took the droid before boarding the ship and taking off.<ref name="Yuko" />

===The vault===
{{Dialogue|BILY|It won't open. All that lies ahead is a grave. Your so called 'treasure' doesn't exist.|Riku and Gura|What?|BILY|Are you satisfied? Now please leave us alone.|attr=BILY to the pirates as Fox-Ear stuns him|audio=ItWontOpen-YukoTreasure.mp3|src=<ref name="Yuko" />}}
[[File:FoxInACave-YukoTreasure.png|thumb|left|300px|Fox-Ear and his gang held BILY captive.]]
Arriving at a crevice in a rock wall of<ref name="Yuko" /> the Jundland Wastes,<ref name="YukoAD" /> the pirates landed their starship and entered. Fox-Ear and his underlings proceeded with a tied-up BILY through a [[Mining|mineshaft]] until they eventually reached a large cavern with a [[Sten family home|vault]] surrounded by large<ref name="Yuko" /> [[tooth|teeth]]<ref name="YukoAD" /> sticking out of the ground. The group of pirates gasped and Fox-Ear wondered to himself what the location was. Fox-Ear approached the vault and activated a panel with a hand-print security measure. BILY spoke up and claimed the door would not open, and that only a grave lied ahead. The droid continued to claim that the treasure did not exist, leaving the other pirates confused.<ref name="Yuko" />

BILY asked if Fox-Ear was satisfied, and after asking the pirates to leave him and Yuko alone, the pirate shot the droid with a [[stun]] blast. The shock also stunned Riku, as they were holding the rope BILY was tied in. Stepping forward, Fox-Ear said that BILY thought he was so clever and told Gura to retrieve Yuko from his house. Gura asked if Fox-Ear planned to use Yuko to open the door, and the pirate raised his arm and asked in return why<ref name="Yuko" /> she<ref name="YukoAD" /> was not doing it already if she knew what he wanted. Gura quickly grabbed hold of a still-dazed Riku and ran off as Fox-Ear's other underlings followed behind. Two underlings stayed behind with a powered crate, and Fox-Ear approached them chuckling. While the others were away, Fox-Ear modified BILY into [[Darth]] Bear, forcing the droid to follow his commands.<ref name="Yuko" />

===Sly fox===
{{Quote|Where's BILY? Give him back to me!''"<br />"''Very well, if that's what you wish.''"<br />"''Really?''"<br />"''Come now, BILY. Why don't you have a nice reunion?|Yuko and Fox-Ear as Darth Bear is revealed|audio=Reunion-YukoTreasure.mp3|<ref name="Yuko" />}}
[[File:KraytDragonCrash-YukoTreasure.png|thumb|right|300px|A leviathan krayt dragon barreled through the cave wall at Fox-Ear.]]
As Fox-Ear waited on his crew to return with Yuko, the cave began to rumble and pebbles fell from the ceiling. He asked to himself what was happening, and a large [[Unidentified leviathan krayt dragon|leviathan krayt dragon]] barreled through the wall with his crew along with Sola, Yuko, and his [[triangle droid]]&mdash;who had snuck into the cave. The [[leviathan krayt dragon]] fell off a ledge into a deep pit as the others fell into a pile before Fox-Ear, who was still standing. Fox-Ear congratulated them on finally arriving and told them he had been waiting for them. Yuko rose from the pile and demanded BILY be returned to him. Chuckling, Fox-Ear granted his wish and stepped aside to reveal BILY's new form as Darth Bear and told the droid to stand up for their reunion.<ref name="Yuko" />

Darth Bear stood up and Yuko ran toward him, only to be attacked by the droid picking up and smashing a boulder in front of the child. Fox-Ear laughed<ref name="Yuko" /> maliciously<ref name="YukoSub">{{Visions|Yuko's Treasure}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref> and informed Yuko of the changes he had made to BILY. As the droid chased and attacked Yuko, Fox-Ear expressed his frustration at Darth Bear's unwillingness to open the vault despite his reprogramming efforts. Watching the droid chase Yuko, Fox-Ear offered to change Darth Bear back into BILY if the child opened the door for the pirates. Receiving encouragement from Sola, Yuko ran to the panel on the vault and opened it with his hand print.<ref name="Yuko" />

===Plot foiled===
{{Quote|This is no time to stand around drooling. Start loading the treasure before the sun s&mdash; Huh? Impossible. How dare they mock me!|Fox-Ear discovering the single Batuuan Spira within the vault|audio=HowDareThey-YukoTreasure.mp3|<ref name="Yuko" />}}
The pirate crew ran to the door past Yuko. Fox-Ear walked close behind them and dismissed Yuko, having lied about wanting to fix BILY. The pirate walked up behind his confused crew and told them to start loading the treasure before realizing that there was nothing but a single [[Batuuan Spira]]. Enraged, Fox-Ear yelled that he was being mocked and threw the measly chip on the floor. The cave began to rumble as the [[krayt dragon]] climbed back into the cavern and stared at the pirates. The group screamed, and Fox-Ear hurriedly yelled for the group to retreat back to the ship. The krayt dragon shifted its focus to Darth Bear and the children while the pirates took the opportunity to escape.<ref name="Yuko" />

[[File:BadGuyBowling-YukoTreasure.png|thumb|left|300px|Fox-ear's pirate gang is defeated by the krayt dragon]]
The children called out to the pirates, but Fox-Ear exclaimed the pirates were not stupid enough to wait for them and kept running. In response, Sola threw a [[cane]] with Yuko's triangle droid attached and nailed Fox-Ear's [[cape]] to the ground. Stuck, the pirate called to his crew and demanded they help him. As Riku and Gura attempted to release the cape, Yuko's droid released a series of sound waves that stunned the crew. The krayt dragon was drawn to the noise, turning its attention back to the pirates and accidentally knocking BILY's [[helmet]] off, freeing him from Fox-Ear's control. The dragon then barreled through Fox-Ear and his pirates, sending the entire group plummeting to the bottom of the chasm.<ref name="Yuko" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|What do we do about the kids?''"<br />"''Burn them with the house.|Riku and Fox-Ear|audio=BurnThem-YukoTreasure.mp3|<ref name="Yuko" />}}
Fox-Ear was a pirate captain that was willing to go far to receive what he desired, including destroying a home and showing complete disregard for children. He threatened the children Yuko and Sola and was willing to order his underlings to burn them in Yuko's house. When chased by the krayt dragon, he was also fine with leaving them behind to make his escape. As a pirate, Fox-Ear was a liar and became angered when he believed others lied to him. He had light skin and brown [[eye]]s. He showed frustration when his reprogramming efforts were not enough for BILY to open the vault. Upon seeing the vault was empty save for a single Batuuan Spira, he believed the Stens were mocking him and threw the coin to the ground out of anger.<ref name="Yuko" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|Ugh. What a nuisance. Bring the bear droid back to the ship. We can reprogram him there.|Fox-Ear|audio=Reprogram-YukoTreasure.mp3|<ref name="Yuko" />}}
Fox-Ear was capable of [[pilot]]ing his starship and could reprogram droids. He was a strong leader, able to command his underlings with ease.<ref name="Yuko" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fox-Ear first appeared in "[[Yuko's Treasure]]," a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Kinema Citrus Co.]], was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Yuko" /> on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="ReleaseDate">{{SW|url=news/why-we-love-star-wars-visions|text=Why We Love the Unique Stories of ''Star Wars: Visions''}}</ref> Fox-Ear was first revealed on [[September 26]] of the same year in a cast reveal on the official [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' website.<ref name="JPN cast">{{Disney|subdomain=starwars|domain=co.jp|url=news/20250926_01|text=日本語オリジナル版＜本予告＞が全世界解禁！『スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ』Volume3}}</ref> The [[Wikipedia:English language|English]] spelling of his name was later confirmed in the short's credits. In the short, he was voiced by [[Kenichirou Matsuda]] in the original Japanese and by [[Steve Buscemi]] in the English dub.<ref name="Yuko" /> Concept art of Fox-Ear and his gang was created by [[Atsuko Nozaki]].<ref name="Concept5">{{SW|url=yukos-treasure-concept-art-gallery-star-wars-visions-volume-3|text="Yuko's Treasure" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars: Visions Volume 3}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref>
