The '''614-AvA speeder bike''', also known as the '''Lothal Speeder Bike''', was an [[Imperial Military]] [[speeder bike]] model manufactured by [[Aratech Repulsor Company]]. The [[Galactic Empire]] utilized the vehicle most notably during its occupation of [[Lothal]].

==Description==
[[File:NautolanBiker-SS.png|thumb|left|270px|A Nautolan riding a customized 614-AvA speeder bike]]
[[Aratech Repulsor Company|Aratech's]] 614-AvA was a small, fast, lightweight [[speeder bike]] used by the [[Imperial Military]]. Designed initially as a portable, modular scout vehicle typically packed into a larger vehicle like a [[walker]] or a [[landspeeder]], the 614-AvA was more akin to a [[swoop]] than to a full-fledged speeder bike. It consisted of little more than a combination repulsor drive-thruster pack, a control pod, and a set of variable-geometry control surfaces mounted to a pair of stout frame rails. The rails were telescopic, allowing the speeder bike to collapse to roughly a third of its full size with no need for tools. With the push of a button, the 614-AvA collapsed into a cube about a meter square; this feature allowed the 614-AvA to be easily shipped in bulk.<ref name="CCNY2" /><ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

The [[Imperial Army]] ordered tens of thousands of 614-AvAs to be used by reconnaissance and military police units. For military use, the speeder bikes had some improvements, such as frame reinforcements, more powerful drive-thrusters, and a pair of light [[blaster cannon]]s.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

Overall, the 614 had proven quite successful in [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] service and had gained a reputation among its [[pilot]]s as reliable, easy to work on, and easy to live with.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

==History==
[[File:614AvAspeederbike-SWBC39.png|thumb|right|300px|An Imperial scout trooper piloting a 614-AvA speeder bike]]
This militarized [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[speeder]] bike model was used by [[Imperial combat driver]]s for rapid transit<ref name="CCNY2" /> and by [[scout trooper]]s.<ref name="Hera's Heroes">{{Rebels|Hera's Heroes}}</ref>

A [[Sabine's speeder bike|614-AvA speeder bike]] owned by the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]] after he stole it from the [[Galactic Empire]]. Bridger acquired the [[speeder]] when it became surplus to the Empire, he [[Painting|repainted]] the speeder to match his own tastes. It had standard steering.<ref name="Ezra Speeder Databank">{{Databank|ezras-speeder-bike|Ezra's Speeder Bike}}</ref> He rode the speeder bike during a [[Raid near Jalath]].<ref name="Call">{{Rebels|Call to Action}}</ref> Following the [[Liberation of Lothal]], Ezra’s speeder bike was owned by [[Sabine Wren]],<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> who modified the speeder.<ref name="Sabine Databank">{{Databank|sabine-s-speeder-bike|Sabine's speeder bike}}</ref> During the [[New Republic Era]], Wren used her new bike to avoid a patrol of [[E-wing starfighter|E-wing escort fighters]], sent by [[Governor]] [[Ryder Azadi]], to get her back to the [[Spectres mural ceremony]].<ref name="Part One" /> The bike was later seen outside the Lothal tower when [[Ahsoka Tano]] visited the tower, searching for clues.<ref name="Part Two”">{{Ahsoka|Part Two: Toil and Trouble}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The design of this speeder bike model was developed from an early illustration by concept artist [[Joe Johnston]] in [[1979]].<ref name="CCNY2" /> The length of this vehicle is listed as 3.98 meters in the young reader's book ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Head to Head]]'',<ref name="Star Wars Rebels: Head to Head" /> while the official [[StarWars.com]] [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] lists the length as 4.4 meters.<ref name="Lothal Speeder Bike" />

In the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[An Inside Man]]," [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] refers to the bike model as a "624 AvA speeder bike."<ref name="AIM">{{Rebels|An Inside Man}}</ref>
