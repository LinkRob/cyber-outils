'''Dijuka''' was a [[Dybrinthe]] [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Yoda 1" /> By [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The present day events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic — The Blade]]'' take place between the [[wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran]] and [[Porter Engle]] [[Siege of Bardotta|earning]] his title the "Blade of Bardotta," both of which are dated to [[382 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> Dijuka served on the [[Jedi High Council]].<ref name="The Blade 4">[[The High Republic — The Blade 4|''The High Republic — The Blade'' 4]]</ref> Sometime later, after the Council received a message of help from the [[Scalvi]], Dijuka discussed the matter with the other Masters. When [[Pra-Tre Veter]] questioned who the Scalvi were, Dijuka responded that what mattered was that the Scalvi were in trouble.<ref name="Yoda 1" />

==Biography==
===Porter's report===
Around [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Jedi]] was present when [[Porter Engle]] informed the Council that [[Barash Silvain]] took a [[Barash Vow|vow]] to distance herself from the [[Jedi Order]] to find out what [[the Force]] wanted her to do.<ref name="The Blade 4" />

===Plea of the Scalvi===
[[File:Dijuka-Yoda1.jpg|thumb|left|140px|Dijuka rebuked the questioning of who the Scalvi were when they were in need]]
{{Quote|What matters is that they are in trouble.|Dijuka, to Pra-Tre Veter|<ref name="Yoda 1" />}}
Master Dijuka would be present during a Jedi High Council meeting where the members heard a message by the [[Scalvi]] requesting help. Although it wasn’t yet known to them who the Scalvi were, as pointed out by master [[Pra-Tre Veter]], Dijuka reinforced the fact that what mattered was that they were in trouble and the council should help them.<ref name="Yoda 1" />
