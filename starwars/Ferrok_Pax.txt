'''Ferrok Pax''', located in the [[Expansion Region]]'s [[Ombakond sector]], was the home of the kingdom of a proto-[[species]] which was reduced to ruins by the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|rule]]. By [[1 BBY]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] went to Ferrok Pax to rescue rebels who had been captured by a [[Unidentified warrior group|group of warriors]]. Traveling by [[Limb|foot]] to reach their target, the 61st lost many [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldiers]] during its journey. After the remaining [[soldier]]s [[Battle of Ferrok Pax|fought]] and defeated the warriors, only thirty-seven remained of what had been barely two-hundred troops.

==Description==
Ferrok Pax was located in an [[Ferrok Pax system|eponymous star system]] in the [[Ombakond sector]], within an intersecting portion of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It lay at the coordinates P-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" />

==History==
{{Quote|Had we taken to the skies, our attempts at stealth would have been futile. But we left behind a trail of soldiers who could no longer walk or whose hunger ravaged their bodies as our supplies dwindled. We lost brave men to dreadful beasts; others vanished, consumed by alien technology we lacked the knowledge to defend against.|Gadren recalls the 61st's losses at Ferrok Pax|<ref name="Twilight" />}}
[[File:Twilight_Company_patch.png|thumb|left|250px|The 61st Mobile Infantry (logo pictured)]]
Ferrok Pax was [[Homeworld|home]] to the kingdom of a proto-[[species]] which was reduced to ruins by the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]].<ref name="Twilight" /> At some point by [[1 BBY]],<ref name="1 BBY">The [[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]] establishes that the [[61st Mobile Infantry]] was [[Battle of Ferrok Pax|deployed]] to Ferrok Pax prior to the [[Battle of Scarif]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]].</ref> a [[Unidentified warrior group|group of warriors]] held members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] captive at Ferrok Pax. The Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] was subsequently deployed to Ferrok Pax to save the captured rebels. Numbering barely two-hundred [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldiers]], the [[company]] approached the enemy by [[Limb|foot]] to maintain their stealth and flank the warriors. The 61st marched for [[Standard day|days]] through the ruins of the ancient proto-species kingdom, their supplies dwindling. Many [[soldier]]s were left behind, unable to walk or starving to [[death]]. Others were lost to creatures or [[alien]] technology the 61st did not understand.<ref name="Twilight" />

The soldiers eventually found and [[Battle of Ferrok Pax|fought]] the warriors, claiming victory and extracting the captured rebels. However, only thirty-seven of the troops sent remained, including the [[Besalisk]] [[Gadren]], a newer member of the company. [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]], the 61st's founder and [[commanding officer]], rebuilt the company after.<ref name="Twilight" /> The Alliance's [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|leader]], [[Mon Mothma]], considered the battle at Ferrok Pax very costly and echoed her thoughts in Document #MH2215, titled "[[Short Notes on the History of the Rebel Alliance Navy]]," within her personal files.<ref name="Rogue One novel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="3 ABY">{{BTCdate}}</ref> while the 61st was at the junker [[Planet|world]] of [[Heap Nine]], Gadren retold his experience of his time at Ferrok Pax to [[Hazram Namir]], the assumed captain of the company at that time.<ref name="Twilight" />
