These chewy little dessert balls that I bought at a food stall in Diadem Square certainly back up that notion.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
'''Corellia Chews''' were a type of small, baked [[Foodstuff|dessert]] ball that were chewy and [[Color|gold]] in color with white dusting. Bursting with a white filling of tart [[fruit]] and sweet [[cream]], the [[Diet|vegetarian]] balls were made by the [[Corellian]] inhabitants of the industrial [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. During a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> taken between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] purchased Corellia Chews at a food stall in [[Diadem Square]] within Corellia's [[capital]], [[Coronet City]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

Later on the tour, he traded a batch of Corellia Chews to a [[Unidentified chef (Sergia)|chef]] on the planet [[Sergia]] in exchange for a recipe for [[Sand Mynock 'n' Cheese]]. He was pleasantly surprised at the amount of baking skill the dessert suggested the Corellians had and included the recipe in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> which was published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> In the book, he claimed that the Chews had almost made his visit to the grotty Coronet City shine.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corellia Chews were created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the rolls treats them as [[Wikipedia:Mochi|mochi]] and makes use of [[Wikipedia:Mascarpone|mascarpone cream]], [[Wikipedia:Powdered sugar|confectioners' sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Cream|heavy cream]], and crushed [[Wikipedia:Pineapple|pineapple]] for the filling and [[Wikipedia:Rice flour|mochiko]], [[Wikipedia:Water|water]], [[Wikipedia:White sugar|granulated sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Food coloring|yellow food gel dye]], and [[Wikipedia:Potato starch|potato starch]] for the mochi casing.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
