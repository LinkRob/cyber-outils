A '''cara beetle''' was a type of small [[insect]].<ref name="Glass" /> Around [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]'' take place one [[Standard week|week]] after the [[Funeral of Qui-Gon Jinn|funeral]] of [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[32 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] [[Mission to Metagos|infiltrated]] an [[Chulok's organization|organization]] run by the gangster [[Chulok]] and [[Sleep|slept]] in an apartment within Chulok's headquarters, the [[Floating Palace (Metagos)|Floating Palace]]. Windu felt restless, as if cara beetles were crawling and biting him beneath his sheets, but he was unable to find any of the [[beetle]]s after searching four times for them and so decided the feeling was likely due to his proximity to his enemy.<ref name="Glass" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cara beetles were mentioned in the [[2024]] novel ''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]'', written by [[Steven Barnes]].<ref name="Glass" />
