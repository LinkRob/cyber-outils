'''Fanbugs''' were a type of flying [[Insect|bug]] that could be found on the [[Arli's moon|moon]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Arli]]. When the [[San Tekka clan]] member [[Jordanna Sparkburn]] lived on the [[moon]] [[Standard year|years]] before<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|DFS}}</ref> she and her [[Gender|brother]] [[Harth Sparkburn]] attended a [[Unidentified school (Birage)|school]] in the [[city]] [[Birage]]. During their lunch breaks, the fanbugs used their wings to flutter along with the siblings. When she returned to the moon<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> in 228 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Jordanna mentally recalled when the fanbugs had drifted with her and Harth.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />
