The '''Epo system''' was situated in the [[Ciutric sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[New Territories]]<ref name="NewTerritories" /> within grid square N-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It contained the [[planet]] [[Epo]], which itself contained [[Ansett Island]] where the [[Battle of Ansett Island]] took place.<ref name="LastOrder" /> During the battle, the [[First Order]] [[Company 77]] of [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] was ordered to [[Death|kill]] a group of civilians<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> at some point between approximately [[27 ABY|27]] and [[33 ABY]].<ref name="Dates">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' establishes that [[Jannah]] and other deserters of the [[Company 77]], a [[company]] of [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]], had [[Colonist|settled]] on [[Kef Bir]] [[Standard year|years]] prior to that film's events. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''The Rise of Skywalker'' to [[35 ABY]] and the flashbacks of "The Last Order" take place in or after [[27 ABY]] based on Finn's age, the desertion of Company 77 must have occurred at some point between 27 and [[33 ABY]].</ref> The entire [[company]] refused to carry out the order and subsequently [[Desertion|deserted]], escaping aboard a [[cruiser]] that members of the company eventually took to the [[Endor system]].<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Epo [[Star system|system]] was first mentioned, as the Ansett system, in a [[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|galaxy map]]<ref name="GalaxyMap">{{GalaxyMapPoster}}</ref> designed by [[Christopher Ibbitt]]<ref name="Fry email">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-31.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-31}}</ref> that was created for the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' and given out as a promotional poster at [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|Celebration Anaheim]]<ref name="TimelinesSWcom">{{SW|url=news/swca-2022-lucasfilm-publishing-behind-the-page-panel|text=SWCA 2022: 7 Things We Learned from the Lucasfilm Publishing Behind the Page Panel|author=[[Bria LaVorgna|LaVorgna, Bria]]date=2022-05-28}}</ref> on [[May 27]], [[2022]]<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|dkpublishing|status/1530292772598534145|[[Dorling Kindersley|DK Books US]]|date=2022-05-27|quote=Just announced at #StarWarsCelebration! Get your exclusive map - only at booth #2525.|archivedate=20220527210041}}</ref> prior to the book's release during [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]]<ref name="Celebration">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-europe-2023-exclusives|text=Check Out ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Exclusives from Hasbro, the LEGO Group, and More|date=2023-04-03}}</ref> of [[April 7]] through [[April 10|10]], [[2023]].<ref name="CelebrationSchedule">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-panel-schedule|text=Plan Ahead with the ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Panel Schedule|date=2023-03-07}}</ref> It was then identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] article on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
