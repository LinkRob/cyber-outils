The '''Free Dac Volunteers Engineering Corps''', also known as '''Quarren Free Dac Volunteers Engineering Corps''' or '''QFD''', was a [[starship]] manufacturer active during the [[Clone Wars]]. They were most notoriously known for producing the [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]].<ref name="SWJB" /> The QFD was primarily comprised of [[Quarren]]s, a [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala|Dac]], who had allied themselves with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. They had a partnership with [[Pammant Docks]],<ref name="Tarkin" /> and [[Factory|factories]] located on the [[planet]] of the [[Pammant|same name]].<ref name="SWJB" />

The Quarren official [[Tikkes]] represented the QFD in the Confederacy.<ref name="OF84">{{GermanFanCite|84|Database}}</ref> Five years after the Clone Wars conclusion, a modified [[Berch Teller's warship|warship]] [[Attack on Sentinel Base|attacked]] [[Sentinel Base]], a remote [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] outpost. Scans revealed that some components of the ship were from the ''[[Invincible]]'' and the ''[[Lucid Voice]]'', ''Providence''-class cruiser-carriers manufactured by the Free Dac Volunteers Engineering Corps. The Empire launched an investigation into whether QFD or Pammant Docks assisted in the assembly of the warship.<ref name="Tarkin" />
