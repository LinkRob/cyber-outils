'''DC-18s''' were a type of weapon considered old by between [[2 BBY|2]] and [[1 BBY]]. A trio of [[Grysk]] that were part of the crew of a [[freighter]] that was captured by the [[Galactic Empire]] over the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] were armed with a mix of DC-18s and [[DL-18 blaster pistol]]s, but set them aside to wipe the [[starship]]'s electronics as it was being captured. The Grysk were then [[stun]]ned by [[stormtrooper]]s of the [[1st Legion|First Legion]] before they could reach their weapons. [[Commander]] [[Kimmund]] of the First [[Legion]] later informed his superiors of the weapons after the battle was over and speculated that they had been purchased locally.<ref name="Thrawn2" />

==Behind the scenes==
DC-18s were first mentioned in the [[2018]] novel ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]'', which was written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Thrawn2" />
