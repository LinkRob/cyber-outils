The [[Duros/Legends|Duros]] known as [[Beadle Lubboon]] was known to have a '''mother.''' This female Duros was the wife of [[Lubboon]] and lived with her family in [[Xakrea]], the largest city on [[Darkknell/Legends|Darkknell]]. She lived during the [[Republic Dark Age]] when the planet Darkknell was the capital of the [[Daimanate]], a [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] princedom ruled by the [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] [[Daiman]]. She dotted over her son Beadle.

Like her husband, she wanted a better life for her son Beadle. In [[1032 BBY/Legends|1032 BBY]], the [[Mercenary/Legends|mercenary]] [[Brigadier]] [[Jarrow Rusher]] visited their shop to purchase spare parts for their ship [[Diligence (New Sith Wars)|''Diligence'']]. In exchange, Beadle Lubboon was recruited by [[Rusher's Brigade]] where he became a Trooper.
