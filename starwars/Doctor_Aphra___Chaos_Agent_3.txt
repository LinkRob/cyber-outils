'''''Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent'' 3''' is the third issue of the [[2025]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent]]'' The issue was written by [[Cherish Chen]], illustrated by [[Gabriel Guzman]] and [[Michael Atiyeh]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[August 27]], 2025.

==Publisher's summary==
''THE DOCTOR AND THE SMUGGLER MEET AGAIN! The DAGGER agency teams up DOCTOR APHRA with the rebel hero and former smuggler HAN SOLO for a life-or-death mission! A prized piece of art must be returned to a gangster from HAN'S past! Can the doctor make it out in one piece?!''

addition to the team. Despite struggling with the deactivation of her electro-tattoos (who the kark could have hacked those?!), she
completed her mission with LUKE SKYWALKER. But that was just the beginning&hellip;.}}
