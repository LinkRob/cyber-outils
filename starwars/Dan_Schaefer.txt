'''Dan Schaefer''' is an [[Wikipedia:Illustrator|illustrator]] and filmmaker. He created a number of illustrations and characters for the [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game|Star Wars Roleplaying Game]] supplement [[Creatures of the Galaxy]], The Last Command Source Book and the Westend Star Wars magazine.
