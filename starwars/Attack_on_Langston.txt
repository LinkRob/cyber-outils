Early in the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] '''attacked''' the resource-rich [[planet]] [[Langston]] and devastated the world's infrastructure during their assault. [[Galactic Republic]] [[Grand Army of the Republic|clone forces]] repelled the Separatist [[battle droid]]s, and a [[Standard week|week]] afterwards, a Republic [[Aid mission to Langston|aid mission]] led by [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[D'urban Wen-Hurd]] was sent to assist Langston's population.<ref name="Brotherhood" />
