The '''Danoria system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Nouane sector]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square N-7 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Danorian wolf]] [[species]].<ref name="Steal" />
