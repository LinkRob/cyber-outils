The '''Darkghast''' was a large [[Species|creature]] that lived in the [[cave]]rns of the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]]. It was similar to the [[rancor]], though it had extra [[limb]]s and mandibles.<ref name="Vader Immortal Ep II" />
