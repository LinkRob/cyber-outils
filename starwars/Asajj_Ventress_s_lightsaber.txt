'''Asajj Ventress's lightsaber''',<ref name="Concept">{{SW|url=the-harbinger-concept-art-gallery-star-wars-the-bad-batch|text="The Harbinger" Concept Art Gallery &#124; Star Wars: The Bad Batch}} {{C|slide 8}}</ref> or simply '''Ventress's lightsaber''', was a [[Color|yellow]]-bladed [[lightsaber]] acquired by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Asajj Ventress]] on the [[black market]] some [[time]] after fallen [[Jedi]] [[Barriss Offee]] stole the [[Asajj Ventress's lightsabers|lightsabers]] that Ventress had used as the [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] of [[Count]] [[Dooku]].<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> She viewed getting a new lightsaber as a way to distance herself from what had been her last physical link to her time as his apprentice.<ref name="FactFile 2014 112" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="DD Date" /> during which the lightsaber had a straight cylindrical-shaped hilt, Ventress used the lightsaber for her bounty hunting career until it was laid to rest with her on<ref name="FactFile 2014 112" /> her [[homeworld]]<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> of [[Dathomir]]<ref name="FactFile 2014 112" /> after her apparent [[death]]<ref name="TBB article">{{SW|url=news/bad-batch-interview-nika-futterman-asajj-ventress|text=Ventress Strikes Back}}</ref> in the [[second battle of Christophsis]].<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> After her revival, Ventress continued to wield it as a bounty hunter during the [[Imperial Era]], during which it had a [[Curved-hilt lightsaber|curved hilt]].<ref name="The Harbinger" />

==Design==
Ventress's lightsaber emitted a yellow-[[color]]ed [[plasma]] blade.<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="DD Date" /> it had a standard, cylindrical-shaped hilt with its [[power cell]] located at the bottom of the lightsaber, under the ridged grip. The ignition trigger was located right over the grill hilt, which covered the rest of the hilt up to the blade emitter.<ref name="FactFile 2014 112" />

[[File:Ventress_lightsaber_concept_art.png|thumb|left|300px|The lightsaber's design in 19 BBY.]]
Holding a fascinating design, the bottom of the lightsaber was a traditional, ridged grip and fully closed off section, which kept the power cell safe. Meanwhile, around three fifths of the hilt was made up by an upper section that was crafted from perforated metal, which left the inner workings of the blade exposed. Nevertheless, the design did not appear to make the weapon vulnerable to moisture damage. In effect, the glowing center of the lightsaber appeared as if it was simultaneously vulnerable and safe, remaining a powerful weapon. As such, the lightsaber reflected much of Ventress's own mental state.<ref name="FactFile 2014 112" />

During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Harbinger Date" /> during the [[Imperial Era]], the lightsaber had a curved hilt and a design<ref name="The Harbinger" /> similar to her old twin red lightsabers.<ref name="TCWFilm">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Ventress's lightsaber was originally designed by [[Will Nichols]] for the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series. It was scheduled to appear in eight episodes starring Ventress and [[Quinlan Vos]]. The show was canceled, however, before the episodes were finished and the story was instead adapted into the novel ''[[Dark Disciple]]'' by [[Christie Golden]],<ref name="Ventress's lightsaber artstation">{{ArtStation|username=songjong|url=artwork/Zx0zw|name=William Nichols|text=Star Wars Clone Wars Props}}</ref> where the lightsaber made its first appearance.<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> Nichols posted the concept design of the lightsaber in his portfolio in 2016. This design<ref name="Ventress's lightsaber artstation" /> was later used in the revived [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|Season 7]] of ''The Clone Wars'', being used as half of [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|Maul's lightsaber]].<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice">{{TCW|The Phantom Apprentice}}</ref>

The [[The Bad Batch Season 3|''The Bad Batch Season'' 3]] trailer first revealed a new design for the lightsaber as part of Ventress's reveal,<ref name="Trailer">{{SW|url=news/the-bad-batch-season-3-trailer|text=Watch the ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 3 Trailer}}</ref> which established that Ventress survived her apparent death in the novel ''[[Dark Disciple]]''.<ref name="TBB article" /> The design made its formal first appearance in the episode "[[The Harbinger]]."<ref name="The Harbinger" /> On [[May 2]], [[2025]], a [[StarWars.com]] article on Ventress's return in ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Underworld]]'' confirmed that the two designs were the same lightsaber.<ref name="TotU article">{{SW|url=news/tales-of-the-underworld-asajj-ventress-nika-futterman|text=Witchy Woman: Asajj Ventress Returns in ''Star Wars: Tales of the Underworld''}}</ref>
