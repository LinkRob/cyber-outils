'''Fiona Shaw''' is an [[Wikipedia:Irish people|Irish]] actress who played [[Maarva Carassi Andor|Maarva Andor]] in the ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' television series. Shaw starred in numerous plays, films, and television series, beginning her career as a classical stage actress and winning the [[1990]] and [[1994]] [[Wikipedia:Laurence Olivier Awards|Laurence Olivier Awards]] for Best Actress for her roles in the plays [[Wikipedia:Electra (Sophocles play)|''Electra'']], [[Wikipedia:As You Like It|''As You Like It'']], and [[Wikipedia:The Good Person of Szechwan|''The Good Person of Szechwan'']], and [[Wikipedia:Machinal|''Machinal'']]. She was made an honorary [[Wikipedia:Order of the British Empire|Commander of the British Empire]] by Queen [[Wikipedia:Elizabeth II|Elizabeth II]] in [[2001]] and is known for playing [[W:c:harrypotter:Petunia Dursley|Petunia Dursley]] in the [[Wikipedia:Harry Potter (film series)|''Harry Potter'']] film series and [[W:c:killingeve:Carolyn Martens|Carolyn Martens]] in the [[Wikipedia:BBC|BBC]] series ''[[Wikipedia:Killing Eve|Killing Eve]]'', for which she won the [[Wikipedia:British Academy Television Award for Best Supporting Actress|BAFTA for Best Supporting Actress]] in [[2019]].

==Biography==
Fiona Shaw was born in [[Wikipedia:Cork (city)|Cork]], [[Wikipedia:Ireland|Ireland]].<ref name="Guardian" /> In [[1979]], she saw ''[[Star Wars]]'' for the first time and could not believe her eyes.<ref name="Reckoning line">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/andor-fiona-shaw-talks-fan-favorite-reckoning-line-1235225155|text=‘Andor' Star Fiona Shaw Enjoys Her “Reckoning” Line, Too|author=Davids, Brian|date=2022-09-21|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20220921223603}}</ref> Executive producer<ref name="That Would Be Me">{{Andor|That Would Be Me}}</ref> and writer [[Tony Gilroy]] asked Shaw if she would be part of the ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' television series, and she was delighted by his enthusiasm for the project.<ref name="Figuren">{{YouTube|video=Y43dwUDkY-E|text=Star Wars Andor &#124; Fiona Shaw Interview &#124; Official clip|channel=@StarWars-Figurencom|name=StarWars-Figuren. com}}</ref> She was drawn into the story by the complexity of family life, which she views as an enriching way to live.<ref name="Daughter">{{SW|url=news/fiona-shaw-andor-interview|text=Daughter of Ferrix: ''Andor'''s Fiona Shaw on the Role of Cassian's Mother, Maarva}}</ref> Shaw was happy to join ''Star Wars'' to play<ref name="Figuren" /> [[Maarva Carassi Andor|Maarva Andor]], the adoptive [[Parent|mother]] of [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]].<ref name="That Would Be Me" /> She liked how Gilroy's complex writing made the show similar to [[Wikipedia:Europe|European]] or [[Wikipedia:Russia|Russian]] films, and the show was a domestic investigation set in a metallic world.<ref name="Reckoning line" />

She thought that the show was a great tale of morality because it showed how individuals should keep an eye on the people in power. She liked how ''[[Star Wars]]'' is dealing with psychology and emotional vocabulary. Shaw viewed her character as a pilgrim wandering through the [[universe]] and trying not to look at [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] power.<ref name="ProductionBrief">{{Getty|url=thewaltdisneystudios/allaccess#!asset/gppr5wpqgb4t74974f8s|text=ANDOR Production Brief V3 FINAL 8-04-22|author=[[Gina McIntyre|McIntyre, Gina]]|date=2022-08-04}}</ref> She wondered if Maarva forgot to have kids due to her and her husband, [[Clem Andor]], being too busy wandering [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Reckoning line" /> Shaw first met [[Diego Luna]], the actor who played Cassian Andor, during the [[Wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic|COVID-19 pandemic]]. Due to [[Wikipedia:COVID-19|COVID]] production protocols, the actors wore a mask during rehearsals, though Shaw managed to get a sense of Luna's compassion and kindness. Shaw thought it was interesting how the people of [[Ferrix]] help each other instead of showing off.<ref name="Daughter" />

[[File:Andor_Maarva_Andor_poster.png|thumb|left|140px|Fiona Shaw as Maarva Andor in her character poster]]
When Shaw went in for her costume fitting, she was impressed by the hundreds of different costumes that had different aesthetics for each [[planet]].<ref name="Figuren" /> Shaw loved the idea of the linen [[jumpsuit]] that costume designer [[Michael Wilkinson]]'s team deveoped for her. He thought she looked great in the clothes and carried so much.<ref name="Wilkinson interview">{{SW|url=news/dressing-the-galaxy-designer-michael-wilkinson-defines-the-look-of-andor-s-costumes|text=Dressing the Galaxy: Designer Michael Wilkinson Defines the Looks of ''Andor'''s Costumes}}</ref> Scenes on Ferrix were filmed in an intricate set in [[Wikipedia:Marlow, Buckinghamshire|Little Marlow]], [[Wikipedia:Buckinghamshire|Buckinghamshire]].<ref name="ProductionBrief" /> According to Tony Gilroy, everything on Ferrix was filmed first.<ref name="Good day">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/andor-season-2-post-credits-scene-tony-gilroy-1235268962|text=‘Andor' Creator Tony Gilroy Talks Luthen's Good Day, That Post-Credit Scene and Season Two|author=Davids, Brian|date=2022-11-23|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20221123185515}}</ref> One of the first scenes Shaw and Luna filmed together was an argument in their [[Andor household|house]].<ref name="Daughter" /> Shaw was very impressed by the amount of detail present in the Ferrix set, which made her feel like she must bring what she can.<ref name="Figuren" /> She also thought that the house was breathtaking, and she would often go out to stare at it.<ref name="Old School">{{WebCite|url=https://www.empireonline.com/tv/news/star-wars-andor-doesnt-use-volume-says-tony-gilroy-exclusive|text=Andor Doesn't Use Star Wars' Volume Video Wall: ‘We're Old-School,' Says Tony Gilroy – Exclusive Image|author=Travis, Ben|date=2022-08-01|work=[[Wikipedia:Empire (magazine)|''Empire'']]|archivedate=20220801153835}}</ref> Shaw found playing an older character interesting because Maarva has compressed her values to what she think matters.<ref name="Daughter" /> She also liked how Maarva had a very different earlier life, and she was completely needed for Andor's life, making her an ignition key to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]].<ref name="Figuren" />

Shaw, Luna, and [[Antonio Viña]], young Kassa's actor, worked together to shape Maarva's journey as an adoptive mother. Shaw enjoyed filming the scenes on [[Kenari]], and she felt Maarva and Kassa saved each other because the latter gave her purpose and love. She found it lovely how Maarva kept the [[droid]] [[B2EMO]] and they got old together.<ref name="Daughter" /> She enjoyed working with B2EMO because he has personality and understands Maarva. Shaw loved getting to reunite with [[Adria Arjona]], the actress who played [[Bix Caleen]], though they did not share any scenes together.<ref name="Figuren" />

Shaw enjoyed the line "That's what a reckoning sounds like" because it suggested Maarva has experienced turbulence before.<ref name="Reckoning line" /> In an interview released in [[August]], [[2022]], she said that Gilroy's story is a "scurrilous" and "[[Wikipedia:Social realism|social-realist]]" take on "the Trumpian world" that was "exploding in different places right now" with people's rights being taken away.<ref name="Trumpian world">{{WebCite|url=https://www.empireonline.com/tv/news/andor-star-wars-take-trumpian-world-fiona-shaw-exclusive|text=Andor Is Star Wars' 'Scurrilous Take On The Trumpian World,' Says Fiona Shaw|author=Ben Travis|date=2022-08-02|work=[[Wikipedia:Empire (magazine)|''Empire Magazine'']]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/IkFld}}</ref> In [[September]], Shaw attended the ''Andor'' launch event in [[Wikipedia:Hollywood, Los Angeles|Hollywood]], [[Wikipedia:California|California]].<ref name="Launch event">{{SWYouTube|Fv6hHyIToC0|Diego Luna and the Cast and Crew of Andor}}</ref>
