The '''battle on Lokori''' was one of the last engagements of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] in the [[Clone Wars]] against [[Galactic Republic]] fought on the [[planet]] of [[Lokori]] in [[19 BBY]].

==The battle==
For the last four [[Standard month|months]] of the war, elements of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Confederacy navy]] had been attempting to break through the lunar picket lines of the [[Bright Nebula Fleet]] of the [[Republic Navy]] in a bid to attack the planet, only sporadically managing to launch attacks against the planet itself.<ref name="Catalyst" />

In the last [[Standard day|days]] of the war, the Separatists broke through and managed to land on the surface, and had reached the point of breaking the Republic lines.<ref name="Catalyst" /> Then, [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] publicly declared himself as the galaxy's [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] and [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>

[[Galen Walton Erso]] and his family, who had been living on Lokori during the prolonged battle, were caught up in the confusion as the [[battle droid]]s landed and were nearly killed by a group<ref name="Catalyst" /> of [[B1-series battle droid]]s.<ref name="Geektionary" /> They were only saved by the [[Mission to Mustafar|shutdown]] of the [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]]<ref name="Catalyst" /> ordered by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on [[Mustafar]].<ref name="Episode III" /> Nevertheless, Galen had nightmares about the battle droids for months.<ref name="Catalyst" />
