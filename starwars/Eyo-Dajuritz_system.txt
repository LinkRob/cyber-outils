The '''Eyo-Dajuritz system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square N-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the planet [[Eyo-Dajuritz]].<ref name="Origin" />

==Behind the scenes==
