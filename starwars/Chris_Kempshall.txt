Dr. '''Chris Kempshall''' is the author of the [[2024]] book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' and the [[2026]] book ''[[Star Wars: Star Pilots]]''. He is a historian of the [[Wikipedia:World War I|First World War]] and popular media representations of warfare.<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=authors/2255716/chris-kempshall|text=Chris Kempshall}}</ref> He had previously co-written [[2021]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'' alongside [[Jason Fry]], [[Cole Horton]], and [[Amy Ratcliffe]] for the [[Star Wars|franchise]],<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> as well as [[2023]]'s "[[The Death Star Stratagem]]" for the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine.
