The '''Criigo insurrection''', also known as the '''insurrection at Criigo''', was a battle at [[Criigo]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[New Republic]] some [[time]] before the [[battle above Desevro]]. The Empire sent in the most merciless squadrons at its disposal. One New Republic [[Unidentified New Republic pilot (Criigo)|pilot]] saved several lives during the battle, and [[Sabine Wren]] later created an inspirational piece of art to honor this iconic individual.<ref name="Squadrons" />
