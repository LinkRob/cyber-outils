'''Blue''' was a [[Devaronian]] [[Gender|female]] who was the leader of a [[Blue's partisans|rebel team]] in the [[Five Points system]]. At some point in the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] Era, [[Jyn Erso]] was blackmailed by Imperial [[Admiral]] [[Bethava Rocwyn]] into infiltrating and spying on the cell.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

Blue's cell, together with Jyn, brought food to the [[Star system|system's]] [[planet]] [[Watassay]]. After they arrived at the planet, they were captured and arrested by Admiral Rocwyn. The Admiral mentioned that Blue would be executed for treason.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

==Personality and traits==
Blue was a dedicated leader who possessed a certain fury that Jyn thought was similar to fellow [[Partisan]] leader [[Saw Gerrera]]. Jyn considered her to be a good person despite not agreeing with her Rebel motives.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
