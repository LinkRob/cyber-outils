A '''brood pouch''' was a part of the anatomy of the [[Hutt]] [[species]]. Hutt [[parent]]s would keep their Huttlet in the pouch for the first fifty [[Standard year|years]] of their life. Afterward, the Huttlets emerged from the pouch and had a mind similar to an [[Human|infant]].<ref name="TCW Movie Rewatch P3">{{SW|url=news/the-clone-wars-rewatch-theatrical-release-3-of-3|text=''The Clone Wars'' Rewatch: A Sith Plot Unravels in the Theatrical Release (Part 3 of 3)}}</ref> Between around [[24 BBY]] and [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Birth">[[Hyperspace Stories 6|''Hyperspace Stories'' 6]] establishes that [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] had recently become a [[Parent|father]] to [[Rotta]]. The events of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] take place between around [[24 BBY]] and [[22 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:HyperspaceStories6Date|here]].</ref> the Huttlet [[Rotta]], [[Desilijic|son]] of the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]], emerged from his parent's brood pouch.<ref name="TCW Movie Rewatch P3 />

==Behind the scenes==
Brood pouches were mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the article "[https://www.starwars.com/news/the-clone-wars-rewatch-theatrical-release-3-of-3 ''The Clone Wars'' Rewatch: A Sith Plot Unravels in the Theatrical Release (Part 3 of 3)]," written by [[Kristin Baver]] and published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[August 16]], [[2018]].<ref name="TCW Movie Rewatch P3 /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Brood pouch/Legends|brood pouches]] were mentioned in the [[2008]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: The Visual Guide]]'', which was based on [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''The Clone Wars'']] animated series, that was written by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Heather Scott]].<ref name="TCW Visual Guide Legends">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>
