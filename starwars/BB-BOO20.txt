'''BB-BOO20''' was a [[BB-series astromech droid]] that had a orange, green, and silver body, with a black sensor. The [[droid]] resided on the dunes of [[Pasaana]] amongst the native [[Aki-Aki]].<ref name="DF" />

==Behind the scenes==
This Halloween-themed droid is a toy that was made available exclusively at [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] Parks in [[2020]] as part of the [[Star Wars: Droid Factory (toy line)|''Droid Factory'']] toy line.<ref name="DF" />
