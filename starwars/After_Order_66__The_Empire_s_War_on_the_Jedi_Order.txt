'''''After Order 66: The Empire's War on the Jedi Order''''' was a text [[author]]ed by the [[scholar]] [[Ril Shiwer]] that questioned why the [[Galactic Empire]], despite facing other more pressing military matters in its early [[Standard year|years]], devoted so much resource to the eradication of the remaining [[Jedi|members]] of the [[Jedi Order]] in the [[Great Jedi Purge]] after [[Death|killing]] the majority during [[Order 66]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] cited the text in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
''After Order 66: The Empire's War on the Jedi Order'' was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
