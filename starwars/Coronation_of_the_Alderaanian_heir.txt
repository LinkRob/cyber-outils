The '''coronation of the Alderaanian heir''' was a ceremony in which the [[Prince|heir]] to the [[Alderaanian]] [[throne]] was formally declared crown princess or prince. The ceremony was a follow-up to the heir's [[Day of Demand]], and was carried out after the heir had completed their three challenges, of the [[Challenge of the Body|Body]], [[Challenge of the Mind|Mind]] and [[Challenge of the Heart|Heart]], proving themselves worthy to inherit. As in the Day of Demand, the heir carried the [[Rhindon Sword]] during the ceremony. At the climax, the heir would be crowned by the monarch with the [[Heir's Crown]].<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

At [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] coronation ceremony in [[3 BBY]], [[Chandrilan]] [[senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] was in attendance as was many of her friends from the [[Apprentice Legislature]], including [[Amilyn Holdo]] of [[Gatalenta]] and [[Chassellon Stevis]] of [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />
