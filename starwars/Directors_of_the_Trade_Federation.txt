The '''Directory''',<ref name="Separatists">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Separatists}}</ref> also known as the '''directors of the Trade Federation'''<ref name="FACPOV">{{InsiderCite|200|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> or '''Federation Directorate''',<ref name="Ships">{{EncyclopediaCite|Miscellaneous Ships and Vehicles (2)}}</ref> formed the executive board of the [[Trade Federation]], a powerful shipping and trade conglomerate.<ref name="FACPOV" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[33 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{InsiderCite|200|A Certain Point of View}} states that the [[Eriadu Trade Summit]] took place 33 [[Standard year|years]] prior to the events of {{Film|IV}}, corresponds to [[33 BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the then incumbent directors were all [[assassin]]ated by the [[Nebula Front]] during the [[Eriadu Conference]]. That incident allowed a group of ambitious [[Neimoidian]]s, led by [[Nute Gunray]] and [[Lott Dod]], to step in as directors. Gunray took the office of [[Viceroy of the Trade Federation|Viceroy]], and Dod that of [[Senator]] in the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Galactic Senate|Senate]].<ref name="FACPOV" />
