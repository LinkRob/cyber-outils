'''Circumtore Hoops''' were a type of hoop-shaped, [[Diet|vegetarian]] [[Foodstuff|dessert]] made from [[Color|brown]] [[sweet fried dough]] with a green topping and a brown garnish. The dish was created by the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]], who modified the sweet fried dough balls served by the people of the [[Hutt Space]] [[planet]] [[Circumtore]] so that the dish's shape resembled the ring shape of the planet. This change in shape resulted in increased surface area for frying, pleasantly surprising Tuggs.<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" />

After a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that included a poriton in Hutt Space booked by members of the [[Hutt]] [[species]],<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs included a recipe for the hoops in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> In the book, Tuggs suggested that the rings could be eaten in the [[Local day|morning]] with a cup of [[caf]] instead of as dessert, and even recommended starting and ending each [[Standard day|day]] with one.<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Circumtore Hoops were created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the [[Wikipedia:Doughnut|doughnuts]] makes use of fresh [[Wikipedia:Wheat flour|cake flour]], [[Wikipedia:White sugar|granulated sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Baking powder|baking powder]], [[Wikipedia:Nutmeg|nutmeg]], [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], [[Wikipedia:Buttermilk|buttermilk]], a large [[Wikipedia:Eggs as food|egg]], unsalted [[Wikipedia:Butter|butter]], [[Wikipedia:Vanilla extract|vanilla extract]], and [[Wikipedia:Vegetable oil|vegetable oil]]. The dish's [[Wikipedia:Glaze (cooking technique)|glaze]] is made using [[Wikipedia:Powdered sugar|confectioner's sugar]], more unsalted butter, whole [[Wikipedia:Milk|milk]], [[Wikipedia:Pandanus amaryllifolius|pandan extract]], [[Wikipedia:Vanilla|vanilla]], and freeze dried [[Wikipedia:Strawberry|strawberries]].<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" />
