'''Corinna Bechko''' is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] author whose work includes [[Wikipedia:Boom! Studios|Boom! Studios]]' ''Planet of the Apes''. Along with ''Apes'' partner [[Gabriel Hardman]], Bechko wrote [[Star Wars: Legacy|''Star Wars: Legacy'''s]] [[2013]] [[Star Wars: Legacy Volume 2|sequel]].
