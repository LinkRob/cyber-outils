'''Fantabu''' were a [[species]] which produced [[Hair|fur]] and wool. The devotees of the [[Church of the Force]] living in [[Tuanul]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] used the hair of fantabu in their [[clothing]], with [[Lor San Tekka]] wearing a home-spun fantabu wool coat and [[Dasha Promenti]] wearing a vest fringed with fantabu-fur.<ref name="TFAVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The fur and wool of a fantabu first appeared as part of a costume in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], a film released in [[2015]]. The substance was not identified in the film, but was named in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released the day after the film.
