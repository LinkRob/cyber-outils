'''Callahan''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served in the [[New Republic]] as a [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] with the [[military rank]] of [[lieutenant]]. Callahan was stationed on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]] alongside [[Captain]] [[Porter (New Republic)|Porter]], and were both tasked by [[Governor]] [[Ryder Azadi]] in locating [[Commander]] [[Sabine Wren]].<ref name="Part One" />

==Biography==
At some point prior to 9 ABY, Callahan enlisted in the [[New Republic Starfighter Corps]] as a pilot, and eventually obtained the rank of lieutenant.<ref name="Part One" /> 

[[File:Lieutenant-Callahan.png|thumb|300px|left|Callahan stood in her cockpit as Sabine Wren sped towards her without slowing.]]
Sometime around that same year, Callahan, along with Captain Porter, were stationed on the planet of Lothal and provided security for a festival celebrating the planet's [[Liberation of Lothal|independence]] from the [[Galactic Empire]] in [[Capital City|the capital city]]. After Sabine Wren had failed to attend the festivities, Governor [[Ryder Azadi]] ordered Callahan and Porter to locate her and bring her into the city. Piloting two [[E-wing starfighter]]s, Callahan and Porter spotted Wren riding out of the city on a [[Sabine's speeder bike|speeder bike]] and moved to intercept her. As Captain Porter attempted to persuade Wren to turn around, Callahan landed [[Callahan's E-wing|her E-wing]] further down Lothal's highway to act as a roadblock. Callahan became increasingly panicked as Wren continued barreling toward her, before eventually abandoning the cockpit of her E-wing in fear as the [[Mandalorian]] managed to slip past Callahan's E-wing by ducking underneath it as she continued speeding out towards the city's [[LothalNet comm tower E-272|comm tower]].<ref name="Part One" />

==Personality and traits==
Callahan was a human female. While initially confident that Sabine Wren would be intimidated by the sight of two New Republic E-wings and return to Lothal's capital, Callahan would soon after abandon her [[starfighter]] in fear for her life after it appeared that Wren would crash in to it.<ref name="Part One" />
