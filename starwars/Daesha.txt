'''Daesha''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Twi'lek]] and the daughter of [[Lady]] [[Cessi]] during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="TftN 1" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Daesha was created for the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Tales From the Nightlands]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] in [[2025]]. Concept art of her as a child was created by [[Soo Lee]], who was the artist of the series's [[Tales From the Nightlands 1|first issue]]. She designed her with "good intentions and charming childlike naivety" and designed her clothes as inspired by her own [[Wikipedia:Asian culture|Asiatic]] cultural background, though she purposefully meshed various styles.<ref name="Insider 236">{{InsiderCite|236|Darker Than Night}}</ref>
