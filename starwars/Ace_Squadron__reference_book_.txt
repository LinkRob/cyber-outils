"'''Ace Squadron'''" is a volume in the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series of reference books published by [[De Agostini]]. It was published on [[May 27]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya" />

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''Used as a defense force by the Colossus supply station during the New Republic era, the Ace Squadron was composed of fighter pilots with special skills and even more unique personalities. These pilots protected the station while racing around the Colossus and were local celebrities. That all changed when the First Order broke into the station and took control. The stakes were high, and soon each member of the Squadron and their closest friends had to make a decision that would change their lives forever: to continue as before, or to stand up to the Empire's ideological and technological heir organization. The lives of every member of Ace Squadron were changed forever.''<ref name="Altaya" />
