'''''Bounty Hunters'' 23''' is the twenty-third issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Natacha Bustos]] and [[Arif Prianto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[June 1]], [[2022]].

==Publisher's summary==
''"A CLEAR AND PRESENT DENGAR" The most underestimated bounty hunter in the galaxy in his greatest adventure yet!
DENGAR makes his move &ndash; and the Underworld will be forever changed. Meanwhile, time is running out for T'ONGA and her crew to break VUKORAH&hellip; Will a young girl pay for their failure with her life?''
