The '''Beta Trawler''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] trawler during the [[New Republic era]] in [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|1}}</ref> along with another [[Alpha Trawler|human]] and a [[Quarren Trawler|Quarren]]. He watched as his two companions threatened a [[The Fledgling Mythrol|Mythrol]] in a [[Boatman's Reach|public house]] at the settlement [[Ferryman's Reach]] on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Pagodon]]. He did not speak during the encounter, allowing the others in the party to make the verbal threats.<ref name="Chapter1" />

During this incident, a [[Din Djarin|Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] named Din Djarin arrived to collect a [[bounty]] on the [[Mythrol]]. The human, along with his two companions, threatened Djarin. When the [[Unidentified bartender (Pagodon)|bartender]] slid a drink towards them, Djarin grabbed the drink and smashed it on the human's head, knocking him out. He then proceeded to incapacitate the other human and kill the Quarren.<ref name="Chapter1" />

The human had light skin, [[Color|blue]] [[eye]]s, a dark brown beard and a [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] left [[Limb|arm]]. He wore a coat over a dark shirt and pants, as well as a [[Hat|knit cap]] on his head.<ref name="Chapter1" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Beta Trawler appeared in the [[Chapter 1: The Mandalorian|first episode]] of the [[2019]] [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'',<ref name="Chapter1" /> which aired on [[November 12]], 2019.<ref name="DTCI">{{WebCite|url=https://lumiere-a.akamaihd.net/v1/documents/the_mandalorian_project_one-sheet_10_ec1e005f.pdf|text=The Mandalorian Media Kit|work=[https://dtcimedia.disney.com Disney Direct-to-Consumer & International]|archivedate=20230315011507}}</ref> Identified in the credits as "Beta Trawler," he was portrayed by [[Ryan Watson]], who was also a stunt coordinator for the show.<ref name="Chapter1" />
