'''Bollux''' was a [[labor droid]] with [[Gender|masculine programming]] and [[Color|silver]] plating. One [[Standard day|day]], he entered [[The Lodge]] at [[Fort Ypso]], [[Vandor]], and told the [[Bartender|barman]], [[Tivoche Bilure|Midnight]], he had a message for the [[droid]] [[SV-38P]]. Bollux claimed to have witnessed the [[Corellian]] scoundrel [[Han Solo]] blowing a [[Corporate Sector Authority]] [[prison]] off the surface of a [[planet]] to free his [[First Mate|first mate]] [[Chewbacca]], and to have helped Solo destroy a battalion of [[Battle droid|war droids]] belonging to [[Xim]] the Despot. He also claimed that he was now working for a [[university]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], tracking down sites and artifacts pertaining to Xim. Bollux had allegedly traveled to Vandor to pursue a tip on a treasure.<ref name="Tales from Vandor" />

Although Midnight suspected that Bollux was merely malfunctioning, he later learned from a former [[Corporate Sector]] official that the new [[Stars' End]] prison had indeed been blown into [[orbit]] by a Corellian who had broken in to rescue a [[Wookiee]].<ref name="Tales from Vandor" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bollux first appeared in the [[1979]] novel ''[[Han Solo at Stars' End]]'', written by [[Brian Daley]],<ref name="Han Solo at Stars' End">''[[Han Solo at Stars' End]]''</ref> and was introduced into [[canon]] with a mention in the [[2018]] replica journal ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Tales from Vandor" /> A droid who resembles Bollux later appeared in "[[Chapter 22: Guns for Hire]]," but it has not been confirmed to be Bollux himself.<ref name="Chapter 22">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 22: Guns for Hire}}</ref>
