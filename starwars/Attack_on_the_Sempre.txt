During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[troop carrier]] ''[[Sempre]]'' was '''attacked''' by two squadrons of stolen V-19 fighters and the frigate Castilus while [[transport]]ing [[Wookiee]] [[Slavery|slaves]]. The attackers damaged and boarded the transport. The ''Sempre''{{'s}} captain transmitted a distress call that was received by the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]'', [[Commodore]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] flagship. Thrawn suspected Nightswan to be behind the attack. Imperial authorities ordered the ''Chimaera''{{'s}} [[stormtrooper]] [[commander]] [[Major]] [[Ayer]] not to tell the ''Chimaera''{{'s}} crew that the ''Sempre'' was transporting slaves. However, Commodore Thrawn and [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] insisted on inspecting the ''Sempre'' and discovered its true cargo.

==Prelude==
Following the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Empire]] subjugated and [[Slavery|enslaved]] the [[Wookiee]]s of [[Kashyyyk]], regarding them as beasts.<ref name="SWRTVG">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> The [[Xenophobia|xenophobic]] Galactic Empire wanted to exploit the Wookiees because their massive, robust physiology allowed them to work in harsh and dangerous conditions. Kashyyyk was completely subjugated by the Empire with the Wookiees being forced to cut [[wroshyr tree]]s, produce crops for the [[Imperial Military]], and extract [[wroshite]] mineral deposits.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> Wookiee slaves were also exported offworld as slave laborers in Imperial mines and projects such as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. In addition, Wookiees were also used as test subjects for weapons and medicines.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>

Before being shipped offworld, Wookiee slaves were taken for processing at [[space station]]s such as [[Lansend Twenty-Six]]. They were then transported in modified [[troop carrier]]s such as the ''[[Sempre]]''. The insurgent leader [[Nevil Cygni|Nightswan]] and his operatives took an interest in liberating enslaved Wookiees as part of [[Nightswan's campaign|his insurgency]] against the Empire. The insurgents stole the [[frigate]] ''[[Castilus]]'' and two squadrons of [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s eight [[Standard week|weeks]] prior to raiding the ''Sempre''.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==The raid==
The insurgents' frigate and V-19 Torrent starfighters ambushed the ''Sempre'' while it was traveling trough [[Realspace|space]]. The boarders stormed the ship and liberated the Wookiee slaves. The liberated Wookiees joined in the fight and slammed several [[stormtrooper]]s and crew members against the bulkheads. The crew of the ''Sempre'' picked up a distress signal that was intercepted by the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]'', the [[Chiss]] [[Commodore]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] flagship. By the time the ''Chimaera'' arrived at the ''Sempre''{{'s}} coordinates one hour and 49 minutes later, the insurgents had escaped with the Wookiees and all the crew had been killed.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==Aftermath==
To maintain the secrecy of the ''Sempre''{{'s}} true cargo, [[Coruscant]] directed the [[stormtrooper]] [[commander]] [[Major]] [[Ayer]] to board the ''Sempre'' while the ''Chimaera''{{'s}} crew dealt with the attacking ships. However, Commodore Thrawn and [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] insisted on inspecting the troop ship. Vanto observed that several of the fallen stormtroopers had [[blaster]] burns while others had injuries to their heads and torsos consistent with being hit. Thrawn also observed that some of blood marks on the bulkhead were higher than the victims. Vanto and Thrawn also found that the assailants had left a blood mark behind.<ref name="Thrawn" />

Despite Major Ayer's protests, Thrawn insisted on inspecting the troop quarters. Thrawn and Vanto discovered that the troop carrier racks had been modified to accommodate Wookiees. Thrawn then deduced that the ''Sempre'' was transporting Wookiee slaves; information that the Empire wanted to keep secret. Under Thrawn's command, the ''Chimaera'' then went to aid the beleaguered station of Lansend Twenty-Six, which was being attacked by the insurgents who wanted to free more Wookiee slaves. Thrawn's reinforcements succeeded in [[Battle at Lansend Twenty-Six|defeating]] these insurgents.<ref name="Thrawn" />
