{{Quote|It is time, friends. Tonight we call ourselves ''Amidalans''&hellip;and together we pledge&hellip;to find whoever murdered our queen&hellip;and ''kill'' them.|Sabé, founding the Amidalans|<ref name="Vader 3" />}}
The '''Amidalans''' were a resistance group founded by [[Sabé]], former [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] of [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of [[Naboo]], following Amidala's [[death]]. Sabé gathered alongside [[Captain]]s [[Tonra]] and [[Gregar Typho]] of the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]], and numerous others loyal to Amidala in the former [[queen]]'s [[Padmé's Apartment|apartment]] on [[Coruscant]], pledging to hunt down and kill whoever was responsible for her death.<ref name="Vader 3" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date note">The events of [[Darth Vader (2020) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 1]] take place concurrently to and immediately after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Empire Strikes Back'' to the [[Standard year|year]] [[3 ABY]], so the events of ''Darth Vader'' 1 must also take place in that year.</ref> Sabé and her team were still active,<ref name="Vader 1" /> having come to the conclusion that Amidala was killed by the [[Galactic Empire]] enforcer [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]&mdash;who was, unbeknownst to them, Amidala's husband and the former [[Jedi Knight]] Anakin Skywalker.<ref name="Vader 3" />

On Naboo, the Amidalans summoned a [[sando aqua monster]] to eat Vader. As the monster ate the Dark Lord, he stabbed it through the upper-mouth, becoming the first person known to kill a sando aqua monster. Sabé soon encountered Vader on [[Vendaxa]],<ref name="Vader 2">[[Darth Vader (2020) 2|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 2]]</ref> leading him back to Naboo, where she, Tonra, and Typho attempted to kill him.<ref name="Vader 3" /> However, Vader managed to use [[the Force]] to [[Force choke|choke]] each of them and escaped. Luckily, each of them survived, but Vader was soon met by an [[army]] of Amidalans led by [[Captain]] [[Ric Olié]] waiting for him and his [[death trooper]]s. Vader managed to subdue each one, however, and stabbed Olié through the shoulder, killing him.<ref name="Vader 4" /><ref name="Ric Death Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places Ric Olié's death to [[3 ABY]]. Therefore, it can be assumed he died from his wounds.</ref> On [[Polis Massa]], Vader [[Battle on Polis Massa|encountered]] the Amidalan Fleet, who tried to kill him, but Vader either used the Force to blow up their engines, or his lightsaber to cut them off. He defeated the fleet, and returned to his master Palpatine,<ref name="Vader 5">[[Darth Vader (2020) 5|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 5]]</ref> leaving a hundred Amidalans bleeding out on the asteroid.<ref name="Vader 29">[[Darth Vader (2020) 29|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 29]]</ref>

At some point after, Imperial forces under the command of [[General]] [[Hurst Romodi]] carried out a [[Massacre of the Amidalans|purge]] of the group, leading to several commendations awarded to Romodi's forces. The bodies of at least seven individuals from the Amidalans were identifiable from a holo image obtained by [[Ochi]] of [[Bestoon]].<ref name="Vader 21">[[Darth Vader (2020) 21|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 21]]</ref> However, the handmaidens survived.<ref name="Vader 28">[[Darth Vader (2020) 28|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 28]]</ref>
