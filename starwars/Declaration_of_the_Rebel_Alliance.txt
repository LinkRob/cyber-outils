The '''Declaration of the Rebel Alliance''' was a declaration by [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] to rally the various rebel cells that formed the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] into the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. It was considered to be the official beginning of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. After a [[Operation Handoff|daring pursuit]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Imperial Military|forces]], Mon Mothma made a speech from the rebel [[VCX-100 light freighter]] ''[[Ghost]]'', on all rebel communications channels and on [[HoloNet]] broadcasts where rebel forces could slice into, in which she resigned from the [[Imperial Senate]] and condemned the actions of the [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].

She continued the speech and called for an alliance to restore the [[Galactic Republic]], vowing to not rest until the Empire had been defeated. Following the speech, dozens of rebel [[starship]]s emerged from [[hyperspace]] above the [[planet]] [[Dantooine]], answering the call to action.

In the time following the Declaration of the Rebel Alliance, the newly created Alliance to Restore the Republic sent the [[Declaration of Rebellion]] to Emperor Palpatine, formally declaring the rebellion against the Empire. Subsequently, the [[Galactic Civil War]] erupted, and by its end the Empire was defeated, Emperor Palpatine was [[Death|dead]], and the Alliance had [[Declaration of a New Republic|reorganized]] into the [[New Republic]].

During the [[New Republic Era]], the [[First Order]], formed from remnants of the Galactic Empire in the [[Unknown Regions]] in the [[Standard decade|decades]] following the end of the war, revealed itself, and was countered by the [[Resistance]], an organization determined to protect the New Republic. It was during this [[time]] that [[the Rebel Files]], a central archive depository, was recovered, which contained highly sensitive Alliance documents and notes, including Mothma's account of the moments prior to the Declaration of the Rebel Alliance.

==Prelude==
===Seeds of resistance===
{{Quote|In broad strokes, our demands are that Chancellor Palpatine relinquish the emergency war powers granted to him, abolish the Sector Governance Decree, and begin cease-fire talks with Count Dooku's Separatists.|Mon Mothma|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
[[File:Sithdelscener1pic2.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A Cantham House meeting between several members of the Delegation of 2,000]]
As the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] entered its final stages, [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the Republic [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] continued to gain emergency powers from the [[Galactic Senate]]. By the final actions of the war, displeased [[senator]]s formed the [[Delegation of 2,000]], to counter Palpatine's growing political power, calling for Palpatine to relinquish the emergency war powers granted to him and the abolishment of the [[Sector Governance Decree]], and called for cease-fire talks with the Confederacy, with the group regularly partaking in the [[Cantham House meetings]], a set of meetings between members of the Delegation of 2,000 during the Clone Wars.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

Eventually, Palpatine declared the [[Jedi]], keepers of peace and justice in [[the galaxy]], and leaders in the [[Republic Military]], as traitors, and [[Great Jedi Purge|quelled]] a supposed [[Order 66|Jedi rebellion]]. Palpatine, secretly the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Sidious, [[Mission to Mustafar|sent]] his newly christened [[Sith apprentice]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to [[Mustafar]] to destroy the [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] and shut down the [[droid]] armies of the Separatists, effectively ending the war.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>

Chancellor Palpatine, taking the opportunity of a devastating war and the eradication of his Jedi enemies, [[Proclamation of the New Order|proclaimed]] a new order, and reformed the Republic into the first [[Galactic Empire]], with Palpatine as its [[Galactic Emperor]].<ref name="Episode III" /> One [[Standard day|day]] after the formation of the Empire, [[Imperial Intelligence]] cracked down on political rivals of Palpatine, conducting a [[Senatorial arrests|mass arrest]] which resulted in the arrest of 63 senators, several of which belonged to the Delegation of 2,000.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

Senators such as [[Mon Mothma]] and [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], who continued to work in the [[Imperial Senate]], continued to politically oppose the Emperor, as well as beginning to monitor [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early resistance movements]] against the Empire,<ref name="EncyclopediaMM">{{SWE|characters|mon-mothma|Mon Mothma}}</ref> such as the [[Raada resistance]].<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref> Years later, more and more rebel cells began to form and began working together.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> By [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Bail Organa regularly conducted surveillance on these resistance groups,<ref name="Droids in Distress">{{Rebels|Droids in Distress}}</ref> with ''[[Fulcrum]]'', secretly the former [[padawan]] of Anakin Skywalker, [[Ahsoka Tano]], who also gave these cells missions. After a [[Action at Mustafar|skirmish]] above Mustafar, operating strategy changed, with these rebel cells operating more closely together, such as [[Phoenix Cell]] and the [[Spectres]].<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref>

Insurgencies were also taking root in the [[Atrivis sector]], with the [[Liberators]] and the [[Atrivis Resistance Group]]. The Liberators were devastatingly [[Battle on Mantooine|defeated]] by the Imperial Navy after they seized a [[Imperial garrison (Mantooine)|garrison]] on [[Mantooine]]. After much deliberation, the Atrivis Resistance Group decided to join Mon Mothma in a rebel group that she nicknamed "the Alliance."<ref name="Rebel Files" />

===Relentless pursuit===
{{Quote|I name the Emperor, himself, for ordering the brutal attacks on the people of Ghorman. Their peaceful world is one of countless systems helpless against his oppressive rule. This massacre is proof that our self-appointed Emperor, is little more than a lying executioner, imposing his tyranny under the pretense of security. We cannot allow this evil to stand.|Mon Mothma denouncing the Emperor's actions during the Ghorman Massacre|<ref name="Secret Cargo" />}}
[[File:Nebula_fight.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Skirmish in the Archeon Nebula]]
After years of being unable to limit Palpatine's power and the occurrence of atrocities such as the [[Ghorman Massacre]],<ref name="Secret Cargo" /> an incident which witnessed the slaughter of peaceful protestors,<ref name="Rogue One VD">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> Mon Mothma denounced the Emperor's actions and resigned from the Senate, quickly becoming a target for the [[Imperial Military]] and designated a traitor,<ref name="Secret Cargo" /> with the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] issuing an alert for Mon Mothma in an attempt to capture her. Escorted by then-[[Axis network]] agent [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] and her aide [[Erskin Semaj]], Mothma succeeded in evading the ISB agents sent to apprehend her and escaping with Andor off-planet.<ref name="Welcome">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref> ISB agents also visited [[Cantham House]] on Coruscant, the meeting place of the Cantham House meetings, based on suspicions they had, and questioned [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and several others there.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

Under orders from Senator Organa, [[Captain]] [[Hera Syndulla]], [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Ezra Bridger]], and Captain [[Garazeb Orrelios]] onboard the ''[[Ghost]]'', a modified [[VCX-100 light freighter]] owned by the Spectres of Phoenix Squadron, rendezvoused with [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]] of [[Massassi Group]], who were escorting Mon Mothma to safety onboard the ''[[Chandrila Mistress]]'' in order to refuel the vessel. Earlier however, the ''Ghost'' was discovered by an Imperial [[E-XD-series infiltrator droid]], sent by [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], who was tasked with finding Mon Mothma.<ref name="Secret Cargo" />

After refueling most of the ships however, they were [[Operation Handoff|ambushed]] by Imperial forces, resulting in the destruction of the ''Chandrila Mistress'' and the rebels being force to take the [[Archeon pass]]. Soon, however, the rebels were ambushed by a second Imperial force, which included the new [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]], pressuring the rebels to flee through the [[Archeon Nebula]] in order to lose their Imperial pursuers, resulting in the rebels shaking off the Imperials, at the disadvantage of the ''Ghost'' and two remaining [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-Wing bombers]] being separated.<ref name="Secret Cargo" />

Two [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyers]], led by [[Arihnda Pryce]], [[Governor of the Lothal sector]], and [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]], soon arrived and captured the ''Ghost'' in a [[tractor beam]]. Imperial forces were defeated when the two bombers, piloted by Ezra Bridger and [[Gold Leader]] [[Jon Vander]] launched [[Proton torpedo]]s at the [[Nebula]] resulting in a release of energy, damaging the [[Star Destroyer]]s and allowing the surviving rebels to escape.<ref name="Secret Cargo" />

==The declaration==
{{Quote|This, my friends, this is our rebellion.|Mon Mothma after the arrival of various rebel starships|<ref name="Secret Cargo" />}}
[[File:Secret_Cargo.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Mon Mothma looking on at the newly assembled fleet in the aftermath of the declaration.]]
After the skirmish, the ''Ghost'', with the two Y-Wings docked with it, exited out of [[hyperspace]] above the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] forested [[Planet|world]] of [[Dantooine]]. As the ''Ghost'' ready to transmit, the rebels gathered in the cockpit and Mon Mothma began her speech.<ref name="Secret Cargo" /> Mon Mothma's speech was broadcast on all rebel communications channels, as well as available [[HoloNet]] channels that were sliced by [[satellite]]s, so that her call to arms could be heard by trillions of [[Sentience|beings]] across the galaxy,<ref name="Rebel Files" /> as well as established resistance groups such as Phoenix Cell, Massassi Group, and the [[Lothal resistance group]].<ref name="Secret Cargo" />

In the moments following Mon Mothma's call for an alliance, a [[CR90 corvette]] exited hyperspace, followed soon after by several other CR90s and [[Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette|''Sphyrna''-class Hammerhead corvettes]], eventually with many vessels exiting hyperspace, including ''[[Home One]]'' and ''[[Phoenix Nest]]''.<ref name="Secret Cargo" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Following is the address I will make within the hour. It will go out across all Alliance comm channels and will be inserted into the public holonet wherever we have a satellite splice. Trillions of beings will soon hear my voice. This is a galaxy-wide call to arms. We can no longer fight in isolation. Today is the first day of the Rebel Alliance. Together, we will restore the Republic.|An account of the moments before the Declaration written by Mon Mothma and uncovered in [[the Rebel Files]] decades later|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
Following the Declaration of the Rebel Alliance, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] was created and its branches and units were organized and structured. The Alliance was divided into two sections, the [[Alliance Civil Government]] with Mon Mothma as its [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] and Chief of State, and composed of civilian bodies such as the [[Alliance Cabinet]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and [[Propaganda Bureau]],<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> and the [[Alliance Military]], with Mon Mothma serving as the [[Commander-in-Chief]] and led by [[Alliance High Command]] and its sub-units.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> A document, the [[Declaration of Rebellion]], was sent to the Emperor to declare open resistance between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Propaganda" />

The declaration was considered an official beginning point to the Galactic Civil War,<ref name="Official beg" /> but the exact beginning date of the conflict was a disputed point among later [[historian]]s.<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> Two years later, the first major battle of the Galactic Civil War saw the Alliance steal the [[Death Star plans|plans]] for the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], a moon-sized [[battle station]] capable of destroying entire planets, at the [[Battle of Scarif]]. The plans were used to destroy the dreaded battle station at the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref><ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> Although the Alliance faced many major defeats, such as the [[Mid Rim Retreat]]<ref name="Twilight Company">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> and the [[Battle of Hoth]],<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref> they were able to continue their campaigns against the Empire, who had constructed a [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]].<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

The second Death Star was then destroyed in the following [[Battle of Endor]], which witnessed the defeat of the [[Imperial Navy]], the death of Emperor Palpatine,<ref name="Episode VI" /> and the [[Declaration of a New Republic]],<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> marking the creation of the formal [[New Republic]] government.<ref name="Galactic Atlas" />

[[File:ImperialCenter-ROTJ.jpg|thumb|250px|Celebrations on [[Coruscant]] after the defeat of the Empire on [[Endor]].]]
Despite Imperial operations such as [[Operation: Cinder]], which sought to devastate loyal Imperial worlds,<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> and the fragmentation of the Empire into various Imperial remnants,<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> the Empire was defeated in the final action of the war, the [[Battle of Jakku]], between the New Republic and the Empire led by [[Counselor to the Empire]] [[Gallius Rax]]. After the defeat of Rax and his forces, [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]], who had been left powerless by Rax, signed the [[Galactic Concordance]] and the [[Imperial Instruments of Surrender]], effectively ending the war and disbanding the Galactic Empire, instead forming into numerous independent governments such as the [[Coruscant's provisional government]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

[[Standard decade|Decades]] later, the [[First Order]] rose from pro-Imperial factions, including a [[Unknown Regions Imperial remnant|faction]] formed after the Galactic Concordance. The First Order would evade notice from the New Republic, [[Cold War|building up]] its [[First Order military|forces]] in the [[Unknown Regions]],<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> and [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroying]] the New Republic in one swift stroke, leaving a paramilitary force, the [[Resistance]], led by General Leia Organa, adoptive [[daughter]] of Bail Organa, to oppose the First Order,<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> and although the Resistance was defeated and suffered heavy losses, opposition against the First Order remained steadfast.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

It was during the fight against the First Order that General Organa received ''[[The Rebel Files]]'', a central data archive containing various documents and notes, including a note by Mon Mothma recording the moments prior to the Declaration of the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
