The '''"Landspeeder" TechPlate''' is a rare fine art print produced by [[Acme Archives Direct]] that depicts the technical schematics of the [[X-34 landspeeder/Legends|X-34 landspeeder]]. It was first unveiled at [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World|Walt Disney World's]] [[Star Wars Weekends|''Star Wars'' Weekends]] in [[May]] [[2013]].
