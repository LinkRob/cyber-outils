The [[Ewok/Legends|Ewoks]] of the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] [[Endor/Legends|Endor]] developed a '''trade language''' following the [[Battle of Endor/Legends|Battle of Endor]] and the establishment of a formal friendship with the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]]. The trade language was a combination of [[Galactic Basic Standard/Legends|Galactic Basic Standard]] and [[Ewokese/Legends|Ewokese]].
