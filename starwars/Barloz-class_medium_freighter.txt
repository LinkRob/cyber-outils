The '''''Barloz''-class medium freighter''', also known simply as the '''Barloz freighter''', was a class of [[freighter]] manufactured by [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]. During development of the [[YT-1000 light freighter]], the YT-1000 was seen as the replacement for the ''Barloz'' freighter. Although ''Barloz'' freighters were popular with independent merchants and [[planetary defense forces]], executives and designers of Corellian Engineering Corporation had started to see the large, wedge-shaped ''Barloz'' freighter as "tired."<ref name="Manual" />
