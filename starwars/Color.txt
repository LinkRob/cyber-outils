The '''color''' of an object was its property of producing different sensations on the [[eye]] as a result of the way it reflected light. The [[planet]] of [[Felucia]] hosted many translucent life-forms that colored the sunlight as its beams penetrated their skin.<ref name="SWEFelu">{{SWE|locations|felucia|Felucia}}</ref> Blue, green,<ref name="SWELightsaberCry">{{SWE|technology|lightsaber-crystals|lightsaber crystals}}</ref> red,<ref name="SWELightsaber">{{SWE|technology|lightsaber|Lightsaber}}</ref> and yellow were examples of colors.<ref name="SWEGuar">{{SWE|groups|jedi-temple-guard|Jedi Temple Guard}}</ref>

==In technology==
{{Quote|He's a BB unit, orange and white. One of a kind.''"<br />"''I don't care what color he is! No droid can be that important.|[[Poe Dameron]] and [[Finn]], regarding [[BB-8]]|<ref name="Episode 7">{{Film|VII}}</ref>}}
[[File:AhsokaTanoJarKai-CEUEEd.png|thumb|left|220px|Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers were white, due to her purifying a pair of red kyber crystals that were "bled."]]
The color of a [[Jedi]] [[lightsaber]]'s blade was defined by its [[kyber crystal]], which was harvested from the [[Crystal Cave]] on [[Ilum]].<ref name="SWELightsaberCry" /> A lightsaber crystal was colorless until it was found by a [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]], where the youngling would then become attuned to the crystal and the crystal would display its color, the color being determined by the youngling's "spiritual alignment."<ref name="The Gathering TG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-gathering-trivia-gallery|text="The Gathering" Trivia Gallery}}; image #5</ref><ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref> A Jedi's lightsaber blade was usually green or blue, but other colors were possible, such as [[Mace Windu]]'s [[Mace Windu's lightsaber|purple-bladed lightsaber]].<ref name="Databank Lightsaber">{{Databank|lightsaber|Lightsaber}}</ref><ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> The [[Sith]] often wielded red lightsabers due to a process known as [[bleeding]], where the user used the [[dark side of the Force]] to bend a [[kyber crystal]] to their will. Additionally Jedi can use the Force to [[Purification|heal]] them of the dark side presence, causing them to turn white.<ref name="Ahsoka" />

Specialized [[B1-series battle droid]]s were distinguished by colored markings on their [[armor]]. Blue denoted [[pilot droid]]s, red denoted [[security droid]]s, and yellow denoted command droids.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars New Edition">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>

==In culture==
{{Quote|Dressed in black. In the armor of the Empire.|[[Sabé]] after being promoted to [[Lieutenant Commander|lieutenant commander]] on [[Gabredor III]]|<ref name="DV24">[[Darth Vader (2020) 24|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 24]]</ref>}}
[[File:Sith_to_be.png|thumb|right|250px|Sheev Palpatine, Supreme Chancellor of the Galactic Republic, favored the color red.]]
In [[the galaxy]], colors were often culturally significant. For example, the color red was associated with diplomatic immunity under the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="SWJ1">''[[Star Wars Journeys: The Phantom Menace]]''</ref> warning others not to attack. As the color of ambassadorial relations and neutrality under the Republic, a completely red color scheme such as that of the [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class space cruiser]] ''[[Radiant VII]]'' indicated a ship came from the [[capital]] world of [[Coruscant]]. As the personal favorite color of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], red was co-opted during his chancellery and assigned across Republic vessels at his direction.<ref name="CVNE">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> Dark red was also the main color scheme on Republic ships and vehicles during the Clone Wars, whereas the Separatist ships and vehicles largely used dark blue.<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> However, the color red was sometimes regarded as intense and aggressive. For that reason, the Sith wielded red lightsaber blades that represented their destructive view of [[the Force]].<ref name="Databank Lightsaber" /> Nevertheless, the members of many Force-worshipping sects native to [[Jedha]] donned scarlet vestments.<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

[[File:JesseKixAnaxes-TBB.png|thumb|left|250px|Clone troopers often painted their armor in accordance with the unit to which they were assigned.]]
Likewise, the white color of [[clone trooper armor]] signified an honorable symbol of defense before becoming skewed into one of a faceless icon of conformity under the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[clone trooper]]s were referred to as "the boys in white."<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> In the days of the Old Republic, silver was used as a status symbol for royalty, as residents of [[Naboo]] used [[chromium]] to cover their [[starfighter]]s and [[cruiser]]s.<ref name="SOTG">''[[Star Wars: Ships of the Galaxy]]''</ref>

All [[language]]s combined, there existed forty billion, eleven million, seven hundred forty-two thousand, nine hundred and eighty-three accepted synonyms for the color blue. In [[Galactic Basic Standard]], known shades of blue included ao, aqua, azure,<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> and royal blue.<ref name="Hope & Courage">''[[Forces of Destiny: Tales of Hope & Courage]]''</ref>

The Galactic Empire favored muted colors of gray, white and black and tried to redesign ships, uniforms, and other government property in this color scheme wherever possible. [[Sabine Wren]] was not in favor of this practice and used [[graffiti]] to provide bursts of brighter color.<ref name="newhero" /> In the [[Imperial Military]], the wearing of white tunics was generally reserved for either [[Grand Admiral]]s or members of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]].<ref name="Complete Visual New">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> Imperial armor was often black,<ref name="DV24" /> as seen on people like [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], [[TIE pilot]]s,<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> members of the [[Inquisitorius]]<ref name="Kenobi">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref> and [[Moff]] [[Gideon]].<ref name="Mando">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 7: The Reckoning}}</ref>

The [[flight suit]]s worn by the [[Resistance pilot|starfighter pilots]] of the [[Resistance]] were dyed in a hue known as "interstellar orange."<ref name="TFAVD" /> Each group was also designated by various hues, such as [[Red Squadron (Resistance)|Red]],<ref name="Episode 7" /> [[Green Squadron (Resistance)|Green]],<ref name="Episode 8">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> and [[Blue Squadron (Resistance)|Blue]] Squadrons,<ref name="Episode 7" /> along with their leaders.<ref name="Episode 7" /><ref name="Episode 8" />

[[File:Boonta_Eve_Podracers.png|thumb|right|250px|Podracers were painted in various colors.]]
On [[Tatooine]], where the sport of [[podracing]] was common, [[Podracer (profession)|racers]] wielded various colors on both their [[Podracer|vehicles]] and flags.<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref>

In [[Taris]]ian culture, the color purple was considered the "color of administration."<ref name="TFAVD" />

[[Caf]] without any [[cream]] or [[sugar]] added was referred to as "black"; [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was known to prefer black caf.<ref name="Brotherhood">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']]</ref> One form of caf was described as "black and sweet".<ref name="STET!">{{StoryCite|story=STET!|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>

==In clothing==
{{Quote|For what it's worth, you actually do look pretty good in black."<br />"''Huh.'' You think so?|A fellow rebel and Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Star Wars 28">[[Star Wars (2020) 28|''Star Wars'' (2020) 28]]</ref>}}
When it came to [[clothing]], individuals had different preferences when it came to color. For example, when [[Luke Skywalker]] put on an [[Imperial officer]]'s uniform while on a [[mission to Coruscant]], one of his fellow rebels commented that he looked good in black.<ref name="Star Wars 28" /> After this, Skywalker began wearing black clothing.<ref name="Star Wars 29">[[Star Wars (2020) 29|''Star Wars'' (2020) 29]]</ref>

==In biology==
Not all species saw color the same way; for instance, the [[Kaminoan]]s lived in dwellings that appeared to be completely white; in reality, the buildings were simply colored with ultraviolet colors invisible to [[human]]s.<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> Colors that the [[Evereni]] [[Marda Ro]] had never seen before and that she could not describe existed within [[The Veil (Sophros)|the Veil]].<ref name="Path Ven">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref>

==In genetics==
A human [[parent]] could pass on their [[eye]] color to their [[Reproduction|offspring]]. [[Ben Solo]] had the same piercing brown eyes as his mother [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who was sure that this feature was inherited from her own birth mother, [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="Family at War">''[[Skywalker: A Family at War]]''</ref>

==Rank==
Traditionally, the color of the armor on a clone trooper denoted ranks such as [[Clone Sergeant|sergeants]] and [[Clone Lieutenant|lieutenants]]. However, this practice was rescinded as the war progressed, instead being used to signify belonging to a certain division or unit.<ref name="Clone Trooper Armor">{{Databank|clone-trooper-armor|Clone Trooper Armor}}</ref>
