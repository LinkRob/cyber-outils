'''Calculus''' was a branch of mathematics that dealt with change. Students in [[Calimondretta]]'s [[Promisorium|schools]] learned calculus. [[Voort saBinring]] was proficient in it and may have taught it, as he used it in an insult on a being's intelligence, by implying they were ineffective at passing tests dealing with it.
