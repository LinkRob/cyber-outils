The '''481st Legion''' was a [[legion]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] that was active at the end of the [[Clone Wars]] during the implementation of [[Order 66]],<ref name="Nexus of Power" /> a secret [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|order]] that forced [[clone trooper]]s to [[Death penalty|execute]] their [[Jedi]] comrades, in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> As part of Order 66, elements of the 481st Legion were dispatched to the [[planet]] [[Ilum]] to secure the [[Jedi Temple (Ilum)|Jedi Temple]] there and [[Great Jedi Purge|eliminate]] any Jedi survivors from the initial onslaught.<ref name="Nexus of Power" />

Once the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|transitioned]] into the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] had firm control of the Empire, the 481st Legion was rotated out and recalled to their home unit. The legion was subsequently replaced by a joint [[Imperial Army]] [[Imperial Garrison (Ilum)|garrison]] and an [[Imperial Navy]] [[Ilum battle group|battle group]] that acted as a [[blockade]].<ref name="Nexus of Power" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 481st Legion was mentioned in ''[[Nexus of Power]]'', a [[2016]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplement book published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Nexus of Power" />
