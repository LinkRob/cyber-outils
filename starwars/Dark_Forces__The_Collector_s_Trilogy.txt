'''''Dark Forces: The Collector's Trilogy''''' was a dramatization written by [[John Whitman]], adapted from the material written by [[William C. Dietz]] in the [[Star Wars: Dark Forces (novellas)|''Dark Forces'' novellas]]. The dramatization was released as an audiobook CDs and tapes as well as broadcast on [[Wikipedia:National Public Radio|NPR]]. The performance was directed by [[Peter Moore]] and featured a score by veteran ''[[Star Wars]]'' composer [[John Williams]].<ref name="High Bridge" />
