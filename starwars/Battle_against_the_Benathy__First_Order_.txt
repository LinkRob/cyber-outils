In [[33 ABY]], Kylo Ren and his [[troop]]s '''fought''' the [[Benathy]] on their [[homeworld]] [[Benath]] to prevent [[King]] [[Kristoff]] and their people from expanding their territory.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|But still, we are walking in Darth Vader's shadow. This is the field where he fought. There is glory here.''"<br />"''Glory? Nah. There's none of that buried out there. There are only wasted lives&hellip;|Kylo Ren and Ruthford|<ref name="Age of Resistance" />}}
[[File:Benathy-AORKyloRen1.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Kylo Ren met with King Kristoff before the battle, attempting to complete his objectives through diplomacy.]]
In [[33 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] traveled to the [[Wild Space]] [[planet]] [[Benath]] to negotiate with the [[Benathy]] about halting their expansions, as they had already conquered four [[planet]]s and wiped out all life on them. Accompanying him was [[Ruthford]], an Imperial [[stormtrooper]] who had fought the Benathy alongside Ren's [[Anakin Skywalker|grandfather]] and was the sole survivor. Ruthford had accompanied Ren to advise him on dealing with the Benathy. He also set troops on the planet, just in case the negotiations went foul.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />

The duo headed to a tent where the [[king]] of the Bethany, [[Kristoff]], waited. He told Ren to have a seat, but Ren declined, saying that that it would make him look like an infant. Ren then proceeded to order Kristoff to quit expanding his land, but the king wouldn't budge. He said he remembered when Vader had come demands. He had killed their last [[Unidentified Benathy king|king]] and a battle ensued, but even he couldn't defeat their god. Ren remarked that he'd put Kristoff's people down, but Kristoff replied by saying that they got back up. Ren tried to again convince the ruler to stop his expansion by using [[the Force]]. But still, the monarch was not agreeable to Ren's terms. Ruthford, who was standing in the back, advised the king to listen to Ren's command, or else. This infuriated the king, but before he could strike out, Ren stabbed him in the head, killing him and beginning a battle.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />

==The battle==
===Outbreak of conflict===
{{Quote|He's killed the king!|A Benathy to his comrades|<ref name="Age of Resistance" />}}
A savage battle broke out. Ruthford advised Ren to retreat, knowing the brute strength of the Benathy. Ren refused, saying Vader never would have. Ruthford told Ren that his grandfather fought well and strong and thousands died on both sides. But the one thing Vader was not able to defeat was their god.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />

===Death of a god===
{{Quote|It's here. Their god. Fire!''"<br />"''You do understand Zillo Beasts are unbreakable, yes?''"<br />"''Nothing's unbreakable.|Kylo Ren and Ruthford|<ref name="Age of Resistance" />}}
[[File:KneelBeforeRen-AORKyloRen1.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Benathy submitted to the First Order after their god was slain by Kylo Ren.]]
As the battle raged, a massive [[Zillo Beast]] arose. This beast was their god and it was virtually invincible, and even Vader had failed to kill the creature. The Zillo Beast raged and was able to attack a First Order ship overhead. However, Kylo Ren was determined to destroy it. He ordered his men to get his shuttle. As Ren leaped onto his shuttle, Ruthford told him that he could not prove anything to his dead grandfather. But Ren pressed on. Ruthford thought that he was a stupid kid as he watched the shuttle fly off. Igniting the crimson blade of his [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|lightsaber]], Ren jumped off the platform of his shuttle and into the mouth of the Zillo Beast. Ruthford watched from below disappointed, saying Kylo would not have impressed Vader, right as the beast came tumbling down. Ren had entered the animal and killed it from inside.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />

The Benathy immediately quit fighting and bowed to the blood-covered Ren. After killing their god, he ordered them to serve the First Order.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />

He had also earned the newfound respect and admiration of Ruthford.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />
