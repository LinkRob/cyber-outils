An '''outpost''' was located on the jungle [[planet]] [[Devaron]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. It existed in conjunction with the [[Temple of Eedit]], a [[Jedi temple]] that served as a separate Republic outpost.

==History==
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] [[Battle of Devaron|besieged]] the [[Galactic Republic]] outpost on [[Devaron]] with an attack force from the [[Trade Federation]]. Bane captured [[Jedi General]] [[Bolla Ropal]], as well as the [[Kyber memory crystal]] that Ropal held in his possession.<ref name="Cargo of Doom" />

==Behind the scenes==
Within the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the Republic outpost briefly seen in "[[Cargo of Doom]]" and the [[Temple of Eedit/Legends|Temple of Eedit]] from "[[Monster]]" were confirmed to be the same facility. Both locations were derived from the same production artwork, entitled "Devaron Jedi outpost."<ref name="Complete Season Two">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Two|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season Two]]</ref> However, in official [[canon]], the [[StarWars.com]] [[Databank (2014-)|Databank's]] entry on Devaron indicated that the two locations were separate.<ref name="Databank: Devaron">{{Databank|devaron|Devaron}}</ref>
