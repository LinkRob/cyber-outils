'''Atai Molec''' was the [[Zygerrian]] [[Gender|male]] [[Prime Minister]] to [[Queen]] [[Miraj Scintel]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. He was also [[Captain of the Guard|captain]] of the [[Zygerrian Royal Guard]]. After betraying Scintel to [[Dooku]], leader of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Molec became the new [[King]] of Zygerria by the [[time]] of the [[Imperial Era]]. His two sons, [[Hordem Molec|Hordem]] and [[Sono Molec|Sono]] became [[prince]]s.

==Biography==
During the [[Clone Wars]], Atai Molec and [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] spoke with [[Commander]] [[Darts D'Nar]] about his last shipment of [[Kiros]]'s colonists. Molec was surprised that D'Nar had not left yet. As [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] entered to speak with D'Nar, Molec growled when Dooku introduced him to Kenobi.<ref name="K" />

[[File:AtaiMolecHS-SWE.png|thumb|left|200px|Atai Molec holding a powered down electro-whip]]
As Molec was about to whip one of his [[Slavery|slaves]], he was stopped by a [[Ahsoka Tano|slave]]. He then saw the slave's master in [[Zygerrian]] armor and that he flew one of their ships. At first, Molec was distrustful of him when he demanded to speak to the Queen about [[Bruno Denturri]]. However, Molec brought in the man known as [[Anakin Skywalker|Lars Quell]] before the queen. Molec was then told that a man in Zygerrian armor had tried to free one of the enslaved colonists. Molec then realized that it was Kenobi. Molec then brought Kenobi before the Queen to punish him for the Jedi's crimes against their empire. However, Molec, the Queen and their guards were then attacked by Quell and his slave, who revealed themselves to be Anakin Skywalker and his apprentice, Ahsoka Tano. Though Molec was knocked down by Tano, the Queen and their guards were eventually able to subdue the Jedi.<ref name="SotR">{{TCW|Slaves of the Republic}}</ref>

Later, Molec visited a caged Tano and revealed that the Queen had promised that after Skywalker was tamed, Tano would be his slave to do with whatever he desired. As an act of defiance, Tano then telekinetically pushed Molec off a precipice, nearly sending him falling to his [[death]]. In retaliation, Molec electrocuted Tano several times with the [[shock collar]] she was wearing as a slave.<ref name="SotR" />

Molec then invited Count Dooku to [[Zygerria]] to speak with the Queen about her defiance in not killing Skywalker. Dooku decided on his arrival that the Jedi should be killed immediately. Molec agreed with the Count to kill the Jedi (except Tano); Miraj, however, refused these terms and was unwilling to let Anakin die. Dooku then deemed that [[Miraj Scintel]] was unfit to be queen. Molec then stood by as Dooku Force-choked her, an act that would prove fatal. Skywalker escaped however, taking the dying Miraj with him.<ref name="EfK">{{TCW|Escape from Kadavo}}</ref>

Having usurped Miraj, Molec later became king and had a son, [[Sono Molec|Sono]].<ref name="Friends" />

==Personality and traits==
Atai Molec was a [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Friends" /> Zygerrian<ref name="K" /> who was 1.9 [[meter]]s, or 6 [[Foot|feet]], 3 inches tall.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> He had [[Color|red]] [[hair]], blue [[eye]]s and tan skin.<ref name="K" /> He served as the Zygerria's prime minister<ref name="SotR" /> and the [[captain]] of the [[Zygerrian Royal Guard|royal guard]]. Molec sought to expand Zygerria's cruel slave-trading empire.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> When Queen Scintel refused to kill Anakin Skywalker, Molec invited Count Dooku to Zygerria to speak with the rebellious monarch. He did not interfere as the Count strangled the queen.<ref name="EfK" />

==Skills and abilities==
Molec was known for armed and unarmed combat, bureaucracy, leadership, intimidation, and security.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Equipment==
Atai Molec was skilled with an [[electro-whip]].<ref name="SotR" />
