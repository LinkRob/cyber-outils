'''Arzo Suun''' was a [[Dathomirian]] [[Zabrak]] [[Gender|male]] from the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]] who was formerly part of a [[Mak Eak's clan|clan]] of the [[Nightbrothers]], a colony of Zabrak warriors, during the [[Clone Wars]]. At some point, Suun escaped Dathomir.<ref name="Collapse" /> Prior to the [[Second Battle of Dathomir]] of [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The ''[[Collapse of the Republic]]'' modular encounter mentioning Arzo Suun features the [[Nightbrother village]]. Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' states that the village was destroyed in the [[Second Battle of Dathomir]] of [[19 BBY]], the modular encounter must take place before that battle.</ref> he hired fellow Nightbrother [[Ruul]] and a group of individuals to [[Attempt to steal from a Nightbrother village|steal]] medical supplies, basic technology, and a long-range [[Comm unit|comms system]] for his former village leader, [[Mak Eak]], from a [[Unidentified Nightbrother warehouse|warehouse-like building]]. While at the building, the group was caught by a group of [[Nightsisters]], a clan of [[witch]]es who commanded the Nightbrothers, led by [[Nelsel]] when she sensed their presence through [[the Force]].<ref name="Collapse" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arzo Suun was mentioned in ''[[Collapse of the Republic]]'', a [[2019]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Collapse" />
