An '''emergency kit''' was a supply kit used on [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]] around the time of the [[Planet/Legends|planet's]] [[Invasion of Naboo/Legends|invasion]]. The contents of the kit included a [[Glowrod/Legends|glowrod]] and two [[Medpac/Legends|medpacs]].<ref name="Invasion">[[Star Wars: Invasion of Theed Adventure Game|''Star Wars: Invasion of Theed'' Adventure Game]]</ref>
