The '''Fire Mountain Rally''' was a sporting event which consisted of several races. It was part of a regional circuit of events.<ref name="Brotherhood" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']] is set between the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] and the [[Battle of Christophsis]], both of which are dated to [[22 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> a few [[Standard week|weeks]] after the beginning of the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] mentioned the Fire Mountain Rally to [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], mentioning that the final race of the event was coming up and that [[Tom-Torre]] was attempting a sweep of the regional circuit.<ref name="Brotherhood" />
