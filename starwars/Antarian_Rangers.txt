The '''Antarian Rangers''' was a [[Law enforcement agency|security force]] that was created in [[620 BBY]] on [[Antar 4|Antar IV]] to aid the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="HC 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Tha Antarian Rangers was mentioned in the [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 2|second issue]], published in [[2016]] by [[De Agostini]], of the fortnightly magazine ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]''.<ref name="HC 2" />
