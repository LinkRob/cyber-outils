During the [[Invasion of Naboo]], the [[Trade Federation Droid Army]] stormed the [[swamp]]s of [[Naboo]] and '''attacked''' the [[Gungan]] [[capital]] of [[Otoh Gunga]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|My troops are in position to begin searching the swamps for these rumored underwater villages. They will not stay hidden for long.|OOM-9 to [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]]|<ref name="Episode I" />}}
[[File:OOM9Reporting-TPM.png|thumb|left|300px|Commander OOM-9 reports the progress of his hunt for the rumored Gungan civilization.]]
In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas note" /> the [[Trade Federation]] [[megacorporation]] launched an [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] at the urging of its mysterious benefactor, [[Darth Sidious]]. Having arrived just before the crisis escalated, the [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] made their way to the surface of Naboo and, with the help of the [[Gungan]] [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] [[Jar Jar Binks]], traveled to the Gungan [[capital]] of [[Otoh Gunga]]. There, [[Boss]] [[Rugor Nass]] ignored Kenobi's warnings that the Federation's [[battle droid]]s would discover his people.<ref name="Episode I" />

After the [[capture of Theed]], amid the Federation's occupation of Naboo, [[OOM command battle droid]] [[Commander]] [[OOM-9]] sent [[troop]]s to investigate the various Gungan [[City|villages]] on the planet, just as Kenobi had predicted.<ref name="Episode I" />

==The attack==
[[File:CaptainTarpals-BaseSeries5.png|thumb|right|Captain Roos Tarpals (pictured) was vital in warning the people of Otoh Gunga of the coming assault.]]
The [[Trade Federation Droid Army]] stormed the Naboo swamps<ref name="SWE">{{SWE|groups|gungans|Gungans}}</ref> and discovered the underwater Gungan civilization, including Otoh Gunga. With its discovery,<ref name="Episode I" /> the Droid Army marshalled itself for an attack. Being stationed on the parameter of the [[city]], [[Captain]] [[Roos Tarpals]] saw the coming assault and, sounding a warning alarm, warned the population about the approaching [[battle droid]]s. He coordinated the Gungan evacuation into the swamps<ref name="SWOTFL" /> of Naboo's surface, from which the Gungans retreated to the [[Gungan Sacred Place|sacred place]].<ref name="Episode I" />

==Aftermath==
Otoh Gunga was left abandoned after the attack. After the [[Battle of Naboo|defeat]] of the Trade Federation,<ref name="Episode I" /> the Gungans were able to return to their cities.<ref name="Shadow Warrior">{{TCW|Shadow Warrior}}</ref>
