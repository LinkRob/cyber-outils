'''''Everything I Need to Know I Learned From a Star Wars Little Golden Book''''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Golden Books|Golden Book]] title. The 96-page title, written by [[Geof Smith]] and featuring illustrations from various past ''Star Wars'' Golden Book titles by various illustrators, was published on [[September 6]], [[2016]].

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
'''''Feel the power of ''Star Wars'' and Little Golden Books!'''''

''Feel like the weight of the galaxy is on your shoulders? Thinking about joining the dark side? To restore your inner peace with the Force, look no further than the timeless ''Star Wars'' Little Golden Books! This inspirational collection features illustrations of epic scenes from the entire ''Star Wars'' saga. It's the perfect gift for ''Star Wars'' and Little Golden Book fans of all ages.''

==Contents==
''Everything I Need to Know I Learned from a Star Wars Little Golden Book'' is a ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Golden Books|Golden Book]] release with an inspirational theme. The title begins by showing examples of tough times presented as various scenes from ''Star Wars'', then shows ways to overcome them, but warns of the dangers of the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]]. The scenes presented are drawn from every Golden Book adaptation of the films in the new [[canon]] continuity, from [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''The Phantom Menace'']] to [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''The Force Awakens'']], as well as the titles [[Star Wars: I Am a Droid|''I Am a Droid'']], [[Star Wars: I Am a Princess|''I Am a Princess'']], [[Star Wars: I Am a Jedi (Golden Book)|''I Am a Jedi'']], [[Star Wars: I Am a Pilot (Golden Book)|''I Am a Pilot'']] and [[Star Wars: I Am a Sith|''I Am a Sith'']].
