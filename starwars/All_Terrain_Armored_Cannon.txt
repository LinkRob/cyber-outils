The '''All Terrain Armored Cannon (AT-AC)''' was a bipedal [[walker]] used by the [[Imperial Military|ground forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] during the early [[Imperial Era]]. The walker was a part of the [[AT-series|all-terrain vehicle]] line and an early model in [[urban suppression walker]] production, and would pave the way towards the eventual production of the [[All Terrain Scout Transport]].

==Description==
The All Terrain Armored Cannon, or AT-AC, was a model of [[AT-series|all-terrain vehicle]] [[walker]]<ref name="DRVG" /> produced by [[Kuat Drive Yards]]<ref name="CGttSWG" /> and used in the early [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Imperial Era]] by the reigning [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Plan 99" /> Further, the walker was an early entry into the [[urban suppression walker]] line<ref name="DRVG" /> and acted as a step in the evolution towards the [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]<ref name="Plan 99 Guide">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/plan-99-episode-guide|text="Plan 99" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}} {{C|Trivia Gallery, slide 1}}</ref> of the all-terrain line.<ref name="CGttSWG" /> The AT-AC contained one set of chin-mounted dual [[cannon]]s,<ref name="Plan 99" /> a large cabin surrounded by armor plating, and a forward view slit along with two large windows equipped with dual-barreled blasters.<ref name="Databank" />

==History==
===First Deployment===
[[File:At-ac.png|thumb|left|300px|AT-ACs were first deployed on Ord Mantell.]]
During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|216}}</ref> several AT-ACs<ref name="Databank" /> were deployed by the Imperial [[Advanced Science Division]] on the [[planet]] [[Ord Mantell]] during a [[Capture of Omega|raid]] to capture the rogue [[Cloning|clone]] [[Omega]] of [[Clone Force 99]]. As Imperial forces filled the streets of [[Ord Mantell City]],<ref name="Plan 99" /> AT-ACs helped establish Imperial rule over the city by marching through its streets.<ref name="Databank" /> As the AT-ACs patrolled the streets near [[Cid's Parlor]], where Imperial [[clone commando]]s were conducting the raid, one of the walkers was hijacked by [[Echo]] and [[AZI-345211896246498721347|AZI-3]]. Echo used the walker to ambush a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|Low Altitude Assault Transport]] that was being prepped to ship his captured squad members off-world. In response, a second AT-AC destroyed the hijacked walker by shooting at its reversed knee brace, causing it to topple over. The AT-AC continued to fire at the Bad Batch as the rogue clones fled the scene.<ref name="Plan 99" />

===Janix Patrol===
Somewhere between [[17 BBY]] and around [[13 BBY]], AT-ACs were later utilized on the Mid Rim planet [[Janix]] during the Empire's [[Hunt for Maul|hunt]] for the renegade Sith Lord [[Maul]] when the [[Tactical Defense Force]] failed to report and capture him.<ref name="Chapter6">{{MSL|Chapter 6: Night of the Hunted}}</ref>

Several AT-ACs began to patrol [[Janix city]], along with the [[TK stormtrooper]]s around [[Janix Civil Defense HQ]] and [[The Mark and Claim]] [[casino]].<ref name="Chapter8">{{MSL|Chapter 8: The Creeping Fear}}</ref>

At the height of the hunt, Maul and his companions attempted to escape [[Janix city]] through a power station, but found the complex guarded by numerous TK [[stormtrooper]]s as well as an AT-AC. Not wanting to wait for the Imperials to move on, Maul ignited his [[lightsaber]] and charged the walker as it began to fire its cannons at him. After dodging several blasts, Maul sliced through the AT-AC'S left foot before subsequently throwing [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|his lightsaber]] into the backside of the armored cabin. As Maul retrieved his lightsaber via [[The Force]], the AT-AC stumbled forward and collapsed before exploding due to the damage it had sustained. A TK [[Stormtrooper Commander]] inspected the damage walker but found no signs of the fugitives, before two [[Inquisitor]]s the [[Marrok|First Brother]] and [[Eleventh Brother]], arrived at the power station.<ref name="Chapter9">{{MSL|Chapter 9: Strange Allies}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The AT-AC walker is described as the "next evolution" towards the AT-ST.<ref name="Plan 99 Guide" />
