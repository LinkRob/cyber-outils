The [[Gender|female]] [[Trandoshan]] '''Doshanalawook''', later also known as "'''Chainbreaker'''," was a famous [[Wookiee]] liberator active during the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Occupation of Kashyyyk]]. Chainbreaker's identity was kept secret and it was assumed by most that she was a Wookiee. She was [[captain]] of the [[gunship]] ''[[Liswarr]]'' and operated it with many Wookiee clans. In [[3 ABY]], the [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] tracked down ''Liswarr'' and boarded the ship. Bossk made his way to the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], intending to capture Chainbreaker. During their confrontation, she revealed that they were [[clutchmate]]s and she convinced him to stop hunting Wookiees.

==Biography==
===Early life===
{{Quote|Kind old Liswarr'arindoo, who couldn't have a cub of her own, exchanged a bottle of Kowakian rum for my puny cracked egg, and saved me and suckled me and raised me like a daughter in her clan.|Doshanalawook, to her brother Bossk|audio=KowakianrumTC.flac|<ref name="Tooth and Claw" />}}
The [[Gender|female]] [[Trandoshan]] who came to be known as Chainbreaker was from the same [[clutch]] as her [[clutchmate]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]]. After hatching, Bossk ate his unborn siblings' eggs, including Chainbreaker's. Their father [[Cradossk]] always told Bossk he had consumed all the eggs, but Chainbreaker secretly survived, albeit crippled. Cradossk exchanged her cracked egg to [[Liswarr'arindoo]] for a bottle of [[Kowakian rum]]. The [[Wookiee]] named the hatchling Doshanalawook and raised her as a Wookiee in her clan.<ref name="Tooth and Claw" />

===Age of the Empire===
After the rise of the [[Galactic Empire]] and its subsequent [[occupation of Kashyyyk]], Doshanalawook [[captain]]ed ''[[Liswarr]]'', a Wookiee [[gunship]] with the [[Chyakk clan|Chyakk]], [[Koom clan|Koom]], and [[Gkrur clan]]s, and helped fight against Imperial rule and freed enslaved Wookiees. She became famous among the Wookiees as a liberator and was given the name Chainbreaker. As Chainbreaker, her identity as a Trandoshan was hidden from most, including the many [[bounty hunter]]s who pursued her. She was assumed to be some enigmatic and skilled Wookiee outlaw.<ref name="Tooth and Claw" />

In [[3 ABY]], Bossk, now a bounty hunter, tracked ''Liswarr'' to the [[Rycep Belt]]. He entered the ship through its [[airlock]] and made his way to the [[bridge]]. [[Rutallaroo]] and other Wookiees on board tried to stop Bossk but were all defeated. Bossk entered the bridge and confronted Chainbreaker, sitting in the dark in a [[mechno-chair]]. Bossk soon realized that Chainbreaker was not a Wookiee but in fact a female Trandoshan. She revealed that she and Bossk were clutchmates and when Bossk devoured her egg, he failed to kill his sister. She also told Bossk how she was given to Liswarr'arindoo and raised as a Wookiee named Doshanalawook. Bossk tried to escape the bridge but became trapped. In exchange for his freedom, and a private message to him from [[Lieutenant]] [[Masil Veit]], Bossk reluctantly agreed to stop hunting Wookiees.<ref name="Tooth and Claw" />
