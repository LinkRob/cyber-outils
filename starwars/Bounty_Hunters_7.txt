'''''Bounty Hunters'' 7''' is the seventh issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. It was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 18]], [[2020]].

==Publisher's summary==
''NO ONE CAN ESCAPE THE FINDSMEN! A badly wounded VALANCE reunites with a long lost love on a secret Rebel base&hellip; but trouble is right on his heels. ZUCKUSS AND 4-LOM, the deadliest bounty hunting duo in the galaxy are closing in, ready to take out their quarry dead or alive! And they brought an army of Clone War-era Battle Droids with them! Valance is trapped and if he wants to escape alive he'll have to make the toughest decision of his entire life''

Vukorah, hired the fierce findsman Zuckuss and his ruthless droid
partner, 4-LOM, to hunt down Cadeliah and Valance.
