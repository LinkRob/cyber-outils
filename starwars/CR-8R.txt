'''CR-8R''', also referred to as "'''Crater'''," was a prim, [[Gender|masculine]]-programmed [[droid]] who belonged to the [[Graf family]]. He was built by [[Rhyssa Graf]]<ref name="The Escape" /> from a mash-up of different droid models, including the lower half of a [[Viper probe droid]], the right arm of a [[3PO-series protocol droid]],<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> the left arm of a [[Binary loadlifter|load lifter]] droid,<ref name="The Dark">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Dark]]''</ref> the barrel-shaped shell of an [[astromech droid]], and the head of an antique [[Mark IV architect droid|Mark IV]] [[architect droid]].<ref name="Droidography" /> CR-8R participated in a [[Fact-finding mission to the Outer Rim|mission to the Outer Rim]].<ref name="Vader's Castle 1" />
