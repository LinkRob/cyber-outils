'''Alistair Petrie''' is a British [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]] who portrayed [[General]] [[Davits Draven]] in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''<ref name="Rogue One" /> and later in the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.<ref name="Season 2 cast">{{SW|url=news/andor-cast-interview|text=Introducing the Cast of ''Andor'' Season 2 - Updated}}</ref> Petrie is a lifelong ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan&mdash;with ''[[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|A New Hope]]'' actually the first film he ever saw in a theater<ref name="DravenIsBack" />&mdash;and stated he remains shocked that he has a role in the universe.<ref name="AsaKid">{{Threads|petriealistair|post/DJgnQ72itVL|[[Alistair Petrie]]|date=2025-05-11|quote=The kid in Salisbury from 1978 remains utterly perplexed. Remind him he stood up from his seat in the cinema, quietly walked down the aisle and through some twist of things impossible to quantify - walked into the screen.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/JVkuP}}</ref> With his return in ''Andor'', Petrie celebrated the show as some of the finest storytelling he had ever acted within and urged people who were not ''Star Wars'' fans to check it out, stating it raised questions of who people were and reflected the modern world.<ref name="DravenIsBack" /> Petrie is also aware that his character dies at the hands of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] in [[Star Wars (2015) 54|''Star Wars'' (2015) 54]] and interprets it as the end of Draven's journey, in which a man did his best with what he knew after a life of conflict, culminating in a final sacrifice.<ref name="Had a Journey">{{Threads|petriealistair|post/DJfTYOpi-x-|[[Alistair Petrie]]|date=2025-05-10|quote=It’s true. He had a journey. He did his best with what he knew. A life spent in conflict and a final sacrifice.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/iJblm}}</ref>
