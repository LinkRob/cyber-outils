'''Depa Billaba''', a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Chalactan|native]] of the [[planet]] [[Chalacta]], was a [[Jedi Master]] who served on the [[Jedi High Council]] during the final [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Jedi Order]]. She was [[Reproduction|born]] on Chalacta and became a member of the Order at a young age, where she received instruction as [[Jedi training|a Padawan]] under [[Jedi]] Master [[Mace Windu]]. Billaba was later appointed to the Council alongside her former teacher and was present when Jedi Master [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] presented [[Anakin Skywalker]], the [[Chosen One]], to the governing body. She later attended [[Funeral of Qui-Gon Jinn|Jinn's funeral]] after the Jedi Master was killed during the [[Battle of Naboo]].

Billaba continued to serve as a member of the Council during the [[Separatist Crisis|political crisis]] that led to the [[Clone Wars]], a pan-[[The galaxy|galactic]] civil war between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During the war, she, like many of the other Jedi, became a general within the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] and would take on [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]] as her [[Padawan]] learner. Following the [[Battle of Kaller]]'s conclusion as was part of the wider [[Outer Rim Sieges]], the two were attacked by Billaba's battalion of [[clone trooper]]s during the issuance of [[Order 66]], a secret protocol branding all Jedi as enemies of the state and calling for their execution. Due to Billaba's [[Death|sacrifice]], Dume survived the [[Great Jedi Purge]] and went into hiding under a new name&mdash;Kanan Jarrus. Initially traveling low and taking odd jobs, he eventually joined a [[Spectres|group of rebels]] who opposed the [[Galactic Empire]]. Although Jarrus hid his true identity as a Jedi for years, he remembered his Master with fondness. After adopting [[Ezra Bridger]] as an apprentice, Jarrus initially relied on his own incomplete training under Billaba in order to teach Bridger the ways of the Jedi and [[the Force]].

==Biography==
===Early life===
Depa Billaba was [[Reproduction|born]] on the [[planet]] [[Chalacta]] many [[Standard year|years]] prior to the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="The Secret Jedi" /> She had a sister, [[Sar Labooda]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> As a [[Youngling|child]], she was held as a captive by space [[pirate]]s who [[Death|killed]] her [[parent]]s.<ref name="SWCEUE" /> However, she managed to send out an encrypted signal for help that was uncovered by [[Mace Windu]],<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> a member of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="The Secret Jedi" /> Decrypting the message and rescuing Billaba,<ref name="Odyssey" /> Windu brought her into the Order and ultimately took her as his [[Padawan]], receiving instruction in the ways of [[the Force]].<ref name="The Secret Jedi" />

During her training under Windu, Billaba studied as a [[Consular Jedi]] but was also allowed to receive instruction in the [[Form VII]] variant of lightsaber combat, ''Vaapad'', which Windu himself famously specialized in.<ref name="KoF">''[[Knights of Fate]]''</ref> She also accompanied her master to numerous locations, including [[Dallenor]] where he taught her usage of munitions such as smoke bombs.<ref name="Living Force">''[[The Living Force]]''</ref> Billaba eventually became a [[Jedi Knight]], renowned for her skills with a [[lightsaber]]<ref name="The Secret Jedi" />, and favored [[Form III]] when teaching other [[Jedi]].<ref name="Rise of the Old Masters">{{Rebels|Rise of the Old Masters}}</ref> Sometime after her training with Windu, Billaba ventured to her homeworld of Chalacta to explore her heritage, improving her perspective on Jedi philosophy. She would also tutor various initiates in meditation techniques, including [[Xaran Raal]] whom she grew close to.<ref name="Living Force" /> Billaba was also a [[Jedi Vanguard]].<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref>

===Jedi Master of the High Council===
====Meeting with Jinn====
By [[33 BBY|40 BBY]], Billaba had ascended to the [[Jedi High Council]], the governing body of the Jedi Order.<ref name="Master & Apprentice date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> Billaba was among the Jedi Masters present when the Council presented Qui-Gon Jinn retiring Master [[Poli Dapatian]]'s seat, believing they would benefit from Jinn's different perspective. She also joked when Jinn asked for time to consider, believing all prospective members should lest they didn't know what they're getting into.<ref name="Master & Apprentice">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> 

====Investigating the Riftwalkers====
In [[33 BBY]], after witnessing Xaran's knighting, she sent him on a mission to investigate piracy in [[the Slice]], particularly the [[Riftwalkers]], but was later grieved to learn of his murder. Determined to solve the case and understand the planet's situation, Billaba took on Xaran's former anti-piracy assignment, going undercover as the thief "Hotwire" and befriending the Riftwalker courier [[Kylah Lohmata]]. Billaba uncovered the Riftwalkers' leader, [[Zilastra]], who was involved in Xaran's death, the assassination of other pirates, and a plot to target the Jedi Council. Captured by Zilastra, Billaba learned the pirate's hatred of the Jedi stemmed from [[Sifo-Dyas]]' past recruitment violations. With Kylah's help, Billaba and her allies foiled Zilastra's plot, leading to the pirate's incarceration. Afterward, Kylah became a cadet on the ''Assurance'' commanded by [[Pell Baylo]].<ref name="Living Force" />

====Helping Mace Windu====
After several children were kidnapped by the [[warlord]] [[Guattako]] on [[Oosalon]], Windu was sent to find and rescue the missing children. Eventually, Windu was able to defeat all of [[Guattako's army]]. When he [[Lightsaber duel|dueled]] with Guattako, Guattako used his [[staff]] to knock Windu out. When Guattako was about to kill Windu, a child shot and killed Guattako. Shortly after, Billaba, [[Plo Koon]], and [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] arrived to help.<ref name="The Weapon">{{StoryCite|story=The Weapon|book=Age of Republic Special 1}}</ref>

====Invasion of Naboo====
[[File:High_Council_Chamber.png|thumb|left|350px|Billaba (foreground, right) and the Jedi Council test Skywalker for possible Jedi training.]]
During the [[Invasion of Naboo]], she was present in the [[Jedi Council Chamber|Council chambers]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] when Jinn told the story of how he [[Duel on Tatooine|encountered]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]] on [[Tatooine]], and how Jinn believed that Maul was a [[Sith Lord]]; Maul had been attempting to capture [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of [[Naboo]]. The Jedi Master also revealed he had found [[Anakin Skywalker]], a young [[Slavery|slave]] he had freed on the [[desert]] planet, and that he believed the boy was the [[Chosen One]] destined to destroy the [[Sith]] and bring balance to the Force. The Council was skeptical of both claims but nonetheless agreed to test the boy and investigate [[Darth]] Maul. Despite being impressed with Skywalker's abilities, the Council initially rejected Jinn's request that the boy be admitted into the Jedi Order, on the basis that Skywalker was too old to begin Jedi training. They instead ordered that Jinn and his apprentice, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], travel to Naboo to protect [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] Amidala, who was returning to her planet to fight back against the [[Trade Federation]] invasion.<ref name="Episode I" />

The Federation's invasion and occupation ended during a [[Battle of Naboo|battle]] to retake the planet. Maul mortally wounded Jinn in [[lightsaber combat]], before Kenobi gravely wounded the Sith [[Lord]]; Maul went into [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] and would not be heard from again for many years. Jinn's dying wish was that Kenobi train Skywalker as a Jedi, a wish that the Council agreed to. Billaba and other members of the Jedi Council traveled to Naboo and attended [[Funeral of Qui-Gon Jinn|Jinn's funeral]], before taking part in a [["Liberation Day" Parade|ceremony]] marking a newfound peace between the [[human]]s and [[Gungan]]s of Naboo; the Federation, as a common enemy, brought the once-feuding peoples together.<ref name="Episode I" />

====Disaster at Bromlarch====
In [[28 BBY]],<ref name="28 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of Queen's Shadow to [[28 BBY]].</ref> Master Billaba was the Jedi representative on the Republic's survey to the planet [[Bromlarch]] alongside Senators [[Mina Bonteri]], [[Rush Clovis]], and Amidala. They traveled with the planet's representative [[Caelor Gaans]] to analyze the damage to the aqueducts after a devastating [[groundquake]]. Billaba joined their ship for an [[orbit]]al review. When a [[Mission to Bromlarch|relief mission]] was finally sent, Billaba was part of the auditing team. The Jedi made sure to speak to Amidala, who she had met in the aftermath of the Invasion of Naboo four years earlier. Shortly afterward, Billaba flew a [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7 starfighter]] as an escort for a supply run, with the [[callsign]] ''[[Hazard Three]]''. She flew interference for the convoy and Amidala's starship against the attacking [[pirate]]s before they were able to escape.<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref>

===The Clone Wars===
====Outbreak====
Six years later, the [[Separatist Crisis|Secessionist Movement]] plunged the [[Galactic Republic]] into a [[Politics|political]] crisis, with thousands of [[star system]]s seceding from the Republic and joining the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]; the conflict was designed by [[Darth Sidious]], the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] publicly known as [[Supreme Chancellor]] Palpatine, as part of a [[Grand Plan|plot]] to destroy the Jedi Order and take over [[the galaxy]]. Billaba was present on the Jedi Council when the body ordered Kenobi to investigate the [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|plot]] to [[assassin]]ate now-[[Senator]] Amidala, an outspoken opponent of the [[Military Creation Act]] that would have allowed the Republic to raise an [[Grand Army of the Republic|army]] to combat the Separatists; Skywalker was further ordered to travel with Amidala to Naboo so she could be protected from harm. These events led Kenobi to discover a [[Cloning|clone]] [[army]] built for the Republic in secret on [[Kamino]] and the [[Galactic Senate]] to grant Chancellor Palpatine emergency executive power. The Chancellor enlisted the clones as the Grand Army of the Republic, and they were put to use in the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], which sparked the Clone Wars between the Republic and the Separatists&mdash;just as Sidious had intended.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>

Her sister, Labooda, was killed during the battle.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Billaba did not take part in the opening battle of the war, but did not escape the fighting. By the time of several [[Standard week|weeks]] into the war, she had seen action, which she spoke about with [[Sabé]], Amidala's former [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] who was disguised as her, at a meeting between the Jedi Order and members of the Senate at the Jedi Temple. Billaba was accompanied by [[Barriss Offee]], who had participated in the battle and seen firsthand how it was affecting the Jedi, especially the younger ones. Sabé shared her own experience with witnessing the horrors of war and Offee expressed that she wished she could do more. To this, Billaba advised her to be open to possibilities in the future.<ref name="Queen's Hope" />

Billaba and [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]] were present during Padawan [[Ahsoka Tano]]'s training test in the Jedi Temple's [[Jedi Temple dojo|dojo]]. After the test ended, Billaba and Dume spoke with each other while exiting the dojo.<ref name="Practice">{{TOTJ|Practice Makes Perfect (episode)}}</ref>

====Battle on Malenko====
At some point during the Clone Wars, Billaba participated in the [[battle on Malenko]], fighting alongside Windu and a detachment of clone troopers against Separatist droid forces led by General [[Kazameer]].<ref name="Ghosts of the Machine">{{StoryCite|story=Ghosts of the Machine|book=Tales from the Rancor Pit}}</ref>

====Hunt on Malathon IX====
[[File:OchiKillsDeez-CrimsonReign2.png|thumb|right|300px|Ochi of Bestoon, who hunted Billaba on Malathon IX.]]
At some point during the Clone Wars, Billaba embarked on a mission to the planet of [[Malathon IX]]. Windu accompanied her as far as the planet's village starport, before seemingly leaving her to her mission. For the next five [[Standard day|days]] Billaba conducted her mission on the planet. At the same time, an assassin named [[Ochi]] of [[Bestoon]] had been [[Hunt on Malathon IX|tracking]] her since her arrival, intent on claiming the Separatist [[bounty]] on her head. On the fifth day, Ochi finally prepared to make his move to kill her with his [[beskar]] deadblade. He watched as Billaba conversed with four [[clone trooper]]s before breaking off and heading close to the [[tree]] that Ochi lay hidden in. As Ochi finally sprung his ambush, he was unexpectedly interrupted and disarmed by Windu, who had been tracking Ochi the whole time he had been tracking her.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref>

====Attack on Mahranee====
Billaba was present at a Council meeting that viewed a live [[Hologram|holographic]] recording of the devastating Separatist [[Atrocity on Mahranee|attack]] on [[Mahranee]], the [[homeworld]] of the [[Mahran]] [[species]]. Following the attack, Windu convinced Billaba and the rest of the council on a secret mission to assassinate [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. At the suggestion of Kenobi, the Council recruited [[Quinlan Vos]], who was experienced in undercover work, and Dooku's former apprentice [[Asajj Ventress]] for this sensitive operation. Ventress agreed to assist Vos with the mission but insisted that the Jedi could only overcome Dooku by training in the ways of the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]].<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>

====Damaged goods====
{{Quote|My young strategist has been thinking again.|Billaba, to Caleb Dume, after the training session with Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="A New Dawn" />}}
[[File:DepaBillabaBactaTank-Kanan7.jpg|thumb|left|260px|Billaba in a coma at the Jedi Temple's medical center.]]
At some point, [[Jedi General|General]] Billaba and her [[troop]]s [[Battle of Haruun Kal|faced]] [[General]] [[Grievous]],<ref name="Kanan 9">[[Kanan 9|''Kanan'' 9]]</ref> a [[Kaleesh]] [[cyborg]] who was the [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander of the Droid Army]],<ref name="SWCEUE" /> on [[Haruun Kal]]. Billaba was<ref name="Kanan 9" /> brutally<ref name="The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> defeated by Grievous,<ref name="Kanan 9" /> who, unaware that she had survived,<ref name="Kanan 11" /> had nearly took her life.<ref name="Kanan 9" /> That day, the Jedi Master's [[Depa Billaba's battalion|battalion]] suffered ninety percent casualties. Billaba herself was badly injured, to the point that she fell in a [[coma]]. After six [[Standard month|months]] of constant care at the [[Jedi temple]]'s [[Medical bay|infirmary]], she came out of her coma, seeming to have made a full physical recovery. The senior members of the Jedi High Council feared that her mental and emotional state might still be too damaged. Consequently, Billaba underwent a full fitness assessment. When it appeared that she had indeed fully recovered, Billaba was allowed to resume her position in the High Council.<ref name="Kanan 7" />

Following her reinstatement, Billaba took an interest in Caleb Dume,<ref name="Kanan 7" /> the same boy she had met before.<ref name="Practice" /> His questioning nature caught her attention.<ref name="A New Dawn" /> She was impressed by Dume's questioning about how the Jedi Knights chose their Padawans when she and Windu were in the [[Jedi Archives]]. Billaba then instructed Dume, [[Sammo Quid]], and [[Tai Uzuma]] in Form III defensive poses.<ref name="Kanan 7" />

Billaba was present during a group training session in which Kenobi, now a Jedi Master and a member of the Jedi Council, told a group of younglings, including Dume, about a [[Jedi beacon|beacon]] in the [[Jedi Temple central security station|Jedi Temple's central security station]] that could call all Jedi back to [[Coruscant]] in [[time]]s of great crisis. Dume asked the Jedi Master if it could be used to warn Jedi away from Coruscant, a thought that Kenobi had never entertained, and that caused concern and commotion amongst the assembled younglings. Billaba attempted to stop Dume from his line of questioning, but Kenobi encouraged him and speculated that the beacon could indeed be used to warn Jedi away in times of unexpected peril. Billaba saw Dume, as she had in the past, as having a mind for strategy, and at the end of the session, Billaba was once again impressed by him.<ref name="A New Dawn" />

Despite her recovery and her reinstatement to the High Council, some of the clone [[trooper]]s started spreading the word that General Billaba was "damaged goods." This rumor reached Caleb Dume who decided to directly ask the Jedi Master. The two were discussing whether she was "damaged goods" despite her recovery, but their discussion was interrupted when they sensed [[Kage Warriors|something]] and saw [[thermal detonator]]s outside her quarters.<ref name="Kanan 7" />

====Jedi Temple Bombing====
{{Quote|It seems I am in desperate need of a Padawan in order to continue my journey.|Depa Billaba, to Caleb Dume|<ref name="Kanan 8">[[Kanan 8|''Kanan'' 8]]</ref>}}
[[File:BillabaDumeJediTempleRoof-Kanan8.png|thumb|right|320px|Billaba and Dume atop the Jedi Temple roof after stopping Rackham Sear.]]
Billaba then took Dume and fled her quarters when the [[Bombing at the Jedi Temple|bombs]] went off. Billaba wanted to hide and wait for information but went after Dume when he attempted to take on the bomber, CIS [[Captain]] [[Rackham Sear]]. Billaba saved Dume and prevented Sear from setting off his bombs. When Sear refused her offer to surrender, they fought until he jumped off the roof and threw bombs at her and Dume. She survived and decided to take Dume as her Padawan.<ref name="Kanan 8" />

She trained him in the ways of the Force, including lightsaber combat. Billaba valued Dume's strategic mind and encouraged him to put it to good use.<ref name="A New Dawn" /> At some point, Billaba brought Dume to one of the thousands of Jedi Temples in the galaxy in order to undergo a rite of passage in which he would need to face and overcome his greatest fears.<ref name="Path of the Jedi">{{Rebels|Path of the Jedi}}</ref> Three weeks after the Jedi temple bombing, Billaba took Dume to get a [[Kyber crystal]]<ref name="Kanan 8" /> and construct [[Kanan Jarrus' lightsaber|his lightsaber]].<ref name="Rebellion Begins">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref>

====Skirmish on Kardoa====
{{Quote|I'm ready for that briefing, Commander. Tell me about the men I'm now fortunate to command.|Depa Billaba|<ref name="Kanan 9" />}}
[[File:BillabaGreetsHaruunKalSurvivors-Kanan9.png|thumb|left|320px|Billaba greets her old comrades-in-arms.]]
By the end of the war, Billaba and Dume went to [[Kardoa]] to take command of a [[Depa Billaba's second battalion|battalion]] led by survivors from her last [[battalion]], [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Grey (clone trooper)|Grey]], [[Clone Captain|Captain]] [[Styles]],<ref name="Kanan 8" /> [[Clone Sergeant|Sergeant]] [[Soot]], and [[Corporal]] [[Big-Mouth]]. Their mission was to verify if there was a Separatist presence. Leading her battalion through some canyons, they were [[Skirmish on Kardoa|attacked]] by [[Coburn Sear]] and his [[droid]]s, whom Billaba managed to force a retreat, although Dume was injured.<ref name="Kanan 9" />

====Third Battle of Mygeeto====
{{Quote|You believe you know how to defeat me&hellip;<br />&hellip; But the Jedi you fought then did die&hellip;<br />&hellip; And the Jedi you face now is one you've never encountered before.|Depa Billaba, to General Grievous|<ref name="Kanan 11">[[Kanan 11|''Kanan'' 11]]</ref>}}
[[File:BillabaVsGrievous-Kanan11.png|thumb|right|320px|Billaba duels General Grievous on Mygeeto.]]
Billaba and her battalion were then [[Third Battle of Mygeeto|sent]] to [[Mygeeto]] to clear the southern [[mesa]]s of [[battle droid]]s. Billaba, Styles, Dume, and [[Stance]] were outnumbered but were able to clear the mesas. They became surrounded during another attack, and Billaba called Grey for an extraction. She and the others fought the droids as they came, but there were too many. They were then saved when [[Skull Leader]] [[Fenn Rau]] and [[Skull Squadron]] bought them more time and were evacuated, but Billaba never got to meet or thank Fenn Rau in person to thank him for saving their lives.<ref name="Kanan 10">[[Kanan 10|''Kanan'' 10]]</ref>

Billaba returned to the base camp. However, the camp came under attack by battle droids led by [[Colonel]] Sear and General Grievous.<ref name="Kanan 10" /> Billaba fought Grievous, cutting off two of his hands. However, Grievous was able to wound her. Despite that, she continued fighting until Dume, Styles, and Grey came to aid her. Grievous and his forces retreated. Billaba was placed in a bacta tank to heal from her wounds sustained in the fight.<ref name="Kanan 11" />

====Conquest of Kaller====
{{Quote|Captain Styles, have your men take the left flank.''"<br />"''Yes, General!''"<br />"''Commander Grey, the right.''"<br />"''Roger that.''"<br />"''Time to end this. All forces attack!|Depa Billaba leads her troops in the final rush of the battle|<ref name="Kanan 1" />}}
[[File:CalebDumeDepaBillabaAmidRemains-Kanan1.png|thumb|left|320px|Billaba stands by Dume and declares the battle for Kaller a Republic victory.]]
Billaba, alongside her apprentice Dume, Grey, and Styles [[Battle of Kaller|fought]] against the [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]] of General [[Kleeve]] on [[Kaller]]. Billaba and her [[Republic Military|forces]] were able to rout Kleeve's forces and forced his retreat. Billaba then spoke with [[Gamut Key]], assuring him that his village would no longer be forced into giving them supplies and labor. Billaba and her troops billeted outside of Key's village.<ref name="Kanan 1" />

That night, Billaba took Dume out for a training session, lamenting that she had been neglecting him. After working with Form III until after sunset, Billaba presented her Padawan with a [[Kanan Jarrus' holocron|holocron]], telling him to use it to explore and satisfy his inquisitiveness. They returned to the camp and relaxed at a fire with Grey, Styles, and the men. They discussed the role of questions in regard to orders and to the Jedi Order, at which Billaba gave Dume a [[Jedi holocron|holocron]]. He was to use it to "study the role of questions" and "peaceful dissent" in the Order.<ref name="Kanan 1" />

In the aftermath of the pivotal [[Battle of Coruscant]], General Grievous and the Separatists launched a massive offensive across several star systems, including on Kaller. Billaba and her men became pinned down by [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tanks]] outside of the snowy woodlands as Dume left to contact reinforcements. Billaba and her men were saved by [[Clone Force 99]]. As their leader, Clone Sergeant [[Hunter]] recommended they launch a counter-attack, to which Billaba agreed and gathered her men to move out, allowing Dume to proceed on ahead with Hunter and his squad.<ref name="TBB Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref>

==== Order 66 — Fall of the Republic ====
{{Quote|You must run! Run, Caleb!|Billaba's last words to Dume|<ref name="TBB Aftermath" />}}
[[File:Billaba_Order_66.png|thumb|right|320px|Billaba's own battalion targets the Jedi Master.]]
It was moments later after this that, at the culmination of his plan, Sidious issued [[Order 66]], a command to all clone troopers, including Grey,<ref name="Kanan 1" /> triggering their [[behavioral modification biochip]]s to kill the Jedi as traitors. As Billaba's men formed up, they began to surround her and fire on her. Billaba became overwhelmed, and as her Padawan realized what was happening and turned back to help her, she instead told Dume to run. Billaba bravely stood her ground as Dume looked on in horror, and as she lost her lightsaber when one of the troopers shot it out of her hands, she was quickly killed. Dume fled into the woods, unaware that most of Clone Force 99 were just as confused as to what was transpiring as he was. Billaba's men then began their pursuit of Dume.<ref name="TBB Aftermath" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|What exactly am I looking for?''"''<br />''"''Nothing, and everything.''"''<br />''"''That doesn't help.''"''<br />''"''I know, but it's what my master told me.|Ezra Bridger and Kanan Jarrus, during Bridger's tests in the Jedi Temple on Lothal|<ref name="Path of the Jedi" />}}
Because of Billaba's sacrifice, Dume was able to escape the clone troopers. Sidious [[Proclamation of the New Order|declared]] himself [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] and transformed the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]], with the Jedi branded as enemies of the state.<ref name="TBB Aftermath" /> After watching a message from Master Kenobi&mdash;a message broadcast over the Jedi beacon, warning Jedi to stay away from Coruscant just as Dume had predicted,<ref name="Kanan 2">[[Kanan 2|''Kanan'' 2]]</ref> Dume was captured by Comm. Grey. Dume pleaded to Grey and the other clones to remember their service to Billaba, though they did not budge. However, after Dume broke free, Grey began to question why he and the clones blindly obeyed Order 66, which ultimately led to him [[Rescue of Caleb Dume|sacrificing himself and saving Dume]].<ref name="Kanan 5">[[Kanan 5|''Kanan'' 5]]</ref>

Dume ultimately went into hiding and changed his name to Kanan Jarrus, keeping his identity as a Jedi secret so as to avoid execution and abandoning the Jedi path for many years. Jarrus eventually joined [[Hera Syndulla]], a [[Twi'lek]] resistance fighter,<ref name="A New Dawn" /> and formed a [[Spectres|rebel cell]] that operated on and around the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet of [[Lothal]].<ref name="Spark of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref> He remained haunted by Billaba's final words, knowing that he ran away while she gave her life.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref>

Shortly after the [[Battle of Fortress Vader]], Darth Vader entered a [[portal]] constructed by [[Darth Momin]] to resurrect his wife Padmé Amidala. While walking through visions, he encountered Billaba along with many Jedi with their lightsabers ready to battle him. However, Vader killed all the illusions, slashing Billaba's throat as the words "[[Ben Solo|Let the past die]]" resounded in his mind.<ref name="Dark Lord 25">[[Darth Vader (2017) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 25]]</ref>

[[File:Master_and_Apprentice.png|thumb|left|300px|Jarrus became a rebel leader and took a Padawan of his own: Ezra Bridger.]]
During their rebellion on Lothal, Jarrus took on his own Padawan, [[Ezra Bridger]], who helped him reconnect with the Jedi path.<ref name="Spark of Rebellion" /> Some of Jarrus' own teachings were recollections of what Billaba had taught him, such as when Jarrus brought his student to a hidden [[Lothal Jedi Temple|Jedi Temple]] on Lothal in order to face and overcome his fears. Jarrus himself also had to face his own fear of being an inadequate [[teacher]], due to his training under Master Billaba having been incomplete by the time the Clone Wars ended.<ref name="Path of the Jedi" />

Her death left Jarrus with a bitter mistrust towards clone troopers, which Jarrus explained to Bridger after meeting Captain Rex and two other clone [[soldier]]s, telling him how she had been gunned down by the same men that had fought beside her for years.<ref name="The Lost Commanders">{{Rebels|The Lost Commanders}}</ref>

The records of the Jedi Temple, which included information about Billaba, also remained after the [[Great Jedi Purge|fall]] of the Jedi Order,<ref name="Rise of the Old Masters" /> and after the Temple was renovated into the Imperial Palace.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> [[The Grand Inquisitor]], a [[dark side adept]] tasked with [[hunting]] down and eliminating Jedi who had survived the Clone Wars, was aware of Billaba from the Temple records. During a [[Duel on Stygeon Prime|confrontation]] with Jarrus, the [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitor]] correctly deduced that Jarrus had been trained by Billaba due to his preference for Form III.<ref name="Rise of the Old Masters" />

Eventually, in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Jarrus was [[Rescue of Hera Syndulla (Lothal)|killed]] when he saved his friends from an explosion caused by [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]]'s [[walker]], being engulfed by the flames after the Spectres escaped to safety.<ref name="Jedi Night">{{Rebels|Jedi Night}}</ref> Through his sacrifice, the Specters and the other Lothal rebels were able to continue to fight on, and soon afterward [[Liberation of Lothal|liberate]] Lothal from the Empire.<ref name="Family Reunion – and Farewell">{{Rebels|Family Reunion – and Farewell}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
===Physical appearance===
Depa Billaba was a human female with [[Color|tan]] skin, and she wore jewels on her forehead and brow.<ref name="The Secret Jedi" /> She also bore the [[Chalactan]] marks of Illumination.<ref name="SWCEUE" /> She wore the simple, traditional garb of the Jedi Order, including a brown [[robe]] and a light tan tunic.<ref name="Episode I" />

===Wise and spiritual Jedi===
{{Quote|You're lucky I'm not my Master. She'd never let you get away with&mdash;''"<br />"''All the things you tried to get away with?|Kanan Jarrus and Ezra Bridger|<ref name="Path of the Jedi" />}}
[[File:DepaBillaba-WotG.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Depa Billaba was a powerful warrior.]]
She was considered a wise and spiritual Jedi<ref name="The Secret Jedi" /> who possessed a glib sense of humor.<ref name="Kanan 7" /> Her tutelage under Windu and her knowledge of Chalactan philosophy granted her both emotional control and the ability to let go of attachments with the belief that emotions are both valuable and not to be suppressed but erred against letting them overtake one in preserving them.<ref name="Living Force" /><ref name="Kanan 1" /> Jarrus recalled her as as being a stricter teacher than he was<ref name="Path of the Jedi" />but was not overly serious.<ref name="Kanan 1" />

During her time as a general, she believed that the Jedi Order made a crucial error in taking [[Military rank|military titles]] in the Clone Wars, believing that was not a matter of leadership, but of role and rank.<ref name="Kanan 1" /> Billaba was especially aware of the irony that a noble, peace-loving order like the Jedi were the military leaders in a galaxy-wide conflict<ref name="Topps women">{{Topps|set=2020 Topps Women of Star Wars|cardname=#19: Depa Billaba|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/216003/cid/14374512/2020-Topps-Women-of-Star-Wars-19-Depa-Billaba|nobackup=1}}</ref> and contemplated the will of the Force as the galaxy further slid into turmoil and war.<ref name="Databank" />

===Personal relationships===
{{Quote|And I'm the whole reason she is in danger at all. She begged us not to let her be taken away. I owe her. Even if I didn't already owe her my life, I owe her.''"<br />"''I know. And we're doing everything we can to find her. But that's not our only responsibility. Kwenn is important to the whole region. The larger picture&mdash;''"<br />"''The larger picture called for you to bombard the Riftwalker base, not come looking for me. But you did.|Mace and Depa discussing rescuing Kylah Lomata or foiling the impeding assassination attempt on Kwenn|<ref name="Living Force" />}}
One of her significant bonds were with her former master. When she was a [[Padawan]], she developed a close bond with Mace Windu and her master regarded her highly, considering her ascension to the Council among the most proudest moments in his Jedi career. He often protected her from both emotional<ref name="Living Force" /> and physical dangers<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> Caring deeply for him in return, Billaba wasn't above reminding her former master of her own independence, status, and confidence in her personal decisions in the wake of moments of grief and pain.<ref name="Living Force" />

Billaba was also close to [[Xaran Raal]], an initiate Billaba tutored. Windu once believed was a prospective candidate for serving as a Padawan under her and noted their closeness. Following his death shortly after becoming a Jedi Knight, she admitted she took his anti-piracy assignment in part of wanting to solve his murder. While she grieved and was sadden by his death, following bringing his killer to justice, the ordeal made her more open to the idea of having a Padawan.<ref name="Living Force" />

[[File:CalebDumeDepaBillabaTraining-Kanan1.png|thumb|Depa trained Caleb Dume, the future Kanan Jarrus and one of the few Jedi rebels during the reign of the Galactic Empire.]]
Eventually, Billaba would take in [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]] (later known as Kanan Jarrus) as her apprentice.<ref name="Kanan 8" /> As his master, she imparted what teachings she could onto Caleb Dume before she was killed during the fall of the Jedi Order. Jarrus survived the ensuing Jedi purge and eventually became a rebel leader, using the strategic mind that Billaba helped cultivate in order to [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|fight against]] the Galactic Empire.<ref name="A New Dawn" /> He also remembered many of her teachings and passed them on to Ezra Bridger when he took the boy as his own Padawan.<ref name="Path of the Jedi" /> One such lesson was Billaba's belief that Jedi had to be willing to sacrifice themselves for the sake of others (which she herself carried out with her death), a lesson Jarrus himself did not fully understand until he allowed himself to be [[Raid on the communications tower|captured]] by the Empire on Lothal so the other rebels could escape.<ref name="Call to Action">{{Rebels|Call to Action}}</ref> Prior to her death, she sometimes gently teased him about his habit of asking many questions<ref name="Kanan 1" /> but at times of mortal danger, she was greatly protective of him.<ref name="Kanan 10" />

Billaba bonded with [[Kylah Lohmata]], a twelve year old orphan courier of the Riftwalkers gang whom she met while undercover as "Hotwire." Acting as her bodyguard, Billaba was disturbed of her lifestyle and particular of her relationship with Zilastra, a pirate leader who was Kylah's surrogate parent. Even undercover and withholding her status as a Jedi, she took Kylah truths of her life regarding Chalacta. While her revelation as a Jedi stained their bond for atime, Kylah helped saved Billaba once realizing Zilastra would use her to achieve vengeance on the Jedi Council, even at the expense of her life. After stopping Zilastra, with Windu's help, she arranged for Kylah to become a cadet on the ''Assurance'' for a stable lifestyle with peers her own age but promised to help her should things not work out.<ref name="Living Force" />

During the Clone Wars, her personal nemesis was General Grievous; however, by the time of the Third Battle of Mygeeto she had been able to learn to live with regret and was fine that Grievous escaped at the end of the battle.<ref name="Kanan 11" />

==Powers and abilities==
{{Quote|The Temple records are quite complete. In close quarter fighting, Billaba's emphasis was always on Form III, which you favor to a ridiculous degree.|The Inquisitor to Kanan Jarrus, during their first confrontation|<ref name="Rise of the Old Masters" />}}
[[File:DepaBillaba-BaseSeries3.png|thumb|right|220px|Depa Billaba was a formidable Jedi Master]]
A member of the [[Jedi High Council]] and [[Jedi Master]], Depa Billaba was considered exceptional in [[lightsaber combat]]<ref name="Lightsaber Collection" />; Billaba was most notable for being proficient in Form III, being her preference in close quarters combat and was a rare master of the Vaapad variant of [[Form VII]], in which was taught to her by Jedi Master Mace Windu.<ref name="Force Collection">{{ForceCollection|Depa Billaba|star=3}}</ref> Her dueling skills enabled her to hold her own against General Grievous on separate occasions, though both times she was ultimately bested by the cyborg.<ref name="Kanan 11" />

Billaba was also known for possessing a deep connection to the [[The Force|Force]]<ref name=":0">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi]]''</ref>, skilled in techniques such as [[telekinesis]]<ref name="Kanan 7" /><ref name="Kanan 11" /> in which her control was specific enough to alt a small bomb from going off by preventing chemicals inside the bomb mixing with one another.<ref name="Kanan 8" /> Other Force abilities she could perform include blocking laser shots with the Force,<ref name="Kanan 2" /> see through the Force without the usage of her eyes,<ref name="Kanan 7" /> and possessed acute senses that could alert her to [[Disturbance in the Force|disturbances in the Force]].<ref name="Kanan 2" />

During her lifetime, she studied as a [[Consular Jedi]]<ref name="Force Collection" /> but learned numerous skills for field work in the galaxy, including armed<ref name="Clone Wars Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> and unarmed combat<ref name="Living Force" /><ref name="Clone Wars Character Encyclopedia" />, [[starfighter]] piloting,<ref name="Clone Wars Character Encyclopedia" /> [[Thief|thievery]], [[Slicer|slicing]], and was a skilled in adopting disguises. These abilities helped her adopted various roles during undercover assignments, some which included bodyguards, thief-for-hire, or a hired driver.<ref name="Living Force" /> Despite her personal doubts and defeats, she was also considered a capable military strategist and leader.<ref name="Kanan 1" />

==Equipment==
[[File:DepaBillaba-JSW.png|thumb|200px|left|Billaba's wielded a lightsaber that could emit a blue or green blade.]]
A highly-trained combatant taught by legendary Jedi Master [[Mace Windu]], Billaba wielded a [[lightsaber]] that emitted a [[Color|green]]<ref name="Kanan 9" /> or blue blade during her lifetime as a Jedi in which consisted of solid metal cylinder ending in a tapered pommel and a multi-ringed emitter, the latter of which was enveloped by a diamond-shaped metal segment that wrapped entirely around the hilt.<ref name="TLC2">''[[Star Wars: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> Billaba often wore a [[Jedi apparel|standard brown Jedi robe]] that concealed a fighting tunic underneath<ref name="Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition]]''</ref>, which contained a [[belt]] and pouches for common Jedi gear such as a [[Comlink|commlink]], [[grappling hook]]s, [[holoprojector]], and a [[rebreather]].<ref name=":0" />

==Behind the scenes==
Depa Billaba first appeared in the in the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], portayed by actress [[Dipika O'Neill Joti]].<ref name="Episode I" /> She originated as a Jedi Council member from one of the earliest character sketches done by the film's art department and had a spiritually inspired design before it underwent multiple revisions before the final version..<ref name="DB-Billaba, Depa">{{DB|character|depabillaba|Billaba, Depa}}</ref>

Archive footage of the council scenes featuring Billaba was reused in the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] and Joti received a credit although she shot no new footage. When casting the humanoid Jedi fighters for the arena scene, director and ''[[Star Wars]]'' creator [[George Lucas]] wanted to maintain visual continuity with Jedi characters scene in ''The Phantom Menace''. As such Billaba's Mark of Illumination and haircut were reused for one of the character, but as the scenes were filmed in [[Fox Studios, Sydney]], [[Wikipedia:Australia|Australia]], she was portayed by a different local actress. However, the "new" Depa looked dissimilar enough to warrant the creation of a new character named Sar Labooda. A similar incident also happened with [[Agen Kolar]] and [[Stass Allie]], originally intended to be [[Eeth Koth]] and [[Adi Gallia]], respectively.<ref name="Insider67">{{InsiderCite|67|Ask Lobot|Star Wars Q&A}}</ref>

[[File:Depa_Billaba_TCW.png|thumb|right|250px|Concept art of Billaba for ''The Clone Wars'' "Crystal Crisis on Utapau" arc]]
The character was slated to appear in the "Crystal Crisis on Utapau" arc in the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' before it's cancellation in [[2013]].<ref name="Crystal Crisis Concept Art">{{SW|url=tv-shows/crystal-crisis-on-utapau-concept-art-gallery|text=Crystal Crisis on Utapau Concept Art Gallery|nolive=1}}</ref> She was created for the series as the production team wanted to have featured all the Council members throughout the series.<ref name="UntoldTCW">{{SWYouTube|7yKM6RExLlI|The Untold Clone Wars Panel &#124; Star Wars Celebration Anaheim}}</ref> Her inclusion was first revealed in concept art released on [[StarWars.com]] as part of [[The Clone Wars Legacy|''The Clone Wars'' Legacy]] project in September [[2014]]. Her model was designed by [[Pat Presley]] for the episode "601" and was finished on [[March 23]], [[2012]]. Her body was sourced from the model of Adi Gallia "with minimal geometry changes and a new texture".In this concept art, she was mislabeled as "Deepa Billaba."<ref name="Crystal Crisis Concept Art" /> Ultimately the character did not appear in the episodes, which were released in story reel form on StarWars.com.<ref name="Utapau1">{{TCW|A Death on Utapau}}</ref><ref name="Utapau2">{{TCW|In Search of the Crystal}}</ref><ref name="Utapau3">{{TCW|Crystal Crisis}}</ref><ref name="Utapau4">{{TCW|The Big Bang}}</ref> Billaba later appeared via hologram in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Dark Disciple]]'', which was based on unproduced episodes of ''The Clone Wars'' for the seventh and eight seasons of the series.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

Despite effectively being a background character, with no lines in either ''The Phantom Menace'' or ''Attack of the Clones'', Billaba was the focus of a number of now-non-[[canon]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] stories. These stories included the [[2004]] novel [[Shatterpoint (novel)|''Shatterpoint'']] by [[Matthew Stover]], which revealed she was the former Padawan of Mace Windu and chronicled her fall to the dark side of the Force.<ref name="Shatterpoint">[[Shatterpoint (novel)|''Shatterpoint'']]</ref> Some elements of her backstory, such as her culture, homeworld, and tutelage under Mace Windu, have since become part of the official canon.<ref name="The Secret Jedi" /> She was first depicted in the new stories of the official ''Star Wars'' canon in the [[2014]] novel [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']] by [[John Jackson Miller]], in which it was revealed she was the master of Kanan Jarrus (or Caleb Dume as a Padawan), one of the main characters of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''.<ref name="A New Dawn" /> She has since been mentioned in a number of the show's episodes<ref name="Rise of the Old Masters" /><ref name="Path of the Jedi" /> and has appeared in the prequel comic series ''[[Star Wars: Kanan]]'' written by ''Rebels''{{'s}} executive producer [[Greg Weisman]]<ref name="Aftermath EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/aftermath-episode-guide|text="Aftermath" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}}</ref> and published in 2015.<ref name="Kanan 1" />

She later appeared as cameos in the episode "[[Old Friends Not Forgotten]]" of ''The Clone Wars''{{'s}} revived [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|seventh season]] in [[2020]]<ref name="OldFriendsNF">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref> and the [[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (television series)||''Tales of the Jedi'']] in [[2022]]<ref name="Choices">{{TOTJ|Choices}}</ref><ref name="Practice" /> and had a major role in the premier episode "[[Aftermath (episode)|Aftermath]]" of ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'',<ref name="TBB Aftermath" /> relased on [[May 4]], [[2021]],<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-may-2021|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in May 2021|nolive=1}}</ref> where she was voiced by actress [[Archie Panjabi]].<ref name="TBB Aftermath" />

===Continuity===
There had been some confusion as to what Billaba's species was. According to the [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]], Billaba was a "Chalactan."<ref name="Databank" /> But according to the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]'', her species was "Human."<ref name="SWCEUE" /> [[Pablo Hidalgo]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] clarified in a tweet that "Chalactan is a culture and heritage. She is biologically human."<ref name="TweetPablo">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/717436495288619008|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2016-04-06|quote=Chalactan is a culture and heritage. She is biologically human.|archivefile=File:Tweet_pablohidalgo_DepaBillabaChalactanHuman.jpg}}</ref>

In the ''Star Wars: Kanan'' comic series, Billaba wields a green lightsaber<ref name="Kanan 1" /> as was the case in the Legends continuity.<ref name="Shatterpoint" /> However the model of the weapon created for ''The Clone Wars'' by [[Amy Beth Christenson]] in 2012 and used in ''The Bad Batch'' showed it with a blue blade<ref name="Aftermath EG" /> and the 2020 reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Lightsaber Collection]]'' also shows the blade being blue.<ref name="Lightsaber Collection">''[[Star Wars: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> Her lightsaber blade was later once again green in a variant cover for [[Legacy of Vader 9|''Legacy of Vader'' 9]] in [[2025]].<ref name="Legacy of Vader 9">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/130738/star_wars_legacy_of_vader_2025_9_variant/variant|text=Star Wars: Legacy of Vader (2025) #9 (Variant)}}</ref>

Order 66 is also portrayed differently in both the comic and the episode.<ref name="Kanan 1" /><ref name="TBB Aftermath" /> Within [[Kanan 1|''Kanan'' 1]], Order 66 is issued at night on Kaller, directly after Dume is gifted his holocron by his master, after the Republic routed Kleeve's forces.<ref name="Kanan 1" /> In [[Kanan 2|''Kanan'' 2]], Dume and his master fight their former friends as the clones succumb to Order 66. The battle ends with Dume fleeing into the night after his master distracts their fire, being hunted by Rostu Squad.<ref name="Kanan 2" /> Whereas in "Aftermath," Billaba was betrayed in the daylight promptly after a battle where she and her men were aided by the Bad Batch.<ref name="TBB Aftermath" /> "Aftermath"'s Episode Guide on StarWars.com states that the depiction in the episode "takes some cues from the comic"<ref name="Aftermath EG" /> and Pablo Hidalgo noted that the broad circumstances remained the same between both,<ref name="Pablo Tweet One">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1390672907320258564|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2021-05-07|quote=If you want, one way to square this circle is the history textbook version of events "persons X and Y where on planet B when A occurred" is the canon; a fictional expression of it is potentially dramatized and embellished for its medium. Your space mileage may vary.|archivefile=File:Pablo-Tweet-About-Canon.png}}</ref> as often occurred in multimedia adapations.<ref name="Pablo Tweet Two">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1390673238376677382|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2021-05-07|quote=(This allows for the inevitable variations that must occur in transmedia adaptations of stories across formats.) okay, enough scholarship. It's early.|archivefile=File:Pablo-Tweet-About-Canon-2.png}}</ref><ref name="Pablo Tweet Three">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1390673817182167041|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]date=2021-05-07||quote=Did a talk about this at the Seattle Film Summit a few years back showing how different various versions of Luke destroying the Death Star are across retellings, yet where they agree is what we accept happened. In line with this<ref name="Mace Windu and Others">{{EncyclopediaCite|Mace Windu and Other Users of the Force}}</ref> and keeping in mind Hidalgo's commentary,<ref name="Pablo Tweet Three" /> this article takes the events of the comic up until Order 66 as canonical, then assumes the more recent ''The Bad Batch'' depiction of Order 66 on Kaller is canonical.
