'''Ayaamii Sledge''' is an actress who portrayed young [[Reva Sevander]] (credited as "[[Jedi Initiate|Jedi Youngling]]") in the [[2022]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]''.<ref>{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref> She is best known for playing young [[W:c:truthbetold:Poppy Parnell|Poppy Parnell]] in the drama streaming series [[Wikipedia:Truth Be Told (2019 TV series)|''Truth Be Told'']].
