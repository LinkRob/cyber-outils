The '''E-5 blaster rifle''', also known as '''Droid blaster''',<ref name="Holocrons bts">{{SW|url=episodes/the-holocrons-of-fate-behind-the-scenes-gallery|text="The Holocrons of Fate" Behind-The-Scenes Gallery}}</ref> was a bulky [[blaster rifle]]<ref name="QH">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref> manufactured by [[Baktoid Armor Workshop]]. It was reverse-engineered from a [[BlasTech Industries|BlasTech]] design<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> of the [[E-series]].<ref name="Blasters/Ranged">{{EncyclopediaCite|Blasters and Ranged Weapons}}</ref> The E-5<ref name="Episode I" /> visually resembled a blaster rifle<ref name="Art of High Republic Phase 1">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume I]]''</ref> nicknamed "[[Beti (rifle)|Beti]]" from the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="OotS">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref> The [[E-5C heavy blaster rifle]] was identical to the default E-5 blaster rifle, save for an added wooden [[stock]] and modified [[barrel]].<ref name="BFII">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

==History==
===The Clone Wars===
[[File:B1BattleDroid-SWBC13cvr.png|thumb|left|250px|The E-5 was the primary weapon of the B1-series battle droid.]]
The E-5 was created<ref name="Blasters/Ranged" /> and adapted from the BlasTech design to be used by [[B1-series battle droid]]s.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> [[Bounty hunter]] [[C-21 Highsinger]] wielded an E-5 while [[Skirmish on Jaloria|attacking]] fellow hunters [[Jango Fett]] and [[Aurra Sing]].<ref name="Jango Fett 4">[[Jango Fett 4|''Jango Fett'' 4]]</ref> It was also used by [[BX-series droid commando]]s. It was used throughout the [[Clone Wars]], making it the most common weapon of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]]. The [[blaster]] generated excessive heat, which was caused by its rapid-fire mode, making it uncomfortable for [[Species|organics]] to use but more than adequate for a [[battle droid]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

Several [[IG-86 sentinel droid]]s during the Clone Wars wielded E-5 blasters.<ref name="Downfall of a Droid">{{TCW|Downfall of a Droid}}</ref> A number of [[Nikto]] guards employed by the [[Hutt Clan]] wielded E-5 blasters. Many such guards were active in [[Gardulla Besadii the Elder|Gardulla the Hutt's]] [[Gardulla the Hutt's Palace|Palace]] on the [[Hutt]] [[capital]] world of [[Nal Hutta]], where they wielded their E-5s in defense of the palace and the [[Grand Hutt Council]]'s operations.<ref name="Eminence" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> those Nikto guards used their E-5 in combat against the [[Mission to Nal Hutta|invading]] [[Shadow Collective]] once the rogue [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] threatened the lives of the council, only for their defense to fail. After taking Gardulla's Palace, the Shadow Collective [[Attack on Jabba's Palace|attacked]] [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]], where another such E-5 equipped Nikto and two E-5-equipped [[Quarren]] were present.<ref name="Eminence" /> Following the Clone Wars, a [[Imperial Police Droid|police droid model]]<ref name="Decommissioned">{{TBB|Decommissioned}}</ref> that resembled the BX-series [[commando]] [[droid]]<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref> also used the E-5. These police droids wielded E-5s while patrolling a [[decommissioning facility]] in [[Coronet City]] on the [[Planet|world]] of [[Corellia]]. Additionally, many E-5s and their B1 owners were set to be melted down in the facility, although a number of the droids were reactivated during an [[Mission to a droid decommissioning facility|infiltration]]. Many reactivated B1s again wielded E-5 blaster rifles, using them to fire upon the police droids.<ref name="Decommissioned" />

===Imperial Era and beyond===
[[File:Databank-NEDB.png|thumb|right|300px|NED-B wields a modified E-5 blaster rifle in the defense of Jabiim.]]
[[NED-B]] used a modified<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> E-5 against [[stormtrooper]]s during the Imperial [[attack on Jabiim]].<ref name="Kenobi V">{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref> Maul made use of three reprogrammed [[RIC-1200]] droids who each wielded an E-5 blaster rifle.<ref name="The Holocrons of Fate">{{Rebels|The Holocrons of Fate}}</ref>

[[Baze Malbus]] wielded a personally modified<ref name="Whills">[[Guardians of the Whills (novel)|''Guardians of the Whills'']]</ref> E-5 blaster<ref name="Whills Manga">''[[Guardians of the Whills: The Manga]]''</ref> identified as an [[E-5 carbine]]<ref name="Whills" /> during his opposition to the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Jedha insurgency|occupation of Jedha]]. He repeatedly needed to fix the blaster until it finally was too broken for further use, which his friend [[Chirrut Îmwe]] considered a shame. Malbus soon replaced the E-5 with a [[Baze's repeating cannon|repeating cannon]].<ref name="Whills Manga" />

The [[HK-87 assassin droid]]s working under [[Morgan Elsbeth]] used these rifles. One was taken by [[Sabine Wren]] when these droids broke in to steal the [[star map]].<ref name="Part One AD">{{Ahsoka|Part One: Master and Apprentice}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> 

The [[Droid Gotra]]'s [[Sentry droid]]s used an E-5 blaster rifle as one of their blaster rifle models. While escaping, [[Din Djarin]] saw he couldn't fit in the [[Anzellan Starship]] and decided to hold the forces off, stealing an E-5 rifle from a [[B-1 series mercenary sentry droid]] and used it to fight off the other sentry droids like the [[B-2 series mercenary sentry droid]]s and the [[mercenary sentry droid]]s when the [[The Twins|Hutt Twins]] sent their makeshift droid army after him, before succumbing to his [[Dragonsnake]] [[poison]].<ref name="The Mandalorian and Grogu" />

During the [[Cold War]], the [[Warbird gang]] continued to use these blaster rifles, although these were more rugged with a longer barrel.<ref name="Synara's Score">{{Resistance|Synara's Score}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The E-5 blaster rifle first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']] as the weapon that equipped the Trade Federation B1-series battle droids. Originally, as seen in animatics for ''[[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|Star War: Attack of the Clones]]'', the [[B2-series super battle droid]]s were to dual wield E-5 blaster rifles instead of having a built-in [[wrist blaster]].<ref name="BlurayScene">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]'', Disc 7, Star Wars Archives: Episode II Deleted Scene: Raid on the Droid Control Ship and Extended Arena Fight</ref>

''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'' mistakenly refers to the E-5 as a [[blaster pistol]].<ref name="Dawn" />
