'''381 BBY''', or '''381<ref name="DantooineDate" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> During the year,<ref name="TogrutaDate" /> tensions between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Togruta]] [[species]] spawned in the aftermath of the [[Republic-Togruta conflict]] existed.<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> Around that same year,<ref name="DantooineDate" /> [[Marda Ro]]&mdash;the [[Evereni]]<ref name="Closed Fist" /> founder of a group of [[pirate]]s who would eventually become the [[Nihil]]<ref name="GrattonInterview">{{YouTube|video=llnvoEG0CjE|text=Interview with Tessa Gratton: "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" (TALES OF LIGHT & LIFE)|channel=@FriendsOfTheForce|name=[[Friends of the Force]]|date=2023-10-06}}</ref>&mdash;began<ref name="Closed Fist" /> her<ref name="GrattonInterview" /> [[Marda Ro's hunt|search]] for others of her species by visiting the [[planet]] [[Dantooine]], where she ended up recruiting the [[Twi'lek]] [[Fori Nagor]] into her group of marauders.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

==Behind the scenes==
381 BBY was first indirectly mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]'', a reference book written by [[Cole Horton]]<ref name="Chronicles" /> and released on [[February 28]], [[2023]],<ref name="Amazon UK">{{TitanBooks|url=71311-star-wars-the-high-republic-chronicles-of-the-jedi-an-illustrated-guide-to-the-galaxys-golden-age|text=Star Wars: The High Republic – Chronicles of the Jedi: An Illustrated Guide to the Galaxy's Golden Age}}</ref> as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Chronicles" /> It later appeared in a flashback in the short story "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]," written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in the young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]''<ref name="Closed Fist" /> released on [[September 5]], 2023,<ref name="Amazon">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/727523/star-wars-the-high-republic-tales-of-light-and-life-by-lucasfilm-press|text=Star Wars: The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life}}</ref> also as a part of the multimedia project.<ref name="Closed Fist" />
