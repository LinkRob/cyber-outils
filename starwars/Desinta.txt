'''Desinta''' was an ice [[planet]]<ref name="Resolve" /> in the [[Morshdine sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Sometime between the [[Standard year|years]] [[42 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Resolve (short story)|"Resolve"]] is set after [[Dooku]]'s departure from the [[Jedi Order]], dated to 42 years before the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] by ''[[The Star Wars Book]]'', which corresponds to [[42 BBY]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system|dating system]] used by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. The story is also set prior to [[Qui-Gon Jinn]]'s [[death]] in the [[Battle of Naboo]], which is dated to [[32 BBY]] by ''Galactic Atlas''.</ref> Desinta was embroiled in a [[Desintian civil war|civil war]] between the [[Desintian government]], led by the nominally elected [[Prefect]] [[Aminar]], and multiple other factions, including the [[Dan'Gar]]. During that [[time]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Lizel Liit]], who had run away from the [[Jedi Order]] while having doubts about her future, was sold to the highest bidder on Desinta by the criminal [[Zarah Bliss]], and [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] was sent to retrieve her.<ref name="Resolve" /> In [[4 ABY]], the planet was the site of an [[Attempt to assassinate Mon Mothma|attempt to assassinate]] the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|leader]] [[Mon Mothma]].<ref name="The Rebellion 1">[[Return of the Jedi – The Rebellion 1|''Return of the Jedi – The Rebellion'' 1]]</ref>
