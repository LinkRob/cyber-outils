An '''attack''' on the [[Tembora Research Station]] took place sometime during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]]. The research station was built by the [[Army Weapons Research Department]] of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and was responsible for the design of the [[KLR series combat droid]]. This made the base a target for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Separatists]], who attacked and bombarded the base, destroying the garage almost completely before the Republic [[Dreadnaught/Legends|dreadnaught]] [[Cruiser/Legends|cruiser]] ''[[Myrkos]]'' joined the battle. Though the ''Myrkos'' was destroyed, and plummeted into the planet, the Separatist forces were damaged badly enough that they could not continue their bombardment. It was thought that they had decided they had destroyed the base anyway, and simply left.
