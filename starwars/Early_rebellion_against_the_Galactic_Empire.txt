{{Quote|Back in the early days, before the Rebellion formally existed, a lot of guerrillas got their hands dirty doing things no one else wanted to do&mdash;or even think about. The Alliance would never have existed without them, Princess. Those men and women were my mentors&mdash;I've spent my entire career trying to live up to their example.|[[Major]] [[Lokmarcha]], to Leia Organa|<ref name="Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref>}}
Between the [[Clone Wars]] and the [[Galactic Civil War]], an '''early rebellion''' in the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] was waged by a collection of rebel cells that ultimately formed the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. These militant cells were covertly organized by [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] and quietly given political support in the [[Imperial Senate]] by such senators as [[Mon Mothma]] of [[Chandrila]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of Alderaan. Major rebel cells included the [[Massassi Group]] led by [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]], [[Phoenix Cell]] led by [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]], the [[Spectres]] led by [[Captain]] [[Hera Syndulla]], and the [[Partisans]] led by resistance fighter [[Saw Gerrera]]. They were often supported by agents known as [[Fulcrum]], including Commander [[Ahsoka Tano]].

The early rebellion against the Empire originated in the last days of the [[Galactic Republic]], when Senator Organa and other members of the [[Galactic Senate]] convened in secret to discuss ways to counter the executive power amassed by [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. When the Clone Wars, a pretext for Palpatine to amass power, came to an end in [[19 BBY]], the Republic was transformed into the Galactic Empire and Organa began organizing armed resistance against the Imperial regime. By [[4 BBY]], many resistance cells began working together after they aided in the [[Action at Mustafar|rescue]] of the captive [[Jedi]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] over [[Mustafar]].

The battle marked the outbreak of the fully open Galactic Civil War, in which the Alliance ultimately triumphed over the Empire and formed the [[New Republic]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|We cannot let a thousand years of democracy disappear without a fight.|Senator Bail Organa, during the last days of the Galactic Republic|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="prelude date">{{FilmDate|II}}</ref> in response to the growing threat that the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] represented to the stability of the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Galactic Senate]] granted [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] emergency executive power to raise the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] and wage the [[Clone Wars]] against the Separatists.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> Palpatine, secretly the [[Sith Lord]] Darth Sidious who was using the conflict as a pretext for [[The galaxy|galactic]] domination, amassed almost total power during the three-year conflict.<ref name="Episode III" /> Numerous [[senator]]s, including [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] and [[Mon Mothma]] of [[Chandrila]], were alarmed by the increasingly-dictatorial moves of the Supreme Chancellor and began [[Delegation of 2,000|meeting]] in secret to discuss how to counter centralized power.<ref name="Bail Organa Databank">{{Databank|bail-organa|Bail Organa}}</ref> At the end of the war, in the name of peace and security, Palpatine declared himself [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] and [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]] and the Galactic Senate into the Imperial Senate.<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:Delegation_of_2000_meeting.png|thumb|left|226x226px|Senators Bail Organa and Mon Mothma began laying the foundations for rebellion during the Clone Wars.]]
In the years after the Clone Wars, disconnected resistance movements began to crop up throughout the galaxy to combat the Empire. [[Berch Teller]], a former [[Republic Intelligence]] [[captain]] and defected member of the [[Imperial Military]], led a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|cell]] that stole the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', the personal [[starship]] of [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]. Their actions targeted operations related to the secret construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], an Imperial [[superweapon]] capable of destroying entire [[planet]]s, and were in response to the Empire's actions during the [[Antar Atrocity]] a few years earlier.<ref name="Tarkin" /> Another resistance cell, the [[Free Ryloth Movement]], was led by [[General]] [[Cham Syndulla]] on [[Ryloth]]. The cell, focused only on liberating Ryloth, attempted to kill [[Emperor]] Palpatine and his [[Sith apprentice|right-hand]], [[Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]], by attacking the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] ''[[Perilous]]''.<ref name="Lords of the Sith">''[[Lords of the Sith]]''</ref> Neither the actions of Teller or Syndulla succeeded in achieving their goals, but they represented some of the first attempts to combat Imperial rule.<ref name="Tarkin" /><ref name="Lords of the Sith" /> The failure of the [[Imperial Rebirth Act]] convinced Mothma and Organa that the path to unseating Palpatine did not lie within the legislature alone, resulting in their alliance with the [[Partisans|Partisan]] [[Saw Gerrera]].<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

==History==
===Early campaigns===
{{Quote|I want to take your recruits and find missions for them. I want to be the one who listens to what people need, who finds out what people can do and then helps them do it.|Ahsoka Tano, to Bail Organa|<ref name="Ahsoka" />}}
In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="18 BBY">According to ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'', the [[invasion of Mon Cala]] took place a year into the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor's]] rule. Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the beginning of Imperial rule to [[19 BBY]], the occupation of Mon Cala, seen in ''Darth Vader: Darth Lord of the Sith 17'', took place in [[18 BBY]].</ref> Emperor Palpatine caught wind of a [[Ferren Barr|Jedi's]] surmised interference in the affairs that concerned the Empire and Mon Cala. In an effort to show the galaxy the price of defiance by making an example of the [[ocean]] world, the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] and [[Commander-in-Chief]] [[Darth]] Vader was appointed to oversee the hunt for [[King of Mon Cala|King]] [[Lee-Char]]'s Jedi advisor, while [[Moff]] Wilhuff Tarkin managed the standard military aspects. Eventually, the Dark Lord killed the elusive Force-user, former [[Padawan]] Ferren Barr, and arrested the King just as the Governor had finished the assault. Though a handful of [[Mon Calamari]] merchant ships escaped and would go on to play a key role in the later rebellion, Mon Cala was successfully subjugated by the Empire,<ref name="Dark Lord 17">[[Darth Vader (2017) 17|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 17]]</ref> whereas its [[King|monarch]] was imprisoned on [[Strokill Prime]].<ref name="MoM4">[[Star Wars (2015) 47|''Star Wars'' (2015) 47]]</ref>

In those early days of the rebellion, Senator Organa worked with members of the [[Alderaan Guard]] to identify resistance movements across the galaxy and find [[Jedi]] who might have survived the [[Order 66|fall]] of the [[Jedi Order]] at the end of the war. Eventually, the [[Viceroy]] of Alderaan heard of an [[Raada Uprising|uprising]] on [[Raada]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that was rumored to have been led by a Jedi. Organa discovered that the [[Force-sensitive|Force-wielder]] in question was [[Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], a former Padawan who had left the Jedi Order during the war after she had been falsely accused of [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|bombing]] the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]].<ref name="Ahsoka" />

[[File:AhsokaTanoForceOperative-EaW.png|thumb|right|250px|Organa recruited Commander Ahsoka Tano to lead rebel intelligence, and she operated under the codename "Fulcrum."]]
After locating the former Padawan, Organa asked Tano to join his growing rebel movement, and she agreed on two conditions: that Organa help her discover the mysterious [[Inquisitorius|Imperial agents]] who were hunting [[Force-sensitive]] children, and that he help rescue her friends in the [[Raada resistance]]. Organa made good on his promise and sent his ship, the ''[[Tantive IV]]'', and [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]s to evacuate her friends on Raada. Tano and the resistance members joined the rebellion, and Tano took on the role of finding missions for recruits. She became the leader of Organa's [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|rebel intelligence]] and began operating under the codename "[[Fulcrum]]."<ref name="Ahsoka" />

Various rebel cells joined Organa's rebellion, dubbed the "Alliance" by Mon Mothma by [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Seige date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]'' to [[4 BBY]]</ref> over the years. [[Phoenix Cell]], led by Commander [[Jun Sato]], had a fleet of its own, along with a number of A-wing [[starfighter]]s.<ref name="The Siege of Lothal">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''</ref> The [[Massassi Group]], led by General [[Jan Dodonna]], also had a large fleet and operated out of the [[Great Temple]] on [[Yavin 4]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> [[Saw Gerrera]], a resistance fighter trained by Jedi such as Commander Tano [[Onderon Civil War|during]] the Clone Wars, led the [[Partisans]] in a number of [[Campaigns of Saw Gerrera's Partisans|campaigns]].<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> The different cells had different ways of conducting themselves. Gerrera, for example, was more militant than other cells and was willing to take any action necessary in order to free the galaxy from the Empire. This put him at odds with leaders such as Organa<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis">{{Rebels|Ghosts of Geonosis}}</ref> and Mothma,<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> and allowed members of the Empire to view rebel activities in a negative light. [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] recalled that one of his earliest missions was an attempt to pacify [[Onderon]] but he encountered brutal resistance from Gerrera and his Partisans.<ref name="The Honorable Ones">{{Rebels|The Honorable Ones}}</ref>

Prior to 4 BBY,<ref name="Fire date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}} to [[4 BBY]]</ref> rebel cells generally did not work together or even know of one another's existence. This protocol ensured that if any cell was compromised by the Empire, the identities and locations of other cells would not be put into jeopardy.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> Tano coordinated many of the activities and missions of several rebel cells under her Fulcrum codename, providing them with Imperial targets and supply opportunities.<ref name="Out of Darkness">{{Rebels|Out of Darkness}}</ref> One early example of coordinated activities was a joint operation between the [[Commenor Underground]] and the Partisans, around [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Rebel Dossier">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''</ref> All the same, Gerrera<ref name="Narkina 5" /> largely<ref name="Daughter of Ferrix">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> kept the Partisans separate from other rebel factions, believing he alone had a clarity of purpose in the battle against the Empire and not wanting to put his fighters at risk for other groups.<ref name="Narkina 5" />

===Growing signs of rebellion===
{{Quote|There is a wound that won't heal at the center of the galaxy. There is a darkness reaching like rust into everything around us. We let it grow, and now it's here. It's here and its not visiting anymore. It wants to stay. The Empire is a disease that thrives in darkness. It is never more alive than when we sleep. It's easy for the dead to tell you to fight, and maybe it's true, maybe fighting is useless. Perhaps it's too late. But I'll tell you this&hellip; If I could do it again, I'd wake up early and be fighting these bastards&hellip; from the start. Fight the Empire!|Maarva Andor|<ref name="Rix Road">{{Andor|Rix Road (episode)}}</ref>|audio=MaarvaSpeech.flac}}
In 5 BBY,<ref name="Atrivis sector Date Note">According to ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', the Atrivis Resistance Group and the [[Liberators]] were active around 14 [[Imperial calendar|AFE]] (or after the formation of the Empire) which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore 14 AFE would be the same as 5 BBY.</ref> at least two resistance movements were active in the [[Atrivis sector]], being the [[Atrivis Resistance Group]], led by [[Travia Chan]], and the [[Liberators]], operating on [[Mantooine]]. The Liberators managed to [[Battle on Mantooine|seize]] the Imperial [[Imperial garrison (Mantooine)|Mantooine garrison]] but shortly afterward, the [[Imperial Navy]] counterattacked, swiftly defeating the Liberators on Mantooine. The Atrivis Resistance Group remained active and soon joined Organa's rebellion.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> By that same year,<ref name="Kassa" /> the recently-promoted [[ISB Supervisor]] [[Dedra Meero]] had started to take notice of multiple high-level thefts aimed at securing Imperial equipment, which she suspected was part of an organized rebel effort.<ref name="Aldhani" />

Similarly, [[Sergeant]] [[Linus Mosk]], who served within the Imperial-aligned [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]] [[Corporate Tactical Forces|tactical forces]] and considered his organization to be the Empire's first line of defense, had taken notice of growing pockets of dissent on Corporate-affiliated planets.<ref name="That Would Be Me">{{Andor|That Would Be Me}}</ref> After the human [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] murdered two members of the [[Pre-Mor Security Inspection team]], [[Verlo Skiff]] and [[Kravas Drezzer]], while searching for his sister [[Kerri]] on [[Morlana One]],<ref name="Kassa" /> Mosk and [[Deputy Inspector]] [[Syril Karn]] led the [[Ambush on Ferrix|search]] for Andor on [[Ferrix]], where their target was being recruited to the wider rebel movement by [[Luthen Rael]],<ref name="Reckoning">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref> who was establishing an alliance of rebel cells to begin a true fight against the Empire. Despite Gerrera's insistence on working alone with only his Partisans, he maintained ties with Rael, who sold equipment to Gerrera's effort.<ref name="Narkina 5" /> The disastrous incident on Ferrix was used by the Empire as an excuse to directly annex the [[Free Trade sector|Morlana sector]] and dissolve Preox-Morlana.<ref name="Aldhani" />

===Rebellion on Lothal===
By 5 BBY,<ref name="Spark date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]'', which depicts the mission to free [[Wookiee]] [[prisoner]]s, in [[5 BBY]].</ref> a growing rebellion on the planet [[Lothal]], a vital production center for the Empire's expansion in the Outer Rim, was led by Captain [[Hera Syndulla]] of the ''[[Ghost]]'' and her Jedi companion [[Kanan Jarrus]]. Together, they organized a [[Spectres|crew]] that included the [[Mandalorian]] warrior [[Sabine Wren]], [[Lasat]] [[soldier]] [[Garazeb Orrelios]], and the [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]].<ref name="Spark of Rebellion" /> Their [[raiding missions on Imperial supply ships]] proved to be a major boom to the early rebellion.<ref name="Ghost Raid" /> The crew grew to include [[Ezra Bridger]], a [[The Force|Force]]-sensitive youth whom Jarrus took on as his apprentice. Their actions came under increased Imperial scrutiny when they [[Mission to free Wookiee prisoners|rescued]] [[Wookiee]] [[Slavery|slaves]] from the [[Spice mines of Kessel|spice mines]] of [[Kessel]]. They were pursued by Agent Kallus, and the actions of the Jedi drew the attention of [[the Grand Inquisitor]] as he hunted down surviving Jedi.<ref name="Spark of Rebellion" />

[[File:Bail_Organa_Droids_in_Distress.png|thumb|left|250px|Organa learns about the ''Ghost'' crew.]]
Organa's [[droid]]s, astromech droid [[R2-D2]] and [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]], encountered the ''Ghost'' crew on [[Garel]] as the rebels [[Mission to steal Imperial weapons|stole]] illegal weapons from the Empire. Artoo monitored the activities of these rebels and reported back to Organa, who took an interest in the growing rebellion on Lothal.<ref name="Droids in Distress" /> Captain Syndulla, who became aware of the protocols of the growing rebellion, began receiving missions from Fulcrum. This caused some tension within the ''Ghost'' ranks, as Wren questioned who Fulcrum was and wondered why they were taking orders from a mysterious stranger. Per rebel protocol, they remained unaware of the existence of a larger rebellion against the Empire.<ref name="Out of Darkness" />

===Disorganized, yet still fighting===
{{Main|Ambush of the Maya Pei Brigade|Theft of the TIE Avenger prototype|Yavin 4 turf war}}
As of [[4 BBY]], various rebel cells remained active in the galaxy,<ref name="OYL">{{Andor|One Year Later}}</ref> but the overall rebellion which they represented was not yet an organized force<ref name="DeclassifiedTime">{{SWYouTube|5SdMzzmeNsY|Andor Season 2 Declassified: Time}}</ref> despite the work attempted by the likes of Rael,<ref name="Narkina 5" /> Mothma, and Organa.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Instead, the rebellion was a largely disorganized force that could not put up an effective, unified fight, something that was epitomized by the [[Maya Pei Brigade]].<ref name="DeclassifiedTime" /> A [[Neo-Republican]] rebel cell commanded by [[Maya Pei]],<ref name="Narkina 5" /> the [[brigade]] found itself [[Ambush of the Maya Pei Brigade|ambushed]] and sustained heavy losses, leaving a few survivors stranded on<ref name="OYL" /> the [[moon]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> [[Yavin 4]]. Without Pei, who might have been killed in the ambush,<ref name="OYL" /> relations between Pei's fighters began to collapse.<ref name="Maya Pei">{{Databank|maya-pei-brigade|Maya Pei Brigade}}</ref>

At the same time, Andor, having become a formal and energized member of Rael's rebel cell,<ref name="DeclassifiedTime" /> carried out a [[Theft of the TIE Avenger prototype|mission to steal]]<ref name="OYL" /> the prototype [[TIE Avenger prototype|TIE Avenger]]<ref name="Avenger">{{Databank|tie-avenger-prototype|TIE Avenger prototype}}</ref> from [[Sienar Test Facility 73|Test Facility 73]]<ref name="OYL" /> on the [[Sienar Fleet Systems]]-owned planet [[Sienar TF73]].<ref name="TestDB">{{Databank|sienar-test-facility|Sienar Test Facility}}</ref> Successfully escaping despite being unfamiliar with the controls, Andor traveled to Yavin 4, where he was supposed to meet a pilot named [[Porko]] who would take the Avenger forward, but instead found the squabbling Maya Pei Brigade, who believed he was a [[Sienar test pilot]] and took him prisoner.<ref name="OYL" /> The experience on Yavin 4 was eye-opening for Andor, who saw just how far the rebellion was from being an organized, capable force.<ref name="DeclassifiedTime" />

===Drilling down on Lothal===
{{Quote|Why did you come here?''"<br />"''Because of you, and your apprentice. Many in this system and beyond have heard your message. You gave them hope in their darkest times. We didn't want that hope to die.|Kanan Jarrus and Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />}}
The rebellion on Lothal became enough of a problem for the Empire that now-Grand Moff Tarkin, [[Governor]] of the Outer Rim, personally traveled to Lothal to oversee an operation aimed at eliminating the ''Ghost'' crew once and for all. Tarkin was particularly concerned that the presence of the Jedi could inspire the people of Lothal to put their faith in something other than the strength of the Empire. The rebels undertook an operation to infiltrate the [[Imperial Communications Center]] on Lothal and broadcast a message to the planet's citizens to bring the people hope. Tarkin and the Empire were ready for the rebel incursion and [[Raid on the communications tower|captured]] Jarrus, but the rebels were able to broadcast a message of hope and tell the people of Lothal that the rebels were fighting for the freedom of all the people.<ref name="Call to Action" />

[[File:Rebel_fleet_Mustafar.png|thumb|right|250px|Phoenix Squadron arrives at Mustafar to help the ''Ghost'' crew escape from the Empire.]]
The ''Ghost'' crew wanted to find a way to rescue Jarrus, but Tano ordered Syndulla to lay low. In defiance of Fulcrum and Syndulla, Bridger led the other rebels in discovering that Jarrus was being taken to [[Mustafar]], a [[Magma|volcanic]] planet that Jarrus had once told Syndulla was a place where Jedi were taken to die.<ref name="Rebel Resolve">{{Rebels|Rebel Resolve}}</ref> The rebels mounted a [[Action at Mustafar|rescue]] mission, one in which Jarrus and Bridger faced off against the Grand Inquisitor aboard Tarkin's Star Destroyer, the ''[[Sovereign]]''. Jarrus defeated the Grand Inquisitor, who fell to his death during their battle, and the rebels fled from the ''Sovereign''. They were nearly killed before the timely arrival of Tano and Phoenix Squadron, who were inspired by the message the rebels sent to Lothal and surrounding [[star system]]s and decided to help rescue Jarrus. The ''Ghost'' crew finally met another cell and Tano face to face, and Tano told them that the protocol had changed: the cells were beginning to work together as a rebellion.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />

The loss at Mustafar, including the destruction of the ''Sovereign'', was an embarrassing defeat for the Empire<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> and Grand Moff Tarkin.<ref name="Secret Cargo">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref> In answer to the blow dealt by the insurgents, the [[Emperor's Fist]], Lord Vader himself, was assigned to see to the quelling of the rebel activities on Lothal in consultation with Governor Tarkin, with Agent Kallus and the planetary government at their disposal. Meanwhile, the ''Ghost'' crew joined Phoenix Squadron and the two cells began working together as one.<ref name="The Siege of Lothal" />

==Legacy==
{{Main|Galactic Civil War}}
{{Quote|There are whispers from Mustafar. Some people see the Empire as weak and vulnerable.|Alexsandr Kallus reports the status of Lothal to Wilhuff Tarkin after the action at Mustafar|<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />}}
[[File:Exds.png|thumb|left|250px|The theft of the Death Star plans led to the rebel victory at Yavin]]
The action at Mustafar had occurred as part of the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="Mustafar DB">{{Databank|url=mustafar-history-gallery|title=Mustafar History Gallery}} {{C|Slide 15}}</ref> a five-year conflict between the Galactic Empire and the Alliance to Restore the Republic.<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> On Lothal alone, the rebel victory at Mustafar was enough to spread anti-Imperial sentiment and unrest, creating rumors amongst the populous that the Empire had weakened and thus was vulnerable to attack.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> Such a public rebel triumph forced the Empire to step up its efforts against the rebel cells scattered throughout the galaxy. In the aftermath of the [[Ghorman Massacre]]<ref name="Secret Cargo">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref> in [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Senator Mon Mothma believed there was no hope left for the Imperial Senate and resigned, leading to her effort to escape Imperial forces in [[Operation Handoff]]. Over the planet [[Dantooine]], Mothma [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|rallied]] the scattered cells across the galaxy into the unified [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Secret Cargo" />

The Empire's victory in the [[Battle of Atollon]]<ref name="In the Name of Rebellion">{{Rebels|In the Name of the Rebellion}}</ref> of 2 BBY<ref name="Timelines" /> left Mothma and others with the belief that the Alliance was not yet prepared for open war, but she also recognized there were others who believed open conflict had already begun.<ref name="In the Name of Rebellion" /> The [[Battle of Scarif]], led by the squad known as [[Rogue One]], marked the Alliance's first major victory of the Civil War, stealing the [[Death Star plans]] at the cost of their own lives. Seeking to recover the plans, Lord Vader pursued Princess Leia's corvette, the ''Tantive IV'', to the planet Tatooine. Despite her capture, Leia managed to hide the plans in R2-D2, who was jettisoned alongside C-3PO to the planet's surface with the task of delivering them to Obi-Wan Kenobi. Unveiling the Death Star's full power, Governor Tarkin destroyed the planet Alderaan to punish its leaders for aiding the Rebellion. Preferring to rule by fear and terror, the Emperor [[Dissolution of the Imperial Senate|dissolved the Imperial Senate]] and delegated power to regional governors. Instead of frightening the galaxy into submission, the destruction of Alderaan only fanned the flames of rebellion.<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

After the plans were delivered, the droids, Kenobi and Skywalker teamed up with the smuggler [[Han Solo]], and the Wookiee co-pilot [[Chewbacca]] to rescue Princess Leia from the Death Star. They then delivered the Death Star plans to the Rebel Alliance base on Yavin 4. Seeking to crush the Rebellion once and for all, Governor Tarkin brought the Death Star to Yavin 4. However, the Alliance discovered that the Death Star's flaw was an exposed [[thermal exhaust port]]. Following a fierce space battle, Skywalker fired a missile down the thermal exhaust port. The destruction of the Death Star marked an important victory for the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Episode IV" />

[[File:A_New_Dawn-SWP.jpg|thumb|right|200px|The rebels fought to restore the Republic]]
For the next five years, the Empire and Alliance battled for control of the galaxy. Despite victories such as the [[Battle of Hoth]], the Empire failed to crush the Rebellion.<ref name="TESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> Undeterred by the loss of the Death Star, Palpatine commissioned the creation of a [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] over the [[Endor|Forest Moon of Endor]]. Seeking to crush the Rebel Alliance once and for all, the Emperor lured the Alliance fleet into a [[Battle of Endor|trap]] at Endor in [[4 ABY]]. However, rebel ground forces and their local [[Ewok]] allies destroyed the planetary shield generator; allowing the Alliance to destroy the superweapon. To save his son Luke, Vader killed Palpatine, thus restoring balance to the Force.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

Following the Battle of Endor, the Rebel Alliance reorganized itself into the [[New Republic]]. The deaths of Emperor Palpatine and Lord Vader triggered the collapse of the Empire and the new government quickly consolidated control over large areas of the galaxy.<ref name="Aftermath novel">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> While a powerless [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] officially ruled the Empire from Coruscant, much of the Empire was actually in the hands of [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]]. As the Emperor's designated successor, Rax implemented the Palpatine's posthumous [[Contingency]] plan to tear down the Empire and to bring a worthy elect into the Unknown Regions to start afresh.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> Following feigned [[Imperial–Republic peace talks|peace talks]] on Chandrila,<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> Rax orchestrated the [[Battle of Jakku]] as the final showdown between the Empire and the New Republic.<ref name="Empire's End" />

The self-styled [[Counselor to the Empire]] intended to implode the planet Jakku with [[Sith]] relics in order to destroy the massed Imperial and New Republic fleets. However, he was stopped by Grand Admiral [[Rae Sloane]] with the help of New Republic operatives. With the Empire defeated, Sloane and other Imperial officers fled into the Unknown Regions to regroup. Meanwhile, the New Republic signed the Galactic Concordance with Lord Amedda, formally ending the Galactic Civil War.<ref name="Empire's End" /> While the New Republic turned its attention to reshaping the galactic order, the [[Imperial Remnants]] consolidated themselves as the [[First Order]], a military junta that was dedicated to restoring the Galactic Empire.<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> In [[34 ABY]], [[Cold War]] tensions between the New Republic, the allied [[Resistance]] paramilitary outfit, and the First Order culminated in [[First Order-Resistance War|open warfare]] between the First Order and the Resistance,<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> paving the way for the open return of the [[Cloning|reborn]] Emperor on the enclave world of [[Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
