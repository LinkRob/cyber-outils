'''Electrified dual guitars''' were a [[music]]al instrument and weapon, combining the function of two [[guitar]]s and an electrical [[melee weapon]]. The [[droid]] [[Musician|guitarist]] [[K-344]] "Kurti" of the band [[Star Waver]] performed with one, which she also used in combat.<ref name="Tatooine Rhapsody" />
