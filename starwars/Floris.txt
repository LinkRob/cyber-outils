'''Floris''' was an uninhabited [[planet]] located far on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] that was distant from the Outer Rim planet [[Eiram]]. In [[228 BBY]], during the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[carnivorous plant]]s known as the [[Drengir]], believing the [[blight]] plague was coming to Eiram, made an [[Escape on Eiram|attempt]] to leave the planet. After the [[Jedi Order]] [[Battle in Arovia park|discovered]] this, they agreed to give the Drengir their [[Elite-class vessel|starship]], the ''[[Innovator]]'', and set it for a distant world so the [[plant]]s could escape the blight until it had been stopped. Before departing, however, the [[Great Progenitor]]&mdash;the Drengir's [[Gender|matriarch]]&mdash;had [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[Petty Officer]] [[Orlis]] change their destination from Floris to the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet [[Kashyyyk]], where she thought they would flourish.

==Description==
Floris was a [[planet]] located far on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] that was distant from [[Eiram]]&mdash;a planet<ref name="Haunted Starlight" /> within the [[Eiram system]] of the Outer Rim's [[Dalnan sector]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:GreatProgenitor-2021THR6.png|thumb|right|270px|The Great Progenitor (pictured) influenced Petty Officer Orlis to change the Drengir's intended course from Floris to Kashyyyk.]]
In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|HS}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi|members]] of the [[Jedi Order]] [[Battle in Arovia park|fought]] against the [[Drengir]]&mdash;[[Sentience|sentient]] [[carnivorous plant]]s led by their [[Gender|matriarch]], the [[Great Progenitor]]&mdash;and learned the Drengir were [[Escape on Eiram|attempting]] to flee the planet Eiram, as they believed the plague known as the [[blight]] was heading there. In response, the Jedi agreed to provide the [[plant]]s with<ref name="Haunted Starlight" /> the [[Elite-class vessel|''Elite''-class vessel]]<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> ''[[Innovator]]'' and a distant world so they could be safe until the blight was stopped.<ref name="Haunted Starlight" />

Before the Drengir left Eiram aboard the ''Innovator'', [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[Petty Officer]] [[Orlis]] arrived on the [[Starship|ship]] to set its [[navigation computer]] to head toward Floris, as it was far from the [[Galactic Republic]] and blight. The Great Progenitor, however, briefly used a [[vine]] to control Orlis' thoughts and had him instead set the [[computer]] to head to<ref name="Haunted Starlight" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> planet [[Kashyyyk]], where she believed the Drengir would flourish. As the ''Innovator'' traveled through [[hyperspace]] on its way to Kashyyyk, the Great Progenitor thought about the original intent for the Drengir to travel to Floris and recalled her control of the petty officer to change their course to their new destination, noting he would not remember.<ref name="Haunted Starlight" /> During their journey, the Drengir [[Force sense|sensed]] the blight and were compelled to alter their course repeatedly to avoid it, before they eventually did travel to Kashyyyk.<ref name="Into the Light">''[[The High Republic: Into the Light]]''</ref>
