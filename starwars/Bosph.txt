'''Bosph''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Bosph sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. In [[3 BBY]], the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Bombardment of Bosph|bombarded]] the world to try and wipe out a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Unidentified Bosph species|species]] that [[Homeworld|dwelt]] there. Surviving members of the [[species]] chose to [[Dis-remembering|dis-remember]] the Empire.

==Description==
Bosph was a [[planet]]<ref name="Rise" /> in the [[Bosph system]] of the [[Bosph sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> It fell within grid square Q-3 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==History==
{{Quote|In response, these survivors have "dis-remembered" the Galactic Empire&mdash;a grave insult in their society. They refuse to acknowledge the Imperial state ever even existed. These are a species who have purposefully turned their backs on the perpetrators of attempted genocide, and their silence should be respected.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>}}
During the [[High Republic Era]],<ref name="AcolyteVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> the Bosph sector was<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-31.png|author=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-31}}</ref> an informal sector known as the unincorporated Bosphian provinces. The [[Bosphian sestertius]] was used as [[currency]] in the sector during the period and shared its name<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> with Bosph.<ref name="Rise" />

[[File:PlanetaryBombardment-SWGTCG.jpg|thumb|right|300px|alt=Two Imperial Super Star Destroyers and a trio of smaller Imperial-class Star Destroyers bombard the cloudy surface of a planet from space, with the light of explosions below visible on the surface.|The Imperial Navy (pictured) bombarded Bosph.]]
During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Imperial Navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] repeatedly carried out large-scale [[Bombardment of Bosph|orbital bombardment]] on Bosph in [[3 BBY]] in an attempt to commit genocide on a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Unidentified Bosph species|species]] that [[Homeworld|dwelt]] there. Surviving members of the [[species]] were then kept under an ongoing quarantine and, because of this, chose to [[Dis-remembering|dis-remember]] the Empire, a grave insult in their society that meant that they refused to acknowledge the existence of the state.<ref name="Rise" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned Bosph and described the damage done by the [[Orbital bombardment|bombardments]] as still being effectively irreparable in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Bosph was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" /> The [[Bosph/Legends|planet]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in "[[Smugglers of the Outer Rim]]," a ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' source article written by [[Doug Shuler]] and published in the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 5|fifth issue]] of [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'' magazine in [[February]] [[1995]].<ref name="SotOR">{{JournalCite|5|Smugglers of the Outer Rim}}</ref>
