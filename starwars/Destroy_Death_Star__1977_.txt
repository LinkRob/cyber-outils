'''''Destroy Death Star''''' is a board game that was released by [[Kenner Products|Kenner]] in [[1977]]. It was based on the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]] from [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''A New Hope'']]. Players moved [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] squadrons along the board to enter the trench and fire the torpedoes that destroy the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|Death Star]]. The game is much more difficult to find than the similar ''[[Escape from Death Star]]'', released in the same year.<ref name="9 Classic" />

A very different [[Destroy Death Star (1978)|game of the same name]] was released in the United Kingdom<ref name="9 Classic" /> in [[1978]].<ref name="Copyright">Copyright printed on game's box</ref>
