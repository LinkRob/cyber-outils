"'''But What Does He Eat?'''" is a short story that was published in the [[2020]] anthology book ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''. It was written by [[S.A. Chakraborty]] and focuses on the [[Cloud City]] [[chef]] [[Torro Sbazzle]].
