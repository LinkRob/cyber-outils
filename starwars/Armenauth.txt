'''Armenauth''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] who served as a [[Squad leader|squadron leader]] of [[Squadron Four (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Four]] and held the [[military rank]] of [[captain]] within the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], which was part of the loyalist forces of the [[Galactic Empire]]. He was given leadership of Squadron Four after [[Jothal Gablerone]]'s death during the [[Attack on Cerberon]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
