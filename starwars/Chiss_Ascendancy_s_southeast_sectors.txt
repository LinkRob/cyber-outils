A group of '''sectors''' in the southeast of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] existed in the [[Unknown Regions]]. The sectors contained the planets [[Celwis]], [[Copero]] and [[Sarvchi]]. In around [[18 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> the [[Xodlak family]], [[Erighal family]] and [[Pommrio family]] gathered their [[Family fleet|fleets]] there in preparation for the [[Hoxim incident]]. Soon after, also in around [[18 BBY]]<ref name="Date2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref>, a [[Standoff at Sarvchi|skirmish]] occurred at Sarvchi.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
