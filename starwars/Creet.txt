'''Creet''' was a [[Gender|female]] Imperial [[TIE series|TIE]] [[Technician|mechanic]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. During a celebration, [[Major]] [[Soran Keize]], the 204th's [[commanding officer]] listened to the stories from crewmembers like Creet, and the mechanic expressed her desire for a counterattack on the [[New Republic]] for its capture of the astronomical object [[Andara]].

While the 204th was at the [[planet]] [[Jakku]], Creet attended another celebration, where she and the since promoted [[Colonel]] Keize carried the [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] [[Bansu Ro]] to his bunk after he became under the influence of narcotics. The New Republic soon [[Battle of Jakku|attacked]] and defeated the Empire's forces at Jakku, and Creet was split from her unit. The ex-[[New Republic pilot]] [[Nath Tensent]] convinced her to join his crew within four [[Standard month|months]] after the battle.

==Biography==
===Stories with Soran===
{{Quote|We need to show them they haven't. Even if we are to bide our time&mdash;we owe the people of Andara. We need to be showing them all that people are still fighting. Fighting for them. Fighting for the Empire.''"<br />"''We will. We ''are''.|Creet and Soran Keize|<ref name="Shadow Fall" />}}
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Creet served as a [[TIE series|TIE]] [[Technician|mechanic]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], serving as crew aboard one of its [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser carriers]]<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> by around [[5 ABY]]. Around that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">{{ShadowFallDate}}</ref> the 204th [[Capture of the Edict|captured]] the<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="New Villain">{{SW|url=news/shadow-fall-novel-first-look-at-new-villain-and-more|text=''Shadow Fall'' Novel: First Look at New Villain and More}}</ref> ''[[Edict]]'', which the [[fighter wing]] brought into its complement. Creet attended a celebration to welcome a number of survivors from the ''Edict'' into the 204th's ranks. During the gathering, though, [[Lieutenant]] [[Nord Kandende]], one of the 204th's [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilots]] declared that the [[New Republic]] had captured the<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> astronomical object<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[Andara]] from the Empire, news which had spread prior between personnel in the ''Aerie''{{'s}} hangar.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

[[Major]] [[Soran Keize]], the [[commanding officer]] of the 204th, had the [[squadron commander]]s present take their [[pilot]]s away, leaving behind the crew of the ''Edict'' and the ''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carriers ''[[Aerie]]'' and ''[[Allegiance]]'', the latter of which had been destroyed not long before to repair the star destroyer. The crew told stories about Andara and the late ''Allegiance'' while Keize listened. Creet then stressed that they could not let the New Republic keep winning, asking the major if the New Republic thought they had already won as<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> he had briefly attempted to live under New Republic rule for a short amount of time.<ref name="Alphabet">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref>

Keize confirmed Creet's suspicions, leading the mechanic to insist that 204th should fight back at the New Republic for the people of Andara. Keize assured her that the fighter wing was already doing so, and Creet asked what it was like for the major to live under New Republic rule. Keize complied with Creet's question and told all of the crew present about how the New Republic appeared to not know how to govern and that worlds suffered from societal breakdown and shortages.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

===Fall of the Empire===
{{Quote|See, I've already won over your friends Creet and Nord Kandende&mdash;that kid was in a ''read'' bad spot, let me tell you&mdash;and I've got a list of other candidates for recruitment. There're plenty of credits out there for a team of freelancers with the right guidance.''"<br />"''Nord Kandende is an idiot.''"<br />"''Sure is. But you ''do'' like Creet, don't you?|Nath Tensent attempts to recruit Bansu Ro, citing the recruitment of Creet|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
The 204th [[Cerberon system campaign|fought]] a New Republic [[Barma Battle Group|battle group]] in the [[Cerberon system]] not long after, losing the ''Edict'' and ''Aerie'' during the initial [[Attack on Cerberon|attack]]. The fighter wing ultimately escaped on a [[bulk freighter]]<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> which became the units headquarters and was named the ''[[Yadeez]]''. Aboard the ''Yadeez'', the 204th operated for the Empire's loyalist forces before eventually amassing with the majority of them at the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. During their time there, a number of the fighter wing's personnel, including Creet, had a gathering at the ''Yadeez''{{'s}} hangar. After Keize joined the celebration, the commanding [[officer]], since promoted to [[colonel]], and the mechanic hauled the pilot [[Bansu Ro]], who was under the influence of narcotics, to his bunk.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

Creet stood behind Keize and squeezed his [[Limb|arm]] while he spoke to Ro. Both then returned to the celebration. Shortly after, the Empire's forces at Jakku were attacked by the New Republic, which claimed a decisive victory. The 204th itself took many casualties and the ''Yadeez'' was brought down. Creet became estranged from the fighter wing and evaded a fate at the hands of the New Republic. Within four [[Standard month|months]] after the Battle of Jakku, she approached by [[Nath Tensent]], an ex-[[New Republic pilot]]. Tensent convinced her to join his crew of freelancers and also recruited Kandende. When he later tried to recruit the Ro, he cited the intake of Creet and Kandende to try and convince the other 204th veteran to join his crew on the planet [[Freerock]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Tell us, what it's like under their rule.|Creet, to Soran Keize, on the New Republic|<ref name="Shadow Fall" />}}
A female individual, Creet spoke in a thick [[Twi'lek]] accent. After learning of the fall of Andara into New Republic hands, she was keen to see the 204th make a counterattack to show the enemy that they were not winning.<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> When Soran Keize spoke to the intoxicated Bansu Ro at the latter's bunk, Creet squeezed the colonel's arm during the talk.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
