'''Cook'em Cakes''' were a type of small instant [[cake]] that were stored in packets and [[Diet|edible]] by [[Heptooinian]]s and [[human]]s. The cakes would instantly form after the packet was opened, with its [[jelly]] giving off an artificial [[berry]] scent. Early into the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Imperial Intelligence]] [[spy]] [[Haki Zeophrine]] kept Cook'em Cakes in the [[Haki Zeophrine's safe house|safe house]] she and her apprentice, [[Chemish Runazha]], shared on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="MOF" />

In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|7}}</ref> Zeophrine returned to the safe house to meet with Runazha and fumbled through its cabinets until she found a packet of Cook'em Cakes. When she opened it, she noticed it sweat jelly and give off an aroma as it took form. Another time not long afterward, when Zeophrine returned from a [[Investigation into the framing of the Jedi|mission]] with [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], she and Runahaza conversed and ate several Cook'em Cakes together while the latter informed their mentor of Coruscant's happenings while she had been away.<ref name="MOF" />
