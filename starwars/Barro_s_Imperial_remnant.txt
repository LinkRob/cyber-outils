An '''Imperial remnant'''<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" /> of the [[neo-Imperial forces]] was under the command of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[warlord]] and member of the [[Shadow Council (Imperial Remnants)|Shadow Council]]<ref name="Chapter 23: The Spies" /> [[Commander]] [[Barro]]. 

== Description ==
The [[Imperial Remnants|Imperial remnant]] was a part of the larger [[Neo-Imperial forces]],<ref name="Chapter 23: The Spies" /> a polity<ref>[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide|''Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide'']]</ref> that coordinated the assets and territories of several rogue [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Warlord|warlords]] after the signing of the [[Galactic Concordance]].<ref name="Chapter 23: The Spies" /> 

It was based from an [[Unidentified ice planet (Outer Rim)|ice planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] during the [[New Republic Era]]. The remnant controlled its territories by exacting a tribute from local communities in exchange for protection from pirates.<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" />

Commander Barro, who sat on the Neo-Imperial forces' [[Shadow Council (Imperial Remnants)|Shadow Council]], led the remnant.<ref name="Chapter 23: The Spies" /> He could summon the local leaders and raise the protection tax at will. One local leader under the remnant was [[Prefect]] [[Nobah]].<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" />

=== Military ===
By at least [[11 ABY]],<ref name="DateM&quot;">{{MandoDate|MandalorianAndGrogu}}</ref> the Remnant's forces included [[Cold weather assault trooper]]s. These troopers had access to a large arsenal of weapons and vehicles, including [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s, 3 [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)|All Terrain Armored Transports]] and at least 1 [[INT-4 Interceptor]]. The snowtroopers used [[DLT-20A blaster rifle]]s and [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]]s. They also had among their ranks [[Imperial Remnant AT-RT Driver|Imperial Remnant AT-RT Drivers]] and [[AT-AT pilot]]s, and used at least one [[polar mouse droid]].<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" />

In addition to Barro himself, the remnant's leadership included Commander [[Fremmet]] and at least one other [[Imperial officer]], both of whom acted as [[Vehicle commander]]s for the AT-AT/Ps the remnant used. <ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" /> Barro and Fremmet both used [[SE-14r light repeating blaster]]s.<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" /> 

== History ==
Commander Barro's Imperial Remnant was in existence by [[9 ABY]],<ref name=date23>{{MandoDate|23}}</ref> and was part of the larger Neo-Imperial forces.<ref name="Chapter 23: The Spies" />

In exchange for a tribute from the locals, the remnant offered protection on the [[planet]] it was based on. However,<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" /> by 11 ABY,<ref name="DateM&quot;" /> pirates and thieves plagued the hyperlanes, resulting in the local leaders operating at a loss. Regardless, Barro convened them to further raise the tribute.<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" /> 

The meeting was interrupted by the irruption of the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]], who was working as a contractor for the [[New Republic]] and helping in hunting down Imperial war criminals. Barro attempted to escape, but he was killed in the [[Attack on Barro's Imperial remnant|attack]].<ref name="MandalorianandGrogu" />
