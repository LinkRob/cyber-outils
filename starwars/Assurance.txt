'''''Assurance''''' was a [[CR56 corvette]] commanded by [[Captain]] [[Pell Baylo]] during the [[Republic Era]]. Under Baylo's command, the [[corvette]] had operated in a variety of roles, including in nine [[planet]]ary defense forces and as commercial security for eight corporations. For some [[time]], ''Assurance'' was part of the [[Republic Diplomatic Fleet|Diplomatic Fleet]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Judicial Forces]], although it was booked to later serve [[Chokoll Indemnity]] along the [[Hydian Way]]&mdash;according to Baylo, the [[starship]]'s name made it popular with insurance companies. ''Assurance'' transported [[Jedi Master]] [[Depa Billaba]] to [[Keldooine]], and Master [[Mace Windu]] later arrived on the corvette in order to meet her for a [[Mission to Kwenn|mission]] to Kwenn. Billaba's own mission, however, had led her to [[Valboraan]], where ''Assurance'' later participated in an [[Attack on Valboraan|attack]] on the [[Riftwalker Headquarters|base]] of the [[Riftwalkers]] [[pirate]] gang. After ''Assurance''{{'s}} assistance in the [[defense of Kwenn]], Windu had the Republic extend its contract with Baylo, also suggesting that he establish a training vessel for the Republic.<ref name="The Living Force" />
