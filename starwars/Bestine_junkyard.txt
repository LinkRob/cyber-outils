The '''Bestine junkyard''' was a junkyard in the [[city]] [[Bestine (city)|Bestine]] on [[Tatooine]], the [[desert]] [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[scavenger]] named [[Otip Ashtep]] operated out of the junkyard.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] visited the junkyard at least twice. Once to locate someone that could install a [[laser turret]] to her [[EML-850 light freighter]], the ''[[Trailblazer]]''. A [[Technician|mechanic]] at the junkyard directed Vess towards a [[Jawa]] named [[Entip]] in the [[South Jundland Wastes]]. During another visit, Vess spoke to a [[Weequay]] [[Unidentified Weequay engineer|engineer]] about their [[Unidentified Weequay engineer's grandfather|grandfather's]] surveying notes about the [[Mos Algo mine]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Bestine junkyard first appeared in canon in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The location was identified in the "The Hotfixer" and "Repulsor Train" quests.<ref name="Outlaws" />
