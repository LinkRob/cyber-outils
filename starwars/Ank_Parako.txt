'''Ank Parako''' was a [[Chadra-Fan]] explosives expert and safecracker who worked for [[Standard decade|decades]] with her [[Marriage|husband]], [[Pirrip]]. During a job in which the pair broke into a [[Black Sun]] vault, Pirrip's [[heart]] [[Death|gave out]], resulting in their mission's failure. Parako went on to accrue a number of debts, including to the [[Ashiga Clan]], leading them to take her [[prisoner]]. The scoundrel [[Kay Vess]], hoping to recruit Parako, sought to have the Ashiga Clan release her.<ref name="Outlaws" />
