The '''CJ-9 electrified bo-rifle''', also known simply as the '''CJ-9 bo-rifle''', was a model of [[bo-rifle]], a unique combination of an [[electrostaff]] and a [[blaster rifle]]. Alongside its decent [[blaster]] fire output, the CJ-9 was capable of delivering [[Death|lethal]] [[Melee weapon|melee strikes]] when its shaft was extended to form an electrified [[staff]]. As the traditional [[weapon]] of the [[Lasan]] [[Lasan High Honor Guard|High Honor Guard]], the bo-rifle was renowned as a weapon owned solely by the one who bested its wielder in combat.

==Description==
{{Listen3|CJ-9Staff.flac|CJ-9 bo-rifle's electrostaff activating}}
The CJ-9 electrified bo-rifle,<ref name="SG" /> or simply the CJ-9 bo-rifle, was a model of [[bo-rifle]], a unique two-[[Limb|handed]] [[weapon]] design that combined an [[electrostaff]] with a [[blaster rifle]]. The weapon was capable of discharging [[Color|red]]-colored [[blaster]] bolts with a reliable fire output. Its signature feature, however, was its forward facing [[staff]] that could emit yellow [[electromagnetic pulse]]s in order to deliver [[Death|lethal]] [[Melee weapon|melee strikes]] to opponents.<ref name="Battlefront" />

[[File:CJ-9_rebel.png|thumb|left|285px|The CJ-9 bo-rifle was capable of delivering lethal melee strikes.]]
Similar in design to<ref name="Battlefront" /> the [[J-19 bo-rifle]], which was one of the later and less artfully designed versions of the bo-rifle,<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> the black-colored CJ-9 consisted of a long, rotund shaft with a smaller [[rifle]] [[barrel]] attached below it that was responsible for discharging blaster bolts. The weapon's shaft contained cooling vents, and it could be extended forward to be transformed into an electrified staff. The rifle also lacked a trigger guard.<ref name="Battlefront" /> Unlike the J-19,<ref name="USW" /> however, the CJ-9 was fitted with a [[scope]] above the shaft<ref name="Battlefront" /> instead of a [[bayonet]] situated along the top edge.<ref name="USW" />

==History==
{{Quote|Only the Honor Guard of Lasan may carry a bo-rifle!|[[Garazeb Orrelios]]|audio=Zeb_bo-rifle.flac|<ref name="DiD">{{Rebels|Droids in Distress}}</ref>}}
[[File:Worried_Lasats.jpg|thumb|right|240px|The CJ-9 bo-rifle was a traditional Lasat weapon.]]
The CJ-9 was a model of bo-rifle,<ref name="Battlefront" /> a type of weapon manufactured by the [[Lasan-Malamut Firearms Corporation]] and traditionally used by the [[Lasan High Honor Guard]], the [[Planetary defense forces|defense forces]] who protected the [[Lasat]] [[homeworld]] of [[Lasan]] until the [[planet]]'s [[Siege of Lasan|pacification]] by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="R:TVG">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> The bo-rifle was renowned as a weapon that could only be obtained by defeating its wielder,<ref name="Battlefront" /> a custom that was known as the [[Boosahn Keeraw]] in Lasat culture.<ref name="THO">{{Rebels|The Honorable Ones}}</ref>

Sometime between [[19 BBY]] and [[10 BBY]],<ref name="SG note">According to ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', [[Masse Goskey]] and [[Ephant Mon]]'s [[weapon]]s purchase took place some [[time]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Mon also mentioned Goskey's [[death]] in the [[in-universe]] ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'', which later came into possession of [[Dryden Vos]], meaning Goskey died before Vos did. The weapons purchase must have therefore occurred between the Empire's formation and [[Showdown on Savareen|Vos's death]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]] and [[10 BBY]], respectively.</ref> [[Masse Goskey]], the proprietor of [[Masse Goskey's Arms Emporium and Explosives Mart]]<ref name="SG" /> in [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the planet [[Tatooine]],<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> and his associate [[Ephant Mon]] bought some weaponry on the [[moon]] of [[Nar Shaddaa]], including a CJ-9 electrified bo-rifle that came at a price of 1,100 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. A complete inventory of the merchandise was recorded in a [[book]] that would eventually be known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''. According to Goskey's note in the book, all the weapons they picked up on Nar Shaddaa were illegal in sixty-six [[sector]]s, compelling them to [[Arms dealer|sell]] the arms to crime syndicates such as the [[Crymorah Syndicate]] and the [[Black Sun]].<ref name="SG" />

At some point, a mysterious [[Scout|explorer]] compiled a [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]] of stories and images, including a sketch that depicted a Lasat wielding a CJ-9 bo-rifle against an aggressive creature. This sketch, along with many others that resembled the work of the [[Ithorian]] [[artist]] [[Gammit Chond]], was later [[Graf Archive restoration effort|restored]], expanded, and displayed by the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="AA">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> on the moon [[Orchis 2]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> at some point no earlier than [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:CJ-9_concept.jpg|thumb|left|295px|Concept art of the CJ-9 bo-rifle by Erik Rading]]
The CJ-9 bo-rifle first appeared in a [[2016]] update for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]'',<ref name="SWGO">{{WebCite|url=https://www.swgameoutpost.com/2016/12/20/cj-9-bo-rifle-battlefront|text=The CJ-9 Bo-Rifle has begun rolling out in Battlefront!|author=Jared|date=2016-12-20|work=[https://swgameoutpost.com ''The Star Wars Game Outpost'']|archivedate=20181110134925}}</ref> after being teased in trailers for the game's previous expansion packs.<ref name="Bespin DLC">{{EASWYouTube|H5HMUAlxkws|Star Wars Battlefront &ndash; Bespin Launch Trailer|date=2016-06-16}}</ref><ref name="DS DLC">{{EASWYouTube|eRtNR_1fMv4|Star Wars Battlefront: Death Star Gameplay Trailer|date=2016-09-16}}</ref> The weapon was designed by 3D artist [[Erik Rading]].<ref name="Rading">{{ArtStation|subdomain=erikrading|url=projects/Zx8Rw|name=[[Erik Rading]]|text=Star Wars Battlefront - Bo Rifle|quote=One of the weapons I made while I was doing my internship at [[DICE]]. I was also responsible for the concept.|nolive=1}}</ref> The [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]], later identified it as CJ-9 electrified bo-rifle.<ref name="SG" />

In ''Star Wars Battlefront''{{'s}} multiplayer modes, the bo-rifle is a unique weapon, as it can only be unlocked by killing another player wielding one.<ref name="Battlefront" /> Originally introduced into the game by game developers who joined multiplayer matches while equipping the CJ-9, the bo-rifle has become a virally propagating weapon since then.<ref name="SWGO" /> Once obtained, the CJ-9 can be used by [[trooper]]s from all factions, including the Galactic Empire, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], and the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
