The '''fleft-wauf''' was an entity that inspired the patterning on the collar of a [[Clothing|coat]] worn by former [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] leader [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] while he lived as a hermit on the [[planet]] [[Pasaana]].<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the fleft-wauf was first mentioned in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]. While the reference book does not specify the nature of the fleft-wauf,<ref name="TROS VD" /> it was a [[Species/Legends|creature]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, originally created for the article "[[Wind Raiders of Taloraan]]," written by [[James L. Cambias]] and published in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 5|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 5]] in [[1995]].<ref name="WROT">{{JournalCite|5|Wind Raiders of Taloraan}}</ref>
