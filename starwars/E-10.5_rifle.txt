A type of [[blaster rifle]] that was effectively an '''E-10.5 rifle''' was an interim design which incorporated elements of the earlier [[E-10 blaster rifle]] and the later [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]].<ref name="Eye Trivia 3" /> The rifle retained the longer [[barrel]] and retractable [[stock]] of the E-10 but included the optical [[rangefinder]] [[scope]] found on the E-11. While the E-11 had already become the standard issue blaster rifle of the [[Stormtrooper Corps]], many of the [[Imperial Army trooper]]s who served as part of the [[Aldhani Garrison]]<ref name="Eye" /> and [[Lieutenant]] [[Krole]]'s [[Krole's contingent|contingent]] on [[Mina-Rau]]<ref name="Harvest">{{Andor|Harvest}}</ref> were equipped with<ref name="Eye" /> the earlier [[rifle]]s.<ref name="Eye Trivia 3" /> They were also later used by [[Imperial security trooper]]s during the [[Ghorman Massacre]] in [[2 BBY]].<ref name="Who Are You" />
