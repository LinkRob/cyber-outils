The '''Frozen Kova''' was the lone [[cantina]] located on the [[planet]] [[Hynestia Prime|Hynestia]]. It was small, with only a few tables and a single bar, and patrons could enjoy games such as [[Sabacc]] with other visitors. The Frozen Kova [[Hynestian royal law|illegally]] served [[purple glandis flower juice]], [[Smuggler|smuggled]] onto the planet by individuals including [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]].<ref name="Lando's Luck" /> It also served a local dish known as [[Hynestia Spheres]].<ref name="UC">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref>
