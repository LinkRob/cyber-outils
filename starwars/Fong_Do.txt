'''Fong Do''' was a [[Nautolan]] [[Gender|male]] who lived in the [[Coruscant Underworld]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. He was close friends with the [[bounty hunter]], [[Castas]], and frequently enjoyed drinking in [[Cantina|bars]] across [[the galaxy]].

==Biography==
====Friendship with Castas====
[[File:Fong_Do.png|thumb|left|175px|Fong Do on Coruscant]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], Fong Do was close friends with the [[bounty hunter]] [[Castas]], who,<ref name="LT" /> in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|222}}</ref> contacted him from the [[planet]] [[Florrum]] following a disagreement on a job with the hunter [[Aurra Sing]]. Do reminded Castas of his warning regarding working with Sing, but Castas dismissed this and quickly revealed he had valuable information. However, before he was able to reveal it, he was shot and [[Death|killed]] by Sing.<ref name="LT" />

Later, Do recounted the event to a [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] who was a member of [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s [[Ohnaka Gang|gang]] in the [[Moshi Bar]] on the planet [[Coruscant]]. Realizing he had been overheard by the [[Jedi]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], Do drew two [[Knife|knives]] and faced Tano, but was stopped when the [[Jedi Master]] [[Plo Koon]] intervened by igniting his [[lightsaber]]. Tano reached into her [[Clothing|tunic]] and pulled out a handful of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], which she threw into the crowd, distracting the patrons and allowing her and the Jedi Master to leave the bar.<ref name="LT" />

===Jabba's Palace===
Do was later present at [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] when [[Supreme Chairman]] [[Notluwiski Papanoida]] and his son [[Ion Papanoida|Ion]] were tracking down the bounty hunter [[Greedo]], who was involved in the [[Kidnapping of the Papanoida daughters|kidnapping]] of Papanoida's daughters.<ref name="Sphere of Influence">{{TCW|Sphere of Influence}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Fong Do was a [[Nautolan]]<ref name="Episode Guide Lethal Trackdown" /> [[Gender|male]]<ref name="LT" /> who stood at 1.88 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]], 2 inches) tall.<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" /> Do had [[Color|black]] [[eye]]s and green skin.<ref name="LT" /> He was one of the many small-time crooks trying to scrape by in a tough galaxy by not worrying about remaining on the right side of the law. Do was frequently found in the grubby [[cantina]]s of [[Coruscant Underworld|Coruscant's lower levels]].<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" />

==Skills and abilities==
As a small-time crook, Do became effective at both armed and unarmed combat, as well as negotiations and intimidation.<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" />

==Equipment==
Fong Do was armed with a pair of knives.<ref name="LT" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fong Do was voiced by [[James Arnold Taylor]] in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Lethal Trackdown]]."<ref name="LT" />
