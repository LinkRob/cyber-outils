'''Cam Lindon'''<ref name="Reveal">{{YouTube|video=Xpxb4IGmZI0|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Panel at Portsmouth Comic Con 2024|channel=@FanthaTracksTV|name=Fantha Tracks TV &#124; Daily Star Wars News}}</ref> was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He used a [[Vocabulator|vocoder]] after being involved in a [[Landspeeder|hovercar]] accident on<ref name="Seeds of Starlight" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] and lost the ability to speak naturally.<ref name="Seeds of Starlight" />

==Biography==
Cam became the Padawan of [[Jedi Master]] [[Eve Byre]] following the death of her then-current Padawan, [[Zaph Mora]], in a [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] attack in [[228 BBY]]. A few weeks later, he traveled with his master, as well as [[Alcedian]] [[Jedi Initiate|younglings]] [[Tep Tep]] and [[Kildo]], on the Republic ship ''[[Innovator]]'' for a scientific mission to study the Blight. But heeding a distress call from Jedi Master [[Ackmora]], the group instead went to [[Barraza City]] on [[Eiram]] to assist in investigating a series of missing people. <ref name="Seeds of Starlight" /> Master Eve Byre was eventually taken as well, and the culprits of all of these kidnappings were revealed to be the [[Drengir]], who survived the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|crash of]] [[Starlight Beacon]] in the oceans of Eiram. Cam helped rescue Master Byre from the Drengir, taking part in the conflict against them in Barraza City, concluding in the plant-like creatures leaving Eiram aboard the ''Innovator.''<ref name="Haunted Starlight">''[[The High Republic: Haunted Starlight]]''</ref>
