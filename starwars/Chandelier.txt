'''Chandeliers''' were decorative, ornate light fixtures suspended from ceilings. Inside the trophy room of [[Gozetta]]'s [[Xirl hunting lodge|hunting lodge]] on [[Xirl]], there was a massive [[gold]] and [[glass]] chandelier.<ref name="The Nest">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Nest]]''</ref> There also existed [[chromatic chandelier]]s, which [[Repulsorlift|hovered]] and whose light cycled through variations of [[color]]s.<ref name="TRB">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref>
