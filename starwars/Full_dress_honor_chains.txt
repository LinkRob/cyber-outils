'''Full dress honor chains''' were an award system in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. Following the [[Mission to Pleknok|rescue of contraband]] at [[Pleknok]] and before the [[Vagaari pirate operations]], [[Mid Commander]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn" was awarded the [[Stybla medal]], which was put on his honor chains in the [[Stybla honoring ceremony]] held at the [[Stybla stronghold|Stybla family stronghold]] on the [[planet]] [[Naporar]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

In the aftermath of [[Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand]] over the planet [[Sunrise]], where [[Thrawn's fleet]] of Chiss vessels had destroyed [[Jixtus's fleet|Jixtus's]], the [[Chiss]] [[Admiral]] [[Dy'lothe]], along with [[Supreme Admiral]] [[Ja'fosk]], Admiral [[Ers'ikaro]], and two other [[Chiss Defense Force]] admirals, were present in a [[Tribunal of Mitth'raw'nuruodo|tribunal]] where [[Senior Captain]] Thrawn was [[Exile (punishment)|exiled]] for destroying Jixtus's fleet, with Dy'lothe removing the honor chains from Thrawn's chest.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
Full dress honor chains first appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', written by [[Timothy Zahn]] as the final novel of ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> The honor chains resemble the [[Ribbon/Legends|rows of colored bits of metal]] that denoted specific campaigns and victories worn by [[Commander/Legends|Commander]] [[Chak Fel]] of the [[Empire of the Hand]] on his regal [[Empire of the Hand dress uniform|dress uniform]] from [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, which first appeared in the [[2004]] novel ''[[Survivor's Quest]]'', also written by Zahn.<ref name="Survivor's Quest">''[[Survivor's Quest]]''</ref>
