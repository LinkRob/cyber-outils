'''Demesel''' was a [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Empire's End" /> represented by [[Senator]] [[Ghoghos]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and later in the [[Imperial Senate]]. During the war, the people of Demesel suffered and were [[Death|dying]], leading to Senator Ghoghos joining the [[Delegation of 2,000]], led by Senator [[Mon Mothma]]. When Mothma tried to gather Ghoghos' support for the [[Imperial Rebirth Act]], Ghoghos did not agree with the idea of limiting [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] power, saying that he had saved his people from destruction by ending the war.<ref name="MOF" /> Around the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Jakku]], Demesel was one of many worlds still under threat from either an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] remnant, criminal syndicate, or gang.<ref name="Empire's End" />
