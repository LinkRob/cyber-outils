The '''Auratera system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Vorzyd sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It contained the [[binary star]]s [[Aurell]] and [[Ryern]], and three [[planet]]s, including [[Auratera]], all of which orbited Aurell, the larger of the [[star]]s.<ref name="NOP" />
