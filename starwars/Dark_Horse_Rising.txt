"'''Dark Horse Rising'''" is an article written by [[Daniel Wallace]] that appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 114|''Star Wars Insider'' 114]]. It discusses how [[Dark Horse Comics]] reinvigorated the ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga after taking over from [[Marvel Comics]].<ref name="sw114" /> The article was later reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2013|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2013]].<ref name="sw2013" />
