'''Clone navigation officers''' were [[clone trooper]]s who staffed or commanded [[starship]]s in the [[Republic Navy|Galactic Republic Navy]].

==Description==
[[File:CloneNavigationOfficer-SWM34.png|thumb|left|300px|A clone navigation officer wearing a cap]]
Clone navigation [[officer]]s assisted in crewing [[Star Destroyer]]s and other [[starship]]s in the [[Republic Navy]] during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="TCW film" /> in a command or staffing role.<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> Many served onboard [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]], controlling the ship's systems. Clone navigation officers wore [[Color|gray]] uniforms with black boots and gloves as well as a belt. They also wore [[rank insignia plaque]]s on their chests.<ref name="TCW film" /> Some also wore kepi caps<ref name="Victory and Death" /> or a headset.<ref name="ARC Troopers">{{TCW|ARC Troopers (episode)}}</ref> During the early days of the [[Galactic Empire]], they also wore black uniforms and caps, similar to other [[Imperial officer]]s.<ref name="Return to Kamino" />
