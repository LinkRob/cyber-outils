The '''Andor team''' was the name of a [[Fire team|team]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] that was led by [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor]]. It initially only consisted of Captain Andor, the [[prisoner]] [[Jyn Erso]], and the [[droid]] [[K-2SO]]. After a [[Battle on Jedha|mission to Jedha]], the team acquired three allies. These were the two [[Guardians of the Whills]] [[Chirrut Îmwe]] and [[Baze Malbus]], along with the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] defector [[Bodhi Rook]].<ref name="Heroes" />

Once the Andor team returned to [[Yavin 4]], not only had they located the [[Death Star plans]], but they also discovered that Imperial [[scientist]] [[Galen Walton Erso]] placed a flaw in its design, allowing for its destruction. Despite the information, [[Alliance High Command|Rebel Alliance High Command]] refused to authorize any operations, and [[Sergeant]] [[Ruescott Melshi]] contacted [[Lieutenant]] [[Taidu Sefla]] to be part of an assault unit that was tasked with retrieving the plans. This group, composed of the Andor team and other Alliance [[special forces]] units, came to create [[Rogue One]].<ref name="Heroes" />
