[[Lord]] '''Espis Zanchal''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[baron]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]]. Zanchal resided in the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]'s [[Zanchal Estate]], where he kept several [[prisoner]]s and fed them to his [[Espis Zanchal's narglatch|domesticated]] [[narglatch]] [[cat]]. In [[3 ABY]], the former [[handmaiden]] [[Sabé]] planted a false list of members of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate, including Zanchal, in [[Crimson Dawn Sub-Headquarters|one]] of the syndicate's bases that was being [[Attack on the Crimson Dawn Sub-Headquarters|attacked]] by the forces of [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. After retrieving the list, the [[Sith Lord]] sent his [[Vader's crew|crew]] of [[assassin]]s to [[Death|eliminate]] him at his estate.

Skeptical about the assassins' claim that he was an associate of Crimson Dawn, Zanchal ordered his [[Espis Zanchal's droid|droid]] to give them access to every room of his residence. Upon seeing the narglatch and the prisoners, one of the assassins, [[Ankala Sahm]], asked the baron if he thought she cared about his connection with Crimson Dawn. The Imperial lord then [[Commanding officer|commanded]] his guards to eliminate the assassins; however, the latter group killed Zanchal and his forces.

==Biography==
===Confrontation with Vader's crew===
{{Quote|<nowiki />''Baron Espis Zanchal''. You've been exposed as a ''Crimson Dawn'' collaborator.''"<br />"''Wh-what? ''No''!|Ankala Sahm and Baron Espis Zanchal|<ref name="Vader 20" />}}
[[File:EspisZanchalStandard-2020DarthVader20.png|thumb|right|300px|Zanchal gave access to his entire estate to Vader's crew, showing them his narglatch.]]
[[Baron]] Espis Zanchal was a [[human]] [[lord]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]]. He resided in the [[Zanchal Estate]] on the<ref name="Vader 20" /> [[planet]]<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> [[Coruscant]], keeping his [[prisoner]]s and his [[Espis Zanchal's narglatch|domesticated]] [[narglatch]]<ref name="Vader 20" /> [[cat]]<ref name="Talz">{{Databank|talz|Talz}}</ref> in a room of his estate.<ref name="Vader 20" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date19">{{OutlawsComicDate|Darth Vader (2020) 19|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 19}}</ref> the former [[handmaiden]] [[Sabé]] planted a list of operatives of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate that included<ref name="Vader 21" /> thirty-two<ref name="Vader 29">[[Darth Vader (2020) 29|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 29]]</ref> individuals who served the Galactic Empire on a [[computer]] within<ref name="Vader 21">[[Darth Vader (2020) 21|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 21]]</ref> the [[Crimson Dawn Sub-Headquarters]]<ref name="Vader 20" /> on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] astronomical object [[Laecor]].<ref name="Vader 21" /> Zanchal was mentioned in the list, and the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] sent his [[Vader's crew|crew]] of [[assassin]]s&mdash;consisting of [[Ankala Sahm]], [[G-90|Gee-Ninety]], [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]], [[S'ira]], and [[Tanka]]&mdash;to [[Death|kill]] him at the Zanchal Estate after retrieving the list.<ref name="Vader 20" />

As Zanchal drank a glass of a beverage, he instructed his [[Espis Zanchal's droid|droid]] to prepare to feed his narglatch. However, they were interrupted when the assassins entered the baron's residence through a window. Sahm announced that the baron had been exposed as a Crimson Dawn operative, but Zanchal claimed he was not, ordering his droid to give the assassins access to his estate's rooms.<ref name="Vader 20" />

===A standard narglatch===
{{Quote|Nothing in the records show any affiliation with any known Crimson Dawn asset.''"<br />"''Oh, the baron wasn't lying. Everything was entirely above board.|Gee-Ninety and Espis Zanchal's droid|<ref name="Vader 20" />}}
[[File:EspisZanchalGuards-2020DarthVader20.png|thumb|left|270px|Zanchal's guards fired at the assassins under his directive.]]
The droid revealed the narglatch's chamber, and Zanchal remarked that it was a standard narglatch that had been registered and certified. Sahm asked him if he thought she "gave a damn" about his allegiance to Crimson Dawn, and the Imperial lord commanded his guards to fire at Vader's crew; however, the assassins killed Zanchal and his guards, freeing the baron's captives. Gee-Ninety accessed the Zanchal Estate's records to confirm Zanchal's affiliation with Crimson Dawn, discovering that they did not establish any connection between the baron and the criminal syndicate.<ref name="Vader 20" />

In response, Zanchal's droid confirmed that everything the lord had said was truthful. Shortly after, Vader arrived at the Zanchal Estate, and the baron's droid told him that the Imperial lord was "unavailable."<ref name="Vader 20" /> After Zanchal's death, Vader's crew traveled to the Imperial<ref name="Vader 21" /> [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]]<ref name="USNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> ''[[Executor]]'', where they celebrated their victory. One of Vader's assassins, [[Chilla Zin]], was uneasy about killing the baron, as the [[Assassin's Guild|Assassins Guild]], which verified assassinations, had not verified Zanchal's. Tanka countered that the baron deserved his death because he fed people to a narglatch while Zin asked if it was fair that his narglatch went hungry.<ref name="Vader 21" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|<nowiki />''Baron Zanchal'' was feeding people to a ''narglatch''. I think he got what was coming to him.|Tanka|<ref name="Vader 21" />}}
A human [[Gender|male]], the baron was willing to feed his prisoners to his narglatch. The baron had [[Color|light]] skin, brown, graying [[hair]], and blue eyes.<ref name="Vader 20" />

==Equipment==
Zanchal wore a blue [[Clothing|scarf]], a black suit, and black shoes. While ordering his guards to open fire, the baron held a [[blaster]]. The Imperial lord's droid had black plating.<ref name="Vader 20" />
