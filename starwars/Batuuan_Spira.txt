'''Batuuan Spira''' was the ancient [[currency]] of [[Black Spire Outpost]]. Around [[34 ABY]], the Spira took the form of a hexagonal chip of either dull yellow or silvery metal. Its obverse face bore the word "Spira" and the letters "BSO" for Black Spire Outpost in [[Aurebesh]] above three columns and a half-circle with a smaller complete circle resting on its edge.<ref name="DFB Spira">{{WebCite|url=http://www.disneyfoodblog.com/2019/05/31/seriously-we-suggest-you-buy-this-when-visiting-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Seriously: We Suggest You Buy THIS When Visiting Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|work=[http://www.disneyfoodblog.com Disney Food Blog]|archivedate=20190601221904}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The real&ndash;world version of the Batuuan Spira is a gift card that can be purchased for a $100 minimum plus a $4.99 activation fee, first made available at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[Disneyland]] upon the theme park area's opening on [[May 31]]st, [[2019]]. It can be used wherever a standard [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] Gift Card is accepted.

The original version of the Spira available from the opening of Galaxy's Edge was dull yellow or golden in color, but it was discontinued and replaced by a silver version on [[September 1]]st, [[2020]].<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2020/08/photos-new-silver-batuuan-spira-coming-september-1st-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge-at-disneys-hollywood-studios|text=NEW Silver Batuuan Spira Coming September 1st to Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge at Disney's Hollywood Studios|work=[https://wdwnt.com/ WDW News Today]|archivedate=20200901053426}}</ref> The silver Spira introduced the added $4.99 activation fee.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2020/09/photos-new-silver-batuuan-spira-disney-gift-card-available-for-5-with-100-minimum-charge-in-star-wars-galaxys-edge-at-disneys-hollywood-studios|text=PHOTOS: New Silver Batuuan Spira Disney Gift Card Available for $5 with $100 Minimum Charge in Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge at Disney's Hollywood Studios|work=[https://wdwnt.com/ WDW News Today]|archivedate=20201219073640}}</ref> A copper version was introduced on May 4, 2024.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2024/05/new-batuuan-spira-disney-gift-card-at-star-wars-galaxys-edge-in-disneys-hollywood-studios/|text=New Batuuan Spira Disney Gift Card at Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge in Disney's Hollywood Studios
