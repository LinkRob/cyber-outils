The [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Religion|religous sect]] had a '''temple''' in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] of the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. The Brothers also lived in the temple, which was underground and could be accessed from a door on a side street. It had [[vine]]s that acted as a defense system, because they could grow onto and restrain intruders. A Brother named [[Mytion]] wished to leave the temple and build a new one among the other religions based in the [[city]].<ref name="TBoJ script">[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha (script)|''The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha'' script]]</ref> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> [[Tilson Graf]] of the [[Graf family]] visited the temple to ask the Brothers to kill [[Jedi Master]]s [[Silandra Sho]] and [[Creighton Sun]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Aida Forte]].<ref name="TBoJ" />
