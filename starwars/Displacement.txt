'''Displacement''' is an article written by [[August Hahn]] and [[Cynthia Hahn]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[November 18]], [[2004]] and related the [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunters]] [[Caranna]] and [[Tendin Vought]] receiving a job. However, neither hunter was prepared to work with the other and ultimately Caranna incapacitated Vought with poison and took on the job herself.

In the context of the Living Force campaign, it marked the availability of certain items from the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game Revised Core Rulebook]]''.
