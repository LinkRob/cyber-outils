The '''EOD-Mk IV explosives disposal remote''' was a model of [[droid]] [[remote]] manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]] for use in construction, demolitions, and explosives ordnance disposal. They were small, spherical, and designed to be operated from a distance via [[datapad]]. EOD-Mk IV remotes could also operate autonomously, traveling in groups with preprogrammed orders. They were equipped with a built-in [[explosives ordnance disposal kit]], an on-board [[demolitions scanner]] that could detect explosive compounds and devices, a small explosive charge that could detonate other explosive devices, and two manipulator [[Limb|arms]]. They cost 500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and were usually operated by [[explosives ordnance disposal trooper]]s or [[demolitions operator]]s.<ref name="Fully Operational" />
