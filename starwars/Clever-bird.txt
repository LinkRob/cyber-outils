'''Clever-birds''' were a type of squawking [[bird]] that lived in the [[Great Basin|Canyon of Akar]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="ALD" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> the Akivan [[Homeworld|native]] and [[pilot]] [[Norra Wexley]] thought about the noise made by the canyon's clever-birds and compared it to the relative silence of the planet [[Kashyyyk]] while visiting there.<ref name="ALD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clever-birds were mentioned in ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', a [[2016]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the second installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="ALD" />
