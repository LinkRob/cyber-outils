[[File:Shield_must_be_deactivated.png|thumb|right|250px|A holographic representation of the energy shield protecting the Death Star II]]
'''Deflector shields''', also known as '''deflector screens''', '''security deflector shields''', '''security shields''', '''force shields''', or simply '''shields''' were energy fields that could protect [[starship]]s,<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[battle station]]s,<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> ground-based structures,<ref name="Episode V" /> [[soldier]]s,<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref> and even [[Army|armies]] from enemy assault,<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> with certain planets even protected by massive [[planetary shield]]s.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> Several varieties of deflector shields existed, including [[ray shield]]s, which deflected or scattered energy (although they were not always powerful enough to fully negate enemy fire), and [[particle shield]]s, which diffused impacts from high-velocity projectiles and proton weapons. A third type of shield, a concussion shield, repelled space debris and other solid objects. Most starships utilized a combination of ray and particle shielding for maximum protection, while larger ships could require multiple projectors to fully protect the ship, with a shield's intensity gradually diminishing with distance from its projector.<ref name="Cross">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>

Some shields were one-way shields that only allowed a person to come in through the shield, but prevented passage back out through the shield. [[Droideka]]s had a built-in shield that deflected normal blaster fire but could not stop solid objects such as a [[thermal detonator]]s or people from passing through, so long as they moved slowly enough.<ref name="The Soft War">{{TCW|A War on Two Fronts}}</ref>

After the "dreadnought era" ended with the development of cutting-edge [[turbolaser]]s that could tear apart [[ray shield]]s, [[battleship]]s only returned to the [[Hyperspace route|spacelanes]] after [[Verpine]] shipbuilders revolutionized deflector shield design, only to fall out of favor once again amid economic strains and Republic restrictions on capital ships larger than modern cruisers.<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>

[[File:MornaKee-SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|The deflector shield protecting a VT-49 Decimator from enemy fire]]
Some uses of deflector-shield technology included the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' shield around its [[Separatist Droid Army|droid forces]] during the [[Battle of Christophsis]], preventing the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] from attacking until the shield generator was destroyed.<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> The ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', a [[light freighter]], also had a deflector shield,<ref name="Episode IV" /> while the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]] had a deflector shield powered by a [[SLD-26 planetary shield generator|deflector dish]] on the [[Tree|forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]].<ref name="Episode VI" />

During the time of the [[Clone Wars]], the militaries of the [[Unknown Regions]] used [[electrostatic barrier]]s similar in purpose to the deflector shields, but much less powerful.<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> This led [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] to bring a [[deflector shield generator]] he termed a "Republic energy shield" from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Mokivj droid factory|droid factory]] on [[Mokivj]] back to his [[Chiss|people]] after assisting [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] on his [[mission to Batuu]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> Thrawn would later use the "Republic energy shield" in the [[Battle over Primea]] to help defeat the [[Nikardun]] [[General]] [[Yiv]] the Benevolent, thus ending the [[Nikardun conquests]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> The [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] [[Steadfast (Chiss heavy cruiser)|''Steadfast'']] under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] of the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] was equipped with deflector shields during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref>

[[File:Personal_Energy_Shield_BF.png|thumb|right|250px|A Rebel soldier using a personal energy shield]]
[[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Military|troops]] used portable shields that could be deployed on any surface. The shield was projected by a small generator and formed a dome that could protect several individuals. The portable shield could deflect any blaster fire coming from outside or inside. However, because it was designed as a shield for [[Infantry|ground troops]], it allowed solid objects and people to pass through it. A well-thrown detonator could destroy the generator and anything inside the shield. The shield also had a limited durability, weakening when shot, with the color of the shield eventually changing from blue to orange and finally red before disappearing as the generator overheated.<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront" />

Some types of deflector shields could not be used under specific environmental circumstances. The [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyer]], for example, could not activate its shields while parked inside the electrically-charged [[atmosphere]] of the planet [[Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

The [[Resistance]] [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] was equipped with experimental deflector shielding. These shields were said to be far stronger than regular ones and took the noticeable appearance of a normally invisible bubble around the ship.<ref name="ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
