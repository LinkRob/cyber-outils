The '''Cyphar system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Spirva sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square L-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It contained the [[planet]] [[Cyphar]], [[homeworld]] of the [[Cyphari]].<ref name="Thrawn" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Cyphar system originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, and was recanonized when it was included in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
