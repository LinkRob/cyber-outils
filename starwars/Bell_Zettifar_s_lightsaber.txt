During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Padawan|Jedi Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] utilized a [[Color|green]]-bladed '''lightsaber'''.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

==History==
Bell Zettifar used his [[lightsaber]] sometime during the [[emergences]] on [[Elphrona]] to defend himself and others during a [[Raid on Elphrona|raid]] on the [[planet]] perpetrated by the [[Nihil]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

[[Standard month|Months]] after the attack on Elphrona, Zettifar used his lightsaber on [[Valo]] during a Nihil [[Valo Atrocity|attack]] during the [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair being held on the planet]] to save some trapped civilians from drowning on the [[Innovator]] which was sinking due to the attack. Later, he used his lightsaber to cut some fallen debris to allow a shuttle carrying [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]]'s [[Gender|son]] [[Kitrep Soh]] to leave the submerged ship. As Zettifar held onto the shuttle's hull as it left the hangar, he lost hold of his lightsaber, leaving it submerged inside the Innovator.<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>
