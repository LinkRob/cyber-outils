{{Quote|Your reputation precedes you, General&mdash;the reputation of a coward and a murderer.''"<br />"''<nowiki></nowiki>''Murderer?'' Is it murder to rid the galaxy of you Jedi filth?|Eeth Koth and General Grievous|audio=CowardAndMurderer-GI.flac|<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />}}
In [[21 BBY]], during the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeth Koth]] was '''captured''' by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] during a battle within the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[Saleucami system]]. Assaulted by a [[Grievous's Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class light destroyer]] [[Flagship|flagged]] by the [[Kaleesh]] [[cyborg]], Koth's flagship, the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Venator-class)|''Steadfast'']], was boarded by the general and his [[battle droid]] forces. Upon boarding, the [[B2-series super battle droid]]s engaged Koth's [[clone trooper]] [[Eeth Koth's unit|forces]], while Grievous and a group of [[BX-series droid commando]]s and [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s infiltrated the command [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] to attack the Jedi Master.

On the bridge, the Jedi Master's accompanying [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s were [[Death|killed]] in the ensuing battle, as well as Grievous's commando [[droid]]s. Soon thereafter, the Separatist general entered [[Lightsaber combat|combat]] with the Jedi Master in a brief [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] aboard the bridge. During the duel, Grievous managed to gain the upperhand with his MagnaGuards, which led to Koth's subsequent capture. Afterwards, the Jedi Master was displayed in the Kaleesh's message to the [[Jedi High Council]] in order to provoke the [[Jedi Order]] into launching an [[Battle of Saleucami|assault]] on Grievous's [[fleet]] over the [[planet]] [[Saleucami]].

==Prelude==
In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="21 BBY">{{TCWcanondates|209}} "[[The Clone Wars Begin]]" establishes that the attacks on [[hyperspace route]]s near the [[Outer Rim Territories]] occurred before the events of "Grievous Intrigue," but after the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to 21 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. Therefore, the attacks must occur in 21 BBY as well.</ref> following the [[Galactic Republic]]'s second [[Second Battle of Geonosis|invasion and capture]] of the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], a [[Attacks on hyperspace routes|series of attacks]] were launched against several [[hyperspace route]]s throughout the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. In response, [[Jedi General]] [[Eeth Koth]] and his Republic [[Eeth Koth's fleet|fleet]] proceeded to track down the conspirator behind the mysterious attacks.<ref name="Clone Wars" /> However, in that same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Timelines" /> as the [[Jedi Master]] traveled through the [[Saleucami system]]<ref name="Lightsabers" /> in deep Outer Rim [[Realspace|space]],<ref name="EpGuide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode209.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Grievous Intrigue}}</ref> aboard his [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[flagship]]<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> [[Steadfast (Venator-class)|''Steadfast'']],<ref name="USWNE" /> Koth and his [[fleet]] came under a surprise attack<ref name="Clone Wars" /> by a Separatist fleet of a single [[Grievous's Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class light destroyer]] and three [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]] led by [[General]] [[Grievous]],<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> who had decided to target members of the [[Jedi High Council]].<ref name="Deserter">{{TCW|The Deserter}}</ref>

==The capture==
===Separatist boarding attack===
{{Quote|Get yourselves to the escape pods. I will deal with Grievous here.''"<br />"''But, sir, they're commando droids.''"<br />"''That does not matter, Captain. Now go.|Eeth Koth and Lock, during the firefight with Grievous's battle droids|<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />}}
[[File:LockUnderAttack-GI.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Eeth Koth's clone troopers, battling Grievous's battle droids aboard the ''Steadfast''.]]
As Koth's fleet came under ambush from Grievous's [[command ship]] and accompanying [[frigate]]s, with the latter remaining away, the [[Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class light destroyer]] and ''Steadfast'' began to trade a barrage of [[turbolaser]] fire against eithers' starboard [[hull]]s during broadside engagements, extensively damaging both vessels in the process. While under fire, Grievous's [[warship]] proceeded to closely approach the ''Steadfast''{{'s}} starboard side, in preparation to attach an armored [[Docking tube|boarding tube]] with one of the Republic vessel's docking ports. Preparing to board the Star Destroyer, General Koth, aboard the [[Bridge (starship)|command bridge]], instructed his onboard [[Eeth Koth's unit|unit]] of [[clone trooper]]s, [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Clone Captain]]<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> [[Lock (Clone Captain)|Lock]],<ref name="EpGuide" /> to standby as Grievous began to board with his flagship. With the entry door reinforced by Lock and several other [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s, the Separatist general deployed multiple groups of [[B2-series super battle droid]]s and [[BX-series droid commando]]s into the ''Steadfast'' to attack the clone forces.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

While the onboard engagement ensued on the warship's lower levels, [[Captain]] Lock was contacted by the [[Jedi]] General from aboard the bridge, who ordered him and his remaining clone troopers to make their way to the [[escape pod]]s and evacuate the ''Steadfast''. Though Koth stated his intentions of withholding the responsibility of [[Lightsaber combat|combating]] Grievous on the bridge, the Clone Captain attempted to reason with the Jedi, but Koth insisted he evacuate. Upon his command, Koth's communications with Lock were subsequently disabled as the Separatist commander arrived outside the [[blast door]]s to the command center on the bridge. Outside the bridge, the Jedi Master and his accompanying clone troopers assumed defensive positions as Grievous breached the blast doors with his [[lightsaber]]s. Cutting a circular hole in the doors, the Separatist commander forcefully entered the bridge as he kicked the penetrated door pieces open, sending them into an unsuspecting clone trooper.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

===Duel aboard the bridge===
{{Quote|Eeth Koth, isn't it? I've been looking forward to meeting you.|Grievous, meeting Eeth Koth for the first time|<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />}}
[[File:Koth_torture.png|thumb|right|300px|During his duel Grievous, Eeth Koth was electrocuted by IG-100 MagnaGuards.]]
Entering the command bridge, a [[squad]] of [[droid]] [[commando]]s followed through the doors and attacked the Jedi and his troopers. Killing several of the troopers and the two [[Clone navigation officer|navigation officers]] on the bridge, the four [[battle droid]]s focused their efforts on the Jedi Master, resulting in Koth being wounded as one of the droids shot his arm, which caused him to drop his lightsaber, but not before he destroyed three of the droids. However, as the remaining droid attempted to eliminate the Jedi Master, a remaining clone trooper prevented the droid from following through as he blasted it. With the commando droids destroyed, the surviving trooper was killed upon the entry of a group of four [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s, which proceeded to surround Koth as Grievous leapt onto the communications table.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

As Grievous entered conversation with Koth, the Separatist general announced his desire to meet the Jedi Master in person, who proceeded to deride Grievous for his cowardly and murderous reputation. To his derision, the Separatist leader pondered if the extermination of the [[Jedi Order]] was to be considered murder, activating his lightsabers in response and kicking Koth backwards, igniting a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] aboard the bridge. Forced back towards the bridge's [[viewport]]s by Grievous's attacks of parries and various swings, who was closely followed by the MagnaGuards, the general created ample distance from the Jedi Master, who launched the [[cyborg]] into the viewports with [[the Force]].<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

However, while Grievous recovered and Koth attempted to strike at the cyborg, the general's MagnaGuards intervened as they electrocuted the Jedi with their [[electrostaff]]s, allowing Grievous to enter a brief saberlock with Koth while the battle droids continued electrocuting him. Weakened by the numerous strikes from the MagnaGuards, Koth soon fell to his knees, which gave Grievous the opportunity to hold the Jedi by his neck and lift him from the ground.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> Unusually, Grievous decided against killing the Jedi Master, as was the general's common procedure,<ref name="Clone Wars" /> but instead chose to take ownership of the Jedi's lightsaber.<ref name="Lightsabers" /> Following his defeat, the Jedi Master was transported aboard Grievous's command ship, where he was held as a hostage and eventually brought to the planet [[Saleucami]].<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Greetings, Jedi. It would seem once again one of your Order has lost his way. And even better, a leading member of your Jedi Council. Listen to me, Jedi. I do not care about your politics. I do not care about your Republic. I only live to see you die!|Grievous, denouncing the Jedi Order|audio=LiveToSeeYouDie-GI.flac|<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />}}
[[File:JediHighCouncilGrievousTransmission-GrievousIntrigue.jpg|thumb|left|300px|After capturing Eeth Koth, Grievous transmitted a message to the Jedi Order on Coruscant.]]
Shortly after Grievous's capture of Koth, the [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] [[warlord]] transmitted a [[Hologram|holographic]] message to the Jedi Order's [[Imperial Palace|headquarters]] on<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> the planet<ref name="USWNE" /> [[Coruscant]],<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> the [[capital]] of the Republic.<ref name="USWNE" /> In his message, viewed by several Jedi, such as the [[Jedi High Council]] and [[Jedi Initiate|younglings]], displaying his defeat of the Jedi Master, whom Grievous stated was a prized capture due to Koth's service as a member of the Jedi Council, the Separatist commander expressed his disdain for the [[Force-sensitive]] organization. Furthermore, Grievous declared that his involvement in the war was not of care for either [[politics]] or the Republic, but because of his desire to kill the Jedi. However, as the Jedi Master was [[torture]]d by a MagnaGuard, the general then explained that Koth would not be killed, stating his utilization as a tool for fear and anguish, understanding that his suffering would pain the Jedi.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

Upon the transmission's end,<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> Republic [[104th Battalion]]<ref name="Rising Malevolence">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]], designated CC-3636, discovered a hidden message within the recording, which revealed Grievous's location in the Saleucami system, at [[Suolriep sector|Sector J-19]]; coordinates zero-eight, zero-five, two-nine. Although under the impression that [[Republic Intelligence]] suggested that neither Grievous or Separatist [[Confederacy military|forces]] were located in the Suolriep [[sector]], the Republic ordered the immediate dispatchment of a [[Skywalker's fleet|task force]] to Saleucami to [[Battle of Saleucami|retrieve]] General Koth from Grievous as well as capture the Separatist leader. Led by Jedi Generals [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Adi Gallia]], the Republic [[task force]] left shortly after, planning to distract Grievous, who had anticipated and planned for a rescue mission, with Kenobi in order for Skywalker and Gallia to board his destroyer and rescue Koth.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Eeth_Koth_TCW.png|thumb|right|300px|The original plot for "Grievous Intrigue" saw Eeth Koth's death at Grievous's hands.]]
The capture of Eeth Koth was introduced in "[[Grievous Intrigue]],"<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> the ninth episode of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series's [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]], which aired on [[January 1]], [[2010]].<ref name="EpGuide" /> In the original plot for the episode, Grievous was set to have killed Eeth Koth during the battle. However, the plot was changed to have Koth captured by Grievous, instead, in order for the character's potential appearance in future episodes as desired by series Director, [[Dave Filoni]].<ref name="GI commentary">{{SW|url=video/grievous-intrigue-episode-featurette|text="Grievous Intrigue" Episode Featurette}}</ref> In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the battle was stated to have been fought near the astronomical object of [[Arda]] in the [[The Official Star Wars Fact File Part 107|107th issue]] of [[The Official Star Wars Fact File (2014)|''The Official Star Wars Fact File'']], a magazine series published by [[De Agostini]] in [[2014]].<ref name="Fact File">{{FactFile2014|107|KOT 1-4|Eeth Koth}}</ref> However, ''[[Star Wars Lightsabers: A Guide to Weapons of the Force]]'', a reference book authored by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="Lightsabers" /> and published on [[April 10]], [[2018]],<ref name="Quarto">{{Quarto|url=books/9780760355404/Star-Wars-Lightsabers.html|text=Star Wars Lightsabers}}</ref> altered its location to the [[Saleucami system]],<ref name="Lightsabers" /> which the article assumes to be the correct location due to ''Star Wars Lightsabers: A Guide to Weapons of the Force'' being the more recent source.

An image of the duel between Grievous and Koth was later used in the [[Star Wars: Build Your Own X-Wing 47|forty-seventh issue]] of De Agostini's ''[[Star Wars: Build Your Own X-Wing]]'' magazine series, published in [[2020]], which was erroneously used to depict the [[battle of Patitite Pattuna]] and Grievous's capture of Jedi Master [[Adi Gallia]]<ref name="X-Wing 47">{{BuildXWingCite|47|Starfighter Aces}}</ref> in "[[Nomad Droids]],"<ref name="Nomad">{{TCW|Nomad Droids}}</ref> the sixth episode of the series's [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|fourth season]], which aired on [[October 14]], [[2011]].<ref name="Episode Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref> In the [[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2 57|fifty-seventh issue]] of De Agostini's ''[[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2]]'' magazine series, published in 2018, Eeth Koth's capture was described as occurring over Saleucami,<ref name="R2-D2 57">{{BuildR2Cite|57|Droid Directory}}</ref> which the article assumes an error due to "Grievous Intrigue" displaying its placement in open space,<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> rather than above Saleucami, coupled with the establishment of its occurrence in the Saleucami system by ''Star Wars Lightsabers: A Guide to Weapons of the Force''<ref name="Lightsabers" /> and deep Outer Rim space by the corresponding episode guide.<ref name="EpGuide" /> The error would later be repeated in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'', which claimed the battle was fought "at" Saleucami.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref>

In the subsequent Battle of Saleucami, Grievous duels Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi aboard the [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]] ''[[Surrogator]]''.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" /> However, ''[[The Star Wars Book]]'', published by [[Dorling Kindersley]]<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> on [[October 20]], 2020,<ref name="Publisher">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/636357/the-star-wars-book-by-cole-horton|text=The Star Wars Book}}</ref> incorrectly establishes that their duel took place aboard the ''Steadfast''.<ref name="TSWB" /> Previously, in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Capture of Eeth Koth/Legends|battle]] was established by ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: New Battlefronts: The Visual Guide]]'', a reference book authored by [[Jason Fry]]<ref name="New Battlefronts">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: New Battlefronts: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> and published in [[August]]<ref name="August">{{SWArchive|url=vault/books/news2010033/index.html|text=First Look: ''New Battlefronts: The Visual Guide''}}</ref> 2010, to have been fought near the world of [[Arda II|Arda]]<ref name="New Battlefronts" /> of the [[Gordian Reach/Legends|Gordian Reach]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
