'''Benath'''<ref name="SWGalaxyMap" /> was a rocky<ref name="Age of Resistance" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|species=[[Benathy]]|homeworld=Benath}}</ref> that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Benathy]].<ref name="Age of Resistance" /> It was located in the [[Benath system]] in grid square I-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], which was considered part of [[Wild Space]]<ref name="Appendix" /> though it fell in the usual borders of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was connected to the<ref name="SWGalaxyMap">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> planet<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> [[Ansion]] and the [[Utegetu Nebula]] by a [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="SWGalaxyMap" />

On Benath, the Benathy were ruled by a [[Benathy kingdom|monarchy]] and worshiped a [[Zillo Beast]] as their [[Deity|god]]. The [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[stormtrooper]]s under [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] were able to [[Battle against the Benathy|subdue]] the Benathy by killing [[Unidentified Benathy king|their king]], but could not defeat the Zillo Beast.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />

The [[First Order]]'s [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] under Vader's grandson, [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] demanded that the current king, [[Kristoff]] end their [[Benathy expansion|expansion]]. Ren also demanded them to submit to the First Order. When Kristoff refused, Ren killed him. The Benathy [[Battle against the Benathy (First Order)|fought]] Ren's forces until he defeated and killed the Zillo Beast. The Benathy bowed to Ren and chose to worship the First Order.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />
