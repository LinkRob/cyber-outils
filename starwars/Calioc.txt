'''Calioc''' was a [[Duros]] [[Gender|female]] and a fan of [[podracing]]. She was the benefactor of the [[podracer]] [[Korlen Skimmer/Canon|Korlen Skimmer]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] but became fearful of her her investment in Skimmer when the [[Galactic Empire]] took an interest in Skimmer. She hired an investigator to determine the nature of the Empire's threats but refused to pull Skimmer from a race when her agents warned of foul-play.<ref name="EV" />
