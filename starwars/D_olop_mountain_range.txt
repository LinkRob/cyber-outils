The '''D'olop mountain range''' was a [[mountain]] range located on the [[agriworld]] [[M'haeli]] in the [[Expansion Region]]. Fragile [[dragite gem]]s were [[Mining|mined]] in the mountain range, and used in [[lightsaber]]s, where they could produce shockwaves when the blade struck an object.<ref name="GaG" />
