"'''An Exclusive Interview with George Lucas'''" is an article in [[Star Wars Insider 26|''Star Wars Insider'' 26]] in [[1995]]. It is a transcript of Lucas's question-and-answer session from the Second ''Star Wars'' Summit that was held at [[Skywalker Ranch]] on [[April 20]].

He notably answers the questions about the [[Sequel trilogy]] - stating he'll think about it after completing the prequel trilogy. The sequel trilogy was later officially announced in [[2012]] when Lucas sold [[Lucasfilm]] to Disney.
