A '''brig''' was the compartment of a [[starship]] or military base where [[prisoner]]s were confined. The [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Carida Academy|Academy]] at [[Carida]] had a brig in which [[Cadet]] [[Han Solo]] spent time after attempting to steal a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]].<ref name="HSIC">[[Han Solo - Imperial Cadet 2|''Han Solo - Imperial Cadet'' 2]]</ref> The [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']], the [[flagship]] of [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[Resistance]], had one,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> and so did some of the [[transport]]s used by the [[Resistance navy]].<ref name="Episode VIII junior novel">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>

[[Tipoca City]] on [[Kamino]] had a brig where [[Clone Force 99]] were held as prisoners just after the [[Proclamation of the New Order]].<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref>
