'''Andimor Gray'''<ref name="BMF 76">{{BuildFalconCite|76|Starship Fact File}}</ref> was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who served as the [[captain]] of [[Mandalorian]] [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]]'s luxury [[Nau'ur-class yacht|''Nau'ur''-class yacht]], the ''[[Coronet]]''. During a flight to [[Coruscant]]<ref name="Voyage of Temptation" /> in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> he was killed by the traitorous [[Senator]] [[Tal Merrik]], who was in league with the Mandalorian [[terrorist]] group [[Death Watch]].<ref name="Voyage of Temptation" />
