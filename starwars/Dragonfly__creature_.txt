'''Dragonflies''' were a type of [[insect]]. Whilst thinking of the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]'s [[bombardment of Mek'tradi]], [[Yrica Quell]] noted that they had chased [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[X-wing starfighter]]s in loops and burned ascending [[shuttle]]s like children tearing wings from dragonflies.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
