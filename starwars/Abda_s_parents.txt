Two [[Kage]] were the '''parents''' of a Kage [[Abda's brother|brother]] and [[Abda|sister]], the latter of whom eventually changed her name to Abda. The [[parent]]s resided on their [[homeworld]] [[Quarzite]] with their [[Youngling|children]] as part of a [[Abda's clan|clan]] and trained their offspring in combat training. When the pair's [[Gender|son]] got [[Marriage|married]] to their [[Abda's brother's spouse|spouse]] at a large [[Abda's brother's wedding|wedding]], their sister was upset with how her parents treated her and her brother disparately and opted to leave Quarzite. In [[382 BBY]], Abda reflected on her negative feelings toward her upbringing and parents' combat training.

==Biography==
[[File:Quarzitecrystals.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Abda's parents lived on Quarzite with their children.]]
Two [[Kage]] were the [[parent]]s of a Kage [[Abda's brother|son]] and a [[Abda|daughter]] in a [[Abda's clan|clan]] on their [[homeworld]], the [[crystal]]line [[Planet|world]] [[Quarzite]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the parents instructed their [[Youngling|children]] in combat training to follow and uphold the [[Soldier|warrior]] traditions that their culture expected of them. At some point, the [[Gender|son]] got [[Marriage|married]] to their [[Abda's brother's spouse|spouse]], with their [[Abda's brother's wedding|wedding]] being one of the largest their clan&mdash;including the parents&mdash;had hosted.<ref name="Convergence" />

The daughter left her homeworld on the [[Local day|day]] of the brother's wedding, unsettled by the knowledge that she would never be as good of a fighter as her brother in her parents' [[eye]]s. She chose to take the name of Abda to symbolically distance herself from her former life, feeling that if she had remained, she would have been expected to follow the same path as her parents and brother in life. After becoming part of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]], Abda came to resent how harshly her parents had treated her compared to her brother, reflecting that [[the galaxy]] was often harder on daughters.<ref name="Convergence" />

While infiltrating a pre-wedding party in the planet [[E'ronoh]]'s [[E'ronoh palace|royal palace]]<ref name="Convergence" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Abda thought of her brother's wedding, remembering her distaste for her family's event and those like it. After being detained for attempting to [[poison]] the [[E'roni]] [[Princess]] [[Xiri A'lbaran]] that [[Standard day|day]], she was held in E'ronoh's [[capital]] [[city]]&mdash;[[the Rook]]. When Path of the Open Hand operative [[Axel Greylark]] attacked her with [[Eirami poison|poison]] under Path [[Mother (title)|leader]] [[Elecia Zeveron|the Mother's]] orders, Abda tried to remember the combat training she had done with her brother and parents to defend herself. However, Greylark succeeded in poisoning her, resulting in her [[death]].<ref name="Convergence" />

==Personality and traits==
Abda resented her family while living with them, thinking that her parents and other relatives would always regard her brother as better at everything than she could be. Dissatisfied, she eventually opted to leave Quarzite and her family behind to seek a new life following her brother's wedding, which was considered one of the biggest her family had hosted.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Skills and abilities==
The parents were proficient in combat, as they followed the warrior ways of their people.<ref name="Convergence" /> Like all Kage, they had [[Eidetic memory|eidetic memories]].<ref name="HC">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]'' &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Kage]]</ref>
