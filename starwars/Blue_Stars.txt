The '''Blue Stars''' were an organization that<ref name="Last Shot" /> around [[3 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> was present along with representatives from the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Droid Gotra]] at an [[Auction for the Phylanx Redux Transmitter|auction]] in [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]] held by [[Cli Pastayra]] of the [[Wandering Star]] for a device known as the [[Phylanx Redux Transmitter]].<ref name="Last Shot" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|If you're reading Last Shot: be on the lookout for a sweet little cameo from perhaps the greatest bootleg Star Wars toy of all time.|[[Matt Martin]]|<ref name="Martin Twitter">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/986324676560367616|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=If you're reading Last Shot: be on the lookout for a sweet little cameo from perhaps the greatest bootleg Star Wars toy of all time.|archivedate=20181227194603}}</ref>}}
The name "Blue Stars" is a reference to a bootleg ''[[Star Wars]]'' figure<ref name="Martin Twitter" /> released as part of a [[Wikipedia:Turkey|Turkish]] toy line known as Uzay Savaşçıları ("Space Warriors").<ref name="Uzay">{{WebCite|url=http://theswca.com/index.php?action=disp_item&item_id=47414|text=Blue Stars|author=Georgoulias, Chris|work=The Star Wars Collectors Archive|quote=This is the most famous of the Uzay bootleg figures because it is so unique and different.|archivedate=20180420061342}}</ref> Said figure was a blue-armored [[Cold weather assault trooper|Cold weather assault stormtrooper]] with yellow eyes.
