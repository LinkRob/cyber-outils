'''Barbara Tarr'''<ref name="Info">{{WebCite|url=https://babsbabsbabs.com/info|work=[https://babsbabsbabs.com Babs Tarr Illustration]|archiveurl=https://archive.vn/CT1v6|title=Info}}</ref> (born [[November 12]]),<ref name="Birthday" /> credited as '''Babs Tarr''' professionally, is an [[Wikipedia:American people|American]] comic book artist.<ref name="Babs" /> For ''[[Star Wars]]'', Tarr illustrated a [[Wikipedia:Pride Month|Pride Month]] variant cover of the [[War of the Bounty Hunters 1|first issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] miniseries.<ref name="Exclusive">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-pride-month-covers-exclusive|text=Check Out All of Marvel's ''Star Wars'' Pride Month Covers &ndash; Exclusive}}</ref> She<ref name="Instagram" /> was first announced as a variant cover artist for the issue on [[March 18]], [[2021]],<ref name="First">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-pride-month-covers|text=Marvel Celebrates Sana Starros and More in ''Star Wars'' Pride Month with Comic Covers This June}}</ref> while the [[:File:WarOfTheBountyHunters-1-PrideVariant.jpg|completed artwork]]&mdash;featuring the [[archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]]&mdash;was revealed on [[StarWars.com]] on [[May 7]] of the same year.<ref name="Exclusive" /> The comic was written by [[Ethan Sacks]] and released by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[June 2]], 2021.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/92290/star_wars_war_of_the_bounty_hunters_2021_1|text=Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters (2021) #1}}</ref> As fanart, she has drawn<ref name="PoeFinn">{{Instagram|username=babsdraws|url=p/Ck1HoV6tmQn|name=Babs Tarr|date=2022-11-11|archiveurl=https://archive.is/eJDXk}}</ref> the [[Resistance]] leaders<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Poe Dameron]] and [[Finn]], indicating that she felt both "beloved starboys" deserved better fates.<ref name="PoeFinn" />
