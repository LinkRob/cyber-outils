This '''personal energy shield''' was used by [[BX-series droid commando]]s. A number of [[commando]]s stationed at the [[Citadel]] used the [[riot shield|energy shield]] model.<ref name="Counterattack" /> One grouping of BX units deployed as a [[strike team]] at one point included several units equipped with the energy shield model.<ref name="AllianceUnits">{{Asmodee|subdomain=cdn.svc|domain=net|url=production-amgcom/uploads/2025/04/DOC13_SeparatistAlliance_Units.pdf|text=''Star Wars: Legion'' 2025 Separatist Alliance Unit Cards (PDF)}}</ref> It was resistant to blaster fire, and weapons could pass through the shield, allowing the user to fire their [[blaster]] from relative safety.<ref name="Counterattack" />
