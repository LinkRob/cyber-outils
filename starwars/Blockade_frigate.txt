A '''blockade frigate''' was a classification of [[frigate]] that was used by the [[Nikardun Destiny]] of the [[Unknown Regions]] of [[the galaxy]]. The [[Nikardun]] used one [[Nikardun blockade frigate|class of blockade frigate]]. One such [[warship]] was captured by the [[Paccian Governance]] and renamed the [[Aelos (frigate)|''Aelos'']].<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>
