During the [[Galactic Civil War]], a '''battle''' took place during the [[Mid Rim Retreat]] in the [[Chonsetta system]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] and [[Imperial Navy|forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] in [[3 ABY]]. The 61st's [[starship]]s were attacked by around a dozen [[TIE/IN interceptor|TIE Interceptors]] tracking a [[hypermatter]] leak from the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]''. The [[TIE series|TIEs]] damaged the starboard side of the [[corvette]] before the 61st left the [[Star system|system]], discovering the hypermatter leak.

==Prelude==
During the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="Twilight" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]'', the headquarters and [[troop carrier]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]], suffered damages to its [[hyperdrive]] which caused a [[hypermatter]] leak. Identifying the subsequent hypermatter trail, [[Captain]] [[Tabor Seitaron]] and [[Prelate]] [[Verge]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] had [[Imperial officer]]s aboard their<ref name="Twilight" /> [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]],<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> the ''[[Herald]]'', to liaise with [[Imperial Navy|Imperial forces]] to attack the 61st. After the [[Battle in the Enrivi system|second attack]], in the [[Enrivi system]], the 61st personnel celebrated winning against their attackers, but the gunnery crew stayed at their stations in fear of another attack.<ref name="Twilight" />

==The battle==
{{Quote|We have engine problems, because my ship keeps being attacked. And my ship keeps being attacked because we have engine problems.|M2-M5, to Hazram Namir|<ref name="Twilight" />}}
In the nineteen [[Standard hour|hours]] after battle in the Enrivi [[Star system|system]], the 61st's [[starship]]s made two course changes to try and throw off [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]]. However, the [[battle group]] was attacked in the [[Chonsetta system]] by around a dozen [[TIE/IN interceptor|TIE Interceptors]] from the cover of a [[comet]] trail. The [[TIE series|TIEs]] dealt damage to the starboard side of the ''Thunderstrike'' before the 61st left the system.<ref name="Twilight" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|That trail was a stroke of fortune, but surely wars are not won through luck?|Verge reflects on tracking the 61st to Tabor Seitaron aboard the ''Herald''|<ref name="Twilight" />}}
After the attack, rumors rose among the 61st that three Imperial battle groups had abandoned rebel engagements to hunt the Alliance battle group. [[M2-M5]], the 61st's [[chief of engineering]], soon discovered the ''Thunderstrike''{{'s}} hypermatter leak. In a meeting after, [[Lieutenant]] [[Sairgon]] reflected that the Imperial forces had merely harried the battle group. To repair the [[corvette]]'s hyperdrive, the 61st [[Raid on an Imperial heavy freighter|raided]] an [[Unidentified Imperial heavy freighter|Imperial heavy freighter]].<ref name="Twilight" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle in the Chonsetta system appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Twilight" />
