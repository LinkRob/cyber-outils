'''First Order stormtroopers''', also known simply as '''stormtroopers''' or "'''bucketheads'''," were the [[infantry]] [[soldier]]s of the [[First Order]], a [[Fascism|fascist]] [[First Order High Command|military junta]] from within the [[Unknown Regions]] of [[the galaxy]]. As the latest evolution of one of [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history's]] most distinctive symbols of military might, the First Order's infantry units were outfitted with [[First Order stormtrooper armor|stark white armor]] derived from that worn by the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[clone trooper]]s and the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Stormtrooper|identically named]] elite shock troops. To ensure absolute loyalty and conformity, First Order [[trooper]]s were identified by alphanumeric designations instead of individual names and programmed from [[Reproduction|birth]] through regular exposure to tightly-controlled [[propaganda]]. Ultimately, members of this new generation of stormtroopers were trained to serve one purpose&mdash;conquering [[the galaxy]] in the name of the First Order.

The [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|First Order Stormtrooper Corps]] was formed during the [[New Republic Era]], following the outlawing of its [[Imperial Era|Imperial-era]] [[Stormtrooper Corps|predecessor]] in the aftermath of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Though the [[Galactic Concordance]] banned the mobilization of stormtroopers, the First Order secretly began its own [[Project Resurrection|stormtrooper program]] under the supervision of [[General]] [[Brendol Hux]] and [[Captain]] [[Archex|Cardinal]], and later General [[Armitage Hux]] and Captain [[Phasma]] beyond the supervision of the [[New Republic]]. Throughout its [[time]], the program was centered on training [[human]] conscripts from early [[Youngling|childhood]]. During the [[Cold War]] between the First Order and the New Republic, stormtroopers were presented as a territorial protection force. After the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of the [[Hosnian Prime|New Republic capital]], their true nature as the First Order's invasion [[army]] was revealed, leading to [[First Order-Resistance War|open war]] against the [[Resistance]].

Having conquered large swathes of territory, stormtroopers were to become its enforcers ruled by [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]&mdash;the [[Knights of Ren|dark warrior]] who [[Battle of Oetchi|succeeded]] [[Snoke]] as [[Dictatorship|absolute ruler]] of the First Order. By the final [[Standard day|days]] of the conflict, stormtroopers were supplemented by the [[Color|red]] [[armor]]ed [[Sith trooper]]s, a [[Sith Eternal army|secret army]] developed on the [[Unknown Regions|hidden]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Exegol]] by the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Religion|cult]]. Despite their combined power, the newly-formed [[Final Order]] was ultimately defeated by Resistance forces and their [[Citizens' Fleet|allies]] at the [[Battle of Exegol]], after which the [[First Order military]] faced a series of [[Uprising against the First Order|uprisings across the galaxy]].

==History==
===Origin===
{{Quote|During the Clone Wars, I served in the Grand Army of the Republic. I was a junior officer who communicated tactics and strategy as determined by our Jedi generals.|Brendol Hux|<ref name="TSA">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref>}}
[[File:ClonesInFormation.png|thumb|left|265px|The origins of First Order stormtroopers dated back to the Clone Wars, when clone troopers fought for the Galactic Republic.]]
The [[armor]]ed [[soldier]]s of the [[First Order]] evolved from one of [[the galaxy]]'s most distinctive symbols of military might&mdash;the [[stormtrooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="TLJ:TVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Representing a new generation of stormtroopers, First Order [[trooper]]s could trace their origins even further back to the [[clone trooper]]s who fought for the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> When the Empire [[Imperial Instruments of Surrender|fell]], suffering catastrophic defeats at [[Battle of Endor|Endor]]<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> and [[Battle of Jakku|Jakku]],<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> the [[New Republic]] crafted a peace treaty that ended the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="NewRepublic">{{Databank|the-new-republic|The New Republic}}</ref> yet also reduced the Empire to a rump state through the enforcement of strict disarmament treaties and punishing war reparations.<ref name="FirstOrder">{{Databank|the-first-order|The First Order}}</ref>

Desperate to end the war and having regained his power, [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]], oversaw the dismantling of the [[Coruscanti government|Imperial regime]] and its replacement by a [[Coruscant's provisional government|provisional government]] on [[Coruscant]] following the [[death]] of [[Counselor to the Empire]] [[Gallius Rax]].<ref name="Empire's End" /> The [[Stormtrooper Corps]] was consequently dissolved due to the [[Galactic Concordance]] banning the recruitment and training of stormtroopers.<ref name="Visual">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

===Rise of the First Order===
====Project Resurrection====
{{Quote|Project Resurrection. Kidnapping children.''"<br />"''Rescuing. Forging them into an army the likes of which this galaxy has never seen.|Iden Versio and Gideon Hask|<ref name="BF 2">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Legitimate_Power.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The First Order's new generation of stormtroopers preserved an enduring symbol of military might.]]
Although the Empire officially capitulated to the Republic, a group of [[Imperial officer]]s, [[noble]]s and technologists refused to accept the outcome of the war. Embarking on an exodus into the [[Unknown Regions]],<ref name="FirstOrder" /> the [[Unknown Regions Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] was ultimately reorganized into<ref name="Visual" /> a hermetic state<ref name="Snoke DB">{{Databank|supreme-leader-snoke|Supreme Leader Snoke}}</ref> known as the First Order.<ref name="Visual" /> By hiding in the unexplored regions of [[Galaxy|galactic]] [[Realspace|space]], the First Order was able to conduct a massive [[First Order military|military]] buildup&mdash;forging new [[First Order Army|armies]] and [[First Order Navy|fleets]] in secret&mdash;in violation of the Galactic Concordance.<ref name="FirstOrder" /> The presence of stormtroopers in the First Order's ranks was a well known fact to the New Republic, but the First Order convinced both the government and much of the galaxy that their soldiers were only for defense. Only the Resistance suspected the stormtroopers' true purpose.<ref name="TLJ:TVD" /> Drawing inspiration from the ideas of [[Brendol Hux]],<ref name="Visual" /> a former [[Republic officer]] and [[Commandant|Imperial commandant]] who found the Empire's stormtroopers lacking,<ref name="TSA" /> the First Order conscripted<ref name="BF 2" /> [[human]]<ref name="ECUC">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]'' establishes that the stormtroopers of the [[First Order]] were all [[human]]s.</ref> [[Youngling|children]] under [[Project Resurrection]],<ref name="BF 2" /> creating a [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|new Stormtrooper Corps]].<ref name="Visual" />

The First Order utilized the training methods of its predecessors, the Galactic Republic and the Galactic Empire, while training the new generation of stormtroopers. Although First Order stormtroopers were non-[[Cloning|clone]] humans like their Imperial precursors, they were trained from an even younger age<ref name="TROS VD" />&mdash;virtually from [[Reproduction|birth]]<ref name="FO-Stormtroopers" />&mdash;whereas Imperial stormtroopers were recruited in their adolescence. In this regard, First Order stormtroopers were modeled on Republic clone troopers<ref name="TROS VD" /> as well as the [[Jedi Knight]]s. Both the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] and the [[Jedi Order]] trained their members from infancy, which influenced Brendol Hux's beliefs on how to train the ideal soldier. His [[Hux family|son]], [[Armitage Hux]], viewed First Order stormtroopers as the [[Jedi]]'s legacy.<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

[[File:Hux_and_Phasma.jpg|thumb|left|250px|General Armitage Hux and Captain Phasma oversaw the stormtrooper training program, determined to create an army of perfect warriors.]]
The new stormtroopers were taken from their families<ref name="Episode VII" /> and raised to be absolutely loyal to the First Order.<ref name="FO-Stormtroopers" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> approximately thirty [[Standard year|years]] after<ref name="Episode VII" /> the Empire's defeat at [[Endor]],<ref name="Episode VI" /> the stormtrooper [[legion]]s were under the command of [[Captain]] [[Phasma]]<ref name="CaptainPhasma" /> as well as other notable leaders such as [[General]] Armitage Hux and [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> Above all, their allegiance was with [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]],<ref name="BTA" /> ruler of the First Order.<ref name="Snoke DB" /> Although all stormtroopers were trained for absolute loyalty to the First Order,<ref name="Episode VII" /> a [[Worker Of The Month]] award could be given to troopers who distinguished themselves; [[FN-0208]] was one stormtrooper who won the award.<ref name="BF 2" />

====The buildup to war====
{{Quote|What's wrong with people? Can't they see the First Order are the bad guys?"''<br />"''Bad guys? They saved Torra from pirate kidnappers. We need better security here!"''<br />"''These guys aren't security. They're stormtroopers, and stormtroopers are only for one thing: War.|Kazuda Xiono and Tamara Ryvora|<ref name="The First Order Occupation">{{Resistance|The First Order Occupation}}</ref>}}
[[File:Holding_Back_the_Chaos.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The First Order concealed the true purpose of its stormtrooper forces by claiming that they were only for defense.]]
In [[29 ABY]],<ref name="29 ABY date">As established in to ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', the [[First Order]] was publicly formed through the [[Centrists]] five [[Standard year|years]] before the "[[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]]." Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the incident to [[34 ABY]], the First Order must have been publicly formed in 29 ABY.</ref> [[Centrists|Centrist]] [[star system]]s seceded from the New Republic, becoming the public face of the First Order. As the New Republic and First Order entered into a [[Cold War]], Stormtroopers again became a common sight in the galaxy, yet the public at large had forgotten the troopers' Imperial legacy. Instead, the stormtroopers were merely portrayed as the First Order's territorial protection force; the soldiers never entered the Republic's borders, respecting treaties with a government they intended to overthrow. The true purpose of the Stormtrooper Corps remained a secret,<ref name="TROS VD" /> but [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], fearing what the First Order was capable of, formed<ref name="BL">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> a private military group known as the [[Resistance]]<ref name="Resistance">{{Databank|the-resistance|The Resistance}}</ref> in response.<ref name="BL" />

During the Cold War, the First Order was opposed by the Resistance,<ref name="Episode VII" /> which fought to prevent the First Order from restoring Imperial rule to the galaxy.<ref name="Resistance" /> Stormtroopers stood in stark contrast to their [[Resistance trooper]] counterparts, who embraced their individuality while the Stormtrooper Corps was designed to stand as a faceless, uniform bloc.<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> The Resistance cataloged that there were special stormtroopers equipped for crowd control, cold weather, and incendiary tasks. Still, intelligence analysts were convinced that there were many more special troopers yet to be discovered.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> Two Resistance agents, [[Iden Versio]] and [[Shriv Suurgav]], discovered Project Resurrection. Versio accused the program of being exploitative, abducting children and brainwashing them to serve the First Order. Her [[Inferno Squad|former comrade]], [[Gideon Hask]], countered that the First Order rescued children by giving them purpose as soldiers in an army of unprecedented military might.<ref name="BF 2" />

Following the [[Torra Doza's abduction|abduction]] of [[Torra Doza]], an event orchestrated by the First Order in order to gain Captain [[Imanuel Doza]]'s trust by rescuing his [[Doza family|daughter]], First Order stormtroopers were posted on the ''[[Colossus]]'' ostensibly to protect the [[Space station|station]] against further [[pirate]] raids.<ref name="TDD">{{Resistance|The Doza Dilemma}}</ref> Their presence on the ''Colossus'' generated mixed opinions among the station's inhabitants, such as [[Tamara Ryvora]] and [[Kazuda Xiono]]. While Ryvora welcomed the stormtroopers as a necessary safeguard against piracy, Xiono&mdash;a [[spy]] [[Mission on the Colossus platform|working]] for the Resistance on [[Castilon]]&mdash;opposed the soldiers whom he viewed as "bad guys." Xiono believed that First Order stormtroopers were trained solely for war. Nevertheless, Ryvora regarded the troops as the solution to the station's security issues.<ref name="The First Order Occupation">{{Resistance|The First Order Occupation}}</ref>

====Treason in the ranks====
{{Quote|Perhaps Leader Snoke should consider using a clone army.|Kylo Ren|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:Stormtroopers-raid-Jakku.png|thumb|left|250px|Tuanul, a village on Jakku, was raided by First Order stormtroopers as part of Kylo Ren's hunt for the last Jedi.]]
In addition to the First Order's goals, which included the destruction of the New Republic and the Resistance, they supported Kylo Ren's quest to destroy the last Jedi, [[Luke Skywalker]]. To that end, Phasma and an armed force of stormtroopers conducted an [[Attack on Tuanul|assault]] on [[Tuanul]], a village located on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] where Ren hoped to gain information pertaining to Skywalker's [[Ahch-To|hidden location]]. The Resistance likewise attempted to locate Skywalker, sending [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] to obtain information from [[Lor San Tekka]]. Dameron killed a few stormtroopers, including [[FN-2003]] during the attack, but was subdued by Ren's [[Force power]]s and subsequently captured by First Order troops.<ref name="Episode VII" />

[[File:Finn_on_Tuanul.png|thumb|right|250px|Unwilling to execute unarmed prisoners, FN-2187 committed desertion after witnessing the massacre of Tuanul.]]
After executing San Tekka for refusing to cooperate, Ren directed Phasma and the stormtroopers to exterminate the village's entire population. With the exception of [[Finn|FN-2187]], every trooper complied with Ren's order, firing into the crowd until the last villager was dead. The experience of witnessing the First Order's brutality and the deaths of his comrades influenced FN-2187's decision to commit desertion by abandoning his life as a stormtrooper. He proceeded to [[Escape from the Finalizer|escape]] from the ''[[Finalizer]]'', a [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]], by freeing Dameron before commandeering a [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]. FN-2187 used the [[starfighter]]'s weapons against the troops in the [[hangar]], killing several stormtroopers that attempted to prevent his escape.<ref name="Episode VII" />

Returning to Jakku, FN-2187 encountered more stormtroopers at [[Niima Outpost]] on Jakku. However, he was able to escape from his former comrades with the help of a [[scavenger]], [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and the Resistance [[astromech droid]] [[BB-8]]. His betrayal of the First Order caused Ren to berate all stormtroopers as susceptible to [[treason]]. However, General Hux maintained his confidence in the First Order's army as well as their conditioning for complete loyalty.<ref name="Episode VII" />

===First Order-Resistance War===
====March of the First Order====
{{Quote|Today is the end of the Republic. The end of a regime that acquiesces to disorder. At this very moment, in a system far from here&hellip; the New Republic lies to the galaxy&hellip; while secretly supporting the treachery&hellip; of the loathsome Resistance. This fierce machine which you have built&hellip; upon which we stand&hellip; will bring an end to the Senate! To their cherished fleet! All remaining systems will bow to the First Order! And will remember this&hellip; as the last day of the Republic!|Armitage Hux|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:First_Order_Military.jpg|thumb|left|250px|First Order stormtroopers saluted General Hux as he announced the imminent destruction of the New Republic.]]
With the completion of the First Order [[superweapon]], [[Starkiller Base]], General Hux declared the end of the New Republic's [[New Republic era|reign]] to a large assembly of stormtroopers and other military personnel. Saluting their general, the stormtroopers witnessed the activation of the superweapon, resulting in the utter [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of [[Hosnian Prime]] along with the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] and a substantial portion of the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

Meanwhile, the hunt for Skywalker led Kylo Ren and an armed force of stormtroopers to the planet [[Takodana]] where the dark warrior attempted to capture BB-8, aware that the [[droid]] contained part of the information regarding the last Jedi's location. The stormtroopers [[Battle of Takodana|assaulted]] a [[Takodana Castle|castle]] belonging to [[Maz Kanata]] during their search for the [[BB-series astromech droid|BB unit]], then pursued BB-8 and Rey into the forest where several troopers were killed before Ren apprehended the scavenger.<ref name="Episode VII" />

[[File:DoctorAphra-37-Muir-Unlettered.jpg|thumb|right|220px|During the invasion of Takodana, FN-2199 attempted to kill FN-2187 for betraying the First Order.]]
During the battle, [[FN-2199]] confronted the traitor FN-2187, who now referred to himself as "Finn." After a brief duel FN-2199 defeated Finn in single combat, only to be shot by the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]]. Nevertheless, the First Order captured Finn and his allies, but the arrival of a Resistance [[X-wing starfighter]] [[squadron]] distracted the troops, granting their [[prisoner]]s an opportunity to escape. As the First Order fended off the Resistance's attack, one of the stormtroopers advised Ren to send for reinforcements. Instead, Ren ordered his troops to withdraw from Takodana, having discovered that Rey already accessed the information he wanted from BB-8.<ref name="Episode VII" />

At Starkiller Base, Kylo Ren [[Mind probe (Force power)|interrogated]] Rey in an attempt to get the map out of her, but his efforts were in vain, as Rey resisted him and revealed herself to be [[Force-sensitive]]. Ren went to inform his Master, Supreme Leader Snoke, about his discovery and left [[FN-1824]] to keep watch over Rey. After two failed attempts, Rey managed to perform a [[mind trick]] on the stormtrooper, forcing him to to free her. When Ren returned and found that Rey had escaped, he angrily called the guards and destroyed the [[interrogation chair]] with [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|his lightsaber]], prompting two passing stormtroopers to quickly turn and walk away to avoid confrontation.<ref name="Episode VII" />

Shortly after, the Resistance attacked Starkiller Base, resulting in a large dogfight in the air as the First Order scrambled a defense force to protect the base. On the ground, Han Solo, [[Chewbacca]], Finn, and Rey headed to the oscillator to destroy it, engaging multiple stormtroopers along the way. As Solo and Chewbacca planted the explosives, Ren and a [[squad]] of stormtroopers arrived at the oscillator to find them. The stormtroopers aimed their [[blaster]]s at Solo when he confronted Ren out in the open, hoping to convince his son to abandon the First Order and return home. Ren was shot by Chewbacca after the First Order [[warlord]] struck down his father, causing the stormtroopers to open fire on the [[Wookiee]] warrior. Despite their efforts, the troopers failed to stop Chewbacca from detonating the explosives, killing the soldiers and damaging the oscillator. As a result, Dameron was able to fly in and destroy it. With the oscillator destroyed, the First Order moved to evacuate its forces from Starkiller Base as the planet collapsed into a [[star]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

====Annihilation of the Resistance====
{{Quote|High alert, Commander. Their ships are dropping like flies. Our time approaches.|Phasma|<ref name="Expanded Edition" />}}
[[File:Episode_VIII_Stormtroopers.png|thumb|left|250px|Protected by an army of stormtroopers, the ''Supremacy'' spearheaded the First Order's efforts to annihilate the Resistance.]]
Despite the loss of Starkiller Base, the First Order moved to impose its rule over the galaxy through military force, having eliminated the New Republic as an obstacle.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> At that time, the First Order's forces included ten thousand combat ready troops.<ref name="TLJ Season">{{EASWYouTube|n0-ye4LXWts|Star Wars Battlefront II: The Last Jedi Season}}</ref> Simultaneously, the First Order endeavored to destroy the Resistance once and for all, forcing Organa and her followers to abandon their [[Resistance base|base]] on [[D'Qar]]. Hoping to disrupt the First Order's ability to track the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet's]] movements through [[hyperspace]], Finn and [[Rose Tico]] infiltrated the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]''&mdash;the [[flagship]] of Supreme Leader Snoke&mdash;but were ultimately captured by Phasma and her stormtroopers. After bringing the prisoners to a hangar containing a large gathering of stormtroopers, Phasma sentenced Finn and Tico both to summary execution. Just as the [[First Order stormtrooper executioner|executioner troopers]] moved to carry out the death sentence, the Resistance flagship [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] crashed into the ''Supremacy'' at [[lightspeed]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

The act split the ''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought in half, leaving a huge gap open to space where many crew members and stormtroopers perished. In their ensuing escape from the crippled ''Supremacy'', Finn and Tico battled surviving stormtroopers in the hangar. Finn bested Phasma, who was sucked down into the flaming wreckage<ref name="Episode VIII" /> and died.<ref name="TROS VD" /> By then, Snoke had been [[assassin]]ated by his apprentice, Kylo Ren, who then defeated the slain leader's [[Praetorian Guard|Elite Praetorian Guards]] with the help of Rey. Their alliance was short-lived, however. Rey rejected Ren's proposal that they rule the galaxy together, causing the dark side warrior to blame Snoke's death on the former scavenger. Discovered by Hux, Ren claimed it was Rey who had killed Snoke and assumed control over the First Order as the new Supreme Leader.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:Fall_of_Crait_TLJ.png|thumb|right|250px|The Resistance base on Crait fell to First Order stormtroopers led by the self-proclaimed Supreme Leader Kylo Ren.]]
Seizing control of the First Order as the new Supreme Leader, his first act in power was to deploy the First Order's forces, including an army of [[First Order snowtrooper|snowtroopers]], to the planet [[Crait]] where he intended to annihilate the remaining members of the Resistance. During the [[Battle of Crait]], the First Order overwhelmed the Resistance's defenses and stormed the [[Crait outpost|outpost]] where they hid, although a small group of survivors managed to escape aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' due to Rey's intervention.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Several [[Standard day|days]] after the battle, Resistance forces encountered stormtroopers during their [[Mission to Bracca|missions]] to [[Bracca]] [[Mission to Corellia|and]] [[Corellia]], which was a planet the First Order had [[Occupation of Corellia|occupied]].<ref name="Resistance Reborn">''[[Resistance Reborn]]''</ref>

Meanwhile, formations of stormtroopers lined up before Ren on the ''Finalizer'', where Ren formally introduced himself as the new Supreme Leader and unveiled Snoke's corpse. Claiming that it was the Resistance who had slain Snoke, Ren promised his military he would usher in a new beginning and lead them into glory. After a moment of confusion, the stormtroopers rallied under Ren with a salute as Snoke's remains drifted out into space.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 1">[[Legacy of Vader 1|''Legacy of Vader'' 1]]</ref>

====Conquest of the galaxy====
{{Quote|You are First Order ''stormtroopers''. You've trained most of your lives to be the very best, and the time for you to prove it is ''now''. Our enemy has their backs to the wall. Their feeble numbers can hardly stand opposed to the First Order's might.|Armitage Hux|<ref name="Loyalty Test">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=30|story=Loyalty Test}}</ref>}}
[[File:KijimiGarrison-TROSVD.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Stormtroopers deployed across the galaxy, projecting the power of the First Order and its new Supreme Leader.]]
After a lifetime of training to fulfill one purpose,<ref name="TLJ:TVD" /> the forces of the First Order deployed throughout the galaxy,<ref name="TROS VD" /> with stormtroopers leading the charge.<ref name="Visual" /> Large swathes of the galaxy fell to legions of white-armored troopers&mdash;an image that was once a symbol of galactic salvation during the Clone Wars, but now appropriated by the First Order. Seemingly appearing everywhere the First Order expanded to, the fall of the New Republic allowed the stormtroopers to carry out their true mandate as an invasion army; the First Order saw no point in respecting the laws of a fallen government, and therefore, they discarded the facade of maintaining a territorial protection force.<ref name="TROS VD" />

At this time, the First Order began abducting and conscripting even more children from across the galaxy to grow its stormtrooper ranks even further. This was ruthlessly carried out under the direction of General [[Amret Engell]], who enthusiastically supported the new Supreme Leader and strove to create an [[army]] that was worthy of his approval. Indeed, some planets such as [[Kijimi]] had most, if not all, of its children taken for conscription at the start of its [[Occupation of Kijimi|occupation]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> During the war, the [[709th Legion|709th "Red Fury" Legion]],<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1">[[Galaxy's Edge 1|''Galaxy's Edge'' 1]]</ref> an elite force made of stormtroopers chosen by Supreme Leader Kylo Ren himself,<ref name="Black Spire">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref> was [[Battle of Batuu|deployed]] to the planet of [[Batuu]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1" /> On Batuu, stormtrooper lieutenant [[Gauge]] was also tasked with operating a re-education camp to turn civilians into soldiers.<ref name="Tales">''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge – Last Call]]''</ref>

===Fall of the First Order===
====Return of the Emperor====
{{Quote|We'll need to increase recruitments. Harvest more of the galaxy's young.|Bellava Parnadee to Amret Engell|<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>}}
[[File:IronwoodWarrior.png|thumb|right|250px|A contingent of stormtroopers accompanied the Supreme Leader to Mustafar where they massacred the Alazmec of Winsit.]]
Despite occupying vast regions of the galaxy, the First Order's forces were smaller than the [[Imperial Military]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> When [[Darth Sidious]]&mdash;the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] and [[Galactic Emperor]]&mdash;announced his [[Palpatine's broadcast|return]], a contingent of stormtroopers accompanied Supreme Leader Ren during his [[Mission to Mustafar (First Order)|excursion]] to [[Mustafar]]<ref name="Episode IX" /> where they fought the [[Alazmec of Winsit]] on the castle grounds of [[Fortress Vader]]. Ren outpaced his stormtrooper escorts, cutting a swathe of destruction through the Alazmec cultists to acquire a [[Sith wayfinder]] that once [[Vader's wayfinder|belonged]] to his [[Skywalker family|grandfather]], [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

The [[wayfinder]] led Ren to [[Exegol]], a planet in the Unknown Regions where the [[Sith Eternal military]] was developed by the [[Emperor]]'s [[Sith cultist|followers]].<ref name="Episode IX" /> Discovering the [[Sith Eternal]]'s forces bolstered the [[First Order Supreme Council]]'s confidence in Ren's leadership;<ref name="TROS VD" /> the units on Exegol would be used to reinforce the First Order's existing forces, allowing Ren to complete his conquest of the galaxy and rule over a true Empire.<ref name="Episode IX" />

====The hunt for Rey====
{{Quote|It's okay that you're here.''"<br />"''It's good.|FN-0606 and FN-1226, under Rey's control|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:NJP-900.png|thumb|left|250px|Rey's Resistance team was located on Pasaana by the First Order's stormtrooper forces.]]
Before Ren could take command of Exegol's forces, he was tasked by Sidious to kill the former scavenger Rey, and by doing so, eliminate the last remnant of the Jedi Order. Using his connection with the Jedi apprentice to determine her location, Ren deployed a force of stormtroopers to the planet [[Pasaana]] where Rey and her companions were pursued by a team of [[First Order Treadspeeder Driver]]s and [[First Order jet trooper]]s. As they chased the Resistance team through the Pasaana desert, the [[125-Z treadspeeder bike]]s catapulted the jet troopers into the air, allowing the First Order to take to the sky much to the surprise of [[C-3PO]], Finn and Poe Dameron. Though the Jedi and Resistance operatives defeated their pursuers, a group of stormtroopers captured [[Chewbacca]] with help from the [[Knights of Ren]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:Stormtroopers-TROS.png|thumb|right|250px|FN-0606 and FN-1226 were forced to help Rey while under the influence of the Jedi apprentice's power.]]
Rey and her allies later made their way to [[Kijimi]], a planet under military occupation by the First Order. A [[Kijimi garrison|stormtrooper garrison]] patrolled the streets of [[Kijimi City]], conducting mass raids<ref name="Episode IX" /> to acquire more child conscripts for their growing army.<ref name="TROS VD" /> Ren's forces also combed through the city searching for Rey, who managed to infiltrate the Supreme Leader's flagship&mdash;the ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']]&mdash;along with her comrades. After killing the guards in the main [[hangar]], the group was ambushed by a pair of stormtroopers, [[FN-0606]] and [[FN-1226]], who attempted to apprehend the infiltrators. However, Rey's connection to the Force allowed her to dominate their minds, forcing the troopers to help the Resistance operatives navigate their way through the warship. While Rey left to collect her belongings from Ren's private chambers, Finn and Dameron managed to rescue Chewbacca, and with the Wookiee's help, they stormed down a corridor of stormtroopers, gunning several troops down before being forced to surrender due to the overwhelming number of First Order units.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:ReuniononSteadfast-RiseIDW.png|thumb|left|250px|Stormtroopers were marshaled in the ''Steadfast''{{'s}} hangar, where the Supreme Leader and the last Jedi confronted each other.]]
A squad of stormtroopers escorted the Resistance prisoners to an area where they were to be summarily executed. Before they could complete their orders, General Hux took a blaster rifle from one of the troopers, ostensibly to carry out the deed himself. Instead, he saved the prisoners by killing his own soldiers, revealing himself as the spy who had been feeding information to the Resistance. As Rey's friends returned to the ''Millennium Falcon'', Ren had returned to the ''Steadfast'' to confront the last Jedi in person. More stormtroopers arrived in the hangar, where they aimed their weapons at Rey but were held back by Ren, who revealed to Rey that she was in fact Sidious' [[Palpatine family|granddaughter]]. He urged her to join him, believing they could destroy the Emperor and the Sith with their combined power as a [[Force dyad]]. Rey refused Ren's entreaty, however, and escaped from the ''Steadfast'' with the help of her friends, who used the engines of the ''Millennium Falcon'' to hold the stormtroopers back.<ref name="Episode IX" />

====The Final Order====
Ren would ultimately abandon his ambitions to rule the galaxy, [[Mission to Kef Bir|renouncing]] his dark persona in the wake of his mother's death on [[Ajan Kloss]]. Without a Supreme Leader, command of the First Order and its forces defaulted to [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]], who executed General Hux with a stormtrooper's blaster rifle after deducing that his younger colleague was the spy in their ranks. Having served [[Darth]] Sidious in the days of the Empire, Pryde swore fealty to him once more, giving control of the entire First Order to the [[Transference|reborn]] Emperor.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:Sith-Troopers-First-Order.png|thumb|right|250px|In the last days of the war, First Order stormtroopers were supplemented by an army of elite Sith troopers from Exegol.]]
With the First Order's military forces under his command, Sidious issued an ultimatum to the entire galaxy: all worlds would submit to the combined might of the new [[Sith Empire]] or be annihilated outright. In addition to the [[Final Order]], a massive armada of [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] armed with [[axial superlaser]]s, the First Order gained an [[Sith Eternal army|army]] of [[Sith trooper]]s.<ref name="Episode IX" /> Like their white-armored counterparts, these red-armored soldiers were elite units whose inception was inspired by Republic clone troopers and Imperial stormtroopers, as well as the [[Sith]] for whom they were named after. Unlike First Order stormtroopers, however, Sith troopers were even more obedient and machine-like due to their flash-imprinting and loyalty conditioning, both of which were built upon and advanced beyond [[Kaminoan]] procedures of earlier generations.<ref name="TROS VD" />

====Defeat on Exegol====

As the Sith fleet prepared to depart from Exegol, Resistance forces attempted to halt their launch in a last-ditch effort to prevent Sidious' new Sith Empire from becoming a reality. In the ensuing [[Battle of Exegol]], a contingent of Sith troopers defended the ''Steadfast'' while Pryde oversaw the launching of the Sith Star Destroyers. As such, the command ship was targeted by Resistance boarding parties, causing the Sith troopers to launch a counterattack in defense of the ''Steadfast''. Though many Resistance fighters fell in battle against the crimson soldiers, the Sith troopers failed to prevent their enemies from destroying the command bridge along with Pryde. Several standard First Order stormtroopers were aboard the bridge as the ship was destroyed as well. Without the ''Steadfast'' to guide the fleet to safe altitude, the remaining Sith Star Destroyers were destroyed by the Resistance and its [[Citizens' Fleet|allies]] before they could escape into hyperspace, and with the death of Sidious the Resistance claimed victory on Exegol<ref name="Episode IX" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="TROS Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] to one [[Standard year|year]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. Without the promise of reinforcements of the Sith, the First Order military was suddenly challenged by multiple [[Uprising against the First Order|uprisings]] across the galaxy. In the aftermath of Resistance victory, ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers were attacked and destroyed in the skies [[Uprising on Cloud City|above]] [[Cloud City]] on [[Bespin]], the [[Uprising on Endor|Forest Moon of Endor]], and [[Uprising on Jakku|Rey's homeworld of Jakku]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Training==
{{Quote|My men are exceptionally trained. Programmed from birth.|Armitage Hux|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:DeckNine-AORFinn1.jpg|thumb|right|250px|After the fall of the Galactic Empire, the First Order conscripted human children to create the next generation of stormtroopers.]]
The First Order's stormtrooper training program was designed to produce an army of super-soldiers.<ref name="Finn">{{Databank|finn|Finn}}</ref> As a result, members of the new generation of stormtroopers were trained from birth to become elite soldiers with a single purpose in life&mdash;to serve the First Order.<ref name="FO-Stormtroopers" /> This form of training drew inspiration from the ideals of Commandant Brendol Hux, who was inspired by the combat prowess and dedication of the Jedi Knights and Republic clone troopers, both of which were instructed from youth to become efficient combatants.<ref name="TSA" />

According to Phasma, the ideology of the First Order stormtroopers did not allow for any emotional elements such as sympathy since they were to specifically act as an extension of the Supreme Leader's will, and by extension that of the First Order, alone, and that the troopers were just as strong as they were weak.<ref name="BTA" /> Likewise, Finn admitted that First Order stormtroopers were considered by their superiors to be expendable; those that were inept in battle were expected to die and not be saved by their fellow allies.<ref name="PD28">[[Poe Dameron 28|''Poe Dameron'' 28]]</ref> During the fall of the Empire, Counselor Gallius Rax conscripted roughly two dozen orphans from his [[Homeworld|home planet]] of Jakku and gave them to Hux, who turned them into a [[Gallius Rax's child soldiers|force of child soldiers]] and assassins loyal to Rax. Prior to his death, Rax placed Hux's illegitimate son, Armitage in command of the child soldiers.<ref name="Empire's End" /> As an adult, Armitage incorporated his father's ideas into the training program for the First Order stormtroopers.<ref name="Visual" /> Captain [[Grummart]] was also a figure involved in trooper training whose specialties included archery.<ref name="TROS Junior">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>

[[File:FOStormtrooperSquad-SWBC54.png|thumb|left|300px|Stormtroopers of the First Order were trained since birth to serve one purpose&mdash;using strength to bring order to chaos.]]
The First Order employed [[Jinata Security]] to abduct infants for the stormtrooper program.<ref name="BF 2" /> Most of the child recruits either came from conquered worlds in the Unknown regions, were children of former Imperials, or were orphans from throughout the galaxy. While in some cases, many impoverished families actually gave up their children to The First Order willing hoping to give them a better future or have less mouths to feed, more often than not, most of the children were taken by force or outright kidnapped.

The First Order processed their conscripts at induction camps where the trainees were separated into groups or "batches." Prior to becoming fully-trained stormtroopers, FN-2187 and trooper [[926]] were members of [[Batch Eight]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> As children, stormtroopers were initially trained under the oversight of Captain [[Archex|Cardinal]], a former child soldier who instructed his [[cadet]]s in [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand]] and melee combat, marksmanship, and battle simulations on the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Absolution]]''. When deemed ready, Cardinal's trainees were transferred to the ''[[Finalizer]]'' where they would complete their training under the watch of Captain Phasma.<ref name="Phasma">[[Phasma (novel)|''Phasma'']]</ref>

[[File:Phasma_FN-2187.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Captain Phasma endeavored to control the stormtroopers in thought and action, making them completely subservient to the First Order command structure.]]
As the commander of the stormtrooper legions, Phasma endeavored to mold the stormtroopers into the finest soldiers in the galaxy and maintained a constant vigil against any signs of weakness or disobedience.<ref name="CaptainPhasma" /> To this end, behavioral and personality conditioning was critical in the training of First Order stormtroopers. In an effort to control their psychological development, the First Order provided its trainees with a steady amount of subliminal sleep messages<ref name="Phasma" /> and First Order propaganda, resulting in the production of soldiers who were completely loyal to the First Order. In addition, rigorous combat exercises and psychological conditioning, the First Order strongly emphasized the values of uniformity and conformity.<ref name="FO-Stormtroopers" /> They were therefore given alphanumeric<ref name="Episode VII" /> designations instead of names.<ref name="FO-Stormtroopers" />

As such, their emphasis of uniformity and conformity was strict enough that the stormtroopers weren't allowed to even remove their helmets unless specifically ordered to do so, with the penalty of doing so usually being mandated psychological counseling.<ref name="Episode VII" /> A trooper that displayed "erratic conditioning" could be scheduled for a full mental wipe and reprogram, also known as a brainscrape.<ref name="The New Trooper">{{Resistance|The New Trooper}}</ref> Likewise, stormtroopers were not allowed to initiate contact with [[First Order officer]]s.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> While children were preferable, it was possible for teenagers and young adults to enlist as a stormtrooper and serve the First Order, as [[propaganda]] posters for new recruits could be found in different parts of the galaxy, such as on [[Castilon]].<ref name="The Disappeared">{{Resistance|The Disappeared}}</ref> In rare cases even older adults were some sometimes accepted into the Stormtrooper ranks such as [[Ruthford]] who was a veteran Stormtrooper of the former Galactic Empire.

Although modern stormtroopers were modeled largely on the stormtroopers of yesteryear, their combat training differed substantially from that of their Imperial predecessors. Unlike the Empire, which resorted to the use of numbing routine for its troopers,<ref name="FO-Stormtroopers" /> the First Order's training simulations, live-fire exercises, and sparring practices encouraged stormtroopers to embrace improvisation on the battlefield.<ref name="BTA" /> They were, therefore, more effective in battle than the stormtroopers who served as their template.<ref name="FO-Stormtroopers" /> In addition to combat training, all First Order stormtroopers received medical training, albeit only for minor injuries such as non-fatal blaster shots.<ref name="Episode VII novel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref> During training, stormtrooper cadets slapped the rears of other troopers to motivate each other.<ref name="TLJ Junior novel">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>

[[File:Phasma_FN_Corps_training.png|thumb|left|250px|Phasma was in charge of the First Order's stormtrooper forces, and therefore responsible for their physical training and mental conditioning.]]
Despite General Hux's confidence in the stormtrooper programming, not every trooper was a successful product by the First Order's standards.<ref name="Episode VII" /> Examples of defective soldiers included [[Company 77]] and Finn. The stormtroopers of Company 77 refused to fire on unarmed prisoners and had a lack of zeal for the First Order's cause. Likewise, Finn had a lack of zeal for the First Order's cause;<ref name="Finn" /> combined with his unwillingness to execute unarmed prisoners, it led him to doubt his loyalties before ultimately forsaking his allegiance to the First Order. Both Phasma and Kylo considered FN-2187 as a renegade, albeit the latter also saw him as a prime example of a stormtrooper training program which he viewed as flawed. He theorized that a clone army could serve the First Order in a better capacity. However, Hux remained confident in the collective reliability of the First Order stormtroopers, believing the rogue FN-2187 to be an anomaly in an army of loyal soldiers.<ref name="Episode VII" />

The average First Order stormtrooper stood 1.83 [[meter]]s tall and weighed 80 [[kilogram]]s&mdash;the same standard requirements that once applied to the Grand Army of the Republic and the Imperial Military.<ref name="TFA head to head">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Head-to-Head]]''</ref> Standard protocol for the soldiers was to always have backup nearby, ensuring their enemies could be swarmed if they were attacked.<ref name="Adventures 32">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=32|story=The Battle for Horizon Base}}</ref> The [[Omicron Formation]] tactic was used for search and destroy missions.<ref name="Ghosts of Kashyyyk, Part 1">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=27|story=Ghosts of Kashyyyk, Part 1}}</ref>

===Resurgent armor===
{{Quote|Stormtroopers get some first aid training&mdash;battlefield stuff&mdash;but not much. We were, pretty, uh, expendable. I think the idea was that whatever trooper who wasn't a good enough soldier to keep from getting wounded deserved to die anyway.''"<br />"''But that's insane.''"<br />"''Welcome to the First Order.|Finn and Poe Dameron|<ref name="Poe Dameron 28">[[Poe Dameron 28|''Poe Dameron'' 28]]</ref>}}
[[File:FOStormtrooper-Fathead.png|thumb|right|200px|First Order stormtrooper armor was derived from Imperial stormtrooper armor and Republic clone trooper armor.]]
Members of the First Order's [[infantry]] forces wore [[First Order stormtrooper armor|white armor]] that was reminiscent of the Galactic Empire's [[stormtrooper armor]], as well as the Galactic Republic's [[Phase I clone trooper armor|Phase I]] and [[Phase II clone trooper armor]]s. Aside from several aesthetic differences, First Order stormtroopers bore a close resemblance to their Imperial predecessors.<ref name="FO-Stormtroopers" /> To denote [[Military rank|rank]], stormtrooper [[officer]]s wore [[color]]-coded [[pauldron]] armor plates.<ref name="Episode VII" /> White pauldrons were worn by stormtroopers who served as [[squad leader]]s, black pauldrons identified stormtroopers who held the rank of [[sergeant]], and red pauldrons were worn by officers.<ref name="TLJ:TVD" />

First Order stormtrooper helmets could filter out smoke, but not toxins.<ref name="Episode VII" /> To deal with toxins, a stormtrooper needed to engage one of several special filters, depending on the specific contaminant. Identification was the province of one or two squad leaders.<ref name="Episode VII novel" /><ref name="Visual" />

First Order stormtrooper armor was strengthened, which provided greater protection,<ref name="SW MTV:Stormtroopers">''[[Star Wars: Meet the Villains: Stormtroopers]]''</ref> and was held in high regard. Captain Phasma once claimed that not even a Wookiee was strong enough to break First Order armor.<ref name="Episode VII novel" /> To keep weight down, most First Order stormtrooper helmets lacked advanced imaging gear, requiring stormtroopers to use separate [[quadnoculars]] in the field to provide them with enhanced imaging. However, standard stormtrooper helmets were still equipped with integral polarized lenses, night vision, communications and targeting systems.<ref name="Visual" /> The armor was also more flexible, allowing troops to move and fight with ease.<ref name="SW MTV:Stormtroopers" />

===Armament===
The [[F-11D blaster rifle]] was the primary weapon of standard stormtroopers. A successor to the Imperial stormtroopers' [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]], the F-11D was mass produced and provided to the First Order's soldiers by [[Sonn-Blas Corporation]]. In addition to the F-11D, Sonn-Blas also designed the [[SE-44C blaster pistol]] as a sidearm for the First Order stormtroopers.<ref name="Visual" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|What would Apple do?|Michael Kaplan, on redesigning stormtrooper armor to be sleeker in the Sequel era|<ref name="EW Rogue One">''[[Entertainment Weekly's Ultimate Guide to Rogue One]]''</ref>}}
The stormtroopers of the First Order first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="Episode VII" /> Their design, a sleeker version of the original stormtrooper armor, was partially inspired by headphones for [[Wikipedia:Apple Inc.|Apple Inc.]] products.<ref name="Commentary">Director [[J.J. Abrams]] commentary</ref> Indeed, costume designer [[Michael Kaplan]], who decided to simplify the armor design for the Sequel era, has said he was inspired by the evolving design of Apple productions, asking himself how Apple would design the armor.<ref name="EW Rogue One" /> According to ''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]'', the design saw many iterations until it was finalized on [[November 28]], [[2013]]. Kaplan and concept artist [[Glyn Dillon]] were the chief architects of design, which was approved by Director [[J.J. Abrams]] on November 28.<ref name="YearByYear">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> The stormtrooper armor depicted in [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] is slightly different<ref name="Episode VIII" /> than the armor seen in ''The Force Awakens''.<ref name="Episode VII" />

[[Daniel Craig]] makes a cameo appearance as the First Order stormtrooper who frees Rey from captivity on Starkiller Base after falling under the influence of her Jedi mind trick.<ref name="Daniel Craig cameo">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/article/2015/12/17/daniel-craig-makes-cameo-star-wars-force-awakens|text=Daniel Craig's Star Wars: The Force Awakens cameo revealed|author=Lee, Chris|date=2015-12-17|archivedate=20200810000308}}</ref> A similar stormtrooper design was used for the [[T-Stormtrooper]]s who served [[TRIGGER Empire|Galactic Empire]] of Master [[Am]] and Lord [[Karre]] in the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' short "[[THE TWINS]]."<ref name="Twins">{{Visions|THE TWINS}}</ref> According to director [[Hiroyuki Imaishi]], the short is set after the fall of the First Order in [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|Star Wars: Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']],<ref name="IGN guide">{{WebCite|url=https://www.ign.com/articles/star-wars-visions-anime-jedi-rock-opera|text=Star Wars Anime Visions Will Include What Became of the Jedi, a Rock Opera, and More|author=Daniel Robson|work=''[[Wikipedia:IGN|IGN]]''|archivedate=20210922124827}}</ref> which would make T-Stormtroopers<ref name="Twins" /> a successor class to First Order soldiers.<ref name="IGN guide" />
