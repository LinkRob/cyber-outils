The '''Clone Walker''' was a two-man, bipedal vehicle used by the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].

==Characteristics==
The Clone Walker, similar in appearance to the [[All Terrain Riot Control Transport|AT-RCT]], had a two-man open [[Cockpit/Legends|cockpit]] mounted upon two slender legs. Its date of first deployment relative to the [[All Terrain Riot Control Transport|AT-RCT]] is unknown. On either side of the cockpit were [[Power pack|power cells]] and a [[Missile/Legends|missile]] launcher. In the front of the cockpit was a heavy [[Blaster cannon/Legends|blaster cannon]]. Although it was usually driven by a [[Pilot/Legends|pilot]] and a [[Gunner/Legends|gunner]], it could be controlled by either one on their own; both pilot and gunner positions had good lines of sight, but were very exposed. The vehicle was often called the chicken walker.

==Behind the scenes==
In mid-December, 2008, LEGO.com released a new wave of Clone Wars sets with the Clone Walker being one of the previews. As a companion to a separate [[Assassin droid speeder|Assassin Droid Battle Pack]], the Clone Walker was released in the ''8014 Clone Walker Battle Pack'' set with two [[Clone trooper/Legends|Clone troopers]], a [[clone tank gunner]], a [[Clone Commander/Legends|Clone trooper commander]], and additional [[ARC trooper armor/Legends|ARC trooper gear]]. The pack was intended to help consumers "build their army" with the walker and clones. The model was also used in the LEGO ''[[Star Wars]]'' animated comics.
