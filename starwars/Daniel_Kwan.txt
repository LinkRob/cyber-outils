'''Daniel Kwan''' (born [[February 10]], [[1988]]) is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] filmmaker who directed an episode of ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'' alongside [[Daniel Scheinert]]. As a duo, the Daniels have directed music videos, the [[2016]] film [[Wikipedia:Swiss Army Man|''Swiss Army Man'']], and the [[2022]] film [[Wikipedia:Everything Everywhere All at Once|''Everything Everywhere All at Once'']].<ref name="SWCB">{{WebCite|url=https://variety.com/2023/tv/news/star-wars-skeleton-crew-1235363983|text='Star Wars: Skeleton Crew' Announces Cast, Reveals First Look at Star Wars Celebration|date=2023-04-07|work=[[Wikipedia:Variety (magazine)|''Variety'']]|archivedate=20230407131137}}</ref>
