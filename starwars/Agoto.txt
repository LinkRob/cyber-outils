'''Agoto''' was an individual who stashed a number of items in a container on top of a building in the [[city]] [[Myrra]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Outlaws Date" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] discovered the stash and stole its contents.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Agoto was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. In the game, the contents of the stash are randomized, as such the [[canon]]ical contents have not been established.<ref name="Outlaws" />
