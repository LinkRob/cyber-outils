The '''Cardogia system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Tashtor sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square J-16 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Cardogian honey]]|system=Cardogia system}}</ref> of the [[Cardogian honey]].<ref name="Origin">{{YJA|The Jumping Jetpack}}</ref>
