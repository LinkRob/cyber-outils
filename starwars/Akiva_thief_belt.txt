An '''Akiva thief belt''' was a type of [[belt]] that used by [[Thief|thieves]] the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]] to help blend in with the [[jungle]] environment. The [[Color|brown]] belt connected in the front with a large triangular gold colored buckle. An additional strap ran from the buckle to the belt's attached [[holster]]. The belt had an attached pouch as well as space to store [[bacta vial]]s and [[blaster module]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> one of the belts had been stored in a secure area of the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel's]] [[Hutt Poaching Station|poaching outpost]] in the [[Hunter's Canopy]] region of Akiva.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] used the [[ion module]] for her [[Kay's blaster|blaster]] to deactivate the [[ray shield|energy barriers]] protecting the secure area and stole the belt from a container.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
An Akiva thief belt first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, the belt restores the player's health when they perform stealth takedowns and when they use [[smoke grenade]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />
