A '''hideout''' was used by [[Amaiza Foxtrain]] on the [[planet]] [[Valtos]]. Foxtrain used the hideout to hide after she stole [[transit chip]]s from the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[Lepi]] [[smuggler]] [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]] attempted to steal the transit chips for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] but was stopped by Foxtrain. But the Empire raided the hideout to take the transit chips back while the [[bounty hunter]] [[Dengar]] also stormed in to capture Tumperakki.<ref name="Rabbit Hunt" />
