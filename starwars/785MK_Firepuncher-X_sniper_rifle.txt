The '''785MK Firepuncher-X sniper rifle''', also known as '''MK sniper rifle''', was a model of [[sniper rifle]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:Shand_wields_blaster.png|thumb|left|250px|Fennec Shand wielded a modified MK sniper rifle]]
785MK Firepuncher-X sniper rifle,<ref name="CGttSWG" /> or MK sniper rifle for short,<ref name="Chapter 5" /> was a type of [[sniper rifle]] produced by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]]<ref name="CGttSWG" /> and capable of hitting targets at considerable range. At least a modified form of the MK [[sniper]] [[rifle]] even possessed enough power to conceivably penetrate [[beskar]] alloy armor, a metal famed for its near-indestructibility. [[Din Djarin's armor|Djarin's beskar armor]] was able to protect him from two hits, but he opined that the bolt would have penetrated the armor and killed him had it been a close-range shot. Shand's modified rifle also had a small blade concealed in the [[stock]] and a [[drone]].<ref name="Chapter 5" /><ref name="Gathering Storm">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref>

==History==
The [[Master Assassin]] [[Fennec Shand]] used a modified MK sniper rifle, including while she was on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. When two [[bounty hunter]]s, [[Din Djarin]] and [[Toro Calican]], undertook a job to [[Hunt for Fennec Shand|hunt]] down Shand, she attempted to [[Death|kill]] them with the rifle. Djarin recognized the weapon from the bolt that it fired, and chose to approach Shand's location at night rather than during the day. Using [[flash charge]]s, it blinded her from using her thermal [[scope]]. Despite their setback, Shand manage to land a hit on Djarin's [[Zephyr-J speeder bike]] and hit his [[Mandalorian armor]].<ref name="Chapter 5" />
