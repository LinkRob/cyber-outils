The '''Apprentice Legislature''', also known as the '''Junior Senate''' or '''junior legislative program''', was an organization for [[Politics|politically]]-minded youth that was founded under the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Imperial Era|continued]] under the [[Galactic Empire]].

==History==
===Republic Era===
[[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Naberrie]], future [[Monarch of Naboo|queen]] and [[Senator]] of [[Naboo]], joined the Apprentice Legislature when she was eight [[Standard year|years]] old<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> where she was given a tour of the [[Imperial Senate Building|Galactic Senate Building]] and would think of her [[Naberrie family house|family home]] when she was lonely.<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref>

However, after she joined the [[Galactic Senate]], Naberrie, now going by the chosen name of Amidala, admitted to [[Rush Clovis]] of [[Scipio]] that it was still a great difficulty adjusting to the demanding life as a full time senator, especially after having served as queen of Naboo.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />

===Age of the Empire===
After the rise of the [[Galactic Empire]], due to the declining power of the [[Imperial Senate]], many planets stopped sending representatives to the Legislature. The [[Apprentice Legislator]]s dealt with real, but minor, issues and the majority hoped for a future career in politics and used the program as an opportunity to form connections. By [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Leia Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] birth in [[19 BBY]]. The events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' commence just after Organa's sixteenth [[birthday]], which places the events of the novel in [[3 BBY]].</ref> only forty worlds still bothered to do send representatives.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

On her [[Leia Organa's Day of Demand|Day of Demand]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] stated that she would represent her [[planet]] of [[Alderaan]]<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> in the Junior Senate<ref name="Kenobi I">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref> for [[Challenge of the Mind]], as part of her trials to become [[crown]] princess. At the opening of the Apprentice Legislature that year, [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] served as the guest speaker. When she joined, Organa served alongside [[Amilyn Holdo]] of [[Gatalenta]], who she was in a [[pathfinding]] class with, along with several other Apprentice Legislators, which had been arranged by Organa's adoptive mother [[Queen]] [[Breha Organa]] and was taught by [[Chief]] [[Pangie]] of the [[Chandrilan Pathfinding Corps]]. Organa would become friends with Holdo along with several other of the Apprentice Legislators including [[Harp Allor]] of [[Chandrila]]. Organa's adoptive father, Senator [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] encouraged her to find allies in the Apprentice Legislature which she attempted to do, starting with Allor.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />
