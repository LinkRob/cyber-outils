A '''beast call''' was a simple and effective tool used by [[Hunting|hunters]] and [[trapper]]s. Little more than small audio-playback machines encased in a sturdy, impact-resistant shell, beast calls were used to lure [[Sentience|creatures]] out of hiding by speaking to them in their own [[language]].<ref name="Gadgets">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref>
