The '''Bpr-99 fusion reactor''', also known as the '''Bpr-99 power generator and fusion reactor''',<ref name="RRaRS">{{EncyclopediaCite|Republic, Rebel, and Resistance Starfighters}}</ref> was a type of [[fusion generator]] manufactured by [[MPS]]. Dual fusion reactors powered the [[Novaldex]] [[J-77 "Event Horizon" sublight engine]]s commonly installed on [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]s.<ref name="IncrediBuildsAwing" />
