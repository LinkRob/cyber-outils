A '''demerit''' was a mark given for misconduct or deficiency given to a person attending an educational institution<ref name="Edge of the Galaxy">''[[Servants of the Empire: Edge of the Galaxy]]''</ref> or serving in a military organization. In the [[Imperial Military|military]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the field corps operated a three-demerit punitive system, while the investigative branches operated a four-demerit system.<ref name="Aphra 15">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 15|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 15]]</ref>

The demerit system was notably used at the [[Junior Academy of Applied Sciences]] on [[Lothal]],<ref name="Edge of the Galaxy" /> and at the various [[Imperial Academy|military academies]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Sergeant]] [[Currahee]], who served as a drill instructor at the [[Academy for Young Imperials]] on Lothal, was prone to awarding demerits.<ref name="RitR" /> Demerits sometimes included an accompanying punishment. When [[Zare Leonis]] was given demerits at the [[Arkanis Academy]], they came with instructions on his [[datapad]] to clean out the academy's [[diplopod]] stables twice that week after dinner.<ref name="secret">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref>

Demerits were also used within the structure of the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Senate|Senate]].<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>
