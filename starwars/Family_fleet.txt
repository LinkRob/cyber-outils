A '''Family fleet''' was a type of [[fleet]] that was used by the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Chiss family|families]]. There were restrictions on family fleets, with the 40 [[Great Families]] not being allowed to have larger [[warship]]s, and the [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]] being able to have true warships.<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref> However, there where still restrictions on the size of the Ruling Families' fleets.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref> Some notable examples of family fleets where [[Xodlak fleet]], the [[Pommrio fleet]], [[Expeditionary Force Alpha]]<ref name="Greater Good" /> the [[Mitth family fleet]] and the [[Clarr family fleet]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> A family could call in officers from the [[Ascendancy military]] to serve on their family fleets by a declaration of a [[Family emergency]]. This was done in around [[18 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> and led to a [[Hoxim incident|battle]] between [[Task Force Xodlak]], the Pommrio fleet, and Expeditionary Force Alpha.<ref name="Greater Good" />
