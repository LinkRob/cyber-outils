'''Dispatches from the ''Halcyon''''' is a video series, based on the theme park experience ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser]]''. It stars [[Kristin Baver]], as she explores the hotel, and it also has the creators of the hotel, talking about its features. It was announced in ''[[This Week! in Star Wars]]''. The video have since been deleted from the Star Wars YouTube channel.
