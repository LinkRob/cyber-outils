The '''Achillea Great Market''' was located in [[Cadriell]], the [[Capital/Legends|capital]] [[City/Legends|city]] of [[Achillea]]. It was the largest market in the [[Tapani sector/Legends|Tapani sector]], and sold all manner of legal merchandise including rare [[Spice/Legends|spices]] and [[Liquor/Legends|liquors]], [[Computer/Legends|computers]], and alien cuisine.<ref name="LE">''[[Lords of the Expanse]]''</ref>
