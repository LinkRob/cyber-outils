An '''escape circuit''' was a [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] implant that allowed [[droid]]s to resist a [[restraining bolt]]. Only installable in droids, the escape circuit was an internal system that functioned much like cybernetics in organics, augmenting the droid's [[verbobrain]]. As a result, the recipient could resist or even overload a restraining bolt with concentrated effort. Free droids who objected to being victims of a restraining bolt had an escape circuit installed by a [[Technician|droid tech]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], an escape circuit cost 1,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Special Modifications" />

==Behind the scenes==
Escape circuits were mentioned in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Special Modifications]]'', published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Special Modifications" />
