'''''Darth Vader'' 21''' is the twenty-first issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[March 23]], [[2022]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE RETURN OF THE HANDMAIDEN! A figure terrifyingly close to VADER'S dark heart returns &ndash; but with a shocking new allegiance that threatens total chaos! The band of heroes who have bound themselves to Vader's cause finally learn the true nature of their new lord. And how long can OCHI OF BESTOON keep his desperate secret?''
