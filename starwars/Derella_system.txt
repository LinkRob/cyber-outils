The '''Derella system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Core Worlds]]. It was situated in grid square M-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Derella]].<ref name="LPOA" /> Derella was the [[homeworld]] of [[Senator]] [[Cinderon Malpe]], who represented it in the [[Imperial Senate]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="LPOA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Derella system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
