"'''Eopie'''" is the fourteenth episode of the webseries ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Creatures]]''.<ref name="SWKids" /> The episode was released on [[StarWarsKids.com]] on [[January 17]], [[2023]],<ref name="Episode" /> alongside the series' thirteenth episode, "[[Bogling (episode)|Bogling]]."<ref name="SWKids" />

==Official description==
''Aree learns to navigate the perils of the Tatooine desert by observing the adaptable eopies.''

==Plot summary==
The [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] sends [[SF-R3]] ("Aree") and [[CAM]] to [[Tatooine]] to find how Eopies survive in the hot Tatooine [[desert]]. Aree remarks that the eopies are tough in body and spirit. The two droids ride a pair of eopies (a mother and her child) through the desert. Aree's circuits begin overheating due to the hot [[Tatoo I|twin]] [[Tatoo II|suns]] of Tatooine. He decides that he and CAM should continue on foot. The two droids and their eopies soon travel through a sandstorm, which wreaks havoc among the [[droid]]s' sensors. The mother eopie licks her child's eyes. Aree gets the idea of washing his and CAM's [[photoreceptor]]s.

Continuing their journey, they have to choose between an "easy" path and a rocky route. Since the droids have trouble climbing the rocky path, he and CAM decide to take the "easy" path only to encounter a [[sarlacc]], which grabs Aree with its tentacles. The mother eopie rescues Aree from the sarlacc. The grateful Aree thanks the eopie, allowing her to lick him. Aree commends the Aree as reliable steeds, saying that it is better to follow their lead. The two droids then continue their journey on [[Aree's ship|SF-R3's starship]].
