'''Bantam Spectra''', the science-fiction imprint of [[Wikipedia:Bantam Books|Bantam Books]] and the Bantam Dell Publishing Group, published the ''[[Star Wars]]'' novels from the publication of ''[[Heir to the Empire]]'' in [[1991]] to the publication of ''[[Tales from the New Republic]]'' in [[1999]]. Afterwards, the novels were published by [[Del Rey]], though reprints of the novels published by Bantam Spectra still bear the Bantam Spectra imprint.

Bantam Books, a division of [[Random House]], was established in [[1940s|1945]] and is America's largest publisher of mass market paperbacks and a leading hardcover publisher.
