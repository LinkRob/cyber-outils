A '''receiving area''' where the [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] met with local [[Star system|system]] [[governor]]s and his close [[Emperor's adviser|advisors]] existed aboard the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. Located in the 100-story [[Emperor's Tower|isolation tower]], it occupied one spoke of the tower's quadripartite summit. It was dominated by the [[color]] red, mirroring the Emperor's [[Emperor's private suites|private suites]] in the [[Imperial Palace]] on [[Coruscant]]. The room featured a conference table fitted with a [[multi-spectral comm system]], at least four seats, and two circular [[viewport]]s similar to those in the nearby [[Emperor's Throne Room|throne room]]. The area also contained a few antiquities and sculptural pieces, including a statue of the [[Atrisia]]n [[Deity|demigod]] [[Wapoe]], a pair of trophies presented to the Emperor by [[Naboo]]'s [[Theed Council]], and an idol gifted by [[Moff]] [[Quarsh Panaka]]. The front door led to the tower [[turbolift]]s and the throne room, while the back door opened on the Emperor's monitored [[Emperor's sleep chamber|sleep chamber]].<ref name="CL 2016" />
