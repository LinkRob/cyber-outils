The '''Fiery Mustafarian''' was an [[alcohol]]ic [[cocktail]] served aboard the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''<ref name="Sublight Lounge" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The beverage was also served at [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]].<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> The drink was served with a vial that contained [[Magma|lava]] extract from [[Mustafar]], which could be added to the drink for flavor. They were held in a "Mustafarian Climate Simulator" (a container with vials), behind the bar.<ref name="Sublight Lounge" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fiery Mustafarian was first available as a specialty cocktail in the Sublight Lounge in [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]].<ref name="Sublight Lounge" /> For the [[Season of the Force]] event at [[Disneyland]] in [[2024]], the drink became available at Oga's Cantina. The beverage could be purchased alone in a souvenir [[Chandrilan Orb Glass]]. The drink is described by [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] as "El Mayor Anejo Tequila, Apertivo Aperol, Peach Purée, Peach Syrup and Lime Juice, served with a dropper of Spicy Lava Syrup."<ref name="Oga Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/breakfast|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Breakfast Menu}}</ref>
