An '''electroharpoon''' was a type of [[harpoon]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Haze Delik's mother|mother]] of the [[Heliko]] [[Gender|girl]] [[Haze Delik]] was a [[historian]] from the [[Waterworld|aquatic world]] [[Helikoprion]] who focused on ancient [[queen]]s of [[Water|underwater]] [[Planet|worlds]] that wielded [[weapon]]s such as electroharpoons and [[trident]]s.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> sometime after the [[death]] of her [[Delik family|parents]], Delik thought about them while on the [[planet]] [[Palagosal]] and mentally recalled the ancient queens that had used electroharpoons which her [[Parent|mother]] had focused on.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

==Behind the scenes==
Electroharpoons were mentioned in the [[2024]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=SWCE 2023: 9 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref>
