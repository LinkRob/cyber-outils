The '''Aldhani Garrison''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[garrison]] that consisted of a 40-member [[regiment]] on the [[Aldhani dam|headquarters]] on the [[planet]] [[Aldhani]].<ref name="Aldhani" /> If necessary, forces from the [[Alkenzi Air Base]] could be deployed to help those of the garrison.<ref name="The Eye" />

==Description==
The Aldhani Garrison was a garrison of the [[Imperial Military|armed forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] that were stationed on Aldhani to reinforce the Empire's presence on the planet. The garrison was headquartered at the [[Aldhani dam]] with a secondary base being [[Alkenzi Air Base]] located 52 [[kilometer]]s from the dam. The garrison's personnel was a 40-man regiment that consisted primarily of [[Imperial Army trooper]]s who were supervised by [[Imperial officer]]s, though a detachment of [[stormtrooper]]s was present at Alkenzi Air Base where they guarded the facility.<ref name="Aldhani" /> The garrison was commanded by [[Commandant]] [[Jayhold Beehaz]] who resided in his personal living quarters at the dam along with [[Jayhold Beehaz's family|his family]].<ref name="The Eye" /> Other notable officers included [[Corporal]] [[Kimzi]] who oversaw communications <ref name="The Axe Forgets" /> and [[Lieutenant]] [[Gorn]] who served under Commandant Beehaz, but had secretly defected and served as a double agent working for a [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team|rebel group]] that sought to rob the facility of its vast amount of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] that were housed in a vault within the dam.<ref name="Aldhani" /> 

The regiment was divided into at least four squads located at the dam along with a [[Low Road Unit|unit]] that guarded the [[Low Road]] leading to the sacred temple of [[Nasma Brani]] that was located outside of the dam and was lead by a [[Unidentified Imperial corporal 1|corporal]].<ref name="The Eye" /> The garrison utilized [[74-Z speeder bike]]s and at least one [[Max-7 Rono box freighter]] at the Aldhani dam with the latter being positioned inside the dam in the same room where the credit vault was located.<ref name="Aldhani" /> At Alkenzi, the facility housed several [[starship]]s including [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s and at least one [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle]]. The starfighters were piloted by [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot]]s and were lead by [[Alkenzi Air Command]]<ref name="The Eye" />
