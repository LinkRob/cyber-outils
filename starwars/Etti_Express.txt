'''Etti Express''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[fathier]] that [[Reproduction|mated]] with the female fathier [[Farana's Folly]], resulting in the birth of the fathier [[Bonadan Star]]<ref name="Rose Tico" /> around [[31 ABY]]. By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The Last Jedi: Rose Tico: Resistance Fighter]]'' establishes that [[Bonadan Star]] is three [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of {{Film|VIII}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]], therefore meaning they must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[31 ABY]].</ref> Bonadan Star had participated in a [[Fathier racing|fathier race]] in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[casino]] [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. A racing guide that listed Etti Express as Bonadan Star's [[Parent|sire]] was distributed prior to the race. The [[Resistance]] [[Resistance technician|mechanic]] [[Rose Tico]] collected a copy of the racing guide and added it to her [[Rose Tico's journal|diary]] while she was on a [[mission to Canto Bight]]<ref name="Rose Tico" /> in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> They shared their name with the planet [[Etti IV]],<ref name="Master of Evil">''[[Master of Evil]]''</ref> which was located in the same [[Corporate Sector|sector]] of [[Realspace|space]]<ref name="Etti">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Etti system]]</ref> as Cantonica.<ref name="Cantonica">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Cantonica system]]</ref>
