The '''Evader-GT ion drive''' was a model of [[ion engine]] manufactured by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]. The [[YV-666 light freighter]] had two Evader-GT ion drives.<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Evader-GT ion drive was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']],<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> a [[2017]] supplement for the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="A Fistful of Credits">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/2/2/a-fistful-of-credits|text=A Fistful of Credits}}</ref> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters|second edition]] of ''[[Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters]]'', a [[1994]] sourcebook published for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' by [[West End Games]].<ref name="GG6">[[Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters (Second Edition)|''Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters'', Second Edition]]</ref>
