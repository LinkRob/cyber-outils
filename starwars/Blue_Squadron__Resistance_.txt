'''Blue Squadron''' was a [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] in the [[Resistance]] active several [[Standard decade|decades]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]. It served as the primary line of defense for the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar]].

==Description==
Blue Squadron was a [[Resistance]] [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] that consisted of [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]s<ref name="Weapon of a Jedi" /> and [[RZ-2 A-wing interceptor]]s.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> As part of the [[Resistance Starfighter Corps]],<ref name="Episode VII" /> it was initially headquartered at the [[Resistance base]] on<ref name="Weapon of a Jedi" /> [[D'Qar]],<ref name="Episode VII" /> but flew training flights from the ''[[Ninka]]'',<ref name="Alone in the Dark" /> and after the D'Qar base's evacuation, was based on the [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Its commanding officer was [[Black Leader]] [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]].<ref name="TVD" /> [[Squad leader|Squadron Leaders]] included [[Captain]]<ref name="TFA Novel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref> [[Temmin Wexley]]<ref name="Flight Log" /> and [[Lieutenant]] [[Tallissan Lintra]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==History==
===Aboard the ''Ninka''===
Sometime before the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation of D'Qar]], A-wing [[pilot]] and [[Blue Leader]] Tallissan Lintra led four [[Color|Blue]] [[Squadron]] X-wings on a training run from the [[Free Virgillia-class Bunkerbuster|''Free Virgillia''-class Bunkerbuster]] ''Ninka'', among them [[Blue Two]] [[Flight officer|Flight Officer]] [[Stomeroni Starck]] and "[[Blue Three]]." Receiving sensor interference from an ionized [[Unidentified gas cloud|gas cloud]], Lintra aborted the run, but followed a [[distress signal]] into the cloud, rescuing [[Duro Interstellar Freight and Shipping]] pilot [[Staaysha Dekkr]].<ref name="Alone in the Dark" />

===Takodana===
Shortly after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]], the [[First Order]] attacked [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on [[Takodana]], in an attempt to capture [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]. [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] led Blue Squadron in a fierce [[Battle of Takodana|dogfight]] with First Order TIEs and strafing runs on stormtroopers on the ground.<ref name="BS DB">{{Databank|blue-squadron|Blue Squadron}}</ref>

===Starkiller Base===
[[File:Jess_Pava_T-70_XWM.png|thumb|left|200px|Jess Pava flew as Blue Three]]
Blue Squadron, along with [[Red Squadron (Resistance)|Red Squadron]], participated in the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]] where they were lead by Black Leader Poe Dameron. Their mission was to destroy Starkiller Base before it could cause further harm to the galaxy.

Flying X-wings, the squadron consisted of Blue Leader Captain Temmin Wexley with [[R6-D8]], [[Yolo Ziff]] and [[M9-G8]]<ref name="Flight Log" /> as Blue Two, [[Jessika Pava]] and [[R0-4L0]] as Blue Three, [[Pallaris Ven]] and [[BR-BA]] as [[Blue Four]],<ref name="Poe Dameron 28">[[Poe Dameron 28|''Poe Dameron'' 28]]</ref> and an [[Blue Nine (Resistance)|unidentified pilot]] as [[Blue Nine]].<ref name="Episode VII" /><ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref>

The mission was successful, albeit with heavy Resistance losses, including Ven and BR-BA.<ref name="TVD" /><ref name="Poe Dameron 28" />

===Evacuation of D'Qar===
[[File:BlueSquadronRecruit-SWZ.png|thumb|right|270px|A Blue Squadron RZ-2 A-wing]]
Following the Battle of Starkiller Base, the Resistance were forced to evacuate their base on [[D'Qar]] after the First Order discovered their location. Blue Squadron were launched alongside Red Squadron to buy time for the evacuation. Blue and Red Squadrons were tasked with defending the bombers of Cobalt and Crimson Squadrons. With former Blue Leader Temmin Wexley away on a [[Mission to the Outer Rim|mission]], Lieutenant Tallisan Lintra lead Blue Squadron in [[Tallissan Lintra's RZ-2 A-wing interceptor|her A-wing]] for the attack on the First Order's [[Fulminatrix|Dreadnought]] with Stomeroni Starck on her wing.<ref name="Episode VIII" /><ref name="DBRP">{{Databank|resistance-pilots|Resistance Pilots}}</ref>

===Attack on Resistance Fleet===
When the Resistance fleet was ambushed by First Order Supreme Leader Snoke's fleet, The pilots of Blue Squadron attempted to launch their starfighters but was stopped by Kylo Ren when he fired torpedoes at the hanger, killing Lintra and Starck, and destroying Blue Squadron's fighters.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Batuu===
[[Blue Five (Batuu)| Blue Five]] escorted an [[Intersystem Transport Ship]] leaving [[Batuu]] carrying new recruits for the cause, along with Poe Dameron and [[Red Two (Batuu)|Red Two]] in a triangle formation of X-Wings. After clearing the atmosphere, their convoy was attacked by [[First Order]] [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] launching from a Star Destroyer, hidden in orbit. Red Two and Blue Five took evasive action, but the TIE fighters shot down Red Two and closed in on Blue Five. She could not shake their overwhelming forces. While she and Red Two were killed and the transport captured, [[Finn]] and other Resistance fighters saved the recruits, and together blew up the Destroyer, escaping with valuable intelligence for Resistance efforts.<ref name="Rise">{{GEAttr|Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance|Rise of the Resistance}}</ref>
