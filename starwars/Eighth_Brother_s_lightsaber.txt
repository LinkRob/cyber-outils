The '''Eighth Brother's lightsaber''' was a [[Double-bladed spinning lightsaber|double-bladed spinning]] [[lightsaber]] wielded by the [[Jedi hunter]] known as the [[Eighth Brother]], similar to other double-bladed spinning lightsabers wielded by [[Inquisitorius|Imperial Inquisitors]], though it uniquely included a buzzsaw-like feature. It was destroyed by [[Kanan Jarrus]] on [[Malachor]].

==Specifications==
[[File:EighthBrotherLightsaber2-SWTLC.png|thumb|left|250px|The Eighth Brother's lightsaber hilt with saw-like blades extended.]]
The weapon featured a ringed emitter, allowing the twin blades to rotate rapidly without effort from the wielder.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.comicbookmovie.com/fansites/nailbiter111/news/?a=104334|text=The Inquisitor's Lightsaber Skills In First STAR WARS REBEL Clip|work=ComicBookMovie.com|archivedate=20140729201113}}</ref> For a Jedi hunter like an Inquisitor, this function likely served as a way to intimidate opponents, as well as deflect any form of frontal attack. However, this was also the weapon's primary weakness, as a lightsaber-wielding opponent could simply destroy the emitter itself and cut the weapon in half, as was demonstrated by Kanan Jarrus during his [[Action at Mustafar|final duel]] with the [[Grand Inquisitor]] aboard the ''[[Sovereign]]''.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref>

It could also be used to fly, spinning in a helicopter-like motion strong enough to lift the wielder off the ground.<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice" />

Additionally, the Eighth Brother's lightsaber was distinct among other [[double-bladed spinning lightsaber]]s used by his fellow members of the [[Inquisitorius]] in that it had the unique ability to convert into a buzz-saw-like weapon that employed the handle while the blades were deactivated. This mechanism appears in place of the hinges on the emitter rails of other Inquisitors' lightsabers, disallowing the Eight Brother from folding the guard into a crescent shape.<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice" />

==History==
[[File:TheEighthBrother-SWRebelsRetro.png|thumb|left|250px|The Eighth Brother wielded his lightsaber on Malachor.]]
The Eighth Brother was trained in the use of this lightsaber alongside his fellow Inquisitors in [[The Works]] on [[Coruscant]] under the tutelage of [[the Grand Inquisitor]].<ref name="Dark Lord 6" />

The Eighth Brother carried his lightsaber while on his assignment tracking [[Maul]], whom he referred to as "the Shadow" on [[Malachor]]. He used it in [[Lightsaber combat|combat]] against the three rebel [[Jedi]]: [[Kanan Jarrus]], [[Ezra Bridger]], and [[Ahsoka Tano]], when they arrived on their [[mission to Malachor]]. Ultimately, following the deaths of the [[Seventh Sister]] and the [[Fifth Brother]] at the hands of Maul, the outnumbered Eighth Brother attempted to flee from the enemy Force-wielders, using his lightsaber's spinning blades to fly from the [[Malachor Sith Temple|Sith temple]]. However, as Maul and Jarrus had struck and damaged the lightsaber during the fight, it malfunctioned and broke in two in midair,<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice" /> its flaming halves falling to the ground and causing the Eighth Brother to fall to his death.<ref name="Eighth Brother">{{Databank|eighth-brother-inquisitor|Eighth Brother Inquisitor}}</ref>
