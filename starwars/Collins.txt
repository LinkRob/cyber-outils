'''Collins''' was an [[Imperial officer|officer]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] who served under [[Ubrik Adelhard]] in the [[Anoat sector]]. Shortly prior to the [[Gassing of Anoat]], Collins argued with another Imperial officer aboard the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of Adelhard's [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] on strategy. After back and forth, Adelhard, who was standing in front of a cadre of his officers, ordered them to [[Gassing of Anoat|gas]] the [[planet]].<ref name="Insurgency Rising 1" />
