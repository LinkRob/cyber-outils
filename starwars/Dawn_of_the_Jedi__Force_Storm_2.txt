'''''Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm'' 2''' is the second issue in the five-issue [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm]]'', the first story arc of the ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi]]'' comic series. Written by [[John Ostrander]] and pencilled by [[Jan Duursema]], ''Force Storm, Part 2'' was published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] and 18,118 copies of the issue were released to comic shops worldwide on [[March 21]], [[2012]]. The issue received generally positive feedback following its release and was Dark Horse's second-highest selling comic in [[March]] 2012. Set in [[25,793 BBY]], ''Force Storm 2'' introduces the series' main characters [[Shae Koda]], [[Sek'nos Rath]], and [[Tasha Ryo]], the three of whom experience a [[Force vision/Legends|Force vision]] of a [[Tau (individual)|shadowy warrior]] that draws them to the site of a crashed [[Starship/Legends|starship]]&mdash;the first ship to enter the [[Tython system/Legends|Tython system]] since [[36,453 BBY]].

==Plot summary==
{{Quote|Why're you here, Sek'nos?''"<br />"''Had a vision of a man in a metal mask. I opened myself to the Force and it led me here. You?''"<br />"''I saw the shadow man, too.|Shae Koda and Sek'nos Rath|<ref name="Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm 4">[[Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm 4|''Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm'' 4]]</ref>}}
In [[25,793 BBY]], the [[Space station/Legends|space station]] [[Furies Gate]] on the edges of the [[Tython system/Legends|Tython system]] briefly detects an incoming [[Starship/Legends|starship]], and though the ship vanishes, [[Je'daii Ranger]] [[Hawk Ryo]] contacts the [[Je'daii Council]] and warns them of a [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark presence]] he sensed entering the [[Star system/Legends|star system]]. On the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Tython/Legends|Tython]], the [[Je'daii Journeyer]] [[Shae Koda]] loses control of her [[rancor-dragon]] [[Butch]] at the [[Je'daii Temples|Je'daii Temple]] [[Anil Kesh]], forcing her master [[Quan-Jang]] to save the two from falling into the dangerous [[Chasm]] when Koda loses consciousness after witnessing a [[Force vision/Legends|Force vision]] of a [[Tau (individual)|dark warrior]]. Koda sets off to follow the call of her vision, and around the same [[Time/Legends|time]], the [[Devaronian/Legends|Devaronian]] [[Assassin/Legends|assassin]] [[Jaris Kan]] infiltrates [[Ryo Fortress]] on the planet [[Shikaakwa]].<ref name="Storm 2" />

Kan intrudes on a conversation between [[Baron/Legends|Baron]] [[Volnos Ryo]] and his Je'daii daughter [[Tasha Ryo]], and as Tasha defends her father from the assassin, she also experiences a vision of the dark warrior. Tasha departs for Tython, refusing to side with her [[Crime lord/Legends|crime lord]] father after Volnos [[Death/Legends|kills]] a disarmed Kan. At the temple of [[Qigong Kesh]], Sek'nos Rath is experimenting with [[Force lightning/Legends|Force lightning]] and showing off for several girls when he witnesses the same vision, causing him to nearly lose control of his lightning. A fourth individual also shares their vision: the former Je'daii [[Daegen Lok]], who is imprisoned on the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] of [[Bogan]]. Koda, Rath, and Ryo follow the call of [[The Force/Legends|the Force]] to the dangerous [[Rift]] on Tython, where they witness an [[Devourer (Rakatan ship)|enormous vessel]] crash onto the planet's surface. As they investigate the wreckage, the three sense a great darkness, and they encounter the source: the dark warrior from their vision, the [[Force Hound]] Xesh.<ref name="Storm 2" />

==Development==
''Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm 2'' was a thirty-page [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] written by [[John Ostrander]] and pencilled by [[Jan Duursema]],<ref name="Storm 2" /> and the issue was published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] on [[March 21]], [[2012]].<ref name="Dark Horse" /> [[Dan Parsons]] was the issue's inker and [[Michael Heisler]] the letterer, and [[Wes Dzioba]] was the colorist while [[Randy Stradley]] was the editor for the issue. The cover art was drawn by Jan Duursema.<ref name="Storm 2" />

==Release and reception==
18,118 copies of ''Force Storm 2'' were shipped to comic shops worldwide for its March 2012 release. It was the 100th highest-selling comic issue of the month, and the second highest for Dark Horse.<ref name="Comichron">{{WebCite|url=http://www.comichron.com/monthlycomicssales/2012/2012-03.html|text=March 2012 Comic Book Sales Figures|archivedate=20180814190608}}</ref>

==Continuity==
''Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm 2'' introduces three of the series' main characters: Shae Koda, Tasha Ryo, and Sek'nos Rath, who had previously been depicted in the preview issue ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi 0]]''. Many of the other characters and locations that premiere in ''Force Storm 2'' were also first showcased in ''Dawn of the Jedi 0'', such as Quan-Jang and Fury Station.<ref name="DOTJ 0">[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi 0|''Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi'' 0]]</ref> ''Force Storm 2'' also introduces the character of Daegen Lok, who will be a major antagonist of the series' next arc, [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: The Prisoner of Bogan|''The Prisoner of Bogan'']].<ref name="DOTJ 6">[[Dawn of the Jedi: The Prisoner of Bogan 2|''Dawn of the Jedi: The Prisoner of Bogan'' 2]]</ref>

The [[Hunter-class starfighter|''Hunter''-class starfighter]] gets miss-named as "Pteron fighter" in the comic by [[Hawk Ryo]] when talking about his own ship. ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi 0]]'' previously established the looks and functions of both the [[Hunter-class starfighter|''Hunter''-class starfighter]], a space-worthy craft, and the [[Pteron glider]], an air-worthy craft. While "Pteron fighter" sounds like a mix between the two types of vehicles, visual identification leaves no doubt as to what model Hawk refers to.<ref name="DOTJ 0" />
