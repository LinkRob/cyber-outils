In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]]<ref name="Vader 24" /> [[Lieutenant Commander]]<ref name="Vader 25">[[Darth Vader (2020) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 25]]</ref> [[Sabé]] launched an '''attack''' on the [[science colony]] on the [[planet]] [[Gabredor III]]<ref name="Vader 24" /> in order to save a group of [[refugee]]s,<ref name="Vader 23">[[Darth Vader (2020) 23|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 23]]</ref> including Vader's childhood friends [[Kitster Banai]] and [[Wald]], from [[Governor]]<ref name="Vader 24" /> [[Tauntaza]]<ref name="Vader 25" /> who was working with the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate.<ref name="Vader 24" />

After they discovered that the governor was not aboard her [[Tauntaza's flagship|flagship]] but instead in the science colony, Vader had his [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|shuttle]] prepared and they made their way to the governor's location. When they arrived, the governor was attempting to extract a piece of [[Unidentified technology|technology]] she had developed with the help of conscripted refugees from the planet that drained the power from living things. To buy her more [[time]], the governor had word sent to Crimson Dawn, and she had [[B1-series battle droid]]s deployed to attack Vader, though he was able to make his way to the governor where she fired her new technology at Vader, which [[stun]]ned the [[Sith Lord]]. At the same time, Crimson Dawn [[Soldier|warriors]] arrived and evaucated the governor, whilst the [[Sith assassin]] [[Ochi]] and helped defeat the governor's remaining forces, including a [[Unidentified B2-series super battle droid|B2-series super battle droid]].<ref name="Vader 24" />

After the governor's escape, Vader [[Force choke]]d Ochi for what he saw as allowing the governor to escape, though Ochi claimed he had arrived to save Vader. Sabé and Vader then made their way to [[Doctor]] [[Aira]], one of the individuals who had been conscripted to help the governor, who revealed what the governor had developed and showed them what it was capable. After seeing what the governor was capable of, Vader took Ochi's [[speeder bike]] and began to track the governor.<ref name="Vader 24" />
