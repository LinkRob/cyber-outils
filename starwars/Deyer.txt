'''Deyer''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Plant" /> in the [[Anoat system]]<ref name="Fry" /> in the [[Anoat sector]] of [[the galaxy]]. For [[Standard year|years]], Deyer was ruled by the [[Galactic Empire]], which established prisons and [[Imperial Outpost (Deyer)|outposts]] on the world. Prior to the [[Iron Blockade]], the [[Data Seekers]], wishing to learn more about the Anoat sector, placed undercover [[agent]]s within the Empire's ranks, discovering the truths about Imperial workings on [[Anoat]], Deyer's prisons, and more.<ref name="Plant" />
