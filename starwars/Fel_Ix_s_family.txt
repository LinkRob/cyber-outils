A '''family''' of [[Kessarine]] lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. Members of the family included [[Fel Ix]], his two partners [[Er Dal]] and [[Ferize]] and their five [[Youngling|children]], including [[Fe Isle]], [[Fe Remine]], and [[Fe Tale]].<ref name="PoD" /> Two of their children were likely to mature into egg-bearers. Around [[385 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'' to [[382 BBY]]. ''Quest for Planet X'' states that the family lived on [[Dalna]] for three years. Therefore, they began living there in [[385 BBY]].</ref> the family moved from their [[Kessar|home planet]] to [[Dalna]] and joined the [[Path of the Open Hand]].<ref name="QFPX" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the family accompanied the rest of the Path to the [[moon]] [[Jedha]]. After the Path left Jedha after a [[Battle of Jedha|battle]] there, they returned to the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' and as they returned to Dalna, Fel Ix was sent on an important mission by [[Elecia Zeveron|the Mother]].<ref name="QFPX" />
