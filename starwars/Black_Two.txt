'''Black Two''' was the [[callsign]] used by the second member of [[Color|Black]] [[Squadron]].

==History==
===Galactic Civil War===
At the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Mithel]] flew as Black Two in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Black Squadron (Galactic Empire)|Black Squadron]] and was [[Death|killed]] in action.<ref name="X-Wing">{{FFGXW2|set=TIE/ln Fighter Expansion Pack|cardname="Mauler" Mithel}}</ref> Later during the [[Galactic Civil War]], a [[pilot]] flew as [[Color|Black]] Two during a [[Skirmish over an unidentified green planet|skirmish]] over a [[Unidentified green planet|green planet]].<ref name="Vader 2" />

===First Order–Resistance conflict===
During the [[Hunt for Lor San Tekka|search]] for [[Lor San Tekka]], [[Captain]] [[Temmin Wexley]] flew as Black Two, for the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 1" /> Following the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], [[Karé Kun]] flew under the callsign for Black Squadron's mission to locate allies of the Resistance.<ref name="Poe Dameron 29">[[Poe Dameron 29|''Poe Dameron'' 29]]</ref>
