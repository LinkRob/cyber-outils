'''Central City''' was a small country [[City|town]] on the [[planet]] of [[Lothal]]. When the [[Galactic Empire]] occupied Lothal, elite [[stormtrooper]]s were charged with patrolling the streets of Central City, despite its minor importance. One [[Friday]], the young street urchin [[Ezra Bridger]] got upset by the Imperial presence in such an unassuming locale. In his private journal, Bridger expressed his intention to knock out stormtroopers in Central City with the help of his [[energy slingshot]].<ref name="Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger" />
