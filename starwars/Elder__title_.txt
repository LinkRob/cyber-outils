'''Elder''' was a title used by [[religion|religious]] figures,<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> members of leadership or authority, and older beings of various [[species]].<ref name="Legends">''[[The Legends of Luke Skywalker]]''</ref> The title was held by the [[Khepi]] [[A'shora]] while they were a part of an envoy from [[Khepi (planet)|their world]] to [[Coruscant]] in the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> During the [[New Republic Era]] the [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]] was rumored to have met a [[Lew'elan]] Elder named [[Kailla-Glon-Vow]] when he visited the [[Water|aquatic]] [[planet]] [[Lew'el]].<ref name="Legends" />
