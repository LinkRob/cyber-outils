'''F4-MF''' was an [[IG-series/Legends|IG-series]] [[Assassin droid/Legends|assassin droid]] who led the [[Rebel Four]], operatives for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], the team [[Mission to Vatleria|travelled]] to the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Vatleria]], where they uncovered a new [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[stone ruins|base]]. F4-MF led the team to attack the Imperials, but they were all caught in traps activated by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith/Legends|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]]. The droid was destroyed by being stretched into pieces.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Looks like our informant was being candid, team. We most definitely have the beginnings of a secret Imperial base over here!|F4-MF to the Rebel Four on Vatleria|<ref name="Rebel Four" />}}
[[File:F4-MF_Fantastic.jpg|thumb|right|250px|F4-MF destroyed by a trap set by Darth Vader]]
F4-MF was an [[IG-series/Legends|IG-series]] [[Assassin droid/Legends|assassin droid]] who led the [[Rebel Four]], a team of freedom fighters operating for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]], whose other members including [[Falaem Onn]], [[Grimgrim]], and [[Soo Rcharrz]].<ref name="Rebel Four" /> Prior to or during [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]] between the Alliance and the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]], an [[Unidentified informant|informant]] told the Rebel Four that the Empire was establishing a base in the [[stone ruins]] on the abandoned<ref name="Rebel Four" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim]]<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Vatleria]]. The team travelled to the ruins, where F4-MF verified the informant's information after sighting Imperial [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]]. After the [[Droid/Legends|droid]] announced the findings to the team, Rcharrz asked what their plan was; F4-MF confirmed that it was to stop the Imperials on Vatleria.<ref name="Rebel Four" />

The team huddled and F4-MF expressed their belief that they would defeat the small number of stormtroopers opposing them before loudly announcing that it was "Rebellin' time." The Rebel Four stormed the ruins, with F4-MF firing at the stormtroopers with a [[Blaster rifle/Legends|blaster rifle]]. The team were defeated, however, by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith/Legends|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]], who activated traps in which ropes pulled the Rebels into [[Death/Legends|death]] chambers. F4-MF was stretched into pieces by the ropes, with each of their [[Limb/Legends|limbs]] ripped from their body. The other members of the team were also eliminated, after which Vader expressed disappointment at how easily they had been defeated.<ref name="Rebel Four" />

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|What's the plan chief?"<br />"To stop them, of course.|Soo Rcharrz and F4-MF, before the assault on Vatleria|<ref name="Rebel Four" />}}
As an IG-series assassin droid, F4-MF had [[Color/Legends|blue-gray]] plating, red [[Sensor/Legends|sensors]], and pincer-like arms. The brave droid served as the leader of the Rebel Four, directing their movements. F4-MF also believed that the team would never hesitate and was confident in their ability to defeat the stormtroopers on Vatleria, although it led to the end of the Rebel Four.<ref name="Rebel Four" />

==Equipment==
F4-MF wore a blue singlet emblazoned with the [[Alliance Starbird]] on the chest and a [[Belt/Legends|belt]]. The droid also carried a blaster rifle in battle.<ref name="Rebel Four" />

==Behind the scenes==
F4-MF appeared in "[[The Rebel Four]]," a [[Canon|non-canon]] comic written and illustrated by [[Jay Stephens]] and published in the [[Star Wars Tales 9|ninth issue]] of the anthology series ''[[Star Wars Tales]]''<ref name="Rebel Four" /> by [[Dark Horse Comics]] on [[October 10]], [[2001]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/00-458/Star-Wars-Tales-9-photo-cover|text=Star Wars Tales 9}}</ref> F4-MF resembles and shares initials<ref name="Rebel Four" /> with [[Wikipedia:Mister Fantastic|Mister Fantastic]], the [[Marvel Comics]] superhero with elasticity powers that leads the [[Wikipedia:Fantastic Four|Fantastic Four]],<ref name="Fantastic Four">{{Marvel|url=teams-and-groups/fantastic-four/in-comics|text=Fantastic Four}}</ref> which F4-MF also shares initials with.<ref name="Rebel Four" /> Like the hero,<ref name="Fantastic Four" /> F4-MF wears a blue uniform and additionally uses the catchphrase "It's Rebellin' time,"<ref name="Rebel Four" /> corresponding to the catchphrase "It's clobberin' time" used by fellow Fantastic Four member [[Wikipedia:Thing (comics)|The Thing]].<ref name="Clobber">{{Marvel|url=characters/the-thing-benjamin-grimm/in-comics|text=The Thing (Benjamin Grimm)}}</ref>
