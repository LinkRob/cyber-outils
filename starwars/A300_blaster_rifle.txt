The '''A300 blaster rifle''', also known as the '''A-300 blaster rifle''', was a [[blaster rifle]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]], as part of the [[A-series]]. It was notably used by many [[Rebel Alliance Special Forces]] members of [[Rogue One]] during the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="Visual Guide" />

==Features==
[[File:A-300-variants.jpg|thumb|250px|left|The rifle, carbine, and sniper variants of the A300 blaster rifle]]
The A-300 contained a [[power cell]] housing and an adjustable [[electroscope]]. The weapon had several variant configurations. The compact version of the A-300 had a collapsed [[barrel]] and removed shoulder [[stock]]. The rifle configuration contained an extended barrel and shoulder stock. It could also be configured into a [[sniper rifle]] by replacing the barrel with an air-cooling barrel shroud, which looked very similar to the [[A310 rifle]].<ref name="Visual Guide" />
