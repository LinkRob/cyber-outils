'''Robert "Bob" Machray''' ([[May 4]], [[1940s#1945|1945]] &mdash; [[January 12]], [[2025]])<ref name="Passing">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-news/robert-machray-dead-cheers-fire-marshal-dobbins-1236108638|text=Robert Machray, Veteran Stage Actor and Fire Marshal Dobbins on 'Cheers,' Dies at 79|author=Mike Barnes|date=2025-01-14|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20250115001950}}</ref> provided the voice of [[Red Leader/Legends|Red Leader]] [[Garven Dreis/Legends|Garven Dreis]] in the [[2001]] video game ''[[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron II: Rogue Leader]]''. Machray also voiced an Imperial Base Commander in that game as well as [[2003]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron III: Rebel Strike]]''.
