'''Brom Titus''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as an [[admiral]] and later as a [[commander]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy|navy]]. A few [[Standard year|years]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]], Titus was in command of the [[Interdictor (Interdictor-class)|Imperial Interdictor]], an experimental [[Interdictor]] vessel that the Empire was testing in the [[Del Zennis system]], using the [[Starship|ship's]] [[gravity well projector]]s to pull rebel ships out of [[hyperspace]]. Titus managed to capture the [[Phoenix Cell]]'s [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]], however they were [[Rescue on the Imperial Interdictor|rescued]] by Jedi [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Captain]] [[Rex]], who disguised themselves as [[stormtrooper]]s. The rebels destroyed the Interdictor by adjusting the gravity well projectors to pull in every ship nearby when activated, even the [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruisers]], however Titus got away in an [[escape pod]] before the ship's destruction.

Following this failure, he was demoted to commander and placed in command of [[Reklam Station]], which was later [[Mission to Reklam Station|destroyed]] by Ezra, after the Phoenix Squadron stole several [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wing]] [[starfighter]]s from the station. Later on, he was placed in command of the [[Marauder (Arquitens-class command cruiser)|''Marauder'']], an ''Arquitens''-class command cruiser. Titus and his ship arrived at the [[Jalindi Station|Jalindi relay]] just as a rebel strike team, composed of Ezra, [[C1-10P|Chopper]] and [[Sabine Wren]], were attempting to hack it. During the skirmish that followed, Titus was killed when the ''Marauder was'' destroyed after [[Saw Gerrera]] and [[Edrio]] arrived and blew up the relay.

==Biography==
===The Imperial Interdictor debacle===
====Important prisoners====
Brom Titus was an [[Admiral]] in the [[Imperial Navy]] who commanded the [[Interdictor (Interdictor-class)|Imperial Interdictor]], an experimental [[starship]] equipped with four [[gravity well projector]]s capable of pulling starships out of [[hyperspace]]. Following Imperial protocol, Titus kept his [[warship]] in the [[Del Zennis system]], which was classified as an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] test zone. On one occasion, Titus' Imperial Interdictor intercepted a patrol from [[Phoenix Cell]], which was part of a wider rebel network that resisted the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Stealth Strike" />

In response, the squadron's leader, [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]], traveled on the [[CR90 corvette]] [[Liberator (CR90 corvette)|''Liberator'']] to investigate the disappearance of that rebel patrol. Sato was accompanied by the young [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]], who had been sent by [[Hera Syndulla]] to help Sato with his mission. Sensing Sato's ship, Titus' Imperial Interdictor pulled the rebel [[corvette]] out of hyperspace. After disabling the ship's systems, the Interdictor pulled the ''Liberator'' into its ventral [[hangar]] bay, where the crew were captured by Titus' [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Stealth Strike" />

Admiral Titus then ordered his stormtroopers to bring Commander Sato and Bridger to the Interdictor's command [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]]. Sato tried to bluff his way out of trouble by claiming that he and his crew were from the [[Corporate Alliance]], and lambasted the Admiral for illegally detaining them. Titus however, was not daunted by Sato's charade and put him in place by recognizing him as the commander of a growing rebel cell. Titus then turned his attention to Bridger, who claimed that he was [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]]. Titus was incredulous of the boy's claims, and stated that, thanks to the chosen alias, he recognized him as none other than Ezra Bridger. Titus then ordered his men to take the [[prisoner]]s to secure holding cells.<ref name="Stealth Strike" />

====Underestimating the enemy====
[[File:Brom_Titus.png|thumb|left|225px|Admiral Titus during the Interdictor debacle]]
The Admiral then used a [[hologram]] to inform the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] that he had captured Commander Sato and Bridger. Kallus, who had encountered Bridger on numerous occasions, warned Titus to lock him in a secure holding cell with triple guard. Despite Kallus' warnings, Titus refused to believe that a young boy could pose a threat to the Imperial [[Navy]]. Unknown to Titus, the rebels [[Kanan Jarrus]], [[Rex]], and [[C1-10P|Chopper]] had [[Rescue on the Imperial Interdictor|infiltrated]] the Imperial Interdictor disguised as stormtroopers and an [[Imperial communications droid|Imperial communications]] [[droid]] respectively. Meanwhile, Bridger managed to overpower his captors and lock them in his cell.<ref name="Stealth Strike" />

Upon learning that Bridger had escaped, Admiral Titus ordered his crew to lock down all levels of the Imperial Interdictor and to secure the ship's hangar bays. He also gave his men permission to [[Death|kill]] Bridger on sight, not wanting to prove that Agent Kallus was right about not underestimating him. The Admiral's problems were further complicated when he received word from his men that other attackers were impersonating security personnel, and that the rebels had also infiltrated the [[reactor]] core controlling the [[gravity]] well projectors. Despite their difficulties, Titus' stormtroopers managed to capture one prisoner, whom the Admiral recognized as a [[clone trooper]]. He learned Rex's identity after having his number scanned.<ref name="Stealth Strike" />

Admiral Titus had Rex brought to the command bridge, where he attempted to make a deal with him. He praised Rex and his kind for bringing peace to [[the galaxy]], and expressed disappointment that the former [[Cloning|clone]] was working with the rebellion. The Admiral then offered to grant Rex immunity and reinstatement at an [[Imperial Academy|Imperial training academy]] if he convinced his rebel compatriots to surrender. However, Rex refused to make any deals with the Empire. In response, Titus ordered two of his stormtroopers to hold down the prisoner while an [[IT-O interrogation droid|Imperial interrogation droid]] [[torture]]d the clone veteran.<ref name="Stealth Strike" />

====Losing control====
After a brief moment of respite, Titus ordered the torture droid to have a second go at Rex. Before it could do anything, Jarrus, who had infiltrated the bridge disguised as a stormtrooper, destroyed the [[Interrogation droid|interrogator droid]] with his [[blaster]]. Instead of standing his ground, the Admiral ordered his stormtroopers to deal with the intruders before fleeing into a separate chamber. After taking out Titus' men, Jarrus freed Rex and the two rebels fled the Imperial Interdictor in an [[escape pod]]. They then rejoined their rebel comrades, who had fled aboard Commander Sato's corvette. In response to the prisoners' escape, Titus ordered his crew to power up the Imperial Interdictor's gravity well projectors. On the verge of victory, the gravity well projectors malfunctioned, having been sabotaged by Chopper, causing the Interdictor's escort [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruisers]] to crash into the Interdictor.<ref name="Stealth Strike" />

Titus' warship was destroyed in the ensuing explosion, but the Admiral managed to escape in an escape pod. Meanwhile, the rebels fled into hyperspace. Shortly later, Agent Kallus arrived in a [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class landing craft]] accompanied by several [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyers]]. When the Admiral contacted Kallus via hologram to inform him of his predicament, a smug Kallus asked him if he had had any problems with Bridger. In response, Titus nodded in embarrassment.<ref name="Stealth Strike" />

===Rematch at Reklam Station===
====Starfighter squabble====
[[File:Reklam_Station_underside.png|thumb|250px|Titus was assigned to Reklam Station.]]
Following his failure to protect the Interdictor, Titus was stripped of his rank of Admiral and demoted to Commander. He was then reassigned to [[Reklam Station]], a scrapyard in the [[atmosphere]] of the [[gas]] [[planet]] of [[Yarma]] where old [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]]s were being dismantled. Commander Titus oversaw a workforce of [[Ugnaught]] laborers and [[DTS-series dismantler droid|DTS-series Dismantler Droids]]. One of the Ugnaught laborers, [[Terba]], tried to escape but was captured and ended up at the [[Naraka prison|Imperial prison]] on [[Naraka]]. There, Terba met the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]], who informed the Phoenix rebels about the Y-wings at Reklam Station. Seeking to obtain [[starfighter]]s, the rebels dispatched a team led by Bridger to spy on the base. Upon discovering that the Y-wings were being actively scrapped, Bridger decided to change the mission objective and recover the fighters.<ref name="Steps">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref>

One of the Imperial dismantler droids spotted the rebels' ship, the ''[[Phantom]]'', and alerted Commander Titus. In response to the perimeter breach, Titus' subordinate [[officer]] activated the dismantler droids and sent them after the rebel ship. After losing contact with the dismantler droids and the ''Phantom'', Titus contacted [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]], who had earlier warned him about the rebels. Despite his efforts, the rebels managed to destroy the dismantler droids and infiltrate Reklam Station. They then convinced the local Ugnaught work force to help them steal the fighters in return for their freedom. When the conveyor belt system shut down, Titus contacted [[Melch|Laborer 429]] and threatened to throw him off the station if he did not restart the machine.<ref name="Steps" />

====Bitter banter====
[[File:TitusAtSaberpoint-DB.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Titus being threatened by Bridger]]
After one of the Imperial dismantler droids spotted the rebels, Titus activated the magnetic locks on the Y-wings and sent a squad of stormtroopers to eliminate them. While Rex defeated the last dismantler droid, Bridger defeated Titus' stormtroopers and made his way up to the control room. By this stage, Titus had established contact with Governor Pryce, who ordered him to contain the rebels until reinforcements arrived. Shortly afterwards, Bridger stormed the command room and defeated Titus' guards. The two quickly recognized each other from their earlier encounter aboard the Imperial Interdictor. Seeing a chance to regain his former status by capturing the rebels, Titus rejected Bridger's demand that he release the magnetic locks. He attempted to shoot the boy with his blaster, but Bridger destroyed it with his [[Sabine Wren's lightsaber|lightsaber]]. In an attempt to discourage Bridger, Titus claimed that he had disabled the control unit and that the only to free the ship was to shut down all power to the station, which would kill everyone aboard. However, Bridger was undaunted and moved to destroy the generator. Titus tried to stop him from doing so, claiming that he would be doing everyone a favor, but Bridger destroyed it, stating that he was confident in his friends' ability to escape, but not him. Titus ordered an evacuation of the station, then ran off to try and escape, which he successfully did.<ref name="Steps" />

Titus was later seen on board the Imperial Star Destroyer ''[[Chimaera]]'' being escorted away from the [[Thrawn's Office|office]] of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] by a pair of stormtroopers, along with Captains [[Brunson]] and [[Slavin]]. The three had been interviewed by the Grand Admiral as part of his investigation into the identity of a rebel spy in the local Imperial ranks. The group was passed by [[Lieutenant]] [[Yogar Lyste]] and Agent Kallus. The former referred to the trio as officers of the Lothal Sector's Command staff.<ref name="TIE" />

===Trilogy bout at the Jalindi relay===
{{Quote|This is Commander Brom Titus.''"<br />"''Is this some kind of a joke?! I am Commander Brom Titus, and I demand an explanation immediately.''"<br />"''Agh. This is so typical of our luck.|Ezra Bridger and Brom Titus|<ref name="INR" />}}
[[File:Titus_Marauder_INR.png|thumb|right|250px|Commander Titus on the bridge of the ''Marauder''.]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Titus, by now in command of the ''Arquitens''-class cruiser [[Marauder (Arquitens-class command cruiser)|''Marauder'']], arrived at the planet [[Jalindi]] to visit the [[Jalindi Station|Imperial relay]] there. His arrival came just as the [[Spectres]] were undertaking a [[Mission to Jalindi|mission]] to hack into the relay so that the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] could know what the Empire was transmitting through the station. Bridger, [[Sabine Wren]], and Chopper were on the relay's dish when the ''Marauder'' arrived, and were forced to hide behind the dish's main antenna.<ref name="INR" />

When Chopper, on Wren's orders, attempted to tap into the relay's secondary comm system, he instead sliced into the main communications channel, picking up everything being transmitted to the station, including a transmission sent from the ''Marauder'' to the relay. Titus was requesting docking clearance from the outpost. Wren ordered Chopper to put the ''Marauder'' through to the relay so the Imperials wouldn't know anything was wrong, but Bridger had another idea, and attempted to impersonate the relay's commander over the comm. Affecting an accent, he claimed that the ''Marauder'' had not been expected until the following rotation, and that their early arrival was causing difficulties. Bridger finished off by asking the ship to come back the next day.<ref name="INR" />

When Titus demanded to know who he was speaking to, Bridger claimed that he was "Commander Brom Titus." Incredulous, Titus demanded an explanation; when none came, he had the relay dish tilted, causing Bridger and Wren to slide into view of the ''Marauder''{{'s}} bridge. When Titus saw Bridger waving at him through [[electrobinoculars]], he was incensed, and ordered stormtroopers onto the dish. As Bridger and Wren fought the troopers, Wren attempting to plant detonators on the dish, the ''[[Ghost]]'' came in to pick them up. In response, Titus had his cruiser's two [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]]s launched, forcing the rebel vessel to retreat. As the ''Ghost'' played cat and mouse with the fighters in the mist and rock pillars below, another rebel ship, a [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|U-wing]] belonging to [[Saw Gerrera]]'s extremist [[Partisans]], arrived and blasted Titus' cruiser, taking out its [[deflector shield]]s. Before Titus could react, Gerrera dropped several [[proton bomb]]s onto the relay's dish, inviting Bridger, Wren, and Chopper aboard his ship. Titus, failing to notice the proton bombs, ordered the ''Marauder'' to chase after the U-Wing. By the time the cruiser was in position, the bombs detonated, creating an explosion which engulfed the cruiser and killed Titus, along with his crew.<ref name="INR" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:Brom_Titus_Reklam_Station.png|thumb|left|200px|Titus after being demoted]]
Admiral Brom Titus was a cold and arrogant [[Gender|male]] Imperial Navy officer who took pride in his experimental Imperial Interdictor and desired to use it to combat the growing rebellion.<ref name="Databank" /> While Titus was not fooled by his rebel captives Commander Sato and Ezra Bridger's charades, he underestimated the young Bridger and refused to believe that the teenager could pose a threat to the entire Imperial Navy. Titus was also sadistic and willing to torture Rex to death for spurning his offer of rejoining the Empire.<ref name="Stealth Strike" />

Due to his failure to stop the Lothal rebels and Rex from destroying the Imperial Interdictor, Titus was demoted to Commander and was reassigned to managing the Imperial junkyard at Reklam Station. He was tasked with overseeing a workforce consisting of Ugnaught laborers. Based on his conversation with Laborer 429, Titus appeared to be able to understand the [[Ugnaught (language)|Ugnaught language]]. Titus appeared to be a harsh boss and threatened to throw 429 off of Reklam Station if he did not repair the malfunction.<ref name="Steps" /> Despite this, Titus showed at least some interest in the crew's and laborers' fate when he ordered the evacuation of the Station, instead of immediately fleeing.<ref name="Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref>

Ever since the incident on the experimental Interdictor, Titus wished to regain his former rank and sought vengeance against Ezra. Titus was visibly enraged when Ezra impersonated him at the Jalindi relay and sought to destroy the young rebel, believing that his capture and execution would restore him to ranking status. However, Titus would never succeed as he was ambushed and killed by Saw Gerrera, the leader of the extremist Partisans, ending his failing career and ultimately his life.<ref name="INR" />

==Skills and abilities==
Despite being a high-ranking [[Imperial Military]] officer, Brom Titus appeared to have no combat skills. When he was ambushed by the Jedi Kanan, who had come to rescue his comrade Rex, Titus did not stand his ground and instead fled to another chamber while ordering his men to kill the intruders.<ref name="Stealth Strike" /> On another occasion, he attempted to repel Ezra with his blaster but the young Jedi destroyed his weapon with his [[lightsaber]] and knocked him to the ground.<ref name="Steps" />
