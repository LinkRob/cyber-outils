A '''Division Chief''' was a title given to [[Imperial Bureau of Standards]] employees.<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" /> In [[3 BBY]], [[Syril Karn]] held the title while running the [[Bureau of Standards Ghorman field office]] in [[Palmo]] the [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Ghorman]].<ref name="Field Office Databank">{{Databank|bureau-of-standards-ghorman-field-office|Bureau of Standards Ghorman field office}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Division Chief was mentioned in the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the live-action series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', first being mentioned as Syril Karn's title in the season's fourth episode, "[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]," which was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" /> on [[April 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref>
