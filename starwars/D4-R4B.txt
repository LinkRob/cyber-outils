'''D4-R4B''', also known as "'''Arforb'''," was a grouchy [[R4 astromech droid]] that served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. The [[astromech droid]] was stationed in the [[Great Temple]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]] where it excelled at ground maintenance in order to compensate for a programming glitch that meant it was afraid of flying, meaning it was not assigned [[starfighter]] duty. In [[1 BBY]], the [[droid]] was in one of the temple's [[hangar]]s on several occasions when the criminal [[Jyn Erso]] passed through.

==Biography==
D4-R4B, nicknamed "Arforb," was an [[R4 astromech droid]]<ref name="Geektionary" /> that served in the [[Massassi Group]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] based in the [[Great Temple]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]]. One of dozens of<ref name="UVG" /> [[Industrial Automaton]]'s<ref name="Stats" /> [[R-series]] [[astromech droid]]s in the base's [[droid]] labor pool, D4-R4B had a programming glitch that made it afraid of flying. As such, the droid was not assigned [[starfighter]] duty, but excelled at ground maintenance to compensate for its shortcomings.<ref name="UVG" />

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> D4-R4B was stood in one of the Great Temple's [[hangar]]s when the criminal [[Jyn Erso]] was escorted through the room by a member of the Alliance's [[Unidentified Rebel MP|military police]]. Soon after, the R4 unit was stood with fellow astromech<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> [[R2-BHD]]<ref name="UVG" /> beside a [[T-65 X-wing starfighter]] in the hangar when Erso was led back through by [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|Alliance Intelligence]] agent [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] before they [[Operation Fracture|departed]] for the moon [[Jedha]]. Sometime later, D4-R4B was stood in the mouth of the hangar as Erso and Andor boarded the [[Mission to Eadu|stolen]] [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Zeta-class Heavy Cargo Shuttle|cargo shuttle]] [[SW-0608]] in order to [[Battle of Scarif|travel]] to the [[planet]] [[Scarif]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

==Characteristics==
[[File:D4R4BHangar-RO.png|thumb|left|300px|D4-R4B (right) did excellent maintenance work to compensate for its fear of flying.]]
D4-R4B was a grouchy droid, a trait that its fellow astromech R2-BHD found to be illogical.<ref name="UVG" /> It felt great guilt over the bug that prevented it from aiding [[Rebel pilot|pilots]] in combat and so compensated with its excellent maintenance work on the ground. D4-R4B had [[Color|white]] paneling with charcoal gray stripes<ref name="Geektionary" /> and a black [[sensor]].<ref name="UVG" /> The droid's coloring matched that of the Imperial droid [[R4-M9]].<ref name="R4-M9">{{KotoCite|set=ARTFX+|pack=R4-M9|link=R4-M9-Celebration-Exclusive-ARTFX-plus-Kotobukiya.asp}}</ref> D4-R4B stood 1.15 [[meter]]s (3 [[Foot|feet]] 9 inches) tall.<ref name="Stats" />

==Behind the scenes==
D4-R4B was created for the [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Rogue One" /> which was released on [[December 16]], [[2016]].<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/rebel-reading-rogue-one-books|text=Rebel Reading: A Guide to ''Rogue One'' Books|author=[[James Floyd|Floyd, James]]|date=2016-12-14}}</ref> Prior to the film's release, the droid was revealed in the first teaser trailer for ''Rogue One'',<ref name="Teaser">{{SWYouTube|Wji-BZ0oCwg|ROGUE ONE: A STAR WARS STORY Official Teaser Trailer}}</ref> which was released on [[April 7]], 2016.<ref name="TeaserRelease">{{SW|url=news/watch-the-rogue-one-a-star-wars-story-teaser-trailer|text=Watch the ''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer!}}</ref> The astromech was first identified in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="UVG" /> and released on the same day as the film.<ref name="SW.com" />
