The '''Droc family''' was a [[Chiss family]] among the [[Great Families]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. The Droc family was strongly rivaled to the [[Ruling Families|Ruling]] [[Boadil family]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

[[Mid Commander]] [[Droc'tri'morhs]] (Octrimo), a pilot in the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] aboard [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]]'s flagship [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']], was a part of this family. Senior Commander Ocpior, serving aboard the Chiss patrol cruiser [[Parala]], was also a member of this family.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
