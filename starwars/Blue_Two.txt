'''Blue Two''' was the [[callsign]] used by the second member of [[Color|Blue]] [[Squadron]].

==History==
===Clone Wars===
[[Clone trooper pilot]] "[[Axe (clone trooper)|Axe]]" flew as Blue Two during the [[Battle of Ryloth]].<ref name="X-Wing" />

===Alliance to Restore the Republic===
During the lead up to the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Torius Chord]] flew as Blue Two for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="UVG" />

===New Republic===
During the [[Battle of Jakku]], a member of [[77 Squadron]] (which flew as Blue Squadron in that battle) flew under the callsign.<ref name="Hyperspace Mountain" />

===Resistance===
During the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], the [[Resistance pilot]] [[Yolo Ziff]] flew as Blue Two.<ref name="Flight Log" /> [[Stomeroni Starck]] served as Blue Two,<ref name="Alone in the Dark">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=26|story=Tales from Wild Space: Alone in the Dark}}</ref> and later participated in the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation]] of [[D'Qar]].<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>
