The '''eleventh episode''' of the animated web series ''[[Grogu Cutest In The Galaxy]]'' was released on [[July 8]], [[2025]]. It was posted by ''[[Star Wars]]'' Japan on [[Wikipedia:Instagram Reels|Instagram Reels]].<ref name="Instagram" />

{{Media-stub}}

==Plot summary==
While [[Din Djarin]] pilots the ''[[Razor Crest]]'', [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] nibbles on a [[blue macaron]]. The ''Razor Crest'' is being chased and fired upon by a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]], and spins a few times as an evasive action. Due to the spinning, Grogu feels sick and vomits. Djarin helps clean Grogu up.
