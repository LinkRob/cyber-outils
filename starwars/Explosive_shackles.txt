'''Explosive shackles''' were a type device used to secure [[Slavery|slaves]] and [[prisoner]]s. They had small but lethal [[explosive charge]]s built into them to deter escape attempts. Typically composed of a collar and belt joined by [[durasteel]] chains, these devices were in possession of [[detonator]]s that could be set to explode at the push of a button, if the wearer left a specific area or moved more than certain distance from the control unit.<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
Explosive shackles were mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets" />
