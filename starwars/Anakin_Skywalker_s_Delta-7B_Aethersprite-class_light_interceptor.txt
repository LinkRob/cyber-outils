[[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] flew a '''Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor''' during the majority of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="TCW film" />

==History==
[[File:HotLanding-SWM41SWM42.png|thumb|left|270px|Anakin Skywalker crashing his Delta-7B into several B1 battle droids]]

===Early use===
Following the [[First Battle of Geonosis|Battle]] of [[Geonosis]],<ref name="TCW film" /> Jedi General Anakin Skywalker used the interceptor during a [[Battle of Corvair|firefight]] in the [[Corvair sector]]. Skywalker then used it to land on [[Kudo III]].<ref name="Age of Republic">[[Age of Republic - Anakin Skywalker 1|''Age of Republic - Anakin Skywalker'' 1]]</ref> Skywalker flew his interceptor alongside Master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|interceptor]] during the [[Battle of Christophsis]].<ref name="TCW film" /> 

===Battle of Bothawui===
Skywalker used his interceptor during the [[Battle of Bothawui|Battle of]] [[Bothawui]] against [[Grievous|General Grievous]]'s fleet. Skywalker led [[Gold Squadron (Galactic Republic)|Gold Squadron]] and attempted to pursue Grievous' fighter, the ''[[Soulless One]]''. Unfortunately, the interceptor was damaged by debris from the ships, and a sudden explosion knocked Skywalker unconscious. Skywalker was rescued by [[Rex|Captain Rex]], however, [[R2-D2]] was left with the interceptor in the asteroid field among battle debris. The fighter was scavenged by [[Gha Nachkt]]'s ship, the ''[[Vulture's Claw]]'', who stole R2.<ref name="DOAD">{{TCW|Downfall of a Droid}}</ref>

===Mission to sweep outer corridor space===
The interceptor was recovered and took on a new astromech, [[R3-S6]]. As Skywalker took the interceptor to search for a separatist spy station,<ref name="DOAD" /> R3, in reality a separatist spy,<ref name="DOTD">{{TCW|Duel of the Droids}}</ref> activated the interceptor's tracking device. Grievous arrived and attacked Skywalker's interceptor. Skywalker attempted to escape but R3 disabled the interceptor's weapons. Skywalker, R3 and the interceptor were then rescued by his apprentice [[Ahsoka Tano]] and the ''[[Twilight]]''.<ref name="DOAD" />

===Mission to Skytop Station===
Skywalker brought his interceptor with him as he hunted for [[Skytop Station]]. Upon arriving at the station, Skywalker, Tano, Captain Rex and a squad of [[clone trooper]]s jumped out of the ''Twilight'' to land on the base, leaving the starfighter onboard. Skywalker would later use the interceptor to rescue R2 from the falling station.<ref name="DOTD" />

===Battle of Ryloth===
Skywalker flew in his starfighter, alongside Tano who was flying in [[Ahsoka's Delta-7B Jedi Starfighter|her own Delta-7B]], in a campaign to destroy [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class droid fighter/bombers]] which were targeting populated villages across the planet. Skywalker and Tano destroyed the last of them before they bombed the [[capital]] city of [[Lessu City|Lessu]].<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref>

===Battle of Malastare===
After the detonation of the [[electro-proton bomb]], [[Mace Windu]] searched for survivors who had fell in a [[sinkhole]] created by the blast. After finding them, Windu got a bad feeling and contacted Skywalker to come down there. Skywalker flew his interceptor into the crater, only to be attacked by the reawakened [[Zillo Beast]]. The beast slashed the fighter's port-side wing with its tail, forcing Skywalker to crash-land into the side of the sinkhole. Skywalker and R2-D2 managed to escape before the Zillo Beast crushed his starfighter with its giant foot.<ref name="The Zillo Beast" />

===Sabotage of the ''Endurance''===
Skywalker used his Delta-7B to flee the ''[[Endurance]]'' as it began to fall into [[Vanqor]]'s atmosphere as a result of [[Boba Fett]]'s [[Sabotage of the Endurance|sabotage]]. [[Clone cadet|Clone Cadet]] [[Sergeant]] [[Crasher]] contacted him and Windu, who was flying [[Mace Windu's Jedi fighter|his own Delta-7B]], that one of the escape pods containing cadets had gone missing. Unbeknownst to them, it was the pod that Fett was using to make his escape. Skywalker and Windu managed to find the pod and recover the missing cadets.<ref name="Death Trap">{{TCW|Death Trap}}</ref>

===Mission to Vanqor===
Shortly after the ''Endurance'' crash-landed on Vanqor, Skywalker and Windu flew their Delta-7Bs to search for survivors on the planet's surface. While there, they told R2-D2 and [[R8-B7]] to search for survivors while they would head up to the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] to find [[Shoan Kilian|Admiral Kilian]] and [[Ponds|Commander Ponds]], who were onboard when the ship crashed. However, when they got there, a bomb planted in a [[Mandalorian]] [[helmet]] exploded, collapsing the bridge on top of Skywalker and Windu. Skywalker got R2 to go back to the starfighters and call the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] for help. On the way to the starfighter, R2 was ambushed by a [[gundark]]. However, using quick thinking, he was able to attach it, via cable, to Skywalker's fighter and activated the ship's engines, crashing it some miles away and killing the gundark in the process. R2 would use Windu's starfighter to flee Vanqor and get help.<ref name="R2 Come Home" />

===Battle of Kamino===
When General Grievous's fleet arrived at [[Kamino]], Skywalker used his Delta-7B to lead fighter squadrons against the fleet, along with [[Broadside]]. As Grievous began sacrificing the larger transports to protect his command ship, debris from the ships fell into orbit and landed in Kamino's oceans. Obi-Wan Kenobi, who was in [[Tipoca City]], suspected that the droids were up to something and investigated the fallen debris, only to find [[AQ-series battle droid|aqua droids]] using it to assemble [[Trident-class assault ship|assault craft]] for an underwater assault on the city. Kenobi quickly called Skywalker back to the planet's surface.<ref name="ARC Troopers">{{TCW|ARC Troopers (episode)}}</ref>

===Battle of Sullust===
Skywalker flew his starfighter alongside his master Obi-Wan Kenobi, who flew [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|his own Delta-7B]], in the [[Battle of Sullust]]. While Kenobi was pursued by [[Asajj Ventress|Assaj Ventress]], who had entered the battle in her [[Ginivex-class fanblade starfighter|''Ginivex''-class fanblade starfighter]], Skywalker was pursued by two groups of [[droid tri-fighter]]s, which had split up to take him down. Skywalker lured both groups towards a [[Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class light destroyer]] and tricked them into colliding with each other, destroying both groups simultaneously. He then pursued Ventress, who had damaged Kenobi's starfighter, and shot down her fighter. Kenobi crash-landed in the hangar of the Separatist [[Unidentified Providence-class carrier/destroyer (Battle of Sullust)|command ship]] and Skywalker followed him inside. After duelling Ventress, the two escaped the flagship, which had been ordered to be destroyed by [[Dooku|Count Dooku]].<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref>

===Later use===
Skywalker and Tano flew their Delta-7Bs to [[Plop Dribble's]] at the [[Stobar Spaceport|spaceport]] on [[Stobar]]. They had intended to rendezvous with their [[cruiser]], but Skywalker was hungry and had grown tired of government rations. Nevertheless, they witnessed the aftermath of events after [[Savage Opress]] assaulted one of the waitresses working at the diner.<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref>

During the [[Onderonian Civil War]], Skywalker flew his Delta-7B to [[Florrum]] to convince [[Hondo Ohnaka]] to deliver several [[Missile launcher|rocket launchers]] to the [[Onderon rebels|rebels]], allowing them to destroy the new [[HMP droid gunship]]s.<ref name="Tipping Points">{{TCW|Tipping Points}}</ref>

By the time of the [[Defense of Cato Neimoidia|Battle of Cato Neimoidia]], Skywalker had ceased to use his Delta-7B and switched to an [[Anakin Skywalker's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor]].<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref>

==Crew==
The starfighter was piloted by [[Anakin Skywalker]], who also functioned as its captain. Skywalker‘s [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] functioned as the ship’s navigator and repairer should it be damaged in open combat.<ref name="TCW film" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
Anakin Skywalker’s Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|Star Wars: The Clone Wars film]]''.<ref name="TCW film" />
