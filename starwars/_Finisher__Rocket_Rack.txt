The '''"Finisher" Rocket Rack''' was a model of [[Under-Barrel Micro-Rocket Rack]], a type of [[Barrel|under-barrel]] [[weapon]] attachment that fired [[Wrist rocket|micro-rockets]], manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]] and sold for 1,200 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Finisher" Rocket Rack was first mentioned in [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']],<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> a [[2017]] supplement for the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="A Fistful of Credits">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/2/2/a-fistful-of-credits|text=A Fistful of Credits}}</ref>
