The '''Enhanced AT-AP Walker''' was a modified model of the [[All Terrain Attack Pod]] that was used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Characteristics==
The Enhanced AT-AP Walker was a modified variant of the [[All Terrain Attack Pod]], and featured heavy experimental [[armor]] that was salvaged from [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] experiments. As such, the up-armored [[hull]] of the tri-legged [[walker]] was [[Color|orange]] and green. The walker was also armed with two [[Heavy Experimental Driver Cannon]]s, which were effective against [[deflector shield]]s.<ref name="Commander" />

==History==
The Enhanced AT-AP Walker was deployed by the [[Tatooine rebel cell]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], participating in [[Operations on Tatooine|operations]] on [[Tatooine]], the [[Dandoran Campaign]], the [[Er'Kit Campaign]], a [[campaign on Yavin IV]], and other conflicts shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Commander" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Enhanced AT-AP Walker debuted in the [[2014]] mobile [[canon]] [[video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''.<ref name="Commander" />
