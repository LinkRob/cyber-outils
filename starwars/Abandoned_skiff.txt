The '''abandoned skiff''' was an abandoned building in a depression in the [[Mirage (Toshara)|Mirage]] region on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a scout game piece for [[Canto Pass]] had been left in a container outside the skiff.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|canon}}
During 3 ABY,<ref name="Date" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] retrieved the Canto Pass game piece.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The abandoned skiff appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The scout piece found at the abandoned skiff can be used to customize the appearance of the [[Trailblazer]] or the [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
