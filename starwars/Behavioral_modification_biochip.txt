'''Behavioral modification biochips''', also known as '''inhibitor chips''', '''control chips''', '''behavioral inhibitor biochips''', and '''Clone Bio Chips''', were a type of organic bio-chip capable of dictating the response and actions of its host. [[Kaminoan]] [[Cloning|cloners]] implanted them within each and every [[clone trooper]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] at the third stage of their embryonic development. When they were activated, the [[trooper]]s responded to take any order without question. This allowed the [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], secretly the [[Sith Lord]] called [[Darth]] Sidious, to initiate [[Order 66|Clone Protocol 66]], which wiped out nearly all [[Jedi]] marked for termination, and any soldier that did not comply with the order, would be executed for treason against the [[Galactic Republic]]. The [[Galactic Empire]] later employed these devices to keep in line its [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[Wookiee]] population on [[Kashyyyk]].

==Description==
===Purpose===
{{Quote|We modified their genetic structure to make them less independent than the original host. They are totally obedient, taking any order without question.|Lama Su, to [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]|<ref name="Ep. II">{{Film|II}}</ref>}}
[[File:Acme-FollowingOrderscropped.png|thumb|left|300px|Clone troopers under the influence of their behavioral modification biochips lost their free will, turning them to "good soldiers" who would "follow orders" without question.]]
Behavioral modification biochips<ref name="HC10">{{HelmetCollectionCite|10|Databank A-Z}}</ref> were implanted in the [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] on the suggestion of [[Jedi Master]] [[Sifo-Dyas]], who initially ordered the clone [[army]], as a contingency against the [[Cloning|clones]] being given orders by rogue [[Jedi]].<ref name="Conspiracy" /> According to [[Doctor]] [[Nala Se]], it was not uncommon for clones of many different kinds to be implanted with inhibitor chips.<ref name="Fugitive" /> However, after the [[Sith]] hijacked the project, the chips were reprogrammed for another purpose: to force the clones to comply with the [[Death penalty|execution]] of [[Order 66]], which wiped out nearly all Jedi.<ref name="ROTS" /> The control chips were extremely effective at taking over the thoughts of those who had been implanted with them, erasing all existing biases and beliefs in the clone [[trooper]]s to force them to [[Death|eliminate]] the Jedi without question.<ref name="TU">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref> The clones would believe their Jedi [[commanding officer]]s were [[Treason|traitors]]<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> even if they had loyally served under them for [[Standard year|years]]. The chips implanted in the clone troopers could be activated by voice command, with Palpatine only needing to say, "Execute Order 66," and the chips would immediately activate, compelling the clones to obey without question.<ref name="ROTS" />

Clone troopers under the sway of their control chips would prioritize carrying out Order 66 above all else, including their own lives. Under this directive, any and all Jedi leadership marked for termination, and any soldier that did not comply with the order, would be executed for treason against the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]].<ref name="Shattered" /> Although they could still make rational decisions, they could not be reasoned with regarding the order, and would unhesitatingly go after even their own brothers if they realized that other clones were not obeying the order.<ref name="Victory and Death" /> The [[brain]]washed<ref name="Journeys: Beginnings">''[[Star Wars Journeys: Beginnings]]''</ref> clones were also aware of Palpatine's true identity as the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Sidious, with clone [[officer]]s who received the order immediately addressing him as "Lord Sidious"<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref> or "my lord,"<ref name="ROTS" /> and clone troopers who had been completely unaware of the [[Supreme Chancellor]]'s double identity beforehand referring to him by his Sith name.<ref name="Victory and Death" /> Pre-activation, the chips also had the side effect of giving the clones [[Dream|nightmares]] relating to carrying out the command, which were described as a "mission" by some dying clones.<ref name="Conspiracy" /> Clones under the sway of the chips occasionally used the phrase "Good soldiers follow orders."<ref name="TU" />

[[File:Rexstruggles.png|thumb|right|300px|Commander CT-7567 "Rex" struggles against the influence of his inhibitor chip as he tries to resist Order 66.]]
It was possible in rare cases<ref name="Kanan 5">[[Kanan 5|''Kanan'' 5]]</ref> for some clones to resist the will of the chips and hesitate from executing the order, but the act was extremely difficult, and only removing the chip entirely could free a clone from the compulsion to carry out Order 66. Clones whose chips were removed after activation still remembered what they had done under its influence.<ref name="Shattered" /> Even without its removal, the chips started to wear off and become less effective over time.<ref name="BB What's Next">{{SW|url=news/brad-rau-jennifer-corbett-interview-the-bad-batch|text=The Creators of ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' on Season 1's Biggest Moments and What's Next}}</ref>

Although extremely rare, a few clones were immune to the chips,<ref name="Battle Scars">{{TBB|Battle Scars}}</ref> with the odds being particularly high for [[Genetically defective clone|genetically defective]] or modified clones. Even if such clones were not immune, their mutations could still interfere with their chips' performance. In such cases, chips could be amplified to make clones totally loyal.<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> Immunity, however, was not absolute: it was possible for the chip to be fully activated and induce brainwashing as long as it remained present, through means such as head injuries.<ref name="Battle Scars" />

The chips were later installed in [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[Wookiee]]s by the [[Galactic Empire]] to keep them in line. Imperial Advisor [[Gallius Rax]] also had biochips installed in [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] prisoners kept at [[Ashmead's Lock]] on [[Kashyyyk]] as part of an assassination plan against officials of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Life Debt" /> When the chips were discovered, they malfunctioned in a planned degradation before Republic [[slicer]]s could learn much about them.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

===Structure===
{{Quote|I believe this is an organic chip built out of cells.|[[AZI-345211896246498721347|AZI-3]]|<ref name="Fugitive" />}}
[[File:Chip_scanner.png|thumb|left|260px|The chips were organic and implanted into the clones of the Republic Army.]]
The biochips were, as the name indicated, made out of biological material, specifically genetically engineered cells. The [[DNA]] of the chips' cells was different from that of the clones they were implanted in.<ref name="Fugitive" /> They were microscopic in size with an amorphous shape and a punctured, fibrous texture, resembling muscle tissue. The microscopic size of the microchips combined with their biological nature made them extremely difficult to detect with standard medical equipment: it took a [[Level 5 atomic brain scan]] to find the chip inside the head of trooper [[Tup]], after lower-level scans failed to register any anomaly.<ref name="Conspiracy" /> [[Ahsoka Tano]] was later unable to detect [[Clone Commander]] [[Rex]]'s chip with the standard medical facilities onboard a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], relying instead on [[the Force]] to locate it.<ref name="Shattered" />

The inhibitor chip was located ninety degrees from the right orbital floor, below the parietal and temporal intersection, in the brain of the genetically defective [[clone commando]] [[Wrecker]]. However, as a mutated clone, he was structurally different in certain ways from a regular [[Jango Fett]] clone.<ref name="Battle Scars" /> Clones who had had their chips removed sported scars on their right temples.<ref name="The Lost Commanders">{{Rebels|The Lost Commanders}}</ref>

The chips were durable, with malfunctions being rare:<ref name="Inhibitor chip DB">{{Databank|inhibitor-chip|Inhibitor chip}}</ref> out of the millions of clones that served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] over the course of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="MIA">{{TCW|Missing in Action}}</ref> Tup<ref name="TU" /> was one of only a few who suffered from chip malfunctions and the resulting erratic behavior.<ref name="Inhibitor chip DB" /> However, a chip malfunction could also prove lethal, as after Tup's chip malfunctioned, it broke down and began to rot,<ref name="Fugitive" /> and his health began to deteriorate rapidly, culminating in his death shortly after the chip was removed.<ref name="Conspiracy" /> Damage to the inhibitor chip could result in [[headache]]s: Tup complained of one just before his chip went haywire,<ref name="TU" /> and Wrecker began suffering headaches connected to his chip<ref name="Decommissioned">{{TBB|Decommissioned}}</ref> after a series of head injuries sustained post-Order 66.<ref name="Replacements">{{TBB|Replacements}}</ref>

==History==
===Creation of the clone army===
{{Quote|You must remember the chip is a safeguard against betrayal from rogue Jedi. Master Sifo-Dyas was the only Jedi who knew of this. But with his passing, we alone know of this secret, and we must keep it that way.|The disguised Count Dooku, to Lama Su|<ref name="Conspiracy" />}}
Prior to the [[Battle of Naboo|Battle]] of [[Naboo]] in [[32 BBY]], former [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] member Master Sifo-Dyas contacted the [[Kaminoan]]s to order a clone [[army]] for the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] due to his [[Force vision|Force Vision]] of the Galaxy being engulfed with far, but he contacted them without approval of the [[Jedi Council]].<ref name="Ep. II" /> He provided them with the inhibitor chip to act as a safeguard against betrayal from rogue Jedi giving orders to the clones.<ref name="Conspiracy" /> However, Sith [[Lord]]s Darth Sidious and his new [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]] (formerly the legendary Jedi Master Count Dooku, who was also a friend of Dyas), had Sifo-Dyas [[Mission to Oba Diah (Sifo-Dyas)|eliminated]]<ref name="The Lost One" />, with Dooku betraying and orchestrating the death of his friend. With Dyas dead they took over his project, keeping it a secret from the Jedi. They reprogrammed the chips for Order 66, programming them to turn the clones against the Jedi upon hearing an activation phrase. Kaminoans like [[Lama Su]] and Nala Se were deceived by Tyranus into believing the chip was still only in case of rogue Jedi.<ref name="Conspiracy" />

===Conspiracy===
{{Quote|I already know this report's going to fall on deaf ears, but I owe it to Fives to record what I saw. I'm not sure I believe it myself, but there's a possibility that the inhibitor chips the Kaminoans put inside of us have a purpose that we don't yet fully understand.|Clone Captain Rex's confidential grievance report on ARC trooper Fives' suspicions about the "inhibitor" chips|<ref name="Shattered" />}}
[[File:PrematureExecution-TU.png|thumb|right|275px|Tup prepares to shoot Tiplar due to his malfunctioning control chip.]]
During the [[19 BBY|third year]] of the Clone Wars, the control chip conspiracy was nearly uncovered when the Order 66 function of the chip in clone trooper Tup was prematurely activated, causing him to betray and execute [[Jedi General]] [[Tiplar]] during the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]].<ref name="TU" /> Though the Jedi discovered the existence of the chips, they never learned of their true purpose: [[Chief Medical Scientist]] Nala Se claimed the chips were there to make the clones less aggressive than their [[clone template]], Jango Fett, and to repress any insanity they may have inherited from him. In addition, [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Fives]], who had been investigating the matter,<ref name="Fugitive" /> was killed by Clone [[Commander]] [[Fox]] of the [[Coruscant Guard]]. Afterwards, the official story was that Tup and Fives had both been infected with a rare [[Ringo Vinda]]n [[parasite]] which caused their chips to break down.<ref name="Orders" />

Though Fives had warned that the conspiracy went to the highest level and that Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine had tried to stop him from revealing what he knew, [[Anakin Skywalker]] stubbornly refused to believe that Palpatine was capable of what Fives was describing. Fives died in the arms of his friend [[Clone Captain]] Rex, whom he warned of the chips' existence and that the conspiracy was bigger than any of them. As he died, Fives' last words were that the "mission" and the "nightmares" were finally over, which was also what Tup said as he died. Due to Fives having been covertly drugged into a state of incoherency prior to his death, making him seem insane,<ref name="Orders" /> Rex mostly did not believe his claims. However, he could not completely dismiss the possibility that the so-called "inhibitor" chips had a hidden purpose. Uncertain as to whether he believed it or not, out of loyalty to his friend, Rex filed a confidential grievance report in which he outlined the theory, although he was aware it was likely to be ignored.<ref name="Shattered" /> The [[Jedi High Council]] eventually [[Mission to Oba Diah (Clone Wars)|discovered]] the Sith involvement in the clones' creation, but chose to keep it a secret, never making a connection between the discovery and the "inhibitor" chips.<ref name="The Lost One">{{TCW|The Lost One}}</ref>

[[File:Dooku_chips.jpg|thumb|left|270px|Dooku and the Kaminoans were ultimately able to silence the clones who had become aware of the nature of the chips.]]
[[501st Legion]] [[medic]] [[Kix]], who had encountered Fives while he was on the run and whom Fives had told he was being framed, eventually grew suspicious of the official explanation for Fives and Tup's behavior.<ref name="Orders" /><ref name="Crimson Corsair">{{StoryCite|story=The Crimson Corsair and the Lost Treasure of Count Dooku|book=Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I}}</ref> At some point after the [[Battle of Anaxes]],<ref name="Kix note">"[[The Bad Batch (episode)|The Bad Batch]]" is the last episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|The Clone Wars]]'' [[Kix]] appears in. As the episode depicts the [[Battle of Anaxes]], his capture as detailed in "[[The Crimson Corsair and the Lost Treasure of Count Dooku]]" must have occurred afterwards.</ref> Kix, doing his own investigating, found out more than Fives had, including not only the true purpose of the chips but that Palpatine was himself a Sith Lord. However, he drew the attention of the Sith and was subsequently captured by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] forces sent by [[Count]] Dooku. His subsequent denials about sharing any of his knowledge failed to convince his captors, who placed him in a [[cryo-cycle stasis pod]] for transit to [[Serenno]] so Dooku could personally interrogate him. However, the [[cruiser]] Kix was being transported on, the ''[[Obrexta III]]'', was [[Attack in an unidentified sector|attacked]] by Republic forces en route, subsequently crashing into the remote [[planet]] [[Ponemah Terminal|Ponemah]].<ref name="Crimson Corsair" /> [[Ponder]], a clone who lost his leg after his [[Ponder's transport|transport]] got shot down by Separatists during the [[Battle on Metalorn (Ponder)|Battle on Metalorn]] and was regulated to being a cook for the other clones on Kamino before eventually meeting the [[Keshiri]] woman [[Sohi]] and falling in love, had his inhibitor chip removed.<ref name="Sanctuary">''[[Sanctuary: A Bad Batch Novel]]''</ref>

===Order 66===
{{Quote|It was at the end, the end of the war. Our fellow soldiers, the clones, the ones we Jedi fought side by side with, suddenly turned and betrayed us. I watched them kill my master. She fought beside them for years and they gunned her down in a second! Then they came for me. Later they said they had chips in their heads that made them do it. Said they had no choice.|Kanan Jarrus describing what happened during Order 66 to his Padawan, Ezra Bridger|<ref name="The Lost Commanders" />}}
When Sidious executed Order 66, the chips worked as intended, with the overwhelming majority of the clone army brainwashed into obedience, resulting in the downfall of the [[Jedi Order]] and eventually, the end of the Clone Wars as a whole.<ref name="ROTS" /> The few exceptions included clones who had managed to remove their chips<ref name="Shattered" /> and the rare clones who were somehow immune.<ref name="Battle Scars" />

[[File:JesseOrder66-Shattered.png|thumb|right|300px|The clone troopers of the 501st turn on Ahsoka]]
Rex's knowledge ultimately saved his own life and that of Anakin Skywalker's former [[Padawan]], Ahsoka Tano: at the end of the war, Rex, by that time having been promoted to Commander, was onboard the ''Venator''-class [[Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Shattered" /> ''[[Tribunal]]''<ref name="TCW Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> en route from [[Mandalore]] to [[Coruscant]]. Having assisted the [[Mandalore resistance]] in winning the recent [[Siege of Mandalore|siege]], Tano and Rex were delivering the captured rogue Sith [[Lord]] [[Maul]] to the Jedi Council. Both Maul and Tano felt a disturbance, a [[Force vision|vision]] of Skywalker's [[Duel in Palpatine's office|fall]] to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]]. Only knowing something terrible had happened, Tano ran to inform Rex. Upon receiving Order 66 from Sidious, Rex realized that Fives had been right, managing to resist his chip long enough to tell Tano to "find" Fives before succumbing to the programming and [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|attacking]] her. During the short time he was brainwashed, Rex ordered some [[Coruscant Guard]] [[Imperial shock trooper|shock troopers]] to execute Maul, which they attempted to do before being stopped by Tano, who reluctantly released the [[Zabrak]] as a diversion. After escaping the initial assault, Tano was able to find Rex's grievance report, leading her to deduce what was going on before capturing Rex with the help of [[astromech droid]]s to remove his chip, freeing him from the mind control.<ref name="Shattered" /> Maul subsequently brought down the [[Star Destroyer]] by destroying its [[hyperdrive]]; only he, Tano and Rex escaped the [[starship]] before it crashed.<ref name="Victory and Death" /> Afterwards, Tano and Rex went into hiding in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] to escape the new Empire.<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref>

[[File:The-Bad-Batch-in-the-snow.png|thumb|left|300px|Most members of the Bad Batch were immune to Order 66 due to their mutations.]]
Most of the members of the irregular [[clone commando]] unit [[Clone Force 99]]&ndash;nicknamed the "Bad Batch"&ndash;were immune to Order 66 thanks to their [[gene]]tic modifications. In the case of [[Echo]], who had previously been a "reg" clone, [[Tech]] speculated that the experimentation he had undergone<ref name="Aftermath" /> at the hands of the [[Techno Union]]<ref name="A Distant Echo">{{TCW|A Distant Echo}}</ref> in the aftermath of his capture at the [[Battle of Lola Sayu]] had broken whatever programming would have caused him to follow Order 66. However, [[Crosshair|CT-9904]] "Crosshair" was the exception, with his inhibitor chip activating, although the effects were less evident than in normal clones. This led him to fire upon [[Jedi Commander]] [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]] on [[Kaller]] when he and [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Hunter]] followed Dume into the woods after the death of Jedi [[General]] [[Depa Billaba]], making Hunter's attempts to help Dume come off as insincere. When the Bad Batch was later imprisoned on [[Kamino]] following a failed [[Mission to Onderon (Clone Force 99)|mission]] to [[Onderon]], [[Admiral]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] had the effects of Crosshair's inhibitor chip amplified, removing what loyalty he had retained to his squad and making him totally obedient to the Empire. The rest of the Batch shortly [[Escape from Kamino|escaped]] from Kamino after a confrontation with their brainwashed former comrade.<ref name="Aftermath" />

===Age of the Empire===
====Aftermath of Order 66====
{{Quote|What's in your head is more dangerous than you can imagine. I've seen what happens when the chip activates, and I don't want to bury any more of our brothers. Trust me. It is not something you can control. I couldn't. It's a risk you do not want to take.|Rex warns the Bad Batch about not having their chips removed|<ref name="Battle Scars" />}}
In the aftermath of Order 66, Rex traveled to [[Saleucami]],<ref name="Cut and Run">{{TBB|Cut and Run}}</ref> where he had encountered the clone deserter [[Cut Lawquane]] during the war.<ref name="The Deserter">{{TCW|The Deserter}}</ref> Cut had been unaffected by the brainwashing due to not hearing Order 66, and Rex warned him and his wife [[Suu Lawquane|Suu]] about the inhibitor chips and how they had made the clones turn on the Jedi. After Rex left Saleucami,<ref name="Cut and Run" /> he began to fight the Empire<ref name="Decommissioned" /> and help those amongst his brothers who were outside Imperial control.<ref name="Rex">{{Databank|clone-captain-rex|Clone Captain Rex}}</ref>

[[File:Suufamily.png|thumb|right|200px|Although active some chips didn't affect the clones.]]
The [[Standard day|day]] after Rex left, the [[Lawquane family|Lawquanes]] were visited by the Bad Batch, who were also acquainted with Cut, and he brought up the implants Rex had mentioned.<ref name="Cut and Run" /> When [[Omega]], a young enhanced clone who had previously worked as Nala Se's medical assistant,<ref name="Aftermath" /> suggested that Rex had meant the inhibitor chips, Hunter was surprised to learn that the programming Tech had theorized about was connected to a physical device, although Tech had already come to that conclusion.<ref name="Cut and Run" />

Subsequently, Tech came to suspect that Crosshair's betrayal was entirely the result of his inhibitor chip, although Echo was shocked that the chips were capable of such a thing. Tech began constructing a scanner to investigate the activity of the Batch's inhibitor chips and check whether they were truly inoperable. However, Wrecker, the Batch's strongman, received a head injury during a crash-landing on the [[Ordo Moon]] which began giving him [[headache]]s in the area of his chip.<ref name="Replacements" /> Hunter took notice of Wrecker's complaints,<ref name="Rampage">{{TBB|Rampage (episode)}}</ref> and after taking another hit to the head during a [[Mission to a droid decommissioning facility|mission]] on [[Corellia]], Wrecker passed out and mumbled the first words of the "Good soldiers follow orders" catchphrase. He was insensate for some [[time]] as the rest of his squad and the [[smuggler]]s [[Trace Martez|Trace]] and [[Rafa Martez]] fought attacking [[police droid]]s, but came to after Omega contacted him and helped fight off the droids, still himself for the time being.<ref name="Decommissioned" />

[[File:Wrecker_inside_a_Surgical_pod_-_Battle_Scars.png|thumb|left|280px|Rex helped convince the Bad Batch to remove their chips.]]
Some time later, Rex tracked down the Batch after learning of their location from the Martezes. He was concerned when he saw Wrecker having a headache and learned that he had been having them frequently, subsequently becoming alarmed when he learned that none of the Batch had removed their chips. Tech had concluded that the chips were not a danger to them, but Rex warned him otherwise, saying that they had no firsthand knowledge of how the chips made clones lose control. Rex had the Batch meet him on the junk planet [[Bracca]] to use the [[medical bay]] of a [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Clone Force 99)|wrecked Venator]] to remove their chips. After reaching the medical bay, Tech used his completed [[chip scanner]] to assess Wrecker, but in the process, the large clone's chip activated and he attacked the others, decrying them as traitors. Eventually, after Wrecker had cornered Omega, Rex managed to [[stun]] him from behind, and his chip was removed. After Wrecker had come around and was confirmed to be okay, the other members of the Batch had their chips removed, and Rex parted ways with them.<ref name="Battle Scars" />

The Batch was [[Mission on Bracca|confronted]] by Imperial forces led by Crosshair before they could leave Bracca, and Hunter and Omega attempted to get through to him that he was being controlled by the chip.<ref name="Reunion">{{TBB|Reunion}}</ref> The claim seemed to upset him,<ref name="Reunion EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/reunion-episode-guide|text="Reunion" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}}</ref> but he ordered his forces to target the girl before the Batch escaped.<ref name="Reunion" /> After Bracca, the Batch began primarily using non-lethal force against enemy clones, not wanting to kill their brainwashed brethren.<ref name="Common Ground">{{TBB|Common Ground}}</ref> At some point, however, Crosshair had his chip removed, but remained loyal to the Empire anyways.<ref name="Return to Kamino">{{TBB|Return to Kamino}}</ref>

====Growing resistance====
{{Quote|Brothers! What are we doing? We came here to free Ryloth from Separatist control, and we succeeded. But look around you. We're now being ordered to target the very people we swore to protect. And I will not be a part of it any longer! Who will stand with me?|Howzer rallies his squad to abandon the Empire|<ref name="Rescue on Ryloth">{{TBB|Rescue on Ryloth (episode)}}</ref>}}
As time went by, the control chips began to fail,<ref name="BB What's Next">{{SW|url=news/brad-rau-jennifer-corbett-interview-the-bad-batch|text=The Creators of ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' on Season 1's Biggest Moments and What's Next}}</ref> enabling a growing number of clone troopers to question Order 66<ref name="Solitary Clone">{{TBB|The Solitary Clone}}</ref> and the further orders they carried out for the Empire. Some clones, despite their failing chips, remained loyal to the Empire,<ref name="BB What's Next" /> while others abandoned the [[Imperial Military]].<ref name="Solitary Clone" /> On [[Ryloth]], Clone Captain "[[Howzer]]" publicly rejected the Empire in front of his [[Howzer's squad|squad]],<ref name="Rescue on Ryloth" /> overcoming his weakened control chip<ref name="BB What's Next" /> and inspiring some of his squad to join him. Other members of his squad, however, refused.<ref name="Rescue on Ryloth" /> The Bad Batch later confronted Crosshair on Kamino while [[Rescue of Hunter|rescuing]] Hunter, who had been [[Extraction on Daro|captured]] on [[Daro]]. After Hunter again attempted to appeal to him by saying serving the Empire was not his true personality, Crosshair revealed he'd removed his chip. The Batch stunned and captured Crosshair, before being forced to flee [[Tipoca City]] as the Empire [[Attack on Kamino|destroyed]] it.<ref name="Return to Kamino" /> Crosshair was left behind on Kamino after refusing his former comrades' entreaties to leave the Empire.<ref name="Kamino Lost">{{TBB|Kamino Lost}}</ref>

As Kanan last saw Gamut Key in [[Kanan 4|''Kanan'' 4]], that comic must be set fourteen years prior to [[Kanan 6|''Kanan'' 6]]. However, as [[Ezra Bridger]] does not yet have his [[Ezra Bridger's first lightsaber|lightsaber]], it must be set before "[[Path of the Jedi]]." "[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]" places "[[Empire Day]]" (which directly precedes "Gathering Forces") and "Path of the Jedi" in [[4 BBY]], meaning that ''Kanan'' 6 must also take place in 4 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. If ''Kanan'' 6 takes place in 4 BBY, ''Kanan'' 4 takes place fourteen years prior, and ''Kanan'' 5 takes place directly afterwards, it must take place in [[18 BBY]].</ref> the clone officer [[Grey (clone trooper)|Grey]], who had formerly served under General Billaba, was able to overcome his control chip and realize that he and his men had somehow been mentally influenced to turn on Billaba and her Padawan, Dume. Grey had spent most of the time since the end of the war hunting Dume, who had survived after Billaba sacrificed herself so he could escape. Realizing that his men would not be able to overcome the brainwashing, Grey deliberately lowered the [[deflector shield]]s on his ship, securing Dume's [[Rescue of Caleb Dume|escape]] with his own death and that of his men when Dume's protectors, [[Janus Kasmir]] and [[Kleeve]], shot the ship down.<ref name="Kanan 5" /> Dume, who later changed his name to Kanan Jarrus, never knew of Grey's role in his escape and harbored a distrust of clones that lasted for years. At some point, Jarrus found out about the existence of the inhibitor chips. Eventually, he and the other members of the [[Spectres]] were [[Assault on Seelos|sent]] to [[Seelos]] by Ahsoka Tano to recruit Captain Rex, who had been living on the remote planet with fellow clones [[Wolffe]] and [[Gregor]], who had also had their chips removed. Uneasy at being around clones, Jarrus told his Padawan, [[Ezra Bridger]], about what had happened.<ref name="The Lost Commanders" />

[[File:FerrenBarr-2017DarthVader16.png|thumb|left|200px|Ferren Barr activated Order 66 himself and used it to combat the Inquisitors]]
In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Mon Cala date">According to ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'', the [[invasion of Mon Cala]] took place a [[Standard year|year]] into the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor's]] rule. Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the beginning of Imperial rule to [[19 BBY]], the occupation of Mon Cala, seen in [[Darth Vader (2017) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 13]], took place in [[18 BBY]].</ref> a former Jedi Padawan named [[Ferren Barr]] on the run from [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]] led by Sidious' current apprentice, Darth Vader, used [[the Force]] to activate the control chips of nearby [[Purge Trooper]]s, as he had noticed they were newer clones who had not yet had their chips activated. As Barr had just revealed that the Inquisitors present were all former Jedi, this caused the troopers to open fire on them. In the ensuing gunfight, the Inquisitor [[Tenth Brother]] was killed.<ref name="Dark Lord 16">[[Darth Vader (2017) 16|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 16]]</ref>

====Occupation of Kashyyyk====
Once the Galactic Empire [[Proclamation of the New Order|gained]] control of [[the galaxy]], it enslaved the Wookiee population of [[Kashyyyk]]. Enslaved Wookiees were implanted with inhibitor chips which could be used to cause them extreme pain should they rebel against their captors. The chip could be activated in a rebelling Wookiee, causing pain until the Wookiee either fell back in line or died from the pain. As the Empire became more savvy, a common tactic was to instead subject family or clan members to pain, as the Wookiees would often be willing to sacrifice their own lives but couldn't bear to see the suffering of clan or family members. During the [[liberation of Kashyyyk]], a strike team led by [[Han Solo]] disabled the field controlling the inhibitor chips, effectively rendering them useless and allowing the Wookiees to strike freely, though this was met with an [[orbital bombardment]] by [[Star Destroyer]]s from the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Life Debt" />

Ultimately, the Imperial occupiers were defeated when a small [[New Republic]] fleet led by [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]] and [[Captain]] [[Wedge Antilles]] entered the Kashyyyk system and forced their surrender.<ref name="Life Debt" /> Wookiee [[Chewbacca]]'s son [[Lumpawaroo]] likened the inhibitor chips to a song which sounded like the thrumming wings of a swarm of [[Drriw-tcha blood-worm fly|drriw-tcha blood-worm flies]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

Rax also installed inorganic bio-chips in the [[brain]] stems of a hundred [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[prisoner]]s at [[Ashmead's Lock]] [[prison]] on Kashyyyk. These prisoners were part of a secret contingency plan to [[assassin]]ate New Republic government officials and [[New Republic Defense Force|military]] [[commander]]s during the [[Liberation Day (New Republic)|Liberation Day]] celebrations on [[Chandrila]]<ref name="Life Debt" /> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Life Debt Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> Rax and his undercover [[agent]] [[Windom Traducier]] used a transponder installed at the [[Hanna City opera house]] to transmit signals which activated the agents.<ref name="Life Debt" />

Prisoners installed with the bio-chips included [[Brentin Lore Wexley]], [[Jonda Jae-Talwar]], and [[Esdo]]. The unwilling assassins [[Attack on Chandrila|killed]] several New Republic officials and commanders, including [[Hostis Ij]], but failed to kill [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]]. The New Republic authorities managed to subdue the prisoners. Investigations by the [[New Republic Security Bureau]] and the [[slicer]] [[Conder Kyl]] uncovered the chips.<ref name="Life Debt" /> However, they malfunctioned in some kind of planned degradation before the New Republic could conduct further investigations.<ref name="Empire's End" />

===New Republic Era===
[[File:The_Crimson_Corsair_cover_art.png|thumb|right|200px|With his info outdated, Kix woke up years after the fall of the Republic.]]
Around fifty years after the end of the Clone Wars, the wreckage of the ''Obrexta III'' was discovered on Ponemah after an ionic storm recharged the ship's [[distress beacon]]. Over the [[Standard decade|decades]], the ship had acquired a mythical reputation as having carried a treasure of great value to Count Dooku. Since Dooku had been the wealthiest man in [[the galaxy]] prior to his death, the wreck attracted many bands of [[pirate]]s willing to venture into Ponemah's dangerous [[Sea of Sand]] to find the ''Obrexta III'' and claim its alleged valuables. The [[Hunt for Dooku's Treasure]] was eventually won by the [[Sidon Ithano's pirate crew|crew]] of [[Sidon Ithano]].<ref name="Crimson Corsair" />

However, they instead discovered Kix, who had been kept alive inside his stasis pod for all that time. Ithano had the rest of his crew escape with the delirious clone, staying behind to what seemed his death as a giant [[sand worm]] destroyed the wreckage. However, he survived and rejoined his crew with the ''Obrexta III''{{'s}} [[Memory bank|memory core]], which contained the location of every Separatist base in the galaxy, including some that remained undisturbed. Kix told his story to the pirates, including his doomed attempt to reveal the truth of the chips to the Republic and Jedi. As he had nowhere else to go, he joined Ithano's crew.<ref name="Crimson Corsair" />

Originally identified as inhibitor chips,<ref name="Conspiracy" /> they were referred to as control chips in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[The Lost Commanders]],"<ref name="The Lost Commanders" /> and first identified as behavioral modification biochips in the [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 10|tenth issue]] of the magazine ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]''.<ref name="HC10">{{HelmetCollectionCite|10|Databank A-Z}}</ref>
