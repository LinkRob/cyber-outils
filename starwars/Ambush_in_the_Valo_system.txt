An '''ambush''' occurred in the [[Valo system]] against the forces of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]. Imperial agent [[Lyle Kullan]] had infiltrated the Rebel Alliance's [[Rogue Squadron]] in an attempt to capture [[Commander]] [[Luke Skywalker]]. The first attempt failed at the [[Taris shipyards]], and the facilities were [[Destruction of the Taris shipyards|destroyed]]. After placing a [[distress signal]] in the Valo system, Rogue Squadron launched to assist Kullan. They were then met by three [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]] led by Commander [[Calister]]. Following the arrival of [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] forces, Imperial vessels retreated in defeat.<ref name="Squad Goals 2" />
