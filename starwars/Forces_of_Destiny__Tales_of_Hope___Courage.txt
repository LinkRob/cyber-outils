'''''Forces of Destiny: Tales of Hope & Courage''''' is a young-readers book based on the animated micro-series ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]'', as told by character [[Maz Kanata]]. It was published by [[Studio Fun International]] on [[October 17]], [[2017]].

The book adapts the events of 6 episodes of the series: "[[The Imposter Inside]]," "[[The Stranger (episode)|The Stranger]]," "[[Beasts of Echo Base]]," "[[Newest Recruit]]," "[[The Padawan Path]]," "[[Sands of Jakku]]."
