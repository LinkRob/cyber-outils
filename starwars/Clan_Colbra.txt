'''Clan Colbra''' was a criminal organization based on [[Craysol]] and active during the [[Imperial Era|reign of the Empire]].<ref name="LEGO" /> It was led by [[Razze Colbra]]. In [[3 ABY]] the group kidnapped [[Maximilian Rebo|Max Rebo]] and stole [[Han Solo]] from the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]]. With Fetts help, Rebo defeated the [[gangster]]s and they both returned to [[Tatooine]].
