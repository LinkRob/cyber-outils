The '''Forrn system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Xan sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It was situated in grid square Q-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]] and contained the [[Planet|world]] [[Forrn]], which had a [[Forrn's moon|moon]] with deposits of the energy-rich material [[carnium]].<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />

Forrn was ruled by [[Empress]] [[Eliaora]], who the [[human]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] had established a connection with during a visit to Forrn she undertook<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> by [[4 ABY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'' to [[4 ABY]].</ref> [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|Rebel Intelligence]] [[Major]] [[Nioma]] provided intelligence to Organa which led her to think that Forrn had either remained independent or at minimum had been forced into a financial agreement with the [[Galactic Empire]]. Reasonably confident she could convince Eliaora into siding with the forming [[New Republic]], Organa sent a [[holomessage]] to the [[Gender|empress]].<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> the Forrn system was part of the New Republic's territories.<ref name="Mandalorian Visual Guide" />
