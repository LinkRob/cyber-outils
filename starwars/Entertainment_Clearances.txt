'''Entertainment Clearances''' was founded in [[1997]] by [[Cassandra Barbour]] and provided intellectual property licensing to ''Star Wars'' projects, including ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''. Barbour was president of the company when she left in [[2020]].<ref name="Cassandra Linked" />
