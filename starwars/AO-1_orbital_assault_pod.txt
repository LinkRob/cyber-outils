The '''AO-1 orbital assault pod''' was an [[orbital assault pod]] designed by [[Cygnus Spaceworks]] for the use of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. The assault pod was deemed to be too dangerous by [[Republic High Command|Republic Command]], and Cygnus lost its contract. The company later salvaged the programed, resulting with the [[AO-2 Cometstrike-class orbital assault pod|AO-2 ''Cometstrike''-class orbital assault pod]].<ref name="Knights of Fate" />
