'''''Bounty Hunters'' 22''' is the twenty-second issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]] and [[Bryan Valenza]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[April 6]], [[2022]].

==Publisher's summary==
''ALL OUT WAR ON THE STREETS OF CORELLIA! Besieged by an entire syndicate of killers, T'ONGA, BOSSK, TASU LEECH ZUCKUSS, 4-LOM and LOSHA have no choice but to fight their way out through the streets. But will all of the crew make it out of CORONET CITY alive? Meanwhile, DENGAR strikes&hellip;and the Underworld may never be the same!''

==Opening crawl==
{{OpeningCrawl
|name=CHAOS IN CORONET CITY
|text=The bounty hunters have traveled to Corellia to capture
Vukorah, the leader of the Unbroken Clan. With her help, T'onga
believes they can track down and save Cadeliah, the young heir
to the Unbroken Clan and Mourner's Wail crime syndicates.

But Vukorah has recently aligned herself with Crimson Dawn,
and her organization has become more powerful and deadly
than T'onga imagined.}}
