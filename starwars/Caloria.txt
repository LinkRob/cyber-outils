'''Caloria''' was a backwater [[planet]]<ref name="Knights of Fate" /> in the [[Caloria system]] in the [[Tapani sector]] of the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was governed by [[House Reena]] and was at the end of the [[Caloria Run]]. After [[Lyxas Sor]] won the [[Tapani Open]], she was awarded a [[noble]] title and [[Sor's estate]] on Caloria.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />
