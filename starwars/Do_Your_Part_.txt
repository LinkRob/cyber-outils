'''''Do Your Part!''''' was a [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] by the [[Intergalactic Association of Amalgamated Droid Builders]]. It depicts a smiling boy in droid engineering clothes, with the words "Never Too Young" up top, and "To Do Your Part!" at the bottom, with a smaller font statement crediting the work to the Intergalactic Association of Amalgamated Droid Builders.<ref name="Propaganda" />
