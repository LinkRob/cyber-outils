'''Ceore''' was a [[Zabrak]] who worked as [[Head Engineer]] of [[Renpalli Station]]'s [[station engineering corps]]. In [[3 ABY]], she illegally sold fifteen units of [[Coaxium|hyperfuel]] to a [[Unidentified droid (Morenia)|droid]] making a resupply run for the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''. Ceore had the engineering corps handle delivery of the [[fuel]] and kept information about it on her [[datapad]]. Seeking to follow the [[droid]] back to the ''Morenia'', the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] learned that he was flying a [[ZH-40 Tribune-class light freighter|ZH-40 ''Tribune''-class light freighter]] from Ceore.

==Biography==
{{Quote|I don't know why she trusts that droid&hellip;''"<br />[Tosharan speaks Toshari]<br />"''Well, I don't like dealing in hyperfuel either, but she's the boss.''"<br />[Tosharan speaks Toshari]<br />"''She set up the sale, she's the one holding the evidence. Ceore was a [[Zabrak]] who served as [[Head Engineer]] of the [[station engineering corps]] on the [[Toshaal system]]'s [[Renpalli Station]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> she organised an illegal black market sale of fifteen units of [[Coaxium|hyperfuel]] to a [[Unidentified droid (Morenia)|droid]] making a resupply run for the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''. The Zabrak approved the [[fuel]] for transit after it was given an All [[Color|Green]] inspection status, making sure that the delivery was managed directly by the corps.<ref name="Outlaws" />

[[File:MoreniaZH-40-WildCard.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Kay Vess learned about a ZH-40 ''Tribune''-class light freighter associated with the casino cruiser ''Morenia'' from Ceore.]]
As her staff prepared the shipment in one of the [[space station]]'s [[hangar]]s, she threatened to send them back to the surface of the [[planet]] [[Toshara]] to sift [[dodecapede]]s from straw if they did not get the fuel ready. While the preparations took place, Ceore carried evidence of the transaction on her [[datapad]] in the form of the hyperfuel requisition form, leaving her liable for the crime if the Empire took notice. One of her [[Unidentified human mechanic (Renpalli Station)|mechanics]] did not like taking part in the hyperfuel deal and was uncertain about why Ceore trusted the droid from the ''Morenia'', complaining to a [[Unidentified Tosharan (Renpalli Station)|Tosharan]] while working in the hangar.<ref name="Outlaws" />

Seeking a way to reach the ''Morenia'', the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] approached the [[engineer]] to try and learn more about the [[droid]] she had dealt with. Although Ceore was unwilling to freely divulge information about her client, Vess learned that the droid was flying a [[ZH-40 Tribune-class light freighter|ZH-40 ''Tribune''-class light freighter]] and used that information to tail the [[freighter]] back to the [[cruiser]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Hyperfuel, huh?''"<br />"''Mind your own business.|Kay Vess and Ceore|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
Ceore trusted the ''Morenia'' droid enough to carry out illegal transactions with him and refused to discuss the hyperfuel if asked, but was willing to reveal information on him in exchange for a bribe.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Equipment==
Ceore carried a datapad while working in the Renpalli Station hangar.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:CeoreRandomAppearance-WildCard.png|thumb|right|300px|One possible appearance for Ceore.]]
{{Quote|Actually I'm from the ''Morenia''. I'm here to pick up the delivery.''"<br />"''You don't look like a droid. Scram.|The result of Kay Vess taking the option to try and bluff Ceore|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
Ceore appeared in the "Wild Card" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[November 21]], 2024.<ref name="Wild Card released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/1Q4y5lMJwdUUt6qXzbWKPI/star-wars-outlaws-game-update-showcase-wrapup|text=Star Wars Outlaws Game Update Showcase Wrap-Up}}</ref> When the player interacts with Ceore they have the option of either bribing her for information on the droid or trying to bluff that Vess is there to pick up the shipment. If they bribe her two-hundred and fifty [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], Ceore reveals that the hyperfuel is being delivered to a ZH-40 and gives Vess the requisition form but refuses to reveal anything further.<ref name="Outlaws" />

If they attempt to bluff the Head Engineer, she is not fooled as she is expecting a droid rather than a human and so tells Vess to leave. The player must then use Vess's [[merqaal]], [[Nix]], to steal Ceore's datapad in order to learn about the droid's ship. Ceore's appearance is randomised for each playthrough, although her species is consistent.<ref name="Outlaws" />
