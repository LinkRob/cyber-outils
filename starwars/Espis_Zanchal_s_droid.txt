The [[baron]] and [[lord]] [[Espis Zanchal]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] owned a '''droid''' that [[Service droid|served]] him at the [[Zanchal Estate]] on the<ref name="Vader 20" /> [[planet]]<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Vader 20" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Darth Vader (2020) 20|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 20]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore, the events of ''Darth Vader'' 20 must take place in one of those [[Standard year|years]].</ref> the baron was mentioned in a list of members of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate, prompting the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to send his [[Vader's crew|crew]] of [[assassin]]s to [[Death|eliminate]] him.<ref name="Vader 20" />

After the assassins arrived and told Zanchal of his supposed allegiance with Crimson Dawn, the baron claimed he was not affiliated with the syndicate. The Imperial lord [[Commanding officer|commanded]] his [[droid]] to give them access to all of the Zanchal Estate's chambers, including a [[narglatch]] [[cat]]'s, inside which cages containing its [[Foodstuff|food]], people, were. The leader of the assailants, [[Ankala Sahm]], saw the caged individuals and began a fight with the baron's guards, resulting in Zanchal's [[death]]. Shortly after, the lord's droid helped the assassin [[G-90|Gee-Ninety]] access the Zanchal Estate's records, confirming that its owner was not a Crimson Dawn associate.<ref name="Vader 20" />
