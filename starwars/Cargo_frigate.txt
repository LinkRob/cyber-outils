A '''cargo frigate''' was a type of hybrid [[frigate]] design used by the [[Resistance]] in their [[First Order-Resistance War|conflict]] with the [[First Order]]. The design was meant for a spacious cargo capacity with armaments for defense.<ref name="TLJICS">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
