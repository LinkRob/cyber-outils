The '''electro sword''' was a type of electrified short [[sword]] used by the [[Kage Warriors]] of the [[planet]] [[Quarzite]].<ref name="Bounty EG" /> Electro swords were used as [[melee weapon]]s but were also often used as throwing blades, as [[Krismo Sodi]] used one in such a fashion to kill the [[Belugan]] [[Major]] [[Rigosso]].<ref name="Bounty" />

Despite being an energized weapon, electro-swords were no match for a [[lightsaber]], which could easily cut it in half.<ref name="Bounty" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Electro-swords first appeared in "[[Bounty (episode)|Bounty]]," the twentieth episode from the [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|fourth season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="Bounty" /> They were first identified in the [[Encyclopedia (StarWars.com)|Encyclopedia]] entry for Kage Warriors.<ref name="SWE Kage Warriors" />

===Contradictions===
In the Clone Wars episode "Bounty," [[Asajj Ventress's lightsabers]], easily cut several electro-swords half.<ref name="Bounty" /> However, Sodi's entry from ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'' mistakenly states that the swords could withstand a lightsaber blade.<ref name="TCWE">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref>
