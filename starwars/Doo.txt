{{Quote|These yellow-and-gray scalefish are one of six species that live together in Naboo's famed Lake Paonga. They have large jaws and black eyes, and they feed on yobshrimp&mdash;small creatures known to live in the gills of other fish.|From the journal of Ardis San Tekka|<ref name="Bestiary" />}}
'''Doo''' were a [[species]] of [[scalefish]] native to the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. They lived alongside six other scalefish species in the waters around the [[city]] [[Otoh Gunga]] in [[Lake Paonga]], and could also be found in [[Naboo Abyss|the Abyss]], where they were preyed upon indiscriminately by the large [[Colo claw fish]] and [[Opee sea killer]]s that lived there. Doo themselves ate tiny [[yobshrimp]].

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Vertical blue stripes aid in camouflage, and small organs on the underside of their bodies help detect movement and function as an early alarm to alert the doo to approaching predators.|From the journal of Ardis San Tekka|<ref name="Bestiary" />}}
Doo were a [[species]] of [[scalefish]] that possessed pectoral fins behind their head, small dorsal fins along their back, and a large caudal fin at their rear to propel them through the [[water]]. They had small organs on the underside of their bodies, and a large set of jaws which contained rows of sharp [[Tooth|teeth]]. Doo had skin ranging from [[Color|pale]] yellow to pink and gray, and they had blue stripes down their sides from the top of their bodies. Their [[eye]]s were black.<ref name="Questions Answered" /> They could be found in the waters around the [[city]] [[Otoh Gunga]] in [[Lake Paonga]], and in [[Naboo Abyss|the Abyss]], a set of natural tunnels running from [[Naboo]]'s surface to its core. Their prey was tiny [[yobshrimp]].<ref name="AEYNTK" />

==History==
{{Quote|Doo are also found in the Abyss, where they are constantly in danger of consumption by the predatory colo claw fish and the opee sea killer.|From the journal of Ardis San Tekka|<ref name="Bestiary" />}}
During the [[Invasion of Naboo]], while escaping invading forces of the [[Trade Federation]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], his [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], and the [[Gungan]] [[Jar Jar Binks]] swam by a number of doo on their way to [[Lake Paonga]]'s underwater Gungan city of [[Otoh Gunga]], to talk with Gungan representatives.<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Doo first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="TPM" /> Though not named in the film, they were first identified
in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Chronicles: The Prequels]]'',<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Star Wars Chronicles: The Prequels]]''</ref> and were confirmed to be [[canon]] in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''.<ref name="AEYNTK" />

Doo, along with fellow scalefish [[ray]], [[mee]], [[faa]], [[see]], [[laa]], and [[tee]], appear to reference the real-world [[Wikipedia:Solfège|musical scale]]; i.e, "Do, Re, Me, Fa, So, La, Ti, Do."
