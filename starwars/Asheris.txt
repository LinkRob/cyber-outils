'''Asheris''' was a terrestrial [[moon]] located in [[Tagge Space]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 37" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 37|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 37]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> the [[droid]]s of the [[Tagge Corporation]] [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Casus Belli]]'', were [[Scourge crisis|infected]] by the [[Scourge]]. [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]]&mdash;the company's [[Executive officer|leader]]&mdash;deemed the ship to be beyond saving, and had it destroyed, along with the droids still aboard it, by crashing it onto Asheris.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 37" />
