'''Flowers''' were [[Color/Legends|colorful]] reproductive structures in certain types of [[Plant/Legends|plants]]. They could be found all across [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]] and were regarded by [[Sentience/Legends|sapient]] beings for their natural beauty. [[Galactic Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] enjoyed a botanical garden complete with many flowers of different colors within his [[Emperor's skyhook|personal skyhook]].
