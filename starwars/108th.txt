The '''108th''' was a unit within the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|armed forces]] that was active during the [[Galactic Civil War]] following the [[Battle of Endor]] and was situated in the [[Gordian Reach]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Imperial [[Colonel]] [[Madrighast]] unsuccessfully sought to have his forces and the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] join before moving to regroup with the 108th.

==Description==
As part of the [[Imperial Military]], the 108th was a unit active during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[New Republic]]. It maintained its forces in the [[Gordian Reach]],<ref name="Alphabet" /> a [[sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|Instead of leaving for the Gordian Reach, pay us a visit in a short while. We'll have you repaired and restocked before you move on.|Colonel Shakara Nuress, to Colonel Madrighast, suggesting he resupply instead of regrouping with the 108th|audio=108th-2.flac|<ref name="Alphabet" />}}
Following the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Alphabet" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> which resulted in the splintering of Imperial forces due to the<ref name="Alphabet" /> first [[death]]<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and the destruction of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]], the 108th was located in<ref name="Alphabet" /> the Outer Rim's<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> Gordian Reach. At that [[time]], Imperial [[Colonel]] [[Madrighast]] contacted Colonel [[Shakara Nuress]] of the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] while aboard<ref name="Alphabet" /> his [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]],<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> the ''[[Unyielding]]'', following the 204th's [[Attack on the Hellion's Dare|attack]] on the New Republic [[frigate]] ''[[Hellion's Dare]]''.<ref name="Alphabet" />

Madrighast proposed that Nuress have her [[fighter wing]] join his forces before moving into the Gordian Reach and regrouping with the 108th. However, Nuress declined after a veiled threat from Madrighast, informing him that she was building an area of operations in the [[Pandem Nai system]]. Nuress invited Madrighast to resupply with her forces instead of moving to the Gordian Reach and linking up with the 108th.<ref name="Alphabet" /> Several [[Standard week|weeks]] later, Madrighast sent another message to the 204th, inviting them to join him in rendezvousing with the remains of the [[Imperial Navy]] led by [[Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]].<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The 108th was mentioned in the [[2019]] novel [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']], written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Alphabet" />
