The "'''All Aces Battle Royale'''," or simply the "'''All Aces'''," was a [[Sky race|race]] hosted every ten [[cycle]]s for [[ace]] [[pilot]]s on the [[ocean]] [[planet]] [[Castilon]]. During the [[Cold War]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]] in [[34 ABY]], the event was hosted on the ''[[Colossus]]'' [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|refueling station]], and many pilots&mdash;including [[Pamarthen]]s, individuals from the planet [[Hosnian Prime]], and the First Order operative [[Marnia Un'la'na]]&mdash;were among the aces who traveled to Castilon to participate.

Under the pseudonym "Bolt-Shatterer-K," Resistance [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] flew in the race using a borrowed [[Unidentified backup racer|backup racer]] and attempted to remain close to [[Marnia Un'la'na's racer|Un'la'na's ship]] to [[Mission on the Colossus platform|intercept]] a transmission she was sending to the First Order. He and several other pilots&mdash;including [[Ace Squadron]] members [[Torra Doza]], [[Hype Fazon]], [[Freya Fenris]], and [[Bo Keevil]]&mdash;began the race together, flying out after announcer [[Jak Sivrak]]'s announcement. After successfully capturing her data transfer using an [[Republic encryption recorder|encryption recorder]], Xiono was knocked out of the race and crash-landed. Un'la'na won the race's top prize and set a new [[time]] record for the course, and Xiono later took the transmission he had captured during the event and delivered it to the Resistance.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|I need you to scomp into the system and reassign a ship to our garage. After the race, we'll return the borrowed ship before the owners even know it's missing. With me flying, it won't have a scratch on it. They'll never even know it was gone.|Kazuda Xiono, to BB-8, while preparing to go undercover during the race|<ref name="AABR" />}}
Every ten [[cycle]]s, the "All Aces Battle Royale" [[Sky race|race]], also known simply as the "All Aces," was hosted for [[ace]] [[pilot]]s on the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]'' stationed on the [[ocean]] [[planet]] [[Castilon]]. Over [[time]], some individuals began considering the event one of the largest within the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]], even compared to the difficult [[Dragon Void Run]] race, and several [[racer]]s were often eliminated within the first few [[Standard minute|minutes]] in their efforts to win the top prize. The event generally resulted in many pilots traveling to the ''Colossus'' for a few [[Standard day|days]] to participate, which [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza]] "Aunt Z"&mdash;the proprietor of [[Aunt Z's Tavern]]&mdash;became accustomed to.<ref name="AABR" />

[[File:BoltShattererKRacerDecals-AABR.png|thumb|left|300px|Kazuda Xiono borrowed a pilot's backup racer to fly in the race as "Bolt-Shatterer-K."]]
Not long before an "All Aces Battle Royale" was set to be hosted during the [[Cold War]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]<ref name="AABR" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> several [[Pamarthen]] pilots, aces from the planet [[Hosnian Prime]], and the First Order operative and pilot [[Marnia Un'la'na]] traveled to the ''Colossus'' to participate alongside the [[Supertanker fuel depot|platform's]] own [[Ace Squadron|aces]]. The Resistance [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] was confused by the sudden influx of activity and learned from Aunt Z what the "All Aces" was and her opinion that the race made the Dragon Void Run seem like a [[Youngling|children's]] [[speeder]] race in comparison. After learning that Un'la'na was using the race as a cover to send a transmission to the First Order, Xiono [[Thief|stole]] a Hosnian Prime [[Unidentified pilot (Hosnian Prime)|pilot's]] [[Unidentified backup racer|backup racer]] to participate in the event under the pseudonym "Bolt-Shatterer-K" to try to [[Mission on the Colossus platform|intercept]] it at the suggestion of [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]].<ref name="AABR" />

==The race==
{{Quote|There's a big turnout here from all corners of the Outer Rim Territories! All the racers are at their marks, and the 'All Aces Battle Royale' is set to start in three&hellip; two&hellip;one! And they're off!|Jak Sivrak opening the race|<ref name="AABR" />}}
[[File:AllAcesBattleRoyale2-AABR.png|thumb|right|300px|At Jak Sivrak's command, the race's participants began the race, vying for first position.]]
Following the announcer<ref name="AABR" /> [[Jak Sivrak]]'s<ref name="Sivrak">{{Twitter|otherland71|status/1441559335386042368|[[Chris "Doc" Wyatt]]|quote=That was our intention at script, yes.|archivedate=20210925013535}} {{C|In response to: "Was the announcer in 'All Aces Battle Royale' (Star Wars Adventures 16) Jak Sivrak from Star Wars Resistance? He was never shown in the show. Thank you for your time."}}</ref> command to begin the race, numerous pilots began flying alongside each other. Ace Squadron pilots<ref name="AABR" /> [[Torra Doza]], [[Hype Fazon]], [[Freya Fenris]], and [[Bo Keevil]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> flew out from the ''Colossus'' with the other participants in their [[Racing starfighter|racers]]<ref name="AABR" />&mdash;''[[Blue Ace]]'', ''[[Green Ace]]'', ''[[Red Ace]]'', and ''[[Yellow Ace]]'', respectively.<ref name="USWNE" /> [[Marnia Un'la'na's racer|Un'la'na's ship]] pulled ahead of the others with Xiono not far behind, as the former attempted to gain enough lead to send her transmission to the First Order safely. Sivrak continued announcing for the race, indicating that several pilots had already been taken out. When Xiono closed the distance between them, he had his [[BB-series astromech droid|astromech droid]] [[BB-8]] use an [[Republic encryption recorder|encryption recorder]] to capture the message but was knocked further back when another [[Starship|craft]] hit his vessel.<ref name="AABR" />

As Xiono fell behind another ship, he struggled to regain his spot near Un'la'na's vessel. The First Order operative, unaware of the spy's intentions, continued maintaining her lead in order to win the event. Sivrak announced when Un'la'na took the lead to the crowd watching, indicating that the new pilot "Bolt-Shatterer-K" was nearing her position. Moments after intercepting the last of Un'la'na's transmission, Xiono collided with Fazon's ship and was forced to pull out of the race and crash-land, which Sivrak mentioned to the event's spectators. Meanwhile, Un'la'na kept pulling ahead and won the race and its top prize, setting a new time record for the course.<ref name="AABR" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|What happened to my backup ship?!|A Hosnian Prime pilot, after finding their ship in a damaged state after Xiono returned it|<ref name="AABR" />}}
After the race, Xiono recovered the data of Un'la'na's race transmission from his damaged ship's [[data log backup]] and delivered it to Dameron. Dameron and Resistance [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] reviewed the data and thanked Xiono for retrieving it during the race to land a blow against the First Order. The Resistance spy also returned his borrowed racer to its owner, who was shocked to see their backup racer in its [[hangar]] in such a damaged state.<ref name="AABR" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "All Aces Battle Royale" race first appeared in "[[All Aces Battle Royale (comic story)|All Aces Battle Royale]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story written by [[Kevin Burke]] and [[Chris "Doc" Wyatt]] and illustrated by [[Valentina Pinto]]. The story was published by [[IDW Publishing]] in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 16|sixteenth issue]] of the [[2017]] [[Star Wars Adventures (2017)|''Star Wars Adventures'']] comic series,<ref name="AABR" /> which was released on [[December 19]], [[2018]].<ref name="Amazon">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2017-2020-16/digital-comic/713512|text=Star Wars Adventures #16|nolive=1}}</ref>
