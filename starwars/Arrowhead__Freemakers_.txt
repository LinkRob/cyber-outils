The '''''Arrowhead''''' was a [[starship]] built by [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]], who believed that it could turn the tide of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Prior to the [[Battle of Endor]], Rowan, his siblings [[Zander Freemaker|Zander]] and [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi]], and their [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]] undertook a [[Quest to build the Arrowhead|quest]] to obtain the various parts needed to build the ''Arrowhead''. After being built on [[Jakku]], the ''Arrowhead'' was destroyed during a [[Skirmish on Shantipole|skirmish]] over [[Shantipole]] but some of its parts were incorporated into the Freemakers' starship ''[[StarScavenger]]'' just in time for the [[Battle of Endor]].

==Description==
[[File:Arrowhead_Lost_Crystals.png|thumb|left|250px|Quarrie and Zander Freemaker working on the ''Arrowhead'']]
The ''Arrowhead'' was an arrowhead&ndash;shaped [[starship]]. It was equipped with various accessories including a pair of [[embersteel]] blades, a [[Farium fusion conduit]], a [[proton suspension housing]], an [[energy matrix activator]], and a large [[kyber crystal]].<ref name="TTOAN">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Tower of Alistan Nor}}</ref> The ship's embersteel blades could cut through just about anything<ref name="TEB" /> and had a pair of [[blaster cannon]]s on either side.<ref name="TTOAN" />

==History==
===Origins===
Following the [[Mission to Tibalt|mission]] to [[Tibalt/LEGO|Tibalt]], [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] experienced a [[Force vision]] of himself building the ''Arrowhead''.<ref name="Trouble on Tibalt" /> Rowan later related his vision to the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] starship builder [[Quarrie]] but was unable to accurately describe the ship. To teach Rowan a sense of style and craftsmanship, Quarrie brought Rowan and the [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]] to the ancient [[Force Builders]] [[city]] of [[Alistan Nor (city)|Alistan Nor]]. After climbing to the top of the [[Tower of Alistan Nor]], Rowan experienced another Force vision of the ''Arrowhead''.<ref name="TTOAN" />

After escaping an attack by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Hunter droid/LEGO|hunter droid]] [[M-OC/LEGO|M-OC]], Rowan assembled a model of the ''Arrowhead''. Quarrie estimated that they needed several items including embersteel blades, a Farium fusion conduit, a proton suspension housing, an energy matrix activator, and a bantha&ndash;sized kyber crystal. While Rowan's older siblings [[Zander Freemaker|Zander]] and [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]] were daunted, Rowan remained optimistic.<ref name="TTOAN" />

===Quest to build the ''Arrowhead''===
{{Main|Quest to build the Arrowhead}}
Under the leadership of Kordi, the Freemakers then undertook a [[Mission to obtain Embersteel wings|mission]] to [[Mygeeto]] to purchase a [[H-2 executive shuttle|''Theta''-class H-2 executive shuttle]] for its embersteel wings. Despite running into entanglements with the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Graballa/LEGO|Graballa]], the Freemakers managed to obtain the shuttle. Its embersteel wings were then melted and forged into frontal blades.<ref name="TEB" />

While undertaking a mission to rescue the rebel agent [[Bren Derlin]], the Freemakers enlisted the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s help in obtaining a proton suspension housing from a wrecked [[Corellian Defender]] on the acidic world of [[Taul]]. Despite Hondo stealing their starship the ''[[StarScavenger]]'' and being attacked by M-OC, the Freemakers managed to escape offworld with the proton suspension housing with the help of Roger, who subdued Hondo.<ref name="TSOT">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Storms of Taul}}</ref>

The Freemakers later traveled to their former home [[Freemaker Salvage and Repair]] on [[The Wheel#Behind the Scenes|the Wheel]] to [[Mission to obtain an energy matrix activator|obtain]] an energy matrix activator. Kordi's plan was to sneak into their former home at night to recover it. However, their mission was complicated by Roger's run-in with several [[Ranat]]s and the arrival of [[Graballa/LEGO|Graballa]] the [[Hutt]] and his [[bounty hunter]]s, who wanted to hand over Rowan to [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. With the help of Kordi, the Freemakers managed to escape their captors and reunite with Roger. After eluding Darth Vader, they escaped back to the rebel fleet with the energy matrix activator.<ref name="RTTW">{{FreemakerAdventures|Return to the Wheel}}</ref>

After a [[Mission to Qalydon|failed attempt]] to acquire kyber crystals on the planet [[Qalydon]],<ref name="TLCOQ">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Lost Crystals of Qalydon}}</ref> the Freemakers traveled to an uncharted [[Unidentified planet (Baird Kantoo)|planet]], where [[Jedi Master]] [[Baird Kantoo]] had forged the [[Kyber Saber]] years earlier. Using his [[Force Builders|Force building]] powers, Rowan forged a giant kyber crystal out of several small crystals in a pit. Having collected all the pieces, the Freemakers then headed back to the rebel fleet.<ref name="TPATP">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Pit and the Pinnacle}}</ref>

Since the rebel fleet was under [[Mission to save the Rebel Fleet|attack]] from Imperial forces, Quarrie convinced the Freemakers that they had to assemble the ''Arrowhead'' offworld. Quarrie tasked the rebel pilot [[Lieutenant]] [[Colvett Valeria]] with flying the ''Arrowhead'' to the planet [[Jakku]]. However, Valeria landed the ''Arrowhead'' in quick sand, causing the ship to sink and explode. Though the managed to recover most of the pieces, the ship's energy matrix activator fell into the hands of the [[Imperial officer]]s [[Durpin]] and [[Plumestriker]].<ref name="Flight of the Arrowhead" />

Following a failed attempt by Zander and Roger to cover the matrix activator, Kordi disguised herself as [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and distracted the Imperial officers long enough for Valeria to knock them out. Valeria and Zander distracted the Imperials long enough for Rowan and Quarrie to rebuild the ''Arrowhead''. After Zander used the ''Arrowhead'' to destroy Durpin and Plumestriker's [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST walker]], he and Rowan used the fully operational starship to destroy an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]], forcing the Empire to retreat. The Freemakers were regarded by the rebels as heroes and received various promotions.<ref name="Flight of the Arrowhead" />

===Rebel service===
The Emperor was furious that the Imperial fleet was being destroyed by the ''Arrowhead'' and summoned his top servants Darth Vader and M-OC.<ref name="Flight of the Arrowhead" /> The Emperor tasked Lord Vader and M-OC with working together to capture Rowan Freemaker, the ''Arrowhead'' and the Kyber Saber.<ref name="APR">{{FreemakerAdventures|A Perilous Rescue}}</ref>

In the service of the Rebel Alliance, the ''Arrowhead'' saw [[Skirmish on Kashyyyk (Freemakers)|action]] against Imperial forces on [[Kashyyyk]]. Aiding Lieutenant Valeria's rebel and Wookiee forces on Kashyyyk, the ''Arrowhead'' destroyed an AT-ST walker and three [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT walkers]]. This incident terrified the [[Unidentified AT-AT commander (Kashyyyk)|AT-AT commander]], who recounted his story to [[Commander]] [[Estoc]] of the Star Destroyer [[Desolator (Imperial-class)|''Desolator'']].<ref name="APR" />

Later, Zander flew the ''Arrowhead'' in support of Lieutenant Valeria and Blue Squadron during a [[Assault near Alzoc III|raid]] on an Imperial repair installation near [[Alzoc III]]. Despite the AT-AT commander's warnings, Estoc underestimated the threat of the ''Arrowhead''. The ''Arrowhead'' destroyed several Star Destroyers with its Embersteel Blade. Estoc's Star Destroyer tried to trap the ''Arrowhead'' but Valeria disabled the Star Destroyer's [[tractor beam projector]]. This allowed the ''Arrowhead'' to destroy the ''Desolator'' and return triumphantly to the rebel fleet. However, the rebel victory was overshadowed by Lord Vader's [[Capture of Rowan Freemaker|capture]] of Rowan.<ref name="APR" />

===Rescuing Rowan===
Unable to get the [[Alliance High Command]] to authorize a mission to [[Rescue of Rowan Freemaker|rescue]] Rowan, Zander, Kordi, and M-OC stole the ''Arrowhead'' from the Rebel Alliance. Lieutenant Valeria allowed them to "steal" the ship due to her sympathies for their plight.<ref name="EFC">{{FreemakerAdventures|Escape from Coruscant}}</ref>

The Freemakers took the ''Arrowhead'' to the heavily fortified Imperial capital of [[Coruscant]]. The ''Arrowhead'' destroyed three Star Destroyers and several TIE fighters. Roger then dropped Kordi and Zander on the top spire of the [[Imperial Palace]] so that they could rescue Rowan. Flying the ''Arrowhead'', Roger destroyed three more TIE fighters and M-OC's starship ''[[Tracker I]]''.<ref name="EFC" />

He then flew the ''Arrowhead'' back to the Imperial Palace, intending to pick up the Freemakers. However, M-OC had clung to the ship's hull and hijacked the ''Arrowhead'' when Roger opened the canopy. After tearing Roger apart and hurling him out, he opened fire on the Freemakers, causing them to fall to the battlements. However, the Freemakers survived the fall due to Rowan's efforts and escaped offworld on an [[Ugly]].<ref name="EFC" />

===In M-OC's hands===
After capturing the ''Arrowhead'', M-OC informed the Emperor that he had captured the starship to atone for his failure to capture Rowan. Having obtained the location of a [[Unidentified planet (Baird Kantoo)|planet]] rich in [[kyber crystal]]s from Rowan, the Emperor tasked M-OC with hunting down and destroying the Freemakers.<ref name="EFC" />

Flying the ''Arrowhead'', M-OC attacked the Freemaker's TIE "[[Ugly]]." The Freemakers tried to surrender but M-OC refused to accept it and blasted their starship's [[solar collector]]s. Before M-OC could finish off the Freemakers, Quarrie arrived aboard the ''StarScavenger'' and rescued them. Though the ''StarScavenger'' managed to escape into hyperspace, M-OC managed to track them down using a [[XX-23 S-thread tracker]].<ref name="Free Fall">{{FreemakerAdventures|Free Fall}}</ref>

The ''Arrowhead'' caught up with the ''StarScavenger'' in an asteroid field but the Freemakers managed to flee to the stormy planet of [[Shantipole]], where Quarrie had a [[Shantipole landing field|base]]. While M-OC was unable to pursue the Freemakers due to the planet's stormy upper atmosphere, the relentless droid circled the planet with his ship, waiting to destroy the Freemakers should they try to escape offworld.<ref name="Free Fall" />

At the urging of Rowan, the Freemakers and Quarrie decided to lure M-OC into a [[Skirmish on Shantipole|trap]]. After baiting M-OC, the Freemakers used a large [[tractor beam projector]] attached to the ''StarScavenger'' to drag the ''Arrowhead'' into the upper atmosphere. Both ships were hit by lightning, causing them to lose power. Rowan then landed on the ''Arrowhead'' and began removing the giant kyber crystal from the ''Arrowhead''{{'s}} power core.<ref name="Free Fall" />

M-OC engaged Rowan in a [[lightsaber duel]]. Rowan managed to retrieve the kyber crystal with the help of Roger, who was flying the [[Mini Scavenger]]. Zander then rammed the ''StarScavenger'' into the ''Arrowhead'' before bailing out with Kordi and Quarrie. Both ships were destroyed in the subsequent explosion with their wreckage crashing on Shantipole's surface.<ref name="Free Fall" />

===Legacy===
Despite the destruction of the ''Arrowhead'', its legacy lived on. M-OC survived the crash and built a mobile starship that incorporated parts of the ''Arrowhead'' including the giant kyber crystal and proton suspension housing. He then traveled to the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] in order to identify a design flaw and protect the battle station.<ref name="Free Fall" />

When rebuilding the Freemakers' starship ''StarScavenger'', Rowan incorporated the starship's Embersteel Blade into the ''StarScavenger''. This allowed the Freemakers to breach the second Death Star during the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="ROTROTJ">{{FreemakerAdventures|Return of the Return of the Jedi}}</ref> After the Battle, the Freemakers attempted to leave in the ship, but an [[Ewok]] befriended by Roger snuck onboard multiple times despite Roger's attempts to leave him on Endor.<ref name="Yub Nub!">{{All-Stars|Yub Nub!}}</ref>

Later on, the ship was used by the Freemakers in the [[Battle of Jakku]]. The Freemakers briefly landed to repair [[Lieutenant]] [[Colvett Valeria]]'s ship, then took off once more after repairs were complete.<ref name="The Power of Jakku">{{All-Stars|The Power at Jakku}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Arrowhead'' forms an important element of the plot of the [[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures Season Two|second season]] of [[Bill Motz]] and [[Bob Roth]]'s ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]''. The starship was designed by [[Lucasfilm]] concept artist [[Ryan Church]], who had previously worked on the [[Prequel trilogy]]. A [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] set of the ''Arrowhead'' was also produced to promote the TV series.<ref name="SWCO 2017">{{SW|url=news/swco-2017-5-things-we-learned-from-the-lego-star-wars-the-freemaker-adventures-panel|text=SWCO 2017: 5 Things We Learned from the ''LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures'' Panel}}</ref>
