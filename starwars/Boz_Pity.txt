'''Boz Pity''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Collapse" /> located in the [[Boz Pity system]]<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="TFABG" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], it was considered a key member of the [[Galactic Republic]], and received a [[World Pavilions|sky-island]] with a representation of its environment at the [[Valo Republic Fair]].<ref name="Rising Storm" /> By the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]], Boz Pity was largely uninhabited, and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] established a presence on it during the conflict, enabling them to frequently strike at nearby [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] targets. It was then subject to a [[Siege of Boz Pity|siege]] by [[Republic Military|Republic forces]];<ref name="Collapse" /> after the conclusion of the [[Siege of Saleucami]] during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]], [[Jedi General]] [[Quinlan Vos]] moved his troops to support the siege.<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" />

During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the ''[[Ghost]]'', a [[light freighter]] used by the [[Spectres]], used a false [[transponder code]] that stated the [[freighter]] had free passage rights between [[Lothal]] and Boz Pity.<ref name="Idiot's Array">{{Rebels|Idiot's Array (episode)}}</ref> A [[Battle of Boz Pity (Galactic Civil War)|Battle of Boz Pity]], in which [[Gial Ackbar]] was injured, was fought during the Imperial Era.<ref name="Rebel Alliance Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Rebel Alliance}}</ref> At some point, [[Nath Tensent]], [[pilot]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and later the [[New Republic]], was involved in a mission on Boz Pity and watched some of his friends [[Death|die]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Boz Pity was first mentioned in the [[2005]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" /> [[Boz Pity/Legends|Boz Pity]] was originally created and featured in [[Obsession 4|''Obsession'' 4]], the fourth issue of the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Obsession]]'', written by [[W. Haden Blackman]].<ref name="O4">[[Obsession 4|''Obsession'' 4]]</ref>
