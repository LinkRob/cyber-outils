The '''Endor Ranger''' was the collective name of a custom [[Repulsorlift chair|repulsorlift]] [[Command Chair]] and a pair of [[Blaster Remote]]s owned by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sprocket]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Command Chair (Sprocket)|standard chair and remote set]]. The chair featured a [[Color|green]] and black base, with glowing yellow and blue components. On top of the technological part was constructs resembling those made by the [[Ewok]]s of [[Endor]], including a cloth and [[Wood|stick]] chair, cloth [[Hang-glider|glider]] wings, a wooden [[Limb|foot]] stand, and various other random bits of cloth, rope, [[vine]]s, leaves, and [[flower]]s protruding from all over. The [[remote]]s also had a matching color scheme, wrapped in brown straps, and possessing black [[sensor]]s and dark gray [[Antenna|antennae]].<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Endor Ranger was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Sprocket's Command Chair, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.18.0 during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Endor Engineer]].<ref name="Hunters" />
