"'''Artoo's Tale'''" is a short story originally published in ''[[Galaxy Guide 1: A New Hope]]''. It was later included in ''[[The Movie Trilogy Sourcebook]]''.

{{Shortstory-stub}}

==Plot summary==
[[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2's]] personal account of his search for [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in the [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] desert, as told to [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] historian [[Voren Na'al/Legends|Voren Na'al]]. His story is occasionally interrupted by [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]].
