'''Abednedo''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> located on the [[Corellian Run]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> and in the [[Abednedo system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Colonies]] region.<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> It was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Abednedo]] [[species]], whose [[Abednedo precursor species|ancestors]] evolved underground and carved out tunnel-dens to create massive cathedrals housing tens of thousands of family units. The species eventually took to living on the planet's surface and constructed sprawling [[City|metropolises]] bristling with ornamental spires, flagpoles, and riotously [[color]]ed carvings. While these cities struck outlanders as chaotic, the Abednedos found them endless sources of amusement and interest.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />

In the aftermath of the [[Battle of Endor]] due to its natives' [[New Republic]] sympathies, the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Attack on Abednedo|damaged]] the planet using a [[climate disruption array]]<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> during [[Operation: Cinder]],<ref name="Part IV" /> a contingency plan issued [[Death|posthumously]] by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Part II">[[Shattered Empire 2|''Shattered Empire'' 2]]</ref> 

Thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the Battle of [[Endor]], [[Brasmon Kee]] represented Abednedo in the New Republic [[New Republic Senate|Senate]].<ref name="The Visual Dictionary" /> Another Abednedo [[Ello Asty]], a [[Gender|male]] Abednedo [[pilot]]<ref name="Flight Log">''[[Poe Dameron: Flight Log]]''</ref> who flew for the New Republic<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> and later the [[Resistance]],<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> was from this world.<ref name="Flight Log" />

==Behind the scenes==
Abednedo was first mentioned in [[Shattered Empire 4|''Shattered Empire'' 4]], the fourth and final issue of [[Marvel Comics]]' ''[[Star Wars: Shattered Empire]]'' miniseries, which was written by [[Greg Rucka]] with art by [[Marco Checchetto]] and released in [[2015]].<ref name="Part IV" />
