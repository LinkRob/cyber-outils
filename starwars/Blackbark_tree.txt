'''Blackbark trees''' were a type of [[tree]] that had a rigid appearance.<ref name="MA" /> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[lord]] [[regent]] [[Rael Averross]] mentally compared the [[Pijali]] [[Captain]] [[Deren]]'s facial expression to the rigidity of a blackbark [[Plant|tree]]. Its [[wood]] was known as [[blackbark-wood]].<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blackbark trees were mentioned in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
