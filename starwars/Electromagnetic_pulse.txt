An '''electromagnetic pulse''' (or '''EMP''' for short) was a short yet powerful burst of electromagnetic energy. EMP devices were commonly used to disable electronics and [[droid]]s. Electromagnetic pulses were used extensively as weapons by [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Separatist Droid Army]].<ref name="SW:AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> [[Electromagnetic pulse grenade]]s were one such example.<ref name="Droid Popper">{{Databank|droid-popper|Droid Popper}}</ref> The [[LOM-series protocol droid]] [[bounty hunter]] [[4-LOM]] also wielded a [[4-LOM's DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle|DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle]] that could fire electromagnetic pulses.<ref name="SWFA">''[[Star Wars: Force Arena]]''</ref> The [[bo-rifle]]s of the [[Lasan High Honor Guard]] featured electromagnetic pulse-generator tips.<ref name="Rebels: The Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Ion cannon]]s and other ion based weapons like [[ion blaster]]s.<ref name="DB">{{Databank|ion-blaster|Ion Blaster}}</ref>
