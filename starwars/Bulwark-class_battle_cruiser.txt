The '''''Bulwark''-class battle cruiser''' was utilized by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval branch]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="Rise" /> between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'' establishes that the ''Bulwark''-class battle cruiser was utilized during the [[Clone Wars]], dated from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> [[Sullustan]] [[Admiral]] [[Dua Ningo]], an [[officer]] of the [[Sullustan Home Guard]], which had allied with the Confederacy, was charged with overseeing the construction of a [[Bulwark flotilla|flotilla]] of [[battlecruiser]]s. Anticipating the [[fleet]]'s completion [[20 BBY|two years]] after the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the war on the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]], Ningo had hoped to deploy the ''Bulwark''-class battle cruisers in an attack against the Republic's [[capital]] world of [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Rise" /> It shared its name with the [[Bulwark system]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
''Bulwark''-class battle cruisers were introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]] for their series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Bulwark Mark I]] was introduced in ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'', a [[2005]] reference book authored by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Kevin J. Anderson]].<ref name="TNEC">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''</ref>
