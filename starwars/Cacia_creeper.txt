'''Cacia creeper''' was a type of [[vine]] found on the [[planet]] [[Siline]]. They exclusively grew in [[oka tree]]s, a relatively abundant variety of [[tree]] on the planet. Cacia creeper vines were very strong but could be harvested easily if sliced at a 45 degree angle. The vines made for a valuable survival resource as a makeshift [[rope]], as they were thin enough to be knotted and woven easily.

Cacia creepers were an important target during the [[survival level]] of the [[Crowning Convocation]] on Siline due to their value in creating ropes for climbing and for hammocks. During the [[Crowning Convocation (Jecki and Yord)|Convocation held]]<ref name="Crown" />  in [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> [[Rhos Arthyst|Rhos]] and [[Sitia Arthyst]] taught [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]s [[Jecki Lon]] and [[Yord Fandar]] about oka trees and cacia creeper as a source of rope. They subsequently used that knowledge extensively during the survival level.<ref name="Crown" />
