'''Booba Fett''' was a [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] of the [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]]. He was [[Reproduction/Legends|cloned]] by [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]], as one of a series of copies created to replace important citizens of [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]] with [[Agent/Legends|agents]] loyal to him. Booba Fett, in his position standing in as Boba, was the [[Mandalore (title)/Legends|Mandalore]] and thus leader of all [[Mandalorian/Legends|Mandalorian]] clans. However, by the [[Standard year/Legends|year]] [[44 ABY]], [[Luuke Skywalker]]&mdash;another of Thrawn's clones&mdash;was not certain that the clone portraying Boba Fett was still Booba.

==Biography==
After [[9 ABY/Legends|9 ABY]],<ref>''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' places the events of ''[[The Last Command]]'', in which [[Luuke Skywalker|Luuke]] was created, in this [[Standard year/Legends|year]].</ref> when [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]] replaced the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] with a [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] named [[Luuke Skywalker|Luuke]], he began [[Reproduction/Legends|creating duplicates]] of other characters prominent in the [[galactic community]] who would do his bidding. Booba Fett was a clone of the influential [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]], one of Thrawn's targets. Between the [[Time/Legends|time]] that Booba was installed<ref name="Best Thing" /> and [[44 ABY]],<ref name="Timeline">{{Facebook|username=people/Star-Wars-Books/100064411132431|url=photo.php?fbid=10150222732008713&set=a.475619223712.256257.67519418712&type=3&theater|name=Star Wars Books|date=2011-06-03|archivedate=20200819120610}}</ref> Luuke was unsure of how many times Boba had been cloned. He speculated that the replacement clone might have been the second or even third one, as the individual filling the role of Fett had been shot at many times during risky escapades. This occurred so frequently that Skywalker also believed that Fett's trademark [[Armor/Legends|armor]] had been fully replaced more than once.<ref name="Timeline" /> In 44 ABY, Boba Fett held the position of [[Mandalore (title)/Legends|leader]] of the [[Mandalorian/Legends|Mandalorian]] clans and was a supporter of the [[The galaxy/Legends|galactic]] government known as the [[Galactic Federation of Free Alliances]].<ref name="Apocalypse" />

==Personality and traits==
As a replacement of Boba Fett,<ref name="Best Thing" /> Booba Fett was genetically identical to the [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Jango Fett/Legends|Jango Fett]], of whom Boba himself was a clone,<ref>Booba Fett was revealed as a clone of [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]] in "[[An Apology]]." [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] shows that Boba Fett was a [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] of [[Jango Fett/Legends|Jango Fett]].</ref> and an exact physical double able to convince others that he was Boba. He possessed the Mandalore's [[Color/Legends|black]] [[Hair/Legends|hair]], as well as his brown [[Eye/Legends|eyes]].<ref name="NEGTC" /> He stood 1.83 [[Meter/Legends|meters]] tall,<ref name="Height" /> and was 78.2 [[Kilogram/Legends|kilograms]] in mass.<ref name="Mass" /> Booba appeared as Boba would have at about seventy-five years of age in 44 ABY.<ref>In the context of "[[An Apology]]," Booba aged as the character portraying [[Boba Fett/Legends|Fett]] from the undisclosed point at which he was [[Cloning/Legends|cloned]]. [[Leland Chee]], the keeper of the [[Holocron continuity database]], stated in {{Blog|holocron|13/comments|Major Character Birth Years}} that Boba was born in [[32 BBY/Legends|31.5 BBY]]. ''[[Fate of the Jedi: Apocalypse]]'' took place in 44 ABY.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Future novels in the unending series will feature Maara Jade, Anaakin Solo, Booba Fett (or is it Boooba Fett?) and more beloved characters.|The announcement of ''Son of Clone Wars''|<ref name="Best Thing" />}}
Booba Fett was mentioned in "[[An Apology]]," a [[Canon|non-canonical]] short story by [[Timothy Zahn]] that was released on [[April 1]], [[2012]] as an [[Wikipedia:April Fools' Day|April Fools' Day]] joke. In the context of the story, any appearance of Boba Fett after the undisclosed point at which Booba was created would be Booba. The short story was an epilogue to the [[Star Wars: Fate of the Jedi|''Fate of the Jedi'']] series, with the story establishing that Booba was created long before the series. He was mentioned by name in the facetious announcement of a new book series that would continue releasing novels for perpetuity, entitled ''Son of Clone Wars'' or ''The Clone Wars 2''. Booba was going to appear in this series, but doubt was cast on whether or not it was the first clone or a second, Boooba Fett.<ref name="Best Thing" />
