'''2714''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] that served in the [[Trade Federation]] [[Trade Federation Droid Army|Droid Army]]. During the [[Invasion of Naboo|Naboo Crisis]], 2714 fought against the [[Gungan Grand Army]] at the [[Great Grass Plains]].<ref name="Episode I" />

The B1's designation was written on the back of its [[comlink booster pack]].<ref name="Episode I" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
2714 first appeared in the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]]. While the [[droid]]'s designation can be spotted in film,<ref name="Episode I" /> the numbers could not be identified until ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'' gave a translation for B1 numerals.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>
