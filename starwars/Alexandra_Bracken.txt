'''Alexandra "Alex" Bracken''' is the author of the novel ''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]'', a retelling of the classic [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. She replaced [[R.J. Palacio]], who was slated to write the novel, but was forced to exit the project due to scheduling conflicts. Bracken is also the author of the ''Darkest Minds'' series, published through [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney Publishing]], as well as her debut novel, ''Brightly Woven''.<ref name="announce" />

Bracken's father was a ''[[Star Wars]]'' collector, and during her childhood she attended numerous ''Star Wars'' conventions with him.<ref name="about">{{WebCite|url=http://www.alexandrabracken.com/?page_id=2|text=About Alex|archivedate=20110625061021}}</ref>
