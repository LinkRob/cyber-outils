'''Croci''' was the [[language]] of the [[Crocin]] [[species]].<ref name="YouTube">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Language in Star Wars Movies|video=CZp8Civk2co|nolive=1}}</ref> The Crocin [[Karjj]] spoke in Croci while playing [[sabacc]] in [[the Lodge]] on the [[planet]] [[Vandor]]<ref name="Solo" /> in [[10 BBY]].<ref name="SoloOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Croci first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" /> It was first identified as Croci in the video "Every Language in ''Star Wars''," which was released in [[2019]] on the ''Star Wars'' Kids [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel.<ref name="YouTube" /> Before the film's release, the language spoken by Karjj was also featured in a tie-in advertisement for the restaurant [[Wikipedia:Denny's|Denny's]] that was released on [[April 4]], 2018.<ref name="Denny's">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@dennys|name=Denny's|video=s8q-y2NkIK0|text=Solo: A Star Wars Story exclusive trading cards, available only at Denny's!|date=2018-04-02|nolive=1}}</ref>
