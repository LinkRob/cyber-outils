'''''Evasive Action''''' was a series of [[Star Wars comic books|webstrips]] published exclusively on [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|''Hyperspace'']], beginning in October [[2004]] and ending in November [[2006]]. Together they chronicle the events of three [[Padawan/Legends|Padawans]] who go from fighting [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Separatists]] on [[Felucia/Legends|Felucia]], to fugitives on the run from the newly formed [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]].
