The '''Cylo Hijacking''' was an incident that occurred shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]] when [[Tarkin Initiative]] [[Doctor|Dr.]] [[Cylo]] and his [[Cylo's whale fleet|fleet]] attempted to hijack the ''[[Executor]]'', an [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]], while it was still in construction in the [[shipyard]]s of [[Kuat Drive Yards]].

==Prelude==
During the [[Shu-Torun War|War on Shu-Torun]], [[Doctor]] [[Cylo]] collaborated with the rebellious [[ore-duke]]s in an attempt to kill the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. However, the [[Sith Lord]] survived and led the Empire to victory. [[Morit Astarte]] had killed his sister [[Aiolin Astarte]], as she was a competitor to him in supplanting Vader, and he fled with Cylo and [[Tulon Voidgazer]].<ref name="DV 19" /> After this, the Dark Lord would be sent by his [[Sith Master|master]] to hunt them down. Meanwhile Vader sent his two [[assassin droid]]s [[0-0-0]] and [[BT-1]], to capture Doctor [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] and bring her to the ''[[Executor]]'', as she knew about his plot against the [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]].<ref name="Darth Vader 20" /> The Dark Lord took the ''[[Devastator]]'' to the [[Crushank Nebulae]] where he led [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] in an attack on the rogue [[scientist]]'s fleet. Cylo escaped, but not before Vader got aboard his flagship.<ref name="Darth Vader 21" /> He went through the labs and was eventually confronted by Voidgazer, who pitted him against her cyberanimate [[rancor]]. However, this opponent was no match for Vader, who slew the beast before killing Voidgazer herself.<ref name="Darth Vader 22" />

==The battle==
===The hijacking===
As Vader was fighting Voidgazer, Cylo's fleet emerged from hyperspace. On the bridge of the ''Executor'', [[Grand General]] [[Cassio Tagge]] ordered the batteries to open fire. However, they found the weapons, doors, and controls offline and watched in horror as sleeping gas emerged from the vents and knocked them out. In the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor's]] throne room, a [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guard]] informed him they are operating on their own life-support system. In a last ditch effort, Cylo and Morit abandoned their [[flagship]] and set it on a collision course with the ''Executor''. Vader reached the bridge of the vessel to stop the collision course, but found that it couldn't be altered.<ref name="Darth Vader 22" />

Aboard the ship, Cylo's forces found the crew knocked out and took over the bridge. They then activated [[ion charge]]s to disable pursuing vessels. However, before they could escape, the power went out. The Dark Lord of the [[Sith]] then contacted them and revealed that he had cut the power and that he was coming for them. Morit went to deal with Vader while Cylo's troops went to capture Palpatine.<ref name="Darth Vader 23" />

===Retaking the ship===
[[File:MoritVSVader-2015DarthVader23.png|thumb|left|300px|Morit jumps at Vader on the hull]]
Meanwhile, Aphra had used the programming of BT-1 and 0-0-0 to get them to help free her: Vader's priority order to both droids was only to bring her aboard the ship, so, with that done, she could give a new priority order that they help her. She decided that in order to get Vader off her tail, she had to report his plot to his own master, Palpatine. She alongside the droids, and the [[Wookiee]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Krrsantan]], who was there because she owed him a great deal of [[Currency|money]], went to defend the Emperor from Cylo's troops. Morit met up with Vader on the hull where they dueled. The Dark Lord [[Force push|Force-pushed]] his adversary onto a ledge where he regained balance, only for Vader to destroy it with the Force, killing Morit.<ref name="Darth Vader 23" />

As the Emperor's Fist approached the bridge, the troops sent to capture the Emperor withdrew and went after the cyborg, who cut them all down. When Vader arrived at the bridge, Cylo reminded him he was his machine as he deactivated him with a button.<ref name="Darth Vader 23" /> Knocked out, Vader had a [[Force vision|vision]] in which he returned to Mustafar and killed [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and Anakin, then choked [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. He then stated Skywalker was dead as he awoke, shocking Cylo. Vader then stabbed Cylo-V and killed him. Aboard Cylo's whale fleet, Cylo-VI awoke desperate to escape. The Dark Lord departed in his [[TIE Advanced x1]] and informed his master that the ship was under Imperial control as he raced off to stop the scientist's escape. Right then, Aphra barged into the Palpatine's chambers to explain Vader's plot.<ref name="Darth Vader 24" />

===Ending Cylo===
Vader pursued Cylo as his fleet tried to stop him from getting to the flagship. The vessel escaped, but not before the Dark Lord had boarded it. He took out all of Cylo's marines and as he went through the cloning chamber, he was confronted by the clones of Cylo. As they taunted him he killed them all with ease. Vader then confronted Cylo-VI on the bridge, who still bragged that he was immortal. However, the Dark Lord used the Force to send the ship into the nearby sun and left as the ship was torn apart, permanently ending the turncoat scientist.<ref name="Darth Vader 25" />

==Aftermath==
The Dark Lord then returned to the ''Executor'' to report his victory. Palpatine revealed that Aphra told him of his treason. Instead of being angry, he congratulated his [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] and left him to deal with the "delightful girl". Vader ejected Aphra out the [[airlock]] and then went to meet Tagge and Palpatine on the bridge. Citing the Grand General's blunder and misplaced trust as reasons for relieving him of his Supreme Commander duties, the Emperor instead granted his apprentice absolute command of both [[Death Squadron]] and the entire [[Imperial Military|Military]]. As soon as Palpatine departed, Tagge attempted to reason with Vader, but he was cut off and met his demise at the hands of his intolerant new superior's [[Force choke|grip]]. The Emperor's Fist gave [[Admiral]] [[Kendal Ozzel]] two weeks to have the ''Executor'' ready for action. Aphra ended up being rescued by Krrsantan, with Vader now off her tail since he believed her to be dead.<ref name="Darth Vader 25" />
