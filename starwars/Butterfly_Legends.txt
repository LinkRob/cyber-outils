A '''butterfly''' was an [[Insect/Legends|insect]] with brilliantly colored wings. There were many types of butterflies across [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]], many of which were displayed on [[Aargau/Legends|Aargau]] in the [[Garden of Butterflies]]. There were also several butterflies in the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple's]] gardens on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].

Included are:

*[[Carrier butterfly/Legends|Carrier butterfly]]
*[[Antarean butterfly/Legends|Antarean butterfly]]
*[[Corellian butterfly]]
*[[Flewt]]
*[[Mmhmm butterfly]]
*[[Yellow-barred Heliconian]]
*Rylothean fire butterfly
