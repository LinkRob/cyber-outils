'''Angor''' was a [[commandant]] at the [[Corulag Military Academy]], an [[Imperial Academy]] run by the [[Galactic Empire]] on [[Corulag]],<ref name="Rise" /> a [[planet]] in the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Servants">''[[Servants of the Empire: Edge of the Galaxy]]''</ref> The academy tended to pair training [[instructor]]s based on their complementary abilities, and Angor was paired with Commandant [[Bedgar]], as his robust combat strategy classes paired with Bedgar's lessons on the intricacies of military administration to produce well-rounded [[Imperial officer|officers]] prepared for both the combat and bureaucracy required for the role. A file on their curriculum titled [[Combined Officer's Curriculum 27]] was stored in the "Corulag Imperial Military Academy" section of the [[Imperial Archives]].<ref name="Rise" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned Angor in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise" />
