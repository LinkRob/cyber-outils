"'''25 to Rescue'''" is a [[Star Wars Miniatures]] and roleplaying scenario written by [[Sterling Hershey]] in which the [[Battle of Christophsis/Legends|Rescue Mission to Christophsis]] is introduced into the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity. The miniatures article was published on [[Wizards of the Coast]]'s webpage on [[December 17]], [[2009]] and the roleplaying article on [[January 28]], [[2010]].
