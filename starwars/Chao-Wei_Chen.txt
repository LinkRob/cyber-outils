'''Chao-Wei Chen''' is a texture artist at [[CGCG]] credited in several ''[[Star Wars]]'' projects, including ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'',<ref name="Jedi Night" /> ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'',<ref name="The Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref> ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="Gone with a Trace">{{TCW|Gone with a Trace}}</ref> and ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]''.<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref>
