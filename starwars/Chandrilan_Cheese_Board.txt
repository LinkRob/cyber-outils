A '''Chandrilan Cheese Board''' was a type of [[cheese]] board that showcased the flavors of the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Chandrila]], a world whose [[Chandrilan|inhabitants]] were famous for putting together high-quality spreads of [[Foodstuff|food]]. The dish included a variety of components served together on a board, including [[deviled bulabird egg]]s, [[Color|red]] and green [[fruit]]s, and [[tintolive]]s plucked fresh from [[Tintolive tree|trees]] that lined the [[crystal]]line cliffs north of Chandrilla's [[Hanna City]]. Popular snacks from across [[the galaxy]] were also included, such as [[Lira San Cracker]]s, [[Hanava Pod]]s, and [[Ylesian White Worm]]s.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

After making a culinary tour of the [[galaxy]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for the dish in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> which was published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> In the book, he encouraged readers to change which components they served on the board so that those eating it would never get tired of the dish.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chandrilan Cheese Board was created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the board makes use of four other recipes included in the book, as well as sliced [[Wikipedia:Pitaya|dragonfruit]] and [[Wikipedia:Grape|grapes]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
