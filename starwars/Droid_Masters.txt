'''''Droid Masters''''' was a [[Wikipedia:Puzzle video game|puzzle]] [[Wikipedia:Browser game|web browser]] game developed and released by [[Disney Interactive]] on [[June 30]], [[2014]]. It was released as a tie-in to the crossover special ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Web" />

{{Game-stub}}

==Gameplay==
''Droid Masters'' is a puzzle game that tasked the players to build a passageway and deliver [[water]] balls from [[moisture vaporator]]s to the [[collection tank]].<ref name="Web" />
