A [[Monarchy|royal]] [[chief]]'s '''fortress''' was located on<ref name="MA" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Rogue TUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Lah'mu]] during the [[Republic Era]]. At some point during his [[time]] as a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] visited the [[fortress]]. During the [[mission to Pijal]]<ref name="MA" /> in [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> Kenobi contemplated the many royal palaces he had visited, and considered the fortress simple in comparison to the [[Alderaanian monarchy|queen's]] [[Royal Palace of Alderaan|palace]] on the [[Core Worlds]] planet [[Alderaan]].<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The chief's fortress was mentioned in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
