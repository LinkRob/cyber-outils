"'''Cruising with Luke'''" is a [[Canon|non-canon]] animated [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short released on [[August 5]], [[2022]] on the LEGO YouTube channel. It is part of both ''[[LEGO Star Wars Summer Shorts]]''<ref name="Playlist" /> and ''[[LEGO STAR WARS: Celebrate the Season]]''.<ref name="SWKids" />

==Official description==
''Young Luke Skywalker zooms around Tatooine in his landspeeder and must make a deal with some Jawas when his joyride goes awry.''<ref name="Luke" />

==Plot summary==
[[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] enters a shop on [[Mos Eisley]] as the ground begins to shake. Outsides a crowd watches [[Luke Skywalker]], [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] arriving in an [[X-34 landspeeder]] while playing music. Calrissian is visibly annoyed by their loud music and tells them to leave. Skywalker and his droids depart on their [[landspeeder]], leaving dust and smoke in their wake.

Traveling into the desert, Luke races a [[Jawa]] [[sandcrawler]], running circles around the larger vehicle. While distracted, Skywalker's speeder hits a rock, causing the vehicle to hit the ground and break into smaller bricks. Luke and his occupants are unharmed but Luke is upset. One of the Jawas laughs.

Luke later comes to an arrangement with the Jawas. In return for attaching a repulsor engine to the sandcrawler, the Jawas rebuild his wrecked landspeeder as a much larger vehicle. After the Jawas depart, Luke and the droids climb into the enlarged landspeeder and depart.
