The '''Dunnak system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> It contained the terrestrial astronomical object [[Dunnak]]<ref name="TRS" /> and was situated in grid square N-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> The [[Vaathkree Trade Corridor]]<ref name="Fry" /> [[hyperspace route]] connected the Dunnak system to the [[Corsin system|Corsin]] and [[Ubduria system|Ubduria systems]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> Dunnak was home to the [[Docha Institute]], an institute featuring [[Docha Institute's medical library|one]] of [[the galaxy]]'s best medical [[Library|libraries]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="TRS" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dunnak system was first mentioned in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' on a [[The galaxy|galaxy]] map authored by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Christopher Ibbitt]],<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> which was released on [[June 17]], [[2025]].<ref name="Edelweiss">{{DK|url=us/book/9780593958605-star-wars-the-acolyte-visual-guide|text=Star Wars The Acolyte Visual Guide}}</ref> The system was identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]] of the same year&mdash;with both works being authored by Fry.<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
