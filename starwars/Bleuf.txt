'''Bleuf''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> in the [[Bleuf system]] of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was a key manufacturing world for the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'' to [[4 ABY]].</ref> after the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[economist]] [[Kriz Tray]] projected how the fall of the Empire would affect Bleuf's economy, with Tray's data showing that the planet's economy would collapse.<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />
