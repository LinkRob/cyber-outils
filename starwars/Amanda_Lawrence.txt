{{Quote|That is something that I love about many of the characters in ''Star Wars''. It's so clever that often we meet them midway through their story. That's something awesome I remember about seeing ''Star Wars: A New Hope'' for the first time, when that huge scroll of text told us where we were at in this story. That was so brave, to start a film by telling the audience all of this has already happened. Human beings are basically storytellers, that's what we inherently are&mdash;we tell stories, and ''Star Wars'' characters have this kind of storytelling weight as well. Stuff has happened to them already. That was genius, and I think you get that with Commander D'Acy.|Amanda Lawrence|<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|226|Amanda Lawrence: A Commanding Performance}}</ref>}}
'''Amanda Lawrence''' is an [[Wikipedia:Actor|actress]] who played [[Commander]] [[Larma D'Acy]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].

In 2012, she appeared in an episode of [[Wikipedia:Midsomer Murders|''Midsomer Murders'']] ("A Rare Bird") with [[Genevieve O'Reilly]] and [[Nicholas Boulton]].
