{{Quote|Triage redeployments, as a result of what happened at Lucazec, I suppose. The same at Centares and Lantillies. No telling where your—uh, the missing ship is going to revert next.|The Captain of the ''Liberator'' informs Tarkin of the redeployments|<ref name="Tarkin" />}}
'''Centares''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> located in the [[Centares system]] in the [[Maldrood sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> at the junction of the [[Perlemian Trade Route]]<ref name="Map" /> and the [[Triellus Trade Route]].<ref name="Triellus" /> [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[capital ship]]s were re-deployed from Centares in order to try and protect targets that the [[Mission to Murkhana|stolen]] Imperial [[corvette]] ''[[Carrion Spike]]'' might attack. [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], owner of the corvette, was informed of these re-deployments by the [[captain]] of the [[Star Destroyer]] [[Liberator (Venator-class)|''Liberator'']].<ref name="Tarkin" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[flight KZ-35]] to Centares that was scheduled to depart from [[Mirogana Spaceport]]'s [[gate W2]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]] was cancelled after the moon's [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Governor]] [[Thorden]] closed down a section of the spaceport.<ref name="Outlaws" />
