'''Energy spheres''' were spherical devices created by the ancient [[Zeffonian]] [[species]]. The spheres could both contain and conduct large amounts of energy, and were used to power Zeffo technology, such as that found within their [[Burial|tombs]], or the [[Tomb Guardian]]s that protected them.

==Description==
The spheres came in a variety of sizes, such as the relatively small orbs located within the chest cavity of the [[Tomb Guardian]]s, or the larger spheres that powered [[Zeffonian]] technology within their [[Burial|tombs]]. These larger spheres, despite their size, could be easily manipulated through use of [[the Force]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

The spheres had Zeffo [[writing]] inscribed across their diameters, and were constructed from a bronze-hued metal. The spheres could be durable, surviving for many [[Standard year|years]] after the disappearance of the Zeffo, though several spheres were damaged and broken by the [[time]] that [[Cal Kestis]] discovered the Zeffo tombs. A faint rumbling could be constantly heard from within the spheres, amplified when they were moved. Some spheres consisted of several layers rotating in various directions around a central glowing blue energy core. On those without rotating portions, this blue energy could be seen emanating from their inscribed writing, and those located within Tomb Guardians were suspended inside their open chests by similar blue energy. It was possible to use the Force to temporarily remove the sphere from a Guardian, incapacitating it momentarily. So long as they were not destroyed, Zeffo energy spheres operated constantly and entirely self-sufficiently.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

The smaller variety of sphere could be found powering Tomb Guardians in an ancient Zeffo [[Temple on Ontotho|temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Ontotho]],<ref name="Dark Temple 4">[[Jedi Fallen Order - Dark Temple 4|''Jedi Fallen Order - Dark Temple'' 4]]</ref> as well as the [[Tomb of Miktrull]] and [[Tomb of Eilram]] on [[Zeffo]]. The larger sphere type was located in an underground area on the planet [[Bogano]], as well as in the two tombs on Zeffo. Additionally, [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]] used an energy sphere to power [[Abandoned Workshop|his workshop]] on Bogano.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==History==
Eno Cordova and his [[Padawan]] [[Cere Junda]] risked their lives to uncover the secrets of the temple on Ontotho, where they encountered the deadly energy sphere-powered Tomb Guardians.<ref name="Dark Temple 4" />

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> five years after the [[Great Jedi Purge|fall]] of the [[Jedi Order]], former Jedi Padawan Cal Kestis explored the Tombs of Eilram and Miktrull with the [[droid]] [[BD-1]], encountering and using the spheres to navigate through them; he also encountered the Tomb Guardians there, which responded hostilely to his presence and used the power of their core spheres to attack him.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the [[2019]] video game ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', energy spheres are often used as an element in puzzle rooms throughout the game. The player is typically required to manipulate the energy spheres using the Force in order to move the spheres to their objective.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
