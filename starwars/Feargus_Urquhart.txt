'''Feargus Urquhart''' is a [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developer and current CEO of [[Obsidian Entertainment]], the company responsible for developing ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]'', the critically acclaimed sequel to ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''.
Prior to helping found Obsidian in 2003, he was a producer with the now defunct [[Wikipedia:Video game developer|entertainment software developing]] company, [[Wikipedia: Black Isle Studios|Black Isle Studios]].
