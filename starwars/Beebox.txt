'''Beebox''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[bounty hunter]] of an unknown [[species]] active during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. After joining [[Chelli Lona Aphra]], [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], [[Krrsantan]], and [[IG-90]] on the [[Son-tuul Pride robbery]], Beebox eventually received a bounty to kill or capture Aphra. When he brought in a disintegrated body that was not Aphra to attempt to claim the bounty, he was [[Death|killed]] by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who sensed his deception.

==Biography==
===Meeting on Son-tuul===
Beebox met up at a [[Cantina|bar]] on [[Son-tuul]] with fellow [[bounty hunter]]s [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] and [[IG-90]], who wanted to know where [[Krrsantan]] was. He explained that Krrsantan was fighting in the pit, confusing to others, especially Bossk, who wondered why an ex-[[Xonti Brothers]] [[gladiator]] would want to relive those moments. Beebox suggested that he got nostalgic. After Krrsantan stopped fighting, Bossk, Beebox, Krrsantan, and IG-90 had a drink in the bar. [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Dr. Aphra]], who had recently worked with [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], explained to the bounty hunter team that [[Darth]] Vader recently killed the [[Son-tuul Pride base|Son-tuul Pride]] [[Yonak's father|leader]], and the [[Sith Lord]] had taken off the [[Hutt]] [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[light cruiser]], which IG-90 explained everyone knew. Aphra told the team that she knew exactly where it was going, and she could make them rich.<ref name="Vader 7" />

===Anthan Prime Heist===
[[File:BeeboxBosskAphraRobbery-2015DarthVader8.jpg|thumb|left|235x235px|Beebox and the other bounty hunters]]
The bounty hunter group headed to the [[Anthan system]], outside the space of [[Anthan Prime]], on Aphra's personal ship, the [[Ark Angel II|''Ark Angel'']], planning to [[Son-tuul Pride robbery|rob]] the [[Starship|ship]]. Aphra blew up an "asteroid," and the ship they were keeping the bounty was surrounded by debris. Aphra sent out an [[Ion cannon|ion]] [[torpedo]] and explained that they would think that their power was cut due to the asteroid [[Asteroid field|field]]. When Beebox and the rest of the crew broke into the ship, they deactivated the shields so Krrsantan, in [[Black Krrsantan's Auzituck anti-slaver gunship|his ship]], could blow the ship's bridge and Aphra's [[Astromech droid|astromech]] [[BT-1|Beetee]] could magnetically pulled as many credits out as he could, although Krrsantan actually used his ship's secret magnetic feature to pull a few credits in for himself.<ref name="Vader 8">[[Darth Vader (2015) 8|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 8]]</ref>

After the heist on Anthan Prime, Beebox and the others received their payment, but since a lot of the money "floated away," the amount was fewer than expected. The group then made plans to attack Aphra, but Aphra explained that she would not take a share in an attempt to dissuade them.<ref name="Vader 8" />

===Hired by Vader and death===
During the [[Shu-Torun War]] Krrsantan, Beebox, Bossk, and IG-90 were given a dead-or-alive bounty hunt on Aphra by Darth Vader. Later, [[0-0-0|Triple-Zero]], Aphra's murderous [[protocol droid]], told Vader that Beebox had found Aphra, but she had already been dead. Vader went to see Beebox, who didn't know it was Vader's bounty, and showed Vader a body reduced to dust and half a skull, claiming he had to use the disintegrator. However, Vader sensed that the body was not Aphra's. Beebox claimed he would not lie to Vader, but the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord]] instead told him, "You would. You did." before [[Force choke|choking]] him to death. After Beebox's execution, Vader ordered Triple-Zero to dispose of Beebox's body as well as warn the deceased's fellow Bounty Hunters not to commit the same fraudulent act. Triple-Zero remarked on his smell as he burned to dust in the [[Magma|lava]] lakes of [[Shu-Torun]].<ref name="V17">[[Darth Vader (2015) 17|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 17]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Beebox was short in stature.<ref name="Vader 7" /> He was deceptive, trying to lie in order to claim the bounty on Aphra.<ref name="V17" /> He wore [[Color|orange]] and yellowish armor.<ref name="Vader 7" />
