The '''ASN courier droid''',<ref name="AEYNTK" /> also known as '''ASN-series'''<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> was a model of [[courier droid]].<ref name="AEYNTK" />

== Description ==
The ASN courier droid,<ref name="AEYNTK" /> also known as ASN-series<ref name="Droidography" /> was a small [[Repulsorlift|flying]] courier droid<ref name="SWTVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> 0.3 [[Meter|meters]] in height,<ref name="AEYNTK" /> which contained a small tube in which a message could be placed for delivery. However, the droid could also be modified to operate as an [[assassin]], dispensing deadly items from its message canister.<ref name="AOTC" />

== History ==
A modified ASN known as [[ASN-121]]<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> was used by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Zam Wesell]] during her [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|attempt]] to [[assassin]]ate [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. Wesell programmed the droid to deliver two [[poison]]ous [[kouhun]]s to Amidala's room, but the creatures were [[Death|killed]] by the Senator's [[Jedi]] guardian [[Anakin Skywalker]]. Skywalker's master, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], then grabbed onto the droid in the hopes it would return to its owner, but before the courier could reach Wesell, the bounty hunter shot it with her [[KiSteer 1284 projectile rifle]].<ref name="AOTC" />

Droids of this model remained in use throughout [[the galaxy]] even [[Standard decade|decades]] after the end of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="CR 2" /> some even being used for [[Galactic Empire|Imperial service]] on [[Murkhana]]. The ASN unit was retrofitted with a [[holotransmitter]], so the [[AC1-series surveillance droid]]s can infiltrate [[Murkhana City]]'s [[medcenter]] that housed a [[deflector shield generator]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> 

[[Ochi]] was given an ASN droid called [[ASN-909]] by [[Crimson Dawn]] for the purpose of [[Assassination of the Emperor's Royal Guard|assassinating]] the [[Emperor's Royal Guard]], using a [[poison]] called [[Ziroxin-12]]. The droid was equipped with a [[cloaking device]], using it to infiltrate [[Inquisitorius Headquarters]] at [[the Works]] and poison the guard's breakfast. Although Ochi discovered one of the guards, <bdi>[[Erstin Deez]], was missing and tracked him to a platform where Ochi fire a [[Yurfloo]]-coated [[poison dart]] with a [[sniper rifle]]</bdi>.<ref name="CR 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
ASN courier droids first appeared in the [[2002]] [[canon]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="AOTC" /> They were first identified in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]'' written by [[Adam Bray]], [[Kerrie Dougherty]], [[Cole Horton]], and [[Michael Kogge]].<ref name="AEYNTK" />

Although ''Absolutely Everything You Need to Know'' gave the ASN courier droid's height as 0.3 [[meter]]s,<ref name="AEYNTK" /> the [[2020]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Extraordinary Droids]]'' by [[Simon Beecroft]] stated they were 0.38 meters.<ref name="Extraordinary Droids">''[[Star Wars: Extraordinary Droids]]''</ref>
