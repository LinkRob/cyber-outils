'''Desix''' was a [[planet]] that seceded from the [[Galactic Republic]] prior to the [[Clone Wars]] and joined the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. After the [[Proclamation of the New Order|reorganization]] of the Galactic Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]], Imperial forces initially attempted to peacefully oust the [[governor]] of the planet, [[Tawni Ames]]. [[Gender|She]] refused and took the Imperial-picked governor deployed to the planet, [[Grotton]], hostage. As a result, the Empire [[Mission to Desix|deployed]] a [[Crosshair and Cody's team|squad]] of [[Clone trooper|clone]] [[stormtrooper]]s led by [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Cody]], which successfully quelled the resistance, eliminated Ames, and installed Imperial rule onto the planet.

==Description==
{{Quote|Your loyalty and determination are commendable. Which is why I have a mission for you on the planet Desix. As far as Separatist worlds are concerned, it's rather remote. I normally wouldn't pay it any mind, but an issue has arisen.|Vice Admiral Edmon Rampart|<ref name="SolitaryClone" />}}
Desix was a remote, arid [[planet]] that was covered in rocky terrain. Frequent [[sandstorm]]s plagued the planet and at least three celestial bodies were close to the planet. The rocks and sand on the planet were tinted [[Color|orange]]-red. From [[Realspace|space]], it looked like an orange ball with shades of purple and brown including a distinct golden glow. A [[Desix City|city]] was located on the planet surrounded by vast [[farm]]lands which served as the seat of power for [[Governor]] [[Tawni Ames]] of Desix then later, Governor [[Grotton]].<ref name="SolitaryClone" />

==History==
===Republic Era===
At some point, before the [[Clone Wars]], Desix seceded from the [[Galactic Republic]] and became an independent system<ref name="SolitaryClone" /> that joined the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Season Companion">{{InsiderCite|223|Star Wars: The Bad Batch Season Two Companion|''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season Two Companion}}</ref> Governor Tawni Ames hailed from the planet. During the Clone Wars, she teamed up with [[Mina Bonteri]] and several Separatist and Republic [[Senator]]s alike to put forth a [[Confederate–Republic peace initiative|treaty]] to end the Clone Wars. The treaty was rejected by [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and the Clone Wars continued<ref name="SolitaryClone" /> until [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the [[trial of Ahsoka Tano]] and the [[mission to Utapau]] to [[19 BBY]]. As the episode this event takes place within takes place between the events of the episodes the other events are attached to according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, this event must take place in 19 BBY.</ref>

===Age of the Empire===
====Maintaining independence====
{{Quote|As I have told your government, Desix does not fall under Imperial jurisdiction.|Governor Tawni Ames|<ref name="SolitaryClone" />}}
[[File:GrottonArrives-TBB203.png|thumb|left|344px|Newly appointed Governor Grotton arrives on Desix]]
After the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the Galactic Empire, Desix became a [[Separatist holdouts|holdout]] of the Confederacy with the Empire attempting to reclaim the planet peacefully. However, Governor Ames refused to submit to Imperial jurisdiction and transfer power to Grotton, leading to him and a [[squad]] of recruited [[TK stormtrooper|TK troopers]] to arrive on the planet and claim control, but quickly failed with a [[Desix droid battalion|battalion]] of [[B1-series battle droid]]s surrounding the newly appointed governor and his squad leading to them becoming hostages.<ref name="SolitaryClone" />

The hostage situation led [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart|Rampart]] to deploy [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Cody]], [[clone commando]] [[Crosshair]], and a [[Crosshair and Cody's team|clone stormtrooper squad]] to the planet in the [[mission to Desix]]. While in Desix's airspace, the [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class attack/transport shuttle]] carrying the [[clone trooper]]s was shot down by [[RPS-6 rocket launcher]]s. This caused the [[shuttle]] to crash land in [[Sector Five]] of the farmlands close to the [[city]].<ref name="SolitaryClone" />

[[File:TheSolitaryClone-TBB203.png|thumb|right|220px|Governor Tawni Ames observing the crash landing at Sector Five.]]
The surviving [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s traveled to the city while facing reprogrammed Separatist [[droid]]s. The squad successfully managed to enter the city with Cody, Crosshair, "[[Nova (clone trooper)|Nova]]," and "[[Wyler]]" splitting off to take the north while the rest of the squad took the south of the city. The squad who took north later entered the [[Desix Bell Tower|bell tower]], where the [[Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Desix)|tactical droid]] and Ames were located. However, they were attacked by [[droideka]]s, who [[Death|killed]] Wyler. After using [[electromagnetic pulse grenade]]s to disable them, they continued their advance before Nova was killed by [[BX-series droid commando|commando droids]]. Cody and Crosshair finally took out the [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|T-series tactical droid]] at the top of the bell tower before advancing in to search for Grotton. They then discovered Ames pointing her [[blaster pistol]] at him. After a brief standoff, Ames let Grotton go after Cody convinced her that they could resolve the conflict between the Empire and her people peacefully. Grotton on the other hand ordered Cody to kill Ames immediately after being released. Cody tried to reason with Grotton, saying he had just promised to spare Ames, so Crosshair killed her instead, with Grotton ordering that her body be put in the [[city square]] to serve as a warning to not rebel against the Empire.<ref name="SolitaryClone" />

====Imperial takeover====
Distraught, Cody left with Crosshair and the remains of the squad on a shuttle departing from Desix while TK trooper reinforcements began securing the planet in the name of the Empire.<ref name="SolitaryClone" />

==Locations==
A city was located on the planet and served as the headquarters of Governor Tawni Ames then later Governor Grotton. The city itself was heavily fortified with only one known entrance that could be accessed via a bridge.<ref name="SolitaryClone" />
