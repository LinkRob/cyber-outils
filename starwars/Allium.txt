'''Allium''' was an [[Allium's species|alien]] [[professor]]. In [[34 ABY]], he was in the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]] conversing with other patrons when the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] arrived. Allium and the castle's other clientele then gathered outside to witness the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of [[Hosnian Prime]] by the [[First Order]]'s [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]]. The First Order proceeded to [[Battle of Takodana|attack]] the castle and destroyed it.

==Biography==
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|VII}} to [[34 ABY]].</ref> [[Professor]] Allium<ref name="AB Update" /> spent [[time]] in the [[Takodana Castle|castle bar]] of the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]].<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> He stood directly in front of the castle's entrance speaking to a seated [[human]] [[Gender|woman]] when the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] entered with the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], the [[BB-series astromech droid|astromech droid]] [[BB-8]], and the [[First Order]] defector [[Finn]]. Kanata noticed her old friend Solo's arrival and shouted for him across the bar, silencing the other patrons and causing Allium to turn and stare at the smuggler. As the bar patrons lost interest in Solo, Allium moved to an alcove table to the left of the door and began speaking to its occupants.<ref name="TFA" />

[[File:Strono_serving_food.png|thumb|left|300px|Professor Allium (middle distance, left) spent time in the castle of Maz Kanata in 34 ABY.]]
Allium later stood at the base of one of the castle's stairwells conversing with another human while Finn left the castle<ref name="TFA" /> with the pirates [[Sidon Ithano]] and [[Quiggold]],<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> but he soon finished the conversation and moved away back toward the bar. Soon after, the patrons of the castle were drawn outside to witness the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of the [[Hosnian system]] by the First Order's [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]], and Allium stood in the castle's doorway next to<ref name="TFA" /> the cook [[Strono Tuggs]]<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> while staring up at the sky. The First Order then [[Battle of Takodana|attacked]] and destroyed the castle itself, before being forced to retreat by the arrival of the [[Resistance]].<ref name="TFA" />

==Personality and traits==
Allium stood 1.9 [[meter]]s tall<ref name="AB Update" /> and was an [[Allium's species|alien]] with [[Color|green]] skin, white [[hair]], and green [[eye]]s.<ref name="TFA" />

==Equipment==
Allium wore a high-collared black [[Clothing|robe]] while in Maz Kanata's castle.<ref name="TFA" />
