The '''Coruscant Jedi Academy''' was a [[Jedi academy]] hosted within the grounds of the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. At this fully equipped facility, young [[Force-sensitive/Legends|Force-sensitive]] [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] learners were tutored in a range of subjects, including subjects related to cultivating their user of [[The Force/Legends|the Force]] such as [[Lightsaber combat/Legends|lightsaber combat]] and advanced [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighter]] [[Pilot/Legends|piloting]], but also those of a classical education, including [[Philosopher/Legends|philosophy]], literature and [[Artist/Legends|art]].<ref name="Jedi Academy" />

This Jedi academy generally followed the practice of only accepting Padawan learners at a very young age, that of a toddler.<ref name="Jedi Academy" /> Despite this two notable alumni, [[Roan Novachez]] and [[Victor Starspeeder]], were accepted after having already completed their basic schooling, at a time when they were considering their prospects for middle school tutelage.<ref name="Jedi Academy" /><ref name="A New Class">''[[Jedi Academy: A New Class]]''</ref>

[[Jedi Initiate/Legends|Jedi Initiates]] at this academy were trained by prestigious [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Masters]], including [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]], [[Garfield|Mr. Garfield]], and the academy's principal, [[Mar (principal)|Mar]].<ref name="Jedi Academy" />
