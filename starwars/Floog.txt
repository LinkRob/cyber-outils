'''Floog''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who ran the [[Drahgor III station]] [[fuel]] [[refinery]] on the [[planet]] [[Drahgor III]] with the help of his nephews [[Flanx]] and [[Flix]], as well as Flix's cousins&mdash;[[Fleck]], [[Fleez]], and [[Flobb]]. At some point, Flix stopped working for Floog.<ref name="From Beneath" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|2|6}} Therefore, Floog must have retired by 34 ABY.</ref> Floog had retired and given control of the refinery to Flanx.<ref name="From Beneath" /> In that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Flix visited the refinery to request fuel for the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]''. He thought his uncle would be helpful and provide him with fuel but learned of his retirement from Flanx.<ref name="From Beneath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Floog was first mentioned in "[[From Beneath]],"<ref name="From Beneath" /> the sixth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season Two|second season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[November 10]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney release">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> The scene that mentioned Floog was previewed [[November 6|four]] days prior in the episode's trailer, which was posted on the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel.<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|lIDRHTM0XR8|"From Beneath" Preview &#124; Star Wars Resistance}}</ref>
