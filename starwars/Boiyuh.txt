'''Boiyuh''' was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> in the [[Boiyuh system]] within the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Interior]] regions,<ref name="Interior" /> situated at the coordinates J-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> It was the [[homeworld]] of the [[human]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[twin]]s [[Lyttan Dree]] and [[Tamu Dree]], who were so hungry that they chose to enlist into the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy|navy]], joining the [[Carida Academy|Imperial Naval Academy]] on<ref name="Imperial Cadet 1" /> the planet<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Carida]]<ref name="Imperial Cadet 1" /> by [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Han Solo - Imperial Cadet 1|''Han Solo - Imperial Cadet'' 1]], including the mention of Boiyuh, to [[10 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Boiyuh was first mentioned in the [[Han Solo - Imperial Cadet 1|first issue]] of the [[2018]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo - Imperial Cadet]]''. The issue was written by [[Robbie Thompson]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Imperial Cadet 1" /> on [[November 7]], 2018.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/71582/star_wars_han_solo_-_imperial_cadet_2018_1|text=Star Wars: Han Solo - Imperial Cadet (2018) #1}}</ref>
