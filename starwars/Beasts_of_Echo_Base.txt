"'''Beasts of Echo Base'''" is the fifth episode of the first season of the animated micro-series ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]''. It was released on [[July 7]], [[2017]]. The episode takes place prior to [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="Beasts of Echo Base" />

==Official description==
''Leia and R2-D2 must free Chewie from the clutches of a ferocious wampa''.<ref name="Beasts of Echo Base" />

==Plot summary==
[[File:LeiaChewieArtooFOD.png|thumb|left|250px|Leia comes up with a plan to contain the wampa inside the passageway.]]
After [[Chewbacca]] goes missing while digging corridors within [[Echo Base]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]] and [[R2-D2]] are searching for him. They have searched everywhere and asked everyone in the base and still cannot find him. However, just as Leia begins to lose hope, they hear Chewbacca's howl coming from inside one of the corridors. Lantern in hand, Leia leads Artoo into the corridor, where deep within they catch sight of their [[Wookiee]] friend.

At first it appears as if everything is all right, but as they take a closer look, Leia realizes that Chewie is in the clutches of a [[wampa]]. Simultaneously, [[C-3PO]] cuts in on her wrist [[comlink]]. The noise seems to startle the wampa; seeing Chewbacca's frightened look, Leia quickly shuts off the comlink. As the wampa rises from his perch, he releases Chewie, who runs over to join Leia and Artoo.

Leia quickly comes up with a plan to escape the agitated beast. She orders Chewie and Artoo to seal the passageway while she distracts the wampa. As Leia lets the wampa chase her as a distraction, Artoo and Chewie quickly get to work on the door. As the wampa corners Leia, her lantern goes out, leaving her in the dark. However, she slides between the wampa's legs and escapes the passageway. As she escapes, Chewie and Artoo complete the installation of the door and shut it just before the wampa can escape. Chewie gives Leia a hug of appreciation, and the trio celebrates that Leia's plan to contain the beast was successful.

==Continuity==
[[File:ThreepioRipsSignonHothDeletedScene.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Threepio rips a warning sign from an Echo Base door in a deleted scene from ''The Empire Strikes Back''.]]
The episode ties in to a [[Content cut from Star Wars|deleted scene]] from ''The Empire Strikes Back'', where wampas would have infiltrated Echo Base and C-3PO placed a warning sign on the door<ref name="ThreepioTwitter">{{Twitter|missingwords/|status/883417111145480192|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=That's Threepio's job.|archivedate=20200814221336}}</ref> after the wampas were trapped inside. The scene would have been followed up by another, in which C-3PO tears the sign off, so invading [[Cold assault stormtrooper|snowtroopers]] would have entered the room with the wampas.<ref name="DeletedTwitter">{{Twitter|missingwords/|status/883387659820728320|[[Matt Martin]]|quote='member that deleted scene in ESB? Forces of Destiny kinda/sorta ties into that today. https://youtu.be/o3II-zqUe6M|archiveurl=https://archive.is/ONMRC}}</ref>

A reference book called ''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]'', released after the short, has confirmed that the deleted scene is [[canon]].<ref name="FrontLines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref>

The events of the episode were later referenced by Leia in ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>
