'''Big Hutt''' was a type of [[foodstuff]]<ref name="Curse" /> that shared its name with the [[Hutt]] [[species]].<ref name="Master & Apprentice">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[The Curse (comic story)|The Curse]]" take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the item was displayed in [[Aurebesh]] on a menu at a [[Unidentified cantina (Sergia)|cantina]] on<ref name="Curse" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Star Wars 68">[[Star Wars (2015) 68|''Star Wars'' (2015) 68]]</ref> [[Sergia]].<ref name="Curse" />

==Behind the scenes==
Big Hutt was mentioned on a menu in "[[The Curse (comic story)|The Curse]]," a story released in the one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Free Comic Book Day 2024: Star Wars 1|Free Comic Book Day 2024: ''Star Wars'' 1]].
