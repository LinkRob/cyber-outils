The '''Cardekkia system''' was located in the [[Core Worlds]]' [[Corellian sector]] and situated in grid square M-11 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[star system]] was the origin point<ref name="Planetnamia" /> of [[Cardekkia cheese]].<ref name="ResistanceReborn" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cardekkia system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
