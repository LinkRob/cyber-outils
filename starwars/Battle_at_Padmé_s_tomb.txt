The '''battle at Padmé's tomb'''<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103061/star_wars_darth_vader_2020_30|text=Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020) #30}}</ref> was fought at the [[Tomb of Padmé Amidala]] between the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and the [[Amidalans]], who sought revenge on the [[Sith Lord]] for [[Death|killing]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], the former [[Monarch of Naboo|queen]] and [[senator]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]], and [[Jedi General]] Anakin Skywalker, though the Amidalans were unaware that Skywalker was in fact still alive, and had become Vader. Vader and his [[death trooper]] [[Darth Vader's guard|guard]] fought past the Amidalans, soon reaching the tomb itself. There, Vader was confronted by Amidala's former [[handmaiden]]s, and, while the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], overcome with memories of his past with Amidala, found [[Gender|himself]] unwilling to kill the women, he successfully defeated them and entered the tomb.<ref name="Vader 4" />
