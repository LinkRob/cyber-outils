The '''BU-series bartender droid''' was a variant of [[Industrial Automaton]]'s [[2-1B surgical droid]] that was designed specifically for use as a [[bartender droid]]. [[BU-11]] was one such unit.<ref name="BuildR2">{{BuildR2Cite|5|Droid Directory}}</ref>

[[Unidentified BU-series droid bartender|Another]] served as a [[bartender]] at [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] on the [[planet]] of [[Tatooine]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Around [[21 BBY]], this droid was serving drinks at the bar when several [[bounty hunter]]s, including [[Asajj Ventress]], were searching through a [[transmitter]] of [[Bounty|bount]]ies. When he told the hunters to stop monopolizing all of the [[transmitter]]s, a [[Weequay]] individual told the droid to shut its speakers.<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref>

A BU-series unit was seen working at the [[79's]] [[cantina]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref>
