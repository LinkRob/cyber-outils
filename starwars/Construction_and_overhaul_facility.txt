The '''construction and overhaul facility''' was a massive [[shipyard]] located over [[Kartoosh]]. [[Galactic Republic]] [[engineer]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]] took part in helping to design the facility<ref name="Catalyst" /> in [[23 BBY]].<ref name="Date note" /> Following the [[Clone Wars]], the facility had seen great damage, yet it remained operational and was used by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Catalyst" />
