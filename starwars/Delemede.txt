'''Delemede''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="EoD" /> in the [[Delemede system]] in the [[Rseik sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'' dates itself to a [[Standard year|year]] the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]].</ref> it was located in the [[Occlusion Zone]], a group of sectors that were under the control of the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s, and as such the Delemede's people were oppressed by the Nihil.<ref name="EoD" />
