The '''41st Ranger Platoon''' was a [[clone trooper]] [[infantry]] [[platoon]] and strike team within the [[41st Elite Corps]] that served the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The platoon participated in [[Saboteur|sabotage]] missions on Separatist supply lines and installations, and later fought in the [[Battle of Kashyyyk]] in support of other 41st Elite [[Corps]] units. The platoon participated in the hunt for [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] after the implementation of [[Order 66]] and the start of the [[Great Jedi Purge]].

==Description==
===Overview and composition===
[[File:41st-Ranger-Specialist-P1.png|thumb|left|170px|A 41st Ranger Platoon clone specialist.]]
The 41st Ranger [[Platoon]] was a [[clone trooper]] [[infantry]] platoon and strike team within the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s [[41st Elite Corps]] that served the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> As a platoon, the unit normally consisted of four [[squad]]s for a total of thirty-six [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s.<ref name="Complete Locations" /> The unit was composed of a variety of clone soldiers, including standard clone troopers, [[Clone sharpshooter|clone specialists]] [[clone trooper officer]]s, [[heavy weapons clone trooper]]s,<ref name="Battlefront II" /> and [[Advanced Recon Force trooper|Advanced Recon Force Scout Troopers]]. The platoon deployed [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s during its operations,<ref name="Complete Vehicles NE" /> and included an [[Unidentified Advanced Recon Force Scout Trooper officer|ARF trooper officer]] that led a patrol of reconnaissance [[walker]]s.<ref name="Episode III" />

===Equipment===
====Phase I====
The platoon was initially equipped with [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] that was painted with [[forest]] [[Color|camouflage]] and dark green color combinations, and further customized with a green<ref name="Battlefront II" /> polarized lense.<ref name="Equipment note">{{HelmetCollectionCite|25|Helmets}} identifies this part of the [[clone trooper]] [[helmet]] as a polarized lense.</ref> Clone sharpshooters in Phase I [[clone trooper armor]] were further equipped with an attached helmet [[macrobinoculars]], and could be supplemented with a black left-shoulder [[pauldron]]. The unit's heavy weapons clone troopers were also additionally outfitted with a helmet-mounted visor, although they could also be stocked with a small, black right-shoulder pauldron, a [[Clothing|clothed]] bag slung across the torso, and additional [[armor]] attachments. Clone trooper officers were provided with a [[rangefinder]] attachment to their helmets.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

====Phase II====
[[File:ATRTdriver-Incredibuilds.png|thumb|right|180px|An ARF trooper of the 41st Ranger Platoon, the divisional markings on their legs denote their affiliation to the platoon.]]
Later equipped with [[Phase II clone trooper armor]], standard clone troopers could either wear a forest camouflaged armor set, or sport additional dark markings on their shoulder armor and two dashes on the right side of the chest armor. Clone specialists continued the use of the attached macrobinoculars on their helmets, and could also be stocked with a left-shoulder pauldron. Specialists without the pauldron were denoted by two reflected diagonal dashes of dark green across their chest, along with dark green shoulder armor. Heavy weapons clone troopers were equipped with a clothed bag slung across their chest and torso, a small right-shoulder pauldron, and additional armor attachments, though they could alternatively have additional dark green markings added to their forest camouflaged armor on the chest, [[Limb|arm]], shoulder, and leg armor. Clone trooper officers of the unit with Phase II were provided with rangefinders, as well as a gray [[kama]] and additional dark green markings on their shoulder and chest armor.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

The 41st Ranger Platoon's Advanced Recon Force Scout Troopers were provisioned with<ref name="Complete Vehicles NE" /> a modified set of Phase II armor, with green helmet markings denoting their affiliation to the 41st Elite Corps<ref name="HC 37">{{HelmetCollectionCite|37|Helmets}}</ref> and the divisional markings on their legs demonstrating their affiliation to the 41st Ranger Platoon.<ref name="Complete Vehicles NE" /> Their armor was further painted in muddy green that faded into a shade of brown camouflage at the limbs, which then turned into a dirty white at the lower legs and feet. Their ARF helmet was outfitted with reinforced side flaps and sported an advanced [[comlink]] device with a projecting [[antenna]].<ref name="HC 37" />

===Weapons===
The soldiers of the unit were armed with various handheld weapons. The standard clone troopers were equipped with [[DC-15A blaster carbine]]s, the clone specialists with [[Valken-38 blaster rifle|Valken-38x]] [[sniper]]s, the officers with [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster]]s, and the heavy weapons clone troopers with [[DC-15A blaster rifle]]s or [[DC-15LE]]s,<ref name="Battlefront II" /> the unit's ARF troopers made use of DC-15A [[blaster rifle]]s, [[grenade]]s, and other explosives and weaponry.<ref name="HC 37" /> The ARF troopers of the unit additionally utilized [[RPC-2 Rocket Launcher]]s that were stored aboard their vehicles.<ref name="On the Move">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2020/3/26/on-the-move|text=On the Move}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:41st-Ranger-Officer-P2.png|thumb|left|180px|A clone trooper officer of the 41st Ranger Platoon.]]
The 41st Ranger Platoon was active during the Clone Wars between the [[Galaxy|Galactic]] Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During the war, the 41st Ranger Platoon was often dispatched to [[Saboteur|sabotage]] enemy supply lines and vulnerable installations, allowing them to draw attention away from Republic interests.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> By the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Mon Cala]], Phase II clone trooper armor was adopted by the [[Republic Military]],<ref name="Water War">{{TCW|Water War}}</ref> including the 41st Ranger Platoon.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

The platoon continued to serve late in the war, and participated in the [[Battle of Kashyyyk]] alongside other forces of the wider 41st Elite Corps presence on [[Kashyyyk]] and the [[Wookiee Army]]. ARF troopers of the platoon fought alongside [[Red Company]] at the beachfront of [[Kachirho]] [[City]], while supported by [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut]]s, [[Infantry Support Platform]]s, and [[All Terrain Attack Pod]]s.<ref name="Episode III" />

Following the implementation of [[Order 66]] and the start of the [[Great Jedi Purge]], a patrol of 41st Ranger Platoon's ARF troopers were tasked with searching the battlefield for any clone survivors and any wounded [[Wookiee]]s that could be [[Death|executed]].<ref name="HC 37" /> Once the patrol reached a downed Wookiee [[Oevvaor jet catamaran|Oevvaor Jet Catamaran]] on the beach, an ARF trooper [[officer]] informed his fellow troopers that the Wookiees were dead, and ordered two troopers to move to the east while he and another went in the opposite direction.<ref name="Episode III" /> The 41st Ranger Platoon's ARF troopers helped hunt [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] after the implementation of Order 66,<ref name="Armor Databank">{{Databank|clone-trooper-armor|Clone Trooper Armor}}</ref> although they failed to stop him from escaping the [[planet]] in an [[E3-standard starship lifeboat]].<ref name="Episode III" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 41st Ranger Platoon was created for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'', which was originally released by [[Electronic Arts]] in [[2017]],<ref name="Battlefront II" /> but the platoon itself was only introduced into the video game as part of the "Elite Corps Update" on [[August 29]], [[2018]]. In this update, however, the 41st Ranger Platoon clone trooper skins for players were released as being the standard 41st Elite Corps appearance.<ref name="Elite Corps">{{EA|subdomain=battlefront-forums|url=discussion/117662/community-transmission-elite-corps-update|text=Community Transmission &ndash; Elite Corps Update}}</ref>

Later, in a customization overhaul of the clone trooper appearances, a different appearance was created for the 41st Elite Corps, and the original appearance was spun-off as the newly-created 41st Ranger Platoon in an update on [[February 27]], [[2019]].<ref name="Battlefront Forum">{{EA|subdomain=battlefront-forums|url=discussion/130124/community-transmission-clone-troopers|text=Community Transmission &ndash; Clone Troopers}}</ref> Shortly prior to the update's release, the 41st Ranger Platoon was originally going to be named the 87th Rangers, but the name was changed.<ref name="Names">{{Twitter|JayKingIngram|status/1098394258476658689|Jay Ingram|quote=Name/legion changes below (original -> new): • Mid Rim Garrison -> 181st Armor Division • 87th Rangers -> 41st Ranger Platoon|archivedate=20200411225216}}</ref> With that same update, the coloring scheme of the 41st Ranger Platoon's armor was slightly modified, adding dark green shoulder armor as well as differing dark green patterns to their camouflage and the modification of equipment.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

[[File:ARFScoutTrooper-DarknessOnUmbara.png|thumb|right|A 501st Legion ARF trooper as depicted in the ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' episode "[[Darkness on Umbara]]."]]
The [[2020]] reference book by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[Jason Fry]], ''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]'' [[Retroactive continuity|retconned]] the 41st Ranger Platoon as being the AT-RT drivers that appeared in the [[2005]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] by the black divisional markings<ref name="Complete Vehicles NE" /> on the drivers' thigh armor seen in the film.<ref name="Episode III" /> Prior to this retcon, the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episodes that made up the [[Battle of Umbara]] arc also depicted the [[501st Legion]]'s ARF troopers with the same black markings on their thigh armor.<ref name="DoU">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref> However, in 2024, [[Sideshow Collectibles]] released a 1:6 scale figure of the 501st ARF trooper with a notable lack of black thigh markings. Instead, the figure featured the regular Phase II 501st clone trooper's blue leg stripes. It is uncertain whether this was an intentional deviation from the cartoon following this retcon, or just a mistake made by the designers who didn't notice the black markings in the episodes.<ref name="Sideshow">{{SideshowCite|set=Sixth Scale Figures|pack=ARF Trooper and 501st Legion AT-RT|link=collectibles/star-wars-arf-trooper-and-501st-legion-at-rt-hot-toys-911125}}</ref>
