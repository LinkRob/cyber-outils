The '''''Esperance''''' was a [[starship]] used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Following the [[Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> rebel leader [[Mon Mothma]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> who had spent the battle on board away from the action on board a [[Mon Mothma's shuttle|shuttle]],<ref name="Contingency">{{StoryCite|story=Contingency Plan|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> was present on board the ''Esperance'' while the [[Evacuation of Yavin|evacuation]] of the rebel [[Great Temple|headquarters]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]] took place<ref name="Rebel Files" /> in [[0 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

The ''Esperance'' was due to depart for Yavin in order to aid with the evacuation, as rebel [[Rebel Alliance Navy|naval]] [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] was deploying all ships to assist, but when Mothma learned from her [[aide]] [[Hendri Underholt]] that the vessel ''[[Nangilima]]'' was departing a [[Standard day|day]] earlier, she and Underholt transferred to the vessel from the ''Esperance'' less then an [[Standard hour|hour]] before its departure. The communication discussing the transfer was preserved in the collection of documents known as ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Esperance'' was mentioned in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
