The '''Coto-Xana system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colonies]]. It was located in grid square L-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Coto-Xana]].<ref name="Origin" />
