'''Coyerti''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]], and was the home of the [[Coyerti (species)|Coyerti]] [[species]]. Due to its rich plant and animal life, it served as a military research outpost for [[Biotoxin|biological weapons]]. It was the location of a [[Coyerti campaign|conflict]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Blaster bolts rip up the atmosphere. Every time you fire, ''something'' gets vaporized. Every planet stinks a little different.|Hazram Namir, to Roach|<ref name="BTC" />}}
Coyerti was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="BTC" /> located in the [[Bortele sector]]'s [[Coyerti system]],<ref name="Systems" /> within [[the Slice]] portion<ref name="Slice" /> of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. Situated at the coordinates S-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> it was at the edge of the Mid Rim, with one [[moon]] in its [[orbit]]. Its terrain included hills and valleys, with [[jungle]] among them. The planet's [[atmosphere]] was humid with a heavy vegetal smell that reacted to [[blaster]] bolts, creating a pungent smell. Coyerti was rich in [[plant]] and animal life, having thick-leaved [[tree]]s and [[Color|red]] ferns as well as being [[Homeworld|home]] to a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] of the [[Coyerti (species)|same name]].<ref name="BTC" />

==History==
===Republic and Imperial era===
Coyerti was the subject of some debate among members of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Galactic Senate|senate]] during the [[Standard year|years]] prior to the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref> which begun in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> It was argued a shipping lane should be opened between Coyerti and<ref name="Queen's Shadow /> the planet<ref name="Malastare DB">{{Databank|malastare|Malastare}}</ref> [[Malastare]] to transport [[permacrete]].<ref name="Queen's Shadow />

In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> the Republic [[Proclamation of the New Order|transitioned]] into the [[Galactic Empire]]. The Empire came to use Coyerti as a research outpost as the planet was rich in plant and animal life, making it a perfect testing and development ground for the Empire's [[Bioweapon|biological weapons]]. The [[poison]]s were manufactured at an Imperial [[Distillery]] in Coyerti's hills and deployed on the planet's ecosystem, leaving behind bogs of rotting trees and composting debris. In the meantime, the native Coyerti fought back at the Imperial occupiers, wasting Imperial resources in over a [[Standard decade|decade]] of war.<ref name="BTC" /> Some [[time]] by the year [[1 BBY]],<ref name="1 BBY">''[[Rebel Rising]]'' establishes that Coyerti was visited by [[Jyn Erso]], who [[Death|died]] during the [[Battle of Scarif]] in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]].</ref> the criminal [[Jyn Erso]] spent time bouncing around the Mid Rim on planets such as Coyerti and [[Cerea]] before settling on the planet [[Takodana]] for almost a year.<ref name="Rebel Rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref>

===Galactic Civil War===
====Twilight attacks====
{{Main|Coyerti campaign}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="3 ABY">{{BTCdate}}</ref> during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], the Coyerti entered their reproductive season, which left the natives defenseless against the Empire's forces on Coyerti. As a result, a message was sent to the [[Alliance High Command]] of the matter. Three [[Standard week|weeks]] later, the High Command sent the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]], better known as Twilight [[Company]], to Coyerti to protect the planet's natives. The deployment came with additional goals, one being to provide cover for the Alliance's forces that were [[Mid Rim Retreat|withdrawing]] from the Mid Rim following a failed campaign. The second was to test knowledge provided by the defecting Imperial [[Governor]] [[Everi Chalis]] on the Distillery.<ref name="BTC" />

Twilight Company deployed [[squad]]s of [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldiers]] onto Coyerti using [[dropship]]s. On the first [[Standard day|day]] of the [[Coyerti campaign|campaign]], two squads&mdash;led by [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] and [[Sergeant]] [[Zab]] respectively&mdash;[[Attack on an Imperial scout post|attacked]] an [[Imperial scout post]] that was being used as logistical aid in the Empire's offensive against the Coyerti natives. The two squads overran the Imperial camp within ten [[Standard minute|minutes]] before rigging its equipment for detonation and escaping into the jungle before [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bomber]] reinforcements could arrive.<ref name="BTC" />

On the second day, Namir's squad prepared to ambush an enemy convoy, only to find out later that the Imperials had changed course. The same day, the first large-scale engagement occurred when a dozen Twilight squads led by squad commander [[Carver]] [[Attack on an Imperial fort|attacked]] an [[Imperial fort (Coyerti)|Imperial fort]]. As the night passed, the battle came to an end, with the rebels coming out victorious. The next day, Namir and his squad were tasked with destroying an Imperial [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]] [[walker]]. The four rebels made hit and run attacks on the walker, but later changed tactic and led the AT-ST into a marsh, where the vehicle sunk into a bog.<ref name="BTC" />

====Imperial losses====
On the fourth day of the Coyerti campaign, Namir's squad travelled from the bog and into Coyerti's highland jungle enroute to the Imperial Distillery, using intelligence provided by Chalis. Stopping to eat at sunset, the squad travelled until midnight before getting some rest. The next day, they stalked the Distillery and observed the patrol patterns and entry points. When a lightly [[armor]]ed [[transport]] arrived around midday, the [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldier]] [[Brand]] used the opportunity to sneak into the compound and plant a [[microdetonator]] at the bottom of a blue chemical barrel, set for thirty [[Standard minute|minutes]].<ref name="BTC" />

When the [[detonator]] set off, releasing the barrel's toxins, the Distillery began an evacuation, with all of its entrances opening up for personnel to make their way out. Namir's squad sneaked to a back entrance and killed its sole stormtrooper guard before making their way in and planting explosives in various rooms, killing an Imperial laborer who had failed to evacuate. Once done with their task, they escaped from the Distillery and made it half a [[kilometer]] before the explosives destroyed the compound completely.<ref name="BTC" />

====Kenobi's plea====
In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> in a conversation on<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> the [[swamp]] planet<ref name="DagobahDB">{{Databank|dagobah|Dagobah}}</ref> [[Dagobah]], the [[Force spirit|Force ghost]] of the [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] told the aspiring Jedi [[Luke Skywalker]] that the fate of Coyerti and many other planets depended on a final confrontation between Skywalker and the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Odyssey" />
