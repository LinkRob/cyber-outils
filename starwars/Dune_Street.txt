'''Dune Street''' was a street in [[Mos Eisley]], [[Tatooine]]. The wreckage of the ''[[Dowager Queen]]'' was located on this street.<ref name="CL 2016" />

On this road, [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] were stopped by an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] checkpoint while they were traveling through the city.<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>
