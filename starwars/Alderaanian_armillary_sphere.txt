The '''Alderaanian armillary sphere''' was a type of armillary sphere of the [[Alderaanian]] culture. They had rings, a central sphere, and bands that tracked constellations through the sky. [[Crimson Dawn]] [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]] had an Alderaanian armillary sphere onboard his [[Dryden's study|personal museum]] in his [[yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]''.<ref name="Official Guide" />
