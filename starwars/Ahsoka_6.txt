'''''Ahsoka'' 6''' is the sixth issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries [[Star Wars: Ahsoka (comic series)|''Star Wars: Ahsoka'' comic adaptation]]. It is an adaptation of the [[Part Six: Far, Far Away|sixth episode]] of the television series [[Star Wars: Ahsoka|''Ahsoka'']]. The issue was written by [[Rodney Barnes]], illustrated by [[Georges Jeanty]], [[Dexter Vines]], and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 18]], 2024.

==Publisher's summary==
''FAR, FAR AWAY&hellip;. The search for GRAND ADMIRAL THRAWN reaches beyond the limits of the galaxy! SABINE WREN continues her search for former REBEL EZRA BRIDGER!''

==Opening crawl==
{{OpeningCrawl
|name=PART SIX: FAR, FAR AWAY
|text=Padawan. Rebel. Ronin. She has been many things, but above all she has defended peace and justice
throughout a galaxy rife with conflict, losing mentors and friends in the process. She is&hellip;.

After being defeated by BAYLAN SKOLL, AHSOKA TANO awakened in a strange dreamlike place, where her former master,
ANAKIN SKYWALKER, finished her training by forcing her to face her past. Meanwhile, on SEATOS, HERA SYNDULLA and her
squad searched for Ahsoka and SABINE WREN. After hearing the sound of lightsabers in the seas below, Hera's son,
JACEN, guided the squadron to the water, where they discovered Ahsoka.

While Hera stayed behind to face the encroaching NEW REPUBLIC fleet, Ahsoka set out to follow Sabine. From inside a
PURRGIL, she flew into hyperspace, beginning her journey to another galaxy far, far away&hellip;.}}
