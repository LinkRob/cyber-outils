The '''Eye of Mesra''' was a small, round toy used for fortune-telling.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> Children in the [[city]] of [[Mos Espa]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] frequently played with "Eyes of Mesra" toys.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Eye of Mesra first appeared in the [[2003]] [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']]. It became canon when it was included in the 2017 reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''.
