'''Dleggman's Outfitters''' was a supply shack which operated out of the settlement [[Ferryman's Reach]] on the [[planet]] [[Pagodon]]<ref name="MandoVG" /> by [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> The shack supplied seasonal [[fishing]] gear for trawlers to utilize while on the frigid planet.<ref name="MandoVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dleggman's Outfitters first appeared in "[[Chapter 1: The Mandalorian]],"<ref name="Chapter 1" /> the first episode of the [[The Mandalorian Season One|first season]] of the live-action [[Disney+]] show ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', which was released on [[November 12]], [[2019]].<ref name="Episode Amount">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/heat-vision/star-wars-new-mandalorian-details-footage-unveiled-1201931|text='The Mandalorian': Jon Favreau Teases Disney+ Series at Star Wars Celebration|author=Aaron Couch & Graeme McMillan|date=2018-12-10|archivedate=20191109001233}}</ref> The supply shack was first identified in the [[2026]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="MandoVG" />
