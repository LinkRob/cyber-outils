'''CG-412''' was a<ref name="Datapad" /> [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] in the [[709th Legion]] of the [[First Order]].<ref name="Datapad" /> Even though other stormtroopers were taken away from their families as [[Youngling|infants]],<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> CG-412 claimed to have met a [[CG-412's grandfather|grandfather]]. According to the stormtrooper, the [[Parent|grandparent]] would often state that being stationed in a hot environment was better than being deployed to the<ref name="Datapad" /> [[Water|ice]] [[planet]]<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> [[Hoth]].<ref name="Datapad" />

Along with the rest of the 709th Legion, CG-412 was deployed at [[Black Spire Outpost]] during the First Order's [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the planet [[Batuu]]<ref name="Datapad" /> around [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">CG-412 was stationed on [[Batuu]] during the [[First Order]]'s [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the planet, which took place in [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> During the occupation, CG-412 spoke with fellow [[trooper]] [[LA-233]], who complained about the warmth of the planet, but CG-412 pointed out that it could have been worse, recalling the grandfather's statement about Hoth. The stormtrooper wore standard [[First Order stormtrooper armor]] with a [[Color|white]] [[pauldron]].<ref name="Datapad" />
