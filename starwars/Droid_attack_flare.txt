A '''droid attack flare''', also known as an '''alert flare''', was a [[flare]] used by the [[Grand Army of the Republic|forces]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. A [[droid]] attack flare could be fired to alert allied forces of danger. Shortly after the [[Defense of Rishi Station|attack]] on the Republic's [[Rishi Moon]] [[Rishi Station|base]] by Separatist [[BX-series droid commando|commando droids]] in [[22 BBY]], the surviving [[Cloning|clones]] alerted the recently arrived [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]] to the threat of [[battle droid]]s using the droid attack flare.

==Description==
A droid attack flare,<ref name="Rookies" /> or alert flare,<ref name="Rookies EG">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode005.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Rookies}}</ref> was a type of [[flare]] used by [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. When fired into the air, the flare could warn allied forces of danger, burning a [[Color|red]] color.<ref name="Rookies" />

==History==
{{Quote|I'll signal them with this flare.|Hevy prepares to fire a droid attack flare|<ref name="Rookies" />}}
[[File:Rex_gun.png|thumb|right|325px|Rex sensed the danger of enemy forces after being warned by an attack flare.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|105}}</ref> shortly after the [[First Battle of Geonosis|onset]] of the Clone Wars, the [[Rishi Station]] [[listening post]] on the [[Rishi Moon]] was [[Defense of Rishi Station|attacked]] and taken over by Separatist [[BX-series droid commando|commando droids]]. The survivors from the attack, the [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s [[Echo]], [[Fives]], and [[Hevy]], escaped the base and fled in the surrounding landscape as [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] arrived at the outpost for an inspection.<ref name="Rookies" />

As the two [[Clone trooper officer|clone officer]]s unknowingly conversed with a [[Battle droid|commando droid]] who was disguised in [[Phase I clone trooper armor|clone armor]], Hevy fired a droid attack flare to alert the pair of the Separatists' infiltration. Although Cody was initially surprised by the flare, Rex quickly realized what the flare meant and shot the [[droid]] they had been speaking to with<ref name="Rookies" /> his<ref name="Rookies EG" /> [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|blaster pistol]]. The two clones were then ambushed, with several more commando droids attacking them. However, they managed to escape and, after being joined by the three surviving clones, soon retook the base.<ref name="Rookies" />

==Behind the scenes==
A droid attack flare first appeared in "[[Rookies (episode)|Rookies]],"<ref name="Rookies" /> the fifth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' that aired on [[October 24]], [[2008]]. It was identified as an alert flare in the corresponding episode guide on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Rookies EG" />
