'''Floid Burbakk''' was a [[Sullustan]] who worked as a barber on [[The Island]] of a [[Unidentified planet (The Island)|planet]] after being stranded there and becoming one of the many [[Castaways (The Island)|Castaways]] who called the island home. He had [[Color|black]] [[eye]]s and light skin. Burbakk ran a barbershop on The Island. He wore a pink [[wig]], which he put a lot of effort into keeping it on his head, a white [[Clothing|shirt]], and black pants.<ref name="Castaway" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|CUSTOMIZE YOUR HAIR COLOR: Meet Floid Burbakk at the new Barbershop to discover a new customization feature!|Version History for update 1.1.4|<ref name="Version History">{{AppStore|url=lego-star-wars-castaways/id1474344996|text=LEGO® Star Wars™: Castaways}}</ref>}}
Floid Burbakk appeared in the [[2021]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Castaways]]''. Floid Burbakk serves as the shop for the [[Unidentified Castaway|player character]] to purchase different [[hair]] styles and change their hair color.<ref name="Castaway" /> Floid Burbakk was added to the game in update 1.1.4, made available to download on [[January 13]], [[2022]].<ref name="Version History" /> Prior to Burbakk's introduction, hair styles were purchasable at [[Kivo Doktut]]'s [[Doktut's Divinities|shop]],<ref name="Video">{{SW|url=news/lego-star-wars-castaways-interview|text=''LEGO Star Wars: Castaways'' Sends Players on a Bricktastic Adventure}}</ref> with no way to customize the hair color.<ref name="Version History" />
