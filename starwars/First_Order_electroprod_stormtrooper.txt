The '''First Order electroprod stormtrooper''', also kown as the '''prisoner control stormtrooper''',<ref name="Batuu">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> '''electroprod stormtrooper''' or '''electroprod trooper''', was a variant of the [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] that was active during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[Resistance]]. They were outfitted with [[electroprod]]s and [[First Order stormtrooper armor]]<ref name="WIRED" /> and were trained to react to social unrest.<ref name="Batuu" /> At least one was [[Mission to Pasaana (Resistance)|deployed]] to [[Pasaana]] and helped capture the [[Resistance]] member [[Chewbacca]]. Another one was present on [[Kijimi]] and hounded a mammal during a [[Mission to Kijimi|crackdown]].<ref name="TROS" />
