'''Clone commandos''', also known as '''Republic commandos''' while serving under the [[Galactic Republic]] and '''Imperial Commandos''' under the [[Galactic Empire]], were elite [[clone trooper]]s that served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s [[Special Operations Brigade]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. A result of the [[Kaminoan]]s' [[DNA|genetic]] [[Bioengineering|experimentation]] with the [[Jango Fett]] [[Clone template|template]], they were special forces [[soldier]]s noted for their training and elite status in the [[Cloning|clone]] [[army]]. In [[22 BBY]], [[Clone Commander]] [[Ponds]] gave command over five [[commando]] units to [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]], who directed them to engage in a frontal assault against the [[Separatist Droid Army]] during the [[First Battle of Geonosis]].

As the conflict between the Republic and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] swept across [[the galaxy]], the commandos were organized into [[squad]]s that supported the Republic war effort as an elite group within the Grand Army. One such team was [[Delta Squad]]; following the [[Devaron massacre|battle]] on [[Devaron]], they reported directly to the [[Jedi High Council|High Council]] of the [[Jedi Order]], informing them that no survivors were found at the massacre site. [[Foxtrot Group]] consisted of commandos led by [[Clone Captain]] [[Gregor]]. They served with the [[212th Attack Battalion]] until the [[Battle of Sarrish]], where they were declared "missing in action."

In the latter [[Standard day|days]] of the war, a squad of clone commandos called [[Clone Force 99]]–better known as the Bad Batch—participated in the [[Battle of Anaxes]], fighting alongside [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and the [[501st Legion]]. Unlike the other enhanced clone commandos who came before them, each member of the squad possessed a unique genetic mutation designed to heighten a specific skill set.

Following the end of the Clone Wars, the clone commandos became part of the [[Imperial Military]]. In the months following the Empire's formation, numerous clone commandos would be reassigned as training instructors to [[TK stormtrooper]]s, non-clone soldiers that were set to bolster and eventually fully supplant clone troopers. Other commandos would form the [[Imperial royal guard]], a specialized unit assigned to protect and assist the [[Advanced Science Division]] under the leadership of [[Royce Hemlock]].

==History==
===Conception===
{{Quote|Armor like that, I take it you're CCs like me?''"<br />"''CT-99s.|Gregor and Hunter|<ref name="War-Mantle" />}}
In [[82 BBY]],<ref name="82BBYDate">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'' establishes that the [[mission to Asusto]] took place immediately following the [[funeral of Countess Anya]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[82 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Dooku]] experienced a [[Force vision]] of the [[Clone Wars]], at that point a mere possibility that could be averted. In the vision,<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> a clone commando<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> was part of the [[One-Eighty-Fourth Attack Battalion]] as one of its [[commanding officer]]s and took part in a battle against [[B1-series battle droid]]s. The commando ordered the battalion to move out shortly before the execution of [[Order 66]]. That vision and the others experienced by Dooku melded together, although his friend [[Jedi Master]] [[Lene Kostana]] assured him those were only possibilities and that the future was not set in stone.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

Ultimately, however, the [[Grand Army of the Republic|clone army]] and its commandos would come to pass,<ref name="War-Mantle" /> with Dooku himself playing a major role in the [[army]]'s creation as he fell to the [[dark side of the Force]].<ref name="TheSith">{{TOTJ|The Sith Lord}}</ref> In an effort to make "super soldiers," [[Kaminoan]] [[scientist]]s who created the clone army [[Bioengineering|changed and augmented]] the [[DNA|genetic]] [[Clone template|template]] of some [[clone trooper]]s, leading to the [[army]]'s first [[commando]] unit.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

===The Clone Wars===
{{Quote|I have five special commando units awaiting your orders, sir.|Ponds, to Mace Windu|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
[[File:Clone-Command-Urban-Fighter.png|thumb|left|210px|A clone commando with customized armor on Kamino]]
Five commando units served under [[Clone Commander]] [[Ponds]] during the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] where the newly-formed Grand Army of the Republic&mdash;the army of [[Cloning|clones]] created by the Kaminoans&mdash;engaged the [[Separatist Droid Army]] for the first [[time]] in the [[Clone Wars]]. During the battle, Ponds and his commandos served under the leadership of [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]], a leading member of the [[Jedi High Council|High Council]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Episode II" /> At one point during the war, a large grouping of clone commandos, joined by at least two jetpack equipped ARC troopers, carried out an assault against a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] stronghold.<ref name="LeaguePacket">{{Asmodee|subdomain=cdn.svc|domain=net|url=production-amgcom/uploads/2025/05/SWQLeaguePacket2025.pdf|text=''Star Wars: Legion'' League Events Packet 2025 (PDF)}}</ref> Per their standing orders for the battle,<ref name="BattleCards">{{Asmodee|subdomain=cdn.svc|domain=net|url=production-amgcom/uploads/2025/04/DOC13_BattleCards-1.pdf|text=''Star Wars: Legion'' 2025 Updated Battle Cards (PDF)}}</ref> commandos advanced against the Separatist base on two fronts: one group of commandos carried out a direct attack via a bridge leading to the base with the support of the ARC troopers, coming into contact with a group of [[B2-series super battle droid]]s, while another group of commandos advanced from the forest line. The latter commandos were also confronted by a force of B2 super battle droids, whose line also included an [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]].<ref name="LeaguePacket" />

During the [[Battle of Christophsis]], several clone commandos served under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], who ordered the commandos to stay in position after a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] retreat.<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref> The [[Jedi]] Council later sent [[Delta Squad]] to investigate the [[Devaron massacre|massacre]] on [[Devaron]], retrieving Master [[Halsey]]'s body alongside that of his [[Padawan|apprentice]] [[Knox]].<ref name="Witches of the Mist" /> [[Gregor]], the [[Clone Captain]] of [[Foxtrot Group]], participated in the [[Battle of Sarrish]]. With the Republic routed by the Separatist [[battle droid]]s and casualties mounting, Gregor took a [[shuttle]] to look for help, but crashed on the remote [[desert]] [[Planet|world]] [[Abafar]]. He survived and was pulled from the wreckage by [[Borkus]], a [[Sullustan]] male who owned [[Power Sliders]], a diner in the small [[mining]] [[City|settlement]] of [[Pons Ora]]. Gregor's file in the [[Republic Military]] database listed him as missing in action. He was discovered by [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]] and [[D-Squad]], and after regaining his memories, Gregor then decided to assist Gascon and his squad in destroying a [[rhydonium]] [[Rhydonium mining installation|mining installation]] being operated by the Separatists. However, he volunteered to be left behind to buy D-Squad time to [[Escape from Abafar|escape]].<ref name="Missing in Action" /> He managed to survive.<ref name="War-Mantle" />

While putting down an [[Insurrection on Yalbec Prime|insurrection]] on [[Yalbec Prime]], [[Clone Force 99]], also known as "The Bad Batch," was summoned by Commander [[Cody]] during the [[Battle of Anaxes]] to assist him and Clone [[Captain]] [[Rex]] in ascertaining the secret behind [[Admiral]] [[Trench]]'s strategies. However, matters escalated when [[Wrecker]], the team's bruiser, cut off the [[Yalbec]] [[Unidentified Yalbec queen|queen's]] [[Yalbec queen stinger|stinger]] with a blade, causing multiple Yalbec males to attempt to mate with the clones. However, the team was able to escape to Anaxes aboard their [[gunship]], the [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|''Marauder'']].<ref name="The Bad Batch" />

[[File:RepublicCloneCommandos-SWL118.png|thumb|right|300px|Republic clone commandos were effective fighting units throughout the war.]]
After fighting alongside Rex on Anaxes, the Bad Batch joined him and [[General]] Skywalker on a [[Mission to Skako Minor (Clone Wars)|mission to Skako Minor]] to rescue the captured [[Advanced Recon Commando]] [[Echo]].<ref name="A Distant Echo">{{TCW|A Distant Echo}}</ref> After the changes he went through, Echo became a [[Genetically defective clone|defective clone trooper]].<ref name="SecretsCloneTroopers">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Clone Troopers]]''</ref> After the rescue of Echo, Rex, Skywalker, Echo, and the Bad Batch rallied together to take out Trench's flagship ''[[Invulnerable]]'' and his Droid Army. After that victory on Anaxes, Echo,<ref name="Unfinished Business">{{TCW|Unfinished Business (episode)}}</ref> who was further promoted to the rank of [[Clone Corporal]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars New">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> joined the Bad Batch due to the [[cybernetics]] he was given on [[Skako Minor]], which he felt separated him from the average clone trooper.<ref name="Unfinished Business" />

Prior to the [[Battle of Kashyyyk]], the [[Wookiee]]<ref name="CVD" /> [[Chief]]tain<ref name="Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Tarfful]] was captured by [[Trandoshan]] [[enslaver]]s. These Trandoshans had made a deal with [[Count]] [[Dooku]], but a group of clone commandos [[Rescue of Tarfful|rescued]] Tarfful<ref name="CVD" /> from the slavers.<ref name="Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition" /> Meanwhile, the Bad Batch continued their one hundred percent success rate on missions, joining the fighting in the [[Battle of Kaller|Conquest of Kaller]] before the execution of [[Order 66]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> Skywalker, as the newly-christened Sith Lord [[Darth]] Vader, marshaled the 501st for a [[Operation: Knightfall|siege of the Jedi Temple]] during the execution of Order 66.<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> Amongst his forces was an elite group of clone commandos, who Vader led into the Temple<ref name="NOP">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref> with the standard troopers to wipe out the all Jedi they found inside the building.<ref name="ROTS" />

===The Empire===
====Training the next generation====
{{Quote|How did you even end up in this place?''"<br />"''I was sent here with other commandos&hellip;and quickly realized I wanted out. It turns out the Empire doesn't take too kindly to desertion.|Echo and Gregor|<ref name="War-Mantle" />}}
[[File:Clone-Commando-TK-trooper.png|thumb|left|250px|Clone commandos trained the first generation of non-clone stormtroopers, known as TK troopers.]]
Following the conclusion of the Clone Wars and the transition of the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="War-Mantle" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> clone commandos, now officially rechristened as Imperial Commandos<ref name="Imperial Commando">{{HotToysCite|set=Television Masterpiece Series|packtype=Sixth Scale Figure|pack=#128: Imperial Commando|link=collectibles/star-wars-imperial-commando-hot-toys-913144}}</ref> continued to serve in the [[Imperial Military]]. In the months following the Empire's formation, fifty commandos, including Gregor, were assigned to an [[Imperial Base on Daro|Imperial Base]] on the [[planet]] [[Daro]] to train the first generation of non-clone Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s, designated as TK troopers, which numbered 1,000 soldiers. As part of [[Project War-Mantle]], the TK troopers were intended to bolster and eventually fully supplant [[Cloning|cloned]] soldiers throughout the Imperial Military. Donned in [[TK stormtrooper armor|new armor]], the TK troopers were trained by the clone commandos, although Captain Gregor did not teach them everything he knew. Eventually, Gregor grew disillusioned with the Galactic Empire and deserted one night, but was tracked through the [[forest]]s of Daro by Imperial forces and was captured and imprisoned. Although captured, Gregor managed to activate a [[homing beacon]] in order to reach out to former clone officer Rex, who was covertly running the [[Clone Underground]], a resistance network of rogue clones.<ref name="War-Mantle" />

The Bad Batch&mdash;who, with the exception of [[Crosshair]] due to him remaining loyal to the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]], had also defected from the Empire and became [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] for the [[information broker]] [[Ciddarin Scaleback|Cid]]&mdash;infiltrated the base to rescue Gregor. After rescuing Gregor, the base's security protocols were triggered by [[Tech]] who used an old clone code, [[Code-16]], which did not work in the facility. Soon after, they were ambushed by a squad of TK troopers and their clone commando instructor. The troopers were incapacitated by [[stun]]s, and the commando was placed in a chokehold and strangled unconscious by [[Hunter]]. The group then made their way to a [[turbolift]] to reach the top of the base and escape, but were cut off by another squad of TK troopers and commando [[Scorch]], who immediately opened fire. The Bad Batch managed to escape by lowering the lift and exiting, but continued to be pursued by Scorch and his TK troopers. Engaging in firefights throughout the base's corridors, Gregor was wounded by Scorch, who in turn was stunned multiple times and incapacitated. Another clone commando and his forces moved in from the opposite direction, and the Bad Batch were forced to escape through the base's exhaust vents that led through the mountain and out to an opening on a cliffside.<ref name="War-Mantle" />

A clone commando and his troops deduced their location, and moved in on the group at the exhaust vents. The commando and the stormtroopers fired on the Bad Batch as they waited for their ship, and the commando was shot by Wrecker who had arrived on the ''Marauder''. As the rogue clones were escaping, the clone commando charged forward through the exhaust vent and engaged in hand-to-hand combat with Sergeant Hunter and Echo, but was restrained. After managing to break free from the hold and stepping back into the exhaust vent, the commando was accidentally struck with a barrage of blaster fire from his own troopers, and subsequently fell down to the bottom of the mountain screaming. Hunter was separated from the rest of the rogue clone group during the skirmish, and was captured by several clone commandos and multiple TK troopers. He was subsequently imprisoned and taken under Imperial custody.<ref name="War-Mantle" />

====Guarding Tantiss====
[[File:Nala-Se-arrives.png|thumb|right|285px|Imperial royal guard clone commandos escort Nala Se to the Mount Tantiss facility on the planet Wayland.]]
Meanwhile, commandos with [[Color|grey]] markings<ref name="Kamino Lost" /> identified as the [[Imperial royal guard]]<ref name="KL EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/kamino-lost-episode-guide|text="Kamino Lost" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}}</ref> were stationed at the [[Tantiss Base]] in [[Mount Tantiss]], the site of a [[cloning]] operation on the planet [[Wayland]] headed by the [[Advanced Science Division]]. There and off-world, commandos in blank armor also served as security for the scientific division.<ref name="Metamorphosis" /> Royal guard commandos escorted the [[Kaminoan]] [[Chief Medical Scientist]] [[Nala Se]] to the Wayland facility, where she was welcomed by the Imperial medical officer [[Scalder]].<ref name="Kamino Lost" /> Scorch would, in time, be transferred to Wayland<ref name="Metamorphosis">{{TBB|Metamorphosis (episode)}}</ref> as a [[Clone Commander]].<ref name="DB Scorch">{{Databank|clone-commander-scorch|Clone Commander Scorch}}</ref>

In [[18 BBY]], over a year after the Empire's formation, an Imperial shuttle carrying a cloned [[Zillo Beast]] was ripped out of [[hyperspace]] after the cloned animal broke out of containment and attacked the vessel's crew. The beast would chase a lone surviving clone commando through the ship's corridors before ultimately killing and eating him. Concurrently, royal guard commandos were present for the arrival of Doctor [[Royce Hemlock]] on Mount Tantiss, as he would be placed in charge of the Advanced Science Division and all cloning operations at the facility. Upon learning that an Imperial transport carrying a cloned [[Zillo Beast]] had crash-landed on the planet [[Silla]], Hemlock dispatched a strike team of royal guard commandos to recapture the beast. After the cloned animal had been subdued and put into stasis, the clone commandos rounded up the populace of a nearby village that had witnessed the Zillo Beasts' rampage and shipped them off-world to be dealt with.<ref name="Metamorphosis" />

Sometime after, a royal guard commando assisted in the transfer of clone test subjects from an [[Balmorra Imperial outpost|outpost]] on [[Balmorra]] to the Wayland facility on Wayland. The commando boarded a [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] along with a security team of [[TK stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] and the Imperial [[Captain]] [[Pearce]]. Before the cruiser could jump to [[hyperspace]], it was [[Attack on the prisoner transport ship|ambushed]] by a shuttle belonging to the Clone Underground. As the rogue clones boarded the Gozanti and fought through its stormtrooper crew, Pearce ordered the royal guard commando to erase the ship's [[databank]] to prevent it from falling into enemy hands. The rogue clones entered the ship's bridge and dispatched the commando before he could complete his task, and held Pearce at gunpoint before the Imperial officer killed himself via a [[Electro capsule|suicide shocker]]. The rogue clones fled the ship as it was strafed and destroyed by Imperial [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|V-wings]].<ref name="Tipping Point" />

===Clone Force 99===
{{Main|Clone Force 99}}
{{Quote|Experimental unit Clone Force 99, they're defective clones with, uh&hellip; desirable mutations.|Cody|<ref name="The Bad Batch" />}}
[[File:TheBadBatch-TheBadBatchOperativeExpansion.png|thumb|right|250px|By the end of the Clone War, Clone Force 99 consisted of five clone commandos: Hunter, Wrecker, Tech, Echo, and Crosshair.]]
During the development of the clone army, the Kaminoans experimented with the genetics of the [[Jango Fett]] template in an attempt to create super [[soldier]]s. While the experiments bore few results, four of the test subjects came together to form the squad, Clone Force 99, referring to themselves as the "Bad Batch."<ref name="Clone Force 99">{{Databank|clone-force-99-bad-batch|Clone Force 99 / The Bad Batch}}</ref> The members of the squad were engineered to be different, but unlike the other enhanced clone commandos who came before them, each member had a unique genetic mutation designed to heighten a specific skill set,<ref name="SecretsCloneTroopers" /> the specialist clone commandos<ref name="Tech">{{Databank|tech|Tech}}</ref> used their "desirable" mutations, along with unorthodox tactics, to accomplish their objectives.<ref name="Clone Force 99" /> Members of Clone Force 99, an elite squad of defective clones, had the prefix "CT"<ref name="War-Mantle" /> (which stands for Clone Trooper)<ref name="SecretsCloneTroopers" /> in contrast to the "CC" or "RC" used by regular clone commandos.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> The group was led by [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Hunter]],<ref name="Hunter">{{Databank|hunter-bad-batch|Hunter (Bad Batch)|nolive=1}}</ref> who was skilled and stoic,<ref name="Clone Force 99" /> and had greater combat prowess than most.<ref name="Hunter" />

Providing the team's muscle was [[Wrecker]],<ref name="Clone Force 99" /> who was gifted extraordinary strength,<ref name="Wrecker">{{Databank|wrecker-bad-batch|Wrecker (Bad Batch)|nolive=1}}</ref> though he had a temper.<ref name="Clone Force 99" />[[Tech]], while not physically imposing,<ref name="Tech old databank">{{Databank|tech-bad-batch|Tech (Bad Batch)|nolive=1}}</ref> was a gifted strategist<ref name="Clone Force 99" /> and unmatched in the use of technology.<ref name="Tech old databank" /> [[Crosshair]] was the squad's no-nonsense<ref name="Crosshair">{{Databank|crosshair|Crosshair|oldversion=20160513135015}}</ref> marksman,<ref name="Clone Force 99" /> though he did not like working with "regs," by which he meant unaltered clones.<ref name="Crosshair" /> [[Clone Corporal]] [[Echo]], a [[cyborg]], joined the squad following the crucial [[Battle of Anaxes|Battle for Anaxes]].<ref name="Unfinished Business" /> Though experimental, they were extremely effective during the Clone Wars.<ref name="Anaxes">{{Databank|anaxes|Anaxes}}</ref><ref name="Aftermath" />

===Foxtrot Group===
{{Main|Foxtrot Group}}
{{Quote|You were a captain and part of an elite squad; a clone commando. The file says you were reported missing in action during the Battle of Sarrish.|Colonel Meebur Gascon|<ref name="Missing in Action" />}}
[[File:Gregor.png|thumb|left|300px|Clone Captain Gregor was the commanding officer of Foxtrot Group.]]
Foxtrot Group, led by Clone Captain Gregor, was an elite unit of clone commandos attached to the [[212th Attack Battalion]].<ref name="Missing in Action" /> The unit was reported missing in action at the [[Battle of Sarrish]]. Captain Gregor was also reported missing after the Battle of Sarrish. However, he later reappeared on [[Abafar]] with amnesia.<ref name="Gregor">{{Databank|gregor|Gregor}}</ref> While he was willing to lay down his life during the escape from Abafar, he promised he would make his way back home<ref name="Missing in Action" /> and indeed survived, remaining active into the Imperial Era. However, he would go onto desert the Empire after being stationed on Daro<ref name="War-Mantle" /> and joined the Clone Underground.<ref name="Infiltration" />

===Imperial royal guard===
{{Main|Imperial royal guard}}
[[File:Royal-Guard-Commandos.png|thumb|right|300px|A duo of Imperial royal guard commandos]]
A large unit of clone commandos, Imperial royal guards served under the Galactic Empire's clandestine Advanced Science Division, tasked with guarding its facilities and recovering its assets. They were mainly stationed at the Wayland facility on Mount Tantiss,<ref name="Kamino Lost" /> but could also be found at Imperial installations like the [[Balmorra Imperial outpost|Balmorra outpost]].<ref name="Tipping Point" /> The vast majority of royal guard commandos were easily distinguishable by gray markings on their armor, while others returned their armor to its blank-white state.<ref name="Kamino Lost" />

===Project War-Mantle===
{{Main|Project War-Mantle}}
Fifty clone commandos, including Clone Captain Gregor and Scorch, were deployed to the [[Imperial Base on Daro]] as part of [[Project War-Mantle]], the training, organization, and implementation of [[TK stormtrooper]] conscript soldiers for the [[Imperial Army]].<ref name="War-Mantle" />

===Recovery Strike Team===
{{Main|Recovery Strike Team}}
Two clone commandos, including [[Clone Lieutenant]] [[Hilo]], were assigned to the Imperial Army's [[Recovery Strike Team]] under the leadership of [[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]]. The strike team consisted of several [[TK stormtrooper]]s and a large group of [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="Extraction" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Sideshow-EchoDC15.png|thumb|right|300px|Advanced Recon Commandos (an example of an ARC trooper in the form of Echo during his days as one such soldier, pictured) and clone commandos are separate classifications of commando-leveled clone troopers despite some mistaken connections.]]
Clone commandos were first mentioned in [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] indirectly during the Battle of Geonosis. The concept of clone commandos was introduced within the ''[[Star Wars]]'' Expanded Universe novel ''[[The Cestus Deception]]'' and the characters were later heavily featured in the game ''[[Star Wars: Republic Commando]]''. Clone commandos were introduced into ''Star Wars'' canon through the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Witches of the Mist]]."

Clone commando concept art for both Scorch<ref name="Mantle Concept 1">{{SW|url=war-mantle-concept-art-gallery|text="War-Mantle" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> and a standard clone commando<ref name="Mantle Concept 2">{{SW|url=war-mantle-concept-art-gallery|text="War-Mantle" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> by [[S. Carey]] for the ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' episode "[[War-Mantle]]" name clone commandos as a variant of ARC trooper, with both model sheets named some variation on "cloneCommandoARC." Both pieces were dated to [[April 22]], 2020, and released on [[StarWars.com]] after the release of "War-Mantle."<ref name="Mantle Concept 1" /><ref name="Mantle Concept 2" /> While [[The Walt Disney Company|Walt Disney Studio]]'s Getty Images page identified clone commandos seen in ''The Bad Batch'' episode "[[Shadows of Tantiss]]" as "Clone ARC Commando Troopers,"<ref name="Getty">{{Getty|url=thewaltdisneystudios/allaccess/#!asset/mjh5wpw9bxpvcss79s8fsnfz|text=ABB-FF-004834|date=2024-02-21}}</ref> the video game ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront II" /> and reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Clone Troopers]]'' depict clone commandos and ARC troopers as separate clone classes.<ref name="SecretsCloneTroopers" />
