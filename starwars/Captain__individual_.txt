'''Captain''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] [[enslaver]] who served [[Allehander Pso]]. He was a slave for a long [[time]] after his [[Captain's stepfather|stepfather]] sold him. Captain's first master was the cruel [[Kiretim]], and [[Standard year|years]] later, Pso bought him. Sometime before [[Jyn Erso]] arrived on the [[Five Points]] [[space station]], he became a slave dealer and sold Pso's workers to the [[Galactic Empire]]. He commanded a [[Amarills-class freighter|''Amarills''-class freighter]] that transported them from Five Points to [[Rumitaka]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

Erso captured him and his crew and rescued the slaves. He was then [[Death|killed]] when one the slaves pushed him and his crew out of an airlock into [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
