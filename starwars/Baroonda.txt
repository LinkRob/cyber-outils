'''Baroonda''' was an astronomical object<ref name="RacingWinner">{{LSWCite|57|The Racing Winner}}</ref> located in the [[Baroonda system]] within the [[Calaron sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> The [[Triellus Trade Route]]<ref name="Triellus" /> connected it to the celestial body [[Marleyvane]] and<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Scarif">{{EncyclopediaCite|Scarif and Other Planets in the Outer Rim}}</ref> [[Oba Diah]]. It was situated in grid square T-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> and shared its name with the [[city]] [[Baroonda (city)|Baroonda]]<ref name="GEStaff">{{GEStaff|BSO 401.72|park}}</ref> and the [[Baroonda Circuit]] [[podracing]] course.<ref name="ScumAndVillainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
===Milestones===
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Baroonda first appeared in "[[The Racing Winner]]," a non-canon [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story written by [[Martin Fisher]] and illustrated by [[Bob Molesworth]]. The story was published in the [[LEGO Star Wars 57|fifty-seventh issue]] of the magazine [[LEGO Star Wars (magazine)|''LEGO Star Wars'']],<ref name="RacingWinner" /> which was released on [[February 22]], [[2020]].<ref name="jedi-bibliothek">{{WebCite|url=https://jedi-bibliothek.de/datenbank/ausgabe/lego-star-wars-magazin-57|text=LEGO Star Wars Magazin 57|archivedate=20240303173302}}</ref> Baroonda's name was subsequently included in a canonical source, [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''.<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Baroonda/Legends|Baroonda]] originated in the [[1999]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']], where it served as the location of several [[Podracing/Legends|podracing]] tracks.<ref name="EpIRacer">[[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']]</ref> "The Racing Winner" referenced elements from the game,<ref name="RacingWinner" /> including the [[Fire Mountain Rally/Legends|Fire Mountain Rally]] and partially submerged statue heads.<ref name="EpIRacer" />

===The Racing Winner===
{{Quote|Welcome, race fans, to another edition of Baroonda's legendary Fire Mountain Rally! Is everyone ready?|An [[Unidentified announcer (Baroonda)|announcer]] starting off the Fire Mountain Rally|<ref name="RacingWinner" />}}
In "The Racing Winner," Baroonda was a terrestrial [[planet]] with a breathable,<ref name="RacingWinner" /> Type I<ref name="Atmosphere">{{LSWCite|57|The Racing Winner}} establishes that Baroonda had a breathable [[atmosphere]] that {{BuildFalconCite|43|Secrets of Spaceflight}} classifies as Type I.</ref> [[atmosphere]] that was comprised of [[ocean]]s, [[forest]]s, and [[beach]]es. [[Palm tree]]s grew on the beaches and alongside a large waterfall, while [[fish]] inhabited the planet's oceans. Near one beach, the weathered head of a statue emerged from the [[water]]. The planet served as the location of the legendary [[podracing]] event [[Fire Mountain Rally]], where the participants would race through the terrain and over the oceans of Baroonda.<ref name="RacingWinner" />

[[File:LegoBaroondaRace.jpg|thumb|right|300px|The Fire Mountain Rally on Baroonda]]
In an edition of the race that took place<ref name="RacingWinner" /> between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="RacingWinnerDate">[[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] joins the cause of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] in {{Film|V}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]]. In "[[The Racing Winner]]," Calrissian is fighting for the Rebel Alliance against [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. ''Star Wars: Timelines'' dates Vader's [[death]] to [[4 ABY]]. The events of "The Racing Winner" must therefore be set between 3 and 4 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]]<ref name="Scarif" /> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] traveled to Baroonda to race against three of his [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s. At the time, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] operated a hidden [[Unidentified Rebel base (Baroonda)|base]] on Baroonda, which was located near the race track. After being notified of the Empire's presence on the planet, Rebel Alliance members [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], and [[Chewbacca]] worried about the Empire discovering the location of the base. Calrissian devised a plan and ordered the rebels to disrupt the race to make the Empire leave the planet.

During the race, Organa released several [[varactyl]]s onto the track, which caused [[Darth Vader's podracer|Vader's podracer]] to scratch against a rock. When [[Unidentified stormtrooper 1 (Baroonda)|one]] of the stormtroopers managed to take the lead, Vader used [[the Force]] to let a palm [[tree]] crash into the [[trooper]]'s [[podracer]] and destroy it. He later destroyed another racer by throwing a brick into its [[engine]]. A [[Unidentified stormtrooper 4 (Baroonda)|stormtrooper]] visiting Baroonda on vacation, who had been [[fishing]] from a small [[boat]] nearby, was thrown into the water by one of the passing podracers. As Vader reached the final turn, Calrissian shot at one of his engines with a [[blaster rifle]], which caused Vader's podracer to come to a halt. The stormtroopers, who had managed to repair the destroyed podracers, then raced across the finish line, which enraged Vader and caused him to leave the planet together with his [[troop]]s.<ref name="RacingWinner" />
