'''Fireflies''' were [[insect]]s, capable of emitting a powerful glow.

==Biology and appearances==
The fireflies<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> were a type of [[insect]] with six [[Limb|legs]], a pair of [[antenna]]e, and a pair of wings.<ref name="SWH" /> During nighttime, the little critters emitted a powerful [[Color|yellow]] glow, which lighted up their given space.<ref name="The Deserter">{{TCW|The Deserter}}</ref>

==History==
Fireflies could be found on the [[planet]] [[Saleucami]]. One night, when [[Jek Lawquane|Jek]] and [[Shaeeah Lawquane]] were playing hide and seek in the fields of [[Cut Lawquane|their father's]] [[Lawquane's Farm|farm]], several fireflies were hovering around the crops.<ref name="The Deserter" />

Fireflies were also found in the [[Aleen Underworld|underworld]] of the planet [[Aleen]], where several harassed a wayward [[C-3PO]] before being dispersed by [[R2-D2]] with his built-in [[fire extinguisher]]. Later, a swarm of them guided the pair of [[droid]]s to the grotto of [[Orphne]], a mysterious [[Reptile|reptilian]] nymph.<ref name="Mercy Mission">{{TCW|Mercy Mission (episode)}}</ref>

When [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] spoke to [[Yoda]] regarding the secret of the [[Force spirit]] process, he told Yoda the Force would guide him. The [[Living Force]] appeared to Yoda in the form of glowing fireflies and led him away.<ref name="Voices" />

These Force fireflies later guided [[Ezra Bridger]] when he was in the [[Lothal Jedi Temple|Jedi Temple]] on [[Lothal]].<ref name="path">{{Rebels|Path of the Jedi}}</ref>

In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SWH Date">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> fireflies were found in [[Aftermath|Survivor Salvage]]&mdash;a battlefield designed after the planet [[Hoth]] on one end and the planet [[Endor]] on the other&mdash;within the [[Grand Arena]] on [[Vespaara]].<ref name="SWH" />
