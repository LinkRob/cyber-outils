The '''Dhen-Moh genocides''' were ordered by the [[Galactic Empire]] on the astronomical object [[Dhen-Moh]]. The [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Death|killed]] many there with his [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|lightsaber]], an act that was marked on his reputation afterward. Various figures compared the atrocity on Dhen-Moh with some of the Empire's other major genocides.

==The genocides==
Between [[19 BBY]] and [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Galactic Empire]] ordered genocides on the astronomical object [[Dhen-Moh]].<ref name="Alphabet" /> The [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] notably took part in the mass [[Death|murder]], massacring many with his [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|lightsaber]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Chemical weapons may have killed many Geonosians but Darth Vader's lightsaber proved just as effective on Dhen-Moh. The motivation behind all these appalling events was the Imperial principle that mass violence was the simplest solution to almost any problem.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
[[File:Beaumont_Kin.png|thumb|right|200px|The historian Beaumont Kin compared the genocides on Dhen-Moh with the extermination of the Geonosians.]]
[[Everi Chalis]], an Imperial [[governor]] who had [[Desertion|defected]] to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]], cited his participation to the rebel [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]]<ref name="BTC" /> in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date 2">{{AlphabetSquadronDate}}</ref> [[Yrica Quell]], an Imperial [[pilot]] who had defected to the [[New Republic]], the Alliance's [[Declaration of a New Republic|successor]], compared the Dhen-Moh genocides&mdash;alongside the atrocities involving<ref name="Alphabet" /> the [[planet]]s<ref name="Rise" /> [[Alderaan]] and [[Lasan]]&mdash;to the Empire's enactment of [[Operation: Cinder]], another genocide ordered by the Empire, in her mind during a conversation. Quell considered the difference between the former genocides and Operation: Cinder as that between ruthlessness and cruelty.<ref name="Alphabet" />

In<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[35 ABY]]<ref name="Date 3">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] commented that Darth Vader using his [[lightsaber]] on Dhen-Moh was just as effective of a mass killing as the Empire's use of [[Bioweapon|chemical weapons]] to [[Sterilization of Geonosis|exterminate]] the [[Geonosian]] [[species]]<ref name="Rise" /> around [[9 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The historian added that the Empire's motivation behind these atrocities was that mass violence was a simple solution to any problem.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dhen-Moh genocides were first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="BTC" />
