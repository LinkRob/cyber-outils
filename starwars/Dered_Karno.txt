'''Dered Karno''' was an [[Unidentified snouted species|alien]] [[mercenary]] who, alongside [[Ommo Twilto]], worked as an enforcer. Karno wielded an [[electroprod]] [[staff]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

Karno wore a dark [[Color|red]] [[Clothing|shirt]] with a brown [[belt]], pants, [[glove]]s and [[boot]]s as well as a gray [[helmet]] and [[armor]] made of [[plastoid]] tiles wrapped in [[leather]] padding. The [[alien]] had brown skin and black [[eye]]s and black, braided [[hair]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

Dered Karno was created for the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']],<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> and was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]. The book includes Karno in a section on the planet [[Pasaana]],<ref name="TROS VD" /> although the character is not present in any scenes on the planet in the film.<ref name="TROS" /> Karno's name is a partial anagram of the name of actor [[Derek Arnold]], who portrayed Karno in the film and referred to the character as Dovine on set.<ref name="Tweet">{{Twitter|tomKwilton|1208762061968363520|[[Tom Wilton]]|quote=Ladies and gentlemen meet your friendly local enforcers, Dered Karno and Ommo Twilto or Dovine and Tovine as @ActorDArnold and I liked to refer to them on set 😀|archivedate=20210601002317}}</ref> Arnold stated that everything about Twilto and Karno and their appearance in ''The Visual Dictionary'' made completing the sequel trilogy worthwhile.<ref name="ArnoldTweet" />
