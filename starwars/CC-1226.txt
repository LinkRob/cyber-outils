'''CC-1226''' was a [[clone trooper]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], who later served the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[stormtrooper]] [[officer]] in the [[Imperial Military]]. After the [[mission to Desix]], [[Clone Force 99|clone commando]] [[Crosshair]], was called to [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]]'s [[Vice Admiral Rampart's office|office]] and instructed to report to the [[Clone trooper officer|clone officer]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Clone loyalty does not seem to be as advertised anymore.|Rampart to Crosshair after the latter questioned Commander Cody's whereabouts|<ref name="Solitary" />}}
[[File:ReportToCC1226-TheSolitaryClone.png|thumb|300px|left|Vice Admiral Rampart ordered CT-9904 to report to CC-1226.]]
CC-1226 was a [[Cloning|clone]]<ref name="Solitary" /> of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]], [[Reproduction|created]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]], to serve as a [[clone trooper]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Join the Battle" /> After the conclusion of the war in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="EP3Date">{{FilmDate|III}}</ref> the clone served the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[stormtrooper]] [[officer]] in the [[Imperial Military]].<ref name="Solitary" /> Following the [[mission to Desix]], which transpired between 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|203}}</ref> CT-9904, "[[Crosshair]]," was called from the [[Imperial Inspectorate HQ]]'s [[Imperial Inspectorate cafeteria|cafeteria]] to [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]]'s [[Vice Admiral Rampart's office|office]]. In Rampart's office, the [[Admiral]] informed the [[Clone Force 99|CT-99]] clone trooper that<ref name="Solitary" /> he<ref name="SWECG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> was assigning the clone to another mission, this [[time]] under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of CC-1226. When Crosshair asked Rampart why<ref name="Solitary" /> he<ref name="SWECG" /> was not being sent alongside CC-2224, [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Cody]], the Admiral<ref name="Solitary" /> scoffed<ref name="SolitaryCC">{{TBB|The Solitary Clone}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref> and informed the clone that Cody had gone [[Desertion|AWOL]]. Rampart elaborated that clone loyalty had become less popular and remarked that that<ref name="Solitary" /> he<ref name="SWECG" /> found it ironic that<ref name="Solitary" /> since the [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishment]] of the Galactic Empire,<ref name="EP3Date" /> that clones were gradually disappearing on a more regular basis.<ref name="Solitary" />

==Personality and traits==
A [[human]] clone of Jango Fett,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> CC-1226 served as a stormtrooper officer in the Imperial Military during the [[Imperial Era]]. The stormtrooper officer was considered loyal to the Galactic Empire by Vice Admiral Rampart.<ref name="Solitary" /> CC-1226 was 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]]) tall, had [[Color|tan]] skin<ref name="Join the Battle" /> and weighed eighty [[kilogram]]s.<ref name="Weight" />

==Skills and abilities==
The [[clone trooper officer]] was proficient in military tactics, as well as both armed and [[unarmed combat]].<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Equipment==
Like all clone troopers, CC-1226 had an [[Behavioral modification biochip|inhibitor chip]] implanted in their [[brain]] by [[Kaminoan]] [[scientist]]s to ensure obedience.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
CC-1226 was first mentioned in "[[The Solitary Clone]]," the third episode of the [[The Bad Batch Season 2|second season]] of the animated [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'',<ref name="Solitary" /> which premiered on [[January 11]], [[2023]].<ref name="BB S2">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-bad-batch-season-2-trailer-key-art|text=Clone Force 99 Is Back in New ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 2 Trailer}}</ref>
