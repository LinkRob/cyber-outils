'''Cara Pifko''' (born [[March 15]], [[1976]]) is a [[Wikipedia:Canada|Canadian]] actress and voice actress. She has provided the voices of [[Suu Lawquane]], [[Doctor]] [[Sionver Boll]], [[TJ-912]], [[Naa'leth]], and [[Orphne]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.

==Career==
A graduate of the [[Wikipedia:National Theatre School of Canada|National Theatre School of Canada]], she started acting at a young age and worked all through the nineties, but it was in [[2000]] that she landed her first starring role, in the series [[Wikipedia:Our Hero|''Our Hero'']], which was aimed at young teens.

In [[2004]] she showed her dramatic range by appearing as a foreign aid worker in the acclaimed mini-series [[Wikipedia:Human Cargo|''Human Cargo'']]. Pifko then landed the lead role in the legal drama [[Wikipedia:This is Wonderland|''This is Wonderland'']], which garnered her a [[Wikipedia:Gemini Award|Gemini Award]] in [[2005]] for best actress. She recently made her return to acting, joining the [[Wikipedia:soap opera|soap opera]] [[Wikipedia:General Hospital|''General Hospital'']] as the recurring character of Louise Addison. In early [[2006]], she appeared as Isolda in [[Wikipedia:Tarragon Theatre|Tarragon Theatre's]] production of ''Léo''. In [[2007]] she starred in the [[Wikipedia:Lifetime (TV network)|Lifetime]] television movie [[Wikipedia:I We Wed|I Me Wed]]. In [[2010]], she voiced [[Yeoman/Legends|Yeoman]] Kelly Chambers in the video games [[W:c:masseffect:Mass Effect 2|''Mass Effect 2'']] and [[W:c:masseffect:Mass Effect 3|''Mass Effect 3'']].
