'''AG-90''' ('''Aygee-Ninety'''), was a "[[monster droid]]" built by the [[scavenger]] [[Dec Hansen's mother|mother]] of [[Dec Hansen]]. The [[Gender|boy]] had no siblings, so his [[Parent|mother]] built AG-90 out of various parts, including the head of a [[J9 worker drone|J9]] and the torso of an [[Agromech droid|agromech]]. AG-90 acted as Hansen's older brother, and the two grew up together on the [[swamp]]-[[planet]] [[Ques]]. The [[droid]] never had his [[memory wipe]]d, giving him a quirky, unpredictable personality. By [[34 ABY]], the two came to serve in the [[Resistance]] against the [[First Order]], training to become [[pilot]]s in [[J-Squadron]]. However, J-Squadron's leader, [[Jothan Tiaan Jerjerrod]], came into conflict with AG-90, believing that the droid was just a tool and needed a memory wipe. During a [[mission to Vodran]], AG-90 and Hansen became separated after being attacked by [[Vodran]]'s [[Species|wildlife]], and the droid stumbled onto a patrol of [[First Order stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Join the Resistance" />
