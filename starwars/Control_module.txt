The '''control module''' was a device which the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Grand Moff]] [[Lozen Tolruck]] used to program and control the [[Behavioral modification biochip|inhibitor chips]] inside all of the [[Wookiee]] [[Slavery|slaves]] on the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]]. The device was a console that Tolruck kept at his [[Lozen Tolruck's island fortress|island fortress]]. Tolruck's control module was of the transmitting variety and was totally wireless.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>

==History==
In [[3 ABY]], <ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[human]] [[thief]] used a control module to remotely control her [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Life Debt Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> the former Imperial [[loyalty officer]]&ndash;turned&ndash;[[New Republic]] operative [[Sinjir Rath Velus]] used a [[hyperwave transceiver]] spike to hack into his control module and disable the inhibitor chips. This led to a mass Wookiee [[Liberation of Kashyyyk|uprising]] that ended Imperial rule on Kashyyyk.<ref name="Life Debt" />
