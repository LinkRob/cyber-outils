A '''brub-brub bush''' was a type of thorny [[bush]] that grew [[Brub berry|brub berries]] and could be found on the [[Endor|Forest Moon of Endor]]. One [[winter]] on Endor<ref name="Kroolok" /> between [[1 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="KroolokDate">[[Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle 4|''Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle'' 4]] depicts [[Logray]] before he became [[shaman]] of the [[Bright Tree tribe]] and is dated to [[1 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. "[[One Normal Day]]" depicts [[Wicket Wystri Warrick]] as no longer being a [[wokling]] and takes place and occurs concurrent with the events of {{Film|VI}}, which ''Timelines'' dates to [[4 ABY]]. As "[[The Kroolok]]" depicts Logray as a shaman and Warrick as a wokling, it must take place between these two dates.</ref> the [[Ewok]] [[Weechee (Ewok)|Weechee]] returned from a [[hunting]] party with many brub [[Berry|berries]]. When his [[Gender|brother]] [[Wicket Wystri Warrick|Wicket Warrick]] noticed the berries, he expected Weechee to boast about how difficult it had been to procure them. Indeed, Weechee later noted how high the [[fruit]]s were up on the bush and how hard it would have been for his brother, who had short [[Limb|legs]].<ref name="Kroolok" />

Brub berries were used to make a [[Brub berry sauce|sauce]], the recipe for which was included in the second [[Book|cookbook]] of [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]], ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]'', which was published after his [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|first]]<ref name="Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]''</ref> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> In the book, Tuggs mentioned the berries' origin of the brub-brub bush.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
The brub-brub bush was first mentioned in "[[The Kroolok]]," a short story written by [[George Mann]] and [[Cavan Scott]] and published in the [[2021]] ''[[Life Day Treasury]]'' short story collection.<ref name="Kroolok" />
