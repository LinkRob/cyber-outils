The '''ARC-200 Wing Cutter Fighter''' was a [[starfighter]] used by the [[Ashiga Clan]] and the [[Hutt Clan]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. The ARC-200 used the same heavy [[blaster cannon]]s<ref name="Outlaws" /> used by the [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter|ARC-170 starfighter]], as well as a similar prow<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> and [[cockpit]] design.<ref name="Outlaws" /> Like the ARC-170,<ref name="Episode III" /> the ARC-200 Wing Cutter also had two rear [[blaster]] [[turret]]s, [[missile launcher]]s, and a dorsal [[astromech socket]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
