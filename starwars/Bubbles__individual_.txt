'''Bubbles''' was an [[Xi'Dec]] present on the [[planet]] [[Lanupa]]<ref name="SC 105" /> around [[9 ABY]].<ref name="SCDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> They were standing at the entry to<ref name="SC 105" /> [[Skull Ridge Mountain Hotel & Spa]]<ref name="Spa name">{{Getty|url=thewaltdisneystudios/allaccess/#!asset/bj8gqtnvm799njt6mjc6t|text=GR-FF-001420|date=2024-12-23}}</ref> beside a few fellows, including<ref name="SC 105" /> a [[Gender|female]]<ref name="SC 105 AD">{{SkeletonCrew|You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> [[Unidentified Lanupa hotelier|hotelier]], when former [[pirate]] [[Captain]] [[Jod Na Nawood]] emerged from an access hatch embedded in the landing path. Bubbles and the other bystanders were greeted by<ref name="SC 105" /> the man,<ref name="SC 102 ST">{{SkeletonCrew|Way, Way Out Past the Barrier}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref> who at the same [[time]] failed to realize the [[hotel]] was under pirate attack. [[Gunter]], one of the pirate invaders, approached Jod from behind and ensured his quick apprehension.<ref name="SC 105" />

Bubbles had [[Color|light-blue]] [[eye]]s and white [[hair]] and skin. A member of the<ref name="SC 105" /> [[insectoid]]an<ref name="TLJ:VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Xi'Dec [[species]], they had four upper limbs<ref name="SC 105" /> and engorged [[egg]] sacs on their belly.<ref name="TLJ:VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bubbles first appeared in "[[Zero Friends Again]]," the fifth episode of the live-action [[Disney+]] show ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'',<ref name="SC 105" /> which was aired on [[December 31]], [[2024]].<ref name="Premiere">{{Twitter|StarWars|status/1861409832349172011|[[Star Wars]]|quote=Mark your calendars. 🗓️ On December 2, stream the two-episode series premiere of #SkeletonCrew, only on @DisneyPlus.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/QwMYe}}</ref> The character later appeared in the official promotional clip of the episode,<ref name="SBFoAS156lQ">{{SWYouTube|SBFoAS156lQ|Skeleton Crew &#124; Official Clip &#124; Episode 6 Now Streaming on Disney+}}</ref> in addition to having a dedicated slide in the episode's [[StarWars.com]] trivia gallery.<ref name="SC 106 EG">{{SW|url=series/skeleton-crew/season-1-episode-6-episode-guide|text="Zero Friends Again" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars: Skeleton Crew}}</ref> Her performance artist for the show was [[Robin Walsh]].<ref name="SC 105" /> While not clear within the episode, fellow performance artist [[Jasper Anderson]] noted which alien was Bubbles in an Instagram post.<ref name="p/DE8QDn6MZJi">{{Instagram|username=jasperjandersonfx|url=p/DE8QDn6MZJi|name=[[Jasper Anderson]]|date=2025-01-17|quote=This one is credited as the Dowager Alien. Bubbles is in the same crowd, and you’ll have no trouble spotting her|archiveurl=https://archive.vn/wa2vJ|archivefile=File:JasperAnderson-Bubbles-IG.jpg}}</ref>
