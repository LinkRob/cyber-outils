'''Cook The Books''' was a type of [[Shift Token]] that was used in the card game [[Kessel Sabacc]]. It inverted the Sabacc ranks during the next reveal.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] won a copy from a [[Pyke Cardroom (Myrra)|cardroom]] in the [[Pyke Syndicate]] [[Pyke Syndicate District (Myrra)|district's]] of [[Myrra]] on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Shift tokens were created for Kessel Sabacc which first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. They are one of several Shift Tokens that could be collected and used in Kessel Sabacc, an optional activity in the game.<ref name="Outlaws" />
