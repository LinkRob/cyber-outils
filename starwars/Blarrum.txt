'''Blarrum''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="MOTP 1" /> in the [[Blarrum system]] in the [[Mayagil sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was inhabited by [[gundark]]s and home to [[Kul Kenada Spaceport]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Force-sensitive]] monster hunter [[Ty Yorrick]] was hired to capture a gundark there by [[Triv Blago]], a creature trader. Yorrick was forced to [[Death|kill]] the gundark when it nearly killed a [[Drewen Qweebjillan|young Segredo]] who had followed her to the hunting area to try and watch her in action. Subsequently, Blago attempted to betray Yorrick for failing to capture the creature alive, but was arrested by two [[Jedi]] for illegally trafficking rare and dangerous animals while Yorrick escaped.<ref name="MOTP 1" />
