'''Docking Bay 441''' was a location on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]]. Docking Bay 441 was listed on the [[Death Star Directory]] in sector 441, and was destroyed alongside the [[Death Star]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] at the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="P&F" />

==Behind the scenes==
Docking Bay 441 was mentioned in the [[2014]] non-[[canon]] ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]'' crossover episode of the television series [[Wikipedia:Phineas and Ferb|''Phineas and Ferb'']].<ref name="P&F" />
