"'''Do or Do Not'''" is a short story adapting the events of [[Luke Skywalker]]'s [[Jedi]] training with [[Yoda]] during the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].

The story was adapted by [[Megan Ilnitzki]] from the previously published [[World of Reading]] adaptation ''[[Use the Force!]]'' by [[Michael Siglain]].<ref name="Galactic Stories" /> It was first published on [[July 20]], [[2015]] as part of [[Star Wars Galactic Adventures (2015)|''Star Wars Galactic Adventures'']], an illustrated storybook collection from [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]], under the name "'''Use the Force'''."<ref name="Galactic Adventures" /> It was later republished as "Do or Do Not", in ''[[Star Wars Galactic Stories: 7 Stories from a Galaxy Far, Far Away....]]'' with the text slightly changed.<ref name="Galactic Stories" /> "Do or Do Not" re-used illustrations previously done by [[Pilot Studio]] for ''Use the Force!''.<ref name="Use the Force">''[[Use the Force!]]''</ref>
