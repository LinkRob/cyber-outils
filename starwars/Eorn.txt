'''Eorn''' was a [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Miraluka/Legends|Miraluka]] [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] stationed on [[Tython/Legends|Tython]] during the [[Cold War (Galactic Republic–Sith Empire)|Cold War]] between the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|reconstituted Sith Empire]]. Eorn served as a [[Jedi Consular]] and combat instructor.

==Behind the scenes==
Eorn appears in the game files for ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''. She was originally going to serve as a Jedi Consular trainer but the content was cut. Eorn's file can be found on the Jedipedia database.<ref name="Jedipedia">{{WebCite|url=https://swtor.jedipedia.net/en/npc/training-master-eorn|text=Training Master Eorn|archivedate=20200812080334}}</ref>
