'''Awglk''' was an ancient [[Mist-Weaver]] who, alongside [[Shareen]] and [[Wkk'e]], became trapped in an [[Exogorth (Agoliba-Tu system)|exogorth]] an eon before the [[New Republic Era|New Republic era]]. Awglk, alongside their fellow Mist-Weavers, wove themselves in a cocoon to slow down [[time]] and survive inside the exogorth. Eventually, [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and a [[Unidentified female biologist|female biologist]] from the [[University of Bar'leth]] would discover the cocooned Mist-Weavers. After communicating with Luke Skywalker, the Mist Weavers allowed him to release them from their cocoon by striking it with his [[lightsaber]], causing a massive energy surge that freed the pair from the exogorth at the cost of the Mist-Weaver's [[Death|lives]].
