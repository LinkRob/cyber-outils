According to legend, many [[Standard year|years]] before<ref name="The Gilded Cage" /> [[1032 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Caldoth]] '''attacked''' the [[Jedi temple (Bathoris)|Jedi temple]] on the astronomical object [[Bathoris]] after learning that a rare fragment of a fresco relating to the ancients and their [[Force power|use]] of [[the Force]] had been transferred there after being uncovered in a Jedi expedition. Members of the [[Jedi Order]] at the [[Jedi temple|temple]] were caught by surprise, and the attack initially went well for Caldoth, who [[Death|killed]] many [[Jedi Temple Guard]]s and several [[Jedi Knight]]s. However, the Jedi Knight [[Bran Ath'Morath]] repelled Caldoth from the temple's inner sanctum and kept him locked in a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] until enough of the surviving [[Jedi]] rallied and forced Caldoth to retreat. Caldoth was shaken by his defeat, and so the [[Nightsisters]]&mdash;[[witch]]es who were Caldoth's enemies&mdash;decided to take revenge on Caldoth by performing a ritual allowing the Nightsister [[Zeldin]] to manipulate his thoughts. Over several years, she made multiple failed attempts to kill Caldoth through her influence, including influencing him to engage in another [[Battle on Kizan|duel]] with Ath'Morath.<ref name="The Gilded Cage" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on Bathoris appeared in the short story "[[The Gilded Cage]]," which was published in the [[2020]] short story collection ''[[Dark Legends]]'', written by [[George Mann]].<ref name="The Gilded Cage" />
