'''Black Spire Brew''' was a [[Color|brown]]-colored, [[caf]]-based [[Foodstuff|beverage]]. It was served [[Temperature|cold]] over [[Water|ice]] in a tall [[glass]] with a speared [[fruit]] garnish consisting of a speared green hemisphere and smaller dark spheres. The caffeinated drink was served at [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]],<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> where it was considered richer than [[Spiran caf]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[cantina]]'s [[bartender]] [[Janott]] listed the drink among those available for the [[human]] [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] to [[Diet|consume]] when she visited the cantina.<ref name="Thrawn2" /> The drink was still available in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE Date">The events of [[Oga's Cantina]] within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Black Spire Brew was first mentioned in the [[2018]] novel ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]'', which was written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Thrawn2" /> The drink has been served in the [[In-universe|real-world]] at Oga's Cantina bar in the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]. When it was announced and pictured for the public on [[February 27]], [[2019]], [[Moogan Tea]] and Black Spire Brew were erroneously identified with their names swapped;<ref name="From Blue Milk to Ronto Wraps">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galaxys-edge-food-beverages|text=From Blue Milk to Ronto Wraps: The Food and Beverages of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> Black Spire Brew was subsequently labelled correctly at the bar.<ref name="Polygon">{{WebCite|url=https://www.polygon.com/2019/5/31/18638513/disneyland-star-wars-land-what-to-eat-drink-food-photos-galaxys-edge|text=A close-up look at the otherworldly food and drink of Star Wars Land|author=Petrana Radulovic & Chris Plante|date=2019-05-31|work=Star Wars Land: a guide to Galaxy's Edge|archivedate=20200421044743}}</ref> The drink debuted for guests to purchase when the land opened in [[Disneyland]] Park on [[May 31]] and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] on [[August 29]].<ref name="DPB Foodie Guide">{{DPB|url=2019/05/foodie-guide-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Foodie Guide to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The menu's description reads, "[[Wikipedia:List of coffee drinks#Cold brew|Cold Brew]] Joffrey's [[Wikipedia:Coffee|Coffee]]&reg;, [[Wikipedia:Honey|Honey]], [[Wikipedia:Falernum|Falernum]], [[Wikipedia:Passion Fruit (fruit)|Passion Fruit]], and [[Wikipedia:Citrus|Citrus]]."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/breakfast|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Breakfast Menu}}</ref>

The [[2020]] [[Canon|non-canon]] video game expansion ''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]'' includes Black Spire Brew as a drink; however, Moogan Tea and Black Spire Brew each have the other's appearance. The drink can be purchased by the player's [[Wikipedia:The Sims|Sims]] at the Oga's Cantina vendor in the Black Spire Outpost neighborhood. Upon consuming the drink for the first time, the Sim automatically learns the recipe and can prepare it at mixology bars in their home neighborhood.<ref name="Sims">''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]''</ref>
