[[Captain]] '''Faldana Ero''' was an [[Imperial officer]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> during which they were assigned to [[Akiva]], a planet in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
Ero's name was listed on an Imperial memo that the [[Merqaal]] [[Nix]] stole from an imperial officer in [[The Alcazar]]. Nix stole the memo for his companion, [[Kay Vess]], while she was searching for the identity of an Imperial [[lieutenant]] who was racing in the [[Canto Cup]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Faldana Ero was mentioned on a [[datapad]] during the "Imperial Race" intel quest in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
