'''1978 Topps ''Star Wars'' Series 4''' was a trading card set released by [[Topps]]<ref name="Volume One" /> in [[1978]].<ref name="Copyright" />

The set was based on [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], and featured stills from the film, promotional photos and behind the scenes photos, all within a green border. Backs of the cards featured "Official Description" or "Movie Facts" themes, or included a portion of a larger image that could be pieced together with other cards like a puzzle. A sub-set of sticker cards was included.<ref name="Volume One" />

Card numbering in the set continued from the [[1977 Topps Star Wars Series 3|previous series]]. A [[1978 Topps Star Wars Series 5|fifth]] and final series of the set followed.<ref name="Volume One" />

==Continuity==
Card 206 features an image from the deleted scene of [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] repairing a [[Moisture vaporator/Legends|moisture vaporator]] on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]].<ref name="blu-ray">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]''</ref> The title of Card 232 is "The Empire strikes back!"
