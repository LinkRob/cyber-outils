'''Deena''' was an individual who worked for [[De'Rruyet Industries]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] at the [[De'Rruyet Center]] on the<ref name="Aphra 10" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Aphra 8">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 8|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 8]]</ref> [[Midarr]].<ref name="Aphra 10" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Deena's boss, [[Beol De'Rruyet]] acquired a fake [[Nihil]] [[Path engine]] [[hyperdrive]]<ref name="Aphra 10" /> via a [[Tase Somer's black market network|black market network]]<ref name="Aphra 18">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 18|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 18]]</ref> which he claimed he could replicate, and planned to unveil at the [[De'Rruyet Industries confluence]].<ref name="Aphra 9">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 9|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 9]]</ref> Before he could unveil the hyperdrive, however, forces of the [[Unbroken Clan]] criminal syndicate led by [[General]] [[Vukorah]], who had learned of De'Rruyet's hyperdrive and wanted it for herself, took over the center and took De'Rruyet and his employees, including Deena, hostage. As one of Deena's fellow employees attempted to bring the hyperdrive online for Vukorah, the general grew impatient and shot Deena, [[Death|killing]] them.<ref name="Aphra 10" />
