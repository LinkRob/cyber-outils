'''Elyhek Rue'''<ref name="Duty Roster">{{StoryCite|story=Duty Roster|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> was a [[human]]<ref name="Episode IV" /> [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Grounded" /> who served as a [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] as a member of [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. He went into the [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] training [[Flight simulator|simulator]] room inside the [[Great Temple|Massassi Temple]] on [[Yavin 4]] along with [[Biggs Darklighter]], [[Bren Quersey]], [[Wenton Chan]], and [[Wedge Antilles]] while [[Col Takbright]] was in there. Takbright became mad when Rue mistook him for Wedge. He was grounded for the [[Battle of Yavin]] and watched the battle with Takbright and the other grounded pilots.<ref name="Duty Roster" />

==Behind the scenes==
"[[Grounded]]"<ref name="Grounded" /> and [[Star Wars (2015) 45|''Star Wars'' (2015) 45]]<ref name="Star Wars 45">[[Star Wars (2015) 45|''Star Wars'' (2015) 45]]</ref> established the Rue fought at the Battle of Yavin, whereas "[[Duty Roster]]" established that he stayed behind during the battle.<ref name="Duty Roster" /> This article assumes Duty Roster is correct.
