'''Chen Yao''' was the corporate [[president]] of [[Fire (city)|Fire]], a [[space station]] and [[city]] of the [[Aakaash system]]. Born to great wealth, Chen lacked the ambition and scheming nature of [[Zhang Jincheng]], a general district manager of Fire. President Chen set his [[eye]]s on the [[Blue Dragon]] [[pirate]]s after they plundered an important [[freighter]] at Fire, causing Zhang to seek to take Chen's place and climb up the [[Realspace|space]] city's hierarchy by eliminating the pirates.<ref name="Vow" />

Zhang thus allowed the pirates to take a shipment of [[Xuanming|enigmatic tenebrae]] from the [[Silver Knights]], a prominent civil [[mercenary]] group in the area, in the hopes of manipulating the Knights into open conflict against the Blue Dragon. However, the Knights' elite [[red apricot squad]] was sent to retrieve their lost shipment, and the [[squad]] leader, [[Captain]] [[An Ping]], realized Zhang's ploy. Red apricot squad's newest member, [[Sean]], therefore proposed to continue with their pursuit of the enigmatic tenebrae and place the blame of recent incidents at Fire on Zhang afterwards, hoping that Chen Yao would become weary of Zhang, making the latter's [[Standard day|days]] miserable.<ref name="Vow" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chen Yao was mentioned in ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'', a [[Wikipedia:Written Chinese|Chinese]] web novel written by "[[His Majesty the King]]." Chen was first mentioned in a chapter released in [[2021]], and he is named '''陈耀''' in the original [[Wikipedia:Simplified Chinese characters|simplified Chinese]] text.<ref name="Vow" />
