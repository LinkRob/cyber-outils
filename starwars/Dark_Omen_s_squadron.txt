During the [[Lothal campaign]], the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|ISD]] ''[[Dark Omen]]'' was the lead [[warship]] of a '''squadron''' of [[Star Destroyer]]s from [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]].<ref name="Battles" /> The [[Battle of Atollon]] began when Grand Admiral Thrawn ordered [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]] to deploy the [[fleet]] to the [[planet]] [[Atollon]] ahead of Thrawn's ISD ''[[Chimaera]]''.<ref name="Zero Hour" /> This force included the squadron of five Star Destroyers that ''Dark Omen'' led, as well as two [[Interdictor-class heavy cruiser|''Interdictor''-class heavy cruisers]]<ref name="Battles" /> and two [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruisers]]. The Seventh Fleet's forces attacked the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] naval contingent above Atollon before the ''Chimaera''{{'s}} arrival. The Battle of Atollon resulted in an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] victory, though a number of rebel ships ultimately managed to escape.<ref name="Zero Hour" />
