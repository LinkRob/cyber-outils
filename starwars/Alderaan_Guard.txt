The '''Alderaan Guard''', also known as the '''Alderaanian royal guards''', were a [[Law enforcement agency|security force]] responsible for the protection of members of the [[House of Organa]], the royal family of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]].

==History==
[[File:AlderaanianGuard-SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|The Alderaan Guard accompanied the royal family in all their travels]]
During the [[Clone Wars]] in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="PursuitOP-Date">{{TCWcanondates|311}}</ref> [[Alderaan]]'s [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]] was attacked in a parking garage by several thugs wanting to stop him from giving a speech in the [[Galactic Senate]]. Organa pushed an emergency button summoning several guards and the [[Coruscant Security Force|Coruscant Police]]. Though the thugs were arrested, Organa's [[Airspeeder|speeder]] crashed and he was severely injured. The guards notified Senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] before taking Organa to a [[Medcenter|hospital]].<ref name="Pursuit of Peace" />

In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Mof date">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'' takes place in the [[Standard week|weeks]] and [[Standard month|months]] following the [[Proclamation of the New Order]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dares to [[19 BBY]].</ref> [[Trayus Castolle]] was the [[chief]] of the Royal Guard. After the guards failed in responding properly to multiple threats on the lives of the [[House of Organa|royal family]], Castolle was reprimanded by [[Queen]] [[Breha Organa|Breha]].<ref name="Mof">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] birth in [[19 BBY]]. The events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' commence just after Organa's sixteenth [[birthday]], which places the events of the novel in [[3 BBY]].</ref> [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] talked to one of the guards about their baby, who had begun sleeping through the night, before she began [[Leia Organa's Day of Demand|her]] [[Day of Demand]] ceremony.<ref name="Leia">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>
