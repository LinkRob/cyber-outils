'''Eskro Casrich''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Rebel trooper|soldier]] who served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Serving as a [[corporal]] in the [[Rebel Alliance Special Forces]],<ref name="UVG" /> Casrich was one of several volunteers who joined an improvised squad of rebel fighters, codenamed "[[Rogue One]]," who took it upon themselves to [[Battle of Scarif|steal]] the [[Death Star plans|plans]] of the Imperial [[superweapon]] known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. While the mission was a success, Casrich and the rest of his team lost their lives in the opening battle of the war.<ref name="Rogue One" /> Casrich was a thrill seeker and reckless fighter. As a result, he rappelled the peaks of [[Scipio]], spelunked the sinkholes of [[Utapau]], and sand-sailed the dunes of [[Ingo]]. He was a skilled fighter. Casrich wore waterproof [[M4D combat boots]] and used a blaster.<ref name="UVG" />
