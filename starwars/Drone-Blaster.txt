'''''Drone-Blaster''''' was one of the many games that [[Torra Doza]] had. It was a singleplayer<ref name="Doza Dilemma" /> [[hologame]]<ref name="Bucket Facts" /> that involved [[Hologram|holographic]] images of [[remote]]s flying around a space, and the player had to shoot the images with a [[Play blaster|toy]] [[blaster]] in order to obtain points and win.<ref name="Doza Dilemma" />

==Behind the scenes==
The holograms from Torra Doza's ''Drone-Blaster'' employed the same style of training remotes used by [[Luke Skywalker]] in the [[1977]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]].<ref name="Bucket Facts">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-doza-dilemma-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 7 Fun Facts from "The Doza Dilemma" - ''Star Wars Resistance''|date=2019-01-28|quote=Torra's Drone-Blaster hologame employs the same style of training remotes used by Luke Skywalker in ''Star Wars: A New Hope''.}}</ref>
