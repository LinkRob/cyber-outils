An ancient inactive '''[[wayfinder]]''' was said to have once led the way to the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. It was on display in the [[Royal Museum of Aldera|Royal Museum]] in the planet's capital [[Aldera]] at the time of [[Destruction of Alderaan|Alderaan's destruction]]<ref name="TROSVD" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> The wayfinder was a [[Color|bronze-colored]] cylinder that was partially surrounded by a [[stone]]-looking material.<ref name="TROSVD" />
