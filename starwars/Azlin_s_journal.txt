'''Azlin's journal'''<ref name="Edge of Balance 3">[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 3|''The High Republic: The Edge of Balance'', Vol. 3]]</ref> was the journal of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Azlin Rell]].<ref name="Precedent">''[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance: Precedent]]''</ref> The journal was long sought by [[Marchion Ro]], the [[Eye of the Nihil]]<ref name="Edge of Balance 2">[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 2|''The High Republic: The Edge of Balance'', Vol. 2]]</ref> because of the knowledge it contained on the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] [[species]].<ref name="Precedent" /> Ro believed that the journal was kept within the [[Jedi temple (Banchii)|Jedi temple]] on [[Banchii]], and ordered several [[Nihil]] to recover it and then destroy the [[Jedi temple|temple]]. The Nihil were unsuccessful in their [[Attack on Banchii|attempts]] to recover the journal, and Ro arrived on Banchii after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]] to find the object<ref name="Edge of Balance 2" /> with [[Vol Garat]], a [[mercenary]] who had seen the journal over a [[Standard century|century]] earlier.<ref name="Precedent" />
