'''Bekk Tunit''' was a famous [[Podracer/Legends|podracer]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]] prior to the [[Invasion of Naboo/Legends|Invasion of Naboo]].

Bekk Tunit was killed in a crash during one particular race, ending his famed career. The junk dealer [[Watto/Legends|Watto]] recovered one of Tunit's racing gloves from the crash site, which he kept on his trophy shelf inside his junk shop on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]].
