'''ED-4''' was a [[slicer droid]] who was one of the three [[droid brain]]s that made up the [[computer]] of the [[starship]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. The other two were known as [[V5-T]] and [[L3-37]]. Long before the ''Millennium Falcon'' was known to [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] or L3-37, the droid's consciousness was uploaded onto the ship to join V5-T, who had been the first to join the ''Falcon''.<ref name="Faith" />
