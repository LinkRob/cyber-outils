The '''Borkeen asteroid belt''' was an [[Asteroid field|asteroid belt]]<ref name="SWRCC" /> in the [[Borkeen system]] in the [[Belsmuth sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> where an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[frigate]] that held security codes resided<ref name="SWRCC" /> at some point between [[5 BBY]] and [[4 BBY]].<ref name="Date Note">{{ChopperChaseDate}}</ref> The [[Spectres|rebel crew]] of the ''[[Ghost]]'' ventured into the belt and stole the codes before escaping.<ref name="SWRCC" />

{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Borkeen asteroid belt appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the browser game ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Chopper Chase]]''. [[Borkeen Belt|It]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via the short-story "[[The Slug Named Grendel]]" published in ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'', where it was the remains of a destroyed [[Planet/Legends|planet]].<ref name="Grendel">{{WEGCite|story=The Slug Named Grendel (short story)|book=The Star Wars Sourcebook}}</ref>
