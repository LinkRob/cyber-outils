'''Bana Breemu''' was a [[human]] [[Homeworld|native]] of [[Humbarine]], [[capital]] [[Planet|world]] of the [[Humbarine sector]]. During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Breemu served as a [[senator]] in the Republic's [[Galactic Senate]], representing the Humbarine sector. The sector saw heavy fighting during the conflict, including the Separatist [[Bombardment of Humbarine|bombardment]] of Humbarine.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Biography==
As [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] accrued emergency powers throughout the Clone Wars with no formal mechanism for them to be rolled back, a number of senators became concerned and formed the [[Delegation of 2,000]], a group associated with a [[Petition of 2,000|petition]] to Palpatine. Breemu was one of many to join the movement. Following the end of the conflict<ref name="Dawn" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> a number of the senators who were a part of the delegation were arrested by the newly [[Proclamation of the New Order|formed]] [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Dawn" />

After the rise of the Empire, Senator Breemu was among those gathered together by Senator [[Mon Mothma]] at Senator [[Lud Marroi]]'s [[Lud Marroi's home|residence]] when she presented her initial concept of what would become the [[Imperial Rebirth Act]]. She excitedly conversed with Senator [[Streamdrinker]] as Mothma began her remarks. Later, as Mothma's allies attempted to rally support for the bill, Breemu and Senator [[Norve-Gloss]] focused on former members of the Delegation of 2,000 who might waver in their support of legislation meant to control the Emperor's unchecked power. Ultimately, the Imperial Rebirth Act would pass, although some elements of it were vetoed by the Emperor, who wanted to hold onto his power.<ref name="MoF" />

==Personality and traits==
Breemu had [[Color|tan]] skin, brown [[eye]]s, and black [[hair]]. She wore a violet [[Clothing|dress]] with a matching hood and sleeves<ref name="Dawn" /> as well as a pair of black shoes.<ref name="Visual">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
===Filming===
{{Quote|When you present the Petition of the 2000 to the Chancellor, things may change.|Part of Bana Breemu's dialogue in the deleted scene|audio=BanaBreemu1.flac|<ref name="Seeds">{{Film|III}} Deleted Scene: Seeds of Rebellion (Padme's Apartment)</ref>}}
Bana Breemu was created for the [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']],<ref name="Seeds" /> which released on [[May 19]], [[2005]].<ref name="YbY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> Portrayed by actress [[Bai Ling]], the character appeared in one scene<ref name="DB">{{DB|character|banabreemu|Breemu, Bana}}</ref> in which several senators meet in Senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] [[Naboo Queen (restaurant)|apartment]] on the plant [[Coruscant]] to discuss forming the Delegation of 2,000 over concerns about Palpatine. When Amidala suggests consulting a [[Anakin Skywalker|Jedi]] that she trusts, Breemu objects that it would be dangerous but later says that things may change once they have presented the petition to the chancellor.<ref name="Seeds" />

Ling got the role after befriending [[Katie Lucas]], daughter of director [[George Lucas]], at the [[2003]] [[Wikipedia:MTV Video Music Awards|MTV Music Awards]], where Katie introduced the actress to George and Ling informed him that it would be a dream come true to appear in ''[[Star Wars]]''. Two weeks later, her agent contacted her saying that she was being offered a role in the upcoming film.<ref name="Interview">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cnet.com/culture/entertainment/star-wars-bai-ling-revenge-of-the-sith-playboy|text=What it's like to be axed from a Star Wars film|author=Trenholm, Richard|date=2017-06-03|work=[[Wikipedia:CNET|CNET]]|archivedate=20230612110826}}</ref> Her scene was then filmed on Stage 1 at [[Fox Studios, Sydney]] on [[July 2]], 2003.<ref name="MakingOf">''[[The Making of Star Wars Revenge of the Sith]]''</ref> Ling describes the experience of filming as like being in an amusement park or like children playing with toys. Before filming, she asked Lucas what she should do, to which he responded "whatever you want," giving the actress an artistic freedom that she appreciated.<ref name="Interview" />

===Scene deletion and further appearances===
Breemu's one scene in the film was cut prior to the theatrical release, later being included as a deleted scene on home releases.<ref name="DB" /> Ling claimed that this may have been the result of her posing for a ''Star Wars''-themed spread in an issue of [[Wikipedia:Playboy|''Playboy'']] magazine that was published at around the time of the film's release,<ref name="Interview" /> although George insisted that the cuts were made due to the film's length nearly a year prior to her posing for the magazine.<ref name="Episode III DVD Commentary">{{Film|III}} DVD commentary</ref>

Prior to the release of ''Revenge of the Sith'',<ref name="Amazon">{{RandomHouseOld|url=author/results.pperl?authorid=18222|text=James Luceno}}</ref> [[Bana Breemu/Legends|Breemu]] debuted in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in [[James Luceno]]'s novel ''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]'', a tie-in prequel to the film<ref name="Labyrinth">''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]''</ref> released on [[January 25]], 2005.<ref name="Amazon" /> In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the character was first mentioned outside of the deleted scene in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''<ref name="Visual" /> and later first appeared in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="MoF" />
