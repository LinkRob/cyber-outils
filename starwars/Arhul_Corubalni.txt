'''Arhul Corubalni''' was a [[scout]] in the [[Republic Scout Service|Scout Service]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Corubalni wrote the initial survey log for the [[planet]] [[Vandor]] that was filed into [[Republic Survey Corps]]. The [[Corubalni Spaceport]]<ref name="Tales from Vandor" /> and the [[Corubalni Pass]] were named after them.<ref name="Official Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
