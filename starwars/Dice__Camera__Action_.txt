"'''Dice, Camera, Action!'''" was a recurring article in ''[[Star Wars Gamer]]''. Issue #10 was written by [[Robin D. Laws]] on how to integrate a twist into roleplaying campaigns. [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Scientist/Legends|scientist]] [[Etha Naru]] was used to illustrate this concept.
