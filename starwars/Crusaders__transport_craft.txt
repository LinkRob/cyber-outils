The [[Crusaders (The Grand Master)|Crusaders]] used [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]] '''transport crafts''' at some point. When the former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] was found on the [[planet]] [[Olcatz]] by the Crusaders, thanks to a report by the [[Rodian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Do N-Yuk]], the Crusaders mobilized transport crafts to the planet to support their leader, who was known as [[the Grand Master]]. Under the Grand Master's order, after his [[Duel on Olcatz|duel]] with the Ronin, the LAATs bombarded the [[AK-BK]] used by the [[Anzellan]] [[Que-Dama]] as his [[To-Ba|mobile gambling hall]].<ref name="Payback" />

During the ensuing [[lockdown of Olcatz]], the LAATs were used in the [[battle on Tkoku Island]] to carry Crusaders to [[Tkoku Island]]. Some of the ships were destroyed by defensive [[missile]]s set up underwater beside the [[Tkoku Bridge]], which provided access to the island.<ref name="Payback" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crusaders&#39; transport craft appeared in "[[The Duel: Payback]]", a non-[[canon]] short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Kamikaze Douga]] and [[ANIMA (company)|ANIMA]], was released on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Payback" />
