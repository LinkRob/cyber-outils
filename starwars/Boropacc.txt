'''Boropacc''' was the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Rapacc]], [[homeworld]] of the [[Paccosh]] [[species]]. When the Paccosh drove out the [[Nikardun]] forces that had invaded their planet, the city underwent damage, both from the Nikardun destroying what they could as they fled and from the Paccosh in their attempt to [[Death|kill]] as many of their invaders as possible.<ref name="Greater Good" />
