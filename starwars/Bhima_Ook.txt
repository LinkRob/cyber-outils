'''Bhima Ook''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Bhima Ook's species|alien]] individual who, along with [[Tulli Mu]], ran their own [[holotactics]] game in [[Pyloon's Saloon]] on [[Koboh]]. Bhima was technically an employee of Mu, but used his outsized personality to entertain players and spectators alike.<ref name="Survivor" />
