A '''climate emulator''' simulated a certain climate. The sublevel greenhouse in the [[Taa Complex]] on [[Troithe]] had one that could have an individual experience the sensation of a snowfall. Governor [[Fara Yadeez]] offered [[Soran Keize]] to utilize this technology while trapped on Troithe.<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>
