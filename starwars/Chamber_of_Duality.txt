The '''Chamber of Duality''' was one of the [[Standard century|centuries]]-old [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Chamber|Meditation Chambers]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Koboh]]. In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="JSDate">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] and his [[droid]] companion [[BD-1]] fell into a collapsed passage while searching [[smuggler]]'s tunnels below [[Pyloon's Saloon]]. From here, they discovered and entered the subterranean chamber. Within the chamber, they found [[ZN-A4]], a droid who served the [[Jedi Order]]. She had been tasked by [[Jedi Master]] [[Santari Khri]] with delivering a [[Republic tuner device|tuner device]] to the [[Forest Array]] and activating it as a [[Great Hyperspace Disaster|cataclysm]] caused pieces of [[Shattered Moon|Koboh's moon]] to pummel the surface. As she passed through the tunnels towards the Forest Array, however, ZN-A4 became trapped in rubble, where she stayed deactivated for [[Standard century|centuries]]. The droid was grateful that a Jedi had finally rescued her, and she gave to Kestis the Republic tuner device such that he could continue her mission.<ref name="Survivor" />

The Chamber of Duality made use of [[Technology (Disney Gallery: The Mandalorian)|technology]] developed in the [[High Republic Era]] surrounding the mysterious [[Koboh Matter|Koboh matter]]. There were several bridges made by solid Koboh matter, as well as modified [[orb amplifier]]s to power mechanisms within the chamber. Additionally, a nearly impenetrable door to a [[turbolift]] could be accessed using a tuner device. This turbolift led up to the [[Rambler's Reach Outpost]], just next to [[Dendra's Antiquities]].<ref name="Survivor" />
