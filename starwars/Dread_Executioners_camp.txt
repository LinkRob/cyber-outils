The '''Dread Executioners camp''' was an [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|Imperial]] outpost of the [[Dread Executioners]] located on [[Oricon]]. The personnel stationed there included [[Lord/Legends|Lord]] [[Hargrev]], [[Maiya Vix]], [[Barleos Choid]], [[Dae'o Soset]], [[Halsia Banso]], [[Seibahn Vabakson]] and [[Traxalis Deagua]].<ref name="SWTOR" />
