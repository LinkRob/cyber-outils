'''''Ahsoka'' 8''' is the eigthth and final issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries [[Star Wars: Ahsoka (comic series)|''Star Wars: Ahsoka'' comic adaptation]]. It is an adaptation of the [[Part Eight: The Jedi, the Witch, and the Warlord|eighth episode]] of the television series [[Star Wars: Ahsoka|''Ahsoka'']]. The issue was written by [[Rodney Barnes]], illustrated by [[Georges Jeanty]], [[Karl Story]], and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[February 19]], [[2025]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE JEDI, THE WITCH, AND THE WARLORD! The heroes race to prevent Grand Admiral Thrawn's escape!''
