The field of science known as '''astrophysics''' included the observation of astronomical objects such as [[nebula]]e and [[black hole]]s. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Nihil]] [[Doctor]] [[Zadina Mkampa]] studied astrophysics. The doctor applied what she had learned in the field of study to build [[light intensifier]]s that allowed her to control the [[Drengir]], a [[species]] of [[Sentience|sentient]] [[plant]]s, by having the devices emulate the wavelengths of light given off by the [[Mulita's sun|sun]] of the Drengir's [[Mulita|homeworld]].

By [[41 BBY]], a [[Jape's Jedi Master|Jedi Master]] specializing in astrophysics began [[Jedi training|training]] a [[Padawan]], [[Jape]], in the field, with the latter quickly grasping the concepts of the field. After being [[Vallt Rescue Operation|rescued]] off the [[planet]] [[Vallt]] in [[21 BBY]], the [[scientist]] [[Galen Walton Erso]] visited the [[Institute of Applied Science]]'s [[Institute of Applied Science astrophysics building|astrophysics building]] on the planet [[Coruscant]]. By the [[time]] of the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Cerberon system]]'s government expended resources to have those knowledgeable in astrophysics&mdash;known as [[astrophysicist]]s&mdash;study the [[Star system|system's]] black hole, the [[Cerberon singularity]].

==Description==
Astrophysics was a field of science<ref name="ITL">''[[The High Republic: Into the Light]]''</ref> in which individuals observed astronomical objects such as [[nebula]]e and [[black hole]]s.<ref name="MA">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> [[Pocket nova]]s were a phenomenon that was known to [[astrophysicist]]s<ref name="TFA Novel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref>&mdash;those knowledgeable in the field<ref name="ShadowFall" />&mdash;and occurred when a [[planet]]ary core was ignited by an outside force, resulting in the formation of a [[star]].<ref name="TFA Novel" /> Others studying astrophysics studied stars, including their levels of [[radiation]] and the wavelengths of the light they emitted.<ref name="ITL" />

==History==
===Republic times===
[[File:Mkampa.jpg|thumb|right|170px|Zadina Mkampa studied astrophysics as part of her wider efforts to find a way to control the Drengir.]]
While working for the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] during the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Doctor]] [[Zadina Mkampa]] studied many fields of science. Although she had rarely delved into astrophysics<ref name="ITL" /> by [[228 BBY]],<ref name="ITLDate">{{IntotheLightDate}}</ref> around that time, she began to study the field with more interest. Mkampa tasked herself with understanding select concepts in astrophysics most relevant to her plans to find a way to control the [[Drengir]], a destructive,<ref name="ITL" /> [[Sentience|sentient]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[species]] of [[plant]].<ref name="ITL" />

Mkampa's research in the field led her to become familiar with stars, learning how to study their levels of radiation and the wavelengths of the light they emitted. She eventually began studying Drengir cuttings directly to learn more about the compact [[Neutron star|neutron]] [[Mulita's sun|sun]] of the [[Mulita|Drengir homeworld]]. With this information, Mkampa developed a [[light intensifier]] device to emit the same wavelengths as the Drengir [[homeworld]]'s star, which they were naturally predisposed to be drawn toward, thus allowing her to control them. At some point, the Nihil operative [[Nan Hague|Nan]] became aware of Mkampa's attempts to create such a device using her astrophysics research, although she did not learn of the doctor's success.<ref name="ITL" /> In 228 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="ITLDate" /> Mkampa reflected on her studies of astrophysics and how they had benefitted her. Not long afterward, several [[Jedi]], the young [[scientist]] [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]], and Nan&mdash;who had by then [[Desertion|defected]] from the Nihil&mdash;[[Mission to Kashyyyk (blight)|discovered]] some of Mkampa's light intensifiers. Familiar with some astrophysics concepts, they discussed how she might have used them to control the Drengir by emulating the light of their homeworld's star.<ref name="ITL" />

Later in the [[Republic Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], a [[Jape's Jedi Master|Jedi Master]] specialized in astrophysics<ref name="MA" /> and, by [[41 BBY]],<ref name="PDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Padawan (novel)|''Padawan'']] to [[41 BBY]]. In ''Padawan'', [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] mentions [[Jape]]'s [[Jedi training|apprenticeship]] to his [[Jape's Jedi Master|Master]], meaning their partnership must have begun by 41 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> took the [[Force-sensitive]] individual [[Jape]] as their Jedi [[Padawan]].<ref name="Padawan">[[Padawan (novel)|''Padawan'']]</ref> Jape easily grasped the concepts of astrophysics and became passionate about learning his [[Jedi Master|Master's]] [[scholar]]ly field, forming a close bond with his [[teacher]].<ref name="MA" /> By [[40 BBY]],<ref name="MADate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> Jape was taken by his Master to see unique phenomena such as nebulae and black holes in [[the galaxy]] as part of his [[Jedi training|training]] in astrophysics and later sent [[Hologram|holos]] of his adventures to his friend, Padawan [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. When not traveling, Jape spent [[time]] in the [[Grand Orbital Observatory of Coruscant]], a structure designed to observe astronomical occurrences.<ref name="MA" />

===Imperial Era===
[[File:Galen-Erso-uniform.png|thumb|left|190px|Galen Erso visited the Institute of Applied Science's astrophysics building on Coruscant.]]
By the time of the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the [[Institute of Applied Science]] had an [[Institute of Applied Science astrophysics building|astrophysics building]] on [[Coruscant]], the Republic's [[capital]] planet. Shortly after being [[Vallt Rescue Operation|rescued]] off the planet [[Vallt]]<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[wedding of Lyra and Galen Erso]] to [[25 BBY]], and ''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]'' states that the [[Erso family]] moved to [[Vallt]] three [[Standard year|years]] later, placing that event in [[22 BBY]]. ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'' establishes that the rescue of the Ersos occurred after they spent one year on Vallt, placing the rescue in [[21 BBY]].</ref> the scientist [[Galen Walton Erso]] met with one of his former colleagues from the [[Republic Futures Program]]&mdash;[[Professor]] [[Reeva Demesne]]&mdash;in the then-busy building's solarium, taking note of the heightened security measures and access restrictions imposed on the building because of the war.<ref name="Catalyst" />

The Republic's successor, the [[Galactic Empire]], eventually [[Proclamation of the New Order|rose]] to power in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> and the [[Cerberon system]] fell within its domain. By the time of the later [[Imperial Era]], the [[Star system|system's]] local government had spent significant resources to have those working in astrophysics research the effects of its black hole, the [[Cerberon singularity]], on surrounding [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="ShadowFall" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ShadowFallDate}}</ref> the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]'s [[advisor]] [[Soran Keize]] proposed [[Cerberon system campaign|striking against]] the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Defense Force|forces]] who had taken control of many Cerberon system worlds by planning an [[Battle of Troithe|attack]] on one of its planets&mdash;[[Troithe]]. To support his argument, Keize mentioned the peculiarities of the system's [[orbit]]al dynamics and how its [[Cerberon debris field|asteroid field]] had been affected by [[Galactic Civil War|wartime]] activities. However, he indicated he was quoting from available reports since he was not an expert in astrophysics and offered to forward the relevant data to others if they proceeded with the plan.<ref name="ShadowFall" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Catalyst_A_Rogue_One_Novel.png|thumb|right|200px|In the current canon, astrophysics was first explicitly mentioned in ''Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel''.]]
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the field of astrophysics was first indirectly mentioned via the introduction of the position of astrophysicist, which was included in the [[2015]] [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|adult novelization]] of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] by [[Alan Dean Foster]].<ref name="TFA Novel" /> Astrophysics was first fully introduced and identified by name in the [[2016]] novel ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'', which was written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the field of [[Astrophysics/Legends|astrophysics]] was likewise first introduced via a mention of the position of [[Astrophysicist/Legends|astrophysicist]] in the [[1984]] reference book ''[[A Guide to the Star Wars Universe]]'', written by [[Raymond L. Velasco]].<ref name="Guide">''[[A Guide to the Star Wars Universe]]''</ref> Astrophysics was first mentioned by name on its own in the [[1987]] ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'', which was written by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and [[Curtis Smith]] and released by [[West End Games]] for use with ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Sourcebook">''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]''</ref> The occupation's first full appearance was marked by the astrophysicist [[Danni Quee]] carrying out work within the discipline in the [[1999]] novel ''[[The New Jedi Order: Vector Prime]]'', written by [[R. A. Salvatore]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: The New Jedi Order]]'' series.<ref name="Vector">''[[The New Jedi Order: Vector Prime]]''</ref> In the real world, [[Wikipedia:Astrophysics|astrophysics]] is a field of [[Wikipedia:Science|scientific]] study focusing on applying the laws of [[Wikipedia:Physics|physics]] and [[Wikipedia:Chemistry|chemistry]] to create physical theories describing small- to medium-scale astronomical objects.<ref name="SpaceCom">{{WebCite|url=https://www.space.com/26218-astrophysics.html|text=What is Astrophysics? &#124; Space|author=[https://www.space.com/author/ariel-balter Ariel Balter]|date=2022-10-28|work=[[Wikipedia:Space.com|Space.com]]|archivedate=20240521141046}}</ref>
