'''Condiment oil''' was an [[oil]] used as a condiment that was created using [[guiji fire pepper]]s. The oil was used in [[the galaxy]]<ref name="Queen's Bloom" /> by around [[396 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' establishes that [[Axel Greylark]] was eighteen [[Standard year|years]] old thirteen years prior to its events. As ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''Convergence'' to [[382 BBY]], he must have been [[Reproduction|born]] in [[413 BBY]]. Greylark is seventeen years old in "[[The Queen's Bloom]]," dating its events to around [[396 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]]. Consumption of condiment oil made one's [[tongue]] feel like it was on [[fire]].<ref name="Queen's Bloom" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Reena University]] student [[Axel Greylark]] took this effect into consideration when using guiji fire [[pepper]]s to fake an affliction of [[malongo pox]], which he did to get out of participating in the [[Queen's Bloom Festival]].<ref name="Queen's Bloom" />

==Behind the scenes==
Condiment oil was mentioned in the short story "[[The Queen's Bloom]]," written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Queen's Bloom" />
