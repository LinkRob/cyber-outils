'''''Choose Your Destiny: A Finn & Poe Adventure''''' is the audio version of the [[canon]] [[Choose Your Destiny: A Finn & Poe Adventure|book]] of the same name, written by [[Cavan Scott]] and part of the ''[[Star Wars: Choose Your Destiny]]'' series. The audiobook was released exclusively on [[Wikipedia:Amazon|Amazon]] FreeTime Unlimited.<ref name="Website" /> As of June 2024, it is exclusively available as an Alexa Skill with an Amazon Kids+ subscription.<ref name="Skill">{{Amazon|url=/Star-Wars-Choose-Your-Destiny/dp/B07LCZGLD7|text=Star Wars: Choose Your Destiny|archivedate=20240615023033}}</ref>

There is one canon ending, one soft ending ("You've done well, but an even greater destiny awaits you, why not go back and try a different path?"), and 26 non-canon endings. The path to the canon ending is ABC,[BBB,BABB,ABBB,ABABB,ABAAABB],BAAA,[AA,BBA],AAAAABABAAAB,ABAABAAB (the brackets indicate a choice of paths); therefore there are 10 different paths to the canon ending, taking between 50 to 52 minutes to reach the ending. The path to the soft ending is ABC,[BA,ABA],ABBB (2 paths). The total duration of all 86 story segments is approximately 94 minutes and there are 241 total paths possible.
