'''CO-34''', also called "'''See-Oh Three Four'''," was a [[CLL-M2 binary loadlifter]] who served the [[Resistance]] during its [[Cold War|conflict]] against the [[First Order]]

==Biography==
Between [[31 ABY|31]]&ndash;[[32 ABY]],<ref name="Year" /> [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]] of the Resistance was captured by the First Order. Afraid that he would be executed, the Resistance created a group with the sole purpose of locating Ackbar. The group, led by [[Captain]] [[Hoff (Resistance captain)|Hoff]], at some point captured a droid of the First Order called [[0-MR1|O-MR1]], who they thought knew the location of Ackbar. On their way back to the Resistance base, the group's ship [[Mission to Taul|crashed]] on [[Taul]] and the only survivors were CO-34, O-MR1, a [[protocol droid]] named [[C-3PO]], a [[medical droid]] named [[2MED2]], and two [[security droid]]s called [[PZ-99]] and [[VL-44]].<ref name="The Phantom Limb" />

The droids, led by C-3PO, picked up a signal from a First Order vessel. As they began following the signal, they were attacked by [[spice spider]]s. PZ-99 sacrificed himself to let the others escape, by getting the attention of the spiders and was destroyed. The rest of the droids were soon attacked once again, by [[Thidaxx]] and [[can-cell]]. VL-44, 2MED2, and CO-34 were all destroyed in the attack.<ref name="The Phantom Limb" />
