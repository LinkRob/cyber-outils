The '''Advanced Repair Unit''' was a model of [[self-repair system]] [[armor]] modification manufactured by [[AccuTronics]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]] the unit was available for 3000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="SM" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Advanced Repair Unit was introduced in ''[[Special Modifications]]'', a [[2016]] roleplaying supplement for [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="SM" />
