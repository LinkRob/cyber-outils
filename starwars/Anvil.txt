The '''''Anvil''''' was a [[Kilji war cruiser]] [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[General]] [[Crofyp]] of the [[Kilji Illumine]], the nation of the [[Kilji]] [[species]]. The ''Anvil'' was one of two Kilji [[warship]]s in [[Crofyp's task force]], the other being the [[Kilji picket cruiser]] [[Hammer (Kilji picket cruiser)|''Hammer'']]. Crofyp's war cruiser was equipped with [[Kilji escape pod|escape pods]] and [[electrostatic barrier]]s, which shielded the ''Anvil''{{'s}} [[hull]]. Its [[Weapon|armament]] included [[spectrum laser]]s.

In [[18 BBY]], the [[Grysk]] operative known as "[[Jixtus]]," who had allied himself with the Illumine's ruler, [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]], sent Crofyp's [[task force]] to the [[Unknown Regions]]' [[Rapacc system]]. The Grysk tasked Crofyp with capturing several [[The Magys's people|refugees]] hiding on the [[planet]] [[Rapacc]]. However, when they arrived at Rapacc, Crofyp's forces were [[Attack on Rapacc|confronted]] by the [[Paccosh]]-[[Thief|commandeered]] [[Nikardun blockade frigate]] [[Aelos (frigate)|''Aelos'']]. After the Kilji ships moved for a [[Space warfare|cross fire attack]] on the ''Aelos'', the [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]'', whose crew codenamed the ''Anvil'' as "'''Cruiser One'''," arrived and fired [[plasma sphere]] weapons at the war cruiser's starboard. The ''Springhawk'' destroyed the ''Anvil'' while the ''Hammer'' fled.

==Description==
{{Quote|They're sister ships to the Kilji warship ''Whetstone''.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Are you sure, sir?''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Quite sure. Even from here I can see the similarities between them and the ship we saw at Zyzek.|Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo and his [[Executive officer|first officer]], [[Mid Captain]] [[Ufsa'mak'ro]], on the ''Anvil'' and the ''Hammer''|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />|translated=1}}
The ''Anvil'' was a [[Kilji war cruiser]] noticeably longer than the [[Kilji picket cruiser]] [[Hammer (Kilji picket cruiser)|''Hammer'']]. The ''Anvil'' was [[Weapon|armed]] with several shoulder and bow weapons clusters that featured [[spectrum laser]]s. It was equipped with [[engine]]s, a [[hyperdrive]] and [[electrostatic barrier node]]s that produced [[electrostatic barrier]]s that shielded the [[war cruiser]]'s [[hull]]. The ''Anvil'' was outfitted with [[sensor]]s on its starboard side.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

The ''Anvil''{{'s}} [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], which featured a [[viewport]], housed several control stations, including the [[Commanding officer|commander's]] station and that of the [[navigator]]. The bridge featured [[Color|orange]]-rimmed doors that led to two [[Kilji escape pod]]s positioned to starboard and portside, which was closer to the navigator's station, respectively. The crew was protected by restraints.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==History==
===Refugees and the Paccosh===
{{Quote|Vassal Four, signal Colonel Tildnis that upon my signal ''Hammer'' is to move past the ''Aelos'' along its portside and continue on to the planet.|General Crofyp to Vassal Four|audio=Vassalfourquoteaudio.flac|translated=1|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
The ''Anvil'' served the [[Kilji Illumine]], the nation [[Kilji]] [[species]], as one of the two cruisers of Illumine [[General]] [[Crofyp]]'s [[Crofyp's task force|task force]], the other being [[Colonel]] [[Tildnis]]'s ''Hammer''. The war cruiser served as Crofyp's [[flagship]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Grysk]] operative known as "[[Jixtus]]," who had allied himself with the ruler of the Illumine, [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]], dispatched Crofyp's [[task force]] to the [[Unknown Regions]]' [[Rapacc system]]. Jixtus, who commanded the Kiljis to retrieve a [[The Magys's people|group]] of [[refugee]]s hiding on the [[planet]] [[Rapacc]], assigned [[Pathfinders (Navigators' Guild)|Pathfinder]] navigator [[Qilori]] to the ''Anvil''. During the Kilji cruisers' [[hyperspace]] journey to the Rapacc [[Star system|system]], the ''Hammer'' slowed down, prompting Qilori to stop Crofyp's war cruiser. After the [[picket cruiser]] appeared nearby, the navigator continued [[Pilot|navigating]] the ''Anvil'' until both cruisers arrived at their destination simultaneously.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

Following the [[warship]]s' arrival at the Rapacc system, Crofyp's task force was [[Attack on Rapacc|confronted]] by the [[Paccosh]]-[[Thief|commandeered]] [[Nikardun blockade frigate]] [[Aelos (frigate)|''Aelos'']]. Crofyp commanded the [[blockade frigate]]'s captain, [[Commander]] [[Uingali foar Marocsaa]], to move aside and let his forces retrieve the refugees; however, the Paccosh refused, and the general prepared a [[Space warfare|cross fire attack]] on the ''Aelos''. The ''Anvil'' moved past the [[frigate]] along its starboard, and the ''Hammer'' did likewise along the vessel's portside.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

===Thrawn's attack===
{{Quote|Unidentified ships, this is Senior Captain Thrawn of the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet warship ''Springhawk''. You stand near our prey. Move clear of the Nikardun blockade frigate ''Aelos'' or suffer the humiliation of becoming collateral damage.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Who are you to speak of prey? And who are you to give orders to warships of the Illumine?''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''I already gave you my name. Do you need me to repeat it?&raquo;<br />&laquo;Your name will die with you, unenlightened fool.|Senior Captain Thrawn and General Crofyp|translated=1|audio=Crofyp3.flac|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
[[File:Chaos-Rising-OOP-Art.png|thumb|right|300px|Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo (pictured) attacked Crofyp's forces, destroying the ''Anvil''.]]
Once the Kilji ships were in position, the [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]'' arrived at the outer system. The crew of the ''Springhawk'' codenamed the ''Anvil'' as "Cruiser One" and fired two ship-disabling [[plasma sphere]] weapons at Crofyp's flagship before joining the confrontation in the inner system. The [[Chiss]] commander [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," who believed that the ''Anvil'' and the ''Hammer'' were sister ships to the Kilji war cruiser ''[[Whetstone]]'', ordered Crofyp to move clear of the ''Aelos'', which Thrawn classified as his prey. The Kilji general, furious upon the senior captain's orders to move clear of the Paccosh frigate, had his vessels fire spectrum [[laser]] salvos at the Chiss [[heavy cruiser]] after the Kilji vessels turned to [[Unidentified maneuver (Nakirre)|face]] their new foe.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

As the Kilji and Chiss cruisers traded laser fire, the plasma spheres disabled the ''Anvil''{{'s}} starboard side, deactivating weapons and defenses. The vessel's Kilji crew attempted to reactivate the paralyzed weapons, but the ''Springhawk'' destroyed those weapons. Qilori told Crofyp to flee; however, the general remarked that they would not be fleeing, explaining that those enlightened to the [[Kilji way of order and enlightenment]] [[religion]] stood, fought, and prevailed. The Pathfinder dismissed the Kilji's claim and ran to the portside [[escape pod]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

Crofyp moved the ''Anvil'' to shield the ''Hammer'' while the picket cruiser fled and attempted to threaten his navigator with a large weapon for fleeing from battle; however, Qilori escaped before the general's flagship exploded after several [[breacher]] [[missile]]s slammed into its paralyzed side. Tildnis's picket cruiser fled to the Unknown Regions [[Sunrise system|"Sunrise" system]]. Qilori&mdash;who did not enjoy his time aboard the ''Anvil'' as he had endured the Kiljis' arrogance and their conversations about the Kilji religion&mdash;later reported to Jixtus that the Chiss and the Paccosh had destroyed the war cruiser.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Commanders and crew==
{{Quote|I am General Crofyp of the Kilji Illumine, move aside or be destroyed.|General Crofyp, to Commander Uingali foar Marocsaa|audio=Crofyp1.flac|translated=1|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
The ''Anvil'', which was commanded by Crofyp, was crewed by several [[Kilji vassal]]s, beings enlightened to the Kilji religious beliefs. The war cruiser's crew included: [[Vassal One (Anvil)|Vassal One]], a [[Weapons officer|weapons operator]]; [[Vassal Two (Anvil)|Vassal Two]], the [[Sensor officer|sensor operator]]; [[Vassal Three]], another weapons operator; and [[Vassal Four (Anvil)|Vassal Four]], the [[Communications officer|comm operator]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
