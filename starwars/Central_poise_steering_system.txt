The '''Central poise steering system''' was a steering system installed on [[speeder bike]]s. The [[S-57 Cardinal]] model of [[speeder]] bike had the system installed for standard.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] stole a [[EML-850 light freighter]] from [[Westhill Palace]] in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. A S57 Cardinal speeder bike had been stored in the [[light freighter]] by the time of the theft. After Vess [[pilot]]ed the stolen [[freighter]] to the [[moon]] [[Toshara]], she regularly used the speeder bike.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
Vess later paid to replace the central poise steering system with a [[CP-0 steering system]] and eventually an [[A-7 steering system]]<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The central poise steering system appeared as a base speeder upgrade in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
