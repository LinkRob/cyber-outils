'''Bix Creo''' was an [[Bix Creo's species|alien]] [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]]<ref name="Gamefront" /> who was active during the reign of [[Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire]] between [[130 ABY]] and [[138 ABY]].<ref name="Dating note">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2014/04/09/art-from-the-darth-maul-game-you-39-ll-never-get-to-play.aspx|text=Art From The Darth Maul Game You'll Never Get To Play|author=Andrew Reiner|date=2014-04-09|work=[[Wikipedia:Game Informer|''Game Informer'']]|archivedate=20240223064541|nolive=1}} &mdash; ''[[Battle of the Sith Lords]]'' would have taken place during the reign of [[Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire]]. [[Legacy (2006) 1|''Legacy'' (2006) 1]] states that [[Darth Krayt|Krayt's]] Empire was founded in [[130 ABY]]. The Empire was dissolved in [[138 ABY]], as depicted in [[Legacy—War 6|''Legacy—War'' 6]]. Therefore, Bix Creo must have been active between those two dates.</ref> Creo had [[Color/Legends|brown-gray]] skin, black [[Eye/Legends|eyes]], and black facial [[Hair/Legends|hair]]. Black [[Tattoo/Legends|tattoos]] were also featured on the Jedi's [[Limb/Legends|forearms]] and head.<ref name="Gamefront" />

Bix Creo was [[Clothing/Legends|clothed]] in silver-and-gold [[Armor/Legends|armor]] with red markings that covered the majority of the Jedi's body. Creo wielded a green [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]].<ref name="Gamefront" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bix Creo is a character created for [[Red Fly Studio]]'s canceled [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Battle of the Sith Lords]]''. Creo was one of several Jedi adversaries, including [[Nexus Wan]] and [[Gareth Lorca]], whom [[Maul's descendant]] would have encountered throughout the game. Creo's appearance was designed by concept artist [[Thomas A. Szakolczay]]<ref name="Gamefront" /> at some point prior to the game's cancellation in [[2011]].<ref name="GI subscription article">{{WebCite|url=http://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2014/05/07/the-story-of-the-darth-maul-game-that-never-came-to-be.aspx?PostPageIndex=1|text=The Story Of The Darth Maul Game That Never Came To Be|author=Reiner, Andrew|date=2014-05-07|archivedate=20160219184903|nolive=1}}</ref>
