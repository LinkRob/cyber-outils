The '''classical architectural Hutt style''' was favored by the [[Hutt]]s at the time when they ruled the [[planet]] [[Mataou]], long before the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Many luxurious settlements built in that style could be found on Mataou,<ref name="SWUweb">{{WebCite|url=https://playstarwarsuprising.com/game-guide/introduction/#locations|text=Locations|work=Star Wars: Uprising Official Website|archivedate=20160624013822}}</ref> including the [[Gnuda Kajidic ancestral estate|estate]] of the [[Gnuda]] [[Kajidic]]. The classical architectural Hutt style was characterized by terraced buildings, with a predominance of off-white hues and ornamental red elements.<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> On [[Jakku]], the Hutt-founded settlement of [[Niima Outpost]]<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> had an entrance gateway in that style as well,<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> and it was the town's sole example of architectural pride.<ref name="VIInovel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref> Similar Hutt style arches could also be seen on [[Takobo]].<ref name="The Future of the Force">{{Rebels|The Future of the Force}}</ref>
