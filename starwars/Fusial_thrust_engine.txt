The ''' fusial thrust engine''' was a type of [[engine]] used on ships such as [[X-wing starfighter]] models. [[Incom Corporation]]'s [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|T-65B X-wing]] featured [[4L4 fusial thrust engine]]s or [[4j.4 fusial thrust engine]]s,<ref name="SWCT" /> and the [[Incom-FreiTek Corporation]] [[T-70 X-wing starfighter|T-70 X-wing]] featured [[5L5 fusial thrust engine]]s.<ref name="FA:ICS" /> The [[T-85 X-wing starfighter|T-85 X-wing]]s also had fusial thrust engines.<ref name="BtA" />

Besides X-wings, Incom's [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]] also had [[4J.7 fusial thrust engine]]s.<ref name="ROVD">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
