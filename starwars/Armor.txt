'''Armor''' was covering worn over the body that was designed to protect living beings and vehicles from weapons and other hazards related to combat and warfare. It was worn by many [[soldier]]s, [[bounty hunter]]s, and guards, as well as natural armor that certain [[species]] evolved into.

==Description==
===Overview===
[[File:DeathWatch-SWBC20.png|thumb|left|250px|Members of Death Watch wore Mandalorian battle armor.]]
The ancient warrior [[Mandalorian clan|clans]] of [[Mandalore]] wore [[Mandalorian armor|battle armor]] that was recognized and feared across [[the galaxy]]. The armor used [[beskar]] in its construction.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Heroes of Mandalore]]''</ref> Their helmets had a distinctive T-shaped visor. During the [[Clone Wars]], a new faction of [[Mandalorian]]s called [[Death Watch]] took up wearing the armor of the ancient Mandalorian warriors, as well as adopting their warrior culture.<ref name="DB Deathwatch">{{Databank|death-watch|Death Watch}}</ref> The [[Death]] Watch armor was [[Color|blue]] and grey, and typically came with a [[jetpack]] which had an integrated single-shot [[missile launcher]].<ref name="The Mandalore Plot">{{TCW|The Mandalore Plot}}</ref> Later, [[Mandalorian super commando]]s loyal to [[Maul]] wore armor of red and black. Some even modified their helmets with horns in honor of their leader.<ref name="DB Mandalorian Super Commando">{{Databank|mandalorian-super-commando|Mandalorian Super Commando}}</ref>

[[Bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] wore Mandalorian armor that was outfitted with various equipment, such as wrist blades, a jetpack, and a wrist-mounted [[flamethrower]].<ref name="Attack of the Clones">{{Film|II}}</ref> His [[Cloning|clone]] and adoptive son [[Boba Fett]] inherited his armor,<ref name="Chapter 14">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 14: The Tragedy}}</ref> also modifying it with various weapons, including a flamethrower gauntlet, a whipcord launcher, and concealed rocket-dart launchers in his kneepads.<ref name="DB Boba Fett bio">{{Databank|boba-fett-biography-gallery|Boba Fett Biography Gallery}}; slide 9 of 14</ref>

[[File:PhaseICloneTroopers-TROSPromoYT.png|thumb|right|250px|The armor worn by Republic clone troopers consisted of white plastoid plates.]]
The [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]] wore full body armor, which was made of [[plastoid]]. Their armor was predominantly white, although colors and markings were sometimes added to denote rank or distinction.<ref name="DB Clone Captain Rex">{{Databank|clone-captain-rex|Clone Captain Rex}}</ref> Clone [[trooper]] helmets could also have two miniature flashlights on each side of the head to provide forward-facing illumination for low-light environments.<ref name="Legacy of Terror">{{TCW|Legacy of Terror}}</ref> [[Clone commando]]s wore armor that was similar to the standard [[clone trooper armor]].<ref name="Missing in Action">{{TCW|Missing in Action}}</ref> Some clones wore armor that was outfitted for underwater combat. These troopers were used during the civil war on [[Mon Cala]]mari.<ref name="Water War">{{TCW|Water War}}</ref>

[[File:Stormtroopers_are_all_wet.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Like their clone predecessors, Imperial stormtroopers were outfitted with white plastoid body armor.]]
The [[stormtrooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]] also wore [[Stormtrooper armor|full white body armor]]. Their armor contained a wide range of equipment enabling them to survive in almost any environment. Other stormtrooper units such as [[scout trooper]]s and snowtroopers wore specialized armor suited to their environments.<ref name="DB Stormtroopers">{{Databank|stormtroopers|Stormtroopers}}</ref> [[Death trooper]]s, a stormtrooper variant specialized in stealth, espionage and lethality, wore black armor and helmets that included voice scramblers and advanced sensors technology. The [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] of the [[First Order]] also wore [[First Order stormtrooper armor|white armor]], which was manufactured by the [[First Order Department of Military Research]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars NE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>

In addition to [[soldier]]s and [[bounty]] hunters, armor was also worn by official guards and [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]]. The [[Senate Guard]]s and [[Senate Commando]]s, charged with protecting the [[Galactic Senate]], wore a distinctive blue armor.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness">{{TCW|Cloak of Darkness}}</ref> The Onderon guards who protected the royal family on [[Onderon]] wore grey armor and helmets.<ref name="The Soft War">{{TCW|The Soft War}}</ref> The [[Mandalorian Guard]]s, elite [[bodyguard]]s charged with protecting [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]], wore body armor including a helmet and a large shield.<ref name="DB Mandalorian Guard">{{Databank|mandalorian-guard|Mandalorian Guard}}</ref> The [[Coruscant underworld police]] wore armor made of leather and metal. Mechanical eyes protruding from their helmets caused many to wonder if they were [[droid]]s, [[humanoid]]s, or something in between.<ref name="DB Coruscant Underworld Police">{{Databank|coruscant-underworld-police|Coruscant Underworld Police}}</ref>

[[File:Darth-Vader-Vader2021-14.png|thumb|right|200px|Darth Vader's armor contained life support technology in order to keep him alive.]]
One individual who wore armor as a form of life support was the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. After a [[Duel on Mustafar|fierce battle]] with his former friend and [[Jedi Master]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], Vader was left severely injured and burned by the flames of [[Mustafar]]. He was rebuilt with [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] limbs and a frightening suit of [[Darth Vader's armor|black armor]], which he was forced to wear for the rest of his life. The armor kept him alive with the help of mechanical lungs, which made an ominous sound whenever he breathed.<ref name="DB Darth Vader">{{Databank|darth-vader|Darth Vader}}</ref>

Jedi wore armors, particularly in war period such as the Clone War.<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" /> The [[Jedi Temple Guard]] wore [[Jedi Temple Guard armor|armor]] and robing that covered their features.<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> At some point during the High Republic era, the Jedi [[Avar Kriss]] wore full helmless armor.<ref name="Kylo 3">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 3|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 3]]</ref> Kriss and the rest of the [[Luminous Nine]] donned suits of [[Luminous Nine's armor|custom armor]]<ref name="TotJ">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref>&mdash;which was similar in appearance<ref name="MichaelSig">{{Bluesky|username=msiglain.bsky.social‬|url=post/3ln2uoqpbic22|name=[[Michael Siglain]]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/tNO2k}}</ref> to Temple Guard gear<ref name="Sabotage" />&mdash;to protect against the [[Shrikarai]] and [[Blight]].<ref name="TotJ" /> Beyond Vader's armor,<ref name="DB Darth Vader" /> armor used by members of the [[Sith]] Order included the [[Ancient Sith Armor]], a type of gear worn by<ref name="Destiny">{{Destiny|set=Covert Missions|cardname=Ancient Sith Armor|link=https://swdestinydb.com/card/12015}}</ref> the legendary<ref name="Dark Side">''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side]]''</ref> Sith Lord [[Darth Bane]].<ref name="Destiny" /> The simply named [[Sith armor]] also existed in the galaxy.<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>

===Natural armor===
Some [[species]] had a naturally thick hide or scales that provided them with inherent protection. This was the case in exceptionally large beasts, such as the [[rancor]], whose armored skin could turn aside [[blaster]] bolts.<ref name="DB rancor">{{Databank|rancor|Rancor}}</ref> The colossal [[Zillo Beast]] had scales so tough that it was actually able to deflect a [[lightsaber]]. [[Unidentified Zillo Beast|One such member of this rare species]]' attributes fascinated [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], who was very keen on examining it further.<ref name="DB Zillo Beast">{{Databank|zillo-beast|Zillo Beast}}</ref> He stopped at nothing in his experiments on the creature in hopes that it would yield a new type of strong armor for the troops, starships and vehicles of the Galactic Republic.<ref name="The Zillo Beast Strikes Back">{{TCW|The Zillo Beast Strikes Back}}</ref>

===Vehicles and starships===
The term "armor" could also refer to the armor plating of a vehicle or the armored [[hull]] of a [[starship]]. Generally, vehicles and starships built for combat were fortified with the heaviest armor.

[[File:Stolen_AAT_Horian.png|left|thumb|253x253px|The Armored Assault Tank was a heavily armored tank used by the [[Trade Federation]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]].]]
Heavily armored vehicles included the [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|AAT battle tank]], which was a floating armored assault tank.<ref name="DB AAT">{{Databank|aat-battle-tank|AAT Battle Tank}}</ref> The [[Multi-Troop Transport]] (MTT) was a large armored vehicle that was able to transport and deploy over a hundred [[battle droid]]s.<ref name="DB MTT">{{Databank|mtt|MTT}}</ref> The largest battle tank of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] was the [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer|AT-TE walker]], which consisted of two armored halves. Small-arms fire was not enough to penetrate its armor, requiring heavier weapons such as tanksmasher [[rocket]]s.<ref name="DB AT-TE">{{Databank|at-te-walker|AT-TE Walker}}</ref> The massive [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] (AT-AT) used by Imperial ground forces had armor plating that was impervious to blaster fire.<ref name="DB AT-AT">{{Databank|at-at-walker|AT-AT Walker (All Terrain Armored Transport)}}</ref> The [[Umbaran mobile heavy cannon|mobile heavy cannon]] walker tank used by the [[Umbaran]] soldiers had armor that was impervious to heavy weapons, including anti-armor infantry rockets.<ref name="Databank Umbaran MHC">{{Databank|umbaran-mhc|Umbaran MHC}}</ref> During the [[Battle of Umbara]], the attacking clone troopers realized that the only way to destroy these massive tanks was to use advanced Umbaran weapons against them, which they did by commandeering [[Zenuas 33 Umbaran starfighter]]s.<ref name="The General">{{TCW|The General (episode)}}</ref>

Heavily armored starships included the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|Republic attack cruiser]], one of the Republic's largest capital ships.<ref name="DB Republic attack cruiser">{{Databank|republic-attack-cruiser|Republic Attack Cruiser}}</ref> An unusual combat ship was the [[Trident-class assault ship|''Trident''-class assault ship]]. It had a powerful drill specifically designed to penetrate the armored hull of starships or bunkers, and then release a boarding party of troops.<ref name="DB Trident">{{Databank|trident-drill-assault-craft|Trident Drill Assault Craft}}</ref>

Heavily armored [[starfighter]]s included the [[HH-87 Starhopper]], a fighter used by the [[Hutt]]s.<ref name="Databank HH-87 Starhopper">{{Databank|hh-87-starhopper|HH-87 Starhopper}}</ref> The [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]] had a sturdy combination of [[Deflector shield|shields]] and armor, enabling it to make attack runs on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Databank Y-wing Starfighter BG">{{Databank|y-wing-starfighter-history-gallery|title=Y-wing Starfighter History Gallery}}; image #10 {{C|of 12}}</ref>
