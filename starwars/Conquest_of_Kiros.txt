The '''conquest of Kiros'''<ref name="Darts Databank" /> was an invasion launched during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Slaves of the Republic">{{TCW|Slaves of the Republic}}</ref> in [[20 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Marching into the pacifist [[Artisan colony]] of [[Togruta]]s on the [[planet]] [[Kiros]], the Confederacy's [[B1-series battle droid]] rounded up all members of the population on the order of [[Zygerrian]] [[Darts D'Nar]].<ref name="Kidnapped" /> The Togrutas were then taken to Zygerrian empire<ref name="Slaves of the Republic" /> by Zygerrian [[enslaver]]s.<ref name="Cody Bio Gallery" /> The conquest came as part of the Separatists' alliance with the rebuilding [[Zygerrian Slave Empire]],<ref name="Kidnapped" /> which the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] hoped to restore in preparation for his coming [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Escape">{{TCW|Escape from Kadavo}}</ref> Aware of the Separatist incursion, the [[212th Attack Battalion]] and [[501st Legion]] of the [[Republic Military]] were then dispatched to [[Battle on Kiros|investigate]] the lack of communications from Kiros.<ref name="Kidnapped" />

{{Battle-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The conquest of Kiros first appeared within "[[Kidnapped]]"<ref name="Kidnapped" /> and was identified through Darts D'Nar's entry on the [[Encyclopedia (StarWars.com)|StarWars.com Encyclopedia]]. However, both it<ref name="Darts SWE">{{SWE|characters|dartsdnar|Darts D'Nar}}</ref> and its later transfer to the [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] misspell Kiros as "Kiro."<ref name="Darts Databank" />
