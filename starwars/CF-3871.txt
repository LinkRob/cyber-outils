'''CF-3871''' was a [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] who served under [[Lieutenant]] [[Wulfgar Kath]]. When Kath was deployed to [[Batuu]] to find the [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]], CF-3871 was one of the [[trooper]]s who [[Wulfgar Kath's squad|accompanied]] him. CF-3871 and [[CF-3363]] confronted the [[smuggler]] [[Zade Kalliday]] in an alley, but were shot [[Death|dead]] by Moradi.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" />
