'''Cietra''' was an individual who lived on the [[moon]] [[Raada]] in a house later claimed by [[Ahsoka Tano]] when she arrived there. She moved out of the house after getting [[Marriage|married]]. Tano found the house in such a mess that she assumed Cietra couldn't have been much of a cleaner.<ref name="Ahsoka" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cietra was first mentioned in the 2016 novel [[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']].<ref name="Ahsoka" />
