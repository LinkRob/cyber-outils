The '''FD4 fighting droid''' was a model<ref name="Kids">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Droid in Star Wars|video=UQg6aX7lqTA|nolive=1}}</ref> of [[gladiator droid]] with a [[Color|brown]] cylindrical body topped by a disc shaped head with a large black [[sensor]].<ref name="Solo" /> A modified<ref name="DB">{{Databank|wg-22|WG-22}}</ref> FD4 fighting [[droid]] was made to fight in the droid arena of [[the Lodge]] on the [[planet]] [[Vandor]]. In [[10 BBY]], it was set to fight against the [[FD3-MN fighting droid]] [[FD3-MN]], but the match was interrupted by the droid [[L3-37]] who tried to convince the FD4 not to fight while the arena manager [[Ralakili]] shouted at her.<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
A FD4 fighting droid first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''. The droid was not named in the film,<ref name="Solo" /> but was identified in the video "Every Droid in Star Wars", which was posted on ''[[StarWarsKids.com]]'' in [[2019]].<ref name="Kids" />
