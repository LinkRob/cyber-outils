'''Capital City Casino''' was a [[casino]] located in [[Capital City]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] had a pet [[puffer pig]] named [[Petunia]] with a bad nose that couldn't sniff out [[mineral]]s. Lando traveled to the casino to find an easy mark to trick into buying the [[pig]] for a lot of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Legends" />
