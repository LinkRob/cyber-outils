{{Quote|There you would be, asleep in your house, or maybe taking a shower, or watching a holovid and&mdash;''squish-squash!''&mdash;flat as a bantha-butter pancake you would be. You can see how this would spoil a pirate's day.|Hondo Ohnaka|<ref name="Pirate's Price" />}}
'''Bantha-butter pancakes''' were a type of [[pancake]] made from [[bantha butter]].<ref name="Pirate's Price" /> Bantha butter was also known as [[Color|blue]] [[butter]] for its hue and was created with the [[Blue milk|milk]] of [[bantha]]s,<ref name="Galactic Baking">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Baking]]''</ref> [[mammal]]s that were [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Bantha DB">{{Databank|bantha|Bantha}}</ref> The [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] once compared the result of a [[turlossus]] stepping on something with its gigantic [[foot]] to being as flat as a bantha-butter pancake, a situation he witnessed on the [[moon]] [[Dhandu 3]] when two [[Pakiphantos]] in pursuit of him were flattened.<ref name="Pirate's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bantha-butter pancakes were first mentioned in the [[canon]] junior novel ''[[Pirate's Price]]'', which was written by [[Lou Anders]] and published in [[2019]].<ref name="Pirate's Price" />
