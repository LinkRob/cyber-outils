The term '''army''' or '''ground force''' was a military service the operated on a [[Planet/Legends|planet]]’s surface. Armies could range from from the war-bands of a few hundred [[Soldier/Legends|soldiers]] used by the clans and tribes of stone-technology-level worlds like [[Endor/Legends|Endor]] and [[Dathomir/Legends|Dathomir]], to a galaxy-spanning war-machine with billions of [[Troop/Legends|troops]]. The term "army" both referred to the ground surface but also could refer to a field army, such as an [[Army (Imperial Army formation)|army]] within the [[Imperial Army/Legends|Imperial Army]]. An Imperial army was commanded by a full [[General/Legends|General]], and consisting of a nominal total of 193,644 front-line soldiers in an establishment strength of 293,686 personnel. An average [[Sector/Legends|Sector]] apparently contained four such [[Surface Army|surface armies]], which served as the main, planetary-assault, and surface-warfare force for the [[Moff/Legends|Moff's]] regime; along with a number of garrisons built around [[Corps/Legends#Corps HQs|Corps HQs]], that made up the [[Systems Army/Legends|Systems Army]].

Until the last years of the Empire, an army was rarely deployed as a single formation on a single planet, but it was designed to operate in concert with a standard troop squadron of the [[Imperial Navy/Legends|Imperial Navy]], and deployment of the entire formation together began to be considered as the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebellion]] grew in force after the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]]. Usually an army would deploy subordinate units to specific battlefields or theaters: its primary subdivisions were four [[Corps/Legends|Corps]], any one of which was considered sufficient to retake a rebellious world.

==[[Imperial Army/Legends|Imperial Army]] organization==
{{Main|Army (Imperial Army formation)}}
[[File:Army.jpg|thumb|left|250px]]
An [[Army/Legends|army]] was commanded by a full [[General/Legends|general]]. The headquarters staff and support element of an army was larger than two full [[Battalion/Legends|battalions]] for a total of 1,855 soldiers and twice as many [[Droid/Legends|droids]]. It had five staff officers (SA1 to SA5) and ten substaff officers (SA11 through SA52), a security company (152 soldiers) and six independent perimeter platoons (total 228 soldiers), 200 [[Imperial Security Bureau/Legends|ISB]] agents for intelligence and counter-intelligence, three companies of [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order/Legends|COMPNOR]] "observers" (each 152 personnel plus a command element of five for a total of 471), four [[Imperial Intelligence/Legends|Imperial Intelligence]] representatives and 784 support personnel.<ref name="ISB2">[[Imperial Sourcebook (Second Edition)|''Imperial Sourcebook'', Second Edition]]</ref>

An army was composed of one of each type of Imperial Army [[Corps/Legends|corps]] and was meant to fit into an Imperial Navy [[troop squadron]]. That gave an army 193,644 troopers, 100,042 support personnel, 16,660 repulsorcraft and at least 3,498 heavy [[Tank/Legends|tanks]]. [[Imperial Army/Legends#Augmentation|Augmented]] armies doubled the number of each corps type starting with [[line corps]]. While an army rarely fought as a coordinated field unit, in the latter years of the Empire the increasing success of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebellion]] forced the Imperial Army to revive and redevelop this doctrine.<ref name="ISB2" />
