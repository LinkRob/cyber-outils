'''Beolars Bribbs''' was the [[Chief Executive Officer]] of [[SoroSuub Corporation]] and represented the [[planet]] [[Sullust]] as a [[senator]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. During the [[Separatist Crisis]], he oversaw the planet's secession from the Republic and its incorporation into the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Scarif" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Beolars was mentioned in the English release of "[[Scarif and Other Planets in the Outer Rim]]," an entry in [[De Agostini]]'s [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series<ref name="Scarif" /> that was originally released in Spanish on [[July 20]], [[2021]].<ref name="De Agostini">{{DeAgostini|url=libros-comics/enciclopedia-star-wars|text=Enciclopedia Star Wars}}</ref> The [[Beolars Bribbs/Legends|character]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where he first appeared in [[HoloNet News Vol. 531 46|''HoloNet News Vol. 531'' #46]], an issue of the [[HoloNet News (original website)|representation]] of the [[in-universe]] HoloNet News<ref name="HNN">{{HoloNetNewsWeb|46|regional/1337_1.html}}</ref> written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[Paul Ens]]<ref name="Credits">{{HoloNetNewsWeb||credits.html|Credits}}</ref> and published on the HoloNet News website on [[March 7]], [[2002]].<ref name="HNN47 date">Per {{JCFCite|url=posts/6042498|board=Literature|thread=The Official HoloNetNews Discussion Thread|user=IAmTheDarkSide|date=2002-03-14|time=5:40 PM|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20220914072556/https://boards.theforce.net/threads/the-official-holonetnews-discussion-thread.5990764/page-4#post-6042498}}, [[HoloNet News Vol. 531 47|''HoloNet News Vol. 531'' #47]] was released on [[March 14]], [[2002]]. As [[HoloNet News (original website)|HoloNet News]] segments were published weekly, and {{HoloNetNewsWeb|46|regional/1337_1.html}} was published in the issue immediately preceding the forty-seventh edition, it must have been released on [[March 7]] of the same year.</ref>
