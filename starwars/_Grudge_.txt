'''"Grudge"''' was the [[callsign]] of a hateful<ref name="Grudge" /> [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> who served as a [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|pilot]] in the [[First Order]]'s [[First Order starfighter corps|starfighter corps]].<ref name="Grudge" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timeslines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Allegiance 1|''Allegiance'' 1]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the First Order and the [[Resistance]], the First Order traced a Resistance transmission to the [[Temperature|ice]] [[planet]] [[Tah'Nuhna]]. In response, [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] ordered the [[Destruction of Tah'Nuhna|destruction]] of the planet.<ref name="Allegiance">[[Allegiance 1|''Allegiance'' 1]]</ref> "Grudge" flew a [[TIE/se Bomber]] alongside three other [[bomber]]s during the conflict, [[bomb]]ing a [[city]].<ref name="Grudge" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Grudge" premiered in ''[[Fury of the First Order Squadron Pack]]'',<ref name="Grudge" /> an expansion for [[Atomic Mass Games]]'s ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition]]'' miniatures game that released on [[September 24]], [[2021]],<ref name="Release">{{WebCite|url=https://www.wargamer.com/star-wars-x-wing/fury-of-the-first-order-release-date|text=Star Wars: X-Wing's Fury of the First Order and Resistance Y-Wings revealed|author=Callum Bains|date=2021-07-28|work=[[Wikipedia:Wargamer (website)|''Wargamer'']]|archivedate=20230929133137}}</ref> which included a card representing "Grudge."<ref name="Grudge" /> The pilot was originally previewed in a tweet by Atomic Mass Games on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] earlier in the year on [[July 26]].<ref name="Tweet">{{Twitter|username=atomicmassgames|url=status/1419749398423253001|name=[[Atomic Mass Games]]|date=2021-07-26|quote=The TIE/se Bomber brings a new facet of gameplay to the First Order Faction – devices! With the new Feedback Ping upgrade card and pilots like "Grudge" you can set up punishing bombing runs that help your other bombers finish the job.|archivedate=20210726200021|archivefile=File:AtomicMassGames-Grudge-Twitter.png}}</ref>
