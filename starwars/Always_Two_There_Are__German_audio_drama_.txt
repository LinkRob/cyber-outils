"'''Always Two There Are'''" ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]]: "'''Doppelte Gefahr'''") is an episode of the [[German Rebels audio dramas]] adapting the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Always Two There Are]]". It was released on [[September 9]], [[2016]] by [[Universal Music Group]] as part of the album [[Star Wars Rebels Episode 9 - Always Two There Are / Brothers of the Broken Horn]]. The episode was written by [[Gabriele Bingenheimer]], directed by [[Marian Szymczyk]] and includes narration by [[Hanns Jörg Krumpholz]].<ref name="Kiddinx" />
