The '''Ermiko family''' lived on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]] in the [[High Republic Era]]. Following the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders']] [[Occupation of Valo|occupation]] of their [[homeworld]]<ref name="EFV" /> in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. The novel establishes that the [[occupation of Valo]] began a year prior to its events, placing its start to [[230 BBY]].</ref> the [[Valon]] family's [[Unidentified Ermiko family son|son]] became sick. Nihil recruit and Valo native [[Driggit Parse]] [[Thief|stole]] a vial of medicine for the [[Gender|boy]] from the marauders, having only joined the occupiers so she could access supplies to help her local community.<ref name="EFV" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Parse reflected on her decision to join the Nihil&mdash;despite the judgment of her fellow Valons&mdash;and considered how she had helped the Ermiko family as one of the reasons it was worth it.<ref name="EFV" />
