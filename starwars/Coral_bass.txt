'''Coral bass''' were a type of [[Fish|bass]]<ref name="TOTF" /> that partially shared their name with [[coral]], stationary [[plant]]-like organisms.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> While in the [[San Tekka compound]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]<ref name="TOTF" /> in [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> coral bass was grilled by the [[San Tekka clan|San Tekka family's]] kitchens. The [[Grilled coral bass|dish]] was then served at a meal for [[Nihil]] [[General]] [[Abediah Viess]]&mdash;who had forcibly taken over the residence following the departure of its [[Gender|patriarchs]], [[Vellis San Tekka|Vellis]] and [[Marlowe San Tekka|Marlowe]]&mdash;and her [[Prisoner|captive]], the [[human]] [[Cair San Tekka]], a younger member of the family who engaged in anti-Nihil resistance activities. Though Cair was reluctant to dine with the Nihil, he [[Brain|mentally]] lamented that refusing the [[Foodstuff|food]] would not be a smart decision for his physical well-being.<ref name="TOTF" />
