'''Dis-remembering''' was an act used as a grave insult within the society of the [[Unidentified Bosph species|species]] that lived on the [[planet]] [[Bosph]]. In [[3 BBY]], the [[Imperial Navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Bombardment of Bosph|bombarded]] Bosph and caused effectively irreparable damage while attempting to commit an act of genocide on the [[species]], enacting an ongoing quarantine on those that survived. In response, the species dis-remembered the Galactic Empire, refusing to acknowledge that the state had ever existed.<ref name="Rise" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] encouraged readers in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' to seek out first hand accounts from the victims of the Empire's attrocities, but noted as an exception the inhabitants of Bosph, stating that their purposeful choice to dis-remember those who had wronged them should be respected.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], dis-remembering was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" /> The act originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as [[disremembering]], where it was first mentioned in "[[Smugglers of the Outer Rim]]," an article written by [[Doug Shuler]] and published in the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 5|fifth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'' in [[February]] [[1995]].<ref name="SotOR">{{JournalCite|5|Smugglers of the Outer Rim}}</ref>
