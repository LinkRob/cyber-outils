{{Quote|Welcome to the Aces' Lounge. Here you'll find the best drinks, the best food, the best views. If you want it, ha, we got it.|[[Hype Fazon]]|<ref name="The High Tower">{{Resistance|The High Tower}}</ref>}}
The '''Aces' Lounge''' was a lounge in [[Doza Tower]], a luxurious section of the ''[[Colossus]]'' [[Supertanker fuel depot|refueling station]], formerly located on the [[planet]] [[Castilon]]. In the lounge, members of [[Ace Squadron]], a group of [[racer]]s who also flew defense for the station, and their crews were able to indulge in the cleaner quarters and upscale food options. The lounge was often served by [[Otoga-222 maintenance droid]]s.<ref name="Doza Tower DB" />
