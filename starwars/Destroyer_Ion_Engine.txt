'''Destroyer Ion Engines''' were a type of [[Sublight engine|sublight]] [[ion engine]]. 30 [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[First Order]] used Destroyer Ion Engines, powered by a single [[III-a1a primary hypermatter-annihilation reactor|hypermatter-annihilation reactor]],<ref name="ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> as propulsion for their [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]].<ref name="SotG">''[[Star Wars: Ships of the Galaxy]]''</ref>
