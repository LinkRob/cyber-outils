'''Boba Fett's gaderffii stick'''<ref name="Objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref> was a gaderffii stick crafted and owned by [[Boba Fett]].<ref name="Chapter 2" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Flashback date" /> while on [[Tatooine]], Fett trained in the use of a gaderffii stick after he was held captive by a [[Unidentified Tusken Raider tribe|Tusken tribe]], eventually earning the right to wield one, which he crafted himself in the [[Gaderffii stick workshop]], with a branch of a [[Wortwood tree]] that he found in the desert, and with the help of a [[Unidentified Tusken Raider craftsman|craftsman]], who utilized [[Tusken Adze]].<ref name="Chapter 2" /> He carried this stick on his back in [[9 ABY]]<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoDate|9}}</ref> as [[Din Djarin]] left [[Freetown|Mos Pelgo]] following [[Mission to Tatooine (Din Djarin)|his exploits]] in aiding the townspeople.<ref name="Chapter 9">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref> Fett would later [[Battle of Tython (9 ABY)|wield]] the weapon on [[Tython]] against [[stormtrooper]]s with brutal efficiency, easily piercing through and even outright shattering their armor with it.<ref name="Chapter 14">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 14: The Tragedy}}</ref> Fett later attempted to utilize it while being [[Assassination attempt on Boba Fett|attacked]] by [[Krrsantan]].<ref name="Chapter 3">{{BOBF|Chapter 3: The Streets of Mos Espa}}</ref> Later, he used the weapon to kill [[Cad Bane]].<ref name="Chapter 7">{{BOBF|Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The stick first appeared in the [[Chapter 9: The Marshal|ninth]] episode of the series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', written and directed by [[Jon Favreau]].<ref name="Chapter 9">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref> [[Temuera Morrison]], who portrayed Boba Fett in the series, is fluent in the [[Wikipedia:Māori people|Māori]] art of stick fighting.<ref name="interview">{{SW|url=news/temuera-morrison-the-mandalorian-interview|text=Boba Fett is Back: Temuera Morrison on the Return of a ''Star Wars'' Icon}}</ref> He specifically asked props master [[Josh Roth]] to make a longer and more slender gaffi stick compared to the sticks from {{Film|IV}}. A thicker and wider stick, as was shown in the previous ''[[Star Wars]]'' films, did not lend itself to the choreography that Morrison wanted for his fight in "[[Chapter 14: The Tragedy]]".<ref name="Season 2 gallery">{{DisneyGallery|Disney Gallery: The Mandalorian: Making of Season Two|Making of Season Two}}</ref>
