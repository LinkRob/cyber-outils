The '''''Coruscant Dawn''''' was a [[starship]] owned by [[Lina Soh]], [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Soh used the vessel to travel to the [[Republic Fair]] on the [[planet]] [[Valo]], accompanied by her subordinates, several [[Jedi]], and the [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Rhil Dairo]]. It was boarded by [[Nihil]] raiders during the [[Valo Atrocity]], but the crew had managed to fight them off.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />
