'''Bonadan Star''' was a [[fathier]] who [[Fathier racing|raced]] at the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack]] on [[Cantonica]], a [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]], in [[34 ABY]]. During that [[Standard year|year]], they were owned by [[Pakkerd Racing]] and ridden by the [[jockey]] [[Turcotte Bel Mont]].

==Biography==
Bonadan Star was a [[fathier]] [[Reproduction|born]]<ref name="Rose Tico" /> around [[30 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> to the [[Gender|female]] fathier [[Farana's Folly]] and the male fathier [[Etti Express]].<ref name="Rose Tico" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the fathier was owned by [[Pakkerd Racing]] and had begun [[Fathier racing|racing]] at the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack]] in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Rose Tico" /> They shared their name with the [[Bonadan system]], which was located in the same [[Corporate Sector|sector]] of [[Realspace|space]] as Cantonica.<ref name="Sector">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Bonadan system]] and the [[Cantonica system]]</ref>

Prior to a race<ref name="Rose Tico" /> that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> a racing guide was published that listed Bonadan Star's owner, [[parent]]s, [[color]]ing, age, "G" classification, and that they had finished third, fifth, and third in their last three races. The guide also indicated that the [[jockey]] [[Turcotte Bel Mont]] would ride Bonadan Star starting at the third post. A copy of the racing guide was later collected by the [[Resistance]] [[Resistance technician|mechanic]] [[Rose Tico]], who added it to her [[Rose Tico's journal|journal]] while she was on a [[mission to Canto Bight]]<ref name="Rose Tico" /> in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> 

==Personality and traits==
Bonadan Star had brown [[Hair|fur]] with white markings and black [[eye]]s.<ref name="Rose Tico" />
