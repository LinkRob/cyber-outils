'''Farana's Folly''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[fathier]] that [[Reproduction|mated]] with the male fathier [[Etti Express]], resulting in the birth of the fathier [[Bonadan Star]]<ref name="Rose Tico" /> around [[31 ABY]]. By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The Last Jedi: Rose Tico: Resistance Fighter]]'' establishes that [[Bonadan Star]] is three [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of {{Film|VIII}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]], therefore meaning they must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[31 ABY]].</ref> Bonadan Star had participated in a [[Fathier racing|fathier race]] in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[casino]] [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. Prior to the race, a racing guide that listed Farana's Folly as Bonadan Star's [[Parent|dam]] was distributed; a copy of which was later collected by the [[Resistance]] [[Resistance technician|mechanic]] [[Rose Tico]] and added to her [[Rose Tico's journal|diary]] while she was on a [[mission to Canto Bight]]<ref name="Rose Tico" /> in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> The fathier shared their name with the [[Wild Space]] [[planet]] [[Farana]]<ref name="BMF 72">{{BuildFalconCite|72|Starship Fact File}}</ref> which was located near the [[Corporate Sector]]<ref name="Galaxy Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> that contained Cantonica.<ref name="Cantonica">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Cantonica system]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Farana's Folly was mentioned in the [[2018]] replica journal ''[[The Last Jedi: Rose Tico: Resistance Fighter]]'', which was written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Rose Tico" />
