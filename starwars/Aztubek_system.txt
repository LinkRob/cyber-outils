The '''Aztubek system'''<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> was located in the [[Javin sector]], a part of the [[Greater Javin]] area of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and the [[Western Reaches]]. It was situated in grid square K-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="OuterRim" /> and lay on the [[Super-hyperroute|hyperspace route]] known as the [[Corellian Trade Spine]], which linked it to the [[Javin system|Javin]] and [[Kumru system]]s.<ref name="BTM" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Aztubek [[Star system|system]] was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] via a mention in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 18|eighteenth issue]] of the [[De Agostini]] magazine ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'',<ref name="BTM" /> which was published around [[May 6]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMF18date">{{BMFdates|18}}</ref> It was then identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

The [[Aztubek system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2004]] [[Wizards of the Coast]] article "[[Galactic Gazetteer: Hoth and the Greater Javin]]," a ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' supplement published on [[Wizards.com]].<ref name="Galactic Gazetteer">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=starwars/article/sw20040325web|text=Galactic Gazetteer: Hoth and the Greater Javin|int=Galactic Gazetteer: Hoth and the Greater Javin}}</ref> It was subsequently identified by name in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
