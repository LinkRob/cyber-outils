The '''Flat Mountain of Yavin''' was, according to the [[Wild Space]] [[cartographer]] [[Emil Graf]], a [[mountain]] located on the [[Yavin 4|fourth]] [[moon]] of [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]]. It earned its name and distinctive shape in [[0 ABY]], when its summit was blasted off by an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] during the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[Evacuation of Yavin 4|evacuation]] of the moon.<ref name="FMOY" />
