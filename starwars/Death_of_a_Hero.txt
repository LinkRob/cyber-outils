"'''Death of a Hero'''" was a [[1989]] role-playing adventure written by [[Barry Osser]].

{{Game-stub}}

==Continuity==
"Death of a Hero" was initially published in the fifth issue of the [[Fanon|fanzine]], ''NorthCoast Roleplaying'' before being released, in an edited format, in ''[[Space Gamer/Fantasy Gamer]]'' Volume 2, Number 1.<ref name="SGV2N1" /> The article was later referenced by [[Glen Allison]]'s ''[[Voyages SF]]'' article, "[[Twinkle, Twinkle Little Star, How I Wonder Where We Are]]".<ref name="VSF13">{{VoyagesCite|13|Twinkle, Twinkle Little Star, How I Wonder Where We Are}} {{C|source}} {{Un}}</ref>
