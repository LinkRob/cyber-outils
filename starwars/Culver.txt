'''Culvers'''<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman AD">{{Andor|Ever Been to Ghorman?}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> were a type of small [[bird]] found on the [[planet]] [[Ghorman]]. They had [[color|white]] feathers with patches of brown and black. They could puff out their necks and would sometimes eat food left behind by patrons at the [[Palmo Plaza]] in the [[capital]] [[city]] [[Palmo]].<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" />

==Behind the scenes==
Culvers first appeared in "[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]," the forth episode of ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''{{'s}} [[Andor Season 2|second season]],<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" /> which premiered on [[April 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref> Culvers where identifed in the episode's [[Wikipedia:English language|English language]] audio descriptions released on [[Disney+]].<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman AD" />
