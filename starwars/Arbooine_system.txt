The '''Arbooine system''' was located in the [[Arboo sector]], a part of the [[freestanding subsectors]] portion of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]]<ref name="Slice" /> in grid square T-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It contained the terrestrial celestial body [[Arbooine]], the surface of which featured [[forest]]s and was home to the [[Poison|venomous]] [[bearsloth]] [[Tree|arboreal]] [[Diet|predator]].<ref name="AA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Arbooine [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

The [[Star system/Legends|star system]] containing the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Arbooine/Legends|Arbooine]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as the [[Arboo system]]. It made its first appearance and was named in ''[[Chronicles of the Gatekeeper]]'', a [[2015]] adventure published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for use with the ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Chronicles of the Gatekeeper]]''</ref> Prior to the adventure's publication on [[November 5]],<ref name="Take Hold of the Future">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2015/11/5/take-hold-of-the-future|text=Take Hold of the Future}}</ref> Arbooine was first pictured in an article previewing the product that was published on [[August 18]], 2015 on Fantasy Flight Games' [https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/ official website].<ref name="Preview">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2015/8/18/in-the-footsteps-of-a-master|text=In the Footsteps of a Master}}</ref> In ''Chronicles of the Gatekeeper'', Arbooine was depicted in several illustrations, including ones by<ref name="Chronicles" /> [[Tiffany Turrill]]<ref name="Chronicles" /><ref name="Tarvix">{{WebCite|url=https://tiffanyturrill.com/games/2i2ja51ioxts0w6qcbndm5hci3xio3|text=Games|work=[https://tiffanyturrill.com/ Tiffany Turrill Illustration]|archiveurl=https://archive.md/v1Qey}}</ref><ref name="Bearsloth">{{WebCite|url=https://tiffanyturrill.com/games/s9kaxmraavpks3le479zuh0ovcsn58|text=Games|work=[https://tiffanyturrill.com/ Tiffany Turrill Illustration]|archiveurl=https://archive.md/CCiQa}}</ref> and [[Ben Zweifel]].<ref name="Chronicles" /><ref name="Quolas">{{Twitter|pixelgarbage|status/586634196849979392|[[Ben Zweifel]]|quote=A chapter plate I did for Chronicle of the Gatekeeper!|archiveurl=https://archive.is/I5mWy}}</ref><ref name="Pillar">{{Twitter|pixelgarbage|status/637286754362507264|[[Ben Zweifel]]|quote=Here’s a closer look at the Pillar of the Ancestors I did for Chronicles of the Gatekeeper|archiveurl=https://archive.is/bwbQx}}</ref>
