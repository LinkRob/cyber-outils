"'''Cynabar's Droid Datalog'''" was a recurring article series published in the ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]s'' published by [[West End Games]]. It detailed several [[Droid/Legends|Droid]]s through information posted on [[Cynabar's InfoNet/Legends|Cynabar's InfoNet]].

==[[Star Wars Adventure Journal 13|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 13]]==
This article detailed several [[Class one droid/Legends|First Degree]] and [[Class two droid/Legends|Second Degree Droids]].
