'''Archduke''' was a title given to the public leader of [[Geonosis]],<ref name="Tarkin" /> though the position answered to [[Queen|monarch]] of Geonosis. Before and during the [[Clone Wars]], [[Poggle the Lesser]] held the title of Archduke, under Queen [[Karina the Great]],<ref name="Karina Databank">{{Databank|queen-karina-the-great|Queen Karina the Great}}</ref> until his [[Mission to Mustafar|death]] on [[Mustafar]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> After Poggle's [[death]], a [[Unidentified Geonosian archduke|new archduke]] was appointed by the [[Unidentified Geonosian queen (Stalgasin hive)|hive queen]].<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>
