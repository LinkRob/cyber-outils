'''Christmas''' was an [[Standard year/Legends|annual]] holiday celebrated by various [[Sentience/Legends|sentients]] throughout [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]]. In celebration of the holiday, sentients would often light Christmas [[Tree/Legends|trees]] and give gifts to each other. Between the preceding [[Christmas Eve]] and Christmas morning, [[Santa Claus/Legends|Santa Claus]] would travel around the entire galaxy to gift presents to [[Youngling/Legends|children]] who he deemed "good." Eventually, Santa became unable to deliver all the presents alone, so he enlisted the help of his son [[S. Claus]].<ref name="Stars" />
