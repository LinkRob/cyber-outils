The '''Battleworn Applicator'''<ref name="Hunters" /> was a long-range [[dart gun]]<ref name="Reveal" /> owned by the [[Rodian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Skora]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Underworld Applicator|standard applicator]]. It featured a glowing [[Color|pink]] core, bluish-green plating, light blue wiring, and white and pink straps wrapped around a basic black and silver frame.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Battleworn Applicator was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Skora's Underworld Applicator, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Battleworn Chemist]].<ref name="Hunters" />
