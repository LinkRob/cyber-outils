A [[human]] '''male''' lived on [[Level 3204]] of the [[Ecumenopolis|city-planet]] of [[Coruscant]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[Gender|man]] lived on the same level as their niece and nephew, [[Anandra Milon|Anandra]] and [[Santigo Milon]], and the two [[Youngling|childrens']] [[parent]]s, who owned an [[Milon family apartment|apartment]] on Level 3204.<ref name="OTLD" />

After the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] by the Empire<ref name="OTLD" /> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> [[Alderaan vigil rioting|riots]] broke out on Level 3204, which had a large population of immigrants from Alderaan, including the Milons. During the riots, [[Reffe]], a man who lived next door to Anandra's uncle, was [[Death|killed]] by a [[stormtrooper]] while ranting about Imperial corruption. When Anandra was later on the run from the Empire, she fought a middle-aged [[Unidentified stormtrooper (Level 1997)|stormtrooper]] with a scarred face that vaguely reminded her of her uncle.<ref name="OTLD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Anandra Milon's uncle was first mentioned in "[[One Thousand Levels Down]]," a short story written by [[Alexander Freed]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 151|''Star Wars Insider'' 151]]<ref name="OTLD" /> on [[July 22]], [[2014]].<ref name="Titan Magazines">{{TitanMagazines|url=t/star-wars-insider/us/151|text=Star Wars Insider #151}}</ref>
