The '''Battle of Yerbana''', also known as the '''Yerbana campaign''', was a battle that took place on the [[planet]] [[Yerbana]] during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]], in the [[19 BBY|final year]] of the [[Clone Wars]]. Under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Cody|CC-2224]] and [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], clone troopers of the [[212th Attack Battalion]] engaged the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance's]] [[Separatist Droid Army|droid forces]] on the planet, which resulted in significant Republic losses and their desperate need for additional reinforcements. As a result, Kenobi received support from the clone troopers of the [[501st Legion]] led by fellow Jedi General [[Anakin Skywalker]], which led to the destruction of the Separatist [[Unidentified super tactical droid (Yerbana)|commander]] alongside his forces and the Republic's capture of Yerbana.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Master, I mean no disrespect. If you want, I can hide here with you, and we can let the people in the city suffer longer.|Skywalker is sarcastic toward Kenobi for hiding behind cover|audio=Anakin-hide-here-with-you.flac|<ref name="Old Friends" />}}
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas" /> during the final [[Standard month|months]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the Galactic Republic and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the confederate government organized a major [[Outer Rim Sieges|offensive]] throughout several [[star system]]s across the [[Outer Rim Territories]] under the direction of the [[Kaleesh]] [[General]] [[Grievous]]. In response to the Confederacy's assaults, the Republic dispatched its [[Jedi General]]s far from the [[Core Worlds]] in order to assist their beleagured forces. As such, the [[clone trooper]]s of the [[501st Legion]] and [[212th Attack Battalion]], led by [[Jedi]] Generals [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], were ordered to the Outer Rim [[Planet|world]] of [[Yerbana]]<ref name="Old Friends" /> in the war's final [[Standard day|days]]<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> to engage the Separatist forces.<ref name="Old Friends" />

==The battle==
{{Quote|Well, I can see things are going splendidly on this front.|Jedi General Obi-Wan Kenobi, to [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Cody]]|audio=Kenobi-Splendidly-on-this-front.flac|<ref name="Old Friends" />}}
[[File:Legion-CodyWan.png|thumb|left|270px|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Commander Cody during the battle.]]
Following their arrival on Yerbana, the clone troopers of both the 501st Legion and 212th Attack Battalion were ordered to separate their forces to two fronts on the planet, with Kenobi's forces mounting an effort to liberate a [[Yerbana city|city]] controlled by the Separatist forces. However, as 212th [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Cody]] and his forces suffered significant losses and was thereby in desperate need of additional reinforcements, the Republic's [[Clone Z-95 starfighter|Clone Z-95 Headhunters]] and [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] gunships continued to engage the Confederacy's [[droid tri-fighter]]s in the air around Kenobi's front. After the 501st had managed to claim victory in the engagements on their front, Skywalker proceeded to assist Kenobi and 212th, who had struggled to advance through the Separatist forces on a bridge leading into the occupied city.<ref name="Old Friends" />

[[File:501st_Pilot.png|thumb|right|250px|501st Legion clone pilots provided air support during the engagement.]]
Forced into a standstill on the bridge as a result of a Separatist [[blockade]] of [[B1-series battle droid]]s, [[B2-series super battle droid]]s, [[DSD1 dwarf spider droid]]s, [[Octuptarra tri-droid]]s, [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I]]s, and [[NR-N99 Persuader-class droid enforcer|NR-N99 ''Persuader''-class droid enforcers]], Cody ordered his troopers to take cover as the Separatist forces fired a series of explosives on their position. However, as Cody was nearly killed by a projectile, alongside the destruction of an [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]], Kenobi arrived and saved the Clone Commander as he deflected the explosive with his [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber|lightsaber]]. With Kenobi on the battlefield, the Jedi General commanded his forces to once more take cover and was in turn informed by Cody of their inability to locate the Confederacy's [[Unidentified super tactical droid (Yerbana)|ST-series super tactical droid]] [[commander]] as a result of the battle droid's continued barrage. Despite their haltered advancements, however, Skywalker arrived on the bridge and proceeded to walk out into the open while the droids continued to fire upon the 212th, as the [[Jedi Knight]] evaded the shots fired on him.<ref name="Old Friends" />

[[File:YerbanaJetpackTroopers.png|thumb|left|270px|Clone jetpack troopers attack the Separatist forces on Yerbana.]]
While he walked further out on the bridge, an [[OOM command battle droid]] identified Skywalker and ordered the droid forces to divert their aim onto the Jedi Knight, but were offered with a feigned surrender from Skywalker. In his false surrender, the droid commander contacted the super tactical droid while Skywalker's [[astromech droid]], [[R2-D2]], informed [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex|CT-7567]], otherwise known as "Rex," and several [[clone jetpack trooper]]s of the Jedi Knight's progress. As the super tactical droid responded to his OOM command battle droid's summons, the droid commander realized that Skywalker was forcing them into a strategem and ordered his battle droids to commence fire. However, Skywalker was able to destroy the droid leader as he used [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|pull]] the droid leader to his [[Skywalker lightsaber|lightsaber]], while his clone troopers revealed themselves and began their attack on the Separatist blockade as they forced the battle droids to retreat with Kenobi's forces on approach from the bridge.<ref name="Old Friends" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|You capture Maul. I'll take care of Grievous. With any luck, this will all be over soon.''"<br />"''Master Kenobi always said there's no such thing as luck.''"<br />"''Good thing I taught you otherwise.''"<br />[Skywalker begins to leave]<br />"''Anakin! Good luck.|Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano's final words to each other as friends|<ref name="Old Friends" />}}
[[File:AhsokaTanoAndBoKatan.png|thumb|right|270px|Ahsoka Tano and Bo-Katan Kryze contact Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi.]]
As the battle was wrapping up,<ref name="Old Friends" /> ending in a Republic victory and granting it control over Yerbana,<ref name="TCW encyclopedia" /> the Jedi were called back to the ship after receiving a message from an individual using Anakin's subspace frequency "[[Fulcrum]]," who they believed to be [[Saw Gerrera]] requesting their assistance in the [[Siege at Onderon|siege]] at [[Onderon]]. However, the person using the frequency was actually Skywalker's former [[Padawan]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], and [[Bo-Katan Kryze]],<ref name="Old Friends" /> the sister of the [[Takeover of Mandalore|late]] [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]],<ref name="The Lawless">{{TCW|The Lawless}}</ref> seeking the assistance of the Republic and the Jedi to help defeat and capture the renegade [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]].<ref name="Old Friends" />

After meeting with Tano and Kryze onboard a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] and hearing their request, Kenobi insisted on waiting for the approval of the [[Jedi High Council]] before committing to the operation. Kryze told Tano she had known this would be a waste of time, but Skywalker had a surprise for his former apprentice, taking her to meet Rex and a [[332nd Company|unit]] of the 501st who had repainted their helmets after her facial markings in her honor once they knew she was coming back. The reunion was interrupted, however, by the [[klaxon]]s going off. When Kenobi arrived at the scene, Skywalker asked if the attack on Mandalore had been approved as the clone troopers ran off to prepare for battle, but the [[Jedi Master]] explained that Grievous had [[Battle of Coruscant|attacked]] [[Coruscant]] and the [[Supreme Chancellor]] was in danger.<ref name="Old Friends" />

Tano was upset, pointing out that the Jedi playing politics instead of helping people who really needed them was why the general public had lost faith in the Order. Skywalker proposed splitting the 501st and creating a [[332nd Division|new unit]] under Tano's command, but Kenobi pointed out that as she was no longer a member of the [[Republic Military]], she could not officially command anyone. Skywalker's solution to this was to promote Rex to [[Commander]] so he could lead the new unit, with Tano officially designated as his [[advisor]], which she was amenable to. As Kenobi, Skywalker and their forces headed for the [[capital]] of the Republic to stop Grievous, Tano, Rex, and Kryze set out for [[Mandalore]] with their forces, beginning a [[Siege of Mandalore|siege]] to draw Maul out of hiding.<ref name="Old Friends" />
