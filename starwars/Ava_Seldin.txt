'''Ava Seldin''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|Imperial]] [[Scientist/Legends|scientist]] during the time of the [[Galactic War]].

==Biography==
Around [[3641 BBY]], she was stationed on [[Belsavis/Legends|Belsavis]] where she formed a series of domination experiments that the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] conducted in order find out the strengths and weaknesses of various [[Alien/Legends|alien]] [[Species/Legends|species]]. While trying to recover the information, her guard attachment was attacked and wiped out by prisoners. Hoping that the Republic's research would give her invaluable insight into the capabilities of the studied alien species, she tasked an [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|Imperial]] spacer to retrieve the research data.
