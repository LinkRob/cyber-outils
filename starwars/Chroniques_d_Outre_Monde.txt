'''Chroniques d'Outre Monde''' was a French [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] magazine published from [[1986]] to [[1993]]. Of the authors who wrote for ''Chroniques d'Outre Monde'', only [[Pierre Zaplotny]] went on to have licensed French ''Star War''s material published by [[Jeux Descartes]].<ref name="JDP5">{{StoryCite|book=Jeux Descartes Plus 5|bformatted=''Jeux Descartes Plus'' 5|story=Aobys : Monde d'aventures}}</ref><ref name="D1">{{DescartesCite|1|Le crime dans Star Wars}} {{C|source}}</ref>
