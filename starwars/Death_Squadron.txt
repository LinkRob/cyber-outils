'''Death Squadron''' was a [[Navy|naval]] [[squadron]] of the [[Imperial Navy]] that served as the personal [[Fleet|armada]] of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the right-hand of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Following the [[Battle of Yavin]], the Death Squadron was led by the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Executor]]'', typically supported by five smaller [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]]. One of those support ships was the ''[[Devastator]]'', which had been the [[Lord]] Vader's [[flagship]] before the ''Executor'' and had participated in the [[Secret mission to Tatooine|capture]] of [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Deploy the fleet so that nothing gets off the system.|Lord Vader, to Admiral Piett|<ref name="Episode V" />}}
The [[Imperial Navy]]'s Death Squadron typically<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> consisted of one [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] as its [[command ship]], the ''[[Executor]]'',<ref name="SWE-Super Star Destroyer" /> as well as five<ref name="Battles" /> [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]],<ref name="Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> including the upgraded<ref name="BS">{{InsiderCite|issue1=149|issue2=150|Blade Squadron (short story)}}</ref><ref name="Conquest">{{SWCT|set=Star Wars Rebels: Blueprints|cardname=Star Destroyer}} identified the tractor beam targeting array the ''Conquest'' was seen to have in {{Film|IV}}. This was replaced by the [[communications array]] as seen in {{Film|V}} on the ''Conquest'', so the ''Conquest'' must have been upgraded.</ref> [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyers]]<ref name="A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[Conquest (Imperial-class)|''Conquest'']], ''[[Devastator]]'', and ''[[Ultimatum]]'',<ref name="Battles" /> as well as the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]]<ref name="Episode V" /> ''[[Avenger]]'', ''[[Stalker]]'', and ''[[Tyrant]]''.<ref name="Battles" />

[[File:ExecutorEscort-BTMF27.png|thumb|left|300px|Death Squadron was led by the ''Executor''.]]
As acting [[Commander-in-Chief]],<ref name="Bust Collection 30">{{BustCollectionCite|30|Star Wars Universe}}</ref> and [[supreme commander of the Imperial fleet]],<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] could requisition any Imperial ship to join his fleet, and could be attached such as the ''Stalker''.<ref name="Battles" /> The ''Devastator'' had been the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord's]] flagship before the ''Executor'', and had been refitted on multiple occasions to keep it up-to-date.<ref name="BS" /> Death Squadron also featured at least three [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruisers]]<ref name="War 5" /> and multiple [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|Imperial ''Gozanti''-class cruisers]].<ref name="Twilight" />

Death Squadron was under the command of Vader<ref name="Episode V" /> and [[Fleet Admiral]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> [[Firmus Piett]], who replaced [[Admiral]] [[Kendal Ozzel]] after the latter's execution. Other officers within the armada included Admiral [[Jhared Montferrat]],<ref name="Episode V" /> [[Commodore]] [[Rae Sloane]],<ref name="LVWSYN" /> and Star Destroyer [[captain]]s [[Canonhaus]], [[Lennox]], and [[Lorth Needa]].<ref name="Episode V" /> Death Squadron's ground forces included the [[1st Legion]] and the [[501st Legion]]. Although the Squadron had multiple [[general]]s, General [[Maximilian Veers]] commanded the 501st's [[Blizzard Force]] at [[Hoth]].<ref name="Hoth and DS2">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Battle of Hoth and the Second Death Star}}</ref>

==History==
===Initial attack on the rebellion===
{{Quote|At the least, we need more strength&mdash;your Death Squadron must be brought to bear immediately. It's ludicrous for us to keep it&mdash;|Admiral Ozzel, trying to persuade Lord Vader to use Death Squadron|<ref name="Shu-Torun Lives">{{StoryCite|story=Shu-Torun Lives|book=Star Wars (2015) 50}}</ref>}}
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Death Squadron was the personal [[fleet]] of [[Darth]] Vader. Following the [[Battle of Yavin]], where the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] lost its [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]] to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], the fleet's [[Commanding officer|leading officer]], [[Admiral]] [[Kendal Ozzel]], tried to convince the [[Lord]] Vader to bring Death Squadron to bear immediately to destroy the rebellion. However, his superior rejected this direct approach, agreeing with another of Death Squadron's [[Imperial officer|officers]], [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]], in that the Alliance was not suitable for such an extermination. Instead, Lord Vader used his [[agent]], [[Monarch of Shu-Torun|Queen]] [[Trios]], to [[Battle for Jedha|infiltrate]] the rebels' command structure, allowing her to provide the location of their own [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] as it finished completion.<ref name="Shu-Torun Lives" />

[[File:AssaultOnMakoTaBegins-2015StarWars50.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Death Squadron arrives at Mako-Ta]]
When Lord Vader's pawn, Queen Trios, informed him of the elusive [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet's]] location,<ref name="Star Wars 49">[[Star Wars (2015) 49|''Star Wars'' (2015) 49]]</ref> Death Squadron was sent to [[Battle of Mako-Ta|attack]] the [[Mako-Ta Space Docks]]. At first, the Sith Lord ordered the [[flotilla]]'s [[cruiser]] to hold their fire as the rebels attempted to ascertain their situation. The [[Squadron]] opened fired on the rebels' cruisers and started with General [[Vanden Huyck Willard|Vanden Willard's]] flagship, ''[[Yavin's Hope]]''.<ref name="Star Wars 50">[[Star Wars (2015) 50|''Star Wars'' (2015) 50]]</ref> The Squadron continued to attack and destroy several more of the Rebels' cruisers.<ref name="Star Wars 51">[[Star Wars (2015) 51|''Star Wars'' (2015) 51]]</ref>

Death Squadron then spotted the arrival of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' and sent in [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], along with the Dark Lord's personal [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced|advanced TIE]] to pursue it. The flotilla also destroyed several rebel [[escape pod]]s.<ref name="Star Wars 51" /> However, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] was able to get some codes from the armada's flagship, the ''Executor'', that were able to restore the systems aboard General [[Jan Dodonna]]'s [[Starship|ship]], the [[Republic (MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser)|''Republic'']].<ref name="Star Wars 54">[[Star Wars (2015) 54|''Star Wars'' (2015) 54]]</ref> The other rebel commanders were able to use the codes to jumpstart their cruisers and escape. The ''Republic'' was immobilized, but covered the retreating rebel fleet before it was destroyed with all hands.<ref name="Star Wars 55">[[Star Wars (2015) 55|''Star Wars'' (2015) 55]]</ref>

===Strike back at Hoth===
{{Quote|General, there's a fleet of Star Destroyers coming out of hyperspace in Sector Four.|a Rebel technician, to General Carlist Rieekan|<ref name="Episode V" />}}
[[File:TheHandOfGod-ESBHD.jpg|thumb|left|260px|Vader's fleet preparing to assault the rebel force at Hoth]]
While Death Squadron was in the [[Juris sector]],<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> a [[XJ9-CS14|probe droid]] found the Rebels' [[Echo Base|base]] in the [[Hoth system]]. Lord Vader ordered the Squadron to [[Hoth]]. However, erroneously thinking to surprise the rebels, his second in command ordered the fleet to emerge from [[hyperspace]] too close to the [[Star system|system]]. Lord Vader, angered by Admiral Ozzel's incompetence, [[Force choke]]d him to death and and promoted his junior, [[Captain]] [[Firmus Piett]], to Admiral as his replacement.<ref name="Episode V" /> As the ''Millennium Falcon'' fled Hoth and entered an asteroid field, Captain [[Lorth Needa]] deployed the TIE fighters of [[Theta Squadron (Galactic Empire)|Theta Squadron]] to patrol the area. Although the pilots disobeyed orders and entered the field, they did not face retribution for their actions after Captain Needa faced execution at the hands of Lord Vader's invisible grip for letting their quarry escape.<ref name="Kel's Rules">{{StoryCite|story=Amara Kel's Rules for TIE Pilot Survival (Probably)|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back|sformatted=Amara Kel's Rules for TIE Pilot Survival (Probably)}}</ref> While Death Squadron was chasing the ''Falcon'' through through the asteroid field, every ship suffered damage, and the ''Ultimatum'' was destroyed<ref name="Battles" /> along with another Star Destroyer.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

===Failure at Endor===
{{Quote|We're not going to attack?''"<br />"''I have my orders from the Emperor himself. He has something special planned for them. We only need to keep them from escaping.|Commander Gherant and Admiral Firmus Piett, regarding the ''Executor''{{'s}} role during the Battle of Endor|audio=WereNotGoingToAttack.flac|<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>}}
[[File:Avenger-ROTJ.png|thumb|right|250px|Star Destroyers of Death Squadron in defensive orbit of the second Death Star over Endor.]]
A large fleet, including Death Squadron and the [[Moddell sector fleet]] participated in the decisive [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="BMF 33">{{BuildFalconCite|33|Starship Fact File}}</ref> The rebellion, having located the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]], attempted to destroy it. However, it was anticipated that the rebels would attack, and Death Squadron executed an ambush once they arrived at the moon. Towards the end of the engagement, the ''Executor'' and Death Star II were destroyed and both [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and Lord Vader were killed.<ref name="Episode VI" />

==Behind the scenes==
Death Squadron first appeared in the [[1980]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="Episode V" /> It was first given its name in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on [[StarWars.com]]'s [[Encyclopedia (StarWars.com)|Encyclopedia]] entry for the [[Super Star Destroyer]].<ref name="SWE-Super Star Destroyer" />
