"'''Darth Vader vs. Hoth Rebels - Crushing the Rebellion'''" is the twenty-eighth episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]''. It premiered on the [[StarWarsKids.com]] website and YouTube on [[May 17]], [[2019]].<ref name="Crushing the Rebellion" /><ref name="Star Wars Kids">{{Disney|url=star-wars-kids|subdomain=lol|text=Take your first step into a larger world. Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures arrives here at Star Wars Kids on November 30!|date=2020-02-25}}</ref>

==Plot summary==
The episode opens with a [[Viper probe droid]] reporting the whereabouts of [[Echo Base]] to the [[Imperial Navy]]. Its transmission is picked up by an [[Imperial Navy Trooper/Legends|Imperial Navy trooper]] who alerts the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Executor]]''. An [[Imperial officer]] tells [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] that they have picked up something. Vader replies that the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] are there and orders [[Death Squadron]] to set a course for the [[Hoth system]]. Vader's fleet exits hyperspace above [[Hoth]].

As [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyers]] circle Hoth, Lord Vader descends to the planet in a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class shuttle]] to lead the forces of the [[Galactic Empire]] against the rebels. [[Cold weather assault trooper|Snowtroopers]] and [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT walkers]] advance across the battlefield. An AT-AT walker destroys Echo Base's [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] with its [[laser cannon]]s. Vader leads the Imperial ground assault forces to the gates of Echo Base.

He uses [[the Force]] to tear down the gates. Several [[Rebel trooper]]s have formed a stockade with crates and open fire on the Imperials. Lord Vader easily deflects their blasts with [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] and cuts down several rebels. Snowtroopers mount an [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]] and mow down more defenders. Other rebel troopers are killed by [[grenade]]s. As the stockade burns, Darth Vader surveys the destruction while snow troopers fan out across the base.
