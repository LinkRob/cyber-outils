The '''Destroyer Saber Design''' was a custom [[lightsaber]] [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]] owned by the [[droid]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[J-3DI]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Jedi Saber Design|standard lightsaber]]. It was designed in a way that was meant to invoke the aesthetics of the various [[Star Destroyer]]s deployed by the [[Galactic Empire]], including a small replica [[ISD-72x shield generator dome|shield generator dome]] attached to the bottom of the hilt.<ref name="SWH" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Destroyer Saber Design was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter J-3DI's Jedi Saber Design, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 5.0.0 alongside Season 5 on [[January 27]], [[2025]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Star Destroyer (J-3DI)|Star Destroyer]]. This wrap could be unlocked as one of two grand prizes within the "Jedi Destroyer Vault."<ref name="SWH" />
