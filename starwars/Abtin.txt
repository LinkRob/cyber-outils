'''Abtin''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Saber for Hire 1" /> in the [[Abtin system]] in the [[Danjar sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> When the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Expansion of the Stormwall|expanded]] the [[Stormwall]] barrier that surrounded their [[Occlusion Zone]] territory, Abtin was one of the worlds that fell into the Nihil's new borders. [[Kitrep Soh]], the son of [[Galactic Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor|chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]], and his boyfriend [[Jom Lariin]], who had been sightseeing on the Outer Rim prior to the Stormwall's initial [[Deployment of the stormseeds|formation]],<ref name="The Eye of Darkness">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]''</ref> visited Abtin, where they learned about its annexation by the Nihil.<ref name="Saber for Hire 1" />
