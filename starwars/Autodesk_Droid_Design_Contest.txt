[[File:AutodeskDroidDesignContest-PressMedia.jpg|250px|thumb|Autodesk Droid Design Contest reveal press media]]
The '''''Autodesk Droid Design Contest''''',<ref name="Reveal" /> also known as the '''''Autodesk Droid Maker Competition''''',<ref name="Grand Winner">{{YouTube|video=shorts/YWFe0oN-GKE|text=Star Wars #Droid Making Contest Winner|channel=autodesk|name=Autodesk|date=2024-09-04|nolive=1}}</ref> was a contest hosted by [[Wikipedia:Autodesk|Autodesk]] in collaboration with [[Lucasfilm]] to promote the [[2024]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''. In response to Special Creature Effects veteran [[Neal Scanlan]] utilizing Autodesk’s "[[Wikipedia:Autodesk Fusion|Fusion]]" software to bring the handheld [[droid]] [[Pip]] to life for the series, Autodesk promoted the contest,<ref name="Reveal" /> where entrants were required to utilize either Autodesk "Fusion" or "[[Wikipedia:Autodesk Maya|Maya]]" to design their droid. Four categories for winners were created, two "Mechanical" submitted as static models, and two "Animated" submitted as moving videos, split among professionals (designated [[Jedi Master]]s) and students 18 years or older (designated [[Padawan]]s).<ref name="Winners">{{WebCite|url=https://www.research.autodesk.com/blog/autodesks-droid-maker-contest-unleashing-the-force-of-creativity|work=[https://www.research.autodesk.com Autodesk Research]|text=Autodesk’s Droid Maker Contest: Unleashing the Force of Creativity|archivedate=20260528135421}}</ref>

Winners of the contest received a trip to and guided tour of the [[Lucasfilm]] headquarters at either the [[Wikipedia:Presidio of San Francisco|Presidio]] in [[Wikipedia:San Francisco|San Francisco]], [[Wikipedia:California|California]], or [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney Studios (Burbank)|The Walt Disney Studios Lot]] in [[Wikipedia:Burbank, California|Burbank]], California, as well as a meet-and-greet session with a Lucasfilm executive on site, a virtual meet-and-greet session with a Disney or Lucasfilm executive, special merchandise from ''The Acolyte'',<ref name="Reveal" /> and a chance to have their design transformed into a real life display model.<ref name="Winners" />

Hundreds of applications were received across the four categories, with winners revealed on [[June 26]], [[2024]]. The grand winner, Jedi Master Mechanical Droid Winner [[Tramaine Thornberry]], had his droid brought to life in one of Autodesk's Technology Centers, which has since been installed as a display at Lucasfilm HQ.<ref name="Grand Winner" />

To promote the competition, industry associate [[Adam Savage]] collaborated with Autodesk and [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]], being offered the opportunity to design his own droid and have it brought to life with the help of Autodesk Fusion, as well as [[Wikipedia:Computer-aided design|CAD]] and prop specialist Bill Doran.<ref name="Savage">{{YouTube|video=LwQ-ssVd0ic|text=Adam Savage Designs His Dream Star Wars-Inspired Droid!|channel=tested|name=[[Adam Savage]]|date=2024-05-16|archivedate=20260528175753}}</ref>

==Winners==
{{Quote|The Droid Maker contest is an exciting opportunity for Autodesk to team up with Lucasfilm, highlighting our commitment to innovation and the endless creativity of our designers and makers. At the Autodesk Technology Centers, we will be bringing the winning droids to life – connecting with the winning designers, learning about their inspirations, and sharing their excitement as we leverage our technology to fabricate their droids.|Joe Aronis, Manager of the Autodesk Technology Center, Boston|<ref name="Winners" />}}
'''Mechanical''':
*Jedi Master Mechanical Droid Winner: [[KT-11]] ([[TK-11 Mining Droid]]), by [[Tramaine Thornberry]]
*Padawan Mechanical Droid Winner: [[Scrapper (droid)|Scrapper]], by [[Jared Teiger]]

'''Animated''':
*Jedi Master Animated Droid Winner: [[KR-D7]] ([[Kyber Raiding Droid]]), by [[Arturo Acevedo Smith]]
*Padawan Animated Droid Winner: [[QZ-M2]], by [[Alexander Lee]]
