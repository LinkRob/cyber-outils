'''Fry''' was a [[human]]<ref name="Script" /> [[counselor]] aboard the [[Galactic Republic]] [[prison ship]] [[Lourna Dee (prison ship)|''Restitution'']] during the [[High Republic Era]]. She worked alongside Councilor [[Jon Wittick]], and was often more hostile toward the ''Restitution''{{'s}} [[prisoner]]s than her counterpart. After a number of [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] captured by the Galactic Republic and [[Jedi Order]] at the [[Battle of Galov]] were transferred to the ''Restitution'', Fry and Wittick oversaw their arrival. The Nihil included [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]], who had managed to escape the Republic's notice and took a false name, with whom Fry conflicted on various occasions.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

Dee's presence aboard the ''Restitution'' eventually led her rival, former Tempest Runner [[Pan Eyta]], to [[Defense of the Restitution|attack]] the vessel. Fry, Wittick, and several prisoners, including Dee herself, survived the initial assault, and were forced to work together against the attackers. When the group came under attack from Nihil [[scav droid]]s, Fry lost an [[Limb|arm]] and received a severe stomach wound. The group attempted to escape from the approaching [[droid]]s through a maintenance shaft, but Fry did not have the strength to make it through. Fry instead chose to stay behind, sacrificing herself to slow down the droids.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />
