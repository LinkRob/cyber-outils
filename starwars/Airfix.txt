'''Airfix''' is a company that produces model kits of aircraft, military vehicles and other subjects. In [[1983]], it produced the "Jabba The Hutt Throne Room Action Scene," which named several characters from [[Jabba's Palace/Legends|Jabba's Palace]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].
