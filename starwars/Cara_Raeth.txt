'''Cara Raeth''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] who lived on [[Corellia/Legends|Corellia]] during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Vryim? He's dead? No. Oh, but I think I knew. I think I felt something when he died. Something that drew me out of the city to this place. I just didn't understand until now. Oh, Vryim. I'll miss you.|Cara Raeth, to a spacer|<ref name="SWG:FCRhV" />}}
By the time following the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]], Cara Raeth lived on Corellia with her husband, [[Vryim Raeth]], a vehicle repairman.<ref name="SWG:FCRhV" /> In [[1 ABY/Legends|1 ABY]],<ref name="SWG_timeline">{{SWG_timeline}}</ref> Vryim Raeth was kidnapped by the criminal organization known as [[Chirq Council]] and taken to the [[First Elite Council Bunker]], near the city of [[Doaba Guerfel/Legends|Doaba Guerfel]]. The criminals suspected him to know too much information about their organization, but they were completely wrong about him. They finally assassinated the man, for no reason. Cara Raeth followed the trail of her husband during weeks. When she arrived at the First Elite Council Bunker, she asked a spacer to enter the bunker and rescue her husband. Unfortunately for her, the spacer only found the body of the dead Vryim Raeth.<ref name="SWG:FCRhV" />
