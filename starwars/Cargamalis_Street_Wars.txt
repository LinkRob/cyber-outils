{{Quote|Sounded like Happy knew him. Anything on that?''"<br />"''Not much, but Bohento and Happy both fought in the Cargamalis Street Wars. Imagine Burnin Konn's worst slum gangs. With plasma rockets.|[[The Smuggler]] and [[Riley]]|<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref>}}
The '''Cargamalis Street Wars''' were a series of conflicts between rival slum gangs that took place on [[Cargamalis system|Cargamalis]]<ref name="One Codebreaker, Please" /> some [[time]] prior to the [[Iron Blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]] by [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Placement" /> in [[4 ABY|7981 C.R.C.]]<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> Because [[blaster]] shortages were common during the conflict, the people of Cargamalis found other ways to keep fighting, using everything from [[shock rod]]s to [[Vibroknife|vibroknives]].<ref name="CSW">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Cargamalis Street Warrior"</ref> Nevertheless, [[plasma rocket]]s were involved on at least one occasion.<ref name="One Codebreaker, Please" />

The [[Aqualish]] [[Bohento Splinth]], the [[human]] [[Dapp|"Happy" Dapp]],<ref name="One Codebreaker, Please" /> and the [[Bith]] [[I'dreth]] all participated in the Cargamalis Street Wars, the latter as a [[sniper]].<ref name="I'dreth">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "I'dreth"</ref> Many of [[Commander]] [[Bragh]]'s [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Purge Trooper (Anoat sector)|Purge Troopers]] were also recruited from gangs that fought in the Cargamalis Street Wars.<ref name="Reinforcements">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Story Mission: "Reinforcements"</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Cargamalis Street Wars were first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'',<ref name="Uprising" /> a [[canon]] mobile [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[Kabam]], [[Disney Interactive]], and [[Lucasfilm]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms, which was released on [[September 10]], [[2015]]<ref name="SWNews">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-uprising-available-now|text=''Star Wars: Uprising'' Available Now}}</ref> and taken offline on [[November 17]], [[2016]].<ref name="Shutting down">{{Kabam|subdomain=community|url=forums/showthread.php?679136-Discussion-Thread-Star-Wars-Uprising-is-Shutting-Down|text=Discussion Thread: Star Wars Uprising is Shutting Down|nolive=1|nobackup=1}}</ref> In the game, the Street Wars were mentioned in dialogue preceding the Story Missions "One Codebreaker, Please"<ref name="One Codebreaker, Please" /> and "Reinforcements,"<ref name="Reinforcements" /> as well as in the crew member profiles for [[I'dreth]]<ref name="I'dreth" /> and the nameless "Cargamalis Street Warrior."<ref name="CSW" />
