The '''CR-2 heavy blaster pistol''', also known more simply as a '''CR-2 blaster pistol''', was a [[heavy blaster pistol]]<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" /> manufactured by [[Corellian Arms]] that was utilized by the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]].<ref name="HC 54" /> Small and agile, it had an [[Repeating blaster|extreme rate of fire]] and shot ionized bolts. It also could be configured with [[night vision]] [[scope]]s or have an extended [[stock]] for reduced recoil.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>
{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The CR-2 heavy blaster pistol props used in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']] were made from dense foam with a resin faux-scope and grip attatchment and a realistic metal barrel. The main body of the pistol was derived from the [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] M960 pistol produced by [[Wikipedia:Calico Light Weapons Systems|Calico]], which then had futuristic knobs, grooves and ridges added to cosmetically enhance it. The prop measured seventeen inches by nine inches.<ref name="Auction">{{WebCite|url=https://entertainment.ha.com/itm/movie-tv-memorabilia/film/star-wars-episode-i-the-phantom-menace-tcf-1999-naboo-security-guard-cr-2-heavy-blaster/a/7456-11048.s|text=Star Wars: Episode I - The Phantom Menace (TCF 1999), Naboo Security Guard CR-2 Heavy Blaster|work=[[Wikipedia:Heritage Auctions|Heritage Auctions]]|archivedate=20260426165856}}</ref>
