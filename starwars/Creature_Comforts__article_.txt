"'''Creature Comforts'''" is an ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' article published in the [[Star Wars Insider 227|227th issue]] of the [[Star Wars Insider]] magazine on<ref name="Insider" /> [[August 27]], [[2024]].<ref name="Titan" /> The article covers a tour of [[Lucasfilm]]'s Creature Shop, run by [[Neal Scanlan]], where creature effects for ''[[Star Wars]]'' movies and television series, including [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''The Force Awakens'']] and ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', were created.<ref name="Insider" />
