The '''Devaron massacre''' was the result of a battle for control of the [[Temple of Eedit]] on the [[planet]] [[Devaron]] by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The battle was a terrible loss for the [[Galactic Republic]], which suffered yet another blow in its long campaign against the CIS, but marked a great success for [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s new [[Sith apprentice]], [[Savage Opress]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Do not be fooled, Savage. The Temple of Eedit serves only as a front for the military operations of the Republic.|Count Dooku, to Savage Opress|<ref name="Monster" />}}
[[File:DroidsOnTheRun-CoD.png|thumb|left|250px|Republic and Separatist forces had already clashed at Devaron earlier in the Clone Wars.]]
Earlier in the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="Cargo of Doom">{{TCW|Cargo of Doom}}</ref> in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Cargo Date">{{TCWcanondates|202}}</ref> the [[planet]] [[Devaron]] had already emerged as a [[Battle of Devaron|flashpoint]]; hired by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]], [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] attacked an [[Devaron New Republic outpost|outpost]] operated by the [[Jedi Order]] with forces of the [[Separatist Droid Army]], wiping out the [[clone trooper]] [[Devaron unit|detachment]] stationed there. Taken along with the [[Kyber memory crystal]], [[Jedi General]] [[Bolla Ropal]] was captured by Bane's forces and [[torture]]d to [[death]]. The arrival of [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s [[Skywalker's fleet|fleet]] saw the destruction of Bane's [[Trade Federation fleet]].<ref name="Cargo of Doom" /> However, Bane escaped with the [[crystal]] and a [[holocron]] he had [[Raid on the Jedi Temple|taken previously]], but the items were later taken back by the Jedi.<ref name="Children of the Force">{{TCW|Children of the Force}}</ref> Devaron, meanwhile, returned to firm Republic and [[Jedi]] control.<ref name="Monster" />

In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Monster Timelines" /> with the Jedi Order in control of the vital [[Temple of Eedit]] on Devaron, [[Count]] [[Dooku]] ordered his recently acquired apprentice, [[Savage Opress]], to take back the outpost to ensure the Separatists could have a foothold in that corner of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Monster" /> The fighting on Devaron also followed the [[Battle of Sarrish]],<ref name="Timelines" /> a massive defeat for the Republic.<ref name="Missing in Action">{{TCW|Missing in Action}}</ref>

==The massacre==
{{Quote|Master!''"<br />"''Stay back!|[[Knox]] and [[Halsey]] upon Opress's arrival|<ref name="Monster" />}}
Prior to Savage Opress arriving to the temple, a force of Separatist [[battle droid]]s, those of the [[B1-series battle droid|B1-series]] and [[B2-series super battle droid|B2-series]] models, had already landed on world and began the assault on the temple, which was defended by [[Jedi Master]] [[Halsey]] and [[Knox]], his [[Padawan]], along with their [[Advanced Recon Force trooper|ARF troopers]] led by [[Clone Commander]] [[Trauma]]. As the Confederate [[troop]]s advanced through [[Eedit Road]], Republic forces were cut off. A group of clones led by Trauma in the front to fend off enemy [[droid]]s while Halsey, Knox, and a handful of clones defended the temple entrance from a group of battle droids.<ref name="Monster" />

[[File:Halsey-Monster.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Knox and Halsey during the battle.]]
Once Savage Opress arrived, he quickly made his way towards the temple, knocking battle droids out of his way. After Opress quickly [[Death|killed]] more than half a dozen ARF troopers with his pike. Commander Trauma then ordered his remaining men to focus their attention on Opress. Trauma rushed towards Opress to face him but was killed when Opress sliced him upwards with his spear, launching Trauma into the air briefly. After Trauma's death, the remaining ARF troopers were killed, and Master Halsey and Knox defeated the droid forces surrounding them. Once Opress had reached the Jedi, Halsey moved forward to face him, ordering his padawan to stay back. Opress was able to quickly dodge the Jedi Master's [[lightsaber]] swings, and after disarming Halsey, they briefly fought in [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand combat]]. Opress quickly gained the upper hand, striking down Halsey to the ground and impaling him with his spear, killing Halsey. After the death of his master, Knox ran towards Opress, but was killed in a single strike of the spear, sending Knox's body all the way back to the temple door. In the end, the entire Republic force was destroyed, as well as all present Separatist forces except for Opress.<ref name="Monster" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|This is not the work of a Sith Lord, or a Jedi... but a reckless, impulsive animal.|Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="WOTM" />}}
After Opress defeated Republic forces, he contacted Count Dooku informing him that the temple was now under their control, and was ordered to return to [[Serenno]]. Upon Opress' return to Dooku's palace, Dooku told Opress that he foresaw that he would do great things as his new apprentice. Dooku believed they would be more powerful than Lord Sidious and rule the galaxy.<ref name="Monster" /> After the massacre, [[Delta Squad]] members [[Boss (clone commando)|Boss]], [[Fixer (clone commando)|Fixer]], [[Sev]], and [[Scorch]] were sent to recover the bodies of the fallen Jedi and the Temple's security footage, reporting to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]].<ref name="WOTM" />

[[File:HalseyDeathHolo-WOTM.png|thumb|left|250px|A holorecording recovered of Halsey's death]]
After Jedi Masters [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Plo Koon]], [[Saesee Tiin]], and [[Adi Gallia]] were informed that everyone at the Temple of Eedit, including the Jedi, were dead, they discussed among themselves who had the ability to carry out the massacre. Master Gallia stated that in the past, only Count Dooku's [[assassin]], [[Asajj Ventress]], was capable of such action. Master Koon believed that it could be a newly appointed Sith Lord. Masters Yoda and Windu showed Kenobi the footage from the temple. Kenobi, upon seeing, thought it was Darth Maul alive. However, Yoda and Windu told him that it was not Maul, but a Zabrak from [[Dathomir]], not [[Iridonia]] as Kenobi originally believed Maul was from. The Jedi Council sent Kenobi and Skywalker to Dathomir the [[Nightbrother village|village]] Maul and Opress came from.<ref name="WOTM" />

Already traumatized by events like the [[Battle of Abrion Bridge]], the massacre proved to be too much for [[Commander]] [[Mekedrix]], a member of the Republic-aligned [[Roonian]] [[Roon's military defense forces|defense force]], to handle. Driven mad and coming to believe the true master of the galaxy was the concept of destruction itself, Mekedrix abandoned the [[Republic Military]], leaving no trace of where he left to. When he began to attack both sides of the war as the leader of the [[Death Wind]] execution squad, Republic officials suspected Mekedrix's involvement but were unable to confirm as such. Three [[Standard month|months]] after the Death Wind's [[Raid on Minntooine|attack]] on [[Minntooine]], [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]] recapped Mekedrix's suspected fall into madness after the Devaron massacre before dispacthing Skywalker and Kenobi on an [[Mission to Ando (Mekedrix)|unofficial mission]] to bring him to justice.<ref name="Obi-Wan 4">[[Obi-Wan 4|''Obi-Wan'' 4]]</ref>
