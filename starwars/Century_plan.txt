The '''century plan''' was [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]'s long-term grand design for [[the galaxy]].<ref name="MoF" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|67}}</ref> when [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]]'s [[Imperial Rebirth Act]] passed in the [[Imperial Senate]] with the goal of limiting Palpatine's executive power, [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] informed Mothma that the sections of her bill that attempted curb the Emperor were vetoed as they interfered with the century plan.<ref name="MoF" />
