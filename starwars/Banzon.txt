'''Banzon''' was a minty [[Spice (seasoning)|spice]] that could be found on the [[Tulakt's homeworld|homeworld]] of the [[alien]] [[fortune teller]] [[Tulakt]].<ref name="Survivor" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> Tulakt mentioned the taste of banzon and described it as enlivening the taste buds during a conversation about what she missed from [[Homeworld|home]] with the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] in the [[Pyloon's Saloon]] [[cantina]] on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]]. Kestis then suggested she ask the [[chef]] [[Greez Dritus]] to cook her something that reminded her of home.<ref name="Survivor" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], banzon was mentioned in an optional conversation in the [[2023]] [[Respawn Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Survivor" /> [[Banzon/Legends|Banzon]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was mentioned in the [[River of Chaos 2|second issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: River of Chaos]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]], written by [[Louise Simonson]] and released by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="RoC">[[River of Chaos 2|''River of Chaos'' 2]]</ref> on [[July 1]], [[1995]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54539/star_wars_river_of_chaos_1995_2|text=Star Wars: River of Chaos (1995) #2}}</ref>
