The '''Battle of Grizal''',<ref name="Trail of Shadows 1">[[The High Republic: Trail of Shadows 1|''The High Republic: Trail of Shadows'' 1]]</ref> also known as the '''infiltration of Grizal''',<ref name="Chronicles" /> was fought in the aftermath of the [[Valo Atrocity|attack on Valo]] when the [[Jedi]] attacked the [[Nihil]] [[Grizal camp|base]] on [[Grizal]]. The Jedi were able to locate the base after allowing the [[pirate]] [[Quin Amarant]] to escape [[Valo]] in a [[shuttle]] aboard which [[Jedi Knight]] [[Indeera Stokes]] and her [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] were hiding, allowing the two to activate a [[homing beacon]] upon their arrival on the planet.<ref name="Rising Storm" />

Once the signal was received, [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]] and his [[Red Group]] departed the ongoing [[Second Battle of Cyclor|skirmish]] at [[Cyclor]] against one [[Tempest]] of the Nihil, arriving at Grizal and assaulting the base.

During the attack, Zettifar was briefly reunited with his thought-dead Master, [[Loden Greatstorm]], who had been a prisoner of the Nihil. However, [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] unleashed a weapon called the [[Great Leveler]] against the Jedi, [[Death|killing]] Greatstorm and traumatizing Zettifar.<ref name="Rising Storm" />

In the aftermath, the Jedi assumed that the [[Twi'lek]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]], who had escaped the battle, was the true leader of the Nihil.<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref>

According to Jedi Master [[Harli Cogra]], the battle came to be counted amongst the Republic's greatest victories over the Nihil.<ref name="Chronicles" />
