==Legends==
*'''[[Mission to Florrum/Legends|Mission to Florrum]]''', in which the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] attempted to gain custody of [[Count/Legends|Count]] [[Dooku/Legends|Dooku]] from the [[Ohnaka Gang/Legends|Ohnaka Gang]]
*'''[[Second mission to Florrum/Legends|Second mission to Florrum]]''', in which [[Plo Koon/Legends|Plo Koon]] and [[Ahsoka Tano/Legends|Ahsoka Tano]] rescued hostages from the [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunters]] [[Aurra Sing/Legends|Aurra Sing]], [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]], and [[Bossk/Legends|Bossk]]
*'''[[Battle of Florrum]]''', fought between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]]
*'''[[Rescue on Florrum/Legends|Rescue on Florrum]]''', in which a class of [[Jedi Initiate/Legends|Jedi Initiates]] tried to rescue Ahsoka Tano from the Ohnaka Gang
*'''[[Invasion of Florrum/Legends|Invasion of Florrum]]''', an attack launched by [[General/Legends|General]] [[Grievous/Legends|Grievous]] against [[Hondo Ohnaka/Legends|Hondo Ohnaka]]
*'''[[Skirmish on Florrum/Legends|Skirmish on Florrum]]''', which took place between [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Adi Gallia/Legends|Adi Gallia]], and the Ohnaka Gang against the [[Nightbrothers/Legends|Nightbrothers]] [[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]] and [[Savage Opress/Legends|Savage Opress]]
