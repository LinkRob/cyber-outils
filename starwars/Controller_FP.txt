The '''Controller FP''' was a model of [[force pike]] manufactured by the [[SoroSuub Corporation]].

==Description==
The Controller FP was a [[force pike]] designed by the [[SoroSuub Corporation]]. It was equipped with a [[Vibro-weapon|vibroactive]] tip that emitted an electric shock that could cause pain or [[stun]] a target. The weapons could kill when used in tandem. The staff also had the ability to project a pain-inducing<ref name="Rebels">{{Rebels|Family Reunion &ndash; and Farewell}}</ref> [[force field]] around a target to immobilize them.<ref name="CGSWG" />

==History==
[[File:EzraInStasisField-FRaF.png|thumb|300px|right|Controller FP force pikes could project a force field to hold someone in stasis.]]
The Controller FP was employed by [[bodyguard]]s and sentries throughout [[the galaxy]],<ref name="CGSWG" /> but it was most famously utilized by the [[Emperor's Royal Guard]] in their work of ensuring the safety of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and enforcing his will.<ref name="ROTJ" /> During the [[Liberation of Lothal|Liberation]] of [[Lothal]], Emperor Palpatine dispatched three Royal Guards to kill [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]] after he failed to coerce the young [[Jedi]] into unlocking the [[World Between Worlds]]. Using Controller FP force pikes, the guards contained Bridger within a force field emitted by the staffs while a [[squad]] of [[stormtrooper]]s attempted to shoot him. In spite of the pain the stasis field caused, Bridger was able to use [[the Force]] to free himself from his attackers.<ref name="Rebels" />
