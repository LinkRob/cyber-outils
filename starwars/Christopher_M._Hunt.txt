'''Christopher M. Hunt''' is a seasoned [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] video game art director, as well as self-defense instructor and 3rd Degree Black Belt in [[Wikipedia:Krav Maga|Krav Maga Worldwide]], who has experience working on a number of ''[[Star Wars]]'' games.<ref name="LinkedIn" /> Hunt was hired by [[Pandemic Studios]] under [[Electronic Arts]] in [[July]] [[2001]], where he served as a "Senior 3D Artist" for one year, working on [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (video game)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'']] during this time, before he was promoted to "Lead Artist" in [[2002]], and then promoted again to "Art Director" another year later in [[May]] [[2003]]. As art director, Hunt would be heavily involved with [[2004]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Battlefront]]'', remaining with Pandemic Studios until they were shut down in late [[2009]]/early [[2010]].<ref name="LinkedIn Experience" />

After working as an art director between a other few companies, including [[Wikipedia:Microsoft|Microsoft]] and [[Wikipedia:Piranha Games|Piranha Games, Inc.]], Hunt would be hired by [[The Walt Disney Company]] in [[2017]], where he would once again be assigned as an art director on a ''Star Wars'' project.<ref name="LinkedIn Experience" /> This time, he would head the ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Challenges]]'' Virtual/Augmented Reality (AR/VR) experience and its subsequent expansions,<ref name="Dark Side Expansion">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-jedi-challenges-dark-side-expansion-kylo-ren|text=Fulfill Your Destiny as Kylo Ren in ''Star Wars: Jedi Challenges''' New Dark Side Expansion}}</ref> a collaborative effort between Disney and tech developer/distributor [[Lenovo]].<ref name="Lenovo">{{WebCite|url=https://news.lenovo.com/pressroom/press-releases/star-wars-jedi-challenges-a-new-smartphone-powered-augmented-reality-experience-launching-this-holiday|text=Star Wars™: Jedi Challenges, A New Smartphone-Powered Augmented Reality Experience Launching this Holiday|work=[https://news.lenovo.com Lenovo StoryHub]|archivedate=20231208102453}}</ref> He was especially influential in the finalized state of the "Dark Side Expansion," stating in an interview with [[StarWars.com]], "We started to formulate a plan on the update being centered on lightsaber combat, with adding new characters and a little bit of a twist on how you wield a lightsaber and who you are while you're playing."<ref name="Dark Side Expansion" /> Hunt would leave Disney in [[December]] [[2019]] to work for [[Wikipedia:Riot Games|Riot Games, Inc.]], and is now an art director at [[Wikipedia:Meta Platforms|Meta]] while also continuing to instruct in Krav Maga.<ref name="LinkedIn Experience" />
