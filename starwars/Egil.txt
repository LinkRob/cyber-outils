'''Egil''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who was the leader of the [[Scyre]] clan where [[Phasma]] lived on the [[planet]] [[Parnassos]]. He was the oldest man of the clan, dying at the age of 40 which was 5 standard years longer than the average deathrate. He trained Phasma and [[Porr]] prior<ref name="Phasma" /> to [[22 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> As he got weaker and older, the people of Scyre started seeing him as unfit to lead. Acting upon this idea, Porr [[Death|murdered]] him in his [[sleep]] in the [[Nautilus]].<ref name="Phasma" />
