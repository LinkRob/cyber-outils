The '''Battle of Eriadu'''<ref name="NYCC">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2024-lucasfilm-publishing-reveals|text=NYCC 2024: All the ''Star Wars'' News and Reveals from New York Comic-Con}}</ref> was a battle fought between the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 14" /> the [[Jedi Order]]<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 15" /> and the [[Eriaduan]]s against the [[Nihil]]<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 14" /> [[Pirate|marauders]]<ref name="Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone 1">[[The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone 1|''The High Republic Adventures – Dispatches from the Occlusion Zone'' 1]]</ref> for control of<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 15" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 10">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 10|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 10]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Eriadu]].<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 15" /> Outside factions who were opposed to the Nihil also arrived and joined the battle.<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 5" />

The battle was a violent affair as the [[Eriaduan]]s, already a combative people, were enraged with the Nihil occupation of their homeworld and finally had a chance to release that anger by working to overthrow their occupiers. The Nihil, meanwhile, were enraged to see one of their major safezones become the site of conflict.<ref name="SparksFly">{{InsiderCite|230|Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Sparks Fly}}</ref> The battle resulted in a Nihil surrender and the end of the [[Nihil conflict]].<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 5" />
