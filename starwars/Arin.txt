'''Arin''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Duros]] who was once in a relationship with the criminal [[Cad Bane]]. While [[Reproduction|pregnant]] with her and Bane's [[Youngling|child]], she was shot in the crossfire of a [[Showdown in an unidentified city|duel]] between Bane and a [[Unidentified city (Cad Bane)|city]]'s [[Unidentified marshal|marshal]].<ref name="A Good Turn" /> Though she survived, married Bane's former friend&mdash;and the new marshal&mdash;[[Niro]] and eventually birthed her and Bane's son, [[Isaac]], she eventually [[Death|died]] some time later. As a result, Niro, who raised Isaac as his own, did so alone until Bane [[Second showdown in an unidentified city|returned]] and killed Niro.<ref name="One Good Deed" />
