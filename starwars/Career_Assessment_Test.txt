==Description==
The Career Assessment Test was taken by [[youngling]]s on the [[planet]] [[At Attin]] took that decided their career path during the [[New Republic Era]]. If an individual failed the test, then they would be held back a class. If a being skipped a section on the test, they would not be able to repeat it.<ref name="Real Adventure" />

==History==
{{Quote|If you skip a section, you will not be able to repeat it. No unauthorized datapads or comlinks are allowed during any of the testing sections. If you must use the refresher, remain seated and utilize your desk controls. If you finish a section early, wait silently for the next section to begin.|Droid going over the rules of the test before it is taken|<ref name="Real Adventure" />}}
Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SCDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> the [[human]] [[Wim]] and his friend [[Neel]] were set to take the test. The day before the test [[Undersecretary]] [[Fara]] came to their class at [[At Attin Middle School Gamma]] to talk about the test. The next day, Wim slept in and missed the tram to school. Trying to make it to school in time for the test, Wim took [[Wim's hoverbike|his hoverbike]] through the [[forest]] hoping it would be a shortcut. However Wim crashed down into a ravine where he [[Onyx Cinder|found]] what he thought was a [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]]. He was found by a [[safety droid]] who took him to school but he still missed the test. [[Wendle|Wim's father]] convinced the [[proctor]] to let Wim take the test the next day with a fifty&ndash;point penalty which meant that he would have to get all the questions correct to pass the test.<ref name="Real Adventure" />
