'''Bry''' was a [[Hoopaloo]] [[Gender|male]] [[thief]] who worked with fellow criminals [[Jorkat]] and [[Velek (Serenno)|Velek]] during the [[Republic Era]]. At a [[Celebration on Serenno|celebration]] on the [[planet]] [[Serenno]] in [[90 BBY]], Bry stole a purse from the [[Serennian]] [[Lady]] [[Jenza]]. Although Bry and his associates restrained Jenza and her [[House Serenno|brother]] [[Ramil]], the [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Dooku]] confronted the criminals. Dooku [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to [[Force push|throw]] the Hoopaloo through the air before revealing his presence and fighting Jorkat with an [[Electroblade training sword|electroblade]]. The [[Jedi]] eventually scared Bry and his accomplices away, saving Jenza and Ramil. Dooku recounted the incident in a [[holo-letter]] to Jenza, a recording of which he later showed to his newly-recruited [[Sith assassin]], [[Asajj Ventress]], around [[23 BBY]].

==Biography==
===Feathered fiend===
{{Quote|Perhaps we should take 'em with us, Jorkat. See what Renza will give us for 'em.|Bry, regarding Jenza and Ramil|<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />}}
[[File:Serenno-Massacre.png|thumb|right|260px|Bry and his accomplices attempted to steal from Lady Jenza on Serenno (pictured).]]
The [[Hoopaloo]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> [[Gender|male]] Bry was a [[thief]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> who lived during the [[Republic Era]], working alongside his criminal accomplices, the [[Karkarodon]] [[Jorkat]] and the [[Askajian]] [[Velek (Serenno)|Velek]]. The three were assoiated with [[Renza]] the [[Hutt]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> In [[90 BBY]],<ref name="Date 90">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Dooku]]'s first visit to [[Serenno]], which corresponds to the [[celebration on Serenno]] according to ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', to [[90 BBY]].</ref> Bry and his associates were present in [[Carannia]],<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> the [[capital]] [[city]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Serenno]], attending a [[Celebration on Serenno|celebration]] for the worlds of the region. During the celebration, Bry stole the purse of the [[Youngling|young]] [[Serennian]] [[Lady]] [[Jenza]]&mdash;daughter of [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Gora]] of [[House Serenno]]&mdash;swiping the item while<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> roughly brushing past her.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> Bry fled and hid behind a [[Repulsorlift|repulsor]] tent, rummaging through the purse's contents. Jenza pursued, snatching the bag back, and the Hoopaloo reprimanded the girl for doing so. When Jenza noticed that a [[crystal]] that had belonged to her [[Jenza's grandmother|grandmother]] was missing, she questioned Bry, who feigned ignorance.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

At that point, Jorkat and Velek approached from behind Jenza, and Bry claimed that the girl had stolen his purse. When Jenza protested, Jorkat called her selfish and asserted that the Hoopaloo had no [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to his name; the Karkarodon suggested stealing the Serennian's [[Jacket|coat]] to give to Bry, who agreed that it would [[Temperature|warm]] his feathers. After Jorkat grabbed Jenza to search her belongings and the girl's brother [[Ramil]] was restrained by Velek when he arrived in an attempt to defend his sister, Bry suggested kidnapping the pair and taking them to Renza, although the Karkarodon was unsure whether the Hutt would be interested. Jenza bit Jorkat and escaped his grip, and Bry attempted to catch her.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

===Fearful flight===
{{Dialogue|Dooku|I'd run too, if I were you.|Jorkat|I'll rip yer head off.|Bry|Jorkat. Please, let's get out of here.|attr=Dooku confronts Bry and Jorkat|src=<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />}}
[[File:Jedi-Dooku-Art.png|thumb|left|210px|Dooku (pictured later in life) confronted Bry and his accomplices.]]
However, Bry suddenly found himself thrown through the air, having been [[Force push|pushed]] [[Force power|using]] [[the Force]] by the [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Dooku]], who had [[Force sense|sensed]] a connection with Jenza&mdash;unbeknownst to both, his sister&mdash;and followed the girl. Bry cried out, insisting that something had knocked him down when questioned by his bewildered Karkarodon accomplice. Dooku revealed his presence, wielding an [[Electroblade training sword|electroblade]] against Jorkat. Bry attempted to intervene but was once again pushed back by Dooku. Velek was soon thrown into the Hoopaloo, almost crushing Bry when the Askajian stood up to run away. Although Jorkat remained aggravated at Dooku, Bry pleaded with his accomplice and the pair also fled.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

Dooku later recounted the incident in a [[holo-letter]] to Jenza.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="Date 23">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Dooku displayed a [[Hologram|holographic]] [[Datacard|data disk]] that detailed the events around meeting his sister, including the skirmish with Bry and his associates, to his recently-recruited [[Sith assassin]], [[Asajj Ventress]]. The pair listened to the recording, with Dooku commenting on the Hoopaloo, after which he gave Ventress several more disks for her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|investigation]] into Jenza's location.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Stealing from me, she was. Got me purse.''"<br />"''That's a lie! It's ''my'' purse.|Bry and Jenza|<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />}}
A thief, Bry lied about the ownership of Jenza's purse and acted as if the girl was the one who was stealing it from him. The Hoopaloo was willing to sell the children Jenza and Ramil to Renza the Hutt. Although he initially attempted to defend Jorkat from Dooku, Bry was soon scared away by the young [[Jedi]]'s prowess. The Hoopaloo punctuated his speech with squawks, while Jenza considered him scrawny and little.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bry appeared in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-script-by-cavan-scott/9780593157664|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> The spelling of his name was later confirmed with the release of the [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> on [[October 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Penguin" />
