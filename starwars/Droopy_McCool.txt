'''Droopy McCool''' was the commonly known stage name<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> of '''Snit''',<ref name="Card Trader">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Droopy McCool - Chindinkalu Flute Player}}</ref> who was a [[Gender|male]] [[Kitonak]] and the lead horn player of the [[Max Rebo Band]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> McCool's real name, however, was actually a series of [[flute]]-like whistles, unpronounceable by any other [[species]].<ref name="Updated and Expanded">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> He played the [[chindinkalu flute]].<ref name="databank" /> His body released a [[vanilla]]-like smell.<ref name="AEYNTK" />

In [[4 ABY]], McCool was present in [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] during the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], where he played "[[Jedi Rocks]]" along with the rest of the [[Max Rebo Band]].<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> While he was a well-integrated member of the Max Rebo Band, he was lonely, and wished for the company of other Kitonaks. He claimed to have heard the faint tones of his fellow kind out in the [[Tatooine]] dunes. After the [[Rescue of Han Solo|death]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]] and the explosion of his [[Khetanna|sail barge]], McCool disappeared into the [[desert]].<ref name="Updated and Expanded" />
