'''Duraplast''' was a white protective [[metal]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] used a [[Color|transparent]] version of this material to protect their [[engineer]]ing [[droid]], [[M2-M5]], from dust.<ref name="bftc">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> [[Boba Fett]]'s [[helmet]] was made of duraplast.<ref name="HC">{{HelmetCollectionCite|2|Helmets}}</ref>
