'''Class IV planet''' was a classification of [[planet]] that required quarantine after contact by the standards of the [[Galactic Empire]] in order to check for infection by unforeseen pathogens. The fertile planet [[Uokara]] in the [[Unknown Regions]] was considered a class IV planet and<ref name="Inhabitant" /> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]]<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] was required to go into quarantine after surveying the world, revealing that he had been infected by [[Ghymnon]], an unrecognized pathogen that covered the entirety of Uokara.<ref name="Inhabitant" />

==Behind the scenes==
The term class IV planet was used in "[[The Inhabitant]]," a story written by [[Steve Orlando]], illustrated by [[Paul Davidson]], and published in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] [[Darth Vader – Black, White & Red 4|''Darth Vader – Black, White & Red'' 4]]<ref name="Inhabitant" /> on [[July 26]], [[2023]].<ref name="Solicit">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/107503/star_wars_darth_vader_-_black_white_red_2023_4|text=Star Wars: Darth Vader &ndash; Black, White & Red (2023) #4}}</ref>
