'''Fermic''' was a terrestrial [[factory]] [[planet]]<ref name="Spark of the Resistance" /> located in the [[Fermic system]] within the [[Wazta sector]] of<ref name="Appendix" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Spark of the Resistance" /> and [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western Reaches" /> It was situated in grid square J-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Fermic had a type I [[atmosphere]]<ref name="Atmosphere" /> that was polluted from the planet's many [[fuel]] [[Refinery|refineries]] and industrial facilities, but was breathable to both [[human]]s and<ref name="Spark of the Resistance" /> the [[Bird|avian]]<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> [[porg]]s.<ref name="Spark of the Resistance" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Spark of the Resistance]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], and several [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Crait]], Resistance operatives [[Poe Dameron]], [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], and [[Rose Tico]] visited Fermic as the final stop on a journey around the Outer Rim to gather supplies. On Fermic, they loaded a number of cargo crates onto the [[freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' before leaving to answer a [[distress signal]] from the planet [[Minfar]]. By the [[time]] of the Resistance members' visit, Fermic was untouched by the First Order's subjugation of the [[galaxy]].<ref name="Spark of the Resistance" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fermic appeared in ''[[Spark of the Resistance]]'', a junior novel written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published in [[2019]].<ref name="Spark of the Resistance" />
