The '''Aaris system''' was located in the [[Kathol sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="WesternReaches" /> The [[star system]] was situated in grid square M-21 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Aaris system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Aaris system/Legends|Aaris system]] first appeared in the [[roleplaying game]] adventure "[[Artifact of Aaris]]," released as part of ''[[The DarkStryder Campaign]]'' by [[West End Games]] for use with ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' in [[1995]]. The adventure was written by [[Peter M. Schweighofer]] and contained visual depictions of the system by artist [[Brian Schomburg]].<ref name="AOA">{{DarkStryder|adventure=Artifact of Aaris|book=The DarkStryder Campaign}}</ref>
