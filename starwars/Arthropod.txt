'''Arthropods''' were a taxonomic division of life-forms characterized by a low-[[water]] biology and a [[chitin]]ous [[Skeleton|exoskeleton]]. [[Insect]] and [[insectoid]] [[species]] like the [[Mustafarian]]s belonged to that group.<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> [[Kouhun]]s were arthropods from [[Indoumodo]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>
