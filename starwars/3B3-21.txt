'''3B3-21'''<ref name="LEGODoodle">''[[LEGO Star Wars: Choose Your Side: Doodle Activity Book]]''</ref> was a [[B1-series battle droid]] that served in the [[Trade Federation Droid Army|Droid Army]] of the [[Trade Federation]].<ref name="TPM" /> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> they were part of the force that [[Invasion of Naboo|invaded]] the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. 3B3-21 was deactivated along with their fellow [[droid]]s when the [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Droid Control Ship]] orbiting the planet was destroyed during the [[Battle of Naboo]].<ref name="TPM" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
3B3-21 first appeared in the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]].<ref name="TPM" /> They were later identified in the [[2016]] [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'' activity book ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Choose Your Side: Doodle Activity Book]]''.<ref name="LEGODoodle" />
