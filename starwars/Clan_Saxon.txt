'''Clan Saxon''',<ref name="Zero Hour" /> also known as the '''Saxon clan''', was a [[Mandalorian]] [[Mandalorian clan|clan]]<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" /> of [[House Vizsla]].<ref name="Zero Hour" /><ref name="Pablo Hidalgo Tweet" />

==History==
===Republic Era===
Clan Saxon was a clan of [[Mandalorian warrior]]s who practiced the warrior traditions of ancient [[Mandalore]].<ref name="ScenetoStats">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/scene-to-stats-mandalorian-super-commandos-2|text=Scene to Stats: Mandalorian Super Commandos}}</ref> After the [[Great Clan Wars]] devastated Mandalore, [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] pushed Mandalore into a new pacifistic direction under the [[New Mandalorians|New Mandalorian movement]].<ref name="Voyage of Temptation">{{TCW|Voyage of Temptation}}</ref> Mandalore's surviving warriors were exiled and thought to have died out, but they in fact survived as members of the [[Death Watch]] movement<ref name="The Mandalore Plot">{{TCW|The Mandalore Plot}}</ref> or out in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Friends">''[[Friends Like These]]''</ref> Indeed, Clan Saxon survived and remained dedicated to its war-like traditions, but the faction found itself disenfranchised.<ref name="ScenetoStats" /> When the rogue [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] allied with Death Watch, eventually taking it over,<ref name="Shades of Reason">{{TCW|Shades of Reason}}</ref> Clan Saxon was revitalized and rallied under Maul's leadership.<ref name="ScenetoStats" />

One member of Clan Saxon<ref name="Zero Hour" /> was [[Gar Saxon]], who served as one of Maul's top lieutenants up until the [[Siege of Mandalore]] that deposed Maul.<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice">{{TCW|The Phantom Apprentice}}</ref>

===Imperial Era begins===
Led by Gar Saxon, they controlled Mandalore with the support of the [[Galactic Empire]]. When Gar Saxon [[Death|died]]&mdash;[[Skirmish on Krownest|killed]] by [[Ursa Wren]]&mdash;a [[Mandalorian Civil War (Imperial Era)|civil war]] broke out between Clan Saxon and [[Clan Wren]].<ref name="Zero Hour" /> In addition to their rivals in Clan Wren, Clan Saxon had also earned themselves the ire of [[House Kryze]] after betraying and deposing them in favor of the Empire.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />

A number of Mandalorians, particularly [[Fenn Rau]] of the [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protectors]] and [[Sabine Wren]], regarded the Saxon clan as traitors to Mandalore, having sold out their planet to an outside rule and betrayed [[Lady]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] who many saw as the rightful ruler of Mandalore. After Gar's death on Krownest, his brother Lord [[Tiber Saxon]] took up his position of [[Governor]] and headed their conflict with Clan Wren and the [[Nite Owls]].<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />

Tiber, who had completely eschewed any vestiges of loyalty towards his heritage, deployed a [[superweapon]] know as "the Duchess" against Clan Wren when they plotted with the Rebels to reclaim Mandalore, which worked by targeting and superheating the [[beskar]] alloy in [[Mandalorian armor]], incinerating the wearer. The weapon was regarded as an abomination, for beskar [[armor]] was an inalienable part of Mandalorian culture. In response, Sabine, who had been unwittingly tricked into designing the weapon during her [[Imperial Academy|Academy]] [[Standard day|days]], led a joint force of Mandalorians and rebels to [[Mission to Mandalore|infiltrate]] [[Tiber Saxon's Star Destroyer|Saxon's Star Destroyer]] at [[Sundari]] and destroy the "Duchess" before it could be used again. This, however, was also a trap orchestrated by Tiber, who wanted to capture Sabine and force her to perfect the weapon's range and capabilities. He confronted her on the ship, [[Torture|torturing]] her, Bo-Katan, and even his loyal subordinate [[Captain]] [[Hark]] with it in order to get her to comply. Sabine, however, used the opportunity to recalibrate it so that it targeted the Imperial's [[plastoid]] armor instead of beskar. Hark took the opportunity to escape before Wren breached the Duchess' power core. This caused the weapon to overload and blow up, taking the [[Star Destroyer]] and all hands aboard, including Tiber, with it.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />
