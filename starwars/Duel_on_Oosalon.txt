In [[3 BBY]], while scouting for a potential location in which the rebellion could build a base, the [[Jedi]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Ezra Bridger]] fought a [[lightsaber]] '''duel''' with the [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]] known as the [[Seventh Sister]] and the [[Fifth Brother]] on the [[planet]] [[Oosalon]]. The [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] ended with the escape of the Jedi.

==Prelude==
Following the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Battle of Garel|attack]] on [[Garel]], the [[Phoenix Cell]] was searching for a remote world to establish a permanent rebel base, with help from the [[Spectres]].<ref name="Legacy">{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref> Two of these rebels were the [[Great Jedi Purge]] survivor [[Kanan Jarrus]] and his [[Padawan|apprentice]] [[Ezra Bridger]]. As [[Jedi]], they were constantly being hunted by the Galactic Empire's [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]]. Two of these Inquisitors, the [[Fifth Brother]] and the [[Seventh Sister]], pursued Jarrus and Bridger for several [[Standard month|months]]; hampering the rebellion's efforts to search for a base.<ref name="Shroud of Darkness" />

==The duel==
In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Year" /> Jarrus and Bridger along with the [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]] visited the stormy [[planet]] of [[Oosalon]]. They were scouting the planet as a possible location for a rebel base. While Jarrus and Bridger were exploring the [[Oosalon Mesa]], they were attacked by the Fifth Brother and the Seventh Sister. A [[lightsaber duel]] ensued between the two groups of opponents. The two Jedi tried to contact Chopper, who was flying the [[VCX-series auxiliary starfighter|auxiliary]] ship ''[[Phantom]]'' but received no response. During the duel, the Jedi managed to destroy one of the Seventh Sister's [[ID9 seeker droid]]s. As the duel progressed, the Fifth Brother used [[the Force]] to shove Bridger off a cliff. However, Jarrus managed to levitate his apprentice to safety and grab his hand.<ref name="Shroud of Darkness" />

Continuing their fight with the Inquisitors, Bridger used the Force to summon a large [[tibidee]]. The apprentice and master leapt off the clip and escaped on the back of the tibidee which flew them to safety. Once airborne, they were picked up by Chopper and the ''Phantom''. The rebels then departed into space on the ''Phantom''. While traveling through [[hyperspace]], Jarrus arranged with [[Hera Syndulla]] and the other crew of the ''[[Ghost]]'' to meet them in deep space in order to protect Phoenix Cell from the Empire's agents.<ref name="Shroud of Darkness" />

==Aftermath==
[[File:LothalTempleEntrance.png|thumb|right|250px|Entrance of the Jedi Temple on Lothal]]
After reuniting with their team aboard the ''Ghost'', Jarrus confided in Syndulla that the Inquisitors were making it hard for him and Bridger to help the rebellion to establish a base, fearing that he and Bridger would have to part company with the rebellion. Later, the two Jedi met with the former Jedi Padawan [[Ahsoka Tano]] who counseled them to seek advice from [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Yoda]] at the [[Lothal Jedi Temple|Jedi Temple]] on [[Lothal]]. Parting company with the other rebels, the Jedi, Tano, and Chopper traveled on the ''Phantom'' to Lothal. While Chopper waited outside, the Jedi and Tano entered the temple.<ref name="Shroud of Darkness" />

There, Jarrus completed his [[Jedi Trials]] and became a [[Jedi Knight]]. Meanwhile, Bridger met with Yoda who counseled the young Jedi not to fight or they would be destroyed. Since Bridger was committed to supporting the rebellion, Yoda counseled Bridger and his companions to find the planet [[Malachor]]. Meanwhile, Tano experienced a [[Force vision]] of her former mentor [[Anakin Skywalker]] and learnt that he had indeed become Darth Vader. The three managed to flee with Chopper shortly before the Seventh Sister and the Fifth Brother breached the temple.<ref name="Shroud of Darkness" /> After helping the rebellion to establish [[Chopper Base]], the two Jedi, Chopper and Tano departed for their [[Mission to Malachor|journey]] to Malachor.<ref name="Mystery">{{Rebels|The Mystery of Chopper Base}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Duel on Oosalon served as the exposition of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Shroud of Darkness]]," which first premiered on [[March 2]], [[2016]].
