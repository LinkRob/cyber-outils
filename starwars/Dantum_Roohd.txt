'''Dantum Roohd'''<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/hostage-crisis-episode-guide|text="Hostage Crisis" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] [[senator]] who served in the [[Galactic Senate]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Hostage Crisis" /> During the war, Roohd was part of the [[Council of Neutral Systems]],<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" /> an outlier [[Politics|political]] wing of the [[Pacifist faction]] in the senate.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> He was later part of a small group of senators who wished to stop a vote on the [[Enhanced Privacy Invasion Bill]] and attended a meeting of those that opposed the bill at the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]] which was interrupted when the [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] took the attendees hostage.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

Roohd was opposed to peace talks with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] in the wake of the [[Bombing of Coruscant's central power distribution grid]].<ref name="Pursuit of Peace" />
