The '''Beroq system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Expansion Region]]'s [[Baroli sector]]. It was situated in grid square N-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the location [[Beroq 4]], which, during the [[Imperial Era]], housed a [[Unidentified fuel depot|fuel depot]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Before the Awakening" />

By [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Gorma date">''[[Poe Dameron: Flight Log]]'' establishes that the [[Liberation of Gorma]] took place before the [[Battle of Endor]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref> less than a [[Standard week|week]] after participating in the [[Liberation of Gorma|liberation]] of<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Gorma">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Gorma system]]</ref> [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Flight Log">''[[Poe Dameron: Flight Log]]''</ref> [[Gorma]],<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> [[rebel pilot]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Shara Bey]] undertook a raid at the [[fuel]] depot during [[Operation: Mynock Bite]]. The raid&mdash;part of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Galactic Civil War|war]] against the Empire&mdash;earned Bey her triple [[ace]] status.<ref name="Before the Awakening" />
