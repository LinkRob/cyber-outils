An '''energy projector''' was device capable of transmitting a [[immobilizer beam]]. The projector was capable of knocking out various technologies such as [[starship]]s and [[droid]]s. The [[Jablogian]] [[crime lord]], [[Azmorigan]] attempted to sell one to the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''{{'s}} [[captain]], [[Han Solo]]. According to Azmorigan, the projector could knock out technologies such as [[starfighter]]s and [[astromech droid]]s.<ref name="Powered Down 1">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=10|story=Powered Down, Part 1}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Energy projectors were first mentioned in [[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords: Prima Official Game Guide|''Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords: Prima Official Game Guide'']].<ref name="Knights of Old Republic 2 Prima official game guide">[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords: Prima Official Game Guide|''Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords'': Prima Official Game Guide]]</ref> It later appeared in the canon comic ''[[Powered Down, Part 1]]''.<ref name="Powered Down 1" />
