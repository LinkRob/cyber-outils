'''Chiewab Amalgamated Pharmaceuticals''', also known as simply '''Chiewab''',<ref name="Visual Guide" /> was a medical and chemical conglomerate that had a notable [[Chiewab Amalgamated Pharmaceuticals exploratory fleet|exploratory fleet]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. They were charter members of the [[Galactic Corporate Policy League]] and key signatories in its efforts that led to the creation of the [[Corporate Sector]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], the company set up a [[Chiewab research installation|research installation]] on the [[planet]] [[Iakar]].<ref name="Visual Guide" /> Chiewab also produced [[skirtopanol]], a powerful [[truth serum]].<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" />
