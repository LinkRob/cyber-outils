'''Abelor''' was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> located in the [[Abelor system]], within the [[Yushan sector]] of<ref name="SWSystems" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> and the [[Western Reaches]] regions,<ref name="Location" /> along the [[Corellian Trade Spine]]. It was situated in grid square K-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> 

By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Abelor survivalist pants]], [[Abelor survivalist belt]], and [[Abelor survivalist vest]] were produced on the planet and available for sale throughout [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> At some point between [[33 ABY]] and [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]]. The novel establishes that [[Izal Garsea]] had met [[Damar Olin]] less than a [[Standard year|year]] prior to the novel's events, meaning the pair's encounter with [[Ana Tolla's crew]] must have been between [[33 ABY]] and 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> the [[smuggler]] [[Damar Olin]] encountered the smuggler [[Ana Tolla]] and her [[Ana Tolla's crew|crew]] after he and his partner [[Izal Garsea]] became stranded with no [[fuel]], [[Foodstuff|food]], or contacts in a dusty [[Abelor spaceport|port]] on Abelor.<ref name="A Crash of Fate" /> In 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> [[historian]] and [[author]] [[Eloc Throno]] included a [[map]] depicting the location of Abelor in his [[book]] ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', which detailed the planet Batuu.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Abelor was first mentioned in the [[2019]] young-adult novel ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />
