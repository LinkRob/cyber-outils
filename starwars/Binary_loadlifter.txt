{{Quote|Vaporators! Sir, my first job was programming binary loadlifters&mdash;very similar to your vaporators in most respects.|[[C-3PO]], to [[Owen Lars]]|<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>}}
'''Binary loadlifters''',<ref name="ANH" /> also known as '''binary load lifters''',<ref name="Last Order">''[[The Last Order]]''</ref> '''Heavy-lifter droids''',<ref name="BTA" /> were a type of [[labor droid]]s that were used to lift heavy loads. The [[binary]] programming [[language]] of those [[droid]]s was similar to that of [[moisture vaporator]]s.<ref name="ANH" /> [[CLL-M2 binary loadlifter]]s were a model of Binary loadlifter<ref name="BR2D2">{{BuildR2Cite|54|Droid Directory}}</ref> in the [[CLL-series]],<ref name="Secrets 20">{{BuildFalconCite|20|Secrets of Spaceflight}}</ref> while [[Type II loadlifter droid]]s were frauds, being repurposed [[demolition droid]]s prone to violence. Late in the [[Clone Wars]], [[Rafa Martez]] was hired by [[Lokann]] and his [[Lokann's business partner|business partner]] to build the Type II [[Loader droid|loadlifter droids]]. The work was done by Rafa's sister [[Trace Martez|Trace]] and her new friend [[Ahsoka Tano]], who eventually realized the danger of the droids. One of the droids went on a rampage through [[Level 1313]] after Trace forgot to put a [[restraining bolt]] on it before activating it, leading her and Tano to have to chase it down.<ref name="Gone with a Trace" /> Other types of binary loadlifters known as the [[CCL]]s were operated in [[Facility 301G]] part of the [[Santhe Shipyards]] that were secretly owned by the [[Morgan Elsbeth's forces|forces]] of [[Morgan Elsbeth]].<ref name="Part Two" /> The [[First Order]] used heavy-lifer droids to carry equipment, often with the help of [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[cadet]]s.<ref name="BTA" />
