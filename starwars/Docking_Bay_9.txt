'''Docking Bay 9''' was a docking bay located in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the planet [[Batuu]], located in close proximity to [[Merchant Row (Batuu)|Merchant Row]]. In [[3 ABY|3 ABY,]] Imperial presence returned to the area when [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] landed his [[TIE/es assault shuttle|TIE echelon]] along with a squad of [[Stormtroopers]] to search the area.<ref name="EotE 1">[[Galaxy's Edge – Echoes of the Empire 1|''Galaxy's Edge – Echoes of the Empire'' 1]]</ref> By [[34 BBY]], the [[First Order]] had a presence there, using it as a recruiting station.<ref name="WDWNewsToday">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2018/01/photos-star-wars-galaxys-edge-trading-cards-provide-new-details-guests-will-find-batuu-2019|text=PHOTOS: Star Wars – Galaxy's Edge Trading Cards Provide New Details of What Guests Will Find on Batuu in 2019|work=WDW News Today|archivedate=20180510032057}}</ref>
