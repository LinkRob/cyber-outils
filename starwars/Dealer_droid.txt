'''Dealer droids''',<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> also known as '''casino droids'''<ref name="Last Shot">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref> or '''croupier droids''',<ref name="Tales">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]''</ref> were [[droid]]s used by [[casino]]s to deal cards. Some of them, for aesthetic and ornamental reasons, were plated in polished [[copper]]. A number of them were located at an underground [[Unidentified casino|resort]] on the [[planet]] [[Bastatha]].<ref name="Bloodline" />

== History ==
In [[3 BBY]], a [[Unidentified dealer droid|dealer droid]] was present in the premier-class car of the new [[luxury train]] [[Exas Jewell|''Exas Jewell'']] on its initial voyage on the [[planet]] [[Tyegin]]. The droid oversaw a high-stakes, invitation [[sabacc]] game between the socialites [[D'Lina]], [[Rakan]], [[Baj]], and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], who was impersonating the designer of the train, [[Shen Kanton]]. During the voyage, the train was attacked by [[Pirate|pirates]] who damaged the train before making off with the winnings of the game. The train dangerously sped up and the guests in the premier-class car sought to slow it down by throwing the train's furniture in front of it to disrupt the magnetic link it had with the rails. Baj attempted to throw the dealer droid in front of the train but was stopped by Calrissian. Eventually the train was eventually stopped due to their efforts.<ref name="Off the Rails" />
