The '''Foless system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained [[Foless]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was situated at the junction of the [[Corellian Trade Spine]]<ref name="Map" /> and the [[Shipwrights' Trace]],<ref name="Shipwrights" /> an area known as the [[Foless Crossroads]],<ref name="Where" /> and was situated at the coordinates M-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />
