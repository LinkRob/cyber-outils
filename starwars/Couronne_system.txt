The '''Couronne system''' was located in the [[Couronne sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Timelines" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It was situated in grid square P-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], and the [[hyperspace route]]<ref name="Timelines" /> known as the [[Great Kashyyyk Branch]]<ref name="GreatKashyyykBranch" /> linked it to the [[Zavian Abyss]] as well as the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Kashyyyk system]].<ref name="Timelines" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Couronne [[Star system|system]] was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|map]]<ref name="Timelines" /> designed by [[Christopher Ibbitt]]<ref name="Fry email">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-31.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-31}}</ref> that was created for the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' and given out as a promotional poster at [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|Celebration Anaheim]]<ref name="TimelinesSWcom">{{SW|url=news/swca-2022-lucasfilm-publishing-behind-the-page-panel|text=SWCA 2022: 7 Things We Learned from the Lucasfilm Publishing Behind the Page Panel}}</ref> on [[May 27]], [[2022]]<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|dkpublishing|status/1530292772598534145|[[Dorling Kindersley|DK Books US]]|date=2022-05-27|quote=Just announced at #StarWarsCelebration! Get your exclusive map - only at booth #2525.|archivedate=20220527210041}}</ref> prior to the book's release during [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]]<ref name="Celebration">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-europe-2023-exclusives|text=Check Out ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Exclusives from Hasbro, the LEGO Group, and More}}</ref> of [[April 7]] through [[April 10|10]], [[2023]].<ref name="CelebrationSchedule">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-panel-schedule|text=Plan Ahead with the ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Panel Schedule}}</ref> It was then identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] article on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Couronne system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via a mention in the StarWars.com [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
