The following events took place in or around the [[Standard year|year]] '''161 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> approximately '''3116 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''142 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7816''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*A second wave of settlers arrive on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Jelucan]] and establishes [[mining]] operations. The second wave considered the ways of the [[First Wavers|initial settlers]] to be backward. The second-wave of settlers used the profits from their mining operations to become wealthy aristocrats, while the first wave settlers remained largely destitute.<ref name="Jelucan date">The prologue of ''[[Lost Stars]]'', in which it is mentioned the second wave of settlers to [[Jelucan]] arrived "a hundred and fifty years ago", takes place when [[Thane Kyrell]] and [[Ciena Ree]] are both eight years old. Both of them are mentioned to have been born the year the [[Galactic Empire]] was founded, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Thus, the prologue is set in [[11 BBY]], setting Jelucan's second wave of colonization in 161 BBY.</ref>
*[[Indara]] [[Knighting Ceremony|became]] a [[Jedi Knight]] and was involved in the [[Farlingan Collapse]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="Wayseeker">''[[The Acolyte: Wayseeker]]''</ref>
