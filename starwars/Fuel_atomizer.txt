A '''fuel atomizer''' was a component of the [[TUG-b13]]'s engines. The atomizers were located directly above the [[Ignition chamber/Canon|ignition chamber]].<ref name="Cross">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> [[Anakin Skywalker]] developed a new fuel atomizer to boost the speed of [[Anakin Skywalker's Podracer|his]] [[podracer]] by 150 [[kilometer]]s per hour.<ref name="2023 Calendar">[[Star Wars Day-at-a-Time Calendar 2023|''Star Wars'' Day-at-a-Time Calendar 2023]]</ref>
