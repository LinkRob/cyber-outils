'''Aitch-Sixteen''' was an [[astromech droid]] that worked with [[starship]]s of the [[Jedi Order]] in the [[hangar]]s of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> Sometime between [[52 BBY|52]] and [[43 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]'' gives the age of [[Dooku]] at the [[time]] of the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] as eighty [[Standard year|years]] old. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the Battle of [[Geonosis]] to [[22 BBY]], placing Dooku's [[Reproduction|birth]] year in [[102 BBY]]. The [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script]] of ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'' states that Dooku is in his fifties at the time of Aitch-Sixteen's appearance, meaning that those events occurred between [[52 BBY]], in which Dooku is fifty years old, and [[43 BBY]], in which he is fifty-nine.</ref> Aitch-Sixteen was ordered to fit [[laser capacitor]]s into the [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|interceptor]] of the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Rael Averross]]. After speaking with the [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]] in the hangar, Averross called out to Aitch-Sixteen, asking whether the [[droid]] had fitted the laser capacitors. Aitch-Sixteen had not finished the job, replying to Averross in [[binary]], and the [[Jedi]] Knight scolded the astromech droid, claiming it had been given enough [[time]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aitch-Sixteen first appeared in the audiobook ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in [[2019]]. As ''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' was released as an audio-only story, the spelling of Aitch-Sixteen's name was unconfirmed<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> until it was given in the [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script release]] of the audiobook.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" />
