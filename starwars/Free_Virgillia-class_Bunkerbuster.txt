The '''''Free Virgillia''-class Bunkerbuster''', also known as the '''Bunker Buster''', the '''Resistance Bunkerbuster''', or the '''Resistance cruiser''', was a type of [[corvette]] model constructed by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]] and used during the [[First Order-Resistance War]] by the [[Resistance]]. [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] commanded a Bunker Buster known as the ''[[Ninka]]'' for the [[Resistance]].

==Characteristics==
The ''Free Virgillia'' was a class of corvette. It was armed with powerful bunker buster [[plasma bomb]]s, [[turbolaser]] [[turret]]s that had dedicated power outputs, and a complement of [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|bomber craft]] or a [[squadron]] of [[A-wing starfighter]]s that could dock on its reinforced pylons.<ref name="TLJ VD" />

==History==
[[File:Ninka_DB.png|thumb|250px|left|The ''Ninka'' was destroyed at the Battle of Oetchi.]]
The Resistance used the bunkerbuster ''[[Ninka]]'' commanded by [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] in [[34 ABY]]. The ''Ninka'' served as an escort for the Resistance [[flagship]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] during the [[Battle of Oetchi]], where it was destroyed when it ran out of [[fuel]] and fell into range of the pursuing ''[[Supremacy]]''.<ref name="TLJ" />

Many ''Free Virgillia''-class corvettes responded to [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian's]] call to action against the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]], and fought with the [[Citizens' Fleet]] during the [[Battle of Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Free Virgillia''-class Bunkerbuster first appeared in the the [[2017]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' as part of the ''The Last Jedi'' season.<ref name="Battlefront" />
