An '''animal export business''' was run by the [[Hutt]] [[Cedraa]] of the [[Hutt Clan]]<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> by [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]]. The business [[transport]]ed creatures such as [[bantha]]s and [[Gelagrub|Felucian ground beetles]] in shipping crates.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> while the Hutt [[Churo]]&mdash;Cedraa's cousin&mdash;was at a [[Nihil]] [[Unidentified factory (Mynos III)|factory]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Mynos III]], he spotted a shipping container holding hostages that he mentally noted was the kind his cousin used for his animal export business.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cedraa's animal export business was mentioned in the [[2024]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=SWCE 2023: 9 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref>
