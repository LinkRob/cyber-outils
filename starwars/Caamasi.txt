The '''Caamasi'''<ref name="Path of Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref> were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]]. They were covered in [[Color|grey]]<ref name="Repair" /> or brown fur, and had two pointed ears and a snout.<ref name="Cassian & K-2SO" /> Like the [[Alderaanian]]s, they were noted for their pacifism.<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> The species shared its name with the [[Caamasi cheese pie]]s<ref name="Bounty Hunters 22">[[Bounty Hunters 22|''Bounty Hunters'' 22]]</ref> and the [[Caamasi light sculpture]]s.<ref name="Fake It Till You Make It">{{StoryCite|story=Fake It Till You Make It|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> The Caamasi [[Jedi Master]] [[Ela Sutan]] was a member of the [[Jedi High Council]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Path of Vengeance" />

==Behind the scenes==
Caamasi first appeared in the [[Han Solo 1|first issue]] of the [[canon]]ical [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo]]'',<ref name="Han 1">[[Han Solo 1|''Han Solo'' 1]]</ref> written by [[Marjorie Liu]], illustrated by [[Mark Brooks]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[June 15]], [[2016]].<ref name="Han 1 Release">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/52695/han_solo_2016_1|text=Han Solo (2016) #1}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Caamasi/Legends|Caamasi]] originated in the [[1997]] novel ''[[Specter of the Past]]'', written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Specter of the Past">''[[Specter of the Past]]''</ref>
