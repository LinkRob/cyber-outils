The '''"Firecaller" light flame projector''' was a type of [[flamethrower]] manufactured by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]] that was banned throughout most of civilized [[Realspace|space]]. Such weapons had a very limited [[fuel]] supply, but the Firecaller relied on [[accelerite]], an unstable [[starship]] fuel ingredient that burned hotter and longer than most substances. However, this substance was easily acquired around [[shipyard]]s and fuel depots. Accelerite enabled the weapon to spray far less fuel than most projectors per burst, as the flames quickly ignited surrounding materials. Favored by the vilest of beings, the Firecaller generated intense heat that caused terrible pain and horrible disfigurements for those who survive.<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Firecaller" light flame projector was mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets" />
