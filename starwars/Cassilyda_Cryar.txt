'''Cassilyda "Cassie" Cryar''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Terrelian Jango Jumper]] criminal who lived on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. She and her secret lover, [[Ione Marcy]], murdered Marcy's partner and Cryar's employer, [[Nack Movers]]. Subsequently, Cryar briefly took possession of [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]'s [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|stolen lightsaber]], and attempted to [[Lightsaber combat|wield]] it against the Jedi before being brought to justice.

==Biography==
Cassilyda "Cassie" Cryar, a [[Gender|female]] [[Terrelian Jango Jumper]]<ref name="Databank" /> from [[Terrelia]],<ref name="JtHaOBHaC" /> operated as a [[thief]] in the [[Coruscant Underworld|underworld]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> She was hired as an [[Aide|assistant]] and [[bodyguard]] by [[Nack Movers]], a [[Trandoshan]] [[Assassin|hitman]] whom the [[Coruscant Security Force]] had tried and failed to bring to justice for his string of murders using [[Sennari]] [[poison]]. He lived in an [[Nack Movers's residence|apartment]] with his girlfriend,<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> a [[Salenga]] criminal<ref name="Join the Battle" /> named [[Ione Marcy]]. Despite Movers' paranoia, he failed to notice Marcy and Cryar grow close to each other and become romantic partners themselves. Ignoring Marcy's entreaties to him, the lecherous Movers propositioned Cryar. The two women subsequently plotted to kill him.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

[[File:MoversCryarMarcy.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Nack Movers failed to notice that Ione Marcy and Cassilyda Cryar had grown close and were plotting to poison him.]]
Sometime after Movers had acquired [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]'s stolen [[lightsaber]],<ref name="VE" /> his [[Foodstuff|drink]] was poisoned by Cryar and Marcy using the same poison he used on his targets. As he convulsed and died, his [[claw]]s raked across a painting of the three beings together.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> While Cryar and Marcy were still in Movers' apartment after ransacking it, they were discovered by Tano and [[Jedi Master]] [[Tera Sinube]], who had questioned the thief [[Bannamu]] after he had stolen and sold [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|Tano's lightsaber]].<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" /> Using her natural agility,<ref name="VE" /> Cryar escaped the apartment with the lightsaber, but she was pursued across the [[Skyscraper|skyline]] by Tano. Marcy was able to escape the apartment as well when the police arrived and take her [[speeder]] to reunite with Cryar. They fled to a [[train station]], and Cryar took hostages as Tano pursued her across a [[hovertrain]]. After the train stopped, Cryar attempted to use Tano's lightsaber, but the weapon was easily disarmed by Sinube. The couple was captured by the Jedi and placed under arrest by the [[GU-series Guardian police droid]]s.<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" />

==Personality and traits==
Cassilyda Cryar was a female Terrelian Jango Jumper who was 1.82 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]]) tall,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> with [[Color|red]] [[hair]], tied atop her head in a topknot,<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" /> had red [[eye]]s<ref name="Databank" /> and turquoise-blue skin. Her body was also long-[[limb]]ed.<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" /> 

Cryar was known as a thief and she stole Ahsoka Tano's lightsaber.<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" /> She also was a bodyguard to Nack Movers. While working as hired muscle for Movers, she became a romantic partner to Movers's girlfriend Ione Marcy. The two women worked together to murder Movers after the Trandoshan made lecherous moves on Cryar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> 

As a Terrelian Jango Jumper, Cryar had impressive physical abilities that made her exceptionally agile. She could leap vast distances comparable to a Jedi's [[Force jump]]s, despite not being [[Force-sensitive]]. Cryar also believed that with her species' abilities, coupled with her use of Tano's stolen lightsaber, she was just as powerful as any Jedi. Cryar was bold, and unafraid of falling from the great heights of Coruscant's rooftops, but was also prone to acting without thinking, evident when she threatened Tano with the Padawan's lightsaber without any training in how to operate the weapon, or even the knowledge of how to ignite the blade. She also had a tendency to swing the stolen lightsaber carelessly at surrounding objects and people, without realizing the damage she could do to herself or the other people in the vicinity. Cryar also took a pair of hostages aboard a moving hover train when Tano confronted her, as Cryar became desperate with nowhere else to run.<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" />

==Skills and abilities==
Cryar's exceptional speed and agility<ref name="Join the Battle" /> made it hard for even Ahsoka Tano to keep up with her pace.<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" /> She was also skilled in armed and unarmed combat,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> and was able to figure out to ignite the blade of Tano's lightsaber as well as block a few strikes from [[Tera Sinube's lightsaber|Sinube's lightsaber]], before Sinube disarmed her.<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Cassie is very mischievous and strong, but I also like that she's not sure what she's doing once she gets the lightsaber. She's not necessarily all bad; she's doing what she has to do in order to survive. She's also quite charming in a way. She has this acrobatic, beautiful way of moving as well, especially during the chase scenes with Ahsoka.|Jaime King|<ref name="Thick as Thieves">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/news20100122.html|text=Jaime King and Meredith Salenger: Thick as Thieves}}</ref>}}
[[File:CassieCryarconcept.jpg|thumb|right|150px|Concept art of Cassie Cryar]]
Cassie Cryar appeared in "[[Lightsaber Lost]]," an episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series that was part of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season's]] ''Rise of the Bounty Hunters'' storyline in [[2010]]. She was voiced by [[Jaime King]], who was drawn to the role for Cryar's strength. She doesn't consider Cryar to be a bad person, believing that she is only doing what she needs to do to survive.<ref name="Thick as Thieves" />

Concept art of Cassie Cryar was done by [[Randy Bantog]]<ref name="Art of TCW">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''</ref> and her design harkens back to one of [[Iain McCaig]]'s early [[Sith]] concepts for the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="Sith Inspiration">{{SW|url=news/back-from-the-drawing-board-recycled-star-wars-aliens|text=Back from the Drawing Board: Recycled ''Star Wars'' Aliens|int=Back from the Drawing Board|nolive=1}}</ref>

In the years after "Lightsaber Lost" aired, some viewers posted in venues such as the [[Jedi Council Forums]] that there appeared to be romantic subtext between Cassie Cryar and Ione Marcy.<ref name="JCFCite">{{JCFCite|url=posts/31842778|board=Star Wars TV- Completed Shows|thread=Grade the Episode: 211: Lightsaber Lost|quote=Kudos to the TCW team for at least implying a lesbian relationship (it's a little unclear), we definitely haven't seen that before, and I'm glad they don't beat us over the head with how "thoughtful" they are to do so; you would think in 2011 it wouldn't be an issue at all to have a lesbian couple in Star Wars.|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20170803190829/https://boards.theforce.net/threads/grade-the-episode-211-lightsaber-lost.31841297}}</ref> [[Pablo Hidalgo]] mentioned via his [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] account in [[2016]] that they were written to be a couple.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/703684857121996800|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2016-02-27|quote=For those talking about the subject, note that Cassie Cryar and Ione Marcy were written as a couple in a relationship.|archivefile=File:CassieCryar_IoneMarcy_PabloHidalgo_tweet.png|nolive=1}}</ref> He subsequently wrote the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', which made this detail more apparent and gave Cryar the full first name "Cassilyda."<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />
