'''Admiral Utu's ship''' was a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] under the command of [[Imperial Navy]] [[Admiral]] [[Utu]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. The [[Star Destroyer]] was part of Utu's [[Telos battle group|battle group]] at [[Telos]], and was deployed along with Utu to serve under [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] and his [[Tarkin's task force|task force]] during the [[Salient Campaign]].<ref name="Catalyst" />

==Behind the scenes==
Admiral Utu's ship first appeared in ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'', a [[2016]] novel by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Catalyst" />
