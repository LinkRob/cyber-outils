'''Baltizaar''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Leia" /> in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Corthenia sector]], located within the [[Baltizaar system]]. It was situated in grid square K-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and connected to a [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="Leia" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]].</ref> [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] traveled to the [[planet]] [[Paucris Major]] from the [[Core Worlds]] planet [[Coruscant]] to [[Evacuation of Paucris Major|warn]] a nascent [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[fleet]] in the [[Paucris system]] of an impending [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] attack. After passing on her message, Organa decided to plot a [[hyperspace]] course to Baltizaar and travel back to Coruscant from there. Her travel companion [[Amilyn Holdo]] agreed with the detour, alluding that breaking up their route would make it harder for Imperials to track where they had been. However, before the pair could depart for Baltizaar, Organa discovered that fellow [[Apprentice Legislator]] [[Kier Domadi]] had followed her to the Paucris [[Star system|system]] aboard a [[Starcutter|cutter]] and moved to make contact with his [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="Leia" />
