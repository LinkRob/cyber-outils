A highly evolved type of [[droid]] that adapted organic material,<ref name="Twit 1">{{Twitter|TheVitaminJay|status/817839900804268033|[[Jay Doherty]]|quote=@LelalMekha This was going to be a new race in SW lore. At least one wore the [[clothing]] of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[officer]].<ref name="Image" />

Character artist [[Jay Doherty]] designed the "Banannaman" for use in the [[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'',<ref name="Twit 2">{{Twitter|TheVitaminJay|status/796588104849195008|[[Jay Doherty]]|quote=Star Wars: Uprising: Part 2|archiveurl=https://archive.is/jJAIB}}</ref> which was developed by [[Kabam]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms,<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> and released on [[September 10]], [[2015]].<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-uprising-available-now|text=''Star Wars: Uprising'' Available Now}}</ref> However, the concept was never approved or added to the game<ref name="Twit 3" /> prior to its shutdown in [[2016]].<ref name="Shutting down">{{Kabam|subdomain=community|url=forums/showthread.php?675751-Star-Wars-Uprising-is-Shutting-Down|text=Discussion Thread: Star Wars Uprising is Shutting Down|date=2016-09-22}}</ref>
