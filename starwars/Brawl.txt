A '''Brawl''' was a term given to a [[Navy|naval]] battle tactic whose employment was made inevitable when one or both sides were in disarray. During a Brawl, each individual [[captain]] had authority&mdash;rather than an [[admiral]] or [[Fleet Commander]]&mdash;and sought to do as much damage to the opposition as possible for as long as possible. Charging in, guns blazing, tended to be a last, desperate act.<ref name="BMF 89" />

However, a Brawl could be misconstrued for a [[Fake/Canon|Fake]], a tactic that saw [[fleet]] groups charged the enemy in apparent disarray. At a precise point of [[time]] and, if successful, the fleet would suddenly pull around hard to present unified broadsides to the enemy. They could proceed to [[Capping the "T"/Canon|Cap the "T"]] if possible and even finish by swinging around to flank the last ships in the enemy battle line.<ref name="BMF 89" />

One the most notable fleet Brawls in naval history was at the [[Battle of Coruscant]] during the final year of the [[Clone Wars]]. The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|fleet]] that made a surprise attack on the [[Galactic Republic]] [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]] destroyed many [[Star Destroyer]]s. In response, the Republic's [[commander]]s rallied together and employed a Brawl, which exposed the weaknesses of the slow-to-react [[droid]] captains,<ref name="BMF 89" /> resulting in a Republic victory.<ref name="Episode III" />
