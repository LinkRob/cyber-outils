'''Bulduga''' was half of a [[Gender|male]] [[Ithorian]] [[bounty hunter]] duo, alongside his brother, [[Onca]]. While Onca preferred long-range weapons, such as rifles, Bulduga preferred the quick-and-easily-accessible [[blaster pistol]]s. Well-known throughout [[the galaxy]], the two brothers gained the attention of [[Count]] [[Dooku]], leader of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]], and was given the challenge of [[Tournament on Serenno|winning]] the [[Box]], a lethal bounty hunter competition. After gathering with Dooku's other assembled bounty hunters, Bulduga was [[Death|killed]] by [[Cad Bane]] over a dispute regarding the Ithorian's wide-brimmed [[fedora]]. Onca was left to compete on his own, but died during the second challenge.

==Biography==
===Brothers in arms===
[[File:OncaBulduga-TB.png|thumb|left|250px|Bulduga and Onca, a legendary bounty hunting pair]]
Bulduga was an [[Ithorian]] [[Gender|male]] who, alongside his brother [[Onca]], operated as a [[bounty hunter]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The two became regarded as a legendary duo.<ref name="TB" /> Preferring quick-draw [[blaster pistol]]s over Onca's preference for long-range [[Blaster rifle|rifles]], Bulduga was able to convince many targets that not all Ithorians fit the peace-loving agriculturist stereotype.<ref name="Databank" />

Bulduga's choice of weapons, coupled by his low-hanging holster, red-tinted [[goggles]], and wide-brimmed [[Fedora|hat]] caused bystanders to theorize that the Ithorian was nothing more than an imitator of the well-known bounty hunter [[Cad Bane]], although no one would say such within earshot of the deadly Ithorian.<ref name="Databank" /> Eventually, Bulduga and Onca attracted the attention of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]], who was in league with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The Count invited the hunters to the [[planet]] [[Serenno]] to compete in a lethal [[Tournament on Serenno|tournament]] called the [[Box]].<ref name="TB" />

===Dooku's challenge===
{{Quote|Nice hat. Where did you get it?|Cad Bane, shortly before killing Bulduga|audio=NiceHat-TB.flac|<ref name="TB" />}}
[[File:Bulduga_Loses.png|thumb|right|250px|Bulduga was gunned down by Cad Bane over the matter of a hat.]]
In preparation for a risky [[Plan to kidnap Chancellor Palpatine|mission]] to kidnap the [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], Dooku invited a posse of bounty hunters to his [[homeworld]] in order to test their mettle in a series of challenges designed by criminal mastermind [[Moralo Eval]]. Those who survived the challenges would take part in Dooku's operation and receive a hefty reward upon the job's completion.<ref name="TB" />

Bulduga and Onca assembled outside of the Box shortly before the tournament and came face to face with their competition, including bounty hunters [[Sixtat]], [[Sinrich]], [[Jakoli]], [[Twazzi]], [[Kiera Swan]], [[Derrown]], [[Mantu]], [[Rako Hardeen]], and [[Cad Bane]]. Before being admitted into the Box, Bane approached Bulduga, commenting that he liked his hat. Bulduga, feeling threatened, drew his [[blaster pistol]] but was outgunned by the faster [[Duros]] bounty hunter and was [[Death|killed]]. Bane took Bulduga's fedora for himself.<ref name="TB" />

Onca, who was given little sympathy, was forced to compete in the Box alone and died during the second challenge.<ref name="TB" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:Bulduga-TCWCEJtB.png|thumb||left|250px|Bulduga preferred quick draw blasters and a wide-brimmed hat.]]
Bulduga was a tall, thin Ithorian who was 2.27 [[meter]]s, or 7 [[Foot|feet]], 5 inches tall.<ref name="Join the Battle" />. Bulduga had brown-[[color]]ed skin.<ref name="Databank" />

Although many galactic citizens believed Ithorians to be being of peace, Bulduga was quite the opposite, being a dangerous bounty hunter.<ref name="Databank" /> Unlike his brother, Bulduga was able to overcome his species' natural bulky build and became a fast-draw [[gunslinger]].<ref name="Databank2">{{Databank|bulduga|Bulduga}}</ref> However, due to his fashion and weapon choices, some theorized that Bulduga attempted to imitate the bounty hunter Cad Bane.<ref name="Databank" /> Upon being approached by Bane, Bulduga became defensive and instinctively reached for his guns. Despite being renowned for his quick draw, Bane was faster.<ref name="TB" /> Bane then took Bulduga's fedora.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Skills and abilities==
Bulduga was known for his skills in armed combat and tracking.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Equipment==
Bulduga carried a [[Penetrator MB-450/Canon|Penetrator MB-450]] blaster pistol in a low slung quick-draw [[holster]] and fashioned himself with a black vest and a wide-brimmed hat. He also wore red-tinted goggles over his [[eye]]s that glowed in the dark.<ref name="TB" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bulduga first appeared in the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars television series'']] episode "[[The Box]]," which aired as a part of the show's [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|fourth season]].
