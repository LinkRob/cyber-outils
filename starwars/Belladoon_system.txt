The '''Belladoon system''' was located in the [[Myto sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> Situated at the coordinates K-3 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> it lay along the [[Veragi Trade Route]]<ref name="Veragi" /> between the [[Dantooine system|Dantooine]] and [[Dubrillion system|Dubrillion systems]].<ref name="Galaxy">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> The [[star system]] contained the [[planet]] [[Belladoon]], which was under the [[Galactic Empire]]'s sway<ref name="LD" /> by [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Belladoon system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix released alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]<ref name="Systems" />&mdash;both works having been written by [[Jason Fry]] and published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
