The '''Bothan Museum heist''' was a [[Thief|heist]] in a [[Bothan]] [[museum]] that was accredited to the [[Gender|female]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Kendoh Voss]]. In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date Note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> during the [[Battle of Batuu]], Voss was accused of being behind the heist by a [[First Order]] [[Unidentified First Order stormtrooper sergeant|stormtrooper sergeant]], while on a mission to steal the hilt of the [[Sword of Khashyun]] from [[collector]] [[Dok-Ondar]]. Voss claimed that the culprit had never officially been found.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 5" />
