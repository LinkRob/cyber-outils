"'''Creature Comfort'''" is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story in ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures Volume 7]]''. It features [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]].

==Plot summary==
On a heavily forested planet, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] are confronted by a [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droid]] fleeing from something. Instead of fighting, the [[Droid/Legends|droid]] recommends that the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] flee, as well. Picking a safe vantage point atop a large tree, the two Jedi leap to apparent safety. They are almost unnoticed when a large creature turns around and begins headbutting the tree. The Jedi flee and are soon safely across a ravine...only to find themselves trapped with an even larger beast. Before they can be consumed by this monstrosity, a large raptor picks them up, taking them to its nest. Flying over the sea, a large sea creature ascends out of the water to devour the bird. Skywalker and Kenobi escape from the talons and drop into the ocean, where Kenobi is promptly seized by a large clam-like creature with tentacles. Skywalker ignites his [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] and rescues his former Master, dragging him onto shore. The two are once again confronted by a creature&mdash;a small yet ferocious crab which Kenobi quickly steps on. Finally able to catch their breath, the Jedi jump in shock when the same battle droid runs across the beach, warning them once again to run.

==Continuity==
"Creature Comfort" is misspelled "Creature Comforts" in the table of contents of ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures Volume 7]]''.
