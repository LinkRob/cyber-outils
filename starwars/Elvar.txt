'''Elvar''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who lived during the [[Imperial Era]] and was the son of [[Karyn Sitra]], a [[human]] [[pilot]] who served as part of the [[Bespin Wing Guard|security]] of [[Cloud City]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Star Wars 3" /> [[gas giant]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Bespin]].<ref name="Star Wars 3" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars (2020) 3|''Star Wars'' (2020) 3]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Elvar was young.<ref name="Star Wars 3" />

When former Cloud City [[Baron Administrator]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] [[Mission to Cloud City|traveled]] back to the [[city]]<ref name="Star Wars 3" /> that [[Standard year|year]]<ref name="Date" /> with [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] members [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Luke Skywalker]], Sitra was among the pilots sent after the [[Starship|ship]] they were traveling in. After Sitra instructed the ship to either transmit clearance codes or depart, Calrissian revealed his presence and asked who he was speaking with. In response, Sitra identified themself to the former Baron Administrator, questioning if it was really him. Calrissian replied by stating it was him and asking Sitra about their son, questioning if the boy was named Elvar, which the pilot confirmed.<ref name="Star Wars 3" />
