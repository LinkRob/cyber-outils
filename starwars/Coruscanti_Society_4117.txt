'''Coruscanti Society #4117''' was an issue of the periodical publication ''[[Coruscanti Society]]'' which detailed the social life of [[Coruscant]]. The "Profile" section of this issue focused on "[[Lady]]" [[Ederlatth Nataasias Pallopides]], a Coruscanti socialite who claimed to be former [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]'s daughter and therefore claimant to the Imperial throne. The issue was used by [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] as a source in his [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' and mentioned in the chapter "Fracture and Cinder".<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Coruscanti Society 4117 was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]], which was published by [[Dorling Kindersley]] in [[July 9]] [[2024]].<ref name="Rise" />
