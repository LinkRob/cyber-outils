'''Asami Seto''' (瀬戸麻沙美) is a [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] voice actress<ref name="JP" /> who voiced [[F]] in the Japanese version of "[[The Village Bride]]," a short film that is part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 1|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]''.<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-trailer-and-cast|text=Stunning New ''Star Wars: Visions'' Trailer Debuts}}</ref> Seto is better known in Japan as the voice actress for Mai Sakurajima in the [[Wikipedia:Rascal Does Not Dream|Rascal Does Not Dream]] series, Chihaya Ayase in [[Wikipedia:Chihayafuru|Chihayafuru]] and Nobara Kugisaki in [[Wikipedia:Jujutsu Kaisen|Jujutsu Kaisen]].<ref name="JP" />
