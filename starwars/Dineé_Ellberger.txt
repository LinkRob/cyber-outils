'''Dineé Ellberger'''<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> was a [[human]] [[Naboo Fighter Pilot|pilot]] who flew an [[N-1 starfighter]] as part of [[Bravo Squadron]],<ref name="Episode I" /> specifically serving as [[Bravo Five]],<ref name="X-Wing">{{FFGXW2|set=Naboo Royal N-1 Starfighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Dineé Ellberger}}</ref> during the [[Invasion of Naboo|Invasion]] of [[Naboo]].<ref name="Episode I" />

As a [[pilot]] of the [[Naboo Royal Space Fighter Corps]], Ellberger wore a pilot uniform consisting of a space fighter corps [[Jacket|overcoat]], a [[flight jacket]] and [[glove]]s, and pilot-issue [[boot]]s.<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" />
