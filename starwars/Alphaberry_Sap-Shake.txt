'''Alphaberry Sap-Shakes''' were a [[Color|green]]<ref name="The Junk Giant" /> [[sap-shake]]<ref name="Battle for the Band" /> beverage with a multicolored garnish on top. Potable to [[human]]s, [[Pantoran]]s,<ref name="The Junk Giant" /> and [[Mon Calamari]],<ref name="Bounty Hunter and The Thief" /> the [[Restaurant|eatery]] [[Hap's Sap Tap]] on<ref name="The Junk Giant" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="TYJ">{{YJA|The Young Jedi}}</ref> [[Tenoo]] served them.<ref name="The Junk Giant" /> Around [[233 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|1}}</ref> [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Kai Brightstar]] and [[Lys Solay]] ordered Alphaberry Sap-Shakes, which [[Hap]], the owner, served to them. After sipping his drink, Brightstar described it as "amazing," a compliment that Hap appreciated.<ref name="The Junk Giant" />

Around the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the beverages were served by [[Merchant|vendors]] providing refreshments for the concert of the [[Ku-Bops]] [[band]] in Tenoo's [[Kublop Springs]].<ref name="The Show Must Go On">{{YJA|The Show Must Go On}}</ref> Later, in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">{{YJADate|206A}}</ref> one of the shakes was served in Hap's Sap Tap during a performance of the band [[Feb Rozo and the Sap Tap Trio]].<ref name="Battle for the Band" /> Another time on Tenoo, a [[Unidentified Mon Calamari youngling's sister|Mon Calamari youngling]] enjoyed an Alphaberry Sap-Shake while sitting on<ref name="Bounty Hunter and The Thief" /> her<ref name="The Missing Kibbin">{{YJA|The Missing Kibbin}}</ref> [[speeder]].<ref name="Bounty Hunter and The Thief" />
