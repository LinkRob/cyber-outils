'''Devi''' was a [[humanoid]] associate of the [[Devaronian]] criminal [[Coir Cion]] on the [[moon of Dawk]].<ref name="DM-BW&R 3" /> Between around [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> when the [[Sith apprentice]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]] [[Mission to the moon of Dawk|arrived]] on the [[moon]] to [[Death|kill]] Cion, Devi and a [[Unidentified Duros (moon of Dawk)|Duros]] attempted to flee the [[Korts Cantina]]. However, Maul entered the [[cantina]] and killed the pair before defeating the rest of Cion's associates and then the Devaronian himself.<ref name="DM-BW&R 3" />
