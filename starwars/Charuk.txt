'''Charuk''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who lived on [[Golak]]. He had a younger sister named [[Rani]] who was [[Force-sensitive]]. Sometime between [[18 BBY]] and [[3 BBY]], Charuk and Rani traveled to the [[Golak Mahal]] and met the hiding [[Jedi]] [[Rugal]].<ref name="Golak" />

==Biography==
[[File:CharukRani-SWVisionsVol2.png|thumb|left|250px|Charuk cared for his younger sister, Rani.]]
Sometime between [[18 BBY]] and [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{VisionsDate|207}}</ref> Charuk lived on the planet [[Golak]] with his younger sister [[Rani]]. At this point, Golak was under the control of the [[Galactic Empire]], forcing Charuk and his sister to flee from their home. After hearing about a place known as the [[Golak Mahal]] from their [[Rani and Charuk's pa|father]], Charuk and Rani boarded a [[train]]. While on the train, Rani asked if she could play her dad's [[flute]], in which Charuk said no. Feeling hungry, Rani asked Charuk if she could eat some [[Le'bi]] when she saw a vendor selling the sweet on the train. Before Charuk could reach the vendor, Rani used her [[Force power]] to take her Dad's flute from Charuk revealing her [[Force-sensitive|Force-sensitivity]] in the process. While Charuk was waiting in line to get Le'bi, a [[scavenger]] approached a [[Stormtrooper]] and told him that there was a girl with special powers. Upon overhearing this, Charuk stole some Le'bi in order to divert attention away from Rani, leading to him getting pursued by two Stormtroopers. Charuk managed to evade the Stormtroopers by climbing to the top of the train. During this situation, the train entered the [[Jangori settlements]] and called for reinforcements. The train was then attacked by Jangorian bandits. While the bandits were attacking the Imperial speeder bikes, Charuk attempted to find a hatch eventually found one, though it was sealed. Before Charuk could get shot, Rani managed to open the seal allowing Charuk to re-enter the train before it passed a tunnel exiting the Jangori settlements.<ref name="Golak" />

Charuk and Rani then managed to reach their [[City|stop]] and traveled to the Golak Mahal. Upon reaching the place, the pair were greeted by [[Rugal]] who allowed them to eat there. Before Rani and Charuk could eat some Le'bi, an [[Unidentified Inquisitor (Golak)|Inquisitor]] and two [[Purge Trooper]]s in [[Phase I Purge Trooper armor|Phase I armor]] arrived. The Inquisitor then questioned Charuk about the reports of a force-sensitive girl on Golak. In an attempt to toy with him the Inquisitor added that he went beyond the train tracks, burned all the farm villages, and left no survivors. Sensing that Rani was the force-sensitive girl, the Inquisitor [[force push]]ed Charuk aside. He then attempted to capture Rani resulting in a frenzy where the patrons of the Golak Mahal started fighting one another. Before the Inquisitor could capture Rani, Rugal revealed herself as a [[Jedi]] to protect Rani and Charuk. She then dueled against the Inquisitor and [[Death|killed]] him by slicing his throat. After the Inquisitor died, Rugal guided Rani to a tunnel hidden in a body of [[water]]. As Rugal told Charuk that Rani would be safer with her, Charuk said his last goodbyes to Rani before she entered the tunnel. After Rani entered the tunnel which closed immediately and became submerged in the body of water, Charuk's father's flute floated to the surface of the water. Charuk kept the flute as a way to remember his sister.<ref name="Golak" />
