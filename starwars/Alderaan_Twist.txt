An '''Alderaan Twist''' was a [[Color|light copper]]-colored beverage [[Diet|potable]] by [[human]]s that could be found on<ref name="Annual 1" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. It could be served with a green garnish in a [[cocktail]] [[glass]]<ref name="Annual 1" /> and shared its name with the Core Worlds planet [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' places the [[Arrth-Eno Mission]], as depicted in [[Star Wars Annual (2015) 1|''Star Wars Annual'' (2015) 1]], between the [[mission to rescue the survivors of Alderaan]] and the [[Battle of Vrogas Vas]], which corresponds to [[0 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> after returning to his [[Eneb Ray's apartment|Coruscant apartment]], [[Eneb Ray]]&mdash;a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[spy]] serving undercover in the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[tax collector]]&mdash;asked his [[Eneb Ray's protocol droid|protocol droid]] to bring him an Alderaan Twist. As Ray began to speak with rebel [[Princess]]<ref name="Annual 1" /> [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]],<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> the [[protocol droid]] arrived with his drink, which the spy took and drank as he continued his conversation with Organa.<ref name="Annual 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
An Alderaan Twist appeared in the [[Star Wars Annual (2015) 1|first annual issue]] in the [[2015]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'']]. The issue was written by [[Kieron Gillen]], illustrated by [[Angel Unzueta]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Annual 1" /> on [[December 9]], 2015.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/58105/star_wars_annual_2015_1|text=Star Wars Annual (2015) #1}}</ref>
