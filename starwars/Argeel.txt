'''Argeel''' was a [[Terionese]] [[Gender|man]] who lived in the [[City|settlement]] of [[Strop's End]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Teriona]]. Between around [[234 BBY]] and [[232 BBY]], they appreciated the efforts of [[Jedi Master]] [[Loden Greatstorm]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Bell Zettifar]], to [[Mission to Teriona|help]] the community of Strop's End. Led by the [[teacher]] [[Hennah]], they and several other Terionese gave them gift baskets and informed the pair of their intent to tell other settlements about the peaceful way of life the [[Jedi]] had introduced them to, compared to the planet's usual state of strife.

==Biography==
{{Quote|We're going to visit a few other settlements near here, talk to them about how we've been living. Not sure we'd have seen it if you two hadn't come around. Be well, Jedi.|Hennah to Loden Greatstorm and Bell Zettifar, on behalf of Argeel and a group of Terionese|<ref name="Tales" />}}
[[File:BellLoden-CharhoundChase.png|thumb|right|240px|Argeel appreciated Loden Greatstorm and Bell Zettifar's efforts to help the Strop's End community.]]
The [[Terionese]] [[Gender|man]] Argeel lived in the [[Strop's End]] [[City|settlement]] on their [[homeworld]],<ref name="Tales" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="AcolyteVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Teriona system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Teriona]], during the [[High Republic Era]]. By that [[time]], Teriona was in a constant state of conflict, and they became well accustomed to war and its accompanying local skirmishes and battles as part of life. At some point, Argeel became familiar with the settlement's local [[Unidentified tavern (Strop's End)|tavern]].<ref name="Tales" />

Between around [[234 BBY]] and [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The flashback events of "[[All Jedi Walk Their Own Path]]" take place after [[Lina Soh]] [[Supreme Chancellor election, 234 BBY|became]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] and prior to the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], which equates to between around [[234 BBY]] and [[232 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Loden Greatstorm]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Bell Zettifar]], visited Teriona on a [[Mission to Teriona|mission]] and repaired the roof of a [[Cantina|tavern]] in Strop's End. Though initially wary of the offworlders, the man eventually appreciated the [[Jedi]] as they continued to help several people within the community and promote a peaceful way of life. Led by the [[teacher]] [[Hennah]], Argeel and some Strop's End residents visited Greatstorm and Zettifar to offer them baskets of [[Foodstuff|food]] and beverages as a gift. Hennah informed the Jedi that their group intended to let other nearby settlements know about how the Jedi had taught them to live&mdash;without as much strife&mdash;and Argeel indicated that they were grateful the pair had fixed the local tavern's roof.<ref name="Tales" />

==Personality and traits==
As a Terionese native, Argeel was used to reprisals and violence as part of everyday life. However, they eventually came to enjoy the peace the Jedi brought to Teriona and were willing to follow Hennah to give the pair gifts as thanks.<ref name="Tales" />

==Behind the scenes==
Argeel appeared in a flashback in the short story "[[All Jedi Walk Their Own Path]]," written by [[Charles Soule]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Tales" />
