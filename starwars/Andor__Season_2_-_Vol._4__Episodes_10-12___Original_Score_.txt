'''''Andor'': Season 2 &ndash; Vol. 4 (Episodes 10-12) [Original Score]''' is the soundtrack for the tenth to twelfth episodes of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'': "[[Make It Stop]]," "[[Who Else Knows?]]," and "[[Jedha, Kyber, Erso]]." It was composed by [[Brandon Roberts]] and [[Nicholas Britell]] and released for digital streaming on [[May 16]], [[2025]].
