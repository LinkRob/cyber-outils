'''227 BBY''', or '''227<ref name="Date" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> that was part of the [[High Republic Era]]. That year,<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Yarzion Vell]] told his [[Jedi Master]], [[Kelnacca]], about the meaning of his [[tattoo]]s.<ref name="Kelnacca 1">[[The Acolyte - Kelnacca 1|''The Acolyte - Kelnacca'' 1]]</ref> Around that year,<ref name="Date" /> Vell was [[Knighting Ceremony|knighted]] after saving the [[Youngling|child]] [[Yesula]], her [[Yesula's father|father]], and their [[droid]] [[Zeekay]] from a [[fire]].<ref name="Kelnacca 1" />
