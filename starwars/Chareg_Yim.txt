'''Chareg Yim''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Jedi]] of the [[Jedi Order]]. On one occasion, Yim responded to a [[distress signal]] from a crew of [[Ugnaught]] lightning [[miner]]s on the [[planet]] [[Tish]], who were being attacked by [[Unidentified creature (Tish)|creatures]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the planet. On Tish, Yim discovered that the [[Species|creatures]] fed on the lightning that the [[mining]] platform was collecting, and told the Ugnaughts to release some of the energy so that the creatures could feed.<ref name="JTC-E8" />
