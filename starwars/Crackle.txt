The '''Crackle''' was a portion of the [[Starship Graveyard]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. Despite the ''[[X'us'R'iia]]'', Jakku's storms, reshaping the terrain, the Crackle was one of the few constants on the planet. The Crackle was formed from a [[capital ship]] which had crashed during the [[Battle of Jakku]], the massive impact leaving only the keel of the ship, nicknamed "the [[Spike (wreckage)|Spike]]," intact. The heat from the crash turned the sand into [[Color|blackened]] glass, which would "crackle" when stepped on&mdash;hence its name. The Crackle was located several [[kilometer]]s from [[Niima Outpost]] and could be reached by [[speeder]].<ref name="BTA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Crackle first appeared in ''[[Before the Awakening]]'', a junior novel written by [[Greg Rucka]] and published in [[2015]].<ref name="BTA" />
