The '''Crimson Dawn vault''' was an room in the [[Crimson Dawn District (Mirogana)|Crimson Dawn district]] in the [[capital]] [[city]] [[Mirogana]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]] that the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal [[syndicate]] kept valuables<ref name="Outlaws" /> during [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The vault could be opened by using three [[keycard]]s assigned to members of the Dawn which included [[Eleera Soi]], [[Zafi]], and [[Odana Campo]].<ref name="Outlaws" />  In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" />  the vault contained [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and the materials necessary to apply a "Crimson Reign" paint scheme to a [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]]. During the same [[Standard year|year]] the secure room just outside the vault contained [[ion energy converter]]s, a stockpile of [[Forger (profession)|counterfeit]] credits, a keycard to a storage room in [[Renpalli Station]] and a terminal containing information about Crimson Dawn.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
The [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] gained access to the secure room outside the vault<ref name="Outlaws" />  in 3 ABY.<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> During which she accessed the data terminal and learned the location of three keycards required to unlock the vault. After recovering the keycards, Vess returned to unlock the vault and steal credits and the speeder bike materials.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

During the same year,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> Vess was hired by a member of the [[Pyke Syndicate]] to investigate the appearance of counterfeit credits in the [[Mirogana Market]] before it was noticed by the [[Imperial Galactic Bank]]. After selling a number of items in the market and confronting the [[merchant]], Vess was directed to Crimson Dawn and confirmed the existence of a stockpile of counterfeit credits near the vault.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Crimson Dawn vault appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The vault was one of several in the game that the player could access after finding three cosponsoring keycards.<ref name="Outlaws" />
