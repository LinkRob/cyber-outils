The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''3 ABY''',<ref name="GA" /> also known as '''3280 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7980''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*The long lasting [[Koshagan uprising]] between the [[Koshaga's ruling class|Koshaga ruling class]] and [[Koshagan People's Movement]] is swiftly put to an end when the [[Galactic Empire]] intervenes, crushing the People's Movement.<ref name="Beyond Hope">{{StoryCite|story=Beyond Hope|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> {{Circa}}<ref name="KoshaganDate" />
**A [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] recruiter quietly arrives on [[Koshaga]] to recruit any surviving People's Movement fighters into [[the galaxy]]-wide struggle against the Empire.<ref name="Beyond Hope" /> {{Circa}}<ref name="KoshaganDate" />
*The Rebel Alliance's [[Echo Base|base]] on [[Hoth]] was [[Battle of Hoth|invaded]] and destroyed by the Empire, forcing the Rebels to evacuate.<ref name="ESB" />
*The Galactic Empire annexes [[Cloud City]].<ref name="ESB" />
*[[Kendy Idele]] joins the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>
*[[Han Solo]] was [[Carbon-freezing|encased]] in [[carbonite]] and delivered to [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] by [[Boba Fett]].<ref name="ESB" />
*[[Luke Skywalker]] began his [[Jedi training|training]] under [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] on [[Dagobah]].<ref name="ESB" />
*During a [[Duel on Cloud City|confrontation]] with [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on Cloud City, Luke Skywalker learns that the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] is his [[Parent|father]].<ref name="ESB" />
*Luke Skywalker, [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], [[Chewbacca]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[C-3PO]], and [[R2-D2]] are reunited with the Rebel forces and began their quest to rescue Han Solo from Jabba's [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="ESB" />
*[[Vendaxan land squid]] [[Reproduction|mating season]] occurs.<ref name="Darth Vader 2">[[Darth Vader (2020) 2|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 2]]</ref>
[[File:VaderStandsOnNaboo-2020DarthVader3.png|thumb|right|200px|Darth Vader returned to the planet Naboo in 3 ABY.]]
*Vader launches a quest to find who hid Luke Skywalker from him, only to instead reunite with [[Sabé]]<ref name="Darth Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2020) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 1]]</ref> and return to [[Naboo]], where he comes into conflict with Sabé and the [[Amidalans]].<ref name="Darth Vader 3">[[Darth Vader (2020) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 3]]</ref>
*After visiting the [[Tomb of Padmé Amidala]], Vader journeys to [[Polis Massa]] and hears the last words of [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], who died believing there was still good in Anakin Skywalker.<ref name="Darth Vader 5">[[Darth Vader (2020) 5|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 5]]</ref>

==Births==
*[[Belen Rakab]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]]. As ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' establishes that [[Julen Rakab]] was nineteen [[Standard year|years]] old, it can be deduced that he was [[Reproduction|born]] in [[15 ABY]]. The book also establishes that [[Belen Rakab]] was 12 years older than Julen, therefore we can deduce that Belen was born in 3 ABY.</ref>
