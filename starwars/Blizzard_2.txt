'''Blizzard 2''' was an [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] which served as [[Brigadier General]] [[Nevar]]'s [[walker]] during the [[Battle of Hoth]], as part of the [[Blizzard Force]], led by [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]].<ref name="AbEverything" /> The walker was destroyed by [[Wedge Antilles]] and [[Wes Janson]], when they used the cable of their [[T-47 airspeeder]] to tie up the legs of Blizzard 2, causing it to crash.<ref name="ESB" /><ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
