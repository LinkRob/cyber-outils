An '''agrirobot''',<ref name="SWHC1">{{HelmetCollectionCite|1|Databank A-Z}}</ref> also known as a '''agridroid''',<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref> or '''farm droid''',<ref name="The Harvest Feast">{{YJA|The Harvest Feast}}</ref> was an umbrella term for any [[droid]] or autonomous machine that was programmed to perform one function in the cycle of producing [[Foodstuff|food]], from preparing the land for crops to packaging products for shipping.<ref name="SWHC1" /> Farm droids were a bulky-type droid programmed to maintain labor around the farms for the crops and greenhouses.<ref name="Uprooted">{{YJA|Uprooted}}</ref>
