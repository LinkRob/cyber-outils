'''Bardram Scoft''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Unknown Regions]]. It was home to the [[Scofti]], whose [[Scoftic government|government]] changed frequently, often with a violent transition. On one occasion where the previous Scofti [[Unidentified Prefect|Prefect]] was [[Death|assassinated]], various outside groups, including the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], [[Mission to Bardram Scoft|sent]] emissaries to Bardram Scoft. While at Bardram Scoft, the [[Chiss]] [[Junior Commander]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Mitth'raw'nuru]] "Thrawn" learned that the [[Lioaoin Regime]] was supporting [[pirate]]s that had recently been attacking at the edges of the Ascendancy.

==Description==
{{Quote|The Lioaoin Regime will do for now. Suppose I wanted to approach from a different direction than the Ascendancy? Say, from here at Bardram Scoft?|Thrawn to Qilori, on travel to [[Lioaoin Space|Lioaoin space]]|<ref name="Chaos Rising" />}}
Located in the [[Unknown Regions]]&mdash;known to its inhabitants as the Chaos&mdash;Bardram Scoft was a [[planet]], situated in a relatively smooth area of the region. It was considered to be a backwater world by the [[Pathfinders (Navigators' Guild)|Pathfinder]] [[Qilori]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==History==
{{Quote|The Scoftic government has changed again, and with it the protocols. Our ambassador must relearn them.''"<br />"''Ah. I didn't know the old Prefect had been unwell.''"<br />"''He wasn't. He was assassinated.|Thrawn and Qilori|<ref name="Chaos Rising" />}}
Several [[Standard year|years]] prior to [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' establishes that the [[Mission to Bardram Scoft|mission]] to Bardram Scoft takes place shortly before the [[skirmish in the Kinoss system]]. That skirmish in turn occurs several [[Standard year|years]] prior to the main events of the novel, which take place in around 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the standing [[Unidentified Prefect|Prefect]] of Bardram Scoft's [[Scofti]] inhabitants was [[Death|assassinated]]. Though such an occurrence was not unheard of, the change in [[Scoftic government|government]] brought a change in protocols. Various nearby groups sent emissaries to Bardram Scoft to meet the new [[Prefect (Scoftic government)|Prefect]], including the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], which [[Mission to Bardram Scoft|dispatched]] a [[Cruiser|Chiss diplomatic cruiser]] carrying an [[Unidentified Chiss ambassador|ambassador]] to the planet.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

After collecting the Pathfinder [[navigator]] Qilori to make the trip to Bardram Scoft, the [[Chiss]] [[cruiser]] arrived at Bardram Scoft several [[Standard minute|minutes]] ahead of schedule. As Scoftic culture necessitated that the highest-ranking military [[officer]] accompany the [[ambassador]], a majority of the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] crew departed after the [[starship]] arrived, though [[Junior Commander]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Mitth'raw'nuru]] "Thrawn" remained behind with Qilori. Having noticed a vessel above Bardram Scoft that resembled those of [[pirate]]s that had struck at the Ascendancy's outer edges, Thrawn questioned Qilori on its design, learning it was [[Lioaoi]]n. Thrawn's discovery would lead him to deduce that the [[Lioaoin Regime]] had been supporting the piracy, prompting him to involve the Ascendancy in a [[Lioaoin pirate campaign|campaign]] against it and the pirates.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|The Scofti change leaders and governments frequently, often under violence or the threat of violence.|Thrawn|<ref name="Chaos Rising" />}}
Bardram Scoft was home to the Scofti [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]], whose leader held the title of Prefect. The Scofti would change leaders and their respective governments often, in many cases under violence or the threat of it; [[assassin]]ation of the former Prefect was not unprecedented. The protocols of the Scoftic government would change with each new Prefect. The new leader would also often publicly destroy the personal items of their predecessor, and as such Scofti items were commonly made to be deliberately fragile.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Locations==
{{Quote|Since each new leader typically reorganizes the prefecture's palace, all the way down to the décor and the tableware, the artisans see no point in making anything for them that will last longer than a year.|Thrawn|<ref name="Chaos Rising" />}}
The Scoftic prefecture resided in a palace. New leaders among the Scofti frequently reorganized the prefecture's palace, including the decoration.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
