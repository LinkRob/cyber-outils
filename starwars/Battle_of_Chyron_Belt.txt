The '''Battle of Chyron Belt''' was a confrontation that took place at some point by the [[time]] of the [[First Order-Resistance War]]<ref name="TLJ" /> in [[34 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|VIII}} to [[34 ABY]].</ref> and shared its name with the [[Expansion Region]]'s [[Chyron system]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> It saw the participation of [[Amilyn Holdo]],<ref name="TLJ" /> who served with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> before eventually becoming a [[Vice Admiral]] of the [[Resistance]].<ref name="TLJ" />

By 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Resistance [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] had heard of Holdo's role in the Battle of Chyron Belt despite not having seen the Vice Admiral in person. That [[Standard year|year]], Dameron attended a briefing aboard the Resistance [[MC85 Star Cruiser|cruiser]]<ref name="TLJ" /> [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']]<ref name="TLJVD" /> in which Holdo, after being placed in [[Commanding officer|command]] of the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]], addressed the attending Resistance personnel. After her speech, Dameron asked his fellow [[Resistance pilot|pilot]],<ref name="TLJ" /> [[C'ai Threnalli]],<ref name="DB Threnalli">{{Databank|c-ai-threnalli|C'ai Threnalli}}</ref> if the unconventional<ref name="DB Holdo">{{Databank|amilyn-holdo|Amilyn Holdo}}</ref> Vice Admiral who had spoken was indeed the same person who had participated in the Battle of Chyron Belt. In response, Threnalli shrugged<ref name="TLJ" /> and mumbled an affirmative<ref name="TLJ Junior">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> in the [[Abednedish]] [[language]].<ref name="SWKids">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Language in Star Wars Movies|video=CZp8Civk2co|nolive=1}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The Battle of Chryon Belt ''[sic]''? That was you, ''huh''?|Poe Dameron, to Amilyn Holdo as presented in ''The Last Jedi Adaptation'' 2|<ref name="TLJ2">[[The Last Jedi Adaptation 2|''The Last Jedi Adaptation'' 2]]</ref>}}
The Battle of Chyron Belt was first mentioned in the [[2017]] [[sequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="TLJ" /> In the [[The Last Jedi Adaptation 2|second issue]] of [[Marvel Comics]]' [[Star Wars: The Last Jedi Adaptation|comic adaptation]] of the film&mdash;which was written by [[Gary Whitta]]<ref name="TLJ2" /> and published on [[May 23]], [[2018]]<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/67560/star_wars_the_last_jedi_adaptation_2018_2|text=Star Wars: The Last Jedi Adaptation (2018) #2}}</ref>&mdash;Poe Dameron instead delivers an alternate version of the line mentioning the battle to Amilyn Holdo, with the battle's name misspelled as the "Battle of Chryon Belt."<ref name="TLJ2" /> The [[July 10]], 2018<ref name="Quirk">{{QuirkBooks|url=book/william-shakespeares-jedi-last|text=William Shakespeare's Jedi the Last}}</ref> [[Canon|non-canon]] adaptation ''[[William Shakespeare's Jedi the Last: Star Wars Part the Eighth]]'' by [[Ian Doescher]]&mdash;in which yet another version of the line is instead delivered as an [[Wikipedia:Aside|aside]]&mdash;introduced the alternate name "Clash of Chyron Belt" for that engagement.<ref name="Shakespeare">''[[William Shakespeare's Jedi the Last: Star Wars Part the Eighth]]''</ref>
