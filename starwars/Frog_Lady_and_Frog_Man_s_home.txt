A '''home''' was located on the<ref name="Chapter 11" /> [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Chapter 10">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 10: The Passenger}}</ref> [[moon]] [[Trask]] in which a two [[Querm Rybet]], a female nicknamed "[[Frog Lady]]" and her [[Marriage|husband]] nicknamed "[[Frog Man]]," resided.<ref name="Chapter 11" />

Before going to a [[Assault on the Imperial Gozanti freighter|mission]], the [[mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] left the [[foundling]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] at the house. After the end of the mission, Djarin returned and took Grogu thanking the couple and congratulating them.<ref name="Chapter 11" />
