The '''14th Sector Fleet''', or the '''14th Fleet''', was a numbered [[sector fleet]] of the [[Imperial Navy]], the [[Navy|naval branch]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Grand Moff]] [[Ravik]] promoted the 14th Fleet's [[Lieutenant]] [[Ralchio Nervi]] to head his own [[Ralchio Nervi's task force|naval task force]] as a [[captain]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Admiral]] [[Rolas]] was a member of the [[fleet]] and unsuccessfully proclaimed themselves [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] on the [[planet]] [[Carida]] after [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] apparent [[Battle of Endor|demise]]. [[Historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned Rolas and the 14th Fleet in his book ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.

==Description==
The 14th Sector Fleet,<ref name="Friends" /> or 14th Fleet,<ref name="Rise" /> was a numbered [[sector fleet]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy]]. Part of the [[fleet]]'s composition included [[Imperial officer]]s.<ref name="Friends" />

==History==
{{Quote|Neither of them was able to inspire much in the way of loyalty and were swiftly deposed and arrested by the forces under their control.|Beaumont Kin, on the fates of Grand General Levinous and the 14th Fleet's Admiral Rolas|<ref name="Rise" />}}
[[File:Gladiator_II-class_Star_Destroyer_SWArnada.png|thumb|right|280px|Ralchio Nervi of the 14th Fleet was promoted to the command of his own task force (pictured).]]
The 14th Sector Fleet was active between [[14 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Date Note">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] establishes that the title of [[Grand Moff]] and [[Oversector]] governance were created in the fifth [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Galactic Empire|Empire's]] [[Imperial Era|reign]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the start of the [[Imperial Era]] in [[19 BBY]], thus placing the creation of Grand Moff and the Oversectors in [[14 BBY]]. Since ''[[Friends Like These]]'' establishes [[Ravik]] as the Grand Moff of the [[Tolonda Oversector]] who promoted [[Ralchio Nervi]] from the 14th Sector Fleet, the [[Sector fleet|fleet]] must have been active by 14 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. Additionally, that same source states that Nervi later participated in the [[Battle of Xorrn]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the Alliance's [[Declaration of a New Republic|reorganization]] into the [[New Republic]] to [[4 ABY]]. Therefore, the 14th Sector Fleet must have been active between 14 BBY and 4 ABY.</ref> [[Lieutenant]] [[Ralchio Nervi]] served in the sector fleet before coming to the attention of [[Grand Moff]] [[Ravik]], [[governor]] of the [[Tolonda Oversector]]. Due to Ralchio's [[marriage]] to [[Lila Nervi]] from the influential [[Nervi family]] and Lila's lobbying to Ravik, Ralchio was promoted to the [[military rank]] of [[captain]] and became the [[commanding officer]] of his own [[Navy|naval]] [[Ralchio Nervi's task force|task force]] prior to the [[Battle of Xorrn]].<ref name="Friends" />

After the supposed [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] at the [[Battle of Endor]] in the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [['ABY-BBY' dating system|4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Admiral]] [[Rolas]] of the 14th Fleet declared themselves [[Emperor]] on<ref name="Rise" /> the [[Colonies]]<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> [[Carida]]. These actions were also undertaken by [[Grand General]] [[Levinous]], and were both quickly deposed and arrested by their own [[Imperial Military|forces]] after failing to inspire loyalty.<ref name="Rise" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Rise Date">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned the 14th Fleet in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise" />

==Members==
The 14th Fleet had a cadre of officers, including Admiral Rolas<ref name="Rise" /> and Lieutenant Ralchio Nervi. Grand Moff Ravik had the authority to promote 14th Fleet officers.<ref name="Friends" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
The 14th Sector Fleet was introduced in the [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] adventure book ''[[Friends Like These]]'' as part of the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]].<ref name="Friends" /> It was identified as the 14th Fleet in Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]]'s [[2024]] book, ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise" />
