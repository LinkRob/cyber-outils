'''Bolas''' were a type of thrown ensnaring weapon. Consisting of a [[rope]] with round weights at either end,<ref name="ROTJ" /> bolas were used to bind opponents. During the [[Imperial Era]], [[bounty hunter]]s [[Fennec Shand]]<ref name="Bounty Lost" /> and [[Durge]] utilized bolas.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 11">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 11|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 11]]</ref> The [[Ewok]]s of the [[Endor|Sanctuary Moon of Endor]] also utilized bolas during the [[Battle of Endor|major battle]] fought there during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="ROTJ" />
