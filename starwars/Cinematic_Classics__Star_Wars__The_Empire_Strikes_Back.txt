'''''Cinematic Classics: Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back''''' is a hardcover book by [[Tom Huddleston]] published as part of the [[Star Wars Cinematic Classics|''Star Wars'' Cinematic Classics]] series on [[September 18]], [[2025]].<ref name="Amazon" /> The book serves as a visual re-telling of {{Film|V}} and uses concept art to that end. It was released alongside the [[Cinematic Classics: Star Wars: Attack of the Clones|''Attack of the Clones'' Cinematic Classics]] book.<ref name="AmazonAOTC">{{Amazon|domain=co.uk|url=gp/product/1835871488|text=Attack of the Clones (Star Wars Cinematic Classics)|archivedate=20260306192410}}</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
'''''A retelling of ''Star Wars'' Episode V: ''The Empire Strikes Back'' featuring stunning development art from the original Lucasfilm concept artists.'''''<ref name="Amazon" />

''Relive the excitement of the cinematic classic ''The Empire Strikes Back.'' The thrilling tale follows the story of the battle between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance, as Luke Skywalker masters the Force to take on Darth Vader.''

''Enjoy the hit movie through this all-ages retelling accompanied by concept art from the original artists. Alongside the art, fascinating facts about each piece give an insight into the design process behind iconic characters and settings like Yoda and Cloud City.''<ref name="Amazon" />

==Continuity==
The book describes [[Boba Fett]] as a [[Mandalorian]] [[Mandalorian warrior|warrior]]. Despite his armor, which was inherited from his father, [[Jango Fett]], Boba was not technically a Mandalorian by creed or culture.
