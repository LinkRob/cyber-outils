'''Egarian silk''' was a type of silk that became harder the more electricity was run through it. It had high durability and fluctuating elasticity, higher than [[duralloy]], and could stretch to mononuclear level.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> It originated<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|system=[[Egaria system]]|other=Egarian silk}}</ref> from the [[Egaria system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref>
