The '''Anklebiter Brigade''' was a group of [[Youngling|youngsters]] on [[Coruscant]] who provided information to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and assaulted [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] positions for control of the [[CoCo Town]] area after the [[Battle of Endor]]. Their members included [[Jak]], [[Streaks]], and [[Iggs]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> In [[5 ABY]], members of the Anklebiter Brigade rescued [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] from the [[Imperial Palace]] during the [[Battle of Jakku]]. His escape allowed Amedda to sign the [[Galactic Concordance]] with the [[New Republic]], ending the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>
