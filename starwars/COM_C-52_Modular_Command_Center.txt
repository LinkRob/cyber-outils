The '''COM C-52 Modular Command Center''',<ref name="Fully Operational" /> known simply as the '''Com C-52''',<ref name="Lead by Example">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref> was a [[Lockslab]] [[modular base structure]] manufactured by [[Delvin Constructs]]. It acted as a command center and could hold roughly twelve individuals. It cost 2,750 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and was most notably used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Alliance Military|military]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Rebel_Command_Center.png|thumb|right|200px|A ''Star Wars'' Legends depiction of the COM C-52 Modular Command Center]]
The COM C-52 Modular Command Center was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Lead by Example]]'',<ref name="Lead by Example" /> a [[2016]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Join the Top Ranks">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2016/2/4/join-the-top-ranks|text=Join the Top Ranks}}</ref> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as the [[COM C-52 Modular Command Center/Legends|Com C-52]], and first appeared in the [[2006]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]'', developed by [[Petroglyph]] and published by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="EaW">''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]''</ref>
