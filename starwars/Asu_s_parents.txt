At least two individuals were the '''parents''' of the [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Asu]], who lived in [[Meevai Village]] on the [[planet]]<ref name="Bride" /> [[Keelia]].<ref name="Meet the Heroes">{{SW|url=news/inside-star-wars-visions-meet-the-heroes|text=Inside ''Star Wars: Visions'': Meet the Heroes}}</ref> At some point, both individuals [[Death|died]].<ref name="Bride" /> Between [[19 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Death" /> Asu carried<ref name="Bride" /> his<ref name="Art of Visions">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Visions]]''</ref> [[Marriage|bride]], [[Haru]], on a [[bridal chair]] to a [[Religion|sacred]] location to perform a [[purification ceremony]] before their [[Wedding of Asu and Haru|wedding]]. On the way, Haru said that she wished her own [[parent]]s could have been there. Asu told her that he had no doubt that they were watching and that they were one with the planet. Haru agreed, and replied that Asu's parents also were.<ref name="Bride" />
