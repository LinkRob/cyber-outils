"'''Doctor Mandible'''" was a [[Gender|male]] [[Reepayto]] who was a regular at the [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] on [[Tatooine]]. In [[9 ABY]], he was playing a game of [[sabacc]] against [[Peli Motto]] until the [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] walked in looking for [[Mandalorian]] coverts and interrupted the game. As Doctor Mandible overheard the conversation, he told Motto that he knew of a [[Frog Lady|contact]] which knew where one was located. However, he said that he would share it if Djarin gave him 500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to cover his call, which he did. However, Motto won the game and Mandible lost. Keeping his word, Doctor Mandible gave the information to Djarin.

==Biography==
As a [[cantina]] regular, Doctor Mandible was sitting at the bar of [[Chalmun]]'s [[Spaceport]] Cantina in [[Mos Eisley]] when the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] walked in, searching for work.<ref name="Chapter 5" />

[[File:MottoMandibleSabacc-TMCh10.png|thumb|left|300px|"Doctor Mandible" played sabacc against Peli Motto.]]
Sometime later, while Doctor Mandible was playing a game of [[sabacc]] against [[Peli Motto]], Djarin entered the cantina once again, on his quest to locate Mandalorian coverts. Mandible overheard the conversation between Motto and Djarin and subsequently told them about a [[Frog Lady|contact]] who could help the Mandalorian for his quest. Via translation Motto informed Djarin that he would share the intel with him, should Djarin cover his sabacc call. Djarin agreed, giving Mandible 500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], with Motto assuring Djarin that the insectoid was on a "hot streak." Motto then promptly won the game, ending Doctor Mandible's winning streak. Mandible kept his word and told Motto about the person that could connect him with the Mandalorians, allowing for her to translate his speech for Djarin.<ref name="Chapter 10" />

==Skills and abilities==
According to Pelli Motto, Doctor Mandible was a capable sabacc player known for his hotstreaks. Although he was unable to speak [[Galactic Basic Standard]], he could understand others who spoke the [[language]]. He had [[Color|orange]] [[eye]]s, brown skin and [[mandible]]s. He had several [[limb]]s.<ref name="Chapter 10" />
