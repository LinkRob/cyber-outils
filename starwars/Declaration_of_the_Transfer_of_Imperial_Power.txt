'''Declaration of the Transfer of Imperial Power''' was a file stored in the "Corusca Sector" section of the [[Imperial Archives]] that related to [[Grand Moff]] [[Plorest]] of the [[Corusca sector]]'s declaration of himself as the new [[Galactic Emperor]] to the [[Imperial Military|forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] across [[the galaxy]] following the [[death]] of [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Imperial forces ignored Plorest, who had invoked the non-existent [[Imperial Security Code 88173.1]], and he was [[Death|killed]] within three [[Standard day|days]] of beginning his "reign." The [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] cited the file when speaking about Plorest in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Declaration of the Transfer of Imperial Power was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
