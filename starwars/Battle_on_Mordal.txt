A '''battle''' between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] took place in<ref name="Aftermath" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Mordal system]]</ref> [[Planet|world]] [[Mordal]]'s [[city]] of [[Maborn]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> The [[Ballast family]] became trapped in the area of contention between the warring sides, which were stationed on opposite sides of Maborn. The [[Corwin Ballast's daughter|daughter]] of the civilian [[Corwin Ballast]] and his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Lynnta]], became immobilized in a street, and the rescue attempt made by her [[parent]]s proved unsuccessful when the pair were wounded by [[blaster]] fire from [[Imperial Military|Imperial]] and [[Alliance Military|rebel forces]] sides prior to reaching their child.<ref name="Aftermath" />

The battle on Mordal ended without a decisive victor, with the combatants choosing to halt hostilities. Ballast, grieved upon learning Lynnta and his daughter had [[Death|died]] in the firefight, decided to depart Mordal and travel to<ref name="Aftermath" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] planet<ref name="Jakku">{{SW|url=news/where-in-the-galaxy-are-the-worlds-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?|int=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?}}</ref> [[Jakku]], a place he felt war would not breach.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle on Mordal appeared in flashbacks in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
