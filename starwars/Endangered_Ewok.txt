'''Endangered Ewok''' was an [[Ewok]] of the [[Bright Tree tribe]] on the forest [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. He took the key to his [[Enraged Ewok|wife's]] treasure chest as a lucky charm, believing it would help him cross a broken bridge, but got stuck once he reached the other side. After being rescued by a [[Force-sensitive|Force-user]], he gave them the key.<ref name="LEGO" />

==Behind the scenes==
Endangered Ewok first appeared in the [[2022]] [[canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', in the puzzle "Ewok Lock".<ref name="LEGO" />
