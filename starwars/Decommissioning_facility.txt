A '''decommissioning facility''' was located in [[Coronet City]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. In the [[time]] following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[battle droid]]s from the [[Clone Wars]] were decommissioned and melted down at the facility. The facility was patrolled by [[Imperial Police Droid|police droids]] and operated by organic workers. Due to the strategic value of intelligence held by [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|T-series tactical droids]], two parties both [[Mission to a droid decommissioning facility|sought]] to retrieve [[Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Corellia)|one]] from the facility&mdash;the unit of former [[clone commando]]s known as the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]], hired by the [[Information broker|informant]] [[Ciddarin Scaleback|Cid]], and the [[Martez family|Martez sisters]], two [[smuggler]]s working for a contact who intended to use the [[tactical droid]]'s data to fight back against the Empire.<ref name="Decommissioned" />

The two groups crossed paths inside the facility, initially competing to retrieve the [[droid]]'s remains, but were forced to work together to fight the [[police droid]]s after the facility was placed into lockdown. They eventually managed to use the tactical droid to activate the other battle droids, allowing them to escape. Though the tactical droid's head was hit and destroyed by fire from the police droids, the [[clone trooper]] [[Tech]] had copied its information to a [[data rod]]. The Bad Batch ultimately gave the data to the Martez sisters, believing their cause was more worthy.<ref name="Decommissioned" />
