'''Che Mosskà''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|dish]] that was sold on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]]. The dish included grilled [[Mosskà]] on the cob and served with [[mashed deikkò root]] and [[jerà pepper]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the dish was sold for 200 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] or seventy five credits for a small serving at the [[Irio]] food stall in the [[Market District (Mirogana)|Market District]] of [[Mirogana]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of Toshara.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{GameMechanics}}
During that same [[Standard year|year]], the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] and her [[Merqaal]] companion [[Nix]] share a meal of Che Mosskà in at Irio.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Che Mosskà was created for the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It was one of the galactic street foods featured in the game which gave equipable perks to Nix. The Che Mosskà perk in ''Star Wars Outlaws'' allowed Nix to kick [[grenade]]s away from the player.<ref name="Outlaws" />
