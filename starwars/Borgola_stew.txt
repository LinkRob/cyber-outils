'''Borgola stew''' was a type of [[stew]] that was available on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> it was listed as costing six on both the menu at the [[Hot Pot]] [[Restaurant|eatery]] in the [[Market District (Mirogana)|market]] of the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] and at the [[Daruda Diner]] in the town of [[Jaunta's Hope]], both located on the moon.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Borgola stew was created for the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" /> Prior to the game's release, the menu mentioning the stew was visible during a gameplay preview released on [[June 13]], [[2023]].<ref name="Trailer">{{UbisoftYouTube|c7K2TA0bBpY|Star Wars Outlaws: Official Gameplay Walkthrough &#124; Ubisoft Forward|sw_url=video/official-gameplay-walkthrough-star-wars-outlaws|date=2023-06-12}}</ref>
