'''Fen slugs''' were a type of [[Slug (creature)|slug]] known to the [[Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="CR" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="CR Date">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' takes place in [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> while flying in one of the [[Chiss shuttle|shuttles]] of the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] [[heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]'' during a [[Mission to the Rapacc system|mission]] in the [[Unknown Regions]] [[Rapacc system]], the [[Chiss]] [[Mitth'ali'astov]], [[core name]] "Thalias," compared the maneuverability of the [[shuttle]] to that of a fen slug.<ref name="CR" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fen slugs were mentioned in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="CR" />
