'''Black Spire Outfitters''',<ref name="Galaxy's Edge" /> also known as '''Arta's shop''',<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref> was a [[clothing]] store that could be found among the many shops in the [[Black Spire Outpost Market|market]] of [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]] that was in operation by at least [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge" /> It was owned by [[Arta Kleidun]] and employed the sewing droid [[GX-8]] and her clothiers to keep up with the demand from her long clientele list.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> The overall  style Arta and team weaved hearkened back to the [[Old Republic-era]] with flowing, hooded robes a top selling item, and her wares decorated the walls.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />


==History==
{{Quote|Your best bet for  new outfit in the Outpost comes from Black Spire Outfitters|Eloc Throno|, ''the Traveler's Guide to Batuu''<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />}}
In recent history, Kleidun opened a storefront the [[Black Spire Outpost Market]] where she could sell her historically designed fabrics and clothes. With the help of her sewing droid, GX-8, and her hired clothiers, Kleidunestablished as a key part of [[Batuuan]] fashion.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />
[[File:GX-8.png|left|thumb|144x144px|Kleidun's GX-8 droid]]

Later during 34 ABY, the [[Resistance]] spy [[Vi Moradi]] crashed onto Batuu and came to the Outfitters in need of extra clothes for disguises and to blend in with the local styles. Along with the now outfitted Resistance agent, many others in Black Spire Outpost wore clothes from the Outfitters including belts, tunics, shawls, [[poncho]]s and other fabric-based items.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" />
When [[Eloc Throno]] visited Black Spire for his guidebook that same year, the explorer noted Arta's shop was a must visit location in the Market.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Black Spire Outfitters is a clothing store inside [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]].<ref name="Building Batuu">{{DPB|url=2019/02/building-batuu-new-details-released-about-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Building Batuu: New Details Released about ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The store first opened with the theme park location in [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="D23 opening">{{D23|url=a-to-z/black-spire-outfitters|text=Disney A to Z: Black Spire Outfitters}}</ref>
