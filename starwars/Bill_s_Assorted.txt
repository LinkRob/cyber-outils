'''Bill's Assorted'''<ref name="Cornered" /><ref name="Infiltration">{{TBB|Infiltration (episode)}}</ref> was a business located in the [[central marketplace]] of [[Ro Station]], a [[city]] on [[Pantora]].<ref name="Cornered" /> Another such business was located in an unnamed city where [[Riyo Chuchi]] met up with [[Avi Singh]]. A [[Color|reddish]]-orange and white sign, stating the name of the business in [[Aurebesh]], was used to attract customers, although the sign in the unnamed city was mirrored,<ref name="Infiltration" /> while the one in Ro Station was not.<ref name="Cornered" /> 

{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The [[Aurebesh]] sign stating the name of the business contains additional text at the bottom; however, in the ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' episode "[[Cornered]]," where the sign first appears, most of this text is obscured,<ref name="Cornered" /> and in the episode "[[Infiltration (episode)|Infiltration]]," most of it is too unclear to decipher.<ref name="Infiltration" />
