"'''C-3PO'''" is the thirteenth booklet in a series of [[canon]] reference booklets titled the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] and was published on [[January 22]], [[2021]].

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''3PO droids were designed for the service of royalty, diplomats, and politicians. Its century long operating life is a good example of the excellent design of this series.''
