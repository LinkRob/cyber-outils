'''Fanki Baus''' was passenger aboard the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> During their time on board the [[cruiser]], an announcement was broadcast over the [[starship|ship's]] [[speaker]] system to inform Baus that their table was ready for them.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fanki Baus was mentioned in the "Wild Card" expansion for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]], published by [[Ubisoft]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> and released on [[November 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Wild Card released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/1Q4y5lMJwdUUt6qXzbWKPI/star-wars-outlaws-game-update-showcase-wrapup|text=Star Wars Outlaws Game Update Showcase Wrap-Up}}</ref> They were mentioned in one of several ambient announcements that player's could hear while aboard the ''Morenia''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
