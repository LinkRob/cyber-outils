The '''fall of the Old Republic''', also called the '''dark age''', described a long period of time during the [[Old Republic-era]], in which the [[Galactic Republic]] slowly declined in its power. Eventually, the Old Republic collapsed, and the galaxy entered a dark age, which ended in what was believed to be the [[Jedi-Sith War|final defeat]] of the [[Sith]] and the reorganization of the Republic.

==History==
Sometime after the [[Jedi Order]] had begun its [[Mandalorian-Jedi War|conflict]] against the [[Mandalorian]]s,<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" /> the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] continued to fend off the onslaught of the [[Sith]], who slowly began gaining the upper hand.<ref name="SWP" /> The [[Middle Era of the Old Republic]] had passed.<ref name="Guardians of the Whills" />

During one of the wars that the Old Republic faced in the period of its fall, a Mandalorian of [[House Vizsla]] [[Sacking of the Jedi Temple|stole]] the [[Darksaber]] from the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="The Mandalore Plot" /> 

Some time between 1050 and 1032 BBY [[Jedi-Sith War|a new war]] erupted between the Sith and the Jedi. The Sith, now organised as the [[Brotherhood of Darkness]], eventually gained the upper hand, [[Battle of Coruscant (Jedi-Sith War)|occupied Coruscant]] and took control of the Jedi Temple. The Old Republic collapsed and the Sith became the dominant power in the Galaxy, as the Jedi were forced into exile; however, the conflict was not over, since both the Jedi and Old Republic loyalists continued the fight against the triumphing Sith.

Eventually, both the Jedi and the remnants of the Old Republic managed to reorganize their forces and fight back against the Sith. The Jedi Order emerged victorious from the war, managing to [[Liberation of Coruscant|recapture]] [[Coruscant]] from the Sith who occupied the [[capital]].<ref name="SWP" /> The war ended with the Sith's defeat, with [[Darth Bane|all but one]] Dark Lord dead.<ref name="Sacrifice">{{TCW|Sacrifice (episode)}}</ref> After [[1032 BBY]], the Old Republic, now having brought peace to the galaxy, reorganized itself, resulting in the establishment of the modern Galactic Republic. [[Tarsus Valorum]] was elected as the first [[Supreme Chancellor]] soon thereafter.<ref name="SWP" />
