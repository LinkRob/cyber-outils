'''Bubbling poma''' was a type of liquid that was distilled from the [[seed]]s of [[poma-drupe fruit]]s. When used in a [[deprivation chamber]], the fluid caused [[hallucination]]s and helped relax an individual's muscles.<ref name="AEE" /> By [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' take place concurrent to the [[Battle of Jakku]], an event ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places to [[5 ABY]].</ref> the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Nakadia]], [[Quarrow]], had a [[Unidentified poma-club|poma-club]] establishment that used bubbling poma.<ref name="AEE" />

While on Nakadia<ref name="AEE" /> in 5 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> several [[New Republic]] operatives visited Quarrow and briefly considered the world's [[poma-club]] and its usage of the substance, wondering how the city's atmosphere would impact their upcoming [[Mission to Nakadia|mission]]. Not long after, the [[New Republic]] [[senator]] [[Rethalow]] utilized bubbling poma while in Quarrow's poma-club.<ref name="AEE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bubbling poma was mentioned in ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', a [[2017]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the third installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="AEE" />
