'''Antillion''' was a backwater [[Planet|world]] located in the [[D'Astan sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> it was located in a [[D'Astan sector|sector]] [[Governor|governed]] by an [[Unidentified Imperial governor (Madelin Sun)|Imperial governor]], and was where former [[University of Bar'leth]] [[Sava]] [[The Archivist|Madelin Sun]] worked as a [[Merchant|junk seller]] after her career was destroyed because of the [[Order 66|destruction]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Crimson Reign 3" /> During this [[time]], she purchased an [[Unidentified urn|urn]] she believed contained the ashes of [[Krestrel D'Naran]], the archrival of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], from [[Augustus Graves]],<ref name="Han Chewie 3" /> the [[Chief Executive Officer|CEO]] of [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]],<ref name="Han Chewie 1">[[Han Solo & Chewbacca 1|''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' 1]]</ref> which she kept within her [[Madelin Sun's home|home]].<ref name="Han Chewie 3" />

The [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]], and the man [[Ovan]], who claimed to be Solo's [[Parent|father]]<ref name="Han Chewie 3" /> though in reality was the criminal [[Corbus Tyra]],<ref name="Han Chewie 5">[[Han Solo & Chewbacca 5|''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' 5]]</ref> traveled to Antillion to recover the ashes for Jabba, though Solo's attempts to buy the urn failed. After the failed attempt, Solo told three [[stormtrooper]]s that Sun had illegal contraband in an attempt to get the her away from her items so they could find it for themselves.<ref name="Han Chewie 3" /> When the [[trooper]]s visited Sun, however, they were [[Death|killed]] by [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]] of [[Crimson Dawn]], and Sun left with Qi'ra.<ref name="Crimson Reign 3" /> With the building empty, Solo and Tyra entered it and found the urn, though just before they left the home, Tyra was shot in the back by the [[Wookiee]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Krrsantan]].<ref name="Han Chewie 3" />
