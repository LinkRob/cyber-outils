'''Convoy 8766''', also referred to as '''Convoy Trill''', was a [[convoy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy|Navy]] that consisted of four [[bulk freighter]]s, six [[Medium freighter/Canon|medium freighters]], and a [[Victory-class Star Destroyer|''Victory''-class Star Destroyer]] for security. The convoy arrived at a [[Unidentified depot (Galactic Empire)|depot]] over the course of three [[Standard day|days]], with the [[Star Destroyer]] arriving on the second day. It was rumored that the convoy was headed for a secret Imperial installation. [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[agent]]s sought to gain supplies from the convoy.<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" />
