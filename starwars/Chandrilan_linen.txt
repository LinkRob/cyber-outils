'''Chandrilan linen''' was a substance used to make sheets<ref name="Cataclysm" /> that parially shared its name with the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Chandrila]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]'' are dated to [[382 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> the [[human]] [[Axel Greylark]] noted that there were no Chandrilan [[linen]] sheets in his [[Republic Correctional Facility|prison]] but admitted to [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]], to whom he was talking and who was visiting him, that she had not come to inquire as to his thoughts on the quality of the facility's bedding.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Behind the scenes==
