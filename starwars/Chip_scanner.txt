The '''chip scanner'''<ref name="Battle Scars" /> was a device built by the rogue former [[clone commando]] [[Tech]] shortly after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], intended to monitor the function of the [[Behavioral modification biochip|inhibitor chips]] that [[clone trooper]]s were implanted with. Although most of the members of his [[squad]], [[Clone Force 99]], were immune<ref name="Replacements" /> to the brainwashing command of [[Order 66]] which was used to activate the chips and turn the rest of the [[Grand Army of the Republic|clone army]] against their [[Jedi]] leaders,<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> Tech wanted to ascertain whether their chips were truly defective.<ref name="Replacements" />

Tech was forced to put off constructing the device when the squad's [[shuttle]], the [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|''Marauder'']], crash-landed on the [[Ordo Moon]] due to damage it had sustained in a [[Skirmish at Saleucami town|firefight]].<ref name="Replacements" /> Coming to believe that the chips were no threat to the remaining members of the Bad Batch, Tech did not finish the scanner until the squad was reunited with their friend and fellow rogue clone [[Captain]] [[Rex]], who was incredulous that they had not removed their chips due to the danger they posed. The Batch subsequently [[Mission on Bracca|rendezvoused]] with Rex on [[Bracca]] to remove their chips, and Tech finished the scanner en route.<ref name="Battle Scars" />

Because Rex's chip was removed, Tech was able to establish a baseline by comparing his brain scan to theirs; taking into account any cellular anomalies. Once inside a [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Clone Force 99)|wrecked]] [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], the group heads to a [[medical bay]] where Echo begins calibrating the [[surgical pod]] and Tech fits his chip scanner over Wrecker's head. Connected to his [[datapad]], he finds Wrecker's inhibitor chip 90 degrees from his right orbital floor, below the parietal and temporal intersection.<ref name="Battle Scars" />
