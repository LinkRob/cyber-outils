The '''Communications Division''' was a division of the [[Imperial Military]]. In [[2 BBY]], during [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] investigation into the identity of the rebel informant [[Fulcrum]], the rogue [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]] theorized that the [[spy]] was in the Communications Division. However, the ISB [[Colonel]] [[Wullf Yularen]] disagreed and contended that a spy to position himself in communication, would be far too obvious.<ref name="Through Imperial Eyes" />
