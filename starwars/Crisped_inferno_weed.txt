'''Crisped inferno weed''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] served at the [[Moncaladrome]] on the [[waterworld]] [[Mon Cala]]. The [[Clawdite]] [[Tunga Arpagion]], who was [[Changeling|impersonating]] [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Moff]] [[Tan Hubi]], was offered crisped inferno weed by [[Queen]] [[Trios]]. Hubi thanked Trios for the food and ate it. While Queen Trios and [[Regent]] [[Dors Urtya]] walked away, Urtya noted that the last [[time]] he offered the Moff the food, he spat it out. As Trios and Urtya returned to their seats, they came to the conclusion that Hubi had come to enjoy the acquired taste of the food. Crisped inferno weed was [[Color|brown]] in color and had an acquired taste. It could be eaten by both [[human]]s and [[Clawdite]]s.<ref name="Star Wars 47" />
