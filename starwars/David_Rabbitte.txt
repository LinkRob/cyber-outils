'''David Rabbitte''' is an artist who drew the illustrations for the short stories ''[[Roll of the Dice]]'' in [[Star Wars Insider 135|''Star Wars Insider'' 135]] and ''[[The Last Battle of Colonel Jace Malcom]]'' in [[Star Wars Insider 137|''Star Wars Insider'' 137]]. He also drew the cover illustration for [[Abel G. Peña]]'s [[EBook|eNovella]] ''[[SkyeWalkers: A Clone Wars Story]]''.

David has also been part of many of [[Topps]]' card sets, including illustrating base card art for multiple [[2010 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 5|''Galaxy Series'']] sets.
