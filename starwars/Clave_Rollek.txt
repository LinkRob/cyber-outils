'''Clave Rollek''' was an [[Clave Rollek's species|alien]] [[pirate]] who was based at [[Yarrum Tower]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He befriended the lost [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Bren]] from [[Tenoo]], who accidentally stowed aboard a [[starship]] bound for [[Yarrum]]. When Bren's friend [[Kai Brightstar]] traveled to [[Yarrum]] to seek Bren, Rollek offered to help repair the damaged ''[[Crimson Firehawk]]'', which needed a new stabilizer coil. Rollek stole one from a local [[Twi'lek]] merchant named [[Hudi]] and escaped with Bren, whom he attempted to recruit as a pirate. Rollek also intended to steal the ''Firehawk''. However, Brightstar caught up with the two and convinced Bren that stealing wasn't right. Together, the two fought and defeated Rollek, who fled into the desert.<ref name="A Jedi or a Pirate" />
