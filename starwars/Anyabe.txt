'''Anyabe''' was an individual who was present on the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|Star Cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' at the time that [[Alderaanian]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] was also on the [[Purrgil-class starcruiser|vessel]] with her [[Wedding of Han Solo and Leia Organa|husband]] [[Han Solo]] for their honeymoon<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> At one point during the cruise, Anyabe learned that Organa was in the ''Halcyon''{{'s}} [[Climate Simulator|climate simulator]] at the time and subsequently [[Comlink|commed]] a [[Unidentified man (Halcyon)|man]] to inform him of Organa's location. After entering a [[turbolift]] with a [[Unidentified second man|companion]], the [[Gender|man]] told his companion about what Anyabe had told him, which was overheard by Solo, who was also on the turbolift.<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />

==Behind the scenes==
Anyabe was mentioned in ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'', a [[2022]] novel written by [[Beth Revis]].<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />
