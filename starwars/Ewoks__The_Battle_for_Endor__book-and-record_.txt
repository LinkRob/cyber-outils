'''''Ewoks: The Battle for Endor''''' was a book-and-record published as part of [[Buena Vista Records]]' [[Read-Along Adventures]] series. It was an adaptation of the TV movie [[Ewoks: The Battle for Endor|of the same name]]. It was produced by [[Ted Kryczko]] and released in [[1985]] as story book with [[Wikipedia:Phonograph record|7" 33 ⅓ rpm record]] and [[Wikipedia:Cassette tape|Cassette tape]]. It is unknown who narrated the record as credits were not provided along with the book or record.<ref name="Record" />
