'''Aeliar''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[officer]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. In order to acquire weapons for the Rebellion, Aeliar intended to purchase from the [[Hidden Hand]] crime syndicate while [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] was distracted by a group of [[bounty hunter]]s [[Hunt for Vader|hunting]] him down. Aeliar's operative, [[Gita]], infiltrated the [[Beilert Valance's crew|team]] of bounty hunters, led by [[Beilert Valance]], but when the mission went awry and Gita was forced to escape, Aeliar abandoned the purchase. The rebels were approached by Valance, who proposed to make a deal with the rebels&mdash;he would lead the Empire to the Hidden Hand while the rebels evacuated his [[homeworld]] of [[Chorin]], allowing Valance to then betray Vader without the threat of his people being punished. In return, Valance used the [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] he had been given by the Hidden Hand to hunt down Vader to purchase weapons for the Rebel Alliance.

==Biography==
===Supplying the Rebellion===
{{Quote|The bounty hunters are on the hunt for Vader.''"<br />"''Excellent. With Vader out of the picture, nothing will stand in the way of the Hidden Hand weapons supply.|A rebel operative and Aeliar|<ref name="Target Vader 1" />}}
[[File:Aeliar-TargetVader1.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Aeliar, at the rebel base in the Fellio Asteroid Belt, prepares to purchase weapons from the Hidden Hand.]]
During the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]], Aeliar<ref name="Target Vader 6">[[Target Vader 6|''Target Vader'' 6]]</ref> served as an [[officer]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 7">[[Bounty Hunters 7|''Bounty Hunters'' 7]]</ref> in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Attempting to equip the Rebellion with weapons and supplies, Aeliar intended to purchase from the crime syndicate known as the [[Hidden Hand]], who were being hunted down by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], a [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] and enforcer of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. When the Hidden Hand began to hire a number of [[bounty hunter]]s to [[Hunt for Vader|hunt down]] Vader, Aeliar sent one of her operatives,<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> the agent<ref name="Bounty Hunters 7" /> [[Gita]], to infiltrate the [[Beilert Valance's crew|team]] disguised as "Urrr'k," a [[Tusken Raider]] [[sniper]].<ref name="Target Vader 4">[[Target Vader 4|''Target Vader'' 4]]</ref> Stationed at a [[Rebel base (Fellio Asteroid Belt)|rebel base]] in the [[Fellio Asteroid Belt]], Aeliar received a message from Gita, informing her that the hunt had began, and Aeliar planned to purchase the weapons while Vader was distracted.<ref name="Target Vader 1" />

However, the hunt was ultimately unsuccessful, and the team of bounty hunters were [[Death|killed]]&mdash;aside from [[Dengar]], who betrayed the team to the Empire; Gita, who managed to escape and return to the Rebellion; and [[Beilert Valance]], who was captured by Vader.<ref name="Target Vader 4" />

===Working with Valance===
{{Quote|I don't like this plan, Gita. And I don't trust Valance.''"<br />"''We're out of options, Aeliar. If we're going to fight back against the Empire, we'll need weapons.|Aeliar and Gita|<ref name="Target Vader 6" />}}
Aeliar returned to a secret [[Rebel outpost (Lowik)|outpost]]<ref name="Target Vader 5">[[Target Vader 5|''Target Vader'' 5]]</ref> on the [[jungle]] [[planet]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 7" /> [[Lowik]], where she met up with Gita. Gita suggested that she go back undercover as Urrr'k, but as the Hidden Hand had gone silent, Aeliar was forced to find another [[Arms dealer|weapons dealer]]. As they talked, Valance, who had been tasked by Vader to destroy the Hidden Hand, entered the outpost, to Aeliar's surprise. Valance suggested making a deal, in which the Rebellion gave him the location of the Hidden Hand, and Valance gave them the weapons they required in return.<ref name="Target Vader 5" />

[[File:GitaAeliarAssurance-TargetVader6.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Aeliar was hesitant to trust Beilert Valance, but ultimately worked with the bounty hunter.]]
Aeliar was reluctant to trust Valance, but, with no other option, she decided to work with the bounty hunter. At Valance's request, Aeliar evacuated the population of a village on Valance's [[homeworld]] of [[Chorin]], including his former lover,<ref name="Target Vader 6" /> [[Yuralla Vega]],<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3">[[Bounty Hunters 3|''Bounty Hunters'' 3]]</ref> preventing the Empire from punishing them when Valance helped the Rebellion. When Valance abandoned the Empire after helping to capture the Hidden Hand's leader, [[Gwi]], the rebels managed to rescue the inhabitants of Chorin. Aeliar and Gita met with Valance on Lowik, where Gita asked Valance to continue working with the Rebellion. The bounty hunter declined the offer, departing from Lowik. In return for assisting him, Valance spent the [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] he received from the Hidden Hand when he was hired to buy weapons for the Rebellion, delivering them to Aeliar.<ref name="Target Vader 6" /> [[Standard year|Years]] later,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 7" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="BH-Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Bounty Hunters 7|''Bounty Hunters'' 7]], in which Aeliar is mentioned, to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Aeliar and Gita no longer operated on Lowik, having been succeeded by [[Lieutenant]] [[Kondra]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 7" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|May the Force be&mdash;''"<br />"''Give it a rest, kid.|Aeliar and Beilert Valance|<ref name="Target Vader 6" />}}
A [[human]] [[Gender|female]], Aeliar had [[Color|blonde]] [[hair]], blue [[eye]]s, and light skin. She was dedicated to the cause of the Rebel Alliance, working with crime syndicates to acquire weapons.<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> Aeliar prevented Gita from going undercover for a second time after the hunt for Vader ended in failure, knowing she would not be able to kill Vader on her own. She was shocked when Valance discovered the secret rebel outpost on Lowik,<ref name="Target Vader 5" /> and was reluctant to work with and trust the bounty hunter, but she followed through with their plan. Aeliar believed in [[the Force]], using the phrase "[[May the Force be with you]]" when Valance left Lowik.<ref name="Target Vader 6" />

==Equipment==
Aeliar wore a gray shirt, brown pants and a dark [[jacket]], over which she wore a black [[cape]].<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> She wore [[goggles]] on her head,<ref name="Target Vader 6" /> and [[glove]]s on her [[Limb|hands]].<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> When evacuating the inhabitants of Chorin, Aeliar and Gita used a [[Theta-class T-2c shuttle|''Theta''-class T-2c shuttle]].<ref name="Target Vader 6" />

Aeliar first appeared in [[Target Vader 1|''Target Vader'' 1]], a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] written by [[Robbie Thompson]], illustrated by [[Marc Laming]] and [[Cris Bolson]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> on [[July 3]], [[2019]]<ref name="Marvel Target Vader 1">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/77664/star_wars_target_vader_2019_1|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #1}}</ref> Aeliar was not named until the series' final issue, [[Target Vader 6|''Target Vader'' 6]], also written by Thompson<ref name="Target Vader 6" /> and published on [[December 11]], 2019.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/83519/star_wars_target_vader_2019_6|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #6}}</ref>
