The '''flesh-eating daggerworm''' was a type of [[Parasite|parasitic]], [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[worm]] that originated at<ref name="Rey" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Trenwyth system]]</ref> [[Planet|world]]<ref name="BMF38">{{BuildFalconCite|38|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> [[Trenwyth]] and lived in the internal organs of [[happabore]]s, [[Limb|quadrupedal]] creatures that could be found on many planets in the [[Western Reaches]]. The [[Galacti-Stok]]&ndash;distributed [[Happy-Bore Medicated Dewormer]], which could be used to control the populations of several types of parasitic happabore worms, was not recommended for treating flesh-eating daggerworm infestations. That fact was noted in an advertisement leaflet for the [[dewormer]] that was eventually included by the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] in the [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]]<ref name="Rey" /> she<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> was [[Author|writing]] before her departure from the planet [[Jakku]]&mdash;where happabores were one of the resident lifeforms<ref name="Rey" />&mdash;in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines" />
