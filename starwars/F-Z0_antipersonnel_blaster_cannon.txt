The '''F-Z0 antipersonnel blaster cannon''' was a model of double-barrel [[blaster cannon]] developed by [[Sienar-Jaemus Army Systems]] and used by the [[First Order]]. It was top-cover mounted on [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport|Atmospheric Assault Landers]], where a single [[First Order stormtrooper gunner|stormtrooper]] gave protective fire to [[troop]]s being deployed in drop zones.<ref name="ICS" />
