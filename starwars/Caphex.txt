The '''Caphex''' were a [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] with wispy hair that grew from the sides of their faces.<ref name="TROS VD" />

The Caphex [[Bryn (Nihil)|Bryn]] was a member of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] during the [[High Republic Era]], serving under [[Melis Shryke]] in the Nihil [[Occlusion Zone]].<ref name="TEOD">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]''</ref> The Caphex [[Dante]] was a member of the [[Jedi Order]] at the end of the [[Clone Wars]] and was selected as a candidate member of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Inquisitorius]].<ref name="Devoted">{{TOTE|Devoted}}</ref>

The Caphex [[Seossra Thwisp]] worked as a [[spy]] for [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] [[Resistance]] during the [[Cold War]] with the [[First Order]]. Thwisp's [[homeworld]] was [[Caphexdis]],<ref name="TROS VD" /> and she was later stationed at Organa's [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|base]] on [[Ajan Kloss]] prior to the [[Battle of Exegol]].<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BF2_Caphex.png|thumb|left|200px|The "Caphex spy" in Battlefront II]]
The Caphex were created for the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], the final installment of the [[sequel trilogy]]. Concept art for the species was drawn by [[Luke Fisher]] and featured in the book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker]]'', where they are referred to as "Wizard 71."<ref name="Art of">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker]]''</ref>

The Caphex first appeared in the Celebration Edition update to the [[2017]] video game ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''. The free update was released in the days prior to Episode IX's release and featured content from the upcoming film.<ref name="BF2 Trailer">{{EASWYouTube|qbVsjbBllKQ|Star Wars Battlefront 2: The Rise of Skywalker Official Trailer}}</ref> In the game, players can take control of the "Caphex spy," a member of the Infiltration class. The spy wields a [[truncheon]] for close-quarters combat and has the ability to call down an [[orbital bombardment]] on opponents.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
