'''De'Rruyet Industries''',<ref name="Aphra 6" /> also known as '''De'Rruyet Tech''',<ref name="Aphra 8">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 8|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 8]]</ref> was a company active during the [[Imperial Era]], founded by [[Beol De'Rruyet]].<ref name="Aphra 9" /> De'Rruyet claimed to possess an ancient [[Nihil]] [[Path engine]] and have the ability to replicate it, which would lead to the rediscovery of a long-lost method of [[lightspeed]] travel. [[Domina Tagge]] of the [[Tagge Corporation]] wished to prevent De'Rruyet from unveiling the technology, recruiting the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] to learn the details of the device.<ref name="Aphra 6" />
