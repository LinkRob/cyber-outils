"'''Chain of Command'''" is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story in ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures Volume 10]]''. It stars the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Anise I'Zak]] undertaking a top secret mission.

==Plot summary==
During the midst of the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] on the planet [[Hitaka]], [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] [[Anise I'Zak]] enters the tent of [[General/Legends|Generals]] [[Aayla Secura/Legends|Aayla Secura]] and [[Ki-Adi-Mundi/Legends|Ki-Adi-Mundi]]. I'Zak is given a top-secret canister and is instructed to deliver it to the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]] camp several clicks to the east. On the way there, she is ambushed by several [[B1-series battle droid/Legends|battle droids]] and [[B2-series super battle droid/Legends|super battle droids]]. After dispatching them, she soon stumbles across a squad of [[Droideka/Legends|droidekas]] in a native village. Although the battle manages to break through the walls of one of the homes, the natives help I'Zak in defeating her [[Droid/Legends|droid]] foes. Before one of the droidekas is taken out, however, it manages to shoot off one last bolt, hitting the secret canister squarely in the middle and destroying it. I'Zak takes her anger out on a nearby team of super battle droids and later returns to the camp disappointed. She is surprised when Secura and Ki-Adi-Mundi reveal themselves, stating that I'Zak's mission was a complete success, as her real mission was to be a decoy as the two generals dealt with a top secret mission, which came directly from [[Supreme Chancellor/Legends|Supreme Chancellor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]]. Elsewhere, [[Count/Legends|Count]] [[Dooku/Legends|Dooku]] meets with the [[Hologram/Legends|hologram]] of Darth Sidious. The count informs his master that the mission was a success and that Secura and Ki-Adi-Mundi had no clue their own mission was a diversion so that Dooku could successfully carry out his task. Sidious smiles, remarking that Dooku has learned the "importance of carrying out orders"... something that Ki-Adi-Mundi told I'Zak only moments before.
