By [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Hutt Clan]] [[Enforcer (occupation)|enforcer]] [[Ola Hest]]<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> was single-handily<ref name="Characters of" /> '''captured''' by the "Blade of Bardotta"<ref name="Tempest Runner script" />&mdash;the title<ref name="Chronicles of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref> held by [[Jedi Master]] [[Porter Engle]] of the [[Jedi Order]]&mdash;on<ref name="Characters of" /> the [[Hutt Space]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Nal Hutta]].<ref name="Characters of" /> The capture came after Engle tracked Hest down after she had [[Destruction of a children's medcenter|destroyed]] a [[Unidentified children's medcenter|children's medcenter]] on the astronomical object [[Novor]], which she claimed had been an accident. When he caught up with Hest, Engle dragged her kicking and screaming out of the [[Gardulla the Hutt's Palace|palace]] of<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> the [[Hutt]] criminal [[Gardulla Besadii the Elder]].<ref name="Absolutely Everything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> At one point in the event, Hest was lying on the planet's surface with Engle standing over her with his [[Porter Engle's blue-bladed lightsaber|lightsaber]] ignited and pointed at her.<ref name="Characters of" />

Afterward, Hest was [[Prison|imprisoned]] on the [[Galactic Republic]] [[prison ship]] [[Lourna Dee (prison ship)|''Restitution'']].<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> In 231 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> while the [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]] was also [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] on the ''Restitution'', her fellow prisoner [[Sestin Blin]] informed her of Hest's capture by the "Blade of Bardotta."<ref name="Tempest Runner script" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
The capture of Ola Hest was first mentioned in ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', a [[2021]] audio drama written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> The event was later depicted in an episode of ''[[Characters of Star Wars: The High Republic]]''&mdash;a series of videos that focused on characters within the multimedia project&mdash;dedicated to Porter Engle. The episode was posted on the official ''Star Wars'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel on [[November 4]], [[2022]].<ref name="Characters of" />
