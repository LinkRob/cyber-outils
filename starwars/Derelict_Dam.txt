The '''Derelict Dam''' was a location on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Koboh]]. Within [[Dredger Gorge]], the Derelict Dam area was actually a series of [[dam]]s that at one point held back deadly [[Color|black]] tar, which allowed [[priorite]] prospectors to access new areas in search of their quarry.<ref name="Survivor" /> By [[9 BBY]],<ref name="JSDate">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> the dams of the area had been rusted through, with cracks and leaks in many sections, allowing the tar to pass through. At least one dam appeared to have been destroyed from deliberate sabotage. The dams still had flow pipes spanning two separate reservoirs, which, when functional, would shunt tar between two dams and maintain safe levels for the prospectors. When [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] arrived at Koboh, he discovered that the Derelict Dam area was under control of the [[Bedlam Raiders]] and their repurposed [[army]] of [[battle droid]]s. Nestled on a cliff overlooking the area lived the [[gorocco matriarch]].<ref name="Survivor" />
