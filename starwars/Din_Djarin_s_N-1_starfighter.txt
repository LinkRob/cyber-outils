{{Quote|Is that a Naboo starfighter?''"<br />"''That's what it started off as.|Cobb Vanth, and Din Djarin on the fighter|<ref name="Chapter 6">{{BOBF|Chapter 6: From the Desert Comes a Stranger}}</ref>}}
'''Din Djarin's N-1 starfighter''', also known as '''The Mandalorian's N-1 starfighter''', was a modified [[N-1 starfighter]] owned by [[Din Djarin]] following the destruction of the ''[[Razor Crest]]''. Djarin and the [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Peli Motto]] worked together to build the N-1 starfighter as a replacement.

==Description==
{{Quote|What I'm gonna do, just because I like you, is I'm gonna add on some custom modifications that'll make her faster than a fathier.|Peli Motto, telling Djarin her plans for the starfighter|<ref name="Chapter 5" />}}
[[File:Hangar35-BoBFCh5.png|thumb|left|300px|Motto and Djarin heavily modified the starfighter]]
Djarin and Motto added several modifications to<ref name="Chapter 5" /> Din Djarin's N-1 starfighter<ref name="Launchpad">{{InsiderCite|219|Launchpad}}</ref> to achieve greater speed. The starfighter had both [[laser cannon]]s replaced with larger models and retained the under-slung [[Missile launcher|torpedo launcher]] beneath its nose. Additionally the front cones of both [[engine]]s were removed, but Motto had at least one on the front of both wings. Only the skeleton remained, and the panels beneath the [[cockpit]] and at the ship's tail were removed during the modification process. Motto added a [[Kineso-switch]] that enabled a starship to access its induction intake charger, which would allow the ship to travel at blinding speeds. Citing Djarin's [[Anti-droid sentiment|aversion to droids]], Motto also removed the [[astromech socket]], instead using the space for a small passenger compartment and transparent observation dome.<ref name="Chapter 5" />

The ship's vapor manifold was replaced by a [[turbonic venturi power assimilator]] from a [[Galactic Republic]]-[[Republic Era|era]] [[starfighter]]. The thrust capacitor had an induction intake charger that Motto fabricated for the ship, and a [[cryogenic density combustion booster]] from a [[Tatooine repulsor train|Pyke repulsortrain]] added to the rear thruster. All of the [[Color|yellow]] paint was removed except a few stripes on each wing, mimicking the ''Razor Crest''.<ref name="Chapter 5" />

==History==
===Reconstruction===
{{Quote|Do you have any idea what this is? This is an N-1 starfighter, handmade for the royal guard and commissioned personally by the Queen of Naboo.|Peli Motto|<ref name="Chapter 5" />}}
[[File:BuildingDjarinsN1-BoBFCh5.png|thumb|right|300px|Djarin and Motto together rebuilt his fighter.]]
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="9 ABY">{{BoBFDate}}</ref> [[Din Djarin]] arrived at [[Hangar 3-5]] in [[Mos Eisley]] on [[Tatooine]] after being contacted by [[Peli Motto]] regarding to a replacement ship following the [[Battle of Tython (9 ABY)|destruction]] of the ''[[Razor Crest]]''. Though initially reluctant to purchase the ship, Djarin agreed to assist Motto in rebuilding the ship with the help of her [[droid]]s, with parts supplied by a pair of [[Jawa]]s. Motto also included several custom modifications designed to make the ship faster. Though initially hesitant about the ship, Djarin bonded with it during the rebuild, and following a test flight came to like it. Peli uses her [[MPH power droid]] to help charge the starfighter up.<ref name="Chapter 5" />

===First flight===
{{Quote|Well? How was it?''"<br />"''Wizard.|Peli Motto and Din Djarin, after his first flight|<ref name="Chapter 5" />}}
After the ship was completed, Djarin took it through a test flight through [[Beggar's Canyon]], noting that it handled bumpily until he throttled forward&mdash;with very tight maneuvering ability. The ship was very agile and smooth while flying at high speed.<ref name="Chapter 5" />

[[File:DinDjarinN1BustedSpeeding-BoBFCh5.png|thumb|left|300px|Djarin was stopped by a New Republic patrol for speeding too close to a commercial flight.]]
Following the run through the canyon, Djarin took the ship out of the [[atmosphere]] and into [[Realspace|space]], flying near a [[passenger liner]]. This maneuver attracted the attention of two [[New Republic]] patrol [[pilot]]s flying in [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s who intercepted him, with [[Reed|one]] of them requesting he run his beacon for them. He was informed that flying that close to a commercial ship was prohibited, and following a brief conversation he used the kineso switch installed by Motto that accelerated the starfighter in such a way that one of the [[New Republic pilot|New Republic patrol pilots]] thought he had engaged a jump into [[hyperspace]]. Djarin then returned to the [[hangar]] with an appreciation for his new spacecraft. There, he was approached by [[Fennec Shand]] who offered Djarin a job to aid [[Boba Fett]] in his [[War against the Pykes|fight]] against the [[Pyke Syndicate]]. Djarin agreed to help, but only after he delivered a [[Grogu's chainmail shirt|gift]] to [[Din Grogu|Grogu]].<ref name="Chapter 5" />

===Making a delivery===
[[File:DjarinsDeliveryServices-BoBFCh6.png|thumb|right|300px|Djarin sets his fighter down near the construction site of Skywalker's Jedi Temple.]]
Djarin used the fighter to travel to [[Ossus]], where [[Luke Skywalker]] was training Grogu as well as building what would become a [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker|Jedi academy]]. He was guided by [[R2-D2]] to a landing spot next to a body of water near where many [[ANT-621 droid|ant droid]]s were building a domed structure from pieces of stone. After waiting, he was greeted by [[Ahsoka Tano]], who convinced Djarin to leave the gift with her so as to not disrupt Grogu's training. Djarin reluctantly agreed and departed back to Tatooine as Grogu watched from a distance.<ref name="Chapter 6" />

===Fighting for Fett===
{{Quote|You want to park your starship, you got to do it out there in the flats.''"<br />"''I'm looking for Marshal Vanth.|Deputy Scott, and Din Djarin|<ref name="Chapter 6" />}}
[[File:CobbVanthAdmiresMandoN1-BoBFCh6.png|thumb|left|300px|Cobb Vanth admires Djarin's starfighter.]]
Djarin traveled with the fighter to Tatooine and landed inside [[Jabba's Palace garage]]. He was greeted by a [[Gamorrean]] guard and informed him that his presence was requested by Fennec Shand. Djarin joined a meeting with Fett's forces, where Shand introduced him and informed all that if the war was to begin, they would need foot soldiers to aid them. Djarin noted that he could arrange that. He traveled with his ship to [[Freetown]], and landed at the end of the town's main street. [[Deputy]] [[Scott]] noticed Djarin, and was quick to demand he park his ship in the flats. As Djarin ignored the request and stated he came to see [[Cobb Vanth]], Scott hovered his hand over his blaster. He was quickly dismissed by the [[marshal]] himself, who greeted his old ally and showed admiration for Djarin's custom fighter. After Djarin presented his request to the marshal, he departed from Freetown in the ship.<ref name="Chapter 6" />

Djarin fought alongside Fett in the war against the Syndicate. During the [[Battle of Mos Espa|battle]], Djarin was reunited with Grogu, who decided to leave his training with Skywalker and return to the Mandalorian. Fett, Djarin, and their allies were ultimately successful in driving the Pykes off the planet. Djarin departed from Tatooine, with Grogu seated in the small pod seat built into the ship. Grogu tapped the dome of the seat, which Djarin responded to with a negative. Grogu repeated this several times, until Djarin agreed for what he said is the last time. He flicked a switch, causing the ship to accelerate rapidly, as Grogu squealed.<ref name="Chapter 7">{{BOBF|Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor}}</ref>

===Redemption===
Eventually, Djarin, in his N-1 starfighter, traveled to a Children of the Watch refuge located on a [[Armorer's planet|planet]] and helped the Mandalorians defeat a [[Giant turtle gator|reptilian creature]] that interrupted a Mandalorian initiation ceremony, and he landed his ship and made his way inside the cave refuge. There, he meets the Armorer. He asks the Armorer of a way to redeem himself, as he broke the creed by removing his helmet in front of others. She informs him of the [[Living Waters]] in the [[Mines of Mandalore]] that he may bathe in to be redeemed. However, she believes the mines to be destroyed during the [[Great Purge of Mandalore]]. Djarin shows her a Mandalorian inscription recovered from Mandalore, showing her that the planet may not be completely destroyed.<ref name="The Apostate">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 17: The Apostate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DinDjarinsN1_StarfighterConceptArt-ReturnOfTheMandalorian.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Concept art of Djarin's N-1 starfighter by Ryan Church]]
Din Djarin's N-1 starfighter first appeared in "[[Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian]]," the fifth episode of the live action television series ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'', which was written by [[Jon Favreau]], directed by [[Bryce Dallas Howard]], and aired on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Chapter 5" /> on [[January 26]], [[2022]].<ref name="Chapter 5 release">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-january-2022|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in January 2022}}</ref> Concept art for the starship's appearance in ''The Book of Boba Fett'' was created by artists [[Christian Alzmann]], [[Ryan Church]],<ref name="Chapter 5 Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=series/the-book-of-boba-fett/chapter-5-return-of-the-mandalorian-episode-guide|text="Return of the Mandalorian" Episode Guide &#124; The Book of Boba Fett}}</ref> [[Anton Grandert]],<ref name="Chapter 6 Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=series/the-book-of-boba-fett/chapter-6-from-the-desert-comes-a-stranger-episode-guide|text="From the Desert Comes a Stranger" Episode Guide &#124; The Book of Boba Fett}}</ref> and [[Doug Chiang]]. Chiang originally designed the N-1 starfighter for {{Film|I}}. The idea of building a customized version of a vehicle with speed modifications in a garage with a mechanic was inspired by the film [[Wikipedia:American Graffiti|''American Graffiti'']], which was directed by [[George Lucas]].<ref name="Disney Gallery">{{DisneyGallery|Disney Gallery: The Book of Boba Fett|The Book of Boba Fett}}</ref>
