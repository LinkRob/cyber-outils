The '''Battle of Endor campaign ribbon''' was a campaign [[ribbon]] that was issued as both a bar ribbon and as a [[medal]] that was issued to to both [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Alliance Army|army]] and [[Rebel Alliance Navy|navy]] personnel who served in the [[Battle of Endor]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="Lead" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Note" /> The medal was a [[bronze]] disc that had a depiction of<ref name="Lead" /> the remote [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]]<ref name="Stewards of the Forest Moon">{{InsiderCite|212|Stewards of the Forest Moon}}</ref> [[Endor]] which hung from a [[Color|dark green]] tapering ribbon with gold stripes along the edges. The bar ribbon was a horizontal bar in dark green and two gold stripes.<ref name="Lead" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Endor campaign ribbon debuted in the [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] [[canon]] sourcebook ''[[Lead by Example]]''.<ref name="Lead" />
