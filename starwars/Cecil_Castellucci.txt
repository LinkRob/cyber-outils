'''Cecil Castellucci''' is a [[Wikipedia:Canadian Americans|Canadian-American]] author who co-wrote, along with [[Jason Fry]], the junior novel ''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''. The novel is a part of the [[Journey to Star Wars: The Force Awakens|Journey to ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'']] multimedia initiative and was released on [[September 4]], [[2015]]. The title focuses heavily on [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/coming-soon-to-your-personal-jedi-archives-journey-to-star-wars-the-force-awakens-sneak-peek|text=Coming Soon to Your Personal Jedi Archives: Journey to ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' &ndash; Sneak Peek!}}</ref>
