The '''Fandélane Rebel base''' was a facility used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]] at some point following the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]]. That base was under the command of the [[Twi'lek/Legends|Twi'lek]] Alliance [[Colonel/Legends|Colonel]] [[Ki Essorel]].<ref name="LDF" />

{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Fandélane Rebel base only appeared in ''[[Les démons de feu]]'', a roleplaying adventure published in the [[Graal 25|25th issue]] of French gaming magazine [[Graal (magazine)|''Graal'']].<ref name="LDF" />
