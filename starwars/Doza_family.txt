The '''Doza family''' was a [[human]] family that became prominent on [[Castilon]], an [[ocean]] [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]], during the [[New Republic Era|era]] of the [[New Republic]]. It was on Castilon where [[Captain]] [[Imanuel Doza]],<ref name="The Recruit" /> former [[Imperial officer|officer]] of the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Secrets & Holograms" /> served as station operator of the ''[[Colossus]]'',<ref name="Fuel for the Fire">{{Resistance|Fuel for the Fire}}</ref> an independent [[Starship|aircraft]] [[Fuel|refueling]] platform, by the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]]. His daughter, [[Torra Doza]], lived with him in the station's upper levels and became a skilled [[pilot]] of the [[Ace Squadron]] as well as an accomplished [[racer]].<ref name="The Recruit" /> Another member of the family was [[Venisa Doza]], a pilot who served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Women of the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Women of the Galaxy]]''</ref>
