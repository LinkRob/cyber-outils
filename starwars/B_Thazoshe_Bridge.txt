'''B'Thazoshe Bridge''' was a rock formation in the [[Jundland Wastes]] on [[Tatooine]]. Formed by ancient drainage channels, B'Thazoshe Bridge was the largest natural bridge on the [[planet]], and it was considered sacred by the native [[Tusken Raider]]s. In [[Galactic Basic Standard]], "B'Thazoshe" translated to "[[bantha]] horn turned to stone." According to Tusken superstition, it was bad luck to not release blaster bolts before passing through the Bridge.<ref name="CL 2016" />
