'''Ap'lek''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[humanoid]] [[Gender|male]] who served as a member of the [[Knights of Ren]], under the command of an individual known as [[Ren (human)|Ren]]<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 1" /> and later [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]].<ref name="Episode IX" /> However, Ap'lek, alongside all his fellow knights, met their end at the hands of a redeemed Ben Solo, who fought and killed them during the climactic [[Battle of Exegol]].

==Biography==
[[File:KyloFacesTheKnightsOfRen.png|thumb|left|250px|The now redeemed Ben Solo faces his former allies.]]
By [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Date">A''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' states [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker]] was [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destroyed]] in [[28 ABY]]. As the events of [[The Rise of Kylo Ren 1|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 1]] take place immediately following, they must have taken place the same [[Standard year|year]]. Ap'lek was part of the [[Knights of Ren]] by this [[time]].</ref> the [[Force-sensitive]] Ap'lek joined the [[Knights of Ren]] led by an individual known only as [[Ren (human)|Ren]].<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 1" /> He came to own a [[Ap'lek's Mandalorian executioner vibro-ax|vibro-ax]] that had once been owned by an ancient [[Unidentified Mandalorian executioner|Mandalorian executioner]].<ref name="EpIXVD" />

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Year" /> after the hunt for [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] ended in [[Ben Solo]]'s redemption, Ap'lek and his fellow Knights of Ren were guarding the entrance to the [[Sith Citadel]] as Solo tried to gain entry to help Rey slay [[Darth Sidious]]. Ben quickly killed all of the Knights of Ren after Rey gave him the [[Skywalker lightsaber]] through their [[Force-bond]]. Ben grazed Ap'lek's ax before he was Force pushed into a chasm, falling to his death.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Personality and traits==
Ap'lek was a strategist who considered himself to possess the most cunning of the Knights of Ren. He prefered to best his opponents though deception, only using violence when necessary.<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>

==Powers and abilities==
Ap'lek was [[Force-sensitive]] and an adept of the [[dark side of the Force]], however his powers were underdeveloped. As a [[Knights of Ren|Knight of Ren]], Ap'lek had a martial prowess very few could beat. However, unlike his fellow Knights, Ap'lek preferred deception and strategy over violence. He employed feints and misdirection to trap an opponent.<ref name="EpIXVD" />

[[File:Aplek.png|thumb|right|220px|Ap'lek's preferred weapon was a modified Mandalorian executioner's ax.]]
Despite his great skill as a warrior, Ap'lek was quickly killed by his redeemed former leader, [[Ben Solo]], during the [[Battle of Exegol]]. Grazed by the [[Skywalker lightsaber]], the wounded Ap'lek was Force pushed into a chasm, falling to his death.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Equipment==
Ap'lek's preferred weapon was an ancient modified Mandalorian executioner's ax made out of [[beskar]] alloy. The [[Axe|ax]] featured an ultrasonic [[Vibro-weapon|vibro-tech]] feature which allowed it to slice through [[armor]] and [[bone]] with comparative ease. In relation to Ap'lek's preference for stealth, the Knight of Ren had a [[Smoke grenade|smoke canister]] attached to his waist which spewed out smoke capable of distorting an opponent's vision and [[sensor]]s.<ref name="EpIXVD" />

Ap'lek wore primarily [[Color|black]] [[clothing]], much like the other Knights of Ren. In particular, Ap'lek donned a [[water]]-shedding [[oil]]ed [[cloak]], and lightweight [[plastoid]] armor underneath his black tunic. He also wore [[gauntlet]]s with a set of raised teeth to assist in parrying and pinning edged attacks. Ap'lek's [[helmet]] conveyed a menacing rictus and was of relatively crude design, being patched together from battered armor.<ref name="EpIXVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ap'lek was portrayed by stunt performer [[Lukaz Leong]] in the [[2019]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']],<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|lukazleong|status/1211798221833613312|[[Lukaz Leong]]|quote=Interesting fact of my character in #StarWarsTheRiseofSkywalker|archiveurl=https://archive.is/6ONYP|archivefile=File:LukazLeong-Aplek-December-31-2019.png}}</ref> although he is only listed as "Knight of Ren" in the movie's credits.<ref name="Episode IX" />
