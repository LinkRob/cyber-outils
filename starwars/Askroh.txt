'''Askroh''' was a [[crime lord]] who lived in [[Cloud City]] on the [[planet]] [[Bespin]].<ref name="Family Affair 1" /> He assisted [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] with a plan to scare [[Jiandy]] out of his [[dream]] for a life in crime.<ref name="Family Affair 2" />

He was interrogating the [[Ugnaught]] Tudd for smuggling [[Alderaanian white]] behind his back when Jiandy stole his brooch. He and his henchman caught them,<ref name="Family Affair 1" /> but Calrissian and Jiandy managed to hop onto a repulsor cargo crate to escape. Askroh and his men chased them and managed to corner them by the docking bay. Askroh ordered his men to throw them out of the city when Lando revealed where Jiandy was hiding his brooch. Jiandy ran off, saying he never wanted to see Calrissian again.<ref name="Family Affair 2" />

After Jiandy ran off, Askroh mentioned that his man had given him a note asking for help with the plan. Lando asked what Askroh would do if he had the choice. This made Askroh very angry, and Calrissian soon left.<ref name="Family Affair 2" />
