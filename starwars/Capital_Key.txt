'''Capital Key''' was an island of the [[Gem Cities of Kwenn]] on the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]], situated within the [[Middle Chain]]. [[Sanctuary Mount]], a [[mountain]] that contained an [[Jedi outpost|outpost]] of the [[Jedi Order]], was situated on Capital Key. The island also contained a government terrace, a media center, and a [[spaceport]]. Capital Key was linked by bridges to [[Vorah Key|Vorah]], [[Gala Key|Gala]], [[Kinnawah Key|Kinnawah]], and [[Rayley Key]]s.<ref name="The Living Force" />
