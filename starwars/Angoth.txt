{{Quote|We've identified a small Nihil base on the planet Angoth, where we believe one of the creatures is being held in preparation for the so-called Jedi hunters the Nihil are using to target our teams in the field.|[[Jedi Master]] [[Ry Ki-Sakka]]|<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" />}}
'''Angoth''' was a small [[planet]] that featured [[Forest|woods]] and a [[Planetary ring|ring]] of scattered [[asteroid]]s. During the [[High Republic Era]], a [[Unidentified settlement (Angoth)|settlement]] existed on Angoth, and the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] also established a [[Nihil base|base]] in a former detention center for [[smuggler]]s. The [[Jedi Order]] and [[Republic Defense Coalition]] embarked on a [[Mission to Angoth|mission]] to capture one of the Nihil's [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures from the base on Angoth.<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" />
