'''Brotwu's''' was a [[cantina]] owned by [[Brotwu]], and run by [[Barton Tep]], on [[The Island]] of a [[Unidentified planet (The Island)|planet]]. Prior to Brotwu's ownership, it was owned by his [[Parent|father]], [[Brotwu (Brotwu's father)|Brotwu]]. The cantina had different menus each with their own set of [[Foodstuff|food]] options. The pilot menu had [[curry]] and [[fruit]], the trooper menu had [[burger]] and [[pie]], the agent menu had [[Hot Dog]] and [[Milk shake|milkshake]], and the force menu had [[soup]] and [[cookie]]s. The cantina also served [[HoloSimulator|HoloSim]]-themed beverages, where the drink order was tied to special in-sim benefits through individuals' [[datapad]]s. These beverages were the [[Battery Recharger]], [["Chip" Magnet]], [[Smooth Shot]], [[Speed Demon]], and [[XP-Resso]]. The cantina did not serve the [[Trandoshan]] [[Kiq Zu]], for a reason only he and the cantina staff knew. Every [[Standard year|year]], around the [[time]] of [[Life Day]], the cantina was decorated with festive lights.<ref name="Castaway" />
