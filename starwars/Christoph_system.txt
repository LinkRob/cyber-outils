The '''Christoph system''',<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> also known as the '''Christophsis system''',<ref name="Clone Trooper Armor">{{Databank|clone-trooper-armor|Clone Trooper Armor}}</ref> was a [[star system]] located in [[Savareen sector]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Christophsis" /> that contained the [[planet]] [[Christophsis]] and its [[Leesis|moon]].<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> The star system was located at grid square Q-16 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="TFABG" />
