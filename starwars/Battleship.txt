A '''battleship''' was a large, [[armor]]ed, and heavily armed<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> type of [[capital ship]] that not only was armed for fighting, but was also typically designed to transport ground troops, launch [[squadron]]s of [[starfighter]]s, and serve as mobile hospitals.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Battleships were designed to be part of force projection, [[fleet]] actions, and for [[Orbital bombardment|bombarding]] [[planet]]s.<ref name="Rise" />

==History==
The [[Alsakan Conflicts]] saw the first deployment of [[capital ship]]s that matched the scale of those used in the later [[Clone Wars]] and [[Galactic Civil War]]. This "dreadnought era" ended with the development of cutting-edge [[turbolaser]]s that could tear apart [[ray shield]]s, therefore rendering large [[warship]]s obsolete. Battleships returned to the [[Hyperspace route|spacelanes]] after [[Verpine]] shipbuilders revolutionized [[deflector shield]] design, only to fall out of favor once again amid economic strains and Republic restrictions on capital ships larger than modern cruisers.<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>

==Types of battleships==
===''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship===
The [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship]] was a modified version of the [[Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter|''Lucrehulk''-class LH-3210 cargo freighter]] used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Visual">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

===''Starhawk''-class battleship===
The [[Starhawk-class battleship|''Starhawk''-class battleship]] was a type of warship created by [[Nadiri Dockyards]] using parts from [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] for the [[New Republic]]. There were two variants, the [[Starhawk-class Battleship Mark I|''Starhawk''-class Battleship Mark I]] and the [[Starhawk-class Battleship Mark II|''Starhawk''-class Battleship Mark II]].<ref name="Rebel Might">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/11/19/rebel-might|text=Rebel Might}}</ref>
