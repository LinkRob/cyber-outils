The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''31 ABY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3308 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''8008''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*The [[First Order]] successfully captures the ''[[Yissira Zyde]]''.<ref name="BTA" />
*[[Poe Dameron]] and [[BB-8]] leave the [[New Republic]] and join the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Poe" />
*[[Clone trooper]] [[Kix|CT-6116]] "Kix" joins [[Sidon Ithano]]'s [[Sidon Ithano's pirate crew|crew]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="TCC">"[[The Crimson Corsair and the Lost Treasure of Count Dooku]]"</ref>
*[[Gannis Ducain]] steals ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' from [[Han Solo]].<ref name="Date1">"Flight of the Falcon, Part 5: Grand Theft Falcon" takes place after the events of "[[The Crimson Corsair and the Lost Treasure of Count Dooku]]," as [[Kix]] is indirectly mentioned, but before [[Life Day 1|''Life Day'' 1]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to c. [[31 ABY]]. "The Crimson Corsair and the Lost Treasure of Count Dooku" refers to the prologue as taking place 50 [[Standard year|years]] prior to the main story. The prologue takes place in between "[[The Wrong Jedi]]" and [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], both of which are placed in [[19 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore the prologue takes place in 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], and the main story takes place around 31 ABY. Therefore, "Flight of the Falcon, Part 5: Grand Theft Falcon" also takes place around 31 ABY.</ref>
