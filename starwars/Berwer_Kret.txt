'''Berwer Kret''' was an individual who served as a [[pilot]] and [[Stalgasin Hive Guard|Hive Guard]] [[captain]] within the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Stalgasin Hive Guard]]. At one point during the [[Clone Wars]], Kret flew a [[Nantex-class territorial defense starfighter|''Nantex''-class starfighter]] in [[Realspace|space]] alongside two [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droid starfighters]].<ref name="X-wing" />
