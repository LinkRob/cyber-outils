The '''Clarr family''' was a [[Chiss family]] that was one of the [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. As such, it was represented in the [[Chiss Syndicure]] and the [[Aristocra]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

In difference to many of the other Ruling families, the Clarr [[Homestead]] was not located on the [[Chiss]] home [[planet]] of [[Csilla]], but on the planet [[Rhigar]]. They also possessed farming and processing facilities on the planet [[Ornfra]] an electronics manufactory on the planet [[Jamiron]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

During and after the [[Nikardun campaigns]], the Clarr family had a rivalry with their fellow Ruling [[Dasklo family]].<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref> According to [[Syndic Prime]] [[Mitth'urf'ianico]], the Clarr were not closely aligned with any other Ruling Family, but had a leaning toward the [[Irizi family]].
