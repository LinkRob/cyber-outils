'''Dizzy''' was an [[Ugnaught]] adrenaline-junkie joyrider<ref name="SWHunters" /> active in the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] tournament at the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] following the fall of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Trailer">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-hunters-trailer|text=Step Inside the Arena in the Latest ''Star Wars: Hunters'' Trailer}}</ref> Reveling in roadkill, Dizzy operated a [[Team Slingshot droideka|modified]] [[droideka]]. The two went by the united persona of [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]].<ref name="SWHunters" /> Over the various fights in the tournament, Slingshot gained a rivalry with fellow competitors [[Utooni]]. The [[Jawa]] [[Gender|brothers]] targeted Dizzy's droideka, whom Dizzy perceived as family.<ref name="7 Things" />During one of his early fights, Dizzy accidentally ran over [[Sentinel]]'s foot while on his team.<ref name="BftA">''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]''</ref> 

When Dizzy first acquired his droideka partner, he was shunned by many people on his homeworld who thought him a fool for restoring a droideka. He enjoyed that his fellow Hunters on Vespaara never made him feel unwelcome. Dizzy often tinkered with his droideka. At one point, he worked on a [[podracer]] kit after a match in the Hunters of the Outer Rim competition.<ref name="BftA" /> 

When goons attacked the Grand Arena intent on destroying it, Dizzy and his droideka fought against the invaders.<ref name="BftA" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:SlingshotLogo-Hunters.png|250px|right|thumb|Slingshot's logo]]
Dizzy, paired with the droideka Slingshot, is one of the playable characters in<ref name="SWHunters" /> the [[2022]] competitive combat game ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', published by [[Zynga]] and [[Lucasfilm Games]].<ref name="Trailer" /> Both Dizzy and his droideka were designed collaboratively by [[Wikipedia:Artist|artists]] [[Clint Young]] and [[Ross Burt]].<ref name="Clint Young Interview">{{WookYouTube|zfdbJGaKx_g|Wookieepedia Interviews - Clint Young}}</ref>
