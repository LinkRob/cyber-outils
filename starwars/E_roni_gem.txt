'''E'roni gems''' were a type of [[gemstone]] from the [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]].<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[saboteur]] [[Serrena]] surveyed the [[E'ronoh's fleet|E'ronoh fleet's]] [[Thylefire Squadron]] as they prepared to leave for a [[Skirmish in the Eiram system|mission]], reflecting that the rallying war cry of the [[squadron]]'s [[captain]]&mdash;[[Princess]] [[Xiri A'lbaran]]&mdash;cut through the shouted voices of those present like [[E'roni]] gems.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
E'roni gems were mentioned in the novel [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Convergence" /> The novel was published on [[November 22]], [[2022]]. Prior to its release, the mention of the gems was included in a [[StarWars.com]] preview for the novel on [[October 4]] of that year.<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-convergence-excerpt|text=A Mysterious Mission Begins in ''The High Republic: Convergence'' &ndash; Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref>
