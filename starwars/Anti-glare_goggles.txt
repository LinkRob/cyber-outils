'''Anti-glare goggles''',<ref name="CEUE" /> also known as '''polarized goggles''',<ref name="TC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> were a make of tinted [[goggles]] that were specialized so that they could protect against the damaging rays of a strong [[Star|sun]]. They were used by members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] while they were stationed in [[Echo Base]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]].<ref name="CEUE" /> They usually came equipped with an [[officer]]'s [[insulated watch cap]].<ref name="Bust Announcement">{{WebCite|url=http://www.the3dstudio.com/specialty-items/show-exclusives/general-mcquarrie-celebration-vii-exclusive-mini-bust|text=General McQuarrie Celebration VII Exclusive Mini Bust|archivedate=20150920224318}}</ref> They were also worn by [[AT-ST driver]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="HC14 HP">{{HelmetCollectionCite|14|Helmets}}</ref> Imperials were provided with polarized goggles when on the [[planet]] [[Sirpar]]. [[Zare Leonis]], however, found that even with the goggles, shadows still seemed strangely shaped. Later, sweat slipped behind his polarized goggles and into his [[eye]]s.<ref name="Secret Academy">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref>
