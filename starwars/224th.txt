The '''224th''' was a unit of [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone troopers]] within the [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Grand Army of the Republic]] at the beginning of the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]]. Among their ranks were [[Trooper/Legends|troopers]] known as the [[Mud Jumpers/Legends|Mud Jumpers]].<ref name="Rookies" /> In [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]],<ref name="Rookies date">''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]'' dates the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]] and ''[[The Clone Wars: Secret Missions 4: Guardians of the Chiss Key]]'' to [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]]. In addition, in ''Guardians of the Chiss Key'', [[Count of Serenno/Legends|Count]] [[Dooku/Legends|Dooku]] mentions a recent [[Mission to Florrum/Legends|run-in]] with a [[Ohnaka Gang/Legends|group]] of [[Weequay/Legends|Weequay]] [[Pirate/Legends|pirates]], which refers to the events of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[The Gungan General]]." Since {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}} places "[[Rookies (episode)|Rookies]]" between ''The Clone Wars'' film and "The Gungan General," it can be concluded that "Rookies" and the [[Battle of Mimban/Legends|Battle of Mimban]] take place in 22 [[Galactic Standard Calendar|BBY]].</ref> the 224th was involved in a [[Battle of Mimban/Legends|battle]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Mimban/Legends|Mimban]].<ref name="Rookies" />

During the [[Ground warfare/Legends|battle]], the MudJumpers of the 224th received a shout-out from the [[BD-3000 luxury droid/Legends|Bettie-Bot]] [[Bettie-Bot VJ|host]] of a [[Music/Legends|musical]] program on the [[HoloNet/Legends|HoloNet's]] [[Grand Army of the Republic broadcast/Legends|Grand Army of the Republic broadcast]] channel. The shout-out, dedicating a song to the group, was heard by other [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] [[Troop/Legends|troopers]]&mdash;[[Echo/Legends|Echo]], [[Hevy/Legends|Hevy]], [[Fives/Legends|Fives]], [[Nub/Legends|Nub]], [[Droidbait/Legends|Droidbait]] and [[Cutup/Legends|Cutup]]&mdash;stationed at a [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]] [[Rishi Station/Legends|listening post]] on the [[Rishi moon|moon]] of the planet [[Rishi/Legends|Rishi]].<ref name="Rookies" />

==Behind the scenes==
