"'''Dark Voyage to Tatooine'''" is a short story printed in ''[[Galaxy Guide 5: Return of the Jedi]]'', written by [[Michael Stern]]. It was also included in the sourcebook's [[Galaxy Guide 5: Return of the Jedi (Second Edition)|second edition]] as well as ''[[The Movie Trilogy Sourcebook]]''. It takes the form of a log entry from [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]] after leaving [[Cloud City/Legends|Cloud City]] with his [[Carbonite/Legends|carbonite cargo]].

==Continuity==
[[Voren Na'al/Legends|Voren Na'al's]] commentary implied that Fett had no conscience.

In the original printing of ''Dark Voyage to Tatooine'', the [[Holowan Massacre]] is mentioned. The mention was removed in the reprints published in [[Galaxy Guide 5: Return of the Jedi (Second Edition)|''Galaxy Guide 5: Return of the Jedi'', Second Edition]] and ''[[The Movie Trilogy Sourcebook]]''.
