'''Cloud cars''' were flying [[Atmosphere|atmospheric]] vehicles that were used to patrol the skies of [[Cloud City]], a [[mining]] colony on the [[planet]] [[Bespin]]. The [[Storm IV Twin-Pod cloud car]]<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> model, featured twin "pods" connected by a [[repulsorlift]] engine, and were fitted with light [[blaster cannon]]s. Each car was crewed by a [[pilot]] and a [[gunner]].<ref name="Databank Cloud Car" />
