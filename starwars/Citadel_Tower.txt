The '''Citadel Tower''' was a large angular tower, part of the [[Imperial Center of Military Research]] on [[Scarif]].

==Description==
[[File:Citadel_Tower_Schematics.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Citadel Tower schematics on a terminal screen.]]
The Citadel Tower was a massive angular tower located in the center of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Center of Military Research]] complex on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]].<ref name="Battlefront" /> It housed a [[Scarif vault|datatape vault]] which contained the plans for multiple top-secret Imperial projects such as the structural [[Data-tape|plans]] for the planet-killing [[battle station]] known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], the details of [[Project War-Mantle]] as well as the classified project known as [[Pax Aurora]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

The Tower featured a command center on its top from where the site command monitored the entirety of the complex, as well as an executive [[Hangar|landing bay]] reserved exclusively for [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]]. It also had a colossal satellite dish situated at very top of the spire for off-world communication purposes and, if necessary, transmission of datatape schematics. The dish's size was necessary as the entire planet was encased in a [[deflector shield]] which prevented ordinary transmissions from passing through. To activate the dish, a complex procedure had to be performed; namely, the site-wide signal disruptor had to be turned off via a master switch located close to the landing bay sector, the dish had to be manually re-oriented via a control panel on its base and the data-tape whose contents would be broadcast had to be manually inserted to a slot.<ref name="Rogue One" /> This lengthy procedure was in place to ensure that no [[droid]]s would access the vault's sensitive contents; theft by organics was considered to be impossible.<ref name="UVG" />

==History==
{{Quote|I know there's a complete engineering archive in the data vault at the Citadel Tower on Scarif.|[[Galen Walton Erso]]|<ref name="Rogue One" />}}
[[File:CitadelTowerDestroyedStarWars.png|thumb|200px|The Citadel Tower is destroyed during the Battle of Scarif.]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the Citadel Tower was targeted by [[agent]]s of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], specifically an infiltration team known as [[Rogue One]], who were attempting to steal the [[Death Star plans]]. By the time the [[Rebel Alliance Navy]] arrived to support the team on the ground, the [[Battle of Scarif]] erupted in space above the planet as well as on the ground. One of the rebels, [[Sergeant]] [[Jyn Erso]], was confronted atop the tower by [[Imperial Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research|Advanced Weapons Research]] [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Krennic]], whilst attempting to transmit the plans to [[Rebel Alliance Navy|ships]] overhead. After a short exchange, she successfully managed to send them to the Alliance [[flagship]] ''[[Profundity]]''. Immediately after this, the security complex was targeted for destruction by the Death Star itself, on the orders of [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], in an attempt to prevent the theft of the plans. On a single-reactor ignition, the battle station's [[superlaser]] fired upon the tower, destroying the top of it which was in the direct path of the beam, with the remains being vaporized in the subsequent shockwave.<ref name="Rogue One" />

==Behind the scenes==
