'''Condor dragons''',<ref name="USW" /> also known as '''mantigrues''',<ref name="Ships">{{EncyclopediaCite|Miscellaneous Ships and Vehicles (2)}}</ref> were a [[species]] of creature native to the [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. The [[Ewok]]s of [[Bright Tree Village]] on Endor posted watchers in the forest canopy above their homes to watch out for condor dragons.<ref name="USW" /> The [[shaman]]<ref name="Logray">{{Databank|logray|Logray}}</ref> [[Logray]] wore a [[Hat|cap]] adorned with the skull of a baby condor dragon.<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref>

{{Creature-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Condor dragons were originally created for the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' movie ''[[Ewoks: The Battle for Endor]]'', which was released in [[1985]]. The species was confirmed to be [[canon]] in the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'' which was released in [[2015]].
