==Behind the scenes==
The [[canon]]ical information on dinosaurs derives from the [[Disney+]] audio descriptions of the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'',<ref name="TM13">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 13: The Jedi}}</ref> ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'',<ref name="Book5">{{BOBF|Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian}}</ref> and ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]''.<ref name="Part II Audio" />

The [[Acklay]] seen in the [[Petranaki Arena|Petranaki arena]] in ''[[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|Attack of the Clones]]'' was inspired by real-life dinosaurs. [[George Lucas]] went out of his way to request a creature bearing characteristics from a [[Wikipedia:Mantis|praying mantis]] and a [[Wikipedia:Velociraptor|velociraptor]].<ref name="Acklay DB">{{DB|creature|acklay|acklay}}</ref>
