"'''Ahsoka's Theme'''," also known as "'''Ahsoka's Triumphant Theme'''," is a theatrical motif written by [[Kevin Kiner]] for the [[Musical score|score]] for [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'']] film and its respective [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (soundtrack)|soundtrack]]. It was written in [[2008]], and used as a leitmotif to represent the character [[Ahsoka Tano]]. It is played throughout the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' TV series, and is featured in the film's "General Loathsom/Ahsoka," "Escape from the Monastery," and "Fight to the End" tracks.<ref name="TCW" />

In ''The Clone Wars'', the motif appears in the episode "[[The Academy]]" when Ahsoka arrives on [[Mandalore]]<ref name="Academy">{{TCW|The Academy}}</ref> and in [[Sabotage (episode)|the]] [[The Jedi Who Knew Too Much|last]] [[To Catch a Jedi|four]] [[The Wrong Jedi|episodes]] of the show's [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|fifth season]], which deal with Ahsoka Tano's confrontation with and eventual resignation from the [[Jedi Order]].

The leitmotif transitions from "[[Yoda's Theme]]," which shares its opening notes with Ahsoka's Theme, in the [[Star Wars Forces of Destiny|''Forces of Destiny'']] episode "[[Teach You, I Will]]," where [[Yoda]] teaches Ahsoka to stay true to herself.<ref name="Teach You">{{FOD|Teach You, I Will}}</ref>

Ahsoka's Theme played as [[Garazeb Orrelios]] let go of [[Ezra Bridger]] aboard an [[Gozanti-class cruiser|Imperial cruiser]] in "[[Spark of Rebellion]]", leaving Bridger to be captured by [[Alexsandr Kallus]].<ref name="Spark">{{Rebels|Spark of Rebellion}}</ref>
