'''Arann Jahr''' was a well-known [[Gender|male]] [[mercenary]] who was considered to be dangerous. He [[Parent|fathered]] a [[Devaronian]] daughter named [[Kada Jahr|Kada]].<ref name="Kada" />

There existed evidence that at some point Jahr had partnered with the [[Aqualish]] male [[Nhego Roolan]] in a large [[Smuggler|smuggling]] operation.<ref name="Nhego">{{Databank|nhego-roolan|Nhego Roolan}}</ref> Eventually the mercenary was [[Death|killed]] in a seemingly routine smuggling operation, a death that was considered mysterious and puzzling.<ref name="Kada" />
