During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] utilized a [[Color|green]]-bladed '''crossguard lightsaber'''.<ref name="THR Show #3" /> The [[Lightsaber|weapon's]] hilt was sheathed in [[Color|silver-white]] [[electrum]], with a spiraling grip of incised green [[seastone]], and had a physical crossguard at one end.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> The hilt was long-handled for a dual-handed grip.<ref name="Collection">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref>

Kriss often used her saber as a meditation tool, spinning it through focused levitation until it emitted a hum. Using the [[the Force]] to change the pitch of the "sabersong", Kriss could connect with multiple minds across the distance of many [[parsec]]s.<ref name="Collection" />
