The '''Delriss system''' was located in the [[Dentari sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]] in grid square O-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Delriss [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Delriss system/Legends|star system]] containing the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Delriss]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it made its first appearance in ''[[Chronicles of the Gatekeeper]]'', a [[2015]] adventure published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for use with the ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Chronicles of the Gatekeeper]]''</ref> Prior to the adventure's publication on [[November 5]],<ref name="Take Hold of the Future">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2015/11/5/take-hold-of-the-future|text=Take Hold of the Future}}</ref> the [[Aquatic moon of Delriss|ocean moon]] of Delriss<ref name="Chronicles" /> was depicted in an article previewing the product that was published on [[April 10]] of the same year on Fantasy Flight Games' [https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/ official website].<ref name="Preview">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2015/4/10/chronicles-of-the-gatekeeper|text=Chronicles of the Gatekeeper}}</ref>
