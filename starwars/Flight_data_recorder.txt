A '''flight data recorder''' was a device installed on nearly every [[Realspace|space]]-faring [[Starship|ship]] in [[the galaxy]] to record flight data. It contained complete records of a ship's range, maximum speed, shield array, weapons payload, and more,<ref name="DB Data Flight Recorder">{{Databank|flight-data-recorder|Flight data recorder}}</ref> making it an invaluable resource for determining the capacities of a particular craft.<ref name="Flight of the Defender" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{RebelsDate|406}}</ref> during a [[Theft of the TIE Defender Elite|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Lothal]], [[Ezra Bridger]] and [[Sabine Wren]] stole the flight data recorder of a prototype [[TIE/D Defender Elite]] so that the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] would know the capabilities of the highly advanced fighter.<ref name="Flight of the Defender" /> Afterwards, the recorder was delivered to [[Alliance High Command]] on [[Yavin 4]] by [[Hera Syndulla]] and [[C1-10P|Chopper]],<ref name="Kindred">{{Rebels|Kindred}}</ref> and the data was analyzed.<ref name="Crawler Commandeers">{{Rebels|Crawler Commandeers}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Flight_data_recorder.jpg|thumb|right|250px|A flight data recorder as depicted in ''Star Wars Rebels''.]]
In real life, a flight data recorder is one half of the device known as the [[black box]],<ref name="Sky1">{{WebCite|url=https://www.skybrary.aero/index.php/Flight_Data_Recorder_(FDR)|text=SKY brary: Flight Data Recorder (FDR)|archivedate=20100602071325}}</ref>  which also exists in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]].<ref name="Poe Dameron: Flight Log">''[[Poe Dameron: Flight Log]]''</ref> A black box, or flight recorder, consist of a flight data recorder (FDR), used to record specific aircraft performance parameters,<ref name="Sky1" /> and a cockpit voice recorder (CVR), which is used to record the audio environment in the flight deck.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.skybrary.aero/index.php/Cockpit_Voice_Recorder_(CVR)|text=SKY brary: Cockpit Voice Recorder (CVR)|archivedate=20100625164138}}</ref>
