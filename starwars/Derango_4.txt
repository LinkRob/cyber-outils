'''Derango 4''' was a hardscrabble astronomical object<ref name="Incredibuilds">''[[Star Wars: A-wing Deluxe Book and 3D Wood Model]]''</ref> in the [[Khuiumin sector]]'s [[Derango system]], within grid square O-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]] and the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> [[Jake Farrell]] briefly retired to Derango 4 after quitting his service to the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[flight instructor]].<ref name="Incredibuilds" /> Disappointed with his retirement life<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Pilots of the Rebel Alliance}}</ref> and wishing to [[pilot]] a [[starfighter]] again,<ref name="Incredibuilds" /> the [[human]] joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]] as part of its [[Yellow Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Yellow Squadron]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
Derango 4 was first mentioned in the [[2017]] activity book ''[[Star Wars: A-wing Deluxe Book and 3D Wood Model]]'', written by [[Michael Kogge]].<ref name="Incredibuilds" />
