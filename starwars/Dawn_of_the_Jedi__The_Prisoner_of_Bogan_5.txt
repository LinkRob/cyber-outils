'''''Dawn of the Jedi: The Prisoner of Bogan'' 5''' is the fifth issue in the [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: The Prisoner of Bogan|''Prisoner of Bogan'']] story arc of the ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series and the tenth issue of the series in total. It is set in [[25,793 BBY]] during the earliest days of the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]], in a [[Pre-Republic era|time before]] the polarization of the [[Light side of the Force/Legends|light]] and [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark sides]] of [[The Force/Legends|the Force]]. This issue was released on [[May 22]], [[2013]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Je'daii Daegen Lok is on the verge of launching a war against the Je'daii. With the Force-strong stranger Xesh as his ally and a young Je'daii captive, Lok is close to his goal.''

''But Je'daii Rangers are close behind, as the Masters at the Temples have unlocked some of the secrets of Xesh's origins. Something worse than the prisoner of Bogan is loose in the Tython system!''
