The '''''Darkflare''''' was a state-of-the-art [[S-46I Medium Scout Ship]] that was manufactured by [[Sienar Fleet Systems]] for the [[Galactic Empire]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|Intelligence]] forces launched [[Operation Night Fire]] to capture the ''Darkflare'' while it was under construction at a Seinar Fleet Systems orbital shipyard. It was commanded by [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Commander]] [[Trey Trellen]].<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" />
