'''Anzellan goggles''' were a type of eyewear that protected a wearers [[eye]]s from dust and sand. They could fit both for members of the [[Anzellan]] [[species]] and [[merqaal]]s species. They had [[Color|black]] lenses with thick brown and tan sockets and a brown and tan strap.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[Unidentified merchant (Anzellan goggles)|human merchant]] had a pair of the goggles while operating a stall in the [[city]] [[Mos Eisley]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> they were approached by the [[merqaal]] [[Nix]] and his human companion [[Kay Vess]]. Interested in the merqaal, the merchant inquired about his [[Nix's homeworld|homeworld]] before offering him a pair of Anzellan googles. Vess initial dismissed the offer citing the assumed price, but the merchant insisted on giving it to Nix for free.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Anzellan goggles appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They are not part of any quest but will give the player the "Dust goggles" Nix cosmetic.<ref name="Outlaws" />
