'''ArmaTek''' was a manufacturing company that produced [[VL-61/79 proton bomb]]s for the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[TIE/sk x1 experimental air superiority fighter]]s.<ref name="ROUVG" /> ArmaTek also produced the [[SJ-91 orbital mine]]s used by the Empire to maintain its rule in locations it did not often patrol, with some of those ArmaTek minefields continuing to exist long after the [[Battle of Jakku|Empire's fall]]. ArmaTek mines were in possession of [[Thrawn's forces]] during their [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] on the [[extragalactic]] [[planet]] [[Peridea]].<ref name="Comprehensive SWE" />
