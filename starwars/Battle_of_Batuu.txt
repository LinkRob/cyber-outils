We need bodies. We need friends. We need skills. We need ships and food and fuel. We need eyes and ears on the ground. The '''mission to Batuu''', later known as the '''Battle of Batuu''', was a battle that took place during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. Initially a mission by the Resistance to find allies and sanctuary on [[Batuu]] and an occupation by the First Order when it deployed the [[709th Legion]] to occupy [[Black Spire Outpost]] and capture or [[Death|kill]] Resistance fugitives, it erupted into a battle between the two factions.

==Prelude==
The loss of the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar]] forced the [[Resistance High Command]] to [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuate]] their base. However, [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] [[Hyperspace tracker|tracked]] the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]] through [[hyperspace]] and [[Battle of Oetchi|gave chase through space]]. [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] eventually sacrificed herself and her [[cruiser]] to allow the remaining [[Resistance]] [[Soldier|fighters]] to escape to the [[planet]] [[Crait]] in the [[Crait system]].<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

Unlike the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]],<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> the [[Battle of Crait]] resulted in a disastrous defeat for the Resistance.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==The mission==
===Establishing strength===
{{Quote|There are Resistance scum hiding out here somewhere on Batuu&mdash;and the ''Red Fury'' will burn down this entire rats' nest to smoke them out, Lieutenant Agnon.|[[Unidentified First Order stormtrooper sergeant|First Order stormtrooper sergeant]]|<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1" />}}
When the [[First Order]] received word that there was a Resistance presence on Batuu, [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] traveled to the planet on board the ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer ''[[Finalizer]]''.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> While Ren remained in orbit, the First Order's [[709th Legion]] was deployed to occupy the Black Spire Outpost and eliminate all Resistance forces in the area, under the command of [[Lieutenant]] [[Agnon]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 1" /> First Order [[Mountain trooper]]s also patrolled the mountain ranges surrounding the outpost for Resistance activity.<ref name="Hasbro Mountain trooper">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|packtype=The First Order 4-Pack|pack=Mountain Trooper, MSE Droid, Kylo Ren & Commander Pyre|altlink=https://galacticfigures.com/figureDetails.aspx?id=20416}}</ref>

The First Order's search for Resistance [[Spy|spies]] led the 709th to the [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities|Den of Antiquities]], where they interrogated the shop's owner, [[Dok-Ondar]]. While questioning the [[Ithorian]] shopkeeper, the sergeant inspected one of the relics&mdash;[[Ki-Adi-Mundi's lightsaber|the lightsaber]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]], who fought in the [[Clone Wars]] until he was [[Order 66|betrayed]] and [[Great Jedi Purge|executed]] by his [[clone trooper]]s. Dok-Ondar interpreted the sergeant's questions as veiled threats and claimed he was nothing more than a humble proprietor, but the sergeant had heard of his reputation for selling valuable relics for the right price, which led him to believe that the Resistance would seek out Dok-Ondar in order to acquire the [[Jedi]] [[lightsaber]]. Before the stormtroopers could leave, having warned Dok-Ondar to report any contact with the Resistance or suffer the confiscation and destruction of property, the Ithorian took note of the sergeant's ignorance of [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]] and felt the need to explain how he procured the lightsaber.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2" />

Resistance operatives led by Finn led a cover mission to infiltrate the orbiting ''Finalizer'', with Finn disguising himself as a stormtrooper.<ref name="Timelines" /> He is given support by Resistance members on the planet who he communicates with via a [[datapad]].<ref name="Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad]]</ref> While on board, he learned that Kylo Ren was closing in on Resistance agents at Black Spire Outpost. Meanwhile, the ''[[Halcyon]]'' passenger liner arrived at Batuu during its [[Halcyon's 275th anniversary cruise|''Halcyon''{{'s}} 275th anniversary cruise]], having just been commandeered by First Order forces led by [[Lieutenant]] [[Harman Croy]] in search of Resistance spies.<ref name="Timelines" /> Despite the First Order's presence, passengers were still allowed to travel to and from Black Spire's [[Docking Bay 3 (Batuu)|Docking Bay 3]].<ref name="Galactic Starcruiser" />

On the surface, Ren confronted Resistance spy [[Vi Moradi]] personally. He used the Force to briefly capture her, but Moradi was freed when the [[Jedi]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] arrived and [[Lightsaber duel|dueled]] Ren. The fight was cut short when Ren was forced to leave upon being alerted to an "incident" on the destroyer.<ref name="Kylo vs Rey Galaxy's Edge">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@attractionsmagazine|name=Attractions Magazine|video=UGUsYWmVV2I|text=Star Wars Lightsaber Battle and Stunt Show in Galaxy's Edge during media event &#124; Attractions Magazine|date=2019-12-04}}</ref>

===Battle of Batuu===
With the base on Batuu discovered, the Resistance elected to flee the planet for a [[Pacara base]]. Members of a Resistance group stationed at a base in the [[Black Spire Ruins]]&mdash;including many new recruits&mdash;were briefed on a mission by Rey via a hologram projected by [[BB-8]]. The team boarded an [[Intersystem Transport Ship]] commanded by [[Lieutenant]] [[Bek]] and piloted by [[Nien Nunb]]. While being escorted by [[Poe Dameron]] in his [[T-70 X-wing starfighter|X-wing]], along with other X-wings, the transport crossed paths with<ref name="ROTR" /> the ''Finalizer'',<ref name="TROS Visual Dictionary" /> got caught in its tractor beam, and was brought on board. Dameron's fighters were forced to flee the scene in order to go for help.<ref name="ROTR" />

[[File:Resistance_fleet_ROTR.png|thumb|left|280px|The Resistance engages the First Order above Batuu.]]
The Resistance members were taken captive and scheduled to be interrogated before they were broken out of their cell by another group of Resistance operatives who had infiltrated the ship. They included [[Finn]], who was secretly on board disguised as a stormtrooper. During their escape in a [[First Order Fleet Transport]] vehicle, the Resistance members crossed paths with [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]], [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]s, and Kylo Ren.<ref name="ROTR" />

The crew of the ''Finalizer'' was taken by surprise when a large group of Resistance [[starfighter]]s, [[bomber]]s, [[frigate]]s, and other vessels jumped out of [[hyperspace]] led by Dameron in his fighter, starting the attack. The Resistance fleet engaged the Star Destroyers, while starfighters attacked one another. During the engagement the fleeing recruits were nearly captured by Kylo Ren before a ''Vakbeor''-class cargo frigate broadsided the Star Destroyer, but was soon destroyed by overwhelming attacks from the ''Finalizer''{{'s}} weapons emplacements. The recruits' transport then boarded an [[First Order Short-Range Evacuation Vehicle|escape pod]] that traveled through the space battle and returned them back to the ruins on Batuu, where their pod and Bek's came in for a rough, but relatively safe landing.<ref name="ROTR" />

==Aftermath==
Following the battle, Rey and [[Chewbacca]] sneaked aboard the ''Halcyon'', where Kylo Ren arrrived and was confronted by Rey.<ref name="Timelines" /> The two engaged in another brief duel before parting ways. Operatives onboard the ship then freed it from First Order control.<ref name="Galactic Starcruiser" />

Due to the heavy damage sustained by the ''Finalizer'' during the battle, Kylo Ren transferred his command to his new flagship, the [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']]. He brought Hux with him to the destroyer, where he would now serve below General [[Enric Pryde|Pryde]] and have little independent authority.<ref name="TROS Visual Dictionary" />

==Behind the scenes==
The mission to Batuu first appeared in the [[Galaxy's Edge 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge (comic series)|''Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge'' comic series]] that was released on [[April 24]], [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/75139/star_wars_galaxys_edge_2019_1|text=Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge (2019) #1}}</ref> It later made an appearance in the novel ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''<ref name="Black Spire" /> and made up the storyline for the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] theme park.<ref name="EW">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/movies/2019/02/28/star-wars-actors-will-appear-in-disneys-rise-of-the-resistance-ride|text=Star Wars actors bring their characters to life in Disney theme parks' Rise of the Resistance ride|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2019-02-27|archivedate=20190914091306}}</ref> A full timeline of events encompassing the ''Galaxy's Edge'' attractions, the comic book, and the events portrayed at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel was published in the ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' book released in [[2023]].<ref name="Timelines" />

Within the ''Rise of the Resistance'' attraction, in the event of the Kylo Ren animatronic not functioning properly, an alternative "B-Mode" final encounter between Ren and the guests takes place where he brings his [[Kylo Ren's TIE silencer|TIE silencer]] to the window outside to locate the guests before a shot knocks him away.<ref name="YouTube">{{YouTube|video=7P7yVcss9Ag|text=Rise of the Resistance Kylo Ren "B Mode" vs. "A Mode" BOTH VERSIONS!|channel=channel/UCBlQTrYOWMMVg9GZrZLgePw|name=Drew the Disney Dude}}</ref> Similarly, delays in unloading transports after the return to Batuu will result in audio dialog of Finn arguing with the R5 units, which want greater compensation for their assistance, as well as comm chatter regarding the search for Bek's escape pod, the continued progress of the space battle, and additional pods coming in behind the park guests.<ref name="WDW">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2020/08/video-r5-droid-haggles-with-finn-fights-over-doors-and-resistance-radio-chatter-fills-minutes-of-time-during-pauses-in-movement-on-star-wars-rise-of-the-resistance|text=VIDEO: R5 Droid Haggles with Finn, Fights Over Doors, and Resistance Radio Chatter Fills Minutes of Time During Pauses in Movement on Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance|author=Alicia|date=2020-08-29|work=[https://wdwnt.com WDW News Network]|archivedate=20201219010750}}</ref>
