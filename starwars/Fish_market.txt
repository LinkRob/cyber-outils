The '''fish market''' was located on<ref name="Aphra 7" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Corellia]].<ref name="Aphra 7" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 7|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 7]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the [[smuggler]]s [[Remy (smuggler)|Remy]] and [[Sana Starros]] visited the [[fish]] market, where they were seen by an individual named [[Mar]]. The next [[Standard day|day]], a member of the [[Unbroken Clan]] criminal syndicate reported Mar's sighting of the two smugglers at the market to the syndicate's [[General]] [[Vukorah]] as she interrogated Remy for information about a [[Path engine]] he had sold to [[Beol De'Rruyet]]<ref name="Aphra 7" />&mdash;the founder of the company [[De'Rruyet Industries]].<ref name="Aphra 10">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 10|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 10]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The fish market was mentioned in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 7|seventh issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 7" /> on [[January 20]], [[2021]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/82210/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2020_7|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020) #7}}</ref>
