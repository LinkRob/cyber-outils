'''Firrhana''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Firrhana system]] in the [[Ojoster sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was once controlled by the [[Sith]], who conducted sacrifice rituals in a [[Sith temple (Firrhana)|temple]] they built there. It was abandoned after an ecological collapse caused by a volcanic eruption.

==Description==
An ancient volcanic eruption rendered Firrhana almost uninhabitable, characterized by alternating fields of lava and [[Fungus|fungi]]. The planet was abandoned in the aftermath, only a few hardy lifeforms managing to continue their existence. A Sith temple remained in ruins atop several blackened peaks in the middle of a lava field.<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />

==History==
{{Quote|I'm tracking a Jedi.|Tualon Yaluna to Iskat Akaris|<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />}}
Firrhana was once a Sith world that housed a temple used to sacrifice victims in blood rituals before a volcanic eruption forced its abandonment. Centuries later, by the end of the [[Clone Wars]], a [[bounty hunter]] named [[Jakadis]] had converted the temple into his personal fortress, where he imprisoned several [[Jedi]] he had captured. Having heard of this, [[Grand Inquisitor|the Grand Inquisitor]] tested former [[Jedi Knight|Knights]] [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] and [[Tualon Yaluna]] by sending them on a [[Mission to Firrhana|mission]] to eliminate the Jedi. With the aid of a [[Purge Trooper]] named [[Sixty-Seven]], Akaris infiltrated Jakadis' defenses and proceeded to kill the three Jedi she found, taking their lightsabers as trophies. On her way out, she slew Jakadis and drank a toast in the temple's sacrifice chamber.<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />
