The '''Exegol Excavation Project''' was an [[Archaeology|archaeological]] endeavour undertaken around [[35 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> and led by [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Exegol]] and the final defeat of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], [[Darth Sidious]]. The project's main focus was the excavation of the [[Sith Citadel]] on [[Exegol]], where its members discovered evidence of [[Galactic Emperor]] Sheev Palpatine's [[Sith]] ties, including scrolls mentioning his [[Sith Master]], [[Darth Plagueis]], and artifacts from [[Planet|worlds]] thought to be mythological, like [[Moraband]] and [[Dromund Kaas]]. It also uncovered scrolls annotated by Sidious himself, detailing a prophesied [[Sith'ari]]: an [[Anakin Skywalker|individual]] who would lead the Sith to glory.<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

The findings of the excavation project contributed to Kin's [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', detailing the [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformation]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] into the [[New Order|titular]] [[Galactic Empire]], as well as its subsequent [[Galactic Civil War|fall]] at the hands of [[Luke Skywalker]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Rise" />
