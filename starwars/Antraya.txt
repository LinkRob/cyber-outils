'''Antraya''' was a [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[artist]] who was a member of the [[Torada Collective]], a [[Naboo]] artists' group which consisted of individuals who did not feel like they fit in on Naboo. She was also the sister of [[Jamillia]], who reigned as the elected [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] of Naboo<ref name="QH" /> between [[26 BBY]] and [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Reign">''[[Queen's Shadow]]'' establishes that [[Monarch of Naboo|monarchs]] of [[Naboo]] can only reign for, at maximum, two two-[[Standard year|year]] terms. In ''Queen's Shadow'', [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] is succeeded by [[Réillata]], who had already served one term in office prior to Amidala's reign, thus limiting her to one more term in office. The novel is set four years after the [[Invasion of Naboo]], which is dated to [[32 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'', dating Réillata's re-election to [[28 BBY]] and the end of her second term to [[26 BBY]]. ''[[Queen's Hope]]'', set in [[22 BBY]], states that Jamillia is nearing the end of her time in office, indicating that she is nearing the end of her second term in office, and thus that she was first elected [[Queen]] in 26 BBY, and that her reign ended that year.</ref> In that [[Standard year|year]], Antraya was one of five members of the collective living in an [[artist commune]] on the [[planet]] [[Nooroyo]]. Upon the onset of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Nooroyo joined the Separatists, while Naboo remained part of the Republic. Concerned for her sister's safety, Jamillia asked Naboo [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] to [[Mission to Nooroyo|retrieve]] the Torada Collective members from behind enemy lines, with only Antraya choosing to return to Naboo.<ref name="QH" />
