An '''alta-wave calibration device''' was a piece of technology that included a flat [[Color|gray]] base with a hooked arm on a stand at one end.<ref name="CL" /> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}}, as depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the [[junk dealer]] [[Watto]] had an illegal alta-wave calibration device sat by the back door of his [[Watto's junkshop|junkshop]] in the [[city]] of [[Mos Espa]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] when [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his allies visited in search of a [[T-14 hyperdrive generator]].<ref name="CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], an alta-wave calibration device was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="CL" /> from the [[2000]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', which was written by [[Kristin Lund]], illustrated by [[Richard Chasemore]] and [[Hans Jenssen]], and marked the first mention of the [[Alta-wave calibration device/Legends|device]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity. The book's illustration of Watto's junkshop<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref> including the alta-wave calibration device, then appeared as an area where players would search out specific items in the<ref name="Spy">{{SWArchive|url=play/online-games/galactic-spy|text=''Star Wars Galactic Spy''|int=Star Wars Galactic Spy}}</ref> [[2012]]<ref name="SpyDate">{{SW|url=news/starwars-com-weekly-roundup-september-28-2012|text=StarWars.com Weekly Roundup: September 28, 2012}}</ref> non-[[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Galactic Spy]]''.<ref name="Spy" />
