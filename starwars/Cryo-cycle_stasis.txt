'''Cryo-cycle stasis''' was a process where items or even lifeforms were preserved by cooling to sub-zero [[temperature]]s. Usually, that process was used only for short periods, and prolonged stasis could cause a form of [[poison]]ing. Having learned of a [[Sith]] plot to [[Order 66|destroy]] the [[Jedi Order]] toward the end of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[clone trooper]] [[Kix|CT-6116]] was abducted and put in a [[cryo-cycle stasis pod]] aboard a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Obrexta III|cruiser]] by [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. He was freed from the stasis [[31 ABY|fifty]] [[Standard year|years]] later, when [[Sidon Ithano]]'s [[Sidon Ithano's pirate crew|crew]] discovered his pod.<ref name="Crimson Corsair">{{StoryCite|story=The Crimson Corsair and the Lost Treasure of Count Dooku|book=Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I}}</ref>
