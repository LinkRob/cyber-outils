"'''Fashion'''" is a short comic story published in [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic 6.23|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars Comic'' 6.23]] and later in [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Magazine 7|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Magazine 7]].

==Publisher's summary==
''A vast leisure satellite high above the towering spires of Coruscant gives the heroes of the Republic a chance to recharge their batteries. The ever-stylish Padmé Amidala spots a familiar caped figure while watching a fashion show &ndash; and she's not there for the clothes! Can Mace Windu thwart the Sith infiltrator?''<ref>{{TitanMagazines|url=t/star-wars-clone-wars-magazine/us/7|text=Star Wars: The Clone Wars Magazine Magazine Issue #7}}</ref>
