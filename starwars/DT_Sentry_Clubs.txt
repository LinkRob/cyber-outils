The '''DT Sentry Clubs''' were a pair of [[Melee weapon|clubs]] owned and utilized by the [[Wookiee]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Grozz]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his standard [[KX Clubs]]. They consisted of a pair of [[DT-series sentry droid]] [[Limb|arms]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
DT Sentry Clubs was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Grozz's KX Clubs, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Shyyyo Defender]]. This wrap could be unlocked by completed Part 3 of the "Welcome to the Arena!" introductory Quest.<ref name="Hunters" />
