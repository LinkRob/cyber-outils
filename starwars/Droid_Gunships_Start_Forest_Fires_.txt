'''''Droid Gunships Start Forest Fires!''''' was a [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]]<ref name="Atlas" /> created in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> It depicted an [[HMP droid gunship]] starting a [[forest]] [[fire]] with the words "Droid Gunships Start Forest Fires!" above it, and the words "Report all sightings to your local [[Wookiee warden]]" below it. The poster was written in [[High Galactic]] alphabet and was eventually collected in ''[[Yoda's Guide to the Galaxy]]'', a book written by the [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] during his exile on [[Dagobah]].<ref name="Atlas" />

==Behind the scenes==
''Droid Gunships Start Forest Fires!'' was first mentioned in the [[2021]] non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Yoda's Galaxy Atlas]]''.<ref name="Atlas" />
