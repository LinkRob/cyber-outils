The '''DB-41 sporting blaster pistol''', or '''DB-41 blaster pistol'''<ref name="Mandalorian VisualGuide" /> was a model of [[blaster pistol]]. The [[Abyssin]] [[gangster]] [[Gor Koresh]] carried one that was modified for Abyssin sight.<ref name="Mandalorian VisualGuide" /> [[Boba Fett]] also carried one in his right [[holster]] while fighting [[Stormtrooper]]s on [[Tython]], causing a [[power generator]] of an [[E-HOB heavy repeating laser cannon]] to explode<ref name="Chapter14" /> and fighting the [[Pyke Syndicate]] enforcers on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="In the Name of Honor">{{BOBF|Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor}}</ref>

The DB-41 blaster was once a consumer-grade sporting pistol. It feature a power caps clipped and its [[barrel]] circuitry resynchronized to increase its lethality. The [[blaster]] is rugged, cheap, and lightweight-making for fast draws. It also has a compact [[power cell]] in handle, along with an indicator to warn low ammunition. There is a gas intermix chamber, an internal optical processors, and a secured objective lens aligned to barrel. The [[scope]] is braced directly above the blaster barrel for accuracy.<ref name="Mandalorian VisualGuide" />
