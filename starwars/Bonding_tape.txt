'''Bonding tape''' was a type of tool. During a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] mission to [[Basteel]], [[Major]] [[Lokmarcha]] used bonding tape to strap a lamp to the muzzle of his [[blaster rifle]].<ref name="Moving" /> The [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] had a roll of bonding tape in her toolkit.<ref name="Survival" /> When [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and the former [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Finn]] stole the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', Rey used a roll of bonding tape lying around, to patch a [[gas]] leak aboard the [[freighter]].<ref name="TFA" />
