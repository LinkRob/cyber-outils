"'''Bogling'''" is the thirteenth episode of the webseries ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Creatures]]''.<ref name="SWKids" /> The episode was released on [[StarWarsKids.com]] on [[January 17]], [[2023]],<ref name="Episode" /> alongside the series' fourteenth episode, "[[Eopie (episode)|Eopie]]."<ref name="SWKids" />

==Official description==
''Aree has to put his expert tracking skills to the test to find the adorable yet timid boglings.''

==Plot summary==
The [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] sends [[SF-R3]] ("Aree") and [[CAM]] to [[Bogano]] to find a bogling burrow. Alarmed at the presence of strangers, the boglings retreat to their burrows. Aree and CAM navigate through the tunnels and reach a fork. The two droids argue about which path to go. Aree decides to follow the footprints left behind by the boglings while CAM takes a different path.

Aree eventually falls down a trapdoor and narrowly avoids landing on sharp rocks by holding onto a lasso. After landing, Aree is chased by a giant boulder and flees deeper underground. Aree regrets not listening to CAM before discovering that he can "listen" for CAM. Hearing the sound of boglings he continues his journey but bumps into CAM. CAM leads him to another chamber where the two droids find the boglings living among ancient ruins. Aree thanks CAM for finding the boglings and agrees that it will be good to use both his [[photoreceptor]]s and audio sensors in the future. The two depart on [[Aree's ship|SF-R3's starship]].
