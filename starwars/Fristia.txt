'''Fristia''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who was a [[Padawan]] in the [[Jedi Order]] during the late [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="TCC" /> In [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> she and [[Yord Fandar]] interrupted their fellow fellow Padawans, [[Jecki Lon]] and [[Entyr]] training in a moving obstacle course in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] and Fristia was impressed by Lon running the course blindfolded. Lon didn't know Fritia well and guessed that she had only recently been chosen as a Padawan and had spent most of her time with her Master away on missions and had only arrived back  on Coruscant that day.<ref name="TCC" />

==Personality and traits==
Fristia was a human female with [[Color|tan]] skin and blond [[hair]], which she had all dyed green except her [[Padawan braid]]. Lon considered her to be pretty.<ref name="TCC" />
