The '''1st Sector Army''' was one of twenty [[Sector Army|Sector Armies]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The [[army]] served as a reserve command along with the subsequent Sector Armies up to the [[6th Sector Army]].<ref name="BMF 18" /> Like all other Sector Armies in the [[Galactic Republic]], the 1st Sector Army had one of the Republic's twenty [[Haven-class medical station|''Haven''-class medical stations]] towed to them.<ref name="Note" /> At the start of the Clone Wars, the Grand Army was organized into ten [[Systems Army|Systems Armies]], with each being split into two Sector Armies and drawn from a specific area of [[the galaxy]]. The Sector Armies were responsible for the defense of their specific areas.<ref name="BMF 18" />

As such, the 1st Sector Army belonged to the same Systems Army as another. Being a Sector Army, the unit<ref name="BMF 18" /> contained 147,456 [[clone trooper]]s who were divided into four [[corps]].<ref name="CL 2016" /> Corresponding to the other Sector Armies, it was attached to one of the twenty numbered [[fleet]]s of the [[Republic Navy]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The 1st Sector Army was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 18|eighteenth issue]] of the [[De Agostini]] magazine ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'',<ref name="BMF 18" /> published around [[May 6]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BuildFalconDates|18}}</ref> The 1st Sector Army was introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via the [[2003]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' sourcebook ''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]'' by [[Wizards of the Coast]] as the [[First Army|Azure Hammer Command]],<ref name="CCW">''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]''</ref> later identified as the First Army in the [[2012]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'' by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Paul R. Urquhart]].<ref name="EGW">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref>
