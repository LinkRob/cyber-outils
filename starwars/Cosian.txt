'''Cosians''' were a [[species]] of long-lived [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[humanoid]]s from [[Cosia]]. [[Jedi Master]] [[Tera Sinube]] was a Cosian who served the [[Jedi Order]] from the [[High Republic Era]] to the [[Clone Wars]].

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:TeraSinubeDetail-SWE.png|thumb|left|180px|Tera Sinube, an elderly Cosian Jedi.]]
Cosians were a [[species]] of [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[humanoid]]s<ref name="Cosian" /> from [[Cosia]]<ref name="DoH" /> who had beaked [[mouth]]s, four-fingered [[Limb|hands]],<ref name="Cosian" /> [[eye]]s with lids that blinked upward instead of down, and a long tail that ended in a tuft of [[hair]]. Due to their reptilian biology, Cosians hatched from [[egg]]s.<ref name="DoH" /> Cosians were also known for being long-lived, with their average life expectancy being about 200 [[Standard year|standard years]],<ref name="DoH" /> although the Jedi Master [[Tera Sinube]], reached an age of over 400 standard years.<ref name="Birth">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic Adventures (2022) 8|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2022) 8]] to [[382 BBY]]. ''The High Republic Adventures'' (2022) 8 establishes that Tera Sinube was older than [[Kaktorf]]. The comic also establishes that Kaktorf was fourty-six [[Standard year|years]] old at that time, so he must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[428 BBY]]. Therefore, Sinube must have been born before 428 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> Cosians could be [[Force-sensitive]].<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

==Society and culture==
Cosians spoke [[Cosian (language)|Cosian]], a trilling, chirping [[language]]. The language mingled with the sounds of their [[homeworld]], Cosia. Over time, the Cosians built villages across their homeworld, each ruled by a council of elders. When Cosian eggs hatched, a [[Cosian egg-hatching celebration|great celebration]] would be held to honor the emergence of new life and wisdom.<ref name="DoH" />

==Cosians in the galaxy==
[[Jedi Master]] [[Tera Sinube]] was a [[Gender|male]] Cosian who was a member of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Tera Sinube">{{Databank|tera-sinube|Tera Sinube}}</ref> Beginning as member of the [[Jedi]] during the [[High Republic Era]],<ref name="Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> Sinube later became a member of the [[Jedi High Council]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> Though he later left the council, Sinube went on to mentor [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] and younger Jedi such as [[Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="Lightsaber Lost">{{TCW|Lightsaber Lost}}</ref> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/future-lucasfilm-projects-revealed|text=Future Lucasfilm Projects Revealed}} states that ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' is set ten [[Standard year|years]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, ''Obi-Wan Kenobi'' must be set in [[9 BBY]].</ref> [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] encountered the [[Death|corpse]] of Sinube in [[Fortress Inquisitorius]] on the [[moon]] [[Nur]].<ref name="K4">{{Kenobi|Part IV}}</ref>

At one point, the [[Nu-Cosian]]s, a [[DNA|genetically]] [[engineer]]ed [[subspecies]] of the Cosians, were created to colonize the [[planet]] [[Cos Secundu]].<ref name="A&S" />
