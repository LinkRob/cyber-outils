'''Asgar Ro''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Evereni]] who held the title of [[Eye of the Nihil]] in the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]]s during the [[High Republic Era]]. A son of [[Shalla Ro]] and an [[Asgar Ro's father|Evereni male]], he eventually had a son named [[Marchion Ro]]. In [[252 BBY]], Asgar and Shalla had a disagreement over the pace of Nihil expansion, which led him to kill Shalla and take the position of Eye of the Nihil for himself.

As Eye, Ro expanded the Nihil quickly and with great magnitude, using [[hyperspace]] [[Path]]s provided by [[Prisoner|captive]] [[Mari San Tekka]]. Ro was eventually killed by a disgruntled [[Lourna Dee]], and kicked by his son during his dying moments. Marchion would go on to replace his father as the Eye of the Nihil, expanding the group's scope even further.

==Biography==
===Early life===
Asgar Ro was an [[Evereni]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> [[Reproduction|born]] to [[Shalla Ro]] and his [[Asgar Ro's father|father]].<ref name="EotS1" /> He eventually fathered a son of his own named [[Marchion Ro]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

===Eye of the Nihil===
====Guardian of the Paths====
{{Quote|And I think you'll see... <br /> ...I'm a man with a ''vision''|Asgar Ro to the Nihil|<ref name="EotS1" />}}
[[File:MarchionAsgar-THREOTS1.jpg|thumb|left|120px|Asgar wagers his son's life]]
Asgar became upset at how long his mother was waiting to combat the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Jedi]]. Onboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', he challenged Shalla's waiting, leading to an argument that led to him pushing her over a railing to her [[death]]. After the death of his mother at his own hands, Ro brought his son to the rest of the [[Nihil]] in [[No-Space]]. There he met with the [[Tempest Runner]]s, making a wager for everything he owned, including the ''Gaze Electric'', if they could shoot down his son in a single fighter, using however many fighters they wanted. The two senior Tempest Runners, [[Pan Eyta]] and [[Kassav Milliko]], eagerly accepted this wager and sent up scores of fighters against the young Marchion. As the fighters approached to engage, Ro called upon [[Mari San Tekka]] to transmit [[Path]]s to Marchion, allowing him to jump into [[hyperspace]] at unpredictable and seemingly random locations. This confused the Nihil and led to Marchion destroying many of their fighters. Ro commanded his son to stop, but Marchion refused to listen and killed more than his [[Parent|father]] wanted before finally stopping at a threat on his own life. This served as a demonstration of the [[Path engine]], which Ro used as leverage to keep the Nihil loyal to his family alone, if they wanted access to the Paths. Asgar took the role of the [[Eye of the Nihil]] for himself after making this deal, even pondering adding a third [[Tempest]].<ref name="EotS1" />

As Eye, Asgar united the Nihil,<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> and quickly expanded the group from a small<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> savage gang of pirates<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> into a much larger and feared organization using the hyperspace Paths provided by San Tekka.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> Just like Shalla and Marchion, he repeatedly asked San Tekka to reveal how she discovered the Paths, but she could not explain it to any of them.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> As the secretive guardian of the Paths which allowed the pirates to expand their power throughout the [[Outer Rim Territories]], the Eye's power was often questioned by the unruly Tempest Runners, but his position held a double vote which allowed him to break deadlocks and steer the decisions the way he desired. In addition, Ro made a pact with the Tempest Runners known as the Rule of Three, a system that devised the distribution of the spoils from Nihil raids among the leadership.<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> One-third of the riches went to the [[Strike]]s, [[Storm (rank)|Storms]] and [[Cloud (rank)|Clouds]] which made up the three Tempests of the Nihil. The second third would go the Tempest Runners, who orchestrated the raids. And, should the Nihil make use of the Paths that Ro had delivered to them, he received his own third.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> Ro constructed the [[Great Hall of the Nihil]], located in the center of No-Space. He also owned a collection of texts in the [[Gaze library|''Gaze'' library]]; his son Marchion would later recall them as forgotten by Asgar and gathering dust.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />

In [[247 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Ro summoned the Nihil for a meeting in the Great Hall of the Nihil. During the meeting, he accused Tempest Runner [[Xanaven]] of trying to subvert the Rule of Three by not sharing the profits from raids he had undertaken in secret. Xanaven denied the accusation and called Marchion a [[leech]]. Xanaven broke free of his restraints and attempted to attack Asgar. [[Storm]] [[Lourna Dee]] assaulted and killed Xanaven to protect Ro. Immediately after, Ro promoted Dee as the new [[Tempest Runner]] to replace Xanaven.<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref> Afterward, Ro was widely seen, including by his own son, as a despot and tyrant.<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>

====Downfall====
{{Quote|Help... Help me..please. Just going to sit there and ''watch'', eh? ... Good boy.|Asgar Ro's last words|<ref name="EotS1" />}}
The Tempest Runners Pan Eyta and Kassav Milliko often chafed under Ro's domineering hand and wanted to return the Nihil exclusively to their authority and retain the third cut they had to surrender for use of the Paths. Enlisting Dee's help, they plotted to assassinate Ro and use his son, whom they viewed as a weakling ripe for controlling, to retain control of the Paths. However, Dee contacted Ro to inform him of their plan. However, Ro ridiculed Dee's suggestion that they eliminate Eyta and Milliko together and restructure the Nihil with themselves in command, instead declaring that the Paths would remain his alone. Ro planned to convince the two Tempest Runners to launch a coordinated attack against [[Shili]], the homeworld of the powerful [[Togruta]], an attack which would be designed to fail and force the two runners to regain the respect of their Tempests. Consumed by the leadership challenges, they would be unable to turn on Ro and would need his assistance to gain the Paths for raids to restore their credentials.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

[[File:DyingAsgar-THREOTS1.jpg|thumb|200px|Asgar dying in front of his son]]
However, Ro had underestimated Dee, who was angered that he had rejected her proposed plan. Unbeknownst even to her fellow Tempest Runners, Dee went to the Great Hall<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> and fatally stabbed Asgar.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> He was found bleeding profusely in his quarters by Marchion whom he begged for help. His son, angry at the abuse he had taken from his father all his life, took pleasure in kicking him viciously, making his death all the more painful. Marchion, who suspected that one of the Tempest Runners had murdered his [[Parent|father]], succeeded him as the Eye of the Nihil.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|Are you going to just stand for that? Are you going to let a creature like ''him'' speak to ''you'' like that. You should kill him for his insolance.|the specter of Asgar Ro, to Marchion, on [[Baron]] [[Boolan]]|<ref name="Eye of Darkness">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]''</ref>}}
After his death, Marchion took Asgar's helmet, donning it in a meeting with the Tempest Runners, claiming the title for himself, and that he had a vision for the Nihil.<ref name="EotS1" /> Marchion Ro believed that Asgar did not bring the Nihil to their full cruel potential; however, much of Marchion's chaotic ambition was inspired by his father's greed.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> Marchion believed that his father lacked vision and should have reorganized the Nihil when he was the Eye.<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> Throughout Marchion's life,<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> Asgar's spectre haunted Marchion, as his son's mind often dwelled on the imagined thoughts and opinions of his father as he led the Nihil against the Republic and Jedi.<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="EyeDate">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'' to [[229 BBY]]</ref> Marchion heard Asgar's voice often.<ref name="Eye of Darkness" />

==Personality and traits==
Asgar treated his son very poorly,<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> belittling him<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> and making his life miserable. He had great adoration for the ''Gaze Electric'' and its symbolism as a relic of a long-past age.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />

==Equipment==
Ro wore ancient Evereni armor that insulated the wearer against lighting,<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> as well as a [[Marchion Ro's helmet|mask]] that was in the Ro family for generations.<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> He also wore a cloak that was later used by his son, Marchion.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />
