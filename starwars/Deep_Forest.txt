"'''Deep Forest'''" is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic story]] featured in ''[[Star Wars: Visionaries]]'', written and drawn by [[Sang Jun Lee]].

==Publisher's summary==
''"''A Wookiee hunting expedition in the forests of Kashyyyk becomes something far larger than expected when a young warrior uncovers the pending Separatist invasion.''"<ref name="Inside Star Wars: Visionaries" />''

==Plot summary==
Eighteen months after the [[First Battle of Geonosis/Legends|Battle of Geonosis]], [[General/Legends|General]] [[Grievous/Legends|Grievous]] questions the [[Separatist Council/Legends|Separatist Council]] whether or not the [[Kashyyyk Royal Families]] have joined the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy]]. When [[Nute Gunray/Legends|Nute Gunray]] reported that they are still deliberating, Grievous orders the deployment of [[Droid/Legends|droids]] to [[Kashyyyk/Legends|Kashyyyk]] to hasten their decision.

In the [[Kashyyyk Royal City/Legends|Kashyyyk Royal City]], [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Grand Master/Legends|Grand Master]] [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] warns [[King/Legends|King]] [[Grakchawwaa/Legends|Grakchawwaa]] via [[Hologram/Legends|hologram]] that the Separatists will attempt to deceive him and that the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]] will provide aid if required. Grakchawwaa answers that his planet is not yet ready to commit to the [[Clone Wars/Legends|war]] before receiving another hologram from Gunray, asking him about whether or not he is ready to join the Confederacy.

Meanwhile, the King's son, [[Prince/Legends|Prince]] [[Rikummee]] and his protector [[Ghraggka]] are hunting in the [[Western Forest|outlying forests]]. Rikummee boasts that he is the greatest hunter on Kashyyyk, but Ghraggka cautions him to be modest. To prove himself, Rikummee sneaks off and tries to swing on a vine, only for it to break. He falls onto a herd of rampaging quadrupedal animals and is carried away into the distance. Ghraggka attempts to catch up to him, only to come face to face with a [[Probe droid/Legends|probe droid]].

The droid immediately fires at Ghraggka with its [[Blaster/Legends|blasters]]. Ghraggka evades the shots, jumps onto the back of the droid, and attempts to strike it with his [[Staff/Legends|staff]], only to be tossed into the water. Inside the water, he finds the dead body of Rikummee. Angered, he leaps out of the water and attacks the droid once again, eventually destroying it.

As Ghraggka cradles Rikummee's body and lets out a cry of anguish, more probe droids appear behind him. Just as he closes his eyes and accepts his fate, however, a group of Wookiee warrior destroy the droids with their [[Bowcaster/Legends|bowcasters]] and ask Ghraggka what has happened.

During King Grakchawwaa's holographic meeting with Nute Gunray, his advisors argue over whether or not to join the Confederacy. The meeting is interrupted by a Wookiee warrior's hologram reporting that the prince has been killed by Separatist droids. Angered by the news, the King finally makes his decision: war against the Separatist invaders.
