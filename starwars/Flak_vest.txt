'''Flak vests''',<ref name="CVD" /> also known as a '''flight vest''',<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> were a type of anti-[[radiation]] vest worn by some [[Rebel pilot|pilots]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The vests were worn over a pressurized [[flight suit]] and had a [[life support]] unit such as the [[Diagnostech life support unit]] worn over the top.<ref name="HC79" />
