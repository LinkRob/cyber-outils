'''Bosnok''' was a [[Gallusian]] with a knack for numbers. He spent the greater part of his life drifting though [[the galaxy]] doing quick jobs with fast [[Currency|payment]]. Bosnok became proficient at counting cards in the card game [[Sabacc]], though after picking the wrong table, he was quickly arrested and became a prisoner of the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. The Imperial [[Governor]] [[Thorden]] offered him a job managing records of the Empire's shady dealings on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]] after seeing great use of the Gallusian's abilities.<ref name="Outlaws" />
