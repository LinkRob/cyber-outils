'''Cosia''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Cosian Space]] part of the [[Deep Core]], a region of [[the galaxy]], within the [[Star system|system]] of the [[Cosia system|same name]]. Cosia was the only inhabited planet in that system, and served as the [[homeworld]] of the [[Cosian]] [[species]], which included [[Jedi Master]] [[Tera Sinube]]. Cosia was home to large [[forest]]s and rolling hills, containing the immense [[cosa tree]]s, in which the Cosians built their [[City|cities]]. All of the villages on Cosia operated independently, so there was no true [[capital]], but the largest city was [[Nu-Cosa]].

Cosia had many small [[Spaceport|starports]] and landing strips, as the Cosians did not boast a formal starport on the planet. [[Cosia Imperial starport|One such starport]] was home to the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] occupiers of the world.

==Description==
Cosia was located in [[Cosian Space]],<ref name="USW" /> a region of the [[Deep Core]], the innermost region of [[the galaxy]], in a [[star system]] that shared [[Cosia system|its name]]. The [[planet]] was the only inhabited world in the system, which was home to only one [[star]]. Cosia had two [[moon]]s, [[Tabu]] and [[Tebu]], which were in full view in the nighttime sky.<ref name="DoH" />

The planet was covered in rolling hills that were home to the immense [[cosa tree]], which made up the planet-spanning [[forest]]s. The cosa [[tree]] produced massive [[Palm tree|palm]] leaves, over a hundred [[meter]]s in length. Within those forests were numerous [[Cosian]] [[City|cities]], like the planet's largest&mdash;[[Nu-Cosa]]&mdash;that were built into the cosa trees. The homes in those cities were connected by finely carved wood stairways, and walkways of etched planks and cultivated vines.<ref name="DoH" />

==History==
At some point, a [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]], the Cosians, emerged into the world's forests as the only sentient species on the planet, and in Cosia's solar system. Over time, the Cosians built villages across their [[homeworld]], each ruled by a council of elders. When Cosian [[egg]]s hatched, a [[Cosian egg-hatching celebration|great celebration]] would be held to honor the emergence of new life and wisdom.<ref name="DoH" />

When the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|rose]], they occupied Cosia and constructed a [[Cosia Imperial starport|starport]] within the planet's forests.<ref name="DoH" />

==Inhabitants==
[[File:Cosian_Jedi_Ascetic_DoH_Audrey_Hotte.png|thumb|right|250px|A Cosian Jedi ascetic]]
Cosia's only sentient inhabitants were the Cosians,<ref name="DoH" /> of whom [[Jedi Master]] [[Tera Sinube]] was a member.<ref name="USW" /> Other than sentient inhabitants, Cosia was home to [[insect]]s with bright carapaces and wings as well as predators that stalked the forest floors.<ref name="DoH" />

==Locations==
Across the entire planet were numerous Cosian villages, each of which operated separately. Due to that, there was no set [[capital]] city; however, Nu-Cosa was the largest of them all. All of the villages were built into the massive cosa trees, connected by wooden walkways and stairways. Alongside that, each village was led by a council of elder Cosians.<ref name="DoH" />

Cosia did not contain a major starport due to the independence of each village, but the Galactic Empire constructed one to serve as the staging ground for their occupation.<ref name="DoH" />
