'''Eugene Lee Yang''' is an actor who voiced [[Toul]] in the English dub of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' short film, "[[Journey to the Dark Head]]."<ref name="JDH" /> Outside of ''[[Star Wars]]'', he is known for being a founding member of the online comedy group [[Wikipedia:The Try Guys|The Try Guys]], as well as his other acting credits such as the film ''[[Wikipedia:Nimona (film)|Nimona]]''.<ref name="People" />
