The '''Cry of the Broken King''' was a [[Force power]].<ref name="THR 1" /> While investigating<ref name="THR 2">[[The High Republic (2022) 2|''The High Republic'' (2022) 2]]</ref> the [[Death|murder]] of the [[Sarravi]] [[V'Kara]]<ref name="THR 1" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic (2022) 1|''The High Republic'' (2022) 1]] to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[Sephi]] [[thief]] [[Tey Sirrek]] was spotted standing next to the Sarravi's corpse by the [[Kiffar]]<ref name="THR 1" /> [[Jedi Master]]<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Vildar Mac]], who had chased Sirrek after seeing<ref name="THR 1" /> him<ref name="THR 2" /> steal from a [[Bpfasshi]] [[Unidentified Truthsayer of Bpfassh|Truthsayer]]. Thinking Sirrek had killed V'Kara, Mac confronted him, attempting to strike him down with [[Vildar Mac's lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. After Sirrek managed to dodge all of Mac's stikes, the annoyed [[Jedi]] asked how he was doing so.<ref name="THR 1" />

Sirrek then claimed to have mastered many [[The Force|Force]] powers, including the Cry of the Broken King, [[Vitali]] and [[Morichro]]. Mac dismissed that as nonsense, stating that Morichro was nothing more than a myth.<ref name="THR 1" /> The Sephi then used biological [[pheromone]]s his ancestors had developed for usage against [[Diet|predators]] to temporarily knock out Mac,<ref name="THR 2" /> while claiming that he was using Morichro on the Jedi.<ref name="THR 1" />
