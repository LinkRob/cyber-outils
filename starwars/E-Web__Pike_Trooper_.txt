An '''E-Web''' was an [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]] owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Prototype E-Web|standard E-Web]]. It featured a dirty frame with a [[Color|white]], red, and dark gray color scheme, as well as brown cloth straps wrapped around at least some of the handles.<ref name="Instagram" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Concept art for this Sentinel "Weapon Wrap" was created by [[Rinehart Appiah]]<ref name="Instagram" /> for the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', though it never made it into the final version of game,<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> only revealed after ''Star Wars: Hunters''' cancellation<ref name="Instagram" /> on [[October 1]], [[2025]].<ref name="Goodbye Hunters">{{Hunters|url=news/sunset-on-vespaara|text=Sunset on Vespaara}}</ref> It was intended to serve as a set with the [[Pike Trooper]] "Costume."<ref name="Instagram" />
