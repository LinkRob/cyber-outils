'''Brigia''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> located in an [[Brigia system|eponymous star system]] within the intersecting portion of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> Situated in the [[Tion Cluster]]<ref name="Cluster" /> in the [[Tion Hegemony]] at the coordinates T-6 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> it was an operations headquarters for [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]]'s [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|organized rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> by [[2 BBY]].<ref name="2 BBY">In ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', the report regarding the rebel locations that included Brigia is set between the introduction of the [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]] and the [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance]], both of which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[2 BBY]].</ref> Brigia was noted as such on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[Star map|map]] sent to Mothma<ref name="Rebel Files" /> that [[Standard year|year]]<ref name="2 BBY" /> that listed [[safe world]]s, [[starfighter]] hubs at level five or higher, operations headquarters, [[shadow planet]]s, and deep [[Realspace|space]] caches. The [[archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]] eventually included the [[map]] in the archive of non-electronic documents known as ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brigia was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Daniel Wallace]]'s [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Brigia/Legends|Brigia]] first appeared in [[Brian Daley]]'s [[1980]] novel ''[[Han Solo and the Lost Legacy]]''<ref name="Han Solo ATLL">''[[Han Solo and the Lost Legacy]]''</ref> and was later pictured in the [[1993]] [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']].<ref name="X-wing">[[Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']]</ref>
