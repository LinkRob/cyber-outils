'''Buoy droids''' were small floating [[droid]]s used as beacons and guides in the [[spaceport]]s of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. When [[Thane Kyrell]] and [[Ciena Ree]] arrived at [[Royal Imperial Academy|Coruscant's]] [[Imperial Academy]] from their [[homeworld]], [[Jelucan]], a number of these droids guided them through the spaceport's corridors to the academy [[shuttle]].<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>
