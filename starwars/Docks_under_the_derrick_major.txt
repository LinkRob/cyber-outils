These '''Coruscant docks''' were located under the [[derrick major]] and was where [[Freighter|cargo ships]] docked on [[Coruscant]] to load and unload [[cargo container]]s.<ref name="Senate Murders" />

==History==
After [[Funeral of Onaconda Farr|his funeral]], [[Senator]]s [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] were directed to the docks while investigating who had killed [[Onaconda Farr]]. They were ambushed by an [[Lolo Purs|assailant]] who managed to escape, and later the docks became the scene of a criminal investigation by [[Lieutenant]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo|Tan Divo]] and the [[Coruscant Security Force]].<ref name="Senate Murders" />
