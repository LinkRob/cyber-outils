[[Lady]] '''Alinka Aloo''', daughter of [[Emperor's adviser|Imperial Adviser]] [[Sim Aloo]], was a [[Coruscant]]-based [[collector]] of [[Sith]] [[Sith artifact|artifacts]] who managed the [[Aloo family]]'s affairs and undertook covert missions on [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Sheev Palpatine|Palpatine]]'s behalf. Known for her cunning and ambition, she orchestrated a dangerous expedition to retrieve an ancient [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[book]] from [[Ushruu]]—recruiting [[Chewbacca]], the captive [[librarian]] [[Mayvlin Trillick]], and an unassuming [[cargo droid]]—only for the mission to unravel when the [[droid]] proved to be the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] agent [[K-2SO]] and the [[Corellian hound|snarler]] creatures of Ushruu followed the team back to Coruscant, leaving Alinka empty-handed and outmaneuvered.

==Biography==
A [[human]] [[Gender|woman]] from [[Coruscant]], [[Lady]] [[Alinka Aloo]] was and the daughter of [[Sim Aloo]],<ref name="TMCAFOF" /> one of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Sheev Palpatine]]'s closest [[Emperor's adviser|advisers]],<ref name="DB Sim">{{Databank|sim-aloo|Sim Aloo}}</ref> from a second [[marriage]].<ref name="Kernels and Husks">{{StoryCite|story=Kernels and Husks|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref> She held the rank of [[princess]] and was properly addressed either as "Your Highness" or "Lady Alinka." Together with her [[Parent|father]], she assisted in acquiring [[Sith]] [[Sith artifact|artifacts]] from across [[the galaxy]] for the Emperor's private [[collector|collection]], and she oversaw the [[Aloo family]]'s business interests during her father's frequent absences. She resided in a [[Alinka Aloo's tower|tower]] situated on one of the highest levels of [[Imperial City]].<ref name="TMCAFOF" />

At some point during the [[Imperial Era]], Palpatine expressed interest in an ancient [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Book|tome]] kept within a [[Unidentified Ushruu temple|temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Ushruu]]. Both Sim Aloo and fellow adviser [[Janus Greejatus]] sought to retrieve the artifact, though Greejatus's attempt&mdash;conducted hastily through a hired group of [[Trandoshan]] [[Mercenary|mercenaries]]&mdash;ended in failure when the team was devoured by Ushruu's native [[Corellian hound|snarler]] creatures. Seeking to ensure her father's success, Alinka devised a more deliberate plan, determining that the mission required a [[Wookiee]] guide capable of navigating Ushruu's dense [[forest]]s, a [[librarian]] to recover the book, and a [[cargo droid]] to transport it.<ref name="TMCAFOF" />

For the librarian, Alinka selected [[Mayvlin Trillick]], an [[Oktarian]] youth apprehended while attempting to steal from one of Sim Aloo's warehouses. Alinka coerced Trillick's cooperation by withholding the ''[[Mola Oktaro]]'', the most sacred text of the Oktaran people. She chose the Wookiee [[Chewbacca]]&mdash;co-[[pilot]] of the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]]&mdash;for his strength and familiarity with hazardous terrain. The cargo droid assignment fell to a [[K-2SO|K-2SB]] unit that volunteered for the mission, as the Aloos paid little attention to droid personnel.<ref name="TMCAFOF" />

To recruit Chewbacca, Alinka traveled to [[Level 1329]] of the [[Coruscant Underworld]], where she knew the Wookiee and Solo had docked a [[Standard week|week]] earlier. Presenting them with her calling card, she instructed them to visit the Aloo tower later that evening. When they arrived, she feigned a need to transport her pet [[tooka]]s to [[Centax-3]], offering 25,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] for the job. She further insisted that Solo remain on Coruscant to keep her company, intending to hold him hostage to ensure Chewbacca's compliance with the true mission.<ref name="TMCAFOF" />

After Trillick, Chewbacca, and the K-2SB boarded the [[Freighter|cargo vessel]], Alinka revealed the actual objective. Chewbacca reluctantly agreed to proceed in order to safeguard Solo's life. Unbeknownst to Alinka, the cargo droid was in fact K-2SO, an Imperial security droid reprogrammed by [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. K-2SO's true assignment was to secure the Ushruu tome for the Alliance.<ref name="TMCAFOF" />

Upon the crew's return to Coruscant, Alinka rushed to the [[landing pad]] to receive them. However, when the ship's doors opened, a pack of snarler creatures&mdash;brought inadvertently from Ushruu&mdash;charged at her and her [[bodyguard]]s. Amid the chaos, Solo escaped with the promised credits, a valuable [[Junarian vase]], and the ''Mola Oktaro''. As Alinka fled toward her private apartments with a snarler in pursuit, Solo, Chewbacca, Trillick, and K-2SO departed in the cargo ship. The Ushruu tome ultimately eluded Alinka and fell into the hands of Cassian Andor.<ref name="TMCAFOF" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|The money means nothing to me… but neither does your friend's life. If you bring back what I want, you get both. Fail and you get nothing but a blaster bolt. The same goes for you, girl.|Alinka Aloo, to Han Solo and Mayvlin Trillick|<ref name="TMCAFOF" />}}
By human standards, Alinka Aloo was regarded as beautiful, though strikingly pale in complexion. She possessed a practiced, flirtatious manner to which Han Solo proved notably susceptible, and she was an accomplished deceiver. Alinka demonstrated little regard for the lives or well‑being of others, focusing solely on the advancement of her own ambitions. Her considerable wealth rendered monetary concerns largely irrelevant to her.<ref name="TMCAFOF" />
