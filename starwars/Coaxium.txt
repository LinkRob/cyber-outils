{{Quote|Coaxium. Know it, you do?''"<br />"''Coaxium? Standard hyper-fuel and incredibly unstable until refined.|[[Yoda]] and [[Mace Windu]]|<ref name="Windu 1">[[Mace Windu 1|''Mace Windu'' 1]]</ref>}}
'''Coaxium''', also known as '''Corellian hyperfuel''', or simply '''hyperfuel''', was a rare form of [[hypermatter]] and type of expensive [[starship]] [[fuel]] mined on [[Kessel]].

==Description==
[[File:Coaxium_-_Star_Wars_Commander.png|thumb|left|182x182px|A container of refined coaxium]]
Coaxium in its raw, unrefined state was a very volatile and highly explosive [[hypermatter]] and had to be refined in order for it to be stable enough to be stored at room temperature. Even refined coaxium had to be handled with caution, as it was still explosive. Unrefined coaxium, once mined, would gradually begin to destabilize and turn red in color before exploding violently if not stored properly in a temperature controlled environment. [[Bis Refinery|One]] such coaxium refinery existed on the [[desert]] [[planet]] of [[Savareen]]. 100 kilograms of refined coaxium was worth around 60 million [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Solo" />

The [[hyperdrive]] reaction chamber of a starship was coated in coaxium, which, when energized, allowed the ship to travel through hyperspace.<ref name="Databank" /> Coaxium, when used as a fuel, was so potent at energy generation that just a small amount of it was sufficient to power an entire fleet of ships. This made it extremely valuable and it was therefore highly sought after by many. The [[Galactic Empire]] transported large shipments of coaxium under high-security measurements, with the intent of gaining complete control over the production of the substance.

Because of the knowledge about [[purrgil]] and the gas [[Clouzon-36]], scientists discovered that coaxium naturally forms itself in places with strong interstellar energy like Kessel.<ref name="OfficialGuide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|Do you know what that really is?''"<br />"''Yeah. About sixty million credits worth of refined coaxium.''"<br />"''No. It's the blood that brings life to something new.''"<br />"''Yeah, what?''"<br />"''A rebellion.|Enfys Nest and Han Solo|<ref name="Solo" />}}
[[File:CoaxiumCase-SWCT.png|thumb|right|220px|A case of refined coaxium]]
By the age of the [[Galactic Empire]], coaxium was a highly valuable substance sought after by the Empire as well as various criminal organizations; the [[White Worms]], the [[Pyke Syndicate]], and [[Crimson Dawn]] among them.<ref name="Solo" /> Control over coaxium trade was one of the Empire's earliest priorities to ensure it had enough for its fleets.<ref name="Galactic Explorer's Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref>

[[13 BBY|Thirteen years]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]], a young [[Han Solo|Han]] acquired a small amount of coaxium, and he and his childhood friend [[Qi'ra]] attempted to use it to barter their way off of [[Corellia]]. He partially failed, only managing to secure his own escape, while Qi'ra was detained at the spaceport. Three years later, Solo became embroiled in a [[Heist on Vandor|plot]] to steal a shipment of the precious fuel from a [[20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport]] on [[Vandor]] for [[Dryden Vos]] and [[Crimson Dawn]]. Due to the interference of [[Enfys Nest]] and her [[Cloud-Riders]], Solo chose to release the tethers holding the coaxium rather than follow [[Tobias Beckett]]'s orders to fly straight towards a mountainside. The Cloud-Riders' swoops were unable to hold up the heavy container on their own and were forced to drop it, causing a massive explosion that collapsed the mountainside.<ref name="Solo" />

Finally, Solo, together with Qi'ra, [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]], and [[L3-37]] made the [[Kessel Run]] to [[Raid on Kessel|obtain]] unrefined coaxium from the Pykes and bring it back to Vos in repayment for their past failures. This time, Solo succeeded, bringing the shipment to [[Savareen]] to be refined. He and his associates were soon [[Showdown on Savareen|confronted]] by the Cloud-Riders, who revealed Crimson Dawn's intention to use the coaxium to spread death across the galaxy.<ref name="Solo" />

[[File:Dryden_and_Han.jpg|thumb|left|332x332px|Dryden Vos accuses Han Solo of giving him fake coaxium.]]
Deciding to double-cross Crimson Dawn, Solo fed false information to Beckett, knowing he would sell him out to Vos, that he would be bringing two crates full of counterfeit coaxium, when in fact he was bringing the real shipment. Both Beckett and Vos were fooled by his ruse as the Crimson Dawn enforcers attempted to ambush the Cloud-Riders only to be fooled by decoys and empty crates before they were dispatched. Beckett soon took Chewbacca hostage and held him at blasterpoint to carry the coaxium off Vos's yacht, but Solo soon caught up to him and killed Beckett. Afterward, he gave the coaxium to the Cloud-Riders to fund their growing rebellion. After declining Enfys's offer for Solo to join them, she provided him a single vial of coaxium, which he used in a [[sabacc]] game against Calrissian in which he won ownership of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="Solo" />

Sometime after the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[MC80 Star Cruiser]] ''[[Aurora Flare]]'' was carrying a large amount of coaxium from the [[Redhurne system]]. Upon hearing the news of [[King of Mon Cala|king]] [[Lee-Char]]'s murder, the ''Aurora Flare'' [[Mutiny on Mon Cala|crashed]] into an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]], creating an opening for many other Mon Calamari cruisers to flee to the rebel-controlled [[Mako-Ta Space Docks]].<ref name="SW49" />

At some point following the [[Galactic Civil War]], the racer [[Marcus Speedstar]] refueled his [[Racing starfighter|racer]] with hyperfuel but underestimated the effect that the hyperfuel would have on his ship and caused his racer to spiral out of control and crash into the stands, killing his [[Jarek Yeager's wife|sister-in-law]] and [[Jarek Yeager's daughter|niece]].<ref name="TPC">{{Resistance|The Platform Classic}}</ref>

Speedstar's older brother [[Jarek Yeager]] had a container of hyperfuel in his private hangar. [[Jace Rucklin]] and [[Rucklin's team|his team]] wanted to steal it for a long time since it could give a racer a boost. They managed to convince [[Kazuda Xiono]] to help them, but the team didn't know that the fuel's drops could turn a ship into a giant bomb. While Xiono managed to save Rucklin, [[Rucklin's racer|his racer]] exploded and Rucklin swore revenge on Xiono.<ref name="Fuel for the Fire">{{Resistance|Fuel for the Fire}}</ref>

After the [[Battle of D'Qar|Evacuation of D'Qar]], [[Kazuda Xiono]] led the [[Warbird gang]] to [[Skirmish over D'Qar|raid]] the remains of the ''[[Fulminatrix]]'' to retrieve its coaxium for the ''[[Colossus]]''.<ref name="A Quick Salvage Run">{{Resistance|A Quick Salvage Run}}</ref> Sometime after the [[Battle of Crait]], the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] hired a team of pilots to use the ''Millennium Falcon'' to [[Heist on a First Order train|steal]] coaxium from a [[First Order]] [[hovertrain]] on [[Corellia]].<ref name="Smuggler's Run">{{GEAttr|Millennium Falcon: Smuggler's Run}}</ref>
