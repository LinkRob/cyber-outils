A '''command cap''', also known as a '''crested command cap''', was a type of [[hat]] worn as part of the [[First Order military uniforms|uniform]] of various personnel within the [[First Order]]'s [[First Order military|military forces]]. Depending on the role of the wearer, the command cap could be either black, light gray, charcoal gray, or teal in [[color]].<ref name="TFAVD" />
