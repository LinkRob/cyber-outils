"'''Belted In: Excursion'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year three of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was the second adventure in the [[Belted In trilogy]] and was written by [[Jae Walker]] and [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]].

==Plot summary==
The heroes search the [[Cularin asteroid belt]] for some missing [[Cularin Militia]] members. However, they instead discover a [[Thaereian military]] where the crew are all dead and a [[Metatheran Cartel]] mining facility, where all the cartel members have been killed and their bodies reanimated by [[Oblee|shadow lurkers]].
