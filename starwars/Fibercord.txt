'''Fibercord''' was a type of thick, durable string. [[Boba Fett]] used a [[fibercord whip]] in his [[whipcord launcher]].<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] of [[Chandrila]] sometimes wore a draped fibercord livery collar over her [[Clothing|robes]].<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
