{{Quote|Press the attack. And... send that ''message'' to Commander Zahra. Inform her that the rebel's ''Eleventh Division'' is all but eradicated and we will rejoin her task force shortly.|Admiral [[Kalaxo]], to [[Lieutenant]] [[Bonnard]]|<ref name="SW 15" />}}
The '''Eleventh Division''' was a [[Navy|naval]] [[division]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. It was created following the Alliance's defeat at the [[Battle of Hoth]] and eventually became grounded on [[Ab Dalis]] and under siege by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s forces. The [[division]]'s [[Ground Defense Command]] defended the [[starship]]s from Imperial ground forces, and were being overwhelmed. The [[Starlight Squadron]] from the [[Fourth Division|Fourth]] and [[Seventh Division]]s eventually arrived and destroyed the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|ISD]] ''[[Ultima II]]'', although at the destruction of Ground Defense Command. [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] and [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] led the division.<ref name="SW 15" />
