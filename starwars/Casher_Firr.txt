'''Casher Firr''' was a [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] who flew a [[Casher Firr's podracer|vehicle]] with rusted [[Color|blue]] plating and a totally enclosed [[cockpit]] that had a yellow strip looped vertically around its center.<ref name="Beyond Victory">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Casher Firr was mentioned in the [[2025]] [[Industrial Light & Magic]] [[Star Wars video games|virtual reality game]] [[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]. Neither the [[racer]] or their [[Podracer|vehicle]] appear in the game's adventure mode but the vehicle is avaliable as a prop in the game's playset mode with the [[pilot]]'s name included in the item's title.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />
