'''Arath Tarrex''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi initiate]] during the [[Republic Era]]. Arath often conflicted with [[Dooku]] when they were [[youngling]]s (the two were part of the [[Hawk-Bat Clan]]), as he was jealous of Dooku's intellectual and combative superiority over him. Arath was the son of [[Jedi Master]] [[Yula Braylon]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

Circa [[86 BBY]], Arath was not selected to become a Padawan during the [[Initiate Trials]] and was forced to wait another year to be considered for selection. Arath mocked Dooku prior to the trials for his desire to see [[Lene Kostana]] attend the event since it was known that Dooku wanted to become her apprentice.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

Arath eventually became disgraced when his corrupt nature was exposed. It was revealed that Arath was corrupt, having robbed the [[ambassador]] [[Ketas]] during [[Summit on Station Zeta|talks]] on [[Station Zeta]] on [[Plin Minor]] (which caused negotiations between the Plinovian and Solodo species to collapse) in order to pay for his [[gambling]] debts and had also used his mother's position to do this in order to prevent further debts. As a result, the [[Jedi High Council]] sentenced him to work in the [[Jedi Archives]] as part of his punishment.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

His duplicity was exposed by Dooku and his Padawan [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] who would confront Arath in the Jedi Archives before the [[Jedi Temple Guard|Temple Guards]] led him away to the [[Chamber of Judgment]]. Before leaving, however, Dooku asked Jinn to keep Arath company.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
