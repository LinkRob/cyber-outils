'''2BB-2''' was a [[Color|blue]]-plated [[BB-series astromech droid]] with [[Gender|masculine programming]] in the service of the [[Resistance]]. The [[droid]] was present aboard the [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] during the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation]] of the [[Resistance base]] on the [[planet]] [[D'Qar]] in [[34 ABY]]. After the destruction of the ''Raddus''{{'s}} [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], 2BB-2 attended a meeting during which command of the Resistance was granted to [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]].

==Biography==
===BB on the bridge===
2BB-2 was a [[BB-series astromech droid]] with [[Gender|masculine programming]]<ref name="Droidography" /> that served in the [[Resistance]] during its [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[First Order]].<ref name="TLJ" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|VIII}} to [[34 ABY]].</ref> 2BB-2 was among the forces stationed aboard the [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] while the Resistance [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuated]] its [[Resistance base|base]]<ref name="TLJ" /> on the [[planet]] [[D'Qar]].<ref name="TLJVD" /> 2BB-2 was on the [[cruiser]]'s command [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] supervising the evacuation<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2018 Topps Finest Star Wars|subset=Droids and Vehicles|cardname=2BB-2|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/192565/cid/12174107/2018-Topps-Finest-Star-Wars---Droids-and-Vehicles-DV-2-2BB-2|nobackup=1}}</ref> when a [[First Order Navy|fleet]] of First Order [[Starship|vessels]] arrived above D'Qar and attacked the retreating Resistance.<ref name="TLJ" />

The [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]] successfully escaped its attacking foes and jumped to [[hyperspace]]. However, upon arriving at its [[Oetchi system|destination]], the [[fleet]] immediately came under [[Battle of Oetchi|attack]] from the First Order again, having been [[Hyperspace tracker|tracked through hyperspace]]. At some point, 2BB-2 exited the command bridge, which was soon destroyed, [[Death|killing]] all who had remained there except for [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="TLJ" />

===BB on the replacement bridge===
[[File:Amilyn_Holdo_Ellen.png|thumb|left|250px|2BB-2 (background, left) served aboard the Resistance cruiser ''Raddus''.]]
The [[astromech droid]] then attended a meeting on the ''Raddus''{{'s}} ventral emergency bridge, where [[Commander]] [[Larma D'Acy]] announced the Resistance's casualties and named [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] the Resistance's new leader while Organa recovered from her injuries. A small portion of the Resistance ultimately survived the attack and escaped from the First Order on the planet [[Crait]].<ref name="TLJ" />

The [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR]] later wrote about 2BB-2 and their fellow Resistance BB unit [[BB-4]] in a work he compiled and called a "[[droidography]]." R0-GR claimed that the two Resistance [[droid]]s were not as popular as another BB unit, [[BB-8]].<ref name="Droidography" />

==Characteristics==
2BB-2 had [[Color|blue]] plating and was equipped with a black security-grade [[photoreceptor]] with a telescopic lens.<ref name="TLJVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The body still has the basic six circular panels, but now other panels break up the Y-sections and intersect those circular panels. The head still keeps the eyes, protected by a cowling, but breaks up the dome to reveal the inner workings.|Jake Lunt Davies describes 2BB-2|<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]''</ref>}}
[[File:2BB-2_concept.png|thumb|right|250px|Concept art of 2BB-2 was created by Jake Lunt Davies.]]
2BB-2 was created as a prop for<ref name="Art" /> the [[2017]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="TLJ" /> The droid was first revealed prior to the film's release on [[September 1]], 2017, as part of the [[Force Friday]] promotional event, where 2BB-2 appeared in the ''[[Find the Force]]'' feature on the [https://www.starwars.com/games-apps/star-wars-app ''Star Wars'' App].<ref name="FtF">{{FindtheForce|text=2BB-2|filename=Find_the_Force_2BB-2.png}}</ref> Creature concept designer [[Jake Lunt Davies]] produced concept art of 2BB-2 for ''The Last Jedi'', titled "Rebel BB Head Design 01," which included a deconstructed diagram of the droid's new head design.<ref name="Art" />

In the [[Canon|non-canon]] ''[[Star Wars Blips]]'' animated short "[[Fan Droids]],"<ref name="Blips">{{Blips|Fan Droids}}</ref> which debuted on [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] on [[September 3]], 2017,<ref name="YouTube">{{SWYouTube|series=Star Wars Blips|text=Fan Droids|video=osXqwvaUCsc}} {{C|[[:File:Fan_Droids_release_date.png|screenshot]]}}</ref> 2BB-2 and several other Droids of the Resistance watch a [[Hologram|holographic]] projection depicting the past exploits of the [[R2-series astromech droid|astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]]. 2BB-2 then spots R2-D2 approaching the group, and the droids all become extremely excited as he passes.<ref name="Blips" /> The [[Star Wars Droids App by Sphero|''Star Wars'' Droids App by Sphero]] mistakenly refers to 2BB-2 as "BB-2."<ref name="Sphero">{{Sphero|text=BB-2 and BB-4, BB Units|filename=BB-unit_Data_Sphero.jpg}}</ref>
