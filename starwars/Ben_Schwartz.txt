'''Ben Schwartz''' (born [[September 15]], [[1981]])<ref name="This Week Mandalorian Trailer" /> is an [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]] who, along with [[Bill Hader]], provided voice work for [[BB-8]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. Schwartz also played a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] in the film.<ref>{{Twitter|rejectedjokes|status/992470518149275649|Ben Schwartz|quote=On this beautiful #MayThe4thBeWithYou I feel like I can finally reveal that I played a Storm Trooper in @starwars The Force Awakens. It was an incredible honor and years later, I'm still smiling from the experience but please do not tell BB8.|archivedate=20200808133129}}</ref> In [[2023]], Schwartz lent his voice to another [[droid]], [[TAY-0]], in the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]''.<ref name="Faster">{{TBB|Faster}}</ref>

On [[April 19]], [[2025]], [[Ahmed Best]] announced at [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan]] that Schwartz would be voicing the [[Lepi]] smuggler [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]] in the non-[[Canon]] animated series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Rebuild the Galaxy - Pieces of the Past]]'', which released on [[Disney+]] on [[September 19]], 2025.<ref name="Pieces of the Past">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-lego-star-wars-rebuild-the-galaxy-pieces-of-the-past|text=''LEGO Star Wars: Rebuild the Galaxy: Pieces of the Past'' Will Feature LEGO BrickHeadz Ahsoka and&hellip; Jaxxon?!}}</ref>
