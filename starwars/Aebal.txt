'''Aebal''' was a [[Rodian]]<ref name="Escape from Valo" /> [[Jedi Master]] in the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> He<ref name="Escape from Valo" /> was the [[Jedi Master]] of the [[human]] [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Gavi Takitaken|Gavi]] and was stationed at the [[Valo Temple|Jedi temple]] on<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]].<ref name="Escape from Valo" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[Attack on Valo|attacked]] Valo and Gavi's Master was [[Death|killed]] by a [[Shrikarai|Nameless]]&mdash;creatures that [[Diet|fed]] on [[Force-sensitive]]s and petrified them&mdash;which the [[youngling]] witnessed. The event left Gavi's connection to [[the Force]] shaken and caused him to become anxious and fearful.<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aebal was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'', a [[2023]] reference book written by [[Megan Crouse]] and [[Amy Richau]].<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" />
