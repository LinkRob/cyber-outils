The '''Andobi Mountains''' were a [[mountain]] range on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] of [[Ando Prime]].<ref name="Coruscant" /> By the [[Standard year|year]] [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Dates">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'' to [[22 BBY]].</ref> [[Podracing|podraces]] were held in these mountains.<ref name="Coruscant" /> One such race was being broadcast on a [[Viewscreen|holoscreen]] at the [[Outlander Club]] when [[Zam Wesell]] attempted to escape the pursuing [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] by hiding there.<ref name="AotC">{{Film|II}}</ref>
