'''Cracken's Crew''' was the name of a [[special forces]] unit led by<ref name="Forged" /> [[Chief of Intelligence]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> [[General]] [[Airen Cracken]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. The unit specialized in surgical raiding operations, and were considered among the best known heroes of the Alliance during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Forged" />
