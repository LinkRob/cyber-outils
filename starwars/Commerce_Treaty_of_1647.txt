The '''Commerce Treaty of 1647''' was a legal covenant of the [[Galactic Republic]] that, during times of war, could assure the neutrality of a group such as the [[Trade Federation]],<ref name="Trivia Gallery" /> which controlled most of the interstellar shipping passing through [[the galaxy]]'s Rim territories.<ref name="Trade Federation">{{Databank|trade-federation|Trade Federation}}</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], [[Senator]] [[Lott Dod]] of the Trade Federation continually invoked the treaty to counter accusations that the corporation was in league with the Confederacy due to doing business with them.<ref name="Sphere of Influence" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Commerce Treaty of 1647 was first mentioned in "[[Sphere of Influence]]," the fourth episode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]],<ref name="Sphere of Influence" /> which originally aired on [[October 1]], [[2010]].<ref name="Sphere of Influence air date">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode304.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Sphere of Influence}}</ref> The creation of the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar]] introduced in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> was, according to [[Pablo Hidalgo]], partially inspired by the line referencing the treaty.<ref name="Pablo Hive">[[:File:Pablo-Hive-CRC-Name-Origin.png]]</ref> However, there has been no official confirmation that "1647" is a C.R.C. date.
