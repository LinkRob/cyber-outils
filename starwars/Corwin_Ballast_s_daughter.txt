The '''daughter''' of the civilian [[Corwin Ballast]] and his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Lynnta]], resided in the [[city]] of [[Maborn]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] [[Mordal]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. In [[4 ABY]], she became immobilized during a [[Battle on Mordal|firefight]] between [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] and [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] in Maborn, and their [[parent]]s' rescue efforts proved unsuccessful. After Lynnta and his daughter's [[death]]s, a grieving Ballast traveled to the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] planet [[Jakku]] with a picture of his [[Ballast family|family]] in his [[boot]] to remember them.

==Biography==
[[File:Jakku-PoeDameronFlightLog.png|thumb|left|180px|After his daughter's death, Corwin Ballast traveled to the desolate world of Jakku to grieve.]]
A daughter was [[Reproduction|born]] to the civilian [[Corwin Ballast]] and his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Lynnta]], by the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Their [[parent]]s settled with their [[Youngling|child]] on<ref name="Aftermath" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Mordal system]]</ref> [[Planet|world]] [[Mordal]] in the [[city]] of [[Maborn]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> she became immobilized during a [[Battle on Mordal|firefight]] between [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] and [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] in Maborn. Her parents attempted to rescue her from the area of contention but were downed by [[blaster]] fire before they could reach her. Ballast recovered from his injuries and woke several [[Standard week|weeks]] after the skirmish to find that his daughter and wife had [[Death|died]] and been [[Burial|buried]].<ref name="Aftermath" />

Grieved, Ballast kept his [[Ballast family|family's]] picture in his [[boot]] to remember them. He left Mordal to travel to<ref name="Aftermath" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] world<ref name="Jakku">{{SW|url=news/where-in-the-galaxy-are-the-worlds-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?|int=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?}}</ref> [[Jakku]], a location he considered desolate enough for war to never reach. In the [[Cantina|drinking establishment]] [[Ergel's Bar]] on Jakku, Ballast explained to the [[bartender]] [[Ergel]] that he had come to the bleak world for his family's sake because it was far from the fighting. Though the man briefly relived the memory of his daughter and wife's deaths in his mind, he broke away from the recollection to continue conversing with Ergel.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Personality and traits==
By the time of the firefight on Mordal, the daughter of Corwin Ballast was young. Ballast loved his daughter and felt emotionally distraught after her death.<ref name="Aftermath" />
