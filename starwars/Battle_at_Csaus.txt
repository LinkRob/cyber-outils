In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during a [[Grysk plot to engineer a Chiss civil war|crisis]] in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nation, the '''battle at Csaus''' was fought between armed [[freighter]]s of the Ascendancy's [[Tahmie]] and [[Droc family|Droc]] [[Great Families|families]] at the Chiss [[Unknown Regions]] astronomical object [[Csaus]]. The freighters exchanged several [[spectrum laser]] shots before [[Chiss Defense Force]] [[Admiral]] [[Ers'ikaro]]'s [[Firewolf man-of-war]] [[Bellicose (Firewolf man-of-war)|''Bellicose'']] stopped the battle in order to prevent further escalation of the crisis.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

[[Clarr family]] [[Patriarch]] [[Clarr'ivl'exow]] later asked Clarr [[Captain]] [[Clarr'os'culry]]&mdash;who considered the battle at Csaus to have been smaller than a skirmish&mdash;if she had heard about the engagement, which the [[officer]] confirmed. Shortly thereafter, the Tahmie and Droc [[Skirmish at Jamiron|resumed]] their hostilities on<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> [[Jamiron]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle at Csaus was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final installment in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|''Thrawn Ascendancy Trilogy'']].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
