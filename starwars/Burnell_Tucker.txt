'''Burnell Tucker''' ([[January 21]], [[1930s#1935|1935]] &ndash; [[March 25]], [[2026]])<ref name="In Memory" /> was an actor who appeared in the first two films of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]]. His role in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]<ref name="Tucker">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodmemorabilia.com/en/star-wars/burnell-tucker-star-wars-autographed-1996-star-wars-ccg-number-100-bas-authenticated-card-with-best-wishes-inscription/t-24061638+p-342245908180674+z-9-2953284177|text=Burnell Tucker Star Wars Autographed 1996 Star Wars CCG #100 BAS Authenticated Card with "Best Wishes" Inscription|archivedate=20230617112514}}</ref> was uncredited, while [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] credited him as one of several "Other Officers" in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Forces]].

In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], Tucker's character in ''A New Hope'' is identified as [[Del Goren/Legends|Del Goren]], a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] [[Technician/Legends|technician]] during the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]]. For ''The Empire Strikes Back'', Tucker plays two different [[Echo Base/Legends|Echo Base]] [[Controller/Legends|controllers]], [[Romas Navander/Legends|Romas Navander]] and [[Wyron Serper/Legends|Wyron Serper]].
