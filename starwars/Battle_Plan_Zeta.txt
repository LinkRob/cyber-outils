'''Battle Plan Zeta''' was a military tactic formulated by the [[battle droid]] [[Agamar garrison|garrison]] of [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|ST-series super tactical droid]] [[General]] [[Kalani]], on<ref name="The Last Battle" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Audio">{{Rebels|The Last Battle}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> [[Agamar]]. Amid the [[Galactic Civil War]] in [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> several members from the rebel [[Phoenix Cell]]&mdash;[[Jedi]]s [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Ezra Bridger]], ex-[[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], [[Garazeb Orrelios]], and [[C1-10P]]&mdash;traveled to Agamar, seeking munitions to combat the [[Galactic Empire]] from a crashed [[Unidentified DH-Omni Support Vessel (Agamar)|supply ship]]. While on the surface, the team discovered Kalani's battle [[droid]]s and were taken prisoner. The general forced them into a battle simulation, where he hoped to end the long-ceased [[Clone Wars]] in a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] victory. Employing Battle Plan Zeta, exciting the battle droids by its use, the battle ultimately failed as Imperial [[Imperial Military|forces]] arrived and convinced the two sides to work together and successfully escape Agamar.<ref name="The Last Battle" />
