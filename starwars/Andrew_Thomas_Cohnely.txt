'''Andrew Thomas "Dirty" Cohnely''' was a [[Human/Legends|human]] [[Gender/Legends|male]]. Cohnely used to work for the [[Imperial Office of Criminal Investigations/Legends|Imperial Office of Criminal Investigations]] where his impeccable service allowed him to rapidly rise through the ranks, even earning him an [[Medal of Honor (Imperial)/Legends|Imperial Medal of Honor]]. However, his career ended after an investigation into the [[Camarina Connection]] revealed to him that the gang's activities were covered up by those in power. Disgusted by this realization, he resigned after shortly after [[Councillor/Legends|Councillor]] [[Wan Tyrama]] "committed [[Death/Legends|suicide]]." After his resignation, Cohnely worked at [[Spaceport 16]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Abregado-rae|Abregado Rae]] where he served as [[Chief of Security/Legends|security chief]] of the [[Spaceport/Legends|spaceport's]] security service with the rank of [[Major/Legends|major]]. He lived alone with his pet [[Jabba Gooey]], a giant [[Slug (creature)/Legends|slug]] from the planet [[Dagobah/Legends|Dagobah]].<ref name="Arrière-plan étoilé" />
