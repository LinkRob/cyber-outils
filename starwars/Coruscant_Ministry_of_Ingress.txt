The '''Coruscant Ministry of Ingress''' was the organization responsible for processing immigrants arriving on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. The ministry had subdivision offices located throughout [[Imperial City|Galactic City]].<ref name="SWCL" /> [[Tannon Praji]] served as the [[First Minister (Coruscant Ministry of Ingress)|First Minister]] of the Coruscant Ministry of Ingress.<ref name="Pushing Boundaries" />
