A '''battle''' occurred during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] on [[Sullust]].

==Battle==
The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] sent forces to [[Sullust]]. Their first move was to capture an Imperial hangar and make it their base. The rebels then sent four [[Rebel trooper]]s to an Imperial Hanger to steal critical Imperial war machine plans. They were successful in finding the plans but had to fight their way out. After subduing multiple waves of Imperials, the [[Millennium Falcon]] arrived with a small rebel fleet consisting of 1 [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] and 2 [[CR90 corvette]]s. The remaining rebels then escaped with the plans aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. Meanwhile, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] found the rebel base and arrived in his Star Destroyer. Vader ordered the Imperial ground force to deploy an [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] escorted by [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]s to destroy the base. [[Gial Ackbar]] then sent an [[MC80 Star Cruiser]] to aid the rebels in their escape. The rebels attacked the AT-AT but failed to destroy to it. The Empire was successful in destroying the base.<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Aftermath==
Once the rebel base was destroyed and Darth Vader left, the planet [[Alliance High Command]] discovered that [[Sullust]] had a direct [[Hyperspace route|Hyperlane]] to [[Endor]] and the new [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]]. They then decided that they would gather their fleet over Sullust, so a small fleet consisting of at least 2 MC80 Star Cruisers and a small amount of frigates and corvettes was sent in to take out any imperial threat before the rest of the fleet arrived.<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Behind the scenes==
