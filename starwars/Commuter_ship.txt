A '''commuter ship''' was a type of [[starship]] used for commercial travel between [[planet]]s. The [[Starspeeder 1000|Star Tours Speeder]] was a ship of this type. They were often operated by [[droid]]s. The most recognizable commuter ship [[pilot]]s were [[RX-series pilot droid|RX-Series pilot droids]]. Before them, [[AC-series pilot droid]]s were often used to operate commuter vehicles.<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref>
