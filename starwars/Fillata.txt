'''Fillata''' was the third [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Orbit/Legends|orbiting]] the [[Star/Legends|star]] [[Prael/Legends|Prael]] in the [[Japrael system/Legends|Japrael system]]. Located in the [[Japrael sector/Legends|Japrael sector]] of the [[Inner Rim/Legends|Inner Rim]] Territories, it was not able to sustain any life due to it being a toxic world.
