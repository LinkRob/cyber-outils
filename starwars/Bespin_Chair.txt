The '''Bespin Chair''' was the collective name of a custom [[Repulsorlift chair|repulsorlift]] [[Command Chair]] and a pair of [[Blaster Remote]]s owned by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sprocket]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Command Chair (Sprocket)|standard chair and remote set]]. The chair featured a [[Color|silver]], blue, orange, silver, and brown color scheme, while the [[remote]]s were orange, blue, and silver, with black [[sensor]]s.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bespin Chair was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Sprocket's Command Chair, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Bespin Skies]]. Unlike most Weapon Wraps in the game, Bespin Chair did not share the same rarity as its cosmetic set counterpart, with Bespin Skies instead being a "Legendary" rarity "Costume."<ref name="Hunters" />
