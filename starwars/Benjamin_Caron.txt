'''Benjamin Caron''' is a director who helmed the [[Announcement|seventh]], [[Daughter of Ferrix|eleventh]], and [[Rix Road (episode)|twelfth episodes]] of [[Andor Season 1|''Andor'' Season 1]].<ref name="Media kit" /> According to<ref name="Collider">{{WebCite|url=https://collider.com/andor-tony-gilroy-directing-comments|text='Andor' Creator Tony Gilroy Reveals Why He Didn't Direct Any Episodes, and Why That's a Good Thing|author=Amin Arezou|date=2022-11-05|archivedate=20221110032415}}</ref> executive producer and writer<ref name="Kassa">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> [[Tony Gilroy]], each director had three to four months to prepare and 55 days to shoot.<ref name="Collider" /> [[Denise Gough]], the actress who played [[Dedra Meero]] said that Caron set up her character. For the scene in which Meero tortures [[Bix Caleen]], Caron instructed Gough to not do any swish and to try the scene with her hands behind her back. Gough told Caron that she was unable to say she needed to say she needed a [[Multi-Sector Data Blend]] going back two years.<ref name="Building Dedra">{{WebCite|url=https://www.empireonline.com/tv/features/andor-denise-gough-interview-building-star-wars-villain-dedra-meero|text=Andor's Denise Gough On Building Dedra Meero &ndash; Star Wars' Terrifying New Villain|author=Ben Travis|date=2022-11-29|archivedate=20221130235403}}</ref> Gilroy wanted to bring Caron back for [[Andor Season 2|Season 2]], but he did not want to come back due to working on the film [[Wikipedia:Sharper (film)|''Sharper'']].<ref name="Good day">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/andor-season-2-post-credits-scene-tony-gilroy-1235268962|text=‘Andor' Creator Tony Gilroy Talks Luthen's Good Day, That Post-Credit Scene and Season Two |author=Brian Davids|date=2022-11-23|archivedate=20221123185515}}</ref>
