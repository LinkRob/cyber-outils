'''Barakat Outpost''' was a small [[mining]] outpost located on [[E'ronoh]]. The inhabitants of Barakat outpost, who were mostly the families of quarry workers, lived in apartments that were built into canyon walls. In the outpost's quarries marble was mined with machines. The mined marble used to be supplied to [[Star system|systems]] surrounding the [[Eiram system]] but when the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]] started again<ref name="thrcvg" /> in [[387 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> off planet contracts had become scarce in number. Thus the marble was used to fix the destruction caused by the war.<ref name="thrcvg" />

While visiting Barakat Outpost, [[Xiri A'lbaran]] was challenged to a battle for honor and the [[throne]] by [[Bruzo]].<ref name="thrcvg" />
