'''Dustin Roberts''' is the former president of the [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Official Star Wars Fan Club]] and the current Content Manager for ''Star Wars'' news sites [[TheForce.net]] and [[Rebelscum.com]]. He also appears on [[The ForceCast]] and runs an R2-D2-specific site called R2D2Central.com. Roberts has also contributed to ''[[Star Wars Insider]]''.
