{{Quote|Have you read the testimonies of Cervil the Uncanny? She states that the whip was sometimes used to defend against the Sith Lords who used the Forbidden Forms.|Vernestra Rwoh, to Imri Cantaros|<ref name="Test of Courage" />}}
'''Cervil the Uncanny''' was a [[Jedi]] who fought in the [[Sith Wars]]. She left behind testimonies about the use of [[lightwhip]]s&mdash;a [[lightsaber]] variant with a flexible blade&mdash;to defend against [[Sith Lord]]s using the [[Forbidden Forms]].<ref name="Test of Courage" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]]<ref name="Test of Courage" /> when the [[Sith]] were believed to be extinct,<ref name="Sith extinction">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' states that the [[Sith]] were believed to have been vanquished in [[1032 BBY]]. ''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' states that the Sith were discovered to still exist during the [[Battle of Naboo]], which ''Timelines'' dates to [[32 BBY]]. ''Timelines'' also establishes that the [[High Republic Era]] began around [[500 BBY]] and ended in [[100 BBY]], meaning that the Sith were believed to be extinct during that era.</ref> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] researched Jedi usages of lightwhips and uncovered<ref name="LC">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> Cervil's testimonies. Rwoh later cited the testimonies to [[Padawan]] [[Imri Cantaros]] as proof that the [[Jedi Order]] had historically used lightwhips, in defense of the lightwhip mode she had added to her own [[Vernestra Rwoh's purple-bladed lightsaber|lightsaber]].<ref name="Test of Courage" />

==Behind the scenes==
