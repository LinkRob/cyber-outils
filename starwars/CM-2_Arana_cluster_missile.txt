A '''CM-2 Arana cluster missile''' was a model of [[cluster missile]] that could be launched from a [[starship]] and could lock onto multiple targets. Upgrading an [[EML-850 light freighter]] previously equipped with [[M-72 concussion missile]]s to CM-2 Arana missiles cost two thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and required a [[X-FP-C28 ship computer]], twenty units of [[quadanium steel]], and eight enhanced [[durasteel]] [[hull]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics}}
During [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] paid for the services of a [[starship]] [[Technician|mechanic]] to upgrade her EML-850 [[light freighter]] ''[[Trailblazer]]'' to launch CM-2 Arana cluster missiles, providing the necessary parts.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:ConcussionMissile-Outlaws.jpg|thumb|right|200px|The video game model used by all types of concussion missiles and cluster missiles in ''Star Wars Outlaws'']]
CM-2 Arana cluster missiles appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, all the missiles launched as part of the Arana cluster missiles and the [[concussion missile]]s use the same model however each type of missile has a unique icon.<ref name="Outlaws" />
