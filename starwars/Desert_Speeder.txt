The '''Desert Speeder''' is a [[Landspeeder/Legends|landspeeder]] used for [[Transport/Legends|transport]] and attack strikes. It was built using technology from [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]].

==Characteristics==
The Desert Speeder is a [[Landspeeder/Legends|landspeeder]] built from technology from the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] featuring three turbine [[Engine/Legends|engines]]. Two of the engines are hinged, and two [[Power converter/Legends|power converters]] are on the rear. It features cooling systems and sand filters. The speeder sports two short-ranger [[Laser cannon/Legends|laser cannons]] along with long-range radar and can reach speeds of 700 [[Kilometer per hour/Legends|kilometers per hour]]. It is [[Pilot/Legends|piloted]] by one person, who also acts as the [[Gunner/Legends|gunner]]. It is defended with medium-grade [[Armor/Legends|hull plating]] and has a [[Duraplex/Legends|duraplex]] windshield.

==History==
[[Gree (clone trooper)/Legends|Commander Gree]] built one during the [[Battle for the Stolen Crystals]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].

==Behind the scenes==
The Desert Speeder appears in the [[2013]] [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] comic ''[[LEGO Star Wars Brickmaster: Battle for the Stolen Crystals]]''.
