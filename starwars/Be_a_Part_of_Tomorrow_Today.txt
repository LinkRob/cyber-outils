'''''Be a Part of Tomorrow Today''''' was a [[propaganda]] [[Painting|mural]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] for the [[Stormtrooper Corps]]. It featured three [[stormtrooper]]s lined up side by side and the title set in a multi-[[color]]ed [[Aurebesh]] font.<ref name="Occupation" />
