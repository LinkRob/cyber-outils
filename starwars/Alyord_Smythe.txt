'''Alyord Smythe''' was a [[human]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] and [[captain]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. Six [[Standard month|months]] before the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]], Smythe and eleven other [[TIE series|TIE]] [[pilot]]s took part in the [[Battle at Trenchenovu|defense]] of the [[Trenchenovu shipyards]], having been loaned to the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Sanction]]''. Smythe's [[squadron]] [[Death|massacred]] an attacking [[Nath Tensent's squadron|squadron]] of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]]s, with only the [[rebel pilot]] [[Nath Tensent]] escaping. At some point in the seven months after [[Trenchenovu]], Smythe perished.

==Biography==
{{Quote|How can they all be dead?''"<br />"''Seven months is a long time for a TIE pilot.''"<br />"''But your guys were ''good''.|Nath Tensent and Shakara Nuress discuss Smythe's squadron|<ref name="Alphabet" />}}
During the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="Alphabet" /> the [[human]]<ref name="USW" /> Alyord Smythe served as a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] with the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[captain]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. Six [[Standard month|months]] prior to the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Alphabet" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> Smythe and eleven [[TIE series|TIE]] [[pilot]]s were loaned to the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Sanction]]'' by 204th [[Colonel]] [[Shakara Nuress]] to defend the [[Trenchenovu shipyards]] against an anticipated [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Battle at Trenchenovu|attack]].<ref name="Alphabet" />

[[File:Nath_Tensent.png|thumb|left|220px|After the loss of his own squadron, Nath Tensent (pictured) sought revenge on Alyord Smythe's squadron, only to find out that they were all dead.]]
When the attack came, Smythe's [[squadron]] ambushed the attacking [[Nath Tensent's squadron|squadron]] of [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]]s as it attempted a bombing run on the [[Trenchenovu]] [[shipyard]]s. The rebel [[bomber]]s were cut off from their initial escape route by the ''Sanction'', and so flew through to the other side of the shipyards. Smythe and the other TIE pilots made chase, [[Death|killing]] all of the enemy pilots aside from [[Nath Tensent]], who escaped into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Alphabet" />

Smythe, along with the other eleven TIE pilots who had fought at Trenchenovu, perished at some point in the following seven months. Tensent confronted Nuress after those seven months, asking for the names of the pilots who had massacred his squadron so that he could get revenge on them. When the colonel revealed to him that the twelve pilots, including Smythe, were already dead, Tensent fatally shot her instead after she confirmed that she had approved the Smythe's squadron's deployment to Trenchenovu.<ref name="Alphabet" />

==Behind the scenes==
