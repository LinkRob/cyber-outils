[[Standard year|Years]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[L3-37]] were '''ambushed''' in the [[Halthor sector]] by an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[interdictor]] as they delivered a shipment of [[All Terrain Defense Turret]] [[walker]]s. The ambush resulted in Calrissian and L3 losing the entirety of the cargo.<ref name="SWC" />

After the [[Battle for Tatooine]], [[Tatooine rebel cell]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] came in possession of Calrissian's [[holojournal]]s, including [[Holojournal Entry 1983712]], and received chatter from the criminal underworld of the older and heavily-armed AT-DT walkers being utilized. [[Saponza]] deduced that someone must have found Calrissian's shipment, and that people would be hunting for the rest of the cargo. Saponza and [[Saponza's partner|his partner]] embarked on a [[mission to recover Lando Calrissian's shipment]].<ref name="SWC" />
