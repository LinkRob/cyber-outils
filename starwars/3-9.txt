'''3-9''' was a [[human]] listener who served the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Information Office|Information Office]] under the command of the controller [[Boon|LT-319]]. They were stationed aboard an [[IGV-55 surveillance vessel]]. One of their colleagues was an [[Unidentified intelligence officer|intelligence officer]]. 3-9 and their colleagues took part in an [[Mission to recover Imperial codes|operation]] to hijack [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]] and discover the location of [[Chopper Base]]. 3-9 and their colleagues perished when the rebel [[Hera Syndulla]] transmitted a data surge that overwhelmed their ship's systems, causing it to explode.<ref name="DAD" />

==Biography==
[[File:IGV-55SurveillanceVesselBridge-DAD.png|thumb|left|300px|3-9 served aboard an IGV-55 surveillance vessel.]]
3-9 was an Imperial intelligence officer who served aboard an [[IGV-55 surveillance vessel]] during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]]. They were part of a group of individuals led by Imperial Controller [[Boon|LT-319]] who gave up their autonomy and wore cyborg constructs. 3-9 was part of a psychological warfare operation by the [[Galactic Empire]] to combat the growing [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Their team was part of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] efforts to gather intelligence on rebel activities.<ref name="Rebels Recon #3.19">{{RebelsRecon|9LJGjPz2RFc|#3.19 &#124; Inside "Double Agent Droid" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-double-agent-droid}}</ref>

In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Death Date" /> 3-9 was stationed aboard their IGV-55 surveillance vessel when the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] droids [[C1-10P|Chopper]] and [[AP-5]] passed through the sensor outside the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]'s [[Killun Station|station]] on [[Killun 71]]. Chopper and AP-5 had been sent by [[Phoenix Leader]] [[Hera Syndulla]] on a [[Mission to recover Imperial codes|mission]] to steal clearance codes for an upcoming raid on the planet [[Lothal]]. Chopper's presence was detected by 3-9, who alerted LT-319. LT-319 rightfully suspected Chopper to be the rebel droid that Thrawn had warned about and ordered their crew to set a course for Killun Station.<ref name="DAD" />

[[File:3-9Reports-DAD.png|thumb|right|300px|3-9 reports the breach at Killun Station to the Controller.]]
Under the Controller's orders, 3-9 tracked scan 7161, which turned out to be the [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class landing craft]] that Chopper and AP-5 had traveled in. After 3-9 confirmed this fact, the Controller checked the archival data on unregistered rebel droids and found that Chopper was an outdated [[C1-series astromech droid|C1-series model]]; the same model that Grand Admiral Thrawn had referenced in his manual on the [[Phoenix Cell]]. 3-9 suggested alerting Killun Station but LT-319 decided to [[Slicer|slice]] into Chopper's systems so that they could find the location of the Phoenix Squadron's [[Chopper Base|base]].<ref name="DAD" />

Monitoring Chopper through surveillance cameras, 3-9 discovered that Chopper was toying with a [[network terminal]] while waiting for AP-5 to collect the clearance codes from the central computer core. Under LT-319's orders, 3-9 "captured" Chopper by using the terminal to remotely reprogram the droid. After taking over Chopper, 3-9 found that the droid's memory banks did not contain the location of the rebel base. Undeterred, LT-319 ordered Chopper to accompany his fellow rebels back to their ship. Chopper traveled with AP-5 and their [[pilot]] [[Wedge Antilles]] back to the [[Spectres]]' rebel cell's [[VCX-100 light freighter]] ''[[Ghost]]''.<ref name="DAD" />

[[File:3-9SystemOverload-DAD.png|thumb|left|300px|3-9's console overloads under the data feedback surge.]]
Under LT-319's direction, Chopper brought the ''Ghost'' out of [[hyperspace]] and trapped his fellow rebels in the cargo bay. 3-9 monitored the situation and was present when LT-319 ordered Chopper to install a [[Computer spike|data spike]] into the rebel ship's [[navigation computer]]. LT-319's plan however unraveled when the rebels regained control of their ship and Chopper. Chopper's owner Hera retaliated against the Imperial intelligence officers by reversing Chopper's data feed back at the Imperial surveillance vessel. As Hera launched her counter-attack, 3-9 reported that their circuits were overloading while their [[Unidentified intelligence officer|colleague]] reported that their fuel cells had been compromised. Shortly later, the surveillance vessel exploded with all hands aboard.<ref name="DAD" />

==Personality and traits==
3-9 was an Imperial intelligence officer who worked on an IGV-55 surveillance vessel. In their service to the Empire, he gave up part of their autonomy and wore a cyborg construct.<ref name="Rebels Recon #3.19" /> 3-9 used their technical skills to reprogram the rebel droid Chopper from afar. Despite their skills, he was unable to deal with a data surge created by the rebel Hera Syndulla, which destroyed the surveillance vessel and all hands aboard including themselves.<ref name="DAD" />

==Behind the scenes==
3-9 first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' [[Star Wars Rebels Season Three|Season Three]] episode "[[Double Agent Droid]]," which premiered on [[Disney XD]] on [[March 11]], [[2017]]. They were voiced by [[Stephen Stanton]].<ref name="DAD" />
