'''Car Affa''' was a [[Weequay]] [[arms dealer]] active on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Affa sold [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] arms to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] until he was arrested by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]. Affa was then brought to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] by the [[Jedi]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Master? Is it all right if I go to the library to do some research?''"<br />"''Sure, I'll take care of our little friend here.|Tano and Skywalker speak while transporting Affa through the temple|<ref name="LL" />}}
The [[Weequay]] Car Affa worked as an [[arms dealer]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and the [[Galactic Republic]]. During the war, he began buying arms on the [[black market]] and selling them to the Separatists. While spending time in [[slum district G17]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], he was tracked down to a bar by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] based on Republic intelligence. Leaving Tano outside to guard for any escape attempt Affa might make, Skywalker entered the establishment alone. Several [[blaster]] shots later, Affa was apprehended and the bar's other occupants had fled. Skywalker brought Affa out to meet Tano, and the trio returned to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]]. At the temple, Tano asked to be excused so she could go to the [[Jedi Archives]] and carry out some research. Skywalker agreed to let her go, promising to finish business with Affa himself.<ref name="LL" />

==Personality and traits==
Affa was a vile and corrupt Weequay war profiteer with [[Color|brown]] skin.<ref name="LL" />
