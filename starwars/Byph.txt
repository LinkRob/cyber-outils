'''Byph''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Ithorian]] [[youngling]] who [[Jedi training|trained]] to become a [[Jedi]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. During the war, Byph and five other [[Jedi Initiate|initiates]] were selected to travel to the [[planet]] [[Ilum]] and take part in the ritual known as [[Gathering|the Gathering]], where they would locate [[kyber crystal]]s to use in the construction of their first [[lightsaber]].

==Biography==
[[File:Byph-CGSWG.png|thumb|250px|left|Byph's lightsaber emitted a blue blade.]]
Around two [[Standard year|years]] into the [[Clone Wars]], Byph and the other [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Petro]], [[Katooni]], [[Gungi]], [[Ganodi]], and [[Zatt]], being at the top of their class were sent to the [[planet]] [[Ilum]] to collect the [[kyber crystal]]s for their [[lightsaber]]s. During the trial, Byph was forced to overcome his own fear in order to retrieve his [[crystal]], which he succeeded in doing.<ref name="The Gathering" /> He and the other younglings were then taken by [[Ahsoka Tano]] to the ''[[Crucible]]'', a [[starship]] where the [[architect droid]] [[Huyang]] would teach them to build their blades. While Byph and the others were trying to construct their lightsabers, the ship was boarded by [[Weequay]] [[pirate]]s led by [[Hondo Ohnaka]]. The Jedi managed to fight off the pirates, but the Weequay captured Tano before returning to the nearby planet [[Florrum]].<ref name="A Test of Strength">{{TCW|A Test of Strength}}</ref>

===Rescuing Ahsoka===
After contacting [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and learning he was unable to aid them, Byph and the other younglings were forced to land on Florrum where Tano was being held. Disguising themselves as part of a [[Preigo's Traveling World of Wonder|circus troupe]], the younglings were able to infiltrate [[Hondo's complex|Ohnaka's lair]] and free Tano before attempting to escape from the pirates.<ref name="Bound for Rescue" />

The attempt was unsuccessful, but shortly after the younglings were captured, [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] forces, led by [[General]] [[Grievous]] attacked Florrum, forcing the Jedi and the pirates to work together. By cooperating, the two groups were able to escape Florrum and meet up again with [[Galactic Republic]] forces, who let the pirates go free.<ref name="A Necessary Bond">{{TCW|A Necessary Bond}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Byph was an [[Ithorian]]<ref name="The Gathering" /> [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Databank" /> who was 
1.22 [[meter]]s (4 [[Foot|feet]]) tall,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> with [[Color|gray]] [[eye]]s that had yellow pupils and brown skin.<ref name="The Gathering" />

Byph was a member of the clan of younglings sent to Ilum to harvest their kyber crystals. His greatest challenge was overcoming his fears, which often made situations seem worse than they actually were. He overcame those fears during [[Gathering|the Gathering]] and successfully retrieved his crystal.<ref name="The Gathering" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a Jedi Initiate and one of the top in his class,<ref name="The Gathering" /> Byph was powerful in [[Force-sensitive|the Force]]. He was capable with [[telekinesis]], successfully making his own lightsaber.<ref name="Bound for Rescue" /> He was also capable in lightsaber combat, being able to strike down several [[battle droid]]s.<ref name="A Necessary Bond" />

==Equipment==
Byph wielded a blue lightsaber,<ref name="Bound for Rescue" /> and wore [[Jedi apparel|Jedi robes]].<ref name="The Gathering" />
