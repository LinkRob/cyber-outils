The '''Bug Hunters''' were [[gangster]]s who kept butterflies in captivity in cages. The [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] tried to free them but were captured. However, Solo's [[Gender|son]], [[Ben Solo]], had hidden in the their [[starship]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. He freed his father and the butterflies, defeating the Bug Hunters. The gang leader moved on a machine with mechanical legs and pliers.<ref name="Ben 2">{{RollOut|Ben Solo and the Bug Hunters – Chapter 2}}</ref><ref name="Ben 3">{{RollOut|Ben Solo and the Bug Hunters – Chapter 3}}</ref>
