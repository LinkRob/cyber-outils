The term '''bounty''' referred to a reward offered in exchange for the capture, or sometimes [[death]], of an individual. [[Bounty hunter]]s were individuals who made a living out of collecting bounties.

The term "bounty" could also refer to the specific individual that was being targeted, for which the reward was offered.<ref name="dark">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> Bounties could be offered in-person to a hunter<ref name="Chapter 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> or posted on the [[Hunter Net]].<ref name="Dark Lord 11">[[Darth Vader (2017) 11|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 11]]</ref>

==History==
[[File:Captured-Transformations.png|thumb|left|300px|Boba Fett captures a human bounty.]]
Around the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Scarif]], a bounty was issued for [[Mon Mothma]], [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Rogue One novel" />

[[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] eventually became so irritated with [[Han Solo]] that he placed a bounty of 224,190 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on his head.<ref name="everything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> [[Luke Skywalker]] once had a bounty of 60,000 credits against him from the [[Galactic Empire]] for the destruction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. This irritated Han Solo because, at the time, it was higher than his own Imperial-issued bounty,<ref name="starwars16">[[Star Wars (2015) 16|''Star Wars'' (2015) 16]]</ref> which was 50,000 credits.<ref name="starwars1">[[Star Wars (2015) 1|''Star Wars'' (2015) 1]]</ref>

Following the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Galactic Empire]] placed a bounty of 10,000,000 credits on the head of [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="Leia 1">[[Princess Leia 1|''Princess Leia'' 1]]</ref><ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

Before the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the [[First Order]] place a 20,000-credit bounty on [[Kel|two]] [[Eila|runaway]] children from [[Tehar]].<ref name="The Children from Tehar">{{Resistance|The Children from Tehar}}</ref>
