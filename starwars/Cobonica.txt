A '''Cobonica''' was a creature native to the [[Golamaran ice flats]] in [[Rystan]]. It had milky [[Color|white]] [[eye]]s, shaggy fur covered tentacles, and serrated teeth. In around [[231 BBY]] when [[Kufa]], [[Marchion Ro]], and [[Udi Dis]] where looking for the [[Great Leveler]], a cobonica attacked them.<ref name="TRS">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>
