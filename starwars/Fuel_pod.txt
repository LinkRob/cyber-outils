A '''fuel pod''' was a large tank designed to hold extra [[fuel]]. They could be carried on the outside of [[starship]]s, such as [[light freighter]]s, for refueling of ships, such as [[starfighter]]s, that had less fuel capacity.<ref name="Secret Cargo">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref>

[[Lothal]] [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]] chose to blow up a fuel pod in order to stop the fleeing [[Spectres]] rebel cell. The sacrifice of [[Kanan Jarrus]], however, allowed the group to escape, while also resulting in the sidelining of the [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]] program on the [[planet]], earning Pryce the wrath of project head [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]].<ref name="DUME">{{Rebels|DUME}}</ref>
