The '''Eggscited Salvagers''' was an outfit owned by the team of [[Jawa]] brothers known collectively as the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Utooni]] that they could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to their [[Suspicious Salvagers|standard costume]]. This garb featured [[Color|dark brown]] [[robe]]s covered in what appeared to be [[mudhorn egg]] yolk. The upper Jawa sported crisscrossed [[bandolier]]s that featured red, brown, tan, and silver components, and a [[Hair|furry]] brown [[mudhorn]] [[egg]] was secured to their back with a buckle, a curved [[knife]] protruding from its leaking top.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eggscited Salvagers was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Utooni, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Eggscited Set]].<ref name="Hunters" />
