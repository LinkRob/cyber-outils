A '''confrontation''' took place on [[Alzoc III]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Following [[Jedi Master]] [[Luminara Unduli]]'s first [[Rescue on the Tranquility|encounter]] with [[Sith]] [[assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]] aboard the ''[[Tranquility]]'', Unduli fought Ventress on Alzoc III alongside Master [[Aayla Secura]]. As a result of the confrontation, the two Jedi knocked out Ventress and trapped her in an [[Water|ice]] [[cave]] which they collapsed.<ref name="Force Users" />
