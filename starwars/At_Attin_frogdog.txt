The '''At Attin frogdog''' was a type of [[Limb|quadrupedal]] [[frog-dog]] [[Homeworld|from]] the [[planet]] [[At Attin]]. Inquisitive and loyal, they were kept as pets, and during the [[New Republic Era]], the [[Ithorian]] [[Gender|Misses]] [[Ikk]] owned [[Ikk's frogdog|one]] and walked him on a leash. Two [[Standard day|days]] in a row, the [[Youngling|child]] [[Wim]] rushed past the pair, causing the frogdog to begin barking. The frogdog also barked at a [[Silvo's pirate frigate|pirate frigate]] that arrived on At Attin to take control of the world.

==Description==
At Attin frogdogs were a type of [[frog-dog]] [[Homeworld|from]]<ref name="DB" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="SC1" /> [[At Attin]]. Standing 0.35 [[meter]]s tall, they were [[Limb|quadrupeds]] with mottled [[Color|brown]] skin and a pair of prominent black [[eye]]s, with small [[ear]] holes behind them. Their large [[mouth]] was filled with long, white [[Tooth|teeth]].<ref name="DB" />

==Behavior==
Bright, inquisitive, loyal, and attentive, At Attin frogdogs were sought for the companionship they offered and kept as domestic pets.<ref name="DB" /> 

==History==
[[File:BarkingFrogDog-SC1.png|thumb|right|300px|Misses Ikk's At Attin frogdog barked at Wim.]]
During the [[New Republic Era]] on At Attin, the [[Ithorian]] [[Gender|Misses]] [[Ikk]] owned a [[Ikk's frogdog|pet At Attin frogdog]], and took<ref name="SC1" /> him<ref name="SC1AD">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> for a walk on a leash through a suburban neighborhood. While on<ref name="SC1" /> his<ref name="SC1AD" /> way to [[At Attin Middle School Gamma|school]], the [[human]] [[Youngling|child]] [[Wim]] ran past Ikk and her At Attin frogdog, causing the pet to begin barking. The Ithorian began speaking in [[Ithorese]] as Wim shouted an apology without stopping.<ref name="SC1" />

The following [[Standard day|day]], Wim [[Sleep|overslept]] and missed the [[Speeder bus|hoverbus]] to school, forcing him to ride a [[Speeder bike|hoverbike]] instead to try and make it on [[time]] for his [[Career Assessment Test]]. As he hurriedly left his house on the bike, he once again raced past Misses Ikk and the frog-dog, causing the pet to begin barking again.<ref name="SC1" />

When [[Silvo's pirates|pirates]] later arrived on At Attin and took control of the world, Ikk and her frog-dog<ref name="SC8">{{SkeletonCrew|The Real Good Guys}}</ref> were out for a walk<ref name="SC8AD">{{SkeletonCrew|The Real Good Guys}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> and stared up at the arrival of the pirate's [[Silvo's pirate frigate|frigate]] over the [[Unidentified city (At Attin)|city]] where they lived. The frog-dog began barking loudly at the vehicle, which soon opened fire on the surface and deployed pirates to round up Ikk and the other residents. The pirates were then defeated when the [[New Republic]] arrived to provide aid.<ref name="SC8" />

==Behind the scenes==
An At Attin frogdog appeared in "[[This Could Be a Real Adventure]],"<ref name="SC1" /> the first episode of the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'', which aired on [[December 2]], [[2024]].<ref name="Early">{{SW|url=news/skeleton-crew-premiere-date|text=''Star Wars: Skeleton Crew'' Will Debut Early|date=2024-11-26}}</ref> Prior to the release of the episode, it was pictured in the show's first trailer, which was released on [[August 10]], 2024.<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|a6voXgBlmpk|Skeleton Crew &#124; Official Trailer &#124; Streaming December 3 on Disney+|date=August 10, 2024}}</ref> The creature was first identified as an At Attin frogdog in a [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry posted on [[StarWars.com]] on [[November 3]], 2024.<ref name="DB" />
