A convoy was '''attacked''' in the [[Midgor]] [[star system]] by the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] following the [[Attack on Kortatka|attack]] on [[Kortatka]] as part of [[Operation: Cinder]]. The survivors of the convoy were later found and rescued by [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]]'s [[New Republic]] forces, during their [[hunt for Shadow Wing]].

==The attack==
{{Quote|We were in a battle. TIE fighters attacked us. Dismantled our escorts in minutes, then moved on.|Captain Oultovar Misk, ''Diamond Tor'', Midgor|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> following the [[Attack on Kortatka|attack]] on [[Kortatka]] as part of [[Operation: Cinder]], the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], under the leadership of [[Colonel]] [[Soran Keize]], attacked a convoy of cargo vessels, who were traveling through [[Midgor]], a [[star system]] in the [[Croynar sector]] and were operating under the protection of the [[Yomo Council]]. The 204th's [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s took out the convoy's escorts within minutes, before damaging and disabling the cargo vessels, which included the [[freighter]] ''[[Diamond Tor]]''. The 204th then left.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==The rescue==
{{Quote|This is Captain Oultovar Misk of the freighter Diamond Tor. We are in need of assistance and are prepared to surrender. Repeat: We surrender!|Captain Oultovar Misk, ''Diamond Tor'', Midgor|<ref name="Victory's Price" />}}
Later, as part of the ongoing [[Hunt for Shadow Wing|hunt]] for the 204th Imperial Fighter Wing, [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] and her [[New Republic]] forces aboard the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]'', arrived in Midgor on a heavy reconnaissance mission, following intelligance transmitted to the ''Deliverance'' by one of the [[Alphabet Squadron]] pilots, [[Captain]] [[Nath Tensent]]. After arrival, the ''Deliverance'' held back in case of a trap, while its [[Deliverance's starfighter wing|starfighter wing]] moved ahead. One of the [[Wild Squadron]] pilots, [[Vitale]] ([[Wild Two]]), picked up something on his sensors. The [[Fighter wing|wing's]] [[commander]], [[Wyl Lark]] went ahead with [[Flare Squadron]] to take a better look, while [[Hail Squadron|Hail]] and Wild Squadrons held position. Flare pilot, [[Ghordansk]], spotted one and recognized it as an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[cargo hauler]]. Lark carried out a fly-by and tried to raise comms with the convoy. Captain [[Oultovar Misk]] of the disabled freighter ''Diamond Tor'' responded, by requesting assistance and offering their surrender. Misk explained that they were attacked by TIE fighters, who took out their escorts in minutes, then moved on. Lark promised to help, then transmitted an all-clear to the ''Deliverance'' and ordered his squadrons to assist with evacuation and damage control.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
