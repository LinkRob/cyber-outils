'''Cynogan''' was a [[language]] spoken by the individual<ref name="Languages" /> known as [[Saucer Head]].<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2018 Topps Solo: A Star Wars Story|cardname=Saucer Head|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/176424/cid/11326611}}</ref> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' featuring the appearance of Cynogan to [[10 BBY]].</ref> Saucer Head was a dealer at a game of<ref name="Solo" /> the [[Corellian Spike]] variant of [[sabacc]] at the [[Cantina|saloon]] known as [[the Lodge]] on<ref name="SoloGuide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="GalaxyMap">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Vandor]].<ref name="SoloGuide" /> During the game, the player spoke Cynogan several times, including while<ref name="Solo" /> he<ref name="Designs">{{SW|url=news/solo-a-star-wars-story-designs|text=Inside the Designs of ''Solo: A Star Wars Story''}}</ref> was shuffling and dealing cards.<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cynogan was first depicted, albeit unidentified, in the [[2018]] ''[[Star Wars Anthology Series]]'' film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" /> The language was then named by the [[November 14]], [[2019]] video "Every Language in ''Star Wars'' Movies," which was part of the ''[[Star Wars By the Numbers]]'' series created by [[StarWarsKids.com]].<ref name="Languages" />
