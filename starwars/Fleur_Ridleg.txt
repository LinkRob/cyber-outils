'''Fleur Ridleg''' was a [[Gender|non-binary]] [[human]] [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. While in the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]]'s [[Arkorayal Cabaret Cantina|Republic outpost]] in [[228 BBY]], they attended [[Admiral]] [[Pevel Kronara]]'s planning meeting for the joint [[Republic Defense Coalition]] and Jedi Order [[Raid on the Korvix Vorn|mission]] to rescue [[prisoner]]s from the [[Nihil]] [[prison ship]] ''[[Korvix Vorn]]''. Afterward, Ridleg [[Pilot|flew]] a [[Jedi Vector]] during the endeavor, and the group's efforts eventually succeeded in saving the captives aboard the [[Starship|vessel]].

Later, Ridleg attended the [[Mrala-Talisola Wedding|wedding]] of the [[Marriage|wives]] [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]] and [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]]. Immediately following the event, Ridleg, as part of the [[Eriaduan resistance]], helped [[Battle of Eriadu|defend]] Eriadu from a [[Invasion of Eriadu|Nihil invasion]], fighting alongside other Jedi Masters under the leadership of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Farzala Tarabal]]. In a group led by Jedi Master [[Yaddle]], Ridleg worked with other [[Jedi]] to stabilize the [[lava reactor core]] under [[Bri-Phrang City]]'s streets, though their efforts were unsuccessful. The Nihil were ultimately defeated on Eriadu and collectively surrendered not long afterward, with Ridleg celebrating the victory amongst allies.

==Biography==
===Raid on the ''Korvix Vorn''===
{{Quote|We're getting chopped up out here! Someone has to quiet those blister bombs!''"<br />"''Workin' on it!|A Republic pilot and Fleur Ridleg during the ''Korvix Vorn'' rescue|<ref name="THRA 4" />}}
[[File:KorvixRaidKronaraRidleg-2023THRA4.jpg|thumb|right|340px|Fleur Ridleg (right) flew a Jedi Vector during the ''Korvix Vorn'' mission.]]
The [[Gender|non-binary]]<ref name="Bluesky" /> [[human]] Fleur Ridleg was a [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="THRA 4" /> They<ref name="Pronouns" /> were present in the [[Arkorayal Cabaret Cantina|Republic outpost]] on<ref name="THRA 4" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="HRCE">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Eriadu]]<ref name="THRA 4" /> in [[228 BBY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date-THRA4">{{StarlightDate|[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4]]|one [[Standard year|year]] and two weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> after being vetted to attend, Ridleg joined a meeting hosted for members of the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] and the Jedi Order that was led by the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Admiral]] [[Pevel Kronara]] to plan a [[Raid on the Korvix Vorn|mission]] to rescue [[prisoner]]s from the [[Nihil]] [[prison ship]] ''[[Korvix Vorn]]''. They stood next to [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Iric Lams]] as Kronara displayed a [[hologram]] of the [[Starship|vessel]] and explained its [[Weapon|armaments]]&mdash;[[laser cannon]]s and [[blister bomb]]s. Jedi Master [[Tani Briwill]] informed Ridleg that their task was to work with the Republic Defense Coalition's [[starfighter]]s to take out the ''Korvix Vorn''{{'s}} [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]], [[engine]]s, and blister [[bomb]]s.<ref name="THRA 4" />

After Ridleg confirmed they could complete those objectives, Jedi Master [[Kantam Sy]] expressed<ref name="THRA 4" /> their<ref name="HRCE" /> agreement that the bombs had to be turned off to complete the rescue. Shortly after the meeting's end, the Republic and Jedi carried out the mission and encountered the prison ship after it entered Republic [[Realspace|space]]. Their forces encountered heavy Nihil resistance due to the noise the blister bombs produced, prompting Ridleg to tell a [[Unidentified Republic Defense Coalition pilot 1 (Korvix Vorn)|Republic pilot]] that they were working on turning the enemy's armaments off.<ref name="THRA 4" /> The group eventually managed to rescue the ''Korvix Vorn''{{'s}} prisoners successfully.<ref name="THRA 5">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 5]]</ref>

===Nuptials and Eriadu invasion===
{{Quote|The people of Eriadu are rising against the Nihil! Their fight is our fight! Their freedom is our freedom! The tide of history sweeps around us. We must move with it as one, and when we rise&hellip; ''We rise together!!|Farzala Tarabal rallying his allies, including Fleur Ridleg, as they repelled the Nihil on Eriadu|<ref name="THRA 14" />}}
[[File:GuestsDatabase-THRAWeddingSpectacular.png|thumb|left|300px|Fleur Ridleg and many other guests attended Lula Talisola and Zeen Mrala's wedding.]]
Not long afterward, Ridleg attended the [[Mrala-Talisola Wedding|wedding]] of the [[Marriage|wives]] [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]] and [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]]. They arrived at the Arkorayal Cantina with a group of other Jedi Masters and, shortly afterward, watched a hologram message from Jedi Master [[Torban Buck]] wishing the pair well before their nuptials. The attendees&mdash;including the Jedi Master&mdash;celebrated the couple's nuptials briefly before leaving the [[cantina]] to [[Battle of Eriadu|fend off]] the Nihil [[Invasion of Eriadu|invading]] the planet.<ref name="THRA WS">''[[The High Republic Adventures Phase III - The Wedding Spectacular]]''</ref> In the streets of [[Bri-Phrang City]], Ridleg and the other Jedi Masters engaged in combat between the buildings lining the [[city]]'s streets. The Jedi Master [[Lightsaber combat|deflected]] [[blaster]] [[fire]] with their [[lightsaber]].<ref name="THRA 14" />

Later, by the [[Ocean|seas]] near the city, the [[Eriaduan resistance]] defending Eriadu&mdash;which included Ridleg&mdash;boarded [[rescue speeder]]s and again clashed with the Nihil under the [[Commanding officer|leadership]] of [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Farzala Tarabal]] and [[Qort]]. Several Nihil, who were operating [[gunboat]]s under the command of their leader, an enforcer known as "[[The Warden]]," were eventually driven back by the group. After taking over at least one of the gunboats, the Jedi regrouped under Tarabal's command after Qort was injured, intending to continue rebuffing the remaining Nihil on Eriadu.<ref name="THRA 14" />

===Lava reactor core troubles===
{{Quote|We must make an attempt! Jedi! Begin immediately!|Yaddle instructing Fleur Ridleg and other Jedi to try to move isolator aqua-tanks into place|<ref name="THRA 15">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 15|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 15]]</ref>}}
[[File:JediLavaReactorCore-2023THRA15.jpg|thumb|right|350px|Fleur Ridleg and their fellow Jedi helped stabilize the lava reactor core beneath Bri-Phrang City.]]
Following this, Ridleg joined several other Jedi Masters in the caverns below Bri-Phrang City. The Jedi Masters attempted to [[Force power|use]] [[the Force]] to stabilize the [[lava reactor core]] beneath the city, knowing that even a single impact from the fighting above them could activate it, which would destroy both Bri-Phrang City and Eriadu City. Despite the efforts of Yaddle, Briwill, Qort, and the [[Aloxian]] [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Zint]], they failed to move the [[isolator aqua-tank]]s into the mainframe of the structure.<ref name="THRA 15" />

Knowing their group would need more help to complete the task, Yaddle called for additional Jedi assistance after being informed that the [[mini-reactor]] in the structure that balanced its [[surface bifurcator]] was lopsided, which would need to be fixed before they could fully stabilize the core. However, before more help arrived, Zint took off her [[vonduun skull helmet]], a [[helmet]] that helped Aloxians regulate their emotions and dampened their strength, hoping it would allow her to help move the isolator aqua-tanks. Yaddle immediately called for the Jedi present to make another attempt to move the tanks, prompting Ridleg to reach out their [[Limb|arm]] and use the Force. After successfully moving the tanks into place, Qort put Zint's helmet back on her as she collapsed to the ground, and Ridleg stopped to check on the [[youngling]].<ref name="THRA 15" />

Despite the group's collective efforts, the lava reactor core remained destabilized, causing concern for the Jedi and their allies.<ref name="THRA 16">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 16|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 16]]</ref> Tarabal then issued orders to begin evacuating the city's residents, an effort which Ridleg and the other Jedi helped with.<ref name="THRA 17">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 17|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 17]]</ref> Not long after, the Eriaduan resistance defeated the Nihil on the world, and the Nihil collectively surrendered to the Republic.<ref name="Trials">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref> On Eriadu, Ridleg and their colleagues celebrated their victory. The Jedi Master attended the [[Knighting Ceremony]] of Padawan [[Ram Jomaram]] and also spent time reminiscing on the allies who had [[Death|lost]] their lives during the [[Nihil conflict]].<ref name="THRA 20">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 20|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 20]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You've all been rigorously vetted, so listen up and speak freely.|Pevel Kronara, to Fleur Ridleg and the others in a meeting|<ref name="THRA 4" />}}
During the Nihil conflict, Ridleg was loyal to the Jedi Order and the Republic during their joint fight against the [[Pirate|marauders]]. They had been among several individuals rigorously vetted to attend the briefing meeting for the ''Korvix Vorn'' mission and were willing to fly in combat for the mission, despite its dangers. Ridleg had [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s and tan skin. The Jedi Master kept their black [[hair]]&mdash;which was graying by the time of the ''Korvix Vorn'' mission&mdash;styled in two buns behind their head.<ref name="THRA 4" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a Jedi Master, Ridleg was a [[Force-sensitive]] individual [[Jedi training|trained]] to wield the Force who could [[pilot]] a Jedi Vector,<ref name="THRA 4" /> a type of starfighter designed specifically for Jedi use due to the high level of concentration it required to fly.<ref name="LOTJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> They were able to operate a rescue speeder in combat, working alongside their colleagues to attack Nihil gunboats.<ref name="THRA 14" /> Ridleg was capable of using [[telekinesis]] to move objects with the Force.<ref name="THRA 15" />

==Equipment==
On Eriadu, the Jedi Master donned brown [[Jedi apparel|Jedi attire]] that included a brown [[cloak]], maroon [[Clothing|tabards]], a lighter undertunic with gold-colored collar trimming, tan pants, a [[utility belt]], and gray [[boot]]s. During the ''Korvix Vorn'' raid, Ridleg flew a Jedi Vector and wore a silver [[headset]].<ref name="THRA 4" /> Later, while at Talisola and Mrala's wedding, the Jedi Master donned a [[Temple robes|lighter set]] of Jedi [[robe]]s paired with a fitted, cream-colored cloak.<ref name="THRA WS" /> They wielded a blue-bladed lightsaber.<ref name="THRA 14" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:TheHighRepublicAdventures2023-4-solicit-cover.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Fleur Ridleg debuted in ''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4.]]
Fleur Ridleg first appeared in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|fourth issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']]. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Elisa Romboli]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="THRA 4" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]] on [[March 13]], [[2024]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-796/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-4|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #4}}</ref> On [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]], Older revealed Ridleg's pronouns as "they/them,"<ref name="Pronouns" /> and later confirmed the Jedi's gender as [[Wikipedia:Non-binary|non-binary]] on [[Wikipedia:Bluesky|Bluesky]].<ref name="Bluesky" />

[[Kristin Baver]]'s [[2025]] reference book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]'' included one of artist [[Harvey Tolibao]]'s unfinished panels from<ref name="Artbook">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume II]]''</ref> the one-shot comic ''[[The High Republic Adventures Phase III - The Wedding Spectacular]]''<ref name="THRA WS" /> that included Ridleg.<ref name="Artbook" /> ''The Wedding Spectacular'' was written by Older and released by Dark Horse<ref name="THRA WS" /> on [[December 18]], 2024.<ref name="Dark Horse-Wedding">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-812/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III--The-Wedding-Spectacular|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III--The Wedding Spectacular}}</ref>
