The '''apartment''' lived in by the [[pilot]] [[Bly Tevin]] and his [[Bly Tevin's mother|mother]] was located on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]]. During the [[time]] of the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]], Tevin's [[Parent|mother]] promised to cook him a fresh batch of [[pilafa stew]] when he returned home from fighting as part of the [[E'ronoh's fleet]]'s [[Thylefire Squadron]]. While on a supply escort [[Skirmish in the Eiram system|mission]]<ref name="Convergence" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Tevin thought about his [[Gender|mother]] sitting in their [[Bly Tevin's family|family's]] apartment in the moments before his [[Saboteur|sabotaged]] [[Bly Tevin's devilfighter|devilfighter]] exploded, resulting in his [[death]].<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bly Tevin's apartment was mentioned in [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], a novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's Phase II<ref name="Convergence" /> that was published on [[November 22]], [[2022]].<ref name="ReleaseDates">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-convergence-excerpt|text=A Mysterious Mission Begins in ''The High Republic: Convergence'' &ndash; Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref> Prior to its release, the apartment's mention was included in a preview for the novel released on the official [[Wikipedia:Penguin Random House|Penguin Random House]] [https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com website]<ref name="Excerpt">{{WebCite|url=https://sites.prh.com/convergence|text=STAR WARS: CONVERGENCE EXTENDED EXCERPT|work=[[Wikipedia:Penguin Random House|Penguin Random House]]|archivedate=20221004162329}}</ref> on [[October 4]] of that year.<ref name="ReleaseDates" />
