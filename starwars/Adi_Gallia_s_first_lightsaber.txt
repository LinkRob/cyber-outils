At some point before [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''lightsaber''' was constructed and was [[Lightsaber combat|used]] by [[Jedi Master]] [[Adi Gallia]], a member of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Complete Visual Dictionary" /> Powered by a [[kyber crystal]],<ref name="Kyber Databank" /> it was destroyed during a mission<ref name="Complete Visual Dictionary" /> by 32 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> Afterward, Gallia began using a [[Adi Gallia's second lightsaber|new lightsaber]].<ref name="Complete Visual Dictionary" />

==Behind the scenes==
The [[lightsaber]] was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'', published in [[2018]] and written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[David West Reynolds]].<ref name="Complete Visual Dictionary" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Adi Gallia's first lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] was first mentioned in the reference book [[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary|''Star Wars: Episode I The Visual Dictionary'']], published in [[1999]] and also written by Reynolds.<ref name="TPM Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> It then made its first full appearance in the [[2000]] [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Episode I Jedi Power Battles|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Jedi Power Battles'']].<ref name="JP">[[Star Wars: Episode I Jedi Power Battles|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Jedi Power Battles'']]</ref>
