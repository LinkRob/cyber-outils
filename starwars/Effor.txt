{{Quote|I hope you have said goodbye to your master. That was the last time you saw him. All but one of the gladiator droids will be destroyed. I wish you luck… but that won't help you.|Effor, to C1-10P|<ref name="ABoC" />}}
'''Effor''' was a [[Verpine]] who worked at a [[Unidentified gladiatorial arena|gladiatorial arena]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="ABoC" />

Between [[5 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"Always Bet on Chop" takes place during the events of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' and includes Rex, placing it after the events of "[[The Lost Commanders]]." ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "The Lost Commanders" to [[4 BBY]] and the ending of ''Star Wars Rebels'' to [[1 BBY]], placing "Always Bet on Chop" between these [[Standard year|years]]. Therefore, "Always Ben on Chop" must be taking place between [[4 BBY]] and 1 BBY. Since the arena incident is narrated in flashbacks, it might have taken place as early as [[5 BBY]], given that, as per ''Star Wars Timelines'', the [[Spectres|Spectre cell]]&mdash;which both Orrelios and Chopper are members of&mdash;was established in that year.</ref> Effor was present as the [[Lasat]] [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Garazeb Orrelios]] and<ref name="ABoC" /> his<ref name="zeb-orrelios">{{Databank|zeb-orrelios|Garazeb "Zeb" Orrelios}}</ref> [[droid]] companion [[C1-10P]] "Chopper" infiltrated the arena complex to steal some important [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] data. The [[Tarsunt]] fight prompter [[Blik (Tarsunt)|Blik]] had mistaken Chopper for a [[gladiator droid]] and Orrelios&mdash;for his owner. In a corridor, Blik insisted on walking Garazeb to the registration desk whereas Effor was supposed to prepare Chopper and take him to the area. While seeing the droid off, Effor told him it was the last he had seen of his "master" before wishing him luck, only to clarify that luck would not come in handy as he fought "[[The Unwinder]]," a [[droideka]] champion in the arena. Ultimately, Chopper avoided being beaten by his rival and made his escape from the arena alongside Orrelios.<ref name="ABoC" />

A [[Color|green]]-skinned Verpine with black [[eye]]s, Effor wore a black [[Clothing|vest]] over a gray, sleeveless shirt, in addition to a pair of gray pants held up by a brown belt.<ref name="ABoC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blik first appeared in "[[Always Bet on Chop]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] written by [[Jeremy Barlow]] and illustrated by [[Bob Molesworth]],<ref name="ABoC" /> which was published on [[September 28]], [[2016]]<ref name="Release">{{Panini|shop=1|url=star-wars-tv-und-film-science-fiction-magazine/star-wars-rebels-magazin-23-ydswrm023|text=Star Wars - Rebels - Magazin 23}}</ref> in the 23rd issue of the [[Wikipedia:Germany|German]] [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]].<ref name="ABoC" />
