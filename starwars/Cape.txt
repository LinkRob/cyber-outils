{{Quote|Can you move? Your cape is in my way.''"<br/>"''No.''"<br/>"''You have no sense of personal space.''"<br/>"''But I have a great sense of style.|Shriv Suurgav and Lando Calrissian|<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>}}
A '''cape''' was a loose-fitting outer [[Clothing|garment]] that was fastened at the wearer's neck and draped from the shoulders. [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]] wore capes as a statement of style and fashion.

==Description==
[[File:LandoCapeCollection-DK.png|thumb|left|200px|Lando Calrissian's many capes.]]
A cape was a sleeveless outer [[Clothing|garment]] that was fastened at the neck and fell loosely from the shoulders.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[planet]] [[Abafar]] hosted a cape retailer named [[Land O' Capes]].<ref name="MIA">{{TCW|Missing in Action}}</ref> The [[human]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]] believed that a cape was a very stylish choice of clothing, but his ally [[Shriv Suurgav]] believed it hampered the personal space he offered to others.<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront II" /> Calrissian's companion [[L3-37]] also once noted that wearing a cape seemed to enhance what she believed to be Calrissian's delusions of grandeur.<ref name="Lando - Double or Nothing 1">[[Lando - Double or Nothing 1|''Lando - Double or Nothing'' 1]]</ref>

==History==
[[File:Darth_Vader_Promo_Rogue_One.png|thumb|right|200px|The armor of Darth Vader featured a cape]]
{{Quote|Please forgive him. I've concluded it has something to do with the cape. It enhances his delusions of granduer.|L3-37, regarding Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|<ref name="Lando - Double or Nothing 1" />}}
[[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], the [[Baron Administrator]] of [[Cloud City]], often wore fashionable capes for style,<ref name="Solo" /> as did the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] and former [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]].<ref name="Episode II" /> The [[Kaleesh]] [[cyborg]] [[General]] [[Grievous]], [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|leader]] of the [[Separatist Droid Army|droid armies]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]], also often wore a [[Kaleesh cloak|cape]],<ref name="Episode III" /> brandishing a [[Kaleesh logo|logo]],<ref name="Topps Star Wars Chrome Black">{{Topps|set=2024 Topps Star Wars Chrome Black|subset=- Galactic Factions|cardname=General Grievous}}</ref> within which he kept [[Jedi]] [[lightsaber]]s he had stolen.<ref name="Episode III" /> The [[Darth Vader's armor|suit of armor]] of another Dark Lord, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], also included a [[Color|black]] cape.<ref name="Episode V" /> [[Imperial swamp trooper]]s and [[wet-weather gear stormtrooper]]s of the [[224th Imperial Armored Division]] on Mimban wore waterproof capes known as "slicks."

[[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] affected a white cape with his [[Imperial military uniforms|Imperial uniform]].<ref name="Rogue One" /> [[Director]] [[Sliro Barsha]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] also wore a cape with his uniform, with his cape featuring fur along its top. [[Imperial officer]]s [[Jayhold Beehaz]],<ref name="The Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref> [[Vanis Tigo]],<ref name="Narkina 5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref> and [[Kaido]]<ref name="WAY?">{{Andor|Who Are You?}}</ref> all wore capes. Upon seeing it during the [[Battle of the Revelator|Battle of the ''Revelator'']], Barsha's brother [[Jaylen Vrax]], shortly before executing his brother, complimented the cape and questioned if the fur was Barsha's own idea.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> The [[pirate]] [[captain]] [[Sidon Ithano]] wore an [[armorweave]]-lined cape.<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] of the [[First Order]] wore a black and red armorweave cape over her [[chromium]]-coated [[First Order stormtrooper armor|armor]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> Her colleague, the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Soldier|warrior]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], began wearing a black cape in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />
