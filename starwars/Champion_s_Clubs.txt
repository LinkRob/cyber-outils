The '''Champion's Clubs''' were a pair of [[Melee weapon|clubs]] owned and utilized by the [[Wookiee]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Grozz]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his standard [[KX Clubs]]. They resembled morning star-style maces, with [[Color|black]] and crimson handles, with golden heads decorated in spikes, [[aurebesh]] engravings, and emerald jewels.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Champion's Clubs was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Grozz's KX Clubs, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 3.0.0 alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Kashyyyk Champion]]. This wrap could be unlocked as one of two grand prizes within the "Wookiee Champion Vault."<ref name="Hunters" />
