"'''A Youth Unpromising'''" ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]] title "'''Ein Junge ohne Aussichten'''") is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] written by [[Jeremy Barlow]], drawn by [[Ingo Römling]] and edited by [[Gunther Nickel]]. The story was published in the 35th issue of the [[Wikipedia:Germany|German]] [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]], which was released on [[August 30]], [[2017]].

==Plot summary==
[[Mart Mattin]] is unsatisfied with his duties within the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|rebels]] tries to get his fellow members, [[Gooti Terez]] and [[Jonner Jin]], of [[Iron Squadron]] to join him in departing [[Chopper Base]] and looking for action, but they refuse, preferring to do their part as rebels. Mattin, angry at being rebuffed, storms out and takes off in Iron Squadron's ship, the [[Sato's Hammer|''Sato's Hammer'']]. Unbeknownst to him, [[Wedge Antilles]] stowed aboard the ship after overhearing Mattin's conversation with his fellow crewmembers. Antilles reveals himself while in the ship [[hyperspace]] and tries to convince Mattin to return. Mattin feels like there is no future for him there.

The pair arrive on the planet [[Mykapo]], Mattin's homeworld, Mattin sets out in the city and realizes things have gotten worse for the planet. Antilles follows Mattin and in their conversation, Mattin reveals he is looking for his [[Sato|father's]] old wingmen from when he ran Iron Squadron. However, while deep in discussion, Antilles walks into a large [[droid]] being controlled by an [[Ithorian]]. Mattin gets into a verbal argument with the Ithorian, leading Antilles to try and calm him down. Mattin, now wound up, explains how he felt underused by the rebels, but Antilles explains that the reason rebel command only lets him do supply missions is because Mattin does not follow orders and rushes into dangerous situations. 

They arrive at a [[Harmon's bar|bar]] that had been his [[Mattin|mother]]'s favorite and despite Antilles' caution, charges in looking for [[Harmon]]. Harmon immediately recognizes Mattin and threatens him, grabbing him by the neck. However, the exchange is interrupted by the arrival of [[Stanger]] and three other gangsters. They begin shooting up the bar, while Harmon explains that they belong to a corrupt culinary guild trying to force Harmon to do what they say. As Harmon, Mattin, Antilles, and [[Jarina]] take cover behind the bar and tables, Antilles asks Jarina why there is animosity between Harmon and Mattin. Jarina explains that when Mattin's father was running Iron Squadron, Mattin had not followed his orders, causing his father to rescue him and dying the process. This upset the other members of Iron Squadron who fell apart as a group, and was further exacerbated when Mattin had resurrected the Iron Squadron name for his small band without the rest of the squadron's permission.

At that moment, Harmon surrendered, offering to do what Stanger said if he stopped shooting up the bar. Stanger refused, wanting to destroy the place. Just then, Mattin gets up and runs out the door. Harmon calls him a coward for running away. Stanger and his gangsters then round up Antilles, Harmon, and Jarina and set about looking for something to burn the establishment with. However, Mattin returns with the large droid that Antilles had walked into and attacks the gangsters. Harmon took the moment of surprise to knock out Stanger. 

With the threat eliminated, Harmon, Jarina, and Mattin were cleaning up the bar when Harmon offered Mattin a place to stay. Mattin thanks him, but refuses, saying he has somewhere to be. He takes a [[speeder bike]] and collects Antilles, who was walking back to the [[spaceport]], and offers him a ride back to the rebel base. The two speed off towards the ''Sato's Hammer.''
