Integrated into [[helmet]]s, an '''enhanced optics suite''' provided an individual with a host of specialized [[sensor]]s for optics and audio. Either system was equipped with passive light amplification, thermal signature readers, ultrasound pickups, [[millimeter]] wave emitters, video capture, and optical enhancements. All sensors increased the wearer's battlespace awareness and allowed them to see items and other sentients in inclement conditions such as smoke, fog, and darkness. An enhanced optics suite cost 1,750 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets" />
