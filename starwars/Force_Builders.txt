{{Quote|The Force Builders...An ancient order of the Jedi, devoted to creating the finest art, architecture, and even...starships.|Quarrie, to Rowan Freemaker about the Force Builders|<ref name="Tower" />}}
The '''Force Builders''' were an ancient [[Force-sensitive]] order who were an offshoot of the [[Jedi]]. They were devoted to creating the finest art, architecture, and starships. The Force Builders built the city of [[Alistan Nor (city)|Alistan Nor]] on a [[Alistan Nor (planet)|remote jungle planet]]. By the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]], Alistan Nor had long been abandoned and overtaken by the jungle. Prior to the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[Mon Calamari]] shipbuilder [[Quarrie]] informed [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] about the history of the Force Builders. Rowan later undertook a [[Mission to Alistan Nor|mission]] to Alistan Nor that brought him into contact with the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Hunter droid/LEGO|hunter droid]] [[M-OC/LEGO|M-OC]]. Quarrie came to regard Rowan as one of the last Force Builders.<ref name="Tower" />
