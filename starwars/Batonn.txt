'''Batonn''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Batonn sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] was victorious in the [[Batonn insurgency]], which led to the [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] promoting him to [[Grand Admiral]].

==Description==
The [[planet]] Batonn<ref name="Thrawn" /> was located in the [[Batonn system]] in the [[Batonn sector]], a subsector of the [[Ash Worlds]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> was known to have [[Batonn's main continent|one main continent]] and a smaller landmass called [[Scrim Island]] that lay 300 [[kilometer]]s to the west of the continent. One known [[city]] on Batonn's main island was [[Paeragosto City]], which was the location of [[Governor]] [[Restos]]' palace and was near the [[Creekpath Mining and Refining complex]]. Scrim Island was the location of an [[Scrim Island Imperial garrison|Imperial garrison]] and was protected by a [[deflector shield]], shoreline defenses, four [[ion cannon]]s, and a [[turbolaser]] [[cannon]].<ref name="Thrawn" />

==History==
During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Mining Guild]] exploited recently-discovered deposits of [[doonium]]<ref name="Card">{{FFGXW2|set=Mining Guild TIE Expansion Pack|cardname=Mining Guild Surveyor}}</ref> and other precious metals to the [[Galactic Empire]], which used them to build the [[Imperial Navy]] and the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. [[Arihnda Pryce]]'s [[parent]]s [[Talmoor Pryce]] and [[Elainye Pryce]] relocated to Batonn's Creekpath Mining and Refining complex after losing control of [[Pryce Mining]] on [[Lothal]]. The planet was governed by the corrupt [[Governor]] [[Restos]], who enforced order through the [[Batonn Defense Force]]. Eventually, resentment and grievances against Restos' rule led to the emergence of an [[Batonn insurgents|insurgent movement]] led by [[Nevil Cygni]], who was known by his pseudonym "Nightswan". The Batonn insurgents were part of a [[Batonn sector insurgency|sector-wide insurgency]] and had ties to [[Sammun insurgents|insurgents]] [[Denash insurgents|on]] [[Sammun]] and [[Denash]].<ref name="Thrawn" />

Around [[2 BBY]], Nightswan [[engineer]]ed the [[Battle of Scrim Island|capture]] of Scrim Island in order to set a trap for the [[Chiss]] [[Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], a rising star in the Imperial [[Navy]]. [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Jok Donassius]] dispatched Thrawn to retake Scrim Island but the Admiral insisted on studying the situation further. As a result, the mission was assigned to Admiral [[Durril]], who launched a hasty attack that resulted in his [[One Oh Third Task Force]] sustaining considerable damage. Thrawn and [[Commander]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] managed to collect intelligence on the Scrim Island defenses and Nightswan.<ref name="Thrawn" />

Using this information, Thrawn's [[96th Task Force|Ninety-Sixth Task Force]] overwhelmed the insurgent defenses on Scrim Island, forcing their surrender. However, eight insurgent [[Starship|ships]] managed to flee to the main continent. Insurgent forces regrouped at Creekpath complex where they took control of the mine with the help of sympathetic locals. Vanto and Thrawn discovered that the rebels had regrouped at Creekpath and imposed a [[Siege of the Creekpath Mining and Refining complex|siege]] of the complex. Thrawn also used three damaged [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|Imperial light cruisers]] to set a trap for Nightswan's insurgent [[fleet]] in [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Thrawn" />

The [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Colonel]] [[Wullf Yularen]] devised a plan to disable the insurgent's deflector shield by sending [[Agent]] [[Gudry]] to blow it up, enabling Thrawn's forces to attack the complex. In an attempt to avoid heavy civilian casualties, Thrawn secretly met with Nightswan and invited him to join the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] in return for peacefully surrendering his forces. While respecting Thrawn, Nightswan declined his offer since he did not trust Restos to show clemency to his followers. Shortly later, insurgent forces tried to attack the [[orbit]]ing Imperial fleet but were ambushed by three [[squadron]]s of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] hidden within repair barges. Despite Thrawn's efforts to minimize civilian casualties, all the insurgents and many civilians were [[Death|killed]] when Pryce detonated Gudry's explosives in an effort to conceal his murder. Pryce had murdered Gudry after he threatened her parents.<ref name="Thrawn" />

The Creekpath mine explosion brought an end to the [[Batonn sector]] insurgency.<ref name="Thrawn" /> Despite the high civilian casualties, the Empire regarded the events on Batonn as a victory since it eliminated the remaining rebel forces in the sector.<ref name="Steps" /> The [[Imperial Senate]] launched an inquiry into the bombing but found little evidence. However, Thrawn along with Yularen and Vanto privately suspected that Pryce was complicit in the bombing despite her professing innocence.<ref name="Thrawn" /> Following the pacification of the [[Batonn insurgency]], Thrawn was promoted to the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[Grand Admiral]] and given command of the elite [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]].<ref name="Steps" />
