The '''Da-Vlunn system''' was situated in the [[Hutt Space]] portion of<ref name="AcolyteVD" /> [[the Slice]]<ref name="The Slice" /> in grid square R-11 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[hyperspace route]] known as the [[Ootmian Pabol]]<ref name="OotmianPabol" /> connected it to the [[Kwenn system|Kwenn]] and [[Y'Toub system]]s.<ref name="AcolyteVD" /> The Da-Vlunn [[Star system|system]] was home to<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Da-Vlunnian]]<ref name="THR 1" /> [[species]].<ref name="THR 1" /><ref name="Facebook">{{Facebook|username=Jake-Lunt-Davies-work-100056465597821|url=permalink.php?story_fbid=pfbid02G39gsRDyRTPXdoYMv1voBfPUeX7VuevnBbKcq37TPeJaQZ561UJmtUaPmC4Tjg8Gl&id=100056465597821|name=[[Jake Lunt Davies]]|date=2023-11-10|quote=Old concepts never die|archiveurl=https://archive.is/vIOKV}}</ref>
