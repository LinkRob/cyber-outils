'''Bilzen''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Bilzen system]] of [[Wild Space]] that featured [[mountain]]s and at least one frozen [[cave]]rn, which contained giant icicles. In [[3 BBY]], [[Jedi]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] traveled there with his [[Padawan|apprentice]] [[Ezra Bridger]] in their [[shuttle]], the ''[[Phantom]]'', to [[Jedi training|practice]] the latter's connection to [[the Force]]. However, their training was interrupted when the [[Seventh Sister]] and the [[Fifth Brother]], two [[Jedi hunter]]s serving in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Inquisitorius]], [[Duel on Bilzen|confronted]] them.

==Description==
Bilzen was terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Ice" /> located in the [[Bilzen system]]<ref name="System" /> of [[Wild Space]]. It was situated in grid square U-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The planet featured a Type I [[atmosphere]].<ref name="Atmosphere" /> Bilzen had at least one [[cave]]rn, filled with [[Temperature|ice]], in its [[mountain]]s.<ref name="Ice" />

==History==
In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{Twitter|username=tronprogram2|url=status/712746787216424960|name=[[Martin Fisher]]|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/nC3Uy|archivefile=File:MartinFisher-IceBreaking-Twitter.png}} places "[[Ice Breaking]]"&mdash;which features the [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] on [[Bilzen]]&mdash;between "[[The Future of the Force]]" and "[[Legacy (episode)|Legacy]]," both of which are placed in [[3 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore, the events of "Ice Breaking" must occur in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]], members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel cell]] the ''[[Spectres]]'', traveled to Bilzen to continue Bridger's [[Jedi training]].<ref name="Ice" />

==Locations==
At least one cavern resided among Bilzen's mountains.<ref name="Ice" />
