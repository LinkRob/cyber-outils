The '''Aldraig system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained [[Aldraig]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> It was situated at the coordinates M-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Aldraig system was mentioned in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].
