{{Quote|FN Twenty-one eighty-seven? You don't remember me. Nine twenty-six, from induct camp. Batch Eight. But I remember ''you''.|926, to Finn|<ref name="TLJ:EE">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>}}
'''Batch Eight''' was a large class of [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[cadet]]s, including [[Finn|FN-2187]] and [[926]]. During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the First Order and the [[Resistance]], 926 was stationed aboard [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]]'s [[flagship]], the ''[[Supremacy]]'', along with other graduates of Batch Eight. By then, however, [[FN Trooper Corps|FN]]-2187 had [[Treason|defected]] from the [[First Order military]].

==History==
{{Quote|I know I'm not supposed to initiate contact with officers, but look at you! Never took you for captain material. Batch Eight, heigh-ho!|926, to Finn|<ref name="TLJ:EE" />}}
[[File:First_Order_Stormtrooper_Corps.jpg|thumb|right|250px|It was common for stormtrooper squadmates to have trained together in large classes called batches, such as Batch Eight.]]
Batch Eight was a large class that consisted of [[First Order military]] [[cadet]]s who trained to become the next generation of [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]].<ref name="TLJ:TVD" /> The graduates of Batch Eight included [[trooper]]s [[926]]<ref name="TLJ:EE" /> and [[Finn|FN-2187]],<ref name="TLJ:TVD" /> a [[trooper]] who would serve in the [[FN Trooper Corps]].<ref name="Before the Awakening">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref> Both were noted for their marksmanship skills.<ref name="TLJ:JN">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> 926 went on to serve aboard the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]'', [[flagship]] of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]],<ref name="TLJ:EE" /> along with other Batch Eight alumni by the [[time]] of the [[First Order]]'s [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] with the [[Resistance]].<ref name="TLJ:TVD" /> Unknown to 926, however,<ref name="TLJ:EE" /> FN-2187 had [[Treason|deserted]] the [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|Stormtrooper Corps]], abandoning his designation and becoming known as "Finn" to members of the Resistance.<ref name="TLJ:TVD" />

During the First Order's [[Battle of Oetchi|assault]] on the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]], 926 encountered Finn&mdash;[[First Order military uniforms|disguised]] as an [[First Order officer|officer]]. Recognizing his former classmate, 926 reintroduced himself to the former stormtrooper. Although it was against protocol for [[soldier]]s to converse with [[officer]]s, 926 expressed pride at seeing a fellow member of Batch Eight attaining the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[captain]].<ref name="TLJ:EE" />
