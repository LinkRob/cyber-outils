The '''Crimson Dawn Sub-Headquarters'''<ref name="Vader 20">[[Darth Vader (2020) 20|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 20]]</ref> was a secret base of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate was situated within foothills on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] astronomical object [[Laecor]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. The [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] led a crew in an [[Attack on the Crimson Dawn Sub-Headquarters|attack]] on the base as part of his quest against Crimson Dawn. After locating and entering the base, Vader discovered Crimson Dawn records that indicated the extent of the syndicate's infiltration into Imperial bureaucracy.<ref name="Vader 19" />
