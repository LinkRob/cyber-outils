'''Blubs''' were a type of [[Ocean|marine]]<ref name="Geonosis" /> [[fish]]<ref name="TOM" /> [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[Water|ocean]] [[moon]] of [[Kef Bir]]. The members of [[Company 77]], a group of [[human]] [[First Order]] [[Desertion|deserters]] living on the moon, [[Fishing|fished]] for blubs and [[striped canth]]s as a [[Foodstuff|food]] source in raging seas<ref name="Geonosis" /> that teemed with shoals of both creatures. On a productive [[Standard day|day]] of fishing, the [[fisher]]s would fill the two [[sea skiff]]s they used with blub and striped canth<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and then bake them over [[fire]]s back on land.<ref name="Geonosis" /> Members of the company used what they [[Death|killed]] efficiently, harvesting not only meat but other materials as well.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blubs were first mentioned in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
