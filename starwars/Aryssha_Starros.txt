'''Aryssha Starros'''<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who was the [[Starros Clan|cousin]] of the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]] and daughter of the [[artist]]<ref name="Sana Starros 1" /> [[Mevera Starros]].<ref name="Sana Starros 2">{{Marvel|url=articles/comics/sana-starros-star-wars-explainer|text=Sana Starros: A Star Wars History}}</ref> At some point she [[Marriage|married]] the [[Imperial officer]] [[Captain]] [[Cerasus Ehllo]]. When Sana, who Aryssha referred to as "Sanisi," visited the Starros [[homeworld]] of [[Hosnian Prime]] on one occasion following a bad breakup<ref name="Sana Starros 1" /> between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SanaStarrosDate}}</ref> Aryssha was pregnant with [[twin]]s and was due to give [[Reproduction|birth]] any [[Standard day|day]].<ref name="Sana Starros 1" />
