'''''A Guide to the Star Wars Universe, Second Edition, Revised and Expanded''''' is a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book, following ''[[A Guide to the Star Wars Universe]]'' in [[1984]]. It was written by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and published by [[Del Rey]] in [[March]] [[1994]]. A [[A Guide to the Star Wars Universe, Third Edition, Revised and Expanded|third edition]] followed in [[2000]].

The book alphabetically details people, places, things, and events in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' universe. Most of the entries are quite short, the longest being a little over two pages. Each entry is divided into [[Canon|G-canon]] and [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]] information, and has sources cited. The second edition also included a brief timeline, and a comparison of sublight speeds in [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighters]].
