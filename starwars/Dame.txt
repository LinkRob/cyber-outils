'''Dame''' was a title used by members of the [[Unknown Regions]] [[Garwian Unity]] nation.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> The title [[Grand Dame]] was held by the [[Tarkin family]] member [[Veermol Tarkin]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 9|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 9]]</ref> By [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The flashbacks of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' take place before the novel's main events, which take place around [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Garwian]] [[artisan]] [[Mimott]] held the title.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
The title of Dame appeared in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> In the real world, a [[Wikipedia:Dame|dame]] is an honorific.<ref name="Dict">{{WebCite|url=https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/dame|text=Dame Definition & Meaning|work=[[Wikipedia:Merriam-Webster|Merriam-Webster]]|archivedate=20221010025747}}</ref>
