"'''''Death in the Undercity'''''" is an adventure for [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]] written by [[Michael Nystul]]. It was published by [[West End Games]] in [[June]] [[1990]]. It was republished in ''[[Classic Adventures: Volume Three]]''.

==Publisher's summary==
''In this adventure, a group of Rebel agents&mdash;the player characters&mdash;are sent to the planet Mon Calamari to investigate sabotage in the Quarren city of Morjanssik. Once they discover the truth behind the so-called "mining accidents", they must work quickly to prevent a civil war between the Quarren and the Mon Calamari.''

''The adventure also contains sample Quarren and Mon Calamari player character templates, and a short "Quarren Fact File" section discussing Quarren history and culture.''
