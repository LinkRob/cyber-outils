The '''Fleet Blaster''' was a modified [[heavy blaster pistol]] utilized by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Zaina]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Veteran Blaster|standard blaster]]. This [[blaster]] [[pistol]] resembled a [[DH-17 blaster pistol]] like those commonly utilized by some members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and [[New Republic]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fleet Blaster was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Zaina's Veteran Blaster, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Fleet Trooper]].<ref name="Hunters" />
