A '''fathier plushy''' was type of a stuffed [[doll]] that resembled a [[fathier]], a [[Sentience|non-sentient]] quadruped. Around [[33 BBY]], the [[human]] [[Roisem Barsha]] purchased two of the plushies from the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]] and gifted both to her [[Gender|son]], [[Jaylen Vrax|Jaylen Barsha]]. 

==Description==
Fathier plushies were stuffed [[doll]]s that resembled [[fathier]]s,<ref name="Low Red Moon" /> a [[species]] of [[Sentience|non-sentient]] quadrupeds<ref name="Bestiary">''[[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]]''</ref> that competed in [[Fathier racing|fathier races]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> The plushies came in at least two [[color]]s: black and brown.<ref name="Low Red Moon" />

==History==
{{Quote|Well, they had only two left, and I couldn’t give you two an equal amount. That’s not fair to the Barsha in either of you.|Roisem Barsha, to Jaylen Barsha|audio=TheyHadOnlyTwoLeft-LowRedMoon.flac|<ref name="Low Red Moon" />}}
[[File:Fathier_TLJUSC.png|thumb|right|300px|The plushy resembled a fathier (pictured).]]
The [[Canto Casino and Racetrack]] in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]]<ref name="Low Red Moon" />on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]] featured fathier racing<ref name="Outlaws" /> and had started to sell fathier plushies<ref name="Low Red Moon" /> by [[33 BBY]]. Around that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Low Red Moon Date">{{LRMDate|Prologue}}</ref> the [[human]] [[Youngling|child]] [[Sliro Barsha]] asked his [[Nnytyl Barsha|father's]] [[Gender|wife]], [[Roisem Barsha]], to bring him a fathier plushy from her Cantonica visit. The request angered Roisem because Sliro was not allowed to ask for gifts. While on Cantonica, Roisem purchased two of the plushies; however, when she returned home, she secretly gave both to [[Jaylen Vrax|Jaylen Barsha]]&mdash;Sliro's half-brother and her biological [[Barsha family|son]]&mdash;who named them [[Acacia]] and [[Riven]]. When Jaylen asked his [[Parent|mother]] why she did not get one for Sliro, she told him there had only been two left and that it would be unfair to give both brothers the same number, citing the family position that Sliro was just "half a Barsha." She told Jaylen not to show the plushies to Sliro, and Jaylen agreed.<ref name="Low Red Moon" />

Two [[Standard day|days]] later, Jaylen entered Sliro's room to check on him after hearing him crying. While the two were talking, Sliro mentioned asking for a fathier plushy and that Roisem had been angry about it. Sliro's reaction caused Jaylen to consider giving one of the plushies to his half-brother to cheer him up. However, since Jaylen did not want to get in trouble and had already named both of the plushies, he decided to keep them and not mention them to Sliro.<ref name="Low Red Moon" /> Later, in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Low Red Moon Date" /> Jaylen thought about his time [[sleep]]ing with the fathier plushies as a child while preparing to become [[Chief Executive Officer]] of his family's company, [[Barsha Corporation]].<ref name="Low Red Moon" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fathier plushies appeared in the [[2026]] novel ''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon]]'', written by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]].<ref name="Low Red Moon" />
