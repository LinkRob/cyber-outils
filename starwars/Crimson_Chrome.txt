The '''Crimson Chrome''' was a bodily configuration owned by the [[droideka]] [[Team Slingshot droideka|member]] of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]] that she could swap for her standard [[Beloved Paintwork|frame]] during the the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. This configuration featured a [[Color|bright red]], dark red, and metallic gray coloration, with various striped markings, as well as glowing orange [[sensor]]s extending from their head, as well as glowing blue interiors of their [[Limb|arm]] [[cannon]]s.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Crimson Chrome was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.16.0 on [[March 13]], [[2023]] during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Chrome Racer]].<ref name="Hunters" />
