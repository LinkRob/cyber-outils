The '''Draay system''' was located in the [[Pelgrin sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="TrailingSectors" /> The [[star system]] was situated in grid square R-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Draay system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Draay system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via a mention in the StarWars.com [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> The first appearance of the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] [[Draay 2]]<ref name="KOTOR35">[[Knights of the Old Republic 35|''Knights of the Old Republic'' 35]]</ref>&mdash;and therefore of the Draay [[Star system/Legends|system]], which contained the moon<ref name="EAOC2">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Draay system/Legends|Draay system]]</ref>&mdash;was in the [[Knights of the Old Republic 35|thirty-fifth issue]] of the [[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic (comic series)|''Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series. The issue was written by [[John Jackson Miller]], illustrated by [[Brian Ching]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] on [[November 19]], [[2008]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54354/star_wars_knights_of_the_old_republic_2006_35|text=Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic (2006) #35}}</ref>
