'''Avrar''' was a [[human]] [[gambler]] and member of the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime [[syndicate]] in [[3 ABY]]. During which Avrar was also a contract for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] who provided him intellinance collected by the Dawn.

==Biography==
Avrar was a member of the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime [[syndicate]] located on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Kijimi]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> While on Kijimi, Avrar regularly played [[Kessel Sabacc]] with two fellow [[human]]s named [[Ju'syl]] and [[Torai]] at a [[Crimson Dawn Cardroom|cardroom]] in the [[Crimson Dawn District (Kijimi)|Crimson Dawn District]] of [[Kijimi City]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

During the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> Avrar secretly provided Crimson Dawn intelligence to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]. In order to camouflage the handoff played a game of [[Sabacc]] with Avrar during which he lost [[Lando's lucky shift token|his lucky shift token]], which in reality was a disguised [[datacard]]. Calrissian later hired a [[gambler]] named [[Kay Vess]] to win back his [[Shift Token]] and bring it back to him. When Vess arrived at the cardroom on [[Kijimi]], she offered to play Avrar for the token. Avrar accepted but insisted that they play house rules. After Vess won the game, Avrar told Vess to inform Calrissian that he was always welcome at the table when he was willing to lose his [[cape]] before handing Lando's token along with three other shift tokens, [[Major Fraud]], [[Immunity]] and [[Exhaustion]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Personality and traits==
Avrar was a [[human]] with tan skin, [[Color|brown]] eyes, and black hair.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Equipment==
While playing Sabacc Avrar wore a red [[jacket]] over a grey [[clothing|shirt]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Avrar first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Ubisoft]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. They were part of "The High Roller" expert quest which was required for players to complete the ''Wild Card'' story expansion of the game. Avrar was also one of several sabacc players that players could choose to play against outside of any quest.<ref name="Outlaws" />
