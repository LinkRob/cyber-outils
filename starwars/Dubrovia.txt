'''Dubrovia''' was a terrestrial astronomical object located<ref name="Out of the Shadows" /> in the [[Dubrovia system]] within the [[Aldino sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[Western Reaches]]<ref name="Western Reaches" /> that was not far from<ref name="Out of the Shadows" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]]<ref name="Berenge sector">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> [[Berenge sector]].<ref name="Out of the Shadows" /> It was situated in grid square J-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], Dubrovia was the site of a [[Temple on Dubrovia|temple]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Out of the Shadows" />

In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]] was present at the [[Jedi temple|temple]] until being summoned by Jedi Master [[Stellan Gios]] to the Berenge [[sector]] to [[Battle at Gravity's Heart|battle]] the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s at the [[space station]] [[Gravity's Heart]]. During the battle, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] overheard a conversation on her [[comlink]] which mentioned Mann's summoning from Dubrovia, and that he had brought [[Republic Defense Coalition]] peacekeepers with him that had been in the area. Rwoh then relayed the conversation to a group aboard the station including [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Reath Silas]].<ref name="Out of the Shadows" />

==Behind the scenes==
