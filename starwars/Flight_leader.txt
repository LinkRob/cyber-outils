'''Flight leader''' or '''Flight Leader''' was the term given to the leader of a group of [[starfighter]]s.

==History==
{{Quote|Governor Pryce, we found the flight leader's ship in the Eastern district, but there's no sign of the pilot or astromech.|A [[stormtrooper]] [[officer]], to [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]] after the attack on Lothal|<ref name="Rebel Assault" />}}
[[Ubbel]] served as Flight Leader of [[Onyx Squadron]] under the [[callsign]] [[Onyx Leader]].<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

During the [[attack on Lothal]], the [[Twi'lek]] [[Hera Syndulla]] was the flight leader of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Alliance's]] [[Alliance Military|forces]] that intended to attack the [[Lothal City fuel depot|fuel depot]] in [[Capital City]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]].<ref name="Rebel Assault" />

During the [[Battle of Mako-Ta]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Luke Skywalker]] served as the flight leader of the newly formed [[Rogue Squadron]].<ref name="SW 52" />

During the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Maus Monare]] served as the flight leader of [[Sapphire Squadron]].<ref name="Levers of Power" />

Rebel [[Lieutenant]] [[Shara Bey]] served as flight leader of a group of [[N-1 starfighter]]s (consisting of herself, [[Prince]]ss [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Queen]] [[Sosha Soruna]]) during the [[Battle of Theed]], a battle that was part of [[Operation: Cinder]].<ref name="SE 3" />
