'''''Art of Coloring: Rogue One: A Star Wars Story''''' is a 128-page adult coloring book based on the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. It was published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[December 16]], [[2016]] as part of Disney's "Art of Coloring" series.<ref name="art">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=?s=Art+of+Coloring|text=Art of Coloring}}</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
''A coloring book for young adults and adults that includes scenes and characters from ''Rogue One''''.<ref name="Amazon" />
