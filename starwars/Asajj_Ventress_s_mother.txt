The '''mother''' of [[Asajj Ventress]] was a [[Gender|female]] [[Dathomirian]] [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] who lived on the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]] some [[time]] before the [[Invasion of Naboo]].

==Biography==
A [[Dathomirian]] [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] from [[Dathomir]], [[Asajj Ventress]]'s [[Parent|mother]] was a member of the Nightsisters clan led by [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]]. In [[50 BBY]],<ref name="Asajj Ventress">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'' dates the [[Reproduction|birth]] of Asajj Ventress on [[Dathomir]] to 50 [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|IV}}, which corresponds to [[50 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> she gave [[Reproduction|birth]] to a daughter, Asajj Ventress; however, she was forced to give her up to the [[Siniteen]] criminal [[Hal'Sted]] to protect the clan, something which was painful for both her and [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]], the clan's leader. Her daughter eventually returned to the clan after growing up and became a Nightsister herself.<ref name="Nightsisters" />
