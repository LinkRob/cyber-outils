'''Baraan-Fa''' was a small, beautiful [[planet]] in the [[Grumani sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that was known for its trade and [[farm]]ing, inhabited by members of the [[human]] [[species]]. It was the [[homeworld]] of [[Sari Nadle]], a [[cadet]] in the [[Resistance]]'s [[J-Squadron]].

==Description==
Baraan-Fa was a small<ref name="Attack on Starkiller Base" /> terrestrial<ref name="Storms of Crait" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Attack on Starkiller Base" /> located in the [[Baraan-Fa system]] in the [[Grumani sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was covered in [[forest]]s and [[grass]]y terrain.<ref name="Storms of Crait" /> It was mostly known for its trade and [[farm]]ing. It had an [[atmosphere]] breathable to [[oxygen]]-[[Oxygen breather|breathers]], such as [[human]]s, and was inhabited by several creatures, including [[kollowarie]]s, [[shonserra]]s, [[spollow]]s, [[tench dove]]s, and [[titterling]]s.<ref name="Attack on Starkiller Base" />

==History==
{{Quote|Rendezvous Point C.''"<br />"''Baraan-Fa?''"<br />"''If you have a better idea, we can discuss it from the safety of Baraan-Fa.|Leia Organa and Mon Mothma|<ref name="Storms of Crait" />}}
Sometime after the [[Battle of Yavin]], Baraan-Fa was used as a [[Rendezvous Point C|rendezvous point]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] after their [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] was [[Attack on the Rebel fleet|attacked]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]. Arriving on Baraan-Fa, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] discussed the establishment of a more permanent base. Organa suggested the planet [[Crait]], leading a [[Mission to Crait|mission]] to survey it.<ref name="Storms of Crait" />

==Inhabitants==
Baraan-Fa was [[Homeworld|home]] to a population of [[human]]s, who were often of a smaller size than usual. The [[Resistance]] [[cadet]] [[Sari Nadle]] was one such human from Baraan-Fa.<ref name="Attack on Starkiller Base" />

==Locations==
The Alliance to Restore the Republic operated, Rendezvous Point C, a temporary base, on Baraan-Fa.<ref name="Storms of Crait" /> [[The Bazaar (Baraan-Fa)|The Bazaar]] was also located on the planet.<ref name="Attack on Starkiller Base" />
