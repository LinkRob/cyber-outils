'''Colin Purves''' is a puppeteer who worked on ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''. He provided directions to fellow puppeteer [[Brian Herring]] while the latter was on set in costume as [[Hirang Birren]], since the costume severely limited Herring's vision.<ref name="Creatures" />
