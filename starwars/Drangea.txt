'''Drangea''' were large aquatic creatures that lived in the deepest, coldest parts of [[Mon Cala]]'s [[ocean]]. Described as being [[squid]]-like, it had two [[brain]]s, one in its crest and one that floated freely around its body. It had long, venomous tentacles that could attack [[Hunting|hunters]] even after its head was cut off. Its roe was considered a delicacy and very valuable. As a result, many people were [[Death|killed]] trying to hunt it, and were fed upon by the headless drangeas.<ref name="TPATS" />
