'''2% Bantha Milk''' was a type of [[blue milk]] from [[bantha]]s. It came in a [[Color|blue]] carton that featured a bantha.<ref name="Blue" /> [[Bantha Burgers]] served 2% Bantha Milk which came from Tatooine Farms and was included in the five [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]] [[Bantha Meal]]. Bantha Burgers' milk came in its own unique carton that featured the eating establishment's name.<ref name="Meal">{{SWCT|set=Galactic Feast|cardname=Try Our New Batha Meal|nolink=1}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
2% Bantha Milk featured in the [[2026]] [[Star Wars: Card Trader]] set ''Galactic Feast''.<ref name="Blue" />
