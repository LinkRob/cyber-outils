'''Charn Roondha''' was an [[Ithorian]] who [[Jabba's criminal empire|worked]] for [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] as a collector of taxes and protection [[Currency|money]] from a variety of [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|worlds]].<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Gran]] [[Ree-Yees]] sought to take over his role. During his [[time]] serving as the [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace's]] [[Accountant|bookkeeper]], the [[Twi'lek]] [[Silvan Kaan]] [[Plot to assassinate Jabba Desilijic Tiure|had]] the [[translator droid]] [[80-M|Eightyem]] inform Roondha that he had lost the faith of Jabba, as the amount of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] the Ithorian had been bringing in of late had fallen off. This caused Roondha to confront Yees, but Jabba ordered him to spare the Gran. Afterwards, Kaan again asked Eightyem to tell Roondha of a supposed rumor that Jabba was to take away Roondha's actions on [[Mandalore]] and give them to Yees. In an attempt to stop this from happening, Roondha suggested removing Jabba from his [[throne]]. Eightyem and Kaan then came up with the idea of the [[droid]] acquiring a [[WESTAR-35 blaster pistol]] from the palace [[armory]], as only those in Jabba's favor or his guards could have weapons in the palace, and Roondha was neither, so that the Ithorian could kill Jabba without having to get too close.<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1" />

After Eightyem gave the weapon to Kaan, he provided it to Roondha, who then attempted to kill Jabba, but it was thwarted by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Bane Malar]]. Roondha was later brought before Jabba, and informed he would be killed swiftly if he gave up the name of who had provided him the [[blaster]]. Roondha responded in [[Ithorese]] that it was Kaan, but Eightyem mistranslated it purposely to say that Roondha had said he would rather die horribly than betray a confidence. The Ithorian then pointed out it was Eightyem, but again the droid lied about what he had said, and informed Jabba that he had said something unkind about the [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure's mother|Hutt's mother]]. This enraged Jabba, who had Roondha fed to the [[sarlacc]] at the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]].<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1" />
