A '''detox team''' was a medical unit called in to handle patients and victims of diseases and contagions. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[Reginard Base|base]] on [[Reginard]] employed a detox team when one their personnel was seemingly killed by contracting the deadly [[triflexia]] disease.
