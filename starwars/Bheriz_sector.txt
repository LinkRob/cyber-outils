The '''Bheriz sector''' was a [[sector]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] of [[the galaxy]]. It contained the [[Eadu system]], which was home to the [[mountain]]ous [[planet]] [[Eadu]]<ref name="RO World Locations" /> and the [[Eadu Energy Conversion Laboratory]], where [[Galen Walton Erso]] directed a [[Kyber crystal research team|team]] of [[scientist]]s studying the energy potential of [[Kyber crystal]] for the [[Tarkin Initiative]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] the [[Cloud-Riders]], a [[swoop]] [[Swoop gang|gang]] led by [[Enfys Nest]], originated in the Bheriz sector.<ref name="Star Wars: Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>
