'''Aga''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who served in the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="TTMH" /> 

== History ==
During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Galactic Empire]] established the listening post on Konkiv. Commanded by an [[Unidentified Imperial admiral (Konkiv)|admiral]], the base became a backwater assignment for the Imperials, with some considering it a punishment to be assigned there. Eventually, the Imperial command forgot about the base and by [[2 BBY]],<ref>{{Facebook|username=starwarsupcomingbooks|url=starwarsupcomingbooks/photos/a.105425279572504/1326199037495116|name=Star Wars Upcoming Books & Comics|quote=Out today (June 7, 2017) in Germany: STAR WARS REBELS MAGAZIN # 32 German magazine, featuring an original 12-page Star Wars Rebels comic According to a private message from Jeremy Barlow, this story takes place between Season Three's "An Inside Man" and "Voices and Visions.|archivefile=File:ThyMetalPlacement.png}} places "To Thy Metal Heart Be True" between "[[An Inside Man]]" and "[[Visions and Voices]]," both of which are dated to [[2 BBY]] in ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the only contact they had with the greater Empire was an automated supply drop once a [[cycle]]. The garrison chose to desert, using the base to establish their own settlement outside the bureaucracies of the Empire. Aga was a part of the garrison and taught the children on the base.<ref name="TTMH" />

At some point during 2 BBY, the settlement was [[Mission to Konkiv|encountered]] by the rebels [[Sabine Wren]], [[Garazeb Orrelios]], and the [[Droid|droids]] [[C1-10P]] ("Chopper") and [[AP-5]], who had come to the base believing it to be abandoned.  The rebels encountered the [[Unidentified DT-series sentry droid|rogue]] [[DT-series sentry droid]] on their arrival at the base and encountered the garrison upon their entry. AP-5 informed the admiral commanding the base that the droid had entered the base. While bringing the rebels to the command center, the admiral popped into Aga's classroom to tell her to close and lock the door, fearing the droid.<ref name="TTMH" />

== Traits and personality ==
Aga was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] with white hair, blue eyes and light skin color. She wore a standard [[Imperial officer's uniform]].<ref name="TTMH" />

== Behind the scenes ==
Aga made her first appearance in the "[[To Thy Metal Heart Be True]]", a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] found in the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 32|thirty-second issue]] of the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]] released on [[June 7]], [[2017]].<ref name="TTMH" />
