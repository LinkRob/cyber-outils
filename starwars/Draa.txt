'''Draa''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served in the [[41st Elite Corps]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. He took part in the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], under the command of [[Jedi Master]] [[Luminara Unduli]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Gree (clone trooper)|Gree]], after the native [[Geonosian]]s broke free from the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] control that had been established on [[Geonosis]] since the [[First Battle of Geonosis|beginning]] of the Clone Wars. Draa took part in the attack on a [[Geonosis primary droid foundry|primary droid foundry]] and, along with most of the Republic [[Grand Army of the Republic|forces]] there, distracted the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Separatist Droid Army|droids]] as two [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]s infiltrated the foundry to destroy the main [[reactor]]. Although Draa and the troops faced heavy opposition from enemy [[battle droid]]s and [[ray shield]]ed [[Separatist super-tank prototype|super tank]]s, the foundry was destroyed and the Republic later won the battle.

==Biography==
[[File:Draa-Dooku_boot.png|thumb|left|210px|Draa exits ''[[Dooku Boot]]'' along with his Jedi commanders and fellow troopers.]]
Draa was the taken name of a [[clone trooper]]<ref name="EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/weapons-factory-episode-guide|text="Weapons Factory" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> [[Reproduction|made]] from the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]]<ref name="Clone troopers" /> who served the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]. Draa was part of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s [[41st Elite Corps]], serving under [[Jedi General]] [[Luminara Unduli]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Barriss Offee]], and [[Clone Commander]] [[Gree (clone trooper)|Gree]]. Early in the [[Clone Wars]], Draa and his fellow [[soldier]]s participated in the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]]. After the initial assault by [[Jedi]] [[General]]s [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and their forces destroyed the [[ray shield]] generator, Draa and the 41st Elite Corps were dispatched to aid General Skywalker and the [[501st Legion]] in destroying the [[Geonosis primary droid foundry|primary droid foundry]]. Draa accompanied [[Buzz]], Commander Offee, Commander Gree, General Unduli, and other clone troopers in an [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunship]].<ref name="WF" />

==Personality and traits==
As a [[Cloning|clone]] of [[Jango Fett]], Draa stood 1.83 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="Clone troopers" /> Draa was an eager trooper, and when called forward by [[Clone Captain]] Rex to destroy the [[droideka]]s, he readily obeyed.<ref name="WF" /> Draa had a symbol of a [[Fire|flaming]] [[asteroid]] on his [[helmet]].<ref name="HC 60">{{HelmetCollectionCite|60|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref>

==Skills and abilities==
Draa was an expert shot with his [[RPS-6 rocket launcher]].<ref name="HC 60" />

==Equipment==
Draa utilized a RPS-6 rocket launcher, as well as a [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] and [[DC-15A blaster rifle]]. Draa was equipped with standard [[phase I clone trooper armor]] that was personalized in the [[Color|green]] markings of the 41st Elite Corps. and an stylized [[asteroid]] on his helmet. He also made use of a [[backpack]].<ref name="WF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Draa debuted in "[[Weapons Factory]]," the sixth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the animated TV series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="WF" /> although Draa was identified in the episode's accompanying episode guide on [[StarWars.com]]. According to the episode guide, the script had Draa state the line, "Buckle your belts and check your cells, soldiers. We're going in." Though the line was actually spoken by "[[Buzz]]" in the episode.<ref name="EG" />
