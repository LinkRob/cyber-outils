A [[Gender|male]] individual was the '''nephew''' of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Admiral]] [[Firmus Piett]].<ref name="Lord Vader" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="FACPOV date">The events of "[[Lord Vader Will See You Now]]" occur parallel to the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> they were set to receive command of the<ref name="Lord Vader" /> [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> [[Vigilance (Imperial-class)|''Vigilance'']] until [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] instead gave command of the [[Starship|vessel]] to [[Commodore]] [[Rae Sloane]] for her discovery that the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' had hidden inside an [[Sy-O|exogorth]] within the [[Hoth asteroid field|Hoth asteroid belt]].<ref name="Lord Vader" /> Piett's nephew was aboard the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] in [[4 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> when Lord Vader arrived to oversee the construction of the Death Star.<ref name="rotj" />

==Behind the scenes==
In canon, Piett's nephew was first mentioned in the short story "[[Lord Vader Will See You Now]]."<ref name="Lord Vader" /> The concept of [[Firmus Piett/Legends|Firmus Piett]] having a nephew can be dated back to [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]], where the "[[Death Star II Limited]]" card game set identified an [[Imperial officer]] aboard the [[Death Star II/Legends|Death Star II]] as [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Sarkli]], Piett's nephew.<ref name="SWCCG">{{CCG|set=Death Star II Limited|cardname=Captain Sarkli}}</ref>
