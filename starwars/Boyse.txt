'''Boyse''' was a [[moff]] within the [[Galactic Empire]] during its [[Imperial Era|reign]]. Moff Boyse [[Commanding officer|commanded]] a [[Scipio blockade|blockade]] of the [[planet]] [[Scipio]] from his [[flagship]], the ''[[Exchequer]]''. The [[blockade]] consisted of [[Star Destroyer]]s, and&mdash;along with [[Emperor's adviser|Imperial advisors]]&mdash;ensured that the [[Core Five]],<ref name="Endless" /> the leading council of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]],<ref name="Clu Lesser">{{Databank|clu-lesser|Clu Lesser}}</ref> would not allocate or loan [[Currency|funds]] to those who were not completely loyal to the Empire.<ref name="Endless" />

==Behind the scenes==
Moff Boyse was mentioned in ''[[Endless Vigil]]'', a [[2016]] sourcebook for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[roleplaying game]].<ref name="Endless" />
