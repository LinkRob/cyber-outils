The '''Chiss patrol ship'''<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> was utilized by the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. Several of these [[Starship|ships]] participated in a exercise at the [[Taharim Academy]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> The Ascendancy's [[Ruling Families|Ruling]] and [[Great Families]] also used Chiss patrol ships, all with the same basic [[hull]]. Each family then added their crest and family design ornamentation.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> The [[Xodlak family]] used the ships as part of their [[Xodlak fleet|planetary defense fleet]].<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref>
