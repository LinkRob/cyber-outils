'''Binz Scoty''' was a [[scavenger]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]] who [[Death|died]] due to the world's [[Temperature|heat]].<ref name="RSG" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Death" /> Scoty, [[Turgot Mynes]], and [[Paos Adina]] were all included on a list of scavengers who had messed up and died due to heat written by another scavenger named [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] in a [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]] where<ref name="RSG" /> she<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> vowed to not meet the same fate.<ref name="RSG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Binz Scoty was mentioned in the replica journal ''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'', which was written by [[Jason Fry]] and released in [[2015]].<ref name="RSG" />
