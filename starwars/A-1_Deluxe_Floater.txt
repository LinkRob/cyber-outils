The '''A-1 Deluxe Floater''' was a capsule-shaped [[landspeeder]] featuring a pod-like [[cockpit]].<ref name="YouTube" /> During the [[secret mission to Tatooine]]<ref name="A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> one such vehicle was parked outside [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in [[Mos Eisley]], [[Tatooine]], as [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] were about to enter the premises.<ref name="A New Hope" />
