The '''dedication of the Starlight Beacon''' was an event that launched the [[Starlight Beacon]], the culmination of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]]'s [[Great Works]]. Starlight Beacon was part of Soh's efforts to expand the [[Galactic Republic]]'s presence in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The ceremony was delayed by several developments including the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] and the resulting [[Emergences]], which were instigated by the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> The ceremony was almost foiled by the pirate prince [[Cyrus Vuundir]], who stole the [[dedication plaque]].<ref name="The Prince and the Pirate">{{YJA|The Prince and the Pirate}}</ref> Despite these obstacles, the ceremony proceeded as planned, with senior [[Jedi]] and Republic figures making speeches.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|A momentous day, this is. This plaque will remind us of the day when Starlight Beacon united the galaxy in peace and hope.|Yoda, on the dedication plaque and its meaning|<ref name="The Prince and the Pirate" />}}
Designed to stand as a symbol of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s promise of peace and hope in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], the [[space station]] heralded everything that Soh wanted her [[Politics|administration]] to stand for. The dedication was almost postponed after the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] and its subsequent [[Emergences]], a series of [[hyperspace]] catastrophes caused by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] that forced the [[Chancellor]] to close [[Hyperspace route|travel lanes]] in the Outer Rim. Starlight Beacon was informally opened to the public at this [[time]] in order to treat victims of the tragedies with its medical facilities. After an intervention from a [[Emergences task force|joint task force]] of [[Republic Defense Coalition]] and [[Jedi]] forces, however, the formal dedication went on as planned<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[232 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" />

[[File:DedicationPlaque-YJAS1-TPATP.png|thumb|left|150px|The Starlight Beacon's dedication plaque.]]
Prior to the ceremony Prince [[Cyrus Vuundir]], who was also secretly the [[pirate]] [[Cyrus Vuundir|Taborr Val Dorn]], attempted to steal Starlight Beacon's [[dedication plaque]]. He tricked the [[Jedi]] [[youngling]]s [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]] and [[Nubs]] into giving him a tour of the facility. After discovering Taborr's presence, the three Jedi pursued him through the station. Following a climatic confrontation, Taborr managed to escape capture by threatening to harm Vuundir, whom he claimed [[Taborr's gang|his gang]] had taken hostage. Due to this and previous encounters with other Jedi, Vuundir gained a better appreciation of his Jedi adversaries and began to question his double life.<ref name="The Prince and the Pirate" />

==The ceremony==
{{Quote|Whenever you feel alone... Whenever darkness closes in...<br />Hear our signal and know that the Force is with you. That we are with you.<br />This is our promise. This is our covenant.|Jedi Master Avar Kriss|<ref name="High Republic 1" />}}
[[File:DedicationSB-YJA-PrincePirate.png|thumb|right|250px|The Jedi ignite their lightsabers during Starlight Beacon's dedication ceremony.]]
The ceremony was held in Starlight Beacon's domed great hall, and was attended by delegates from across [[the galaxy]], including many members of the [[Jedi Order]]. Speeches were given by the [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]],<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /><ref name="The Prince and the Pirate" /> [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]],<ref name="The Prince and the Pirate" /> [[Queen]] [[Consort]] [[Thandeka]] of the nearby [[planet]] [[Eiram]],<ref name="Into the Dark" /> and [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]], who had been named [[marshal]] of the station by Grand Masters [[Pra-Tre Veter]] and Yoda. The historic dedication culminated with every Jedi raising their [[lightsaber]]s to the [[star]]s, symbolically representing the light that the Beacon would spread across the Republic. The Jedi raised their voices together in a declaration of their purpose and what they serve light and life.<ref name="High Republic 1" /> 

==Aftermath==
Following the ceremony, newly elevated Jedi Master [[Elzar Mann]] was struck with a haunting [[Force vision]] that saw the [[death]]s of himself and his Jedi comrades. Alone, Mann's thoughts on that [[Standard day|day]] went from beauty and hope to that of terror and fear.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> Meanwhile, elsewhere on the beacon, Jedi Master [[Sskeer]] began to break down, succumbing to the turbulent events that led up to the Beacon's dedication.<ref name="High Republic 1" />
