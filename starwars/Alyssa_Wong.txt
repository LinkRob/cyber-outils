'''Alyssa Wong''' is the writer of the [[2020]] [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']], featuring [[Chelli Lona Aphra]]. They also wrote the one-shot [[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]], for which they created the [[Ubese]] [[bounty hunter]]s [[Brene]], [[Ilini]], [[Hareck]], and [[Tans]] with artist [[David Baldeón]].

==Biography==
===Writing ''Aphra'' and more===
{{Quote|Deeply satisfying, as one queer Asian disaster, to write another queer Asian disaster|Alyssa Wong, on Chelli Lona Aphra|<ref name="Tweet">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1344375299706818560|Alyssa Wong|quote=Deeply satisfying, as one queer Asian disaster, to write another queer Asian disaster|archivedate=20201231090553}}</ref>}}
Alyssa Wong is an author who wrote the [[2020]] [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']], the second series headlined by the character [[Chelli Lona Aphra]].<ref name="Aphra Announcement" /> Wong is [[W:c:lgbtqia:Non-binary|non-binary]] and [[W:c:lgbtqia:Genderfluid|gender fluid]].<ref name="It's a Big Galaxy">{{SW|url=news/kieron-gillen-alyssa-wong-interview|text="It's a Big Galaxy": Kieron Gillen and Alyssa Wong Discuss Doctor Aphra and More for Pride Month}}</ref> As described by Wong, both Aphra and Wong are "[[W:c:lgbtqia:Queer|queer]] Asian disaster[s]," which adds to the satisfaction of writing the character.<ref name="Tweet" /> Wong told [[StarWars.com]], "I think Aphra is a delight. She's out for herself, for fun and profit, and that drives her actions more than any internal moral compass. It makes her really fun to write!" They also enjoy how Aphra's creator [[Kieron Gillen]] gave her emotional complexity, and how prior writer [[Simon Spurrier|Si Spurrier]] made her face the consequences of her actions.<ref name="Aphra Announcement" />

Wong attended weekly meetings via [[Wikipedia:Zoom Video Communications|Zoom Video Communications]] with fellow writers, including ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]'' writer [[Ethan Sacks]], and [[Marvel Comics]] editors.<ref name="Sacks Zoom">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1729278120279015514|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=One of the joys of my Star Wars career has been the weekly zooms with fellow Marvel writers & editors. Listening to Alyssa reveal the diabolical & delightful mischief they planned to inflict on Aphra & friends (those plot twists!) was joy. Perfect marriage of talent & character.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/5Wdlp}}</ref> The meetings were a highlight of Wong's time on ''Aphra'' for them<ref name="Zoom Wong">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1729326501550501930|Alyssa Wong|quote=You're so sweet. Those were highlights for me too!|archivedate=20231216144826}} {{C|In response to "One of the joys of my Star Wars career has been the weekly zooms with fellow Marvel writers & editors. Listening to Alyssa reveal the diabolical & delightful mischief they planned to inflict on Aphra & friends (those plot twists!) was joy. Perfect marriage of talent & character."}}</ref> and Sacks, who enjoyed learning of Wong's plans for the ''Aphra'' cast.<ref name="Sacks Zoom" /> ''Aphra'' was Wong's first time writing an ongoing comic series and, thus, was a major learning experience for the writer. With all the time they spent writing on ''Aphra'', Wong would also reflect that the "little dirtbag [[archaeologist]]" managed to "worm her way" into their heart.<ref name="Wong Aphra Family">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1752736424913166720|[[Alyssa Wong]]|quote=A letter to my DOCTOR APHRA family 🖤🏳️‍🌈🏳️‍⚧️ tldr - I appreciate you more than I can truly say.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/LBfw6}}</ref>

For the [[2021]] ''[[Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters]]'' comic book crossover event, Wong wrote the one-shot issue [[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]] with art by [[David Baldeón]]. The issue featured the [[Ubese]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Boushh]] and a [[Boushh's crew|crew]] of new characters: [[Brene]], [[Ilini]], [[Hareck]], and [[Tans]].<ref name="Boushh">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]]</ref> After the release of the issue, Wong confirmed and shared further details from their script notes via [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]], including the genders of each character and observations about their personalities.<ref name="Twitter - Boushh's crew">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1438218186638311425|Alyssa Wong|archivedate=20210916134142}}</ref>

[[File:AlysOngwa-THRAAnnual2021.png|thumb|right|200px|The character Alys "Crash" Ongwa is a tribute to Alyssa Wong.]]
The character [[Alys Ongwa]], nicknamed "Crash," was created by [[Daniel José Older]] for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' publishing initiative. Ongwa debuted in the comic story "[[Crash and the Crew Do What They Do]]" in the [[The High Republic Adventures Annual 2021|''The High Republic Adventures Annual'' 2021]]<ref name="THRA Annual 2021">{{StoryCite|story=Crash and the Crew Do What They Do|book=The High Republic Adventures Annual 2021|bformatted=''The High Republic Adventures Annual'' 2021}}</ref> before featuring as a major character in Older's [[2022]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''.<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref> Since then, "Crash" has appeared in further ''The High Republic'' works.<ref name="Rogue Element">{{StoryCite|story=Rogue Element|book=The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life}}</ref><ref name="Crash Landing">''[[The High Republic Adventures: Crash Landing]]''</ref> Older created the character as a tribute to Wong and [[Tuckerization|tuckerized]] their name; "crashwong" is Wong's screenname.<ref name="Twitter Tribute">{{Twitter|djolder|status/1461084533806276612|[[Daniel José Older]]|quote=My tribute to @crashwong is ALIVE AND IN THE WORLD! amazing concept art by @jesselonergans|archivedate=20211126032741}}</ref> Ongwa's occupation and role within the ''Star Wars'' universe were based upon Wong's hypothetical answer of who they would be in the ''Star Wars'' universe: "the stressed out chaotic manager of an assassin squad (or something like that)."<ref name="Twitter Role">{{Twitter|djolder|status/1461392098016509956|[[Daniel José Older]]|quote=in a round about sort of way I asked @crashwong if [they were] in SW what role would [they] play and [they] said [they]'d be the stressed out chaotic manager of an assassin squad (or something like that). Switched assassin to bodyguard and thus: Crash. Art by @jesselonergans|archivedate=20211126032741}}</ref> Wong later had a chance to write "Crash" with their [[2023]] short story "[[Rogue Element]],"<ref name="Rogue Element" /> their ''The High Republic'' debut.<ref name="Five days">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1697395705835356510|[[Alyssa Wong]]|quote=Five days!!! So soon!!! Get you the Barnes & Noble edition if you can, because it's got my High Republic debut short story in it!!|archivedate=20230902201738}}</ref>

In [[June]] 2022, Wong and Gillen were interviewed by [[Bria LaVorgna]] for a [[W:c:lgbtqia:Pride|Pride Month]] feature on StarWars.com. The three discussed their experiences as queer people, the [[In-universe|real world]] compared to the ''Star Wars'' universe, and the Doctor Aphra comic books and character.<ref name="It's a Big Galaxy" />

===''Aphra''{{'s}} end, other projects===
Wong participated in several projects released in 2023 in celebration of the 40th anniversary of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi]]. Wong wrote the one-shot comic [[Return of the Jedi – Ewoks 1]]. Wong's short story "[[Wolf Trap]]" was published in the anthology book ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]'', continuing the story of [[scout trooper]] [[Hoyel]] from Wong's one-shot comic.<ref name="Hoyel">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1696511895337910317|[[Alyssa Wong]]|quote=Today!! FROM A CERTAIN POINT OF VIEW: RETURN OF THE JEDI is out! I've got a story called "Wolf Trap" about an unlucky scout trooper on Endor... this guy from "Peekpa's Story" in my + @Casparnova's Marvel EWOKS comic. Enjoy--and tell me what you think! #FromaCertainPOVReturns|archivedate=20230829184130}}</ref> They also made their aforementioned ''The High Republic'' writing debut in 2023 with "Rogue Element," which was released in the Barnes and Noble edition of ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]''.<ref name="Five days" /> Wong wrote the middle grade novel ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' with Daniel José Older<ref name="Escape from Valo">''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]''</ref> and was very pleased with the end result, even noting they found it hard to believe it was published and canon.<ref name="Wong Valo Reread">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1753269233721700521|[[Alyssa Wong]]|quote=I accidentally sped-reread all of ESCAPE FROM VALO last night while hunting for a specific passage, and it's really hammered home that this book is real. It's canon, and it's exactly what it is. We did it.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/P9zvF}}</ref>

The ''[[Star Wars: Dark Droids]]'' comic event gave Wong a chance to return to their horror roots.<ref name="Wong Dark Droids">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1717277067446788269|[[Alyssa Wong]]|quote=Horror is where the heart lives 😌💕 Dark Droids gave me the chance to go back to my horror roots and I am LIVING for it|archiveurl=https://archive.li/qCmH8}}</ref> Wong teased they were working on another High Republic project on [[November 2]], 2023.<ref name="Wong clouds">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1720123071808315483|[[Alyssa Wong]]|quote=Head in the clouds today, and by clouds I mean the high republic|archiveurl=https://archive.li/hRYLy}}</ref> On [[November 23]], Wong confirmed the 2020 run of ''Doctor Aphra'' would be ending with [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 40|issue 40]], much like the 2016 run, but promised future stories its fans would enjoy were in the works.<ref name="Wong ending">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1729250456730570846|[[Alyssa Wong]]|quote=Now that solicits have gone out, I can confirm: our current run of Doctor Aphra wraps with issue #40, just like the previous volume. But! Without spoiling the ending... keep an eye toward the future (and don't miss Revelations 2023)!|archiveurl=https://archive.is/tITSK}}</ref> They also thanked the readers of ''Aphra'' for the fandom they had formed around the comic<ref name="Wong the world">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1729251223008911642|[[Alyssa Wong]]|quote=Writing Doctor Aphra has been incredible; this comic means the world to me. And most importantly, all the love, enthusiasm, and excitement--the way you've built community with each other and responded to the story, making it your own--has changed my life.💜 See you soon for #39!|archiveurl=https://archive.is/7cEy1}}</ref> and their support.<ref name="Wong Thank You">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1747691047071654065|[[Alyssa Wong]]|quote=Doctor Aphra fanbase: thank you, all of you, for your support and love for our book and the terrible doctor. I'm so glad I get to write stories for you. 💜🏳️‍🌈🏳️‍⚧️|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/I4m5S}}</ref> After its release, Wong thanked their "''Doctor Aphra'' Family," including the comic's fans.<ref name="Wong Aphra Family" /> In particular, they thanked fans for their positive response to issue 40, along with the positive response to ''Escape from Valo''.<ref name="Wong Response">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1752934432594366975|[[Alyssa Wong]]|quote=I am overwhelmed with love and gratitude for the response to DOCTOR APHRA #40 and ESCAPE FROM VALO. So many people sharing their stories and experiences. So many celebrations and affirmations of each other. So much joy. I'll write more tomorrow, but for now: thank you. 🏳️‍🌈🏳️‍⚧️|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/PmsaZ}}</ref> ''Aphra'' would go onto win the [[Wikipedia:35th GLAAD Media Awards|2024 GLAAD award]] for Outstanding Comic Book, much to Wong's delight. In response, Wong thanked their team and the fans of ''Aphra''.<ref name="Wong GLAAD">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1770135534943240411|[[Alyssa Wong]]|quote=WE WON A GLAAD!!!!!!! 🏳️‍🌈🏳️‍⚧️✨✨ DOCTOR APHRA won the GLAAD Media Award for Outstanding Comic Book this year!! So proud of our team and the stories we got to tell. Thank you so much for all of your support!!!|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/Uky6i}}</ref>

On [[November 28]] of 2023, Wong teased they had a new ''Star Wars'' story in the works.<ref name="Wong dreaming">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1729504221374840832|[[Alyssa Wong]]|quote=Wrapping some non-Star Wars scripts today and spending the rest of my time dreaming about something new 👀|archiveurl=https://archive.is/I1qzf}}</ref> On [[April 10]], [[2024]], Wong teased that they had a chance to write a character they had never expected to be able to as part of their ''High Republic'' work.<ref name="Wong Character">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1778096321905283537|[[Alyssa Wong]]|quote=I'm writing a character I never thought I would, and I'm really stoked about it?! I'm writing High Republic comics!! 💖 I'm so delighted to take part in DISPATCHES FROM THE OCCLUSION ZONE with @djolder and Cavan! I'm thrilled to be writing issue #2  DISPATCHES FROM THE OCCLUSION ZONE! Featuring my sweet, scary boy Ruu, ever-clever Crash... and perhaps somebody quite unexpected! 👀💖 Cover by the inimitable @JR_Bartok!|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/YB1cy}}</ref> After rumors swirled that Wong would be joining the initiative's comic writers, it was confirmed with the announcement of ''Dispatches'' 2,<ref name="Wong Rumors" /> which Wong teased would feature an unexpected character.<ref name="Wong Dispatches" />
