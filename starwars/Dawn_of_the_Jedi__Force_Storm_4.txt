'''''Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm'' 4''' is the fourth issue in the [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm|''Force Storm'']] story arc of the ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series and the fourth issue of the series in total. It is set in the year [[25,793 BBY]], in a [[Pre-Republic era|time before]] the polarization of the [[Light side of the Force/Legends|light]] and [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark sides]] of [[The Force/Legends|the Force]]. This issue was released on [[May 16]], [[2012]].

==Publisher's summary==
''For millennia, the Je'daii Masters have protected the planet Tython and maintained a balance within the Force. But now a stranger from another world has come, destroying the balance and engulfing the world in a Force storm that threatens every inhabitant!''

''The ancient Je'daii Order stands on the brink of a powerful discovery. But will this knowledge bring wisdom...or peril? Three young Je'daii students may hold the fate of the world in their hands!''

==Plot summary==
''The Jad’aii masters discuss the force storm and crashed ship. During the discussion they receive a messenger droid with a report from Master Quan-Jang. He reports that the dark energy permeates from a lone survivor of the crash and he believes that the Journeyers are further ahead in pursuing this survivor. The masters decide to concentrate their efforts on meditation to help quell the force storm.

Meanwhile the journeyers are tested by the figurative darkness of the Abyss and begin infighting as a result. They resolve their differences and continue forward, but the same cannot be said for Xesh.

Xesh has a flashback dream of him killing his Predor Tul’Kar but he does not remember why. He wakes up, captured in a spider web. He makes short work of the spiders and escapes.

Quan-Jang contemplates the mistakes he made while training Daegen and pledges to not make the same with Shae. Rori Fenn and Hawk Ryo hint at a previous romantic fling.

Xesh battles wraiths while flashing back to his early force hound days. He participates in gladiator fights and has learned to always keep fighting and always be merciless. The journeyers step in and help Xesh when he gets in deep water with a rift worm. Shae is no longer able to activate the forcesaber. As the combined group falters in the fight Xesh asks for the forcesaber so he may vanquish the worm. Shae eventually relents and in an expected act of betrayal Xesh runs away from the fight, leaving the journeyers to fend for themselves.''
