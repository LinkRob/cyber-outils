An '''abandoned construction site''' was located in the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]'s [[Coruscant Underworld|Underworld]], the lowest levels of [[Imperial City|Galactic City]].<ref name="Inquisitor"/> Sometime between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]'' establishes that [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] trained at the construction site sometime in the over two [[Standard year|years]] between the [[Mission to Thule|missions to Thule]] and [[Mission to Olgothon 3|Olgothon 3]]. The former is established to have taken place concurrent to [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Kenobi and Skywalker)|mission to Cato Neimoidia]] and the latter prior to the execution of [[Order 66]], dated to [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]] respectively per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] discovered the construction site during one of her nightly runs across Coruscant. Having been grounded by the [[Jedi Council]] after a [[Mission to Thule|mission]] to the planet [[Thule]]<ref name="Inquisitor"/> in 22 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date"/> Akaris had begun conducting these excursions in secret in order to self-train, following advice given to her by her confidant, the [[droid]] [[Technician|tech]] [[Heezo]].<ref name="Inquisitor"/>

Being far from homes and businesses made the construction site an excellent place for her to practice telekinesis without fear of harming anyone or otherwise keeping her power in check. Akaris was thus able to stretch the limits of her [[Force power|Force abilities]], gaining strength and subtlety in the use of [[the Force]] in a more direct manner than her [[Sember Vey|dead master]] would have wished. After she constructed a [[Iskat's blade|second lightsaber]], Akaris also practiced her [[Jar'Kai|dual-wielding technique]] during sessions at the construction site.<ref name="Inquisitor" />
