An '''individual''' served as a [[sergeant]] in the [[Mirial's military|military]] of<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="Force Collector">''[[Force Collector]]''</ref> [[Mirial]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> By [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> they were the sergeant of the [[Mirialan]] [[Gender|woman]] [[Abediah Viess]], who served as a [[soldier]] in the military. When Viess [[Desertion|deserted]] Mirial's military, she [[Death|killed]] the sergeant. though she eventually [[Desertion|deserted]] the military.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />

During the [[Battle of Eriadu]]<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> in [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">{{THRContent228BBY}}</ref> Viess&mdash;by then serving as a [[mercenary]] and<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> [[Minister of Protection]]<ref name="Temptation of the Force">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> in the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]&mdash;mentally noted she intended to include her killing of the sergeant when she deserted the [[army]] in the [[Abediah Viess' book|book]] she intended to [[Author|write]]. She decided she would leave the detail about the sergeant's passing in the [[book]], considering killing to be killing all the same regardless of the reason behind it. Viess was ultimately [[Death|killed]] by [[Jedi Master]] [[Porter Engle]] before she could write the book.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />
