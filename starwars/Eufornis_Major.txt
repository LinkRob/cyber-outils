'''Eufornis Major''', sometimes referred to as simply '''Eufornis''', was a prestigious [[ecumenopolis]] [[planet]] located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Core Worlds]]. Due to their similar names, Eufornis Major was occasionally confused with [[Eufornis Minor]], a remote [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] colony world.<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks" /> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Kassa">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> [[Telgordo Travel]] operated a transport service from [[Coruscant]] that stopped at Eufornis Major, as well as [[Plexis]] and [[Hosnian Prime]].<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Eufornis Major was first mentioned in [[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks|''Rebel in the Ranks'']], the [[2015]] second installment in the [[Star Wars Rebels: Servants of the Empire|''Servants of the Empire'']] series of junior novels written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks" />
