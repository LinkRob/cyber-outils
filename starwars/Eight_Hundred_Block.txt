'''Eight Hundred Block''' was a location on the agricultural world [[Mina-Rau]].<ref name="Harvest" /> By [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Harvest Date">{{AndorDate|203}}</ref> [[Kellen]] gave [[Brasso]] an adulterated [[Emergency Work Order]] for the Eight Hundred Block so Brasso, [[Bix Caleen]] and [[Wilmon Paak]] could hide from the [[Galactic Empire]], as they did not possess [[visa]]s and were thus undocumented workers on Mina-Rau.<ref name="Harvest" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eight Hundred Block was mentioned during the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the [[Disney+]] live-action series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', which was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Harvest" /> on [[April 22]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref>
