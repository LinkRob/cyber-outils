The '''''Arbitrage''''' was a [[freighter]] utilized by the company [[Garello Technologies]] on the [[planet]] [[Ab Dalis]], [[captain]]ed by the [[Shistavanen]] [[Odabba]]. After Ab Dalis was hit by an [[Emergences|Emergence]] of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], the executive of the company, [[Larence Garello]], ordered his workforce to evacuate on a [[convoy]] of six freighters, with the ''Arbitrage'' in the front. Before the convoy could escape, it was [[Attack on the Garello convoy|attacked]] by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization, who destroyed two of the other freighters. The ''Arbitrage'' was boarded by the Nihil, who filled its [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] with [[poison gas]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
