'''''Dark Droids: D-Squad'' 4''' is the fourth and final issue of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Dark Droids: D-Squad]]''. The issue was written by [[Marc Guggenheim]], illustrated by [[Salva Espín]] and [[Israel Silva]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 6]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''INTRODUCING THE ALL-NEW, ALL-DIFFERENT D-SQUAD! &ndash; A ''DARK DROIDS'' TIE-IN! The D-SQUAD's mission takes them into the palace of JABBA THE HUTT! R2-D2 and QT-KT take on the malevolent torture droid EV-9D9! This issue ties directly into ''DARK DROIDS''! Not to be missed!''
