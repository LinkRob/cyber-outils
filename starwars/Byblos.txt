'''Byblos''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> located in the [[Byblos system]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Colonies]] region<ref name="Appendix" /> that was situated along the [[Corellian Run]].<ref name="Map" /> The former [[pirate]] [[Queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] marked it as the location of an outlaw garage on a map that she included in the [[book]] that became known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> Around [[10 BBY]], the [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]] owned a storehouse on Byblos which contained items he had [[Collector|collected]].<ref name="Solo Guide" />
