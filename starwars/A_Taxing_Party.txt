"'''A Taxing Party'''" was a gamemaster adventure written as part of [[Wizards of the Coast]]'s [[2003]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]] sourcebook, ''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]''. The adventure was part of the book's [[Esseles/Legends|Esseles]] entry and focused on an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] plot to instigate riots on Esseles in order to obtain greater security taxes. The player characters were intended to be a group of individuals hired by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] to film a conference where the Imperials discussed the plot.
