The '''Corellian Chemical Corporation''',<ref name="Episode Guide" /> also known under the abbreviated names '''Corellian Che'''<ref name="An Old Friend">{{TCW|An Old Friend}}</ref> and '''CorChemCo''',<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" /> was a subsidiary of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] that went bankrupt during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Episode Guide" />

The company manufactured the [[Beastmaster Chem Lure]] and [[fire paste]], a substance that could ignite almost any flammable object.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Corellian Chemical Corperation was first mentioned as Corellian Chem on a data-screen in "[[An Old Friend]]," the fifth episode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''' [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|sixth season]].<ref name="An Old Friend" /> Its full name was provided by its accompanying episode guide on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Episode Guide" />
