The '''Bionic Eye'''<ref name="LL Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/lightsaber-lost-episode-guide|text="Lightsaber Lost" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> was a model of [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] prosthetic [[eye]]. The implant was round and grey and glowed turquoise in the center. It was visually similar<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" /> to the [[BioTech Implant]].<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> The [[Quarren]] criminal [[Lauli Wahlo]] wore a Bionic Eye prosthetic as his right eye.<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" />
