The '''Emergency Powers Act''' may refer to one of several acts:

==Canon==
*The '''[[Emergency Powers Act]]''', an amendment to the [[Galactic Constitution]] passed to allow [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] to remain [[Supreme Chancellor]] after the expiration of his elected terms.

==Legends==
*The '''[[Emergency Powers Act (war against Zakuul)|Emergency Powers Act]]''', enacted by the time of the [[war against Zakuul]].
*The '''[[Emergency Powers Act/Legends|Emergency Powers Act]]''', an amendment to the [[Galactic Constitution/Legends|Galactic Constitution]] that eliminated term limits for [[Supreme Chancellor/Legends|Supreme Chancellors]].
*The '''[[Emergency Powers Act (Galactic Alliance)|Emergency Powers Act]]''', enacted by the [[Galactic Alliance Senate|Senate]] of the [[Galactic Federation of Free Alliances]] after the [[Second Galactic Civil War]].
