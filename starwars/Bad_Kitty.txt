'''''Bad Kitty'''''<ref name="Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/landing-at-point-rain-trivia-gallery|text="Landing at Point Rain" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> was an [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT]] [[gunship]]. It had customized nose art painted on either of its sides, with a [[nexu]] riding a large bomb and the ship's name written in [[Aurebesh]] on the bomb. It was used by the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] during the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]] in [[21 BBY]], when the Republic attacked the [[Geonosis primary droid foundry]].<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" />
