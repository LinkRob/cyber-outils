'''''Classic Star Wars'' 2''' is the second issue of the ''[[Classic Star Wars]]'' series, published by [[Dark Horse Comics]].

This particular ''Classic'' series were reformatted and colored reprints of the [[Star Wars (comic strip)|''Star Wars'' daily newspaper strip]] by [[Archie Goodwin]] and [[Al Williamson]] from the early 1980s, including the second half of ''[[The Bounty Hunter of Ord Mantell]]''.
Williamson also supplied new panels and art to flow the stories for traditional comic format.

==Publisher's summary==
''Classic Star Wars features the adventures of everyone's favorite Rebels during the period between the first and second Star Wars films. In this issue, Darth Vader plots new ways to trip up the Rebels, but a turncoat Admiral may thwart his plans. Meanwhile, Luke, Leia, Han, and Chewie make good their escape from a bounty hunter &ndash; only to run afoul of the Imperial fleet!''
