'''Dressellians''' were a [[species]] of [[Sentience|sentient]] [[humanoid]]s that were [[Homeworld|native]] to [[Dressel]]. They lived in peaceful isolation from any conflict until the [[Galactic Civil War]]. As a species, they were loyal to the [[Galactic Republic]] and after its demise many Dressellians joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. They had [[hair]]less heads and wrinkled visages and cranium. [[Orrimaarko]], a [[Gender|male]] Dressellian, was a soldier for the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War.<ref name="Alien Archive" />

Following the [[dissolution of the Imperial Senate]], the Rebel Alliance's [[Commander-in-Chief]] [[Mon Mothma]] noted in a journal entry within ''[[the Rebel Files]]'' that she considered the Dressellian population, along with the [[Mon Calamari|Mon Cal]] and [[Sullustan]]s to be especially vulnerable and that they should consider taking in [[refugee]]s.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

During the Galactic Civil War, a Dressellian resistance fighter named [[Lokmarcha]] served as a [[major]] in the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Moving Target" /> Shortly before the [[Battle of Endor]], several other Dressellians attended the Alliance briefing during which the attack on the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] was prepared.<ref name="Episode VI" />
