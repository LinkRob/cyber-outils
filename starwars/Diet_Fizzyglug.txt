A subsidiary product of [[Fizzyglug]], '''Diet Fizzyglug''' was a well known, popular drink. It was on the menu of [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] and was served there regularly. At Dex's, it was sold for 1.7 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]].
