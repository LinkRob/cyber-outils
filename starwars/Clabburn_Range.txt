The '''Clabburn Range''' was a [[mountain]] range on the snow [[planet]] [[Hoth]]. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] built their [[Echo Base|base]] into the mountains of the Clabburn Range and used a [[South Ridge|relatively flat ground to the base's immediate south]] as a [[staging area]] for [[Battle of Hoth|evacuation]].<ref name="CL" /><ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>
