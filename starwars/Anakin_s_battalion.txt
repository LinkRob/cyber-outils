'''Anakin's battalion'''<ref name="Darkness" /> was a [[battalion]] of the [[501st Legion]]<ref name="The Clone Wars">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Clone Wars}}</ref> that participated in the [[Battle of Umbara]] during the [[Clone Wars]] under [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]. The battalion consisted of multiple [[platoon]]s of standard [[clone trooper]]s along with a large contingent of [[Advanced Recon Force trooper]]s on [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport|AT-RT walkers]]. [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] served as Skywalker's first-in-command, while [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Fives]] assist Skywalker's battalion on special assignment. Others included [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Appo]]<ref name="Darkness" /> and a [[Clone Commander]].<ref name="Standing Out" />

==Description==
[[File:501st-Clone-Commander-Umbara.png|thumb|left|250px|A Clone Commander of the battalion with clone medic Kix.]]
Anakin's battalion was a [[Grand Army of the Republic]] [[infantry]] and [[cavalry]] [[battalion]] of the [[501st Legion]], under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]],<ref name="Darkness" /> with further [[officer]]s including a [[Clone Commander]]<ref name="Standing Out" /> and [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Appo]].<ref name="The General" /> Despite the presence of that commander,<ref name="Standing Out" /> it was Rex who served as Skywalker's first-in-command. During the [[Battle of Umbara]], it was temporarily commanded by Jedi General [[Pong Krell]], and [[Advanced Recon Commando]] [[Fives]] was assigned to the battalion.<ref name="Darkness" />

Unlike other 501st Legion [[color]] schemes,<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> some members of this battalion had a blue, thick, vertical line on their [[helmet]] up to their visors,<ref name="Darkness" /> while in other armor sets the line descended to the bottom of the helmet.<ref name="Episode III" /> The battalion was composed of [[clone trooper]]s that were divided into multiple [[platoon]]s, and was supplemented by dozens of [[Advanced Recon Force trooper]]s on [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s.<ref name="Darkness" />
