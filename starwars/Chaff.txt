'''Chaff''' was a type of [[Electronic countermeasures|electronic countermeasure]] that could be used to combat [[missile]]s.  Chaff could be released from a ship's engines, creating a cloud of particles behind them.  This would scramble the sensors of any ships or missiles passing through it, breaking "[[targeting computer|target lock]]".  The effect would last until the cloud eventually dissipated.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref><ref>{{EA|url=games/starwars/squadrons/overview/components/countermeasures|text=STAR WARS™: Squadrons Components - Countermeasures|archivedate=20230304211024|nolive=1}}</ref>

Chaff could also be electrified and deployed from [[electro-chaff missile]]s to damage or disable [[starship]]s.<ref name="SWZ">{{FFGXW2|set=Fury of the First Order Squadron Pack|cardname=Electro-Chaff Missiles}}</ref>
