'''Ebbe Casset''' was a [[ensign]] of the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="No Big Deal" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: No Big Deal]]" take place one year after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]]. Therefore, the story must take place in [[229 BBY]]</ref> she was stationed on [[Eriadu]] with her fellow the [[Republic Defense Coalition officer|RDC officers]] [[Captain]] [[Pikka Adren]], Captain [[Joss Adren]] and [[Admiral]] [[Pevel Kronara]]. She was the niece of [[Hedda Casset]], who had [[Death|died]] during the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]].<ref name="No Big Deal" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date2">{{THRContent228BBY|TOTF}}</ref> she accompanied [[Cair San Tekka]] from the ''[[Third Horizon]]'' to the wreckage of the ''[[Precipitate Fire]]'' to help extract the [[Path engine]].<ref name="Temptation">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref>
