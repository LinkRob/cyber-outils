At some point between approximately [[5000 BBY]] and many years prior to [[1032 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> an '''individual''' trained the [[Duros]] [[Darth Caldoth]]&mdash;whose career spanned [[Standard century|centuries]]&mdash;as a member of the [[Sith]] order of [[Force-sensitive|Force-users]]. Per Sith tradition, Caldoth eventually caused the [[death]] of his [[Sith Master|master]], and the latter's body came to lie deep in the crypts of a ruined sacred [[Sith temple (abandoned moon)|Sith temple]] on a long-abandoned [[Unidentified abandoned moon|moon]]. Much later, Caldoth abandoned his own [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], [[Ry Nymbis]], inside the same temple as part of a trial for him.<ref name="Gaze of Stone" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Caldoth's master was mentioned in "[[Gaze of Stone]]," a short story written by [[George Mann]] and included in the [[2019]] collection ''[[Myths & Fables]]''.<ref name="Gaze of Stone" />
