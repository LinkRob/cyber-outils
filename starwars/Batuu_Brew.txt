'''Batuu Brew''' was a type of beverage [[Diet|consumable]] by [[human]]s that shared its name with the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. During the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], a [[Oga's Cantina|cantina]] in Batuu's [[Black Spire Outpost]] settlement served the drink.<ref name="THA" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s [[Mission to Batuu|mission]] to [[Batuu]], which is depicted in ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]'', to [[19 BBY]].</ref> the [[cantina]]'s [[bartender]], [[Janott]], offered Batuu Brew and other beverage selections to the human [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] when she visited the establishment, though she ultimately chose a [[Andoan White|different beverage]].<ref name="THA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Batuu Brew was mentioned in the second storyline of the [[2018]] novel ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]'', the second installment in the [[Star Wars: The Imperial Trilogy|''Star Wars: Thrawn'']] novel series written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="THA" />
