'''Blue Grill''' was the name of a [[Restaurant|dining]] related business operating in a [[Unidentified refinery town|refinery town]] on the [[Water|ice]] [[moon]] [[Ro Mira]]. It was in a ground level high building out of [[Color|red]] bricks with a flat roof. The business' name was written in [[aurebesh]] letters across two illuminated red signs at the building's front and another purple glowing sign advertised it with the numbers "69". At some point during the later [[Republic Era]], the undercover [[Jedi Knight]] [[Mace Windu]] ran across the roof of Blue Grill as he was pursuing the criminal [[Azita Cruuz]] who had [[Thief|stolen]] the formula or the experimental [[starship]] [[fuel]] [[coaxium ultra]].<ref name="Mace Windu 1" />
