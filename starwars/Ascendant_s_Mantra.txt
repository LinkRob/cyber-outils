The '''Ascendant's Mantra''' was a mantra in [[ur-Kittât]]<ref name="Aphra 20" /> spoken by the [[Ascendant]], a [[Religion|cult]] who sought to replicate the effects of the [[dark side of the Force]]. During [[Gender|their]] [[time]] as a student at the [[University of Bar'leth]], [[Kho Phon Farrus]] studied the Ascendant and, according to [[Sava]] [[Iglan'tine Nos]], had the best student translation of the mantra in her class.<ref name="Aphra 19">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 19|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 19]]</ref> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 19|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 19]] take place between the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> Farrus returned to the [[university]] in search of the [[Spark Eternal]], an artifact of the Ascendant, and in order to find it believed they needed to recover the [[Unyielding Heart]] in order to complete the Ascendant's Mantra. As Nos led Farrus to the Unyielding Heart, she had Farrus recite their interpretation of the mantra. After Farrus had spoken their translation, the sava asked what it meant, to which Farrus responded that it was instructions for a ritual. Farrus then questioned if it was like a spell to summon the Spark Eternal.<ref name="Aphra 19" />
