'''CT-66-3238''' was a [[clone cadet]] who served in [[Bravo Squad (clone cadets)|Bravo Squad]], a training unit, early during the [[Clone Wars]]. He was marked with a three on his [[clone training armor]].

==Biography==
CT-66-3238 was a [[Cloning|clone]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] who was [[Reproduction|born]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]]. Like his comrades, CT-66-3238 was made to serve in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. Early during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], CT-66-3238 served in [[Bravo Squad (clone cadets)|Bravo Squad]], a [[squad]] of [[clone cadet]]s training on Kamino. During their [[time]] in training, Bravo [[Squad]] managed to complete the practice exam in record time. When [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Colt]] of [[Rancor Battalion]] arrived to watch over the final exams, Bravo Squad, along with [[Domino Squad]] and two other units, were summoned to a [[hangar]] in [[Tipoca City Military Complex]] for Colt's speech. During the speech, Colt selected Bravo Squad for the final test first due to their record time in completing the practice test.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

Starting [[Citadel Challenge]] version THX variable 1138, CT-66-3238, [[CT-66-3339]], and [[CT-27-3555]] took the right flank while [[CT-66-3232]] and [[CT-66-3233]] took the left flank. CT-66-3232 directed CT-66-3238 and CT-66-3339 to move forward and engage the training [[battle droid]]s while they followed close behind and the others provided cover fire. The squad moved up and eventually reached the base of the citadel. CT-66-3339 directed CT-66-3238 to move forward to the citadel while the rest of the squad provided cover fire. CT-66-3238 was able to rappel up and capture the light post, completing the exam. While exiting the arena, CT-66-3238 and his teammates passed the approaching Domino Squad and CT-66-3233 mocked them.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Personality and traits==
A clone of Jango Fett, CT-66-3238 was born on Kamino, was 1.83 [[meter]]s tall, had [[Color|black]] [[hair]], tan skin, and was a [[human]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Skills and abilities==
CT-66-3238 demonstrated teamwork skills, which helped them to pass the final exam. He was able to take directions and used his [[ascension cable]] to scale the citadel and capture the light post.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Equipment==
CT-66-3238 was equipped with [[clone training armor]] which contained the red coloring of Bravo Squad, the number three on his chest plate and left shoulder [[armor]], had his designation on his left leg, and utilized a [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] with non-lethal bolts and an ascension cable.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Behind the scenes==
This Bravo Squad cadet's identification number was first depicted in the episode "[[Clone Cadets]]" of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''. It says 66-3238, and the prefix CT- was predetermined. This way of determining identification numbers was proved with the troopers in [[Domino Squad]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

CT-66-3238's identification number can also on the armor for the third member of the unidentified squad of cadets with yellow markings.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />
