'''''Characters of Star Wars: The High Republic''''' is a series of animated shorts released on [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] tied to the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia initiative. Each episode focuses on a particular character from the project, explaining their histories and motivations prior to the events of the books and comics. The series was produced by [[Eastham Studios]].<ref name="SWcom">{{SW|url=news/characters-of-star-wars-the-high-republic|text=Learn More about the Characters of ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' in New Series of Digital Shorts}}</ref> Some episodes of the series are featured as segments within the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Show]]''.<ref name="SWTHRS01">{{HighRepublicShow|v5phi7oXDms|A Deep Dive Into Star Wars: The High Republic, Bringing A Younger Yoda to Life (in VR), and More!|sw_url=video/a-deep-dive-into-star-wars-the-high-republic-bringing-a-younger-yoda-to-life-in-vr-and-more}}</ref>
