{{Quote|Made a clean break with Bunji after the Cron scam, only to wind up in the decaying orbit of the galaxy's cheapest swindlers&mdash;the Briil Twins.|Han Solo, in the ''Smuggler's Guide''|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>}}
The '''Briil Twins''' were a group, whom [[Han Solo]] considered [[the galaxy]]'s cheapests [[con artist]]s.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> Following a scam in the [[Cron Drift]], between [[10 BBY]] and [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The entry in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'' that mentions 373-CR takes place after [[Han Solo]] has completed the [[Kessel Run]], but before he encounters a [[bounty hunter]] on [[Ord Mantell]], which is mentioned as having occurred prior to the [[Battle of Hoth]] in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' establishes that Solo completes the [[Kessel]] Run nine [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]], meaning Solo's Kessel Run must take place in [[10 BBY]]. The ''Atlas'' also dates the Battle of [[Hoth]] to [[3 ABY]]; therefore, the entry in the [[in-universe]] [[book]] ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' must have been written between 10 BBY and 3 ABY.</ref> [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] worked with the Briil Twins smuggling hundred bottles of [[R'alla mineral water]] to the people of [[Rampa II]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Briil Twins were first mentioned in the [[2018]] [[canon]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the Briil Twins were first mentioned in the [[1979]] novel ''[[Han Solo's Revenge]]'', written by [[Brian Daley]].<ref name="hsr">''[[Han Solo's Revenge]]''</ref>
