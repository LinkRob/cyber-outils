SW: New Republic: Bloodline coming 2016. Set 6 years before TFA.|archivefile=File:New Republic Bloodline Twitter Post.jpg}} establishes that the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']] take place six [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|VII}}. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], so ''Bloodline'' must take place in [[28 ABY]].</ref>
