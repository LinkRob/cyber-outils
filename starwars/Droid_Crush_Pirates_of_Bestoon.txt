The '''Droid Crush Pirates of Bestoon''', known more simply as the '''Droid Crush''' and also referred to as the '''Crush''', were [[droid]] [[pirate]]s associated with [[Bestoon]] and led by [[Captain]] [[Ought-Six]] and were known to be recruited by the [[Jedi hunter]] [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]].

==History==
{{Quote|When I hired the Droid Crush Pirates of Bestoon. I expected results. You said the Droid Crush was different now. Let bygones be bygones, you said. The Droid Crush Pirates weren't like what they were in the old days. You were professional. Renowned, even. But it seems I made the same mistake as I did all those years ago.|Ochi, to Ought-Six after the Nightside raid|<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref>}}
The [[Droid]] Crush [[Pirate]]s of [[Bestoon]], or simply the Droid Crush, were a crew of droid pirates. A number of [[Unidentified monster droid model|B1-KX monster droids]] were among the ranks of the Crush.<ref name="Vader 16">[[Darth Vader (2020) 16|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 16]]</ref> At some point, [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] and the [[smuggler]] [[Just Lucky]] stole from the Droid Crush. Eventually, the pirates tracked the two down with the intention of [[Death|killing]] them both for revenge, proving Lucky's point to Aphra that mistakes could catch up to a person. Their attempt to kill the two [[Thief|thieves]] failed.<ref name="Aphra 22">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 22|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 22]]</ref>

The Droid Crush Pirates were tasked by the [[Sith assassin]] [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]] in [[hunting]] the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on [[Mustafar]], having been promised with the high-quality technology within [[Darth Vader's armor|Vader's armor]]. After retrieving his [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|lightsaber]], Vader destroyed Captain Ought-Six and the rest of the droids present.<ref name="Vader 9" /> The captain was later repaired and again [[Plot to assassinate Darth Vader|attacked]] Vader with more droids in [[Zee-Nine City Seven]] with the help of [[IG-88B|IG-88]]. The droids fled after IG-88 was defeated by Vader and an aerial bombardment was fired by [[Bokku|Bokku the Hutt's]] [[Bokku's barge|ship]].<ref name="Vader 13">[[Darth Vader (2020) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 13]]</ref> After [[Crimson Dawn]]'s [[Auction for Han Solo|auction of Han Solo]], [[Sly Moore]] commanded the Droid Crush to attack [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced]] and repair [[Luke Skywalker]]'s [[Red Five (T-65B X-wing starfighter)|X-wing]], allowing Skywalker to escape.<ref name="Vader 16">[[Darth Vader (2020) 16|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 16]]</ref> Repaired once more, Ought-Six gathered a grouping of droids who had also fallen to Vader in the past to form the [[droid uprising]]. However, Vader's spy [[ZED-6-7]] tricked the droids into a [[Battle with the droid uprising|battle]] that left all gathered destroyed, including the Crush Pirates. The Crush Pirates and every other uprising droid was then rebuilt<ref name="Vader 36" /> and reprogrammed to serve Vader, although Ought-Six retained the ability to be annoyed by his new duty to the Sith Lord.<ref name="Vader 37">[[Darth Vader (2020) 37|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 37]]</ref>

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> while guarding their base in the [[Khepi Tomb]], a [[humanoid]] member of the [[Rokana Raiders]] [[pirate]] group gossiped with a fellow raider that they heard that their leader, [[Stinger Tash]], wanted to start trading with the Droid Crush. They admitted they didn't like the idea but that they were always outvoted.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

By [[21 ABY]],<ref name="SotSDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> the Droid Crush had grown in prestige and professionalism, according to Ought-Six. The Crush was hired by Ochi to participate in a [[raid on Nightside]] in the [[Therezar system]]. When the Crush failed and suffered heavy causalities, Ochi destroyed the captain and contacted his [[Sith Eternal]] benefactors, requesting that they assign him [[trooper]]s to help in his [[Quest for Exegol|hunt for Rey]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />
