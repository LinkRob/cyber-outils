The '''''Brightbird''''' was a [[Cruiser|minicruiser]] that was the [[starship]] of [[pilot]] [[Sky Graf]]. In [[384 BBY]]<ref name="build-date">The ship was built and finished two years before the events of ''The High Republic: Quest for Planet X''. The novel begins one [[Standard day|day]] after the [[Battle of Jedha]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]] and spans two [[Standard week|weeks]] to two [[Standard day|days]] after the [[Night of Sorrow]], which ''Star Wars: Timelines'' also dates to 382 BBY.</ref>, it was built offworld but finished on [[Thelj]].<ref name="Quest" /> The ''Brightbird'' eventually became a personal cruiser for some time and then became a [[warship]] of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]. The vessel later came to be owned by [[San Tekka clan|San Tekka family]] member [[Cair San Tekka]], who refurbished the ''Brightbird''. San Tekka additionally registered the vessel with the Nihil as the '''''Blood Vulture''''', acting as a [[smuggler]].<ref name="Defy the Storm" />
