'''Count of Serenno''' was a title held by [[Dooku]]<ref name="CE">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> of [[House Serenno]] as [[count]] of the [[planet]] [[Serenno]], with his father [[Gora]] and his brother [[Ramil]], who lost the title with the [[Incident on Serenno|invasion of Serenno]], having held the title previously.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> During a dinner between the two of them, [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] once referred to Dooku by his title and suggested it would be the name Dooku would try to use as leader of a galaxy-wide autocracy, which Dooku claimed was not his end goal.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> Dooku continued to hold the title until his [[death]] during the [[Battle of Coruscant]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> The [[cape]] Dooku wore was the emblem of the Count of Serenno.<ref name="CE" /> In [[10 BBY]], [[Chrodber]] served as the Count of Serenno and was present aboard the ''[[First Light]]'', a [[yacht]] owned by [[Crimson Dawn]] [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]].<ref name="Official Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Count of Serenno title first appeared through Dooku in the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], the second installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]].<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> The title was first identified in [[canon]] via ''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''.<ref name="CE" /> In ''[[Tarkin (novel)|Tarkin]]'', the title was used but with incorrect capitalization: "Count" was left lowercase.<ref name="Tarkin" />
