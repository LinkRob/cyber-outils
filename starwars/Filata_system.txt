The '''Filata system''' was located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="The Interior" /> It was situated in grid square H-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Filata [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The system was listed as a reference to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity's celestial body [[Filata]] and its [[Third moon of Filata|third moon]], which were mentioned in ''[[Keeping the Peace]]'', a [[2015]] sourcebook published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for use with the ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="Keeping">''[[Keeping the Peace]]''</ref>
