'''CGCG''' ('''西基電腦動畫''' in [[Wikipedia:Traditional Chinese characters|traditional Chinese]]) is a [[Wikipedia:Taiwan|Taiwanese]] 3D [[Wikipedia:Computer-generated imagery|computer graphics]] animation company founded in [[1988]] that has provided production services to [[Lucasfilm Animation]]'s ''[[Star Wars]]'' animation projects, including the [[2008]] [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'']] film and the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' (2008&ndash;[[2014]], [[2020]]), ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' (2014&ndash;[[2018]]), ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' (2018&ndash;2020), ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' ([[2021]]&ndash;[[2024]]), and [[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (television series)|''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi'']] ([[2022]]).<ref name="CGCG" />

For [[Star Wars Rebels Season Two|season two]] of ''Rebels'', CGCG produced seventeen episodes while the remaining five were produced by [[Wikipedia:Vietnam|Vietnam's]] [[Sparx*]].<ref name="ASC" />
