'''Exile''' was a form of punishment or penance used by numerous beings across [[the galaxy]] in which the punished were banished from society. The term '''hiding''' referred to those who lived in exile, though not as a form of punishment. 

==History==
{{Quote|&hellip;he lives in the Outer Rim, in exile.|Mother Talzin tells Savage Opress about his long-lost brother Maul|<ref name="Witches of the Mist">{{TCW|Witches of the Mist}}</ref>}}
During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Aniseya]]'s [[Brendok coven|coven]] has been forced into exile as the [[witch]]es were hunted and persecuted to the brink of extinction.<ref name="Episode III">{{Acolyte|Destiny (The Acolyte)}}</ref>

At the end of the [[Great Clan Wars|Mandalorian Civil War]], those who continued to embrace the Mandalorian warrior culture were exiled to the [[moon]] of [[Concordia]]. Concordia's governor, [[Pre Vizsla]] of [[Clan Vizsla]], would reorganize these Mandalorians into [[Death Watch]] with the intent of retaking Mandalore from the [[New Mandalorians]].<ref name="TMP">{{TCW|The Mandalore Plot}}</ref>

[[File:UneasyAlliance-Eminence.png|thumb|left|250px|Maul joined forces with Pre Vizsla and the exiled forces of Death Watch.]]
After his defeat at the hands of [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] during the [[Battle of Naboo]],<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> the [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]] was abandoned and entered exile<ref name="Witches of the Mist" /> on the junk world of [[Lotho Minor]] until his brother [[Savage Opress]] discovered and rescued him.<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref> Maul would be brought to [[Dathomir]], where [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]] restored his sanity as well as giving her son new cybernetic legs.<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref> Soon after, he and his brother would ally themselves with Vizsla and the exiled warriors of Death Watch.<ref name="Eminence">{{TCW|Eminence (episode)}}</ref>

At the end of the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] went into self-imposed exile on [[Dagobah]] after he failed to defeat the [[Sith Lord]], [[Darth Sidious]], who was directly responsible for the near extinction of the [[Jedi Order]]. After his [[Duel on Mustafar|duel]] with [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on [[Mustafar]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], entered self-imposed exile on [[Tatooine]] to watch over and protect Vader's secret son, [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]].<ref name="RotS" /> Many Jedi who survived [[Order 66]] and the [[Great Jedi Purge]] went into exile to escape the [[Galactic Empire]] and their [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]].<ref name="DV:DLotS">[[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2017)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'' (2017)]]</ref>

The [[Chiss]] known as [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] claimed to have been exiled by his own people, but secretly engineered matters with their aid to create the appearance of this, in order to gain acceptance into and study the Galactic Empire. When questioned by the Imperials as to his background, Thrawn described himself using the [[Sy Bisti]] term for "exiled": "Xishu azwane."<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> Later, during the [[Liberation of Lothal]] in [[1 BBY]], Thrawn was banished into a forced exile on the extragalactic planet of [[Peridea]] when [[Padawan|Jedi Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]] summoned a pod of [[purrgil]] to aid the rebel effort. The purrgil dragged both Thrawn and Bridger, along with Thrawn's Destroyer, the ''[[Chimaera]],'' and crew to [[Far galaxy|another galaxy]].<ref name="Ahsoka 2">{{Ahsoka|Part Two: Toil and Trouble}}</ref> By [[9 ABY]], [[Thrawn's forces]], as well as Bridger, would [[Battle of Peridea|escape]] Peridea thanks to the efforts of [[Morgan Elsbeth]].<ref name="Ahsoka 8">{{Ahsoka|Part Eight: The Jedi, the Witch, and the Warlord}}</ref>

After his failure to train a new generation of Jedi, Luke Skywalker went into self-exile on the planet [[Ahch-To]],<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> [[Sever Force|shutting himself off from the Force]] and intending to spend his last days marooned and alone.<ref name="USW,NE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[35 ABY|A year later]], his apprentice [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] intended to replicate the feat, but was convinced by the [[Force spirit]] of her master to instead continue the [[First Order-Resistance War|fight]] against the [[First Order]].<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

Following his [[Battle of Endor|death]] at [[Endor]], [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth]] Sidious was secretly resurrected in a [[Cloning|clone]] body on [[Exegol]],<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> where he remained in exile. In exile<ref name="Sith Eternal DB">{{Databank|sith-eternal|Sith Eternal}}</ref> on Exegol, Sidious was attended to by [[Sith cultist|Sith loyalists]], who toiled endlessly to keep their venerated master alive by coupling their [[DNA|genetic]] [[Bioengineering|engineering]]<ref name="TROS EE" /> technology with the occult of the [[dark side of the Force]],<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> while the deposed [[Emperor]] built the [[Sith Eternal|Sith cult]] into a [[Sith Eternal military|military force]] of [[Sith trooper|soldiers]] and workers.<ref name="Sith Eternal DB" />
