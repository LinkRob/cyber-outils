'''Chiffonage''' was a [[human]] who served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Alliance Military|military]] as a [[commander]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. In [[3 ABY]], Chiffonage was stationed in [[Echo Base]] on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Hoth]], where they attended a strategy conference alongside members of [[Alliance High Command]] to formulate strategies in order to achieve victory in the war.

Chiffonage later operated in the command center, at one point seated next to [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. During the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Battle of Hoth|invasion]] of Hoth, the command center was hit by enemy fire, and the commander, Organa and [[communications officer]] [[Toryn Farr]] chose to stay while others left. After [[Captain]] [[Han Solo]] came to help Organa, the princess gave orders to Chiffonage, who sat at the last comm station before evacuating with Solo and their [[protocol droid]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Send all troops in Sector Twelve to the south slope to protect the fighters! ''[&hellip;]'' Give the evacuation code signal. And get to your transports!|Leia Organa's original and revised orders to Chiffonage|<ref name="Episode V" />}}
Chiffonage served as a [[commander]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Alliance Military|military branch]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Twilight Company" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The section of ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'' where Chiffonage is mentioned is set twenty-two [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Clone Wars]], which [[Mission to Mustafar|ended]] in [[19 BBY]], according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. The mention must have therefore happened in [[3 ABY]].</ref> Chiffonage was stationed in the Rebel Alliance's [[Echo Base]] on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Hoth]]. Chiffonage attended a strategy conference with various members of [[Alliance High Command]] that lasted several [[Standard day|days]], discussing strategies that would help the Rebel Alliance win the war as quickly as possible. Base sentries discussed Chiffonage, [[General]] [[Carlist Rieekan]], and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] attendance in the conference over comms. Thanks to information provided by Imperial defector [[Everi Chalis]], the attendees were able to devise a plan, and pardoned Chalis of her past deeds as an Imperial [[governor]] in return.<ref name="Twilight Company" />

[[File:K-3PO_destroyed.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Chiffonage at the last remaining comm station]]
Later on, Chiffonage was monitoring a screen in Echo Base's command center while seated next to Organa when rebel [[Captain]] [[Han Solo]] reported to Rieekan of his need to leave the Rebel Alliance in order to settle personal issues. When<ref name="Episode V" /> the [[Viper probe droid]] [[XJ9-CS14]]<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> discovered the base's location and reported it to the Empire, the rebels began a [[Code K-one-zero|mass evacuation]].<ref name="Episode V" /> The majority of the base's personnel were able to escape Echo Base as its personnel [[Battle of Hoth|fought]] invading [[Blizzard Force|Imperial forces]].<ref name="HC9">{{HelmetCollectionCite|9|Highlights of the Saga}}</ref>

During the battle, the command centre was soon hit by enemy [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT]] fire,<ref name="Twilight Company" /> damaging much of the area and prompting some of the personnel to evacuate, leaving Chiffonage, Organa, her [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] and [[communications officer]] [[Toryn Farr]]. After Solo came to check on Organa, and the princess ordered Chiffonage, who operated the last comm station, to direct troops from [[Sector Twelve]] to the south slope. Organa was interrupted as Echo Base took more fire, and after enemy troops were reported to have entered the base, the princess told Chiffonage send an evacuation signal and get to their transport.<ref name="Ion Control">{{StoryCite|story=Ion Control|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> Beyond Organa's orders to send out a signal,<ref name="Episode V" /> Chiffonage also verbally gave an order for all rebel troops to retreat from the battlefront.<ref name="Invasion of Echo Base" />

==Personality and traits==
Chiffonage was a [[human]] with light skin and [[Color|brown]] [[hair]].<ref name="Episode V" />

==Equipment==
While operating in Echo Base's command center, Chiffonage utilized a black [[headset]] and wore a white [[Clothing|bodysuit]] under a brown, sleeveless, high-collar [[jacket]].<ref name="Episode V" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chiffonage first appeared as a background extra portrayed by an uncredited actor in the [[1980]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="Episode V" /> The scenes in the Echo Base control room featuring Chiffonage were shot on Stage 1 at [[Elstree Studios]] between [[March 26]] and [[March 28|28]], [[1979]].<ref name="MakingOf">''[[The Making of The Empire Strikes Back]]''</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the character of [[Beryl Chiffonage]] was created for [[West End Games]]' [[1988]] board game ''[[Assault on Hoth]]''.<ref name="Assault on Hoth">''[[Assault on Hoth]]''</ref> He was [[Retroactive continuity|retconned]] into ''Empire Strikes Back'' in the article [[Who's Who in Echo Base: The Unsung Rebels of the Battle of Hoth|''Who's Who in Echo Base'']], which was written by [[Josh Radke]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 74|''Star Wars Insider'' 74]]<ref name="Who's Who">{{InsiderCite|74|Who's Who in Echo Base: The Unsung Rebels of the Battle of Hoth}}</ref> on [[February 24]], [[2004]].<ref name="SW.com">{{SWArchive|url=community/news/2004/01/news20040126.html|text=Clone Trooper Front and Center in ''Insider'' #74}}</ref> The name was partially brought into [[canon]] when the character was mentioned in [[Alexander Freed]]'s [[2015]] novel, ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''.<ref name="Twilight Company" />
