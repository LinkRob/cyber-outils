'''Cellheim Anujo''' was the [[Humanoid/Legends|humanoid]] [[Aide/Legends|aide]] to [[Senator/Legends|Senator]] [[Tundra Dowmeia/Legends|Tundra Dowmeia]] during the latter stages of the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].

A mysterious being, Cellheim Anujo was often draped in black robes and veils that masked both [[Gender/Legends|gender]] and [[Species/Legends|species]].

==Behind the scenes==
Cellheim Anujo was principally played by [[T.V. Moore]] on all the Australian main unit scenes and also by [[Dean Mitchell]] for the UK Unit pick-up shots on [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="DM">{{WebCite|url=http://www.deanmitchell.net/html/star_wars.html|text=deanmitchell.net|archivedate=20080723222127|nolive=1}}</ref>

Cellheim Anujo was originally known only as "Tundra Dowmeia's aide."
