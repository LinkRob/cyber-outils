'''Darren Johnson''' has worked on many Star Wars games between the years 1994 to 2001, his first being [[Star Wars: X-Wing (video game)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']] and last being ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan]]''. Other Star Wars games include ''[[Star Wars: Dark Forces]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Mysteries of the Sith]]'', ''[[Star Wars: DroidWorks]]'', [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (video game)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'']], ''[[Star Wars: Rebel Assault II: The Hidden Empire]]'', [[Star Wars: TIE Fighter (video game)|''Star Wars: TIE Fighter'']] and ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing vs. TIE Fighter]]''.
