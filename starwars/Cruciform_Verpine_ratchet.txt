{{Quote|As a scavenger, your tools are your life. Here's what's in my satchel ''[&hellip;]'' bag of quick-switch modulars, including everything from Mon Calamari hex-clamps to cruciform Verpine ratchets|Rey lists her tools in her survival guide|<ref name="RSG" />}}
'''Cruciform Verpine ratchets''' were a type of ratchet made by the [[Verpine]]<ref name="RSG" /> [[species]].<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> The [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] began carrying cruciform Verpine ratchets and [[Mon Calamari hex-clamp]]s after finding herself in an [[X-wing starfighter]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]] where she was unable to salvage a cache of [[Battery|batteries]] because she lacked the implements. For at least two [[Standard year|years]]<ref name="Chronicles" /> she carried the ratchets in a bag of quick-switch modulars that she kept in her satchel and included them on a list of her tools in a [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]] she compiled<ref name="RSG" /> by [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'' includes an addendum at the end describing some of the events of {{Film|VII}}, written several [[Standard day|days]] after they take place. The addendum is the final entry in the guide and is included chronologically after the entry mentioning cruciform Verpine ratchets. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the film to [[34 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Cruciform Verpine ratchets were first mentioned in ''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'', a [[2015]] replica journal written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="RSG" />
