'''Eric Johnson''' is an [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]] who portrayed the [[human]] [[Crime lord]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> [[Jaylen Vrax]]<ref name="Sports Illustrated" /> in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Johnson also narrated the [[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon (audiobook)|audiobook version]] of the [[2026]] tie-in novel ''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon]]'' written by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]].<ref name="Low Red Moon">[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon (audiobook)|''Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon'' audiobook]]</ref>
