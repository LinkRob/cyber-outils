'''Body bags''' were used to transport [[Death|deceased]] individuals. By the [[time]] of the [[Imperial Era]], many custom [[bounty hunter]] [[Starship|ships]] stored them for their kills, which were then storied within self-refrigerated storage. Some individuals felt that body bags could always be reliably found on such ships, including the [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]], who acquired one for [[Nakari Kelen]] after her passing.<ref name="HOTJ">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref>
