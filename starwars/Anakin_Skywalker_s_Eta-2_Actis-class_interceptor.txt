During the late [[Clone Wars]], the [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Knight|Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] flew an '''[[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor]]'''. Anakin Skywalker [[pilot]]ed the [[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor|interceptor]] during a mission to rescue [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] during the [[Battle of Coruscant|Battle of]] [[Coruscant]]. The [[starfighter]] was destroyed aboard the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[flagship]] ''[[Invisible Hand]]''.

==History==
===Introduction===
By [[19 BBY]], [[Kuat Systems Engineering]] had introduced the Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor as a successor to the [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7 ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]] and its [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7B variant]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> Replacing [[Anakin Skywalker's Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|his Delta-7B]],<ref name="SWECG" /> Anakin Skywalker was one of the first Jedi to make use of the new starfighter and used it extensively during the late Clone Wars. Like he did with his Delta-7B, painted it with a distinctive bright yellow color scheme, which allegedly was a tribute to the [[Anakin Skywalker's Podracer|Podracer]] he had used to win the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] when he was a child.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" />

===Battle of Cato Neimoidia===
[[File:InterceptorsSoar-S.jpg|left|thumb|Skywalker and Tano leading a squadron in their Eta-2s.|250x250px]]
Skywalker flew his first Eta-2 at the [[Defense of Cato Neimoidia|Battle of Cato Neimoidia]], alongside his padawan [[Ahsoka Tano]] who flew in [[Ahsoka Tano's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|her own Eta-2]]. During the battle, a [[discord missile]], deployed by a [[droid tri-fighter]], released a swarm of [[Pistoeka sabotage droid]]s which latched onto his fighter and disabled it, causing him to lose consciousness. Tano ordered [[R2-D2]] to land the ship on a nearby mountain plain, so that she could jump from her starfighter to rescue him. Tano succeeded just as Skywalker's starfighter went over the cliff to its destruction, but not before R2 escaped.<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> The Eta-2 was recovered and repaired.<ref name="SWECG" />

===Mission to Batuu===
After receiving a message that [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] had gone missing and that her ship had been found on [[Batuu]], Skywalker would fly a new Eta-2, along with R2-D2, to find her. After finding the ship at the [[Black Spire Outpost]], Skywalker and [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], simply known as Thrawn, came up with a plan to capture an assailant. Skywalker took another assailant's body and pretended to have suffered engine damage to his Eta-2. After landing, he was attacked by an [[Assassin droid]] but managed to destroy it. Thrawn would capture the assailant and found his [[freighter]], which belonged to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

===Destruction of the Separatist supply storage base===
When the [[Jedi Order]] received a tip about the location of a [[Separatist supply storage base]] in the interior of an [[asteroid]], Masters [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Quinlan Vos]] requested for Skywalker and another [[Jedi Knight]] named [[Akar-Deshu]] to accompany them in seizing the base and its supplies. The four Jedi flew to the base in their Eta-2's and engaged six [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]] defending it. Skywalker, Kenobi and Vos landed their starfighters at the base, while Desh returned to their [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]] to bring reinforcements.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>

===Destruction of the Vanqor listening station===
After their failure to extract the supplies at the storage base, the [[Jedi High Council]] sent Vos, accompanied by Skywalker and Kenobi, to lead an operation to destroy a [[Vanqor listening station|Separatist listening post]] on the [[planet]] [[Vanqor]]. The three flew in their Eta-2s, alongside twenty-four [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter|Aggressive ReConnaissance-170]] starfighters. Upon arriving at the facility, they discovered it to be abandoned, as [[Dooku|Count Dooku]] had evacuated all personnel before they had arrived. Unbeknownst to the Jedi, Vos had leaked information about the operation to Dooku, as part of his plan to work as a double agent in order to reach [[Darth Sidious]] and destroy the [[Sith]] once and for all.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

===Battle of Anaxes===
Skywalker flew his Eta-2 in the [[Battle of Anaxes]], where he would help to contain the [[Battle droid|droid]] invasion. The Separatists would eventually gain the upper hand, as a result of [[Trench|Admiral Trench]]'s access to a stolen algorithm extracted from [[Clone trooper]] [[Echo]], forcing Skywalker and the other [[Grand Army of the Republic|Republic forces]] to call on [[Clone Force 99]].<ref name="Bad Batch">{{TCW|The Bad Batch (episode)}}</ref> 

===Battle of Coruscant===

[[File:ROTS-RacingthroughCoruscant.png|left|thumb|Skywalker flying his Eta-2 at the Battle of Coruscant.|250x250px]]
After [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] had been kidnapped during the [[Battle of Coruscant]], Skywalker flew in his Eta-2 to rescue him, alongside his master Obi-Wan Kenobi who flew in [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|his own Eta-2]]. During the battle, a vulture droid fired four discord missiles at Skywalker and Kenobi. To shake off the two missiles following him, Skywalker rolled his starfighter continuously, causing the missiles to collide with each other. However, the missiles tailing Kenobi released a swarm of buzz droids that latched onto his starfighter and began shutting down its systems. Skywalker attempted to shoot the droids off Kenobi's starfighter, but ended up blowing off his port-side s-foil, much to Kenobi's displeasure. Skywalker then tried ramming into him to destroy the remaining droids and succeeded. However, one crawled onto his own starfighter. R2-D2 manged to destroy the last droid by electrocuting its central [[photoreceptor]]. Skywalker used the starfighter's [[ion cannon]]s to disable the ''[[Invisible Hand]]''{{'s}} shields, and the pair crash-landed inside the [[dreadnought]]'s [[hangar]] bay. The starfighter was destroyed when the ''Invisible Hand''{{'s}} aft section, containing the hangar, broke off from the front upon entry into [[Coruscant]]'s atmosphere due to the damage it had sustained in the battle.<ref name="RotS" />

Upon becoming Darth Vader, Skywalker would begin using a [[Darth Vader's green Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|green Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor]], to replace the one he lost in the Battle of Coruscant.<ref name="RotS" />

==Crew==
The starfighter was piloted by [[Anakin Skywalker]], who also functioned as its captain. Skywalker‘s [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] functioned as the ship’s navigator and repairer should it be damaged in open combat.<ref name="RotS" />
