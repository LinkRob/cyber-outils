'''Djinn Altis''' was an individual whose name was carved into the walls of safehouses belonging to the [[Hidden Path]] network, which protected [[Force-sensitive]] individuals&mdash;including [[Jedi]]&mdash;from the [[Galactic Empire]] and smuggled them to<ref name="P3">{{Kenobi|Part III}}</ref> the [[planet]]<ref name="Insider 214">{{InsiderCite|214|Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi Companion|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi'' Companion}}</ref> [[Jabiim]], where they would receive new identities in order to hide.<ref name="P3" />

The Hidden Path [[Mapuzo safehouse|safehouse]] on the planet [[Mapuzo]] featured one such carving of their name, [[Writing|written]] in the [[Aurebesh]] script, which [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] saw when she and Jedi [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] [[Battle on Mapuzo|visited]] the safehouse on Mapuzo<ref name="P3" /> in [[9 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Part III]]" and "[[Part V]]" to [[9 BBY]].</ref> A second carving of Altis' name&mdash;also in Aurebesh&mdash;was on the walls of the Jabiim [[Jabiim Hidden Path safehouse|safehouse]]<ref name="PV">{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref> at that [[time]].<ref name="Date" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:InscriptionWall-ObiWanKenobi5.png|thumb|right|250px|The comic adaptation's version of the inscription wall on Jabiim]]
Djinn Altis was first mentioned in Aurebesh in "[[Part III]],"<ref name="P3" /> the third episode of the live-action [[Disney+]] series [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi|''Obi-Wan Kenobi'']], which aired on [[June 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="Insider 214" /> The trivia gallery in the episode guide for "Part III" established that Altis' name was one of the references to Jedi originating in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity whose names were carved into the walls of the Mapuzo safehouse for the episode.<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=obi-wan-kenobi-part-iii-trivia-gallery|text=Part III Trivia Gallery &#124; Obi-Wan Kenobi}}</ref> In the Legends continuity, [[Djinn Altis/Legends|Djinn Altis]] was first mentioned in the [[1995]] novel [[Children of the Jedi (novel)|''Children of the Jedi'']] written by [[Barbara Hambly]]<ref name="Children">[[Children of the Jedi (novel)|''Children of the Jedi'']]</ref> and first appeared in the novel ''[[The Clone Wars: No Prisoners]]'' written by [[Karen Traviss]] and published in [[2009]].<ref name="No Prisoners">''[[The Clone Wars: No Prisoners]]''</ref>

The [[Obi-Wan Kenobi 5|fifth issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi (comic series)|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi'']], which adapts the ''Obi-Wan Kenobi'' series, depicts the Jabiim safehouse wall with inscriptions<ref name="PVComic" /> that the episode establishes included Altis' name,<ref name="P3" /> though the issue does not include Altis' name in a recognizable form. The issue was written by [[Jody Houser]], illustrated by [[Salvador Larroca]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="PVComic">[[Obi-Wan Kenobi 5|''Obi-Wan Kenobi'' 5]]</ref> on [[February 7]], [[2024]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/108448/star_wars_obi-wan_kenobi_2023_5|text=Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi (2023) #5}}</ref>
