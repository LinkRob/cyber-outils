'''Bespin Breeze''' was an [[alcohol]]ic beverage that was served at the [[Singularity (club)|Singularity]] on [[Tellik Four Station]]. Whilst attempting to [[Mission to the Tellik Four Station|extract]] [[Bokk Naarg]] from the [[Space station|station]], [[Gideon Hask]] ordered a [[Bespin]] Breeze and later ordered two more when Naarg and the [[Pantoran]] [[Sharima]] joined him at his table.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bespin Breezes first appeared in [[Christie Golden]]'s [[2017]] novel ''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]''.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
