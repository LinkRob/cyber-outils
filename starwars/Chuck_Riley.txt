'''Chuck Riley''' (born [[1940s|1940]]; died [[May 10]], [[2007]]) was an [[Wikipedia:American|American]] [[Wikipedia:Voice actor|voice artist]] who voiced thousands of toy commercials and hundreds of audiobooks.<ref name="Obit" />

For [[Star Wars]], Riley began by narrating the [[1983]] ''[[The Story of Star Wars: Return of the Jedi]]'' audio dramatization of the [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|film]].<ref name="Story of RotJ">''[[The Story of Star Wars: Return of the Jedi]]''</ref> In [[1997]], Riley narrated the three read&ndash;along storybook adaptions of the [[Original trilogy]].<ref name="ANH RA">''[[A New Hope Read-Along]]''</ref> The same audio from these read&ndash;along storybooks were reused for the [[2015]] read&ndash;along storybook adaptions.<ref name="ANH RA Storybook">''[[A New Hope Read-Along Storybook and CD]]''</ref>
