{{Quote|The Sith warrior Darth Maul fought with a rare double-bladed lightsaber – an ungainly weapon that compelled him to push his physical and Force abilities to their highest potential.|The ''[[Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''|<ref name="Galactic Explorer's Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref>}}
'''Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber''', also known as '''Maul's saberstaff''', was the first personal [[double-bladed lightsaber]] of [[Maul|Darth Maul]], a [[Sith Lord]] who took part in the [[Invasion of Naboo]] and the [[Clone Wars]]. The [[Sith lightsaber]] was cut in half by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] during their duel on Naboo, and Maul continued to use the remaining half in battle until he temporarily lost it in his [[Mandalorian Civil War (19 BBY)|duel]] with his [[Darth Sidious|former master]] on [[Mandalore]]. He recovered the half after being freed from prison, and reformed it into a double-bladed hilt but lost it again after a duel with [[Ahsoka Tano]]. The saber was recovered and he modified it to be a [[split saber]], but it was eventually replaced by [[Maul's lightsaber|another double-bladed lightsaber]] that he would use up until his death.

==Description==
The saberstaff consisted of two lightsabers fused together so the blades extend in a single line. Each blade could be ignited separately with blade modulation controls depending on the situation and the opponent being faced. Its design was based on ancient plans found deep within a [[Sith holocron]] owned by [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /><ref name="Lightsabers" /> While capable of being separated into two single lightsabers,<ref name="Life Size" /> it was not a [[split saber]] initially,<ref name="Episode I" /> though was later modified to become one.<ref name="Whispers" />

==History==
===Usage as Sidious's disciple===
After learning to use a [[double-bladed lightsaber]],<ref name="Darth Maul II">[[Darth Maul (2017) 2|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 2]]</ref> [[Sith apprentice]] Maul built his own based on a design found in [[Darth Sidious]]' [[Sith holocron]].<ref name="Lightsabers" /> Maul used his lightsaber in his [[Skirmish on the moon of Drazkel|duel]] with [[Eldra Kaitis]] on the [[moon of Drazkel]].<ref name="Darth Maul V">[[Darth Maul (2017) 5|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 5]]</ref>

[[File:DarthMaul-TSSOCE.png|thumb|right|220px|Maul wielding his double-bladed lightsaber on Naboo.]]
Darth Maul used his lightsaber when he [[Duel on Tatooine|dueled]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] on [[Tatooine]]. Igniting only one end of his weapon, Maul quickly pressed the attack, but his apprentice [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] had the Queen's pilot guide the ship over towards the duel; allowing Jinn to leap aboard and escape. Maul was left fuming on the desert floor. During the [[Battle of Naboo|Battle]] of [[Naboo]], Maul used his lightsaber against Jinn and Kenobi. Maul was able to defeat Jinn by striking him under the chin with his weapon's hilt before stabbing the stunned [[Jedi Master]] through the stomach; mortally wounding him. Kenobi then attacked Maul in a rage and managed to land a hit on his lightsaber hilt, bisecting the weapon and knocking the nonfunctional half into the wall. This left Maul with only one functional half of it. Despite this, Maul was able to push Kenobi and kick his [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's first lightsaber|lightsaber]] into a pit and used his lightsaber to shower sparks at the young Jedi and force him to let go. When Maul lowered his guard, Kenobi saw his chance and jumped towards him. Using [[Qui-Gon Jinn's lightsaber|Jinn's lightsaber]], Kenobi seemingly killed Maul by cutting him in half. Maul and his lightsaber fell down the shaft, presumed dead and lost.<ref name="Episode I" />

===Maul's return===
====Return of the Sith====
[[File:Aphra37-Maultextless.png|thumb|left|250px|Maul returns with the functioning half of his formerly double-bladed lightsaber]]
In truth, Maul survived and was found on [[Lotho Minor]] by his brother, [[Savage Opress]],<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref> who returned Maul to Dathomir to be healed by their mother, [[Talzin]]. Maul's lightsaber was returned to him by his brother and he used it in his quest for vengeance against Kenobi.<ref name="Revenge" />

Maul used it to [[Massacre on Raydonia|kill]] innocent villagers on [[Raydonia]] in order to bring Kenobi to him. When Kenobi arrived, Maul and his brother fought and subdued him and planned to use his lightsaber to torture Kenobi until [[Asajj Ventress]] arrived. Maul and his brother fought Kenobi and Ventress until they escaped.<ref name="Revenge" />

====Outer Rim swath====
Maul continued to use this lightsaber as he and his brother cut a swath across the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Maul used his lightsaber [[Duel at unidentified Outer Rim spaceport|against]] Jedi Knight [[Finn Ertay]] and an [[unidentified Nikto Jedi]] at an [[Unidentified Outer Rim spaceport|Outer Rim spaceport]], murdering the former by running her through from behind whilst she was distracted. Following his duel with the two Jedi, Maul used his lightsaber to cut through several [[Spaceport police droid]]s at the [[Cybloc Transfer Station]] in order to raid the station. After finding a vault, Maul used his lightsaber against his brother in order to prove himself as the master and Savage, his apprentice.<ref name="Revival">{{TCW|Revival (episode)}}</ref>

At [[Florrum]], Maul used his lightsaber to force several pirates under [[Jiro]] of Captain [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s [[Ohnaka Gang|gang]] to join him. After killing [[Sabo|one]] of Hondo's loyal lieutenants, Maul fought Kenobi while Savage fought [[Adi Gallia]].<ref name="Revival" />

====Rise to power====
[[File:SidiousVsMaulSavage-TL.png|thumb|right|280px|Maul used his lightsaber against his former Master at Sundari Royal Palace.]]
When Maul and Opress were found by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Death Watch]], [[Pre Vizsla]] took Maul's lightsaber as the two Zabraks recovered. Maul was given back his lightsaber after Vizsla agreed to an alliance between Death Watch and Maul. Maul used his lightsaber against the various criminals of [[Black Sun]] and the [[Hutt Clan]], as he was planning to form a united criminal organization, the [[Shadow Collective]].<ref name="Eminence" />

Following the Shadow Collective's [[Takeover of Mandalore|takeover]] of [[Mandalore]], the Death Watch betrayed Maul and Savage and imprisoned them, with [[Bo-Katan Kryze|Bo-Katan]] wearing Maul's lightsaber on her belt. After escaping from the [[Royal prison|Sundari prison]], Maul challenged Vizsla to a duel to the death, and Vizsla order Katan to give Maul his weapon so Maul can duel Vizsla. Despite losing it during their duel when Vizsla shot it out of his hand, Maul was able to defeat Vizsla and use Vizsla's [[Darksaber]] to behead him.<ref name="SoR">{{TCW|Shades of Reason}}</ref>

====Dueling Sidious====
Following Kenobi's escape from Mandalore, Maul wielded both his lightsaber and the Darksaber against his former master, Darth Sidious. Consequently, Maul lost both his lightsaber and the Darksaber when Sidious subdued and captured him.<ref name="The Lawless">{{TCW|The Lawless}}</ref>

====Reforge and loss====
Maul later recovered both weapons, although he used the Darksaber for a brief time before he eventually recovered his half function saberstaff. He reforged the end of his original lightsaber into a second blade to create another double-bladed weapon which he used during his duel with [[Ahsoka Tano]] during the [[Siege of Mandalore]]. At the conclusion of the duel, a disarmed Ahsoka grappled with Maul, cutting the catwalk he was standing on and causing him to fall. However, Maul was saved by Ahsoka and subsequently taken into custody by her [[332nd Company|forces]] while the lightsaber plummeted to the city below.<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice">{{TCW|The Phantom Apprentice}}</ref> 

[[Rook Kast]] and Mandalorian loyalists later recovered the saber, which Maul modified<ref name="Shadow Lord Trivia">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-maul-shadow-lord-trivia|text=Inside the Making of ''Star Wars: Maul – Shadow Lord''}}</ref> to be a [[split saber]], and continued to use during his time on [[Janix]]. On one mission he used the new function to [[Assassination of Marg Krim|assassinate]] [[Marg Krim]] for his betrayal; lending the original half to [[Scorn]] and when Krim noticed the ruse he was quickly killed and slaughtered most of his guards, reclaiming control of the [[Pyke Syndicate]].<ref name="Whispers" /> He later used his saberstaff to fight the [[Inquisitor]]s and the [[TK stormtrooper]]s in a underground passage. After everyone made it to the other side via [[skiff]] or was killed, Maul used the split function against the [[First Brother]] and [[Eleventh Brother]] while fighting underground in an [[acid]] water. After getting into the forest, Maul witness his [[lieutenant]] Kast get killed by the Inquisitor's master [[Darth Vader]] before engaging him with the Jedi.<ref name="Strange Allies">{{MSL|Chapter 9: Strange Allies}}</ref> He used his saberstaff to fight the Sith Lord, but proved to be outmatched by Vader's skill. After escaping, he used the splitting function again when he gifted [[Devon Izara]] the original half to fight the Eleventh Brother after witnessing Darth Vader killing her Jedi Master [[Eeko-Dio Daki]].<ref name="Chapter10" />

Eventually he replaced his original saber with<ref name="Lightsaber Collection" /> an [[Maul's lightsaber|Inquisitor lightsaber]],<ref name="Star Wars Show">{{StarWarsShow|QdRgY2gtxgk|Sam Witwer on Voicing Maul and Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge News!|sw_url=video/sam-witwer-on-voicing-maul-and-star-wars-galaxy-s-edge-news}}</ref> which he used up to his [[death]] at the hands of Kenobi on Tatooine.<ref name="Lightsaber Collection" />

==Behind the scenes==
In an interview with ''[[The Star Wars Show]]'', [[Sam Witwer]] explained that the production of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' originally planned to reintroduce Maul's double-bladed lightsaber during his scene with [[Qi'ra]]. Witwer, however, suggested using either the [[Darksaber]] or his ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' [[Maul's lightsaber|lightsaber]], with the latter ultimately being used.<ref name="Star Wars Show">{{StarWarsShow|QdRgY2gtxgk|Sam Witwer on Voicing Maul and Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge News!|sw_url=video/sam-witwer-on-voicing-maul-and-star-wars-galaxy-s-edge-news}}</ref>

During Star Wars Celebration Europe 2016 Ray Park revealed that originally Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber was the same size as a regular single hilt lightsaber.

The new second half of the saber seen in Season 7 of ''The Clone Wars'' shares the appearance of concept art for [[Asajj Ventress's lightsaber|Ventress's lightsaber]], that ultimately went unused due to the episodes in question turning into the novel ''[[Dark Disciple]]''.<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice" />
