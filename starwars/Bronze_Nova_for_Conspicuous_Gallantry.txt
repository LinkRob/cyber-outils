The '''Bronze Nova for Conspicuous Gallantry''' was an award handed out by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. [[Rebel pilot]] [[Shara Bey]] earned this award for her actions during the [[Liberation of Gorma]].<ref name="Before the Awakening">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY">{{SW|url=news/shadow-fall-author-interview|text=In ''Shadow Fall'', the Dark Secrets of ''Alphabet Squadron'' Revealed}} establishes that ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'' takes place approximately half a [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], while ''[[Before the Awakening]]'' states [[Kes Dameron]] and [[Shara Bey]] moved to [[Yavin 4]] around six [[Standard month|months]] after the Battle of [[Endor]]. Given that ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates their move to [[5 ABY]], the events of ''Shadow Fall'' must also take place around that [[time]].</ref> [[Alphabet Squadron]] pilot [[Nath Tensent]] of the [[New Republic]] was awarded it after he and his droid [[T5]] dove their [[BTL Y-wing starfighter]] into a [[missile]] launched by [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing|Shadow Wing]]. He wore it to a party a few days after the [[Troithe operation]] wrapped up.<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Bronze Nova was first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Before the Awakening]]'.<ref name="Before the Awakening" />
