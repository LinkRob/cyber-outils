'''Barbara Hambly''' is the author of the ''Star Wars'' novels [[Children of the Jedi (novel)|''Children of the Jedi'']] and ''[[Planet of Twilight]]'' published by [[Bantam Spectra]] in 1995 and 1997, respectively. She also wrote short stories for ''[[Tales from the Mos Eisley Cantina]]'' and ''[[Tales from Jabba's Palace]]'', also published by Bantam Spectra in 1995, and for the ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'', which was published by [[West End Games]] in 1997.
