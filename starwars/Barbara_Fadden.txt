'''Barbara Fadden''' is an actor and creature performer who portrayed multiple characters in several ''[[Star Wars]]'' films. In {{Film|VII}}, she portrayed a "panda-like creature." In ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', she portrayed [[Faddera Rabar]]. In {{Film|VIII}}, she portrayed one of the attendants of [[Snoke]]. In {{Film|IX}}, she portrayed [[Fadda-Rarb Arda]], [[Faddaff Davenspon]], and [[Odla Sur]].<ref name="Official Website">{{WebCite|url=https://www.barbarafadden.com|text=Barbara Fadden &ndash; Creature Performer|author=Fadden, Barbara|archivedate=20220404171843}}</ref>
