'''Dodgeball''' was a sporting activity sometimes played by [[Padawan/Legends|students]] at the [[Jedi academy]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. As the name suggested, it involved attempting to dodge a thrown ball. [[Kitmum]], the [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] coach for [[Physical Education]], would sometimes lead students in sessions of dodgeball. [[Roan Novachez]] would later credit skills in dodgeball with helping him in a [[Foodstuff/Legends|food]] fight.<ref name="return">''[[Jedi Academy: Return of the Padawan]]''</ref> Despite being a new pupil the academy and claiming to have never played before, [[Lilly]] proved an apt player of dodgeball.<ref name="phantom">''[[Jedi Academy: The Phantom Bully]]''</ref>
