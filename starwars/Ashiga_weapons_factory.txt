The '''Ashiga weapons factory''' was a [[weapons factory]] located in the caverns on [[planet]] [[Kijimi]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Before [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Ashiga Clan]] had been making [[blaster rifle]]s and [[vibroblade]]s at the factory for generations. In 3 ABY, the leader of the clan, [[Queen]] [[Ashiga]], repurposed the factory to produce explosives for the [[Galactic Empire]]. Some members of the clan viewed the deal with the Empire as a betrayal of the clan's values.<ref name="Outlaws" />

In 35 ABY, the weapons factory was destroyed along with the planet during the [[Destruction of Kijimi]] by the [[Sith Eternal]] with the [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Derriphan]]'' of the [[Final Order]] [[fleet]].<ref name="Destruction" />
