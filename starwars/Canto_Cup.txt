The '''Canto Cup''' was a [[Fathier racing|fathier race]] held in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. One Canto Cup was held<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> At least one race of the cup was sponsored by the [[Galactic Empire]], who with the help of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] rigged the race to ensure it's imperial representative, Vinis Karyvin, would win it.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{Event-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Canto Cup appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and the [[Ubisoft]]. It is part of an optional activity that the player can [[gambling|bet]] on to win [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. The player can find intel on the rigged races allowing them to ensure they will win their bets.<ref name="Outlaws" />
