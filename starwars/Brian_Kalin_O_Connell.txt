'''Brian Kalin O&#39;Connell''' is one of the episodic directors of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series.

==Biography==
Brian Kalin O'Connell attended school at [[Wikipedia:Otis College of Art and Design|Otis College of Art and Design]] from [[1988]] to [[1990]]. Five years later, he began work as a conceptual, character, and creature artist at [[Industrial Light & Magic]], where he stayed from [[1995]] to [[2006]], before moving to a director position at [[Lucasfilm Animation]] for ''The Clone Wars'' series.<ref name="LinkedIn">{{LinkedIn|url=in/brian-kalin-o-connell-4b55107|text=Brian Kalin O'Connell|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/opbC}}</ref>
