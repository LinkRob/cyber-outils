The '''Daystar Casino''' was a [[casino]] built on [[Ahakista]]. The [[Dug]] [[gambler]] [[Gronson Takkaro]] worked as the casino's operations manager for a period of time, before his debts forced him to flee the [[Ahakista system]].<ref name="EzraGamble" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Daystar Casino was first mentioned in the [[2014]] middle grade book ''[[Ezra's Gamble]]'' by [[Ryder Windham]].<ref name="EzraGamble" /> The casino was later mentioned in an entry in [[Jet Kordo]]'s journal in the [[2024]] [[Ubisoft]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
