The '''Dresscol system''' was located in the [[Trax sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]],<ref name="The Slice" /> situated in grid square Q-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was among the many places to which the [[Galactic Empire]] sent [[probe droid]]s in [[Project Swarm|search]] of the [[Echo Base|hidden base]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dresscol was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]'', a [[2021]] adaptation of ''Star Wars''{{'}} [[original trilogy]].<ref name="Odyssey" /> It was then identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Dresscol system/Legends|Dresscol system]] was first mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Kit]]'', a [[1991]] supplement for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[Bill Smith]] and [[Ed Stark]].<ref name="GamemasterKit">''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Kit]]''</ref>
