'''Dren Feelu''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Moisture farmer/Legends|moisture farmer]] who lived on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]. Sometime following the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]], Dren requested a spacer with retrieving his [[harvester droid]], a retasked [[LIN demolitionmech autonomous minelayer/Legends|LIN demolitionmech]], that was stolen by [[Jawa/Legends|Jawas]]. His friend [[Lyrissa]] later helped the spacer to fix the droid.<ref name="SWG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dren Feelu appears in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']], a [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online roleplaying game]] developed by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment and published by [[LucasArts]],<ref name="SWG" /> prior to its closure on [[December 15]], [[2011]].<ref name="Shutdown">{{SonyCite|url=playerinfo|text=IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT ''STAR WARS'' GALAXIES™}}</ref> According to these files, introduced with the [[2005]] "[[W:c:swg:New Game Enhancements|New Game Enhancements]]," Dren would have been involved in a quest named "Fixing Jawa" on Tatooine. However, he is not known to have appeared on the game's live servers as a proper [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]]. Nevertheless, an emulated depiction pulled from this character's Interchange File Format reveals what he would have looked like if he were added to the game. This character is also named '''Dren Fetix''' in several game files. The game's creature database erroneously identifies this character with the [[Droid/Legends|droid]] name '''MX-C1'''.<ref name="SWG" />
