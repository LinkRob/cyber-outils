The '''Battle of Sedratis''' took place during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Heir to the Jedi" /> a conflict that pitted the [[Galactic Republic]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref> [[Jedi General]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Huulik]] led the forces of the [[Republic Military]] to victory in the battle. [[Luke Skywalker]] learned of the battle and his [[Parent|father's]] involvement in it [[Standard decade|decades]] later during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Heir to the Jedi" />

==The battle==
[[File:Sabotage-Anakincockpit.png|thumb|left|300px|Anakin Skywalker (pictured during a piloting operation) took part in the Battle of Sedratis.]]
The leaders of the [[Republic Military|Republic forces]] included the [[Jedi Knight]]s and ace [[pilot]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Huulik]], onboard their [[Jedi]] [[starfighter]]s. At some point during the battle, the Jedi found themselves swarmed by Confederate [[Droid starfighter|droid fighters]], and the [[Deflector shield|energy shields]] of Huulik's [[Starship|starfighter]] were depleted, making him vulnerable to the enemy ships' attacks. However, Skywalker flew in-between him and the next blast from the droid fighters, saving the life of his fellow [[Jedi]]. The battle ultimately resulted in a victory of the Republic. Following their adventures at Sedratis, Skywalker and Huulik came to fight together on several other occasions.<ref name="Heir to the Jedi" />

==Aftermath==
About two [[Standard decade|decades]] after the event, Huulik's niece [[Taneetch Soonta]] shared a brief account of the Battle of Sedratis with the son of Skywalker, a [[Padawan|trainee Jedi]] called [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]].<ref name="Heir to the Jedi" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Sedratis was first mentioned in ''[[Heir to the Jedi]]'', a [[2015]] novel written by [[Kevin Hearne]].<ref name="Heir to the Jedi" />
