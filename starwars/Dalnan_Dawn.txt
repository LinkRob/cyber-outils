The '''''Dalnan Dawn''''' was a [[Nihil]] [[stormcruiser]] owned by [[Ithorian]] [[scientist]] [[Baron]] [[Boolan]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="The High Republic 9" /> It shared its name with the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Dalna]] where Boolan had been a [[Littles|member]] of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] as a [[Youngling|child]].<ref name="Path of Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-show-302-reveals|text=Burryaga's Fate and More Reveals on ''Star Wars: The High Republic Show''}} establish that the events of [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']], including [[The High Republic (2023) 9|''The High Republic'' (2023) 9]], take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]].</ref> Boolan used the ''Dalnan Dawn'' to escape from his base of operations, [[Shingcu Station]], after it was [[Rescue of a Yacombe girl|attacked]] by a [[Jedi Order|Jedi]] team.<ref name="The High Republic 9" />
