During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Marshal]] [[Phara]] led her [[Phara's faction|faction]] in a '''coup''' against the ruling [[Vallt's government|government]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Vallt]]. Phara's rebellious ideals were already public knowledge on Vallt, with it being believed she lacked the resources to successfully rebel against [[King]] [[Chai (king)|Chai]]. Indeed, Phara was only able to launch her attack when she allied her faction with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], which was interested in Vallt after learning the renowned [[scientist]] [[Galen Walton Erso]] was working on the planet for [[Zerpen Industries]].<ref name="Catalyst" />
