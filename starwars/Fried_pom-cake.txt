'''Fried pom-cake''' was a type of [[cake]] that was [[Color|golden]] in color and [[Diet|edible]] by [[human]]s and [[Iktotchi]].<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|DFS}}</ref> the humans [[Jordanna Sparkburn]] and [[Sylvestri Yarrow]] invited [[Jedi Master]] [[Mirro Lox]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Amadeo Azzazzo]], to a meal at the [[Pop's canteen|canteen]] on the [[Republic Defense Coalition mobile launch and repair station]] to discuss their friend [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] and the [[Stormwall]], a barrier [[Deployment of the stormseeds|created]] by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] that prevented travel through [[hyperspace]]. During the meal, [[Pop Teeno]], the Iktotchi who ran the [[Cantina|canteen]], served the group fried pom-cakes with [[spider seed]] and scale flakes from [[Ithorian cactus]]es, which he said added a delicate flavor and went well with the fluff of the cake. Sparkburn began eating her cake, which was [[Temperature|hot]] enough to burn her [[Mouth|upper palate]], immediately after being served, while Azzazzo focused on pulling apart one of the [[seed]]pods that had been fried into his cake.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />
