'''Baron''' was a title of [[Noble|nobility]] in the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="Senate Spy" /> [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="New Dawn" /> and on various worlds. People holding the title of baron included [[Senator]] [[Rush Clovis]],<ref name="Senate Spy" /> [[Lero Danthe]],<ref name="New Dawn" /> [[Supreme Chairman]] [[Notluwiski Papanoida]],<ref name="DBPantora" /> and the individual known as [[The Cyber-Baron]].<ref name="SWU" /> Individuals who controlled [[mining]] operations were sometimes known as "mining barons."<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>

Certain [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot]]s were named [[Baron of the Empire|Barons of the Empire]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> Baron [[Elrik Vonreg]] was a [[First Order TIE fighter pilot]] [[ace]] who held the rank of [[major]] and flew the crimson-[[color]]ed [[TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor]].<ref name="Echoes of War">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/8/1/echoes-of-war|text=Echoes of War}}</ref>
