An '''electrostatic barrier''', or '''electrostatic shield'''<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref> was a type of shield that served as the first line of defense aboard warships of the peoples of the Unknown Regions up to the end of the [[Clone Wars]]. One such example was [[Chiss]] [[Chiss Defense Force|Defense Force]] [[warship]]s such as the ''[[Springhawk]]'', although electrostatic barriers were also employed by the [[Paataatus Hiveborn]] and the [[Nikardun Destiny]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> They were also used by ships of the [[Grysk Hegemony]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref>

Shortly after the [[attack on the Paataatus]], [[Ufsa'mak'ro]] supervised checks on the ''Springhawk''{{'s}} electrostatic barrier. Although similar in purpose to the [[Deflector shield|shields]] used by the [[Galactic Republic]], electrostatic barriers were much weaker.<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> This led [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] to seek out a so-called "Republic energy shield" for his people during his [[mission to Batuu]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> During the [[battle over Primea]], [[Nikardun Destiny|Nikardun]] General [[Yiv]] the Benevolent attacked Thrawn's ''[[Springhawk]]'', expecting the [[Chiss heavy cruiser|cruiser]] to only be protected by an electrostatic barrier. However, the ''Springhawk'' was outfitted with the much stronger [[Deflector shield generator|Republic energy shield]], helping the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] win the battle and end the [[Nikardun conquests]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

The ''[[Whetstone]]'' and the ''[[Anvil]]'', both [[Kilji war cruiser]]s, used electrostatic barriers. [[Kilji picket cruiser]] also used them.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>
