'''Akshaya Ponta '''was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] cargo [[pilot]] that lived on the [[planet]] [[Skuhl]]. She was the [[Parent|mother]] of two children, [[Tanith Ponta|Tanith]] and [[Hadder Ponta]]. When [[Jyn Erso]] was looking for transportation, Akshaya took her to Skuhl for the price of fixing her broken [[droid]]. Akshaya would look after Erso for some time before she and Hadder were [[Death|killed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]].

==Biography==
===Life on Skuhl===
For many [[Standard year|years]], Akshaya lived in a [[Ponta house|house]] on the [[planet]] [[Skuhl]] with her [[Hadder Ponta's father|husband]], her daughter [[Tanith Ponta|Tanith]], and her son [[Hadder Ponta|Hadder]]. She worked as a freelance cargo [[pilot]], often carrying [[ore]] for [[mining]] operations.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

When Akshaya's [[Marriage|husband]] [[Death|died]], she started letting Tanith pilot the family's [[shuttle]]. Tanith's flying later resulted in the terminal illness [[bloodburn]]. To keep her disease in check, Tanith took [[Hadeira serum]], and became addicted to the substance. Tanith died of an overdose in [[15 BBY]]. After Tanith's death, Akshaya became paranoid of Hadder contracting the disease. She began forbidding her son from flying, and developed the idea that no young person should, either.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

===Taking in Jyn Erso===
{{Quote|It wasn't that long ago when my son cooked for me, but now there's a pretty girl to distract him.''"<br />"''Yup.|Akshaya and Hadder Ponta on the new family dynamics Jyn introduced|<ref name="Rebel Rising" />}}
While Akshaya was on a cargo run, she encountered the sixteen-year old [[Jyn Erso]]. Unknown to Akshaya, the girl had recently been abandoned on [[Tamsye Prime]] by [[Saw Gerrera]]. Jyn was desperate for a ride offworld, and lied that she could fix Ponta's broken [[droid]]. She proved completely unable to repair the [[Astromech droid|astromech]], but paid for her passage by forging clearance codes. Akshaya pitied Jyn, and allowed her to stay at her home on Skuhl, giving her Tanith's old room upstairs.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

When the [[Galactic Empire]] arrived on Skuhl, Akshaya's business was disrupted. Although Hadder and Jyn advocated for leaving Skuhl due to the Imperial presence, Akshaya wanted to stay and not leave behind her life. However, she eventually agreed to go. The same [[Standard day|day]] as they were going to leave, [[stormtrooper]]s showed up at their house searching for Jyn. She had just managed to escape undetected but the stormtroopers took Akshaya and Hadder as [[prisoner]]s instead, while setting their house on [[fire]]. Jyn had taken the family's [[planet hopper]], and attacked the stormtroopers holding Akshaya and Hadder, rescuing them. Akshaya and Hadder then took the family's other ship, the [[freighter]] ''[[Ponta One]]''. Together they flew up in the sky past a dogfight between rebels and [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] intending to fly to [[Five Points]] [[Space station|Station]]. Unknown to Jyn, Akshaya's and Hadder's [[Starship|ship]] was destroyed right before she flew into [[hyperspace]], killing both of them.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

==Personality and traits==
Akshaya Ponta was a firm pacifist, disapproving of all weaponry. When Jyn first entered her ship with a [[blaster]] in her pack, Akshaya insisted on confiscating it, saying she was "a peaceful operation." She was also known to be very forgiving and motherly, ignoring Jyn's checkered history and sympathizing with her.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

Akshaya was known for her hobby of painting [[mandalas]] on floors, ceilings, and any spare surface. She stopped this practice for a period of [[time]] after her daughter died, but eventually started again, saying it was calming.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
