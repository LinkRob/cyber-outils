"'''Acolyte'''" may refer to:

==[[Canon]]==
*'''[[Dark Acolyte]]''', a role held by [[Asajj Ventress]] and [[Savage Opress]] as they were trained under [[Dooku]]
*'''[[Forbidden Acolytes]]''', a term used by the self-crowned [[Yathros Condorius the First]] of [[Cloud City]]
*The '''[[Acolytes of the Beyond]]''', a [[Sith cult]] that emerged following [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[death]] at the [[Battle of Endor]]
*'''[[Nightsisters|Nightsister acolyte]]s'''
*'''[[Sith acolyte]]''', an [[agent]] of the [[Sith]]

==[[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]]==
*'''[[Sith acolyte/Legends|Sith acolyte]]''', a term for individuals beginning their training prior to becoming a [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord's]] proper [[Sith apprentice/Legends|Sith apprentice]]
*[[Dark Acolyte/Legends|'''Dark Acolyte''']], a role held by the many [[Dark Jedi/Legends|Dark Jedi]] under [[Dooku/Legends|Dooku]]
*'''[[Imperial Sith Acolyte]]''', [[Soldier/Legends|soldiers]] used by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]
