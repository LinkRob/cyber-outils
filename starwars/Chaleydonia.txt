'''Chaleydonia''', also known as the '''Crystal City''',<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> was the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Christophsis]]. It was a modern city<ref name="The Visual Encyclopedia" /> made out of organic [[crystal]] construction. [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and [[Asajj Ventress]] had their [[Second battle of Christophsis|final confrontation]] at a [[Christophsis Separatist tower|tower]] in the city.<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> The [[Arrests on Christophsis|pacification of Christophsis]] in [[3 BBY]]<ref name="3 BBY Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', which include the [[arrests on Christophsis]], in [[3 BBY]].</ref> saw three [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyers]] [[Orbital bombardment|bombard]] the city from [[orbit]].<ref name="RiseFall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>
