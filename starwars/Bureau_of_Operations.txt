The '''Bureau of Operations''', or simply known as the '''Bureau''', was a branch of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Intelligence]] organization that handled important covert operations that were beyond the standard operating scope of local resources. It had a variety of responsibilities, including monitoring enemy activity, industrial and commercial surveillance, [[Diplomat|diplomacy]] issues, and occasionally the destabilization of entities and implementation of [[assassin]]ations.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bureau of Operations was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2018]].<ref name="Dawn" /> The [[Bureau of Operations/Legends|Bureau]] also existed in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was introduced in the [[1989]] ''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]'', which was authored by [[Greg Gorden]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="IS">''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]''</ref> It performed a similar role of carrying out covert operations and performing surveillance.<ref name="Imperial Handbook">''[[Star Wars: Imperial Handbook: A Commander's Guide]]''</ref>
