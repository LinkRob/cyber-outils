'''''Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising'' 3''' also stylized as '''''The Battle of Jakku &mdash; Insurgency Rising'' 3''', is the third issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] limited series ''[[Star Wars: Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising]]''. The issue was written by [[Alex Segura]], illustrated by [[Leonard Kirk]], [[Jethro Morales]], and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 30]], 2024.

Does the DEFIANT IMPERIAL have an INQUISITOR by his side?! Our villain consolidates his power&mdash;but at what cost? An iconic, familiar face arrives to save the captured heroes!''

==Plot summary==
On the fringes of the [[Anoat sector]], an elite strike force of [[New Republic]] operatives find their [[starship|ship]], the [[Barloz-class medium freighter|''Barloz''-class medium freighter]] ''[[Oggrud]]'', surrounded by vessels of the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]]. The operatives were sent by [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] to investigate the reports coming from the [[sector]] of a rogue [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Moff]] and his supposed consolidation of power. One Spice Runner, a [[Klatooinian]] named [[Vigilch]], orders the ''Oggrud'' to allow the Spice Runners to board or be destroyed. He adds, however, that they won't take any prisoners. 

Obviously skeptical of what sounds like a losing game, [[Therisa Alern]] and [[Kes Dameron]] deduce that the Spice Runners have been hired by Moff [[Ubrik Adelhard]]. Meanwhile, [[pilot]] [[Rynn Zenat]] begins plotting an unconventional escape, hoping to leverage their disguise as a [[merchant]] ship to help confound their enemy. She replies to the comms, claiming they are fellow [[spice]] runners passing through a corner of the sector as a short cut. She then offers to cut the Spice Runners of Kijimi in on the deal. 

Leader of the Spice Runners of Kijimi, [[Zeva Bliss]], is unconvinced by the New Republic strike team's bluff. [[Tomasso]], Vigilch, and [[Marinda Gan]] chime in on the debate of what to do. Gan seems to be the most trusting of the ''Oggrud'' crew's claim, skeptical of their alliance with Adelhard in the first place. Her derisive words beget a swift strike to the face from Bliss. The leader decides to contact Adelhard to alert him of their status, thinking he will want to board what is likely a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] ship. 

Meanwhile, on the ''Oggrud'', the strike team remains wary of their precarious situation. Zenat then reveals to her squad mates her contingency plan. Aboard the ''[[Ragged Claw]]'', the Spice Runners notice their prey quickly lurching straight towards them at an alarming rate. The Spice Runners veer out of the way to avoid the explosive ramming maneuver employed by the ''Oggrud''.

On [[Chandrila]], the provisional home of the New Republic, [[Agent]] [[Calder Behrens]] receives a [[hologram]] call from intelligence operative [[Buccel Trune]] alerting him to the alignment of the Spice Runners of Kijimi with Moff Adelhard. Behrens immediately recognizes the peril this puts their nascent government in. He is interrupted by a summons from [[Mon Mothma]] herself. He joins her and Leia to discuss even more bad news: "The [[Gallius Rax|Operator]]" provided them with more reports of desperate Imperial attacks on New Republic Targets. He informs the Princess of the bad news about the Anoat sector, which spells likely doom for her strike team. 

On Adelhard's [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] [[Scepter (Imperial-class)|''Scepter'']], [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Kith Alaytia]] informs Adelhard of the significant damage caused to the Spice Runners of Kijimi after Zenat's maneuver, which involved using a large cache of powerful explosives hidden in the freighter, effectively making the ''Oggrud'' into a [[bomb]]. Adelhard strains to see the bigger picture of the event, confident it is not a simple act of terror. The two Imperials are then approached by the [[Anzati]] mystic [[Reyna Oskure]], who believes the perpetrators to be alive. She and Adelhard soon commence an argument in which Oskure claims Adelhard needs to consider [[the Force]] in order to win, evoking the powers of [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Adelhard is dismissive, particularly as he sees the role of the Force in the [[Sith|Sith's]] downfall. Before their argument reaches a head, however, Alaytia informs Adelhard that the New Republic strike team has been found and captured. 

Back on Chandrila, Leia speaks with her brother [[Luke Skywalker]] about her guilt for likely sending the strike team to their [[death]]s. She feels guilty for even having the power to decide whether people live or die. Additionally, she struggles with the lack of finality after the victory at [[Battle of Endor|Endor]], how there is already a new darkness in the form of Adelhard. Luke comforts his sister, reminding her that even in complete darkness, they must look in order to find the light and save their people.

In a cell on the ''Scepter'', the New Republic strike team argues about the lack of transparency between the team members, particularly the secrecy of the bombs. Alern defends Zenat's actions, but also calls for them to spring into action, a seemingly pointless gesture when locked in a cell. Alern is soon proven to be rightfully confident when the cell door is opened by Kith Alaytia, who is in league with Alern and the New Republic. As the strike team makes their escape from the cell block, they are ambushed by Adelhard's second in command, [[Commander]] [[Bragh]]. He viciously pummels Dameron before Alern can call for the others to scatter. The strike team's strategy is foiled by the arrival of an individual claiming to be an [[Inquisitor]], brandishing their signature [[double-bladed spinning lightsaber]]. The Inquisitor slices into Alern's lateral torso, and helps Bragh incapacitate the rebel scum. 

In the aftermath of the melee, Bragh thanks the shadowy figure for their help. When he states his intention to deliver the escaped prisoners to Adelhard, the Inquisitor mentions their preference to show Adelhard the power now at his fingertips: The power of the [[dark side of the Force]]. Elsewhere on the ship, another shadowy figure sneaks around, brandishing a [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber|green lightsaber]]. He takes out two [[stormtrooper]]s, seeing through the darkness and into the light. It is none other than Luke Skywalker.
