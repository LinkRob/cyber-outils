"'''Creating Clones!'''" is an article appearing in [[Star Wars Insider 107|''Star Wars Insider'' 107]]. It was written by [[Jonathan Wilkins]]. Animation Director [[Rob Coleman]] was interviewed regarding his participation in creating the TV series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''. The article<ref name="SW" /> was reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2012|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2012]].<ref name="SE" />
