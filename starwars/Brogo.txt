'''Brogo''' was a portly [[Toydarian]] [[junk dealer]] who lived on the [[planet]] [[Toydaria]]. While [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] were on a mission on Toydaria, Brogo captured them and tried to sell them off. A group of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] were then sent to Toydaria to retrieve the [[droid]]s from Brogo.<ref name="Legends" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brogo first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Assault]]'' companion app ''[[Legends of the Alliance]]'', in the "In Trouble Again" ally encounter.<ref name="Legends" />
