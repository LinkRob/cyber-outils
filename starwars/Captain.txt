In civilian life, a '''captain''' was an individual who was in charge of a [[starship]].<ref name="Episode IV" /> In military organizations such as the [[Imperial Navy]]<ref name="Episode V" /> or the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]],<ref name="Episode I" /> a captain was an [[officer]] of high [[Military rank|rank]] with leadership responsibilities.<ref name="Episode V" /><ref name="Episode I" /> [[Pirate]] captains like [[Hondo Ohnaka]], [[Lassa Rhayme]], [[Ought-Six]], and [[Sidon Ithano]] were leader of a particular pirate group. Although pirate captains can be overthrown due to mutiny as [[Jod Na Nawood|Silvo]] was overthrown by his [[First Mate]] [[Brutus]].

==Military organizations==
===Galactic Republic===
{{Quote|My name's Rex, but you'll call me Captain or Sir.|[[Rex]]|<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:TarkinRex-TC.png|thumb|left|250px|Captain Tarkin of the Republic Navy and Clone Captain Rex of the 501st Legion]]
During the [[Republic Era]], the [[Galactic Republic]] used the position of captain in its [[Judicial Forces]].<ref name="Episode I" /> The [[Republic Defense Coalition]] of the [[High Republic Era]] also made use of the rank of captain, with such individuals able to captain their own RDC starship.<ref name="Survivors" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the government created a proper [[Grand Army of the Republic|army]]<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> and [[Republic Navy|navy]].<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> This new [[Republic Military]], made up of [[clone trooper]]s from the [[planet]] of [[Kamino]], used the [[Military rank|rank]] of captain in both its [[army]]<ref name="Episode II" /> and [[Republic Navy|navy]].<ref name="Ambush" /> [[Clone trooper officer]]s who held the rank were known as [[Clone Captain]]s,<ref name="Rex DB">{{Databank|clone-captain-rex|Clone Captain Rex}}</ref> although non-[[Cloning|clone]] [[officer]]s could also serve as captains.<ref name="The Citadel" />

Many [[clone navigation officer]]s, some of whom held the rank of captain, wore a single-rowed [[Rank insignia plaque|plaque]], with two [[Color|red]] and one blue square (from left to right).<ref name="Ambush" /> In the army, Clone Captains led [[Company|companies]] of 144 [[trooper]]s and directly reported to a [[Clone Commander]]. However, as many of the [[Jedi General]]s had a creative approach to the chain of command, some captains worked directly with to their [[Jedi]] officer.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> Clone Captain Captain [[Rex]] was a notable Clone Officer.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> Rex led the [[501st Legion]]'s [[Torrent Company]]<ref name="Rex DB" /> and worked directly with Jedi [[General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], serving as the Jedi's first in command.<ref name="Umbara">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref>

Clone Captains in standard [[Phase I clone trooper armor|Phase I armor]] were initially distinguished by maroon markings on their [[Clone trooper armor|white armor]], but many captains began to customize their [[armor]] to showcase their individuality, show their company's image, and keep from being easily identified by [[sniper]]s.<ref name="Rise" />

===Confederacy of Independent Systems===
{{Quote|Captain! Show this droid what happens when you use that word.|Osi Sobeck, to a droid commando captain|<ref name="The Citadel" />}}
The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] used the rank of captain. In the [[Separatist Droid Army]] both organic officers<ref name="Kanan 8" /> and [[droid]]s could hold the rank.<ref name="The Citadel" /> [[Rackham Sear]], a [[Gender|male]] [[Kage]] [[Kage Warriors|Warrior]], served as a captain in the [[Confederacy military]].<ref name="Kanan 8" /> The Separatists also utilized [[commando droid captain]]s; [[Unidentified BX-series droid commando captain (Citadel)|one such captain]] served at the [[Citadel]].<ref name="The Citadel" />

The rank was also used in the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Confederacy navy]]. Captain [[Mar Tuuk]] served in the Confederacy [[navy]], and commanded the [[Ryloth blockade]] from his [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship]].<ref name="Storm Over Ryloth">{{TCW|Storm Over Ryloth}}</ref>

===Galactic Empire===
<div style="float: right">[[File:Rank_insignia_black.png|thumb|150px|An Imperial captain, wearing the rank insignia plaque of a captain]]</div>
In the [[Galactic Empire|First Galactic Empire]], there were captains in the [[Imperial Navy]]<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref><ref name="Episode IV" /><ref name="Episode V" /> and [[Imperial Army]]. A [[Unidentified stormtrooper captain|stormtrooper captain]] from the army served as a military [[advisor]] to [[Queen]] [[Trios]] of [[Shu-Torun]].<ref name="V19">[[Darth Vader (2015) 19|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 19]]</ref>

The Imperial insignia plaque of the rank for navy officers had three red squares over three blue,<ref name="Episode V" /><ref name="Rogue One" /> or, alternatively, three blue squares followed by two red,<ref name="Rogue One" /> for security officers, a single row of of six yellow squares,<ref name="Rogue One" /><ref name="Episode IV" /> and for army officers, three red followed by one blue square.<ref name="Kenobi III">{{Kenobi|Part III}}</ref>

The term could also refer to anyone who captained a vessel, regardless of their military rank. [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] captained the ''[[Chimaera]]'' while holding the rank of [[commodore]]. He continued to rise through the ranks, becoming a [[Grand Admiral]].<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref>

===Alliance to Restore the Republic===
[[File:Rebel_captain_badge.svg|thumb|left|150px|The command insignia of a Rebel captain]]
In the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] there were captains in both the [[Alliance Army|army]] and the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|navy]].<ref name="Rogue One" /><ref name="Episode IV" /><ref name="Episode V" /><ref name="Episode VI" />

The [[Rebel command insignia|insignia]] of a Rebel captain had two pips over each other.<ref name="Rogue One" /><ref name="Episode IV" /><ref name="Episode V" /><ref name="Episode VI" /> The color of the pips (red, blue, or green) indicated the branch of service.<ref name="ROVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

This held for the early rebellion as well.<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref>

===First Order===
{{Quote|I know I'm not supposed to initiate contact with officers, but look at you! Never took you for captain material. Batch Eight, heigh-ho!|Stormtrooper 926, to a disguised Finn|<ref name="Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>}}
The [[First Order]] had captains in the [[First Order Navy|navy]]<ref name="Episode VII" /><ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and [[First Order Army|Army]]. Two captains in the army's [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|Stormtrooper Corps]] were [[Archex|Cardinal]] and [[Phasma]].<ref name="Phasma">[[Phasma (novel)|''Phasma'']]</ref>

The First Order ranks were marked by a combination of [[First Order military uniforms|uniform]] color and arm [[Rank insignia of the First Order|insignia]], worn on the left sleeve of the uniform.<ref name="Episode VII" /> The First Order captains wore a gray uniform with a black armband edged in silver, bearing the name of [[Dillon]], a particular Imperial hero of the [[Galactic Civil War]], depicted in silver [[Aurebesh]] letters.<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" />

===Resistance===
{{Quote|Our plan, Captain? Not commander, right? Wasn't it Leia's last official act to demote you?|[[Amilyn Holdo]], to [[Poe Dameron]]|<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>}}
<div style="float: right">[[File:Resistance_Army_Captain_badge.svg|thumb|150px|The command insignia of a Resistance Army Captain]]</div>
[[File:Resistance_Navy_Captain_badge.svg|thumb|left|150px|The command insignia of a Resistance Navy Captain]]
In the [[Resistance]], there were captains in both the [[Resistance Army|army]] and the [[Resistance navy|navy]].<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" />

The [[Resistance rank badge|rank badge]] of the Resistance captains bore a colored vertical line, red for personnel in the army and blue for the navy.<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" />

In the Resistance Army, the rank of Captain was lower than that of [[Commander]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Other militaries===
The rank of lieutenant existed in the [[E'ronoh's military]]. [[Jerrod Segaru]] served as a lieutenant and advisor for the [[E'ronoh royal family]].<ref name="Convergence" />
