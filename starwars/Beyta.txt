'''Beyta''' was a [[Mon Calamari]] who served as a [[lieutenant]] and [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] in the [[New Republic Starfighter Corps|Starfighter Corps]] of the [[New Republic]] during the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="Part Three" /> She often worked closely with [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Ahsoka series date">{{AhsokaSeriesDate}}</ref> Beyta wore a [[Color|blue]]-colored [[flight suit]] and was present during a meeting between Syndulla and [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]].<ref name="Part Three" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Beyta first appeared in the teaser trailer of the [[Star Wars: Ahsoka|''Ahsoka'' television series]] for [[Disney+]].<ref name="Ahsoka">{{SWYouTube|HnzNZ0Mdx4I|Ahsoka &#124; Teaser Trailer &#124; Disney+|sw_url=video/ahsoka-teaser-trailer}}</ref> The character appeared in "[[Part Three: Time to Fly|Time to Fly]]," the third episode<ref name="Part Three" /> of the [[2023]] television series ''Ahsoka''. Beyta was first identified with the announcement of the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] set [[w:c:brickipedia:75357 Ghost & Phantom II|75357 ''Ghost'' & ''Phantom II'']], which was released on [[September 1]], [[2023]].<ref name="LEGOGhost">{{LEGOWeb|url=en-us/product/ghost-phantom-ii-75357|text=Ghost & Phantom II}}</ref>
