'''Antiplasma charges''' could be used to dispel [[plasma]] [[fire]]. In [[40 BBY]], the [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] [[Meryx (starship)|''Meryx''{{'s}}]] [[pilot]], the [[Gemstone|jewel]] [[thief]] [[Rahara Wick]], fired a single antiplasma charge to save a struggling [[Unidentified soulcraft|soulcraft]] that had been [[Strike on a soulcraft|attacked]] by [[Pijali]] [[blackguard]]s above the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]]. Afterward, Wick explained her actions to her colleague, [[Pax Maripher]], and again later to [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].

==Description==
A type of charge, antiplasma charges were capable of dispelling [[plasma]] [[fire]] caused by [[plasma bomb]]s.<ref name="MA" />

==History==
{{Quote|Look. Their manifest wasn't locked. So this was here for everyone to see. That means Czerka property. Let's get this straight. During the [[Republic Era]], the [[Gemstone|jewel]] [[Thief|thieves]] [[Pax Maripher]] and [[Rahara Wick]] kept at least one antiplasma charge equipped on their [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]], the [[Meryx (starship)|''Meryx'']].<ref name="MA" /> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> [[Pijali]] [[blackguard]] [[soldier]]s&mdash;who were secretly [[Blackguard campaign|operating]] under the orders of Pijal's [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] to derail the [[Governance Treaty]] initiative's signing ahead of her [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]]&mdash;[[Strike on a soulcraft|attacked]] a Pijali [[Unidentified soulcraft|soulcraft]] [[Starship|vessel]] with a plasma [[bomb]] above the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]]. The [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who had arrived in the [[Pijal system]] moments before, donned [[Environment suit|space-suits]] and began dispelling the plasma fire on the [[soulcraft]]'s [[hull]].<ref name="MA" />

[[File:JediMasterAndPadawan-TPM.png|thumb|left|300px|Qui-Gon Jinn and Obi-Wan Kenobi (pictured) asked Rahara Wick why she had saved the soulcraft with an antiplasma charge, only to flee after.]]
Maripher and Wick, who were [[scout]]ing the [[Star system|system]] for gems, witnessed the attack from nearby. Wick&mdash;who had been formerly [[Slavery|enslaved]] by the [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]]&mdash;noticed that soulcraft's [[Cargo manifest|manifest]] listed thirty-seven "items of [[Sentience|sentient]] property," meaning the vessel had enslaved individuals aboard. She [[pilot]]ed the ''Meryx'' forward and fired a single antiplasma charge, saving the soulcraft and its occupants, before moving their [[cruiser]] safely out of view behind [[Pijal's moon]]. Maripher was astonished and questioned her actions afterward, wondering why she would risk endangering their anonymity. Wick firmly explained her reasoning for saving the individuals aboard, indicating that she would always seek to help those in need.<ref name="MA" />

Later, Jinn and Kenobi tracked Wick and Maripher to Pijal's [[moon]]. After Jinn pointed out that she had saved the soulcraft with the antiplasma charge, Kenobi questioned why she had decided to flee afterward. The jewel thief indicated that there had been no reason not to help, showing them the scar on her [[Limb|hand]] that marked her as formerly enslaved as both the reason she had felt compelled to help and why she had needed to not be re-discovered by Czerka.<ref name="MA" />
