'''Dowut'''<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> was a frigid [[planet]] of windswept tundras<ref name="KoF" /> located in the [[Dowut system]],<ref name="Appendix" /> a [[Trinary star system|trinary]] [[star system]] in the [[Core Worlds]] of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> The [[homeworld]] of the [[Dowutin]] [[species]],<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> Dowut was also home to predatory [[gor-cat]]s and [[nabir]]s, which were known to [[Death|kill]] members of the [[Dowutin precursor species]]. Despite Dowut having joined the [[Galactic Republic]], the Dowutin generally avoided [[politics]], and their [[senator]] made appearances on [[Coruscant]] sparingly. During the rule of the [[Galactic Empire]], Dowut suffered from an indefinite Imperial [[blockade]].<ref name="KoF" />
