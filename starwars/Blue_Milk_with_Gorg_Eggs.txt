'''Blue Milk with Gorg Eggs''' was a [[Foodstuff|beverage]] made from [[blue milk]] and [[gorg]] [[egg]]s sold at the [[Milk Stand]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. The drink was suitable for [[human]] consumption<ref name="SWGE" /> and sold in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Blue Milk with Gorg Eggs was available for purchase in the [[In-universe|real world]] at the Milk Stand in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]] exclusively for [[Star Wars Nite|''Star Wars'' Nites]] in [[2024]].<ref name="2024 Program">{{WebCite|url=https://www.laughingplace.com/w/disney-parks/disneyland-after-dark-star-wars-nite-guide-map-with-list-of-entertainment-characters-and-food-items|text=Disneyland After Dark: Star Wars Nite &ndash; Guide Map with List of Entertainment, Characters, and Food Items|work=LaughingPlace.com|archiveurl=https://archive.is/3D7ci}}</ref> The menu at the Milk Stand described the drink as blue milk "with [[Wikipedia:Pineapple|Pineapple]] Chunks, topped with [[Wikipedia:Yogurt|Yogurt]] [[Wikipedia:Popping boba|Popping Spheres]]."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/milk-stand|text=Disneyland: Milk Stand}}</ref><ref name="Big Red">{{YouTube|video=PJqLIvDsCC4|text=STAR WARS NITE 2024 &ndash; Disneyland After Dark &#124; First Time Full Experience &ndash; Food, Characters & More!|channel=@BigRedJourneys|name=Big Red Journeys|date=2024-04-19}}</ref>
