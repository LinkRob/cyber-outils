'''Fork-nosed ice worms''' were a rare [[species]] of [[worm]] that could be found on [[Hoth]],<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" /> a [[Temperature|frozen]] [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]].<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 1|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 1]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] visited the world and recovered a fossilized fork-[[nose]]d [[Water|ice]] worm. Aphra brought the fossil to the [[Shadow University]], where she sold it to [[Sava]] [[Estat]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" />
