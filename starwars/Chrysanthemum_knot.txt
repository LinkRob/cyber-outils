'''Chrysanthemum knots''' were a type of knot used on the [[planet]] [[Tao]]. A chrysanthemum knot and tassel was tied to the [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]] of a [[Yasaburō family heirloom lightsaber|lightsaber]]<ref name="Art of Visions">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Visions]]''</ref> that was passed down through the generations of the [[Yasaburō clan]], a notable family on Tao.<ref name="Lop & Ochō">{{Visions|Lop & Ochō}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A chrysanthemum knot first appeared, albeit unidentified, in "[[Lop & Ochō]]," a short film that was released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 1|first volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short was written by [[Sayawaka]], directed by [[Yuki Igarashi]], produced by [[Geno Studio]], and released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Lop & Ochō" /> on [[September 22]], [[2021]].<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-trailer-and-cast|text=Stunning New ''Star Wars: Visions'' Trailer Debuts}}</ref> Prior to the short's release, a chrysanthemum knot was first depicted simultaneously in the original Japanese and English dub trailers for the series, which were both uploaded to the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel on [[August 17]] of that year.<ref name="JP Trailer">{{SWYouTube|lle0NNmvIyU|Star Wars: Visions &#124; Original Trailer &#124; Disney+|sw_url=video/original-trailer-star-wars-visions}}</ref><ref name="English Trailer">{{SWYouTube|jtAsl-0o3O0|Star Wars: Visions &#124; English Dub Trailer &#124; Disney+|sw_url=video/english-dub-trailer-star-wars-visions}}</ref>

[[File:AncestralSwordConceptArt.png|thumb|right|200px|Concept art for the lightsaber with its chrysanthemum knot and tassle]]
The chrysanthemum knot was identified in the [[2022]] art book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: Visions]]'', written by [[Zack Davisson]].<ref name="Art of Visions" /> The Yasaburō clan's lightsaber, including its chrysanthemum knot and tassel, was designed by [[Ren Ishimori]] and concept art of it was colored by [[Yuichi Kuboki]].<ref name="EP guide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-visions/episode-guide-lop-and-ocho|text="Lop & Ochō"" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars: Visions}}</ref> In the real world, chrysanthemum knots are a [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] variation of the [[Wikipedia:China|Chinese]] [[Wikipedia:Good luck knot|good luck knot]].<ref name="knots">{{WebCite|url=https://chineseknotting.org/luck|text=Chinese Knotting: The Good Luck Knot|work=[https://chineseknotting.org chineseknotting.org]|archivedate=20241113153425}}</ref>
