'''Cara Makami''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] who served as a [[Jedi]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. She was a skilled [[pilot]] and an ace at flying [[Jedi Vector]]s.<ref name="Visual Guide" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> when [[Jedi Master]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] led a group of Jedi on a [[Duel on Brendok|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Brendok]] to find Jedi Master [[Sol]], Makami volunteered to pilot her [[VKCE-7100x Starcutter|ship]], the ''[[Cantaros]]''.<ref name="Visual Guide" />
