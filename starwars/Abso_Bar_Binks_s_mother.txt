'''Abso Bar Binks's mother''' was a [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Gungan/Legends|Gungan]] and the mate of [[Jar Jar Binks/Legends|Jar Jar Binks]]. Sometime before the beginning of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], she gave birth to a son, [[Abso Bar Binks]]. Jar Jar would attempt to bring home presents for his mate and their child, but these gifts were confiscated by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire]] at their checkpoints. This eventually caused Abso Bar to join the fledgling [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]].

==Behind the scenes==
This individual is only mentioned in the story [[Tantive IV (comic story)|''Tantive IV'']], published in ''[[Star Wars Manga: Black]]'' issue of [[Tokyopop's Star Wars Manga]]. Originally published in Japan, those stories were considered to be of "fuzzy" continuity by Lucasfilm.<ref>{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=980#15895005|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-11-06|time=6:03 PM}}</ref> Since then, they have been republished in English in the United Kingdom under [[Infinities]] label, which established the material in them as [[Canon|non-canonical]].
