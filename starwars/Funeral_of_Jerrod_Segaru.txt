In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''funeral''' for [[Jerrod Segaru]] was held at [[The Rook]] on the [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]] after he was found dead after a [[Summit aboard the Paxion|peace summit]] aboard the ''Paxion'', secretly assassinated by [[E'roni]] [[Viceroy]] [[Ferrol]]'s son [[Rev Ferrol]] and his body left in the bedroom of [[Prince]] [[Phan-tu Zenn]] of [[Eiram]]. The [[E'ronoh royal family]], members of the [[Jedi Order]], [[Chancellor]] [[Orlen Mollo]], [[Axel Greylark]], and the [[Eirami royalty|Eiram royal family]] were present at the funeral, the latter of whom was attacked by an anti-Eiram and pro-[[Eiram and E'ronoh War|war]] E'roni terrorist group called the [[Children of E'ronoh]] during the procession, though they were protected by the [[Jedi]].<ref name="Convergence" />
