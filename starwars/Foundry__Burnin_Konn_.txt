The '''Foundry''' was a [[chromium]] [[mining]] site on [[Burnin Konn]]. Reportedly built on the richest vein of ore on the [[planet]], it had fallen out of sight or into disuse by the time of the [[Iron Blockade]]. However, several [[Standard month|months]] into the lock down, the [[Uprising]] learned of the site's existence, and sought to secure it before anyone else, in hopes of obtaining the funds necessary to establish an open, armed rebellion against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref>
