'''Ardennians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]], [[Primate|simian]]-like [[species]] that were [[Homeworld|native]] to [[Ardennia]]. Due to their extra pair of arms and prehensile toes, the species was known for being extremely agile, nimble and quick-thinking under pressure. They were able to move about while operating multiple pieces of machinery simultaneously, thus being well-suited to careers involving the military or [[Pilot|aviation]], among other choices. Notable Ardennians include [[Rio Durant]], who operated as a [[pilot]] and criminal with [[Beckett's gang]], and [[Chio Fain]], who worked as a [[slicer]].

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Are all Ardennians good cooks? Their multiple limbs do seem to make tasks easier!|Comment from an [[Unidentified artist's journal]]|<ref name="Alien Archive" />}}
Native to Ardennia,<ref name="Alien Archive" /> the diminutive<ref name="Durant DB" /> but stealthy Ardennians had six<ref name="Tales from Vandor" /> dexterous [[limb|limbs]] ending in hands with five fingers or toes each, the lowest two limbs were functioned as legs. They had a strangely fluid, steady gait, enhanced by the prehensile toes on their feet,<ref name="Solo Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> and were extremely agile and quick.<ref name="Alien Archive" /> Their skin tones ranged from blue to tan<ref name="Durant DB" /> and gray,<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> and were covered in fur.<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>
==Society and culture==
{{Quote|I don't know, you've never been to a mynock roast on Ardennia. It's nuts!|Rio Durant|<ref name="Solo" />}}
The Ardennians' prehensile toes and multiple limbs meant that the species was well-suited to military or aviation professions, as they could be extremely quick-thinking and self-sufficient in combat. These features also made other, mundane tasks much simpler, such as preparing food, suggesting that many, if not all, Ardennians were good [[Chef|cooks]].<ref name="Alien Archive" /> Members of the species could be capable [[pilot]]s, skilled tacticians, and all were lovers of good stories.<ref name="Durant DB" /> Ardennians held [[mynock]] roasts on their home planet.<ref name="Solo" />
==History==
Ardennians [[homeworld|hailed]] from [[Ardennia]]<ref name="Alien Archive" />, a [[planet]]<ref name="Bestiary">''[[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]]''</ref> in the [[Ardennia system]] of the [[Fath sector]] situated within the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Ardennia system]]</ref> Ardennians were already known off-world<ref name="QFPX">[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]</ref> by [[382 BBY]].<ref name="Character EncyclopediaDate">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''The High Republic: Quest for Planet X'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref>

Sometime after [[34 ABY]]<ref name="AlienArchiveJournalDate">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>, a journal containing an illustration of the Ardennian Rio Durant was displayed at the [[Graf Archive]] on [[Orchis 2]].<ref name="Alien Archive" /> The illustration was presumebly drawn by an [[Unidentified artist|unknown artist]] and was later restored and expanded as part of the [[Graf Archive restoration effort]] during the tenure of [[Xoddam Lothipp]] as [[Deputy Director]] of the archive.<ref name="Alien Archive" />
==Ardennians in the galaxy==
{{Quote|Some folks with less than perfect pasts are suspicious of their own words, as if they might be traps. But Rio was the opposite, a six-armed Ardennian chatterbox who'd fill a room with words because he was afraid of silence.|[[Tivoche Bilure]], on Rio Durant in [[Midnight's book]]|<ref name="Tales from Vandor" />}}
In [[382 BBY]]<ref name="Character EncyclopediaDate" /> the [[Gender|non-binary]] Ardennian [[Chal]] served as a member of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] on [[Dalna]]. Chal left the planet in [[382 BBY]] when the cult departed aboard its [[starship]], the [[Gaze Electric]]. As part of the Path’s operations, the group’s leader, the [[Mother (title)|leader]], later assigned Chal to a [[Saboteur|sabotaging]] mission alongside fellow members [[Fel Ix]] and [[Severn]]. Traveling aboard the [[shuttle]] ''[[Way of Clarity]]'', the trio disabled [[communications buoy]]s throughout the [[Galactic Frontier]] and briefly crossed paths with the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] ship ''[[Felicitations]]''. Their mission eventually brought them to the [[Tybal system]], where they attempted to seize the [[Prospector|prospecting]] ship ''[[Brightbird]]'' and its crew. The operation failed when the pilot, [[Sky Graf]], opened fire with a blaster, forcing Chal and Severn to retreat while Fel Ix was left behind.<ref name="QFPX" />

[[File:MadaVangu-YJA-BattleBand.png|thumb|left|200px|The Ardennian Mada Vangu was a member of Feb Rozo and the Sap Tap Trio]]
[[Mada Vangu]] was an Ardennian [[musician]] during the [[High Republic Era]] who performed as a member of [[Feb Rozo and the Sap Tap Trio]] on [[Tenoo]], playing a [[Red ball jett organ]]. After [[Hap]] emerged from the counter of his now-ruined bar, [[Hap's Sap Tap]], he informed Jedi [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]], [[Wes Vinik]], and [[Nubs]] that the [[Ganguls]], led by [[Sellaccc Orryak]], had kidnapped the band. The group took a ride aboard [[Nash Durango]]'s ''[[Crimson Firehawk]]''. Circling [[Yarrum Tower]], they found no sign of the Ganguls until Lys Solay spotted their flagship, ''[[Rathtar's Revenge]]'', hovering over the desert on Tenoo.<ref name="BftB">{{YJA|Battle for the Band}}</ref>

Using [[Macrobinoculars]], [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Wes Vinik]], and [[Hap]] spotted a [[sail barge]] with [[Feb Rozo]]’s band, [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Bulcha]], and security droids. Hap distracted a guard, allowing the band to play and please Bulcha, who agreed to trade the droids for the captives. When Vinik ignited [[Wes Vinik's lightsaber|his lightsaber]], Bulcha deployed three [[vibroblade|vibroblade]]-armed droids, forcing the Jedi to deactivate their sabers and be escorted to the brig. Brightstar called [[Nash Durango]] via [[holoprojector]], and the ''Firehawk'' arrived to extract the Jedi, Hap, and the band, leaving Bulcha behind.<ref name="BftB" />

Several Ardennians were among twenty settlers of various ages and species aboard the ''[[Legacy Run]]'' when it was caught in the [[hyperspace]] accident known as the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />  in [[232 BBY]].<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> In [[231 BBY]]<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> the Ardennian delegate [[Retar]] attended the [[Valo Republic Fair]] alongside other members of the [[Ardennian Guild of Technicians]]. 

He spent half an hour engaged in a detailed discussion with the Jedi [[Stellan Gios]] on the nuances of [[carbon scoring|carbon score]] removal, while Stellan offered only nods and brief encouragements. When asked about touring the exhibition, Retar shook his head, steering the conversation back to ship maintenance. Stellan then suggested that Retar accompany archivist [[OrbaLin]] to the "[[Secrets of the Jedi]]" exhibition, where he showed particular interest in the [[Cassadrean Matrix]], before moving toward the ornate double doors leading to the exhibit.<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>

Prior to the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]] of [[Starlight Beacon]], An Ardennian cargo pilot was among those [[Bell Zettifar]] gathered to help seal off the cargo bay [[airlock|airlocks]]. Stationed near the back of the group, she listened warily as he outlined his plan to evacuate passengers by forcing open the cargo bay doors and allowing the ships inside to escape. Folding all four of her arms across her chest, she asked whether carrying out the plan would require dumping cargo. When Zettifar confirmed that it would, she gave a resigned nod, accepting the loss as the price of saving lives.<ref name="Fallen Star">''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]''</ref>

[[File:Prap-2023THRA11.png|thumb|right|200px|Prap dressed as a member of the Scarlet Skull Cult]]
The Ardennian [[Prap]] was a resident of the [[planet]] [[Valo]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Following the [[Raid of the Innovator|raid]] on the ''[[Innovator]]'', which weakened the [[Nihil]] occupation of Valo, Prap joined a local resistance led by the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Gavi Takitaken]]. The group posed as members of the [[Scarlet Skull Cult]], painting their faces red to support the ruse. When [[the Warden]], a [[Nihil]] [[pirate]], sought to conscript Valo’s population for his [[Invasion of Eriadu|campaign against Eriadu]], Prap took part in the ensuing [[Revolt on Valo|uprising]] and led a charge of [[Valon]]s against the marauders.<ref name="THRA 11">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 11|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 11]]</ref>

During the [[Mission to Begamor]] an Ardennian pilot supervised two [[maintenance droid|maintenance droids]] as they carried out routine work on the [[President]] of [[Begamor]], [[Jamie Brasson]]’s [[Droplet-class airspeeder]] in the [[Presidential manor|manor]] courtyard. He acknowledged that he was responsible for the craft and had stepped away briefly, only to be left stunned as [[Sean]] stole the speeder and launched it at tremendous speed, its engines flaring with multicolored light. In the aftermath, the pilot reacted first by leaping and gesturing wildly in disbelief as the airship vanished, while [[Manor guard|guards]] fired ineffectively after it while the President’s furious shouting voice echoed over the comms. The stolen vessel possessed a complex cockpit built for the pilot, a reinforced bridge tailored to Ardennian physiology, a lightweight but durable alloy hull, and an engine far more powerful than comparable craft, demanding exceptional precision to operate.<ref name="TVoSD">''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''</ref>

[[File:Rio MtC.png|thumb|left|250px|Rio Durant was an Ardennian criminal who served with Tobias Beckett]]
[[Rio Durant]], a fighter with [[Freedom's Sons]] in his youth, operated as a criminal in [[Tobias Beckett]]'s [[Beckett's gang|gang]].<ref name="Solo Guide" /> He posed as an [[Imperial Army]] [[Imperial swamp trooper|mudtrooper]] on [[Mimban]] during the [[Mimban Campaign]], as part of a heist to commandeer a [[Y-45 armored transport hauler]]. Though Rio attempted to blend in as a [[humanoid]], his strange gait and surprising number of arms were noticeable, and fellow mudtrooper [[Corporal]] [[Han Solo]] correctly identified him as an Ardennian after watching him use his lower arms to hike up his pants. <ref name="Solo" />

Durant doubted about letting Solo join them, but Beckett gave the boy a chance by having him locked up for insubordination. Solo eventually escaped with the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]], who had initially been meant to eat him. Beckett then ordered Rio to lower the ship, surprising him with Chewbacca aboard. Once they reached [[Vandor]], Beckett's crew spotted the [[20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport]] transport they planned to  [[Heist on Vandor|steal]] its [[coaxium]] from to settle Beckett's debt to [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]]. They hoped Enfys Nest and the Cloud-Riders wouldn’t appear the next day. That night, beside their campfire, Durant teased Solo about the girl he mentioned. Durant participated in the heist on Vandor, but was fatally shot in the shoulder by [[Baroosh Pawk]], one of [[Enfys Nest]]'s [[Cloud-Riders]]. In his final moments, Durant told the young [[Han Solo]] that [[Val]] was right in saying it was no good to die alone. He was later buried by Beckett, with his goggles hung on the stick marking his grave.<ref name="Solo" />

[[File:ChioFain-TargetVader4.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Chio Fain was an Ardennian Slicer who tried to assassinate Darth Vader]]
During the [[Imperial Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the Ardennian [[Chio Fain]] worked as a [[slicer]] alongside his partner [[Rone]] until Rone’s death in a skirmish with [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]<ref name="Target Vader 4">[[Target Vader 4|''Target Vader'' 4]]</ref>, after which Fain joined [[Beilert Valance]]’s team to hunt [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]<ref name="Target Vader 1" />. In [[1 BBY]]<ref name="Date 3">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Target Vader 1|''Target Vader'' 1]] to [[1 BBY]].</ref>, Fain worked with Valance, [[Dengar]], the [[Gamorrean]] tracker [[Honnah]], the droid [[Arr-Nine-Nineteen]], and sniper [[Gita|Urrr'k]] under a plan to kill Vader. Fain sliced into databases, piloted bot-drones, and coordinated attacks, including merging drones to destroy the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Formidable]]'' during a battle above [[Heva]]<ref name="Target Vader 3">[[Target Vader 3|''Target Vader'' 3]]</ref>. When Vader landed on Heva, Fain attempted to confront him, firing blasters while dodging attacks<ref name="Target Vader 4" />. Overpowered, Fain had three arms sliced off before [[Honnah]] attacked Vader to aid him. Vader used the Force to push Fain into Honnah’s ax, fatally wounding him. Fain fell, whispering Rone’s name before dying.<ref name="Target Vader 4" />

As ''Bounty Hunters'' 27 takes place between ''War of the Bounty Hunters'' and ''Dark Droids'', its events must take place around 3 ABY.</ref> an Ardennian DJ performed at a dance club on the [[Accretion Disco]] when the space station was hired out by the [[Pyke Syndicate]] [[gangster]] [[Omel Sampak]] so he could celebrate a [[Bombing of a Nar Kanji fight school|victory]] over their enemies. When a group of [[Kanjiklub]] [[Assassin|assassins]] attempted to kill the Pyke at the party, [[Havoc at the Accretion Disco|chaos]] ensued, which resulted in Sampak's [[death]] and also the destruction of the Disco, which fell into the black hole around which the station was situated.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27">[[Bounty Hunters 27|''Bounty Hunters'' 27]]</ref>

[[File:Ardennian-Bothan-5-MANDOCH6.png|thumb|left|250px|An Ardennian prisoner in a cell on the Bothan-5]]
In the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Era|era]], in [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|6}}</ref> an Ardennian was [[prisoner|imprisoned]] on the [[New Republic Correctional Transport]]<ref name="Chapter 6">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 6: The Prisoner}}</ref> ''[[Bothan-5]]''<ref name="Chapter 10">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 10: The Passenger}}</ref> when [[Ranzar Malk]]'s [[Ranzar Malk's crew|crew]] launched a [[Bothan-Five incident|rescue]] of the [[Twi'lek]] prisoner [[Qin]]. The prisoner looked out of their cell, clutched the bars, and emitted a low moan as [[Din Djarin]], [[Migs Mayfeld]], [[Xi'an]], and [[Burg]] passed by.<ref name="Chapter 6" />

That same year,<ref name="SC Date">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> two Ardennians were present on [[Lanupa]], bathing in the [[Mud Pits]] at the [[Skull Ridge Mountain Hotel & Spa]] when the [[Skeleton Crew]], on a [[Mission to Skull Ridge Mountain|mission]] to [[Tak Rennod]]'s lair, visited the resort during the [[Economic summit on Lanupa]] hosted by the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]].<ref name="Learn About Pirates">{{SkeletonCrew|You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates}}</ref>

Later around that same time, an Ardennian named [[Hugo Durant]] worked as a [[Fry cook|frycook]] on [[Shakari]] and came into contact with Din Djarin, who asked for information on local crimeboss [[Janu Coin]].<ref name="TMaG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Scarlet-Quickdraw-Rio-2.png|thumb|right|240px|Early concept of Rio Durant by Tyler Scarlet]]
Ardennians were first created for the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', directed by [[Ron Howard]] as the species of the character Rio Durant.<ref name="Solo" /> The final Ardennian design emerged from combining two characters, Rio and Zapf. Rio was conceived as a gruff humanoid alien, while Zapf was described as a “squid-monkey.” Multiple directions were explored, including a grub-like concept by [[Luke Fisher]], squid-like designs by [[Luis Carrasco]] and [[Ivan Manzella]], and a turtle-like head design by Manzella. Manzella also produced concepts depicting a pudgier Ardennian with a trunk-like nose.<ref name="Art of Solo">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

Several early concepts differed markedly from the final look, one by Manzella and two by [[Jake Lunt Davies]] gave Rio four eyes instead of two, with one of Davies’s designs later evolving into the [[Dyplotid]] species. Designs by both Manzella and Davies featured an orange beard or mustache. At various points, Rio was envisioned as slimmer or smaller, with such designs drawn by [[James Clyne]], [[Tyler Scarlet]], Davies, and Manzella.<ref name="Art of Solo" />
