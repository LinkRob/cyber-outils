'''Dea Tore''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Doctor/Legends|doctor]] who stayed on [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]] during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="AED" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dea Tore appears in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online roleplaying game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']]. According to these files, Dr. Tore would have been on [[W:c:swg:Naboo|Naboo]]. However, Dr. Tore is not known to have appeared on the [[Star Wars video games|video game]]'s live servers as a proper [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]]. Nevertheless, an emulated depiction pulled from this character's Interchange File Format reveals what she would have looked like if she were added to the game.<ref name="AED" />
