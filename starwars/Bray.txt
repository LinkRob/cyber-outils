'''Bray''' was a dark [[planet]] home to the [[Brayan]] [[species]]. It was located in the [[Piryn Shar sector]] in the [[Expansion Region]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]] resided in a [[Citadel of Light|citadel]] along with [[Ravna]], a mutated creature who could convert others into his [[Ravna's species|kind]]. Dooku planned to allow Ravna to infect the Brayan population in exchange for using converted [[clone trooper]]s to infiltrate the ranks of the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Republic army]], but their plans were halted by the [[Jedi Council]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Like you, I am but a guest on this gloom-ridden planet.|Dooku, to Adi Gallia|<ref name="TFVC 2" />}}
A dark and shadowy world, Bray appeared to be in perpetual night, overcast with dark clouds. Its surface was [[Color|gray]] and rocky, covered in many natural rock formations. It was located close to a [[Asteroid field|belt]] of [[asteroid]]s<ref name="TFVC 2" /> in the [[Bray system]], a [[star system]] in the [[Piryn Shar sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="SWSystems" />

==History==
{{Quote|The Jedi Council never ignores a cry for help.''"<br />"''Especially on a planet suspected of Separatist activity.|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Adi Gallia|<ref name="TFVC 2" />}}
The creature known as [[Lord]] [[Ravna]] inhabited Bray, taking over the [[Citadel of Light]] and using it as his home. From the Citadel, Ravna infected the [[Brayan]] population of a nearby [[Unidentified village (Bray)|village]], turning them into [[Ravna's species|mutant creatures]] similar to himself.<ref name="TFVC 2" />

[[File:CitadelExplosion-TalesFromVadersCastle2.jpg|thumb|left|270px|The Citadel of Light is destroyed]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Republic]] suspected the [[planet]] of housing [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] activity. Because of this, the [[Jedi Council]] were eager to investigate when a [[distress signal]] was received from the planet. Two [[Jedi Master]]s, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Adi Gallia]], were [[Mission to Bray|sent]] to Bray with [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Cody]] and his [[7th Sky Corps]]. Kenobi and Gallia encountered one of Ravna's creatures, who infected the [[clone trooper]] [[Peel]]. The [[Jedi]] investigated the Citadel, discovering that [[Count]] [[Dooku]] was working with Lord Ravna, planning to infect the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Republic army]] with Ravna's creatures and cause it to collapse. As Dooku confronted the Jedi Masters, he was infected by Ravna, but managed to fight back with help from Kenobi. As Dooku fought Ravna, the Jedi collapsed the Citadel, trapping the creature under its ruins. With Ravna entombed, the Brayans were freed and his existence became a myth.<ref name="TFVC 2" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|Life on Bray has always been hard, but since the arrival of the Dark Lord, it has been a living nightmare.|A Brayan villager|<ref name="TFVC 2" />}}
Bray was the [[homeworld]] of the [[humanoid]] [[Brayan]] [[species]], who lived in [[City|villages]] that had working power, lights, and many [[droid]]s. The Brayans found living on the planet difficult, and even more so while Ravna's creatures attacked their villages.<ref name="TFVC 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bray first appeared in the flashback section of the [[2018]] [[canon]] comic [[Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle 2|''Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle'' 2]], which was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and illustrated by [[Kelley Jones]].<ref name="TFVC 2" /> It was named after the film and television facility [[Wikipedia:Bray Studios (UK)|Bray Studios]], where many famous horror films were filmed, such as the [[1960s#1966|1966]] film [[Wikipedia:Dracula: Prince of Darkness|''Dracula: Prince of Darkness'']], in which Count Dooku actor [[Christopher Lee]] starred in the [[Wikipedia:Count Dracula|titular role]].<ref name="Homage/Tribute">{{SW|url=news/the-spooky-secrets-of-idws-tales-from-vaders-castle|text=The Spooky Secrets of IDW's ''Tales from Vader's Castle''}}</ref>
