"'''Flight and Fight!'''" is a short story published in ''[[The Original Trilogy Stories]]''. It was written by [[Trey King]].<ref name="Original" /> It adapts the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi]] from the [[Tyderium]]'s arrival over [[Endor]] to the search for [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].
