During the [[Galactic Civil War]], an individual flew under the callsign '''Blade One''' four [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]]. A member of [[Blade Squadron]], a [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] composed of [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter]]s, [[Blade One]] flew as [[Wing Commander]] [[Adon Fox]]'s wingman during the [[Battle of Endor]]. The [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] was later [[Death|killed]] by a [[TIE/IN interceptor|TIE interceptor]].<ref name="Blade Squadron" />
