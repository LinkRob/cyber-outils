'''Bith beans''' were a type of [[Vegetable/Legends|vegetable]] that were used in cooking and could be eaten by members of the [[Besalisk/Legends|Besalisk]] [[Species/Legends|species]]. The Besalisk [[Chef/Legends|chef]] [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|Dexter Jettster's]] [[Restaurant/Legends|eatery]], [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]], sold baked Bith beans as a dinner side dish for 3.5 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]]. They were served at the diner, which was located in the [[CoCo Town/Legends|CoCo Town]] district of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]],<ref name="DaD" /> at some point prior to the establishment's destruction during the first two [[Standard year/Legends|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire's]] rule.<ref name="DexEra">''[[The Last of the Jedi: Underworld]]'' established that [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] was destroyed by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. ''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]'' later stated that the novel took place around the [[Standard year/Legends|year]] [[18 BBY/Legends|18 BBY]]. ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' states that the Empire was formed in [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]], so Dex's Diner was destroyed either during 19 BBY or 18 BBY.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Bith beans were first mentioned in the [[2009]] [[StarWars.com]] [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]] exclusive article, "[[Dining at Dex's]]," which was written by [[Gregory Walker]] in the form of an [[in-universe]] menu for Dex's Diner. The article did not make clear if there was any connection between the beans and the [[Bith/Legends|Bith]] species.<ref name="DaD" />
