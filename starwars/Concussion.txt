'''Concussions''' were a type of head injury. They were categorized in levels of seriousness, with a [[level nine concussion]] being fairly bad. [[Ghal Tarpfen]], the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Head of Security]] for the [[Starlight Beacon]], received a concussion during an attempt to subdue a [[Medoslean]] [[Unidentified Medoslean|patient]] who was having a seizure and reacting badly to a treatment.<ref name="First Duty">{{InsiderCite|issue1=201|issue2=202|Starlight: First Duty}}</ref>
