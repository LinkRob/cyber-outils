'''Coruscant rodents''' were a type of [[rodent]] [[Homeworld|native]] to<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] and liked to [[Diet|eat]] the [[flower]]s within the [[garden]]s of the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]]. On at least one occasion after the [[Alcedian]] [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Tep Tep]] arrived at the [[Jedi temple|Jedi Temple]] after leaving the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Valo]]<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> in [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Valo date">''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'' establishes that [[Tep Tep]] was stationed at the [[Coruscant]] [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] after she departed [[Valo]], an event depicted in ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]''. The events of ''Escape from Valo'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> the [[human]] [[Jedi]] [[youngling]] [[Jamil Sollis]] found Tep Tep<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />&mdash;who had a special [[Taming beasts|Force connection]] to animals<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref>&mdash;playing with the rodents.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> as Sollis went to find Tep Tep in the Temple, he mentally noted that if he did not find her playing with the Coruscant rodents, he would find her in the youngling dormitories or within the cargo hold of the [[Elite-class vessel|research ship]] ''[[Innovator]]'' in a [[hangar]]. He found the Alcedian in the cargo hold.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />
