'''Am'balaar City''' was a [[city]] located on the river [[moon]] of [[Al'doleem]]. The city was destroyed when [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] burst the [[Am'balaar city dam|local dam]], resulting in the entire city being flooded.<ref name="Dark Lord 4" />

{{City-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Am'balaar city debuted in [[Darth Vader (2017) 4|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 4]], a [[2017]] [[canon]] comic written by [[Charles Soule]] and pencilled by [[Giuseppe Camuncoli]].<ref name="Dark Lord 4" />
