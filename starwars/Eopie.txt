'''Eopies''' were quadruped [[mammal]]ian [[Diet|herbivores]] native to the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. These tough creatures were adapted to their [[homeworld]]'s endless [[desert]]s, and as a result were domesticated by the planet's inhabitants. They often worked as transports or beast of burden, though they were often bad-tempered and stubborn, and could break wind if their load was too heavy.

==Biology and appearance==
Eopies were a [[species]] of quadrupedal [[mammal]]lian [[Diet|herbivores]]<ref name="VE" /> native to the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. These tough creatures were averaged at the height of 1.75 [[meter]]s and were acclimated to their [[homeworld]]'s endless [[desert]]s, and as a result were domesticated by the planet's inhabitants.<ref name="SWE" /> The eopie's rough skin helped to protect it against the [[Temperature|heat]] of the twin [[Star|suns]] of its homeworld while its hooves were adapted to climbing rocky cliffs.<ref name="Eopie">{{GoC|Eopie}}</ref>

The eopie's trunked mouth was capable of secreting an adhesive spit that stuck to its skin. Eopies would layer this spit over their [[eye]]s as a means of protection during sandstorms.<ref name="Eopie" /> Though herbivorous by nature, they, much like [[bantha]]s, were able to consume meat with no ill effects, as shown when [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'s personal eopie [[Akkani]] happily munched at the scraps of meat he fed her on a daily basis during his exile on Tatooine.<ref name="Part I">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref>

[[Horned eopie]]s were a type of Eopie<ref name="Episode Guide" /> that lived on the planet [[At Achrann]].<ref name="No At Attin" />

==Behavior==
Eopies were known for being tough and were protective of their young. Young eopies would travel in their parent's shadow as a way to keep cool. During sandstorms, the parent Eopie would personally apply a layer of protective spit over the young's head.<ref name="Eopie" /> Eopies were observed to exhibit flatulence.<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> As such, when riding a caravan of eopies, it was recommended to always ride on the foremost animal.<ref name="Complete Visual New">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

==Eopies in the galaxy==
On Tatooine, Eopies often worked as [[transport]]s or beast of burden, though they were often bad-tempered and stubborn, and could break wind if their load was too heavy.<ref name="SWE" /><ref name="AbEverything" /> Eopies were also found on the planet [[Saleucami]],<ref name="The Deserter">{{TCW|The Deserter}}</ref> and the desert [[moon]] [[Zardossa Stix]],<ref name="The Disappeared, Part II">{{TCW|The Disappeared, Part II}}</ref> as well as in the [[forest]]s and [[jungle]]s near the [[Black Spire Ruins]] of [[Batuu]].<ref name="SWGE ruins">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]</ref>

On some planets, eopie was served as a delicacy. [[Power Sliders]], a small diner located in a [[Pons Ora|small settlement]] on the remote world of [[Abafar]], had [[eopie stew]] on its menu.<ref name="MIA">{{TCW|Missing in Action}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Originally, the role of the eopie was to be filled by the [[kaadu]] of [[Naboo]].<ref name="DB">{{DB|creature|eopie|eopie}}</ref> However, due to the evolving story of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], which called for the [[Gungan]]s to be beast-riding soldiers, the kaadu was transported to Naboo,<ref name="Databank Old">{{DB|creature|kaadu|kaadu}}</ref> leading to the creation of the eopie. An early production painting by [[Doug Chiang]] of the kaadu hauling a [[podracer]] engine on Tatooine was retouched in [[Wikipedia:Adobe Photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] to create the eopie.<ref name="DB" />
