The '''105th Battalion''' was a [[battalion]] of the [[First Order military]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|Stormtrooper Corps]]. Consisting of [[Sith trooper]]s and [[Sith Jetpack Trooper]]s<ref name="SW.com" />&mdash;elite [[soldier]]s in the [[Sith Eternal army|army]] of the [[Sith Eternal]]<ref name="TROS VD" />&mdash;the 105th served in the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]<ref name="SW.com" /> under the strict command of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] and his [[Knights of Ren]].<ref name="SWCT" />
