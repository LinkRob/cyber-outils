The '''Battle of Thaldo''' was a battle that occurred on [[Thaldo]] during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].

==Prelude==
[[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] and [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] headed to Thaldo to warn the planet's [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] outpost about an upcoming [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] attack. On Thaldo, Luke liberated a [[Unidentified Rebel prisoner|Rebel prisoner]] from Imperial custody, who gave him an ID card that would allow him access to the base.

==The battle==
Luke discovered a small outpost staffed by a Rebel officer, who escorted him to the main base about a [[T-47 airspeeder/Legends|T-47 airspeeder]]. Luke was too late to warn the base&mdash;the Imperial attack began just as he arrived. After fighting off the initial wave, Luke managed to repair the base's [[Ion cannon/Legends|ion cannon]], allowing the Rebels stationed on [[Thaldo]] to escape the planet.
