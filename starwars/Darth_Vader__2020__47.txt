'''''Darth Vader'' 47''' is the forty-seventh issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]] and [[Federico Blee]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[June 12]], [[2024]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE RAZING OF EXEGOL CONTINUES! DARTH VADER and the SCHISM IMPERIAL launch their boldest challenge to the power of PALPATINE with an assault on EXEGOL, the home of the EMPEROR'S greatest secrets and treasures! But if sheer brute force can't overcome the defenses of the SITH CITADEL, what terrifying new resources will the DARK LORD draw upon? Also: CAPTAIN ENRIC PRYDE faces his greatest test!''

==Plot summary==
On [[Exegol]], [[Senior Lieutenant]] [[Enric Pryde]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] leave the [[Sith Citadel]] with a massive [[Color|red]] [[kyber crystal]] to load onto their [[kyberite]]-plated [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]]. Pryde, who constantly celebrates Vader's achievements, rebukes [[Governor]] [[Tauntaza]] for her jaded attitude. The other members of the [[Schism Imperial]] are still worried of retribution from the cultists, especially because they summoned a number of [[Unidentified summa-verminoth subspecies|summa-verminoth]] with the ability to cause mental anguish in their prey. 

As they quickly load the kyber crystal onto the [[shuttle]], [[ZED-6-7]] points out that the members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[M.A.R. Corps]] had broken free from their implants. Vader uncaringly leaves them to the effects of the summa-verminoth and boards the [[starship|ship]]. 

Inside, the group is protected by the kyberite plating. [[Administrator]] [[Sly Moore]] takes the moment to celebrate the victories of her Schism, culminating in the retrieval of the massive crystal, the likes of which powered the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. She reasons that such power will make it so they never will have to run again. 

Vader, however, stares at the crystal, seeing the face of his [[Sith Master|Master]] [[Darth Sidious]]. The [[Emperor]] congratulates Vader on finally seizing the power of technology for himself, for feeding into his [[Sith]] nature. Palpatine then presents a problem for Vader: Since their can only be [[Rule of Two|two Sith]], with [[Luke Skywalker]] in the mix, who shall the two be?

Meanwhile, Luke Skywalker gazes into the [[star]]s aboard a Rebel ship, sensing that his father is seeking him out. He confides to [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] these feelings, fearing that Vader wants him to grow stronger in order to rule [[the galaxy]] at his side. He decides to get some space, which inspires Leia to play a message from [[Jedi]] pretender and [[thief]] [[Warba Calip]] from [[Sergia]] with [[R2-D2|R2-D2's]] [[holoprojector]]. Though certain it is some kind of scam or trap, Luke decides to answer Calip's call. 

Back on Exegol, Vader uses [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] to roughly slice off a couple of chunks from the larger kyber crystal to implant into his [[Zaly Shield]] and [[Darth Vader's electrostaff|his electrostaff]]. Ignoring the warnings of ZED-6-7 that the imprecision of Vader's approach, especially in powering his [[electrostaff]] with a [[Tauntaza's fortress|death machine]] [[power cell]], was dangerous, Vader prepares to take on the summa-verminoth.

Although they were initially affected by the psychic attacks of the summa-verminoth, the cyborgs come to their senses since they had overcome so much anguish being trapped in their own bodies under Vader's control. Back in the skies, Vader stands atop the shuttle and uses his shield and staff to send massive amounts of energy at the creatures, subduing them. The energy of this action sends ripples through [[the Force]] to the Emperor and Luke. This causes Palpatine to give Vader his blessing to seek out Luke once and for all.

At the rendezvous point with Warba, Luke approaches the thief with some hesitancy, but reminds her that he sees so much more in her than she does herself. As they approach [[Sabé's H-type Nubian yacht|her H-type Nubian yacht]], [[Sabé]], former [[handmaiden]] of Luke's mother [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], steps onto the boarding ramp and reveals that she knows his father. Sabé [[stun]]s Calip and tries to stun Luke before getting R2-D2. She demands that if Luke has a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]], it was time for him to show it.
