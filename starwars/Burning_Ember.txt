The '''Burning Ember''' was a military unit of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]], and one of its most dreaded divisions during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]]. [[General/Legends|General]] [[Gentis]] led seventy [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone troopers]] against this division and won, further perpetuating his reputation by [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]].
