An '''electrostatic discharge coupling''' was a small [[starship]] component.<ref name="The Escape" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 ABY">{{ResistanceDate|1|1}}</ref> When [[Kazuda Xiono]] was fixing his [[racing starfighter]], the ''[[Fireball]]'', static discharge couplings were among the spare parts that had to be bought from [[Orka]] and [[Flix]]'s [[Office of Acquisitions]].<ref name="Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref> When [[Tamara Ryvora|Tam Ryvora]] was repairing Xiono's racing starfighter later, her [[Aurebesh]] list of parts included a "static discharge coupling."<ref name="Dangerous">{{Resistance|Dangerous Business}}</ref> Later, the [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Neeku Vozo]] asked if Xiono could pass him the component while the two were performing maintenance on the ''Fireball''. Xiono complied, kicking the starship part up off the ground and catching it before tossing it to Vozo.<ref name="The Escape" />
