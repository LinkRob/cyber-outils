The '''Civic Level''' was the highest of [[Pau City]]'s eleven levels. It contained the [[city]]'s government, bureaucracy, civic and ceremonial buildings, and cultural and recreational facilities, such as the [[Pahum Cultural Center]]. The [[Pau'an]] elite also lived on the level.<ref name="SWCL" />

During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] occupied Pau City and began using part of the Civic Level to produce [[Mankvim-814 light interceptor|Mankvim-814 interceptors]]. Soon after, the [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] arrived on [[Utapau]] and landed on a landing platform on the level.<ref name="SWCL" />
