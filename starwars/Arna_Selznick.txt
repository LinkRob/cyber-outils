'''Arna Selznick''' is a storyboard artist and supervisor, layout artist, director and writer for animated television series and film. Selznick began her career as a layout artist for the seminal [[Nelvana]] film ''Rock & Rule''. She is known as one of only four women to have directed a full-length animated feature, having directed Nelvana's ''The Care Bears Movie'', as well as the television or direct-to-video features ''Strawberry Shortcake and the Baby Without a Name'' and ''Back to School with Franklin''. Selznick's extensive storyboard artwork credits include installments of series such as ''Miss Spider's Sunny Patch Friends'', ''Clone High'', ''Doug'', ''Beetlejuice'' and ''Babar''.

For ''[[Star Wars]]'', Selznick served as storyboard supervisor for the entirety of ''[[Star Wars Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO]]'' and for the first season of [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'']].<ref name="dancingmonkeys">{{WebCite|url=http://www.dancingmonkeys.com/ArnaBio/Arnapage.htm|text=Arna Selznick|work=dancingmonkeys.com|archivedate=20200121131543}}</ref>
