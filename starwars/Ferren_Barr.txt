'''Ferren Barr''' was an [[Iktotchi]] [[Gender|male]] fallen [[Jedi]] who sought to [[Great Jedi Purge|avenge]] the [[Jedi Order]] by overthrowing the [[Sith]]-ruled [[Galactic Empire]]. Despite his altruistic motives, Barr resorted to methods contrary to the [[Jedi Code]], using [[the Force]] to [[Mind trick|control]] his [[Ferren Barr's disciples|followers]] and [[assassin]]ating the [[Telvar|Imperial ambassador]] to [[Mon Cala]]. However, his actions ensured that the [[Mon Calamari]] would ultimately join the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], becoming an essential part of the [[Galactic Civil War|fight]] to restore [[democracy]] to [[the galaxy]].

A [[Padawan]] during the last [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Republic Era]], Barr served in the [[Clone Wars]] until the [[Galactic Republic]] was [[Proclamation of the New Order|reformed]] as the [[Galaxy|Galactic]] Empire. He managed to survive the immediate execution of [[Order 66]], unlike other Jedi [[officer]]s who were [[Death|killed]] by their [[clone trooper]]s, and escaped into [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] while the [[New Order]] rose to power. During the early [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Imperial Era]], Barr formed a plan to undermine [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious]]' rule. Using his position as an [[advisor]] to [[King of Mon Cala|King]] [[Lee-Char]], Barr orchestrated a conflict between the Empire and the [[Mon Calamari monarchy]], resulting in the [[king]]'s [[Prisoner|capture]], the [[Imperial Military]]'s [[invasion of Mon Cala]], and Barr's death in a [[lightsaber duel]] against the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].

Ultimately, Barr's prophecy was realized through the victory of the Rebel Alliance. Bolstered by [[Mon Calamari Exodus Fleet|remnants]] of the [[Mon Cala Mercantile Fleet]], the [[Alliance Military]] defeated the Empire's forces in the [[Battle of Endor]], while the deaths of Sidious and Vader fulfilled the [[Chosen One|prophesied destruction]] of the Sith.

==Biography==
===Jedi career and Clone Wars===
{{Quote|I fought alongside the clones once, but now I know they are merely machines. Little better than droids. They must obey their programming. As the Inquisitors were once Jedi themselves, and the clones were built to kill Jedi, well&hellip;you see the result.|Ferren Barr, on the clone troopers|<ref name="Dark Lord 17" />}}
Ferren Barr began his [[Jedi training|training]] in the ways of [[the Force]] as a [[Padawan]]<ref name="Dark Lord 16">[[Darth Vader (2017) 16|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 16]]</ref> of the [[Jedi Order]] during the last [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. In his time as a padawan, he sparred with [[Anakin Skywalker]] in [[lightsaber combat]] on at least one occasion.<ref name="Dark Lord 14" /> Like his fellow [[Jedi]], he participated in the [[Clone Wars]] as part of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] alongside the Republic [[clone trooper]]s until the commencement of [[Order 66]]&mdash;the command to [[Death|execute]] all Jedi as [[Treason|enemies of the state]].<ref name="Dark Lord 17" /> Although Barr survived the immediate massacre,<ref name="Dark Lord 14" /> thousands of his brothers and sisters in the Force were [[Great Jedi Purge|exterminated]] by their own [[soldier]]s, both at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] and across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> As a result, he fled into [[Exile (punishment)|exile]]<ref name="Dark Lord 14" /> while the [[Sith]], ancient enemies of the Jedi Order, returned to power through the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Episode III" />

===Exile and investigation===
{{Quote|I've been studying the purge, trying to understand how we could lose so completely, so quickly. I learned about Order 66, poor deluded Dooku, just how deeply it went&hellip;but still, we should have been able to foresee our doom. We were peering outward, when we should have been looking at ourselves.|Ferren Barr, on the fall of the Jedi Order|<ref name="Dark Lord 14" />}}
[[File:BarrsFiles-2017DarthVader14.jpg|thumb|left|300px|By investigating the fall of the Jedi Order, Ferren Barr discovered the conspiracy of Order 66, the return of the Sith, and Darth Vader's former identity.]]
Fascinated by how swiftly the Sith decimated the Jedi Order, Barr set out to uncover the truth behind the conspiracy that enabled the Jedi's enemies to regain control over the [[galaxy]]. In [[time]] he learned about Order 66 as well as the roles that [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and the [[Cloning|clones]] played in it. He also learned that Anakin Skywalker&mdash;[[Jedi Knight]] and the [[Chosen One]] of the Force&mdash;had turned to the [[dark side of the Force]]. Aided by a [[Unidentified slicer|slicer]], Barr hacked into a [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[Holocamera|holocam]] and discovered footage of Skywalker leading the [[501st Legion]]'s [[Operation: Knightfall|siege of the Jedi Temple]]. Knowing that Skywalker was, in fact, [[Darth]] Vader, [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] and enforcer, Barr was also aware that other Jedi had fallen from the [[light side of the Force]] and became an elite group of [[Jedi hunter]]s known as the [[Inquisitorius]]. His findings were downloaded into the [[R1-series astromech droid]] [[Endee]] for record keeping.<ref name="Dark Lord 14" />

In addition to his investigation, Barr recruited several acolytes to his cause. [[Nipaltoo]], whose [[Palabee|sister]] was one of the [[Jedi Initiate|younglings]] in the Jedi Order, was distraught when he witnessed Palpatine's speech regarding the Republic [[army]]'s [[Republic Military|military]] response to an alleged Jedi rebellion. [[Orvek]] was one of many civilians who were forcefully relocated from their homes by the [[Imperial shock trooper|clone shock troopers]] of the [[Coruscant Guard]]; [[Daren]] felt a debt of gratitude to the Jedi&mdash;in particular, Anakin Skywalker&mdash;for saving his life during the Clone Wars; [[Rebb]] and [[Stell]] opposed the Empire and all that it stood for. Although Barr's acolytes sympathized with the Jedi Order and resented the Empire, he utilized his [[Force power]]s to guarantee their allegiance.<ref name="Dark Lord 16" />

===Planning for the future===
{{Quote|Once the galaxy sees what the Empire is capable of, they will ''resist''. Jedi and Sith are abstract ideas for most. Already, they feel like relics of a lost age. But oppression and freedom&mdash;these are concepts anyone can understand.|Ferren Barr|<ref name="Dark Lord 14" />}}
[[File:Barr-2017DarthVader16.png|thumb|right|300px|Barr's work on Mon Cala was only the beginning; he intended to sow the seeds of rebellion against the Galactic Empire across the galaxy.]]
After recruiting [[Verla]] as his sixth acolyte, Barr and his followers journeyed to the [[ocean]]ic [[planet]] of [[Mon Cala]]. Serving as a Jedi [[advisor]] to [[King of Mon Cala|King]] [[Lee-Char]], Barr encouraged the [[Mon Calamari monarchy]] to inspire hope across the galaxy by openly defying the Empire. The Empire dispatched [[Ambassador]] [[Telvar]] to Mon Cala in an effort to resolve the situation through diplomacy, although the Emperor had already decided to make an example out of the [[Mon Calamari]] to the rest of the galaxy. In order to ensure that nothing would delay the inevitable war against the Empire, Barr sabotaged Telvar's [[shuttle]], killing the Imperial [[diplomat]] in a [[Fire|fiery]] explosion. The [[king]] denied any involvement in Telvar's [[assassin]]ation; nevertheless, the Empire had been given a pretext to invade Mon Cala. At the same time, the Emperor ordered Vader to track down and eliminate Lee-Char's Jedi advisor with the help of the Inquisitors. The king contacted his ally shortly after Vader's arrival, hoping to warn Barr that the Emperor's Jedi hunters had come and were led by a [[Color|black]]-[[Darth Vader's armor|armored]] warrior. Based on the king's description of Vader's appearance, Barr knew that it was, in fact, his former brother in the Force&mdash;Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker.<ref name="Dark Lord 13" />

Using his [[astromech droid]]'s archival footage of Skywalker's exploits during his time as a Jedi, as well as his actions during the execution of Order 66 in the Jedi [[Jedi temple|Temple]], Barr told his acolytes the story of the rise and fall of Skywalker&mdash;how Skywalker became one of the greatest Jedi Knights of his generation until he turned against the Order on the first night of the Purge. His followers insisted on taking the fight to Vader, desiring to avenge the Jedi Order by killing the traitor, but Barr overruled them stating that the former Jedi was a formidable adversary. However, he was fully confident that his plan presented another path; one that would eventually see the Jedi avenged, the Sith destroyed, and the galaxy liberated from the rule of the Empire.<ref name="Dark Lord 14" />

To ensure his plan's success, Barr used his influence with the king to prevent the Mon Calamari from cooperating with the Empire's investigation. Possessed of insight into future events through the Force, Barr revealed that the attack on Mon Cala was only the beginning of the erosion of freedom across every planet in every [[star system]] under the Empire's control. However, he also foresaw that the Mon Calamari would be at the forefront of a rebellious movement that would herald the liberation of the galaxy [[Standard day|day]]. Inspired by Barr's [[Force vision|visions]], King Lee-Char ordered his [[officer]]s, [[Gial Ackbar]] and [[Raddus]] to oversee the [[Mon Cala planetary defense services|military's]] defense of the Mon Calamari [[homeworld]].<ref name="Dark Lord 14" />

===Confronting former comrades===
{{Quote|The past matters. History matters. Truth matters. Once a Jedi, always a Jedi. Execute Order 66.|Ferren Barr|<ref name="Dark Lord 16" />}}
[[File:ExecuteOrder66-2017DarthVader17.png|thumb|left|300px|Using the Force on a squad of Purge Troopers, Barr tricked them into executing Order 66 against a group of renegade Jedi who betrayed the Order.]]
During the Imperial [[Battle of Dac City|assault]] on [[Dac City]], the king informed his Jedi advisor that he had given the location of Barr's [[Ferren Barr's cave refuge|refuge]] to the Inquisitors while under the duress of [[torture]].<ref name="Dark Lord 15">[[Darth Vader (2017) 15|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 15]]</ref> Having achieved his objective to start a war on Mon Cala between its inhabitants and the Empire, Barr intended to do the same thing again on as many worlds as possible until the Rebellion was finally formed. Upon evacuating the chamber, the group was pursued by an [[Imperial submarine]] carrying Vader and the Inquisitors.<ref name="Dark Lord 16" />

The pursuit resulted in the deaths of Nipaltoo and Orvek. Rebb and Stell elected to remain behind and ambush the Inquisitors, hoping to delay their advance while Verla and Barr searched for a way off of Mon Cala. Nevertheless, Barr and his last remaining disciples were cornered in [[Bel City]] by the Inquisitors and a [[squad]] of [[Purge Trooper]]s, with Daren being cut in half by one of them. Deducing that the [[trooper]]s were clones&mdash;the last generation to be activated before the complete shutdown of the cloning production line on [[Kamino]]&mdash;Barr informed them that the Inquisitors were still Jedi in spite of their allegiance to the Empire. Persuaded by Barr's logic and influence through the Force, the clones' indoctrination compelled them to carry out Order 66 against the Inquisitorius<ref name="Dark Lord 16" /> just as Barr anticipated, aware that the clones would follow their programming like [[droid]]s. Barr and Verla were able to use the distraction as an opportunity to escape deeper into the [[city]], leaving the Inquisitors and their soldiers to kill each other.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" />

===Duel with Vader and death===
{{Quote|You assassinated the Imperial ambassador.''"<br />"''Of ''course'' I did. I couldn't take the risk that negotiations would bear fruit, that this inevitable conflict would be delayed. And with you and your hounds hunting me, my time was short. I needed to light a fire&hellip;and so I did.|Darth Vader and Ferren Barr|<ref name="Dark Lord 17" />}}
[[File:BarrVSVader-2017DarthVader17.png|thumb|right|300px|Barr, who died defending King Lee-Char against Vader, had faith that his actions would lead to the eventual destruction of the Sith.]]
Unlike the other acolytes, Verla was attuned to the Force. Serving as her [[Jedi Master]], Barr began instructing her in the Jedi arts but suspected that he would not be able to complete her training. Therefore, he commanded her to leave him and seek out other survivors of the Purge, such as [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], or even [[Quinlan Vos]]. It was his hope that Verla would find a new Master in order to become a full-fledged Jedi.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" />

After parting ways with his apprentice, Barr moved to confront Vader in person. By the time he reached the [[Royal Command Bunker]], [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s [[Imperial Navy|Imperial fleet]] of [[Star Destroyer]]s had begun [[Orbital bombardment|bombarding the planet from orbit]], resulting in billions of casualties. While engaging the Padawan in a [[lightsaber duel]], Vader deduced that Barr was responsible for Telvar's death, an accusation that Barr confirmed. In response to Barr's confession, Lee-Char ordered his forces to stand down and surrender to the Empire, but Tarkin proceeded with the bombardment of Mon Cala regardless. Barr assured the king that the sacrifice of billions would save trillions of lives in the long run, all the while inspiring hope and rebellion across the galaxy.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" />

Although Barr confronted Vader with every intention of defeating him, in the end he was slain in battle by the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]. However, he died confident in the knowledge that his actions would eventually lead to the destruction of the [[Sith Lord]]s and the restoration of freedom throughout the galaxy. In the aftermath of the battle, three Mon Calamari [[cruiser]]s escaped into [[hyperspace]] but Mon Cala was lost. The Empire placed the planet under [[Imperial Military|military]] [[Invasion of Mon Cala|occupation]] and Lee-Char was taken [[prisoner]] by Vader.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" />

===Legacy===
{{Dialogue|Lee-Char|But ''billions'' of people are dying.|Ferren Barr|Billions, who will inspire trillions. As was my plan.|Darth Vader|You are no Jedi.|Ferren Barr|Perhaps not. Not anymore. Makes two of us, eh? But I made my choices. And I might not be a Jedi&hellip;but I still beat the Sith.|attr=Lee-Char, Ferren Barr, and Darth Vader|src=<ref name="Dark Lord 17" />}}
[[File:BarrProphecy-2017DarthVader17.png|thumb|left|300px|The warships of the Mon Calamari would play a vital role in the Rebel Alliance's victory over the Galactic Empire, as prophesied by Ferren Barr.]]
Shortly after the [[Battle of Fortress Vader]], Vader entered a [[portal]] constructed by [[Darth Momin]] to resurrect his wife [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. While walking through visions, he encountered Barr along with many Jedi with their lightsabers ready to battle him. However, Vader killed all the illusions, impaling Barr through the chest along with [[Eeth Koth]] as the words "[[Ben Solo|Let the past die]]" resounded in his mind.<ref name="Dark Lord 25">[[Darth Vader (2017) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 25]]</ref>

Barr's prophecies came to pass many years after his death. As Barr foresaw,<ref name="Dark Lord 17" /> the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]] culminated in the rise of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Alliance DB">{{Databank|rebel-alliance|Rebel Alliance}}</ref> Although his plan led to the subjugation of Mon Cala, his actions ensured<ref name="Dark Lord 17" /> that the Mon Calamari retaliated against the Empire by becoming one of the key [[species]] in the Rebel Alliance,<ref name="Mon Calamari DB">{{Databank|mon-calamari|Mon Calamari}}</ref> as prophesied by Barr.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" /> Determined to restore freedom to the galaxy, the Mon Calamari provided a powerful [[fleet]] of [[capital ship]]s<ref name="Cruisers DB">{{Databank|mon-calamari-star-cruiser|Mon Calamari star cruiser}}</ref> that became the backbone of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]].<ref name="Mon Calamari DB" /> Armed with [[turbolaser]]s, [[ion cannon]]s, [[tractor beam]] projectors and [[deflector shield]] [[Deflector shield generator|generators]], Mon Calamari [[Star Cruiser]]s possessed the firepower and defense capabilities<ref name="Cruisers DB" /> the outnumbered Alliance<ref name="Mon Calamari DB" /> needed in order to contend with the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]].<ref name="Cruisers DB" />

In addition, the Alliance gained the knowledge and tactical expertise of Lee-Char's veteran [[commander]]s, [[admiral]]s Raddus<ref name="Raddus DB">{{Databank|admiral-raddus|Admiral Raddus}}</ref> and Ackbar.<ref name="Ackbar DB">{{Databank|admiral-ackbar|Admiral Ackbar}}</ref> A strong proponent of openly defying the Empire with a powerful military force,<ref name="Raddus DB" /> Admiral Raddus led the Alliance forces against the Empire at the [[Battle of Scarif]] where the Rebels secured [[Death Star plans|vital schematics]] to the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> As a result, the Rebels exploited a flaw in the [[battle station]]'s design that led to its destruction in the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> While the Empire was in the middle of constructing a [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]], Admiral Ackbar masterminded the Rebel attack on the new [[superweapon]] at the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Ackbar DB" /> By the end of the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Sith [[Lord]]s were dead and the Empire was [[Battle of Jakku|defeated]] by the [[New Republic Defense Force|forces]] of the [[New Republic]],<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> again as Barr predicted.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" />

[[Standard decade|Decades]] later, the [[First Order]]<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref>&mdash;a successor to the Empire in all but name<ref name="Battlefront II" />&mdash;[[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroyed]] the Republic [[capital]] of [[Hosnian Prime]] and thereby ended the [[New Republic era|era]] of renewed peace and democracy that the Rebel Alliance fought to create.<ref name="Episode VII" /> Yet, as Barr had foreseen, the Mon Calamari [[First Order-Resistance War|opposed]] the rise of the First Order by contributing their ships to the [[Resistance]], a paramilitary organization led by General [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. The [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] served as Organa's [[flagship]] during the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation of D'Qar]]. [[Hyperspace tracker|Pursued through hyperspace]] by the [[First Order Navy]], the [[Resistance navy|Resistance fleet]] was [[Battle of Oetchi|decimated]] until only the ''Raddus'' remained. In an effort to save the survivors who were in the process of fleeing to the planet [[Crait]], [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] rammed the ''Raddus'' into the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]'' at lightspeed, severely damaging the First Order flagship in the process.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|The past does not die. I ''study'' the past, you see. It's all I've done since I went into hiding after the purge. I tracked down every bit of information about how Palpatine tricked the galaxy.|Ferren Barr, to Verla|<ref name="Dark Lord 16" />}}
[[File:Order66Recording-2017DarthVader14.png|thumb|right|300px|The fall of the Jedi Order had a profound impact on the life of Ferren Barr, who was still a Padawan at the time of Order 66.]]
Ferren Barr was an [[Iktotchi]] [[Gender|male]] with orange skin and brown [[eye]]s as well as two large, curved horns protruding downward from his head,<ref name="Dark Lord 14" /> although the right horn was damaged by the time of the Empire's formation.<ref name="Dark Lord 16" /> As a member of the Jedi Order, Barr believed in the Force and its power to shape the future according to its will. The loss of his Jedi brothers and sisters drove Barr to seek the truth behind the conspiracy that brought down the order he served.<ref name="Dark Lord 14" />

A studious and resourceful investigator, Barr discovered a considerable amount of information pertaining to the rise of Emperor Palpatine as well as Anakin Skywalker's transformation into Darth Vader. Barr regarded Vader and the Inquisitors as traitors to the Jedi Order; because of their betrayal, he felt that the true threat to the order came not from outside the Jedi ranks, but from within. He made no distinction between the personas of Skywalker and Vader; to Barr, they were the same person responsible for the deaths of many Jedi, including the [[Youngling|youngest]] of the order. Like other Jedi of his time, such as Obi-Wan Kenobi whom Barr held in high esteem, Barr believed in Skywalker's destiny as the Chosen One&mdash;the prophesied one who would herald the restoration of balance in the Force by destroying the Sith. The revelation that Skywalker instead betrayed the Jedi Order disillusioned Barr,<ref name="Dark Lord 14" /> who deviated from the Jedi way in his desire to destroy the Sith.<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref>

[[File:BarrScholar-2017DarthVader16.png|thumb|left|300px|Barr's determination to seek the truth enabled him to discover the Sith conspiracy against the Jedi Order.]]
Barr was willing to use the Force to influence his acolytes, ensuring their support for his quest to avenge the Jedi Order.<ref name="Dark Lord 16" /> His long term goal for defeating the Sith entailed a plot to inspire dissent and opposition against the Emperor by maneuvering the Empire into committing genocide on Mon Cala. Although his plan required the loss of billions of lives, he believed it was a necessary sacrifice that had to be made in order to inspire trillions of beings to rise up against the Empire.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" />

In addition to his intellect and propensity for research, Barr was adept at improvisation and adapting to unanticipated setbacks. When the Inquisitorius cornered him in Bel City,<ref name="Dark Lord 16" /> Barr knew that he could use their own troopers to his advantage by making them see the Inquisitors as targets of Order 66 due to their past affiliation with the Jedi Order.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" /> Although the Inquisitors had forsaken their former identities, believing the past to be irrelevant, Barr disagreed. From his perspective, the past, history, and the truth still mattered. Therefore, he argued, a fallen Jedi was still, in fact, a Jedi. As for the clones, Barr had come to view them as mere machines who were bound by programming to be as obedient as a droid.<ref name="Dark Lord 16" />

Barr did not wish to see the Jedi Order end with himself or the few others who managed to survive the initial execution of Order 66. Having found no evidence of the deaths of Yoda, Kenobi, or Quinlan Vos, it was his hope that Verla would complete her training in the ways of the Force under a different Jedi Master, just in case Barr did not survive his encounter with Vader. Although he knew that he could not defeat the Dark Lord in single combat, Barr still confronted his former brother in the Force&mdash;an act motivated by pride, as sensed by Vader. The Dark Lord accused Barr of failing to follow the Jedi way, an assessment which Barr concurred with. Nevertheless, the former Padawan had made peace with his decisions and died believing that everything he had done was in service to the greater good of the galaxy.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" />

==Powers and abilities==
{{Quote|Follow me, and I will teach you how to use this gift.|Ferren Barr, to Verla|<ref name="Dark Lord 16" />}}
[[File:BarrMindTrick-2017DarthVader16.png|thumb|right|300px|As a trained Jedi, Barr was adept at the use of several Force techniques such as telekinesis and mind trick.]]
Like all Jedi, Ferren Barr was [[Force-sensitive]], and therefore, selected for Jedi training. He held the rank of Padawan at the time of the Clone Wars, but his training was incomplete when the Jedi Order fell. As such, he never completed the [[Jedi Trials]] to become a true Jedi Knight. Despite his incomplete training,<ref name="Dark Lord 14" /> Barr was talented in certain aspects of the Force such as [[telekinesis]] and [[mind trick]], which he successfully applied on his followers and the Purge Troopers.<ref name="Dark Lord 16" /> He also possessed a natural affinity to foresee events long after his time. Hence, he knew for certain that the Mon Calamari were vital to the rebellion that would oppose and ultimately defeat the Empire.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" /> Yet despite his ability to predict outcomes well into the future, his foresight was rather limited when it came to events closer to the present. Vader's arrival on Mon Cala came as a surprise to Barr, who had thought that the Force would reveal the time when he would confront the Sith Lord well in advance.<ref name="Dark Lord 14" />

Even without the application of the [[Brain|mind]] trick, Barr was skilled in persuasion. Using his position as both a Jedi and advisor to the king, Barr managed to persuade Lee-Char into openly resisting the Empire in spite of the consequences of defiance.<ref name="Dark Lord 14" /> He also possessed skills in lightsaber combat, to the extent that Barr held his own against Darth Vader, one of the most skilled duelists of the time, albeit briefly. Ultimately, Barr was unable to match the skill of Vader, who slew the former Padawan in single combat. Prior to his death, Barr used his limited training to pass on the Jedi ways to his apprentice, Verla.<ref name="Dark Lord 17" />
