The '''Father''' was a [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Visions 1" /> [[Deity|god]]<ref name="Interview">{{YouTube|video=tcBILt2_hvc|text=Takashi Okazaki, The Creator of AFRO SAMURAI, Shoot Interview!|channel=@CartoonistKayfabe|name=Cartoonist Kayfabe|date=2022-12-14}}</ref> who appeared to the [[Jedi]] known as [[Shogun]] in a [[Force vision|vision]]. Near the end of a [[Sith rebellion|war]]<ref name="Visions 1" /> between the [[Jedi clan]]s and the [[Sith]],<ref name="Ronin">''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]''</ref> Shogun was struck by what seemed like lightning while standing on the top of a tower. The impact caused them to fall from the tower, and they then saw a vision of the Father, who told them to visit the temple [[Temple of Elujoji|Elujoji]] on the [[planet]] [[Sukalan]]. When Shogun asked what awaited there, the Father told them they would find "truth." Later, when Shogun met<ref name="Visions 1" /> the former Sith<ref name="Ronin" /> known as [[the Ronin]], they told him about their vision of the Father and how they were still searching for the temple. The Ronin had heard of the temple but did not know its location.<ref name="Visions 1" />

In Shogun's vision, the Father appeared massive in size. The Father wore large [[Piercing|earrings]] and a [[crown]] and had a long [[Hair|beard]].<ref name="Visions 1" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
The Father appeared in a vision in a flashback in the [[Canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Visions 1|''Visions'' 1]], written and illustrated by [[Takashi Okazaki]],<ref name="Visions 1" /> and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 12]], [[2022]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/101547/star_wars_visions_2022_1|text=Star Wars: Visions (2022) #1}}</ref> The Father's design was based on [[George Lucas]],<ref name="Interview" /> the creator of ''[[Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Art of Visions">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Visions]]''</ref> The scene in which the Father appeared was influenced by [[Wikipedia:The Incal|''The Incal'']],<ref name="Interview" /> a comic series by [[Wikipedia:Alejandro Jodorowsky|Alejandro Jodorowsky]] and [[Wikipedia:Jean Giraud|Mœbius]].<ref name="Incal">{{WebCite|url=https://www.humanoids.com/book/564|text=THE INCAL|work=[https://www.humanoids.com Humanoids]|archivedate=20240524171857}}</ref>
