The '''Duor's language''' was used by members of the [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]], [[dolphin]]-like [[Duor]] [[Species/Legends|species]] which was [[Homeworld/Legends|native]] to the [[Waterworld/Legends|waterworld]] [[Arrakan]]. It incorporated whistling and clicking sounds, and the Duor, who could also communicate [[Telepathy/Legends|telepathically]], often used the [[Language/Legends|language]] to discuss philosophy or high science with other members of the species. To individuals who did not speak the Duor's language its sounds could have an endearing quality.<ref name="Valkyrie20" />

{{RPG|"[[Flight of the Jedi]]"}}
At one point during the [[Great Jedi Purge/Legends|Great Jedi Purge]] and the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], a [[Unidentified protocol droid (Carran)|protocol droid]] who had [[Mission to Arrakan|accompanied]] a group of [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] and their companions to Arrakan's principal [[City/Legends|city]], [[Kearleonis]], he attempted to decipher the language used by a group of Duor who appeared to be playing in a nearby [[Water/Legends|pool]]. To the [[Protocol droid/Legends|droid's]] surprise, he discovered that the Duor were in fact discussing quantum physics. The Jedi and their companions subsequently went on to establish telepathic contact with the Duor.<ref name="Valkyrie20" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Duor's language appeared in the second part of [[Phil Sidebotham]]'s [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure "[[Flight of the Jedi]]," which was published in the Spring [[2000]] [[Valkyrie 20|twentieth issue]] of the ''[[Valkyrie]]'' magazine for use with [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. Since that article was released outside of the [[Lucas Licensing]] process,<ref name="Valkyrie20" /> it was considered non-[[canon]]ical with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="UnlicensedStatus">{{UnlicensedStatus}}</ref>
