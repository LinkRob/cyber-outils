==Behind the scenes==
Cassian Jeron Andor's father was first mentioned in the [[2016]] book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''.<ref name="Visual Guide" /> The television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' later established that [[Clem Andor]], who [[Death|died]] on the [[planet]] [[Ferrix]], was Cassian's adoptive [[Parent|father]].<ref name="Episode 3" /> As this is different from his fate on [[Carida]], established in ''Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide'', the two must be distinct individuals. Furthermore, the article "Cassian Andor 101: The Story of a Rebel" by [[Kelly Knox]] opens up the possibility that the story of Andor's father dying in a Clone Wars protest was false and simply something included in his file.<ref name="Cass 101" /> Additionally, the [[Star Wars Insider 212|''Star Wars Insider'' 212]] article "[[From the Shadows: The Dangerous Life of a Rebel Agent]]" confirmed that records surrounding Andor's early life were poorly documented and of dubious accuracy.<ref name="Insider" />
