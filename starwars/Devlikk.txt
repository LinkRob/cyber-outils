'''Devlikk ''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]. [[Wan Sandage]], a [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] from [[Ord Radama]], who participated in the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] race held on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] during [[32 BBY]] was a Devlikk.<ref name="CL 2016" />

During the early [[Imperial Era]], a Devlikk was present on [[Janix]] in [[The Mark and Claim]] [[casino]], owned by [[Rheena Sul]], where the patron tipped off the [[Galactic Empire]] of a group of fugitives, [[Brander Lawson]], his son [[Rylee Lawson]], and two Jedi [[Devon Izara]], and her [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]].<ref name="C6 NotH">{{MSL|Chapter 6: Night of the Hunted}}</ref>
