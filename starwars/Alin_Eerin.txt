'''Alin Eerin''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Mon Calamari]]<ref name="Uprising" /> and a member of the [[Data Seekers]], a secretive organization that sought to unravel the mysteries of the [[Anoat sector]]'s shrouded history<ref name="Forums" /> during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Caught behind the [[Iron Blockade]] enacted by Imperial [[Governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] in the wake of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star's]] [[Battle of Endor|destruction]], Eerin operated in the [[sector]] on [[Planet|worlds]] such as [[Bespin]] and [[Burnin Konn]],<ref name="Uprising" /> where she publicly posed as a traveling [[merchant]], while covertly trading in a variety of secrets stored on [[Hologram disc|holodiscs]].<ref name="Forums" /> She directed the acquisition specialists, whose role was to travel to locations and acquire any data, cargo, or artifacts that would further expand the Seeker's archives.<ref name="AcqSpec">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Acquisition Specialist"</ref>

During a period of [[Uprising|rebellious unrest]] in the [[sector]], the Mon Calamari merchant came into contact with an up-and-coming [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] from Burnin Konn. On a number of occasions, Eerin passed along information and tips leading to opportunities for work to the [[smuggler]], and the two colleagues forged a friendly working relationship. Eerin ensured her associate got a good [[Galactic Credit Standard|deal]] on needed [[droid]] parts, and even asked the smuggler out several times for [[Alcohol|drinks]] after work.<ref name="Uprising" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alin Eerin first appeared in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the multiplayer [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', created by the [[Kabam]] company and released on [[September 10]], [[2015]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms. Within the scope of the game, Eerin acts as one of several quest givers for the player character.
