'''Darth Krall''', originally known as '''Radaki''',<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> was a [[Force-sensitive]]<ref name="Dooku" /> [[Vollick]].<ref name="Species" /> A former [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]], Radaki became part of what was later known as the [[Lost Twenty]] and was seduced by the [[dark side of the Force]] as a result of his belief that the [[Jedi]] should retain their family ties and wealth. Having forsaken his allegiance to the Order, he adopted the name of [[Darth]] Krall and became a powerful [[Sith Lord]].<ref name="Dooku" />

As a [[Sith]], Krall wore a [[Color|black]] and red [[cloak]] and wielded a [[Sith lightsaber]] with a golden [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]].<ref name="THR7 variant reveal">{{Twitter|SWSplashPagePod|status/1409952485649879043|Star Wars Splash Page|quote=Artist Mike Mayhew's variant for #TheHighRepublic #7 features #DarthKrall. Fans of @CavanScott's "Dooku: Jedi Lost" this one's for you. Pre-sales begin Friday, July 2nd at 9am PST/12pm EST at http://mikemayhewstudio.com. #StarWarsComics|archivedate=20210629193437}}</ref> Krall participated in the [[Battle of Wasted Years]], emerged victorious, and tamed the [[Nightmare Conjunction]]. His [[lightsaber]] was stored in the [[Archive of Forbidden Artifacts]], a [[Bogan Collection]], at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Dooku" />

After [[Jedi Knight]] [[Keeve Trennis]] was infected by the [[Drengir]], Krall appeared in her [[Force vision|visions]].<ref name="The High Republic 7" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Krall was first mentioned in the [[2019]] audiobook ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]].<ref name="Dooku" /> The spelling of his name was given in the [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script]] of the audiobook.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> He later debuted in a vision in the [[The High Republic (2021) 7|seventh issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2021)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']], written by Scott, illustrated by [[Georges Jeanty]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="The High Republic 7" /> on [[July 28]], [[2021]].<ref name="THR7 release date">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/94640/star_wars_the_high_republic_2021_7|text=Star Wars: The High Republic (2021) #7}}</ref> Prior to the release of the issue, Krall was first pictured in a preview of [[Mike Mayhew]]'s variant cover released on social media on [[June 29]].<ref name="THR7 variant reveal" /> In [[2025]], Scott confirmed Darth Krall's species to be the same as [[Osika Kirske/Legends|Osika Kirske]]&mdash;a [[Vollick/Legends|Vollick]]&mdash;which was introduced via the [[Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse]] comic book series ''[[Star Wars: Republic]]'' of the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Species" />
