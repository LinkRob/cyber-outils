'''Delta Group''' was a [[Battle group|group]] within the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] under the command of [[General]] [[Vanden Huyck Willard]]. The group was present at the [[Mako-Ta Space Docks]], where it and other sub-groups were to disperse from the rest of the [[fleet]] until it was required to [[Galactic Civil War|fight]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Star Wars 50" />

As the groups were ready to launch into [[hyperspace]], a fault occurred resulting in the [[Starship|ships]] being unable to launch. Shortly after the failed launch, [[Death Squadron]] under the command of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Battle of Mako-Ta|arrived]] and destroyed Willard's flagship, ''[[Yavin's Hope]]''.<ref name="Star Wars 50" />
