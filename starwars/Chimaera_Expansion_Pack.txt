The '''''Chimaera'' Expansion Pack''' is a supplement to the tactical naval warfare game [[Star Wars: Armada|''Star Wars'': Armada]], published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]], and part of the seventh wave. The expansion features a detailed miniature of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] flagship, the ''[[Chimaera]]'', as well as two miniatures of Mandalorian Gauntlets. It was announced on August 25 of 2017,<ref name="Announcement">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/8/25/the-grand-admiral-arrives|text=The Grand Admiral Arrives}}</ref> and released on February 1, 2018.<ref name="Release" />

==Publisher's summary==
''Grand Admiral Thrawn arrives to the battles of Star Wars™: Armada in the Chimaera Expansion Pack. Along with the chance to command your fleet with this brilliant, blue-skinned Chiss tactician, the Chimaera Expansion Pack introduces an Imperial Star Destroyer adorned with a distinctive chimaera design, two Mandalorian Gauntlet squadrons, six ship cards, and seventeen other upgrades. Altogether, these materials don't just give your Star Destroyer a different aesthetic; they allow it to fill entirely new tactical and strategic roles within your Imperial fleet.''

==Contents==
{{Mediacat|from=1|imagecat=Images from the Chimaera Expansion Pack}}
The ''Chimaera Expansion Pack'' contains three miniatures (one for the ''Chimaera'' and two Mandalorian Gauntlets), plus maneuver dials and action tokens, as well as ship and upgrade cards.

==Continuity==
The ''Chimaera'' model in the pack appears to be an [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer/Legends|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]], just as the [[Starship/Legends|ship]] was in the [[Star Wars Legends|old Expanded Universe]].<ref name="HttES">''[[Heir to the Empire Sourcebook]]''</ref> However, the art design instead depicts the ''Chimaera'' as an [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]], as it is in [[canon]].<ref name="Iron Squadron">{{Rebels|Iron Squadron (episode)}}</ref>
