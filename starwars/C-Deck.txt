'''C-Deck''' was a deck on the [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] that was [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|super tactical droid]] [[Aut-O]]'s [[Aut-O's flagship|flagship]]. It contained the [[Starship|ship's]] communications center and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[encryption module]]. This module was sought out by the [[Galactic Republic]] to decode a Confederate transmission from the [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander of the Droid Army]], [[General]] [[Grievous]]. To recover this module, the Republic [[Mission to steal a Separatist encryption module|sent]] [[D-Squad]], an elite [[squad]] of Republic [[droid]]s to infiltrate the ship, and recover it.<ref name="SW" />
