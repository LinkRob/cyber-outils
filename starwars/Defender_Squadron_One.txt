'''Defender Squadron One''', or simply '''Defender Squadron''', was a [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]]. It was made up of twelve [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]]s and its [[commander]] was [[Captain]] [[Vult Skerris]] until his [[death]]<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> during the [[Lothal campaign]], in which Skerris used a [[TIE/D Defender Elite]].<ref name="Rebel Assault">{{Rebels|Rebel Assault}}</ref> Captain [[Benj Dobbs]] led the squadron after the death of Captain Skerris.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />
