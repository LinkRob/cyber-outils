The '''Ewok Slicer''' was a costume owned by the [[Ewok]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Pilbush]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Explosive Ewok|standard outfit]]. This outfit included a pair of [[goggles]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ewok Slicer was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Pilbush, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 3.0.0 alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Slicer AT-ET]].<ref name="Hunters" />
