'''Fikkesstarrl''' was an individual who wrote their signature alongside several others in the [[book]] known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''<ref name="SG">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> at some point between [[10 BBY]] and [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Dates">Fikkesstarrl's signature in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'' is placed chronologically after [[Han Solo]]'s first entry in the [[book]], which mentions the [[death]] of [[Tobias Beckett]]. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates his death to [[10 BBY]]. Also it is placed prior to Solo's second entry in the book, which details events after he had begun working with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], as depicted during the [[Battle of Yavin]], but before he encounters a [[bounty hunter]] on [[Ord Mantell]], which is mentioned as having occurred prior to the [[Battle of Hoth]]. ''Timelines'' also establishes that the [[Standard year|year]] [[0 ABY]] begins following the conclusion of the Battle of [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]] and dates the Battle of [[Hoth]] to [[3 ABY]]. Therefore, Fikkesstarrl's signature in the [[in-universe]] [[book]] ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' must have been written between 10 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and 3 ABY, with Solo's second entry being written after 0 ABY.</ref> The group of signatures were written below an entry by the [[Manumission|manumitted]] [[gladiator droid]] [[Stabinator]] that detailed a [[Grakkus Arena slave uprising|slave uprising]] that had occurred at the [[Grakkus Arena]]. The next entry after the signatures was written by the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]]<ref name="SG" /> at some point after [[0 ABY]].<ref name="Dates" /> Solo's co-[[pilot]], the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]], had carried the book for several [[Standard month|months]] before Solo wrote in it, and claimed that before he obtained it, the book had been passed around between various [[Wookiee warrior]]s.<ref name="SG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fikkesstarrl was mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="SG" />
