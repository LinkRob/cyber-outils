In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> an '''engagement''' between took place in a [[Unidentified system (Sector 56)|system]] in [[Sector 56]]. The battle was between dissidents who were attacking shipping in the sector and the<ref name="TA1" /> [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|7th Fleet]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]'', commanded by [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "[[Core name|Thrawn]]",<ref name="TA1" /> who had recently<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> led the Empire to a tactical victory in the [[Battle of Atollon]].<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

The battle in Sector 56 resulted in a truce which neutralized the threat to shipping, and brought the [[Star system|system]] under [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] control. Following the battle, Thrawn reported his success to [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] in the [[Imperial Palace]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="TA1" />
