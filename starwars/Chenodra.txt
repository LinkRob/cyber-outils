'''Chenodra''' was a stench-ridden [[Spaceport|port]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Battlefront" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Heroes of Rogue One">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Heroes of Rogue One}}</ref> [[Bamayar]]. The port was captured by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] during its [[Battle of Bamayar|deployment]] to the world as a part of the Alliance's [[Mid Rim Retreat|Mid Rim offensive]],<ref name="Battlefront" /> which began around [[1 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> During a cleanup at Chenodra, [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]], better known as Howl, approached the [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldier]] [[Twitch]], who was convinced she and fellow [[soldier]] [[Maximian Ajax]] had made a big mistake during their [[time]] on Bamayar. Instead of bringing up her mishap, Howl talked about the buildings at Chenodra and their columns and arches. Twitch later recounted this to a number of 61st personnel at a gathering after Howl's [[funeral]]<ref name="Battlefront" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="3 ABY">{{BTCdate}}</ref>
