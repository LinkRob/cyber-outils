'''Corellian Reserve''' was an [[alcohol]]ic beverage. When [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]], along with many others in the [[Resistance]], attended a funeral for [[L'ulo L'ampar]] some time prior to the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] shared a bottle of Corellian reserve with her fellow Resistance members during the wake. The bottle had been given to her by her husband [[Han Solo]] a few [[Standard year|years]] earlier.<ref name="Dameron14" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corellian Reserve originates in [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the short story ''[[Simple Tricks]]'', published in the anthology ''[[Tales from the New Republic]]'' from [[1999]].<ref name="ST">{{StoryCite|story=Simple Tricks|book=Tales from the New Republic}}</ref> It became [[canon]] when it was mentioned in the comic issue [[Poe Dameron 14|''Poe Dameron'' 14]], published on [[May 3]], [[2017]].<ref name="Newsarama">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/62841/poe_dameron_2016_14|text=Poe Dameron (2016) #14}}</ref>
