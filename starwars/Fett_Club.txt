'''Fett Club''' was a club organized by [[Dooku/Legends|Darth Tyrannus]] when searching for the [[Clone template/Legends|clone template]] for the [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Grand Army of the Republic]]. It was named for [[Jango Fett/Legends|Jango Fett]], the assumed last surviving [[Mandalorian/Legends|Mandalorian]], who also was a member. Fett was the last surviving member of the club, and was chosen by Tyrannus as the [[Cloning/Legends|prime clone]].<ref name="Club" />
