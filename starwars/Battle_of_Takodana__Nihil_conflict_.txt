The '''Battle of Takodana''',<ref name="Lightsaber Collection">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> also known as the '''attack on Takodana''', was a [[Nihil]] raid on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. [[Krix Kamerat]], the leader of a [[Krix Kamerat's cell|specialized cell]] of the [[pirate]]s who reported to [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]], had developed an obsession with [[Death|killing]] [[Jedi]]. In response, Ro ordered him to find a way to prove himself after a [[Debacle on Vrant Tarnum|debacle]] at [[Vrant Tarnum]] which had caused him to lose much of his unit's strength. Kamerat chose to attack Takodana, which had a [[Jedi Temple (Takodana)|Jedi Temple]], in the hopes of destroying the [[Jedi temple|temple]] and demonstrating to the pirate [[Maz Kanata]], a long-time Takodana resident, that the Nihil were the only true marauders in the area. The lone Jedi at the Takodana temple, [[Savina Besatrix Malagán|Sav Malagán]], was able to drive off the first wave of the attack, but called for reinforcements from [[Starlight Beacon]] as she knew the Nihil would return. Kamerat retreated to [[Dol'har Hyde]] to gather more of his forces, vowing to destroy Malagán's temple.<ref name="HRA 8" />
