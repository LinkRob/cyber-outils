'''Embryo tubes''' were tanks used by cloners to grow [[Cloning|clones]].<ref name="Bounty Lost EG" /> Clones were also stored in embryo tubes, which were filled with preserving liquid.<ref name="Bounty Lost audio">Per the audio description for "[[Bounty Lost]]" on [[Disney+]].</ref>

The embryo tubes from the Bora Vio cloning facility contained, among other things, clones of Kaminoans.
