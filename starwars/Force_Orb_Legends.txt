'''Force Orb''' was a [[The Force/Legends|Force]] [[Force power/Legends|power]] which served as an attack. Through the manifestion of the force an Orb was created of pure [[Force energy]] that obstructed the surrounding environment. Under normal circumstances, it only worked underwater.

[[Kit Fisto/Legends|Kit Fisto]] utilized it during the [[Battle of Mon Calamari (Clone Wars)/Legends|Battle of Mon Calamari]], creating a ball of air underwater and directing it through the legs of a [[Quarren]] [[Crab cannon|artillery cannon]], disabling it.

During the [[Battle of Mon Cala/Legends|Battle of Mon Cala]], Kit Fisto and [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] attempted to use this power to make a makeshift air helmet for [[Padmé Amidala/Legends|Padmé Amidala]] to breathe when [[Riff Tamson/Legends|Riff Tamson]] broke her helmet spitefully with his jaws. They succeeded, but [[Jar Jar Binks/Legends|Jar Jar Binks's]] [[Gungan/Legends|Gungan]] saliva proved a more effective seal.
