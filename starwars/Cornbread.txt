'''Cornbread''' was a type of [[Color|brown]] [[Diet|vegetarian]] [[bread]]. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] created the [[Foodstuff|dish]] known as [[Melitto Sap Cake]]s by sweetening individual cornbreads with the [[Bleeding-rock sap|sap]] of the [[bleeding rock]] organism found on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[Planet|world]] [[Li-Toran]] during the culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]]. By 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]]<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> he had published the recipe for the [[cake]]s, including a mention of cornbreads, in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]''.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[Wikipedia:Cornbread|Cornbread]] was mentioned in the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
