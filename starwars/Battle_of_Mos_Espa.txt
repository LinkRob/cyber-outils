==Prelude==
After the [[Pyke Syndicate]] [[Destruction of Garsa Fwip's Sanctuary|bombed]] [[Garsa Fwip's Sanctuary]], [[Boba Fett]] and his enforcers [[Fennec Shand]], [[Din Djarin]], [[Krrsantan]], his two [[Gamorrean]] guards and the [[Mods]] took their positions around the city of [[Mos Espa]] in preparation for the oncoming battle. Fett, Shand, and Djarin took defensive stance at the ruined remains of the Sanctuary, anticipating the arrival of the citizens of [[Freetown]] to reinforce them soon, as [[Cobb Vanth]] opposed the spice trade.<ref name="Chapter 7" />

Fett was soon greeted by his former mentor [[Cad Bane]], who was working on behalf of the Pykes to persuade Fett to surrender and allow the Pykes to continue the [[spice]] trade on [[Tatooine]]. He told Fett that he had dealt with the people of Freetown and their sheriff, telling him that there was no one coming to help him. He also revealed that the Pykes were responsible for the massacre of the [[Unidentified Tusken Raider tribe|Tusken Raider tribe]] that had taken Fett in after his escape for the [[sarlacc]] pit. Fett kept his composure and told Bane that the Pyke presence would not be tolerated. Bane stalked away, saying that if he did not surrender, then he and his forces would be crushed.<ref name="Chapter 7" />

==The battle==
Fett weighed his options with Shand and Djarin, but they soon received multiple distress calls from their other [[Fett gotra|gotra]] forces informing them that the three crime families that Fett had negotiated a truce with had turned on them, as they secretly opposed Fett's rule due to him limiting their choices of revenue and wanting to fill the power vacuum that would emerge with his disposal.<ref name="Chapter 7" />

The Aqualish family engaged the [[Mods]] in the alleys of Mos Espa, the Trandoshan family fought Krrsantan in the streets while the Klatooinians killed Boba's Gamorrean guards at the upper sprawl. As Fett was informed of the situation, he asked Shaiz's [[Mok Shaiz's majordomo|majordomo]] where the syndicate operated out of. In his usual response, the majordomo tried to evade the answer, only for Shand to threaten to shoot him, so he told them that they were operating out of the Desert Survey Office in [[Mos Eisley]]. Fett ordered Shand to go there to [[Assassination of the Pyke Syndicate leaders|assassinate them]], while he and the others would fend the syndicate off.<ref name="Chapter 7" />

Krrsantan was initially overwhelmed by the sheer amount of Trandoshans while the Mods were nearly overrun by the Aqualish, but Shand answered their call and slew most of the Aqualish attackers, forcing them to retreat. Shand then told the surviving Mods to head to Sanctuary before leaving the town. Likewise, Krrsantan was able to defeat the Trandoshans and made his way to Sanctuary.<ref name="Chapter 7" />

Back at Sanctuary, the Pykes emerged, with an [[Unidentified Pyke capo|Pyke capo]] demanding Fett's surrender. The majordomo offered to surrender in Fett's place, and he agreed, writing what he thought was surrender terms. When he came out to meet the capo, he only then discovered that Fett did not make any concessions and tried to change the subject to no avail. Fett and Djarin then appeared, having used the majordomo as a distraction. They slew many Pykes, and while they managed to get a several shots on them, their [[beskar]] armor protected them from serious injuries, though they were nearly overwhelmed from the sheer amount of enemies. However, the Pykes were forced to retreat when the people of Freetown came to help them along with Fett's remaining forces; the people of Freetown had been angered at how Vanth was shot in cold blood and knew that the spice trade would harm the planet.<ref name="Chapter 7" />

The Pykes then deployed two [[Scorpenek annihilator droid]]s to resume their offensive. Due to the droids' shielding and wanting to avoid collateral damage to the town, Fett left for [[Boba Fett's Palace|his palace]] to get his [[Boba's rancor|rancor]] while tasking Djarin with protecting their forces. Djarin managed to distract one droid while another went for the remaining forces. Fett returned with his rancor and with the help of Djarin and Grogu, managed to destroy the droid chasing them. Meanwhile, the Mods and Freetown forces managed to inflict sizeable casualties onto the Pykes before Fett arrived and destroyed the other droid.<ref name="Chapter 7" />

Fett and the rancor proceeded to kill a few more Pykes before Bane appeared and scared the rancor off with his flamethrowers. Bane initially had the upper hand on Fett, but Fett was able to recover and slew him with his [[Gaderffii stick]]. Djarin tried to calm Fett's rancor down, who had gone on a rampage and was further exacerbated by the Mods and Freetown forces firing on it. He was unsuccessful in doing so, but Grogu managed to use [[the Force]] to lull the rancor into sleep, concluding the battle.<ref name="Chapter 7" />

==Aftermath==
Unwilling to incur any more losses, the Pyke boss ordered his remaining forces to fall back to [[Mos Eisley]]. Meeting with Mayor Shaiz and the three family dons, he informed them of his decision to withdraw from Tatooine, which was met with protests from the dons, who argued they suffered losses too and that they had a deal. The Pyke boss stated that their deal was that Tatooine would be a hospitable place to do business, citing the Pykes' losses in Mos Espa when Shaiz tried arguing the contrary.<ref name="Chapter 7" />

Suddenly, their meeting place came under attack by Fennec Shand, who quickly killed the Pykes guarding the outside. The Pyke boss tried calling out for his guards as he and the dons drew their blasters. Shand soon shot through the walls, immediately killing the dons and then the two Pyke bodyguards. A panicking Shaiz was then snagged by a [[grappling line]] from an opening in the ceiling and was strangled to death. Left as the last one standing, the Pyke boss tried shooting through the ceiling before noticing a shadow pass by the window in front of him. Shand used the distraction to sneak up and stab him in the back, killing him. She then left the dwelling soon afterwards.<ref name="Chapter 7" />

As peace returned to Mos Espa and the citizens beginning to rebuild, Fett and Shand walked the streets, with the Daimyo frequently being thanked for his efforts.<ref name="Chapter 7" />

Meanwhile, a reunited Din Djarin and Grogu depart Tatooine in his new [[Din Djarin's N-1 starfighter|N-1 starfighter]].<ref name="Chapter 7" />
