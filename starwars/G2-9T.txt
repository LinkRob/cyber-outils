'''G2-9T''' was one of the supposedly rogue [[droid]]s that attacked [[Constable]] [[Zuvio]] during his investigation of a [[Unidentified Western Reaches Exchange Banking Ship|banking ship]] robbery in [[Niima Outpost]].

==Biography==
As a part of [[Rikard Lovas]]'s plot to steal the electronic [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] of the citizens of [[Niima Outpost]] on [[Jakku]], G2-9T and several other [[droid]]s were sent to an [[Unidentified moisture farm (Jakku)|abandoned farmhouse]] to wait for [[Zuvio]] as he tracked the missing [[secretary droid]] [[CZ-1G5]]. Before Zuvio arrived, G2-9T distributed the weapons CZ-1G5 had stolen from the [[Office of the Constable]] among the droids. G2-9T was destroyed when an [[EG-6 power droid]] caused the farmhouse to explode.<ref name="HNJ" />

==Characteristics==
G2-9T had a broken [[Speech scrambler|voice modulator]] which emitted a grinding noise.<ref name="HNJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
G2-9T first appeared in [[Star Wars]] [[canon]] in the [[2015]] short story "[[High Noon on Jakku]]," which was written by [[Landry Q. Walker]].<ref name="HNJ" />

G2-9T shares its name with the [[G2-9T/Legends|G2 repair droid]] which appears in [[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']], part of the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity.
