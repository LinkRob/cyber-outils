{{Quote|Well, I admire anyone who can crawl their way out of the sewer. Especially a sewer as putrid as Corellia.|[[Dryden Vos]], to Han Solo|<ref name="Solo" />}}
'''Corellia''' was a [[planet]] located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Core Worlds]] known for its ace [[pilot]]s and large [[starship]]s. The planet's vast [[shipyard]]s produced many vessels over hundreds of years, including [[starfighter]]s and [[Star Destroyer]]s for the [[Galactic Empire]] as well as other famous models, such as the [[YT-1300 light freighter]].

It was the [[homeworld]] of [[Han Solo]], [[Qi'ra]],<ref name="Most Wanted" /> [[Wedge Antilles]], [[Crix Madine]], [[BoShek]],<ref name="Ultimate SW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> the [[Besalisk]] [[Gadren]],<ref name="Twilight Company" /> the [[Executive Records Officer]] [[Winshur Bratt]],<ref name="Resistance Reborn" /> the [[bounty hunter]]s [[Dengar]]<ref name="Galactic Defense">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''</ref> and [[Mercurial Swift]],<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> and the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Kalifa]]<ref name="SWE">{{SWE|characters|kalifa|Kalifa}}</ref> and [[Petro]].<ref name="Episode Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-gathering-episode-gallery|text="The Gathering" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 8}}</ref>

==Description==
[[File:Corellia_Flag.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Flag of Corellia]]
Corellia was located in the [[Corellian sector]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> in the [[Core Worlds]]. It had a temperate climate and was covered in [[forest]]s, [[jungle]]s, and urban centers.<ref name="Corellia" /> The official [[planet]]ary [[color]]s of Corellia were blue and gold.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> One major [[city]] was [[Coronet City]] (also the planet's capital), which was home to [[Santhe Shipyards|shipyards]] that, during the [[Imperial Era]], produced [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] and [[Star Destroyer]]s for the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Solo" /> The shipyards were one of Corellia's most prominent characteristics in addition to being known for producing capable [[pilot]]s.<ref name="BioGal1">{{Databank|han-solo-biography-gallery|Han Solo Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 12}}</ref> Corellia also manufactured the [[YT-1300 light freighter]], including the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="MillenniumFalconSWE">{{SWE|technology|millenniumfalcon|''Millennium Falcon''}}</ref>

The planet's [[human]] inhabitants were called [[Corellian]]s<ref name="Twilight Company" /> and were known for their wanderlust.<ref name="Corellia" /> Besides humans, Corellia was also inhabited by several [[alien]] [[species]] including [[Barbadelan]]s,<ref name="Solo" /> [[Besalisk]]s,<ref name="Twilight Company" /> [[Czerialan]]s, [[Grindalid]]s,<ref name="Solo" /> [[Kel Dor]], [[Pa'lowick]]s, [[Rodian]]s, [[Sullustan]]s,<ref name="Most Wanted" /> [[Twi'lek]]s, and [[Ugor]]s.<ref name="Solo" /> In addition, Corellia was also home to several native creatures including [[Corellian hound]]s<ref name="Corellia" /> and [[fleek eel]]s.<ref name="The Official Guide" /> The [[politics]] of Corellia were often dangerous and sometimes drew blood, with different politicians vying for new positions and engaging in shady deals.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

==History==
===Early history===
{{Quote|There's so much history on Corellia! Too bad it's such an awful place.|[[DK-RA-43]] in the ''[[Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''|<ref name="Explorer's Guide" />}}
An ancient Core World,<ref name="Corellia" /> Corellia was the [[homeworld]] of natives known for their wanderlust. In the time before they explored the [[star]]s, Corellians embarked on [[ocean]] voyages to satisfy that wanderlust,<ref name="The Official Guide" /> traversing the seas to discover new continents and catch vast quantities of [[fish]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> After the advent of space travel, Corellian royalty sponsored exploration and colonization efforts that helped expand galactic civilization by furthering the frontiers of the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] in its youth.<ref name="The Official Guide" /> Corellian colonists settled on thousands of worlds, and its [[hyperspace]] [[scout]]s paved many of [[the galaxy]]'s major [[hyperspace route]]s, making Corellia an important planet in the Galactic Republic.<ref name="Explorer's Guide" />

The planet became known through the [[galaxy]] for the Corellian people's wanderlust and its massive [[shipyard]]s.<ref name="Corellia" /> Eventually, the ancient Corellian royalty, which included [[Prince-Admiral]] [[Jonashe Solo]], faded away and the throne stood vacant for many [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="Tales from Vandor" /> In the [[Millennium|millennia]] leading up to the Imperial Era, Corellia's key role in the expansion of galactic civilization<ref name="Corellia" /> decreased and power shifted to younger, more vibrant planets. The once influential world gained a reputation as a polluted planet with extensive poverty and crime.<ref name="Explorer's Guide" /> With its importance in galactic affairs reduced, Corellia was nonetheless recognized for its historic significance.<ref name="The Official Guide" />

===High Republic Era===
{{Quote|Help us stop the Nihil. Help us save Corellia. If this planet falls to the Nihil, who knows what planet will be next, and you may have your gripes with the Republic, but that's nothing compared to the brutality and horror that life will become under the Nihil. Come to the shipyard! Come now! Fight for Corellia! Fight for the galaxy! Fight for light and life!|[[Alys Ongwa]]|<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />}}
The population came to show isolationist tendencies during the [[High Republic Era]], leading to the creation of the satirical song ''[[Corellia for Corellians]]''. The [[Nihil]] launched an [[Infiltration on Corellia|infiltration]] of the Corellian government to help with the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], but the infiltrators were defeated by a force of [[Jedi]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> Nevertheless, the infiltration set the stage for a [[War on Corellia|Corellian civil war]] in [[229 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> A [[hostage situation in Coronet City]] dragged on for several weeks.<ref name="Adventures 3">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 3|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 3]]</ref> The [[massacre at the Greens]] effectively wiped out the Nihil presence on Corellia.<ref name="Adventures 7">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 7|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 7]]</ref>

===Twilight of the Republic===
The [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] was involved in an incident on Corellia that he later said was humiliating.<ref name="Bane's story">{{StoryCite|story=Bane's Story|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}}</ref> [[Senator]] [[Garm Bel Iblis]] represented Corellia in the [[Galactic Senate]] by the end of the [[Clone Wars]], but he was one of several senators who grew opposed to [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] governance.<ref name="The End of the Clone Wars">{{EncyclopediaCite|The End of the Clone Wars}}</ref> The obscure [[Republic Group]] allegedly had ties to holding companies on Corellia, as well as some on Coruscant.<ref name="YTManual">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>

===Age of the Empire===
{{Quote|I don't like the Empire ''[&hellip;]'' because it's what I ran away from on Corellia. It's a machine that turns people and places into credits, leaving the grass dead and the air polluted and the sea empty.|[[Tivoche Bilure|Midnight]], on the Galactic Empire's effect on Corellia|<ref name="Tales from Vandor" />}}
[[File:Coronet_City.png|thumb|200px|left|Coronet City in 13 BBY]]
When the Galactic Empire came to power, Corellia's shipyards were transformed into Imperial facilities,<ref name="The Official Guide" /> and though Corellia was a faded industrial world, it was used to build [[TIE series|TIE]] [[Starfighter|fighters]] and Star Destroyers for the [[Imperial Navy]]. Many impoverished Corellian commoners sought to escape their homeworld for a better life.<ref name="Corellia" /> In the immediate aftermath of the Clone Wars, the [[Barsha family]] was set to take the lead in Imperial [[destroyer]]-production on Corellia through its factories, with family heir [[Jaylen Vrax|Jaylen Barsha]] expecting them to become the richest family on Corellia thanks to these contracts and for thousands of destroyers to be built in their facilities. However, the family "mistake" [[Sliro Barsha]] spotted an opportunity to make a name for himself and accused his family of plotting against Palpatine, destroying the Barsha family's standing and leading to his own rise through the Imperial ranks. Additionally, Sliro dispatched the reprogrammed [[BX-series droid commando]] [[ND-5]] to wipe out his fellow Barshas, although Jaylen would manage to subdue and twist the droid to his own ends.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

By [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Solo ate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' which take place on [[Corellia]] to [[13 BBY]].</ref> the [[Corellian Security Forces]] including its [[Imperial Emigration Officer|emigration officers]] had become an extension of the Imperial security forces on Corellia with [[Patrol trooper|Patrol Troopers]] assisting in policing matters.<ref name="The Official Guide" /> Several crime syndicates including [[Lady]] [[Proxima]]'s [[White Worms]] operated in the coastal Coronet City.<ref name="White Worms">{{Databank|white-worms|White Worms}}</ref> The White Worms used local street urchins called [[scrumrat]]s as vermin-catchers, pick-pockets, errand runners, and crime operatives.<ref name="Scrumrats">{{Databank|scrumrats|Scrumrats}}</ref> Two notable scrumrats were [[Han Solo|Han]] and [[Qi'ra]] who sought to escape the White Worms and Corellia by going offworld.<ref name="Solo" /> The [[Unbroken Clan]] had a [[Unbroken Clan Palace|palace]] located on Corellia where the [[Grand Leader]] resided.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 20" />

After escaping the [[Den of the White Worms]], Han and Qi'ra tried to escape through [[Coronet City Spaceport]] by bribing an [[Falthina Sharest|emigration officer]]. However, Qi'ra was recaptured and sold to [[Sarkin Enneb]]. Han managed to travel offworld and join the [[Carida Academy]] as an [[Imperial cadet]] with the hopes of becoming a [[pilot]]. Due to his insubordination, however, Solo was reassigned to the [[Imperial Army]] and became a [[Imperial swamp trooper]] on [[Mimban]]. Meanwhile, Qi'ra became the chief [[lieutenant]] to [[Dryden Vos]] in the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime syndicate.<ref name="Solo" />

Around [[10 BBY]], [[Nakano Lash's crew]] undertook a [[Mission to Corellia (Nakano Lash's crew)|mission to Corellia]],<ref name="Timelines" /> as part of a war between the Unbroken Clan and the Mourner's Wail crime syndicates.<ref name="BH2" >[[Bounty Hunters 2|''Bounty Hunters'' 2]]</ref> Also around ten years before the [[Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="Date Tweet">{{Twitter|AlexanderMFreed|status/594533955975557120|[[Alexander Freed]]|quote=I'd guess around 10 BBY, give or take a few years. But if it's not in the story, it's up for debate (and therefore not my call).|archivefile=File:AlexanderFreed-AntronBach.png|archivedate=20200624065212}}</ref> an organization known as the [[Corellian Resistance]] operated on the planet, [[Corellian insurgency|fighting]] against the Empire. One of their members, [[Miru Nadrinakar]], attempted to warn them of an upcoming crackdown.<ref name="TeoH">{{InsiderCite|154|The End of History}}</ref> [[Smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]] made use of a [[Sana Starros' crash pad|crash pad]] on Corellia that she stocked with supplies, including [[tea]],<ref name="Aphra 7">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 7|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 7]]</ref> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="3 ABY Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 7|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 7]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref>

Around that same time, Jaylen Barsha&mdash;by that time going by the name Jaylen Vrax&mdash;embarked on a [[Jaylen Vrax's campaign|campaign]] to finally unseat his brother Sliro, who by that time was a [[director]] in the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] and leader of the undercover crime syndicate [[Zerek Besh]], as revenge for their family and himself losing the fortune their Corellian industrial might would have offered. In the end, Vrax succeeded and was granted control of Zerek Besh by the Empire, only for Vrax himself to be killed shortly after by the freed ND-5.<ref name="Outlaws" />

===Rise of the New Republic===
Following the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]], the [[bounty hunter]] [[Dengar]] tried to offer [[Mercurial Swift]] the opportunity to work in a partnership. However, the younger [[bounty]] hunter refused and the two men fought. Dengar warned Swift that the [[New Republic]], the successor to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], would be imposing bounties on bounty hunters and that bounty hunters needed to form a union to work together. However, Swift ignored his advice.<ref name="Aftermath" />

[[File:ShadowofSith-Kizamasked.png|thumb|right|150px|Kiza, Shadow of the Acolytes of the Beyond, was recruited on Corellia.]]
In [[5 ABY]], the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] cult [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] recruited a low-class Corellian [[Pantoran]] girl named [[Kiza]]. The Acolytes worshiped the [[Sith]] and the dark side.<ref name="Empire's End" /> That year, the Acolytes staged an [[Coronet City revolution|attack]] on Coronet City so that Kiza and a boy named [[Remi|Oblivion]] could infiltrate the [[Peace and Security Headquarters]] and steal a [[Kiza's lightsaber|lightsaber]] from the archives beneath the prison. In the process, Kiza [[Death|killed]] numerous [[Peace and Security]] officers while Oblivion [[Dismemberment|severed]] [[detective]] [[Erno]]'s [[blaster]] hand.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>

In the [[Standard month|months]] leading up to the [[Battle of Jakku]], there was closer cooperation between the New Republic and Corellian [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]]. Coronet City Peace and Security began linking its records to the growing New Republic database. This allowed [[Norra Wexley]] and her [[Norra Wexley's team|team]] to [[Capture of Mercurial Swift|track down]] the bounty hunter Swift.<ref name="Empire's End" />

[[File:CorellianNeoImperialShipyard-ToilAndTrouble.png|thumb|180px|left|Facility 301G, a New Republic shipyard on Corellia.]]
In [[9 ABY]], the [[Santhe Shipyards]] were visited by [[Ahsoka Tano]] and General [[Hera Syndulla]] while investigating [[Imperial Remnant]] activities, directed there by an [[HK-87 assassin droid]]. On Corellia, the two met with [[Regional Supervisor]] [[Myn Weaver]], who showed them the facilities of his New Republic shipyard, [[Facility 301G]], where Imperial naval assets are being refitted for the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]]. Inside the shipyard's command center, Tano and Syndulla were attacked by former-Imperial workers while they questioned Weaver's actions.<ref name="Part Two">{{Ahsoka|Part Two: Toil and Trouble}}</ref>

Tano broke out of the command center's window, descending to the factory floor, where she was challenged by the former [[Inquisitor]] [[Marrok]], and the two dueled. Meanwhile, General Syndulla returned to the [[Phantom II]] and chased a [[Super Star Destroyer]] [[hyperdrive]] being ferried into orbit. The confrontations ended with the hyperdrive escaping, and Marrok departing, while [[New Republic soldier]]s arrested several shipyard workers, including Weaver.<ref name="Part Two" />

===First Order===
[[File:ConquestOfCorelliaDogfight-LegacyOfVader6.png|thumb|right|250px|The First Order engages Corellian forces in the air.]]
The [[First Order]] [[Conquest of Corellia|took over]] Corellia to claim its shipyards.<ref name="Legacy of Vader">[[Legacy of Vader 6|''Legacy of Vader'' 6]]</ref> Afterwards, they established a presence on Corellia, beginning a [[Occupation of Corellia|planetary occupation]] and nationalizing the Corellian Engineering Corporation. Soon after the [[Battle of Crait]], the [[Resistance]] launched a [[mission to Corellia]] to free Resistance sympathizers from the First Order. [[Poe Dameron]] and [[Finn]] went undercover to the birthday party of [[Nifera Shu]] to steal a list of Resistance members, but [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] raided the party, forcing the Resistance to flee. As they escaped the planet, they were able to free [[Ransolm Casterfo]] and several other prisoners, and stole more ships for the fledgling [[Resistance navy]].<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />

Around the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Batuu|mission to Batuu]], the [[smuggler]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] led a [[Heist on a First Order train|raid]] against a First Order [[Hovertrain|cargo train]], using the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to steal a shipment of [[coaxium]] from the [[train]] and deliver it to the Resistance.<ref name="Smuggler's Run" />

By [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|IX}}</ref> the [[Corellian Merchants' Guild]] was an influential guild in [[Thieves' Quarter]] on the planet [[Kijimi]].<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Following the [[Battle of Exegol]], Corellia was in [[Uprising on Corellia|open rebellion]] against the First Order.<ref name="TROS Junior Novel">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Corellia has appeared in several spin-off media, including both [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] and [[canon]] works, before making its first onscreen appearance in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" />

In the DVD commentary of ''Solo'', set decorate [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Sandales Lee Sandales] revealed that the film's crew pictured Corellia as the ''Star Wars'' version of [[Wikipedia:Venice|Venice]], [[Wikipedia:Italy|Italy]], specifically an "industrial" Venice. Filming of Corellia scenes took place in [[Wikipedia:Southampton|Southampton]], [[Wikipedia:England|England]], most notably at [[Fawley Power Station]]<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.dailyecho.co.uk/news/16234603.photos-behind-scenes-solo-star-wars-story-films-fawley-power-station|text=PHOTOS: Behind the scenes as Solo: A Star Wars Story films|work=Fawley Power Station &verbar; Daily Echo|archivedate=20240408102802}}</ref>. Additionally, during the film's development, [[Lucasfilm]] design supervisor [[James Clyne]] was asked by the filmmakers to design a complete map of Corellia for the chase scene. Clyne did so, and he drew designs for other locations too, such as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] and [[Cloud City]].<ref name="Solo DVD Commentary">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' DVD commentary</ref>
