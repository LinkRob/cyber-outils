Around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] carried out an '''assault on Coruscant''',<ref name="Comp SWE" /> also known as a '''mission to Coruscant''',<ref name="Star Wars 44">[[Star Wars (2020) 44|''Star Wars'' (2020) 44]]</ref> to damage a statue of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]: at that time, both Palpatine and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] were dealing with an [[Force Wave|anomaly]] in [[the Force]], so the Alliance hoped the assault would rattle the pair, pushing them further off-balance, and also remind [[the galaxy]] that the Alliance continued to fight the [[Galactic Empire]]. By striking at the Imperial [[capital]] [[planet]] of [[Coruscant]], the rebels hoped to show they were strong and unafraid. The plan for the assault was devised by [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who, along with [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]], presented the plan to a group of elite [[rebel pilot]]s.<ref name="Star Wars 36" />

[[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor|A-wing]] pilots [[Nien Nunb]], [[Freyta Smyth]], and [[Bryen Rowne]] joined [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Chewbacca]] for the mission. Although the mission resulted in the [[death]]s of Smyth and Rowne, it was ultimately a success, as the target was taken out and news of the daring rebel attack reached the [[HoloNet]].<ref name="Star Wars 36" />
