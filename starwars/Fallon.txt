The '''''Fallon''''' was a [[WD-2550 Whydah-class Yield Conveyer|WD-2550 ''Whydah''-class yield conveyer]]<ref name="Databank" /> operated by the [[Trade Federation]] during the [[High Republic Era]], commanded by the [[Neimoidian]] [[captain]] [[Blex]]. [[Verosha Aniseya]] and [[Fillik]] worked as [[meknek]]s aboard the [[freighter]] for some [[time]]. When Aniseya was incriminated with her [[twin]] sister [[Mae-ho Aniseya]]'s [[Death|murder]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Indara]], the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Yord Fandar]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Tasi Lowa]], [[Mission to arrest Osha Aniseya|visited the freighter]] to apprehend her. Although Osha claimed she was not the murderer, a [[Cabuck|witness]] brought in by the [[Jedi]] identified her, and the Jedi sent her to [[Coruscant]] aboard a [[Palwick|prison transport]].<ref name="Lost / Found" />
