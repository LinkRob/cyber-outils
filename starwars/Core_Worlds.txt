{{Quote|Located at the very center of the known galaxy, the Core Worlds have been regarded as some of the galaxy's most influential planets since way back in the days of the Old Republic.|[[Strono Tuggs]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]''|<ref name="TUC">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref>}}
The '''Core Worlds''', also known as the '''Galactic Core''', the '''Core Territories''', the '''Coruscant Core''', the '''Core systems''', or simply the '''Core''', was the area of [[the galaxy]] that bordered the [[Deep Core]] and was bordered by the [[Colonies]]. The Core Worlds contained some of the wealthiest and most prestigious [[planet]]s. [[Coruscant]], the [[Galaxy|galactic]] [[capital]] during the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] and subsequent [[Galactic Empire]], was located in the Core Worlds.

Core World design emphasized freedom of customization, which showed in the modular construction techniques of Core-built ships.<ref name="TLJ Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Citizens from the Core Worlds were stereotyped as being snobs, rich, and speaking with a clipped, practiced accent.<ref name="Twilight Company" />

Inequalities between the richer Core Worlds and the poorer [[Outer Rim Territories]] set the stage for resentments that fed several conflicts through the [[Timeline of galactic history|history of the galaxy]], including the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> and the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Twilight Company" />

==Description==
The Core Worlds was a region of [[the galaxy]] considered part of the "inner [[planet]]s" and consisted of a ring wrapped around the [[Deep Core]], with the [[Colonies]] region in turn wrapped around the Core Worlds. Worlds of the region were made hubs for fashion, education, finance, and technology due to important [[hyperspace route]]s, earning more fame and notoriety than more distant worlds in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==History==
===Early history===
Although it was disputed, Coruscant<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>&mdash;an [[ecumenopolis]] located in the Core Worlds region of the galaxy<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref>&mdash;was generally considered the [[homeworld]] of the [[human]] [[species]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> For [[Millennium|millennia]] the Core Worlds served as the seat of [[Galaxy|galactic]] power,<ref name="VE" /> and with a [[Timeline of galactic history|history]] dating back to the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]], Coruscant served as the galactic [[capital]] throughout the [[Republic Era|rise]] and [[Republic Era|fall]] of the modern Galactic Republic, and retained its [[Politics|political]] status during the subsequent [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Due to Coruscant's importance to galactic society and the economy, it held the coordinates 0-0-0 on all standard navigation charts.<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> Important Republic institutions such as the [[Galactic Senate]]<ref name="Episode I" /> and the [[Supreme Court]] were based on Coruscant.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>

In the ancient past, the [[Sith]] built a [[Shrine in the Depths|shrine]] on Coruscant during their period of galactic domination. Later, the [[Jedi]] built [[Imperial Palace|their temple]] on the foundations of the former Sith shrine.<ref name="Tarkin" /> At one point, the Core planet Chandrila was colonized by settlers who built the [[Old Gather-House]], where generations of colonists lived on different strata for their entire lives.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> Chandrila was regarded as the origin point for concepts like freedom, justice, and [[democracy]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> Core Worlds such as Corellia<ref name="Corellia">{{Databank|corellia|Corellia}}</ref> and Kuat were known for their shipbuilding industry. One important Core-based [[Starship|ship]] manufacturer was [[Kuat Drive Yards]], which supplied vehicles and starships to both the Galactic Republic and the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

===The Great Peace===
[[File:SenateDistrict-ROTS.png|thumb|left|250px|The vibrant heart of galactic politics and culture, Coruscant was the capital of the galaxy for over a millennium.]]
When the Galactic Republic was formed<ref name="Tarkin" /> about [[Millennium|one thousand]] [[Standard year|years]] before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|onslaught]] of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Episode II" /> the Core Worlds became ravenous. They were granted permission to settle new planets and establish trading colonies, either by cutting deals with indigenous populations or simply by overrunning them.<ref name="Tarkin" /> Inhabitants of the Core Worlds had a distinctly clipped, superior accent, and were generally regarded as snobs.<ref name="Edge of the Galaxy" /> The peaceful planet of Alderaan was regarded as one of the founding members of the Galactic Republic.<ref name="DB Ald">{{Databank|alderaan|Alderaan}}</ref>

During the [[High Republic Era]], the Core Worlds were overpopulated, leading to many of their poorer citizens, along with those of the Colonies, to move to the [[Outer Rim Territories]] in search of new lives. By the time of the High Republic, Coruscant served as the headquarters of both the Galactic Republic and the [[Jedi Order]] where important decisions were made.<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> The Core Worlds largely wrote off the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] as a problem for the Outer Rim, with even the Jedi based in the Core not being immune to the sentiment. However, the Nihil launched a major operation in the Core through the [[infiltration on Corellia]], which occurred at the same time as the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref> With it clear the Nihil had a special weapon, creatures known as the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]], that could kill Jedi, the Order withdrew all its members back to Coruscant after [[Starlight Beacon]]'s fall to keep them safe and draft new plans. The mass Jedi retreat back to the Core helped [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] declare the [[sector]]s in<ref name="Eye 2">[[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 2|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 2]]</ref> the [[Occlusion Zone]]<ref name="Timelines">{{SW|url=news/high-republic-show-march-2023|text=New Concept Art and More Revealed on ''Star Wars: The High Republic Show''}}</ref> independent.<ref name="Eye 2" />

Dissatisfaction with [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]]'s handling of the [[Invasion of Naboo|Naboo Crisis]] brought about his political demise and contributed to the ascension of [[Naboo]] [[Senator]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] to the Chancellorship.<ref name="Episode I" /> In secret, Palpatine was the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Sidious, who was dedicated to destroying the Jedi and imposing Sith rule on the galaxy.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> During the Clone Wars, the Republic capital of Coruscant was the subject of [[Bombing of Coruscant's central power distribution grid|Separatist]] [[Battle of Coruscant|attacks]].

===Clone Wars===
The [[Kuat shipyards|shipyards]] of Kuat Drive Yards above Kuat churned out numerous [[warship]]s for the [[Republic Navy]] during the Clone Wars.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> During the conflict, the Republic and the Separatists fought for access to the [[Nexus Route]], a key [[hyperspace route]] that stretched the [[heart]] of both the Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. The [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] feared that the Separatists could use the Nexus Route to slip through their defenses and attack Coruscant.<ref name="The Citadel" /> This fear was realized when a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Separatist fleet]] [[Battle of Coruscant|attacked]] Coruscant in [[19 BBY]] and tried to kidnap Palpatine. The battle ended in the death of the Separatist [[Head of State|political leader]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]].<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:Aldera_City.png|thumb|right|250px|Alderaan was a member of the Core Worlds noted for its influence and peaceful ways.]]
At the end of the Clone Wars, numerous Jedi were [[Operation: Knightfall|killed]] at the [[Jedi temple|Jedi Temple]] by Skywalker, who had become Sidious' new [[Sith apprentice]] Darth Vader and members of the [[501st Legion]]. Shortly later, Palpatine dissolved the Republic and [[Proclamation of the New Order|proclaimed]] the establishment of the first Galactic Empire.<ref name="Episode III" />

===Age of the Empire===
{{Quote|The Empire sat at the center of the galaxy like an enormous black hole&mdash;forever sucking wealth and resources inward. Coruscant glittered while those at the edges suffered.|[[Beaumont Kin]], ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''|<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Declaration2.png|thumb|left|250px|After a thousand years of democracy, Coruscant became the center of Imperial rule.]]
During the Imperial Era, the Empire encouraged skilled agricultural [[scientist]]s like the [[Leonis family]] and [[computer]] experts such as the [[Spanjaf family]] to move from the Core Worlds to Outer Rim worlds such as [[Lothal]], where the Empire had embarked on an ambitious industrial program. Some Core World migrants like [[Leo Leonis]] looked down on their Outer Rim counterparts as unsophisticated and small-minded. In return, Outer Rim locals such as [[Beck Ollet]] regarded Core Worlders as uppity snobs.<ref name="Edge of the Galaxy" /> The [[Royal Imperial Academy]] on Coruscant was regarded as the most prestigious of the Empire's network of [[Imperial Academy|Imperial academies]]. The Royal Academy supplied [[Imperial cadet]]s to the expanding [[Imperial Navy]].<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> Another known Core-based academy was the [[Corulag Military Academy]].<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks" /> While [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] would claim the Empire's system of Moffs existed to advance life across the entire galaxy, not just the Core Worlds,<ref name="Tarkin" /> the Empire, in practice, established a supposed "natural hierarchy" in which those in the Core Worlds were to prosper under the [[New Order]] while those further out in the galaxy would suffer in order to build up the former. The center-based planets, particularly Coruscant, were rewarded with the wealth and resources of the people across the outer systems who bore the brunt of Imperial rule.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />

By [[13 BBY]], the planet Corellia was a faded industrial world that manufactured warships and starfighters for the Empire.<ref name="Corellia" /> It was also dominated by crime syndicates including [[Lady]] [[Proxima]]'s [[White Worms]].<ref name="White Worms">{{Databank|white-worms|White Worms}}</ref> Many Corellians, including<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> future Rebellion leader<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> [[Han Solo]] and future [[Crimson Dawn]] [[lieutenant]] [[Qi'ra]], sought better lives offworld.<ref name="Solo" /> During the Imperial Era, the [[Corellian Resistance]] [[Corellian insurgency|operated]] on the planet Corellia.<ref name="TeoH">{{InsiderCite|154|The End of History}}</ref>

[[File:Alderaanexplosion.jpg|thumb|right|250px|As a demonstration of the Death Star's power, the Empire destroyed Alderaan.]]
Following the [[Battle of Scarif|theft]] of the [[Death Star plans]], [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] used the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]] to [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroy]] the Core planet of Alderaan to punish its inhabitants for supporting the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. With [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] made to watch, the planet's destruction and the deaths of billions of [[Alderaanian]]s was felt [[Disturbance in the Force|through]] [[the Force]] by the [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. Alderaan's destruction followed the Emperor's decision to [[Dissolution of the Imperial Senate|abolish]] the [[Imperial Senate]] and to transfer powers to regional [[governor]]s. Despite the Emperor's intention to use the Death Star to rule the Empire through terror,<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> Alderaan's destruction only increased support for the Rebellion.<ref name="Lost Stars" /> The Death Star was later destroyed during the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Episode IV" />

Solo and Organa later needed to carry out a [[Mission to Lanz Carpo|rebel mission]] to the planet [[Lanz Carpo]], which was one of the smallest worlds in the Core, but also possessed one of its highest crime rates.<ref name="Star Wars 68" /> As the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the Rebellion dragged on, the Core planet of Chandrila supported the Rebel Alliance. As a result, the planet was designated a target of the Empire's [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]].<ref name="Moving Target">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref> Following the deaths of the Emperor and Vader, and the destruction of the second Death Star at the [[Battle of Endor]], crowds celebrated in the streets of the galactic capital Coruscant. Heavy-handed police action led to rioting which eventually descended into an armed insurrection<ref name="Aftermath" /> and [[Coruscant civil war|civil war]].<ref name="Life Debt" />

===New Republic Era===
[[File:ImperialCenter-ROTJ.jpg|thumb|left|250px|After two decades of Imperial rule, the people of Coruscant rejoiced at the death of the Emperor.]]
The [[New Republic]], the successor government to the Rebel Alliance, established its capital on Chandrila in [[4 ABY]]. The [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] was also reconvened in Chandrila's capital [[Hanna City]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> By [[5 ABY]], meager [[Imperial Security Bureau]] forces under [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] struggled to maintain control over a fractured Coruscant. [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]], who had assumed control over the scattered Imperial remnants, ordered [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] not to relieve Coruscant because he regarded it as a symbol of decadence. Meanwhile, the New Republic gained control of the [[HoloNet]] and encouraged the formation of new broadcasters such as the [[Queen of the Core Network]]. The New Republic also used the wreckage of the Death Star to help the Alderaanians build a new [[space station]] as their home.<ref name="Life Debt" />

That same year, members of the shadowy [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] [[Coronet City revolution|attacked]] a [[Peace and Security Headquarters|Peace and Security station]] in [[Coronet City]] on Corellia. [[New Republic Defense Force|New Republic forces]] under Admiral [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]] and [[Commodore]] [[Kyrsta Agate]] later led a [[Defense of Kuat|successful attack]] on the planet Kuat, which led to the surrender of Imperial forces there and the capture of Kuat Drive Yards. As a result, [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]], under orders from Rax, offered to enter into peace negotiations with [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]]. A [[Standard month|month]] later, the [[Imperial–Republic peace talks]] were scheduled to take place on Chandrila. However, the talks were derailed by the [[attack on Chandrila]], which saw numerous New Republic officials killed and wounded.<ref name="Life Debt" />

In the month following the [[Battle of Jakku]], the Empire developed into a [[Inner systems' Imperial remnant|rump state]] that was confined to predetermined areas under Imperial sovereignty in the Core Worlds and the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]].<ref name="Lost Stars" /> The New Republic and the defeated Empire later signed the [[Galactic Concordance]], which imposed harsh war reparations on the Imperial remnant and limited it ability to wage war. The Galactic Concordance also ordered the Empire to cede the former galactic capital Coruscant to the New Republic and to dissolve itself. To ensure that all worlds had an equal say in the governance of the New Republic, the Republic rotated its capital between member worlds. By [[34 ABY]], Hosnian Prime was the capital of the New Republic.<ref name="TFA VD" />

By [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Bloodline Date">''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' establishes that events of the novel ''Bloodline'' occur 28 [[Standard year|years]] after those of the film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to the year [[0 ABY]]; therefore, ''Bloodline'' occurs in [[28 ABY]].</ref> several Core Worlds were represented at the New Republic's Galactic Senate including Coruscant, [[Hevurion]], and Kuat. These planets were represented by Senators [[Arbo]], [[Orris Madmund]], [[Erudo Ro-Kiintor]], [[Ormes Apolin]] respectively; who were aligned with the [[Centrists]] faction. The Centrists advocated a stronger central government and a stronger military. The upmarket planet of [[Harloff Minor]] was near Coruscant. Now a [[Populists|Populist]] senator, Leia Organa visited Harloff for a meeting with the [[Nikto]] [[crime lord]] [[Rinnrivin Di]] during her [[Amaxine warrior crisis|investigation]] into the [[Amaxine warriors (New Republic Era)|Amaxine warriors]]; a front organization run by the [[First Order]], a remnant of the Old Empire. While the Core Worlds remained peaceful for much of the New Republic period, Hosnian Prime saw some outbreaks of violence including the [[Napkin Bombing]] and the [[Tai-Lin Garr's assassination|assassination]] of Senator [[Tai-Lin Garr]].<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>

===Rise of the First Order===
[[File:DestructionoftheHosniansystem.png|thumb|right|250px|The New Republic capital of Hosnian Prime was destroyed by the First Order.]]
In 34 ABY, the First Order, who were based in the [[Unknown Regions]], used [[Starkiller Base]] to destroy the New Republic capital Hosnian Prime and the entire [[Hosnian system]]. The [[Hosnian Cataclysm]] was witnessed by many people across the galaxy. In response, the [[Resistance]] [[Battle of Starkiller Base|attacked and destroyed]] Starkiller Base.<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> Even so, the New Republic collapsed, and the First Order began its [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] to seize [[First Order military|military]] control over the galaxy, with it being predicted that all the major systems would fall to the First Order within weeks of the war's beginning.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> Shortly before the [[Battle of Exegol]] [[35 ABY|one]] [[Standard year|year]] later, the Resistance sent [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Chewbacca]] on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to the Core systems to send out a distress call for help. Ultimately, the two arrived to [[Exegol]] at the head of a [[Citizens' Fleet]] to reinforce the Resistance, helping them decisively win the battle against the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]].<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
