'''Botekin''' was a team sports involving a ball that players passed using a scooped stick.<ref name="Inside the Making">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-maul-shadow-lord-trivia|text=Inside the Making of ''Star Wars: Maul – Shadow Lord''}}</ref> During or after [[19 BBY]],<ref name="SoM Date">{{ShadowofMaulDate}}</ref> [[Marka Weitz]] was considered a professional Botekin player.<ref name="SoM2" />

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Botekin was first mentioned in the [[StarWars.com]] article "Inside the Making of ''Star Wars: Maul – Shadow Lord'',"<ref name="Inside the Making" /> as well as the site's [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry for [[Rylee Lawson]]<ref name="DB Rylee">{{Databank|Rylee-Lawson|Rylee Lawson}}</ref> on [[April 6]], [[2026]].<ref name="Inside the Making" /> The sport then made its appearance in "[[Chapter 3: Whispers in the Unknown]]," the [[Maul - Shadow Lord Season 1|first season]]'s third episode of the [[Disney+]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'', which premiered on [[April 13]].<ref name="Whispers in the Unknown">{{MSL|Chapter 3: Whispers in the Unknown}}</ref> Created for the series, botekin was influenced by real world sports&mdash;[[Wikipedia:Lacrosse|lacrosse]] and [[Wikipedia:Jai alai|jai alai]]. While the sport itself was not seen fully in the episode, series creators had figured out a rule set as reference.<ref name="Inside the Making" />
