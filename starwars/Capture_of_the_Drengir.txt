{{Quote|Long ago, the Sith froze you at the heart of their totems of darkness, cutting you off from the root-mind.|[[Keeve Trennis]], to the Great Progenitor|<ref name="THR 8">[[The High Republic (2021) 8|''The High Republic'' (2021) 8]]</ref>}}
Around [[2500 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Sith]] '''captured''' the [[sentience|sentient]] [[Diet|carnivorous]] [[plant]]-like [[Drengir]] on the [[Amaxine Space Station]].<ref name="Into the Dark">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref> After working alongside each other for a [[time]], the Sith betrayed the Drengir, with the creatures attacking them in response. Fearful of the Drengir's power<ref name="THR 4">[[The High Republic (2021) 4|''The High Republic'' (2021) 4]]</ref> and refusing to be controlled by them,<ref name="THR 7" /> the Sith used four [[binding statues]] to sever the [[Great Progenitor]], the first of the Drengir, from the [[species]]'s hive mind.<ref name="THR 8" /> This caused the Drengir to lie dormant on the Amaxine Station for [[Standard century|centuries]].<ref name="Into the Dark" /> According to the Drengir's perspective of the event, the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Krall]] was one of the Sith leading the fight, attacking the Drengir personally.<ref name="THR 7" />
