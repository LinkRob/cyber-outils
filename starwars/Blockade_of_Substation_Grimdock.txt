{{Quote|And so, of course, we had to bring the fleet in, didn't we? So we did, and blockaded the fools in, but here we are, or were, I suppose, a bit of a standoff really.|[[Captain]] [[Krull]]|<ref name="Last Shot" />}}
The '''blockade of Substation Grimdock''',<ref name="Last Shot" /> also known as the '''Battle of Grimdock''',<ref name="Timelines" /> occurred above the [[prison]] [[moon]] [[Substation Grimdock]] after [[Sergeant]] [[Magernon]] granted [[Magernon's Amnesty|amnesty]] to many of the [[prisoner]]s. [[Starship]]s of many [[pirate]] organizations arrived at the moon to collect the prisoners, and the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] was mobilized, [[Blockade|blockading]] them in and preventing their escape. Eventually, the [[Pau'an]] [[gangster]] [[Fyzen Gor]], who was aboard the pirate [[frigate]] ''[[Radium Destrobar]]'', rammed the ship into one of the other pirate [[flagship]]s, escaping during the destruction. Following the loss of their two largest [[warship]]s, the pirates surrendered.<ref name="Last Shot" />
