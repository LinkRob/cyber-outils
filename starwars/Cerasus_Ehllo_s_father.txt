A [[Gender|male]] '''individual''' who lived during the [[Imperial Era]] was the [[Ehllo family|father]] of the [[human]] male [[Cerasus Ehllo]], who eventually became a [[captain]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]. According to Cerasus, his [[Parent|father]] knew<ref name="Sana Starros 1" /> [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]],<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> and believed there was nothing that an Ehllo could not do.<ref name="Sana Starros 1" /> By around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SanaStarrosDate}}</ref> Cerasus had told his [[Marriage|wife]]<ref name="Sana Starros 1" /> [[Aryssha Starros]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> about his father knowing the [[Emperor]].<ref name="Sana Starros 1" />

Around that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> after [[Attack on the Starros family homestead|capturing]] Starros from her [[Starros Clan|family's]] [[Starros family homestead|ancestral home]] on<ref name="Sana Starros 1" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Hosnian Prime]] and taking her to his [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvette]] ''[[King's Ransom]]'', Cerasus reminded Starros that his father knew the Emperor.<ref name="Sana Starros 1" /> Later, Aryssha's cousin<ref name="Sana Starros 4">[[Sana Starros 4|''Sana Starros'' 4]]</ref> [[Phel Starros]]<ref name="Sana Starros 1" /> noted to her that Cerasus was a fine and well-respected [[Imperial officer]], before adding that his father knew the Emperor. In response, Aryssha dismissively stated she had heard that before.<ref name="Sana Starros 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cerasus Ehllo's father was first mentioned in the [[Sana Starros 1|first issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Sana Starros]]''. The issue was written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Sana Starros 1" /> on [[February 1]], 2023.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/96860/star_wars_sana_starros_2023_1|text=Star Wars: Sana Starros (2023) #1}}</ref>
