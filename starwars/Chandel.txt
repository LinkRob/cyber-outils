'''Chandel''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/the-future-of-the-force-trivia-gallery|text="The Future of the Force" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], specifically at [[Standard Galactic Grid]] square N-6. It lay within the [[Chandel system]] of the [[Ojoster sector]],<ref name="Appendix" /> which was also a part of the [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> Chandel had [[water]] [[Waterworld|covering]] its surface and appeared [[Color|blue]] from [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="The Future of the Force" /> [[Bev Kranti clam]]s, which produced valuable [[Bev Kranti pearl|pearls]], could be found buried in the seabed beneath Chandel's [[ocean]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Timeline">{{RebelsDate|210}}</ref> a [[Taylander shuttle]] heading for Chandel was stopped by an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] and boarded by the [[Seventh Sister]] and [[Fifth Brother]], two [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Jedi hunter|Jedi hunting]] [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]]. The pair captured the [[Force-sensitive]] [[youngling]] [[Alora]], taking the child from her [[Gender|grandmother]], [[Darja]], before the two could reach the world. Shortly after the attack, the former [[Jedi]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] followed decoded coordinates to Chandel after intercepting Imperial transmissions from<ref name="The Future of the Force" /> the planet<ref name="Mustafar">{{Databank|mustafar|Mustafar}}</ref> [[Mustafar]]. Above Chandel, she discovered the derelict [[shuttle]] and spoke with Darja about the incident before vowing to recover the child.<ref name="The Future of the Force" />
