'''Arti Shah''' is an actress and performer who has played several ''[[Star Wars]]'' characters. She worked as the scale motion capture double for [[Lupita Nyong'o]] ([[Maz Kanata]]), and [[Gwellis Bagnoro]] in [[Takodana Castle]] and a [[GNK-series power droid]] in {{Film|VII}} (2015).<ref name="Interview">{{WebCite|url=https://starwarsinterviews.com/sequel-trilogy/episode-vii-the-force-awakens/arti-shah-maz-kanata|text=Arti Shah (Maz Kanata)|author=[[Dennis Pellegrom]]|date=2016-02|work=[[Star Wars Interviews]]|archivedate=20230205201458|nolive=1}}</ref>
