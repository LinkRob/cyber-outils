'''Eurivos HoloDynamics''' was a [[company]] that manufactured [[holoprojector]] components. Some of its processes included [[laser]]-cutting mirrors and prisms.<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> By [[19 BBY]],<ref name="date">{{MOFdate|4}}</ref> the company was one of the few remaining manufacturers on Coruscant. Around that time, Eurivos HoloDynamics hosted [[Imperial Senate|Imperial Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]], who gave a speech on industrial revitalization to a sparse crowd.<ref name="MoF" />
