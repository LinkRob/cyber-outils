"'''Darth Maul and the Rathtars'''" is a short story in the anthology ''[[Galactic Adventures Storybook Collection]]'' written by [[Steve Behling]] and illustrated by [[TomatoFarm]]. It is an adaptation of the comic [[Darth Maul (2017) 1|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 1]].<ref name="Galactic Adventures Storybook Collection" />

{{Shortstory-stub}}

==Plot summary==
A Brutal Training Ground 

Set in 33 BBY, shortly before the Sith reveal themselves to the Jedi Order, Darth Maul finds himself consumed by a restless, stifled rage. Under the strict command of his master, Darth Sidious, Maul is forbidden from engaging with the Jedi, forced instead to remain a ghost in the shadows of the Republic. To hone his lethality and vent his frustrations, Maul travels to the remote Twon Ketee system. His objective is not political or strategic, but primal: he seeks to hunt Rathtars, the galaxy’s most voracious apex predators. Maul views these mindless, tentacled beasts as the only prey capable of challenging his physical limits, treating the hunt as a savage substitute for the Jedi duel he desperately craves.

The Smugglers' Prize 

While tracking a Rathtar through the dense, dangerous terrain, Maul’s hunt is interrupted by the arrival of a group of smugglers known as the Xexto. Using high-tech traps and heavy weaponry, the smugglers successfully subdue several Rathtars, intending to sell them on the black market. Maul, observing from the darkness, initially intends to slaughter the interlopers for interfering with his sport. However, his focus shifts entirely when he discovers the smugglers' secret cargo: a captured Jedi Padawan named Eldra Kaitis. The sight of a Force-sensitive captive acts as a catalyst for Maul’s obsession, presenting a forbidden opportunity to test his Sith training against the very enemy he was born to destroy.

The Dangerous Game 

Maul is faced with a critical dilemma: he must satisfy his bloodlust without alerting the Jedi Council or defying Sidious’s mandate of secrecy. Rather than a direct assault, Maul decides to orchestrate a chaotic environment where he can eliminate the smugglers and duel the Padawan under the guise of the Rathtars' escape. He begins a calculated sabotage of the Xexto operation, manipulating the beasts and the terrain to ensure that no witnesses remain to speak of a red-skinned warrior with a double-bladed lightsaber. This arc highlights Maul's early tactical brilliance and his willingness to risk the entire Sith Grand Plan just to experience the "satisfaction" of a kill, marking the beginning of his lifelong obsession with the Jedi.
