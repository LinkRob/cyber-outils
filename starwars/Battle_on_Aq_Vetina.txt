The '''battle on Aq Vetina''' was a conflict during the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Post-Galactic">{{EncyclopediaCite|Post-Galactic Civil War and the Mandalorian}}</ref> that saw the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="Guide to Season One" /> carry out an attack<ref name="Chapter 8" /> on [[Aq Vetina]],<ref name="The Star Wars Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> a [[planet]]<ref name="EncyclopediaCite">{{EncyclopediaCite|Din Djarin and Other Characters of the Underworld}}</ref> of [[human]]s. The civilians of the settlement were [[Death|slaughtered]] by the [[Separatist Droid Army|droid forces]] of the Separatists, including [[B2-series super battle droid]]s and [[HMP droid gunship]]s. During the attack, a number of [[Mandalorian]] warriors of [[Death Watch]] intervened, fighting back against the [[battle droid]]s. The young [[Din Djarin]], whose [[parent]]s were killed in the attack, was rescued by a [[Unidentified Mandalorian warrior|Mandalorian warrior]] and taken in as a [[foundling]].<ref name="Chapter 8" /> Djarin often thought back on the battle during his adult life.<ref name="Post-Galactic" />
