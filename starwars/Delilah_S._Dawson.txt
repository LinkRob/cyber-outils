'''Delilah S. Dawson''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] author best known for creating the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge]] character [[Vi Moradi]], who has appeared in her books [[Phasma (novel)|''Phasma'']] and ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''. Dawson has also written numerous comics and short stories, including her first ''Star Wars'' project, "[[The Perfect Weapon]]"&mdash;set before the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="The Perfect Weapon" />

Outside of ''Star Wars'', she is known for the ''Blud'' series and the young-adult novels ''Servants of the Storm'' and ''Hit''.<ref name="Simon&Schuster">{{Simon&Schuster|url=authors/Delilah-S-Dawson/84881573|text=Delilah S. Dawson}}</ref>
