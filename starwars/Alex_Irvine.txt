'''Alexander C. Irvine''' is a writer who was writing the canceled [[Mandorla|Nomi Sunrider novel]]<ref name="Sue">{{Blog|eeusuestornii|11|A bit about the new novels.....}}</ref>. Many of his works have appeared under the simpler moniker "Alex Irvine".<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.scifi.com/scifiction/originals/originals_archive/irvine2/irvine2_bio.html|text=Works on Scifi.com|archivedate=20120923134627}}</ref>

His academic background includes an M.A. in English from the [[Wikipedia:University of Maine|University of Maine]] and a PhD from the [[Wikipedia:University of Denver|University of Denver]]. He is an assistant professor of English at the University of Maine. He also worked for a time as a reporter at the [[Wikipedia:The Phoenix (newspaper)|Portland Phoenix]].<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.umaine.edu/teaching/NewFaculty05.htm#Irvine,%20Alex|text=University of Maine page on new faculty|archivedate=20100516074852}}</ref>
