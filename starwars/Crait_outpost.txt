The '''Crait outpost''', also known as '''Crait Base''', or the '''Resistance base''', was an outpost of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] on the [[mineral]] [[planet]] of [[Crait]]. [[Rebel pilot]] [[Heff Tobber]] was stationed there as a [[transport]] [[pilot]] until its [[Evacuation of Crait base|abandonment]] prior to the [[Battle of Scarif]] in [[1 BBY]]. During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], in [[34 ABY]], the abandoned base was used by the [[Resistance]] in a [[Battle of Crait|battle]] against the [[First Order]] on the planet.

==Description==
[[File:CraitTurret.png|thumb|left|250px|One of the base's V-232 turrets]]
The outpost was a rough, bare-[[bone]]s facility. It was mostly underground and had a small [[tractor beam]] for snagging approaching [[Starship|ships]].<ref name="Leia" /> A massive metal door protected it from exterior attack and served as the only obvious entry and exit. Outside of the door were a number of long trenches as well as six [[V-120 artillery emplacement|V-120]] and eight [[V-232 artillery emplacement|V-232]] artillery emplacements to defend against enemy attacks. The base also had a [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] that was powerful enough to prevent [[orbital bombardment]]. The base was protected by a series of artillery batteries and trenches, as well as being home to a large mineral mine.

==History==
===The rebellion===
The base was created before [[3 BBY]] as an outpost of the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]]. After finding information leading to the planet, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] traveled to Crait on the royal [[yacht]] ''[[Polestar]]''. Upon landing, she was taken to the outpost, where she discovered that her [[Parent|father]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] was involved in the rebellion.<ref name="Leia" /> [[Heff Tobber]], a renowned [[Rebel pilot]], was stationed at the base as a [[transport]] [[pilot]]. A short [[time]] before the [[Battle of Scarif]]<ref name="Saga of Rey">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Saga of Rey}}</ref> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Helmet Collection 79">{{HelmetCollectionCite|79|Highlights of the Saga}}</ref><ref name="1 BBY">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the [[Battle of Scarif]] at the end of [[1 BBY]], meaning a short time before the battle would still be within 1 BBY.</ref> the rebels abandoned the base.<ref name="Saga of Rey" />

[[File:CraitMission-TheStormsOfCrait1.png|thumb|left|250px|The Imperials of SCAR Squadron chased the rebels off Crait, causing the outpost to be abandoned for the rest of the civil war.]]
Following the [[Battle of Yavin|destruction]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first Death Star]], Organa, [[Captain]] [[Han Solo]], and [[Luke Skywalker]] took a team to Crait to see if the abandoned stronghold would work as a new base for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Leia was then taken into the outpost's old mineral mines. However, [[Trusk Berinato]] alerted [[Task Force 99|SCAR Squadron]] and the Rebels were forced to abandon the outpost after a [[Mission to Crait|firefight]] with the [[Special Commando Advanced Recon Trooper|SCAR troopers]].<ref name="Storms of Crait">[[The Last Jedi – The Storms of Crait 1|''The Last Jedi – The Storms of Crait'' 1]]</ref>

===First Order–Resistance war===
[[File:Resistance-at-Crait.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Resistance forces in the outpost's trenches]]
After the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], the [[Resistance]] evacuated to Crait after their original base on the planet of [[D'Qar]] was discovered by the [[First Order]]. The First Order followed the Resistance to the world, which sparked the [[Battle of Crait]].<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Crait outpost was first mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="UVG" /> The base later appeared in the [[2017]] young-adult novel ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="Leia" />
