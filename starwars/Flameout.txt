A '''flameout''' was a mixed [[alcohol]]ic drink that consisted of [[Hull stripper]] and [[Vaschean rye]]. It could be served in a [[jet juice]] flask.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> A flameout could also be spiked with [[spice]].<ref name="RotJ novel" /> According to the [[human]] [[Qi'ra]], the leader of the criminal organization [[Crimson Dawn]], the flameout was an old [[Corellian]] concoction.<ref name="SW 18" /> The drink was among several mixed alcoholic beverages served on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] at [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="3 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'' (2020) 18 to [[3 ABY]].</ref> when Qi'ra met with [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], a [[general]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], she brought with her a golden bottle of flameout to share. However, Organa, suspecting Qi'ra of ill-intentions, was not interested and user her [[blaster]] to shoot the bottle out of Qi'ra's hand before she could serve it.<ref name="SW 18" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="4 Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] in [[4 ABY]].</ref> when the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] was briefly forced to work as an ambulatory bar aboard [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]]'s [[sail barge]] ''[[Khetanna]]'', flameouts were among the drinks that were served.<ref name="RotJ novel" />
