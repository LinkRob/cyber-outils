During the [[Battle of Hoth/Legends|Battle of Hoth]], a '''funeral''' was held for the ace [[Pilot/Legends|pilot]] [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], hero of the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]], who [[Death/Legends|died]] one day before following a [[One-Arm|wampa's]] attack.

==Prelude==
Before the beginning of the [[Battle of Hoth/Legends|Battle of Hoth]], [[Commander/Legends|Commander]] [[Luke Skywalker]] was tasked to do a recon mission with [[Gender/Legends|his]] [[Tauntaun/Legends|tauntaun]]. However, a [[Unidentified wampa/Legends|wampa]] appeared to assault Skywalker, knocking him unconscious and dragging him to his cave. Though Skywalker managed to escape and slain [[One-Arm|another wampa]] at the cave, he was injured and freezing in the cold. Shortly thereafter, Commander [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] arrived after his tauntaun [[Death/Legends|died]]. Unfortunately, there was no more salvation for Skywalker. Before dying, Luke asked Han to go to [[Dagobah/Legends|Dagobah]], meet the legendary [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] and train as a [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]]. Then, Skywalker passed away on the [[Corellian/Legends|Corellian's]] arms. Immediately after Skywalker's death, the [[Alliance Starfighter Corps/Legends|Alliance Starfighter Corp]] [[Zev Senesca]], member of the [[Rogue Squadron/Legends|Rogue Squadron]], asked Skywalker about his position, only for having Solo, who put Skywalker's corpse to rest on a tent, to inform him about what happened to their [[Rogue Leader/Legends|Rogue Leader]].<ref name="Episode V Infinities" />

==The funeral==
{{Quote|&hellip;Skywalker was loved by all those who knew him, and his contributions to the fight against galactic tyranny were without measure.|[[Carlist Rieekan/Legends|Carlist Rieekan]]|<ref name="Episode V Infinities" />}}
The next [[Standard day/Legends|day]], during the Battle of [[Hoth]], a [[funeral]] was held by the Rebels in the memory of Skywalker, famous for being the hero of the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]] in which he destroyed the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire's]] [[Superweapon/Legends|superweapon]] the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|Death Star]]. Among the guests to the funeral, were [[Princess/Legends|Princess]] [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]], [[General/Legends|General]] [[Carlist Rieekan/Legends|Carlist Rieekan]] and Solo's [[First Mate/Legends|First Mate]] the [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]]. After the funeral's end, the Rebels reasumed their positions at the battle, in which [[Wedge Antilles/Legends|Wedge Antilles]] and [[Wes Janson/Legends|Wes Janson]] had already been shot down by Imperial forces leaded by General [[Maximilian Veers/Legends|Maximilian Veers]].<ref name="Episode V Infinities" />

==Aftermath==
Following the memorial's service end, Solo said to Organa that he had the fault of Skywalker's death, saying that maybe if he had arrived earlier Skywalker would still be alive, but Leia comforted him saying that it wasn't his fault. Then, Solo informed Leia that he needed to go to Dagobah to be trained as a Jedi by Master Yoda. Once at Dagobah, however, Yoda informed Han that it was Leia who needed to be trained instead of him. Ultimately, Leia occupied what his brother's place was going to be and became a [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]].<ref name="Episode V Infinities" />

On the other hand, the Imperials didn't found any exact evidence regarding Skywalker's death, leading them to leave his demise as a rumor. [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]], however, not aware of Luke's fate, was sure that Skywalker was still alive and went to search him. Eventually, Vader managed to track down Leia to Dagobah, but during his confrontation against Yoda, shortly before being killed by Solo, he learned about what really happened to Skywalker at Hoth.<ref name="Episode V Infinities" />
