'''Alasdair Bumbalough''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] criminal who lived on [[Corellia/Legends|Corellia]] during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]. After the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]], he worked for the [[Crime lord/Legends|crime lord]] [[Olag Greck]]. During that time, the Greck criminals went into the investigation of the [[Tyrena/Legends|Tyrena]] city councilmen [[Lyman Zavala]] and [[Corellian Security Force|CorSec]] [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Dool]]. At some point, Lyman Zavala dispatched a group of spacers to strike the Greck headquarters. The spacers fought Alasdair Bumbalough to get access to Olag Greck's private sanctum and eventually killed Greck himself.<ref name="SWG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dialogue pertaining to Alasdair Bumbalough appeared in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']], a [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online roleplaying game]] developed by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment and published by [[LucasArts]],<ref name="SWG" /> prior to its closure on [[December 15]], [[2011]].<ref name="Shutdown">{{SonyCite|url=playerinfo|text=IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT ''STAR WARS'' GALAXIES™}}</ref> According to these files, introduced with the "[[W:c:swg:Publish 28 Notes for April 26, 2006|Publish 28]]," on [[April 26]], [[2006]],<ref name="SonyForumsCite-What's On Deck! Publish 28 Update Notes, April 26th">{{SonyForumsCite|url=swg/board/message?board.id=Announcements&message.id=3077&jump=true|text=What's On Deck! Publish 28 Update Notes, April 26th}}</ref> Alasdair Bumbalough would have been involved in a questline named "Tyrena - Lyman Zavala." However, he is not known to have appeared on the game's live servers as a proper [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]].<ref name="SWG" />
