'''Chromite''' was a type of [[ore]] that could notably be found on the [[planet]] [[Allyuen]], in the [[Anoat sector]]. During the [[Iron Blockade]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] had a cache of [[armor]] hidden in a disused chromite [[Mining|mine]] on Allyuen.<ref name="BS">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Run: "Bunker Spelunker"</ref>
