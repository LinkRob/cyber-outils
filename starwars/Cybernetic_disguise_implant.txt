'''Cybernetic disguise implants''' were a type of [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] implant that were composed of a series of systems embedded beneath an individual's skin that allowed their users to alter their appearance. These systems were implanted throughout the body and allowed near-total alteration of the user's physical appearance. The cost of a cybernetic disguise implant was 18,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
The cybernetic disguise implant was mentioned in ''[[Cyphers and Masks]]'',<ref name="Cyphers and Masks">''[[Cyphers and Masks]]''</ref> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Accept Your Mission">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/8/16/accept-your-mission|text=Accept Your Mission}}</ref>
