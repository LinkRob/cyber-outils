'''Breacher Two''' was a [[breacher]] [[missile launcher]] used by the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] [[Nightdragon man-of-war]] [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']].<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' takes place in [[18 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> during a [[Battle at listening post KR21|battle]] against the [[Jixtus's fleet|forces]] of the [[Grysk]] operative "[[Jixtus]]" at [[Listening post KR21]], Ar'alani ordered [[Junior Captain]] [[Evroes'ky'mormi]], [[core name]] "Oeskym," to disable the [[Engine|thrusters]] in Breacher Two so the ''Vigilant'' could drop the missiles into [[Realspace|space]] without firing them.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
Breacher Two was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
