The '''ear''' was the organ of hearing, whose visible part was a flap of tissue. Many [[species]] had a pair of ears placed symmetrically on opposite sides of the head.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Thanks to their bigger ears, some species like the [[ruping]]s,<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|ruping|Ruping}}</ref> [[Lannik (species)|Lanniks]],<ref name="AbEverything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> and the [[Lasat]]s had better hearing than [[human]]s.<ref name="USW" /> The [[Pau'an]]s of [[Utapau]] had hypersensitive hearing, and were forced to protect their fragile ears with special coverings.<ref name="AbEverything" /> The ears of the [[Yuzzum]] were shaped like [[antenna]]e.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> The [[Gungan]]s had long fin-like ears whose prominent lobes were referred to as [[haillu]].<ref name="GungansSWE">{{SWE|groups|gungans|Gungans}}</ref> [[Twi'lek]] ears were sexually dimorphic: [[Gender|males]] had slightly pointed ears similar to those of humans, while females had conal nodes.<ref name="Twi'lek culture">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-inside-intel-twilek-culture|text=''Star Wars'' Inside Intel: Twi'lek Culture|int=Star Wars Inside Intel}}</ref>
