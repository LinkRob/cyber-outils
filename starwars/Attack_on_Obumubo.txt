The '''attack on Obumubo''' was launched in [[3 ABY]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] against an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Obumubo Imperial garrison|garrison]] on the [[moon]] [[Obumubo]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] as a part of [[Operation Ringbreaker]].

==Prelude==
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Twilight Company had been attacking several planets in an effort to divert troops and supplies away from the [[Kuat shipyards|shipyards]] of Kuat. The fighting had been bloody. No troopers had died at the [[Attack on the dockyards of Najan-Rovi]], but injuries and exhaustion had taken a toll.<ref name="Battlefront" />

==The Battle==
The [[Obumubo Imperial garrison|garrison]] had been from fighting off the sea creatures of Obumubo for months and gained experience. The Battlefield favored the defenders and the garrison expertly defended the fort. In the initial landing, 2 Twilight soldiers and a medic drowned after jumping from the dropships. Another dozen died in the first assault. It took two more days for the siege cannons to be prepped on the fluid landscape and the cannons brought down the garrison at last.<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Aftermath==
The ''[[Thunderstrike]]'' sped out of system as Imperial reinforcement arrived. Former [[Governor]] [[Everi Chalis]] promised that their actions could lead the Alliance to victory.<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on Obumubo first appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
