Diara's mother was a character that appeared in the [[holodrama]] series ''[[Baradium Dynasty]]'', which had released at least sixty episodes<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> She was the [[Parent|mother]] to the series' character [[Diara]] and employed the character [[Talesh Makeric]]. By the fifty-eighth episode,  Makeric had become lovers with Diara while also agreeing to [[Marriage|marry]] Diara's mother. He confessed the latter to Diara after they professed their love to him. A [[Standard week|week]] before the wedding, prior to the fifty-ninth episode, Talesh met with Diara's mother and told her that he had confessed the planned marriage to Diara. He went on to share his reservations about the speed of the wedding and the possibility that it could be about his wealth. Diara's mother reassured Makeric and reminded him that she had a prenuptial written up that just needed his [[holo-seal]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

However, by the sixtieth episode, Diara had confronted their mother about her relationship with Makeric and their mother admitted that they were only marrying him for his [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. She went on to assure her child that the pair would be divorced in a [[cycle]] and Diara could have him afterward. Their child responded that Makeric was not interested in them anymore, prompting Diara's mother to say that all Makerics were cold and heartless. Their conversation was then interrupted when Makeric arrived and informed them that his [[Talesh Makeric's father|father]] had been [[death|killed]] in a [[refinery]] accident.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Personality and traits==
Diara's mother was a [[Gender|female]] individual who was willing to lie as well as use marriage in order to gain wealth.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Diara's mother voice appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, the Baradium Dynasty collectibles identify the character as simply "Diara's mother."<ref name="Outlaws" />
