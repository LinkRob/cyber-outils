'''Diagnostic patches''' could be used to study an individual's [[sleep]] cycles.<ref name="GG" /> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' takes place in [[18 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> [[Caregiver]] [[Mitth'ali'astov]], [[core name]] "Thalias," had the [[sky-walker]] [[navigator]] [[Che'ri]] wear a diagnostic patch to monitor the [[Youngling|young]] [[Gender|girl's]] sleep patterns on the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]''.<ref name="GG" />
