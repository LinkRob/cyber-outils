'''Belsus''' was the [[moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Gentes]], in the [[Anoat sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. At the [[time]] of the [[Iron Blockade]], there were rumors that [[General]] [[Grievous]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] had once [[Grievous' laboratory|built]] a [[laboratory]] there.<ref name="Laboratory of Grievous" />

{{GameMechanics}}
A [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] sent a member of their crew on Belsus in order to salvage valuable equipment from the ruins of Grievous' laboratory.<ref name="Laboratory of Grievous" />
