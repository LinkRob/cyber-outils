'''Camo stormtrooper armor''' was worn by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s, specifically equipped for warfare in forested or overgrown environments. This [[armor]] was coated with [[Color|green]] and brown camouflage patterns consisting of various splotchy patches. The [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]] owned a [[Stormtrooper Commander]] [[Camo Commander|variant set]] of this [[trooper]] armor that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" />
