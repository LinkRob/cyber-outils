'''Amplifier pylons''' were thin devices used in [[power fence]]s. When powered by a [[power fence generator]], they produced crackling walls of solid, translucent [[Color|blue-white]] energy that prevented objects and energy beams from passing through. Amplifier pylons were made out of [[durasteel]] and their bases were made out of [[stresscrete]]. They were generally five [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
Amplifier pylons were first mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Fully Operational Is Online">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/3/29/fully-operational-is-online|text=Fully Operational Is Online}}</ref>
