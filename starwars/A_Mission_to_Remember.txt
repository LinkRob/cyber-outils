"'''A Mission to Remember'''" is the first part of the [[A Mission to Remember / The Bounty Hunter and the Thief|twenty-second]] episode of the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|second season]] the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''. It was released on [[Disney+]] on [[March 19]], [[2025]].

==Official description==
''The Jedi help a droid.''

==Plot summary==
===The lost droid===
[[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Lys Solay]], [[Nubs]] and [[Kai Brightstar]] exit [[Hap's Sap Tap]] after enjoying some [[Mossberry Biscuit]]s. A small [[droid]] runs past them and collides with an [[Ootoo]] before crashing into a box of purple [[fruit]]s. Brightstar and his friends rush to the droid's aid. The droid, however, says he cannot remember what it was doing. The droid introduces himself as [[MB-4]], though he is not certain of his identity. Brightstar inspects the droid's scorched head and believes that his [[memory circuit]] has been damaged. The young [[Jedi]] discern that they should try to get the droid to his home so he can get repairs.

MB-4 cannot remember where home is. To start jogging MB-4's memory, Brightstar asks him why he was in a big rush. MB-4 recalls that he was being pursued by a [[thief]]. Just then, the [[human]] bandit [[Emmett]] arrives on a [[speeder bike]] looking for MB-4. He attempts to snatch the droid, but Brightstar pulls him out of the way, causing Emmett to crash his bike into a pile of boxes. Solay recognizes Emmett as the thief who tried to steal the [[Tenoo Jedi Temple]]'s [[dunnel]]s. Emmett admits to the attempted theft and announces his intention to take MB-4 to extract valuable intelligence. 

Brightstar and his friends rush towards their own speeder bikes with MB-4, hoping to flee Emmett. Kai suggests they ride through the streets of [[Kublop Springs]], whereas Solay proposes getting out of town since the streets are so crowded with pedestrians. Despite Solay's protests, Brightstar makes the decision to take the streets. The three Jedi younglings and MB-4 depart on [[speeder bike]]s with Emmett following close behind. The trio race through the packed streets, dodging pedestrians and [[Clothing|clothes]] lines. Solay crashes into a box of [[mossberry|mossberries]], getting covered in the fruit, and Nubs gets briefly covered by a sheet. He tosses the sheet off into the path of Emmett, who then collides with a [[landspeeder]]. The landspeeder driver berates Emmett for his reckless driving.

===Forest flight===
Emmett's associates [[Chipp]] and [[Z-3OR]] contact him via [[holoprojector]]. Emmett tells them that he has not captured the droid yet but will need their help in dealing with the Jedi younglings. The Jedi ride their speeder bikes and MB-4 into the [[Tenoo tree forest]]. Solay and Nubs express their displeasure with the risks taken speeding through town, for which Brightstar apologizes. After coming to a stop near a [[Water|river]], Brightstar and Solay ask MB-4 if any of the surroundings jog MB-4's memory for his home, and he recalls that he lives by the forest. 

Gazing at the waterfall, MB-4 recalls that his home is near a tall tree from which three waterfalls flow. Nubs thinks that MB-4 may be describing [[Conjyym Valley]]. Not knowing the way from their location, Nubs climbs up a [[Tenoo tree]] to have a look at the surroundings. Solay and Brightstar follow with MB-4 in tow. Meanwhile, Emmett and his associates enter the forest on their hoverbikes. Emmett scans the forest with a pair of [[macrobinoculars]]. The bandit is frustrated with Chipp, but Z-3OR spots some [[bird]]s disturbed nearby. The three decide to follow the commotion. 

Back at the river, the Jedi younglings and MB-4 reach the canopy of the Tenoo tree. The four spot a tall three with the three waterfalls in the distance. However, Emmett and his gang catch up with them. Against Brightstar's warnings, Nubs charges at the bandits with [[Nubs's lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. Emmett however uses his rope gun to bind Nubs. Solay charges forward with [[Lys's lightsaber|her lightsaber]] but is also trapped by Emmett. Brightstar charges at Emmett but also rushes and gets trapped. Brightstar then has no choice but to defend his friends, and he too gets restrained. While MB-4 attempts to free the Jedi, Emmett mocks the, and reveals that he wants MB-4 because he belongs to a [[prospector]] who owns [[gem]] [[mining|mines]]. MB-4 knows the location of every mine on Tenoo. 

Chipp adds that the gems from one mine will make them wealthy. As the bandits chuckle, Solay informs them that MB-4 experienced an accident while they were chasing him and that his memory is not working right. When MB-4 corroborates that he cannot remember, Emmett says that they will take him back to their camp to coerce him into remembering. 

===Rescuing MB-4===
After Emmett's gang leaves with MB-4, Brightstar asks why they were not working as a team. Solay explains that they have been so focused on getting MB-4 home that they were not listening to each other. Nubs agrees. Brightstar says his friends are right and asks for ideas on rescuing MB-4. Solay manages to free one of her hands which she uses to untie Nubs from his ropes. Nubs frees Solay, who frees Brightstar. Seeing Emmett and his gang leaving on their bikesa, Nubs proposes cutting them off by going through the forest. 

Emmett gloats about his victory as his posse heads back to camp. However, the three bandits soon find the three Jedi younglings blocking their path. When Emmett asks how they got there, Brightstar demands they return MB-4. Emmett and his gang retreat into the forest but argue about which way to go. Not working as a team, Emmett and Chipp bang their bikes against one another. With Chipp and Z-30R blocking Brightstar's speeder bike, Brightstar tasks Solay and Nubs with distracting the two bandits to clear a path for him. Nubs and Solay tail Emmett's accomplices while Brightstar pursues Emmett. MB-4 jumps onto Brightstar's speeder bike. Emmett crashes his hoverbike into a tree and falls into the river. Chipp and Z-30R give chase and attempt to grab MB-4. However, they grab each other instead and crash into the river. The three bandits cling onto a log, with Emmett cursing their misfortune. 

===MB-4's homecoming===
MB-4 thanks the Jedi younglings for saving him. The Jedi younglings bring him home to his master, the prospector [[Freet Chaddio]]. MB-4 asks if Chaddio is his master. Chaddio reassures his droid that he is indeed, and he asks if MB-4 is alright. Solay explains that MB-4 had an accident in Kublop Springs and that his memory circuit needs to be repaired. Chaddio embraces the droid and introduces himself to the Jedi younglings. He asks the Jedi if he can repay them for rescuing his droid. Nubs asks for permission to see one of Chaddio's mines. Chaddio then leads the Jedi towards a mine for a tour.
