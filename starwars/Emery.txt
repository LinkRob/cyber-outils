The '''''Emery''''' was one of three [[StoneCrusher-class WarMaster|''StoneCrusher''-class WarMasters]] that served in the [[Jixtus's fleet|fleet]] of the [[Grysk]] operative "[[Jixtus]]." Around [[18 BBY]], the StoneCrusher [[Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand|engaged]] a [[Thrawn's fleet|group]] of forces of the [[Chiss]] and [[Paccosh]] [[species]] over the [[planet]] [[Sunrise]] in the [[Sunrise system]]. While engaging the [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] ''[[Grayshrike]]'', its [[engine]]s were destroyed, and the ''Emery'' exploded.

==Description==
The ''Emery'' was a [[StoneCrusher-class WarMaster|''StoneCrusher''-class WarMasters]] and was equipped with an [[engine]] and a [[hyperdrive]]. It was armed with [[spectrum laser]]s and shielded with [[electrostatic barrier]]s.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Role==
The ''Emery'' was part of "[[Jixtus]]"'s [[Jixtus's fleet|fleet]] to conquer and destroy the Chiss Ascendancy on behalf of the Grysk Hegemony. It was one of three ''StoneCrusher''-class WarMasters in the fleet, [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by a [[Emery's commander|Grysk commander]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==History==
The ''Emery'' took part in the [[Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand|final battle over Sunrise]] when [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] defeated Jixtus fleet. During the battle, the ''Emery'' first fought against a [[patrol cruiser]] and [[gunboat]]s of the combined forces of the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] and the [[Paccosh]]. After getting rid of them, it charged into a battle with the already crippled Chiss heavy cruiser ''[[Grayshrike]]'', clearly aiming to destroy it.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

The ''Grayshrike'' could escape with a very short [[Microjump|in-system jump]], its destination so close that it was still visible from the aboard ''Emery'' to encourage it to follow. But as the ''Emery'' was about to follow, its hyperdrive failed because of the [[Vagaari]] [[Gravity well projector|gravity-well generator]] that had just been activated by [[Mid Captain]] [[Csap'ro'strob|Apros]]. Confused by the failing hyperdrive, since the Grysks did not know yet of the gravity-well generator, the ''Emery'' was not prepared for the returning ''Grayshrike'', which used the edge of the gravity well to arrive precisely to attack without delay.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

The ''Grayshrike'' first sped alongside the ''Emery's'' flank and firing at it, until it at last reached the Grysk ship's stern and destroyed its main thrusters. As a result, the ''Emery'' exploded as a whole, making it the first ship that was destroyed during the battle.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
