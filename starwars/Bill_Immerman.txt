'''Bill Immerman''' worked as the head of business affiars at [[20th Century Studios|20th Century Fox]] during negotiations to make the [[1977]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. Director [[George Lucas]]'s agent, [[Jeff Berg]], and his lawyer, [[Tom Pollock]], met with Immerman in [[August]] [[1974]] to negotiate a contract.<ref name="Archives" />
