The '''Chevin'''<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> were a large [[Sentience|sentient]] [[elephantine]] [[species]]<ref name="ROTJ" /> with massive heads that stretched the length of their entire bodies.<ref name="ACGAS">{{StoryCite|story=All Creatures Great and Small|book=Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I}}</ref> They could have [[Color|gray]] skin, black [[eye]]s, and blonde [[hair]].<ref name="ROTJ" /> The [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]'s head of security [[Ephant Mon]] was a Chevin.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> A colonial species, they had migrated and adapted to dominate many worlds.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>
