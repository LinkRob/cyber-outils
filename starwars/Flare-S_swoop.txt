The '''Flare-S swoop''',<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> also known as the '''S-swoop''',<ref name="CL2016" /> was a high-performance, one-[[pilot]] sport [[swoop]] manufactured by [[Mobquet Swoops and Speeders]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

Shortly before [[Anakin Skywalker]] won the [[Boonta Eve Classic]], several S-swoops were seen in the [[spaceport]] of [[Mos Espa]] on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>

One [[Standard day|day]] in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> as [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] arrived in [[Mos Eisley]] and headed for [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]], a recklessly-[[pilot]]ed S-swoop startled a [[ronto]] on [[Corporations Road]], forcing the creature to rear to avoid a collision.<ref name="CL2016" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:S-swoop-Geektionary.jpg|thumb|left|Profile view of a Flare-S swoop]]
The Flare-S swoop was created for the extended Mos Eisley sequences in the [[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition|Special Edition]] of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], which was released in [[1997]]. Both the vehicle and its pilot were entirely computer-generated. Although it was designed for the Special Edition, the swoop was in fact first revealed in the [[Star Wars Legends]] [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire|''Shadows of the Empire'']] multimedia project. Its design was resurrected and modified for a brief appearance in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="Old DB">{{DB|vehicle|flaresswoop|Flare-S swoop}}</ref>
