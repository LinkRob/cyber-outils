'''Caben Jior''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] member of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> they held the rank of [[Jedi Knight]] and were tasked with acting as a liaison to the influential [[Byne Guild]] of [[spacer]]s, monitoring reports from far-flung travelers.<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Jior walked with a human in<ref name="LostFound">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref> [[Clothing|regulation survival gear]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> through a corridor by the launch bays of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="LostFound" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]], passing by [[Jedi Master]] [[Sol]] and<ref name="LostFound" /> his<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]].<ref name="LostFound" /> While at the temple, Jior wore [[Color|white]] and yellow [[temple robes]] with a brown [[Jedi utility belt]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> and brown [[boot]]s.<ref name="LostFound" /> The human had light skin, brown [[eye]]s, and brown [[hair]] with a graying beard.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
