"'''Eyewitness to Apocalypse'''" is a vignette written by [[Michael Allen Horne]] for the ''[[Dark Empire Sourcebook]]'' in [[1993]].

==Plot summary==
The story takes the form of a personal communication by [[Rivoche Tarkin]] to fellow [[Historian/Legends|historian]] [[Voren Na'al/Legends|Voren Na'al]] during the [[Second Battle of Coruscant]] in [[10 ABY/Legends|10 ABY]]. Tarkin speaks of her experiences during the fighting and laments at the destruction caused to [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. She went to various parts of the ruined city, such as the [[Temple of the Circle]], with her escorts, Mr. [[Greybird]] and [[Demolinn]]. She noted that the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] mutineers were taking most of the supplies, leaving little for the survivors. Tarkin later overhears a [[Technician/Legends|technician]] saying he detected new ships leaving [[Hyperspace/Legends|hyperspace]], which she hopes are friendly, and ends her message by giving her regards to [[Arhul Hextrophon/Legends|Arhul Hextrophon]].
