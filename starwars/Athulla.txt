'''Athulla''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Jinata system]] in the [[Core Worlds]].

==Description==
The planet was inhabited by numerous [[Miner|miners]]. It once had a [[Moon|moon]] that was ravaged in a minerary disaster sometime before [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> creating the debris that surrounded the planet. [[Jinata Security]] [[officer]] [[Leema Kai]] considered it extremely unpleasant to live on.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> 

==History==
By 34 ABY, Athulla was under the control of [[Jinata Security]]. After falling behind in schedule and allowing [[Del Meeko]] to learn of their plans for clandestine [[Project Resurrection]], [[First Order]] [[Commander]] [[Gideon Hask]] informed the [[Aqualish]] [[Gleb]] that he was shutting down the project. Gleb assured Hask that it was a mistake and that her men would triple their efforts on Athulla, to which the [[First Order officer]] responded that he would leave a few of his men behind to ensure Gleb kept her word.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

During the same year, while searching for her husband Del Meeko, former [[Inferno Squad]] leader [[Iden Versio]] fought Jinata Security officer Leema Kai and his fleet of modified vessels in the [[asteroid field]] surrounding the planet. During the battle Iden and her daughter [[Zay Versio]] piloted their [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing starfighters]] and destroyed three modified armored [[GR-75 medium transport|GR-75 medium transports]] that were escorting Kai's modified [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] the [[Opportunity (Nebulon-B)|''Opportunity'']] while [[Resistance]] fighter [[Shriv Suurgav]] commanded the [[Raider II-class corvette|''Raider II''-class corvette]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|corvus-ship|Corvus (ship)}}</ref> [[Corvus (Raider II-class)|''Corvus'']] in pursuit of the ''Opportunity''. After Zay's fighter was hit she returned to the ''Corvus'' while her mother engaged in a dogfight with Leema Kai himself, having left his frigate with his heavily modified [[Leema Kai's N-1 starfighter|N-1 starfighter]]. Kai was shot down and killed by Iden and Zay conned the ''Opportunity'' into surrender, exploiting their unawareness of the ''Corvus''' lack of heavy weaponry, thus ending the battle.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
