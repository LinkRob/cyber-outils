'''Bravo''' was a [[Color|brown]] [[Clothing|poncho]] with two white stripes across it and a braided brown [[bandolier]]. The poncho was woven and had a loose thread collar.<ref name="JFO" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="JFO Date5">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> the fugitive former [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] discovered the Bravo poncho in a chest inside a [[Derelict Hangar]] on the [[planet]] [[Zeffo]].<ref name="JFO" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bravo appeared in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' as an optional collectable cosmetic for players to find.<ref name="JFO" />
