A group of '''insurgents''' emerged on the [[planet]] [[Denash]] in the [[Batonn sector]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. The Denash insurgents had ties with the [[Batonn insurgents]] and the [[Sammun insurgents]]. During the [[Batonn sector insurgency]], the Denash insurgents evacuated most of their ships and materiel to [[Batonn]]. However, some stragglers were captured by [[Admiral]] [[Kinshara]]'s [[One Twenty-Fifth task force]]. The other Denash insurgents were [[Death|killed]] during the [[Batonn insurgency]].<ref name="Thrawn" />
