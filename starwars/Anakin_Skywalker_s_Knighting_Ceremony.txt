In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Jedi Order]] held a mass '''Knighting Ceremony''' to promote a group of senior [[Padawan]]s to the rank of [[Jedi Knight]]. The [[Jedi]]'s need for more Knights had been fueled by the demands of the recently-started [[Clone Wars]], for which the Jedi had found themselves serving as [[commanding officer]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Grand Army of the Republic|new clone army]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During the [[Knighting Ceremony|ceremony]], held in the [[Jedi Temple training ground|training ground]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]], a group including [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Olana Chion]], [[Keer Stenwyt]], [[Cyruss Okent]], and [[D'urban Wen-Hurd]] were formally named Jedi Knights, although at least Skywalker had already been given the title beforehand. As such, he deemed the ceremony to be a waste of [[time]].<ref name="Brotherhood" />

Also present at this ceremony was Padawan [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]], who to her surprise, was suddenly given the promotion to knighthood following the unfortunate death of her master [[Sember Vey]] during the [[Jedi assault team|rescue mission]] on [[Geonosis]]. Akaris was promoted alongside [[Onielle]], [[Zeeth]], [[Charlin Plaka]], and [[Tualon Yaluna]], all being Padawans she had grown up with.<ref name="Rise of the Red Blade" />

The ceremony was overseen by [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] and [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]], and attended by the new Knights' former Masters, Padawans, [[Jedi Initiate|younglings]], and members of the [[Jedi High Council]]. [[Senator]]s and [[Republic Military]] [[officer]]s also attended. [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] was supposed to come in person, but he was unable to attend as more than a [[hologram]] due to word of a recent [[Cadesura disaster|bombing]] on the neutral [[planet]] [[Cato Neimoidia]].<ref name="Brotherhood" />
