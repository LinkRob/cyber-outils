The '''Canyon Dune Turn''' was a large turn that was a part of the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]] course located on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Episode I" /> [[Tusken Raider]]s had been chased away from [[The Pinnacle]], a sacred place for them, which is why they regularly shot at [[podracer]]s during competitions to avenge their ancestors.<ref name="CL"> [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> During the [[Boonta Eve Classic (32 BBY)/Legends|32 BBY race]], Tusken Raiders camped out on the Canyon Dune Turn to take pot shots at podracers with their [[slugthrower]]s. While initially unsuccessful, one Tusken shot down the podracer of [[Teemto Pagalies]] during the second lap.<ref name="Episode I" />
