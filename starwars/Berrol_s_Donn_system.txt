The '''Berrol's Donn system''' was located in the [[Kriz sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="WesternReaches" /> Situated in grid square K-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it was located on the [[super-hyperroute]] known as the [[Corellian Trade Spine]],<ref name="TFABG" /> which linked it to the [[Trenchenovu system|Trenchenovu]] and [[Tureen system]]s<ref name="GalaxyMap">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> as well as the [[Togominda system]] of the [[Yarith sector]].<ref name="BTM">{{BuildFalconCite|18|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Berrol's Donn [[Star system|system]] was introduced to the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' boxed set.<ref name="TFABG" /> It was then identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Berrol's Donn system/Legends|Berrol's Donn system]] was originally introduced via an appearance of the celestial body [[Berrol's Donn/Legends|Berrol's Donn]] in ''[[The Abduction]]'', a [[1992]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure authored by [[Chuck Truett]] and published by [[West End Games]] for use with ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Abduction">''[[The Abduction]]''</ref> The [[Star system/Legends|system]] was subsequently identified by name in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
