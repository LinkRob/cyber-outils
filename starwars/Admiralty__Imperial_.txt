{{Quote|As I've said on countless occasions, my lord, the fleet is already too scattered. On the Admiralty's counsel, the navy is now redirecting resources from as far away as Rothana and Bothawui.|Harus Ison|<ref name="Tarkin" />}}
The '''Admiralty''' was the leading department of the [[Galactic Empire]] that administered the [[Imperial Navy]], giving counsel and governing policy of the Imperial Navy. Members of the Admiralty were primarily administrative positions, however are able to oversee and manage operations whenever they see fit. It played a role in supervising operations during the [[Berch Teller campaign]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> It was generally known to favor the doctrine of relying on large numbers of specialized [[Starship|ships]], and for this reason viewed the [[TIE/sk x1 experimental air superiority fighter]] as a wasteful expenditure.<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> The Admiralty was part of [[Imperial High Command]].<ref name="Dawn" />
