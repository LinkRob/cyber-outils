{{Quote|Well besides the dead body, the hyperdrive is down, navigations has been blasted, and the controls are on fire. But she has a fun name—the Cutlass!|Hondo Ohnaka, over the ''Cutlass''|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
The '''''Cutlass''''' was a [[R93 refractor]] of the [[Rokana Raiders]]. In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Starship|ship]] was present in the [[Khepi system]], where it was boarded and hijacked by [[Hondo Ohnaka]] after the [[Weequay]] sneaked aboard through a cargo crate. [[Kay Vess' crew]] would later travel to [[Tatooine]] and the [[Khepi Tomb]] within the [[Sayma Nebula]] with the ship. Before Kay Vess' crew could return onto the ''Cutlass'', a fraction of Rokana Raiders escaped with the ship after the tomb began to collapse.<ref name="Outlaws" />
