The '''Archaeology Department''' was a department of the [[University of Bar'leth]]. The [[Iglan'tine Nos' office|office]] of [[Sava]] [[Iglan'tine Nos]] was located on the top floor of the department. While she was a student at the [[university]] [[Standard year|years]] before<ref name="Aphra 19" /> [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']] take place between the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] came to be banned from the top level of the department, and tricked [[Kho Phon Farrus]], on the [[Standard day|day]] of their enrollment into the university, with going to Nos' office and distracting the sava so that she, [[Sana Starros]], and [[Eustacia Okka]] could break into the office and steal Nos' collection of artifacts. Years later, when Farrus returned to the university in search of the [[Unyielding Heart]] in order to claim the [[Spark Eternal]], Farrus noted that the department was the same as it had been, and that they hated the place.<ref name="Aphra 19" />
