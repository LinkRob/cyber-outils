'''Amir El-Masry''' is a British actor who portrays [[Masir Trach]] in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. In addition to ''Star Wars'', his roles of note include [[Wikipedia:Jon Stewart|Jon Stewart's]] directorial debut [[Wikipedia:Rosewater (film)|''Rosewater'']] (2016),<ref name="BAFTA">{{WebCite|url=http://www.bafta.org/supporting-talent/elevate/amir-el-masry|text=Amir El-Masry|archivedate=20200412133944}}</ref>, and episodes of the French-Canadian television series [[Wikipedia:Transporter: The Series|''Transporter: The Series'']],<ref name="EgyptIndependent">{{WebCite|url=https://egyptindependent.com/egyptian-british-actor-amir-el-masry-announces-role-in-the-rise-of-skywalker|text=Egyptian-British actor Amir El-Masry announces role in 'The Rise of Skywalker'|date=2019-12-20|archivedate=20191229152132}}</ref> the British drama series [[Wikipedia:The Night Manager|''The Night Manager'']], the British drama series [[Wikipedia:The State (2017 TV series)|''The State'']] and the BBC series [[Wikipedia:Age Before Beauty|''Age Before Beauty'']].<ref name="BAFTA" />
