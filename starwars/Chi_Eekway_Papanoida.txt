'''Chi Eekway Papanoida''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Pantoran]] and a daughter of [[Baron]] [[Notluwiski Papanoida]], who served as [[Supreme Chairman]] of [[Pantora]]. She served as [[senator]] of [[Pantora]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] at the end of the [[Clone Wars]].

==Biography==
[[File:N_Papanoida_And_His_Family.png|thumb|left|200px|Chi with her father, brother, and sister on Coruscant]]
Chi Eekway Papanoida was the daughter of [[Notluwiski Papanoida]].<ref name="Databank" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Pantora]] was placed under a [[Blockade of Pantora|blockade]]. This led to her being [[Kidnapping of the Papanoida daughters|abducted]] by [[Greedo]] and a [[Unidentified Gotal bounty hunter|Gotal]] on orders from the [[Trade Federation]] along with her sister [[Che Amanwe Papanoida]]. Eekway was [[Prison|imprisoned]] on the Federation's [[flagship]]. There she lingered for [[Standard day|days]] alone and afraid until [[Senator]] [[Riyo Chuchi]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] came to her [[Rescue on the Pantora Droid Control Ship|rescue]]. Papanoida then returned to the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[capital]]. She later attended the senate meetings where the blockade was lifted from her [[planet]].<ref name="SoI">{{TCW|Sphere of Influence}}</ref>

By the [[Standard year|year]] [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Papanoida became a [[Junior Representative]] for Pantora, a position that saw her work under Chuchi and attend meetings in the senator's stead.<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> Following his [[Battle of Coruscant|abduction]], [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] came back to a royal welcome as he was greeted by several senators; Chi Eekway was among these distinguished [[ambassador]]s. She stood by [[Terr Taneel]] and wore [[Color|red]] [[robe]]s covering her back and head.<ref name="RotS">{{Film|III}}</ref>

[[File:Papanoida_at_the_opera.jpg|thumb|left|150px|Eekway with her father at the Opera]]
Eekway attended the [[Galaxies Opera House]] with her father and watched the [[Squid Lake|performance]]. For the event, Chi dressed in a brown velvet cloak and a black floor-length dress, and she also wore [[moon]] [[crystal]]s.<ref name="RotS" /> Near the close of the Clone Wars, Eekway joined the [[Delegation of 2,000]],<ref name="PABG" /> a group of senators who demanded that after the war ended, Chancellor Palpatine return his emergency powers to the senate,<ref name="DBMT">{{Databank|meena-tills|Meena Tills}}</ref> in Chuchi's stead.<ref name="Dawn" /> At some point, Papanoida succeeded Chuchi as senator of Pantora.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Personality and traits==
Eekway was a Pantoran [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Databank" /> who stood at 1.65 [[meter]]s (5 [[Foot|feet]], 5 inches) tall.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> She had [[Color|gold]] [[eye]]s and blue skin.<ref name="Databank" />

==Skills and abilities==
Eekway was had a vast knowledge in legislation and was also skilled at diplomacy.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chi Eekway was portrayed as a cameo by film writer and director and ''[[Star Wars]]'' creator [[George Lucas]]' daughter [[Katie Lucas]] in ''[[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith]]'',  the final film of the [[prequel trilogy]] released in [[2005]]<ref name="RotS" /> and was later voiced by [[Nika Futterman]] in the episode "[[Sphere of Influence]]" of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="SoI" /> released on [[October 1]], [[2010]].<ref name="SWcomTCWArticle">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/sphere_preview/index.html|text=George Lucas in Action in ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars''!}}</ref>

Concept art of her costume was drawn by [[Iain McCaig]] and the accompanying notes identified her as a "Mystic Senator".<ref name="old-db">{{DB|character|chieekway|Eekway, Chi}}</ref> The scenes at the opera house were filmed at [[Shepperton Studios]] in [[Wikipedia:England|England]] on [[August 23]], [[2004]], but Lucas and her father only filmed their shots in November of that year<ref name="MakingofROTS">''[[The Making of Star Wars Revenge of the Sith]]''</ref> in front of [[Wikipedia:Greenscreen|greenscreen]] and were composited in the scene.<ref name="baron old-DB">{{DB|character|baronpapanoida|Papanoida, Baron}}</ref> Lucas' other daughter [[Amanda Lucas]] also appeared as the character Senator [[Terr Taneel]] in the scene.<ref name="Taneel">{{DB|character|terrtaneel|Taneel, Terr}}</ref>

Baron Papanoida later appeared in the ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' episode "Sphere of Influence", written by Katie Lucas and [[Steven Melching]].<ref name="SoI" /> Apart from him, Chi Eekway also appeared in the episode as his character's daughter and was first identified as a Papanoida and the character's other two children Amanwe and Ion were also based on his other children Amanda and [[Jett Lucas]].<ref name="Sphere-EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/sphere-of-influence-trivia-gallery|text="Sphere of Influence" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> A maquette of Chi Eekway was created by [[Darren Marshall]].<ref name="Marshall">{{WebCite|url=https://dmarshallart.com/portfolio/the-clone-wars-season-3/|text=The Clone Wars – Season 3|work=[[Darren Marshall]]'s website|archivedate=20260123053253}}</ref> Chi Eekway had originally been identified as a [[Twi'lek/Legends|Twi'lek]] in her [[Databank (original)|Databank]] entry on [[StarWars.com]] as recently as [[June]] [[2008]]<ref name="ChiEekway old-DB">{{DB|character|chieekway|Chi Eekway|oldversion=20080623065231}} {{C|original version}}</ref> and Baron Papanoida had been identified as a [[Wroonian/Legends|Wroonian]],<ref name="Baron old-DB original">{{DB|character|baronpapanoida|text=Baron Papanoida|oldversion=20051224032152}} {{C|original version}}</ref> but in the episode they were [[Retroactive continuity|retconned]] to be Pantorans<ref name="SoI" /> which had been created for the Season One episode "[[Trespass]]" and had been inspired by a concept illustration drawn by [[Iain McCaig]] for [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="Pantoran old-DB">{{DB|species|pantoran|Pantoran}}</ref>
