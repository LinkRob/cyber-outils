'''Fuel''' was a type of material used to power vehicles. [[Starship]]s, such as the ''[[Ghost]]'', were powered by fuel.<ref name="Droids in Distress">{{Rebels|Droids in Distress}}</ref> During the [[Nihil conflict]], fuel was very expensive for civilians.<ref name="Out of the Shadows">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref> The [[Wookiee]] planet [[Kashyyyk]] hosted an [[Kashyyyk oil refinery|oil refinery]], which was a critical location during the [[Battle of Kashyyyk]].<ref name="GD">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''</ref> Fuel prices heavily rose during<ref name="Mando1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> the [[galactic recession]] of c. [[9 ABY]].<ref name="sw100">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref>

==Types==
===Starship fuel===
Types of starship fuel in the galaxy included [[rhydonium]], a volatile type of fuel found on the [[planet]] [[Abafar]],<ref name="Mission in Action">{{TCW|Missing in Action}}</ref> [[baradium nitrate]],<ref name="Survival">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]''</ref> [[deuterium]],<ref name="Cross">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> [[ipsium]], found exclusively on [[Ipsidon]],<ref name="The Crossing">{{TBB|The Crossing}}</ref> [[kessoline]], mined on [[Kessel]]<ref name="SoloOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> [[Malastarian fuel]],<ref name="The Zillo Beast">{{TCW|The Zillo Beast}}</ref> [[megonite]], [[Nergon-14]],<ref name="Survival" /> [[rippinnium]],<ref name="OWM">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> [[sodium]],<ref name="Survival" /> [[tibanna]],<ref name="HC 54">{{HelmetCollectionCite|54|Databank A-Z}}</ref> and [[Zylium 12]].<ref name="RTCT">''[[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower]]''</ref>

Some fuels were used specifically to power [[hyperdrive]] engines, such as the ultra-efficient [[coaxium]]<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> and [[Clouzon-36]].<ref name="The Call">{{Rebels|The Call}}</ref>

===Light fuel===
Some types of fuel were used to create lighting, either through their ignition in [[lantern]]s or by powering [[lamp]]s. Those included [[bloggin-oil]], harvested from the [[bloggin]] of [[Jakku]]<ref name="Survival" /> and [[Neehwa oil]].<ref name="TROSEE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

===Specialized fuels===
Some exotic types of fuel were used to power highly specialized devices. Those were [[accelerite]]<ref name="Gadgets">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref> and [[Conflagrine-14]], both highly flammable and used in [[flamethrower]]s,<ref name="Visual">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref><ref name="Gadgets" /> [[Hydro-glycolic fuel cell|hydro-glycolic fuel]], used to power [[astromech droid]]s,<ref name="BYOR2D21">[[Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2 1|''Star Wars: Build Your Own R2-D2'' 1]]</ref> and [[paralene]], used to power [[jetpack]]s.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref>

==In culture==
[[Podracer (profession)|Podracers]] believed that consuming [[engine]] fuel brought good luck, leading to a tradition of [[pilot]]s drinking a cup of the liquid just before a [[Podracing|race]].<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
