'''Eeusu Estornii''' was a [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Zeuol]] [[Senator/Legends|Senator]] from [[Ord Zeuol]] who worked in the [[Galactic Senate/Legends|Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]] during the final of years of the [[Old Republic era|Old Republic]]. After [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] announced the [[Declaration of a New Order]] in [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]], Eeusu was among the mourners at the [[Padmé Amidala's funeral/Legends|funeral]] of Senator [[Padmé Amidala/Legends|Padmé Amidala]].

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|One other thing that I'd like to explain is the character Eeusu Estornii -- The character wasn't actually named after me in the usual sense. There were a handful of characters in ''Revenge of the Sith'' that didn't have names. The characters were sent around to a group of us and I was given the opportunity to pick a character and name it, which I did. So I rather named the character after myself -- it was nice to be included in the group that got to do this, but it's not like somebody named this character after me (well, I guess I'm somebody...).|[[Sue Rostoni]]|<ref name="TFN Rostoni">{{JCFCite|url=posts/31762425|board=Literature|thread=Sue Rostoni Retiring on July 2011|user=SueRostoni|date=2011-06-14|time=3:14 PM|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20200406052330/https://boards.theforce.net/threads/sue-rostoni-retiring-on-july-2011.31755274/page-3}}</ref>}}
Eeusu Estornii was a character named after [[Sue Rostoni]], and portrayed by [[Jacqui Louez]], for [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Revenge of the Sith'']]. The character was one of a few left unnamed in the production of ''Revenge of the Sith''; Rostoni was one of several people given the opportunity to name a character, and chose to name this one after herself.<ref name="TFN Rostoni" /> However, Estornii never made it into the final release of the film.

''Eeusu Estornii'' was Sue Rostoni's screen name on [[StarWars.com]].

[[Star Wars Insider 85|''Star Wars Insider'' 85]] features a misspelling of her name: ''E'''c'''usu Estornii''. The StarWars.com databank spells her first name as ''Eesu''.

In early references, including ''[[The Making of Star Wars Revenge of the Sith]]'', Estornii was referred to simply as "Senator Jacquito" after the actress Jacqui Louez who played her.
