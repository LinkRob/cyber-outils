'''Cott Theefe''' was a individual who served as the [[captain]] of an [[F-143 emergency speeder|emergency fire suppression speeder]]<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> in the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Coruscant Rescue Ops]].<ref name="Encyclopedia Starfighters" /> Theefe and their crew were on duty when [[Anakin Skywalker]] managed to land the burning remains of the ''[[Invisible Hand]]'' during the [[Battle of Coruscant]]<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>
