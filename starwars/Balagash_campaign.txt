During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="MOF" /> which lasted from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> '''battles''' were fought on all seventeen of the [[moon]]s of the [[gas giant]] [[Balagash]], including [[Ma'a]], [[Rhoremone]], and [[Balumbra]]
<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> At the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the war, the Republic was [[Proclamation of the New Order|reorganized]] into the [[Galactic Empire]]. Around [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|23}}</ref>  the [[Imperial Intelligence]] operative [[Haki Zeophrine]] accompanied [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] to Balumbra while the latter was [[Investigation into the framing of the Jedi|investigating]] the Empire's framing of the [[Jedi Order]] for [[treason]], where she mentally recalled the battles.<ref name="MOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
The campaign was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MOF" />
