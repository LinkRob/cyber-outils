'''Cinda Tarheel''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] who served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. She was known for her temper, and as a result was grounded by [[General]] [[Carlist Rieekan]] on [[Hoth]], with [[Wedge Antilles]] saying it was because everyone feared she would fly her [[T-47 airspeeder]] into a mountain. Following the [[Battle of Hoth]], Tarheel was chosen by [[Wes Janson]] to be recruited into the rebuilt [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]], led by Antilles. She was assigned the [[callsign]] [[Red Twelve]] and flew as [[Will Scotian]]'s wingmate in an [[Attack on an asteroid mining station|attack]] on a nearby [[pirate]] base, that threatened the safety of the Alliance's rendezvous point.<ref name="FACPOV" />
