The '''''Crimson Firehawk'''''<ref name="Caper">{{OfficialYouTube|series=Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures|text=Short 2: Lys' Creature Caper|channel=@disneyjunior|name=Disney Junior|video=DgcjI0HYgGw}}</ref> was a [[shuttle]] [[pilot]]ed by [[Nash Durango]]<ref name="Meet" /> during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/swca-2022-young-jedi-adventures|text=SWCA 2022: Gather Your Younglings for the ''Young Jedi Adventures'' Series}}</ref> The ship was named after the [[Bird|avian]] [[firehawk]].<ref name="Feather Frenzy">{{YJA|Feather Frenzy}}</ref> [[Kai Brightstar|Kai]], [[Lys Solay|Lys]] and [[Nubs]] often used this shuttle to their missions.

==Behind the scenes==
The Crimson Firehawk first appeared in the short [[Meet the Young Jedi]] from the Disney Jr. animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''.<ref name="The Young Jedi" /> One concept art of the ship was designed by [[Joey Chou]]<ref name="Chou Concept">{{WebCite|url=https://www.joeyart.com/star-wars-young-jedi-adventures|text=Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures|work=[[Joey Chou]]'s [https://www.joeyart.com official website]|archivedate=20250625183522}}</ref> while textures for the upgraded ship was designed by [[Erick D. Acosta Arenas]] in the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|second season]].<ref>{{ArtStation|username=erickacosta|url=artwork/oJOawz|name=Erick D. Acosta Arenas|text=Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures Season 2|quote=Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures Season 2 (first part) On this second season I had the opportunity to work with a very wide variety of tasks, worked with all types of assets, meaning learning, exploring and fixing in all aspects. I personally enjoyed working with hero assets as they are the most difficult tasks to tackle. The second (and best) part of the season is coming soon! Special thanks to Andrew Justice, Cheng Ye, Gustavo Espinel, Larissa Nicolau and Brendan Lee.}}</ref>
