'''Endorian Chicken Tip-Yip Salad''' was a [[Foodstuff|dish]] served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE Date">The events of ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'' and those within the original timeline of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The [[tip-yip]]-based dish was suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="Docking Bay 7" />

==Behind the scenes==
Endorian Chicken Tip-Yip Salad is available in the [[In-universe|real-world]] Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo restaurant at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]]. It debuted on [[May 1]], [[2026]]. The menu's description reads, "[[Wikipedia:Guava|Guava]] [[Wikipedia:Barbecue chicken|BBQ chicken]] with [[Wikipedia:Avocado|avocado]]-[[Wikipedia:Jalapeño|jalapeño]] dressing, [[Wikipedia:Eruca sativa|arugula]], [[Wikipedia:Radish|radish]], [[Wikipedia:Romaine lettuce|romaine lettuce]], [[Wikipedia:Cucumber|cucumber]], [[Wikipedia:Red onion|red onion]], [[Wikipedia:Green bean|haricot vert]], [[Wikipedia:Pineapple|pineapple]], and [[Wikipedia:Rice noodles|rice noodles]]​."<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=disney-experiences/disney-eats-may-the-4th-foodie-guide|text=Disney Eats: May the 4th Foodie Guide 2026}}</ref>
