'''Feral Group''' was a [[starfighter]] [[flight group]] that served as part of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps|starfighter corps]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The squadron most notably participated in the [[Battle of Cawa City]].<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" />

==Description==
Feral Group were a [[starfighter]] [[flight group]] in the [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Feral Group's starfighters consisted of [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]]s that were painted [[Color|gray]] with red stripes.<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" />

==History==
===Battle of Cawa City===
{{Quote|Feral Leader...you may make your run!''"<br />"''Copy, Feral Two and Three, release your clusters now!|Green One and Feral Leader|<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" />}}
Seventeen days after the [[Battle of Endor]], Feral Group's Y-wing bombers were tasked with destroying an [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] with support from [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron's]] [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor|A-wings]], who fought off any [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. The Y-wings dropped [[magnetising cluster bomb]]s that magnetized to the hull of the AT-AT, which guaranteed the kill. At the time of the battle, an [[Abednedo]] served as a member of the unit.<ref name="Shattered Empire 2" />
