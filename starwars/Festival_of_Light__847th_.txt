The [[Festival of Light]] was a [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] tradition, which celebrated the [[Standard year|anniversary]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] joining the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Crisis on Naboo" />

The '''eight hundred and forty-seventh Festival of Light''' was held during the [[Clone Wars]], when a group of [[bounty hunter]]s hired by [[Count]] [[Dooku]] attempted to [[Plan to kidnap Chancellor Palpatine|kidnap]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Crisis on Naboo" />
