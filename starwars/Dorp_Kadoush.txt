'''Dorp Kadoush''' was [[lawyer|legal professional]] that offered free legal counsel to passengers of the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> in [[3 ABY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an announcement advertising Kadoush's services was made by a [[droid]] over the [[cruiser]]'s [[speaker]] system.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dorp Kadoush was mentioned in the "Wild Card" expansion for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> on [[November 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Wild Card released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/1Q4y5lMJwdUUt6qXzbWKPI/star-wars-outlaws-game-update-showcase-wrapup|text=Star Wars Outlaws Game Update Showcase Wrap-Up}}</ref>
