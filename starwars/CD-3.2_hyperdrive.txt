The '''CD-3.2 hyperdrives''' were a model of high performance hyperdrives incorporated and uitilised aboard [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class space cruisers]], enabling them to travel at [[lightspeed]]. They were manufactured by [[Longe Voltrans]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and provided the vessels of the [[Judicial Forces]], the capacity to enter and traverse [[hyperspace]], enabling them to carry out [[tibanna]] [[gas]] exploration,<ref name="O&A V">[[Obi-Wan and Anakin 5|''Obi-Wan and Anakin'' 5]]</ref> in addition to their usual diplomatic and peacekeeping missions.<ref name="TPM" />
