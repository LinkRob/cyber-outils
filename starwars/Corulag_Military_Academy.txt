The '''Corulag Military Academy''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] service academy on the [[planet]] [[Corulag]]. It was part of the [[Imperial Academy]] program, a network of academies that supplied [[troop]]s and personnel to the [[Imperial Military]]. It accepted talented [[Imperial cadet]]s who had reached the age of eighteen [[Standard year|years]]. It was regarded as less prestigious than the [[Raithal Academy]].<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks" /> The [[criminal]] [[Boonda]] failed to graduate from the Corulag Military Academy.<ref name="BMF42">{{BuildFalconCite|42|Starship Fact File}}</ref>
