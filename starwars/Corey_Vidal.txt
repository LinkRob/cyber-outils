'''Corey Vidal''' is a Canadian ''Star Wars'' video content producer, digital media consultant, and YouTube Partner. His YouTube videos have been seen over 71 million times, and his YouTube channel has been viewed over 9 million times. His most viewed video, "[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lk5_OSsawz4 Star Wars (John Williams Is The Man) A Cappella Tribute]," has over 19.9 million views. In December 2007, Corey was one of the first Canadians to join the [[Wikipedia: YouTube Partnership Program|YouTube Partnership Program]].<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.thespec.com/Entertainment/article/441449|text=Article on Corey Vidal's YouTube Partnership Status. "Our YouTube wonder guy"|archivedate=20080929175208}}</ref>

Corey was also one of the hosts for the [[Star Wars]] [[Celebration Anaheim (2015)|Celebration Anaheim]] Live, along with [[Anthony Carboni]] and [[Andi Gutierrez]].<ref>{{SW|url=news/watch-star-wars-celebration-anaheim-live-on-starwars-com-powered-by-verizon|text=''Star Wars'' Celebration Anaheim Live on StarWars.com, Powered by Verizon!}}</ref>
