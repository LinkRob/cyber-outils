'''Cha-Lee Yoon''' was a stunt performer for the television mini-series ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]''. Yoon was credited as '''Cha-Leen Yoon''' in [[Part II]], where Yoon played a [[Unidentified human spice den guard|spice den guard]].
