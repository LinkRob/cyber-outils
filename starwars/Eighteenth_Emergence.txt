The '''eighteenth Emergence''' was one of the [[Emergences]] in the aftermath of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], an incident when the [[freighter]] ''[[Legacy Run]]'' was destroyed in [[hyperspace]] and its debris began reverting into [[realspace]] in unpredictable locations.<ref name="LotJ" /> Occurring in the [[Dantooine system]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the Emergence led [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] to extend an [[Outer Rim Territories]] hyperspace blockade she had initiated to prevent further disasters for another fifty [[parsec]]s.<ref name="LotJ" />
