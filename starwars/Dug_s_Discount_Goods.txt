'''Dug's Discount Goods''' was a small establishment located within a corner of the [[Dune Sea Outpost]] battlefield of the [[Grand Arena]], utilized by the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. The store was associated with at least one [[Dug]], and its signage included neon purple lines, the word "Dug's" in [[Domabesh]] on the left, "Discount Goods" in [[Aurebesh]] on the right, and a stylistic depiction of a Dug at the center.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dug's Discount Goods first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It was added to the game via the inclusion of the Dune Sea Outpost map.<ref name="Hunters" />
