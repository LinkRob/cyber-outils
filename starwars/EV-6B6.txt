'''EV-6B6 (Eevee-Sixbeesix)''', known as '''Eevee''', was a helpful and talkative [[EV-series|EV-class]] [[droid]] who originally served the [[Pyke Syndicate]] aboard a [[Unidentified Moraysian cruiser|Moraysian cruiser]]. The [[Moraysian cruiser]] was stolen by the [[mercenary]] [[Ledesmar]], and EV-6B6 was thus forced to serve her. When members of the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] boarded the [[cruiser]] to [[Skirmish aboard a Moraysian cruiser|locate]] Ledesmar, EV-6B6 encountered [[Poe Dameron]] and [[Zorii Bliss]] and led them to Ledesmar, but regretted having to do so. Dameron and Bliss rescued Eevee from Ledesmar, and the droid worked with their cell of the Spice Runners.<ref name="Free Fall" />

EV-6B6 was eventually shot and destroyed under orders from the gang's leader, [[Zeva Bliss]], due to being former property of a competitor. Dameron retrieved Eevee's body and attempted to repair her, and Bliss, having noticed him doing so, had the [[droidsmith]] [[Babu Frik]] repair the droid on [[Kijimi]]. EV-6B6 continued to work with Dameron and Bliss, and accompanied Dameron when he betrayed the Spice Runners during the [[Kijimi summit]], when Zeva Bliss intended to execute numerous criminal allies and enemies. However, EV-6B6 was destroyed once again by the Spice Runner guard [[Gezlar]], suffering damage too severe to be repaired.<ref name="Free Fall" />
