'''Biller''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Police officer/Legends|police officer]] with the [[Kura City Guard]]. She held the [[Police rank|rank]] of [[Detective/Legends|Detective]] and was partnered up with [[Detective Sergeant]] [[Husin]]. She was sent to investigate the kidnapping of [[Ledina Chott]] by the hands of [[Wraith Squadron]], who were out to frame [[Galactic Federation of Free Alliances|Galactic Alliance]] [[General/Legends|General]] [[Stavin Thaal]] in [[44 ABY]]. Biller and Husin received the information of Chott's whereabouts from [[Kadd Yinkle]], who had tracked down Chott's transponder signal to one of Thaal's warehouses, which he used to operate his various criminal activities.
