'''Ace Squadron''', also known as the '''Aces''', was a [[racer]] [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] who were also [[Mercenary|employed]] by the ''[[Colossus]]'' [[Fuel|refueling]] station as it main defense during the [[New Republic Era|era]] of the [[New Republic]]. The squadron consisted of the five pilots [[Hype Fazon]], [[Freya Fenris]], [[Griff Halloran]], [[Bo Keevil]], and [[Torra Doza]]. Besides their defense and escort work, Ace Squadron also participated in the station's numerous races.

==Description==
Ace Squadron was a starfighter squadron that defended the ''[[Colossus]]'' [[Fuel|refueling]] station on the planet [[Castilon]]. It was also tasked with the handling of critical shipments. The Squadron consisted of the seven best [[pilot]]s&mdash;[[Jarek Yeager]], [[Hype Fazon]], [[Torra Doza]], [[Kazuda Xiono]], [[Freya Fenris]], [[Griff Halloran]], and [[Bo Keevil]]&mdash;in the facility. They were also considered some of the best pilots in [[the galaxy]]. Aside from their role in station security, Ace Squadron was known to engage in competitive [[starship]] racing for leisure.<ref name="The Recruit" /><ref name="The High Tower" />

Ace Squadron had seven [[racer]]s which doubled as starfighters: Fazon's ''[[Green Ace]]'', Doza's ''[[Blue Ace]]'', Xiono’s ''[[Fireball]]'', Yeager’s [[Jarek Yeager's racer|ship]], Fenris' ''[[Red Ace]]'', Halloran's ''[[Black Ace]]'', and Keevil's ''[[Yellow Ace]]''.<ref name="The Recruit" /> Ace Squadron resided in the luxurious [[Doza Tower]] where they relaxed in the [[Aces' Lounge]].<ref name="The High Tower" /> Fazon kept his ship in his own [[Hype Fazon's hangar bay|personal hangar]] located inside Doza Tower.<ref name="Disappeared">{{Resistance|The Disappeared}}</ref>

==History==
===Juggling defense and racing===
[[File:AcesLounge-TheHighTower.jpg|thumb|right|225px|Ace Squadron acted as both racing champions on the ''Colossus'' as well as the station's personal defensive fighter squadron, earning them a lavish lifestyle aboard the ''Colossus''.]]
During the [[New Republic]] Era, Ace Squadron defended the ''Colossus'' from an [[Attack on the Colossus|attack]] by [[Captain]] [[Kragan Gorr]]'s [[Warbird gang]] of pirates.<ref name="The Triple Dark">{{Resistance|The Triple Dark}}</ref> While Ace Squadron was escorting an important supply shipment, the Warbird gang launched another [[Raid on the Colossus|attack]] on the ''Colossus'' but were repelled by [[Team Fireball|Team ''Fireball'']] and the undercover pirate [[Synara San]]. The second attack led [[Captain]] [[Imanuel Doza]] to consider the [[First Order]]'s proposal to defend the station.<ref name="Synara's Score">{{Resistance|Synara's Score}}</ref> Later, Ace Squadron took part in defending the ''Colossus'' from the enraged [[rokkna]] [[Bibo's mother|mother]] who was seeking her child [[Bibo]] during the "[[Bibo incident]]."<ref name="Bibo">{{Resistance|Bibo (episode)}}</ref>

Aside from their defensive role, Ace Squadron participated in several races including a [[Race between Kazuda Xiono and Torra Doza|race]] with [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] and the [[Platform Classic]] featuring [[Marcus Speedstar]], one of the greatest racing champions in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="The Recruit" /><ref name="Platform Classic">{{Resistance|The Platform Classic}}</ref> One of their members, Captain Doza’s daughter, [[Torra Doza|Torra]], was kidnapped by the Warbird gang at the behest of the First Order in return for triple their usual rate. Her comrades pursued the pirates but the First Order managed to rescue Torra first. This forced Captain Doza to consent to the First Order's [[Occupation of the Colossus|military occupation]] of the ''Colossus''.<ref name="The First Order Occupation">{{Resistance|The First Order Occupation}}</ref>

===The First Order occupation===
[[File:DozaFreesAces-NoEscapePart2.png|thumb|left|225px|The Aces are released from imprisonment by Captain Imanuel Doza]]
After the First Order arrived on the ''Colossus'' under the guise of providing security, races were immediately suspended and all starfighters were grounded. Fazon protested and got arrested by [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]]. He was able to [[Rescue on the Colossus|escape]] [[Prison|captivity]] with the help of Xiono and Torra.<ref name="Disappeared" /> When [[Team Colossus]], led by Xiono, deliberately sunk the ''Colossus'' into the ocean to send a message to [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], Ace Squadron scrambled to their stations.<ref name="Descent">{{Resistance|Descent (episode)}}</ref>

Following the sinking of the ''Colossus'', [[Stormtrooper Commander]] [[Pyre]] ordered the imprisonment of Ace Squadron, deeming them a threat to the First Order after Captain Doza was apprehended for insubordination. However, Torra escaped and joined Xiono.<ref name="No Escape 1">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 1}}</ref> After Team Colossus retook the station, Ace Squadron, and Xiono with his friend [[Jarek Yeager]], participated in a dogfight with First Order starfighters during the ''Colossus''{{'}} [[Battle of Castilon|escape]] from Castilon. They then escaped on board the ''Colossus'' into [[hyperspace]] with the intent of joining the Resistance.<ref name="No Escape 2">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 2}}</ref>

===Station's voyage===
[[File:TeamColossus-NoEscapeP2.jpg|thumb|right|225px|After escaping the First Order, Ace Squadron joined the ''Colossus'' cell of the Resistance, continuing to defend the station from the First Order.]]
Shortly afterward, the ''Colossus''{{'}} hyperdrive ran out of [[coaxium]], stranding it three parsecs from [[D'Qar]]. They soon after ran low on supplies while the First Order continued on their quest to capture or destroy the station.<ref name="Into the Unknown">{{Resistance|Into the Unknown}}</ref>

After limping into the [[Ileenium system]], the crew discovered the remnants of a [[Battle of D'Qar|fierce battle]] and the [[Resistance base]]. Xiono hatched a plan to recover coaxium from the wreckage of a [[Fulminatrix|dreadnought]] destroyed by the friends of his mentor [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]]. After Pyre's [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] arrived in the system, Ace Squadron was scrambled to defend the salvage team from [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]]. Once the team returned to the ''Colossus'' and refueled the hyperdrive, Ace Squadron landed back aboard, allowing them to escape back into hyperspace.<ref name="Quick Salvage Run">{{Resistance|A Quick Salvage Run}}</ref> However, the conflict highlighted the limitations of Ace Squadron when pitted against the trained pilots of the First Order, exposing these flaws to both Captain Doza and the Aces themselves.<ref name="Live Fire" />

[[File:Celsor3Aces-ConceptArt-PathOfResistance.png|thumb|left|225px|Ace Squadron flying in the atmosphere of Celsor 3 attempting to hunt down a jakoosk to feed the residents of the ''Colossus''.]]
Eventually, after the [[Skirmish over D'Qar]], Xiono became their newest member and Yeager became their new Squadron Leader so the others could become good combat pilots. Their first exercise was a disaster, with the two newcomers getting the drop on the Squadron. After Yeager split them into pairs, they learned to fight together, saving their new Squadron Leader after his ship was grabbed by a [[jakoosk]].<ref name="Live Fire" /> Following a failed attempt by the Warbird gang to hunt the jakoosk, Kaz, Torra, and his astromech droid [[CB-23]] worked together to hunt down the creature, providing enough meat for the residents of the ''Colossus''.<ref name="Hunt on Celsor 3">{{Resistance|Hunt on Celsor 3}}</ref>

After the depot was sabotaged by mercenary [[Nenavakasa Nalor]], Ace Squadron were scrambled to defend it from Pyre's Star Destroyer while the engineering team restored functionality.<ref name="The Engineer">{{Resistance|The Engineer}}</ref>

[[File:AshasRee-TheRelicRaiders.jpg|thumb|right|225px|Ace Squadron members Torra Doza, Kazuda Xiono, and Freya Fenris undertook a mission to the planet Ashas Rae, where they uncovered a Sith Temple.]]
Later, Xiono along with fellow Ace pilots Torra, Freya, and their respective droids undertook a [[Mission to Ashas Ree|supply mission]] to the planet [[Ashas Ree]]. [[Kel]] and [[Eila]] stowed aboard Xiono's shuttle. Together, with Xiono and CB-23, they explored a local [[Ashas Ree Sith temple|Sith temple]] and rescued an archaeologist named [[Mika Grey]]. The group was subsequently captured by a group of [[First Order Raider]]s led by [[Agent]] [[Raith]] but were saved by Grey, who activated a Sith relic that killed them. Grey accompanied the Aces, Kel and Eila back to the ''Colossus''.<ref name="Relic Raiders">{{Resistance|The Relic Raiders}}</ref>

Later, Doza subsequently took the station to hide in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. As they deployed a beacon at a rendezvous point, Ace Squadron was forced to [[Skirmish by a signal beacon|defend it]] until they were beaten back, landing back aboard the station to escape.<ref name="Rendezvous Point">{{Resistance|Rendezvous Point (episode)}}</ref>

===Continued adventures===
[[File:VrankisHotelAndCasino-ConceptArt-PathOfResistance.png|thumb|left|225px|Ace Squadron was forced to compete in Vranki the Hutt's Voxx Vortex 5000 to raise funds for the ''Colossus''.]]
Seeking to raise [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] for the ''Colossus'' and growing bored, members of Ace Squadron agreed to race with Hype’s old employer the [[Hutt]] businessman [[Vranki]]'s [[Droid racer|racing droids]]. During the [[Voxx Vortex 5000]], the Aces were systematically outmaneuvered by Vranki, who used unscrupulous tactics to win the various legs of the race. In the final leg, Vranki bid on the entire ''Colossus'' and the Aces. However, Xiono managed to outwit him with the help of Vozo, who used his technical savvy to beat Vranki's droid algorithms. As a result, Vranki was forced to let Ace Squadron go and gave the ''Colossus'' half a million credits.<ref name="Voxx Vortex 5000">{{Resistance|The Voxx Vortex 5000}}</ref>

[[File:GDSSkirmish-KazsCurse.png|thumb|right|225px|Ace Squadron fought against the Guavian Death Gang in defense of the ''Colossus''.]]
As the ''Colossus'' continued hiding in the Outer Rim Territories, the station traveled through [[Guavian Drift|Guavian Death Space]]. Their home got [[Skirmish in Guavian Death Space|attacked]] by the Gang, and when Captain Doza refused to give into their demands, Ace Squadron was scrambled. Using Kaz's guidance, they took out all the attacking [[Avandor-class fighter/transport|fighter ships]].<ref name="Kaz's Curse">{{Resistance|Kaz's Curse}}</ref>

After Xiono, Yeager, San, and CB-23 undertook a [[Rescue of Norath Kev|mission]] to rescue fellow Resistance spy [[Norath Kev]] on the planet [[Varkana]], Ace Squadron led by Torra escorted Xiono's team and Kev offworld. Ace Squadron also fought several TIE fighters. The ''Colossus'' then evacuated them offworld.<ref name="Breakout">{{Resistance|Breakout}}</ref>

[[File:GriffKazAeos-TheNewWorld.jpg|thumb|left|225px|Ace Squadron members Griff Halloran and Kazuda Xiono were initially captured by the native Aeosians while exploring Aeos Prime.]]
Later, Ace Squadron explored the ocean planet of [[Aeos Prime]], which Captain Doza intended to establish as the ''Colossus’'' new home. Xiono and CB-23 accompanied Griff and his astromech droid [[R5-G9]]. While scouting a nearby island, they encountered several indigenous [[Aeosian]]s, who initially reacted with hostility, blaming the offworlders for a recent First Order [[Attack on Aeos Prime|attack]] on [[Unidentified Aeosian village|their village]]. Meanwhile, Freya and Bo Keevil were involved a dogfight with Aeosians riding [[Krakavora]]. Xiono managed to convince the [[Unidentified Aeosian Queen|Aeosian Queen]] that the ''Colossus'' did not pose a threat by healing one of their members. As a result, the Aeosian Queen ordered her people to stand down and made peace with Captain Doza. She allowed the ''Colossus'' to stay for as long as they wanted.<ref name="The New World">{{Resistance|The New World}}</ref>

[[File:AeosPrimeEscape-NoPlaceSafe.jpg|thumb|right|225px|Ace Squadron fought against the First Order Navy during the Battle of Aeos Prime, working alongside the native Aeosians to fend off the First Order.]]
While staying on Aeos Prime, Ace Squadron resumed their races on the ocean planet, which had a similar environment to Castilon. Xiono subsequently departed on the ''[[Fireball]]'' with CB-23 to rendezvous with the Resistance since he could not stay out of the fight because he did not want the First Order to make others lose everything like they did with him during the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]]. However, he was forced to abandon his plan after encountering a [[spider probe droid]] in space, which alerted Commander Pyre and [[First Order Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Tierny]] to their presence. Ace Squadron along with Xiono subsequently took part in a [[Battle of Aeos Prime|battle]] with First Order forces, downing several TIE fighters and [[TIE/se Bomber]]s. With the help of their Aeosian allies, the ''Colossus'' inhabitants managed to escape back into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="No Place Safe">{{Resistance|No Place Safe}}</ref>

===Joining the Resistance===
[[File:ResistanceDantooineBriefing-RebuildingTheResistance.png|thumb|left|225px|Ace Squadron officially worked alongside the Resistance under Commander Venisa Doza and her Jade Squadron to save Resistance recruits from the First Order on Dantooine.]]
Following a request for help from Captain Doza's wife and Torra’s mother Commander [[Venisa Doza]] and [[Jade Squadron]], Ace Squadron accompanied Jade Squadron on a mission to breach the First Order's [[Blockade of Dantooine|blockade]] of [[Dantooine]] and evacuate three shuttles of Resistance recruits. During the mission, the convoy were attacked by the ''[[Thunderer]]'' and one got destroyed. After the convoy returned to the ''Colossus'' with the Resistance recruits, Captain Doza let his wife’s forces stay and decided to use the ''Colossus'' to help the Resistance since he was done running from the fight.<ref name="Rebuilding the Resistance">{{Resistance|Rebuilding the Resistance}}</ref>

[[File:TeamColossus-TheEscape.png|thumb|right|225px|Ace Squadron celebrated their victory at the Battle of Barabesh, where they destroyed the star destroyer ''Thunderer'', and with it the detachment of the First Order assigned to hunt the ''Colossus''.]]
During a mission by Xiono, Yeager, and CB-23 to [[Battle of Barabesh|rescue]] [[Tamara Ryvora]] from the First Order, the last [[Imperial Remnants|remnant]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] caught up with the ''Colossus'' in the [[Barabesh system]]. Ace and Jade Squadrons, alongside San, took part in the defense of the ''Colossus'', fighting TIE fighters and the ''Thunderer''. After CB-23 lowered the Star Destroyer's shields, the two Squadrons blew up the ship's engines, destroying the vessel. Following the defeat of the First Order’s hunting party, Captain Doza and the Ace and Jade Squadrons joined Xiono, Yeager, and Ryvora in celebrating her homecoming. Several months later, Ace Squadron and Captain Doza joined the [[Citizens' Fleet]] assembled by [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] to help the Resistance win the [[Battle of Exegol|decisive battle]] against the [[Sith Eternal]]'s [[Sith Eternal military|forces]] on the planet [[Exegol]] in the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="The Escape">{{Resistance|The Escape (episode)}}</ref>
