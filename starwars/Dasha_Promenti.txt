'''Dasha Promenti''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] member<ref name="TFAVD" /> of the [[Church of the Force]],<ref name="RSG" /> who lived in [[Tuanul]] village on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. There, along with the other villagers, she followed an ascetic lifestyle which rejected comfort, luxury and the trappings of war. Around thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="TFAVD" /> Promenti was present in the village when it was [[Attack on Tuanul|attacked]] by sixty [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] of the [[First Order]], who were searching for the village resident [[Lor San Tekka]] and the [[Map to Skywalker|map]] fragment he possessed. Promenti and the other surviving villagers, including Tekka, were then rounded up and executed by the First Order forces.<ref name="TFA" /> Promenti wore a simple [[Clothing|linen dress]] dyed [[Color|turquoise]] underneath a [[fantabu]]-[[Hair|fur]] fringed<ref name="TFAVD" /> sleeveless overcoat,<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> which she tied the [[Sash of the Balanced]] on top of. She kept her brown hair unadorned and had fair skin. In her final battle, she fought using a [[blaster pistol]] with a handle made from a carved [[dune zaywar]] tusk.<ref name="TFAVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DashaPromentisBlasterPistol-TFA.png|thumb|left|250px|Dasha Promenti's blaster pistol]]
Dasha Promenti was portrayed by [[Ana-Maria Leonte]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], although her name was first revealed in an exclusive article on [[Wikipedia:Wired (website)|''Wired.com'']] detailing a number of the prop weapons used in the film, published several weeks before the film's released in [[2015]].<ref name="Wired">{{WebCite|url=https://www.wired.com/2015/11/star-wars-force-awakens-props|text=''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Arsenal of Battle Props|work=Wired|archivedate=20190129062057}}</ref> Which character in the film was Promenti was then revealed by the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released in conjunction with the film.<ref name="TFAVD" />

Though the character's role in ''The Force Awakens'' was minimal, the [[2016]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'' gave a more important role to Dasha Promenti during the attack on Tuanul sequence. There, she joins [[Poe Dameron]] and [[BB-8]] in their&ndash;ultimately unsuccessful&ndash; escape attempt from Tuanul. Unlike her canon counterpart, she seemingly survives the First Order's attack by escaping to a safe location along with the youngest villagers of Tuanul.<ref name="LEGO TFA">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref>
