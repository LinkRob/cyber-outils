'''Byss''' was a [[Planet|world]] located in [[the Interior]] and the [[Deep Core]] regions of [[the galaxy]]. It acted as the endpoint of the [[Byss Run]] and was part of the larger [[Nexus Route]]. Discovered late in the [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]], Byss was used as a secret [[throneworld]] by [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. 

==Description==
Byss was a terrestrial<ref name="Topps" /> [[Planet|world]] with an eerie glow caused by its [[Star|sun]], and was considered to be strange.<ref name="BMF36" /> From [[orbit]], it appeared to have bands of brown covering it, each in a different shade.<ref name="BMF36" /> The world was located in [[the Interior]]<ref name="Interior" /> and [[Deep Core]] regions of [[the galaxy]] at [[Standard Galactic Grid]] square K-11.<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> Byss acted as the endpoint of the [[Byss Run]] [[hyperspace route]], which connected it to<ref name="BMF36" /> the planet<ref name="Abyss">''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]''</ref> [[Prakith]].<ref name="BMF36" /> The Byss Run was part of the larger [[Nexus Route]],<ref name="NexusRoute" /> which connected Byss to the [[Abregado system]] in the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Galaxy Map" />

==History==
[[File:ByssRun-BTMF36.png|thumb|left|300px|Byss was the end point of the Byss Run.]]
Byss was only discovered late in the [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]]<ref name="BMF36" /> and shared its name with [[homeworld]] of the [[Abyssin]] [[species]].<ref name="M&A">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> The Deep Core world became important to [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="BMF36" /> Palpatine used the world as a secret [[throneworld]] and had an [[Imperial citadel]] there which [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]], one of Palpatine's underlings, at one point retreated to in order to outline plans for the next ten thousand [[Standard year|years]] of the Empire's [[New Order]].<ref name="Topps" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Starcruiserdate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> [[Chandrila Star Line]]'s [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|Star Cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' displayed a partial map of the [[galaxy]] that showed Byss on several [[Viewscreen|screens]] while on a cruise from the [[Core Worlds]]' [[Chandrila system]] to the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="GS">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref> Byss also featured as one of the worlds in the Interior section of the game [[Sector Set]], which could be played in the [[cruiser]]'s atrium during the cruise.<ref name="Interior" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Byss was first mentioned on a card in the [[2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives|2015 Topps ''Star Wars'' Chrome Perspectives]] set released by [[Topps|The Topps Company, Inc.]]<ref name="Topps" /> on [[August 21]], [[2015]]. It was then pictured for the first time in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 36|thirty-sixth]] issue of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF36" /> published around [[September 9]], 2015.<ref name="BMFdate">{{BuildFalconDates|36}}</ref> The [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'', written by [[Charles Soule]], mentions a Byss far from the [[planet]] [[Hetzal Prime]] where systems [[engineer]]s are aiding the [[technician]] [[Keven Tarr]] during the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], but does not specify if it is referring to the Deep Core world or the Abyssin homeworld.<ref name="LOTJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

The world's first [[in-universe]] appearance came in the form of being depicted on a map viewable in [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]], an immersive roleplaying experience at [[W:c:disney:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]] in Florida<ref name="GS" /> that opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="31 Things">{{SW|url=news/31-things-we-learned-about-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=31 Things We Learned in an Exclusive Preview of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser|nolive=1}}</ref> [[Byss/Legends|Byss]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[Dark Empire 2|second issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Empire]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book series]], which was written by [[Tom Veitch]], illustrated by [[Cam Kennedy]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="DE2">[[Dark Empire 2|''Dark Empire'' 2]]</ref> on [[February 18]], [[1992]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54058/star_wars_dark_empire_1991_2|text=Star Wars: Dark Empire (1991) #2}}</ref>
