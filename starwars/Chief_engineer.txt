'''Chief engineer''' was an [[engineer]]ing [[Assignment|position]] held by [[Del Meeko]] aboard the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Star Destroyer]] [[Implacable (Star Destroyer)|''Implacable'']]. After Meeko was reassigned to join [[Inferno Squad]], [[Naylyn Bashan]] was to take his place as [[chief]] [[engineer]] aboard the [[Starship|vessel]].<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> [[Neeku Vozo]] served as the chief engineer of the ''[[Colossus]]'' during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], a position which made Vozo seen as useful in the eyes of the [[Warbird gang]], a group of [[pirate]]s who intended to use his work to stage a [[Mutiny on the Colossus|mutiny]].<ref name="The Mutiny" />
