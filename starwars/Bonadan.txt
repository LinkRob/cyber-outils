'''Bonadan''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[Corporate Sector]] that marked the start of the [[Hydian Way]]. By [[10 BBY]], the [[arms dealer]]s [[Ephant Mon]] and [[Masse Goskey]] had attended an arms exchange on the planet.

==Description==
Bonadan was a [[planet]] located in the [[Corporate Sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Scarif" /> at coordinates Q-4 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="The Force Awakens Beginner Game" /> The world was the starting point of the [[Hydian Way]] [[hyperspace route]],<ref name="Scarif" /> which linked it to the [[Salient system]].<ref name="Timeline">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:Mon-Gosskey.png|thumb|right|300px|Ephant Mon and Masse Goskey attended an arms deal on Bonadan.]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], the future [[Jedi Master]] [[Torbin]] was born on Bonadan to [[Torbin's parents|paupers]].<ref name="Choice">{{Acolyte|Choice}}</ref> By [[22 BBY]],<ref name="AOTCDate">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}}, as depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[22 BBY]].</ref> the planet had an [[Bonadan Embassy|embassy]] in the [[Federal District]] of [[Coruscant]], the [[capital]] world of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> By [[10 BBY]]<ref name="Date">Ephant Mon's entry in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'' is written prior to an entry by [[Dryden Vos]]. Vos [[Death|dies]] during a portion of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' that ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates to nine years after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the formation to [[19 BBY]], Vos's death must occur in [[10 BBY]] and Mon's entry in the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' must be written at some point prior to that.</ref> the [[arms dealer]]s [[Ephant Mon]] and [[Masse Goskey]] attended an arms exchange on Bonadan where Mon purchased a new writing stylus and a billy-bag of finely shredded savory [[Foodstuff|sweetmeats]]. He then mentioned the planet when writing about the exchange in the [[book]] that would become known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

The following entry in the book contained a post from a user named "[[BonadanBouncer]]" on [[Cynabar's InfoNet]] that claimed that the [[crime lord]] [[Azmorigan]] should be avoided as he had not paid for clean-up after the user transported [[puffer pig]]s for him.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> The [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] had flown to Bonadan<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> by [[1 BBY]].<ref name="ANH">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Bonadan was first mentioned on a map in the [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' boxed set.<ref name="The Force Awakens Beginner Game" /> The world originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[1979]] novel ''[[Han Solo's Revenge]]'', which was written by [[Brian Daley]].<ref name="Revenge">''[[Han Solo's Revenge]]''</ref>
