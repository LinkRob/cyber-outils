'''Arkanian Chill''' was an online supplement for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website in [[February]] [[2003]] as part of the site's ''[[Planet Hoppers]]'' series. It was written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]]. The supplement was split into four separate articles, focusing on the exploits of [[Gand/Legends|Gand]] [[Findsman/Legends|Findsman]] [[Vaskau Farg]] on [[Arkania/Legends|Arkania]].

==Contents==
===Part 1: A Cold Reception===
''&hellip;the [[Gand/Legends|Gand]] [[Findsman/Legends|Findsman]] called [[Vaskau Farg]] arrives on [[Water/Legends|icy]] [[Arkania/Legends|Arkania]] and we learn how little it takes to offend a [[Hyrim Focela|native]] -- and how much it takes to appease one.''<ref name="Arkania" />

===Part 2: Hyrim Focela===
''&hellip;Vaskau Farg must subdue his compulsion to perpetrate violence against the annoying but powerful [[Administrator/Legends|administrator]] Hyrim Focela.''<ref name="Arkania" />

===Part 3: Ice Palaces===
''&hellip;Vaskau Farg stumbles across the [[Unidentified Arkanian Microtechnologies installation|hidden base]] of [[Arkanian Microtechnologies]], and we learn of its complex (and [[Death/Legends|lethal]]) security system.''<ref name="Arkania" />

===Part 4: The Re-Engineered===
''&hellip;Vaskau Farg encounters a [[Unidentified Sakiyan scientist|scientist]] responsible for terrible [[Gene/Legends|genetic]] experiments, and we learn of the [[Re-engineering|Re-Engineered]].''<ref name="Arkania" />
