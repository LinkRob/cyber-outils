'''Bacco''' was an individual who served as a [[sava]],<ref name="Chaos Agent 1" /> a position held by a [[scholar]].<ref name="Crimson Reign 3">[[Crimson Reign 3|''Crimson Reign'' 3]]</ref> At one point, they carried out research on [[Luovian Stars]], which the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] read texts of.<ref name="Chaos Agent 1" /> No earlier than [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{Marvel|url=articles/comics/star-wars-doctor-aphra-chaos-agent-1-cover-the-new-republic|text=Star Wars' Doctor Aphra Navigates the New Republic in New Ongoing Comic Series}} establishes the events of ''[[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent]]'' take place after the [[Battle of Jakku]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[5 ABY]].</ref> Aphra and the [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]] met with the criminal [[Baff Baksheen]] on<ref name="Chaos Agent 1" /> the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Complete Locations">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Daiyu]] about a [[Luovian Gauntlet]] he owned and claimed had been experimented on by the [[Galactic Empire]] to harness a powerful source of energy. Upon examining the [[gauntlet]], Aphra noticed Luovian Stars on it and noted they were the same as the ones in the texts she had read of Bacco's research.<ref name="Chaos Agent 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bacco was mentioned in the [[Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent 1|first issue]] of the [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent]]''. The issue was written by [[Cherish Chen]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Chaos Agent 1" /> on [[June 18]], 2025.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/122232/star_wars_doctor_aphra_-_chaos_agent_2025_1|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra - Chaos Agent (2025) #1}}</ref>
