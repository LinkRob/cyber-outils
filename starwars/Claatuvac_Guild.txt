The '''Claatuvac Guild''' was an ancient organization of [[Wookiee]] [[cartographer]]s and [[navigator]]s reputed for their knowledge of secret [[hyperspace]] [[Hyperspace route|routes]]. The Guild's headquarters were located in the [[Tree Vikkilynn]], in the settlement of [[Kachirho]], [[Kashyyyk]].<ref name="CL" />
