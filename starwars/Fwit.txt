'''Fwits''' were a [[species]] native to the [[planet]] [[Maridun]]. They were [[Hunting|hunted]] by the [[Amani]]n, a [[Sentience|sentient]] species from the same planet.<ref name="Mustafar and Other Planets" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Fwit-UA.jpg|thumb|right|150px|A ''Star Wars'' Legends depiction of a Fwit]]
Fwits originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, and were first mentioned in ''[[Ultimate Adversaries]]'',<ref name="Ultimate Adversaries">''[[Ultimate Adversaries]]''</ref> a [[2004]] [[roleplaying game]] sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'', published by [[Wizards of the Coast]].<ref name="Catalog of Enemies">{{SWArchive|url=gaming/other/rpg/f20040616/index.html|text=Catalog of Enemies}}</ref> They were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Mustafar and Other Planets in the Outer Rim]],"<ref name="Mustafar and Other Planets" /> a [[2022]] booklet in a series of reference booklets titled the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']].<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|lang=fr|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Official Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref>
