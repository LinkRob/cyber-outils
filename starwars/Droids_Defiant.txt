"'''Droids Defiant'''" is an adventure for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' which appeared in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 9|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 9]]. It was written by [[Thomas Bowling]] and illustrated by [[Matt Busch]].

==Plot summary==
Recent [[Privateer/Legends|privateer]] activity on [[Cosmohaul Shipping]] results in a Cosmohaul executive having the idea to hire cheap mercenaries to guard their shipment. The mercenaries are hired onto the ''[[Argent Lady]]'', a [[Cargo Empress-class super freighter|''Cargo Empress''-class super freighter]] captained by [[Xalto Sneerzick]] tasked with hauling a group of [[R5-series astromech droid]]. 

Unbeknownst to them, Sneerzick and his crew are secretly part of the [[Droid Abolitionist Movement]] and upload an [[emancipation virus]] into the droids in hopes of causing them to break their programming and become independent. However, a malfunction in some of the R5 causes them to revolt against their liberators and kill the ''Argent Lady'' crew. 

The mercenaries then have to escape the newly arrived [[Cutlass (IPV-1 System Patrol Craft)|''Cutlass'']], an [[IPV-1 System Patrol Craft|Imperial Patrol Ship]] demanding that the ship power down for a 'routine inspection.'
