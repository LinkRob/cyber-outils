'''Cherami Leigh''' (born [[July 19]], [[1988]] in [[Wikipedia:Dallas|Dallas]], [[Wikipedia:Texas|Texas]], [[Wikipedia:United States|United States]])<ref name="Birth" /> is an actor and voice actor.<ref name="Cheramileigh" /> Leigh voiced the [[New Republic]] [[pilot]] [[Mia Gabon]] in the first part of "[[The Recruit]],"<ref name="The Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref> the first episode of the ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' animated television series, which aired [[October 7]], [[2018]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> They posted on social media announcing their involvement in ''Star Wars Resistance'' the day of the premiere, noting they were excited and honored to be part of the series.<ref name="Instagram" />
