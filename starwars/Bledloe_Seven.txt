'''Bledloe Seven''' was a [[Gotal]] member of the [[Nihil]] during the [[High Republic Era]] among the Nihil presence on<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 7" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref> [[Corellia]]. They provided Nihil [[Commander]] [[Krix Kamerat]]&mdash;who unbeknownst to Bledloe Seven had [[Desertion|defected]] from the Nihil&mdash;with a [[Krix Kamerat's ship|ship]] equipped with a [[Path engine]] to travel to<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 7" /> [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]]'s [[flagship]],<ref name="Temptation of the Force">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' in the [[Occlusion Zone]], unaware of the former commander's true intentions to [[Death|assassinate]] Ro.<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 7" />
