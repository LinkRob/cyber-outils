'''Culper''' was a [[Species|human]] [[Gender|female]] who was an [[aide]] to [[Moff]] [[Ghadi]]. She met with [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] to offer him a position within the [[Imperial Military]], in order to separate him from [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]. This was due to jealousy of Thrawn's achievements as well as anti-[[alien]] [[Xenophobia|views]] from senior [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] officials.<ref name="Thrawn" />
