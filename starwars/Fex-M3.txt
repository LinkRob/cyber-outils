'''Fex-M3''' was a [[Poison|toxin]] that, when used in conjunction with [[Malkite themfar]], could cause [[death]] in less than ten [[second]]s. This deadly mixture was loaded into the poison chamber of the [[Kamino saberdart]], a type of [[Poison dart|toxic dart]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />
