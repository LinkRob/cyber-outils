The '''Declaration of Rebellion''' was a document addressed to the [[Galactic Emperor]], [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], that marked the formal declaration of open resistance against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Its [[High Galactic|text]] was [[Writing|written]] by [[Senator]]-in-Exile [[Mon Mothma]], the [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|chancellor]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], and was designed to push back against the Imperial notion that the Rebel Alliance was a group of [[terrorist]]s. The Declaration came to be known as one of the foundational documents of the [[New Republic]], which arose to replace the fallen Empire after the signing of the [[Galactic Concordance]], which brought the [[Battle of Jakku|end]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==History==
[[File:Mon_Mothma_Rogue_One_promo.jpg|thumb|left|200px|The Declaration of Rebellion was delivered by Senator-in-Exile Mon Mothma, leader of the Rebel Alliance.]]
In the [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] in [[19 BBY]], various rebel cells began a campaign of armed resistance against the [[Galactic Emperor]], [[Darth Sidious]], and his Imperial regime. Many of these cells eventually came together under the leadership of such individuals as [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] of [[Chandrila]], [[Viceroy]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] of [[Alderaan]], and [[Captain]] [[Hera Syndulla]] of [[Ryloth]]. Together, these cells formed the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. The Empire painted the Rebel Alliance as [[blood]]thirsty [[Terrorist|anarchists]], so the Alliance sought to push back on that notion and make clear that their goal was to restore the [[Galactic Republic]] that Sidious, known publicly as Emperor Palpatine, had [[Proclamation of the New Order|supplanted]] at the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="SWP" />

To counter the Imperial [[propaganda]], Senator-in-Exile Mon Mothma crafted the Declaration of Rebellion. The document was a condemnation of Emperor Palpatine and detailed what the Alliance saw as his crimes against [[the galaxy]]. The Alliance purposefully crafted the document in [[language]] based around the law to both legitimize the rebel cause and to emphasize that the Empire was an illegal regime. The document stressed that the Rebellion sought to build a better galaxy, not destroy it as the Empire claimed, and that the Rebellion's true name was the Alliance to Restore the Republic.<ref name="SWP" />

Mothma delivered the Declaration of Rebellion from an undisclosed location using the [[HoloNet]] and other channels. The message spread throughout the galaxy, with various posters and other artwork being created with Mothma's words. In some cases, this artwork was crafted by the Rebel Alliance itself to spread its own message, but mostly was the work of revolutionaries who sought a unifying message and banner to fight under. In the years that followed, the [[Galactic Civil War]] led to the defeat of the Empire. The Declaration came to be seen as one of the foundational documents of the [[New Republic]], which came to power towards the [[Battle of Jakku|end]] of the war and replaced the [[New Order|Imperial regime]].<ref name="SWP" />

==Full text==
{{ExcerptIU|<em>
'''W'''e, the beings of the Rebel Alliance, do this day send forth this Declaration to His Majesty, the Emperor, and to all sentient beings in the Galaxy, to make clear to all the Purposes and Goals of this Rebellion.

'''W'''e firmly acknowledge the importance and necessity of the institution of Galactic Government. We accept that all must subjugate themselves to that Government, giving up certain rights and freedoms, in return for peace, prosperity and happiness for all.<br />We believe that the Galactic Government derives its power and right to rule from the consent of the governed. We believe that, should the rights of free beings be willfully and malignantly usurped, it is the unalienable right of said beings to alter or abolish said Government.

'''W'''e believe that the Galactic Empire has willfully and malignantly usurped the rights of the free beings of the Galaxy and therefore, it is our unalienable right to abolish it from the Galaxy.

We do not take this course lightly. Governments long established should not be changed for light and transient causes, but when a Government displays a history of usurpation, abuse and moral atrocity, displaying a clear design to subjugate totally and absolutely beings born free under the auspices of nature, it is our right&mdash;our duty&mdash;to depose of that Government.

'''T'''he history of the present Galactic Empire is of repeated injuries upon its members, with the direct objective of establishing you, Emperor Palpatine, as absolute tyrant over the Galaxy:
:You have disbanded the Senate, the voice of the people;
:You have instituted a policy of blatant speciesism and genocide against the nonhuman peoples of the Galaxy;
:You have overthrown the chosen leaders of planets, replacing them with Moffs and Governors of your choice;
:You have raised taxes without the consent of those taxed;
:You have murdered and imprisoned millions without benefit of trial;
:You have unlawfully taken land and property;
:You have expanded the military far beyond what is necessary and prudent, for the sole purpose of oppressing your subjects.

'''W'''e, the Rebel Alliance, do therefore in the name&mdash;and by the authority&mdash;of the free beings of the Galaxy, solemnly publish and declare our intentions:
:To fight and oppose you and your forces, by any and all means at our disposal;
:To refuse any Imperial law contrary to the rights of free beings;
:To bring about your destruction and the destruction of the Galactic Empire;
:To make forever free all beings in the galaxy.

'''T'''o these ends, we pledge our property, our honor, and our lives.
</em>|attrib=The Declaration of Rebellion<ref name="SWP" />}}

==Behind the scenes==
The text of the Declaration of Rebellion first appeared in the ''[[The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook]]'', a [[1990]] reference book written by [[Paul Murphy]].<ref name="Rebel Alliance Sourcebook">''[[The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook]]''</ref> The text was brought into the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', by [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] creative executive [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SWP" /> The declaration shares some overall similarities in structure, tone, and choice of words with the [[Wikipedia:United States Declaration of Independence|United States Declaration of Independence]] of 1776, which was influenced by and further influenced several similar documents in real life. Indeed, ''The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook'' has an illustration of the presentation of the declaration to Mon Mothma and her comrades that emulates the painting, ''The Declaration of Independence''.
