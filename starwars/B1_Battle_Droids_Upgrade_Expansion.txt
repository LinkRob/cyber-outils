The '''B1 Battle Droids Upgrade Expansion''' ('''SWL54''') is an expansion to ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'', an asymmetrical two player infantry-based wargame published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]]. It was announced on [[October 30]], [[2019]]<ref name="New Models">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/10/30/new-models|text=New Models}}</ref> and released on [[January 24]], [[2020]]. It includes four unpainted statues of [[B1-series battle droid]]s.<ref name="Available Now" />

==Publisher's summary==
===Online summary===
''The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance's]] [[B1-series battle droid|B1 Battle Droids]] are manufactured to attack in massive numbers and wear down enemy forces through raw attrition alone. But that does not mean they don't have other purposes to serve as the Separatists seek to overthrow the [[Galactic Republic]]. Other series of [[battle droid]]s perform a variety of tasks for the [[Confederacy military|Separatist military]] and a select few [[droid]]s are outfitted with different weapons that give them unique capabilities.''<ref name="B1 Upgrade" />

''The B1 Battle Droids Upgrade Expansion contains four unpainted, finely sculpted B1 Battle Droids different from those found in the [[Clone Wars Core Set]] or the [[B1 Battle Droids Unit Expansion]]. These miniatures arrive on frames, giving you freedom to customize their appearance. On the battlefield, these miniatures can become the basis for a new unit of B1 Battle Droids, or you can use the new heavy weapon and personnel upgrade cards options they bring to enhance your existing units.''<ref name="B1 Upgrade" />
*''Upgrade your B1 Battle Droids' personnel! An [[OOM-series battle droid|OOM-Series Battle Droid]] miniature and a [[OOM-series security droid|B1 Security Droid]] add even more tactical options to your units.''<ref name="B1 Upgrade" />
*''Bring more firepower to the battlefield with an [[E-5s sniper rifle|E-5s]] B1 [[Trooper]] or a [[Radiation Cannon]] B1 Trooper.''<ref name="B1 Upgrade" />
