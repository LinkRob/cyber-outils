The '''Crash Site''' was an area on the [[planet]] [[Zeffo]] formed when a [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Zeffo)|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] crashed onto Zeffo's surface after suffering heavy damage in a [[Battle against a Separatist dreadnought|battle]] against a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Providence-class Dreadnought|dreadnought]]. The location served as a center where to the right side of the location was the [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Zeffo)|Venator Wreckage]], on the left was the [[Ice Caves]] and [[Imperial Headquarters (Zeffo)|Imperial Headquarters]] with the middle side being the [[Broken Wing (location)|Broken Wing]]. Much of the location was flooded<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> and by [[14 BBY]],<ref name="JFO Date5">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> the only known entrance to the Venator Wreckage was via an [[Ion engine|ion thrust engine]] located inside the Crash Site.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

At some point in the [[Imperial Era]], the villagers who lived on Zeffo came across the Crash Site and built a memorial honouring the fallen [[clone trooper]]s. They were unaware however of the truth of [[Order 66]].

By 14 BBY,<ref name="JFO Date5" /> the [[Galactic Empire]] occupied the planet and built the [[Imperial Dig Site]] near the crashed Venator Class Star Destroyer which could be accessed using a [[turbolift]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

In 14 BBY,<ref name="JFO Date5" /> [[Cal Kestis]] would come across the Crash Site while exploring the Imperial Excavation near it.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 14 BBY,<ref name="JFO Date5" /> Cal Kestis found and harvested a [[Dreamwort]] [[seed]]. He grew the seed in a [[terrarium]] in [[Greez Dritus|Greez's]] ship, the [[Stinger Mantis]] as a way to thank Greez Dritus for his help during the [[secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
{{EndGame}}
