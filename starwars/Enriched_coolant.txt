'''Enriched coolant''' was a special mix of [[coolant]] developed by the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Despite being restricted to the [[Imperial Military]], at least one canister of it fell into the hands of the [[Rokana Raiders]], who stored it in an encryption-sealed container in the [[Khepi system]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> after the leader of the Rokana Raiders, [[Stinger Tash]], was killed in the [[Khepi Tomb]] while trying to access its treasure, a member of the [[Miyuki Trade League]] recovered one of the raider's [[R93 refractor]] [[starfighter]]s. They later offered [[Kay Vess]] the chance to fly the ship and recover the coolant from its container.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Enriched coolant first appeared in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> The coolant was a ship upgrade awarded to the player after completing the "[[Hauler's Keep]] Mechanic" intel quest. It reduced the coolant of the player's active ship system.<ref name="Outlaws" />
