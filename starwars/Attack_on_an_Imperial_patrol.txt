{{Quote|We all have things we don't forget. I remember my first unit, the boys and I were sent to Onderon to bring peace to a troubled world. We were on a routine patrol when we ran into one of your rebel friends, a Lasat mercenary who worked for Saw Gerrera. I was lucky, knocked out by the first blast. I came to, but found I couldn't move, and then I saw him. The Lasat calmly walked through smoke and fire to finish off my unit one by one. The injured never had a chance, always wondered why he let me live.|Agent Kallus, to [[Garazeb Orrelios]]|<ref name="The Honorable Ones">{{Rebels|The Honorable Ones}}</ref>}}
Around [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Partisans]] '''attacked'''<ref name="Dawn" /> [[Alexsandr Kallus]] and his [[Alexsandr Kallus' squad|squad]] of [[stormtrooper]]s,<ref name="Comp Ency" /> who had been sent to [[Onderon]] as part of Imperial efforts to bring order to the planet. The unit had been on a routine patrol<ref name="The Honorable Ones" /> led by Kallus<ref name="Dawn" /> when the Partisans crossed paths with the Imperials. A [[Unidentified Lasat mercenary|Lasat mercenary]] destroyed the entire unit except for Kallus.<ref name="The Honorable Ones" />

==Behind the scenes==
''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'',<ref name="Timelines" /> first published on [[April 18]], [[2023]],<ref name="AU release">{{PenguinBooks|lang=com.au|url=books/star-wars-timelines-9780241543832|text=Star Wars Timelines}}</ref> dates the mission to around 18 BBY,<ref name="Timelines" /> while ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'',<ref name="Dawn" />  first published on [[November 2]], 2023,<ref name="Amazon UK">{{DK|url=uk/book/9780241666371-star-wars-dawn-of-rebellion-the-visual-guide|text=Star Wars Dawn of Rebellion The Visual Guide}}</ref> dates the mission to [[7 BBY]].<ref name="Dawn" /> The [[2019]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> and its [[2024]] update ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' provided further detail by noting that the events on Onderon were Kallus's first mission for the Empire.<ref name="Comp Ency" /> ''Timelines'' dates another one of Kallus's Imperial missions&mdash;his appearance in "[[Flight of the Falcon, Part 1: Spy Games]]"&mdash;to circa [[10 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> If the mission to Onderon is Kallus's first Imperial mission,<ref name="Comp Ency" /> then it must occur before 7 BBY, per Timelines. Therefore, this article assumes ''Dawn of Rebellion'' is mistaken.
