The '''electroguillotine''' was a device used in executions by beheading the condemned. An [[electrostaff]] could be used to activate an electroguillotine.<ref name="SoftWar" /> The [[guillotine]] was made of laser energy.<ref name="SoftWarEG" /> Late in the [[Onderonian Civil War]], an electroguillotine was to be used in the execution of [[Ramsis Dendup]]. The event was interrupted by the [[Onderon rebels]] who nearly rescued the former [[king]]. The rebels were cornered by [[B2-series super battle droid]]s, resulting in [[Sanjay Rash]] announcing they were all to be killed on the electroguillotine. However, [[Akenathen Tandin]] and the [[Royal Onderon Militia]] stopped Rash, enabling them and the rebels to bring Dendup to safety in the [[Onderon rebel safe house]].<ref name="SoftWar" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Electroguillotine first appeared in "[[The Soft War]],"<ref name="SoftWar" /> the fourth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', which premiered on [[October 20]], 2012.
It was first identified in the episode guide for "The Soft War."<ref name="SoftWarEG" />
