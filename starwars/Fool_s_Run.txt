'''Fool's Run''' was a private race course owned by [[Galus Vez]], a [[Besalisk]] criminal. It ran in between the [[asteroid]]s that made up the rings of the [[Unidentified ringed planet|planet]] around which [[Osisis Station]] [[orbit]]ed. About five [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], the freedom fighter [[Hera Syndulla]] raced against Vez in Fool's Run.<ref name="RR" />
