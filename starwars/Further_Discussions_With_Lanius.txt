'''Further Discussions With Lanius''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[August 29]] [[2002]] and took the form of an interview of [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Masters]] [[Kirlocca]] and [[Lanius Qel-Bertuk]] on happenings in the Cularin systems, specifically the activity of the [[Believers]].
