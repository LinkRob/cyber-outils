'''Brittany Hermann''' is a 2D illustrator, UI artist<ref name="HermannArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=naastika|name=Brittany Hermann|url=artwork/L3BvNv|text=Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes - Gear Icons|date=2021-01-13}}</ref> and texture artist<ref name="HermannForums2">{{EA|subdomain=forums|url=discussions/swgoh-strategy-and-tips-en/everything-you-never-needed-to-know-about-gear/4039897/replies/4039912|text=Reply|author=[[Brittany Hermann]]|date=2017-03-07}} {{C|second reply}}</ref> who worked on the [[2015]] mobile [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'', developed by [[EA Capital Games]].<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> Hermann designed the game's "Gear" icons, with additional help by<ref name="HermannForums1">{{EA|subdomain=forums|url=discussions/swgoh-strategy-and-tips-en/everything-you-never-needed-to-know-about-gear/4039897/replies/4039908|text=Reply|author=[[Brittany Hermann]]|date=2017-03-07}}</ref> by [[Paul Martin]]<ref name="Martin1">{{ArtStation|username=morepaul|name=[[Paul Martin]]|url=artwork/8wwOxx|text=Star Wars Galaxy of Heroes - Gear Icons|date=2021-12-06}}</ref> and another artist,<ref name="HermannForums1" /> as well as various other UI items and textures, the concepts for most level environments in the initial game and designed some of the app icons.<ref name="HermannForums2" />
