'''Char-Ryl-Roy's lightsaber'''<ref name="Cataclysm" /> was a [[Color|green]]-bladed [[lightsaber]] wielded by [[Jedi Master]] [[Char-Ryl-Roy]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Convergence" /> After Roy's [[death]] during the [[Night of Sorrow]], his lightsaber was kept by his [[Padawan]] [[Enya Keen]] after she spoke with Jedi Master [[Yoda]] and he agreed with the idea.<ref name="Cataclysm" />
