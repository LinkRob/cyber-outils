'''Agricultural Hub 42''' was a [[factory]] located on the [[Rseik sector]] astronomical object [[Chortose]], ran by [[Chortose (species)|Chortose]] workers. The facility held a variety of [[plant]]s and utilized [[sprayer droid]]s. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Attack on Chortose|attacked]] Agricultural Hub 42 in order to attain its sprayer [[droid]]s. Two Chortose brothers, [[Pango]] and [[Gru (Chortose)|Gru]], were in the factory at the time; while Pango attempted to send a [[distress signal]] to the [[Jedi Order]] on [[Starlight Beacon]], Gru did not believe they would respond. As the Nihil entered the factory, the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Keeve Trennis]] intervened, defending the Chortose. Though Trennis herself was overwhelmed before she could defeat the marauders, the [[Jedi]] [[Wayseeker]] [[Orla Jareni]] arrived to assist her.<ref name="THR 7" />
