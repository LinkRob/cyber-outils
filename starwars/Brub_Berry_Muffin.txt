'''Brub Berry Muffins''' were a [[muffin]] served at [[Kat Saka's Kettle]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="KSK" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Made with the [[Endor]]an [[fruit]] [[brub berry]], the treat was suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="KSK" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brub Berry Muffins are available in the [[In-universe|real world]] to guests visiting Kat Saka's Kettle in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]]. They began being sold in early [[January]], [[2025]]. The menu describes it as being served "with Creamy Dipping Sauce."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/kat-sakas-kettle/menus|text=Disneyland: Kat Saka's Kettle Snack Menu}}</ref>
