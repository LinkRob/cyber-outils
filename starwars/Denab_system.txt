The '''Denab system''' was a [[star system]] that contained the [[planet]] [[Seatos]]. The ''[[Eye of Sion]]'', a [[starship]] constructed under the supervision of [[Morgan Elsbeth]] with the purpose of traveling to a separate [[galaxy]], was built above the planet.<ref name="Toil and Trouble" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Denab system first appeared in "[[Part Two: Toil and Trouble]],"<ref name="Toil and Trouble" /> of the ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]'' television series, which aired on [[August 22]], [[2023]].<ref name="New Release Date">{{Twitter|starwars|status/1692552080764887366|Star Wars|quote=.Thank you to all the fans who celebrated with us at our @AhsokaOfficial fan events around the world last night! We're excited to announce that new episodes of #Ahsoka will now launch Tuesdays at 6PM PT, starting with our two-episode premiere on August 22, only on @DisneyPlus.|archivedate=20230818152836}}</ref> The [[Denab system/Legends|Denab system]] was originally first identified in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', a reference book authored by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Daniel Wallace]], and published on [[August 18]], [[2009]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
