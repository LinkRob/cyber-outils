'''Carticus''' was an individual who tutored [[Sicarus]] during his time as a member of the [[Brotherhood of the Ninth Door]] [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] organization. Sicarus, since becoming a member of [[Baron]] [[Boolan]]'s [[Children of the Storm]], wondered what Carticus would have thought of him while waiting to [[Hunting|hunt]] the former [[Jedi Master]] [[Cohmac Vitus]] on the [[planet]] [[Thall]]. He felt that Carticus would be shocked and envious of his power and freedom, though soon tried to force himself to stop recalling the memories.<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" />
