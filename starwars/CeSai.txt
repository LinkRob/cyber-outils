'''CeSai''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Force Collector" /> located in the [[Illisurevimurasi sector]]'s [[CeSai system]]. It specifically lay in the [[Standard Galactic Grid]] space Q-4 of the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Appendix" /> which was also a part of the [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> At one point, CeSai was controlled by the [[Galactic Empire]] and benefitted from the Imperial presence. However, after the Empire was [[Galactic Civil War|forced out]] by the [[New Republic]], CeSai was left without leadership, and the planet fell into disarray. The wealthy [[Raynshi family]] called CeSai home for some time during the [[New Republic Era]] until [[Vroc Raynshi]], an [[First Order officer|officer]] of the [[First Order]], moved the family to the planet [[Merokia]]<ref name="Force Collector" /> in [[32 ABY]]<ref name="Year">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Force Collector]]'' to [[32 ABY]].</ref> on account of his job. Due to the move, [[Maize Raynshi]], the half-[[Mirialan]], half-[[human]] [[hybrid]] [[Gender|daughter]] of Vroc and [[Anaya Raynshi]], was forced to leave her friends from CeSai behind, leaving her lonely and upset.<ref name="Force Collector" />
