'''''Bounty Hunters'' 11''' is the eleventh issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. It was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]] and [[Arif Prianto]], and first published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[April 14]], [[2021]]. It features the story "'''The Great Hunt of Malastare'''," which follows [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] on a solo mission.

==Publisher's summary==
''THE GREAT HUNT OF MALASTARE! BOSSK awakens to find himself as the hunted instead of the hunter on the forest planet of MALASTARE. But who is the mysterious figure behind the hunting party stalking the infamous TRANDOSHAN? A special solo adventure starring the most savage bounty hunter of all!''
