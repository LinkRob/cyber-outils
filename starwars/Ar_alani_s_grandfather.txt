A [[Gender|male]] member of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Ruling Families|ruling]] [[Irizi family]] was the '''grandfather''' of [[Chiss]] [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] of the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]]. They were an avid [[collector]] of art.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> Before [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' take place in [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. As this event took place in the flashbacks of the novel, it must have taken place before 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> while on the Ascendancy [[capital]] [[planet]] of [[Csilla]], then&ndash;[[Senior Captain]] Ar'alani, known as Irizi'ar'alani, [[core name]] "Ziara," invited [[Senior Commander]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Mitth'raw'nuru]], core name "Thrawn," to the [[Irizi family homestead]]. The commander initially declined the invite but then accepted it after Ziara mentioned that her grandfather was a collector of art, which Thrawn took interest in.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ar'alani's grandfather was mentioned in flashbacks of the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
