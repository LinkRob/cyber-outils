'''Black Rock Passage''' was an area of [[Realspace|space]] located in the [[Akkadese Maelstrom]],<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> a region of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="OuterRim" /> and [[the Slice]]<ref name="Slice" /> consisting of ionized [[gas]], dust [[cloud]]s, [[water]] vapor, [[ammonia]],<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> and [[planet]]-sized<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> [[carbonberg]]s as well as [[gravity well]]s. [[Starship]] [[pilot]]s leaving the planet [[Kessel]], which was situated inside the Akkadese Maelstrom, sometimes attempted to find shortcuts to decrease the travel [[time]] needed to exit the Maelstrom that would otherwise be spent on the meandering<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> official<ref name="SoloGuide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> [[hyperspace route]] known as [[the Channel]].<ref name="Vandor">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]''</ref> Black Rock Passage was among several such potential alternate routes, with [[New Republic]] [[Law enforcement agency|Police]] [[Commissioner]] [[Exantor Divo]] characterizing the pilots risking travel through there as either "foolhardy" or "insane" in his<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> [[34 ABY]]<ref name="ScumAndVillainyDate">{{ScumAndVillainyDate}}</ref> [[book]] ''[[Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Behind the scenes==
Black Rock Passage was mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', authored by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />
