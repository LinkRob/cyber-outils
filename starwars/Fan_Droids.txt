"'''Fan Droids'''" is the seventh episode of the animated shorts series ''[[Star Wars Blips]]''. It was first released [[September 1]]. [[2017]] during the [[Force Friday]] on the official Star Wars app.<ref name="FtF" /> Two days later it was released on YouTube.<ref name="Star Wars Blips">{{SWYouTube|series=Star Wars Blips|text=Fan Droids|video=osXqwvaUCsc}}</ref>

==Plot summary==
The episode opens with [[BB-8]] being manhandled by two [[B2-series super battle droid]]s. However, [[R2-D2]] uses one of his appendages to pierce the two battle droids' chassis, causing oil to leak out. He then ignites the oil, setting the two droids aflame. Later, R2-D2 sprays several [[stormtrooper]]s with a smoke canister. The camera then turns to the exploding [[Death Star]] before zooming in on R2-D2 riding in [[Luke Skywalker]]'s [[X-wing starfighter]].

In the present, BB-8 uses a [[holoprojector]] to recount R2-D2's life story to several other droids including [[BB-8]], the blue [[BB-series astromech droid]] [[2BB-2]], the [[GNK-series power droid]] [[4B-EG-6]], the [[R4 astromech droid]] [[M9-G8]], and the tan-plated BB-series droid [[BB-4]]. As the droids squabble in [[Binary]], R2-D2 passes by. The droids react with excitement, with 2BB-2 short-circuiting in the process.
