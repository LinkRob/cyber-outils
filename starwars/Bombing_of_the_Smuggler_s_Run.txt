The '''Bombing of the Smuggler's Run''' occurred in [[17 ABY/Legends|17 ABY]] when [[Kueller]] detonated the bombs hidden in droids bound for [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. As all the droids there had been deactivated, the only ones that blew up were the ones on [[Smuggler's Run/Legends|Smuggler's Run]], that were going to be smuggled there. The bombing cause damage to [[Skip 1]] and killed many smugglers there. [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] speculated that similar things had happened in other Skips on the Run.
