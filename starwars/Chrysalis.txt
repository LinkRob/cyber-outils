'''Chrysalis''' was a ritual of the [[Sinsaran]]s from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Sinsara]]. Those who underwent the ordeal, emerged with superior fighting skills and an altered their visual appearance, making them seem as if they were from a different [[species]]. The ritual was highly dangerous, with the vast majority of the few that underwent it [[Death|dying]]. The Chrysalis was seen as a rite of passage by the Sinsarans. When asked by [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] about ways that individuals could change their appearance or if she could identify the species of his [[assassin]] [[Corlis Rath]], [[Jedi librarian]] [[Jocasta Nu]] suspected that he was a Sinsaran who had undergone the Chrysalis.<ref name="Jedi Knights 9">[[Jedi Knights 9|''Jedi Knights'' 9]]</ref>
