'''Codo'''<ref name="Rogue One Novel" /> was [[human]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Rebel Rising" /> who was a member of [[Saw Gerrera]]'s rebel cell, the [[Partisans]], during [[Jyn Erso]]'s [[time]] in the cell before being abandoned by Gerrera<ref name="Rogue One Novel" /> in [[5 BBY]].<ref name="UVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> Codo taught Jyn how to swim but would not talk to Jyn after being refused when he tried to kiss her. After being brought back to Gerrera's base in the [[Catacombs of Cadera]] following the [[Battle on Jedha]], Jyn asked a human female about Codo, to which the women responded by putting an imaginary [[blaster]] to her head and pulled the trigger, conveying that he had [[Death|died]].<ref name="Rogue One Novel" />
