The '''Batuu Wilds''' was an undeveloped region of the planet [[Batuu]]. It was composed of giant spires and rugged, rocky canyons. Some points of interest included the [[Three Suns Overlook]], the [[Dead Root Refuge]] and the [[Hissiq Springs]].<ref name="Tales" />
