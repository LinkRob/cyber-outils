'''Diet Coke''' was a beverage found at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]].<ref name="DPB Coke" /> It came in spherical "orb" bottles composed primarily of a silver color with a red line down the middle as well as white font inside the red, with its brand name printed on the side in stylized [[Aurebesh]]. It was stored in red-colored box-shaped containers filled with [[Water|ice]] to maintain a low temperature.<ref name="Coke website">{{WebCite|url=https://www.coca-colacompany.com/stories/coca-cola-and-disney-designed-these-galactic-bottles-for-star-wars-galaxy-s-edge|text=Coca-Cola and Disney Designed These Galactic Bottles for Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|author=Moye, Jay|date=2019-04-13|work=[https://coca-colacompany.com Coca-Cola Journey]|archivedate=20190620211503}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Diet Coke is a product based on [[Wikipedia:Diet Coke|the real-life sugar-free and zero-calorie soft fountain drink of the same name]] made by [[Wikipedia:The Coca-Cola Company|the Coca-Cola Company]]. Specialized spherical containers for the products were created specifically for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]].<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/6-things-we-learned-at-the-star-wars-galaxys-edge-panel|text=SWCC 2019: 6 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge Panel}}</ref>
[[File:DietCokeCart.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A Jat Kaa's Coolers vehicle delivers Diet Coke near Black Spire Ruins.]]
The idea originated from a tour conducted by the Walt Disney Imagineering members at the Coke Archives, in particular the Spencerian script used for the various products' logos. Matt Cooper, an associate industrial designer at Coke, mentioned that the resulting project challenged his design team to "take their brands places they've never taken them to," namely the [[Batuu]] setting. They intended to design the bottles to look as if they were pulled off a ship or left behind at Batuu and then repurposed, all while keeping to the aesthetics of the ''Star Wars'' franchise. This is not the first time Coca-Cola has partnered with Disney for one of its products. Coca-Cola was present in aiding Disney since the opening of [[Disneyland]] in [[1950s|1955]].<ref name="Coke website" />

A bottle of Diet Coke appears as an [[Easter egg (virtual)|easter egg]] in the [[2020]] web series ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Temple Challenge]]'', a children's gameshow set in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[The galaxy|galaxy]]. The bottle can be seen during a non-[[canon]] competition portion of each episode, appearing near the Bridge of Balance, an obstacle in the show's Jedi Temple set.<ref name="Easter Eggs">{{SW|url=news/20-jedi-temple-challenge-easter-eggs|text=20 ''Star Wars: Jedi Temple Challenge'' Easter Eggs You Probably Didn't Notice}}</ref>
