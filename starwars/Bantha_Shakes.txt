"'''Bantha Shakes'''" is the first short [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO]] original video in a [[LEGO Star Wars: Microfighters (video series)|series]] featuring the line of Microfighters. It was uploaded on [[February 19]], [[2014]].<ref name="Episode 1" />

{{Media-stub}}

==Plot summary==
[[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] is on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]], where he is taunted by an [[Imperial Navy crewman|Imperial crewman]] after crashing into him at the [[Cantina/Legends|Mos Eisley Diner]]. They join the [[Boonta Eve Classic/Legends|Boonta Eve Classic]], and when the officer crashes his Star Destroyer and is arrested, Solo goes on to win.

==Continuity==
It was originally released on [[January 26]], 2014 as the ''LEGO Star Wars: Microfighters'' animation, a sort of trailer for [[LEGO Star Wars: Microfighters (video game)|''LEGO Star Wars: Microfighters'']] mobile game.

It features two of the fangirls from ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Out]]''.
