The '''Endor Commando''' was a set of [[armor]] worn by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Aran Tal]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Clan Tal Warrior|standard costume]]. This [[Color|white]] and light gray [[Mandalorian armor|armor]] featured a red [[Rebel Alliance starbird]] on the shoulder pad.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Endor Commando was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Aran Tal, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 3.0.0 alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Frost MT-97]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing the Frosty Aran Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
