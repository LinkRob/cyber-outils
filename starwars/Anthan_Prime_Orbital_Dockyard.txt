The '''Anthan Prime Orbital Dockyard''' was a [[space station]] in [[orbit]] of the [[planet]] [[Anthan Prime]]. A few [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]], at least one [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] and at least four [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruisers]] were present at the station, along with several [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s.<ref name="Shadows and Secrets, Part III" />
