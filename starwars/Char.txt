"'''Char'''" was the nickname of a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], nicknamed as such by the [[pilot]]s of the [[New Republic]] due to the [[carbon scoring]] on their [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]]. Char took part on the attack in [[Oridol Cluster]] and died trying to help the rebel pilot [[Wyl Lark]] to save the planet [[Pandem Nai]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
