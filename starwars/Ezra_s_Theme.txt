"'''Ezra's Theme'''" is the musical theme in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' television show that is used to represent the character [[Ezra Bridger]]. It was composed by [[Kevin Kiner]], along with all the music for the show, and was released by [[Walt Disney Records]] for download as the fifth track on the [[Star Wars Rebels: Season One (Original Soundtrack)|soundtrack]] for ''[[Star Wars Rebels Season One]]'' on [[September 16]], [[2016]].
