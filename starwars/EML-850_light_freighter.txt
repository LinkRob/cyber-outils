The '''EML-850 light freighter''' was a type of [[light freighter]]. The high material [[Galactic Credit Standard|cost]] of the [[freighter]] thwarted any chance of mass production. The ''[[Trailblazer]]'' was an EML-850 light freighter designed in the [[Republic Era]].

==History==
{{Quote|Do you know what happened to these ships? Destroyed. Repurposed. Scrapped.|Sliro Barsha, to Vail Tormin|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
[[File:TrailblazerDogfightPromo-Outlaws.png|thumb|235px|The ''Trailblazer'' being pursued by TIE fighters]]
The EML-850 [[light freighter]]<ref name="Forward" /> was designed and produced by the [[Barsha family]] of [[Corellian]] industrialists<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="Forward" /> It was designed to be equally well-[[armor]]ed and cargo-capable, making it ideal for [[smuggler]]s and other criminals,<ref name="Outlaws" /> however its high [[Galactic Credit Standard|cost]] resulted in mass production being abandoned<ref name="Gameplay Easter Eggs">{{UbisoftYouTube|490LVhVGAqo|Star Wars Outlaws Official Gameplay: All the Star Wars Lore, Details & Easter Eggs &#124; Ubisoft Forward}}</ref> in favor of other models. Most of the remaining [[freighter]]s were scrapped, with the exception of the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', which was used by [[Sliro Barsha]] as his personal [[starship]]. The ''Trailblazer'' was later stolen from Barsha's mansion [[Westhill Palace]] by [[Canto Bight]] thief [[Kay Vess]], who used it as her own. Although Sliro lost the Trailblazer definitively to Vess, the starship was previously stolen by [[Jet Kordo]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|But they were expensive. Durasteel plating alone cost a fortune.|Sliro Barsha|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
EML-850s were light and fast single-[[pilot]] [[freighter]]s. They had an exterior plating of [[durasteel]] which made the craft durable but very expensive.<ref name="Outlaws" /> They were also equipped with a [[hyperdrive]], [[deflector shield]]s, [[laser cannon]]s<ref name="Forward" />, and a cargo bay. The middle hallway connecting the cargo hold to the living quarters and bridge contains a small washroom and [[toilet]]. It used standard [[Fuel|Starship fuel]] for maintaining stable and reliable sublight engine though could be modified to utilize special engine fuels.<ref name="Outlaws" />
