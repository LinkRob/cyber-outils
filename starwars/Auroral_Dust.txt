'''Auroral Dust''' was a substance that was available in [[Hadd's Powder Lounge]] on the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> An advert was made by a [[droid]] over the [[cruiser]]'s [[speaker]] system notifying passengers that complimentary Auroral Dust was available in the lounge during happy [[Standard hour|hour]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Auroral Dust was mentioned in the "Wild Card" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[November 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Wild Card released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/1Q4y5lMJwdUUt6qXzbWKPI/star-wars-outlaws-game-update-showcase-wrapup|text=Star Wars Outlaws Game Update Showcase Wrap-Up}}</ref>
