The '''''Black Hole''''' was a cantina in [[Doom City]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].<ref name="CB">{{CasusBelliCite|HS24|Il faut sauver Miss Pickalho}}</ref>

Considered the best of the district's many food-and-drink establishments, it was popular with smugglers, mercenaries and bounty hunters, including [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]].<ref name="CB" />

The cantina offered its clientel a discreet meeting-place for discussions, but the security was much heavier than other comparable establishments such as the notorious [[Mos Eisley Cantina/Legends|Mos Eisley Cantina]] on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]], and its bar prices were at least three times higher than normal.<ref name="CB" />

{{stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Black Hole'' and Doom City appear in ''[[Il faut sauver Miss Pickalho]]'', a French-language scenario for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' which appeared in ''[[Casus Belli]]'' magazine in March 1999. As this material was not approved through the [[Lucas Licensing]] process, it is considered non-canon..

In French, the cantina is called the ''Trou Noir'', a term which has the same double meaning as in English, referring to both a dark pit and the astrophysical phenomenon.<ref name="CB" />
