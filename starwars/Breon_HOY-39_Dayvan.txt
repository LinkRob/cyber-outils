The '''Breon HOY-39 Dayvan''',<ref name="SecondEdition">[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles (second edition)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles'' (second edition)]]</ref> also called the '''Breon Dayvan''',<ref name="Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/andor/andor-season-1-episode-1-episode-guide|text="Kassa" Episode Guide &#124; Andor}} {{1stID|Breon Dayvan}}</ref> was a [[Dayvan]] [[starship]]<ref name="2024 Encyclopedia" />. One such vessel was in [[Zorby's Western Shiplot]] and borrowed by [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] to travel to [[Morlana One]] in search of his sister, [[Kerri]]. He later changed the ship’s [[ID chip log]] before his friend [[Pegla]] caught him.<ref name="Kassa">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> Following the [[Ferrix Riot]], the ship was piloted by [[Jezzi]] to evacuate herself, [[Bix Caleen]], [[B2EMO]], [[Brasso]], and [[Wilmon Paak]] to safety away from Ferrix.<ref name="Rix Road">{{Andor|Rix Road (episode)}}</ref>

One year later, this ship could be found on [[Yavin 4|Yavin 4.]]<ref> ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' — "[[One Year Later]]"</ref>
