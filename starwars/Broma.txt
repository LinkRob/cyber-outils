'''Broma''' was a type of spiky [[flower]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Yaddle]] kept [[plant]]s from across [[the galaxy]], including broma, in her [[Master Yaddle's living quarters|quarters]] in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Wayseeker" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Wayseeker" /> Around [[160 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{WayseekerDate}}</ref> Jedi Master [[Vernestra Rwoh]] met with Yaddle in her quarters and noticed the broma flowers and other plants.<ref name="Wayseeker" />

==Behind the scenes==
