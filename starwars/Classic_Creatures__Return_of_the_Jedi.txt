'''''Classic Creatures: Return of the Jedi''''' is a documentary on the making of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], originally aired on [[CBS]] [[November 21]]st, [[1983]].<ref name="BT 35" /> Behind-the-scenes footage is interspersed with clips and information from classic monster movies. It was hosted and narrated by [[Billy Dee Williams]] and [[Carrie Fisher]].

==Release and reception==
After the initial broadcast of the special, it was first rebroadcast on CBS [[May 19]]th, [[1984]].<ref name="eBay Photo">{{WebCite|url=https://www.ebay.com/itm/362186584367|text=1983 Star Wars Creatures "Behind the Scenes on Return of the Jedi" Rare Photo|work=eBay|archivedate=20260317174038}}</ref> Early home video releases for the documentary included a [[1985]] VHS release from [[Wikipedia:Playhouse Video|Playhouse Video]]<ref name="Butler YouTube">{{YouTube|video=0fcmfXNJApE|text=From the Star Wars Home Video Library #89: Classic Creatures: Return of the Jedi|channel=@ChronoRadio|name=Nathan Butler|archivedate=20250814203609}}</ref>, and a [[1986]] Laserdisc sold in Japan by [[Wikipedia:VAP, Inc.|VAP]].<ref name="Hollywood Laserdisc">{{WebCite|url=https://hollywoodlaserdisc.com/products/star-wars-classic-creatures-return-of-the-jedi|text=Star Wars: Return of the Jedi- Classic Creatures (1983) CLV|work=Hollywood Laserdisc|archivedate=20251014201009}}</ref>

[[Wikipedia:20th Century Home Entertainment|CBS/Fox Video]] released the special on VHS in [[1991]]<ref name="eBay VHS">{{WebCite|url=https://www.ebay.co.uk/itm/365225143526?customid=&toolid=10050|text=STAR WARS - Classic Creatures Return Of The Jedi - VHS Video|work=eBay|archivedate=20260317144428}}</ref> and [[1994]].<ref name="eBid VHS">{{WebCite|url=https://www.ebid.net/uk/for-sale/5013738147157-sale-star-wars-classic-creatures-vhs-video-vintage-1993-cbs-fox-empire-jedi-dvd-210308712.htm|text=Star Wars CLASSIC CREATURES VHS VIDEO Vintage 1993 CBS Fox Empire Jedi DVD|work=eBid.com|archivedate=20260317144802}}</ref> It was then reissued on its own on Laserdisc in Japan in [[1995]],<ref name="eBay Laserdisc">{{WebCite|url=https://www.ebay.com/itm/386806480842|text=Classic Creatures: Return Of The Jedi (Laserdisc, 1995) Japan Pilf-2081 NEW Obi|work=eBay|archiveurl=https://archive.is/sXPw8}}</ref> and then as a triple feature with ''The Making of Star Wars'' and [[1980]]'s ''[[SP FX: The Empire Strikes Back]]'' under the title ''The Making of Trilogy.''<ref name="SoV Japan Laserdisc Making Trilogy">{{WebCite|url=https://swonvideo.com/laserdisc/vldswtrilogyjapanpilf2071bonus.htm|text=The Making of Trilogy - Laserdisc 1995|work=Star Wars on Video|archivedate=20241112064653}}</ref> It was also included in ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]'',<ref name="Complete Saga">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]''</ref> a Blu-ray box set released in [[2011]].<ref name="Complete Saga release">{{SWArchive|url=news/starwars_blu-ray.html|text=The Star Wars Saga on Blu-Ray}}</ref>

The initial airing of the special was the 34th ranked show in the US broadcast network television ratings for that week.<ref name="Newspapers.com">{{WebCite|url=http://newspapers.com/image/189755546/|text=Star Tribune - Thu, Dec 01, 1983 - Page 43|work=Newspapers.com|archivedate=20260317180954}}</ref>
