In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''crew''' was formed by the [[Hidden Hand]] criminal syndicate to [[Hunt for Vader|kill]] the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Assembled by the syndicate's leader, [[Gwi]], the crew consisted of multiple [[bounty hunter]]s, namely [[Dengar]], [[Chio Fain]], [[Honnah]], and [[Beilert Valance]], and the [[sniper]] [[Gita|Urrr'k]],<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> secretly an undercover [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]<ref name="Target Vader 5">[[Target Vader 5|''Target Vader'' 5]]</ref> agent named Gita.<ref name="Target Vader 4" /> Prior to the missions start, the team had included the [[Hutt Clan]] [[spy]] [[Arr-Nine-Nineteen]], who had posed as a [[Jedi hunter]] before he was exposed and killed by Valance.<ref name="Target Vader 1" />
