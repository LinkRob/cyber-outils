The '''Beltire system''' was located in the [[Narrant sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="TimelinesMap" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="WesternReaches" /> It was situated in grid square J-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]] and lay on<ref name="TimelinesMap" /> the [[Cerean Reach]]<ref name="CereanReach" /> [[hyperspace route]] that connected it to the astronomical objects [[Riflor]] and the [[Entropian Hive]].<ref name="TimelinesMap" /> The [[Star system|system]] contained the location of the [[Beltire|same name]].<ref name="Timelines" />

In [[4 ABY]], after the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Beltire Liberation|liberated]] Beltire.<ref name="Timelines" /> [[9 ABY|Five years]] later,<ref name="MandoVGDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> the Beltire system fell within the borders of the Alliance's successor government, the [[New Republic]].<ref name="MandoVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Beltire system was first mentioned on a [[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|map]]<ref name="Timelines" /> designed by [[Christopher Ibbitt]]<ref name="Fry email">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-31.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-31}}</ref> that was created for the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' and given out as a promotional poster at [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|Celebration Anaheim]]<ref name="TimelinesSWcom">{{SW|url=news/swca-2022-lucasfilm-publishing-behind-the-page-panel|text=SWCA 2022: 7 Things We Learned from the Lucasfilm Publishing Behind the Page Panel}}</ref> on [[May 27]], [[2022]]<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|dkpublishing|status/1530292772598534145|[[Dorling Kindersley|DK Books US]]|date=2022-05-27|quote=Just announced at #StarWarsCelebration! Get your exclusive map - only at booth #2525.|archivedate=20220527210041}}</ref> prior to the book's release during [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]]<ref name="Celebration">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-europe-2023-exclusives|text=Check Out ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Exclusives from Hasbro, the LEGO Group, and More}}</ref> of [[April 7]] through [[April 10|10]], [[2023]].<ref name="CelebrationSchedule">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-panel-schedule|text=Plan Ahead with the ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Panel Schedule}}</ref> It was then identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] article on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025.
