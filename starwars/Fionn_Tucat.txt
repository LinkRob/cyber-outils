'''Fionn Tucat''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Scout|explorer]]. Along with her associate, [[Romina Foss]], and a hired crew, Tucat led an [[Mission to Lupal|expedition]] to the abandoned [[moon]] of [[Lupal]], despite the perilous results of all previous explorers who visited the moon. The warnings proved to be correct when the [[Color|red]] light emitted by the solar flares of Lupal's [[Unidentified star (Lupal)|star]] turned the [[Shistavanen]] [[Kordus Vrak]] into a feral creature and drove him to [[Death|kill]] the other crew members. While attempting to escape with Foss, Tucat was attacked and killed by Vrak.<ref name="Blood Moon" />
