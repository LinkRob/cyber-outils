The '''Commission for the Preservation of the New Order''' ('''COMPNOR''') was a government agency responsible for the promotion of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[New Order]] ideology. It was originally formed as the [[Commission for the Protection of the Republic]] (COMPOR), a populist movement that supported the [[Galactic Republic]] until [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|became]] the [[Galactic Emperor]] by the end of the [[Clone Wars]] in [[19 BBY]].

Over the course of Palpatine's [[Imperial Era|reign]], COMPNOR served as a [[Politics|political]] tool for the dissemination of state-sponsored [[propaganda]] throughout the Empire. In addition, COMPNOR was also an umbrella organization that consisted of multiple sub-divisions, such as the [[Coalition for Progress]] and the [[Sub-Adult Group]] (SAGroup). 

Following the Emperor's [[death]] at the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]], COMPNOR was reorganized into the [[Office of Imperial Promotion, Galactic Truth, and Fact Correction]], in an effort to countermand the influence of the [[New Republic]].

==History==
===Background===
{{Quote|Yes, Count Dooku succeeded in using the HoloNet for Separatist propaganda purposes, but Republic forces were quick to shut down those Shadowfeeds. If memory serves, COMPOR itself was established as a result of the navy's actions at the time.|[[Dodd Rancit]]|<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>}}
[[File:Boys_in_white.jpg|thumb|left|200px|COMPOR used propaganda to support the Galactic Republic in the Clone Wars.]]
The Commission for the Preservation of the [[New Order]] (COMPNOR) started as the [[Commission for the Protection of the Republic]] (COMPOR), a populist movement composed of [[loyalist]]s during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Propaganda" />

===Establishment===
When in [[19 BBY]], [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], then [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], [[Proclamation of the New Order|proclaimed]] himself [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] of the [[Galactic Empire|First Galactic Empire]], one of Palpatine's first acts was to reshuffle the [[Politics|political]] apparatus, reforming the Republic-era COMPOR into COMPNOR. This new division of government was wielded as a political tool for newly appointed Imperial [[advisor]]s with a gift for shaping public sentiment.<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref>

===Imperial Era===
[[File:COMPNOR_Recruitment-SW_Propaganda.jpg|thumb|right|180px|COMPNOR recruitment poster ''[[COMPNOR Recruitment]]'' by [[Dasita Lyros]].]]
In the first few [[Standard year|years]] of the Galactic Empire, civic pride boomed. COMPNOR capitalized on the successful [[propaganda]] campaigns of the Galactic Republic to accelerate the vast military expansion decreed by the Emperor. A series of sub-commissions began blueprinting the image of the ideal Imperial citizen and what role he or she would play in the greater glory of the Empire.<ref name="Propaganda" /> Various propaganda [[Painting|posters]] and patriotic ploys were designed to achieve these ends.<ref name="VG">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>

By [[14 BBY]], COMPNOR was on the rise in the Imperial hegemony. That year, [[Armand Isard]] was made [[director]] of the agency.<ref name="Tarkin" />

===Dissolution===
{{Main|Office of Imperial Promotion, Galactic Truth, and Fact Correction}}
In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Life Debt date" /> [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], the ashes of COMPNOR were reorganized into the [[Office of Imperial Promotion, Galactic Truth, and Fact Correction]]. This new [[propaganda]] service role was to countermand the growing influence of the [[New Republic]] in the [[Standard month|months]] following the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Aftermath LD" />

==Ideology==
{{Main|New Order}}
COMPNOR was the largest entity of the Imperial bureaucracy, and influenced many aspects of Imperial life, including the [[Art Group|arts]], [[science]]s, [[Youngling|youth]] education, [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]], and even [[Imperial Military]] functions.<ref name="Dawn" /> COMPNOR supported the state-sponsored political religion of the New Order, a set of ideals embodied within Imperial society dedicated to order, control, and the rule of law.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> COMPNOR supported civic pride, militarism, and unyielding patriotism to the Empire. It criticized the Galactic Republic as lachrymose, corrupt, and ignorant to the demands of its populace, flaws with which it purported the Empire had resolved. As such, the Empire represented the pinnacle of a thousand years of galactic culture and sophistication, refined and molded into a simpler, yet purer form.<ref name="Propaganda" />

COMPNOR supported the state-sponsored [[xenophobia]] and humanocentric policies rampant within the Empire. This was justified by COMPNOR as a natural extension of the human right to rule, exemplified by COMPNOR's teachings of human accomplishments throughout galactic history while minimizing the influences of alien species. To the Empire, aliens were "foreign" and "strange," and were portrayed as dangerous animals who were willing to lie, cheat, and manipulate others to get their way. Anti-alien xenophobia was so strong that even near-human species exhibited inherent distrust of their alien neighbors.<ref name="Aftermath LD" /><ref name="Propaganda" />

==Organization==
===Overview===
COMPNOR was led by the [[Select Committee]]<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> which was commanded by a chairman and a vice chairman. Each organization underneath the arm of COMPNOR had its own directors, who either reported to each other's agencies or branches of the Imperial Military.<ref name="Propaganda" />

As the [[Imperial Military]] underwent considerable growth, the civilian trunk of government followed the carefully crafted template of COMPNOR.<ref name="Propaganda" /> All divisions of the Commission were housed in the [[COMPNOR arcology]], a massive building located in the [[Federal District]] of [[Coruscant]], in the vicinity of the [[Imperial Palace]] and the [[Imperial Inspectorate HQ|Naval Intelligence headquarters]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

===Coalition for Progress===
{{Main|Coalition for Progress}}
Sub-bureau of COMPNOR, responsible for monitoring the cultural achievements of the Empire and overseeing the operations of its numerous sub-divisions.<ref name="Propaganda" />

===Coalition for Improvements===
{{Main|Coalition for Improvements}}
Sub-bureau of COMPNOR tasked with restructuring existing Republic administrative systems into an approved Imperial standard, including transferring officials in desirable positions and relocating troublesome populations.<ref name="Rise" />

===Sub-Adult Group===
{{Main|Sub-Adult Group}}
The Sub-Adult Group (abbreviated to SAGroup) was the branch responsible for monitoring and organizing the education, recreation and recruitment of Imperial youth, including drafting approved school curricula, sporting activities and pre-military training.<ref name="Rise" />

===Imperial Security Bureau===
{{Main|Imperial Security Bureau}}
Imperial internal intelligence-gathering, enforcement, and anti-insurrectionist division tasked with ensuring morale, loyalty, and adherence to the New Order.<ref name="AbEverything" />

It had several sub-divisions including [[Surveillance (Imperial Security Bureau)|Surveillance]], [[Interrogation (Imperial Security Bureau)|Interrogation]], [[Re-Education]], [[Enforcement (Imperial Security Bureau)|Enforcement]], [[Investigations]], [[Internal Affairs]],<ref name="AbEverything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> and [[Special Operations Division|Special Operations]].<ref name="Commander Echoes">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'', Rebel Campaign: ''Echoes of the Force''</ref> It was also responsible for the secrecy of the [[Imperial Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research]] agency.<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

===CompForce===
{{Main|CompForce}}
COMPNOR's military branch, tasked with enforcing their policies around the galaxy.<ref name="Rise" />

It has 2 sub-divisions which are Assault and Observation.
