The '''Duinuogwuin''' were a group who were [[The galaxy|galactic]] neighbors of the expanding [[Galactic Republic]] by the [[time]] of the [[High Republic Era]]. By hosting a [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]<ref name="TRS" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] hoped that the event's showcase of the Republic's offerings&mdash;along with her other [[Great Works]], such as the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]]&mdash;would allow for the government to build bridges with nearby groups such as the Duinuogwuin. After a [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] planning meeting for the [[Republic Fair|fair]], Soh expressed her opinion about the Duinuogwuin to her [[Vice Chair|Vice Chancellor]], [[Larep Reza]].<ref name="TRS" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Star_Wing.jpg|thumb|right|230px|A Duinuogwuin, as depicted in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity]]
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Duinuogwuin were mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="TRS" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's Phase I.<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Duinuogwuin/Legends|Duinuogwuin]] were first indirectly mentioned in "[[Dread Discovery!]]," the third installment of the "[[The Keeper's World]]" serialized ''Star Wars'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story that was written by [[Roy Thomas]] and published in the [[Pizzazz 3|third issue]] of the ''[[Pizzazz]]'' magazine in [[December]] [[1977]].<ref name="Pizzazz 3">{{StoryCite|story=Dread Discovery!|book=Pizzazz 3}}</ref>

They were first described as a [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Species/Legends|species]] known as the "Star Dragons" in the [[1989]] ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' supplement ''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]'', written by [[Troy Denning]].<ref name="GG4">''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]''</ref> The Duinogwuin first appeared in a flashback scene in the [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure "[[The Most Dangerous Foe]]" by [[Angela Phillips]], which was published in the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 11|eleventh issue]] of ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'' in [[November]] [[1996]].<ref name="Most Dangerous Foe">{{JournalCite|11|The Most Dangerous Foe}}</ref>
