'''Carbon-freezing''' was an industrial process by which high-strength<ref name="The Manga, Vol 1">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian: The Manga]]''</ref> liquid [[carbonite]] was flash-frozen<ref name="SYWtBaJ?">''[[The Empire Strikes Back: So You Want to Be a Jedi?]]''</ref> via combination with high-pressure [[gas]]:<ref name="The Manga, Vol 1" /> that gas would then be encased within the carbonite<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|carbon-freezing|Carbon-freezing}}</ref> as it turned from a liquid to a solid state. The process was designed to encase,<ref name="SYWtBaJ?" /> preserve, and entrap otherwise unstable<ref name="The Manga, Vol 1" /> materials for transport,<ref name="SYWtBaJ?" /> such as gases,<ref name="Databank" /> in a strong metal alloy.<ref name="SYWtBaJ?" /> Highly pressurized gases such as [[tibanna]] were designed to be safely encased by the solidified carbonite<ref name="Databank" /><ref name="2021 Day-at-a-Time Calendar">[[Star Wars Day-at-a-Time Calendar 2021|''Star Wars'' Day-at-a-Time Calendar 2021]]</ref> for transport.<ref name="SYWtBaJ?" /> While it was not the original intention of carbon-freezing, encased materials could even include [[Sentience|sentient]] beings, should the individual in question survive the freezing process.<ref name="The Manga, Vol 1" />

==History==
[[File:StarWars2020-11-Sprouse-textless.png|250px|thumb|left|Lando Calrissian inspects Han Solo after he underwent carbon-freezing to ensure his friend survived.]]
Legend held that the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Siberus]] froze herself in carbonite.<ref name="Sleep of Ages">{{StoryCite|story=The Sleep of Ages|book=Dark Legends}}</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] came up with the idea to be frozen in carbonite to avoid being detected by the the [[battle droid]]'s scanners of [[Lola Sayu]]'s [[Lola Sayu defense fleet|blockade]] and the [[Citadel]], which ultimately led to the [[Battle of Lola Sayu]].<ref name="Citadel">{{TCW|The Citadel (episode)}}</ref> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Galactic Empire]] froze a giant [[kyber crystal]].<ref name="ItNotR">{{Rebels|In the Name of the Rebellion}}</ref>

On the order of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Vader, the [[Sith]] identity of Anakin Skywalker, [[Han Solo]] was frozen in carbonite on [[Cloud City]] in a crude<ref name="Episode VI" /> [[Class-3 CFC]] chamber<ref name="Fascinating Facts">''[[Star Wars: Fascinating Facts]]''</ref> as a test for how the chamber would handle [[human]] freezing. After Solo was frozen and survived, Vader attempted to freeze his son, the young [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]], but Skywalker escaped.<ref name="Episode VI" /> During a return [[mission to Cloud City]], Princess [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and several other residents of Cloud City under went carbon-freezing by the [[Imperial Garrison (Cloud City)|Imperial Garrison]] under Captain [[Tanch]].<ref name="SW 3">[[Star Wars (2020) 3|''Star Wars'' (2020) 3]]</ref><ref name="SW 4">[[Star Wars (2020) 4|''Star Wars'' (2020) 4]]</ref> Solo's freezing process resulted in [[hibernation sickness]], causing temporary blindness, when he was released<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> almost a year later.<ref name="PS">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref> At some point, Solo's carbonite block was nicknamed the "[[Hansicle]]."<ref name="Star Wars 50">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gamesradar.com/comics/marvel-comics/star-wars-50-series-finale-preview|text=Star Wars #50 ends the current era of Marvel's Star Wars comics with a confrontation between Luke Skywalker and Ben Solo|archivedate=20240905221125}}</ref>

[[Surat Nuat]] had an office desk made with the frozen block of his [[Rutar Nuat|brother]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> The bounty hunter, [[Din Djarin]], would carbon-freeze several of his bounties aboard his ship, the ''[[Razor Crest]]''.<ref name="The Mandalorian">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref>
