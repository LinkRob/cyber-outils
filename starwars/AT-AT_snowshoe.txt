'''AT-AT snowshoes''' were [[snowshoe]]s that resembled the [[Limb|feet]] of [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT]]<ref name="Article" /> [[walker]]s.<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> The snowshoes were round and had flat claws on three sides. Each snowshoe had a strap in the middle that went over the wearer's foot. Additionally, strings could be tied to holes in the snowshoes and strapped over the foot of the wearer. The middle of the snowshoes had a ridged base for the wearer's feet to be placed on.<ref name="Article" /> The former [[Sith]]<ref name="Ronin">''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]''</ref> known as [[the Ronin]] wore AT-AT snowshoes while traversing [[Water|snow]] on [[Mount Hakamori]] on an [[Unidentified astronomical object (Shogun)|astronomical object]].<ref name="Article" /> His [[astromech droid]], [[R5-D56]], wore a similar pair of snowshoes that lacked the claws on the front and back and had one straight side.<ref name="Article" />

==Behind the scenes==
AT-AT snowshoes were created for the [[Canon|non-canon]] one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Visions 1|''Visions'' 1]], written and illustrated by [[Takashi Okazaki]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 12]], [[2022]].<ref name="Article" /> However, they were not visible in the final comic.<ref name="Visions 1">[[Visions 1|''Visions'' 1]]</ref> Before the release of the comic, a [[StarWars.com]] article published on [[June 15]] of the same year revealed concept art of the snowshoes by Okazaki. They were identified in [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]] as AT-AT かんじき<ref name="Article" /> (AT-AT ''kanjiki''), meaning AT-AT snowshoe.<ref name="JishoKanjiki">{{WebCite|url=https://jisho.org/word/橇-1|text=橇-1|work=Jisho.org|archivedate=20221023164232}}</ref>
