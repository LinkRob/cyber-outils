'''Flood basalt''' was a type of [[basalt]] found on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]].<ref name="Dark Lord 21" /> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Darth Vader (2017) 21|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 21]] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[architect]] [[Colonel]] [[Alva Brenne]] came to Mustafar to design a [[Fortress Vader|personal base]] for the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. While surveying the planet's fiery terrain, she encountered flood basalt along Mustafar's [[Magma|lava]] flows, later instructing [[Lieutenant]] [[Roggo]] to run tests on the strength of the substance so that she might incorporate it into Vader's fortress.<ref name="Dark Lord 21" />
