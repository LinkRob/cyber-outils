The '''Bitthævrian''' were a group from<ref name="Falcon 65">{{BuildFalconCite|65|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> the [[Wild Space]]<ref name="SWGalaxy">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> [[planet]] [[Guiteica]]. [[Warlord]]s<ref name="Falcon 65" /> and [[Soldier|warriors]]<ref name="German Falcon 65">[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 65|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 65]] {{C|German version}}</ref> of the [[Kaleesh]] [[species]],<ref name="Falcon 65" /> native to the neighboring planet [[Kalee]],<ref name="Topps Star Wars Hyperspace">{{Topps|set=2024 Topps Star Wars Hyperspace|cardname=Roggwarts &ndash; Mustafar}}</ref> historically took the creatures known as [[roggwart]]s as trophies from the Bitthævrian [[homeworld]].<ref name="Falcon 65" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bitthævrian were first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 65|sixty-fifth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine, published in [[2016]].<ref name="Falcon 65" /> The [[Bitthævrian/Legends|Bitthævrian]] [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Species/Legends|species]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[Alliance Intelligence Reports]]'', sourcebook for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' published in [[1995]].<ref name="AIR">''[[Alliance Intelligence Reports]]''</ref>
