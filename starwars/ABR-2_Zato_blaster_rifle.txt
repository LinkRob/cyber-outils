The '''ABR-2 Zato blaster rifle''' was a model of [[blaster rifle]] built by the [[Ashiga Clan]] at their [[Ashiga weapons factory|weapons factory]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Kijimi]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> members of the clan carried the rifles while patrolling the planet. [[Queen]] [[Ashiga]] shifted the weapons factory from creating their blaster rifles to creating explosives for the [[Galactic Empire]] to use against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. This change caused strife within the clan which ultimately resulted in a coup attempt by the Queen's daughter, [[Krisk Ashiga]]. As part of the coup, Krisk hired [[Kay Vess]] for several jobs to assist the coup, during which Vess faced a number of members of the clan who were using the ABR-2 Zato blaster rifle.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
ABR-2 Zato blaster rifle first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
