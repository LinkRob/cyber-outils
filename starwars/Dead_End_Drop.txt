"'''Dead End Drop'''" is a short narrative that was written by [[Peter M. Schweighofer]] and published in the ''[[Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook]]''.

==Plot summary==
Around [[3 ABY/Legends|3 ABY]], [[Rixen]], an [[Alliance Intelligence/Legends|Alliance Intelligence]] spy, arrived at the [[Kuat Passenger Port]] after attempting to evade the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Spy/Legends|spy]] [[Do'naal]], who had been trailing her when she was at [[Byblos/Legends|Byblos]] earlier. At the port, she consulted a nondescript information [[Droid/Legends|droid]], [[L1V-IN]], who had been programmed by the Rebellion to serve as a drop point for information and secret messages between Alliance agents. Rixen gave the droid the code phrase needed to access the information, and the droid played back three messages for her. The first detailed the capture of Rebel agent [[Vewin]] by Imperial authorities [[Kelada/Legends|Kelada]], and the second informed Rixen that another agent, [[Stabris]], had fled to the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]] because of increased Imperial security in the Core Worlds and [[Colonies/Legends|Colonies]]. Upon hearing this, Rixen decided it would be best for her to flee the Core Worlds as well.

Rixen was surprised when the third message, from Do'naal, told her to surrender. Immediately after playing the message, she was surrounded by undercover [[Imperial Intelligence/Legends|Imperial Intelligence]] agents. Rixen attempted to detonate a device that would [[Death/Legends|kill]] both her and the agents surrounding her, but Do'naal was able to apprehend her first. She was taken away by [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]] to be held for interrogation by Do'naal in Imperial lock-up.
