'''Corde''', known as the '''Half''', was a [[bounty hunter]] who fought [[Cal Kestis]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] alongside [[PR-85T]]. She took pride in her precise aim and [[slicer]]'s intuition, a skill she used to upgrade PR-85T. She was further known for cannibalizing parts from [[droid]]s she considered lesser.<ref name="Survivor" />
