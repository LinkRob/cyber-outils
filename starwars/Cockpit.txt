The '''cockpit''' or '''control pod'''<ref name="Star Wars: Star Pilot (2015)">[[Star Wars: Star Pilot (2015)|''Star Wars: Star Pilot'']]</ref> was, usually, the primary center of control for various kinds of vehicles. [[Pilot]]s operated their vessels from cockpits, managing a variety of instruments that controlled the vehicle's speed, direction, and other operations. [[Anakin Skywalker]] was once ordered to stay in the cockpit of an [[N-1 starfighter]] during the [[Battle of Naboo|Battle of]] [[Naboo]] in order to stay out of harm's way. Skywalker inadvertently flew the craft into battle, however. Although cockpits were normally a part of a vessel's main body, [[podracer]] cockpits were tethered to the [[engine]]s they controlled via cables.<ref name="Episode 1">{{Film|I}}</ref> Some ground vehicles had an open cockpit, such as the [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]] (AT-RT) walker.<ref name="Ep3">{{Film|III}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[Pablo Hidalgo]] has noted "cockpit" as an example of real world term that is accepted amongst the ''[[Star Wars]]'' fandom as an in-universe term, effectively having been grandfathered into acceptance thanks to its common usage amongst ''Star Wars'' works.<ref name="Cockpit Pablo">{{Bluesky|username=infinata.bsky.social|url=post/3lmiisghqsk2n|name=[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2025-04-10|quote=Editing the Star Wars-iness of text is an idiosyncratic art and not a defined science. I balk at the use of “footage” due to its outdated terrestrial origins, but we’ve all given “cockpit” a pass because it’s so grandfathered in.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/a1m1v}}</ref>
