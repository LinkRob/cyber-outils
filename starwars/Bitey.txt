'''Bitey''' was the pet [[Castilon gorg]] of the merchants [[Flix]] and [[Orka]], so named because he tended to think people's fingers were food and bite them. The [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] acquired Bitey as food for Flix and Orka. However, the two merchants adopted him as their pet. Bitey later helped Kaz to stop the [[First Order]] from [[Theft of the phase connector|stealing]] a [[phase connector]].

==Biography==
===Finding a new home===
[[File:TradingBiteyForParts-TheRecruitPart2.png|thumb|250px|Bitey at the Office of Acquisition.]]
Bitey was one of the [[gorg]]s being sold by the [[Klatooinian]] merchant [[Bolza Grool]] aboard the ''[[Colossus]]'' during the [[New Republic era]]. The [[Resistance]] spy [[Kazuda Xiono]] had acquired the gorg as part of a deal he made with Flix and Orka to trade them lunch in exchange for used [[starship]] parts he could use to repair the ''[[Fireball]]'' for his [[Race between Kazuda Xiono and Torra Doza|race]] against [[Torra Doza]], by promising the vendor, Grool, to put his logo on the ship in exchange for the animal. Due to how long it took Xiono, Flix and Orka were already eating lunch, but took the gorg and kept the deal anyway.<ref name="The Recruit">{{Resistance|The Recruit}}</ref>

Instead of eating the gorg, Flix and Orka decided to keep him as a pet and gave him a name. Kaz and the [[BB-series astromech droid]] [[BB-8]] encountered Bitey while visiting the [[Office of Acquisitions]] to obtain a new [[CoMar Tri-Tracker chip]] for the ''Colossus''{{'}} turbolaser defense system. Bitey landed on top of BB-8, annoying him.<ref name="Synaras Score">{{Resistance|Synara's Score}}</ref>

===The phase connector heist===
Later, Bitey was present at the Office of Acquisitions when Kaz was manning the store while Flix and Orka were away visiting [[Flix's mother]]. Kaz also look after the gorg but was annoyed by Bitey's tendency to bite people's fingers. Bitey also bit the [[Keteerian]] criminal [[Teroj Kee]], who had come to [[Theft of the phase connector|steal]] a [[phase connector]].<ref name="Dangerous Business" />

[[File:BiteyAndBB8-DangerousBusiness.jpg|thumb|250px|left|Bitey and BB-8]]
Bitey accompanied Kaz aboard Terroj's [[Darius G-class freighter|''Darius G''-class freighter]] during an attempt to recover the phase connector. Bitey helped Kaz by disabling the power cables, causing the ship to crash into the sea. Though the two were separated, Bitey managed to escape the sinking ship and was rescued by Kaz. As a result, Kaz gained a better appreciation for the gorg.<ref name="Dangerous Business" />

===The occupation===
During the [[First Order]]'s [[Occupation of the Colossus|occupation of the ''Colossus'']], Bitey along with his owners Flix and Orka, and the [[DUM-series pit droid]] [[G-LN]] hid inside a crate. Flix's plan was to smuggle themselves offworld in order to escape First Order occupation. During the [[Battle of Castilon]], Kaz found them hiding inside and informed that the station was underwater.<ref name="No Escape Part 1">{{Resistance|No Escape: Part 1}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Bitey was a [[Gender|male]] gorg with gray skin and turquoise eyes. He had a habit of biting people and earned the nicknamed "Bitey." Bitey got along with his owners Flix and Orka. He was intelligent enough to help Kaz disable a First Order freighter carrying a phase connector offworld.<ref name="Dangerous Business" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bitey first appeared in the first episode of ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' "[[The Recruit]]," which premiered on [[October 7]], [[2018]]. He made several cameo appearances through the series' [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]].
