'''Code-16''' was utilized by the [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="War-Mantle" /> with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]].<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of [[The Bad Batch Season 1|''The Bad Batch'' Season 1]] in [[19 BBY]].</ref> following the war's end and the Republic's [[Proclamation of the New Order|reorganization]] into the [[Galactic Empire]], the Empire worked towards replacing its [[Cloning|clone]] [[soldier]]s with recruited bodies and expelling other [[Republic Military]] aspects, including the use of Code-16. By the [[time]] of [[Clone Force 99]]'s [[Extraction on Daro|extraction mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Daro]]<ref name="War-Mantle" /> the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Timelines" /> when they rescued ex-Imperial [[clone commando]] [[Gregor]], all Republic-related codes had been abolished. [[Commando]] [[Tech]] discovered this when<ref name="War-Mantle" /> he<ref name="Tech Databank">{{Databank|tech|Tech}}</ref> attempted to issue a Code-16 from a terminal and stage a diversion, but instead triggered a security alert across the [[Imperial Base on Daro|Imperial facility]] and brought [[TK stormtrooper]]s to them.<ref name="War-Mantle" />

==Behind the scenes==
Code-16 was introduced via mention in "[[War-Mantle]],"<ref name="War-Mantle" /> the fourteenth episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' [[The Bad Batch Season 1|season one]], which aired on [[July 30]], [[2021]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-july-2021|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in July 2021|nolive=1}}</ref>
