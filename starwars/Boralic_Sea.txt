The '''Boralic Sea''' was an [[ocean]] located on the [[planet]] [[Cerosha]]. The [[City|cities]] of [[Mock]] and [[Solace]] were both located close to the edge of the Boralic Sea. At some point before Solace's [[Destruction of Solace|destruction]], tidal waves from the Boralic Sea threatened to hit the city, but an individual known as [[the Wanderer]] held back the waves, saving Solace.<ref name="The Wanderer" />
