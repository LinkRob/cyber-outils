'''Candledroids''' were a type of hovering [[droid]] used for illumination purposes. They were used for lighting in the [[throne]] room of the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan]].<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref> [[Lessu City|Lessu]], the [[capital]] of [[Ryloth]], had candledroids in its cavernous [[Ryloth archives|archives]], and [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] used candledroids in her office on [[Hosnian Prime]]. Candledroids were also used in the [[Great Hall of Birren]]'s treasure vault.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> The [[Blue Convor]], a famous nightclub on [[Kerev Doi]], used candledroids to light the establishment while still leaving alluring shadows.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> As a [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] used the illumination from candledroids to do his work inside the [[Jedi Archives]] in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Master & Apprentice">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> They were also used to light the [[Shrine in the Depths]] at the Jedi Temple on Coruscant.<ref name="ITD">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref>
