'''"Irrefutable Logic"''' was a short story published in the ''[[Heroes & Rogues]]'' sourcebook by [[West End Games]] in [[1995]]. The brief vignette introduces [[Hesoc]], a [[Rebel pilot/Legends|Rebel pilot]] who serves as an example of the kinds of colorful characters that players might create using the sourcebook.

==Plot summary==
"Hotshot Hesoc" is one of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebellion's]] best [[Pilot/Legends|pilots]] and wants nothing more than to keep flying. During the [[Matacorn campaign]], Hesoc's [[X-wing starfighter/Legends|X-wing]] is damaged, and after crash-landing, he loses his right leg and requires a [[Cybernetics/Legends|cybernetic]] replacement. However, he continues to demand that he remain on active duty. Defying orders, he argues to his [[sector commander]] that his cybernetic leg actually leaves him better prepared for action than his crewmates, since he'll be saved from having to put on a sock and boot when [[Starfighter/Legends|fighters]] are scrambled. General [[Lesilk]] smiles at this "irrefutable logic" and returns Hesoc to his squadron.
