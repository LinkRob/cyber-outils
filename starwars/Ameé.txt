'''Ameé'''<ref name="Timelines" /> was a [[Gender|female]] individual who was [[Reproduction|born]] on<ref name="MA" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Naboo]]<ref name="MA" /> around [[54 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> In [[40 BBY]], they began serving as the [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] of Naboo for the [[Royal House of Naboo]] after their predecessor, [[Monarchy|Queen]] [[Ekay]], left the position.<ref name="Timelines" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] planet Pijal considered inviting Ameé and other young [[Galaxy|galactic]] leaders to her world for a summit. However, her [[lord]] [[regent]] [[Rael Averross]] rejected the idea. [[Standard day|Days]] later, Fanry decried [[democracy]] after seizing [[Dictatorship|absolute power]] at her [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]], insisting that she wished to become a warrior queen, similar to Ameé and other queens of Naboo, on behalf of her [[Pijali|people]].<ref name="MA" /> In [[36 BBY]], Ameé left their position as Naboo's monarch, and Queen [[Réillata]] took their place.<ref name="Timelines" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ameé was first mentioned, albeit indirectly, in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" /> The queen was first identified by name in ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', a [[2023]] reference book.<ref name="Timelines" /> Gray indicated that the inclusion of Fanry and the other young rulers the princess mentioned in the novel&mdash;which included Ameé&mdash;was important, stating in an interview that she did not judge a world's decision to have relatively inexperienced leaders if it worked for them.<ref name="DragonCon">{{WebCite|url=https://toschestation.net/dragon-con-2019-e-k-johnston-and-claudia-gray-talk-the-prequels|text=Dragon Con 2019: E.K.
