'''Chanteuse of the Stars''' was a title held by the [[Musician|singer]] [[Aurodia Ventafoli]].<ref name="OfficialGuide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The title "Chanteuse of the Stars" originates in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] comic [[Star Wars (1977) 77|''Star Wars'' (1977) 77]], published by [[Marvel Comics]] in [[1983]].
