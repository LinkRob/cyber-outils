'''Daiyu City'''<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> was a [[city]] located on the [[planet]] [[Daiyu]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Part II" /> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="KenobiDate">{{KenobiDate|I}}</ref> [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister]] of the [[Inquisitorius]] devised a plan to lure [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] out of hiding by learning of his relationship with [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]]. The Third Sister had [[Vect Nokru]] and his gang [[Ambush on Alderaan|kidnap]] Organa's daughter [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]].<ref name="Part I">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref> Kenobi traveled in Daiyu in order to [[Search for Princess Leia|rescue]] Leia.<ref name="Part II">{{Kenobi|Part II}}</ref>
