"'''A Bazaar Encounter'''" is a scenario for the ''[[Star Wars Miniatures Battles]]'' game, written by [[Stephen Crane]] and published in [[Imperial Entanglements (Star Wars Miniatures Battles)|''Imperial Entanglements'']]. It depicts a battle between [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] [[Rebel trooper/Legends|troops]] and some members of the [[Krayt Draggers]] [[Swoop gang/Legends|swoop gang]] at a [[Jawa/Legends|Jawa]] bazaar, while panicked Jawas scurry around underfoot.

==Plot summary==
Rebel troops engaging in a bit of R&R at a Jawa bazaar find themselves involved in a little dust-up with some members of the Krayt Draggers swoop gang, while panicked Jawas scurry around underfoot.
