A '''crispy Yaya skewer''' was a [[Foodstuff|dish]] made from the local [[Ya-ya fly|Ya-ya flies]] and sold in the [[Market District (Myrra)|Market District]] of [[Myrra]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an [[Imperial officer]] informed a [[merchant]] and [[Chef|cook]] who sold crispy Yaya skewers in the market district that they were behind on their taxes after the rate was increased by two points. Despite their objections, the officer gave them a [[Standard week|week]] to pay it or their belongings would be repossessed.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crispy Yaya skewers were mentioned in an ambient conversation the player can overhear at the [[Myrra Spaceport]] in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
