"'''Chapter 20'''" is the twentieth episode of the ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' animated television series, the tenth and final episode in [[Clone Wars: Season Two|Season Two]], and the twentieth and final episode in [[Star Wars: Clone Wars Volume One|Volume One]]. Directed by [[Genndy Tartakovsky]], the episode originally aired on [[Cartoon Network]] on [[April 8]], [[2004]].

The episode occurs midway through the total time length of the series. It shows the end of the [[Battle of Muunilinst/Legends|Battle of Muunilinst]], which started with the debut of the series, and introduces [[General/Legends|General]] [[Grievous/Legends|Grievous]], who will dominate the rest of the storyline.

==Plot summary==
{{Quote|Blast him!|A battle droid telling droids to kill Voolvif Monn|<ref name="Episode">{{CW|20}}</ref>}}
On [[Muunilinst/Legends|Muunilinst]], [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Voolvif Monn]] leads his [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone troopers]] into battle against the last remaining pockets of [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droids]]. Suddenly, the [[Droid/Legends|droids]] deactivate, and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] tells Monn that the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan/Legends|InterGalactic Banking Clan]] have surrendered, and the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] is victorious. As Kenobi supervises the loading of [[Muun/Legends|Muun]] and [[Neimoidian/Legends|Neimoidian]] [[Prisoner/Legends|prisoners]]-of-war onto Republic [[Transport/Legends|transports]], [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Fordo]] reports that a ship matching the one that [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] had pursued is approaching. Kenobi orders him to hold fire, and Skywalker exits the [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighter]], apologizing for his disobedience and confirming the chase had been a [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] trap. As Kenobi upbraids his [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]], he receives an urgent distress call from Jedi Master [[Daakman Barrek]] on [[Hypori/Legends|Hypori]]. Barrek's forces have been wiped out by "a new droid general." The signal cuts out abruptly.

On Hypori, Barrek's [[Death/Legends|dead]] hand drops his holo-communicator, which is crushed by a robotic clawed foot. Thousands of [[B2-series super battle droid/Legends|B2 super battle droids]] encircle a crashed Republic [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship/Legends|Acclamator]], firing their [[Blaster/Legends|blasters]] at it until the clawed hand of the caped figure in front of them signals for them to stop. Inside the wreckage, Jedi Master [[Ki-Adi-Mundi/Legends|Ki-Adi-Mundi]] wonders why the attack has ceased. Five other [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]], wounded and weary, surround him: [[Shaak Ti/Legends|Shaak Ti]], [[K'Kruhk]], [[Aayla Secura/Legends|Aayla Secura]], [[Tarr Seirr]], and [[Sha'a Gi]]. As the Jedi wonder how they could have been defeated by mere battle droids, a voice calls out to them: [[General/Legends|General]] [[Grievous/Legends|Grievous]].
{{Quote|Jedi, you are surrounded. Your armies decimated. Make peace with the Force now, for this is your final hour. But know that I, General Grievous, am not completely without mercy. I will grant you a warrior's death. Prepare!|General Grievous|audio=MakePeaceWithTheForce-CW20.flac|<ref name="Episode" />}}
[[File:Aayla_and_Ki-Adi_vs._Grievous.jpg|thumb|right|230px|[[Grievous/Legends|Grievous]] fighting against [[Aayla Secura/Legends|Aayla Secura]], [[K'Kruhk]], and [[Ki-Adi-Mundi/Legends|Ki-Adi-Mundi]]]]

The sound of Grievous's approaching footsteps panics some of the Jedi, but Ki-Adi-Mundi urges them to remain calm.
{{Quote|We can't face him!"<br />"''We'' must ''try, Padawan!''|Sha'a Gi and Ki-Adi-Mundi|<ref name="Episode" />}}
It's too much for [[Sha'a Gi]], who charges into the open air where he is crushed to death as the metallic frame of General Grievous lands on top of him. Just as quickly, the general leaps out of sight, before dropping onto the remaining Jedi wielding two [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsabers]]. Spinning his torso around, Grievous easily holds off the four attacking Jedi, finally downing K'Kruhk. His clawed metal limbs get hold of Tarr Seirr and snap his neck, before hurling him and Aayla Secura across the chamber, followed by Ki-Adi-Mundi. Shaak Ti manages to hold off Grievous for some time, before she is hit by one of his spinning lightsabers and thrown into the wreckage. His own lightsaber held by Grievous's foot, Mundi uses [[The Force/Legends|the Force]] to pull one from Grievous's belt, which he ignites and raises the green lightsaber into a guard. The two stare at each other for a moment before the droid general leaps at him, lightsabers angled for the kill.

On [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]], Master [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] senses a disturbance in the Force: "Mmmm. Darker, the coming storm grows. I fear the dark cloud of the Sith shrouds us all." ({{Audio|DarkerTheComingStormGrows-CW20.flac|Listen}})

==Development==
Originally, the last chapter was going to involve Anakin and Obi-Wan fighting [[Durge/Legends|Durge]]. This was changed when [[George Lucas]] requested that they introduce [[General/Legends|General]] [[Grievous/Legends|Grievous]].

The introduction of Grievous in animated form in "Chapter 20" prior to the release of ''Revenge of the Sith'' ([[2005]]) is reminiscent of [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett's]] introduction in animated form in ''[[The Star Wars Holiday Special]]'' prior to the release of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''The Empire Strikes Back'']] ([[1980]]).

==Continuity==
This episode marked the first appearance of General Grievous, who is voiced by [[John DiMaggio]] (best known for playing Bender the robot in [[Wikipedia:Futurama|''Futurama'']]). In [[Clone Wars: Season Three|Season Three]], [[Richard McGonagle]] voiced the character.

This episode is more than twice as long as the previous episodes in the series at 7 minutes 15 seconds.

After Grievous fights the five Jedi initially, jumps to a wall, and jumps again near them, Shaak Ti's lightsaber blade appears green, though it is blue seconds before and later.

In this episode, Grievous is depicted with five fingers and only uses two of his arms, although Grievous has six fingers and uses all four of his arms in [[Chapter 25 (Star Wars: Clone Wars)|Chapter 25]].

In this episode, Grievous holds his defeated Jedi lightsabers on his belt. In [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Revenge of the Sith'']], they are held in his cape.

Over the course of the first and second season, [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Fordo]]'s rank changes. In "[[Chapter 3 (Star Wars: Clone Wars)|Chapter 3]]" he is a [[Sergeant/Legends|sergeant]], in "[[Chapter 9 (Star Wars: Clone Wars)|Chapter 9]]" Obi-Wan calls him [[Commander/Legends|commander]], and here he is a [[Captain/Legends|captain]].
