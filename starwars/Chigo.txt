'''Chigo''' was a [[Rodian]] and an ally of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Even before being won over fully to the Alliance's cause, Chigo had run [[black market]] goods to dissident worlds for [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="Chigo" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
During the [[Iron Blockade|blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]] by the [[Galactic Empire]], Chigo joined the crew of [[the Smuggler]], who dispatched the Rodian on a variety of missions throughout the [[sector]].<ref name="Chigo" />
{{EndGame}}

==Personality and traits==
Coming from a criminal background and having a preference for defensive tactics, Chigo was lewd and hot-blooded.<ref name="Chigo" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chigo was created for the [[canon]] mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', which was developed by [[Kabam]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms and released in [[2015]],<ref name="SW:U">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> where he is a unique 5 star crew member.<ref name="Chigo" />
