'''Conversations With Lanius''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] [[Wizards.com|website]] on [[June 9]], [[2005]] and took the form of a transcript of interview of [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Lanius Qel-Bertuk]]. It was the second ''Living Force'' article to be titled ''Conversations With Lanius'', with the [[Conversations With Lanius|first one]] being added to Wizards.com in [[2003]].
