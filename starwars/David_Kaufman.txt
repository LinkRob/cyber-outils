'''David Kaufman''' (born [[July 23]], [[1960s#1961|1961]]) is an [[Wikipedia:America|American]] voice-over artist and character actor notable for voicing Danny Fenton/Phantom on the Nickelodeon show [[Wikipedia:Danny Phantom|''Danny Phantom'']]. Kaufman voiced [[Jaybo Hood]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="gg">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode018.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Mystery of a Thousand Moons}}</ref>.
