'''Derrida''' was a [[Ketton]] [[assassin]] active during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Longsnoot" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of "[[The Secrets of Long Snoot]]" to [[1 BBY]].</ref> she needed a partner for a [[Local day|nighttime]] [[Death|assassination]] job against the Alliance on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]; however, no one wanted to take it, as it involved taking sides in the war.<ref name="Longsnoot" />

The [[Devaronian]] [[Kardue'sai'Malloc]] considered the job too much work to take personally, but on the [[Standard day|day]] the job was due to take place, he informed the [[Kubaz]] [[spy]] [[Garindan ezz Zavor]] of Derrida's need for a second when the Kubaz asked him about quick work while the pair were in the [[city]] [[Mos Eisley]] on Tatooine. Desperate for [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], the spy told the Devaronian he would take the job, and so Kardue'sai'Malloc sent Derrida a message on his [[datapad]], informing Zavor that she would be in contact to provide the Kubaz with the coordinates.<ref name="Longsnoot" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Derrida was mentioned in "[[The Secrets of Long Snoot]]," a short story written by [[Delilah S. Dawson]] and published in the [[2017]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''.<ref name="Longsnoot" /> The [[Derrida/Legends|character]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where she was first mentioned in the [[1995]] ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' sourcebook ''[[Alliance Intelligence Reports]]'', which was published by [[West End Games]] and included an illustration of a Derrida by artist [[Matt Busch]].<ref name="AIR">''[[Alliance Intelligence Reports]]''</ref>
