'''Frel''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] citizen of the [[Eternal Empire]] of [[Zakuul]]. During the [[Eternal Empire conquest]] Frel served in the [[Eternal Empire military]] under the command of [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Koth Vortena]] and was eventually killed by an infection on [[Quesh]].<ref name="TOR" />
