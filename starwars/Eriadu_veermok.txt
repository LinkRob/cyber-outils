The '''Eriadu veermok''' was a [[subspecies]] of [[veermok]] that could be found on [[Eriadu]], a [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. While the regular veermok was a solitary and [[Tree|arboreal]] creature, the Eriadu subspecies was social and adapted to the dry conditions of plateaus. Additionally, the Eriadu veermoks were sleek-skinned and stood only a [[meter]] high,<ref name="Tarkin" /> while the regular ones looked about twice the size of a [[human]]<ref name="The Nest">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Nest]]''</ref> and were covered in wooly [[hair]].<ref name="Tarkin" />
