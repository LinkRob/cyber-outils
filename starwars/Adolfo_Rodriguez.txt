'''Adolfo Rodriguez''' is a [[Wikipedia:Spain|Spanish]] ''Star Wars'' fan and a member of the [[Dark Horse Comics]] official forum. In [[December]] [[2012]], Rodriguez brought the attention of his fellow forum users to a series of long-forgotten stories published in [[1986]] under the ''[[MyComyc]]'' imprint. When ''Star Wars'' authors [[Rich Handley]] and [[Abel G. Peña]] expressed their interest in translating the ''MyComyc'' strips, Rodriguez provided scans of the eight issues with the help of [[Eddie van der Heijden]], another fan of note and contributor to ''[[The Essential Atlas]]''. Rodriguez' contribution to the rediscovery of the MyComyc strips was mentioned by Handley on the official [[Star Wars Blog]], in a post titled "Droids and Ewoks Return: Spain's Lost ''Star Wars'' Comic Strips."<ref name="Blog">{{SW|url=news/droids-and-ewoks-return-spains-lost-star-wars-comic-strips|text=Droids and Ewoks Return: Spain's Lost ''Star Wars'' Comic Strips}}</ref>
