'''Friedhelm Ptok''' is a [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice artist]] who voiced [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] in the [[Wikipedia:German|German]] audio drama adaptations of [[Labyrinth of Evil (German audio drama)|''Labyrinth of Evil'']]<ref name="Labyrinth" /> and [[Dark Lord (German audio drama)|''Dark Lord'']].<ref name="Dark Lord">[[Dark Lord (German audio drama)|''Dark Lord'']]</ref> Ptok would continue to perform Palpatine in the [[German Clone Wars audio dramas]] and [[German Rebels audio dramas]] which adapted a number of episodes from their animated television series.
