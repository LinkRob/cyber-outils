'''C2-B9''' was an [[R5 series astromech droid]] manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]]. It at some point served in the [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]]<ref name="Datapad" /> of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] on the [[planet]]<ref name="SW:VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Tatooine]]. While there, it recorded an internal data record of reprimanding someone for their attitude and asking about repairing a [[Sail barge|barge]]. The record included an image of several denizens of the palace, including two [[Jawa]]s,<ref name="Datapad" /> the [[Frog-dog]] [[Buboicullaar]], and the [[Snivvian]] [[Geezum]].<ref name="SW:VE" />

By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> C2-B9 was refurbished and acting as a [[Maintenance droid|repair droid]].<ref name="Datapad" /> It ended up in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the planet [[Batuu]] during its [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] by the [[First Order]]. The [[droid]] was owned by the outpost's [[Droid Depot]] and was part of a display of several droids stood behind a railing on a plinth outside the front of the depot. C2-B9 had an [[R5 series astromech droid|R5 dome]] with a black [[sensor]] and [[Color|orange]] and white paneling.<ref name="GEDD" /> It carried a [[star map]] showing a portion of [[the Slice]] region of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Datapad" />

==Behind the scenes==
C2-B9 first appeared as an animatronic prop within the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="GEDD" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The prop is capable of spinning its head and emitting beeping sounds.<ref name="GEDD" /> The droid simultaneously appeared in the [[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad]] application launched alongside the themed land.<ref name="Datapad" />
