The '''Engineering Corps''' was a [[Resistance]] subdivision that made the necessary modifications to keep the Resistance's gear operational as well as countering new advancements in technology of the [[First Order]].<ref name="SW:TBS" /> [[Commander]] [[Sesfan]] was a member of the [[corps]] before being [[Death|killed]] during the [[Battle of Oetchi]].<ref name="Training of Rey and Battle of Crait">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Training of Rey and the Battle of Crait}}</ref> [[Resistance technician]] [[Rose Tico]] was assigned to lead the Corps after rising through the ranks.<ref name="SW:TBS" />
