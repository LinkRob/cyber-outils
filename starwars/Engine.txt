'''Engines''', also known as '''thrusters''', were devices used to propel vehicles across land, through a [[planet]]'s [[atmosphere]], or through the void of [[Realspace|space]]. Engines could vary in size and shape, with some vehicles like [[podracer]]s consisting of little more than two engines and a [[cockpit]].<ref name="TPM" />

There were at least three categories of engines: [[repulsorlift]]s (which required a large mass to push against in order to function), [[sublight engine]]s (which drove ships in realspace at speeds below that of light), and [[hyperdrive]]s (which attained lightspeed and propelled ships in [[hyperspace]]).<ref name="ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>

Engine power in some [[starship]]s could be boosted to increase the vehicle's speed. [[Asteroid thruster]]s were a type of engine which could be attached to [[asteroid]]s and used to move them.<ref name="RR">{{RebelsMagCite|1|Ring Race}}</ref>
