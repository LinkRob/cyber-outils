The '''Castilon gorgs''' were a [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[Species|subspecies]] of the [[amphibian|amphibious]] [[gorg]]. [[Bitey]] was a member of the [[species]].

==Biology and appearance==
The Castilon gorg<ref name="Creature Stall" /> was a [[Sentience|non-sentient]]<ref name="Bestiary" /> [[Species|subspecies]] of the [[amphibian]] [[gorg]]. They were considered as a [[Diet|carnivorous]] creature, often eating large [[insect]]s and other animals.<ref name="Creature Stall" />

==History==
Between around [[233 BBY]] and [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|FWN}}</ref> a Castilon gorg found it's way into a toolbox in [[Nash's garage]] where it was discovered by [[RJ-83]]. RJ-83 chased the Castilon gorg, but when the Castilon gorg helped clean up the garage, RJ-83 allowed the Castilon gorg to rest in a toolbox.<ref name="RJ Makes a Friend">{{FunWithNubs|RJ Makes a Friend}}</ref> Another Castilon gorg made noise in the dark while [[Nubs]] attempted to sleep, which bothered him until he located the Castilon gorg and figured out that by using it as an eye mask with the lights on, he was able to sleep.<ref name="Nubs Tries to Sleep">{{FunWithNubs|Nubs Tries To Sleep}}</ref>

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> a Castilon gorg was acquired by the [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] after a deal he made with the merchants [[Flix]] and [[Orka]] in exchange for [[starship]] parts needed to fix his [[racing starfighter]] the ''[[Fireball]]''. The Castilon gorg was originally planned to be eaten as lunch, but the pair merchants decided to keep the amphibian and named him [[Bitey]].<ref name="The Recruit" />

Thank you, Colas!|archivedate=20211120004547}}</ref>
