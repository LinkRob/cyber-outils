"'''False Histories'''" is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story published in the [[Battle of Jakku — Last Stand 1|first issue]] of the [[2024]] comic limited series ''[[Star Wars: Battle of Jakku — Last Stand]]''. The story was written by [[Alex Segura]], illustrated by [[Leonard Kirk]] and [[Jim Campbell]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 25]], 2024.

==Plot summary==
[[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] reflects on the feeble nature of memory, how it serves the narrative one wants to believe. As he reclaims his [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] ''[[Scepter (Imperial-class)|Scepter]]'' from the traitorous clutches of the [[Anzati]] mystic [[Reyna Oskure]], he thinks on how he has always preferred the harshness of reality to romanticism, even when inconvenient. He contemplates how in the chaos wrought by [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] [[death]], he envisioned a better version of the Empire. However, he found himself stranded on [[Gallowyss]], a remote [[planet]] in his own [[Anoat sector]], clinging to life due to being misled. He thought that when [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] told him that the Empire has great plans for him, that it meant they foresaw greatness; instead, Vader meant they would use him as a pawn. 

With everything taken from him, Adelhard's driving force becomes his thirst for revenge. Before the [[Trandoshan]] [[assassin]] [[Kranor]] stuffed him into an [[escape pod]], Adelhard activated his own secret contingency plan in hopes to raze the Empire to create a new one from its ashes. He planned [[terrorist]] attacks on the [[New Republic|New Republic's]] home, [[Chandrila]], for instance. Adelhard orders [[Commander]] [[Bragh]] to burn it all. He thinks of his rise through the Imperial ranks and realizes it is time to find a better way. 

Elsewhere in [[the galaxy]], [[Captain]] [[Stansel Malgreen]] and his [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] crew notice a rapidly-approaching Imperial ship while on their way to [[Jakku]]. The supposedly friendly ship opens fire, but Malgreen's ship cannot evade and they crash into it. Ubrik Adelhard recognizes that the Empire's contingency plans never involved him, but that they are centralized at Jakku. He intends to take his last stand there too, hoping to be the last party standing in the upcoming [[Battle of Jakku|battle]], or leave no one standing at all.
