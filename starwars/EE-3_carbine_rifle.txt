The '''EE-3 carbine rifle''' was a model of [[blaster carbine]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]]. The most notorious user of the EE-3 was the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] during and after the [[Galactic Civil War]], with the weapon being considered his signature weapon.

==Description==
[[File:ASimpleMan-SWL104.png|thumb|left|250px|Boba Fett, arguably the most famous individual to wield the EE-3.]]
Produced by [[BlasTech Industries]] and designed with [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement agencies]] and private security in mind, only for [[bounty hunter]]s<ref name="Collapse" /> and even the [[Galactic Empire]]'s formal [[Imperial Military]] to make use of it,<ref name="Battlefront" /> the EE-3 was a short, lightweight [[blaster carbine]] based off an older full-sized BlasTech rifle. Rugged, reliable,<ref name="Collapse" /> and notable for its size,<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> the EE-3 came equipped with a short and heavily shrouded [[barrel]], pistol grip, a solid [[stock]], and [[Electronic scope|optical sight]].<ref name="Collapse" /> To make up for the smaller handle, the weapon relied on its wielder holding it with two [[Limb|hands]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> The EE-3 was select-fire. It could fire single shots or bursts, which was an oddity for carbines.<ref name="Collapse" />

The EE-3 also boasted heavy fire capability, which was on par with many rifles of its time<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> but with a faster rate of fire than larger [[blaster rifle]]s. However, the weapon&mdash;at least in its a standard, non-modified form&mdash;had less stopping power and accuracy than those larger [[rifle]]s.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> Its moderate range was typical of carbine blaster rifles,<ref name="Collapse" /> but the weapon could be modified for greater accuracy, more stopping power, and to have a slower firing speed.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> To see an individual wielding an EE-3 was a sign that the individual in question was serious about their job.<ref name="TCW CE">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> The weapon was shorter and lighter than the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]], another BlasTech product that served as the standard armament of the Empire's [[Stormtrooper Corps]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> The [[EE-4 carbine rifle]] was the successor to the EE-3; the EE-4 removed the shoulder stock but added a vertical handgrip, as well as having a faster rate of fire, making the EE-4 superior at closer ranges than the EE-3, but at the expense of range.<ref name="Battlefront" />

==History==
[[File:Sugi-SWI125.png|thumb|right|250px|The EE-3 was popular amongst bounty hunters, such as Sugi.]]
The EE-3 was one of BlasTech Industries's most popular non-military weapons. While it was primarily designed for law enforcement and private security forces, its standard features and low price quickly made the EE-3 extremely popular with bounty hunters and paramilitary groups across the galaxy.<ref name="Collapse" /> Within the bounty hunting circle, the weapon was also popular due to its size.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the EE-3 was used by the [[bounty]] hunters [[Sugi (bounty hunter)|Sugi]] and [[Kiera Swan]].<ref name="TCW CE" /> Beyond the EE-3 becoming one of her favorite pieces of gear to use,<ref name="SecretsBountyHunters">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref> Sugi's sawed-off<ref name="SWE">{{SWE|characters|sugi|Sugi}}</ref> EE-3 was enough to signify her commitment to the job of bounty hunting.<ref name="TCW CE" /> She also used it to great effect during a [[shootout at Serolonis]], during which the bounty hunter [[Boba Fett]] took notice of it.<ref name="MandoVG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref>

Favoring the weapon after seeing its usefulness at [[Serolonis]],<ref name="MandoVG" /> Fett, who became regarded as the greatest bounty hunter in the galaxy,<ref name="Darth7">[[Darth Vader (2015) 7|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 7]] </ref> proved to be very effective with the EE-3; becoming his favorite and preferred blaster, Fett modified the weapon to his liking and, building off his already excellent skills as a [[Sniper|sharpshooter]], used the technology in his [[Mandalorian]] [[helmet]] to help ensure he hit his targets. All together, these factors made the EE-3 a deadly weapon in Fett's hands.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> The EE-3's weight became a familiar feeling in his hands over his career.<ref Name="Reputation">{{StoryCite|story=Reputation (Boba Fett)|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref> Fett's EE-3 also had a modified stock.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

The EE-3 was used by [[trooper]]s of both the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Battlefront" /> [[Thea Starros]] had an EE-3 in her armory at the [[Starros family homestead]].<ref name="Sana Starros 1">[[Sana Starros 1|''Sana Starros'' 1]]</ref> The [[mercenary]] [[protocol droid]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Q9-0]] used one during the [[New Republic Era|era]] of the [[New Republic]], almost killing Grogu before [[Din Djarin]] intervened and claim the weapon as part of his weapon locker.<ref name="Chapter 6">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 6: The Prisoner}}</ref> After giving up bounty hunting to become the [[daimyo]] of his own [[Fett gotra|gotra]], Fett obtained a new EE-3<ref name="Chapter 7" /> from the ''[[Razor Crest]]'',<ref name="Mandalorian VisualGuide">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> and continued to use the rifle<ref name="Chapter 7" /> after his old one was destroyed by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref Name="EPVI" /> Fett's new EE-3 carbine had a dial for [[safety]]/[[stun]]/power settings along with a [[Melihat]] [[WSM1N1 macroscope]].<ref name="Mandalorian VisualGuide" />

Several Mandalorian [[Children of the Watch]] who were members of [[the Tribe]], [[Idalia Desta]], [[Kort Bralor]], and [[Hewitt Dala]], carried EE-3s during the [[incident on Nevarro]].<ref name="Chapter 3">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 3: The Sin}}</ref>

Gist, on Twitter|<ref name="GistTweet">{{Twitter|username=E_M_Gist|url=status/1959708028149879122|name=[[E.M. Gist]]|date=2025-08-24|quote=The ee-3 is different in nearly every shot of it. [[File:Acme-LastOneStanding.png|thumb|left|300px|The EE-3 was first seen being used by Boba Fett]]
In the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', the EE-3 carbine rifle was misidentified as "ESB carbine rifle" in its entry in the technology section of the book. However, in the characters section, under the [[bounty hunter]] [[Sugi (bounty hunter)|Sugi]], it is identified under its name that was introduced in the now non-[[canon]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] line as the "EE-3 carbine rifle."<ref name="Ultimate" /> Artist [[E.M. Gist]] observed that the EE-3 has a habit of differing in appearance between different shots. When he needed to paint Boba Fett's EE-3<ref name="GistTweet" /> as part of his cover for [[Boba Fett — Black, White & Red 3|''Boba Fett — Black, White & Red'' 3]],<ref name="Solicit">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/123261/star_wars_boba_fett_-_black_white_red_2025_3|text=Star Wars: Boba Fett - Black, White & Red (2025) #3}}</ref> Gist chose to feature its design with a shining barrel, as he felt it would better "pop"<ref name="GistTweet" /> on the red background.<ref name="Solicit" />
