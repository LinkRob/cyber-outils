A '''body glove''',<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> also known as a '''body suit''',<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> or a '''skinsuit''',<ref name="Last Order">''[[The Last Order]]''</ref> was a full-body, form-fitting piece of [[clothing]]. The [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[stormtrooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> the [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] of the [[First Order]],<ref name="Visual">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and the [[Sith trooper]]s of the [[Sith Eternal]] all wore [[Color|black]] body gloves under their [[armor]].<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

The clone troopers' temperature-controlled [[Phase I clone trooper armor|phase I armor]] was sealed to twenty form-fitting [[plastoid]]-alloy composite plates by [[Magnatomic grip panel|magnatomic gription panels]]. Their pressurized body gloves also gave the clones temporary protection against the vacuum of [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="SWECG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>
