An '''alphanumeric designated star system''' was located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]' [[Croynar sector]], home to a clutter of [[planetoid]]s and debris. The [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] was [[Fourth encounter with the Yadeez|pursued]] to the [[star system]] in [[5 ABY]] by the [[New Republic]] refitted [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]''. A short skirmish occurred before the Imperials were able to escape.

==Description==
The alphanumeric [[star system]] was located in the [[Croynar sector]] within the [[Trailing Sectors]] portion of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]. Situated around the coordinates M-14 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Note" /> it was cluttered with [[planetoid]]s and debris.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==History==
[[File:204thLegion-TIEFighter1.png|thumb|left|300px|The 204th Imperial Fighter Wing (pictured) was pursued in the alphanumeric designated star system.]]
The alphanumeric designated star system had not been mapped by the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] traveled to the system after being attacked in three other systems by the [[New Republic]] refitted [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]'' and its [[Deliverance's starfighter wing|starfighter wing]]. The ''Deliverance'' arrived out of [[hyperspace]] deep into the system to surprise the 204th, having followed a communications signal burst from the 204th's [[bulk freighter]] ''[[Yadeez]]''.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

The ''Deliverance'' [[Fourth encounter with the Yadeez|forced]] the [[starship]]s of the Imperial [[fighter wing]] toward one of the system's planetoids, delaying their escape and allowing for a dogfight between the [[starfighter]]s of both sides. The 204th managed to escape after 387 [[second]]s of fighting, down two [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilots]], while the New Republic lost none. The duration the skirmish lasted was declared a new record during a celebration held by the [[New Republic pilot]]s afterward. The ''Deliverance'' continued to pursue the 204th through more comm bursts.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
The alphanumeric designated system was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel [[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel|''Victory's Price'']], written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the final installment of his ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
