A '''comatoir''' was a type of hostel in [[Doom City]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].<ref name="CB">{{CasusBelliCite|HS24|Il faut sauver Miss Pickalho}}</ref>

Accommodation within them took the form of corridors lined with tube-shaped sleeping chambers. The chambers measured about 2.5 meters long by one meter in diameter, secured by a code-lock. In [[0 ABY/Legends|0 ABY]], six hours' use of a chamber could be bought for five credits.<ref name="CB" />

There was also a [[Sonic shower/Legends|sonic shower]] at the end of each corridor - requiring an additional two credits for each minute of use.<ref name="CB" />

{{stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Doom City and its "comatoirs" appear in ''[[Il faut sauver Miss Pickalho]]'', a French-language scenario for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' which appeared in ''[[Casus Belli]]'' magazine in March 1999. As this material was not approved through the [[Lucas Licensing]] process, it is considered non-canon.

The comatoirs resemble even more austere versions of the undercity [[resiblock|resicubes]] of later official material. The term appears to be a portmanteau of ''coma'' and ''abbatoir''.
