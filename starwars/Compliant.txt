The '''''Compliant''''' was a [[Star Destroyer]] used by the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. In [[14 BBY]], the [[Starship|ship]] was stationed at the [[planet]] [[Pii]]'s [[Imperial marshaling station Pii|Imperial marshaling station]], along with another Star Destroyer, the ''[[Enforcer]]''. When a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]] [[Battle near the Gulf of Tatooine|attacked]] an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[convoy]] near the [[Gulf of Tatooine]], both Star Destroyers were dispatched to the battle. However, by the [[time]] they arrived, the rebels had already been defeated by [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] from the ''[[Executrix]]''.

==Description==
A [[Star Destroyer]], the ''Compliant'' featured both [[engine]]s and a [[hyperdrive]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

==History==
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> during the [[Imperial Era]], the ''Compliant'' was stationed at the [[planet]] [[Pii]]'s [[Imperial marshaling station Pii|Imperial marshaling station]], along with the ''[[Enforcer]]'', another Star Destroyer. At that [[time]], a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]] led by [[Berch Teller]] [[Battle near the Gulf of Tatooine|attacked]] an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[convoy]] in a [[star system]] [[Core Worlds|Coreward]] of the [[Gulf of Tatooine]]. With the attack, Teller aimed to impede the progress of an Imperial [[battle station]],<ref name="Tarkin" /> later known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]],<ref name="Rogue One guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> which was being constructed over the planet [[Geonosis]] and was the convoy's final destination.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Due to the attack, the ''Compliant'' and the ''Enforcer'' were dispatched from Pii to assist in the battle. However, by the time the two Star Destroyers arrived in the system through [[hyperspace]], the rebels had just been defeated, as Imperial [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] had arrived beforehand in his [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Executrix]]'' to protect the convoy. As the ''Compliant'' and ''Enforcer'' arrived, an [[Unidentified Imperial technician (Executrix)|Imperial technician]] on the ''Executrix''{{'s}} [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] informed Tarkin that two marks were reverting from hyperspace. Tarkin thought for a moment he had fallen into a trap, but the [[technician]] then clarified that the two marks were Star Destroyers dispatched from Pii.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Behind the scenes==
