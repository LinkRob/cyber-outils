The '''Coruscant Accords''' were regulations in the [[New Republic]] after the [[Galactic Civil War]]. 

==Guidelines==
Section 13, subparagraph seven of the [[Coruscant]] Accords prohibited research of [[cloning]] and [[Bioengineering|genetic engineering]].<ref name="Chapter 19" />

==History==
During the [[New Republic Era]], [[New Republic Amnesty Program|Amnesty]] [[Scientist]] [[Doctor]] [[Penn Pershing]] asked his [[Unidentified parole droid|parole droid]] if he was allowed to conduct recreational research, having previously worked in the field of cloning and [[gene]]tic engineering. The [[droid]] informed the former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] scientist that his type of research was restricted by the Coruscant Accords.<ref name="Chapter 19" />
