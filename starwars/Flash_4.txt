The '''Flash 4''' was a [[blaster]] model manufactured by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]] to compete with [[BlasTech Industries]]'s [[DL-44 heavy blaster pistol]]. It succeeded on the market and became known as a legal, low-power alternative to the DL-44.<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Flash 4 first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as the [["Flash" 4]] in ''[[Planet of Twilight]]'', a novel by [[Barbara Hambly]] which was released in [[1997]].<ref name="Twilight">''[[Planet of Twilight]]''</ref> The Flash 4 was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[De Agostini|Altaya's]] "[[Blasters and Ranged Weapons]]" reference book, released exclusively in France on [[February 23]], [[2021]].<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" />
