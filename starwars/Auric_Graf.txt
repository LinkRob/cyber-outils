'''Auric Graf''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who was [[Marriage|married]] to [[Rhyssa Graf]]. Together, the two had worked as [[cartographer]]s in [[Wild Space]] for fifteen [[Standard year|years]] during the [[Imperial Era]], producing maps of the vast area. Auric and Rhyssa had two [[Youngling|children]], [[Lina Graf|Lina]] and [[Milo Graf|Milo]], who accompanied their parents on expeditions. While on an [[Graf-World|uncharted swampworld]], [[Captain]] [[Visler Aqorian Korda III]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] arrested Auric and his wife, leaving Lina and Milo behind.<ref name="The Escape" />
