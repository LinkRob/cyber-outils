'''Crys''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. In [[21 BBY]], Crys served under [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]] and [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] during the [[Battle of Saleucami]], where the [[trooper]] assisted Kenobi in tracking the Separatist [[General]] [[Grievous]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Saleucami]]. Crys's [[robolobotomy]] skills were put to use by Kenobi in the hunt for Grievous as they dismantled a [[B1-series battle droid|battle droid]] to gain intelligence on Grievous's whereabouts.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Alert the men. We've picked up the scent.|Obi-Wan Kenobi, to Crys|audio=AlertTheMen-TD.flac|<ref name="TCW:TD" />}}
Crys was the name assumed by a [[clone trooper]]<ref name="TCW:TD" /> who was created on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]]<ref name="CT DB" /> and was a member of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and the Republic, the Confederacy's [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander of the Droid Army]], [[General]] [[Grievous]], escaped to the surface of<ref name="TCW:TD" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet<ref name="Saleucami DB">{{Databank|saleucami|Saleucami}}</ref> [[Saleucami]] in a [[C-9979 landing craft]] during the [[Battle of Saleucami]]<ref name="TCW:TD" /> in [[21 BBY]].<ref name="Dates">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates of the "[[The Deserter]]" to [[21 BBY]].</ref> As the Confederacy's general descended upon the planet, his [[landing craft]] was damaged and its crew was forced to jettison numerous [[escape pod]]s, one of which included the general himself. [[Republic Military|Republic forces]], including Crys&mdash;acting as a [[Technician|technical specialist]] due to his [[robolobotomy]] skills&mdash;were quickly deployed to the planet to track down the elusive general.<ref name="TCW:TD" />

After Republic forces landed near the crash site in an [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class assault ship]], Crys accompanied [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]],<ref name="TCW:TD" /> [[Clone Commander]]<ref name="Cody SWE">{{SWE|characters|clonecommandercody|Clone Commander Cody}}</ref> [[Cody]], [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], and many other Republic [[troop]]s and vehicles in the proceeding search of the planet. As Republic forces began to inspect the landing craft's crash site and failed to locate Grievous, Kenobi split his forces to investigate the landing sites of the escape pods. Crys accompanied [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]] [[walker]]s, Kenobi, and Cody to an escape pod and inspected a destroyed [[B1-series battle droid|B1 battle droid]] for any possible intelligence. He deduced that the [[droid]] was too damaged for any reliable information to be extracted. However, a fellow clone [[trooper]] spotted an intact&mdash;albeit inactive&mdash;[[battle droid]] inside the escape pod, and Kenobi ordered the droid to be brought into one of the AT-TE walkers.<ref name="TCW:TD" />

[[File:Crys-Examiner.png|thumb|left|275px|Cody, Obi-Wan Kenobi, and Crys examine the battle droid.]]
Inside the walker, Crys and Cody conducted a robolobotomy, breaking through the droid's access codes and activating its guidance systems. When Kenobi later asked if the pair had found any data regarding Grievous's whereabouts, the [[soldier]]s briefed him that the droid's escape pod had crashed to avoid a midair collision with another pod, the latter being the final undiscovered pod. Meanwhile, General Grievous was proceeding from his crash site with his remaining troops towards the final intact escape pod at the same [[time]] the Republic forces pinpointed its location. The Republic and Confederacy forces engaged in combat, with the clone forces emerging victorious. However, Grievous&mdash;the primary objective of the mission&mdash;escaped after a [[Sheathipede-class transport shuttle|''Sheathipede''-class transport shuttle]] arrived over the area to extract him.<ref name="TCW:TD" />

==Personality and traits==
Crys was a clone of the human [[Gender|male]] [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] Jango Fett, and as such stood 1.83 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="CT DB" /> Crys also had [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s, tan skin, and yellow [[hair]].<ref name="TCW:TD" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|What do you have?''"<br />"''We've broken the access codes and powered up the droid's guidance system.|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Crys|audio=BrokenTheAccessCodes-TD.flac|<ref name="TCW:TD" />}}
[[File:Crys_full_armor.png|thumb|right|250px|Crys inspecting a droid on Saleucami]]
Crys was capable of inspecting destroyed battle droids to determine whether or not they could be useful for intelligence. He also had the skill to successfully perform a robolobotomy on a B1 battle droid in field conditions on Saleucami, being able to break through the droid's access codes and initiate the droid's guidance system.<ref name="TCW:TD" />

==Equipment==
During the Battle of Saleucami, Crys was equipped with a set of [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] marked with yellow designs on the breastplates and on his helmet.<ref name="TCW:TD" />
