"'''Dark Vengeance'''," subtitled "'''''The True Story of Darth Maul and His Revenge Against the Jedi Known as Obi-Wan Kenobi'''''," is a short story that was published in the anthology ''[[The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark]]'', adapting the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episodes "[[Brothers (episode)|Brothers]]" and "[[Revenge (episode)|Revenge]]." It was written by [[Rebecca Roanhorse]], and published on [[August 25]], [[2020]] by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]].
