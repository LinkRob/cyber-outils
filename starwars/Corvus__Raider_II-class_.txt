The '''''Corvus''''' was a [[Raider II-class corvette|''Raider II''-class corvette]] used by [[Inferno Squad]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Originally an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Starship|vessel]], the ''Corvus'' joined the [[New Republic]] following [[Commander]] [[Iden Versio]] and [[Agent]] [[Del Meeko]]'s [[Skirmish on Vardos|defection]] from the Empire. Some time after joining the Republic, the ''Corvus'' participated in the [[Battle of Jakku|final battle]] that marked the end of the war and the Empire. [[Standard decade|Decades]] later, it was used as a cargo hauler by Meeko and was captured by the First Order on Pillio while looking for Lor San Tekka and the map to Luke Skywalker. It would later be shot down and destroyed over [[Vardos]] by the ''[[Retribution]]'' under the orders of [[First Order]] Commander [[Gideon Hask]] in an attempt to kill [[Zay Versio]], Iden and Del's daughter.

==Characteristics==
The ''Corvus'', as an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Raider II-class corvette|''Raider II''-class corvette]],<ref name="Databank" /> was a 150 [[meter]]-long assault [[starship]].<ref name="Inferno Squad" />

Before it was handed over to [[Inferno Squad]], it had been gutted and installed with state-of-the-art [[computer]] systems.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />

It originally only required two [[pilot]]s, with the Squad all taking turns at the controls.<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> Eventually, however, the ship was able to be piloted by only one person.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==History==
===Galactic Civil War===
{{Quote|You'll take it on your next mission. And any other missions when it is an asset rather than a liability. And of course, as time goes on, we'll get you a full crew.|[[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]]|<ref name="Inferno Squad" />}}
[[File:SWZ_Epic_Battles.png|thumb|left|250px|The ''Corvus'' engages with Alliance forces in the debris field over Endor.]]
Following the successful completion of their first assignment, the starship was assigned to Inferno [[Squad]] for use on missions when the ship would be an asset rather than a liability.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />

The ship was present throughout many of Inferno Squad's missions, including during the [[Escape from the Invincible Faith|escape from the ''Invincible Faith'']], the [[Battle of Endor]], and the [[Attack on Fondor]]. During the [[Skirmish on Vardos]], two of Inferno Squad's members, Versio and Meeko, defected from the Empire, taking the ''Corvus'' along with a group of [[refugee]]s from Vardos. After fleeing the Imperials, some of the ''Corvus''{{'s}} crew chose to stay with the defectors, and protected the Vardos refugees from those who chose not to.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

[[File:CorvusJakku-BTCG.png|thumb|right|270px|The ''Corvus'' participated in the Battle of Jakku.]]
After the defection of Agent Del Meeko and Commander [[Iden Versio]], the ''Corvus'' was handed over to the [[New Republic]] [[Inferno Squad (Resistance)|operation]], upon which the ship was later given a paint job with New Republic livery and heavily retrofitted. The [[Solar collector|solar panels]] on its sides were removed and its [[engine]]s replaced.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

===Cold war===
{{Quote|Make sure you bury Del Meeko and his crew... but leave the ''Corvus'' alone.''"''<br />''"''You know it will lead her back here.''"''<br />''"''I'm planning on it.|Gideon Hask and Gleb|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
[[File:BF2_Battle_of_Vardos.png|thumb|left|250px|The ''Corvus'' is shot down]]
During the [[time]] of the [[First Order]], the ship was captured on the [[planet]] [[Pillio]] by [[Jinata Security]] forces. [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] was in search of [[Del Meeko]] who was on board the ship. After killing Meeko's crew and interrogating him on the location of [[Lor San Tekka]], Ren turned the former Imperial over to Gideon Hask, Meeko's former squadmate who executed him on the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of the ''Corvus''. Hask then instructed Protectorate [[Gleb]] to leave the ship on the planet in order to lure Versio into a trap.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

Sometime later, it was picked up by Shriv Suurgav's scanners while he was investigating [[Project Resurrection]] for the [[Resistance]]. Not knowing what happened to Meeko, Iden, Zay, and Shriv inspected the ship's logs and found that it had previously visited the planet of [[Athulla]]. With the logs being their only lead, the trio decided to travel to the planet on the ''Corvus'' in order to learn the truth of Meeko's disappearance. Shortly after arriving, a Jinata Security fleet appeared. After a short battle between the two forces, the ''Corvus'' flew to Vardos where it was later shot down and destroyed by the ''[[Retribution]]'' in an attempt to kill Zay.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
