The '''Cina system''' was a [[star system]] in [[the galaxy]] that was the site of a [[spaceport]] built into an [[asteroid]]. During the first [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] sought passage from the Cina system to the [[planet]] [[Janix]]. While at the spaceport, a [[Unidentified human (Cina system)|human]] welcomed Maul aboard their [[Starship|ship]] to offer the [[Zabrak]] clandestine passage to Janix. While showcasing their ship's capabilities, Maul stated that he would take the ship before [[Death|murdering]] the individual, commandeering their vessel, and leaving the Cina system.<ref name="Shadow of Maul 1" />
