'''''Club Penguin Star Wars Rebels'' Takeover''' '''Takeover''' was an event held within [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] web-based children's [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online game|MMO]] [[Wikipedia:Virtual world|virtual world]], ''[[Club Penguin]]''. It began on [[January 22]] and ended on [[February 4]], [[2015]].<ref name="trailer" /> The event was initially announced on [[November 26]], [[2014]] in an official blog post.<ref name="website">{{ClubPenguin|url=blog/2014/11/guess-whats-coming-club-penguin-january-2015|text=Guess What's Coming to Club Penguin in January 2015|nolive=1}}</ref>

Players had to complete tasks to earn exclusive clothing items. Players with a membership subscription could receive a lightsaber after completing every tasks, wielding it, they could [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] against other players wielding a lightsaber and challenge [[the Grand Inquisitor]] to a [[lightsaber]] duel to earn more exclusive items. Some special features for the event were locked without a membership subscription. The storyline was heavily based on the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''.<ref name="Gameplay">{{YouTube|video=Lt5-hL_nFWs|text=Club Penguin: Star Wars Rebels Takeover|channel=@EthanGamerTV|name=EthanGamer|date=2015-02-03}}</ref>

With this being the second ''Star Wars'' event, some clothing items from [[Club Penguin Star Wars Takeover|''Club Penguin Star Wars'' Takeover]] returned and were available to purchase once again via the in-game shop.<ref name="Penguin Catalog">{{WebCite|url=https://clubpenguinmemories.com/2015/01/club-penguin-january-2015-penguin-style-released|text=Club Penguin January 2015 Penguin Style Released|work=Club Penguin Memories|archivedate=20230224195838}}</ref>

==Plot summary==
[[File:KananIntroCP.jpg|thumb|225x225px|Kanan introduces himself and invites players to join them against the Empire.]]
[[Kanan Jarrus]] introduced himself and asked if the players would like to join the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebels]] to fight against the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]]. After players accepted the invitation, they were give a mission tab that they could check for available missions given to them by the members of the [[Spectres]]. After completing each mission, players will be given a special reward. However, players with an active membership subscription were given extra rewards.<ref name="Gameplay" />

The first mission was given by [[Sabine Wren]], she tasked players to throw snowballs onto 8 Imperial posters. When players threw a snowballs onto an Imperial poster, the snowball turned into a Rebel mural. All players that completed the task were given [[Sabine Wren's airbrushes|Sabine's airbrush]] that they could wear as an accessory, while players with an active membership subscription also got [[Sabine Wren's armor|Sabine's armor]] and [[Sabine Wren's modified Nite Owl helmet|helmet]].<ref name="Gameplay" />

For the second mission, players met up with [[Ezra Bridger]], he stated that he know how it feels to have to prove himself to the crew. Ezra asked the players to retrieve 10 boxes of [[meiloorun fruit]] that the Empire stole. The boxes were scattered around the island. After players had retrieved all 10 boxes, Ezra sent a message that they need to get the food to the citizens before those "[[Stormtrooper|bucketheads]]" noticed anything. Kanan jumped in to say that the player still need to finish two more missions, then he'll notify on what to do next. All players were given [[Energy slingshot|Ezra's slingshot]] as a reward, while players with an active membership subscription also got Ezra's outfit and a [[wig]] that looked like his [[hair]].<ref name="Gameplay" />

Right after that, [[Garazeb Orrelios]] introduced himself and gave players the third mission. He told the player to destroy Imperial defense towers by throwing snowballs at it. There were three defense towers on the island. Two snowballs was enough to destroy a single defense tower. After all three Imperial defense towers got destroyed, Zeb said that the ''[[Ghost]]'' should have no problem flying. As a reward, all players got a flag with the [[Starbird (symbol)|phoenix crest]] on it that could be used as an accessory, while players with an active membership subscription also got a costume that resembled Zeb.<ref name="Gameplay" />[[File:Join_the_Crew_Background_photo.png|thumb|250x250px|A picture of the Spectres for the player card background. This was also the only time Chopper was depicted as an astromech.|left]][[Hera Syndulla]] introduced herself and complemented the player for being brave, she then asked the players to collect droid batteries for [[C1-10P|Chopper]]. Players needed to collect eight droid batteries. Similar to Ezra's mission, the batteries were scattered throughout the island. After collected all eight droid batteries, Hera told the player that she had a good feeling about them. All players were then given a hydrospanner as a reward, while players with an active membership subscription also got Hera's outfit and a headpiece that mimicked her [[lekku]].<ref name="Gameplay" />

For the last mission, the player needed to rescue Kanan from the Empire. After accepting the mission, the player was then teleported to a [[Sovereign|prison ship]]. Inside the ship, the player saw that Kanan had been captured inside a cell, he then told the player that it's a trap and to throw snowball at the oncoming stormtroopers. There were three [[blast door]]s with stormtroopers coming from it. The left blast door opened first, there were five oncoming stormtroopers from it. After the player took out all five stormtroopers from the first blast doors, [[the Grand Inquisitor]] appeared briefly to announce that the Rebel ends there shortly before the right blast door opened. Similar to first blast, the right blast door also consisted of five stormtroopers. This time, after taking out all five stormtrooper, all three blast doors opened. Both left and right blast doors had five stormtroopers each, but the middle blast door had six. After the player took down all stormtroopers, the cell that held Kanan was unlocked. Kanan told the player that they really proved themselves, he added that they need to get out of there before the Grand Inquisitor comes down in person. As a reward, all players were given a special background for their player card, while players with an active membership subscription also got a [[Color|blue]] [[lightsaber]], Kanan's outfit, and a wig that resembled his hair and facial hair.<ref name="Gameplay" />

[[File:Mission_Tab.png|thumb|230x230px|The two progression bars along with the rule of the lightsaber duel in the mission tab.]]
Players with an active membership subscription got an exclusive gameplay and an extra progression bar to unlock even more special items. With the obtained lightsaber from Kanan, players could [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] other each other and the [[Grand Inquisitor]]. The duel system worked similarly to the game of ''rock, paper, scissor''. The progress bar would add regardless if the player won or not. There are six checkpoints in the progression bar that the player could collect the prizes from. The items are a [[614-AvA speeder bike|614-AvA]] [[speeder bike]] as an igloo furniture, a purple lightsaber, [[Blurrg-1120 holdout blaster|Hera's blaster]], dual lightsaber, an igloo design based on the ''Ghost'', and a costume that resembled the Grand Inquisitor along with his [[The Grand Inquisitor's lightsaber|lightsaber]].<ref name="Gameplay" />

==Gameplay==
In this event, many familiar characters from ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' made their first appearance in ''[[Club Penguin]]''. All members of the [[Spectres]] were present as a penguin. [[The Grand Inquisitor]] also made his first appearance in the game as the main villain of the event.<ref name="Gameplay" /> Side characters from the show like [[Alexsandr Kallus]], [[Maketh Tua]], and [[Cikatro Vizago]] were also present during the event, but they didn't take part in the story. Instead, they made their appearances as costumes for players with membership subscription to purchase via the in-game shop, although they were still fully depicted as penguins in the catalog.<ref name="Penguin Catalog" />

[[File:CPMap_Lothal.png|thumb|left|275px|The ''Club Penguin'' island was redesigned to look like Lothal throughout the event.]]
During the event, the ''Club Penguin'' island was redesigned to look like [[Lothal]]. Along with it, Imperial bases, housings, and Lothal architectures were also added as decorations. Many familiar vehicles from the show were also scattered around as a decoration piece.<ref name="Gameplay" />

Similar to [[Club Penguin Star Wars Takeover|''Club Penguin Star Wars'' Takeover]], the [[lightsaber]] [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] system was brought back for players with membership subscription. Players were given the chance to duel against the [[Grand Inquisitor]].<ref name="BTS Sneak Peek">{{ClubPenguinYouTube|video=whbW3-TYRM8|text=Star Wars Rebels Takeover - Behind the Scenes Sneak Peek|nolive=1}}</ref>

The storyline of the event was heavily based on the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]] of ''Star Wars Rebels''. Most of the missions were based on [[Mission in Capital City]], but the final mission was based on [[Action at Mustafar]].<ref name="Gameplay" />

The gameplay was mainly a point and click, it was fairly simple and intended for a younger audience to experience. The missions had players exploring around the island to find and do specific things depending on the active mission they were on. With each completion, players were given a special exclusive rewards, although players with an active membership subscription got an extra reward.

The event also had an exclusive gameplay only for players with an active membership subscription, it was the lightsaber duel. The exclusive game mode worked similarly to a game of ''[[Wikipedia:Rock paper scissors|rock paper scissors]]''. Players could duel other people with a membership or challenge the Grand Inquisitor to progress and get even more exclusive rewards.

==Continuity==
*Two weeks before the party started, the ''[[Ghost]]'' became visible in the [[telescope]] at the [[W:c:clubpenguin:Lighthouse|lighthouse]]. Then it became closer the nearer the event became.<ref name="Trivia 0">{{WebCite|url=http://archives.clubpenguinwiki.info/static/images/archives/8/83/StarWarsRebelsTakeoverTelescopeEmpty.swf|text=StarWarsRebelsTakeoverTelescopeEmpty(.swf)|work=Club Penguin Archives|archivedate=20171109224501|nolive=1}} on the [[W:c:clubpenguin|Club Penguin Wiki]]</ref>
*Although he was not present in the story or event, [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] appeared as a logo for the [[W:c:clubpenguin:Coffee Shop|coffee shop]] called Javva the Hutt. "Javva the Hutt" was also a word-play on [[Wikipedia:Java|Java]], an island in [[Wikipedia:Indonesia|Indonesia]] popular for its [[Caf|coffee]] and Jabba's title as a Hutt, Jabba the Hutt.<ref name="Trivia 1">{{ClubPenguin|url=blog/2015/01/star-wars-rebels-takeover-now|text=Star Wars Rebels Takeover - On Now!|nolive=1}}</ref>
*Similarly, [[Maximilian Rebo|Max Rebo]] was also not present in the story or the event, but his name was used as the name of the [[W:c:clubpenguin:Night Club|night club]] called Rebo Nights.<ref name="Trivia 1" />
*Despite being mentioned in the story, the only time [[C1-10P|Chopper]] appeared as an [[Astromech droid|astromech]] was in the [[W:c:clubpenguin:Player Card|player card]] [[W:c:clubpenguin:Backgrounds|background]] obtained through the completion of the event.<ref name="Trivia 2">{{ClubPenguin|subdomain=media1|url=play/v2/content/global/clothing/photos/9291.swf|text=Join the Crew Background (.swf)|nolive=1}}</ref>
*At the [[W:c:clubpenguin:Dock|dock]], there was a hidden message in [[Aurebesh]], when translated, it read, "''There are more mysteries to be seen. Keep an eye on the wilds.''" It was soon revealed that it was a hint for an upcoming event in ''[[Club Penguin]]''.<ref name="Trivia 3">{{WebCite|url=https://clubpenguinmemories.com/2015/01/secret-club-penguin-message-decoded-there-are-more-mysteries-to-be-seen|text=Secret Club Penguin Message Decoded: There Are More Mysteries To Be Seen|work=Club Penguin Memories|archivedate=20230220173843}}</ref>
