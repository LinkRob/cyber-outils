'''Epaulets''' were a a type of ornament worn on the shoulders of [[Uniform|military uniforms]], typically worn by officers. As of [[14 BBY]], most Imperial military officers' tunics did not have epaulets.<ref name="Tarkin" /> Exceptions to this were the [[Grand Admiral's uniform|uniforms]] of [[Grand Admiral]]s, which bore gold epaulets,<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref> and the uniforms of both [[Imperial Security Bureau|ISB]] [[Major]] [[Lio Partagaz|Partagaz]] and [[Colonel]] [[Wullf Yularen]], which bore silver epaulets.<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> For formal gatherings, ISB personnel like [[ISB Supervisor]]s and [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]] wore silver epaulets as well.<ref name="WAFE">{{Andor|What a Festive Evening}}</ref> Additionally, [[Imperial Army|Army]] [[Commandant]] [[Jayhold Beehaz]]'s ceremonial uniform bore silver epaulets.<ref name="The Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref>

[[General]] [[Yiv]] the Benevolent of the [[Nikardun Destiny]] wore [[symbiont]]s on his shoulders which were likened to epaulets.<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref>
