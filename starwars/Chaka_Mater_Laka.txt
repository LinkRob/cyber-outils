'''Chaka Mater Laka''' was a [[musician]] who performed in the<ref name="R3X's Playlist" /> [[Huttese]]<ref name="Discord">{{WookDiscord|discordname=.holocronkeeper|name=Leland Chee|date=2022-04-29|messagelink=https://discord.com/channels/397848917036761090/834861729061142569/969632333147807875|quote=My Star Wars Karaoke song list.|archivefile=File:LelandChee-SongList-Discord.png}}</ref> [[Music|song]] "[[Batuu Boogie]]" alongside [[MW-59]].<ref name="R3X's Playlist" /> The duo's song was featured by the [[droid]] [[DJ (occupation)|DJ]] [[R-3X]] in [[Oga's Cantina]], which was located in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]],<ref name="Cantina">{{GEAttr|Oga's Cantina}}</ref> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE Date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The [[cantina]] playlist was broadcast over the local radio station, [[BSO 401.72]],<ref name="Radio">{{GEAttr|BSO 401.72}}</ref> which could be heard in the [[refresher]]s near the [[Droid Depot]] store,<ref name="Service Yard">{{GEAttr|Service Yard}}</ref> inside the [[Black Spire Station]] garage,<ref name="Garage">{{GEAttr|Black Spire Station}}</ref> inside the [[Black Spire Outpost Market]],<ref name="Market">{{GEAttr|Black Spire Outpost Market}}</ref> and inside [[Docking Bay 5]] at the local [[Spaceport (Batuu)|spaceport]].<ref name="Falcon">{{GEAttr|Millennium Falcon: Smuggler's Run|''Millennium Falcon'': Smuggler's Run}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:R3Xs_Playlist_1.jpg|thumb|right|220px|The cover of ''Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge Oga's Cantina: R3X's Playlist #1'']]
Chaka Mater Laka's musical recording "Batuu Boogie" first appeared in the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="Cantina" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The track is used in the Oga's Cantina bar<ref name="Cantina" /> and included in the background audio for the restrooms near Droid Depot,<ref name="Service Yard" /> Black Spire Station,<ref name="Garage" /> the market,<ref name="Market" /> and in the stand-by queue for the attraction [[Millennium Falcon: Smuggler's Run|''Millennium Falcon'': Smuggler's Run]].<ref name="Falcon" /> The [[in-universe]] artists behind the track were not revealed, however, until the release of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge - Oga's Cantina: R-3X's Playlist 1|''Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge - Oga's Cantina: R-3X's Playlist #1'']],<ref name="R3X's Playlist" /> a digital album released on [[September 6]], 2019.<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galaxys-edge-soundtrack|text=Listen to a Track from the ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge Soundtrack}}</ref> The song was written by [[Jason Mater]], whose surname is used in the name of the in-universe artist. [[Matthew Wood]] and [[Ben Burtt]] were consulted for the alien languages used throughout the cantina playlist.<ref name="D23 panel">{{YouTube|video=pWWm8kUK-nc|text=D23 Expo <nowiki>|</nowiki> 2019 <nowiki>|</nowiki> Star Wars <nowiki>|</nowiki> Music and Sounds <nowiki>|</nowiki> Galaxy's Edge <nowiki>|</nowiki> Walt Disney World <nowiki>|</nowiki> Disneyland|channel=@SamsDisneyDiary|name=Sam's Disney Diary|date=2019-11-10}}</ref>
