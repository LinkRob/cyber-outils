'''Abhean''' was an astronomical object<ref name="R1">{{SW|url=news/where-in-the-galaxy-are-the-worlds-of-rogue-one|text=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Rogue One''?|int=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Rogue One?}}</ref> located in the [[Abhean system]] of the [[Maldrood sector]],<ref name="Appendix" /> a part of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="TFABG" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It lay in grid square R-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="TFABG" /> and the [[super-hyperroute]]<ref name="BMF 48">{{BuildFalconCite|48|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> known as the [[Perlemian Trade Route]] connected it to the [[Besh Gorgon system|Besh Gorgon]] and [[Shantipole system]]s.<ref name="TFABG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Abhean's name was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' boxed set.<ref name="TFABG" /> It was then identified as a distinct celestial body in the [[January 31]], [[2017]] [[StarWars.com]] article "[[Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Rogue One?|Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Rogue One''?]]" by [[Mark Newbold]].<ref name="R1" /> The [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Abhean/Legends|Abhean]]<ref name="CSWE">{{CSWECite|I|6|Abhean}}</ref> was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2002]] ''[[Power of the Jedi Sourcebook]]'', which was published by [[Wizards of the Coast]] for use with the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="POTJ">''[[Power of the Jedi Sourcebook]]''</ref>
