'''Bo Bahaahaa''' was a [[Gotal]] who, along with [[Klen Hoofber]], was in a standoff with [[IG-88B|IG-88]]. IG-88 claimed to have a [[bounty]] on Ba Bohaahaa, mispronouncing the Gotal's name to mislead Hoofber, who believed that in betraying and [[stun]]ning Bahaahaa would share the bounty with IG-88.<ref name="A Rebel Bounty" />
