A '''dry eel''' was a type of [[eel]] [[Diet|edible]] to [[Quarren]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], some of the eels were stored aboard [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Orlen Mollo]]'s [[Alif-class Longbeam|''Alif''-class Longbeam]]&mdash;the ''[[Paxion]]''&mdash;for him to consume.<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> while hosting a [[Summit aboard the Paxion|peace summit]] aboard the ''Paxion'' in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Eiram system]], the [[protocol droid]] [[AR-K4]] offered Mollo a bowl of dry eels, which he took from her and devoured.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dry eels appeared in [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], a [[2022]] novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Convergence" />
