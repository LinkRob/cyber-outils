'''Crystalline earrings''' were [[Piercing|earrings]] decorated with small [[crystal]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a pair of crystalline earrings could be sold for 120 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crystalline earrings appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. In the game, the ring could only be sold for credits, and did not have another function.<ref name="Outlaws" />
