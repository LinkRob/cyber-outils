'''Blackout''' was a [[Special ops clone trooper|special ops]] [[Clone Commander]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]], he led the [[clone trooper]] crew of an [[IPV-2C Stealth Corvette]] on a [[Battle of Christophsis|relief mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Christophsis]] in [[22 BBY]]. Before their departure, Blackout greeted replacement trooper, [[Spark]], and showed him to his work station. On board the corvette, Blackout and Spark helped Skywalker bypass the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist's]] [[Christophsis blockade|blockade]] over the planet by utilizing the ship's cloaking technology.

When Separatist Admiral [[Trench]] ordered a bombing run on [[Senator]] [[Bail Organa's Christophsis refugee camp|Bail Organa's refugee camp]], Skywalker opted to cripple the blockade, and Blackout complied with his altered objective. Spark was surprised by their new orders, but the commander assured him that such drastic change was not out of the ordinary for the maverick [[Jedi]]. Blackout and his crew aided Skywalker in the destruction of [[Invincible|Trench's flagship]], and the corvette finally delivered the supplies to Organa's camp. At the conclusion of their mission, Blackout rewarded Spark's efforts aboard the ship with induction into his crew.

==Biography==
{{Quote|I thought we were on a relief mission?''"<br />"''You learn pretty quick that when you serve under General Skywalker, you're always on the offensive. Now get ready to cycle those shields.|Spark and Blackout|<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />}}
[[File:BlockadeChristophsis.png|thumb|left|250px|Republic Attack cruisers attempt to break the Separatist blockade of Christophsis.]]
The [[special ops clone trooper]]<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> who adopted the name Blackout<ref name="Cat and Mouse Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/cat-and-mouse-episode-guide|text="Cat and Mouse" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> was a [[Cloning|clone]] of the [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]], born and raised on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]]. He was created to serve in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]], a conflict between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="CT DB" /> and acquired the rank of [[Clone Commander]]. In the [[Standard year|year]] [[22 BBY]], in the midst of the [[Battle of Christophsis]], Blackout was present aboard the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Negotiator]]''. There, he led the [[clone trooper]] crew that manned the Republic's experimental [[IPV-2C Stealth Corvette]] and served under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]]. Skywalker and the crew were tasked with sneaking past a Separatist [[Christophsis blockade|blockade]] in order to deliver vital supplies to [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], on the surface of the [[planet]] [[Christophsis]]. The blockade was led by Admiral [[Trench]] aboard his [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]], the ''[[Invincible]]'', who had re-emerged after his presumed death at the [[Battle of Malastare Narrows]]. Before they departed in the corvette, enthusiastic newcomer [[Spark]] arrived at the ship's door and was greeted by Blackout. The commander told Spark that he would be fulfilling the role of one of their "starting line-up," a trooper who was unable to partake in the mission. Blackout positioned the rookie's station across from his, so that he could keep track of his progress.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />

[[File:Stealth_ship_cloaking.png|thumb|right|250px|The IPV-2C initiates a cloak.]]
Not long after taking off, Skywalker narrowly avoided collision with a contingent of [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class Droid Bombers]] headed to [[Bail Organa's Christophsis refugee camp]]. In response to Trench's cruelty, Skywalker decided to divert from the mission's primary objective and engage the admiral in a deadly game of "cat and mouse." A confused Spark was assured by Blackout that this was the norm for the ever-offensive Skywalker, implying that the commander had experienced the [[Jedi]]'s style before. He and Spark locked on to the ''Invincible'', and Skywalker fired a volley of torpedoes at the ship. They were dispatched by the ship's [[thermal shield]]s, but the corvette re-cloaked and avoided Trench's immediate retaliatory missiles. A further spread of laser fire was also avoided, with Blackout and the rest of the crew visibly relieved at Skywalker's evasion. Following Skywalker's attack and escape, Trench sent a message over the open frequency that was picked up by Spark's station. Blackout asked the rookie about the notification, who told him that the transmission was coming from their enemy. On the commander's advice, Spark notified Skywalker of the message, and Trench's hologram was quickly displayed throughout the corvette. The message appeared to disturb the crew, as the [[Harch]] admiral promised that their allies on the planet would die and warned Skywalker that he had fought against ships with cloaking devices before.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />

[[File:StealthShipTargetsInvincible.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Skywalker flies the corvette towards the ''Invincible''{{'s}} bridge, followed by a barrage of the dreadnought's own torpedos.]]
After deducing that Trench planned to use [[tracking torpedo]]es to lock onto the corvette's magnetic signature, Skywalker de-cloaked the corvette and attacked the ''Invincible'' once more. The corvette re-cloaked shortly thereafter and Trench was forced to lower his ship's shields to launch a counterattack. Blackout informed Skywalker that their cloak was successful and that the ''Invincible'' had locked on to the corvette's magnetic signature. The Jedi, pleased that the admiral's arrogance had gotten the better of him, flew the corvette directly towards the ''Invincible''{{'s}} bridge and used Trench's tracking torpedoes to cripple it. With Trench presumed dead and the ''Invincible'' left destroyed, the crew of the corvette celebrated and landed on the surface of Christophsis. After they had fulfilled their original mission, Blackout congratulated Spark on his work aboard the corvette and officially inducted him into his crew.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Good job, kid. Looks like you're now officially part of the crew.''"<br />"''Thanks, commander.|Blackout and Spark|<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />}}
Like all clones of the human male Jango Fett, Blackout stood at 1.83 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="CT DB" /> He was a stern, yet compassionate commanding [[officer]] who took Spark under his wing with a watchful eye. He guided the rookie in an effort to make sure he was up to speed with the rest of the corvette's crew, and congratulated him with induction after their successful mission under General Skywalker.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|General, cloaking device is active but they're locked on to our magnetic signature!|Blackout, to Skywalker|<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />}}
As a special ops trooper, Blackout was trained to operate stealthily and detect enemies at a distance.<ref name="CT Armor">{{Databank|clone-trooper-armor|Clone Trooper Armor}}</ref> He was also fully capable of serving dutifully aboard an IPV-2C Stealth Corvette, whilst performing his service as a Clone Commander. Alongside Spark, he managed the ship's torpedo output and quickly notified Skywalker of the ''Invincible''{{'s}} lock-on of their magnetic signature.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />

==Equipment==
Blackout was equipped with armor that befitted a special ops clone trooper. His helmet featured red-patterned rings on its side and was also decorated with [[Jaig Eyes]],<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> a battle honor given to those that had shown bravery on the field.<ref name="Updated and Expanded">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref>
