The '''Beskar Destroyer''' was an [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]] owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Prototype E-Web|standard E-Web]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Beskar Destroyer was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Sentinel's Prototype E-Web, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 0.20.0 update during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Beskar Buckethead]]. This costume could be unlocked by reaching the final rank, Beskar I, within ''Hunter's'' "Ranked" mode during both the early access season it released with, as well as Season 3 following the game's global launch.<ref name="Hunters" />
