'''''Doctor Aphra'' 4''' is the fourth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. It was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Marika Cresta]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[September 30]], [[2020]]. It features the story "'''Fortune and Fate Part 4: The Deal'''," in which [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] and company make their escape from [[Vaale]], and [[Just Lucky]] strikes a deal with [[Ronen Tagge]].

==Publisher's summary==
''RING OF FIRE! Trapped and betrayed, DOCTOR APHRA fights to escape the cursed city of VAALE before madness overcomes her! Meanwhile, RONEN TAGGE's sinister plan for the RINGS OF VAALE approaches fruition. Will Aphra be able to thwart him in time?''

==Plot summary==
Trapped in the catacombs of [[Vaale]] by [[Just Lucky]] and her crew, [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Doctor Aphra]] vows a painful revenge. Lucky, having secured the [[Rings of Vaale]], justifies her betrayal as a simple act of survival. While [[Krrsantan]] tries to smash through the blocked entrance, Aphra's team searches for another way out, eventually escaping through a garbage chute into the city's labyrinthine sewers.

As they navigate the pulsing sewer walls, [[Eustacia Okka|Doctor Okka]] reveals a stunning discovery: the city itself is a living organism. Its bone-like structure emits a "song"—a frequency that targets brain waves, inducing madness. The legendary insanity of Vaale wasn't caused by the rings, but by a rogue security system that grew, mutated, and now affects them. They escape to the surface just in time to see the entire ancient city obliterated by ships commanded by [[Domina Tagge]]. Aphra grimly notes, "Whatever Tagge can't have, he destroys."

Meanwhile, Lucky's crew celebrates their score, though the ring continues to emit a strange sound even in space. Their victory is short-lived. On [[Canto Bight]], Tagge is displeased, noting they brought only one of the two rings and failed to capture Doctor Okka. He cuts their pay by a third and delivers a veiled threat against Lucky’s brother before dismissing them to the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|Canto Casino]].

Amid the ruins of Vaale, Aphra's team finds a functioning [[High Republic Era|High Republic]] ship. Aphra deduces that since Tagge only has one ring, the other must not have been in the city when it was destroyed. With a new plan in mind, she hails Tagge's fleet, provokes a chase, and allows her ship to be captured and boarded. As Tagge greets his new prisoners on Canto Bight, Aphra privately hopes her risky gambit pays off.
