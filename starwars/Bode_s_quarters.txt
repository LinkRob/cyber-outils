{{Quote|The intruder is in my quarters!|Bode Akuna alerts II-0810 Satellite Station's security about Cal Kestis|<ref name="Jedi Survivor" />}} '''Bode's quarters''' were the living quarters of [[Bode Akuna]] and his [[reproduction|daughter]], [[Kata Akuna]], within the [[Officer's Quarters]] on the [[Imperial Security Bureau|Imperial Security Bureau's]] [[II-0810 Satellite Station]] in the [[Nova Garon system]]. The station's [[Chief supervisor]], [[Commander]] [[Lank Denvik]], provided the quarters to the Akunas, keeping former [[Jedi]] and his daugter hidden from the rest of the [[Galactic Empire]], in exchange for Bode's continued usefulness on clandestine missions. In [[9 BBY]], [[Cal Kestis]], a fellow Jedi [[Order 66|survivor]], [[Mission to Nova Garon|made his way]] to the quarters, seeking to confront Bode, who'd [[Raid on the Archive|betrayed]] him and his allies. He found Kata instead, though Bode arrived shortly thereafter, explaining the reasoning behind both his betrayal and his occupancy of the quarters. The Akunas then fled the room, with Bode alerting the station's security to Kestis' presence within them as he left. 

==Description==
[[File:BodesQuartersKata.png|thumb|left|300px|Kata Akuna had constructed a pillow fort in one corner of Bode's quarters by the time Cal Kestis met her and her toy Mookie there in 9 BBY]]
Bode's quarters<ref name="Art of Survivor">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> were located within the wider Officer's Quarters on the Imperial Security Bureau's II-0810 Satellite Station listening post in the Nova Garon system. The two large windows that ran down either side of the quarters looked out onto the pale frozen crystalline [[asteroid]]s of the [[star system|system]], as well as the base's exterior. A large white [[holotable]] sat in the center of the room. The upholstery in Bode's quarters was [[bronze]]-[[color|colored]], while the rest of the room was largely white and gray.<ref name="Jedi Survivor" />

By Cal Kestis' arrival in 9 BBY,<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> Kata Akuna had constructed a [[pillow]] [[fortress|fort]] in one corner of the room, whilst the table in the sitting area in another corner lay cluttered. Several shelving units existed on the far wall opposite the door that led into the wider station, and next to another door from whence Kata emerged when Kestis first met her. The quarters also contained an audio message from Bode to Kata, a collection of data from Bode's undercover mission, and a family portrait of Bode, Kata, and [[Tayala Akuna]].<ref name="Jedi Survivor" />

==History==
{{Quote|Kata. Did your dad tell you where he was taking you?''"<br />"''It was supposed to be a surprise. But you got here a little sooner than I expected.|Cal Kestis to Kata Akuna, and Bode Akuna's reply|<ref name="Jedi Survivor" />}}
[[File:BodesQuartersCalKhri.png|thumb|right|300px|Cal Kestis and BD-1 watch a holorecording of Santari Khri in Bode's quarters]]
In 9 BBY, the Jedi survivor Cal Kestis made his way to Bode's quarters, intent upon confronting its resident, who had recently betrayed him. Upon arriving with his [[droid]], [[BD-1]], he found and retrieved a [[Holomessage|recording]] of [[High Republic Era]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Santari Khri]] upon a [[datacard|datadisc]] in the holotable in the center of the room, recalling aloud having witnessed [[Dark side of the Force|fallen]] Jedi [[Dagan Gera]] viewing the holomessage not long ago.  Gera was a contemporary of Khri's who had recently been [[Mission to the Forest Array|reawakened]] from two [[Standard century|centuries]] of slumber within a [[bacta tank]], and had just [[Battle at the Koboh Observatory|died in battle]] against Kestis.<ref name="Jedi Survivor" />

Suddenly, a door behind Kestis opened, prompting him to spin toward the potential threat and grab [[Cal Kestis' second lightsaber|his]] [[lightsaber]]. When he saw it was Kata Akuna, however, he relaxed, apologizing and introducing himself. Kata responded in kind, and when asked where her dad was, she simply said that he'd told her to pack for a trip. The young girl then inquired as to whether Kestis actually worked on the [[space station|station]], seeing though the [[Imperial officer's uniform]] he wore, which had been stolen from the base's commander, Lank Denvik. Kestis admitted as such and praised her perceptiveness, again answering truthfully when, looking at his lightsaber, she asked if he was a Jedi. Kata then returned to packing, searching the quarters for her [[Mookie]] [[doll]], which she declared she couldn't find.<ref name="Jedi Survivor" />

Kestis and BD helped in her search, finding Mookie under the holotable and handing him back to her. The ginger Jedi joked that the orange-maned doll somewhat resembled him, but Kata was unconvinced and solemnly told him that her mother had given her the toy. With the change in mood, Kestis leaned down and again asked if Bode had shared his intended destination with her, only for the doors behind them to slide open and the man himself to announce his presence by answering that he'd wanted it to be a surprise, but that Kestis had arrived sooner than expected.<ref name="Jedi Survivor" />

Kata ran over to her father when called for, asking what was going on. Bode explained that Kestis was his best friend, and downplayed his recent betrayal on [[Jedha]], which had led to the [[death]]s of Jedi Masters [[Eno Cordova]] and [[Cere Junda]], as well as numerous [[Narkis Anchorite]]s, the destruction of much of the [[The Archive|remains]] of the [[Jedi Archives]], and Akuna's theft of the last remaining [[Abyss compass|compass]] though the [[Koboh Abyss]] to [[Tanalorr]], as a mere fight which could be settled. Kestis let go of his lightsaber, and the Akunas went to sit on the quarters' couch.<ref name="Jedi Survivor" />

Bode confirmed for Kestis that he was once a Jedi, and explained that he'd survived the [[Great Jedi Purge]] by using skills he'd learned in [[Republic Intelligence]], where he'd been assigned by the [[Jedi High Council]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. He shared that he'd gone into hiding, unsure of who to trust, until meeting Kata's mother, Tayala, but that one day whilst out running errand with his daughter, he'd received a message not to return home. Kestis vocalized his understanding that [[Inquisitor]]s, Imperial Jedi hunters with whom he'd had his own run deadly encounters, had found the Akunas, and Bode nodded. Voice heavy with emotion, Bode hugged his daughter as he revealed his [[marriage|wife]] had sacrificed herself to buy her [[Akuna family|family]] time, and Kestis sat down at the table.<ref name="Jedi Survivor" />

Bode expounded that, believing he couldn't run forever, he had turned to ISB officer Lank Denvik, who he'd worked with and been a protege of during the Clone Wars. Denvik would keep Bode and Kata hidden, furnishing them with the quarters in which they now sat, so long as his former colleague remained useful by providing clandestine services, such as his infiltration of the [[Stinger Mantis crew]] and betrayal of [[Hidden Path]] on Jedha. Kestis was dumbstruck that he'd been lied to from the start, but Bode argued his betrayal was justified as Kestis was going to give access to the protected world of Tanalorr to the Hidden Path when it could have been a way out for just both their loved ones instead. At this, Kestis grew angry, calling Bode a monster, but as he stood with his daughter, Bode replied that he was actually a father.<ref name="Jedi Survivor" />

Standing and putting Kata's [[backpack]] on, he opined that Kestis couldn't understand the depths of his love for his daughter. Kestis retorted that he did understand betrayal, and that he'd been lured there to take down the base, and Bode confirmed that the latter comment was accurate, commenting that fighting the Empire was what Kestis did best. Suddenly, he [[force push]]ed the table into Kestis, knowing the Jedi down as he told his daughter to run and fled from the room with her. As Kestis got back onto his feet, he heard Bode inform the base's security personnel of his location in Bode's quarters, and instruct them kill him. Following after the Akunas, Kestis went on a rampage, temporarily embracing his darkness and doing as Bode intended, slaughtering many of the base's personnel.<ref name="Jedi Survivor" />

Though the Akunas escaped in [[Bode's Z-95 Headhunter]], Kestis' companion, the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Merrin]], calmed him down, and, upon reviewing the Santari Khri's full holo-recording, he realized there was another way to achieve safe passage through the Koboh Abyss other than the sole compass in Bode's possession. Following this path, he, Merrin, and [[Greez Dritus]] pursued the Akunas to Tanalorr, where Kestis [[Duel on Tanalorr|dueled]] and killed Bode in a final confrontation.<ref name="Jedi Survivor" />

Sometime later, Kestis returned to Bode's quarters in the Nova Garon ISB listening station, coming across an old recording of a conversation from when Bode sent Cere Junda's location to Denvik, angered that the ISB commander was altering their deal. In the communication, Denvik replied that "deals change" and called the altered terms a "contingency plan". When Bode responded angrily, Denvik admonished him, then assured his operative that he would reveal the identity of the Inquisitor that killed his wife when Junda was delivered to him.<ref name="Jedi Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bode's quarters first appeared in the [[2023]] single-player video game ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'', though were only first identified as such in the [[2024]] art reference book ''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''. According to the book, Bode's quarters were inspired dwellings seen in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope]]'', invoking the [[Erso homestead|Ersos' homestead]] and [[Ersos' apartment|apartment]] in the former movie and the [[Lars homestead]] and [[Ben Kenobi's home]] from the latter. The concept art by [[Russell Story]] aimed to soften the [[Brutalist (architecture)|Imperial Brutalism]] of the ISB base with as much comfort as could be expected on an asteroid.<ref name="Art of Survivor" />
