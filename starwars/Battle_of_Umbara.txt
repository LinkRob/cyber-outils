{{Quote|Umbara was one of the major Separatist planets. The locals fought pretty hard, and got stomped pretty solidly.|Eli Vanto|audio=Vanto-Umbara.flac|<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref>}}
The '''Battle of Umbara''', also known as the '''Umbaran Campaign''', was a battle that took place during the [[Clone Wars]] on the [[planet]] [[Umbara]]. It was also the event during which the [[treason]] of [[Jedi]] [[Jedi General|General]] [[Pong Krell]] was revealed, whose ultimate goal was recognition by [[Count]] [[Dooku]] to become his new [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Onaconda and I were often at odds, but I respected him. I respected him greatly.|Mee Deechi, whose murder was followed by Umbara's withdrawal from the Republic|<ref name="Senate Murders">{{TCW|Senate Murders}}</ref>}}
[[File:CloneBriefing-Umbara.png|thumb|left|230px|Skywalker and Kenobi lead a briefing for their clone troops prior to landing.]]
Following the death of [[Umbara]]'s [[senator]], [[Mee Deechi]], by [[Lolo Purs]], the Umbarans withdrew from the Republic<ref name="Ultimate">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> and committed their advanced technology,<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|umbara|Umbara}}</ref> including that seen in their [[Umbaran Militia|militia]], to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. In part due to their control over Umbara, the Separatists were able to tighten their grip over vital and isolated supply routes throughout the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="Darkness" /> In particular, Umbara sat upon a critical supply route<ref name="Star Wars: Battles that changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> and a hyperspace route that was connected to a great many Separatist industrial worlds.<ref name="Timelines" /> Umbara was also valuable as a home for [[Doonium]] [[mining]].<ref name="Thrawn" /> In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the Republic sent [[Jedi Master]] [[Pong Krell]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] members [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Saesee Tiin]] to retake Umbara with their troops.<ref name="Darkness" />

Krell was a recognized war hero, but was known for his exceptionally high rates of troop casualties compared to other [[Jedi]].<ref name="The General" /><ref name="Plan of Dissent" /> Unbeknownst to anyone else, Krell had had a [[Force vision|vision]] of the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Great Jedi Purge|fall]] and the rise of a [[Galactic Empire|new order]], leading him to fall to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] and begin sabotaging campaigns he fought in with the aim of attracting the attention of Separatist leader [[Count]] [[Dooku]], who he hoped would reward him by making him his new [[Sith apprentice]].<ref name="Carnage of Krell" />

Though the Umbaran militia was strong and possessed technology more powerful than much of the Republic's own weapons, the Separatists deployed a sizeable fleet to the system to assist the militia in defending their home and supplying arms.<ref name="Plan of Dissent" /> As a result, the Separatist fleet established a large blockade around the planet and supplied the Umbarans with necessary arms.<ref name="Darkness" />

==The battle==
===Landings on Umbara===
{{Quote|This battalion will take the main road straight to the capital. You will not stop and you will not turn back, regardless of the resistance you meet.|General Pong Krell|<ref name="Darkness" />}}
[[File:RexSkywalker-Umbara.jpg|thumb|left|220px|Skywalker and Rex lead the 501st's charge on Umbara.]]Spearheaded by [[Z-95 Headhunter]]s led by [[Jedi Commander]]s [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Barriss Offee]], the Republic forces [[Space warfare|broke through]] the Separatist blockade, allowing the [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship]]s to reach the planet. As they made their [[Ground warfare|landing]], Kenobi spoke to Skywalker, Cody, Rex, and their troops to discuss their strategy. Masters Krell and Tiin, along with several [[battalion]]s, would support Kenobi and the [[212th Attack Battalion]] in the south, while Anakin Skywalker and the [[501st Legion]] would come in from the north and take out enemy reinforcements. The main objective was to conquer the [[Umbaran capital city]] as quickly as possible and hold it. As the [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''s]] entered the atmosphere, the ground forces were deployed in gunships protected by the Headhunters.<ref name="Darkness" />

Kenobi, along with the 212th and other Republic forces, began pushing towards the capital from the south, but was met with heavy and fierce resistance from the Umbaran Militia. The Umbarans' powerful technology, coupled with the planet's dangerous wildlife, prevented a swift capture of the heavily-fortified capital, and Kenobi's strike force was bogged down in prolonged skirmishes around the city's perimeter. This forced Kenobi to count on the 501st's support in finding an opening in the capital's defenses.<ref name="Star Wars: Battles that changed the Galaxy" /> At one point during the battle, [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Cody]] directed his troops to bring down an enemy target.<ref name="Scene to Stats">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/scene-to-stats-clone-commander-cody|text=Scene to Stats: Clone Commander Cody}}</ref>

The gunships carrying the 501st Legion deployed numerous [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport|AT-RTs]] alongside the troops. As the clones suffered heavy losses to Umbaran armored elements early into the battle, AT-RT platoons charged and successfully destroyed the Umbaran Militia's [[Umbaran hover tank|hover tanks]], clearing the way for their ground forces. Skywalker's troops then landed and moved towards a ridge, fighting the Umbarans and forcing them to retreat. However, one of the troopers was eaten by a [[vixus]], and another trooper, [[Hardcase]], had to be saved from the creature by [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Fives]]. At the ridge, the clones then entrenched themselves, digging in and resting before they would move out. However, the Umbarans ambushed them with [[Scutiger-100 stealth droid|millicreep droids]], using the natural cover provided by the shadowy terrain to kill many clones. The Umbarans then circled around the clones' encampment. As his men quickly evacuated the ridge, Skywalker called in [[clone trooper pilot]] [[Davijaan]], callsign "Odd Ball," to bombard the area with [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber|BTL-B Y-wing bombers]] to wipe out the Umbarans.<ref name="Darkness" />

[[File:OddballCC2237-SWZ.png|thumb|left|220px|Odd Ball and his wingman bomb the ridge.]]
As Skywalker's forces regrouped for the attack on the capital, Master Krell arrived with orders from the Jedi Council ordering Anakin to return to Coruscant at the [[Supreme Chancellor]]'s request. Skywalker reluctantly followed the orders and command of the 501st went to Krell. Krell then ordered the 501st to march onward towards the capital. En route, the clones came under attack by a pair of [[Banshee (species)|banshees]]. They then resumed the march after Krell killed the last one. When the 501st reached the outskirts of the capital, Krell decided to launch a full forward assault on the capital along the main road, despite Rex's objections. Their momentum was stalled by a [[mine]]field, however, which killed [[Oz]] and [[Ringo]] before Fives was able to find a way through. The 501st then came under attack by the Umbarans, holding their ground despite being exposed. Rex then ordered a retreat to rejoin Krell and his troops. Together, they were able repel the Umbarans. Krell was disappointed at Rex for not holding the road, but was ultimately convinced that Rex and Fives did what was best for their men. Rex and the 501st then fought off an Umbaran counterattack. Meanwhile, Generals Kenobi and Tiin learned of an Umbaran Airbase that was resupplying the capital's defenses.<ref name="Darkness" />

===Capturing the airbase===
{{Quote|We've gathered intel on an airbase to the West. It is resupplying the capital's defenses. If you could capture that airbase, it will sever the capital's supply lines, allowing the rest of our forces to move in.''"<br />"''I'll see to it that the airbase is placed under our control. |Obi-Wan Kenobi and General Krell|<ref name="The General">{{TCW|The General (episode)}}</ref>}}
Krell, Rex, and the 501st continued to hold their ground against the Umbarans from both their air and ground forces. Krell was then contacted by Kenobi, who ordered him and the 501st to capture an [[Umbaran airbase]] to the west that Kenobi's forces had gathered intel on. The airbase was supplying the capital with supplies that prevented the 212th from taking the capital. The 501st then regrouped and moved towards the airbase.<ref name="The General" />

[[File:UmbaranCrawlerTank-BattleOfUmbara.png|thumb|281x281px|Umbaran Weapons were a new threat to the clones]]
As the 501st reached the outskirts of the airbase, Rex suggested that they scout the area first. Krell, concerned with the urgency of Kenobi's situation, ordered the 501st to go through a gorge single file without conducting reconnaissance. The 501st troopers started to criticize Krell's tactics, knowing they would suffer heavy casualties. Despite this, Rex was able to convince the 501st to go ahead with the attack. As the 501st entered the gorge, Rex divided his troops into three groups with Fives and Hardcase. As they made their way through, they came under attack by [[Impeding Assault Tank]]s, barely able to destroy the tanks with their [[RPS-6 rocket launcher|rocket launchers]] and [[thermal detonator]]s. They resumed moving towards the airbase, until they came under fire from Umbaran [[Umbaran mobile heavy cannon|mobile heavy cannons]]. Rex and the 501st attempted to use the rocket launchers against the cannons to no avail.<ref name="The General" />

[[File:Epguide408.png|left|thumb|220x220px|General Krell and the 501st shortly after the fall of the Umbaran base]]
Rex then sent Fives and Hardcase to infiltrate the airbase and commandeer a pair of [[Zenuas 33 Umbaran starfighter]]s to use against the heavy cannons. Krell, however, ordered Rex to launch a full-scale attack, attempting to hold their ground against the mobile cannons. Meanwhile, Fives and Hardcase were able to pass the sensor fence by using a tree. After Fives blew up the tree to distract the Umbarans, he and Hardcase were able to board a pair of starfighters and used them to destroy the mobile heavy cannons. Krell and Sergeant [[Appo]] and brought in the rest of the 501st and took the airbase. The 501st then rounded up the remaining Umbarans as prisoners and held the base before moving on to taking the capital with Kenobi.<ref name="The General" />

===Attack on the supply ship===
{{Quote|Can't we destroy the supply ship?"<br />"We're trying, but with little success. Their fleet outnumbers ours, and the extra ships we were promised have not yet arrived.|Captain Rex and Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Plan of Dissent" />}}
[[File:UmbaranAirbase-PoD.png|thumb|283x283px|The captured airbase]]
After securing the airbase for the Republic, the Umbarans stepped up their attacks in their attempts to retake the airbase and began firing long-range missiles. Additionally, the capital shifted to receive supplies from a [[Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel|Separatist supply ship]] protected by a Separatist fleet that outnumbered the Republic's fleet.<ref name="Plan of Dissent" /> During that phase of the naval battle, clone pilot [[Stub (clone pilot)|Stub]] engaged Umbaran fighters.<ref name="X-wing">{{FFGXW2|set=Clone Z-95 Headhunter Expansion Pack|cardname="Stub"}}</ref>

Krell and the 501st made sure security measures were reconfigured and that all the troopers stayed on alert. Krell was then contacted by Kenobi, who congratulated him on taking the airbase, though Krell remarked that he would wait to celebrate until the battle was won. Kenobi told Krell that the 212th was being pushed away from the capital by long-range missiles and informed them of the supply ship. Krell suggested that they would have to take the capital regardless of the missiles, and agreed to meet up with Kenobi and 212th at the rendezvous point. Kenobi's transmission was then lost. Despite this, Krell ordered the 501st to be prepared in twelve hours to advance to the capital; Rex then asked Krell if he should try to get a message to Kenobi in order to coordinate the attack. Krell insisted that Kenobi had his hands full and that the the 501st would need to throw everything they had at the capital. Krell further disagreed with Rex's assertion that they would be walking into a blast zone and ordered him to prepare the 501st.<ref name="Plan of Dissent" />

[[File:Separatist_supply_ship.png|left|thumb|220x220px|The destruction of the Separatist supply ship.]]
After Rex briefed his troops, Fives told him that he had broken Umbaran access codes and that they could use the captured starfighters to infiltrate the supply ship and destroy it. Rex presented the idea to Krell, who shot it down immediately. Fives disobeyed Krell, proceeding with the mission alongside [[Jesse]] and Hardcase. The three embarked on their mission with the starfighters. After their launch, Krell was informed by a [[Flight officer|Flight Officer]] about the launch of three fighters, and questioned Rex about these actions. Rex then lied to the General, and stated he launched the fighters for reconnaissance on the Umbaran capital city. By then, Fives's strike team had entered the airspace over a nearby battle. Since the Separatists were too busy fighting the Republic, they were assumed as friendlies and used this opportunity to reach the supply ship.<ref name="Plan of Dissent" />

Fives explained to Jesse and Hardcase that Skywalker had told him a story about how [[Battle of Naboo|he blew up a control ship on Naboo]], and that they were going to use the same method to destroy the supply ship. However, the plan quickly went into shambles when a [[B1-series battle droid|B1 battle droid]] noticed the clones in the starfighters. The [[TM-171|T-series tactical droid]] in command of the ship believed it was impossible that clones were on the ship, but after other battle droids verified the intruders, the droids immediately set up a ray shield which prevented the clones from critically damaging the ship. Hardcase sacrificed himself by heading in alone with a part of his starfighter, which destroyed the supply ship.<ref name="Plan of Dissent" /> With the loss of all armed shipments and supply lines to the Umbara's capital, Kenobi's forces begun their attack on the Capital to capture it.<ref name="Star Wars: Battles that changed the Galaxy" />

===Carnage of Krell===
[[File:RexArrestsKrell-Carnage.png|thumb|287x287px|Rex and the rest of the clones confront Krell]]
Jesse and Fives were then threatened with court-martial, though Krell quickly decided upon execution instead. The pair were led by Appo and other troopers to their execution. [[Dogma]] was in charge of their firing squad, which consisted of [[Kix]], [[Tup]], and several other troopers. Fives appealed to his brothers, which resulted in the soldiers dropping their weapons. Krell, outraged, ordered Rex and Dogma to his tower.<ref name="Carnage of Krell" />

Before Rex and Krell could argue over the matter, the tower received a transmission from a clone scout, informing them that the Umbarans were preparing another offensive, possibly with the aid of clone armor and weapons captured from a platoon. Krell decided on a preemptive strike. The troops moved out, but soon ran into a firefight and began targeting what they believed to be the enemy in disguise. Rex, suspicious of the supposed enemy's identity, took off the helmet of one of the killed "clones" and was horrified to find out that they were firing on friendly forces. Rex immediately ran to the middle of the battlefield and yelled at both sides to take off their helmets, and both the 501st and [[Waxer's platoon|a platoon]] from the 212th were horrified to learn that they had slaughtered each other.

After verifying the casualties, Rex demanded to see the 212th's platoon leader, who Kix found out was [[Waxer]]. Waxer had sustained fatal wounds in the firefight, but managed to tell Rex that Krell had told them that they were going to fight enemies who had taken clone armor and equipment.<ref name="Carnage of Krell" />

[[File:KrellVsClones-CoK.png|left|thumb|220x220px|Pong Krell fighting his own troops]]
Enraged by the deception, Rex led the 501st and 212th to the airbase, where they confronted Krell. Initially, Dogma wanted to join in the coup to avenge his fallen brothers, but ultimately sided with Krell due to his loyalty to the chain of command, and was subsequently neutralized by the others. Rex confronted Krell with the evidence that he ordered both groups to attack each other; Krell admitted that he did not expect a clone like him to piece together the puzzle. Krell revealed to the clones that he had a vision of the future, and that he would become Dooku's newest apprentice by allowing the Separatists to defeat the Republic. The 501st and 212th attempted to subdue Krell, but were overpowered by the traitorous Jedi.<ref name="Carnage of Krell" />

As Krell leaped out the window of the tower, more clones attempted to stop him, but were unsuccessful and he managed to escape into the forests. Rex, knowing that Krell had information on everything the Republic had, ordered the clones to chase after him. He was soon stopped by Dogma, who tried to protect the General. However, after being convinced to lower his weapon, he was tackled and sent to lockup. The clones managed to track Krell in the shadowy forests, but were still overpowered.<ref name="Carnage of Krell" />

During the battle, Tup was sent flying by one of Krell's attacks, which ended up landing him near a vixus that briefly awoke. Tup took notice of this and told Rex and the others to lure Krell towards him. The clones retreated to Tup's position while he fired several shots at Krell, getting the [[Besalisk]]'s attention. Krell, who was too focused slaughtering the clones, failed to notice the vixus and was caught in its grasp. While he managed to defend himself from both the vixus and clones, Tup stunned him during the confusion, ending the skirmish.<ref name="Carnage of Krell" />

[[File:Rexecutioner-CoK.png|thumb|241x241px|Krell's execution]]
As Krell was taken captive by the clones, Appo reported that Kenobi's 212th Attack Battalion had captured and held the Umbaran capital city, sending the remaining Umbarans towards their position. The troopers realized that if they lost control of the airbase and Krell was freed, he would be able to turn over the Republic's security codes and other intel. Knowing that this couldn't happen, Rex, Jesse, Fives, Kix, and Tup went to confront him and execute the former Jedi. Dogma was also released to stand with his brothers during this. Rex aimed his [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|blaster pistol]] at Krell, but he chuckled and taunted the captain, stating that he would never be able to take the shot. Krell was then unexpectedly shot mid-sentence, and as the troopers turned around in shock they saw that Dogma had taken Fives's blaster pistol and had been the one to fire. Dogma took a gasp of breath and stated that he had to kill him for the betrayal.<ref name="Carnage of Krell" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|What's the point of all this? I mean, why?''"<br />"''I don't know, sir. I don't think anybody knows. But I ''do'' know that someday this war is gonna end.''"<br />"''Then what? We're soldiers. What happens to us then?|Rex and Fives, directly after the battle|<ref name="Carnage of Krell" />}}
Dogma was taken into custody for killing a Jedi, though Rex gave him a reassuring nod to let him know he had done what was right. Fives informed Rex that the last holdouts of Umbarans had been routed by General Kenobi's battalions, securing all sectors and bringing Umbara back under Republic control. Rex did not feel like celebrating it however, and he spoke with Fives about what the point of the war was, and what would happen to the clones when it ended.<ref name="Carnage of Krell" />

Krell's betrayal was remembered by the Jedi;<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> a short time after the rogue Jedi had endangered Republic operations on Umbara,<ref name="FactFile Kenobi 112">{{FactFile2014|112|KEN 47-52|Obi-Wan Kenobi}}</ref> Skywalker used Krell's betrayal as an example of how there were be Jedi that would disappoint the [[Jedi Order|Order]].<ref name="Sabotage" /> [[Mace Windu]] later recalled Krell's betrayal when the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] was discussing the fallen Jedi [[Quinlan Vos]].<ref name="DD">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>
