A '''Flare Jacket''' was a specialized self-defense [[jacket]] manufactured by [[Ayelixe/Krongbing Textiles]].<ref name="GAG" />

Designed with several capacitive charges woven into the seams of the jacket, a wearer could activate these charges and release a blinding and stunning flash of concentrated light - allowing for a quick escape. The main drawback to the jacket was that its internal power source was small, and required several minutes to recharge between uses.<ref name="GAG" />
