'''Corovene Manakor''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Troglof]] Bank employee who worked for the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] in the [[Scipio system]]. He participated in the [[Heist on Scipio|robbery]] of the planet [[Scipio]], but only because he promised to raise money to free [[Najiba]]. He helped the thieves bypass the security system, but as soon as they got into the treasury, they left him to took the heat. After that, he visited [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]] and asked to help him and his family to get to the planet [[Lysatra]]. Lando organized the transport, but the Imperials occupied a [[Cloud City]]. Eventually, [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]] helped them escape from the planet [[Bespin]].<ref name="FACPOV ESB" />
