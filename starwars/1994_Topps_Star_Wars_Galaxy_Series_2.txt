'''1994 Topps ''Star Wars Galaxy'' Series 2''' was a trading card set released by [[Topps]] in spring [[1994]]. [[Daniel Brereton]], [[John Bolton]], and [[Jae Lee]] were among the artists who provided artwork for the cards.<ref name="SWG" />
