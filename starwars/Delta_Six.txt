'''Delta Six''' was the [[callsign]] of a [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]] that was stolen by [[Iden Versio]] and her daughter [[Zay Versio]] during a [[Battle of Kestro City|battle]] on the [[planet]] [[Vardos]] concurrent to the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]] in [[34 ABY]]. Iden and Zay were accompanied by [[Shriv Suurgav]] of the [[Resistance]] on another [[starfighter]], and used them to board the ''[[Retribution]]''.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
