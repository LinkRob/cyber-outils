'''DT-5537''' was the designation of a [[human]] [[death trooper]] that was part of [[Unit TI-23]]. Having undergone advanced training at a [[Death trooper training camp|camp]] on [[Scarif]] and received classified augmentations, DT-5537 was proficient in demolitions, improvised weaponry, and [[Guerrilla warfare|guerrilla tactics]].<ref name="UVG" />
