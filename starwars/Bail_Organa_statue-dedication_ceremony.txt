In [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> a '''dedication ceremony''' was held on [[Hosnian Prime]], in honor of [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], a founder of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. During the ceremony, a seventy-[[meter]]-high [[Jelucani fogstone]] statue of him,<ref name="Bloodline" /> designed by [[Janyor]] of [[Bith (planet)|Bith]],<ref name="SWP" /> was revealed.<ref name="Bloodline" />

[[Senator]] [[Tai-Lin Garr]] and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], adopted daughter of Bail himself, both addressed the vast crowd of a thousand different [[species]] that had gathered for the occasion, reminding them of the sacrifices [[the galaxy]] made to [[New Republic|restore]] the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]]. The ceremony was concluded with an [[air show]], featuring a variety of sophisticated [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] maneuvers.<ref name="Bloodline" />
