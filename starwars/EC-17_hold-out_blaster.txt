The '''EC-17 hold-out blaster''',<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> also known as the '''EC-17 blaster''',<ref name="SWH">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> '''scout pistol''', '''flash pistol''', or '''scout trooper blaster''',<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref> was a standard-issue [[Hold-out blaster|hold-out]] [[blaster pistol]] for [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[scout trooper]]s and [[patrol trooper]]s, manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]].

==Description==
[[File:Corporal_oberk.png|thumb|left|250px|A scout trooper with an EC-17.]]
A compact, small, one-handed [[blaster]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]], the EC-17 hold-out blaster featured a built-in targeting [[scope]] to assist its user's aim, and a short-range laser emitter. Some models were additionally equipped with a small but intensely-bright light appended to the primary barrel that could be triggered to "flash-stun" a target; temporarily blinding them. The EC-17's most unique feature compared to other [[blaster pistol]]s of the same size and caliber was its unique trigger, it was located on the side of the gun rather than directly underneath the barrel and made it easier to fire while wearing [[glove]]s. The blaster was designed for short-range targets, and therefore lost much of its accuracy over greater distances.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

==History==
The EC-17 became the standard-issue sidearm of [[scout trooper]]s and [[patrol trooper]]s during the time of the [[Galactic Empire]], and was commonly holstered in a [[trooper]]'s boot.<ref name="DRVG">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> The weapon was also used by soldiers of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="Planets Battlefront" /> and was wielded by scout troopers on [[Endor]] during the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>
