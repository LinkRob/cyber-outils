'''Aila Bren''' was a [[human]] [[computer]] expert and [[slicer]] who lived during the [[Imperial Era]]. Bren operated on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] by freelancing for the criminal syndicates on the moon, and had a reputation for her effectiveness. The scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] sought out Bren's slicing expertise.<ref name="Outlaws" />
