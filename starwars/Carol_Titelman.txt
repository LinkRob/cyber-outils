'''Carol Titelman''' (spouse name: '''Wikarska''') ([[1940s#1946|1946]] &ndash; [[December 7]], [[2019]]) was a key member of the staff during the early days of [[Lucasfilm]]. She joined the company months before the release of the first movie, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'']],<ref name="death" /> as [[Charles Lippincott]] secretary, while they were romantically engaged together.<ref name="Lippincott">{{Facebook|username=charles.lippincott.9|url=photo?fbid=570384289753980&set=pb.100003472411630.-2207520000|name=[[Charles Lippincott]]|date=September 27, 2014|archivedate=20201105185317}}</ref> She soon came to direct three departments : Art, Publishing and Creative Services in the late 1970's. In particular, she helped with the creation of art-focused books. She also oversaw the conception and production of the first [[Star Wars (radio)|''Star Wars'' radio drama]], for which she was credited as an executive producer. In that role Titlelman hired [[Brian Daley]] as scriptwriter and oversaw the film's faithful adaptation to the radio.<ref name="NPR Dramatization">''[[Star Wars: The National Public Radio Dramatization]]''</ref> Titelman left Lucasfilm when its offices moved to [[Wikipedia:Northern California|Northern California]].<ref name="death" />
