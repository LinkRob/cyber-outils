{{Quote|We don't even deliver ion disruptors! They're illegal, you know that, heh heh.|Hondo Ohnaka|<ref name="Smuggler's Run">''[[Millennium Falcon: Smuggler's Run]]''</ref>}}
'''Disruptors''' were a type of particularly dangerous energy weapon which was banned by Imperial law,<ref name="Smuggler's Run" /> as well as [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] regulations, during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Twilight Company">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref>

One model of disruptor was the [[T-7 ion disruptor rifle]],<ref name="DiD" /> which were used by the Empire during the fall of the [[planet]] [[Lasan]] on its native [[Lasat]] population.<ref name="The Honorable Ones">{{Rebels|The Honorable Ones}}</ref> The [[Aqualish]] weapon designer [[Amda Wabo]] later tried to sell a large supply of the weapons to Imperial Minister [[Maketh Tua]], but was [[Mission to steal Imperial weapons|prevented]] from doing so by the [[Spectres|crew]] of the [[starship]] known as the ''[[Ghost]]''.<ref name="DiD" /> Other models of disruptor included the [[T-8 disruptor]], which was produced by the Empire,<ref name="Empire Day">{{Rebels|Empire Day (episode)}}</ref> the [[Amban phase-pulse blaster]],<ref name="Chapter 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> and the [[DX-2 disruptor blaster pistol]].<ref name="Twilight Company" />

==Capabilities==
{{Quote|These were banned by the Senate. You can short circuit an entire ship with these."<br />"That's not why they were banned&hellip;|Sabine Wren and Garazeb Orrelios, on T-7 ion disruptors|<ref name="DiD" />}}
[[T-7 ion disruptor rifle]]s were capable of disabling an [[All Terrain Defense Pod]] with a single shot, and were capable of short-circuiting an entire [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="DiD" />

When fired upon a biological target, the victim was disintegrated atom by atom, resulting in a slow, painful [[death]].<ref name="Droids in Distress (book)">[[Droids in Distress (book)|''Droids in Distress'' chapter book]]</ref> After T-7's were used against the [[Lasat]] during the [[Siege of Lasan|fall]] of [[Lasan|their homeworld]], the weapons were banned by the [[Imperial Senate]]. The mere sight of a T-7 was enough to cause surviving [[Lasat]] to recoil in shock.<ref name="DiD" />
