'''Arvala-6''',<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> or '''Arvala-Six''', was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Aldhani" /> in the [[Arvala system]] in the [[Sevetta sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It and the neighboring [[planet]] [[Arvala-7]] were considered to be on the frontier,<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|arvala-7|Arvala-7}}</ref> and the [[Galactic Empire]] occupied its lands in order to [[Mining|mine]] resources.<ref name="Aldhani" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], Arvala-6 was the site of a [[Arvala-6 conflict|conflict]], which had diminished in scale enough for mining operations to resume in the Imperial-controlled lands<ref name="Aldhani" /> in [[5 BBY]].<ref name="5 BBY">{{AndorDate|104}}</ref> [[Supervisor]] [[Lagret]] reported to a meeting of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] this information, to which [[Major]] [[Lio Partagaz|Partagaz]] chastised Lagret for failing to finish a memorandum of recommendations for storage issues regarding displaced people. Lagret informed Partagaz that he was awaiting an assessment from Arvala-6's [[Arvala-6's ministerial director|ministerial director]].<ref name="Aldhani" />
