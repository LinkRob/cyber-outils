'''Anzellan mystery novels''' were a literature genre<ref name="MOF" /> that shared their name with the [[Anzellan]] [[species]].<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> By the time of the early [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[spy]] [[Chemish Runazha]] read some of the novels as a hobby. While in the [[Haki Zeophrine's safe house|safe house]] they and their mentor [[Haki Zeophrine]] shared on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]<ref name="MOF" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate}}</ref> Runahaza sat and researched the [[Imperial Rebirth Act]] and the measures [[Senator]]s [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] and [[Mon Mothma]] were taking to promote it. They found the topic interesting, resolving to study some texts concerning [[Politics|political]] science between reading Anzellan mystery novels in the future.<ref name="MOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Anzellan mystery novels were mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MOF" />
