'''''Droids''''' is a picture-book adaptation of the [[Star Wars: Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO|''Star Wars: Droids'']] episode "[[The White Witch]]." It was released exclusively in [[Wikipedia:Spain|Spain]] in [[1986]] by [[Plaza Joven]]. In late [[2014]], an English translation by [[Abel G. Peña]] was made available via [[StarWars.com]].
