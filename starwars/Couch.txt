A '''couch''' was a type of furniture.<ref name="Double or Nothing 1">[[Lando - Double or Nothing 1|''Lando - Double or Nothing'' 1]]</ref> A couch stood in the office of [[Ricta Varsh]] in the [[HoloNet News building]]. Over the many years, Varsh used the couch to store various items, to the point that it was impossible to sit on it and he forgot how comfortable the it actually was.<ref name="GT:AitC">{{InsiderCite|237|Galactic Tales: Another Face in the Crowd}}</ref>
