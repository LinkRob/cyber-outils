The [[First Order]] field-tested its [[All Terrain Heavy Hauler]] on '''Dromondar Beta'''<ref name="ICS" /> in a [[star system]] of the [[Dromondar Beta system|same name]] in the [[Unknown Regions]]<ref name="Appendix" /> by [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]'' states that the [[All Terrain Heavy Hauler]] had operated on Dromondar Beta by the events of {{Film|VII}}, which take place in [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> in grid square H-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> An [[AT-series|all-terrain vehicle]] manufactured by [[Kuat-Entralla Drive Yards]], the hauler was able to complete its mission objectives there despite over forty percent of its [[limb]]s having been rendered defunct.<ref name="ICS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dromondar Beta was mentioned in [[2017]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]] as part of the ''[[Incredible Cross-Sections]]'' reference book series.<ref name="ICS" />
