'''Colonial Station Chaf''' was a terrestrial [[planet]] within the territory of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] in the [[Unknown Regions]]. It shared its name with the [[Chaf family]], one of the [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]] of the Chiss Ascendancy.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref> The [[communications triad]] of Colonial Station Chaf was used for relaying communication through to the [[Xodlak family]] [[Task Force Xodlak|task force]] led by [[Senior Captain]] [[Irizi'in'daro|Xodlak'in'daro]] on their way to the planet [[Hoxim]].<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref>

The [[Kilji Illumine]] government's [[Illumine Kilhorde]] battle [[fleet]] [[long-range triad transmitter]] located on Colonal Station Chaf while they [[Conquests of enlightenment|conquered]] several nations to the south and southeast of the Ascendancy to communicate with its [[Commanding officer|leader]], [[Generalirius]] [[Nakirre]]. Through the transmitter, Nakirre received reports of victory from his [[Field Commander|field commanders]]; however, one [[Standard day|day]] the reports ceased, leading him to fear that his [[general]]s had foolishly extended their conquests to Colonial Station Chaf, which would have suspended the long-range triad transmitter's operation. In fact, however, the Kilhorde had been [[Attack on the Illumine Kilhorde|destroyed]] by the [[Paataatus Hiveborn]] government, which deemed the fleet's operations near their borders unacceptable and sent a [[Paataatus attack fleet]] to end them.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:ChissAscendancy.jpg|thumb|right|200px|In ''Star Wars'' Legends, the Colonial Station Chaf system was at the Southeast zenith border of the core territory of the [[Chiss Ascendancy/Legends|Chiss Ascendancy]].]]
In ''Star Wars'' [[canon]], Colonial Station Chaf was first mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'', written by [[Timothy Zahn]] as the second novel of ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''<ref name="Greater Good" /> and later appeared on a [[:File:Chiss_Ascendancy_map.jpg|map]] provided by publisher [[Del Rey]] as a bonus for preordering<ref name="Lesser Evil map" /> the final novel of ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]'', ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> It shares its name and location<ref name="Lesser Evil map" /> with the [[Chiss/Legends|Chiss]] [[Colonial Station Chaf system/Legends|Colonial Station Chaf system]] from [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], which was introduced in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
