'''Foodstuff''',<ref name="Battle of Jedha">''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]''</ref> or '''food''', was [[Diet|nourishment]] needed for [[Sentience|sentient]] beings to survive.<ref name="Droids in Distress">{{Rebels|Droids in Distress}}</ref> On [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[starship]]s, [[Imperial officer|officers]] were encouraged to avoid consuming solid food and drink nutritive beverages instead, because it was more efficient in terms of ship resources and [[officer]] [[time]].<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> Food included [[cake]]s,<ref name="EP">{{TCW|Evil Plans}}</ref> [[fruit]],<ref name="Spark">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref> and [[ration pack]]s.<ref name="DB">{{Databank|ration-packs|Ration Packs}}</ref>

Some types of food contained [[bone]]s; when [[Peli Motto]] instructed her [[DUM-series pit droid|pit droids]] to fetch food for [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], she asked them to get something with bones in it.<ref name="Gunslinger">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 5: The Gunslinger}}</ref>

[[The Tribe]] had a rule that none of them could remove their [[helmet]] in the presence of others. Consequently, when sharing a meal, each person took their food to a secluded area and ate alone there, so that they could all maintain their anonymity.<ref name="Chapter 20: The Foundling">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 20: The Foundling}}</ref>

Among [[human]]s, it was common to feel irritable if one had not eaten for an extended period; when [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] was captured by the [[Maya Pei Brigade]], he pointed out to the guard, [[Plug]], that the Brigade had not eaten for two [[Standard day|days]], leading to their cranky demeanor.<ref name="One Year Later">{{Andor|One Year Later}}</ref>

"Put food on the table" was a term for providing enough [[Currency|money]] to cover basic necessities, such as food.<ref name="The New Trooper">{{Resistance|The New Trooper}}</ref>
