'''Beauchen''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Alphabet" /> located in an [[Beauchen system|eponymous star system]] within the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Vensori sector]]. Situated in the grid square P-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> it was where the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] conducted a [[Raid on Beauchen|raid]]<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Date">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']] establishes that the [[raid on Beauchen]] took place within the between the [[Battle of Endor]] and the formation of [[Alphabet Squadron]], which are both dated to [[4 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> [[Caern Adan]], an operative in the [[New Republic Intelligence]], suspected the [[fighter wing]] of being involved in the raid and voiced his suspicions in a conversation with the reprogrammed [[IT-O interrogation droid|IT-O Interrogation Unit]] [[IT-O]]<ref name="Alphabet" /> that same [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="4 ABY">{{AlphabetSquadronDate}}</ref>

In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY">{{ShadowFallDate}}</ref> Adan and the [[New Republic pilot]] [[Yrica Quell]], while aboard the [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class transgalactic military assault ship]] ''[[Lodestar]]'', reviewed the [[Massacre at Anx Minor|massacre]] at the<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> astronomical object<ref name="MandoVG" /> [[Anx Minor]] and Quell considered the event as in line and as [[blood]]y as 204th's raid on Beauchen.<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> Beauchen was part of the territories of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="MandoVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Beauchen was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2019]] novel [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']], the first instalment of the ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy which was written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Alphabet" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], [[Beauchen/Legends|Beauchen]] was originally introduced in the [[1997]] novel ''[[Specter of the Past]]'', written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Specter of the Past">''[[Specter of the Past]]''</ref>
