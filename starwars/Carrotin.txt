'''Carrotins''' were a type of vegetarian [[Foodstuff|food]] [[Diet|edible]] by [[Wookiee]]s.<ref name="LOTJ" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> some of the food was available on a refreshment table aboard the [[Salvation-class Republic medical frigate|''Salvation''-class Republic medical frigate]] ''[[Panacea]]''. The Wookiee [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Burryaga Agaburry]] was dismayed at the lack of meat options available, having wanted something more substantial to eat than carrotins and [[pipfruit]].<ref name="LOTJ" />
