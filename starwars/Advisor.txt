{{Quote|So&hellip; ''advisor''. What do you ''advise''?|Darth Vader, to Sabé|<ref name="Vader 28">[[Darth Vader (2020) 28|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 28]]</ref>}}
An '''advisor''', or '''adviser''', was an individual who helped another individual&mdash;often a leader, such as a military [[officer]],<ref name="Old Friends">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref> [[king]], or an [[emperor]]&mdash;make decisions. On the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]], [[Mon Calamari]] [[Captain]] [[Gial Ackbar]] served as a [[Mon Cala planetary defense services|military]] advisor to King [[Yos Kolina]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Ultimate">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> After leaving the [[Jedi Order]] and no longer being part of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] was unable to serve as the [[commander]] of a [[332nd Division|Republic division]] for the [[Siege of Mandalore]]. As such, [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] was made [[Clone Commander|the leader]] of the unit, and Tano was dubbed an advisor.<ref name="Old Friends" /> '''Clone Advisors''' were [[clone trooper]]s who served in an advisor role, such as overseeing the actions of [[clone commando]] units.<ref name="DeltaSquad">{{Shatterpoint|set=Delta Squad, Form Up Squad Pack}}</ref>

[[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] employed several [[bureaucrat]]s throughout [[Imperial Era|his reign]] that [[Emperor's adviser|advised]] him on how to rule his [[The galaxy|galaxy]]-wide [[Galactic Empire|Empire]].<ref name="Trkn">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] served as a Chief Advisor to the Emperor concerning matters with the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> During the hunt for [[Crimson Dawn]], the former [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|Naboo Royal Handmaiden]] [[Sabé]] was made a [[Lieutenant Commander]] and advisor in the [[Imperial Military]] on the order of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Vader 28" /> Approximately four [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Gallius Rax]] proclaimed himself [[Counselor to the Empire]], an unprecedented title tasked to act as an adviser to the shattered Empire.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>
