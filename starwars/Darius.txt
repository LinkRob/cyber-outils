'''Darius''' was a [[starship]] manufacturing company. The company produced [[Darius G-class freighter|''Darius G''-class freighter]]s, a spacious cargo [[freighter]] model used throughout [[the galaxy]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> by [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> by criminal organizations such as the [[Hutt Clan]] and the [[Pyke Syndicate]], as well as civilians and the [[Mining Guild]] [[miner]]s' organization.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> ''Darius G''-class freighters were also in operation during the [[First Order-Resistance War|conflict]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]],<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> which took place from [[34 ABY]] to [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> including some owned by the First Order.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> In the final battle of the conflict<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> several [[Mining Guild Freighter]]s&mdash;a type of modified ''Darius G''-class freighter<ref name="Kids">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Ship in the Star Wars Sequel Trilogy|video=bb3vhWXClTY|nolive=1}}</ref>&mdash;were part of the [[Citizens' Fleet]] that participated in the [[Battle of Exegol]].<ref name="TROS" />
