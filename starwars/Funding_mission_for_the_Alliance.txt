[[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] undertook a '''funding mission for the Alliance''' to the [[planet]] [[Uveron]],<ref name="SW44" /> between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> Originally, [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] was supposed to join Mothma on the mission but was arrested for suspected [[treason]] prior. During a break in the mission, Mothma took part in the [[Trial of Lando Calrissian]] and was [[Kidnapping of Mon Mothma|kidnapped]] by a group of [[Scarabi]] mercenaries during the trial.<ref name="SW44" />
