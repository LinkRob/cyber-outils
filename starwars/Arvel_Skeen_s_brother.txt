A [[Gender|male]] '''individual''' was claimed to be the brother of [[Arvel Skeen]], a [[human]] male. Arvel told the [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team|team]] of rebels on [[Aldhani]] that his brother had grown [[pepper tree]]s on his [[farm]] and later committed [[Death|suicide]] after an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Prefect (Galactic Empire)|Prefect]] possessed and flooded his land. Prior to the [[Aldhani heist|mission]], the story of his brother's [[death]] was used by Arvel to explain his motivation in rebelling against the Empire.<ref name="The Axe Forgets" /> After the mission, Arvel invited [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] to abandon their surviving leader, [[Vel Sartha]], and evenly split the [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] that the team had stolen. Arvel said that his actual rebellion was him against everyone else. When Andor asked about Arvel's brother, Arvel said that he did not have a brother.<ref name="The Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref>
