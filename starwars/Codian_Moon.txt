The '''Codian Moon''' [[orbit]]ed the [[planet]] [[Codia I]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It featured [[forest]]s, and [[wood-moss]] grew there. At some point, herds of the creatures known as [[reek]]s were transplanted to the Codian Moon from their [[homeworld]], [[Ylesia]]. On the Codian Moon, [[Farmer|ranchers]] eventually came to [[Reproduction|breed]] the reeks as aggressive combat beasts to be exported into the wider [[The galaxy|galaxy]].

==Description==
The Codian Moon was a terrestrial [[moon]]<ref name="USWNE" /> of the [[planet]] [[Codia I]], located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Codia system]]<ref name="Obi-Wan 2" /> within grid square H-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Timelines Codia" /> Its surface featured [[forest]]s and was covered with [[moss]],<ref name="Obi-Wan 2" /> specifically including [[wood-moss]], which grew there in lesser quantities than on<ref name="Geonosis" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> planet [[Ylesia]].<ref name="Geonosis" /> The moon was illuminated by [[star]]light.<ref name="Obi-Wan 2" />

==History==
{{Quote|Reeks are found throughout the galaxy, from Ylesia to Saleucami, to the Codian Moon.|Ardis San Tekka's journal|<ref name="Bestiary">''[[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Reek_SWCT.png|thumb|right|300px|Herds of reeks were transplanted onto the Codian Moon.]]
Many of<ref name="Geonosis" /> the small,<ref name="ExploreGalaxy">[[Star Wars: Explore the Galaxy (2015)|''Star Wars: Explore the Galaxy'']]</ref> highly territorial herds<ref name="Geonosis" /> of the [[Diet|herbivorous]] [[grass]]land creatures known as [[reek]]s<ref name="USWNE" /> were transplanted to the Codian Moon from their [[homeworld]], Ylesia. On the Codian Moon, [[Farmer|ranchers]] originally [[Reproduction|bred]] reeks for [[Foodstuff|meat]]. Due to the relative scarcity of [[wood]]-moss on the moon, the reeks there eventually became more territorially aggressive than their Ylesian relatives.<ref name="Geonosis" />

At some point, the ranchers on the Codian Moon discovered that the reeks could be forced into becoming carnivorous, and they also learned of the high demand in the wider [[The galaxy|galaxy]] for aggressive creatures. Those discoveries eventually led to<ref name="Geonosis" /> the reeks being exported across the [[galaxy]].<ref name="USWNE" /> Around [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The frame story of the [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan (comic series)|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series, which includes the mention of the Codian Moon in [[Obi-Wan 2|''Obi-Wan'' 2]], occurs around [[1 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:Obi-WanFrameDate|here]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] briefly described the Codian Moon in his [[The Journals of Ben Kenobi|journals]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 2" /> The habitation of the Codian Moon by reeks was also noted in the [[Ardis San Tekka's journal|journal]] of the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] [[cartographer]] [[Ardis San Tekka]],<ref name="Bestiary" /> which was published at some point no earlier than [[35 ABY]].<ref name="35 ABY">{{BestiaryVol1Date}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Codian Moon was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile app]] ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'',<ref name="Force Collection J'Quille">{{ForceCollection|Reek|star=3}}</ref> which was released on [[September 4]], [[2013]].<ref name="Force Collection Release date">{{SW|url=news/announcing-star-wars-force-collection|text=Announcing Star Wars: Force Collection|nolive=1}}</ref> Although its release predates the reset of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on [[April 25]], [[2014]] and the rebranding of the former Expanded Universe continuity as [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]],<ref name="Legends">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Leland Chee]] later confirmed that the game was kept updated to fit with the current canon.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|holocronkeeper|status/846807700482666496|[[Leland Chee]]|quote=I'm not gonna go through the trouble of changing them and I don't have any reason to believe currently that anyone would. [&hellip;] On the off-chance that we DID have a current need to change any of these, we would have for Force Collection.|archivefile=File:Leland_Chee_Force_Collection.png|nolive=1}} &mdash; [[Leland Chee]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] has confirmed that [[in-universe]] information found within ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'' conforms with ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]].</ref>

The [[Codian Moon/Legends|moon]] originated in ''Star Wars'' Legends, where it was first mentioned in the [[2002]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]'', authored by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]].<ref name="Ep2TVD">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The [[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine 2|second issue]] of the [[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine|''Star Wars Jedi Master'' Magazine]],<ref name="JMM2">[[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine 2|''Star Wars Jedi Master'' Magazine 2]]</ref> published on [[January 19]], [[2016]],<ref name="Titan Magazines">{{TitanMagazines|url=t/star-wars-jedi-master-magazine|text=Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine|nolive=1}}</ref> misspelled the moon's name as the "Coodian moon."<ref name="JMM2" />
