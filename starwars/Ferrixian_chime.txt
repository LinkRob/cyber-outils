A '''Ferrixian chime''' was a type of [[Music|musical instrument]] that rung when struck and<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> shared its name with the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Ferrix]].<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> After the [[prospector]] [[Radicaz Dobbs|Sunshine Dobbs]] [[Mission to Planet X (Path of the Open Hand)|crashed]] his [[yacht]], the ''[[Scupper]]'', on<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> the [[Wild Space]]<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> planet [[Sophros|Planet X]]<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> he was found by his associates sprawling over the controls of the ''Scupper'' with his head awkwardly resting against the yoke.<ref name="Path of Vengeance" />

The [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] [[Children of the Open Hand|member]] [[Shea Ganandra]] then shook Dobbs and asked if he was still with them, leading the [[Guide of the Closed Fist|Guide of the Open Hand]] [[Marda Ro]] to begin to ask if he was [[Death|dead]] or alive. In response, the prospector groaned and stated he was alive, noting his head was ringing like a Ferrixian chime, and warned them to back off.<ref name="Path of Vengeance" />

==Behind the scenes==
