'''Ablative foam''' was a substance used to dampen impacts. [[Starship]] [[Technician|mechanics]] could install [[ablative foam dispenser]]s in a ship's hold in order to protect fragile cargo from the impact of extreme maneuvers.<ref name="Outlaws" /> During a [[skirmish with Grysk warships]]<ref name="Thrawn Teason" /> in [[1 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Thrawn: Treason'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> the ''[[Steadfast (Chiss heavy cruiser)|Steadfast]]'', a [[Chiss Ascendancy|Chiss]] [[heavy cruiser]], deployed ablative foam as a response to receiving [[hull]] damage.<ref name="Thrawn Teason" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[Kay Vess]] paid a mechanic a thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to install ablative foam dispensers in the hold of the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}
