'''Dor Namethians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] from the [[Dor Nameth system]]. [[Lexo Sooger]], who worked as a [[masseur]] was a member of this species.<ref name="Hear Nothing, See Nothing, Say Nothing" />

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:Blue_Dor_Namethian.jpg|thumb|154x154px|left|A blue Dor Namethian]]
The Dor Namethians were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="Hear Nothing, See Nothing, Say Nothing" /> with [[Color|blue]]<ref name="Day Off" /> or gray colored skin. They could release [[pheromone]]s at will which had a powerful influence on others, though overuse of the ability could result in exhaustion. They also possessed a very strong sense of smell.<ref name="Hear Nothing, See Nothing, Say Nothing" />

==Dor Namethians in the galaxy==
During the [[Imperial Era]], a Dor Namethian was present when [[Vullen Candroon]] chased [[Chewbacca]] on [[Marquinn]].<ref name="Day Off" />

[[Lexo Sooger]], a [[Gender|male]] Dor Namethian, was the star [[masseur]] at [[Zord's Spa and Bathhouse]] in [[Canto Bight]].<ref name="Hear Nothing, See Nothing, Say Nothing" />
