'''Electrobinoculars''' were handheld viewing devices that enabled users to view objects from afar. Unlike [[macrobinoculars]], electrobinoculars enhanced images via built-in hardware.<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> Examples of the types of enhancements electrobinoculars boasted included [[sensor]]s to detect life signs, power frequencies, invisible [[Deflector shield|defensive screens]] or radiation, [[night vision]] and rangefinding capability, and/or screen out atmospheric interference.<ref name="Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>
