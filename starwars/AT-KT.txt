The '''AT-KT''', nicknamed the "'''Hunter'''," was a less common variant of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]. The AT-KT was widely used on the [[forest]] [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]] during the [[Occupation of Kashyyyk|Imperial occupation]], and was used to subjugate the [[Wookiee]]s. Its weaponry was created to [[stun]] mobile targets, in order to then [[Slavery|enslave]] them.<ref name="Walkers" />
