A '''communications relay''' or a '''relay dish''' was a communications unit widely used by militaries to send and receive transmissions.

==Description==
[[File:CommsRelay-SWL.png|thumb|left|250px|A portable communications relay]]
A communications relay consisted of a main dish through which an [[antenna]] was installed.<ref name="Recon Missions" /><ref name="Jalindi Station" /> A relay was used for sending, receiving, and in certain cases, intercept transmissions.<ref name="Jalindi Station" />

Widely used by the [[Imperial Military|military]] of the<ref name="Recon Missions" /> [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Jalindi Station" /> communications relays were a common sight throughout Imperial worlds, perched atop Imperial ground-based installations.<ref name="Recon Missions" /> The Empire's [[KX-series security droid]]s were also each fitted with a full comm relay.<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

The [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]] of the [[First Order]] were optionally installed with a Comm Relay upgrade.<ref name="TIE/fo" />

The [[Comm station|comm booth]] and [[holobooth]] both functioned in a similar way, only being public stations for communications.<ref name="Secret Academy">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref><ref name="To Catch a Jedi">{{TCW|To Catch a Jedi}}</ref>

==History==
There were communications relay emplacements throughout Imperial worlds, including [[Mustafar]], [[Stygeon Prime]], and [[Lothal]]. In particular, during Lothal's period of Imperial occupation, an Imperial installation there had a communications relay that was disabled along with the facility itself during a sabotage mission conducted by the [[Spectres]], a rebel cell seeking to undermine Imperial control. The rebel [[Ezra Bridger]] first assaulted the facility with an appropriated<ref name="Recon Missions" /> [[All Terrain Missile Platform]]<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> during the extraction of the [[Ithorian]] rebel sympathiser [[Dorthu Santim]], before the rebel group's leader, [[Hera Syndulla]], arrived with her modified [[VCX-100 light freighter]],<ref name="Seen a ghost?">{{SW|url=news/join-the-rebellion|text=Seen a ghost?}}</ref> the ''[[Ghost]]'', and landed the rebel [[Sabine Wren]], who broke into the Imperial base amidst confusion in its [[Stormtrooper|personnel]] and sabotaged it.<ref name="Recon Missions" />

[[Jalindi Station]] was a massive relay dish developed and used by the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Jalindi Station" />
