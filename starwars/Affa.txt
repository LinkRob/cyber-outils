'''Affa''' was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="HC12" /> that housed great [[droid]] [[Factory|factories]].<ref name="GEG" /> Located in the [[Affa system]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories]], it was situated in grid square M-13<ref name="Appendix" /> and was located along the [[Shipwrights' Trace]] [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="Shipwright" />

[[Arakyd Industries]]'s<ref name="CEUE">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> [[RA-7 protocol droid]]s, including [[DK-RA-43]], were manufactured in the factories,<ref name="GEG" /> while parts for [[Cybot Galactica]]'s [[3PO-series protocol droid]] were also manufactured on the planet.<ref name="HC12" />
