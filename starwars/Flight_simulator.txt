A '''flight simulator''' was a device used to train and test [[starfighter]] [[pilot]]s. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] used a simulator which resembled the interior of an [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] [[cockpit]] removed from its frame and connected to a large monitor. The highest score on this simulator was held by [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] [[Wedge Antilles]], although his record was trumped by new recruit [[Luke Skywalker]] shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. The simulator was designed to present Rebel pilots with a no-win scenario. It immediate plunged the pilot into a [[Realspace|space]] battle with simulated [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Military|forces]], steadily increasing the number until the battle became overwhelming and even the most skilled pilot could no longer survive under the onslaught.<ref name="ANH">''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]''</ref>

During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], the [[Jelucan]] aristocrat [[Thane Kyrell]]'s [[Kyrell family|family]] owned an old flight simulator. He invited his new-found friend [[Ciena Ree]] to practice with him. The two later joined the [[Imperial Navy]].<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> In the [[Standard month|months]] before the [[attack on Tuanul]], [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], a [[scavenger]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]], practiced flying using an [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor|A-wing]] simulator.<ref name="Rey's Story">''[[The Force Awakens: Rey's Story]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In her notes for ''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]'', author [[Alexandra Bracken]] describes that some material in the story was adapted from the [[Star Wars (radio)|radio drama]] of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'']]. This radio drama included a sequence in which Skywalker was tested in a [[Flight simulator/Legends|holosimulator]] prior to joining Red Squadron.
