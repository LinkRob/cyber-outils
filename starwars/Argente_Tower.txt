'''Argente Tower''' was the largest building in [[Murkhana City]], dominating the skyline. Named after [[Passel Argente]], [[Magistrate]] of the [[Corporate Alliance]], the tower was reduced to a husk at the conclusion of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Tarkin" />
