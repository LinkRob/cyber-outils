The '''CX Dagger vessel'''<ref name="Infiltration Concept Art">{{SW|url=infiltration-concept-art-gallery-star-wars-the-bad-batch|text="Infiltration" Concept Art Gallery &#124; The Bad Batch}} {{C|slide 4}}</ref> was a type of [[transport]] used by [[Clone X trooper]]s part of a [[Royce Hemlock's deep undercover program|deep undercover program]] within the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Advanced Science Division]]<ref name="Infiltration" /> during or after [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|308}}</ref> It was [[dagger]]-shaped and featured twin [[Color|red]]-colored [[engine]]s in the back. It could be remotely-controlled by a small remote built on the arms of the [[Cloning|clone]] X troopers' [[armor]], with features including providing a long-range [[comlink|communications]] boost to the [[assassin]]s. An [[entry ramp]] was located on the back underneath the engines for the assassin to disembark the ship.<ref name="Infiltration" /> It also possessed a special stealth function that [[Jammer|jams]] the ship from [[sensor]]s.<ref name="Point of No Return (The Bad Batch)" />

The clone assassin [[CX-2]] used a Dagger vessel to travel to the [[planet]] [[Teth]] on a mission to eliminate [[CX-1]], a fellow clone assassin who had been captured by the [[Clone Underground]].<ref name="Infiltration" /> Upon arriving on [[Pabu]], CX-2 drove the ship to a cove while jamming the [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|''Maurader'']]<nowiki/>'s [[sensor beacon]] to prevent him from being spotted. After destroying the ''Marauder'' and capturing [[Omega]], CX-2 summoned his ship and left before [[Crosshair]] could plant a [[homing beacon]] on it.<ref name="Point of No Return (The Bad Batch)" /> During the [[Skirmish on Wayland|skirmish]] on [[Wayland]], [[Chief Scientist]] [[Doctor]] [[Royce Hemlock]] took CX-2's remote and spare [[RK-3 blaster]] to summon his CX Dagger vessel to take himself, [[Commander]] [[Scorch]], and the captured clone Omega away from [[Tantiss Base]], only for Omega's clone companions [[Hunter]] and [[Crosshair]] to destroy the ship's engines, with a [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] and a [[773 Firepuncher sniper rifle]], before they killed the former two to save Omega.''<ref name="The Cavalry Has Arrived">{{TBB|The Cavalry Has Arrived}}</ref>''
