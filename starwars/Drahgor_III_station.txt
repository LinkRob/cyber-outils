This '''fuel refinery''' on the planet [[Drahgor III]] was run by a [[Flix's flock|flock]] of [[Gozzo]] related to the ''[[Colossus]]'' resident [[Flix]]. It was the only [[fuel]]ling station in the area, which mean the ''Colossus'' was forced to [[Mission to Drahgor III|go there]] for fuel when it ran low during its [[Hunt for the Colossus|flight]] from the [[First Order]].<ref name="From Beneath" />
