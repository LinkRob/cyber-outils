The '''First Order starfighter corps''', also known as the '''First Order TIE corps''', also known as the '''TIE pilot corps''', was a [[Starfighter Corps]] of the [[First Order]] that consisted of [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|First Order TIE Fighter Pilots]]. Pilots from the [[corps]] [[Battle of Oetchi|pursued the Resistance fleet]], and participated in the [[Battle of Crait]]. Several of the members, who had been surviving members of [[Starkiller aerial defense force|Starkiller Base's TIE defenses]], were especially eager to end the Resistance in order to prove their worth after their failure to prevent [[Starkiller Base]]'s [[Battle of Starkiller Base|destruction]].<ref name="TLJ VD" />

==Description==
The First Order starfighter corps,<ref name="SW Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> also known as the First Order TIE corps<ref name="Updated and Expanded">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> or simply as the TIE pilot corps, was the [[Starfighter Corps]] of the [[First Order]].<ref name="TLJ VD" /> The [[Corps]] consisted of [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|First Order TIE Fighter Pilots]] and participated in a number of battles during the [[First Order-Resistance War]].<ref name="Episode VIII" /> The First Order starfighter corps had an organizational military structure that included [[squadron]]s<ref name="TLJ VD" /> and [[Starfighter|fighter]] [[battalion]]s.<ref name="A Quick" />
