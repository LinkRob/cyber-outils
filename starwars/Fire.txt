'''Fire''' was a process in which a substance (a gas, for example), reacted with [[oxygen]] in the environment<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> to give heat, light, and other reaction products like smoke.<ref name="The Rescue">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Rescue]]''</ref> The [[Jedi]] used fire for ceremonial purposes; [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] was cremated during his [[Funeral of Qui-Gon Jinn|funeral]] on [[Naboo]],<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> as was the [[Darth Vader's armor|armor]] of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] during his [[Funeral of Anakin Skywalker|funeral]] on [[Endor]].<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> Vader had previously been [[Duel on Mustafar|burned alive]] by fire on the [[Magma|lava]] rivers of [[Mustafar]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> [[Clone flametrooper|Clone flame troopers]] used [[flamethrower]]s against [[Geonosian]]s during the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]].<ref name="LAPR">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref> [[First Order]] [[First Order flametrooper|Flametroopers]] used [[D-93 Incinerator flamethrower]]s to burn the [[City|village]] of [[Tuanul]].<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> Fire could also occur in space, such as when blasts from [[laser cannon]]s strike a [[starship]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> [[Moth]]s were known to be attracted to flames.<ref name="Inquisitors 4">[[Inquisitors 4|''Inquisitors'' 4]]</ref>
