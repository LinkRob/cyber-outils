An '''archivist''' was an individual in charge of collecting and cataloguing archives. Organizations who employed archivists included the [[Graf Archive]] of [[Orchis 2]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> and the [[Noble Court]] of the [[Anoat sector]].<ref name="Archivist">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Archivist"</ref> [[Jedi archivist]]s were the archivists of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="ItD">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref> At the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]], the Noble Court was infiltrated by [[Data Seekers]], who took advantage of their unrestricted access to the [[noble]]s' archives to learn their secrets.<ref name="Archivist" />
