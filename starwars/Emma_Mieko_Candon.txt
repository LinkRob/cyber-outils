Besides being Japanese, I'm also a chronically ill, queer cyborg, and all of that showed up in some regard.|Emma Mieko Candon, on writing ''Ronin: A Visions Novel''|<ref name="Cover reveal">{{SW|url=news/ronin-star-wars-visions|text=A Mysterious Former Sith Wanders The Galaxy In ''Ronin'', The Upcoming ''Star Wars: Visions'' Novel &mdash; EXCLUSIVE REVEAL}}</ref>}}
'''Emma Mieko Candon''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] author and editor of [[Wikipedia:Japanese people|Japanese]] descent who was born in [[Wikipedia:Hawaii|Hawaii]].<ref name="Press Kit" /> They are the author of ''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]'', a tie-in to the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' short film "[[The Duel (short film)|The Duel]]"<ref name="Cover reveal" /> that was published by [[Del Rey]] on [[October 12]], [[2021]].<ref name="Edelweiss">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/678695/star-wars-visions-ronin-by-emma-mieko-candon/9780593358665|text=Star Wars Visions: Ronin}}</ref> ''Ronin: A Visions Novel'' is their first work in ''[[Star Wars]]''<ref name="Cover reveal" /> as well as their first published book.<ref name="Debut">{{Twitter|EmmaCandon|status/1433242042738438144|Emma Mieko Candon|quote=Q: How does it feel to have your debut book, a Star Wars novel, out in digital ARCs?<br />A: oh, you know, the mortifying ordeal of being known|archivedate=20210902020649}}</ref>

Candon has described themselves as a [[Wikipedia:Chronic condition|chronically ill]], [[Wikipedia:Queer|queer]] [[cyborg]] who incorporates these traits into their writing on ''Ronin''. Other writing inspirations include [[Wikipedia:Akira Kurosawa|Akira Kurosawa]],<ref name="Cover reveal" /> who was also an inspiration for ''Star Wars'' creator [[George Lucas]],<ref name="Making of">''[[The Making of Star Wars]]''</ref> as well as other Japanese period dramas, folklore, and war trauma.<ref name="Cover reveal" />
