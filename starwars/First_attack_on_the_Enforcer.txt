During the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], an '''attack''' was carried out by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] forces under [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] on the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought/Legends|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] [[Enforcer (Executor-class)|''Enforcer'']] near [[Bespin/Legends|Bespin]]. After the arrival of additional [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Starship/Legends|ships]], the Rebels were forced to retreat. The ''Enforcer'' sustained heavy damage during the attack and had to be repaired at an [[Phaylenn repair yard|orbital facility]] above [[Phaylenn]].
