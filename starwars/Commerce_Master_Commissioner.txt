'''Commerce Master Commissioner''' was the title of the individual who controlled the [[Velcar Free Commerce Zone]]. During the latter days of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]] and early years of the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]], it was held by [[Gregor Raquoran]].
