'''Caamasi cheese pies''' were a type of circular, [[Color|tan-brown]] [[cheese]] [[pie]] that were plated with two white, fang-like decorative pieces.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 22" /> The pies shared their name with the [[Caamasi]] [[species]].<ref name="Path of Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref> The [[human]] [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 22" />&mdash;the [[Crime lord|leader]] of the criminal syndicate<ref name="Bounty Hunters 20">[[Bounty Hunters 20|''Bounty Hunters'' 20]]</ref> [[Crimson Dawn]]&mdash;considered the pies to be sweet but a tad savory.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 22" />

Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-marvel|text=SWCE 2023: Marvel Reveals ''Star Wars: Dark Droids'' Horror Event and More}} states that the events of ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]'', including [[Bounty Hunters 22|''Bounty Hunters'' 22]], take place between the events of {{Film|V}} and {{Film|VI}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively.</ref> Qi'ra [[Diet|ate]] a Caamasi cheese pie as<ref name="Bounty Hunters 22" /> she<ref name="Bounty Hunters 20" /> dined with the human [[Cadeliah]]&mdash;the heir to the criminal syndicates [[Unbroken Clan]] and [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate|Mourner's Wail]]&mdash;at a [[Secret Crimson Dawn Base|Crimson Dawn base]]. After Cadeliah attempted to [[Death|kill]] Qi'ra and was stopped by<ref name="Bounty Hunters 22" /> the [[Knights of Ren|Knight of Ren]] [[Vicrul]],<ref name="Dark Side Pocket Expert">''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side Pocket Expert]]''</ref> Qi'ra recommended the pies to the [[Youngling|young]] girl. After she sat back down, Cadeliah began to cut one of the pies.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 22" />

==Behind the scenes==
Caamasi cheese pies appeared and were identified in the [[Bounty Hunters 22|twenty-second issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 22" /> on [[April 6]], [[2022]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/96838/star_wars_bounty_hunters_2020_22|text=Star Wars: Bounty Hunters (2020) #22}}</ref> Prior to the release of the issue, the pie was depicted in a preview article of the issue that was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[March 30]] of the same year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-bounty-hunters-22-exclusive-preview|text=Things Don't Go as Planned in Marvel's ''Star Wars: Bounty Hunters'' #22 &ndash; Exclusive Preview}}</ref>
