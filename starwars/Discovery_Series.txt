The '''''Discovery Series''''' was a series of educational book-and-record and book-and-tape sets released by [[Buena Vista Records]] in [[1984]]. Two of these were ''[[Star Wars]]''-themed: ''[[Star Wars Adventures in Colors and Shapes]]'' and ''[[Star Wars Adventures in ABC]]''.

The sets were similar to Buena Vista's [[Read-Along Adventures]] series. The record company, a subsidiary of [[The Walt Disney Company]], also released sets in the series based on Disney characters.
