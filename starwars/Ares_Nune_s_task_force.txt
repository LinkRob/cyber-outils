A '''task force''' of three [[Republic Navy]] [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]]<ref name="BMF 43" /> was under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Ares Nune]]<ref name="German BMF 43" /> during the [[Clone Wars]]. During the [[Malevolence Campaign|''Malevolence'' Campaign]]<ref name="BMF 43" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> After the destruction of several Republic [[fleet]]s by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[warship]] and [[flagship]] of [[General]] [[Grievous]], ''[[Malevolence]]'',<ref name="German BMF 43" /> [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Plo Koon]] of the [[Fourth Fleet]] dispatched multiple ''Venator''-class Star Destroyers to locate and destroy the new Separatist [[superweapon]].<ref name="BMF 43" />

Nunes and their task force discovered the vessel in the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim's]],<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[Phu system]].<ref name="BMF 43" /> where Grievous had transmissions blocked and attacked the task force, which resulted in [[Nune's Venator-class cruiser|Nune's ''Venator''-class cruiser]] and its two escort<ref name="German BMF 43" /> Venators being destroyed. The [[Galactic Republic]] subsequently discovered the loss, and Jedi General [[Plo Koon]] tracked down the ''Malevolence'' to the [[Abregado system]],<ref name="BMF 43" /> where his [[Plo Koon's fleet|task force]] was also destroyed.<ref name="Rising Malevolence">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Ares Nune's task force was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the German release of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 43|forty-third issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine<ref name="BMF 43" /> which was released around [[October 28]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BuildFalconDates|43}}</ref> [[Master Nune's task force]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity with the release of ''[[The Clone Wars: Shakedown]]'', a [[Star Wars comic books|webcomic]]<ref name="Shakedown">''[[The Clone Wars: Shakedown]]''</ref> authored by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="Hidalgo">''[[The Clone Wars: Shakedown]]''</ref> and released on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="SW.com">{{SWArchive|url=clonewars/comic/?book=season1#/?page=11|text=''The Clone Wars'' webcomic (p.11)|nobackup=1}}</ref> on [[October 2]], [[2008]]<ref name="Release Date">{{SWArchive|url=archives/index.html?c=d6de5d83-4da5-48ab-bf34-75cd3af8fbef&k=&page=11|text=''The Clone Wars'' archives}}</ref> that tied directly into the episode "[[Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence'']]," the second episode of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' that released the [[October 3|next day]].<ref name="Rising Malevolence EG">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode002.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref>
