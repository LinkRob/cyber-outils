'''Cortosis weave''' was a rare, energy-resistant mesh that some armorers shaped [[cortosis]] [[ore]] into. Cortosis weave made [[armor]] nearly impervious to penetration by energy [[weapon]]s, including not just [[blaster]]s, but also [[lightsaber]]s. Even if cortosis ore was not available, other substances like [[Beskar|Mandalorian iron]], [[phrik]] alloy, and [[ultrachrome]] had similar effects if properly treated, though they tended to add considerably more weight to a suit of armor than cortosis weave.<ref name="GaG" />
