'''Churkk''' was a [[Gand]] from the [[planet]] of the [[Gand (planet)|same name]]. When Churkk was young, Churkk left the Gand [[homeworld]], despite the fact that Churkk felt that the Gand should not leave Gand, eventually ending up on the desolate planet [[Parnassos]]. Residing at a fence on the edge of the dead lands, Churkk became inactive for a number of [[Standard year|years]] and awoke when [[Phasma]], several of her [[Scyre]] warriors, [[Brendol Hux]], and two [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] came across the area in which Churkk resided. The Gand was able to communicate to some extent with the [[Parnassos beetle|sand beetles]], which crawled over him without causing harm, and warned travelers of the [[Radiation|dangers]] of entering the [[dead lands]]. Churkk was later [[Death|killed]] by a [[blaster]] wound when a small skirmish broke out between Phasma's group and forces made up of other members of the Scyre and [[Claw (clan)|Claw]] clans.<ref name="Phasma" />
