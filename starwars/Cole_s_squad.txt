A '''squad''' of [[Clone trooper|clone troopers]] led by [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Cole]]<ref name="RogueAgents1" /> served the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The [[squad]]'s members included [[trooper]]s [[Charger (Bad Batch)|Charger]], [[Dirk]], [[Hank]], and [[Rice (clone trooper)|Rice]]. During a skirmish against [[battle droid]]s, Dirk was [[Death|killed]], which led the other [[Cloning|clones]] to go [[Desertion|rogue]] and take up [[mercenary]] work.<ref name="RogueAgents3" /> At a later point in the war, the squad of clones took on a [[bounty]] to find the [[scientist]] [[Ergin Lazlo]] for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During their search, they crossed paths with another clone squad, [[Clone Force 99]].<ref name="RogueAgents1" />
