'''Commodore''' was a senior [[Military rank|rank]] in [[Navy|naval forces]] found in various militaries. Commodore was used in the [[Imperial Navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]] with [[Almudin]] and [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] having the rank before Thrawn's promotion to [[Admiral]].<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> At the time of his [[Death|suicide]] in the [[Castell system]], [[Idel]] was also a commodore and commanded his own [[Idel's Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]].<ref name="Lando 2" /> [[Karyn Faro]] of the [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]] was a commodore, and was assigned [[Task Force 231]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Krovis]] also served as a commodore.<ref name="Twilight Company">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref>

In the Galactic Empire, an individual with the rank of commodore was issued a [[rank insignia plaque]] with either three blue tiles on the left and three red tiles on the right that was accompanied with four [[code cylinder]]s<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>, a plaque with four red tiles on top of four blue tiles and three code cylinders,<ref name="Lando 2" /> or five red tiles on top of five blue tiles with two code cylinders.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>

During the civil war, the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] used the rank of commodore, with [[Kyrsta Agate]] having the rank and commanding the [[Starhawk-class battleship|''Starhawk''-class battleship]] ''[[Concord]]''.<ref name="Life Debt" /> [[Meta]] also served as a commodore within the [[Resistance navy|navy]] of the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Episode VII" />
