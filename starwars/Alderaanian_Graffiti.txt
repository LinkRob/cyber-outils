'''''Alderaanian Graffiti''''' was a [[holovid]] that shared a name with<ref name="Insider" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{SWE|locations|alderaan|Alderaan}}</ref> [[Alderaan]]. It was played by an [[astromech droid]] for a group of [[Jawa]]s on [[Tatooine]], which annoyed a [[Unidentified moisture farmer (Jawas)|moisture farmer]] because their [[Moisture farm|farm]] was destroyed to create a drive-in cinema.<ref name="Insider" />

==Behind the scenes==
''Alderaanian Graffiti'' is featured in a [[Canon|non-canonical]] section of [[Launchpad]] in [[Star Wars Insider 220|''Star Wars Insider'' 220]] that was written by [[Jamie Cosley]].<ref name="Insider" /> It has a similar name to [[Wikipedia:American Graffiti|''American Graffiti'']], a [[1973]] film by [[George Lucas]].<ref name="Lucasfilm">{{InsiderCite|207|Lucasfilm: 50 Years of Innovation}}</ref>
