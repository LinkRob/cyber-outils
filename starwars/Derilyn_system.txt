The '''Derilyn system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Elrood sector]] that was located a short distance from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Iakar]].<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[Han Solo & Chewbacca 6|''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' 6]] labels Iakar's system as the Derilyn system;<ref name="Han Chewie 6">[[Han Solo & Chewbacca 6|''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' 6]]</ref> however, the reference book ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'' identified as the Iakar system,<ref name="Dawn" /> and the [[2025]] "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix establish the Derilyn system to be a separate system.<ref name="Appendix" />
