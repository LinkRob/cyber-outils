My father was a senator on Coruscant, and he was working with the Jedi to negotiate the release of the Daan's leader held captive by the Melida.|Axel Greylark, to Gella Nattai|audio=IAccompaniedMyFatherToTheCadavineSector-Convergence.flac|<ref name="Convergence" />}}
A '''diplomatic mission to Melida/Daan''' took place around [[395 BBY]], when [[Galactic Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Lexxir Greylark]], his [[Greylark family|son]] [[Axel Greylark]], and a [[Unidentified Jedi Knight (Axel Greylark)|Jedi Knight]] traveled to the location [[Melida/Daan]] to release the [[Unidentified Daan leader|leader]] of the [[Daan (people)|Daan]], who had been captured by the opposing [[Melida]]. When the time came to extract the leader, the Melida set off an explosion, injuring Axel and trapping Lexxir beneath rubble. Although Axel tried to rescue his [[Parent|father]], the [[Jedi Knight]] forced Axel to leave Lexxir behind to [[Death|die]] in order to save the others from the Melida. As as result, Axel blamed the [[Jedi]] for his [[Gender|father's]] death and came to resent them.

==Prelude==
During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Cadavine sector]] location [[Melida/Daan]] was inhabited by two opposing groups, the [[Melida]] and the [[Daan (people)|Daan]].<ref name="Convergence" /> Around [[395 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> an individual who served as the [[Unidentified Daan leader|leader]] of the Daan had been captured by the Melida. [[Galactic Republic]] [[Senator]]<ref name="Convergence" /> [[Lexxir Greylark]]<ref name="Queen's Bloom">{{StoryCite|story=The Queen's Bloom|book=The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life}}</ref> worked with the [[Jedi Order]] to negotiate the release of the Daan leader.<ref name="Convergence" />

==The mission==
{{Quote|My father was trapped under the rubble, and I was trying to get him out. I could hear him, but the Melida were coming and we had to go. I wouldn't budge.|Axel Greylark, to Gella Nattai|audio=MyFatherWasTrappedUnderTheRubble-Convergence.flac|<ref name="Convergence" />}}
[[File:Axel-Greylark-headshot.png|thumb|right|200px|Axel Greylark (pictured) accompanied his father on the mission to Melida/Daan.]]
As part of the negotiations, Lexxir Greylark, his [[Greylark family|son]] [[Axel Greylark]], and a [[Unidentified Jedi Knight (Axel Greylark)|Jedi Knight]] embarked on a diplomatic mission to Melida/Daan. When it was time to extract the Daan leader, the Melida set off an explosion, and the mission team realized they had walked into a trap intended to show the Daan's allies what could happen. The group was separated, with Axel sustaining bad injuries, including a wound on his chest, and Lexxir becoming trapped beneath rubble. Axel could hear his [[Parent|father]] and tried to save him, refusing to leave him behind even as the Melida were coming. To save Axel and the others, the [[Jedi Knight]] used her [[Force power|abilities]] to drag him back to their [[starship]], leaving Lexxir behind to [[Death|die]]. Once they had returned to the ship, Axel attempted to turn it around but was once again stopped by the Jedi Knight, after which he passed out from exhaustion.<ref name="Convergence" /> 

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|That is maddening.''"<br />"''Why? Because you don't want to be friends with a ''Jedi''?''"<br />"''No, I don't.''"<br />"''Tell me.|Axel Greylark and Gella Nattai|audio=YouDontWantToBeFriendsWithAJedi-Convergence.flac|<ref name="Convergence" />}}
Axel blamed the [[Jedi]] for his father's death during the mission, causing him to resent them.<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Axel recounted the mission to Melida/Daan to Jedi Knight [[Gella Nattai]] to explain his dislike for the Jedi. Shortly after, Axel rubbed a scar on his chest he had received from his injuries during the mission.<ref name="Convergence" /> Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Cat Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> while Axel was speaking with<ref name="Cataclysm">''[[The High Republic: Cataclysm]]''</ref> [[Elecia Zeveron]],<ref name="THRCE">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> the [[Mother (title)|leader]] of the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]], he reflected on his belief that the Jedi were to blame for Lexxir's death.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Behind the scenes==
