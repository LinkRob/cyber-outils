{{Quote|A former rebel base. Discovered years ago and bombarded by Imperial troops. I detect no surviving organic life-forms.|ZED-6-7, to [[Darth]] Vader|<ref name="Darth Vader 5" />}}
[[Standard year|Years]] before [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Galactic Empire]] discovered and '''bombarded''' a [[Polis Massa Base|base]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] on<ref name="Darth Vader 5" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planetoid]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|polis-massa|Polis Massa}}</ref> [[Polis Massa]] during its [[Galactic Civil War|war]] with the rebels. The bombardment resulted in the damage of at least one [[Chroon-Tan B-Machine]] [[midwife droid]].<ref name="Darth Vader 5" />

In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the bombardment was recalled by the [[SP-4 ISC crime scene analysis droid|Imperial forensics droid]] [[ZED-6-7]] when<ref name="Darth Vader 5" /> he<ref name="Darth Vader 39">[[Darth Vader (2020) 39|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 39]]</ref> visited Polis Massa with the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]&mdash;who was on a quest to take revenge against those who had hidden his [[Luke Skywalker|son]] from him&mdash;and they came across the remains of the base. The [[droid]] also stated that he detected no surviving organic life-forms.<ref name="Darth Vader 5" />

==Behind the scenes==
The bombardment on Polis Massa was first mentioned in the [[Darth Vader (2020) 5|fifth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Darth Vader 5" /> on [[September 16]], 2020.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/80537/star_wars_darth_vader_2020_5|text=Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020) #5}}</ref>
