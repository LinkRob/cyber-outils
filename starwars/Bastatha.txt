'''Bastatha''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Bastatha system]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]. Independent from the [[New Republic]] in the [[Standard year|years]] following the [[Galactic Civil War]], Bastatha became a haven for organized crime, and the [[Kajain'sa'Nikto]] [[crime lord]] [[Rinnrivin Di]] ran [[Rinnrivin Di's cartel|his cartel]] on the planet. Bastatha's surface was superheated by [[Bastatha system sun|its]] [[red giant]] [[star]], forcing all life on the planet, except for some [[bacteria]], to live in underground [[cave]]rns and tunnels.<ref name="Bloodline" /> Due to the heat, residents of Bastatha preferred chilled foods.<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref>
