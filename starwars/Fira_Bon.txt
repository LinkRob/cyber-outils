'''Fira Bon''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[soldier]] who was active during the [[New Republic Era]].

==Biography==
===Early life===
Fira Bon joined the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Defense Force|military]] in the wake of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. She quickly rose through the ranks to become a top reconnaissance specialist. While the New Republic believed the military should be a streamlined peacekeeping force, Fira believed they should be probing deeper into the clues of something was trying to rebuild the [[Galactic Empire]] on the frontier. Unable to pursue the type of operations she thought were needed, she left military service.<ref name="TFA Beginner game" />

===Life on the Frontier===
Fira spent time as a wandering adventurer and mercenary until a botched job left her stranded on [[Jakku]]. She ended up staying on the planet, hoping to spend the rest of her life in peace. While on Jakku, she worked with a team of adventurers to discover the secrets of a crashed New Republic [[Starlight Wanderer|corvette]].<ref name="TFA Beginner game" />

==Personality and traits==
Her time spent on the New Republic frontier has made Fira believe that the Galactic Empire was not as dead as everyone thinks. She makes a point of talking with junk traders and others who come to Jakku to gather news of the New Republic and the [[Resistance]], wishing that she had joined the resistance fighters.<ref name="TFA Beginner game" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fira Bon is one of the pre-generated characters from [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying box set]] which was released in late [[2016]].<ref name="TFA Beginner game" />
