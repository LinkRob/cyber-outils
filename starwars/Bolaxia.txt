'''Bolaxia''' was a location in the fictional [[holodrama]] ''[[The Adventures of Flux Magnetar]]''. The series released at least twenty nine episodes<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> In the series, Bolaxia is ruled over by a [[king]]. After an initial uprising failed [[Flux Magnetar]] is sentenced to trial by combat in the burning pits of [[Mount Vardosh]] by [[Dornigar]]. After surviving the ordeal and defeating Dornigar, Flux regroups with a group of [[krill]] [[farmer]]s. Vowing to not leave until the people of Bolaxia are free, Flux plans to train the farmers to be warriors to lead a new uprising.<ref name="Outlaws" />
