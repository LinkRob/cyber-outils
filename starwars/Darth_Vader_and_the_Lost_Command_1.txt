'''''Darth Vader and the Lost Command'' 1''' is the first issue of five in the ''[[Star Wars: Darth Vader and the Lost Command]]'' comic mini-series written by [[W. Haden Blackman]]. It was released on [[January 26]], [[2011]]. In addition to the regular cover, an alternate cover was also published as part of the 25th Anniversary.

==Publisher's summary==
''Still haunted by the events in Revenge of the Sith, Darth Vader must set aside his past and put his future on hold for a mission to the mysterious Ghost Nebula to locate&mdash;and if possible, rescue&mdash;a missing Imperial expeditionary force.''

''But what seems a perfect opportunity to lose himself in his duty is complicated by two factors: he is paired with a willful yet highly competent co-commander, and the officer they are being sent to locate is the son of Vader's rising nemesis, Moff Tarkin.''
