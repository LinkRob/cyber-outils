The '''Centrality''' was a [[sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] which contained the [[Oseon system|Oseon]], [[Renatasia system|Renatasia]], and [[Tund system]]s<ref name="TFABG" />, and many other systems besides.<ref name="Appendix" /> It was an independently-governed sector that was founded by idealist settlers who broke with the [[Galactic Republic]] out of a belief that the government was corrupt.<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|18|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>

{{Sec-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Centrality first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel ''[[Lando Calrissian and the Mindharp of Sharu]]'', which was written by [[L. Neil Smith]] and released in [[1983]].
