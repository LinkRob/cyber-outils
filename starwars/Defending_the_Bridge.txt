"'''Defending the Bridge'''" is a scenario in [[Star Wars Miniatures Battles (first edition)|''Star Wars Miniatures Battles'']].

==Plot summary==
During a battle in the [[Kwymar sector/Legends|Kwymar sector]], an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Assault shuttle/Legends|assault shuttle]] was forced to land on a [[Planet/Legends|planet]]. Both the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]] sent forces to recover the vessel.
