A [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] '''base''' was established on the [[planet]] [[Aargonar]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. The [[Mina Bonteri's husband|husband]] of [[Senator]] [[Mina Bonteri]], an officer in the [[Confederacy military]], assisted in the installation's establishment when Republic [[clone trooper]]s were sent to Aargonar and [[Attack on Aargonar|attacked]] the site, resulting in the [[death]] of Bonteri's husband.<ref name="Heroes" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Aargonar Separatist base was first mentioned in "[[Heroes on Both Sides]],"<ref name="Heroes" /> the tenth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which aired on [[November 19]], [[2010]].<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode310.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref>
