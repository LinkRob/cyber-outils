The '''Caltrop-5 chaff gun''' was a shrapnel weapon designed by [[Arakyd Industries]]. It was developed to spoof [[sensor]]s, though could be used as an anti-[[Starship|ship]] weapon. [[Jadthu-class landing ship|''Jadthu''-class landing ships]] were equipped with the Caltrop-5, which saw use against the [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Battle of Haruun Kal]].<ref name="Knights of Fate" />
