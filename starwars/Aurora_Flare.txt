The '''''Aurora Flare''''' was an [[MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80A ''Home One'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]] that was part of the [[Mon Cala Mercantile Fleet|Mon Cala Imperial Trade Fleet]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and carried a shipment of [[coaxium]] that was meant to be taken to the [[Redhurne system]]. After the trade [[fleet]] [[Mutiny on Mon Cala|mutinied]], the vessel's [[captain]], [[Pysk Lila]], piloted the ''Aurora Flare'' near Imperial [[Admiral]] [[Tyrus Meori]]'s ''[[Aquilifer]]'' in order to break the Empire's [[Mon Cala blockade|blockade]] of Mon Cala. Meori's ship fired on the ''Aurora Flare'', causing its shipment to explode. The ''Aurora Flare'' was destroyed and the ''Aquilifer'' was damaged. The ''Aurora Flare''{{'s}} sacrifice allowed for the remaining vessels to escape the [[blockade]].<ref name="SW49" />
