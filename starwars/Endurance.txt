The '''''Endurance''''' was a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] that served in the [[Open Circle Fleet]]'s [[Fifth Fleet]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Navy|naval forces]], where it served as the [[flagship]] of [[Admiral]] [[Shoan Kilian]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Death Trap" /> It later crashed on [[Vanqor]] following crippling [[Sabotage of the Endurance|sabotage]] perpetrated by the young [[Boba Fett]], who had attempted to kill the on-board [[Jedi]] [[Mace Windu]] using a bomb planted within his [[Jango Fett|father]]'s [[Boba Fett's armor|helmet]]. Windu and [[Anakin Skywalker]] were trapped in the ship's wreckage following the bomb's detonation until [[R2-D2]] returned to [[Coruscant]] and requested help. Jedi Master [[Plo Koon]], the [[Wolfpack]], and [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] soon came to their aid and rescued Windu and Skywalker.<ref name="R2 Come Home">{{TCW|R2 Come Home}}</ref>

==Description==
The ''Endurance'' was a 1,137 [[meter]]-long<ref name="Size" /> [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] with two [[Bridge (starship)|bridges]] and a ventral [[hangar]] bay. The [[Star Destroyer]] was also equipped with a [[navigation computer]], [[Escape pod|escape craft]], and was armed with [[DBY-827 heavy turbolaser turret]]s and medium dual [[turbolaser]] [[cannon]]s.<ref name="Death Trap" />
