'''Aemon Gremm''' was a [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Databank" /> [[Hylobon]] who served as [[captain]] of [[Dryden Vos]]' [[Hylobon Enforcers|security forces]].<ref name="Solo" /> He<ref name="Databank" /> [[Death|died]] on [[Savareen]] when [[Enfys Nest]] [[Showdown on Savareen|attacked]] his team and broke his neck.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Aemon has been with me for a while, and he remembers the old days. Back then, I would have had three times as many guards at a party like this. But that was when the five syndicates worked against each other. Now that we are allies, we cooperate. I suppose he misses the good old days, when we could operate however we wanted.|Dryden Vos, on Aemon Gremm|<ref name="Crimson Climb">''[[Crimson Climb]]''</ref>}}
Gremm took his responsibilities as [[bodyguard]] to Vos very seriously, and expected the same dedication from the security staff who reported to him.<ref name="Stickers">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story Ultimate Sticker Collection]]''</ref> By insisting all security concerns come to him, he would take credit for his team's work and made himself essential to Crimson Dawn's operation. Like most individuals of his [[species]], he possessed a strong instinct for physical domination.<ref name="TOG" />
