The [[Rodian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Do N-Yuk]] owned<ref name="Payback" /> a '''fighter'''<ref name="PaybackAD">{{Visions|The Duel: Payback}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> [[Standard decade|decades]] after the [[Sith rebellion]].<ref name="Ronin">''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]''</ref> The [[starship]] docked to the [[AK-BK]] [[walker]] where the [[gambling]] hall [[To-Ba]] was located, on the [[planet]] [[Olcatz]]. When the former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] [[Duel on Olcatz|dueled]] the [[Twi'lek]] [[Aneé-san]], Do N-Yuk informed the [[Crusaders (The Grand Master)|Crusaders]] of the Ronin's presence, shortly afterwards taking off on his fighter before the destruction of the establishment. Sometime later, after the Crusaders were defeated, the Rodian loaded up [[the Grand Master]]'s [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] implants onto his ship to re-sell them.<ref name="Payback" />

==Behind the scenes==
The design of Do N-Yuk's fighter resembles that of the 1983 Kenner vehicle toy [[AST-5 Armored Sentinel Transport]].<ref name="AFA">''[[Star Wars: The Action Figure Archive]]''</ref>
