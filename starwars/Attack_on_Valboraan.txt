During the [[Republic Era]], [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]] forces '''attacked''' the [[Riftwalker Headquarters|headquarters]] of the [[Riftwalkers]] [[pirate]] gang on the [[planet]] [[Valboraan]]. [[Zilastra]], the leader of the Riftwalkers, had previously captured [[Jedi Master]] [[Depa Billaba]], but the [[Youngling|young]] [[Gender|girl]] [[Kylah Lohmata]] sent out a call for help that was picked up by Jedi Master [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. The pair subsequently summoned Jedi Master [[Mace Windu]] aboard the [[Republic Diplomatic Fleet]] [[CR56 corvette|corvette]] ''[[Assurance]]'', commanded by [[Captain]] [[Pell Baylo]]. ''Assurance'' fired upon the headquarters, causing significant damage, while the Jedi landed to rescue Billaba. Although the intervention saved Billaba, Zilastra threatened Lohmata's life, forcing the Jedi to allow her and the Riftwalkers to escape aboard her [[flagship]], the ''[[Randomizer]]''.<ref name="The Living Force" />
