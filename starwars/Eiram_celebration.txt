The '''Eiram celebration''' was an event that took place on [[Eiram]] in [[230 BBY]].<ref name="date" /> It was held to celebrate the arrival of [[Starlight Beacon]] to assist with the recovery of the [[planet]] from a hemisphere-wide storm that damaged the desalination structures that provided fresh water.
