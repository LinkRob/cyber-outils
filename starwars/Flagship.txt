A '''flagship''' referred to the personal [[starship]] of a [[fleet]] [[commander]] in command of a group of [[Navy|naval]] vessels. The [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Executor]]'' served as the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] flagship as well as the [[command ship]] of [[Death Squadron]].<ref name="Executor">{{Databank|executor|''Executor''}}</ref> The ''[[Ravager]]'' served as the command ship of [[Counselor to the Empire]] [[Gallius Rax]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] at the [[Battle of Jakku]]. [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] utilized the [[Eclipse (Executor-class)|''Eclipse'']] as his personal flagship.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>

The [[MC80 Star Cruiser]] ''[[Home One]]'' served as the flagship of [[Admiral]] and later [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]], as well as the command ship for the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance]] and [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Defense Fleet|fleets]].<ref name="charencupdated">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> The [[MC75 Star Cruiser]] ''[[Profundity]]'' served as the flagship of Admiral [[Raddus]] at the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> ''[[Phoenix Home]]'' served as the flagship of [[Jun Sato]], [[Commander]] of the Rebel cell known as [[Phoenix Cell]].<ref name="RR">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''</ref>

In the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]'' served as the flagship and mobile headquarters of the First Order and [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]]'s command ship, while the [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] was the flagship of the Resistance. Both suffered catastrophic damage when Resistance [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] crashed the ''Raddus'' into the ''Supremacy'' through [[hyperspace]], ending the [[Battle of Oetchi]].<ref name="TLJ Expanded">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>
