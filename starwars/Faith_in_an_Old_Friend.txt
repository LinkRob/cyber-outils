"'''Faith in an Old Friend'''" is a short story that was published in the [[2020]] anthology book ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''. It was written by [[Brittany N. Williams]] and focuses on [[L3-37]] and the [[Millennium Collective|droid brains]] of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.
