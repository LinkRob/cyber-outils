The '''Dolla system''' was located in the [[Videnda sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="TheAcolyteVG" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western" /> It was situated in grid square L-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. The Dolla [[Star system|system]] was connected via a [[Karfeddion–Dolla hyperlane|hyperspace route]] to the [[Karfeddion system]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]], while [[Gerrenthum–Eriadu hyperlane|another route]] linked it to the Outer Rim's [[Urber system|Urber]]<ref name="TheAcolyteVG" /> and [[Bavva system]]s.<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|18|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoVGDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> the Dolla system fell within the borders of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="MandoVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dolla system was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[January 29]], [[2016]] [[StarWars.com]] article "[[Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?|Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?]]" by [[Mark Newbold]].<ref name="TFA">{{SW|url=news/where-in-the-galaxy-are-the-worlds-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?|int=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?}}</ref> It was then identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on StarWars.com<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Dolla system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
