The '''''Aurora''''' was an [[MC80 Liberty Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80 ''Liberty'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]] that served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Rebel Alliance Navy|navy]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Description==
The ''Aurora'' was an [[MC80 Liberty Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80 ''Liberty'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]] that served as the flagship of a small [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Alliance]] [[Gial Ackbar's fleet|fleet]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] under the command of [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]]. The fleet consisted of nine [[CR90 corvette]]s, seven [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]]s, and five [[GR-75 medium transport]]s, around the time of the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Squadrons" />

==History==
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the ''Aurora'' notably participated in a [[Battle at Fostar Haven|battle]] at [[Fostar Haven]], where it fought against part of a [[Rae Sloane's fleet|fleet]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] led by Imperial [[Commodore]] [[Rae Sloane]], which was attempting to intercept a [[Alderaanian refugee convoy|convoy]] of [[GR-75 medium transport]]s carrying [[Alderaanian]] refugees following the destruction of their homeworld of Alderaan.<ref name="Squadrons" />

After [[Echo Squadron]] engaged Imperial forces, the ''Aurora'' arrived behind the Imperial [[flagship]] [[Vigilance (Imperial-class)|''Vigilance'']] and other Imperial [[warship]]s and began to attack. The ''Aurora'' was reinforced by several ships from its fleet, including three CR90 corvettes and one EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate. After the convoy managed to jump into [[hyperspace]], the rebel forces, including the ''Aurora'', retreated.<ref name="Squadrons" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Aurora'' first appeared in the [[2020]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]'' by [[Motive Studios]], a division of [[Electronic Arts]]. The ''Aurora'' was identified within the game when the vessel is targeted by the player.<ref name="Squadrons" />
