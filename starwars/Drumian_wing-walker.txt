A '''Drumian wing-walker''' was an entity known to [[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]]. While defending the [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] against the [[Nihil]] maurauders' [[Valo Atrocity|attack]] on the [[planet]] [[Valo]], Mann was astonished by the sight of fellow Master [[Porter Engle]] riding atop a [[Jedi Vector]] [[starfighter]] mid-battle, comparing the feat to a wing-walker.<ref name="TRS">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>
