A '''cargo signature scrambler''' was a type of circuity that could be used to impede detection and reading of cargo signatures. As such, the [[Galactic Empire]] had made them illegal<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> However, the scrambler continued to be available on the [[black market]] and used by experienced [[smuggler]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Miyuki Trade League]] would regularly give out scramblers to individuals who successfully completed smuggling contracts for them. [[Merchant]]s affiliated with the Trade League would also be willing to sell the scramblers.<ref name="Outlaws" />
