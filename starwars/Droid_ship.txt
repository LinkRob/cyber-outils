A model of '''droid ship''' was utilized by [[pirate]] [[captain]] [[Orkaro]] in an attack on [[New Republic]] [[Diplomat|diplomats]] on the outskirts of the [[Hosnian system]] between [[31 ABY|31]] and [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">In this issue, [[Poe Dameron]] is already a member of the [[Resistance]] and is subject to the [[New Republic]]'s authority. According to ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', Poe Dameron joined the Resistance three years prior to the events of {{Film|VII}}, which corresponds to [[31 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. As the New Republic is destroyed in ''The Force Awakens'', the events must take place between these two events, meaning it must take place between 31 and [[34 ABY]].</ref> She utilized four droid ships in the attack, but they were all destroyed by the timely appearance of [[Resistance]] pilot [[Poe Dameron]]. With the droid ships shot down, and her own vessel disabled by heat seekers, Orkaro ordered her crew to retreat back to their base.<ref name="Codebreaker" />
