The '''Dianoga Set''' was the name given to a set of two weapons, an [[Arc Caster]] and a [[Pulse Blaster]], utilized by the team of [[Jawa]] brothers known collectively as the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Utooni]] that they could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to their [[Suspicious Set|standard set]]. These weathered algae-covered [[weapon]]s featured a [[Color|brown]], gray, and silver color scheme, with many circular components, exposed red and blue wires, and fake bright red [[dianoga]] tentacles wrapped around them.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dianoga Set was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Utooni's Suspicious Set, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 4.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Dianoga Diver]].<ref name="Hunters" />
