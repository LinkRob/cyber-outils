"'''Droïd Connexion'''" is a [[roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure published in the [[Backstab 11|eleventh issue]] of the French magazine [[Backstab (magazine)|''Backstab'']] in [[September]] [[1998]]. It was written by [[Timbre Poste]], with illustrations by [[Emmanuel Milachak]] and [[Frank Poterlot]].
