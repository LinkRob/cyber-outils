'''Boarding pods''' were [[escape pod]]s modified as [[boarding craft]] and used by the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]'', the headquarters and [[troop carrier]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]]. Equipped with [[Magnetic grapple/Canon|magnetic grapples]] and [[laserdrill]]s, the maneuverability of the escape pod it once was was traded for launch power and the [[armor]] was reinforced. The pods were used by the 61st to insert personnel onto other [[starship]]s, notably during a [[Raid on an Imperial heavy freighter|raid]] on an [[Unidentified Imperial heavy freighter|Imperial freighter]].<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Boarding pods appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="BTC" />
