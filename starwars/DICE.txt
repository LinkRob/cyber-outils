'''EA Digital Illusions Creative Entertainment AB''' (commonly shortened as EA DICE or DICE) is a [[Star Wars video games|video game]] development company founded in [[1992]] and located in Stockholm, Sweden. The company is responsible for the creation of the first ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]'' in the reboot series and the sequel ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''.

==History==
On [[June 10]], [[2013]], EA announced that DICE would be the developers behind ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]'', a new game part of the [[Star Wars Battlefront (series)|''Battlefront'']] series started by [[Pandemic Studios]]<ref name="Star Wars: Battlefront Official E3 Preview">{{EASWYouTube|Vx4JQf8qR-k|Star Wars: Battlefront Official E3 Preview}}</ref>. The game released on November 17, 2015<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=games-apps/star-wars-battlefront|text=Star Wars Battlefront}}</ref>. 6 downloadable content packs were released over the game's lifespan, those being:
*Star Wars Battlefront: Battle of Jakku; released [[December]] [[1]] 2015 (pre-orders), December 8 2015 (all players)<ref name="Battle of Jakku Free DLC">{{EA|subdomain=starwars|url=starwars/battlefront/features/battle-of-jakku|text=Star Wars Battlefront Battle of Jakku - Star Wars - Official EA Site}}.</ref>
*Star Wars Battlefront: Outer Rim; released March 22, 2016 (season pass holders)<ref name="Outer Rim Gameplay trailer">{{EASWYouTube|77CMcPbLeKs|Star Wars Battlefront - Outer Rim Gameplay Trailer}}</ref>, April 5, 2016 (all players).
*Star Wars Battlefront: Bespin; released June 21, 2016
*Star Wars Battlefront: Death Star; released September 20, 2016
*Star Wars Battlefront: Rogue One: Scarif; released December 6, 2016
*Star Wars Battlefront: Rogue One: X-wing VR Mission; released December, 2016<ref name="Rogue One: X-wing VR Mission announcement">{{EA|subdomain=starwars|url=starwars/battlefront/news/x-wing-vr-mission-announce|text=Star Wars™ Battlefront: X-Wing VR Mission on Target for Release Date this Holiday - Star Wars - Official EA Site}}</ref>.
