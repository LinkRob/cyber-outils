The '''ash angels''' were a [[species]] of scavenging [[bird]]s that lived on the [[planet]] of [[Sullust]].<ref name="Twilight Company" /> They were featherless, covered in thin strips of tissue. Despite lacking feathers, they flew gracefully above the surface of Sullust's lava rivers and could execute precise maneuvers such as corkscrews.<ref name="FACPOV" />
