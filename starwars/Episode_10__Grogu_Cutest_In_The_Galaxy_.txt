The '''tenth episode''' of the animated web series ''[[Grogu Cutest In The Galaxy]]'' released on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. It was posted by ''[[Star Wars]]'' Japan on [[Wikipedia:Instagram Reels|Instagram Reels]].<ref name="Instagram" />

{{Media-stub}}

==Plot summary==
[[Din Grogu|Grogu]], while sitting on a rock with his eyes closed, notices that [[Luke Skywalker]], seated next to him, raises his arm, using [[the Force]] to levitate eight [[Arges frog]]s nearby. Skywalker then drops them, and one of the frogs lands on Grogu's head.
