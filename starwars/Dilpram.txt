'''Dilpram''' was a [[Chiss]] [[Gender|female]] and biological [[scientist]] who lived on the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[planet]] [[Naporar]] with her [[Marriage|husband]], [[Lakbulbup]].<ref name="Greater Good" /> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' take place in [[18 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Lakbulbup's [[Xodlak family|cousin]], [[Xodlak'phr'ooa]], [[core name]] "Lakphro," [[Homeworld|from]] the planet [[Celwis]], sent the couple a [[nyix brooch]] given to him by [[Haplif]] of the [[Agbui]] for analysis as having it analyzed on Celwis was too risky. Although Dilpram worked in a biological [[laboratory]], Lakphro hoped she would be able to send the brooch to a metallurgist. However, the brooch was instead sent to Chiss [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn," renowned for his ability to analyze alien artwork.<ref name="Greater Good" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dilpram was mentioned in ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'', the second installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Greater Good" />
