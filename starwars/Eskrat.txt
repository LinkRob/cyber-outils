{{Quote|And now you're all going to be running like eskrats, because the Empire's dropped by.|Skelly compares Kanan Jarrus and the miners of Moonglow Polychemical to eskrats|<ref name="AND" />}}
'''Eskrats''' were a [[species]] of [[rat]] that ran around a lot. The demolitions expert [[Skelly]] compared the [[pilot]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] and the [[miner]]s of the [[Moonglow Polychemical]] company to eskrats after Jarrus showed no interest in hearing about a potential [[mining]] hazard.<ref name="AND" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eskrats were first mentioned in [[Millennium Falcon (novel)|''Millennium Falcon'']], a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel written by [[James Luceno]] that was published in [[2008]].<ref name="MF">[[Millennium Falcon (novel)|''Millennium Falcon'']]</ref> They were made [[canon]] by a mention in [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']] by [[John Jackson Miller]], a novel published in [[2014]].<ref name="AND" />
