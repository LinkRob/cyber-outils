The '''Devoted Guardian''' was a bodily configuration equipped by the [[droid]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[J-3DI]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Noble Jedi|standard configuration]]. It was designed to resemble the [[Color|cream]] and gold aesthetic of the [[Jedi Temple Guard]] [[Jedi Temple Guard armor|armor]].<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Devoted Guardian was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter J-3DI, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Devoted Saber Design]]. This costume could be unlocked by reaching J-3DI Hunter Fame level 24.<ref name="Hunters" />
