'''2022 Topps ''Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett''''' is a trading card set released by [[Topps]] on [[November 23]], [[2022]] featuring characters from the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]''.

==Publisher's summary==
''From Bounty Hunter to Daimyo, join Boba Fett in his destiny to rule the underworld of the Star Wars galaxy, in Topps Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett trading cards!''

''Collect new autograph signers, screen-used prop relics, and exclusive Metal Card Variations!''
