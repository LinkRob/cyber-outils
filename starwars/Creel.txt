'''Creel''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who [[Commander|commanded]] the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Riot Squadron]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. Sometime prior to the [[Skirmish at Jiruus|skirmish]] above the [[planet]] [[Jiruus]], which occurred several [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Alphabet" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Creel retrieved what he assumed were orders that his computer had decrypted for him which detailed a redeployment to an Alliance base on the terrestrial astronomical object [[Kalarba]].<ref name="Alphabet" />

Riot Squadron spent six [[Standard hour|hours]] looking for the supposed base, first assuming that it had been well hidden but later becoming worried that the Alliance personnel on Kalarba were no longer there. Creel began to decrypt the orders using different codes, soon realizing that he had accidentally downloaded the contents of a [[flight simulator]] and mistaken it for a real mission. [[Sata Neek]], a [[pilot]] in [[Riot Squadron]], later recounted Creel's mishap when aboard the [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] ''[[Hellion's Dare]]'' several weeks after the Battle of [[Endor]].<ref name="Alphabet" />
