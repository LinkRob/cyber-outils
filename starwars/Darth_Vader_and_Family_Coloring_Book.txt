The '''''Darth Vader and Family Coloring Book''''' is a coloring book that features artwork from writer/artist [[Jeffrey Brown]]'s [[Darth Vader and Son (series)|''Darth Vader and Son'']] series, along with nine new illustrations. It was published by [[Chronicle Books]] on [[March 28]], [[2017]].
