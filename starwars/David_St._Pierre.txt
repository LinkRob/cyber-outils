'''David St. Pierre''' worked as a creature performer for five episodes of the live-action [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]''.<ref name="Part1" /> They continued to work as a performance artist for various aliens in the live-action series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''<ref name="Chapter17">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 17: The Apostate}}</ref> and ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]''.<ref name="SC2">{{SkeletonCrew|Way, Way Out Past the Barrier}}</ref>
