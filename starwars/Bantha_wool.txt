'''Bantha wool''' also spelled '''Bantha-wool'''<ref name="Guide" />, or '''Bantha-fiber wool'''<ref name="MandoVG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> was a type of [[Hair|wool]] harvested from [[bantha]]s, a hairy, domesticated [[species]] [[Reproduction|bred]] for their wool as well as their [[Foodstuff|meat]], [[Bantha hide|hide]], and [[Blue milk|milk]]. The wool was mentioned in an entry on banthas in the section concerning the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] in the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s [[book]], ''[[Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''.<ref name="Guide" /> [[Vi Moradi]] liked to knit using bantha wool yarn to make fuzzy hats.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref>
