The '''Dargas''' were a [[Near-Human]] [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Species/Legends|species]] native to the red desert planet [[Zender (planet)|Zender]].<ref name="Froid" />

==Biology and appearance==
The Dargas were a near-Human species, differing from baseline Humans by their [[Color/Legends|blue]] skin and red [[Eye/Legends|eyes]]. They were the genetic descendants of the original [[Human/Legends|Human]] inhabitants of Zender, who were mutated via exposure to radioactive [[dorium]] in the planet's crust.<ref name="Froid" />

==Society and culture==
Darga society was nomadic, using [[Bantha/Legends|banthas]] to move between oases in the vast deserts of their homeworld. Over [[Time/Legends|time]], the Dargas organized into tribes, who perpetually warred against each other over possession of the oases and the fruit trees that grew there.<ref name="Froid" />

==History==
When the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] made contact with the Dargas, the feuding tribes put aside their differences and united against the invaders, fighting them with guerrilla tactics. The tribal alliance was led by a chief mystic named [[Zindati]].<ref name="Froid" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dargas were created by [[Jean Balczesak]] for the ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] scenario "[[Plus froid que l'espace]]," which appeared in the French magazine ''[[Casus Belli HS12]]''.
