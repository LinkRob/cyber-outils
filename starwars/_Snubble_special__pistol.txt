The '''"snubble special" pistol''', also known as just the '''"snubble"''', was a [[Color|black-colored]] model of double-snub nosed [[blaster pistol]]. The [[blaster]] had limited ammunition but packed a powerful punch and included a [[stun]] setting. The [[Grindalid]] [[Moloch]], a [[lieutenant]] of the [[White Worms]] gang, favored using a "snubble" that was equipped with a [[scope]] with built in [[luma-compensator]]s. He also removed the stun setting on his pistol to ensure he always used [[Death|lethal]] force.<ref name="SoloOG" />

In [[13 BBY]], Moloch was carrying the pistol when the [[scrumrat]] [[Han Solo|Han]] was brought before [[Lady]] [[Proxima]], leader of the White Worms. The lieutenant used it to threaten Han by pointing it at his head; however, Han escaped with the aid of fellow scrumrat [[Qi'ra]]. Moloch gave chase, firing his "snubble" at them from the driver's seat of his [[A-A4B truckspeeder|A-A4B landspeeder]] as they raced through the streets of [[Coronet City]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The "snubble special" pistol was created for the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Solo" /> and was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SoloOG" />
