'''Botanist''' was a scientific profession concerning [[Plant|plants]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], numerous botanists and [[horticulturalist]]s visited the [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]].<ref name="Hidden Danger">{{InsiderCite|issue1=203|issue2=204|Starlight: Hidden Danger}}</ref> The [[alien]] [[Pili Walde]] was a botanist<ref name="SWJS">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> and the [[human]] [[Fadros Angon]] was formally a botanist.<ref name="TRoS">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
