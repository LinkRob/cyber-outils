'''Chi-Chi''' was a [[tooka]] [[cat]] that lived in the [[Imperial Elderblock 26-0640]] on the [[Ecumenopolis|city]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. He had originally belonged to [[Chi-Chi's original owner|a later deceased resident]]. After his death, [[Chi-Chi's original owner's family member|a family member]] of Chi-Chi's original owner had taken him back to their home which was half-an hour's [[speeder]] ride away, but Chi-Chi had escaped and returned to the Elderblock and stayed there ever since. He was clever and took advantage of the facility's automated doors to get inside rooms, with the residents often being surprised by him entering their apartments and attempting to outsmart the tooka. Chi-Chi mostly lived with the the resident [[Urbra Procura]], with none of the current residents remembering a time without the cat. Procura enjoyed Chi-Chi's the company and considered him friendly and fearless and a little endearingly dim-witted like an [[Dog|hound]].<ref name="Elderblock" />
