'''Alma''' was an innkeeper ran a [[Alma's bed and breakfast|bed and breakfast]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Ah Re Won]]. When the [[cloak]]ed [[Sith apprentice]] [[Darth]] [[Maul]] arrived to her [[city|town]] to [[meditation|meditate]] at the ruins of a [[Ah Re Won Sith temple|Sith temple]]<ref name="DM-BW&R 4" /> sometime between around [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Darth Maul – Black, White & Red 4|''Darth Maul – Black, White & Red'' 4]] take place during [[Maul|Darth Maul's]] [[time]] as the [[Sith apprentice]] of [[Darth Sidious]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to between around [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]].</ref> Alma offered the [[Zabrak]] stranger a room and [[foodstuff|meals]] for a fair price. He took her up on the deal as long as he could have quietude.<ref name="DM-BW&R 4" />

Alma was an early settler of the area, and during her time there, the settlement grew significantly. Nestled between a [[beach]] and the ruins of the Sith temple, the town attracted enough tourism to lead the newly appointed [[Unidentified Ah Re Won town mayor|mayor]], a greedy [[Sullustan]], to construct a flashy [[Unidentified hotel (Ah Re Won)|hotel]]. The [[mayor]] attempted to force Alma out of her building so he could improve his visitors' view of the ruins. He attempted stiff taxation, bothering her with noise, and, when those attempts failed, to [[fire|burn]] down her inn. The mayor's scheming was cut short by Maul, who grew annoyed by the commotion the Sullustan was creating. The next morning, the mayor's [[security droid]]s went to Alma for help when their boss went missing.<ref name="DM-BW&R 4" /> 

Later during Maul's visit to the ruins, Alma was accused by the [[Pirate King of Sector Nine]] for being complicit in the death of [[Afolonkin|his son]] at the hands of local [[Karafankata|beasts]]. The vengeful [[Pirate King|pirate king]] attempted to blast the woman with a [[tank]], but she was able to evade the blasts and get to a [[starship]]. Though the pirate king ordered his crew to fire upon the ship, they were unable due to Maul's intervention, allowing Alma to escape.<ref name="DM-BW&R 4" />
