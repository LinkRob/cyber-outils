A '''combat stick''' was a type of handheld [[melee weapon]]. [[Soft-coat stick]]s were a type of combat stick and, while they maintained the same weight and balance as real combat sticks, were mainly used for training and sparring purposes.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> Between [[39 BBY]] and [[37 BBY]],<ref name="Dojo Date">[[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] begins instruction at [[Taharim Academy]] around [[39 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:WhenThrassMetThrawnDate|here]]. ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' establishes that he graduated from the academy in two [[Standard year|years]], around [[37 BBY]]. As [[Ar'alani|Ziara]] held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[Senior Cadet|senior cadet]] during Thrawn's tenure at the academy, it must have taken place between 39 and 37 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the [[Chiss]] [[cadet]]s [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," and [[Ar'alani|Irizi'ar'alani]], core name "Ziara," sparred with soft-coat sticks at a [[Unidentified dojo (Naporar)|dojo]] on the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[planet]] of [[Naporar]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]'' to [[2 BBY]].</ref> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and Thrawn, who had since become a [[Grand Admiral]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Mission to Batuu (Galactic Empire)|visited]] a [[Oga's Cantina|cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the planet [[Batuu]]. While in the [[cantina]], several [[Darshi]] [[Soldier|warriors]] passed<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> tan-[[color]]ed<ref name="Thrawn Alliances 1">[[Thrawn: Alliances 1|''Thrawn: Alliances'' 1]]</ref> combat sticks among themselves before they used them to attack the pair. The [[Sith Lord]] and the Chiss quickly defeated the Darshi, [[Death|killing]] and neutralizing them.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

==Behind the scenes==
Combat sticks first appeared in the [[2018]] novel ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]'', the second installment in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[Star Wars: The Imperial Trilogy|''Star Wars: Thrawn'']] series.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> The sticks were first pictured in the [[Thrawn: Alliances 1|first issue]] of the [[2024]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries [[Star Wars: Thrawn: Alliances (comic series)|''Star Wars: Thrawn: Alliances'']] adapting the book's events. The issue was written by Zahn and [[Jody Houser]], illustrated by [[Pat Olliffe]] and [[Andrea Di Vito]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Thrawn Alliances 1" /> on [[January 24]], 2024.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/80019/star_wars_thrawn_alliances_2024_1|text=Star Wars: Thrawn Alliances (2024) #1}}</ref> The series' first issue,<ref name="Thrawn Alliances 1" /> as well as its [[Thrawn: Alliances 2|second issue]]&mdash;written by Zahn and Houser, illustrated by Olliffe and Di Vito, and published by Marvel<ref name="Thrawn Alliances 2">[[Thrawn: Alliances 2|''Thrawn: Alliances'' 2]]</ref> on [[February 28]], 2024<ref name="Marvel 2">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/80021/star_wars_thrawn_alliances_2024_2|text=Star Wars: Thrawn Alliances (2024) #2}}</ref>&mdash;depict Thrawn using a combat stick against the Darshi,<ref name="Thrawn Alliances 1" /><ref name="Thrawn Alliances 2" /> while the novel establishes Thrawn used a [[Thrawn's combat baton|combat baton]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> This article follows the original novel's events.
