A '''dart''' was a type projectile that was usually fired from a dart launcher.

Such a projectile could be filled with a [[Poison|toxin]] to create a [[poison dart]] capable of incapacitating or killing a target, such as the lethal [[Kamino saberdart]].

[[Electroshock dart]]s were used by [[Mandalorian warrior]]s, including [[Bo-Katan Kryze]].<ref name="Chapter 22">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 22: Guns for Hire}}</ref> [[Mandalorian]]s also utilized [[Mandalorian training darts|training darts]] during mock combat matches.<ref name="Chapter 20">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 20: The Foundling}}</ref>
