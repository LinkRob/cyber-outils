'''Adarlon''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Head" /> in the [[Adarlon system]], a [[star system]] in the [[Minos Cluster]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It hosted an [[Alderaanian embassy]] during the [[Imperial Era]],<ref name="Head" /> Its population supposedly suffered a [[Adarlon famine|famine]] while under the control of the [[New Republic]], according to claims made in the news feeds of the [[First Order]]. The [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Finn|FN-2187]] viewed such a news report during a morale session within his training on [[Starkiller Base]], shortly before his deployment to the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]<ref name="BTA">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], which depicts [[Finn]]'s deployment on [[Jakku]], to [[34 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Adarlon was first [[canon]]ically mentioned in the [[2014]] young-readers' book ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Head to Head]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] as a tie-in to the animated series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''.<ref name="Head to Head">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Head to Head]]''</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Adarlon/Legends|Adarlon]] was first mentioned in the [[1990]] [[West End Games]] ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' supplement ''[[Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters]]'', written by [[Mark Rein-Hagen]] and [[Stewart Wieck]].<ref name="GG 6">''[[Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters]]''</ref> Adarlon made its first appearance in the "[[Galaxywide NewsNets]]" feature in the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 5|fifth issue]] of the magazine ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]''.<ref name="GNN">{{JournalCite|5|Galaxywide NewsNets}}</ref>
