"'''A clone by any other name'''" is the fifteenth in a series of comics on the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] website. It is preceded by "[[A clone trooper always obeys orders]]" and is followed by "[[Clones are famous all across the galaxy]]." It was placed on the website [[August 21]], [[2009]].

==Plot summary==
The comic begins with the group of [[Cloning/Legends|clones]] assigned to find [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] leaving the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] from the previous comic. The clones are then shown continuing their search by traveling throughout [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]], occasionally getting into fights. At one point the group comes across [[IG-86 sentinel droid/Legends|assassin droids]] that managed to capture Artoo, but the clones fail to stop the [[Assassin droid/Legends|assassin droids]]. During this time, [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]] [[1139]] expresses to his companion, [[1137]], his desire to have his own name and not just a number. 1139 thinks up several names for himself and his comrade, but none suit. On the last planet, the team's [[Gunner/Legends|gunner]], [[Blasty]], takes out an [[OG-9 homing spider droid/Legends|OG-9 homing spider droid]]. The group's [[Clone Commander/Legends|commander]] says he will put in a good word for Blasty, and their pilot adds he may even get a new tank. 1139 becomes infuriated since he has developed a strong dislike for Blasty, since he has always wanted to drive a tank.
