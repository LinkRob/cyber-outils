'''Air shrimps''' were a type of creature found on the [[gas giant]] [[Bespin]], where they lived in the [[planet]]'s breathable layer of upper [[atmosphere]] amongst the [[Algae|algal trees]] that grew there.<ref name="USWNE" /> The shrimps had [[Color|pink]] skin and black [[eye]]s, with a segmented body ending in a barbed tail. They possessed a proboscis on the front of their head and three tendril like [[limb]]s located just behind their eyes. The creatures' wings were veined and tattered.<ref name="Atlas" /> Air shrimps traveled in schools and shimmered in rainbow colors to warn each other of [[Diet|predators]] like the [[rawwk]], which would chase air shrimp schools<ref name="USWNE" /> and prey upon them.<ref name="Atlas" />

At some point after [[34 ABY]], an illustration of an air shrimp was included on a map of Bespin thought to have been drawn by the [[artist]] [[Gammit Chond]] based purely on travelers' tales. The map was later restored and published by the [[Graf Archive]] in the ''[[Galactic Atlas]]''<ref name="Atlas" /> and was also included in the updated version of the [[book]].<ref name="AtlasUpdated">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Air shrimp were first mentioned in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> The creature was then pictured in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'', which was written by [[Emil Fortune]] and illustrated by [[Tim McDonagh]].<ref name="Atlas" />
