'''Carlos Garzón''' is a comic-book illustrator who produced a number of comics in [[Star Wars (1977)|Marvel's ''Star Wars'']] series. Garzon has worked professionally in comics since [[1975]] for [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] and [[Wikipedia:DC Comics|DC Comics]]. He assisted [[Al Williamson]] on his ''[[Star Wars]]'' comic adaptations of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''The Empire Strikes Back'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Return of the Jedi'']].
