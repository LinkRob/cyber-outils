A pair of '''Sith lightsabers''' were the personal weapons of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Momin]]. Sometime before the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Momin utilized these [[lightsaber]]s while training under [[Darth Shaa]]. Eventually he would use his sabers to cut down his [[Sith Master]].

==History==
===Early use===
At some point during his apprenticeship to [[Darth Shaa]], [[Darth Momin]] constructed a pair of [[Sith lightsaber]]s which he used to train with [[Sith Lord|Lady]] Shaa. However, Momin found the role of apprentice disgusting, considering himself second to no one. His [[Sith Master|master]] disagreed with him, so during one session, he proved his point by [[Death|impaling]] her through the chest with his blades.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

With his lightsabers, Momin embarked on his newest project to become worthy for [[the Force]], which he believed to be his only audience. Enlisting the help of a [[Darth Momin's acolytes|group of devoted acolytes]], Momin built a [[Darth Momin's superweapon|superweapon]] and used it to [[Darth Momin's destruction of a city|destroy]] a [[Unidentified city (Momin)|city]], planning to pour the Force into the [[engine]] at the moment of ignition and use it to slow down [[time]] forever trapping the expressions of pain and horror on the inhabitants. However, two [[Jedi]] arrived and slaughtered the acolytes, causing Momin to lose concentration and lose control over the [[Superweapon|weapon]], vaporizing into nothing and leaving only his spirit inside his [[Mask of Lord Momin|mask]].<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

===Return to Momin===
At some point after the [[Coruscant Incident|rogue Inquisitor chase]], Momin's spirit manifested to [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the [[Sith]] enforcer of the [[Galactic Empire]], told him his story and offered him his services into constructing a [[Fortress Vader|fortress]] for himself in addition to a [[portal]] which would open the gates to the [[dark side of the Force]], which would allow Vader, to reunite with his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. Vader accepted Momin's offer but warned him to not betray him.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

After eight failed designs, Momin finally achieved what Vader wanted for his fortress with the ninth design. Before he could prove the portal to the dark side Momin constructed in a [[Sith cave]], an [[army]] of [[Mustafarian]]s led by [[Father (title)|Father]] [[Kkkt]] [[Battle of Fortress Vader|battled]] [[Fortress Vader Imperial garrison|Imperial forces]]. But Vader hadn't realized Momin's true intentions: alone with the portal, Momin opened the gates and returned his [[Sith artifact|mask]] to his original body, killing the Imperial construction worker he was using as a host and wielding the pair of lightsabers once again.<ref name="Dark Lord 23">[[Darth Vader (2017) 23|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 23]]</ref>

===Final defeat===
After finishing with [[Kkkt's clan]], Vader returned to the [[cave]] only to find Momin waiting for him, who thanked him for protecting the castle from the Mustafarians. Vader drew [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] and they began [[Lightsaber duel|dueling]]. Momin used one of his blades to cut a part of Vader's left [[Limb|arm]].<ref name="Dark Lord 24">[[Darth Vader (2017) 24|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 24]]</ref>

Engaging Vader again, Momin [[Dismemberment|cut]] off Vader's right arm. Having seemingly beat Vader, Momin prepared to give the final blow, but he was unable to do so as Vader threw a massive [[stone]] at Momin, leading Momin to drop the sabers as the stone crushed him against a wall and killed him.<ref name="Dark Lord 24" />
