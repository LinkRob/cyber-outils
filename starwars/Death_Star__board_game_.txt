'''''Death Star''''' was a strategy board game released by the [[Tsukuda Hobby]] company in [[1982]], exclusively in Japan. It was based on the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]]. The game was designed by [[Atsutoshi Okada]].<ref name="SWI 195" /> The game could either be found with a ''[[Star Wars]]'' logo or a [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''The Empire Strikes Back'']] logo on the box.<ref name="Cargo Bay">{{CargoBay|url=item-detail/9777|text=Death Star}}</ref>

Tsukuda Hobby also released similar games for the other [[original trilogy]] films: [[Hoth (board game)|''Hoth'']] and [[Endor (board game)|''Endor'']].
