A '''crew''' was led by the [[pirate]] [[Crimson Jack]].<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 5" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Halcyon Legacy 1|''Halcyon Legacy'' 1]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the crew [[Attack on the Halcyon|attempted]] to claim a [[bounty]] from the [[First Order]] after learning that a [[Resistance]] [[spy]] had sent a transmission about a drive of secret codes. Jack was successful in his attempts to capture the spy, the [[Tholothian]] [[Shorr Komrrin]], though when he presented Komrrin to [[Lieutenant]] [[Harman Croy]], the pirate learned that Komrrin was not in possession of the drive and so was unable to claim the reward.<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 5" />
