'''Boba's rancor''' was a [[rancor]] that was owned by [[Boba Fett]], gifted to him by the [[Hutt]]s known as [[the Twins]]. A [[Unidentified rancor keeper|rancor keeper]] assisted Fett by taking care of the rancor, and arrived at [[Boba Fett's Palace]] along with the rancor as part of the Twins' tribute. Fett expressed a desire to learn to ride the rancor after hearing that the [[Nightsisters|Witches]] of [[Dathomir]] had been known to do so.

==History==
[[File:RancorKeeper-SWChrome2024.png|thumb|200px|left|Boba's rancor was tended by a rancor keeper.]]
Sometime around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="BoBF Date">{{BoBFDate}}</ref> the rancor was gifted to [[Daimyo]] [[Boba Fett]] by the [[Hutt]]s known as [[the Twins]]. A [[Unidentified rancor keeper|rancor keeper]] assisted Fett by taking care of the rancor, and arrived at [[Boba Fett's Palace]] along with the rancor as part of the Twins' tribute. The keeper informed the former [[bounty hunter]] that the rancor would bond with the first [[Human]] it saw. Fett expressed a desire to learn to ride the rancor after hearing that the [[Nightsisters|Witches]] of [[Dathomir]] had been known to do so. Wondering what to name his new pet, the keeper then instructed Fett to stand in front of the rancor as he removed its blinders. After recovering its sight, it immediately noticed Fett and imprinted on him. Approaching the rancor with ease, Fett began to pet the creature and found the spot it loved to be rubbed the most closest to its right eye. However, at that moment [[8D8]] interrupted them to inform them that [[Mayor]] [[Mok Shaiz]] was unavailable for the next 20 rotations. Leaving the rancor to be feed and to confront the mayor, the rancor looked disappointed. The rancor keeper however reassured it that Fett would be back.<ref name="Chapter 3" />

[[File:GroguCalmsRancor-BoBFCh7.png|thumb|right|250px|Grogu used the Force to calm the rampaging young rancor.]]
Fett went on to keep it in the rancor pit in Jabba's Palace, and used it to intimidate the crime bosses of [[Mos Espa]] when one of them threatened to kill him. Fett was able to calm his pet down by feeding it, but also threatened the other bosses that it was hungry, implying that he would feed them to it.<ref name="Chapter 4">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref> During the [[Battle of Mos Espa|Battle for Mos Espa]], the rancor has become Boba's personal mount. He used it to help destroy the two [[Scorpenek annihilator droid]]s that were set loose by the [[Pyke Syndicate]]. In the battle, Fett was knocked off, and the rancor started climbing the buildings of the city. The allied fighters tried to shoot it, but the rancor was then subdued and put to sleep by the [[Force-sensitive]] [[youngling]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], ending its rampage and saving its life.<ref name="In the Name of Honor">{{BOBF|Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor}}</ref>
