'''Ardees Beverage Company''' was a beverage manufacturing company that produced the [[Moogan]] [[tea]], [[Ardees Beverage]].<ref name="Star Wars: Scum and Villainy" /> The company was caught up in a controversy when [[Smuggler|smuggled]] Ardees Beverage mixed with the potentially [[poison]]ous diluting agent [[slabin]] caused an outbreak of illness among schoolchildren on [[Mandalore]].<ref name="Corruption" /> The incident was precipitated by an individual within the company who helped the smuggler [[Tee Va]] acquire the Ardees Beverage concentrate that caused the illness once mixed with slabin.<ref name="Star Wars: Scum and Villainy" />
