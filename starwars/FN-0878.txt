'''FN-0878''' was a [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> who served in the [[First Order]]'s [[FN Trooper Corps]]<ref name="BTA" /> as a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]]<ref name="Episode IX" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="IX year">According to ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]]. The Hosnian Cataclysm was dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' meaning Episode IX, in which FN-0878 appears, takes place in [[35 ABY]].</ref>
