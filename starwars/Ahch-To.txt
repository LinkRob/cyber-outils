{{Quote|Do you think I came to the most unfindable place in the galaxy for no reason at all?|Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
'''Ahch-To''' {{Pronounced|ɑkˈtoʊ}}<ref name="Pronounciation">{{StarWarsShow|p09c0MXDxto|Star Wars: The Last Jedi Secrets Explained|sw_url=video/secrets-explained-the-last-jedi}}</ref>, known by many names in ancient legends, was a [[planet]] mostly covered in [[water]], with numerous rocky islands blanketed with [[Color|green]] [[tree]]s located in the [[Unknown Regions]]. It was the birthplace of the [[Jedi Order]] and held the [[Sacred Jedi texts|ancient Jedi texts]]. During the [[New Republic Era]], it served as the home of [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] who went into [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] seeking the [[first Jedi Temple]] after his [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker|temple]] and his [[Jedi]] [[Padawan|students]] were [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destroyed]] by [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]. After the [[Resistance]] pieced together two [[Map to Skywalker|map]] fragments to reveal Skywalker's location, [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] traveled to Ahch-To in the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' accompanied by [[R2-D2]] and [[Chewbacca]], in search of him.

==Description==
===Astrography and atmosphere===
Ahch-To was an uncharted world,<ref name="TLJVD" /> located in the [[Ahch-To system]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition" /> within the [[Unknown Regions]] of [[the galaxy]], positioned at the coordinates F-13<ref name="SW Galaxy Map" /> on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Rebel Dossier">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''</ref> Ahch-To [[orbit]]ed twin [[star]]s,<ref name="TLJVD" /> including the [[Ahch-To's second sun|second sun]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> In turn, the planet was orbited by [[Moon of Ahch-To|one]] [[moon]].<ref name="Last Jedi Junior" /> The planet supported a breathable [[atmosphere]].<ref name="Episode VIII" /> The world shared a mysterious [[time]]-related quality with such locales as [[Mortis]] and [[Dagobah]].<ref name="TLJVD" />

Due to the the planet's great distance relative to its twin suns, Ahch-To supported a [[Temperature|temperate]]<ref name="TROSVD" /> and damp,<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> but volatile climate that frequently caused salt spray, cold winds, and violent storms,<ref name="TLJVD" /> unlike the [[desert]] world of [[Tatooine]].<ref name="TROSVD" /> Ahch-To also experienced [[summer]]s, [[autumn]]s, and [[winter]]s.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

===Geography===
{{Quote|You imagine an ocean. I see it. I see the island.|Kylo Ren|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:Ahch-To_surface.png|thumb|left|250px|The landscape of Ahch-To]]
The surface of the [[planet]] was nothing but deep [[Color|blue]] [[ocean]]s.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Dotting the oceans, however, were [[Volcano|volcanic]]<ref name="TFA novel" /> island archipelagos<ref name="Rey's Story" /> formed of [[Color|black]] [[Stone|rock]]<ref name="TFA novel" /> that were covered with few<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> green [[tree]]s<ref name="Rey's Story">''[[The Force Awakens: Rey's Story]]''</ref> and [[grass]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> [[Temple Island]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> was situated between the [[Sea of Pallaskea]] and the [[Bonthian Sea]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas UE" />

===Flora and fauna===
Ahch-To's islands' rugged cliffs were home to [[moss]], grass, and [[Fern (plant)|fern]]s.<ref name="VE" /> An [[algae]] flourished in the seashore pools.<ref name="Last Jedi Junior" /> Two [[Force-sensitive]] plants, the [[uneti tree]]s<ref name="Exegol" />&mdash;native to Ahch-To<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref>&mdash;and [[uneti shrub]]s, were found on the planet.<ref name="Exegol" /> Fragrant [[flower]]s,<ref name="Last Jedi Junior" /> [[nightkelp]]s,<ref name="TLJVD" /> [[Reed (plant)|reed]], and the [[seaweed]] also grew on the planet.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

[[File:AhchTo-Biomes.png|thumb|right|250px|Three porgs flying towards the Temple Island]]
Ahch-To was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Lanai]], an [[Bird|avian]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]],<ref name="Episode VIII" /> and of a large variety of creatures, such as birds, [[fish]], sea [[mammal]]s, and great leviathans of the deep, with [[gull]]s<ref name="Last Jedi Junior" /> and [[frondtrailer]]s among them.<ref name="TLJVD" /> Small, flighted [[porg]]s nested in colonies along the cliffs, feeding their young with regurgitated fish and [[Ahch-Tonian grub]]s.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition" /> A species of chirping stick-like [[insect]]s inhabited the hardy grasses, while others flew in the air.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

Several species of fish inhabited Ahch-To, the edible ones such as [[fingerlip garpon]], [[twinfin hyacander]], and [[spetan channelfish]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition" /> [[coppergrin]], [[billfish]], [[needlegawp]],<ref name="Exegol" /> while others included [[tytahuso fish]] and an [[Unidentified fish (Opah-bladder bagpipes)|unidentified fish]].<ref name="TLJVD" />

There were larger creatures, such as the [[pallaskean]] [[Snake|sea-serpents]] that inhabited the oceans, the [[wharlithan]]s,<ref name="Exegol" /> and the [[thala-siren]]s that nursed their young on the rocky shores, providing a dairy source for other island residents.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition" />

==History==
===Early history===
{{Quote|So this is where they built the original Jedi temple? How long ago?''"<br />''"Go away.|Rey and Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Expanded Edition" />}}
[[File:Prime_Jedi.png|thumb|right|200px|The Jedi Order began with the Prime Jedi over a thousand generations before the Battle of Yavin.]]
The Jedi Order was founded on planet Ahch-To by the [[Prime Jedi]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> around [[25,025 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The Lanais' secret songs recalled long period in which there were no outside visitors, and brief stretches in which a group of outsiders dwelled on the Temple Island together. Some of them were kind, and others were mad&mdash;songs recalled [[Standard year|years]] of fire and ruin that had forced the Lanais from their homes until things resumed their proper course.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> Due to its many secrets, the location of Ahch-To had been hidden from the galaxy.<ref name="Last Jedi Junior" />

Before the [[Great Jedi Purge]] and the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], [[Jedi]] [[scholar]]s argued that a number of locations could be considered the birthplace of the Order. The scholars argued that [[Coruscant]], [[Jedha]], and [[Ossus]] as well as worlds shrouded in myth, such as [[Tython]] and Ahch-To could all be considered candidate worlds for the Order's birth and the location of the first [[Jedi temple]]. Despite such deliberation,<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> however, the location of the first Jedi temple was a mystery by the time the [[Galactic Civil War]] came to a close.<ref name="Episode VII" />

===Imperial Era===
{{Quote|The Jedi Order goes back a long way.|An imprint of Elzar Mann|<ref name="SW 20" />}}
[[File:AhchToGazianImprint-2020StarWars20.png|thumb|left|270px|An imprint of Ahch-To shown to Luke Skywalker in the Living Sea of Gazian.]]
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="SW 20 Date">{{OutlawsComicDate|Star Wars (2020) 20|''Star Wars'' (2020) 20}}</ref> Jedi-in-[[Jedi training|training]] [[Luke Skywalker]] visited the living planet [[Gazian]] in search of knowledge of the Jedi. After landing on a [[Hangar|platform]], Skywalker fell into the [[mushroom]]s of the [[Living Sea]] while attempting to venture out, losing his breathing mask. Inhaling the hallucinogenic spores, he saw an imprint of the planet Ahch-To and spoke to an imprint of the [[High Republic Era]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]]. After their conversation, Skywalker received a [[Unidentified sacred Jedi text|sacred Jedi text]] from a [[skeleton]] of a [[Death|dead]] Jedi.<ref name="SW 20">[[Star Wars (2020) 20|''Star Wars'' (2020) 20]]</ref>

===Rediscovery===
{{Quote|I'd like to keep this compass, if you don't mind.''"<br />"''My mission is to destroy this place. Why would I let you do that?''"<br />"''Because I asked.|Luke Skywalker and Del Meeko|<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>}}
[[File:LukeCompass.jpg|thumb|right|282px|Skywalker's study of an ancient Jedi star compass helped him uncover a path to Ahch-To.]]
Following the [[Battle of Jakku]], Luke Skywalker dedicated much of these years to finding the site of the first Jedi Temple. He uncovered many tantalizing clues that hinted at the origins of the Jedi, but the precise location was a mystery.<ref name="TLJVD" /> He made use of the [[Jedi star compass]] he had discovered in the [[Emperor's Observatory]] on [[Pillio]]. With a [[plasma]]-encased [[supraluminite]] [[lodestone]] at its center, the compass functioned much like the ancient [[wayfinder]]s and was attuned to specific points in [[hyperspace]]. Through studying the compass, Skywalker uncovered a path to Ahch-To, the site of Temple Island and the first Jedi temple.<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

[[Lieutenant]] [[Shara Bey]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] assisted Skywalker in recovering two small uneti trees from [[Vetine]], the only remains of the [[Great Tree (Coruscant)|Great Tree]] that once grew within the [[Jedi Temple training ground|training ground]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on Coruscant.<ref name="Shattered Empire 4">[[Shattered Empire 4|''Shattered Empire'' 4]]</ref> The key to finding the island itself was his study of the spread of the Force-sensitive uneti saplings. With the help of [[Lor San Tekka]], Skywalker finally put together the many clues and learned the location of the temple, keeping this information to himself.<ref name="TLJVD" />

===Exile of Luke Skywalker===
{{Quote|People who knew him best think he went looking for the first Jedi temple.|Han Solo, on the whereabouts of Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:Old_Luke.png|thumb|left|270x270px|After exiling himself to Ahch-To, Skywalker resolved to die as the last Jedi.]]
Following the [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destruction of his temple]] and the students of his Jedi Order in [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Temple destruction date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that the [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker]] was [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destroyed]] six [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the destruction must have taken place in [[28 ABY]].</ref> Skywalker withdrew from the galactic events, going into hiding and [[Sever Force|cutting himself]] off from [[the Force]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition" /> Taking his [[Red Five (T-65B X-wing starfighter)|X-wing]] [[starfighter]] of the [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|T-65B]] line<ref name="Episode VII" /> and many lost Jedi relics,<ref name="TLJVD" /> Skywalker sought out the first Jedi temple on the world of Ahch-To and landed his [[X-wing starfighter]] in a shallow inlet, residing on an island with [[stone]] ruins.<ref name="Episode VII" /> He had pledged to protect the hidden world and its many secrets.<ref name="Last Jedi Junior" /> At some point, Skywalker amended the ''[[Rammahgon]]'' with an insert connecting Ahch-To to the book's route to [[Exegol]].<ref name="TROSVD" />

Among the relics Luke had collected and brought into his [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] were the [[sacred Jedi texts]] and the [[Jedi Crusader pendant]].<ref name="TLJVD" /> Some of which were mentioned as being taken from the island long before in the Lanais' songs. The [[Caretakers]] tolerated Skywalker's presence, though he had learned the [[Lanai's language]] and ways, insisted on doing most of their duties to care for him himself, and had appeared each [[Standard month|month]] on the [[Festival of Return]], something which puzzled [[Alcida-Auka]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

[[File:LukeWillOfTheForce-TheLastJedi1.jpg|thumb|right|230px|Prior to Rey's arrival, Luke Skywalker hears the will of the Force despite his efforts to block it out.]]
Prior to [[Rey Skywalker|Rey's]] arrival on the planet, Luke Skywalker had a dream while sleeping in his hut. In his dream, he saw the life he would never have, one in which he never left Tatooine and remained in the moisture farming business with his uncle, marrying Camie Marstrap and allowing the Galactic Empire to shatter the Rebel Alliance and destroy more planets with the Death Star. Luke eventually awoke, trying to shake away his dream, and left his hut. He was still astonished at the oceans of Ahch-To, which he attributed to growing up in the deserts of Tatooine. As he reached a meadow overlooking the sea, he was still unable to shake the dream away, believing it wasn't an ordinary dream, but [[Force vision|the Force at work]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> He also reflected that his efforts to block the Force out were impossible as it always demanded to be heard. In that moment, the old Jedi Master felt that someone had found him.<ref name="TLJ Adaptation 1">[[The Last Jedi Adaptation 1|''The Last Jedi Adaptation'' 1]]</ref>

===Search for Skywalker===
{{Quote|The map! It is complete!''"<br />"''Luke...|[[C-3PO]] and Leia Organa|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:Rey_on_Ahch-To.png|thumb|left|300px|Rey arrives on Ahch-To to find Luke Skywalker.]]
As the Resistance began its fight against the First Order, [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], Skywalker's sister, hoped to find her brother and bring him back into the fight against the [[dark side of the Force]]. She sent [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] to [[Jakku]], where he retrieved a [[Map to Skywalker|partial map]] held by Lor San Tekka that gave part of the direction to Ahch-To, which he stored in his [[astromech droid]], [[BB-8]]. Later, after the partial map was given to the Resistance, Skywalker's astromech droid, [[R2-D2]], who had been in low power mode since Skywalker's exile, returned to full power and provided the rest of the map to Ahch-To.<ref name="Episode VII" /> R2-D2 unknowingly had the map in his databanks for many years, having found it in the network of the [[Galactic Empire]] aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] during the Galactic Civil War.<ref name="R2-D2">{{EncyclopediaCite|R2-D2}}</ref> <!-- this is possibly outdated -->

After landing on one of the planet's small islands, Rey ventured throughout the Temple Island's ancient ruins. At the top, she found the old Jedi Master in seclusion.<ref name="Episode VII" /> Rey thought she was meant to come to this planet, and compared it to something plucked from a dream unlike Jakku.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> Skywalker said nothing as Rey handed him his [[Skywalker lightsaber|father's lightsaber]].<ref name="TFA novel" />

[[File:Rey_returns_lightsaber_TLJ.jpg|thumb|right|245px|Rey returned Skywalker's lightsaber to him, only to discover that the last Jedi wanted nothing to do with it or her.]]
Upon offering Skywalker his old [[lightsaber]], Rey was shocked to see him discard the weapon after briefly examining it, and immediately leave the [[Saddle (Ahch-To)|saddle]]. The entire way down, Rey followed Luke to his hut and desperately tried to recruit Skywalker to help the remaining members of the Resistance, but he blatantly refused. After recovering the lightsaber from some porgs, Rey discovered Skywalker's X-wing starfighter within a shallow bay and learned that he had stranded himself on the planet. She continued to follow him across the island, despite being ordered to leave.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:TLJ_Rey_inside_Uneti_Tree.jpg|thumb|left|250px|right|Rey visited the sacred uneti tree as the books called to her during her time on Ahch-To.]]
Eventually, she heard a hum of energy calling her to the [[Tree library|old tree]] containing<ref name="Last Jedi Junior" /> the sacred Jedi texts dating back to the dawn of the Jedi Order,<ref name="TROSVD" /> emanating from the books.<ref name="Last Jedi Junior" /> She claimed the location was familiar to her, and that she has seen it in her dreams. Intrigued, Skywalker asked Rey to elaborate, but she continued to press him to come out of hiding and help fight the First Order. Skywalker once more refused, explaining that he did not come to the planet to hide but to die where the Jedi Order first started. Afterward, the old Jedi Master pressed Rey for her true motives for coming to Ahch-To. Rey explained that she had a power inside of her that she did not understand and was frightened of; she also sought a mentor to help her discover her place in the galaxy.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:LeiaMessageTLJ.jpg|thumb|right|280px|Though reluctant to take on another apprentice, Skywalker chose to train Rey after R2-D2 replayed Organa's call for help.]]
The next night, the former Jedi Master slipped into the ''Millennium Falcon'' and recalled the adventures he had experienced. While aboard, Skywalker reunited with R2 who, after he insisted that he could not be convinced to help, replayed Leia's holographic message to Kenobi. Although Skywalker grumbled that the tactic "was a cheap move" on the droid's part, it nevertheless inspired Skywalker to agree to give Rey some basic initiation training as a Jedi, explaining the basic foundations of the Force. That night, Rey woke to see Skywalker staring at her; he agreed to give Rey no more than three lessons of the Jedi Order and that they would start at dawn.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Training ground of Rey===
====Rey's first lesson====
{{Quote|Lesson One. […] To say that if the Jedi die, the light dies, is vanity.|Luke Skywalker to Rey|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
On the first day of her training with Skywalker, Rey awoke to find Kylo Ren sitting across from her.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> She instinctively aimed her blaster at him and fired, only to blow a hole through her [[Rey's hut|stone hut]]. She could not harm him, yet neither could Ren use his powers to reach into her mind due to the actual distance between them. Rey was both terrified and infuriated to see the dark warlord, but decided to keep this new development between them from Skywalker, fearing that she would lose the Jedi Master's trust if he mistook her and Ren's newfound [[Force-bond|connection]] as a betrayal.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

[[File:Rey_and_Luke_First_Lesson_TLJ.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Skywalker reluctantly agreed to train Rey, but the scavenger's raw power reminded him of another Force prodigy&mdash;his nephew, Ben Solo.]]
She followed him to the [[meditation ledge]] outside the first Jedi Temple where he described the Force as an energy that bound everything together, including the light and the darkness. After some miscommunication, Rey reached out into the Force with her senses,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> and had seen the image of the Temple Island that was identical to her dreams back on Jakku. She also speculated that the Jedi [[Scout|explorers]] who found Ahch-To were drawn to a powerful resonance in the Force and had performed Force-assisted hyperspace navigation, trusting it to find the [[hyperspace route]]s through the Unknown Regions.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> She was also drawn to a cavern immersed in dark side energy.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Having been effortlessly drawn to the darkness despite Skywalker's warnings, Skywalker backed away in fear as Rey's raw strength in the Force reminded him of another gifted prodigy&mdash;his nephew and fallen apprentice, [[Ben Solo]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> At the same time, Rey sensed nothing from the Jedi exile and came to realize that Skywalker had blocked himself off from the Force.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

During her training, Rey used the Force to hold rocks in place; she expressed annoyance at the task. Skywalker responded by saying it was for something bigger. Rey then acted aggressively, using her Force powers to levitate two of the Caretakers. Skywalker told her that was enough, and she gently put them down, apologizing. Skywalker told her the training can't be rushed, and Rey stormed off, telling the Jedi Master that she thought he would be different. She then used the Force to push a large rock; in doing so, she accidentally disturbed the nest of a pallaskean. She distracted it while Skywalker and Chewbacca pushed the rock back into place over the eggs. Rey then replied that she would learn the lesson about rushing off, and that Luke Skywalker still dashed into danger to save his friends.<ref name="Life Lessons" />

====Rey's second lesson====
{{Quote|Lesson Two. Now that they're extinct, the Jedi are romanticized&mdash;deified. But if you strip away the myth and look at their deeds, from the birth of the Sith to the fall of the Republic the legacy of the Jedi is failure. Hypocrisy. Hubris.'|Luke Skywalker to Rey|<ref name="Expanded Edition" />}}
For the second lesson, Rey was made to listen to Skywalker's interpretation of the history of the Jedi Order, which he saw as an example of hubris and hypocrisy. He further credited his former mentor, Obi-Wan Kenobi, as the Jedi Master responsible for the training of Darth Vader and also explained to her that the Jedi from the past allowed [[Darth Sidious]] to rise. In spite of his arguments, Rey was adamant that the return of the Jedi Order was the galaxy's last hope against the rising power of the First Order. She also countered Skywalker's views by exclaiming how it was Skywalker himself who saw the light in Vader and fought to redeem him. Yet despite her assertions to the contrary, Skywalker felt responsible for his nephew's fall to the dark side, lamenting how he had once hoped to pass on his lessons to Solo and a new generation of Jedi. It was then that Rey learned about the night of Solo's betrayal as explained from Skywalker's point of view.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

====Rey's third lesson====
{{Quote|I thought they were in danger. I was trying to do something.''"<br />"''And that's what the Resistance needs, not some old failed husk of a religion.|Rey and Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Expanded Edition" />}}
The lesson was interrupted as Rey heard a bell and observed the [[Visitors]] traveling to the festival. Skywalker joined her, stating the group was a tribe from a neighboring island who every month came to raid and plunder the [[Caretaker village]]. Rey, aghast, began running, stating that they had to stop it, only for Skywalker to state that a true Jedi would do nothing. If she were to respond with force, then the raiding party would be back in greater numbers the next month and that the sacred Jedi texts would advise her to ignore her anger and only act when she could maintain balance.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

Rey, however, refused to accept this, thinking of other atrocities she had been helpless to stop, such as the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]]. She made her way to the village, [[Tooth|teeth]] bared and raising her lightsaber, only to discover a party atmosphere. Realizing her mistake in time, she twirled the lightsaber in a ceremonial fashion and was warmly welcomed. She spotted [[Chewbacca]] with a mug of some beverage, resting a fist on the dome of the astromech droid R2-D2. As she furiously stared out at the planet's moon-lit ocean, Skywalker joined her and she after a lengthy pause, she questioned his terminology, "raid and plunder." He responded that it was true "in a way," but when she asked if it was a joke, he sheepishly responded that he did not think that she would run so fast. As they talked, a group of Lanai [[musician]]s began playing a tune and Skywalker held out his hand for Rey to dance. Rey admitted that she had never before danced and Skywalker pointed out that she had also never before singlehandedly fought a [[Bonthian raiding party]]. The two danced for a time before returning to their conversation, Rey stating that she was just trying to do something. Skywalker responded that this was exactly what the Resistance she was trying to help needed, rather than a "failed husk of a religion" like the Jedi Order, asking if she understood. Rey, however, remained unconvinced, stating that what she understood was that their friends were dying and that she had believed in the legend of Luke Skywalker he hated so much. With that, she left him alone.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

After he had taught Rey three lessons, Skywalker decided to reconnect with the Force on the meditation ledge outside the temple. Behind him, as he regained his Force abilities which were suppressed for years after he cut himself off, the still surface of the water in the ancient font<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> with the mosaic of the Prime Jedi<ref name="Episode VIII" /> began to ripple and dance.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> During the meditation, Luke called out to his sister from across the galaxy, who had heard the call. Having realized how dire the situation was, he decided to end his exile and return with Rey.<ref name="Last Jedi Junior" />

====The cave====
{{Quote|Let me see them. My parents, please.|Rey|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:Ahch-To_Mirror_Cave.jpg|thumb|right|280px|In failing to discover the identities of her parents, Rey was left feeling more alone than ever.]]
As her training with Skywalker continued,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> her connection with Ren became stronger<ref name="Rey Bio Gallery">{{Databank|rey-biography-gallery|Rey Biography Gallery}}</ref> and resulted in more frequent interactions between them.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> At first, Rey was inconvenienced by the fact that she was bonded with a man whom she regarded as a murderer and a monster.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> To her surprise, however, Ren no longer tried to hide his feelings of pain<ref name="Rey Bio Gallery" /> and misery<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> from Rey.<ref name="Rey Bio Gallery" /> At one point she inquired&mdash;while distracted by the sight of Ren stripped to the waist&mdash;as to how he could kill his own father, to which Ren replied by comparing Rey's experience with abandonment to his own. His knowledge of Rey's parents confounded her, as well as reminded her that the first contact between their minds had allowed them to access each other's memories. Ren then took the opportunity to share his recollection of the night he destroyed his uncle's temple, revealing to Rey that Skywalker had made an attempt on his life while he slept. She wanted to believe that Ren was lying, but her feelings told her that he was telling the truth.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

Determined to see her parents once again after so many years, Rey followed the pull to the dark side which led her to a [[Mirror cave|cave]] filled with numerous mirror versions of herself. Staring into a clouded mirror of polished stone, she beseeched the Force to reveal her parents but was only met by her own reflection. Having failed to find the answers she sought, either with her parents or Skywalker, Rey began to despair as years of isolation began to weigh upon her. As a result, she turned to the one person whom she felt could relate to her sense of solitude and loss: Kylo Ren. The dark warrior listened intently as Rey recalled her experience in the [[cave]], and he insisted she was not alone despite her sense of loneliness. Rey returned his gesture, in kind, and came to hope that it was not too late for him to return to the light.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

====Revelations====
{{Quote|Is it true? Did you try to murder him?''"<br />"''Leave this island now!|Rey and Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:Luke_interrupting_Rey_and_Kylo_Ren.png|thumb|left|266px|Disillusioned by Skywalker's rejection of the Jedi legacy, Rey turned to Kylo Ren, with whom she could communicate across the stars.]]
By then, their bond had reached an unprecedented level in strength and raw power<ref name="TLJVD" /> to the extent that Rey compared it to touching a live wire. She tentatively raised her hand to touch him, although she expected their hands to simply pass through each other. When their fingers actually touched, Rey gasped and grasped Ren's hand. At that moment, however, Skywalker&mdash;now reconnected with the Force and planning to return with her&mdash;witnessed their bond with his own eyes and interrupted the connection,<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> thinking that they were in league. Luke had gestured at the walls and the ceiling of the hut, and all the stone blocks were shot outward<ref name="Last Jedi Junior" /> from the hut's center, scattering them around the bench.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> Having decided that Rey could not be trusted anymore, Skywalker ordered her to leave the island.<ref name="Last Jedi Junior" />

Confronting the Jedi Master, Rey demanded to know if he tried to murder Ren. The two fought until Skywalker admitted that he had contemplated killing his nephew in a flash of instinct after sensing the darkness within him. Rey did not believe that Ren was fully committed to the dark side as there was still much conflict within him, convinced by the vision of the future she saw when they touched hands. She therefore set out to confront him, in spite of Skywalker's warnings, under the belief that Ben Solo's redemption was the key to saving the galaxy.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> Unknown to Skywalker, Rey took the sacred Jedi texts with her aboard the ''Falcon'' before leaving Ahch-To. Upon returning to the beleaguered Resistance fleet, Rey ordered Chewbacca to launch her from an escape pod toward Snoke's flagship, the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]'', where Ren awaited her arrival.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===A larger world===
{{Quote|Heeded my words not, did you? Pass on what you have learned. Strength, mastery. But weakness&hellip; folly&hellip; failure, also. Yes, failure most of all. The greatest teacher, failure is. Luke&hellip; we are what they grow beyond. That is the true burden of all masters.|Yoda's Force spirit, to Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:Luke_listens_to_Yoda_TLJ.png|thumb|right|300px|The spirit of Yoda communed with Skywalker, urging him to embrace failure as the greatest teacher.]]
The following night, Skywalker tried to burn down what few Jedi artifacts and writings were left in hopes that it would rid the galaxy of the Jedi once and for all, who, through him, had failed. Luke was surprised by the sudden appearance of Master [[Yoda]] as a [[Force spirit]], who not only didn't seem to mind the ancient scripts burning up, but was the one to actually start the fire by causing lightning to strike the tree. In fact, unbeknownst to Luke, Rey had already taken the sacred Jedi texts when she left. During their meeting, Yoda pointed out that mistakes and failure were important lessons, if not the most important lesson, for a Jedi Master. Ultimately, Yoda helped Skywalker come to terms with his failed training of Ben Solo, and to realize that despite his fear of failing another student he could still be what Rey needed, inspiring Luke to face his past and forgive himself.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:LukesFinalMoments-TLJ.png|thumb|left|290px|Ahch-To's binary sunset was the last thing Skywalker saw before passing away into the Force.]]
Meanwhile on the ''Supremacy'', the [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] commanded Rey to reveal Skywalker's location. When she refused, he tortuously probed her mind to discover Skywalker's whereabouts.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> A memory of her waking up during the sunset of [[Jakku's sun]] after she experienced a dream of the Ahch-To's Temple Island in a gray sea came back to Rey.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> After obtaining the knowledge he sought and contemptuously dropping the girl, Snoke laughed and mused that Skywalker had been "so wise" to hide from him. He decided that once the Resistance was destroyed, he would command his forces to Ahch-To and obliterate the entire Temple Island, so Skywalker and the Jedi Order could be given the death he desired. However, he was killed by Kylo Ren, therefore keeping Skywalker’s location secret again.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Following his meeting with Yoda, Skywalker decided to [[Battle of Crait|confront]] his fallen pupil Kylo Ren.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Assisted by the power of the [[vergence]] at the site,<ref name="HC 72">{{HelmetCollectionCite|72|Highlights of the Saga}}</ref> Luke [[Force projection|projected]] himself to Crait to buy the Resistance time to escape. Projecting himself required a massive amount of Force energy and ultimately led to his demise, although Luke found inner peace and purpose in his final moments as he gazed upon Ahch-To's binary sunset and became one with the Force.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

===Later history===
{{Quote|I saw myself on the dark throne. I won't let it happen. I'm never leaving this place. I'm doing what you did.''"<br />"''I was wrong. It was fear that kept me here.|Rey and Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>}}
After Rey's departure and Skywalker's death, the [[Clan Matron]] Alcida-Auka did not pay them much heed, instead turning her mind towards the many things that needed fixing in their aftermath. In addition to their daily chores and the gathering of supplies for winter, the ancient uneti tree had been destroyed by a lightning strike, Rey's hut needed rebuilding and Luke's required a new [[Lightning rod/Canon|lightning rod]]. In regards to Skywalker, she had her fellow Caretakers gather up his possessions, including [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber|his lightsaber]], and place them into a repository of other such items left by visitors. After ensuring these things were done, the matron took a moment to reflect on the history and duties of the Caretakers, recalling previous outside visitors who either lived alongside the Lanais or treated them cruelly and wondering if Luke would be the last. After this moment of reflection, Alcida-Auka decided it did not matter but if anyone did come, they would find everything in order for them because the Caretakers would do their duty, as always.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

[[File:ReyandForceGhostLuke-TROS.jpg|thumb|right|300px|The spirit of Luke Skywalker appeared to Rey on Ahch-To, encouraging her to continue her Jedi path.]]
[[35 ABY|One year later]],<ref name="TROSVD" /> following her [[Mission to Kef Bir|confrontation]] against Ren and the revelation that the resurrected Darth Sidious was her grandfather, Rey fled to Ahch-To in [[Kylo Ren's TIE whisper]] and intended to live out the rest of her days there in self-imposed exile as Skywalker had previously done, fearing that she would fall to the dark side of the Force. However, Skywalker's spirit appeared to her and encouraged Rey to not give up like he did and to realize that her [[Palpatine family|blood]] would not define her legacy nor her actions. He gave her [[Leia's lightsaber]], which he had kept hidden in his hut during his exile. Since Rey had purposely crashed the TIE whisper, Skywalker lifted his old X-wing out of the water for her to fly to Exegol. With Skywalker's starfighter and Organa's blade, she helped lead the Resistance and [[Citizens' Fleet]] to victory against the [[Sith Eternal]] in the [[Battle of Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Main|Lanai}}
{{Quote|Caretakers. Island natives. They've kept up the Jedi structures since they were built.|Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
[[File:CaretakerVillage-SoH.png|thumb|left|250px|Caretakers attend to their duties in their village.]]
Ahch-To was the homeworld of the mysterious Lanai,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> an avian sentient species. The species of Lanai evolved from the same evolutionary stock of seabirds, that produced unintelligent porgs.<ref name="TLJVD" /> They were distinguished by their gray skin and bird-like skinny [[Limb|feet]].<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Innate watershedding ability of their skin, hair and plumage afforded by natural oils allowed Lanais to withstand intense oceans of their planet. They possessed salt glands, that helped to extract sea salt from their food and drink, and extra membranes that enabled sharp underwater vision.<ref name="TLJVD" /> They inhabited many islands and their cultures differed significantly on each.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition" />

The [[Gender|females]] of the species, who inhabited the [[Temple Island]] for [[Millennium|thousands]] of years, led simple, unassuming lives and wore white [[robe]]s with matching headdresses; they took the role of Caretakers and considered it their religious duty to maintain the temple island's ancient structures<ref name="Caretakers DB" /> whose existence they kept a secret<ref name="HC 72" /> including the first Jedi Temple<ref name="Exegol" /> and the huts of the Jedi village<ref name="TLJVD" /> and to provide care for those who traveled to their isolated world seeking truth<ref name="Caretakers DB">{{Databank|caretakers|Caretakers}}</ref>&mdash;while the males were known as the Visitors, who spent their time at sea as hunter-gatherers using hand-crafted [[boat]]s to sail along the rough seas, they came back to shore monthly with a large enough haul to keep them fed until the next gathering of [[Foodstuff|food]] and resources. They interacted together only during this monthly gathering event, this multi-day reunion became a festival, with music, dance and food, known as the Festival of Return.<ref name="TLJVD" />

To keep rhythm during their repetitive chores, their culture was intrinsically musical. [[Lanai-song]] was a mix of whistles and guttural oscillations that echo from their village and worksites. The Lanais' technology was solely derived from fish, useful skins and skeletons were gathered during their fishing in bountiful oceans of their homeworld.<ref name="TLJVD" /> Lanais spoke a non-[[Galactic Basic Standard|Basic]] [[language]] of the same name,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> which was combination of spoken words and hand motions. Written language, which consisted of cuneiform marks based on the shape of their feet, was rare, and fire signals were preferred for long-range communications.<ref name="TLJVD" />

==Locations==
===Temple Island===
{{Main|Temple Island}}
{{Quote|I came to this island to die.|Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
[[File:Temple_island.png|thumb|right|300px|The Temple Island.]]
The Temple Island<ref name="HC 72" /> was an island on Ahch-To where the Jedi Order was founded over a thousand generations before the [[Republic Era|fall]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="TLJVD" /> A [[Jedi village]] was founded there to house the first members of the Order and the eponymous [[first Jedi Temple]] was built.<ref name="Exegol">{{EncyclopediaCite|Exegol, the Unknown Regions and Wild Space}}</ref> The island was where Jedi Master Luke Skywalker chose to spend the rest of his life in self-imposed exile after his [[Skywalker family|nephew]], Ben Solo, destroyed the new generation of Jedi. He was discovered on Temple Island by Rey, a Force prodigy. Skywalker died on the island after projecting his appearance into the Battle of Crait.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Behind the scenes==
The planet Ahch-To first appeared in the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="Episode VII" /> The planet is identified as Ahch-To in the film's script.<ref name="Script">{{WebCite|url=https://www.slashfilm.com/star-wars-the-force-awakens-ending|text=Star Wars: The Force Awakens Ending Details And More Revealed In Official Screenplay|author=Sciretta, Peter|date=2015-12-31|work=/Film|archivedate=20190115112534}}</ref>

Ahch-To was filmed on site at the 6th-century monastic settlement of UNESCO World Heritage Site Skellig Michael, an island located off the southern coast of Ireland.

For the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="Episode VIII" /> [[Lucasfilm]] built a replica of the monastic "beehive" huts atop the Ceann Sibéal headland in the nearby village of [[Wikipedia:Ballyferriter|Ballyferriter]] in [[Wikipedia:County Kerry|County Kerry]], Ireland.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.irishexaminer.com/ireland/kerry-set-to-be-a-scene-stealer-in-latest-star-wars-film-400574.html|text=Kerry set to be a scene stealer in latest 'Star Wars' film|author=Lucey, Anne|date=2016-05-20|work=Irish Examiner|archivedate=20180117184826}}</ref>

Aerial footage of Skellig Michael was used for the establishing shot of Ahch-To in [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. The same film features scenes between Rey and Luke Skywalker on Ahch-To, which were filmed on a prop set of the island.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://screenrant.com/rise-skywalker-luke-mark-hamill-behind-scenes|text=Mark Hamill Takes Luke's Rise of Skywalker Scene Very Seriously|author=Stinson, Katherine|date=2020-05-29|work=[https://screenrant.com ScreenRant]|archivedate=20210227001123}}</ref>
