'''Adam Howden''' is an English voice actor who provided voices for several characters in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]]s ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' and ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''.

Howden is also known for his roles as Shulk and the various incarnations of Professor Klaus in the ''Xenoblade Chronicles'' series, as well as the former's appearances in the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series.
