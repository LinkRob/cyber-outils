The '''attack on Garn''', also known as the '''doom of the Ordu Aspectu''', was a battle that took place many [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra 5" /> when [[droid]]s controlled by [[Eternal Rur]] attacked<ref name="Doctor Aphra 6" /> members of the [[Jedi Order]] and a [[Jedi]] splinter group known as the [[Ordu Aspectu]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 5" /> Upon the arrival of Jedi inspectors to the [[Citadel of Rur]], Eternal Rur seized control of the facility's droids and began to [[Death|kill]] the citadel's inhabitants. Seeking to contain Eternal Rur, the mortal [[Rur]] jumped the citadel through [[hyperspace]] and managed to deactivate Eternal Rur at the cost of his own life.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 6" />
