'''Desler Gizh Outworld Mobility Corporation''' was a company that was part of the [[Mining Guild]].<ref name="Land, Sea and Air Speeders" /> It produced the [[Koro-2 all-environment Exodrive airspeeder]]. Shortly before the outbreak of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Clawdite]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Zam Wesell]] flew in a Koro-2 [[airspeeder]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] when trying to [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|assassinate]] [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="USW" />

==Behind the scenes==
Desler Gizh Outworld Mobility Corporation was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|Star Wars ''Legends'']] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', which was written by [[Curtis Saxton]] and released in [[2002]]. The company was confirmed to be [[canon]] by the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', which was released in [[2015]].
