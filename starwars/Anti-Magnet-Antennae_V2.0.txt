'''Anti-Magnet-Antennae V2.0''' were devices created by [[Galactic Empire]] [[Scientist]] [[Heinz Darthenshmirtz]] that disabled magnets nearby them. Darthenshmirtz used them against [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[agent]] [[Perry (Platypus)|Perry]] so that he could not use his [[wrist magnet]] after Perry infiltrated the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] and confronted Darthenshmirtz.<ref name="Agent P" />
