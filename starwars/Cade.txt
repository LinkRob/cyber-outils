'''Cade''' was a [[Clone trooper|clone]] [[stormtrooper]] that served the [[Galactic Empire]]. He served aboard [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]]'s [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] during the [[Attack on Kamino|attack]] on [[Kamino]] and the [[Aerial bombardment|bombardment]] of [[Tipoca City]] and was later [[Death|assassinated]] when he began to take action to expose Rampart's cover up of the attack.

==Biography==
The [[clone trooper]] nicknamed Cade was in service to the [[Galactic Empire]], specifically serving on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]]<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy" /> in the year [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> When [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]] ordered the destruction of [[Tipoca City]], Cade was aboard Rampart's [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[VZ-114]], which participated in the [[aerial bombardment]] and [[Attack on Kamino|destruction]] of the city. Later, Cade was complicit in the subsequent cover-up that Rampart attempted in order to convince the [[Imperial Senate]] that a storm had been responsible for the destruction. Following this, Rampart attempted to pass the [[Imperial Defense Recruitment Bill]], which would phase out the Kaminoan-born clone troopers in the wake of the destruction of the cloning facilities there.<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy" />

[[File:AssassinationOfCade-TCC.png|thumb|left|300px|Cade is assassinated by an unknown assailant.]]
Several [[Standard month|months]] later, Cade was drinking at [[79's]], a [[cantina]] on [[Coruscant]] which catered to [[Cloning|clone]] troopers, with fellow clone [[Slip (clone trooper)|Slip]], who was also present for the Attack on Kamino. Cade announced his intention to whistleblow about the true nature of the operation. Although Slip was not convinced it was the right thing to do, as they had just been following orders, Cade insisted that what they had done was wrong and that it had to be made right.<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy" />

Cade left the bar, followed by Slip, who warned him about Rampart's unpredictable nature and his cruelty towards clones. Cade was then killed by [[Clone X]], a [[Clone X trooper|clone assassin]] deployed by Rampart, while Slip was narrowly able to escape on a [[speeder bike]].<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy" />

===Legacy===
Although Cade's actions convinced Slip to work towards uncovering the truth, Slip was later killed, also by Clone X. However, Slip was first able to contact former [[Clone Commander]] [[Rex]],<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy" /> who, along with the help of [[Clone Force 99]], were able to acquire evidence that would be used against Rampart in the Senate.<ref name="T&C">{{TBB|Truth and Consequences}}</ref>
