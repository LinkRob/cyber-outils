'''Cholganna''' was a dark, cool [[forest]] [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[nexu]]s, a [[species]] of predatory [[feline]]s that spread off-world as a result of their value as attack beasts.

==Description==
Cholganna was a dark, cool<ref name="Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars]]''</ref> [[planet]] with a mountainous terrain, [[jungle]]s,<ref name="Nexu GoC" /> and [[forest]]s<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> in grid square T-5 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Coordinates" />

[[File:Cholganna_Nexu.png|thumb|left|180px|A pack of wild nexu on Cholganna.]]
Cholganna was the [[homeworld]] of various creatures, including the [[nexu]]s, a [[species]] of predatory [[feline]]s<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> who lived in the northern hemisphere,<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Nexu - Creature}}</ref> the [[bark rat]], a [[rodent]] that was often prey for the nexu, and [[Arboreal octopus|arboreal octopi]].<ref name="CVD" /> Because of their incredibly aggressive and deadly nature, the nexu were often captured by [[Poaching|poachers]],<ref name="Nexu GoC" /> and spread off-world as attack beasts in [[gladiator]] matches.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cholganna was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]]<ref name="Attack of the Clones The Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> that was released on [[April 22]], [[2002]].<ref name="Star Wars: Attack of the Clones The Visual Dictionary release date">{{SWArchive|url=eu/lit/ref/news20020227.html|text=Book Preview: DK Episode II Books}}</ref> It became [[canon]] when it was mentioned in ''[[Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars]]'', a young readers activity book written and edited by [[David Fentiman]] and [[Sadie Smith]]<ref name="Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars" /> that was released on [[June 16]], [[2014]].<ref name="Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars release date">{{DK|url=uk/book/9781409352600-star-wars-ultimate-factivity-collection|text=Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars}}</ref>
