'''Blue-algae''' was a [[Species|type]] of [[algae]] that was found on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] [[farmer]]s used it as a [[fertilizer]] for their fields. It was seen as more effective, as well as better smelling, than the commonly used [[shaak]] scat, as it nearly doubled their normal output during Naboo's growing season when it was used on a limited number of fields.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />
