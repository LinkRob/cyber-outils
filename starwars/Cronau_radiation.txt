'''Cronau radiation''' was a type of [[radiation]] emitted by [[starship]]s upon jumping into or out of [[hyperspace]], along with an apparent twisting of [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> The [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] were equipped with [[sensor]]s that could detect Cronau radiation, thus enabling their crews to detail instances of vessels entering hyperspace.<ref name="Tarkin" />
