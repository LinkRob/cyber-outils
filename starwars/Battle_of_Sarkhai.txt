During the [[Galactic War]] between the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|Sith Empire]], the [[Syo Bakarn|First Son]], leader of the [[Children of the Emperor]], ordered the Empire to '''attack''' the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Sarkhai/Legends|Sarkhai]] in a bid to distract [[the Barsen'thor]]. However, a fleet under the command of [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] [[Nadia Grell]] was able to drive back the attackers.

==Behind the scenes==
In the MMO ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', the Jedi Consular's companion [[Nadia Grell]] was originally supposed to leave after the mission "Old Blood" in order to save her homeworld, which the First Son had attacked with an Imperial fleet. While her departure was cut from the game, the attack is still mentioned in the mission logs.
