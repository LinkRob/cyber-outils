The '''Command Articles''',<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> also known as the '''Expansionary Defense Fleet's charter''',<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref> were a set of laws for the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] and the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> The centerpiece of the Articles was the exploration of distant regions outside the Ascendancy and search for potential threats to the [[Chiss]] [[species]].<ref name="Greater Good" /> The Articles allowed a [[Chiss]] [[commander]] to board a foreign [[starship]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

==History==
{{Quote|I was under the impression that exploring distant regions and searching for potential threats was the centerpiece of the Expansionary Defense Fleet's charter."''<br />''"It is. But your, shall we say, enthusiasm in identifying and confronting General Yiv has made certain of the Aristocra nervous about sending too many forces outside the Ascendancy and leaving its own worlds less than thoroughly defended. Even bolstered by the Ruling Families' private fleets, the Defense Force is thought by many to be stretched too thin.|Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo and Supreme General Ba'kif|<ref name="Greater Good" />}}
Following the [[First skirmish over Sunrise|first skirmish]] against the ''[[HopeBreaker]]'' over the [[planet]] "[[Sunrise]]," [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," asked [[Supreme General]] [[Ba'kif]] whether the ''[[Grayshrike]]'' would be sent back to investigate Sunrise and the force that had attacked the [[Chiss]] there. Ba'kif responded that the [[Chiss Syndicure]] and the [[Defense Hierarchy Council]] were weary of sending a major warship so far from the Ascendancy. Thrawn used the fact that the cornerstone of the Expansionary Defense Fleet's charter was about exploring regions outside the Ascendancy to convince the Supreme General. Ba'kif reminded Thrawn that his "enthusiam" in identifying and confronting [[General]] [[Yiv]] had made some of the [[Aristocra (organization)|Aristocra]] reconsider. In turn, Thrawn found their assessment to be invalid.<ref name="Greater Good" />

The Articles did not prevent a [[Chiss]] [[commander]] from boarding a foreign [[starship]], a fact that [[Grand Admiral]] Thrawn attempted to use to convince [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] to accompany the [[navigator]] [[Vah'nya]] aboard the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]''.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />
