The '''De'Rruyet Industries confluence''' was an event in which [[Beol De'Rruyet]], the founder and leader of [[De'Rruyet Industries]], intended to unveil his version of a [[Nihil]] [[Path engine]] [[hyperdrive]], which De'Rruyet claimed could replicate the Nihil's methods of [[lightspeed]] travel, at the [[De'Rruyet Center]] on the<ref name="Aphra 9" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Aphra 8">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 8|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 8]]</ref> [[Midarr]].<ref name="Aphra 9" /> The launch of the hyperdrive, according to [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]] of [[Tagge Corporation]], could change the galactic economy or end the current [[Galactic Civil War|war]]<ref name="Aphra 6">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 6|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 6]]</ref> between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Aphra 8" /> which Tagge claimed would be financially ruinous for her company. In response, Tagge hired the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] to find out where De'Rruyet's device had come from, and whether it worked.<ref name="Aphra 6" />

As the confluence grew close, Aphra and [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]] discovered that the hyperdrive was actually a fake, and that if a lightspeed jump was attempted it would combust the ship and [[Death|kill]] the [[pilot]], and realized that if it went off in the middle of the confluence, it would kill all those present. The pair also discovered that De'Rruyet had become nervous that the [[engine]] would not work as he had proposed, and in response, had booked a one-way ticket to the<ref name="Aphra 9" /> the planet<ref name="Mon Cala">{{Databank|mon-cala|Mon Cala}}</ref> [[Mon Cala]] in the event that the Path engine didn't work.<ref name="Aphra 9" />

On the [[Standard day|day]] of the confluence, [[General]] [[Vukorah]] of the [[Unbroken Clan]] broke into the center in an attempt to claim the hyperdrive for herself, and took De'Rruyet and his employees hostage. Vukorah demanded that the engine be brought online, and regularly killed De'Rruyet's employees as she grew impatient. As Starros attempted to get the employees out of the center and to safety, Aphra, under of the guise that she was fixing the Path engine for Vukorah, secretly disarmed it. When Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s entered the center to get the Unbroken Clan forces to surrender, a firefight broke out, and a stray [[blaster]] [[Laser|bolt]] hit the hyperdrive, which caused it to explode. The event finished De'Rruyet Industries as a company and<ref name="Aphra 10">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 10|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 10]]</ref> ruined De'Rruyet's life.<ref name="Aphra 18">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 18|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 18]]</ref>
