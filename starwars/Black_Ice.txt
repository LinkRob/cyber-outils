'''''Black Ice''''' was the name of a [[train]] of two [[Field Secured Container Vessel]]s containing nine linked force-field [[fuel]] spheres that was used by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy]]. ''Black Ice'' was manufactured by [[Rendili StarDrive]] and was assigned to the [[Imperial Replenishment Fleet DK-209]] as a key part of the support [[fleet]]. ''Black Ice'' was roughly five [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] in length, and each fuel sphere could hold 110 million tons of fuel each, in total nearing one billion tons of [[starship]] fuel. The quantity was enough to supply a full Imperial [[battle fleet]] for one [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="BMF 34" />

''Black Ice'' was stolen by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] after the [[Battle of the Choah Belt|destruction of the Choah Belt fuel refinery]] by the Empire, although ''Black Ice'' was later destroyed in a collision.<ref name="BMF 34" />
