The '''Clan Eldar Warrior''' was a set of [[armor]] worn by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Aran Tal]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Clan Tal Warrior|standard costume]]. Though part of [[Clan Tal]] himself, Aran wore this [[Mandalorian armor|armor]] out of respect for members of [[Clan Eldar]], displaying their [[lion]] sigil on the front of his [[helmet]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Clan Eldar Warrior was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Aran Tal, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 4.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Clan Eldar MT-97]]. This costume could be unlocked by reaching Tier 4 on the Season 4 Arena Pass' premium track.<ref name="Hunters" />
