A [[Devaronian]] [[Gender|male]] '''bail bondsman''' operated a [[Bail Bonds|bail bonds office]] in the [[Coruscant Underworld|lower levels]] of [[Coruscant]]'s [[Imperial City|Galactic City]]. He offered [[Bounty|jobs]] to [[bounty hunter]]s during the [[Clone Wars]]. [[Asajj Ventress]] accepted the bounty posting on the fugitive [[Padawan]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], from the [[bail bondsman]].<ref name="Sisters" />

The bail bondsman had two horns protruding from his head, [[Color|red]] skin, and brown facial [[hair]] grown in a goatee.<ref name="Sisters" />
