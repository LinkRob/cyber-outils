The '''Black Line''' was a [[subgrav train]] line that connected the [[Verity District]] with the neighboring [[Federal District]] on<ref name="LifeDebt" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="LifeDebt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> the de facto leader of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]], covertly visited Coruscant to research [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]], her mysterious superior. After exiting the Verity District's [[Hall of Imperial Register]], the [[admiral]] noted a lack of power in the district and decided to vacate. After initially considering using the Black Line to travel to the Federal District, Sloane then realized that any power loss would have likely disrupted the line too.<ref name="LifeDebt" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Black Line was mentioned in the [[2016]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', the second installment of [[Chuck Wendig]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]].<ref name="LifeDebt" />
