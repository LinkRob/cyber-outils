'''Dizcaro''' was a [[Corellian]] [[Gender|male]] [[bodyguard]] who ran a [[Dizcaro's bodyguard company|bodyguard agency]] which was a rival to [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection]], led by [[Alys Ongwa]]. He was known by the code name "Team Leader One". When the [[Nihil conflict]] came to [[Corellia]]<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Death" /> Dizcaro was [[Death|killed]] protecting his client [[Ovus Buckell]] from the [[Nihil]] member and rogue [[City Father]] [[Nomar Tralmat|Nomar "Respriler" Tralmat]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
