'''Code Force Seven''' was a tactic issued by [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] during the [[Galactic Empire]]'s occupation of [[Cloud City]]. It was executed by [[Lobot]] and the [[Bespin Wing Guard]] to free [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[Chewbacca]], and [[C-3PO]] from the Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s holding them captive.<ref name="SWCE" />
