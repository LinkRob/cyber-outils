'''Beastmaster's vibro-glaive''' was a type of [[vibro-glaive]], that was most often found in the hands of the [[Weequay]]. They were popular among the dark wolf [[Beastmasters of Sriluur|beastmasters]] of [[Sriluur]], who used the flat edge of the polearm to keep their packs in line, exploiting the glaive's reach to maintain a relatively safe distance from the lethal tails of the [[raquor'daan]]. The Weequay also made use of the vibro-glaives in their [[Houk–Weequay wars|wars]] with the [[Houk]], wielding these vicious weapons to even the odds in close combat against their larger, thick-skinned adversaries.<ref name="GaG" />
