'''''Classic Star Wars: A New Hope''''' is a a children's flap book based on {{Film|IV}}, adapted by [[Cynthia Alvarez]] and illustrated by [[Isidre Mones]].<ref name="Archive" /> It was published by [[Random House]] in [[1996]].<ref name="Amazon" /> The pages featured flaps that could open up to reveal hidden images.<ref name="Amazon" />
