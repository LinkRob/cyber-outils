A '''celebration''' took place on the [[planet]] [[Serenno]] in the [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Carannia]]<ref name="Dooku: JL" /> in [[90 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> The celebration's objective was to show what the [[Outer Rim Territories]] planets could offer to the [[Galactic Republic]]. The [[count of Serenno]] [[Gora]] presided over the event. [[Jedi Master]]s [[Yoda]], [[Tera Sinube]], and [[Yula Braylon]] were present at the celebration together with [[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Dooku]], [[Sifo-Dyas]], [[Arath Tarrex]] and several others.<ref name="Dooku: JL" />
