An '''electrostatic repeller''' was a circular device designed to repel sand particles. The [[Lars family]] of the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] used an electrostatic repeller to keep the courtyard and room entrances of [[Lars homestead|their]] [[moisture farm]] free of blowing sand.<ref name="CL" />
