'''Al'Moab III''' was the third world in the [[Al'Moab system]]. A relatively imposing [[Planet/Legends|planet]], Al'Moab III was mostly covered in [[Desert/Legends|deserts]]. However, that had not prevented the colonization of the land. At the time of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], Al'Moab III was mostly populated by [[Human/Legends|Humans]] who built their [[City/Legends|cities]] in its wide barren zones. Those colonists had been there for an undetermined, and yet quite ancient, period of [[Time/Legends|time]], and the planet was known for both its archaeological remains and troubled history.<ref name="AA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Al'Moab III has only been mentioned in ''[[L'Arche d'Alliance]]'' ("The Ark of Covenant"), a scenario published in the French RPG magazine [[Dragon Radieux 17|''Dragon Radieux'' #17]].<ref name="AA" />
