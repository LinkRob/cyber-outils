'''Dalnan pepper''' was a variety of [[pepper]] edible by the [[Evereni]] [[species]] that shared its name with<ref name="Closed Fist" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> [[Dalna]].<ref name="Closed Fist" /> At some point no earlier than [[379 BBY]],<ref name="FaerDate">The segment of "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" depicting a [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi's]] [[death]] onboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' takes place around [[379 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:ClosedFistJediDeathDate|here]]. [[Marda Ro]] met the [[Faers]] after the [[Jedi]]'s death; therefore, she must have met them no earlier than 379 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> [[Marda Ro]]&mdash;the Evereni<ref name="Closed Fist" /> founder of a [[pirate]] group who would eventually become the [[Nihil]]<ref name="GrattonInterview">{{YouTube|video=llnvoEG0CjE|text=Interview with Tessa Gratton: "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" (TALES OF LIGHT & LIFE)|channel=@FriendsOfTheForce|name=[[Friends of the Force]]|date=2023-10-06}}</ref>&mdash;and the [[Faers|siblings]] [[Vika Faer|Vika]] and [[Velya Faer]] ate a [[Foodstuff|meal]] prepared by Ro's [[Aide|assistant]] [[Alirya]], which contained a chemical [[Spice (seasoning)|spice]]. Ro later mentioned this event a [[Marda Ro's journal|journal entry]]<ref name="Closed Fist" /> she<ref name="Temptation">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> recorded for her [[Ro family|descendants]] and noted that the taste of the spice reminded her of Dalnan pepper.<ref name="Closed Fist" /> By [[252 BBY]],<ref name="ShallaDate">"The Lie" chapter in [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]], which includes [[Shalla Ro]]'s [[death]], features [[Asgar Ro]]'s ascension to [[Eye of the Nihil]], an event dated to [[252 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'' establishes she introduced [[Marchion Ro]] to [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]], he must have listened to it by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> during his youth, Ro's [[Gender|great-great-grandson]] [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to her memoirs, including the message where Dalnan spice was mentioned.<ref name="Temptation" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dalnan pepper was mentioned in the short story "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]," written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Closed Fist" />
