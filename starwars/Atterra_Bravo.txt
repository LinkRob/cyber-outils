'''Atterra Bravo''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Atterra system]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The [[Firestone Islands]] were located on Atterra Bravo. It had a sister planet named [[Atterra Alpha]] and both were surrounded by a thick [[Atterra Belt|asteroid belt]].<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" />

During the [[Cold War]] between the [[First Order]] and [[New Republic]], the planet went on lockdown with a [[blockade]] over all the system. Very little ships were left from the First Order's sweep of the old security forces. Many people did not have their own [[Moisture vaporator|Moisture Vaporators]], so the blockade left many dead from thirst. The brutality of the First Order lead to the development of [[Bravo Rising]], a local resistance movement. The [[Resistance]] arrived with [[Cobalt Squadron]] important medical, food, water and fuel supplies to aid their fight against the First Order.<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" />
