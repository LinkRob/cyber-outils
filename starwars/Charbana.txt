'''Charbana''' was a [[City|village]] located on the [[planet]] [[Mikkia]] near a [[Red Mine mining complex|mining complex]].<ref name="Call of Coruscant" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[The Call of Coruscant]]" takes place concurrently with the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]].</ref> the village suffered a [[Groundquake on Mikkia|groundquake]] due to activity at the mine. [[Jedi Master]] [[Mirro Lox]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Amadeo Azzazzo]] assisted the residents of the village before confronting the [[Lor Botho|leader]] of the mining corporation, ultimately taking him into custody after a brief skirmish.<ref name="Call of Coruscant" />
