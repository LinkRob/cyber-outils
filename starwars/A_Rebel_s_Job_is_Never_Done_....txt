"'''A Rebel's Job is Never Done ...'''" is a short story written by [[Michael Allen Horne]] for the ''[[Dark Empire Sourcebook]]''.

==Plot summary==
The story takes the form of a personal communication from [[Arhul Hextrophon/Legends|Arhul Hextrophon]] to [[Voren Na'al/Legends|Voren Na'al]] during [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine's]] [[Operation Shadow Hand|return to power]]. Hextrophon has just heard about the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Second Battle of Coruscant|attack]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] and expresses his shock. Hextrophon was injured on [[Caprioril]] and is now resting at [[Mon Mothma/Legends|Mon Mothma's]] [[dacha]] by [[Lake Sah'ot|Lake Sah'Ot]] on [[Chandrila/Legends|Chandrila]]. He believes it is a dark time for [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]] but it is much better off than it was ten years ago. Hextrophon mentions that though the Imperials are gaining victories, they can't seem to cooperate with each other. He then asks Voren to forward the recordings of the fighting on Coruscant to some of the more skeptical members of his staff and reassures Voren about [[Rivoche Tarkin]]'s safety.
