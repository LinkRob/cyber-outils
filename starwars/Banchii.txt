'''Banchii''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Inugg system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], it housed a [[Jedi temple (Banchii)|Jedi outpost]] which [[Jedi Knight]] [[Lily Tora-Asi]] was stationed at in the aftermath of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]].<ref name="Edge of Balance 1" />
