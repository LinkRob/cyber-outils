The '''Domancion system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Suolriep sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square S-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the site of<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Battlenamia|event=[[Domancion Accord]]|system=Domancion [[Star system|system]]}}</ref> the [[Domancion Accord]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 6">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 6|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 6]]</ref>
