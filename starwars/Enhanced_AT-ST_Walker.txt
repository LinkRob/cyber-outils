The '''Enhanced AT-ST Walker''' was a model of [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]] [[walker]] used by the [[Imperial Army|Army]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. It had been outfitted with enhanced power couplings and sub-systems, which allowed its weapons to fire high powered blasts at a faster rate than average. The performance modifications were built around existing systems, which visibly differentiated the Enhanced AT-ST from standard AT-ST walkers, with two such differences being dark [[color]]ed [[armor]] plates and the absence of side-mounted weapons on the command pod.<ref name="Commander" />
