'''Caldra Prime''' was a location<ref name="FACPOV" /> in the [[Caldra Prime system]] in the [[Ado sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was situated near [[Caldra Tertius]]. A number of trade [[Planet|worlds]] were located around Caldra Prime and Caldra Tertius. Around the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Hoth]]<ref name="FACPOV" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> a group of [[pirate]]s blazed a [[hyperspace route]] from the [[Vosch Cluster]] to the trade worlds around Caldra Prime and Caldra Tertius. The presence of the pirates threatened a [[Seventh Division|portion]] of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], which had escaped the battle, grouping at a [[Unidentified rendezvous point|rendezvous point]] that laid between the Vosch Cluster and the trade worlds.<ref name="FACPOV" />
