The '''Alliance to Restore the Republic''' ([[2 BBY]]&ndash;[[4 ABY]]), commonly known as the '''Rebel Alliance''', '''Alliance''', and the '''Rebellion''', was a resistance movement to oppose the [[Imperial Era|rule]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and to [[New Republic|restore]] the [[Galactic Republic]]. The Alliance was publicly and formally declared with the [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance]] by [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]], which brought numerous rebel cells together as a common force opposed to the Empire. Indeed, the concept of a "Rebel Alliance" had its roots in smaller movements that had worked to unite disconnected cells, such as a [[Bail Organa's resistance movement|movement]] secretly led by fellow Senator [[Bail Prestor Organa]] and a [[Axis network|rebel network]] formed by revolutionary [[Luthen Rael]], who received funding and aid from Mothma. Such early groups and the eventual Alliance came together from a number of unrelated rebel cells, including the [[Spectres|crew]] of the ''[[Ghost]]'' and the [[Phoenix Cell]]. The [[Atrivis Resistance Group]] was one of the first cells to join what Mothma began calling "'''the Alliance'''" before the formal formation of the Alliance to Restore the Republic.

The Alliance and the cells it united were born from the overall discontent within the Empire of those who wished the Galactic Republic restored. Its efforts were by supportive senators in the [[Imperial Senate]] such as Mothma, Organa, and later, his daughter [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. Its members were citizens of the Empire or defectors from the [[Imperial Military]], such as [[Jan Dodonna]], [[Ryder Azadi]], and [[Wedge Antilles]]. Founded and united by Organa and [[Ahsoka Tano]], the early rebels were assisted by surviving [[Jedi]], such as [[Kanan Jarrus]], and his [[Padawan]], [[Ezra Bridger]]. [[Clone Wars]] veterans, such as [[Captain]] [[Rex]] also assisted in the initial movement.

As opposed to waging all-out war, the Alliance waged an [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|insurgency]] against the Empire, establishing small bases across [[the galaxy]] instead of controlling an entire planet. When the Alliance [[Battle of Scarif|stole]] the [[Death Star plans|plans]] for the Empire's ultimate weapon, the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], and destroyed the space station in the [[Battle of Yavin]] with the help of [[Luke Skywalker]], the tide of the war began to turn, and the Rebel threat began to prove a severe thorn in the Empire's side. The Rebellion continued to engage in battles with the Empire in the [[Galactic Civil War]]. However, under the command of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the Imperial forces succeeded in [[Battle of Hoth|defeating]] the Alliance on [[Hoth]], forcing the Alliance to scatter its forces in order to survive.

Eventually, the Alliance prevailed against the Empire and critically damaged it by launching an attack against the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] during its construction in what would become the [[Battle of Endor]]. Not only did the Alliance manage to destroy the [[battle station]], but the [[Imperial Navy]] was severely crippled by the loss of [[Executor|one]] of its [[Super Star Destroyer]]s, and Luke Skywalker redeemed his father, [[Darth]] Vader, who killed [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], before dying himself in the battle. 

The Alliance was soon [[Declaration of a New Republic|reorganized]] into the fledgling [[New Republic]], restoring the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] and continued the rebellion against the shattered Empire, lasting until the [[Battle of Jakku]] one [[Standard year|year]] after [[Endor]], forcing the Galactic Empire to [[Imperial Instruments of Surrender|end]] the war and finally overthrowing it by the signing of the [[Galactic Concordance]].

==History==
===Early foundations===
====Resistance movements aided by the Jedi====
{{Quote|The Separatists have strength in numbers. We're going to show you how to target and destroy them.''"<br />"''Now to be clear, we are not here to fight your war&mdash;rather, to show you how to conduct it in the most efficient and successful way possible.|Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi brief the Onderon rebels|<ref name="Two Fronts">{{TCW|A War on Two Fronts}}</ref>}}
[[File:OnderonianRebellion-WOTF.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Onderon rebels, the first of many militias that were created, receive training from the Jedi.]]
While the Alliance to Restore the Republic was a resistance movement opposed to the tyranny of the [[Galactic Empire]] and its [[New Order]], its roots predated the actual birth of the Empire, when the [[Galactic Republic]] was in its [[Republic Era|last years of existence]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], a conflict that pitted the Republic against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Attack of the Clones">{{Film|II}}</ref> the [[Jedi Order]] helped create resistance units on Separatist-held worlds as part of a plan proposed by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], one example being the [[Onderon rebels]].<ref name="Two Fronts" /> However, it was not for many more [[Standard year|years]] that such [[Rebel cell/Canon|rebel cells]] would coalesce into a single Rebellion with a centralized leadership.<ref name="Alliance DB" />

====Delegation of 2,000====
{{Quote|So this is how liberty dies... with thunderous applause.|Padmé Amidala, to Bail Organa following the declaration of a New Order|audio=HowLibertyDies-ROTS.flac|<ref name="Revenge of the Sith">{{Film|III}}</ref>}}
[[File:Delegation_of_2000_meeting.png|thumb|right|250px|Mon Mothma, Bail Organa, and Padmé Amidala (left) were among the first to oppose Sheev Palpatine, even before he became Emperor.]]
Also during the Clone Wars, [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] had managed to obtain many emergency powers from the [[Galactic Senate]], which caused certain members of the Chancellor's own [[Loyalist Committee]], including [[Bail Prestor Organa]] of [[Alderaan]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of [[Naboo]],<ref name="Bail DB">{{Databank|bail-organa|Bail Organa}}</ref> and [[Mon Mothma]] of [[Chandrila]],<ref name="Mon Mothma DB">{{Databank|mon-mothma|Mon Mothma}}</ref> to start to complain about the chief of state's conduct.<ref name="Bail DB" /> The discontented [[senator]]ial representatives ended up forming the [[Delegation of 2,000]]. They signed the [[Petition of 2,000]], a document that formally expressed its signatories' concerns with Palpatine's rule and called for him to relinquish his emergency powers as soon as possible. Senator [[Meena Tills]] of [[Mon Cala]] was among the petitioners.<ref name="Meena DB">{{Databank|meena-tills|Meena Tills}}</ref>

[[File:Bail Organa Order 66.png|thumb|left|250px|After being branded traitors, the Jedi Order was nearly wiped out by the Republic's clone army, something Bail Organa would personally witness on Coruscant.]]
Shortly thereafter, the Clone Wars was brought to an [[Mission to Mustafar|end]]. Far from relinquishing his [[Dictatorship|dictatorial]] status, Chancellor Palpatine unilaterally abolished the Republic in front of the whole Senate and anointed himself the [[Galactic Emperor]], thus transforming the democratic government into an authoritarian regime, the Galactic Empire. Unbeknownst to most of the galactic community, the self-promoted Emperor Palpatine was in fact the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Sidious, who had masterminded the Clone Wars so that he could gain power and overthrow the Republic. The Emperor immediately started to subvert democracy in [[the galaxy]] and declared the peacekeeping [[Jedi]] Knights enemies of the state.<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" /> In the immediate aftermath of the Clone Wars and Senator Amidala's death at the hands of her husband, Darth Vader — the identity adopted by Anakin Skywalker when he became Sidious's [[Sith apprentice]] — Senators Organa and Mothma started working in secret to overthrow the Empire.<ref name="Alliance DB" />

====Age of the Empire====
{{Quote|They're not trying to make a difference. They're just trying to strike back.''"<br />"''And I understand that. But if the people who have a beef with the Empire act solely in their own interests, it won't do anybody else any good. In fact, it might make it harder for any kind of real rebellion to flower&mdash;|Kanan Jarrus and Hera Syndulla|<ref name="AND">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref>}}
[[File:Declaration2.png|thumb|right|250px|The Clone Wars ended with Chancellor Palpatine declaring himself Emperor of the Galactic Empire.]]
In the absence of any major opposition, the Galactic Empire thrived during the early days of Palpatine's reign, amassing a vast [[Imperial Military|military]] and using it to instill fear in the civilian population,<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> yet public opinion<ref name="Infiltration">{{TBB|Infiltration (episode)}}</ref> and the supposed power of the [[Imperial Senate]] remained vital<ref name="Darth Vader (2017) 13">[[Darth Vader (2017) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 13]]</ref> until Palpatine could fully cement his power.<ref name="Nobody's Listening!">{{Andor|Nobody's Listening!}}</ref> All the same, the reformed Senate, being even less effective than it was during the last years of the Republic, did nothing to restrain that massive military expansion<ref name="Tarkin" /> as its senators, in time, gave into Palpatine's demands time and time again.<ref name="Rogue One 1">[[Rogue One Adaptation 1|''Rogue One Adaptation'' 1]]</ref> Meanwhile, in the deepest secrecy, the Empire worked on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], a moon-sized [[battle station]] capable of destroying entire [[planet]]s.<ref name="Tarkin" />

[[File:Gerrera speaks to the Bad Batch.png|thumb|left|250px|The Onderon milita, led by Saw Gerrera, became one of the first groups to oppose the Empire.]]
Senators [[Mon Mothma]] and [[Bail Prestor Organa]] attempted to re-assert the power of the Senate through the [[Imperial Rebirth Act]]<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> around 19 BBY.<ref name="ActDate">{{MOFdate|60}}</ref> While the act would pass, Palpatine claimed he had the authority to veto portions of it, curtailing whatever effectiveness it might have had. Mothma responded to the news by threating a prolonged political struggle, but [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] explained all the ways Palpatine had rigged the political game in his favor and forced Mothma to admit she was not yet ready to wage a war against Palpatine, who had both the love of the populous and the means to win such a struggle. In the wake of the act's failure, Mothma and Organa would meet with the [[Partisans|Partisan]] leader [[Saw Gerrera]] and established something of an unofficial alliance with the rebel: deciding to work together to overthrow Palpatine, Gerrera promised to provide the basis of a rebel army, Organa set himself and Alderaan up as their alliance's ideological heart, and Mothma, in what she realized was the weakest contribution of the three, promised to continue building up coalitions to oppose the Emperor as their political heart.<ref name="MOF" />

[[File:RescueOfHeraSyndulla-DD.png|thumb|right|250px|Despite initially trying to find peace with the Empire, Cham Syndulla would organize a resistance cell against the Imperial occupation of Ryloth.]]
Although many people held a grudge against the Empire, few dared to act. Those who did often acted solely in their own interests, which actually made it harder for any kind of real, organized rebellion to appear.<ref name="AND" /> A rare example of organized resistance against the Empire was that of [[Cham Syndulla]]'s [[Free Ryloth Movement]], which wanted to end the Imperial occupation on the planet [[Ryloth]]. It was formed from the [[Twi'lek Resistance]], which had [[Battle of Ryloth|resisted]] the occupation of Ryloth by the Separatists during the Clone Wars. Over the years, the movement established hidden bases across the [[Ryloth system]] and executed raids against the Imperials.<ref name="Lords of the Sith">''[[Lords of the Sith]]''</ref> Meanwhile, [[Separatist holdouts]] remained active in the galaxy and continued to fight<ref name="Tarkin" /> with hopes of securing independence from Imperial control.<ref name="Infiltration">{{TBB|Infiltration (episode)}}</ref><ref name="The Solitary Clone">{{TBB|The Solitary Clone}}</ref> The former Separatist capital, [[Raxus Secundus]], was [[Occupation of Raxus Secundus|occupied]] by the Imperial Military,<ref name="Common">{{TBB|Common Ground}}</ref> yet that did little to quell the spirit of independence amongst its people.<ref name="Infiltration" />

[[File:ChuchiSingh-TBB-Infiltration.png|thumb|left|250px|Protected by rogue clone troopers, Imperial Senator Riyo Chuchi met with former Separatist Senator Avi Singh to discuss opposing the Empire.]]
During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="BB3 Date">{{TBBDate|306}}</ref> rumors swirled that Palpatine was scared about the prospect of planets and whole star systems allying together in the name of overthrowing him. While such a possible rebellion lacked any unifying leaders at that time, Palpatine and his agents were unable to act out in the open because public opinion was still so important to his legitimacy, even if he had the excuse to occupy worlds like Raxus. At that time, a growing number of [[Pantoran]]s wished for true independence instead of remaining under the Imperial flag. To that end, Pantoran Senator [[Riyo Chuchi]] [[Riyo Chuchi and Avi Singh's secret meeting|met]] with the former Separatist Senator of Raxus, [[Avi Singh]], to discuss Palpatine's fears and the prospect of an allied Rebellion. To try and silence a revolution before it began, a [[Clone X trooper|clone assassin]] codenamed [[CX-1]] was dispatched to kill Singh, but his efforts were halted by the [[Clone Underground]], a network of clones who had rebelled against the Empire and hoped to help their brethren.<ref name="Infiltration" />

[[File:Mission to Desix.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Various Separatist holdouts attempted to resist Imperial assimilation, only to be crushed by the Empire.]]
Another rebel cell emerged one year after the end of the Clone Wars when the Empire decided to severely [[Antar Atrocity|punish]] the world of [[Antar 4]] for its past ties with the Confederacy of Independent Systems. That murderous operation, spearheaded by [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], prompted the former [[Republic Intelligence]] agent [[Berch Teller]] to found his own [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|resistance cell]]. Teller hoped they were contributing to what would eventually grow into a cause against the Empire. Four years after its formation, the insurgents managed to steal the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', the personal [[starship]] of Tarkin, to attack various Imperial targets with information provided to them by an Imperial traitor, [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]]. Teller's insurgents later launched an attack on an Imperial convoy transporting important parts for the Death Star project. Their plans were soon thwarted by Tarkin's timely intervention, which resulted in the killing of every insurgent except Teller.<ref name="Tarkin" />

======Assorted insurgencies, a single Empire======

[[File:LuthenRaelAntiqueShop.png|thumb|left|250px|Mon Mothma began working alongside Luthen Rael to fund rebel operations.]]
However, by [[5 BBY]], a number of rebel cells had formed, operating all across the galaxy as part of an [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] against growing Imperial oppression, especially in light of the Empire's continuing expansion throughout the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]].<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref> One insurgency began in the [[Atrivis sector]], where the [[Liberators]] [[Battle on Mantooine|seized]] an [[Imperial garrison (Mantooine)|Imperial garrison]] but were devastatingly defeated by the [[Imperial Navy]] soon afterwards.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> A rebel operative named [[Luthen Rael]] did much of the on-the-ground early work in forming a [[Axis network|network]] of rebel cells,<ref name="Announcement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref> receiving funding from<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> and working alongside Senator Mothma.<ref name="Announcement" /> Mothma also established a [[Mon Mothma's rebel cell|rebel cell]] of her own at some point.<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> The three principle architects of the Rebel Alliance would prove to be Mothma, Organa, and Gerrera,<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/735101/star-wars-the-mask-of-fear-reign-of-the-empire-by-alexander-freed|text=Star Wars: The Mask of Fear (Reign of the Empire)}}</ref> the most extreme of the three.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> However, the Alliance might never have been established if not for Rael's efforts,<ref name="Episodes10-12">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-finale-episodes-10-11-12|text=''Andor'' Explained: Episodes 10-12 - "Make It Stop", "Who Else Knows?", and "Jedha, Kyber, Erso"}}</ref> with future rebel [[Wilmon Paak]], indeed, arguing the Alliance owed its existence to him.<ref name="WhoElseKnows">{{Andor|Who Else Knows?}}</ref>

[[File:Fulcrumisborn-DoR.png|thumb|right|250px|Working with Ahsoka Tano, Organa would establish his own network of rebel cells.]]
Organa and former [[Jedi]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] operated as part of their own [[Bail Organa's resistance movement|resistance movement]] that would fall into the larger whole of<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> an emerging alliance of rebel cells.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> With the Empire beginning to take notice of Mothma's banking activities, Rael set up a [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team|rebel infiltration team]]<ref name="Aldhani" /> under Mothma's cousin<ref name="Nobody's Listening">{{Andor|Nobody's Listening!}}</ref> [[Vel Sartha]] to [[Aldhani heist|steal]] the quarterly payroll for the Imperial sector surrounding the [[planet]] [[Aldhani]].<ref name="Aldhani" /> Rael also reasoned such a major strike against the Empire would make the regime lash out, inspiring more people to rise up as they suffered under a sudden burst of Imperial tyranny.<ref name="Annoucement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref> However, the success of the Aldhani mission effectively froze Mothma's bank accounts<ref name="Daughter of Ferrix">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> under new laws,<ref name="One Way Out">{{Andor|One Way Out}}</ref> thereby leaving Mothma with few ways to even out her accounts before the Empire fully investigated. In the end, the only solution before Mothma was to marry her daughter [[Leida Mothma|Leida]] off to the [[Stekan Sculdun|son]] of [[Chandrilan]]<ref name="Daughter of Ferrix" /> oligarch<ref name="Episode Highlights">{{SW|url=news/andor-episode-9-nobodys-listening-highlights|text=''Andor'' Analyzed: 5 Highlights from Episode 9, "Nobody's Listening!"}}</ref> [[Davo Sculdun]]. If she went through with the proposal, Sculdun promised to deposit into her account,<ref name="One Way Out" /> thereby covering up the missing funds she had delivered to Rael.<ref name="Nobody's Listening" />

======Building the fight======
{{Quote|We need to pull together, Saw! Whatever our final version of success looks like, there's no chance any of us can make it real on our own.|Luthen Rael, to Saw Gerrera|<ref name="Narkina 5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:AldhaniHeistTeam-CGSWG.png|thumb|left|250px|Under Luthen's orders, Vel Sartha led a successful heist of an entire quarterly Imperial payroll on Aldhani.]]
While Mothma could find Rael's tactics hard to stomach, particularly his usage of the Aldhani heist to hasten the speed of Imperial oppression in the name of inspiring more people to rebel,<ref name="Annoucement" /> she did reflect in her personal journal that the Rebellion she was helping to form needed to show its teeth, lest Palpatine's military crush it in its infancy.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> While she did have hope of a peaceful end to the conflict in the Senate,<ref name="Name of Rebellion">{{Rebels|In the Name of the Rebellion}}</ref> she and Bail Organa helped organize paramilitary attacks from the shadows, all while presenting themselves as principled dissenters in public. Furthermore, she knew that the galaxy's assorted rebel cells needed to unify in the name of opposing Palpatine or else each would be hunted down one by one.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

[[File:Rael-and-Gerrera.png|thumb|right|300px|Luthen Rael met with Saw Gerrera to argue that the rebel cells needed to band into a unified force.]]
Rael also met with Saw Gerrera of the Partisans in an effort to convince him to work alongside former Separatist [[Anto Kreegyr]]'s [[Anto Kreegyr's group|group]], arguing that the rebel cells, despite their political differences, needed to join together to become a true threat to the Empire.<ref name="Narkina 5" /> By the time Gerrera agreed, the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] had discovered Kreegyr's looming [[Spellhaus attack|raid]] on the [[Spellhaus Imperial power station]]. Reasoning that Kreegyr's demise would make the ISB overconfident and protect Rael's agent within the ISB, [[Lonni Jung]], the two reluctantly agreed to sacrifice Kreegyr's forces for the greater good of their war effort.<ref name="Daughter of Ferrix" /> Shortly afterward, the raid indeed ended in the total destruction of Kreegyr's attack party. Following the [[Ferrix Riot|Rix Road riot]], Rael also formally recruited [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] into his network.<ref name="Rix Road" /> The [[Atrivis Resistance Group]], led by [[Travia Chan]], was also active in the Atrivis sector. After deliberation, they decided to join Mothma, becoming one of the first groups of what Mothma had begun calling "the Alliance."<ref name="Rebel Files" />

======Rise of the Spectres======
{{Main|Spectres|Phoenix Squadron}}
[[File:Droids in Distress thumb.png|thumb|left|300px|The Specters would come into contact with Organa's rebel network through his droids C-3PO and R2-D2.]]
One rebel cell was the [[Spectres]], which largely fought on the Imperial-controlled Outer Rim planet of [[Lothal]].<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> Another rebel cell was [[Phoenix Cell]], led by [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]],<ref name="Fire Recon">{{RebelsRecon|xggYEpemcck|#1.14: Inside "Fire Across the Galaxy" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-fire-across-the-galaxy}}</ref> which was a part of the larger rebel network led by Tano and Senator Organa. At that point, members of those cells were not supposed to know they were part of a larger movement known as the rebellion, so if captured, they could not give information on the other rebel cells. The cells were only provided with intelligence on a need-to-know basis by Tano, using the codename "[[Fulcrum]]."<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />

[[File:Ezra transmits a message of rebellion.png|thumb|right|250px|After a string of missions, Specter member Ezra Bridger would broadcast an inspiring call to arms to potential rebels across the galaxy.]]
Due to the increased rebel activities around Lothal, the Imperial Security Bureau dispatched [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]] to hunt down the Spectres. Kallus saw the rebel activities as part of a pattern which could lead to a spark of rebellion.<ref name="Spark of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref> The Spectres further came to the attention of Organa and received information from Tano as "Fulcrum."<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" /> After the Spectres frustrated several Imperial efforts to capture them, [[Grand Moff]] Tarkin visited Lothal and warned the Imperial officials and officers that while the various cells lacked unity, the rebels on Lothal were unique because they had unity, which made them a credible threat to the Empire. Tarkin warned that the failures of Imperial forces on Lothal allowed the Spectres to represent hope, and ordered [[the Grand Inquisitor]] to execute two of the incompetent Imperial officers. He warned the others that failure would have consequences.<ref name="Call to Action">{{Rebels|Call to Action}}</ref>

[[File:Rebel fleet arrives at Mustafar.png|thumb|left|250px|Under Ahsoka's command, forces from Phoenix Cell would rescue the Specters from an Imperial fleet over Mustafar, marking the first major fleet engagement between rebel and Imperial naval forces.]]
The Rebellion's existence was finally revealed to the Empire when they [[Raid on the communications tower|captured]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] after he and the rest of the Spectres were able to hack and send out a rebellious transmission to Lothal and many surrounding systems. His capture prompted his fellow Spectres to launch a mission to [[Action at Mustafar|rescue]] him from Tarkin's personal Imperial Star Destroyer, the ''[[Sovereign]]'', over [[Mustafar]]. In order to save one of their cells and the only other Jedi involved in the movement other than herself, Tano led Phoenix Squadron to assist in their escape. Following the successful rescue, she informed the Spectres that their protocol of secrecy had changed. In response to the transmission sent by the Spectres and the rumors of the rebel action over Mustafar, anti-Imperial unrest began on Lothal and several other worlds. In response, the Emperor dispatched [[Darth]] Vader to deal with the issue.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />

=====New alliances and expansions=====
{{Quote|There comes a time when refusing to stop violence can no longer be called nonviolence. We cease to be objectors and become bystanders. At some point, morality must be wedded to action, or else it's no more than mere&hellip; vanity.|Senator Mon Mothma|<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Vader attacks the Phoenix fleet.png|thumb|right|250px|Darth Vader would personally cripple the fleet of Phoenix Cell, though a handful of rebels would escape.]]
Following the [[Siege of Lothal]], the Spectres were forced to retreat from Lothal and Phoenix Cell was crippled when Vader [[Attack on Phoenix Squadron (4 BBY)|destroyed]] their [[Phoenix Home|command ship]]. As a result of their encounter with Vader on Lothal, the Spectres decided to permanently join Phoenix Cell.<ref name="TSoL">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''</ref> Despite the attack, the Spectres were able to [[Assault on Seelos|recruit]] retired [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] to their cause.<ref name="RotOR">{{Rebels|Relics of the Old Republic}}</ref> Phoenix Cell was also able to acquire a prototype [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing starfighter]] from [[Mon Calamari]] engineer [[Quarrie]].<ref name="WotM">{{Rebels|Wings of the Master}}</ref> Working with Rex, the Spectres were able to [[Rescue on the Imperial Interdictor|destroy]] the [[Interdictor (Interdictor-class)|Imperial Interdictor]].<ref name="Stealth Strike">{{Rebels|Stealth Strike}}</ref> The Spectres also prevented members of the [[Inquisitorius]] from capturing several Force-sensitive children.<ref name="The Future of the Force">{{Rebels|The Future of the Force}}</ref> A rebel fleet was constructed over the planet [[Paucris Major]] and [[Evacuation of Paucris Major|evacuated]] upon being warned by [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], daughter of Senator Organa, of a coming Imperial force.<ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan" />

[[File:Stormtroopers_watch_the_Hammerheads.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Phoenix Cell would be supplemented with three Hammerhead corvettes which they would "steal" from Leia Organa, daughter of Bail.]]
Later, Phoenix Cell suffered a major blow when the Empire learned they were hiding on the planet [[Garel]].<ref name="Legacy">{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref> The Phoenix Squadron managed to escape but suffered some losses during the [[Battle of Garel]]. After Senator Organa learned of their losses, he sent Leia to [[Mission on Lothal|deliver]] three [[Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette|''Sphyrna''-class Hammerhead corvettes]] to them on Lothal.<ref name="A Princess on Lothal">{{Rebels|A Princess on Lothal}}</ref> In [[3 BBY]], Phoenix Cell undertook a mission to seek safe passage for the Rebellion through the [[Concord Dawn system]]. After [[Dogfight over Concord Dawn|meeting resistance]] from [[Fenn Rau]]'s [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protectors]], the Spectres managed to kidnap him and force him to meet their demands for safe passage after a second attempt.<ref name="The Protector of Concord Dawn">{{Rebels|The Protector of Concord Dawn}}</ref> All the while, the [[Telaris rebel stardocks]] were hard at work converting Mon Calamari [[Star Cruiser]]s into warships.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

[[File:Rebel fleet TFD.png|thumb|right|250px|Working with Cham Syndulla, Phoenix Cell would steal an Imperial carrier for use as their new capital ship.]]
Afterward, the Spectres and Cham's Free Ryloth movement joined forces to steal a [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier]] above the planet Ryloth.<ref name="Homecoming">{{Rebels|Homecoming}}</ref> The stolen carrier became the new flagship ''[[Phoenix Nest]]'' of Phoenix Squadron. With the help of Chopper and the former Imperial [[RA-7 protocol droid]] [[AP-5]], Phoenix Squadron established a new base<ref name="The Forgotten Droid">{{Rebels|The Forgotten Droid}}</ref> called [[Chopper Base]] on the planet [[Atollon]].<ref name="Mystery of Chopper Base">{{Rebels|The Mystery of Chopper Base}}</ref> By [[2 BBY]],<ref name="WelcomeDate">{{AndorDate|210}}</ref> the in-progress Rebel Alliance was also establishing a base in the [[Great Temple]] of the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]], where Organa and Mothma intended for the revolution to be headquartered in due time.<ref name="Welcome">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref> As a temporary base that ended up being expanded more than originally intended,<ref name="BMF 37">{{BuildFalconCite|37|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> however, the Alliance's first headquarters would nonetheless be established through the [[Dantooine base]].<ref name="Dantooine Databank">{{Databank|dantooine|Dantooine}}</ref>

=====Eluding Thrawn=====
[[File:Thrawn_ponders.png|thumb|left|250px|Grand Admiral Thrawn was tasked to eliminate the burgeoning rebel threat.]]
The Imperial tactician [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] was promoted by the Emperor to [[Grand Admiral]] for his role in [[Batonn insurgency|obliterating]] the [[Batonn insurgents|rebel insurgency]] in the [[Batonn sector]] and the mastermind vigilante [[Nevil Cygni|Nightswan]]. Nightswan once planned on bringing various rebel and insurgent groups together as an alliance, but, as he had recalled to Thrawn, he stopped because of paranoia, distrust, "squabbling," and pride. Nightswan did not know if any individual would succeed in creating a rebel alliance, simply knowing he was not the man who could.<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref>

[[File:Reklam Station magnetic locks.png|thumb|right|250px|Phoenix Cell would steal several Republic-era Y-wings for Jan Dodanna's Massassi Group.]]
Grand Admiral Thrawn believed the Empire was facing a galaxy-wide insurgency and studied his enemies' philosophy, art, and history in order to defeat them. In response to the activities of Phoenix Cell in the [[Lothal sector]], [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]] enlisted his help in defeating them. Thrawn was not content with destroying Phoenix Squadron but sought to destroy the entire rebel fleet. Consequently, he allowed the squadron to escape with several stolen [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wings]] during a [[mission to Reklam Station]]. These Y-wings were sent to [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]]'s [[Massassi Group|unit]].<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref>

[[File:The Antilles Extraction thumb.png|thumb|left|250px|Phoenix Cell agent Sabine Wren aided Imperial pilots Wedge Antilles and Derek Klivian in defecting to the rebellion.]]
The Rebellion also received an unexpected ally in the form of Agent Kallus, a former adversary of Phoenix Cell and the Spectres.<ref name="Antilles Extraction">{{Rebels|The Antilles Extraction}}</ref> Kallus had undergone a change of heart after befriending [[Garazeb Orrelios|Zeb Orrelios]] on the ice moon of [[Bahryn]].<ref name="The Honorable Ones">{{Rebels|The Honorable Ones}}</ref> Kallus became the rebel spy known as Fulcrum and informed Phoenix Cell that several [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] cadets at the [[Skystrike Academy]] were planning to defect. Kallus also helped the rebel Wren escape with the defecting pilots [[Wedge Antilles]] and [[Derek Klivian]],<ref name="Antilles Extraction" /> who would later join the Alliance's [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps|Starfighter Corps]].<ref name="Rebels Recon Antilles Extraction">{{RebelsRecon|6em1j3nQcSk|#3.03: Inside "The Antilles Extraction" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-the-antilles-extraction}}</ref>

After Governor [[Gar Saxon]] massacred the Mandalorian Protectors, Rau finally joined the Rebellion.<ref name="Imperial Super Commandos">{{Rebels|Imperial Supercommandos}}</ref> After the Phoenix rebels evacuated rebel sympathizers from the planet [[Mykapo]], the rebel cell [[Iron Squadron]] joined the Rebellion.<ref name="Iron Squadron">{{Rebels|Iron Squadron (episode)}}</ref> The Spectres later undertook a mission with the former and original Governor of Lpthal [[Ryder Azadi]]'s [[Lothal resistance group|rebel cell]] to steal Thrawn's plans for a [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter|new prototype starfighter]] from the [[Imperial Armory Complex]] on Lothal. During the course of their [[Infiltration of the Imperial Armory Complex|mission]], the rebels were helped by Agent Kallus.<ref name="An Inside Man">{{Rebels|An Inside Man}}</ref>

[[File:Klik Klak Geonosis nest Saw Gerrera.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Specters would come into conflict with Saw Gerrera over his treatment of a surviving Geonosian, who Saw planned to interrogate for Imperial secrets.]]
Later, [[Alliance High Command|Rebel Command]] dispatched Gerrera and his Partisans on a [[Mission to investigate Geonosis|mission]] to investigate the disappearance of the [[Geonosian]] species. With the help of the Spectres and Geonosian survivor [[Klik-Klak]], the rebels discovered evidence that the Empire perpetrated genocide against the Geonosians. Although Organa hoped to use the information to raise awareness of Imperial atrocities and to sway more worlds to their cause, the evidence, canisters used in the genocide, was lost when the Spectres and Gerrera were forced to escape, although they did manage to take images of the canisters.<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis">{{Rebels|Ghosts of Geonosis}}</ref> As the Rebellion continued to grow, Gerrera's violent tactics continued to put him at odds with the rest of the Rebels. In time, Mothma requested an official [[resolution of Censure Condemning Saw Gerrera of the Partisans]] to separate him from their growing alliance.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

Thrawn also embarked on an ambitious program to search for Phoenix Squadron's base by dispatching [[E-XD-series infiltrator droid]]s to sweep the Outer Rim Territories for signs of rebel activity. One unit, [[EXD-9]], [[EXD-9's infiltration of Chopper Base|discovered]] Chopper Base but Orrelios, [[C1-10P|Chopper]], and AP-5 reprogrammed him into an improvised bomb that was sent back to its base and destroyed one of Thrawn's [[Star Destroyer]]s. Despite this setback, Thrawn managed to narrow down the search for Chopper Base to 94 systems.<ref name="Warhead">{{Rebels|Warhead (episode)}}</ref> Thrawn also later discovered that Kallus was a rebel [[spy]] and decided to use him against the Rebellion.<ref name="TIE">{{Rebels|Through Imperial Eyes}}</ref>

===Rise of the Rebellion===
====Official formation====
{{Main|Declaration of Rebellion}}
{{Quote|This is Senator Mon Mothma, I have been called a traitor for speaking out against a corrupt Galactic Senate. A Senate manipulated by the sinister tactics of the Emperor. For too long I have watched the heavy hand of the Empire strangle our liberties, stifling our freedoms in the name of ensuring our safety. No longer! Despite Imperial threats, despite the Emperor himself, I have no fear as I take new action. For I am not alone. Beginning today we stand together as allies. I hereby resign from the Senate to fight for you, not from the distant hall of politics but from the front lines. We will not rest until we bring an end to the Empire, until we restore our Republic! Are you with me?|Mon Mothma to the various rebel cells during the Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|<ref name="Secret Cargo" />}}
[[File:Secret_Cargo.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Mon Mothma, Jon Vander, Gold Two, Erskin Semaj, and the Spectres witnessing the birth of the Rebel Alliance.]]
The Rebel Alliance formally came into existence after Mothma resigned from the Imperial Senate and denounced Emperor Palpatine for his role in the [[Ghorman Massacre]]. With the help of [[Phoenix Leader]] [[Hera Syndulla]] and her Spectres, Mothma escaped Thrawn's forces by [[Operation Handoff|traveling]] through the dangerous [[Archeon pass]] to reach [[Dantooine]],<ref name="Secret Cargo" /> the site of a [[Dantooine base|temporary base]] that was increasingly built up as the Alliance grew.<ref name="BMF 37">{{BuildFalconCite|37|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> While Alliance had already established a base on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]]<ref name="Messenger">{{Andor|Messenger}}</ref> that Mothma and Bail Organa intended to become Alliance headquarters, they recognized it was not yet ready<ref name="WelcomeRebel">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref> as of 2 BBY. Nonetheless, the makings of the [[Alliance Army]] could be found at Yavin.<ref name="Messenger" /> Over Dantooine, Mon Mothma gave a [[HoloNet]] speech calling for the galaxy to rise up in rebellion against the Empire. Mothma's message was heard by the rebel cells throughout the galaxy including Lothal and Atollon. The [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Rebel Fleet]] converged above Dantooine, and the cells were [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|unified]] into the Alliance to Restore the Republic, which officially began the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Secret Cargo" />

[[File:Mon Calamari Exodus Fleet SWA.png|thumb|right|250px|After its official formation, the Rebel Alliance would be supported by the Mon Calamari Exodus Fleet led by Admiral Raddus.]]
Around the same time as the Alliance's formal formation, [[Garven Dreis]] and [[Antoc Merrick]] joined the rebellion.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> As the Alliance grew in publicity<ref name="Roguenovelization">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref> and around that same time, a grouping of [[Mon Calamari]] [[cruiser]]s joined the Alliance fleet.<ref name="Timelines" /> While Mothma admitted in [[Short Notes on the History of the Rebel Alliance Navy]] that the Alliance perhaps did not deserve the Mon Calamari [[city-ship]]s that joined its ranks, as it failed to carry out significant action against the [[occupation of Mon Cala]], she believed the Alliance's ability to secure these ships and the boon to the fleet they represented was proof of how important it was to win the hearts of the galaxy.<ref name="Roguenovelization" />

As part of the Attack on Lothal, Syndulla dispatched the droids AP-5 and Chopper along with the former Imperial cadet Wedge Antilles to steal clearance codes from the Imperial Security Bureau [[Killun Station|station]] on the [[moon]] [[Killun 71]]. During the course of the mission, Chopper was [[Slicer|hijacked]] by a group of Imperial slicers and intelligence officers led by [[Boon|LT-319]], who were based aboard an [[IGV-55 surveillance vessel]]. LT-319 tried to use Chopper to kill the Spectres and find the location of Chopper Base. However, Syndulla managed to reprogram Chopper and transmitted a data surge that destroyed the Imperial surveillance vessel.<ref name="DAD">{{Rebels|Double Agent Droid}}</ref>

[[File:Sentinel-Flare.png|thumb|left|250px|Now with proper organization, the rebellion would launch several operations against the Empire including Operation Shield Bash.]]
Having obtained the clearance codes, the Phoenix Squadron began training for their Attack on Lothal. [[Ezra Bridger]] was briefly distracted when he departed with Chopper on an [[Mission to Tatooine (Imperial Era)|errant mission]] to [[Tatooine]] to save Jedi Master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] from his arch-nemesis the renegade Sith Lord [[Maul]]. Kenobi told Bridger to return to his place with the Rebellion and defeated Maul in combat.<ref name="Twin Suns">{{Rebels|Twin Suns (episode)}}</ref> At some stage of its existence, the Rebel Alliance undertook an [[assault on Vivonah]] and the [[slaughter on Noult]], which were decried as [[terrorist]] actions by rebel enemies.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> The rebellion maintained forces on<ref name="Rebel Files" /> the planet<ref name="Heroes">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Heroes of Rogue One}}</ref> [[Gaulus]] until effecting a [[Retreat from Gaulus|withdrawal]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> [[Operation Shield Bash]] was launched as a joint venture between the Alliance and [[Truwel system rebels]] at some point.<ref name="Fully Operational">[[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']]</ref>

====Ambush on Atollon====
{{Main|Battle of Atollon}}
{{Quote|We'll never surrender to you, Thrawn.''"<br />"''You misunderstand, captain. I'm not accepting surrenders at this time. I want you to know failure. Utter defeat. And know that it is I who delivers it crashing down upon you.|Hera Syndulla and Thrawn|<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>}}
As part of the final stage of the Attack on Lothal, General Dodonna and the Massassi Group traveled to Atollon to rendezvous with Phoenix Cell. However, Thrawn's spies reported the trajectory of Dodonna's forces to the Grand Admiral. After exposing Kallus as Fulcrum, Thrawn used the trajectories of Massassi Group and Kallus' Fulcrum transmission to determine the location of Chopper Base and Atollon. Thrawn's [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]], using Interdictor cruisers [[Battle of Atollon|blockaded]] the [[Atollon system]], preventing the rebels from carrying out their attack on Lothal and almost instantly taking [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]]. Trapped in the system, the rebels were forced to attack blockade using a tactic which included bombing runs which would try to break the Imperial positions. Thrawn knew about this and ordered his ships to keep to their positions. He then quickly fragmented [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]] as they attempted to cripple his Star Destroyers. He then counterattacked with his TIE squadrons, destroying several rebel corvettes including the ''Sphyrna''-class corvette ''[[P2]]''. Realizing his tactic was hopeless, Commander Sato ordered the fleet to charge the blockade, and the Star Destroyers took out a second Nebulon-B frigate and the [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunship]] [[Orion (starship)|''Orion'']] almost instantly.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

[[File:Zero_Hour_thumb.png|thumb|right|350px|The ''Ghost'' and three ''Braha'tok''-class gunships during the Battle of Atollon.]]
Bridger and Chopper managed to escape on the [[Kom'rk-class fighter/transport|Gauntlet fighter]] [[Nightbrother (Gauntlet fighter)|''Nightbrother'']] after Commander Sato sacrificed his life to destroy an Imperial [[interdictor]] vessel that was blocking hyperspace jumps. Ezra appealed to Mon Mothma for help, but the rebel leader was unwilling to expose the Alliance's true strength to Thrawn. Mothma agreed that Senator Organa was right when he said that the rebels were not yet ready to enter into open warfare with the Empire. With the Rebel Alliance unwilling to help, Ezra solicited the help of Sabine's [[Clan Wren]], who destroyed Thrawn's second interdictor cruiser.<ref name="Zero Hour" />

As a result, the few surviving rebel forces, under Captain Syndulla and General Dodonna, emerged from the surface taking heavy losses along the way and were able to flee to [[Yavin 4]]. Only a few ships, including the ''Ghost'', the [[CR90 corvette]] [[Liberator (CR90 corvette)|''Liberator'']], two ''Sphyrna''-class corvettes ''[[P1]]'' and ''[[P3]]'' and an assortment of [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]s and Y-wings got out.<ref name="Zero Hour" /> To Mothma and other members of the Alliance, the defeat at Atollon was proof that their rebellion was not yet ready for open war. All the same, other actors across the galaxy believed such a conflict had already begun.<ref name="Name of Rebellion" /> As thanks for Clan Wren's aid, rebel forces<ref name="Zero Hour" />&mdash;specifically Jarrus, Bridger, and C1-10P&mdash;joined Sabine Wren and her Mandalorian Clan in the [[Mandalorian Civil War (Imperial Era)|Mandalorian Civil War]].<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Heroes of Mandalore]]''</ref>

====Building the Alliance====
{{Quote|This is a time of difficult choices. Sometimes impossible ones.|Mon Mothma|<ref name="Name of Rebellion" />}}
[[File:Yavin_4_Temple.png|thumb|left|250px|The Alliance headquarters on Yavin 4]]
By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the Alliance became a full-fledged resistance movement headed by Organa and Mothma,<ref name="Alliance DB" /> with the latter bearing the title of [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chancellor]].<ref name="Skywalker Strikes IV" /> Now in possession of a respectable starfleet that included [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s, BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bombers, A-wing starfighters, and large ships that included [[MC80 Star Cruiser]]s, at least one [[MC75 Star Cruiser]], EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate, and various [[corvette]]s, gunships, and [[transport]]s, the Alliance was finally able to fight the Empire on better terms.<ref name="Alliance DB" /> At the time, the Alliance was headquartered on the lush moon of Yavin 4, in an ancient stone structure known as the [[Great Temple]].<ref name="Episode IV" />

Leia, the surrogate daughter of Organa and crown Princess of Alderaan, succeeded her adopted father in the Imperial Senate and was also a secret member of the Alliance.<ref name="Episode IV" /> Prominent Alliance war leaders of the time included General Dodonna and [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]].<ref name="Leia 1">[[Princess Leia 1|''Princess Leia'' 1]]</ref> Some of the Empire's [[Joint Chiefs of the Imperial Military|Joint Chiefs]], like General [[Cassio Tagge]], realized the full threat of what they called "the Rebellion." Others, like Admiral [[Conan Antonio Motti]], believed in the infallibility of the Empire's might.<ref name="Episode IV" /> The Imperials, in an attempt to avoid dissent and sympathy for the Rebellion amongst loyal Imperial citizens, ensured that, through the use of propaganda, they did not know how large of a threat the Alliance truly was, assuring them that it was merely an unorganized and vengeful terrorist organization.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

====Return to Lothal====
{{Quote|Lothal looks like it's dying.''"<br />"''Your home isn't exactly the place you remember, kid. Better prepare yourself.|Ezra Bridger and Cikatro Vizago|<ref name="The Occupation">{{Rebels|The Occupation}}</ref>}}
[[File:Attack on Lothal.png|thumb|right|250px|The rebellion sent Green Squadron to Lothal hoping to destroy Thrawn's TIE Defender factory, through they would fail.]]
After their attempt to attack Lothal ended with the loss at Atollon, the Spectres returned to Lothal to help the Lothal resistance group and Azadi. They got information of Thrawn's newly upgraded TIE/D Defender. Bridger and Wren [[Theft of the TIE Defender Elite|stole]] the prototype but didn't get far thanks to its kill switch and had to escape with the [[hyperdrive]]. They sent Syndulla back to Alliance High Command to call for an attack on the Imperial Armory Complex of which they accepted. Syndulla got promoted to General and returned with a [[Squadron]] of [[X-wing starfighter]]s and Y-wants piloted by Iron Squadron’s leader and the late Commander Sato’s nephew Captain [[Mart Mattin]], [[Cleat]], [[Duke (pilot)|Duke]], [[Secon Daree]], and many others but Thrawn was there and had a trap set for them. One by one they fell, and Syndulla, Mattin, and Daree were the only confirmed survivors. Thrawn sent his assassin [[Rukh]] to find them, and he quickly captured Daree and Syndulla.<ref name="Rebel Assault">{{Rebels|Rebel Assault}}</ref>

[[File:KananStopsHera-JediNight.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Desperate to destroy the Specters, Governor Pryce would destroy the Empire's fuel depo on Lothal, inadvertently accomplishing the rebel's mission for them, though she would kill Kanan Jarrus.]]
Shortly after, Bridger, Wren, and Jarrus went to save Syndulla. Jarrus broke into Governor Arihnda Pryce's office and rescued Hera whilst Bridger and Wren, disguised as [[TIE pilot]]s, went to find them a way out. Jarrus and Syndulla escaped on a glider to the [[Lothal City fuel depot]] where they were met by Bridger and Wren in a [[LAAT/le patrol gunship|patrol transport]]. Pryce then arrived on an [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] which fired upon the fuel pod. Jarrus sacrificed himself to save the others, and the fuel depot was lost, halting the production of the TIE/D Defender on Lothal.<ref name="Jedi Knight">{{Rebels|Jedi Knight}}</ref>

====Victory at Scarif====
{{Main|Battle of Scarif}}
{{Quote|They've no idea we're coming. They've no reason to expect us. If we can make it to the ground, we'll take the next chance, and the next, on and on until we win, or the chances are spent.|Sergeant Jyn Erso|audio=Jyn-TakeTheNextChance.flac|<ref name="Rogue One: A Star Wars Story">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>}}
After mainly relying on surgical operations in the pre-open war period,<ref name="UVG" /> the Alliance's first major battle with the Empire was on the tropical planet of [[Scarif]], in which the Alliance scored its first large-scale victory in the War. Prior to the battle, the Alliance received stolen intel describing a secret Imperial weapon being constructed, which connected to recent [[Ghorman Massacre|events]] on the planet [[Ghorman]] and the ongoing Imperial crackdown on the moon [[Jedha]]. The information also suggested the renowned scientist and engineer [[Doctor]] [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]] was affiliated with the weapon.<ref name="JKE">{{Andor|Jedha, Kyber, Erso}}</ref>

[[File:Rebel_Fleet_above_Scarif.jpg|thumb|right|265x265px|The Alliance Fleet above Scarif]]
Soon after, Alliance Intelligence intercepted information confirming Erso was building an Imperial superweapon capable of destroying entire planets. Alliance leadership sent Galen's daughter, Sergeant [[Jyn Erso]], as well as Captain [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] to learn more about her father's involvement. Their subsequent efforts saw the Alliance discover the existence of the Death Star, as well as a fatal flaw in the design placed there by Galen Erso. During the Battle, the Alliance managed to steal the [[Death Star plans|blueprints]] of the Death Star, which had just been completed after nearly two decades of work, from the Imperial satellite base on Scarif. The plans to the Death Star were then transported to Princess Leia on the ''[[Tantive IV]]''.<ref name="Rogue One: A Star Wars Story" /> Meanwhile, Erso's declaration of "[[rebellions are built on hope]]" before the battle was recorded by journalist [[Corwi Selgrothe]] and would be used as a rallying cry for the Alliance in propaganda.<ref name="Heroes of the Rebellion">{{StoryCite|story=Heroes of the Rebellion|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>

====Victory at Yavin====
{{Main|Battle of Yavin}}
{{Quote|Thanks to your courage, we have delivered a telling blow to the Empire with the destruction of their Death Star. But our own casualties were not small.|Leia Organa|<ref name="Leia 1" />}}
Following the Battle of Scarif, Princess Leia's ship fell under Imperial attack over Tatooine, and she was captured by Darth Vader, which prevented her from bringing the blueprints to her father back on Alderaan. She still managed to entrust the plans to the [[droid]]s [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]], who escaped the ship in an escape pod and landed on Tatooine below. After Grand Moff Tarkin ordered the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of Alderaan with the Death Star's [[superlaser]], Leia was rescued from the battle station's jail by the crew of the [[Corellian]] [[YT-1300 light freighter|YT-1300f light freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', who returned her the two droids. The group&mdash;except for Kenobi, who gave his life battling Darth Vader&mdash;escaped the Death Star aboard the ''Falcon'' and headed for the Alliance headquarters on Yavin 4, where the plans of Empire's superweapon were analyzed.<ref name="Episode IV" />

[[File:Trench_Run_promo.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Alliance launches its attack run on the Death Star.]]
Having traced the fleeing rebels, the Empire discovered the location of the Alliance base, and the Death Star closed into Yavin 4. The Alliance then launched its [[Battle of Yavin|assault]] against the battle station, with Princess Leia monitoring in the command center. Leia’s older twin brother Jedi Lieutenant [[Luke Skywalker]] joined the mission in his own way, piloting an X-wing under the call sign of [[Red Five]]. However, the assault would go bad for the rebels as the stationary defenses and the TIE Fighter defenders would shot down nearly the entire strike force. As the Empire readied to destroy Yavin 4 and the Alliance with a single blow of its superlaser, Captain [[Han Solo]] unexpectedly saved Skywalker by blasting a group of Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE/ln]] [[starfighter]]s that were giving chase. Skywalker managed to fire a [[proton torpedo]] into the Death Star's [[thermal exhaust port]], destroying the battle station by exploiting its only weakness and killed Governor Tarkin and many of the Empire’s best forces at his hand.<ref name="Episode IV" />

===Post-Yavin advancements===
====Aftermath of victory====
[[File:Medals.png|thumb|right|250px|Luke Skywalker and Han Solo were honored by Princess Leia Organa for saving the Alliance in the Battle of Yavin.]]
In the shadow of Yavin, the Alliance had won its first major tactical victory, but at an enormous cost in lives. In addition to the Alliance pilots who died during the assault, at least millions of [[Alderaanian]]s had perished with their planet, Bail Organa among them. After an [[Royal Award Ceremony|award ceremony]] during which Princess Leia presented the heroes of the day with [[Medal of Bravery|medals of Bravery]], General Dodonna made it his priority to have Yavin 4 [[Evacuation of Yavin 4|evacuated]]. Now that the Empire knew their location, they had to find a new base of operations as soon as possible.<ref name="Leia 1" /> Darth Vader became obsessed with finding the young rebel who destroyed the Death Star and killed Governor Tarkin and most of the Empire’s best forces right under his nose. He recognized it as his old saber and began his hunt to find the origins of Luke.<ref name="Star Wars (2015) 3" />

====Mid Rim Retreat====
{{Main|Mid Rim Retreat}}
{{Quote|Talrezan Four. Hope Station. Unroola Dawn. All lost while we were escorting the fleet out of the Mid Rim. General Amrashad is dead.|Micha Evon lists losses the Alliance has suffered|audio=Amrashad.flac|<ref name="BattlefrontTC" />}}
The [[Mid Rim Retreat]] was the Empire pushing the Alliance back from the Mid Rim following their former advancement. Companies such as Twilight Company, [[Sixty-Eighth Infantry|Fish Eye Company]], [[Bitter Pill Company]], and the [[Twenty-First]] were either defending, distracting or rescuing. It was part of the Alliance's withdrawal from the Mid Rim.<ref name="BattlefrontTC" />

Twilight Company launched a campaign on [[Haidoral Prime]] as a part of a distraction for the Alliance Fleet to withdraw from the Mid Rim. During the campaign, [[Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] and his squad extracted the defecting Governor [[Everi Chalis]]. She offered herself as a necessary asset to the Alliance, as she knew a lot about the inner-workings of the "Imperial war machine." [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]] took an interest in this, but the soldiers weren't having it. One of them, [[Corbo]], took a knife to her holding cell, because he was still grief-stricken after one of her laws had his pet [[Felinx]] killed. They then suffered a run-in with an Imperial patrol. But in the [[Coyerti campaign]], Chalis gave them valuable intelligence on a distillery of which Namir and his squad destroyed. Twilight Company then suffered three more attacks in the [[Metatessu sector]] and realized the CR90 corvette ''[[Thunderstrike]]''{{'s}} hyperdrive was leaving a trail. Chalis arranged a [[raid on an Imperial heavy freighter]] for them to get supplies. Her plan worked flawlessly, but with the loss of two sergeants, [[Fektrin]] and [[Maximian Ajax]].<ref name="BattlefrontTC" />

Meanwhile, the Alliance was taking heavy losses during their withdrawal from the Mid Rim. Whilst the Twenty-First battled on [[Bestine IV|Bestine]] and Bitter Pill Company lost their transport on a trash heap of a planet, the Alliance lost [[Unroola Dawn]], [[Talrezan Four]], and [[Hope Station]], General [[Amrashad]] was killed.<ref name="BattlefrontTC" />

Chalis and Captain Evon then got an invite to [[Echo Base]] and they took Namir, [[Roja]], and [[Beak (individual)|Beak]] with them. Meanwhile, the rest of Twilight Company went to the [[Elochar sector]] where a flotilla, including the [[Sixmoon (starship)|Sixmoon]], was stationed. Chalis, Evon and the other three got to Echo Base on [[Hoth]], where they met up with General [[Philap Bygar]], who took them to a strategy conference. The other three did jobs around the Base in the meantime and Namir got into a fight with an [[Rebel Alliance Special Forces]] soldier called [[Kryndal]], over the ethics of the Alliance. The strategy conference went as planned, but the alarm soon went off that the Empire had found them.<ref name="BattlefrontTC" />

===Rebounding from Hoth===
====Defeat at Hoth====
{{Main|Battle of Hoth}}
{{Quote|Your Highness, we must take this last transport. It's our only hope.''"<br />"''Send all troops in sector twelve to the south slope to protect the fighters!|C-3PO and Princess Leia Organa|<ref name="Episode V" />}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> [[XJ9-CS14|one]] of the many Imperial [[probe droid]]s searching the galaxy ended up discovering the Rebels' presence. [[Death Squadron]], Darth Vader's personal armada, headed for Hoth with a contingent of ground troops, ready to crush the rebellion once and for all. However, due to a mistake by Admiral [[Kendal Ozzel]], the Alliance forces were warned of the Empire's arrival. The Rebels had time to activate the [[deflector shield]] protecting Echo Base, rendering orbital bombardment impossible. As the Alliance prepared for evacuation, Imperial troops disembarked on the surface of Hoth, beyond the range of the energy shields. Once there, several legions of [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtroopers]] advanced on Echo Base. A squadron of All Terrain Armored Transports under the command of General [[Maximilian Veers]] was tasked with destroying the massive generators powering the Rebel base's shield. [[Perimeter Outpost Delta]] fell first with Sergeant Hazram Namir, Roja, and Beak being forced to retreat back to the main base to find Captain Micha Evon and Everi Chalis.<ref name="Episode V" />

[[File:Detach-Cable.png|thumb|left|200px|Rebel snowspeeders engage the AT-ATs of Blizzard Force during the Battle of Hoth.]]
Luke, who was promoted to Commander by Mothma, led [[Rogue Squadron]] of snowspeeders [[Battle of Hoth|against]] General Veers' armored transports. By using a harpoon gun and tow-cable, the Rebels managed to bring down one of the transports, but Skywalker's snowspeeder was shot down. Unlike his gunner, [[Dak Ralter]], the Rogue commander survived the accident. With the help of his [[lightsaber]] and a grenade, he still managed to destroy another walker. Despite the Rebels' best efforts to delay Veers' armored transports, the Imperial general successfully blew up the protective shield generator, allowing Darth Vader and his men to enter Echo Base. While the Rebel command personnel and material was loaded into transports and whisked away just in time, the unprepared Alliance soldiers engaged the Imperials in conventional warfare. Namir and his two men got back to the main base to find Chalis with and Captain Evon, who was unconscious. They then encountered Darth Vader who killed Captain Evon, Roja, and Beak. Namir and Chalis escaped on a shuttle with Chalis' clearance codes.<ref name="BattlefrontTC" />

Unable to reach a transport, Princess Leia Organa, Han Solo, Chewbacca, and the droid C-3PO took their flight in the ''Millennium Falcon''. The Alliance delayed the Imperial troops long enough to get its most important staff off the planet, and its ground-based [[ion cannon]] temporarily disabled all ships of Death Squadron, preventing them from attacking the escaping transports. All in all, the Empire still won a major victory on Hoth, as the Alliance was routed from its base once again and barely escaped Death Squadron's blockade.<ref name="Episode V" /> The Imperial victory left the Alliance scattered and General Philap Bygar was killed during or after the battle when running from the Imperials.<ref name="BattlefrontTC" />

====Rebel Alliance on the run====
Organa, Solo, Chewbacca, and C-3PO were on the run on the ''Millennium Falcon''. They evaded many [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] before escaping the clutches of Captain [[Lorth Needa]]. They then traveled to [[Cloud City]] on the gas giant [[Bespin]] to meet with Solo's old friend [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]. Calrissian had the ''Millennium Falcon'' repaired but when the time was right, he betrayed them, handing them over to Darth Vader, who wanted to set a trap for Luke Skywalker. He had Solo frozen in [[carbonite]] as Skywalker came to Cloud City with R2-D2. Vader then confronted Skywalker, and after defeating him in a duel, revealed that he was his father. Meanwhile, Calrissian had his security turn on the Empire and Cloud City descended into chaos.<ref name="Episode V" />

Skywalker ran from Vader and was rescued by the two as they escaped Cloud City with Chewbacca, C-3PO, and R2-D2. Solo was taken to be [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] by Boba Fett, who had initially tracked them to Bespin.<ref name="Episode V" /> The [[Second Division]] of the Rebel Fleet came to be based off a [[Temporary rebel cell base|temporary base]] in the Outer Rim on [[Panisia]].<ref name="Crimson Reign 2">[[Crimson Reign 2|''Crimson Reign'' 2]]</ref> Although the Alliance had demonstrated strength on occasions, especially in destroying the Death Star, many still strongly doubted the rebels' ability to win the war. The thief [[Kay Vess]] and her colleague [[ND-5]], a [[BX-series droid commando]], were examples. However, others argued the opposite. The Imperial Governor of the moon [[Toshara]], [[Thorden]], strongly believed that the rebels would triumph over the Empire, leading him to prepare to create his own government on Toshara in the chaos that would follow the shift in power in the galaxy.<ref>[[Star Wars Outlaws]]</ref>

====Operation Ringbreaker====
{{Main|Operation Ringbreaker}}
{{Quote|Operation Ringbreaker has been vetted by Alliance Intelligence. Its goal is to shut down the naval shipyards at Kuat.|Admiral Gial Ackbar in a report to Mon Mothma|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
Shortly following the Battle of Hoth, the Sixty-First had found itself beheaded with their captain Micha Evon killed on Hoth and the rest of the officers [[Infiltration of the Thunderstrike|killed]] in the Elochar sector. They were stranded on [[Ankhural]] with vital repairs being done to the ''Thunderstrike'', and they needed a purpose, so Chalis and Namir (who took charge) initiated [[Operation Ringbreaker]], which sought to force Imperial redeployments from the shipbuilding world of [[Kuat]] by assaulting worlds such as [[Obumubo]], [[Naator]], [[Xagobah]], and the asteroid mines of the [[Kuliquo belt]], thus allowing the Alliance to assault its ultimate goal. This was led by the Alliance's Sixty-First Mobile Infantry, commonly known as Twilight Company. One of the final planets in the chain before the attack on Kuat itself was the mining world of [[Sullust]]. The Twilight Company [[Siege of Inyusu Tor|attacked]] the [[Inyusu Tor]] mineral processing facility. The facility was quickly overtaken, and victory seemed sure until Twilight Company's ship, the ''Thunderstrike'', was shot down and damaged beyond repair while attempting to pick up the division.<ref name="BattlefrontTC" />

====Liberating Sullust====
{{Main|Siege of Inyusu Tor}}
{{Quote|As I recall, Twilight Company already had the situation well in hand.|Caluan Ematt|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}

In the following days, an interim government would be installed in Pinyumb, while heavy losses sustained by the Sixty-First would ultimately cancel Operation Ringbreaker, allowing the company to act as a temporary police force for the Sullustan people. Their ''Braha'tok''-class gunship the ''[[Apailana's Promise]]'' had barely survived after returning to help them against the ''Herald''. Subsequent pirate broadcasts and intercepted Imperial signals indicated further insurrections across the planet, delaying the inevitable Imperial counterattack.<ref name="BattlefrontTC" />

====The Second Death Star====
{{Quote|"''Operation Yellow Moon.''"<br />"''You see? We even have a name.|Leia Organa and General Carlist Rieekan|<ref name="MT">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref>}}
Rebounding from their losses at Hoth after [[Operation Starlight]], the Rebel Alliance formally revealed its survival by [[Unity Day attack|attacking]] the [[Empire Day|Imperial Unity Day]] parade on [[Bar'leth]], which was being broadcast to the entire galaxy. The victory was therefore incredibly public and showcased that the Rebels took care to not hurt civilians.<ref name="Star Wars 26">[[Star Wars (2020) 26|''Star Wars'' (2020) 26]]</ref> After Rebel Command [[Mission to reveal the Death Star II|learned]] of the existence of a [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] from Skywalker,<ref name="Star Wars 28">[[Star Wars (2020) 28|''Star Wars'' (2020) 28]]</ref> the [[Bothan Spynet]] began to dig for more information. Emperor Palpatine found out about this and sought to set a trap for the Alliance. He let more information be fed more to the [[Bothan]]s, with many of them dying in order to get it, and hid the fact that the Death Star II was operational. The new information revealed the location of the Death Star II, [[Endor]], the shield protecting it and the shafts within it big enough for starfighters to pass through to the core.<ref name="MT" />

When Alliance High Command got this information, they launched [[Operation Yellow Moon]] in order to distract Imperial forces across the galaxy. This was led by Princess Organa and her strike team.<ref name="MT" /> Meanwhile, the rebels on the MC80 Star Cruiser ''[[Invincible Faith]]'' had intercepted a message from [[Moff]] [[Derrek Raythe]], which would reveal Emperor Palpatine's trap if they decrypted it in time, but they also captured Commander [[Iden Versio]] of the Empire's [[Inferno Squad]], who let it happen so she could get rid of the message. She broke free, forced her way through the ship and deleted the message making sure no-one in the Alliance could know about the trap. Versio escaped with the help of the other members of Inferno Squad.<ref name="BF II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

====Triumph at Endor====
{{Main|Battle of Endor}}
{{Quote|Admiral Ackbar is right. The Death Star isn't our only enemy&mdash;time is, as well. Our attack must come before the battle station is operational&mdash;or all will be lost.|Chancellor Mon Mothma|<ref name="MT" />}}
In 4 ABY,<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> the Alliance to Restore the Republic was determined to deal a fatal blow to Emperor Palpatine's tyranny. The Alliance Fleet rendezvoused near Sullust to plan an attack. Thanks to its Bothan spies and learned Palpatine was overseeing the final construction stages of the new battle station.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

[[File:Battle_over_Endor.png|thumb|right|250px|The Death Star II in orbit over Endor during the Battle of Endor.]]
It was decided that a rebel strike team led by Skywalker, Organa, Solo, and Chewbacca would land on Endor in a stolen [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]], with the mission to deactivate the Death Star's energy defense shield. Little did they know that they fell into a trap. When they reached the shield's bunker, the Imperial Army was waiting for them. In orbit of Endor, the Alliance Fleet was ambushed by a large Imperial armada led by the [[Super Star Destroyer]] ''[[Executor]]'' that emerged from hyperspace. Unable to attack the Death Star II until its shield was destroyed, the Rebel Fleet focused its attack on the Star Destroyers. Although its starfighters were able to maneuver easily around the Death Star II, they were still vulnerable to the battle station's superlaser, which destroyed the ''[[Liberty]]'' and the ''[[Nautilian]]''.<ref name="Episode VI" />

On Endor, the rebel strike team ended up allying with the native [[Ewok]]s, who used their knowledge of their homeworld's forests to take the Imperial army by surprise. The strike team was finally able to destroy the shield generator after a lengthy ground battle, allowing its starfleet to attack the Empire's dreaded superweapon. General Calrissian flew the ''Millennium Falcon'' into the Death Star with Antilles, [[Norra Wexley]], [[Keir Santage]], [[Jake Farrell]], and another A-wing pilot. They flew through the shafts and were pursued by several TIEs, which gunned down Keir's X-wing starfighter. Calrissian then ordered the group to split up, and Farrell, Wexley and the other A-wing pilot flew down another shaft to lure TIEs back out. Calrissian and Antilles then made it to the reactor core and destroyed the battle station's power plant before retreating back out as the Death Star began to detonate. During an attack on the ''Executor'', Green Leader Commander [[Arvel Crynyd]]'s A-wing was crippled and crashed into the bridge, killing Admiral [[Firmus Piett]] and his staff. Having already taken considerable damage from the Alliance's starfighters, the ''Executor'' failed and drifted into the Death Star's gravity well, detonating on impact.<ref name="Episode VI" />

Meanwhile, aboard the station, a blow of an entirely different kind was struck against the Empire. A captive Luke Skywalker, now a JediKnight in his own right, appealed to Vader's paternal love, convincing him to turn on his Master. Vader&mdash;once again Anakin Skywalker&mdash;seized the Imperial Head of State and hurled him down a shaft to his death, but at the cost of his own life. The Death Star II soon exploded from the damages to its core. With the loss of Emperor Palpatine and his apprentice, the destruction of the Death Star II, and significant losses inflicted on the Imperial Fleet, the Imperials were forced to withdraw. The Empire suffered a decisive defeat, leaving the Rebels to celebrate in victory.<ref name="Episode VI" />

===Legacy===
One year after the destruction of the second Death Star,<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> Republic and Imperial forces fought a [[Battle of Jakku|major battle]] on the [[desert]] world of [[Jakku]], which saw a Republic victory and the end of the Galactic Civil War.<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]'': Battle of Jakku DLC''<nowiki/>''</ref> The Alliance also dispatched [[shock trooper]]s to eliminate former Imperials that became [[warlord]]s. The [[Rebel Alliance starbird|symbol]] of the Alliance to Restore the Republic was used in the insignia of the New Republic<ref name="Aftermath" /> and later became the emblem of the [[Resistance]], a military force founded by Rebel veteran Leia and made up of several [[Alliance officer]]s, such as Ackbar and [[Caluan Ematt]], to combat the [[First Order]], the successor of the Empire.<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> During the [[Bail Organa statue-dedication ceremony]]<ref name="Bloodline" /> in [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Bloodline date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Bloodline'' to [[28 ABY]]</ref> speeches were delivered by Senators [[Tai-Lin Garr]] and Organa that reminded the gathered audience of the sacrifices made by the Rebel Alliance, which were further reinforced by an [[air show]] performed by Republic X-wings.<ref name="Bloodline" />

When her holocron was later discovered by Luke Skywalker and Leia Organa, they remarked that though the Archivist was incorrect regarding Palpatine being the Alliance's founder, he certainly did use it to his advantage.<ref name="Hidden Empire 2">[[Hidden Empire 2|''Hidden Empire'' 2]]</ref>

==Organization==
===Government===
{{Quote|Stealing food won't help us win. Killing enemies won't, either. Against might on the scale of the Empire, conventional victory is impossible&mdash;when our objectives become purely military, we've already lost the larger fight.|Captain Micha "Howl" Evon of the Sixty-First Mobile Infantry|<ref name="BattlefrontTC" />}}
{{Main|Alliance High Command|Alliance Civil Government}}
The Alliance to Restore the Republic was a military resistance movement, which was headed by the Alliance High Command and dedicated to upholding the ideals of the Old Republic,<ref name="The Weapon of a Jedi">''[[The Weapon of a Jedi: A Luke Skywalker Adventure]]''</ref> but also contained the [[Alliance Civil Government]]. The [[Alliance Cabinet]] served as political High Command, existing separate from the Alliance Military High Command, and represented both civilian and military interests.<ref name="UVG" /><ref name="Rebel Files" /> It was led by a Chief of State and had six cabinet posts: [[Minister of Education]], [[Minister of Finance (Alliance)|Minister of Finance]], [[Minister of Industry]], [[Minister of State (Alliance)|Minister of State]], [[Minister of Supply]], and [[Minister of War (Alliance)|Minister of War]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

The Alliance frequently changed its base of operations, ranging from numerous worlds across the galaxy in order to avoid detection or regroup from an attack by its nemesis, the massive Galactic Empire. A [[diplomatic corps]] who sought to maintain friendly relations and procure support from various worlds in the galaxy was maintained.<ref name="The Weapon of a Jedi" /> Despite this, the Alliance had few worlds openly declare their support for the movement, as those few that did found their worlds cordoned behind Imperial blockades.<ref name="BattlefrontTC" />

[[File:Star_Wars_44_Textless.jpg|thumb|250px|left|Members and starfighters of the Rebel Alliance military.]]
Prior to the Alliance's official founding, the various rebel cells operated their own military forces. As the cells began coordinating, joint military endeavors were often overseen by cell leaders such as Jun Sato of Phoenix Group,<ref name="TSoL" /> Cham Syndulla of the Free Ryloth movement,<ref name="Homecoming" /> and Ryder Azadi of the Lothal resistance.<ref name="An Inside Man" /> Rebel cells received information from "Fulcrum" intelligence operatives<ref name="Out of Darkness">{{Rebels|Out of Darkness}}</ref>

The formal [[Alliance Military]] was served by capable military leaders such as Admiral Gial Ackbar, and received intelligence updates through its own intelligence branch known as [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service]].<ref name="MT" /><ref name="Shattered Empire 1" /><ref name="Episode VI" /> It could also receive information through the Bothan spynet, and maintained the Alliance Fleet and [[Alliance Army]], along with the Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps.<ref name="Heir" /> [[Rebel trooper]]s across the galaxy fought for an end to galactic Imperial rule in units, such as the Sixty-First Mobile Infantry and [[Thirty-Second Infantry]], while the Alliance Special Forces was composed of elite commandos of the trooper ranks.<ref name="BattlefrontTC" /> Owing to the larger size of the Imperial war machine, the Alliance relied on [[guerrilla warfare]],<ref name="HC 23">{{HelmetCollectionCite|23|Helmets}}</ref> hit-and-run tactics, and surgical strikes<ref name="Complete Vehicles">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> instead of outright engaging in a more typical war for territory.<ref name="BattlefrontTC" />

The Alliance Military will allow anyone in their ranks including imperial defectors, who left the Galactic Empire to join the Rebel Alliance. These individuals often made this choice after witnessing the Empire’s atrocities or experiencing personal betrayals. Imperial defector would greatly benefit the Alliance, such as: [[Han Solo]], [[Crix Madine]], [[Alexsandr Kallus]], [[Wedge Antilles]], [[Derek Klivian]], [[Trey Callum]], [[Lindon Javes]], [[Iden Versio]], [[Del Meeko]], [[Jan Dodonna]], [[Bodhi Rook]], [[Jon Vander]], and many more.<ref name="Star Wars: Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref><ref name="Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref><ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref><ref name="Star Wars Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref><ref name="The Antilles Extraction">{{Rebels|The Antilles Extraction}}</ref>

The Alliance Military was controlled by a [[Commander-in-Chief]] and was divided into seven sections: [[Rebel Fleet Command]], [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Command]], [[Sector Command (Alliance)|Sector Command]], Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service, Rebel Alliance Special Forces, [[Ordnance and Supply]], and [[Support Services]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Before the Empire takes over, we already had these little groups out there&hellip; and they need a way to come together. And that's really where your Bail Organas and your Mon Mothmas come in. They're the way that you can galvanize these rebels, and you get a Rebel Alliance.|Dave Filoni, on how the Onderon rebels help set the stage for the Rebel Alliance|<ref name="Seeds of Rebellion">{{SW|url=video/sowing-the-seeds-of-rebellion|series=Clone Wars Download|text=Sowing the Seeds of Rebellion}}</ref>}}
[[Dave Filoni]], the supervising director of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated television series, stated that he and [[George Lucas]] intended the Onderon rebels, seen in the [[Onderon]] arc, to be the first of many militias that were created by the Jedi during the Clone Wars, militias that would be brought together by characters such as Bail Organa and Mon Mothma to become the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Seeds of Rebellion" />

The visual design of the Rebel Alliance in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] was modelled on the costumes of the [[wikipedia:Western (genre)|western genre]] and the [[wikipedia:United States Marine Corps|United States Marine Corps]].<ref name="The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film">''[[The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film]]''</ref>
