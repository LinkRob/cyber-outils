'''Caissons''' were huge hollowed columns that were filled with pressurized air. They underpinned the [[Ord Mynock Freeport]], keeping the platforms and upper levels stabilized. The caissons also prevented the [[City/Legends|city]] from sinking into the [[Ord Mynock/Legends|Ord Mynock's]] ever-present mud.<ref name="GTLAA">''[[Gamemaster Toolkit: Live-Action Adventures]]''</ref>
