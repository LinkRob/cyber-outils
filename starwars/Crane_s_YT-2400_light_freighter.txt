Crane sat in the [[YT-2400 light freighter]]'s [[cockpit]] with the [[Reconnaissance mission on Joetz|rescued]] [[Gigoran]] [[Youngling|child]] [[Woopas]] and spoke with [[Admiral]] [[Basil Kiucee]] via [[hologram]].<ref name="Four Wings" />
