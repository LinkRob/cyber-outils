==Events==
*The [[Citadel]] on the [[planet]] [[Lola Sayu]] is utilized to imprison the last known rogue [[Jedi]] of the [[Jedi Order]]<ref name="BMF 75" /> until [[20 BBY]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="520 BBY">{{BuildFalconCite|75|Guide to the Galaxy}} states that it was five [[Standard century|centuries]] before the [[Battle of Lola Sayu]] since the last [[Jedi]] was imprisoned within the [[Citadel]]. As the Battle of Lola Sayu takes place in 1 BFE according to ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'', which corresponds to [[20 BBY]] as established by ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', it can be deduced that it had been since approximately [[520 BBY]] since the last Jedi was held in the Citadel.</ref>
