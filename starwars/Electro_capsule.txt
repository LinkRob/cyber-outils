The '''electro capsule''' or '''suicide shocker''' was a [[Death|suicide]]-assisting [[weapon]] that could be implanted inside an individual's [[mouth]]. It consisted of a triangular [[tooth]] implant that was activated by biting down, resulting in an electric shock that killed the user.

==Description==
The suicide shocker<ref name="Chapter 11a">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> was a small triangular electric device that could be implanted and hidden in an individual's [[tooth]].<ref name="Infiltration" /> The device was activated by biting down on the tooth containing it, which released an electric charge that [[Death|killed]] the wearer.<ref name="Chapter 11" /> When not worn,<ref name="Infiltration" /> the electro-capsule<ref name="Tipping SG">{{SW|url=tipping-point-story-gallery|text="Tipping Point" Story Gallery &#124; The Bad Batch}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> could be stored in a liquid-filled canister.<ref name="Infiltration" />

It was intended to be a last-resort measure to prevent agents from having information recovered from them through interrogation, allowing them to kill themselves before they could be questioned.<ref name="CC" /> However, the device could be discovered and removed by an interrogator aware of its existence.<ref name="Infiltration" />

==History==
{{Quote|Not long after the Clone Wars ended, I encountered a handful of specialized clone assassins who operated in total secrecy. [&hellip;] They called themselves "believers" and were willing to take their own lives to protect the truth about their missions|Rex, ''The Secrets of the Clone Troopers''|<ref name="SecretsCloneTroopers">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Clone Troopers]]''</ref>}}
In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="The Bad Date">{{TBBDate|207}}</ref> [[Clone X]],<ref name="BB">{{Databank|clone-x|Clone X}}</ref> a [[Clone X trooper]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], used the device when [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] interrogated him on<ref name="CC" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]].<ref name="CC" /> During or after 18 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="TippingDate">{{TBBDate|214}}</ref> [[Captain]] [[Pearce]] killed himself<ref name="Tipping Point AD">{{TBB|Tipping Point}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> by using a suicide shocker after the [[Clone Underground]] took over his [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] over<ref name="Tipping">{{TBB|Tipping Point}}</ref> the planet<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Balmorra]].<ref name="Tipping" /> Sometime later,<ref name="S3 date">{{TBBDate|S3}}</ref> another Clone X trooper, [[CX-1]] was captured by the Clone Underground when he attempted to kill fugitive [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Senator]] [[Avi Singh]] during his [[Riyo Chuchi and Avi Singh's secret meeting|secret meeting]] on<ref name="Infiltration" /> the [[moon]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Pantora]] with Imperial Senator [[Riyo Chuchi]]. CX-1 was prevented from using his shocker when Rex had Captain [[Howzer]] remove it from his mouth while stunned.<ref name="Infiltration" />

[[File:Imp_captain_suicide_pill.png|left|thumb|270px|The Imperial Remnant Captain Tribus dies by suicide using an electro capsule.]]
Rex indirectly referenced electro capsules in<ref name="SecretsCloneTroopers" /> his<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[book]]&mdash;''[[The Secrets of the Clone Troopers]]''&mdash;which was published at some point following<ref name="SecretsCloneTroopers" /> the [[5 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Galactic Concordance|end]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Proclamation of the New Order|successor]] state, the Empire, and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. He noted that the Empire's Clone X troopers were often willing to end their own lives to keep the details of their missions secret,<ref name="SecretsCloneTroopers" /> which they sometimes did by using the devices to escape interrogation.<ref name="CC" /> On<ref name="Chapter 11" /> the moon<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Trask]], [[Captain]] [[Tribus]], who served an [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]], also used a suicide shocker<ref name="Chapter 11" /> in [[9 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|11}}</ref> when<ref name="Chapter 11" /> he<ref name="Chapter 11a" /> was being interrogated by [[Mandalorian]] [[Lady]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze]], who [[Assault on the Imperial Gozanti freighter|took over]] his [[Unidentified Gozanti-class Assault Carrier|''Gozanti''-class Assault cruiser]].<ref name="Chapter 11" />

==Behind the scenes==
The electro capsule first appeared, albeit unidentified, in "[[Chapter 11: The Heiress]],"<ref name="Chapter 11" /> the third episode in the [[The Mandalorian Season Two|second season]] of the [[Disney+]] live-action series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', which aired on [[November 13]], [[2020]].<ref name="Insider 202">{{InsiderCite|202|Star Wars: The Mandalorian Season Two Companion|''Star Wars: The Mandalorian'' Season Two Companion}}</ref> The episode's audio description identified it as the suicide shocker.<ref name="Chapter 11a" /> The episode guide of "[[Tipping Point]]," the fourteenth episode of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]''{{'s}} [[The Bad Batch Season 2|second season]] that aired on [[March 22]], [[2023]], later identified it as the electro capsule.<ref name="Tipping EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/tipping-point-episode-guide|text="Tipping Point" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}}</ref>
