The '''Eiram&ndash;E'ronoh peace treaty'''<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> was a peace treaty between the two worlds [[Eiram]] and [[E'ronoh]]. It was supposed to be signed at the [[summit on Jedha]] by the [[ambassador]]s [[Lian Cerox]] and [[Tintak]].<ref name="Battle of Jedha" />
