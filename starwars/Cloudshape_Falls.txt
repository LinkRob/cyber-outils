The '''Cloudshape Falls''' were [[water]]falls located on the [[planet]] of [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> [[Quiggold]] lived luxuriously at Cloudshape Falls on the planet from his comfy rooms, fooling the innocent and stashing credits until the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] eventually caught up with him.<ref name="Sinister Secret of the Binary Star">{{InsiderCite|234|Galactic Tales: The Crimson Corsair and the Sinister Secret of the Binary Star}}</ref> [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[Prince|heir]] to the [[House of Organa|Royal House of Alderaan]], compared their great billowing spray to a cloud that came to nest on the river.<ref name="Bloodline" /> [[Nash Windrider]], a [[Alderaanian|native]] of Alderaan, had offered [[Thane Kyrell]] to take him to the Falls one [[Standard day|day]].<ref name="Lost Stars" /> However, in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> the Galactic Empire used its [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]] to [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroy]] the entire planet before Kyrell had a chance to set foot on Alderaan.<ref name="Lost Stars" />
