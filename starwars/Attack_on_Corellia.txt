A [[Nihil]] '''attack''' occurred in [[Coronet City]] on [[Corellia]] in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> in [[230 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> as part of the [[infiltration on Corellia]]. The attack was only launched when the [[bodyguard]] [[Prybolt Garavult]] grew suspicious of the meeting his client, [[Father of Finances]] [[Nomar Tralmat]], was involved in and feared for the man's life. In reality, Tralmat was having a meeting with his Nihil allies, only for Garavult's entry to risk the public learning of his secret alliance. As such, Tralmat ordered [[Sabata Krill]] to [[Death|kill]] the bodyguard. Because Garavult was able to get a [[hologram]] message about the Nihil out to his [[boss]] and friend [[Alys Ongwa]], kicking off a series of events that made the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Supreme Coronet City Diplomat Protection]] aware of the Nihil's infiltration on Corellia. Tralmat also used the incident as a chance to fake his death. During the [[Battle of the Corellian Shipyards]], Tralmat revealed himself to be alive, only to be killed later in the battle by [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Fastidima]] when she learned he was behind Garavult's death.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
