'''Force pikes''' were a type of [[staff]]-like weapon<ref name="DB Force pike">{{Databank|force-pike|Force Pike}}</ref> with a [[Vibro-weapon|vibro-edged head]]. They also had a [[stun]] setting, and were capable of causing immense pain at their lowest setting.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> At the highest setting, the pikes could be used to cut through [[durasteel]].<ref name="Bloodline" /> The [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Red Guard]] of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] wielded [[Controller FP]]s.<ref name="RotJ" /> They were able to fire purple blasts from their tips.<ref name="Vader 28">[[Darth Vader (2020) 28|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 28]]</ref>

The [[RQ protocol droid|droid guards]] of [[Senator]]-in-exile [[Gall Trayvis]] were also equipped with these.<ref name="Vision of Hope">{{Rebels|Vision of Hope}}</ref> While on [[Daxam IV]] Senator [[Ransolm Casterfo]] used a force pike to fight an [[Amaxine warriors (New Republic Era)|Amaxine warrior]] while buying an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[helmet]] to win over [[Arliz Hadrassian]]'s favor.<ref name="Bloodline" /> The weapons could also be modified with [[magnetic clamp]]s, projecting a field around a target that the pike was pointed at to suspend them in the air and restrict their ability to move. During this time, the end of the pike and the target would glow [[orange]]. This feature notably allowed a group of red guards to subdue [[Ezra Bridger]], attempting to trap him long enough for three incoming [[stormtrooper]]s to shoot him. After avoiding multiple shots, Bridger managed to [[Telekinesis|send]] several boulders flying at them, breaking their hold.<ref name="Family&Farewell">{{Rebels|Family Reunion &ndash; and Farewell}}</ref>

A lighter and longer version of the force pike was the [[vibro-lance]], which could act as a [[spear]] in addition to being a [[melee weapon]].<ref name="Tarkin" />
