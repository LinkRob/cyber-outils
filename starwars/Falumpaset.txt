The '''falumpaset''' was a [[Diet|herbivorous]] [[species]] of [[mammal]] found on the [[planet]]s [[Naboo]] and [[Onderon]]. In the wild, the species lived in [[swamp]]s, forests, and plains in family groups.<ref name="VE" /><ref name="AA" /> They were domesticated by the [[Gungan]]s and, along with other large and hardy animals such as [[fambaa]]s, were used to transport heavy loads such as the [[Deflector shield generator|shield generators]] used in the [[Battle of Naboo]].<ref name="Databank Gungan Grand Army">{{Databank|gungan-grand-army|Gungan Grand Army}}</ref> Both the Gungans and the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] used falumpasets to pull carts.<ref name="VE" /> Falumpasets were great swimmers and their disproportionately long legs made them very maneuverable.<ref name="AA" /> Falumpasets shared their name with [[falumpaset cheese]], an ingredient in the [[Batuuan]] dish [[mashed chokeroot]].<ref name="Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref>
