'''''Doctor Aphra'' 15''' is the fifteenth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']], and is part of the ''[[Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters]]'' event. It was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]], [[Victor Olazaba]], and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 13]], [[2021]].

==Publisher's summary==
''"PARTY FAVORS" CRIMSON DAWN is back, and DOCTOR APHRA is trapped aboard their ship! What treasures lie aboard the ''VERMILLION''? And what price will Aphra have to pay to obtain them?''
