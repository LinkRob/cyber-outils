The '''B1 Battle Droids Unit Expansion''' ('''SWQ30''') is an expansion to the tabletop game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. An updated version of the [[B1 Battle Droids Unit Expansion]], it will be released on [[November 11]], [[2025]] as part of Atomic Mass Games' overhaul of the game.

==Publisher's summary==
''What B1-series Battle Droids lack in durability and combat prowess they make up for in ease of production. The sheer number of droids the Separatist Alliance can produce has allowed them to strike quickly and decisively across the galaxy with overwhelming force.''

''This ''Star WarsTM: Legion'' B1 Battle Droids expansion contains: 1 B1 Battle Droid Unit Leader, 12 B1 Battle Droids, and 2 B1 Battle Droid heavy.''

==Contents==
*15 miniatures
*5 cards
**1 Unit Card
**4 Upgrade Cards
