'''Blue Eight''' was the [[callsign]] of the eighth member of the [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]].<ref name="Rogue One" /> A renowned [[Rebel pilot]] named [[Heff Tobber]]<ref name="Saga of Rey">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Saga of Rey}}</ref> flew as Blue Eight during the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Blue Eight callsign made its first appearance in the [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]'', which was published by [[Electronic Arts]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
