'''Dai Bendu tea leaf''' was a special [[tea]] mix that could be traced back to the [[Dai Bendu]] monks.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the tea was drank on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Kijimi]] and could be sold for forty [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dai Bendu tea leaf appeared as an inventory item in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. In the game, the bottles of tea leaf could only be sold for credits, and did not have another function.<ref name="Outlaws" />
