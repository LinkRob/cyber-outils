'''Bamayar IX''' was a [[gas giant]] formed of emerald- and turquoise-[[color]]ed [[gas]] [[cloud]]s. Between [[7 BBY]] and around [[5 BBY]], the trainee [[pilot]] [[Bodhi Rook]] skillfully flew a [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class transport]] through the clouds of Bamayar IX with a number of his classmates in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Terrabe Sector Service Academy]] aboard. When escaping the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]]'s [[Destruction of Jedha City|blast]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] in [[1 BBY]], Rook vividly imagined his flight through Bamayar IX when watching the storm of the blast's shockwave.

==Description==
Bamayar IX was a [[gas giant]] located in the ninth [[orbit]]al position<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Bamayar system]],<ref name="Systems" /> alongside the [[planet]] [[Bamayar]].<ref name="Heroes of Rogue One">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Heroes of Rogue One}}</ref> It was situated in the [[Maldrood sector]] at the coordinates R-7 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> The world made up of titanic emerald- and turquoise-[[color]]ed [[gas]] [[cloud]]s that were frequently ignited by [[lightning]] storms.<ref name="Rogue One novel" />

==History==
[[File:Bodhi_Rook_Fathead.png|thumb|left|200px|Bodhi Rook (pictured) flew through the clouds of Bamayar IX during his academy days.]]
Between [[7 BBY]] and around [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] issue "[[The Heroes of Rogue One]]" establishes that [[Bodhi Rook]] flew through Bamayar IX during his [[time]] in the [[Terrabe Sector Service Academy]]. The issue also states that Rook entered the academy in [[7 BBY]] and spent twenty-four [[Standard month|months]] there. ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]'' establishes that a [[standard year]] was 365 [[Standard day|days]] long, while the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 61|sixty-first]] issue of ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' sets the length of a standard month at thirty-five days, with twenty-four months therefore being approximately 2.3 standard years. Therefore, Rook's time in the academy, including his flight through Bamayar IX, took place between 7 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and around [[5 BBY]].</ref> [[Bodhi Rook]], a [[pilot]]-in-training at the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Terrabe Sector Service Academy]], flew an old [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class transport]], which was in a poor state for flight, through the clouds of Bamayar IX<ref name="Heroes of Rogue One" /> with a number of his classmates as passengers.<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> Rook perfectly controlled the [[transport]],<ref name="Heroes of Rogue One" /> which bounced and twirled between the clouds and lightning,<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> and earned the respect of his classmates,<ref name="Heroes of Rogue One" /> who cheered him on.<ref name="Rogue One novel" />

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Rogue One Date">{{FilmDate|RO}}</ref> Rook, who had since defected from the Empire, escaped the [[moon]] of [[Jedha]] on a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|UT-60D U-wing transport]] after the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]] [[Destruction of Jedha City|fired upon]] Jedha's [[Jedha City|Holy City]]. Witnessing the colossal dust and rock storm caused by the shockwave of the Death Star's blast, Rook, aboard the U-wing, vividly imagined his [[time]] flying through Bamayar IX.<ref name="Rogue One novel" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bamayar IX first appeared through imagination in the [[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]], written by [[Alexander Freed]] and published in [[2016]].<ref name="Rogue One novel" />
