'''Dave Bullock''' was a [[Wikipedia:Animation director|director]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', a [[Wikipedia:Computer-generated imagery|CGI]]-based television series that debuted [[October 3]], [[2008]] on [[Cartoon Network]] as a continuation of the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|film with the same name]], released two months prior. He left the project after directing only one episode.
