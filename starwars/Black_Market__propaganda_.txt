'''''Black Market''''' was a [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]], encouraging its citizens to report [[Black market|unauthorized trade]]. The poster depicted a crate that outwardly states "[[Power converter]]s," but is shown to have at least four [[DL-44 heavy blaster pistol]]s and some other blaster barrels coming out. The font is in [[High Galactic]], and has on top "There's a reason it's called the [[Black market]]," with the last two words being enlarged for emphasis, and at the bottom has the following given in orange: "Shadowy dealings only benefit those who operate from the shadows: Our Enemies! Republic Law demands that you report all unauthorized trade," with the "Our Enemies" phrase being highlighted in yellow.<ref name="Propaganda" />
