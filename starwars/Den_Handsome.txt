The individual known as '''Den Handsome''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who was one of [[the galaxy]]'s most prolific liars and accomplished [[Thief|thieves]]. It is not known whether he used his real name, or if Den Handsome was actually an alias. Either way, Handsome was known to claim that he stole [[Commander]] [[Bragh]]'s [[Purge Trooper (Anoat sector)|Purge Trooper]] [[armor]], and the set that Bragh wore in combat was actually his backup set.<ref name="Uprising" />

==Behind the scenes==
Handsome was created by [[Kabam]] for ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', a [[canon]] mobile video game for iOS and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] operating systems and released in [[2015]]. The player could send Handsome on crew run missions in order to collect useful items.
