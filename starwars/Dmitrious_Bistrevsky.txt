'''Dmitrious Bistrevsky''' is a Ukrainian-American actor and circus artist<ref name="Instagram" /> who portrayed a [[Quarren Trawler]] in the [[Chapter 1: The Mandalorian|debut episode]] of the [[Disney+]] live-action TV series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.<ref name="Chapter 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> He also served as one of three actors playing [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] in the live-action series ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'', with Bistrevsky, credited as "Performance artist – Darth Vader,"<ref name="Kenobi">{{Kenobi|Part III}}</ref> doubling for [[Hayden Christensen]] in full body shots where Vader is wearing his [[Darth Vader's armor/Legends|helmet]]; and stunt double [[Tom O'Connell]] doing the fight scenes.<ref name="Instagram2" />

==Biography==
[[File:VaderStuntDoublesandMovementCoach.png|thumb|right|200px|Darth Vader was played by stunt doubles Tom O'Connell (left) and Dmitrious Bistrevsky (right), and their movement coordinator was Olga Sokolova]]
Because of Bistrevsky being 6'7" tall, he was used to bring Vader to life in full body-shots because it looked better having an actual actor, as opposed to a CGI creation. The actor spent five months of training ten hours a day for the role. The first scene that he shot, was the street scene on [[Mapuzo]], which was also his favorite scene to film. Some scenes required switching back and forth between multiple actors. At one point, all three of Vader's actors were present at the same time.<ref name="Instagram2" />

Even though the character's voice was artificially created with [[Respeecher]],<ref name="Respeecher">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2022/09/darth-vaders-voice-emanated-from-war-torn-ukraine|text=Darth Vader's Voice Emanated From War-Torn Ukraine|author=Anthony Breznican|date=2022-09-23|work=[[Wikipedia:Vanity Fair (magazine)|''Vanity Fair'']]|archivedate=20220923151721}}</ref>  Bistrevsky still learned all of Vader's lines for the shooting. However, if he spoke with anger, Vader's helmet would shake, so a voice actor named Dorian was present during filming and voice acted the scenes on set, with Bistrevsky moving in accordance with the lines. At the same time, a movement coach/consultant named [[Olga Sokolova]] would give him instructions via hand signals. Bistrevsky used meditation to stay in Vader's state of mind on set. According to the actor, "It was dark and heavy playing the character, it tore me up from the inside."<ref name="Instagram2" />
