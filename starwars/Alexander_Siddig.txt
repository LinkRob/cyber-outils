'''Alexander Siddig''' is a [[Wikipedia:Sudanese people|Sudanese]]&ndash;[[Wikipedia:British people|British]] [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|actor]]<ref name="britishTrek" /> who voiced [[Senator]] [[Avi Singh]] in the [[Disney+]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]''{{'s}} [[The Bad Batch Season 1|first season]] episode "[[Common Ground]],"<ref name="Common Ground" /> released [[July 2]], [[2021]].<ref name="Releasedate">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-july-2021|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in July 2021}}</ref> Siddig did not reprise his role for Singh's appearance in the episode "[[Infiltration (episode)|Infiltration]],"<ref name="Infiltration">{{TBB|Infiltration (episode)}}</ref> which aired as part of the [[The Bad Batch Season 3|third season]] on [[March 13]], [[2024]],<ref name="Watch">{{SW|url=news/the-bad-batch-season-3-trailer|text=Watch the ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 3 Trailer}}</ref> with the character instead being voiced by [[Danny Jacobs (The Freemaker Adventures)|Danny Jacobs]].<ref name="Infiltration" />

Born in [[Wikipedia:Omdurman|Omdurman]], [[Wikipedia:Sudan|Sudan]]<ref name="Sudan" /> as '''Siddig El Tahir El Fadil El Siddig Abderrahman Mohammed Ahmed Abdel Karim El Mahdi''' (shortened to '''Siddig El Fadil'''),<ref name="Name">{{WebCite|url=https://www.huffpost.com/entry/alexander-siddig-discover_b_817826|text=Alexander Siddig — Discovering the Uncommon Hero|author=Rothe, E. Nina|date=2011-02-03|work=[[Wikipedia:HuffPost|HuffPost]]|archivedate=20201023051912}}</ref> Siddig is well-known for his role as Dr. [[W:c:memoryalpha:Julian Bashir|Julian Bashir]] in the television series [[W:c:memoryalpha:Star Trek: Deep Space Nine|''Star Trek: Deep Space Nine'']].<ref name="britishTrek" />
