'''Aaila''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who lived in the settlement [[Haven (Cethis)|Haven]] on the astronomical object [[Cethis]] with her [[Parent|father]], [[Mikal]], during the [[High Republic Era]]. When the residents of Haven were [[Mission to Cethis|evacuated]] by the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] after the [[blight]] affliction threatened the settlement, Aaila and Mikal escaped, but were forced to leave many of their neighbors behind. While waiting to board Republic [[transport]]s at a makeshift [[refugee]] camp, Aaila argued with her father, not wishing to leave without those they had left. [[Jedi Master]] [[Mirro Lox]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Amadeo Azzazzo]], overheard Aaila's distress and approached, learning of the situation. The two [[Jedi]] headed for Aaila and Mikal's former home and rescued their friends and neighbors.<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" />
