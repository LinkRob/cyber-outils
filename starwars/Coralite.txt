'''Coralite''' was a substance that could be grown to produce structures on<ref name="LOTJ" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Mon Cala]]. Structures made of the material functioned as a reeflike habitat for [[Ocean|sea]] creatures. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Mon Cala Jedi outpost|Jedi outpost]] on Mon Cala was grown out of coralite, which was intended to make the [[Jedi outpost|outpost]] fit with the its natural surroundings.<ref name="LOTJ" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]]&mdash;who was stationed at the [[Elphrona Outpost]]&mdash;thought about coralite and reflected he would have been happier being posted on Mon Cala due to the Outer Rim planet [[Elphrona]]'s harsh environment.<ref name="LOTJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coralite was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'', written by [[Charles Soule]]<ref name="LOTJ" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
