'''Cozler''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who joined the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[New Republic Era]]. A [[Padawan|student]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]], Cozler [[Jedi training|learned]] the ways of [[the Force]] alongside other students, including Skywalker's [[Skywalker family|nephew]] [[Ben Solo]]. During his time at the [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker|Jedi Temple]], he learned several Jedi techniques including [[Telekinesis]].<ref name="Terrifying Tales" />
