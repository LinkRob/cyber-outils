'''Clip beetles''' were a [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[species]] of [[Color|purple]]-shelled [[beetle]]. They were [[Diet|edible]] by certain species, including [[Kowakian monkey-lizard]]s and [[Sullustan]]s. According to the Sullustan [[Dil Pexton]], who found them delicious, dried clip beetles tasted like [[colo claw fish]].<ref name="The Snare" />
