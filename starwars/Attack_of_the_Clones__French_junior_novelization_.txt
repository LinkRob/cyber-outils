'''''Attack of the Clones''''' ([[Wikipedia:French language|French]]: '''''L'Attaque des clones''''') is a junior novel adaptation of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]. ''The Attack of the Clones'' was written by [[Catherine Kalengula]] and published by [[Hachette Jeunesse]] on [[June 7]], [[2017]].

==Publisher's summary==
''A long time ago, in a galaxy far, far away... . Many planets want to leave the Republic. A war is about to break out. But young Padmé is determined to protect the peace, with the help of the Jedi!''<ref name="Hachette" />
