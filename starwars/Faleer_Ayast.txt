'''Faleer Ayast''' was an individual who served as an [[advisor]] to [[Queen]] [[Cheraama]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. After Queen Cheraama's trade reforms backfired, a wave of pro-[[Galactic Empire]] sentiments arose and Ayast was concerned what an Imperial [[governor]]ship would mean for the freedoms of the [[planet]] of [[Alun]].<ref name="Unlimited Power" />
