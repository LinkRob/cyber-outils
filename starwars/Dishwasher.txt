'''Dishwasher''' was a menial job, often found in [[restaurant]]s. The [[clone commando]] [[Gregor]] worked as a dishwasher job after suffering from [[amnesia]].

==Description==
Someone who worked as a dishwasher was expected to wash dishes and might also perform other menial labor such as taking out trash.<ref name="missing" />

==History==
After the [[clone commando]] [[Gregor]] [[Amnesia|lost his memory]] and ended up on the [[planet]] [[Abafar]], he was taken in by Chef [[Borkus]] of the [[Power Sliders]] diner and given a job as a dishwasher for nothing more than room and board. He seemed to perform the job with reasonable competency, but showed no great enthusiasm for his work and gladly left it when he was informed by [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]] that he had been a clone commando.<ref name="missing" />

==Behind the scenes==
A dishwasher first appeared in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' in the episode "[[Missing in Action]]."<ref name="missing" />
