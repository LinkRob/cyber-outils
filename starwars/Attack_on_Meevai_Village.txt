A '''battle''' took place in [[Meevai Village]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Keelia]]. [[Lord]] [[Izuma]] led a group of [[Izuma's raiders|raiders]] to take the [[Unidentified village chief (Meevai Village)|chief]] of Meevai Village as collateral for pillaging the village. His granddaughter [[Haru]] decided to take his place and go with Izuma. However, her sister [[Saku]] insisted on fighting the raiders but was captured. Before Izuma could execute her, the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[F]] stopped Izuma and defeated him when he tried to take Haru as a captive.<ref name="The Village Bride" />
