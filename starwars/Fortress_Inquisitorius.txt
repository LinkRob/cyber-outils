'''Fortress Inquisitorius''' was an [[Water|underwater]] stronghold located on the [[ocean]] [[moon]] [[Nur]] in the [[Mustafar system]]. The stronghold served as the central seat of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitor Program]] following their expulsion from their previous [[Inquisitorius Headquarters]] on [[Coruscant]] due to an [[Coruscant Incident|incident]] that occurred in [[14 BBY]]. The existence of the fortress was hidden from the Empire at large, although rumors swirled of a "dark site" where [[Force-sensitive|Force-users]] from across [[the galaxy]] were extradited by Imperial forces for interrogation, torture, and study. The bodies of [[Jedi]] killed during the [[Great Jedi Purge]], were kept in a [[Jedi tomb|tomb]] within the lower levels of the bastion, while a significant garrison of [[stormtrooper]]s, [[Purge Trooper]]s, and [[Imperial officer]]s worked alongside the Inquisitors and [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to further their mission of hunting Force sensitives.

In [[14 BBY]], during the Great Jedi Purge, a [[Mission to Nur|mission]] involving the [[Jedi]] [[Cal Kestis]] and [[Cere Junda]] resulted in significant damage to the facility, as well as the execution of the [[Second Sister]] by Darth Vader. In [[9 BBY]], the Jedi [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and members of the [[Hidden Path]] resistance group also [[Rescue of Princess Leia (9 BBY)|infiltrated]] the Fortress in a successful effort to rescue [[Alderaanian]] [[princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who had been kidnapped by the [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister]] [[Battle on Mapuzo|on Mapuzo]]. Despite extensive efforts by the [[Imperial Military|Imperial military]] to secure the facility, their forces were unable to prevent the Jedi and their associates involved from invading the fortress or escaping the planet.

==Description==
===Layout===
{{Quote|They broke them, beat them down, and turned them to the dark side.|Cal Kestis, after [[BD-1]] inspects the Interrogation Chamber|<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />}}
The Fortress Inquisitorius was located on the [[ocean]] [[moon]] [[Nur]] which was orbiting the [[planet]] [[Jestefad]] in the [[Mustafar system]].<ref name="Devoted" /> It was built to complement [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]'s personal residence [[Fortress Vader]]<ref name="DoR:TVG" /> on Jestefad's smaller twin world<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'', 2016 edition]]</ref> [[Mustafar]], and was constructed using black stones from Mustafar.<ref name="DoR:TVG">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> Though an imposing blade-like spire rose from Nur's corrosive waters, much of the Fortress was under the waves and embedded deep into the moon's surface. The entirety of the facility was heavily guarded by a garrison of stormtroopers and bristled with a number of [[turbolaser]]s.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

Located at the top of the citadel was a central hangar bay that stretched around the tower and hosted [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]]s, [[V-82 dropship]]s, and the Inquisitors' ships, such as the [[Scythe (Phi-class)|''Scythe'']]. [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s were docked on racks on the ceiling of the hangar, similar to that of a [[Star Destroyer]]. At least four entrance doors were located on each side of the hangar. Any ships entering went through thorough scans whenever arriving or departing.<ref name="DoR:TVG" /> A deployment system and launch bay was also used to deploy [[Viper probe droid]]s in [[hyperdrive pod]]s to scour the galaxy for [[Jedi]].<ref name="Part IV" /> Near the surface [[Water|underwater]] was a grand hall that stretched from the hangar to the entrance into the inner sections of the fortress. Access to the inner fortress was granted via passing a security checkpoint<ref name="Part III" /> which featured [[security scanner]]s that detected any [[weapon]]s, [[bomb]]s, or other threats to the fortress.<ref name="DoR:TVG" />

[[File:Inquisitorius_-_OWK.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Inquisitors in a meeting room.]]
The Office of the Inquisitorius,<ref name="DoR:TVG" /> also known as the board room,<ref name="Dark Times">{{HomeVideoCite|set=Obi-Wan Kenobi: The Complete Series|featurette=The Dark Times: Villains}}</ref> was a conference room not unlike [[Death Star conference room|those]] of the later [[Death Star]], with the head seat being reserved for [[The Grand Inquisitor]]. It was a large room with a window that could see out underwater. It also had a strategy table in the middle of the room and was guarded by two [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Part III" /> There was also a separate [[Fortress Inquisitorius audience chamber|audience chamber]].<ref name="Devoted" />

The fortress' dojos were the training rooms of the Inquisitorius, where Inquisitors and [[Purge Trooper]]s honed their lethal skills. The fear-based, dark, and abusive nature of this training was diametrically opposed to that of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. Deep within the [[Prison Block]], captured [[Jedi]] and other Force-sensitives were kept to experience the next level of interrogation, [[torture]], and experimentation. The design of the prisoners' holding cells and interrogation areas were specifically intended to break the spirits of those interned there. The central interrogation chamber of the Fortress was a torture room where captured Jedi and Force-users underwent the dark procedures necessary to transform them into Inquisitors. Under direct orders from [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], this facility was specifically designed to psychologically and physically drain a prisoner's will, forcing them to acquiesce to the indoctrination of the Inquisitors.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

===Security systems===
{{Quote|Never seen anything like this. Advanced, even for the Empire.|Cal Kestis|<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />}}
[[File:FortressInquisitoriusJediMausoleum-OWK.png|thumb|right|300px|Obi-Wan Kenobi discovers the Jedi corpses housed in the fortress.]]The security systems within the Fortress were a complex network controlling bridges, wings, gates and portals. The fortress was protected by shields controlled by a multi-shield system that could be compromised or disabled. This information was contradicted by [[Hidden Path]] member [[Kawlan Roken]], however, who asserted the stronghold was unshielded as the Inquisitors believed no one would be foolish to attack it directly.<ref name="Part IV" /> A number of other security features, including laser gates and ray shields, were employed in the detainment wing of the Fortress; the former were modified extensively at the instruction of the [[Grand Inquisitor]] following a successful prisoner escape.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

The entire fortress was designed with the ability to be purposely compromised or flooded to prevent the escape of prisoners. However, it was also possible for areas of the Fortress to become flooded unintentionally if the shield system was compromised, rendering it unable to protect the Fortress from hydrostatic pressure. The Fortress may be vulnerable to flooding if airlocks are open while the shield system is disabled. Sectioned wings of the Fortress are designed for maximum security and stability in extreme undersea conditions. A complex system of partitions provides the ability to flood an entire wing or specific sector in response to any escape attempt by a high-value detainee. Structurally reinforced shielding is able to withstand an external breach or change in pressure at any depth.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==History==
===Early history===
[[File:FortressInquisitoriusConstruction-Devoted.png|thumb|right|250px|The Fortress Inquisitorius during its construction]]
Fortress Inquisitorius was a facility made operational by the early [[Imperial Era]],<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> with some construction happening<ref name="Devoted" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Devoted Date" /> At that point,<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> it was not yet the main base of the Inquisitorius, who instead operated from a [[Inquisitorius Headquarters|headquarters]] located in [[the Works]] of [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Dark Lord 20">[[Darth Vader (2017) 20|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 20]]</ref>

In the wake of [[Order 66]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cere Junda]] was imprisoned in the stronghold and questioned relentlessly regarding the whereabouts of her [[Padawan]] [[Second Sister|Trilla Suduri]] and a group of [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] under her care. Despite a prolonged period of silence, Junda eventually cracked and gave up the location of the younger Jedi. Suduri and the younglings were subsequently captured and imprisoned in Fortress Inquisitorius, resulting in the Padawan turning to the [[dark side of the Force]] and becoming an Imperial Inquisitor known as the "Second Sister." When Junda learned of her protege's fate, she used the dark side to break out of her confinements and escape the facility.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> Around the same time, the Jedi Knight [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] was also briefly imprisoned at the facility before being transferred to Coruscant.<ref name="Rise of the Red Blade">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''</ref>

[[File:BrothersSistersKneel-Devoted.png|left|thumb|250x250px|The Inquisitors knelt before their new master, the Dark Lord of the Sith, Darth Vader.]]
A short time after Order 66, three candidates to become Inquisitors, including [[Barriss Offee]], the former Jedi who had been imprisoned after committing an [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|attack]] on the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]], were brought to the fortress. One of the candidates, [[Ahmar]], was killed when he attempted to escape Fortress Inquisitorius. Barriss was tested by the [[The Grand Inquisitor|Grand Inquisitor]] in the use of the dark side of the Force, while the other candidate, [[Dante]], was assessed by the [[Fourth Sister]]. Although both demonstrated potential, only one would be accepted, with the result being decided by a fight to the death. Offee became an Inquisitor after killing Dante, although, unlike him, she hesitated to kill. After that, she, now with the appearance of an Inquisitor, participated in a meeting in the audience chamber with the Grand Inquisitor, the [[Marrok|First Brother]], the [[Eleventh Brother]], and the Fourth Sister so that some of them could meet their new master, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Devoted" />

===Becoming the main base===
{{Quote|I will move the Inquisitors to a new base, off-planet. This will not happen again.|Darth Sidious, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 20" />}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Dark Lord 20 date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]]. ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' places [[Darth Vader (2017) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 18]] after the events of ''Tarkin''. The later issue [[Darth Vader (2017) 19|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 19]] featured the Second Sister, who was killed in ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''. ''Fallen Order'' takes place five years after the Great Jedi Purge, which is dated to 19 BBY by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore, ''Jedi: Fallen Order'' is also set in 14 BBY. As ''Darth Vader: Dark Lord of the Sith 18'' is between both ''Tarkin'' and ''Darth Vader: Dark Lord of the Sith 19'', the events of the issue must take place in 14 BBY. Since [[Darth Vader (2017) 20|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 20]] takes places immediately after the 19th issue, the events of this issue must also take place in 14 BBY.</ref> the primary headquarters of the Inquisitorius on Coruscant became potentially compromised after an [[Coruscant Incident|incident]] involving Darth Vader and a pair of rogue Inquisitors resulted in the death of [[Maklooq]], an Imperial [[Senator]]. Hoping to maintain the organization's clandestine nature, [[Darth Sidious]] had the Inquisitorius move its main operations<ref name="Dark Lord 20" /> to Fortress Inquisitorius on Nur.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
[[File:Vader-defeats-Cal.png|thumb|250x250px|Newly appointed Jedi Knight Cal Kestis faces Darth Vader at the Inquisitorius Fortress.]]
During that same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Dark Lord 20 date" /> during the Second Sister's hunt for the surviving Padawan [[Cal Kestis]], she acquired a [[Eno Cordova's holocron|holocron]] containing a list of the locations of Force-sensitive youths across the galaxy after confronting him on the planet [[Bogano]] and took the holocron to the fortress. The Fortress Inquisitorius turned out to be the scene of a [[Mission to Nur|mission]] that resulted in the Second Sister's death at the hands of Darth Vader due to her failure and betrayal when Kestis and Junda stormed the fortress. Despite Vader's presence, Kestis and Junda narrowly escaped with the holocron.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> Despite the success and damage caused to the place, the invasion of Kestis and Junda was not widely known. Five years later, when the [[Hidden Path]], a secret network with the goal of helping [[Force-sensitive|Force-sensitives]] escape the Empire's persecution, showed [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] a hologram of Fortress Inquisitorius during the planning of an invasion, it was stated that the fortress was impenetrable and that the structure was unknown.<ref name="Part IV" />

===Obi-Wan Kenobi's break-in===
{{SeeAlso|Rescue of Princess Leia (9 BBY)}}
In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Kenobi date">{{SW|url=news/future-lucasfilm-projects-revealed|text=Future Lucasfilm Projects Revealed}} states that ''Obi-Wan Kenobi'' is set ten [[Standard year|years]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, ''Obi-Wan Kenobi'' must be set in [[9 BBY]].</ref> the [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister]], [[Fifth Brother]], and [[Fourth Sister]] spearheaded the [[Hunt for Obi-Wan|hunt]] for [[Jedi Master]] Obi-Wan Kenobi from Fortress Inquisitorius. Shortly after the [[Search for Princess Leia]], during which Kenobi escaped capture, the three Inquisitors conferred in a meeting room in the fortress regarding their next step. The Third Sister, who had been given overall command of the operation by Lord Vader, had three [[viper probe droid]]s launched into space to monitor the locations where Kenobi was likely to emerge next. One of the droids discovered Kenobi on the planet [[Mapuzo]]. After a [[Battle on Mapuzo|skirmish]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who was accompanying Kenobi, was taken into captivity and brought to Fortress Inquisitorius.<ref name="Part III" />
[[File:WhatDefeatWouldBring-OWKPartIV.png|left|thumb|250x250px|Due to Kenobi's escape, the Third Sister was nearly executed by Vader, but was spared after informing him that she was tracking him.]]
The Third Sister interrogated Organa regarding the location of Kenobi and a [[Hidden Path|network]] that had helped him evade capture on Mapuzo. At the same time, Kenobi and a traitorous [[Imperial officer]] named [[Tala Durith]] planned to rescue Organa. Using her [[Code cylinder|credentials]], Durith gained access to the fortress and helped Kenobi enter and navigate the facility. Kenobi freed Organa, although a [[Seeker (droid)|seeker]] [[droid]] alerted the facility to his presence. After fighting off Imperial forces, Kenobi, Durith, and Organa made it to the hangar, where they were surrounded by Imperial forces including the Inquisitors. Before they could be captured, a pair of anti-Imperial network [[T-47 airspeeder]]s arrived and safely extracted them.<ref name="Part IV">{{Kenobi|Part IV}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Fortress_Inquisitorius_Rey_concept_art.jpg|thumb|left|220px|Concept art of Fortress Inquisitorius by Jean-Francois Rey]]
Fortress Inquisitorius was first mentioned, albeit indirectly, in the [[Darth Vader (2017) 20|twentieth issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2017)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']], which was written by [[Charles Soule]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Dark Lord 20" /> on [[August 22]], [[2018]].<ref name="Dark Lord 20 release">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/68124/darth_vader_2017_20|text=Darth Vader (2017) #20}}</ref> It later debuted in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars Jedi]]'' series by [[Respawn Entertainment]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> During the development of ''Jedi: Fallen Order'', the design concept of Fortress Inquisitorius changed more than any other location featured in the game. Concepts considered included an [[asteroid]] base, a base hidden in a [[gas giant]] or [[nebula]], a [[hyperspace]]-capable [[battle station]],<ref name="Art of Fallen Order">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> and even an Imperial Tribunal Citadel.<ref name="sweditororg.com">{{WebCite|url=https://starwarseditorswe.wixsite.com/mysite/post/matt-michnovetz-jedi-fallen-order-interview|text=Matt Michnovetz Jedi: Fallen Order Interview|author=Cicchitti, Conor|date=2021-07-05|work=[https://starwarseditorswe.wixsite.com/mysite Star Wars Editor]|archivedate=20250125083138}}</ref> Concept art for the final design of Fortress Inquisitorius was created by artist [[Jean-Francois Rey]], and inspired by [[Wikipedia:Gothic cathedrals and churches|Gothic cathedrals]].<ref name="Art of Fallen Order" /> In the ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' television series, scenes in the Fortress' meeting room were filmed in<ref name="Return">''[[Obi-Wan Kenobi: A Jedi's Return]]''</ref> the virtual LED set<ref name="17 year">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/tv/obi-wan-kenobi-ewan-mcgregor-hayden-christensen-cover-story|text=Inside the 17-year journey to reunite Ewan McGregor and Hayden Christensen for ''Obi-Wan Kenobi''|author=Ross, Dalton|date=2022-03-10|archivedate=20220310151626}}</ref> known as the Volume.<ref name="Return" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the similarily named [[Citadel Inquisitorius]] was the headquarters of the [[Inquisitorius/Legends|Inquisitorius]], though it was based on the [[Deep Core/Legends|Deep Core]] [[Planet/Legends|world]] of [[Prakith/Legends|Prakith]].<ref name="Sword of the Empire">{{DoD|Sword of the Empire}}</ref>

The novel ''[[Ahsoka (novel)|Ahsoka]]'', which is set in [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Ahsoka Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Ahsoka (novel)|Ahsoka]]'' to [[18 BBY]].</ref> features the Inquisitor known as the [[Sixth Brother]] contact his personal headquarters, but it is unclear<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref> if that refers to the Inquisitorius Headquarters on Coruscant or the Fortress Inquisitorius building, which both existed during that time.
