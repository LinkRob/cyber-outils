'''Dorin gas''' was one of the two major constituents of [[Atmosphere|air]] on the [[planet]] [[Dorin]], the other being [[helium]]. It was essential for the lives of [[Kel Dor]]s<ref name="AotG">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref> in the same way that [[oxygen]] was essential to [[Oxygen breather|other life forms]] like [[human]]s.<ref name="DB A99">{{Databank|jedi-breathing-device|Jedi Breathing Device}}</ref> Conversely, oxygen was [[poison]]ous to Kel Dors.<ref name="Everything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref>
