A [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]] used the callsign "'''Dark Five'''" when flying as part of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Dark Squadron]] between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]]. When the [[squadron]] [[Assassination of Tarl Sokoli|attacked]] the Imperial [[light cruiser]] ''[[Forerunner II]]'' to [[Death|eliminate]] the alleged [[Crimson Dawn]] mole [[Tarl Sokoli]], [[Dark Five]] was able to take out the [[cruiser]]'s [[hyperdrive]]. The [[pilot]] continued to destroy enemy [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] as two of their comrades were killed. The mission was ultimately completed thanks to "[[Dark One]]" [[Beilert Valance]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|<nowiki/><nowiki/>''Kill! Kill!"<br />"''Where did the Empire ''find'' that guy?|Dark Five and Dark Four|<ref name="BH19" />}}
Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Bounty Hunters 19|''Bounty Hunters'' 19]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore, the events of ''Bounty Hunters'' 19 must take place in one of those [[Standard year|years]].</ref> the [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> [[Gender|male]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]] was assigned to Empire's [[Dark Squadron]] under the callsign "[[Dark Five]]."<ref name="BH19" /> The squadron flew [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter|TIE defenders]] and were stationed aboard the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Executor]]'', the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[flagship]], reporting to the [[Sith assassin]] [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]]. The [[cyborg]] [[Beilert Valance]] was placed in [[Squadron commander|command]] of Dark Squadron<ref name="BH18">[[Bounty Hunters 18|''Bounty Hunters'' 18]]</ref> as "[[Dark One]]"<ref name="BH19" /> after being captured and forced into service by Vader.<ref name="BH18" /> The squadron was then given the task of [[Death|eliminating]] the Imperial [[Admiral]] [[Tarl Sokoli]], who was alleged to have become one of the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate's mole in the Empire.<ref name="BH19" />

[[File:DarkFiveDestroys-BountyHunters19.jpg|thumb|left|300px|"Dark Five" attacks the ''Forerunner II''{{'s}} hyperdrive.]]
After Sokoli's [[light cruiser]], the ''[[Forerunner II]]'' had been redirected outside the [[Eriadu system]] under Vader's orders, "Dark Five" traveled there with the rest of their squadron. Formed of five TIE defenders, Dark Squadron was quick to begin its attack under Valance's orders, with the [[cruiser]] deploying [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] in response to its visitors. As the battle raged on, Dark Five was able to eliminate the ''Forerunner II''{{'s}} [[hyperdrive]], uttering "destroy" when Valance commended their shooting.<ref name="BH19" />

Following the loss of "[[Dark Three (pilot)|Dark Three]]," Dark Five shouted "kill" over the comm as they took out an enemy TIE fighter, "[[Dark Four (pilot)|Dark Four]]" questioning where the Empire had found the [[pilot]]. [[Dark Four]] fell shortly after, and Valance crashed his defender into the ''Forerunner II''{{'s}} [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] to reach Sokoli. The squadron commander was able to kill the admiral and complete the squadron's mission.<ref name="BH19" />
