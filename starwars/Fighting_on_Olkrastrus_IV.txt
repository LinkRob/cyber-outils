The '''fighting on Olkrastrus IV''' was a protracted campaign waged against [[Separatist holdouts]] on the planet [[Olkrastrus IV]] against the emergent [[Galactic Empire]] despite the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Mask of Fear" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The [[Olkrastrus IV holdouts]] waged a campaign that included [[guerrilla warfare]]. The bulk of the holdout attempted to bring their wartime factories back online. Meanwhile, secondary groups&mdash;whose numbers ranged from [[Army|armies]] worth of hundreds of fighters to small bands of twelve or else guerilla fighters&mdash;harassed the [[Imperial Military]].<ref name="Mask of Fear" />
