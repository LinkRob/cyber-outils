'''Evo''' was a [[human]] who served as a [[crew chief]] for the [[New Republic]]'s [[New Republic Defense Fleet Starfighter Corps|Starfighter Corps]] during its [[New Republic Era|reign]].<ref name="Mando&Grogu" /> During or after [[11 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoDate|MandalorianAndGrogu}}</ref> Evo was stationed at [[Adelphi Base]],<ref name="Mando&Grogu" /> a New Republic base on the [[planet]] [[Adelphi]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="MandoVG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> After the [[Adelphi Rangers|Adelphi Squadron]] of [[New Republic pilot|pilots]] returned to the base following an [[Battle of Nal Hutta|attack]] on the [[Hutt Palace]] of the planet [[Nal Hutta]], Evo took the [[K-22995 light flight helmet]] of [[Colonel]] [[Ward]], the base's [[Commanding officer|commander]], as she dismounted from her [[X-wing starfighter]] on one of the base's landing pads.<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />

==Behind the scenes==
Evo was portrayed by [[Landis Fields]] of [[Industrial Light & Magic]]<ref name="Blog">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-mandalorian-and-grogu-trivia|text=''Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu'' Behind-the-Scenes Facts and Trivia}}</ref> in the [[2026]] film ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''. The character's name was not spoken in the film but appeared in the credits.<ref name="Mando&Grogu" />
