"'''An Invitation to Defect'''" is a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure outline in the [[September]] [[1996]] ''[[Shadows of the Empire Planets Guide]]'', published by [[West End Games]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' as part of the ''[[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire]]'' multimedia project. As a sequel to the preceding outline, ''[[A Case of Mistaken Identities]]'', the adventure tasks [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] players with aiding in the defection of a minor [[Unidentified Imperial defector|bureaucrat]] from the personal staff of [[Dandamont Pring]], [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Consul-General|consul-general]] of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Bothawui/Legends|Bothawui]]. ''An Invitation to Defect'' is followed by the adventure ''[[A Most Deadly Hunt]]''.
