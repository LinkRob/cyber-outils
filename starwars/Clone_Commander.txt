'''Clone Commander''', also known as '''clone trooper commander''', was a [[Military rank|rank]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] held by [[clone trooper officer]]s who led [[regiment]]s of 2,304 [[trooper]]s. The sixteen [[Cloning|clone]] [[commander]]s in each [[corps]] were in turn commanded by their respective [[Jedi General]].

[[Advanced Recon Commando]]s who served as commanders were known as '''Clone ARC Commanders'''. [[Clone trooper pilot]]s could also serve as commanders. During the [[Clone Wars]], Clone Commanders served under [[Jedi]] [[General]]s and, like the rest of their clone brethren, fought against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Near [[Mission to Mustafar|the end]] of the war, all clones were ordered by [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] to [[Order 66|betray and kill]] their Jedi [[officer]]s.

The rank was retained in the [[Imperial Military]] during the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] as the clones troopers became the first generation of [[stormtrooper]]s.

==Description==
===Overview===
{{Quote|I'm Commander Cody. Your new boss.|Cody, to the [[Rishi Station unit]]|<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:RexInCommand-Shattered.png|thumb|right|300px|Granted a high level of authority, a Clone Commander was a high-ranking clone trooper officer in the Grand Army of the Republic.]]
A [[Cloning|Clone]] [[Commander]],<ref name="CC-3636 DB">{{Databank|clone-commander-wolffe|Clone Commander Wolffe}}</ref> also referred to as a [[clone trooper]] commander,<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> was a [[military rank]] given to [[clone trooper officer]]s during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Episode II" /> and early [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Replacements" /> The rank was used by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], the [[Republic Navy]],<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" /> and the [[Republic Starfighter Corps]].<ref name="HC 45" /> In the [[Galactic Republic]], the rank of Clone Commander could be given to clone [[trooper]] [[officer]]s who also served as [[special ops clone trooper]]s,<ref name="Landing at Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref> [[Biker Advanced Recon Commando]]s,<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> [[Clone SCUBA trooper]]s,<ref name="Water War" /> [[Advanced Recon Force trooper|Advanced Recon Force Scout Troopers]],<ref name="Massacre">{{TCW|Massacre}}</ref> and more.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>

[[Clone trooper pilot]]s<ref name="Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> and [[Clone navigation officer|navigation officers]] could also hold the rank, holding command over [[Realspace|spatial]] operations instead of ground units.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" /> Clone Commanders could also be important enough to be given their own guard details, with two lesser ranking clone troopers protecting their officer by marching behind them.<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref> The rank was later used during the early days of the [[Imperial Military]], which started its existence as a force mainly composed of clones. During the early implementation of [[Project War-Mantle]], the rank even remained in effect over recruited [[Elite Squad Trooper]]s.<ref name="Replacements" /> As the early [[Imperial Era]] dragged on, however, Clone Commanders found their authority superseded by recruited officers; for example, a recruited [[lieutenant]] could assign tasks and give orders to a Clone Commander.<ref name="The Outpost">{{TBB|The Outpost}}</ref>

===Training===
Clone Commanders of the [[Republic Military]] typically received [[ARC program|ARC training]], although some, like [[Ponds]], were not provided such regimen. Despite this, Clone Commanders who did not receive ARC training could be trained by senior instructors<ref name="HC 76">{{HelmetCollectionCite|76|Helmets}}</ref> and become specialized, such as in military reconnaissance.<ref name="Front Lines" /> Clone trooper officers were selected to be part of a program that trained clone officers on how to work as liaisons between [[Jedi General]]s and their subordinate clone troopers, though it was possible for some Clone Commanders to not be included<ref name="HC 76" /> and to not be briefed on how to interact with [[Jedi high command]].<ref name="Front Lines" />

===Equipment===
[[File:CoruscantCloneTroopers-AOTC.png|thumb|left|250px|At first, Clone Commanders were identified by yellow markings on their armor.]]
Initially, Clone Commanders within the Grand [[Army]] of the Republic were outfitted with standard<ref name="CL" /> [[Phase I clone trooper armor|Phase I]]<ref name="USW" /> [[clone trooper armor]] featuring four [[Color|yellow]] circles on the left [[Armor|chestplate]] as well as a stripe of yellow down the length of each [[Limb|arm]], vertically up the middle of the [[helmet]] and laterally around the visor.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>

Later, Clone Commanders, as with other clone officers, customized their armor with additional equipment including [[kama]]s, [[pauldron]]s, [[rangefinder]]s, secondary communication [[antenna]]s, visors, [[jetpack]]s, [[pistol]] holsters, [[macrobinoculars]], [[bandolier]]s, [[rank insignia plaque]]s,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> built-in lights, primary communications antenna,<ref name="HC 25">{{HelmetCollectionCite|25|Helmets}}</ref> unit affiliation markings, and more.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> [[Jaig Eyes]] could also be added to the commander's armor.<ref name="Cat and Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref> [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] allowed for greater modifications.<ref name="Rise" /> Clone Commanders within the Republic [[Navy]] were equipped with a blue [[Clothing|cloth fabric]] [[Republic military uniforms|uniform]], black [[glove]]s, and a rank insignia plaque.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

===Galactic Republic===
====Grand Army of the Republic====
{{Quote|We'll promote Rex to Commander and have him lead the new division. Ahsoka can go with him as an adviser.|Anakin Skywalker, to Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref>}}
[[File:CodyLoT.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Cody served as Clone Marshal Commander of the 7th Sky Corps.]]
The rank of Clone Commander was the highest rank achieved by a clone trooper, and was ranked higher than a [[Clone Captain]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> There were several variations of the rank, including [[Clone Marshal Commander]]<ref name="HC 25" /> and Clone ARC Commander<ref name="Blitz">{{SWCT|set=Rank & File|cardname=Clone ARC Commander Blitz}}</ref> for the different positions and roles Clone Commanders undertook. A Clone Marshal Commander served as the [[commanding officer]] of a [[corps]] within the Grand Army of the Republic, such as [[Cody]] of the [[7th Sky Corps]],<ref name="HC 25" /> [[Bacara]] of the [[Galactic Marines|21st Nova Corps]],<ref name="Bacara FC">{{ForceCollection|Bacara|star=4}}</ref> [[Neyo]] of the [[91st Mobile Reconnaissance Corps]],<ref name="GAR">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Grand Army of the Republic}}</ref> and [[Bly]] of the [[327th Star Corps]].<ref name="Bly">{{Databank|aayla-secura|Aayla Secura}}</ref> Other commanders who led a corps included [[Gree (clone trooper)|Gree]] of the [[41st Elite Corps]]<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" /> and [[9th Assault Corps]].<ref name="Mace Windu">{{EncyclopediaCite|Mace Windu and Other Users of the Force}}</ref>

Clone ARC Commanders were [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s who served as Clone Commanders.<ref name="Card Trader">{{SWCT|set=Rank & File|cardname=Clone ARC Commander Blitz}}</ref> Multiple ARC Commanders could serve within the same unit, such as ARC Commanders [[Blitz]], [[Colt]], [[Havoc (ARC trooper)|Havoc]],<ref name="Clone Cadets">{{TCW|Clone Cadets}}</ref> and [[Hammer (ARC trooper)|Hammer]]<ref name="Hammer">{{SWCT|set=Star Wars: The Clone Wars - Classic Card Art|cardname=Clone Arc Commander Hammer}}</ref> from the [[Rancor Battalion]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> Clone officers could be promoted to the rank of Clone Commander and be placed in command of half of a [[legion]]<ref name="Join the Battle" /> or the entirety of a [[division]].<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> A standard Clone Commander typically led a full [[regiment]] that consisted of sixteen [[Company|companies]].<ref name="Rise" /> One such commander was [[Tacks]], who served throughout the Clone Wars.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

[[File:CloneCmdrWolffe-Transformations.png|thumb|left|180px|The 104th Battalion served under Clone Commander Wolffe.]]
Clone Commanders could also command smaller military units such as [[battalion]]s, which consisted of four companies.<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> Such commanders included [[Wolffe]] of the [[104th Battalion]],<ref name="Rising Malevolence">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> [[Jet]] of his [[Jet's battalion|own special operations battalion]],<ref name="Ki-Adi-Mundi">{{Databank|ki-adi-mundi-biography-gallery|Ki-Adi-Mundi Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref> and [[Crane (clone trooper)|Crane]] of the [[One-Eighty-Fourth Attack Battalion|One-Eighty-Fourth]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> Clone Commanders could also command small specialized companies, such as a company of Clone SCUBA troopers. Clone Commander [[Monnk]] led a [[Monnk's company|company]] of such troopers during the war.<ref name="Water War" /> Commanders such as Ponds, who was not trained to serve directly under Jedi Generals, could be placed as their second-in-command anyway.<ref name="HC 76" /> Ponds also served as the commanding officer of [[Lightning Squadron]], a light ground cavalry squadron.<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref>

====Republic Navy and Starfighter Corps====
Clone Commanders additionally served within the Republic Navy<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" /> and the Republic's [[Starfighter Corps]].<ref name="HC 45" /> A clone trooper pilot,<ref name="Front Lines" /> [[Davijaan]], or "Odd Ball," held the rank of Clone Commander.<ref name="HC 45" /> Clone Commanders could serve as the commanding officer of a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], or form part of its crew.<ref name="Destroy Malevolence">{{TCW|Destroy Malevolence|Destroy ''Malevolence''}}</ref> Additionally, Clone Commanders had the capability of leading an entire [[flight group]] that consisted of multiple [[squadron]]s and [[flight]]s.<ref name="HC 45" />

===Galactic Empire===
{{Quote|Impressive. The Clone Commander's new squad has followed through where his brethren did not.|Wilhuff Tarkin, to Rampart|<ref name="Replacements" />}}
[[File:Elite-Squad-Full.png|thumb|right|250px|Clone Commander Crosshair was placed in charge of the first Elite Squad, non-clone soldiers who enlisted with the Imperial Military.]]
The rank of Clone Commander continued to exist in the Imperial Military during the early [[Standard year|years]] of Sidious' reign as [[Galactic Emperor]] as the clones continued their military service as the first generation of [[stormtrooper]]s. Shortly after the Galactic Republic's transition into the [[Galaxy|Galactic]] Empire, some Clone Commanders were picked to lead new experimental non-clone units comprised of volunteers and conscripts. The first such unit, the [[Elite Squad|first Elite Squad]], consisted of four [[Elite Squad Trooper]]s under the leadership of Clone Commander [[Crosshair]].<ref name="Replacements" /> The clone commando [[Scorch]] acted as a Clone Commander within the [[Advanced Science Division]].<ref name="IC">{{TBB|Identity Crisis}}</ref>

==History==
===Clone Wars===
{{Quote|The droid army is in full retreat.''"<br />"''Well done, Commander.|A Clone Commander and Yoda|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
[[File:Clone_Commander_and_Yoda-AOTC.png|thumb|left|250px|The Grand Army of the Republic achieved its first victory under the leadership of Clone Commanders and Jedi Knights.]]
Clone Commanders were trained by [[Jango Fett]], the [[clone template]] of the Republic's clone forces.<ref name="HC 76" /> They debuted in the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], in which multiple Clone Commanders participated.<ref name="Episode II" /> While the members of the [[Jedi Order]] did not hold a formal rank in the Republic Military at the time, the clone troopers and their commanders served under the [[Jedi]] who acted as ''de facto'' [[general]]s on [[Geonosis]].<ref name="CL" /> During the battle, Commander CT-411 reported to [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]], who took command of five special [[commando]] units. While Windu and other Jedi took to the field, leading clone troopers into battle against an army of [[battle droid]]s, [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] oversaw the battle from the [[Forward Command Center|forward command center]] where a [[Unidentified Clone Commander (Geonosis)|Clone Commander]] informed him of the Republic Army's progress. Yoda commended the commander after he reported that the [[Separatist Droid Army]] had fallen into retreat.<ref name="Episode II" />

After the fall of Geonosis, the Grand Army of the Republic assembled on the Republic [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]]. [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], along with members of the [[Galactic Senate]], observed the newly formed Republic Military as Clone Commanders and their [[troop]]s boarded the [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class transgalactic military assault ships]], preparing to deploy throughout [[the galaxy]] to fight the [[Confederacy military|forces]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Episode II" />

[[File:CodyObiWan.png|thumb|right|250px|During the Clone Wars a sense of mutual respect developed between the Clone Commanders and their Jedi Generals.]]
Clone Commanders were assigned to Jedi Generals after the Jedi adopted the role of leadership in the Grand Army of the Republic. Relationships of trust and respect developed between the commanders and their generals as they served together throughout the many campaigns of the Clone Wars. General [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and Commander Cody served alongside each other as commanding officers of the 7th Sky Corps, including the [[212th Attack Battalion]].<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> Cody was particularly loyal to Kenobi,<ref name="CC-2224 DB">{{Databank|clone-commander-cody|Clone Commander Cody}}</ref> who found the Clone Commander to be a capable officer.<ref name="Rookies" />

Clone Commander Wolffe and Jedi General [[Plo Koon]] also served together for the duration of the Clone Wars.<ref name="TSWB" /> While the clones considered themselves expendable due to their artificial nature, Koon valued their lives and risked his own to protect them.<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> As a result, he developed a close bond with Wolffe.<ref name="Plo Koon DB">{{Databank|plo-koon|Plo Koon}}</ref> Under their leadership, the [[Wolfpack]] became one of the most renowned clone trooper squads in the Grand Army of the Republic.<ref name="CC-3636 DB" />

===Treason and purge===
{{Quote|Commander Rex, you're in violation of Order 66. I accuse you of treason against the Grand Army of the Republic. You'll be demoted in rank from Commander and subject to execution along with the traitor Ahsoka Tano.|Jesse, to Rex|audio=JesseDemotesRex-VaD.flac|<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref>}}
[[File:327th_Star_Corps.png|thumb|left|250px|Clone Commanders continued to serve at the side of their Jedi Generals during the final days of the Clone Wars.]]
Despite the reservations of [[Admiral]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] regarding leadership in the Republic Military,<ref name="Counterattack">{{TCW|Counterattack}}</ref> Clone Commanders and Jedi Generals continued to lead the armies of the Galactic Republic as the Clone Wars entered its [[19 BBY|final year]]. The Republic's [[Outer Rim Sieges|strategy]] now focused on the elimination of the remaining Separatist forces in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Clone Commander Bly and Jedi General [[Aayla Secura]] oversaw the [[Battle of Felucia (Outer Rim Sieges)|campaign]] on [[Felucia]],<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> while Clone Commander Bacara and Jedi General [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] led the Republic [[Fourth Battle of Mygeeto|assault]] on [[Mygeeto]]. Other battlefronts included [[Saleucami]],<ref name="Episode III" /> where Clone Commander Neyo served alongside Jedi General [[Stass Allie]], and [[Bracca]], a [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] that the [[13th Battalion]] [[Bracca Invasion|invaded]] under the command of Jedi General [[Jaro Tapal]] and his [[Unidentified Clone Commander (13th Battalion)|Clone Commander]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

In the aftermath of the [[Battle of Yerbana]], Jedi General [[Anakin Skywalker]] committed half of the [[501st Legion]] to the [[Siege of Mandalore]], creating the [[332nd Division]] while the remainder of his forces returned to the [[Core Worlds]] to defend the capital in the [[Battle of Coruscant]]. The 332nd was created in response to Skywalker's former [[Padawan]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], who supported [[Lady]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze]]'s efforts to retake [[Mandalore]] from the rogue [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] and his [[Mandalorian super commando]]s. However, as Tano declined to rejoin the Jedi Order, she had forfeited her rank as a [[Jedi Commander]] and therefore could not lead Republic soldiers in an official capacity. As a result, Skywalker promoted his second-in-command, [[Rex]], to the rank of Clone Commander, giving the former Clone Captain the authority to lead the new division.<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" />

[[File:Grey-vs-Depa.png|thumb|right|250px|Order 66 turned the Clone Commanders against the Jedi officers whom they had served with throughout the Clone Wars.]]
Following the successful defense of Coruscant, Clone Commander Gree and the [[41st Scout Battalion]] accompanied Jedi Generals Yoda and [[Luminara Unduli]] to [[Kashyyyk]], intending to protect the strategically located [[homeworld]] of the [[Wookiee]] [[species]]. Following Yoda's departure, Cody and Kenobi deployed their battalion to [[Utapau]], where General [[Grievous]] retreated in the wake of [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[death]]. However, with the [[Jedi High Council]]'s [[Duel in Palpatine's office|failed arrest]] of Chancellor Palpatine&mdash;revealed to be the Sith Lord [[Darth]] Sidious&mdash;subsequent to Grievous' destruction during the [[Battle of Utapau]], the chancellor ordered the Clone Commanders and their clone troopers to execute [[Order 66]],<ref name="Episode III" /> immediately turning the Jedi's own army against them.<ref name="Shattered" />

Apart from the [[genetically defective clone]]s of [[Clone Force 99]], the [[behavioral modification biochip]]s within their [[brain]]s compelled the vast majority of the standard clone troopers to comply with Order 66,<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> including the Clone Commanders<ref name="Episode III" /> who worked the closest with the Jedi Generals.<ref name="TSWB" /> Across the galaxy, the Jedi were gunned down by the soldiers and commanders<ref name="Episode III" /> whom they trusted,<ref name="TSWB" /> including Mundi, who was killed by Bacara, and Secura, who was fatally shot in the back by Bly and his troops.<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:JesseDemotesRex-VaD.png|thumb|left|250px|Clone Commander Rex was accused of treason and demoted for disobeying Order 66.]]
Clone Commanders also had strict orders from Sidious to execute any clone trooper who defied his plan to [[Great Jedi Purge|systematically purge]] the Jedi Order. The surgical removal of Rex's biochip, however, allowed him to [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|defend]] Ahsoka Tano against his own soldiers, who were still beholden to their programming.<ref name="Shattered" /> Subsequently, his subordinate [[Clone Lieutenant]] [[Jesse]] accused him of committing [[treason]] against the Republic and declared that Rex would be demoted from the rank of Commander and executed. In response, Rex remarked to Tano that he had never liked being a Commander anyway.<ref name="Victory and Death" /> After becoming a fugitive from the Empire, which officially declared him dead,<ref name="Battle Scars">{{TBB|Battle Scars}}</ref> Rex went back to calling himself by his former rank of Captain.<ref name="The Lost Commanders">{{Rebels|The Lost Commanders}}</ref>

===Changing of the guard===
{{Quote|What is the status of Project War-Mantle?''"<br />"''On schedule, sir. Our top recruits are here to begin their training under their new commander.|Wilhuff Tarkin and Rampart|<ref name="Replacements" />}}
[[File:CodyCrosshair-SWI220.png|thumb|right|250px|Crosshair and Cody were both clones who served as commanders during the early Imperial Era.]]
In the [[Standard day|days]] following the Galactic Empire's formation, the rank of Clone Commander was granted to Crosshair, the only member of Clone Force 99 who remained loyal to the Galactic Empire. Seeking to test the initial results of Project War-Mantle, Admiral Tarkin ordered Commander Crosshair to take a squad of enlisted soldiers to [[Onderon]] and destroy [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Onderon rebels|insurgents]].<ref name="Replacements" />

[[ES-01]], one of the [[Reproduction|natural-born]] [[human]] soldiers in Crosshair's squad, doubted that the clone was fit to be a commander, especially as the Empire had already begun its efforts to decommission the clone trooper program. However, Crosshair asserted his authority by executing ES-01 for refusing to complete the [[Mission to Onderon (Elite Squad)|mission]], which included killing the civilians who traveled with Gerrera's group. The squad's success reinforced [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart|Rampart]]'s belief that the [[Imperial Army]] could eventually be molded into the mightiest fighting force previously unseen in [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]], provided that non-clone soldiers continued to learn from the experience of veteran clones such as Commander Crosshair.<ref name="Replacements" />

Between [[19 BBY]] and [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date-TBBs2">{{TBBDate|203}}</ref> [[Clone Marshal Commander]] Cody led a unit of clone stormtroopers in [[Mission to Desix|quashing]] a [[Desix Separatist holdout|Separatist holdout]] on the planet [[Desix]]. Having returned to the Empire despite being abandoned on Kamino during the [[Attack on Kamino|Imperial withdrawal and attack]], Crosshair, who had been stuck on the planet for thirty-two planetary rotations, was assigned to the squad. While he initially assumed he would be leading the squad, Crosshair was not yet cleared to take back on a command, but was happy to work under Cody once more. However, Cody would desert after the mission after becoming disillusioned with the Imperial cause.<ref name="The Solitary">{{TBB|The Solitary Clone}}</ref> 

During or after 18 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Circa 18 BBY">{{TBBDate|212}}</ref> Clone Commander [[Mayday]] would be killed in action after a failed attempt to recapture [[TK stormtrooper armor|Imperial supplies]] that had been stolen by insurgents on the planet [[Barton IV]]. Mayday's death would prove to the final straw for Crosshair's own loyalty for the Empire.<ref name="The Outpost" /> Clone commando Scorch served in the Advanced Science Division as a Clone Commander<ref name="Scorch">{{Databank|clone-commander-scorch|Clone Commander Scorch}}</ref> but met his end during the [[skirmish on Wayland]].<ref name="TCHA">{{TBB|The Cavalry Has Arrived}}</ref>
