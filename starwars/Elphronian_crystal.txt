{{Quote|They shoot yellow flames from their mouths, which are usually hot enough to melt nearly any organic material, except for heat-resistant Elphronian crystals.|Ardis San Tekka, in her journal|<ref name="Bestiary" />}}
'''Elphronian crystals''' were a type of [[Temperature|heat]]-resistant [[crystal]] found on the [[planet]] [[Elphrona]].<ref name="Bestiary" /> No earlier than [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BestiaryVol1Date}}</ref> [[Ardis San Tekka]], the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]]'s [[Director of Creature Cartography]], compiled a [[Ardis San Tekka's journal|journal]] about various [[Sentience|non-sentient]] creatures. Within the journal, San Tekka noted that the [[Fire|flames]] [[charhound]]s shot from their [[mouth]]s were usually hot enough to melt nearly any organic material, except for Elphronian crystals.<ref name="Bestiary" />
