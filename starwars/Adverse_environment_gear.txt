'''Adverse environment gear''' was a form of specialized [[clothing]] that helped the wearer to survive in dangerous and inhospitable environments. Different models of adverse environment gear were designed for extreme [[Temperature|heat]] and cold, and could also include polarized [[goggles]]. A set of adverse environment gear cost 500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
Adverse environment gear was mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
