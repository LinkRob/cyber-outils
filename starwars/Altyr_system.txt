The '''Altyr system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Morshdine sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> It fell within grid square O-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and shared its name<ref name="Outlaws" /> with the [[Altyr 5 Light Freighter Race]].<ref name="ChopperChase">{{Disney|subdomain=lol|url=games/star-wars-rebels-chopper-chase|text=Star Wars Rebels: Chopper Chase|int=Star Wars Rebels: Chopper Chase|nolive=1}}</ref> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> as part of the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Imperial Navy]] [[Battle in the Altyr system|destroyed]] a [[Unidentified rebel capital ship|capital ship]] belonging to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in the system. [[Commander]] [[Folimar]] called the battle a "reminder of the Empire's superior force compared to the rebels," which was quoted in coverage of the battle by [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Dorna Parros]] on the [[HoloNet News]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Altyr system was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" /> The [[Altyr system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Mysteries of the Sith]]'', the [[1998]] expansion for<ref name="Mysteries">''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Mysteries of the Sith]]''</ref> the [[1997]] [[LucasArts]] video game ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]''.<ref name="DarkForces2">''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II]]''</ref>
