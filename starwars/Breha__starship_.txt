The '''''Breha''''' was a [[diplomat]]ic [[Starship|vessel]] used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Its design greatly resembled<ref name="Trouble at Tibrin" /> that of a [[J-type 327 Nubian royal starship|J-type 327 Nubian starship]].<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> At some point following the [[Battle of Yavin|destruction]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Luke Skywalker]] used the ''Breha'' to go on a secret [[Mission to Tibrin|mission]] to [[Tibrin]], posing as diplomats from [[Naboo]]'s [[House Berenko]] seeking to negotiate a trade deal. The ''Breha'' was blown into the water by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces during the mission.<ref name="Trouble at Tibrin" />
