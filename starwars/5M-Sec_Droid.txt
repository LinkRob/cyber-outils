The '''5M-Sec Droid''' was a model of hulking, three-[[Limb|legged]] [[security droid]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. At the time of the [[Iron Blockade]], one such droid guarded the site of a [[chromium]] [[Mining|mine]] near [[Wickridge]], [[Burnin Konn]], where the [[Jedi]] [[Khandra]] and [[Nuhj]] had once been [[Death|killed]] by a member of the Imperial [[Inquisitorius]].<ref name="Respond in kind" />
