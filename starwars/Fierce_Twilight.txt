'''Fierce Twilight''' is an online flash player game found at [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''The Clone Wars'']] page on the [[Cartoon Network]] website.

==Publisher's summary==
''Grievous has us surrounded.''

''Hop aboard the ''Twilight'' and defend the fleet from attacking droid fighters''.

''Good hunting!''

==Gameplay==
Take command of [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker's]] ship, and blast the seemingly endless attacking [[Vulture-class starfighter/Legends|droid starfighters]] and [[Rogue-class Porax-38 starfighter/Legends|''Rogue''-class starfighters]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Separatist]] forces.

Help Anakin lead the [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone]] [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|army]] of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]] against [[General/Legends|General]] Grievous and the Droid Army of the Separatists. Fly to various [[Star system/Legends|star systems]], grab [[Blaster/Legends|blaster]] upgrades, [[Deflector shield/Legends|shield]] recharges and extra lives to continue blasting the flying enemy forces!

The game has 8 levels.
