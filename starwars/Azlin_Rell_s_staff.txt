The fallen [[Jedi]] [[Azlin Rell]] used a [[wood]]en '''staff''' with a [[Color|purple]] [[crystal]] embedded in the top of the staff.<ref name="NYCC" /> 

In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|EOB1}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], Azlin Rell carried his staff while arriving on [[Banchii]] with [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Reath Silas]]. When a [[Mission to Banchii|battle]] broke out on Banchii, Rell held the staff while fighting off a [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] using [[the Force]].<ref name="TEOB 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
Azlin Rell's staff in the [[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 3|third volume]] of the manga series [[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Edge of Balance]], written by [[Daniel José Older]] and [[Shima Shinya]], and illustrated by [[Mizuki Sakakibara]].<ref name="TEOB 3" /> During the [[2024]] New York Comic Con's [[Lucasfilm]] publishing panel, a colored concept art of Azlin Rell carrying his staff was shown. The concept art revealed that a purple crystal was embedded in the staff.<ref name="NYCC" />
