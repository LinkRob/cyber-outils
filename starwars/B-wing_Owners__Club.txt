The '''B-wing Owners' Club''' was a club for [[pilot]]s that owned [[B-wing heavy assault starfighter|B-wings]]. They issued [[bumper sticker]]s with a pun that compared a B-wing to a picture of a [[bee]], and one of these stickers was collected by [[Poe Dameron]] in a [[Unidentified book|vehicle guidebook]].<ref name="Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
The B-wing Owners' Club was mentioned in the [[2022]] non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Awesome Vehicles]]''.<ref name="Vehicles" />
