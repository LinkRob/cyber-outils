'''Battle droids''', also known as '''combat droids''', '''war droids''',<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> '''clankers''', '''droid soldiers''', or '''battle bots''', were a type of [[droid]] designed for combat. Over the [[Standard year|years]], many different models of battle droids were used by various factions throughout [[the galaxy]]. One such faction was the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], which used a number of different models of battle droid during the [[Clone Wars]] to make up the [[Separatist Droid Army]]. Under the command of [[General]] [[Grievous]], varying models of battle droids fought against the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[clone trooper]]s until the final [[Standard day|days]] of the war, when the Confederate battle droids were deactivated by the [[Galactic Empire]] and became prohibited by the [[Imperial Senate]].

==History==
===Clone Wars===
{{Quote|A Republic military presence is the only sure defense against the Separatists.''"<br />"''Even extremists can be reasoned with!''"<br />"''Perhaps if one can be heard over the clanking of their battle droids!|[[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Satine Kryze]]|<ref name="Voyage of Temptation">{{TCW|Voyage of Temptation}}</ref>}}
[[File:Collapse_of_the_Republic_cover_art.png|thumb|left|300px|Battle droids fought Republic clone troopers as the Separatist Droid Army.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] used a variety of battle [[droid]]s in their [[Separatist Droid Army|droid army]]. These droids, including [[B1-series battle droid]]s,<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> which were the main [[infantry]] of the Separatists,<ref name="Battle Droids - Separatist Droid Army">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Battle Droids - Separatist Droid Army}}</ref> [[B2-series super battle droid]]s, and [[droideka]]s, were produced in large factories on [[planet]]s such as [[Geonosis]].<ref name="Episode II" /> The Separatist Droid [[Army]] was led by the<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|general-grievous|General Grievous}}</ref> [[Kaleesh]]<ref name="GrievousSWE">{{SWE|characters|generalgrievous|General Grievous}}</ref> [[cyborg]] [[General]] [[Grievous]].<ref name="Databank" /> Droids like the B1 battle droid were ineffective in small numbers,<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> but they had the ability to overwhelm enemies in large groups.<ref name="Battle Droid">{{Databank|battle-droid|Battle Droid}}</ref> Battle droids were led by [[commander]]s such as Grievous or [[Asajj Ventress]],<ref name="ARC Troopers">{{TCW|ARC Troopers (episode)}}</ref> and [[Species|organic]] [[officer]]s could be assisted by specialized [[OOM command battle droid]]s.<ref name="Downfall of a Droid">{{TCW|Downfall of a Droid}}</ref> [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|T-series tactical droids]]<ref name="Innocents of Ryloth">{{TCW|Innocents of Ryloth}}</ref> and [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|super tactical droids]] also commanded battle droids throughout the Clone Wars, and these units could assist organic leaders. One super [[tactical droid]] was [[Kalani]], a general who led Confederate forces against [[Onderon rebels|insurgents]] on [[Onderon]], but, after the [[Onderonian Civil War]], Kalani escaped to [[Agamar]].<ref name="TCW:TP">{{TCW|Tipping Points}}</ref>

[[File:C21Highsinger-BHS.png|thumb|right|250px|c-21 Highsinger was a combat droid bounty hunter during the Clone Wars and into the reign of the Empire.]]
The main enemies of the battle droids were the [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]]. The [[trooper]]s were able to think independently and creatively, while battle droids like the B1 were "dim-witted"<ref name="battle droid">{{SWE|technology|battledroid|battle droid}}</ref> and easily taken down by [[Cloning|clones]]. However, the battle droids of the Separatists outnumbered the clones, and [[Count]] [[Dooku]] once claimed that clones were outnumbered one hundred to one. During the war, clone troopers,<ref name="Ambush" /> and even some of the [[Jedi]], referred to battle droids by the nickname of "clankers."<ref name="Mace Windu 1">[[Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu 1|''Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu'' 1]]</ref> The Jedi adapted their [[Battle droid remote|training remotes]] to mimic the moves of battle droids to better prepare [[Padawan]]s for the war against the Droid Army.<ref name="PMP">{{TOTJ|Practice Makes Perfect (episode)}}</ref>

The [[Chiss Ascendancy]] came to learn of the Clone Wars and that battle droids made up the majority of the [[Confederacy military]]; when [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn" recounted that information to [[sky-walker]] [[Che'ri]], she questioned the tactical advantage of such a military, understanding that an enemy had the chance to deactivate droids upon accessing their controls and that their programming could be altered during an enemy incursion into a [[factory]]. Thrawn also noted that their intended programming may possess exploitable weaknesses, concluding that a desire to lessen [[death]]s by using droids instead of organics as soldiers was useless if it resulted in losing the war.<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> [[Balmorran Arms]] produced the [[SD-series battle droid]] at some point.<ref name="Disciples of Harmony">''[[Disciples of Harmony]]''</ref>

===Age of the Empire===
{{Quote|I thought everything was being dismantled or torched in one star or another. What does anyone need with battle droids when the Empire has Star Destroyers?|Has Obitt|<ref name="Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>}}
[[File:The_Last_Battle_thumb.png|thumb|left|300px|B1 battle droids that remained active on Agamar.]]
Although battle droids were outlawed by the [[Galactic Empire]] in the wake of the Clone Wars, companies such as [[Holowan Laboratories]]<ref name="Star Wars Rebels: Head to Head">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Head to Head]]''</ref> and [[Arakyd Industries]] were able to evade the ban by having their products reclassified as [[security droid]]s.<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> The galactic population's general distrust of [[class four droid]]s helped to turn the [[Joint Chiefs of the Imperial Military|Joint Chiefs]] away from using battle droids.<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> Nevertheless, [[Instructor]] [[Pell Baylo]] felt that [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] expected his military personnel to obey orders as if they were mindless droids.<ref name="Orientation">{{InsiderCite|157|Orientation}}</ref>

Even during the early [[Standard month|months]] of the [[Imperial Era]], battle droids and other pieces of surviving Separatist equipment were considered useless in the face of Imperial technology, such as the [[Star Destroyer]]s of the [[Imperial Navy]]. The [[smuggler]] [[Has Obitt]] believed that surviving CIS equipment, such as the remaining battle droids, would be dismantled or destroyed.<ref name="Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel" /> Indeed, surviving battle droids were all authorized to be decommissioned, which really meant destroyed, in [[Decommissioning facility|specialized decommissioning facilities]] on worlds like [[Corellia]],<ref name="Droid Decommissioning Facility">{{Databank|droid-decommissioning-facility|Droid decommissioning facility}}</ref> although some surviving Separatist equipment was allowed to fall into the hands of anti-Imperial insurgents, which gave an excuse for the [[Imperial Military]] to take over [[legacy world]]s.<ref name="Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel" />

The [[core ship]] of the [[Lucrehulk-class LH-3210 cargo freighter]], known as the ''[[Primacy]]'', crashed on the planet [[Koboh]]. The [[Bedlam Raiders]], a group of marauders, reprogrammed the ship's former Separatist battle droids to serve them. With the Primacy's numerous droids and resources, enough to conquer a planet, the marauders imposed their will on the world without facing much opposition.<ref name="Jedi: Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>

The [[TRD-2 sparring droid]] was based upon the design of the [[SD-series battle droid]].<ref name="Disciples of Harmony">''[[Disciples of Harmony]]''</ref> Former battle droids formed the [[Droid Gotra]] during the Imperial Era. The [[crime lord]] [[Faazah]] also made use of repurposed battle droids to bolster his ranks of [[bodyguard]]s on the former Separatist stronghold of [[Murkhana]].<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> Battle droids [[Agamar garrison|remained active]] on [[Agamar]] due to the intervention of [[Kalani]], who was able to block the deactivation order<ref name="The Last Battle">{{Rebels|The Last Battle}}</ref> initiated by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] after he [[Mission to Mustafar|killed]] the [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist leaders]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> When the Empire [[Mission to Agamar (2 BBY)|arrived]] on Agamar, Kalani was able to flee with several B1 battle droids.<ref name="The Last Battle" />

===Galactic Civil War and New Republic Era===
{{Quote|I'm R0-GR— last of the B1 battle droids, nearly decorated veteran of the Clone Wars, and oft-disputed hero of the Rebellion. But you can just call me Roger.|R0-GR|<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref>}}
[[File:MisterBones-Droidography.png|thumb|left|250px|Mister Bones served in [[Norra Wexley's team]] during the Galactic Civil War.]]
Later, during the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Temmin Wexley]] owned a modified battle droid named [[Mister Bones]] who acted as a companion to him. After the [[rebellion on Akiva]] in [[4 ABY]], the two joined the [[New Republic]] and Wexley's mother [[Norra Wexley]] in the fight against the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> Mister Bones perished during the [[Battle of Jakku]]<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>

A Separatist ship was en route to Agamar, but was brought down at [[Loradil]]. The battle droids remained on the world for years. When [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Han Solo]] and his allies visited the world, the droids attacked them.<ref name="SW5">[[Star Wars (2025) 5|''Star Wars'' (2025) 5]]</ref> Using [[Phase II clone trooper armor|armor]] and weapons from clone troopers they found on the site, they fought the droids and left.<ref name="SW6">[[Star Wars (2025) 6|''Star Wars'' (2025) 6]]</ref>

In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|16}}</ref> an [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial Remnant]] led by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]] continued upon the Empire's [[Dark Trooper project]] by successfully fielding several [[Phase-III Dark Trooper]]s.<ref name="Chapter 16">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 16: The Rescue}}</ref> Considered [[third-generation battle droid]]s,<ref name="SWCEUAE">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> Gideon's [[Gideon's Dark Trooper platoon|entire platoon]] of combat droids was destroyed by the [[Jedi Master]] Luke Skywalker during the [[second rescue of Grogu]].<ref name="Chapter 16" /> At the same time, battle droids dating back to the Clone Wars, originally intended to be destroyed by the New Republic, were used on [[Plazir-15]] for mass work.<ref name="Chapter 22: Guns for Hire">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 22: Guns for Hire}}</ref>

Also during the [[New Republic Era]], the [[Acolytes of the Beyond|Acolyte of the Beyond]] [[Kiza]] came under the influence of the [[Mask of Viceroy Exim Panshard|mask]] of the long dead [[Sith]] [[Viceroy]] [[Exim Panshard]], who desperately held onto life through the mask and promised Kiza power if they reached the Sith world of [[Exegol]]. Under Panshard's guidance, Kiza collected an army of old battle droids from recent and old wars alike, keeping them inside a crashed Separatist core ship on a [[Kiza's world|large asteroid]] next to the [[Red Honeycomb Zone]], which blocked Exegol off from the wider galaxy. Kiza's droid army,<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref> which included B1 battle droids,<ref name="Adam Christopher B1 tweet">{{Twitter|ghostfinder|status/1720173066779537631|[[Adam Christopher]]|quote=Yep, definitely. I can't remember if any designations were given? {{C|In response to: "do you think any of the battle droids in Kiza's droid army were B1s :P"}}|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/ZfIPW}}</ref> was left huddled and turned off inside the core ship<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> until [[21 ABY]];<ref name="21 ABY date">The events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' takes place in [[21 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> years after any of the battle droids had last moved, Kiza modified at least some of them to each contain a fragment of a [[Bleeding|bled]] [[kyber crystal]] to lead her towards Skywalker, who had in his possession other fragments of the same crystal; just as how the battle droids could track Skywalker through the fragments in their systems, Panshard hoped that combining Skywalker's crystals with their own would lead them through the Red Honeycomb Zone to Exegol, where the larger crystal mound was kept by the [[Sith Eternal]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />

Kiza ultimately sent a mix of several different battle droids after Skywalker, but the eleven droids were destroyed during a [[skirmish on Polaar]]. When Skywalker traveled to Kiza's asteroid, he found the deactivated army aboard the core ship before beginning a [[Duel on Kiza's world|lightsaber duel]] against her. Over the course of the duel, many of the battle droids were destroyed by lightsaber strikes. The subsequent overload of the core ship's forge melted down many of the others, and Kiza herself was killed. Nine surviving battle droids, while unable to process their new programming from the kyber crystals, ensured Panshard's mask was salvaged and adorned with the kyber fragments, placing it upon Kiza's dead body before they deactivated. One of the droids also scarified an arm for its lord, ensuring that Kiza's broken arm could be replaced. However, their master soon met his [[Skirmish on Taw Provode|final end]] on [[Taw Provode's solitary moon]] when Skywalker destroyed the mask. Kiza's body and the droid arm were left limp and consumed by a native [[Snake|serpent]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />

One B1 battle droid, [[R0-GR]], came to serve under the [[Resistance]] and write "[[droidography]]," a guide to the various models of droids in [[the galaxy]], to teach [[Species|organics]] more about the droids they interacted with. R0-GR also stated that he was the last B1 battle droid, but he had learned of the existence of Mister Bones, and this led R0-GR to say that his statement might have not been entirely true.<ref name="Droidography" /> Another [[Unidentified battle droid|battle droid]] was being worked on by the [[droidsmith]] [[Babu Frik]], but he needed to abandon the unit prior to the [[destruction of Kijimi]].<ref name="TROS Expanded">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>
