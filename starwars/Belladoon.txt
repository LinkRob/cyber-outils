'''Belladoon''' was a [[Planet|world]]<ref name="LD" /> located in the [[Myto sector]]'s [[Belladoon system]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and the [[New Territories]] regions.<ref name="New Territories" /> Situated within grid square K-3 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Galaxy" /> it lay along the [[Veragi Trade Route]] [[Hyperspace route|hyperlane]]<ref name="Veragi" /> between the astronomical objects [[Dantooine]] and [[Dubrillion]].<ref name="Galaxy" /> Belladoon contributed to the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|military]]<ref name="LD" /> by [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the [[New Republic]]. In a meeting with his new Imperial [[Shadow Council]]<ref name="LD" /> that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]] spoke about the Empire using its Outer Rim holdings to its advantage, listing Belladoon, [[Korrus]], and [[Zhadalene]] as worlds there that were already under Imperial sway.<ref name="LD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Belladoon was first mentioned in the [[2016]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', the second volume in [[Chuck Wendig]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="LD" />
