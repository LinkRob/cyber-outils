The '''Droid uprising on Telti''' occurred alongside the [[Almanian Uprising]]. When [[Cole Fardreamer]], [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]] traveled to [[Telti/Legends|Telti]], the source of the [[Droid/Legends|droids]] with implanted detonators that [[Kueller]] was using against [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]], Cole was captured by [[Brakiss]], who was commanding the facility, and was sent to be interrogated by [[EV-9D9.2]]. R2 rallied a large number of old [[Astromech droid/Legends|astromech droids]] that were in storage on Telti and had them attack the [[Warden 10-24 security droid|Red Terror]], a group of modified [[Gladiator droid/Legends|gladiator droids]] that guarded the moon. Although the Red Terror destroyed many of the astromechs, their numbers were large enough to overwhelm and destroy them. R2 and C-3PO were able to find Brakiss's remote, deactivating Kueller's detonators, while the astromechs chased Brakiss to his [[Shuttle/Legends|shuttle]] and forced him to flee.
