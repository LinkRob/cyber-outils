A '''chef''' or '''cook''' was an individual who prepared [[Foodstuff|food]]. [[Dexter Jettster]], the owner of [[Dex's Diner]] on [[Coruscant]], cooked food for his customers.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> [[Strono Tuggs]], a [[Gender|male]] [[Artiodac]], served as a chef in [[Takodana Castle]],<ref name="VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and another Artiodac chef was staffed in the crew of a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]].<ref name="Crew">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Staff Cook"</ref> A [[Chef (Haven)|human chef]] was known to operate from within the [[Haven]], a refuge for defenseless citizens created by the [[Spectres]].<ref name="Recon Missions">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Recon Missions]]''</ref>
