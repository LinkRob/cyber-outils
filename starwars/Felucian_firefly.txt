'''Felucian fireflies''' were a [[species]] of [[firefly]] native to the [[planet]] [[Felucia]]. By [[34 ABY]], [[Dok-Ondar]] kept a terrarium with Felucian fireflies in his [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities|Den of Antiquities]] on the [[planet]] of [[Batuu]].<ref name="SWGE" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Luke_Roger_Rowan_Crossing_Paths.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Felucian fireflies as depicted in the series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]''.]]
The Felucian fireflies first appearead in the non-[[canon]] series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]''.<ref name="Freemakers">{{FreemakerAdventures|Crossing Paths}}</ref> The fireflies were later canonized by appearing at Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]].<ref name="SWGE" />
