'''Chargutt Lo''' was a [[Gamorrean]] who was serving a fifteen [[Standard year|year]] sentence on [[Garen IV]] for aggravated assault. The criminal's profile in the [[New Republic]] prison registry was one of several that [[Marshal]] [[Carasynthia Dune]] looked at from her office on the [[planet]] [[Nevarro]]<ref name="Mando14" /> in [[9 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|14|the appearance of Chargutt Lo's profile}}</ref> while searching for the profile of prisoner [[Migs Mayfeld]].<ref name="Mando14" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chargutt Lo's profile first appeared in "[[Chapter 14: The Tragedy]],"<ref name="Mando14" /> the sixth episode of the [[The Mandalorian Season Two|second season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', which was released on [[December 4]], [[2020]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-december-2020|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in December 2020}}</ref>
