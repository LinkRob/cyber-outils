The '''Cartel Scatter Gun''' was a [[scatter gun]] owned by the [[Trandoshan]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Charr]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Cunning Scatter Gun|standard slugthrower]]. It's color scheme resembled the overall aesthetics of the [[White Worms]] criminal organization, including featuring the word [[worm]] inscribed in [[Aurebesh]] on the side.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cartel Scatter Gun was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Charr's Cunning Scatter Gun, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Cartel Hunter]]. This wrap could be unlocked by purchasing it individually within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
