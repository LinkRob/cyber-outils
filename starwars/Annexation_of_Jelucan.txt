In [[11 BBY]], the [[Galactic Empire]] formally '''annexed''' the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Jelucan]] in the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Imperial Era]]. The planet had been allied with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]], which is why it had taken eight [[Standard year|years]] for the Empire to formalize the annexation, due to lingering mistrust. On the day of the event, there was a celebration in the [[capital]], [[Valentia]], attended by both [[First Wavers|first]] and [[second-wave]]rs. Despite their differences, both peoples supported the annexation. [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] personally attended, and gave a speech. The day of the annexation was also when first-waver [[Ciena Ree]] and second-waver [[Thane Kyrell]] met.<ref name="Lost Stars" />
