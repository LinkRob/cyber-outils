A '''Destran Ice Cap''' was a non-[[alcohol]]ic beverage sold at [[Ronto Roasters]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]]. Its ingredients included [[Sprite]], fruit-flavored syrup and cream [[cheese]].<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ronto-roasters/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Ronto Roasters: Lunch and Dinner Menu}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DestranIceCap-SWGE.jpg|thumb|Orange-tangerine variety of Destran Ice Cap]]
The Destran Ice Cap is a non-alcoholic drink sold in the [[In-universe|real world]] at Ronto Roasters in the [[Disneyland]] version of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]. It debuted a seasonal winter drink only available from [[November]] [[2022]] to [[February]] [[2023]], and originally included green [[apple]] syrup<ref name="WDWNT">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2022/11/review-destran-ice-cap-at-disneyland-2022|text=REVIEW: Celebrate Life Day in Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge With NEW 'Destran Ice Cap' Cream Cheese Soda at Disneyland|work=WDW News Today|archivedate=20221210084408}}</ref> The drink returned in early 2023, where it was served with an orange-tangerine syrup.<ref name="Magic Journeys">{{Instagram|username=magicjourneys|url=reel/CpBLD-5AWIO|name=Magic Journeys|date=2023-02-23|quote=Destran Ice Cap is no longer Apple but is now Tangerine! Taste like an Orange Creamsicle. I enjoyed the drink but I felt the sweet cream cheese was a little too much but overall satisfying. Great for a hot day ☀️ This drink is Sprite, Orange-Tangerine Syrup, topped with Sweet Cream Cheese and you can get it at Rontos Roasters.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/n1An3}}</ref> The drink returned again on [[May 4]], [[2024]], once again with a new recipe, scheduled to be available until [[August 22]].<ref name="DPB">{{DPB|url=2024/04/disney-eats-may-the-4th-foodie-guide-2024|text=Disney Eats: May the 4th Foodie Guide 2024}}</ref> This menu description read "Sprite®, [[Wikipedia:Kiwifruit|Kiwi]] Syrup with [[Wikipedia:Strawberry|Strawberry]] Sweet Cream."<ref name="Menu" /> Its name likely refers to the Batuu moon of [[Destra]].
