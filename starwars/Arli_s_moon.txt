'''Arli's moon''' was a [[moon]] that [[orbit]]ed the [[ocean]] [[planet]] [[Arli]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the family of [[Jordanna Sparkburn]], a member of the [[San Tekka clan]], resided on the moon. After the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Deployment of the stormseeds|established]] the [[Occlusion Zone]], Arli's moon fell behind the [[Stormwall]], trapping its residents, including Sparkburn's brother, [[Harth Sparkburn|Harth]]. The moon was also afflicted by what was known as the [[blight]], which slowly consumed matter and drained the life from it. Sparkburn and [[Cair San Tekka]] [[Mission to Arli's moon|visited]] Arli's moon to find the former's family, also learning of the blight. The pair rescued the remaining residents of the moon and took them to [[Seswenna]], also delivering a sample of the blight to the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Defy the Storm" />
