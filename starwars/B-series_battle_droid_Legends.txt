The '''B-series''' was a heavily mass-produced series of [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droids]] used primarily by the [[Trade Federation/Legends|Trade Federation]], [[Techno Union/Legends|Techno Union]], and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]] up to and during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].

The B-series was fielded mostly by the Separatists, who used vast numbers of these droids to overwhelm their enemies. The [[B3 ultra battle droid]] was never mass produced though.
