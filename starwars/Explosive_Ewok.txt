The '''Explosive Ewok''' was the standard outfit owned by the [[Ewok]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Pilbush]] that she wore for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Explosive Ewok was a "Base" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Pilbush, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 3.0.0 alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Original At-ET]]. This costume was unlocked by default when Pilbush became playable.<ref name="Hunters" />
