'''Controller''' was a title given to [[officer]]s. [[Boon|LT-319]] operated as a controller and the lead intelligence officer in the [[Imperial Information Office]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="DAD">{{Rebels|Double Agent Droid}}</ref><ref name="LT-319">{{Databank|controller-lt-319|Controller LT-319}}</ref> The Empire also utilized [[flight controller]]s<ref name="Beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> and [[controller droid]]s such as the [[RQ protocol droid]] oversaw operations in [[spaceport]]s on [[planet]]s including [[Lothal]].<ref name="Head to Head">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Head to Head]]''</ref>
