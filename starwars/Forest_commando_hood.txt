A '''forest commando hood''' was a type of [[Color|brown]] [[Clothing|hood]] that could protect a being's head in thick [[forest]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] found a forest commando hood in a container aboard the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A forest commando hood appeared as part of the "Forest Commando Pack" for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> The pack was released on [[November 21]], [[2024]] and was included in the game's Ultimate edition.<ref name="Ubisoft Plus">{{Ubisoft|subdomain=store|url=us/star-wars-outlaws---forest-commando-pack/66cdccf470da031a08675d25.html|text=Star Wars Outlaws - Forest Commando Pack}}</ref>
