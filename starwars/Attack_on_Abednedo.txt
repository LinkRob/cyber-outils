After the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[planet]] [[Abednedo (planet)|Abednedo]] was '''attacked''' by the [[Galactic Empire]] with a [[climate disruption array]]<ref name="Databank" /> as part of [[Operation: Cinder]]. The planet was able to barely escape destruction due to local resistance and a [[Unidentified New Republic strike team|New Republic special forces team]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

As a result of the attack, many Abednedo supported the [[New Republic]] and [[Resistance]].<ref name="Databank" />
