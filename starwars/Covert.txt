A '''covert''' was a hidden location where [[Mandalorian]] clans resided following the [[Great Purge of Mandalore]] at the hands of the [[Galactic Empire]]. During the [[New Republic Era|time]] of the [[New Republic]], the surviving Mandalorians were scattered in coverts across [[the galaxy]], preserving their way of life. One [[Nevarro Mandalorian covert|covert]], situated on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Nevarro]],<ref name="Star Wars Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> was home to the group known as [[the Tribe]],<ref name="Chapter 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> who practiced<ref name="Chapter 3" /> the ancient<ref name="Chapter 11">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}}</ref> [[Way of the Mandalore]].<ref name="Chapter 3" /> After the Nevarro covert came under [[Attack on the Tribe|attack]] from the forces of the [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] led by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]], one of its members, the [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]],<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> intended to find other Mandalorians and chart a path through the network of coverts in order to return the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Youngling|infant]] known as [[Din Grogu|Grogu]]<ref name="Chapter 9">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref> to the [[Jedi]].<ref name="Chapter 8" /> [[The Armorer]] and [[Paz Vizsla]], who were members of the Tribe that survived the remnant's attack, established a new [[Mandalorian covert|covert]] on the [[space station]] [[Glavis Ringworld]].<ref name="BoBF Chapter 5">{{BOBF|Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian}}</ref> They later relocated to [[Mandalorian secret covert|another covert]].<ref name="Chapter 17">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 17: The Apostate}}</ref>
