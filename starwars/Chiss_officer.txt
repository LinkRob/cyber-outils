'''Chiss officers''', also known as '''Chiss military officers''',<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> were [[Officer|military officers]] who served in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Chiss Defense Force|Defense Force]].<ref name="Thrawn" />

Officers promoted to [[Flag officer|flag rank]] ([[commodore]] or higher) were stripped of their [[Chiss family|family]] ties in a [[Unidentified promotion ceremony|ceremony]]. This was done so the upper echelons of the Defense Force would not be swayed or biased by family favoritism or rivalries. Chiss officers below flag rank wore black uniform, while officers with flag rank wore blazing white. The rank was displayed by [[full dress honor chains]] attached to the uniform.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
