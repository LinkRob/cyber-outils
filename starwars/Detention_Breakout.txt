"'''Detention Breakout'''" was a scenario for [[Wizards of the Coast]]'s [[Star Wars Miniatures]] in [[2007]] which was written by [[Sterling Hershey]] and released online. It focuses on a rescue mission by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]].
