"'''Ezra Bridger and Other Users of the Force'''" is a volume in the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series of reference books published by [[De Agostini]]. It was published on [[August 5]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya" />

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''The Force was a mysterious energy. Even the greatest Jedi sages did not know why some beings were susceptible to its power or not. The Force could be found anywhere in the galaxy and in any life form. A young street thief from the planet Lothal named Ezra Bridger found himself inhabited by this energy. After crossing paths with former Jedi Kanan Jarrus, Ezra turned his back on his life and began his apprenticeship. In this way, he learned about the galaxy and about himself. Ezra's role was not limited to the Galactic Civil War; he even traveled back in time, only to mysteriously disappear after actively participating in the liberation of Lothal.''<ref name="Altaya" />
