'''Chiss uniforms''' were a type of [[clothing]] used by the [[Ascendancy military]], notably including the [[Chiss Defense Force]] and the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]].

==Description==
{{Quote|I'll be armed, and I have every confidence that General Skywalker will be a powerful ally. But I believe I'll also put on my combat uniform. Just in case.|Mitth'raw'nuruodo, to Che'ri|<ref name="Chaos Rising" />}}
[[File:Senior_Captain_Thrawn.png|thumb|left|250px|As with other Chiss officers, Thrawn's uniform had a shoulder patch bearing his family's insignia.]]
Chiss uniforms had shoulder patches bearing the insignia of their owner's [[Chiss family]], as well as [[insignia pins]] on their collar indicating the owner's [[military rank]],<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> a military shoulder belt, and an [[utility belt]] holding a [[comlink]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> In the case of [[cadet]]s, the pins were absent as they had no official rank yet, although the family shoulder patch was still present.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

Chiss uniforms were colored black for cadets and officers below [[Flag officer|flag rank]], which began at the rank of [[Commodore]]. Upon promotion to flag officer, a [[Chiss officer]] was stripped of their family ties, with the [[Supreme Admiral]] declaring "The Ascendancy is your family." At that point, flag officers received new white uniforms whose shoulder patch insignia was now the [[symbol of the Chiss Ascendancy]]. As with those below flag rank, flag officers' white uniforms bore insignia pins indicating their military rank.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> In the case of [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]], a rare [[human]] [[alien]] in the service of the Chiss Ascendancy, his black uniform also bore the family shoulder patch of the Ascendancy.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> The uniforms were designed for absorbing energy and had [[armor]] plating, protecting the wearer from [[blaster]] [[Laser|fire]]. A [[holster]] on the right hip carried a [[charric]], while the one on the left carried a [[combat stick]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

==History==
During his [[Mission to Batuu|investigations]] on [[Batuu]], [[Senior Captain]]<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] wore a [[Color|black]] uniform with a burgundy patch on one shoulder and insignia pins on the collar. That uniform absorbed much of the energy of blaster bolts, impressing the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]. The burgundy shoulder patch<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> bore the sunrise insignia of Thrawn's [[Mitth family]], and the silver insignia pins denoted the rank of Senior Captain.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
