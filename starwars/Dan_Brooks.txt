'''Dan Brooks''' is a senior writer and editor on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Profile" /> Daniel also wrote the short stories "[[A Battle for the Galaxy]]" and "[[Danger in the Desert]]," published in [[5-Minute Star Wars Stories (2020)|''5-Minute Star Wars Stories'']].<ref name="5 Minute">[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories (second edition)|''5-Minute Star Wars Stories'', second edition]]</ref> He was one of the authors of the reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' published by [[Dorling Kindersley]].<ref name="CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>
