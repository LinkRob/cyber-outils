The '''0-LT utility droid''' was a model of [[maintenance droid]] in use during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. The [[droid]]s were equipped with treaded foot pads and repurposed domes from obsolete [[astromech droid]] models.<ref name="TROS VD" />
