The "'''Dantooine Tactical Report'''" was an article published on the ''Star Wars: The Old Republic'' website on [[May 3]], [[2019]]. The article presents a confidential [[Tactical Report Assessment DNT-2238.553b|assessment report]] about the planet [[Dantooine/Legends|Dantooine]] for the new update of ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Knights of the Eternal Throne]]'', serving as a prelude to the events of the [[Dantooine Incursion]].
