'''Ayo Bons''' was a [[humanoid]] [[mercenary]] who worked with [[Vect Nokru]] and [[Vidara Jurness]]<ref name="Guide">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> during the year [[9 BBY]]. The [[mercenary]] captured the [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] on the orders of the [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister]].<ref name="Part I" /> The mercenaries then took Organa to the [[planet]] [[Daiyu]] and held her hostage in order to wait for the rogue [[Jedi Master]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], to come and save her. Bons, Nokru, and Jurness ambushed Kenobi, but Kenobi escaped by using a [[spice]] he obtained earlier and took Leia to [[Mapuzo]].<ref name="Kenobi II">{{Kenobi|Part II}}</ref><ref name="Kenobi III">{{Kenobi|Part III}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The mercenary first appeared in "[[Part I]]" the first episode of the live-action series ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]''. He is portrayed by [[Aviel Ayoung]].<ref name="Part I" />
