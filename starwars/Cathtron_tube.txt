A '''cathtron tube''' was a mechanical part necessary to the operation of [[ion cannon]]s. One of the ion [[cannon]]s used by the [[Batonn insurgents]] was revealed to have been operational after it fired, with [[Commander]] [[Karyn Faro]] deducing that the insurgents did in fact have a spare cathtron tube.<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref>
