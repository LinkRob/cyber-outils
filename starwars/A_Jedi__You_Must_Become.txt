"'''A Jedi, You Must Become'''" is a short story initially published in ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories]]''. It adapts the film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="5-Minute Star Wars Stories" />

The story re-used illustrations previously done by [[Stéphane Roux]] in the [[2015]] book ''[[Use the Force!]]'' by [[Michael Siglain]],<ref name="UTF">''[[Use the Force!]]''</ref> with additional text written by [[Andy Schmidt]].<ref name="5-Minute Star Wars Stories" />
