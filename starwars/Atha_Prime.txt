'''Atha Prime''' was a [[humanoid]] [[scientist]], interested in growing creatures.  By [[32 BBY]], Atha Prime came into [[Mission to Veeros|conflict]] with [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vetna Mooncrest]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Jedi… This is a concern. What do they suspect?"<br />"''Nothing'', I believe."<br />"Pray you are correct. It's a dangerous business. ''Killing Jedi''.|Atha Prime and Blue-Tark|<ref name="Jedi Knights 2" />}}
[[File:HesReal-JediKnights2.png|thumb|left|350px|Atha Prime stands before Grand Master Yoda of the Jedi Order.]]
At some point around the late [[Republic Era]], Atha Prime became the sole resident of the remote [[moon]] [[Veeros]], building his own private [[Veeros palace|palace]] on the moon which he used as a base to conduct a variety of [[bioengineering]] experiments. These included his [[Bio-fields|bio-field operation]], a massive agricultural program that produced fields of bioengineered crops; these would become one of Atha Prime's more "renowned" public experiments. Secretly, however, in the lower levels of his palace, Atha Prime had created an army of bio-engineered creatures known as "[[Xerexi]]" which he grew out of vats.<ref name="Jedi Knights 2" />

By [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediKnightsDate}}</ref> Atha Prime appeared in a [[Force vision]] to [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] while [[Meditation|meditating]].<ref name="Jedi Knights 1">[[Jedi Knights 1|''Jedi Knights'' 1]]</ref> Soon afterwards, the [[Jedi Order]] had caught wind of a nefarious experiment being conducted on Veeros and thus [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vetna Mooncrest]] [[Mission to Veeros|were dispatched]] to confront Prime. While Prime initially had his [[droid]] [[majordomo]] [[Blue-Tark]] greet and distract the [[Jedi]] with his grand bio-fields, Yoda and Vetna quickly discovered the Xerexi vats, which prompted Prime to unleash the creatures upon them while he watched from a vantage point. When Yoda noticed Prime spectating, he used the [[the Force]] to catch up to him, Prime fought the elderly Jedi, managing to pin him to the ground before Yoda used his [[lightsaber]] to damage Prime's [[helmet]], prompting the bio-engineer to bash Yoda against a wall and retreat aboard [[Atha Prime's starship|his starship]]. Afterwards, Yoda contacted [[Mace Windu]] and several other Jedi to exterminate the remaining Xerexi on Veeros, unaware that Atha Prime had dozens more stored aboard his ship when he fled.<ref name="Jedi Knights 2" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|The thing about scalpels, Master Jedi, the deepest cut leaves the biggest scar.|Atha Prime to Yoda|<ref name="Jedi Knights 2" />}}
Atha Prime was a skilled scientist, able to design and breed the Xerexi. Despite being a scientist Prime was also a remarkable fighter, wielding his club with incredible speed and power, able to quickly defeat a legendary fighter like Grand Master Yoda to the lengths of impaling the Grand Master.<ref name="Jedi Knights 2" />

==Equipment==
Prime wore a red cloak and a [[helmet]] and [[armor]] at his shoulders and upper chest and wielded a [[staff]]. Additionally, he owned a starship.<ref name="Jedi Knights 2" />

Atha Prime first appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in a vision in the [[Jedi Knights 1|first issue]] of the [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Knights]]'',<ref name="Jedi Knights 1" /> published on [[March 5]], 2025,<ref name="Marvel 1">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/122252/star_wars_jedi_knights_2025_1|text=Star Wars: Jedi Knights (2025) #1}}</ref> before appearing in [[Jedi Knights 2|the following issue]]<ref name="Jedi Knights 2" /> on [[April 9]] of that year.<ref name="Marvel 2">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/122253/star_wars_jedi_knights_2025_2|text=Star Wars: Jedi Knights (2025) #2}}</ref> Both issue were written by [[Marc Guggenheim]] and illustrated by [[Madibek Musabekov]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]].<ref name="Jedi Knights 1" /><ref name="Jedi Knights 2" /> Prior to the release of ''Jedi Knights'', Atha Prime was pictured on a "Sneak Peak Preview" variant cover of [[Legacy of Vader 1|''Legacy of Vader'' 1]] by [[Ramon Rosanas]],<ref name="LoV1">[[Legacy of Vader 1|''Legacy of Vader'' 1]]</ref> published on [[February 5]], 2025.<ref name="Marvel LoV1">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/119562/star_wars_legacy_of_vader_2025_1|text=Star Wars: Legacy of Vader (2025) #1}}</ref>

[[Atha Prime/Legends|Atha Prime]] originated from the unproduced [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] ''[[The Epic Continues]]'' project, an original toyline planned by [[Kenner Products|Kenner]] to keep producing ''[[Star Wars]]'' action figures after the release of the [[1983]] film ''[[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi]]'', the last installment of the ''Star Wars'' [[original trilogy]]. With no more films on the horizon back then, Kenner developed a story where Prime would become [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy's]] biggest threat following [[Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine's]] [[Death/Legends|death]]. The character would proceed to engage not only [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]], and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]], but also a surviving [[Grand Moff/Legends|Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin/Legends|Wilhuff Tarkin]] and his surviving [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] forces following the [[Battle of Endor/Legends|Battle of Endor]].<ref name="topt">{{GalaxyCite|2|Galactic Bazaar|Galactic Bazaar: Tales of Phantom Toys}}</ref> Kenner based their concept art for Atha Prime on rejected concept art for the [[Emperor's Royal Guard/Legends|Emperor's Royal Guard]] done by [[Nilo Rodis-Jamero]] in [[1981]].<ref name="sobar">{{SWArchive|url=episode-i/bts/design/f20000313/index.html|text=Shades of Blue and Red}}</ref> Kenner's proposal was ultimately rejected by [[Lucasfilm]] though, who while grateful for their efforts, did not feel comfortable starting an Expanded Universe set after ''Return of the Jedi'' back then.<ref name="topt" />
