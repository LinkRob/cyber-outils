"'''Dizzy'''" was the nickname of a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], nicknamed as such by the [[pilot]]s of the [[New Republic]] due to the characteristics of their flying that distinguished them from other TIE pilots. "Dizzy" was one of the newer pilots the New Republic noted of their enemy roster following the [[Cerberon system campaign]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
