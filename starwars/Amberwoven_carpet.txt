An '''Amberwoven carpet''' was a style of [[carpet]] that was woven with strands of [[amberine]]. This type of carpet was made on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an individual named [[Lo'no]] left two rolls of carpet as a part of a stash on the roof of a building in [[city|town]] of [[Jaunta's Hope]] on Toshara.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During 3 ABY,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|Scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] found the stash and stole it.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Amberwoven carpet was created for the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. It was a trophy that could be used to customize the appearance of the player's [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
