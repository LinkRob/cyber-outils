'''Bay thirty-one''' was a [[Hangar|docking bay]] operated by the [[port control]] of the [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="QH" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Queen's Hope]]'' to [[22 BBY]].</ref> the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] [[Sabé]] and [[Tonra]] traveled to Tatooine aboard [[Freighter One Seventeen]], a [[Starship|vessel]] they had acquired. As their [[freighter]] approached the world, a [[Unidentified port control worker|port control worker]] took Sabé and Tonra's permits and told them to land at bay thirty-one. After preparing their ship for descent, the pair disembarked at the docking bay.<ref name="QH" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bay thirty-one first appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Queen's Hope]]'',<ref name="QH" /> a [[2022]] young-adult novel written by [[E. K. Johnston]]<ref name="Disney">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/queens-hope|text=Queen's Hope}}</ref> as the third installment of ''[[Star Wars: Queen's Series]]''.<ref name="QH" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the bay was first introduced in "[[A Bith Saved Is A Bith Learned]]," an adventure included in the [[West End Games]] source book ''[[Galaxy Guide 7: Mos Eisley]]'', written by [[Martin Wixted]]<ref name="GG7">{{WEGCite|book=Galaxy Guide 7: Mos Eisley|story=A Bith Saved Is A Bith Learned}}</ref> and published [[January]]&ndash;[[February]] [[1993]].<ref name="Copyright">The copyright text for ''[[Galaxy Guide 7: Mos Eisley]]'' states that it was published in 1993, and ''[[Galaxy Guide 8: Scouts]]'' was published in [[March]] [[1993]] per {{BN|url=w/star-wars-the-roleplaying-game-west-end-games-incorporated/1018175929|text=Star Wars the Roleplaying Game: Galaxy Guide #8: Scouts}}</ref>
