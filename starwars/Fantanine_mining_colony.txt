The '''Fantanine mining colony''' was a [[mining]] operation ran by the [[Ben Diffle Fantanines]], members of the [[Fantanine]] [[species]]. The [[Unidentified planet (Fantanines)|planet]] it was located on was strip-mined for resources, and its [[Unidentified species (Fantanines)|native species]] exported to facilities such as the [[Orgo-harvest facility]] on [[Dannamore]].<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />
