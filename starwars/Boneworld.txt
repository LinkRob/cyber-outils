The '''Boneworld''' was a far-off terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Blade 1" /> in the [[Boneworld system]] in the [[Toblain sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> with rocky terrain that was covered in massive [[skeleton]]s. It was usually visited mainly for its supply of [[ambercryst]] and fossilized [[bone]] marrow, both of which were very valuable.<ref name="Blade 1" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The present day events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic — The Blade]]'' take place between the [[wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran]] and [[Porter Engle]] [[Siege of Bardotta|earning]] his title the "Blade of Bardotta," both of which are dated to [[382 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> [[Jedi Master]]s<ref name="THR show">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-high-republic-show-204-reveals|text=Sav Malagán Returns and More Concept Art on ''Star Wars: The High Republic Show''}}</ref> [[Porter Engle]] and [[Barash Silvain]] visited the planet, and Engle [[Chef|cooked]] [[stew]] for some of its inhabitants using ingredients he found there. But after settling a dispute over ambercryst between a [[Twi'lek]] [[Unidentified Twi'lek (Dregg)|individual]] and [[Nautolan]] named [[Ralen]], the two Jedi left the world.<ref name="Blade 1" />
