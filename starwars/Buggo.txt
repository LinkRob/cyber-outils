'''Buggo''' was a [[Gender|male]] member of [[Lourna Dee]]'s [[Lourna Dee's Tempest|Tempest]] and a [[Strike]] working under [[Cloud (rank)|Cloud]] [[Dent (Nihil)|Dent]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He was the [[Marriage|husband]] of Dent's [[Dent's second cousin|second cousin]]. Buggo was present at the [[Raid on Elphrona]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> in [[232 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> He was killed at the hands of Jedi [[Indeera Stokes]] while she was rescuing [[Ronn Blythe]] from Margrona's [[starship]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
