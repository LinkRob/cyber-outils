'''Deluxe nutmeat and jelly sandwich''' was a type of [[Sandwich/Legends|sandwich]] served in [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. Costing 4.5 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]], it was prepared with freshly ground [[ettel nut]]s and gourmet [[Andorian jelly/Legends|Andorian jelly]] and served on bleached [[Pseudograin bread]]. According to [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|Dexter Jettster]], it was a childhood favorite among many patrons.
