A '''fisher''',<ref name="Solo: EE">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> also known as an '''angler''',<ref name="Aftermath: Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> '''fisherman''', or a '''fisherfolk''',<ref name="Jedi: Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> was an individual or [[droid]]<ref name="Aftermath: Life Debt" /> who engaged in [[fishing]] as their occupation<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> or primary role within their culture. On the [[planet]] [[Ahch-To]], the [[Gender|male]] [[Lanai]]&mdash;known as [[Visitors]]&mdash;spent most [[Standard day|days]] of each [[Standard month|month]] catching [[fish]] in the [[water]]s covering the planet.<ref name="TLJ: VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Many businesses in [[Scaparus Port]] on [[Arkanis]] specifically served the local fisherfolk.<ref name="The Secret Academy">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref> [[Peka]] community inhabitants on [[Batuu]] often worked as [[farmer]]s or fishermen.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> A [[Keredian]] called [[Dewi Pamular]] worked alongside an [[alien]] called [[Freedi Pamular]] as a fisher<ref name="Andor11">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> in [[5 BBY]],<ref name="5 BBY date">{{AndorDate|111}}</ref> visiting the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[prison]] [[moon]]<ref name="Narkina 5 Audio">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> [[Narkina 5]] with their [[TUG-b13]] to fish for [[Squiggly|squigglies]]. They, however, found that the waters were polluted with waste from the moon's [[Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex|Prison Complex]], which greatly diminished squiggly populations.<ref name="Andor11" />

[[Coronet City]] on [[Corellia]] had longstanding fish markets, some of which were operated across multiple generations of a single family, where fishmongers sold [[species]] of fish from that planet's [[ocean]]s.<ref name="Solo: TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> [[Corellian]] fishers utilized [[harpoon]]s.<ref name="Solo: EE" />
