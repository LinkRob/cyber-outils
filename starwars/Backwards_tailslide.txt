The '''backwards tailslide''' was a [[starfighter]] maneuver in which the user would briefly drift next to an [[asteroid]] before throttling their [[Starship|ship]] and blasting off away from the asteroid. A [[pilot]] would perform the maneuver by piloting their starfighter into an arc-like movement, avoiding the asteroid themselves and causing pursuers to crash. The maneuver was utilized by both [[Resistance]] [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] and Resistance [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] during the [[investigation of a G-class freighter]] in an [[Castilon asteroid field|asteroid field]] near the [[planet]] of [[Castilon]] in [[34 ABY]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Ha, see what I did there? Adjust the stabilizers, engage the throttle, and pull back on the stick.''"<br />"''I can do that in my sleep.|Poe Dameron and Kazuda Xiono|audio=PoeDemonstratesTailslide-SignalFromSectorSix.flac|<ref name="SignalSector6" />}}
[[File:BackwardsTailslideXWing2.jpg|thumb|left|300px|A TIE fighter is destroyed after an X-wing starfighter pilot performs the backwards tailslide.]]
The backwards tailslide was an evasive [[starfighter]] tactic utilized to redirect pursuing combatants into [[asteroid]]s, primarily by [[pilot]]ing a fighter into an arc above an asteroid. The maneuver was performed by first adjusting the fighter's [[stabilizer]]s and then engaging the throttle before pulling back on the [[Starship|ship's]] control stick, causing it to arc upward, briefly drifting next to the asteroid before blasting off and forcing pursuing craft to crash into the asteroid. However, failing the maneuver could also result in the starfighter itself colliding with the asteroid and thus scraping or otherwise damaging its [[hull]].<ref name="SignalSector6" /> The maneuver could also be performed with slight modifications.<ref name="Sector6EpisodeGallery">{{SW|url=star-wars-resistance/signal-from-sector-six-episode-gallery|text="Signal From Sector Six" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 8}}</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|Don't worry CB-23. I know what to do.''"<br />[CB-23 warbles]<br />"''Trust me. I can do this! I hope.|Kazuda Xiono and [[CB-23]]|audio=KazPerformsTailslide-SignalFromSectorSix.flac|<ref name="SignalSector6" />}}
[[File:BackwardsTailslide-SignalFromSectorSix.gif|thumb|right|300px|Kazuda Xiono successfully performs the backwards tailslide.]]
At some point, a pilot flying a [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] outmaneuvered a [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]] via the backwards tailslide, causing the [[TIE series|TIE fighter]] to collide with a nearby asteroid and explode.<ref name="XwingTailslide">{{FFGXW2|set=Heralds of Hope Squadron Pack|cardname=Backwards Tailslide}}</ref> T-70 X-wings were especially adept at performing the maneuver, and notable pilots skilled in the maneuver included [[Temmin Wexley]] and [[C'ai Threnalli]].<ref name="FightToFinish">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2020/9/23/fight-to-the-finish|text=Fight to the Finish}}</ref> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Cold War]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[New Republic]],<ref name="SignalSector6" /> the [[Resistance pilot]] and [[Commander]]<ref name="PoeRank">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref> [[Poe Dameron]] demonstrated the maneuver to [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] using a T-70 [[X-wing starfighter]] in an [[Castilon asteroid field|asteroid field]] near the [[planet]] [[Castilon]]. Xiono attempted to replicate the maneuver in his own X-wing twice but failed both times, scraping his starfighter on asteroids. Xiono stated after the first attempt that he had gone too fast and not given himself enough [[time]] to arc, while on the second attempt, Dameron told him that he was overthinking it.<ref name="SignalSector6" /> He attempted a slightly-customized<ref name="Sector6EpisodeGallery" /> version of the maneuver once more when the two were [[Investigation of a G-class freighter|ambushed]] by [[Warbird gang]] [[pirate]]s and forced to split up. Xiono successfully used the tactic to outmaneuver the pirate pursuing him, after which he escaped alongside Dameron as the remaining pirate also fled.<ref name="SignalSector6" />

==Behind the scenes==
The backwards tailslide first appeared in "[[Signal from Sector Six]],"<ref name="SignalSector6" /> the seventh episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' animated television series which premiered on [[November 11]], [[2018]].<ref name="AirDate">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> The maneuver was first previewed on [[September 14]] in a trailer to introduce the [[Ace Squadron]], which was posted on the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[:Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel.<ref name="The Aces">{{SWYouTube|MDtY2vVlfaQ|The Aces &#124; Star Wars Resistance|sw_url=video/disneyxd-meet-the-aces-star-wars-resistance|date=2018-09-14}}</ref> It was indentified as the backwards tailslide when the episode premiered.<ref name="SignalSector6" />
