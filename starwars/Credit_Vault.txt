'''Credit Vaults''' were structures utilized by both the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. They were used to store [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] earned during the war effort that could be used to enhance various military bases owned by both factions. These vaults could be upgraded to increase their defenses, as well as their storage capacity.<ref name="Commander" />
