The '''B1-NA blaster pistol''',<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> also known as a '''Corpo blaster''', was one of several types of [[blaster pistol]]s used by [[Tac-Corpo]] [[trooper]]s of the [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces|Pre-Mor Enforcement]], [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]]'s security branch. Although produced by [[BlasTech Industries]], they had a Preox-Morlana corporate branding and serial number printed on them, and featured a detachable sight.<ref name="The Axe Forgets" />

In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="5BBY">{{AndorDate|103}}</ref> [[Syril Karn]] carried one such [[blaster]]&mdash;designated [[AD-616K6KA]]&mdash;while he was part of a [[Pre-Mor Security Inspection team]] attempting to find and arrest [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] while he was hiding on [[Ferrix]].<ref name="Reckon">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref> Karn's blaster was stolen by Andor, who carried the weapon for a time. During the preparation for a [[Aldhani heist|mission]] on [[Aldhani]], [[Arvel Skeen]] became suspicious of Andor after seeing the corporate-issue blaster and questioned Andor as to whom it belonged to.<ref name="The Axe Forgets" /> After the mission, Andor stashed the weapon away in a hidden box on [[Niamos]], where it was left after he was arrested and detained in the [[Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex]].<ref name="Announcement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref> When Andor later returned to the planet with [[Ruescott Melshi]] after their [[Narkina 5 prison escape|escape]] from [[Narkina 5]], he retrieved the box and handed the blaster off to Melshi before the two parted ways.<ref name="DF">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> In [[2 BBY]], the same blaster was recognized by [[Vel Sartha]] during a training instruction at the [[Great Temple|Rebel base on Yavin IV]]. As she inquired about the owner of the blaster, Melshi identified himself to her.<ref name="Welcome to the Rebellion">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref>
