'''Class four droids''',  also known as '''fourth class droids'''<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> or '''fourth-degree droids''',<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> were [[droid]]s skilled in security and military functions.<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" />

==Models==
Fourth-degree models included [[Baktoid Combat Automata]]'s<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> [[B2-series super battle droid]]s,<ref name="Propaganda" /> [[tactical droid]]s, and [[BX-series droid commando]]s,<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> all of which were classed as [[battle droid]]s.<ref name="USW" /><ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref><ref name="TCWRookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Most fourth class droids were not programmed to speak and interact with organic beings. The [[KX-series security droid]] was an exception, though it was not proficient when compared with a [[protocol droid]],<ref name="Rebel Dossier">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''</ref> whose capabilities were similar to that of the [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|T-series]] and [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|its successor]], both of which were tactical droid models used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederacy]].<ref name="USW" />
