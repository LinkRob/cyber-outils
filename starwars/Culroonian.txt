The '''Culroonians''' were the inhabitants of the astronomical object [[Culroon III]].<ref name="So" /> Shortly before the [[Battle of Hoth]]<ref name="Ships">{{ShipsandVehiclesCite|5|Pilots and Crew Members}}</ref> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> a [[Culroon III campaign|campaign]]<ref name="GEG">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref> between the Culroonians and Imperial forces took place on Culroon III<ref name="So" /> to put down a revolt there.<ref name="Helmet">{{HelmetCollectionCite|65|Databank A-Z}}</ref> The [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Maximilian Veers]] fought the Culroonians on behalf of the Empire, demonstrating cunning, courage,<ref name="So" /> and leadership in the battle,<ref name="Ships" /> which he won through the use of new tactics.<ref name="Helmet" /> These attributes led to his eventual promotion to the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[general]] in the [[Imperial Army]].<ref name="So" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Culroonians were first mentioned in [[canon]] in [[Adam Gidwitz]]'s [[2015]] children's book ''[[The Empire Strikes Back: So You Want to Be a Jedi?]]'', a retelling of the<ref name="So" /> [[1980]]<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'' Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="So" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the inhabitants of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Culroon III/Legends|Culroon III]] were a [[Species/Legends|species]] called the [[Culroon]], which first appeared in the short story "[[The Promotion of Lieutenant Veers]]," published in ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'' in [[1987]]. The story depicted an [[insurrection on Culroon III|ambush]] of [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] forces committed by the Culroon that was repelled by [[Lieutenant/Legends|Lieutenant]] [[Maximilian Veers/Legends|Maximilian Veers]], who was promoted to [[Major/Legends|major]] for his actions.<ref name="Promotion">{{WEGCite|story=The Promotion of Lieutenant Veers|book=The Star Wars Sourcebook}}</ref>
