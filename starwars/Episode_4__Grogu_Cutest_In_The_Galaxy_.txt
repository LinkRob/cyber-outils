The '''fourth episode''' of the animated web series ''[[Grogu Cutest In The Galaxy]]'' released on [[January 14]], [[2025]]. It was posted by ''[[Star Wars]]'' Japan on [[Wikipedia:Instagram Reels|Instagram Reels]].<ref name="Instagram" />

{{Media-stub}}

==Plot summary==
While [[Din Djarin]] and [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] are traveling through [[realspace]] on [[Din Djarin's N-1 starfighter|Djarin's N-1 starfighter]], Grogu knocks on the window dome to get Din's attention, to which Din replies, "No". Grogu knocks again on the glass, to Din's frustration. Din then speeds up the fighter, slightly disorienting Grogu.
