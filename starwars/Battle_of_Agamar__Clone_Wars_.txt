Around [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''battle''' occurred<ref name="The Last Battle" /> on the [[planet]] [[Agamar]]<ref name="Build R2 51" /> during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Databank" /> and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]].<ref name="The Last Battle" /> Prior to the battle, the [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]] [[General]] [[Kalani]] was ordered by Separatist [[Head of State|leader]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]], to await deployment<ref name="Build R2 30">{{BuildR2Cite|30|Droid Directory}}</ref> at Agamar alongside the remaining forces from the government's [[Onderonian Civil War|defeat]] at<ref name="Tipping Points">{{TCW|Tipping Points}}</ref> the planet<ref name="Onderon">{{SWE|locations|onderon|Onderon}}</ref> [[Onderon]]<ref name="Tipping Points" /> in [[20 BBY]].<ref name="20 BBY">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the closing events of the [[Onderonian Civil War]], including [[Kalani]]'s dispatchment to [[Agamar]], to [[20 BBY]].</ref> Above Agamar, however, Kalani's [[Unidentified DH-Omni Support Vessel (Agamar)|DH-Omni Support Vessel]] was attacked by Republic forces<ref name="Databank" /> and crashed on the planet's surface.<ref name="Build R2 30" />

Not long after the engagements, following the activation of [[Order 66]], which forced the Republic's [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] to [[Death penalty|execute]] the [[Jedi Order]], a command was issued by<ref name="Build R2 51" /> the [[Sith apprentice]]<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] that ordered the deactivation of the [[Separatist Droid Army]]. However, assuming that it was a Republic trick, Kalani refused the order and left his [[battle droid]]s online, and they remained on Agamar's surface<ref name="Build R2 51" /> for [[2 BBY|seventeen years]]<ref name="The Last Battle date">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' states that the events of "[[The Last Battle]]" occur seventeen [[Standard year|years]] after the end of the [[Clone Wars]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, the events of ''The Last Battle'' must take place in [[2 BBY]].</ref> before being discovered during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Build R2 51" />

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Salvage the remaining arms and withdraw the army. Bring them to Agamar.|Dooku, to Kalani|<ref name="Tipping Points" />}}
During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Tipping Points">{{TCW|Tipping Points}}</ref> in [[20 BBY]],<ref name="20 BBY">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the closing events of the [[Onderonian Civil War]], including [[Kalani]]'s dispatchment to [[Agamar]], to [[20 BBY]].</ref> the [[Separatist Droid Army]] had suffered several losses<ref name="Front Runners">{{TCW|Front Runners}}</ref> on the [[planet]]<ref name="Onderon">{{SWE|locations|onderon|Onderon}}</ref> [[Onderon]] during their [[Onderonian Civil War|civil war]] with a [[Onderon rebels|group]] of rebels. In response, the Separatist [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]], [[General]] [[Kalani]] was dispatched to the planet in order to defeat the resistance fighters by Separatist [[Head of State]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]].<ref name="Front Runners" /> However, following additional losses, Kalani was ordered by Dooku to withdraw the remaining forces from the planet and bring them to the planet [[Agamar]]<ref name="Tipping Points" /> to await deployment.<ref name="Build R2 30">{{BuildR2Cite|30|Droid Directory}}</ref>

==The battle==
[[File:General-Kalani-Shatterpoint.png|thumb|right|250px|General Kalani (pictured) was the commander of droid forces during the battle.]]
Around the [[time]] of [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> while awaiting further instructions, Kalani's [[Unidentified DH-Omni Support Vessel (Agamar)|DH-Omni Support Vessel]] came under assault from Republic forces<ref name="Databank" /> above Agamar. As a result, amid the battle, the [[Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel|supply ship]] crashed onto the planet, which left the [[droid]] general and his [[battle droid]]s abandoned on Agamar's surface while the Clone Wars proceeded to its closure.<ref name="Build R2 30" /> [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] was aware of the battle and the massive number of battle droids active on Agamar itself during the war.<ref name="The Last Battle" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Send a message to the ships of the Trade Federation. All droid units must shut down immediately.|Darth Sidious, ordering the droid army's deactivation|<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>}}
Sometime after the Agamar engagements,<ref name="Build R2 51" /> the [[Jedi Order]] was [[Great Jedi Purge|eradicated]] across [[the galaxy]] as the [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] received orders from [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]&mdash;secretly the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Sidious&mdash;to [[Death penalty|execute]] their [[Jedi General]]s in accordance with [[Order 66]]. Following the beginning of the Jedi's purge from the [[galaxy]], the [[Sith apprentice]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] issued an immediate deactivation command to the [[Trade Federation]], which ordered the shut-down of all battle droid units within the [[Separatist Droid Army]], after his [[Mission to Mustafar|slaughtering]] of the [[Executive Separatist Council]] on the planet [[Mustafar]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> In assumption of it being a Republic deception, however, Kalani refused the order and neglected the deactivation of himself and his droid [[Agamar garrison|garrison]] on Agamar.<ref name="Build R2 51" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle of Agamar was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[The Last Battle]],"<ref name="The Last Battle" /> the sixth episode of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Three|third season]] of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' television series,<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/the-last-battle-episode-guide|text="The Last Battle" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars Rebels}}</ref> which aired on [[October 22]], [[2016]].<ref name="Zap2it The Last Battle">{{WebCite|url=http://tvlistings.zap2it.com/tv/star-wars-rebels-the-last-battle/EP018796240061?aid=zap2it|text=Star Wars Rebels : ''The Last Battle''|work=[http://tvlistings.zap2it.com Screener]|archivedate=20170704001134}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Mirgoshir campaign]] was first indirectly mentioned in the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (video game)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'']], a [[2002]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] published by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="TCW Game">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (video game)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' video game]]</ref> It was later identified in the [[StarWars.com]] [[Databank (original)|Databank]] entry for the [[Unstable Terrain Artillery Transport/Legends|Unstable Terrain Artillery Transport]].<ref name="UT-AT">{{DB|vehicle|utat|Unstable Terrain Artillery Transport (UT-AT)}}</ref>
