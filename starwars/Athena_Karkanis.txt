'''Athena Karkanis''' is a [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice artist]] who voiced the [[Gender|female]] [[Jedi Consular]] in the [[2011]] [[BioWare]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''<ref name="TOR credits">{{TORcite|type=other|Credits}}</ref> and its expansions.<ref name="TOR rothc">{{TORcite|exp=rothc|type=other|Credits}}</ref> Karkanis went on to perform the [[Twi'lek]] [[contract broker]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> [[Eleera Soi]]<ref name="Sports Illustrated" /> in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
