'''Foss''' was an [[admiral]] in the [[Imperial Academy]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] informed [[Captain]] [[Voss Parck]] that [[Imperial cadet]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] and the [[Chiss]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] were to be admitted into the [[Royal Imperial Academy]]. Parck acknowledged and informed Foss of the change. Foss later sent a message to [[Commandant]] [[Deenlark]] of the Royal [[Imperial Academy]], informing him that Thrawn was already a capable [[soldier]], and only needed an orientation in Imperial procedures, equipment, and terminology. Foss also instructed Deenlark that Thrawn was to graduate with a rank of a [[lieutenant]], instead of the usual rank of an [[ensign]], in order to get him to a command position more quickly.<ref name="Thrawn" />
