'''Dani Gammill''' was a [[Zeltron]]. They attended the [[third Decennial Imperial Jubilee]] in [[2 BBY]] along with the [[Rodian]] [[Chihdo Jo]]. Sometime by [[4 ABY]], Gammill and Jo were recruited to work for the [[Hutt]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]. 

==Biography==
In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Legacy">{{StoryCite|story=The Grand Imperial Jubilee|book=A New Legacy 1}}</ref> Dani Gammill,<ref name="Rev" /> along with<ref name="Legacy" /> the [[Rodian]] [[Chihdo Jo]],<ref name="Rev" /> attended the [[third Decennial Imperial Jubilee]], an event to showcase [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] art collection on the<ref name="Legacy" /> [[Core Worlds|Core]] [[planet]]<ref name="SWE" /> [[Alderaan]]. There, Gammill took notice of the [[stormtrooper]] [[Bil]], whom had become possessed by [[Darth Momin]] after wearing the<ref name="Legacy" /> [[Sith Lord]]'s<ref name="SWE" /> [[Mask of Lord Momin|mask]].<ref name="Legacy" /> Sometime by [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">"[[Duel of the Reprobates]]" takes place prior to the [[death]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref> [[Bib Fortuna]],<ref name="Rev" /> [[majordomo]] to the [[Hutt]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]],<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> selected Gammill, whom Fortuna considered qualified, as part of a team for a challenging job on behalf of Jabba, along with the Jo. After reprobate [[Rik Duel]] completed a few missions, Fortuna recruited him to the team. Upon introducing Duel to his other teammates on<ref name="Rev" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet<ref name="SWE" /> [[Tatooine]], Gammill and Jo, Duel expressed distaste. Fortuna was satisfied the trio knew each other already.<ref name="Rev" /> 

Sometime during the [[New Republic Era]], [[New Republic]] [[Captain]] [[Carson Teva]] authored a survival compendium titled "[[Species From A to Z]]." Teva included an image of Gammill for the entry on Zeltrons.<ref name="Zeltron" />

==Personality and traits==
Dani Gammill was a [[Zeltron]] with [[Color|pink]] [[hair]] and skin.<ref name="Zeltron" />

==Equipment==
At the Imperial Jubilee, Gamill wore a black [[suit]], [[belt]] [[Limb|arm]] bands, and [[glove]]s. On Tatooine, the Zeltron wore a blue [[Clothing|suit]] with white gloves, [[boot]]s, and a headband.<ref name="Rev" />
