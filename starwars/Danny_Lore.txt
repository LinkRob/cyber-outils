'''Danny Lore''' is an [[Wikipedia:American people|American]] writer and editor.<ref name="Website" /> They wrote "[[Tales of Villainy: Trade Relations (Star Wars Adventures 8)|Tales of Villainy: Trade Relations]]," the secondary story featured in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2020) 8|eighth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic series]] [[Star Wars Adventures (2020)|''Star Wars Adventures'']] from [[IDW Publishing]]''.<ref name="Comic">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=8|story=Tales of Villainy: Trade Relations (Star Wars Adventures 8)}}</ref>'' Lore also wrote the canon short story [[The Burden of Leadership|"The Burden of Leadership"]] as part of the anthology book ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]],'' featuring [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]''.''
