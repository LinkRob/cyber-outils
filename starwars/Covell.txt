[[Captain]] '''Covell''' was a [[human]] [[Imperial officer]] that served the [[Galactic Empire]] and was stationed on [[Toshara]], a [[moon]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], in [[3 ABY]]. That [[Standard year|year]], he came into possession of a [[Crimson Dawn]] [[datapad]] containing sensitive information about the criminal organization.

==Biography==
{{Quote|I've got a Crimson Dawn datapad back at the Imperial compound.''"<br/>"''Didn't think to bring it with you?''"<br/>"''Tell Gorak what I can offer, if he'll cut me in on his deals.|Covell and to Kay Vess|audio=IveGotACrimsonDawnDatapad-Outlaws.flac|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Covell had joined the [[Galactic Empire]] and achieved the rank of [[captain]] in the [[Imperial Officer Corps]]. He was stationed on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> where he came into possession of a [[datapad]] containing information about the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime syndicate. Realizing that he could sell the information to the rival [[Pyke Syndicate]], Covell stored the data at the [[Imperial Forward Base]] on Toshara and traveled to the [[Pyke market]] in Toshara's [[capital]] [[city]], [[Mirogana]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
[[File:ImperialForwardBase-Toshara-Outlaws.jpg|thumb|right|230px|Covell stored the datapad at an Imperial base on Toshara.]]
Covell eventually approached the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] in the market, knowing that the young thief had connections with the [[Pyke]] Syndicate. Covell proceeded to inform Vess of the Crimson Dawn datapad he had at the Imperial base and commanded Vess to pass along their willingness to exchange the information for a cut of deals made by [[Gorak Palas]], the Pyke's [[underboss]] on Toshara. Refusing to [[Currency|pay]] Vess for passing along the message, Covell ended the conversation by invoking his name and [[military rank]]. Vess later infiltrated the Imperial facility and stole the tablet for herself before facing the decision of whether she would be best served selling Covell's information to the Pyke Syndicate or back to Crimson Dawn.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|People like me don't pay people like you.|Covell to Kay Vess|audio=PeopleLikeMeDontPayPeopleLikeYou-Outlaws.flac|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
Covell was a human with [[Color|dark]] skin, black [[hair]], and brown [[eye]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Skills and abilities==
He spoke [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]] with a [[Core Worlds]] accent.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Equipment==
While at the Pyke Market, Covell wore his [[Imperial officer's uniform]], an [[Imperial kepi]], black [[glove]]s, and a [[rank insignia plaque]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Covell appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, interacting with Covell will start the intel quest "Impressing Gorak." During the quest, Vess has the option of taking the datapad to the Gorak, thus increasing their reputation with the Pykes, or instead taking it to the [[Twi'lek]] [[Eleera Soi]] and increasing their reputation with Crimson Dawn. Regardless of the player's choices, Vess will not mention Covell's name to either of the syndicate leaders, despite his initial demands.<ref name="Outlaws" />
