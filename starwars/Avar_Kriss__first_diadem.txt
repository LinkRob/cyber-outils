[[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] owned a '''diadem''' during the [[High Republic Era]]. It was a gift from her friends [[Stellan Gios]] and [[Elzar Mann]] when she was [[Knighting Ceremony|knighted]]. It was ornated with a [[Color|blue-green]] gem. While being trapped inside the [[Nihil]] [[Occlusion Zone]], Kriss sold the diadem to buy food to help people on [[Merisee]]. After her escape from Nihil space, Elzar Mann gave her a [[Avar Kriss' second diadem|new diadem]].<ref name="TotF" />

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The [[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 1|first issue]] of the [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi]]'' incorrectly depicts Avar Kriss wearing her first diadem,<ref name="THR1" /> which ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'' had previously stated she had sold and features Kriss being given a new [[Avar Kriss' second diadem|diadem]] by [[Elzar Mann]].<ref name="TotF" />
