'''"Wessex-head" bit-drivers''' were a type of [[Pilex bit driver]] which could be used for [[Scavenger|scavenging]]. Around thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], the scavenger [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] carried a set of Pilex bit drivers including both "Wessex-head" and [["Blissex-head" bit-driver]]s in her satchel of tools while she lived on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. She at some point wrote about these and her other tools in her [[Rey's survival guide|survival guide]].<ref name="RSG" /> After she [[Escape from Jakku|fled]] Jakku with the former [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Finn]] and the [[BB-series astromech droid|BB unit]] [[BB-8]] on the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', Rey discovered that the [[starship]]'s [[motivator]] needed repair. While fixing it she had Finn pass her one of her Pilex drivers, which he could not find until BB-8 pointed it out amongst her tools.<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
"Wessex-head" bit drivers first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], which was released in [[2015]]. It was not identified by the film<ref name="TFA" /> but was named simultaneously in ''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]''<ref name="RSG" /> and ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'',<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> two reference books written by [[Jason Fry]]<ref name="RSG" /> and [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TFAVD" /> respectively for release in conjunction with ''The Force Awakens''. It is unclear which of the two types of bit driver Finn passes Rey onboard the ''Falcon'',<ref name="TFA" /> but as the visual dictionary shows props for both this article assumes both types were in the film.<ref name="TFAVD" /> The "Blissex-head" and "Wessex-head" bit-drivers are a reference to the [[Blissex-Wessex family]], a family of prominent starship designers in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="EGTW">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref>
