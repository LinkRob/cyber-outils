'''Delaya''' was a location<ref name="Forged">''[[Forged in Battle]]''</ref> in the [[Alderaan system]],<ref name="Fry" /> a [[star system]] in the [[Alderaan sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Explorer's Guide" /> Like the nearby<ref name="Forged" /> [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] and the rest of the system, Delaya was situated along the [[Commenor Run]] [[hyperspace route]],<ref name="Dawn" /> and was situated in grid square M-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> [[Belaya Rist]], the [[Chief Executive Officer]] of [[CryonCorp]] and a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] sympathizer, witnessed the [[destruction of Alderaan]] from Delaya. In the aftermath of Alderaan's destruction, Rist invited the Alliance to hijack several shipments of her company's [[low-feedback scanner]]s.<ref name="Forged" />

==Behind the scenes==
Delaya was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] by the [[2016]] sourcebook ''[[Forged in Battle]]'', a supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="Forged" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Delaya/Legends|Delaya]] was a [[Planet/Legends|planet]] in the [[Alderaan system/Legends|Alderaan system]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> It was first mentioned in ''[[Galaxy Guide 3: The Empire Strikes Back]]'', a [[1989]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplement authored by [[Michael Stern]] for use with [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Galaxy Guide 3">''[[Galaxy Guide 3: The Empire Strikes Back]]''</ref> Delaya then made its first appearance in the [[1998]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']].<ref name="Star Wars: Rebellion">[[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']]</ref>
