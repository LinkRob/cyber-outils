An [[Evereni]] [[Gender|male]] '''individual''' lived during the [[High Republic Era]] and was a member of the [[Nihil]]<ref name="Eye of the Storm 1" /> [[pirate]]s.<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Standard year|Years]] before<ref name="Eye of the Storm 1" /> [[252 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The part of [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]] which mentions [[Asgar Ro]]'s [[Parent|father]] takes place twenty [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[232 BBY]].</ref> they [[Reproduction|had]] a [[Ro family|son]]&mdash;[[Asgar Ro]]&mdash;with the woman [[Shalla Ro]].<ref name="Eye of the Storm 1" /> By 252 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> they had left the family and Nihil to ensure that [[the galaxy]] continued to fear the Evereni [[Species|people]].<ref name="Eye of the Storm 1" />

That year,<ref name="Date" /> Shalla and Asgar clashed about their plans for the group while aboard the Nihil<ref name="Eye of the Storm 1" /> [[flagship]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> ''[[Gaze Electric]]''. During their argument, Shalla suggested that it was perhaps [[time]] for her son to leave and help spread fear of the Evereni like his [[Parent|father]] and [[Asgar Ro's grandfather|grandfather]] had and make some [[Death|kills]]. Asgar responded that the latter was a good idea and pushed his mother over a railing, which resulted in her death.<ref name="Eye of the Storm 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Asgar Ro's father was mentioned in flashbacks in the [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|first issue]] of the [[2022]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Eye of the Storm]]'' released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Eye of the Storm 1" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]]. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 12]], 2022.<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
