'''Brendok''',<ref name="Brendok DB" /> or '''Brendock''',<ref name="Getty">{{Getty|subdomain=assets.dam|url=shhvmqrz3tm66g4gs4xn8znz/v/1195115777/original/THE%20ACOLYTE%20Production%20Brief%20FINAL%205-01-24.pdf?Expires=1733467677&KeyName=gcs-bfldr-prod&Signature=j9R4EDHEDFchnAWLwIuynqAkUog=|text=THE ACOLYTE Production Brief FINAL 5-01-24|date=2024-05-28}}</ref> was a mysterious [[planet]]<ref name="Brendok DB" /> located in [[Wild Space]].<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> During the [[Emergences]]<ref name="Brendok DB" /> in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the planet was rendered lifeless but life had mysteriously returned to the planet<ref name="Choice">{{Acolyte|Choice}}</ref> by [[148 BBY]].<ref name="Acolyte_timeline">The events of "Choice" take place sixteen [[Standard year|years]] before the present day events of ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', which occur in [[132 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:TheAcolyteDate|here]].</ref> A Jedi expedition led by [[Jedi Master]] [[Indara]] was sent to investigate the cause of this mysterious rejuvenation, which was attributed to a [[vergence]] in [[the Force]].<ref name="Choice" /> The Jedi encountered [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Aniseya]] and her [[Brendok coven|coven]], leading to a conflict that destroyed the coven and the adoption of survivor [[Verosha Aniseya]] by the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Choice" /><ref name="Destiny" /> Osha's sister [[Mae-ho Aniseya]] vowed revenge on the Jedi involved in her coven's demise.<ref name="Destiny" />
