The '''Cerroban system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Bon'nyuw-Luq sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square N-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Cerroban system originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, and was recanonized when it was included in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The first version of the appendix incorrectly listed the system as being in the Mid Rim, but the N-18 grid square and the Bon'nyuw-Luq sector fall in the Outer Rim;<ref name="Appendix" /> this discrepancy was a carryover from the [[Cerroban system/Legends|Cerroban system's]] original listing in the Legends [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion]].<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref> The [[October 13]] update to the appendix resolved this error.<ref name="Substack 2">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref>
