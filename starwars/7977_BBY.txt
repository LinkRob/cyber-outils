==Events==
*The [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] is introduced.<ref name="CRC date">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'' dates the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] to the year 7945 in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the same event to the year [[32 BBY]]. Thus, basic math dates the epoch of the Hosnian Reckoning to 7977 BBY.</ref>
