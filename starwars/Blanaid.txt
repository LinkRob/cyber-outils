'''Blanaid,'''<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 4|cardname=Lieutenant Blanaid}}</ref> also known as '''Watts'''<ref name="AMG">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/scene-to-stat-tempest-force-battle-force|text=Scene to Stat: Tempest Force Battle Force}}</ref> was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who served as an [[AT-ST driver]] in the [[Imperial Army]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], he took part in the Empire's struggle against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. At the [[Battle of Endor]], Blanaid served under [[Major]] [[Newland]] aboard [[Tempest Scout 2]], an [[All Terrain Scout Transport]] walker.

==Biography==
A [[Gender|male]] of the [[human]] [[species]], Blanaid served as a [[lieutenant]]<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 4|cardname=Lieutenant Blanaid}}</ref> in the [[Imperial Army|ground forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], an autocratic government led by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. In that capacity, he was involved in the [[Galactic Civil War]], during which the Empire tried to crush the resistance movement known as the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Four [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]], Blanaid served as [[Major]] [[Newland]]'s copilot and gunner during the [[Battle of Endor]]. They were tasked with prowling the forest of [[Endor]] and firing at the [[planet]]'s natives, the [[Ewok]]s,<ref name="ROJ" /> aboard [[Tempest Scout 2]],<ref name="Tempest">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/scene-to-stat-tempest-force-battle-force|text=Scene to Stat: Tempest Force Battle Force}}</ref> an [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]] walker. As Blanaid and Newland were performing that mission, their vehicle was assaulted by the Rebel [[Chewbacca]], who swung on a vine to the machine's roof, with two Ewok fighters clinging to him. As the three assailants landed on the top of the walker, one of the Ewoks peeked through the cockpit's window. Blanaid noticed the upside-down Ewok hanging in front of his viewport, and he promptly warned Newland of the situation. Upon being ordered to remove the intruder, Blanaid opened the roof-mounted entry hatch. As soon as he put his head through the aperture, the lieutenant was yanked out and tossed overboard by Chewbacca. The two Ewoks subsequently leaped down into the cockpit and knocked Newland unconscious.<ref name="ROJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blanaid first appeared in the [[1983]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. Although he had one line, he was neither identified on-screen nor credited, and the movie script referred to him as "walker pilot #1."<ref name="ROJ" /> The pilot was portrayed by [[Robert Watts]], co-producer of ''Return of the Jedi'', who had served in real-world armed forces as a lieutenant.<ref name="SWI 101">[[Star Wars Insider 101|''Star Wars Insider'' 101]]</ref> Because of that, the character was christened "[[Watts]]" in [[1999]], in the [[Endor Limited]] expansion set of the [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game]].<ref name="CCGWatts">{{CCG|set=Endor Limited|cardname=Lieutenant Watts}}</ref> However, the card game is part of [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], previously known as the Expanded Universe, and is therefore not [[canon]].<ref name="EUNewPage">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> The name "Blanaid" was first revealed in the [[2014]] reference book ''[[Star Wars in 100 Scenes]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Star Wars in 100 Scenes">''[[Star Wars in 100 Scenes]]''</ref> Before the character was identified as either Watts or Blanaid, Robert Watts had once claimed that the pilot he portrayed was actually [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]] from [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="VeersDB">{{DB|character|generalmaximilianveers|Veers, General Maximilian}}</ref>
