A special and secure '''conference room'''<ref name="SWHC1">{{HelmetCollectionCite|1|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> was located on the [[officer's deck]]<ref name="Incident">{{StoryCite|story=An Incident Report|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> of the [[Overbridge (Death Star I)|Overbridge]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]]. [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] held regular meetings with his senior staff in that room.<ref name="SWHC1" /> It contained a polished [[Color|black]] table, around which twelve seats were arranged. Drinks in cups were provided for each attendant.<ref name="Episode IV" />

Tarkin held a [[Death Star Summit|summit]] of the [[Joint Chiefs of the Imperial Military]] there announcing that the [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] had just [[Dissolution of the Imperial Senate|disbanded]] the [[Imperial Senate]].<ref name="Episode IV" /> [[Imperial officer]]s present at the meeting included [[Imperial Security Bureau|ISB]] [[Colonel]] [[Wullf Yularen]], [[General]] [[Hurst Romodi]], General [[Trech Molock]], [[Chief of the Imperial Navy]] [[Admiral]] [[Conan Antonio Motti]], [[Chief of the Imperial Army]] General [[Cassio Tagge]], [[Chief]] [[Siward Cass]]<ref name="Fully Operational">{{StoryCite|story=Fully Operational (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> and General [[Moradmin Bast]]. [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] and [[Emperor's Fist]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], was also present.<ref name="Bast">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Chief Bast - Imperial Aide}}</ref> Like the rest of the station, the conference room was destroyed during the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Episode IV" />

==Description==
[[File:Holoprojector1.jpg|130px|thumb|Holoprojector.]]
The room had gray, metallic walls and a polished black circular table. The table, in the center of the room, had twelve seats arranged around it.<ref name="Episode IV" />

During the conference immediately following the [[Secret mission to Tatooine|capture]] of [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], only nine of these seats were occupied, as one belonged to [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]] who was [[Death|killed]] during the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="Fully Operational" />

Although the table was round the seating arrangement had a distinct hierarchy. One of the chairs around the table had an extended back and was occupied by the most senior Imperial official present for the meeting. During the period before the [[Battle of Yavin]], Grand Moff Tarkin held this position.<ref name="Episode IV" />

There were computer terminals installed at each position on the table. In the center of the table was a highly polished [[holoprojector]] and could be used to display three-dimensional models of potential targets, as well as providing holographic tactical readouts for evaluations by Tarkin and the various Imperial strategists within the [[Imperial Military]].<ref name="Episode IV" />

==History==
{{Quote|You may or may not have already reviewed the footage&mdash;it's my opinion that attempted murder at a meeting of the Joint Chiefs of the Galactic Empire merits a thorough, personal investigation by the members of High Command, but you gentlemen will of course act as you see fit.|Conan Motti files an incident report over Darth Vader Force choking him during a meeting in the room|<ref name="AIR">{{StoryCite|story=An Incident Report|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>}}
The Death Star's conference room was used by the [[Joint Chiefs of the Imperial Military]], high-ranking members of the Galactic Empire. These included [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], [[Emperor's Fist]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], [[Chief of the Imperial Army]] and [[General]] [[Cassio Tagge]], [[Chief of the Imperial Navy]] and [[Admiral of the Fleet]] [[Conan Antonio Motti]], [[General]] [[Moradmin Bast]], [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Colonel]] [[Wullf Yularen]], General [[Trech Molock]], General [[Hurst Romodi]], [[Director]] of [[Imperial Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research|Advanced Weapons Research]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> and Chief [[Siward Cass]]. [[Death Star Trooper]]s provided security for the conference room.<ref name="Episode IV" />

In a meeting around the time of the [[dissolution of the Imperial Senate]], the Joint Chiefs<ref name="AIR" /> gathered in the room and debated the evolving [[Galactic Civil War]]. Tarkin and Vader joined the meeting just as Tagge voiced his fear that the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] would continue to gain sympathy in the Senate, with Tarkin announcing the [[Imperial Senate]]'s dissolution as Tagge was speaking. When Motti disrespected [[the Force]] and insulted Vader, arguing that the Death Star was a far superior weapon to the Force, Vader [[Force choke]]d the [[admiral]] and only ceased when Tarkin asked him to.<ref name="Episode IV" /> Afterward, Motti submitted an incident report over Vader's actions. Footage was taken of the conference room during the meeting, including Vader's punishment of Motti, that he pointed to in the incident report.<ref name="AIR" />
