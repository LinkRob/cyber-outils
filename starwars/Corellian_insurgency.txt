The '''insurgency''' took place on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. At one point, [[Miru Nadrinakar]] crash-landed on a [[Geonosian]] Colony [[Moon]] while running from the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] back to Corellia to warn the [[Corellian Resistance|resistance]] of an upcoming crackdown on their activities. Miru was helped by [[Antiques dealer]] and keeper of [[Jedi]] artifacts [[Antron Bach]] to escape the Imperials when he gave her his old [[Starship|ship]] when the Imperial's started bombing his home, allowing Miru to escape.<ref name="TeoH" />
