'''Fungus''' (plural form: '''fungi''') was a group of organisms which included [[lichen]], [[kahel cave fungus]], and [[marsh fungi]], which grew on the [[planet]]s [[Hoth]], [[Mustafar]], and [[Dagobah]] respectively.<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> The planet [[Narq]] had entire [[forest]]s of [[Color|blue]] fungi.<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Some fungi, like the [[Wirutid]], were both ambulatory and [[Sentience|sentient]].<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> Some types of fungi were used to make [[wine]] as a substitute for fermented [[fruit]] or other [[Plant|vegetation]] with a rising agent such as [[yeast]]. According to the famous [[liquor]] trader [[Derla Pidys]], she had tasted some "truly remarkable" fungal wines.<ref name="Wine in Dreams">{{StoryCite|story=The Wine in Dreams|book=Canto Bight (novella collection)}}</ref> The small creature known as the [[Blismal]] ate fungus.<ref name="NoP">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref>
