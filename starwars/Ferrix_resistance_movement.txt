An spontaneous '''resistance movement'''<ref name="Space Moms" /> erupted into being on the [[planet]] [[Ferrix]] during the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Ferrix occupation|occupation]],<ref name="Rix Road" /> which had been upsetting the [[Sons of Ferrix|sons]] and [[Daughters of Ferrix]],<ref name="On Program">{{InsiderCite|220|On Program}}</ref> in [[5 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> After the [[death]] of [[Maarva Carassi Andor]], a [[Maarva's funeral procession|massive funeral procession]] was held against Imperial regulations that culminated in a pre-recorded [[hologram]] speech from Andor, who implored her people to fight against the Empire. Inspired by her words and enraged by the actions of Imperial [[captain]] [[Vanis Tigo]], who threw aside Andor's droid [[B2EMO]] for playing her speech, the gathered people of Ferrix [[Ferrix Riot|attacked]] the Imperials<ref name="Rix Road" /> and formed a resistance movement.<ref name="Space Moms" /> The Empire's counterattack largely shut down the rioters, many of whom were killed, while others fled Ferrix.<ref name="Rix Road" />
