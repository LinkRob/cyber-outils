'''Belin''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Ugnaught]] [[pilot]] trapped in the [[Occlusion Zone]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]]. He piloted a [[Belin's ship|transport ship]], regularly shipping grain. While piloting his [[Starship|ship]], the Ugnaught met [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]], who was trying to take over the ship to redirect the ship's cargo, a grain shipment, to the [[Rodian]] colony [[Prandril]] which was experiencing an extreme food shortage. Belin was reluctant at first thinking the idea of stealing from the [[Nihil]] was insane, later he grew into liking what he did with Kriss, feeling that he did something meaningful. After the [[skirmish above Seswenna]] he decided that, instead of fleeing from the Occlusion Zone with Kriss, that he would instead stay inside the zone with [[Rhil Dairo]] to report on the atrocities that the Nihil perpetrated.<ref name="TEOD" />
