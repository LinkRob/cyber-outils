The '''Dragonflower Nebula''' was a [[nebula]] in the [[Doldur sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square P-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Dragonflower Nebula was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Dragonflower Nebula/Legends|Dragonflower Nebula]] was first mentioned in "[[To Fight Another Day]]" a short story by [[Kathy Tyers]] that was first published by [[West End Games]] in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 6|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 6]] in [[May]] [[1995]].<ref name="TFAD">{{JournalCite|6|To Fight Another Day}}</ref>
