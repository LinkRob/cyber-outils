'''Ardees Beverage''' was a branded version of [[Moogan Tea]] produced by the [[Commerce Guild]] after it took control of the [[planet]] [[Mooga]]. The [[Ardees Beverage Company]] was created to produce the newly-trademarked [[Foodstuff|beverage]] from its now-proprietary recipe.

==Description==
Ardees Beverage was a [[Color|green]]-colored [[tea]] sold in bottles<ref name="Corruption" /> or as a fizzy canned variety. It was a corporate-branded version of [[Moogan Tea]] with characteristics that diverged over time from the original.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==History==
After the [[Commerce Guild]] took control of the [[planet]] [[Mooga]] where the tea originated, they deemed Moogan tea a trademarked beverage with a proprietary recipe despite its widespread popularity across the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]. Ardees Beverage was produced at the [[Ardees Beverage Company]] manufacturing plant, which was created by the Commerce Guild for that purpose. The guild utilized corporate surveillance [[droid]]s and issued cease and desist transmissions to establishments that attempted to sell their own Moogan tea.<ref name="Cookbook" /> Ardees Beverage declared itself "the galaxy's favorite" on its containers.<ref name="Star Wars: Scum and Villainy" />

During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Moogan]] [[smuggler]] [[Tee Va]] obtained Ardees Beverage concentrate from a source within the company and mixed the drink with the potentially poisonous diluting agent [[slabin]] to increase profits.<ref name="Star Wars: Scum and Villainy" /> Va then sold the contraband tea to schools on the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]], where the improper mixing of the concentrate and diluting agent caused an outbreak of illness among [[Mandalorian]] schoolchildren. [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]], her [[Journeyman Protector|personal bodyguards]], and [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] investigated the situation and found that the children had all recently been to the same [[Sundari Mandalorian school|school]] in [[Sundari]], and they had consumed the same new beverage. The [[Unidentified Sundari school superintendent|school superintendent]] had received the bottles of tea from [[Siddiq]], an importer for a [[Unidentified Mandalorian shipping company|shipping company]] and middleman in a [[Mandalorian black market conspiracy|black market conspiracy]] involving the superintendent, a corrupt customs official, and the Moogan smugglers. Following Siddiq's tip, the group led by Kryze and Amidala located the toxic beverages stored at a [[Sundari dock warehouse|warehouse]], witnessed a shipment in progress, and had a [[Skirmish at the Sundari docks|shoot-out]] with the smuggles. Kryze ordered the warehouse burned along with the toxic beverages.<ref name="Corruption" />

Mandalorian Police [[Captain]] [[Patrok Ru-Saxon]] initially did not believe that such corruption could occur within his jurisdiction. After he was presented with the evidence in the warehouse, he joined the investigation and arrested the smugglers.<ref name="Corruption" /> After uncovering further evidence that the product had passed through [[Coruscant]] and a shipment was on the way to the [[Coruscant Underworld|underlevels]], Ru-Saxon notified [[Lieutenant]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo|Tan Divo]] of the [[Coruscant Security Force]]. Divo intercepted the shipment, and sent Ru-Saxon a bottle of artisanal [[Tarine tea]] in thanks; Ru-Saxon in turn sent Divo a bottle of [[kri'gee]].<ref name="Star Wars: Scum and Villainy" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], [[human]] [[Lothal]] resident [[Gandr Spanjaf]] sometimes enjoyed a can of fizzy tea, which he kept in a cooler.<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Cookbook date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] defied the trademark and risked legal consequences by including his recipe for the original, authentic Moogan Tea in his [[Book|cookbook]] ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''.<ref name="Cookbook" />
