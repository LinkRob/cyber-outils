The '''droch''' was a type of [[Color|cerulean]]-colored creature from the [[planet]] [[Felucia]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> In [[10 BBY]], the [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]] kept the remains of a cerulean droch on display in a case in his [[Dryden's study|study]] on board his [[yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]''.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The corpse of a droch first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology series]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" /> They were first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SoloOG" />
