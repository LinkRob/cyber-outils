"'''25 Years'''" is a [[Canon|non-canon]] animated [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short released on [[April 26]], [[2024]] as part of the ''[[LEGO STAR WARS: Celebrate the Season]]'' series.<ref name="25 Years" />

==Official description==
''Celebrate 25 years of LEGO Star Wars with your favorite characters from across the galaxy.
''<ref name="25 Years" />

==Plot summary==
The video opens with [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Sebulba]] racing on [[podracer]]s during the [[Boonta Eve Classic]]. Skywalker catches up with Sebulba, who jeers at him. Sebulba attempts to ram [[Anakin Skywalker's Podracer|Skywalker's podracer]] but ends up colliding with a rock. Skywalker wins the race and is cheered by the crowd. Shortly later, [[Maul|Darth Maul]] arrives in his [[starship]] ''[[Scimitar]]''. Skywalker hides behind a wall while Maul searches. A shadow of [[Darth]] Vader appears on the wall.

The video then flashes forward to Darth Vader attacking [[rebel trooper]]s with [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] aboard the ''[[Tantive IV]]''. [[Stormtrooper]]s advance into the ship. [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] flee past the [[Sith Lord]]. The video then flashes to R2-D2 encouraging [[Luke Skywalker]], who is stuck in the swamps of [[Dagobah]]. Luke uses [[the Force]] to levitate his [[T-65 X-wing starfighter]]. [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] is sitting on the [[starfighter]]'s canopy.

The video then flashes back in time to Yoda giving [[Brightstar's second lightsaber|his lightsaber]] to [[Kai Brightstar]] in the presence of his friends [[Lys Solay]], [[Nubs]], and [[Nash Durango]]. They then depart on the ''[[Crimson Firehawk]]''. The video then switches forward to an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] pursuing the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' through an [[asteroid field]]. When the Star Destroyer gets trapped by asteroids, [[Han Solo]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], Luke, [[Chewbacca]], and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] cheer.

The video then switches to Kenobi and a young Princess Leia fleeing [[the Grand Inquisitor]], [[Reva Sevander]], and [[Fifth Brother]] through a forest. When they are cornered by a log, Kenobi activates [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. The video then switches backward to Kenobi, Anakin and a [[clone trooper]] battling [[droideka]]s and [[battle droid]]s on [[Geonosis]]. [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Huyang]] arrive aboard their [[T-6 shuttle]] and blast the battle droids. Skywalker jumps onto the hull of the shuttle.

The video then flashes to the ''[[Ghost]]'' landing on a forest world. [[Hera Syndulla]], [[C1-10P|Chopper]], [[Ezra Bridger]], and [[Sabine Wren]] disembark and engage in a gun battle with stormtroopers. [[Saw Gerrera]], [[Cassian Jeron Andor]], [[Cal Kestis]], and [[BD-1]] come to their rescue and drive the stormtroopers away. The stormtroopers flee into the path of [[Din Grogu]] and [[Din Djarin]], who defeat them with a mixture of Force powers and the [[Darksaber]].

Grogu and Djarin then race on a speeder against the ''Millennium Falcon'' and [[Poe Dameron]]'s ''[[Black One]]'' [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]. They fly to the [[Great Temple]] on [[Yavin 4]] for a celebratory gathering.
