This [[Gender|female]] individual was the '''mother''' of [[Theelin]] [[Alphabet Squadron]] [[pilot]] [[Chass na Chadic]]. After she had left [[Winker's]] [[cantina]] on [[Verzan]] and punched a member of the [[Children of the Empty Sun]] [[Religion|cult]], Chadic returned to her [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing]] and began traveling to [[Troithe]]. During the course of the journey, Chadic thought of her [[Parent|mother]] and all the cults she had known throughout her life, hoping to have forgotten the memory of Winker's by the [[time]] she woke up.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
