'''Chandrakant''' was a [[moon]] orbiting the [[planet]] [[Chandrila]] in the [[Chandrila system]].<ref name="SWGS Location" /> It appeared to have a [[Color|brownish-gold]] and [[Color|violet]] hue when seen from the starboard-side [[viewport]] on the atrium of the ''[[Halcyon]]'' on the first day of the cruise. Crew members sometimes explained that the moon served as a [[mineral]]-rich [[mining]] resource for the planet of Chandrila.<ref name="SWGS">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref>

{{Moon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Chandrakant appeared on the route of the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] and could be seen out the atrium's starboard-side window when first coming aboard, as well as on the in-cabin itinerary screen during the voyage.<ref name="SWGS" />
