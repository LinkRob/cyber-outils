"'''Did You Know?'''" is an appendix of ''[[Star Wars: The Official Starships & Vehicles Collection]]'' magazine, a basic quiz appearing on the second-to-last page of every issue. It features three questions with accompanying images, related to the vehicle or character that the issue focuses on.
