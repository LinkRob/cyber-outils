The '''773 Firepuncher sniper rifle''', also known as the '''773 Firepuncher rifle'''<ref name="2020 Base Series 2">{{SWCT|set=2020 Base Series 2|cardname=Crosshair}}</ref> or simply '''773 Firepuncher''', was a model of a versatile<ref name="Comprehensive Guide">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[sniper rifle]]<ref name="Bad Batch Concept Art Gallery" /> manufactured by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]] that featured ablative coating and a wide-beam "[[Fire|burning]]" mode.<ref name="Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger" /> The rifle could also fire bolts that could reflect off of surfaces.<ref name="On the Wings of Keeradaks">{{TCW|On the Wings of Keeradaks}}</ref> The 773 Firepuncher rifle was a customizable weapon,<ref name="2020 Base Series">{{SWCT|set=2020 Base Series 2|cardname=Crosshair}}</ref> but even a standard-issue 773 Firepuncher rifle was a powerful weapon.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|773-firepuncher-rifle|773 Firepuncher rifle}}</ref> It could act as a [[blaster rifle]], [[grappling hook]] launcher, and [[Homing beacon|homing-beacon]] dispatch. The weapon was capable of firing many types of ammo, including [[Electroshock dart|electro-dart]]s and tripwires.<ref name="Comprehensive Guide" />

==History==
===In Crosshair's crosshairs===
[[File:Crosshair773Firepuncher.png|thumb|left|300px|Crosshair takes aim with a 773]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], the 773 Firepuncher was a favored weapon of [[Galactic Republic]] [[sniper]]s, including [[Clone Force 99]] [[clone trooper]] [[Crosshair]].<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> He utilized a customized [[Blaster rifle|rifle]] and became a master in using the weapon in its many configurations including, but not exclusively, a [[blaster rifle]], a [[grappling hook]] launcher, and even fire [[dart]]s that electrified a target on contact. Crosshair also carried a special type of mirrored reflector disks that could stick to surfaces and cause his blaster bolts to ricochet. Crosshair stored his rifle on his [[backpack]] by taking the [[scope]] and long barrel off. The weapon also had alternate barrel configurations that could be manually swapped out, such as a under[[barrel]] attachment that fired a long [[Grappling hook|grappling cable]]. Crosshair continued to use the weapon to deadly effect in service to the [[Galactic Empire]], where he commanded an [[Elite Squad]] of [[Elite Squad Trooper|specialized human recruits]] on a [[Mission to Onderon (Elite Squad)|mission]] to [[Onderon]].<ref name="Replacements">{{TBB|Replacements}}</ref> On several occasions, Crosshair’s sniper rifle was equipped to fire a [[homing beacon]]<ref name="Devil's Deal">{{TBB|Devil's Deal}}</ref> and a [[glowrod]].<ref name="The Outpost">{{TBB|The Outpost}}</ref> Crosshair used another 773 after the [[Attack on Kamino]] where his former squad confiscated his rifle when they left<ref name="Kamino Lost">{{TBB|Kamino Lost}}</ref>, though his old rifle was eventually given back to him.<ref name="Bad Territory">{{TBB|Bad Territory}}</ref>

===X marks the spot===
[[File:CloneXAim-TCC.png|thumb|right|300px|Clone X taking aim with his sniper rifle.]]
During the early [[Imperial Era]], [[Clone X trooper]]s [[Clone X]], [[CX-1]], and [[CX-2]] also used 773 Firepuncher sniper rifles, though some versions had a [[wood]]en stock and a longer scope.<ref name="Infiltration" /> Clone X used his 773 rifle to assassinate [[Cade]] and [[Slip (clone trooper)|Slip]] on [[Coruscant]]. He later attempted to kill [[Senator]] [[Riyo Chuchi]] but only killed two of her [[Pantoran guard]]s before being stunned by [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], making him drop the rifle in a warehouse before being taken to a [[Martez sisters' repair shop|repair shop]] owned by a couple of [[Trace Martez|old]] [[Rafa Martez|friends]] of Rex.<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy">{{TBB|The Clone Conspiracy}}</ref> CX-1 used a 773 rifle on [[Pantora]] in order to assassinate former [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[senator]] [[Avi Singh]], who was meeting with Chuchi to discuss a [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|potential rebellion]] against the Empire. The [[Clone Underground]] network manage to prevent CX-1 from escaping with [[Howzer]] stunning the assassin before taking him to [[Teth]].<ref name="Infiltration" /> CX-2 used his rifle to fire several time-rigged [[sticky bomb]]s onto the hull of the ''[[Marauder (Omicron-class)|Marauder]]'' and shot the [[conscription trooper pilot]] piloting a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i]] [[gunship]] on the [[planet]] [[Pabu]].<ref name="PoNR">{{TBB|Point of No Return (The Bad Batch)}}</ref> CX-2 later used his rifle on the [[planet]] [[Wayland]] to pin down Clone Force 99 within a ruined [[hangar]] within [[Tantiss Base]]. After Clone Force 99 members [[Hunter]], [[Wrecker]], and [[Omega]] were captured, CX-2 entered the [[Tantiss Base training room|training room]] where they were being held with compact rifle strapped across his back.<ref name="TCHA">{{TBB|The Cavalry Has Arrived}}</ref>

When Wrecker tried to escape, CX-2 drew his sniper rifle, but Wrecker threw a large piece of metal, knocking CX-2 down. As CX-2 tried to reorient himself, he attempted to shoot Hunter, only for Crosshair to kick his rifle out of the way, causing him to miss. Hunter responded by throwing one of the [[assassin]]'s [[electrostaff]] at CX-2, killing him. Hunter passed CX-2's rifle to Crosshair before supporting him out of the room to rescue Omega. On the landing pad, Crosshair used CX-2's rifle to destroy the [[engine]]s of a [[CX Dagger vessel]] before using it to kill [[Scorch]], shoot Omega's [[Stuncuffs|binders]], and kill [[Doctor]] [[Royce Hemlock]].<ref name="TCHA" />

===Azmorigan's armory===
[[File:Chopper_double_barrel.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Chopper attacking Azmorigan.]]
[[Azmorigan's henchmen|Henchmen]] serving the [[crime lord]] [[Azmorigan]] used 773 Firepunchers on multiple occasions, including during a [[Mission to Lando Calrissian's farm|skirmish]] against the [[smuggler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and the [[Spectres]] at [[Lando Calrissian's farm|Calrissian's farm]] on [[Lothal]]<ref name="Idiot's Array">{{Rebels|Idiot's Array (episode)}}</ref> and during a [[Skirmish in Nixus Hub 218|confrontation]] at [[Nixus Hub 218]] on the [[planet]] [[Nixus]]. During the latter fight, the [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]] attacked Azmorigan, using a [[blaster pistol]] and one of the Firepuncher snipers he picked up from one of his henchmen. Several of [[Cikatro Vizago]]'s [[IG-RM bodyguard and enforcer droid]]s also wielded these weapons when the [[arms dealer]]s unleashed them on the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]] and the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]].<ref name="Brothers of the Broken Horn" />
