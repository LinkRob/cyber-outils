Sometime between [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]], a '''duel''' occurred when [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], accompanied by his [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], confronted the [[assassin]] [[Corlis Rath]] to reconcile the latter's vendetta on the Jedi Master. Jinn took a pacifist approach, but Kenobi dueled Rath to protect his master. Jinn then intervened before Rath could [[Death|kill]] his padawan, convincing the assassin to consider leaving his path of hate for good. The two parties thus parted ways.<ref name="Jedi Knights 10" />
