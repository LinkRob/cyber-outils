'''CF-8011''' was a<ref name="Datapad" /> [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] who served in the [[709th Legion]] of the [[First Order]] military regime. CF-8011 was stationed on the<ref name="Datapad" /> [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Thrawn">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Batuu]] during the First Order's [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] there<ref name="Datapad" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">CF-8011 was stationed on [[Batuu]] during the [[First Order]]'s [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] there, which took place in [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> During the occupation, CF-8011 asked a fellow [[trooper]], [[RS-318]], why they were stationed on Batuu. The trooper had no clue, noting that the planet was full of lowlives and [[smuggler]]s, to which CF-8011 replied that it was not any wonder why the stormtrooper's possessions were repeatedly [[Thief|stolen]]. CF-8011 also wondered and asked RS-318 why [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] seemed more impatient than usual.<ref name="Datapad" />

At another point during the occupation, CF-8011 overheard [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] mention that he [[Disturbance in the Force|sensed]] something. The trooper went on to ask a fellow comrade, [[AD-2508]], what the Supreme Leader meant by that, only for the latter to make a sarcastic retort at the Supreme Leader's expense. CF-8011 wore standard [[First Order stormtrooper armor]] with a [[Color|white]] [[pauldron]].<ref name="Datapad" />
