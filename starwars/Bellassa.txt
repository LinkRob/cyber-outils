'''Bellassa''' was a [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Astrogation">{{SkeletonCrew|Very Interesting, As an Astrogation Problem}}</ref> located in the [[Bellassa system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Core Worlds]]. It was situated within grid square M-11 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. The [[Hydian Way]]<ref name="TFABG" /> [[super-hyperroute]]<ref name="BMF 48">{{BuildFalconCite|48|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> connected Bellassa to<ref name="TFABG" /> the planets<ref name="Heir">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref><ref name="Shadow">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> [[Exodeen]] and [[Fedalle]]. Bellassa shared its name with<ref name="TFABG" /> the [[Bellassan pepper]]<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> and the [[Bellassan Galaxy Petitionists]] [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|rebel]] group.<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>

Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="9 ABY">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> the [[Skeleton Crew|crew]] of<ref name="Astrogation" /> the ''[[Onyx Cinder]]''<ref name="Onyx Cinder">{{Databank|onyx-cinder|Onyx Cinder}}</ref> [[starship]] [[Mission to At Attin|traveled]] to the [[Observatory Moon]] to ask<ref name="Astrogation" /> the [[astronomer]]<ref name="DB Khymm">{{Databank|khymm|Kh'ymm}}</ref> [[Kh'ymm]] for assistance with finding the lost planet [[At Attin]]. While Kh'ymm searched her archive for potential locations of At Attin, Bellassa was one of multiple locations that was listed on her [[computer]]'s screen in the [[Aurebesh]] [[Writing|script]].<ref name="Astrogation" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Starcruiserdate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> [[Chandrila Star Line]]'s [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|Star Cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' passed by Bellassa on a cruise from the Core Worlds' [[Chandrila system]] to the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Several [[Viewscreen|screens]] aboard the [[Star Cruiser|starship]] displayed a partial [[Star map|map]] of [[the galaxy]] that indicated Bellassa's position.<ref name="GS">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Last_of_the_Jedi_by_Chris_Trevas.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Bellassa depicted in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity]]
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Bellassa was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 52|fifty-second issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 52">{{BuildFalconCite|52|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> in an article written by [[Mark Newbold]],<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> published by [[De Agostini]] around [[December 30]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMF52date">{{BMFdates|52}}</ref> Bellassa first appeared [[in-universe]] when it was depicted on a map viewable in [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]], an immersive roleplaying experience at [[W:c:disney:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]] in [[Wikipedia:Florida|Florida]]<ref name="GS" /> that opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]],<ref name="31 Things">{{SW|url=news/31-things-we-learned-about-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=31 Things We Learned in an Exclusive Preview of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser|nolive=1}}</ref> and had its final voyage from [[September 28]] to [[September 30|30]], [[2023]].<ref name="StarcruiserClosure">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=destinations/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Bellassa/Legends|Bellassa]] first appeared in the [[2005]] young reader novel ''[[The Last of the Jedi: The Desperate Mission]]'', written by [[Jude Watson]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: The Last of the Jedi]]'' series.<ref name="Last">''[[The Last of the Jedi: The Desperate Mission]]''</ref> It was then first pictured in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s [[2012]] reference book ''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]''.<ref name="TERC">''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]''</ref>
