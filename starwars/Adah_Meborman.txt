'''Adah Meborman''' was a [[Smuggler/Legends|smuggler]]<ref name="TFUII">''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II]]'' {{C|[[Wikipedia:PlayStation 3|PS3]]/[[Wikipedia:Xbox 360|Xbox 360]] "[[Tarko-se/Legends|Tarko-se]]" databank entry}}</ref> during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the reign of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. At some point between the [[Standard year/Legends|years]] [[19 BBY/Legends|19]] and [[1 BBY/Legends|1 BBY]],<ref name="date">According to [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] was formed in the year [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]]. Since the events of ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II]]'' occur in the year [[1 BBY/Legends|1 BBY]], we can conclude that Adah Meborman was alive sometime between then.</ref> Meborman met a [[Unidentified Neimoidian (drinking hole)|Neimoidian]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]]. The [[Neimoidian/Legends|Neimoidian]] claimed to be related to an individual by the name of [[Medd Valii]], who had reportedly made a lot of money while alive. According to the Neimoidian, Valii had hid his money in the form of pure [[Gold/Legends|gold]] in the arch of the [[City/Legends|city]] [[Tarko-se/Legends|Tarko-se]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Cato Neimoidia/Legends|Cato Neimoidia]]. Upon hearing that, Meborman became thrilled, knowing that it was a great opportunity. At some later point in time, the smuggler and his interest in the alleged gold were recorded in an Imperial investigation of said smuggler.<ref name="TFUII" />

==Behind the scenes==
Adah Meborman was first mentioned in the "Tarko-se" entry in the in-game databank of the [[Wikipedia:PlayStation 3|PlayStation 3]]/[[Wikipedia:Xbox 360|Xbox 360]] versions of ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II]]'', a [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] released in [[2010]].<ref name="TFU2" />
