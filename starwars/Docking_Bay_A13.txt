'''Docking Bay A13''' was a docking bay in [[Talos]]. The bay included hand umbilicals, a machine shop and mechanical locks. It also had [[Shower/Legends|showers]] and a [[Refresher/Legends|refresher]]. The bay was lacking storm cover. [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] and [[Akanah Norand Goss Pell]] docked the ''[[Mud Sloth]]'' here.<ref name="Shield of Lies" />
