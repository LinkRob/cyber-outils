'''Foreman''' was the title held by the lead [[engineer]] of the [[Techno Union]]. [[Wat Tambor]] was the foreman of the Techno Union during the final [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] until his [[death]]<ref name="Emir Wat Tambor" /> and the conglomerate's nationalization by the new [[Galactic Empire]] at the end of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Confederacy of Independent Systems">{{Databank|confederacy-of-independent-systems|Confederacy of Independent Systems}}</ref> [[Okadiah Garson]] was the foreman of a [[Moonglow Polychemical]] [[mining]] team.<ref name="A New Dawn" /> [[Proach]] was a foreman for the [[Mining Guild]].<ref name="Crawler Commandeers" /> [[Darrus Rantarovo]] was a foreman on the [[Big Settlement hydro farm]].<ref name="Cobalt Squadron">''[[The Last Jedi: Cobalt Squadron]]''</ref>
