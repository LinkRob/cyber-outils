'''AlderaanMotors''' was a company that produced the [[heat sink]] power generator, a portable, medium-output [[power generator]] that was common throughout [[the galaxy]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. The company shared its name with the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]].<ref name="LbE" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], AlderaanMotors was mentioned in the [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying]] supplement ''[[Lead by Example]]''.<ref name="LbE" /> The company originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was mentioned in the [[1996]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Vehicles and Vessels]]'' by [[Bill Smith]].<ref name="EGtVaV">''[[The Essential Guide to Vehicles and Vessels]]''</ref>
