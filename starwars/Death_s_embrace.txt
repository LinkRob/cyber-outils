'''Death's embrace''' was a [[species]] of large tentacled [[plant]] native to the [[planet]] [[Umbara]], a world where it was eternally [[Standard day|night]].<ref name="VE" /> Each of the plant's tentacles was black in [[color]] with [[Bioluminescence|bioluminescent]] ridges along one side.<ref name="TG" /> The ridges could be pink<ref name="DoU">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref> or red in color, and sometimes faded to gray before lighting up again. The plant grew amongst [[forest]]s of [[Zabrak Spine]]s and could grow far larger than a [[human]].<ref name="TG" /> It was a particularly aggressive plant, and few had escaped the embrace of its tendrils.<ref name="VE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Death's Embrace first appeared in "[[Darkness on Umbara]],"<ref name="DoU" /> the seventh episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|Season Four]], which aired on [[October 28]], [[2011]].<ref name="Insider 129: Launch Pad">{{InsiderCite|129|Launchpad}}</ref> The species was first identified in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''.<ref name="VE" />
