'''Doric Prime''' was a [[planet]] that existed in the [[the galaxy]]. It was a<ref name="BB: GA 3" /> mostly<ref name="BB: GA 4" /> [[Ocean|oceanic]] [[Waterworld|world]],<ref name="BB: GA 3" /> with few islands that harbored forests.<ref name="BB: GA 4" /> A type of settlement was located near one of the islands and was supplied by a [[Doric Prime power plant|nearby]] hydro [[power plant]]. At some point during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]], the elite Republic [[clone commando]] squad [[Clone Force 99]] the "Bad Batch",<ref name="BB: GA 3" /> aswell as the [[Kyuzo]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Embo]],<ref name="BB: GA 4" /> [[Mission to Doric Prime|traveled]] to the planet [[Hunt for a thief|in pursuit]] of the Separatist [[Dark Acolyte]] [[Asajj Ventress]] who met at the power plant with the bounty hunter [[Aurra Sing]] to hand over a list of undercover Republic [[Ghost Agent]]s she had stolen<ref name="BB: GA 3" /> from the Republic capital [[Coruscant]].<ref name="BB: GA 1">[[Hyperspace Stories: The Bad Batch—Ghost Agents 1|''Hyperspace Stories: The Bad Batch—Ghost Agents'' 1]]</ref>
