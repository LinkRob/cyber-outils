'''Artt Butler''' started working in the [[Wikipedia:Voice-over|voice-over]] industry in 1991 as the lead booth director of a talent agency, providing instruction and direction to voice actors during their recordings.<ref name="Bio">{{WebCite|url=http://arttbutler.com/artt_butler_bio.php|text=Bio|archivedate=20180823042824}}</ref> In [[2011]], Butler responded to a casting call by ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' director [[Dave Filoni]] to portray the [[Mon Calamari/Legends|Mon Calamari]] [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] [[Gial Ackbar/Legends|Ackbar]] on the series.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://usatoday30.usatoday.com/life/television/news/story/2011-09-10/Admiral-Ackbar-stars-in-animated-Star-Wars/50324746/1|text=Admiral Ackbar stars in animated 'Star Wars'.|author=Truitt, Brian|date=2011-09-08|work=[[Wikipedia:USA Today|USA Today]]|archivedate=20190512123506}}</ref> While Butler auditioned for the role mimicking the voice of Ackbar from [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] ([[1983]]), most of the other actors seeking the part used their normal voices in auditions. Butler was cast and appeared in the first three episodes of the show's [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|fourth season]]: "[[Water War]]," "[[Gungan Attack]]," and "[[Prisoners]]."<ref>{{YouTube|video=8U2CHYzuLeI|text=TCW Season 4 Premiere Event: Artt Butler Interview|channel=UCHhi9NhCNzypoAlXoIvzVWQ|name=TheForce.Net|archivedate=20160414025404}}</ref> In a review of "Water War," Eric Geller of [[TheForce.net]] said Butler's voice was a "great match" to that of Ackbar in ''Return of the Jedi''.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://theforce.net/latestnews/story/TFN_Review_Water_War_140623.asp|text=TFN Review: Water War|author=Geller, Eric|date=2011-09-17|archivedate=20190730033537}}</ref>
