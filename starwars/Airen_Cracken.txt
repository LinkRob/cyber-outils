[[General]] '''Airen Cracken''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who served as [[Chief of Intelligence]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], and later the [[New Republic]], during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Biography==
===Chief of Alliance Intelligence===
{{Quote|This is the pilot causing us so much distress? I'm underwhelmed to say the least.|Airen Cracken upon meeting Han Solo|<ref name="HS1" />}}
[[File:LeiaAndCracken-HanSolo1.png|thumb|left|250px|Leia Organa and Cracken.]]
Even before the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] was established, the [[human]] Airen Cracken was an accomplished freedom fighter. It was he who led the [[Contruum militia/Canon|planetary militia]] that freed his [[homeworld]] from the oppression of the [[Galactic Empire]], which led him to be fast-tracked to a [[general]]'s commission within the [[Alliance Military]].<ref name="FC">{{ForceCollection|General Cracken|star=4}}</ref>

By the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Scarif]], Cracken served as the [[Chief of Intelligence]] for the Rebel Alliance, thus leading the [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service]].<ref name="RORD">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''</ref> Cracken was personally involved in setting up [[spy]] [[Rebel Alliance spy networks|networks]] throughout the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]], so Rebel Intelligence was represented on the [[Alliance High Command|rebel council]] by one of his trusted field operatives, General [[Davits Draven]].<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> Despite his absence, he still compiled a [[Operation Fracture dossier|dossier]] regarding [[Operation Fracture]] at [[Mon Mothma]]'s request.<ref name="RORD" />

Sometime after the [[Battle of Yavin]], while serving in his capacity of Head of Security and Intelligence and confronted with the responsibility of rescuing three rebel spies in danger of capture by the Empire, Cracken showed reluctance in recruiting [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] for the mission. However, he was convinced by [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], citing the fact that they were running out of options and out of time.<ref name="HS1" />

====A Rebel superweapon====
{{Quote|We stole the plans&mdash;used 'em to destroy the damn thing. Why the hell wouldn't we build our own?|Cracken, to Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 33" />}}
Despite their victory against the Empire at [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]], the Rebels were forced to [[Evacuation of Yavin 4|abandon]] their [[Great Temple|headquarters]] on [[Yavin 4]].<ref name="Princess Leia 1">[[Princess Leia 1|''Princess Leia'' 1]]</ref> Protecting [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]], the Alliance's [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chief-of-State]], became a priority for Intelligence. At some point, Chief Cracken came to the conclusion that killing [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] could end the war in one swift blow. However, previous assassination attempts were known to have failed due to the Emperor's [[The Force|Force]]-fueled ability to sense intentions. Therefore, Cracken thought it necessary to create a [[superweapon]] that could be fired from half [[the galaxy]] away, making it impossible to detect. When analysis of Imperial [[propaganda]] revealed that the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] possessed the ancient [[lightsaber]]/[[sniper rifle]] known as "the [[Farkiller]]," Cracken sent [[Strike Team Misericorde|Extraction Team Misericorde]] to retrieve both Aphra and the artifact, and bring them to the [[Trading Post Sh'ung-tesk]]. There, the Chief of Intelligence attempted to rally the archaeologist to the Alliance's cause. He explained to her how the Rebels could create a high energy superweapon by reverse-engineering the Farkiller, giving them a chance to destroy the [[Imperial Palace]] at a great distance.<ref name="Aphra 33" />

====A battle to end all battles====
[[File:AirenCracken-SWISE2018.png|thumb|right|250px|Cracken served aboard the ''Millennium Falcon'' during the Battle of Endor.]]
In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the Alliance learned from [[Bothan]] operatives that the Empire had begun construction on a [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] in [[orbit]] of [[Endor]]. Being Chief of Intelligence, Cracken was among the first to know, along with Chancellor Mothma and [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]]. He was present at the emergency summit on [[Zastiga]], when the Chancellor revealed the existence of the new superweapon to the rest of the Alliance's top brass. Although General [[Crix Madine]] said that they would simply destroy that new Death Star like the first one, Cracken pointed out that the Empire was likely to have eliminated the particular flaw that had been exploited at Yavin.<ref name="Moving Target" />

After a sleepless night, the Rebel leaders convened again. It was determined that, since the second Death Star was unfinished, its main reactor was vulnerable. Therefore, provided that the station's deflector shield was knocked out beforehand, it would be possible to destroy it. [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] then came up with a ruse to divert the Empire's attention to the [[Corva sector]], allowing the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] to [[Staging area|gather]] over [[Sullust]] in order to launch an assault on the Death Star. Although Chancellor Mothma had reservations, Cracken was quick to acknowledge the merits of that plan, and his approval tipped the scale in Organa's favor. Towards the end of the reunion, the Chief of Intelligence asked Organa who she had in mind for her "[[Operation Yellow Moon]]," and he assigned [[Kidi Aleri]] as the team's [[Communications officer|communications specialist]]. Since it was vital that the Empire fell for the trick, Cracken had his agents seed various communications networks with rumors about their mission.<ref name="Moving Target" />

Although there were casualties, Operation Yellow Moon was ultimately a success, allowing the [[Battle of Endor]] to take place. As was decided on Zastiga,<ref name="Moving Target" /> the Rebels decided to attack on two fronts: a [[Endor strike team|strike team]] would disable the battle station's shield generator stationed on Endor, while a squadron of fighters would target the Death Star's exposed reactor.<ref name="Return of the Jedi" /> Chief Cracken participated in the fighter assault, serving aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' with [[Lieutenant]] [[Blount]].<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> The two were initially in the [[Starship|ship's]] [[cockpit]] with its [[pilot]], General [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], co-pilot [[Nien Nunb]], and a [[navigator]],<ref name="Return of the Jedi" /> but they soon manned the ship's [[Quad laser cannon|quad]] [[turret]]s and served as [[gunner]]s.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> The Rebels managed to destroy the second Death Star, and Emperor Palpatine was finally killed.<ref name="Return of the Jedi" /> The Alliance then reorganized itself into the [[New Republic]], and Cracken took command of the [[New Republic Intelligence]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

===Chief of New Republic Intelligence===
A few [[Standard week|weeks]] into the New Republic's existence, [[Agent]] [[Caern Adan]] met with Cracken to discuss the [[Hunt for Shadow Wing|mission]] to destroy the [[Special forces|lethal force]] of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] known as [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing|Shadow Wing]]. The meeting was short, as Cracken made it clear that Shadow Wing was a secondary concern for the provisional government.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Crackencanon.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Airen Cracken in ''Return of the Jedi'']]
In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Canon|continuity]], the character of General Airen Cracken was created for the [[1991]] sourcebook [[Cracken's Rebel Field Guide (sourcebook)|''Cracken's Rebel Field Guide'']],<ref name="Cracken's Rebel Field Guide">[[Cracken's Rebel Field Guide (sourcebook)|''Cracken's Rebel Field Guide'']]</ref> and an unnamed Rebel aboard the ''Millennium Falcon'' in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], played by [[Michael Stevens]],<ref name="Insider 141">{{InsiderCite|141|Making Star Wars: Return of the Jedi Unseen!|Making ''Star Wars: Return of the Jedi'' Unseen! Part II}}</ref> was later consolidated with that character.<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=Death Star II Limited|cardname=Colonel Cracken}}</ref> Current ''[[Star Wars]]'' continuity has maintained the identity of General Cracken for that Rebel from ''Jedi''.<ref name="AEYNTK" />

===Continuity===
The [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'' states that Cracken was a spy and [[colonel]] who flew missions for General [[Crix Madine]]'s [[Rebel Commando]]s and was later promoted to general and Chief of Rebel Intelligence.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> This, however, contradicts various sources including ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' that had established Cracken was already Chief and a general since the early days of the Alliance.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>
