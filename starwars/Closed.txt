The '''Closed''' were a faction of [[near-human]]s with three vertical slits instead of [[ear]]s, that lived on the [[planet]] [[Carnelion IV]]. They were at [[Open-Closed War|war]] with the [[Open]], a rival faction. The Closed came in contact with the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and his [[Padawan]] learner, [[Anakin Skywalker]], when they came to Carnelion IV to [[Mission to Carnelion IV|investigate]] a [[distress signal]] they had received.<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin 2">[[Obi-Wan and Anakin 2|''Obi-Wan and Anakin'' 2]]</ref>
