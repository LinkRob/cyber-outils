The '''First Guard''' was an [[Praetorian Guard|Elite Praetorian Guard]] who served as a [[bodyguard]] of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]], ruler of the [[First Order]]. Equipped with segmented plate [[armor]] and an [[electro-bisento]], the First Guard eschewed ranged weapons in favor of [[Melee weapon|melee]] combat. The First Guard was [[Death|killed]] on board the ''[[Supremacy]]'' when [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], Snoke's [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] apprentice, [[Battle of Oetchi|betrayed]] him with the help of [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], another [[Force-sensitive]] individual. Together they defeated and killed all of the Praetorian Guards in [[Snoke's throne room]].

==Biography==
The First [[Bodyguard|Guard]] was a member of the [[Praetorian Guard|Elite Praetorian Guard]]&mdash;[[Color|crimson]] [[Soldier|warriors]] who served as the personal bodyguards of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] of the [[First Order]].<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The First Guard was stationed in [[Snoke's throne room]] with an [[electro-bisento]] along with the other Praetorian Guards at the [[time]] of [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren's]] audience with the Supreme Leader<ref name="Episode VIII" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> Angered by Snoke's disapproving appraisal of his [[Battle of Starkiller Base|defeat]] on [[Starkiller Base]],<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> the [[Ren|Master]] of the [[Knights of Ren]] took a step in his master's direction, causing the First Guard to snap into a combat stance against the [[Dark side of the Force|dark]] apprentice as his fellows.<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

[[File:KyloRenElitePraetorianGuardJarKai-TLJ.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Kylo Ren and a Praetorian Guard duel]]
The First Guard was still present in the [[throne]] room when Ren returned with the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan|learner]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] as his [[prisoner]]. However, instead of executing the former [[scavenger]] as Snoke ordered, Ren chose to [[assassin]]ate his master&mdash;resulting in a [[Battle of Oetchi|confrontation]] between the two [[Force-sensitive]]s and the Praetorian Guards. Like the other Praetorians, the First Guard attempted to avenge the slain Supreme Leader,<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> engaging the traitorous apprentice and Rey. After defeating several guards, Ren was left with just the First Guard and another guard wielding an electro-bisento. The Knight of Ren stole one of their bisentos and fought off the other with both [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|his]] [[Crossguard lightsaber|lightsaber]] and the electro-bisento. The other guard then attempted to fight Ren with their armored hands, and was able to parry Ren's lightsaber, but was then stabbed in the back with their stolen electro-bisento and forced to the ground by Ren, who then turned his lightsaber and impaled them in the neck with one of the smaller blades protruding from the sides. The other was then able to wrangle their bisento around Kylo Ren's neck. Rey noticed him in danger and threw [[Skywalker lightsaber|her]] [[lightsaber]] to him, which he then swiftly ignited, through the Guard's head, instantly killing them.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Personality and traits==
Serving as Snoke's last line of defense, the First Guard was one of eight elite sentinels entrusted with the security of the Supreme Leader. Like the other Praetorian Guards, the First Guard was equipped with a suit of [[armor]] that was painful to the wearer, yet endured it out of unwavering loyalty and duty to the ruler of the First Order. A lethal and ever-vigilant sentry,<ref name="TLJVD" /> the First Guard snapped into a combat stance when Ren took a step in Snoke's direction&mdash;his rage becoming too strong to contain. The guard resumed a standing posture when signaled by Snoke to stand down, yet nonetheless regarded the Supreme Leader's disciple with wary suspicion.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

As one of Snoke's guardians, the First Guard's fighting style was a mix of several forms of martial arts such as [[Teräs Käsi]], [[Bakuuni Hand]], [[Echani (martial art)|Echani unarmed forms]], and [[Nar Kanji]] "Blind Alley" techniques.<ref name="TLJVD" /> After Snoke's death, rather than swearing loyalty to his master's murderer, the First Guard perished along with the other Praetorians while attempting to avenge the Supreme Leader.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

==Equipment==
As a member of the Elite Praetorian Guard, the First Guard wore a brilliant red uniform that deliberately evoked the memory of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guard]]. Unlike the Emperor's guards, the First Guard's combat armor was not encumbered by ceremonial [[robe]]s. Designed for flexibility, the First Guard's segmented plate armor also included large pauldrons to deflect vertical melee strikes. Foregoing ranged weaponry, the First Guard wielded an [[electro-bisento]]&mdash;a high-tech [[melee weapon]] with a tempered metal blade connected to an [[ultrasonic generator]] for greater lethality.<ref name="TLJVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The First Guard first appeared along with the rest of the Elite Praetorian Guard in the [[sequel trilogy]]'s [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], directed by [[Rian Johnson]],<ref name="Episode VIII" /> and was first identified in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''.<ref name="TLJVD" />
