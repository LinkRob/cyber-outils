'''First Order jet troopers''', or simply '''jet troopers''', were a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] variant in the [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|Stormtrooper Corps]] of the [[First Order]]. Unlike the standard [[trooper]], who was mostly trained in ground operations, these specialists were trained in [[Atmosphere|aerial]] combat.

==Equipment==
===Overview===
{{Dialogue|C-3PO|Oh! They fly now!|Finn|They fly now?|Dameron|They fly now.|attr=C-3PO, Finn, and Poe Dameron, whilst being pursued by jet troopers on Pasaana|src=<ref name="SW:TRoS" />}}
Like the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[jumptrooper]]s,<ref name="SWBF2" /> [[First Order]] jet troopers<ref name="FO jet trooper DB">{{Databank|first-order-jet-troopers|First Order jet troopers}}</ref> were specialized [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] that were trained in aerial combat utilizing specialized [[armor]] and weaponry.<ref name="SWBF2" /> They were each armed with a [[comlink]], a [[G125 projectile launcher]], and equipped with an [[NJP-900 integrated jetpack]] for aerial combat.<ref name="SW:TRoS:TVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

===Variants===
[[File:TreadspeederLaunch-TROS.png|thumb|left|300px|Jet troopers could be launched from 125-Z treadspeeders.]]
All First Order jet troopers wore [[jetpack]]s attached to their stormtrooper armor. Those deployed toward the end of the [[First Order-Resistance War]] wore a distinct helmet, donned chest armor with [[Aurebesh]] inscription alongside red markings, and operated with [[G125 projectile launcher]]s. They also had [[C1 personal comlink]]<nowiki/>s to facilitate communication with other [[First Order military|military personnel]]. [[First Order Treadspeeder Driver]] wore similar armor, albeit with standard stormtrooper helmets and more flexible leggings. The [[Sith Eternal military]]'s [[Sith Jetpack Trooper]]s had the same armor design and function as their First Order counterparts, but wore armor plates in red and black colors.<ref name="SW:TRoS:TVD" />

During the [[Cold War]] with the Resistance, a variant of the First Order jet trooper was instead equipped with a standard stormtrooper helmet, a [[control box]] attached to their chest armor, [[F-11D blaster rifle]]s.<ref name="PD 1" /> Around that [[time]], another variant was equipped a similar helmet with a dark [[color]]ed jetpack with straps and could operate in the [[vacuum]] of [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="PD 25" />

==History==
[[File:RocketTroopers-PoeDameron2.png|thumb|right|200px|Jet troopers, each with a standard stormtrooper helmet, operating on Ovanis]]
During a [[mission to Ovanis]] in the Cold War undertaken by Resistance [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] and his [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] to locate [[Lor San Tekka]] for information on [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]]'s location, First Order [[Agent]] [[Terex]] deployed multiple jet troopers alongside [[First Order flametrooper|flametroopers]] with [[unidentified flametrooper transport]]s.<ref name="PD 1" /> Eventually, the First Order forces were defeated on [[Ovanis]].<ref name="PD 3">[[Poe Dameron 3|''Poe Dameron'' 3]]</ref>

Several jet troopers were involved in the [[rescue of Lor San Tekka]] by the Resistance in the [[Great Forveen Nebula]], where they captured San Tekka and attempted to bring him aboard a [[First Order Light Cruiser]], though they were [[Death|eliminated]] by a Resistance retrieval [[squad]].<ref name="PD 25" /> During the First Order-Resistance War, jet troopers were deployed in complement with [[125-Z treadspeeder bike]]s piloted by Treadspeeder Drivers in the [[Mission to Pasaana (Resistance)|mission]] to [[Pasaana]], but they were ultimately defeated by Resistance forces.<ref name="SW:TRoS" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:SWBF2-RocketTrooperFO.jpg|thumb|right|220px|The original depiction of a First Order jet trooper in ''Star Wars Battlefront II'']]
The trooper first appeared in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars comic books|''Star Wars'' comic book]] [[Poe Dameron 1|''Poe Dameron'' 1]], authored by [[Charles Soule]] and illustrated by [[Phil Noto]].<ref name="PD 1" /> They were first identified as "'''rocket trooper[s]'''" in the [[2017]] video game ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'',<ref name="SWBF2" /> before receiving the name "First Order jet trooper" on [[StarWars.com]]'s [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] in [[2019]].<ref name="FO jet trooper DB" />

With ''The Rise of Skywalker'' Update on Battlefront II, the rocket trooper's appearance and name was updated<ref name="SWBF2" /> to match with the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="SW:TRoS" />

According to [[Lucasfilm]]'s collections and exhibitions archivist [[Madlyn Burkert]], the unique design of the jet trooper variant seen in ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' incorporates nods to unused [[Ralph McQuarrie]] designs for the [[First Order snowtrooper|snowtrooper]] for the [[1980]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] and the [[death trooper]]s of the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="D23 2019: JT from SW:TRoS Revealed">{{SW|url=news/d23-expo-2019-jet-trooper-from-star-wars-the-rise-of-skywalker-revealed|text=D23 Expo 2019: Jet Trooper from ''Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker'' Revealed}}</ref>
