'''Ayelixe Fabrico''' was a company that produced [[clothes steamer]]s. It partly shared its name with the [[clothing]] manufacturer [[Ayelixe/Krongbing Textiles]]. Ayelixe Fabrico clothes steamers were available during the [[High Republic Era]] and the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Yord Fandar]] owned one<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> by [[132 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Ayelixe Fabrico were mentioned in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''.<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> The [[Ayelixe Fabrico/Legends|company]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, in which it was first mentioned in "''Arms & Equipment Guide Extras!''," a ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' article written by [[Jeff Grubb]] and published on the website of [[Wizards of the Coast]] on [[October 17]], [[2002]].<ref name="Arms">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=starwars/article/sw20021017web|text=''Arms & Equipment Guide'' Extras!}}</ref>
