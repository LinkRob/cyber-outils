'''Dewbacks''' were thick-skinned [[reptile]]s [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[desert]] [[planet]] of [[Tatooine]], where they were used as beasts of burden, as well as mounts for specialized [[Desert stormtrooper|sandtroopers]] known as [[Dewback trooper]]s.

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Yeah, well. You don't know borcat scat from dewback dung, pal.|[[Temmin Wexley]] to [[Sinjir Rath Velus]]|<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>}}
[[File:KenobiDewback-SWI211.png|thumb|left|250px|A dewback ridden by Obi-Wan Kenobi]]
Rugged [[Reptile|reptilian]]<ref name="Databank" /> [[lizard]]s [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[desert]]s of [[Tatooine]], dewbacks were able to withstand the heat of the world's [[Binary star system|binary suns]], as well as the dust that caused mechanical breakdowns in high-tech conveyances.<ref name="USW" /> They had thick hides<ref name="Databank" /> of scaly<ref name="The Nest">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Nest]]''</ref> [[Color|green]] skin, with long, rounded heads and short tails.<ref name="ANH" /> They plodded through the desert sands on four clawed feet, and while capable of brief bursts of speed,<ref name="Databank" /> typically became lethargic at night and in colder climates.<ref name="USW" /> [[Gender|Female]] dewbacks laid fifty to eighty-five [[egg]]s each [[standard year]]. The [[species]] was so named because of their habit of licking the morning dew from their backs with their<ref name="AEYNTK" /> flicking [[tongue]]s.<ref name="Aftermath" />

Dewbacks ate [[tuber]]s, [[grass]] patches, [[womp rat]]s,<ref name="AEYNTK" /> [[sage]], and other desert [[Plant|vegetation]].<ref name="Bestiary" /> They made strange, lowing noises, similar to that of a [[bantha]].<ref name="TWoaJ">''[[The Weapon of a Jedi: A Luke Skywalker Adventure]]''</ref> Due to their strength and hardiness, dewbacks were well suited for use as transportation.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==Dewbacks in the galaxy==
{{Quote|I really don't know what the mounties are so fussy about. While we futz around like holograms on the dejarik board, these lucky moes get to roll nobly across the desert on dewbacks.|Sardis Ramsin, on Dewback troopers|<ref name="Born in the Storm" />}}
[[File:T-21_Blaster_Rifle_SWL.png|thumb|right|250px|A sandtrooper firing a T-21 repeating blaster astride a dewback]]
Dewbacks were utilized on [[Tatooine]] as plodding but reliable mounts,<ref name="Databank" /> and were ridden by citizens of [[Mos Eisley]] along with other creatures such as [[ronto]]s and [[jerba]]s.<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> They were also used as beasts of burden to haul goods for [[merchant]]s or [[moisture farmer]]s, and pulled [[podracer]] parts to starting grids,<ref name="USW" /> as during the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]] held at the [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]].<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>

[[Gozetta]] kept a dewback corpse among the [[Big game hunter|big-game]] trophies at the [[Xirl hunting lodge]], which was discovered by [[Milo Graf|Milo]] and [[Lina Graf]].<ref name="The Nest" /> [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] owned a dewback while hiding on [[Tatooine]]. He allowed [[Ezra Bridger]] to use his dewback to ride north, getting him and his [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P]] to [[Maul]]'s ship [[Nightbrother (Gauntlet fighter)|''Nightbrother'']] and flying it back to [[Atollon]]. After the pair left, Kenobi rode his dewback to the [[Lars homestead|Lars moisture farm]], spotting [[Luke Skywalker]], whom Kenobi believed to be the [[Chosen One]].<ref name="Twin Suns">{{Rebels|Twin Suns (episode)}}</ref> 

During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], dewbacks served [[stormtrooper]] patrols assigned to Tatooine's [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[garrison]].<ref name="USW" /> These [[Dewback trooper]]s used jolts of electricity to control their mounts, using them to batter down property of enemies of the Empire.<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> When prodded, a dewback would put on a short burst of speed.<ref name="Databank" /> A contingent of [[Desert stormtrooper|sandtroopers]] dispatched on Tatooine to retrieve the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[Death Star plans|plans]] stolen during the [[Battle of Scarif]] utilized native dewbacks as their mounts.<ref name="Sandtroopers" /> Two sandtroopers rode dewbacks as part of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] escort for his meeting with [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] at the [[Hutt]]'s [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] following the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="SW4">[[Star Wars (2015) 4|''Star Wars'' (2015) 4]]</ref> Jabba's [[beastmaster]], [[Malakili]], kept a small [[water shiv]] made from dewback [[bone]].<ref name="ALD">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>

During the New Republic era, two [[bounty hunter]]s [[Toro Calican]] and [[Din Djarin]] saw a [[Jird Loomis|masked man]] on a dewback while [[Hunt for Fennec Shand|hunting]] for [[Fennec Shand]]. When Djarin got a closer look, he noticed a [[tracking fob]] which revealed a dead bounty hunter. After capturing Shand, Djarin went after the drewback who was scared by Shand's blaster shots. When Djarin came back he found Shand dead and rode the dewback back to [[Hangar 3-5]] upon discovering Calican's treachery.<ref name="Gunslinger">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 5: The Gunslinger}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Dewback_btm.jpg|thumb|right|250px|A dewback used in the original version of ''A New Hope'', depicting the creature with some fur.]]
The dewback first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] as an animatronic creation. The [[1997]] [[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition|''Special Edition'']] introduced a new CGI version. ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'' correctly states their average height as six [[Foot|feet]], although incorrectly translates the figure into nine [[meter]]s, rather than two. ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' correctly lists the dewback's average length as nine meters which would be 29.5 feet, but incorrectly lists it as six feet.
