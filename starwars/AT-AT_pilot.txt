'''AT-AT pilots''',<ref name="AT-AT Pilots">{{Databank|at-at-pilots|AT-AT Pilots}}</ref> also known as '''AT-AT Drivers''',<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=AT-AT Driver - Galactic Empire}}</ref> were [[Imperial combat driver]]s specialized in operating the [[Imperial Army]]'s [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT]] [[walker]]s.<ref name="AT-AT Pilots" /> Working in groups of two, they operated the walker's driving and firing controls, and were, in certain cases, overseen by a [[Vehicle commander|commander]] who also occupied the walker's [[cockpit]].<ref name="PrincessLothal">{{Rebels|A Princess on Lothal}}</ref>

==Training==
Pulling the strongest [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[soldier]]s to become the next generation of [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT]] [[pilot]]s, many considered themselves all-powerful. Almost all pilots were [[human]]s averaging 1.83 [[meter]]s (6ft) in height.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref>

==Uniform and equipment==
[[File:Walker_pilots.jpg|thumb|left|300px|An AT-AT cockpit contained two seats on which the walker's pilots operate]]
Donning gear similar to that worn by [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot]]s,<ref name="2020AT-ATCard">{{Topps|set=2020 Topps Star Wars Masterwork|subset=Troopers of the Galactic Empire|cardname=Imperial AT-AT Pilot|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/253067/cid/15634566}}</ref> pilots wore an all-[[Color|white]] insulated jumpsuit. It featured white [[boot]]s and driving gauntlets. Along with sporting a reinforced [[helmet]] and life-support pack on their chest, pilots also had a standard [[blaster pistol]], [[thermal detonator]]s, and [[grenade]]s for potential ground-based encounters.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> Other weaponry at the disposal of AT-AT drivers was the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]] and a larger [[blaster rifle]] model.<ref name="AT-AT Exclusive">{{WebCite|url=https://www.sideshow.com/collectibles/star-wars-imperial-at-at-driver-sideshow-collectibles-100124|text=Imperial AT-AT Driver|author=Sideshow Collectibles|archivedate=20220815033740}} {{C|Exclusive Edition}}</ref>

AT-AT pilots wore specialized insulated suits on frozen [[planet]]s such as [[Hoth]]. These suits were designed to protect their wearer if their AT-AT's pressurized [[cockpit]] was compromised, along with the AT-AT's lack of climate control.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" />

AT-AT pilots working for [[Commander]] [[Barro]] in his [[Barro's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] wielded an [[SE-14r light repeating blaster]].<ref name="TMAG" />
