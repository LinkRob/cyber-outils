'''Drutash grubs''' were a type of [[Grub/Canon|grub]] that were [[farm]]ed as [[Foodstuff|food]] by the [[Sullustan]] [[species]] in large dank caverns on the [[Volcano|volcanic]] [[planet]] [[Sullust]].<ref name="Scarif" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], drutash grubs were mentioned in the English release of "[[Scarif and Other Planets in the Outer Rim]]," an entry in [[De Agostini]]'s [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series<ref name="Scarif" /> that was originally released in Spanish on [[July 20]], [[2021]].<ref name="De Agostini">{{DeAgostini|url=libros-comics/enciclopedia-star-wars|text=Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref> The [[Drutash grub/Legends|grubs]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first mentioned in ''[[X-Wing: The Official Strategy Guide]]'', which was written by [[Rusel DeMaria]] and published in [[1993]].<ref name="X-Wing">''[[X-Wing: The Official Strategy Guide]]''</ref>
