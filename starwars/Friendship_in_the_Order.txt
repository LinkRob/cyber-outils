'''Friendship in the Order''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] adventure ''[[Twi'light Storm]]''. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[February 6]] [[2003]] and took the form of an interview with Master [[Lanius Qel-Bertuk]] on how attachment and friendship fit with the Jedi concept of non-attachment. An excerpt of this conversation was later quoted in [[Jedi vs. Sith: The Essential Guide to the Force (real-life book)|''Jedi vs. Sith: The Essential Guide to the Force'']].
