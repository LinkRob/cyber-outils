'''Astor Grane''' was a [[Jedi Master]] in the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="trials" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="trialsdate">{{TrialsoftheJediDate|18}}</ref> he was present in the bottom of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] at the [[Shrine in the Depths]] assisting with preventing the spread of the [[Blight]]. When the sudden surge of the infection happened, he came out of meditation and noticed his ears were bleeding. At [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]]'s urging, he sat back down and resumed his task.<ref name="trials" />
