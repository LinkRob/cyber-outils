'''Edvard Toth''' is a [[Wikipedia:Hungary|Hungarian]]-born, [[Wikipedia:California|California]]-based multi-disciplinary creative professional and interactive entertainment industry veteran.<ref name="About" /> He notably worked on the [[2000]] video game ''[[Star Wars: Demolition]]'', for which he modeled some and textured all vehicles and set up their object hierarchies, modeled some and textured all level assets, created level-of-detail objects, made ground-tilesets and sky backdrop mattes, textured the [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba's]] [[Jabba's Palace/Legends|Palace]]-themed shell, researched and developed the [[Hologram/Legends|holographic projection]].<ref name="Toth">{{WebCite|url=http://edvardtoth.com/games/star-wars-demolition/#.UfbKP417JXN|text=Star Wars: Demolition|work=[http://edvardtoth.com Edvard Toth's official website]|archivedate=20200129174922}}</ref>
