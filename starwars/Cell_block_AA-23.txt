'''Cell block AA-23''' was a detention cell controlled by the [[Galactic Empire]] on [[Lothal]]. [[Foodstuff|Fruit]] merchant [[Yoffar]] was nearly arrested for [[treason]] by two [[Imperial officer]]s Cumberlayne Aresko and [[Myles Grint]] and was determined to be dispatched to cell block AA-23. But by the help of street urchin [[Ezra Bridger]], he was saved and thanked him by giving one of his [[jogan fruit]]s, although Bridger took more from his basket.<ref name="SoR" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cell block AA-23 was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]'',<ref name="SoR" /> a one-hour television movie that serves as the premiere for the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''.<ref name="Rebels Premiere Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-rebels-spark-of-rebellion-premieres-friday-october-3-on-disney-channel|text=''Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion'' Premieres Friday, October 3 on Disney Channel}}</ref> The name of this cell block is most likely to be an homage to [[Detention Block AA-23]] from [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>
