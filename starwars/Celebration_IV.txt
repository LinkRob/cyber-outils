'''Celebration IV''', commonly abbreviated as '''C4''' or '''CIV''', was the fourth [[Celebration]] of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga. It was held at the [[Wikipedia:Los Angeles Convention Center|Los Angeles Convention Center]] in [[Wikipedia:Los Angeles, California|Los Angeles, California]] from [[May 24]] to [[May 28]], [[2007]]. Its primary purpose was to celebrate the 30th anniversary of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], the original ''Star Wars'' film which debuted on [[May 25]], [[1977]]. May 24 was exclusively for members of the [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace fan club]].

[[Jay Laga'aia]] was the Celebrity Master of Ceremonies for the [[#Concourse stage|concourse stage]], and Doug Steves was the MC for the [[#Behind-the-scenes stage (515A)|behind-the-scenes stage]].

The event brought in over 35,000 people&mdash;the largest attendance of any ''Star Wars'' Celebration until [[Celebration Orlando]].<ref>{{SWArchive|url=community/event/celebration/news20070614.html|text=It's a Hit: A Record-Breaking Celebration IV}}</ref>

==Background==
On [[February 16]], [[2006]], [[StarWars.com]] posted an article hinting at a Celebration IV.<ref>{{Hyperspace|url=hyperspace/about/news/news20060216.html|text=''Cinefex'' Trilogy Set to Hyperspace Members}}</ref> Up until that point, such an event had been only fan speculation. This announcement by the Official Site led fans to believe that [[Lucasfilm]] might be in the planning stages at that point. During a question and answer session at the 2006 Star Wars Disney Weekends, [[Rick McCallum]] confirmed that there would be a Celebration IV in 2007 as fans had expected.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.theforce.net/latestnews/story/Rick_McCallum_Disney_Weekends_98614.asp|text=Rick McCallum & Disney Weekends|author=Roberts, Dustin|date=2006-05-24|archivedate=20081122092212}}</ref> Concurrent with the previous rumor,<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.theforce.net/latestnews/story/Celebration_4_Rumors_98155.asp|text=Celebration 4 Rumors|author=Roberts, Dustin|date=2006-04-24|archivedate=20070103163800}}</ref> on [[May 26]], 2006, StarWars.com confirmed officially that there would be a Celebration IV, and that it would be in Los Angeles, California from May 24 to May 28 of 2007.<ref>{{SWArchive|url=community/event/celebration/news20060526.html|text=''Star Wars'' Celebration IV in Los Angeles To Mark 30th Anniversary of Saga}}</ref> Tickets for the event went on sale at approximately 12:00 PM PST on [[October 30]], 2006, and online pre-registration was available until [[March 31]], 2007.

[[Official Pix]] was again in charge of the Star Wars celebrity autographs, likely with more guests than the previous Celebration (which had 41).<ref>{{SWArchive|url=community/event/celebration/news20070131.html|text=Celebration IV: Look for More Autograph Stars Than Ever!}}</ref>

On [[March 24]], Los Angeles' [[Wikipedia:California Science Center|California Science Center]] offered fans tours of their exhibit ''[[Star Wars: Where Science Meets Imagination]]''.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.californiasciencecenter.org/Exhibits/Weingart/starwars/starwars.php|text=Special Admission Information|archivedate=20070408185821}}</ref><ref>{{WebCite|url=http://theforce.net/latestnews/story/March_24th_Is_Fan_Day_104415.asp|text=March 24th Is Fan Day|author=Roberts, Dustin|date=2007-03-21|archivedate=20070327185732}}</ref>

At the 2006 Comic-Con, [[Stephen J. Sansweet]] stated that footage from the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|3D ''Clone Wars'' cartoon]] would be shown at Celebration IV.

===''Star Wars'' six-film marathon===
[[File:SixMovieMarathon.jpg|thumb|right|180px|Six-Movie Marathon Commemorative Button]]
Beginning on Wednesday, May 23, 2007 at 9:00 AM in Hall K of the Los Angeles Convention Center, there was held an official six-movie Star Wars marathon screened to those who had purchased tickets. The 17-hour long event, including intermissions, rolled directly into the first day of Celebration IV, only open to Hyperspace members. Fans attending the marathon received one of the commemorative buttons created exclusively for the event so that fans could brag that they were there. Tickets were free, available to those attending Celebration IV, but seats were limited and tickets had to be reserved in advance. Concessions were available during the screenings.<ref>{{SWArchive|url=community/event/celebration/news20070314.html|text=Six-Movie Star Wars Marathon at Celebration IV}}</ref>

Fans could register at the Star Wars Celebration site [http://registration.gencon.com/celebration here].

Sansweet stated that the versions of the films to be shown would be the DVD edits, and that he expects the attendance to the marathon to be around 2,000 - 4,200 people.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://ifmagazine.com/feature.asp?article=2020|text=Exclusive: STEVE SANSWEET SNEAK PEAKS STAR WARS' CELEBRATION IV|author=Ferrante, Anthony C.|date=2007-04-09|archivedate=20100102164503}}</ref>

===Celebration store===
Occupying a majority of Exhibit Hall A, the Celebration Store introduced a new way to gain access to their merchandise by way of Quick Path shopping time reservations. Those who purchased a badge online could obtain one 2-hour timeslot at [http:///www.gencon.com/2007/swciv/events/quickpath.aspx the Gencon site]. The store was open 24 hours a day starting at 12 PM Thursday, May 24, and continued until 2 PM Monday, May 28.

In addition, there was a repaint of the Luke Skywalker X-wing helmet mini-helmet released by [[Master Replicas]], made exclusively for the Celebration IV conference and called the "Wedge Antilles Celebration IV Conference exclusive."

===Star Wars celebrity contest===
[[Jay Laga'aia]] hosted the first annual ''Star Wars'' Celebrity Contest from the Concourse Stage, which showcased fans gifted with talents involving Star Wars ranging from songwriters to character impressionists to comedians. Audition videos were uploaded to YouTube under the tag "starwarscelebrity", and were limited to 90 seconds of footage, and the contestants were chosen from these videos. The winner received a life-sized [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]] statue with second place taking home a [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] in [[Carbonite/Legends|carbonite]] statue and third bringing in a [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]]/[[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] bust with removable helmet. All contestants, however, received a consolation prize.

===Costume pageant===
Held on Friday, May 25 and Sunday, May 27 from 4 to 6 PM on the Petree Stage, 75 participants each for two pageants competed for the title of finest costumers in the galaxy.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.gencon.com/2007/swciv/events/costumepageant.aspx|text=Costume Pageant|nobackup=1}}</ref>

===''Fanboys'' preview===
Fans were able to preview scenes from the new Weinstein Company film [[Wikipedia:Fanboys (film)|''Fanboys'']] on the afternoon of Friday, May 25 in the Celebration Theater. On Saturday, May 26, a special panel discussion with the movie's creators and some of the actors was held on the Concourse Stage. A comic book detailing events of the movie was also offered to fans at CIV.
