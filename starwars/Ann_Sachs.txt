'''Ann Sachs''' is an actress. She voiced [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Princess Leia]] in all three [[Star Wars radio dramas|radio adaptations of the original ''Star Wars'' trilogy]].<ref name="Audiosphere">{{InsiderCite|201|Adventures in the Audiosphere}}</ref>

Sachs worked as a professional actress, mostly on the stage, beginning in 1969.<ref name="About" />
