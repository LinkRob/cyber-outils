The '''Chieftain's hut''' was located in the [[Talz village]] on [[Orto Plutonia]]. As the villages main hut, it served as the place for negotiation between the [[Talz]] villagers and the [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] when they were investigating an [[Skirmish on Orto Plutonia|attack]] on a [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] base.<ref name="Trespass">{{TCW|Trespass}}</ref> Like all Talz huts, it was made out of animal skin stretched over [[bone]]s.<ref name="EpGuide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode015.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Trespass}}</ref>
