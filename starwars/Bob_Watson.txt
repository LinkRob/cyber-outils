'''Bob Watson''' is an actor who portrayed a patron of [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina/Legends|Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. According to the website Nerf-Herders Anonymous, he also portrayed a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] [[Technician/Legends|Technician]].
