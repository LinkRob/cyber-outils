The '''Abersyn system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Cyrillian Protectorate]] of the [[Expansion Region]]. It was located in grid square O-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Abersyn symbiote]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
