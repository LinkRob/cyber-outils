'''Fedalle''' was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> within the [[Fedalle system]] in the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was situated along the [[Hydian Way]] [[hyperspace route]] between [[Skako]] and [[Bellassa]], and sat at the head of a [[Fedalle–Kuat hyperlane|second hyperlane]] that linked it to [[Kuat]].<ref name="Map" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fedalle was first mentioned in the ''[[The Jedi Academy Sourcebook]]'', a piece of supplementary material [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] released by [[West End Games]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' in [[1996]].<ref name="JAS">''[[The Jedi Academy Sourcebook]]''</ref> The world was confirmed to exist in [[canon]] when it appeared on a galactic map included with [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a roleplaying game released by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] on [[August 4]], [[2016]].
