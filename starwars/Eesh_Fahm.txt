'''Eesh Fahm''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who worked as a [[miner]] on the [[planet]] [[Ibaar]] about four [[Standard year|years]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]]. Fahm operated an [[Ibaar rebel network|underground network]] that resisted the Empire's rule on Ibaar.

==Biography==
{{Quote|The Empire has doubled our work quotas and has cut our rations.|Eesh Fahm describes the situation on Ibaar|<ref name="WotM" />}}
Eesh Fahm operated an [[Ibaar rebel network|underground network]] on his [[homeworld]], providing aid to his people. After the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Blockade of Ibaar|cut off]] [[Foodstuff|food]] rations to the [[planet]], Fahm worked with [[Phoenix Cell]] in order to bring food through the [[blockade]].<ref name="WotM" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You have our deepest gratitude Phoenix Squadron.|Eash Fahm to the crew of the ''Ghost''|<ref name="WotM" />}}
Eesh Fahm was a determined leader for his people and did all he could to look after them. This quality was shown as Fahm operated an underground network on his homeworld of Ibaar which provided aid to his people. During the blockade of Ibaar, he wore a helmet, a patterned jacket and black gloves.<ref name="WotM" />
