'''Big-Hands Leduu''' was an individual who had joined the [[Rokana Raiders]] [[pirate]] group and was subsequently punished for [[sleep]]ing through the start of their shift<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate|APF=1}}</ref> Their punishment was decided by a crew vote in line with the pirate's [[Rokana Code]], and they were sentenced to stand first watch every [[Standard day|day]] for an entire [[Standard month|month]]. Their offense and punishment were recorded on a list of punishments administered to members of the pirate group, and a copy of the list was stored on a [[datapad]] in the raider's [[brig]] while they were based at the [[Khepi Tomb]] in the [[Sayma Nebula]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> around 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
Around that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] read the datapad detailing Leduu's punishment after she escaped her cell in the brig with the help of [[Nix]], her [[merqaal]] companion.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Big-Hands Leduu was mentioned in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> They were mentioned in a datapad titled "Punishments" which players could read during the "Brave the Unknown" main quest.<ref name="Outlaws" />
