The '''Citadel of Ktath'atn''', also known as the '''Screaming Citadel''' was a citadel located on the [[planet]] [[Ktath'atn]]. [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]] traveled to the Citadel of Ktath'atn with [[Luke Skywalker]] to attend a party hosted by the [[Unidentified Queen of Ktath'atn|Queen of Ktath'atn]] and then get her to open an ancient [[Jedi]] artifact which contained the personality of a Jedi named [[Rur]].<ref name="The Screaming Citadel 1" />
