'''Dee-Twelve''' was a [[droid]] owned by the [[Jerlyn's family|family]] of [[Jerlyn]], a resident of [[Peka]] [[City|village]] on the [[Planet|world]] [[Batuu]]. Around [[379 BBY]], while working on the family's [[Jerlyn's family's farm|farm]] at [[Local day|night]], Dee-Twelve was taken by a [[Unidentified salmaca|salmaca]] that was raiding Peka and brought to its nest. The next [[Standard day|day]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] arrived in Peka, and after speaking with Jerlyn, agreed to recover Dee-Twelve. After traveling to the [[salmaca]]'s [[Salmaca cave|cave]], she successfully rescued Dee-Twelve and returned him to Jerlyn.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Thanks. For bringing Dee-Twelve back. And for whatever you did in there.|Jerlyn, to Rooper Nitani|<ref name="Shield" />}}
Dee-Twelve was a [[droid]] owned by the [[Jerlyn's family|family]] of [[Jerlyn]], a resident of [[Peka]] [[City|village]] on the [[Planet|world]] [[Batuu]]. One [[Local day|night]],<ref name="Shield" /> around [[379 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[Shield of the Jedi]]" establishes that [[Rooper Nitani]] was around eighteen [[Standard year|years]] old during its events. In ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'', which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of to [[382 BBY]], Nitani was fifteen years old. Therefore, "Shield of the Jedi" must take place around [[379 BBY]].</ref> Jerlyn forgot to lock up his family's [[Jerlyn's family's farm|farm]] while Dee-Twelve was plowing a field, which allowed a [[Unidentified salmaca|salmaca]] that was raiding Peka to take Dee-Twelve and bring him to to its nest.<ref name="Shield" /> 

[[File:RooperNitani.png|thumb|right|225px|Dee-Twelve was rescued by Padawan Rooper Nitani (pictured).]]
The next [[Standard day|day]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] arrived in Peka during her [[Rooper Nitani's Jedi Trials|Jedi Trials]], and spoke with Jerlyn, who told her about the [[salmaca]] that had been raiding Peka and how it had taken Dee-Twelve. Jerlyn blamed himself for allowing Dee-Twelve to be taken, and he was upset both because his family could not afford a replacement droid to perform the important work Dee-Twelve did on the [[farm]] and because he considered Dee-Twelve to be part of his family. After hearing this, Nitani agreed to recover Dee-Twelve from the salmaca's [[Salmaca cave|cave]], and Jerlyn provided her with a description of the droid.<ref name="Shield" /> 

After entering the [[cave]] and locating the salmaca's nest, Nitani found Dee-Twelve, who was underneath the [[sleep]]ing salmaca's paw and buried in a pile of electrical components. She [[Force power|used]] [[the Force]] to [[Taming beasts|influence]] the salmaca into rolling over, and then [[Telekinesis|nudged]] the droid free. After setting him down, a series of diodes on Dee-Twelve's casing winked in an elaborate pattern, which Nitani interpreted as either excitement or thanks. Nitani then had [[P3-7A]], a [[church droid]] accompanying her, guide Dee-Twelve out of the cave while she looked for a [[Silandra Sho's shield|shield]] belonging to her [[Silandra Sho|Master]], which she had been tasked with finding in order to complete her [[Jedi Trials]]. After Nitani found the [[Plasma shield|shield]] and exited the cave, she found the two droids with Jerlyn, who thanked her for finding Dee-Twelve.<ref name="Shield" />

==Characteristics==
Dee-Twelve was [[Color|gold]] and black, and about the same size and shape as the [[GT astromech droid|GT model droids]] used by [[Outer Rim Exploration Team|Pathfinder teams]], but slightly more battered. It had modifications to help it interact with farming equipment.<ref name="Shield" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
Dee-Twelve appeared in the short story "[[Shield of the Jedi]]," written by [[George Mann]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Shield" />
