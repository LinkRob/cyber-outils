"'''Easy Money'''" is an adventure scenario written by [[Eric S. Trautmann]] and published in ''[[The Politics of Contraband]]'', a [[West End Games]] supplement to ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. It was published in [[September]] [[1992]] and reprinted in [[Classic Adventures|''Classic Adventures'' Volume 1]], published in [[1995]].

The story is set in the [[New Republic era]], but before the [[Thrawn campaign]]: between [[5 ABY/Legends|5 ABY]] and [[9 ABY/Legends|9 ABY]].

==Plot summary==
A pair of [[Smuggler/Legends|smugglers]] undertake a delivery to the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Seikosha/Legends|Seikosha]] for [[Simms Jonndril]]. The cargo, an [[R1-series astromech droid/Legends|R1-series astromech droid]], contained information relating to a storehouse of the [[Emperor/Legends|Emperor's]] hidden weapons. Several factions, including [[Crime lord/Legends|crime lords]], the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]], and the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]], sought to acquire the [[Droid/Legends|droid]].
