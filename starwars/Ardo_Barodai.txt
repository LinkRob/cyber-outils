'''Ardo Barodai''' was a [[Mon Calamari]] [[New Republic officer|officer]] in the [[New Republic]]'s [[Fleet Intelligence]] who oversaw operations of [[Vanguard Squadron]].<ref name="Squadrons" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Biography==
[[File:ArdoBarodai.jpg|left|thumb|200px|Barodai served with the ''Temperance'' and its battle group]]
Ardo Barodai was a [[Mon Calamari]] who lived on [[Mon Cala]] during the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Occupation of Mon Cala|occupation]] of the [[planet]]. There he<ref name="Squadrons" /> worked as a [[bartender]], listening to [[Imperial officer]]s and passing intelligence to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="DB" />

==Skills and abilities==
Barodai was a skilled strategist and [[spy]]master.<ref name="DB" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ardo Barodai first appeared in the 2020 video game ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]'' where he was voiced by [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keythe_Farley Keythe Farley].<ref name="Squadrons" />
