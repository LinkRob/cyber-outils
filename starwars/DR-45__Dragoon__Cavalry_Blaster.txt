The '''DR-45 "Dragoon" Cavalry Blaster''' was a type of [[blaster]] weapon produced by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]], who used the design of the [[DD6 blaster pistol]] to create the DR-45. A convertible carbine long-barreled variation of the DD6, the DR-45 possessed a quick-release clip-on [[rifle]] housing and buttstock, which allowed the weapon to be configured from [[pistol]] to rifle.<ref name="Gadgets" />

Instead of providing mounted cavalry units with a rifle and sidearm, Merr-Sonn hoped they would prefer to carry a single [[Reconfigurable blaster|reconfigurable weapon]] suitable for scouting, patrolling, and frontline combat situations. However, despite their desires, the DR-45 was rejected by local defense forces, who were unwilling to take the field without a backup weapon.<ref name="Gadgets" />
