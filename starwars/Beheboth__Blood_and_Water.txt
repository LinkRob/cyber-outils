'''Beheboth: Blood and Water''' was a four-part article series from [[June]] [[2004]], featuring the planet [[Beheboth]] and other characters and settings appearing in [[Marvel Comics]]' [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'']] series. The articles were written by [[Cory J. Herndon]] and published as part of the ''[[Planet Hoppers]]'' series on [[Wizards.com]].

==Contents==
===Part 1: Return to Prosperity===
''&hellip;[[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] answers an urgent call for help from an [[Darial Anglethorn|old friend]].''<ref name="Beheboth" />

===Part 2: Who Slew Berl Matoone?===
''&hellip;[[Moisture farmer/Legends|moisture farmer]] and elected peacekeeper Darial Anglethorn investigates a bizarre [[Death/Legends|killing]].''<ref name="Beheboth" />

===Part 3: The Tirrith Trap===
''&hellip;a disgraced former Imperial hatches a plan to seize power on the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim]], starting with [[Beheboth]].''<ref name="Beheboth" />

===Part 4: Hit and Mist===
''&hellip;peacekeeper Anglethorn regains consciousness and Luke learns the fate of an old enemy.''<ref name="Beheboth" />
