'''2013 Topps ''Star Wars'' Galactic Files Series 2''' is a trading card set released by [[Topps]] on [[August 9]], [[2013]]. It is the sequel to Topps' [[2012 Topps Star Wars Galactic Files Series 1|2012 Topps ''Star Wars'' Galactic Files Series 1]] and comes with 350 new base cards. The base set features four categories: Characters, Weapons, Planets and Places.
