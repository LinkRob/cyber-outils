'''Boonta''' was an astronomical object located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]], in grid square S-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], that was connected to [[Bimmisaari system|Bimmisaari]], [[Junkfort Station]], [[Klatooine]], [[Sriluur]], and [[Sy Myrth]] by [[hyperspace route]]s. [[Hondo Ohnaka]] claimed to have begun his career in the criminal underworld on Boonta, in the service of [[Porla]] the Hutt.<ref name="Secrets of the Bounty Hunters">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[Boonta/Legends|Boonta]] was originally first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[1985]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO|Star Wars: Droids]]'' episode "[[The White Witch]]," and first depicted in the episode "[[A Race to the Finish]]" as the location of the [[Boonta Speeder Race]].<ref name="whitewitch">''{{DroidsCite|The White Witch}}''</ref><ref name="racetothefinish">''{{DroidsCite|A Race to the Finish}}''</ref>
