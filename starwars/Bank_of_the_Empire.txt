The '''Bank of the Empire''' was a financial institution linked to the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] and active during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]. The [[Bank/Legends|bank]] is known to have made advertisements in [[High Galactic alphabet|High Galactic]] [[Galactic Basic Standard/Legends|Basic]] in the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|DS-1 Orbital Battle Station]], calling for its residents to invest in the Empire by buying Imperial Bux.
