A [[Gender|female]] freedom fighter with a strong sense of ethics, the '''mother''' of [[Bib'vena]], a [[Rutian]] [[Twi'lek]], passed this morality on to the [[Reproduction|child]]. He also became a freedom fighter, in the [[Anoat sector]] shortly after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="SWU" /> between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="SWT">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> There, Bib'vena assisted [[The Smuggler]], leader of the [[Uprising]] rebel group that [[Iron Blockade|fought]] against [[Governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] and his [[Ubrik Adelhard's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]].<ref name="SWU" />
