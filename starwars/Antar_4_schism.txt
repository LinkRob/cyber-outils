During the [[Separatist Crisis]], the [[moon]] of [[Antar 4]] was embroiled in a '''schism''' between the pro-[[Galactic Republic]] loyalists and those who wished to align with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Tarkin" /> including the [[Gotal Assembly for Separation]] and its radical splinter group, the [[Roshu Sune]]. Despite a protracted [[terrorist]] [[Roshu Sune bombing campaign|bombing campaign]] mounted by the Roshu Sune,<ref name="Propaganda" /> the loyalists were able to retain power until the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], the first conflict of the [[Clone Wars]], after which the moon fell to the Separatists. It remained in Separatist control until the end of the Clone Wars, after which the [[Galactic Empire]]&mdash;which the Republic had been [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] into at the end of the war&mdash;carried out mass arrests and executions on Antar 4. This event was known as the [[Antar Atrocity]].<ref name="Tarkin" />
