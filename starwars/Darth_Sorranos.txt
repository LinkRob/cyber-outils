'''Darth Sorranos''' was a [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] of the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|reconstituted Sith Empire]].

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Sorranos was intended to appear in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', but was ultimately cut. The character exists within the game files with a placeholder model.
