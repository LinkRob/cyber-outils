The '''Black Pits''' were a [[Navigator|navigational]] hazard that had been named by wary [[spacer]]s.<ref name="Vandor" /> They were located in [[the Slice]],<ref name="Slice" /> in the vicinity of<ref name="Vandor" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="GalaxyMap">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> [[Planet|world]] [[Kessel]] as well as the surrounding [[Akkadese Maelstrom]],<ref name="Vandor" /> a region consisting of ionized [[gas]], dust [[cloud]]s, [[water]] vapor, [[ammonia]], [[carbonberg]]s, and [[gravity well]]s.<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> At some point between [[10 BBY]] and approximately [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MidnightBookDate}}</ref> the [[Bartender|barkeep]] known as [[Tivoche Bilure|Midnight]] mentioned the Black Pits as one of Kessel's nearby hazards in a [[Midnight's book|book]] they were [[Author|writing]].<ref name="Vandor" />

==Behind the scenes==
