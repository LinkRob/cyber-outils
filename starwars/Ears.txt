'''Ears''' was the nickname of a [[human]] volunteer of the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He was one of ten personnel who embarked on a Republic and [[Jedi Order]] [[Mission to Angoth|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Angoth]] in order to capture one of the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures used by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]. Ears assisted [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Amadeo Azzazzo]] after he was affected by the [[The Force|Force]]-draining effect of the creatures.<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" />
