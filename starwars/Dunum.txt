'''Dunum''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as an [[Imperial officer|officer]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] in the [[Standard year|years]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]]. 

== History ==
For a time, Dunum served at the [[Imperial Complex (Lothal)|Imperial Complex]] on [[Lothal]] until he was assigned to the office of [[Assessor (title)|Assessor]] [[Potalla]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="assessment" /> In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{EmailCite|email=Wookieepedia:Email/Martin Fisher|creator=[[Martin Fisher]]|date=2016-01-14}} states that "Assessment" takes place between "[[Vision of Hope]]" and "[[Call to Action]]." ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates both of these episodes to [[4 BBY]], meaning that "Assessment" must also take place in 4 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> Dunum accompanied Potalla to Lothal, where she was tasked to evaluate the suitability of [[Commandant]] [[Cumberlayne Aresko]] and [[Taskmaster]] [[Myles Grint]], who oversaw the [[Academy for Young Imperials]]. He reviewed Taskmaster Grint's training methods and found them wanting. Grint accused him of plotting to replace Aresko as head of the academy but was interrupted by a rebel incursion into the academy. While Aresko and Grint organized a response to the incursion, Dunum slipped away and found the [[Mandalorian]] [[Sabine Wren]] and the [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]] raiding one of the facility's armories. He attempted to arrest them but was [[Stun|stunned]] by an unknown assailant. Wren and Chopper escaped through a hole in the wall, but Aresko and stormtroopers arrived to rescue him before the charges the rebels had planted exploded.<ref name="assessment" /> 

Unknown to him, Aresko and Grint framed Dunum by making it appear he had helped Sabine Wren infiltrate the Academy and programmed a [[probe droid]] to stun him to prevent him from succeeding in his arrest. By framing Dunum, they intended to impress Potalla while eliminating a threat to their continued control of the facility. Dunum protested his innocence but was arrested on Potalla's orders nonetheless.<ref name="assessment" />

== Personality and traits ==
Dunum was a human male with blond hair, blue eyes and light skin tone. He believed Taskmaster Grint's training methods were too harsh on the [[Imperial cadet|cadets]] at the Academy and sought to improve the Academy.<ref name="assessment" />
