The [[Nihil]] [[Faksir]] was a member of a [[Sentience|sentient]] '''species''' with [[Color|bright orange]] [[blood]]. The [[species]] was slightly more resilient to the [[poison]] that [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] used to [[Death|kill]] Faksir, as he did not dissolve from the inside-out as Ro had expected. Their blood was also slightly [[acid]]ic, as it had burned Ro when Faksir had spat some of his blood into the Eye's face.<ref name="TrialsOfTheJedi" />

==Behind the scenes==
Faksir's species appeared in the [[2025]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]'', written by [[Charles Soule]] and published by [[Random House Worlds]].<ref name="TrialsOfTheJedi" />
