'''Carmeen''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="What a Festive Evening" /> in the [[Carmeen system]] in the [[Altier sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]], located in grid square O-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> It was the home of the [[Carmeenian]] people and the site of the [[Battle of Carmeen]].<ref name="What a Festive Evening" />
