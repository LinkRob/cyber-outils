'''Dyn Pilga''' was a [[human]] who worked as a lumberjack in [[summer]] and a [[trapper]] in [[winter]] on the [[planet]] [[Ueda]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. In [[132 BBY]], he visited the [[Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop]] in [[Point Ueda]] and sat talking with the [[Jedi Master]] [[Indara]]. When the [[Sith assassin]] [[Mae-ho Aniseya]] approached and demanded that Indara attack her, Pilga laughed. When Aniseya then attacked those sitting with the [[Jedi]], Pilga got up and fought back, but the [[assassin]] defeated Pilga and then [[Indara's murder|killed]] Indara.

[[File:DynPilgaLaughing-LostFound.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Dyn Pilga and Lobereki sit together laughing at a table in the Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop.|Dyn Pilga laughed at Mae-ho Aniseya's demand to fight Indara.]]
Dyn Pilga was a [[human]] local to the [[planet]] [[Ueda]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. During the world's [[summer]]s, he worked as a lumberjack and cleared out overgrowth in the coastal [[forest]]s of Ueda's archipelagos. In the [[winter]], he would instead work as a [[trapper]] and helped keep the size of the [[pinedriller]] population under control.<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> Pilga visited<ref name="LostFound" /> the [[Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop]] in [[Point Ueda]], the [[capital]] of Ueda,<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> and sat at a table in the back corner<ref name="LostFoundAD">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> with<ref name="LostFound" /> three other locals: [[Lobereki]],  [[Ohal Alond]], and [[Tianris Mez]],<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> plus the [[Jedi Master]] [[Indara]].<ref name="LostFound" />

Pilga was talking to Indara when the [[Sith assassin]] [[Mae-ho Aniseya]] approached the table and addressed Indara, telling the [[Jedi]] that they had unfinished business. She then lowered herself into a fighting stance and demanded that Indara attack her. After a moment of silence, Mez began laughing, and Pilga and the other two locals quickly joined in; however, Indara silenced them with a hand motion and then declined to fight. Angered by Indara's claim that Jedi did not attack the unarmed, Aniseya then attacked Alond and subdued both him and Mez when the latter tried to intervene. Pilga and Lobereki then leapt up from their side of the table, and the trapper swung a fist at Aniseya, although she dodged and punched him in the [[stomach]], causing him to hunch over and stagger away.<ref name="LostFound" />

[[File:DynPilgaHeadlock-LostFound.png|thumb|left|300px|alt=Mae-ho Aniseya stands in the Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop with Dyn Pilga pulled in a reverse headlock over he shoulder.|Dyn Pilga was defeated in combat by Mae-ho Aniseya.]]
After Aniseya threw Lobereki across a table, Pilga lunged at her again to grab her, but the assassin dodged and kicked him before flipping him into a reverse headlock. As she held a struggling Pilga, Aniseya saw Lobereki go for his [[blaster]] and so dropped Pilga with a punch to the chest before leaping at the last of Indara's four dining companions. Pilga then remained sprawled on the floor as Aniseya defeated Lobereki and finally Indara, whom she [[Indara's murder|killed]]. The [[Jedi Order]] later investigated the incident.<ref name="LostFound" />

==Personality and traits==
Pilga felt protective of Ueda's environment. He had light skin, [[Color|brown]] [[hair]], and [[eye]]s.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==Equipment==
While at the [[noodle]] shop, Pilga wore a black [[hat]] made from [[pinedriller wool]], a gray [[Clothing|shirt]], and a green sleeveless [[jacket]] with a brown [[bandolier]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
