This '''individual''' was a [[Diathim]] who took up primary residence on [[Iego]] after her homeworld [[Millius Prime]] was invaded by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]. They met with [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] during their [[Mission to Iego|mission]] to free the people of [[Iego]] from the malevolent [[Deity|god]] called "[[Drol]]." After hearing from their words, the two [[Jedi]] deduced that Drol was not a god, but in fact a Confederate [[LaserWeb Defense Station]]. They successfully destroyed the station and freed the people of Iego as a result.<ref name="Mystery of a Thousand Moons" />

==Personality and traits==
As a member of the Diathim [[species]], the refugee was 2.35 [[meter]]s (7 [[Foot|feet]], 9 inches) tall.<ref name="TCWE" /> They had [[Color|blonde]] [[hair]], gray [[eye]]s, glowing white skin, a yellow aura and six [[Limb|wings]].<ref name="Mystery of a Thousand Moons" />
