==Events==

*[[Canonhaus]] becomes a [[midshipman]] with the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Canonhaus">The events of "[[The Final Order]]" occur parallel to the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]]. ==Births==
*[[Enric Pryde]]<ref name="Timelines" /> on [[Alsakan]].<ref name="TROS Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
*[[Gideon Hask]]<ref name="Hask Birth date">''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]'' establishes Gideon Hask is twenty-seven [[Standard year|years]] old. As this is mentioned three [[Standard day|days]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[0 ABY]], it places Hask's [[Reproduction|birth]] in 27 BBY.</ref>
*[[Milo Graf]]<ref name="Milo Graf's Birth">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Escape]]'' is set a "[[Standard year|year]] or so" after the end of the [[Clone Wars]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates as [[19 BBY]]; thus, it can be deduced that the story takes place around [[18 BBY]]. It can also be deduced that Milo Graf was born circa 27 BBY, since ''Adventures in Wild Space: The Escape'' establishes that he was nine years old during that [[time]].</ref>
*[[Nera Kase]]<ref name="Kase">The events of "[[Grounded]]" occur parallel to the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[0 ABY]]. In "Grounded," Kase is said to be "three years shy of thirty," which places her birth in 27 BBY.</ref>
