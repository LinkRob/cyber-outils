'''Coastal defender stormtroopers'''&mdash;more commonly known as '''shoretroopers'''<ref name="UVG" /> and also known as the '''coastal guard''',<ref name="IT">{{EncyclopediaCite|Imperial Troops}}</ref> with those stationed on the [[planet]] [[Scarif]] referred to as '''Scarif stormtroopers'''<ref name="Vintage Collection">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Vintage Collection|pack=#133: Scarif Stormtrooper|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/VC133-Scarif-Stormtrooper-The-Vintage-Collection-Hasbro.asp}}</ref>&mdash;were a specialized variant of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[stormtrooper]]s trained and equipped for combat in tropical environments.<ref name="Commander" /> Organized into the [[Coastal Defense Division]],<ref name="Imperial Troops French" /> shoretroopers operated under the command of [[sergeant]]s, leading [[squad]]s of regular stormtroopers. One of the notable aspects of coastal defender stormtroopers was that the position they served in was temporary and subject to change along with their rank. On a [[Galaxy|galactic]] scale, shoretroopers as a whole were scarce, as the conditions for necessitating their employment were few and far between, as few stations or garrisons were located on coastal/tropical planets.<ref name="UVG" />

==History==
===Imperial Era===
[[File:BustCollection9-Scarif-Troopers.png|thumb|left|300px|Shoretroopers and stormtroopers fight against the rebel incursion on Scarif.]]
Many coastal defender stormtroopers were stationed at the top secret [[Imperial Center of Military Research|Imperial security complex]] on the tropical planet [[Scarif]], where they patrolled the [[beach]]es and bunkers of the facility.<ref name="Databank" /> [[Del Meeko]], later a member of [[Inferno Squad]], served as a shoretrooper on Scarif prior to becoming a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]].<ref name="Inferno Squad">''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]''</ref> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> shoretroopers participated in the [[Battle of Scarif]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], where many of them were killed when the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] blew up the Imperial security complex to wipe out the remaining Rebels.<ref name="Rogue One" />

Around [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> shoretroopers came into [[Escape from an unidentified ocean planet|conflict]] with Rebel leaders [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Luke Skywalker]] after they crashed on an [[Unidentified ocean planet|ocean planet]].<ref name="Star Wars 33" />

Around [[3 ABY]], shoretroopers came into a [[Mission to Bestine IV|conflict]] on [[Bestine IV]], where two were beheaded by [[Tasu Leech]], when he and [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] attempt to destroy the [[Unidentified Imperial transport|transport]]'s [[Engine|engines]] so [[T'onga's crew]] can infiltrate a [[transport]]. During the fight, [[T'onga]] and the rest of the crew were cornered by more shoretroopers.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 29">[[Bounty Hunters 29|''Bounty Hunters'' 29]]</ref> Upon being rounded up and waiting for their [[cyborg]] officer's instructions, Leech began to mock the troopers, ignore [[Zuckuss]] warning not to, and added that [[Stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] were not known for their aim. One shoretrooper then hit Leech with his E-11 blaster rifle, stating he could hit him pretty good from where they were. The shoretrooper threaten to kill the prisoners but [[Beilert Valance]] intervened and killed the trooper, which the other shoretroopers open fire on Valance.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 30">[[Bounty Hunters 30|''Bounty Hunters'' 30]]</ref>

===New Republic Era===
During the [[New Republic Era]], shoretroopers served among the [[Morak Imperial remnants|grouping of Imperial remnants]] based on the [[planet]] [[Morak]], where they worked to defend an [[Morak Imperial refinery|Imperial refinery]] from insurgent and rebel attacks in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="The Believer" />

During the [[Cold War]] between the [[Resistance]] and the [[First Order]], [[Agent]] [[Terex]] of the [[First Order Security Bureau]] had possession of a shoretrooper [[helmet]] aboard the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'' that was displayed next to the helmets of an [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Imperial Royal Guard]], an [[AT-AT pilot]], a [[death trooper]], and others, along with a custom set of [[armor]].<ref name="Dameron 8">[[Poe Dameron 8|''Poe Dameron'' 8]]</ref> The [[First Order military]] also fielded [[mountain trooper]]s who wore armor that resembled that of the Imperial shoretroopers.<ref name="FO 4Pack">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|pack=The First Order|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/story/actionfigure/Hasbro_The_Black_Series_6Inch_First_Order_Disney_Parks_Exclusive_Figure_4Pack_183277.asp}}</ref><ref name="Rogue One" />

==Armor and equipment==
[[File:Shoretrooper_Squad_Leader_-_Hasbro.png|thumb|right|200px|A shoretrooper squad leader]]
Shoretroopers wielded both the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]] and the upgraded [[E-22 reciprocating double-barreled blaster rifle]] while stationed on Scarif.<ref name="Launch Bay">{{YouTube|video=LWS1elNXu54|text=NEW Star Wars Launch Bay Rogue One Additions & Changes Update|channel=@TheDaniDiaries|name=The Dani Diaries}} {{C|at 7m24s}}</ref> They also carried a [[C-25 fragmentation grenade]], a highly explosive [[grenade]] that was used to destroy ''[[SW-0608]],'' an Imperial cargo [[shuttle]] the [[Rogue One]] team used to bypass the Empire's [[Shield Gate]] in [[orbit]] over the world.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> Other equipment used by shoretroopers included [[zap rod|zap rods]]<ref name="Narkina">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref> and the [[E-11P blaster]], a type of [[blaster pistol]] with water-sealed cooling vanes, which themselves were complete with sand filters.<ref name="Dawn of Rebels">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> When not in use, the pistol was worn on a holster.<ref name="Narkina" />

There were several distinct colorations of shoretroopers, all with unique markings on their [[shoretrooper armor]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Regular shoretroopers featured sand-[[color]]ed armor, with a red band around their right [[Limb|arm]] and a white stripe around their left shoulder guard. [[Squad]] leaders were identified by a sand blue stripe that went along the top of their breastplates and onto the top of their shoulder guards. Squad leaders also sported a [[kama]] attached to their [[belt]].<ref name="Ranks">{{WebCite|url=http://makingstarwars.net/2016/10/highlights-from-the-hasbro-panel-at-nycc|text=HIGHLIGHTS FROM THE HASBRO PANEL AT NYCC!|author=Ward, Amanda|date=2016-10-06|work=[http://makingstarwars.net Making Star Wars]|archivedate=20170501202519}}</ref> Shoretrooper [[captain]]s had almost all of their breastplates blue, along with a small plate of armor that was on their left side. Their right shoulder guard was sand colored, but their left shoulder guard was all sand blue, save for the white stripe. Further down the same arm were stripes of sand blue and yellow. They also had limited or no upper leg armor, similar to [[scout trooper]]s.<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref>

[[File:E22BlasterRifle-ImperialForces.png|thumb|left|300px|Shoretroopers commonly used E-22 reciprocating double-barreled blaster rifle.]]
Another variant of shoretrooper armor came with the [[Coastal defender stormtrooper specialist|shoretrooper snipers]], whose armor featured red accents and were deployed as snipers.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> [[Heavy Shoretrooper]]s, meanwhile, wielded heavy weaponry and were equipped with extra ammo.<ref name="Commander" /> On [[Niamos]], a shoretrooper coloration variant existed in [[5 BBY]]. This shoretrooper variant could also be seen with a white line going across their upper breastplate, a red band on their lower-right arm, a bag on their upper-right leg, and white shoulder armor, the left of which had two black stripes across it. Shoretroopers guarding the [[Niamos Courthouse]] used zap rods as well as the E-11P blaster and featured a white band with two black lines on the edges on their upper-left arm, and a red band on their lower-right arm.<ref name="Announcement" />

==Behind the scenes==
The first footage of the shoretrooper was shown in the teaser trailer for the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Anthology Series|standalone]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', on [[April 7]], [[2016]].<ref name="Teaser">{{SWYouTube|2BDyeARyIkw|Rogue One: A Star Wars Story Trailer Tease}}</ref> The shoretroopers were identified by name in the third episode of ''[[The Star Wars Show]]'' on [[May 25]], 2016.<ref name="SW Show">{{StarWarsShow|-IVW0aIO2Lg|Chewbacca Mom, Rogue One Character Reveal, A Star Wars Birthday Party|sw_url=video/the-star-wars-show-episode-3}}</ref>

According to Production Designer Doug Chiang: "Glyn [Dillon] actually modeled the color scheme on the [WWII fighter] Messerschmitt, where the base color that was sort of tannish, but then it had patches of this blue and orange that were so elegant. Again, it makes that reinforcement of connecting it with something that we know from history, it makes that connection to Episode IV, which was World War II technology, vehicles, and colorations. So the shoretrooper, I think, is a wonderful blend of all those requirements into something new."<ref name="Chiang">{{SW|url=news/we-set-the-bar-so-high-doug-chiang-on-designing-rogue-one|text="We Set the Bar So High": Doug Chiang on Designing ''Rogue One''}}</ref>
