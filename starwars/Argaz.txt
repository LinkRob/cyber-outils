'''Argaz''' was an [[Ereesi/Legends|Ereesi]] who served in the [[Nihil]] as a [[She'ar]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Tempest Breaker" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|TB}}</ref> Argaz attended a [[Gala on Waskiro|gala]] hosted by [[Waskiro]]'s [[Royal House of Jadalla]] along with the gala's guest of honor, [[Marchion Ro]], [[Eye of the Nihil]]. When two of the other She'ar disappeared at the gala, Ro handed over the chain leash of the [[Great Leveler|Leveler]] to Argaz so the two could search for [[Jedi]]. In a [[Palace of Jadalla|palace]] courtyard, the Leveler began to become rowdy in its thirst for the hunt, and Argaz released it. Later in the evening, [[bounty hunter]] and former Nihil [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]] bit Ro in his neck, and Argaz rushed to the Eye's aid, soon heeding the leader's orders to [[death|murder]] everyone in attendance who may have seen him bleed. In the ensuing battle, Dee [[blaster|shot]] Argaz.<ref name="Script">[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker (script)|''The High Republic: Tempest Breaker'' script]]</ref>
