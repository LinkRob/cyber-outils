'''Coronation Eve''' was a [[Pijali]] holiday earmarking the [[Standard day|day]] before a [[Pijali monarchy|Pijal monarch's]] coronation that was celebrated on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]].<ref name="MA" /> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> Coronation Eve was celebrated across the world the day prior to [[Princess]] [[Fanry]]'s [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|crowning]]. Staff within the [[Pijali palace|palace]] hurriedly prepared for their future [[queen]]'s [[crown]]ing during the holiday, and a [[Unidentified cook|cook]] worked to ensure that there would be ample [[fruit]] displays for the upcoming celebrations. The same day, [[Minister]] [[Orth]] and [[Lord]] [[Regent]] [[Rael Averross]] conducted a final inspection of the world's [[Celestial Chalice]] temple.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coronation Eve appeared in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
