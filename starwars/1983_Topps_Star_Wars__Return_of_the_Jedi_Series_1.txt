'''1983 Topps ''Star Wars: Return of the Jedi'' Series 1''' was a trading card set released by [[Topps]] in [[1983]].<ref name="Volume Three" />

The set was based on [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], and featured stills from the film and promotional photos, all within a red and yellow border. The backs of the cards include character profiles and tell the story of the movie.<ref name="Volume Three" />

A sub-set of sticker cards were included, the backs of which included a portion of a larger image that could be pieced together with other cards like a puzzle.<ref name="Volume Three" />

A [[1983 Topps Star Wars: Return of the Jedi Series 2|second]] and final series of the set would follow.

==Continuity==
Card 36 features an image from a cut scene where Luke Skywalker hangs from a grate above the rancor pit. The scene appears in the [[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (novelization)|''Return of the Jedi'' novelization]], ''[[Return of the Jedi: The Storybook Based on the Movie]]'', and in behind the scenes sources.<ref name="Making">''[[The Making of Return of the Jedi]]'' Enhanced ebook edition, clip here: {{YouTube|video=b6-WYw3BGhM|text=The Making of Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (Enhanced Edition) Ebook video clip (CH09-VIDEO_02)|channel=channel/UCFBtqzQ7-nFI2lriMxnXC-w|name=Tad Crutcher}}</ref>
