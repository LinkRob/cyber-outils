The '''Anoat sector''' was a [[Sector|subsector]] of the [[Javin sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Appendix" /> forming part of the [[Greater Javin]] region.<ref name="Uprising Art" /> It included multiple [[Planet|worlds]] such as [[Bespin]], [[Burnin Konn]], and [[Hoth]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> The sector was once administered from the [[Ecumenopolis|city-world]] of [[Anoat]], though the capital was [[Gassing of Anoat|moved]] to [[Gerrenthum]] during the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Uprising" />

The Anoat sector was valuable in part thanks to the [[tibanna]] gas generated and refined on the gas giant Bespin.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> It was historically ruled by the [[Noble Court]], the [[Nothoiin (species)|Nothoiin]] aristocracy that for centuries maintained the sector's independence from the [[Galactic Republic]]. Other regional powers included the [[Trade Spine League]] and the [[Hutt]]-led [[Ivax Syndicate]],<ref name="Uprising" /> as well as a number of cults and secret societies like the [[Kouhun (order)|Kouhun]] that promoted warfare and bloodshed.<ref name="Reformed Cultist">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Reformed Cultist"</ref>

In the [[Standard day|days]] following the [[Battle of Endor]], the sector was [[Iron Blockade|blockaded]] by [[Governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]], who sought to enforce the authority of his [[Ubrik Adelhard's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]].<ref name="Uprising" />
