The '''''Castellan''''' was a [[cruiser]] operational during the [[High Republic Era]]. After falling under [[Attack on the Castellan|attack]] by a [[fleet]] of [[Nihil]] [[starship]]s, the ''Castellan'' made an emergency jump to [[hyperspace]], crashing on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]. In the many years following the crash, the wreckage was scavenged for parts by scavengers. During the [[Imperial Era]], the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] entered the wreckage of the cruiser in search of a [[Navigation computer|nav computer]] for the [[EML-850 light freighter]] ''[[Trailblazer]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
