'''Ap'lek's Mandalorian executioner vibro-ax''' was an antiquated [[Munit'kad halberd|Mandalorian executioner's vibro-ax]] that was the weapon of choice for the [[Knights of Ren|Knight of Ren]] [[Ap'lek]]. Ap'lek fought with the [[vibro-ax]] [[Battle of Exegol|against]] [[Ben Solo]] on [[Exegol]], but it availed him naught and he perished at the hands of his former leader.

==Description==
[[File:ApleksVibroAx-TROSSeries2Weapons.png|left|thumb|250px|Ap'lek wielding his vibro-ax]]
A [[Munit'kad halberd|Mandalorian executioner's vibro-ax]] forged in ancient [[time]]s, it was equipped with ultrasonic transmission plates that enhanced its cutting power, turning the [[Axe|ax]] into a [[vibro-ax]]. It was also made of [[beskar]], alluding to the weapon's [[Mandalorian]] origins and which helped it to resist the blade of a lightsaber. There were gore channels carved into the face of the blade, as well as vibro-insulator plates to protect the handle of the ax from the vibrations.<ref name="Episode IX VD" />

==History==
The [[Vibro-weapon|vibro weapon]] was used by [[Ap'lek]], a member of the [[Knights of Ren]], in conjunction with his [[Smoke grenade|smoke canister]], incorporated so for his preference for stealth.<ref name="Episode IX VD" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}} to [[35 ABY]].</ref> the weapon was used to [[Battle of Exegol|battle]] [[Ben Solo]], former leader of the [[Knights of Ren]], within the [[Sith Citadel]] on [[Exegol]]. The axe was ultimately left forgotten on the battlefield, as its wielder had dropped it when he was [[force push]]ed to his death.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
