The '''''Aurora''''' was a specialized refueling [[tanker]] utilized by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval forces]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. It possessed a series of oval-shaped [[fuel]] storage tanks, which were separated from each other by several [[hangar]] bays. From these bays, Separatist star [[frigate]]s were able to dock with the tanker and gain fuel.<ref name="BMF 34" />

At some point during the war, the ''Aurora'' became [[Attack on the Aurora|targeted]] in a mock attack by a [[squadron]] of Republic [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] in an attempt to draw out the [[battleship]] known as the ''[[Starcrusher]]''. However, before the Republic could force the ''Starcrusher'' into battle, the ''Aurora'' was able to flee from the [[star system]] and the [[Star Destroyer]]s.<ref name="BMF 34" />

Barr]], illustrated by the [[Fillbach Brothers]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Starcrusher">''[[The Clone Wars: The Starcrusher Trap]]''</ref> on [[July 27]], [[2011]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Books/17-857/Star-Wars-The-Clone-Wars-The-Starcrusher-Trap|text=Star Wars: The Clone Wars: The Starcrusher Trap}}</ref>
