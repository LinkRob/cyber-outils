'''Bakkar''', nicknamed "'''the Butcher'''," was a [[human]] who owned the [[restaurant]] [[Ronto Roasters]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. A big fan of [[podracing]], Bakkar originally [[Homeworld|hailed]] from the planet [[Tatooine]] and came from a [[Bakkar's family|family]] that worked as butchers for four generations. Bakkar opened his restaurant with the goal of bringing his famous [[ronto]] meat to new customers after moving to Batuu with his [[8D-series smelter droid|droid]], [[8D-J8]].

Bakkar had an unusual way of grilling his meats, utilizing a [[podracer engine]] that he purchased and modified. In [[34 ABY]], 8D-J8's [[Programmer|reprogramming]] that was intended to make the [[Smelter droid|droid]] man the grill had the side effect of him talking too much; in order to solve the issue, Bakkar contacted the [[Utai]] [[Mubo]]&mdash;8D-J8's original programmer&mdash;who eventually agreed to help fix the [[droid]].

==Biography==
===Opening a restaurant===
{{Quote|This human is a fourth-generation butcher from Tatooine who uses the flames from a modified podracer engine to cook up giant hunks of ronto (a pack animal from his planet) to perfection.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
[[File:RontoRoasters-TGTB.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Bakkar opened Ronto Roasters.]]
Bakkar was a fourth-generation butcher who [[Homeworld|grew up]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]],<ref name="Cookbook" /> where<ref name="guide" /> his<ref name="ge:bs" /> [[Bakkar's family|family's]] specialty was the [[ronto]] creature. He was a big fan of the [[podracing]] sport even after it was outlawed across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="guide" /> He sold different kinds of specialty meats, but like his family, he was largely known for ronto. He left Tatooine when he came of age, [[dream]]ing of selling his family's famous meat on another [[Outer Rim Territories]] planet. Once he had earned enough [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], he bought a [[8D-series smelter droid|smelter droid]], [[8D-J8]],<ref name="staff" /> who was later designated as a [[pitmaster droid]]. 8D-J8 never left his side.<ref name="guide" />

Bakkar traveled to the Outer Rim planet [[Batuu]], where he bought a [[Podracer engine|podracing engine]] from [[Savi & Son Salvage]] at a fair price that he could use to cook his meats. With his ronto recipe ready to go,<ref name="staff" /> he opened a [[restaurant]] called [[Ronto Roasters]] in the [[Black Spire Outpost]] [[City|settlement]] in order to bring the ronto to new customers.<ref name="guide" /> Bakkar built a large, elaborate mechanical grill,<ref name="staff" /> with 8D-J8 serving as the pitmaster of the restaurant,<ref name="guide" /> as Bakkar had had<ref name="staff" /> the [[Utai]]<ref name="guide" /> [[droid]] scrapper [[Mubo]] [[Programmer|reprogram]] him so that his sole duty was to turn the spit, making sure that the meat was cooked evenly and well.<ref name="staff" />

Bakkar employed several others to help run the establishment alongside the [[Smelter droid|droid]].<ref name="guide" /> The [[human]] was a [[collector]] of podracing flags, which he displayed in Ronto Roasters despite the sport being banned. He also enjoyed watching reruns of races, showcasing his fandom. Due to his being a butcher, Bakkar was aptly nicknamed "the Butcher."<ref name="staff" />

===Best butcher in town===
{{Quote|You want to sink your teeth into the most delicious meat in the Outer Rim? Then head on over to the market and talk to Bakkar.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
[[File:Ronto_Wrap_SWGE.jpg|thumb|right|270px|The Ronto Wrap was Bakkar's most popular dish.]]
Bakkar and his pitmaster sold several meats and [[vegetable]]s at Ronto Roasters, but the establishment's best-selling dish was the [[Ronto Wrap]],<ref name="Cookbook" /> which was made with the signature [[Clutch Sauce]]. Ronto Roasters also sold drinks, with Bakkar's signature beverage being the [[Tatooine Sunset]].<ref name="guide" /> On multiple occasions, the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] tried to persuade Bakkar to share his family's secret spice rub for Ronto Wraps, but Bakkar always refused. Tuggs respected it being a secret, but he still attempted to make out the recipe for himself.<ref name="Cookbook" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Cookbook date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs published a [[Book|cookbook]] titled ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''. In the book, he discussed Bakkar and his secret recipe while also proclaiming that Bakkar's stand in Black Spire Outpost had the "most delicious" meat one could find in the Outer Rim Territories.<ref name="Cookbook" /> In the same time,<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]] also discussed Bakkar's restaurant and signature dishes in his book ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', recommending the [[Foodstuff|food]] there.<ref name="guide" /> [[Bakkar Spire]], a location in Black Spire Outpost, shared its name with Bakkar.<ref name="cards">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge trading cards|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge trading cards]]</ref> In the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="date2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the local [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Salju]] took [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]] on a tour of Black Spire Outpost that included a visit to Ronto Roasters. Salju told Moradi about Bakkar and his nickname of "the Butcher," and she warned the spy not to bring up podracing around him, as he was "crazy" for it.<ref name="ge:bs" />

In Strono Tuggs's second cookbook, ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]'', the Artiodac included a recipe for an [[Eopie Roast]] made of the [[eopie]] creature. While Tuggs had initially been wary of using the eopie's tough meat, after sampling a slice of its rump from Bakkar at Ronto Roasters, he was impressed by how tender Bakkar was able to make it as well as how seemingly magical his special seasoning for it was.<ref name="Life Day">''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]''</ref> As with his ronto seasoning,<ref name="Cookbook" /> Bakkar was not quick to share the secret even with a friend such as Tuggs. The chef was only able to obtain the recipe by trading him a [[vibroknife]], which he felt was more than a fair deal.<ref name="Life Day" />

===A troublesome droid===
{{Quote|Hey, look, Bakkar. I've reprogrammed 8D-J8 four, five, six times already. You're never happy!''"<br />"''Mubo&hellip;you need to do it again or I'm going to put a restraining bolt on him.|Mubo and Bakkar|<ref name="dp" />}}
[[File:Mubo-TGTB.png|thumb|left|280px|Bakkar contacted Mubo (pictured).]]
Also in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which include those described in [[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad]], take place in [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Bakkar messaged Mubo, as he was frustrated with Mubo's reprogramming of 8D-J8. Mubo insisted that his job had been done correctly, as the droid turned the spit, but Bakkar told him that his droid refused to stop talking. Mubo asked if Bakkar had tried deactivating and reactivating him in order to fix the issue.<ref name="dp" />

Mubo then reminded Bakkar that he had already reprogrammed 8D-J8 several times and that Bakkar was simply never pleased with the results. Bakkar insisted that another reprogramming was necessary, or he would need to fasten a [[restraining bolt]] onto the droid. Mubo beseeched him not to put a bolt on the pitmaster, but Bakkar pointed out that 8D-J8 needed to stop talking as he had begun thinking himself to be a droid chef despite simply being a pitmaster. Mubo relented, and despite being swamped with orders, he told Bakkar that he would stop by later.<ref name="dp" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You gotta reprogram this droid, I can't take it anymore!|Bakkar|<ref name="dp" />}}
Bakkar had a love for podracing and was willing to talk about it at length,<ref name="ge:bs" /> and he displayed boyish passion when watching reruns of the sport. He also longed to share his ronto meat with others.<ref name="staff" /> He was overwhelmed by his droid's constant talking, and despite the programmer Mubo's resistance to performing yet another reprogramming on 8D-J8, Bakkar's threat of using a restraining bolt on the machine forced the Utai to give in. Bakkar was a human with [[Color|light]] skin, light-colored [[hair]], and dark [[eye]]s.<ref name="dp" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|There's only one planet to go to if you want to taste the best ronto that's ever been cooked&mdash;Batuu. At his stand in Black Spire Market, Bakkar the Butcher works his magic roastin' huge ronto shanks under an open flame.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
Bakkar was a skilled chef, having been a fourth-generation butcher.<ref name="Cookbook" /> His dishes were praised in both ''Traveler's Guide to Batuu''<ref name="dp" /> and ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''. The latter noted, however, that it was possible that most of the credit could be given to 8D-J8.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Bakkar-Cookbook.jpg|thumb|right|260px|Bakkar, as depicted in ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook'']]
Bakkar was created as part of the backstory for the [[In-universe|real-world]] Ronto Roasters restaurant at the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] themed land,<ref name="rr">{{GEAttr|Ronto Roasters}}</ref> which opened in [[2019]] at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]].<ref name="GE date">{{D23|url=a-to-z/ronto-roasters|text=Disney A to Z: Ronto Roasters}}</ref> He then made a full appearance in the [[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad]] app, which pictured him for the first time.<ref name="dp" />

Although ''Star Wars'': Datapad depicts Bakkar as an individual with short, light-colored hair,<ref name="dp" /> he is shown with long, brown hair in the 2019 recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''.<ref name="Cookbook" /> As Datapad uses a live-action image<ref name="dp" /> as opposed to a drawn illustration, such as the one featured in the cookbook,<ref name="Cookbook" /> this article presents Datapad's image as the [[canon]]ical depiction.
