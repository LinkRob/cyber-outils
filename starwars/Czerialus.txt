'''Czerialus''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="TOG" /> in the [[Czerialus system]] in the [[Kailion sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Czerialan]] [[species]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], the planet was under [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] control and the [[Xoczuko family]] sought to return after being disheartened on [[Corellia]], unaware of the Empire's control on the planet.<ref name="TOG" />
