The '''Eye of the Nihil''' was a position held by a leading member of the [[Nihil]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Like his [[Parent|father]], [[Asgar Ro]], before him, [[Marchion Ro]] was a member of the Nihil marauders who held the important post of Eye of the Nihil, and although he did not give orders&mdash;which the [[Tempest Runner]]s were responsible for&mdash;everything revolved around Ro, who provided the factors that made the Nihil more than just a horde of [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Pirate|raiders]].<ref name="Villains" /> The Eye of the Nihil had a tie-breaking vote in matters related to Paths.<ref name="Inside Intel: The Nihil Hierarchy">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-inside-intel-the-nihil-hierarchy|text=''Star Wars'' Inside Intel: The Nihil Hierarchy|int=Star Wars Inside Intel}}</ref> The Eye was placed in charge of protecting, discovering, and distributing [[Path]]s. This changed in [[232 BBY]] when Marchion Ro had united all of the [[Tempest]]s under his rule after the [[Battle of Kur]] by promising to expand their operations throughout [[the galaxy]] and defend their way of life from the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> The Jedi were unaware of Ro's existence and mistakenly believed that the [[Twi'lek]] Tempest Runner [[Lourna Dee]] was the Eye. After the [[Battle of Galov]], Dee was believed [[Death|killed]], though she had secretly survived.<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref>

Still unaware of Ro's existence, [[Jedi High Council]] member [[Stellan Gios]] reported that there was unsubstantiated reports of a number of possible candidates for her replacement, including the [[Talpini]] [[Zeetar]].<ref name="The High Republic 9">[[The High Republic (2021) 9|''The High Republic'' (2021) 9]]</ref> Dee later revealed her survival to the Jedi,<ref name="The High Republic 10">[[The High Republic (2021) 10|''The High Republic'' (2021) 10]]</ref> and the [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] believed her to still be the Eye. Dee was [[Battle for No-Space|captured]] by the Jedi and brought to [[Starlight Beacon]], though she was able to escape thanks to Marchion's [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]] of the [[Space station|station]].<ref name="The High Republic 15">[[The High Republic (2021) 15|''The High Republic'' (2021) 15]]</ref> Following Starlight's destruction, Marchion [[Marchion's Declaration|revealed]] to the galaxy that he was in fact the Eye of the Nihil.<ref name="Eye of the Storm 2">[[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 2|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 2]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The title of Eye of the Nihil first appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'', written by [[Charles Soule]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> In Soule's [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]],<ref name="Eye of the Storm 1" /> released [[January 12]], [[2022]],<ref name="EotS 1 Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/96252/star_wars_the_high_republic_-_eye_of_the_storm_2022_1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic - Eye of the Storm (2022) #1}}</ref> [[Shalla Ro]] is referred to as the Eye,<ref name="Eye of the Storm 2" /> but ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'', written by [[Amy Richau]] and [[Megan Crouse]]<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/thrs-205-reveals|text=''Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia'' Revealed on ''Star Wars: The High Republic Show''}}</ref> and released [[November 9]], [[2023]],<ref name="Amazon UK">{{DK|url=uk/book/9780241605875-star-wars-the-high-republic-character-encyclopedia|text=Star Wars The High Republic Character Encyclopedia}}</ref> states that [[Asgar Ro]], her son who took control of the Nihil after her death, was the first Eye.<ref name="THR Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> This article includes Shalla Ro as an Eye for the purposes of its succession table.
