In [[14 BBY]], [[Berch Teller]]'s [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]] launched an attack against the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Sentinel Base]]. Using communications equipment supplied by [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]], the rebels sent a fake holotransmission claiming that [[Rampart Station]] was under attack. [[Wilhuff Tarkin|Tarkin]] detected that the message was a fraud and sent his [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], [[Electrum (Venator-class)|''Electrum'']] on a [[microjump]] to the edge of the system. Shortly after, a modified [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] entered the [[Sentinel Base system|system]]. Tarkin then deployed the ''Venator'' and ordered it to ambush the intruders. The ''Providence'' cruiser retreated to [[hyperspace]] shortly afterward.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Prelude==
In [[17 BBY]],<ref name="Catalyst date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the end of ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'' to [[17 BBY]].</ref> [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] was assigned to [[Sentinel Base]] to keep an eye over [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]] and ensure further setbacks did not occur with a [[Project Stardust|battle station project]]<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> known in time as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station]].<ref name="Rogue One novel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref> It was Tarkin's ambition to take control of the project, something also desired by Krennic. However, Krennic's standing in the [[Galactic Empire]] had been damaged by the escape of the key scientist [[Galen Walton Erso]],<ref name="Catalyst" /> As of [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date" /> the project involved several different locations including [[Geonosis]], [[Desolation Station]], and Sentinel Base. Sentinel Base was an outpost used to relay supplies along a supply route that ended at Geonosis. Tarkin's appointment came at the expense of [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]], who was reassigned as Director of the [[Naval Intelligence Agency]], a branch of [[Imperial Intelligence]]. Resenting Tarkin's position, Rancit conspired with the former [[Republic Intelligence]] officer [[Berch Teller]], another enemy of Tarkin who hated the Moff for his role in the [[Antar Atrocity]], which saw the mass murder of many [[Gotal]] and [[Koorivar]] civilians on [[Antar 4]] including [[loyalist]]s. Teller became the leader of a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|group of dissidents]] who opposed the Empire.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Using his position and connections, Rancit supplied the dissidents with proscribed armaments, communications equipment, and confiscated Separatist war materiel including a [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]], [[Vulture-class starfighter|Vulture droids]], and [[droid tri-fighter]]s. The [[Berch Teller's warship|modified Separatist warship]] was assembled by the [[Tenloss Syndicate]] and members of the [[Crymorah Syndicate]] at shipyards in the [[Bajic sector]]. Teller also had a network of helpers in the form of workers, salvagers, and scientists who supplied materials and intelligence to his cell.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==The raid==
While trying an [[Imperial military uniforms|Imperial officer's uniform]], Moff Tarkin received word of a priority dispatch from [[Rampart Station]], claiming that the station was under attack. Posing as [[Lieutenant]] [[Thon]], Teller's rebels had created a fake holotransmission to lure Sentinel Station's garrison away from the base. While examining the footage, Tarkin suspected that the transmission was a fake but kept the matter to himself. After checking the base's inventory, Tarkin ordered the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Electrum (Venator-class)|''Electrum'']] to do a microjump to the Rimward edge of Sentinel Base's system. He ordered [[Captain]] [[Burque]] to remain there until further orders.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Shortly later, Teller's warship exited hyperspace and disgorged several [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]]-model droid starfighters. The droid starighters led some of the Sentinel Base officers to suggest they may have been attacked by a [[Separatist holdouts|Separatist holdout]]. Tarkin ordered [[Commander]] [[Cassel (commander)|Cassel]] to sound general quarters, boost power to the base's [[deflector shield]]s, and to initiate counter-measures. Under Tarkin's orders, Sentinel Base's artillery bombarded the unidentified warship and its droid starfighters. Due to the strident defense, the intruders began to retreat. After the ''Electrum'' reverted to realspace, Tarkin instructed Captain Burque to deploy his starfighters to harry the enemy droid starfighters and to incapacitate the carrier.<ref name="Tarkin" />

Amidst the battle, Tarkin was contacted by Rampart Station's Commander Jae, who informed him that their base's [[HoloNet relay]] had been down. Despite the ''Electrum''{{'s}} efforts, the enemy carrier managed to escape into hyperspace. The remaining droid starfighters were wiped out by Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. Tarkin instructed Commander Cassel to dispatch a forensic team to examine the enemy droid starfighters. While chatting with a specialist, Tarkin revealed that he knew that the holotransmission from Rampart Station was a counterfeit due to the interval noise in the replay. They deduced that someone had managed to introduce a false real-time feed into the local HoloNet relay.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Aftermath==
Following the failed attack on Sentinel Base, Moff Tarkin attended a meeting of the [[Ruling Council]] on [[Coruscant]]. He and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] were [[Mission to Murkhana|dispatched]] by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] to investigate a communications cache on [[Murkhana]]. However, the communications cache was a ruse set up by Vice Admiral Rancit to enable Teller and his cell to steal Tarkin's personal starship ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', which was equipped with an advanced [[hyperdrive]] and prototype stealth technology. Teller planned to use the ''Carrion Spike'' to attack several Imperial facilities along the [[Perlemian Trade Route]] as part of his [[Berch Teller campaign|campaign]] against the Empire.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on Sentinel Base serves as the exposition of [[James Luceno]]'s [[2014]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], the second in a line of new [[Canon]] adult novels published by [[Del Rey]]. The battle is told from the point of view of the titular character Tarkin.
