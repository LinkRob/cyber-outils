'''Bragh''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Purge Trooper (Anoat sector)|Purge Trooper]] [[commander]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Following the destruction of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]], he fought for [[Governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]]'s [[Ubrik Adelhard's Imperial remnant|remnant]]. During his [[Imperial Military|military]] service, Bragh was regarded as a fearless [[soldier]] who indulged in inflicting pain on others.

==Biography==
{{Quote|A man who lives for his Empire knows no fear. Anyone else is not a man.|Commander Bragh|<ref name="SWU">{{WebCite|url=https://playstarwarsuprising.com|text=Star Wars: Uprising Official Website|archivedate=20161014225915}}</ref>}}
[[File:Adelhard_speech.png|thumb|left|250px|Bragh with Governor Adelhard, shortly after the Battle of Endor]]
Bragh was [[Reproduction|born]] on [[Burnin Konn]], one of the [[Mining Worlds]] of the [[Anoat sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. He entered [[Imperial Military|military]] service during the [[Imperial Era]] as a member of the [[Stormtrooper Corps]], an elite [[Corps|unit]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. As a [[stormtrooper]], he spent many [[Standard year|years]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] region of [[the galaxy]], where he learned to suppress [[Slavery|slave]] revolts and rebel uprisings. Upon his promotion to the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[commander]], Bragh was transferred to the [[Anoat]] [[sector]], where he quickly befriended [[Governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]]. In battle, he utilized a heavy [[blaster rifle]], a [[Z-6 rotary blaster cannon|rotary]] [[blaster cannon]] and a [[Missile launcher|rocket launcher]].<ref name="Uprising" />

After the [[Battle of Endor]], when Adelhard initiated the [[Iron Blockade]], Bragh spent much of his time on board the [[Star Destroyer]] [[Scepter (Imperial-class)|''Scepter'']], serving as the ship's Master-At-Arms.<ref name="Insurgency Rising 1" /> In this capacity, he helped capture a New Republic task force assigned to do a threat assessment on the Blockade. When the prisoners attempted to escape, Bragh overpowered them with help from [[Reyna Oskure]].<ref name="Insurgency Rising 3">[[Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising 3|''Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising'' 3]]</ref> [[Luke Skywalker]] soon arrived to rescue the prisoners. Skywalker defeated Oskure in a lightsaber duel, while Bragh was shot by one of the prisoners - his armor enabling him to survive a wound that would have been fatal to an ordinary stormtrooper. Oskure and Bragh were taken prisoner by the Rebels, but an ambush by stormtroopers enabled both of them to escape captivity.<ref name="Insurgency Rising 4">[[Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising 4|''Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising'' 4]]</ref>

Not long after Bragh recovered from his injury and repaired his armor, a Rebel prisoner named Pessin attempted to bargain for his freedom by revealing to Adelhard there was a traitor in his ranks.<ref name="Republic Under Siege 1">[[Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege 1|''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 1]]</ref>  Adelhard - already paranoid as he had began to suspect he was an unwitting pawn in [[Gallius Rax]]'s machinations<ref name="Consolidation">{{StoryCite|story=Consolidation|book=Battle of Jakku &mdash; Republic Under Siege 1}}</ref> - ordered Bragh to investigate. Bragh suspected [[Lieutenant]] [[Kith Alaytia]], as he had never liked her, but the trail went cold after Pessin was found murdered in his cell.<ref name="Republic Under Siege 1" /> 

When Oskure and Alaytia led a mutiny against Adelhard, Bragh stepped up to defend his commanding officer. Bragh was overpowered by the [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Kranor]], who expressed disappointment that Bragh was not the worthy opponent he expected. Bragh was placed in a sabotaged escape pod along with Adelhard and other stormtoopers loyal to him. The pod crashed onto the nearby planet Gallowyss, impacting hard into the surface. Bragh was the first survivor to claw his way out of wreckage<ref name="Republic Under Siege 4">[[Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege 4|''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 4]]</ref>, and he extricated Adelhard and the surviving stormtroopers in short order.<ref name="Last Stand 1" />

[[File:BraghStabbed-BattleOfJakkuLastStand4.png|thumb|right|250px|Bragh meets his end at the Battle of Jakku]]
Several [[Standard month|months]] into the Iron Blockade, the [[Uprising]] plotted to kidnap Bragh. The plan was to attract Bragh in the middle of Anoat's ruined [[city]]. As soon as the commander would have set a foot on [[planet]] [[Anoat]], [[the Smuggler]] and the [[Kouhun (order)|Kouhun]] [[assassin]]s would ambush him, while several [[Throneship]]s of the [[Noble Court]] would keep his [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] at bay in [[orbit]]. However, the plan failed, and most of the assault team were taken to [[The Crypt (prison)|The Crypt]] to be imprisoned.<ref name="Uprising" />

This led to a [[Rescue mission to the Crypt|rescue mission]] which was mounted by the Smuggler, and Bragh was defeated during the mission,<ref name="Uprising" /> but survived. Bragh was later present with Adelhard as part of his plan to attack both the Empire and New Republic during the [[Battle of Jakku]],<ref name="Last Stand 3">[[Battle of Jakku — Last Stand 3|''Battle of Jakku — Last Stand'' 3]]</ref> where he was [[Death|killed]] by the [[Acolytes of the Beyond|Acolyte of the Beyond]] [[Reyna Oskure]].<ref name="Last Stand 4">[[Battle of Jakku — Last Stand 4|''Battle of Jakku — Last Stand'' 4]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Bragh made his debut in the now-offline game ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''.<ref name="Uprising" /> He later appeared as a major character in the ''[[Star Wars: Battle of Jakku]]'' comic line.<ref name="Last Stand 3" /> In ''Uprising'', Bragh seemingly meets his end in the rescue mission to the Crypt: the character is shot by the player and then only is referenced in the past tense after the mission.<ref name="Uprising" /> However, Bragh appears alive all the way until the Battle of Jakku itself in the later comic line,<ref name="Last Stand 3" /> in which he is killed.<ref name="Last Stand 4" />
