'''First Order Raiders''' were specialized [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] used by the [[First Order]] during their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[Resistance]]. Skilled hunters, they were tasked with acquiring ancient [[Sith artifact]]s by [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]. In [[34 ABY]], a [[Raith's squad|squad]] of Raiders led by [[Agent]] [[Raith]] chased [[Mika Grey]] to [[Vargos 9]]. The [[archaeologist]] was able to escape them and later arrived on the [[planet]] [[Ashas Ree]], in search of an ancient [[Unidentified Sith relic|Sith relic]]. Raith and his Raiders also traveled to the planet, and captured members of [[Team Colossus|Team ''Colossus'']] who had come to Ashas Ree for supplies. Their attempts to interrogate their newfound prisoners were thwarted, however, when Grey unleashed the power of the Sith relic, which she had found inside a [[Ashas Ree Sith temple|Sith temple]]. The Raiders attempted to contain the relic but its activation released a wave of energy which neutralized them, and the ''[[Colossus]]'' resistance fighters returned to their [[Starship|ship]] with Grey now in tow.

==History==
{{Quote|They don't have what your looking for! But I do&hellip;''"<br />"''It's her!''"<br />"''Took you long enough to catch up with me.|Mika Grey and Agent Raith|<ref name="Relic Raiders" />}}
[[File:FirstOrderRaider-TheRelicRaiders.png|thumb|left|300px|Raith, a First Order Raider]]
First Order Raiders were specialized [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] of the [[First Order]], who served the regime during their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[Resistance]]. They were employed by [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] to locate and obtain [[Sith]] [[Sith artifact|artifacts]], with their ranks consisting of highly-skilled hunters of a very specific variety.<ref name="Relic Raiders" /><ref name="Databank">{{Databank|first-order-raiders|First Order Raiders}}</ref> In [[34 ABY]], a [[Raith's squad|contingent]] of Raiders under the command of [[Agent]] [[Raith]] pursued [[archaeologist]] [[Mika Grey]] to [[Vargos 9]]. Grey escaped and traveled to the [[planet]] [[Ashas Ree]], to locate an ancient [[Unidentified Sith relic|Sith relic]] that she hoped to prevent the First Order from acquiring. When the Raiders arrived on Ashas Ree, in search of this relic, they entered a [[Ashas Ree outpost|trading outpost]] that housed two ancient [[temple]]s of [[the Force]]: one [[Ashas Ree Temple of the Force|belonging to the Jedi]] and the other [[Ashas Ree Sith temple|claimed by the Sith]]. In the outpost, they encountered [[Torra Doza]] and [[Freya Fenris]], alongside their accompanying [[droid]]s. Unbeknownst to them, the duo were actually members of [[Team Colossus|Team ''Colossus'']]. Labelling them 'trespassers', the main body of Raiders captured them and attempted to conduct an interrogation outside of the duo's [[Starship|ship]]. Mika Grey's ship was also discovered and destroyed in an explosion, courtesy of the Raiders.<ref name="Relic Raiders" />

[[File:FirstOrderRaidersDeath-TheRelicRaiders.jpg|thumb|right|300px|The First Order Raider squad rendered unconscious by the Sith relic.]]
Meanwhile, two other [[trooper]]s were ordered by Raith to search the area further and came across four more members of the ''[[Colossus]]'' resistance. One of them, [[Kazuda Xiono]], urged the Raiders not to open fire as they were unarmed. They were quickly escorted to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple's]] entrance and Raith's body of troopers and [[prisoner]]s soon congregated there as well. Xiono stressed that they were only looking for supplies but Raith was unconvinced and demanded to know what they had found inside the [[Sith temple]]. Just then, Grey revealed herself and taunted the Raiders for their inability to catch her or acquire the relic. Raith threatened to execute the two adolescent trespassers, [[Kel]] and [[Eila]], but Grey acted quickly. She activated the relic and proceeded to throw it as far as she could behind the Raiders' position. The prisoners ran and the troopers, aware of the danger they were in, attempted to deactivate the relic. However, they were not fast enough, and the relic unleashed a powerful wave of red energy that electrified the Raiders, neutralizing them. With their enemies defeated and the Sith relic rendered obsolete, Grey accompanied the resistance members back to their home ship, the ''Colossus''.<ref name="Relic Raiders" />

==Equipment==
Like standard First Order stormtroopers, First Order Raiders were equipped with [[armor]] that could accommodate [[Military rank|rank]] [[pauldron]]s. Additionally, they wielded [[F-11D blaster rifle]]s.<ref name="Relic Raiders" /> The raiders also donned a gray [[macro scope]] [[magnet]]ically attached to the back of their armor<ref name="Art">{{SW|url=the-relic-raider-concept-art-gallery|text="The Relic Raider" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 10}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:FirstOrderRaiders-ConceptArt.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Concept art of the First Order Raiders.]]
First Order Raiders first appeared in "[[The Relic Raiders]]," the seventh episode in the [[Star Wars Resistance Season Two|second season]] of the animated series [[Star Wars Resistance]].<ref name="Relic Raiders" /> They were first unveiled in the trailer for Season Two, released on the official [[Star Wars]] YouTube channel.<ref name="S2 Trailer">{{SWYouTube|JkJtdJKCVto|Star Wars Resistance Season 2 - Trailer (Official)|sw_url=video/disneyxd-season-2-trailer-star-wars-resistance}}</ref> The Raiders were a new and original design created for the show.<ref name="RR Bucket's List">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-relic-raider-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 7 Fun Facts from "The Relic Raider" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref>

In the game ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'', the First Order Raiders have a unit portrait but are not playable units. They appear as opponents in the "Heir to the Light Side" event to unlock Galactic Legend Rey. The name of the portrait is: "tex.charui_firstorder_raider."<ref name="GOH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref>
