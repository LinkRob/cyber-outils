'''Chief Inspector''' was a title given to the head of or high ranking members of law enforcement agencies or scholarly organizations. The title was used by the head [[inspector]] of the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the position of Chief Inspector in the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts was held by the [[Twi'lek]] [[Deemus Abrus]].<ref name="MH" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]] corporate authority used the title of Chief Inspector for the lead [[inspector]] of the [[Pre-Mor Security Inspection team]], and was ranked above that of [[Deputy Inspector]]. [[Hyne]] was the Chief Inspector in [[5 BBY]].<ref name="Episode 1" />
