The '''BA-4 four directions alloyed shield''' was a [[Riot shield]] used by anti-riot forces on [[Fire (city)|Fire]] in the [[Aakaash system]]. It was manufactured from a [[Metal]] alloy locally within the [[Star system|system]]. The tool was designed to be wielded single-handedly with a [[Stun baton]].<ref name="Vow" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the original Chinese, this model was called the BA-4型四方合金盾 (Pinyin: BA-4 Xíng Sìfānghéjīndùn) meaning "BA-4 type four-directions alloyed shield."
