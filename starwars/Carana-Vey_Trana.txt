'''Carana-Vey Trana''' was an<ref name="Script">[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker (script)|''The High Republic: Tempest Breaker'' script]]</ref> [[Aki-Aki]] who served as<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" /> a [[Jedi Master]]<ref name="Tempest Breaker" /> of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Alongside<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" /> his [[Padawan]] [[Faris Karan]],<ref name="Tempest Breaker" /> the [[Aki-Aki]] [[Jedi]] ventured to the astronomical object [[Blarrum]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], searching for [[Triv Blago]], a beast-[[Merchant|trader]] who illegally captured and transported dangerous creatures. As the two Jedi searched [[Kul Kenada Spaceport]], the Aki-Aki momentarily sensed [[Ty Yorrick]], a former Jedi [[Padawan]] and monster [[Hunting|hunter]] who had been hired by Blago. The two Jedi confronted Blago as the trader spoke with Yorrick, who fled, leaving Blago to the Jedi.<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" />
