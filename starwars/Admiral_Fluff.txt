'''Admiral Fluff''' was the [[Doll|stuffed mascot]] of the [[Resistance]].

In [[34 ABY]], [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] gave a [[First Order]] operative the task of retrieving Admiral Fluff from the Resistance base on [[D'Qar]] to land a devastating blow to their enemy's morale.
