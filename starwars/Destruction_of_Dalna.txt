The '''Destruction of Dalna''' was a [[volcano|volcanic]] cataclysmic event on the [[planet]] [[Dalna]] in [[230 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> It was triggered by the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] organization and their [[scientist]] [[Zadina Mkampa]] using a [[crystal matrix]] built into the planet's crust that created [[groundquake]]s. Mkampa activated it after [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] attacked her as part of her [[Mission to Dalna|mission]] to rescue her friend the child scientist [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]]. While some feared Dalna would never recover, [[Honesty Weft]] had faith that [[scientist]]s would find a way to stablize the world.<ref name="Mission to Disaster" /> Dalna eventually recovered.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref>
