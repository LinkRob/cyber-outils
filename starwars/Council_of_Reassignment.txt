The '''Council of Reassignment''' was one of the four [[Jedi Council|councils]] of the [[Jedi Order]] in operation during the rule of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="CL 2016" /> It managed the [[Jedi Service Corps]] and organized work for [[Jedi Initiate]]s who were not chosen to become [[Padawan]]s<ref name="SWSG" /> and met in [[Reassignment Council Tower|one]] of the [[Council Towers|four towers]] surrounding the [[Palace spire|Temple Spire]] atop the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="CL 2016" />
