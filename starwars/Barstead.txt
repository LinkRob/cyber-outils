'''Barstead''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Captain/Legends|Captain]] of the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|reconstituted Sith Empire]] living during the [[Galactic War]]. In [[3640 BBY]], he was stationed on the [[Imperial waystation]] of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Ilum/Legends|Ilum]].

==Behind the scenes==
Captain Barstead first appeared in the MMORPG ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', released by [[BioWare]] in [[2011]].

He is only accessible to Imperial players in the game on Ilum.
