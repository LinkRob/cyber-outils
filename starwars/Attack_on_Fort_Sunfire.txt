During the [[Operations on Tatooine]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] base called [[Fort Sunfire]] was '''attacked''' by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] forces. Under the guidence of [[Colonel]] [[Jennica Pierce]], [[Saponza]], and [[Saponza's partner]] as well as the support of [[Sergeant]] [[Oto Vans]] and six [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s the rebels emerged victorious, though they would catch the ire of Imperial [[Captain]] [[Kosh]], the leader of Imperial forces on [[Tatooine]], who vowed that the attack on Fort Sunfire would be repaid in kind.<ref name="Commander" /> Sometime after the rebel attack, Fort Sunfire would be rebuilt by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
