The '''central market''' was a market in the [[Lower Mirogana]] area of the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[fire]] destroyed a number of [[merchant]] stalls in the market and the event was covered on the local [[Holonet News Toshara]] by [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Kotera Braal]]. The [[HoloNet News]] coverage described the destroyed stalls as well-established and stated that the incident was being investigated as arson according to their sources.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The central market was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
