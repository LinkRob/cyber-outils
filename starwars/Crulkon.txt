The '''Crulkon''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] with a presence on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Turrak]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], there were tensions between the Crulkon and the native [[Scalvi]], the latter of whom contacted the [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] for assistance.<ref name="Yoda 1" />
