'''Alois Akrone'''<ref name="LCATZA" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="USW" /> [[Gender|male]] who served as a [[pilot]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] before the [[Galactic Civil War]]. A [[Imperial cadet|student]] at the [[Imperial Academy]], he was friends with [[Sax Hastur]] and [[Poul Ashanto]] on [[Prefsbelt IV|Prefsbelt]]. He had a brother, [[Heiwei Akrone]], who served in the [[Imperial Navy]] as part of [[Banshee Squadron]] shortly before the Galactic Civil War. Shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], Akrone was remembered by the pilots in the [[Cantina|bar]] "[[Zero Angle]]," where his [[hologram]] was on the wall of the honor of the [[Death|dead]] pilots.<ref name="LCATZA" />
