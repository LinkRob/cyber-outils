The '''Font-Alor system''' was a [[star system]] located near the border of the [[Nihil]]'s [[Stormwall]] [[hyperspace]] barrier<ref name="TOTF">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> by [[228 BBY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''The High Republic: Temptation of the Force''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> the ''[[Blue Range]]'' rendezoused with the ''[[Axiom]]'' near the edge of the system.<ref name="TOTF" />
