'''DK-3''' was an [[R-series]] [[astromech droid]] in the service of a [[Unidentified Gran trader|Gran trader]] on [[Pantora]]. The [[protocol droid]] [[CG-67]] supervised DK-3 and the [[Merchant|trader's]] other [[droid]]s, fellow astromech [[225]] and the [[LE-series droid|LE-series]] [[Clink]]. DK-3 and the other droids assisted in repairing the [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|''Marauder'']], the [[Omicron-class attack shuttle|shuttle]] of the former [[clone trooper]] unit known as the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]].<ref name="Cornered" />
