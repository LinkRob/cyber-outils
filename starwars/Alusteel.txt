'''Alusteel''' was a type of alloy used in the [[titanium]]-reinforced [[hull]]s of various models of the [[BTL Y-wing starfighter]],<ref name="RRaRS">{{EncyclopediaCite|Republic, Rebel, and Resistance Starfighters}}</ref> such as the [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|BTL-A4 Y-wing]] and the [[BTA-NR2 Y-wing starfighter|BTA-NR2 Y-wing]].<ref name="RSM">''[[Rebel Starfighters Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> It was also utilized in a hull of similar material composition for the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Super Star Destroyer]], which was constructed to reflect the design of the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]].<ref name="BtMF 27">{{BuildFalconCite|27|Starship Fact File}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Alusteel was first identified in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 27|twenty-seventh]] issue of ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'', published by [[De Agostini]] on [[July 8]], [[2015]], in which it was listed as a material used in the construction of the ''Executor''-class [[starship]]'s hull. It was later identified in a similar manner for the BTL Y-wing in the [[2019]] reference novel ''[[Rebel Starfighters Owners' Workshop Manual]]'', which was written by [[Ryder Windham]]. Illustrations in the novel, provided by [[Chris Reiff]] and [[Chris Trevas]], showed the use of alusteel in the hulls of various Y-wing models. This information was echoed in De Agostini's ''[[Republic, Rebel, and Resistance Starfighters]]'', published on [[November 13]], [[2020]] as the eighth edition of the ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|Star Wars Encyclopedia]]'' booklet series.
