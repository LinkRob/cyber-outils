An '''individual''' was a [[human]] [[Imperial officer]] tasked as the [[commanding officer]] of [[Candace]] under the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Phineas" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{PnFDate|1}}</ref> the commanding officer was aboard the ''[[Tantive IV]]'' to chase after the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellions]]. He was ordered to [[Mission to buy socks for Darth Vader|purchase]] a new pair of socks for [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], which he passed the task onto Candace, [[Baljeet]], and [[Buford]]. Candace reluctantly agreed and went to get a new pair of socks.<ref name="Phineas" />

Soon after, on [[Tatooine]], the commanding officer met Candace again and accidentally leaked about the missing [[Death Star plans]], which peaked Candace's interest in searching for the plans. He quickly shut down Candace's curiosity and told her to leave and go on with her mission. Candace eventually completed her task and handed a bag to the commanding officer, thinking that it was the socks. He then left the [[desert]] [[planet]] using a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle]] to hand the new pair of socks on [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]] to Darth Vader.<ref name="Phineas" />

As he reached his destination, the officer then went ahead to meet Darth Vader and complete the mission. Unfortunately, the bag did not contain any socks, but rather a fresh [[bantha cheese hoagie]] which was supposed to be Buford's meal. In his anger and disappointment, Vader [[Force choke]]d the commanding officer and [[Death|killed]] him in the process.<ref name="Phineas" />
