The '''Chortose''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that resided on [[Chortose]], an astronomical object in the [[Rseik sector]]<ref name="THR 7" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> The species had a small stature and was covered in [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Attack on Chortose|attacked]] [[Agricultural Hub 42]], a [[factory]] on Chortose ran by Chortose workers, for the [[sprayer droid]]s that it utilized. Two Chortose brothers, [[Pango]] and [[Gru (Chortose)|Gru]], sent a [[distress signal]] to the [[Jedi Order]] on [[Starlight Beacon]], who sent the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Keeve Trennis]] to assist them. Trennis arrived to defend the Chortose workers from being [[Death|killed]] by the Nihil, and was [[Gender|herself]] assisted by the [[Jedi]] [[Wayseeker]] [[Orla Jareni]].<ref name="THR 7" />
