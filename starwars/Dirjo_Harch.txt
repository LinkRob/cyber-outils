'''Dirjo Harch''' was a member of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Swarm transmission recovery and processing unit|transmission processing unit]] for [[Project Swarm]], searching for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] using [[Viper probe droid|probe droids]]. By the [[time]] that he worked on Project Swarm, Harch was new to Imperial service and had not yet been able to climb up the ranks. He took command of the Swarm processing team and was eager to report the success of his colleague [[Maela]] to [[Captain]] [[Firmus Piett]].<ref name="FACPOV" />
