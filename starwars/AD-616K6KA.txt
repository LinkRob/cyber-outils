During his tenure as a [[Deputy Inspector]] with [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]]'s [[Pre-Mor Security Inspection team|security service]], [[Syril Karn]] carried a [[blaster pistol]], of a [[B1-NA blaster pistol|model]] commonly carried by Pre-Mor personnel, stamped with the serial number '''AD-616K6KA'''. After Karn lost the weapon, it exchanged hands several times and became peripherally involved in certain central events of the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]].

==History==
===Preserving order===
In [[5 BBY]], Karn led an [[Ambush on Ferrix|operation]] on [[Ferrix]] which was intended to result in a simple arrest but instead devolved into a [[Death|fatal]] shootout.<ref name="Ann">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref> During the chaos, Karn's suspect, [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], was able to hold Karn at blasterpoint and relieve him of his weapon. When Andor made his escape from Ferrix, he took the weapon with him.<ref name="Reckon" />

===Using the tools of an enemy===
{{Quote|Corporate issue. Interesting&hellip; ''[&hellip;]'' Whose is this?''"<br />"''Didn't get a name.|Arvel Skeen and Cassian Andor discuss the pistol's former owner|<ref name="TAF" />}}
[[File:Corpo-blaster.png|thumb|250px|left|AD-616K6KA was among the belongings Cassian Andor brought with him to Aldhani.]]
Andor brought the Pre-Mor blaster to [[Aldhani]] when he traveled there to work a [[mercenary]] [[Aldhani heist|job]] for a [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|rebel]] [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team|unit]] led by [[Vel Sartha]]. Andor's mercenary role was kept secret from the unit's other members, and fellow rebel [[Arvel Skeen]] became suspicious of his new comrade's origins when he discovered the corporate weapon in Andor's [[backpack]].<ref name="TAF" /> Andor took the pistol into the [[Aldhani dam]] during the heist, but did not use it when combat started, relying instead on his [[Clem's blaster pistol|own]] [[MW-20 Bryar pistol]].<ref name="Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref>

In the aftermath of the Aldhani heist, Andor fled to [[Niamos]], where he lived under the alias "Keef Girgo", supposedly a tourist. During this time, he kept the Pre-Mor pistol alongside his own weapon in a kit stashed above the [[shower]] in his [[Niamos hotel|hotel]] room,<ref name="Andor7Audio">{{Andor|Announcement}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> hidden from his girlfriend [[Windi]]. When Andor was arrested and sent to [[Narkina 5]] for [[Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex|incarceration]],<ref name="N5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref> his belongings, including the pistol, were unceremoniously left behind in the hotel room.<ref name="Ann" />

===A show of solidarity===
{{Quote|We need to split up. Double our chances. One of us has to make it. People have to know what's going on.''"<br />"''I know. Take this.|Ruescott Melshi and Cassian Andor, before giving the pistol to the first|<ref name="DoF" />}}
Roughly one [[Standard month|month]] later, Andor [[Narkina 5 prison escape|escaped]] from prison alongside [[Ruescott Melshi]], and with the aid of [[Dewi Pamular|two]] [[Freedi Pamular|fishermen]], the pair traveled to Niamos to retrieve Andor's belongings. Andor snuck into the hotel room and reclaimed his travel kit without anyone the wiser. When Melshi proposed he and Andor split up and each tell their story of the horrors of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] imprisonment to anyone who would listen, Andor agreed and gifted him the [[B1-NA blaster pistol|corpo blaster]] as they parted ways.<ref name="DoF" />

Melshi continued to hold onto the corpo blaster until [[2 BBY]], when he joined the [[Massassi Group]] on [[Yavin 4]]. As part of the intake process, Melshi surrendered his blaster for inspection along with the other recruits. The intake officer, Vel Sartha, immediately recognized the blaster and held it up, asking whom it belonged to. Melshi quickly raised his hand and identified himself as the owner.<ref name="Rebellion">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The blaster designated AD-616K6KA first appeared in [[That Would Be Me]], the second episode of ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', which was made available for streaming on [[Disney+]] on [[September 21]], [[2022]] as part of the series' three-episode premier.<ref name="TWBM" />
