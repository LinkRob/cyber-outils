The '''Battle of Kamino''', also known as the '''Raid on Kamino''' or the '''Attack on Kamino''', was fought during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Jedi]]-led [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederacy of Independent systems]]. The Separatists launched the battle as one of their key campaigns during the war, hoping to capture [[Kamino]] and eliminate the [[cloning]] facilities in [[Tipoca City]]&mdash;resulting in the '''Battle of Tipoca City''', also known as the '''defense of Tipoca City'''&mdash;in order to try to deprive the Republic from creating more [[clone trooper]]s for their army. The Separatists, led by [[General]] [[Grievous]] and [[Asajj Ventress]], were repelled by the Republic. However, the attack on Kamino proved the Republic had intelligence leaks and that every Republic planet was vulnerable to assault.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|The clone planet of Kamino will be a dangerous target."<br />"Just make sure you hold up your half of the mission. We must stop the production of new clones if we are to win this war.|[[Asajj Ventress]] and General [[Grievous]]|<ref name="ARC Troopers" />}}
The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] had attempted an assault on Kamino earlier in the [[Clone Wars]]. However, the attempt was stopped by the [[Defense of Rishi Station|destruction]] of [[Rishi Station]].<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref>

[[File:WelcomeToKamino-ARCT.png|left|thumb|220x220px|Kenobi, Skywalker, and clone reinforcements arrive on Kamino.]]
The following year [[Jedi]] [[Jedi General|Generals]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] intercepted a transmission between Confederate [[General]] [[Grievous]] and [[assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]]. They were planing an attack on [[Kamino]]. Thus, Skywalker, Obi-Wan, [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Cody]], [[Clone Captain|Captain]] [[Rex]] and the [[trooper]]s of the [[501st Legion]] were then sent to reinforce the [[Kamino blockade|blockade]] and General [[Shaak Ti]]'s<ref name="ARC Troopers" /> [[Kamino garrison|garrison]]<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> stationed at the [[cloning]] facilities in [[Tipoca City]].<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

During this [[time]], Ventress and her [[Confederacy military|forces]] were hiding in the deep [[ocean]]s of Kamino aboard a [[Trident-class assault ship|''Trident''-class assault ship]]. There they awaited Grievous's arrival.<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

==The battle==
===Space distraction===
{{Quote|I'm going to press the attack, Master.''"<br />"''No, Anakin. Wait! It's too easy. Not even Grievous would attack so recklessly.''"<br />"''Master, the battle's up here in space, not down there.''"<br />"''The debris from the destroyed Separatist ships is the key.|Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="ARC Troopers" />}}
[[File:ReentryCapsules-ARCT.png|thumb|left|250px|Grievous's frigates endure extensive damage to deploy the disguised re-entry capsules.]]
When Grievous's [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|fleet]] approached Kamino, the blockade under [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]] was ready to hold the line against the Confederate [[fleet]]. Grievous's [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|T-series tactical droid]] [[TV-94B]] reported that all ships were in [[Echo-3|position]] and the Confederate fleet attacked. Skywalker and [[Broadside]] then led the [[Starfighter|fighter]] [[squadron]]s against the fleet. Grievous sacrificed his larger [[transport]]s to protect his [[command ship]]. However, there was more to this strategy, as the debris from the destroyed ships fell into orbit and into the oceans of Kamino. This debris actually contained [[Trident-class assault ship|assault craft]] to be used to attack the city. When the debris fell into the ocean, Ventress ordered her [[AQ-series battle droid|aqua droids]] to assemble the assault crafts.<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

However, General Kenobi suspected that debris was a clue. So, Kenobi took a [[KE-UW33|Kamino sub]] down into the ocean to investigate. There, he discovered the aqua [[droid]]s assembling the assault crafts. Kenobi was attacked by four of the aqua droids, and then forced to abandon the submarine. Kenobi was subsequently saved by an [[aiwha]].<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

===Assault on Tipoca City===
{{Quote|Keep playing with your droids. I'll handle breaking into the DNA room.''"<br />"''Shall I provide you with a droid escort?''"<br />"''My dear General, there's nothing you have that I could want.|Asajj Ventress and General Grievous, separating to complete their mission objectives|<ref name="ARC Troopers" />}}
[[File:BattleOfKamino-ARCT.png|thumb|right|250px|Tipoca City under a full scale assault by Separatist forces.]]
The Trident assault [[Starship|ships]] then came out of the ocean and attacked Tipoca [[City]]. The aqua droids under Ventress led the attack against the [[clone trooper]]s under [[Commander]] [[Colt]] and his fellow [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Havoc (ARC trooper)|Havoc]]. General Grievous then landed with his [[battle droid]]s. After [[Death|killing]] Colt, Ventress and Grievous discussed their plans. While Grievous would attack the clone [[barracks]], Ventress would secure a sample of the clones' [[DNA]].<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

[[File:KenobiVsGrievous-ARCTroopers.png|left|thumb|220x220px|The fight between Grievous and Kenobi ended on one of the platforms in Tipoca City.]]
During this time, [[troop]]ers [[Fives]] and [[Echo]] were acting as [[sniper]]s. They were then given [[grenade]]s by [[Clone 99|99]] after Havoc was killed. They later found a group of [[clone cadet]]s and took them back to the barracks. Once there, they were joined by Captain Rex and Commander Cody. Together, they prepared to fight Grievous's [[Separatist Droid Army|forces]]. When battle droids were about to enter the barracks, Cody, Rex and the others were able to attack them. Grievous was then confronted by Kenobi and the two [[Lightsaber duel|dueled]]. They fought until they got to a platform where a Trident assault ship destroyed the platform. Grievous thought Kenobi had fallen to his death, but was actually saved once again by an aiwha.<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

[[File:Ventress_Push_ARCT.png|thumb|220x220px|Ventress Force pushes Skywalker and the clone troopers.]]
Ventress then entered the [[Clone DNA room|DNA room]] and retrieved a DNA sample. However, she was confronted by Skywalker. The two then fought until Ventress lost the sample and was surrounded by clone troopers. As Skywalker was about to let his troopers execute Ventress, Grievous came in a [[Kaminoan flight pod|flight pod]]. Ventress boarded the Pod and retreated.<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

During this time, Rex, Cody, their troops, and the clone [[cadet]]s were able to destroy the battle droids, though 99 was killed in the crossfire. Several [[Advanced Recon Force trooper|ARF troopers]] then destroyed the last Trident [[assault ship]].<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

==Aftermath==
[[File:FivesEchoPromoted-ARCT.png|thumb|240x240px|Captain Rex and Commander Cody revealed Fives and Echo's promotion to be ARC troopers.|left]]
For their actions during the battle, Echo and Fives were promoted to ARC troopers.<ref name="ARC Troopers" /> [[Republic officer]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] was decorated at the battle.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>

Despite the Republic victory, the facilities on Kamino suffered widespread damage and intense fighting, although they were able to remain operational. Grievous's attack on the planet also proved that any Republic planet was vulnerable to Separatist assault, and the Republic had intelligence leaks. The attack proved to be a morale boost for the Confederacy.<ref name="Rise" />
