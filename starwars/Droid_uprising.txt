The '''droid uprising''' was a massive gathering of [[droid]]s<ref name="Vader 37" /> assembled by [[Captain]] [[Ought-Six]] of the [[Droid Crush Pirates of Bestoon]] with the aim of [[Death|killing]] the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who had destroyed every droid who joined the uprising at some point prior in their mechanical lives. The droids took advantage of an Imperial mandate<ref name="Vader 36" /> put out by [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], who sought to test Vader amid the [[Force Wave]],<ref name="Vader 35">[[Darth Vader (2020) 35|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 35]]</ref> to kill Vader himself. However, one of the uprising's key members, [[ZED-6-7]], was working as a double [[agent]] for Vader within its ranks. After the [[battle with the droid uprising]], the uprising droids were reprogrammed by Vader<ref name="Vader 36" /> for his own uses. As many of the droids underwent repairs aboard the ''[[Executor]]'', however, they would be infected by the [[Scourge]], forcing Vader and the uninfected of the uprising to [[Attack on the Executor (Scourge crisis)|engage those corrupted]].<ref name="Vader 37" />

Later, Vader would try to take control of the Scourge to place its [[legion]]s of droids under his own control, intending to create his own [[army]] with which he could finally best his [[Sith Master]], [[Darth Sidious]]. However, the strain of trying to manage so many droid minds on his own proved to be too much.<ref name="Vader 41">[[Darth Vader (2020) 41|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 41]]</ref> Afterward, Vader focused his efforts on trying to find a [[M.A.R. Corps|team]] of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[cyborg]]s, whom he intended to control by implanting [[droid brain]]s within their [[neuro port]]s; as Vader and ZED-6-7 reasoned, battle droids were unable to betray Vader because of their programming, yet they were not as skilled as organic warriors. However, organic soldiers, such as [[Darth Vader's guard|Vader's own death troopers]], had independent minds and could betray their leader:<ref name="Tool of the Empire">{{StoryCite|story=Tool of the Empire|book=Revelations (2023) 1}}</ref> during the [[Scourge of Coruscant]], those [[death trooper]]s had indeed turned on Vader on the order of Sidious.<ref name="Vader 40">[[Darth Vader (2020) 40|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 40]]</ref> As such, Vader and ZED plotted to combine the best of both worlds: Vader would control the power of the rebel cyborgs and ensure their loyalty via the installed droid brains. For the cyborgs themselves, who would need to remain conscious for their [[cybernetics]] to function, they would be trapped in the horror of being unable to control their own bodies. All the while, Vader still commanded the loyalty of remaining droid uprising members, such as ZED<ref name="Tool of the Empire" /> and the Crush Pirates. Vader would unleash a number of the battle droids under his command into the [[assault on Exegol]] during his alliance with the [[Schism Imperial]].<ref name="Vader 46" />
