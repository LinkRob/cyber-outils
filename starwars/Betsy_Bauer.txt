'''Betsy Bauer''' is an [[Wikipedia:Artist|artist]] who worked as a designer and lighting concept artist for the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season One|first]] and [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|second season]] of ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''.<ref name="The Young Jedi">{{YJA|The Young Jedi / Yoda's Mission}}</ref> Bauer was responsible for designing [[Varish]] and the [[Malangsha]] [[species]].<ref name="Designer">{{Twitter|username=madidrawsthings|url=status/1757925265500762114|name=[[Madi Hodges]]|date=2024-02-14|quote=Betsy Baur [''sic''] ! And we likely won't be able to share concept art, but that's the designer|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/wGGoV|archivefile=File:VarishConcept-Twitter.png}} {{C|In response to: Incredibly helpful, thanks again! I really want to get my hands on the concept art! Also, while I have your ear, do you possibly know who designed Varish? This is the other unidentified one whose creator I can’t seem to pinpoint? 😅}}</ref>
