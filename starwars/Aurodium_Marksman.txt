The '''Aurodium Marksman''' was a costume owned by the [[Miraluka]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Diago Velaar]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Frontier Sharpshooter|standard costume]]. This dark outfit featured [[Color|golden]] embellishments.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Aurodium Marksman was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Diago, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 4.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Aurodium Bullseye]]. This costume could be unlocked by reaching the rank Kyber I within ''Hunter's'' "Ranked" mode during the game's fourth season.<ref name="Hunters" />
