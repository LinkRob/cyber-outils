A '''capacitor'''<ref name="Catalyst" /> or '''power capacitor'''<ref name="Replacements EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/replacements-episode-guide|text="Replacements" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}}</ref> was a device used in [[the galaxy]] to store energy. Capacitors were used in a variety of machines, including [[weapon]]s,<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> [[starship]]s,<ref name="Replacements">{{TBB|Replacements}}</ref> and even [[droid]]s.<ref name="Beginnings">''[[Star Wars Journeys: Beginnings]]''</ref>

The [[scientist]] [[Galen Walton Erso]] hoped to generate outbursts of raw power that could be harvested and stored in capacitors for use on worlds in need.<ref name="Catalyst" /> Capacitors were also used in droids like [[B1-series battle droid]]s. In these models of [[battle droid]]s their capacitors were a weak point, and [[Clone sharpshooter|clone snipers]] could hit between the droid's capacitors to take out the unit.<ref name="Beginnings" /> The ends of [[electrostaff]]s contained a discharge capacitor.<ref name="CVD" /> Shortly after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]] had their [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|ship's]] capacitor stolen by an [[Ordo Moon Dragon]].<ref name="Replacements" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[ND-5]] gave a power capacitor to [[Kay Vess]] after she recovered a stolen artifact for the [[Ashiga Clan]] from their rivals [[Crimson Dawn]]. Vess used the capacitor to create a powerful [[blaster]] module for her [[VM-19 heavy blaster pistol]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
