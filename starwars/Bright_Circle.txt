'''Bright Circle''' was a criminal organization that was lead by [[Ullan Yite]]. Around [[377 BBY]]<ref name="MissingPiecesDate">"Tales of Enlightenment: Missing Pieces" takes place around five [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Jedha]], dated to [[382 BBY]] in ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>, it put together a deal with a pirate group to sell smuggled weapons. The deal was broken up and Yite was captured by [[Silandra Sho]], [[Rooper Nitani]], and [[P3-7A]].<ref name="Insider"  />
