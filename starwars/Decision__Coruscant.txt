"'''Decision: Coruscant'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year four of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was the second adventure in the [[Decisions trilogy]] and was written by [[Ronald A. Heintz]].

==Plot summary==
The heroes must take an important [[Datacard/Legends|datacard]] to [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant (Notron)]] and then speak to the [[Galactic Senate/Legends|Galactic Senate]], to persuade them to force the [[Thaereian military]] out of the [[Cularin system/Legends|Cularin system]]
