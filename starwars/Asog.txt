{{Quote|The Sith have no Seeds, since what they Bury does not Grow. They are the Despoilers of Worlds, and have Laid to Waste once Fertile Habitats such as Korriban, Ziost, Ixigul, Asog, and Others.|Kli the Elder, ''Rammahgon''|<ref name="TROS junior" />}}
'''Asog''' was a terrestrial<ref name="TROS junior" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Exegol">{{EncyclopediaCite|Exegol, the Unknown Regions and Wild Space}}</ref> located in the [[Asog system]], a part of the [[Esstran sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> It was situated in grid square Q-4 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

At one point, Asog was a fertile habitat. Sometime prior to the [[Author|composition]] of the [[Jedi]] [[book]] ''[[Rammahgon]]''<ref name="TROS junior" /> at least five [[Millennium|thousand years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="GeneralEquipment1">{{EncyclopediaCite|General Equipment (1)}}</ref> adherents of the [[Sith]] tradition of [[Force-sensitive|Force-users]] rendered Asog barren, which was subsequently noted by the Jedi [[Kli the Elder]] in a chapter of the ''Rammahgon''.<ref name="TROS junior" />
