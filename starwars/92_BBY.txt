'''92 BBY''', or '''92<ref name="JMM1">[[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine 1|''Star Wars Jedi Master'' Magazine 1]]</ref> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that fell within the [[Republic Era|era]] in which the [[Galactic Republic]] governed [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], 92 BBY was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine 1|first issue]] of ''[[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine]]'',<ref name="JMM1" /> published by [[Titan Magazines]] on [[November 16]], [[2015]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanMagazines|url=t/star-wars-jedi-master-magazine|text=Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine}}</ref> Both ''Jedi Master Magazine'' 1<ref name="JMM1" /> and [[2017]]'s<ref name="Chronicle">{{Chronicle|url=products/star-wars-super-graphic|text=Star Wars® Super Graphic}}</ref> ''[[Star Wars Super Graphic: A Visual Guide to a Galaxy Far, Far Away]]'' establish 92 BBY as the [[Reproduction|birth]] year of the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]].<ref name="Super Graphic>''[[Star Wars Super Graphic: A Visual Guide to a Galaxy Far, Far Away]]'' dates the the [[Reproduction|birth]] of [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] to 92 [[standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|IV}}, which corresponds to 92 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> However, the [[2019]] novel ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' by [[Claudia Gray]],<ref name="MA">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> which is placed in [[40 BBY]] per the [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'',<ref name="Timelines" /> sets the age of Qui-Gon during the novel's events as forty years old.<ref name="MA" /> This article assumes that, as older sources, ''Jedi Master Magazine'' 1 and ''Star Wars Super Graphic'' are in error.

The [[Standard year/Legends|year]] [[92 BBY/Legends|92 BBY]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first indirectly mentioned in "[[Shintel Downtime]]," an adventure written by [[Paul Sudlow]] and published in the [[1995]] [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' supplement ''[[The DarkStryder Campaign]]'', which mentions events that took place "about a century" before the adventure's setting<ref name="Shintel Downtime">{{DarkStryder|adventure=Shintel Downtime|book=The DarkStryder Campaign}}</ref> in [[8 ABY/Legends|8 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[The DarkStryder Campaign]]'' is set four [[Standard year/Legends|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor/Legends|Battle of Endor]], which corresponds to [[8 ABY/Legends|8 ABY]] per ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''.</ref> ''[[Vader: The Ultimate Guide]]'',<ref name="VTUG">''[[Vader: The Ultimate Guide]]''</ref> a souvenir magazine released in [[July]] [[2005]],<ref name="VaderDate">{{SWArchive|url=community/fanclub/mag/news20050711.html|text=Inside ''Vader: The Ultimate Guide''}}</ref> was the first source to mention the year explicitly.<ref name="VTUG" />
