A '''Felucian flickerbird''' was a type of sensitive [[bird]] that shared its name with<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> the [[planet]] [[Felucia]].<ref name="Felucia">{{Databank|felucia|Felucia}}</ref> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">{{TrialsoftheJediDate|14}}</ref> while aboard the [[Emissary-class Republic Cruiser|''Emissary''-class megacruiser]] ''[[Third Horizon]]'', [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[Admiral]] [[Pevel Kronara]] reflected that while a [[Commanding officer|commander]] needed to externally appear stone-faced and implacable, they internally needed to be as sensitive as a Felucian flickerbird and attuned to changes in battle conditions, tactical situations, and the emotions of their forces.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />

==Behind the scenes==
A Felucian flickerbird was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]'', written by [[Charles Soule]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref>
