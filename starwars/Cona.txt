'''Cona''' was a [[desert]] [[planet]]<ref name="Alien Archive" /> located in the [[Cona system]] in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Arcona]] [[species]]. [[Hem Dazon]], a [[Gender|male]] Arcona frequently spent [[time]] in the [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina|Mos Eisley cantina]] after becoming stranded on [[Tatooine]]. He was unable to return to his home planet because he had spent all his [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on [[salt]] and [[juri juice]].<ref name="Alien Archive" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cona was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]].<ref name="Beginner Game">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref>

In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], [[Cona/Legends|Cona]] was first mentioned in ''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]'', a supplement written by [[Troy Denning]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', that was released in [[1989]].<ref name="Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races">''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]''</ref>
