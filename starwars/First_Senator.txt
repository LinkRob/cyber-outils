'''First Senator''' was the proposed [[Politics|executive]] position of the [[New Republic]], that would have replaced the largely ceremonial [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] as the highest office in the New Republic. Intended as an influential and charismatic figure, the First [[Senator]] would have been tasked with bringing order to a divided [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] and have had increased powers over economic and [[New Republic Defense Force|military]] matters.

==History==
{{Quote|After Palpatine, nobody wanted to hand over that much power again, so we don't have an executive, only a chancellor with no real authority. Mon Mothma got things done through sheer charisma, but almost every chancellor since her has been''&hellip;"<br />"''Useless.|Leia Organa and [[Han Solo]]|<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>}}
The role of First Senator was proposed by [[Lady]] [[Carise Sindian]], a [[Centrists|Centrist]], who wished to have a unifying figure who could break the impasse in the [[The galaxy|Galactic]] Senate between the [[Populists]] and Centrists. Populist Senator [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] was considered a prime candidate by many for this position, until the true nature of her [[Anakin Skywalker|parentage]] was exposed by Centrist Senator [[Ransolm Casterfo]].<ref name="Bloodline" />

After her withdrawal from the role, Senator [[Tai-Lin Garr]] ran as the new Populist candidate, but he was [[Tai-Lin Garr's assassination|assassinated]]. Following his [[Death|assassination]], both parties agreed that the election for First Senator should be postponed indefinitely.<ref name="Bloodline" />
