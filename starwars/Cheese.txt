'''Cheese''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|food]]. Shortly after the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]], an [[Unidentified old man (Hangra's Meat Shack)|old man]] operating the stall known as [[Hangra's Meat Shack]] on the planet [[Coruscant]] gave some scraps of meat and cheese to the [[human]] fugitive [[Anandra Milon]] when she approached him and seemed confused. Ananadra later ate the meat and cheese with her brother [[Santigo Milon]] in an alley.<ref name="OTLD" /> [[Falumpaset cheese]] was a type of cheese that served as an ingredient in the dish [[mashed chokeroot]], which was served in the [[restaurant]] [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|Tuggs' Grub]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]].<ref name="Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref>

[[Wookiee]]s could eat cheese,<ref name="Lost Stars" /> as could members of the [[Chiss]] [[species]].<ref name="Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good" />
