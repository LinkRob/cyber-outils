'''Derra IV''',<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> also known simply as '''Derra''', was an astronomical object<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> located in the [[Derra system]] in the [[Mikaster sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]], in grid square N-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], it housed the [[Derra Outpost]] which was [[Marshal|headed]] by [[Jedi Master]] [[Tera Sinube]]<ref name="Rising Storm" /> in [[231 BBY]].<ref name="Date note">The events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', including the appearance of [[Tera Sinube]] as the [[marshal]] of the [[Derra Outpost]], take place in [[231 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Derra was relatively close to the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Cyclor]], so when the [[Nihil]] [[First Battle of Cyclor|attacked]] the [[Cyclor Shipyards]], the [[Jedi outpost]] there was asked to assist. Sinube sent a [[Drift]] of [[Jedi Vector]]s headed by Master [[Porter Engle]].<ref name="Rising Storm" />
