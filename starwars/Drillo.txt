'''Drillo''' was an individual who was friends with the cantankerous [[prospector]]s [[Gido]] and [[Gulu]], long-[[time]] residents of [[Rambler's Reach Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]].<ref name="Survivor" /> By [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Drillo had [[Death|perished]] while attempting to ride a [[gorocco]], a type of powerful [[Diet|predator]] found on Koboh.<ref name="Survivor" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 9 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Gulu and Gido included Drillo in a list of their deceased friends in a conversation with the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] where they tried to prove that they had made attempts at friendship in the past.<ref name="Survivor" />
{{EndGame}}
