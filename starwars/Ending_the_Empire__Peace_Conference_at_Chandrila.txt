'''''Ending the Empire: Peace Conference at Chandrila''''' was an account [[author]]ed by [[Terag Liman]] of the negotiations that took place around the [[Galactic Concordance]] on the [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> which ended the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] strongly recommended the text in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
''Ending the Empire: Peace Conference at Chandrila'' was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
