During the [[Battle of Ryloth]], the [[Galactic Republic]] managed to '''break through''' the [[Ryloth blockade]] during its counteroffensive to liberate the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. While the Republic's first assault on the [[blockade]] ended in failure, its second attack&mdash;a [[ramming]] attack brought about by a false surrender masterminded by [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], followed shortly thereafter by a [[Marg Sabl]] maneuver devised by [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]&mdash;successfully shattered the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|CIS fleet]] over Ryloth.<ref name="Storm" /> [[Skywalker's fleet]] continued to engage the enemy over Ryloth until the CIS [[fleet]] retreated from Ryloth space.<ref name="Liberty" />
