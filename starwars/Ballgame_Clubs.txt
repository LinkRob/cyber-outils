The '''Ballgame Clubs''' were a pair of [[Melee weapon|clubs]] owned and utilized by the [[Wookiee]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Grozz]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his standard [[KX Clubs]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ballgame Clubs was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Grozz's KX Clubs, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. Unlike most Weapon Wraps in the game, they possessed no direct Costume counterpart, thus not being part of an in-game cosmetic set.<ref name="Hunters" />
