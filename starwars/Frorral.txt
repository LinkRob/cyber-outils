'''Frorral''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[corporal]] in the [[Rebel Alliance Special Forces|Special Forces]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Frorral was recommended by [[General]] [[Crix Madine]] to be part of the [[Endor strike team]] to take out the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star's]] [[Death Star's shield generator|shield generator]] on [[Endor]] but according to [[Caluan Ematt]] Frorral said she had come down with [[Hair|fur]] rash, resulting in her not making the [[squad]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
