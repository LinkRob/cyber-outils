The [[Scourge]] '''captured''' the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Scourge 01|''Chelator'']]<ref name="DD1" /> around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="DD1 date" /> beginning to spread the [[Scourge crisis|scourge of the droids]]. The entire [[human]] crew of the ''Chelator'', numbering 46,785, were [[Death|massacred]] by [[droid]]s corrupted by the Scourge soon after the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] departed the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Amaxine Space Station]], where [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] had ordered the [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] to retrieve all material and take them to [[Al'doleem]],<ref name="DD1" /> a [[moon]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]].<ref name="DV20173">[[Darth Vader (2017) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 3]]</ref>

Amidst the debris in the [[space station]] was a [[Unidentified droid (Fermata Cage)|spider-droid]] that was an ancient [[Artificial intelligence|intelligence]] merged with the [[Spark Eternal]], and it was able to corrupt other droids into vessels of its own, beginning with a [[Unidentified KX-series security droid (Scourge)|KX-series security droid]] that had been deployed from the ''Chelator'' as part of the recovery mission. Afterward, the Scourge spread from the [[KX-series security droid|security droid]] to [[astromech droid]]s and [[protocol droid]]s. The 2,518 droids were quickly corrupted, catching the humans by surprise, and killed the organic beings in various ways when the [[Star Destroyer]] was in [[Realspace|space]]. The [[MSE-6 series repair droid]] [[Petyr]], who worked undercover aboard the ''Chelator'' for the [[Second Revelation]] group of independent droids, reported the tragedy to the [[Colony of the Second Revelation]] before he too was corrupted by a Scourge [[spider-droid]].<ref name="DD1" />

Having conquered the Imperial [[starship]], the Scourge renamed it the ''Scourge 01''<ref name="DD3" /> and deployed spider-droids encased in the chasis of MSE-6 units, including Petyr, throughout [[the galaxy]] in order to spread its own consciousness.<ref name="DD1" /> The ''Scourge 01'' gradually transformed into a monstrous vessel that was [[Color|black]] on color and featured many irregular spikes.<ref name="DD3" />
