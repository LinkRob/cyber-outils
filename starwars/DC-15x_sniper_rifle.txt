The '''DC-15x sniper rifle''', also called the '''DC-15X Long Rifle''', was a type of [[sniper rifle]] used by the [[Galactic Republic]]. It was a modified version of the [[DC-15A blaster rifle]], dealing more damage, but containing fewer shots.<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s were known to use it.<ref name="ARC Troopers Unit Expansion">{{Legion|set=ARC Troopers Unit Expansion}}</ref>
