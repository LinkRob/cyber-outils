'''Drexelian shell-diggers''' were individuals associated with<ref name="MA">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> the astronomical object<ref name="Adventures Annual 2019">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|annual=2019|story=Hare-Brained Heist}}</ref> [[Drexel]] that were known for wearing ragged [[clothing]].<ref name="MA" /> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' to [[40 BBY]].</ref> when meeting the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Rael Averross]], who was serving as the [[lord]] [[regent]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] mentally compared Averross' clothing to that of a Drexelian shell-digger.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Drexelian shell diggers were mentioned in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
