Sometime between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Republic]] targeted the [[Aurora (fuel tanker)|''Aurora'']] [[fuel]] [[tanker]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Launching a mock '''attack''' against the tanker, the Republic dispatched a [[squadron]] of [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] to a [[star system]] to engage the ''Aurora'', which the government hoped would draw out the [[battleship]] known as the ''[[Starcrusher]]''. However, the ''Aurora'' was able to flee from the [[star]] system and the Republic [[Star Destroyer]]s before the battleship could be lured into the engagement.<ref name="BMF 34" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on the ''Aurora'' was mentioned in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 34|thirty-fourth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 34" /> published around [[August 26]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdates">{{BMFdates|34}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Infiltration of the Starcrusher|infiltration on the ''Starcrusher'']] was introduced in ''[[The Clone Wars: The Starcrusher Trap]]'', the sixth installment of the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (graphic novellas)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' graphic novellas]], authored by [[Mike W. Barr]], illustrated by the [[Fillbach Brothers]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Starcrusher">''[[The Clone Wars: The Starcrusher Trap]]''</ref> on [[July 27]], [[2011]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Books/17-857/Star-Wars-The-Clone-Wars-The-Starcrusher-Trap|text=Star Wars: The Clone Wars: The Starcrusher Trap}}</ref>
