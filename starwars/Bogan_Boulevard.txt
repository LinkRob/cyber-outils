'''Bogan Boulevard''', nicknamed "'''Broken Boulevard'''", was a road in the [[Gorse|Gorsian]] settlement of [[Shaketown]]. [[Moonglow Polychemical]]'s [[Moonglow Polychemical refinery|refinery]] was located on the street, as was [[Besalisk]] chef [[Drakka]]'s [[Drakka's Diner|eponymous diner]]. The bombing of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|shuttle]] [[Truncheon (Lambda-class)|''Truncheon'']] during the [[Gorse Conflict]] caused extensive damage to several properties on the street, including Drakka's Diner. [[Kanan Jarrus]] noted that the damage from the bombing made the street's nickname more descriptive than usual.<ref name="AND" />
