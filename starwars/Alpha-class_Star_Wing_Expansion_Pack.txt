The '''''Alpha''-class Star Wing Expansion Pack''' ('''SWX69''') was an expansion for the ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Miniatures Game]]'' from [[Fantasy Flight Games]]. The expansion features a detailed miniature of the [[Alpha-class Xg-1 Star Wing/Legends|''Alpha''-class Xg-1 Star Wing]]. It was released on [[December 8]], [[2017]] as part of the game's twelfth wave of expansions.

==Publisher's summary==
''The first widely deployed Imperial starfighter to boast shields and a hyperdrive, the Alpha-class Star Wing had limited maneuverability, but was heavily armed for its size. The fighter remains impressively durable and destructive in X-Wing™, where its two agility, four hull, and three shields are paired with the ability to unleash cascading torrents of missile and cannon fire. The Star Wing's pre-painted miniature is accompanied by four ship cards, seven upgrades, one maneuver dial, reference cards for the reload action and jam tokens, and all the plastic and tokens you need to fly the ship to battle.''

==Contents==
{{Mediacat|from=1|imagecat=Images from the Alpha-class Star Wing Expansion Pack}}
The ''Alpha''-class Star Wing Expansion Pack contains a miniature of the ''Alpha''-class Xg-1 Star Wing, plus two maneuver dials and action tokens, as well as pilot and upgrade cards.

*''Alpha''-class Xg-1 Star Wing miniature
*Plastic base (2)
*[[Starship/Legends|Ship]] token (4)
*[[Maneuvering jet/Legends|Maneuver]] dial (2)
*Critical Hit token
*Focus token (2)
*Evade token
*[[Deflector shield/Legends|Shield]] token
*[[Transponder code/Legends|ID]] token (43-44)
*[[Targeting computer/Legends|Target Lock]] token
*Stress token
*Cloak token
