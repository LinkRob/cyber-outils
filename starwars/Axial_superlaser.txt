'''Axial superlasers''' were massive [[planet]]-decimating [[superlaser]] [[cannon]]s equipped aboard [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] of the [[Sith Eternal]]'s [[fleet]], the [[Final Order]].

==Description==
{{Quote|The Emperor sent a ship from Exegol? Does that mean every ship in the fleet&hellip;''"<br />"''&hellip;has planet-killing weapons. Of course they do. All of them. This is how he finishes it.|[[Beaumont Kin]] and [[Poe Dameron]]|<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref>}}
[[File:OchiXyston-2020DarthVader11.png|thumb|left|230px|[[Ochi]] inspects the superlaser's schematics.]]
Focused by [[Bleeding|bled]]<ref name="Darth Vader 2020 11">[[Darth Vader (2020) 11|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 11]]</ref> [[kyber crystal]]s,<ref name="TROS VD" /><ref name="Complete Vehicles New">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> harvested from a subterranean mountain of kyber crystal on [[Exegol]],<ref name="Darth Vader 2020 11" /> axial [[superlaser]]s were attached to the ventral [[hull]] of the [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] that served [[Darth Sidious]]' [[Final Order]].<ref name="TROS VD" /><ref name="Complete Vehicles New" /> Similar to the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Death Star]] superlasers,<ref name="CL2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> the axial superlaser fired a [[Color|red]] beam in a sustained burst to strike through a [[planet]]'s surface from [[orbit]], with the energy beam delving deeper by pulsating at a fast rate to unleash shock waves that tore apart the planet's crust and mantle, eventually reaching its core and ripping the target apart. Such power, however, required the superlaser to be directly connected to the [[Star Destroyer]]'s [[reactor]], and severe damage to such weaponry would destroy its host [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==History==
{{Quote|Hit those underbelly cannons. Every one we knock out is a world saved.|Poe Dameron|<ref name="TROS" />}}
Axial superlaser cannons were attached to the hundreds of ''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers in the Final Order, a [[fleet]] of [[Darth]] Sidious' [[Sith Eternal]] cult. Developed in preparation for his return to power as [[Galactic Emperor]], he was confident that the new [[weapon]] would inspire fear across [[the galaxy]], discouraging any further attempt at resistance. Sidious declared that any world that opposed him would be destroyed like [[Jedha]] and [[Alderaan]]&mdash;which fell victim to the Galactic Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first Death Star]]&mdash;and [[Hosnian Prime]]&mdash;which was [[Hosnian Cataclysm|annihilated]] alongside its entire [[Hosnian system|star system]] by the [[First Order]]'s [[Starkiller Base]].<ref name="SotS" />

[[File:DerriphanFiresAtKijimi-TROS.png|thumb|right|300px|The axial superlaser destroying Kijimi]]
[[Kijimi]]&mdash;a planet with an estimated population of 310,000,000<ref name="TROS VD" />&mdash;was a [[Destruction of Kijimi|victim]] of the axial superlaser<ref name="TROS" />&mdash;specifically the one affixed to the ''[[Derriphan]]'' of the ''Xyston''-class, at the disposal of the [[Sith]] fleet.<ref name="TROS VD" /> The [[warship]] was given to the [[Commanding officer|command]] of<ref name="TROS" /> First Order [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]], loyal to Sidious himself,<ref name="TROS VD" /> who ordered the ''Derriphan'' to eliminate Kijimi due to its ties to the [[Resistance]] and as a demonstration of Pryde's steadfast allegiance to his Emperor.<ref name="TROS" />

==Behind the scenes==
The axial superlaser was introduced to the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="TROS" /> The [[Axial superlaser/Legends|axial superlaser]] originated from the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as the primary [[Superweapon/Legends|weapon]] of the reborn [[Palpatine/Legends|Emperor Palpatine's]] [[Flagship/Legends|flagship]], the [[Eclipse-class dreadnought|''Eclipse'']].<ref name="DES">''[[Dark Empire Sourcebook]]''</ref>
