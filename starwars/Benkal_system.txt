The '''Benkal system''' was a [[star system]] situated in grid square H-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], in [[Wild Space]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Benkal system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] via the appendix [[Star Systems of the Galaxy]], published on the [[StarWars.com]] website<ref name="Appendix" /> as part of [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]<ref name="Galaxy">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Benkal system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' written by Fry and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
