The [[Givin]] [[Drusil Bephorin]] was a noted cryptologist.<ref name="Heir to the Jedi">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref> According to [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]], [[Seyn Marana]] was a master cryptologist.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
