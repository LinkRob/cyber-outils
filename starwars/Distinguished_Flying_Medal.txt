The '''Distinguished Flying Medal''' was an award that was given to both [[Alliance Military]] or civilian [[pilot]]s who demonstrated heroism, conspicuous gallantry, or incredible prowess while operating or crewing a [[starship]] or [[airspeeder]] in support of military operations. Although it was commonly awarded to combat [[Rebel pilot|Alliance pilots]], the medal was not a specific combat [[medal]]. The Distinguished Flying Medal could be awarded to pilots or crews who ran [[blockade]]s, by rebel [[smuggler]]s, or by another other pilot who went above and beyond their call of duty while flying for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Lead" />

The medal was characterized by a round, [[silver]] alloy medallion that portrayed a stylized [[Ion engine|ion turbine]] that was suspended from a broad, rectangular [[Color|red]] [[ribbon]] with a broad central silver stripe flanked by two thin blue stripes. The medal was typically only issued once during a pilot's lifetime.<ref name="Lead" />
