'''Baskol Yeesrim''' was a [[Gran]] from the [[planet]] [[Malastare]] who [[Senator|represented]] the [[Gran Protectorate]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]].<ref name="Geektionary" /> Yeesrim was part of the same [[Malastare delegation]] with Senator [[Aks Moe]], who represented the [[Congress of Malastare]]. The pair were sharing a [[repulsorpod]] with a third Gran in the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] when [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of the planet [[Naboo]] came before the Senate to ask for their aid in ending the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo|occupation]] of her [[homeworld]].<ref name="TPM" />

Senator [[Lott Dod]] of the Trade Federation demanded proof of these accusations,<ref name="TPM" /> and Yeesrim pointedly agreed with Dod's suggestion that a committee be formed to investigate the claims.<ref name="Geektionary" /> After Moe stated his support for the committee, Amidala called for a [[Vote of No Confidence]] in [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]]. Yeesrim had [[Color|pink]] skin and black [[eye]]s. The senator wore a red and brown set of [[Clothing|robes]] while in the Senate Building.<ref name="TPM" />

==Behind the scenes==
Baskol Yeesrim appeared in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. The character was first identified in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''.<ref name="Geektionary" /> The name was first used in the 1999 [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book [[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary|''Star Wars: Episode I The Visual Dictionary'']], although the book incorrectly associated it with an image of Aks Moe.<ref name="TPMVD">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The name was then first associated with a separate Gran on a card in the [[2001]] "[[Coruscant Limited]]" set of the ''[[Star Wars Customizable Card Game]]'' produced by [[Decipher]]<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=Coruscant Limited|cardname=Baskol Yeesrim}}</ref>
