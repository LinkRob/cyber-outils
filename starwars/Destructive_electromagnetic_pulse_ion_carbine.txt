The '''Destructive Electromagnetic Pulse Ion Carbine''' (abbreviated as '''DEMP Ion Carbine''' or just '''DEMP''') was an [[electromagnetic pulse]] weapon in use by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Inferno Squad]],<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> and manufactured by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]]. The DEMP Ion Carbine was the [[blaster carbine]] version of the [[DEMP gun]].<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" />

[[Iden Versio]] wielded one while undercover among the [[Dreamers' encampment]] on [[Jeosyn]].<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
