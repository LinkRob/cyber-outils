A '''freighter'''<ref name="Mace Windu 4" /> was owned by the [[smuggler]] [[Azita Cruuz]]. When Cruuz obtained the formula for [[coaxium ultra]] on the [[moon]] [[Ro Mira]], she and the [[Jedi]] [[Mace Windu]] traveled to her [[freighter]], which was parked at the [[Radial Spaceport]]. However, [[Diya]] and [[Muro]], [[Dusk Weaver]]s of the [[Had'le Path]], were waiting at the [[starship]] and confronted the two.<ref name="Mace Windu 3" /> In the ensuing fight, Windu let the freighter fall on top of the Path's leader known as [[The Shroud]], but the [[Devaronian]] managed to [[Telekinesis|lift]] the ship back up with [[the Force]]. Cruuz then shot at her freighter from [[Mace Windu's Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Windu's]] [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7 ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]] and it exploded, burying the Shroud underneath.<ref name="Mace Windu 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
The freighter first appeared in [[Mace Windu 3|the third issue]] of the [[2024]] comic miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Mace Windu]]'',<ref name="Mace Windu 3" /> before appearing in the following [[Mace Windu 4|fourth and final issue]].<ref name="Mace Windu 4" /> Both issues were written by [[Marc Bernardin]] and drawn by [[Georges Jeanty]]. Throughout the two issues, the ship can be only seen in two panels and is never shown in full.<ref name="Mace Windu 3" /><ref name="Mace Windu 4" />
