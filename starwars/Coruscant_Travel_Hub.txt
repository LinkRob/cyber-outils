The '''Coruscant Travel Hub''' was a one-room building found within the [[Coruscant Underworld (battlefield)|Coruscant Underworld]] battlefield in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. It contained seating on one side, as well as two kiosks staffed by [[Spa droid|droid attendants]] on the other, allowing access to a back platform where custom painted [[hover taxi]]s could dock. The walls of this building were lined with advertisements, and two screens displaying a greeting message from the hub extended down from the ceiling. The flooring had been pulled up in various areas, revealing the supports underneath, and the building as a whole seemed to be in disrepair.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Coruscant Travel Hub first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It was added to the game via the inclusion of the Coruscant Underworld map.<ref name="Hunters" />
