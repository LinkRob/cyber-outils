"'''Ballast Breaker'''" was the name given to an [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[Wookiee]] trained as a [[gladiator]] by the [[Xonti Brothers]] for [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus]] the [[Hutt]]'s [[Grakkus Arena|arena]] on the [[moon]] [[Nar Shaddaa]]<ref name="Smuggler" /> by [[0 ABY]].<ref name="Date">[[Star Wars (2015) 12|''Star Wars'' (2015) 12]] depicts the shut down of [[Grakkus Arena]] and [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi]]'s arrest by the [[Galactic Empire]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] to [[0 ABY]], "Ballast Breaker" must have been delivered by that date.</ref> They, alongside two other fighters known as "[[Wrollanppf|One-Eyed Volant]]" and "[[Snarrl the Feral]]," were part of [[Kashyyyk Group B9]] and were shipped together.<ref name="Smuggler" />

On a receipt for the delivery of the Wookiee gladiators, Ballast Breaker was referred to as a mid-grade specimen and as a [[Heavyweight Grappler Class]]. The receipt also claimed that the Wookiees were certified [[disease]]-free by the [[Trandoshan port authority]]. The receipt was placed in the pages of the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'', a book kept by various [[pirate]]s, criminals, and [[smuggler]]s.<ref name="Smuggler" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ballast Breaker was mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', a [[2018]] reference book written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler" />
