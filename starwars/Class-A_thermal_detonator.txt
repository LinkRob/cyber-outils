The '''Class-A thermal detonator''' was a model of [[thermal detonator]] manufactured by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]]

==Description==
The detonator's power and range made it illegal for any use other than military purposes. Featuring a blast radius of up to 20 [[meter]]s, it could be tuned to limit the radius depending on the situation. An activation panel was located on top of the detonator, in which an internal fusion reaction would be activated via the control panel. Powered by volatile [[baradium]], the detonator was protected by a [[thermite]] casing.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Certain seasoned criminals owned modified Class-A thermal detonators with blast radiuses ten times as big as the original.<ref name="EHWaUE">{{EncyclopediaCite|Explosives, Heavy Weapons, and Power Sources}}</ref>

The control pins of the detonator's triggers could be programmed to act as a dead man's switch, one that got automatically operated if the operator became incapacitated or otherwise removed from control.<ref name="RotJ paperback novel">[[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (paperback novel)|''Star Wars: Return of the Jedi'' junior novelization]]</ref> One version of the Class-A was the [[magnetic thermal detonator]].<ref name="TogetherAgain">{{TCW|Together Again}}</ref>

==History==
The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="Supply" /> and [[Galactic Republic]] both made use of Class-A thermal detonators during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="The General" /> [[Stormtrooper grenadier]]s of the [[Imperial Military|military]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] made use of Class-A thermal detonators.<ref name="Class-A thermal detonator">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'' identifies the [[thermal detonator]] shown in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]'' and [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] as a Class-A thermal detonator. Since the [[grenade]] fired by [[stormtrooper grenadier]]s, [[soldier]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|military]], in ''Star Wars Rebels: Recon Missions'' functions and appear visually congruent with the Class-A thermal detonators in ''Star Wars Battlefront'' and ''Return of the Jedi'', the grenades in ''Recon Missions'' must also be Class-A thermal detonators.</ref> In the hands of the [[stormtrooper]] grenadier, detonators were fired at targets through the use of [[grenade launcher]]s.<ref name="Recon Missions" />
