The '''Ferrixian Spiced Puffer Pig Slider''' was a type of [[sandwich]] that could be purchased at [[Ronto Roasters]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Ronto Roasters" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The [[Foodstuff|meal]] was [[Diet|consumable]] by [[human]]s. It contained the meat of [[puffer pig]]s.<ref name="Ronto Roasters" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ferrixian Spiced Puffer Pig Slider first appeared as an offering available to guests at the [[In-universe|real-world]] Ronto Roasters restaurant in the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]], exclusively for [[Star Wars Nite]]s in [[2023]].<ref name="2023 brochure">{{Disney|subdomain=cdn1.parksmedia.wdprapps|url=vision-dam/digital/parks-platform/parks-standard-assets/disneyland/events/star-wars-nite/Disneyland-After_Dark-Star_Wars-Nite-2023-Event_Guide.pdf|text=Disneyland After Dark: Star Wars Nite 2023}}</ref> The menu described it as "Fried [[Bread|Bun]], Chile-marinated [[Wikipedia:Pork belly|Pork Belly]], [[Wikipedia:Pickling|Pickled]] [[Vegetable]]s, [[Wikipedia:Jalapeño|Jalapeño]] Rings, [[Wikipedia:Coriander|Cilantro]]."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ronto-roasters/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Ronto Roasters: Lunch and Dinner Menu}}</ref> The dish references the planet [[Ferrix]], introduced in the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.
