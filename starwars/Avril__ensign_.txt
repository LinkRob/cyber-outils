[[Ensign]] '''Avril''' was an individual who served within the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] for the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="The Will to Fight" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: The Will to Fight]]" features the [[Battle of Eriadu]], which takes place in [[228 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:THRContent228BBY|here]].</ref> during the [[Battle of Eriadu]], the ensign contacted the [[human]] [[pilot]] [[Pikka Adren]] to alert her of a message that contained her name in it.<ref name="The Will to Fight" />

==Behind the scenes==
Avril appeared as a voice in the short story "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: The Will to Fight]]," written by [[Lydia Kang]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="The Will to Fight" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=SWCE 2023: 9 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref> The story was published in the [[Star Wars Insider 232|232nd issue]] of the magazine ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' as the ninth and final installment in the [[The High Republic: Tales from the Occlusion Zone|''Tales from the Occlusion Zone'']] serialized fiction on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=m/182-star-wars-insider-232|text=Star Wars Insider #232}}</ref>
