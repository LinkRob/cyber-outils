'''''Dark Droids'' 2''' is the second issue of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] limited series ''[[Star Wars: Dark Droids]]''. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Luke Ross]] and [[Alex Sinclair]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[September 6]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''The horror continues as THE SCOURGE begins to execute its grand plan, orchestrating its droid minions across the galaxy. It learns more with every passing moment, grows stronger&hellip;and is selecting the next targets on its path to total dominion over all mechanical intelligence! Standing in its way, only the warrior-priest droid AJAX SIGMA and the sentient droids of the SECOND REVELATION.''
