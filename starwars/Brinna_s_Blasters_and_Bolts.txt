'''Brinna's Blasters and Bolts''',<ref name="SWH" /> simply known as '''Brinna's Blasters''',<ref name="Sentinel M2">{{HuntersCite|quest=Sentinel Mastery II}}</ref> was a store that sold ranged [[weapon]]ries.<ref name="SWH" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Hunters Date">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> Brinna's Blasters and Bolts became a sponsor for the competitive sporting show [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]]<ref name="SWH" /> on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]<ref name="Game">{{Hunters|url=game|text=The Game}}</ref> and was advertised during matches by the [[Dowutin]] announcer [[Boz Vega]].<ref name="SWH" /> During an interview for the show, it was suggested that<ref name="Sentinel M2" /> the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]]<ref name="CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Sentinel]] bought his [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon|E-Web heavy repeater]],<ref name="Sentinel M2" /> the [[Prototype E-Web]],<ref name="Prototype E-Web">{{HuntersCite|character=Sentinel|costume=Prototype E-Web}}</ref> from the store after [[Imara Vex]]<ref name="Sentinel M2" />&mdash;another competitor of the show<ref name="SWH" />&mdash;debunked Sentinel's dubious claims of how he originally obtained the weapon.<ref name="Sentinel M2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brinna's Blasters and Bolts was first introduced in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', which was developed by [[NaturalMotion]]'s [[BossAlien]]<ref name="SWH" /> and initially published by [[Zynga]] on [[November 16]], [[2021]], as a [[Wikipedia:Soft launch|soft launch]]<ref name="Soft">{{Twitter|ZyngaStarWars|status/1460656552734605317|[[Zynga]] Star Wars|date=2021-11-16|quote=We are excited to announce Star Wars: Hunters is now entering soft launch and is available on Android for players in India, Philippines, Malaysia & Indonesia.|archivedate=20220625084625|archivefile=File:ZyngaStarWars-SoftLaunchDate-Twitter.png}}</ref> before the global release on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Global">{{SWYouTube|4dZcq6m3y40|Star Wars: Hunters Launch Date Reveal Trailer}}</ref> The game was later shut down on [[October 1]], [[2025]].<ref name="Offline">{{Hunters|url=news/sunset-on-vespaara|text=Sunset on Vespaara}}</ref> It was mentioned as one of the interchangeable audio lines Vega says during gameplay.<ref name="SWH" /> Additionally, the establishment was identified as Brinna's Blasters in the "Sentinel Mastery II" questline.<ref name="Sentinel M2" />
