"'''Copper'''" was the nickname of a [[protocol droid]] owned by the [[Galactic Empire]]. As an experiment, Imperial [[technician]]s equipped the [[droid]] with [[deflector shield generator]]s to provide defense for ground [[troop]]s. However, the project was abandoned, and Copper was recruited by a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]].<ref name="Copper" />

==Behind the scenes==
Copper first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', a [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] released by [[Kabam]] for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]] and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] platforms. In ''Uprising'', Copper is a Unique Crew Member who can be assigned on Crew Runs to collect helpful items.<ref name="Copper" />
