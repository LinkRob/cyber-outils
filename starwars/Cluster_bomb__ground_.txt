'''Cluster bombs''' were a type of [[bomb|explosive]] projectile utilized by the [[Ewok]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Pilbush]] within the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. These bombs could be fired from the [[grenade launcher]] equipped to Pilbush's custom [[All Terrain Ewok Transport]]. Upon impact, the primary projectile would detonate, scattering smaller bombs across the surrounding area, which would then individually detonate, dealing a wide area of devastation.<ref name="Hunters" />
