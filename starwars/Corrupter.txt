The '''''Corrupter''''' was a [[Victory-class Star Destroyer|''Victory''-class Star Destroyer]] that, during the [[Imperial Era]], belonged to the [[Imperial Navy]]. It was equipped with [[sublight engine]]s and [[turbolaser]]s. At some point, the [[Star Destroyer]] was flanked by at least five [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bombers]] in [[Realspace|space]] and fired multiple shots at a target.<ref name="Armada" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Corrupter'' was introduced to the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Swayed by the Dark Side]]'', a [[2017]] expansion of [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Card Game]]''. The [[Starship|ship's]] card was titled "''Corruptor''" and featured artwork attributed to [[Madeline Boni]].<ref name="Card Game">{{FFGTCG|set=Swayed by the Dark Side|cardname=''Corruptor''|link=alliances-cycle/swayed-by-the-dark-side/corruptor-r2056}}</ref> However, [[Christoph Peters]] claimed that<ref name="ArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=christophpeters|url=artwork/zOgJqZ|name=Christoph Peters|text=FFG : Star Wars Armada|quote=Let's make it clear, because I've seen other people giving credits for this image to other artists. This is Christoph Peters hands.}}</ref> he<ref name="DeviantArt">{{DeviantArt|username=trejoeeee|url=trejoeeee/about|name=Christoph Peters|text=About|quote=He / Him}}</ref> had created the artwork, which had been misattributed to other artists.<ref name="ArtStation" /> The illustration was reused from the [[2015]] [[Victory-class Star Destroyer Expansion Pack|''Victory''-class Star Destroyer Expansion Pack]], a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] expansion for Fantasy Flight Games' miniatures game [[Star Wars: Armada|''Star Wars'': Armada]].<ref name="Victory Expansion">{{Armada|set=Victory-class Star Destroyer Expansion Pack|cardname=''Corrupter''}}</ref> In [[2020]], the ''[[Upgrade Card Collection]]'' for ''Star Wars'': Armada also included a card for the Star Destroyer, titled "''Corrupter''."<ref name="Armada" /> This article assumes the more recent spelling is correct.

The [[Corrupter/Legends|starship]] originated in the ''Star Wars'' Legends continuity, first appearing in ''[[X-Wing: The Bacta War]]'', which was written by [[Michael A. Stackpole]] and released in [[1997]] as the fourth novel in the [[Star Wars: X-Wing (novel series)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']] series.<ref name="Bacta War">''[[X-Wing: The Bacta War]]''</ref> The ''Corrupter'' was then first pictured in the [[2006]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]'', in which its name was among several that could be randomly applied to a [[Victory-class Star Destroyer/Legends|''Victory''-class Star Destroyer]].<ref name="EAW">''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]''</ref>
