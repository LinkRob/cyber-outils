'''Daladit-Chan''' was the [[Parent|father]] of the [[Klatooinian]] [[musician]] [[Umpass-stay]]. In [[4 ABY]], Daladit-Chan gave the order to his son to [[Death|kill]] the [[Hutt]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] through a [[DRK-1 Dark Eye probe droid]]. This command started the [[second plot to assassinate Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], which almost got [[Maximilian Rebo]] killed.<ref name="Max" />
