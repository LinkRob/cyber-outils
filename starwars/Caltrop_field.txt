A '''caltrop field'''<ref name="Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode205.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Landing at Point Rain}}</ref> was a field of large spikes that protruded from the ground on the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]].<ref name="Landing" /> In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> during the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]] of the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] and the [[Jet's battalion|battalion]] of his [[Clone Commander]], [[Jet]], were stymied by a massive caltrop field which prevented the movement of their [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]s from reaching the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Point Rain|rendevzous point]] for the battle. However, while passing through the caltrop field, Mundi used his [[Ki-Adi-Mundi's lightsaber|lightsaber]] to cut through several of the spikes, which allowed the [[walker]]s to move forward.<ref name="Landing" />

==Behind the scenes==
A caltrop field first appeared in "[[Landing at Point Rain]],"<ref name="Landing" /> the fifth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which aired on [[November 4]], [[2009]]. It was identified in the corresponding episode guide on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Ep Guide" />
