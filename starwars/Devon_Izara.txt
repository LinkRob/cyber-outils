'''Devon Izara''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]] who was [[Jedi training|trained]] as a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] by [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]] and later sought revenge on the [[Galactic Empire]] for the murder of her master, accepting training in the ways of the [[dark side of the Force]] by the former [[Sith Lord]] and [[crime lord]] [[Maul]]. 

Following the rise of the Galactic Empire, she and her master hid on the [[planet]] [[Janix]]. Between [[17 BBY]] and around [[13 BBY]], Izara was captured by the world's [[Tactical Defense Force|police force]] after attempting to get [[Foodstuff|food]] for her master. While jailed at the [[Janix Civil Defense HQ|police headquarters]], the girl was discovered by Maul, who took the unwilling Padawan in his escape from the facility.

==Biography==
===Padawan on the run===
Devon Izara, a [[Twi'lek]]<ref name="Devon DB" /> [[Gender|female]],<ref name="Chapter2" /> was a young<ref name="Chapter1">{{MSL|Chapter 1: The Dark Revenge}}</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]]<ref name="Devon DB" /> of the [[Jedi Order]]<ref name="Daki DB">{{Databank|eeko-dio-daki|Eeko-Dio Daki}}</ref> [[Jedi training|trained]] by<ref name="Devon DB">{{Databank|devon-izara|Devon Izara}}</ref> the [[Mosyk]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]].<ref name="Daki DB" /> In the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Devon DB" /> the pair hid from the Empire<ref name="Interview" /> on the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="SOM1" /> [[planet]] [[Janix]], a world out of the view of the Imperials.<ref name="Janix DB">{{Databank|janix|Janix}}</ref>

===Jailed on Janix===
{{Quote|I told you already. We do not steal.''"<br/>"''I'm tired of begging.''"<br/>"''We could always rely on the kindness of strangers.|Eeko-Dio Daki and Devon Izara|<ref name="Chapter1" />}}
[[File:MSL-Chapter5-Thumbnail.png|thumb|300px|right|Izara and her master hid from the Empire on Janix.]]
At some point between [[17 BBY]] and around [[13 BBY]],<ref name="ShadowLordDate">{{ShadowLordDate}}</ref> the teenage<ref name="Step Into the Shadows">{{SWYouTube|afCvDF7qOpw|Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord &#124; Step Into the Shadows &#124; Streaming on Disney+}}</ref> Padawan and her master visited a marketplace on Janix. Izara looked upwards and noticed a [[Law enforcement agency|police]] chase sparked by a [[bank]] robbery just prior. Daki told her it was not their concern, and the pair turned their attention to the Jedi Master's hunger. Izara encouraged her master to eat, but Daki walked away from the [[Foodstuff|produce]] stall he took interest in. The Padawan then told her master to steal, causing him to reiterate to her that they did not steal. The girl told her master that she was tired of begging, but her master responded back that they could always rely on the kindness of others and kept walking.<ref name="Chapter1" />

Seeing her master's failed attempt at using kindness with a passerby, Izara<ref name="Chapter1" /> decisively<ref name="Chapter1AD" /> hopped over the ledge she was on and walked to the food stall. While the stall's [[Unidentified fruit vendor|attendant]] was distracted, Izara stole a handful of<ref name="Chapter1" /> [[melon|melons]]<ref name="Chapter1AD">{{MSL|Chapter 1: The Dark Revenge}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> unnoticed. Making her way back to Daki, she bumped into a Janix [[Unidentified TDF droid (Devon Izara)|police droid]], knocking the produce out of her hands. The stall worker noticed and shouted at the [[police droid]], who came back to the stall to arrest the Padawan. Daki told her to not cause a disturbance and Izara complied with the [[droid]], handing her [[cloak]] to her master and walking away with it and another police droid.<ref name="Chapter1" />

The droids brought Izara to<ref name="Chapter1" /> the Janix [[Janix Civil Defense HQ|police headquarters]],<ref name="SOM1">[[Shadow of Maul 1|''Shadow of Maul'' 1]]</ref> where one of the police droids asked her for her name and identification. The uncooperative Padawan was then noticed by<ref name="Chapter1" /> [[Captain]] [[Brander Lawson]],<ref name="Lawson DB">{{Databank|Captain-Brander-Lawson|Captain Brander Lawson}}</ref> who asked the droid for her story. Recognizing her youth, Lawson had Izara and her report brought by his desk. Lawson asked her for her name, but the droid reiterated she was not complying. As the droid talked, the Padawan fiddled with<ref name="Chapter1" /> a ball<ref name="Chapter1AD" /> she took from the captain's desk. Seeing a picture of his family on his desk, Izara asked Lawson if a [[Rylee Lawson|boy]] in the picture was his son. Realizing she had taken the ball off of his desk, Lawson took it back and offered the girl a choice: produce identification or spent a [[Local day|night]] in a cell. With no response again, the captain had her sent her to get processed.<ref name="Chapter1" />

===The breakout===
[[File:Devon Izara DB.png|thumb|left|280px|Devon Izara during the power outage caused during Maul's break-in]]
Izara spent some [[time]] in her cell<ref name="Chapter1" /> on a detention level<ref name="Chapter1AD" /> before another, the [[Aleena]] criminal [[Looti Vario]], was imprisoned near her. Noticing the Padawan looking at him, the Aleena scoffed at her before he was placed in his cell. During their stay at the headquarters,<ref name="Chapter1" /> the former [[Sith Lord]]<ref name="Interview" /> [[Maul]] [[Raid on the Janix Civil Defense HQ|broke into and took control]] of the building, as he planned on killing Vario. When Lawson ran past Izara's cell, the Twi'lek yelled to be let out, but was kept contained,<ref name="Chapter1" /> allowing her to listen to the commotion caused by Maul.<ref name="Chapter1AD" /> He was able to find the Aleena's cell, where the criminal was able to convince the [[Zabrak]] to not kill him. As [[Shadow Collective|Maul's forces]] took Vario and left him behind, the former Sith Lord noticed Izara in her cell and opened it. The Padawan identified him<ref name="Chapter1" /> before they both left the cell.<ref name="Chapter2" />

The Padawan, Maul, Vario, and Maul's forces made their escape from the headquarters down a service tunnel<ref name="Chapter2AD" /> while being met with police resistance. Izara tried to get free of<ref name="Chapter2" /> [[Scorn]], the Zabrak thug<ref name="Scorn DB">{{Databank|scorn|Scorn}}</ref> she was kept with, kicking him in the face as they climbed through the shaft, though the Zabrak grabbed her by her [[Limb|leg]]. Izara was able to kick herself free from Scorn, backflipping off the ladder and landing on Maul's [[Spybot]] droid. The Padawan went though a corridor within the shaft and tried to escape through it, but Spybot was able to [[stun]] her. Maul's group then escaped though the passageway and destroyed the door controls to it, preventing Lawson from catching up with them. Maul and his forces, with Izara, then left the area on<ref name="Chapter2" /> a modified [[bank transport]].<ref name="Chapter2AD" />

The shuttle was taken onto a roadway where it was noticed by Daki, who jumped from<ref name="Chapter2" /> the sky bridge he was on<ref name="Chapter2AD" /> and used [[the Force]] to crash it. Izara, now conscious, tried to escape from the crash site, though she was noticed by [[Mandalorian]] warrior [[Rook Kast]], who bound her in<ref name="Chapter2" /> a grappling cable.<ref name="Chapter2AD" /> Maul, surrounded by Daki and police, used the Force to break a walkway full of pedestrians, allowing them to escape to<ref name="Chapter2" /> an old terminal in the city underbelly used as a [[Maul's safe house|safehouse]]<ref name="Chapter2AD" /> by the Zabrak. The next day, Izara's image was used by the police force while they investigated the escape.<ref name="Chapter2" />

===Captured by Maul===
{{Quote|How long are you gonna keep me here?''"<br/>"''You may leave any time you'd like.''"<br/>"''I wanna leave now. But clearly, that's not possible.''"<br/>"''If that is your point of view, and if I may be so bold, that greatly concerns me.|Devon Izara and Maul|<ref name="Chapter2" />}}
At the location, Izara agreed to receive Mandalorian medical treatment for her injured [[Limb|arm]] at the advice of Maul. While the Twi'lek was treated for her wound, Maul discussed her with Kast, telling<ref name="Chapter2" /> her<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> he would form the Jedi into a [[weapon]] against those who had betrayed him, including the [[Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]]. Izara was then brought to Maul and Kast, the former of which questioned her on who she was. Only responding back that she was a [[prisoner]], the Padawan was then taken to another cell,<ref name="Chapter2" /> this time in the form of a [[tanker tube]].<ref name="Chapter2AD" /> Maul told the girl that she must adapt before the cell door closed,<ref name="Chapter2" /> leaving Izara to survey her surroundings.<ref name="Chapter2AD" /> Shortly afterwards, Izara was an onlooker as Vario was ordered by the Zabrak to help him disrupt the criminal [[Pyke Syndicate]].<ref name="Chapter2" /> 

In her cell, Izara questioned Maul on how long she would be kept at his location, to which he answered she could leave anytime she'd like. The Padawan did not believe him, greatly concerning the former Sith Lord. He then offered the girl a way to power not taught by the Jedi Order, which she declined. Maul questioned her on what it meant to be a Jedi in a time of lawlessness. Izara still believed the Jedi principles and told Maul stop pretending like he cared, citing the stories she had heard about him. The Zabrak combatted her viewpoint and asked her how he'd prove to her that they were allies. Izara asked to be freed, which Maul made her do herself. He then left the girl to go disturb the Pyke Syndicate.<ref name="Chapter2" /> 

Sometime later, Izara noticed Maul staring at her, causing her to turn her back to him. Maul then told the Padawan that it was time for her to view the galaxy as it truly was, before disappearing out of the girl's view. Taking advantage of the moment, Izara attempted to open the door of the tanker tube with the Force, but to no avail. Defeated, the Padawan then went to sit down at the back of her cell, where she noticed a gap between the tanker tube and the wall. Using the Force again, she pushed back against the wall, pushing the tube back and creating a bigger gap for her to escape from.<ref name="Chapter2" />

===Escape from Maul===
{{Quote|I offer not only to train you to reach your full potential, but also because I need your help ... to fight a common enemy.|Maul|<ref name="Chapter3">{{MSL|Chapter 3: Whispers in the Unknown}}</ref>}}
Now free from her cell, Izara moved cautiously around the outside of it,<ref name="Chapter3AD">{{MSL|Chapter 3: Whispers in the Unknown}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> though she still caught the attention of Vario, who was shocked and demanded to know how she had escaped.<ref name="Chapter3" /> Kast and three Mandalorian commandos then entered the detention area and blocked her path to the exit and directed her to Maul. Izara briefly considered it, then reluctantly walked towards him in the vestibule where he had prepared a [[tea]] ceremony.<ref name="Chapter3AD" /> 

Maul, seeing her presence, began pouring the [[Cassius tea|tea]] and asked her for her name, which she refused. Izara, who had spotted his [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|lightsaber]] next to the tea, then sat down with him. The criminal asked to see her arm, a command she ignored, causing Maul to ask the Jedi if she wanted him to be the person she thought he was. Izara then gave him her arm to inspect it, though quickly withdrew it. Maul commented that his caring for her well-being was contradictory to what she thought of him, but invited her to reposition her view on him. The criminal tried to appeal to what Izara went through as a Jedi, comparing it to what his master did to him and the [[Death|murders]] of his family. Izara then used the Force to quickly grab the Zabrak's [[lightsaber]] and tried to leave. In reaction, Maul snuffed out the [[lantern]] in the room, plunging Izara and her surroundings in darkness.<ref name="Chapter3" /> 

Using the [[Color|red]] lightsaber to light the way through the darkness, Izara wandered through the vestibule as Maul taunted her. The Twi'lek discovered a door in her wandering, but found its controls unresponsive. Maul then appeared in and out the darkness behind her, continuing to make comments about her and her abilities. Returning to the door,<ref name="Chapter3" /> she sensed<ref name="Chapter3AD" /> Maul behind her, causing her to whip around in defense, though Maul was no longer behind her. Frightened, Izara continued to avoid Maul in the darkness, noticing an exit above her and a way to access it while doing so. The former Sith offered to train the girl so they could fight their common enemy, which Izara inferred to be the Empire. Maul questioned the girl if she had ever encountered the Empire's [[Jedi hunter]]s, and she responded that she would take her chances. Izara then extinguished Maul's lightsaber and scaled a rock wall to get to the exit, seeing no sign of Maul when looking back.<ref name="Chapter3" />

She soon arrived at a sunlit exit, only for her path to be blocked by Maul, who quipped that she was foolish to believe he would allow her to escape. Unafraid, Izara drew her lightsaber, and the two dueled. Maul mocked her, claiming she had no future and hope and she was squandering her Jedi talents. Though he knocked her down, she got back up and they continued their battle. When Maul taunted her and said she had no purpose and her life was in ruins, an enraged Izara managed to clear a path to the exit. Maul reclaimed his lightsaber and told her he would need that back, and called her by name for the first time. Horrified that Maul knew her name, Izara ran out into the light while he smiled.<ref name="Chapter3" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:DevonIzara-ShadowOfMaul3.png|thumb|right|200px|Izara's desire to take action clashed with her master's want to hide.]]
Izara was an angsty<ref name="Interview" /> and savvy Padawan whose yearning to take action sometimes clashed with her master's desire to stay hidden.<ref name="Devon DB" /> She struggled to find her footing with the rise of the Empire and frequently had to figure out right and wrong,<ref name="Interview" /> possessing some innocence while doing so.<ref name="ShadowLordTrailer" /> The girl stood at 1.51 [[Meter|meters]], and had [[Color|pink]] skin,<ref name="Devon DB" /> pale green<ref name="Chapter2AD" /> eyes, and a series of purple striped markings on her [[lekku]].<ref name="Devon DB" />

==Powers and abilities==
The Twi'lek was [[Force-sensitive]]<ref name="Chapter2" /> and well-trained.<ref name="Devon DB" /> Izara was also a skilled fighter able to defend herself with her [[Devon Izara's lightsaber|lightsaber]] when she needed to, using mostly [[Form III|Soresu]] and [[Form IV|Ataru]].<ref name="Interview" />

==Equipment==
She used a silver and black lightsaber that emitted a blue blade.<ref name="Interview" /> While on Janix, Izara wore a blue [[Clothing|shirt]] with white sleeves and green shoulder pads, a gray [[belt]], light blue pants with tan stripes down the side, brown [[Boot|boots]], brown head wrappings, and a tan cloak.<ref name="Chapter1" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|She makes me feel like I'm doing what I should be doing. Because of the things I've been through as a young girl, when I was a teenager, and as a woman, she came very easy to me. I feel like she's like my sister. We're very much a part of each other.|Gideon Adlon|<ref name="Interview" />}}
[[File:DevonIzara-MaulShadowLord-CharacterPoster.jpg|thumb|left|230px|A promotional character poster for Devon Izara]]
Devon Izara first appeared in the first two episodes of the [[Maul - Shadow Lord Season 1|first season]] of the [[Disney+]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'', where the character was voiced by [[Gideon Adlon]]. The episodes, "[[Chapter 1: The Dark Revenge]]" and "[[Chapter 2: Sinister Schemes]],"<ref name="Chapter2" /><ref name="Chapter1" /> premiered on [[April 6]], [[2026]].<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|starwars|status/2033951658485682588|''[[Star Wars]]''|date=2026-03-17|archivefile=File:MaulShadowLord-EpisodeTitles-Twitter.jpg}}</ref> Adlon shared a sister-like connection to the character, as Izara made her feel like she was doing what she should be doing. Because of what Adlon went through growing up, Izara came easy to her. When creating scenes for the character that matched Adlon's skill, Head Writer [[Matt Michnovetz]] and the crew found it difficult due to how talented the voice actor was.<ref name="Interview" /> 

Before the episodes released, the character was first identified in an article on [[StarWars.com]] accompanying the series' first public teaser trailer, published on [[January 22]], 2026. In the article, the show's Supervising Director [[Brad Rau]] described Gideon's performance of Izara as bringing a sense of grounded reality to the show. Vice President of Lucasfilm Animation [[Athena Yvette Portillo|Athena Portillo]] added that seeing Adlon and [[Sam Witwer]], the voice of Maul, together in the booth was like seeing the characters interact with each other.<ref name="ShadowLordTrailer" /> 

In the article "Meet the Cast of Star Wars: Maul ''&ndash; Shadow Lord''," first published on StarWars.com on [[March 30]], 2026,<ref name="Interview old">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-maul-shadow-lord-cast|text=Meet the Cast of ''Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord''|oldversion=20260330202744}}</ref> Rau described Adlon as powerful in her role and Izara's character arc as awesome. Michnovitz also said the character's relationship with Maul was one of the most important in the series, and that the crew was excited to see how fans saw it.<ref name="Interview" />

The melons Izara steals in "Chapter 1" were originally going to be [[meiloorun fruit]] but were changed as they were too big for Izara to easily conceal.<ref name="Shadow Lord Trivia">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-maul-shadow-lord-trivia|text=Inside the Making of ''Star Wars: Maul – Shadow Lord''}}</ref>
