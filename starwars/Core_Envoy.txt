The '''''Core Envoy''''' was an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] used by the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. The [[Star Destroyer]] was equipped with [[engine]]s<ref name="Tarkin" /> and measured 1,600 [[meter]]s long.<ref name="Length" /> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> the ''Core Envoy'' was the sole ''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer in the [[Sentinel Base's flotilla|flotilla]] belonging to [[Sentinel Base]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] facility [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] which oversaw the construction of the Imperial [[battle station]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> later known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Rogue One guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

At some point that [[Standard year|year]], the ''Core Envoy'' and most of the [[flotilla]]'s other [[capital ship]]s were away from Sentinel Base, escorting supply [[convoy]]s to the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]], where the battle [[Space station|station]] was being constructed. This meant that when the base was [[Attack on Sentinel Base|attacked]] by a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]], Tarkin was forced to use the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Electrum (Venator-class)|''Electrum'']] to defend Sentinel instead.<ref name="Tarkin" />
