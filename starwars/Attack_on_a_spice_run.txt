In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Unbroken Clan]] carried out an '''attack on a spice run''' that was being undertaken by the [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate]] at the [[Smugglers' Refueling Station]] in the [[Kessel sector]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" />

Following a [[bombing on Chorgad]], in which a number of Unbroken Clan [[soldier]]s were [[Death|killed]], the Clan's [[General]] [[Vukorah]] sought to avenge those lost by attacking the Mourner's Wail, even without the approval of the Clan's<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" /> [[Krynthia's father|Grand Leader]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 20">[[Bounty Hunters 20|''Bounty Hunters'' 20]]</ref> Vukorah and four soldiers traveled to the [[Smuggler]]s' [[Fuel|Refueling]] [[Space station|Station]] where forces of the Mourner's Wail, led by an [[Unidentified Iktotchi criminal|Iktotchi criminal]] and accompanied by two [[Pyke sentinel]]s, were attempting to load [[spice]] onto a [[Starship|ship]] so it could be taken to their distributors. The group were running late and the [[Iktotchi]] insisted they hurry as the syndicate's leader, [[Khamdek]], was not known for his patience.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" />

Immediately after, Vukorah and her soldiers revealed themselves and killed the individuals transporting the spice and the [[Pyke]]s whilst Vukorah declared that the spice would not arrive to its distributors and that the Clan would be taking the spice. The Iktotchi responded but before they could finish, Vukorah killed them with a shot to the face.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" />

In response to the attack on its spice run, the Mourner's Wail [[Bombing on Caviness IV|bombed]] an [[Unbroken Clan district]] on [[Caviness IV]], which resulted in the deaths of twenty-seven adults and several [[Youngling|children]] being wounded.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" />
