'''Chasmeene''' was a [[planet]] that was punished for failing to meet [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] quotas by the irradiation of vast swaths of its farmland. As a result, it was stricken by famine for years.<ref name="Leia" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] birth in [[19 BBY]]. The events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' commence just after Organa's sixteenth [[birthday]], which places the events of the novel in [[3 BBY]].</ref> [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] undertook a relief mission to the planet and brought [[Foodstuff|food]], [[seed]]s, and agricultural equipment. She instructed [[R2-D2]] to collect scans of the planet to prove its devastation was the Empire's fault. The droid also collected additional data to back up the scans taken, which Organa transferred to several [[datacard]]s.<ref name="Leia" />
