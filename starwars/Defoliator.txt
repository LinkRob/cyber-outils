{{Quote|We test a new weapon, of my own design, which is capable of catastrophic destruction. A weapon which will destroy organic matter, but leave machines unharmed.|Lok Durd, to Count Dooku|audio=LokDurd2.flac|<ref name="DoP" />}}
The '''defoliator''' was a weapon conceived of by the [[Neimoidian]] [[General]] [[Lok Durd]] for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]<ref name="DDT tank" /> as part of the research effort of [[Jedi]]-proof biological weapons.<ref name="Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> Actually developed by [[Pune Zignat]], only for Durd to take credit for it,<ref name="HC 13">{{HelmetCollectionCite|13|Databank A-Z}}</ref> the defoliator was used in the [[Defoliator Deployment Tank|DDT]], a modified [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tank]]. The weapon was specialized to destroy organic life&mdash;such as [[clone trooper]]s&mdash;while preserving machines&mdash;such as [[battle droid]]s&mdash;in combat during the [[Clone Wars]]. The weapon was funded by [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and tested on [[Maridun]], where it was [[Defense of Maridun|destroyed]] by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="DoP" /> The second and final prototype<ref name="HC 13" /> was used by [[General]] [[Grievous]] to [[Battle of Dathomir|wipe out]] the [[Nightsisters]] on [[Dathomir]], save their leader, [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]], and [[Asajj Ventress]], both of whom managed to escape.<ref name="Massacre" />
