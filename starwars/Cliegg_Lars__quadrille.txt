The [[moisture farmer]] [[Cliegg Lars]] formed a '''quadrille''' of dozens of [[moisture farmer]]s to rescue his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Shmi Skywalker Lars]] from a band of [[Tusken Raider]]s who kidnapped her<ref name="Episode II" /> when they passed near the [[Lars homestead|Lars moisture farm]].<ref name="CL 2016" /> Enraged, Lars gathered twenty-nine other farmers to save Shmi, but only four, including Lars, returned after being attacked by the Tuskens, at the cost of Lars' leg.<ref name="Episode II" /> While searching for the Tusken camp, Shmi's son, the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], investigated the place where the massacre of Lars' quadrille took place, as it had been the last place where the Tuskens had been seen.<ref name="CL 2016" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cliegg Lars' quadrille was first mentioned in the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], the second installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]].<ref name="Episode II" />
