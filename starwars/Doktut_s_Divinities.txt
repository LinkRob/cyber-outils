'''Doktut's Divinities''' was a shop owned by [[Twi'lek]] [[Kivo Doktut]] and located on [[The Island]] of a [[Unidentified planet (The Island)|planet]]. The shop sold a large variety of [[hat]]s, [[helmet]]s, and hoods. During the [[Standard month|month]] of [[Life Day]], [[Color|colorful]], festive lights were set up above the shop.<ref name="Castaways" />

==Behind the scenes==
Doktut's Divinities appeared in the [[2021]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Castaways]]''.<ref name="Castaways" />
