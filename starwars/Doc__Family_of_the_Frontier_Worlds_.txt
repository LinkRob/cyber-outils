'''Doc''' was a fictional character that appeared in the [[holodrama]] series ''[[Family of the Frontier Worlds]]'' which had released at least seventeen episodes<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> In the fourteenth episode, Doc treated a wounded [[bantha]] that belonged to the [[Gender|female]] character [[Malina]] and their child [[Suma]]. While cleaning her wound, Doc discovered that the infection was worse then they thought and informed Malina that the bantha would need [[bacta]] within the next [[Standard hour|hour]] or she would not survive the night. Malina then decided to venture out into an ongoing storm to acquire the required bacta; stating that they would not lose another bantha.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Doc's voice appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
