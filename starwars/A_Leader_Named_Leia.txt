'''''A Leader Named Leia''''' is a young-readers [[World of Reading]] book that was released as part of [[Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi|Journey to ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'']]. It was written by [[Jennifer Heddle]] and published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[September 1]], [[2017]].<ref name="Disney" /> It received an [[A Leader Named Leia (audiobook)|audio version]] on [[September 19]], [[2024]].<ref name="Audiobook">[[A Leader Named Leia (audiobook)|''A Leader Named Leia'' audiobook]]</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
''To some she's a princess, to others a general, but to everyone Leia Organa is one of the galaxy's greatest leaders. Learn all about Leia's exciting adventures with A Leader Named Leia.''<ref name="Disney" />
