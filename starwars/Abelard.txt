'''Abelard''' was a [[Planet/Legends|planet]] in the [[Abelard system]] on which the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] had established a [[Unidentified Imperial base (Abelard)|base]] during the [[Rebellion era]]. Sometime after the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[Battle of Abelard|attacked]] the base and managed to steal a stabilized [[ytterbium]] cargo.<ref name="CasusBelli" />
