'''''Darth Vader'' 40''' is the fortieth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]] and [[Federico Blee]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 8]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''SCOURGE OF CORUSCANT! &ndash; A ''DARK DROIDS'' TIE-IN! What greater prize could the DROID SCOURGE imagine than CORUSCANT, an ecumenopolis filled with millions of droids? And what greater TERROR could the people of Coruscant imagine than a horde of scourged droids&hellip;led by LORD VADER himself?''

==Development==
Artist [[Raffaele Ienco]] shared line art for ''Vader'' 40 as a teaser for the issue.<ref name="Raff tweet 1">{{Twitter|Raffaele_Ienco|status/1722139713958494551|[[Raffaele Ienco]]|quote=Darth Vader #40, out today, Nov 8, 2023...|archiveurl=https://archive.is/veuTu}}</ref> The way in which Vader blocks Sidious's [[Force lightning]] was speculated to be an example of the [[Tutaminis]] Force power by fans. Ienco confirmed this was not his intention, yet left the possibility open that it could be retconned into Tutaminis because he was not overly familiar with the subject.<ref name="Raff tweet 2">{{Twitter|Raffaele_Ienco|status/1723839250750148642|[[Raffaele Ienco]]|quote=Maybe, I don't really know. I didn't have that intention when I drew it though. {{C|In response to: "Thank you! So, there are two things, I'll split it up: 1). I've seen it claimed that in the following page, Vader was using the Force technique Tutaminis to absorb/block Sidious' blast of Force Lightning. Was it intended to be this way or it it just the art design/style?"}}|archiveurl=https://archive.is/XIQJF}}</ref>
