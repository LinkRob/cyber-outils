The '''DT-12 heavy blaster pistol''' was a model of a [[heavy blaster pistol]]. 

==Characteristics==
A small looking yet weighty blaster, the DT-12 possessed a hefty power source, giving it a hard-hitting punch.<ref name="Battlefront" /> The DT-12 was capable of being modified, with [[Migs Mayfeld]] utilizing a set of three DT-12's equipped with different [[barrel]]s.<ref name="Prisoner">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 6: The Prisoner}}</ref> The former [[Cloud-Riders|Cloud-Rider]] and [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]] member [[Chussido]] had a DT-12 with a long barrel.<ref name="Sin">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 3: The Sin}}</ref>

==History==
Members of the [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team]] used a DT-12.<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> [[Jezzi]] armed herself with a DT-12 when she evauated herself, [[Bix Caleen]], [[B2EMO]], [[Brasso]], and [[Wilmon Paak]] aboard a [[Breon HOY-39 Dayvan]] in [[Zorby's Western Shiplot]] to a safe place from [[Ferrix]].<ref name="Rix Road">{{Andor|Rix Road (episode)}}</ref> A DT-12 was used by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Greedo]] during his unsuccessful confrontation with the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] at [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]].<ref name="Battlefront" /> It was also the weapon of choice for [[human]] [[mercenary]] [[Migs Mayfeld]], who kept three on his person: two in chest [[holster]]s and a third in a [[automated weapon mounting]] attached to his [[backpack]].<ref name="Prisoner" />
