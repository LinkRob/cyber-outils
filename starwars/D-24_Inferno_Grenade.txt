The '''D-24 Inferno Grenade''' was a type of [[incendiary grenade]] produced by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]]<ref name="Knights of Fate" /> Upon detonation, the D-24 created intense heat that ignited any and all flammable materials within its blast radius. Initially created primarily for firefighters initiating controlled burns to reduce the risk of wildfires, the D-24 was marketed for military applications as well.<ref name="Gadgets" />
