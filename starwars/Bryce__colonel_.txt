'''Bryce''' was a [[human]] who served as a [[colonel]] in the [[Imperial Army|army]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. They accompanied [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and a team of [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtroopers]] to [[Tython]], following a lead regarding the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] base provided by [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]]. Though Bryce was unsure of Aphra's claim, as their [[fleet]]'s scans detected no trace of a base in the area, Vader instructed the colonel to conduct scans of the [[Martyrium of Frozen Tears]]. Aphra, who had intentionally led Vader away from the [[Echo Base|true base]] on [[Hoth]], turned on the Imperial forces. Vader ordered Bryce to withdraw, but the colonel and their snowtroopers were [[Death|killed]] by the [[assassin droid]] [[0-0-0]], who slashed Bryce with the blades mounted on his [[Limb|hand]].<ref name="Aphra 40" />
