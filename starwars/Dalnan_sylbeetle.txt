'''Dalnan sylbeetles''' were a type of [[beetle]] from the [[planet]] [[Dalna]].<ref name="Cataclysm" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Night of Sorrow]] to [[382 BBY]].</ref> during a [[Jedi]] [[Night of Sorrow|attack]] on the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[The Force|Force]] [[religion]] on Dalna, [[Jedi Master]] [[Char-Ryl-Roy]] paused and heard Dalnan sylbeetles chirping in the [[grass]] and [[Plant|weeds]] at the end of a field.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dalnan sylbeetles are heard in [[The High Republic: Cataclysm|''Cataclysm'']], a novel written by [[Lydia Kang]] and published in [[2023]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Cataclysm" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase II">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-cataclysm-excerpt|text=The Jedi Arrive at Dalna in ''The High Republic: Cataclysm'' - Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref>
