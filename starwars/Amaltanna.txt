'''Amaltanna''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="FotL" /> within the [[Amaltanna system]], a [[star system]] in the [[Kanz sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was believed to be far from anything or anyone important, according to its own inhabitants, but nevertheless it became the site of the devastating [[Battle of Amaltanna]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. In the end, the [[Galactic Republic]] won over the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], but the extent of the damage was so severe that its inhabitants were relocated as refugees to worlds such as [[Naboo]] and [[Alderaan]].<ref name="FotL" />
