'''Flechettes''' were needle-like projectiles that were fired from [[Flechette launcher|launchers]] such as the [[FC-1 flechette launcher]] and [[DF-D1 Duo-Flechette Rifle]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Flechettes were first mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="SWSG">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>
