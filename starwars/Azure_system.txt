The '''Azure system''' was located in the [[Azure sector]] of the regions known as the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and [[the Slice]]. It contained the [[planet]] [[Anaxes]], as well as its remains, the [[Anaxes asteroid belt]], which was home to two [[planetoid]]s&mdash;[[PM-1203]] and [[PM-6986]]&mdash;formed from a [[Destruction of Anaxes|cataclysm]] that destroyed the world towards the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the [[Clone Wars]]. In [[19 BBY]], during the war, Anaxes served as the site of a [[Battle of Anaxes|major engagement]] between forces of the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], each of whom desired to claim the planet and its cruciality as a [[Anaxes shipyards|shipyard]] for the Republic.

==Description==
The Azure [[Star system|system]] was located within the [[Azure sector]]<ref name="USWNE" /> in the Coreward edge of the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Battles" /> as well as [[the Slice]],<ref name="The Slice" /> where it lay at grid square R-7 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Coordinates" /> It contained the [[planet]] [[Anaxes]],<ref name="USWNE" /> which served as a crucial [[Galactic Republic]] [[shipyard]] for [[starship]]s,<ref name="Bad Batch">{{TCW|The Bad Batch (episode)}}</ref> as well as the world's remains, which were formed into an [[Anaxes asteroid belt|asteroid belt]] following a [[Destruction of Anaxes|cataclysm]] that took the planet, while also creating at least two [[planetoid]]s&mdash;[[PM-1203]] and [[PM-6986]].<ref name="Dawn" />

Situated along the [[Perlemian Trade Route]] [[super-hyperroute]],<ref name="Battles" /> the Azure system also contained a single [[star]], whose rotational period of the system's astronomical objects was altered following Anaxes's destruction.<ref name="Out of Darkness" /> In addition to the planet's cataclysm, PM-1203 and PM-6986 served as the locations of both [[Fort Anaxes]], a former [[Galactic Republic]] military base, and a [[Separatist stronghold (PM-6986)|stronghold]] previously used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]].<ref name="Dawn" />

==History==
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Battle of Anaxes]] to [[19 BBY]].</ref> in the final [[Standard year|weeks]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the Galactic Republic and Separatist Alliance, the [[Harch]] Separatist [[Admiral]] [[Trench]] had disrupted the Republic's [[Outer Rim Sieges]] campaign<ref name="Battles" /> by launching an [[Battle of Anaxes|offensive]] in an attempt to gain control of the planet Anaxes,<ref name="Bad Batch" /> a Republic stronghold<ref name="Battles" /> and crucial [[Anaxes shipyards|shipyard]] to their [[Republic Navy|naval forces]].<ref name="Bad Batch" /> As a result, Trench managed to claim victory in a series of vital early engagements, which forced the Republic to dispatch [[Jedi General]]s [[Mace Windu]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], and [[Anakin Skywalker]] to intervene in the battle,<ref name="Battles" /> but were unable to repel the Confederacy as Trench gained the upper hand through various tactics.<ref name="Bad Batch" /> However, despite both Trench's and the Confederacy's efforts to claim Anaxes, the Republic was able to defeat and [[Death|kill]] the Separatist admiral and his entire [[droid]] [[Trench's army|army]], as well as prevent Anaxes's destruction at the Harch's hands.<ref name="Unfinished Business">{{TCW|Unfinished Business (episode)}}</ref>

Sometime after Trench's defeat, following the end of the war, Anaxes was destroyed in a cataclysmic event, which led to the formation of a field of [[asteroid]]s and planetoids in the aftermath. Following the planet's destruction, the cataclysm was theorized to have been caused by the malfunction of the Anaxes shipyard's main [[reactor]], while other, more wild explanations, ranged from the utilization of Separatist [[superweapon]]s to [[Jedi]]-engineered planetary collisions. Despite Anaxes's destruction, however, the former Republic military base, Fort Anaxes, alongside a former Separatist stronghold with a [[Separatist holdout (PM-6986)|vestigial force]] of Separatist droids, either of which were located on the planetoids PM-1203 and PM-6986.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Azure system first appeared in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Out of Darkness]],"<ref name="Out of Darkness" /> the seventh episode of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' television series,<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/out-of-darkness-episode-guide|text="Out of Darkness" Episode Guide &#124; Star Wars Rebels}}</ref> which first aired on [[November 10]], [[2014]].<ref name="Insider 156">{{InsiderCite|156|Star Wars Rebels Recounted|''Star Wars Rebels'' Recounted}}</ref> It later received its name in the [[2019]] reference book, ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'', which places it in the Core.<ref name="USWNE" />

The [[2022]] attraction ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser]]'', set between [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''The Last Jedi'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''The Rise of Skywalker'']], placed Anaxes in the Core.<ref name="GalacticStarcruiser">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Axum system]] was introduced in the [[2006]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]'', developed and released by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="Empire at War">''[[Star Wars: Empire at War]]''</ref> In the Legends continuity, the system was first identified in ''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]'', a [[2003]] sourcebook published by [[Wizards of the Coast]] for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Corsucant">''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]''</ref>
