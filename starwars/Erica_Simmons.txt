'''Erica Simmons''' is an American-born actress who played the female [[Nimbanel/Legends|Nimbanel]], [[Tawss Khaa/Legends|Tawss Khaa]], during the cantina scene in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].
[[File:Tawss-khaa.jpg|thumb|Tawss Khaa]]
Erica was also a stand-in for [[Carrie Fisher]] during [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].
