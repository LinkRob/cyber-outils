'''Essie''' was [[Jyn Erso]]'s name for<ref name="Build R2 38">{{BuildR2Cite|38|Droid Directory}}</ref> an [[SE-2 worker droid]] with [[Gender|masculine programming]] who worked on the [[Erso homestead|farm]] of [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]], keeping an [[eye]] on the horizon for any intruders. Despite being a menial [[labor droid]], Essie had a very pronounced loyalty subroutine.<ref name="UVG" /> In [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Capture of Galen Erso]] to [[13 BBY]].</ref> he alerted the [[Erso family]] when he spotted [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic's]] [[ST-149|Imperial shuttle]] approaching the homestead to [[Capture of Galen Erso|capture the Ersos]].<ref name="Rogue One" />
