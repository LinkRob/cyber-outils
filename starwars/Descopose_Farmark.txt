'''Descopose Farmark''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> in the [[Descopose Farmark system|system of the same name]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[pirate]] [[Queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] hid a portion of the spoils from her reign there in a buried vault equipped with a [[antitamper sub-space alarm]]. As of when she began writing the book that would become known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'', the alarm protecting the cache of treasure on Descopose Farmark had not sounded. The archaeologist [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] later obtained the Guide and professed her desire to find the treasure in that location.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
