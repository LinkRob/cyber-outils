The '''central marketplace''' was a district of [[Ro Station]], a [[city]] on [[Pantora]]. A bustling locale, the central marketplace contained numerous businesses. At the beginning of the [[Imperial Era]], a [[Chase on Pantora|fight]] between the [[bounty hunter]] [[Fennec Shand]] and the renegade [[clone commando]] [[Hunter]] over the former's attempt to kidnap the child [[Cloning|clone]] [[Omega]] began in the central marketplace, drawing the attention of [[Pantoran Security]].<ref name="Cornered" />
