'''Amy Ratcliffe''' is a writer and regular contributor to [[StarWars.com]] and ''[[Star Wars Insider]]''. Ratcliffe is the author of the books ''[[Star Wars: Women of the Galaxy]]'' and ''[[The Art of Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge]]''.
