'''CT2''' was the designation of a [[clone trooper]] of a [[Jumpy's unit|unit]] within the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. 

==Biography==
Created on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]],<ref name="Clones" /> CT2 was a [[human]] [[Cloning|clone]]<ref name="Trade Relations" /> of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]]<ref name="Clones" /> designed to serve as a [[clone trooper]] in the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. At some point during the war, CT2, along with [[Clone Lieutenant]] [[Jumpy]] and another clone [[trooper]], accompanied a [[Unidentified Galactic Republic senator (Rylorii Minor)|senator]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] to the [[mining]] planet [[Rylorii Minor]], located in the [[Western Reaches]]. A neutral world during the war, the Republic's presence was to establish a trade agreement with the planet's [[Mayor]] [[Lup]]. Arriving via an [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i]] [[gunship]], the [[senator]] and the [[Jumpy's unit|unit]] of clone troopers met with Lup and his [[Unidentified Separatist|aide]].<ref name="Trade Relations" />

Soon after they began conversing, a cloaked assailant attempted to strike at Lup and his aide with an [[electrostaff]]. As Lieutenant Jumpy pursued the fleeing [[assassin]], CT2 and the other clone stayed behind with the officials. When Jumpy returned, having only been able to acquire the assailant's cloak, he took note that it looked to be made on [[Coruscant]]. Hearing this, Mayor Lup's distrust of the Republic grew more, the Republic senator first offered to have one of the clones act as a [[bodyguard]] for the mayor. Distrusting the clone troopers as well, the senator then agreed to have them wait outside as the trade negotiations took place.<ref name="Trade Relations" />

As the three clones waited in the night as the negotiations went on, the mayor's aide came to offer them some [[water]]. CT2 took note of the aide's uninterest in the agreement before accepting a cup of water. The group was then ambushed; two assailants knocked down CT2 and the other trooper as the third grabbed the aide the took her away on a [[speeder]]. On the ground, CT2 wrestled with the masked assassin while Jumpy went after the aide and the third clone punched the other assailant. The two clones were able to best their attackers and tie up the two up before Jumpy returned with the incapacitated third. Identifying the three assassins as locals, there was enough evidence that it was not a Republic kidnapping attempt and the trade negotiations were able to proceed into an agreement. Leaving the planet, CT2 co[[pilot]]ed the LAAT/i with Jumpy where they apologized for not trusting the lieutenant's judgement.<ref name="Trade Relations" />

==Personality and traits==
CT2 had [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s and tan skin,<ref name="Trade Relations" /> and as a clone of Jango Fett, they stood at 1.83 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="Clones" /> CT2 had a shaven head with a design of two large gray circles and a smaller gray circle in a line extending from their forehead towards their neck. CT2 was able to pilot an [[Low Altitude Assault Transport|LAAT gunship]].<ref name="Trade Relations" />

==Equipment==
CT2 wore white [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] that featured a vertical green stripe on each of the [[armor]]'s shoulder plates and a horizontal green stripe on the [[helmet]].<ref name="Trade Relations" /> As with all clone troopers of the Republic, CT2 was implanted with a [[behavioral modification biochip]] by the [[Kaminoan]] [[Scientist|scientists]] to ensure that they would not resist certain [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|orders]].<ref name="Clones" />
