'''Berrus''' was a [[Captain/Legends|captain]] in service to the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] in [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]]. He was dispatched by [[Galactic Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] to assist [[Galen Marek|Starkiller]]&mdash;the Emperor's newest [[Sith apprentice/Legends|Sith apprentice]]&mdash;during a mission to search for the [[Droid/Legends|droids]] [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]], whom the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] had tasked with safeguarding the [[Death Star plans/Legends|stolen plans]] for the Empire's new [[Planet/Legends|planet]]-destroying [[Superweapon/Legends|superweapon]], the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|Death Star]]. Starkiller's hunt for the droids led him to the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim]] world of [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]]. As the apprentice commenced his mission, Berrus contacted him at various times, offering the apprentice advice on completing his mission. The apprentice ultimately fulfilled his assignment&mdash;with little help from the captain&mdash;when he located the two droids in the [[City/Legends|city]] of [[Mos Eisley/Legends|Mos Eisley]] and placed a [[Homing beacon/Legends|tracking device]] on the [[Millennium Falcon/Legends|starship]] aboard which they escaped.

==Biography==

[[File:GalenStalkerArmor-TFUUSE.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Starkiller, the Sith apprentice whom Berrus was assigned to assist]]
In [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]], Berrus served as a [[Captain/Legends|captain]] in the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] when [[Galactic Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] assigned his [[Sith apprentice/Legends|Sith apprentice]], [[Galen Marek|Starkiller]], the task of locating the [[Droid/Legends|droids]] [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]], who had been charged by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] agents with the [[Death Star plans/Legends|plans]] to the Empire's [[Planet/Legends|planet]]-destroying [[Superweapon/Legends|superweapon]] known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|Death Star]]. Likewise, Berrus was personally tasked by the Emperor to assist Starkiller.<ref name="TFUUSE" />

Starkiller's search led him to the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim]] world of [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]]. When the droids could not be located, the Emperor instructed the apprentice to visit [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] at the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt's]] [[Jabba's Palace/Legends|Tatooine palace]] and use the [[Crime lord/Legends|crime lord's]] [[Spy/Legends|spy]] network to locate the droids. As he began his journey, Starkiller was contacted by Berrus via [[Comlink/Legends|comlink]]. The captain informed Starkiller of his duty to offer assistance should the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] apprentice require it. After the apprentice [[Death/Legends|dealt]] with a group of [[Jawa/Legends|Jawas]], Berrus noted that an immobile [[Sandcrawler/Legends|sandcrawler]] was blocking the quickest path to Jabba's palace. Starkiller had no difficulty in removing the obstacle, [[Telekinesis/Legends|Force-pushing]] it out of his way down into the desert below.<ref name="TFUUSE" />

After reaching the palace and defeating several [[Gamorrean/Legends|Gamorrean]] guards, Starkiller was permitted to come before Jabba. Although the Hutt claimed that his spies would be unable to find two droids among the numerous others on the world, his [[Protocol droid/Legends|protocol droid]] blurted out that a pair of droids matching the specifications of the ones that Starkiller was searching for had been located in the [[City/Legends|city]] of [[Mos Eisley/Legends|Mos Eisley]]. Before the apprentice could respond, Jabba dropped him down into the [[Rancor/Legends|rancor]] pit beneath his throne room. Starkiller fought his way past the rancor and additional guards before Berrus contacted him again. The captain stated that a [[Squadron/Legends|squadron]] of [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]] would be dispatched to capture the droids in Mos Eisley, but Starkiller immediately dismissed the idea, preferring to handle the task himself.<ref name="TFUUSE" />

The Sith apprentice continued his escape from Jabba's palace, and as he approached the [[Hangar/Legends|hangar bay]] containing Jabba's [[Khetanna/Legends|sail barge]] and [[Bantha-II cargo skiff/Legends|desert skiffs]], Berrus tried to warn Starkiller that their communications were being [[Jammer/Legends|jammed]]. However, his transmission only partially came through, the rest being static. Starkiller left the palace on a skiff to Mos Eisley, where he found the two droids escaping onboard the [[YT-1300 light freighter/Legends|starship]] [[Millennium Falcon/Legends|''Millennium Falcon'']] and placed a [[Homing beacon/Legends|tracking device]] on the ''Falcon'' before it fled.<ref name="TFUUSE" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|It appears that you've already discovered the Jawas. Disgusting creatures.|Berrus|<ref name="TFUUSE" />}}
While initially contacting Starkiller, Captain Berrus revealed his disgust for the native Jawas. When Starkiller countermanded the [[Imperial officer/Legends|officer's]] plan to dispatch a squadron of [[Trooper/Legends|troopers]] to capture the droids in Mos Eisley, he nervously affirmed the apprentice's order.<ref name="TFUUSE" />
