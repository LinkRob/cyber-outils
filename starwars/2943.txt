'''2943''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] that served in the [[Trade Federation]] [[Trade Federation Droid Army|Droid Army]]. During the [[Invasion of Naboo|Naboo Crisis]], 2943 was escorting [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and other captives throughout [[Theed]]. 2943 was destroyed by [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], who jumped from a ledge and bisected the [[droid]] in half.<ref name="tpm" />

The B1's designation was written on the back of its [[comlink booster pack]].<ref name="tpm" />

==Behind the scenes==
2943 first appeared in the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]]. While the [[droid]]'s designation can be spotted in film,<ref name="tpm" /> the numbers could not be identified until ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'' gave a translation for B1 numerals.<ref name="Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
