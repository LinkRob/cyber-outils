The '''Firestone Islands''' were an archipelago on the [[planet]] [[Atterra Bravo]]. The terrain mostly consisted of porous ground and towering [[Volcano|volcanic]] columns of [[pumice]]. The outlying islets were uninhabited, while the main island was home to big hydro farms.<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" />
