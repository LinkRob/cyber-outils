{{Quote|The syringe on the platform contains a highly electrolytic serum that will allow you to pass through the shield. However, it is toxic to all but one of you.|Moralo Eval to the tournament contestants|<ref name="The Box">{{TCW|The Box}}</ref>}}
'''Electrolytic serum''' was a substance that when injected into the bloodstream of a particular species, allowed them to pass through an electrified [[ray shield]]. [[Parwan]]s could survive the effects of the serum, but it is toxic to other life forms.

==History==
[[File:DerrownPassesThruShield-TB.png|thumb|left|200px|Derrown passes through the ray shield]]
{{Quote|He's a Parwan. His blood can take the electrolytic serum.|Obi-Wan Kenobi, to the other contestants|<ref name="The Box" />}}
During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Jedi]] discovered a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] plot to kidnap [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] during a celebration on his [[Homeworld|home]] [[planet]] of [[Naboo]]. [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], disguised as [[assassin]] [[Rako Hardeen]], joined forces with [[Cad Bane]] and [[Moralo Eval]], the mastermind of the kidnapping plot. After arriving on [[Serenno]], Bane and Hardeen were invited by [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] to join ten other bounty hunters named [[Sixtat]], [[Embo]], [[Twazzi]], [[Mantu]], [[Jakoli]], [[Kiera Swan]], [[Onca]], [[Bulduga]], [[Derrown]], and [[Sinrich]] in a [[Tournament on Serenno|tournament]] of skill within [[Box|The Box]].<ref name="The Box" />

After two tests were completed, eight surviving competitors were enclosed in a room blocked by electrified [[ray shield]]ed floor and three walls. The fourth wall contained a switch to deactivate the ray shields, hidden behind a small ray shield. A syringe containing a highly concentrated electrolytic serum toxic to all but one of the six remaining contestants stood in the middle of the room. The [[Parwan]] Derrown's physiology allowed him to survive the serum injection in order to pass through the shield relatively unharmed and deactivate the remaining shields.<ref name="The Box" />
