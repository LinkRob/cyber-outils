'''Dig-rigs''' were digging machinery used in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Vernid]]'s [[worm]] [[Farmer|farming]] industry. They were worked by crews including rigsmen, haulers, cranksmen, and light signallers. A number of [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[Desertion|deserted]] [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Major]] [[Soran Keize]], going by "Devon Lhent," found work as a dig-rig worker on Vernid, operating the signalling lights, an attempt to find a new life after leaving the Empire. [[Gender|He]] worked with other crewmembers including hauler [[Klevin]], rigswoman [[Nanchia]], cranksman [[Vi'i'che]], and the boy [[Tyros]]. [[Grahamos]] was another dig-rig worker during that time.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
