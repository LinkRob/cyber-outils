'''Chitin''' was an organic substance included in the anatomy of some [[species]]. The scavenger [[Sarco Plank]] had chitin plates covering the front of his head and his [[Limb|forearms]].<ref name="WOTJ">''[[The Weapon of a Jedi: A Luke Skywalker Adventure]]''</ref> [[Bastak]]s were a species of [[crustacean]] that had chitinous armor.<ref name="Last Shot">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref>
