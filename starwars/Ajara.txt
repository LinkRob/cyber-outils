'''Ajara''' was a [[gas giant]] [[planet]] located in the [[Ajara system]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> within the [[Cademimu sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]], situated in a partially explored region of [[Realspace|space]] off the [[Celanon Spur]]. It was [[orbit]]ed by the<ref name="TROS VD" /> much smaller<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[jungle]] [[moon]] [[Ajan Kloss]], which had two moons itself. Ajara's intense [[magnetic field]] shielded Ajan Kloss from [[cosmic radiation]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ajara was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a [[2019]] reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
