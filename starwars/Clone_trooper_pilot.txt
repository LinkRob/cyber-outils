'''Clone trooper pilots''',<ref name="SWE" /> also known as '''clone pilots''',<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|clone-pilot-odd-ball|Clone Pilot Odd Ball}}</ref> were [[clone trooper]]s that, due to having exceptional reflexes and vision,<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> as well as good spatial awareness,<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> were spotted early and brought into specialized training, with those who made the grade "earning their wings" as [[pilot]]s.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> The [[Cloning|clones]] piloted various [[Starship|ships]]<ref name="CVD" /> and ground vehicles. Over the course of the [[Clone Wars]], pilots were placed within vehicles like the [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]]-family, [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s,<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> [[Clone Z-95 starfighter|Clone Z-95 Headhunters]],<ref name="Darkness">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref> [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter|ARC-170 starfighters]], [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wing starfighters]]<ref name="Episode III" />  and [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber|BTL-B Y-wing bombers]].<ref name="Shadow">{{TCW|Shadow of Malevolence}}</ref> Clone pilots were known for their skills as [[starfighter]] and starship pilots. They were also effective at armed and [[unarmed combat]], survival and military tactics.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Like most clone troopers,<ref name="clonetroopers">{{Databank|clone-troopers|Clone Troopers}}</ref> clone pilots were 1.83 [[meter]]s or 6 [[Foot|feet]] tall.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Overview==
{{Quote|Sir, we're taking heavy fire.''"<br />"''Close the blast shields, lieutenant! Get us under those guns!|Hawk, who served as a clone pilot with the rank of lieutenant, and [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] in a gunship|<ref name="TCW film" />}}
[[File:ATTEDriver-DownfallOfADroid.jpg|thumb|left|250px|An AT-TE Driver]]
[[Clone trooper]] [[pilot]]s were also able to possess [[military rank]]s. [[Hawk]] held the rank of [[Clone Lieutenant]] during the [[Battle of Teth]],<ref name="TCW film" /> [[Jag]] held the rank of [[Clone Captain]],<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> and [[Davijaan|Odd Ball]] held the rank of [[Clone Commander]].<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> Clone pilots who were tasked with controlling an [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]] were known as AT-TE Drivers.<ref name="AT-TE Driver">{{SWCT|set=Troopers Through Time|cardname=AT-TE Driver}}</ref>

Throughout the [[Clone Wars]], pilots wore at least three types of pilot armor. [[Phase I clone trooper armor|Phase I]] pilot armor was mostly the same as their [[trooper]] counterparts, but their helmet lacked a fin. While not all AT-TE Drivers adopted it,<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> some such pilots wore the armor with grey and gold markings.<ref name="AT-TE Driver" /> During the [[Standard day|days]] of [[Phase II clone trooper armor|Phase II armor]], two variants came to exist.<ref name="Darkness" /><ref name="Episode III" /> One variant had identical body armor to the Phase II armor, but had a different helmet.<ref name="CoK Trivia Slide 1">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/carnage-of-krell-concept-art-gallery|text="Carnage of Krell" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> The second variant had a blue bodysuit, and plating covering the torso, as well as minor plating covering the knees and elbows. The helmet was open.<ref name="Episode III" /> Clone pilots served the [[Galactic Empire]] as [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot]]s.<ref name="Dark Lord 2" />
