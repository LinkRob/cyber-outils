An '''Airgun''' was a type of [[airship]] that was used by the rival factions [[Open]] and [[Closed]] during the [[Open-Closed War|Open-Closed war]]. Airguns consisted of a [[gondola]] suspended by [[rope]]s and cords beneath a large buoyant [[airbag]] and were the primiary airship design used by the Open.<ref name="Anakin 2" /> During the [[mission to Carnelion IV]] in [[29 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], a gifted [[Technician|mechanic]], spliced together an airgun from a crashed Open airbag and a scavenged Closed gondola, creating his [[Jedi airgun|own airgun]].<ref name="Anakin 3" />
