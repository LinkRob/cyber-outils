The '''''Arbiter''''' was a name which [[Yrica Quell]], a [[New Republic pilot]] who was working as a mole in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], conjured when trying to remember the name of the Imperial [[Colonel]] [[Madrighast]]'s [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]], the ''[[Unyielding]]'',<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{VictorysPriceDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Arbiter'' was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the third and final installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy which was written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
