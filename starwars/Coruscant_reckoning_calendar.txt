The '''Coruscant reckoning calendar''',<ref name="This week">{{ThisWeek|fjHmSJy3BFM|Star Wars: The High Republic Box Set Reveal, Andor Props, and More!|sw_url=video/star-wars-the-high-republic-box-set-reveal-andor-props-and-more}}</ref> commonly abbreviated '''C.R.C.''', was a calendar system used in [[the galaxy]]. Its epoch was such that 7972.216.4 was the date when [[Maarva Carassi Andor]] died ,<ref name="Trivia 2">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e12-trivia-gallery|text="Rix Road" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> an event which was dated to the [[Standard year|year]] [[5 BBY]] in [[Galactic standard calendar|another calendar]].<ref name="Andor date">{{AndorDate|111}}</ref> Within a given [[millennium]], it was acceptable to indicate a date with only the last three digits of the first number preceded by an apostrophe (e.g. '945 instead of 7945). Within a [[Standard century|century]], the date could be indicated by the last two digits of that number; for instance, [[Exantor Divo]] recorded that he graduated from the [[Hosnian Prime Academy of Law Enforcement]] as part of the "Class of '96 (Hosnian Reckoning)."<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
C.R.C. dating was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]. The calendar dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] to the C.R.C. year 7977;<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files" /> the film itself was released in [[1977]].<ref name="A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref> The calendar first appeared in "[[Rix Road (episode)|Rix Road]]," the twelfth and final episode of the [[Andor Season 1|first season]] of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.<ref name="Rix Road">{{Andor|Rix Road (episode)}}</ref> Maarva Andor's off-screen death<ref name="Daughter of Ferrix">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> was dated to 7972.216.4,<ref name="Trivia 1" /> corresponding to the season's setting in 5 BBY.<ref name="Andor Dates">{{AndorDate|1}}</ref> The ''Andor'' series used the C.R.C. calendar for several reasons, including that its characters would not know of the coming [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Pablo tweet">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1595453224864972802|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=Why use the CRC calendar system in narrative? 1) sometimes you have an event recorded by people who don't know the Battle of Yavin is coming 2) Sometimes you have an event set before the 90s-era '35 year' mark that used to denote the Clone Wars 3) it's just window dressing.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/5XoOD}}</ref>

On his [[Wikipedia:Hive Social|Hive Social]] account, Hidalgo revealed the name "Coruscant Reckoning Calendar" was inspired by the "Shire Reckoning" from ''Lord of the Rings'' lore, and that the calendar's creation had been partially inspired by<ref name="Pablo Hive CRC Name">[[:File:Pablo-Hive-CRC-Name-Origin.png]]</ref> the [[Commerce Treaty of 1647]] mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Sphere of Influence]]."<ref name="Sphere of Influence">{{TCW|Sphere of Influence}}</ref> He also revealed that in ''Scum and Villainy'', the parts of the dating following the decimal points were mostly based on "vibes" without corresponding to something specific, while the dating in ''Andor'' was done with more focus to suggest days within the year and times of day.<ref name="Pablo Hive Vibes">[[:File:Pablo-Hive-CRC-Vibes.png]]</ref> According to Hidalgo, the C.R.C calendar and [['ABY-BBY' dating system]] are not one-to-one, thereby explaining any supposed differences between both.<ref name="Calendar Different">{{Bluesky|infinata.bsky.social|post/3ku4i3fxkbq2b|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2024-06-04|quote=They would all have different start dates, as two are event-based and one is an arbitary calendar. {{C|In response to: "Hi Pabro. I purchased "Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary" yesterday and have enjoyed reading it. Does the fact that the consolation to Rosal [''sic''] is placed at 36 BSI mean that BSI/ASI and BBY/ABY are not just 34 years apart, but have different "Januarys"? Also could this also be said about the C.R.C.?"}}|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/4wbBM}}</ref>
