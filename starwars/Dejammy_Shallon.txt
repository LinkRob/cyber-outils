'''Dejammy Shallon''' was a [[Gender|female]] individual who served as a [[teacher]] and a [[priestess]] on [[D'janis IV]]. She was quoted comparing [[water]] to [[the Force]] in ''[[Collected Poems, Prayers, and Meditations on the Force]]'', a compilation of material relating to the Force compiled by the [[Disciples of the Whills|Disciple of the Whills]] [[Kozem Pel]].<ref name="GotW" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dejammy Shallon was first mentioned as part of a chapter quote in the novel [[Guardians of the Whills (novel)|''Guardians of the Whills'']], which was written by [[Greg Rucka]]<ref name="GotW" /> and released on [[May 2]], [[2017]].<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/new-novels-rebel-rising-and-guardians-of-the-whills-will-explore-rogue-one-backstories|text=New Novels ''Rebel Rising'' and ''Guardians of the Whills'' Will Explore ''Rogue One'' Backstories}}</ref>
