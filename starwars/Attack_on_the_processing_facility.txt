In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> members of the [[Jedi Order]] and the [[Aricho's resistance force|resistance force]] of the [[planet]] [[Aricho]] '''attacked''' a [[Nihil]] [[glaka root]] [[processing facility]] to liberate workers, mainly [[Youngling|children]], who were being forced to work there by Nihil [[Pirate|marauders]] attempting to occupy the planet. The [[Jedi]], including [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Yacek Sparkburn]] and [[Lyssa Votz]] as well as [[Padawan]] [[Imri Cantaros]], had been helping the [[Arichoan]]s, who were led by [[Jaffrey Knoice]], resist the Nihil forces after Aricho was shuttered behind the [[Stormwall]] in the Nihil's [[Occlusion Zone]]. [[Protocol droid]] [[J-6]] also arrived with a team of [[K-series droid|K droids]] and Jedi Knight [[Vernestra Rwoh]], the latter helping to capture Nihil leader [[Kara Xoo]] when she attempted to flee.<ref name="DtS" />
