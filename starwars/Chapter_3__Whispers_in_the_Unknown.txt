"'''Chapter 3: Whispers in the Unknown'''" is the third episode of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'' and was released on [[April 13]], [[2026]] alongside "[[Chapter 4: Pride and Vengeance]]". Its title was revealed on [[March 17]], 2026 by the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' Twitter account.<ref name="Twitter" />

==Official description==
''As Maul's plans come together, Devon considers her options.''

==Plot summary==
===Tea with Maul===
The episode opens with two crescent moons hanging above the night sky of [[Janix]]. In the lower levels of the city, fugitive [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Devon Izara]] emerges from a her broken cylindrical cell, which she shattered using [[the Force]]. [[Looti Vario]] reacts with shock and amazement, asking how she did it. Izara's actions attract the attention of [[Rook Kast]] and several [[Mandalorian]]s. Izara walks past them into a tunnel that leads to a chamber where [[Maul]] is squatting while brewing [[Cassius tea]]. He asks Izara if she has started to see things from a different perspective and invites her to share tea with him.

Maul says he needs no introduction and asks Izara her name. Izara is reluctant to disclose her identity to Maul so he asks how her arm is faring. When Izara replies that her arm is fine, Maul says he senses that she regards him as the enemy and asks if she would like him to play that role. After examining her arm, Maul remarks that she is healing well. Sensing that Izara still distrusts him, Maul invites her to look deeper. Izara replies that she regards him as a murderer. 

Maul responds that he does what he needs to survive and says that she could relate given the predicament facing the Jedi following the [[Great Jedi Purge]]. When Izara says they are nothing alike, Maul begs to differ. He reminds Izara that she was of the [[Jedi Order]] which served the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]]. Maul says that institutions are fragile and recounts how he believed he had a purpose until [[Darth Sidious|his master]] cast him aside. Maul explains that he had to find his own way and that [[Savage Opress|his brother]] and [[Talzin|mother]] were murdered by the same enemy who destroyed her Order. He suggests that they are more alike than she thinks. 

While Maul is speaking, Izara eyes a red [[lightsaber]] and grabs it with the Force. She demands to be allowed to leave, prompting Maul to switch off the lights. He says that she can have her way but warns that she won't like it.

===Police tactics===
At the [[Janix Civil Defense HQ]], a [[Tactical Defense Droid]] informs [[Captain]] [[Brander Lawson]] that the police have conducted a sweep of Sectors 5 to 8, and will do Sectors 9 to 12 next. A blonde [[Gender|male]] officer named [[Reb]] suggests the [[Shadow Collective]] fugitives may have left the city but Lawson disagrees and orders that police continue their search. 

[[2B0T]] ("Two Boots") returns to duty after recharging and asks whether Lawson has obtained new intelligence through his "deviant" contacts. He takes notes of pictures of Izara and Vario on the computer screen. Lawson sidesteps his question and counters that they are targeting Maul, a crime lord who has links with several syndicates including Vario's crime syndicate. Lawson says they have to compile information on the routes and drop off locations of these syndicates. Seeing his master is tired and stressed, Two Boots volunteers to check out the spaceport for Maul's associates. 

===Mind games===
Back at Maul's lair, Izara is surrounded by darkness and uses her stolen red lightsaber for illumination. In the darkness, Maul taunts her about her fear. When Izara counters that she is not foolish enough to fight him, Maul describes her as untested and says that she was promised a future that was taken from her too early. Maul tells Izara that he can relate to her training extensively only to be denied a future she was promised. 

Izara finds the door locked. While Maul continues his monologue about her despair and frustration, Izara searches for other exits. Maul senses her potential and asks if [[Eeko-Dio Daki|her master]] would be impressed if she killed him. Maul continues menacing Izara in the darkened room. Meanwhile, Kast visits Vario's cell. Vario tells her that he had nothing to do with Izara's escape. Kast is not interested and asks about the location of the next [[Pyke]] shipment. 

===Raid and ambush===
Meanwhile, Two Boots and Reb travel in a [[landspeeder]] to an area where the next Pyke shipment is expected to arrive. They exit their vehicle and head into a warehouse where they stumble upon a group of [[Pyke Syndicate]] enforcers interrogating two of Vario's [[smuggler]]s about a lost shipment, shooting one to send a message to Vario while restraining the last one. Before they can torture him, the two officers attack the enforcers, using the boxes and crates as hiding places to shoot and duck. They take out two or three of the Pykes and detain the two Pykes and smuggler. Reb orders the Pykes to open the crate, which contains some glowing material. 

[[Scorn]], [[Icarus]] and Kast [[Attack on a Pyke Syndicate shipment|attack]] them with a [[Missile launcher|rocket launcher]] from upstairs, scattering the group and allowing the remaining Pykes to flee. Reb is wounded during the gunfight and sends Two Boots after the Shadow Collective attackers. He uses his mechanical body to climb onto the upstairs deck. Kast manages to delay him with a second blast from her rocket launcher, allowing the group to escape in a waiting shuttle. 

===Juggling parenting and work===
Elsewhere, Captain Lawson meets his son [[Rylee Lawson]], who is about to join a game at a stadium. Rylee is pleased to see his father, who encourages him before he joins his teammates in the match. Lawson finds that a cloaked reptilian alien is following him. The alien turns out to be [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]], who says he wants to be speak with him. Daki claims to be Izara's uncle. When Lawson asks about her whereabouts, Daki says he had come to ask about her whereabouts. Lawson says that the matter is an ongoing investigation.

While Lawson waves to his son, Daki says that Izara was an innocent bystander caught in the wrong place at the wrong time by an [[Maul|extremely dangerous individual]]. When Lawson asks if he knows anymore information about Maul, Daki warns him to tread carefully and that he is something he has never dealt with before. When Lawson asks why Maul is interested in his niece, Daki says it is a mystery. Lawson expresses frustration about the situation. Before Daki leaves, he empathises with the difficulty that Lawson faces as a lawman and suggests the [[Galactic Empire]] could help. 

Lawson says he wants to avoid Imperial attention and asks if Daki is no fan of the Empire. Their conversation is interrupted by Lawson's [[comlink]] ringing. Lawson is contacted by another police officer who tells him about a [[Code 93]] situation at the docks. When Lawson turns around, Daki has disappeared. Rylee scores another point and the crowd cheers. Lawson takes leave. When Rylee looks at the balcony, he is saddened to see his father has left for work again. 

===Spurning Maul's offer===
Back in Maul's lair, Maul offers to train Izara as his apprentice but says he needs her help against a common enemy. When Izara asks if it is the Empire, Maul says that the Jedi were once revered protectors of the galaxy who have been smeared as traitors. Maul says that this smear must be hurting her, as well as the daily grind of living as a fugitive. Maul offers to help train Izara to deal with the Empire's [[Inquisitorius]], who hunt Jedi. Izara responds that she would rather take her chances. She [[Force jump]]s out of the underground pit and climbs towards the surface.

===No Imperial entanglements===
After Lawson arrives at the docks, Two Boots informs him that Reb was injured and confirms Lawson's observation that the Shadow Collective was targeting the Pykes. Two Boots says he doesn’t know the Shadow Collective's motives for targeting the Pykes but reports that their vehicle was heading towards [[Sector 17-K]]. Lawson and Two Boots head towards Section 17-K in their [[airspeeder]]. During the journey, Two Boots proposes alerting the Empire. 

When Lawson insists on handling the situation himself, Two Boots disagrees and reminds him that in the past similar situations warranted Republic intervention. He explains that the Republic would have assigned a [[Jedi Knight]] to deal with the situation. Lawson replies that the Empire is different from the Republic. Two Boots asks if his distrust of the Empire is rooted in rational reasoning or if his judgement is clouded by his former wife being an Imperial. Lawson responds that once the Empire intervenes, they never leave. He says that he has seen that the Empire intervenes and ruin lives. Two Boots tries to argue back but Lawson’s orders him to drop the matter. The droid apologizes and says it was not his intention to supersede his orders. 

===Setting a trap===
Meanwhile, Vario complains about not being fed when Kast visits his cell. She leads him to a gathering area where she explains that the Pykes have made contact. Mandalorians and the two [[Zabrak]] Scorn and Icarus are also present. When Vario grumbles again, he is threatened by [[Spybot]]. Kast says that she will dictate to him what he needs to say to the Pykes. 

At the [[Pyke Palace]] on [[Oba Diah]], the Pyke Syndicate's leader [[Marg Krim]] receives a [[hologram]] transmission from Vario. After Krim chastises him for his late response, Vario apologizes and blames the disruption of the shipment on Janix to rogue lieutenants of [[Nico Deemis]]. He claims that these rogue elements and their former boss have been dealt with and no longer pose a threat to their syndicates. Vario offers to make amends by delivering the lost payments to Krim plus the value of the destroyed Janix cargo. When Vario professes his loyalty, Krim warns him that if this is a trap he will hunt him down and feed him to his [[nexu]] horde. Vario agrees before terminating the transmission. 

===Workplace politics===
Back at the Janix Civil Defense headquarters, Chief [[Klyce]] tells Lawson that Two Boots came to her proposing they contact the Empire for help. When she tells him to get the droid under control, Lawson says that Two Boots has been under a lot of pressure lately. Klyce is reluctant to get the Empire involved but is concerned about the threat posed by Maul to public safety.

Lawson says they have patrols working round the hour searching for Maul and vows to get the situation under control. When Lawson says he will get Two Boots in line, Klyce says that she has reassigned the droid to desk duty. A weary Lawson sits down facing his desk. Opposite his desk, a sullen Two Boots rearranges tapes and covers a picture showing him and Lawson standing together.

===Duel and flight===
Meanwhile, Izara climbs out of the pit onto a walkway that leads towards a sunlit entrance. She finds her path blocked by Maul, who quips that she was foolish to think he would let her escape. Izara says she is not afraid and draws her lightsaber. Maul counters that she is weak and draws his lightsaber. As he parries with her, he tells her that she has no future and hope. The two clash and Maul chides her for squandering her Jedi talents.

He knocks her to her feet but she gets back up and they continue the duel. Maul goads her by telling Izara that she has no purpose and that her life is in ruins. Izara howls in rage and manages to clear a path on the walkway, but instead of running away she attacks Maul and Force pushes him away. As she runs towards the light, Maul uses the Force to snatch her stolen lightsaber out of hands, telling her that he needs it and addressing her by name. Maul watches with a smile as Izara runs out into the light.
