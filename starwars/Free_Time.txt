"'''Free Time'''" is a [[roleplaying game]] adventure written by [[Gary Haynes]] that appears in ''[[The Politics of Contraband]]'', published by [[West End Games]] in [[September]] [[1992]].

The story is set in the [[New Republic era]], but before the [[Thrawn campaign]]: between [[5 ABY/Legends|5 ABY]] and [[9 ABY/Legends|9 ABY]].
