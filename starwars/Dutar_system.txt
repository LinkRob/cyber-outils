The '''Dutar system''' was located in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="AcolyteVD" /> and [[the Interior]]<ref name="The Interior" /> in grid square L-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. It was situated between the [[Bar'leth system|Bar'leth]] and [[Wukkar system]]s on the [[Corellian Run]] [[Super-hyperroute|hyperlane]]<ref name="AcolyteVD" /> and contained the [[moons of Dutar]].<ref name="Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dutar [[Star system|system]] was first mentioned in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s<ref name="AcolyteVD" /> [[June 17]], [[2025]]<ref name="Phase III">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-lucasfilm-publishing-panel|text=Phase III of ''The High Republic'' And All The Lucasfilm Publishing News from ''Star Wars'' Celebration Japan 2025 – UPDATE}}</ref> reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' on a [[The galaxy|galaxy]] map authored by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Christopher Ibbitt]].<ref name="AcolyteVD" /> The [[October 14]], 2025 update to<ref name="SubstackUpdate">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref> "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> on [[June 24]] of the same year&mdash;with both works being authored by Fry<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>&mdash;subsequently identified the system by name.<ref name="Appendix" />
