The '''"Breaker" heavy hydrospanner''', known simply as the '''Breaker''', was a heavy, long-handled [[hydrospanner]] manufactured by [[Regallis Engineering]]. It was nearly one [[meter]] long and was built out of heavy reinforced alloys. Its head was forged and hydraulic-assisted, and could be adjusted to fit most fasteners. It had a long handle that allowed for extra leverage on fasteners that were stuck, and its sturdy build prevented stripping, warping, and breaking. At least one Breaker was kept in the toolboxes of most field [[Technician|mechanics]]. The heavy hydrospanner could also be used as a [[melee weapon]] and cost 250 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market.<ref name="Fully Operational" />
