"'''For a Few Kilotons More'''" is an adventure for [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition|''Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game'', 2nd ed.]] It was written by [[Michael Allen Horne]] and published as part of the book [[Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)|''Twin Stars of Kira'']] in [[1993]]. "For a Few Kilotons More" is the fifth of ten adventures in the book, all of which deal with the [[Star system/Legends|systems]] along the [[Enarc Run/Legends|Enarc Run]] and the [[Harrin Trade Corridor]] in the [[Binary star/Legends|Twin Stars]] of [[Kira IV|Kira]] region of [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]]. The adventure takes place during the [[New Republic era]].<ref name="TSoK 4">{{WEGCite|story=For a Few Kilotons More|book=Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)}}, p. 4.</ref>

==Plot summary==
The adventure sends the player characters to seek their fortunes on the planet [[Krann]], where the [[Mon Tondievz, Ltd.]] mining company has experienced sabotage that threatens its output of [[Nova crystal/Legends|nova crystals]] and, more broadly, the stability of the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic's]] currency.<ref name="TSoK 39">{{WEGCite|story=For a Few Kilotons More|book=Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)}}, p. 39.</ref>

The group disembarks from a [[Shuttle/Legends|shuttle]] at the planet's main spaceport. There, they meet with a representative of Mon Tondievz named [[Vanel (Human)|Vanel]]<ref name="TSoK 39" /> at the [[Wages of Phy'r]] cantina.<ref name="TSoK 40 42">{{WEGCite|story=For a Few Kilotons More|book=Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)}}, pp. 40&ndash;42.</ref> She explains that her company is currently in competition with several newcomers, including operations owned by the [[House of Tagge/Legends|House of Tagge]] and the [[Hutt Jeweler's Guild]]. She takes the newcomers to a warehouse, where they speak to [[Quarlo I'Shibix]] via [[Hologram/Legends|hologram]]. He tells them about the troubles Mon Tondievz is facing, blames it on the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]], and offers them a reward<ref name="TSoK 42">{{WEGCite|story=For a Few Kilotons More|book=Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)}}, p. 42.</ref> if they can safely escort a shipment of [[Nova crystal/Legends|nova crystals]] to the [[Tagge camp]] for shuttling offworld.<ref name="TSoK 44">{{WEGCite|story=For a Few Kilotons More|book=Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)}}, p. 44.</ref>

Vanel escorts the group to the Mon Tondievz mining port.<ref name="TSoK 44" /> They board [[Speeder truck/Legends|speeder trucks]] and head off into the jungle. They stumbled upon the [[Cathedral of Shade]], a crumbling temple of the native [[Kluuzot]] species, and began investigating it.<ref name="TSoK 45">{{WEGCite|story=For a Few Kilotons More|book=Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)}}, p. 45.</ref> There they find a Kluuzot under the effects of [[eldratz]] spice. A group of thugs then attacks the party only to be interrupted again by several more drug-crazed Kluuzot. The offworlders escape, but find themselves off course and with a speeder truck in need of repairs. Inside the coolant system, they discover not just nova crystals, but a [[Thermal detonator/Legends|thermal detonator]] set to explode when they reach the Tagge camp. They also discover an additional cargo: flasks of eldratz, ostensibly the real source of Mon Tondievz's revenue. An explosion in the distance alerts them that the other truck of miners must have been destroyed, alerting them to their own fate had they not found the explosives.<ref name="TSoK 46">{{WEGCite|story=For a Few Kilotons More|book=Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)}}, p. 46.</ref>

The group continues to the Tagge camp, where they encounter Vanel and Quarlo, now known to be part of the eldratz ring. There they confront Vanel and her [[Assassin droid/Legends|assassin droid]], an [[Eradicator E8U]].<ref name="TSoK 48">{{WEGCite|story=For a Few Kilotons More|book=Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)}}, p. 48.</ref> In the ensuing battle, the player characters defeat Quarlo and Vanel. A New Republic [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate/Legends|Nebulon-B frigate]], the [[Salamander (frigate)|''Salamander'']], comes on the scene a few hours later. The player characters, having defeated the person who hired them (albeit as patsies) are left unpaid but with the possibility of salvaging and selling off nova crystals.<ref name="TSoK 49">{{WEGCite|story=For a Few Kilotons More|book=Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)}}, p. 49.</ref>
