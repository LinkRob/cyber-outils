Located on [[Level 5000]],<ref name="Trivia Gallery" /> the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]'s '''central power distribution grid'''<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/heroes-on-both-sides-episode-guide|text="Heroes on Both Sides" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> was comprised of massive [[Power generator|generator]] towers that powered the entire [[ecumenopolis]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], a series of conflicts fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the breakaway [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], it was [[Bombing of Coruscant's central power distribution grid|bombed]] on the orders of [[Count]] [[Dooku]], the Separatist [[Head of State]], to sabotage [[Confederate–Republic peace initiative|peace talks]] between the two factions, resulting in large fires and a planetwide blackout on Coruscant. After receiving confirmation that the Separatist were responsible for the attack, the Republic Senate rejected their peace offer.<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" /> While Coruscant's power grid was eventually restored, [[Coruscant mandatory blackouts|mandatory blackouts]] were put in place to help conserve energy and some districts were rarely ever provided with electricity.<ref name="Pursuit of Peace">{{TCW|Pursuit of Peace}}</ref>

[[Mich Matt]] was [[technician]] for Coruscant's central power generator before the Empire kidnapped from his job, since he didn't want to leave his family but the Empire took him anyway for a [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|secret project]].<ref name="In the Name of the Rebellion" />
