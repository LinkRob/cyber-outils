The '''Big One'''<ref name="BMF 39">{{BuildFalconCite|39|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> was the accretion shell of the [[exogorth]] [[Sy-O]], which was mistaken for an [[asteroid]] located in the [[Hoth asteroid field|Hoth asteroid belt]], itself a gathering of exogorths in a [[Clew]].<ref name="FACPOV">{{StoryCite|story=This Is No Cave|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> Following the [[Battle of Hoth]], [[Han Solo]] flew the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' into a large crater within the asteroid in order to hide from Imperial pursuers, unaware that the "crater" he had flown into was actually Sy-O's gullet.<ref name="Episode 5" />
