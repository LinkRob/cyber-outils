A '''Councilor''' was a member of one of the [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]] or the Forty [[Great Families]]<ref name="Greater Good" /> of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] who handled the [[Chiss family|family's]] local affairs. Councilors were the second-lowest known political rank within a Ruling Family, above [[Aristocra]] and below [[Patriel]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

Councilors served in a judicial capacity for non-criminal disputes in a special setting called a [[White Judgment Seat]]. The Councilors and Patriels of various families on a given Ascendancy [[planet]] worked together to improve the standard of living and prestige of that planet. Councilors could request some of the [[Patrol ship|patrol boats]] managed by their family's Patriel for the same planet, to use for specific purposes.<ref name="Greater Good" />

Informally, Councilors and higher ranking [[Chiss family]] officials were often referred to as [[Aristocra]], suggesting that that title indicated a minimum rank in common usage.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

[[Xodlak'uvi'vil]] was a Councilor of the [[Xodlak family]] in the [[Redhill province]] of the Chiss Ascendancy [[planet]] of [[Celwis]]. Councilor Lakuviv's ambitions, [[Jixtus's plan|manipulated]] by [[Haplif]] of the [[Agbui]], [[Hoxim incident|nearly led]] to a civil war in the Chiss Ascendancy. Lakuviv staged a coup against Patriel [[Xodlak'oo'nifis]] and impersonated Lakooni to declare a [[family emergency]], which he used to try to seize the [[Hoxim|Agbui mining world]].<ref name="Greater Good" />
