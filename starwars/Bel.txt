'''Bel''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] under [[Jedi General]]s [[Nahdar Vebb]] and his former [[Jedi Master|master]] [[Kit Fisto]].

==Biography==
Bel, along with [[Niner]], was assigned by [[Clone Commander]] [[Fil]] to look after the [[Starship|ships]] while they went on their [[Mission to Grievous's castle|mission]] to [[Vassek]]'s third [[moon]]. Bel and Niner were then informed by [[R6-H5|R6]] that a [[Soulless One|ship]] matching the description of [[General]] [[Grievous]]'s [[starfighter]] was approaching the [[Grievous's fortress|fortress]]. A short while into the mission Bel received call that the [[commander]], the [[Jedi]] and the remaining [[Clone trooper|clones]] were trapped inside the fortress. They and Niner attempted to contact the Republic fleet at [[Bestine IV|Bestine]], but were [[Death|killed]] by Grievous's [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s, who fired a [[Missile launcher|rocket launcher]] at the ship the two clones were in.<ref name="Lair" />
