The '''DF.12 Com Turret''', also known as a '''Resistance Turret''', was a ground-based turret emplacement that served as a reliable first line of defense. It was used at [[Resistance]] encampments and temporary air bases.<ref name="D23">{{SW|url=news/d23-doug-chiang-interview-star-wars-themed-land-disney-parks|text=D23 2017: Doug Chiang Talks Designing ''Star Wars''-Themed Land for Disney Parks}} - Text from a display of the model</ref> One such turret could be found at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]], specifically at the Resistance encampment at the [[Black Spire Ruins|ancient ruins]].<ref name="Rise">{{GEAttr|Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance|''Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance''}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DF12_Com_Turret_model.jpg|thumb|250px|A model of the turret from D23 Expo]]
The turret was first seen as part of the model of the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] theme park area. It serves as the entrance to the queue leading to the ''[[Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance]]'' attraction.<ref name="EW Rise">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/movies/2019/02/28/star-wars-actors-will-appear-in-disneys-rise-of-the-resistance-ride|text=Star Wars actors bring their characters to life in Disney theme parks' Rise of the Resistance ride|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2019-02-27|archivedate=20190914091306}}</ref>
