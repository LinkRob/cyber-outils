The '''Eriaduans''' were a cultural group of [[Sentience|sentients]] who lived on the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]],<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> a world whose population included [[Human]]s and [[Rodian]]s.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> Game hunting was important to their culture, which was also known for personal vendettas and political intrigue.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[alien]] populations of Eriadu were the target of discrimination by [[Imperial Military]] authorities.<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>
