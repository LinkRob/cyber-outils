'''Founder's Day''' was a holiday on which celebrants would dine on a type of [[bird]]<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> and hang [[color]]ed lanterns.<ref name="The Cold">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Cold]]''</ref> An [[Unidentified Imperial officer (Akiva)|Imperial officer]] on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]] grinned as though he had been granted first bite of the bird on Founder's [[Standard day|day]] when he cornered the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jas Emari]], the ex-[[loyalty officer]] [[Sinjir Rath Velus]] and the [[junk dealer]] [[Temmin Wexley]] in an alley shortly before the [[Rebellion on Akiva]].<ref name="Aftermath" />

The [[Graf family]] had Founder's Day lanterns aboard their starship<ref name="The Cold" /> the ''[[Whisper Bird]]'' around [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Wild Space">In ''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Steal]]'', [[Ezra Bridger]] is an infant. Since Ezra was born on [[Empire Day]] ([[19 BBY]]), ''[[Star Wars: Adventures in Wild Space]]'' must be set around [[18 BBY]].</ref> [[Milo Graf]] used the lanterns after the ship crashed on an ice moon.<ref name="The Cold" />

==Behind the scenes==
Founder's Day was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel ''[[Lando Calrissian and the Flamewind of Oseon]]'', which was written by [[L. Neil Smith]] and released in [[1983]]. The holiday was [[canon]]ized when it was mentioned in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] and released in [[2015]].
