A '''farm''' was a piece of land generally used for the purposes of agriculture. On [[Tatooine]], [[moisture farm]]ers extracted moisture from the [[atmosphere]] of the [[desert]] [[planet]].<ref name="Lars farm DB">{{Databank|lars-moisture-farm|Lars Moisture Farm}}</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], agricultural trade was cut off between many [[star system]]s, and during the [[Imperial Era]], confiscations of farmland by the [[Galactic Empire]] caused shortages and price rises.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>
