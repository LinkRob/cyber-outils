"'''A Day's Duty'''" is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] written by [[Martin Fisher]], drawn by [[Eva Widermann]] and edited by [[Gunther Nickel]]. The story was published in the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 15|fifteenth issue]] of the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]] series by [[Egmont]] UK Ltd, which was released on [[February 24]], [[2016]].

According to Fisher, the story takes place between the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episodes "[[Stealth Strike]]" and "[[The Future of the Force]]."<ref name="Twit">{{Twitter|username=tronprogram2|url=/status/702946976934002688|name=[[Martin Fisher]]|date=2016-02-25|quote=@LelalMekha between Stealth Strike and Future of the Force|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/aOsBm|archivefile=File:MartinFisher-ADaysDuty-Twitter.png}} {{C|In response to: @tronprogram2 And now, my usual question, if you don't mind: where in the timeline? :-)}}</ref>

==Plot summary==
The crew of the ''[[Ghost]]'' leave for a supply run to [[Crandel]], leaving [[Ezra Bridger]] on the [[Phoenix Home|''Phoenix Home'']] [[frigate]] to learn from [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]] how the larger [[Starship|ships]] in the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|rebel fleet]] operate. He is joined by [[Mizel Pomdak]] who rushes into the lesson late, tripping and falling on the way in. Pomdak complains to Sato that he couldn't get enough sleep due to being ordered to work through a number of technical manuals, Sato in turn threatens to have Pomdak kicked off the ship before Bridger intervenes, defusing the situation and introducing himself to Pomdak. Pomdak has heard of Bridger and is impressed by him. The lesson begins with the [[engine]]s and [[reactor]] systems of a [[CR90 corvette]] and how they work together.

Later, the two students work together on the ship and Bridger asks Pomdak what led him to fight against the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]]. Bridger stowed away to get there, his family was blind to the lies of the Empire but he saw through them and wanted to do something right. But Pomdak feels he hasn't done anything right since arriving at the rebel fleet. He is disheartened but glad Bridger is there, citing the [[Jedi]] as his inspiration. Bridger explained that he isn't a Jedi yet and that even Jedi aren't perfect and can't solve every problem alone. Pomdak's circuitry was faulty and Bridger pointed out his mistake before helping and encouraging the other boy.

Later on the two have finished altering a defense battery, Sato arrives and commends Bridger for his work but Pomdak's work was lacking. Sato wants to see Pomdak's potential unlocked. A friendly [[A-wing starfighter|A-wing]] jumps out of [[hyperspace]] from a patrol near [[Rinn]] after being attacked, quickly followed by a number of Imperial ships, which quickly launch [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. [[Phoenix Squadron]] moves to intercept. Bridger mans a station and lets Sato know that a battery is down, Pomdak is sent to fix it, he wasn't confident, but Sato reassures Pomdak that he believed in him. [[Phoenix Three]] was destroyed in the battle. Pomdak thought he has fixed the battery but it blows up in his face, he tries again realizing that Ezra had previously made a mistake; he fixes it and the tide of the battle turned in their favor.

''Ghost'' arrives and together the fleet jumps to hyperspace, successfully escaping the battle. Bridger tells the the rest of the [[Spectres]] about his day and Pomdak before Sato arrives and commends both Bridger and Pomdak. Pomdak reveals that Bridger made a mistake and Bridger gratefully returns to being the student to [[Kanan Jarrus|Kanan Jarrus']] teacher.
