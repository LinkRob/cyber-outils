A '''datachip''', also called a '''data chip''', was a device used for storing data.<ref name="Uprising" /> At some point, a [[Orla Jareni|Jedi]] hid a number of datachips on the [[planet]] [[Lenahra]]. Long afterward, [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] found the datachips.<ref name="Brotherhood">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']]</ref> During the [[Cold War]], [[Lor San Tekka]] was in possession of a data chip that contained part of a [[map to Skywalker|map]] to the location of the [[Exile (punishment)|self-exiled]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="100 Objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref>
