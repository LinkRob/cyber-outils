A '''poster''' was put up in [[Cad Bane]] and [[Moralo Eval]]'s cell in the [[Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center]],<ref name="Deception">{{TCW|Deception}}</ref> in the [[cockpit]] of a [[Unidentified Turtle Tanker|Turtle tanker]] commandeered by [[Savage Opress]] on [[Stobar]],<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref> on an [[air taxi]] driven by [[Jay Igno]] on [[Coruscant]], and on air taxis in [[Ro Station]] on [[Pantora]].<ref name="Cornered">{{TBB|Cornered}}</ref> It showed the [[planet]] [[Aleen]] and two [[arbozoic tree]]s,<ref name="Orders" /> a [[crystal]]line [[species]] of [[tree]]s native to the planet.<ref name="Mercy">{{TCW|Mercy Mission (episode)}}</ref> The [[Aurebesh]] caption ended in "eena", presumably "[[Aleena]],"<ref name="Orders" /> one of the planet's native species.<ref name="Mercy" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
The [[Painting|poster]] first appears in [[Cad Bane]] and [[Moralo Eval]]'s cell in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Deception]]," but most of it is obscured by Eval's body.<ref name="Deception" /> It also appears in the [[cockpit]] of a [[Unidentified Turtle Tanker|Turtle tanker]] commandeered by [[Savage Opress]] in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Brothers (episode)|Brothers]]." It is best seen in the shot where the door to the cockpit slams shut after Savage throws out the [[Unidentified Sakiyan cargo hauler|Sakiyan cargo hauler]]. However, the lighting is so dim that the poster is completely illegible, and you can only the silhouette of the motif.<ref name="Brothers" /> It is seen in the cockpit again in "[[Revenge (episode)|Revenge]]," under the same lighting conditions.<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref> The poster is seen clearly for the first time on [[Jay Igno]]'s [[air taxi]] in the episode "[[Orders (episode)|Orders]]."<ref name="Orders" /> It reappears on air taxis on [[Pantora]] in the ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' episode "[[Cornered]]," although it is only seen from a distance.<ref name="Cornered" />
