The '''GB-III''' was a hovering [[Droid/Legends|droid]] used by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] to protect the corridors and walkways of the [[Death Star/Legends|Death Star]] in [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]].<ref name="FamiMaga">{{BookCite|author=|container=Star Wars|title=Family Computer Magazine|number=1987 #22|publisher=Tokuma Shoten|date=December 4, 1987|issn=|link=https://archive.org/details/zines_famimaga_1987_22}}</ref> They were vaguely spherical and could move incredibly quickly.<ref name="sw87" />

[[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], just prior to [[Rescue of Princess Leia/Legends|rescuing]] Princess [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]] in [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]], encountered these droids while exploring the Death Star.<ref name="sw87" />

==Behind the scenes==
The GB-III appeared in the 1987 [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] [[Star Wars (1987 video game)|''Star Wars'' game]].<ref name="sw87" /> Their name was revealed in the [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese language]] guide of the game serialized in Family Computer Magazine.<ref name="FamiMaga" />
