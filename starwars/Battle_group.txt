A '''battle group''', also referred to as an '''assault group''', was a formation in several militaries through [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]]. In the context of a [[navy]], it was also known as a '''fleet group'''. Fleet groups saw use in the naval forces of the [[Galactic Republic]], [[New Republic]], [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].

==Land forces==
A battle group could be used to define a complement of [[army]] units. Within the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], battle groups referred to ground units that were supplemented and transported by [[sub-capital ship]]s. The [[61st Mobile Infantry]] [[Company]] was one such battle group that was headquartered on the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]'', and supported by the [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunship]] ''[[Apailana's Promise]]''. The 61st Mobile [[Infantry]] was one of many such battle groups within the [[Alliance Army]].<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref>

==Naval forces==
===Overview===
Conversely, a battle group in<ref name="Downfall" /> naval terminology was also referred to as a [[fleet]] group<ref name="HC 45">{{HelmetCollectionCite|45|Databank A-Z}}</ref> or assault group<ref name="Star Wars 8">[[Star Wars (2020) 8|''Star Wars'' (2020) 8]]</ref> and was a military organizational unit.<ref name="Downfall" />

===CIS Navy===
The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy]] deployed battle groups against the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]], which were one type of military organizational unit.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

===Republic Navy===
Battle groups were deployed by the [[Republic Navy]] during the Clone Wars against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], with two such units being the [[Falleen battle group|battle group]] located at [[Falleen]]<ref name="Downfall" /> and the [[Even Piell's fleet group|battle group]] led by [[Jedi General]] [[Even Piell]].<ref name="HC 45" /> In the Republic Navy, a [[carrier battle group]] were a type of specialized battle group that was centered around a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], and was considered lethal against Separatist naval forces.<ref name="Rise" /> A [[captain]] in the Republic Navy could serve as the [[commander]] of a battle group.<ref name="HC 45" />

The Falleen battle group was destroyed during the [[Battle of Falleen]] and shortly prior to the [[Battle of Bothawui]], [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] reported the loss of the battle group to [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]. Skywalker then led his [[Skywalker's fleet|forces]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Separatist fleet]] of [[General]] [[Grievous]] in the [[Battle of Bothawui]].<ref name="Downfall" />

===Imperial Navy===
Inheriting the assets and heritage of the Republic Navy,<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> the [[Imperial Navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] also utilized battle groups within its [[Order of Battle]], although of varying sizes and roles. On the smaller scale, a [[Ilum battle group|battle group]] that was composed of a [[Gladiator-class Star Destroyer|''Gladiator''-class Star Destroyer]] and several [[frigate]]s and [[gunboat]]s served as a [[blockade]] force in the [[Ilum system]] to defend the combined [[Imperial Military]] [[Imperial Garrison (Ilum)|garrison]] there early in the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Nexus">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref> Another [[Cymoon system battle group|battle group]] composed of multiple [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] defended the [[Cymoon system]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Forged">''[[Forged in Battle]]''</ref> [[Battlegroup Echelon]] was also active during the Imperial Era,<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> as was [[Unidentified battle group|another battle group]] towards the end of the war.<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>

On the larger scale of battle groups, a [[Hunter Fleet|battle group]] led by [[Commander]] [[Ellian Zahra]] during the Galactic Civil War consisted of a [[Hunter Fleet's secondary strike force|secondary strike force]] as well as three ''Imperial II''-class [[Star Destroyer]]s and fourteen [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruisers]].<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> Additionally, a [[Telos battle group|battle group]] was located on [[Telos]] and operated at least two Star Destroyers,<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> while the [[Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Assertor]]'' was the [[flagship]] of its own [[Assertor battle group|battle group]] that consisted of dozens of vessels including [[dreadnought]]s, Star Destroyers, [[cruiser]]s, and support vessels. The sister ship [[Wrath (Assertor-class)|''Wrath'']] also maintained its own similar [[Wrath battle group|battle group]].<ref name="Lead">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref>

===New Republic Navy===
The [[New Republic Defense Fleet]], successor to the Rebel [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]], also utilized battle groups during the Galactic Civil War. One such battle group was the [[Barma Battle Group]], led by [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] from the [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class cruiser]] ''[[Lodestar]]''.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> Another such unit was [[Talus Group]], which was led by a CR90 [[corvette]], as well as the [[Temperance battle group|battle group]] of the [[MC75 Star Cruiser]] ''[[Temperance]]'', which included at least four [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]]s, at least five CR90 corvettes, and at least seven [[GR-75 medium transport]]s.<ref name="Squadrons" />

===First Order Navy===
The [[First Order Navy]], successor to the Imperial Navy decades later, also utilized battle groups within its organizational unit hierarchy. The [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] was the [[flagship]] of its own [[Steadfast battlegroup|battle group]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war against]] the [[Resistance]].<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Two large battle groups were deployed on for the [[Operation on Coruscant]], but were stopped.<ref name="RiseAndFall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Battle groups were first mentioned in "[[Downfall of a Droid]]" from the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="Downfall" />
