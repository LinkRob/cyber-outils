'''Bail bondsman''', or simply '''bondsman''', was a position held by an individual that brokered [[Bounty|bounties]].<ref name="Sisters" />

A [[Devaronian]] [[Devaronian bail bondsman|bail bondsman]] operated out of the [[Coruscant Underworld|lower levels]] of [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The intermediary offered jobs to [[bounty hunter]]s and paid out upon their completion. [[Asajj Ventress]] accepted the bounty posting on the fugitive [[Padawan]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], who was framed for the [[bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar]],<ref name="Catch Jedi">{{TCW|To Catch a Jedi}}</ref> from this bondsman.<ref name="Sisters" /> Later captured by Ventress, the rogue [[Jedi]] protested her, who was an infamous [[Clone Wars|war]] criminal at the [[time]], that she could not possibly simply deliver her to the [[Jedi Order]]. In responce, Ventress said that she could pass Ahsoka to a bondsman.<ref name="Catch Jedi" />
