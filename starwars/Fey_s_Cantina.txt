'''Fey's Cantina'''<ref name="NewCantina">{{EA|subdomain=forums|url=blog/swgoh-game-info-hub-en/upcoming-cantina-update--more/13363839|text=Upcoming Cantina Update + More}}</ref> was a [[cantina]] and entertainment venue owned by the [[Twi'lek]] [[Fey Zara]]. She was assisted by the [[protocol droid]] [[B7-N5]]. The original cantina hosted a series of [[holotable]]s over at least three rooms upon which patrons could play games based on the pivotal conflicts of [[The galaxy|galactic]] history and collect and upgrade [[Hologram|holographic]] versions of famous individuals and [[starship]]s active in these conflicts. Patrons included Master<ref name="GoH" /> [[Shar Seff]],<ref name="NewCantina" /> [[Doctor]] [[M'ak Nar]], [[Adjunct]] [[Bam Teel]], [[Vryssk]], [[Garudda]] the [[Hutt]] and the [[Jawa]] [[scavenger]]<ref name="GoH" /> [[Rillik (Jawa)|Rillik]].<ref name="NewCantina" /> At some point, the cantina closed for some time for large scale renovations which, among others, reduced the numbers of tables and moved them all into one room.<ref name="GoH" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Hey there, we just reopened after renovations. How do you like the new look?|Voice line of Fey Zara greeting the player after the April 2026 cantina update|<ref name="GoH" />|audio=WeJustReopenedAfterRenovations-SWGoH.mp3}}
This cantina serves as the setting of the [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'', developed by [[EA Capital Games]]<ref name="GoH" /> and first released on [[November 24]], [[2015]].<ref name="Holotable Master">{{SW|url=news/how-to-become-a-master-of-the-holotables-in-star-wars-galaxy-of-heroes|text=How to Become a Master of the Holotables in ''Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes''|nolive=1}}</ref> The cantina characters were first named in "Version Update [[May 23|5/23]]/[[2018]]",<ref name="EA-Version Update 5/23/2018">{{EA|subdomain=forums.galaxy-of-heroes.starwars|url=discussion/170303/version-update-5-23-2018|text=Version Update 5/23/2018}}</ref> after which they gave quests to the player.<ref name="Quests Summary">{{EA|subdomain=forums.galaxy-of-heroes.starwars|url=discussion/170302/quests-summary-from-a-cg-developer|text=Quests Summary from a CG Developer|archivedate=20190917193924|nolive=1}}</ref> On [[April 27]], [[2026]], the cantina was completely replaced and Vryssk and Garudda were removed and Fey Zara, B7-N5, Shar Seff, B7-N5 and the newly-named Rillik received updated models.<ref name="NewCantina" /> The unnamed patrons were also largely replaced.<ref name="GoH" />
