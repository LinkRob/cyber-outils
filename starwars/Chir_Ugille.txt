'''Chir Ugille''' was the [[author]] of the study ''[[Life under the Empire, Peace under the New Republic]]'', which covered the topic of cultures that had been institutionalized under the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Rise" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] strongly recommended Ugille's study and called it groundbreaking in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> own [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chir Ugille was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
