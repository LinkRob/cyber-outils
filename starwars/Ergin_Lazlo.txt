'''Ergin Lazlo''' was a [[Pantoran]] [[scientist]] who was employed by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. Lazlo created a [[Ergin Lazlo's device|dangerous experimental device]] and, after a test didn't bring the desired results, went missing. Subsequently the Separatists gave out a [[bounty]] on Lazlo while the Republic sent out the [[special forces]] [[Clone Force 99]] to [[Hunt for Ergin Lazlo|find]] Lazlo and his device. During their search, Clone Force 99 came into conflict with the rogue deserted [[clone trooper]]s of [[Cole's squad]] who were also trying to find the scientists.<ref name="HSBB RA" />
