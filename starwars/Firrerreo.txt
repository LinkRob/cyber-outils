The '''Firrerreo''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="Brotherhood" /> from<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Firrerre system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Firrerre system]]</ref> The [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Connis Dav]], who was active shortly after the [[First Battle of Geonosis|beginning]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="Brotherhood" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="SWT">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> was a Firrerreo.<ref name="Brotherhood" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Firrerreo was a deep cut species suggested by the Story Group team&mdash;it might have even been Pablo. I was looking for deep cuts to diversify the Order and I basically ran out so I asked for suggestions.|Mike Chen|<ref name="Email">{{EmailCite|email=File:MikeChenEmail2.png|creator=[[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]]|date=2023-12-13}}</ref>}}
The Firrerreo appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2022]] novel [[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']], written by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]].<ref name="Brotherhood" /> While drafting ''Brotherhood'', Chen sought to include a diverse range of species in the [[Jedi Order]]. At the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]]'s suggestion, a Firrerreo was included in the novel as a deep cut from the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Email" /> In Legends, the [[Firrerreo/Legends|Firrerreo]] were a [[Near-Human]] [[Species/Legends|species]] from<ref name="TEGtAS">[[The Essential Guide to Alien Species (real-world book)|''The Essential Guide to Alien Species'']]</ref> the [[Planet/Legends|planet]]<ref name="The Crystal Star">''[[The Crystal Star]]''</ref> [[Firrerre/Legends|Firrerre]].<ref name="TEGtAS" /> They first appeared in the [[1994]] novel ''[[The Crystal Star]]'', written by [[Vonda N. McIntyre]],<ref name="The Crystal Star" /> and were first pictured in the [[1998]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and illustrated by [[Brandon McKinney]] and [[Scott Kolins]].<ref name="TEGtPaM">''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]''</ref>
