'''Echo Support''' was the [[callsign]] assigned to the designated support unit of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Echo Squadron]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], a [[Gender|female]] [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|U-wing]] [[Echo Support (Fostar Haven)|pilot]] using this callsign participated in a [[Battle at Fostar Haven|battle]] at [[Fostar Haven]] against [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces.<ref name="Squadrons" />
