'''Corellian Personal Defense''' was a [[Corellian]] company. It manufactured the [[Stun Pad]] [[weapon]] attachment, a model of [[shock pulse emitter]] for ranged weapons.<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />

==Behind the scenes==
Corellian Personal Defense was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']],<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> a [[2017]] supplement for the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="A Fistful of Credits">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/2/2/a-fistful-of-credits|text=A Fistful of Credits}}</ref> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, and it was first mentioned in the "[[Outlaw Battle Armor]]" feature of [[West End Games]]' [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 3|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 3]], which was published in [[August]] [[1994]]. The feature was written by [[Philip Tobin]].<ref name="SWAJ3">{{JournalCite|3|Outlaw Battle Armor}}</ref>
