'''Clare Grant''' (born [[August 23]], [[1979]]<ref name="SWYT 27-08-2020" />) is an American actress. She voiced [[Latts Razzi]] in the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' and [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Princess Leia]] in the audio drama ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Gambit]]''.
