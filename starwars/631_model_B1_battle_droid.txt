The '''631 model B1 battle droid''' was a model of [[B1-series battle droid]] used by the [[Separatist Droid Army]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. They were a more cheaply made [[droid]] than the standard B1.<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|56|Starship Fact File}}</ref> Like other B1s,<ref name="Duel of the Droids" /> they were created by the [[Geonosian]]s,<ref name="Battle Droid" /> had [[Gender|masculine programming]] with [[Color|black]] [[photoreceptor]]s,<ref name="Duel of the Droids" /> [[bone]]-white plating,<ref name="battle droid" /> a [[comlink booster pack]],<ref name="Duel of the Droids" /> and stood 1.93 [[meter]]s in height.<ref name="Battle Droid" /> However, 631s were more clumsy<ref name="Duel of the Droids" /> and intellectually underpowered compared to other B1s,<ref name="BMF" /> who regarded them as inferior.<ref name="Duel of the Droids" />

In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> 631 units were stationed on [[Skytop Station]], a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[listening post]] located on the [[Ruusan 2|Ruusan moon]].<ref name="Duel of the Droids" /> Despite the corrosive [[atmosphere]] causing issues with the 631s' systems, the station's [[computer]]s gave the 631s many menial tasks that the inferior droids were able to perform.<ref name="BMF" /> One 631 was tasked with carrying an item upstairs without dropping it, but no sooner had the droid turned around than he immediately tripped and dropped the item.<ref name="Duel of the Droids" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 631 first appeared in "[[Duel of the Droids]]," the seventh episode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]],<ref name="Duel of the Droids" /> which originally aired on [[November 14]], [[2008]].<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/duel-of-the-droids-episode-guide|text="Duel of the Droids" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> Their full name of 631 model B1 battle droid was first used in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 56|fifty-sixth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine series,<ref name="BMF" /> in a section written by [[Mark Newbold]],<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> published around [[January 27]], [[2016]].<ref name="date">{{BMFdates|56}}</ref>
