According to rumors about [[Sophros|Planet X]], a mysterious [[planet]] many individuals believed to be a myth, '''cloud angels''' were [[Sentience|sentient]] beings who could grant any wish and were found on the planet.<ref name="Quest" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> while on the planet [[Batuu]], the [[pilot]] [[Sky Graf]] told [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] about cloud angels and other rumors they had heard regarding Planet X. However, the [[hyperspace]] [[prospector]] [[Dass Leffbruk]], who had previously been to the planet, refuted the rumors.<ref name="Quest" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cloud angels were mentioned in the [[2023]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Quest" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref>
