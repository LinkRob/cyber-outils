A '''bodyguard droid''' was a form of [[droid]] that acted as a [[bodyguard]]. [[Holowan Mechanicals]] produced the fearsome [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]], and these bodyguard droids saw use under the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> [[XM-G3]] was a former bodyguard droid who joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Lina Graf's crew|crew]] of [[Commander]] [[Lina Graf]].<ref name="TFVC 1" />
