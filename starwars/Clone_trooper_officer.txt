'''Clone trooper officers''',<ref name="EA" /> also known as '''Clone Officers''',<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> were [[clone trooper]]s who served as [[Republic officer|officers]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]], a [[The galaxy|pan-galactic]] conflict between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> [[Cloning|Clones]] serving in the [[Republic Navy]] were known as [[clone navigation officer]]s.<ref name="Storm" />

Clone trooper officers had a number of ranks, with some clones being trained for a higher rank, while others were promoted to it. The highest rank given to clones was normally that of [[Clone Commander]]. These [[commander]]s led [[regiment]]s of sixteen [[Company|companies]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

==Overview==
===Description===
{{Quote|So if you're a captain, and I'm a Jedi, then technically I outrank you, right?''"<br />"''In my book, experience outranks everything.|[[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Rex]]|<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref>}}
[[File:Vaughn-Rex-Jesse.png|thumb|left|300px|Clone trooper officers, such as Clone Captains, Clone Commanders, Clone Lieutenant, or Clone Sergeants, held positions of authority in the Republic Military.]]
[[Clone trooper]] [[officer]]s,<ref name="EA" /> also referred to as [[Cloning|Clone]] Officers,<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> were clone [[trooper]]s who served as military officers in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]],<ref name="Battlefront II" /> the [[Republic Navy|Galactic Republic Navy]], and medical units in the [[Republic Military]].<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence">{{TCW|Shadow of Malevolence|Shadow of ''Malevolence''}}</ref> When foregoing [[clone trooper armor]], clone officers used a [[Color|dark blue]] [[Republic military uniforms|uniform]] also seen<ref name="Rise of Malevolence">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> on [[clone navigation officer]]s, who crewed<ref name="Destroy Malevolence">{{TCW|Destroy Malevolence|Destroy ''Malevolence''}}</ref> or commanded [[Galactic Republic]] [[starship]]s<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> and aided superior [[Republic officer]]s.<ref name="TCW film" />

[[Clone medical officer]]s wore a white version of the [[Navy|naval]] clone uniforms, and were present at medical [[space station]]s and commanded [[hospital ship]]s.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" /> Eventually, medical officer clones had a different uniform, with red stripes and insignia.<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref>

===Ranks and duties===
====Grand Army of the Republic====
{{Quote|I thought we were bred for battle, not running ''errands''.''"<br />"''Hey, we were bred to follow ''orders'', Pulsar. You got a problem with that?''"<br />"''No sir, no problem.''"<br />"''That's what I thought, now ''move''.|[[Pulsar (clone trooper)|Pulsar]] and [[Trap]]|<ref name="Brain Invaders">{{TCW|Brain Invaders}}</ref>}}
[[File:JediCoronetBriefing.png|thumb|right|300px|The Grand Army of the Republic was commanded by clone trooper officers, who in turn served under Jedi officers.]]
Multiple [[military rank]]s were used by clone trooper officers, each with different or additional responsibilities and powers. In the Grand Army of the Republic, a [[Clone Lieutenant]] served as the [[Platoon leader|leader]] of a [[platoon]] and its four [[Clone Sergeant]]s. In turn, a [[Clone Captain]] commanded a [[company]] of troopers, which consisted of four platoons.<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> Additionally, Clone Captains could also serve as the first-in-command of a [[legion]]'s [[commanding officer]].<ref name="Darkness on Umbara">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref>

Above Clone Captains was the military rank of [[Clone Commander]]. Clone troopers with that rank could lead [[battalion]]s, [[regiment]]s, or legions within the Grand Army. Above Clone Commander was the high rank of [[Clone Marshal Commander]], who had the capability of leading an entire [[corps]].<ref name="CL 2016" /> [[Special forces]], specifically [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s, could attain the rank of [[Clone Commander|Clone ARC Commander]].<ref name="Blitz">{{SWCT|set=Rank & File|cardname=Clone ARC Commander Blitz}}</ref> [[Clone major]]s and [[Clone Corporal|Clone corporals]] also served within the Republic Army.<ref name="Complete Vehicles">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref>

====Republic Navy====
{{Main|Clone navigation officer}}
{{Quote|We've entered the atmosphere. Fire retro-rockets!''"<br />"''We've lost lateral control. We're gonna break apart&mdash;|Clone navigation officers|<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref>}}
[[File:TribunalBridgeCrew-VaD.png|thumb|left|300px|Republic warships were crewed by clone naval officers.]]
The Republic Navy's clone officers used the military ranks of Clone Captain and Clone Commander. A Clone Captain could serve onboard a [[Star Destroyer]], in consort with a Clone Commander.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness">{{TCW|Cloak of Darkness}}</ref> Additionally, Clone Captains could also serve as the commanding officers of [[sub-capital ship]]s including a [[Pelta-class Medical Frigate|''Pelta''-class Medical Frigate]]<ref name="R2 Come Home">{{TCW|R2 Come Home}}</ref> or a [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|''Consular''-class c70 cruiser]].<ref name="Ambush" />
