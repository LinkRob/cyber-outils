'''Apple Brook''', born '''Anita Burman''', ([[January 20]], [[1930s|1931]] - [[January 6]], [[2023]])<ref name="Obituary: Apple Brook" /> was a British actress who portrayed a neighbor in ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''. The role was her last credited on-screen appearance, as part of a career in stage and screen acting that began in 1950 and continued into her 80s. She also worked at times as a comedian.

Among her many co-stars can be counted fellow ''Star Wars'' alum [[Domhnall Gleeson]], [[Jason Isaacs]] and [[Fiona Shaw]] in ''Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix;'' [[Brian Blessed]], [[Harry Fielder]] and others in the long-running British series ''Z-Cars''; and [[Sandeep Mohan]] and [[Tina Simmons]] in the series ''Cuckoo''.
