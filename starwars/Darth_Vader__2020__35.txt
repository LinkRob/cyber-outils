'''''Darth Vader'' 35''' is the thirty-fifth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]] and [[Federico Blee]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[June 14]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''VADER UNBOUND! The DARK LORD OF THE SITH has called upon the galaxy's greatest archaeologist, DOCTOR APHRA, for help. He's lost control of THE FORCE, and only APHRA knows what can tame his path of destruction&hellip;But will an archaic KYBERITE weapon only lead to more devastation?''
