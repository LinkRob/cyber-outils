The '''Eastern Mesra Plateau''' was the eastern portion of the [[Great Mesra Plateau]] in the [[Desert Valley]] on [[Tatooine]], a [[desert]] [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]. It was located west of the [[city]] of [[Mos Eisley]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Hutt Clan]] operated a [[Hutt Bunkhouse|bunkhouse]] out of the area..<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Eastern Mesra Plateau first appeared in the [[canon]] continuity in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It is identified in the game by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] communications about the player's last seen location while they have a wanted level. As such the specific extent of the region is difficult to determine.<ref name="Outlaws" />
