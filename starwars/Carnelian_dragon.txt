'''Carnelian dragons''' were a type of [[Diet|carnivorous]] [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[Dragon (creature)|dragon]]<ref name="Creatures of SW pt. 2" /> [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Carnelion IV]]. They had four [[Limb|legs]], eight [[Color|blue]] [[eye]]s, red and green skin  and an aggressive behavior towards other organisms.<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin IV" /> In [[29 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Obi-Wan and Anakin 4|''Obi-Wan and Anakin'' 4]] to [[29 BBY]].</ref> during a [[mission to Carnelion IV|mission]] to investigate a [[distress signal]] on Carnelion IV, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Grecker]], a resident of the planet, encountered three Carnelian dragons in the [[Celadon Sea]]&mdash;an [[Water|icy]] [[mountain]] range filled with [[poison]]ous mist that was toxic to [[human]]s. Kenobi drew his [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's second lightsaber|lightsaber]], and, under distress, Grecker fired a [[flare]], only for it to catch the attention of the dragons further. A bystander, [[Sera]], noticed their distress and tried to help. While helping Grecker into a safe building, he threatened Sera at [[Blaster|gunpoint]] to close the door and leave Kenobi for [[Death|dead]], but Sera shot him instead, ultimately saving the [[Jedi]]. Sera then exclaimed to Kenobi that<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin IV" /> she<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin V">[[Obi-Wan and Anakin 5|''Obi-Wan and Anakin'' 5]]</ref> saved him from the beasts.<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin IV" />

==Behind the scenes==
Carnelian dragons first appeared, albeit unidentified, in the [[Obi-Wan and Anakin 4|fourth issue]] of the [[2016]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan and Anakin]]''. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Marco Checchetto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin IV" /> on [[April 20]], 2016.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/57733/obi-wan_and_anakin_2016_4|text=Obi-Wan and Anakin (2016) #4}}</ref> The dragons were later named in an article published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[September 29]] of the same year.<ref name="Creatures of SW pt. 2" />
