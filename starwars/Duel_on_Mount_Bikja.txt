A '''duel''' took place<ref name="Temple Peak 4" /> in [[241 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> atop [[Mount Bikja]] on the [[planet]] [[Alaris Prime]]. After having ascended the [[mountain]] and entered the [[Yallow Shrine|shrine]] of a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] cult known as the [[Yallow Fellowship]], the [[Zeltron]] [[Padawan]] [[Klias Teradine]] encountered a [[vitalicron]] that corrupted his mind. In a possessed state, he lashed out against fellow [[Jedi]] student [[Ty Yorrick|Tylera Yorrick]], who was forced to [[Death|kill]] her friend.<ref name="Temple Peak 4" />
