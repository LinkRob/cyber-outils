'''Bright Suns Morning''' was a breakfast dish served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Batuu]]'s [[Black Spire Outpost]]. It was made with three [[cheese]]s, [[egg]]s, [[sausage]], and potato, along with a small [[Mustafarian Lava Roll]]. A version for [[youngling]]s called [[Bright Suns Youngling Breakfast]] was also available.<ref name="DB7" />

==Behind the scenes==
This dish is found in the real world Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]. The menu's description originally read, "Three-cheese egg bite, pork sausage, purple potato hash, mini Mustafarian Lava Roll."<ref name="Menu WDW">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=dining/hollywood-studios/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/breakfast|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Breakfast Menu}}</ref> Since its debut, the dish has been renamed to Bright Suns Breakfast Platter, the roll has been renamed to Mustafarian Cinnamon Roll,<ref name="Menu DL">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/breakfast|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Breakfast Menu}}</ref> and breakfast is no longer served at the Disney's Hollywood Studios location.<ref name="DB7 at WDW">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=dining/hollywood-studios/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo|text=Disney's Hollywood Studios: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo}}</ref>
