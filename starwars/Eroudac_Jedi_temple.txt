A '''Jedi temple''' of the [[Jedi Order]] was located on<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Eroudac]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> It was built into the side of a [[mountain]] and was where the [[human]] [[Jedi Initiate|Jeid youngling]] [[Jamil Sollis]] grew up until being recalled to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> the [[Core Worlds]] planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> Coruscant.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />
