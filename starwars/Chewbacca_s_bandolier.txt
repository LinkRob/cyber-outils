'''Chewbacca's bandolier''' was the [[bandolier]] worn by the [[Wookiee]], [[Chewbacca]]. It was often used for carrying equipment or ammunition which could be attached onto the strap for easy access. Made of [[Leather|animal hide]], the pouch was large enough to carry [[Chewbacca's bowcaster]] when it was dismantled. It was worn by the Wookiee though many adventures beginning in the [[Clone Wars]] continuing though the [[Galactic Civil War]] and the [[First Order-Resistance War]].<ref name="100 Objects" /> As a Wookiee, covered in fur, [[Chewbacca]] wore nothing but his bandolier.<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
