{{Quote|If [''Myths & Fables''] is filled with the stories that might have put Luke and Leia to bed at night, the ''Dark Legends'' book is the collection of spooky tales and ghost stories that would have kept them awake at night, all set in the world of ''Star Wars''.|Disney Imagineer Scott Trowbridge|<ref name="D23" />}}
'''''Dark Legends''''' is a [[canon]] short story collection written by [[George Mann]] and illustrated by [[Grant Griffin]]. It is a follow-up to the [[2019]] book ''[[Myths & Fables]]'', and it was first published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[July 28]], [[2020]].

A [[Wikipedia:Target Corporation|Target]]-exclusive edition containing three new stories was released on [[August 30]], [[2020]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Read on if you dare! This unique in-world collection hallows the spooky tales and ghost stories that would have kept young Luke and Leia up at night. Created by George Mann and Grant Griffin&mdash;the same team behind the stunning ''Star Wars: Myths & Fables''&mdash;these six [''sic''] frightful fables have been carefully woven from the expansive fabric that is the ''Star Wars'' galaxy (including the thrilling landscape from Galaxy's Edge at Disneyland and Disney World), and beautifully painted in a lush illustrative style that feels intergalactic yet innately archetypal and timeless.''
