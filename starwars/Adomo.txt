'''Adomo''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[fathier]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> who was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[0 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> They took part in the [[Canto Cup]] [[Fathier racing|fathier race]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> that took place in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> and was held in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. By the time of the race, Adomo at 295 [[centimeter]]s tall and weighed 901 [[kilogram]]s, had [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]] and black [[eye]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}

==Behind the scenes==
Adomo appeared in a hologram in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They are one of the fathiers that can randomly feature in the optional Canto Fathier Racing activity. In most races, the six fathiers are assigned a random [[jockey]] and a random symbol. As such, this article does not assume one pairing or symbol color to be [[canon]]ical.<ref name="Outlaws" />
