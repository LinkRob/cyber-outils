The '''Coxixian Prayer for the Departed''' was a prayer<ref name="GotW" /> from<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=Coxixian Prayer for the Departed|system=[[Coxixia system]]}}</ref> the [[Coxixia system]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> that was included in ''[[Collected Poems, Prayers, and Meditations on the Force]]'', a compilation of writings on [[the Force]] edited by the [[Disciples of the Whills|Disciple of the Whills]] [[Kozem Pel]].<ref name="GotW" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Coxixian Prayer for the Departed appeared as a chapter quote in the novel [[Guardians of the Whills (novel)|''Guardians of the Whills'']], which was written by [[Greg Rucka]]<ref name="GotW" /> and released on [[May 2]], [[2017]].<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/new-novels-rebel-rising-and-guardians-of-the-whills-will-explore-rogue-one-backstories|text=New Novels ''Rebel Rising'' and ''Guardians of the Whills'' Will Explore ''Rogue One'' Backstories}}</ref>
