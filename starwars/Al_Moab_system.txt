The '''Al'Moab system''' was a [[Star system/Legends|star system]] that consisted of four [[Planet/Legends|planets]] [[Orbit/Legends|orbiting]] a [[Dwarf star (Al'Moab)|dwarf star]]. Although populated, the Al'Moab system was largely ignored by the [[galactic community]]. Its main inhabited planet was [[Al'Moab III]], a [[Desert/Legends|desert]] world that hosted mostly [[Human/Legends|Humans]].<ref name="AA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Al'Moab system has only been mentioned in ''[[L'Arche d'Alliance]]'' ("The Ark of Covenant"), a scenario published in the French RPG magazine [[Dragon Radieux 17|''Dragon Radieux'' #17]].<ref name="AA" />
