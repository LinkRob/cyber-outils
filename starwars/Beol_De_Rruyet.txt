'''Beol De'Rruyet''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who was the founder of [[De'Rruyet Industries]], operating from the [[planet]] [[Midarr]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], De'Rruyet acquired a [[Path engine]], an ancient [[Nihil]] [[hyperdrive]] that De'Rruyet claimed he was able to replicate, revolutionizing [[lightspeed]] travel. Due to the resulting effect on the economy that such technology would make, [[Domina Tagge]] of the [[House of Tagge]] wished to prevent De'Rruyet from releasing the device, recruiting the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] to investigate the Path engine and verify De'Rruyet's claims.

However, De'Rruyet and his company were unable to create a functioning replica of the Path engine; despite his lack of success, De'Rruyet intended to [[De'Rruyet Industries confluence|unveil]] the device and give the impression that it worked. He was unaware of the danger it posed if it was activated, but made plans to run away with his [[Currency|money]] in the event that the Path engine did not live up to his claims. On the [[Standard day|day]] before the expo was set to occur, De'Rruyet's [[De'Rruyet Center|headquarters]] was attacked by [[General]] [[Vukorah]] of the [[Unbroken Clan]], who wished to acquire the Path engine.

==Biography==
===Revolutionary claims===
{{Quote|Beol De'Rruyet claims this artifact holds the key to a long lost, incredibly fast method of ''lightspeed'' travel&hellip;and he's found a way to replicate it. If that's true, it could ''completely change'' the galactic economy&hellip;or ''end the war'' once and for all.|Domina Tagge|<ref name="Aphra 6">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 6|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 6]]</ref>}}
During the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[human]] [[Gender|male]] Beol<ref name="Aphra 7" /> was the founder of the company [[De'Rruyet Industries]], based out of [[De'Rruyet Center]]<ref name="Aphra 9" /> on the [[planet]]<ref name="Aphra 8">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 8|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 8]]</ref> [[Midarr]].<ref name="Aphra 9" /> De'Rruyet and his company were a rival of the [[Tagge Corporation]], led by [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]].<ref name="Aphra 6" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra: The Engine Job]]'' to [[3 ABY]].</ref> De'Rruyet, on a clandestine [[Tase Somer's black market network|network]] for the purchase and sale of rare artifacts, discovered a [[Path engine]], an ancient<ref name="Aphra 9" /> [[hyperdrive]] used by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization that allowed them to access unconventional [[hyperspace route]]s known as [[Path]]s.<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> De'Rruyet met with the [[smuggler]] [[Remy (smuggler)|Remy]],<ref name="Aphra 7" /> who had found the device within a wrecked Nihil [[starship]] on the planet [[Dol'har Hyde]],<ref name="Aphra 8" /> on the planet [[Corellia]] to buy the Path engine.<ref name="Aphra 7" />

[[File:PathEngine-2020DoctorAphra6.jpg|thumb|left|230px|De'Rruyet acquired a Nihil Path engine, unaware that it was incomplete.]]
After obtaining the Path engine, De'Rruyet attempted to replicate the device.<ref name="Aphra 9" /> He claimed that he had found a way to do so and that his discovery would revolutionize [[lightspeed]] travel.<ref name="Aphra 6" /> With his company having created a reproduction of the Path engine and installed it into a starship, De'Rruyet was set to unveil the technology at the [[De'Rruyet Industries confluence]] on Midarr.<ref name="Aphra 9" /> However, the device that he had obtained was only a part of the larger hyperdrive system, and could not work on its own;<ref name="Aphra 8" /> indeed, in the [[Standard day|days]] leading up to the confluence, the replica was not yet complete, and would combust if it were to be activated, posing potential for catastrophe at the expo. Worried that the Path engine would not work as he had claimed, De'Rruyet booked a one-way ticket to<ref name="Aphra 9" /> the planet<ref name="Mon Cala">{{Databank|mon-cala|Mon Cala}}</ref> [[Mon Cala]] so that he could run with the [[Currency|money]] if the device did fail.<ref name="Aphra 9" />

Believing that De'Rruyet's discovery would either drastically change the [[The galaxy|galactic]] economy or end the ongoing [[Galactic Civil War]]&mdash;both of which would financially ruin her [[House of Tagge|dynasty]]&mdash;Domina Tagge wished to stop her rival from releasing the technology. Tagge recruited the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] to determine the origins of the Path engine and whether De'Rruyet's claims were true before the expo could take place.<ref name="Aphra 6" />

===The path to disaster===
{{Quote|Sir, I don't think you understand. We're still ''weeks away'' from having a conclusive&mdash;''"<br />"''All I hear are excuses. It doesn't have to be perfect&hellip;just ''convincing''.|An employee and Beol De'Rruyet|<ref name="Aphra 9" />}}
On the day before the confluence was to take place, De'Rruyet gave an interview to numerous [[Journalist|reporters]], affirming the revolutionary nature of the hyperdrive and his intention to unveil it. As his company prepared for the expo, however, one of De'Rruyet's employees warned him that the reproduction of the device was incomplete. De'Rruyet, determined to please his investors, ordered the protesting employee to make the device appear convincing. He was then approached by Aphra and the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]]&mdash;disguised as interns&mdash;who provided him with [[caf]]. De'Rruyet retched at the taste, claiming it was not what he had ordered, and disparaged the "interns" before walking away. Unknown to De'Rruyet, Aphra had stolen his access card, and she and Starros proceeded to inspect the Path engine replica before investigating his office.<ref name="Aphra 9" />

[[File:VukorahConfrontsDeRruyet-2020DoctorAphra9.jpg|thumb|right|310px|General Vukorah holds Beol De'Rruyet at gunpoint.]]
Entering his office to find Aphra and Starros at his desk, De'Rruyet confronted the two. Aphra claimed that they were investigators on behalf of the Empire, telling De'Rruyet that the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor]] would be disappointed with the fraud. De'Rruyet pulled his [[blaster]] on Aphra, asserting that the engine was a success, and Starros ordered him to call off the demonstration and surrender the device. They were interrupted, however, by the arrival of [[General]] [[Vukorah]] of the [[Unbroken Clan]] criminal syndicate, who also wished to acquire the Path engine. Blasting a hole through the wall of De'Rruyet Center, Vukorah held the interns and workers hostage until they could make the device operational. De'Rruyet rushed to the main hall, despite protest from Aphra to wait, where Vukorah pointed her blaster at him, requesting that he join her on the demonstration stage.<ref name="Aphra 9" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Blasted interns.''"<br />"''Don't take it personally. He's always like that.|Beol De'Rruyet and an employee, to a disguised Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 9" />}}
De'Rruyet was frequently harsh and dismissive toward his employees and interns at De'Rruyet Industries. He was also particular about his caf and was impatient when made to wait for it to be delivered. Though his attempt to replicate the Path engine was not met with success, De'Rruyet nevertheless insisted that the device would be revolutionary. He demanded that his employees give the appearance that the Path engine was functional, wishing to impress his investors and believing that there was no risk of his fraud being discovered. However, with the prospect of his engine failing likely, De'Rruyet intended to take his money and run in the event that it did not work.<ref name="Aphra 9" /> Doctor Aphra remarked that De'Rruyet would need to have a large ego to believe that he could replicate the device.<ref name="Aphra 8" />

Beol De'Rruyet was a [[human]] male with [[Color|black]] [[hair]], brown [[eye]]s, and light skin.<ref name="Aphra 9" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|Well, if it isn't Beol De'Rruyet himself. Don't even think about reaching for your blaster.|General Vukorah|<ref name="Aphra 9" />}}
De'Rruyet [[Clothing|wore]] a white tunic with gold lining and khaki wrappings on the sleeves, as well as brown pants and black shoes. He wielded a [[blaster pistol]], which he kept in a [[holster]] on his [[belt]]. De'Rruyet also carried an access card, which he kept unsecured in an outside pocket. De'Rruyet utilized a starship, in which he had the reproduced Path engine installed.<ref name="Aphra 9" />
