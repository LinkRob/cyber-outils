'''Ben Fox''' is an actor who played a background character credited as "Table #2" in the [[2022]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.<ref name="N5" /> He has also appeared in minor roles in TV series such as ''[[Wikipedia:Game of Thrones|Game of Thrones]]'', ''[[Wikipedia:Pennyworth (TV series)|Pennyworth]]'', and ''[[Wikipedia:Doctors (2000 TV series)|Doctors]]''.
