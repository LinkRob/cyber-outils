The '''City Bigspace Refreshment Bubble''',<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> also known as just the '''Bigspace Refreshment Bubble''', was a [[hydrostatic bubble]] built in the [[City Bigspace]], a popular public concourse and entertainment district commissioned by [[Boss]] [[Rugor Nass]] and built in the [[Gungan]] [[city]] of [[Otoh Gunga]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naboo]].<ref name="NoP" /> Built by [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}}, as depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[32 BBY]].</ref> the bubble catered to nearly every taste imaginable with a wide variety of [[tapcafe]]s and mobile [[Foodstuff|food]] vendors.<ref name="NoP" />

The bubble contained a large flat area with a lit central portion beneath a large amount of open space in the top of the bubble that contained a cluster of lights. [[Color|Brown]] arms reached up around the sides of the bubble from the base, which had four spheres held below it on a protrusion. A similar spiked protrusion rose from the cap on top of the bubble.<ref name="SWCL" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Bigspace Refreshment Bubble was first mentioned in ''[[Nexus of Power]]'', a ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] for the ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[roleplaying game]]<ref name="NoP" /> on [[March 17]], [[2016]].<ref name="Places of Power">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2016/3/17/places-of-power|text=Places of Power}}</ref> It was then depicted and identified as the City Bigspace Refreshment Bubble in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], a reference book<ref name="SWCL" /> published on [[September 27]], [[2016]].<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/545478/star-wars-complete-locations-by-dk|text=Star Wars: Complete Locations}}</ref>

The [[City Bigspace Refreshment Bubble/Legends|location]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', a [[2000]] reference book written by [[Kristin Lund]] and illustrated by [[Richard Chasemore]] and [[Hans Jenssen]].<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref> The material in ''Star Wars: Complete Locations'' was reprinted<ref name="SWCL" /> from this book.<ref name="Inside" />
