A '''training course''' was located on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Ajan Kloss]]. Situated in the [[Klosslands]], the course was used by [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] to instruct her [[Padawan]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] in the ways of the [[Jedi]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> Rey ran the course several times during her stay on the moon, honing her skills in [[the Force]] against both the natural [[jungle]] terrain of Ajan Kloss as well as a group of [[Marksman-H training remote]]s left behind by Organa's late brother and [[Jedi Master]], [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
