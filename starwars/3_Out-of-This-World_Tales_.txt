'''''3 Out-of-This-World Tales!''''' is a compendium title that collects three previously published ''[[Star Wars]]'' young-readers stories in the [[World of Reading]] series: [[Escape from Darth Vader (World of Reading)|''Escape from Darth Vader'']], [[AT-AT Attack! (book)|''AT-AT Attack!'']], and ''[[Ewoks Join the Fight]]''. It was published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[September 26]], [[2017]], and includes a companion CD to help readers listen along with the story.

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''This Level 1 Star Wars reader bind up features three stories (''Escape from Darth Vader'', ''AT-AT Attack!'', and ''Ewoks Join the Fight'') and comes with an accompanying audio CD featuring word-for-word narration of the text.''<ref name="Disney" />
