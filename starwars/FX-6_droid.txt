The '''FX-6 droid''' was a [[medical droid]] model that existed during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="TVoSD" /> The FX-6 had the ability to detect [[poison|toxins]] and [[poison]]s, and was equipped with an [[alarm system]]. By [[200 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]].</ref> a [[FX-6 (Begamor)|modified unit]] was owned by [[President]] [[Jamie Brasson]] at his estate on [[Begamor]].<ref name="TVoSD" />
