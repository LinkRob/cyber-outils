'''Feeorins''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] with thick tendrils hanging from the back of their heads. During the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]], a Feeorin named [[Warjak]] performed as a [[gladiator]], holding the title of champion of the [[Outer Rim Carve-up]].<ref name="EG" /> [[Zira]] was a [[Gender|female]] [[pirate]] who was also a member of this species.<ref name="Off the Rails" />

{{Species-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Feeorins made their first [[canon]]ical appearance in ''[[Ezra's Gamble]]'', a ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' junior novel by [[Ryder Windham]] that was released on [[August 5]], [[2014]].<ref name="EG" /> They originated in [[Star Wars: Starfighter (video game)|''Star Wars: Starfighter'']], a [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] flight simulation video game that was released on [[February 19]], [[2001]].<ref name="Starfighter">[[Star Wars: Starfighter (video game)|''Star Wars: Starfighter'']]</ref>
