'''David S. Goyer''' is an American screenwriter and director who wrote ''[[Star Wars: Secrets of the Empire]]''<ref name="SW Show">{{StarWarsShow|K5Q_ub4AIVQ|Inside Secrets of the Empire with David S. Goyer, the New runDisney Dark Side Medals, and More!|sw_url=video/inside-secrets-of-the-empire-with-david-s-goyer-the-new-rundisney-dark-side-medals-and-more}}</ref> and ''[[Vader Immortal: A Star Wars VR Series]]''.<ref name="Vader Immortal">{{SW|url=news/vader-immortal|text=''Vader Immortal: A Star Wars VR Series &ndash; Episode I'' Revealed}}</ref> 

On [[September 21]], [[2023]], Goyer revealed that at some point around [[2019]], he was signed on to write [[Untitled Star Wars film (James Mangold)|a film]] regarding the Jedi's origins [[25,000 BBY|25,000]] [[Standard year|years]] before [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''The Phantom Menace'']], alongside [[Untitled Jabba the Hutt film|a separate project]] under director [[Guillermo del Toro]]. However, Goyer left his work for Lucasfilm.<ref name="DelToroMovie">{{WebCite|url=https://variety.com/2023/film/news/guillermo-del-toro-star-wars-movie-axed-4-years-ago-1235731222/amp|text=Guillermo del Toro Confirms Axed 'Star Wars' Movie, Writer David S. Goyer Scripted It Four Years Ago: 'There Is a Lot of Cool Art Work From It'|author=Zack Sharf|work=[[Wikipedia:Variety (magazine)|''Variety'']]|date=2023-09-21|archivedate=20230922001458}}</ref> Del Toro later confirmed that his cancelled film was about the rise and fall of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]].<ref name="Collider2">{{WebCite|url=https://collider.com/guillermo-del-toro-star-wars-movie-comments|text=What Guillermo Del Toro Learned From Developing His 'Star Wars' Movie|author=Diego Peralta|date=2023-10-07|work=[[Wikipedia:Collider (website)|''Collider'']]|archivedate=20231010183903}}</ref>
