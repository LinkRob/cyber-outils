'''Bellows''' was a [[clone trooper]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] who later worked with the [[Clan Assembly (Xorrn)|Clan Assembly]]. After suffering severe head trauma just before [[Order 66]] was issued by [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], Bellows was discovered by [[Lira Hardin]]. Once he was awake, Bellows discovered that the clones had been affected by Order 66, and went into hiding with Hardin once he convinced her that he was not under the influence of Order 66 himself.<ref name="Friends Like These" />

Over [[time]], Bellows grew a bushy, [[Color|grey]] beard and gained weight. He was named the [[Chief of Security|chief]] for [[Foundry Four]]'s [[Foundry Four security team|security team]]. While he occasionally handled scuffles and domestic disputes, Chief Bellows was mainly responsible for ensuing that the shady customers parading to [[Xorrn]] for [[Starship|ship]] upgrades paid their bills. Although Bellows was not formerly on the Clan Assembly, he was frequently included in meetings to provide his input on security.<ref name="Friends Like These" />
