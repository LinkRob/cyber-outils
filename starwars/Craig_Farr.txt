'''Craig Farr''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan who designed [[Brock Lar]], [[Gryphon]], and ''[[Martika]]'' along with [[Matthew Berry]], in the [[Design An Alien]] contest held in the ''[[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine]]s''. The entry was given an honorable mention in the 16 years and older category. He was 18 at the time his entry was published.
