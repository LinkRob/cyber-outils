A '''flag officer''' was a position held in a faction's [[Navy|naval]] branch.<ref name="Thrawn novel">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> In the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], a flag [[officer]] was an [[Chiss officer|officer]] including and above the rank of [[Commodore]]. Upon promotion to flag rank, a [[Chiss officer]] was stripped of ties to their [[Chiss family]] in a [[Unidentified promotion ceremony|ceremony]] in which the [[Supreme Admiral]] declared "the Ascendancy is your family." This step was introduced to ensure that family [[politics]] did not interfere with the decisions of the [[Chiss Defense Force|military]].<ref name="Chaos Rising />

In the [[Imperial Navy|starfleet]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], flags officers were (from higher to lower order) [[Admiral of the Fleet]], [[Grand Admiral]], [[Fleet Admiral]], [[Admiral]], [[Vice Admiral]], [[Rear Admiral]] and [[Commodore]].<ref name="Thrawn novel" /> [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]] was described as a perfect model for an Imperial [[Navy]] flag [[Imperial officer|officer]].<ref name="Dawn" />
