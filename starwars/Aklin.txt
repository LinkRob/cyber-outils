'''Aklin''' was an individual who served the [[Galactic Empire]]. He had knowledge that a powerful [[unidentified ring|ring]], which had the power to raise the [[death|dead]] and turn them into worker drones, was contained in [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] [[Darth Sidious' collection|private collection]] and was being transported onto the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]]. [[Imperial Engineering Corps]] [[technician]] [[Anna Param]] felt that Aklin was generally an unreliable source of information.<ref name="Double" />
