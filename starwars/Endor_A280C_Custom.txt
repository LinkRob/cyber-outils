The '''Endor A280C Custom''' was a custom [[A280C blaster rifle|A280C]] [[blaster rifle]] utilized by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Firestorm Rifle|standard rifle]]. This modified [[blaster]] [[rifle]] featured a sleek, metallic, [[Color|gray]] and silver frame, with vertically stacked twin barrels, tube-like stock, and glowing orange components.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Endor A280C Custom was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Imara Vex's Firestorm Rifle, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Endor Fortification]]. This wrap could be unlocked by reaching Tier 55 on the Season 1 Arena Pass' premium track.<ref name="Hunters" />
