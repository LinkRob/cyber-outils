An '''enhanced durasteel hull''' was a [[metal]] component that was used within and to install various [[starship]] systems and weapons. These include [[LC-70 laser cannon]]s, [[C-K10A rapid fire cannon]]s, [[CM-2 Arana cluster missile]] launchers, [[PT-240 proton torpedo]] launchers, [[E-410 sublight engine]]s, [[H-RD duralloy hull]]s, and [[H-SD duralloy hull]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

In [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> it was commonly sold for 230 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] a unit by a wide variety of [[merchant]]s in [[the galaxy]] including on [[Toshara]], [[Tatooine]] and [[Akiva]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Enhanced durasteel hulls appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. In the game, it is used as an upgrade material.<ref name="Outlaws" />
