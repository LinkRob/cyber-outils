The '''Caramel-filled Éclair''' was a portable [[Foodstuff|dessert]] served at [[Kat Saka's Kettle]] at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Kat Saka's Kettle" /> in [[34 ABY]]<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> during [[Black Spire Day]]. It was consumable by [[human]]s.<ref name="Kat Saka's Kettle" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Caramel-filled Éclair was a pastry available for purchase in the [[In-universe|real world]] at Kat Saka's Kettle in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]] exclusively for [[Star Wars Nite|''Star Wars'' Nites]] in [[2024]].<ref name="2024 Program">{{WebCite|url=https://www.laughingplace.com/w/disney-parks/disneyland-after-dark-star-wars-nite-guide-map-with-list-of-entertainment-characters-and-food-items|text=Disneyland After Dark: Star Wars Nite &ndash; Guide Map with List of Entertainment, Characters, and Food Items|work=LaughingPlace.com|archiveurl=https://archive.is/3D7ci}}</ref> The menu described the [[Wikipedia:Éclair|éclair]] as being "dipped in [[Chocolate]] and topped with [[Seed]] Mix."<ref name="DLP Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/kat-sakas-kettle/menus|text=Disneyland: Kat Saka's Kettle Snack Menu}}</ref><ref name="Dougall 2024">{{YouTube|video=q5aEnXcsNao|text=Disneyland After Dark: Star Wars Nite! &#124; Disneyland 2024!|channel=@PatrickDougall|name=Patrick Dougall|date=2024-04-18}}</ref>
