[[Dryden Vos]] was a member of a [[near-human]]<ref name="TOG" /> '''species''' which were nearly identical to baseline [[human]]s with the exception of their facial markings, which would change [[color]] when they were angry, and their [[claw]]ed thumbs.<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
The species first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology film]] ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" />  [[Leland Chee]], a member of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]], hinted that [[Naare]], a character from ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'', could be of the same species, given that she has similar markings to [[Dryden Vos|Dryden]].<ref name="Naare1">{{Twitter|HolocronKeeper|status/1000122619096526848|[[Leland Chee]]|quote=Dryden Vos isn't the only one who has facial markings that show up when they're angry. Just sayin'. #Naare|archivedate=20190722102318}}</ref>
