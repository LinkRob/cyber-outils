During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Jedi Order]] held a '''funeral''' for the victims who were [[Death|killed]] in a [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|bombing]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]]. Various [[Jedi]] and [[Galactic Republic]] dignitaries were in attendance, including [[Padawan]] [[Barriss Offee]],<ref name="TJWKTM" /> who was later revealed to be the mastermind of the attack.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref>
