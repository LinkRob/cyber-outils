'''Alan Ruscoe''' ([[April 14]], [[1972]]) is an [[Wikipedia:British people|English]] actor who played [[Plo Koon]] and [[Daultay Dofine]] in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. He was also credited as playing "[[Lott Dod]]" in [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Attack of the Clones'']], even though the character has later been established as [[Rune Haako]]. He also briefly reprised his role as Plo Koon during pickup shots in England (when normal actor, [[Matt Sloan]], couldn't be present). Most of his scenes were omitted from the final film, however.

Ruscoe also played one of the [[Twi'lek]] aides of [[Senator]] [[Orn Free Taa]] on [[Coruscant]] in ''The Phantom Menace'' and was nicknamed "Bib Fortuna" by the production crew, but was ultimately cut from the final film. The actual Fortuna in the film was portrayed by [[Matthew Wood]].<ref name="old DB">{{DB|character|bibfortuna|Fortuna, Bib}}</ref> However, Ruscoe still described his character as Fortuna in an interview with [[Star Wars Insider 117|''Star Wars Insider'' 117]] in [[2010]]. Ruscoe was initially only contracted to portray [[Plo Koon]] in the film, but ended up also portraying [[Daultay Dofine]] and "Fortuna". He wore a major three piece prosthetic that took four hours to apply and two additional hours to remove. In the interview, Ruscoe empathized that it was "nothing" compared to the time actor [[Michael Carter]] needed for his makeup and that the real work was done by the makeup artists and not him.<ref name="Insider 117">{{InsiderCite|117|Phantom Menaces (article)}}</ref>

Ruscoe is best known for appearing in ''Doctor Who'' in the stories [https://tardis.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_(TV_story) ''Rose''] (2005), [https://tardis.fandom.com/wiki/The_End_of_the_World_(TV_story) ''The End of the World''] (2005), [https://tardis.fandom.com/wiki/Aliens_of_London_(TV_story) ''Aliens of London''] and [https://tardis.fandom.com/wiki/World_War_Three_(TV_story) ''World War Three''] (2005), [https://tardis.fandom.com/wiki/Bad_Wolf_(TV_story) ''Bad Wolf''] (2005), [https://tardis.fandom.com/wiki/The_Parting_of_the_Ways_(TV_story) ''The Parting of the Ways''] (2005) and [https://tardis.fandom.com/wiki/The_Waters_of_Mars_(TV_story) ''The Waters of Mars''] (2009).
