'''Eldie Starros''' was a [[Gender|female]] member of the [[Starros Clan]] and the [[Parent|great-grandmother]] of the [[human]] [[scientist]] [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]]. At some point before Avon was sent to the outpost [[Port Haileap]] on the [[planet]] [[Haileap]]<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> by [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Eldie had [[Death|died]], and the Starros family gathered for exactly one [[Standard hour|hour]] to pay their respects to Eldie and [[Diet|eat]] a selection of Eldie's favorite [[Foodstuff|foods]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Avon was asked by the [[Dalnan]] [[Honesty Weft]] about the reasoning for why she had been sent to Port Haileap. In response, the scientist mentally noted that after she had been [[Kidnapping of Avon Starros|kidnapped]]&mdash;the reason why she had been sent to Haileap&mdash;she had been told by an [[Unidentified emotional-support droid|emotional-support droid]] to talk about the experience. Avon then noted that talking about her experience was not the way her family lived their lives, before recalling Eldie's death and what the family had done after.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eldie Starros was mentioned in the [[2021]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published as part of<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
