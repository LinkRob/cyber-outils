'''Dianoga's Kiss''' was a [[species]] of [[Color|brown]] tentacled [[plant]] found on the [[planet]] [[Balnab]],<ref name="VE" /> a primitive stellar remenant in its emerging stages of organic development. The plants grew alongside [[umbrella tree]]s.<ref name="ND">{{TCW|Nomad Droids}}</ref>

{{Flora-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dianoga's Kiss first appeared in "[[Nomad Droids]],"<ref name="ND" /> the sixth episode of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television show's [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]], which aired on [[October 14]], [[2011]].<ref name="Explore: Nomad Droids">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/nomad-droids-episode-guide|text="Nomad Droids" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> The plants were first named in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''.<ref name="VE" />

Concept artist [[Pat Presley]] created multiple pieces of concept art including Dianoga's Kiss for the episode including a "Balnab Ext" environment design sheet showing the different types of Balnab vegetation to scale and another design sheet for the Balnab crash site inhabited by the raiders.<ref name="Explore: Nomad Droids" />
