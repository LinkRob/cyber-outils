"'''Blood Inheritance'''" is an adventure for [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]], which appeared in the sourcebook ''[[Tapani Sector Instant Adventures]]''.

==Plot summary==
A [[Spacer/Legends|spacer]] learns that they are the primary inheritor of a [[Noble/Legends|noble's]] estate. Reaching the estate, the spacer and their colleagues have to deal with family politics and [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side]] machinations.
