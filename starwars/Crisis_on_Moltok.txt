A '''crisis''' began on [[Moltok]] when a [[Makurth]] [[Unidentified Makurth boss|boss]] shot at a [[Ho'Din]] [[Unidentified Ho'Din settlement|settlement]]. This lead the Ho'Din to request [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] support to prevent the crisis from becoming a full-fledged war. At the [[time]] of the crisis the [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] ''[[Blood Crow]]'' was assisting the [[freighter]] ''[[Dromedar]]'' when it was requested to help settle the matter. While members of the ''Blood Crow''{{'s}} crew stayed behind to fix the ''Dromedar'', the ''Blood Crow'' jumped into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Thrawn" />
