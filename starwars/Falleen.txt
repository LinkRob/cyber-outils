'''Falleen''' were a [[Reptomammal|repto-mammalian]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] of the [[Falleen (planet)|same name]]. They were capable of projecting powerful [[pheromone]]s. During the [[Clone Wars]], the Falleen [[Ziton Moj]] replaced a cabal of Falleen [[noble]]s in leading the [[Black Sun]] criminal syndicate from their [[Black Sun fortress|fortress]] on [[Mustafar]].

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:Uil-HanSolo2.png|thumb|left|150px|The Falleen [[U'il]]]]
The Falleen were a [[Reptomammal|repto-mammalian]]<ref name="Helmet Collection 17" /> [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="Eminence" /> who had a distinctive [[alien]] appearance.<ref name="DB" /> They were distinguished by their mottled [[Color|green]]<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> skin, ridged [[Bone|skulls]], and their long, black hair which they typically wore in ponytails.<ref name="DB" /> Falleen exuded powerful [[pheromone]]s which they used to attract mates.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

==History==
Falleen were [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Falleen (planet)|Falleen]],<ref name="USW" /> but were a colonial species who had migrated and adapted to dominate many worlds.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> and so also inhabited [[Ord Mantell]] alongside a [[human]] population.<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>

During the [[Clone Wars]], a cabal of Falleen nobles led the [[Black Sun]] criminal syndicate, which flourished while law enforcement declined as the [[Jedi]] focused on battling the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Black Sun became notorious in the lawless [[Outer Rim Territories]] and elsewhere, and its Falleen leadership operated out of a well-defended [[Black Sun fortress|fortress]] on [[Mustafar]].<ref name="Black Sun">{{Databank|black-sun|Black Sun}}</ref> [[Xomit Grunseit]] led the cabal<ref name="Xomit Grunseit">{{Databank|xomit-grunseit|Xomit Grunseit}}</ref> until [[Maul]] ultimately usurped control of Black Sun, and installed the leg-breaking [[Lieutenant (criminal)|lieutenant]] [[Ziton Moj]] as its ruler.<ref name="Ziton Moj">{{Databank|ziton-moj|Ziton Moj}}</ref>

<div style="float: right">[[File:BlackSunLeaders-Eminence.png|thumb|250px|The Falleen nobles of [[Black Sun]]]]</div>
Black Sun was also involved in an underground dispute with the [[Pyke Syndicate]], which led to [[Asajj Ventress]] and [[Quinlan Vos]] investigating a kidnapping by the Pykes. Ventress coerced a Falleen guard on Mustafar, leading him to believe his powerful pheromones had worked on her, and she and Vos strong-armed him into giving up information on the Pykes. The pair infiltrated the Black Sun fortress, fighting more Falleen to reach the kidnapped family.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Falleen like other [[alien]] species such as [[Umbaran]]s and [[Neimoidian]]s, were typically the butt of running jokes at [[Imperial Academy|Imperial Academies]] including the [[Royal Imperial Academy]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> At this time, the Falleen [[Ango Croom]] led the [[Varluk Organization]].<ref name="PP">{{RebelsMagCite|21|Puffer Problems}}</ref> A Falleen [[gangster]] named [[Annaz]] was a member of the [[Malandro syndicate]] before she was attacked and [[torture]]d by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Brand]], whose parents Annaz had formerly killed.<ref name="IB">{{InsiderCite|161|Inbrief}}</ref> A number of Falleen worked as overseers in [[MaDall]]'s [[MaDall's empire|enslaving operation]] on [[Oon]], and treated [[Sabine Wren]] roughly when she posed as a [[Slavery|slave]] to infiltrate the operation. She inspired an uprising against the slavers, and the Falleen guards were attacked and overpowered.<ref name="RSlavers">{{RebelsMagCite|5|Return of the Slavers}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Falleen first appeared in "[[Eminence (episode)|Eminence]]," the fourteenth episode of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="Eminence" /> which aired on [[January 19]], [[2013]].<ref name="Eminence-TV">{{TVcom|url=shows/star-wars-the-clone-wars/eminence-2631849|text=Star Wars: The Clone Wars - Season 5 Episode 14: Eminence}}</ref> Within the first installment of the [[Prequel trilogy]], [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], micro-machine toy figurines of [[Prince]] [[Xizor]],<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> a major [[Falleen/Legends|Falleen]] antagonist from [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (novel)|''Shadows of the Empire'']],<ref name="Shadows">[[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (novel)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'' novel]]</ref> were used in the [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]].<ref name="Episode I" /> Because this "appearance" was only used to fill-in the background, it is not known if the cameo would be considered an actual appearance of the species.<ref name="Xizor cameo">{{Hyperspace|url=hyperspace/member/photoreceptor/2004/02/photoreceptor20040211.html|series=Photoreceptor|text=Xizor's Cameo?}}</ref> Although ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'' brought the character into the [[canon]] continuity, it did not confirm if he<ref name="Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref> was at the [[Boonta Eve Classic]].<ref name="Episode I" />

In [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity, the [[Falleen/Legends|Falleen]] species first appeared in the novel ''Shadows of the Empire'', which was written by [[Steve Perry]]<ref name="Shadows" /> and published in [[April]] [[1996]].<ref name="Insider 28">{{InsiderCite|28|Lucasfilm's Latest}}</ref>
