The '''14th Atrisian Emperor''',<ref name="TLJ VD" /> otherwise known as the '''14th Emperor of Kitel Phard''',<ref name="Comprehensive">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> was an individual who served as the fourteenth [[Atrisian Emperor]]<ref name="TLJ VD" /> of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="WorldsTFA">{{SW|url=news/where-in-the-galaxy-are-the-worlds-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''?|int=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Star Wars: The Force Awakens?}}</ref> astronomical body<ref name="TLJ VD" /> [[Atrisia]].<ref name="Fry" /> The name of the [[Praetorian Guard]], an elite cadre of [[bodyguard]]s<ref name="TLJ VD" /> affiliated with the [[Shadow Council (Imperial Remnants)|Shadow Council]] of the [[Imperial Remnants]]<ref name="Chapter 23">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 23: The Spies}}</ref> by [[9 ABY]]<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoDate|23}}</ref> and later the group used by [[First Order]] [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]]<ref name="TLJ VD" /> by [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> traced its roots to the [[Emperor]].<ref name="TLJ VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 14th Atrisian Emperor was first mentioned in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was authored by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TLJ VD" /> Alternatively, the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' named them the 14th Emperor of Kitel Phard.<ref name="Comprehensive" />
