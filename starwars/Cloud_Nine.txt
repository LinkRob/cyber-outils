The '''Cloud Nine''' was a costume owned by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sprocket]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Prodigal Programmer|standard costume]]. This [[Clothing|costume]] consisted of a [[Color|brown]], tan, light gray, and orange, uniform, as well as a tan and orange [[helmet]], dark gray [[belt]], and a gray and black [[Sprocket's datapad|datapad]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cloud Nine was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Sprocket, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Cloud Chair]]. Unlike most Costumes in the game, Cloud Nine did not share the same rarity as its cosmetic set counterpart, with Cloud Chair instead being an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap."<ref name="Hunters" />
