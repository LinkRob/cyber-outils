{{Quote|On arrival at the temple, Initiates are sorted in clans, an arbitrary grouping in many ways, but one that is supposed to foster an atmosphere of trust and kinship.|Count Dooku on the Initiate clans|<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />}}
During their [[time]] at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]], [[Jedi Initiate]]s were placed into '''clans'''. Such Clans were the [[Bear Clan]],<ref name="Bear Clan" /> the [[Clan Kowak]],<ref name="What a Jedi Makes" /> the [[Hawk-Bat Clan]], the [[Heliost Clan]], the [[Thranta Clan]],<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> and a [[Unidentified Clan (The Gathering)|clan]] that consisted of the Younglings [[Petro]], [[Ganodi]], [[Katooni]], [[Byph]], [[Zatt]] and [[Gungi]]. When its members reached a certain age, a clan embarked on a journey known as "[[Gathering|the Gathering]]," in which the Initiates would be guided by [[the Force]] to their [[Kyber crystal|lightsaber crystal]] on the [[planet]] [[Ilum]].<ref name="The Gathering" />
