The '''212th Recon Division''' was a subunit under the [[212th Attack Battalion]], mobilized by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. Under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], the unit participated in the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]] when the [[Galactic Republic]] invaded the [[planet]] to regain control.<ref name="LPR" />

==Characteristics==
The 212th Recon Division was outfitted with [[Phase I ARF armor]] to specialize in covert operations behind enemy lines. They mobilized with agile [[AT-series|All-terrain vehicles]] such as the [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]] and the [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]].<ref name="BFII Info" /> During the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], the unit wore desert camo to match the [[planet]]'s [[desert]] environment. [[Trooper]]s [[Waxer]] and [[Boil]]<ref name="LPR" /> of [[Ghost Company]]<ref name="Waxer Databank">{{Databank|waxer|Clone Trooper Waxer}}</ref> served alongside members of the 212th Recon Division during the battle.<ref name="LPR" />
