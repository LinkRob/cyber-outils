At some point between [[2 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> but before the [[battle on Vir Aphshire]],<ref name="B:TC" /> a '''battle''' took place between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] led by [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]] and the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|forces]] on [[Allst Prime]] led by [[Governor]] [[Magé]]. In the midst of the battle, the Forward Air Control [[Officer]] [[Cait]] was [[Death|killed]] when directing the 61st's [[X-wing starfighter|X-Wings]] and Evon had to take her place. At the same [[time]] 61st [[Soldier]] [[Brand]] rescued [[Lylee Anaraku]] from an [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]]. Brand was distracted by Evon, standing over Cait's body and shouting orders into her comm, when Anaraku was taken down by an Imperial. Brand killed the Imperial and went to Anaraku's aid. Later in the battle Brand and Evon stormed into the governor's office and Brand stunned Magé. Brand then pointed her gun at Evon as she a had taken a [[bounty]] on him. Evon revealed what he knew about her past and convinced her to stay with the 61st and abandon her old life as a [[bounty hunter]].<ref name="IB" />
