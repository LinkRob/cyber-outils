'''Derrech Wren''' was an individual in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a nineteen course [[goblosh]] dinner named after Wren was offered at the [[Crescent Royale Bistro]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> an advertisement for the dinner was broadcast over the [[speaker]] system of the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
