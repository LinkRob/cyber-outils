'''Fompa bread''' was a type of [[bread]] that was available on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> it was listed as costing two [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on both the menu at the [[Hot Pot]] [[Restaurant|eatery]] in the [[Market District (Mirogana)|market]] of the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] and at the [[Daruda Diner]] in the town of [[Jaunta's Hope]], both located on the moon.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fompa bread was created for the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. Prior to the game's release, the menu mentioning the bread was visible during a gameplay preview released on [[June 13]], [[2023]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
