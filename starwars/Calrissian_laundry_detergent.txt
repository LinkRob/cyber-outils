An advertisement for a '''laundry detergent''' was put up at [[Rafa's Laundry]], a launderette on [[Coruscant]]'s [[Level 1313]]. It was written in [[Aurebesh]] and read "CALRISSIAN FR<small>''[obscured]''</small>" – "SO FRESH SO <small>''[obscured]''</small>".<ref name="Gone" />

==Behind the scenes==
The advertisement was originally created by [[Will Nichols]] along with various other posters for the cockpit of the [[TR-RL3 Terrapin-class bulk tanker|Turtle Tanker]] for the episode "[[Brothers (episode)|Brothers]]" of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|fourth season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="Nichols BlogSpot">{{Blogspot|subdomain=willnicholsportfolio|url=2018/04/vehicles.html|name=WILL NICHOLS PORTFOLIO|text=VEHICLES|date=2018-04-02|archivedate=20251219110853}}</ref><ref name="NicholsArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=songjong|name=[[Will Nichols|William Nichols]]|url=artwork/w9OyZ/|text=Star Wars Clone Wars Revenge 4x22 ship|date=2016-10-03|quote=This was a ship (space truck) I designed for Clone Wars. It was stolen and piloted by Savage and Maul. Ventress and Kenobi would team up to fight them in the ship. There were a lot of little details in the ship (posters, twi'lek hula girl) that I designed that didnt make it into the ship but would be used later in other episodes.|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20201228223904/https://songjong.artstation.com/projects/w9OyZ}}</ref> which was released on [[March 9]], [[2012]].<ref name="Explore: Brothers">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep421|text="Brothers" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide|nolive=1}}</ref> It later appeared in the episode "[[Gone with a Trace]]" from [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|Season Seven]] of ''The Clone Wars'', but is partially obscured by the washing machines at Rafa's Laundry.<ref name="Gone" /> The full original design and read "CALRISSIAN FRESH" – "SO FRESH SO CLEAN" and also featured a humanoid figure with long hair waiving a piece of cloth.<ref name="Nichols BlogSpot" /><ref name="NicholsArtStation" />

The name Calrissian seems to be a nod to the character [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]],<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> who, like owner [[Rafa Martez]],<ref name="Deal">{{TCW|Deal No Deal}}</ref> deals in [[gambling]].<ref name="ESB" /> In addition, a sticker on a washing machine says "landomat", seemingly a pun on "Lando" and "laundromat."<ref name="Gone" />
