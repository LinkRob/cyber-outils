'''Conan Christopher O'Brien''' (born [[April 18]], [[1960s#1963|1963]]) is an [[Wikipedia:United States of America|American]] television host, comedian, writer, and producer. He is best known for hosting the late-night talk shows [[Wikipedia:Late Night with Conan O'Brien|''Late Night with Conan O'Brien'']], [[Wikipedia:The Tonight Show with Conan O'Brien|''The Tonight Show with Conan O'Brien'']], and [[Wikipedia:Conan (talk show)|''Conan'']].

On [[May 1]], [[2007]], after talking with ''[[Star Wars]]'' creator [[George Lucas]] about [[Notluwiski Papanoida|his cameo]] in [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] and his feelings for having finished, by then, the ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga, O'Brien invited his associate producer Jordan Schlansky to ask Lucas which was the name of the [[Imperial officer]] who gets [[Force choke|strangled]] by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] at the [[Death Star conference room]] scene from [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].

After thinking it for a while, Lucas responded that the character's complete name, as he was already known as Admiral Motti, was "[[Conan Antonio Motti]]," a [[tuckerization]] of O'Brien's name. The name was then [[canon]]ized and the next day O'Brien shared his surprise that the name was [[Special:Diff/1315629|quickly adopted]] by [[Wookieepedia]].<ref name="Conan's Blog">{{WebCite|url=http://blog.nbc.com/latenight/2007/05/breaking_hearts_and_making_far.php|text=Blog I Find Your Lack Of Faith Disturbing&hellip;|work=NBC.com|archivedate=20080303214713}}</ref>
