'''Fizzy soup''' was a type of [[soup]] that was available on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> there was an advertisement for "[[Kijimi City]]'s famous Fizzy soup" in entrance of [[the Domak Refectory]] in the [[Thieves' Quarter]] of Kijimi City.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fizzy soup was first mentioned in [[Aurebesh]] on a sign in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. Though the sign continues in a [[Unidentified Aurebesh script (battle helmet)|type of Aruebesh]] it translates to nonsense with the first two words translating to "[[Wikipedia:Lorem ipsum|Lorem ipsum]]" part of a common real life filler text.<ref name="Outlaws" />
