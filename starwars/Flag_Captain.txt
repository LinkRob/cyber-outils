A '''Flag Captain''' was a senior [[officer]] [[Military rank|rank]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] that was given to an individual who [[captain]]ed the [[flagship]] of an [[admiral]]. [[Albus Marinith]] served as Flag Captain of the ''[[Chimaera]]'', the flagship of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] and his [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]].<ref name="Dawn" /> [[Firmus Piett]] served as the Flag Captain of the ''[[Executor]]'', flagship of [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and Admiral [[Kendal Ozzel]].<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref>
