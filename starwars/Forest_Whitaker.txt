'''Forest Whitaker''' (born [[July 15]], [[1960s#1961|1961]]) is an actor who played [[Saw Gerrera]] in the [[Star Wars Anthology Series|stand-alone]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. He later returned to voice the character in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episodes "[[Ghosts of Geonosis]]" and "[[In the Name of the Rebellion]]." He reprised the role once more in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''. In [[2022]], he returned to live-action in ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.

==Biography==
Forest Whitaker was born on [[July 15]], [[1960s|1961]].<ref name="SWYT 2020-07-16" /> He played the [[Partisans|Partisan]] [[Saw Gerrera]] in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Anthology Series|anthology]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Rogue One" /> He was the only actor considered for the role, according to<ref name="Only Forest">{{Twitter|garywhitta|status/1219451136572133377|[[Gary Whitta]]|date=2020-01-20|quote=Yes. Forrest was always the only choice for Saw.|archivedate=20200121030231}}</ref> writer<ref name="Rogue One" /> [[Gary Whitta]].<ref name="Only Forest" /> Whitaker later voiced the character in the episodes "[[Ghosts of Geonosis]]"<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis">{{Rebels|Ghosts of Geonosis}}</ref> and "[[In the Name of the Rebellion]]" of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' animated series.<ref name="In the Name">{{Rebels|In the Name of the Rebellion}}</ref> He also voiced Gerrera in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''.<ref name="Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

[[File:Andor_Saw_Gerrera_Poster.jpg|thumb|left|130px|Forest Whitaker as Saw Gerrera in his character poster]]
In [[2022]], Whitaker reprised his role as Gerrera in the episodes "[[Narkina 5 (episode)|Narkina 5]]"<ref name="Narkina 5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref> and "[[Daughter of Ferrix]]" in the ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' television series.<ref name="Daughter of Ferrix">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> The night before a rehearsal for Gerrera and [[Luthen Rael]]'s argument in "Narkina 5," Whitaker called director [[Toby Haynes]] to ask about backstory and [[kyber crystal]]s. During the rehearsal, Whitaker and [[Stellan Skarsgård]], the actor who played Rael, read the scene for the first time and went at each other. According to Haynes, when the scene was filmed for real, Whitaker and Skarsgård almost deconstructed what they did in a spontaneous and natural way. The actors then reconstructed the scene from scratch, and it was hard to get to the same pitch. Haynes wanted to end Gerrera's rant with a very direct, face-on shot of Whitaker to build the intensity and bring the audience into the argument. To achieve this shot, Haynes had Whitaker repeat his speech many times and told him to "go crazy with it."<ref name="Career Highlight">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/andor-episode-8-toby-haynes-forest-whitaker-stellan-skarsgard-1235250954|text='Andor' Director Toby Haynes Talks That Forest Whitaker-Stellan Skarsgard Scene: "It Was a Career Highlight"|author=Brian Davids|date=2022-10-28|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20221029050743}}</ref>
