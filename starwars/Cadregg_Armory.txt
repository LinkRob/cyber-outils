'''Cadregg Armory''' was an arms manufacturing company. It was considered a newcomer to the arms manufacturing scene by the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Its first creation, the [["Precision-X" Marksman Rifle]], was so sought after that the company couldn't keep up with demand.<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> Due to the [[blaster rifle]]'s popularity, Cadregg Armory quickly became the darling of the arms industry.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref> The company also produced the [[Model-87 Weapon Sensor]], a model of [[Marked-Target Firing Lock]].<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cadregg Armory was first mentioned in [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']],<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> a [[2017]] supplement for the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="A Fistful of Credits">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/2/2/a-fistful-of-credits|text=A Fistful of Credits}}</ref>
