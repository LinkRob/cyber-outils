The '''Cyclorria system'''<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> was located within the [[Inner Rim Territories]] in grid square N-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Timelines" /> It shared its name with the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Cyclorrian]] [[species]], who lived on the [[Mid Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Cyclor]].<ref name="TheRisingStorm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Cyclorria shouldn't have been mapped but once it was, it seemed weird to strike it. So probably a colony never to be mentioned again.|Jason Fry|<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-09-15.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-09-15}}</ref>}}
The Cyclorria [[Star system|system]] was first mentioned on a [[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|map]]<ref name="Timelines" /> created for the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'',<ref name="TimelinesSWcom">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> designed by [[Christopher Ibbitt]]<ref name="Fry email">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-31.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-31}}</ref> and given out as a promotional poster at [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|Celebration Anaheim]]<ref name="TimelinesSWcom" /> on [[May 27]], [[2022]]<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|dkpublishing|status/1530292772598534145|[[Dorling Kindersley|DK Books US]]|date=2022-05-27|quote=Just announced at #StarWarsCelebration! Get your exclusive map - only at booth #2525.|archivedate=20220527210041}}</ref> prior to the book's release during [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]]<ref name="Celebration">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-europe-2023-exclusives|text=Check Out ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Exclusives from Hasbro, the LEGO Group, and More}}</ref> of [[April 7]] through [[April 10|10]], [[2023]].<ref name="CelebrationSchedule">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-panel-schedule|text=Plan Ahead with the ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Panel Schedule}}</ref> It was then identified by name in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] article on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

The Cyclorria system was originally added to the map as the home of the Cyclorrian species;<ref name="Fry" /> however, the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="TheRisingStorm" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]],<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> had already identified Cyclor as their [[homeworld]].<ref name="TheRisingStorm" /> Although Fry admitted that the mapping of Cyclorria had stemmed from an error, the author nevertheless also included the system in "Star Systems of the Galaxy," reasoning that it could have potentially been a [[Colonist|colony]] of the Cyclorrians.<ref name="Fry" />
