'''Boolan's virus''' was a highly-communicable airborne pathogen that was [[bioengineering|engineered]] by the [[Ithorian]] [[scientist]] [[Baron]] [[Boolan]]<ref name="Script" /> in [[228 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> Harkening back to his upbringing in the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[religion|religious]] group, he created the virus with the intention to strip [[Force-sensitive]]s' ability to manipulate [[the Force]], forever making the Force free. Boolan brought his virus to the [[Yarvellian Institute]] [[medcenter]] on [[Protobranch]], touting it as his cure for the [[Blight]] ravaging [[the galaxy]]. Although the virus temporarily could slow the progress of the Blight, the effect was fleeting.<ref name="Script" />

As the [[Anacondan]] [[Doctor|physician]] [[Gino'le|Dr. Gino'le]] administered the "cure" to a [[Unidentified Lasat man|Lasat patient]], Boolan triggered a [[computer virus]] to disrupt internal systems throughout the medcenter. Boolan expected the [[Lasat]] to easily transmit the virus to the [[Jedi]] observing the procedure without the man being harmed, but the Ithorian did not recognize that the [[Living Force]] resided in all beings, and that stripping the Living Force from someone is invariably [[death|fatal]].<ref name="Script" />

As the Lasat deteriorated, he transmitted the virus to Boolan himself. Boolan realized his mistake and hoped to engineer an antidote, but quickly fell unconscious from the infection. In a desperate move, [[bounty hunter]] [[Lourna Dee]] placed the quarantined infected and herself in [[stasis field]]s, keeping them in alive for a [[standard month|month]] until an antidote was reverse engineered by Yarvellian Institute scientists.<ref name="Script" />
