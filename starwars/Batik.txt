'''Batik''' was an [[Ithorian]] [[bounty hunter]] and a member of the team of [[Gorben Frak]], who was hired by the [[Droid Gotra]] to get the [[Phylanx Redux Transmitter]]. The group found it on board the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', but the [[Parapa Cartel]] attacked them. Batik was [[Death|killed]] by a member of the Parapa Cartel.<ref name="Last Shot" />
