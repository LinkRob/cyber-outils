'''Bane Back Spiders''' were large [[spider]]s [[Homeworld|native]] to various regions of the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]]. They were able to project their [[poison]]ous saliva to ward off threats. Shortly before [[14 BBY]], the spiders were domesticated by the [[Nightbrothers]], a group of [[Soldier|warriors]] who were managed and led by the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Dathomiri clan|clan]] of [[Dathomirian]] [[witch]]es. During his [[secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order]] in 14 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] [[Mission to Dathomir (Cal Kestis)|encountered]] several Bane Back Spiders on Dathomir.

==Biology and appearance==
[[Homeworld|Native]] to<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Dathomir]], Bane Back Spiders were large [[spider|arachnids]] that lived in the holes of the [[swamp]]s and cliffs of the planet. They possessed eight [[Limb|legs]] and a [[Color|dark]], [[armor]]ed exoskeleton, under which was a green body with bulbous protrusions. Each leg had two [[claw]]s on its bottom, and four fangs were located below each of their four black [[eye]]s, which also had claws present at the ends. The spiders were known to skitter and climb with agility. Above their eyes, the spiders had a green sack with gray claws that held<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> corrosive,<ref name="Bespin">{{EncyclopediaCite|Bespin and Other Planets in the Outer Rim}}</ref> [[poison]]ous saliva. Upon their [[death]], the spiders secreted the saliva in the form of an [[acid]]. A group of Bane Back spiders was called a "swarm."<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==Behavior==
The spiders were hostile to most [[Sentience|sentient]] lifeforms. When threatened, they were capable of quick, swift attacks and took control of their agility, jumping back short distances. They could<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> spit<ref name="Bespin" /> their saliva from the green sack at enemies up to twelve [[meter]]s. They could be domesticated, but the task was occasionally deadly. Bane Back Spiders could bond with an owner if it was raised from youth.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==History==
[[File:DeadBBSandNB.png|thumb|left|300px|A dead Bane Back Spider and its Nightbrother owner]]
In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[Nightsisters]], a [[Dathomiri clan|clan]] of [[Dathomirian]] [[witch]]es, were [[Battle of Dathomir|wiped out]] by [[General]] [[Grievous]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. This left the [[Nightbrothers]], a group of Dathomirian [[Gender|male]] [[Soldier|warriors]] under the leadership of the Nightsisters, without the [[magick]]s and abilities of the Nightsisters.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> Shortly before [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]]. The [[Star Wars video games|game]] states that the [[Nightbrothers]] had recently began domesticating Bane Back Spiders prior to its events. Therefore, the Nightbrothers' domestication of the [[spider]]s must have taken place shortly before that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the Nightbrothers begun efforts to domesticate the Bane Back Spider. Without the Nightsisters' magickal antivenoms, they occasionally ran into issues. During one accident located in the [[Halls of the Nydak]] region, a failure in domestication led to the death of both a Nightbrother and a Bane Back Spider. The spiders that were domesticated lived with their Nightbrother owners in their [[Unidentified Nightbrother village|village]]. However, the Nightbrothers had numerous encounters with dangerous and non-domesticated swarms of Bane Back Spiders.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
During a second visit to Dathomir, Kestis encountered the bodies of the dead Nightbrother and Bane Back Spider from the domestication accident in the Halls of the Nydak, which he had his [[BD explorer droid|droid]] [[BD-1]] scan.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
{{EndGame}}

==Bane Back Spiders in the galaxy==
By 14 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Haxion Brood]] crime syndicate had numerous Bane Back Spiders from Dathomir kept within the [[Prison|holding cells]] of the [[asteroid]] [[space station]] [[Ordo Eris]]' [[Gelid Dungeon]] for use within the [[gladiator]]ial fights of the [[Haxion Fight Pit]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> In 14 BBY,<ref name="Date" /> during his [[secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] [[Mission to Dathomir (Cal Kestis)|traveled]] to Dathomir where he encountered many Bane Back Spiders. Not long afterward, Kestis was [[Second mission to Zeffo|captured]] by the Haxion Brood and [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] within Ordo Eris. During his subsequent escape, he fought against several Bane Back Spiders in the Haxion Fight Pit.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bane Back Spiders appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> In early concept art drawn by [[Bruno Werneck]], the animals to accompany the Nightbrothers were originally [[dog]]-like creatures with spikes on their backs.<ref name="AoJFO">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> [[Bane Back spider]]s originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were created for the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies]]'', developed by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment.<ref name="Star Wars Galaxies">{{SWGcite|exp=NGE|type=location|Greater Misty Falls|Dathomir}}</ref>
