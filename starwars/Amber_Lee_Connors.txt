'''Amber Lee Connors''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice actress]] who narrated the audiobook for [[2023]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]''<ref name="Quest for Planet X">[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X (audiobook)|''The High Republic: Quest for Planet X'' audiobook]]</ref> and the [[2024]] young adult novels ''[[The High Republic: Defy the Storm]]''<ref name="Defy the Storm">[[The High Republic: Defy the Storm (audiobook)|''The High Republic: Defy the Storm'' audiobook]]</ref> and ''[[The High Republic: Tears of the Nameless]]''<ref name="Tears of the Nameless">[[The High Republic: Tears of the Nameless (audiobook)|''The High Republic: Tears of the Nameless'' audiobook]]</ref>
