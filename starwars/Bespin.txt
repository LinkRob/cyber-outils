'''Bespin''' was a [[gas giant]] in the [[Bespin system|star system]] of the same name in the [[Anoat sector]], its riches manifested in the form of rare [[tibanna]] [[gas]]. Attracting [[mining]] interests and remaining unaffiliated in [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[Politics|affairs]], [[Ugnaught]]s were employed to extract tibanna from [[repulsorlift]]ed facilities whilst Bespin's mostly [[human]] population resided in the open upper levels, exposed to a thin layer of breathable [[atmosphere]] in an area known as a "[[Life Zone]]."

==Description==
[[File:StarWars2020-9-Sprouse-textless.png|thumb|left|170px|The ''Millennium Falcon'' approaches Cloud City, a floating settlement on the gas giant of Bespin.]]
Bespin was an immense [[gas giant]] located in the vicinity of the [[Anoat sector]], a desolate [[sector]] of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="sweBE" /> Bespin had two [[moon]]s,<ref name="CL2016" /> and its [[gas]]eous mass contained a thin stratum of habitable [[atmosphere]] above a source of rare<ref name="sweBE" /> and costly [[tibanna]] gas, although there was no habitable land or [[water]] on the planet.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> The [[planet]] completed a full [[orbit]] around its [[star]] every 14 [[Standard year|standard-years]] and a full rotation every 12 [[Standard hour|standard-hours]]. Viewed from the upper levels of [[Cloud City]], in the breathable layer of the world, sunsets lasted 2 hours.<ref name="CL2016" />

Tibanna gas was harvested and refined in several [[mining]] complexes, including Cloud [[City]], an opulent metropolis suspended in the planet's huge billowing [[Water|clouds]], and [[Chinook Station]], a [[fuel depot]] and refueling station. The [[Ugnaught]] city of [[Ugnorgrad]] also was located over the planet, being ruled by [[King]] [[Ozz (Ugnaught)|Ozz]] in the [[time]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="sweBE" /> Cloud City processed tibanna to be sold off-world.<ref name="VE" />

Bespin was home to many types of life, which was rare for a gas giant. The planet had two sister worlds, from which it was separated by an asteroid belt called Velser's Ring.<ref name="CL2016" />

Swimming pools were considered a rare luxury on Bespin.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>

==History==
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[General]] [[Grievous]] led [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] forces and invaded several planets and [[Outer Rim blockades|blockaded]] several vital [[hyperspace route]]s leading to Bespin, [[Kessel]], and [[Mon Cala]]mari within the [[Realspace|space]] of a single [[Standard month|month]].<ref name="The Separatists">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Separatists}}</ref> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Bespin remained unaffiliated, but that did not prevent the [[Galactic Empire]] from [[garrison]]ing the planet and seizing Cloud City. However, with the Empire's [[Battle of Endor|defeat]] at [[Endor]], the gas giant and its prosperous mining colony ended up recovering their freedom.<ref name="sweBE" />

Following the [[Battle of Exegol|Battle of]] [[Exegol]]<ref name="TROS Junior Novel">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> in [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' as taking place one [[Standard year|year]] after the "[[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]]." As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the Starkiller Incident to [[34 ABY]], it can be deduced the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' take place in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> Bespin was in [[Uprising on Cloud City|open rebellion]] against the [[First Order]].<ref name="TROS Junior Novel" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bespin first appeared in the [[1980]] film {{Film|V}},<ref name="ESB" /> although it was first mentioned in the magazine [[Star Wars Official Poster Monthly 18|''Star Wars Official Poster Monthly'' 18]] as part of a preview of the second installment of the [[Star Wars saga|''Star Wars'' saga]]. The issue was published in [[March]], [[1979]].<ref name="Official Poster Monthly 18">[[Star Wars Official Poster Monthly 18|''Star Wars Official Poster Monthly'' 18]]</ref> The [[Star Wars Sequel, from The Adventures of Luke Skywalker|first draft]] of ''The Empire Strikes Back'', includes a beautiful, jewel-like planet known as '''Besspin Kaalieda'''.<ref name="TSWA">''[[The Star Wars Archives: Episodes IV–VI, 1977–1983]]''</ref>

Both the [[Encyclopedia (StarWars.com)|Encyclopedia]] and the [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entries for Bespin on [[StarWars.com]] says the gas giant was "surrounded by a number of moons."<ref name="sweBE" /><ref name="Bespin DB">{{Databank|bespin|Bespin}}</ref> [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], published in [[2016]], establishes that the planet had two moons.<ref name="CL2016" />
