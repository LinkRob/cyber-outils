The '''Dusty Sinkhole''' was a pit in the [[Mirogana Valley]] region on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> materials that could be used to apply a [[Color|red]] [[keffi]] paint job to a [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]] were stashed in the pit.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|canon}}
During 3 ABY,<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] retrieved the materials from the pit.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The dusty sinkhole appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The pit contained the paint job that can be used as a trophy to customize the appearance of the player's speeder bike.<ref name="Outlaws" />
