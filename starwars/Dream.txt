'''Dreams''' were images, thoughts, and feelings, a phenomena experienced while a being was [[sleep]]ing. Particularly disturbing dreams were known as '''nightmares'''. [[Force-sensitive|Force users]] sometimes had [[Force vision]]s of the future during dreams, but the content and purpose of these were often poorly understood.

==History==
{{Quote|Dreams pass in time."<br />"I'd much rather dream about Padmé.|Obi-Wan Kenobi to Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>}}
As a [[Slavery|slave]] in [[Tatooine]], [[Anakin Skywalker]] dreamed of becoming a [[Jedi]].<ref name="Episode I" /> About ten years later, though [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] claimed dreams pass in [[time]], Skywalker experienced emotionally powerful Force visions while he dreamed.<ref name="Episode II" /> Shortly before the [[death]] of both his [[Shmi Skywalker Lars|mother]] and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|wife]], Anakin experienced visions of their deaths.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> The [[Behavioral modification biochip|inhibitor chips]] implanted in the [[clone trooper]]s to force them to carry out [[Order 66]]<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref> had the side effect of giving them indistinct nightmares about a never-ending "mission" relating to the command long before the chips were activated.<ref name="Conspiracy">{{TCW|Conspiracy}}</ref> Around [[9 BBY]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] had dreams of his former life as a Jedi while he was in exile in Tatooine.<ref name="Part I">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref> In [[5 BBY]], [[Bix Caleen]] was interrogated and tortured by [[ISB Supervisor]] [[Dedra Meero]] and [[Doctor]] [[Gorst]] in the [[Hunt for Cassian Andor|search]] for leads to [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], though the doctor appeared in Caleen's nightmares and [[hallucination]]s in the couple years following her torture.<ref name="Andor One Year Later">{{Andor|One Year Later}}</ref><ref name="Andor Ever Been to Ghorman">{{Andor|Ever Been to Ghorman?}}</ref><ref name="Andor I Have Friends Everywhere">{{Andor|I Have Friends Everywhere}}</ref>
