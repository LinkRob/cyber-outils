'''Arok''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Hutt]] that led [[Besadii]] kajidic of the [[Grand Hutt Council|Five Hutt families]] and their [[Hutt Clan|crime syndicate]] during the [[Clone Wars]], along with [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]], [[Gorga Desilijic Aarrpo|Gorga]], [[Oruba]], and [[Marlo]].

==Biography==
[[File:HuttGrandCouncil-EP.png|thumb|left|200px|Arok discussing Ziro's release.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], Arok was a member of the [[Besadii]], a [[Hutt]] [[kajidic]].<ref name="AbEverything" /> Arok was smoking when he answered [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba's]] summoning of the [[Grand Hutt Council]] becoming the third member of the council to have his [[hologram]] appear in Jabba's [[Boba Fett's Palace#Throne room|throne room]]. Speaking in Ancient [[Huttese]], Arok addressed of need to release [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]] from [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] custody with Jabba and the other council members. When he was not speaking, Arok continued to smoke during the meeting. He asked Jabba if Ziro would be brought to the council after he was freed to which Jabba confirmed. Arok agreed with Jabba and the other council members to have [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] break Ziro out of Republic custody and deliver him to the council.<ref name="EP" />

Arok was present when Ziro was brought before the Hutt Council on the planet [[Nal Hutta]] after being freed by Bane. When Ziro stated he possessed a [[holodiary]] detailing all of the council's deeds, Arok groaned and became agitated. Speaking in [[Huttese]], he threatened Ziro that they could kill him. After Ziro had been taken away to the detention block, Marlo and the rest of the council were entrained by a performance by [[Pa'lowick]] singer [[Sy Snootles]]. Arok chuckled as Snootles danced her way past him and along the other council members.<ref name="HFZ" />

[[File:GardullaArokandGorga.png|thumb|right|200px|Arok, with Gardulla and Gorga speaking to Cad Bane.]]
After Snootles' performance, Arok was enjoying the entertainment of the [[Twi'lek]] dancers when [[Jedi Master]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Quinlan Vos]] entered the council chamber. Arok did not speak when Kenobi informed the council of Ziro's escape.<ref name="HFZ" />

A short time later, Arok accompanied [[Gardulla Besadii the Elder|Gardulla]], [[Gorga Desilijic Aarrpo|Gorga]] and Cad Bane to the detention block where they found Ziro's cell empty. Arok suggested the Jedi were the ones who broke Ziro out to which Gardulla agreed with but Bane disagreed. When Bane stated he was done working for the Hutts, Arok became alarmed and agreed with his fellow Hutts to have Bane track down Ziro.<ref name="HFZ" />

Two [[19 BBY|years later]],<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|514}}</ref> a [[Sith]] named [[Maul]], his [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] [[Savage Opress]] and the leader of [[Death Watch]], [[Pre Vizsla]], arrived in the Hutt Council chambers, requesting their allegiance and their territories in exchange for their lives being spared. Arok, Marlo and Oruba were the only Hutts present, while Gorga and Jabba attended by [[hologram]]. The Hutt council laughed at the Sith's demands and summoned numerous [[bounty hunter]]s to attack the intruders. During the skirmish, one of the bounty hunters was thrown into Marlo, which Arok was shocked by because the intruders proved to be more powerful than the bounty hunters.<ref name="Eminence" /> When Maul and his forces retreated outside, Arok and Marlo managed to escape, but left Oruba behind, leading to his [[death]].<ref name="Eminence">{{TCW|Eminence (episode)}}</ref>

Later on, Arok was a guest of [[Prime Minister]] [[Lom Pyke]] on [[Oba Diah]]. Along with Gorga, Arok was present when [[Anakin Skywalker]] and Obi-Wan Kenobi arrived to speak to Lom about [[Silman]] and [[Sifo-Dyas]].<ref name="TLO">{{TCW|The Lost One}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Arok was a [[Gender|male]] Hutt of the Besadii family, who was 2.09 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]], 10 inches) tall.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> He had [[Color|orange]] [[eye]]s and blue-yellow skin.<ref name="HFZ" /> Arok was one of the leading members of the Five Hutt Clans and a representative of the Hutt Council.<ref name="EP" /> He had many bad habits including smelly [[Sakiyan]] cheroots, much to the annoyance of the other Hutt council members.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Just like the rest of the Hutt Council, Arok at first ordered [[Maul]], [[Savage Opress]] and [[Death Watch]] to be killed. However, he later submitted to their wishes.<ref name="Eminence" />

==Skills and abilities==
As a member of the Hutt Council, Arok was highly experienced in persuasion, intimidation and leadership.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
Arok was voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]].<ref name="EP" /> Arok's voice and appearance was based on well-known American gangster actor [[Wikipedia:Edward G. Robinson|Edward G. Robinson]].
