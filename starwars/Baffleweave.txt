'''Baffleweave''' was a material used to jam [[sensor]]s. The material was thin and flexible enough to be used for [[clothing]], such as the dress worn by [[Bazine Netal]] thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="DB" /> [[Dryden Vos]] wore a [[Color|black]] baffleweave [[cape]], which was difficult for [[Weapon detector/Canon|weapons scanners]] to penetrate.<ref name="Solo Guide" />
