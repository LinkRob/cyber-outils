'''Fori's team''' was a team of [[Pirate|raiders]] led by the [[Twi'lek]] [[Fori Nagor]] that was formed<ref name="Closed Fist" /> at some point no earlier than [[379 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> and served [[Marda Ro]]&mdash;the [[Evereni]]<ref name="Closed Fist" /> founder of a group of pirates who would eventually become the [[Nihil]].<ref name="GrattonInterview">{{YouTube|video=llnvoEG0CjE|text=Interview with Tessa Gratton: "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" (TALES OF LIGHT & LIFE)|channel=@FriendsOfTheForce|name=[[Friends of the Force]]|date=2023-10-06}}</ref> At some point, the team was involved in a firefight with [[Star system|system]] security and during the team's escape two of its members were [[Death|killed]]. After the incident, Ro took<ref name="Closed Fist" /> her<ref name="Temptation">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', away from known systems so her pirates could lie low.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

Sometime later&mdash;while the Evereni [[Faers|twins]] [[Vika Faer|Vika]] and [[Velya Faer]] were staying with the raiders&mdash;a feast was thrown on the ''Gaze Electric'', and a [[Unidentified team member|member]] of the team baked [[finger cake]]s for the occasion. Later, Ro mentioned the team in a [[Marda Ro's journal|recording]] she made for her [[Ro family|descendants]].<ref name="Closed Fist" /> By [[252 BBY]],<ref name="ShallaDate">"The Lie" chapter in [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]], which includes [[Shalla Ro]]'s [[death]], features [[Asgar Ro]]'s ascension to [[Eye of the Nihil]], an event dated to [[252 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'' establishes she introduced [[Marchion Ro]] to [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]], he must have listened to it by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> during his youth, Ro's [[Gender|great-great-grandson]] [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to her journal, including the message where Fori's team was mentioned.<ref name="Temptation" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fori's team was mentioned in the short story "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]," written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Closed Fist" />
