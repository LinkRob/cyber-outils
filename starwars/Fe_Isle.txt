'''Fe Isle''' was a [[Kessarine]] [[Gender|male]] who was the child of [[Ferize]], [[Er Dal]], and [[Fel Ix]]. When he was born<ref name="POD" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> he was put through a [[Naming ceremony]] that inducted him into the [[Path of the Open Hand]], a [[Religion|cult]] that his parents were a part of. That same year, the [[Path compound]] on [[Dalna]] where they all lived suffered massive flooding, and Fe Isle was stuck with Er Dal underground, with Ferize trying to get to them. After their friend and fellow Path member [[Marda Ro]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Kevmo Zink]] rescued Er Dal and Fe Isle's sibling [[Fe Remine]], Fe Isle was lost in the water. Zink managed to find him using the light of his [[lightsaber]] underwater, and gave Fe Isle to Ro to escape with while he held back the water with [[The Force]]. Luckily, Fe Isle and his entire family survived the disaster.<ref name="POD" />
