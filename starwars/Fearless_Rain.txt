The '''''Fearless Rain''''' was a [[starship]] owned by the [[Nihil]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. After the ''[[Brightbird]]'' exited out of [[hyperspace]], the ''Fearless Rain'' contacted them and told the [[pilot]], [[Cair San Tekka]], to leave immediately. Tekka refused, which resulted in a dogfight between the two and backup Nihil ships. The ''Brightbird'' managed to evade the ''Fearless Rain'' by descending to the [[Arli's moon|moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Arli]].<ref name="Defy the Storm" />
