{{Quote|Gone are the gargantuan trees of old and now a smaller species—known as Batuu trees—fill in much of the planet surface.|[[Eloc Throno]], ''the Traveler's Guide to Batuu''|<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />}}
The '''Batuu tree''' was a tree [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] Batuu.<ref name="GEStaff">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] has official backstory elements that, though not directly published for the public, are officially canon details that can be obtained verbally from cast members working in the land.</ref> They were dark green conifers that had robust roots which dug into the rocky soil and clung hard to the stony earth. They also served as home to the common [[Terra tree toad]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> that were heard chirping from the trees.<ref name="GE">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge]]''</ref>
[[File:Black-Spires-SWGE.jpg|thumb|A petrified ancestor of the Batuu tree]]
The tree's distant ancestor once stood tall over the landscape. After the cataclysm which petrified them like the [[Black Spire]]<ref name="GEStaff" />, the smaller Batuu tree filled its gap in the ecosystem.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />
