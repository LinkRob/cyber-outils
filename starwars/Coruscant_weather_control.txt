A '''weather control''' system manipulated the weather of [[Imperial City|Galactic City]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], the [[capital]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] and later the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> Coruscant thus had an artificial, temperate climate.<ref name="Coruscant DB">{{Databank|coruscant|Coruscant}}</ref>

==Description==
===Climate solution===
[[File:Galactic_Senate.png|thumb|right|300px|The weather of Coruscant was artificially maintained]]
Coruscant's relatively far [[orbit]] from its small [[Coruscant Prime|sun]] posed a not particularly habitable climate for many [[species]], although the use of technology and the vast amount of heat generated by the multi-leveled city overcame this issue. However, the troposphere-piercing buildings that covered the galactic capital's [[Level 5127|surface]] affected its weather patterns to the extent of creating unusual and unpredictable microclimates through large, dichotomous differences in temperature and air pressure between the higher and lower altitudes within the tallest of buildings.<ref name="CL2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

===Air of the wealthy===
The air in the [[city]]'s upper levels was filtered, with the rich air being breathed by wealthy citizens of the [[Core Worlds|Core World]]. However, the [[Coruscant Underworld|Coruscant undercity]], buried by the constant upwards growth of [[skyscraper]]s and the resulting addition of megastructure levels to the sprawling [[Ecumenopolis|planetary metropolis]], had no such system, and its inhabitants instead inhaled the<ref name="AEYNTK" /> barely breathable<ref name="CL2016" /> toxic fumes produced by [[Millennium|millennia]] of the planet-wide cityscape's vehicle and [[factory]] waste.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> Residing within their room in a low-level sector, the typical [[Coruscanti]] lived on artificial air.<ref name="CL2016" /> As remembered by [[Imperial officer]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], Coruscant's [[weather control]] was one of the many luxuries the Galactic City possessed that his [[homeworld]] of [[Eriadu]] lacked.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>

Despite the weather control, the richest of citizens in Galactic City breathed high-grade, purified [[gas]] of their preferance, and their buildings were routinely scanned for issues in relation to the purity of gas, which were handled by quality-control droid teams. Many regular visitors from off-world also chose to bring private supplies of air for their stay.<ref name="CL2016" />

==History==
{{Quote|Each life, a flame in the Force is. Beautiful. Unique. Growing and precious, it stands, to bravely cast its own small light against the darkness that would consume it. But grows, this darkness does, with each minute that Dooku continues his attacks. Stop him, we must.|Yoda uses an analogy between a growing cloud on Coruscant and all life to justify the Jedi's decision to assassinate Dooku|<ref name="DD">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>}}
[[File:AhsokaEscape.png|thumb|left|320px|A storm pours down on Coruscant]]

In the aftermath of the [[Battle of Coruscant]] during which the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] kidnapped then [[Galactic Republic]] [[Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], the weather control system was damaged, allowing the weather to become unseasonably cold at times.<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

The weather control system cleansed the [[Federal District]]'s skyscrapers during the [[Imperial Era]], leaving its towering structures shining like new before clearing clouds away.<ref name="Tarkin" /> Seen from [[Realspace|space]], the planet's hazy cloud cover only slightly dimmed the artificial lights of Coruscant.<ref name="CL2016" /> Rain also touched other areas of the Galactic City. A lightning storm took place over the [[Imperial Inspectorate HQ|Republic Center for Military Operations]] during [[Ahsoka Tano]]'s [[escape from the Republic military base]] at the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Knew Too Much">{{TCW|The Jedi Who Knew Too Much}}</ref> and a large [[Color|grey]] cloud prevailed over the [[Palace Court|Temple Precinct]] as the [[Jedi High Council]] discussed [[assassin]]ating the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Head of State]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] to end the [[The galaxy|galactic]] conflict, the Jedi [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] using an analogy between the cloud's covering of [[Coruscant Prime|Coruscant's sun]] and all life to justify the Council's decision.<ref name="DD" /> Just after the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the war and the [[Proclamation of the New Order|founding]] of the Galactic Empire, rain fell over [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] as he was admitted to the [[Grand Republic Medical Facility]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref>
