'''Dhuz''' was a [[Palandag]] [[musician]] who was part of the house band alongside fellow Palandags [[Jhat]] and [[Hhex]] for the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|Canto Casino]] in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. All three members of the band played [[f'nonc horn]] gas bags. While playing at the [[casino]], the musician wore [[Color|black]] traditional [[yekermo outer robe]]s over a brown [[Clothing|robe]] with a golden trim. The Palandag had green skin and black [[eye]]s.<ref name="TLJVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dhuz first appeared in the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] and identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> and released alongside the film on [[December 15]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/new-star-wars-the-last-jedi-books-and-more-revealed-at-sdcc-2017|text=New ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Books and More Revealed at SDCC 2017}}</ref>
