'''Disney Junior''' (launched on [[March 23]], [[2012]]) is the flagship property of Disney Channels Worldwide, a subsidiary of the Disney-ABC Television Group, which is itself an arm of [[The Walt Disney Company]]. It was created to replace the Playhouse Disney channel. Disney Junior currently airs children's programming, with its primary demographic being boys ages 2 through 7. 

Disney Junior, along with [[Disney+]], currently air ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'', series focused for preschoolers, grade schoolers, and their families.
