The '''Alderaanian Graveyard''',<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> known as the '''Graveyard of Alderaan''' to [[smuggler]]s,<ref name="Star Wars Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> was an [[asteroid field]] in the [[Alderaan system]] created by the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]]. Under the [[Galactic Empire]], the graveyard was held under a [[blockade]] until the Empire's defeat at the [[Battle of Endor]]. After the battle the [[Alderaan Flotilla]] accessed the graveyard, looking for relics of Alderaan in the asteroids.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> The flotilla, carrying the survivors of Alderaan, was gifted the remains of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], to build a station in the graveyard.<ref name="Life Debt" />
