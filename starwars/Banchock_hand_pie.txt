'''Banchock hand pies''' were a type of [[Limb|hand]] pie made with [[Color|brown]] filling that were [[Diet|edible]] to [[human]]s. The pastries originated from the [[planet]] [[Synjax]], where they were considered a delicacy.<ref name="PATS" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'' to [[4 ABY]].</ref> following [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[General]] [[Han Solo]]'s [[Wedding of Han Solo and Leia Organa|wedding]], they enjoyed their honeymoon aboard the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|Star Cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''. While visiting the [[Crown of Corellia Dining Room]], Solo asked a [[Unidentified server (Banchock hand pie)|server]] what the hand pies were. The server dutifully informed him that the pastries were banchock hand pies from Synjax, procured to feature the local cuisine of the locations their cruise would be visiting.<ref name="PATS" />

Solo ordered for six to be boxed up for him, and the server whisked away without reacting, despite the extravagance of the order.<ref name="PATS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Banchock hand pies appeared in ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'', a [[2022]] novel written by [[Beth Revis]].<ref name="PATS" />
