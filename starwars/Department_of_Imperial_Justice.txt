The '''Department of Imperial Justice'''<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> was a bureaucratic department of the [[Galactic Empire]] that was active during the formative years of the new Imperial government. During this [[time]], the Department and the [[Imperial Navy]] were both interested in seeking funding for a [[warship]] that specialized in seizing operations.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

Both branches believed in the usefulness of such a [[starship]] that was capable of capturing [[smuggler]]s, [[pirate]]s, and dissidents for public trial. The program was approved and after a development cycle of three [[Standard year|years]], the [[Cantwell-class Arrestor Cruiser|''Cantwell''-class Arrestor Cruiser]] was launched.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />
