'''Ahrisa''' was a type of [[Spice (seasoning)|spicy]] [[Foodstuff|food]] that was baked and served in as a sphere. The [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[human]] [[Shmi Skywalker Lars|Shmi Skywalker]] had ahrisa in a bowl on the kitchen counter of her [[Skywalker home|house]] in the [[city]] of [[Mos Espa]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] in [[32 BBY]]. In [[4 ABY]], the human [[Jess]] [[Diet|ate]] some ahrisa in [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on Tatooine. Around [[9 ABY]], ahrisa was listed on the menus of a [[Lafete diner|diner]] on the [[desert]] world [[Lafete]] and a [[Nevarro City cafe|cafe]] in [[Nevarro City]] on the planet [[Nevarro]].

==Description==
Ahrisa was a type of [[Spice (seasoning)|spicy]], spherical, baked [[Foodstuff|food]]<ref name="VE" /> [[Diet|edible]] to [[human]]s<ref name="FACPOV" /> that was [[Color|brown]] in color with small black bits mixed in.<ref name="VE" />

==History==
[[File:Skywalker_home.png|thumb|right|300px|Shmi Skywalker had ahrisa in her kitchen on Tatooine.]]
In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}} to [[32 BBY]].</ref> a bowl containing two balls of ahrisa,<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> some [[haroun bread]], and a [[lamta]]<ref name="VE" /> sat on the kitchen counter of the [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[Shmi Skywalker Lars|Shmi Skywalker's]] [[Skywalker home|house]] in the [[city]] [[Mos Espa]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] when she hosted the [[Jedi]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of the planet [[Naboo]], and the [[Gungan]] [[Jar Jar Binks]] for dinner during a [[sandstorm]].<ref name="TPM" /> During their time together<ref name="BattleScars">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Battle Scars]]''</ref> between [[12 BBY]] and [[11 BBY]],<ref name="ScarsDate">{{BattleScarsDate}}</ref> the [[Dathomirian]] [[Merrin]] thought that the [[Keshiri]] [[Chellwinark Frethylrin]]'s skin smelt of spiced ahrisa and [[wax]] on [[Grave Thorn]] [[Plant|wood]].<ref name="BattleScars" />

While in [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on Tatooine<ref name="FACPOV" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="FACPOV Date">The main story of "[[Dune Sea Songs of Salt and Moonlight]]" takes place during the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the human servant [[Jess]] ate some ahrisa while watching [[Cooking droid|kitchen droid]]s working around her.<ref name="FACPOV" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoDate|16}}</ref> the [[bounty hunter]]s [[Boba Fett]] and [[Din Djarin]] met with [[Mandalorian]]s [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] and [[Koska Reeves]] in a [[Lafete diner|diner]] on the [[desert]]<ref name="Chap16" /> world<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Lafete]] that listed ahrisa [[Currency|costing]] 6.12 on its menu.<ref name="Chap16" /> Around that same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Mando21Date">{{MandoDate|21}}</ref> ahrisa costing 3.11 was listed on the menu of a [[Nevarro City cafe|cafe]] in [[Nevarro City]] on the planet [[Nevarro]].<ref name="Chap21" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ahrisa first appeared in the [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']],<ref name="TPM" /> which was released on [[May 19]], [[1999]].<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> In the current [[canon]], it was first identified in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'',<ref name="VE" /> in a section written by [[Cole Horton]].<ref name="SWcom">{{SW|url=news/7-things-you-didnt-know-about-star-wars-the-visual-encyclopedia|text=7 Things You Didn't Know About ''Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia''|nolive=1}}</ref> Prior to the film's release, Ahrisa was first pictured in the "[[Prequel Update]]" section of the [[Star Wars Insider 35|thirty-fifth]] issue of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'',<ref name="Insider35">{{InsiderCite|35|Prequel Update}}</ref> published in [[September]] [[1997]].<ref name="InsiderDate">While the credits for [[Star Wars Insider 35|''Star Wars Insider'' 35]] list a Winter [[1997]] publication date, "The Editor's Desk" welcome in [[Star Wars Insider 34|''Star Wars Insider'' 34]] confirms that issue 35 was published in [[September]] 1997. The issue 35 feature article "[[The Jedi Sandstorm]]" and the advertisement on the inside back cover further confirm that issue 35 was published after the release of the book ''[[Star Wars: The Annotated Screenplays]]'', which Amazon [https://www.amazon.com/Star-Wars-Screenplays-Laurent-Bouzereau/dp/0345409817 dates] to [[September 8]], 1997.</ref>

[[Ahrisa/Legends|Ahrisa]] originally received its name in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first identified in the 1999 reference book ''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]]<ref name="TPMVD">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and released on [[May 26]], 1999.<ref name="Amazon">{{CollectorCite|6|Books with Bite}}</ref> In ''Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia'', one ball of ahrisa is erroneously labeled as haroun bread.<ref name="VE" />
