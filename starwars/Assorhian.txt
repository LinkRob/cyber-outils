The '''Assorhians''' were the native [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Species/Legends|species]] of [[Assorhan]].<ref name="Casus86" />

==Biology and appearance==
The Assorhians were a sentient species divided into two races: the black-skinned [[Ossapohan]]s and the white-skinned [[Assopohan]]s.<ref name="Casus86" />

==Society and culture==
The Assorhians ruled the [[Assorhian Kingdom]]. The race of each Assorhian determined their position in society: the black-skinned Osoppohans were the ruling caste, while the white-skinned Asoppohans were the [[Slavery/Legends|slave]] caste.<ref name="Casus86" />
