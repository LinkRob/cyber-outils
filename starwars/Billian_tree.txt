'''Billian trees''' were a type of [[tree]] that could be found on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. The [[plant]]s featured [[flutestem]]s, which whistled in the world's wind when breezy. By the [[time]] of the [[High Republic Era]], a grove of the trees grew in the greenspace of the world's [[Monument Plaza]], which was located near the [[Umate]] [[mountain]] peak.<ref name="LOTJ" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] visited Monument Plaza with her retinue and followed a path curving around a grove of billian trees, noticing their flutestems whistling in the wind.<ref name="LOTJ" />
