During a [[Sith war (Old Republic)|war]] against the [[Jedi Order]], the [[Sith]] took control of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], [[capital]] world of the [[Galactic Republic]]. During their occupation the Sith constructed a [[Shrine in the Depths|shrine]] over<ref name="NoP" /> a [[sacred spire]],<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> a powerful [[Light side of the Force|light side]] [[vergence]] on the planet.<ref name="NoP" />

The Jedi laid a '''siege''' to the shrine, burning it to its foundations and defeating the Sith.<ref name="NoP" /> The [[Four Masters]]<ref name="CL" /> would eventually construct a [[Imperial Palace|temple]] above the former site of the shrine to contain the dark energy emanating from<ref name="Tark">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> the corrupted vergence and metaphorically bury the Sith legacy.<ref name="NoP" />
