A [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], the '''''Defender''''' served as part of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Navy|naval branch]], in the [[Open Circle Fleet]], during the [[Clone Wars]]. Under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], the [[Star Destroyer]] was incorporated into the [[Jedi Knight]]'s [[Skywalker's fleet|fleet]] by the [[time]] of [[21 BBY]]. In that [[Standard year|year]], the ''Defender'' and Skywalker's [[fleet]], which also consisted of the ''Venator''-class Star Destroyers ''[[Resolute]]''&mdash;Skywalker's [[flagship]]&mdash;and ''[[Redeemer]]'', were sent to [[Battle of Ryloth|attack]] and [[Fall of the Ryloth blockade|break through]] a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Ryloth blockade|blockade]] over the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]].

Following their arrival, the ''Defender'' and the accompanying Venators were lured into a trap designed by the [[blockade]]'s commander, [[Captain]] [[Mar Tuuk]]. Amid the engagement, the [[Neimoidian]] Separatist ordered additional reinforcements into the [[Ryloth system]], catching the Republic off guard and forcing the three Venators to retreat to [[hyperspace]], but not before the ''Redeemer'' was destroyed. After its escape, the ''Defender''{{'s}} personnel were evacuated and transferred to the ''Resolute'', as Skywalker planned to assume command over the vessel. Returning to Ryloth, Skywalker, after offering his false surrender to the Confederacy, was able to destroy Tuuk's [[Unidentified Lucrehulk-class battleship (Ryloth)|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship]] by sending the ''Defender'' crashing into it, allowing for the ''Resolute''{{'s}} arrival and the destruction of the remaining Separatist [[warship]]s.

==Description==
{{Quote|Boost the engines, and let's get out of here.|Anakin Skywalker, to R2-D2|<ref name="Storm" />}}
[[File:DefenderBridge.png|thumb|left|300px|The bridge of the ''Defender'']]
A member of the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], the ''Defender'' was active in the [[Republic Navy|Galactic Republic Navy]],<ref name="Storm" /> stretching to 1,137 [[meter]]s in length, 548 meters in width and 268 meters in height.<ref name="Size" /> Doubly used as a [[battleship]] and [[starfighter]] [[carrier]],<ref name="Venator" /> the [[Star Destroyer]] possessed separated [[Bridge (starship)|command bridges]] on its [[conning tower]]s,<ref name="Storm" /> the port-mounted one of which for starfighter flight control and the other for overall command.<ref name="Bridges">{{BuildFalconCite|35|Starship Fact File}} states the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer's]] [[Bridge (starship)|bridges]] were for [[starfighter]] flight control and general command.</ref> The command suite inside the bridge housed a single [[Holotable|holographic table]] that could perform live transmissions with other [[Starship|vessels]], and two [[Color|blue]] translucent tactical display [[Viewscreen|screens]] and work stations on either side of the table. Connected via [[blast door]]s to the [[escape pod]] bay and bridge, the latter contained two sunken pits for crew and [[viewport]]s with an 180-degree view. Along the dorsal [[hull]] was a [[hangar]] bay that carried the ship's [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s.<ref name="Storm" />

Located at the stern,<ref name="Storm" /> the ''Defender'' had eight<ref name="Equipment" /> [[Sublight engine|sublight]]-capable<ref name="Storm" /> [[Ion engine|ion drive]] [[Engine|thrusters]],<ref name="Equipment" /> and a [[hyperdrive]] generator that allowed the vessel to quickly enter [[hyperspace]]. From a [[scomp link]] and [[astromech droid]], the engines' propulsion power could be accelerated to provide greater speeds up to ramming scales. Its [[deflector shield]]s, meanwhile, were projected from a forward-angled [[Deflector shield generator|generator]] whose functionality repelled the heavy [[turbolaser]] fire of multiple [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]] and a [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship]], and could be strengthened from the routed power of other systems.<ref name="Storm" /> But in terms of offensive measures, the ''Defender'' was armed with eight port and starboard [[DBY-827 heavy turbolaser turret]]s for inter-ship combat, two port and starboard medium dual turbolaser [[cannon]]s, fifty-two [[point-defense laser cannon]]s for anti-starfighter and [[missile]] purposes, four [[proton torpedo launcher]]s, and six high-power [[tractor beam projector]]s.<ref name="Equipment" /> Like all [[Galactic Republic]] ''Venator''-class vessels, the ''Defender'' was painted with the symbol of the [[Open Circle Fleet]], accompanied by a singular red strip on the central dorsal surface and three red livery stripes on the port and starboard wings.<ref name="Storm" />

==History==
===Assaulting the blockade===
{{Quote|Captain, are you prepared?''"<br />"''Our blockade is impenetrable. When the Republic attacks, they will get quite a surprise.''"<br />"''I'm counting on you, Captain. We cannot allow the Republic to invade this planet.|Wat Tambor and Mar Tuuk, in response to Anakin Skywalker's dispatchment to Ryloth|<ref name="Storm" />}}
[[File:DefenderResoluteRedeemer-SOR.png|thumb|right|300px|The ''Defender'' arrives in the Ryloth system with the ''Resolute'' and the ''Redeemer''.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> during the [[Clone Wars]] between the Galactic Republic and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the latter's [[Separatist Droid Army]] launched an [[Battle of Ryloth|invasion]] of the [[planet]]<ref name="Supply Lines">{{TCW|Supply Lines}}</ref> [[Ryloth]] under the leadership of [[Techno Union]] [[Foreman]] [[Wat Tambor]], which resulted in the establishment of a [[Ryloth blockade|blockade]] of Separatist [[warship]]s [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Neimoidian]] [[Captain]] [[Mar Tuuk]]. Responding to the [[blockade]] as well as the [[Twi'lek]] [[Homeworld|inhabitants']] dire situation,<ref name="Storm" /> in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the Republic sought to liberate the planet from Separatist occupation. Consequently, the ''Defender'' was dispatched alongside a [[Skywalker's fleet|fleet]] of two other ''Venator''-class Star Destroyers&mdash;the [[flagship]] ''[[Resolute]]'' and the ''[[Redeemer]]''&mdash;under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] to [[Fall of the Ryloth blockade|break the blockade]] and allow for the landing of an [[Ryloth invasion fleet|invasion fleet]] led by Jedi Generals [[Mace Windu]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] on the surface.<ref name="Storm" />

Arriving from [[hyperspace]] in the [[Ryloth system]], the ''Defender'' and the rest of the fleet began their assault against Tuuk's blockade, which was flagged by a [[Unidentified Lucrehulk-class battleship (Ryloth)|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship]] and accompanied by two ''Munificent''-class star [[frigate]]s. The Republic fleet launched [[Blue Squadron (Galactic Republic)|Blue Squadron]], comprised of [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s under the command of Skywalker's [[Padawan]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]. While the squadron engaged Tuuk's ''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship, the ''Defender'' and the two other Venators remained further back. Remaining at a distance from the Separatist blockade, Tano's [[squadron]] was able to carve a path through a swarm of Separatist [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class droid starfighters]], putting them on a direct course to Tuuk's [[command ship]].<ref name="Storm" />

[[File:Storm_Over_Ryloth.png|thumb|left|300px|The ''Defender'' (left), ''Resolute'', and ''Redeemer'' were heavily damaged in the Republic's first attack.]]
While on approach to the Lucrehulk, the Neimoidian captain ordered four additional frigates to the [[star system]] in order to reinforce his blockade. Upon their arrival, the ''Defender'', alongside its accompanying Venators, were caught off guard by the Separatists as they became overwhelmed and suffered damage from a series of [[Death|suicidal]] attacks by the vulture [[droid]]s. As a result, Republic [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]] ordered Tano and her squadron to return and assist the three Star Destroyers after their battle lines were broken by the [[droid starfighter]]s. Disobeying the order, the Padawan explained that her squadron was still able to break through the blockade, but relented shortly after when Skywalker ordered her back to the ''Resolute''. After Tano returned to the fleet, the damaged ''Defender'' and ''Resolute'' began to withdraw from the battle into hyperspace, while the ''Redeemer''{{'s}} hull was torn apart in [[Realspace|space]] amid its retreat.<ref name="Storm" />

===Return to Ryloth===
{{Quote|We have completed the scan of the enemy ship.''"<br />"''And?''"<br />"''The ship is heavily damaged. All power is diverted to the forward shields. There is only one life-form on board.|A [[Unidentified B1-series battle droid (Ryloth blockade)|B1-series battle droid]], reporting the ''Defender''{{'s}} status to Mar Tuuk|<ref name="Storm" />}}
[[File:ResoluteDefender-SoR.png|thumb|right|300px|The ''Defender'' (left), damaged after the first assault.]]
Following their retreat, the Republic began to devise an attack strategy against the blockade, while the ''Defender'', which had lost one of its command towers in the battle, had developed several fires across its hull. As the Republic fleet recuperated, Skywalker convened with Generals Kenobi and Windu regarding the situation and their losses, but he was informed by Windu that, if the blockade was not breached before the next [[Standard day|planetary rotation]], the Republic would be required to postpone its invasion, despite Skywalker's dwindled forces. After convening with his fellow Jedi, the [[Jedi Knight]] devised a plan to destroy the blockade as he ordered the evacuation of all personnel from the ''Defender'' to the ''Resolute'' in order for him to assume control over the damaged Star Destroyer alongside his astromech droid, [[R2-D2]], and prevent further Republic losses. Explaining the plan to his Padawan after she became alerted to the ''Defender''{{'s}} evacuation, Skywalker told Tano that he planned to pilot the damaged Star Destroyer into Tuuk's command ship, while the Padawan would arrive aboard the ''Resolute'' to destroy the frigates as Skywalker waited in an [[escape pod]].<ref name="Storm" />

After the ''Defender''{{'s}} personnel were transferred to the ''Resolute'', Skywalker and his astromech boarded the Star Destroyer and returned to the Ryloth system. Upon his arrival, as Tuuk had anticipated, the Neimoidian's Lucrehulk was contacted by Skywalker aboard the ''Defender'', who promised the false surrendering of the Star Destroyer and its crew to Tuuk in exchange for the safe transport of [[foodstuff]]s and medical supplies to the displaced Twi'leks on the surface. Initially in belief of Skywalker's deception, the Separatist captain was informed that the ''Defender'' was heavily damaged and the starship's power had been diverted to the forward [[deflector shield]]s, with only a single life-form aboard. As Tuuk discovered the Jedi Knight's deception, the ''Resolute'' exited from hyperspace directly behind the ''Defender'', to which Skywalker instructed R2 to increase the engine power before the two of them fled the doomed vessel aboard an escape pod.<ref name="Storm" />

[[File:Defender_Venator.png|thumb|left|300px|The ''Defender'' hurtles into Mar Tuuk's command ship.]]
As the ''Defender'' hurtled toward Tuuk's [[battleship]], the Lucrehulk's crew of [[B1-series battle droid]]s and the surrounding frigates opened fire on the Star Destroyer in an attempt to prevent its collision with their command ship. However, unable to destroy it, Tuuk proceeded to board an escape pod and left the battle droids in control of the remaining frigates as the Star Destroyer crashed into the Lucrehulk's central sphere, destroying the command ship and sending the [[OOM command battle droid]]s into a period of confusion. Following the Lucrehulk's destruction, the blockade was broken by the Republic, which allowed for the arrival of their invasion fleet of three [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class assault ships]] on the planet's surface.<ref name="Storm" /> Afterward, the Republic successfully furthered their campaign and liberated Ryloth from Separatist control.<ref name="Liberty">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref>

===Legacy===
Following the war's end,<ref name="Lords">''[[Lords of the Sith]]''</ref> in [[14 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> [[Galactic Emperor]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and his [[Sith apprentice]] [[Darth]] Vader&mdash;[[Duel in Palpatine's office|formerly]] Anakin Skywalker&mdash;[[Mission to Ryloth|traveled]] to Ryloth to cease a [[Free Ryloth Movement|growing rebellion]] led by General [[Cham Syndulla]]. During their mission, while Vader piloted an Imperial [[Vader's shuttle (Mission to Ryloth)|shuttle]], the [[Sith Lord]] recalled his memory from the Battle of Ryloth, when he had once drifted alone in an escape pod above the planet after destroying the Separatist flagship.<ref name="Lords" />

==Commanders and crew==
During the Battle of Ryloth, the ''Defender'' was placed within the fleet of Jedi General Anakin Skywalker under his command from his flagship, the ''Resolute''. It was operated by its own crew, who were evacuated from the vessel in order for Skywalker and R2-D2 to control the Star Destroyer.<ref name="Storm" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Defender'' was introduced in the opening newsreel of "[[Jedi Crash]],"<ref name="Jedi Crash">{{TCW|Jedi Crash}}</ref> the thirteenth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which aired on [[January 16]], [[2009]].<ref name="Jedi Crash Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode013.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Jedi Crash}}</ref> It later made its full appearance and was identified in "[[Storm Over Ryloth]],"<ref name="Storm" /> the first season's nineteenth episode, which aired on [[February 27]], 2009.<ref name="Storm Over Ryloth Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode019.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Storm Over Ryloth}}</ref> In "Storm Over Ryloth," one of the ''Defender''{{'s}} command bridges was removed as a result of the battle, but was restored in a subsequent scene, although it was once again removed in its next on-screen appearance.<ref name="Storm" />
