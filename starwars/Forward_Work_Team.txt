The '''Forward Work Team''' was a team that worked in the crew of [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers]]. They worked in the front of the [[battlecruiser]], as opposed to the [[Rear Work Team]] who worked in the back. A poster advertising the team was collected by [[Poe Dameron]] in a [[Unidentified book|vehicle guidebook]].<ref name="Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Forward Work Team was mentioned in the [[2022]] non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Awesome Vehicles]]''.<ref name="Vehicles" />
