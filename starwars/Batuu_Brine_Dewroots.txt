'''Batuu Brine Dewroots''' were a [[Foodstuff|snack provision]] served at [[Oga's Cantina]], on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]],<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The snack was served with [[Galma Garlic Puffed Cheese Chips|Galma Garlic Puffs]] and was edible by [[human]]s<ref name="Oga's Cantina" />

==Behind the scenes==
Batuu Brine Dewroots first appeared as a snack provision available to guests at the [[In-universe|real-world]] Oga's Cantina in the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]] in late [[May]], [[2025]].<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/dinner|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Dinner Menu}}</ref><ref name="Yelp Review">{{WebCite|url=https://www.yelp.com/biz/ogas-cantina-anaheim-3|text=Oga's Cantina|author=Matthew S.|date=2025-05-21|format=Comment|work=[https://www.yelp.com Yelp]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/ExzTc}}</ref> The menu described it as "[[Wikipedia:Cucumber|Cucumbers]], [[Wikipedia:Chili oil|Chile Oil]], Crispy [[Wikipedia:Shallot|Shallots]], [[Galma Garlic Puffed Cheese Chips|Galma Chips]]."<ref name="Menu" />
