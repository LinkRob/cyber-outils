'''Cocktail Umbrella''' was a small parasol often used as a garnish for decorative [[Foodstuff|mixed drinks]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Ugnaught]] [[smuggler]] [[Jet Kordo]] had stashed a Cocktail Umbrella in the hidden back room of his [[Jet Kordo's vault|vault]] in the [[Lost Steppe]] region of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[BX-series droid commando]] [[ND-5]] analyzed six [[Hologram disc|holo discs]] belonging to Kordo and created a new disc to unlock the back room of the vault. His companion, the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]], later used the newly created holo disc to open the back room and collect its contents, including the Cocktail Umbrella.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cocktail Umbrella appeared as a reward for completing the "Jet Kordo Legacy" in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, when the umbrella is equipped as a major charm it counts as any gear set for the purpose of unlocking gear set bonuses.<ref name="Outlaws" />
