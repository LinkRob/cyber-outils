{{Quote|The lack of sleep, the depression, and the fact that I hadn't eaten anything other than some cheese curls in the last 18 hours contributed greatly to my failing mental state.|Darth Vader, writing in his diary|<ref name="Lunch" />}}
'''Cheese curls''' were a type of [[Foodstuff/Legends|food]] made with [[Cheese/Legends|cheese]] that was [[Diet/Legends|edible]] to [[Human/Legends|Humans]].<ref name="Lunch" /> [[Time/Legends|Sometime]] between [[0 ABY/Legends|0 ABY]] and [[3 ABY/Legends|3 ABY]],<ref name="Lunch date">"[[Lunch Break]]" takes place in between when [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] learns of [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker's]] name and their [[Duel on Cloud City/Legends|duel on Cloud City]]. The former event occurs in [[Vader's Quest 1|''Vader's Quest'' 1]], which takes place zero [[Standard year/Legends|years]] after {{Film|IV}} and corresponds to [[0 ABY/Legends|0 ABY]], per ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''. ''The New Essential Chronology'' also dates the [[Lightsaber duel/Legends|duel]] on [[Cloud City/Legends|Cloud City]] to [[3 ABY/Legends|3 ABY]]. Therefore, "Lunch Break" must have taken place in between those two [[Standard year/Legends|years]].</ref> the Human [[Dark Lord of the Sith/Legends|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] ate only cheese curls in the eighteen [[Standard hour/Legends|hours]] prior to the [[Standard day/Legends|night]] of his [[Birthday/Legends|birthday]]. Vader experienced a deterioration in his mental state that night, as none of his [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]] had remembered the occasion. When writing about the incident in his [[Vader's diary|diary]], the [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] partially attributed his failing mental state to having only eaten cheese curls, along with a lack of [[Sleep/Legends|sleep]] and depression.<ref name="Lunch" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cheese curls were mentioned in "[[Lunch Break]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] written and illustrated by [[Jonathan Adams]] and released in [[Star Wars Tales 16|''Star Wars Tales'' 16]],<ref name="Lunch" /> published on [[June 25]], [[2003]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/53994/star_wars_tales_1999_16|text=Star Wars Tales (1999) #16}}</ref>
