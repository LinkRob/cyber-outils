The '''abandoned dump shack''' was a former dwelling which was located on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Pagodon]] in the settlement [[Ferryman's Reach]].<ref name="MandoVG" /> By [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|1}} According to ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'', the abandoned dump shack is specified to be active during its appearance in the episode, which corresponds to 9 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]], thus meaning the dump shack was actively in use by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the hut had been abandoned, becoming retrofitted into a junk heap for the local community.<ref name="MandoVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The abandoned dump shack first appeared in "[[Chapter 1: The Mandalorian]],"<ref name="Chapter 1" /> the first episode of the [[The Mandalorian Season One|first season]] of the live-action [[Disney+]] show ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', which was released on [[November 12]], [[2019]].<ref name="Episode Amount">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/heat-vision/star-wars-new-mandalorian-details-footage-unveiled-1201931|text='The Mandalorian': Jon Favreau Teases Disney+ Series at Star Wars Celebration|author=Aaron Couch & Graeme McMillan|date=2018-12-10|archivedate=20191109001233}}</ref> The dump shack was first identified in the [[2026]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="MandoVG" />
