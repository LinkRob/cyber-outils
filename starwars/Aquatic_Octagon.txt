The '''Aquatic Octagon''' was an event that involved [[Siluvian Seahorse]] races and took place<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> At one point during the event, the competitor [[Lillobell]] was ahead of fellow competitor [[Kirilee]]. Passengers aboard the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]'' could place [[Gambling|bets]] on the races. An advertisement for the event was broadcast over the [[cruiser]]'s [[speaker]] system that encouraged passengers to bet on it.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Aquatic Octagon was mentioned in the "Wild Card" expansion for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]], published by [[Ubisoft]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> and released on [[November 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Wild Card released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/1Q4y5lMJwdUUt6qXzbWKPI/star-wars-outlaws-game-update-showcase-wrapup|text=Star Wars Outlaws Game Update Showcase Wrap-Up}}</ref>
