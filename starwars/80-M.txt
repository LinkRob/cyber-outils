'''80-M''',<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> also know as '''Eightyem''', was a [[protocol droid]]<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1">[[Return of the Jedi – Jabba's Palace 1|''Return of the Jedi – Jabba's Palace'' 1]]</ref> who served as the [[Translator droid|interpreter]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], a [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] who led his own [[Jabba's criminal empire|criminal empire]], until he angered his master and was disintegrated. Shortly before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]]<ref name="Beware the Power of the Dark Side!" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[EV-9D9]], the [[supervisor]] of [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace's]] droid pool, supervised the [[torture]] of the old [[droid]] when [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]], Jabba's new [[Translator|interpreter]], reported to her. EV-9D9 told C-3PO of his predecessor's fate and gestured towards the droid.<ref name="Beware the Power of the Dark Side!" /> C-3PO was horrified to witness the droid on a vertical torture rack that stretched its manacled [[limb]]s further than they could extend,<ref name="Junior novel">[[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (paperback novel)|''Star Wars Episode VI: Return of the Jedi'' junior novelization]]</ref> causing the droid to scream in pain as its arms<ref name="Return of the Jedi" /> and legs were slowly pulled off.<ref name="Beware the Power of the Dark Side!" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Eve-Ninedenine, I believe master Jabba requires a new protocol droid. Our approach was a bit extreme on the last one.''"<br />"''I thought we were restrained. He was frightfully annoying.''"<br />"''His Excellency Jabba the Hutt is prone to exaggeration, so perhaps we shouldn't be so literal next time.|[[8D8]] and EV-9D9, in "The Key to Remembering"|<ref name="The Key to Remembering">{{StoryCite|story=The Key to Remembering|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref>}}
80-M first appeared, albeit unidentified, in the [[1983]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], the final installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]].<ref name="Return of the Jedi" /> The character was later identified in a preview article on [[StarWars.com]] for the comic [[Return of the Jedi – Jabba's Palace 1|''Return of the Jedi – Jabba's Palace'' 1]], that was published on [[March 22]], [[2023]].<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1 Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-jabbas-palace-1|text=Learn the Untold Story of Jabba's Last Protocol Droid in Marvel's ''Star Wars: Jabba's Palace'' #1 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref> The [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'', later provided the droid's designation.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

[[File:New_Aquisitions_ROTJ.png|thumb|200px|right|A CZ-series droid dumped near the doorway of the torture chamber.]]
The [[2015]] young-readers novelization ''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]'' written by [[Tom Angleberger]], originally identified the droid as a separate character to the droid shown in the film and that his remains had been dumped near the door of a furnace in her [[torture]] chamber.<ref name="Beware the Power of the Dark Side!" /> A [[CZ-series secretary/business communications droid|CZ-series droid]], having been dumped besides the doorway, appears during the events of ''Return of the Jedi''.<ref name="Return of the Jedi" /> "[[The Key to Remembering]]," a short story written by [[Olivia Chadha]] as part of the 2023 anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]'', indicates Jabba's previous interpreter was melted down in a pool of [[acid]] and separately identifies the droid present on the torture rack as a [[courier droid]].<ref name="The Key to Remembering" />
