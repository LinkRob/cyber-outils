The '''Dock Worker Chair''' was the collective name of a custom [[Repulsorlift chair|repulsorlift]] [[Command Chair]] and a pair of [[Blaster Remote]]s owned by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sprocket]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Command Chair (Sprocket)|standard chair and remote set]]. The chair featured worn gray and light blue plating, with a dark blue seat. Glowing yellow lights encircled the circumference of the base, with metallic gray and bronze colored components protruding from the sides.<ref name="Kay" />

Extending from the back was a small crane, holding a net filled with colorful [[fish]], while both [[starfish]] and [[barnacle]]s can be observed attached to the back of the seat and the underside of the frame.<ref name="Kay" /> It vaguely resembled the [[Heavy industrial crane transport|HI-CT]] [[walker]]s deployed on the [[Water|estuary]] [[moon]] of [[Trask]].<ref name="Chapter 11">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Concept art for this Sprocket "Weapon Wrap" was created by [[Anna Kay]]<ref name="Kay" /> for the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', though it never made it into the final version of game,<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> only revealed after ''Star Wars: Hunters''' cancellation<ref name="Kay" /> on [[October 1]], [[2025]].<ref name="Goodbye Hunters">{{Hunters|url=news/sunset-on-vespaara|text=Sunset on Vespaara}}</ref> It was intended to serve as a set with the [[Dock Worker]] "Costume."<ref name="Kay" />
