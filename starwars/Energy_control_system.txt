A '''energy control system''' was a [[starship]] system that used a dedicated [[Droid brain|data-brain]] to optimize a ship's reactive systems to better protect a ships cargo from the effects of weapon impacts.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] paid a starship [[Technician|mechanics]] two thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and supplied them with twenty [[Cargo signature scrambler]]s in order to install the system in the hold of the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Energy control systems appeared as an upgrade in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> In the expansion, players could purchase the "energy control system" upgrade to reduce the time lost when taking damage while transporting timed cargo.<ref name="Outlaws" />
