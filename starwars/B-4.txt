'''B-4''' was a [[droid]] who served the [[Galactic Republic]], and was supposed to be assigned to [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]] to be part of [[D-Squad]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. However, B-4 was not present for the meeting.<ref name="Insider" />
