An '''ape''' was a [[humanoid]] creature related to [[primate]]s that lived in [[the galaxy]]. Ape was often used as a way to insult an individual as exemplified when [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] referred to the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] as an oversized ape,<ref name="PSFB">''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]''</ref> or when [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus]] the [[Hutt]] referred to the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] as a [[bantha]] [[brain]]ed ape.<ref name="Star Wars 35" />
