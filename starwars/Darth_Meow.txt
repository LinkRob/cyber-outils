'''Darth Meow''' was a [[cat]] who served in the [[Galactic Empire]]. While investigating a [[distress signal]] from an abandoned [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] (a trap set by [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]]), [[Sabine Wren]] was distracted by Darth Meow, allowing a [[stormtrooper]] to capture her.<ref name="LEGO" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Meow first appeared in "[[Rescue Sabine!]]," a comic featured in the magazine [[LEGO Star Wars 14|''LEGO Star Wars'' 14]]. In the [[Wikipedia:Polish language|Polish]] translation of the comic, the name is spelled "Darth Miau".<ref name="LEGO" />
