'''Deneba''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" /> in the [[Deneba system]] in the [[Maerdocian sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that housed a [[Unidentified temple (Deneba)|temple]] known to former [[Jedi Master]] [[Cohmac Vitus]].<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" /> When [[Commerce Guild]] [[scout]] [[Tibbs Ospe]] discovered the [[planet]] [[Vandor]], he figured the planet could be a stopping place for [[Merchant|traders]] heading towards Deneba and [[Manda]].<ref name="Solo Tales from Vandor">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]''</ref> By [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' dates the events on [[Kessel]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to nine years after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the formation of the Empire to [[19 BBY]], therefore the event on Kessel in ''Solo'' must take place in [[10 BBY]].</ref> Ospe fixed the ruins of [[Ypsobay Trading Company]]'s headquarters and started advertising that traders could find [[fuel]] and [[Foodstuff|food]] at [[Fort Ypso]] for their travels. At some point [[bartender]] [[Tivoche Bilure]] learned Ospe's story about Vandor and wrote it in his [[Midnight's book|journal]].<ref name="Solo Tales from Vandor" />
