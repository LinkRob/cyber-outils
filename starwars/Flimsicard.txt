'''Flimsicard '''was a substance used to make items such as boxes to hold [[Foodstuff|food]]. The dining establishment [[Sakhet's Noodles]] used flimsicard boxes to give food, such as [[Corellian buckwheat noodles]] or [[nerf nugget]]s, to their customers. In [[0 ABY]], [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Nakari Kelen]] received food from Sakhet's Noodles in flimsicard boxes.
