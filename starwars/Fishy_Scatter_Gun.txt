The '''Fishy Scatter Gun''' was a [[scatter gun]] owned by the [[Trandoshan]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Charr]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Cunning Scatter Gun|standard slugthrower]]. This [[Slugthrower|gun]] was covered in a fishnet print, with a [[fish]] [[Bone|skeleton]] attached to the top.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fishy Scatter Gun was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Charr's Cunning Scatter Gun, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 3.0.0 alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Fishy Trapper]].<ref name="Hunters" />
