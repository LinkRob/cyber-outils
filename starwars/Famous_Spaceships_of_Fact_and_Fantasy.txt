'''''Famous Spaceships of Fact and Fantasy''''' is a reference book published by [[Kalmbach Books]] in [[April]] [[1979]]. It was written by [[Harold A. Edmonson]].

The book has entries for numerous ships from multiple sci-fi franchises, including ''Star Wars''. The two ''Star Wars'' spacecraft focused on are the [[T-65 X-wing starfighter/Legends|X-Wing]] and [[TIE Advanced x1/Legends|Darth Vader's TIE Fighter]].
