{{Quote|Seriously? I'm in the prime of life, I'll have you know. Fit as the proverbial fiddle.|Tey Sirrek, to Boolan|<ref name="THR 9">[[The High Republic (2023) 9|''The High Republic'' (2023) 9]]</ref>}}
An individual could be proverbially compared with a '''fiddle''' to indicate that that they were fit.<ref name="THR 9" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-show-302-reveals|text=Burryaga's Fate and More Reveals on ''Star Wars: The High Republic Show''}} establish that the events of [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']] take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]].</ref> while attempting to [[Rescue of a Yacombe girl|rescue]] a [[Unidentified Yacombe girl|Yacombe girl]] who was being held [[prisoner]] by the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s,<ref name="THR 9" /> the [[Force-sensitive]]<ref name="THR 6">[[The High Republic (2023) 6|''The High Republic'' (2023) 6]]</ref> [[Sephi]] [[Tey Sirrek]] was captured on the Nihil [[space station]] [[Shingcu Station]]. When the Nihil<ref name="THR 9" /> [[Minister of Advancement]]<ref name="THR 6" /> [[Baron]] [[Boolan]] told his [[Aide|assistant]] [[Reek (Nihil)|Reek]]&mdash;who had captured Sirrek&mdash;that the Sephi was too old and not strong enough in [[the Force]] to be suitable for his experiments, Sirrek protested, saying that he was "fit as the proverbial fiddle."<ref name="THR 9" />

==Behind the scenes==
The phrase "fit as a fiddle" was mentioned in the [[The High Republic (2023) 9|ninth issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic series]] [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']]. The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="THR 9" /> on [[July 3]], [[2024]], as part of the [[Star Wars: The High Republic]] multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/120254/star_wars_the_high_republic_phase_iii_2023_9|text=Star Wars: The High Republic &lsqb;Phase III&rsqb; (2023) #9}}</ref> In the real world, [[Wikipedia:Fiddle|fiddle]] is another word for a [[Wikipedia:Violin|violin]],<ref name="Fiddle">{{WebCite|url=https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/fiddle|text=fiddle|work=[[Wikipedia:Merriam-Webster|Merriam-Webster]]|archivedate=20240223083257}}</ref> and "fit as a fiddle" means to be in good physical condition.<ref name="Fit">{{WebCite|url=https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/fit%20as%20a%20fiddle|text=fit as a fiddle|work=[[Wikipedia:Merriam-Webster|Merriam-Webster]]|archivedate=20240605015320}}</ref>
