'''Forks''' were eating utensils. During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], the [[Imperial cadet]] [[Nazhros Oleg]] attempted to use his fork to force fellow cadet [[Pandak Symes]] to hand over his fruit bar. However, [[Zare Leonis]] sprung to Symes' aid and knocked Oleg's fork out of his hand.<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks">''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]''</ref>
