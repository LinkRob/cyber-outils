'''Carosi Eight''' was a location<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> in the [[Carosi system]] within the [[Nembus sector]] of<ref name="Appendix" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> and [[New Territories]].<ref name="New Territories" /> It was situated in grid square P-4 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Carosi Eight was represented at a [[Celebration on Serenno|celebration]] for the [[Planet|worlds]] of the Outer Rim on the planet [[Serenno]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> in [[90 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Dooku]]'s first visit to [[Serenno]], which corresponds to the [[celebration on Serenno]] according to ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', to [[90 BBY]].</ref> While exploring the celebration, [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Dooku]] and his [[Gender|sister]]&mdash;[[Lady]] [[Jenza]] of [[House Serenno]]&mdash;[[Diet|ate]] [[lalaren bloom]]s, a [[foodstuff]] from Carosi Eight.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" />

Dooku later recounted the incident in a [[holo-letter]] to Jenza.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="Date 23">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Dooku displayed a [[Hologram|holographic]] [[Datacard|data disk]] that detailed the events around meeting his sister, including their consumption of lalaren blooms from Carosi Eight, to his recently-recruited [[Sith assassin]], [[Asajj Ventress]]. The pair listened to the recording, after which Dooku gave Ventress several more disks for her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|investigation]] into Jenza's location.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" />

==Behind the scenes==
Carosi Eight was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the audiobook ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin-Download">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-cavan-scott/9780593102817|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> Its spelling was confirmed by the [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script release]] of the audiobook<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> on [[October 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Penguin-Download" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Carosi VIII]] was first mentioned in ''[[Planets of the Galaxy, Volume Two]]'', a ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' book written by [[John Terra]] and published by [[West End Games]] in [[1992]].<ref name="PotGVII">''[[Planets of the Galaxy, Volume Two]]''</ref>
