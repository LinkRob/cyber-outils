'''Axe''' was a [[clone trooper pilot]] who served within of the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Republic Navy|naval forces']] [[Blue Squadron (Galactic Republic)|Blue Squadron]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. In [[21 BBY]], a Republic [[Skywalker's fleet|fleet]] under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] was dispatched to the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] in order to break through the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' orbiting blockade led by [[Neimoidian]] [[Captain]] [[Mar Tuuk]]. During the battle, Axe and the rest of Blue Squadron, led by Skywalker's [[Padawan]], [[Jedi Commander]] and [[Blue Leader]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], was launched against the blockade, where they were engaged by several [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class droid starfighters]].

However, after the Republic became overwhelmed following the arrival of additional Separatist reinforcements, Blue Squadron, which had lost several of its ten [[pilot]]s, was ordered to withdraw. Amid their retreat, though, Axe was [[Death|killed]] by a vulture [[droid]] before successfully returning to the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Resolute]]'' in order for the Republic to flee the [[Ryloth system|system]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Overload! Overload! Gaaahh!|Axe's final words|<ref name="Storm" />}}
[[File:AxeV19TorrentSquadron-GRFS.png|thumb|left|250px|Axe at the Battle of Ryloth]]
Like all [[clone trooper]]s serving in the [[Republic Military]], Axe was created by the [[Kaminoan]]s for the sole purpose of serving the [[Galactic Republic]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], Axe served as Blue Leader, flying a [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]] under [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] aboard the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer]], ''[[Resolute]]''.<ref name="Storm" />

In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Axe stepped aside so [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] could take the position of Squadron Commander for the first time, which led to him assuming the title of Blue Two. He and the rest of [[Blue Squadron (Galactic Republic)|Blue Squadron]] were deployed to serve in the [[Battle of Ryloth]] in an attempt to break the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Ryloth blockade]]. Under the command of Jedi Commander Ahsoka Tano, the [[squadron]] attempted to break the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[blockade]] around the [[planet]]. When the Separatist reinforcements arrived in the [[Ryloth system|system]], [[Commander]] Tano recklessly delayed the squadron's withdrawal, despite commands from [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]] to return to the Resolute immediately.<ref name="Storm" />

During the retreat, Axe took Tano's right wing while [[Swoop (clone trooper)|Swoop]] and [[Kickback]] took the forward positions. Axe's [[power converter]]s would begin to fail, and as he was trying to fix it, he was [[Death|killed]] by three [[Vulture-class starfighter|Vulture-class droid starfighters]] tailing his V-19.<ref name="Storm" />

==Personality and traits==
As a clone of Jango Fett,<ref name="ClonesData" /> Axe<ref name="Storm" /> was 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]]) tall, had [[Color|tan]] skin,<ref name="ClonesData" /> and weighed eighty [[kilogram]]s.<ref name="Weight" />

==Equipment==
At birth, like the other clone troopers, Jumpy was implanted by the [[Kaminoan]] [[scientist]]s with a [[behavioral modification biochip]] to ensure that the trooper would not resist certain [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|orders]].<ref name="ClonesData" />
