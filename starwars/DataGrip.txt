A '''DataGrip''' was a wrist gauntlet that allowed the wearer to track their health, receive transmissions, scan objects and provide orientation when the user was lost. [[Unidentified droid repair technician|A droid repair technician]] was known to wear a DataGrip as part of his job<ref name="Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge">''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge]]''</ref> around [[34 ABY]].<ref>''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref>
