'''Bohento Splinth''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Aqualish]]. Originally a [[Meteorite|meteor]] [[miner]], Splinth turned to robbery and was a [[scavenger]] by the time of the [[Iron Blockade]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|You're the scumsucker who's been hitting my salvage crews.|Bohento Splinth, confronting "Happy" Dapp's scoundrel employee|<ref name="Uprising" />}}
Prior to the [[Battle of Endor]], Bohento Splinth was acquainted with [[Dapp|"Happy" Dapp]], and the two fought together in the [[Cargamalis Street Wars]]. Some time later, Splinth became a [[Meteorite|meteor]] [[miner]], but later turned to robbery.<ref name="Uprising" />

After the Battle of [[Endor]], which saw the destruction of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] and the [[death]] of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], [[Governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] of the [[Anoat sector]] issued a [[Iron Blockade|blockade]] to restrict news of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] victory at Endor. During the [[blockade]], Bohento Splinth was on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Hoth]], where he was conducting salvage missions with his crew, the "[[Wreckers]]." At some point, his team recovered an Imperial [[codebreaker]]. "Happy" Dapp, now a [[gangster]] located on the planet [[Burnin Konn]], was looking for a way to break through Adelhard's blockade. Working together with his [[Trade Spine League]] ally, the [[Chadra-Fan]] [[Shortpaw]], as well as a [[The Smuggler|scoundrel]] forced into his service to pay a debt, Dapp discoved that Splinth had located an Imperial codebreaker&mdash;a necessary component for Dapp's plans. The gangster sent the scoundrel to Hoth to find Splinth in an effort to collect the codebreaker.<ref name="Uprising" />

[[File:Bohento_Splinth_Hoth.png|thumb|left|130px|Splinth on Hoth]]
After fighting a number of wrecker operatives, the scoundrel found Splinth atop a [[mountain]] where an [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] had fallen. Angry, Splinth confronted the scoundrel, who, in addition to his mission to find Splinth, had been conducting a number of missions on Hoth that saw confrontations with the wreckers. The scoundrel offered to purchase the codebreaker, but upon hearing the scoundrel's employer was Dapp, the Aqualish moved to attack. Dapp's employee managed to defeat Splinth, killing him in the process, and collected the codebreaker.<ref name="Uprising" />

With Splinth's death, his second, an [[Ugnaught]] called [[Ozgurk]], decided to steal his name and reputation. That fake Bohento Splinth became the leader of an army of scavengers.<ref name="Uprising" />
