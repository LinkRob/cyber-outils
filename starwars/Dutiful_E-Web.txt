The '''Dutiful E-Web''' was an [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]] owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Prototype E-Web|standard E-Web]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dutiful E-Web was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Sentinel's Prototype E-Web, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Dutiful Soldier]]. This wrap could be unlocked by purchasing it individually within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
