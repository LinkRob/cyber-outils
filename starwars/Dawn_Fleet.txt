The '''Dawn Fleet''' was a [[fleet]] created in secret by [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]], the [[Crime lord|leader]] of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate, to deploy against the [[Sith]] during her [[War against the Sith|attempts]] to destroy the reigning [[Sith Lord]]s [[Darth Sidious]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Hidden Empire 2" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Hidden Empire 2|''Hidden Empire'' 2]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> after the [[Battle against Crimson Dawn|destruction]] of her [[flagship]], the [[Vermillion (Crimson Dawn)|''Vermillion'']], Qi'ra retreated to [[Cataract Base|Dawnfall Base]] where the fleet was assembled, and informed the [[Orphans]] of her plan to unleash the fleet when the [[time]] was right.<ref name="Hidden Empire 2" /> The fleet consisted of [[Class One]], [[Class-Two]], [[Class Three]] vessels. Specific ship models within included [[Nau'ur-class yacht|''Nau'ur''-class yachts]] and several [[Unidentified Crimson Dawn transport|Dawn transport ships]].<ref name="Hidden Empire 4" />
