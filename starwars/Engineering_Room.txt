The '''Engineering Room''' was a location on board the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' that housed some of the ships's innermost systems. Although it was restricted to crew members, guests on the starship would occasionally sneak into the room.<ref name="SWGS page">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=destinations/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser}}</ref> It was located on Deck 4.<ref name="SWGS Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)|''Star Wars'': Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)]]</ref>
