'''Bursant''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Bursantian" /> in [[the galaxy]] which was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Bursantian]] [[species]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], a group of [[Jedi]] and [[senator]]s, including [[Jedi Master]]s [[Yoda]] and [[Mace Windu]], and Senator [[Yarua]], visited an [[Unidentified archaeological dig site|archaeological dig site]] on the planet.<ref name="Hyperspace Stories 11" />
