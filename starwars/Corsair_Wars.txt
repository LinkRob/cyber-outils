The '''Corsair Wars''' were a conflict in [[the galaxy]]<ref name="Aphra 33" /> that took place some [[time]] prior to [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Date note 1">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the beginning of the [[Great Jedi Purge]], which saw the destruction of the [[Jedi Order]], to [[19 BBY]]. As [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 33|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 33]] establishes that the Corsair Wars took place during a [[time]] when the [[Jedi]] Order existed, it must have taken place prior to that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> In its aftermath, the [[Jedi Master]] [[Oo'ob]] broke [[Jedi]] doctrine and recruited the [[crystallist]] [[Var-Whill]] to create the [[Farkiller]], a [[sniper rifle]] modified with [[lightsaber]] technology.<ref name="Aphra 33" />

The Corsair Wars were mentioned in [[Professor]] [[Korin Aphra]]'s "[[Notes on Jedi Heresies]]," which explained Oo'ob and Var-Whill's actions in the aftermath of the conflict. The notes were later recounted to Aphra's daughter, [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli]], by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[protocol droid]] [[TZ-2|Teezee-Too]]<ref name="Aphra 33" /> prior to the [[Battle of Hoth]] in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date note 2">The events of [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 33|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 33]] take place prior to the events of [[Empire Ascendant 1|''Empire Ascendant'' 1]], which takes place before the [[Battle of Hoth]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the Battle of [[Hoth]] to [[3 ABY]], meaning that [[TZ-2|Teezee-Too]] must have told [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] about the Corsair Wars some [[time]] prior.</ref>
