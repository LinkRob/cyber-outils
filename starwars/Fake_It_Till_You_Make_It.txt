"'''Fake It Till You Make It'''" is a short story that was published in the [[2020]] anthology book ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''. It was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and focuses on the [[smuggler]] [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]]. The [[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back (audiobook)|audiobook]] version of the story was read by [[Sean Kenin Elias-Reyes]].<ref name="Sean">{{Twitter|seankenin|status/1313557913861083138|[[Sean Kenin Elias-Reyes|Sean Kenin]]|quote=I can officially report that I narrate & play the characters in this story! I get to play #Jaxxon!! Between that & #TripleZero I am overjoyed to voice original
@starwars characters. Its a phenomenal story written by an awesome author. Thay all are. Read & Listen to the #audiobook|archivedate=20201007023831}}</ref>
