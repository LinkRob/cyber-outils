'''Crystal linflower liquor''' was a type of [[liquor]] [[Diet|potable]] to [[human]]s. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[San Tekka manor]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] had a [[San Tekka manor wine cave|wine cave]] that stored the [[alcohol]]ic beverage.<ref name="TOTF" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> [[Cair San Tekka]]&mdash;a member of the [[San Tekka clan]]&mdash;returned to the manor to act as a spoiled scion of his family, though he was secretly operating within the [[Nihil]]'s [[Occlusion Zone]] to fight the [[Pirate|marauders]].<ref name="TOTF" />

When several Nihil apprehended him upon entering the residence, he resigned himself to suffering through a dinner with their [[Minister of Protection]], [[General]] [[Abediah Viess]], and having the family's [[Aide|attendant]] [[Ino'olo]] fetch some of the liquor from the [[wine]] [[cave]] for it. However, they instead forcefully ushered him away to Viess's location, not intending to have a meal served.<ref name="TOTF" />
