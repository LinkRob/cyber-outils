'''David Langtry''' was a model used by illustrator [[Mike Jackson]] in his drawing of [[Kolor Delan]],<ref name="DG" /> a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] and leader of the [[Pendarran's Revenge]] resistance group. The image of Langtry as Delan appeared in the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' supplementary book [[Wanted by Cracken (sourcebook)|''Wanted by Cracken'']], published in [[1993]] by [[West End Games]]. Langtry was not credited for the role.<ref name="Wanted">[[Wanted by Cracken (sourcebook)|''Wanted by Cracken'']]</ref>
