'''''Doctor Aphra'' 19''' is the nineteenth issue of the [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The comic was written by [[Simon Spurrier]] and [[Kieron Gillen]] with art by [[Emilio Laiso]] and released on [[April 25]], [[2018]]. The issue contains the story "'''Remastered Part VI'''," in which [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]] completes her [[Infiltration of Hivebase-1|mission]] for [[0-0-0|Triple-Zero]].

==Plot summary==
Under heavy fire inside [[Hivebase-1]]'s data core, Dr. [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]] concocts a desperate escape. Arming General [[Hera Syndulla]], Aphra initiates a plan to steal the station's data while simultaneously setting the hijacked Rebel ship on a collision course with the base. The Imperial commander diverts all power to weapons, but discovers the firing patterns have been renamed. When the names are read aloud over the comms, they trigger dozens of explosive [[tooka]]s Aphra had hidden throughout the station, causing the entire Hivebase to detonate catastrophically.

In the aftermath, Imperial Inspector [[Magna Tolvan|Tolvan]] records a sanitized log, framing the disaster as a lesson learned and blaming the responsible parties while positioning herself for a promotion. Meanwhile, Aphra gives the stolen data to Hera, who grants her a ship just to be rid of her. It is then revealed that Tolvan had disguised herself as one of Aphra's crew to stay with her. The two share a brief romantic interlude before Aphra leaves, revealing her full name—Chelli Lona Aphra—as a parting warning.

Aphra delivers a copy of the data to the droid [[0-0-0]], who reveals his true goal: he orchestrated the entire affair to prove that Aphra is just as ruthless and manipulative as he is, listing her many betrayals as evidence of her "talent." Just as Aphra protests, they are ambushed. Tolvan's log reveals her real mission was to dismantle Triple-Zero's crime syndicate, and her troops descend on the droids.

The troopers also capture Aphra, who Tolvan had secretly bugged. In a final act of complex loyalty, Tolvan has Aphra sent to the low-risk [[Accresker Jail]] under a fake name, simultaneously imprisoning and protecting her. The final twist is revealed when both the Rebels and Triple-Zero discover their copies of the data are locked behind a powerful encryption that demands a ransom for access, confirming Aphra had outplayed everyone.
