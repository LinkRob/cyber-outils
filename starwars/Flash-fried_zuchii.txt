'''Flash-fried zuchii''' was a dish made with fried [[zuchii]], a [[Color|green]] [[vegetable]]. When [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] used a cooking competition to determine who murdered [[Robbs Ely]] on [[Takodana]], he ate a poor representation of the dish cooked by [[Devaronian]] prep cook [[Sama Macoy]]. After [[Takodana Castle]] was destroyed, Tuggs served the snack at [[Tuggs' Grub]] included a recipe for flash-fried zuchii in ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''.

==Description==
Flash-fried zuchii was a dish based on fresh [[zuchii]], a juicy and sweet [[Color|green]] [[vegetable]]. The zuchii was cut into sticks, coated with [[bread]]ing, and flash-fried to make it golden-brown and crispy, then it was served with tangy [[sauce]].<ref name="Cookbook" /> It was considered a classic dish.<ref name="ARFD" />

==History==
Before the [[Battle of Takodana]] in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] ran the kitchen at [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on [[Takodana]]. After his sous-chef [[Robbs Ely]] was [[Death|murdered]], Tuggs held a cooking competition to replace Ely and secretly uncover which cook was the killer. When [[Devaronian]] prep cook [[Sama Macoy]]'s [[Devaronian Soufflé]] detonated, she made a last-minute replacement with flash-fried zuchii. It was inferior in flavor and texture and failed to impress Tuggs or the judges, a pair of [[BD-3000 luxury droid]]s named [[Carly]] and [[J'Nell]] who had murdered Ely.<ref name="ARFD" />

Tuggs grew zuchii in his [[garden]] and cooked the dish while on Takodana. Following the destruction of the castle, he served the dish at his traveling [[restaurant]], [[Tuggs' Grub]], which he operated out of a [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|modified transport]] and was known to station at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. He also included the recipe in his [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]].''<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flash-fried zuchii first appeared in the [[2016]] short story "[[A Recipe for Death]]" written by [[Landry Q. Walker]].<ref name="ARFD" /> It was first pictured in the [[2019]] recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which was authored by [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe requires an oil such as [[Wikipedia:Canola oil|canola oil]] for frying and the ingredients [[Wikipedia:Bread_crumbs#Panko|panko breadcrumbs]], [[Wikipedia:Parmigiano-Reggiano|grated parmesan cheese]], [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|pepper]], [[Wikipedia:Flour|all-purpose flour]], [[Wikipedia:Egg as food|egg]], [[Wikipedia:Buttermilk|buttermilk]], and [[Wikipedia:Zucchini|zucchini]].<ref name="Cookbook" />
