'''Blatterborn''' were a type of creature.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> In [[90 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Dooku]]'s first visit to [[Serenno]], which corresponds to the [[celebration on Serenno]] according to ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', to [[90 BBY]].</ref> they were on display at the [[Dianectric Menagerie]] during a [[Celebration on Serenno|celebration]] on the [[planet]] [[Serenno]] and observed by [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Dooku]] and his [[House Serenno|sister]], [[Lady]] [[Jenza]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> When celebrating the return of his cousin [[Crakka]], [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] had blatterborn served in his [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]].<ref name="RTVC 4">[[Star Wars Adventures: Return to Vader's Castle 4|''Star Wars Adventures: Return to Vader's Castle'' 4]]</ref>

Dooku later recounted the incident in a [[holo-letter]] to Jenza.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="Date 23">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Dooku displayed a [[Hologram|holographic]] [[Datacard|data disk]] that detailed the events around meeting his sister, including their observation of the blatterborn, to his recently-recruited [[Sith assassin]], [[Asajj Ventress]]. The pair listened to the recording, after which Dooku gave Ventress several more disks for her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|investigation]] into Jenza's location.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

A blatterborn could be found on the planet [[Jakku]]<ref name="The Jaws of Jakku">''[[The Jaws of Jakku]]''</ref> by the time between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]].<ref name="The Jaws of Jakku date">The events of ''[[The Jaws of Jakku]]'' take place between the events of {{Film|VIII}} and {{Film|IX}}, which corresponds to between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> At one point, the blatterborn sat on the [[Limb|foot]] of the [[human]] Jakku [[scavenger]] [[Dex]], which resulted in him crying, an incident later recalled by the [[Kessurian]] [[Leesa Chaffni]].<ref name="The Jaws of Jakku" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blatterborn were first mentioned in a flashback sequence in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin-Download">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-cavan-scott/9780593102817|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> The spelling of Blatterborn was confirmed by the audio drama's [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script release]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> on [[October 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Penguin-Download" />
