The '''Dead Moon of Gothol''' was a [[moon]] that was used by the [[Ancients (Echo Stone)|ancients]] to hide away the [[Echo Stone]]. Multiple puzzles were installed by the ancients to prevent people from obtaining the artifact. At some point, [[Darth Ravi]] [[Mission to the Dead Moon of Gothol|visited]] the moon to obtain the [[Echo Stone]].<ref name="Echoes of Fear 1" />
