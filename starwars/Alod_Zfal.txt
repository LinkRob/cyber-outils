'''Alod Zfal''' was a [[commander]] in the [[Coalition for Improvements]], a part of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]]. Zfal responded to [[Motion DP6218b|Motion #DP6218b]] in the [[Imperial Senate]] by claiming that [[Alien|non-humans]] should want to live in segregated communities if they took issue with what they considered [[human]] [[High Human|supremacy]]. The commander was quoted by [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]], who called Zfal's statement hypocritical, in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Alod Zfal was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
