'''Black Four''' was a [[callsign]] used by the [[Resistance]]'s [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]]. The [[Resistance pilot]] [[Karé Kun]] flew under this callsign during the [[Cold War]].<ref name="PD3" /> Following the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], [[Suralinda Javos]] flew under the callsign during [[Color|Black]] [[Squadron]]'s mission to gather the Resistance's allies and bring new [[Star system|systems]] into the Resistance.<ref name="Poe Dameron 29">[[Poe Dameron 29|''Poe Dameron'' 29]]</ref>
