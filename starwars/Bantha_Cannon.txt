"'''Bantha Cannon'''" was an article published in ''[[Challenge 70]]'' in March [[1993]]. It is an adventure for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]].

{{Media-stub}}

==Plot summary==
A group of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebels]] were assigned to investigate [[Entooine]]. where an outpost was being established for the season. There they encountered the hunter [[Jowart Typok]], who was there harvesting horns from the [[Blue bantha|Blue Bantha]].
