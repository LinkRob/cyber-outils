The '''Ash Worlds''' was an [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[sector]] which contained the [[Iego system]], home to the [[planet]] [[Iego]] and its [[Moons of Iego|thousand moons]], and the rocky planet [[Sran]]. During the [[Nihil conflict]] of the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Nihil]] [[Strike]] [[Andrik Keller]] visited Sran, where he was [[Death|killed]] by former Nihil [[Tempest Runner]] [[Pan Eyta]] after unwittingly tipping Eyta off to the whereabouts of Eyta's rival, Tempest Runner [[Lourna Dee]]. Later, during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Iego was briefly occupied by the Separatists, who left behind a [[LaserWeb Defense Station|laser web]] installed in the planet's [[moon]]s which kept the residents trapped there until [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] discovered the problem while [[Mission to Iego|visiting]] Iego in search of [[reeksa]] root.

==Description==
The Ash Worlds was a [[sector]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Hoth" /> At least part of the sector, including the [[Iego system]], was also situated in [[the Slice]] region.<ref name="The Slice" /> The Iego [[Star system|system]] contained the [[planet]] [[Iego]],<ref name="Hoth" /> famous for its [[Moons of Iego|one thousand moons]].<ref name="MoaTM" /> The Ash Worlds also contained the rocky planet [[Sran]].<ref name="TR script">[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner (script)|''The High Republic: Tempest Runner'' script]]</ref>

==History==
In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date 1">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> [[Andrik Keller]], a [[Strike]] of the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s who had escaped the defeat of [[Lourna Dee's Tempest]] at the [[Battle of Galov]], visited Sran. Commenting on Sran's propensity for dust storms, the [[Unidentified serving droid|serving droid]] in a [[Unidentified cantina (Sran)|cantina]] Keller entered suggested that the celestial body's weather was the reason for the Ash Worlds' name. In the [[cantina]], Keller met a [[Dowutin]], the former [[Tempest Runner]] [[Pan Eyta]], who sought revenge upon [[Lourna Dee]]. Not knowing who he was speaking to, Keller informed Eyta of Dee's condition and whereabouts, leading Eyta to [[Death|kill]] Keller and depart Sran.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

Early in the [[Clone Wars]], a conflict between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the breakaway [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]] came to occupy Iego, installing a [[LaserWeb Defense Station|laser web]] amidst the planet's [[moon]]s. The Separatists left the security system behind upon abandoning Iego, leaving its residents trapped as the [[laser web]] destroyed any [[starship]] which attempted to leave. The Iegoans began attributing the carnage to a supposed curse laid by [[Drol]], Iego's mythical phantom ruler.<ref name="MoaTM" />

In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">{{TCWcanondates|118}}</ref> [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] [[Mission to Iego|visited]] Iego in search of [[reeksa]] root, the only known [[Antidote|cure]] for the lethal [[Blue Shadow Virus]], in the hopes of saving people on the planet [[Naboo]] who had been infected with the disease,<ref name="MoaTM" /> which had been recreated by Separatist [[scientist]] [[Nuvo Vindi]].<ref name="BSV">{{TCW|Blue Shadow Virus (episode)}}</ref> After retrieving the root, the two [[Jedi]] discovered the laser web when they attempted to leave, immediately recognizing it as a security system. Subsequently, Kenobi, Skywalker, and the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] used repurposed [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]] left behind by the Separatists to destroy the laser web and its primary generator, allowing the Iegoans to once again travel offworld.<ref name="MoaTM" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ash Worlds first [[canon]]ically appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] episode "[[Mystery of a Thousand Moons]],"<ref name="MoaTM" /> which premiered on [[Cartoon Network]] on [[February 13]], [[2009]].<ref name="MOATM Old EG">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode018.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Mystery of a Thousand Moons}}</ref> The sector was first canonically identified in the [[2021]] audio drama ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project and performed by a full cast.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />
