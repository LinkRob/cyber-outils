'''Beetase''' was a female [[Ugnaught]] miner who lived in [[Ugnaught Town]] on [[Bespin]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. She was the cousin of [[Lonaste]]. When the Imperials took control of the city, she helped Lonaste to find a spaceship with which their whole clan could escape from Bespin.<ref name="BespinEscape" />
