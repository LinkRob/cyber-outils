'''Bark rats''' were stoutly built [[rat]]s [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Cholganna]]. They made up the bulk of the [[diet]] of the predatory [[nexu]], a [[species]] that hunted in Cholganna's [[Temperature|cool]] [[forest]]s using their [[infrared]] vision to spot warm-[[blood]]ed prey.<ref name="CVD" /> The rats were more common than Cholganna's [[Arboreal octopus|arboreal octopi]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Geonosis and Other Planets in the Outer Rim}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], bark rats were first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''.<ref name="CVD" /> They originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they first appeared in the [[2002]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]].<ref name="AOTCVD">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
