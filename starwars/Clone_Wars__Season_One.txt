'''''Clone Wars'': Season One''' was the first season of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]''. It originally aired from [[November 7]] to [[November 20]], [[2003]] (Chapters 1&ndash;10). Together with [[Clone Wars: Season Two|Season Two]], it comprises [[Star Wars: Clone Wars Volume One|Volume One]].
