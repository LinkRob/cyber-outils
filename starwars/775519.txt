{{Quote|The hull shows substantial scoring and wear. Whenever it crashed, it wasn't recently. General Plo, the scans check out. It's a T-6 shuttle, all right, serial number 775519.|[[Clone Commander]] [[Wolffe]]|<ref name="The Lost One" />}}
'''775519''' was a [[T-6 shuttle]] used by [[Jedi Master]] [[Sifo-Dyas]] around the [[time]] of the [[Invasion of Naboo]]. It was shot down over the [[Oba Diah moon|moon]] of [[Oba Diah]], [[Death|killing]] Sifo-Dyas whereas [[Silman]] survived to be [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] by the [[Pyke Syndicate]], and its remains were later discovered by the [[clone trooper]]s of the [[104th Battalion]] under the leadership of [[Jedi General|General]] [[Plo Koon]] during the last [[Standard month|months]] of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="The Lost One" />

==Behind the scenes==
Jedi T-6 shuttle 775519 was first, although only in a [[hologram]] and as wreckage, in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[The Lost One]]." Its name was revealed in the hologram case file featured in the episode.<ref name="The Lost One" />
