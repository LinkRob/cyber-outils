'''Diva Funquita''' was a [[Theelin]] [[Gender|female]] who worked as an [[Aide|assistant]] to the [[Hutt]] criminal [[Gardulla Besadii the Elder]]. She accompanied Gardulla to view the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] in [[32 BBY]].

==Biography==
The [[Theelin]] [[Gender|female]] Diva Funquita served the [[Hutt]] criminal [[Gardulla Besadii the Elder]] as an [[Aide|assistant]] during the reign of the [[Galactic Republic]] after being gifted to her by the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]. She often accompanied Gardulla to events,<ref name="Geektionary" /> and in [[32 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] to [[32 BBY]].</ref> she attended the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] with the Hutt.<ref name="TPM" />

The pair viewed the event from Jabba's private viewing box in the [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]] along with the crime lord and various members of his entourage. When the race finished she left the box with Gardulla, leaving behind Jabba who had fallen [[Sleep|asleep]].<ref name="TPM" />

==Personality and traits==
Funquita had spiky [[Color|scarlet]] [[hair]],<ref name="Geektionary" /> brown [[eye]]s, and light skin with red spots covering the left half of her face as well as her left shoulder and upper [[Limb|arm]].<ref name="TPM" />

==Equipment==
While at the podrace she wore a black [[Clothing|dress]] with a jeweled [[belt]].<ref name="TPM" />

==Behind the scenes==
Diva Funquita first appeared in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], in which she was portrayed by director [[George Lucas]]' daughter [[Amanda Lucas]].<ref name="Actress">{{SWArchive|url=episode-iii/bts/profile/f20051221/index.html|text=The Force Runs Strong In This Family: Jett, Amanda & Katie}}</ref> In the current [[canon]], Funquita's name was first revealed in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''. The name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used in the 1999 reference book [[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary|''Star Wars: Episode I The Visual Dictionary'']], which was written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]].<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
