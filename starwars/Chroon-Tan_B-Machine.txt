Fortunately, the Chroon-Tan B-Machine is a midwife droid programmed to safely deliver your latest models off your internal assembly line and into the world with minimal startup issues|[[R0-GR/LEGO|R0-GR]]|<ref name="Droidography" />}}
The '''Chroon-Tan B-Machine''',<ref name="Droidography" /> also known as the '''Chroon-Tan midwife droid''',<ref name="BMF22">{{BuildFalconCite|22|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> was was a type of [[midwife droid]] designed to assist with the organic birthing process. The floating [[medical droid]] was designed to assist a variety of [[species]] with birth and newborn care. These droids had [[Color|white]] faces inset with green and blue light sensors,<ref name="Droidography" /> rotund silver bodies,<ref name="ROTS" /> and thermal cushions to warm and cradle newborns.<ref name="Droidography" /> They were programmed to make soothing cooing sounds during childbirth.<ref name="Databank" /> One of these medical units assisted with the birth of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s twin children, [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]], in the [[Polis Massa Base]].<ref name="ROTS" />

Years after, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] visited the [[Polis Massa Base]], with his [[forensics droid]] [[ZED-6-7]] accessing the [[memory bank]] of a damaged Chroon-Tan B-Machine, allowing Vader to watch footage his wife's plea that there was still good in Anakin, directed to his former master, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], before dying.<ref name="Darth Vader 5">[[Darth Vader (2020) 5|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 5]]</ref> Sometime after that, [[Sabé]] returned to the ruined base where she spoke to the damaged droid and saw the same recording of Amidala's last words.<ref name="Darth Vader 18">[[Darth Vader (2020) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 18]]</ref>
