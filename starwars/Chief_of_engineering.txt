The '''chief of engineering''', or '''head of engineering''', was a title held by the individual who led the [[engineer]]ing teams in the [[61st Mobile Infantry]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]],<ref name="TC" /> and eventually the [[New Republic]].<ref name="SF">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> The engineering [[droid]] [[M2-M5]] and later the engineer [[Vifra]] held the title.<ref name="TC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The chief of engineering first was appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="TC" />
