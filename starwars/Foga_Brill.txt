'''Foga Brill''' was an individual who worked for the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Judicial Department]]'s [[Republic Office of Criminal Investigations|Office of Criminal Investigations]]. During the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Brill served as the office's [[Director of Investigation]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Foga Brill was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Foga Brill/Legends|Foga Brill]] was introduced in ''[[Shield of Lies]]'', a [[1996]] novel authored by [[Michael P. Kube-McDowell]].<ref name="SoL">''[[Shield of Lies]]''</ref>
