'''Darth Tigran''' was a [[Zabrak/Legends|Zabrak]] [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]]<ref name="ArtStation" /> who was active during the reign of [[Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire]], between [[130 ABY]] and [[138 ABY]].<ref name="Dating note">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2014/04/09/art-from-the-darth-maul-game-you-39-ll-never-get-to-play.aspx|text=Art From The Darth Maul Game You'll Never Get To Play|author=Andrew Reiner|date=2014-04-09|work=[[Wikipedia:Game Informer|''Game Informer'']]|archivedate=20240223064541|nolive=1}} &mdash; ''[[Battle of the Sith Lords]]'' would have taken place during the reign of [[Darth Krayt's Galactic Empire]]. [[Legacy (2006) 1|''Legacy'' (2006) 1]] states that [[Darth Krayt|Krayt's]] Empire was founded in [[130 ABY]]. The Empire was dissolved in [[138 ABY]], as depicted in [[Legacy—War 6|''Legacy—War'' 6]]. Therefore, Darth Tigran must have been active between those two dates.</ref> Tigran had [[Color/Legends|yellow]] [[Eye/Legends|eyes]], straight shoulder-length black [[Hair/Legends|hair]], green-yellow skin, horns, and [[Sith tattoo]]s on their face.<ref name="ArtStation" />

Darth Tigran was [[Clothing/Legends|clothed]] in a red and black cloak with chest [[Armor/Legends|armor]] that resembled a [[Skeleton/Legends|ribcage]]. They wielded a single red-bladed [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]].<ref name="ArtStation" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darth Tigran is a character created for [[Red Fly Studio]]'s [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Battle of the Sith Lords]]''. Tigran's design was created by concept artist [[Thomas A. Szakolczay]]<ref name="ArtStation" /> at some point prior to the game's cancellation in [[2011]].<ref name="GI article">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2014/04/09/art-from-the-darth-maul-game-you-39-ll-never-get-to-play.aspx|text=Art From The Darth Maul Game You'll Never Get To Play|author=Andrew Reiner|date=2014-04-09|work=[[Wikipedia:Game Informer|''Game Informer'']]|archivedate=20240223064541|nolive=1}}</ref>
