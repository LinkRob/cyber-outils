The '''''Eclipse''''' was an [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] in service of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It also served as the [[flagship]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]].

==Description==
The ''Eclipse'' was an [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] and was the twin sister [[Starship|ship]] of the ''[[Executor]]''. The ''Eclipse'' measured at 19,000 [[meter]]s in length, and could reach speeds of 100 [[Kilometer per hour|kilometers per hour]] utilizing its thirteen [[engine]] thrusters. The ''Eclipse'' was crewed by thousands of personnel, and was outfitted with a class 1 [[hyperdrive]], a [[titanium]]-reinforced hull, over 5,000 [[turbolaser]], [[ion cannon]] batteries,<ref name="Starfighters" /> and [[concussion missile]] tubes.<ref name="UltimateSW" />

A [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] [[deflector shield]] was also included as a layer of defense<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}} establishes that ''Executor''-class Star Dreadnoughts have [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] [[deflector shield]]s. Since the ''Eclipse'' is of this class, then it must also have bridge deflector shields.</ref> and maintained a complement of thousands of [[starship]]s<ref name="Starfighters" /> including [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s, [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bombers]], and [[TIE/IN interceptor]]s. Vessels of the ''Executor''-class also had at least one [[hangar]] bay<ref name="UltimateSW" /> and [[escape pod]]s.<ref name="Empire's End note" />

==Role==
The ''Eclipse'' served as the [[flagship]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="Life Debt" />

==History==
{{Quote|There.''"<br />"''Take us in. It's time to rejoin with those who came before.|Brendol Hux, and Rae Sloane upon seeing the ''Eclipse''|<ref name="Empire's End" />}}
[[Time|Sometime]] after the [[Arrth-Eno Mission]] undertaken by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], Rebel Alliance [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] wrote a report to [[Commander-in-Chief]] [[Mon Mothma]] regarding the ''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought. In the report, Ackbar mentioned the ''Eclipse'', noting that it was rumored to be the flagship of Emperor Palpatine,<ref name="Rebel Files" /> which turned out to be accurate.<ref name="Life Debt" /> At one point during its service, the ''Eclipse'' [[Orbital bombardment|bombarded]] a celestial body along with three [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]].<ref name="Armada">{{Armada|set=Super Star Destroyer Expansion Pack|cardname=''Eclipse''}}</ref>

It was believed to have been destroyed by a [[fleet]] of [[New Republic]] vessels with Ackbar's [[MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser|flagship]], ''[[Home One]]'', firing the ship-killing shot<ref name="Life Debt" /> by the [[Standard year|year]] [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Life Debt Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> During the [[Battle of Jakku]], after former [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] defeated her former ally, [[Counselor to the Empire]] [[Gallius Rax]], she was instructed by Rax before he died to take a replica of the ''[[Imperialis (Jakku)|Imperialis]], ''find the ''Eclipse'' and reform the Empire. The New Republic defeated the Empire at the Battle of [[Jakku]], and Sloane, together with [[Commandant]] [[Brendol Hux]], his son [[Armitage Hux|Armitage]], and a cadre of [[Gallius Rax's child soldiers|child]] [[soldier]]s, boarded the ''Imperialis'' and traveled to the [[Unknown Regions]] of [[the galaxy]], where they discovered the ''Eclipse''. After Brendol spotted the ''Eclipse'', Sloane ordered the [[Sentinel (Contingency)|Sentinel]] piloting the ''Imperialis'' to bring the ship in and rejoin with the others. Sloane intended to board the ''Eclipse'' and build a [[First Order|new Empire]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Eclipse'' was first mentioned in the [[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!|junior novelization]] of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] by [[Tom Angleberger]].<ref name="Beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> However, the ship being referred to has since been confirmed to be the ''[[Executor]]''.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|OrigamiYoda|status/651441665950007296|[[Tom Angleberger]]|quote=@LordZack01 @Coopx30 @LelalMekha @wookieepedia Are you guys ready for this? Here's Pablo's note on my manuscript: [http://ow . ly/i/dwr9R ow . ly/i/dwr9R]|archivedate=20160517113243}}</ref> The ship's image originated in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], with an unidentified [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought/Legends|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] in the [[2016]] board game [[Star Wars: Rebellion (board game)|''Star Wars'' Rebellion]]. It was later confirmed to be Sheev Palpatine's command ship in ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''.<ref name="Life Debt" /> The ship was first canonically pictured in the [[Super Star Destroyer Expansion Pack]] from the miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: Armada]]''.<ref name="Armada" />
