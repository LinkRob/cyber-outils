An '''attack''' was carried out by the mauraders known as the [[Nihil]] against civilians in the [[Echerta system]]<ref name="Fallen Star" /> in [[230 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> The Echerta system served as a hub for [[Hyperspace route|hyperspace lanes]] causing dozens of [[Starship|ships]] to have emerged there and thus be attacked by the Nihil. Among these ships attacked was the ''[[Vessel]]'', which had been hoping to change direction in the system. [[Jedi]] [[Wayseeker]] [[Orla Jareni]] was a passenger on the ''Vessel'' that suggested that they flood the frequencies of the Nihil ships, as the ''Vessel'' would not have been screened out due to its small size. The captain of the ''Vessel'' [[Leox Gyasi]], followed Jareni's plan and flooded the Nihil [[Comm unit|communications]] with the popular song the '[[margengai glide]]' with the help of his co-pilot [[Affie Hollow]]. This successfully disorganized the Nihil enough to allow the civilians and themselves to escape.<ref name="Fallen Star" />
