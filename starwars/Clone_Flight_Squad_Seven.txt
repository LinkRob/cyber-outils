'''Clone Flight Squad Seven''',<ref name="BMF 36" /> also known as '''Clone Flight Seven''' or as '''Squad Seven'''<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> was a [[starfighter]] [[flight group]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] that was part of the [[Open Circle Fleet|Open Circle Armada]]. The flight group was led by [[Clone Commander]] [[Davijaan|Davijann]],<ref name="Episode III" /> otherwise known by his [[Callsign|call sign]] "Odd Ball,"<ref name="CVNE">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> and participated in the [[Battle of Coruscant]] where they deployed from the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Episode III" /> ''[[Ro-ti-Mundi]]''.<ref name="CL" /> After the [[Clone Wars]], Odd Ball and Squad Seven served under the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="HC 45" />

==History==
During the [[Battle of Coruscant]], one of the last major engagements during the [[Clone Wars]], Squad Seven provided cover for [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] as they made their way to rescue the captured [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] from the [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] ''[[Invisible Hand]]'', the [[flagship]] of [[General]] [[Grievous]].<ref name="Episode III" />
