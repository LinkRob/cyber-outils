The '''DBY-827 heavy turbolaser turret''' was a [[turbolaser]] turret used on the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] during the [[Clone Wars]].

==Description==
[[File:DBY-827_heavy_turbolaser_turret_cross-section.png|thumb|left|180px|A cross-section of a DBY-827 heavy turbolaser turret]]
The DBY-827 was the standard requirement in [[Navy|naval]] gunnery around the time of the [[Clone Wars]] for inter-[[Starship|ship]] combat and [[planet]]ary bombardment. Each [[turret]] was mounted with powerful tracking sensors as well as a large power capacitor which quickly discharged energy to the guns. [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] had eight DBY-827s, four on either side of the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] area. They were the main source of firepower on the ship.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" />

The DBY-827 had two different modes of firing: one was to fire alternating between the two barrels, at variable but relatively rapid rates,<ref name="Citadel Rescue">{{TCW|Citadel Rescue}}</ref> while another was to fire both barrels simultaneously, but at a generally lower but still variable rate.<ref name="Citadel Rescue" /><ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> During close-range fights, the turret could rotate in three seconds with its fast-tracking mode in order to engage close-range targets,<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> as well as having precise, long-range tracking that was suited for hitting targets at a distance. The DBY-827 had seven different blast intensities so that the damage inflicted could be modulated under various circumstances.<ref name="CV NE">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref>
