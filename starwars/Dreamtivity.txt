'''Dreamtivity''' is the children's activity book imprint of Retail Centric Marketing,<ref name="Announcement" /> a subsidiary of [[Printer's Row]].<ref name="Official site" /> They are known for their [[Colortivity]] series of coloring books. Founded in [[2015]] by Beth Peters and Chad Wiggins,<ref name="Announcement" /> the imprint published a number of activity books focused on ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.

===Titles with contested release dates===
These Dreamtivity releases have published release dates of [[January 7]], [[2020]]. However, each of these books uses the name "[[Din Grogu|Grogu]]" in the title or contents. At that time, the character was only known as "The Child", and was not revealed to be named Grogu until the release of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'' [[Chapter 13: The Jedi]]<ref name="The Jedi">[[Chapter 13: The Jedi]]</ref> on [[November 27]], 2020.<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-november-2020|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in November 2020}}</ref> Furthermore, in early [[2026]], these titles appeared on Dreamtivity's website as current products, while products released as late as [[2023]] were no longer featured.<ref name="2026 Features">{{Dreamtivity|url=search/mandalorian/?utm_source=internal&utm_medium=banner&utm_campaign=mandalorian|text=Search: Mandalorian|archivedate=20260307154335}}</ref> As such, this article assumes that these products were not released in January 2020, but were instead released sometime after 2023 and backdated by the publisher.
