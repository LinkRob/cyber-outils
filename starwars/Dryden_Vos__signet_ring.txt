'''Dryden Vos' signet ring''' was a [[Signet ring|ring]] used only by the crime syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]]'s most trusted [[capo]]s that was [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]]'s most prized possession. It was encoded to open protected data systems, and was used to contact Vos' secret superior, the former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]]. After the [[death]] of Vos in [[10 BBY]], [[Qi'ra]] used the ring to contact Maul and inform him of the situation.

==Description==
The [[ring]] itself was made of pure [[bronzium]], and the signet was a [[Color|blood red]] [[aurodium]]-cinnebar matrix.<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> The [[ring]] was encoded to open protected data systems, and was used to contact the former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]], the secret true leader of Crimson Dawn.<ref name="Solo" />

==History==
[[File:Drydens_Signet_Ring_SASWS.png|thumb|left|250px|Dryden wears his signet ring as a show of his authority and his dominance over Qi'ra]]
As the public face of Crimson Dawn, Dryden Vos wore the [[signet ring]] at all [[time]]s when around others, such as on [[First Light|his yacht]]. When meeting with and then murdering [[Diles Anevi]], Vos wore the [[ring]]. Afterwards, after a brief exchange with [[Tobias Beckett]], Vos was introduced to [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]], with the ring still on his finger. When Vos and his top lieutenant Qi'ra received the three scoundrels in [[Dryden's study|Vos's office]], both wore their respective versions of the ring.<ref name="Solo" />

[[File:MaulHologram-Solo.png|thumb|right|250px|Maul was contacted by Qi'ra after Dryden Vos was killed]]
Later, when the ''First Light'' arrived on [[Savareen]], Vos was engaged in combat against Han Solo and Qi'ra. With the ring on his finger, Vos fought valiantly but was ultimately defeated and killed, having been surprised by the fellow ring-wielder Qi'ra's betrayal. After Vos's death, Qi'ra removed his ring from his finger and used it to contact Darth Maul using the slot on the [[Dryden's desk]] to inform him what had occurred. After receiving instructions to regroup with Maul on [[Dathomir]], Qi'ra abandoned Han on Savareen, with both rings now in her possession.<ref name="Solo" />
