'''C. J. Tramontana''' is a [[Star Wars]] author and editor who worked on many books from [[West End Games]]. Tramontana was one of three authors who wrote the [[Yavin 4/Legends|Yavin 4]] design for the supplement, ''[[Galaxy Guide 2: Yavin and Bespin]]''. Tramontana was also the editor of ''[[Crisis on Cloud City (adventure book)|Crisis on Cloud City]]'' and [[Riders of the Maelstrom (West End Games)|''Riders of the Maelstrom'']].
