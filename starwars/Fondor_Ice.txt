{{Quote|I even found a little 'great'&mdash;includin' this velvety ice cream with a vibrant violet hue that matches the planet's purple surface. One decadent dessert doesn't completely forgive Fondor for its role in the Empire's galactic reign of terror, but it's certainly a fine start (and a fine finish for any meal).|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
'''Fondor Ice''' was a type of velvety, [[Diet|vegetarian]] [[ice cream]] with a vibrant, [[Color|violet]] hue that matched the surface color of the [[Colonies]] [[planet]] [[Fondor]], where the [[Foodstuff|dessert]] originated. During a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] visited Fondor and tried to focus on good on the world rather than the role it had played in [[starship]] manufacturer and repair for the [[Galactic Empire]], which he considered evil.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

During the visit, Tuggs discovered the ice cream, which he considered decadent and great, and he later included a recipe for it in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> In the book, he stated that Fondor Ice was a fine end to any meal, even calling it a good start on the road to forgiveness for Fondor.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fondor Ice was created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the salad makes use of [[Wikipedia:Coconut|coconut cream]], [[Wikipedia:Coconut milk|coconut milk]], [[Wikipedia:White sugar|granulated sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Dioscorea alata|ube extract]], [[Wikipedia:Vanilla extract|vanilla extract]], and diced [[Wikipedia:Mango|mango]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
