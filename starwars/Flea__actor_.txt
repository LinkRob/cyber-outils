'''Michael Peter Balzary''', known professionally as '''Flea,''' is an Australian musician and actor who portrayed [[Vect Nokru]] in ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]''.<ref name="Part I" /> He is best known for being the bassist in the rock band [[Wikipedia:Red Hot Chili Peppers|Red Hot Chili Peppers]].<ref name="RHCP">{{WebCite|url=https://www.eonline.com/news/1332610/this-red-hot-chili-peppers-member-has-a-surprise-obi-wan-kenobi-cameo|text=This Red Hot Chili Peppers Member Has a Surprise Obi-Wan Kenobi Cameo|author=Contreras, Cydney|date=2022-05-27|work=[[Wikipedia:E!|E!]]|archivedate=20220629181644}}</ref> He is also notable for playing [[W:c:backtothefuture:Douglas J. Needles|Douglas J. Needles]] in the [[Wikipedia:Back to the Future Part II|second]] and [[Wikipedia:Back to the Future Part III|third films]] of the [[Wikipedia:Back to the Future (franchise)|''Back to the Future'' trilogy]]. [[Needles]], a friend of [[Han Solo]] mentioned in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', was named after his character.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|JonKasdan|status/1000259740331474944|[[Jon Kasdan]]|quote=NEEDLES is 100% a reference to BACK TO THE FUTURE III. Don't race Needles! It'll ruin your life!|date=2018-05-25|archivedate=20200814122915}} {{C|Note: The original tweet has been taken offline}}</ref>
