The '''wife''' of [[Adon Fox]] was a member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] prior to the [[Battle of Hoth]]. [[Gender|She]] and her [[Marriage|husband]] were stationed at [[Echo Base]] when it came under attack from [[Death Squadron|Imperial forces]]. Whilst her husband was escorting an escape [[transport]], her [[Adon Fox's wife's transport|own]] [[Starship|ship]] came under fire from the Imperial [[Star Destroyer]] [[blockade]] and was destroyed by the ''[[Devastator]], ''killing all on board, including Adon Fox's wife.<ref name="BS" />
