'''Begol Toulin''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Bothan/Legends|Bothan]] who lived in [[Mos Eisley/Legends|Mos Eisley]], on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]], during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]. Sometime following the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]] in [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]], Begol asked a [[Spacer/Legends|spacer]] to enact revenge against a number of [[Drug dealer|spice dealers]] for getting his daughter [[Shan'ri Toulin|Shan'ri]] hooked on [[Death stick/Legends|death sticks]], thus resulting in her [[Death/Legends|death]].<ref name="SWG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Begol Toulin appears in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']], a [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online roleplaying game]] developed by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment and published by [[LucasArts]],<ref name="SWG" /> prior to its closure on [[December 15]], [[2011]].<ref name="Shutdown">{{SonyCite|url=playerinfo|text=IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT ''STAR WARS'' GALAXIES™}}</ref> According to these files, introduced with the [[2005]] "[[W:c:swg:New Game Enhancements|New Game Enhancements]]," Begol would have been a quest giver, giving to players a quest named "Destroy the Spice" during the Mos Eisley part of the "[[W:c:swg:Legacy Quest|Legacy Quest]]." However, Begol Toulin is not known to have appeared on the game's live servers as a proper [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]]. Nevertheless, an emulated depiction pulled from this character's Interchange File Format reveals what he would have looked like if he were added to the game. This character is also known as '''Begol Reigel''' in several game files.<ref name="SWG" />
