The '''E522 assassin droid''' was an [[assassin droid]] model originally designed during the [[Separatist Crisis]] by [[Baktoid Combat Automata]], at the [[time]] under the [[Techno Union]]. By the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]], they were one of the most feared [[droid]]s operating in [[the galaxy]].

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|Essentially, the E522 consists of a large weapon platform with a droid brain and a mini-rocket pack that moves on two tracks.|WAC-47|<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />}}
The E522 [[assassin droid]] was a large, hulking construct laced with heavy weapons and equipped with thick military [[armor]]. It had a rounded, diamond-shaped torso with no noticeable head, and a very narrow waist that could rotate 360 degrees, two heavy [[tank]] treads that were covered in armored skirts, and two long and multi-jointed [[Limb|arms]] that were attached to armored shoulders. The arms were equipped with a [[heavy repeating blaster]], heavy [[ion blaster]], and a rapid-fire [[needler]] gun rather than hands.<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> The needler gun was believed to be capable of penetrating [[Ray shield|energy shields]].<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />

Three smaller arms with highly nimble hands that were mounted low on the [[droid]]'s torso and a [[Model 20 mini-missile pack]] was mounted on the back. The E522's body was covered with an assortment of [[sensor]]s, antennae, and [[photoreceptor]]s which gave the E522 a full 360 degree view, which worked in combination with the powerful internal combat computer and targeting system.<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" />

==History==
===Republic Era===
The E522 assassin droid was initially designed by [[Baktoid Combat Automata]] as part of their [[B-series battle droid]] line,<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> as a successor to the [[B1-series battle droid]]s,<ref name="Official Magazine 118" /> or to serve as a light anti-armor and anti-aircraft unit for B1 and [[B2-series super battle droid]]s, but were too expensive and complex to serve as disposable units, and the project was scrapped in favor of B2 super [[battle droid]]s and [[B3 battle droid]]s.<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> However, the ones that were produced were distributed to [[Separatist Droid Army|Separatist forces]] during the war. Republic [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]] was aware of these droids.<ref name="Official Magazine 118" /> [[Corporal]]<ref name="sunny">{{TCW|A Sunny Day in the Void}}</ref> [[WAC-47]] borrowed Gascon's [[datapad]] while he was sleeping and learned about the E522.<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />

WAC used this information to write an intelligence report on the droids for the [[91st Mobile Reconnaissance Corps|91st Recon Corps]], which was published in the survival guide "[[Neyo's Almanac of Clankers]]." WAC found the droids ugly.<ref name="Official Magazine 118" /> After the [[Clone Wars]] ended with the [[Mission to Mustafar|dissolution]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and the [[Proclamation of the New Order|transition]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] into the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Techno Union]] was dissolved, and its subsidiaries either became independent or were nationalized by the Empire.<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" />

===Imperial Era===
{{Quote|&hellip;like everyone's least favorite Master Sergeant.|Rebel agents, on the E522|<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" />}}
Baktoid Combat Automata was nationalized by the Empire, and immediately planned for breakup. Through the process of selling BCA's assets, all data from the E522 project was given to [[Sienar Intelligence Systems]]. At the time, SIS was developing its own line of combat and assassin droids, and the addition of the E522 and its data was a large boost. SIS immediately scrapped its own project and invested large amounts of time and capital into launching the E522 project. After a [[Standard year|year]] of development, the first E522s launched from SIS's assembly line and brought into service to combat the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|growing unrest]] against the Empire.<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" />

By the time of the Galactic Empire, there were fewer than one hundred E522 droids in operation throughout [[the galaxy]]. Most were operated by [[Imperial Army Intelligence]] and the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] as [[assassin]]s and support units for black ops. The droids were constantly [[memory wipe]]d and received maintenance, giving them silent and terrifying personalities. A few E522s were in service to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], mostly working in the role of battlefield salvage and roles similar to the Empire's. Rebel E522s in [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service]] developed cynical and demanding personalities, so much so that [[agent]]s described them as "everyone's least favorite [[Master Sergeant]]." However, Rebel E522s were well liked and respected for their bravery and competence during missions.<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" />
