'''B1 Unit 275''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] that served the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]], where a [[Akiva production plant|droid factory]] produced units for the Separatist cause until an [[Battle of Akiva|attack]] by the [[Galactic Republic]]. 275 was destroyed, and their remains were left in the factory.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[ND-5]] was shot by an [[Imperial Military|Imperial]], causing his memory to malfunction and to call B1 unit 275 to respond upon entering the facility.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
B1 Unit 275 appeared in "The Legacy" quest of the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
