An allegedly '''abandoned weapons depot''' was located on the [[planet]] [[Sullust]]. By [[5 ABY]], the demands of the [[Galactic Empire]] had outgrown the depot's supply, and [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]] ordered the depot to double production of weapons and vehicles immediately, as the depot provided vital armaments to the Empire's operations in the [[Western Reaches]]. [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Shriv Suurgav]] of the [[New Republic]] [[Destruction of an abandoned weapons depot|destroyed]] the depot to prevent the [[Galactic Empire]] from recovering weapons from the depot, which severely impacted Imperial forces.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
