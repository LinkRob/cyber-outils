'''Battle of Mustafar''' might refer to:

==[[Canon]]==
===[[Clone Wars]]===
*[[Mission to Mustafar (Skywalker and Tano)|Mission to Mustafar]], undertaken by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] to locate missing [[Force-sensitive]] children.
*[[Mission to Mustafar (Death Watch)|Mission to Mustafar]], executed by the [[Death Watch]] seeking an alliance with the [[Black Sun]].
*[[Mission to Mustafar]], the final event of the [[Clone Wars]], undertaken by [[Darth]] Vader against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].

===[[Great Jedi Purge]]===
*[[Duel on Mustafar]], also known as the Battle on Mustafar, a confrontation between [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and Darth Vader.

===[[Imperial Era]]===
*[[Battle of Fortress Vader]], a battle between the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Mustafarian]]s during the construction of [[Fortress Vader]].
*[[Raid on Fortress Vader]], a raid conducted by [[Southern Mustafarian]]s on Fortress Vader.
*[[Attack on Fortress Vader]], an attack again made by Mustafarians against Fortress Vader.
*[[Action at Mustafar]], a confrontation between [[Phoenix Cell]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] above [[Mustafar]] to rescue Jedi [[Kanan Jarrus]].
*[[Mission to Fortress Vader]], a mission undertaken by Commander [[Lina Graf]] to rescue [[Thom Hudd|a member]] of her crew who was imprisoned in Fortress Vader.
*[[Mission to Mustafar (Rebel Alliance)|Mission to Mustafar]], undertaken by members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], including [[K-2SO]] to an [[Imperial base (Mustafar)|Imperial base]] on Mustafar.
*[[Skirmish in Fortress Vader]], a skirmish between the Empire and a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Corvax descendant|smuggler]] who was attempting to escape.
*[[Battle on Mustafar]], a battle fought between the Force-sensitive smuggler and an [[army]] of Mustafarians and the [[Marching Horde]] against the Empire on Fortress Vader.

===[[First Order-Resistance War]]===
*[[Mission to Mustafar (Kylo Ren)|Mission to Mustafar]], a mission undertaken by [[First Order]] [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] to find and destroy every vestige of his grandfather's past.
*[[Mission to Mustafar (First Order)|Mission to Mustafar]], undertaken by Supreme Leader Kylo Ren in search of a [[Sith wayfinder]] to [[Darth Sidious|the Emperor]] after [[Exegol Broadcast|Palpatine's broadcast]] of his revival, intention for [[Final Order|revenge]], and the threat of the [[Sith Eternal|return]] of the [[Sith]].
