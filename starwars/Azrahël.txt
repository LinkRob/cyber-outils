'''Azrahël''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Globlour]] from the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Amistophane VI]]. He was experiencing problems with making money, so he traveled to the planet [[Abregado-rae/Legends|Abregado Rae]] to gamble at [[The Star of Chance]] [[Casino/Legends|casino]]. As a Globlour, he possessed the unique ability to see the future. The Globlours naturally produced an enzyme that prevented them from using their ability, but the secretion of the enzyme could be blocked by a large intake of glucose. At the casino, Azrahël won two million [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]] by betting on the [[Rodanian wheel]] while nonchalantly sucking on candy canes.<ref name="Arrière-plan étoilé" />
