'''Bloy's riot squad''' was a riot-control [[squad]] of [[Imperial security trooper]] [[cadet]]s assigned to [[Sergeant]] [[Bloy]]. The inexperienced cadets were marched into anti-[[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] protests on the [[planet]] [[Ghorman]] in [[2 BBY]], which helped to ignite the [[Ghorman Massacre]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Fall out! Quick time! Tighten up! Look lively!|Bloy|audio=Bloy-Orders-Messenger.flac|<ref name="Messenger" />}}
Bloy's riot squad was a riot-control [[squad]]<ref name="Who Are You AD" /> composed of [[Imperial security trooper]] [[cadet]]s<ref name="Andor Explained">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-episodes-7-8-9|text=''Andor'' Explained: Episodes 7-9 - "Messenger", "Who Are You?", and "Welcome to the Rebellion"}}</ref> under the command of [[Sergeant]] [[Bloy]].<ref name="Messenger" /> It fell into [[Ghorman security trooper regiment|overall security trooper regiment]] under Bloy's supervision. While Bloy himself was an experienced [[officer]],<ref name="Bloy DB">{{Databank|sergeant-bloy|Sergeant Bloy}}</ref> the cadets of his squad,<ref name="Andor Explained" /> and even the overall membership of his regiment, were untested and not ready for the situation awaiting them when they were deployed to<ref name="Bloy DB" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Messenger" /> [[Ghorman]] amid growing anti-Imperial tensions on world<ref name="Bloy DB" /> in [[2 BBY]].<ref name="Messenger" />

==History==

Bloy's squad of inexperienced cadets was deployed to the [[Imperial office complex]] in [[Palmo]] on the planet Ghorman in 2 BBY. After disembarking from a [[Unidentified troop transport|troop transport]] in front of the complex the members of the squad lined up in [[Palmo Plaza]] while carrying their equipment before heading into the complex.<ref name="Messenger" /> The group was later present in the complex after [[Galactic Empire]] removed barriers to the plaza allowing a crowd of [[Ghorman (people)|Ghorman]] protestors to gather at the plaza.<ref name="Who Are You">{{Andor|Who Are You?}}</ref>

The squad was later ordered by [[Captain]] [[Kaido]] to patrol a barricade lane to the [[Monument to the Fallen]] at the center of the crowd. The squad exited the office complex equipped with [[riot shield]]s and [[E-11 medium blaster rifle|E-11 blaster rifles]] as the crowd began singing the [[We Are the Ghor|Ghorman National Anthem]]. As they slowly made their way along the barricade lane tenses grew between the unit and the crowd until an [[Unidentified Imperial sniper|Imperial sniper]] under orders from Kaido shot and [[death|killed]] one of the army troopers. See the man getting shot down, Bloy commanded his unit to open fire, beginning what would be known as the [[Ghorman Massacre]].<ref name="Who Are You" />
