The [[planet]] [[Naboo]] had a '''constitution'''. The constitution limited the [[Monarch of Naboo|monarch of the planet]] to two terms in office, although it was almost amended<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> in [[28 BBY]]<ref name="Date">The main story takes place 4 [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Invasion of Naboo]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places in [[32 BBY]].</ref> to allow for [[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] to continue to serve for more terms after the [[Invasion of Naboo]]<ref name="TSWB" /> but she refused to do so, though she admitted to her [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]], [[Sabé]] that she was tempted to.<ref name="Shadow" />

In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Amidala discussed the matter with [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], saying that she was relived when her two terms were up.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>
