'''Bryce Dallas Howard''' is an actress and director, best known for her performance in the [[2015]] film [[Wikipedia:Jurassic World|''Jurassic World'']] and her work writing and directing the 2015 short film ''Solemates''.<ref name="Mandalorian Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-mandalorian-revealed|text=''The Mandalorian'' First Image, Directors Revealed}}</ref>

Howard directed three episodes of the live-action ''[[Star Wars]]'' television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'',<ref name="Mandalorian Announcement" /> and one episode of the series ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]''.<ref name="Return of the Mandalorian">{{BOBF|Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian}}</ref> Howard had spent a lot of time on the set of ''Solo'', where she received filmmaking tips from her father, [[Ron Howard]], and from [[George Lucas]].<ref name="Ron Howard">{{WebCite|url=https://variety.com/2019/film/news/bryce-dallas-howard-the-mandalorian-star-wars-george-lucas-1203326358|text=What Bryce Dallas Howard Learned From George Lucas That She's Bringing to 'The Mandalorian'|author=Jordan Moreau|date=2019-09-06|archivedate=20191103202328}}</ref> She voiced [[Yaddle]] in ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (television series)|Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="The Sith Lord">{{TOTJ|The Sith Lord}}</ref>

Howard is the neice of actor [[Clint Howard]].<ref name="EW">{{WebCite|url=http://ew.com/movies/2018/04/21/han-solo-star-wars-clint-howard-character|text=Ron Howard reveals brother Clint Howard's Solo: A Star Wars Story character|author=Nick Romano|date=2018-04-21|archivedate=20180627134349}}</ref> She is also married to [[Seth Gabel]],<ref name="Seth Gabel">{{WebCite|url=https://people.com/movies/bryce-dallas-howard-marks-21-years-since-first-date-with-husband-seth-gabel|text=Bryce Dallas Howard Celebrates 21 Years Since First Date with Husband Seth Gabel: 'My Love'|date=2022-02-22|work=[[Wikipedia:People (magazine)|''People'']]|archivedate=20230604184145}}</ref> who voiced the [[M0|Bartender Droid]] in "[[Chapter 22: Guns for Hire]]".<ref name="Chapter 22">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 22: Guns for Hire}}</ref>
