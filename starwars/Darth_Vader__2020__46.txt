'''''Darth Vader'' 46''' is the forty-sixth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]] and [[Federico Blee]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[May 8]], [[2024]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE RAZING OF EXEGOL, PART ONE! DARTH VADER and the SCHISM IMPERIAL launch their boldest challenge to the power of PALPATINE with an assault on EXEGOL, the home of the EMPEROR'S greatest secrets and treasures! But if sheer brute force can't overcome the defenses of the SITH CITADEL, what terrifying new resources will the DARK LORD draw upon? Also: CAPTAIN ENRIC PRYDE faces his greatest test!''

==Plot summary==
On the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Sergia]], [[Sabé]], former [[handmaiden]] of [[Naboo]] and former associate of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], meets with [[Jedi]] pretender and [[thief]] [[Warba Calip]] at [[Sergia Seven Spaceport]] [[Bay Twelve]]. The two had previously arranged to collaborate in locating [[Luke Skywalker]]. They board [[Sabé's H-type Nubian yacht]] and take off into [[realspace|space]], where they discuss Luke and the risks they take in seeking him out in light of Vader.

Near the [[Red Honeycomb Zone]], [[Captain]] [[Corleque]] of the [[Schism Imperial]] prepares for a cargo drop-off with the [[Sith Eternal]] on [[Eta-class supply barge|''Eta''-class supply barge]] [[SW-0222]]. The Sith Eternal approach in a few of their [[Unidentified Sith Eternal starship model|starships]] to collect the cargo. As a few of the [[Sith acolyte|acolytes]] approach him, they look deeply into Corleque and see his intense anger towards Vader.

After the Sith Eternal leave, Corleque [[comlink|calls]] [[Administrator]] [[Sly Moore]] about the exchange, how they saw his hate for Vader. Overhearing, [[Senior Lieutenant]] [[Enric Pryde]] chimes in to say he should have been the one chosen for the exchange. Moore scoffs at the idea since Pryde does not understand the ideology of the [[Sith]]; they are all one another's enemy, so someone so devoted to Vader would have inadvertently revealed the subterfuge. Sending someone like Corleque would help keep the Sith Eternal's guard down. 

Moore and Pryde, aboard a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] enter the Red Honeycomb Nebula en route to [[Exegol]]. Within, they find a massive [[Unidentified summa-verminoth subspecies|subspecies]] of [[summa-verminoth]] with psycho-venomous abilities. Although the gargantuan creature sleeps and the shuttle is plated with [[kyberite]], the proximity of the creature caused significant pain to all aboard except for Vader. This includes the four [[cyborg]]s of [[M.A.R. Corps]] whose executive function has been overridden by Vader's [[droid brain]]s. After passing through the nebula, Moore asks Pryde what the summa-verminoth showed him, and he reveals it was a vision of him [[death|dying]] on Exegol.

The [[shuttle]] lands on Exegol, and the cultists angrily approach the unwelcome [[starship|ship]]. Vader leads the M.A.R. Corps members, armed with [[Darth Vader's electrostaff|his electrostaff]], to attack the cultists. The cultists send a cadre of powerful [[Tanker (Exegol)|Tankers]] to cover, but Vader shocks a number of them. Eventually, however, the Tankers overtake Vader and pin him to the ground. In victory, the cultists celebrate Pryde's grief at Vader's defeat and direct their workers to finish unloading the shipment so they can observe Vader's imminent death. 

With all of the cultists and Tankers distracted, a large group of Vader's [[Unidentified monster droid model|droids]] burst from the shipping [[crate]]s to attack the cultists. To the Sith Eternal's relief, though, they recognize that the only individual strong enough to claim the [[Kyber crystal|kyber]] of the [[Kyber Temple]] is the incapacitated Vader. The Tankers gaze at the fallen Sith, only to see that the split helmet reveals [[droid]] components.

Finally revealing his deceit, Vader slices open the container in which he hid and storms the Temple of the Kyber. Using [[the Force]], he stimulates the large pile of kyber crystals, releasing a massive amount of energy that takes out many cultists. He then [[telekinesis|raises]] one large crystal, only for the temple to collapse. Pryde rushes to the scene in grief, grabbing [[Darth Vader's armor|Vader's helmet]], which was really that of the decoy. He soon sees Vader emerge from the rubble with the massive crystal in tow on a [[repulsorlift]].

Nevertheless, Vader's enemies are not yet fully defeated. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] cyborgs were freed from their servitude by the energy burst and a cultist, though impressed Vader learned to deceive, manages to trigger a massive [[laser]] before being defeated by Vader. Pryde assumes the laser was meant to hit their location and that Vader managed to deflect it. In reality, the blast was aimed at the summa-verminoth who now awakens.
