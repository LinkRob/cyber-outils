'''Analysis droids''',<ref name="Episode 2" /> also called '''analyst droids''' or '''information droids''',<ref name="SoloOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> were [[droid]]s in use to analyze data. [[SP-4 analysis droid]]s and [[JN-66 analysis droid]]s were popular models of analysis droids.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

Analysis droids were designed to assist in the analysis of everything from physical items to [[Sentience|sentient]] behavior. They were used for a variety of tasks including scientific research and law enforcement.<ref name="Helmet2">''{{HelmetCollectionCite|2|Databank A-Z}}''</ref> Analysis droids saw use in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]], where they assisted [[Jedi]] with their assignments,<ref name="Episode 2" /> working alongside members of the [[Jedi Order]]. Many analysis droids, such as [[Russo-ISC]] who assisted [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] in their investigation of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|hangar bombing]],<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> were designed to analyze crime scenes and even interview witnesses. Analysis droids worked alongside Jedi [[Jedi investigator|investigators]] and various law enforcement organizations and officials relevant to any case. At first, analysis droids were criticized for being too literal and unable to make leaps of insight and imagination. In time, more experienced analysis units became able to make such 'intuitive' judgments — or at least they appeared to.<ref name="Helmet2" />

Although the Jedi owned a large number of analysis droids, they could be found far from the towering walls of the Jedi Temple. In [[Medcenter|hospitals]] across the galaxy, analysis droids analyzed medical data, particularly in the field of infection control. Their sensitive [[photoreceptor]]s and imaging scanning were invaluable for examinations, and the droids made initial assessments to determine if patients needed to be seen by a specialist. During the [[Clone Wars]], many analysis droids worked in the [[Grand Imperial Medical Facility|Grand Republic Medical Facility]] on [[Coruscant]], where they streamlined the processing of injured [[clone trooper]]s, who arrived in large numbers almost constantly.<ref name="R247">{{BuildR2Cite|47|Droid Directory}}</ref>

Analyst droids were also often specialized in analyzing battle strategy. [[AP-5]] was a [[RA-7 protocol droid]] who served as an analyst droid during the [[Battle of Ryloth|Ryloth campaign]].<ref name="The Forgotten Droid">{{Rebels|The Forgotten Droid}}</ref>
