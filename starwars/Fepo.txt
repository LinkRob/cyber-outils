'''Fepo''' was a [[Sullustan]] [[gambler]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> during [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> They frequently played [[Kessel Sabacc]] with the [[human]] [[Kufu]] near [[Kijimiko Square]] in [[Kijimi City|Kijimi city]]. Fepo had [[Color|brown]] skin and black eyes.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fepo first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Ubisoft]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. They were one of several sabacc players that could be played against by [[Kay Vess]] in Kessel Sabacc, an optional activity.<ref name="Outlaws" />
