'''Finn's blaster pistol''' was a [[Glie-44 blaster pistol|Glie-44]] [[blaster pistol]] used by [[Finn]], a defected [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] from the [[First Order]] who joined the [[Resistance]] in [[34 ABY]] to aid them in their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the First Order. Finn's lifetime of combat training made him adept with a wide variety of small arms, well beyond the standard-issue weaponry of the First Order. The pistol had a power cartridge as ammunition and a reactant gas port cap in its stock.<ref name="TLJVD" /> He used it as a sidearm during his [[Mission to Canto Bight]]<ref name="Episode VIII" /> and a [[mission to Kijimi]].<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
