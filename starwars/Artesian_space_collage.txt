The '''Artesian space collage''' was an art form that originated on the planet [[Artesia]].<ref name="CE">{{CSWECite|I||Artesian space collage}}</ref> In [[17 ABY/Legends|17 ABY]], the mechanic [[Cole Fardreamer]] compared the appearance of the droid [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]], when various tools were hanging from him, to a part of an Artesian space collage.<ref name="NR">''[[The New Rebellion]]''</ref>
