The '''Enrivi system''' was located in the [[Metatessu sector]] within an intersecting portion of the [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="BFTC" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> The [[star system]] was situated at the coordinates R-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Timelines" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref> two [[starship]]s of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]]&mdash;the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]'' and the [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunship]] ''[[Apailana's Promise]]''&mdash;were [[Battle in the Enrivi system|ambushed]] in the Enrivi system by [[Imperial Military|forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], consisting of a [[light cruiser]] and a [[squadron]] of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], just thirty [[Standard hour|hours]] after escaping a [[Battle in the Metatessu sector|battle]] in another [[Unidentified star system (Metatessu sector)|star system]].<ref name="BFTC" />

The Imperial forces had followed a [[hypermatter]] trail being left by the ''Thunderstrike''{{'s}} damaged [[hyperdrive]], but were ultimately obliterated in the ensuing battle, with all Imperial personnel being [[Death|killed]]. After leaving the Enrivi system, the rebel [[company]] discovered that its [[starship]]s had been tracked.<ref name="BFTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Enrivi system first appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="BFTC" />
