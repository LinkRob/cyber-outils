The '''Droidekas Unit Expansion''' ('''SWQ36''') is an expansion to the tabletop game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. An updated version of the earlier [[Droidekas Unit Expansion]], it was released on [[February 13]], [[2026]] as part of Atomic Mass Games' overhaul of the game.

==Publisher's summary==
''This Unit Expansion for Star Wars<sup>TM</sup>: Legion includes four unit miniatures usable by Separatist Alliance armies. This re-issue product, previously SWL50, now SWQ36, brings the Droidekas to the refreshed Star Wars: Legion. With updated packaging, featuring new art, along with updated cards to the new SWQ multilingual format, this product is an exciting product for Separatist Alliance army players.''

==Contents==
*4 Miniatures (2 wheelmode)
*1 Unit Card
