A '''drumheller harp''' was a type of [[Color|brown]] [[Music|percussion]] instrument consisting of a wide circular base with a central pole with three [[O'Tawa cymbal]]s and then a [[Tryna chime/Canon|tryna chime]] at the top. An additional pole extended from one side of the base with a [[Screamer gong/Canon|screamer gong]] at the top. At least six optional [[centressar string]]s could be strung between the base and the lowest of the cymbals, which was also the largest.<ref name="VE" />

In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], which includes the drumheller harp's appearance, to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the [[Max Rebo Band]]'s [[Weequay]] [[Musician|drummer]], [[Ak-rev]],<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> played a drumheller harp alongside a much larger [[drum]] in the throne room of the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]'s [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. During a performance of the song "[[Jedi Rocks]]," he alternated between playing the large drum with both drum sticks and the screamer gong and O'Tawa cymbals on his drumheller harp with one drum stick.<ref name="ROTJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
A drumheller harp first appeared in the [[1997]] [[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition|''Special Edition'']] re-release of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], having been added in new footage to Max Rebo Band scene in Jabba's Palace.<ref name="ROTJ" /> In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the instrument was first identified in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'',<ref name="VE" /> although the name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used in the [[1998]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]].<ref name="VD">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
