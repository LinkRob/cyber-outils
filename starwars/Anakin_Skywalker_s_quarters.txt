'''Anakin Skywalker's quarters''' were the private quarters of the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]. They were located in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Temple Gallery 16">{{Databank|url=jedi-temple-location-gallery|title=Jedi Temple Location Gallery}}; image #16 {{C|of 22}}</ref> While most [[Jedi]] quarters were usually simple and sparsely furnished,<ref name="Temple Gallery 15">{{Databank|url=jedi-temple-location-gallery|title=Jedi Temple Location Gallery}}; image #15 {{C|of 22}}</ref> Skywalker's room was abundantly decorated with old [[Podracing]] [[Painting|posters]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], he would spend his off-[[Standard hour|hours]] tinkering with various pieces of machinery.<ref name="Temple Gallery 16" />

==Behind the scenes==
In "[[The Rise of Clovis]]", Anakin's quarters is seen to have keep a toy version of a prototype Jedi interceptor and absentmindedly plays with it, much like [[Luke Skywalker]] does with [[Luke's T-16 skyhopper|his toy]] [[T-16 skyhopper|T-16 model]].<ref name="The Rise of Clovis Trivia">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-rise-of-clovis-trivia-gallery|text="The Rise of Clovis" Trivia Gallery}}</ref>
