'''Arcetron''' was a [[Color|silver-colored]]<ref name="Fallen Order" /> [[metal]] that was a common choice<ref name="TLC" /> for use in hilts of [[Jedi]] [[lightsaber]]s.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> the Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] discovered some arcetron in the [[Ice Caves]] on the [[planet]] [[Zeffo]] and kept it for making modifications to his [[Jaro Tapal|former master's]] [[Jaro Tapal's lightsaber|lightsaber]].<ref name="Fallen Order" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], arcetron appeared in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]]. In the game, it is one of the twenty-four materials players can collect in order to customize the appearance of Cal Kestis' lightsaber.<ref name="Fallen Order" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Arcetron/Legends|arcetron]] first appeared in the [[2001]] video game [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan (video game)|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan'']], developed by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="SWOBW">[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan (video game)|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan'']]</ref> It would later be identified in the [[2006]] reference book ''[[The New Essential Guide to Droids]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Droids">''[[The New Essential Guide to Droids]]''</ref>
