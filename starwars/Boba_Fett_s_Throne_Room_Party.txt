"'''Boba Fett's Throne Room Party'''" is a [[Canon|non-canon]] animated [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short that aired online on [[February 17]], [[2022]] as part of the ''[[LEGO STAR WARS: Celebrate the Season]]'' webseries.

==Official description==
''When Boba Fett and Fennec Shand step away for a mission, their underlings throw an impromptu party in the throne room that spirals out of control.<ref name="Throne Room" />''

==Plot summary==
After polishing his helmet, [[Boba Fett]] speaks with [[Fennec Shand]] before the two depart his throne room at [[Boba Fett's Palace]]. After Fett and Shand depart aboard the ''[[Slave I]]'', [[Rystáll Sant]] encourages the other members of Fett's court to throw a disco party. A [[Gamorrean]] roasts some chicken while two [[Jawa]]s fight over a drumstick. A [[Weequay]] sits on Fett's throne, prompting a fight among the other courtiers including a Jawa and a Gamorrean for the right to sit on the throne. The fight destroys Fett's throne.

Shortly later, the Weequay is contacted via [[hologram]] by Fett, who approaches his palace in the ''Slave I''. The courtiers proceed to tidy up the court but struggle to reassemble the throne. Fett and Shand soon enter the throne room after parking the ''Slave I''. Wile the courtiers watch nervously, Fett finds a drumstick on the throne which he bites. He shares the drumstick with Shand, who finishes it. Everyone laughs with joy and relief.
