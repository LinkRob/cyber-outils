The '''''Apogee''''' was a [[light cruiser]] in service to the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Xodlak family]], serving as part of their [[Xodlak fleet|planetary defense fleet]], though it was stationed in orbit as a defense platform. The ''Apogee'' was activated when the Xodlak family declared an emergency, allowing it to use its private military forces and call its family members from across the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] to serve on its [[starship]]s, which would be used to "colonize" a [[Nyix]] [[Mining|mine]] owned by the [[Agbui]]. The ''Apogee'' joined the [[Chiss frigate|frigate]] ''[[Midsummer]]'', the [[flagship]] of [[Task Force Xodlak]] under the command of [[Senior Captain]] [[Irizi'in'daro]].<ref name="Greater Good" />
