'''''Disney Magic Kingdoms''''' is a world-building game developed by Gameloft. Seven ''[[Star Wars]]''-themed updates were released between [[2019]]<ref name="Date" /> and [[2023]].<ref name="Official site" />

==Plot summary==
Players build their own Disney park using characters and attractions from Disney, Pixar, and ''Star Wars''. Maleficent has cursed the kingdom, and it's up to Merlin, [[Mickey Mouse]], and the other characters to remove the curse and restore the kingdom to its former glory. The game has a dedicated ''Star Wars'' "land," with permanent and limited-time ''Star Wars'' characters and attractions that players can unlock.<ref name="Official site" />

*'''Main Storyline (December 17, 2019):''' In the ''Star Wars'' portion of the main storyline, [[C-3PO]] is tasked with exploring an uncharted territory in the player's kingdom for the [[Resistance]]. After welcoming C-3PO, players unlock the ''Star Wars'' area in the kingdom.<ref name="Date" />

[[File:DMK_Star_Wars_Promo_Screen.jpg|250px|thumb|The event's splash screen]]
*'''"A Galaxy Far, Far Away..." Event (2019):''' [[Finn]], [[Poe Dameron]], and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] set out to stop [[Armitage Hux|General Hux]], [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], and the [[First Order]] from occupying territory in the kingdom.<ref name="Official site" />

*'''"The Mandalorian" Event ([[November 10]], [[2020]]):''' Framed as a flashback, C-3PO reads off a [[Datacard]] from the [[Nevarro]] chapter of the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]]. Set during the events of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''{{'s}} [[The Mandalorian Season One|first season]], [[Din Djarin|The Mandalorian]], [[Greef Karga]], and [[Carasynthia Dune|Cara Dune]] work together to get past the Imperials in order to allow The Mandalorian and [[Din Grogu|The Child]] to escape from Nevarro.<ref name="Official site" />

*'''"A New Hope" Event ([[April 20]], [[2021]]):''' As C-3PO reviews his data banks, he recalls the series of events that led to the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Official site" />

*'''"The Mandalorian" Season 2 Event ([[November 9]], 2021):''' [[Ahsoka Tano]], [[Fennec Shand]], and [[Boba Fett]] fight [[blurrg]]s, [[Knobby white ice spider|ice spiders]], and [[reptavian]]s.<ref name="Official site" />

*'''"The Empire Strikes Back" Event ([[April 26]], [[2022]]):''' C-3PO continues to review his data banks, this time on the events following the [[Battle of Hoth]]. He encounters a number of time-index errors before identifying the correct memories of [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Boba Fett]]. Luke is on [[Dagobah]], after his vision quest and preparing to leave [[Yoda]]; meanwhile, [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] "welcomes" [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and the bounty hunter [[Boba Fett]] to [[Cloud City]]. At that point, C-3PO experiences destabilization of his memory subroutines and they all start coming out at once; frustrated, he shouts "ENOUGH!" and that seems to resolve the problem. The remaining memories show Fett finding he's being tailed by [[IG-88B|IG-88]] and laying a successful ambush for the rival hunter. Meanwhile, Yoda contemplates the will of the Force and asks [[R2-D2]] to watch over Luke.<ref name="Official site" />

*'''"Return of the Jedi & The Mandalorian" Event ([[May 9]], 2023):''' As C-3PO finally isolates the source of his data corruption, an Imperial virus in the form of [[Gideon|Moff Gideon]] takes over C-3PO's memory system. The virus attempts to rewrite historical events from the perspective of the Empire, particularly the confrontation between [[Darth Sidious|Emperor Palpatine]] and Luke Skywalker. C-3PO's antivirus protocol eventually destroys the virus, with everything returning to normal.<ref name="Official site" />

==Gameplay==
[[File:Magic_Kingdoms-Far_Far_Away.jpg|250px|thumb|Kylo Ren, Rey, and other ''Star Wars'' elements in the game]]
A ''Star Wars''-themed area is available for players to unlock after doing storyline quests with C-3PO, containing three unlockable plots of land. The area is surrounded by buildings, objects, and decorations from [[Tatooine]], [[Hoth]], [[Endor]], [[Jakku]], [[Takodana]], and [[Ahch-To]]. ''Star Wars'' attractions placed in the area garner 15% more magic.

Permanent characters and attractions are unlocked throughout the storyline. Limited-time characters and attractions are only available for the duration of an event, and then made available through Legendary Chests that players can buy with gems or real money.<ref name="Official site" />
