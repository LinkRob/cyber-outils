The '''Castell system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colonies]] region of [[the galaxy]]. The [[planet]] orbited within the system, and was in turn orbited by [[Sienar Fleet Systems]] [[Orbital Shipyard CC-24]]. During the reign of the [[Galactic Empire]], a [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian's crew|group]] of [[Thief|thieves]] led by the [[smuggler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] stole [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] personal pleasure [[yacht]], the ''[[Imperialis]]'', from CC-24. A trio of [[Star Destroyer]]s were sent to recover the yacht before it could escape and destroy the [[shipyard]] as punishment for allowing the robbery to take place. The Imperials were successful in destroying CC-24, but failed to stop the ''Imperialis'' escaping.<ref name="Lando 2" />
