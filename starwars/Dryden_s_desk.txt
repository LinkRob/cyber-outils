'''Dryden's desk''' was a desk owned by the [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]] that was located in [[Dryden's study]] on the [[yacht]] ''[[First Light]]'' during the [[Imperial Era]]. The desk was built with an [[obsidian]] base after Dryden plundered it from a [[Unidentified Sith temple|Sith temple]]. The [[stone]] was carved with the warding spells,<ref name="Solo Guide" /> as well as an [[Domabesh|inscription]] reading "Our temple honouring [[Sith Lord]] [[Exar Kun]]."<ref name="Solo Guide" /><ref name="Inscription" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The central desk, which represents the administrative seat of his power, is a bespoke piece of furniture. The stone base, that Dryden has plundered from the Sith temple of Exar Kun, is made from polished obsidian and decorated with carved hieroglyphs representing spells that protected its former home.|[[Lee Sandales]]|<ref name="The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>}}
Dryden's desk first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" /> It also was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Solo Guide" /> The stone base's [[Domabesh|inscription]] was translated by the Twitter user AurekFonts.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|AurekFonts|status/1339662884318576641|AurekFonts|date=2020-12-17|quote=Put it all together, and we get a final translation that is wholly intelligible:<br />OUR TEMPLE<br />HONOURING<br />SITH LORD<br />EXAR KUN|archivedate=20201218011804}}</ref>
