'''By Bluss''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Leyakian]] who served as a [[senator]] in the [[Separatist Senate]] during the [[Clone Wars]].

==Biography==
By Bluss<ref name="EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/heroes-on-both-sides-episode-guide|text="Heroes on Both Sides" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> was a [[Politics|politician]] who, along with the [[Head of State]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]], created the [[Separatist Senate]].<ref name="Chancellors Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|Chancellors, Senators and States Persons of the Republic}}</ref> Bluss served in the legislative body as a [[senator]]<ref name="HOBS" /> and as a member of the Senate's [[Peace Faction]].<ref name="Rise" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], Bluss was present in the Senate chamber where Senator [[Mina Bonteri]] of [[Onderon]] made a plea with other senators that peace talks should be proposed with the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] to end the war peacefully. Bluss supported Mina by voting "aye", as she [[Confederate–Republic peace initiative|proposed]] the Confederacy to attempt to establish peace talks with the Republic, while other senators like [[Voe Atell]] and [[Punn Rimbaud]] voted "nay" to continue the war.<ref name="HOBS" />

==Behind the scenes==
By Bluss first appeared in "[[Heroes on Both Sides]],"<ref name="HOBS" /> the tenth episode in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which released on [[November 19]], [[2010]].<ref name="EpGuideBook">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref> The senator was first identified by name in the accompanying [[StarWars.com]] [[The Clone Wars Episode Guides|Episode Guide]], which released on the same day.<ref name="EG" /> Despite appearing in concept art made for "[[The Disappeared, Part I]],"<ref name="The Disappeared, Part I CAG" /> the eighth episode of the show's [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|sixth season]].<ref name="Disappeared">{{TCW|The Disappeared, Part I}}</ref> Bluss did not appear in the final product.<ref name="Disappeared" /> Bluss appeared in an animatic for a cancelled episode later adapted in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Dark Disciple]]'' in a scene<ref name="Legacyvideo">{{SWYouTube|vnH1JKr4o_8|Star Wars: The Clone Wars Legacy|sw_url=video/the-clone-wars-legacy}}</ref> at the gala for Dooku's [[Raxian Humanitarian Award]].<ref name="DD">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>
