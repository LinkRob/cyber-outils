'''Artemis Oophanoe''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|boy]] who attended the [[Jedi academy]] based at the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. A member of the school's [[Drama Club]], Oophanoe's [[Color/Legends|dark]] [[Robe/Legends|robes]] led some, such as [[Victor Starspeeder]], to believe that he was secretly [[Sith training|training]] to become a [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]]. This mistaken belief was compounded by Oophanoe's carrying of a cooler containing his allergy medications, producing a cold [[Atmosphere/Legends|air]] around him similar to the feeling of the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side of the Force]]. In actuality, Oophanoe was a studious pupil and a helpful friend. He and Starspeeder became friends after Starspeeder joined the Drama Club and he saved Starspeeder from a [[Gorax/Legends|gorax]] on [[Endor/Legends|Endor]].<ref name="newclass" />
