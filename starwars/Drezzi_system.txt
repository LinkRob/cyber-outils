The '''Drezzi system''' was located in the [[Esstran sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] in [[the galaxy]]. The [[Star system|system]] was located in grid square Q-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Drezzi system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the article "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> The [[Drezzi system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion]] released on [[August 26]], [[2009]].<ref name="TEAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
