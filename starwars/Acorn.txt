An '''acorn''' was a type of [[seed]] that could grow into the [[sanctuary tree]]s of the [[moon]] [[Endor]].<ref name="Life Debt" /> [[Sweet roasted spire acorn]]s were a [[Foodstuff|food]] sold in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] during the [[High Republic Era]] that shared their name with acorns, but were actually made from cooked [[rootwood]].<ref name="Quest">''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]''</ref> The [[Ewok]] [[Wicket Wystri Warrick]] gave an acorn to [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who later planted it in a pot of soil from the [[planet]] [[Chandrila]].<ref name="Life Debt" />
