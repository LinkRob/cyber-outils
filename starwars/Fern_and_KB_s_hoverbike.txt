'''Fern and KB's hoverbike''' was a modified two&ndash;being [[hoverbike]] owned by the [[human]] [[Fern]] and her friend [[KB]] on the [[planet]] [[At Attin]] during the [[New Republic Era]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:FernAndKBsHoverbike-SC-RealAdventure.jpg|thumb|300px|left|Fern and KB's hoverbike was designed to carry both girls.]]
The [[hoverbike]] stared as simple two&ndash;being speeder owned by the [[human]] [[Fern]] and her friend [[KB]]<ref name="DB" /> on the [[planet]] [[At Attin]] during the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="Real Adventure">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}}</ref> The pair modified the speeder with a more powerful [[engine]]. It was 1.64 [[meter]]s long, 1.12 meters wide, 0.84 meters high with a side seat.<ref name="DB" />

==History==
Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SCDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> the day before the [[Career Assessment Test]], Fern and KB rode to [[At Attin Middle School Gamma|school]] on the bike, taking a short cut through the [[forest]]. After school while taking a jump out of a tunnel the speeder scraped the ground and eventually came to a stop. After getting it back to [[KB's home]], KB noticed that the [[power converter]] was burnt out and they needed a replacement.<ref name="Real Adventure" />

==Behind the scenes==
The hoverbike first appeared in "[[This Could Be a Real Adventure]],"<ref name="Real Adventure" /> the first episode of the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'', which aired on [[December 2]], [[2024]].<ref name="Early">{{SW|url=news/skeleton-crew-premiere-date|text=''Star Wars: Skeleton Crew'' Will Debut Early|date=2024-11-26}}</ref>
