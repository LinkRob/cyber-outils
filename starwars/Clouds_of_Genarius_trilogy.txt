The '''Clouds of Genarius trilogy''' is a trilogy of adventures for the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign written by [[Jason Nichols]] and [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]]. The adventures had the players work for [[Karae Nalvas|Fesvk Wefos]] tracking down two lost employees. In doing so they met the [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] [[Alec (Jedi)|Alec]], who revealed that Wefos was actually preparing to attack cities on [[Genarius]] using the [[Conkesta]].

The adventures were supported by an interactive at [[Gen Con Game Fair 2001]], and an article on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website.
