'''''Bantha Tracks'' 5''' was the fifth issue of ''[[Bantha Tracks (department)|Bantha Tracks]]'', previously known as the ''Official Star Wars Fan Club'' newsletter. It was the only issue of the newsletter to be licensed by the short-lived [[Lucasfilm]] subsidiary [[Black Falcon]].

==Contents==
The issue is notable for being the first source to identify the the digital display on [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett's]] chest plate as a "[[Digital life support system readout]]" as well as being the first mention of "Imperial Shocktroopers"<ref name="BT5" /> who would later be named [[Mandalorian/Legends|Mandalorians]].<ref name="The Search Begins">[[Star Wars (1977) 68|''Star Wars'' (1977) 68]]</ref>
