The '''CX squad'''<ref name="Declassified" /> was a [[special forces]] [[squad]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] created of a batch of [[Clone X trooper|clone assassins]] of the [[Advanced Science Division]]'s [[Clone X trooper division]]. The CX squad was first deployed by [[Doctor|Dr.]] [[Royce Hemlock]] during [[Clone Force 99]]'s [[Skirmish on Wayland|rescue]] of [[Omega]] and [[Cloning|clone]] [[prisoner]]s. The squad was made up of [[Sniper CloneX3]], [[Sniper CloneX4]], [[Sniper CloneX5]], and [[Unidentified Clone X trooper|another Clone X trooper]]. During the skirmish on [[Wayland]], the entire squad of troopers were activated by Royce Hemlock. Through the combined efforts of Clone Force 99 and [[Vik (clone)|Vik]] in [[Tantiss Base]], the squad alongside [[CX-2]] was wiped out.<ref name="Cavalry" />
