'''Blade Three''' was the [[callsign]] attributed to the third member of the [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] [[Blade Squadron]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Gina Moonsong]] served as Blade Three during battles such as [[Battle of Endor|Endor]]<ref name="BS" /> and [[Battle of Malastare (Galactic Civil War)|Malastare]]<ref name="BS: Zero Hour">{{InsiderCite|160|Blade Squadron: Zero Hour}}</ref>
