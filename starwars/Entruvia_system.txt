The '''Entruvia system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Halla sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square R-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the site of<ref name="Battletnamia">{{Battlenamia|other=[[Entruvia Conflict]]|system=Entruvia [[Star system|system]]}}</ref> the [[Entruvia Conflict]] between the [[Ordu Aspectu]] and the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Aphra">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 6|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 6]]</ref>
