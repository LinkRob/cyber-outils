The '''EE-730''' was a [[transport]] manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]] that could hold six passengers and crew total. It could be equipped with six beds. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] had at least one EE-730, which the Alliance used in a mission around the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Yavin]]. The transport was [[Ambush on Taanab|ambushed]] on [[Taanab]] and [[Caluan Ematt]] was captured.<ref name="SR" />
