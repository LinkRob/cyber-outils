'''''COMPNOR Recruitment''''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] created by [[Dasita Lyros]]. It depicted two [[Imperial officer]]s, one of whom resembled the [[artist]] herself, while the other was modeled on [[Captain]] [[Edmos Khurgee]]. Because Lyros used to choose her models from people who already shared a good relationship with [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order|COMPNOR]], rumors started that she and Khurgee were romantically involved. The poster featured a [[Color|yellow]] and red background with the words "Loyal? Prove It!" at the top and the words on the bottom reading "Join COMPNOR Today!" with the smaller print below giving the full name for COMPNOR, all [[Writing|written]] in [[High Galactic]].<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Behind the scenes==
''COMPNOR Recruitment'' is an [[in-universe]] artwork in the [[canon]] art book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''. It was illustrated by [[Russell Walks]].<ref name="Propaganda" /> It had earlier appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] sourcebook ''[[Star Wars: Imperial Handbook: A Commander's Guide]]''. A similar situation was seen with ''[[Strength & Obedience]]'' and ''[[Expose, Pursue, Destroy]]'', which had both also earlier appeared in the same book.<ref name="IHB">''[[Star Wars: Imperial Handbook: A Commander's Guide]]''</ref> The illustration of [[Dasita Lyros]] was based on a photo of a costumer [[Bria Silivren]] by Amy King.<ref name="Dasita Lyros">{{DeviantArt|username=bria-silivren|url=bria-silivren/art/Imperial-Officer-1-119433189|name=[[Bria Silivren]]|text=Imperial Officer 1}}</ref>
