'''DTS-series dismantler droids''', or simply '''dismantler droids''', were a type of [[demolition droid]] manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]] and used by the [[Galactic Empire]] for dismantling [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]]s at [[Reklam Station]].

==History==
[[File:Dismantler_droid.png|thumb|left|250px|Chopper caught in the grip of a dismantler droid.]]
During the early rebellion's [[Mission to Reklam Station|mission]] to [[Reklam Station]], three of the dismantlers were activated by [[Sabine Wren]] during their flight. One of the [[droid]]s were destroyed by the ''[[Phantom]]''{{'s}} [[laser cannon]], another by [[Rex]]'s [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|DC-17]] [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|blaster pistol]], and the third was hit by a lightning storm. However, the last one survived and later attacked and damaged [[C1-10P|Chopper]]. The droid attacked Sabine and [[Garazeb Orrelios|Zeb]], nearly [[Death|killing]] them; even withstanding firepower from their [[WESTAR-35 blaster pistol]] and [[AB-75 bo-rifle]], until Rex came to their aid, smacking the droid with a nearby crane arm. He almost pushed it over the edge until the droid attacked with its [[flamethrower]]. The old [[Cloning|clone]] then shoved the droid into a crevice, jamming it in and shooting it, heavily damaging it. The [[captain]] pushed the droid into [[Yarma]]'s lightning storm and destroyed it.<ref name="Steps Into Shadow" />

==Armament==
Dismantler droids were equipped with [[blaster]]s, strong mechanical claws, high-power flamethrowers, and a razor cutter under their claws. They were resilient and could survive several blaster shots.<ref name="Steps Into Shadow" />
