'''Chalcopyrite''', is a common [[mineral]] found in [[the galaxy]]. It was used in various equipment including [[blaster]]s.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Chalcopyrite first appeared in the [[canon]] continuity in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' that was published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game it was used as an upgrade material for the player's blaster.<ref name="Outlaws" />

It was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends]] in the [[1990]] sourcebook ''[[Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters]]'' for [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]] by [[West End Games]].<ref name="Galaxy Guide 6">''[[Galaxy Guide 6: Tramp Freighters]]''</ref>

[[In-universe|Out of universe]], chalcopyrite is a copper iron sulfide mineral that was a common source for copper throughout history.<ref name="Geology.com">{{WebCite|url=https://geology.com/minerals/chalcopyrite.shtml|text=Chalcopyrite
The world's most important ore of copper for at least five thousand years|archivedate=20241229125216}}</ref>
