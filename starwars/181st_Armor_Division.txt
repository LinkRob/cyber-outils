The '''181st Armor Division''' was an [[Tank|armored]] [[division]] of [[clone trooper]]s in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

The division was identified by various combinations of [[Color|green]] markings on their [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] and consisted of standard clone [[trooper]]s as well as [[clone sharpshooter]]s, [[clone trooper officer]]s, and [[heavy weapons clone trooper]]s. Respectively, the [[soldier]]s of the unit were armed with [[DC-15A blaster carbine]]s, [[Valken-38 blaster rifle|Valken-38xs]], [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|DC-17 hand blasters]], and [[DC-15A blaster rifle]]s or [[DC-15LE]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

The 181st Armor Division utilized [[infantry]], [[AT-series|All-terrain vehicles]] such as [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s, and [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut]]s that allowed the 181st to conduct safe [[transport]] in harsh environments and reinforce Republic [[regiment]]s that were stuck in remote locations.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 181st Armor Division first appeared in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' released by [[Electronic Arts]] in [[2017]] and were the default clone trooper skin for [[Kashyyyk]]. The clone trooper skin only appears by default on Kashyyyk on the large and small-scale versions of the multiplayer map, and in all modes.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> The 181st Armor Division was not named until an update for the game on [[February 27]], [[2019]].<ref name="Battlefront Forum">{{EA|subdomain=battlefront-forums|url=discussion/130124/community-transmission-clone-troopers|text=Community Transmission &ndash; Clone Troopers}}</ref>

With the update, the appearance of the 181st clone troopers was also slightly modified in that the green coloring was slightly darker, different patterns were added, and equipment was modified.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> The 181st Armor Division was originally intended to be named the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[Garrison]] prior to the release of the update.<ref name="Names">{{Twitter|JayKingIngram|status/1098394258476658689|Jay Ingram|quote=Name/legion changes below (original -> new): • Mid Rim Garrison -> 181st Armor Division • 87th Rangers -> 41st Ranger Platoon|archivedate=20200411225216}}</ref>
