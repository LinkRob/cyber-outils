During the [[Fenril crisis]], a '''funeral''' was held for [[Nagai rebellion]] leader [[Garlev]] on [[Cloud City]]. The Nagai was [[Crisis on Cloud City|killed]] by a [[Reyna Oskure|masked figure]] while trying to get essential information to [[Han Solo]] and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]. Garlev was injured as his ship crashed on a landing platform, but was assassinated in a medical area while waiting for Solo to arrive.<ref name="SW3" />
