'''Bababbb''' was a [[Winter/Legends|winter]] month in a [[Unidentified calendar (Raspar system)|calendar]] used on a [[Raspar system/Legends|Raspar system]] [[Unidentified planet (West Fissure)|world]]. On the calendar, Bababbb was the eighth of nine months, following [[Dha]] and preceding [[Aaaaaalt]]. During this [[Time/Legends|time]], the [[Atmosphere/Legends|atmosphere]] usually had a pink [[Color/Legends|coloration]] and [[glacial cyst]]s were expected. A weather forecast recommended [[overnothing]]s during Bababbb.<ref name="MAGL" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bababbb was mentioned in a one-page weather forecast titled "Region 29: The West Fissure," written by [[Bob Carrau]] for [[1993]]'s ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that collects concept designs and photographs from various [[George Lucas]] projects.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]] to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
