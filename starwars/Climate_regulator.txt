A '''climate regulator''' was a device that puffed out small clouds of [[Water|mist]] at the push of a button.<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Iana Percei]] wore such a device around her neck to deal with the [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]]'s [[desert]] climate, though it sometimes made her voice phlegmy.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
A climate regulator appeared in the [[2022]] novel [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Convergence" />
