"'''Deader than a Triton Moon'''" is a short story written by [[Jason Fry]], that was originally exclusive to [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]]. It was released on [[August 19]], [[2008]]. On [[October 1]], [[2012]], it was republished on [[Unbound Worlds|Suvudu]], a subsidiary website of [[Random House]] publishing.

==Plot summary==
When he sees a youngster carrying a [[Tract (object)|tract]] of [[Gactimus]], a religious figure revered on the [[Triton Moons|moons of Triton]], an older [[Shandy Fanaso]] thinks back to his own particular dealings with the religion. The adherents of Gactimus' teachings preach intense asceticism, such that Triton's moon have a reputation for being "dead" – no fun at all. Young Shady figured out a loophole in laws and scripture that let him open up a [[Cantina/Legends|club]] on his [[Starship/Legends|ship]] while docked at [[Triton Besh]]. The followers of Gactimus revised their rules to end such offense, and Shady's punishment was to spend [[Standard year/Legends|years]] publicly reciting the hundreds of verses of the tracts of Gactimus.
