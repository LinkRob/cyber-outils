'''Catkit''' was an [[Alzarian]] who served in the [[Nihil]] as a [[She'ar]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Script" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|TB}}</ref> Catkit attended a [[Gala on Waskiro|gala]] hosted by [[Waskiro]]'s [[Royal House of Jadalla]] along with the gala's guest of honor, [[Marchion Ro]], [[Eye of the Nihil]]. When [[comlink|comms]] to fellow She'ar [[Vulax]] became disrupted, Catkit investigated a [[Palace of Jadalla|palace]] balcony to find Vulax deep in combat with two [[Jedi]] and the [[Sephi]] [[Tey Sirrek]]. Catkit took on the latter in [[unarmed combat|combat]], and was subsequently [[flash grenade|flash-banged]] by Sirrek's [[droid]], [[SK-0T]]. In Catkit's blinded state, he was kicked off the side of the balcony by Sirrek, [[death|dying]] as he hit the rocks below.<ref name="Script" />

==Behind the scenes==
Catkit first appeared in flashbacks in ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]'', a [[2024]] audio drama written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Tempest Breaker">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]''</ref> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The spelling of his name and his pronouns were later confirmed with the release of the audio drama's [[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker (script)|script]] in [[2025]].<ref name="Script" />
