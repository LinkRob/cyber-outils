'''Aidan Barton''' portrayed [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] as infants in [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Looper" /> Though uncredited, he is given special thanks in the film's end credits.<ref name="SWArchive">{{SWArchive|url=movies/episode-iii|text=''Star Wars'' Episode III: ''Revenge of the Sith''}}</ref>

==Biography==
[[File:Aidan_Barton_set.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The infant Aidan Barton on set]]
Barton is the son of the film's editor, [[Roger Barton]], and his wife [[Andrea Wagner-Barton]]. When it was time to shoot close-ups of the infant Luke and Leia, the older Barton revealed that he had just had a baby and suggested that they used his son for the scenes. According to the younger Barton, they only used him for close-ups in the scenes where Luke and Leia are handed off to their new families on [[Tatooine]] and [[Alderaan]] – the babies in the scenes where [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] gives birth on [[Polis Massa]] were not him. For Barton's actual scenes, his mother brought him to the set and doubled as the people holding the babies, those being [[Rebecca Jackson Mendoza]] as [[Breha Organa]] and [[Bonnie Maree Piesse]] as [[Beru Whitesun Lars]]; except for the moments where the babies are handed off, she was the only one who actually held him.<ref name="Looper" />

In [[2019]], original Luke Skywalker actor [[Mark Hamill]] tweeted that Barton played both Luke and Leia,<ref name="TwitterWrong">{{Twitter|markhamill|status/1195761514071724033|[[Mark Hamill]]|date=2019-11-16|quote=FUN FACTS: A) Aidan Barton played both Luke AND Leia in #StarWarsEpisodeIIIRevengeOfTheSith. B) He is the son of that film's editor, Roger Barton. C) I have never met him. D) He is now in high school. E) YIKES! 😵|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/F8if0}}</ref> but accidentally posted pictures of someone else, leading to Barton's mother sending an email to Hamill's manager to set the record straight,<ref name="Looper" /> and Hamill making a new post to correct the error.<ref name="Looper" /><ref name="TwitterRight">{{Twitter|markhamill|status/1196929487927005184|[[Mark Hamill]]|date=2019-11-20|quote=WHEN FUN FACTS GO WRONG: A) Aidan Barton played both Luke AND Leia in #EPIII B) He is the son of that film's editor, Roger Barton. His mom kindly sent me these photos of the #ActualAidan-#MyBad|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/0fAxx}}</ref> After the incident, Barton and his family were going to have lunch with Hamill because Hamill lived close-by, but, the day they were supposed to meet, the [[Wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic|COVID-19]] lockdown hit and the plans were scrapped.<ref name="Looper" />
