A '''floor sweeper''', also known as '''sonic mop''', was a device designed to mechanically clean floors. It was designed to be pushed by its operator. [[Opeepit]], the janitor of the ''[[Colossus]]'' platform, used a floor sweeper to clean until it was confiscated by [[Occupation of the Colossus|occupying]] [[First Order]] forces, after which he was forced to scrub the floor by hand.<ref name="The Recruit" /><ref name="The First Order Occupation">{{Resistance|The First Order Occupation}}</ref><ref name="The New Trooper" /> [[Finn]] once used one such device to knock an [[First Order officer|officer]] of the [[First Order]] off his feet due to the sonic energy emitted.<ref name="Choose Your Destiny">''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Finn & Poe Adventure]]''</ref>
