'''Djovi Resmia''' was a [[human]] [[Jedi Initiate]] on the [[planet]] [[Tenoo]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Clash" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You're not the only one who feels like they have to be the best, you know? I feel that way all the time.|Djovi Resmia, to Kai Brightstar|<ref name="Clash" />}}
Djovi Resmia was [[human]] with [[Color|tan]] skin and dark brown hair. They were extremely competitive, driven by a need to prove themself to others even in situations when cooperation was more appropriate. They appreciated a challenge and desired to improve. Resmia enjoyed training with [[Kai Brightstar]], as they considered Brightstar to be their only strong competitor in [[lightsaber]] combat.<ref name="Clash" /> They had a fear of [[Tanglevine snake|snakes]], one which they were unwilling to confide in Brightstar, not wanting him to think they were weak. They also found [[spider]]s cute.<ref name="Treasure">{{YJA|The Lost Treasure of Tenoo}}</ref>

==Powers and abilities==
{{Quote|Hey, we haven't learned that form yet.''"<br />"''Master Zia wanted this to be a challenge, right? I like a good challenge.|Kai Brightstar and Djovi Resmia|<ref name="Clash" />}}
Resmia was a skilled lightsaber duelist, including knowledge of lightsaber forms that had not been taught to their fellow [[youngling]]s. They bested fellow trainee Kai Brightstar in a practice duel using such a form, earning his respect. Resmia also had the ability to use [[Telekinesis|Force telekinesis]] and used it alongside Brightstar to lift a beacon stone out of a pit in the [[Tenoo]] tree forest.<ref name="Clash" />
