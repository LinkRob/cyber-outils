'''EG-01'''<ref name="GEStaff">{{GEStaff|Droid Depot|store}}</ref> was a [[GNK-series power droid]]<ref name="GEDD" /> manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]]<ref name="Stats" /> with [[Color|green]] plating. During the [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] by the [[First Order]]<ref name="GEDD" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[power droid]] was displayed alongside several other [[droid]]s on a plinth behind railings located outside of the [[Droid Depot]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on Batuu.<ref name="GEDD" />

==Behind the scenes==
EG-01 first appeared as a prop within the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="GEDD" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref>
