The '''15th Deep Core Reserve Fleet''' was an [[Imperial Navy]] [[reserve fleet]] that was in service to the [[Galactic Empire]] during the rule of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]],<ref name="Nexus" /> which lasted from [[19 BBY]] to [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> The [[fleet]] was placed under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Admiral]] [[Yzu]].<ref name="Nexus" />

Due to the strategic nature of [[Empress Teta]]'s resources, as well as its position as a gateway to the [[Deep Core]], the [[planet]] was used as an important stronghold by the Empire. As a result, official documents reported that the 15th Deep Core Reserve Fleet's [[Star Destroyer]]s and support [[Starship|ships]] were conducting customs enforcement patrols to fight against [[smuggler]]s and [[pirate]]s in the area. Despite that, the fleet's duties also included operating a [[hyperspace]] interdiction checkpoint along the only known [[hyperspace route]] between the [[Empress Teta system]] and the interior of the Deep Core.<ref name="Nexus" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 15th Deep Core Reserve Fleet was mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] sourcebook, ''[[Nexus of Power]]''.<ref name="Nexus" /> It was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[1995]] [[West End Games]] ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' boxed set ''[[The DarkStryder Campaign]]''.<ref name="TDC">''[[The DarkStryder Campaign]]''</ref>
