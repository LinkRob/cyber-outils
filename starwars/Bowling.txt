'''Bowling''' was a target sport and recreational activity in which a player rolled a ball toward [[Bowling pin|pins]], across a long lane, with the goal of knocking them down. The pins had a form of upright elongated solids of rotation with a flat base for setting. The game was hosted in venues known as the [[bowling alley]]s. Some of such venues were present at the ''[[Death Star]]'' space station, which was launched, and subsequently destroyed,<ref name="PnF">''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]''</ref> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{PnFDate|1}}</ref> Prior to its explosion during the [[Battle of Yavin]], the bowling alley employees were evacuated from the space station by the [[Firestar Girls]] squadron.<ref name="PnF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bowling was first mentioned in the non-canonical television special ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]'', which aired in 2014. During its runtime, A few employees of the bowling alleys, can be seen in the special onboard the [[Firestar Girls]] squadron ship, holding the bowling pins.<ref name="PnF" />
