The '''FWG-7 Flechette Smart Carbine''' was a [[blaster carbine]] produced by [[Malaxan Firepower Incorporated]]. Derived from the [[FWG-5 smart pistol]], the FWG-7 fired small canisters that exploded upon reaching their target, releasing a deadly cloud of [[flechette]]s.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />
