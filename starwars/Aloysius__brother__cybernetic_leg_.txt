A [[Houk]] '''brother''' was a criminal who, alongside [[Aloysius]] and another [[Aloysius' brother (flamethrower)|brother]], went after the [[Zeltron]] criminal [[Samu Mavolak]] after he cheated in a game of [[Sabacc]] with them. After cornering Mavolak and threatening to harm his [[Samu Mavolak's son|son]] on the [[Planet|world]] [[Meddan]], the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]], who was also hunting Mavolak, swept in and [[Death|killed]] the Houk along with their [[Aloysius' kin|kin]].<ref name="Boba Fett BWR 2" />
