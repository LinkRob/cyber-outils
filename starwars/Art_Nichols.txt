'''Art Nichols''' is an American [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] artist who did the inks in four issues of the [[Star Wars (1977)|Marvel Star Wars]] run from 1985 to 1986. He also did work on the cover to [[Star Wars (1977) 97|''Star Wars'' (1977) 97]].
