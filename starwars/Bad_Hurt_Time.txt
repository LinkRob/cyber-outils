The '''Bad Hurt Time''' was the name given by the [[Gender|female]] [[charhound]] [[Ember]] to an event that occurred when she was a [[Dog|puppy]] where a [[spike-sparrow]] [[Death|killed]] all but one member of her charhound [[Ember's first pack|pack]]. The incident took place on Ember's [[homeworld]],<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Planet|world]] [[Elphrona]],<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> by [[232 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> during the [[High Republic Era]]. After the [[sparrow]] had killed the other charhounds in her pack, Ember engaged in a fight with the spike-sparrow, ultimately crawling away from the fight.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />

Shortly after the Bad Hurt Time, Ember became part of a second pack, coming to be owned by the [[Jedi]] [[Bell Zettifar]] and living with<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> the Jedi stationed at the [[Elphrona Outpost|Jedi outpost]] on Elphrona.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> Around [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">{{TrialsoftheJediDate|26}}</ref> Ember mentally recalled the Bad Hurt Time when she reflected on her life while on<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> the [[Wild Space]]<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> planet [[Sophros]], recalling she had known friends ever since just after the Bad Hurt Time.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />
