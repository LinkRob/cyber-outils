'''Ellenne Wune''' was a [[Gender|female]] individual who worked as a [[professor]] and [[engineer]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. At one point, one of her students, [[Pon Kenti]], approached her after class and successfully recruited her into the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] as a [[spy]] and [[Saboteur|sabotage]] the [[Galactic Empire]]'s war effort.<ref name="Fully Operational" />
