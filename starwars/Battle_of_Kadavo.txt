The '''Battle of Kadavo''' was fought on the [[planet]] [[Kadavo]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. [[Galactic Republic]] forces sought to save [[Togruta]] colonists from [[Kiros]] who had been kidnapped and [[Slavery|enslaved]] by the [[Zygerrian Slave Empire]], which had allied itself with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The [[Jedi]] had launched a [[Mission to Zygerria|mission]] to try and find the captured Togruta, but the mission's operatives had been captured during the [[Royal Slave Auction]]. After escaping, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] learned from the [[Death|dying]] [[Zygerrian]] [[Queen]], [[Miraj Scintel]], that the colonists, as well as [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], had been transported to Kadavo. Calling on [[Jedi General]] [[Plo Koon]] for reinforcements, Skywalker and Tano traveled to the Kadavo slave processing facility, commanded by [[Keeper]] [[Agruss]]. In the ensuing battle, the surviving Togruta captives were rescued, before Republic forces destroyed the Kadavo facility.

==The battle==
A [[Galactic Republic]] task force led by [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], and Skywalker's [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], [[Battle on Kiros|liberated]] Kiros and discovered links leading to Zygerria. The [[Jedi]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] soon [[Mission to Zygerria|infiltrated]] the auction and tried to rescue the Togruta but were captured by the [[Zygerrian]]s and enslaved.<ref name="Escape from Kadavo" />

Skywalker and his Padawan, however, escaped from the Zygerrians and headed for Kadavo to rescue Kenobi, Rex, and the Togruta, who had been transferred to the [[Kadavo slave processing facility|Zygerrian slave processing facility]] on the planet. While en route, the two also called for reinforcements from the Republic. Tano and her Master arrived on the planet first on the [[YV-865 Aurore-class freighter|''Aurore''-class freighter]] ''[[Tecora]]'' and tried to break into the slave processing facility. When ordered to cease by the Zygerrian [[Keeper]] [[Agruss]], lest the slaves be [[Death|killed]], Skywalker did not comply. At that moment, the reinforcements arrived, led by [[Admiral]] [[Barton Coburn]] and Jedi Master [[Plo Koon]].<ref name="Escape from Kadavo" /> The Republic's [[Shadow Squadron]] also took part in the battle,<ref name="Shadow Squadron Veteran" /> as did [[Clone trooper pilot|clone pilots]] [[Boost]]<ref name="X-wing">{{Twitter|atomicmassgames|status/1525129066025832450|Atomic Mass Games|quote="Boost" and "Slider" have unique ways to maneuver into position and unleash a volley of Homing Torpedoes on their targets. Add them to your squadrons with the Clone Z-95 Headhunter Expansion Pack for #StarWarsXWing! #StarWars #XWing #Miniatures|archivedate=20220513152501}}</ref> and [[Warthog]].<ref name="Escape from Kadavo" />

Koon's [[104th Battalion|Wolfpack]] detachment attacked the facility with [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]] and [[clone Z-95 starfighter]]s, but were halted by its [[ray shield]]s. However, Skywalker was able to destroy the defensive towers, while Kenobi and Rex rebelled and killed Agruss.<ref name="Escape from Kadavo" />

As a last act before his death, the Keeper tried to throw the Togruta into the volcanic crevice below the facility, but, following a plan devised by Tano, the Republic forces evacuated them on Admiral Coburn's [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]]. After picking up Skywalker, Kenobi, Tano, and Rex, the Republic strike team destroyed the slave facility.<ref name="Escape from Kadavo" />

==Aftermath==
With the slaves rescued, Skywalker informed [[Governor]] [[Gupat Roshti]] of Tano's major role in the rescue operation, earning her his thanks. Although he feared the people of Kiros would not recover from their time in slavery, Tano was confident their will would be strengthened from their victory, and she supported his idea to join the Republic.<ref name="Escape from Kadavo" /> The battle at Kadavo proved to be one of the many victories Shadow Squadron took part in.<ref name="Shadow Squadron Veteran" />
