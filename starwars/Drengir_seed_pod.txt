The '''Drengir seed pod''' was a vehicle grown by the [[Drengir]]<ref name="Haunted Starlight" /> in [[228 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> in [[Barraza City]] on [[Eiram]]. The seed pod was supposed to be filled with fermented [[gas]] and fly away helping the Drengir [[Escape on Eiram|escape]] [[Eiram]].<ref name="Haunted Starlight" />
