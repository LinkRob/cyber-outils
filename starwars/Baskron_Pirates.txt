The '''Baskron Pirates''' were a [[pirate]] group<ref name="Battlefront" /> originating from the<ref name="Planetnamia" /><ref name="Battlefront" /> [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Baskron system]].<ref name="Systems">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> that was active in a region of [[Realspace|space]]. In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] held a meeting between its top staff and the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] crews of their two [[starship]]s&mdash;the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]'' and the [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunship]] ''[[Apailana's Promise]]''&mdash;regarding a [[hypermatter]] leak from the ''Thunderstrike''. In the meeting, two crew members from the ''Apailana's Promise'' proposed traveling to the Baskron Pirate territory and making a deal for materials to repair the [[corvette]]. The 61st ultimately [[Raid on an Imperial heavy freighter|raided]] an [[Unidentified Imperial heavy freighter|Imperial heavy freighter]] instead.<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Baskron Pirates were mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
