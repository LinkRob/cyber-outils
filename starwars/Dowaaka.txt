The '''Dowaaka''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[species]] with lizard-like heads and four breasts. A Dowaaka served as the host of the Clear River News Network<ref name="Vow" /> around [[200 BBY]].<ref name="VowDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Dowaaka species, written as 达瓦阿卡人 in Chinese, was created for the Chinese webnovel ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'', where they first appeared.<ref name="Vow" />
