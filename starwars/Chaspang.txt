'''Chaspang''' was a location where [[mineral]]s could be acquired and which was situated in a [[sector]] adjacent to<ref name="Insurgency Rising 3" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]]<ref name="Insurgency Rising 2">[[Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising 2|''Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising'' 2]]</ref> [[Anoat sector]].<ref name="Insurgency Rising 3" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Star Wars: Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising]]'' take place after the [[Battle of Endor]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]]. The series is also set before the events of ''[[Star Wars: Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege]]'', which in turn are before the [[Liberation of Sullust]], an event dated to 4 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] by ''Timelines''.</ref> when a [[New Republic]] team including the [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] [[Rynn Zenat]]<ref name="Insurgency Rising 2" /> [[Mission to the Anoat sector|reached]] the Anoat sector<ref name="Insurgency Rising 3" /> to assess the situation of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]]<ref name="Insurgency Rising 2" /> [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]]'s<ref name="Insurgency Rising 1">[[Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising 1|''Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising'' 1]]</ref> [[Iron Blockade]] there, their [[Oggrud|starship]] was surrounded by [[Starship|vessels]] of the criminal [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]].<ref name="Insurgency Rising 2" /> The criminals then demanded they be allowed to board the New Republic ship or they would destroy it.<ref name="Insurgency Rising 3" />

In an attempt to off-balance the [[Spicerunner|Spice Runners]], Zenat told them<ref name="Insurgency Rising 3" /> her<ref name="Insurgency Rising 2" /> team was a [[merchant]] ship loaded with minerals from Chaspang, though the Spice Runner's leader&mdash;[[Zeva Bliss]]&mdash;recognized the deception. As the criminals discussed their next steps, Zenat used the pause to [[pilot]] the New Republic ship, which was armed with [[bomb]]s, toward the Spice Runners' [[Ragged Claw|lead ship]] and then began ramming it into the criminals' other vessels.<ref name="Insurgency Rising 3" />
