'''Dascha''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as a [[Nihil]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Dascha, alongside the [[Pantoran]] female [[Niquita]], were stationed on a [[Belin's ship|ship]] inside the [[Occlusion Zone]]<ref name="Eye" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[229 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> After a quick confrontation with the [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] and her [[droid]] companion [[KC-78]], the two were launched into [[Realspace|space]] via an [[escape pod]] after being knocked unconscious by Kriss. Dascha, alongside Niquita, were [[Death penalty|executed]] by the Nihil for failure after being interrogated by the [[Mirialan]] [[General]] [[Abediah Viess]].<ref name="Eye" />
