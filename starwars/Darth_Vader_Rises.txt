"'''Darth Vader Rises'''" is a [[canon]] short story that appears in ''[[The Prequel Trilogy Stories]]''. It was written by [[Rebecca L. Schmidt]].<ref name="TPTS" /> It adapts [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] from the [[Duel in the Galactic Senate|Duel in the Senate]] to the epilogue on [[Tatooine]].
