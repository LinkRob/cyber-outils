"'''Darth Vader - Power of the Dark Side'''" is the second episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]''. It premiered on the [[StarWarsKids.com]]<ref name="Star Wars Kids" /> and the [[The Walt Disney Company|DisneyNOW]]<ref name="Disney Now">{{DisneyNOW|url=shows/star-wars-galaxy-of-adventures|text=Galaxy of Adventures}}</ref> websites on [[November 30]], [[2018]].<ref name="YouTube" />

==Plot summary==
During the [[secret mission to Tatooine]] a squad of [[rebel trooper]]s take up position in a dark corridor on the ''[[Tantive IV]]''. [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] approaches them and ignites [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. He tells them that they don't know the power of the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]]. The rebel troopers fire their [[blaster]]s but he deflects their blaster bolts with his lightsaber. Armed with his lightsaber, Lord Vader proceeds to cut down the rebel troopers. He also uses [[the Force]] to snatch their blasters, hurl his lightsaber at the troopers, and hurls two of the troopers against each other.

Soon Lord Vader finds himself facing one last rebel trooper. After slicing his blaster, he [[Telekinesis|levitates]] the man with the Force and proceeds to [[Force choke]] him in mid-air before hurling him across the corridor. The lights in the corridors then come back on, leaving Darth Vader towering over the fallen bodies of the rebel troopers. As a squad of [[stormtrooper]]s join him, Vader surmises it was all too easy before striking a fallen rebel soldier with his lightsaber.
