'''Bubo Wamba Family Farms''',<ref name="DPB Foodie Guide">{{DPB|url=2019/05/foodie-guide-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Foodie Guide to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> also known as '''Bubo's Blue & Green Milk'''<ref name="Arena News">{{ArenaNews|url=0122de64c2544c938cd376a6e9cc75f3-en-gb|text=First Edition}}</ref> or '''Bubo's Blue Milk''',<ref name="SWH" /> was a farming business located on [[Batuu]], and was connected to the [[Milk Stand]] in the planet's [[Black Spire Outpost]],<ref name="DPB Foodie Guide" /> where they held the monopoly on [[blue milk]]. Some residents of Batuu raised [[bantha]]s for the farm, which produced the blue [[milk]] they sold, while the farms' [[green milk]] was imported and came from [[thala-siren]]s, which could be found on a number of worlds.<ref name="GEStaff">{{GEStaff|Milk Stand|food stall}}</ref> The Milk Stand was founded by [[Aqualish]] dairy farmer [[Bubo Wamba]],<ref name="Cookbook" /> whose [[Wamba family|family]] had farmed banthas on [[Tatooine]] for four generations.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> It was advertised on [[Arena News]].<ref name="Arena News" />

==Behind the scenes==
Articles released on the [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] Parks Blog prior to the opening of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] revealed the [[in-universe]] story detail that the Milk Stand sells milk fresh from the Bubo Wamba Family Farms,<ref name="DPB Building Batuu">{{DPB|url=2019/02/building-batuu-new-details-released-about-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Building Batuu: New Details Released about ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> and that it sells the [[Bubo Wamba Family Farms Novelty Drink Vessel]] which real-world customers can purchase.<ref name="DPB Foodie Guide" />
