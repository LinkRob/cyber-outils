The '''fluid sac''' was an organ that the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Pa'lowick]] [[species]]<ref name="Hunt for Ziro" /> had in place of a [[heart]].<ref name="Max Rebo">{{Databank|max-rebo|Max Rebo}}</ref> It was sometimes used in the phrase "from the bottom of my fluid sac," which was used by Pa'lowicks to emphasize sincerity.<ref name="Hunt for Ziro" />

==Behind the scenes==
The fluid sac was first mentioned in "[[Hunt for Ziro]]," the ninth epsiode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]]. Although the subtitles for "Hunt for Ziro" spelled sac as sack,<ref name="Hunt for Ziro" /> the letter k was removed from fluid sac's spelling in later sources published on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Max Rebo" /><ref name="Sy Snootles Visits Ziro in Jail">{{SW|url=video/sy-snootles-visits-ziro-in-jail|text=Sy Snootles Visits Ziro in Jail}}</ref> This article assumes that sac is the correct spelling.
