'''William Stoneham''' is a 3d artist who worked on the background and cutscenes for several [[Star Wars video games|video games]].

He worked in the production team for the [[1996]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (video game)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'']], creating sceneries for the cutscenes, such as [[Coruscant/Legends|Imperial City]], [[Mos Eisley/Legends|Mos Eisley]] and the [[Beggar's Canyon/Legends|Beggar's Canyon]], and [[Ord Mantell/Legends|Ord Mantell]] scrapyard.
