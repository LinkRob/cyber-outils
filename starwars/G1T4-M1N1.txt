A '''G1T4-M1N1''' was a model of [[droid]] that could carry cargo.<ref name="SW.com" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|PFF turns everyday mobility challenges into opportunities for connection and exploration. Star Wars has inspired generations with its imaginative galaxy and futuristic droids assisting humans with everyday tasks, matching our vision for the future of robotics.|Piaggio Fast Forward CEO Greg Lynn, on collaborating with ''Star Wars''|<ref name="SW.com" />}}
G1T4-M1N1 is the limited edition of the robotics company Piaggio Fast Forward's cargo robot, gita''mini''. It was made as a collaboration between the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise and the robotics company.<ref name="SW.com" />
