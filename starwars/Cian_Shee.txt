'''Cian Shee'''<ref name="FC">{{ForceCollection|Kalyn Farnmir|star=1}}</ref> was a [[human]].<ref name="AOTC" /> The [[bounty hunter]] [[Kalyn Farnmir]] unsuccessfully attempted to pin her crimes on Shee, who was her former partner.<ref name="FC" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}} to [[22 BBY]].</ref> Shee and Farnmir spent [[time]] together in<ref name="AOTC" /> the [[Outlander Club]], a nightclub in the [[Uscru District]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Coruscant">{{EncyclopediaCite|Coruscant}}</ref> Farnmir smiled and nodded as the pair embraced at the club's bar, after which Shee departed as<ref name="AOTC" /> the [[slythmonger]] [[Elan Sel'Sabagno]]<ref name="Coruscant" /> began conversing with the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] further along the bar. Shee had [[Color|tan]] skin and wore an [[Clothing|outfit]] including a purple [[hat]] and shirt while at the club.<ref name="AOTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cian Shee appeared in the [[2002]] [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="AOTC" /> The character's scene was filmed on Stage 3 at [[Fox Studios, Sydney]] on [[July 17]], [[2000]].<ref name="365Days">''[[Creating the Worlds of Star Wars: 365 Days]]''</ref>

In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Cian Shee's name was used in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile card game]] ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'',<ref name="FC" /> which initially launched in [[2013]]. Although the launch of ''Force Collection'' predates<ref name="Force Collection release">{{SW|url=news/announcing-star-wars-force-collection|text=Announcing Star Wars: Force Collection|nolive=1}}</ref> the ''Star Wars'' canon reset of [[2014]],<ref name="Legends">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Leland Chee]] confirmed that the game was kept updated to fit with canon.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|holocronkeeper|status/846807700482666496|[[Leland Chee]]|quote=I'm not gonna go through the trouble of changing them and I don't have any reason to believe currently that anyone would. [&hellip;] On the off-chance that we DID have a current need to change any of these, we would have for Force Collection.|archivedate=20210505080831}} &mdash; [[Leland Chee]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] has confirmed that [[in-universe]] information from ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'' conforms with ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]].</ref> The name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where [[Cian Shee/Legends|Cian Shee]] was first identified in a photo caption posted as part of the "[[Image Attack]]" feature on [[StarWars.com]] on [[November 11]], [[2003]].<ref name="ArtAttack">{{SWArchive|url=episode-ii/explore/attack/2003/11/imageattack20031111.html|series=Image Attack|text=Cian Shee}}</ref>
