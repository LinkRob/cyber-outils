'''2015 Topps ''Star Wars Rebels''''' trading card set was released by [[Topps]]. It is based on the television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' and features cards for many characters the series. The base set, released in [[2015]], featured 100 cards, 20 stickers, and 10 temporary tattoos. Cards came in packs of six.<ref name="Topps Announces">{{SW|url=news/topps-announces-star-wars-rebels-trading-cards-and-more-exclusive|text=Topps Announces ''Star Wars Rebels'' Trading Cards and More - Exclusive!}}</ref>
