The '''barghests''' were a [[species]] of [[Sentience|non-sentient]] quadrupeds that could be kept as pets for protection. They had dark [[Hair|fur]] and skin, a large [[mouth]] filled with sharp [[Tooth|teeth]], and six [[Color|red]] [[eye]]s.<ref name="TFAVD" /> Although they looked monstrous, [[Diet|carnivorous]] barghests showed great loyalty toward their owners.<ref name="VE" />

[[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[warlord]] [[Janu Coin]] kept a barghest head mounted on his office wall during the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="TMAG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref> At the [[time]] of the [[Cold War]], the [[Onodone]] forger [[Gwellis Bagnoro]] owned a pet barghest named [[Izby]].<ref name="TFAVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
A barghest first appeared in the [[2015]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> The creature's name, however, was revealed in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released the same day as the film.<ref name="TFAVD" /> In the folklore of northern [[Wikipedia:England|England]], barghests are monstrous dogs with huge teeth and claws.<ref name="Britannica">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/art/Barghest|text=Barghest|work=Encyclopædia Britannica|archivedate=20200619024649}}</ref>
