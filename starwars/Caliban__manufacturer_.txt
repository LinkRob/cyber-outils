'''Caliban''' was a weapon manufacturing company that produced the [[Model X]] [[heavy blaster pistol]].<ref name="G&G" />

==Behind the scenes==
Caliban was mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2019]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] roleplaying supplement ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''.<ref name="G&G" /> The company originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[1993]] roleplaying supplement ''[[Galaxy Guide 9: Fragments from the Rim]]'', which was written by [[Simon Smith]] and [[Eric S. Trautmann]] for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. In the ''Galaxy Guide'', the company's name was spelt as "Calban,"<ref name="GG9">''[[Galaxy Guide 9: Fragments from the Rim]]''</ref> with the Caliban spelling first being used in the [[2002]] roleplaying supplement ''[[Arms & Equipment Guide]]'', which was written by [[Jeff Grubb]] and [[Owen K.C. Stephens]] for [[Wizards of the Coast]]'s ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="A&E">''[[Arms & Equipment Guide]]''</ref>
