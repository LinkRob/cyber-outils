'''''Age of Rebellion - Princess Leia'' 1''' is the first one-shot in the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] anthology series [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (comic series)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']]. It was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Chris Sprouse]] and [[Karl Story]], and published on [[April 10]], [[2019]] by [[Marvel Comics]].

The story is set after [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], and it follows the events shown in the ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]'' episode "[[Bounty Hunted]]."

==Publisher's summary==
''BOUNTY HUNTER PRINCESS! With HAN SOLO frozen in carbonite in the palace of JABBA THE HUTT, PRINCESS LEIA prepares for a daring rescue by taking on the identity of the bounty hunter BOUSHH. But in order to succeed, she'll have to prove herself in her new identity to the ruthless bounty hunter BOSSK! With her life and all of her principles on the line, how far will Leia go to rescue a scoundrel? Co-starring CHEWBACCA and LANDO, who almost certainly have a bad feeling about this!''
