The '''Endor Outrider''' was a costume owned by the [[Miraluka]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Diago Velaar]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Frontier Sharpshooter|standard costume]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Endor Outrider was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Diago, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 3.0.0 alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Endor Slugthrower]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing the Endor Diago Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
