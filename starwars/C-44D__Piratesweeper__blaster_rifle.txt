The '''C-44D "Piratesweeper" blaster rifle''' was a model of [[Color|black]] [[blaster rifle]] with a [[scope]] and silver [[barrel]] tip. The [[haulcraft]] [[pilot]] [[Trowee Astur]] wore a C-44D on a shoulder strap while in [[Point Ueda]] on the [[planet]] [[Ueda]] during the [[High Republic Era]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> in [[132 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The C-44D "Piratesweeper" blaster rifle was mentioned in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]. The book shows the rifle as part the costume worn by Trowee Astur<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> in "[[Lost / Found]],"<ref name="LostFound">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref> the first episode of the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', which aired on [[Disney+]] on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref> However, the weapon is not visible in the episode itself.<ref name="LostFound" />
