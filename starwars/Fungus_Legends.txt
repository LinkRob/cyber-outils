'''Fungus''' (plural form: '''fungi''') was an organism found on countless [[Planet/Legends|worlds]] across [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]]. While some fungi were [[Poison/Legends|poisonous]], such as the [[blood-eating fungus]],<ref name="TEGtPaM">''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]''</ref> many varieties were edible.<ref name="The Lost Ones">''[[Young Jedi Knights: The Lost Ones]]''</ref><ref name="Jedi Bounty">''[[Young Jedi Knights: Jedi Bounty]]''</ref>

Fungus came in a variety of [[Color/Legends|colors]], including blue,<ref name="Darkest Knight">''[[Young Jedi Knights: Darkest Knight]]''</ref> blue-green,<ref name="The Paradise Snare">''[[The Paradise Snare]]''</ref> pink, lavender, peach,<ref name="Diversity Alliance">''[[Young Jedi Knights: Diversity Alliance]]''</ref> violet,<ref name="BF:ANT">''[[Boba Fett: A New Threat]]''</ref> yellow,<ref name="POT">''[[Planet of Twilight]]''</ref> and white. Some types of fungi were also luminescent.<ref name="The Hive">''[[The Hive]]''</ref> Fungi from [[Bakura/Legends|Bakura]] were rainbow-colored.<ref name="DW">''[[The New Jedi Order: Destiny's Way]]''</ref>

Fungi on the [[Yuuzhan Vong]] world of [[Zonama Sekot/Legends|Zonama Sekot]] were capable of movement on [[bora]]s in order to search sunlight.<ref name="Reunion">''[[The New Jedi Order: Force Heretic III: Reunion]]''</ref>

The [[Grenade fungi/Legends|Grenade fungi]] were white, globular fungi found on [[Yavin 4/Legends|Yavin 4]]. When stepped on they exploded in a cloud of poisonous spores.<ref>''[[Galaxy Guide 2: Yavin and Bespin]]'' </ref>

[[Lokka Fungus|Lokka Fungi]] were used in [[Totem magic|Totem Magic]] on [[Endor]]<ref name="Episode">{{Ewoks|Sunstar vs. Shadowstone}}</ref><ref>[[Galaxy Guide 2: Yavin and Bespin (Second Edition)|''Galaxy Guide 2: Yavin and Bespin'', Second Edition]]</ref>
