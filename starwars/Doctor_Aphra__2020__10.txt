'''''Doctor Aphra'' 10''' is the tenth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. It was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Ray-Anthony Height]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[May 26]], [[2021]]. It features the prelude story "'''The Invitation'''," which leads into the ''[[Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters]]'' crossover event.

==Publisher's summary==
''PRELUDE TO WAR OF THE BOUNTY HUNTERS: "THE INVITATION" DOCTOR APHRA and SANA STARROS find themselves cornered by VUKORAH and the UNBROKEN CLAN! Can they pull off a daring escape&mdash;even if that means surrendering the NIHIL HYPERDRIVE? And what sets them on a collision course with one of the most deadly BOUNTY HUNTERS in the galaxy?''
