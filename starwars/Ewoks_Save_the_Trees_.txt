'''''Ewoks Save the Trees!''''' was a board game released by [[Parker Brothers]] in [[1984]],<ref name="Vintage Vault" /> as part of their Funburst line of games.<ref name="Instructions" /> The game is partially based on the storybook ''[[How the Ewoks Saved the Trees: An Old Ewok Legend]]'', released that same year. The artwork in the board game is similar to that of the storybook and others released around the same time.<ref name="How">''[[How the Ewoks Saved the Trees: An Old Ewok Legend]]''</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
''There's action aplenty when you help WICKET and his friends escape from monsters called PHLOGS who are trying to cut the EWOKS' precious trees.''

''Now as every EWOK child knows, trees are very important and no one must ever think of chopping one down. After all, EWOK families live in trees and children can even talk to them!''

''So like a true EWOK child, you'll have to rely on your quickness and wits to escape the PHLOGS and run along the forest path for help.''

''You'll win if you're the first to climb the stairs, enter the treehouse, and warn the EWOK elders.''
