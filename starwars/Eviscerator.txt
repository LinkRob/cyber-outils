The '''''Eviscerator''''' was an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] under the command of [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]].

==History==
{{Quote|Del! The ''Eviscerator'' is going down. Do you copy?|Iden Versio during the Battle of Jakku|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
Under the command of [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]], the [[Starship|ship]] participated in the [[Battle of Endor]], where it was part of the [[fleet]] defending the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]] from the coming [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] assault. Although the [[Galactic Empire]] lost at [[Endor]], with the destruction of the [[battle station]] and the [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor|the]] [[Darth Sidious|Emperor]], Versio and the ''Eviscerator'' escaped, and retreated to the [[Jinata system]]. While [[orbit]]ing [[Vardos]], the admiral received a posthumous [[Sentinel (Contingency)|message]] from the Emperor about [[Operation: Cinder]], which formed part of the Imperial [[Contingency]] plan.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

The ship later took part in the [[Battle of Jakku]], where it was launched [[TIE series|TIE fighter forces]] led by [[Gideon Hask]] against the ''[[Corvus]]''. Throughout the final stages of the battle it was badly damaged while battling the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] resulting it beginning to lose its structural integrity. As the ship burnt in the skies of Jakku, it then suffered critical damage to the reactor, resulting in it losing stability. Prior to ''Eviscerator'' going down, the rapidly disintegrating ship was boarded by Versio's daughter, [[Iden Versio|Iden]], who fought her way to the bridge and attempted to convince her father to come with her. He refused and parted ways with Iden, who quickly rushed to an [[escape pod]] before the doomed ship crashed onto Jakku's deserts.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
