"'''30 Years of ''Star Wars'' Radio Drama Part II: How the Empire Almost Won and the Jedi Never Returned'''" is the second part of an article to commemorate the 30th anniversary of the [[Star Wars (radio)|''Star Wars'' radio drama]]. The article was written by [[Michael Kogge]] and appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 128|''Star Wars Insider'' 128]].<ref name="SW128" /> It was later reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2014|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2014]].<ref name="SE2014" />
