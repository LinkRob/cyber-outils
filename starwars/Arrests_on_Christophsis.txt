{{Quote|When an attack by early aspects of the Rebel Alliance was traced back to the world of Christophsis, the planet was "pacified" in retribution. The language of this act belies the reality of three Imperial Star Destroyers entering orbit above the capital city and opening fire. The purpose of this attack was to create such a fear in the population, and by proxy in the Senate when it was reported by Grand Moff Tarkin, as to render future resistance inconceivable.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="RiseFall" />}}
In [[3 BBY]], the [[Galactic Empire]] '''arrested''' many officials of the government of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Christophsis]] after discovering that they had backed rebel activity. The Empire also "pacified" the planet by cracking down on the city of [[Tophen]], which they determined was the hotspot of the planet's rebel activity, and [[Orbital bombardment|orbitally bombarding]] the [[capital]] city of [[Chaleydonia]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|False reports circulating through various informal channels claim that a medical frigate was captured. Although many members of the frigate's crew were found to harbor treasonous beliefs and attempted to aid in the attack, the ship remains in the control of the proper authorities.|A [[HoloNet]] [[Journalist|reporter]] reporting on the incident|<ref name="LPOA" />}}
In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Hospital ship|medical frigate]] was stolen by the rebellion, with the assistance of the [[Starship|ship's]] [[captain]] and a [[squadron]] of rebel [[starfighter]]s. The Empire, investigating the theft, discovered that the [[Unidentified prime minister|prime minister]] of [[Christophsis]], along with many other government officials, including the [[planet]]'s [[senator]]s, were backing the [[Christophsis insurgents|Christophsian rebels]], who had been involved in the incident.<ref name="LPOA" />

==The crackdown==
{{Quote|Therefore, we have made an example of these leaders&mdash;I should say, former leaders. While the ex-senators and territorial supervisors have been jailed for terms of four years or more, the prime minister has been tried, convicted, and executed for treason. Furthermore, his home city of Tophen&mdash;found to be the center of this activity&mdash;has already been pacified.|Wilhuff Tarkin|<ref name="LPOA" />}}
The Empire moved swiftly<ref name="LPOA" /> to "pacify" Christophsis,<ref name="RiseFall" /> with part of the operation seeing the arrest of the [[Prime Minister|prime minister]], senators, and territorial supervisors. The prime minister was executed for [[treason]], and the senators and territorial supervisors were sent to Imperial work camps. Furthermore, the [[city]] of [[Tophen]], which was the prime minister's hometown and would be determined to be the center of the planet's rebel activity, was "pacified,"<ref name="LPOA" /> as was the planetary capital<ref name="RiseFall" /> of [[Chaleydonia]].<ref name="The Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> The crackdown on Chaleydonia saw three [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]]s enter [[orbit]] and [[Orbital bombardment|bombard]] the city.<ref name="RiseFall" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|It is hoped that this action, drastic though it is, has served to quell the radical elements at work on Christophsis, to restore the planet to order, and to deliver a message to all those who would threaten the peace our Emperor has given us: we stand here now, always, and forevermore. No matter what else may come, the Empire will endure.|Wilhuff Tarkin|<ref name="LPOA" />}}
The Christophsis crackdown was painted as a "pacification" operation by the Empire.<ref name="RiseFall" /> [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] gave a speech detailing the Empire's crackdown to the entire [[Imperial Senate]] as a warning about what would happen to other worlds that dared to defy the Empire.<ref name="LPOA" /> Historian [[Beaumont Kin]] touched on the "pacification" of Christophsis and the effect the Empire intended for it to leave on the galaxy in his book ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', in which he disagreed with calling the event a "pacification" due to the ferocity displayed on the Empire's part.<ref name="RiseFall" />

==Behind the scenes==
The arrests were first mentioned in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', a [[2017]] young-adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="LPOA" />
