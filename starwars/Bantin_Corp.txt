'''Bantin Corp''' was a manufacturing company. They were responsible for the production of the [[Mk I NightStalker Infiltration Suit]].<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bantin Corp was mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets" />
