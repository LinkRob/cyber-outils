{{Quote|''For the removal and control of:'' bladderworm, crop microserps, multilegged throat croakers, intestinal fangworm colonies, lesser and greater nasal nesting spit-crawlers, cranial nodular worms.|Advertisement leaflet for Happy-Bore Medicated Dewormer included in Rey's survival guide|<ref name="RSG" />}}
'''Crop microserps''' were a type of [[Parasite|parasitic]] [[worm]] that could infest the domesticated [[happabore]] [[species]], which was found on [[planet]]s across the [[Western Reaches]]. [[Happy-Bore Medicated Dewormer]]&mdash;a product produced by [[Galacti-Stok]] that contained [[Trestibinexocycline 5-Gamma]], [[thlank oil]], [[greel-root acid]] and forage products&mdash;could be used to treat crop microserps infestation in happabores. Use of the [[dewormer]] on the parasite was suggested in the instructions for the slurry formulation of the product. The [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] found a copy of these instructions while living on the [[desert]] planet [[Jakku]] and included them alongside information about happabores on the world in a [[The Force Awakens: Rey's Story|survival guide]] that<ref name="RSG" /> she<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> compiled<ref name="RSG" /> by [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'' includes an addendum at the end describing some of the events of {{Film|VII}}, written several [[Standard day|days]] after they take place. The addendum is the final entry in the guide and is included chronologically after the entry mentioning crop microserps. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the film to [[34 ABY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Crop microserps were mentioned in ''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]'', a [[2015]] replica journal written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="RSG" />
