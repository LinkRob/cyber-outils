'''Camp Four''' was a [[prison]] camp established by the [[Trade Federation]] during their [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]] and occupation of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]],<ref name="Episode I" /> located in [[Theed]]'s largest [[Theed market square|market square]] and designated for government personnel such as [[Theed Royal Palace]] staff and members of the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]].<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and her inner circle were sentenced to Camp Four, but they were rescued by [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], and [[Gungan]] exile [[Jar Jar Binks]] while being escorted to the camp by the [[Trade Federation Droid Army]].<ref name="Episode I" /> After Amidala's escape from the planet, several members of her staff who remained behind were imprisoned there, including [[Mariek Panaka]], [[Tonra]], and [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaidens]] [[Saché]] and [[Yané]],<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> as was [[Naboo Palace Guard]] member [[Perosei]].<ref name="Force Collection">{{ForceCollection|Perosei|star=3}}</ref>
