'''Combat engineers''' were [[rebel trooper]]s of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] that specialized in [[engineer]]ing operations. They were typically in the center of a [[squad]] and worked in conjunction with [[Special-weapons flamethrower]]s to take down fortifications.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> [[Sergeant]] [[Kala Tanto]] served as a combat engineer. [[Sapper (occupation)|Sapper]]s were combat engineers that specialized in constructing bases for Alliance as well as in destroying those same structures belonging to the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="FO" />
