During the [[Galactic Civil War]], not long after the [[Battle of Yavin]], a [[Droid|robotic]] [[CR90 corvette]] belonging to [[pirate]]s&mdash;who were funded by the [[Crymorah Syndicate]]&mdash;[[Skirmish over an unidentified green planet|attacked]] an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[shuttle]] carrying Imperial weapons. The [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], stopped the [[corvette]]. Soon after, [[Grand General]] [[Cassio Tagge]] sent Vader aboard the captured pirate [[Starship|ship]] to '''attack''' the pirate's [[Unidentified pirate space station|base]], and to learn the larger organization behind the constant raids&mdash;after assigning [[Lieutenant]] [[Oon-ai]] to serve as a Tagge's [[spy]] to Vader. Once at the base, Vader, Oon-ai, and six [[stormtrooper]]s quickly killed all the resistance, including a pair of customized [[droideka]]s, while a [[Unidentified black astromech droid|black astromech droid]] planted false evidence in the station's [[computer]] incriminating Oon-ai as a [[Treason|traitor]]. Vader, with Oon-ai in tow, went farther into the station to retrieve the evidence as the station [[Death|self-destructed]] around them. After obtaining the information, they boarded the corvette as the station exploded entirely. The strike team returned to the ''Annihilator'' where Vader claimed Oon-ai was a traitor and that the Crymorah Syndicate had been backing the pirates. Later, Vader located [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]], the [[archaeologist]] who had provided the pirates with the droidekas, and enlisted her services for a [[Secret mission to Geonosis|secret mission]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|We will repair the corvette to pass a cursory examination, use it to locate the base it was operating from and then storm the station.''"<br />"''...If the base is taken, it will be set to self-destruct. Whoever is behind this will sacrifice a finger to avoid the Empire's wrath descending on their whole body.|Tagge and Vader discuss the how to best deal with the pirates|<ref name="Darth Vader 2" />}}
During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], [[Luke Skywalker]] of the Rebel Alliance managed to [[Death|destroy]] the Empire's greatest weapon: the [[planet]]-destroying [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]].<ref name="A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

With the Death Star gone, the Empire had become weaker than [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] had ever imagined it would;<ref name="Darth Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2015) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 1]]</ref> so he was forced to deal with the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]]. Jabba sent an envoy to [[Cymoon 1]] to negotiate the terms, but the Rebel Alliance had recently launched a [[The galaxy|galaxy-wide]] campaign to take advantage of their victory at [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]]. A [[Heroes of Yavin|rebel team]] led by [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] intercepted and posed as the Hutt's envoy, and [[Assault on Cymoon|assaulted]] [[Weapons Factory Alpha]].<ref name="Star Wars 1">[[Star Wars (2015) 1|''Star Wars'' (2015) 1]]</ref> Vader fought against the attackers, but failed to stop them before Skywalker succeeded in their task, and blew up the [[factory]]'s power core; causing the entire facility to explode.<ref name="Star Wars 3">[[Star Wars (2015) 3|''Star Wars'' (2015) 3]]</ref>

Vader, now in an even worse position with his master for losing the Death Star and [[Weapons Factory]] Alpha, was ordered to continue the negotiations with Jabba on Vader's [[homeworld]] of [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Darth Vader 1" /> The Empire's terms were ultimately agreed upon, and Jabba became a supplier for the Empire.<ref name="Star Wars 4">[[Star Wars (2015) 4|''Star Wars'' (2015) 4]]</ref>

[[File:VaderShootsCorvette-2015DarthVader2.png|thumb|left|255px|Vader disables the corvette's engines]]
Not long after, Vader, now under the [[Commander|command]] of [[Grand General]] [[Cassio Tagge]], were working to stop [[pirate]]s and other lawless [[Sentience|beings]] who took advantage of the Empire's disarray, with neither the [[Imperial Senate]] or the Death Star holding order, they pillaged and plundered Imperial ships and equipment at a much higher rate than was classified as normal. One such group was [[Galactic Credit Standard|funded]] by the [[Crymorah Syndicate]], and enacted several raids using a [[Droid|robotic]] [[CR90 corvette]]. Vader and Tagge soon tracked down the pirate ship when it [[Skirmish over an unidentified green planet|attacked]] [[CZ-246]], a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] carrying recently supplied weapons from Jabba's enterprise. The corvette soon disabled the [[shuttle]]'s [[engine]]s, however the [[Unidentified shuttle pilot|pilot of the shuttle]] [[Death|blew themselves up]] to prevent the pirates from gaining the Empire's property. The robotic corvette began to flee, but Vader arrived in his [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced|TIE Advanced]] [[starfighter]] and quickly blew up the corvette's engine block, ending the skirmish.<ref name="Darth Vader 2" />

Soon thereafter, Vader returned to report to Tagge aboard his [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] [[Liberty's Misrule|''Annihilator'']]. The pirate ship was put into a [[Hangar|hanger]] where it was repaired well enough to pass a cursory exam. Then Tagge conceived a plan to send Vader, their greatest warrior, to trace the pirates to their home base using the captured corvette, and learn the larger organization behind the raids, before assigning an [[Adjutant]] to Vader who would serve as Tagge's spy. Vader was unhappy with the situation but obeyed in respect for the Emperor. However, he planted incriminating evidence into a [[Unidentified black astromech droid|black astromech droid]] that would identify Adjutant [[Oon-ai]] as a [[Treason|traitor]]. Soon, Vader, Oon-ai and a [[squad]] of six [[stormtrooper]]s set out aboard the corvette and located the pirate's [[Unidentified pirate space station|base of operations]]; a [[space station]] on the extreme edge of the Outer Rim.<ref name="Darth Vader 2" />

==The attack==
{{Quote|Welcome home, boys. Brought a fine haul of Imperial weapons with you?''"<br />"''I have.|[[Unidentified Twi'lek pirate|The Twi'lek]] and Vader, before Vader [[Death|killed]] him using a [[Force choke]]|<ref name="Darth Vader 2" />}}
The pirates, a [[Unidentified Twi'lek pirate|Twi'lek]], a [[Unidentified Mon Calamari pirate|Mon Calamari]], and a [[Unidentified Sullustan (pirate base)|Sullustan]], noticed the return of their corvette and prepared to unload the contraband and begin repairs on it. However, the [[Twi'lek]] was killed by Vader when the Twi'lek was instead met by Vader's strike team. The [[astromech droid]] went outside of the station's airlock and made its way to the command center where it planted the information that would convict Oon-ai of treason.<ref name="Darth Vader 2" />

[[File:PiratesGotDroidekas-2015DarthVader2.jpg|thumb|right|270px|The pirates prepare to repel the invaders]]
The other two pirates realized they were outmatched, so they released a pair of customized [[droideka]] [[security droid]]s sporting [[missile launcher]]s to repel the boarders. The droidekas killed four troopers before Vader, at Oon-ai's urging, used [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|throw]] a pair of [[missile]]s back at the droidekas and pirates&mdash;killing them all. Except for the [[Mon Calamari]], who activated the station's self-destruct as he lay, dying. The station went to [[Color|red]]-light, and Vader ordered the surviving stormtroopers to return to the corvette and prepare it for launch. The troopers obediently did so while Vader went to the station's command center to retrieve the computer records, and the "evidence" the [[droid]] had planted&mdash;much to Oon-ai's dismay, as he was to follow per his orders from Tagge. Vader ordered the Lieutenant not to abandon his orders while Vader retrieved the information from the computer console. He finished and he casually strode to the awaiting corvette as the station exploded around them. The corvette blasted away as the pirate's base exploded entirely. Oon-ai was surprised they had survived the ordeal, Vader was not and told them they would immediately report their findings to Tagge.<ref name="Darth Vader 2" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|I recently destroyed some of your reactivated droids. They impressed me.''"<br />"''Thank you, Mr. Lord Vader.|Vader enlists the services of [[Chelli Lona Aphra]]|<ref name="Darth Vader 3" />}}
Wanting to rid himself of the meddling Oon-ai by framing the adjutant as a traitor,<ref name="Timelines" /> Vader then killed Oon-ai to finish his plan of using the attack to rid himself of Tagge's spy, and send a message of who was really in control to Tagge. And once back on the ''Annihilator'', Vader reported to the Grand [[General]], telling him he had been impressed with the results of Tagge's plan: the intelligence he had gathered from the pirate's computer confirmed the raiders had been backed by the Crymorah Syndicate. Vader also claimed Oon-ai was a traitor, and that he had used his closeness to Tagge to achieve his ends. Vader then went to the black astromech droid and insured it had deleted all records of its data upload to the pirate station. It then ejected itself into space and exploded, leaving Vader to lament how only droids were truly trustworthy and loyal.<ref name="Darth Vader 2" />

[[File:SecretMissionGeonosianQueen-2015DarthVader4.png|thumb|left|250px|Vader and Aphra on the secret mission to Geonosis]]
Soon thereafter, Vader learned from the information obtained from the pirate base's computer, that the droidekas the pirates had utilized had been supplied by a rogue [[archaeologist]], [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]]. Vader had been impressed by the customized droids, and wished to have some in his personal service to use against his enemies and on private missions. So he left the ''Annihilator'' to [[Theft of the Triple-Zero protocol personality matrix|search for the Doctor]]. He searched many worlds, and killed many witnesses before learning that Aphra was on [[Quarantine World III]] in [[Kallidahin Space]]. Vader arrived on the quarantined [[asteroid]] and saved her from [[Prison|imprisonment]] and told her of his plight. She was a "fan" of the Dark Lord's, and agreed to help him [[Secret mission to Geonosis|secure his own army]] of unquestionably loyal [[battle droid]]s on the old warzone of [[Geonosis]].<ref name="Darth Vader 3" /><ref name="Darth Vader 4">[[Darth Vader (2015) 4|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 4]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Due to his responsibility for the disaster, Darth Vader has fallen in the eyes of Emperor Palpatine. Grand General Tagge is to command the response to the Rebellion.|The second paragraph of [[Darth Vader (2015) 2|''Darth Vader'' 2's]] opening crawl|<ref name="Darth Vader 2" />}}
The attack on an unidentified pirate base first appeared in the [[Marvel Comics|Marvel comic book]] series [[Darth Vader (2015) 2|second issue]] of their [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2015)|Darth Vader series]]; it was released in [[2015]] and was written by [[Kieron Gillen]], with art by [[Salvador Larroca]].<ref name="Darth Vader 2" /> Gillen has been working alongside the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] to develop the story and events of the ongoing comic series.<ref name="Marvel Darth Vader">[[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2015)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'' (2015)]]</ref>
