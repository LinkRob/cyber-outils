Following the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], members of the [[Resistance]]'s [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] were unintentionally involved in a local '''battle''' on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] [[Pastoria]]. Visiting the planet in the hopes of persuading [[King]] [[Siroc]] into allying with the Resistance, [[Color|Black]] [[Squadron]], led by [[Temmin Wexley]], were instead tricked into assisting with the [[death]] of the king's [[Unidentified rival (Siroc)|primary rival]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Our first stop was an Outer Rim planet called Pastoria. Not the strongest world militarily, but they had fuel resources we could use, and their ruler was influential.|[[Jessika Pava]]|<ref name="Poe Dameron 29" />}}
[[File:PastorianSwarm-PoeDameron29.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Siroc shows Black Squadron around his kingdom]]
Shortly after the [[Resistance]]'s victory at the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], the [[First Order]] discovered the location of the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar]].<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> As the Resistance [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuated]] their base, [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] assigned the members of [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] to find and gather allies for the Resistance. Excluding [[Poe Dameron]], who remained behind to assist with the evacuation, the [[squadron]] departed from the Resistance base, heading for the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Pastoria]], where they planned to use their [[fuel]] resources and the influence of the [[Pastorian]] [[king]] [[Siroc]] to spread the word of the Resistance.<ref name="Poe Dameron 29" />

==The battle==
{{Quote|Siroc's fighters&hellip;they're&hellip;just hunting the others down. Shooting them in the back.|Karé Kun|<ref name="Poe Dameron 29" />}}
Black Squadron landed on Pastoria, meeting with King Siroc. [[Temmin Wexley]] greeted the king, who acted as if he was planning to assist them, telling the squadron that he agreed with their views on the First Order. Believing that the king would help them, Black Squadron asked the king to send out a broadcast that would influence other [[Star system|systems]] to fight against the First Order. Siroc, who knew that resisting against the First Order would lead to severe consequences, instead asked Black Squadron to provide assistance with a group of [[terrorist]]s who sought to depose him. He introduced them to [[Colonel]] [[Squill]], requesting that the squadron help escort [[Unidentified transport (Siroc)|one]] of his [[transport]]s through what he claimed was a region occupied by the terrorists.<ref name="Poe Dameron 29" />

[[File:PastorianTransportDestruction-PoeDameron29.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Black Squadron watch as the transport is destroyed]]
As they traveled through the region, the group were met by forces of Siroc's [[Unidentified rival (Siroc)|closest rival]], who thought they were being invaded by Black Squadron and Siroc's forces, and were attempting to defend against it. In retaliation, Colonel Squill ordered the king's forces to defend the transport he was on. As the king's forces were taking damage, Wexley decided that Black Squadron had to engage the enemy forces, but only to shoot to disable and ensure minimal casualties.<ref name="Poe Dameron 29" />

After attempting to disable an enemy [[Starfighter|fighter]], [[Suralinda Javos]] discovered that the [[Deflector shield|shields]] on the Pastorian fighters were far weaker than they had expected. As the enemy forces pulled back, Siroc's fighters opened fire on them and headed for [[Unidentified transport (Siroc's rival)|their]] [[transport]], and destroyed it, [[Death|killing]] Siroc's rival in the process.<ref name="Poe Dameron 29" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Snap&hellip;what happened here? What did we do?''"<br />"''I don't know, Karé. But I think we should go find out.|Karé Kun and Temmin Wexley|<ref name="Poe Dameron 29" />}}
[[File:SirocBlackSquadron-PoeDameron29.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Wexley confronts Siroc]]
Black Squadron returned to Siroc, and Wexley demanded to know what they had been used for. Siroc confessed that the transport they had helped destroy held the closest claimant to his [[throne]] aboard, and that he had wanted to eliminate him so that when the First Order inevitably arrived on the planet, they would negotiate with him rather than his rival, who would resist and get the [[Pastorian]]s killed. Black Squadron tried to convince Siroc that there was no possibility of negotiating with the First Order, but Siroc continued to believe that he could get them to do what he wanted. Realizing Siroc was never going to help the Resistance, Black Squadron left Pastoria, heading elsewhere in search of allies.<ref name="Poe Dameron 29" />
