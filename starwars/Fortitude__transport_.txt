The '''''Fortitude''''', nicknamed "'''lucky lander'''" by some troops, was a [[YC-123B transport hauler]] in the service of the [[Resistance]] during their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[First Order]]. During the [[Battle of Exegol]], the ''Fortitude'' carried several troops and [[Orbak]]s to attack the [[Sith Eternal]]'s navigation tower, though it would be redirected and covered to attack the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] instead when the tower signal was transferred to the First Order ship. The hauler landed on the hull of the ''Steadfast'' and deployed Resistance [[troop]]s, [[pilot]]ed by [[Dreanna Conunda]] and [[Gandris Dyun]]. The ''Fortitude'' would ultimately survive the battle and return to the [[Resistance]] [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|base]] on [[Ajan Kloss]] to celebrate the victory against the [[Sith Eternal|Sith]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
