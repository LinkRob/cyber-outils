A [[Gender|male]] individual who lived during the [[High Republic Era]] was the '''husband''' of [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Franek]]. Prior to the [[Galactic Republic]]'s evacuation of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Palagosal]]<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> between [[230 BBY]] and [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">Per the reasoning [[:Template:StarlightDate|here]], the events of ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', which take place a [[Standard year|year]] and a half after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]] of [[Starlight Beacon]], must take place in [[228 BBY]]. ''Beware the Nameless'' establishes that the [[Galactic Republic]] evacuated [[Palagosal]] between the novel's events and the [[Jedi Order]]'s recall to [[Coruscant]], an event ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]]. Therefore, the evacuation must have occurred between 230 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and 228 BBY.</ref> the man had wanted to visit the planet to go to a [[Unidentified luxury rainforest resort|luxury rainforest resort]].<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> In 228 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Franek recalled his [[Marriage|husband's]] desire to visit the resort when speaking with several [[Jedi]], Republic Defense Coalition personnel, and Republic [[Senator]] [[Rina Greylark]] about why the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s would be drawn to the world.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />
