The '''''Flarestar''-class attack shuttle''', also known simply as the '''rapier ship''', was a model of saucer-shaped [[shuttle]] used by the criminal [[Ohnaka Gang]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="AEYNTK" /> They were often deployed from the Gang's larger [[Corona-class armed frigate|''Corona''-class armed frigate]].<ref name="SWE Pirate saucer mothership">{{SWE|technology|pirate-saucer-mothership|Pirate Saucer Mothership}}</ref>

Around [[33 BBY]], a ''Flarestar''-class attack shuttle was stolen by a [[Cad Bane's group|group of bounty hunters]], after crashing onto the [[moon of Drazkel]], due to [[Aurra Sing]] thinking the [[Weequay]]'s have more profitable cargo. Sing took control of the shuttle, piloting it while [[Vorhdeilo]] meet [[Cad Bane]] at the [[entry ramp]] before they destroyed the other ships and went to pick up their employer [[Maul]] and his three [[DRK-1 Dark Eye probe droid]]s.<ref name="Darth Maul 5" />

A ship of this kind transported [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and Ohnaka to the surface of [[Florrum]], after the pirates found him on [[Vanqor]].<ref name="DC">{{TCW|Dooku Captured}}</ref> Later, [[Barb Mentir]], an accomplice of [[Turk Falso]], used another saucer to attack and take down a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Kharrus's Nu-class attack shuttle|''Nu''-class attack shuttle]] bringing a [[ransom]] of [[spice]] proving that the craft was very capable as a [[starfighter]] against enemy craft. Count Dooku used the vessel as an escape craft after breaking out of the Weequay prison, killing the two [[Treason|treacherous]] pirates before taking the ship.<ref name="TGG">{{TCW|The Gungan General}}</ref>

Several of these shuttles were used by the Ohnaka gang members who decided to work for Maul and [[Savage Opress]]. Three were deployed from their larger ''Corona''-class armed frigates opening fire on the base, with the pirates taking cover but Hondo Okhana was the only one standing his ground.<ref name="Revival">{{TCW|Revival (episode)}}</ref>
