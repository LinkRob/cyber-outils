'''Abommy the Gig''' was a toy owned by the [[Youngling|child]] [[Jyn Erso]] while she lived in a [[Erso homestead|homestead]] on the [[planet]] [[Lah'mu]] with her [[parent]]s [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen]] and [[Lyra Erso]]. It was generally kept in Jyn's toybox,<ref name="ROUVG" /> although she would sometimes leave Abommy underneath her bed in order to protect her from monsters. In the [[Standard year|year]] [[13 BBY]], it was out on her bed with several of her other toys when the [[Galactic Empire]] arrived at the homestead in search of Galen. While she waited alone in a cave, Jyn briefly wished for Abommy the Gig.<ref name="Rebel Rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref>

Abommy the Gig had the shape of a [[wampa]]<ref name="ROUVG" /> and was covered in [[Color|white]] wooly material with a brown patch of fabric making up a face with two button [[eye]]s.<ref name="RO">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Abommy the Gig first appeared as a prop in the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which was released in North America on [[December 16]], [[2016]]. The prop was not identified in the film, but received a name in the ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released alongside the film.
