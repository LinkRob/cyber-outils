The '''Endorian Scatter Gun''' was a [[scatter gun]] owned by the [[Trandoshan]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Charr]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Cunning Scatter Gun|standard slugthrower]]. Much of the exterior of this [[Slugthrower|gun]] consisted of [[bone]] and [[Clothing|cloth]] bound by string.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Endorian Scatter Gun was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Charr's Cunning Scatter Gun, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 3.0.0 alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Endorian Trapper]]. This wrap could be unlocked by purchasing the Endorian Charr Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
