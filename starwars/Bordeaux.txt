'''Bordeaux''' was a [[human]] individual who served under [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]] at the [[Battle of Hoth]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> During the battle, Bordeaux carried out an assault on the rear [[blast door]]s of [[Echo Base]] which tipped the battle to the Empire. For Bordeaux's actions, they became known as the '''Hero of Hoth''' and received a promotion to general.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27" /> Between 3 ABY and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Bounty Hunters 27|''Bounty Hunters'' 27]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> Bordeaux encountered Imperial<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27" /> [[lieutenant]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 25">[[Bounty Hunters 25|''Bounty Hunters'' 25]]</ref> [[Beilert Valance]] on the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] ''[[Righteous Fist]]'' whilst Valance awaited the briefing for his next mission.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27" />
