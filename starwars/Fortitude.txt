The '''''Fortitude''''' was a [[Maxima-A-class heavy cruiser|''Maxima-A''-class heavy cruiser]] in the service in the [[First Order Navy|naval forces]] of the [[First Order]] under the command of [[Colonel]] [[Barrut]]. It [[Battle of Ikkrukk|attacked]] [[Grail City]] after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of the [[New Republic]]'s [[capital]] [[planet]] [[Hosnian Prime]] but was defeated by [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 30" />
