'''Felucian Snack Bites''' were a type of [[foodstuff|snack]] that was served on board the ''[[Halcyon]]'', an [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]],<ref name="Cruiser" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Consumable by [[human]]s, this assortment was available to order in the [[Sublight Lounge]] aboard the ship.<ref name="Cruiser" />

==Behind the scenes==
Felucian Snack Bites were available in the [[In-universe|real world]] at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]], Florida,<ref name="Cruiser" /> which opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref> It was last served at the hotel during the [[September 28]][[September 29|&ndash;]][[September 30|30]] voyage of [[2023]], the final voyage before the closure of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser.<ref name="Official website">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/overview|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Overview}}</ref> The menu did not include a description of the snack,<ref name="Cruiser" /><ref name="DFB">{{WebCite|url=https://www.disneyfoodblog.com/the-sublight-lounge-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=Sublight Lounge &ndash; Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|work=[https://www.disneyfoodblog.com Disney Food Blog]|archivedate=20240407161554}}</ref> but it has been speculated that it contained [[Wikipedia:Almond|almonds]], dried [[Wikipedia:Cherry|cherries]], toasted [[Wikipedia:Chickpea|chickpeas]], [[Wikipedia:Pumpkin seed|pepitas]], dried [[Wikipedia:Mango|mango]] pieces, all salted with a little bit of pepper.<ref name="WDWNT">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2022/03/review-all-the-snacks-available-at-the-sublight-lounge-on-the-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=REVIEW: All the Snacks Available at the Sublight Lounge on the Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|work=[https://www.wdwnt.com WDW News Today]|archivedate=20220820055103}}</ref> At times, it may have also contained a rice-based cereal similar to [[Wikipedia:Rice Krispies|Rice Krispies]].<ref name="DFB-2">{{WebCite|url=https://www.disneyfoodblog.com/2022/02/25/the-best-food-is-hiding-at-the-star-wars-hotel-in-disney-world/#more-633726|text=The BEST Food Is HIDING at the Star Wars Hotel in Disney World|work=[https://www.disneyfoodblog.com Disney Food Blog]|archivedate=20240529135207}}</ref>
