'''Club Lazuli''' was a club<ref name="Aphra 9" /> owned by the [[bartender]] [[Di'reni]] in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]]<ref name="Di'reni">{{Twitter|crashwong|status/1483488065213460480|[[Alyssa Wong]]|quote=Also, I adore that variant cover with Aphra hanging out with Di'reni, owner-bartender of the Canto Bight nightclub Lazuli (and Just Lucky + Ariole Yu's childhood friend). My girl!|archivedate=20220118172000}}</ref> on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Cantonica">{{Databank|cantonica|Cantonica}}</ref> The club was under the protection of the [[Sixth Kin]], a criminal organization led by [[Wen Delphis]]. After [[Koz]], a criminal who owed debt to the Sixth Kin, was confronted by [[Ariole Yu]], one of the organization's [[Lieutenant (criminal)|lieutenants]], and the [[sniper]] [[Just Lucky]], a fight broke out within Club Lazuli between the Sixth Kin operatives and Koz's associates. Koz escaped through the club's back exit, and Yu and Lucky pursued him as Di'reni retrieved a [[blaster]] and shot down their attackers.<ref name="Aphra 9" />
