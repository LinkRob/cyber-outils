"'''All That's Left'''" is a short story written by [[Caitlin Sullivan Kelly]]<ref name="Tweet" /> and released on the [https://www.swtor.com official site] for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' on [[August 4]], [[2022]]. It follows [[Sa'har Kateen]]'s journey after escaping from [[Elom (planet)/Legends|Elom]] with [[Darth Nul]]'s [[Holocron/Legends|holocron]].<ref name="All That's Left" />

==Publisher's summary==
''Without her master's guidance, Padawan Sa'har Kateen must face her most difficult trial yet: forgiveness.''<ref>{{Twitter|username=SWTOR|url=status/1555272813077995522|name=Star Wars: The Old Republic|quote=Without her master's guidance, Padawan Sa'har Kateen must face her most difficult trial yet: forgiveness.|archivedate=20220804192247}}</ref>
