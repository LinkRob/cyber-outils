An '''airlock''' was a structure built into most [[starship]]s that allowed beings to enter or exit pressurized [[atmosphere]]s from the [[vacuum]] of [[Realspace|space]].

Shortly before the [[Battle of Endor]], [[Admiral]] [[Jhared Montferrat]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] used an airlock on the ''[[Devastator]]'' to [[Death|execute]] suspected [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[Spy|spies]].<ref name="Blade Squadron">{{InsiderCite|issue1=149|issue2=150|Blade Squadron (short story)}}</ref>

==Description==
[[File:Airlock-2015DarthVader25.png|left|thumb|339x339px|A complex airlock aboard the ''Executor'']]
Airlocks were equipped to most [[Realspace|space]]-faring vehicles for the purpose of allowing their passengers to enter from and exit to the vacuum of space. They were also used as connection points between spaceships: by attaching two ships' airlocks and pressurizing the passage their passengers could safely pass from one to the other as long as the connection remained intact. This method of mid-space docking was mostly used to connect similarly sized spaceships that couldn't land in each others' [[hangar]]<ref name="Idiot's Array">{{Rebels|Idiot's Array (episode)}}</ref> and was particularly dangerous as a hit to the airlocks' connection point could fuse them together, causing the ships to be stuck and requiring their crew to perform a manual disconnect using emergency controls in the airlocks' vicinity.<ref name="Secret Cargo">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref>

As an extremely common feature shared between most spaceships, airlocks came in vastly different shapes and sizes, from small [[hatch]]es in a starship's floor<ref name="Iron Squadron">{{Rebels|Iron Squadron (episode)}}</ref> to large circular tunnels with the seal in their end.<ref name="Death Trap">{{TCW|Death Trap}}</ref> Some were as simple as a room with a vacuum worthy seal on the space end and a [[Deflector shield|shield]] on the spaceship end<ref name="Cargo of Doom" /> and others such as those aboard the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought]]s were extremely complex structures with an attached control room and [[transparisteel]] observation windows.<ref name="Vader 25">[[Darth Vader (2015) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 25]]</ref>
