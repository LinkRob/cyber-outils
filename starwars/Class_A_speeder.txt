The '''class A speeder''' was a type of [[speeder]]. [[Baron]] [[Slandarv]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] owned one such speeder, receiving numerous fines for it. The [[Unidentified Imperial supervisor|supervisor]] of [[Assistant Data Processor]] [[Fabarian]] ordered him to delete the tickets, but Fabarian, secretly intending to investigate the baron for the [[Schism Imperial]], left one undeleted so that Slandarv would summon him to his office.<ref name="Vader 44" />
