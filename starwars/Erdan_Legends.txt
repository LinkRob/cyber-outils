'''Erdan''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] who lived in [[Mos Espa/Legends|Mos Espa]],<ref name="CSWE" /> a [[City/Legends|city]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]],<ref name="MosEspa">{{DB|location|mosespa|Mos Espa}}</ref> and was [[Slavery/Legends|enslaved]].<ref name="CSWE" /> He had the normalcy of a standard Human before his enslaver chemically [[Giantism|enlarged]] him<ref name="CSWE" /> to increase his work rate,<ref name="TVD">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]'' </ref> giving him greater strength and endurance.<ref name="CSWE" /> The permanent heat-bonded [[Color/Legends|black]] and white patterned paint located on his face served as a symbol of ownership over him. Erdan had tanned skin, black [[Hair/Legends|hair]], and stood at a towering height.<ref name="CSWE" />
