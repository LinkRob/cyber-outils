'''Clone ordnance specialists''',<ref name="Databank armor">{{Databank|clone-trooper-armor-history-gallery|Clone Trooper Armor History Gallery}} {{C|Slide 12}}</ref> also known as '''Bomb Squad Clone Troopers''',<ref name="Militaries">{{SideshowCite|set=Sixth Scale Figures|pack=Bomb Squad Clone Trooper|link=collectibles/star-wars-bomb-squad-clone-trooper-ordnance-specialist-sideshow-collectibles-100192}}</ref> were specialized [[clone trooper]]s who specialized in disarming ordnance,<ref name="TCWVirus" /> especially the defusing of bombs, which was one of the most dangerous jobs in the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] military.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> They also were known for using bombs and other ordnance in combat. Ordnance specialists were organized into [[squad]] units. Bomb squads were stationed within larger [[Cloning|clone]] [[battalion]]s.<ref name="Brotherhood">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']]</ref> Like most clone troopers,<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'' &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[Clone trooper]]s</ref> the ordnance specialists were 1.83 [[meter]]s or 6 [[Foot|feet]] tall.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

Clone ordnance specialists' uniform consisted of the standard [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] with bright [[Color|orange]] markings on the upper legs, the lower arms; and on the skull, the back of the head, and cheeks of the helmet. In addition to having special tools for bomb disarming, ordnance specialists could be armed with standard weapons of the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Republic Army]] such as the [[DC-15A blaster carbine]].<ref name="TCWVirus" /> Clones chosen for the perilous work as a bomb squad trooper needed to have steady nerves, a gentle touch and be able to focus amid the chaos of war. Bomb squad troopers were also skilled in armed and unarmed combat, military tactics, infiltration and security.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==History==
During the year [[21 BBY]], these troopers were called in to protect the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] from biological warfare after [[Doctor]] [[Nuvo Vindi]] re-created the [[Blue Shadow Virus]]. They followed [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] and their troops into [[Trade Federation laboratory|Vindi's secret lab]] to disarm [[viral bomb]]s containing the deadly virus. Although they successfully disarmed almost all of the bombs, the [[LEP-series service droid]] [[LEP-86C8]] succeeded in activating a bomb, releasing the virus and causing Republic forces to lock themselves in the lab to prevent it from spreading.<ref name="TCWVirus" />
