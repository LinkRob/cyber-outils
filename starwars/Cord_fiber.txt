'''Cord fibers''' could be produced from processed [[kelivan root]]s from the [[planet]] [[Ronaphaven]] and used to secure cargo. While on a [[Mission to Ronaphaven|mission]] to Ronaphaven<ref name="TOTF" /> in [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Bell Zettifar]] and [[Burryaga Agaburry]] listened to a [[Unidentified Ronaphaven governor|local governor]] on the world fervently insist that his planet's high-quality kelivan roots could be processed into both standard cord fibers and stronger variants coveted by [[Corellian]]s to secure cargo on [[cruiser]]s.<ref name="TOTF" />
