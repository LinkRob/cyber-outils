'''Alonzo Simon''' is an individual who served as an editor for a number of ''[[Star Wars]]'' graphic novels, including ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Graphic Novel Adaptation]]'',<ref name="SWTFAGNA">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Graphic Novel Adaptation]]''</ref> the ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One Graphic Novel Adaptation]]'',<ref name="SWROGNA">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One Graphic Novel Adaptation]]''</ref>, the ''[[Star Wars: A New Hope Graphic Novel Adaptation]]'',<ref name="SWANHGNA">''[[Star Wars: A New Hope Graphic Novel Adaptation]]''</ref>, and the ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi Graphic Novel Adaptation]]''.<ref name="SWTLJGNA">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi Graphic Novel Adaptation]]''</ref>
