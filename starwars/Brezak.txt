'''Brezaks''', also known as '''Zygerrian gliding lizards''',<ref name="AA">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> were giant<ref name="Databank Brezak" /> [[reptavian]]<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[lizard]]s that were able to glide via their skin flaps.<ref name="Databank Brezak" /> 

==Biology and appearance==
They had a light bone structure and a flat tail which they used to steer while flying,<ref name="AA" /> though brezaks preferred to run, jump, and climb. 

==History==
They were trained by the [[Zygerrian]]s to be used as transportation<ref name="BMF 91">{{BuildFalconCite|91|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> by both the [[Zygerrian Royal Guard|royal guards]] as well as the high class of the [[planet]] [[Zygerria]].<ref name="Slaves of the Republic">{{TCW|Slaves of the Republic}}</ref> Brezaks on Zygerria could be outfitted with saddles, although rides will still known to be bumpy.<ref name="BMF 91" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], the slaver [[Raney]] and his group used brezaks on the planet [[Ord Mantell]]. One of the Brezak fought the adolescent [[rancor]] [[Muchi]].<ref name="Rampage">{{TBB|Rampage (episode)}}</ref>
