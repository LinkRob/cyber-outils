'''Crypt'''<ref name="Poe Dameron: Flight Log" /> was the nickname<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-09-2.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-09}}</ref> of a location in [[the galaxy]]. After [[Attack on Tuanul|capturing]] the [[Resistance]] [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] on [[Jakku]], [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] of the [[First Order]] filed a report and stated the [[black box]] of Dameron's [[Poe Dameron's blue X-wing|T-70 X-wing Starfighter]] was to be sent to Crypt for examination.<ref name="Poe Dameron: Flight Log" />
