The '''Class-6 escape pod''' was a model of [[escape pod]]. The ''[[Tantive IV]]'' was equipped with eight Class-6 escape pods, one of which was used by [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]] during the [[secret mission to Tatooine]].<ref name="BMF" />
