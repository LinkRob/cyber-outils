The [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Grand Army of the Republic]]&mdash;assisted by the [[Jedi Order]]&mdash;and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] waged a '''battle''' on the [[planet]] [[Hypori]] in [[22 BBY]] as part of the [[Clone Wars]]. The Republic's [[442nd Siege Battalion]] and [[212th Attack Battalion]] were deployed in the destructive [[Ground warfare|battle]], though the Republic was ultimately defeated by the [[Kaleesh]] [[cyborg]] [[warlord]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] and his [[Separatist Droid Army]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Even after losing so many Jedi on Geonosis, we had managed to turn it into a costly victory against the Confederacy of Independent Systems. Then we won again on Christophsis. And once more on Dantooine, against all odds.|Obi-Wan Kenobi's recounting of the early stages of the Clone Wars|<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />}}
[[File:DefenseOfDantooine-ObiWan3.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The defense of Dantooine (pictured) was a Republic victory that led into the battle of Hypori.]]
The onset of the [[Clone Wars]] in [[22 BBY]]<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> was sparked by the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], where the [[Galactic Republic]] had seen a first victory against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] that had been costly due to the loss of many [[Jedi]]. The trend of success that began with the Battle of Geonosis continued during the [[Battle of Christophsis]], and the [[defense of Dantooine]],<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" /> which was still ongoing by the [[time]] the battle of [[Hypori]] began,<ref name="Dantooine note" /> also met the Republic's [[Grand Army of the Republic|forces]] with victory.<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />

==The battle==
In 22 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> following the battles of Christophsis and [[Dantooine]], the Galactic Republic and [[Jedi Order]] were involved in a [[Ground warfare|battle]] on the<ref name="Catalyst" /> [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[Planet|world]] Hypori<ref name="Catalyst" /> against the Confederacy of Independent Systems. The Grand Army of the Republic's [[442nd Siege Battalion]] and the [[212th Attack Battalion]] fought in the battle.<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" /> During the engagement, a group of [[Jedi Knight]]s were taken by surprise and an [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship|''Acclamator''-class cruiser]] crashed into Hypori.<ref name="Catalyst" /> The battle ultimately ended with a slaughter of the Republic forces, and the [[death]] of a number of [[clone trooper]]s and Jedi at the hands of the [[Kaleesh]] [[cyborg]] [[warlord]]<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" /> and [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies]]<ref name="ForceCollection LS Grievous">{{ForceCollection|General Grievous [Last Stand]|star=5}}</ref> [[General]] [[Grievous]]. At least one participating [[B1-series battle droid]] was destroyed in the battle as well.<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Many of our forces were still demoralized following the losses at Hypori and exhausted by our tough fight on Christophsis.|Obi-Wan Kenobi's recounting of the effects of Clone Wars' battles on the Republic's forces|<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />}}
[[File:CutOffByIGs-ObiWan3.png|thumb|right|250px|Kenobi hoped that volunteering his troops for the Battle of Abrion Bridge (pictured) would revitalize their morale following their loss on Hypori.]]
The events of the battle on Hypori led to a wave of fear and demoralization among Republic forces, much of which concerned the threat of Grievous, as well as the rapid expansion of the war. This fear&mdash;alongside a newfound sense of responsibility from the newly anointed [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and exhaustion caused by the Battle of Christophsis&mdash;lingered as the [[Jedi Master]] prepared for the oncoming [[Battle of Abrion Bridge]]. In order to encourage his forces, Kenobi attempted to live up to his newfound leadership role and volunteered the 212th Attack Battalion to lead the assault on [[Abrion Bridge]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" /> As a result of the Republic's loss on Hypori, [[Baktoid Armor Workshop]] was able to finance the relocation of a [[Geonosian hive]] colony to Hypori in order to bolster [[droid]] production on the planet.<ref name="GermanMag121">{{GermanFanCite|121|Database}}</ref> In [[19 BBY]]<ref name="TamborDate">[[Clone Commander]] [[Neyo]]'s dossier in [[Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin 121|''Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin'' 121]] is stated to have been released during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]] of the [[Clone Wars]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]].</ref> during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]] campaign of the Clone Wars, [[Clone Commander]] [[Neyo]] of the [[91st Mobile Reconnaissance Corps]] lamented the losses the 442nd Siege Battalion suffered at Hypori&mdash;and the resulting production efforts undertaken by Baktoid Armor Workshop&mdash;in "[[Neyo's Almanac of Clankers]]," a dossier regarding various Separatist droid threats facing the Republic.<ref name="GermanMag121" />

When an [[engineer]]ing team led by [[Commander]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] moved into a former Techno Union [[Hypori Imperial research station|droid factory]] located on Hypori to [[Project Stardust|work]] on weapons design and production for a secret mobile [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|battle station]] during the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Hull|hulk]] of the ''Acclamator''-class [[cruiser]] stood as a grim memorial of the battle.<ref name="Catalyst" /> Around [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date2">The frame story from [[Obi-Wan 3|''Obi-Wan'' 3]] occurs during around [[1 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:Obi-WanFrameDate|here]].</ref> Kenobi thought about the aftermath of the battle of Hypori, recounting its disastrous course as well as the manner in which the engagement had demoralized Republic forces.<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Aayla_and_Ki-Adi_vs._Grievous.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Battle of Hypori in ''Star Wars'' Legends]]
The battle of Hypori was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2016]] novel ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'', written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Catalyst" /> It later went on to make its first appearance in the [[Obi-Wan 3|third issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan (comic series)|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan'']], which was written by [[Christopher Cantwell]], pencilled by [[Alessandro Miracolo]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" /> on [[July 27]], [[2022]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/101189/star_wars_obi-wan_2022_3|text=Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi (2022) #3}}</ref> Prior to the issue's release, the battle was first pictured in a preview for it published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[July 20]].<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-obi-wan-3-exclusive-preview|text=Ben Kenobi Remembers the Clone Wars in Marvel's ''Star Wars: Obi-Wan'' #3 – Exclusive Preview}}</ref>

The [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]]-era [[Battle of Hypori/Legends|battle]] on [[Hypori/Legends|Hypori]] was originally first depicted in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in [[Chapter 20 (Star Wars: Clone Wars)|Chapter 20]] of the animated microseries ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="Clone Wars 20">{{CW|20}}</ref> which aired on [[April 8]], [[2004]] on [[Cartoon Network]].<ref name="Episode date">{{TVcom|url=shows/star-wars-clone-wars/chapter-xx-276626|text=''Star Wars: Clone Wars'' &ndash; Season 1, Episode 20: Chapter XX}}</ref> It was first identified as the "Battle of Hypori" in the Legends continuity in ''[[Star Wars Fandex Deluxe Edition]]'', a [[2008]] fold-out reference guide written by [[Christopher Cerasi]].<ref name="Fandex">''[[Star Wars Fandex Deluxe Edition]]''</ref> The canon novel [[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']], written by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]] and<ref name="Brotherhood">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']]</ref> published on [[May 10]], 2022,<ref name="Brotherhood release">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/678652/star-wars-brotherhood-by-mike-chen|text=Star Wars: Brotherhood}}</ref> includes an indirect reference to the Legends Battle of Hypori<ref name="CW references">{{Twitter|mikechenwriter|status/1721944833500299344|[[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]]|quote=Things I brought over from Genndy into BROTHERHOOD: "Like fire across the galaxy" Mace Windu on Dantooine General Grievous vs Ki-Adi-Mundi Anakin vs Ventress (it's very subtle and comes with some caveats but it's there: "I've seen that ship before." "It's you, isn't it?)"|archiveurl=https://archive.li/PsVkF|nolive=1}}</ref> regarding Jedi Master [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] healing from scars he sustained during a recent encounter with Grievous.<ref name="Brotherhood" /> Chen later revealed it was part of his aim to work aspects of the ''Clone Wars'' animated series into his book.<ref name="Sneak in">{{Twitter|mikechenwriter|status/1721948902495273379|[[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]]|quote=I love Genndy as much as you, so I wanted to sneak in as much as possible!|archiveurl=https://archive.li/5GxaG|nolive=1}}</ref>
