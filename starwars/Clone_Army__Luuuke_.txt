After replacing [[Luuke Skywalker]] as the replacement for [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] following the [[Lost Tribe of Sith emergence]] in [[43 ABY]], [[Luuuke Skywalker]] set out to gather all the [[Cloning/Legends|clones]] created by [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]] into one force. Luuuke aimed to use this '''Clone Army''' to conquer [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]]. Luuuke worked with [[Streeen]] to create a [[Time travel/Legends|time machine]] they used to transport the Clone [[Army/Legends|Army]] to the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]], when they took on the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic's]] [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Clone Army]].
