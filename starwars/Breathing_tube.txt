'''Breathing tubes''' were designed to help individuals with breathing, as well as survive in harsh environments, such as [[Realspace|space]]. One user of breathing tubes was the notorious [[bounty hunter]], [[Cad Bane]]. Bane's were designed to counter [[Force choke|Force-choking]].<ref name="Build R2 52">{{BuildR2Cite|52|Droid Directory}}</ref>
