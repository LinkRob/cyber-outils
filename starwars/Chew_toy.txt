'''Chew toys''' were durable toys that emitted a gentle glow to appeal to a creature's curiosity.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[merqaal]] [[Nix]] found a chew toy and hid it in his nest aboard the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', and [[EML-850 light freighter]] operated by his companion, the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
A chew toy appeared as an item in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].
The fang has a chance to spawn when the player interacts with Nix’s pile of scraps on the Trailblazer. When it is equipped as a minor charm, the player will gain more [[adrenaline]] when defeating an enemy with a stealth takedown while they are distracted by Nix.<ref name="Outlaws" />
