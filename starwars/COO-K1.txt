'''COO-K1''', also called '''Cooki''', was a cranky [[COO-series cook droid]] who worked as a [[Chef|line cook]].<ref name="New Droid Cooki" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
COO-K1 was created as a live entertainment [[droid]] that debuted at [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan 2025]]. Cooki was brought to life by a team of droid builders from around the world that included [[David Ferreira]], [[Chris Stroud]], [[Trevor Zaharichuk]], [[Matt Hobbs]], [[Patrick Gray]], [[Luke Daley]], [[Psy DeLacy]], [[Seth Kelly]], and [[Neil Hutchison]].<ref name="New Droid Cooki" />
