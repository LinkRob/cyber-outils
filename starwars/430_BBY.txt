The following events took place in or around the year '''430 BBY''', aka '''2847 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]], '''411 BFE''' according to the [[Imperial calendar]], and '''Year 7547''' according to the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.

==Events==
*"[[Seismic Events and Their Role in Force Confluences]]" is written after a survey of [[Dalna]]'s [[Maawat Mountains]] by the [[Galactic Republic]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="Survey">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]'' to [[230 BBY]]. The [[Galactic Republic]] survey of the [[Maawat Mountains]] is stated to have been conducted over two centuries prior, meaning it must have happened by around 430 BBY.</ref>
*A [[Spiral-class Viz-Core Eviscerator|''Spiral''-class Viz-Core Eviscerator]] is constructed. {{Circa}}<ref name="Spiral">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]'' to [[230 BBY]]. ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' takes place shortly before and concurrently to ''The Fallen Star'', meaning it must be set around the same year. As the Eviscerator is stated to have been constructed about 200 years prior, it must have been constructed around 430 BBY.</ref>
