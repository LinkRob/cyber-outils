'''Donclode Onstruss''' was an independent [[HoloNet]] node operator who was recruited by the [[Commission for the Protection of the Republic]] after his transmissions and posting caught the attention of the [[Twi'lek]] [[senator]] [[Orn Free Taa]]. He was relocated to the [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[capital]] [[Coruscant]] after being airlifted from a [[Battle of Zequardia|combat zone]] on [[Zequardia]]. During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Onstruss produced a [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] titled "[[Evil Has Its Eyes On You]]," which depicted ghoulish caricatures of [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and [[General]] [[Grievous]]; the leaders of the Confederacy.<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Behind the scenes==
Donclode Onstruss was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released on [[October 25]], [[2016]].
