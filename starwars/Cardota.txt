'''Cardota''',<ref name="Graphics" /> also known as '''Hosnian Cardota''',<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> was one of the five [[planet]]s<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> that made up the [[Hosnian system]], in the [[Core Worlds|Core]] region of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Graphics" /> The [[Star system|system]], including Cardota, was [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroyed]] by the [[First Order]]<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>
