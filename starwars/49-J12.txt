'''49-J12''',<ref name="Darth Vader 48">{{DarthVaderCite|48|Galactic Villains}}</ref> or '''Fournine-Jaytwelve''', was a [[droid]] that worked for the [[Hutt Clan|Hutts]] at the [[H'unn Cabaret Pit]] on [[Nar Kaaga]]. The droid attempted to serve the [[bounty hunter]] [[Beilert Valance]] when he entered the establishment to meet with the [[bail bondsman]] [[Syphacc]], but Valance refused Fournine-Jaytwelve's offer of drinks. After Valance was attacked by the [[assassin]] [[Tasu Leech]], the bounty hunter threw Fournine-Jaytwelve in the way of Leech's [[Knife|knives]] despite the droid's protests.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 8" />
