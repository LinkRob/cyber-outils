'''''Bounty Hunters'' 36''' is the thirty-sixth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Lan Medina]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[July 12]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''BOBA FETT: MARKED FOR DEATH! THE BLACK SUN SYNDICATE wants BOBA FETT'S HEAD! But the new team of BOUNTY HUNTERS has something to say about that! Featuring DURGE, BOSSK, 4-LOM, ZUCKUSS, DEATHSTICK, VALANCE, KHEL and T'ONGA!''
