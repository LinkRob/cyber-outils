The '''Bormter system''' was located in [[Wild Space]] in grid square H-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bormter [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Bormter system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as the "Svekk" [[Star system/Legends|system]] in "[[The Galaxy Dragon and crew|The ''Galaxy Dragon'' and crew]]," an article written by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and published in the [[Dragon Magazine 200|200th issue]] of [[Dragon (magazine)|''Dragon Magazine'']] in [[December]] [[1993]] for use with [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Galaxy Dragon">{{DragonCite|200|The Galaxy Dragon and crew|The ''Galaxy Dragon'' and crew}}</ref> The article is considered noncanonical with respect to Legends.<ref name="Unlicensed">{{UnlicensedStatus}}</ref> The [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' later identified the canonical Legends system as the Bormter system.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
