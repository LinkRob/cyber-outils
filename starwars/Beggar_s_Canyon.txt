'''Beggar's Canyon''' was a dry river bed that cut through [[Ben's Mesa]], on the [[desert]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Tatooine]]. Its landmarks included the so-called [[Dead Man's Turn]], the [[Diablo Cut]], and the [[Stone Needle]]. Beggar's Canyon led to the [[Desert Plain]] on one side, and to [[Ebe Crater Valley]] on the other side, with the junction being known as [[the Notch]].<ref name="CL" />

In the last [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], Beggar's Canyon was inhabited by [[hermit]]s who had been chased out of [[Mos Espa]] as well as [[bounty hunter]]s on the run. They resided in small houses nested in the canyon's rock walls. Despite being inhabited, Beggar's Canyon was also part of the notoriously hazardous [[Boonta Eve Classic]] race track. On non-race [[Standard day|days]], Tatooinian youngsters would sneak into the canyon to perform dangerous runs aboard their customized [[landspeeder]]s or [[skyhopper]]s.<ref name="CL" />

[[File:BeggarsCanyon-2015StarWars15.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Beggar's Canyon on the desert world Tatooine.]]
In their youth, [[pilot]]s [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Biggs Darklighter]] flew [[T-16 skyhopper]]s through the narrow canyon, and Skywalker once threaded the Stone Needle.<ref name="ANH">''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]''</ref> They later recalled Beggar's Canyon while fighting in the [[Battle of Yavin]]; during the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] assault on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], the two pilots entered into a narrow trench leading towards the planet-destroying space station's reactor shaft, and the trench reminded them of the canyon.<ref name="Episode IV" />

==Behind the scenes==
In [[2015]], Beggar's Canyon appeared in the Korean web-[[Star Wars comic books|comic]] simply titled [[Star Wars (LINE Webtoon)|''Star Wars'']]. The comic depicted a young Luke Skywalker racing his friends through the canyon in his T-16 skyhopper, crashing the vehicle soon after.<ref name="LINE">[[Star Wars (LINE Webtoon)|''Star Wars'' LINE Webtoon]]</ref> However, due to cultural leeway, the comic's [[canon]]ical status is in a "grey area."<ref name="Heddle">{{Twitter|jenniferheddle|status/604430151309365249|[[Jennifer Heddle]]|quote=It is approved but its canon status is grey area. We gave them leeway for cultural interpretation|archivefile=File:LINE Webtoon Tweet.png}}</ref>
