"'''Fuel for the Fire'''" ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]] title "'''Gefährlicher Treibstoff'''") is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] written by [[Alec Worley]], drawn by [[Cosmo White]] and edited by [[Gunther Nickel]]. The story was published in the 4th issue of the [[Wikipedia:Germany|German]] [[Star Wars Resistance Animation|''Star Wars Resistance Animation''-Magazine]], released on [[November 20]], [[2019]].

==Plot summary==
{{Plot|comic}}

==Continuity==
The comic partially adapts the [[Fuel for the Fire|episode of the same name]] of the television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''.
