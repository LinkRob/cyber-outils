"'''Across the Burning Sands of Tatooine'''" is a short story by [[Grant S. Boucher]] originally published in the [[West End Games]] sourcebook ''[[Galaxy Guide 1: A New Hope]]''. It was later included in ''[[The Movie Trilogy Sourcebook]]''.

==Plot summary==
In [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[Historian/Legends|historian]] [[Voren Na'al/Legends|Voren Na'al's]] personal account of his arrival on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] to research the events that occurred there leading up to the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]], he encounters a group of [[Jawa/Legends|Jawas]] at the [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina/Legends|Mos Eisley Cantina]] who sell him [[R5-D4/Legends|R5-D4]] and a [[Power droid/Legends|power droid]]. Initially upset upon discovering the R5 [[Droid/Legends|droid's]] faulty motivator, he is pleased to learn that they were present for the sale of [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] to [[Owen Lars/Legends|Owen Lars]] and [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]]. After retrieving the information he needed from the droids, Na'al gave the power droid to a [[Moisture farmer/Legends|moisture farmer]] and sold R5-D4 to the [[Imperial Prefect]]'s assistant administrator. He intends for the R5 droid&mdash;which he nicknames Red&mdash;to serve as a spy for the Alliance, a fact known only to [[Momaw Nadon/Legends|Momaw Nadon]].
