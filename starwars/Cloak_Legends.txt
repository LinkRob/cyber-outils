A '''cloak''' was a type of hooded [[Clothing/Legends|apparel]]. [[Galactic Emperor/Legends|Galactic Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] wore a simple hooded cloak to hide [[Gender/Legends|his]] face. Cloaks could be worn for fashion ([[Alderaanian cloak]]) or as a status-symbol ([[Command cloak]]). Most notably they were used for protection against the elements ([[All-Temperature Cloak]]) or other people ([[Stun cloak]]). Specially imbued cloaks could even protect the user from [[Force-sensitive/Legends|force-wielders]], such include the [[Yuuzhan Vong]] [[Cloak of Nuun]] and the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] [[Cloak of Hate]].
