The '''Corellian Engineering Corporation''', abbreviated '''CEC''', was a [[Corellia]]n [[starship]] manufacturer. Its products included several models of [[freighter]]s, such as the [[YV-666 light freighter]],<ref name="Ezra's Gamble">''[[Ezra's Gamble]]''</ref> the [[YT-1300 light freighter]],<ref name="CrossICS TFA">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> the [[VCX-100 light freighter]],<ref name="Rebels Guide" /> the [[VCX-150 freighter]],<ref name="Battlefront: Twilight Company">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> and the [[Baleen-class heavy freighter|''Baleen''-class heavy freighter]].<ref name="TVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

Other CEC ships of note included the [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]]<ref name="Rebels Guide" /> and its [[C-ROC Gozanti-class light cruiser|C-ROC variant]],<ref name="DB Broken Horn">{{Databank|broken-horn|Broken Horn}}</ref> the [[CR90 corvette]],<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> the [[interstellar trash hauler]],<ref name="Beware the Power of the Dark Side">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> the [[Consular-class light cruiser|''Consular''-class space cruiser]], and the [[G9 Rigger-class light freighter|G9 ''Rigger''-class light freighter]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

Aside from ships, CEC also built [[masking transponder]]s,<ref name="DB Transponders">{{Databank|transponders|Transponders}}</ref> weapons systems like the [[AG-2G quad laser cannon]],<ref name="IncrediBuilds">''[[Star Wars: Millennium Falcon Deluxe Book and Model Set]]''</ref> [[escape pod]]s such as the [[CEC Class-1 escape pod|Model CEC Class-1]],<ref name="AbEverything" /> and [[ion engine]]s like the [[CEC-3900hh ion engine|3900hh]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> or the [[CEC-5900hh ion engine|5900hh]].<ref name="Mando Handbook">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Handbook]]''</ref>

[[Ovan]], the [[Parent|father]] of [[Han Solo]] worked for CEC, building the YT-1300s.<ref name="Han Chewie 1" />

During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Augustus Graves]] served as [[Chief Executive Officer|CEO]] of the company.<ref name="Han Chewie 1" />

By the time of the [[Battle of Scarif]], CEC had been [[Imperialization|imperialized]] by the Galactic Empire.<ref name="R1VD" /> Later, the company would be taken over by the [[First Order]].<ref name="Resistance Reborn">''[[Resistance Reborn]]''</ref>
