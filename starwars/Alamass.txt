'''Alamass''' was a distant and [[Temperature|frigid]] [[planet]] that was covered in [[Water|icy]] tundra and turbulent floes of [[ammonia]]<ref name="The Droid with a Heart" /> within the [[Alamass system]], a [[star system]] in the [[Calaron sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="SWGalaxyMap" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Republic]] operated a [[Republic stronghold|stronghold]] on the planet, which was [[Battle of Alamass|attacked]] by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. According to legend, the [[Unidentified T-series tactical droid (Alamass)|tactical droid]] who [[General]] [[Grievous]] placed in charge of the attack calculated that their general's plan would cause the unnecessary loss of many [[battle droid]]s due to the planet's dangerous terrain. As a result, the [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|tactical droid]], refusing to let any more [[droid]]s get destroyed for no reason, disobeyed its orders, sending their [[Separatist Droid Army|forces]] into a safer region. Because of the droids' location, the Republic anticipated the attack and were ready to defend their stronghold. The tactical droid's modified plans caused the Separatists to lose the battle, but droid losses had been minimized.<ref name="The Droid with a Heart" />
