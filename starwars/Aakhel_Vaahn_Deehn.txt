'''Aakhel Vaahn Deehn''' was a [[Scientist/Legends|scientist]] who worked for [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. He developed a process capable of inducing controlled mutations on any [[Species/Legends|organism]]. He carried out his mutation experiments in his laboratory on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Ethark]], with the goal of creating the perfect warrior.<ref name="LHI" />
