A '''black dwarf''' was a class of dark, dead [[dwarf star]].<ref name="No Cave" /> When [[white dwarf]]s became very cold because of extreme age, they no longer gave off detectable light or [[Temperature|heat]], appearing [[Color|black]]. This process took billions of [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="BMF 34" />

The [[exogorth]] [[Sy-O]] was born near [[Unidentified black dwarf|one]] such star with an [[iron]] core, and fed off of the energy that it emitted.<ref name="No Cave">{{StoryCite|story=This Is No Cave|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>
