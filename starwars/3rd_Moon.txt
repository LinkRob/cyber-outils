The '''3rd Moon''' was a terrestrial [[moon]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Aphra 21" /> that was inhabited by various [[species]].<ref name="Aphra 20" /> Around [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{Aphra2016Date|20}}</ref> the [[mercenary]] [[Sana Starros]] played a game of [[dejarik]] with a [[Unidentified Twi'lek (3rd Moon)|Twi'lek]] in a crowded room on the moon during which the pair eventually drew [[blaster]]s on each other. At the same [[time]],<ref name="Aphra 20" /> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[general]] [[Hera Syndulla]] reached out to Starros<ref name="Aphra 21" /> via [[holocomm]].<ref name="Aphra 20" />

While two [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s confronted a [[Rodian]] outside the room, an [[Unidentified alien (3rd Moon)|alien]] took the holocomm to Starros while<ref name="Aphra 20" /> she<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> was at a standoff with the [[Twi'lek]].<ref name="Aphra 20" /> The mercenary [[Death|killed]] her opponent as well as other individuals in the room before answering the comm. During the ensuing conversation, Syndulla recruited the mercenary to bring the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] to the Alliance. While speaking with the general, Starros also stole a small bag from the Twi'lek's body. She then made her way to the [[Senex-Juvex]]<ref name="Aphra 21" /> [[sector]]s,<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> where Aphra was aboard the mobile [[wreckage-prison]] [[Accresker Jail]].<ref name="Aphra 21" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 3rd Moon first appeared in the [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 20|twentieth issue]] of the [[2016]] [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series,<ref name="Aphra 20" /> published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[May 23]], [[2018]].<ref name="Marvel Aphra 20">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/67557/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2016_20|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016) #20}}</ref> The moon was identified in the [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 21|following issue]], released on [[June 27]] of the same year.<ref name="Marvel Aphra 21">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/69595/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2016_21|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016) #21}}</ref> Both issues were written by [[Simon Spurrier|Si Spurrier]] and illustrated by [[Kev Walker]].<ref name="Aphra 21" /><ref name="Aphra 20" />
