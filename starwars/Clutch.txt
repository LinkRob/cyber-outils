'''Clutch''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Trandoshan]] [[big game hunter]] who was a member of a [[Garnac's hunting guild|hunting guild]] that hunted down [[Sentience|sentient]] prey on the [[moon]] of [[Wasskah]]. During the [[Standard year|year]] [[20 BBY]], Clutch served as a [[pilot]] for the [[guild]]'s [[HCT-2001 Dragonboat-class Reugeot 905 freighter|Trandoshan prisoner ship]]. During an expedition to find new prey, Clutch and his co-pilot [[Goron]] captured the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]].

However, while attempting to drop the prisoner off on an [[Island Four|island]], the previously captured [[Jedi]] [[padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], along with [[O-Mer|two]] [[Jinx|other]] captured Jedi, attacked the Trandoshan [[freighter]] in a bid to get off of the island. Tano managed to break into the [[cockpit]] and dueled Clutch, with the latter's [[shotgun]] damaging the [[cockpit]]'s controls and overloading the [[computer]] systems. The damage caused the ship to nosedive into the island's [[beach]], with Clutch and Goron both dying in the incident.

==Biography==
===Garnac's blood-sport===
{{Quote|The sun has risen. Let the hunt begin.|Garnac initiates a hunt|<ref name="Padawan Lost">{{TCW|Padawan Lost}}</ref>}}
[[File:GarnacsHuntingGuild-PL.png|thumb|left|250px|Clutch awaits a hunt on the guild's lodge.]]
Clutch was a [[Trandoshan]] [[Gender|male]] [[pilot]] who was a member of a [[Garnac's hunting guild|hunting guild]] led by the Trandoshan [[Garnac]].<ref name="Wookiee Hunt" /> The [[guild]], populated solely by other Trandoshans, captured prey from across [[the galaxy]]&mdash;namely [[Sentience|sentient]] individuals, although not exclusively&mdash;before bringing it back to the [[moon]] of [[Wasskah]].<ref name="Padawan Lost Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/padawan-lost-episode-guide|text="Padawan Lost" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> Prey would be dumped off on the moon and then summarily [[Hunting|hunted]] by the Trandoshans for sport.<ref name="Padawan Lost" /> Occasionally, Clutch would pilot the guild's [[HCT-2001 Dragonboat-class Reugeot 905 freighter|Trandoshan prisoner ship]] and travel to capture the guild's targets.<ref name="Wookiee Hunt" />

During the [[Standard year|year]] [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Garnac's son [[Dar]] was preparing to enter the guild as an official member. Clutch participated in a hunting outing on the day of Dar's initiation ceremony, and later attended the ceremony itself, where Clutch cheered on Dar as the young hunter was initiated into the guild. However, Dar was killed the very next day by the captured [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] during his first hunt.<ref name="Padawan Lost" />

===Duel with a Jedi===
{{Quote|Ahh, it's the younglings that did this. They're getting bold.''"<br />"''What about survivors?''"<br />"''Both of our men were killed, and there was only one prisoner, a Wookiee. And he's missing.|Lo-Taren reports the deaths of Clutch and Goron to Garnac|<ref name="Wookiee Hunt" />}}
At some point, Clutch and a co-pilot, [[Goron]], took the Reugot freighter and departed Wasskah to search for more prey. They managed to capture the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] during their outing, and later returned to Wasskah with their single prisoner. By this point, Tano had teamed up with the stranded Jedi [[youngling]]s [[O-Mer]] and [[Jinx]]. Wishing to escape the moon at any cost, the Jedi decided to attack the freighter head on and overwhelm its crew.<ref name="Wookiee Hunt" />

While Clutch steered the ship towards Island Four, Tano appeared on the ship's windshield. Surprised, Clutch sent Goron after her. However, upon walking onto the ship's long hull, Goron was distracted by O-Mer and Jinx, who had also climbed onto the ship. With Goron occupied, Tano snuck inside the ship. However, Clutch was aware of the padawan, and pretended to be oblivious to her presence as she approached him from behind. Finally, Clutch spun around in his pilot seat, [[shotgun]] in hand, and began opening fire on the Jedi. Tano either evaded the shots or used [[the Force]] to chuck off Clutch's killing aim, which caused the [[cockpit]]'s controls and computer panels to sustain substantial damage. The destruction caused the ship's power to flucuate, and the ship began to free-fall to the ground.<ref name="Wookiee Hunt" />

[[File:ClutchDead_WH.png|thumb|right|250px|Clutch is killed by Ahsoka Tano]]

Clutch tried to attack Tano again, but the Jedi [[Force push|telekinetically pushed]] Clutch through the ship's windshield, killing the Trandoshan pilot. Meanwhile, Goron had fallen from the ship to his own death and the ship crashed into Island Four's beach soon after. The Jedi recovered Chewbacca, who had survived the crash, and escaped back into the hunting island's jungle. Clutch and Goron were found the next day by the Trandoshans, and Clutch's body was pulled from the wreckage by the hunters [[Krix]] and [[Ratter]]. Clutch's death led to [[Lo-Taren]] having conclusions that the Jedi had grown more bold in their efforts to escape.<ref name="Wookiee Hunt" />

Lo-Taren's hunch proved correct when the Jedi, alongside Wookiee reinforcements, [[Battle on Wasskah|attacked and dismantled]] the hunting guild entirely.<ref name="Wookiee Hunt" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Dar! Dar! Dar!|Clutch and the other hunters cheer Dar's name|<ref name="Wookiee Hunt" />}}
[[File:Clutch_celebrating.png|thumb|left|250px|Clutch endorsing the beginning of a hunt]]
Clutch was a stocky, [[Color|golden]]-scaled Trandoshan with orange [[eye]]s who was excited by Garnac opening the announcement of the new hunt. When preparing for what would be his<ref name="Padawan Lost" /> final<ref name="Wookiee Hunt" /> hunt on Wasskah, Clutch cheered by hooting and thrusting his arms in the air as he grabbed his weapon and proceeded to Wasskah's surface. Clutch was happily among his peers during Dar's initiation ceremony and excitedly chanted the young hunter's name along with the other Trandoshans.<ref name="Padawan Lost" />

During the Jedi attack of the Reugot freighter it was Clutch who took charge and gave the orders when it came to repelling the attack. In addition, Clutch exhibited patience and a knack for ambush when he pretended to ignore Tano's sneak-attack before spinning around in his pilot seat, with his shotgun readily in hand in an attempt to shoot the Jedi. However, he proved unable to best Tano, and was instead killed by her. Clutch referred to the Jedi as "whelps."<ref name="Wookiee Hunt" />

==Equipment==
As one of the guild's pilots, Clutch commanded their Trandoshan prisoner ship, identifiable as guild property due to the custom "teeth" paint-job on the ship's nose, during missions to capture prey.<ref name="Wookiee Hunt" /> Going through horrific combats, Clutch utilized either a [[blaster]]<ref name="Padawan Lost" /> or a shotgun.<ref name="Wookiee Hunt" />

Clutch wore gray [[Clothing|fatigues]] during Garnac's Jedi hunt, which included a gray jacket that featured yellow geometric tactical lining of hinting shapes and sleeves. A small pouch was strapped to his left [[Limb|arm]], and he wore an electronically powered [[gauntlet]] around his other wrist.<ref name="Padawan Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|There's Clutch&mdash;clutch of eggs.|''The Clone Wars'' series lead [[Dave Filoni]] mentions the source of Clutch's name|<ref name="Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Three">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Three|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season Three]]</ref>}}
[[File:Clutch_Concept.png|thumb|right|250px|Conceptual artwork of Clutch for "Padawan Lost"]]
Clutch was one of many Trandoshan characters created for the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episodes "[[Padawan Lost]]" and "[[Wookiee Hunt]]," both of which aired as a part of the show's [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|season three]] on [[April 1]], [[2011]].<ref name="Padawan Lost Ep Guide" /><ref name="Wookiee Hunt Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/wookiee-hunt-episode-guide|text="Wookiee Hunt" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> Clutch's voice was provided by [[Matt Lanter]], who also voiced series regular [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="Wookiee Hunt" />

All of the Trandoshans that appeared in the Wasskah story arc, including Clutch, were designed by concept artist [[Pat Presley]]. Clutch's stocky appearance was designed in a deliberate effort to diversify the physical builds of the Trandoshan characters.<ref name="Season 3">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Three|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season Three]] &mdash; Behind the Story: "Chewbacca Returns"</ref> Clutch's name is taken from the term for the terminology for a group of eggs, which is a "clutch."<ref name="Season 3-Name">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Three|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season Three]] &mdash; Behind the Story: "Chewbacca Returns" Easter Egg</ref>
