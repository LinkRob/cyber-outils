'''Beckett's gang''',<ref name="Insider 180" /> also known as '''Tobias Beckett's gang''',<ref name="Chewbacca">{{Databank|chewbacca|Chewbacca}}</ref> '''Tobias Beckett's crew''',<ref name="Val 2021 Base">{{SWCT|set=2021 Base|cardname=Val}}</ref> '''Beckett's crew''',<ref name="Darth Vader 26">{{DarthVaderCite|26|Galactic Villains}}</ref> or '''Tobias Beckett's group''',<ref name="IT">{{EncyclopediaCite|Imperial Troops}}</ref> was a gang led by the criminal [[Tobias Beckett]] some [[time]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Insider 180" /> Composed of specialized scoundrels, Beckett assembled the team to carry out profitable but risky heists.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|tobias-beckett|Tobias Beckett}}</ref> [[Rio Durant]] was the [[pilot]]<ref name="Rio Durant 2021 Base">{{SWCT|set=2021 Base|cardname=Rio Durant}}</ref> and [[Val]] was handy with explosives.<ref name="Gear & Gadgets">{{SWCT|set=Solo: A Star Wars Story - Gear & Gadgets|cardname=Val's Bomb & Detonator Kit}}</ref> The [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] were offered a place in the crew.<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
Tobias Beckett's crew was first revealed in the very first look at the upcoming ''[[Star Wars Anthology Series]]'' film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Big Game">{{SWYouTube|9Szts88zY4o|Solo: A Star Wars Story "Big Game" TV Spot (:45)|sw_url=video/big-game-tv-spot-solo-a-star-wars-story}}</ref> the "Big Game" TV spot released during [[Wikipedia:Super Bowl LII|Super Bowl LII]] on [[February 4]], [[2018]].<ref name="Super Bowl">{{SW|url=news/punch-it-solo-a-star-wars-story-tv-spot-debuts|text=Punch It: ''Solo: A Star Wars Story'' TV Spot Debuts}}</ref>

Early story outlines for ''Solo'' included an additional member of the gang, a "squid-monkey" named Zapf, who was eventually amalgamated into Rio Durant's character.<ref name="Art of Solo">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
