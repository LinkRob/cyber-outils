'''Amrashad''' was a [[general]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Alliance Military|military]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="BTC" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Mid Rim Retreat]], Amrashad [[Death|died]] in the midst of a final effort to escort the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] from the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]. [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]] of the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] cited the general's death and other recent losses while attempting to persuade his command staff to use intelligence from the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] turncoat [[Everi Chalis]], claiming that the Alliance needed the victories that Chalis's information could provide.<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Amrashad was mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="BTC" />
