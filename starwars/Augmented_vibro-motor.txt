'''Augmented vibro-motors''' were an attachment used to modify [[Vibro-weapon|vibro weapons]]. Modifying vibro [[weapon]]s with vibro-motors could make the weapon more powerful, capable of cutting through [[armor]]ed plating with ease. However, this augmentation also resulted in the weapon being more difficult to handle. Vibro-motors cost 500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Rise" />

Vibro-motors that were installed alongside a [[gene-lock]]&mdash;which kept weapons from activating without their owner's use by reading DNA&mdash;would be disabled. They could also be utilized in tandem with an [["Accu-Strike" integrated targeting computer]] in order to improve a user's accuracy. Usage of vibro-motors with the [[targeting computer]]s involved installing them into the [[gauntlet]]s or [[glove]]s of the user, then connecting them to the [[helmet]] system of the armor.<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
Augmented vibro-motors were introduced in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" />
