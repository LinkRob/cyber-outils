'''Anra''' was a member of the staff of [[Senator]] [[Lud Marroi]], who represented the [[Cerberon system]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Senate]].<ref name="MOF" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate}}</ref> Senator [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] proposed the [[Imperial Rebirth Act]], which would limit the powers of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. After the bill was brought to the floor in the [[Galactic Senate Chamber|Senate chamber]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], Marroi asked his [[Chief of Staff|chief of staff]] [[Spigh]] what would happen if he voted to support the bill. Spigh responded by glibly asking if Marroi meant whether his constituents would [[Death|kill]] him before setting him on [[fire]], then added that he could have Anra do a flash poll.<ref name="MOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Anra was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MOF" />
