A '''cooling supply unit''' was an appliance used to cool objects such as [[foodstuff]]. The [[Corellian]] [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' had cooling supply units for guests to use in the [[passenger cabin]]s.<ref name="SWGS">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
"Cooling supply unit" was the [[in-universe]] name for mini-fridges in the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] attraction at the Walt Disney World Resort.<ref name="SWGS" />
