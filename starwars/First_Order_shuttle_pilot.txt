'''First Order shuttle pilots''' were an elite [[unit]] of [[pilot]]s of the [[First Order]]'s<ref name="Ultimate New">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Transport Corps]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> tasked with piloting the [[shuttle]]s transporting high-ranking [[officer]]s, dignitaries, and wealthy First Order families.<ref name="Ultimate New" /> One such pilot was [[Jober Tavson]] who piloted [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]'s [[Kylo Ren's command shuttle|personal]] [[Upsilon-class command shuttle|''Upsilon''-class command shuttle]] during the [[Battle of Crait]].<ref name="TLJVD" />
