The '''four great families''' were a group of families in charge of the [[Land & Sky Corporation]]'s [[Land & Sky Corporation board of directors|board of directors]]. The families included the [[Nan family]], [[Xia family]], [[Chen family]], and [[Ma family]]. 

==History==
When the [[Aakaash system]] was first colonized there was only one great family: the Ma family. The other three eventually rose to prominence from among the vassal families of the Ma.<ref name="Vow" />
