The '''Cheunh script,''' sometimes called '''Chiss Script''',<ref name="Thrawn: Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref> was a [[writing]] system used to transcribe [[Cheunh]], the primary [[language]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> After joining the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], [[Lieutenant]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] paged through data listings written in Cheunh script aboard the [[Steadfast (Chiss heavy cruiser)|''Steadfast'']].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> Some fonts of Cheunh script were deliberately designed to be difficult to read upside down, such as from across a desk.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> The [[Pathfinder]] [[Qilori]] of Uandualon was able to read Cheunh well.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>
