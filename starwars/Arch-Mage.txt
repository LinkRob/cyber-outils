'''Arch-Mage''' was a position within the [[Acolytes of the Beyond]], a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Religion|cult]]. The [[human]] [[Yuan Howzell]] held the position during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. After Howell ordered members of the cult to retrieve a [[Ky Narec's compendium|compendium]] from the [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and the [[pilot]] [[Rynn Zenat]], one individual addressed her with the title.<ref name="Republic Under Siege 1" />
