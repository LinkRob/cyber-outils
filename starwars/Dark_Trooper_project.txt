The '''Dark Trooper project''',<ref name="Sideshow">{{IronStudiosCite|set=Art Scale Battle Diorama Series|packtype=1:10 Scale Statue|pack=Dark Trooper|link=collectibles/star-wars-dark-trooper-iron-studios-908873}}</ref> or the '''dark trooper program'''<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> was an [[Imperial Department of Military Research]]<ref name="SWCEUAE" /> program whose goal was to create<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> a force of [[soldier]]s known as "[[Dark Trooper]]s"<ref name="Chapter 16" /> for the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> It was originally launched as a way to supply [[human]]s with a new [[armor]].<ref name="SWCEUAE">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> The first form of the [[Dark Trooper (original)|Dark Trooper]] came from taking [[clone trooper]]s and providing them with [[cybernetics]].<ref name="Sideshow" />

The experimental [[Phase II Dark Trooper]] was [[Battle for Tatooine|evaluated]] by the [[Imperial Military]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> By the [[Phase-III Dark Trooper|third generation]] of the dark trooper, it was decided the [[pilot]] was the final weakness in the design. Thus, it was decided to make the dark trooper a type of [[battle droid]]. A [[platoon]] of third generation dark troopers was used by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]]'s [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]]<ref name="Chapter 16" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|12}}</ref> IDMR [[scientist]]s were stationed aboard Gideon's [[Gideon's light cruiser|light cruiser]] to maintain these dark troopers,<ref name="Chapter 12" /> which were held in [[Cryo-cycle stasis|cold storage]] due to their excessive power requirements.<ref name="Chapter 16" />

==Behind the scenes==
The dark trooper program was first mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[2018]] sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]] Game'' by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> It made its first appearance in "[[Chapter 12: The Siege]]," the twelfth episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.<ref name="Chapter 12" /> It was originally created for the [[1995]] [[Star Wars Legends|Star Wars ''Legends'']] video game ''[[Star Wars: Dark Forces]]''.<ref name="Dark Forces">''[[Star Wars: Dark Forces]]''</ref>

''Dawn of Rebellion'' references the dark trooper program as an Imperial program designed on creating Imperial battle droids, only for it to end in failure. The failure contributed to the [[Joint Chiefs of the Imperial Military|Joint Chiefs]] becoming disinterested in the use of combat [[droid]]s, with the notable exception of the [[KX-series security droid]].<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> However, most canon sources have framed the dark trooper program as starting with combat suits and only later shifting to create combat droids.<ref name="SWCEUAE" /><ref name="Scene to Stats Dark Troopers">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/scene-to-stat-dark-troopers|text=Scene to Stats: Dark Troopers|date=2023-01-10}}</ref>
