The '''seventh episode''' of the animated web series ''[[Grogu Cutest In The Galaxy]]'' released on [[March 4]], [[2025]]. It was posted by ''[[Star Wars]]'' Japan on [[Wikipedia:Instagram Reels|Instagram Reels]].<ref name="Instagram" />

{{Media-stub}}

==Plot summary==
[[Din Grogu|Grogu]], while controlling [[IG-12]], has the droid say, "Yes" multiple times and grabs a bucket filled with [[popcorn]]-like food to eat. [[Din Djarin]] says, "No," and takes the bucket from IG-12's hands. Frustrated, Grogu then has the [[droid]] take a [[meiloorun fruit]]. Din again says, "No," and tries to take the fruit away, but Grogu avoids him. In the ensuing brouhaha, IG-12's hands squeeze the meiloorun too tightly, and juices ooze out of it. The fruit then falls from IG's hands, to Grogu's disappointment.
