'''Argent Dawn''' was a sculpture in honor of [[Jedi]] [[Thraymus Argent]], found among [[Alderaan]]'s [[Eastwind Manor]] garden statues<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> by [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|69}}</ref> when [[Bail Prestor Organa]] decided to have it warehoused. Organa's great-uncle had commissioned the art piece upon Argent's departure after a [[Thraymus Argent's mission|mission]] the Jedi undertook to investigate feuds and [[assassin]]ation attempts that thwarted [[House Prestor]].<ref name="MoF" />

It was an abstract metal rendering of Thraymus Argent, with odd blocks and silver spokes, as well as a plaque underneath that read "Argent Dawn." Bail Organa liked the piece, evoking fond memories of his time as a child when Argent was stationed at the manor. He liked what the sculpture represented, and enjoyed showing it to guests. Organa imagined a potential situation in which the [[Governor-General]] of the sector questioned him about the piece, and pictured friends his daughter [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] playing in the garden and becoming curious about Argent Dawn.<ref name="MoF" />
