'''Adam Rex''' (born [[May 16]], [[1973]]) is an author and illustrator of children's literature. He is known for books of poetry such as ''Frankenstein Makes a Sandwich'' and ''Frankenstein Takes the Cake''.<ref name="adamrexofficial">{{OfficialSite|url=http://www.adamrex.com/adamrexbooks.html|archivedate=20070621091800}}</ref> His novel ''The True Meaning of Smekday'' was adapted by the animation studio Dreamworks as the CGI-animated feature film ''Home''.<ref name="home">{{WebCite|url=https://www.thesuburbanmom.com/2015/04/10/the-book-that-inspired-the-movie-home|text=The Book That Inspired The Movie Home|date=2015-04-10|archivedate=20160311171400}}</ref>

Rex is the author and illustrator of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' title ''[[Are You Scared, Darth Vader?]]'', which was published on [[July 3]], [[2018]].<ref name="Bibliothek">{{WebCite|url=https://jedi-bibliothek.de/datenbank/literatur/are-you-scared-darth-vader-9781484704974|text=Are You Scared, Darth Vader?|dateaccessed=2018-06-25|language=German|work=[https://jedi-bibliothek.de Jedi-Bibliothek]|archivedate=20180625220544}}</ref>
