The '''crimson jelly spire''' were a species of [[plant]] that [[Homeworld|originated]] from an [[Unidentified Planet (crimson jelly spire)|unidentified planet]]. It was able to withstand extreme cold and heat as well as punishing winds thanks to it's gelatinous exterior and durable core stem. They became an oddity for [[collector]]s which spread the crimson jelly spire across the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It grew on the planet [[Koboh]] where it could be found in colors like [[Naboo (people)|Nabooan]] Green, [[Felucian]] Yellow, [[Alderaanian]] Blue, and [[Dathomirian]] Red.<ref name="Survivor" />

{{GameMechanics|Canon=1|Star Wars Jedi: Survivor}}
Human [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] acquired and planted several crimson jelly spire seeds in the garden at [[Pyloon's Saloon]] in the [[Rambler's Reach Outpost]].<ref name="Survivor" />
