'''Bantha fodder''' ([[Huttese]]: "'''bantha poodoo'''") was a slang term for a person deemed to have no value beyond something for a [[bantha]] to feed on.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> It literally referred to [[Foodstuff|food]] for banthas, which was known for having a terrible smell<ref name="Monsters">''[[The Star Wars Book of Monsters, Ooze and Slime]]''</ref> and attracting [[stink-fly|stink-flies]].<ref name="Jedi Lost">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dokuu: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> The term was also used as a synonym for nonsense, e.g. "Enough bantha poodoo flowing out of [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|you]] to fill up this whole lake."<ref name="Star Wars 2020 40">[[Star Wars (2020) 40|''Star Wars'' (2020) 40]]</ref> The phrase "sack of bantha fodder" referred to a despicable person.<ref name="The Wynkahthu Job" />

==History==
===High Republic Era===
In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Valo Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> [[Haze Delik]] was on a mission for the [[Nihil]] [[scientist]] [[Niv Drendow Apruk]] to retrieve a [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] [[egg]]. Recognizing Apruk's significance to the Nihil, Delik sought to gain Apruk's favor, despite her belief that he "had the personality of bantha poodoo."<ref name="Escape from Valo">''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]''</ref>

In c. [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Wayseeker Date">{{WayseekerDate}}</ref> [[Nilsson Summach]], a [[spice]] addict and arms dealer, listened to his subordinates attempting to clarify their failure in executing a straightforward deal, but found it difficult to comprehend their absurd narrative. To endure the "load of bantha fodder" being presented by his subordinates, he opted to smoking a type of spice known as [[Lake Country Daydreams]], which induced a soothing haze. Later, as he spoke to a collection of business partners on [[Spira]], Summach found himself growing weary of the unending questions, believing that his associates were attempting to "baffle him with bantha fodder."<ref name="Wayseeker">''[[The Acolyte: Wayseeker]]''</ref>

===Twilight of the Republic===
In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Rael Averross]] explained to [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] that he had attempted to contact his former mentor, [[Count]] [[Dooku]], on the [[planet]] [[Serenno]], but did not receive a response. When Jinn inquired about this, Averross merely shrugged, explaining that Dooku had become a ruler and was likely preoccupied with "courtiers, bureaucracy, and all the other bantha poodoo" that Averross himself had been contending with for the past eight [[Standard year|year]]s on [[Pijal]].<ref name="Master & Apprentice">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref>

During a card game on the [[moon]] of [[Dawk]] with the [[Devaronian]] criminal [[Coir Cion]], [[Devi (moon of Dawk)|Devi]] heard Cion's boast that he would soon be a free man. He asserted that after obtaining his pardon, he would abandon his life of crime and live legitimately, no longer needing to evade the law. However, Devi expressed doubt regarding Cion's chances of receiving a pardon, referring to it as "bantha poodoo."<ref name="Darth Maul 3">[[Darth Maul – Black, White & Red 3|''Darth Maul – Black, White & Red'' 3]]</ref>

While on [[Asusto]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Lene Kostana]] observed that the entire planet was enveloped in the [[dark side of the Force]], noting that trash was attracted to the planet "like stink-flies to bantha fodder."<ref name="Jedi Lost" />

===Fall of the Republic===
During the year [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> there was a fierce competition between the [[Dug]] [[Sebulba]] and another [[Podracing|podracer pilot]], [[Anakin Skywalker]]. Just before the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] was about to start, the Dug couldn't resist one last taunt directed at young Skywalker, and the two exchanged insults in [[Huttese]]. The young boy called Sebulba a "[[sleemo]]," and Sebulba retaliated by calling Skywalker "bantha poodoo."<ref name="Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace">{{Film|I}}</ref>

In [[Theed]] on [[Naboo]], an [[Unidentified Gungan (Viiveenn's doll)|unnamed Gungan child]] on the street was terrified by [[Asajj Ventress]], who warned the young [[Gungan]] to "watch it, or you'll be bantha fodder."<ref name="Hyperspace Stories 5">[[Hyperspace Stories 5|''Hyperspace Stories'' 5]]</ref>

===Imperial Era===
In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Spectres]] joined forces with the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] and the [[crime lord]] [[Azmorigan]] in a [[mission to loot an Imperial cargo ship]]. Upon boarding the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]]' [[VCX-100 light freighter|ship]] ''[[VCX-100 light freighter|Ghost]]'', Azmorigan received a cold reception from the Spectres, particularly [[Hera Syndulla]], who remembered him as the "sack of bantha fodder" who tried to [[Slavery|buy]] her from "that sleemo" [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and make her his servant. Azmorigan responded that she was the feisty one who had hit him with a tray and claimed that he still technically "owned" her.<ref name="The Wynkahthu Job" />

During the [[hunt for Vader]]<ref name="Target Vader 4">[[Target Vader 4|''Target Vader'' 4]]</ref> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Dengar]] grew increasingly impatient as he received no reply from [[Gita|Urrr'k]], exclaiming, "I know you can hear me, you bantha poodoo."<ref name="Target Vader 4" />

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas Updated">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> [[Greedo]] watched as [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] accompanied [[Luke Skywalker]] into the [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina|Mos Eisley cantina]], where [[Wuher]] cautioned that the [[droid]]s must remain outside. Greedo reflected on Skywalker's foolishness, believing he would be lucky to make it through an entire [[Alcohol|drink]] in the [[cantina]] or to live for another year, labeling him as "untried bantha fodder."<ref name="The Luckless Rodian">{{StoryCite|story=The Luckless Rodian|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>

[[3 ABY|Three years later]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Zev Senesca]] was among the rebels stationed at [[Echo Base]] on the [[Water|ice]]-planet of [[Hoth]]. Senesca observed that some the Rebels attempted to find solace in their surroundings on Hoth by extolling the natural beauty of the region, highlighting the magnificence of the expansive ice plains and glaciers. Nevertheless, he regarded such sentiments as  "a load of bantha fodder" and would have willingly exchanged all the natural splendor in [[the galaxy]] for a [[toilet]] that did not subject one to freezing temperatures while seated.<ref name="Rogue Two">{{StoryCite|story=Rogue Two (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>

In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> following his capture by the ruthless gangster [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]], [[Han Solo]] was presented to Jabba for judgement. Solo attempted to beg for his life, but Jabba showed no interest. He declared that Solo may have been a skilled [[smuggler]] in the past, but now he was nothing more than "bantha poodoo."<ref name="Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

===Rise of the First Order===
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date Game">{{SW|url=news/4-reasons-canto-bight-is-an-essential-read|text=4 Reasons ''Canto Bight'' Is an Essential Read}} states that [[Canto Bight (novella collection)|''Canto Bight'']] takes place a few [[Standard day|days]] prior to {{Film|VIII}}, and ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''The Last Jedi'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> after [[Kedpin Shoklop]] arrived at the planet [[Cantonica]], where his prize was a two week all expenses paid vacation to [[Canto Bight]], he was stopped by [[Ohlos]], an officer at the [[Custom House of Cantonica]], for lacking a sponsorship chip. However, Ohlos' fellow officer [[Lorala]] opted to let Shoklop go due to the significant amount of data work involved in keeping him detained. Ohlos decided to turn a blind eye to this, but warned his colleague that if she got caught skipping forms by their superiors, she was the one who was "bantha fodder," as he planned to feign ignorance regarding this matter.<ref name="Rules of the Game">{{StoryCite|story=Rules of the Game|book=Canto Bight (novella collection)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A common misconception is that "poodoo" refers to a [[bantha]]'s feces.<ref name="Blog">{{Blog|abelgpena|5|A Mouth Like An Intergalactic Princess: Cussing In A Galaxy Far, Far Away}}</ref> However, despite referring to fodder,<ref name="Monsters" /> "bantha poodoo" as a synonym for nonsense<ref name="Star Wars 2020 40" /> is similar to the real-world noun "bullshit." The phrase "sack of bantha fodder," which refers to a despicable person,<ref name="The Wynkahthu Job" /> is similar to the real-world noun "dirtbag,"<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/dirtbag|text=dirtbag|quote=an unpleasant person who has done something dishonest or unacceptable: ''He's a cheating, lying dirtbag!'' a person who is dirty and untidy: ''I saw him dressed like a dirtbag.''|work=[[Wikipedia:Cambridge University Press and Assessment|''Cambridge Dictionary'']] |archivedate=20250114234209}}</ref> and the vulgar slang phrase "piece of shit."<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://idioms.thefreedictionary.com/piece+of+shit|text=piece of shit|quote=1. ''rude slang'' A derisive term for something that one considers utterly worthless or inferior. (...) 2. ''rude slang'' A derisive term for someone whom one considers contemptible or deplorable.|work=[[Wikipedia:The Free Dictionary|''The Free Dictionary'']]|archivedate=20250105211257}}</ref>
