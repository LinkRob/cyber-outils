The '''Coyote cargo ship''' was a model of [[freighter]]. It had an appearance similar to that of an animal squatting on its haunches. The [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] owned [[H-dash-five-one-eight-zero-two|one]] such [[Starship|vessel]] at his business [[Ohnaka Transport Solutions]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]].<ref name="RtaSP" />
