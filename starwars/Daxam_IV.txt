'''Daxam IV''',<ref name="Bloodline" /> also referred to simply as '''Daxam''',<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> was a cold, [[desert]] [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], at the edge of the binary [[star system]]<ref name="Bloodline" /> of the [[Daxam system|same name]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was connected by [[hyperspace route]] to [[Rior]] and [[Azzameen Station]],<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> and was also near the planet [[Pamarthe]]. Consequently, many [[Pamarthen]] [[pilot]]s were recruited to work there.<ref name="Bloodline" />

By [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Timeframe">{{Twitter|DelReyStarWars|status/652893780236988420|[[Del Rey]]|quote=So excited to have @claudiagray writing a #StarWars novel with us. SW: New Republic: Bloodline coming 2016. Set 6 years before TFA.|archivefile=File:New Republic Bloodline Twitter Post.jpg}} establishes that the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']] take place six [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|VII}}. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], so ''Bloodline'' must take place in [[28 ABY]].</ref> Daxam IV had joined the [[New Republic]]. Despite the planet's self-reliance and limited off-planet commerce, it was aligned with the [[Centrists]], which favored a stronger galactic government and idealized the old [[Galactic Empire]]. During this time, the world was also home to the [[Amaxine warriors (New Republic Era)|Amaxines]], a paramilitary and terrorist group that was secretly aligned with the [[First Order]].<ref name="Bloodline" />
