"'''Almas, Sweet Almas: The Kaluthin Are Always Greener'''" is an adventure from the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign that was written by [[Adam Burns]] and released in [[March]] [[2002]]. It was the first adventure in the [[Almas, Sweet Almas trilogy]].

==Plot summary==
The heroes saved [[Welrin Maduk]], a [[Duros/Legends|Duros]] trader, after he was attacked on [[Cularin/Legends|Cularin]] by some [[Dark Jedi/Legends|Dark Jedi]]. They then traveled with him to [[Almas]], where they discover that someone is trying to kill off the [[kaluthin]] and so make Almas unable to sustain life. However, they thwart the plot and confront the [[Gungan/Legends|Gungan]] Dark Jedi [[Kosa-Yin Hadu]].
