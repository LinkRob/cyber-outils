'''Blue-milk pancakes''' were a type of [[Color|blue]]-colored [[Pancake/LEGO|pancake]] made with [[blue milk]]. The [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|R0-GR]] made a batch of blue-milk pancakes for his friend [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]], but she lied to the [[droid]] to get out of eating them. R0-GR also made pancakes for [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Major]] [[Bren Derlin]] after the [[Battle droid|droid]] and a family of [[scavenger]]s, the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]], [[Rescue of Bren Derlin|rescued]] him.

==Description==
A blue-milk pancake was a type of [[Color|blue]]-colored [[Pancake/LEGO|pancake]] made with [[blue milk]],<ref name="The Freemaker Adventures" /> a rich [[milk]] produced by [[Gender|female]] [[bantha]]s.<ref name="Absolutely Everything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> The pancake was cut into circular slices and had bubbles on the top of it.<ref name="The Freemaker Adventures" />

==History==

[[File:DerlinEatingBMPancakes.png|thumb|right|200px|Major Bren Derlin eats a blue-milk pancake aboard the ''StarScavenger''.]]
A blue-milk pancake was made by [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|R0-GR]] for his friend [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]], but she did not like the pancake and lied about being allergic to blue milk to avoid having to eat the [[Foodstuff|food]] R0-GR had made.<ref name="The Freemaker Adventures" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date Note">According to {{SW|url=news/lego-star-wars-the-freemaker-adventures-season-2-coming-this-summer|text=''LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures'' Season 2 Coming This Summer}}, the events of ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' take place between the events of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' establishes take place in [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively. In "[[Return of the Kyber Saber]]," it is the twenty-third anniversary of the [[proclamation of the New Order]], which ''Galactic Atlas'' establishes took place in [[19 BBY]]. "Return of the Kyber Saber" must therefore be set in 4 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]], and "[[The Storms of Taul]]," which takes place after "Return of the Kyber Saber," must be set in the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] had absconded with the ''[[StarScavenger]]'', a [[starship]] owned by R0-GR and a family of [[scavenger]]s known as the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]]. R0-GR encountered Ohnaka and questioned him regarding his presence on the ship, so the pirate explained that the Freemakers had sent him on a [[blue milk run]].<ref name="The Freemaker Adventures" />

Recalling his friend Kordi being allergic to his blue-milk pancakes, R0-GR realized Ohnaka was lying and was in fact stealing the ship. However, R0-GR then remembered that Kordi had drank [[blue milkshake]]s and realized that she had been lying to him in order to avoid eating his batch of pancakes. Later, after the Freemakers [[Rescue of Bren Derlin|rescued]] [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Major]] [[Bren Derlin]], R0-GR made blue-milk pancakes for him. Derlin exclaimed his joy for the food, stating his preference of the pancakes over the [[Galactic Empire]]'s freeze-dried food. R0-GR then expressed his appreciation for Derlin's comments on the pancakes.<ref name="The Freemaker Adventures" />
