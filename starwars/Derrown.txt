'''Derrown''', also known as "'''the Exterminator'''" because of the intense electric fields his body produced, was a [[Gender|male]] [[Parwan]] [[bounty hunter]] active during the [[Clone Wars]].

In [[20 BBY]], Derrown was brought to [[Serenno]], along with ten other bounty hunters including [[Cad Bane]], [[Embo]], as well as [[Rako Hardeen]], who was in reality [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in disguise. Derrown and the other bounty hunters, were sent [[Tournament on Serenno|into the Box]], to see who would earn the honor of partaking in a plot to [[Plan to kidnap Chancellor Palpatine|kidnap]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. After surviving the [[Box]], Derrown became part of [[Cad Bane's group]] and participated in the kidnapping, which was eventually foiled by Kenobi.

==Biography==
===The Box===
{{Quote|Does anyone know what this gasbag is saying?|Cad Bane|<ref name="The Box" />}}
A [[Gender|male]] [[Parwan]] [[bounty hunter]], Derrown was active during the [[Clone Wars]]; a [[The galaxy|galactic]] war between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="The Box" /> In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|417}}</ref> [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] invited Derrown to his [[Castle Serenno|palace]] on [[Serenno]], along with eleven other bounty hunters including [[Cad Bane]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] disguised as [[Rako Hardeen]] and [[Embo]] to participate in the [[Box]], a deadly, maze-like [[Tournament on Serenno|tournament]] designed by criminal mastermind [[Moralo Eval]]. The intentions of the tournament were to test the hunters' skills, with certain situations that could arise on the job.<ref name="The Box" /> The box contained a series of constantly shifting obstacle courses and traps that spanned five levels, testing both the bounty hunters' skills and intelligence.<ref name="SWCE" />

After entering the giant cubic structure, the first challenge was initiated. Derrown and the other bounty hunters survived the [[Dioxis]], a [[poison]]ous gas, as a result of the disguised Kenobi's quick plan to escape. The second challenge, was a room filled with spinning [[electrified laser blade]]s, where Derrown was able to once again escape quickly by using his Parwan abilities float to the hole in the roof and safely make his way out, following Bane and Kenobi.<ref name="The Box" />

[[File:DerrownPassesThruShield-TB.png|thumb|left|220px|Derrown passes through the ray shield]]
Once the bounty hunters arrived at the third challenge, Derrown realized he played a vital role to the survival of the other hunters. In this challenge, [[Ray shield|ray-shielded]] walls closed in on the hunters. The switch to deactivate the walls was located on the opposite side of another ray shield. Derrown injected himself with an [[Electrolytic serum|electrified serum]], provided by Eval, and was deadly to all the other bounty hunters. Derrown used the serum, enabling his electrified Parawan body to pass through the ray shield and turn off the other ray shields. As a result, the other surviving bounty hunters were able to pass through and enter the final challenge.<ref name="The Box" />

After the disguised Kenobi completed the fourth and final challenge, Derrown and was assigned the mission to [[Plan to kidnap Chancellor Palpatine|kidnap]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] on [[Naboo]], alongside Bane, Embo, Kenobi and [[Twazzi]]; the other survivors of the Box.<ref name="The Box" />

===Failed kidnapping of the Chancellor===
Upon arrival on Naboo, the bounty hunters infiltrated a [[hangar]]. Derrown [[Death|killed]] two of the [[Technician|mechanics]], strangling one with his [[Limb|tentacles]] and snapping the neck of another. Once the hunters secured the hangar, the bounty hunters were given their instructions by Bane. Bane assigned Derrown to be disguised as a [[Senate Commando]]. Derrown would breach the ray shield surrounding the [[Chancellor]] and destroy the main generator, allowing the rest of the hunters to capture the chancellor.<ref name="Crisis on Naboo" />

On the trail leading to the stage, Derrown was confronted by a [[SG-1984|Senate Commando]], who told the bounty hunter to remain alert. With nothing left to stop him, Derrown sneaked up to the [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]], deactivated it, and set it for self-destruct. The blast incapacitated Chancellor Palpatine and blew his cover. [[Anakin Skywalker]] rushed to attack Derrown, while the Parwan used his [[blaster pistol]]s to attack Skywalker. However, Skywalker cut Derrown's [[blaster]]s in two with [[Skywalker lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. In response, Derrown subdued Skywalker, sending an electric shock into the [[Jedi]] with his tentacles. As Derrown attempted to escape using his [[jetpack]], Kenobi who was still in disguise as Hardeen, used a [[sniper rifle]] to disable the jetpack, sending the Parwan spinning out of control, landing on the ground.<ref name="Crisis on Naboo" />

===Later missions===
Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="BrothersDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Brothers (episode)|Brothers]] to [[20 BBY]].</ref> Derrown was later seen at [[Plop Dribble's]] on [[Stobar]]. He hid behind a bar, while [[Savage Opress]] choked the [[Waiter|waitress]] [[Sassi]].<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref>

In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="EminenceDate">{{TCWcanondates|514}}</ref> Derrown was seen at [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's palace]] on [[Tatooine]] when he saw former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]], [[Pre Vizsla]] and Opress confront [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] and [[Gorga Desilijic Aarrpo]]. Derrown witnessed both [[Hutt]]s submitting to Maul's rule and join Maul's [[Shadow Collective]].<ref name="Eminence">{{TCW|Eminence (episode)}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Derrown was a male Parwan who was considered one of the most ruthless and best bounty hunters in the galaxy during the Clone Wars.<ref name="The Box" /> Derrown was 2.21 [[meter]]s (7 [[Foot|feet]], 3 inches) tall<ref name="Join the Battle" /> and he had brown skin.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> As a Parwan, Derrown's head was [[mushroom]]-shaped, he had skinny arms<ref name="Parwan">{{Databank|parwan|Parwan}}</ref> and he had 3 [[Color|gray]] [[eye]]s.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> His body was gas-filled, lighter-than-air body allowed him to float on air currents, by anchoring himself with four leg-like tentacles and two arms. Derrown's buoyancy let him reach heights and vantage points not easily accessible to standard density competitors, but he lacked the speed and control that came with his mass.<ref name="Derrown Databank"> {{Databank|derrown|Derrown}}</ref> Derrown's tendrils were capable of delivering deadly electric shocks,<ref name="Crisis on Naboo" /> which led to him receiving the grim nickname "the Exterminator."<ref name="The Box" />

==Skills and abilities==
Derrown was skilled in armed and unarmed combat. Derrown's Parwan physiology, coupled with his jetpack expertise,<ref name="Crisis on Naboo" /> enabled him to excel at aerial maneuvers.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Derrown's infiltration skills combined with his physiology also enabled him to play a key role as a member of [[Cad Bane's group]], as they broke into and seized control of a hangar in [[Theed]]. His skills in infiltration also enabled him to assist in disabling a [[Deflector shield|shield]] that protected the Supreme Chancellor during Bane's kidnapping attempt on Naboo.<ref name="Crisis on Naboo" />

==Equipment==
Derrown wore a jetpack that sported a [[shoulder-mounted armament]] blaster and wore special [[bandolier]] that insulate his [[bioelectricity]] from triggering the explosives and has [[holster]]s for his blaster pistols. Derrown was armed with unique [[blaster pistol]]s that squeeze the triggers with enough force to fire blaster bolts.<ref name="TheBox-Concept>{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-box-concept-art-gallery|text="The Box" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> He used the [[holographic disguise matrix]] during the [[Festival of Light]], posing as a Senate Commando.<ref name="Crisis on Naboo" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Development===
The character of Derrown first appeared in the episode "[[The Box]]" from the fourth season of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="The Box" /> released on [[February 3]], [[2012]].<ref name="Episode Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref> The character was voiced by sound designer [[Matthew Wood]],<ref name="The Box" /> who also provided the voices for Separatist [[battle droid]]s and [[General Grievous]] in the series.<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref>

Derrown's design was based on a piece of artwork done by [[Ron Cobb]] of a colorful and odd [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] alien from the [[1977]] original ''[[Star Wars]]'' film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] that ultimately went unused. Unlike Derrown, who is more like a "sentient gasbag", Cobb's artwork described the alien as a "tropical, ambulatory plant".<ref name="TheBox-Trivia">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-box-trivia-gallery|text="The Box" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> The design had also been used for the [[Revwien]] species.<ref name="Drawing Board">{{SW|url=news/back-from-the-drawing-board-recycled-star-wars-aliens|text=Back from the Drawing Board: Recycled ''Star Wars'' Aliens|int=Back from the Drawing Board|nolive=1}}</ref> Cobb's design was adapted by [[Darren Marshall]] and went through atleast eight iterations. The final model was created by Marshall under the title "Derrown Bounty Hunter Alien".<ref name="Marshall">{{WebCite|url=https://dmarshallart.com/portfolio/the-clone-wars-season-4/|text=The Clone Wars – Season 4|work=[[Darren Marshall]]'s website|archivedate=20260215170847}}</ref>
