'''Dewback-oil''' was a type of [[oil]] that that shared its name with [[dewback]]s, a type of [[reptile]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawDate">{{OutlawsDate|APF=1}}</ref> the [[scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] dismissed the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] as a "Dewback-oil salesman" for promising her a whole [[planet]] if she helped him escape from the [[brig]] in the [[Rokana Raiders]] [[Khepi Tomb|base]] located in the [[Sayma Nebula]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dewback-oil was mentioned in the "Brave the Unknown" quest of the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> The term "Dewback-oil salesman" is akin to the [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] term "snake-oil salesman" meaning a fraudulent or deceptive individual.<ref name="Snake-oil salesman">{{WebCite|url=https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/how-snake-oil-became-a-symbol-of-fraud-and-deception-180985300|text=How Snake Oil Became a Symbol of Fraud and Deception|work=[https://www.smithsonianmag.com/ Smithsonian Magazine]|archivedate=20250511204246}}</ref>
