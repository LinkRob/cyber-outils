'''Cardekkia cheese''' was a type of [[cheese]]<ref name="Resistance Reborn" /> that originated from the [[Cardekkia system]]<ref name="Resistance Reborn" /><ref name="Planetnamia" /> in the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> During his childhood, the [[Duros]] [[Shriv Suurgav]] once entered a nest of [[bluebarb wasp]]s. The resulting stings he received from the [[Species|creatures]] caused his face to swell up to the size of a Cardekkia cheese wheel.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cardekkia cheese was mentioned in ''[[Resistance Reborn]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Rebecca Roanhorse]].<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />
