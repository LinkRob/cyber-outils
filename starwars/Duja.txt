'''Duja''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who was a close friend and former [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of [[Naboo]]. She helped Amidala decipher an unintelligible communication from an angry ambassador (a potential diplomatic crisis) realizing the ambassador had not liked the way Padmé pronounced the name of a fellow ambassador. She was [[Death|murdered]] by [[assassin]]s sent by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Duke]] [[Solha]] while investigating the duke's [[Mokivj droid factory|droid factory]]. Amidala later recited a poem to remember her.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />
