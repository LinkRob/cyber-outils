'''Bororn''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Saber for Hire 1" /> in the [[Bororn system]] in the [[Rseik sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], located in grid square N-20 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was the site of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the location known as the [[Mudfields of Bororn]].<ref name="Saber for Hire 1" />

In [[230 BBY]], the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Deployment of the stormseeds|formed]] the [[Occlusion Zone]]&mdash;an area of space situated behind the [[Stormwall]] barrier that prevented [[hyperspace]] travel to and from the rest of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s territory.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Bororn was situated within the former region.<ref name="AcolyteVD" /> Around [[228 BBY]],<ref name="TrialsDate">{{TrialsoftheJediDate|39|the [[Mission to Planet X (Jedi Order)|Stormwall's fall]]}}</ref> the Nihil surrendered to the Republic following the [[Mission to Planet X (Jedi Order)|Stormwall's fall]], which dissolved the Occlusion Zone as a territory. Locations in the former Occlusion Zone were welcomed back into the Republic if they wished to re-join. Some of them, however, opted to retain their independence, while others remained under the control of individual Nihil [[warlord]]s.<ref name="TOTJ">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Bororn was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Pablo Hidalgo]]'s [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''.<ref name="AcolyteVD" />
