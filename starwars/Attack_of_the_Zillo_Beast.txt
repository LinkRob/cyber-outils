'''Attack of the Zillo Beast''' is a featurette included in the Blu-ray and DVD release of [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Two|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season Two]]. It covers the development of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series' [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|season two]] episodes "[[The Zillo Beast]]" and "[[The Zillo Beast Strikes Back]]."
