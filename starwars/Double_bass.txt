The '''double bass''' was a type of musical instrument consisting of four strings pulled along the length of a [[billfish]] beak and down over a skin stretched across a wooden body. The wooden body of the instrument was then supported on three legs made of [[bone]]. The double bass was played with a bow consisting of a piece of string tied between the two ends of a curved stick. The instrument was made by members of the [[Lanai]] [[species]] on their [[homeworld]] [[Ahch-To]], and the inhabitants of the [[Caretaker village]] on [[Temple Island]] owned one.<ref name="TLJVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
A double bass was created to be a part of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']];<ref name="TLJVD" /> however, the scene they appeared in was ultimately cut before the final release.<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> Despite this, the instrument still appeared in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> and released alongside the film on [[December 15]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/new-star-wars-the-last-jedi-books-and-more-revealed-at-sdcc-2017|text=New ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Books and More Revealed at SDCC 2017}}</ref>

The double bass (also called the contrabass) is a real instrument, but has a very different appearance.
