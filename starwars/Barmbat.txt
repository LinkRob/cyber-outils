'''Barmbat''' was a [[Nihil]] marauder during the [[High Republic Era]]. He and two other Nihil members traveled to the planet [[Vorzyd V]] to find and kill [[Arathab Fal]] and his partner. They find the hideout and killed the [[Pau'an]], who were an undercover agent of the Republic. Later Arathab found and killed them.<ref name="TOS 2" />
