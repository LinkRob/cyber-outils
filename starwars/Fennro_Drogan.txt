'''Fennro Drogan''' was a [[human]] [[Ghorman (people)|native]] [[Homeworld|of]] the [[planet]] [[Ghorman]] [[Reproduction|born]] with a [[limb]] difference that gave him a short right arm with only four fingers. He eventually joined the [[Ghorman Front]],<ref name="swgoh" /> a group formed against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Front">{{Andor|Ever Been to Ghorman?}}</ref> When the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] called for stalwart [[soldier]]s, Drogan was quick to lend his talents and joined as a member of [[Bitter Pill Company]] with pride. He became a respected [[captain]] who employed calculated strikes against enemy forces. As an expert tactician and [[technician]], he participated in multiple engagements, including the [[Battle of Endor]] with the [[Endor strike team]]<ref name="swgoh" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

Drogan carried a [[blaster pistol]] and a [[datapad]] that controlled a [[flame turret]]. He wore a [[bandolier]] over a camouflaged [[Clothing|outfit]] with a [[pathfinder helmet]]. A human, he had light skin and [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s.<ref name="swgoh" /> He had a sense of justice and passion to help those in need.<ref name="Road">{{EA|subdomain=forums.galaxy-of-heroes.starwars|url=discussion/264561/road-ahead-july-2023|text=Road Ahead - July 2023}}</ref> Per his service record, Drogan was qualified to pilot [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter]]s.<ref name="BWing">{{EA|subdomain=forums|url=blog/swgoh-game-info-hub-en/kit-reveal-b-wings-and-fries-chips/12687032|text=Kit Reveal: B-Wings and Fries (Chips)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|It's important that our players can see themselves in our game. People with disabilities belong in Star Wars, belong in games, and belong in our community.|''Galaxy of Heroes''{{'s}} kit reveal for Captain Drogan|<ref name="EA">{{EA|url=games/starwars/galaxy-of-heroes/news/kit-reveal-captain-drogan|text=Kit Reveal - Captain Drogan|nolive=1}}</ref>}}
Fennro Drogan was created for<ref name="Road" /> the [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'', by [[EA Capital Games]].<ref name="swgoh" /> He was first revealed in a road ahead post in [[Electronic Arts]]' forums on [[July 5]], [[2023]].<ref name="Road" /> His first name "Fennro" was first revealed in his ''Galaxy of Heroes'' kit reveal on [[August 29]] of that year.<ref name="Kit">{{EA|url=games/starwars/galaxy-of-heroes/news/kit-reveal-captain-drogan|text=Kit Reveal - Captain Drogan|nolive=1}}</ref> Drogan was later added to the game on [[August 29]], 2023, and became unlockable through his marquee event days later on [[August 31]].<ref name="EA" /> 

[[File:Star_Wars_Galaxy_of_Heroes_logo.png|thumb|right|300px|Fennro Drogan is an original character introduced in the 2015 mobile game ''Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes'']]
Inspiration for Drogan came from the development team's want to have their players feel seen, stating that people with disabilities belong in ''[[Star Wars]]'', belong in games, and belong in their community.<ref name="Kit" /> The team was excited to introduce Drogan to the game and looked forward to other ways they could broaden inclusive design in ''Galaxy of Heroes''.<ref name="Road" /> When asked if Drogan had been injured in battle, it was clarified that he was born with [[Wikipedia:Phocomelia|phocomelia]],<ref name="twitch">{{Twitch|video=1911875369|text=SWGOH KIT REVEAL - Captain Drogan|channel=capitalgames|name=CapitalGames_ExtraLife|date=2023-8-29|archivedate=20230830005209}}</ref> a real-world condition,<ref name="dict">{{WebCite|url=http://www.dictionary.com/browse/phocomelia|text=Phocomelia &#124; Define Phocomelia at Dictionary.com|work=[[Wikipedia:Dictionary.com|''Dictionary.com'']]|archivedate=20170507034722}}</ref> and noted that he did not have any [[Cybernetics|prosthetics]] or futuristic [[weapon]]s.<ref name="twitch" />

Drogan was created to be part of a squad led by Galactic Legend [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], but was also created with [[Mon Mothma]] and [[Saw Gerrera]] in mind as other possible leaders. He was added with an exclusive buff titled Keen Strategem that could spread to allies, granting +25% offense and potency. Drogan's unique ability was titled Second in Command.<ref name="Kit" /> As the rebel [[Hera Syndulla]] was already a crew member for a [[starship]] unit in-game, Drogan was later included as the crew member of the Rebel B-Wing unit in her place, which released in a marquee event on [[October 7]], [[2025]].<ref name="BWing" />
