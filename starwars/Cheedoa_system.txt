The '''Cheedoa system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was the origin point<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Cheedoan whiskey]]|system=Cheedoa system}}</ref> of [[Cheedoan whiskey]].<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> It was situated at the coordinates M-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cheedoa system was mentioned in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].
