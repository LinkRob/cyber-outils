{{Quote|For all their pronouncements of non-interference in others' activities, the Aristocras have a deep desire to know what those activities consist of.|Grand Admiral [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] to [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]|<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>}}
The '''Aristocra''' were midlevel members of the [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]]. They therefore formed part<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> of the [[Noble|ruling class]] of the [[Chiss]] [[species]] in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="Thrawn novel">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> The [[Aristocra (organization)|Aristocra]] was also an organization within the [[Chiss Syndicure]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

As a title, Aristocra were the lowest known political rank within a Ruling Family, below [[Councilor (Chiss Ascendancy)|Councilor]] and other ranks within the Syndicure. Aristocra were charged with recruiting promising students and [[cadet]]s from the various Chiss academies to [[Rematching|join their family]]. Aristocra [[Mitth'urf'ianico]] described recruitment as one of the worst duties "foisted" upon midlevel officials of the family, seeing it as a "boring" waste of time.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

"Aristocra" was also a less formal title for any [[Chiss family]] official at or above the rank of Aristocra proper. For example, when both [[Gender|men]] were Councilors, [[Mitth'urf'ianico]] and [[Irizi'stal'mustro]] greeted each other as "Aristocra Thurfian" and "Aristocra Zistalmu" within the [[March of Silence]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
