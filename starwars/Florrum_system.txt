The '''Florrum system''' was a [[star system]] located within the [[Sertar sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] region of [[the galaxy]]. It contained the [[planet]] of [[Florrum]], which hosted the [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s [[Ohnaka Gang|gang]] of [[pirate]]s during the [[Clone Wars]].
