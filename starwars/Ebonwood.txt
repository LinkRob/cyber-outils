'''Ebonwood''' was a type of [[Color|black]] [[wood]] with a variant known as [[greel ebonwood]]. During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], [[First Order]] [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]] carried a [[Swagger stick|swaggerstick]] made from lacquered ebonwood, the design being from military riding traditions on Pryde's [[homeworld]] of [[Alsakan]]. In addition, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] veteran [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] owned a lacquered greel ebonwood [[cane]] during that time.<ref name="TROS VD" /> A [[Unidentified historical book|book]] describing objects relating to [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]] included an entry on the cane, elaborating on the materials it was crafted from, including greel ebonwood.<ref name="100 Objects" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:GalaxyNeedsYouCover.jpg|thumb|right|200px|''The Galaxy Needs You'' contained ebonwood's debut.]]
Ebonwood first appeared in the form of Lando Calrissian's greel ebonwood cane in the picture book ''[[The Galaxy Needs You]]'', which was written by [[Caitlin Kennedy]], illustrated by [[Eda Kaban]],<ref name="GalaxyNeedsYou">''[[The Galaxy Needs You]]''</ref> and released on [[December 17]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/the-galaxy-needs-you|text=The Galaxy Needs You}}</ref> The depiction of the cane drew from Calrissian's look in<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> the final [[sequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], which debuted on [[December 20]] of that year.<ref name="YearByYear">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref>

Lando Calrissian's greel ebonwood cane was depicted earlier on in several concept art drawings by chief costume concept artist [[Glyn Dillon]] and prop concept designer [[Matthew Savage]].<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker]]''</ref> The cane was first pictured prior to its [[in-universe]] debut in a [[StarWars.com]] [https://web.archive.org/web/20200102160517/https://www.starwars.com/news/triple-force-friday-products article] released on [[September 26]], [[2019]] that previewed then-upcoming ''[[Star Wars]]'' merchandise, some relating to ''The Rise of Skywalker''.<ref name="Triple">{{SW|url=news/triple-force-friday-products|text=Check Out New ''Star Wars'' Products Just Revealed for Triple Force Friday|date=2019-09-26}}</ref> Ebonwood, and greel ebonwood, were first identified by name in the film's accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
