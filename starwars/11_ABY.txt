==Events==
*[[Din Djarin]] and [[Din Grogu]], working as [[Mercenary|contractors]] for the [[New Republic]], [[Death|kill]] [[Neo-Imperialism|neo-Imperial]] [[warlord]] [[Commander]] [[Barro]]. {{C|minimum date}}<ref name=":0">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref>
*Djarin and Grogu head to [[Shakari]] to [[Mission to Shakari|find]] [[Rotta]] and hand him over to [[the Twins]], who will in return give them information on the warlord Commander [[Janu Coin|Coin]]. After doing so, Rotta reveals that Coin is the local crime boss [[Lord]] Janu. Therefore, Djarin captures him with the help of Grogu and [[New Republic pilot]] [[Garazeb Orrelios|Garazeb Orellios]]. {{C|minimum date}}<ref name=":0" />
*With Djarin having broken his agreement with them, the Twins hire the [[bounty hunter]] [[Embo]] to [[Ambush on the Clan Mudhorn cabin|capture]] him and bring him to [[Nal Hutta]], along with Rotta. Grogu follows them alongside [[the Anzellan Droidsmiths]], before [[Rescue of Din Djarin|finding Djarin]] and [[Force healing|healing]] him after the latter was bitten by a [[dragonsnake]]. {{C|minimum date}}<ref name=":0" />
*Djarin and Grogu return to rescue Rotta after alerting the New Republic. After they rescue Rotta and eliminate the Twins, New Republic [[Colonel]] [[Ward]] and the [[Adelphi Rangers]] [[Battle of Nal Hutta (Din Djarin)|destroy]] their [[Hutt Palace|palace]]. {{C|minimum date}}<ref name=":0" />
