'''Dried galcot''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] that could be consumed by [[human]]s. After escaping [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] custody on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]] during the [[Battle of Jakku]]<ref name="Empire's End" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[New Republic pilot]] [[Norra Wexley]] was picked up by a [[shuttle]] that her [[Norra Wexley's team|teammate]] [[Jas Emari]] had [[Thief|stolen]] from the [[bounty hunter]] [[Mercurial Swift]]. Wexley consumed the [[Starship|vessel's]] survival [[Ration pack|rations]], which included dried galcot, [[kukuia nut]]s, and [[kalpa sea-thread]]s.<ref name="Empire's End" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dried galcot appeared in ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', a [[2017]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the third installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Empire's End" />
