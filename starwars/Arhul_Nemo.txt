'''Arhul Nemo''' was a [[lieutenant]] in the [[Commenor Underground]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Rebel Dossier" /> Around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Timeline">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' establishes that [[Jyn Erso]] was [[Reproduction|born]] in [[22 BBY]]. ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]'' states that Erso was twelve at the time she met Nemo, and therefore it can be deduced that the meeting took place around [[10 BBY]].</ref> he met the freedom fighter [[Saw Gerrera]] and his [[partisans]], including the twelve-[[Standard year|year]]-old [[Jyn Erso]]. Gerrera told Nemo that he had rescued Erso&mdash;who was Nemo's favorite member of the group&mdash;and raised her as his own. Nemo later reported the encounter to the [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|intelligence agency]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. The report was included in a<ref name="Rebel Dossier" /> [[1 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[1 BBY]], and since ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''{{'s}} establishes that its publication was immediately preceding the film, it must have been released in that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> [[Operation Fracture dossier|dossier]] of information provided by [[General]] [[Airen Cracken]] to [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|Chief of State]] [[Mon Mothma]] concerning [[Operation Fracture]], a mission in which Erso was a vital asset.<ref name="Rebel Dossier" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arhul Nemo was mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]'', a reference book written by [[Jason Fry]] and released in [[2016]].<ref name="Rebel Dossier" />
