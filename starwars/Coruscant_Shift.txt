'''Coruscant Shift''' was a variant of the card game [[Sabacc]]. This version of the game was featured on the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'', in the ship's [[Sublight Lounge]], where lessons were taught to guests.<ref name="SWGS Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)|''Star Wars'': Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)]]</ref>
