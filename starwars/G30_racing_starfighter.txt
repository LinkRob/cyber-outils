The '''G30 racing starfighter''' was a type of [[Racing starfighter|racing]] [[Starship|ship]] manufactured by [[Incom-FreiTek Corporation|Incom-Freitek]]. The [[starfighter]]'s [[S-foils]] could be adjusted for better aim of the wingtip-mounted [[Taim & Bak]] [[KX10B laser cannon]]s or to accommodate the need for speed or maneuverability.<ref name="SWE CGSWG" />  The ''[[Green Ace]]'' flown by [[racer]] [[Hype Fazon]] was a modified G30.<ref name="The High Tower">{{Resistance|The High Tower}}</ref>
