'''Freemaker Salvage and Repair''', also known as the '''Freemaker Garage''', or originally preferred as '''Pace's Garage''' by [[Pace Freemaker/LEGO|Pace Freemaker]] was a garage and repair business that was located in [[the Wheel]] [[space station]] in the [[Abrion system]] that was made by Pace Freemaker and [[Lena Freemaker]] who were the parents of [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi]], [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan]], and [[Zander Freemaker]]. Due to insufficient customers, Pace and Lena's children struggled to pay the rent for their business to their [[Aqualish]] landlord [[Furlac]].

The Freemakers were forced to flee their garage after discovering that [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker's]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Naare]] was actually a Sith agent. The garage suffered extensive damaged when Naare and the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Graballa/LEGO|Graballa]] attacked it in order to force Rowan to surrender the last [[Kyber Saber]] crystal. Rowan later used [[the Force]] to rebuild the garage. However, the Freemakers were forced to abandon it after [[Darth Sidious|the Emperor]] placed a bounty on Rowan's head for destroying the Kyber Saber.

==Description==
Freemaker Salvage and Repair was a family-owned full service [[hangar]] based in the [[Middle Ring/LEGO|Middle Ring]] of [[the Wheel]] [[space station]] in the [[Abrion system]]. It serviced [[speeder]]s, [[shuttle]]s, [[starfighter]]s, and even [[light cruiser]]s. The business specialized in procuring and restoring antique starships. The Freemakers also traveled through [[the galaxy]] to find starship spareparts and technology for repairing and building starships. In addition, the Freemakers also repaired damage inflicted by [[asteroid]]s and [[blaster]] blasts on starships.<ref name="Freemaker Salvage and Repair" />

The Freemaker middle child [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi]] served as the company's manager while her older brother [[Zander Freemaker|Zander]] was its leading repair expert and builder. Zander built starships from scrap called "[[Ugly|Uglies]]" or "Z-wings."<ref name="A Hero Discovered" /> Despite Zander's skill with starships, he was also a daredevil racer who was willing to take dangerous risks.<ref name="Zander's Joyride">{{FreemakerAdventures|Zander's Joyride}}</ref> The siblings were assisted by their younger brother [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan]] and the [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|R0-GR]], who worked as their butler and Rowan's minder. The garage also housed the Freemakers' starship ''[[StarScavenger]]''.<ref name="A Hero Discovered" />

==History==
===Early history===
[[File:Pace_and_Lena_in_their_new_shop.jpg|thumb|left|248x248px|Lena and Pace Freemaker in their new repair shop]]
During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] convinced the fugitives [[Pace Freemaker/LEGO|Pace]] and [[Lena Freemaker]] to start a new life on [[the Wheel]] to escape the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Graballa/LEGO|Graballa]]. Pace transformed the hangar into a repair shop which later became Freemaker Salvage and Repair. As the years passed, they got married and produced two children including [[Zander Freemaker|Zander]] and [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]]. Pace struggled to make ends meet.<ref name="Dealing with Lando; Han and Chewie Strike Back">{{All-Stars|Dealing with Lando / Han and Chewie Strike Back}}</ref>

Pace's estranged friend [[Han Solo]] and his [[Wookiee]] co-pilot [[Chewbacca]] later arrived to seek his help in obtaining a transmuter coil. The Freemakers, Solo, and Chewbacca subsequently traveled to [[Ord Tellarom/LEGO|Ord Tellarom]] to obtain one from a [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|former Droid Control Ship]]. There, they encountered the [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s [[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor]]. Pace and Han subsequently reconciled.<ref name="Dealing with Lando; Han and Chewie Strike Back" />

Pace finished restoring the Jedi starfighter but Roger accidentally sent them to the planet [[Dathomir]] while trying to serve some [[bantha bread]]. Though Pace and Roger escaped in an [[Ugly]], they were forced to abandon Skywalker's Jedi starfighter, which remained there for years.<ref name="It Gets Ugly">{{All-Stars|It Gets Ugly}}</ref> Pace and Lena later produced a third child named [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]].<ref name="A Hero Discovered" />

By the time of the [[Battle of Hoth]], Kordi and Zander had taken over the repair shop from their parents. They also struggled to pay their rent to the [[Aqualish]] landlord [[Furlac]]. While the Freemakers were out picking wrecked starship parts from a dogfight between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], Furlac sent a [[Flood droid]] to block the entrance to their garage. Kordi managed to convince Furlac to give them one more day to pay their rent.<ref name="A Hero Discovered" />

===Naare and the Kyber Saber===
After Rowan accidentally destroyed a starship that Zander and Kordi had assembled from the wreckage, Zander refused to sell any of his Z-wings to raise funds. As a result, the Freemakers and Roger were forced to travel to the [[Clone Wars]] battle site of [[Nal Kapok/LEGO|Nal Kapok]]. There, Rowan [[Mission to Nal Kapok|discovered]] that he was [[Force-sensitive]] when he discovered the hilt of the [[Kyber Saber]], the first [[lightsaber]]. He also encountered the false [[Jedi]] [[Naare]], who convinced Rowan to embark on a [[Quest for the Kyber Saber Crystals|quest]] to collect the pieces of the Kyber Saber. In secret, Naare was actually a [[Sith]] agent working for [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. After returning to the Freemaker Garage, Naare played a [[mind trick]] on Furlac to convince him that they had paid their rent. She also made him lease the garage opposite for just one [[Galactic Credit Standard|Credit]].<ref name="A Hero Discovered" />

Later, Naare began teaching Rowan the ways of the Force in the Freemaker Garage. With the boy unable to focus during a levitation lesson, she brought out a [[holomap]] and got him to pinpoint the location of the second Kyber Saber. After Rowan sensed that the crystal was in the [[Belgaroth asteroid field]], Naare and Rowan tried to leave but were stopped by Kordi, who was skeptical of Naare's mysticism. While the adults quarreled, Rowan tricked Roger into escaping with him in one of Zander's Z-wings by claiming that there was a gas leak. They then [[Skirmish on the Rancor's Fist|traveled]] to the Belgaroth asteroid field. Back at the Garage, Kordi and Naare argued. Unable to play a mind trick on Kordi, Naare lost her temper and damaged a box. This caught Zander's attention and the three quickly realized that Rowan had departed for the Belgaroth system.<ref name="Mines of Graballa">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Mines of Graballa}}</ref>

===Jobs and entanglements===
At a later date, [[Wick Cooper]], a wealthy man who lived in the Wheel's [[Upper Ring/LEGO|Upper Ring]], visited the Freemaker Garage to get his vintage [[N-1 starfighter]] serviced by the Freemakers. While Cooper was wealthy enough to afford a more expensive garage, he wanted to cut costs by having it serviced by the Freemakers. However, Zander, who had poor impulse control, took the fighter on a joyride. After Zander was captured by the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] [[Vendetta (Freemarker Adventures)|''Vendetta'']], he contacted Kordi and Rowan by [[hologram]] to [[Rescue of Zander Freemaker|rescue]] him. Kordi left Roger to amuse Wick while they and Naare rescued Zander and retrieved the N-1 starfighter. However, the N-1 starfighter was badly damaged and totaled. As a result, Zander gave Wick his [[Blazemaker]] starfighter as compensation.<ref name="Zander's Joyride" />

After receiving a [[Mission to Cloud City (Kyber Saber)|job]] from the rebel [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] to retrieve his "lost treasure" from Imperial-occupied [[Cloud City]], Rowan, Kordi, and Zander departed for [[Bespin]] and left Roger to look after Freemaker Garage. After Roger accidentally revealed the Freemakers' mission, Naare followed them there in her ship, the ''[[Eclipse Fighter]]''. The Freemakers presented the "lost treasure" to Lando, which turned out to be his cape. In return, he rewarded them with gold bars.<ref name="Lost Treasure">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Lost Treasure of Cloud City}}</ref>

Later, the [[Twi'lek]] captain [[Ignacio Wortan]] visited the Freemaker Garage and hired the Freemakers to obtain a piece of [[wroshyr wood]] from the [[Wookiee]] homeworld of [[Kashyyyk]]. The Freemakers managed to [[Skirmish on Kashyyyk (Kyber Saber)|obtain]] the wroyshyr wood and the third Kyber Saber crystal. However, the wroshyr wood was damaged and Ignacio called off the deal, leaving the Freemakers further in debt.<ref name="Peril on Kashyyyk">{{FreemakerAdventures|Peril on Kashyyyk}}</ref>

Struggling with debt, Kordi accepted services jobs from Wick Cooper, and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] operatives [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Luke Skywalker]]. When Imperial forces led by [[Estoc]] turned up [[Manhunt on the Wheel|searching]] for the rebels, Kordi and Zander smuggled Leia out of their garage in a waste bin. Meanwhile, Luke departed with Rowan and Roger on an accidental trip in the ''StarScavenger'' to the planet [[Felucia]]. Following their adventures, the two groups regrouped at the Freemaker Garage. The Freemakers managed to trick the Imperials into calling off their hunt after sending Leia and Luke's [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wing]] as a decoy to be destroyed. Kordi then allowed Leia and Luke to purchase a Z-wing at a highly discounted rate.<ref name="Crossing Paths">{{FreemakerAdventures|Crossing Paths}}</ref>

===Trouble with Naare and Graballa===
Later, Kordi reprimanded her younger brother Rowan for neglecting to maintain their garage's astromech motivators. Shortly later, the Freemakers [[Mission to Tatooine (Kyber Saber)|departed]] for [[Tatooine]] after Zander received a job offer from the [[podracer]] [[pilot]] [[Ben Quadinaros]] to service his podracer. However, this turned out to be a trap set by the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Graballa/LEGO|Graballa]], whom Rowan and Roger had encountered earlier in the Belgaroth asteroid field.<ref name="Race on Tatooine">{{FreemakerAdventures|Race on Tatooine}}</ref>

After discovering evidence that Naare was actually a Sith agent, the Freemakers made preparations to evacuate their Garage in the ''StarScavenger''. However, Naare forced her way inside by slicing through the blast door. Following a struggle, the Freemakers managed to escape into space. Later, Naare returned to the garage only to encounter Graballa and his henchmen. The two sides struck an alliance to hunt for the remaining Kyber Saber crystals in return for sharing the crystals.<ref name="The Test">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Test}}</ref> Using a [[Homing beacon|tracking device]] planted on Roger's [[transmission pack]], Naare and Graballa managed to [[Race to Ningoth/LEGO|steal]] [[Mission to Naboo (Kyber Saber)|the crystals]] from the Freemakers on [[Takodana]], [[Ningoth/LEGO|Ningoth]], and [[Naboo]].<ref name="Crystal Chase">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Kyber Saber Crystal Chase}}</ref>

However, Rowan and Roger managed to [[Mission to Hoth (Kyber Saber)|obtain]] the seventh Kyber Saber crystal and fled to a remote planet on the [[Mini Scavenger]]. Unable to find Rowan, Kordi and Zander returned to Freemaker Salvage and Repair only to encounter Furlac, who reminded them their rent was overdue and demanded compensation for their damaged door. At that point, Naare, Graballa, and his henchmen entered the Garage and took the Freemakers prisoner. Naare then tortured Kordi and Zander psychologically in order to force Rowan out of hiding. This ploy worked but Rowan and his siblings put up a fierce [[Duel on the Wheel|fight]] against Naare and Graballa. Despite their efforts, Naare still learnt about the location of the seventh crystal from Roger and stole his head. Naare then turned on Graballa and his henchmen and departed on the ''Eclipse Fighter'' to claim the crystal.<ref name="Duel of Destiny">{{FreemakerAdventures|Duel of Destiny}}</ref>

===Joining the Rebellion===
After Naare and Graballa had left, Rowan used the Force to repair the damaged garage and ''StarScavenger''. The Freemakers then embarked on a [[Skirmish on Coruscant (Kyber Saber)|mission to steal the Kyber Saber]] from Naare and the Emperor on [[Coruscant]]. After the Emperor placed Rowan on his most wanted list, the Freemakers were unable to return the to the garage and instead joined the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Return of the Kyber Saber">{{FreemakerAdventures|Return of the Kyber Saber}}</ref>

After joining the Rebel Alliance, the Freemakers repaired ships for the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]]. Rowan offered one of Freemaker Salvage and Repair's cards to the [[Mon Calamari]] rebel starship builder [[Quarrie]] and convinced him to accompany the Freemakers on their [[Mission to Tibalt|mission]] to [[Tibalt/LEGO|Tibalt]].<ref name="Trouble on Tibalt" />

[[Wick Cooper]] later transformed the Freemakers' former garage and home into a [[cantina]] called "Wick Cooper's Caffeine and Kitsch Club." Seeking to [[Mission to obtain an energy matrix activator|obtain]] an energy matrix activator to [[Quest to build the Arrowhead|build]] a starship called the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']], the Freemakers infiltrated their former home disguised as cleaners. However, they were ambushed by Graballa and his bounty hunters Dengar, [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], [[IG-88B|IG-88]], [[Zuckuss]], and [[4-LOM]]. After starting a fight between the bounty hunters, the Freemakers managed to escape Graballa and Darth Vader and obtain the energy matrix activator.<ref name="RTTW">{{FreemakerAdventures|Return to the Wheel}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DesigningFreemakerGarage.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Erik Varszegi and the Freemaker Garage model]]
Freemaker Salvage and Repair first appeared in the non-[[Canon]] [[2016]] [[Disney XD]] animated television series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]''. During conceptualization, [[The LEGO Group|LEGO]] Lead Model Designer [[Erik Varszegi]] built a scale model of the garage using LEGO bricks.<ref name="Designing the Freemaker Garage">{{LEGOWebCite|url=en-us/starwars/videos/freemaker-garage-designer-video-ce691c98a6e4459abada5932ca68c4e1|text=Designing the Freemaker Garage from LEGO Star Wars Freemaker Adventures}}</ref> Freemaker Salvage and Repair was later confirmed as the official name of the garage in a promotional LEGO video released on [[August 29]], 2016.<ref name="Freemaker Salvage and Repair" />
