A small [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] implant affixed to suprarenal glands, '''adrenal implants''' stimulated the production of [[Epinephrine/Canon|epinephrine]] in the body when an individual's nervous system was subjected to strain. While the effect was not as rapid as a direct injection of epinephrine into the [[blood]]stream, it did reduce recovery time from fatigue. The cost of adrenal implants was 2,250 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets" />

Beings of all walks of life could benefit from adrenal implants, which would allow them to recover swiftly during protracted combat or a lengthy debate.<ref name="Gadgets" />
