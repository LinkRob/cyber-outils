A '''docking ring''' was a sealed circular hatch with external magnetic couplings that allowed a medium-sized [[starship]] to dock to a larger [[capital ship]] or a [[space station]]. It could also be used to dock with a [[transparisteel]] gantry. The ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' had two docking rings, situated respectively on the port and starboard sides of the ship.<ref name="OWM">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>
