An individual was the '''history tutor''' of the [[Hutt]] [[Gender|male]] [[Churo]], who resided on the [[Hutt Space]] [[planet]] [[Nal Hutta]], during the [[High Republic Era]]. At one point, the [[Teacher|tutor]] told their student about the coming-of-age [[Rite of passage|ritual]] that the ancient [[E'roni|people]] of<ref name="Beware the Nameless">''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''</ref> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet<ref name="Convergence">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]''</ref> [[E'ronoh]] underwent, which involved [[Youngling|children]] being left in the [[desert]] with only a [[Bane blade|dagger]], with the expectation that they survive and leave as a [[Soldier|warrior]].<ref name="Beware the Nameless">''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''</ref> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|BTN}}</ref> Churo mentally recalled what his tutor had informed him of about the ancient people of E'ronoh, and the ritual they carried out, when he felt pressured to prove himself to his own [[Devirsivik Clan|family]].<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

==Behind the scenes==
Churo's history tutor was mentioned in the [[2024]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref>
