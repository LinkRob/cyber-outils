Between [[28 ABY]] and [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Knights of Ren]], [[Ren|led]] by [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], '''assassinated''' an [[Unidentified Inner Rim warlord|Inner Rim warlord]]. Kylo Ren had secretly been directed to perform the mission by the [[First Order]] military junta and its [[Supreme Leader]], [[Snoke]], who arrived with First Order forces once the assassination was complete. Snoke convinced Ren to abandon the Knights and ordered his legion of [[First Order stormtrooper]]s to fire on the Knights, forcing them to flee and leaving them without a leader.<ref name="LoV 8" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="TFA date">{{FilmDate|VII}}</ref>  the [[Force-sensitive]] [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] had a vision of the assassination when she grabbed the [[Skywalker lightsaber]] in [[Takodana Castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]].<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="LoV Date">The events of ''Legacy of Vader'' 8 take place in [[34 ABY]] per the reasoning [[Template:LegacyOfVaderDate|here]].</ref> [[Cardo]] and the Knights explained the assassination and the state the First Order and Kylo Ren had left them in to their new leader [[Tava Ren]] after a failed [[Raid on Vandor|mission]] on the planet [[Vandor]].<ref name="LoV 8" />

==Prelude==
===A new master===
{{Quote|When he first came to us, he used another name. Ben Solo. Stronger in the shadow than any knight in a very long time. You could sense it burning through him. It was awful. Cold fire.|Cardo, to Tava Ren|<ref name="LoV 8" />}}
[[File:BenFightsRen-LegacyOfVader8.jpg|thumb|250px|left|After a fierce duel, Ben Solo took Ren's life and became the new master of his knights.]]
Following the [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destruction]] of the [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker|Jedi Temple]], Ben Solo began his descent to the dark side and seeked out [[Snoke]], a mysterious [[Force-sensitive|force wielder]] that recommended him to join the [[Knights of Ren]] as his first step on that path. Meeting [[Ren (human)|Ren]], the current leader of the knights, Ben was allowed to join them on their next mission as an opportunity for Ben to prove if he was worthy of joining them.<ref name="Kylo Ren 2">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 2|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 2]]</ref><ref name="Kylo Ren 3">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 3|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 3]]</ref>

Helping the knights during their [[Skirmish on the Minemoon|mission]] on the [[Minemoon]] of [[Mimban]], Ben was surprised by the arrival of [[Tai]] and [[Voe]], his former [[Jedi]] comrades, who had come to avenge the [[Battle of Elphrona|death]] of [[Hennix]]. While his former friend Tai tried to reason with Ben, he was quickly killed by Ren, leading a grief-stricken Ben to engage in a duel with the knight, killing Ren along with his former comrade Voe, falling to the dark side in the process. Fulfilling the requirement of "a good death" and having eliminated the former master of the knights, Ben gained leadership of the knights and adopted the alias of Kylo Ren.<ref name="Kylo Ren 4">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 4|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 4]]</ref><ref name="LoV 8" />

===The ensuing years===
{{Quote|We went where we wanted, did what we wanted. Took what we wanted. Credits, artifacts&hellip; and life. Lots and lots of life. Kylo Ren was real good at that.|Cardo, to Tava Ren|<ref name="LoV 8" />}}
[[File:KnightsOfRenHeist-LegacyOfVader8.jpg|thumb|250px|right|Under Kylo's leadership, the knights enjoyed a time of glory and success.]]
In the years following Kylo Ren's take over, the knights enjoyed a time filled with glory and victories, being able to take whatever they wanted as Ren proved to be more than capable of taking any life that threatened their goals. As time went on however, Kylo's mentality seemed to change, something that the knights took note of, seeing that he began to isolate himself a lot more from the rest of his knights to the point where he began talking to himself or to someone the other knights couldn't hear. The darkness that grew within Ren eventually began to scare even the knights, who usually reveled in it.<ref name="LoV 8" />

Following this change on his demeanor, Kylo started to bring his knights on multiple missions that went beyond the robberies or heists they used to do. These new missions had a purpose, with the knights feeling now like a task force that was being aimed at particular targets. In reality, Kylo Ren by this point had joined the [[First Order]] junta under the leadership of [[Supreme Leader]] Snoke, who was responsible for the recent missions the Knights of Ren were tasked with. Despite their doubts and questions regarding who was really pulling the strings, the knights wouldn't dare to ask Kylo anything as they believed it to be a death sentence.<ref name="LoV 8" />

==The mission==
===Carrying the assassination===
{{Quote|I saw Kylo Ren get into plenty of battles when he ran the knights. Some tough opponents, no doubt. But in the end&hellip; no one ever came close. You went up against Kylo Ren, you lost. And most of the time&hellip; you died.|Cardo, to Tava Ren|<ref name="LoV 8" />}}
[[File:WarlordAssassination-LegacyOfVader8.jpg|thumb|250px|left|Kylo and his knights killed the warlord after a chase.]]
[[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] eventually tasked [[Ben Solo|Ren]] and his knights to assassinate an [[Unidentified Inner Rim warlord|Inner Rim warlord]]. Traveling to his stronghold, the [[Knights of Ren]] were able to successfully root him out which soon lead to a chase. One of the warlord's men shot a missile at the knights, but Kylo was able to launch it back to them using the force. Despite the warlord and his people putting up a good fight, it ultimatly was pointless, as the knights prove victorious and the warlord was promptly stabbed in the back by [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|Ren's lightsaber]].<ref name="LoV 8" />

===Revelations and betrayal===
{{Quote|In the end, all we could really do&hellip; was run. Like Snoke said. Like the rats of the dark side. Not even scrabbling for scraps&hellip; running for our blasted lives.|Cardo, to Tava Ren|<ref name="LoV 8" />}}
[[File:KnightsBetrayed-LegacyOfVader8.jpg|thumb|250px|right|After the mission, Kylo officially joined the First Order and betrayed his knights per Snoke's orders.]]
Soon after finishing their mission, Kylo felt a presence through the force, which was the arrival of Supreme Leader Snoke and the [[First Order]]. Surrounded by a legion of [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] and his [[Praetorian Guard]], the Supreme Leader told Kylo that it was time to officially join the First Order and leave his knights behind, saying that they should no longer be in neither of their presence. [[Vicrul]] threatened Snoke for his insults, but he was quickly electrocuted and thrown back by the Supreme Leader's [[force lightning]].<ref name="LoV 8" />

Ren not only was quick to disown the knights, but even asked Snoke if he should kill them, to which the Supreme Leader answered by ordering his stormtroopers to blast them, thinking they were beneath Ren. The knights fought against Snoke's death squad of stormtroopers but quickly realized that they wouldn't be able to win as Vicrul was still injured and they lacked the fire power. Instead, the knights decided to flee the battle and, despite being chased by the First Order, managed to escape their own assassination attempt.<ref name="LoV 8" />

==Aftermath==
===The new Ren===
{{Quote|I took control. Maybe because they wanted me to, maybe because they didn't care. They didn't care about much of anything.|Tava Ren, to Kylo Ren|<ref name="LoV 9">[[Legacy of Vader 9|''Legacy of Vader'' 9]]</ref>}}
[[File:TavaWithKnightsOnVandor-LegacyOfVader8.jpg|thumb|left|250px|After escaping the First Order, the Knights of Ren found a new leader in Tava.]]
Following their betrayal, the [[Knights of Ren]] took a job as muscle for the gang leader [[Bedat]], shaking down shopkeepers for protection money. One day, the knights made a mistake during a mission and killed a person they weren't supposed to, leading Bedat to lock them up and prepare them for execution.<ref name="LoV 9" />

However, the knights were rescued by one of Bedat's enforcers, who offered to free them in exchange for leading them. While the knights were happy to accept this offer, they mentioned that she needed a good death to join their ranks and lead them, the woman followed suit by murdering Bedat and his people, which allowed her to take control of the knights as [[Tava Ren]].<ref name="LoV 9" />

===Visions and remembrance===
{{Quote|I am called Tava. I am a knight of Ren. I am at your service.|Tava Ren, to Kylo Ren|<ref name="LoV 8" />}}
[[File:KnightsOfRenVisionRey-TFA.png|thumb|right|260px|Rey had a vision of the assassination after touching Luke Skywalker's lightsaber.]]
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="TFA date" /> as Kylo Ren had continue to take an active role within the First Order, eventually leading to an [[First Order-Resistance War|all-out war]] between the junta and the [[Resistance]] movement, a [[Force-sensitive]] [[scavenger]] named [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] had a vision of the assassination when she grabbed the [[Skywalker lightsaber]] in [[Takodana Castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]].<ref name="TFA" /> 

Later that [[Standard year|year]], Kylo had assassinated Supreme Leader Snoke and taken over the First Order, having completely forgotten about his knights.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> Following a failed mission on the planet Vandor, Tava Ren questioned her knights, to which [[Cardo]] answered by telling her about the assassination, Kylo Ren's betrayal and the First Order. This explanation motivated Tava Ren to [[Duel on the Finalizer|confront]] Kylo.<ref name="LoV 9" />

==Behind the scenes==
The assassiantion was first seen in ''[[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens]]'' as a vision of [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] after touching the [[Skywalker lightsaber]].<ref name="TFA" /> The details of the assassination and their aftermath were first revealed in the [[Legacy of Vader 8|eight issue]] of the comic book series ''[[Star Wars: Legacy of Vader]]'', which also doubles as an explanation as to why the [[Knights of Ren]] weren't present alongside Kylo during the events of the film.<ref name="LoV 8" />
