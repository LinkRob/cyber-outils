'''Begaral Moghare''' was a [[humanoid]] individual who was a wanted fugitive by the [[time]] of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. Wearing a facial wrap, Moghare attended the [[Aki-Aki]] [[Festival of the Ancestors]] on [[Pasaana]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
