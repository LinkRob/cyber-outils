'''Arthur Miller''' is an [[Wikipedia:Audio engineer|audio engineer]]. Miller first contribution to the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise was providing sound effects for the [[1995]] [[Bantam Doubleday Dell Audio Publishing|Bantam]] ''[[Tales from the Mos Eisley Cantina]]'' audio dramas: [[We Don't Do Weddings: The Band's Tale (audio drama)|''We Don't Do Weddings: The Band's Tale'']]<ref name="We Don't Do Weddings" /> and [[Nightlily: The Lovers' Tale (audio drama)|''Nightlily: The Lovers' Tale'']].<ref name="Nightlily">[[Nightlily: The Lovers' Tale (audio drama)|''Nightlily: The Lovers' Tale'' audio drama]]</ref>
