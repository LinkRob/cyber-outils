The '''''Canny Bargain''''' was a [[starship]] equipped with [[deflector shield generator]]s that served in the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> the vessel participated in the [[New Republic]]'s [[Battle of Jakku|assault]] on the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|loyalist forces]] above the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. During the subsequent battle, the ''Canny Bargain'' lost its [[Deflector shield|shields]] and suffered a [[hull]] breach. The ship subsequently withdrew from the fray, and news of its retreat reached the refitted New Republic [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]'', where the latter vessel's [[commanding officer]], [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]], was informed of the ''Canny Bargain''{{'s}} withdrawal by one of her [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] [[officer]]s.<ref name="Victory's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Canny Bargain'' was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', the final installment of [[Alexander Freed]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
