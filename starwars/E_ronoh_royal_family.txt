A '''royal family''' was the ruling family of the planet [[E'ronoh]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> it was led by [[King|Monarch]] [[A'lbaran]], who had two children&mdash; a son, [[Prince]] [[Niko A'lbaran]] and a daughter, Princess [[Xiri A'lbaran]]. During the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]], Xiri married Prince [[Phan-tu Zenn]] of [[Eiram]], joining both royal families together to end the conflict.<ref name="Convergence" />
