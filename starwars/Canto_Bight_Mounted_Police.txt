The '''Canto Bight Mounted Police''', abbreviated as '''CBMP''', were [[police officer]]s of the [[Canto Bight Police Department]] that used [[Zephyr GB-134 speeder]]s. In [[34 ABY]], during a [[Mission to Canto Bight|mission]] undertaken by [[Rose Tico]], [[Finn]] and [[BB-8]] of the [[Resistance]], the CBMP chased them down as they rode a [[fathier]]. Eventually, Finn, Rose, and BB-8 were able to escape.<ref name="Episode VIII" />
