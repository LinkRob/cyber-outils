The '''Excarga system''' was a [[star system]] located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
Excarga was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Canon]] in the [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]],<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> released in [[2016]] by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="What Will You Do?">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2016/7/26/what-will-you-do|text=What Will You Do?}}</ref> Its first mention was in the [[Star Wars Legends]] instant [[Roleplaying game|RPG adventure]] "[[Countdown to Disaster]]," released in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 7|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 7]] in [[1995]].<ref name="AdventureJournalCenter">{{JournalCite|7|Countdown to Disaster}}</ref>
