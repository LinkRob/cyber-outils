The '''Conquest of Regis Kor''' was the name given by the [[Galactic Empire]] for a mission<ref name="Inquisitors 1" /> during the [[Great Jedi Purge]]<ref name="Date" /> carried out by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Tensu Run]], who sought to free the prisoners of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial outpost (Regis Kor)|outpost]] on the rocky astronomical object, [[Regis Kor]]. The mysteries of the outpost kept the locals in check and kept dissidence at bay, except for Run. The [[Jedi]] engaged in [[starfighter combat]] with Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] before landing on Regis Kor and infiltrating the outpost. Run fought through the [[stormtrooper]]s<ref name="Inquisitors 1" /> and then singlehandedly freed 100 prisoners<ref name="Free Comic Book Day 2025: ''Star Wars'' 1" /> by bringing them to a [[transport]] ship.<ref name="Inquisitors 1" />
