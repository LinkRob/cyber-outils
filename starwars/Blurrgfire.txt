'''Blurrgfire''' was a [[Alcohol|non-alcoholic]] [[Foodstuff|beverage]] that imitated the sweet, sour, and spicy flavor of [[citrom]] juice. It was found throughout [[the galaxy]], but it was allegedly invented by a [[Unidentified bartender (Oga's Cantina)|bartender]] at [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]].

==Description==
Blurrgfire was a classic [[Alcohol|non-alcoholic]] [[Foodstuff|mixed drink]] found at [[cantina]]s throughout [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> Its flavor imitated juice from a [[citrom]], a [[fruit]] that did not stay fresh for long, but was both sweet and sour with a burst of heat. Blurrgfire was suitable for consumption by [[Artiodac]]s,<ref name="Cookbook" /> [[human]]s,<ref name="Secrets and Holograms" /> and [[Chadra-Fan]]s. When consumed by [[DUM-series pit droid|pit droids]], Blurrgfire would cause erratic behavior.<ref name="G-LN" />

==History==
[[File:Ogas-Blurrgfire.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Blurrgfire at Oga's Cantina]]
According to [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]], Blurrgfire was invented by a [[bartender]] at [[Oga's Cantina]], an establishment that was owned by [[Oga Garra]] and located in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. In Tuggs' account, the bartender came up with a recipe that combined various juices and spices to mimic the flavor of citrom. Tuggs claimed that he could not tell the difference between citrom and Blurrgfire because the imitation was so perfect, and he included the anecdote and a recipe for the drink in his cookbook, ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''. Although Tuggs did not specify the bartender's name,<ref name="Cookbook" /> author and explorer [[Eloc Throno]] wrote in his ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'' that Garra put "her own twist" on the drink.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

Near the end of the [[Clone Wars]], Blurrgfire was among the drink selections that the bartender [[Janott]] offered to [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] when she visited Oga's Cantina for a [[Mission to Batuu|personal mission]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> It remained available at the cantina<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] and in the novel ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'' take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> and on one occasion [[Resistance]] spy [[Vi Moradi]] drank it to stay sober and hangover-free while her [[First Order|ex-First Order]] companion [[Archex]] got intoxicated from a potent [[Jedi Mind Trick (cocktail)|Jedi Mind Trick]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" />

Also during 34 ABY,<ref name="Date">{{InsiderCite|190|Resistance Revisited|''Resistance'' Revisited}} establishes that the events of [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|''Star Wars Resistance'' Season One]], which the short "[[G-LN (episode)|G-LN]]" occurs currently to, takes place thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], which corresponds to [[34 ABY]], according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> Blurrgfire was served at [[Aunt Z's Tavern]]<ref name="G-LN" /> on the ''[[Colossus]]'', a mobile [[Starship|aircraft]] [[Fuel|refueling]] [[Space station|station]] on the [[ocean]] [[planet]] [[Castilon]], around the time of the [[First Order-Resistance War]].<ref name="Secrets and Holograms" /> While at the tavern, the Chadra-Fan [[Orka]] was drinking the beverage. After realizing that his pit [[droid]] [[G-LN]] was bored, Orka decided to let the [[Maintenance droid|repair droid]] try some of his Blurrgfire. Upon sipping the drink, G-LN began to cough and then became erratic, running around the tavern and disturbing other patrons. Shortly thereafter,<ref name="G-LN" /> the [[Klatooinian]]<ref name="Bolza Grool DB">{{Databank|bolza-grool|Bolza Grool}}</ref> [[Bolza Grool]] hit G-LN on the head, incapacitating the droid and stopping the adverse effects of the Blurrgfire. Upon seeing G-LN unconscious, Orka made a joke<ref name="G-LN" /> to his partner [[Flix]]<ref name="Ridge & Auman Interview">{{WebCite|url=https://www.coffeewithkenobi.com/cwk-show-298-star-wars-resistance-season-two-press-day-coverage|text=CWK Show #298: Star Wars Resistance Season Two Press Day Coverage|archivedate=20191001213059}}</ref> and ordered another round of Blurrgfire.<ref name="G-LN" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blurrgfire was first mentioned on a trading card available at [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] [[December 16]], [[2017]] "Galactic Nights" session, which featured the cantina in the upcoming [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] park area.<ref name="WDWNT">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2018/01/photos-star-wars-galaxys-edge-trading-cards-provide-new-details-guests-will-find-batuu-2019|text=PHOTOS: Star Wars – Galaxy's Edge Trading Cards Provide New Details of What Guests Will Find on Batuu in 2019|work=WDW News Today|archivedate=20180510032057}}</ref> At the [[In-universe|real-world]] Oga's Cantina bar, the menu's description reads, "Lemonade, pomegranate juice, and habanero-lime."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/lunch|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Lunch Menu}}</ref>

Blurrgfire first appeared in "[[G-LN (episode)|G-LN]]," a short that aired on [[December 17]], [[2018]] as part of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''. During its appearance, Blurrgfire was depicted as a pale orange substance as opposed to the deep red coloring it was depicted with in its other appearances.<ref name="G-LN" />

A recipe for Blurrgfire is included in the [[2019]] cookbook ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which was written by [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The ingredients for the beverage are [[Wikipedia:Pomegranate juice|pomegranate juice]], [[Wikipedia:Habanero|habanero]] lime syrup, and prepared [[Wikipedia:Lemonade|lemonade]]; before serving, a [[Wikipedia:Lime|lime]] is run around the rim of a glass which is then dipped in [[Wikipedia:Alaea salt|red sea salt]].<ref name="Cookbook" />

The [[2020]] [[Canon|non-canon]] video game expansion ''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]'' includes Blurrgfire as a drink sold to the player's [[Wikipedia:The Sims|Sims]] at the Oga's Cantina vendor in the Black Spire Outpost neighborhood. Upon consuming the drink for the first time, the Sim automatically learns the recipe and can prepare it at mixology bars in their home neighborhood.<ref name="Sims">''[[The Sims 4 Star Wars: Journey to Batuu]]''</ref>
