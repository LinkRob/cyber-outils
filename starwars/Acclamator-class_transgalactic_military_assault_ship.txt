The '''''Acclamator''-class transgalactic military assault ship''', also known as '''''Acclamator''-class Republic assault ship''', '''''Acclamator''-class Planetary Assault Ship''', '''''Acclamator''-class battleship''', or '''''Acclamator''-class cruiser''', was a type of [[cruiser]]-class proto-[[Star Destroyer]] utilized by the [[Galactic Republic]] for ferrying [[clone trooper]]s across [[the galaxy]]. They were first deployed at the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] after transporting the [[Cloning|clone]] [[army]] from [[Kamino]], and subsequently carried the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] from the [[capital]] [[Coruscant]] to the various arenas of the [[Clone Wars]], a conflict in which they would see extensive use against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].

==Characteristics==
===Design and systems===
[[File:Acclamator-CotR.png|thumb|left|220px|An ''Acclamator II''-class transport entering a planet's atmosphere.]]
The ''Acclamator''-class [[assault ship]] was developed by [[Rothana Heavy Engineering]], a subsidiary of [[Kuat Drive Yards]]. The predecessor to later [[Star Destroyer]] designs like the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class]], the ''Acclamator''-class was part of the secret construction program initiated by [[Darth Sidious]] (under false orders of the [[Jedi High Council]]) to create a Grand Army of the Republic.<ref name="Starfighters">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref><ref name="USW,NE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> It had a broad, arrowhead shaped [[hull]], with a single command and observation deck toward the aft of the ship, with most of the interior of the ship devoted to cargo space and passenger berthing. During the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], Acclamators were unpainted in a gray [[color]]. Some time after this, most Acclamators featured a red strip through the center, as well as stripes along it sides. The ''Acclamator''-class was equipped with long-range [[sensor]]s.<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> Although [[planetary defense forces]] had previously built much larger ships to patrol local space lanes, the ''Acclamator''-class was notable for representing the return of the first truly transgalactic warships to the galaxy.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" />

The ''Acclamator''-class was propelled by a series of electromagnetic thrust-vectoring [[sublight engine]]s<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> and extraordinarily fast<ref name="SOTG" /> Class .6<ref name="Collapse" /> twin [[hyperdrive]] engines,<ref name="Starfighters" /> that utilized a [[navigation computer]]. It additionally possessed a backup hyperdrive of Class 10<ref name="Collapse" /> The hyperdrives were housed within the tail of the ship, along with [[neutrino radiator]]s for the power system. The main [[reactor]] was hidden deep within the ship's hull terraces and fed by condensed annihilation reactant many orders more dense than the ship itself. [[Tensor field|Tensorial integrity]] and [[Acceleration compensator|inertial compensator]] field conduits ran throughout the length of the ship. Towards the stern of the ship was the [[conning tower]], which housed the main [[sensor]] and communications assembly at the fore, behind which was the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] and battle operations room.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" />

===Passengers and complement===
[[File:Acclamators-ST2.jpg|thumb|300px|right|''Acclamator''-class assault ships take off from Coruscant at the start of the Clone Wars.]]
The ''Acclamator''-class was crewed with 700 officers and enlisted crew. While it carried no starfighters, it carried 320 [[speeder bike]]s, sixty-six [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]], fourteen [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/carrier|Low Altitude Assault Transport carriers]], forty-eight [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer|AT-TEs]], and thirty-six [[Self-Propelled Heavy Artillery|SPHA]] [[walker]]s. The ''Acclamator''-class could carry a full legion of clone troopers&ndash; 9,000 fighting [[troop]]s and 6,000 HQ and support troops. The ''Acclamator'' additionally carried six months of consumables.<ref name="Collapse" /> Unlike many craft of its size, the ''Acclamator''-class was capable of both ground and water landings, where it could unload its complement of clone troopers and vehicles<ref name="Starfighters">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref><ref name="USW,NE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> in a matter of mere [[Standard hour|hours]].<ref name="Collapse" /> In order to support the ship's weight, a combination of antigravity [[repulsorlift]]s and landing pads are utilized. Troops and vehicles could exit from the main [[Hangar|hanger]] via ramps extending from the sides or a ventral hanger opening.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /><ref name="TCWfilm">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> Barracks for most troops were near the dorsal terrace of the ship. [[Low Altitude Assault Transport|LAAT]] [[gunship]]s were carried on a circulating rail in the main hanger, with platforms allowing for maintenance and loading of troops, while other combat vehicles were stored in dedicated service bays.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" />

===Defense===
In order to defend itself from attack, the ''Acclamator''-class was cladded in super-dispersive [[neutronium]]-impregnated armor, making it quite impervious against [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[fusion rocket]]s. It was also protected by strong [[deflector shield]]ing, with regional shield generator nodes located at different points on the ship.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> However, as displayed during the [[Battle of Ryloth]], Separatist [[J-1 proton cannon]]s could easily penetrate the shields of an ''Acclamator''-class assault ship.<ref name="Innocents">{{TCW|Innocents of Ryloth}}</ref>

===Armament===
[[File:AcclamatorProfile-MF49.png|thumb|300px|left|Starboard profile view of an ''Acclamator''-class assault ship]]
The ''Acclamator''-class possessed three starboard, three port and six forward [[Quad turbolaser cannon|quad light turbolaser batteries]], twelve port and twelve starboard [[heavy laser cannon]]s, four [[proton torpedo launcher]]s, and [[tractor beam]]s.<ref name="Collapse" /> The ship was ringed by a series of [[point-defense laser cannon]]s. These armaments allowed it to conduct a range of [[orbital bombardment]]s in support of ground forces. These could vary from precise surgical strikes to massively devastating [[Base Delta Zero]] bombardments.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" />

==Role==
During the Clone Wars, the ''Acclamator''-class's primary role was as a [[troop transport]], although it also had a secondary function as a [[warship]].<ref name="Starfighters">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref><ref name="USW,NE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> Designed to monitor ground movements and coordinate attacks, each [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]] was equipped with a tracking device in their [[Clone trooper armor|helmet]] that allowed controllers on ''Acclamator''-class [[Starship|ships]] to better establish a picture of the combat zone. Their versatility and role as a troop transport proved essential during the Clone Wars.<ref name="SOTG" /> As vessels of the Republic Navy, the warship was also regarded as something deployed to defend galactic [[democracy]].<ref name="Insider230">{{InsiderCite|230|Launchpad}}</ref>

==History==
===Clone Wars===
====Action at Geonosis====
[[File:Clone_Army_Charge.jpg|thumb|right|250px|''Acclamator''-class assault ships landing on Geonosis.]]
Under [[Project Icefang]],<ref name="93Database">{{GermanFanCite|93|Database}}</ref> Acclamators were secretly produced by [[Rothana Heavy Engineering]] via a contract with the [[Ruling Council (Kamino)|Kaminoan government]]. The first starship developed by RHE,<ref name="HC33">{{HelmetCollectionCite|33|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> the Acclamator would see production on [[Rothana]]<ref name="Republic/Sep Ships Encyclopedia 1">{{EncyclopediaCite|Republic and Separatist Ships (1)}}</ref> and in the [[Kuat shipyards]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>

During the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> which marked the end of the [[Separatist Crisis]] and the beginning of the [[Clone Wars]], the ''Acclamator''-class assault ship saw extensive use in the battle.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> There, twelve Acclamators<ref name="CL2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> of the [[Rothana assault ship fleet]],<ref name="Republic/Sep Ships Encyclopedia 1" /> including the ''[[Implacable (Acclamator-class)|Implacable]],'' were sent alongside [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] to [[Geonosis]] to reinforce the Jedi, where they transported a total of 192,000 clone troopers, 1,600 Low Altitude Assault Transport/i gunships, 400 Low Altitude Assault Transport carriers, 100 Self-Propelled Heavy Artillery Turbolasers, and 2,160 All Terrain Tactical Enforcers,<ref name="CL2016" /> eventually resulting in a victory for the Republic.<ref name="Episode II" />

====Deployment to Christophsis and Teth====
[[File:Acclamator_over_Teth.png|thumb|left|250px|An Acclamator makes its descent on Teth.]]
Following the Battle of Geonosis, the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Christophsis blockade|blockaded]] the [[crystal]]line planet [[Christophsis]] as part of their attempts to take possession of [[the galaxy]]'s major [[hyperspace route]]s.<ref name="TCWfilm" /> However, after Republic forces led by [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] broke through the [[blockade]],<ref name="Cat and Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref> they were left trapped, dwindling, and outnumbered on the planet's surface, where they [[Battle of Christophsis|fought]] the [[Whorm Loathsom's army|army]] of [[Kerkoiden]] [[General]] [[Whorm Loathsom]]. Amid the battle, the Jedi contacted the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] requesting assistance, resulting in the [[Wurtz's fleet|fleet]] of [[Admiral]] [[Wurtz]] under Yoda and Admiral [[Wullf Yularen]], which consisted of four ''Acclamator''-class assault ships and three [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]], being sent.<ref name="TCWfilm" />

After their victory at Christophsis, Skywalker and his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] were dispatched to the planet [[Teth]] aboard an ''Acclamator''-class assault ship in order to [[Battle of Teth|rescue]] the [[Hutt]]let [[Rotta]], the son of [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]].<ref name="TCWfilm" />

====Landing on Ryloth====
{{Quote|Commander, the invasion fleet is coming out of hyperspace.|Wullf Yularen to Ahsoka Tano, referring to the Republic invasion fleet|<ref name="Storm Over Ryloth">{{TCW|Storm Over Ryloth}}</ref>}}
[[File:Acclamators_damaged_over_Ryloth.png|thumb|right|250px|While landing on Ryloth, one of the Acclamators is hit by a J-1 proton cannon.]]
Later, during the [[Battle of Ryloth]], after Skywalker and his [[Skywalker's fleet|fleet]] of three Venators managed to break through the Separatist [[Ryloth blockade|blockade]] [[orbit]]ing the planet, the Republic dispatched an [[Ryloth invasion fleet|invasion fleet]] of three Acclamators under Generals [[Mace Windu]] and Kenobi, which subsequently prepared to make their descent on the planet. However, while the three assault ships made their landing, the Separatists, using [[J-1 proton cannon]]s, proceeded to fire on the invasion [[fleet]], where they managed to break through the deflector shield of one of the assault ships, destroying one of its engines and and sending the damaged vessel plunging to the surface, which forced the remaining two to break off their descent, dispatching several [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i]] gunships to the surface to destroy the cannons.<ref name="Innocents">{{TCW|Innocents of Ryloth}}</ref>

Kenobi, leading the [[212th Attack Battalion]]'s [[Ghost Company]], soon managed to destroy the [[proton cannon]]s after navigating through the [[Twi'lek]] settlement of [[Nabat]], allowing the remaining ships to land on the surface, where the Republic would proceed to free Ryloth from the Confederacy's occupation.<ref name="Innocents" />

====Return to Geonosis====
[[File:RepublicInvasionForce-LaPR.png|thumb|left|250px|The Republic fleet arrives in the [[Geonosis system]].]]
Later in the [[21 BBY|second year]] of the war,<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> the Republic, having learned that the Confederacy, with help from the [[Trade Federation]] and the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]], were establishing a new [[Geonosis primary droid foundry|droid factory]] on Geonosis, returned to the [[desert]] world with a massive fleet of ten Acclamators and six Venators under the command of Generals Kenobi, [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]], [[Luminara Unduli]], Skywalker, and [[Jedi Commander]]s Tano and [[Barriss Offee]], in hopes of [[Second Battle of Geonosis|destroying]] the factory and retaking the planet from the Confederacy.<ref name="Landing">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref>

Exiting [[hyperspace]], the Acclamators made their descent on the planet's surface, where the Jedi Generals proceeded to board their gunships while a fleet of carriers delivered All Terrain Tactical Enforcers down to the surface,<ref name="Landing" /> which resulted in the costly destruction of the factory itself and the<ref name="Weapons Factory">{{TCW|Weapons Factory}}</ref> capture of [[Geonosian]] [[Archduke]] [[Poggle the Lesser]] by the end of the battle.<ref name="Legacy of Terror">{{TCW|Legacy of Terror}}</ref>

====Engagement on Saleucami====
[[File:Saleucami-TD.png|thumb|right|250px|Three Venators escort an Acclamator to Saleucami.]]
Sometime later, after Separatist forces under the command of [[Kaleesh]] Separatist General [[Grievous]] captured [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeth Koth]] aboard his [[Steadfast|Star Destroyer]], the Republic proceeded to dispatch Generals Kenobi and Skywalker, along with his fleet, to the planet [[Saleucami]], where Grievous and his fleet awaited their arrival, in hopes of [[Battle of Saleucami|rescuing]] General Koth from the Separatists. However, despite their successes in rescuing Koth from the enemy [[Grievous's Recusant-class light destroyer|flagship]], Grievous managed to escape his doomed vessel to the planet's surface,<ref name="Grievous Intrigue">{{TCW|Grievous Intrigue}}</ref> where an ''Acclamator''-class assault ship was sent to deploy Republic ground forces to locate and defeat the Kaleesh general. Once more, despite their efforts, Grievous managed to escape Saleucami and the Republic aboard a [[Sheathipede-class transport shuttle|''Sheathipede''-class transport shuttle]].<ref name="The Deserter">{{TCW|The Deserter}}</ref>

====Battles of Kamino and Sullust====
[[File:WelcomeToKamino-ARCT.png|thumb|left|250px|Clone troopers exit from an ''Acclamator'' before the Battle of Kamino.]]
Sometime later, the Republic discovered that the [[Kaleesh]] Separatist General [[Grievous]] and the [[Dark Acolyte]] [[Asajj Ventress]] were planning to launch a second [[Battle of Kamino|attack]] on the cloning planet of [[Kamino]]<ref name="ARC Troopers">{{TCW|ARC Troopers (episode)}}</ref> after their first attempts [[Defense of Rishi Station|failed]] earlier in the war,<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref> in hopes of destroying the cloning facilities to tip the scales of the war in favor of the Confederacy. In response, the [[501st Legion]], under Generals Skywalker and Kenobi left for Kamino, were deployed to the planet aboard an Acclamator to reinforce [[Tipoca City]] from the attack. The Acclamator later participated in the battle with the Republic's [[Kamino blockade|blockade]], where it engaged Grievous's fleet above the planet.<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

Later in the war, an ''Acclamator''-class assault ship fought alongside the fleet of Anakin Skywalker in the [[Battle of Sullust]] against the Separatist [[Asajj Ventress's fleet|fleet]] of Ventress, which resulted in the destruction of Skywalker's flagship, the ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer ''[[Resolute]]''.<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref>

====Battle of Umbara====
[[File:UmbaraSystem-DoU.jpg|thumb|right|250px|An Acclamator proceeds to land on Umbara.]]
After the [[Umbaran government|government]] of the planet [[Umbara]] seceded from the Republic<ref name="Darkness">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref> in [[20 BBY]],<ref name="20 BBY">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the [[Battle of Mon Cala]] and the [[Plan to kidnap Chancellor Palpatine|skirmish in Theed]] to [[20 BBY]]. As the episode this event takes place within takes place between the events of the episodes the other events are attached to according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, this event must also take place 20 BBY.</ref> following the [[death]] of [[Senator]] [[Mee Deechi]],<ref name="Senate Murders">{{TCW|Senate Murders}}</ref> who represented the planet in the [[Galactic Senate]], and aligned themselves with the Confederacy, which resulted in the establishment of a Separatist [[Umbara blockade|blockade]]. However, the blockade was later engaged by a fleet of three Venators and three Acclamators, which allowed one of the Acclamators to reach the planet's surface after punching a hole through the blockade, where it deployed ground forces to engage the native [[Umbaran Militia|Umbaran combatants]].<ref name="Darkness" />

====Action on Kiros and Naboo====
[[File:Acclamator_Kiros.jpg|thumb|left|250px|An Acclamator proceeds to land on Kiros.]]
Later on the planet [[Kiros]], Separatist forces under [[Zygerrian]] [[Darts D'Nar]] and Count Dooku [[Conquest of Kiros|arrived]] on the planet, where they proceeded to capture and transport all [[Togruta]] colonists to the planet [[Kadavo]]. However, not long after the Separatist invasion, Republic forces under Generals Kenobi and Skywalker arrived on the planet, where they utilized an ''Acclamator''-class assault ship to deploy ground forces [[Battle on Kiros|against]] the Separatist Droid Army.<ref name="Kidnapped">{{TCW|Kidnapped}}</ref>

Sometime later on [[Naboo]], Republic forces, with the intent of ensuring [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] protection against a plot devised by Separatist [[Head of State]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] to capture the Chancellor, dispatched Generals Windu and Skywalker, and his Padawan, alongside an ''Acclamator''-class assault ship to Naboo, which was ferrying several All Terrain Tactical Enforcers and squads of clone troopers.<ref name="Crisis on Naboo">{{TCW|Crisis on Naboo}}</ref>

====Relief mission to Aleen====
After a series of devastating [[groundquake]]s rocked the planet [[Aleen]], the Republic received a distress signal from the native [[Aleena]] who were requesting aid. As a result, Clone Commander [[Wolffe]] and the clones of [[104th Battalion]] were sent with a [[Wolffe's Acclamator-class assault ship|lone ''Acclamator''-class assault ship]] and three [[Pelta-class frigate|''Pelta''-class frigates]] to [[Mission to Aleen|deliver]] relief supplies and install new equipment in their communications center.<ref name="Mercy Mission">{{TCW|Mercy Mission (episode)}}</ref>

====Battle of Ringo Vinda====
Later in the [[19 BBY|final year]] of the war,<ref name="19 BBY">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the [[trial of Ahsoka Tano]] and the [[mission to Utapau]] to [[19 BBY]]. As the episode this event takes place within takes place between the events of the episodes the other events are attached to according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, this event must take place in 19 BBY.</ref> Republic forces under the command of Generals Skywalker, [[Tiplar]] and [[Tiplee]], engaged Separatist forces led by [[Harch]] Admiral [[Trench]] at the planet [[Ringo Vinda]]. There, the Republic utilized a blockade from the [[Open Circle Fleet]] consisted of twenty ''Venator''-class Star Destroyers, an [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]], three [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|''Consular''-class cruisers]] and a single ''Acclamator''-class assault ship against Trench's fleet, while the Jedi and their clone forces engaged the Separatist Droid Army on the [[Ringo Vinda space station]].<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref>

====Action in the Ruusan system====
Sometime during the war, Republic and Separatist forces [[Battle in the Ruusan system|fought]] in the [[Ruusan system]]. The Republic's forces, which consisted of at least one Venator, one Acclamator, and at least two [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]]s, fought against at least one [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4">[[Bounty Hunters 4|''Bounty Hunters'' 4]]</ref> Around [[3 ABY]] during the [[Imperial Era]],<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4 Date">The events of [[Bounty Hunters 3|''Bounty Hunters'' 3]] take place shortly after the the [[Escape from Cloud City]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]]. Therefore, the events of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] and the [[Bounty Hunters 4|following issue]] take place in approximately the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the [[Nautolan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Nakano Lash]] hid amongst the wreckage of the battle.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4" />

===Age of the Empire===
By the time of the Imperial Era, the ''Acclamator''-class assault ship was considered to be an old model.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> The wreckage of an Acclamator was left in the [[Wastelands of Kullgroon]].<ref name="Double or Nothing 2">[[Lando - Double or Nothing 2|''Lando - Double or Nothing'' 2]]</ref> Despite the ship model's age, at least one, the ''[[Lodestar]]'', was still active during the [[Galactic Civil War]], serving as the [[flagship]] of [[New Republic]] [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]]'s [[Barma Battle Group]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Acclamator-conceptart.jpg|thumb|180px|Concept art of the ''Acclamator''-class assault ship.]]
The ''Acclamator''-class transgalactic military assault ship first appeared in the [[2002]] episodic film, [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="Episode II" /> Initially referred to as the "Jedi troop transport," the triangular shape of the starship was intended to be a nod to the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyers]] of the [[original trilogy]].<ref name="SW:DB">{{DB|starship|republicassaultship|Republic assault ship}}</ref> They were later identified in the [[2015]] [[canon]] reference book, ''[[Star Wars: Ships of the Galaxy]]'', authored by [[Benjamin Harper]].<ref name="SOTG" />
