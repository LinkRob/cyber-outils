'''Belsmuth Consolidations Ltd.''' was a subsidiary of [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]] that manufactured the ''[[Razor Crest]]'', a ship flown by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]].<ref name="MandoVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Belsmuth Consolidations Ltd. was first mentioned in the [[2022]] non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Awesome Vehicles]]''.<ref name="Vehicles">''[[LEGO Star Wars Awesome Vehicles]]''</ref> In the current canon, the ''Razor Crest'' was manufactured by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]], according to ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> Later, the [[2026]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'' revealed Belsmuth Consolidations was a subsidiary of [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]].<ref name="MandoVG" />
