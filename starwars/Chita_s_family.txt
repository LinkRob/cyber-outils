{{Quote|To tell the truth, the Empire also made a mess of my planet and my family right along with it.|Chita|<ref name="Smuggler" />}}
The [[human]] [[Chita]] was a member of a '''family''' on a [[Chita's homeworld|planet]]<ref name="Smuggler" /> at some point between [[19 BBY]] and [[0 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{Visions|The Smuggler}} establishes that the [[Galactic Empire]] made a mess of [[Chita]]'s [[Chita's homeworld|planet]] and family before the events of the short. The short features the [[Great Temple]] inhabited by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the formation of the Galactic Empire and the [[Evacuation of Yavin 4]] to [[19 BBY]] and [[0 ABY]] respectively.</ref>
