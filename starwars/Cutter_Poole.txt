'''Cutter Poole''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who was a new recruit into [[Blade Squadron]], a [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing]] unit. [[Lieutenant]] [[Gina Moonsong]] considered him to be a "glory boy." Shortly after his induction, he was [[Death|killed]] during the [[Battle of Malastare (Galactic Civil War)|battle]] of [[Malastare]] when a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] got behind his craft.<ref name="Zero Hour" />
