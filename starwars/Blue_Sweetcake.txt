'''Blue Sweetcakes''' were a type of [[sweetcake]] that was served during the Decadent Finale course of the Taste Around [[the galaxy|the Galaxy]] dinner for those passengers seated at the [[captain's table]] in the [[Crown of Corellia Dining Room]] on the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''<ref name="Cruiser" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The [[Foodstuff|dessert]] was safe for [[human]]s to consume, and was often served alongside a variety of [[cookie]]s.<ref name="Cruiser" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
Blue Sweetcakes were [[Wikipedia:Macaron|macarons]] available in the [[In-universe|real world]] at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]], Florida,<ref name="Cruiser" /> which opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref> It was last served at the hotel during the [[September 28]][[September 29|&ndash;]][[September 30|30]] voyage of [[2023]], the final voyage before the closure of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser.<ref name="Official website">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/overview|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Overview}}</ref> The dessert was served during dinner on the second night of the voyage for guests who had purchased seating at the Captain's table. They bore a resemblance to the [[blue macaron]]s featured in the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''. The menu described the dessert as "Petite Blue Macaron with Sea Salt [[Wikipedia:Caramel|Caramel]]."<ref name="WDWMag">{{WebCite|url=https://www.wdw-magazine.com/review-the-captains-table-at-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=REVIEW: The Captain's Table at Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|author=Danny Shuster|date=2022-07-26|work=[https://www.wdw-magazine.com WDW Magazine]|archivedate=20240613063848}}</ref> 

Although the menu description of this dessert calls it a blue macaron, the inconsistency of menu descriptions aboard the Galactic Starcruiser being in-universe or out-of-universe terms leads to ambiguity for whether Blue Sweetcakes are, at least [[in-universe]], a type of blue macaron or are something distinct. This article assumes the latter due to sea salt caramel being a clearly out-of-universe term.
