'''''Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege'' 3''' is the third issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Battle of Jakku — Republic Under Siege]]''. The issue was written by [[Alex Segura]], illustrated by [[Stefano Raffaele]] and [[Alex Sinclair]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 4]], 2024.

==Publisher's summary==
''A STARTLING REVELATION! An assassination on Chandrila! LANDO takes the offensive against the consolidated Imperial forces &mdash; but is it too late? A hero falls at the hands of a deadly bounty hunter! A mysterious killer stalks the Imperial leader &mdash; but can they get past his defenses?''

==Plot summary==
At a [[Kijimi]] headquarters of the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]], the leader [[Zeva Bliss]] speaks with [[Tomasso]] about her skepticism of how their alliance with rogue [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] has been evolving. Tomasso concurs, adding that if Adelhard's power grab grows too much, the Spice Runners may no longer benefit. Zeva shelves the issue and calls in Tomasso's informant, [[Weazel]]. The informant warns Bliss that the threat facing the Spice Runners is larger than she imagines. He says that Adelhard is playing all the sides, working with the [[Acolytes of the Beyond]] and making contracts in the criminal underworld. One such plan he reveals is that Adelhard has enlisted two [[bounty hunter]]s to [[death|kill]] [[Luke Skywalker]]. Bliss dismisses the man and reveals to him that it's she who owns that very contract. On their way out, Tomasso and Weazel discuss how the revelation of the bounty hunters working for the Spice Runners was a surprise to Tomasso. He asks Weazel to call on [[New Republic]] intelligence [[agent]] [[Calder Behrens]] to inform him of the threat facing Skywalker and who could protect the [[Jedi]]. 

In a massive brawl of fisticuffs at [[Hamix Outpost]], on the fringes of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]], the [[cyborg]] [[Beilert Valance]] throws punches amidst the crowd. Hardly looking up, he starts conversing with Behrens, who just entered. He suspects the agent is there to ask for help against the [[Shadow Council]], but Behrens has another reason to call on the cyborg. 

In an Imperial stronghold, the [[Exterior]], Grand Moff [[Randd]] speaks with a shadowy [[hologram]] of the Shadow Council's [[spy|mole]] in Adelhard's ranks. He activates their mole to make their move against the rogue [[governor]]. The contact is wary of acting too early against Adelhard, given his tactical skills and having been already tipped off about a traitor in his ranks. 

In the New Republic provisional [[capital]] [[Chandrila]], someone observes Luke Skywalker and his [[Reproduction|pregnant]] sister [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] via [[binoculars]]. The two siblings discuss an encrypted message from Behrens concerning more allies Adelhard has added into his cause. They are interrupted by [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]], who just spoke to Behrens and now carries an urgent warning. Before she can share it, Luke senses imminent danger through [[the Force]]. 

At that moment, the lurking Imperial behind the screen sends in the ferocious [[Trandoshan]] bounty hunter [[Kranor]], armed with an [[E-5C heavy blaster rifle]] and an [[electro sword]], to burst through the window. Luke immediately ignites [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber|his lightsaber]] and assumes a defensive position. The Trandoshan taunts the Jedi and starts to slash at Luke, knocking him back before aiming his [[blaster]] at the man. A skillful shot from Leia strikes the attacker and disarms him of his electro sword. Luke takes the opportunity to slice at the bounty hunter, disarming him of his blaster too. Luke orders the Trandoshan to reveal his identity and employer, but instead the bounty hunter lurches at Mothma and Organa. He punches Mothma in the face before Leia is able to get another shot at the [[assassin]]. Luke slashes at the Trandoshan again, and the attacker announces that the message has been sent that no one is safe before leaping out the window. 

Aboard Adelhard's [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] [[Scepter (Imperial-class)|''Scepter'']], the Grand Moff is stealthily approached by a shadowy figure brandishing a sharp [[dagger]]. At the last moment, Adelhard registers the threat and catches [[Reyna Oskure|Reyna Oskure's]] wrist. He angrily demands to know what is going on, and the mystic gleefully compliments the man's reflexes. He calls out the betrayal of the Acolytes, as well as from the other factions. He asks her if she will act of her own accord and join his cause in earnest before pulling her in for a kiss. 

In [[Krassinique]], capital [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Krassis]], Calder Behrens calls Mon Mothma about another would-be recuited [[senator]] who has been assassinated, [[Galward Paronis]]. Standing over the late senator's corpse, he details what he knows about the murder and emphasizes the urgency of their collaboration with Beilert Valance. Their conversation is interrupted by Behrens being fatally shot by the [[Arcona]] bounty hunter [[Lan-Drus]].
