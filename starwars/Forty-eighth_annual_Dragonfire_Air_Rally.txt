The '''forty-eighth annual Dragonfire Air Rally''' was an instance of the [[Dragonfire Air Rally]] [[airspeeder]] race that took place on [[Coruscant]], sponsored by [[Daystar Entertainment]]. During the race, [[Ramil]] of [[Serenno]] was [[Attack at the Dragonfire Air Rally|sabotaged]] by the [[crime lord]] [[Cenevax]] so that her [[gambling]] ring could earn profits from those who bet on him. After Ramil's [[airspeeder]] crashed, [[Thrar]] of [[Coachelle Prime]] won the race.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
