The '''Byrnum Maw''' was a [[nebula]] located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]] that was [[Formation of the Byrnum Maw|formed]] through a dying [[Unidentified star (Byrnum Maw)|star's]] explosion [[Standard century|centuries]] before [[40 BBY]]. In the [[Standard year|years]] following its formation, the [[hyperspace route]]s surrounding the nebula were disrupted and rendered unusable. Despite [[Galactic Republic]] [[scientist]]s' best efforts, their attempts to [[Star map|chart]] a path through the Byrnum Maw proved unsuccessful.

In 40 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], scientists working for the Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Kirames Kaj]] discovered that it was possible to use [[hyperspace anchor]]s on the [[planet]] [[Pijal]]&mdash;a world near the nebula's center&mdash;and its [[Pijal's moon|moon]] to stabilize a central section of the area for new hyperspace routes to be established. Afterward, Kaj signed off on the [[Governance Treaty]] initiative drafted by Pijal's [[lord]] [[regent]]&mdash;[[Rael Averross]]&mdash;and [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] officials, which the world's Queen [[Lamia]] eventually signed after the refusal of her predecessor, Queen [[Fanry]]. Afterward, the Republic established the [[Pijal Hyperspace Corridor]], restoring hyperspace traffic to the regions affected by the Byrnum Maw's formation.

==Description==
The Byrnum Maw was a [[nebula]] located in the regions known as the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="MA" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It surrounded the [[planet]] [[Pijal]] and its [[Pijal system|system]],<ref name="MA" /> and situated in grid square L-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[hyperspace route]] known as the [[Pijal Hyperspace Corridor]] was established and provided a path through the nebula.<ref name="MA" />

==History==
{{Quote|&hellip;scientists have determined that two hyperspace anchors&mdash;one on the planet Pijal, and one on its moon&mdash;could together generate a field capable of stabilizing a central section of the maw. With that stabilized, several traffic pathways become possible. The worlds on those routes have been left behind, shut out of progress, for far too long. Now that can all change.|Supreme Chancellor Kirames Kaj|audio=Pijal2.flac|<ref name="MA" />}}
[[File:Pijal-SWT.jpg|thumb|left|280px|The planet Pijal was situated near the center of the Byrnum Maw.]]
[[Standard century|Centuries]] before 40 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a dying [[Unidentified star (Byrnum Maw)|star]] exploded and [[Formation of the Byrnum Maw|formed]] the Byrnum Maw. The [[hyperspace]] routes connecting the surrounding region of [[Realspace|space]] were disrupted by the nebula's emergence, rendering them unusable. After the event, [[Galactic Republic]] [[scientist]]s attempted to find a stable path through the nebula to restore hyperspace traffic, but their attempts proved fruitless. Over [[time]], the Byrnum Maw's disruption of nearby hyperspace routes isolated many worlds from the wider [[The galaxy|galaxy]], and at some point, the [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] began exerting heavier influence over the region as the latter's economic prosperity dwindled. As [[Standard year|years]] passed, several worlds affected by the Byrnum Maw's formation, including one near its center, Pijal, maintained sparing contact with the Republic in the hopes that pathways through the nebula would someday be [[Star map|charted]].<ref name="MA" />

[[File:Republic_Emblem.svg|thumb|right|195px|Scientists working for the Galactic Republic (emblem pictured) discovered a way to use hyperspace anchors to chart a path through the Byrnum Maw.]]
In 40 BBY,<ref name="Date" /> scientists working under the Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Kirames Kaj]] discovered that placing a [[hyperspace anchor]] on both Pijal and its [[Pijal's moon|moon]] could stabilize a portion of the Byrnum Maw, which would allow the establishment of several new hyperspace routes. The planet's [[lord]] [[regent]], the [[Jedi]] [[Rael Averross]], worked with Czerka officials to draft the [[Governance Treaty]]&mdash;an initiative that would strip the [[Pijali monarchy]] of its authority and grant it to a [[Democracy|democratic]] [[Assembly (Pijal)|assembly]] that would be able to make legally binding agreements with Republic officials.<ref name="MA" />

Kaj signed off on the initiative, hoping to reconnect [[Star system|systems]] near the Byrnum Maw with other Republic worlds. The Supreme Chancellor sent [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], on a [[mission to Pijal]] to ratify the Governance Treaty after its signing. While briefing them in her [[Office of the Chancellor|office]], Kaj informed the pair of their mission objectives, including the context that a path had been discovered through the nebula. However, the [[Pijali]] [[Queen]] [[Fanry]] refused to sign the Governance Treaty, ultimately leaving its ratification to her successor, Queen [[Lamia]]. Afterward, the Republic established the Pijal Hyperspace Corridor through the Byrnum Maw in order to restore hyperspace traffic to the region.<ref name="MA" />
