The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''4 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''BBY 4''','''<ref name="One Year Later" />''' as '''3273 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7973''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*The [[Galactic Empire]] [[Empire Day|celebrates]] its fifteenth anniversary.<ref name="Empire Day" />
*After obtaining a [[kyber crystal]] from the [[Lothal Jedi Temple]], [[Ezra Bridger]] constructs [[Ezra Bridger's first lightsaber|his first lightsaber]].<ref name="POTJ">{{Rebels|Path of the Jedi}}</ref>
*Kanan Jarrus is [[Raid on the communications tower|captured]] by [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]].<ref name="CTA">{{Rebels|Call to Action}}</ref>
*The [[Grand Inquisitor]] is killed after a duel against Jedi [[Kanan Jarrus]].<ref name="FATG">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref>
*The [[Maya Pei Brigade]] is [[Ambush of the Maya Pei Brigade|attacked]] over [[Yavin 4]]. [[Maya Pei]] herself is presumed killed.<ref name="One Year Later" />
*[[Cassian Jeron Andor]] steals the [[TIE Avenger prototype|TIE Avenger]] prototype from [[Sienar Test Facility 73]].<ref name="One Year Later" />
*A gathering of [[Imperial officer]]s, including [[Director of Advanced Weapons Research|Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]], [[Major]] [[Lio Partagaz]] and Lieutenants [[Dedra Meero]] and [[Moy]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]], [[Admiral]] [[Daysar]] of the [[Imperial Navy]] and representives from the [[Imperial Department of Military Research]] and [[Science and Engineering Division|Engineering Division]], as well as [[Dee Shambo]] and [[Nisus Osar]] of the [[Ministry of Enlightenment]], [[Ghorman project|confer]] at the [[Maltheen Divide]] regarding the planet [[Ghorman]] and [[kalkite]].<ref name="One Year Later" /> 
*[[Leida Mothma]] is [[Wedding of Stekan Sculdun and Leida Mothma|married]] to [[Stekan Sculdun]] on [[Chandrila]].<ref name="Harvest">{{Andor|Harvest}}</ref>
*[[Wilmon Paak]] and [[Bix Caleen]] are picked up from [[Mina-Rau]] by Cassian Andor in his stolen TIE Avenger.<ref name="Harvest" />
*[[Minister]] [[Maketh Tua]] is killed during the [[Siege of Lothal]].<ref name="Siege of Lothal">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''</ref>
*[[R5-D4]] is captured by [[Jawa]]s.<ref name="The Red One">{{StoryCite|story=The Red One|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>
