'''Blade Leader''' was the [[callsign]] attributed to the lead [[pilot]] of the [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] [[Blade Squadron]]. During the [[Battle of Endor|battle]] of [[Endor]], [[Wing Commander]] [[Adon Fox]] served as Blade Leader until his [[death]] during the battle.<ref name="BS" /> After the battle's success, the [[human]] male [[Braylen Stramm]] was given the title.<ref name="BS: Zero Hour">{{InsiderCite|160|Blade Squadron: Zero Hour}}</ref>
