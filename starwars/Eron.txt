'''Eron''' was a [[Gender|female]] individual active during the [[Imperial Era]]. Around [[2 BBY]], they and their female partner [[Rhee]] became aunts to the [[Youngling|child]] [[Laina]] following her [[Reproduction|birth]] to the civilian [[Ryland]] and the [[human]] [[Fiona]]. In [[1 BBY]], Ryland&mdash;who was now serving in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]&mdash;asked the couple to take Laina into their care, as he wished for her to grow up somewhere safe. Eron and Rhee agreed, intending to raise the child on the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]].

==Biography==
[[File:Alderaan.jpg||thumb|left|230px|Eron intended to live with Laina on Alderaan (pictured), hoping it would be safe to reside there.]]
The [[Gender|woman]] Eron lived during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and maintained an affiliation with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. They<ref name="Laina" /> and their female partner [[Rhee]]<ref name="Tumblr" /> became aunts to the [[Youngling|child]] [[Laina]] after her [[Reproduction|birth]] to the civilian [[Ryland]] and the [[human]] [[Fiona]]<ref name="Laina" /> around [[2 BBY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[Laina (short story)|Laina]]" occur in the [[Standard day|days]] prior to the events of {{Film|IV}}, placing the story in [[1 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. As [[Ryland]] and [[Laina]]'s escape from the [[Unidentified airless moon (Outer Rim)|moon]] took place roughly a [[Standard year|year]] and a half prior to the short story, the escape must have been around [[2 BBY]]. As Laina is nearly two years old during the story's events, her [[Reproduction|birth]] can also be dated to around 2 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> after the [[Imperial officer]] [[Duggan]] [[Death|killed]] Fiona, Eron and Rhee helped Laina and Ryland flee from their home [[Unidentified underground mining colony|mining colony]] on an [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Unidentified airless moon (Outer Rim)|moon]]. Afterward, Ryland joined the Rebel Alliance after being introduced to the organization by the women.<ref name="Laina" />

While residing in an Alliance [[Great Temple|military base]] on<ref name="Laina" /> the [[moon]]<ref name="Film IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[Yavin 4]]<ref name="Laina" /> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Ryland asked Eron and Rhee to take Laina into their care to grow up somewhere safer than the base. The pair agreed, intending to raise the young girl on the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. The [[Local day|night]] of Laina's departure, Ryland recorded a message for his daughter to view when she was older, promising the child that she would be well-loved and cherished by her aunts and that they would tell her more about her origins someday. Not long after, Laina boarded the [[transport]] ''[[Echo Delta One]]'' to leave Yavin and meet Rhee and Eron at her new home.<ref name="Laina" /> However, within [[Standard day|days]], Alderaan was [[Destruction of Alderaan|annihilated]] by the Galactic Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]].<ref name="Film IV" />

==Personality and traits==
Eron was aligned with the Rebel Alliance during the [[Galactic Civil War]] and introduced Ryland to the organization after helping him flee his home colony. They cherished their niece, Laina, and were willing to care for her at Ryland's request by raising her somewhere peaceful alongside Rhee,<ref name="Laina" /> their cherished partner.<ref name="Tumblr" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|They were written to be a couple. Where I come from, "Auntie" and "Uncle" don't necessarily reflect blood relations, and more frequently reflect relationships. Laina's aunts love each other, and her, very much.|Wil Wheaton, about Eron and Rhee's relationship|<ref name="Tumblr" />}}
Eron was mentioned in "[[Laina (short story)|Laina]]," a short story written for the [[2017]] ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]'' anthology collection that was authored by [[Wil Wheaton]].<ref name="Laina" /> Wheaton confirmed Eron and Rhee were in a relationship in a [[Wikipedia:Tumblr|Tumblr]] post, indicating his authorial intent for the aunts to be written as a couple.<ref name="Tumblr" />
