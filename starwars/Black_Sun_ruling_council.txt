The '''Black Sun ruling council'''<ref name="Ziton Moj">{{SWE|characters|ziton-moj|Ziton Moj}}</ref> was a cabal of [[Falleen]] [[noble]]s that controlled the [[Black Sun]] crime syndicate during the [[Clone Wars]]. It operated out of a [[Black Sun fortress|fortress]] located on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]].<ref name="Black Sun" /> Led by [[Xomit Grunseit]],<ref name="Xomit Grunseit">{{Databank|xomit-grunseit|Xomit Grunseit}}</ref> the [[crime lord]]s<ref name="Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/shades-of-reason-trivia-gallery|text="Shades of Reason" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> were [[Death|executed]] by [[Savage Opress]] when they refused to align themselves with [[Maul]]'s [[Shadow Collective]]<ref name="Eminence" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> Maul then installed their [[lieutenant]],<ref name="Ziton Moj" /> [[Captain of the Guard]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> [[Ziton Moj]], as leader of the Black Sun.<ref name="Ziton Moj" />
