The '''Citadel''', also known as '''Citadel Station''', was a [[prison]] constructed on the [[planet]] [[Lola Sayu]] by the [[Galactic Republic]] to hold rogue [[Jedi]]. Early in the [[Clone Wars]], it was captured by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Osi Sobeck]] became its [[warden]]. The prison held [[Jedi Master]] [[Even Piell]] and several officers which included [[Captain]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] where they were interrogated for the coordinates of the Nexus hyperspace route. They were rescued however by a team of Jedi and [[clone trooper]]s who were forced to contact the Republic to send a [[fleet]] to the planet after their only means of escape was destroyed. During the [[Imperial Era|Imperial era]], the Citadel was home to a [[Separatist holdout (Citadel)|Separatist holdout]].

==Description==
Built on the [[planet]] [[Lola Sayu]], the tower-like Citadel sat on a bridge between two [[Color|yellow]] [[Magma|lava]] lakes and was powered by geothermal energy. The [[prison]]'s structure was widely believed to be impregnable, with its front section sticking out over the lava, the back giving out to the prison's landing field, which was protected by fences and security emplacements.<ref name="BMF 75">{{BuildFalconCite|75|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> The outer walls of the Citadel were laced with hundreds of [[Electro-mine|electromines]],<ref name="TC" /> which, when combined with the planet's high winds, prevented infiltrations by pushing would-be infiltrators into the lava lakes below.<ref name="BMF 75" /> Searchlights could pick out anyone attempting to enter or exit the prison, and every door along the outer wall was protected by a [[ray shield]].<ref name="TC" />

Within the Citadel was a labyrinth of identical hallways and corridors. Each hallway was protected by electrified walls, magnetic ceilings and floors, rapidly closing [[blast door]]s, [[camera]]s, and automatic [[laser]]s. All security equipment was monitored from the central control room located at the top of the prison tower. Outside the prison was a landing platform, which was protected by two manned [[turret]]s.<ref name="Counterattack" />

==History==
===Republic Era===
{{Quote|The Citadel wasn't designed to hold common criminals, it was created to hold Jedi if any of us lost our way.|Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="The Citadel" />}}
Constructed in [[522 BBY]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|the-citadel|The Citadel}}</ref>, the Citadel was originally designed by the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="BMF 75" /> to hold rogue or [[Dark Jedi|dark]] [[Jedi]].<ref name="TC" /><ref name="BMF 75" /> In 520 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="520 BBY">{{BuildFalconCite|75|Guide to the Galaxy}} states that it was five [[Standard century|centuries]] before the [[Battle of Lola Sayu]] since the last [[Jedi]] was imprisoned within the [[Citadel]]. As the Battle of Lola Sayu takes place in 1 BFE according to ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'', which corresponds to [[20 BBY]] as established by ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', it can be deduced that it had been since approximately [[520 BBY]] since the last Jedi was held in the Citadel.</ref> the Citadel housed a rogue [[Unidentified rogue Jedi (Citadel)|Jedi]] who was imprisoned there.<ref name="BMF 75" /> Between [[82 BBY]] and [[68 BBY]], there was a rumor circulating amongst the Jedi that the [[Jedi Council]] opened a new correctional unit at the Citadel for those that had foresight. Following this, [[Jedi Master]] [[Lene Kostana]] refused to tell the Jedi Council that the [[Force vision]]s plaguing Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Sifo-Dyas]] grew worse than when then Jedi Padawan Dooku and Dyas last met in the [[Mission to Asusto|mission]] to the Outer Rim planet [[Asusto]], in fear that Dyas would be locked up at the Citadel. At some point, Kostana met with Dooku at a [[hangar]] located in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[Core Worlds]] planet [[Coruscant]] after Dooku had a sparring session with Jedi [[Rael Averross]]. While talking to Dooku, she told him about the rumor. Dooku did not believe the rumor but promised to not cause Dyas any "distress" over the matter.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> By the [[time]] of the Clone Wars, the [[Jedi Archives]] were known to have information on the original layout of the facility, but this was considered extremely old and outdated at the time.<ref name="TC" />

===Clone Wars===
{{Quote|As you are aware, the Citadel is their most isolated and impenetrable detention facility.|[[Plo Koon]]|<ref name="TC" />}}
During the [[Clone Wars]], the citadel fell under control of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="TC" /> It was run by [[warden]] [[Osi Sobeck]] who was considered to be sadistic.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Under Separatist control, the Citadel became known as the Confederacy's most isolated holding facility that held high value [[Prisoner|detainees]]. The prison was heavily guarded by a number of [[droid]]s including [[BX-series droid commando]]s, and it had hundreds of electromines. The [[clone trooper]] training facilities in [[Tipoca City]] on the [[extragalactic]] planet [[Kamino]] used a [[Citadel Challenge]] training course named after the prison.<ref name="Citadel Rescue Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/citadel-rescue-trivia-gallery|text="Citadel Rescue" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref>

[[File:OOMSecurityBattleDroid-Counterattack.png|thumb|201x201px|A [[OOM-series security droid|security battle droid]] at the Citadel.]]
In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|318}}</ref> [[Jedi General]] Even Piell and his [[Republic officer|officers]] were [[Capture of Even Piell|captured]]. Piell was then incarcerated and [[torture]]d for information regarding the [[Nexus Route]] in the Citadel, prompting the Jedi Council to send an elite rescue team led by Jedi [[General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] to free him. After the team snuck past the life-form sensors employed by the Separatist fleet guarding the planet, they landed just outside the Citadel using a stolen [[Sheathipede-class transport shuttle|''Sheathipede''-class transport shuttle]]. They began to scale a steep cliff-face dotted with electromines, but when [[clone trooper]] "[[Charger]]" slipped and fell on one of the mines resulting in him [[Death|dying]], this alerted the Separatists of their presence. The task force nevertheless managed to infiltrate the prison and rescue Piell, [[Captain]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], as well as several [[Clone navigation officer|Clone naval officers]] and troopers.<ref name="TC" />

When they prepared to rendezvous with the evacuation team that consisted of [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] and a group of reprogrammed Separatist [[battle droid]]s, a firefight broke out on the landing pad. Republic [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Echo|CT-1409]], "Echo" was presumed [[Death|dead]] when a [[BX-series droid commando]] blew up the Republic's stolen [[shuttle]] on the platform outside the facility. During the Jedi's escape with their [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s across Lola Sayu's unstable terrain, a group of [[anooba]]s and droids led by Sobeck attacked them. General Piell was fatally injured by one of the anoobas, but Piell managed to whisper his half of the Nexus Route information to Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] before succumbing to his injuries. Sobeck was later killed by Ahsoka Tano while attempting to throw Tarkin into a [[Magma|lava]] gorge. The group managed to reach the rendezvous point where they were extracted from the planet by a Republic [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]] [[gunship]].<ref name="CR">{{TCW|Citadel Rescue}}</ref>

===A Separatist holdout and New Republic era===
After the Clone Wars, a [[Unidentified droid (Citadel)|droid]] led a [[Separatist holdout (Citadel)|Separatist holdout]] at the Citadel. However, the droid leading the [[garrison]] of [[battle droid]]s malfunctioned and instead followed its last directive from the previous war. It had adjusted its programming for a wider target range and targeted all [[Force-sensitive]]s. The droid eventually came to have a [[Unidentified Force-sensitive child (Citadel)|Force-sensitive youngling]] in the prison. This caused a [[Battle at the Citadel|battle]] where a Jedi Warrior managed to free the child.<ref name="Knights of Fate" /> By [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date2">{{MandoDate|14|the appearance of Unga Toba's profile}}</ref> [[Unga Toba]] served a twenty-five [[Standard year|year]] sentence presumably at the citadel for unlawfully accessing a computer.<ref name="Mando14">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 14: The Tragedy}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:The_Citadel_concept_art.png|thumb|left|175px|Character illustration of the Citadel by Tara Rueping]]
The location first appeared in [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]] [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season's]] eighteenth episode "[[The Citadel (episode)|The Citadel]]" which aired on [[February 18]], [[2011]].<ref name="TC" /> The design illustration of the Citadel was done by design and concept artist [[Tara Rueping]].<ref name="The Citadel Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-citadel-concept-art-gallery|text="The Citadel" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> The appearance of the Citadel was meant to be visually similar to the [[Death Star]]'s surface when seen from afar<ref name="The Citadel Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-citadel-trivia-gallery|text="The Citadel" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> and the animation sequence when Jedi Masters Obi-Wan Kenobi and Even Piell emerge from the ventilation shaft was originally shot on top of the Citadel tower however it was later moved with minimal pickups to the landing area behind the tower. The monitoring devices lining the hallways of the Citadel were designed from similar looking devices aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|DS-1 Orbital Battle Station]] in the movie, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope]].<ref name="Counterattack Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/counterattack-trivia-gallery|text="Counterattack" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref>
