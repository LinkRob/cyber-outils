'''Drongar''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="CYD Maz" /> located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]], positioned at the coordinates T-5<ref name="TFABG" /> on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Rebel Dossier">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]''</ref> A [[Unidentified smuggler (Modrol)|smuggler]] grew up on Drongar and, prior to the [[First Order-Resistance War]], used to climb the [[tree]]s there.<ref name="CYD Maz" /> [[Padawan]] [[Barriss Offee]] was assigned on her first [[Mission on Drongar|single mission]] to Drongar and came into contact with the [[dark side of the Force]] when her [[Force-sensitive|Force sensitivity]] was drastically elevated by a natural [[plant]] extract.<ref name="English Ahsoka and Others">{{EncyclopediaCite|Ahsoka Tano and Other Users of the Force}} (English version)</ref>
