'''''Elee & Me''''' is a [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] children's book that was published on [[November 3]], [[2020]] by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]. It tells the story of [[Elee]], a [[therii]], and her friend [[Salju]], who live in [[Black Spire Outpost]]. The book was written by [[Amy Ratcliffe]] and illustrated by [[Anna Daviscourt]].<ref name="D23" />

{{Book-stub}}

==Plot summary==
One [[Standard day|day]] Salju's [[The galaxy|galaxy]]-hopping [[Parent|father]], [[Harta]], brought her a gift upon his return-- a tiny boar-like creature called a therii, which she named Elee. Salju took Elee to the local [[Creature Stall]] in Black Spire Outpost’s marketplace, where its owner [[Bina]] helped her figure out how to take care of her new pet. After that, the two became inseparable, and Salju and Elee romped around Batuu and had adventures, all while Elee grew to enormous proportions. As Elee continued to grow, Salju began taking her to the distant [[Surabat River Valley]] to play. There, they discovered other therii, who welcomed them like family. After Elee grew so large that she knocked over an oil drum, Salju took her friend to live with her fellow therii in the River Valley.

==Continuity==
''Elee & Me'' depicts [[Salju]] owning [[Porg]] dolls on [[Batuu]]. However, ''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge]]'' established that Porgs weren't brought to Batuu until [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Tales">''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge]]'' tales place in [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>

==Behind the Scenes==
Elee was conceived for an unbuilt, third attraction for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] often informally referred to as "the [[Bantha]] ride", where guests could ride a full-sized animatronic Elee around Black Spire Outpost, a concept which was eventually dropped for budgetary reasons.<ref name="Blog Mickey">{{WebCite|url=https://blogmickey.com/2021/04/scrapped-plans-for-kalikori-club-restaurant-bounty-hunter-ride-and-more-revealed-for-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Scrapped Plans for "Kalikori Club" Restaurant, Bounty Hunter Ride, and More Revealed for Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|archivedate=20210423152621}}</ref>
