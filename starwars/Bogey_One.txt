'''Bogey One''' was the name given to a [[warship]] of the [[Grysk Hegemony]] by the crew of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]''. It was nearly as big as the Star Destroyer. Along with Bogeys [[Bogey Two|Two]], [[Bogey Three|Three]] and [[Bogey Four|Four]] Bogey One joined a [[Battle against Grysk forces|battle]] against ''Chimaera'', during which it was attacked by the Star Destroyer itself.<ref name="Alliances" />
