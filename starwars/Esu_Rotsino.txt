'''Esu Rotsino''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Politics|politician]] who served within the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] as [[senator]] for her [[homeworld]], the [[agriworld]] [[Abrion Major]], prior to the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]. Following the war's beginning, Rotsino directed Abrion Major and the other [[Planet|worlds]] of the [[Abrion sector]] in its secession from the Republic to the Confederacy, as a result of inequitable trade and stifling bureaucracy.<ref name="Rise" />

With their defection, Rotsino continued to serve as senator for the [[sector]] in the [[Separatist Senate]]. Furthermore, the senator's directed secession of the Abrion sector allowed the Confederacy to utilize its supply of [[foodstuff]]s in order to convince starving [[star system]]s into joining them.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Esu Rotsino was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Esu Rotsino/Legends|Esu Rotsino]] was introduced in the [[HoloNet News (original website)|HoloNet News]] article "[[HoloNet News Vol. 531 52]],"<ref name="HNN">{{HoloNetNewsWeb|52|news/13418_1.html|Abrion Sector Secedes; Separatists Get Agri-Worlds}}</ref> authored by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[Paul Ens]]<ref name="Credits">{{HoloNetNewsWeb||credits.html|Credits}}</ref> and published on [[April 18]], [[2002]].<ref name="HNN52 date">{{JCFCite|url=posts/6493893|board=Literature|thread=The Official HoloNetNews Discussion Thread|user=IAmTheDarkSide|date=2002-04-18|time=6:27 PM|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20200305125429/https://boards.theforce.net/threads/the-official-holonetnews-discussion-thread.5990764/page-13}}</ref> The character's name is a [[tuckerization]] of [[Sue Rostoni]], the executive editor at [[Lucas Licensing]] at the time.<ref name="Sue Rostoni blog">{{Blog|listing=true|eeusuestornii}}</ref>
