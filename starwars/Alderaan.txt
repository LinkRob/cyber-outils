{{Quote|The galaxy knows Alderaan as 'the planet of beauty.' Nature, poetry, philosophy, art, couture, cuisine&mdash;we freely share all with all.|Bail Organa|<ref name="Leia 2" />}}
'''Alderaan''', located in the [[Core Worlds]], was a terrestrial [[planet]] covered with [[mountain]]s. During the waning [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], it was ruled by [[Alderaanian monarchy|Queen]] [[Breha Organa]] and represented in the [[Galactic Senate]] by her husband, [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]]. Alderaan was regarded for its natural beauty, its tranquility, and the sophistication of its arts and culture.

Following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] [[Galactic Empire]], Alderaan played a pivotal role in the establishment of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], a movement that sought to restore the Old Republic's values, and was one of its earliest supporters. Because of its ties with the Alliance, the planet was [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] when [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] decided to test the [[superlaser]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], a [[moon]]-sized [[battle station]] developed by the Empire, at full strength. Among the few survivors of that disaster was [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], adopted daughter of Bail and Breha Organa and heiress to the [[House of Organa|royal house of Alderaan]].

==Description==
{{Quote|This is going to sit inside you forever&hellip; and if I'm going to die, I want to really think about this. Imagine actually killing Alderaan. Alderaan of all places! Alderaan is nice. Who'd blow up Alderaan? Hell of a place. Incredible history. Good party town. Hell, even had great sunsets. Now it doesn't even have a sky.|Chelli Lona Aphra|<ref name="The Trigger">{{StoryCite|story=The Trigger|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>}}
[[File:Alderaan_mountains.png|thumb|left|250px|Alderaan's snow-capped mountains.]]
From [[Realspace|space]], the [[planet]] of Alderaan, located in the [[star system]] of the [[Alderaan system|same name]]<ref name="Episode IV" /> within [[the galaxy]]'s [[Core Worlds]],<ref name="Ultimate SW" /> appeared as a [[Color|blue-green]] orb enveloped in a white web of clouds.<ref name="Episode III" /> Its surface had vast bodies of [[water]]<ref name="Episode IV" /> and was covered in snow-capped [[mountain]]s,<ref name="Episode III" /> with patches of green [[grass]]y hills.<ref name="TCWas">{{TCW|Assassin (episode)}}</ref> Alderaan was known to [[the galaxy]] as "the planet of beauty."<ref name="Leia 2" /> Alderaanians were very artistic and lived for this beauty, but the planet still hid treacherous cliffs and forgotten crevasses.<ref name="Queen's Hope" /> Famous natural landmarks of Alderaan included the [[Cloudshape Falls]],<ref name="LS" /> the [[Glarus Lagoons]],<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> and the [[Isatabith rain forest]].<ref name="LS" />

A variety of [[grape]]vines grew on Alderaan, which notably allowed to produce [[Toniray]]<ref name="Bloodline" /> and [[emerald wine]].<ref name="rebellion">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref> The local fauna included the [[Alderaanian wolf-cat]],<ref name="SYWtBaJ?" /> [[nerf]]s,<ref name="Moving Target" /> and a species of [[Unidentified white-winged bird|white-winged bird]].<ref name="Bloodline" />

The [[City|cities]] on Alderaan were carefully designed to respect the natural beauty of their environment.<ref name="Ultimate SW" /> For example, the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan|palace]] of the [[King|royal]] family, which had been the work of over a millennium,<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> and the city around it blended into the local snow-capped peaks. Alderaanian architecture made use of white [[synthstone]], which inspired the architecture of certain [[mining]] economies of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], like the floating colony of [[Cloud City]], and the planets [[Lothal]] and [[Garel]].<ref name="Ultimate SW" />

Despite the lyrics of the traditional Alderaanian lullaby "[[Mirrorbright (song)|Mirrorbright]]" referencing a moon, the planet never possessed any natural satellites.<ref name="Bloodline" />

==History==
===The Old Republic===
The peaceful world of Alderaan was among the oldest members of the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="DB Ald">{{Databank|alderaan|Alderaan}}</ref> the [[Democracy|democratic]] union that governed most of the galaxy,<ref name="RepublicDB">{{Databank|galactic-republic|Galactic Republic}}</ref> and was among the worlds that originally founded it.<ref name="SWHC1">[[Star Wars Helmet Collection 1|''Star Wars Helmet Collection'': Darth Vader]]</ref> Upon the formation of its [[Alderaanian monarchy|monarchy]] it was established that the heir apparent to Alderaan must complete a [[Challenge of the Heart]], [[Challenge of the Mind]] and [[Challenge of the Body]] which would be announced on their [[Day of Demand]] and upon completion they would be [[Coronation of the Alderaanian heir|officially invested]] as heir. In ancient times, Alderaanian heirs who partook in their Day of Demand had to fight their way into the palace throne room.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> Before they permanently settled on Coruscant, the [[Jedi High Council]] was known to have a council on Alderaan.<ref name="JMM1">[[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine 1|''Star Wars Jedi Master'' Magazine 1]]</ref> Alderaan at one point had a [[Alderaan's neo-classical period|neo-classical period]], which produced unique architecture. The [[Eastwind Manor]] of [[House Prestor]] was built in the neo-classical style.<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

Figuring that she would like the world, [[Yana Ro]] wished to visit Alderaan after the [[Night of Sorrow]] and finally breaking her ties with the [[Path of the Open Hand]] cult. Ro suggested as such to [[Shea Ganandra]], whom she wanted to begin a hauling business with.<ref name="Path of Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref> During the [[High Republic Era]], Alderaan was a prosperous world and, as a signatory of the [[Republic Defense Coalition treaties]], was required to lend [[starship]]s and personnel to be commanded by Republic military officers during times of crisis. These ships and personnel would then be returned to Alderaan after the crisis ended. Alderaan was called upon to fulfill this obligation in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> to aid the Republic in fighting the [[Nihil]], a group of [[Pirate|marauders]] who caused the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

At some point between [[40 BBY]]<ref name="Terminus post quem">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]]. ''Master & Apprentice'' also establishes that Breha was a [[prince]]ss, which means she became [[queen]] of [[Alderaan]] sometime after 40 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> and [[28 BBY]],<ref name="Terminus ante quem">Breha was Queen of Alderaan in the novel ''[[Queen's Shadow]]'', whose events take place four years after [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] went to [[Tatooine]], as depicted in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places ''The Phantom Menace'' in [[32 BBY]], it can be deduced that Breha was queen by [[28 BBY]].</ref> the heir apparent of Alderaan, sixteen year old [[Princess]] [[Breha Organa]], took part in her Day of Demand where she announced her challenges of the heart, mind and body. However during her Challenge of the Body Breha [[The accident|fell]] from the famous Alderaaian mountain [[Appenza Peak]] and would have died had it not been for the quick action of the guards watching from a distance. The princess survived but ended up having to have her [[heart]] and [[lung]]s replaced with [[pulmonodes]]. After her fall Breha was officially invested as crown princess and heir to the throne of Alderaan. Years later the people would still speak of Breha's accident.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

Breha Organa eventually succeeded [[Breha Organa's mother|her mother]]<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> as queen of Alderaan.<ref name="Episode III" />

By the [[time]] of the [[Invasion of Naboo]], Alderaan had not seen conflict in generations.<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref> The planet was represented in the [[Galactic Senate]] by [[Senator]] [[Bail Antilles]]. That [[Standard year|year]], the then-[[Supreme Chancellor]] of the Republic, [[Finis Valorum]], was ousted from office, and Antilles was nominated for the position in the resulting election. However, the [[Alderaanian]] senator was defeated by Senator [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], who represented the sovereign [[Naboo system|system of Naboo]].<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>

In 28 BBY,<ref name="Shadow Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Queen's Shadow'' to [[28 BBY]].</ref> former [[Monarch of Naboo|queen of Naboo]] Senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and her entourage were invited to Alderaan by Queen Breha and stayed at the royal palace for a week before returning home to [[Naboo]].<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />

===The Clone Wars===
<div style="float: right">[[File:Aldera_City.png|thumb|right|250px|[[Aldera]], the planet's capital.]]</div>
Ten years later, Alderaan was represented in the Senate by [[Viceroy]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]],<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> the [[Consort|prince consort]] of the planet's ruler, Queen Breha Organa.<ref name="Episode III" /> At that time, the Republic was facing a [[Separatist Crisis|secessionist crisis]] due to the existence of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] led by [[Count]] [[Dooku]], pushing the galaxy to the brink of war. Through Organa, Alderaan was a member of Chancellor Palpatine's [[Loyalist Committee]], which concerned itself with the stability of the Republic during the Separatist crisis and would later strive to limit Palpatine's increasing executive authority. That year, an [[Grand Army of the Republic|army]] of [[Cloning|clones]] was discovered on the planet [[Kamino]], which would be appropriated by the Senate in order to combat the Separatists. To save the Republic from the impending threat, the Supreme Chancellor was granted emergency powers, a decision that was contrary to the pacifist values of Alderaan and its representative and which ultimately led to the outbreak of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Episode II" /> During the Clone Wars Viceroy Bail Organa became Alderaan's military leader.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

[[File:AlderaanRefugeeConference-Assassin.png|thumb|left|250px|Alderaan Refugee Conference during the Clone Wars.]]
At some point during the Clone Wars, Alderaan hosted a [[Alderaan Refugee Conference|conference]] concerned with aiding war [[refugee]]s. Padmé Amidala, then senator of Naboo, was nearly [[assassin]]ated during the conference when the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]], whom Amidala had sent to [[Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center|prison]], hired the [[bounty hunter]] [[Aurra Sing]] to take revenge. The plot, however, was thwarted by the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan|apprentice]] [[Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="TCWas" /> Many refugees from all over the Republic came to stay on Alderaan during the war to find their way again or to stay.<ref name="Queen's Hope">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref>

Queen Breha and Viceroy Bail would send aid to worlds that had been ravaged by the Separatists, sending not just [[Currency|money]] and supplies but also [[doctor]]s, [[teacher]]s, [[architect]]s, builders, [[medic]]s, and [[droid]]s.<ref name="Queen's Hope" />

Around the time of the [[Battle of Kadavo]], two Alderaanian tourists visiting [[Tatooine]] died in a speeder-bike accident. The [[Zygerrian News]] implied that the two had gotten intoxicated in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina|the cantina]] of [[Mos Eisley]], which caused the accident.<ref name="Crisis">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/escape-from-kadavo-trivia-gallery|text="Escape from Kadavo" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref>

The Clone Wars saw a change in Alderaan. Although it was still a peaceful planet with no large weaponry, the [[Royal Alderaan Civil Fleet]] had "more [[Tooth|teeth]]," their [[officer]]s had seen more hardship, they manufactured more practical items in larger quantities, and the planet had begun to quietly fill up due to the number of refugees.<ref name="Queen's Hope" />

Following the end of the Clone Wars, Viceroy Bail and Queen Breha adopted [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Amidala Skywalker]], the daughter of the now deceased Padmé Amidala and former [[Jedi Knight]] turned [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], renaming her to Leia Organa and making her princess of Alderaan.<ref name="Episode III" /> Leia's adoption was announced on her [[Name Day]] three or four days after her birth and was publicly celebrated on Alderaan.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

===Age of the Empire===
{{Quote|It's a long way to Alderaan.|The codephrase spoken to [[EG-86]] identifying Ezra Bridger and Sabine Wren as rebels|<ref name="Blood Sisters">{{Rebels|Blood Sisters}}</ref>}}
[[File:CR70_alderaan.png|thumb|right|250px|The ''[[Tantive III]]'' lands in Aldera in [[19 BBY]], right after the end of the Clone Wars.]]
The [[Alderaan Trade Alliance]] was an organization active during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Daughter">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Leia Organa took part in [[Leia Organa's Day of Demand|her Day of Demand]] and after completing her three challenges became invested as crown princess and the future queen of Alderaan.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

With the Clone Wars coming to an end, Chancellor Palpatine had abolished the Republic, [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishing]] the [[Galactic Empire]] in its stead.<ref name="Episode III" /> However, a small number of systems rebelled against this new regime,<ref name="Episode IV" /> and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] was ultimately formed by Bail Organa and his former colleague in the Galactic Senate, Senator [[Mon Mothma]] of [[Chandrila]].<ref name="swe-Alliance">{{SWE|groups|rebelalliance|Rebel Alliance}}</ref> During this time, Alderaan became the Alliance's main source of munitions. The planet's crown princess and representative in the [[Imperial Senate]], Princess Leia Organa,<ref name="Episode IV" /> began using her diplomatic immunity as an Imperial senator to carry out Rebel missions in restricted Imperial systems.<ref name="swe-Alliance" /><ref name="A Princess on Lothal">{{Rebels|A Princess on Lothal}}</ref>

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Alderaan was listed as an Alliance [[safe world]]. It was included in a map of the galaxy with a legend listing the various Alliance safe worlds, [[starfighter]] hubs at level five or higher, rebel operations sectors and regional headquarters, and shadow planets with deep space caches. The map would later be incorporated into ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

About three years before the [[Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="Year">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]'' states that [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] was 16 [[Standard year|years]] old during her mission on [[Lothal]]. Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' establishes that Organa was [[Reproduction|born]] in [[19 BBY]], the mission must take place in [[3 BBY]].</ref> Senator Bail Organa dispatched three [[Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette|''Sphyrna''-class Hammerhead corvettes]] to the [[Phoenix Cell]], which has sustained losses [[Battle of Garel|while fleeing]] Garel. Since Alderaan could not be seen directly supporting the rebels, Senator Organa arranged for his adopted daughter Leia to deliver the vessels to Lothal. The Alderaanians risked losing their seat in the Imperial Senate among other Imperial sanctions. Senator Organa had arranged for the [[Spectres|crew]] of the ''[[Ghost]]'' to "steal" the ships from an Imperial depot on Lothal so that Alderaan could not be blamed for the "theft." With the help of the crew of the ''Ghost'' and the former [[Governor]] [[Ryder Azadi]], Leia managed to deliver the three Hammerhead corvettes to the Phoenix rebels following a [[Mission on Lothal|skirmish]] at the Imperial depot.<ref name="A Princess on Lothal" />

===Destruction===
{{Quote|Princess Leia, before your execution, I would like you to be my guest at a ceremony that will make this battle station operational. No star system will dare oppose the Emperor now.''"<br />"''The more you tighten your grip, Tarkin, the more star systems will slip through your fingers.''"<br />"''Not after we demonstrate the power of this station. In a way, you have determined the choice of the planet that will be destroyed first. Since you are reluctant to provide us with the location of the Rebel base, I have chosen to test this station's destructive power on your home planet of Alderaan.|Wilhuff Tarkin and Leia Organa|<ref name="Episode IV" />}}
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> shortly before the [[Battle of Scarif]], and three weeks prior to the [[equinox]] festivities,<ref name="Eclipse" /> Senator Bail Organa headed towards Alderaan to tell their people that there would "be no peace,"<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> after the plans for the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], a planet-killing Imperial [[superlaser]], were discovered. The plans were later stolen from the data vault on [[Scarif]], and transferred aboard the ''[[Tantive IV]]''. Princess Leia of Alderaan, Bail's adopted daughter and thus member of the [[Elder Houses]] was captured by the [[Imperial Navy]] while smuggling the plans to the Alliance base. Organa was taken prisoner by Vader, the Emperor's second-in-command, and brought before the Death Star's commanding officer, [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]].<ref name="Episode IV" />

[[File:DestructionOfAlderaan-ANH.png|thumb|left|250px|The Death Star fires on Alderaan.]]
Under threat of destroying her planet, Leia gave a fake location of the rebel base to Tarkin, only for Tarkin to nevertheless destroy Alderaan&mdash;noting that [[Dantooine]] was too remote for an "effective demonstration"<ref name="Episode IV" />&mdash;to punish the world for supporting the Rebellion,<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> hoping the annihilation of the entire world would make the rest of the galaxy fear Imperial retribution for supporting the Rebels. The Imperials forced the princess to witness the obliteration of her home planet. The planet's destruction left only an [[asteroid field]] behind,<ref name="Episode IV" /> which was blockaded by the Empire.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>

On [[Coruscant]], the Empire's planetary [[capital]], the numerous Alderaanian residents of [[Level 3204]] began to hold vigils for those who had perished in what they called "[[Destruction of Alderaan|the Disaster]]." However, as more information became available about the Empire's role in the destruction of Alderaan, the vigils soon turned into riots, which were quickly repressed by brute force.<ref name="OTLD" /> The Empire defended the destruction of the planet to its [[Imperial Military|military]], claiming that its population had chosen its destruction by supporting anarchy.<ref name="Squadrons" /> A short time after Alderaan's destruction, the Death Star was destroyed in the Battle of Yavin, taking Tarkin<ref name="Episode IV" /> and many other high ranking Imperials with it.<ref name="Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2015) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 1]]</ref>

Under the orders of Vader, the Imperial Military was to "complete" the destruction of Alderaan by hunting any remaining Alderaanians.<ref name="Squadrons" /> Almost immediately after the Death Star's destruction, Leia Organa would embark on a [[Mission to rescue the survivors of Alderaan|mission]] to rescue the survivors of Alderaan from Imperial reprisals.<ref name="Leia 1">[[Princess Leia 1|''Princess Leia'' 1]]</ref> Also following the Battle of Yavin, Vader took the remains of Alderaan to serve as reminders to worlds that attempted to resist the Emperor; following an [[Mission to Shu-Torun|assassination attempt]] on himself at [[Shu-Torun]], Vader then gave [[Trios]], the new [[Monarch of Shu-Torun|Queen]] of that planet, one of these reminders.<ref name="DV Annual 1">[[Darth Vader Annual 1|''Darth Vader Annual'' 1]]</ref> Finding himself the last of [[House Glorio]], [[Rabael Dir Glorio]], who had also been 26th in line to the throne, tried to have his own revenge on the Empire but was left behind and for dead by a [[Aphra's crew|crew]] working for the [[Son-tuul Pride]].<ref name="Aphra 15">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 15|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 15]]</ref>

In [[4 ABY]], Leia married [[Han Solo]] on the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. Due to the destruction of Alderaan, Leia's mother, Breha Organa, couldn't attach the [[Rhindon Sword]] to her waist before Leia went to meet her husband, though, during the [[Wedding of Han Solo and Leia Organa|ceremony]], Leia felt as if her mother was attaching the sword and her father, Bail Organa, was kissing her on the cheek. After the ceremony, Leia mused to herself that had Alderaan not been destroyed she would have been married at the [[Aldera cathedral|cathedral]] in Aldera, with a customary lace dress, an [[Standard hour|hour]] dedicated to the bell service, three different traditional [[Foodstuff|meals]] with guests of rank, and more.<ref name="Princess and the Scoundrel">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref>

By [[5 ABY]], a [[Alderaan Flotilla|flotilla]] of starships orbited the [[Alderaanian Graveyard]], the new name for the debris remaining of the planet. Princess Leia managed to use her political connections to take scrap from the Death Star, initially intended for incineration, to be used to construct a new space station around the planet.<ref name="Life Debt" /> A dedicated [[Alderaan Memorial]] was also established on Coruscant.<ref name="Backstories">''[[Princess Leia: Royal Rebel]]''</ref>

By the time of the [[New Republic]], Alderaan's destruction would become a stark reminder for future generations about the terrors of the Galactic Empire, and would often be an 'uncomfortable subject' for those that brought up the planet's destruction in conversation. As such, surviving Alderaanians would be treated with great respect for their lost world and people. For collectors, rare bottles of the alcoholic beverage Toniray would become a highly-prized commodity.<ref name="Bloodline" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|Soldiers are a thing of Alderaan's past, Leia.|Bail Organa, to his adopted daughter Leia|<ref name="Leia 2" />}}
[[File:Young_Leia_studying.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Alderaanians valued culture and education.]]
Alderaan hosted a population of [[human]]s, known as Alderaanians, who were known for their philosophy of paciﬁsm. Even in time of galactic conflicts, the Alderaanians shunned weapons. They were not, however, bereft of spirit, as many of them chose to join the rebellion against the Galactic Empire. Their society was ruled by the [[House of Organa]],<ref name="swe-Bail" /> in conjunction with an individual bearing the titles of Viceroy and [[First Chairman]].<ref name="Card Trader">[[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]]</ref> The female child of the royal couple was referred to as a princess.<ref name="Episode IV" /> For diplomatic missions, the Royal Family used streamlined cruisers.<ref name="swe-CR90">{{SWE|technology|alderaancruiser|Alderaan cruiser}}</ref>

According to [[Nash Windrider]], on Alderaan, its people were encouraged to learn and grow. All education was free, and people would volunteer to teach various skills or crafts just for fun.<ref name="LS" /> Queen Breha herself served as the [[Minister of Education|minister of education]],<ref name="Princess Leia: Royal Rebel" /> and personally mentored a select number of young Alderaanians, including [[Evaan Verlaine]], teaching them about the culture and heritage of their homeworld.<ref name="Leia 1" />

Alderaan also had a number of traditions involving [[braid]]s such as who wore them, when and why that varied from continent to continent and age to age. The Alderaaian monarch was expected to wear braids.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

Every child of Alderaan owned a [[keepsake chest]] whose designs would be carved by the child's [[parent]]s and grandparents though the child themselves choose what went inside. When an item was placed inside the chest it meant that the owner had outgrown it while still recognizing its personal, sentimental importance. In adulthood, an Alderaanian could then open their chest, look back, and see how they had chosen to tell their own story.<ref name="Bloodline" /> It was traditional for the owner of the chest, upon coming of age, to give it to their parents as a sign that they were now an adult, although it was not mandatory. Many heirs of Alderaan would give up their keepsake chest before their investiture as crown prince or princess though the ritual didn't demand it.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The Alderaan system actually consists of seven planets. Alderaan was the capital planet of that system. There was a Senate and my father was the president of that Senate.|George Lucas, assuming Leia Organa's character|<ref name="TMSW">''[[The Making of Star Wars]]''</ref>}}
Alderaan first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], the seminal movie of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga. In that movie, the planet was only seen from space during the scene depicting its destruction.<ref name="Episode IV" /> In the [[The Star Wars story synopsis|treatment]], [[The Star Wars rough draft|rough draft]], [[Adventures of the Starkiller, Episode I: The Star Wars|second draft]], and [[The Star Wars: From The Adventures of Luke Starkiller|third draft]] of what would become ''A New Hope'', the name of Alderaan was used for the capital of the "New Galactic Empire." At that time, it was described as a giant gas planet that resembled [[Bespin]], with a [[Imperial City of Alderaan|city]] that resembled Cloud City on it. In the second draft of ''A New Hope'', the home planet of Princess Leia was called [[Ogana Major]]. [[Aquilae (The Star Wars)|Aquilae]], [[Utapau (Kessil system)|Utapau]], and [[Ophuchi]] were also considered as names for worlds partially corresponding to Alderaan.<ref name="The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film">''[[The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film]]''</ref>

Following the success of ''A New Hope'', a full [[Star Wars Legends|ancillary world]] was being built around the movie. In order to get everyone on the same page, George Lucas decided to create a few background stories and ideas. In those notes, Alderaan was described as a four or five million years old Earth-like planet, covered with high snowcapped mountains and green pastures. The same notes also described the planet as being the capital of its star system and possessing its own Senate.<ref name="TMSW" /> In [[canon]], the surface of Alderaan only made its first appearance in the [[2005]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Episode III" /> The planet's mountainous terrain was modelled after a location in the [[Wikipedia:Swiss Alps|Swiss Alps]].<ref name="AoSWIII">''[[The Art of Star Wars Episode III: Revenge of the Sith]]''</ref>

In the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', Alderaan was mistakenly depicted as being in the [[Colonies]] instead of the Core Worlds.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
