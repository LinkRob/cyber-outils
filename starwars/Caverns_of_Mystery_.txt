"'''Caverns of Mystery!'''" was the seventh installment of [[The Keeper's World]], published in [[Pizzazz 7]] in April [[1978]]. The entire story arc was reprinted in the UK in ''[[Star Wars Weekly 47]]&ndash;[[Star Wars Weekly 50|50]]'' in [[1978]]&ndash;[[1979|79]], and in the [[1999]] limited-release comic ''[[Star Wars 0]]''.

==Plot summary==
[[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]] are closing in on [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]], [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]], and [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] when they are saved by the elemental powers of [[Fire (individual)|Fire]], [[Rain (droid)|Rain]], [[Wind (android)|Wind]], and [[Earth (droid)|Earth]]. While the Imperials are distracted, the Rebels and the elemental children flee deeper into the [[Keeper's Temple]], where they come face to face with [[The Keeper]], a powerful mass of computerized technology. The Keeper chastises the children for allowing her presence to be discovered, and despite the protests of the children she strikes the Rebels with a powerful [[Keeper's beam|energy beam]].
