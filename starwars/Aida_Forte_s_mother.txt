A [[Gender|female]] individual was the '''mother''' of the [[Kadas'sa'Nikto]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Aida Forte]].<ref name="Cataclysm" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> when Forte and [[Jedi Master]] [[Creighton Sun]] [[Mission to Dalna (Sun and Forte)|traveled]] to<ref name="Cataclysm" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Dalna]] to investigate the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]], they witnessed a [[Unidentified Teevan man|Teevan man]] being brought before the [[Elecia Zeveron|Mother]] and the [[Great Leveler|Leveler]], the Path's [[Mother (title)|leader]] and a [[Shrikarai|creature]] which [[Diet|fed]] on [[Force-sensitive]]s, respectively. As the Leveler consumed the [[Teevan]], the two [[Jedi]] also became overwhelmed by fear.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

After it was over, Forte compared the feeling of the incident to her [[Death|killing]] her own [[Parent|mother]]. The Jedi Knight then noted she had not felt as much fear before as she had during the ordeal, and even though it felt real, it could not have been. Forte then concluded that the experience was horrible, and as she did not know her mother, it would not have made any sense.<ref name="Cataclysm" />
