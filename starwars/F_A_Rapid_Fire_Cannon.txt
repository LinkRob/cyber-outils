'''F/A rapid fire cannon''' was a [[laser cannon]] created by [[Sienar Fleet Systems]] for the [[Galactic Empire]], being an optional component on the [[TIE/IN interceptor|TIE/ln interceptor]]. The weapon had a fast rate of fire but a shorter range and clip size, being more effective for hit and run tactics.<ref name="Squadrons" />
