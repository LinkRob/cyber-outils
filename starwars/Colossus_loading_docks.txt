The '''loading docks''' on the ''[[Colossus]]'' were a location where [[Scavenger|salvagers]] worked pulling wreckage from the [[ocean]] below. They were located on one end of the platform. [[Synara San]] got a job there after arriving on the ''Colossus''.<ref name="Synara's Score" />
