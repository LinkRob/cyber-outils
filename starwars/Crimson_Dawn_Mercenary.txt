The '''Crimson Dawn Mercenary''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Bounty Hunter (Imara Vex)|standard costume]]. This sleek [[Clothing|outfit]] and [[armor]] feature a [[Color|black]], dark gray, and gold color scheme, resembling a common color scheme worn by many members of the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime [[syndicate]], such as the [[Hylobon Enforcers]], with a glowing yellow visor on the [[helmet]], and a black boxy [[shoulder-mounted armament]] housing a golden payload.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Crimson Dawn Mercenary was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Imara Vex, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 4.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Dawn Rifle]].<ref name="Hunters" />
