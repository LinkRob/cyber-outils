'''Corvax Fen''' was a [[forest]]ed<ref name="Episode IX Visual" /> and [[swamp]]y<ref name="Droid Factory" /> [[forest]] region on the [[planet]] of [[Mustafar]],<ref name="Episode IX Visual" /> inhabited by the cult, [[Alazmec of Winsit]], who attempted to attempted attempt to replenish Mustafar's ecosystem, by planting [[Irontree|Irontrees]].

==History==
Corvax Fen developed after a [[Corvax descendant|descendant]] of [[Lady]] [[Corvax]] destroyed the [[Bright Star]] during the [[battle on Mustafar]] decades before. The destruction of the Bright Star unleashed its energy upon the [[planet]]'s desolate soil, eventually developing the forest.<ref name="Immortal 3">{{VaderImmortal|III}}</ref> The [[astromech droid]] [[R5-Z0MB]], who visibly resembled an [[Species|organic]] [[zombie]], took to wandering the Corvax Fen swamps at some point.<ref name="Droid Factory" />

[[File:IronwoodWarrior.png|thumb|left|300px|Supreme Leader Kylo Ren and his stormtroopers march through the Corvax Fen and massacre its cultist inhabitants.]]
By [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the Corvax Fen had become home to a cult called the [[Alazmec of Winsit]], who dedicated themselves to restoring Mustafar to a thriving state and guarding the ruins of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[Fortress Vader|castle]]. The Alazmec cultists believed that the soil of the fen was fertile because it had been soaked by Lady Corvax's tears. Devoted to protecting and reinvigorating the grove, the cultists took to planting a forest of irontrees.<ref name="Episode IX Visual" /> In 35 ABY,<ref name="Timelines" /> during his [[Search for Exegol|search]] for the source of [[Palpatine's broadcast]], [[Supreme Leader]] of the [[First Order]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] [[Mission to Mustafar (First Order)|arrived]] in the Corvax Fen with a group of [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]], massacring the Alazmec cultists and stealing a [[Vader's wayfinder|Sith wayfinder]] that had once belonged to Vader.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
