'''Crawfin''' was an individual who once chased a [[Unidentified smuggler (Five Points)|smuggler]] but lost track of him when the [[smuggler]] took his [[Starship|ship]] into [[Smuggler's Run]]&mdash;an [[Asteroid field|asteroid belt]]<ref name="Rebel Rising" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> Around [[4 BBY]],<ref name="4 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[mission to Tamsye Prime]] to [[5 BBY]]. ''[[Rebel Rising]]'' establishes that directly following the mission, [[Jyn Erso]] arrived on [[Skuhl]], and around one [[Standard year|year]] later, left the [[planet]] and ended up on the [[Five Points]] [[space station]]. Therefore, she arrived on Five Points around [[4 BBY]].</ref> the former [[Partisans|rebel]] [[Jyn Erso]] overheard the story from the smuggler on the [[space station]] [[Five Points]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
