'''Ays Varga''' was a [[human]] [[superweapon]]s [[engineer]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Varga [[Desertion|defected]] from the Empire, intending to join the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate through the [[contract broker]] [[EE'zok]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. However, Varga instead made a deal with [[pirate]]s, who then sold her out to the Empire.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
After Varga stopped answering communications, EE'zok dispatched the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] to find her. Vess located Varga anfter discovering she had been captured by a [[pirate]], who was holding her hostage to claim a reward for turning her in to the Imperials. Vess rescued her from the pirate and escaped Imperial forces, bringing her to the [[moon]] of [[Toshara]] about the [[EML-850 light freighter|EML-850 freighter]] ''[[Trailblazer]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" /> Upon arrival above the world, [[Bib Fortuna]], who had an interest in Ays’ skills, demanded she be brought to the [[Hutt Clan|Hutts]] instead. Kay Vess would leave it up to Ays to decide, and advised her on who she believed was better.
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ays Varga appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', featuring in the syndicate quest "Defector." After the player, as Vess, rescues Varga from Tatooine, the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] also expresses interest in recruiting her. The engineer is unsure, and the player can convince her to either join Crimson Dawn or the Hutts. If Crimson Dawn is chosen, Varga is brought to [[Eleera Soi]]; if the player sides with the Hutts, [[Verrick]] instead meets the engineer.<ref name="Outlaws" />
