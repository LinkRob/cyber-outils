'''Empath tests''' were used by the [[Jedi Order]] to [[Jedi training|determine]] if its [[Jedi|members]] possessed the rare [[Force power|Force ability]] to feel the emotions of other beings that was<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> known as [[Force empathy]].<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> The tests were performed inside the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] asked [[Padawan]] [[Imri Cantaros]]&mdash;who she knew was a lot more sensitive to the emotions of others than herself&mdash;if he had considered asking his [[Jedi Master]], [[Douglas Sunvale]], to take him to the [[Jedi temple|Jedi Temple]] to undergo the empath tests. In response, Cantaros shook his head and stated his insight was a result of paying attention rather than anything to do with [[the Force]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> The Padawan, in fact, had exceptional levels of Force empathy.<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
Empath tests were mentioned in the [[2021]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref>
