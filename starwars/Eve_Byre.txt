'''Eve Byre''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served as a [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" /> After the [[death]] of her first [[Padawan]], [[Zaph Mora]], she took on another Padawan, [[Cam Lindon]].<ref name="Seeds of Starlight" />

==Biography==
In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|TOTN}}</ref> Eve Byre travelled onboard the ''[[Vessel]]'' with her Padawan Zaph Mora to [[Oisin]] to [[Mission to Oisin|deliver supplies]] to the locals. During the trip, she and Zaph sparred and meditated. When [[Affie Hollow]] came to check on them, she explained that their mission was to help the [[Oisinian]]s to develop a sustainable society regardless of what disruptions the [[Nihil conflict]] might have caused. As they prepared to land on Oisin, they crossed paths with a [[Nihil]] ship coming to raid the planet. She ordered the crew to bring the ''Vessel'' twenty [[Meter|meters]] above the raiding ship and then jumped out with her Padawan. They breached the hull of the Nihil ship and caused it to crash land. After downing the ship, she directed the Osinian soldiers to secure three Nihil prisoners. She thanked Hollow for her help after the cargo was unloaded. Hollow gave her a specialized [[comlink]] with a homing device that was directly connected to the ''Vessel'' so Byre could contact them when she needed to be picked up. Byre and Mora stayed on Oisin to help the Oisians with their fortification plans. While there, the [[Jedi hunter]] [[Sicarus]] landed on the [[planet]] and attacked the two Jedi with a pack of [[Shrikarai|Nameless]]. Byre was captured when she was overwhelmed by the effect of the creatures. She was kept in a cage on [[Sicarus's shuttle]] when he travelled to [[Tolis]] to kill [[Reath Silas]] and [[Cohmac Vitus]]. Left alone when Sicarus took the Nameless to hunt the Jedi, she managed to activate the homing signal on the comlink she had gotten from Hollow. She was almost catatonic and covered in filth when she was rescued by Hollow from the cage she was confined to. Byre was returned to Coruscant for treatment and care at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]]. She was visited by Hollow and thanked the woman for rescuing her. They talked about Mora's death and how Byre was doing in her recovery.<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" />

Following her rescue and recovery, Eve Byre took Cam Lindon as her Padawan. The two were joined by [[Alcedian]] [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi Initiates]] [[Tep Tep]] and [[Kildo]] aboard the ''[[Innovator]]'' for a scientific mission studying the [[Blight]], but following a request by Jedi Master [[Ackmora]], the ''Innovator'' instead traveled to [[Barraza City]] on [[Eiram]] to investigate a series of missing people. The culprits were the [[Drengir]], who survived in the Eiram oceans following [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|the fall of]] [[Starlight Beacon|Starlight Beacon.]] <ref name="Seeds of Starlight" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Tears of the Nameless also introduces the character of Jedi Master Eve Byre. [S]he speaks with a classic British accent and can sometimes come across as a little prim, but once you get to know her she has a soft centre with a streak of dry humour. ''She’s a master of endurance, as people will soon discover—she has to face a LOT, and do it without breaking.'' In many ways, for me, she very much typifies the Jedi of the High Republic and what they represent—the Order at its peak; resolute, strong-willed, powerful and sure of their beliefs.|George Mann|<ref name="Collider">{{WebCite|url=https://collider.com/star-wars-the-high-republic-tears-of-the-nameless-excerpt|text=Read an Excerpt From 'Tears of the Nameless,' The Next Thrilling Addition to 'Star Wars: The High Republic'|work=[[Wikipedia:Collider (website)|Collider]]|archivedate=20240913163012}}</ref>}}
Eve Byre first appeared in the [[2024]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Tears of the Nameless]]'', written by [[George Mann]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=SWCE 2023: 9 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref> In an interview, Mann stated that Byre was key to future ''The High Republic'' storytelling.<ref name="Collider" /> When coming up with the design for Byre, artist [[Jake Bartok]] was provided with a brief description by Mann and had a lot of room to come up with her design. One piece that Bartok submitted included a Hakama styled skirt that called back to attire worn by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Ben Kenobi]] or [[Jocasta Nu]], with the author noting it would be interesting to see the skirt's twirl if he ever had the chance to draw her in action.<ref name="Wook Interview">{{WookCite|url=p/4400000000003892811|text=Interview with Jake Bartok|forum=1}}</ref>
