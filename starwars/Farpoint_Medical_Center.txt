'''Farpoint Medical Center''' was a large [[Medcenter/Legends|hospital]] facility in [[Farpoint]], the only [[City/Legends|city]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Fhost]]. The surviving [[Cloning/Legends|clones]] of the [[Community]] journeyed there to get medicine to slow down their sickness before their reach their planned [[Mother (Rakatan entity)|destination]].
