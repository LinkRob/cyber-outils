The '''''Contra Mini-Game''''' is a mini-game inspired by the NES and arcade classic [[W:c:contra:Contra (video game)|''Contra'']]. It could be played in [[Konami]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'' digital card game in through the months of [[April]] and [[May]] [[2015]].<ref name="Contra video" />

The mini-game is a four-level run-and-gun adventure featuring [[Chewbacca]] blasting [[stormtrooper]]s on [[Endor]].<ref name="Contra video" /> A very similar mini-game titled ''[[Operation: Going Solo]]'' was made available in [[2017]], featuring [[Han Solo]] as the player instead of Chewbacca.<ref name="TFN">{{WebCite|url=http://www.theforce.net/story/front/Konamis_Star_Wars_Force_Collection_ReReleases_Contra_MiniGame_173519.asp|text=Konami's Star Wars: Force Collection Re-Releases Contra Mini-Game|work=TheForce.net|archivedate=20180517055806}}</ref>
