'''FN-1226''' was a [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> [[Gender|male]] who served in the [[First Order]]'s<ref name="Episode IX" /> [[FN Trooper Corps]]<ref name="BTA" /> as a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]]<ref name="Episode IX" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="IX year">According to ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]]. The Hosnian Cataclysm was dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' meaning Episode IX, in which FN-1226 appears, takes place in [[35 ABY]].</ref> When [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] arrived on the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] to rescue [[Chewbacca]], the young [[Jedi]] confused FN-1226 and [[FN-0606|another stormtrooper]] with a [[mind trick]].<ref name="Episode IX" />
