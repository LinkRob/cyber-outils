An '''eelfish''' was a type of [[fish]] known for digging. While searching for a [[datapad]] in the [[Qorvino's cave hideout|hideout]] of the [[pirate]] [[Captain]] [[Qorvino]] on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> one [[Mercenary|hired gun]] ordered another to "make like an eelfish and get digging." When the other hired gun was confused by the phrase, the first sighed and clarified that they should just look for the datapad.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eelfish were mentioned in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune|text=Steal a Look at ''Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune'', Available May 15}}</ref>
