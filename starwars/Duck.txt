A '''duck ''' was a [[species]] found in [[the galaxy]], including the [[planet]] [[Naboo]],<ref name="AotC">{{Film|II}}</ref> where they could be seen swimming on the [[Solleu River]].<ref name="Episode I" /> A duck's young was called a duckling, and tended to follow elder ducks around.<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref> In [[Galactic Basic Standard]], the [[List of phrases and slang|expression]] "sitting duck" meant someone or something that was very easy to shoot or attack.<ref name="Episode I" />
