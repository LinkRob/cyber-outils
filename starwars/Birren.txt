'''Birren''' was a small, quiet [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] that was originally settled by both [[Alderaanian]] and [[Arkanis]]ian [[Scout|explorers]].<ref name="Bloodline" /> After the [[destruction of Alderaan]], it was one of the few places where [[candlewick flower]]s still grew naturally.<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" />

Its head of state was a ceremonial [[Monarchy|monarch]] referred to as the [[supreme governor]]. Birren was reputed for its rituals that invariably lasted for several [[Standard week|weeks]]. When [[Lord]] [[Mellowyn]] [[Death|died]] without an heir, the figurehead governorship of Birren should have passed to his closest living relative, [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of Alderaan. However, since Organa had no interest in claiming the title, she gave the throne of Birren to Lady [[Carise Sindian]] of Arkanis. [[Standard day|Days]] later, after discovering that Sindian had broken the [[royal seal]] by exposing the identity of Organa's [[Anakin Skywalker|true father]], the senator managed to convince the [[Elder Houses]] to strip Sindian of all her titles.<ref name="Bloodline" />

The [[Akuna family]] lived on Birren for a time during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>
