'''Benjamin A. Burtt''' is a sound editor who has worked on the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]''.<ref name="The Marshal" /><ref name="Stranger">{{BOBF|Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land}}</ref> He is the son of [[Ben Burtt]], the [[Wikipedia:Academy Awards|Academy Award&ndash;winning]] sound editor of the [[1977]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] and many other ''[[Star Wars]]'' projects.

The younger Burtt began his work while still in high school, sent off by his father to collect sound effects throughout [[Wikipedia:Sydney, Austrailia|Sydney, Australia]] for use in the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]. He later apprenticed under his father, digitizing old sound libraries, and went on to a full-time job as a sound-effects editor at [[Skywalker Sound]],<ref name="Hollywood Reporter Black Panther" /> working alongside individuals like [[J.R. Grubbs]] on ''The Mandalorian''<ref name="The Marshal" /> and [[David W. Collins]] on ''The Book of Boba Fett''.<ref name="Stranger" />
