During the [[Coyerti campaign]] in [[3 ABY]], two [[squad]]s, led by [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] and [[Sergeant]] [[Zab]], launched an '''attack''' on an [[Imperial scout post]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Coyerti]]. They managed to overrun the defending Imperial forces and take the camp before retreating to avoid incoming [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bombers]].

==Prelude==
The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[61st Mobile Infantry]] launched a [[Coyerti campaign|campaign]] on [[Coyerti]]<ref name="BTC" /> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> during the Alliance's [[Mid Rim Retreat]]. The campaign had multiple objectives: the first was to protect the [[Coyerti (species)|Coyerti species]] during their breeding season; the second was to affect the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[bioweapon]] production on Coyerti; the third was to create an additional distraction to assist the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet's]] [[Mid Rim Retreat|retreat]] from the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="BTC" />

==The battle==
[[File:PowerInNumbers.png|thumb|left|250px|Many stormtroopers (pictured) were involved in the battle]]
Two [[squad]]s, led by [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] and [[Sergeant]] [[Zab]], respectively, were deployed to locate and destroy [[Imperial scout post]], which had been established to provide logistical support to the Empire's ongoing offensive against the Coyerti. Having discussed their strategy prior to the mission, [[Namir's squad]], provided covering fire for the second [[Zab's squad|squad's]] direct assault. Early on in the attack, [[Besalisk]] [[Gadren]], from Namir's squad, charged through with his [[blaster cannon]]. An Imperial [[grenadier]] almost killed the Besalisk but, luckily for Gadren, the [[grenade]] did not detonate.<ref name="BTC" />

Namir led [[Roach]] to cover the others and two of Zab's men supported Gadren at the center of the camp to keep the Imperials from grouping up. After an intense ten minutes of combat, the camp was taken. Although Zab requested that Twilight Company requisition the [[speeder bike]]s that remained at the base, Namir stated that they would likely have [[homing beacon]]s attached, and therefore would give away their location. Shortly after destroying the remaining equipment at the base, the squads retreated back into the jungle before [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bomber]] sorties located them.<ref name="BTC" />
