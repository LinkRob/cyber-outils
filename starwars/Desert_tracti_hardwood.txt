'''Desert tracti hardwood''' was a variety of [[hardwood]] found on [[Tatooine]]. Along with [[krayt dragon]] horn and bones and metal, the hardwood was used by [[Tusken Raider]]s to create [[Gaderffii stick|gaffi sticks]], a type of melee weapon.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

{{Substance-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Desert tracti hardwood was mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
