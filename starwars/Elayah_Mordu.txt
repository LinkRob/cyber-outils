'''Elayah Mordu''' was a [[Nothoiin (species)|Nothoiin]] [[Gender|female]] who served as a [[lieutenant]] of the [[Bespin Wing Guard]] of [[Cloud City]] in the later stages of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The only official hostage negotiator in the Guard, Mordu was impeccably honest, while most of her superiors had grown corrupt. Sickened by the dishonesty of her bosses, Mordu reluctantly started "freelancing."<ref name="Mordu" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
Several [[Standard month|months]] into the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Iron Blockade]] of the [[Anoat]] [[sector]], Mordu joined the crew of a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] from [[Burnin Konn]], assisting them on various missions.<ref name="Mordu" />
{{EndGame}}
