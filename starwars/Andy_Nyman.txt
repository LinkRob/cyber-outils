'''Andy Nyman''' (born [[April 13]], [[1960s#1966|1966]]) is a British actor who portrayed a [[Canto Bight]] [[Canto Bight Police Department|jail guard]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref>{{Film|VIII}} credits</ref>

His other roles include appearances on ''Hanna'' (with [[Mark Bazeley]] and [[Valene Kane]]), ''Wanderlust'' and ''Unforgotten''. Other roles include multiple appearances as Winston Churchill on ''Peaky Blinders'' (with [[Kenneth Colley]], [[Ralph Ineson]] and [[Tom Hardy]]) and ''The Commuter'' (with [[Liam Neeson]] and [[Stuart Whelan]]).

In addition to voice acting work for multiple ''Shaun the Sheep'' shorts and ''Despicable Me 3'' (with [[Tara Strong]]), Nyman has also worked on video games including ''Saints Row 2'' (with [[Kari Wahlgren]]).
