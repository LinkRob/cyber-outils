'''Eloc Throno''' was an adventurer, historian, author, and [[Scout|explorer]] of [[the galaxy]]. After leaving his homeworld of [[Davnar II]], he studied [[Cartographer|cartography]] at [[Orchis 2]]'s [[Graf Archive]] under director [[Amel Fortoon]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

Throno often enjoyed visiting the [[planet]] [[Batuu]], writing a [[Traveler's Guide to Batuu|guide]] about the planet and its inhabitants, which he updated more than once. He authored more than a dozen guides including ''[[The Traveler's Guide to Tatooine]]'', ''[[The Traveler's Guide to Xibariz]]'', ''[[Volcanic Planets: Your Guide to the Galaxy's Hottest Destinations]]'', and ''[[Mandalorian Battlefield Tours]]''.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> For the tenth edition of ''Mandalorian Battlefield Tours'', Throno published a supplement titled "[[Mandalorians Through the Ages]]."<ref name="OSWM">{{GermanFanCite|104|Database}}</ref> He also made frequent appearances on regional [[HoloNet]] programs.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eloc Throno was first mentioned in the [[2020]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', written by [[Cole Horton]]. The character's name is an [[Tuckerization|anagram]] of Horton's own.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />
