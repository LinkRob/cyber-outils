'''Amit Noloff''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Quarren]] who was a prominent leader on [[Iego]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. He lived in [[Cliffhold]]. After the [[planet]] was sealed off by a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] laser field situated in its [[Moons of Iego|moons]], Noloff began to despair, believing that it was caused by the "curse of [[Drol]]". He believed that fighting against the "curse" was pointless. When [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] came to [[Iego]], he told them that they can't leave Iego due to the curse and showed them a [[hologram]] of [[Taquito]]'s [[death]]. However, they attempted to leave anyway and discovered that "Drol" was actually [[LaserWeb Defense Station|laser emitters]] left by the Confederacy of Independent Systems. However, Noloff stood by with his belief, and when the laser emitters were destroyed, he looked awestruck although everyone else was cheering joyfully.<ref name="Mystery Moons" />

==Personality and traits==
Noloff was 1.82 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]]) tall,<ref name="TCWE" /> with [[Color|white]] [[eye]]s, pale orange skin and four [[Limb|tentacles]].<ref name="Mystery Moons" /> He was once a [[spice dealer]], but after the Separatists arrival on Iego, he became trapped and went mad. He told everyone Iego was cursed by Drol, jabbing his finger at the sky for emphasis.<ref name="TCWE" /> As Noloff was a prominent leader on Iego, he was effective at public speaking, successfully convincing other marooned [[spacer]]s of his ideologies.<ref name="Mystery Moons" />

==Skills and abilities==
The Quarren was proficient in [[starship]] [[pilot]]ing, as well as business negotiations.<ref name="TCWE" />
