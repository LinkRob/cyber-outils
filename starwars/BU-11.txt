'''BU-11''' was a [[BU-series bartender droid]] who worked in [[Ziro's Palace]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="BuildR2" /> It served a [[Trandoshan]] a glowing [[color|green]] cup. It reacted as [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] entered the room with [[HELIOS-3D]].<ref name="TCW" />
