The '''''Brighthome''''' was a [[space station]] used by the [[Jedi Order]] as an [[Jedi outpost|outpost]] in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]. Sometime after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], a group of [[stormtrooper]]s were tasked with clearing out [[Jedi]] artifacts from the station.

==Characteristics==
''Brighthome'' was a [[space station]] which consisted of several towers and was outfitted with at least two [[hangar]] bays which stuck out from the main structure like wings. The core the facility consisted of a central, square tower with a sweeping stairway leading up to an open entrance. At least four shorter towers were clustered around the central tower, each with a stepped roof. ''Brighthome'' was adorned by four large statues of [[humanoid]] [[Jedi]] wielding [[lightsaber]]s; a single statue stood above each hangar bay, and two stood back to back on the peak of the station's central tower. On the underside of the the station, a long, narrow spire protruded downward.<ref name="Dark Lord 2" />

==Role==
The ''Brighthome'' served as a [[Jedi outpost]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]], and contained a [[databank]] of information relating to the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Dark Lord 2" />

==History==
[[File:VaderAttackStance-2017DarthVader2.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Vader claimed a lightsaber from the station wall and used it to cut through the clone garrison.]]
Originally serving the [[Jedi Order]] as a Mid Rim outpost, the [[Galactic Empire]] took control of the ''Brighthome'' shortly after [[Order 66]] and the [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishment]] of the Empire. The Empire deployed<ref name="Dark Lord 2" /> a [[Brighthome garrison|garrison]]<ref name="X-Wing 54">{{BuildXWingCite|54|Starfighter Aces}}</ref> of formerly-[[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[clone trooper]]s, who now served as Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s, to archive the [[Jedi artifact|artifacts]] within the station, along with a group of four [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter|ARC-170 starfighters]] as security. The [[Sith apprentice]] to [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], [[Attack on Brighthome|traveled]] to the ''Brighthome'' to begin his quest of finding a living Jedi, from whom he could capture a [[kyber crystal]].<ref name="Dark Lord 2" />

As Vader approached the station, he declined to communicate the docking codes and instead chose to fight his own men, eliminating the enemy [[starfighter]]s with ease before landing and tearing a path through the clone stormtroopers onboard. He then reached the control center, where, with the help of [[Darth Vader's droid|his unwanted]] [[droid]] assistant, located the [[Jedi Master]] [[Kirak Infil'a]], who had taken the [[Barash Vow]] and thus had survived the activation of [[Order 66|Protocol 66]].<ref name="Dark Lord 2" /> Vader left the station a lifeless ruin.<ref name="X-Wing 54" />
