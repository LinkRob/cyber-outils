A '''butterbug remote''' was a type of small, palm-sized [[remote]] that could be linked together in batches as a networked swarm. Used to transmit localized information to one another and the user, the remotes came equipped with micro-tools, including [[drill]]s. Butterbug remotes were vulnerable to blasts. Around the [[Galactic Civil War]], a butterbug remote cost 700 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Special Modifications" />

==Behind the scenes==
Butterbug remotes were mentioned in ''[[Special Modifications]]'', a sourcebook for ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]].<ref name="Special Modifications" />
