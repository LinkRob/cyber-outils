"'''Crisis of Faith'''" is a novella by [[Timothy Zahn]] released on [[September 6]], [[2011]], that was included in ''[[Heir to the Empire: The 20th Anniversary Edition]]''. It follows [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]] and [[bowlcarrier]] [[Trevik]] during a [[Attack on the Red City|battle]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Quethold]] against [[Warlord/Legends|Warlord]] [[Nuso Esva]] and the [[Queen of the Red (8 ABY)|Queen of the Red's]] [[Quesoth Soldier]]s.

==Plot summary==
{{Quote|The threat remains, O Queen, ''[&hellip;]'' Let us take further counsel together as to how we may deal with our common enemy. ''[&hellip;]''Let us speak to the destruction of Grand Admiral Thrawn.|[[Nuso Esva]], to the [[Queen of the Red (8 ABY)|Queen of the Red]]|translated=1|<ref name="COF" />}}
[[File:ChosenNusoEsva.png|thumb|right|300px|Nuso Esva's Chosen, who fought alongside the Quesoth]]
By [[8 ABY/Legends|8 ABY]], [[Nuso Esva]] allies himself with the [[Quesoth]] [[Insectoid/Legends|insectoid]] [[Species/Legends|species]], promising the [[Queen of the Red (8 ABY)|Queen of the Red]], who leads Quethold's [[Red City]], to [[Death/Legends|kill]] the [[Queen of the White]], who would succeed the Red. [[Trevik]] becomes the Queen's [[Bowlcarrier|bowlcarrier]], and with [[Borosiv]] of the [[Circling of the First of the Red|Circling of the First of the Red's]] assistance, he allows the queen to drink [[Nectar/Legends|nectar]]. Above Quethold, aboard the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Admonitor]]'', [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]] plans an assault against Esva's forces with the aid of the [[Stromma]], the former allies of the Quesoth. The Stromma are doubtful of the [[Chiss/Legends|Chiss's]] ability to know what the [[Alien/Legends|alien]] [[Warlord/Legends|warlord]] will do next through art and withdraw their support, but not before translating some [[Quesoth Soldier Speak]] phrases for Thrawn's [[Unidentified council|council]] to use in the upcoming attack on Quethold.

The Stromma also retrieve information gathered from the Quesoth, including a recording of the artwork with which Esva has decorated his quarters, the [[Dwelling of Guests]]. Although the information was sent on Esva's directive to trick Thrawn into attacking Quethold in a way that would give the warlord the upper hand, Thrawn realizes that, and applies that to his strategy. When the battle begins, thousands of [[Quesoth Soldier|Quesoth Soldiers]] attack thirty-six [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]], however, [[Baron/Legends|Baron]] [[Soontir Fel/Legends|Soontir Fel]] destroys the loudspeakers that allow the Queen to give orders to her forces, and uses one to transmit an order to retreat to the palace from the Queen. Esva notices the Soldiers coming to the palace, and in anger kills the Queen, Borosiv, and all Circling and [[Midli|Midlis]] but Trevik, who he sends to tell Thrawn that he may be dead, but his empire was not. Esva's [[Chosen (Nuso Esva)|Chosen]] and the warlord himself are killed shortly after, allowing Thrawn to announce his plans: restore the Galactic Empire.

==Continuity==
Reading through the [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]] in chronological order means that this story would be the first time readers would be exposed to the [[Empire of the Hand]] faction, [[Soontir Fel/Legends|Soontir Fel's]] [[181st Imperial Fighter Wing/Legends|TIE squadron]], and a [[501st Legion (Empire of the Hand)|501st Stormtrooper Legion]] consisting of [[Human/Legends|human]] and [[Alien/Legends|nonhuman]] soldiers. None of these elements were introduced in a manner to properly explain their origins in ''Crisis of Faith'', as they had appeared in print in other sources first, such as ''[[Specter of the Past]]'' ([[1998]]) and ''[[Fool's Bargain]]'' ([[2004]]).<ref name="TERC" />
