'''Betrayed''' is the fifth cinematic trailer released by [[BioWare]] for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', and serves as a reveal trailer for the [[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Knights of the Eternal Throne|''Knights of the Eternal Throne'']].<ref name="Betrayed" /> Released on [[October 7]], [[2016]],<ref name="Released" /> the video depicts the story of [[Vaylin]]. The video runs six minutes and nine seconds.<ref name="Betrayed" /> The trailer was re-released on [[November 15]], [[2021]] in 4K.<ref name="4K" />

==Plot summary==
[[Senya Tirall]] carries her daughter [[Vaylin]] out of the [[asylum]] on [[Nathema]]. She gazes into her daughter's face and remembers a scene from when Vaylin was a little girl. Young Vaylin plays with her mother in a forest on [[Zakuul]], and she finds a small branch that resembles an animal. The two return to the capital city, where Vaylin holds the carved toy in the air with [[The Force/Legends|the Force]], but Senya stops her and ties it to her hand.<ref name="Betrayed" />

With the toy strapped to her arm, Vaylin joins Senya as her twin brothers [[Thexan]] and [[Arcann]] fight the [[Knights of Zakuul]] in an arena. She wishes to be in the arena for herself and tries to use the Force to move an old suit of armor from one of the Knights when she is alone in the woods. The armor doesn't budge, and Vaylin screams, releasing a shock wave that resonates through the forest.<ref name="Betrayed" />

A few years later, Vaylin fights with the Knights in the arena. She is knocked down but dusts herself off as Senya tells them to begin again. The lead Knight charges her, but Vaylin uses the Force to suspend him and the others in the air. She concentrates, causing the Knight's armor to crack. Senya stops her and examines the Knights, who fell when Vaylin stopped concentrating, and they both look nervously at [[Tenebrae|Valkorion]].<ref name="Betrayed" />

Valkorion takes Vaylin to an asylum on Nathema, where he plans to have the [[Nathema Zealot]]s restrain her powers so that she will not surpass his own. Vaylin is locked away on Nathema for years, and the Zealots shave her hair and [[Tattoo/Legends|tattoo]] her arms to make her permanently darker. Senya has a [[Force vision/Legends|vision]] of Vaylin's suffering and secretly travels to Nathema to rescue her daughter. They are confronted by a party of Zealots on a bridge outside the asylum, and Senya kills them before beckoning Vaylin to join her. Vaylin refuses, and Valkorion arrives with the Zealots. Senya is captured by the Knights and taken away from Nathema.<ref name="Betrayed" />

Several years later, Senya explores a devastated field on [[Ord Mantell/Legends|Ord Mantell]], using her [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] to see in the dark. The field is full of dead Knights, and Vaylin's old toy is lying in a puddle. Senya sees a darkly clad figure approaching from a distance and readies herself for a fight, and the figure uses the Force to engulf the field in fire. Senya's lightsaber is yanked away, and she grabs one from a dead Knight just before Vaylin attacks her.<ref name="Betrayed" />
