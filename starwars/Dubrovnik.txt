'''Dubrovnik''', historically known as '''Ragusa''', is a city in Croatia on the Dalmatian coast. It is one of the most prominent tourist destinations in the Mediterranean, a seaport and the centre of the Dubrovnik-Neretva County. Its total population is 42,615 (2011 census).

Scenes of the planet [[Canto Bight]] from [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi]] were filmed here.<ref>[VIDEO] Star Wars Episode VIII Starts Shooting in Dubrovnik this Week | Croatia Week</ref>
