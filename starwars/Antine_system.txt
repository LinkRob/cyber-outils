The '''Antine system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained the [[third moon of Antine]].<ref name="Survivor" /> It was situated at the coordinates I-19 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
