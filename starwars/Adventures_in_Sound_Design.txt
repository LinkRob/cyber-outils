"'''Adventures in Sound Design'''" is an article that appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 123|''Star Wars Insider'' 123]]. The article covers the appearance of [[Ben Burtt]], [[Matthew Wood]] and [[David W. Collins|David Collins]] at [[Celebration V]]. Collins, lead sound designer and voice director at [[LucasArts]], hosted the gathering and interviewed Burtt, lead sound designer for the ''[[Star Wars]]'' and ''[[Indiana Jones]]'' films, and [[Matthew Wood]], supervising sound director for the [[prequel trilogy]] and [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''The Clone Wars'']] series. The article was later reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2013|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2013]].
