The '''Balosar system''' was located in the [[Core Worlds]] in [[the galaxy]], situated in grid square L-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> The [[Star system|system]] contained the [[planet]] [[Balosar]].<ref name="CandM" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Balosar system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published on the [[StarWars.com]] website on [[June 24]], [[2025]],<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> as part of the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]].<ref name="Galaxy">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> The [[Balosar system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2006]] reference book ''[[The New Essential Guide to Alien Species]]'', published by [[Del Rey]].<ref name="TNEGtAS">''[[The New Essential Guide to Alien Species]]''</ref>
