'''Duriak''' was a [[Foodstuff|delicacy]].<ref name="Bucket's List">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-bibo-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 9 Fun Facts from "Bibo" - ''Star Wars Resistance''|date=2019-01-14}}</ref> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 ABY">{{ResistanceDate|1|12}}</ref> a patron described the [[rokkna]] [[Bibo]]'s odor as being similar to that of duriak.<ref name="Bucket's List" />

==Behind the scenes==
Duriak was originally to be mentioned in "[[Bibo (episode)|Bibo]],"<ref name="Bucket's List" /> the twelfth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[January 13]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney release">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> It was intended to be a reference to the real-world [[Wikipedia:Durian|durian fruit]]. The mention of the delicacy was ultimately removed from the episode, but it was included in the article "Bucket's List Extra: 9 Fun Facts from 'Bibo' &ndash; ''Star Wars Resistance''," which was posted on [[StarWars.com]] on [[January 14]], 2019.<ref name="Bucket's List" />
