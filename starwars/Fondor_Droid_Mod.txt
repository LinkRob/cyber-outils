The '''Fondor Droid Mod''' was a type of [[artificial intelligence]] that could be equipped in a [[V-21.1 Chevlex light haulcraft|Fondor Haulcraft]]. [[Luthen Rael]] had one equipped in the cockpit of the [[Luthen Rael's Fondor Haulcraft|modified]] haulcraft he was using as his personal [[transport]] in [[5 BBY]].

==Characteristics==
The Fondor Droid Mod<ref name="AldhaniST">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref> installed within [[Luthen Rael's Fondor Haulcraft]] was an intelligent and capable [[artificial intelligence]] companion to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[spy]]master [[Luthen Rael]]. It would function as a [[Pilot|co-pilot]] and [[navigator]] to Rael, trusted to suggest optimal travel routes and perform planetary landings selected from a variety of predetermined patterns. The droid would also guard the ship against intruders, using its [[photoreceptor]] in the ship's [[cockpit]] to observe the behavior of unsupervised passengers<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> and its [[audio sensor]] to listen for Rael's whistling which it would interpret as a sign to unlock the ship for him.<ref name="Reckoning">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref> It could also fluently communicate with Rael using an integrated [[vocabulator]] and make suggestions or raise concerns regarding the ship's safety and performance.<ref name="DOF">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref>

As it was connected with the entirety of the ship's systems, the Mod would also be used as a [[gunner]] and [[commander]] in combat situations. It would identify enemy ships through the [[radar]] and relay information regarding their model and capabilities to Rael, who would then suggest an optimal response based on the nature of the threat. The Mod could independently target enemy ships and then take control of the ship's [[laser cannon]] [[turret]], [[laser beams]], and [[projectile launcher]] countermeasures to destroy them while Rael focused on piloting the ship to safety. It could also find fake [[transponder code]]s and assign them to the ship within seconds to avoid detection from [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] patrols.<ref name="DOF" />

==History==
Luthen Rael's Droid Mod assisted Rael in landing to [[Ferrix]], where his master would meet [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] to recruit him in his [[Axis network|spy network]] or buy a stolen [[Imperial N-S9 Starpath Unit]] from him. While landing, it advised Rael to land 2.3 [[kilometer]]s away from the [[Ferrix City|city]], elaborating when questioned by an annoyed Luthen that it was the safest spot it could detect. Later, when Rael and Andor were [[Ambush on Ferrix|escaping]] from pursuing [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces|Pre-Mor Enforcement]] [[Tac-Corpo|corpos]], Rael, in a hurry, whistled to the Mod and it recognized the signal, unlocking the ship for him and Andor to make a quick getaway.<ref name="Aldhani" />

After landing in [[Aldhani]] where Rael was to drop Andor off to [[Vel Sartha]]'s [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team|team]], Andor was left alone in the Fondor's cockpit and briefly considered stealing the ship, but was deterred when the Mod turned its photoreceptor unit to look at him and asked if there was something it could help him with. Later the same day the Mod was asked to take the controls and successfully performed a Landing Protocol 037 to land on Coruscant.<ref name="Aldhani" />

A month later, the Mod assisted Rael when he came under attack by Imperial forces above Segra Milo: it fetched and assigned Alderaan Trade Guild transponder ID 12912505 to the ship, charged its countermeasures, and prompted by Rael it used them to destroy a pursuing [[TIE boarding craft|TIE Boarding Craft]] and a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]. At the same time, it performed the hyperspace calculations necessary for Rael's successful escape.<ref name="DOF" />

==Equipment==
The droid had a photoreceptor unit comprising one blue and two black photoreceptors, located in the cockpit of Rael's Fondor. It also had a vocabulator unit installed somewhere in the ship which allowed it to communicate with its passengers.<ref name="Aldhani" />
