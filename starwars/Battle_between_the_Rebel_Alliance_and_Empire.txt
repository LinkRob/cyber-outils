A '''battle''' took place during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. During the battle, Imperial [[Star Destroyer]]s clashed against mainly [[Mon Cala]] warships including some dating back to the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="FO Conversion Kit" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle goes on unnamed in its only appearance in Canon where it is depicted on the "Card: Predator" from [[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition]]. But it was previously named the [[Disaster at Deepspace Besh]] in Legends.<ref name="FO Conversion Kit" /><ref name="Rebellion2">[[Rebellion 4|''Rebellion'' 4]]</ref>
