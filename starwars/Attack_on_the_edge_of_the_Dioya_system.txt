The '''attack on the edge of the Dioya system''' was an incident that occurred shortly after two [[Paccosh]] [[refugee]] [[starship]]s escaped from their [[homeworld]] of [[Rapacc]] while it was under [[blockade]] by [[General]] [[Yiv]] of the [[Nikardun Destiny]]. The two vessels rendezvoused in a [[Unidentified quadruple star system|quadruple star system]], but one was unable to travel further. The working ship entered the territory of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], but the [[Nikardun]] located it at its exit point in the [[Dioya system]] due to its [[Void Guides|Void Guide]] [[navigator]] and destroyed it. The [[Chiss]] witnessed the incident, but had their attention drawn away when the Nikardun orchestrated a [[Paataatus]] [[Attack on Csilla|attack]] on the Chiss homeworld of [[Csilla]]. [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], looking into the attack on Csilla, investigated the wreckage in the Dioya [[Star system|system]], leading to his discovery of the [[Nikardun conquests]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> The attack helped cover up the [[Sunrise's civil war]] orchestrated by [[Lord]] "[[Jixtus]]" and [[Haplif]], as it prevented the Chiss from learning about the action for sometime.<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref>
