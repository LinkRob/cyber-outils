'''''Darth Vader and the Cry of Shadows'' 1''' is the first issue of five in the ''[[Star Wars: Darth Vader and the Cry of Shadows]]'' comic mini-series written by [[Tim Siedell]]. It was released on [[December 18]], [[2013]].

==Publisher's summary==
''During the Clone Wars, a single trooper&mdash;left for dead during battle&mdash;comes to hate the Jedi generals who abandoned him. Years later, he lives on, bitter and angry, until hearing tales of a great warrior. Now, this clone believes he may have found the leader he should have been following all along&hellip; Darth Vader!''

*''All-new series starring Darth Vader!''
