'''Air Commodore''' was a title held by the [[Utai]] [[Senin Vant]], who led the [[Utapau Skyforce]]&mdash;[[Planetary defense forces|planetary defense force]] of the Utai and [[Pau'an]] [[homeworld]] [[Utapau]]&mdash;during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="CV" />

==Behind the scenes==
The title of air commodore was first used in the current [[canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']].<ref name="CV" /> The title was originally created for the [[2005]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', which was written by [[Curtis Saxton]].<ref name="RotS C-S">''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>
