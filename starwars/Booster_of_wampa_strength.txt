The '''booster of wampa strength''' was an item offered for sale by a [[Unidentified merchant (Mos Eisley)|merchant]] working out of a stall in the [[Wonto District market]] bazaar in the [[city]] [[Mos Eisley]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The [[merchant]] claimed that the booster provided the consumer with the strength of the [[wampa]] [[species]], but in actuality it was a vial of medicinal [[bacta]] with added coloring.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] approached the merchant, who offered to sell her the booster or a [[solution of Loth-Cat dexterity]] to dispel her skepticism. However, when Vess suggested that the scam products were [[zoochberry]] juice in a vial, the merchant told her to keep her voice down and then offered one of the two products as a free sample in exchange for the thief not informing the criminal [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] of their [[Con artist|scams]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The booster of wampa strength appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The encounter with the merchant is optional, and the player can choose to select either the booster or the solution, although both options result in them gaining a single bacta vial.<ref name="Outlaws" />
