'''Burial pods''' were pods in which [[Nightsisters]] were [[Burial|buried]].<ref name="MassacreTriviaGallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/massacre-trivia-gallery|text="Massacre" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> The pods were hung on [[Grave Thorn]]s.<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==Description==
Burial pods could be made of cloth<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> or animal skins, which was mainly [[rancor]] hide.<ref name="Bug" /> When the [[Chant of Resurrection]] was completed, [[Nightsister Zombie]]s crawled out of the pods.<ref name="Massacre"> {{TCW|Massacre}}</ref>

During Nightsister [[Funeral|funerary rites]], cloth was washed in [[magick]]al [[water]]. Then the cloth was hand-braided together while reciting a [[spell of protection]]. Finally, the bodies were cleansed with fragrant [[oil]]s.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
