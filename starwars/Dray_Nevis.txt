'''Dray Nevis''' was an individual who served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] as a member of [[Rogue One]] alongside [[K-2SO]], [[Jyn Erso]], [[Moroff|Senna]], [[Lunak]], [[Sabine Wren|Ria Talla]] and [[Jerris Kestal]].<ref name="rogueart" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dray Nevis was one of several characters conceived to be a part of the Rogue One team seen in the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', but was cut from the final film.<ref name="rogueart" />
