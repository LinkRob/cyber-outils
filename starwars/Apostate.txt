The '''''Apostate''''' was a [[starship]] owned by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Nik Hepho]]. While Hepho was searching for adherents of the [[Church of the Force]] in [[Jedha City]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]]<ref name="ROUVG" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', including [[Nik Hepho]]'s [[time]] on [[Jedha]], to [[1 BBY]].</ref> the ship hovered in [[orbit]] above, operated by a [[Droid brain|droid intelligence]] that waited for the hunter to make contact via his [[backpack]]-mounted long-range [[transmitter]].<ref name="ROUVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Apostate'' was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released in [[2016]].<ref name="ROUVG" />
