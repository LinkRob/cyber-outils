'''Aurelia Tagge''' was an individual who owned the [[Nau'ur-class yacht|''Nau'ur''-class yacht]] known as ''[[Scarlet Dominion]]''. During the [[Imperial Era]], lifestyle [[Journalist|paparazzi]] often highlighted the [[yacht]] and its custom [[chromium]] finish.<ref name="S&S">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> Aurellia shared their name with the [[House of Tagge]].<ref name="WOTBH Boushh">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Aurelia Tagge was first mentioned in the [[2020]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Starships and Speeders]]''.<ref name="S&S" />
