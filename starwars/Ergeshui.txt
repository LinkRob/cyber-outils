'''Ergeshui''' was a [[Planet|world]] that existed within [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Boba Fett BWR 1">[[Boba Fett — Black, White & Red 1|''Boba Fett — Black, White & Red'' 1]]</ref> Between [[2 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Boba Fett — Black, White & Red 1|''Boba Fett — Black, White & Red'' 1]] take place after the [[Declaration of the Rebel Alliance|formation]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and before it is [[Declaration of a New Republic|reorganized]] into the [[New Republic]], which corresponds to between [[2 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] went to the world to deliver a [[human]] [[Unidentified human bounty|bounty]] to the [[alien]] criminal [[Tusk]], who subsequently [[Death|executed]] the human for backstabbing them sometime in the past.<ref name="Boba Fett BWR 1" />
