The [[Nikardun Destiny]] [[Yiv's battle forces|navy]] '''blockaded''' the [[Rapacc system]] as part of the [[Nikardun conquests]], a series of wars of conquest led by [[Nikardun]] [[General]] [[Yiv]] the Benevolent in the [[Unknown Regions]] of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> The blockade began by [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Start date" /> and lasted until around [[18 BBY]].<ref name="End date" />

During the blockade, [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn" of the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] led a [[mission to the Rapacc system]] and later a [[raid on Rapacc]], bypassing the blockade on behalf of the [[Ascendancy military|military]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

The blockade was ended with a [[Liberation of Rapacc|revolt]] of the [[Paccosh]] [[species]], [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Rapacc]], that occurred between the [[battle over Primea]] and Thrawn's [[Mission to Rapacc|return]] to Rapacc. During the revolt, the [[Paccian Governance]] commandeered and appropriated the [[Aelos (frigate)|''Aelos'']], one of the [[Nikardun blockade frigate]]s that had interdicted their system. [[Uingali foar Marocsaa]], leader of the [[Marocsaa subclan]] of Paccosh, painted the ''Aelos''{{'}} ventral surface with an image of a [[Chimaera (symbol)|chimaera]].<ref name="Greater Good" />
