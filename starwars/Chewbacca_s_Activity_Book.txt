'''''Chewbacca's Activity Book''''' was a children's activity book published by [[Random House]] in [[March]] of [[1979]], alongside ''[[Artoo Detoo's Activity Book]]'', ''[[Luke Skywalker's Activity Book]]'', and ''[[Darth Vader's Activity Book]]''. It was one of the few works licensed by the short-lived [[Lucasfilm]] subsidiary [[Black Falcon]].
