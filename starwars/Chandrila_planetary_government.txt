A '''planetary government''' governed the [[Core Worlds|Core World]] [[planet]] [[Chandrila]] during the [[Republic Era]]. The government was represented in the [[Galactic Senate]] by [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]], while her mother, [[Tanis (Chandrila)|Tanis]], served as the [[governor]] of [[Hanna City]] and the head of the government.<ref name="Senators" /> The [[Chandrilan]] government maintained an [[Chandrilan Embassy|embassy]] on [[Coruscant]], the [[capital]] of [[the galaxy]], where Senator Mothma and her [[Mothma family|family]] resided.<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> By [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|104}}</ref> another [[Unidentified governor of Hanna|individual]] was the governor of Hanna.<ref name="Aldhani" />
