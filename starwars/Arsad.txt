'''Arsad''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who worked as a [[doctor]] for the [[New Republic]] in a [[Unidentified New Republic veteran hospital|veteran's hospital]] in [[Darropolis]] on the [[planet]] [[Hosnian Prime]] in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> One of her patients at this time was the New Republic veteran [[Dade Hetkins]], who had lost his right [[Limb|leg]] during the cleanup on the [[moon]] of [[Endor]] following the [[Battle of Endor]]. Hetkins received a [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] replacement limb, and she aided him in first attempting to use it. After running several simple physical tests with the aid of an [[FX-7 medical assistant droid]], she declared that everything seemed fine and questioned Hetkins about his mental health. She was not fooled by his claims of being all right, and after her [[Medical droid|FX-7]] confirmed he was lying based on his medical readout. Arsad then instructed him to return to the [[Medcenter|hospital]] regularly for group therapy with other veterans, or attend sessions she would set up elsewhere if he wished to leave the planet. She also provided him with a prototype therapy droid, [[QT-9]], whom he begrudgingly took home.<ref name="LD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arsad first appeared in the novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', which was written by [[Chuck Wendig]] and released in [[2016]].<ref name="LD" />
