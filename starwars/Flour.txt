'''Flour''' was an ingredient used in [[foodstuff]]s. There were multiple varieties of flour, including [[basa root flour]],<ref name="A Recipe for Death">{{StoryCite|story=A Recipe for Death|book=Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I}}</ref> [[polystarch flour]],<ref name="Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref> and a pastry flour with a [[Color|midnight]] hue.<ref name="But What Does He Eat?">{{StoryCite|story=But What Does He Eat?|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>
