"'''Against the Pirate Armada'''" is a short narrative story published in ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'' in [[1987]]. It recounts how the [[Victory-class Star Destroyer/Legends|''Victory''-class Star Destroyers]] [[Crusader (Victory-class)|''Crusader'']] and ''[[Bombard]]'' [[Battle of Khuiumin|crushed]] the [[Eyttyrmin Batiiv]] [[Pirate/Legends|pirates]]. It was reprinted in the [[The Star Wars Sourcebook (Second Edition)|second edition]] of the book in [[1994]].

==Plot summary==
The [[Victory-class Star Destroyer/Legends|''Victory''-class Star Destroyers]] ''[[Bombard]]'' and [[Crusader (Victory-class)|''Crusader'']] are dispatched by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] to eliminate the [[Eyttyrmin Batiiv]] [[Pirate/Legends|pirates]]. Despite being vastly outnumbered, the pair of [[Star Destroyer/Legends|Star Destroyers]] comprehensively decimate the pirates during the [[Battle of Khuiumin]].<ref name="SWS" />
