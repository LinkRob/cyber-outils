The '''Atrocity on Mahranee''' occurred in [[19 BBY]] toward the end of the [[Clone Wars]] and resulted in the [[Death|genocide]] of all [[Mahran]] [[refugee]]s who were escaping from their embattled [[homeworld]] of [[Mahranee]]. When the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] targeted Mahranee for its plentiful resources, the native Mahran people appealed to the [[Jedi Order]] for aid. [[Jedi General]] [[Chubor]] brought a [[Galactic Republic]] task force of [[clone trooper]]s to Mahranee, but they were too late to prevent the [[Confederacy military|Confederate forces]] from laying waste to the [[planet]]'s [[Mahranee capital city|capital city]].

Although Chubor's detachment tried to escort thousands of Mahran civilians off-world to safety, the Separatists were unwilling to leave any survivors, killing the Mahran refugees alongside the [[Jedi]] and [[Cloning|clone]] [[soldier]]s. Separatist [[Head of State|leader]] [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] unequivocally blamed the Jedi Order for the massacre, leading the [[Jedi High Council]] to finally sanction an [[Mission to Raxus Secundus|operation]] to [[assassin]]ate Dooku as a means of stemming the war's casualties.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|For almost three standard years, this war has raged. We can barely even count the numbers of the fallen. But this&hellip;''"<br />"''All directly because of one man's ambition and evil.|Jedi Master [[Plo Koon]] and [[Mace Windu]] discuss Dooku's determination to annex Mahranee|<ref name="Dark Disciple" />}}
In the [[Standard year|year]] [[19 BBY]], near the end of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] set its sights on capturing [[Mahranee]], a member [[Planet|world]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] whose abundant resources made it a valuable target. [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]], the [[Head of State|leader]] of the Confederacy, had unsuccessfully attempted to win the planet over using false promises and lies before planning an assault to take the planet's resources by force. As a result, the planet's [[Homeworld|native]] [[Mahran]] inhabitants transmitted a [[Distress signal|distress call]] to the [[Jedi Order]], which responded by dispatching a detachment of [[clone trooper]]s under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Chubor]] to aid the Mahran against the Separatists.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

==The atrocity==
{{Quote|You've got your victory, Dooku. The planet is yours&hellip; let us have the people. We have entire families aboard, many of whom are injured. They're innocents!''"<br />"''My dear General Chubor. You should know by now that in a war, there is no such thing as an innocent.''"<br />"''Count, I repeat, our passengers are civilian families. Half of the refugees are younglings. Permit them, at least, to&mdash;''"<br />"''Younglings whose parents, unwisely, chose to ally with the Republic.|Chubor pleads Dooku to call off the Separatist attack|<ref name="Dark Disciple" />}}
[[File:Darth_Tyranus_RotS.png|thumb|left|280px|Dooku (pictured) refused Chubor's pleas to let the Mahran escape, resulting in their massacre.]]
When the [[Mahranee government]] refused to capitulate to their demands, the [[Confederacy military|Confederacy forces]] initiated an assault on the planet, turning it into a battlefield. Chubor and his [[Cloning|clone]] [[soldier]]s arrived too late to stop the Separatists from obliterating the planet's [[Mahranee capital city|capital city]], which was summarily reduced to rubble. Consequently, thousands of Mahran civilians&mdash;many of whom were injured&mdash;attempted to escape from their homeworld. While some of the [[refugee]]s departed aboard their planet's [[Starship|vessels]], others were ferried off-world aboard Republic craft, such as Chubor's [[Chubor's Republic frigate|Republic frigate]], with clone [[trooper]]s aiding in the escort operation. The Republic and Separatist forces battled each other in [[orbit]] of Mahranee, exchanging barrages of [[laser]] fire through the [[vacuum]] of [[Realspace|space]], while Mahran ships made their attempts to flee.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

Desperate to save the refugees, Chubor opened a direct [[Hologram|holographic]] transmission to Dooku. Chubor pleaded with Dooku to spare the Mahran, stating that his own [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|frigate]] was filled with civilian families; moreover, half of the Mahran were only [[youngling]]s. However, Dooku was unsympathetic to the plight of the Mahran. Knowing their conversation was being broadcast to the [[Jedi High Council]], Dooku took the opportunity to blame the Jedi Order for the billions of [[Death|casualties]] that the war had caused, claiming that such was the price of the Republic's resistance to his Confederacy. Chubor's [[frigate]] was the next ship to fall to Separatist fire, with members of Mahranee's prominent [[blood]]line of [[Ashu]]&mdash;including [[Ashu-Nyamal]], the Firstborn of Ashu&mdash;being among the deaths. Altogether, it took the Separatists a matter of [[Standard hour|hours]] to capture Mahranee and slaughter its thousands of inhabitants, resulting in a victory for their side.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Mahranee's fall is terrible. But it's only the most recent crime Dooku has committed against a world and its people.''"<br />"''The Mahran who died today already have more than enough company. Do we want to increase those numbers? One man's life must be weighted against those of potentially millions of innocents. Isn't protecting the innocent the very definition of what it means to be a Jedi? We are failing the Republic and its citizens. We must stop this&mdash;''now''.|Anakin Skywalker and Mace Windu, explicating the latter's proposal to assassinate Dooku|<ref name="Dark Disciple" />}}
[[File:JediCouncil-Conspiracy.png|thumb|right|280px|The massacre of the Mahran impelled the Jedi Council to sanction a plan to assassinate Dooku.]]
Once they had established control of Mahranee, the Confederate forces all but issued a death warrant for any surviving Mahran, claiming that they were extremely hostile and were to be killed on sight. Dooku's message to the [[Jedi Council]], meanwhile, was met with dismay and anguish from its recipients. At a subsequent session, the Council decided that the [[time]] had come to [[assassin]]ate Dooku, an act that [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]&mdash;the latter of whom had been invited by Master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] to sit in on the meeting&mdash;believed would collapse the Separatist regime and also prevent further bloodshed from occurring. [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] also agreed to such a plan, acknowledging that Dooku's schemes placed too many innocent lives at risk.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

The maverick Jedi Master [[Quinlan Vos]] was assigned the task of killing Dooku with the aid of [[Asajj Ventress]], Dooku's jilted [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]]&ndash;turned&ndash;[[bounty hunter]]. Although they [[Mission to Raxus Secundus|failed]] to execute Dooku,<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> the [[count]] would eventually meet his demise at the hands of Skywalker during the [[Battle of Coruscant]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Atrocity on Mahranee served as the opening scene of author [[Christie Golden]]'s [[2015]] novel, ''[[Dark Disciple]]'', based on an unfinished eight-episode story arc for the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series written by [[Katie Lucas]], [[Matt Michnovetz]], and [[Dave Filoni]].<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> The [[2019]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' later identified the event as the "Atrocity on Mahranee."<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> The [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' incorrectly dates the event to [[20 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
