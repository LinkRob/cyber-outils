'''8422''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] that served the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During the [[Battle of Murkhana]], the [[droid]] approached [[Captain]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] with a [[E-5 blaster rifle]] in hand.<ref name="Tarkin Revealed" />

==Behind the scenes==
8422 was pictured in "[[Tarkin Revealed]]," a [[2014]] article released in [[Star Wars Insider 153|''Star Wars Insider'' 153]]. While the [[droid]]'s designation can be spotted on an image,<ref name="Tarkin Revealed" /> the numbers could not be identified until ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'' gave a translation for B1 numerals.<ref name="Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
