'''Beepers''' was an [[astromech droid]] from the [[Old Republic-era]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], he served the [[droid]] mechanic [[Rek Minuu]] before learning of Minuu's [[Thief|thievery]] of droid parts. Beepers left Minuu and joined the [[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Nubs]], and [[Lys Solay]] at [[Dotti's Droid Workshop]]&mdash;alongside fellow astromechs [[Dozer]] and [[Gigi (droid)|Gigi]]&mdash;<ref name="DroidFriends" />sometime between [[232 BBY]] and [[230 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|301A}}</ref> At the workshop, [[Dotti]] replaced Beepers' old [[vocabulator]] with a new one.<ref name="DroidFriends" />
