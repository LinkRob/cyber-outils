'''Celena''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who founded an anti-violence [[artist commune]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Nooroyo]]. At some point, five [[Torada Collective]] [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] [[artist]]s&mdash;including the man [[Kharl]] and the woman [[Antraya]], who was the sister of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet [[Naboo]]'s incumbent [[Monarch of Naboo|queen]], [[Jamillia]]&mdash;left their [[homeworld]] to join the commune. In [[22 BBY]], [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of Naboo and her secret paramour, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Mission to Nooroyo|met]] with Celena in the commune, having been sent by Jamillia to bring the Torada Collective artists back to Naboo. Celena led the pair through the compound and introduced them to Kharl before leaving them to talk.

==Biography==
{{Quote|We're anti-violence, so conflict is not permitted. Any disputes must be settled by me. Residents can't be unreasonably messy in their quarters, and they must help maintain the gardens and such while they stay here.|Celena, to Anakin Skywalker and Padmé Amidala about the former's artist commune|<ref name="QH" />}}
The [[human]] [[Gender|woman]] Celena lived during the [[Republic Era]]. At some point, she established an anti-violence [[artist commune]] on<ref name="QH" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Naboo system|Naboo]] and [[Nooroyo system]]s</ref> [[planet]] [[Nooroyo]]. Celena personally managed the residents living in the commune's homes, requiring that the [[artist]]s staying there adopt a peaceful lifestyle as they practiced their crafts. Residents were also expected to maintain the artist commune's [[garden]]s and abide by its other rules, such as those surrounding the [[Foodstuff|food]] they [[Diet|consumed]], with any disputes being handled by Celena. At some point, five [[Torada Collective]] [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] artists&mdash;including the man [[Kharl]] and the woman [[Antraya]], who was the sister of<ref name="QH" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> planet [[Naboo]]'s incumbent [[Monarch of Naboo|queen]], [[Jamillia]]&mdash;left their [[homeworld]] to join the commune. On Nooroyo, the Naboo artists integrated into the commune under Celena's leadership and formed an attachment to their newfound home.<ref name="QH" />

[[File:Battle_couple_on_Geonosis.png|thumb|left|300px|Padmé Amidala and Anakin Skywalker (center left) spoke to Celena in the artist commune.]]
Following the [[First Battle of Geonosis|start]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="QH" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Naboo's [[senator]]&mdash;[[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]&mdash;and her secret paramour, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Mission to Nooroyo|flew]] the former's [[Padmé Amidala's H-type Nubian yacht (Malevolence)|Nubian yacht]] to the commune. Upon entry, Celena welcomed the pair, though her demeanor stiffened upon learning that Jamillia had sent them to bring the Torada Collective artists home. The woman agreed to bring them to the artists, leading them along one of the commune's pathways.<ref name="QH" />

She then further explained some of the rules its residents followed but eventually hinted to the pair that she did not wish to reveal more information about the artist commune, as neither intended to stay long. Celena then entered and argued with Kharl, eventually escorting him out to speak with Amidala and Skywalker before returning to the house before they began conversing. Though the other artists ultimately refused to leave, Antraya opted to reluctantly depart Celena's artist commune to reunite with Jamillia on Naboo.<ref name="QH" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|We're here on behalf of the Queen of Naboo. Some of her people are here, and she wanted to be sure they were safe, given recent events.''"<br />"''That is understandable. We are all galactic citizens, regardless of our political affiliations.|Padmé Amidala and Celena|<ref name="QH" />}}
Celena led an anti-violence commune that welcomed all beings who wished to practice their crafts without oversight. She felt that those staying as residents on her property needed to follow its rules and handled any disputes that arose between visitors herself. Though welcoming to Amidala and Skywalker initially, after learning why they had come, she adopted a more stiff demeanor. Celena had [[Color|porcelain]] skin.<ref name="QH" />
