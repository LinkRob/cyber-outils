'''C-deck''' was a location in the [[Undercity]] of [[Sundari]] on [[Mandalore]]. During the [[Siege of Mandalore]], a ground unit of the [[332nd Company]] were dispatched to reinforce C-deck, but were ambushed by the renegade [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]], sustaining heavy casualties. After the attack, former [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Rex]] visited the spot of the ambush, and after learning what had happened, Tano ordered Rex to make sure C-deck was properly fortified.<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice" />

==Behind the scenes==
C-deck was first mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|Season Seven]] episode "[[The Phantom Apprentice]]."<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice" />
