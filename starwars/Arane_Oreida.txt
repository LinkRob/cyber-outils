'''Arane Oreida''' was a [[Zabrak]] [[Gender|female]] rebel. She had a distinct dislike of [[human]]s, but she notably worked with [[Saw Gerrera]] and his [[Partisans]]. Oreida also had a group of [[Arane's partisans|her own partisans]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

Oreida had [[Color|yellow]] skin and green-black horns. She wore a faceplate that was also adorned with metal spikes. She had an imperious voice and also possessed monetary resources that allowed her to hire other partisans such as Gerrera.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
