'''Epfer-hens''' were a type of [[chicken]] that could be [[Diet|eaten]] as [[Foodstuff|food]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Nihil]] [[I'nah]]'s [[I'nah's cruiser|cruiser]] kept roasted epfer-hen available in the [[Starship|vessel's]] galley. While on the [[cruiser]]<ref name="EFV" /> in [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> the aspiring [[pirate]] [[Zyle Keem]] visited the galley and began taking food, staring unblinkingly at the Nihil nearby as they shoved half a roasted epfer-hen in their [[clothing]]. Afterward, Keem fled with the provisions in an [[Zyle's shuttle|escape shuttle]] to travel to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]].<ref name="EFV" />
