"'''Duel of the Reprobates'''" is a story in the [[canon]] one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Revelations (2023) 1|''Revelations'' (2023) 1]]. The story was written by [[Marc Guggenheim]], illustrated by [[Salva Espín]] and [[Israel Silva]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 20]], [[2023]].

According to the contents page of ''Revelations'', the story is related to the ''[[Star Wars: Jabba the Hutt (Marvel)|Star Wars: Jabba the Hutt]]'' comic series, which has seen no further information since this release.

==Plot summary==
{{Quote|Reach for that blaster, lose your hand.''"<br />"''If you're gonna kill me, what do I need my ''hand'' for? You&hellip;''are'' here to kill me, right?|Bane Malar and Rik Duel|<ref name="Reprobates">{{StoryCite|story=Duel of the Reprobates|book=Revelations (2023) 1}}</ref>}}
[[Rik Duel]] attempts to outrun [[Bounty hunter|bounty hunter]] [[Bane Malar]] before being hit by a [[Stun|stun blast]] and falling to the ground. Bane informs the bounty that he is acting on orders of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]] to kill him for owing eighty thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. Rik attempts to reason with the hunter that he could be more use to the [[Crime lord]] alive than dead. This leads to Rik being employed by the [[Hutt]] as his top [[Lieutenant]], running several missions for the crime syndicate to pay back the credits he owes. Rik infiltrates [[Gulhadar prison|the prison planet Gulhadar]] getting in trouble with the [[Benelex Marshal Service]], assassinates [[Voss|Boss Voss]] of the [[Enthian mob]] by causing him to accidentally choke and accidentally knocking over an imperial bank [[Vaultspeeder]] all for Jabba. Back on [[Tatooine]], inside [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace,]] Rik attempts to negotiate that all he has done for the crime lord has been enough to get a significant discount on the debt he owes. However, Jabba reiterates that his reward is not having his internal organs removed. Continuing with the debt to Jabba, Rik is taken to [[Bib Fortuna]] to discuss a new assignment. Rik is taken to meet with the other members of the group, [[Dani Gammill]] and [[Chihdo Jo]], who Rik is familiar with, to work together on the new job.
