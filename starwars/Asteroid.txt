An '''asteroid''' was a large solid object orbiting a [[star]] that was smaller than a [[planet]]. Asteroids could be found in [[asteroid field]]s,<ref name="Episode V" /> as well as around planets.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> Asteroids housed facilities such as the [[Polis Massa]] medical facility.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> Asteroids could support life, especially if they had their own thin [[atmosphere]], with rocks and minerals forming the basis of many [[Foodstuff|food]] chains. Many monstrous creatures dwelt within asteroids, with [[mynock]]s and [[fyrnock]]s thriving on asteroids<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> and [[Exogorth|Space slugs]] also known to inhabit them.<ref name="Episode V" />

==History==
[[File:Acme-GamblerRush.png|thumb|left|250px|The ''Millennium Falcon'' flies through asteroids]]
Over the course of their existence, asteroids struck the eight [[moon]]s of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[gas giant]] [[planet]] [[Navlaas]]. Those eight moons, including the ice moon [[Laanen]], were thus left scarred by asteroid impacts.<ref name="TLYB">{{EA|url=games/starwars/squadrons/news/the-light-you-bring-short-story|text=The Light You Bring|int=The Light You Bring}}</ref> [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Jango Fett]] had a [[Dogfight over Geonosis|dogfight]] in an asteroid field above [[Geonosis]].<ref name="Episode II" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Han Solo]], [[captain]] of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', escaped from the [[Death Squadron]] by flying into the [[Hoth asteroid field|Hoth asteroid belt]].<ref name="Episode V" /> By the time of the [[Battle of Hoth]], there were no official records of exploration of the center of asteroids.<ref name="SYWtBaJ?">''[[The Empire Strikes Back: So You Want to Be a Jedi?]]''</ref> When Solo and his comrades entered what they believed to be a cave within<ref name="Episode V" /> the "[[Big One]]" asteroid,<ref name="BMF 39">{{BuildFalconCite|39|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> they discovered they had actually entered an [[exogorth]]<ref name="Episode V" /> named [[Sy-O]]<ref name="No Cave">{{StoryCite|story=This Is No Cave|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> and escaped,<ref name="Episode V" /> much to Sy-O's sorrow.<ref name="No Cave" />
