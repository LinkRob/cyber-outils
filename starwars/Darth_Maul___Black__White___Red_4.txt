'''''Darth Maul – Black, White & Red'' 4''' is the fourth and final issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] anthology miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Darth Maul – Black, White & Red]]''. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Luca Pizzari]], [[Will Sliney]], [[Jay Leisten]], [[Roberto Poggi]], and [[Andres Mossa]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[July 31]], 2024.

==Publisher's summary==
''WHERE IS THE DARKEST SIDE OF THE FORCE? An assassin in the shadows. A forgotten temple. A quest for silence. Why does PALPATINE send DARTH MAUL to an ancient world? And who pays the price&hellip;for patience?''

==Plot summary==
On an unknown [[planet]], [[Sith]] [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] [[Darth]] [[Maul]] infiltrates the [[fortress]] of a [[governor]] and [[assassin]]ates him and his entire [[bodyguard|elite garrison]] with ease. After leaving on his [[starship|ship]] the ''[[Scimitar]]'', Maul is contacted by his [[Sith Master|master]] [[Darth Sidious]] who reminds Maul that his only task was to [[death|eliminate]] the governor; the Sith's success depends on strategy just as much as strength. He is given his next [[Mission to Ah Re Won|mission]], which is to go to a [[Ah Re Won Sith temple|forgotten Sith temple]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] world [[Ah Re Won]] and control his rage by [[meditation|meditating]] in stillness and quiet until Sidious arrives. 

On Ah Re Won, Darth Maul arrives and is greeted by an [[Alma (Ah Re Won)|innkeeper]] who offers a room and two [[foodstuff|meals]] a [[Standard day|day]] for only two [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. When she asks what else he could want, Maul responds with peace and quiet. Venturing off into the [[forest]], Maul locates the ruins of an ancient [[Sith temple]] and begins to meditate. Later, the [[Unidentified town (Ah Re Won)|village's]] [[Sullustan]] [[mayor]] pays the innkeeper a visit and informs her that she's three months behind on payments. When the innkeeper unable to fully pay up, the mayor begins to try and force her out by tearing down [[Alma's bed and breakfast|her building]], and when he tries to burn it down, he is met by Maul who was disturbed by the racket.

The mayor meets a grizzly fate at Maul's hands, and the next day multiple [[security droid]]s take notice of the mayor's absence. As they search the town, Maul spots a large native beast called a [[Karafankata]] digging up and maiming the mayor's corpse. Maul has no problem with this since it takes the problem of the missing mayor off his hands. The next day, a [[Alcohol|drunken]] group arrives looking for a good time and immediately refuse the innkeeper's offers, preferring to stay in the fancier [[Unidentified hotel (Ah Re Won)|hotel]]. When they go into the forest, the party's leader [[Prince]] [[Afolonkin]] stumbles across the tracks of a Karafankata and clumsily damages the ruins of the Sith temple, angering Maul.

[[File:DarthsMaulandSidious-MaulBWR4.png|thumb|left|300px|Darth Sidious takes joy in Darth Maul's destruction.]]
Later that night, Prince Afolonkin prepares his hunting party to search after the beast, but when they find it protecting its babies, they are ambushed by Darth Maul who picks them off with [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|his lightsaber]] one by one. They are cut down, granting Maul some time of peace. The following morning, a convoy of ships and [[Tank|tanks]] arrive. The father of Prince Afolonkin, the [[Pirate King of Sector Nine]], fires upon the hotel with a tank until the security droids arrive. The [[king]] questions where his son and his group is, but is informed that he is likely dead; the hunter became the hunted. 

Furious that his son died, he looks for people to blame and gets angered at Alma since she supposedly "stood by while his son died". He orders the tank to fire at her inn. Alma flees to her ship and tries to get away. The [[Pirate]] King orders his men to open fire on the starship, but he turns around to find many of them dead at the hands of the Darth Maul. When the Pirate King sounds the charge to kill Maul, the Sith apprentice uses [[the Force]] to weave his way through and dodge their attacks, allowing him to swiftly take them out. This allows for the Innkeeper to escape unharmed, and while the remaining few pirates try to flee, Maul uses the Force to [[Telekinesis|hurl]] a tank at their ship, obliterating the remaining few. 

Having finally achieved peace and quiet, Maul returns to the Sith Temple to mediate. Darth Sidious soon arrives and is pleased with the destruction, as it allowed for Maul to meditate in stillness and quiet.
