'''Albert''', also known as '''Albert the Hutt''', was a [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]] [[Professor/Legends|professor]] who wore circular [[Spectacles|glasses]].<ref name="Jedi Academy" /> Around [[196 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Jedi Academy (novel)|''Jedi Academy'']] states that [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]], who was born in [[896 BBY/Legends|896 BBY]] according to ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'', is 700 [[Standard year/Legends|years]] old. Therefore, ''Jedi Academy'' takes place around [[196 BBY]].</ref> the student news group [[The Padawan Observer]] made several announcements on a [[Duoday]] in the fifth issue of their 1012th volume. One announcement claimed that Albert would be visiting the [[Jedi academy]] on the [[planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] on [[Quadday]] at [[Time/Legends|1200]] for a lecture about "the Physics of [[The Force/Legends|the Force]]."<ref name="Jedi Academy" />

==Behind the scenes==
