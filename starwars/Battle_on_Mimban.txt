The '''battle on Mimban''',<ref name="Solo Meet the Crew">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Meet the Crew]]''</ref> also known as the '''battle at Mimban''',<ref name="Timelines" /> was one of the bloodiest battles<ref name="Bust Collection 51">{{BustCollectionCite|51|Character}}</ref> of the [[Imperial Military]]'s [[Mimban Campaign]]. The engagement was also infiltrated by [[Beckett's gang]] in order to steal an [[Y-45 armored transport hauler]] for their planned [[heist on Vandor]].<ref name="Solo" /> One member of the gang, [[Korso]], was [[Death|killed]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> Charging back to [[Imperial Forward Field Operations|Camp Forward]] under "[[Captain]]" [[Tobias Beckett]]'s "command," the [[Corporal]] [[Han Solo]] took notice of the fact that the gang were not [[Imperial Army trooper]]s.<ref name="Solo" /> In his ensuing '''escape from Mimban''',<ref name="Bust Collection 51" /> Solo teamed up with the im[[prison]]ed [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] to unite with the gang and escape the active warzone. Solo hoped to use his earnings from the heist with the gang to return to his [[homeworld]] [[Corellia]] and reunite with his lover [[Qi'ra]], while Chewbacca hoped to find Wookiees from his tribe or family.<ref name="Solo" />

==Aftermath==
After escaping Mimban, Solo and Chewbacca traveled to the planet [[Vandor]], where Beckett enlisted their services in stealing [[coaxium]] from an Imperial [[20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport|conveyx transport]] in order to pay his debts to [[Dryden Vos]], the [[crime lord]] of the [[Crimson Dawn]]. Due to the interference of [[Enfys Nest]] and her [[Cloud-Riders]], the heist failed and Val and Durant were killed in the process.<ref name="Solo" />

[[File:Punch-it-chewie-joe-corroney.png|thumb|left|250px|After he left the Empire on Mimban, Solo had set the stage for the rest of his life.]]
Solo and Beckett managed to convince Dryden Vos to give them a second chance with the help of the crime lord's lieutenant [[Qi'ra]], an old friend of Solo. After enlisting the services of [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and his ship the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', Solo and his companions managed to [[Raid on Kessel|steal]] coaxium from the [[Pyke Syndicate]]'s mines on the planet [[Kessel]].<ref name="Solo" />

After making the [[Kessel Run]] in 12 [[parsec]]s, Han and his companions traveled to the planet [[Savareen]] to rendezvous with Dryden Vos. However, Enfys Nest and her Cloud-Riders convinced them not to give the coaxium to Vos. Despite Beckett's betrayal, Han and Qi'ra managed to [[Showdown on Savareen|defeat]] Vos and his [[Hylobon Enforcers]]. Beckett was later killed during a confrontation with Solo. Even though Qi'ra left him, Solo and Chewbacca left together,<ref name="Solo" /> ultimately becoming [[smuggler]]s<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> and winning the ''Falcon'' from Calrissian.<ref name="Solo" />
