'''''Ewoks''''' is a picture-book based on the [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'']] tv series with exclusive new stories. It was released exclusively in [[Wikipedia:Spain|Spain]] in [[1986]] by [[Plaza Joven]]. In late [[2014]], an English translation by [[Abel G. Peña]] was made available via [[StarWars.com]].
