'''''A New Hope''''' ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]]: '''''Eine Neue Hoffnung''''' ) is an original audio drama adaptation of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope]] written by [[Oliver Döring]]. It contains dramatized passages from the film script performed by a cast in German translation along with narration by [[Joachim Kerzel]]. The compact disc was released by the German label [[WortArt]] in 2004 as part of a series of six single-disc audio adaptations of the original and prequel trilogies.
