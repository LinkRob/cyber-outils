The '''blue water''' was an oasis found in a [[desert]] on the [[Unknown location (Phantidean)|celestial body]] on which the [[Phantidean]] [[species]] conducted a sacred [[Pilgrimage (Phantidean)|pilgrimage]] to end their life cycle. During the [[High Republic Era]], Phantideans would embark on the pilgrimage to deliver their elders to the blue water. There, the elders would wade into the [[water]] and pass on into the [[Cosmic Force]]. Due to the high spiritual importance of their pilgrimage and their cultural dedication to pacifism, the Phantideans were joined by [[Jedi]] to defend them from the desert's many predators as they journeyed to the blue water. [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]]'s memory of joining the pilgrimage to the blue water as a [[Padawan]] stuck with her and guided her into the climax of the [[Nihil conflict]].<ref name="Stellan's Wisdom" />
