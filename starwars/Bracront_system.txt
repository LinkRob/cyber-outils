The '''Bracront system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Tarabba sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square M-19 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the moon [[Bracront]].<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 1" />
