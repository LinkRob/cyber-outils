The '''mother''' of [[Chassellon Stevis]], a [[human]] [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Apprentice Legislator]] from the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], was a [[Gender|female]] individual who lived during the [[Imperial Era]]. At some point, they became the head of [[Coruscant diplomatic legation|Coruscant's diplomatic legation]].<ref name="LPOA" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]].</ref> Stevis told the [[Alderaanian]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] about his [[Parent|mother's]] occupation and reflected that their participation in the Imperial [[Apprentice Legislature]] program put them on the same [[diplomat]]ic path as their parents.<ref name="LPOA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chassellon Stevis' mother was mentioned in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', a [[2017]] young-adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="LPOA" />
