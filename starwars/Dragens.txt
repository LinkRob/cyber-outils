The '''Dragens''' were a group active during the [[Imperial Era]] whose members included [[Kin Dragen]].<ref name="Aphra 3" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 3|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 3]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Kin had supposedly made a rude remark about the [[Domina Tagge|aunt]] of the [[House of Tagge]] scion [[Ronen Tagge]], which Tagge reacted to by having Kin's "manners corrected."<ref name="Aphra 3" />

While hosting a party in his [[Ronen Tagge's Private Penthouse|penthouse]] in [[Canto Bight]]<ref name="Aphra 3" />&mdash;the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Cantonica]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref>&mdash;that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Tagge was told by a [[Unidentified party guest|guest]] that they were surprised to not see a member of the Dragens at the event. In response, Tagge informed the guest of what Kin had allegedly done during their last conversation and that he had corrected Kin's manners.<ref name="Aphra 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dragens were mentioned in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 3|third issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 3" /> on [[August 26]], 2020. Prior to the release of the comic, the group was mentioned in a [https://www.starwars.com/news/doctor-aphra-3-preview preview article] of the issue that was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[August 19]] of the same year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/doctor-aphra-3-preview|text=Running Out of Options in Marvel's ''Doctor Aphra'' #3 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref>
