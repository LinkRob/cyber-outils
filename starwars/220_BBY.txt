The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''220 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3057 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''201 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7757''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*By this point, the [[Blue Dragon]] pirates had begun wrecking significant damage on [[Fire (city)|Fire]], remaining the single most destructive force to plague the station for the next twenty years.<ref name="Vow" /><ref name="Dragon 20">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the webnovel states that [[Fire (city)|Fire]] had been plagued by the [[Blue Dragon]] for [[Standard decade|decades]], the series of events must have began by around [[220 BBY]].</ref>
*[[Zhang Jincheng]] begins to ascend the hierarchy of Fire, the city in the Aakaash system.<ref name="Zhang ascension">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the webnovel states that [[Zhang Jincheng]] began to ascend the hierarchy of [[Fire (city)|Fire]], a [[city]] in the [[Aakaash system]], twenty [[Standard year|years]] before. Therefore, Zhang must have began ascending the ranks around [[220 BBY]].</ref>
*[[Jamie Brasson]] becomes the [[President]] of [[Begamor]] and is elected to represent the planet as a [[senator]] of the Republic Senate. While Brasson appears to be a benevolent [[Politics|politician]], he indulges in corruption and transforms Begamor into a [[dictatorship]]. Brasson develops a cult of personality, brainwashing citizens with holographic programs worshipping himself, isolating his world from the Republic [[HoloNet]], and enforcing strict surveillance over Begamor. An appearance of prosperity for the planet's [[Green Dawn|capital city]] is maintained while poverty, slavery, and organ trading is left to fester in the streets.<ref name="Vow">''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''</ref><ref name="Jamie politician">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the webnovel states that [[Jamie Brasson]] had risen to power for twenty [[Standard year|years]], the [[Politics|politician's]] rise must have taken place around [[220 BBY]].</ref>
*[[Ma]], an executive of the Land & Sky Corporation that rules the Aakaash system, dies of suspicious circumstances.<ref name="Ma death">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the webnovel states that [[Ma]], an executive of the [[Land & Sky Corporation]] that rules the [[Aakaash system]], [[Death|died]] of suspicious circumstances twenty [[Standard year|years]] ago, Ma must have died around [[220 BBY]].</ref>
*[[Ady Sun'Zee]] began training [[Nooa]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Tales">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "The Sacred Garden" from ''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge – Last Call]]'' to around 200 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref>
