'''Corey Macourek''' is an artist. He provided illustrations for several [[Wizards of the Coast]] products, including ''[[The New Jedi Order Sourcebook]]'', several articles in ''[[Star Wars Gamer]]'' magazine and several illustrations for the [[Star Wars Trading Card Game]].
