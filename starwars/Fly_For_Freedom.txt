'''''Fly For Freedom''''' was a [[Propaganda|recruitment]] [[Painting|poster]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] created by the [[Mandalorian]] [[artist]] [[Sabine Wren]]. Initially, Wren only intended it as a gift for her friend and fellow [[Spectres|rebel]] [[Hera Syndulla]], [[Phoenix Leader|lead]] [[pilot]] of [[Phoenix Squadron]]. However, the artwork caught the [[eye]] of [[Mon Mothma]], founder of the Alliance, who thought it a perfect representation of the effectiveness of rebel [[Starfighter|fighter]] forces. Mothma then asked Wren to complete the painting and add a call to action, the phrase "Fly For Freedom!" [[Writing|written]] in [[High Galactic]] letters.<ref name="Propaganda" />
