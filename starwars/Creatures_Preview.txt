"'''Creatures Preview'''" was an article in the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 4|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 4]]. It was written by [[Bill Smith]]. Parts of the article were subsequently reprinted on [[Hyperspace/Legends|Hyperspace]] under the titles ''Pantolomin Halfback'' and ''Maungur''. It served as a sample of content from the sourcebook [[Creatures of the Galaxy]].
