'''Dark Squadron''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] active during the [[Galactic Civil War]], led by former [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[flight instructor]] [[Yad Grelin]]. Two of Grelin's former [[cadet]]s, who had defected with him, were also part of the squadron. The squadron was defeated during [[Rogue Squadron]]'s attack on [[Tibanna]] [[gas]] platforms on [[Alaban]].<ref name="LEGO" />
