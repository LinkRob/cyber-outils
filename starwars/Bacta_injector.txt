A '''bacta injector''' was a device that injected [[bacta]] into an individual. A bacta injector could have a [[burst injector]] installed to increase the speed and effectiveness of bacta it injected.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] traded nine units of [[durasteel]] and three [[4XB Calculation Unit]]s with the [[Jawa]] [[merchant]] [[Pinota Pakinota]] in exchange for a burst injector which she used to improve the bacta injector she used.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bacta injectors were mentioned item in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The player must obtain a burst injector, fifteen units of [[transparisteel]] and six [[helicyclic gear]]s to unlock the "Enhanced Bacta Injector" expert ability which results in the player recovering more health instantly when using [[bacta]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
