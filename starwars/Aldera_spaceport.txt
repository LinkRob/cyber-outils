The '''Aldera spaceport''' was the central public spaceflight [[Spaceport|facility]] located in [[Aldera]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Leia" /> It was considered one of the best starports in the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref>

In [[9 BBY]],<ref name=" Kenobi Date">{{KenobiDate|I}}</ref> the [[House of Organa|Royal Family of Alderaan]] greeted [[Queen]] [[Breha Organa]]'s sister [[Duchess]] [[Celly Organa]] and her family at the spaceport upon their arrival.<ref name="Part I">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref> Later the family greeted [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] at the spaceport who returned [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] [[toy droid]] [[L0-LA59|Lola]] to her.<ref name="Part VI">{{Kenobi|Part VI}}</ref>

In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Leia Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] birth in [[19 BBY]]. The events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' commence just after Organa's sixteenth [[birthday]], which places the events of the novel in [[3 BBY]].</ref> Leia traveled through the spaceport to get to the ''[[Tantive IV]]'' in order for to carry out a her [[human]]itarian mission to the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet [[Wobani]] to deliver [[Foodstuff|food]] [[Ration pack|rations]] to [[refugee]]s on the planet as part of her [[Challenge of the Heart]].<ref name="Leia" />
