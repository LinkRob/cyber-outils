'''Abednedos''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] of the [[Abednedo (planet)|same name]] in the [[Colonies]] region of the [[Galaxy]]. Abednedo were [[humanoid]] and [[mammal]]ian, and their face featured dangling [[mouth]] tendrils and two fleshy nostrils.

They were a common sight in the galaxy, and were found in multiple, contrasting professions. Notable Abednedo included [[Prauf]] (a friend of [[Cal Kestis]]), [[Resistance pilot]]s [[Ello Asty]] and [[C'ai Threnalli]], [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] tech specialist [[Antrot]], and [[Senator]] [[Brasmon Kee]] of Abednedo.

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:Brasmon_Kee.jpg|thumb|left|Abednedo senator Brasmon Kee]]
Abednedos were a [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that were [[Homeworld|native]] to a [[planet]] also called [[Abednedo (planet)|Abednedo]], located in the [[Colonies]] region of [[the galaxy]]. A [[mammal]]ian species,<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> They could be [[Color|brown]],<ref name="TFA" /> cream,<ref name="SW:U" /> gray,<ref name="Visual" /> orange,<ref name="Adventures 5" /> pink, or tan<ref name="Visual" /> in color, and those who were brown often had skin that was mottled with gray. Abednedo had fleshy, thin nostrils and black [[eye]]s, which sat in sockets protruding from the side of their elongated head. Abednedo also possessed wattles, or facial tendrils, remnants of sensory organs that helped their [[Abednedo precursor species|ancestors]] to navigate underground in the dark.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> They could grow hair above their eyes and on the side<ref name="Visual" /> and back<ref name="Star Wars: Poe Dameron 6: Lockdown, Part III" /> of their heads, which could be blonde,<ref name="Poe Dameron 1">[[Poe Dameron 1|''Poe Dameron'' 1]]</ref> brown,<ref name="TFA" /> gray,<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> or white<ref name="Visual" /> in color. Abednedo had [[Limb|arms]] that terminated in five digits, and legs that ended in three.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> At least one Abednedo, the [[scavenger]] [[Roodown|"Crusher" Roodown]], was significantly larger than all other known Abednedo.<ref name="Roodown Databank">{{Databank|crusher-roodown|Crusher Roodown}}</ref>

==Society and culture==
A gregarious and clever species, the Abednedos' curiosity and acceptance of other species, as well as their skill with languages, made them a common sight in the galaxy. Many Abednedos were able to understand and speak [[Galactic Basic Standard|Basic]].<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> Their own [[language]], [[Abednedish]],<ref name="Databank" /> had its own [[writing]] system.<ref name="Visual" /> A colonial species, they had migrated and adapted to dominate many worlds.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

Abednedos were strong supporters of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />

==History==
[[File:Ello Asty XWM 2017 Quarter 2 Tournament Kit.png|thumb|300px|right|Ello Asty joined the Resistance as a pilot.]]
Abednedos evolved from an underground [[precursor species]]. They carved out a series of interconnected tunnel-dens, or warrens, to create massive cathedrals, which housed tens of thousands of family units.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />

At some point, the precursor species evolved into the Abednedo, and they began to start living on the surface of their planet. There, they built multiple [[City|cities]], spanning the surface of Abednedo. The cities were endless sources of amusement and interest for Abednedos, but in contrast, could strike outsiders as chaotic, due to them including many ornamental spires, flagpoles, and riotously colored carvings.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />

Shortly after the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> due to their allegiance to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]],<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] occupied Abednedo, and the planet was [[Attack on Abednedo|subject]] to a [[climate disruption array]].<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> As such, they later elected to become part of the New Republic.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> After the [[Resistance]] was formed by [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], the Abednedo were early supporters of Organa, and eventually several Abednedo joined the Resistance.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Abednedo in the galaxy==
[[File:SlowenLo-CGSWG.png|thumb|left|200px|Slowen Lo]]
A common sight in the galaxy, Abednedos were found in multiple professions, including freight crewmembers, soldiers, bureaucrats, and slicers.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" />

Some Abednedo served in the Alliance to Restore the Republic. Shortly before the Battle of [[Endor]], [[Antrot]] was a [[Gender|male]] Abednedo tech specialist who was part of [[Operation Yellow Moon]], a mission led by Leia Organa that would see the placement of [[Hyperwave transceiver|hyper-transceivers]] in the [[Corva sector]] in an attempt to distract the Galactic Empire from the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] gathering over [[Sullust]]. This was done in order to launch its assault on the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] at Endor. During the operation, Antrot [[Death|died]] in a [[Death|suicide]] attack that heavily damaged the Imperial ship the ''[[Shieldmaiden]]''.<ref name="Moving Target" /> Another Abednedo was known to have served in the Alliance. The individual was present at a briefing on the status of stopping [[Operation: Cinder]], an Imperial [[contingency]] plan devised posthumously by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Poe Dameron 1" /> [[Awls Ooteek]] was another rebellion member.<ref name="Empire's End" />

Similarly, at least three Abednedo joined the Resistance,<ref name="TFA" /><ref name="Poe Dameron 1" /> including [[Ello Asty]], who was a member of the New Republic's [[precision air team]] prior to joining the Resistance.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> During the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], Asty was killed.<ref name="TFA" /> Other Abednedos who joined the resistance included [[technician]] [[Oddy Muva]]<ref name="Poe Dameron 1" /> and [[pilot]] [[C'ai Threnalli]].<ref name="TFA" />

Abednedo occasionally became [[Politics|politicians]].<ref name="Empire's End" /><ref name="Visual" /> In the [[Standard year|year]] [[5 ABY]],<ref name="SW:GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Bushar]] was an Abednedo [[senator]] who served in the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] of the New Republic.<ref name="Empire's End" /> Twenty-nine years later,<ref name="SW:GA" /> [[Brasmon Kee]] represented Abednedo in the New Republic. He was killed by the [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]] in the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]].<ref name="Visual" />

The species could also become thugs and criminals. [[Arno Xan]] was a [[thief]] who built a collection of stolen Imperial [[armor]]s, which he sometimes wore.<ref name="SW:U" /> [[Toomata Wree]], who was also known as Tooms, was a male Abednedo who was employed by [[Surat Nuat]] on the planet [[Akiva]]. Shortly after the Battle of Endor, Wree aided his fellow henchmen, [[Makarial Gravin]] and [[Herf (Ithorian)|Herf]], and attempted to retrieve goods stolen by spare parts shop owner [[Temmin Wexley]] from Surat's transport [[Starship|ship]]. However, they were rendered unconscious by Wexley's reprogrammed [[B1-series battle droid]], [[Mister Bones]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> [[Dazur Dar]] was a member of the [[Data Seekers]], a group of [[Spy|espionage]] experts who sought to unravel mysteries about the [[Anoat sector]]'s ancient history. [[Marn Kel]] was another member of the Data Seekers who used his expert piloting skills to extract operatives from dangerous situations.<ref name="SW:U" />

[[File:Crusher_Roodown.png|thumb|right|200px|"Crusher" Roodown was a scavenger on Jakku.]]
A female Abednedo, [[Sowa Chuan]] was employed by [[Agent]] [[Terex]] of the [[First Order Security Bureau]] as a servant.<ref name="Star Wars: Poe Dameron 6: Lockdown, Part III">[[Poe Dameron 6|''Poe Dameron'' 6]]</ref> [[Durm Bormo]] was a male Abednedo [[scout]]. He worked with a team of adventurers on [[Jakku]] to discover the secrets of a crashed New Republic [[Starlight Wanderer|corvette]].<ref name="TFA Beginner Game">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> Roodown, nicknamed "Crusher" Roodown, was a scavenger on the planet Jakku. His employer, the [[junk dealer]] [[Unkar Plutt]], suspected he was selling salvage to a different buyer, and so [[Unkar's thugs]] cut off his arms. He was significantly larger than regular Abednedos. [[Ilco Munica]] was a [[Church of the Force]] member who lived in the [[Sacred Villages|village]] of [[Tuanul]], also on Jakku. Munica was killed by the [[First Order]], after they [[Attack on Tuanul|attacked]] Tuanul, looking for former explorer [[Lor San Tekka]], and his [[Map to Skywalker|map]] to [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="Visual" /> [[Slowen Lo]] was a male Abednedo who resided on [[Canto Bight]].<ref name="Slowen Lo DB" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|This character here is an animatronic character, so he can blink, his eyes can move, he can sniff his nose. Obviously it is worn by a performer and his head's inside.|Creature Effects Supervisor Neal Scanlan describes Ello Asty's animatronic mask in the "Crafting Creatures" featurette|<ref name="TFA" />}}
[[File:Bullhead_rebel_pilot.png|thumb|left|200px|Concept art for what became the Abednedo.]]
The Abednedo species were created by creature concept designer [[Jake Lunt Davies]] for the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], and first appeared in the ''[[Journey to Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'' prelude series.<ref name="Aftermath" /> Five Abednedos appeared in the film, being Ilco Munica, "Crusher" Roodown, Ello Asty, Brasmon Kee<ref name="Visual" /> and C'ai Threnalli.<ref name="C'ai Threnalli Twitter">{{Twitter|holocronkeeper|status/856883334948847616|[[Leland Chee]]|date=2017-04-25|quote=That's the idea, yes. Could have been Ello Asty but then Ello died.|archivedate=20190904233341|nolive=1}}</ref> All characters were played by actors in costumes and animatronic masks, apart from Roodown, who was not animatronic. Ello Asty was performed by [[Paul Kasey]]<ref name="Creatures">{{Film|VII}} &mdash; "Crafting Creatures" BluRay featurette</ref> and voiced by [[Matthew Wood]].<ref name="Voices of Star Wars">{{SW|url=news/from-blast-that-x-wing-to-traitor-the-voices-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=From "Blast That X-wing!" to "Traitor!": The Voices of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''}}</ref> Asty's costume was also used for C'ai Threnalli.<ref name="Threnalli">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/703128144656478208|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=That's another Abednedo (well, it's the same costume, but we did see Ello die, so therefore, it's another one).|date=2016-02-28|archivefile=File:Other costume tweet.png|nolive=1}}</ref> Crusher Roodown was portrayed by [[Ian Whyte]].<ref name="Ian Whyte">{{WebCite|url=http://aminoapps.com/page/star-wars/6271242/ian-whyte-aka-crusher-roodown-bollie-chewie|text=Ian Whyte aka Crusher Roodown/Bollie/Chewie|work=[http://aminoapps.com Amino]|archivedate=20210224082108}}</ref> Ilco Munica was voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]].<ref name="Voices of Star Wars" />

During the production of ''The Force Awakens'', Ello Asty was named as a reference to the album [[Wikipedia:Hello Nasty|''Hello Nasty'']] by the hip hop group [[Wikipedia:Beastie Boys|Beastie Boys]].<ref name="Beasty Boys">{{Instagram|username=glyn__dillon|url=p/BZo-wgJh8LL|name=[[Glyn Dillon]]|date=2017-09-29|quote=Back on The Force Awakens I came up with the decals for the rebel helmets (which is why my two boys are on Poe's helmet). JJ had asked me what it said when I was trying to sneak it under the radar, and when I told him it was my boy's names he kindly gave his blessing. Because of this I thought it'd be nice to sneak some Beastie Boys references on one, because of JJ's fondness for getting their songs in his films. So when it came to doing the helmets for the creatures, I thought it'd be nice to do some Aurebesh in a calligraphic way, as if they'd scrawled on their helmets like the young G.I.s and Huey pilots in Vietnam. So on the custom made helmet of this big guy, we did BORN TO ILL on one side and HELLO NASTY on the other (both Beastie Boys albums)... (and of course 'Born to ill' is like the famous Full Metal Jacket BORN TO KILL helmet). Then I forgot all about it... some time later I heard that the CFX dept were referring to this creature as 'Ello Asty' and I realised that the H and the N had been rubbed off in the breakdown process. Tim Shanahan and his team do the most amazing work. They can make everything look as old and lived in as you like, using lots of clever tricks, artistry and good old fashioned elbow grease (as well as real grease). It's one of my favourite depts because really they're the last process on the production line, they're the icing on the cake. And it was in that magic breakdown process that the H and N were lost... and Ello Asty was christened.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/TeQlp}}</ref> The director of the film, [[J.J. Abrams]], is a fan of the band, and said that the name was a suggestion from the creature department. The name was approved by him, due to the Beastie Boys reference, having previously featured their song "[[Wikipedia:Sabotage (song)|Sabotage]]" in [[Wikipedia:Star Trek (film)|''Star Trek'']] and "[[Wikipedia:Body Movin'|Body Movin']]" in [[Wikipedia:Star Trek Into Darkness|''Star Trek Into Darkness'']]. In addition, it served as a phonetic reference to his television show [[Wikipedia:Lost (TV series)|''Lost'']].<ref name="JJ's fandom">{{WebCite|url=https://www.yahoo.com/movies/j-j-abrams-confirms-name-1318519424016438.html|text=J.J. Abrams Confirms Name of New 'Star Wars' Character Is Nod to Both Beastie Boys and 'Lost'|author=Kevin Polowy|date=2015-12-10|work=[[Wikipedia:Yahoo!|Yahoo!]] Movies|archiveurl=https://www.webcitation.org/6kESVVf5h}}</ref>

Taking note of this, [[Lucasfilm]] [[Lucasfilm Story Group|Story Group]] member and author of the ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', [[Pablo Hidalgo]], named all other Abednedo that appear in ''The Visual Dictionary'' as references to the Beastie Boys: Roodown is likely a reference to the song "[[Wikipedia:Root Down (EP)|Root Down]]," Munica's to the album [[Wikipedia:Ill Communication|''Ill Communication'']], and Brasmon Kee to the song "[[Wikipedia:Brass Monkey (song)|Brass Monkey]]."<ref name="SWBW 53">{{WebCite|url=http://www.starwarsreport.com/2016/02/19/pablo-hidalgo-interview-swbw-53|text=Star Wars Bookworms Podcast 53: Interview with Pablo Hidalgo|work=The Star Wars Report|archivedate=20190308223239}}</ref> The trend was later adopted by other authors. In the [[Marvel Comics]] series ''[[Star Wars: Poe Dameron]]'', Sowa Chuan and Oddy Muva are likely named after the songs [[Wikipedia:So What'cha Want|''So What'cha Want'']] and ''Body Movin''', respectively.<ref name="Star Wars: Poe Dameron 6: Lockdown, Part III" /><ref name="Poe Dameron 1" /> In [[Chuck Wendig]]'s ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', Awls Ooteek evokes the album [[Wikipedia:Paul's Boutique|''Paul's Boutique'']].<ref name="Empire's End" /> In ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'', Slowen Lo, portrayed vocally by [[Joseph Gordon-Levitt]], is yet another reference to the Beastie Boys, this time to their song ''Slow And Low'' from the album [[Wikipedia:Licensed to Ill|''Licensed to Ill'']].<ref name="Slowen Lo">{{WebCite|url=http://comicbook.com/starwars/2017/12/17/star-wars-the-last-jedi-joseph-gordon-levitt-cameo|text=What Was Joseph Gordon Levitt's Cameo in 'Star Wars: The Last Jedi'?|author=Schmidt, Joe|date=2017-12-17|work=[http://comicbook.com comicbook]|archivedate=20180807095029}}</ref> Finally, in "[[Teach / Corrupt]]," the sixth episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', Senator [[Isedwa Chuwant]] also refers to a phrase in the Beastie Boys song, ''So What'cha Want''.<ref name="The Acolyte">{{SW|url=series/the-acolyte/season-1-episode-6-teach-corrupt-episode-guide|text="Teach / Corrupt" - Episode Guide &#124; The Acolyte}}</ref>

The name Abednedo itself is a reference to the song "Shadrach" from ''Paul's Boutique'', which at a certain point namedrops the Biblical character Abednego.<ref name="aside">{{WebCite|url=https://ontheaside.com/uncategorized/this-new-star-wars-character-is-named-after-the-beastie-boys|text=This new Star Wars character is named after the Beastie Boys|author=[https://ontheaside.com/author/jesselocke Locke, Jesse]|date=2015-10-01|work=[https://ontheaside.com A.Side]|archivedate=20220501104838}}</ref>
