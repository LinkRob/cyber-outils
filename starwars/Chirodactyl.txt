'''Chirodactyls''',<ref name="Databank" /> also known as '''Dathomirian bats''',<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> were a [[species]] of winged [[Diet|carnivorous]] creatures native to the planet [[Dathomir]]. 

==Biology and appearance==
Chirodactyls had sharp claws and teeth that they used to rip apart their prey. Their ears were triangular in shape and their wings were covered in leathery skin. They could attack with a sonic howl, either by aiming at a specific target or by causing a shockwave.<ref name="Bestiary" />

==History==
{{Quote|The strongest Nightbrothers faced a rite of passage by venturing into the lair of this creature|Cal Kestis|<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />}}
The strongest [[Nightbrothers]] were tested in a rite of passage that consisted of venturing into these creatures' territory.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

During or before the [[High Republic Era]], a chirodactyl threatened the people of a city. The threat posed by the ferocious beast ended when [[Jedi Master]] [[Barabo]] confronted and drove it away. A wall sculpture was made in the Jedi's honor. An image of Barabo was carved into the wall above three smaller images, depicting how the Jedi saved the city from the chirodactyl.<ref name="Nubs Mistake">{{YJA|Nubs's Big Mistake}}</ref>

One chirodactyl known as [[Gorgara]] was especially notorious among the [[Dathomirian|natives]] of [[Dathomir]].<ref name="Databank" /> In [[14 BBY]], Gorgara died fighting [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] when he visited the [[planet]]. Although the beast persistently tried to kill Kestis, the Jedi mourned its [[death]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chirodactyls first appeared in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> During development, inspiration for the design of the chirodactyls was found in bats and owls. Chirodactyl concept art was done by artist [[Jean-Francois Rey]].<ref name="Art JFO">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>
