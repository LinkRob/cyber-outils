The '''Avatar-10''' [[hyperdrive]] was the standard engine for the [[YT-1300 light freighter]]. One was installed on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' until [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] replaced it with a [[Isu-Sim]] [[SSP05 hyperdrive generator|SSP05 hyperdrive]].<ref name="OM" />
