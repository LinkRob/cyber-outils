The '''CRC-X80 repulsor generator''' was a heavy-duty [[repulsorlift generator]]. It was used to feed six powerful coils spread throughout the [[hull]] of the [[Model 90 Ranger]], a model of [[landspeeder]] manufactured by the [[Cryospac Repulsorlift Corporation]].<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />

==Behind the scenes==
The CRC-X80 repulsor generator was first mentioned in [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']],<ref name="NoDisintegrations" /> a [[2017]] supplement for the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="A Fistful of Credits">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/2/2/a-fistful-of-credits|text=A Fistful of Credits}}</ref>
