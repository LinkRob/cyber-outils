'''Clone trooper armor''', also known as '''clone armor''' or '''generation one armor''', was the [[armor]] used by [[Galactic Republic]] [[clone trooper]]s during the [[Clone Wars]]. Designed by [[Kaminoan]] armorsmiths, [[Cloning|clone]] armor consisted of a set of form-fitting [[plastoid]] plates attached to a [[Color|black]] [[body glove]]. It could withstand impacts, pressure, heat and acrid vapors, yet was vulnerable to direct [[blaster]] fire. The armor was also standardized and produced rapidly as the clones were [[DNA|genetically]] identical [[soldier]]s modeled on a [[human]] [[Clone template|template]], the [[Mandalorian]] [[foundling]] and [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]].

The first version of clone armor was called [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] which was inspired by [[Mandalorian armor]], most notably its helmet. Over the course of the war, improvements were made to the armor's design, creating lighter and stronger plates. Phase I was eventually retired as [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] saw widespread distribution through the ranks of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. By then, a wide array of armor variants were created for specialized [[trooper]]s, such as the [[ARC trooper armor]] of [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s, the [[Katarn-class commando armor|''Katarn''-class Commando armor]] of [[clone commando]]s, and the [[Clone Dive Armor]] of [[clone SCUBA trooper]]s.

With the [[Imperial Era|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[New Order|new regime]] initially retained usage of clone trooper armor before it was eventually mothballed and replaced with [[stormtrooper armor]], worn by [[Stormtrooper|Imperial stormtroopers]]. However, some clone veterans continued to wear their old armor [[Standard year|years]] after their service in the Clone Wars, while [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] and [[pirate]]s utilized [[Scavenger|scavenged]] armor plates. The legacy of clone armor ultimately [[New Republic Era|outlived]] the Empire that [[Galactic Civil War|fell]] to the [[New Republic]], influencing a [[First Order stormtrooper armor|new model of armor]] made for a [[First Order stormtrooper|new generation of stormtroopers]] by the [[First Order]]. This was followed by the [[Sith trooper armor|crimson armor]] of the [[Sith trooper]]s&mdash;soldiers of the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Sith cultist|cult]]&mdash;which was reminiscent of clone trooper armor, particularly the helmet's [[Macrobinocular viewplate|T-shaped visor]].

Due to the combined influence of Imperial and First Order use, the heritage of clone armor was effectively appropriated by its successors, transforming what civilians once saw as a symbol of honorable defense and [[Galaxy|galactic]] salvation into a faceless icon of [[fascism]]. Nevertheless, the combined legacy of clone trooper armor and stormtrooper armor made white-armored soldiers one of [[the galaxy]]'s most enduring symbols of military might.

==Description==
===Clone armor===
{{Quote|During the Clone Wars, all troopers were outfitted with standard-issue armor that was custom-manufactured at our home base on Kamino. The kit consisted of a series of white plastoid plates strapped over a black body suit. Standard components included a chest plate, arm and leg guards, a utility belt, and a helmet. Clone armor was designed to withstand strong impacts, intense pressure, and unfavorable environmental conditions. A direct hit from a blaster bolt, though? Not so much.|Captain Rex, ''[[The Secrets of the Clone Troopers]]''|<ref name="Secrets of Clones">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Clone Troopers]]''</ref>}}
[[File:ClonesonKamino.png|thumb|left|250px|The clones of Jango Fett wore standard white armor that was developed on their homeworld of Kamino.]]
[[Clone trooper]] [[armor]] was a set of [[Color|white]] [[plastoid]] plates worn over a black [[body glove]]. Its form-fitting design made the armor appear claustrophobic, but to the [[Cloning|clones]] who trained with it from an early age the armor felt comfortable and secure.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" /> Though vulnerable to [[lightsaber]]s<ref name="ROTS" /> and direct [[blaster]] fire, clone armor was impervious to impacts, pressure, heat, and acrid vapors. Its quality was such that by the [[time]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] arrived on [[Kamino]] to take charge of the [[army]], the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] was considered the finest military force in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Clone Armor DB" />

It took over a [[Standard decade|decade]] for clone trooper armor to degrade, so clones took to laying the helmets of their fallen family where they [[Death|died]] as a form of honoring them as a makeshift monument.<ref name="Queen's Hope">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref> [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] was the first iteration in the design of clone armor.<ref name="USW" /> Constructed by [[Kaminoan]] armorsmiths, Phase I featured a [[life support]] system, a tracking device for monitoring [[troop]] movements and a display screen, all built within the [[helmet]].<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> The armor's design was also reminiscent of the [[Mandalorian armor]] worn by the [[clone template]], the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]], particularly in the helmet which featured a similar [[Macrobinocular viewplate|T-shaped visor]].<ref name="USW" /> The armor could be standardized and produced rapidly due to the fact that clone troopers were [[DNA|genetically]] identical [[soldier]]s.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" /> A number of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s [[clone trooper]]s, such as [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]]<ref name="Jedi Crash">{{TCW|Jedi Crash}}</ref> and [[trooper]] [[Tup]],<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref> carried [[locator]]s as part of their battlefield equipment.

Despite the advantages it offered, Phase I was uncomfortable to wear due to the Kaminoans' unfamiliarity with [[human]] ergonomics.<ref name="USW" /> [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] was the improved version of its Phase I counterpart, providing clone troopers with better vision as well as armor plates that were both lighter and stronger.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" /> The new armor was capable of supporting more specialized equipment,<ref name="UFC">''[[Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars]]''</ref> such as an [[Breathmask survival gear|external respirator]] as the helmet did not feature an internal life support system.<ref name="The Unknown" /> It was also more expensive to make, costing 3,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]<ref name="Collapse" /> per armor set as opposed to Phase I's 2,000 credits.<ref name="Rise" />

===Variant forms===
====Specialized clone armor====
[[File:DC-15S_blaster.png|thumb|right|250px|HT-77 Cold Assault Armor allowed clone troopers to operate in arctic conditions.]]
The standard clone armor was supplemented by variant forms used by [[trooper]]s in specialized roles, including [[pilot]]s and [[bomb]]-disposal teams. One frequently used armor variant<ref name="Clone Armor DB" /> was the [[HT-77 Cold Assault Armor]],<ref name="Rise" /> which provided [[clone cold assault trooper]]s with improved breath filters and insulation for arctic conditions. [[Insulated armor]] was also required for hot conditions,<ref name="Clone Armor DB" /> as in the case of the [[clone flametrooper]]s who wore [[fire]]-resistant suits<ref name="Clone Commander Jet">{{SWE|characters|clone-commander-jet|Clone Commander Jet}}</ref> while wielding [[BT X-42 heavy flame projector]]s.<ref name="Rise" />

[[Advanced Recon Force trooper|Advanced Recon Force Scout Troopers]], also known as ARF troopers, were outfitted with [[Phase I ARF armor]];<ref name="BFII Info">{{EA|subdomain=battlefront-forums|url=discussion/137644/community-transmission-where-are-those-droidekas|text=Community Transmission &ndash; Where Are Those Droidekas}}</ref> designed for stealth and speed, it was a lighter version of the standard Phase I clone armor.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" /> Its successor, [[Phase II ARF armor]],<ref name="BFII Info" /> served a similar role, consisting of lightweight armor made for the reconnaissance work of [[clone scout trooper]]s. ARF troopers also possessed a camouflaged version of their Phase II armor, which they utilized in conjunction with [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" />

[[File:PhaseIClonePilot-TROSPromoYT.png|thumb|left|180px|Clone trooper pilots wore additional life-support gear and helmets with improved sensors.]]
The gear worn by [[clone gunner]]s featured helmets with additional noise insulation and reinforced chest armor as safeguards against the intense recoil of the [[cannon]]s they controlled. [[Clone ordnance specialist]]s wore armor with bright orange markings, indicating to others the dangerous nature of their role. Apart from this aesthetic distinction, their armor offered no additional protection from explosions. [[Special ops clone trooper]]s were trained for stealth and detecting enemies at a distance, and were given advanced gear that improved their senses.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" />

[[Clone trooper pilot]]s considered themselves as a breed apart, having been selected early in their development to learn special skills. Their version of Phase I armor featured additional life-support gear and a helmet with improved [[sensor]]s. Like rank-and-file clones, the clone pilot "[[Warthog]]" wore an updated rig&mdash;customized to his liking. As the Clone Wars ground on, pilots received improvements to their gear. [[Stealth clone pilot]]s wore their own unique armor due to their role in flying [[IPV-2C Stealth Corvette|experimental]] [[starship]]s. By the final [[Standard day|days]] of the war, pilots wore new flight gear, featuring open-faced helmets that offered superior visibility during [[Realspace|space]] battles.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" />

[[Clone SCUBA trooper]]s were outfitted with [[Clone Dive Armor]] for [[Water|underwater]] combat. As such, SCUBA gear was lighter than regular armor and featured fins, life-support equipment<ref name="Clone Armor DB" />&mdash;providing [[oxygen]] to breathe<ref name="Collapse" />&mdash;and propulsion packs.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" /> It was also designed to assist SCUBA troopers in their aquatic maneuverability and protected them from the pressure levels of [[ocean]] [[planet]]s such as [[Mon Cala]].<ref name="Collapse" />

[[File:ARCTroopers-SWL70.png|thumb|right|200px|New armor was field-tested by Advanced Recon Commandos.]]
New improvements to the clone trooper armor were often field tested by the independent-minded, highly trained [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s, also known as ARC troopers, who were the first to receive new equipment. Their field reports helped researchers improve gear before it was distributed to the rest of the army.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" /> Like the standard clone trooper armor, there were two types of [[ARC trooper armor]], beginning with [[Phase I ARC Trooper Armor]]. It was a heavier version of the regular kit, particularly the forearms, shins, and chest plate. Each armor set had an advanced helmet that contained a hardened long-range [[comlink]]; a [[kama]] that was worn around the waist, providing extra protection to the [[Limb|legs]] and groin; and a built-in load-bearing harness and survival pack.<ref name="Knights of Fate">''[[Knights of Fate]]''</ref>

[[Phase II ARC Trooper Armor]] was the upgraded version that the ARC troopers had switched to in the final battles of the Clone Wars. Having successfully field tested its Phase I predecessor, which served as the basis for the standard Phase II clone trooper armor, ARC troopers received similar gear while retaining the accessories that included additional armor, [[pauldron]]s, kamas, and survival packs.<ref name="Collapse" />

[[File:DeltaSquadSev-TCWs3BR2.png|thumb|left|250px|Clone commandos wore ''Katarn''-class armor, a heavier version of the standard-issue clone trooper armor.]]
[[Katarn-class commando armor|''Katarn''-class Commando Armor]]<ref name="Knights of Fate" /> was a specialized set of armor and gear used by [[clone commando]]s, who were considered to be among the most respected units in the clone army.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" /> A type of heavy and highly sophisticated clone trooper armor,<ref name="Knights of Fate" /> ''Katarn''-class armor included a [[backpack]] that contained [[grenade]]s and sabotage gear.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

The [[Mk I Katarn-class Commando Armor|Mk I ''Katarn''-class Commando Armor]] was a variant of the [[commando]] armor. Consisting of an environmentally sealed [[durasteel]] suit worn over an insulated and breathable, ballistic-fiber bodysuit, the Mk I version was considered one of the most advanced sets of armor ever produced by the [[Republic Military]].<ref name="Knights of Fate" />

Other units that received their own versions of clone trooper armor included [[Biker Advanced Recon Commando]] "BARC troopers," the [[Galactic Marines|21st Nova Corps]], and [[clone paratrooper]]s. BARC troopers wore helmets designed to focus their attention forward, helping them to avoid distractions that could be deadly while traveling at high speed on a [[BARC speeder]]. The 21st Nova [[Corps]], also known as [[Galaxy|Galactic]] Marines, had filtered visors that protected them from pollutants and other hazards. Clone paratroopers were specially trained for airborne assaults; as such, they wore kamas for additional protection as well as unique helmets shaped like a "beehive."<ref name="Clone Armor DB" />

The [[Clone X trooper]]s had a unique black armor that contained common features like a built-in comlink in the [[helmet]]s, and could have a rangefinder attached. Their helmets lack the T-shaped visor but were pressurized and possessed a mechanized visor that would retract and protract as needed.<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy">{{TBB|The Clone Conspiracy}}</ref>

====Jedi armor====
{{Quote|Because of the Jedi's dangerous position on the front lines, traditional robes weren't always enough to protect members of the Order from the constant barrage of attacks unleashed by the Separatists' droids. To meet this threat, many Jedi incorporated armor&mdash;usually modified from standard-issue clone trooper gear&mdash;into their ceremonial garb.|Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi]]''</ref>}}
[[File:KnowledgeAndDefense-CWCS.png|thumb|right|250px|Jedi Generals wore a modified version of clone armor at the behest of the clone troopers who were concerned for their safety.]]
Clone trooper armor also served as the basis for a non-clone variant, tailor-made for the [[Jedi Knight]]s who served as the clone troopers' [[commanding officer]]s. Recognizing the Clone Wars as the new common reality that the [[Jedi Order]] faced, [[Jedi General]]s adopted the modified clone armor known as [[Jedi Commander armor]]. This set of armor consisted of only the most important pieces in order to avoid hampering the [[Jedi]]'s [[Lightsaber combat|lightsaber forms]] and movement.<ref name="Engines of War" /> It was more expensive than the standard-issue clone armor, however, costing 5,000 Republic credits.<ref name="Rise" />

[[Jedi Reconnaissance Armor]] was a lightweight<ref name="Collapse" /> variant of the [[Jedi Commander]] armor.<ref name="Engines of War" /> It was created in response to the concerns of their clone troopers, who feared that, despite their [[general]]s' [[Force-sensitive|connection]] to [[the Force]], even the most experienced [[Jedi Master]] could be felled by the sheer amount of [[blaster]] fire on the battlefield. The Jedi, therefore, wore light armor with limited protection against blasters, while still leaving them largely unencumbered in battle or overland travel. Reconnaissance Armor cost more than regular clone armor, though at 4,000 credits<ref name="Collapse" /> it was cheaper than its standard Jedi counterpart.<ref name="Rise" />

==History==
===Clone Wars===
{{Quote|Looks like we got ourselves a batch of shinies, Commander.''"<br />"''Shinies, sir?''"<br />"''That's right. Your armor, it's shiny and new, just like you.|Captain Rex and the rookie [[Echo]]|<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:Boys_in_white.jpg|thumb|left|200px|During the Clone Wars, armored clone troopers served as a collective symbol of the Galactic Republic.]]
At the [[First Battle of Geonosis|beginning]] of the Clone Wars<ref name="Clone Armor DB" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> clone troopers were outfitted with standard white armor that had been designed for their use on Kamino. The Kaminoans who created the clones were also responsible for the production of the army's armor, weapons and gear. Before attaining the status of trooper, however, [[clone cadet]]s wore a training variant of the standard-issue models used on the battlefield.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" /> When conflict ensued between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Grand Master Yoda journeyed to Kamino to inspect the army that the Kaminoans had built for the Republic. He then departed for the Separatist world of [[Geonosis]]<ref name="AOTC" /> at the head of the finest military force in the galaxy.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" /> Due to the armor's standard white appearance, Republic citizens took to calling the clone troopers "Boys in White." This civilian-coined nickname was [[Support the Boys in White|promoted]] through [[propaganda]] by the [[Commission for the Protection of the Republic]].<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref>

Initially, the Grand Army of the Republic used color splashes to distinguish [[clone trooper officer]]s from the rank-and-file soldiers who served under their command. This approach to [[military rank]] identification was considered a benefit for non-clones on the battlefield as well as clones whose helmet displays had malfunctioned. It ultimately fell into disuse as a result of the Jedi's support of the clones. Once in command of the clone army, the Jedi Generals allowed the clones to express their individuality, permitting entire clone units<ref name="Clone Armor DB" />&mdash;from the [[212th Attack Battalion]] and the [[501st Legion]], to the [[Coruscant Guard]] and the [[104th Battalion]]'s [[Wolfpack]]<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>&mdash;to decorate their armor with unique color schemes and markings.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" />

[[File:PhaseIIArmor-SM.png|thumb|right|250px|Phase II was the new and improved armor introduced to the ranks of the clone army as the Clone Wars ground on.]]
This new trend was taken a step further by [[Clone Commander]]s, who increasingly customized their armor over the course of the Clone Wars. [[Commander]]s often made extensive modifications to their armor, which came to reflect their experience on the battlefield as well as their own inclinations. [[Officer]]s such as [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] and Clone Commander [[Cody]] upgraded their kits with blast-resistant kamas, pauldrons, [[macrobinoculars]], communication packs, and other gear.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" />

Improvements were made to the clone trooper armor throughout the Clone Wars, and were successfully field-tested by the elite ARC troopers. This led to a widespread distribution of the improved Phase II armor, resulting in the retirement of its Phase I predecessor as nearly all clones switched to the new kit by the final days of the conflict. By then, several variant forms had been created for the various specialized troopers in the Grand Army of the Republic.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" />

===Imperial Era===
{{Quote|Under the Republic, clone soldiers had been renowned for personalizing their armor and units with different symbols and slogans. Such individuality was heavily frowned upon within the ranks of the Imperial military.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>}}
The Phase II clone trooper armor was retained by the clones<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> who served as the first generation of [[stormtrooper]]s<ref name="Stormtroopers DB">{{Databank|stormtroopers|Stormtroopers}}</ref> in the [[Galactic Empire]] that [[Proclamation of the New Order|replaced the Republic]] after the [[Battle of Utapau|end of the Clone Wars]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> The [[Imperial Army]] ended the practice of using color to denote unit affiliation, which the clones had adopted after allowing themselves to individualise under the Jedi's command. As a result, most of the remaining clone troopers took on the unformed appearance of white-armored soldiers, their individual personalities replaced by programmed conformity.<ref name="Aftermath Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/aftermath-episode-guide|text="Aftermath" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}} Trivia Gallery</ref> Despite this, [[Imperial shock trooper]]s retained the distinctive red markings which identified them as members of the [[Coruscant]] Guard.<ref name="Aftermath" />

[[File:Clone_Stormtroopers_Raxus.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Clone troopers retained use of their Phase II armor during the early years of the Galactic Empire.]]
During the first [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Imperial Era]], Phase II-armored clone troopers were stationed throughout the newly-risen Galactic Empire, from their [[homeworld]] of Kamino in [[extragalactic]] [[Realspace|space]],<ref name="Aftermath" /> to various planets such as [[Saleucami]]<ref name="Cut and Run">{{TBB|Cut and Run}}</ref> and [[Pantora]].<ref name="Cornered">{{TBB|Cornered}}</ref> Eventually, the Empire started using conscription as a cost-effective alternative method to the Kaminoan cloning production. Recruited through [[Project War-Mantle]], [[ES-01]], [[ES-02]], [[ES-03]], and [[ES-04]] were enlisted as [[Elite Squad Trooper]]s.<ref name="Replacements">{{TBB|Replacements}}</ref> They were issued clone trooper armor that had been modified to fit the non-clone soldiers who were physically different from their clone counterparts as well as each other. Additionally, their version of the clone armor was black instead of white, and their helmets featured green visors.<ref name="Replacements Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/replacements-episode-guide|text="Replacements" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}}</ref>

The Phase II model was eventually succeeded by the Imperial [[stormtrooper armor]] and, as a result, was mothballed by the Empire, leading to large stocks of clone trooper armor being recycled while its replacement was distributed throughout the ranks of the [[Stormtrooper Corps]].<ref name="Clone Armor DB" /> By then, most clones had been supplanted by [[Reproduction|birth-born]] human recruits<ref name="Stormtroopers DB" /> due to [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious]]' decision to shut down Kamino's cloning facilities.<ref name="Dark Lord 2">[[Darth Vader (2017) 2|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 2]]</ref>

[[File:OldCloneTrio-SWR.png|thumb|right|250px|The clones Gregor, Rex, and Wolffe retained some of their armor plates long after their military service ended.]]
During the later Imperial Era, clone armor remained an occasional sight in the galaxy despite being retired by the Empire. While clone gear was [[Scavenger|scavenged]] for its armor plates by [[Mercenary|Mercenaries]] and [[pirate]]s, a handful of renegade clones continued to wear the armor from their days as Republic soldiers.<ref name="Clone Armor DB" /> [[Captain]] Rex, a retired clone trooper who supported the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], was highly vocal in his criticism of stormtrooper gear, referring to it as "junk armor." He also found his ability to see limited by the helmet's [[eye]] lenses, further adding to his frustration with the new kit.<ref name="Stealth Strike">{{Rebels|Stealth Strike}}</ref> His opinions on stormtrooper armor were in broad contrast to his views on his old "Generation One" armor, which Rex continued to hold in high regard long after the Empire deemed it obsolete.<ref name="The Last Battle" />

Moreover, a variant of the Phase II model was provided for the [[Purge Trooper]]s, a death [[squad]] originally consisting of the last batch of Fett clones that had been repurposed to serve the [[Inquisitorius]] as [[Jedi hunter]]s.<ref name="Dark Lord 13">[[Darth Vader (2017) 13|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 13]]</ref> Due to their role in the [[Great Jedi Purge]] of [[Order 66]], these [[special forces]] units wore armor that offered some protection against lightsabers.<ref name="Dark Lord 16">[[Darth Vader (2017) 16|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 16]]</ref>

===Legacy and survival===
{{Quote|This garbage is nothing like clone armor.''"<br />"''Looks a little tight on you, old man.''"<br />"''Yeah? Well, at least I know how to wear it.|Rex and [[Kanan Jarrus]], referring to stormtrooper armor|<ref name="Stealth Strike" />}}
[[File:Reflection-VaD.png|thumb|left|250px|Despite its legacy, some clone trooper armor was left abandoned after the Clone Wars ended.]]
The legacy of clone trooper armor endured for [[Standard decade|decades]] and was remembered as the first phase in one of the most distinctive symbols of military might in [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]]. Imperial stormtrooper armor and its [[First Order stormtrooper armor|First Order successor]] were both influenced by the design of clone armor. The Galactic Empire had accordingly appropriated the clone troopers' legacy, which in turn transformed a symbol of honor, defense and salvation into a "faceless icon" of [[fascism]].<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

Captain Rex considered Imperial body armor "garbage" compared to the armor that he wore during his service to the Galactic Republic. When offered the chance to wear it permanently as an Imperial stormtrooper, he refused out of loyalty to the Republic and his personal hatred for Imperial armor. While Rex was critical in his views about stormtrooper armor, he nevertheless knew how to wear it due to his experience with clone armor.<ref name="Stealth Strike" /> Around [[6 ABY]]<ref name="6Date">{{StarWars2025Date|5}}</ref> during the [[New Republic Era]], [[New Republic]] heroes [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Han Solo]], who were also joined by the bounty hunter [[Beilert Valance]], discovered old suits of clone trooper armor while in [[Mission to Loradil|conflict]] with a surviving [[Separatist holdouts|Separatist droid holdout]] on the planet [[Loradil]]; in need of some way to defend themselves against the droid hordes,<ref name="SW5">[[Star Wars (2025) 5|''Star Wars'' (2025) 5]]</ref> the defenders of the New Republic would don the gear of their fallen precursor and fought the same foes as the clone troopers before them.<ref name="SW6">[[Star Wars (2025) 6|''Star Wars'' (2025) 6]]</ref>

[[File:KixNewRepublic.jpg|thumb|right|130px|Kix wore a set of Phase I clone trooper armor during the New Republic Era.]]
Later into the New Republic Era, [[First Order stormtrooper]]s were regarded as the next evolutionary stage for the galaxy's white-armored soldiers.<ref name="TROS VD" /> During the [[Cold War]], [[Sidon Ithano]] and [[Sidon Ithano's pirate crew|his crew]] sought after and eventually awakened the clone trooper [[Kix]] from stasis. Kix joined the [[pirate]]'s crew and donned  a Phase I helmet and a patched-up dark set of [[First Order stormtrooper armor|First Order Stormtrooper armor]] with red details, carrying on his legacy as the last clone trooper.<ref name="Adventures 7" />

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> during the final days of the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the [[Sith Eternal]] cult<ref name="TROS VD" />&mdash;allies to the First Order<ref name="Rise and Fall" />&mdash;unveiled a new breed of soldier&mdash;the crimson-armored [[Sith trooper]]s. Serving as the backbone of a [[Sith Eternal army|secret army]] on [[Exegol]], Sith troopers were modeled on the stormtroopers who preceded them as well as the clone troopers, whose training and armor inspired that of their Sith counterparts. As a result, the [[Sith trooper armor]]'s helmet incorporated a version of the clone trooper armor's T-shaped visor.<ref name="TROS VD" /> After the defeat of the Sith Eternal and First Order, historian [[Beaumont Kin]] would compose the text ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', in which he mentioned the Clone Army's ability to adopt personalized markings and slogans for their gear, contrasting that to the uniformity expected throughout the Imperial Military.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />
