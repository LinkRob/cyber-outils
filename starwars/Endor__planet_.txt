{{Quote|The Forest Moon of Endor circles a gas giant in a system near the galactic frontier.|The ''[[Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''|<ref name="Galactic Explorer's Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref>}}
'''Endor''', also called '''Tana''' by the [[Ewok]]s [[Homeworld|native]] to [[Endor|one]] of its [[moon]]s, was a [[gas giant]] located in the [[Endor system]],<ref name="TROS VD" /> a [[Binary star system|binary]]<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know" /> [[star system]] positioned in the [[Moddell sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Endor acted almost like a third sun to the moons with its clouds of hydrogen reflecting light.<ref name="TROS VD" /> Located in grid square H-16 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it was connected to [[Cerea]] and [[Bakura]] by a [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="Beginner Game" /> The [[planet]] was [[orbit]]ed by nine major moons,<ref name="TROS VD" /> the largest of which<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> was known as the [[Endor|Forest Moon of Endor]]. The [[ocean]] moon of [[Kef Bir]] was also one of Endor's moons. Additionally, 48 "sub-lunar" objects [[orbit]]ed the planet.<ref name="TROS VD" />

Before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of the [[New Republic]], the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] attempted to rename the planet Tana, in honor of the Ewoks.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Endor was originally created for the [[1983]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']],<ref name="Return of the Jedi">{{Film|VI}}</ref> where it appeared as a distant orb, labeled "Planet Endor" by special effects storyboards, above its Forest Moon, not large enough to be definitively labeled a planet.<ref name="Endor">{{DB|location|endor|Endor}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|rough draft]] and revised rough draft of the script for ''Return of the Jedi'', the [[Forest]] Moon of Endor, then known as the "Green Moon," instead orbited the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[capital]] world of [[Had Abbadon system|Had Abbadon]],<ref name="annotated">''[[Star Wars: The Annotated Screenplays]]''</ref> which would later evolve into [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Coruscant">{{DB|location|coruscant|Coruscant}}</ref>
