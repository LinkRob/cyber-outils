An '''attack''' was carried out on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] world of [[Ahn Krantarium]] by [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] led by [[Governor]] [[Ahr]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], which happened during the early days of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The aim was to destroy deadly [[droid]]s that had overrun the world long before the [[Imperial Era]]. The droids had rebuilt themselves to be more dangerous than regular [[security droid]]s. Ahr sent out a [[stormtrooper]] patrol, but all of them perished, so instead of sending out more to die for nothing, the Governor sent out Darth Vader. The [[Sith Lord]] destroyed all of the droids in the vicinity, surprising Ahr.<ref name="Darth Vader" />
