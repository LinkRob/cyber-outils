'''Bulcha''', also referred to as '''Bulcha the Hutt''', was a [[Hutt]] [[gangster]] who operated within [[Bri'ahl sector|Tenoo's sector]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He was known as one of the most feared criminals of the [[sector]]. At one point, Bulcha had a desire for a [[band]] to operate as entertainment onboard his [[sail barge]]. Reaching out to the [[pirate]] gang of the [[Ganguls]], Bulcha made a deal where he would trade his force of heavy [[security droid]]s, in exchange for the band [[Feb Rozo and the Sap Tap Trio]], whom the Ganguls had captured. Though the deal was successful, and the Ganguls made away with new [[droid]]s, [[Feb Rozo]] and his band were shortly freed afterwards by [[Hap]] and [[Jedi]] from [[Tenoo]].<ref name="Battle for the Band" />

Later, Bulcha captured [[Ace Kallisto]]'s droid [[JNX-09]] ("Jinx"), seeking to exploit the droid's codebreaking skills. Kallisto rescued Jinx from Bulcha's camp on Yarrum and the two sought sanctuary at the [[Tenoo Jedi Temple]]. Bulcha pursued the fugitives and laid siege to the temple with his security and spider droids. Since [[Jedi Master]] [[Zia Zaldor Zanna]] and most of the Jedi residents were away, [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Kai Brightstar]] led the defense of the temple. He, Kallisto and Jinx managed to incapacitate Bulcha's droids and trapped the Hutt crime lord under some book cases in the [[Temple Library]]. When Zia, her [[Padawan]] [[Wes Vinik]] and the other Jedi younglings returned, Bulcha and his droids were expelled from the Temple.<ref name="Home Sweet Temple">{{YJA|Home Sweet Temple}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Bulcha had mottled [[Color|brown]] skin and brown [[eye]]s. As a Hutt, Bulcha had limited tolerance for the desert heat of [[Yarrum]]. He possessed a force of guards and security droids onboard his sail barge, though the droids were traded in exchange for a new band.<ref name="Battle for the Band" />
