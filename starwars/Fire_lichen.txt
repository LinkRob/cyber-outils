'''Fire Lichen''' was a type of [[lichen]] found on the walls of [[Dathomir]]. It was not known to be edible and was able to survive in the desolate planet due to the low amount of nutrition needed. It was also incredibly resilient so that it was almost impossible to rip from rock. The [[Nightbrothers]] of Dathomir used [[climbing claw]]s to ascend surfaces covered in the lichen.<ref name="JFO" />
