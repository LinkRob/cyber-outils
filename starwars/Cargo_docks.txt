The '''cargo docks''' were located in a [[Unidentified town (Lau)|town]] on the [[planet]] [[Lau]]. [[Omega]] and [[Crosshair]], who were on the run from the [[Galactic Empire]], freed their companion, the [[lurca hound]] [[Batcher]], from the cargo docks after she was taken by the Imperial [[officer]] [[Mann]]. They then escaped the planet by stealing a Lau [[cargo ship]] that was landed at the docks.<ref name="A Different Approach" />
