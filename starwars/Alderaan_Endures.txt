'''''Alderaan Endures''''' was a [[starship]] that was active during the [[Imperial Era]]. [[First Engineer]] [[Josiah Diar]] was a crewmember of the vessel and had designed the [[Deflector shield|shields]] of the ship. At one point, Diar proclaimed that there was no need to worry about the shields as Diar designed them and as such they would hold. The starship was named after<ref name="Fully Operational" /> the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]], which was [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>
