'''Danny Mak''' is a senior character artist who served as a concept artist for [[2017]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Challenges]]'' augmented/virtual reality experience, establishing a pipeline for modeling and texturing 3D, hand painted characters, environment and vehicles for the game, as well as menotoring junior artist.<ref name="LinkedIn" /> Mak worked on ''Jedi Challenges'' closely alongside [[Adam Schuman]] while employed by [[Blind Squirrel Games]],<ref name="ArtStation Models">{{ArtStation|username=tricyclecaptain|url=artwork/yaEE3|name=[[Danny Mak]]|text=STAR WARS: Jedi Challenges &#124; Strategic Combat 2|date=2018-07-20|quote=I had the pleasure of working on countless characters, environments/weapons and ships for Disney's Star Wars: Jedi Challanges &#124; Strategic Combat. An AR game for mobile. Here are some assets I can show from the game. They're all hand painted textures. Characters/weapons were 512x512 while some of the ships/vehicles are 1k. Used 3DCoat to bake textures and paint. Learned a lot on this project. Specifically pushing the amount of detail that I could fit onto a 515x512. Special thanks to Adam Schuman for helping establish a style.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/5bt5T}}</ref> starting with the game development company in [[June]] of [[2015]] but leaving in [[February]] [[2019]], going on to work for companies like [[Wikipedia:Funcom|Funcom]] and Singularity 6, though he now operates as a freelance concept artist and modeler-for-hire.<ref name="LinkedIn" />
