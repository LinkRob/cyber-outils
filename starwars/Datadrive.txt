A '''Datadrive''' was a device used to store data. The [[Castaways (The Island)|Castaways]] of [[The Island]] used it as a "digital wallet" to store [[databits]] and [[probits]]. A fully upgraded Datadrive could store maximum of 40,000 databits and 40,000 probits.<ref name="Castaways" />
