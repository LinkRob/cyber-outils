'''Chandra''' was a [[moon]] orbiting the [[planet]] [[Chandrila]] in the [[Chandrila system]].<ref name="SWGS Location" />

{{Moon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Chandra was not visible out of the [[viewport]]s during the [[Halcyon|Starcruiser's]] journey; however, it could be seen on one of the in-cabin itinerary map screens, and was sometimes mentioned as one of Chandrila's two moons by crew members.<ref name="SWGS">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref>
