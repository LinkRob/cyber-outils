The '''Finance Committee''' was a committee in the [[Galactic Senate]] that related to the [[Galactic Republic]]'s budgetary and financial matters. Several subcommittees were placed under the Finance Committee which were concerned with matters such as revenue generation, government grant approvals, annual budgets, and emergency relief fund appropriations. Despite his corrupt reputation, [[Twi'lek]] [[Senator]] [[Orn Free Taa]] of the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] was a member of the Finance Committee and chaired several of its subcommittees.<ref name="Rise" /> Another finance-focused committee under the Senate was the [[Senate Budgetary Committee]].<ref name="Coruscant">{{EncyclopediaCite|Coruscant}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Finance Committee was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> The [[Finance Committee/Legends|Finance Committee]] was originally mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook]]'', a [[1990]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] book authored by [[Paul Murphy]] for the ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' by [[West End Games]].<ref name="TRAS">''[[The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook]]''</ref>
