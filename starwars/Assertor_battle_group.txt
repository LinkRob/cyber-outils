A large [[Imperial Navy|naval]] '''battle group''' of the [[Galactic Empire]], containing the [[Assertor-class Star Dreadnought|''Assertor''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Assertor]]'', was active sometime after the [[Battle of Yavin]]. The [[fleet]] was deployed along the border between the [[Mid Rim Territories]] and [[Outer Rim Territories]] to assist in operations against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The [[battle group]] was presumed to have comprised of dozens of other [[starship]]s, including smaller [[dreadnought]]s, [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class]] and [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]], various [[cruiser]]-sized vessels, and numerous support ships.<ref name="Lead by Example" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle group was mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Lead by Example]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]] for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="Lead by Example" />
