'''Diara''' was a fictional character that appeared in the [[holodrama]] series ''[[Baradium Dynasty]]'', which had released at least sixty episodes<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> By the sixtieth episode, Diara had become lovers with [[Talesh Makeric]], an individual who worked with their [[Diara's mother|mother]]. When Diara professed their love to Makeric, he confessed that Diara's [[Gender|mother]] had already [[Marriage|proposed]] to him and that he had agreed. Diara later confronted their mother about the engagement and learned that she was only marrying Makeric for his [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. She went on to promise Diara that the pair would be divorced in a [[cycle]] and Diara could have him then. Their conversation was interrupted when Makeric arrived and informed them that his [[Talesh Makeric's father|father]] had been [[death|killed]] in a [[refinery]] accident.<ref name="Outlaws" />
