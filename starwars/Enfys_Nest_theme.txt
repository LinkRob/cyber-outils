A '''theme''' was composed by [[John Powell]] representing [[Enfys Nest]] and the [[Cloud-Riders]] in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Solo" /> The ''Solo'' theme was recorded in [[March]] of 2018 with a 98-piece orchestra in London's [[Abbey Road Studios]], and features the 36-voice<ref name="Variety" /> [[Vanya Moneva]] Bulgarian women's choir.<ref name="Vanya" />

Powell stated that his intention in composing the theme was to add in a novel, exotic voice to the film's [[Solo: A Star Wars Story (soundtrack)|soundtrack]]. Powell took inspiration from [[John Williams]]' choral writing in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], establishing the new choral sound while harkening back to Williams' style. Knowing that the orchestration would be very aggressive, Powell brought in the Vanya Moneva Bulgarian choir for their particular sound, since they do not sing vibrato and contribute a "real aggression."<ref name="SW.com" />

In ''Solo: A Star Wars Story'', the theme is first played when Enfys Nest and her Cloud-Riders arrive at the [[20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport|train]] [[heist on Vandor]] to take [[coaxium]] from [[Beckett's gang]],<ref name="Solo" /> with Powell using the Bulgarian choir to change the musical movement to frighten audiences.<ref name="SW.com" /> At the climax of the film, when Enfys Nest and her crew are revealed to be vigilantes helping out the innocents of [[the galaxy]] and take over the [[Savareen]] outpost,<ref name="Solo" /> Powell again uses the choir to change the musical movement.<ref name="SW.com" />
