The '''Aggarda system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Metatessu sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square R-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Aggarda]].<ref name="Origin" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Aggarda system was first mentioned in the second iteration of the [[Star Systems of the Galaxy]],<ref name="Appendix" /> an appendix of star systems written by [[Jason Fry]] and released on [[StarWars.com]] on [[October 13]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref>
