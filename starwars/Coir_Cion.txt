'''Coir Cion''' was a [[Devaronian]] criminal who lived during the [[Republic Era]]. He operated out of and ran a territory on the [[moon of Dawk]].<ref name="DM-BW&R 3" /> Between around [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> while in the [[Coruscant Underworld]], Cion witnessed a deal between a [[pirate]] he knew and a [[Mas Amedda|Chagrian]], whom the Devaronian followed to the [[Federal District|Senate District]], where the [[Chagrian]] met with [[Senator]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. The fugitive Cion hoped to use his information on Palpatine's dealings to blackmail the senator into pardoning him. However, Cion was unaware that Palpatine was truly the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]], who [[Mission to the moon of Dawk|dispatched]] his [[Sith apprentice]], [[Maul|Darth Maul]], to [[Death|kill]] him.<ref name="DM-BW&R 3" />
