'''Frenks''' were a slender [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]].

==Biology and appearance==
Frenks were a slender [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] with diamond-shaped heads,<ref name="Frenk" /> large [[Color|brown]]-,<ref name="Padmé Amidala 1" /> blue-,<ref name="Bounty Hunters" /> or cerulean-colored [[eye]]s,<ref name="Day Off" /> and green or pink skin.<ref name="Frenk" />

==Frenks in the galaxy==
[[File:Risha_Synata.jpg|thumb|left|178px|Risha Synata, a famous Frenk actress]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], some Frenks found work as [[bounty hunter]]s, the most notable being [[Twazzi]], who was present in the "[[Box]]" and was a notable acrobat. [[Rumi Paramita]] was a member of a [[Sugi's bounty hunters|cadre of bounty hunters]] who were hired to protect a village of [[Felucian]] [[nysillin]] [[farmer]]s from a [[Ohnaka Gang|group]] of [[pirate]]s.<ref name="Bounty Hunters" /> She [[Death|perished]] while [[Skirmish on Felucia|defending]] the village.<ref name="Rumi Paramita's Fate">{{SW|url=video/the-clone-wars-answers-rumi-paramita-s-fate|text=The Clone Wars Answers: Rumi Paramita's Fate}}</ref>

[[Risha Synata]] was a [[Gender|female]] Frenk who was a famous [[Actor|actress]] during the Clone Wars.<ref name="Intermission 1">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=12|story=Intermission, Part 1}}</ref> Synata was later caught working for [[Count]] [[Dooku]] by [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. She was later sent to [[prison]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Intermission 2">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=13|story=Intermission, Part 2}}</ref>

[[Lis Mohles]] was a female Frenk [[assassin]] who was wanted by the [[Galactic Republic]] during the Clone Wars. On [[Clabron]], she tried to assassinate [[Senator]] Amidala but was later incapacitated by Amidala.<ref name="Padmé Amidala 1" />
