'''Axil Research Station''', also known as '''Axil Station''', was an [[Water|underwater]] research station sponsored by the [[Galactic Republic]] on the [[planet]] [[Tordus]]. The station was constructed to explore the ruins of the [[Axiloth]] civilization. It was supported by four legs and had twelve [[escape pod]]s in the undercarriage in case of emergency.<ref name="ToT 1" />
