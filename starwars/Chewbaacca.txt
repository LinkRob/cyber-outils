'''Chewbaacca''' was a [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] of the legendary [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] [[warrior]] [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]]. He was cloned by [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo/Legends|Thrawn]]. Thrawn used to pit [[Duurga]] against him for arm-wrestling. But after Chewbaacca pulled one of Duurga's arms out of its socket during a match, he stopped them. He didn't let Chewbacca anywhere near [[Duuurga]] after that.<ref name="Best Thing" />

==Behind the scenes==
'''Chewbaacca''' was mentioned in "[[An Apology]]," a short story by [[Timothy Zahn]] that was released on [[April 1]], [[2012]] as an April Fool's Day joke. Chewbaacca was set to appear in the second novel, ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars 2: Woookiee'', in which [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] would go to a distant moon where the clone of his best friend Chewbacca, Chewbaacca, had amassed a huge army of Chewbacca clones. Han's soul was broken by the presence of the evil clone army of his best friend. He would have to destroy them in honor of Chewbacca.
