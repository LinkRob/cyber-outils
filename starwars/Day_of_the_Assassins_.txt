"'''Day of the Assassins!'''" was a comic originally published in [[Star Wars Weekly 105|''Star Wars Weekly'' 105]]. It was later reprinted in [[Star Wars (Marvel Illustrated Books)|Marvel Illustrated Books' ''Star Wars'']] as part of the story ''[[The Weapons Master!]]''.
