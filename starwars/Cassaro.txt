'''Cassaro''' was a world located in [[the galaxy]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Galactic Empire]] had a presence on Cassaro. At one point, the [[bounty hunter]]s [[Latts Razzi]] and [[C-21 Highsinger]], their former associate [[Asajj Ventress]], and Ventress' companion [[Lyco Strata]] embarked on a [[Heist at Cassaro shipyard|heist on Cassaro]] to steal a [[Imperial shield relay unit]] from an Imperial [[Cassaro shipyard|shipyard]]. The four traveled to Cassaro in [[Latts Razzi's ship|Razzi's starship]], and after they landed outside of the [[shipyard]], Highsinger, Ventress, and Strata infiltrated it. As Highsinger provided a distraction, Ventress and Strata acquired the shield relay unit.<ref name="Friends" />
