'''''Ahsoka'' 5''' is the fifth issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries [[Star Wars: Ahsoka (comic series)|''Star Wars: Ahsoka'' comic adaptation]]. It is an adaptation of the [[Part Five: Shadow Warrior|fifth episode]] of the television series [[Star Wars: Ahsoka|''Ahsoka'']]. The issue was written by [[Rodney Barnes]], illustrated by [[Steven Cummings]], [[Wayne Faucher]], and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 6]], 2024.

==Publisher's summary==
''SHADOW WARRIOR! AHSOKA confronts her past with ANAKIN SKYWALKER while HERA and her allies undertake a rescue mission!''

==Opening crawl==
{{OpeningCrawl
|name=PART FIVE: SHADOW WARRIOR
|text=Padawan. Rebel. Ronin. She has been many things, but above all she has defended peace and justice
throughout a galaxy rife with conflict, losing mentors and friends in the process. She is&hellip;.

Stranded on SEATOS, AHSOKA TANO and SABINE WREN tracked down the enemy, who, with the map in their
possession, were preparing the ''EYE OF SION'' to travel to PERIDEA. Elsewhere, GENERAL HERA SYNDULLA
disobeyed orders and assembled a small squadron of trusted NEW REPUBLIC allies to assist Ahsoka and Sabine.

After BAYLAN SKOLL defeated Ahsoka in combat and knocked her into the waters, Sabine regained the map, only
to be faced with an impossible choice--destroy the map and any chance of finding EZRA BRIDGER, or hand it over
to the enemy and risk GRAND ADMIRAL THRAWN's return. Sabine surrendered the map and went with Baylan and
his apprentice, SHIN HATI.

Now, left for dead by her adversaries, Ahsoka has awakened in a mysterious place&hellip;and has been greeted by
her former master, ANAKIN SKYWALKER&hellip;.}}
