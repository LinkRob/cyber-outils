'''Adegan crystals''' were [[kyber crystal]]s found in and around the [[Adega system]]. There were five different types of Adegan crystal. One type was the [[mephite crystal]]. Adegan crystals were [[The Force|Force]]-reactive and could be infused with a small amount of a [[Force-sensitive]]'s power.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
