'''Clone cadet''' was a [[Military rank|rank]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. It was assigned to [[clone trooper]]s who were still training to become [[soldier]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Bred on the [[extragalactic]] [[planet]] [[Kamino]], [[cadet]]s were trained in [[Tipoca City]] and other [[cloning]] facilities spread across the [[Kaminoan]] [[homeworld]].

==Description==
While they were cadets, [[clone trooper]]s spent most of their accelerated childhoods in military training, learning tactics and engaging in countless drills. Most of the clone cadets' education took place on [[Kamino]], although some did pursue offworld missions as part of the [[Clone Youth Brigade]]. Clone cadets were trained in armed and [[unarmed combat]], military tactics and heavy [[weapon]]ry.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref>

==History==
===Education on Kamino===
{{Quote|We take great pride in our combat education and training programs. This group was created about five years ago.|Lama Su|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
[[File:Jax-TCWCEJtB.png|thumb|left|180px|A juvenile clone cadet named Jax.]]
Utilizing the [[DNA|genetic code]] of [[Jango Fett]], the [[Kaminoan]]s [[Bioengineering|engineered]] an [[army]] of [[human]] [[Cloning|clones]] for the [[Galactic Republic]]. In addition to greater docility, the [[clone template]] was modified to allow for growth acceleration, which was an essential aspect in the clone army's design as it ensured the units would reach adulthood in only a [[Standard decade|decade's]] [[time]]. [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was given an inspection tour of [[Tipoca City]], where he observed clone [[cadet]]s in various stages of development. One group in particular had been created approximately five [[Standard year|years]] prior to Kenobi's arrival.<ref name="Episode II" />

Young clone cadets were given an intense, rapid education<ref name="CVD New Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> within the cloning facilities of the [[extragalactic]] [[planet]] Kamino.<ref name="SWCC2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> This form of training required the use of focus-enhancing<ref name="CVD New Edition" /> [[even-class training helmet]]s.<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> They were also divided into separate teams as a means of encouraging teamwork and competitiveness among their ranks.<ref name="CVD New Edition" /> At this stage in their training, cadets were supervised by Kaminoan [[scientist]]s<ref name="Episode II" /> and fully-grown clone troopers who held the [[military rank]] of [[Clone Sergeant]].<ref name="Death Trap" />

[[File:Youngcloneslearning.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Clone cadets experienced a rapid military education during their early accelerated years.]]
By the time of the [[Clone Wars]], the older cadets were overseen by hired [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] and [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s, who were on the lookout for clones who showed exceptional skill and potential for joining the ARC [[trooper]]s' ranks. [[Jedi Master]] [[Shaak Ti]], a member of the [[Jedi High Council]], oversaw the clone trooper training program as the [[Jedi Order]]'s liaison to the [[Ruling Council (Kamino)|Kaminoan government]] led by [[Prime Minister]] [[Lama Su]].<ref name="Clone Cadets">{{TCW|Clone Cadets}}</ref> Prior to the [[Jedi]]'s involvement, cadets were threatened with disposal by their engineers if they failed to pass their training. Clones who displayed signs of divergent behavior were placed in [[retraining pod]]s for isolation. These practices were ended by the [[Jedi General]]s who controlled the [[Grand Army of the Republic]],<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> and failed clones were reassigned to non-combat roles such as maintenance duty.<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> At some point during the history of the clone army, [[CT-380]] and the rest of their squad was involved in a training match, in which the squad members needed to support each other under fire.<ref name="Squad">{{SWU|set=Twilight of the Republic (Star Wars: Unlimited)|cardname=Squad Support|link=3292172753}}</ref>

===Clone Wars===
{{Quote|Look around! We're one and the same; same heart, same blood. Your training is in your blood. And my blood's boiling for a fight!|Fives, to a group of young cadets|<ref name="ARC Troopers">{{TCW|ARC Troopers (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:Training_Room_Kamino_AtG.png|thumb|left|250px|The training simulations became more rigorous as the cadets grew older and closer to becoming soldiers.]]
During the Clone Wars, Shaak Ti observed the difficulty that [[Domino Squad]] encountered in their efforts to complete the final test in their training. One of their trainers, the [[bounty hunter]] [[Bric]], recommended failing the [[squad]] and reassigning them as maintenance workers. The [[Togruta]] Jedi Master considered his opinion, but ultimately gave the Domino cadets a second chance. After succeeding in their trial, [[Droidbait|CT-00-2010]], [[Echo|CT-1409]], [[Cutup|CT-4040]], [[Fives|CT-5555]], and [[Hevy|CT-782]] [[Graduation medal|graduated]] and became full-fledged clone troopers.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

As the conflict progressed, the Clone Youth Brigade&mdash;a [[brigade]] of young clone cadets&mdash;was allowed to leave Kamino under the supervision of [[Sergeant]] [[Crasher]]. They were sent to the ''[[Endurance]]'', a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] in the [[Republic Navy]] to observe the duties of battle-ready clone troopers as part of their training. Unbeknownst to the cadets, their ranks were infiltrated by [[Boba Fett]], an unmodified clone who sought to [[Sabotage of the Endurance|assassinate]] Jedi [[General]] [[Mace Windu]].<ref name="Death Trap" />

===Age of the Empire===
{{Quote|When the Empire transferred us off Kamino, we thought we were getting more training. Instead, they made us prisoners. Took samples of our blood. [...]''"<br />"''At least we escaped before they destroyed the facility.''"<br />"''And you survived out here ever since?''"<br />"''We're soldiers. Or, we were supposed to be.|Deke and Stak speaking to Hunter|<ref name="Paths Unknown" />}}
In the months following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the cloning facilities on Kamino were decommissioned, and the clone troopers and clone cadets were redeployed off-world to other locations. According to [[Nala Se]], the training of the clone cadets would be continued elsewhere, and they would still become soldiers.<ref name="War-Mantle" />

However, several of the young clones were imprisoned in a laboratory on the jungle planet [[Setron]] and experimented upon by Doctor [[Royce Hemlock]], the head of the [[Advanced Science Division]]. After using them as test subjects for [[Project Necromancer]], the Empire abandoned the lab and the cadets, attempting to destroy the facility via [[orbital bombardment]]. Three clones named [[Mox]], [[Deke]], and [[Stak (cadet)|Stak]] survived and remained on the planet until being discovered by members of [[Clone Force 99]], namely [[Hunter]] and [[Wrecker]]. After helping the two commandos access a terminal within the ruins of the [[slither vine]]-infested laboratory, the boys were rescued by Hunter and Wrecker and flown off-world to the island refuge of [[Pabu]].<ref name="Paths Unknown">{{TBB|Paths Unknown}}</ref>

==Equipment==
[[File:CloneCadets-CC.png|thumb|right|250px|Clone Cadets in training armor]]
In combat simulations, clone cadets utilized a type of [[Clone training armor|training armor]] and [[helmet]]s that were different from the standard-issue [[Clone trooper armor|battle armor]] worn by fully-trained clone troopers.<ref name="Clone Armor DB">{{Databank|clone-trooper-armor|Clone Trooper Armor}}</ref> The cadets' [[armor]] included [[color]]-coded [[pauldron]]s and gauntlets, such as red for [[Bravo Squad (clone cadets)|Bravo Squad]] or green for Domino Squad.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />
