'''Carbon dioxide''' was a [[gas]] that [[human]]s expelled from their [[lung]]s during respiration. When she crashed her [[escape pod]] into a river on the [[planet]] [[Mokivj]] to avoid pursuit from the [[Batuu police]]<ref name="Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]'' set during the [[Clone Wars]] are dated to [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] took several large breaths to flush as much carbon dioxide as possible from her lungs before entering the [[water]].<ref name="Alliances" /> The planet [[Dagobah]] was covered with thick clouds of [[water]] vapor and carbon dioxide.<ref name="Nexus">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref>
