A '''droidsmith''', also spelled '''Droid-smith''', was a craftsperson who specialized in the creation, repair, and modification of [[droid]]s. The [[Rodian]] [[Gedeek Obaz]] was a skilled droidsmith who worked with the [[Imperial Engineering Corps]] on [[Akiva]] before being recruited into [[Kay Vess' crew]]. <ref name="Star Wars Outlaws">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> The [[Anzellan Droidsmiths]] were a group of [[Anzellan]]s worked on the [[planet]] [[Nevarro]]<ref name="Apostate">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 17: The Apostate}}</ref> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Mando Date">{{MandoDate|17}}</ref> The Anzellan [[Babu Frik]] was a skilled droidsmith who worked with the [[smuggler]] gang known as the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]] out of a [[Babu Frik's workshop|workshop]] on the planet [[Kijimi]]. Another [[Unidentified droidsmith|droidsmith]] built the droid [[D-O]], but was [[Death|killed]] by the [[assassin]] [[Ochi]], who stole D-O.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
