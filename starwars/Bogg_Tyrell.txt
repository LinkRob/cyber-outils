'''Bogg Tyrell''' was an [[Aleena]] [[Gender|female]] from<ref name="CL2016" /> her [[species]]' [[homeworld]], [[Aleen]], which she [[Senator|represented]] in the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="BMF" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}} to [[22 BBY]].</ref> Tyrell [[pilot]]ed<ref name="AOTC" /> a [[speeder]] through the skylanes of [[Coruscant]], [[capital]] of the Republic, while being entertained by the [[Dug]] [[holodrama]] star [[Seboca]].<ref name="CL2016" />

As they flew through traffic, Tyrell was forced to swerve out of the way to avoid colliding with the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who was hanging from the [[assassin droid]] [[ASN-121]]. Tyrell cried out during the near miss, while Seboca cursed at Kenobi. Tyrell later entered [[Dex's Diner]] in the [[CoCo Town]] district of Coruscant while both Kenobi and Seboca were seated inside. The Aleena staggered out again soon after with a drink in hand. The senator had [[Color|blue]]-and-tan skin, blue [[eye]]s, and wore a brown [[Clothing|jacket]] over a red shirt with tan sleeves.<ref name="AOTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bogg Tyrell appeared in the [[2002]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="AOTC" /> The character was created by modifying the computer-generated model of [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Ratts Tyerell]] made for the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="Chronicle">''[[Star Wars Chronicles: The Prequels]]''</ref> [[Dermot Power]] created concept art of Tyrell's costume in [[August]] [[2000]] with the production code "dp479", although he was not the designer of the character herself.<ref name="IG">{{Instagram|username=powerdermot|name=[[Dermot Power]]|url=p/COzOKivDA3y|date=2021-05-13|quote=Costume design for Ep 2 creature from a long time ago ... etc. Did not design Bogg Tyrell .|archivefile=File:BoggTyrell-DermotPower-Instagram.png}}</ref>

In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the character was first identified in "[[Guide to the Galaxy|The Dual Worlds of Aleen]]," an article<ref name="BMF" /> written by [[Tim Veekhoven]]<ref name="SM">{{DMCite|dm=File:Credits-TimVeekhoven-Twitter.png|creator=[[Tim Veekhoven]]}}</ref> that was published in [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 56|issue fifty-six]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine series<ref name="BMF" /> around [[January 27]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|56}}</ref> The name was first used in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2003]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]'', which was written by [[Simon Beecroft]] and [[Curtis Saxton]].<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]''</ref>
