A '''hangar''' was located on [[Geonosis]] which once held [[Count Dooku's solar sailer|Count Dooku's ''Punworcca 116-''class interstellar sloop]]. During the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]] fought [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] in a [[Lightsaber duel]]. After he defeated them both, he then fought [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]].<ref name="Episode II" />
