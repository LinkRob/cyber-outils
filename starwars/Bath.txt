A '''bath''' was a tub of [[water]] used for bathing. Alternatively, the term "bath" could also refer to the process of bathing.<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref>

Several [[species]] across the [[galaxy]] enjoyed having a bath<ref name="SC 105">{{SkeletonCrew|You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates}}</ref> and utilized various bathing techniques. [[Chiss]], for example, bathed using [[soap]],<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> and other species, such as [[Hutt]]s or [[Muun]]s, were fond of [[mud bath]]s.<ref name="SC 105" /> [[Droid]]s, on the other hand, had what was known as [[oil bath]]s.<ref name="Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref> While in a bath, some individuals, like a certain<ref name="SC 105" /> [[Unidentified Hutt (Lanupa)|male Hutt]],<ref name="SC 105 AD">{{SkeletonCrew|You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> liked to have simultaneous meals.<ref name="SC 105" />

On one occasion, when [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] was a [[Youngling|toddler]], she ran from her bath into the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan]]'s formal dining room wet and naked during a diplomatic [[Foodstuff|dinner]], causing her [[nanny droid]] [[WA-2V]] to chase after her.<ref name="Leia">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>
