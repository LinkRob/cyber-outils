'''''Doctor Aphra'' 35''' is the thirty-fifth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[August 23]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''LEGACY ISSUE #75! A ''DARK DROIDS'' TIE-IN! Sent by Domina Tagge to investigate a series of strange droid malfunctions, DOCTOR APHRA faces a deadly enemy from the past. A horde of CLONE WAR-Era PROTOTYPE BATTLE DROIDS designed by Domina herself!''
