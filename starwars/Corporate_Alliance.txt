The '''Corporate Alliance''' was a conglomerate active during the final [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="Episode II" /> [[Corporate Alliance headquarters|headquartered]] on [[Murkhana]].<ref name="Rise" /> It would pledge its resources to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Separatist Crisis|Secessionist Movement]], earning its [[magistrate]] [[Passel Argente]] a place on the [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Episode II" /> During the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], the Corporate Alliance would have been able to supply ground reinforcements to the Separatists, but they were stopped by the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[orbit]]al cordon. The [[Geonosian]]s launched thousands of [[Nantex-class territorial defense starfighter|''Nantex'' starfighters]] to break the cordon,<ref name="Complete Vehicles">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> but the Republic ultimately won the battle.<ref name="Episode II" />

The Alliance sponsored the Separatist cause<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> and had representation in the [[Separatist Senate|Separatist Parliament]] through [[Senator]] [[Voe Atell]].<ref name="HOBS">{{TCW|Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref> With the defeat of the CIS and rise of the [[Galactic Empire|First Galactic Empire]] in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> however, the Corporate Alliance, like other Separatist sponsors, was quick to withdraw all support towards the CIS and enter into peace talks with the new empire, effectively groveling before the new regime in order to survive.<ref name="MoF" /> While other Separatist-loyal corporations,<ref name="CIS DB">{{Databank|confederacy-of-independent-systems|Confederacy of Independent Systems}}</ref> such as the [[Trade Federation]]<ref name="BustCollection13">{{BustCollectionCite|13|Star Wars Universe}}</ref> and [[Techno Union]], were nationalized,<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> the Corporate Alliance was among those who survived and was permitted to continue its operations<ref name="Stealth Strike">{{Rebels|Stealth Strike}}</ref> after its show of fidelity.<ref name="MoF" /> On one occasion, [[Phoenix Cell]]'s [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]] attempted to bluff the Imperial [[Admiral]] [[Brom Titus]] by claiming that he and his crew were members of the Corporate Alliance. However, Titus was not fooled.<ref name="Stealth Strike" />
