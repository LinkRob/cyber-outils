The '''At Attin pledge''' was a pledge given by children at [[At Attin Middle School Gamma]], a learning institution on the [[planet]] [[At Attin]].<ref name="Episode">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}}</ref> The pledge was a vow of allegiance to the [[Great Work]].<ref name="SoundInterview">{{SW|url=news/skeleton-crew-skywalker-sound-interview|text=The Strange and Familiar Sounds of ''Star Wars: Skeleton Crew''}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The At Attin pledge was first featured in [[This Could Be a Real Adventure]], the first episode of the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]''.<ref name="Episode" /> According to [[David W. Collins]], who worked as a sound designer in the show, the At Attin pledge was inspired by the [[Wikipedia:Pledge of Allegiance|U.S. Pledge of Allegiance]].<ref name="SoundInterview" />
