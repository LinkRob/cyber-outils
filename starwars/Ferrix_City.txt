'''Ferrix City'''<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|219|Galactic Britain II: Cassian's Travels}}</ref> was a [[city]] located on the [[Free Trade sector]] [[planet]] [[Ferrix]].<ref name="Andor 1" /> [[Rix Road]] ran through the heart of Ferrix City.<ref name="NewEdition">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref>

It was home to [[Maarva Carassi Andor]], her husband [[Clem Andor]] and their adopted son [[Cassian Jeron Andor]].<ref name="Andor 1" /> It was the site of a [[Rix Road Protest|protest]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Announcement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion date">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'' states that [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] was arrested for confronting Imperials in [[19 BBY]]. As seen in "[[Announcement]]," that attack was preceded by the [[Rix Road Protest|Rix Road protest]] of the [[Imperial Era]]: as the [[Galactic Empire]] is [[Proclamation of the New Order|formed]] in 19 BBY according to ''Dawn of Rebellion'', the protest occurs in 19 BBY by virtue of occurring after the formation of the Empire but before Andor's arrest.</ref> an [[Ambush on Ferrix|attack]] against a [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces|Pre-Mor]] [[Pre-Mor Security Inspection team|security Inspection team]]<ref name="Andor 3">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref> in [[5 BBY]]<ref name="Date2">{{AndorDate|103}}</ref> as well as a massive [[Ferrix Riot|riot]] against the [[Galactic Empire]] later that [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="Andor 12">{{Andor|Rix Road (episode)}}</ref>

==History==
===Imperial occupation===
Located in the [[Free Trade sector]] [[planet]] [[Ferrix]] of the [[Morlani system]], Ferrix City was aligned with the [[Galactic Republic]] during the time of the [[Clone Wars]]. After the war's end, it came under [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] occupation sometime after the [[Proclamation of the New Order]], with the first Imperial [[clone trooper]] units arriving to the city accompanied by [[Imperial officer]]s<ref name="Announcement" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion date" /> The townspeople reacted with concern in the sight of the new administration and a radical faction of Ferrixians sprung into a spontaneous [[Rix Road Protest|protest]], pelting the clone units with stones as they marched through the main street, [[Rix Road]]. Their [[Unidentified Imperial officer (Ferrix)|commanding officer]] ordered the parading units to turn and point their [[DC-15A blaster carbine|weapons]] at the protestors, who were all swiftly arrested. Some, including [[Clem Andor]] were publicly [[Death penalty|executed]], their bodies left [[hanging]] in [[Fountain Square]] for days after the protest.<ref name="Announcement" /> Although the Empire left the planet soon after, distrust and hate for the new authority remained among the locals who hadn't forgotten their neighbors' terrible fate.<ref name="Andor 12" />

===Return of the Empire===
{{Expandsect}}
Ferrix, along with the rest of the Morlani system, was passed over to the [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]] corporation sometime before [[5 BBY]].<ref name="Date3">"[[Kassa]]" dates itself to 5 BBY and the Morlani system is already under Preox-Morlana control by then.</ref> The corporation and its [[security]] forces, the [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces|Pre-Mor Enforcement]], preferred to leave the planet alone, as they thought that "locals had their own way of doing things."<ref name="Andor 1" /> The last census was performed in [[11 BBY]]<ref name="Date4">It is mentioned in [[Kassa]], which dates itself to 5 BBY, that the last census was performed "six years ago," thus placing it in [[11 BBY]].</ref> and the locals were rarely visited by any [[Tac-Corpo|corpos]], whom they referred to as "the blues."<ref name="Andor 3" /> The calm was interrupted, however, when local citizen [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] visited the system capital [[Morlana One]] searching for his sister [[Kerri]], along the way having a fight with two local corpos, [[Verlo Skiff]] and [[Kravas Drezzer]], whom he ended up killing. Pre-Mor [[Deputy Inspector]] [[Syril Karn]] took over the case and was quickly able to identify Andor and track him down to his hometown in Ferrix,<ref name="Andor 1" /> organizing a [[Pre-Mor Security Inspection team]] led by [[Sergeant]] [[Linus Mosk]] to arrest him.<ref name="Andor 3" />

Arriving in the city in three [[RK-392 mobile tac-pod|Mobile Tac-Pods]], the corpos of the team quickly encountered resistance from the locals when they attempted to interrogate Andor's beloved mother and ex-[[President]] of the local [[Daughters of Ferrix]], [[Maarva Carassi Andor]]; a crowd quickly gathered outside her [[Andor household|house]] and harassed the officers by banging on metallic fixtures along their path and removing street signs to confuse and intimidate them. The disoriented officers were quickly [[Ambush on Ferrix|ambushed]] by Andor who, along with [[Axis network|rebel]] spymaster [[Luthen Rael]] had planted [[explosive charge]]s on the doors of the warehouse they were hiding in, killing the corpos who attempted the surround them and surprising the rest with a trapped [[speeder]]. Leaving many officers dead or severely injured, Andor and Rael escaped aboard the latter's [[Luthen Rael's Fondor Haulcraft|Fondor Haulcraft]].<ref name="Andor 3" />
