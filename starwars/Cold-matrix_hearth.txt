A '''cold-matrix hearth''' was a type of [[Temperature|thermal]] technology used to create [[Color|pale silver]] [[Fire|flames]] and produce light breezes. During the [[High Republic Era]], a [[Unidentified Coruscant café|café]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] had the hearths as decor.<ref name="TOTF" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> the [[Nihil]] [[Minister of Information (Nihil)|Minister of Information]] [[Ghirra Starros]] visited the [[Cafe|establishment]], noticing the cold-matrix hearths while enjoying a [[glass]] of [[cream tea]].<ref name="TOTF" />

==Behind the scenes==
