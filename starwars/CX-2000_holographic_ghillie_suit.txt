The '''CX-2000 holographic ghillie suit''' was a [[Clothing|ghillie suit]] that used intergrated [[Hologram|holographic]] [[Holoprojector|projectors]] that made the wearer indistinguishable from surrounding [[Plant|foliage]] while immobile. The wearer was able to blend in seamlessly due to the fact that the electronics in the suit included phototropic [[sensor]]s that analyzed the surrounding area, as well as [[Atmosphere|atmospheric]] sensors that altered the image to account for wind direction or [[Water|precipitation]] and other weather.<ref name="G&G" />

The CX-2000 was used by [[sniper]]s and other special operations personnel on missions were standard phototropic camouflaging was insufficient for extended concealment. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the suites cost eight hundred [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and dauntingly hard to find for purchase.<ref name="G&G" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the CX-2000 holographic ghillie suit was mentioned in the [[2019]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="G&G" /> The [[CX-2000 holographic ghillie suit/Legends|suit]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were mentioned in ''[[Dangerous Covenants]]'', a [[2014]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' that was also published by Fantasy Flight Games.<ref name="LoNH">''[[Lords of Nal Hutta]]''</ref>
