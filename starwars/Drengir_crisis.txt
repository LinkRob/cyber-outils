The '''Drengir crisis''', also known as the '''Drengir campaign''', ([[232 BBY]]&ndash;[[231 BBY]]) was a major infestation by the amorphous [[Sentience|sentient]] [[carnivorous plant]]s known as the [[Drengir]], who began to overrun the [[Galactic Frontier|edge]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Hutt Space|territory]] of the [[Hutt Clan]]. Containment efforts by the [[Jedi Order]] were undertaken to prevent the threat of the Drengir and their harvest.

The Drengir emerged from [[Mulita]], deep within [[Wild Space]] in the aftermath of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], and partook in a number of attacks and even overrunning entire [[Planet|worlds]] in [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]].

==History==
After the [[Drengir]] were [[Mission on the Amaxine station|encountered]] on the [[Amaxine Space Station|Amaxine space station]] following the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]],<ref name="Into the Dark" /> they began to wake up across the [[galaxy]]. The beings undertook a number of attacks across the frontier, including an [[Assault on Starlight Beacon|assault]] on the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]],<ref name="THR 5" /> with entire [[Planet|worlds]] becoming overrun by the Drengir. The [[Jedi]] of Starlight, under [[Marshal]] [[Avar Kriss]], were tasked with handling the situation.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />
