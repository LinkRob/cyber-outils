'''2P-1'''<ref name="GEStaff">{{GEStaff|Droid Depot|store}}</ref> was a [[2-1B surgical droid]] with [[Color|yellow]] and white plating.<ref name="GEDD" /> During the [[First Order]]'s [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> 2P-1 was kept on display in the [[Droid Depot]], a store located in [[Black Spire Outpost]]. The [[medical droid]] was displayed in a cage next to<ref name="GEDD" /> the [[KX-series security droid]] [[K-7R1]]<ref name="GEStaff" /> near the depot's counter.<ref name="GEDD" /> Like all 2-1B surgical [[droid]]s, 2P-1 was manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]] and stood 1.77 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="CEUE" />

==Behind the scenes==
2P-1 appeared as a prop within the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="GEDD" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref>
