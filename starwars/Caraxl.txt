'''Caraxl''' was an astronomical object located in the [[Cassander sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. In [[34 ABY]], during the [[Cold War]] between the [[Resistance]] and the [[First Order]], the Resistance attempted to [[Mission to Caraxl|deliver]] relief supplies to Caraxl; however, the mission was aborted after [[Resistance pilot]] [[Tallissan Lintra]] reported that First Order [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] were on route to intercept them.

==Description==
Caraxl was an astronomical object<ref name="Bomber Command" /> located in the [[Caraxl system]] within the [[Cassander sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square K-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> within the [[New Territories]].<ref name="Location" />

==History==
During the [[Cold War]] between the [[Resistance]],<ref name="Bomber Command" /> a small private military force created by the [[New Republic]] [[senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]];<ref name="Resistance Databank">{{Databank|the-resistance|The Resistance}}</ref> and the [[First Order]],<ref name="Bomber Command" /> the successor of the [[Galactic Empire]];<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> Caraxl fell within the [[Borderlands]]<ref name="Bomber Command" /> region between the territories of the First Order and New Republic,<ref name="BTA">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref> and a [[Sheh Soahi's pirates|group]] of [[pirate]]s backed by the First Order started to raid Caraxl and other locations in the Cassander [[sector]].<ref name="Bomber Command" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date Note">According to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', the [[missions against pirates in the Cassander sector]] take place in [[34 ABY]].</ref> the Resistance [[Missions against pirates in the Cassander sector|sent]] a group of [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|bombers]] and [[A-wing starfighter]]s to stop both the pirates' and First Order's plans of taking control of the sector. Three [[Standard day|days]] after the Resistance's [[Battle of Mamkoda|mission]] to the celestial body [[Mamkoda]], Resistance [[bomber]]s [[Mission to Caraxl|tried]] to deliver supplies to Caraxl. After [[Resistance pilot]] [[Tallissan Lintra]] reported that First Order [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] were on an intercept course to their position, the bombers had to abort the mission due to orders to avoid any confrontation with the First Order that could cause problems in the [[New Republic Senate]].<ref name="Bomber Command" />

==Behind the scenes==
Caraxl was mentioned in the [[2017]] [[canon]] replica journal book ''[[The Last Jedi: Bomber Command]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Bomber Command" />
