The '''Elespads''' were a [[pachydermoid]], [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Species/Legends|species]]. They had brown or orange skin [[Color/Legends|color]], two large and floppy [[Ear/Legends|ears]], a [[Nose/Legends|trunk]] and a pair of [[Tooth/Legends|tusks]]. [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], a young [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooinian]] [[Moisture farmer/Legends|farmer]], encountered several hostile Elespads while looking for his [[Droid/Legends|droid]] [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]], who had been stolen by a [[Changeling/Legends|changeling]] named [[Sasori Vader]].

==Biology and appearance==
The Elespads were a [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Species/Legends|species]] of [[pachydermoid]]. Each Elespad had two small [[Eye/Legends|eyes]] with a [[Color/Legends|white]] scelera and black pupil. These eyes framed a long, flexible [[Nose/Legends|trunk]] marked with concentric wrinkles down its length. Below the trunk, an Elespad could grow pointed, white [[Tooth/Legends|tusks]] that jutted out from the lower jaw. Atop the [[Bone/Legends|skull]] of each Elespad grew two large [[Ear/Legends|ears]], which flopped over loosely.<ref name="SW87" />

Elespads had two [[Limb/Legends|arms]], which ended in four fingered hands. Each finger was tipped with a pointed nail. Elespads had brown or orange skin pigmentation, with a darker shade on the underside of their ears.<ref name="SW87" /> At least some members of the species stood shorter than 1.72 [[Meter/Legends|meters]].<ref name="height" />

==Society and culture==
[[File:Luke_vs_the_Ellespad.png|thumb|left|Luke Skywalker confronts an Elespad.]]
Elespads wore [[Clothing/Legends|clothing]] that included long sleeved, flowing gowns in white or beige; red hooded aprons; and decorated shoulder pads and collars.<ref name="SW87" />

==History==
Approximately around the [[Standard year|year]] [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]], several Elespads were resident on the desert [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]],<ref name="SW87" /> located in [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy's]] [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Atlas">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> Those Elespads encountered the [[Moisture farmer/Legends|moisture farmer]], [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], while he was searching for his [[Astromech droid/Legends|astromech droid]], [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]]. The droid had been stolen by a [[Changeling/Legends|changeling]] named [[Sasori Vader]]. The pachydermoids fought Skywalker, but were defeated by the [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]]-wielding [[Human/Legends|Human]].<ref name="SW87" />

==Behind the scenes==
Elespads appeared in [[Star Wars (1987 video game)|''Star Wars'']], a video game produced by [[Namco]] in [[1987]] and only released in Japan. Their name was revealed in the [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese language]] manual of the game, in which it was rendered as エレスパッド in [[Wikipedia:Katakana|katakana]] syllabary.<ref name="SW87" />

The status, and therefore timing, of the plot in this game is unclear. It is based loosely on the events of the first two ''Star Wars'' films, but departs from the script. The article "''[[Game On!]]''," published in [[Star Wars Insider 135|''Star Wars Insider'' 135]], and the game manual, indicate that the changelings in the game were "intended as illusions representing Luke's fears, like the [[Cave of Evil/Legends|Cave of Evil]] scene in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''The Empire Strikes Back'']],"<ref name="SWI135">{{InsiderCite|135|Game On!}}</ref> while a [[blog]] article on [[StarWars.com]] described the changelings as "weird apprentices of Darth Vader."<ref name="Weird">{{SW|url=news/the-weirdest-star-wars-games|text=The Weirdest ''Star Wars'' Games|nolive=1}}</ref> These articles offer conflicting interpretations of how the Elespads may fit within the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.
