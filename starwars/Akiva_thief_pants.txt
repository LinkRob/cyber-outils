An '''Akiva thief pants''' was a type of [[belt]] that used by [[Thief|thieves]] the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]] to help blend in with the [[jungle]] environment. The pants were [[Color|grey]] in color and included a pair of dark green [[boot]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Twi'lek]] [[merchant]] [[Sato Bizaz]] had at least one pair of Akiva thief pants for sale as part of an exclusive inventory they offered to member and allies of the criminal [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] while operating their [[Bizaz|stall]] in the [[Wonto District]] of [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the [[desert]] planet [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] bought a pair of the pants from Bizaz for [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A pair of Akiva thief pants appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, the pants increased the player's crouched movement speed as well as making them move more quietly. The pants also reduced the cooldown for using [[smoke grenade]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />
