'''Firehawks''' were rare [[bird]]s native to the [[planet]] [[Tenoo]]. [[Nash Durango]] named her [[starship]], the ''[[Crimson Firehawk]]'', after them. They are known for their stubbornness and their beautiful feathers.<ref name="FF" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The very namesake for Nash's faithful ship, here are the beasts themselves! The babies especially proved to be a challenge, as I needed to make them look different enough from the adults so that the kids wouldn't realize they were the same species at first.|Steph Laberis|<ref name="Laberis">{{Instagram|username=steph_laberis|url=p/C_ZrVPmRIwy|name=[[Steph Laberis]]|date=2024-09-02|quote=The very namesake for Nash's faithful ship, here are the beasts themselves!|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/fJS5g|archivefile=File:FirehawkDesign-StephLaberis-IG.png}}</ref>}}
Firehawk first appeared in "[[Feather Frenzy]]," the second part of the [[Aftershock / Feather Frenzy|twentienth episode]] of the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season One|first season]] of the [[Disney+]] and [[Disney Junior]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'',<ref name="FF" /> which aired on [[February 14]], [[2024]].<ref name="Futon">{{WebCite|url=http://www.thefutoncritic.com/showatch/star-wars-young-jedi-adventures/listings|text=Shows A-Z - Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures on Disney Plus|work=[http://www.thefutoncritic.com The Futon Critic]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/2GItc}}</ref> Both the adult and baby versions of the species were designed by [[Wikipedia:Illustrator|illustrator]] [[Steph Laberis]], who used various species of [[Wikipedia:Vulture|vultures]] and [[Wikipedia:Eagle|eagles]] as reference. Laberis also mentioned the challenge of developing distinct designs for both life stages of the firehawk.<ref name="Laberis" />
