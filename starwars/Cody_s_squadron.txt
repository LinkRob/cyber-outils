'''Cody's squadron'''<ref name="Landing AD" /> was a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]] [[squadron]] that transported part of the [[7th Sky Corps]] to the [[Point Rain]] landing zone during the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]] and was led by [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Cody]]. Although [[High Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's squadron|squadron]] departed prior, Cody's squadron of at least three [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]] and at least three [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/carrier|LAAT/c carriers]] arrived first and deployed their [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer|AT-TE walkers]], with some of the transports touching down as part of the landing site's perimeter.<ref name="Landing" />

After arriving, [[Commander]] Cody contacted General Kenobi, warning him to not land his gunships due to the heavy enemy fire. Kenobi's gunship was soon after shot down and crashed after [[Geonosian]] [[Nantex-class territorial defense starfighter|''Nantex''-class starfighters]]<ref name="Landing" /> engaged his squadron.<ref name="Landing AD" />
