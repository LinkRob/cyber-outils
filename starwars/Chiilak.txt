'''Chiilaks''' were a type of creature upon which stuffed toys were based.<ref name="ACS">{{StoryCite|story=A Coruscant Solstice|book=Life Day Treasury}}</ref> In around [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/life-day-treasury-exclusive-reveal|text=Celebrate the ''Star Wars'' Galaxy's Greatest Holiday with ''Life Day Treasury'' - Exclusive Reveal}} states that "A Coruscant Solstice" takes place during the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' project, which {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-high-republic|text=Lucasfilm to Launch ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Publishing Campaign in 2021}} places 200 [[Standard year|years]] before [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places in [[32 BBY]]. Therefore, "A Coruscant Solstice" takes place around the same time.</ref> a pair of [[Youngling|children]] rushed through [[Republic Avenue]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] on [[Solstice Tide]], not looking where they were going because their minds were focused on what toys they hoped to find at the foot of their beds the next [[Standard day|morning]], which included stuffed chiilaks and model [[starship]]s.<ref name="ACS" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], chiilaks were first mentioned in "[[A Coruscant Solstice]]," a short story written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in the [[2021]] ''[[Life Day Treasury]]'' anthology.<ref name="ACS" /> The [[Chiilak/Legends|creatures]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were created for ''[[Creatures of the Galaxy]]'', a [[1994]] supplement to ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="CotG">''[[Creatures of the Galaxy]]''</ref>
