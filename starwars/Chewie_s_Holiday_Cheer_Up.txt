"'''Chewie's Holiday Cheer Up'''" is a [[Canon|non-canon]] animated [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short released on [[December 12]], [[2023]] as part of the ''[[LEGO STAR WARS: Celebrate the Season]]'' series.<ref name="Chewie's Holiday" />

==Official description==
''Noticing that Chewbacca is disappointed to not celebrate Life Day with his family, Han rallies the rebels at Yavin base to surprise Chewbacca with a Life Day celebration of their own.''<ref name="Chewie's Holiday" />

==Plot summary==
On [[Life Day]], a sad [[Chewbacca]] reflects on a [[hologram]] image of his family including his father [[Attichitcuk]], wife [[Mallatobuck]], and son [[Lumpawaroo]]. He places his [[Life Day Orb]] on the cabinet. [[Han Solo]] notices that the [[Wookiee]] is sad and gathers his fellow rebels [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[Luke Skywalker]], [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] at the rebel command room at the [[Great Temple]] on [[Yavin 4]]. Solo convinces his friends to help him cheer up Chewbacca.

Putting their plans into actions, they and the other rebels gather outside a large tree in the jungles of Yavin 4. C-3PO and R2-D2 also borrow several electrical lanterns. Princess Leia briefs several rebels about decorations before they depart. In the cafeteria, Solo and a [[rebel trooper]] make plans for food. 

Later, Chewbacca finds the rebel base deserted. Departing the Great Temple, he finds a path of electrical lamps which leads him to a forest clearing. There, his rebel comrades have thrown a surprise Life Day celebration by hanging several Life Day Orbs. Chewbacca is amazed and grateful that his rebel comrades care for him. After hanging his Life Day orb, he roars joyfully.
