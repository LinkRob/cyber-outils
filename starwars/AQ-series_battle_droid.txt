'''AQ-series battle droids''', more commonly known as '''aqua droids''', were an amphibious model of [[battle droid]] and [[droid tank]] manufactured by [[Haor Chall Engineering Corporation]]. They were excellent swimmers, posessed a retractable [[laser cannon]] on their right wrist, and were used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] to lay siege to [[Water|aquatic]] [[planet]]s such as [[Kamino]] and [[Mon Cala]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Aqua Droid" /> The heavy aqua droid variant existed which had an integrated [[missile launcher]] on the right arm, similar to the [[B2-HA super battle droid]].<ref name="SWQ136">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/scene-to-stats-aqua-droids|text=Scene to Stats: Aqua Droids}}</ref>

==Description==
{{Quote|Hey, it looks like it's not programmed to avoid hitting bystanders!|[[Rose Tico]], on [[RK-9|Arkay-Nine]]|<ref name="Allegiance 3">[[Allegiance 3|''Allegiance'' 3]]</ref>}}
[[File:AquaDroid-TCWs4BR1.png|thumb|left|300px|An aqua droid in swimming mode]]
Standing at 2.83 [[meter]]s tall<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> and classified as a [[droid tank]],<ref name="USWNE" /> the AQ-series battle droid manufactured by [[Haor Chall Engineering Corporation]]<ref name="Join the Battle" /> and the [[Techno Union]]<ref name="Robotics R2 44">{{BuildR2Cite|44|Understanding Robotics}}</ref> had a bulkier physique when compared to the standard [[B1-series battle droid|B1 model]], boasting a massive chest with ridged shoulders, elongated legs, a polygon shaped head which could be retracted into the droid's body to reduce drag while swimming, and a set of feet that could switch between a standard walking stance on land or fold into propellers underwater. The droids were also given a blue color scheme to match the waters they were designed to operate in.<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

Made with amphibious deployments in mind,<ref name="Robotics R2 44" /> the aqua droid was created by the Techno Union, which made the droid by adapting the [[B2-series super battle droid]] for underwater warfare. Underwater, the aqua droids were more streamlined and agile than their B2 model cousins were on land,<ref name="Join the Battle" />  also using less metal during production.<ref name="Official Magazine 118">{{GermanFanCite|118|Database}}</ref> However, due to their programming, they were no less dangerous or aggressive.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Despite being incredibly mobile underwater, the droids marched in a slow lumbering pattern similar to super battle droids while operating on land, which made them targets for enemy snipers. To defend itself, some aqua droids were equipped with a single retractable laser cannon positioned on their right wrist and could also use their bulky arms to execute sufficient damage against enemy vehicles, such as [[KE-UW33|Kamino submarines]], underwater.<ref name="ARC Troopers" /> Other more heavily armored models were equipped with [[grenade launcher]]s or [[missile launcher]]s.<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />

==History==
===Battle of Kamino===
{{Quote|Aqua droids! It looks as though they're assembling assault craft.|Obi-Wan Kenobi, upon discovering a contingent of aqua droids on Kamino|<ref name="ARC Troopers" />}}
Aqua droids were deployed by the Confederacy of Independent Systems' [[Separatist Droid Army|droid army]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Aqua droids were seen during their attempted [[Battle of Kamino|invasion]] of the oceanic world of Kamino in [[21 BBY]], where several units were secretly deployed to the planet under the command of [[Supreme Commander]] [[Asajj Ventress]]. When a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Confederate Fleet]] led by [[General]] [[Grievous]] arrived in [[orbit]] and began engaging the [[Galactic Republic|Republic's]] [[Kamino blockade|defense fleet]], debris from the Separatist support ships fell from orbit and crashed into the ocean. While the Republic thought little of the debris, in secret they were carrying [[Trident-class assault ship|assault ships]] which Ventress' aqua droids began assembling. During this process, the droid's progress was discovered by [[Jedi]] General [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] who had grown suspicious of Grievous' willingness to sacrifice his support ships and traveled underwater in a submarine to investigate. Upon discovering Ventress' aqua droids, the Jedi's craft became swarmed as the aqua droids launched themselves onto the submarine and began disabling its engine systems. Out of desperation, Kenobi abandoned his craft and escaped the aqua droids by hitching a riding on a nearby [[aiwha]].<ref name="ARC Troopers" />

[[File:BattleOfKamino-ARCT.png|thumb|right|300px|Aqua droids lay siege to Kamino.]]
Almost immediately after being discovered, the aqua droids launched their assault on the Kaminoan cloning facilities in [[Tipoca City]], deploying assault crafts which drilled through the hull of the cloning facilities at various points and dispensed several aqua droids and B1-series battle droids into the facility. Although the droids managed to quickly spread across Tipoca City, they were soon pushed back by local [[Clone trooper|clone]] forces led by their Jedi Generals. The droid's main assault was intended to act as a diversion to allow Ventress to capture the genetic template of [[Jango Fett]] in order to halt the Republic's production of new clones, but this was ultimately thwarted by Jedi General [[Anakin Skywalker]], forcing Ventress and the rest of the Separatist forces to retreat.<ref name="ARC Troopers" /> While they failed to accomplish their objectives, the assault on Kamino would provide increased moral for the Separatists as it proved that all Republic worlds were vulnerable.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

===Battle of Mon Cala===
Later in [[20 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> aqua droids would be deployed to great affect by [[Riff Tamson]] during the [[Battle of Mon Cala|civil war]] perpetuated on [[Mon Cala]] by the Separatists. After Tamson murdered [[Lee-Char]]'s [[Yos Kolina|father]], Tamson's army of aqua droids were deployed after the [[Quarren]]s renounced their allegiance to the Republic and joined the Separatists. Tamson led his army of aqua droids and Quarrens against the [[Mon Calamari]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and Anakin Skywalker, igniting the war. Soon after, a [[Monnk's company|company of clone troopers]] led by Commander [[Monnk]], alongside [[Jedi Master]] [[Kit Fisto]] and Skywalker's [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] were deployed to reinforce the Republic, forcing the Tamson, the Quarren and the aqua droids to retreat. Tamson and the aqua droids regrouped and were reinforced the Separatists second wave, which was led by the cyborg [[Hydroid Medusa|hydroid medusas]]. Together, the droids forced [[Captain]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]], the Prince and his Jedi escort to retreat to the caves.<ref name="Water War">{{TCW|Water War}}</ref>
[[File:Tamson_WW.png|thumb|300px|left|Tamson lead aqua droids into battle.]]
After Tamson ordered the [[Unidentified Consular-class cruiser (Mon Cala)|Jedi's cruiser]] to be destroyed, multiple aqua droids ambushed them and the Prince amongst the failing debris. Soon after Skywalker's group tore down the building which housed the sonar and tracking devices, Tamson to order his forces to prepare for an attack. One aqua droid reported to Tamson that there were two groups of Jedi: one in the lower city center and two releasing prisoners near the coral base. In response, Tamson sent the reinforcements from [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] to those regions of the battlefield, capturing all the [[Gungan]]s, Amidala, Skywalker, Fisto and most of the Mon Calamari besides Lee-Char.<ref name="Gungan Attack">{{TCW|Gungan Attack}}</ref>

Many aqua droids became guards of the prisoner camps, while other aqua droids searched far and wide, forcing Lee-Char and Tano to remain on the move. Several aqua droids escorted a group of Mon Calamari prisoners into one camp, while Tano and the Prince smuggled themselves into the same camp. Soon after, one aqua droid spotted them while they talked with Ackbar and a few other Mon Calamari. The droids surrounded them, but Lee-Char demanded an audience with their commanders, which was granted. Later, aqua droids escorted the prisoners to Lee-Char's execution, so they could all witness his downfall. However, the Prince and Tano's plan succeeded with the Quarren defecting to aid their Mon Calamari associates. Together with the aid of the Jedi, clones and Gungans, most of Tamson's forces, including the aqua droids were defeated.<ref name="Prisoners">{{TCW|Prisoners}}</ref>

Aqua droids were later mentioned in "[[Neyo's Almanac of Clankers]]," a survival compendium sent out by [[Clone Commander]] [[Neyo]] that listed notable battle droids, in an entry written by Commander [[Monnk]].<ref name="Official Magazine 118" />
