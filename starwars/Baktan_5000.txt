The '''Baktan 5000''' was a race participated in by the [[starship]] [[racer]] [[Marcus Speedstar]].<ref name="PClassic" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="PClassicDate">{{ResistanceDate|1|9}}</ref> the [[Resistance]] [[spy]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Kazuda Xiono]]&mdash;acting as a [[Technician|mechanic]]&mdash;was helping Speedstar work on his [[Racing starfighter|ship]],<ref name="PClassic" /> the ''[[Galaxy's Glory]]'',<ref name="USWNE" /> in preparation for the race known as the [[Platform Classic]]<ref name="PClassic" /> on the [[planet]]<ref name="USWNE" /> [[Castilon]]. While speaking to Speedstar during the work, he mentioned the Baktan 5000 to be one of the racer's accomplishments.<ref name="PClassic" />

==Behind the scenes==
