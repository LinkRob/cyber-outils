'''Brian Drummond''' (born [[August 10]], [[1960s#1969|1969]]) is a [[Wikipedia:Canada|Canadian]] voice actor who provided the voice of [[Bobby]] in ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Yoda Chronicles]]'' and [[Admiral]] [[Conan Antonio Motti|Motti]], [[Garazeb Orrelios|Zeb]] and [[Watto]] in ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Droid Tales]]''.
