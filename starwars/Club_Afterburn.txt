'''''Club Afterburn''''',<ref name="Star Wars 14" /> also known as the '''Afterburner''',<ref name="Star Wars 15" /> was a drinking area located on the [[hangar]] deck of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] ''[[Redemption]]''. The club had a banner with [[Aurebesh]] text spelling "Club Afterburn" on it. Patrons would utilize makeshift seating and tables, usually nearby crates or boxes.<ref name="Star Wars 14" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars (2020) 14|''Star Wars'' (2020) 14]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the [[smuggler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] drank with his friend [[Lobot]] at the club when he was approached by the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] to help [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] recover [[Han Solo]] from [[Crimson Dawn]].<ref name="Star Wars 14" /> Sometime later, the [[rebel pilot]] [[L'ulo L'ampar]] had drinks with [[Kes Dameron]] at the Afterburner.<ref name="Star Wars 15" />
