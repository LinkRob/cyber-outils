'''Ardo Banch''' was a [[human]] [[lieutenant]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] who was [[Tatooine garrison|stationed]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. In [[3 ABY]], Banch greeted the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] when he arrived in search of information about his [[Skywalker family|son]], [[Luke Skywalker]], and directed the [[Sith]] to a terminal containing local records. Vader then traveled to the [[Lars homestead|Lars Homestead]], where Banch reached him in a [[repulsorcraft]] to inform him about transmissions the Imperials had picked up; however, the lieutenant was [[Death|killed]] when the repulsorcraft was fired upon by a [[Unidentified gang (Tatooine)|criminal gang]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Lord Vader! So sorry to disturb, but we've picked up some transmissions&mdash;Aaaaagh!|Ardo Banch's last words|<ref name="Vader 1" />}}
Ardo Banch was a [[human]] who served in the [[Imperial Officer Corps|officer corps]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Vader 1" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Banch held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[lieutenant]] and was stationed with the [[Tatooine garrison|Imperial garrison]] on<ref name="Vader 1" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[Tatooine]] when the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] arrived there with a [[Darth Vader's guard|pair]] of [[death trooper]]s and the [[analysis droid]] [[ZED-6-7]] in search of information about his [[Skywalker family|son]], [[Luke Skywalker]]. The [[Imperial officer]] and a dozen [[stormtrooper]]s greeted the [[Sith Lord]] as he disembarked from his [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]], with Banch introducing themself and offering any assistance they could; however, they were cut off by one of the death troopers. Unable to understand the [[trooper]], Banch again offered assistance and provided the location of a terminal with local records when ZED-6-7 requested one.<ref name="Vader 1" />

[[File:BanchRepulsorcraft-2020DarthVader1.png|thumb|right|300px|Ardo Banch was killed trying to deliver news to Lord Vader on Tatooine.]]
Banch then tried to follow Vader and the [[droid]] to the terminal but was halted by a death trooper and so remained outside. The [[Sith]] and his entourage then departed in the [[shuttle]] for the [[Lars homestead|Lars Homestead]] where Skywalker had grown up, but after the lieutenant's men picked up some transmissions, Banch traveled out on a [[LAVr QH-7 Chariot]] [[repulsorcraft]] to inform Vader. As the lieutenant hailed the Sith from the vehicle, it was hit by fire from a [[Starship|vessel]] belonging to a [[Unidentified gang (Tatooine)|gang]] that aimed to [[Thief|rob]] Vader, [[Death|killing]] Banch. Vader then slew those responsible.<ref name="Vader 1" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Perhaps I could&hellip; ah&hellip; yes, I'll just wait, then&hellip;|Ardo Banch is halted by a Death Trooper|<ref name="Vader 1" />}}
Ardo Banch had light skin and [[Color|brown]] [[hair]]. The lieutenant repeatedly offered aid to Vader despite a lack of response from the Sith, but was uncertain of how to deal with his death troopers and did not understand their [[Death trooper encryption|encrypted speech]].<ref name="Vader 1" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|If&hellip; if there's anything you ''need'', Lord Vader&mdash;''"<br />"''Your terminal? With any local records?''"<br />"''Ah! Second door on the right!''"<br />"''Perfect!|Ardo Banch and ZED-6-7|<ref name="Vader 1" />}}
When greeting Vader, Banch wore a gray [[Imperial officer's uniform]] including a gray [[Imperial kepi|cap]], black [[boot]]s, and a black [[belt]]. The lieutenant's tunic bore an [[Imperial code cylinder]] on each [[breast]] and a [[rank insignia plaque]] with three blue pips above three red. Banch had access to a terminal and a repulsorcraft.<ref name="Vader 1" />
