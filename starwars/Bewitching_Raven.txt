The '''Bewitching Raven''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Rieve]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Sith Apprentice (Rieve)|standard costume]]. It featured feather-like spikes protruding from the shoulders, as well as a [[Bone|skull]] with antlers attached to the top of the hood on her head.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bewitching Raven was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Rieve, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.18.0 during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Raven's Jewel]].<ref name="Hunters" /> While first available during the soft launch, this costume could later be unlocked via the Raven Rieve Pack on the official Nintendo website.<ref name="Nintendo Store">{{WebCite|url=https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/star-wars-hunters-raven-rieve-pack-switch|text=STAR WARS: Hunters™ - Raven Rieve Pack|work=[https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/ My Nintendo Store]|nolive=1|nobackup=1}}</ref> This costume combines elements from multiple [[Endor/Legends|Endorian]] [[Witch/Legends|witches]] such as [[Morag]]<ref name="Cries of the Trees">{{Ewoks|The Cries of the Trees}}</ref> and [[Charal]] from the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] [[Canon|continuity]].<ref name="E:SOE">''[[Star Wars: Ewoks—Shadows of Endor]]''</ref>
