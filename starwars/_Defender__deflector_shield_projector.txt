The '''"Defender" deflector shield projector''' was a device produced by [[Chempat|Chepat]]. Combined with a [[deflector shield generator]], it was an essential component in a [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]]<ref name="SWCT Y-wing" /> and [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]'s<ref name="SWCT X-wing">{{SWCT|set=Blueprints|cardname=X-wing Starfighter}}</ref> [[Deflector shield|shielding system]].<ref name="SWCT Y-wing" />
