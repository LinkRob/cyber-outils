'''Droid starfighters''' were a type of automated [[starfighter]] that were [[droid]]s themselves instead of needing a living [[pilot]] or even a [[Pilot droid|droid]]. They were used prior to and during the [[Clone Wars]]. There were several types of droid starfighters like [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class droid bombers]], [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droid starfighters]], and [[droid tri-fighter]]s.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>
