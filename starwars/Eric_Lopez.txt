'''Eric Lopez''' (born [[April 20]], [[1978]])<ref name="Birthday" /> is an American voice actor. He voiced [[Nazhros Oleg]] and a technician in "[[Breaking Ranks]]," the fourth episode of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. Lopez returned to voice [[Jonner Jin]] in "[[Iron Squadron (episode)|Iron Squadron]]," the eighth episode of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Three|third season]] of ''Star Wars Rebels''.
