"'''Energetic GNK'''" was a [[EG-6 power droid]] who participated in a [[gonk droid beauty pageant]] run by a [[Pageant Host]]<ref name="LEGOTSS" /> on the [[planet]]<ref name="Pasaana">{{Databank|pasaana|Pasaana}}</ref> [[Pasaana]] in<ref name="LEGOTSS" /> [[35 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|IX}}</ref> during the [[Festival of the Ancestors]]. They were excitable, spirited and constantly jumped up and down. The GNK droid was also sometimes anxious: after the pageant ended, they confessed to an individual that the pressure of the pageant "almost fried [their] circuits".<ref name="LEGOTSS" />

They enjoyed racing, not even caring where, exactly, they raced, nor how feasible: while waiting to be called up to the runway, they asked an individual if they had enough time to race against them around the festival, but they immediately changed their mind, offering to instead race around the planet or even [[the galaxy]].<ref name="LEGOTSS" />

They were only required to charge infrequently, and developed a reputation for having an infinite battery life and never conserving power, to the point where the Pageant Host suggested their energy use was irresponsible. Their day job, which they had briefly taken a break from in order to participate in the pageant, was sitting in a power socket all day.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Energetic GNK" appeared in the non-[[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', which was released in [[2022]]. They feature in the side quest "Drop Dead Gonkgeous."
As the player character has to pick the winner, it is possible for "Energetic GNK" to win the pageant.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
