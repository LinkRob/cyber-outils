'''Cian Shee''' was a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Slicer/Legends|slicer]] who was seen in the [[Outlander Club/Legends|Outlander Club]] embracing her criminal partner, the [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Kalyn Farnmir/Legends|Kalyn Farnmir]]. Shee had been hired during Farnmir's pursuit of the [[Zealots of Psusan/Legends|Zealots of Psusan's]] corrupt leader, [[Scri Oscuro]].

At one point, Shee was contacted by members of the Zealots of Psusan and was offered a hefty payout in exchange for selling out her partner. Farnmir was informed of these dealings, and confronted Shee at the [[Outlander Club/Legends|Outlander Club]], but Shee escaped. Later, during the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire's]] reign, Farnmir would [[Death/Legends|kill]] Shee for her betrayal, besting her in single combat on [[Nar Shaddaa/Legends|Nar Shaddaa]].

==Biography==
===Joining with Farnmir===
The [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] Cian Shee was a criminal<ref name="db shee" /> and skilled [[Slicer/Legends|slicer]] taken on as a partner by the [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Kalyn Farnmir/Legends|Kalyn Farnmir]] after the hunter was discharged from the<ref name="db farnmir" /> [[Kuati Security Forces|police force]] of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Kuat/Legends|Kuat]].<ref name="CATCW">''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]''</ref> Together, the pair enjoyed success routing out small-time criminals and corporate thieves.<ref name="db farnmir">{{DB|character|kalynfarnmir|Farnmir, Kalyn}}</ref> Farnmir hired Shee during her pursuit of [[Scri Oscuro]],<ref name="db shee" /> the [[High Priest (Psusan)|High Priest]] of the [[Zealots of Psusan/Legends|Zealots of Psusan]], who was using the church as a front for illicit activities.<ref name="db farnmir" />

===Betraying her partner===
[[File:SineadOConnorInSpace-AotC.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Farnmir and Shee embrace at the Outlander Club]]
Some time after their takedown of Oscuro, Shee was contacted by members of the Zealots of Psusan, and offered a hefty payout if she were to sell out her partner, Farnmir. An informant also told Farnmir what the Zealots were planning, and that Shee was in on the plot. Farnmir then contacted Shee and organized a meeting at the [[Outlander Club/Legends|Outlander Club]], a nightclub located in [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant's]] [[Uscru Entertainment District/Legends|Uscru Entertainment District]]<ref name="db farnmir" />

This meeting took place the same night that [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] were in [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala/Legends|pursuit]] of [[Zam Wesell/Legends|Zam Wesell]], after her attempted [[Assassin/Legends|assassination]] of [[Senator/Legends|Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala/Legends|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="Attack of the Clones">{{Film|II}}</ref> Farnmir arrived at the club first, and interrogated the Zealot of Psusan [[Nyrat Agira/Legends|Nyrat Agira]], who confirmed the High Priest was on Coruscant and Shee had met with him. Farnmir and Shee met, Farnmir acting as if nothing was wrong, embracing her friend.<ref name="db farnmir" />

The two witnessed Kenobi and Skywalker apprehending Wesell, and as the two left the club, Farnmir attempted to pin Shee before Shee could attack her first, but Shee avoided and fled into the Coruscanti night. Shee called off the deal with Oscuro, now aware that Farnmir was expecting an ambush.<ref name="db farnmir" />

===Work under the Empire and death===
Shee was still active at the time of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire's]] [[Declaration of a New Order|formation]],<ref name="db farnmir" /> and also by the time the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] was founded, in [[2 BBY/Legends|2 BBY]].<ref name="TFU" /> Shee took up Imperial contracts as a slicer, while her former companion Farnmir aided the Rebellion. At one point, Shee took a contract to take in Farnmir, and the two met on [[Nar Shaddaa/Legends|Nar Shaddaa]], where Farnmir would best Shee in single combat and kill her former partner.<ref name="db farnmir" />
