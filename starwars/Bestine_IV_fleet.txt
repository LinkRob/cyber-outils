During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the [[Republic Navy]] maintained a '''fleet''' at [[Bestine IV]]. During a [[Mission to Grievous's castle|mission to Vassek 3]] to capture [[General]] [[Grievous]], at the implication of [[Jedi General]] [[Kit Fisto]], [[Clone Commander]] [[Fil]] contacted [[Niner]] to contact the [[fleet]] for additional reinforcements. Niner was [[Death|killed]] subsequently by [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s before he could contact the fleet.<ref name="Lair" />
