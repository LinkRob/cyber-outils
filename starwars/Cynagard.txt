'''Cynagard''' was an overweight Sullustan who ran the best equipment shop in [[Doom City]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].<ref name="CB">{{CasusBelliCite|HS24|Il faut sauver Miss Pickalho}}</ref>

Like many of Doom City's merchants, Cynagard acted as a pawnbroker and a fence for stolen goods, and much of his stock was obtained this way.<ref name="CB" />

Prices could be negotiated by haggling, and if buyers lacked the necessary [[Credits/Legends|credits]], Cynagard might offer a loan, sometimes at punitive rates of interest.<ref name="CB" />

{{char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cynagard is mentioned in ''[[Il faut sauver Miss Pickalho]]'', a French-language scenario for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' which appeared in ''[[Casus Belli]]'' magazine in March 1999. As this material was not approved through the [[Lucas Licensing]] process, it is considered non-canon.

In the original French-language text, Cynagard is described as selling ''le matériel courant'', which roughly translates as "commonly-available equipment".<ref name="CB" />

The implicit purpose of mentioning him in the scenario is to allow players' characters to obtain equipment they might need.
