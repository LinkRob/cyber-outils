'''Alex Segura''' is an author, primarily in the mystery genre. He is known for having written the ''Peter Fernandez'' mystery series. Segura wrote the young-adult novel ''[[Poe Dameron: Free Fall]]'', which was released on [[August 4]], [[2020]].<ref name="Free Fall Release">{{WebCite|url=https://www.polygon.com/2020/1/29/21113799/star-wars-poe-dameron-freefall-release-date-zorii-bliss|text=Exclusive: New Star Wars novel will shed light on Poe Dameron's past|author=Patches, Matt|date=2020-01-29|archivedate=20200129194935}}</ref>
