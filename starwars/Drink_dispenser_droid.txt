'''Drink dispenser droids'''<ref name="Kids">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Droid in Star Wars|video=UQg6aX7lqTA|nolive=1}}</ref> were a repurposed [[bacta]]-administering [[Nanny droid|nurse droid]] designed to dispense drinks.<ref name="SoloGuide" /> The [[service droid]]<ref name="SoloJr" /> consisted of a [[Color|silver]] sphere with a lit container of beverage attached beneath and two arms with the dispenser built in. It had at least four sensors, which could glow blue or green. One such [[droid]] worked at the establishment known as [[the Lodge]] on the [[planet]] [[Vandor]]. In [[10 BBY]], the droid served a blue drink to the [[Gambling|gambler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] as he sat at a table in the Lodge's back room playing [[sabacc]]. Calrissian looked back at the droid and thanked it, which was unusual behavior.<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
A drink dispenser droid first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''. The droid was not named in the film,<ref name="Solo" /> but was identified in the video "Every Droid in Star Wars", which was posted on ''[[StarWarsKids.com]]'' in [[2019]].<ref name="Kids" />
