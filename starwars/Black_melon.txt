{{Quote|They're black melons that grow in the Jundland Wastes. When you crack them open, there's milk inside. It tastes horrible, but it's safe to drink.|Ben Kenobi|<ref name="SW7" />}}
'''Black melons''' were a type of [[melon]]<ref name="SW7" /> and gourd<ref name="Boba Collector">''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett Collector's Edition]]''</ref> that grew in the [[Jundland Wastes]] of the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. They were [[egg]]-shaped with a crackled [[Color|black]] outer shell.<ref name="SW7" /> Inside, they contained either [[water]]<ref name="Stranger in a Strange Land">{{BOBF|Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land}}</ref> produced by the plant<ref name="Boba Collector" /> or a type of [[milk]]. Black melon milk was safe to drink for [[Tusken Raider]]s and [[human]]s, although [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] once commented that it tasted horrible.<ref name="SW7" /> [[Boba Fett]] also said that the taste took some getting used to.<ref name="GS">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref>

Black melons were an important food item in Tusken Raider culture.<ref name="The Marshal">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref> During Kenobi's [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] on Tatooine, he attempted to sell some during a [[Great Drought]] because citizens were [[Death|dying]] due to a lack of water and much of the water they did have was taken as [[water tax]]es for [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]].<ref name="SW7" />

One [[Unidentified Tusken Raider tribe|tribe]] of Tuskens would force captured prisoners to dig for the fruit, which at one point included the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] after he was found exhausted by the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]], and a [[Unidentified Rodian prisoner|Rodian prisoner]]. When Fett had attempted to drink a melon that he found, a [[Unidentified Tusken Raider youngling|Tusken youngling]] refused to let him drink, taking the melon and pouring it to a [[massiff]] instead. Later, they were attacked by a [[sand beast]]. Fett killed the creature, earning the respect of the tribe's [[Tusken Chieftain|chief]], which handed Fett a melon to drink.<ref name="Stranger in a Strange Land" />

After Fett rescued [[Fennec Shand]], he offered her a black melon, saying it would help her recover.<ref name="GS" /> Tuskens felt insulted when [[Cobb Vanth]] refused to drink the milk inside a black melon while camping out with a group of them.<ref name="The Marshal">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref>
