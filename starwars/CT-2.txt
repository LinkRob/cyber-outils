'''CT-2'''<ref name="CT-Number">{{Destiny|set=Convergence (Star Wars: Destiny)|cardname=Squad Tactics|link=https://swdestinydb.com/card/09131}} depicts this [[clone cadet]] with the number "2" written in [[aurebesh]] on their left [[Limb|leg]]. The [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|season three]] [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]] episode "[[Clone Cadets]]" also depicts [[Cloning|clone]] cadets with numbers on their left legs, which the second image in the episode's [https://www.starwars.com/series/clone-wars/clone-cadets-trivia-gallery trivia gallery] establishes these numbers to be their CT designation. Therefore, this clone's full designation must be CT-2.</ref> was a [[clone cadet]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. They trained in a [[squad]] with four other [[Cloning|clones]] and wore [[Clone training armor|training armor]].<ref name="SquadTactics" /> CT-2 was created on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] from the [[clone template]] of [[Jango Fett]], and as such they stood at 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|ft]]) tall<ref name="CloneTroopers" /> and had [[Color|tan]] skin.<ref name="SquadTactics" /> CT-2 also had a weight of 80 [[kilogram]]s.<ref name="Weight" />

==Behind the scenes==
CT-2, along with their designation, were first depicted on the "Squad Tactics" card from the [[Convergence (Star Wars: Destiny)|''Convergence'']] set for the card and dice game [[Star Wars: Destiny|''Star Wars'': Destiny]].<ref name="SquadTactics" /> The set released on [[March 28]], [[2019]],<ref name="Date">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/3/28/available-now-march-28|text=Available Now — March 28}}</ref> with the card being illustrated by [[Nasrul Hakim]].<ref name="SquadTactics" />
