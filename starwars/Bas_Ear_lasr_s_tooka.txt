A three-[[Limb|legged]] '''tooka''' was [[Diet|fed]] by the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] [[Bas Ear'lasr]] on the Nihil [[flagship]] ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' during the [[High Republic Era]]. In [[228 BBY]], as [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] investigated a calcifying [[Blight|plague]] at a [[Norisyn crystal farm|crystal farm]] on the [[Norisyn's fifth moon|fifth moon]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Norisyn]], he requested that his [[secretary]] [[Thaya Ferr]] have the [[tooka]] brought to him. Shortly afterward, a [[Unidentified human Nihil (tooka)|human Nihil]] arrived with the tooka and handed it to Ro, who dropped the [[cat]] onto [[crystal]]s that had been infected by the plague. While the tooka briefly survived, it was ultimately consumed by the plague and [[Death|died]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Where is Di'ir?|Thaya Ferr, to Marchion Ro|<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />}}
A [[tooka]] [[cat]] lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. The tooka was [[Diet|fed]] by [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] [[Bas Ear'lasr]], who was stationed aboard [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]]'s [[flagship]], the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', within one of its [[hangar]]s. Eventually, this came to Ro's attention.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Ro and the ''Gaze Electric'' traveled to the [[Norisyn's fifth moon|fifth moon]] of<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Norisyn system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Norisyn]] in the Nihil's [[Occlusion Zone]] region of [[Realspace|space]], where the Eye personally investigated a [[Blight|plague]] that had begun to calcify a [[Norisyn crystal farm|crystal farm]]. Sometime later, Ro contacted his [[secretary]], [[Thaya Ferr]], and asked her to send someone to bring the tooka, as well as several other items, to him at the [[farm]]. Ferr complied with the request and sent a [[Unidentified human Nihil (tooka)|human Nihil]] with the tooka in a bag and items aboard a [[Unidentified Nihil shuttle|shuttle]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

[[File:MarchionRo-THRSOS4.png|thumb|right|300px|Marchion Ro (pictured) requested that the tooka be brought to him]]
Not long later, the [[shuttle]] reached the Eye's [[Unidentified barge|barge]], and the [[human]] made his way to Ro with the bag containing the cat over his shoulder. As the Nihil walked to him, the Eye heard the tooka squeak and hiss while also noticing it use its [[claw]]s to pierce the bag. Ro then grabbed the bag and dumped the cat over the [[Starship|barge's]] rail into the plagued [[crystal]] farm. The tooka landed on its three [[Limb|legs]], though it briefly struggled to find its footing on a crystal, eventually managing to stabilize itself before resting on the [[mineral]] matrix. The cat then began to carefully navigate its way along the calcified crystal farm as its [[ear]]s directed the tooka toward the only sound nearby: the humming of the barge's [[engine]]s.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

Ro then pushed the human over the rail and watched, ignoring the tooka's shrieks and the Nihil's yells and complaints. The Eye witnessed how the cat was consumed by the plague and [[Death|died]]. After the tooka's passing, Ro [[Brain|mentally]] noted that while its death had not been quick, it had not been the slow, drawn out demise that the farm's [[false kyber]] crystals had suffered. When the next [[Local day|nighttime]] reached the [[moon]], the human died after his [[lung]]s were turned to dust by the plague. After Ro returned to the ''Gaze Electric'', Ferr asked him where Di'ir was, which caused the Eye to wonder if the name referred to the tooka or the Nihil he had shoved over the barge's rail. Ro did not respond, and the secretary came to her own conclusion on where Di'ir was.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

==Personality and traits==
The small tooka had [[Color|purple]] stripes, tall ears, and tiny claws. When Marchion Ro dropped the three-legged cat over his barge's rail, the tooka yowled in distress but was able to secure its footing after briefly struggling. While the cat made its way through the farm, it did so cautiously.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
