'''Cup of Gold''' was a type of [[salad]] [[Foodstuff|dish]] made from a [[plant]] with [[Demiran corn|golden kernals]] native to the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] of [[Demir]]. After a tour of [[the galaxy]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included the recipe for salad in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> which was published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cup of Gold was created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the dish makes use of [[Wikipedia:Butter|butter]], [[Wikipedia:Maize|corn]], [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic]], [[Wikipedia:Kosher salt|kosher salt]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]], [[Wikipedia:Mayonnaise|mayonnaise]], [[Wikipedia:Cotija cheese|cotija cheese]], [[Wikipedia:Cilantro|cilantro]], [[Wikipedia:Crema (dairy product)|crema]], [[Wikipedia:Chili powder|chili powder]] and [[Wikipedia:Lime|lime]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
