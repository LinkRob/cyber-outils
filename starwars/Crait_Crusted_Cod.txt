{{Quote|By encasin' a whole fish in a thick crust of salt and bakin' it to perfection, you get not only a moist and tender meal but also a showstoppin' presentation that is ideal for any holiday table.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Life Day Cookbook''|<ref name="LDCookbook" />}}
'''Crait Crusted Cod''' was a [[Foodstuff|dish]] consisting of a [[cod]] and sliced [[Color|yellow]] [[fruit]], both fully encased in white [[salt]] from the [[planet]] [[Crait]] and then baked until the white [[fish]] meat was browned. The salt was then shattered, and a green garnish was added. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] created the dish in order to showcase the salt from Crait, which he used in just about every dish he cooked.<ref name="LDCookbook" />

At some point after Tuggs published his first [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]]<ref name="LDCookbook" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> the chef included a recipe for Crait Crusted Cod in the main courses section of his second cookbook, ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]''. He described the dish as moist and tender if baked to perfection and claimed its show-stopping presentation made it ideal for any holiday table.<ref name="LDCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crait Crusted Cod was created as a recipe for the [[2021]] ''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe uses [[Wikipedia:Kosher salt|kosher salt]], lightly beaten [[Wikipedia:Egg white|egg white]], [[Wikipedia:Water|water]], sliced [[Wikipedia:Lemon|lemons]], [[Wikipedia:Cod|cod]] fillets, [[Wikipedia:Olive oil|extra virgin olive oil]], [[Wikipedia:Paprika|paprika]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]], fresh [[Wikipedia:Dill|dill]], and fresh [[Wikipedia:Thyme|thyme]].<ref name="LDCookbook" />
