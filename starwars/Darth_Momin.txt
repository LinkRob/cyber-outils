{{Quote|Empires have risen and fallen since my birth, great ages of the galaxy have passed&hellip;but here I stand.|Darth Momin|<ref name="Dark Lord 23">[[Darth Vader (2017) 23|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 23]]</ref>}}
'''Darth Momin''', also known simply as '''Momin''', was a [[humanoid]] [[Gender|male]] [[Sith Lord]], [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] scholar and [[sculptor]]. At a young age, his artwork led to his [[Prisoner|imprisonment]] by those who found it disturbing and repulsive for the very materials he had employed. The [[Lord|Sith Lady]] [[Darth Shaa]] freed him, claimed Momin as her [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], and trained him in the dark side of [[the Force]]. However, feeling he was second to none and that calling oneself "Master" was anathema to the dark side, Momin killed Shaa and began exploring its lore on his own. He eventually designed weapons like the [[Fermata Cage]] and a [[Darth Momin's superweapon|superweapon]] capable of destroying an entire city, but his deepest desire was to freeze people in time during their moment of terror. As the [[Jedi]] intervened during Momin's [[Darth Momin's destruction of a city|attempt]], he lost control of the energy he wielded and his physical body was destroyed, leaving only his [[Mask of Lord Momin|mask]] with his consciousness inside.

Many [[Standard year|years]] later, [[Darth Sidious]] recovered Momin's [[Helmet|mask]] from the [[Jedi Archives|Jedi Archive]] [[Jedi Archive vault|vault]]. After learning of Momin's story from the mask, Sidious gave it to his Sith apprentice [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] as a gift for his journey to [[Mustafar]], which Vader sought to make his personal stronghold. There, the mask possessed [[Roggo|one of the officers]] and began designing a fortress. After Vader killed the host body and examined the mask, it told him of Momin's past. Vader then allowed it to possess a [[Sssp|Mustafarian]] and grant Momin's consciousness a new physical form. Vader discussed the fortress Momin had designed, and soon permitted its construction.

Momin, with the help of Imperial resources supplied by Vader and a succession of host bodies, began designing a [[Fortress Vader|fortress]] that could tune the energies of the dark side locus on Mustafar and open the door to the dark side of the Force. While Momin's fortresses were repeatedly destroyed by the harsh natural conditions on the planet, the ninth design was successful. Shortly after the fortress was finished, whilst Vader was distracted by an [[Battle of Fortress Vader|invasion]] of Mustafarians, Momin undermined him and opened the door to the dark side. He used it to resurrect his physical body. Vader soon returned to the fortress and used it to obliterate the Mustafarian attackers. Momin then confronted him and, after a short [[Lightsaber duel|duel]], Vader used the Force to ram him with a stone slab. Momin was crushed against the wall and died.

Years later, Momin's mask was on Darth Sidious's personal [[yacht]], the ''[[Imperialis]]''. During the ship's [[Theft of the Imperialis|infiltration]], the mask corrupted two infiltrators, forcing the others to abandon and destroy the ''Imperialis''. Momin's death at the hands of Vader created a Force nexus in the cave, thus creating visions that could haunt people. Sometime in between [[3 ABY|3]] and [[4 ABY]], the [[Knights of Ren]] [[Heist on Vader's Castle|encountered]] a vision of Momin at Fortress Vader.

==Biography==
===Becoming a Sith===
{{Quote|I, Momin, was a sculptor of the mind. They locked me away so they would not have to see it.|Darth Momin, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />}}
[[File:Momin-2017DarthVader22.png|thumb|left|250px|Momin was imprisoned for his creations.]]
Early into his life, Momin experimented with [[the Force]] to create. One [[Standard day|day]], he mutilated his family's pet [[rada-cat]], [[Flitta]], whom his [[Momin's father|father]] had convinced his [[Momin's mother|mother]] to get, and made a sculpture out of its flesh, [[bone]]s and organs. When Momin's [[Parent|mother]] searched around for it, thinking Flitta had run away again, she walked in on Momin's sculpture, horrified at her son's new creation. Around that point in his life, Momin came to believe that if an individual did not feel anything when seeing his creations, then he had created nothing. This principle stuck with him as he sculpted more of his own creations using animal skulls and other materials that were obscene to the normal individual. When he showed his work in front of others, they scowled and looked with abject horror at it. Subsequently, they had Momin arrested, confiscating his tools and locking him away.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

Although he was [[Prison|imprisoned]], Momin's name spread throughout [[the galaxy]], and, eventually, the [[Sith Lord|Sith Lady]] [[Darth Shaa]] came and freed him. She made him her [[Sith apprentice]] and taught him about the [[dark side of the Force]]. Momin received a pair of [[Darth Momin's lightsabers|lightsabers]] which he used to sculpt his own [[Mask of Lord Momin|mask]], before disfiguring his face to wear it.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> Under the [[Sith]] tutelage, Momin adopted the [[Darth]] title.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> During his training, many things that Momin had known about himself but had never fully understood finally became clear to him. However, he found the role of apprentice distasteful, feeling he was second to none. Shaa disagreed with him, so he proved his point to her by [[Death|killing]] her during a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]]<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> in between 1100 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[1000 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" />

===A masterpiece gone wrong===
{{Quote|With resources inherited from Lady Shaa and the help of acolytes desperate to aid me in my work, I designed a great ''engine''. It was a weapon, of course, powerful enough to burn the city to ash.|Darth Momin, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />}}
[[File:MominActivatesWeapon-2017DarthVader22.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Momin activates his weapon.]]
Now without a master, Darth Momin aspired to learn about the dark side and visited many great sites. He never took an apprentice of his own, finding the idea of calling oneself a "Master" offensive, and, instead, became enamored with the idea of creating a work of art that would satisfy the dark side of the Force itself. To that end, he continued his journey and sought the knowledge of places strong in the dark side, as well as [[Sith holocron]]s.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> A project Momin created was the [[Fermata Cage]].<ref name="Hidden Empire 2">[[Hidden Empire 2|''Hidden Empire'' 2]]</ref> He also reached out to the Force for guidance in what he should create, and it told him to choose a [[city]].<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

With the guidance given to him by the Force, Momin embarked upon his newest project. Using resources he had inherited from Shaa, and the assistance of [[Darth Momin's acolytes|acolytes]] who were desperate to help him in his work, he designed [[Darth Momin's superweapon|his own weapon]]: an [[engine]] with enough power to obliterate an entire city. To make it worthy of the Force, Momin planned to pour the Force into his engine at the moment of ignition and use it to slow down time, forever trapping the expressions of horror and pain on the city's inhabitants. Momin chose a [[Unidentified city (Momin)|city]] to [[Darth Momin's destruction of a city|attempt]] this on, and brought his weapon before it to enact his plan. However, the [[Jedi]] found him and boarded the weapon as he activated it. The presence of the [[light side of the Force]] disrupted his concentration and made him lose control over the engine. This led to his own apparent destruction<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> in between 1100 BBY and 1000 BBY<ref name="Timelines" /> by disintegrating into nothing, leaving only his mask<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> which contained his spirit.<ref name="Dark Lord 23" />

===Meeting Darth Vader===
{{Quote|You created this design, in your last body. Tell me what it is.''"<br />"''The door to the dark side is locked. This is the key. Your beloved waits for you there, Vader. My fortress will tune the energies of the locus. It is your first step towards using the power of the dark side to pierce the veil of time, between life and death.|Darth Vader and Momin|<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />}}
Momin was considered a heretic by the Sith and his teachings were covered up, with his unique applications of the Force never being replicated. His name was never recorded in either [[Jedi holocron]]s or Sith [[holocron]]s,<ref name="Dark Lord 21">[[Darth Vader (2017) 21|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 21]]</ref> and many [[Standard year|years]] after his death, his works were thought to have been lost.<ref name="Lando 4" /> Momin's mask, with his spirit still inside, was eventually stored in a [[Jedi Archive vault|secret vault]] in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Dark Lord 8">[[Darth Vader (2015) 8|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 8]]</ref> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the Sith Lord [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Hunt for Jocasta Nu|fought]] the former [[Chief Librarian]] [[Jocasta Nu]] in the [[Jedi temple|Temple]]; Vader learned of the vault's existence when Nu fled into it. After killing the Jedi,<ref name="Dark Lord 10">[[Darth Vader (2017) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 10]]</ref> Vader informed his master, [[Darth Sidious]], of the vault and the Sith Lord took the [[helmet]] into his collection of [[Sith artifact]]s.<ref name="Dark Lord 12">[[Darth Vader (2017) 12|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 12]]</ref>

When Sidious later examined the mask, Momin's spirit revealed his past to the [[Emperor]]. Sidious eventually gifted the helmet to Vader, believing that the artifact would guide his apprentice during his studies on [[Mustafar]]. Vader brought with him Sidious' [[Chief Architect]], [[Colonel]] [[Alva Brenne]], and her aide, [[Lieutenant]] [[Roggo]], to design a sanctum for his use. Brenne examined the helmet on their journey to Mustafar, questioning its origin. While she consulted with Vader on Mustafar, the mask came to possess Roggo, and Momin used the [[officer]]'s body to design a [[Fortress Vader|fortress]]. Brenne walked in on this and Momin shot her. Vader was alerted to the commotion and walked in on Momin's completed design, killing the host body.<ref name="Dark Lord 21" />

[[File:VaderMomin-2017DarthVader22.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Momin conversing with Vader through a possessed Mustafarian.]]
Vader took the mask to the [[Sith cave]] on Mustafar and reached into it with the Force. Momin's spirit responded by showing his past to Vader. As he closed his story, he attempted to possess Vader with the mask, but the Sith Lord resisted. When leaving the [[cave]], however, Vader ran into a group of [[Mustafarian]]s. He [[Skirmish on Mustafar|killed]] two of them, but used the mask to possess [[Sssp|another one]], giving Momin a body again. The two returned to the cave and Momin claimed the [[fortress]] he designed could tune the energies of the locus inside the Sith cave and open the door to the dark side. Momin then told Vader he would find his late love, [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], there. Vader did not believe this and [[Force choke]]d him. Momin, though, assured Vader he was not lying and yearned to build the fortress he had designed. Vader allowed this, but assured him he would suffer if he betrayed him.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

===Constructing Fortress Vader===
{{Quote|The Force is denied to me in this form. But with your assistance, we should achieve significant results. My design is strong.|Darth Momin, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 23" />}}
Construction began with Momin's design for Vader's fortress. With the help of Imperial resources sent to Mustafar, the structure was soon finished. However, when Vader attempted to open the door to the dark side, it failed, and the fortress was wrecked. The pair tried again with more designs, with Momin's guidance, but they too failed. As a side effect, Vader's efforts to open the door affected the rest of Mustafar, causing lava storms to wreak havoc on the Mustafarians. [[Lava flea]]s also began regularly attacking the construction site. Following the failure of Momin's fifth design, he concluded that he was using too many focusing spires on the fortress, making the energy of the dark side locus uncontrollable. Vader, though, killed his Mustafarian host as punishment for his failures, having his mask possess a [[stormtrooper]] in its place.<ref name="Dark Lord 23" />

Once Momin was in control of his new host, he told Vader his next fortress design would be successful and ordered the construction workers to clean up the wreckage of his most recent design. After he said that, they were interrupted by a lava flea attack. When Momin asked a magma trooper why the attack was occurring, the trooper claimed it was one of many that were happening daily, speculating that the construction site may be a breeding ground for the [[insectoid]] creatures. The trooper joined the fight, and Momin expressed his fascination at how the lava fleas were conspiring to destroy the site and his vision.<ref name="Dark Lord 23" />

===Resurrecting himself===
{{Dialogue|Momin|Hello, me.|Momin's mask|Hello, me.|Momin wearing his mask|I am my masterpiece.|attr=A newly resurrected Momin receives his mask|src=<ref name="Dark Lord 24">[[Darth Vader (2017) 24|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 24]]</ref>}}
Momin designed a sixth fortress which, despite his assurance of its success, also failed, and was wrecked like the others. Vader killed his stormtrooper host and had his helmet possess an [[Imperial officer]]. Momin had confidence in the seventh design, but it too failed, and Vader replaced the host with a lava flea. When Momin's eighth design failed, Vader killed the lava flea and had the mask possess a construction worker.<ref name="Dark Lord 23" /> By [[12 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Momin had a great amount of confidence in his ninth design, which Vader assured would be the last. Vader too felt a great amount of power in the focusing chamber built into the Sith cave and began to channel the Force from inside it. As the door to the dark side called on Vader to open it, Momin told him he may want to wait for the right moment to do so. Vader [[Force push]]ed him aside and concluded that that it was the moment.<ref name="Dark Lord 23" />

[[File:MominResurrection-2017DarthVader23.jpg|thumb|right|280px|Momin resurrects himself]]
As Vader began opening the door to the dark side, [[Captain]] [[Junus]], the [[commander]] of the fortress' [[garrison]], then contacted him about another [[Battle of Fortress Vader|attack]], one the Imperial forces could not thwart on their own. Vader was forced to leave the cave to see this for himself. Momin then double-crossed Vader and began channeling the Force through the fortress himself. As Vader witnessed the attackers outside, he realized what Momin was doing and was struck by a lightning bolt. Momin then opened the door to the dark side, where his original body was waiting. After donning the mask and killing his previous host, Momin's physical and spiritual forms were reunited.<ref name="Dark Lord 23" />

===Vader's consequences===
{{Quote|It saddens me to see that the Sith have become such Jedi-obsessed weaklings. It saddens me, and I know it ''disgusts'' the dark side.|Momin insults Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 24" />}}
[[File:LordMominsDeath-2017DarthVader24.png|thumb|left|250px|Momin is killed by Darth Vader]]
Vader fought the attacking army of Mustafarians, but their chieftains were able manipulate the lava to wipe out his army. Vader survived, albeit barely, and returned to the chamber where Momin was waiting. Momin stayed out of sight as the [[cyborg]] controlled the fortress with the Force to obliterate the invading army.<ref name="Dark Lord 24" />

Momin then stepped out, sensing that Vader was exhausted from the brutal ordeal of the battle outside. He accused his pawn of attempting to control the dark side, whereas he should have been seeking to serve it. He attacked Vader and claimed that he was weak, lamenting the apparent regression of Sith power in the millennia since his death. He also claimed that Vader would not be able to open the door to the dark side, as the dark side would have rejected him.<ref name="Dark Lord 24" />

As the two Sith Lords dueled, the exhausted Vader was dealt several devastating wounds, and eventually Momin sliced off his sword arm. As Momin gloated over his apparent victory, Vader, unperturbed, asked Momin if he had lied about what opening the portal to the dark side could accomplish. Momin stated that he had not lied, but that Vader was too weak and impure to open such a portal. Satisfied with Momin's confirmation, Vader used the Force to slam a massive stone slab into Momin. Momin attempted to hold off the attack, exclaiming that, because the dark side loved him, it would never allow him to be killed. Vader simply stated that, if that were the case, then he would indeed live. Ultimately, Vader's power overwhelmed his incredulous foe and smashed the Sith Lord flat against the cave wall, fatally crushing Momin's body once again.<ref name="Dark Lord 24" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|Lando, unless I miss my guess, these are the pieces by Lord Momin&mdash;an ancient Sith sculptor. All of his works were thought lost.|Korin Pers, to Lando Calrissian|<ref name="Lando 4" />}}
[[File:MominHelmetActivated-Lando3.png|thumb|right|180px|Although Darth Momin was dead, a dark essence remained in his helmet]]
Immediately after killing Momin, Vader entered through the [[portal]] the Sith Lord had built. Once there, he saw parts of his past life when he went by the name Anakin Skywalker and some of his future. However, Vader was ultimately unable to resurrect Amidala as she refused to come with the Sith Lord in his inability to let go, stating that she no longer knew him as she jumped away from his reach and died. Having failed, Vader left the portal and destroyed Momin's final work<ref name="Dark Lord 25">[[Darth Vader (2017) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 25]]</ref> due to the failure to resurrect his wife.<ref name="Encyclopedia Maul">{{EncyclopediaCite|Darth Maul and Other Followers of the Dark Side}}</ref> Nevertheless, the experience led to Vader fully committing himself to the dark side of the Force.<ref name="Dark Lord 25 />

Though Momin's body was killed in his confrontation with Vader,<ref name="Dark Lord 25" /> some portion of his spirit remained within his helmet,<ref name="Lando 3">[[Lando 3|''Lando'' 3]]</ref> which Vader returned to Sidious.<ref name="Encyclopedia Maul" />

In [[2 BBY]], during the Third Decennial Imperial Jubilee, an event to celebrate [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] ascension to the office of [[Supreme Chancellor]] that would have pieces of art from worlds across [[the galaxy]] for display, including some pieces from the Emperor's personal collection, among which was [[Mask of Lord Momin|Momin's mask]], the [[Stormtrooper Commander]] [[Bil]] found the ancient [[Sith Lord]]'s mask, and having been intrigued by it, put it on. The [[Commander]] was immediately possessed by the spirit of Momin, who was happy to realize that he was at an art exhibition. Momin did not think the Commander's armor was a suitable garment for an artist. Refusing to be seen by the public like this, he took a fabric with the [[Galactic Empire]] emblem on it and used it to improve his appearance. While looking at the works of art, he found one he recognized: a piece created by Momin himself. While reading the information about the piece, Momin learned that the artist who made the piece was unknown. Enraged by this, Momin revealed his identity to everyone present, saying that none of them deserved him or his piece of art. After destroying the sculpture, Momin used [[the Force]] to kill many by throwing sharp pieces of the piece, demanding that they say his name. Receiving an order from [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] to deal with Momin, Commander [[Ellian Zahra]] did as ordered. The [[stormtrooper]]s opened fire on Momin, but the Sith Lord used the Force to deflect the [[blaster]] fire, saying that none of them had vision and that they were clods. When Momin saw Zahra run towards him, he attacked her with sharp pieces of the sculpture, but she managed to avoid being hit and stabbed him with her blade. Being face to face with Zahra, Momin asked if she wasn't a work of art. Zahra didn't respond and just removed the blade from Momin before pushing him away. The fall dislodged the mask, leaving it next to the dead Stormtrooper Commander on the ground.<ref name="Grand Imperial Jubilee" />

A short time after the Battle of [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]], Momin's mask was stored on Sidious' personal [[yacht]], the ''[[Imperialis]]''. It was located in a vault of Sith artifacts, under the protection of two [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guards]]. The guards came to be corrupted by the mask's influence<ref name="Lando 3" /> until [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and his [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian's crew|team]]&mdash;consisting of [[Lobot]], [[Korin Pers]], [[Aleksin]], and [[Pavol]]&mdash;[[Theft of the Imperialis|stole]] the Emperor's [[Starship|ship]]<ref name="Lando 1">[[Lando 1|''Lando'' 1]]</ref> and killed them.<ref name="Lando 3" /> Pers believed the Sith artifacts were created by Momin. While they examined at the artifacts, Momin's mask corrupted Aleksin, and he turned on the rest of the infiltrators. Pavol was soon corrupted as well,<ref name="Lando 4" /> and the others were forced to abandon and destroy the ''Imperialis''.<ref name="Lando 5">[[Lando 5|''Lando'' 5]]</ref>

Sometime after Momin was killed by Vader in Fortress Vader, the death of Momin's body created a Dark Side Force nexus that surrounded the cave, thus creating visions that could haunt visitors.<ref name="CR 4">[[Crimson Reign 4|''Crimson Reign'' 4]]</ref> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Crimson Reign 4 date">The events of [[Crimson Reign 4|''Crimson Reign'' 4]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> [[Ren (human)|Ren]] and the [[Knights of Ren]] were assigned by [[Crimson Dawn]] leader [[Qi'ra]] to [[Heist on Vader's Castle|steal]] the [[Screaming Key]] from Fortress Vader, upon arriving at the cave located below the castle, the Force nexus created a vision in which Momin's severed head was lying on the ground, and the eyes of the mask started to glow; terrified, Knight of Ren [[Bazzra]] shot at the illusion, causing the vision to disintegrate to ash. Ren told Bazzra not to be concerned about the things that were dead, but about the things that were alive.<ref name="CR 4" />

In the [[New Republic Era|years following]] the fall of the Galactic Empire, Vader's son, the [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]], traveled the galaxy to rediscover the history of the Jedi Order that had been suppressed by the Empire.<ref name="LJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Shortly after the [[Raid on Nightside]], Luke Skywalker explained to Lando Calrissian the possibility of the Sith [[Darth Noctyss]] influencing a female individual via the lightsaber that the Sith had once owned. This led to Calrissian recounting his own encounter with the helmet of Momin during his attempted theft of the Imperialis.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref> During his self-imposed exile on [[Ahch-To]], Skywalker wrote a book he titled ''[[The Secrets of the Jedi]]'', which chronicled the information he had learned during his travels. In the book, Skywalker described Momin's involvement in the construction of Fortress Vader, taking note of how the unique shape of the structure was based on designs by the ancient Sith Lord.<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|The dark side does not serve us. We serve the dark side. If we glorify it through our acts and our work and our art, it gives us power. It gives us life. Even life eternal.|Momin, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />}}
From a young age, Momin came to believe in the simple principle that only two emotions were truly pure: pain and fear. As the emotions that were felt by any being from the moment they were born, he believed that experiencing these emotions was the only way to reconnect with the animal that exists within everyone. Momin's principle was to create work that would elicit these emotions in others. He believed that if an individual did not feel anything when encountering a creation, then its creator had made nothing. He did not realize his true potential until Lady Shaa began training him. Following her death, Momin became very independent and sought out the lore of the dark side for himself. He came to realize that normal people did not understand his creations, having locked him away when he was younger, and only the Force was his audience. In order to be worthy of it, he cultivated an idea that involved freezing a city of innocents in their expressions of horror and pain.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

[[File:ShaasDeath-2017DarthVader22.png|thumb|left|250px|Momin disliked his status as apprentice and killed Shaa]]
Momin was different from most other Sith in that, rather than pursuing power through destruction, he preferred to create and learn through intellectual and artistic pursuits.<ref name="Dark Lord 21" /> Momin insisted on receveing recognition for his works. When a [[Stormtrooper Commander]] donned his mask and became possessed by the spirit of the ancient [[Sith Lord]], Momin was enraged when he found one of his artworks in an exhibition and learn that the artist responsible was unknown. This made him reveal his identity and claim that no one present deserved him or his artwork. After destroying the piece, he used sharp pieces of it to [[Death|murder]] many while demanding that they say his name.<ref name="Grand Imperial Jubilee" /> He eventually believed that if he served and glorified the dark side, it would give him eternal life.<ref name="Dark Lord 24" /> His views of the Force were viewed as so extreme that neither Jedi nor Sith holocrons mentioned him.<ref name="Dark Lord 21" />

Momin, like all Sith, was deceitful and power hungry. He considered himself second to no one but the Force and killed his master, Shaa, during a duel. When Momin first conversed with Vader, he showed himself to be eager to build a design he created.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> To Vader's disillusionment, Momin's true goal was to resurrect himself by opening the door to the dark side. When the door was able to be opened, Momin took his chance and reunited his helmet with his original body. However, Vader later returned and killed Momin in a duel.<ref name="Dark Lord 23" />

Momin was a male individual with [[Color|gray]] skin and hazel [[eye]]s. He had long, blond [[hair]] which spiked out more as he got older. He disfigured his face during his training with Shaa, hiding this with his mask.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

==Power and abilities==
{{Quote|I learned much from her, about the Force, and its dark side.|Momin, on his training with Shaa|<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />}}
[[File:MominAndShaa-2017DarthVader22.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Momin trains under Shaa]]
Although Momin was a sculptor, his creations were made in ways that others looked at with horror. As a result, he was locked away because of it.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> Momin created unconventional uses for the Force that were never again replicated. His expertise in creating outweighed that of Alva Brenne's when she attempted to satisfy the needs of Darth Vader for his castle on Mustafar.<ref name="Dark Lord 21" /> Momin's skill in combat was learned from Shaa. She trained him in the ways of the dark side and trained him in the art of [[lightsaber]] [[Lightsaber combat|dueling]].<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> He eventually killed his master and, after resurrecting himself, dueled with Darth Vader.<ref name="Dark Lord 24" />

After killing Shaa, Momin began learning more for himself and discovered a great deal about the lore of the dark side. Momin planned to pour the Force with his version of [[Force lightning]] into his engine at the moment of ignition and use it to slow down time, forever trapping the expressions of horror and pain on the city's inhabitants forever. Following Momin's death, his spirit became trapped inside his mask and could possess other individuals. When forcing a vision upon Darth Vader, he almost caused the Sith Lord to put his mask on.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

He possessed considerable skill in [[Telekinesis]], able to lift a large sculpture and hurl parts of its base like spears. He was also able to [[Force deflection|deflect]] blaster bolts with the Force.<ref name="Grand Imperial Jubilee" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|The Mask of Lord ''Momin''. A powerful artifact of the dark side.|Darth Sidious, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 21" />}}
During his [[time]] as a Sith, Momin wielded a pair of red Sith lightsabers resembling rapiers. Using these lightsabers, he sculpted his own helmet, which he wore frequently for as long as he lived and beyond.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Wrote Darth Vader #22 today - FORTRESS VADER Part 4, or 'The Tale of Lord Momin.' I really like it. I've had this story in mind since my Lando book (with @alexmaleev & @paulmounts) in 2015. Wasn't sure I'd ever get a chance to tell it.|Charles Soule|<ref name="Soule's Tweet 1">{{Twitter|CharlesSoule|status/1016139077635231745|[[Charles Soule]]|quote=Wrote Darth Vader #22 today - FORTRESS VADER Part 4, or "The Tale of Lord Momin." I really like it. I've had this story in mind since my Lando book (with @alexmaleev & @paulmounts) in 2015. Wasn't sure I'd ever get a chance to tell it.|archivefile=File:Momin-Charles-Soule-tweet.png}}</ref>}}
Momin was created by writer [[Charles Soule]] for the [[Lando 4|fourth issue]] of the [[2015]] [[Star Wars: Lando (comic series)|''Star Wars: Lando'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series, in which he was first mentioned.<ref name="Lando 4" /> Since then, Soule had a further story in mind for Momin, which he ultimately told in the [[2017]] [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2017)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']] series.<ref name="Soule's Tweet 1" /> Momin later appeared through flashbacks in [[Darth Vader (2017) 22|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 22]], written by Soule and illustrated by [[Giuseppe Camuncoli]], in [[2018]].<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> After hearing numerous pronunciations of Momin's name, Soule eventually announced on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] that it was pronounced "MOH-min" {{Pronounced|ˈmoʊˌmɪn}}.<ref name="Pronunciation">{{Twitter|CharlesSoule|status/1080553278323212289|[[Charles Soule]]|quote=For those wondering, Lord Momin's name is pronounced MOH-min. (I've heard Mau-min, Mo-Meen, Meow-min, you name it. Nope. MOH-min.|archivefile=File:Momin-pronunciation.png}}</ref> He was first identified as "Darth" in the index of the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

When Momin's mask returned under Vader's castle in the [[Crimson Reign 4|fourth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Crimson Reign]]'' miniseries, some fans raised questions on how Momin's spirit could still be inhabiting that area if the ''Lando'' series established it had been moved to the ''Imperialis''. While [[Matt Martin]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] believed it to indeed be a mistake and recalled the script calling for a skeleton to appear,<ref name="Martin Crimson Reign tweet">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1519830485022560256|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=I'd have to look to be certain but I do think that one was an error. I believe the script just called for it to be the skeleton.|archivedate=20220530221149}}</ref> Soule, the writer of the comic, confirmed it was not a mistake by explaining it was merely a ghost appearing before the Knights of Ren,<ref name="Soule Crimson Reign tweet 1">{{Twitter|CharlesSoule|status/1519315214163922946|[[Charles Soule]]|quote=It's a ghost.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/84hPd}}</ref> just as Ren implied in the issue.<ref name="CR 4" /> Maintaining the mystery, he refused to explain how the mysterious projection could seem as though it reacted to blaster shots, merely replying with "Yes" to confirm it was a ghost.<ref name="Soule Crimson Reign tweet 2">{{Twitter|CharlesSoule|status/1519319301496397824|[[Charles Soule]]|quote=Yes.|archivedate=20220530220953}}</ref>
