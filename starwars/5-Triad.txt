'''5-Triad''' was a [[security protocol droid]] with [[Gender|masculine programming]] who worked at a [[Prison|detention center]] in [[Lonisa City]] on [[Valo]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. When [[Ty Yorrick]], [[Mantessa Chekkat]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] were all locked up in the facility at the beginning of the [[Republic Fair]], he served as their jailer, disbelieving Jomaram's protests regarding his identity.<ref name="RtCT" />
