"'''Extinction'''" is a two-part comic, totaling 32 pages, written by [[Ron Marz]]. It was featured in [[Star Wars Tales 1|''Star Wars Tales'' 1]] and [[Star Wars Tales 2|2]] and collected in ''[[Star Wars Tales Volume 1]]''. The art was by [[Claudio Castellini]], letters by [[Michael Taylor]], and colors by [[Guy Major]], and the editor was [[Peet Janes]]. It takes place shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="tnegtc" /> It is also notably the first use of [[Plant Surge]].

==Plot summary==
[[File:Vader-SWT1Extinction.jpg|thumb|left|180px|Darth Vader arrives on Coruscant.]]

[[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] arrives on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] and enters the [[Palpatine/Legends|Emperor's]] chambers. Palpatine tells his apprentice that [[Mara Jade Skywalker|Mara Jade]] has found another [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]]. Mara explains that a man trying to escape [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] custody on [[Kuat/Legends|Kuat]] gave her information about a hidden Jedi known only as the [[The Dark Woman|Dark Woman]], who has been hiding on the [[Cophrigin V|fifth planet]] in the [[Cophrigin system/Legends|Cophrigin system]]. Vader and Mara both demand to be the one to kill her. Palpatine declares that Vader shall carry out the mission.

Vader lands on Cophrigin's fifth planet in his [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle/Legends|''Lambda''-class shuttle]] and seeks out the Dark Woman. He engages her in a vicious [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] duel, lasting several minutes. In the end, Vader knocks a tree down with [[The Force/Legends|the Force]], landing on top of the Dark Woman, incapacitating her. Vader goes over to her upper body, sticking out from beneath the tree, and stabs his lightsaber down, but the Dark Woman disappears, in much the same way [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] will a year later. She appears as a [[Force ghost/Legends|Force ghost]] and tells Vader to let go of the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side]] and return to being [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]]. Vader refuses to do so, slashing his lightsaber through the Dark Woman's transparent form. She disappears, and Vader slowly walks back to his shuttle, successful in his mission.

==Continuity==
The dialogue was altered between the two-part version found in ''Star Wars Tales 1'' and ''Star Wars Tales 2'' and the version in ''Star Wars Tales Volume 1'', which combined the two parts. The changed dialogue is found when the Dark Woman's Force ghost appears before Vader.

In the original version, her dialogue reads:
{{Quote|Anakin&hellip;I see within you the power to release yourself from the dark side.''"<br />"''NO! I seek no release! Anakin no longer exists!|Original dialogue between An'ya Kuro's ghost and Vader|<ref name="Extinction">{{Tales|issue1=1|issue2=2|Extinction}}</ref>}}
In the altered version, her dialogue reads:
{{Quote|Do you now see? Your might is nothing compared to mine. Remember, Skywalker, even a faint light shines brightest when it is surrounded by darkness.|Altered dialogue of An'ya Kuro's ghost to Darth Vader|<ref name="Extinction" />}}
No one has officially explained why the dialogue was changed. However, it bears a strong resemblance to the prefaces in the [[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (novelization)|Episode III novel]].
