'''Chirrut's staff''' was a [[Fire|flame]]-hardened [[uneti-wood]] [[staff]] used as a [[Cane|walking stick]] and [[Melee weapon|weapon]] by the blind [[monk]] [[Chirrut Îmwe]]. The staff had two handles of metal. One end of the staff was capped with an enclosure for a [[kyber crystal]] sliver, offering a symbolic source of inner illumination and allowing Îmwe to better gauge where the end of the staff was through the sound of the battery and [[crystal]] harmonic.<ref name="ROUVG" /> During the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] occupation of [[Jedha]]'s [[Jedha City|Holy City]], Îmwe used the staff as a weapon against the Empire's [[stormtrooper]]s,<ref name="Rogue One" /> misleading his victims into believing the staff was merely a blind man's walking stick.<ref name="Chirrut's staff DB" /> Îmwe later went on to use the staff in the [[Battle of Scarif]], where he was instrumental in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] successful theft of the [[Death Star plans]].<ref name="Rogue One" />
