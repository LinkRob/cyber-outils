'''Clone jetpack troopers''' or '''clone jet troopers''' were a variant of [[clone trooper]] trained for aerial combat, most commonly through the use of the [[JT-12 jetpack]]. Clone jetpack troopers were present during the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] as well as the [[Defense of Cato Neimoidia|Battle of Cato Neimoidia]] and [[Battle of Anaxes]]. The [[501st Legion]] had jetpack troopers within its ranks, the armor of the troopers was marked in blue to designate their unit affiliation. Following the execution of [[Order 66]], the [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformation]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] into the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] and the phasing out of clones in favor of [[stormtrooper]]s, the jetpack troopers were replaced by [[jumptrooper]]s.

==Description==
Amongst the most skilled [[soldier]]s in [[the galaxy]],<ref name="Black Series">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|packtype=Gaming Greats Figure|pack=#06: Jet Trooper|altlink=https://www.gamestop.com/collectibles/figures/products/hasbro-the-black-series-star-wars-battlefront-ii-jet-trooper-action-figure/298775.html}}</ref> clone jet [[trooper]]s were [[clone trooper]]s trained for aerial maneuvers and combat. They used [[jetpack]]s to scout ahead of [[Cloning|clone]] units and deny enemies the tactical advantage of the high ground. Jet troopers were skilled in military tactics as well as armed and [[unarmed combat]]. They were also proficient in their survival skills as well as [[starfighter]] and [[starship]] piloting.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:Phase1Jumptrooper_BFII.png|thumb|left|200px|Phase I jetpack trooper]]
At least one clone jetpack trooper participated in the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], the first action of the [[Clone Wars]], against the [[Separatist Droid Army]].<ref name="Mace Windu 1" /> Jet troopers from the [[501st Legion]] took part in the [[Battle of Christophsis]]<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> and were present for the [[defense of Cato Neimoidia]], during which a grouping of jetpack troopers traveled with [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] aboard a [[gunship]].<ref name="Sabotage" /> The [[327th Star Corps]] also featured clone jetpack troopers.<ref name="Official Magazine 118">{{GermanFanCite|118|Database}}</ref>

Jetpack troopers from both the 501st and the [[7th Sky Corps]] took part in the [[Battle of Anaxes]].<ref name="Unfinished Business" /> 501st jump troopers were again present at the [[Battle of Yerbana]] to assist the 7th's [[212th Attack Battalion]].<ref name="Old Friends">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref> During the last major Separatist offensive of the Clone Wars, the [[Battle of Coruscant]], vacuum-suited jetpack troopers boarded enemy [[Starship|vessels]] to capture their [[Bridge (starship)|bridges]].<ref name="Front Lines" /> At the same time, 501st troopers equipped with jetpacks helped carry out the [[Siege of Mandalore]] alongside the [[Mandalore resistance]].<ref name="Old Friends" />

==Equipment==
Clone jetpack troopers commonly made use of the [[JT-12 jetpack]].<ref name="Battlefront 2">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> They were armed with the [[BlasTech Industries|BlasTech]] [[DC-15A blaster rifle]], [[DC-15A blaster carbine]],<ref name="Old Friends" /> and [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|DC-17 hand blaster]] as well as the [[Smart Rocket]] [[missile launcher]]s.<ref name="Battlefront 2" /> Clone jetpack troopers assigned to specific [[Grand Army of the Republic]] detachments often donned their [[armor]] with the color-scheme representative of that unit; for example, 501st jetpack troopers wore blue armor.<ref name="Old Friends" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clone jetpack troopers first appeared in "[[Sabotage (episode)|Sabotage]]," the seventeenth episode of [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|season five]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="Sabotage" /> and were identified in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''.<ref name="Front Lines" />
