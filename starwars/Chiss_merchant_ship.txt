The '''Chiss merchant ship''' was a class of [[merchant]] [[starship]] used by the [[Unknown Regions]] [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nation.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> By [[36 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Skirmish in the Kinoss system]] to [[36 BBY]]. As the [[Mission to Bardram Scoft]], in which [[Qilori]] remembers [[Navigator|navigating]] Chiss merchant ships, takes place just before the skirmish, that event must take place in that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the [[Pathfinders (Navigators' Guild)|Pathfinder]] [[navigator]] [[Qilori]] had served as the navigator for a number of Chiss merchant ships.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> In that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> he [[Mission to Bardram Scoft|served]] as the navigator for a [[Chiss]] diplomatic [[cruiser]], and privately remarked that the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] was identical to every Chiss merchant ship he had navigated.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chiss merchant ships were mentioned in flashbacks in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
