{{Quote|We were about to be unloaded, and the Jedi attacked. We took advantage. Rioted. We took the fight to the slavers.|Lourna Dee|<ref name="Tempest Runner" />}}
Around [[255 BBY]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Oppo Rancisis]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Dal Azim]] '''attacked''' a group of [[Zygerrian]] enslavers, freeing a number of [[Slavery|enslaved]] individuals including [[Lourna Dee]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

Dee took advantage of the Jedi attack to incite a rebellion among her fellow enslaved individuals. Master Rancisis later recognized and commended Dee for leading the revolt.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Hello there!''"<br />"''Hi.''"<br />"''I heard that you led the revolt.''"<br />"''I&hellip; I'm not sure you could call it that.''"<br />"''It was very brave of you, but I sense much conflict in you.|Oppo Rancisis and Lourna Dee|<ref name="Tempest Runner" />}}
After the battle concluded, Azim led [[Oppo Rancisis]] to [[Lourna Dee]]. The [[Jedi Master]] congratulated her for leading the revolt.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on the Zygerrian camp appeared in flashbacks in the [[2021]] audio drama ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] and performed by a full voice cast.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />
