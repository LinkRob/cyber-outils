'''Boushh's armor'''<ref name="Secrets of the Bounty Hunters">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref> was a set of [[armor]] owned by the [[Ubese]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Boushh]]. The armor consisted of a thick [[leather]] suit covered by several hard leather plates. Boushh also wore leather [[glove]]s and [[boot]]s as well as a [[helmet]]. The helmet covered his physical appearance and had a [[speech scrambler]] to distorted the sound of his voice, it also allowed him to adapt to almost every type of [[atmosphere]] and was equipped with a laser visor that increased his shooting accuracy.<ref name="Bust Collection 11" />
