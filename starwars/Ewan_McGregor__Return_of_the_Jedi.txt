"'''Ewan McGregor: Return of the Jedi'''" is an article published in [[Star Wars Insider 60|''Star Wars Insider'' 60]]. An interview with [[Ewan McGregor]] about his role as [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in {{Film|II}}, it was written by [[Scott Chernoff]], but a translated version actually premiered several months before in [[Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin 25|''Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin'' 25]].
