'''Cliff Chiang''' is a comic book artist. He created several illustrations for the [[2010]] trading card series ''[[2010 Topps Star Wars Galaxy Series 5]]'',<ref name="Collider">{{WebCite|url=https://collider.com/the-empire-wants-you-new-star-wars-trading-cards-feature-amazing-propaganda-style-artwork-from-cliff-chiang|text=The Empire Wants YOU: New STAR WARS Trading Cards Feature Amazing Propaganda-Style Artwork from Cliff Chiang|author=Goldberg, Matt|date=2010-02-22|work=[[Wikipedia:Collider (website)|Collider]]|archivedate=20200105231654}}</ref> and these illustrations later appeared in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> He also created one of the variant covers for the 2016 comic [[Darth Vader (2015) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 25]].<ref name="Darth Vader 25" />
