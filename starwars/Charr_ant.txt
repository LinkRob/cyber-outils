The '''charr ant''' was a type of [[ant]] found on<ref name="The High Republic 8" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Kashyyyk]] that shared a [[Symbiosis|symbiotic]] relationship with the [[shi-shok tree]].<ref name="The High Republic 8" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Hutt Clan]] undertook an [[Battle on Mulita|assault]] against<ref name="The High Republic 8" /> the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[carnivorous plant]]s<ref name="Timelines" /> known as the [[Drengir]]<ref name="The High Republic 8" /> on their [[homeworld]]<ref name="Timelines" />&mdash;the [[Wild Space]] world [[Mulita]]. During the assault,<ref name="The High Republic 8" /> the [[Kotabi]] [[bond-twins]] [[Jedi Knight]]s<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Ceret]] and [[Terec]] compared the symbiotic relationship of the charr ant and shi-shok [[tree]] to that of the Drengir and a local type of [[Unidentified arthropod|arthropod]] they had [[weapon]]ized to act as guards.<ref name="The High Republic 8" />

==Behind the scenes==
The charr ant was mentioned in the [[The High Republic (2021) 8|eighth issue]] of the [[2021]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2021)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']],<ref name="The High Republic 8" /> released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[August 11]], 2021.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/94641/star_wars_the_high_republic_2021_8|text=Star Wars: The High Republic (2021) #8}}</ref>
