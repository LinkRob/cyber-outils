'''Anke Reitzenstein''' is a [[Wikipedia:German|German]] [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice artist]] who voiced [[Celana Aldrete]] in the audio drama [[Dark Lord (German audio drama)|''Dark Lord'']].<ref name="Dark Lord" /> Reitzenstein would later voice the [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Aurra Sing/Legends|Aurra Sing]] in the [[German Clone Wars audio dramas]] which adapted a number of episodes of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated television series.<ref name="German Clone Wars">[[German Clone Wars audio dramas]]</ref>
