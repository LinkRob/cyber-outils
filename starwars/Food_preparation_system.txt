'''Food preparation systems''', also known as '''field stoves''' or "'''hot plates'''," were a type of collapsible tray with integrated thermal circuitry that could be used to prepare [[Foodstuff|food]]. Often used by military personnel, they could be powered by several means. They cost 20 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="GG">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref>
