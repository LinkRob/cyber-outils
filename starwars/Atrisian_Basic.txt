'''Atrisian Basic''' was a [[language]]<ref name="Propaganda" /> that shared its name with the [[Galactic Basic Standard]] language<ref name="BMF63">{{BuildFalconCite|63|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> and the celestial body [[Atrisia]]. The Atrisian Basic phrase ''Imperius Unitada ober Totallex'', meaning "Empire united over all," was written in the [[High Galactic]] [[Writing|alphabet]] on the [[Galactic Empire]] [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] of the [[Imperius Unitada ober Totallex|same name]]. The poster was commissioned by the Imperial [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]] and created by the [[artist]]s [[Janyor]] and [[Dalraga de Cueravon]]. Janyor would later note his disgust with the [[Fascism|fascist]] imagery of the work in his [[autobiocron]] ''[[Dear Anguillon]]''. The poster was eventually included in the publication ''[[A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', which was published not long after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]]<ref name="Propaganda" /> of [[34 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> and described the motto depicted on it as "brutalist."<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Behind the scenes==
Atrisian Basic was introduced in ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', a [[2016]] book authored by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]. The Atrisian Basic word ''ober'' written on the ''Imperius Unitada ober Totallex'' poster depicted in the book<ref name="Propaganda" /> is similar to the word ''ober'', an [[Wikipedia:Austrian German|Austrian German]] word for "over, above" that is in turn similar to<ref name="Ober">{{WebCite|url=https://www.woerter.net/prepositions/ober.htm|text=Definition preposition "ober" (over, top) - meaning, synonyms, translations, grammar|work=[https://www.woerter.net/ Netzverb Dictionary]|archivedate=20240415184618}}</ref> the [[Wikipedia:Standard German|Standard German]] word ''über'' of the same meaning<ref name="Uber">{{WebCite|url=https://www.woerter.net/prepositions/u3ber.htm|text=Definition preposition "über" (above, over) - meaning, synonyms, translations, grammar|work=[https://www.woerter.net/ Netzverb Dictionary]|archivedate=20240415185307}}</ref>&mdash;as used in "Deutschland, Deutschland über alles" ("Germany, Germany above all"), the first line of the [[Wikipedia:Nazi Germany|Nazi Germany's]] [[Wikipedia:Deutschlandlied|national anthem]].<ref name="Deutschland">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/topic/Deutschlandlied|text=Deutschlandlied &#124; History, Lyrics, Meaning, & Facts|author=The Editors of Encyclopædia Britannica|date=2023-11-14|work=[[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica|''Encyclopædia Britannica'']]|archivedate=20240408202617}}</ref>
