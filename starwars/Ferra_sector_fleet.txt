A '''sector fleet''' served within the [[Republic Navy]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] in the [[Ferra sector]]. After several [[Battle of Xorrn (Clone Wars)|battles]] on [[Xorrn]] by the Republic, it decided that it was too costly to continue. It then had the [[sector fleet]] [[Bombardment of Xorrn|bombard]] Xorrn, destroying the [[Unidentified Separatist flotilla|Confederate flotilla]] there.<ref name="Friends" />
