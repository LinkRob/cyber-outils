"'''Double Trouble: Anakin & Ahsoka!'''" is an article appearing in ''[[Star Wars Insider 108]]'' written by [[Jonathan Wilkins]]. The article covers the characters of [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Ahsoka Tano/Legends|Ahsoka Tano]], with commentary from [[Matt Lanter]] and [[Ashley Eckstein]].<ref name="SI" />
