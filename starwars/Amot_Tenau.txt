}}'''Amot Tenau'''<ref name="Mandalorian VisualGuide">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> was a [[humanoid]] [[Mandalorian]] member of a Mandalorian [[Nevarro Mandalorian covert|covert]] on [[Nevarro]] that existed in [[the galaxy]] in [[9 ABY]]. Tenau was present when [[Din Djarin]] first entered the covert to meet the [[The Armorer]], during which Djarin presented [[Calamari Flan]] as payment for the forging of a pauldron from a [[beskar]] ingot.<ref name="Chapter 1" /> Tenau was also present when Djarin returned to the covert with a [[camtono]] of beskar for The Armorer. As she inspected the beskar and noted that it would draw attention, members of the Tribe began to gather, and Tenau was among those Mandalorians who approached Djarin as the crowd formed around him.<ref name="Manga">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian: The Manga]]''</ref>

Following the Tribe’s intervention on Nevarro, the covert was targeted by the [[Imperial Remnants]], resulting in heavy losses among its members. While some Mandalorians, such as [[Paz Vizsla]], were confirmed to have survived, the fate of others remained unconfirmed, as it was believed that several may have escaped the planet.<ref name="Redemption">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref>

==Equipment==
Amot Tenau wore [[Mandalorian armor]] consisting of light [[grey]] and orange plating over a dark grey [[flight suit]]. Their helmet was a [[Nite Owl helmet|Nite Owl]] design, light grey with orange detailing around the visor, and equipped with a [[rangefinder]]. They wore light grey chest armor paired with an orange [[Ka'rta]], a light grey left shoulder pauldron, orange [[Mandalorian vambrace|vambraces]], an orange left kneeguard, a dark grey right kneeguard fitted with a magnetic mount for a throwing [[knife]], and a [[Mandalorian hunting kama]]. A dark grey cape was draped over their shoulders.<ref name="Mandalorian VisualGuide"/>
