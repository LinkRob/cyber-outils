'''398'''<ref name="The New Trooper" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] who served in the [[First Order]]. They were one of the many [[trooper]]s stationed on the ''[[Colossus]]'' refueling station, and accompanied [[Sergeant]] [[CS-812]] to chase down [[CS-515]], who [[Commander]] [[Pyre]] believed to have erratic programming. In reality, it was [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] wearing CS-515's [[First Order stormtrooper armor|armor]] after the trooper had been knocked unconscious. Xiono managed to [[Theft of First Order data rod|escape]] and put the [[armor]] back onto CS-515, who was tracked down by 398 and taken away for reconditioning.<ref name="The New Trooper" />
