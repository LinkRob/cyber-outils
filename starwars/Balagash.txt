'''Balagash''' was a [[gas giant]] in the [[Expansion Region]] that was [[orbit]]ed by seventeen [[moon]]s, including [[Ma'a]], [[Rhoremone]], and [[Balumbra]]. During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], which lasted from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]], [[Balagash campaign|battles]] were fought on all of Balagash's moons. In 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], [[Imperial Intelligence]] operative [[Haki Zeophrine]] [[Investigation into the framing of the Jedi|accompanied]] [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] to Balumbra and noticed Balagash burning with a pustulant [[Color|green]] light.

==Description==
Balagash was a [[gas giant]]<ref name="MOF" />  in the [[Balagash system]] of the [[Expansion Region]]'s [[Hali sector]]. It lay in grid square 0-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> in [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It was [[orbit]]ed by seventeen [[moon]]s, including [[Ma'a]], [[Rhoremone]], and [[Balumbra]], its eighth moon. Balagash appeared [[Color|green]] when viewed from Balumbra.<ref name="MOF" />

==History==
During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="MOF" /> which lasted from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Balagash campaign|battles]] were fought on all seventeen of Balagash's moons.<ref name="MOF" /> In 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate}}</ref> after the Republic was [[Proclamation of the New Order|reorganized]] into the [[Galactic Empire]] at the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the Clone Wars, [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] and [[Imperial Intelligence]] operative [[Haki Zeophrine]] traveled to Balumbra during Organa's [[Investigation into the framing of the Jedi|investigation]] into the Empire's [[Great Jedi Purge|framing]] of the [[Jedi Order]] for [[treason]]. Zeophrine considered Balumbra to be the most defensible of Balagash's moons, as it was formed from [[metal]]s resistant to blasting and bombarding and was sheltered by the orbits of three larger moons. As Organa and Zeophrine walked through a [[Balumbra spaceport|spaceport]] on Balumbra, Zeophrine noticed Balagash burning with a pustulant green light through a [[Magnetic field|magnetic shield]].<ref name="MOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Balagash first appeared in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MOF" />
