The '''Baragwin'''<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> were a large [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] with [[Color|pink]] and gray skin and yellow [[eye]]s.<ref name="ROTJ" /> The thug [[Hermi Odle]] was a Baragwin.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> As a colonial species, they had migrated and adapted to dominate many worlds.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==Biology and appearance==
Baragwin were a bipedal species with two [[limb|legs]], as well as two arms, each ending in three-fingered hands. They had a naturally hunched posture with tall backs and broad shoulders.<ref name="ROTJ" /> Their heads were rounded and featured a large [[mouth]] with a prominent [[tongue]]. Their skin could be [[Color|brown]]<ref name="Lando 1" /> or bluish-gray in coloration, with mottled pink patches. They had beady yellow<ref name="ROTJ" /> or orange [[eye]]<ref name="Lando 1" /> and vertical [[Nose|nostrils]] situated at the end of their snouts. The Baragwin [[Hermi Odle]] had facial orifices that were often filled with pus.<ref name="ROTJ" />

==History==
Baragwin hailed from the [[Old Barag system]]<ref name="Planetnamia" /> within the [[Epsi Collective sector]], which itself was situated within the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> The species traveled throughout and were known and present with [[The galaxy|the greater galaxy]] by at least [[0 ABY]].<ref name="Star Wars 15 8">[[Star Wars (2015) 8|''Star Wars'' (2015) 8]]</ref>

==Baragwin in the galaxy==
In 0 ABY<ref name="SW15 8 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'' (2015) 8 to [[0 ABY]].</ref>, shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], a Baragwin was present in a [[Unidentified bar (Nar Shaddaa)|bar]] on [[Nar Shaddaa]] when [[Luke Skywalker]] entered the establishment following a brief brawl that ended when he drew his [[Skywalker lightsaber|lightsaber]].<ref name="Star Wars 15 8" /> During the tenure of [[80-M]], in which he served under [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]], Hermi Odle was present at [[Jabba's Throne Room|Jabba's throne room]] when a [[Rodian]] came to speak before Jabba. Odle was also present when a fight between [[Charn Roondha|Roondha]] and [[Ree-Yees]] broke out within the same location.<ref name="Jabba's Palace 1">[[Return of the Jedi – Jabba's Palace 1|''Return of the Jedi – Jabba's Palace'' 1]]</ref>

Additionally, Odle was present within [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] when [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] visited shortly before the [[Mission to Cloud City|rescue]] of [[Lobot]] from [[Cloud City]].<ref name="Star Wars 2">[[Star Wars (2020) 2|''Star Wars'' (2020) 2]]</ref> He also witnessed [[Boba Fett]] intimidate [[Bib Fortuna]] into revealing why Jabba had placed a bounty on the [[bounty hunter]].<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters 1">[[War of the Bounty Hunters 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters'' 1]]</ref>

In [[4 ABY]]<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/the-illustrious-jabba-the-hutt-bids-you-welcome-to-his-new-marvel-comic-jabbas-palace-exclusive-reveal|text=The Illustrious Jabba the Hutt Bids You Welcome to His New Marvel Comic, ''Jabba's Palace'' - Exclusive Reveal}} establishes that [[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (2023)|''Star Wars: Return of the Jedi'']] takes place in the era of {{Film|VI}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref>, a Baragwin watched Lando Calrissian and [[Elocin]] gamble inside [[Elocin's club]].<ref name="Lando 1" /> Later that same year, Hermi Odle<ref name="EP6Date">{{FilmDate|VI}}</ref> was once again present within Jabba's Palace, specifically when Luke Skywalker, Lando Calrissian, and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] [[Rescue of Han Solo|rescued]] Han Solo from [[carbonite]].<ref name="ROTJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Baragwin first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], directed by [[Richard Marquand]]<ref name="ROTJ" /> and released in [[1983]].<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> While the species were not named on screen in the film, they were eventually identified within [[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]<ref name="Absolutely Everything">[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]</ref>
released on [[September 4]], [[2015]].<ref name="DK">{{DK|url=us/9781465437853-star-wars-absolutely-everything-you-need-to-know-journey-to-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know|nolive=1}}</ref>
