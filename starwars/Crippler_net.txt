A '''Crippler net''' was a weapon used by the [[Chiss Defense Force]]. When a Crippler net hit an enemy [[starship]], it released a jolt of electricity that disabled the ship's capabilities. The ''[[Springhawk]]'' was equipped with Crippler nets.<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref> Crippler nets discharged electricity to the hulls of starships they impacted, similar to but weaker than [[plasma sphere]]s. Also unlike plasma spheres, Crippler nets could not chase down their targets as they were launched with gas propulsion.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
