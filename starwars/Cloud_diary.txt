'''Cloud diaries''' were journals used by [[Chiss]] as diaries and artistic canvases depicting nature. The [[writing]] in cloud diaries was stylized into small features that composed the details of the drawing. [[Yomie]] described the [[Agbui]]'s manipulations in her cloud diary and was [[Death|killed]] by Haplif to silence her revelations.

==Description==
Cloud diaries were multi-page journals used by [[Chiss]] to write and draw natural landscapes such as [[Grass|plains]], [[tree]]s, [[cloud]]s, [[mountain]]s, and [[ocean]]s. Cloud diaries' pages could be removed and re-inserted. The [[writing]] in them was small and stylized, and formed the details of the pictures drawn.<ref name="Greater Good" />

==History==
[[Yomie]], a Chiss [[Gender|female]], kept a cloud diary when [[Wandering year|traveling]] with her [[Marriage#Betrothal|betrothed]] [[Coduyo'po'nekri|Yoponek]], which she continued writing after joining a group of [[Agbui]] led by [[Haplif]]<ref name="Greater Good" /> around [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' establishes that Yomie and Yoponek were joined by the [[Agbui]] prior to the main events of the novel and after the [[battle over Primea]], depicted in ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''. ''Chaos Rising'' takes place in the year [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. ''Greater Good'' establishes that the [[attack on the Paataatus]], which was depicted in ''Chaos Rising'', took place "last year." Therefore, ''Greater Good'' must be set around [[18 BBY]], so the Agbui must have joined Yomie and Yoponek between those two years.</ref> The Agbui claimed to be "cultural nomads", but were attempting to [[Jixtus's plan|subvert]] the [[Chiss Ascendancy|Chiss nation]] on behalf of an [[Grysk Hegemony|expansionist empire]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

Haplif hoped to read Yomie's cloud diary to learn her motivations to better manipulate her, but she did not let him. When Yomie left the Agbui's [[starship]], she took her diary so Haplif could not read it. After spending several [[Standard week|weeks]] [[Grand Migration|bird-watching]] on the [[planet]] [[Shihon]], Yomie deduced that the Agbui possessed [[telepathy]] and were manipulating the Chiss. Yomie recorded everything she knew about the Agbui in her cloud diary. To prevent that knowledge's spread, Haplif [[Death|murdered]] Yomie, dumping her and her diary into the [[vacuum]] of [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Greater Good" />
