'''266 BBY''', or '''266<ref name="Date 266" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The [[Hayananeya]], a [[singing stone]] sacred to the [[Twi'lek]] people of the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]],<ref name="GS">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref> went missing in that year.<ref name="Date 266" /> The Hayananeya was stolen from Ryloth and ended up in the [[hyperspace compass]] of the [[Purrgil-class starcruiser|starcruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''<ref name="GS" /> by the [[time]] of its tenth anniversary voyage in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date 231">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Halcyon Legacy 1|''Halcyon Legacy'' 1]], which shows the Hayananeya in the ''[[Halcyon]]''{{'s}} [[hyperspace compass]], to [[231 BBY]].</ref> though the Twi'lek [[Gaya]] considered the stone to have been missing for three [[Standard century|hundred years]] when she took it from the ''Halcyon''<ref name="GS" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines" />

==Behind the scenes==
266 BBY was indirectly mentioned in the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] immersive two-day roleplaying experience, which opened at the [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World|Walt Disney World Resort]] in Florida<ref name="GS" /> on [[March 1]], [[2022]],<ref name="31 Things">{{SW|url=news/31-things-we-learned-about-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=31 Things We Learned in an Exclusive Preview of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser|nolive=1}}</ref> and was launched for the final time on [[September 28]], [[2023]].<ref name="GalacticStarcruiserClosure">{{WebCite|url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/travel/2023/05/18/star-wars-hotel-closing|text=Disney to close its $5,000 Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser hotel|author=Hannah Sampson|date=2023-05-18|work=[[Wikipedia:The Washington Post|''The Washington Post'']]|archivedate=20230519064024}}</ref>
