'''Contruum''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> in the [[Contruum system]] in the [[Truum sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]], in grid square O-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> [[Angilar Bosh]], who created the [[Galactic Republic]] [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] ''[[Strength and Unity]]'', was an art director for a leading Contruum advertising agency.<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[general]] [[Airen Cracken]] was from Contruum.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> The [[Vaathkree Trade Corridor]]<ref name="Vaathkree" /> [[hyperspace route]] connected Contruum to [[Montross]] and [[Obroa-skai]].<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref>
