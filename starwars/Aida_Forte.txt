'''Aida Forte''' was a [[Kadas'sa'Nikto]] [[Gender|female]] who served as a [[Jedi Knight]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Cataclysm" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Forte attended the [[Wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran]] on [[Eiram]] and helped with the search for fugitive and saboteur [[Axel Greylark]] as well as fighting bounty hunters that had arrived at the wedding in search of Greylark.<ref name="Convergence" /> Afterward, Forte and [[Jedi Master]] [[Creighton Sun]] traveled to the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] with delegations from the [[planet]]s [[Eiram]] and [[E'ronoh]], where the delegations were to sign a [[Eiram–E'ronoh peace treaty|treaty]] during a [[Summit on Jedha|summit]] to end the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|war between the two planets]]. However, the summit failed and the city erupted into the [[Battle of Jedha]], Forte and Sun fighting alongside the [[Guardians of the Whills]] to mitigate loss of life.<ref name="Battle of Jedha" /> After the battle was over, Forte and Sun decided to travel to [[Dalna]] to investigate the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[religion|cult]], who were believed to have incited the Battle of Jedha, alongside Jedi Master [[Yaddle]]. They discovered that the Path had many weapons including the [[bioweapon]] [[klytobacter]] and the living weapons that preyed on [[Force-sensitive]]s called the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]]. After even more Jedi arrived on Dalna to investigate, the Path attacked the Jedi beginning the [[Night of Sorrow]], a devastating battle in which Forte lost her life. She was remembered in a [[funeral at the Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] alongside other fallen Jedi.<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Personality and traits==
Aida Forte was a [[Kadas'sa'Nikto]] [[Gender|female]] with [[Color|green]] skin and horns that circled her face.<ref name="Cataclysm" /> She was relentlessly optimistic and always saw the good in a situation even when it was dire.<ref name="Battle of Jedha" />

==Powers and abilities==
Aida Forte could use a [[Force push]] to knock back three people and a droid at once. She could use the Force to prevent people from noticing her presence.<ref name="Cataclysm" />
