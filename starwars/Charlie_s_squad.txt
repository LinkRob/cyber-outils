A '''squad''' of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] was active during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Sometime prior to an [[assault on a Separatist weapons depot]], [[clone trooper]] [[Charlie]] deserted the [[squad]] out of fear and later recounted his desertion with his [[Heater's squad|squad]] under the command of [[Heater]].<ref name="Battle Tales 4" />
