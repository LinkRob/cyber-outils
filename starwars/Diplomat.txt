A '''diplomat''' was someone involved in diplomacy, the art and practice of conducting negotiations between representatives of groups or states. Diplomats were commonly assisted by [[protocol droid]]s, a type of [[droid]] programmed in etiquette and equipped with formidable language skills.<ref name="Protocol Droid">{{Databank|protocol-droid|Protocol Droid}}</ref> Diplomats enjoyed a specific form of immunity that exempted from many ordinary criminal and civil laws.<ref name="PSFB">''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]''</ref>
