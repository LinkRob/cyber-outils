The [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] maintained a private '''collection''' of [[Sith artifact]]s.

==Description==
The [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]] kept a private [[Sith]] collection which consisted of many items, including the [[Great Crystal of Aantonaii]], a [[Sith holocron (Grand Republic Medical Facility)|massive Sith holocron]] from [[Moraband]], a [[Sith Holocron repository]], the [[Great Sith Wars Bas-Relief]], and numerous Sith weapons.<ref name="CL2016" />

==History==
===Clandestine works===
The personal collection's owner was the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Sidious, who, during the [[time]] of [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]]' [[Sith apprentice|apprenticeship]] to [[Sith Master|himself]], stored the bulk of his [[Sith artifact]] collection within a desolate structure in [[The Works]] district of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], of which was also home to the [[Inquisitorius Headquarters|secret lair of the Sith]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="CL2016" />

In the height of the [[galaxy]]-wide conflict, the rogue [[clone trooper]] [[Fives]] made an [[escape from the Grand Republic Medical Facility]] after threatening the [[Supreme Chancellor]] Sheev Palpatine's life, who was in truth the public figure of Sidious, which led to the [[Grand Republic Medical Facility|Medical Facility's]] temporary closure. This securing of the facility was taken advantage of by the [[Sith Lord]], who made way for a [[Sith retreat]] in the lower levels of the building, populated by his collection transferred from The Works. This move was made as a result of Sidious' fears that the [[Jedi Order]] may discover the derelict structure, along with its illicit contents, in the abandoned industrial zone.<ref name="CL2016" />

Now forming a strong [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Vergence|reservoir]], the collection sits upon walkways above a repository for large [[kyber crystal]]s that await use for the contruction of [[Sith lightsaber]]s in the [[Imperial Era]]. This vergence point in [[the Force]] was drawed upon for the completion of the rebirth of the fallen Jedi, [[Anakin Skywalker]], as [[Darth]] Vader, [[Sith apprentice]] of Sidious.<ref name="CL2016" />

===Under the nose of the Jedi===
As his public persona, Sheev Palpatine, Sith artifacts, including a [[Darth Sidious' Sith Chalice|Sith Chalice]],<ref name="CL2016" /> a pair of [[spirit urn]]s,<ref name="VE" /> and the [[bronzium]] statues of the [[Four Sages of Dwartii]], were kept and openly displayed in the [[Emperor's office|Chancellor's office]] during Palpatine's tenure in the [[Office of the Chancellor|Office of the Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Even against a Sith-red [[color]]ed wall as complement for the Sith-themed suite, the [[Jedi]] had yet to detect the true nature of the long-incumbent Chancellor.<ref name="CL2016" />
