A [[Chiss]] [[Gender|woman]] served as the '''caregiver''' to the [[sky-walker]] [[Ab'begh]] while she was stationed on the [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Biography==
Chiss sky-walker Ab'begh and Ab'begh's caregiver are set aside by Supreme General [[Ba'kif]] for the service of Admiral Ar'alani's ship, the [[Vigilant]]. The ship is to rendezvous with Senior Caption Mitth'raw'nuruodo's ship, the [[Springhawk]], at the [[Rapacc system]], where that ship's sky-walker, [[Che'ri]], was to be transferred to the Vigilant for purposes of safety related to an upcoming mission. When Che'ri and Ab'begh are playing with [[building snap]]s, the caregiver interrupts and tells them to read. Admiral Ar'alani arrives to inform Che'ri about the safety of the Springhawk on its mission, but states that she will have to spend the night on the Vigilant. When Che'ri and Ab'begh plan to sleep in the same room, the caregiver reflects upon her own memories of sleepovers as a child. When Ar'alani leaves, the caregiver instructs the girls to read for half an hour.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
