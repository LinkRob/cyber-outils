'''Clan Tal''' was a [[Mandalorian clan]] that dated as far back as the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="High Republic" /> [[Aran Tal]], a [[human]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> who participated in the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] sporting competition, was the last remaining member of Clan Tal,<ref name="YouTube" /> who were [[Death|killed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Night of a Thousand Tears]].<ref name="All About Aran" /> Aran Tal wore the clan's ancestral [[Clan Tal Warrior|armor]].<ref name="Aran Tal">{{Hunters|url=characters/aran-tal|text=Aran Tal}}</ref> Clan Tal had a rivalry with [[Clan Vizsla]].<ref name="Clan Vizsla">{{HuntersCite|character=Aran Tal|costume=Clan Vizsla Warrior}}</ref>
