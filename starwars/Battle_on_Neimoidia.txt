Sometime between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], a '''battle''' occurred on the [[planet]] [[Neimoidia]]. As a result of the planet's blitzing by the Republic, the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Neimoidian]] [[species]]' grub hatcheries were left devastated and the Neimoidian's continued operations on the world were at risk.<ref name="BMF 52" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle on Neimoidia was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 52|fifty-second issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 52" /> in an article written by [[Mark Newbold]],<ref name="Newbold">{{DMCite|dm=File:MarkNewbold-CreditsBMF.png|creator=[[Mark Newbold]]}}</ref> which was published around [[December 30]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdates">{{BMFdates|52}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Battle on Neimoidia/Legends|battle of Neimoidia]] was first indirectly mentioned in ''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]'', a [[2005]] novel authored by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Labyrinth">''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]''</ref>
