'''Colicoid''' was a [[language]] of [[the galaxy]]. It was notably used by the [[Pistoeka sabotage droid]]s,<ref name="YouTube">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Language in Star Wars Movies|video=CZp8Civk2co|nolive=1}}</ref> which were manufactured by the [[Colicoid Creation Nest]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Also, [[D1-series aerial battle droid]]s and [[ANT-621 droid]]s spoke the language.<ref name="On the Wings of Keeradaks">{{TCW|On the Wings of Keeradaks}}</ref><ref name="Chapter 6">{{BOBF|Chapter 6: From the Desert Comes a Stranger}}</ref>
