'''Deputy Director''' was a high position in the [[Galactic Empire]]. The leader of the [[Naval Intelligence Agency]] held the title of Deputy Director,<ref name="Tarkin" /> and in [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> was held by [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]] and later [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Colonel]] [[Wullf Yularen]]. At the same [[time]], [[Harus Ison]] held the title of Deputy Director of the ISB.<ref name="Tarkin" /> ISB [[Inspector General]] [[Bozeden Jeems]] sought to be promoted to Deputy Director.<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
