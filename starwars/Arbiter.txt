'''Arbiter''' was a title given to the person who crowned the [[supreme governor]] of the [[planet]] [[Birren]]. The arbiter would bestow the supreme governor with a cloth-of-[[gold]] [[mantle]] and a [[scepter]], which symbolized sheltering and defending the people of Birren.<ref name="Bloodline" /> After becoming disillusioned with the [[Jedi Order]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Tenth Brother|Prosset Dibs]] accused the [[Jedi High Council]] of being self-proclaimed arbiters of [[the Force]].<ref name="Mace Windu 5">[[Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu 5|''Jedi of the Republic – Mace Windu'' 5]]</ref>
