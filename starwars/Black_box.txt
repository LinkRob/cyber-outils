A '''black box''',<ref name="Poe Dameron: Flight Log">''[[Poe Dameron: Flight Log]]''</ref> also known as a '''flight recorder''',<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> was a piece of equipment installed aboard [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]s, which logged the events aboard the [[Starship|ship]]. When [[Resistance]] [[pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]] was captured by the [[First Order]] during the [[Attack on Tuanul|Massacre at Tuanul]], the black box was removed from his [[Poe Dameron's blue X-wing|X-wing]] and sent to the encryption department for examination.<ref name="Poe Dameron: Flight Log">''[[Poe Dameron: Flight Log]]''</ref>

{{Tech-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
In real life, a black box, or flight recorder, consist of a [[flight data recorder]] (FDR),<ref name="SKY 1">{{WebCite|url=https://www.skybrary.aero/index.php/Flight_Data_Recorder_(FDR)|text=SKY brary: Flight Data Recorder (FDR)|archivedate=20210115105438}}</ref> which also exists in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]],<ref name="Flight of the Defender">{{Rebels|Flight of the Defender}}</ref> and a cockpit voice recorder (CVR). The flight data recorder is used to record specific aircraft performance parameters,<ref name="SKY 1" /> while the cockpit voice recorder is used to record the audio environment in the flight deck.<ref name="SKY 2">{{WebCite|url=https://skybrary.aero/index.php/Cockpit_Voice_Recorder_(CVR)|text=SKY brary: Cockpit Voice Recorder (CVR)|archivedate=20201127003238}}</ref>
