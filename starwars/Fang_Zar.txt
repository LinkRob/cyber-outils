'''Fang Zar''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] [[senator]] who served in the [[Galactic Senate]] during the [[Clone Wars]] representing [[Sern Prime]] and its [[Sern sector|sector]]. Prior to the war's end, he was part of the [[Delegation of 2,000]] out of concern over various amendments to the [[Galactic Constitution|constitution]]. As a result, he and his allies presented the [[Petition of 2,000]] to [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. Palpatine later [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]] after [[Order 66]] was enacted. One [[Standard day|day]] after the formation of the Empire, [[Imperial Intelligence]] conducted a [[Senatorial arrests|mass arrest]] of senators, which included Zar.

==Biography==
===Clone Wars===
Fang Zar<ref name="Rebel Files" /> was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]]<ref name="The Rise of Clovis" /> who served as the [[senator]] of [[Sern Prime]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and the [[Sern sector]]<ref name="End of CW">{{EncyclopediaCite|The End of the Clone Wars}}</ref> in the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During the war, Zar was present in the [[Galactic Senate Chamber]] when Senator [[Rush Clovis]] spoke about his and Senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s discovery of corruption in the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]].<ref name="The Rise of Clovis" />

After Clovis was nominated as the head of the Banking Clan and substantially increased the interest rates on loans undertaken by the Republic and not on the loans by the Separatists, Zar was present in the Senate Chamber as the Senate reacted to the news.<ref name="Crisis at the Heart">{{TCW|Crisis at the Heart}}</ref>

After a [[Mission to Oba Diah (Clone Wars)|mission]] to [[Oba Diah]] undertaken by the [[Jedi Order]], Zar was present in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] accompanying fellow [[Mon Calamari]] Senator [[Meena Tills]] when [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] approached Master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and spoke to him about his concern for [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]]'s health.<ref name="Voices">{{TCW|Voices}}</ref>

===Dawn of the New Order===
[[File:2000 meeting.png|thumb|right|300px|Fang Zar (right) joined the Delegation of 2,000 in opposition to Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine's growing executive power.]]
By the final stages of the Clone Wars, Zar was part of the [[Delegation of 2,000]], a group of 2,000 senators that were concerned with [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] growing [[Politics|political]] power. The Delegation presented the [[Petition of 2,000]], a formal protest to Palpatine's growing power.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Following the [[Jedi High Council]]'s discovery of Palpatine's secret existence as the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Sidious, four [[Jedi Master]]s&mdash;[[Mace Windu]], [[Kit Fisto]], [[Agen Kolar]], and [[Saesee Tiin]]&mdash;attempted to [[Duel in Palpatine's office|capture or kill]] the Chancellor, only for [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]&mdash;who would fall to the [[dark side of the Force]] himself as Sidious's new [[Sith apprentice]], [[Darth]] Vader&mdash;to come to Palpatine's rescue. Afterward, Palpatine proclaimed the [[Jedi]] [[Order 66|traitors]] to the Republic and [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]], with himself as its [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> Knowing little more than that the Jedi Council had launched an assassination attempt, Zar, while he saw the results as tragic and aggressive, believed Palpatine acted within his rights when he declared the Jedi Order an enemy of the state.<ref name="Mof" />

While he did not display upset towards the [[Great Jedi Purge]], Zar did continue to oppose Palpatine and his efforts to claim executive power.<ref name="Mof" /> One [[Standard day|day]] [[Imperial calendar|after the formation of the Empire]], [[Imperial Intelligence]] [[Senatorial arrests|arrested]] sixty-three senators from the Delegation, including Zar, on charges of conspiracy and [[treason]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Personality and traits==
Zar had a long, graying beard and wore a diamond-patterned lilac [[Clothing|robe]].<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

==Equipment==
Many senators proudly wore the traditional dress of their people. However, others used their statues and substantial budgets of their offices to pay for the luxurious outfits. On Coruscant they wore flamboyant creations made by the most famous designers using delicately important fabrics. Senator Fang Zar was one of them.<ref name="VE" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:TCW_Fang_Zar_concept.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Concept art of Fang Zar for ''The Clone Wars'']]
Fang Zar first appeared in the [[2005]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], the final installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]].<ref name="Episode III" /> He was portrayed by [[Warren Owens]].<ref name="Zar, Fang">{{DB|character|fangzar|Zar, Fang}}</ref>

The original concept art for Fang Zar called for the character to count with strong [[Wikipedia:Aboriginal Australians|aboriginal]] facial features, which prompted casting director [[Christine King]] to hire Owens for the role.<ref name="Zar, Fang" /> 

Zar was later identified in the episode guide of "[[An Old Friend]]," the fifth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|sixth season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', released in [[2014]], where he was slated to appear but was cut from the final version of the episode.<ref name="Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/an-old-friend-concept-art-gallery|text="An Old Friend" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> He would later appear in the season starting from the episode "[[The Rise of Clovis]]."<ref name="The Rise of Clovis" />
