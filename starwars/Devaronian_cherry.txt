'''Devaronian cherries''' were a rare and expensive [[chocolate]]-coated [[Foodstuff|dessert]] that had a tart sweetness with a chocolatey tang.<ref name="The Buy-In" /> The [[Cherry|cherries]] shared their name with the [[Devaronian]] [[species]].<ref name="Dev">{{Databank|devaronian|Devaronian}}</ref> During the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="The Buy-In" /> the [[human]]<ref name="Yummy" /> [[rebel pilot]] [[Norra Wexley]] saved up enough [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to purchase a bag of one hundred cherries, savoring them over the course of five [[Standard month|months]].<ref name="The Buy-In" />

The [[Standard day|day]] before the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Alliance]] was set to [[Battle of Endor|attack]] the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] [[battle station]]<ref name="The Buy-In" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> Wexley joined fellow [[pilot]]s [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], [[Wedge Antilles]], [[Wes Janson]], [[Zyrka Tuhn]], and a [[Kel Dor]] in a game of [[sabacc]], [[Diet|eating]] two cherries throughout the game and noting that she only had ten remaining. Although she was teased about having the cherries and encouraged to use them to [[Gambling|bet]], with Janson specifically keen on winning the desserts, Wexley held off until late in the game, finally betting the cherries.<ref name="The Buy-In" />

==Behind the scenes==
Devaronian cherries appeared in "[[The Buy-In]]," a short story written by [[Suzanne Walker]] as part of the [[2023]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="The Buy-In" />
