'''''Bounty Hunters'' 35''' is the thirty-fifth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Lan Medina]] and [[Arif Prianto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[June 21]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''A NEW ERA FOR THE BOUNTY HUNTERS! In the aftermath of the battle with INFERNO SQUAD, VALANCE and T'ONGA are joined by the most dangerous bounty hunters in the galaxy&hellip;KHEL TANNA! DEATHSTICK! DURGE!&hellip;and BOBA FETT?!''

==Development==
Before its release, [[Ethan Sacks]] revealed ''Bounty Hunters'' 35 contained a story element he had conceived of three years prior:<ref name="Sacks 35">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1671362822721138689|[[Ethan Sacks]]|date=2023-06-20|quote=There's a beat in Bounty Hunters 35 that I've literally been waiting 3 years to play.|archivedate=20230726192952}}</ref> Boba Fett defeating the rest of [[T'onga's crew]] but being defeated by [[T'onga]] herself,<ref name="Sacks Boba">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1752328403913470154|[[Ethan Sacks]]|date=2024-01-30|quote=T'onga being the one to take down Bobs Fett after he systematically wipes the floor with the rest of her crew. {{C|In response to "so what was it?"}}|archiveurl=https://archive.is/kedfy}}</ref> whom Fett had shot back in [[Bounty Hunters 4|''Bounty Hunters'' 4]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 4">[[Bounty Hunters 4|''Bounty Hunters'' 4]]</ref> In defeating Fett, T'onga overcomes the trauma he inflicted upon her. Her victory in that moment is, in a sentimental perspective, Sack's favorite moment in ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''.<ref name="Sacks Trauma">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1748121188021178466|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=And 2.) my sentimental favorite. T'onga working through her PTSD and paralyzing phobia of Boba Fett (who left her for dead in the beginning of the series) to be the one to take him down after he wipes the floor with her crew. Overcoming trauma was a major theme.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/nkooL}}</ref> According to Sacks, [[Lucasfilm]] fully supported the decision to have Fett lose his helmet in the battle.<ref name="Sacks Helmet">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1748186748650189168|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=I don't remember anything other than support for that moment. {{C|In response to: "I loved your run! Soooo much. Did you have to fight for OT Boba Fett to remove his helmet, or was it a simple yes from LucasFilm? 😁"}}|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/uYikf}}</ref>
