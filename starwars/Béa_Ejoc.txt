'''Béa Ejoc''' was a [[Human/Legends|human]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Scientist/Legends|scientist]], who designed [[Weapon/Legends|weapons]] for the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. While on vacation onboard the [[Kuari Princess/Legends|''Kuari Princess'']] she met and befriended [[Tanith Seyze]], secretly a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|rebel]] agent hoping to acquire plans of her inventions and a powerful [[Force-sensitive/Legends|force-sensitive]] [[Changeling/Legends|shapeshifter]]. Throughtout the [[Grand Galactic Tour|cruise]], the two women grew close. One night, Béa invited Tanith for a private [[Foodstuff/Legends|meal]], having put aphrodisiacs in it to intensify her attraction. Unable to control her own body and powers, Tanith accidentaly killed Béa during sex.<ref name="GuestStar" />

==Behind the scenes==
Béa Ejoc first appeared in the article ''[[Tanith: the Guest-Star Wars]]'', written by [[Olivier Frot]] and published in the French [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] magazine ''[[Graal 24]]'' in [[1990]]. Alongside [[Tanith Seyze]], she is the first [[Wikipedia:LGBT community|LGBTQ+]] character in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' universe.<ref name="GuestStar" />
