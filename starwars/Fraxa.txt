'''Fraxa''' was a [[Muun]] who was the owner of [[Kwikhaul Livery Corporation]] on the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]]. She owned an [[Fraxa's ARC-8 starfighter|antique]] [[ARC-8 starfighter]] from the [[High Republic Era]] that she attempted to restore. Due to a lack of tourism, the company was unable to afford paying its organic drivers; as such, Fraxa replaced them with [[droid]]s. The drivers rioted outside the [[Kwikhaul complex]], destroying many of the droids, until the [[Jedi Master]]s [[Plo Koon]] and [[Saesee Tiin]] intervened and handled the dispute.<ref name="The Living Force" />
