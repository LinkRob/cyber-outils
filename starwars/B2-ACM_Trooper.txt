The '''B2-ACM Trooper''' was a type of [[B2-series super battle droid]] used by the [[Separatist Droid Army]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Featuring a more powerful [[wrist blaster]] than the standard model, B2-ACMs were deployed when the firepower of a standard B2 was not enough.<ref name="Manufactured Might" />
