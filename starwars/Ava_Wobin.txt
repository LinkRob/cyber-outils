'''Ava Wobin''' was a [[Human]] who worked as a [[starship]] [[Technician|mechanic]] and ran [[Ava's Garage]] in the [[Mirogana Spaceport|spaceport]] of the [[city]] of [[Mirogana]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]. At the garage, Wobin used provided materials to upgrade ships and was capable of working on an [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the mechanic wore a [[Color|tan]] [[Clothing|shirt]], a brown [[belt]], black [[glove]]s, green pants, black [[boot]]s, and a black [[hat]] while at the garage. Wobin had brown [[eye]]s, light skin, and blond [[hair]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ava Wobin appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The player cannot access Wobin's services until they have unlocked full access to their ship, the ''[[Trailblazer]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
