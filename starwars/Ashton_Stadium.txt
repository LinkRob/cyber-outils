The '''Ashton Stadium''' was a stadium located on [[the Wheel]]'s [[third level]]. The game [[shockball]] was played in the stadium, featuring the local team, the [[Galactic Globetrotters]].<ref name="Obi-Wan and Anakin" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ashton Stadium appeared in ''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Obi-Wan & Anakin Adventure]]'', a [[2019]] book in the ''[[Star Wars: Choose Your Destiny]]'' series, written by [[Cavan Scott]] and illustrated by [[Elsa Charretier]].<ref name="Obi-Wan and Anakin" /> Scott named the stadium<ref name="Twitter 1">{{Twitter|cavanscott|status/1449054481715503105|[[Cavan Scott]]|quote=Place. {{C|In response to "Character or place?"}}|archivedate=20211015211124}}</ref> after the [[Wikipedia:Ashton Gate Stadium|Ashton Gate Stadium]].<ref name="Twitter 2">{{Twitter|cavanscott|status/1449051827996176394|[[Cavan Scott]]|quote=Well, I already got Ashton Gate into it!|archivedate=20211015211333}}</ref>
