The '''Ballast''' family was a family that lived on the [[Planet|world]] [[Mordal]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> around [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] occur [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]]. [[Lynnta]] and her [[Corwin Ballast's daughter|daughter]] [[Death|died]] several [[Standard week|weeks]] prior to the novel, placing their deaths to around to around that [[time]]/</ref> The family consisted of [[Corwin Ballast]], his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Lynnta]], and their [[Corwin Ballast's daughter|daughter]].<ref name="Aftermath" />
