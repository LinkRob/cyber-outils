{{Quote|Following repeated acts of vandalism and graffiti on Imperial propaganda and recruitment posters on the small world of Ivera X, the major city was strafed by TIE fighters and bombers until seventy-three percent of its original buildings had been destroyed.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
'''Attacks''' were made on the [[planet]] [[Ivera X]] during the [[Imperial Era]] after [[Galactic Empire]] [[propaganda]] and recruitment posters on the [[planet]] [[Ivera X]] saw repeated vandalism, which led to Imperial officials ordering the destruction of its [[City|cities]]. Notably, Ivera X's [[Unidentified city (Ivera X)|major city]] was strafed by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] and [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE bombers]] until seventy-three percent of its original buildings had been destroyed. The resident [[Iverian]]s were then forced to erect new monuments to the Empire from the rubble before they were allowed rebuild their own homes.<ref name="Rise" />
