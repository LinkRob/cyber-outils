'''Casseroles''' were a [[foodstuff]] that was [[Diet|edible]] by the [[Latero]] [[species]]. [[Pyloon]], the great-grandmother of [[pilot]] [[Greez Dritus]] made a special casserole which Greez had obtained the recipe for. After his former [[Stinger Mantis crew|crewmate]] [[Cere Junda]] payed for a [[Pyloon's Saloon|cantina]] in his grandmother's name on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]], he later considered paying Junda back by making her one of Pyloon's casseroles.<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>
