{{Quote|Corporate Sector Authority. I'd heard they were back in play, building themselves up, but I haven't seen that many together, ever. Looks like the rumors were true, they've formed some kind of army.|Lando Calrissian, to Komat|<ref name="SotS" />}}
'''CSA troopers''', nicknamed "'''Espos'''," were the [[infantry]] [[soldier]]s of the [[Corporate Sector Authority]]. The [[Color|brown]] [[CSA trooper armor|armored]] [[troop]]s of the CSA functioned as an [[army]], utilizing military formations such as [[Company|companies]] and [[platoon]]s. While they were considered an improvement on the [[Corporate Sector Authority Security Police]], CSA [[trooper]]s lacked discipline, training, and tactical experience according to [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Komat]]. [[High Colonel]] [[Enric Pryde]] was also largely unimpressed by CSA troopers, although he also admitted that they possessed a degree of efficiency following their [[Mission on Basta Core|victory]] over the [[Basta Core insurgents|insurgents]] of [[Basta Core]].

In [[21 ABY]], a detachment of CSA troopers assisted the [[Jedi hunter]] [[Ochi]] of [[Bestoon]] in the search for [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]. Ochi failed in his initial efforts to capture the [[Youngling|child]], despite the support of the CSA troopers, two of whom were incapacitated by Rey's [[Parent|father]] [[Dathan]].

==History==
{{Quote|All of them.''"<br />"''All of them? Impossible. That's an entire company. Three platoons.''"<br />"''Plus transport.|Ochi and Coromun|<ref name="SotS" />}}
[[File:Security_police-EotECR.png|thumb|right|200px|CSA troopers, also known as Espos, evolved from the CSA security police.]]
In the [[New Republic Era|era]] of the [[New Republic]], the [[Corporate Sector Authority]] engaged in a secret military buildup. The number of CSA [[trooper]]s or "Espos" was increased as the [[Corporate Sector Authority Security Police|CSA security police]] expanded to become an [[army]] of professional [[soldier]]s.<ref name="SotS" /> By [[21 ABY]],<ref name="SotS Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> CSA [[infantry]] [[troop]]s were organized into military formations, with a single [[company]] consisting of three [[platoon]]s.<ref name="SotS" />

The [[Unknown Regions Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] in the [[Unknown Regions]]<ref name="SotS" /> that would one day soon would form into the [[First Order]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> sent [[High Colonel]] [[Enric Pryde]] to the CSA as a military [[supervisor]]. According to Pryde, who was known as [[Callsign|Steadfast]] during his covert assignment, the CSA believed it controlled an army. However, the [[Imperial officer|Imperial veteran]] was unimpressed by the uniform of the CSA troopers, preferring the [[Stormtrooper armor|white armored]] appearance of the [[Stormtrooper|Imperial stormtrooper]]. In Steadfast's view, the CSA and its [[troop]]s were [[privateer]]s trying to emulate the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="SotS" />

Despite Steadfast's opinion about the CSA, he found the CSA troopers to be "reasonably efficient" after witnessing their combat skills in a [[Mission on Basta Core|firefight]] against [[Basta Core insurgents|insurgents]] on [[Basta Core]]<ref name="SotS" /> in 21 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="SotS Date" /> Their victory over the insurgents was achieved within a matter of [[Standard minute|minutes]], leading the high [[colonel]] to consider the idea that the CSA troopers were not as "militarily inept" as he initially believed.<ref name="SotS" />

Against the protests of [[Viceprex]] [[Coromun]], Steadfast reassigned a company of CSA troopers and a [[transport]] to assist [[Ochi]] of [[Bestoon]], a [[Jedi hunter]] who served the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Sith cult|cult]] during the search for [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]. Later, however, he rejected a second request by Ochi for more CSA troopers, having terminated his alliance with the [[alien]] hunter.<ref name="SotS" />

[[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Komat]] discovered a large contingent of CSA troopers stationed at an [[Imperial fuel dump|Imperial facility]] on [[Taw Provode]]<ref name="SotS" /> in 21 ABY.<ref name="SotS Date" /> Calrissian had heard only rumors about the CSA's activities in the aftermath of the [[Galactic Civil War]]; the number of CSA troopers, as well as their heavily [[armor]]ed state, indicated to Calrissian that the CSA had in fact built its own private army.<ref name="SotS" />

==Training==
{{Quote|If they have turned the Espos into an army, that would be logical. This is an advanced operation.|Komat, to Lando Calrissian|<ref name="SotS" />}}
CSA troopers were an evolution of the old CSA [[Law enforcement agency|security police]] that existed during the [[Imperial Era]]. Although they inherited the nickname "Espos" from their predecessors, CSA troopers were trained as soldiers and therefore functioned as a military rather than a police force. Lando Calrissian considered them an improvement on the Imperial-era Espos, and even Enric Pryde admitted to being impressed by the CSA troopers during their battle with the insurgents on Basta Core. However, according to Calrissian the CSA troopers were not the disciplined, battle-hardened soldiers that they believed themselves to be. They acted on adrenaline and instinct instead of tactical experience, their confidence shaken by setbacks. Komat concluded that CSA troopers were deficient in training and experience after assessing their overall quality.<ref name="SotS" />

[[Dathan]], a [[Strand-Cast|strandcast]] who lacked the [[Force-sensitive]] nature of his [[Clone template|template]], the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]], managed to incapacitate two CSA troopers while protecting his [[Palpatine family|family]]. Possessing neither military training nor a strong connection to [[the Force]], the [[Junk dealer|junk trader]] from [[Jakku]] was uncertain if he was merely lucky or if the CSA troopers were simply "bad at their jobs."<ref name="SotS" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|They don't look like the Espos though. Not the regular ones, anyway.''"<br />"''What is an Espos, Lando Calrissian?''"<br />"''CSA security police. But these have been seriously beefed up.|Lando Calrissian and Komat|<ref name="SotS" />}}
During the New Republic Era, CSA troopers wore dull [[Color|brown]] [[CSA trooper armor|field armor]] that was made of [[plastoid]]. Their [[Clothing|uniform]] included a [[helmet]] and a [[Glowrod|flashlight]] mounted to the front of the breastplate. The red helmets that were worn by [[CSA officer]]s, also known as "redcaps," distinguished [[officer]]s from regular troopers.<ref name="SotS" />

CSA troopers were more heavily armored than their predecessors, and were therefore different in appearance to the "regular Espos" of the CSA security police.<ref name="SotS" />
