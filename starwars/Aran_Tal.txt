'''Aran Tal''' was a [[human]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Gender|male]] [[Mandalorian]] who was active on [[Vespaara]], where he fought in the [[Grand Arena]] in the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition.<ref name="Trailer" /> He joined the Hunters roster in [[9 ABY]], soon after [[Balada]] and [[Daq Dragus]] established a broadcasting deal for their competition with the [[Outer Rim Sports Network]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> While [[Arena News]] would advertise Tal as the competition's newest hunter<ref name="Tal Me">{{ArenaNews|url=56adfce346974e1b80272941d65a00f0-en-gb|text=Tal Me About It}}</ref> after already covering news about [[Rieve]] and [[J-3DI]],<ref name="First Edition">{{ArenaNews|url=0122de64c2544c938cd376a6e9cc75f3-en-gb|text=First Edition}}</ref> Rieve had only become a hunter after Tal had already joined, meaning she was in fact the newer of the two. Tal defended the Grand Arena from attacking goons who were intent on destruction.<ref name="BftA" />

As a Hunter, Tal was said to be the last remaining scion of [[Clan Tal]] after [[Mandalore]] was devastated by the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Great Purge of Mandalore|Purge of Mandalore]]. He was said to deeply cherish the traditions of his clan and bore the weight of familial responsibility in their [[Death|passing.]]<ref name="SWH">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> In the Arena, Tal was said to be a bit of a loner, not making many friends.<ref name="Intro news">{{Hunters|url=news/the-man-the-myth-the-mandalorian-aran-tal|text=The Man, the Myth, the Mandalorian Aran Tal!}}</ref> Further, he was said to have a rivalry with [[Imara Vex]] given her history as a [[bounty hunter]], a career that Tal supposedly found as lacking integrity.<ref name="SWH" />

==Biography==
===Losing his clan===
[[File:KX-droids-Mandalore-Purge.png|thumb|left|300px|Aran Tal lost his clan in the Night of a Thousand Tears (pictured).]]
Part of [[Clan Tal]], Aran Tal [[Homeworld|hailed]] from [[Mandalore]], where his clan and his home were destroyed by the [[Galactic Empire]] as part of the [[Night of a Thousand Tears]]. In the aftermath of the cataclysm, Tal sought both vengeance<ref name="All About Aran">{{ArenaNews|url=aee06c26397345ebab2ca79da250e587-en-gb|text=Imara's Vexxed}}</ref> and to honor the memory of his clan.<ref name="Tal Me" /> At some point, Tal took on a few [[mercenary]] jobs, but denied ever doing [[Bounty hunter|bounty hunting]].<ref name="All About Aran" />

In [[9 ABY]], Aran Tal joined the roster of [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]]<ref name="Timelines" /> on the [[planet]] Vespaara. He was notably the first Mandalorian to participate in the competition.<ref name="Tal Me" /> When a group of goons led by a [[Rothwell|mysterious figure]] attacked the Grand Arena intent on destruction, Aran Tal helped fight off the invaders and directed his roster of Hunters towards the [[Aftermath]] battlefield.<ref name="BftA" />

===The Pinnacle Event===
[[File:ChowRoom-ArenaNews.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Aran Tal brawled with Imara Vex in the Arena's chow room.]]
During the debut season<ref name="Crossed">{{ArenaNews|url=cdc7c0c90f34461fa7445c1525437a88-en-gb|text=Crossed Challengers}}</ref> after Hunters of the Outer Rim's broadcasting deal with the ORSN, tensions began to rise between Aran Tal and the former bounty hunter Imara Vex.<ref name="SWH" /> After making public comments denigrating the bounty hunting profession, Imara Vex grew frustrated of Tal's prejudice. When she asked the Mandalorian for an apology, Tal doubled down and escalated his charged insult.<ref name="Heating">{{ArenaNews|url=2ee0b5b0c5df4d2899b63b75d4c15be5-en-gb|text=Heating Things Up}}</ref> Vex subsequently was interviewed by [[Boz Vega]] for Arena News, where she called out Tal's hypocrisy, insisting that mercenary work was philosophically the same as bounty hunting. She felt disdain for what she perceived was Tal's holier-than-thou attitude.<ref name="Vexxed">{{ArenaNews|url=aee06c26397345ebab2ca79da250e587-en-gb|text=Imara's Vexxed}}</ref> Tensions continued to grow as Tal and Vex had a brawl in the Grand Arena's [[chow room]] in which weapons were drawn and furniture was destroyed.<ref name="Chow Room">{{ArenaNews|url=47b94621fee5431384d0316302f36d0e-en-gb|text=Chow Room Brawl Gets Out of Control!}}</ref>

Soon, Tal and Vex simultaneously held opposing press conferences in which they challenged each other to fight in the battlefield. At his conference, Tal demanded that if he won, Vex would have to admit that bounty hunting was a low-life profession, whereas in her press conference, Vex demanded that Tal would have to go on her next bounty hunting job with her if she won. Although neither agreed to each other's terms, they agreed to brawl in the arena.<ref name="Crossed" />

Ultimately, Tal won their challenge. He reinforced his anti-bounty hunter rhetoric, and declared he would donate half of his winnings to [[foundling]]s.<ref name="Tal Triumphant">{{ArenaNews|url=c142ee8c93ca4d869e9a7c6b6049bdbf-en-gb|text=Tal with the Triumph!}}</ref>

Shortly after the Clash, Tal found a gift-wrapped box in his dressing room. Assuming it to be a trap, Tal incinerated it with his flamethrower. It turned out to be a gift from Grozz - a doll in the likeness of Tal, crafted by the [[Wookiee]] himself. Tal apologized and returned what was left of the doll to Grozz.<ref name="Left in the Dust">{{ArenaNews|url=b07a50c78b644145b6ab8210c5f2d0e2-en-gb|text=Left in the Dust}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Tal emphasized honor and nobility in himself and expected it from others.<ref name="SWH" /> His disdain for scum and villainy, as well as his tendency to be a loner, led to him not having many allies within the arena. As part of his concept of honor, Tal opted to give most of his earnings from Hunters of the Outer Rim to charity.<ref name="Live 1.1">{{Hunters|url=news/live-from-the-arena-1-1|text=Live From the Arena 1.1}}</ref> Although a Mandalorian, Tal did not subscribe to the belief held by some that their [[helmet]]s were to stay on.<ref name="All About Aran" /> He also believed that in-fighting between Mandalorians, such as the conflicts between [[Clan Vizsla]] and Clan Tal, to be wasteful.<ref name="SWH" />

Tal was widely critical of bounty hunters, believing them to be lowlifes and scum. This led to conflict with fellow Hunter Imara Vex, who was a bounty hunter.<ref name="SWH" /> Although he had taken mercenary work in the past, Tal vehemently believed in some professions being more honorable than others.<ref name="All About Aran" /> Tal preferred Huttball to podracing due to the former's higher level of physicality.<ref name="BftA" /> 

==Equipment==
Tal was equipped with his ancestral [[Mandalorian armor]]<ref name="Spotlight" /> made partially of [[beskar]] and [[MT-97]] [[blaster pistol]]s, as well as a [[jetpack]] and [[flamethrower]].<ref name="Trailer" /> Also available to him were [[Concordian Crescent Technologies]] blaster pistols [[WESTAR-34 blaster pistol|WESTAR-34]] and [[WESTAR-35 blaster pistol|WESTAR-35]], as well as [[MT-62]]s.<ref name="SWH" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
===In game availability===
[[File:AranTalLogo-Hunters.png|250px|right|thumb|Aran Tal's logo]]
Aran Tal was a character made for the [[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', which was developed by [[NaturalMotion]]'s [[BossAlien]] and published by [[Zynga]].<ref name="SWH" /> However, Tal first appeared in the tie-in novel ''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]'', authored by [[Mark Oshiro]] and published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]<ref name="BftA" /> on [[March 7]], [[2023]].<ref name="Amazon">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-hunters-battle-for-the-arena|text=Star Wars Hunters: Battle for the Arena}}</ref> before making his in-game debut during the game's global release on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Debut">{{Hunters|url=news/worldwide-launch-patch-notes|text=The Galaxy is Watching. Worldwide Launch Patch Notes}}</ref> [[Wikipedia:Concept art|Concept artist]] [[Luke Kendall]] was responsible for designing the character.<ref name="Design">{{ArtStation|subdomain=purplepixelrat|url=projects/YK9bAb|name=[[Luke Kendall]]|text=Star Wars: Hunters|date=2022-10-09|quote=I've been working away over the last few years on some awesome characters for Star Wars: Hunters, in this trailer you can see some great up close shots of Aran Tal the Mandalorian Crusader who was the first SW character I got to design, worked on him closely with LFL and our amazing team at Boss Alien. This is the first of many I can't wait to share more details and actual art on soon!|archivefile=File:LukeKendall-AranTal-ArtStation.png}}</ref>

At launch, Tal had three active abilities. With the Jetpack Boost ability, players could use launch a short distance in any direction, knocking back and damaging opponents. The Tal's Breath ability, a small cone of fire shot from Tal's flamethrower would damage enemies. The Air Assault ability allowed players to use the jetpack to launch Tal some distance while maintaining ability to fire. Once landing, the shockwave would push back opponents and cause damage.<ref name="Intro news" /> His ultimate, Fury of Clan Tal, made a sustained flamethrower assault that rapidly damaged enemies caught in its proximity. Tal was turned into a Hunter's Path character in ''Hunter''{{'s}} "Season 3" release, ensuring he would be free to unlock.<ref name="SWH" />

===Contradictions===
Sometime after Tal's official release in ''Star Wars Hunters'' via its global launch,<ref name="SWH" /> an Arena News piece would be released advertising Tal in-universe as game's newest hunter.<ref name="Tal Me" /> However, even though prior Arena News pieces had made references to Rieve as an active hunter,<ref name="First Edition" /><ref name="Power of the Dark">{{ArenaNews|url=64ac3da05edb46f7bd7a05f7539b3f85-en-gb|text=Power of the Dark Side}}</ref> the official prequel novel ''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]'' depicts Rieve as joining the game's ranks sometime after Tal had become a hunter himself. Therefore, Tal would not be the game's newest hunter in-universe.<ref name="BftA" /> As Arena News represents an in-universe source, this article presents it as an intentionally incorrect fact compared to the reality.
