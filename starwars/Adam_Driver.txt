'''Adam Driver''' (born [[November 19]], [[1983]]) is an [[Wikipedia:Academy Awards|Academy Award]]&ndash;nominated [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]] who starred in the [[Sequel trilogy]] films [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], and [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] as the villainous [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]].<ref name="Episode VII Vanity Fair">{{WebCite|url=http://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2015/05/star-wars-force-awakens-photos|text=Adam Driver and Lupita Nyong'o's Characters Revealed in Exclusive ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Photos|author=Leibovitz, Annie|date=2015-05-04|work=Vanity Fair|archivedate=20150531235346}}</ref><ref name="Episode VIII Vanity Fair">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2017/05/adam-driver-kylo-ren-the-last-jedi|text=Here's How Seriously Adam Driver Takes Playing Kylo Ren|author=Robinson, Joanna|date=2017-05-25|work=Vanity Fair|archivedate=20200111201442}}</ref><ref name="Episode IX Vanity Fair">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2019/05/the-final-skywalker-film-has-been-planned-from-the-very-beginning|text=A Lot of the Final Skywalker Film Has Been Planned "from the Very Beginning"|author=Robinson, Joanna|date=2017-05-22|work=Vanity Fair|archivedate=20200131152400}}</ref>

==Biography==
Adam Driver's involvement in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise was first announced on [[April 29]], [[2014]] when he was named as a cast member for [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="Cast">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-7-cast-announced|text=Star Wars: Episode VII Cast Announced}}</ref> In an interview for the [[Wikipedia:Time (magazine)|Time]], he revealed that [[J.J. Abrams]] advised him to look into [[Wikipedia:Akira Kurosawa|Akira Kurosawa's]] movies for inspiration, much like George Lucas did before. However, after several viewings, Driver decided to take another path, a more personal approach for playing [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]].<ref name="Kurosawa">{{WebCite|url=http://time.com/4129579/adam-driver-masters-dark-side-star-wars-the-force-awakens|text=Here's How Adam Driver Learned to Master the Dark Side|author=McCluskey, Megan|date=2015-12-01|work=Time|archivedate=20190428122332}}</ref> For his performance in ''The Force Awakens'' as the antagonistic Kylo Ren, Driver was voted Best Villain at the [[Wikipedia:2016 MTV Movie Awards|2016 MTV Movie Awards]].<ref name="CBS">{{WebCite|url=http://www.cbsnews.com/news/mtv-movie-awards-2016-highlights-and-winners|text="The Force Awakens" wins big at MTV Movie Awards 2016|date=2016-04-10|work=CBS|archivedate=20160703061616}}</ref> In interviews, he has said that he tends to avoid watching his own performances.<ref name="People">{{WebCite|url=http://people.com/movies/adam-driver-undecided-on-seeing-star-wars-the-last-jedi|text=Adam Driver Is 'Undecided' on Seeing ''Star Wars: The Last'' Jedi Despite Being in It|author=Guglielmi, Jodi|date=2017-11-20|archivedate=20180331152823}}</ref> Driver reprised his role as Kylo Ren in the [[Sequel trilogy]] films [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] (2017) and [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] (2019).<ref name="Episode VIII Vanity Fair" /><ref name="Episode IX Vanity Fair" />

Adam Driver was the focus of two ''[[Saturday Night Live]]'' sketches as Kylo Ren. First, disguised as Matt the Radar Technician, in the 2016 sketch "Undercover Boss: Starkiller Base,"<ref name="Undercover Starkiller">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=channel/UCqFzWxSCi39LnW1JKFR3efg|name=Saturday Night Live|video=FaOSCASqLsE|text=Star Wars Undercover Boss: Starkiller Base - SNL|archivedate=20200808180540}}</ref> and again, disguised as Randy the Intern, in its 2020 follow-up "Undercover Boss: Where Are They Now".<ref name="Undercover 2">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=channel/UCqFzWxSCi39LnW1JKFR3efg|name=Saturday Night Live|video=Brbrdnh74yA|text=Undercover Boss: Where Are They Now - SNL|archivedate=20200804080221}}</ref>
