'''A1-A1''', also known as '''Ay-One-Ay-One''', was a [[protocol droid]] who oversaw daily functions at the [[Barsha family]]'s [[Barsha compound|compound]] on the [[moon]] [[Gus Treta]]. The droid provided [[Jaylen Vrax|Jaylen Barsha]] with news archives so he could search for his missing brother [[Sliro Barsha]].<ref name="Low Red Moon" />
