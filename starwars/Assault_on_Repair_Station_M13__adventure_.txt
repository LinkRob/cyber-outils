"'''Assault on Repair Station M13'''" is a [[roleplaying game]] adventure, and part of "[[The Long Shot Campaign]]." It was first published in the ''[[Star Wars Campaign Pack]]'' in [[1988]] and reprinted in ''[[Classic Campaigns]]'' in [[1994]].

==Plot summary==
In the adventure, the players take on the role of [[Green squad]], part of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic's]] [[The Irregulars|Irregular]] unit, [[Reekeene's Roughnecks]]. The squad is tasked with the destruction of Repair Station [[M13]] on [[Mycroft]] as part of [[Operation Retribution]].<ref name="Assault">{{WEGCite|story=Assault on Repair Station M13 (adventure)|book=Star Wars Campaign Pack}}</ref>

==Continuity==
First published in ''[[Star Wars Campaign Pack]]'' in [[1988]], "Assault on Repair Station M13" was later republished in ''[[Classic Campaigns]]'' in [[1994]]. When the adventure was reprinted, there were several minor changes made to the text. These were:
*A reference to "[[corvette-class patrol boat]]s" was replaced with a reference to "multi-system patrol boats."
*[[Stormtrooper/Legends|Stormtroopers]] were originally described as carrying heavy [[Blaster/Legends|blasters]]; this was changed to them carrying [[Blaster rifle/Legends|blaster rifles]].
*The stormtroopers were described as having [[Landspeeder/Legends|landspeeders]] armed with blaster rifles; this was changed to the landspeeders being armed with [[Repeating blaster/Legends|repeating blasters]].
*Reference to [[Mycroft]] as an "Earth-type planet" is changed to "terrestrial planet"
*Reference to a creature's "12-inch poisonous fangs" is changed to "30 centimeter long poisonous fangs"
