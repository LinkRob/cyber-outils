'''Candace Payne''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan who gained online viral fame as '''Chewbacca Mom'''. Her video, showing her wearing a talking [[Chewbacca]] mask and joyfully laughing as it spoke when she moved her mouth, was posted on [[May 19]], [[2016]] to Payne's Facebook page and has since been viewed over 140 million times.

=="Chewbacca Mom" video==
{{Quote|I'm such a happy Chewbacca!|Candace Payne, the "Chewbacca Mom"|<ref name="Chewbacca Mom Video" />}}
On [[May 19]], [[2016]], Candace Payne posted a video on her [[Wikipedia:Facebook|Facebook]] page after purchasing a [[Chewbacca]] mask at a Kohl's retail store. The video depicts Payne showing viewers the mask, which growls and speaks like Chewbacca when the wearer moves their mouth. As Payne does so, she begins to laugh hysterically over the sounds the mask makes.<ref name="Chewbacca Mom Video">{{Facebook|username=candaceSpayne|url=candaceSpayne/videos/10209653193067040|name=Candace Payne|date=2016-05-19|archivedate=20200817064759}}
</ref> Payne, a stay-at-home mom, posted the video a few minutes before picking up her children from school because she wanted to show her Facebook friends that the mask was hers&mdash;knowing full well that her children would likely want to claim it as their own. After posting the video, Payne was surprised to see that the video had racked up 1,000 views. By the end of the day on May 19, the video had been viewed over one million times.<ref name="Late Late Interview" /> As of [[May 24]], only five days after the video was posted, the video has over 140 million views.<ref name="Chewbacca Mom Video" />

On [[May 23]], Payne was a guest on ''The Late Late Show with James Corden''. They filmed a sketch in which Payne was driving host James Corden to work while wearing the Chewbacca mask. They were joined by [[J.J. Abrams]], the director of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'']]. Corden also read Payne a note from [[Peter Mayhew]], who plays Chewbacca in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' films, calling her an inspiration and inviting her to a private meet-and-greet at Dallas Fan Expo.<ref name="Late Late Interview" />

[[File:Candace_Payne_on_The_Star_Wars_Show.png|thumb|right|250px|Candace Payne on ''The Star Wars Show'']]
On [[May 25]], Payne appeared on the third episode of ''[[The Star Wars Show]]'' in an interview with the show's co-host, [[Andi Gutierrez]], to discuss her ''Star Wars'' fandom, her "Chewbacca Mom" video, and her tour of the [[Lucasfilm]] headquarters in San Francisco, California.<ref name="Star Wars Show">{{StarWarsShow|-IVW0aIO2Lg|Chewbacca Mom, Rogue One Character Reveal, A Star Wars Birthday Party &#124; The Star Wars Show}}</ref>

Her phenomenon was parodied in the [[2018]] [[W:c:disney:Disney Animated Canon|Disney animated]] film [[Wikipedia:Ralph_Breaks_the_Internet|''Ralph Breaks the Internet'']], the sequel to [[2012]]'s [[Wikipedia:Wreck-It-Ralph|''Wreck-It-Ralph'']]. In the film, before Ralph and Vanellope meet her and while determining which videos must be on BuzzzTube, Yesss and her assistant Maybe see a video entitled "Chewbacca Dad".<ref name="RBtI">{{VisualMediaCite|title=[[Wikipedia:Ralph_Breaks_the_Internet|''Ralph Breaks the Internet'']]|director=[[Wikipedia:Rich Moore|Rich Moore]] and [[Wikipedia:Phil Johnston (filmmaker)|Phil Johnston]]|prod=[[Wikipedia:Walt Disney Animation Studios|Walt Disney Animation Studios]]|year=[[2018]]}}</ref>
