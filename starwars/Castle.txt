{{Otheruses|Castle}}
[[File:Maz_Kanatas_castle.png|thumb|347x347px|[[Takodana Castle]]]]
A '''castle''' was a kind stationary [[stone]] structure built on the surfaces of [[planet]]s, used at least a [[millennium]] before [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> One such [[Takodana Castle|castle]] was built on the planet of [[Takodana]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] and was owned by the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]], who established it as a safe haven for all kinds of [[Species|individuals]], prior to its [[Battle of Takodana|destruction]].<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref>{{Struct-stub}}
