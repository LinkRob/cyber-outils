'''David M. Buisán''' is a [[Wikipedia:Spain|Spanish]] illustrator based in [[Wikipedia:Barcelona|Barcelona]]. He creates the covers and inside illustrations for the ongoing ''[[Star Wars: Adventures in Wild Space]]'' junior novel series published by [[Egmont UK Ltd]].
