'''Docha Institute''' was an institute located on<ref name="TRS" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="AcolyteVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> astronomical object [[Dunnak]] during the [[High Republic Era]] that hosted [[Docha Institute's medical library|one]] of [[the galaxy]]'s best medical [[Library|libraries]]. While aboard the [[Elite-class vessel|''Elite''-class]] research [[Starship|vessel]] ''[[Innovator]]''<ref name="TRS" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] thought about the ship's state-of-the-art facilities, including a medical library he felt was second only to Docha Institute's.<ref name="TRS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Docha Institute was mentioned in the novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="TRS" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's Phase I and published on [[June 29]], [[2021]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> Prior to its release, the institute's mention was included in a [[StarWars.com]] preview for the novel on [[May 21]] of that year.<ref name="Excerpt">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-high-republic-the-rising-storm-exclusive-excerpt-3|text=Bell Zettifar is at War with His Emotions in ''Star Wars: The High Republic: The Rising Storm'' - Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref>
