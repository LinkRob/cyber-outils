A '''Frequency Agile subspace transceiver''' was a type of [[subspace transceiver]] that was manufactured by [[Chedak]]. It was relatively common, and allowed for faster-than-light audio, video and [[hologram]] communications. It had a [[Subspace radio antenna|subspace antenna]] that was 12 [[kilometer]]s of ultrathin, tightly wound, superconducting wire that enabled the [[subspace]] transceiver to transmit with a range of forty [[light-year]]s. The ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''{{'s}} Frequency Agile subspace transceiver was equipped with a [[Carbanti]] [[Whistler encryption module/Canon|Whistler encryption module]] in order to receive and send encrypted messages.<ref name="OWM" />
