The '''escape from the Senate''' was an incident during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] in [[2 BBY]] that saw [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]], the representative of the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Chandrila]] and a secret organizer of the growing rebellion against the Empire, denounce [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and flee the Imperial [[capital]] world of [[Coruscant]].

==Prelude==
===The rise of Mon Mothma===
[[File:Costume-MonMothmaGoldandBlueDress.png|thumb|left|270px|Senator Mon Mothma (pictured) would emerge as the central figure of the Alliance to the Restore the Republic after her years in the Senate.]]
[[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] was a [[human]] [[Politics|politican]] who represented the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Chandrila]] as a [[senator]] in the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="RogueOneVD">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> beginning her career as a teenager<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> and following the example of her politician parents. As a [[Loyalist]] who adhered to the Republic's ideals, she would find herself opposing [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]'s expansion of executive power during the [[Clone Wars]] waged against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="RogueOneVD" /> from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> In what would become the final weeks of the war,<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Mothma helped organize the [[Cantham House meetings]] with likeminded Senator [[Bail Prestor Organa]]<ref name="RogueOneVD" /> in order to finalize the [[Petition of 2,000]], which would demand Palpatine relinquish his emergency powers and work towards a ceasefire.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> In these meetings, the conversation would turn to venues outside of the Senate, thereby planting the seeds of an eventual rebellion and leading to discussions on how to structure that rebellion.<ref name="RogueOneVD" />

Despite the issuance of the Petition,<ref name="RogueOneVD" /> Palpatine&mdash;secretly the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]], who was working to [[Grand Plan|overthrow]] the Republic to replace it with his own [[Galactic Empire]]&mdash;would finalize his plans and reform the Republic into his new regime in 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Mission to Mustafar|ending the Clone Wars]] with the promise of a peaceful, secure society. In the new reality that was the [[Imperial Era]], Mothma continued her struggle to oppose Palpatine's increasingly authoritarian rule. To that end, she continued to work with Organa and also met with [[Partisans|Partisan]] leader [[Saw Gerrera]],<ref name="MaskFear">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> who would go onto become known as a violent rebel leader.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Together, the three would form the three main architects of the eventual [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]],<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/735101/star-wars-the-mask-of-fear-reign-of-the-empire-by-alexander-freed|text=Star Wars: The Mask of Fear (Reign of the Empire)}}</ref> which would one day wage [[Galactic Civil War|war with the Empire]] in the name of restoring the Republic.<ref name="RogueOneVD" />

While she had hope of resolving the battle against Palpatine's [[Imperial Senate]],<ref name="Secret Cargo">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref> with legislative initiatives such as the [[Imperial Rebirth Act]],<ref name="MaskFear" /> she helped organize paramilitary strikes against the Empire with Organa and attempted to unite those assorted rebel cells to more effectively battle the Empire, forming a group she started to call "the Alliance."<ref name="Rebel Files" /> She also began working with an antiques dealer named [[Luthen Rael]], helping to fund his [[Axis network|rebel network]]. Despite facing difficulties in her home life with her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Perrin Fertha]]<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> and daughter [[Leida Mothma|Leida]], whom she would be forced to [[Wedding of Stekan Sculdun and Leida Mothma|marry away]]<ref name="Harvest">{{Andor|Harvest}}</ref> in [[4 BBY]]<ref name="Costumes">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-costume-design|text=The Costumes of ''Andor'' Season 2}}</ref> in order to secure funds for Rael's network,<ref name="Daughter">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> Mothma continued her work in the Senate<ref name="Secret Cargo" /> and with the growing rebellion,<ref name="Rebel Files" /> all while the [[Imperial Military]] carried out a series of actions that, to Mothma, were increasingly outrageous,<ref name="RogueOneVD" /> culminating in an [[Ghorman Massacre|incident]]<ref name="Secret Cargo" /> on the planet [[Ghorman]] in [[2 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" />

===The Ghorman crisis===
{{Main|Ghorman Debates|Suppression of Ghorman|Ghorman Massacre}}
[[File:GhormanMassacre-AndorRebelionDigitalZine.png|thumb|right|300px|The people of Ghorman rallied against the Galactic Empire in an event that would devolve into what became known as the "Ghorman Massacre."]]
Ghorman was a planet<ref name="Timelines" /> in the [[Colonies]] region of [[the galaxy]]<ref name="Galaxy Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> that had been the site of increasing struggle between local resistance elements and the Empire.<ref name="WFE">{{Andor|What a Festive Evening}}</ref> As far back as 19 BBY,<ref name="19BBY">"[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]" dates itself to [[3 BBY]] and establishes the [[Tarkin Massacre]] occurred sixteen years prior, putting that event in [[19 BBY]].</ref> [[Ghorman (people)|Ghormans]] had protested the Empire, which resulted in the [[Tarkin Massacre|massacre of five hundred unarmed protestors]] on the order of [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman">{{Andor|Ever Been to Ghorman?}}</ref> in that year.<ref name="19BBY" /> In the Senate, Mothma stood as a defender of the Ghorman people<ref name="The Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref> in the so-called [[Ghorman Debates]]<ref name="TRAFOTGE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> and the struggle against the [[Public Order Resentencing Directive]].<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" />

By [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Costumes" /> the Empire's interest in Ghorman has been deepened thanks to the discovery of the [[kalkite]] mineral under its crust. In preparation for an invasive [[mining]] operation, the Empire plotted out the [[suppression of Ghorman]] that would remove the population and turn the galaxy against the Ghormans to provide the regime's actions with a positive public perception.<ref name="One Year Later">{{Andor|One Year Later}}</ref> In secret, the Empire encouraged the [[Ghorman Front]]'s local resistance efforts<ref name="WFE" /> in order to give itself an excuse to enhance its control over Ghorman.<ref name="One Year Later" /> The Ghorman Front also came to the attention of Rael, who hoped to use the Empire's crackdown on such a notable world as a springboard to bring about the wider, galactic rebellion.<ref name="WFE" /> In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> thousands of peaceful protestors on Ghorman began a procession against the Empire.<ref name="TRAFOTGE" /> Imperial [[troop]]s responded to the peaceful protests by attacking those gathered, committing an act remembered as the [[Ghorman Massacre]].<ref name="Timelines" />

===Preparing a speech===
Word of the Ghorman Massacre reached Coruscant, the Imperial Senate, and, with it, Mothma,<ref name="Welcome" /> who was outraged.<ref name="RogueOneVD" /> The senator for Ghorman, [[Dasi Oran]], was [[Arrest of Dasi Oran|arrested]] by Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s. Dragged away by the troopers, Oran called out to the surrounding senators to watch what was happening to him and "remember Ghorman", shocking Mothma and fellow senator [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] who were standing nearby.<ref name="Welcome" /> Believing she had no other option,<ref name="RogueOneVD" /> Mothma decided to take to the Imperial Senate<ref name="Rebel Files" /> to publicly denounce Palpatine<ref name="RogueOneVD" /> in front of the assembled legislature<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and over the [[HoloNet]].<ref name="RogueOneVD" /> Understanding that her career on politics would be over by then, she urged Organa to escape with her to [[Yavin 4]] after the speech, an offer which he refused, as he believed one of them should stay behind and prepare the ground for a rebellion from the inside. However, he offered to prepare an exit plan for Mothma, tasking members of [[Bail Organa's resistance movement|his resistance movement]] to smuggle her off-world. He also mentioned that a bug had been found on senator [[Ton]]'s office, warning Mon to be careful while preparing the speech.<ref name="Welcome" />

[[File:MothmaSpeechwriting-WttR.png|thumb|300px|left|Mothma worked on her speech throughout the night.]]
Following their conversation, Mon returned to [[Mon Mothma's Senate office|her office]] in the [[Imperial Executive Building]], where her aide [[Erskin Semaj]] scanned the room for bugs, discovering one which Mothma immediately destroyed, alerting the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]. [[ISB Attendant]] [[Felzonis]], the officer in charge of monitoring, immediately notified the [[Supervisor]] in charge, [[Lagret]], who arrived to the [[Imperial Security Bureau control center|ISB control room]] to check on the situation. Lagret contacted [[Exmar Kloris]], Mothma's personal driver and and ISB agent, who reported that Mothma had stayed in the Executive building for the night instead of returning to the [[Chandrilan Embassy]]. As Mothma was a high-priority observation target and the ISB had been warned of paying particular attention to the Senate following the Ghorman massacre, Lagret took special interest on the situation and warned Kloris to stand by.<ref name="Welcome" />

Despite having destroyed the bug, Erskin warned Mon that the Empire could have planted more and that she should be particularly careful. Mothma thus decided to take a walk and practice her speech in the Senate plaza. While there, she was met with [[Luthen Rael]], her rebel ally and head of the [[Axis network]], who told her that he knew about Bail Organa's plan to extract her and that his team was already compromised. Mothma asked him how he had found her, forcing Rael to admit that Erskin had been a spy working for Axis. Feeling betrayed, Mothma started to walk away, but Rael stopped her and told her he had his own agent ready to extract her the next day; she would recognize him when he would say to her he has "friends everywhere." Mothma returned to her office, where Erskin told her he had finished scanning and removed all bugs, to which Mothma simply replied that he was fired and asked him to immediately leave the office.<ref name="Welcome" />

==The escape==
===Denouncing the Emperor===
[[File:BailInvokesArticle17252-WttRE.png|thumb|300px|right|Bail Organa invoked Article 17-252 to allow Mothma to address the senate.]]
The next day, the Senate was assembled in a special session to follow up on the events on Ghorman. Several prominent senators made speeches honoring the memories of the "imperial martyrs" and justifying the Empire's response; it had been predetermined that Mothma would not be allowed to speak. However, [[Erveen]] used her privileges as [[Senior Senator]] to yield her allotted time to Organa. He, in turn, invoked [[Article 17-252]] which stated that, in an emergency, a Senior Senator could yield his full time to a fellow senator, who would be allowed to speak freely and without interruption. As Senator [[Karloo]] had already used the term "emergency" in his own speech, and there were no objections, Organa's argument was accepted by the [[Oathkeeper]] and Mothma was allowed to speak.<ref name="Welcome" />

In the widely transmitted statement that she aimed directly at the [[Emperor]],<ref name="RogueOneVD" /> Mothma decried him as a monster who had taken advantage of a lack of truth in order to spread his own narrative and commit genocide on Ghorman, as well as castigating the Senate for empowering Palpatine in the first place.<ref name="Welcome" /> Mothma's speech shocked the Senate,<ref name="Secret Cargo" /> with many shouting that she was a traitor and demanding her to be silenced.<ref name="Welcome" /> Mothma regarded her comments as a call-to-arms for the scattered rebel cells, hoping it would serve as a rallying cry that could bring the cells together as a formal alliance.<ref name="Secret Cargo" /> Furthermore, she knew that, by leaving the Senate, she could more effectively lead the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

Immediately as Mothma was given the floor, Lagret ordered the Senate broadcasting be severed; however two techs had already locked the door to the transmission and power system as per protocol, forcing the officer in charge to have to run to the security office to retrieve the sequence key. Ultimately, the techs managed to delay him enough for the entire speech to be broadcast, and the footage was cut just as Mothma finished her speech.<ref name="Welcome" />

===On the run===
====The hunt begins====
[[File:Andor-SpeederEscape.png|thumb|left|250px|Cassian and Mon Mothma flee the Imperial Senate Building aboard a JPP-192 limospeeder]]
Mothma's speech was the moment that marked the end of her double life.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Mothma's comments immediately resulted in her being placed at the top of the Empire's "Most Wanted" list<ref name="RogueOneVD" /> and charged with treason,<ref name="Secret Cargo" /> with Supervisor Lagret ordering her immediate arrest. As Mothma exited her Senate pod, she was met with Luthen Rael's agent, [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], who was undercover as a [[journalist]]. Andor revealed his identity to her through his codephrase and the two made their way to a [[turbolift]]. Andor also told Mothma that he knew and worked with her [[cousin]] [[Vel Sartha]] on the [[Aldhani]] [[Aldhani heist|heist]]. He acknowledged how ruthless Rael could be, but insisted that if his claims about Organa's team being compromised were correct, going with him was her only option. Before they exited the turbolift, Andor pulled out his [[Bryar pistol]] and told Mothma to simply walk ahead of him, promising that he'd have her back.<ref name="Welcome" /> 

As Mothma and Andor exited into the main hall, they were swarmed by a group of journalists with questions on Mothma's speech. [[Beska]] and [[Selko]], two of the three members of Bail Organa's group sent to rescue Mothma, approached them; unbeknownst to Selko, Beska has already killed the third member of the group, [[Charval]], when he discovered her identity as an ISB operative. When Beska attempted to convince Mon Mothma they were there to help, Erskin, sensing something wrong, intervened and told Beska they did not need her help. Beska immediately pulled out her [[blaster pistol]] and forced everyone on the ground, much to Selko's shock, who demanded to know what she was doing. Beska then declared this was an ISB-sanctioned arrest, advising [[Senate Security]] to contact a supervisor. Beska then ordered Andor to move away from Mon Mothma, to which Andor responded by standing his ground. Erskin then shouted that Beska was a rebel spy, and as Beska took aim at him, Andor pulled out his weapon and shot her before she could shoot Erskin, leaving the room in shock. Erskin then quickly grabbed Beska's weapon and aimed it at Selko, unsure of his loyalties. Andor too approached Selko demanding to know who he was with. Selko stated he had just met Beska and was "nobody". As Andor and Erskin planned their next move, Selko attempted to explain he was not ISB but was quickly shut down by Andor, who ordered him on the ground. Despite her protests, Andor grabbed her arm and led her to the loading docks, leaving Mothma's senator cloak behind while Erskin stayed to guard Selko for a few minutes and ensure he was not ISB.<ref name="Welcome" />

As Andor and Mothma were making their way to the docks, the building was put into lockdown and surrounded by [[stormtrooper]]s who blocked all exits and forced Rael and his assistant [[Kleya Marki]], who were waiting to receive Mothma, to escape. The main entrance was also shut down, and all journalists and senators were confined inside. Andor and Mothma were forced to make a run for the open Imperial Plaza and the senate [[limousine]] pool, where they were confronted by Kloris. Upon learning his name from Mothma, Andor pretended to be an Imperial ally who had just caught the rogue Senator, catching him off-guard, before Andor shot the agent, and stole Mothma's [[JPP-192 limospeeder]],<ref name="Welcome" /> fleeing the Senate<ref name="Fully">{{StoryCite|story=Fully Operational (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> and bringing her to the [[Axis network safehouse]]. Lagret was then contacted by ISB Colonel [[Wullf Yularen]], who demanded an immediate explanation for how Mothma had managed to evade capture.<ref name="Welcome" />

====Escape from Coruscant====
[[File:RebelFiles-ISBWarrant.png|thumb|right|300px|An ISB citizen's notice for Mothma]]
Once safely in the [[Axis network safehouse|safehouse]], Mothma and Erskin were handed over to the [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]], who escorted them<ref name="Welcome" /> off Coruscant<ref name="Rebel Files" /> aboard Mothma's personal [[shuttle]] ''[[Chandrila Mistress]]''.<ref name="TS Databank">{{Databank|taylander-shuttle|Taylander shuttle}}</ref> The Imperial [[HoloNet News]] would go on to report the story as an act of treason.<ref name="Secret Cargo" /> In response to Mothma's escape, the Imperial Security Bureau issued a citizen's alert calling on the galactic populace to look out for and report any information about Mothma's movements, promising further updates were forthcoming.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

[[General]] [[Cassio Tagge]] regarded Mothma's escape from the Imperial Senate as a dramatic affair that had opened up the possibility of the Senate revolting against Palpatine.<ref name="Fully" /> [[Agent]]s of the Imperial Security Bureau made their way to [[Cantham House]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Senator Organa's private Coruscant estate,<ref name="RogueOneVD" /> to look for leads about Mothma's location. The ISB was correct to suspect those present, including Organa's daughter [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia]], knew something. While the ISB did question them, they walked away without any evidence.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Aftermath==
===Forming an Alliance===
{{Main|Operation Handoff|Declaration of Rebellion}}
====Meet up with Gold Squadron and second speech====
{{Quote|I name the Emperor himself for ordering the brutal attacks on the people of Ghorman. Their peaceful world is one of countless systems helpless under his oppressive rule. This massacre is proof that our self-appointed Emperor is little more than a lying executioner.|Mon Mothma|<ref name="Secret Cargo" />}}
While Semaj believed Mothma should retreat to Chandrila, Mothma was determined to reach the planet [[Dantooine]] for a meeting that would formally establish the Rebel Alliance. For the journey to Dantooine, designated [[Operation Handoff]], the [[BTL Y-wing starfighter]]s [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]]<ref name="Secret Cargo" />&mdash;a [[starfighter]] unit that attached to [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]]'s [[Massassi Group|rebel cell]]<ref name="Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=episodes/steps-into-shadow-trivia-gallery|text="Steps Into Shadow" Trivia Gallery}}</ref>&mdash;met up with the ''Chandrila Mistress'' to serve as Mothma's escort.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> After meeting up with Gold Squadron, Mothma delivered a further speech<ref name="ChangedSpeech">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/why-andor-diverged-from-canon-mon-mothmas-epic-senate-speech-11729531|text=Andor star Genevieve O'Reilly and creator break down Mon Mothma's epic Senate speech, diverging from canon|author=Maureen Lee Lenker|date=2025-05-07|work=''Entertainment Weekly''|archivedate=20250507183159}}</ref> on the [[HoloNet]]<ref name="Vader 54">{{DarthVaderCite|54|The Dark Side}}</ref> in which she decried Palpatine as a "lying executioner" who was imposing tyranny upon thousands of [[star system]]s, directly blamed him for the Ghorman Massacre, and accused him of being an "evil" that the galaxy could not allow to stand.<ref name="Secret Cargo" />

====Meet up with the Spectres====
During their journey, their ships moved to a pre-arranged meetup with the rebel [[freighter]] ''[[Ghost]]'' of the [[Phoenix Squadron]] as a refueling stop. However, an Imperial [[E-XD-series infiltrator droid]] had managed to transmit their location, leading to<ref name="Secret Cargo" /> an Imperial patrol<ref name="Rebel Files" /> crippling the ''Chandrila Mistress''. Mothma and Semaj thus boarded the ''Ghost'' and used the ship to travel to Dantooine, where Mothma issued the formal [[declaration of the Rebel Alliance]]. With that, the Alliance to Restore the Republic was officially formed.<ref name="Secret Cargo" />

Shortly after, Mothma received word from her aide [[Hendri Underholt]], who had been offered transport offworld by Semaj. While Mothma had wanted to offer Underholt that chance herself, she believed it would have been unfair due to the danger associating with her after her speech posed, but Underholt was determined to stay by Mothma. Underholt would include that correspondence and other matters relating to the escape from the Senate in ''[[The Rebel Files]]'', a non-electronic guide to rebel history she was composing at Mothma's request.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

===Legacy===
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="IV Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> around the time of the [[dissolution of the Imperial Senate]], Tagge reflected on Mothma's escape from the Senate and the standing of other rebellious senators, such as Organa. However, news of the Senate's dissolution came as a shock to Tagge, who believed it was a necessary bureaucracy and that it had held back the rebellion.<ref name="Fully" /> During the [[New Republic]], the [[excavation on Durkteel]] uncovered ''The Rebel Files''. Organa&mdash;who by that point was general of the [[Resistance]]&mdash;wrote in her own commentary, including in relation to the escape from the Senate.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Mon Mothma's full speech==

And as I look around now, I realize I have almost no memories that pre-date my arrival and few bonds of affection that cleave so tightly. Through these many years, I believe I have served my constituents honorably and upheld our code of conduct. This chamber is a cauldron of opinions and we’ve certainly all had our patience and tempers tested in pursuit of our ideals. Disagree as we might, I am hopeful that those of you who know me will vouch for my credibility in the days to come. I stand this morning with a difficult message. I believe we are in crisis. The distance between what is said and what is known to be true has become an abyss. Of all the things at risk, the loss of an objective reality is perhaps the most dangerous. The death of truth is the ultimate victory of evil. When truth leaves us, when we let it slip away, when it is ripped from our hands, we become vulnerable to the appetite of whatever monster screams the loudest. This Chamber’s hold on the truth was finally lost on the Ghorman Plaza. What took place yesterday… what happened yesterday on Ghorman was unprovoked genocide! Yes! Genocide! And that truth has been exiled from this chamber! And the monster screaming the loudest? The monster we’ve helped create? The monster who will come for us all soon enough is Emperor Palpatine!''|attrib=[[Mon Mothma]]<ref name="Welcome" />}}

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:RebelFiles-MothmaSpeech.png|thumb|left|250px|Mon Mothma's speech in ''The Rebel Files'']]
Mon Mothma's escape from the Senate was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''.<ref name="RogueOneVD" /> It was first identified in "[[Fully Operational (short story)|Fully Operational]]," a short story by [[Beth Revis]] published as part of the anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''.<ref name="Fully" /> The aftermath of the escape was depicted through the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Secret Cargo]]," which included a hologram of Mothma giving a speech denouncing the Emperor<ref name="Secret Cargo" /> in what was originally intended to be her speech within the Senate.<ref name="ChangedSpeech" /> The escape was first fully depicted within the ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' episode "[[Welcome to the Rebellion]]."<ref name="Welcome" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Mon Mothma/Legends|Mon Mothma]] was established to have gone underground to resist the Empire in the [[1983]] [[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (novelization)|novelization]] by [[James Kahn]] of the [[original trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="ROTJ">[[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (novelization)|''Star Wars: Return of the Jedi'' novelization]]</ref> Her move was later described as an [[Mon Mothma's escape from Coruscant|escape]] from the [[Imperial Security Bureau/Legends|Imperial Security Bureau]] in ''[[The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook]]'' written by [[Paul Murphy]] and published for use with [[West End Games]] ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' in [[1990]]. The sourcebook stated that Mothma travelled "off-planet" during the escape rather than explicitly stating where she fled from.<ref name="Rebel Alliance Sourcebook">''[[The Rebel Alliance Sourcebook]]''</ref> The [[1993]] novella "[[The Farlander Papers]] by [[Rusel DeMaria]] later stated that she escaped from the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Capital/Legends|capital]],<ref name="Farlander">"[[The Farlander Papers]]"</ref> which was previously stated to be [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] by the [[1991]] novel ''[[Heir to the Empire]]'' by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Heir to the Empire">''[[Heir to the Empire]]''</ref> The [[2002]] reference book ''[[The New Essential Guide to Characters]]'' later explicitly confirmed that Mothma escaped from Coruscant.<ref name="NEGTC">''[[The New Essential Guide to Characters]]''</ref>

"Welcome to the Rebellion" featured a different speech from that what was featured in ''Rebels''. In an interview with ''Entertainment Weekly'', showrunner [[Tony Gilroy]] revealed he and writer [[Dan Gilroy]] had felt constrained by the ''Rebels'' speech and did not want to copy another creative's work. However, the two realized they could write a speech of their own and rationalize her statements in ''Rebels'' as being a message broadcast from aboard a starship after she met up with Gold Squadron.<ref name="ChangedSpeech" /> This was later affirmed by the reference magazine [[Star Wars: Darth Vader 54|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'' 54]], which confirmed the speech heard in ''Rebels'' was a second denunciation she made over the Holonet after her escape from the Senate.<ref name="Vader 54" />

Before the release of ''Andor'',<ref name="ChangedSpeech" /> the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' featured<ref name="Rebel Files" /> her speech from ''Rebels''<ref name="Secret Cargo" /> as her Senate speech. Depicted via a news story exclusively broken by the Imperial-loyal [[TriNebulon News]], the book featured Mothma standing beside a human and [[Twi'lek]] in the Senate. According to this source, the speech resulted in near-unanimous condemnation from her peers, with loyalist senators shouting down Mothma, but there were a few who applauded her comments, even if their applause were drowned out by the loyalists. Seemingly embattled, Mothma and her aides then departed from the Senate rotunda. Tribulon was then unable to receive comment from Mothma's office, with a representative simply saying the Senator could not be reached for comment.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
