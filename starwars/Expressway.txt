The '''Expressway''' was a dedicated way for [[airspeeder]]s on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="The Axe Forgets">{{Andor|The Axe Forgets}}</ref>

In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="AndorDate">{{AndorDate|105}}</ref> [[Exmar Kloris]] drove [[Mon Mothma]] and [[Perrin Fertha]] when they attended an event. On the way back Fertha and Mothma briefly discussed the latter's [[Chandrilan charitable outreach program|new foundation]] in the back and, after asking his wife for Kloris' name, Fertha requested the driver to take the Expressway.<ref name="The Axe Forgets" />
