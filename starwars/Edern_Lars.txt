'''Edern Lars''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] from [[Tatooine]]. He was the son of [[Gredda Lars|Gredda]] and [[Lef Lars]], and the younger brother of [[Cliegg Lars]]. Sometime prior to [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s [[Mission to rescue Shmi Skywalker Lars|mission]] to rescue [[Shmi Skywalker Lars]], Edern was [[Death|killed]] at age fourteen when he lost control of the [[Lars family|family's]] [[V-35 Courier]] [[landspeeder]].<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> He and his [[parent]]s were [[Burial|buried]] next to each other near the [[Lars homestead]].<ref name="SWCT" /> Cliegg's wife, Shmi, was later buried alongside his grave.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>
