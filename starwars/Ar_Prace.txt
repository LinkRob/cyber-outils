'''Ar Prace''' was a [[Fian]] who served as a maintenance [[supervisor]] aboard the [[Galactic Republic]] [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Due to her [[species]], Prace was more resistant to [[radiation]] than<ref name="Fallen Star" /> a [[human]].<ref name="Elzar Mann">''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]'' establishes that Ar Prace belonged to a [[Fian|species]] more resistant to [[radiation]] than [[Elzar Mann]], who ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' states is a [[human]].</ref> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> Prace was on the station's upper half when it was [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|sabotaged]] by the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s, and as it plummeted towards the [[planet]] [[Eiram]], the station split apart, with the top half disintegrating.<ref name="Fallen Star" />

The human [[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]] and the [[logistics droid]] [[JJ-5145]] attempted to reach the [[positional thruster]]s on the station's bottom half to free themselves from Eiram's [[Gravity|gravitational]] pull. Due to radiation, however, it was not possible for Mann to reach the [[Engine|thruster's]] controls or [[radiation suit]]s. In response, the Jedi Master asked JJ-5145 if any members of more radiation-resistant species were aboard. The [[droid]] mentioned Prace, briefly giving Mann hope, but noted she had been on the top half. Afterward, the [[Jedi]] mentally noted he had yet to reckon with Prace.<ref name="Fallen Star" />
