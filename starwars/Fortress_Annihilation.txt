The '''Fortress Annihilation''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Bounty Hunter (Imara Vex)|standard costume]]. The [[Clothing|outfit]] and [[armor]] featured a [[Color|pink]], brown, light gray, dark gray, and cream color scheme, with a glowing orange visor on the [[helmet]] and a dark triangular [[shoulder-mounted armament]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fortress Annihilation was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Imara Vex, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. Unlike most Costumes in the game, they possessed no direct Weapon Wrap counterpart, thus not being part of an in-game cosmetic set.<ref name="Hunters" />
