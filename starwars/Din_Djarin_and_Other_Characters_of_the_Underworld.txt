"'''Din Djarin and Other Characters of the Underworld'''" is a volume in the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series of reference books published by [[De Agostini]]. It was published on [[August 3]], [[2021]].<ref name="De Agostini" />

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''The last phases of the Galactic Civil War and the years after the fall of the Empire were a time when bounty hunters and all manner of criminals flourished like never before in the galaxy. A good example of this was Din Djarin, the man known to all as "the Mandalorian", who made a career as a bounty hunter and who would be involved in a rugged adventure when he agreed to recover a delicate merchandise that ended up turning out to be a living being. Years later, in the middle of the cold war between the First Order and the Resistance, these types of characters continued to proliferate everywhere and, in some cases, such as that of the ex-spice smuggler Zorii Bliss, they would play an important role in the events that eventually brought about the downfall of the Final Order.''<ref name="De Agostini" />
