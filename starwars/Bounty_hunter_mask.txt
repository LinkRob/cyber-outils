A '''bounty hunter mask''' was a type of mask that was worn by [[bounty hunter]]s. It was black in [[color]] with a narrow oblong above an indented rectangle and two protrusions on each cheek.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> one such mask had been stored in an underground room of the [[Guardhouse Ruins|ruins]] of a guardhouse in the [[North Jundland Wastes]] region of the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] visited the ruins and accessed the underground room via a vent. Inside she found and stole the mask.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bounty hunter masks appeared in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />  Prior to the game's release on [[August 30]], [[2024]],<ref name="Story trailer">{{SWYouTube|DUyk6D5rgcE|Star Wars Outlaws: Official Story Trailer}}</ref> a mask appeared in the game's "World Premiere Trailer" released on [[June 8]], [[2023]].<ref name="World Premiere Trailer">{{SWYouTube|XF0kMT39GNY|Star Wars Outlaws: World Premiere Trailer|sw_url=video/world-premiere-trailer-star-wars-outlaws}}</ref>
