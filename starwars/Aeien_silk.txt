'''Aeien silk''' was a variety of [[silk]] used to make [[clothing]]. [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]] wore a cloak of the cheapest aeien silk when he visited [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] in an attempt to convince him to invest in Tumperakki's [[Tumperakki Haulage Company|new shipping company]].<ref name="Fake It Till You Make It" /> The stylist [[Neshee]] sold [[Jailyn Cirri]] a cloak made out of the same material.<ref name="Into the Clouds" />
