'''Adam Christopher''' (born on [[February 2]], [[1978]] in [[Wikipedia:New Zealand|New Zealand]]) is a novel and [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Wikipedia:Author|author]]. For ''[[Star Wars]]'', he has authored the short stories "[[End of Watch]]," "[[The Witness]]," and "[[The Steadfast Soldier]]" for the ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]'' anthologies. In [[2019]], he penned the comic story "[[Tales from Wild Space: Alone in the Dark]]." Christopher was announced as the author for ''[[The Mandalorian: An Original Novel]]'', though the novel was ultimately cancelled.

Afterward, the author worked with the [[Del Rey|same publisher]] for the [[2022]] novel ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'', his first novel for the franchise. [[2025|Three years later]], Christopher penned the novel ''[[Master of Evil]]''. The [[Ghostfinder fleet]] first introduced in the [[2017]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', the [[Jedi]] [[Ma-Dok Risto]] first introduced in the 2022 novel [[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']], and the fictional [[artificial intelligence]] [[ADAM]]  that debuted in the [[2016]] novel ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' are named after him.

==Biography==
===Entry to the franchise===
{{Quote|I'm delighted to have been able to write a little story for Tallie. I thought she was a great character in The Last Jedi, and I'm kinda hoping she survived Kylo's attack on the hangar bay and will reappear in some form later. And, y'know, I have the perfect idea of how to bring her back&hellip;|Adam Christopher, not long after "Tales from Wild Space: Alone in the Dark"{{'s}} release|<ref name="SWA26 Article">{{WebCite|url=https://www.adamchristopher.me/index.php/2019/09/27/a-long-time-ago-in-a-galaxy-far-far-away-star-wars-adventures-26-is-out-this-week|text=A long time ago in a galaxy far, far away: STAR WARS ADVENTURES #26 is out this week!|author=Adam Christopher|date=2019-09-27|archiveurl=https://archive.is/jqtz2}}</ref>}}
Adam Christopher was born on [[February 2]],<ref name="Twitter" /> in [[Wikipedia:New Zealand|New Zealand]], though is now [[Wikipedia:Great Britain|Great Britain]]-based.<ref name="About Me" /> He has authored several works for the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise<ref name="Twitter" /> and been a lifelong fan.<ref name="CNET" /> The [[2016]] novel<ref name="ALD">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'',</ref> ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'',<ref name="ADAM AI">{{Twitter|ghostfinder|status/1845749615162802669|Adam Christopher|quote=Actually one more thing - ADAM, from Life Debt, is based on my novel Made to Kill.|date=2024-10-14|archiveurl=https://archive.is/ndXFO}}</ref> written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the second installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]'',<ref name="ALD" /> introduces the fictional [[artificial intelligence]] [[ADAM]], which is based on Christopher's novel, ''Made to Kill''.<ref name="ADAM AI" /> His first work for ''Star Wars'' was "[[End of Watch]]," a short story published in the ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]'' anthology<ref name="EOW">{{StoryCite|story=End of Watch|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> that was published on [[October 3]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/jon-hamm-as-boba-fett-leads-an-all-star-cast-on-the-from-a-certain-point-of-view-audiobook-exclusive|text=''From a Certain Point of View'' Audiobook Exclusive - Jon Hamm (as Boba Fett!) Leads an All-Star Cast}}</ref> The author's involvement in the project was first announced via [[StarWars.com]] on [[April 7]] of the same year.<ref name="EOW Announce">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-from-a-certain-point-of-view-anthology-book-celebrates-40-years-of-a-galaxy-far-far-away|text=''Star Wars: From a Certain Point of View'' Anthology Book Celebrates 40 Years of A Galaxy Far, Far Away}}</ref> "End of Watch" centers around the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Commander]] [[Pamel Poul]], who served on the [[DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="EOW" />

[[File:StarWarsAdventures-26.jpg|thumb|right|270px|"Tales from Wild Space: Alone in the Dark," which was published in ''Star Wars Adventures'' (2017) 26 (cover pictured), marked Christopher's ''Star Wars'' comic debut.]]
Christopher also penned "[[Tales from Wild Space: Alone in the Dark]]," a comic story in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 26|twenty-sixth issue]] of [[IDW Publishing]]'s [[Star Wars Adventures (2017)|''Star Wars Adventures'']] comic series<ref name="Adventures 26">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=26|story=Tales from Wild Space: Alone in the Dark}}</ref> that was released on [[September 25]], [[2019]]. The story features the [[Resistance]] [[pilot]] [[Tallissan Lintra]],<ref name="Adventures 26" /> who the author enjoyed as a character in [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="SWA26 Article" /> a [[sequel trilogy]] film released on [[December 15]], 2017.<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-viii-to-open-december-15-2017|text=''Star Wars: Episode VIII'' to Open December 15, 2017}}</ref> Though Lintra [[Death|died]] in the film,<ref name="Film VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> Christopher expressed hope that she had survived and had story ideas on how to bring her back.<ref name="SWA26 Article" /> The [[Ghostfinder fleet]], was named after the author who uses the [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] username "Ghostfinder."<ref name="Ghostfinderfleet">{{Twitter|ghostfinder|status/912193698661888001|Adam Christopher|quote=Yep. Named after me :)|archivedate=20220620232624}}</ref> The [[fleet]] debuted in the novel ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', which was authored by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the final installment of ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''<ref name="EE">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> and released on [[February 21]], 2017.<ref name="AEE DATE">{{Penguin|url=books/531278/empires-end-aftermath-star-wars-by-chuck-wendig/9781101966969|text=Empire's End: Aftermath (Star Wars)}}</ref>

Christopher's short story, "[[The Witness]]," was published as part of the anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''<ref name="TESB">{{StoryCite|story=The Witness|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> on [[November 10]], [[2020]]. His involvement in the project was first officially revealed via StarWars.com on [[June 15]] of the same year,<ref name="Announce TW">{{SW|url=news/empire-at-40-from-a-certain-point-of-view-the-empire-strikes-back-authors-revealed|text=''Empire'' at 40 &#124; ''From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back'' Authors Revealed}}</ref> though had also been teased by the author on his website [[June 12|three days prior]]. Having enjoyed working on the first ''From a Certain Point of View'' anthology, he was pleased to be able to contribute to the second as well.<ref name="FACPOVESB Article">{{WebCite|url=https://www.adamchristopher.me/index.php/2020/06/12/so-i-wrote-a-star-war-again-fromacertainpovstrikesback|text=So I wrote a Star War&hellip; again&hellip; #FromaCertainPOVStrikesBack|author=Adam Christopher|date=2020-06-12|archiveurl=https://archive.is/ikhC9}}</ref> "The Witness" centers around the [[stormtrooper]] [[Deena Lorn]],<ref name="TESB" /> a character who first appeared in the [[1980]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back |''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="FILMV">{{Film|V}}</ref> Christopher created Lorn's stormtrooper designation as a reference to the writer [[Bria LaVorgna]],<ref name="Twitter 1">{{Twitter|ghostfinder|status/1313517111898234883|Adam Christopher|quote=YOU CANON NOW. {{C|In response to "THAT'S MY TKID"}}|archivedate=20201006194559}}</ref> who identifies as TK-27342 in the [[501st Legion (fan organization)|501st Legion]] fan organization.<ref name="Twitter 2">{{Twitter|chaosbria|status/798982959566983168|[[Bria LaVorgna]]|quote=I'm now ID-27342. I will give a gold star to whoever figures out why I picked those numbers. (Don't say if I already told you.)|archivedate=20201006194931}}</ref> The author enjoyed that the anthology's cover artist&mdash;[[Will Staehle]]&mdash;had also worked on several of his other original projects, finding it to be a coincidence.<ref name="FACPOVESB Article" />

===''Shadow of the Sith'' and later work===
{{Quote|So, this is a deep, secret, magic in-depth look at the writing process: I had a friend at school called Dathan. And I just thought it was a cool name. I think it's a more common name in the States, but I wanted something that was not super common, but also like an earthy name. Star Wars is that magical thing where there's a mix of kind of strange alien names and then you've got Luke and Ben. And when you dig into the books and the comics, you find even more examples.|Adam Christopher|<ref name="CNET" />}}
Christopher was slated to write ''[[The Mandalorian: An Original Novel]]'', originally set for release in [[December]] 2020 and later rescheduled to fall [[2021]]<ref name="Mando Article">{{WebCite|url=https://www.adamchristopher.me/index.php/2020/06/30/1264|text=Star Wars: The Mandalorian: An Original Novel – coming Fall 2021! [Update: book cancelled, new book coming]|author=Adam Christopher|date=2020-06-30|archiveurl=https://archive.is/SNFyk}}</ref> before its eventual cancellation by the publisher [[Del Rey]] on [[March 9]], 2021.<ref name="twitter1">{{Twitter|DelRayStarWars|status/1369332524288212997|[[Del Rey]]|quote=Due to the ever-expanding world of The Mandalorian, we will no longer be publishing THE MANDALORIAN ORIGINAL NOVEL at this time, as the story continues to unfold on screen.|archivedate=20210309180724}}</ref> His involvement in the project was announced on [[June 30]], 2020, simultaneously on StarWars.com<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-mandalorian-publishing-program-revealed-exclusive|text=''The Mandalorian'' Publishing Program Revealed - Exclusive}}</ref> and Christopher's website. During this time period, due to the [[Wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic|COVID-19 pandemic]], Christopher spent a lot of time rewatching ''Star Wars'' media.<ref name="Mando Article" /> Not long after the cancellation, the publisher confirmed that Christopher had been given the opportunity to write a new ''Star Wars'' novel.<ref name="twitter1">{{Twitter|DelRayStarWars|status/1369332524288212997|[[Del Rey]]|quote=Due to the ever-expanding world of The Mandalorian, we will no longer be publishing THE MANDALORIAN ORIGINAL NOVEL at this time, as the story continues to unfold on screen.|archivedate=20210309180724}}</ref><ref name="twitter2">{{Twitter|DelRayStarWars|status/1369332525005475847|[[Del Rey]]|quote=We love working with author Adam Christopher (@ghostfinder) and are already working with him on a different book. We're excited to share details with you in the future.|archivedate=20210415092507}}</ref>

[[File:Shadow-of-the-Sith-Cover.jpg|thumb|left|250px|''Shadow of the Sith'', which was authored by Adam Christopher]]
Christopher's next project for ''Star Wars'' was announced as ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' on [[October 7]], 2021, and later released on [[June 28]], [[2022]]. The story featured an adventure focusing on [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and his friend and former [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Alliance]] colleague [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian]],<ref name="New Books">{{SW|url=news/new-books-revealed|text=New Books Starring Luke and Lando, Obi-Wan and Anakin, and More Revealed &ndash; Exclusive}}</ref> in which the latter searches for his [[Gender|daughter]] [[Kadara Calrissian]].<ref name="SOS">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref> To promote ''Shadow of the Sith'', was interviewed by [[Brandon Wainerdi]] for the feature "[[Playing With Shadows]]," which was published in the [[Star Wars Insider 212|212th issue]] of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]''<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|212|Playing With Shadows}}</ref> on [[August 2]], 2022.<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=m/106-star-wars-insider-212|text=Star Wars Insider #212}}</ref> Earlier same year, he attended [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]]<ref name="Authors">{{Twitter|SW_Celebration|status/1640821106465726464|[[Celebration]]|quote=𝘚𝘵𝘢𝘳 𝘞𝘢𝘳𝘴 authors are coming to Celebration! Join them for multiple panels throughout the weekend for discussions on 𝘚𝘵𝘢𝘳 𝘞𝘢𝘳𝘴: The High Republic, Marvel Comics, Dark Horse Comics, and more!|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/7SYT1}}</ref> held from April 7 to [[April 10|10th]].<ref name="Twitter Announcement">{{Twitter|starwars|status/1531047358141587456|[[Star Wars]]|quote=Let's do it again next year. #StarWarsCelebration returns to Europe in 2023.|archivedate=20220530055619}}</ref> Christopher confirmed that Kadara's name was a reference to Lando's original name of "Lando Kadar" in [[Leigh Brackett]]'s script for ''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back''.<ref name="Kadara">{{Twitter|ghostfinder|status/1545480601595256833|Adam Christopher|date=2022-07-08|quote=@ElijahSawyer7 Yep.|archivedate=https://web.archive.org/web/20220708185727/https://twitter.com/ghostfinder/status/1545480601595256833}}</ref> He also named the [[Strand-Cast]] [[Dathan]] after a childhood friend named Dathan, wanting, a name that sounded earthly, like Luke and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Ben]], but not too common. He also named [[Miramir]] after [[Wikipedia:Miramar, New Zealand|Miramar]], a suburb of [[Wikipedia:Wellington|Wellington]], New Zealand. According to the author, he wanted a fantasy name to go with Miramir's [[homeworld]] [[Hyperkarn]], which is mysterious and has a twilight forest. The author typed in the name and switched around some of the letters, and thought the name Miramir had a magical quality, almost Elven.<ref name="CNET" />

[[Darth Voord]] was named in reference to the science fiction franchise [[W:c:tardis:Doctor Who|''Doctor Who'']],<ref name="Twitter Voord">{{Twitter|ghostfinder|status/1542785194796716035|Adam Christopher|date=2022-07-01|quote=Yep! And there are more&hellip;|archivedate=20220701102351}} {{C|In response to "Hello! Here I come with the improbable, trifle question of the day: did you mean it to be a Doctor Who reference when you mentioned a "Voord" in "Shadow of the Sith"? :-)"}}</ref> in which the [[W:c:tardis:Voord|Voord]] are a hostile alien species.<ref name="Keys of Marinus">{{WebCite|url=https://www.doctorwho.tv/stories/the-keys-of-marinus|text=The Keys of Marinus|date=2022-01-06|archivedate=20220415034741}}</ref> The [[Jedi]] [[Ma-Dok Risto]], who debuted<ref name="Mai-Dok Twitter">{{Twitter|ghostfinder|status/1524033574231580676|Adam Christopher|quote=I am pleased to report Team Trash Compactor is legit Star Wars canon! Also I officially hold rank and get to order @kierstenwhite around.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/LBCJV}}</ref> in the [[May 10]], 2022<ref name="Penguin">{{Penguin|url=books/678652/star-wars-brotherhood-by-mike-chen|text=Star Wars: Brotherhood}}</ref> novel [[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']] by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]], is a [[tuckerization]] of Christopher's name.<ref name="Mai-Dok Twitter" /> For the anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi]]'', Christopher authored the short story "[[The Steadfast Soldier]]," which focused on the [[Imperial officer]] [[Enric Pryde]].<ref name="TSS">{{StoryCite|story=The Steadfast Soldier|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref> The authors for the anthology were announced on March 9, [[2023]], and later published on August 2 of the same year.<ref name="FACPOVROTJ SW.COM">{{SW|url=news/from-a-certain-point-of-view-return-of-the-jedi|text=A Rebel Celebration on the Cover of ''From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi'' &ndash; Exclusive Reveal}}</ref> Christopher wrote a significant amount of Enric Pryde's backstory between both ''Shadow of the Sith'' and the "The Steadfast Soldier,"<ref name="SOS" /><ref name="TSS" /> describing Pryde as a "handsome chap" with a "bit of a chip on his shoulder"<ref name="Chip on shoulder">{{Twitter|ghostfinder|status/1710751463285399920|Adam Christopher|quote=Handsome chap. Bit of a chip on his shoulder though.|archivedate=20231008194056}}</ref> and a "loveable psychopath."<ref name="Loveable Psychopath">{{Twitter|ghostfinder|status/1819629712638881818|Adam Christopher|quote=Ah, Pryde, that loveable psychopath.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/rgY6B}}</ref> Agreeing with a post that heralded the "chilling and calculated" Pryde as one of the [[Star Wars saga|Skywalker Saga's]] best villains, Christopher confirmed he has further ideas for the character.<ref name="Such ideas">{{Twitter|ghostfinder|status/1736277716473712640|Adam Christopher|quote=Agreed! I have such ideas for him! {{C|I think General Pryde is one of the best antagonists in the Star Wars saga. He's so chilling and calculated}}|archiveurl=https://archive.is/yoO3v}}</ref>
