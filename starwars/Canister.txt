'''Canister''' was a special boon game piece used in the [[Canto Pass]] game. The piece was a narrow cylinder with a series of holes placed orthogonal and spanning approximately two thirds of its length. The pieces came in various shades including [[Color|blue]], green, and red. In Canto Pass, a single Canister was worth just one point, but two Canisters were worth an additional three points.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an individual named [[Wido]] had stashed three Canister pieces in a container on a hill west of a [[Unidentified settlement (Myrra Valley)|settlement]] in the [[Myrra Valley]] region of the [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Canister Canto Pass piece appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They are one of several Canto Pass pieces that could be collected in the game and used as trinkets to customize the appearance of the player's [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]] and the interior of the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', the player's [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
