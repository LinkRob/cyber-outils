The '''Dobatan''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] found within [[the galaxy]]. This [[humanoid]] species was distinguished by their short stature, with large heads, ribbed chins, downturned [[mouth]]s, wideset [[eye]]s, and large, pointed [[ear]]s.<ref name="BV" /> By around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> one Dobatan from [[Ylesia]], [[Weebahn]], had been a [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] working for [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi]], though had since gone missing.<ref name="BV" />
