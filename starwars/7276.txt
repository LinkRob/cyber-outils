'''7276''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] in the [[Trade Federation Droid Army]]. During the closing phases of the [[Battle of Naboo]], the [[battle droid]] approached [[Bombad General]] [[Jar Jar Binks]] and told him to surrender, which the [[Gungan]] quickly did. 7276 was deactivated, like all other Federation droids on the planet, when the [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Droid Control Ship]] orbiting [[Naboo]] was destroyed by [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="tpm" />
