'''Ceremonial sabers''' were [[sword]]s worn as part of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] dress uniforms at the [[Arkanis Academy]]. If a [[cadet]] was expelled from the Academy, their sword was broken as part of the expulsion.<ref name="SOTE">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The sabers appeared in the [[2015]] young-readers book ''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="SOTE" />
