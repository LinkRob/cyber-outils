The '''Annaj system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Moddell sector]], a [[subsector]] of the [[Zuma sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Appendix" /> at the end of the [[Spar Trade Route]] and containing the astronomical object [[Annaj]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> During the [[Battle of Endor]]'s final moments, after the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] and the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Executor]]'' were destroyed, [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Vice Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]] sent coordinates to all remaining Imperial [[Starship|vessels]] and ordered them to jump immediately to the Annaj system, effectively ending the battle.<ref name="Levers" />
