'''Fizzwater''' or '''fizz-water''' was a type of [[water]] that existed in [[the galaxy]]. Recirculated fizz[[water]] was an ingredient in the [[Red dwarf (alcohol)|red dwarf]], an [[alcohol]]ic beverage served in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="SG" /> It was often served with a [[jun-lime]] wedge. [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] often drank it while visiting [[The Lodge]]. The water was free, but the wedge was extra.<ref name="Tales from Vandor" />
