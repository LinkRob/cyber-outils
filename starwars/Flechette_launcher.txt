'''Flechette launchers''' were weapons that fired [[flechette]]s. [[Golan Arms]]'s [[FC-1 flechette launcher]] and the [[Salus Corporation]]'s [[DF-D1 Duo-Flechette Rifle]] were both models of flechette launcher.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" /> [[Werth Plouth]] used a flechette launcher during the [[Night of Sorrow]].<ref name="Path of Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Flechette launchers were first mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
