The '''Alignment Control Center''', also referred to as the '''Valley Control Center''', was a [[High Republic Era]] [[Scientist|scientific]] installation overlooking [[Rambler's Reach Valley]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Koboh]]. The towering center had a subterranean monitoring system and state-of-the-art security for its day, as well as an observation deck from which at least three massive arrays in the [[Koboh system]] could be controlled. It was considered the nerve center of research and experimentation on Koboh.<ref name="Survivor" /> 

In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="JSDate">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] spent time on Koboh investigating the hidden world of [[Tanalorr]] as a possible refuge from [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] oppression. After defeating [[Dark side of the Force|fallen]] [[Jedi]] [[Dagan Gera]] in [[Lightsaber duel|combat]], Kestis was betrayed by an [[Bode Akuna|ally and friend]] who stole the final remaining [[Abyss compass]], believed to be the only pathway to accessing Tanalorr. However, Kestis discovered an alternate pathway to Tanalorr within a message from [[Jedi Master]] [[Santari Khri]], the lead scientist of Jedi and [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] operations on Koboh during the High Republic Era. Master Khri explained that using the Alignment Control Center to activate the [[Forest Array]], [[Shattered Moon|lunar]] array, and the [[Koboh Observatory]] array, aligning them based on a set of [[Galactic coordinates|coordinates]] embedded in the [[Holomessage|message]], it would create a safe pathway through the [[Death|deadly]] [[Koboh Abyss]].<ref name="Survivor" /> 

Kestis returned to Koboh and entered the Alignment Control Center, only to be ambushed by [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]]. After defeating the enemy combatants, Kestis used [[the Force]] to remove the locks the Jedi had placed on an internal [[turbolift]] during their evacuation of the planet, and ascended to the observation deck. Khri had left behind audio messages in the facility to guide future Jedi towards proper alignment of the arrays. Once reaching the observation deck, Kestis was met by Master Khri's [[droid]] [[ZN-A4]], who stayed behind to operate the arrays while Kestis and the [[Stinger Mantis crew|crew of the ''Stinger Mantis'']] made the treacherous journey through the Koboh Abyss.<ref name="Survivor" /> 

Once activated, the Alignment Control Center coordinated the simultaneous firing of all three arrays into the Abyss, creating a relatively clear pathway. Nevertheless, there were still massive, volatile accumulations of [[Koboh Matter|Koboh matter]] which could prove deadly. Although making significant progress through the Abyss, the arrays soon began overloading, with ZN-A4 unable to help from within the Alignment Control Center. Kestis made a Force-guided short [[hyperspace]] jump, and the crew arrived in orbit over Tanalorr.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Alignment_Control_Center_Computer_Terminals_-_Survivor.png|thumb|The computer terminals in the subterranean portion of the Alignment Control Center on Koboh.]]
The Alignment Control Center first appeared in the [[2023]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'' by [[Respawn Entertainment]] as a playable area. Passage through the area was essential for completion of the main story of the game. Within the subterranean portion of the area with seven [[computer]] terminals. Upon successful completion of the puzzle in each Koboh [[Jedi Chamber]], another screen would turn from [[Color|red]] to green. Once all seven were completed, a small terminal would open, allowing [[BD-1]] to interact. This unlocked a [[map]] upgrade that revealed the locations of any remaining Force Essence points. Additional collectibles found in the area included the Harmony [[lightsaber]] [[Pommel cap|pommel]], a collectible [[Datacard|datadisc]], and a [[databank]] entry.<ref name="Survivor" />
