The '''Advozsec'''<ref name="SWHC1" /> (singular '''Advozse''')<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> were a [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[Volcano|volcanic]] [[planet]] [[Riflor]], located in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]. Thanks to their thick skin and sensitive [[eye]]s, they manage to survive in a harsh environment. On Riflor, [[Volcano|volcanic]] eruptions frequently destroyed Advozsec [[City|settlements]], so they tended to care very little about material goods. Instead, they strived to accumulate as much [[Currency|monetary]] wealth as possible so they could continually rebuild after disaster strikes.<ref name="Alien Archive" /> They also had pointed [[ear]]s and a single cranial horn at the crown of their heads.<ref name="ANH" /> Because they had to rebuild frequently, they tended to have a pessimistic outlook on life and a fixation on financial stability.<ref name="SWHC1" /> The [[mercenary]] [[Bom Vimdin]] was an Advozse.<ref name="AEYNTK" />
