'''Curtis''' was a [[Shistavanen]] [[Gender|male]] who worked as a [[Bouncer (occupation)|bouncer]] for the [[cantina]] inside the ''[[Serendipity]]'', a [[1550-LEX yacht]] owned by [[Delia Leighton]]. Curtis was sympathetic towards the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], and shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], he assisted [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] in rescuing the rebel [[Shrikes|Shrike]] operative [[Caluan Ematt]] from [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces on the [[planet]] [[Cyrkon]].<ref name="SR" />
