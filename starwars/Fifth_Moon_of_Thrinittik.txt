The '''Fifth Moon of Thrinittik''' was a terrestrial [[moon]] located in<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" /> the [[Thrinittik system]] of the [[Colonies]].<ref name="Timelines" /> It hosted a [[university library]], which was, at some point, partially destroyed during an attack. The [[scholar]] [[Korin Aphra]] undertook a mission with his daughter [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli]] to retrieve information about the [[Ordu Aspectu]] from the moon.<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" />
