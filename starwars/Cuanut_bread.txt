'''Cuanut bread''' was a type of [[bread]]<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> [[Diet|edible]] by [[human]]s.<ref name="Tasty" /> In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the section of ''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]'' which features cuanut [[bread]] to [[0 ABY]].</ref> [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Senior Lieutenant]] [[Iden Versio]] ate cuanut bread with [[butter]] for breakfast while staying at the [[Diplomat Hotel]] on the [[Planet|world]] [[Coruscant]] with her newly-assembled [[squad|team]], [[Inferno Squad]]. A short time later, Iden's [[Versio family|father]], [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]], ate cuanut bread alongside [[steak]] and roasted [[vegetable]]s as part of his lunch.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />

==Behind the scenes==
