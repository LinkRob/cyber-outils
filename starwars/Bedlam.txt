'''Bedlam''' was a mysterious [[planet]] of which little was known other than stories. [[Dok-Ondar]] once acquired a relic that was supposedly from the planet.<ref name="GEstaff">{{GEAttr|Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities}} has backstory elements that, though not directly published for the public, are officially [[canon]] details that can be obtained verbally from cast members working in the store.</ref> The [[Gen'Dai]] [[Rayvis]] led a marauder group known as the [[Bedlam Raiders]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[Wiktionary:bedlam|Bedlam]] is a noun meaning chaos and uproar, and originated as a term for the [[Wikipedia:Bethlem Royal Hospital|Bethlem Royal Hospital]] mental asylum in London.<ref name="Britannica">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/topic/Bedlam|text=Bedlam|author=The Editors of Encyclopædia Britannica|date=2013-09-13|work=[[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica|''Encyclopædia Britannica'']]|archivedate=20230920051852}}</ref>

Bedlam had its first appearance in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] comic story "[[Tilotny Throws a Shape]]" written by [[Alan Moore]] and originally published in Marvel UK's [[The Empire Strikes Back Monthly 154|''The Empire Strikes Back Monthly'' 154]].<ref name="comic">{{StoryCite|story=Tilotny Throws a Shape|book=The Empire Strikes Back Monthly 154}}</ref>
