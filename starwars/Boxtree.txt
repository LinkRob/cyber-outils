'''Boxtree''' was an imprint of [[Wikipedia:Macmillan Publishers|Macmillan Publishers]] based in London that specialized in film and TV tie-ins, illustrated books and humor. In cooperation with [[Lucasfilm]], they made "choose your own adventure"–style roleplaying books set in the ''Star Wars'' universe like ''[[Jedi Dawn]]'', ''[[The Bounty Hunter]]'', and the compilation ''[[The Lost Jedi Adventure Game Book]]''.

They also published trade paperbacks of the [[Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' comics like ''[[Star Wars: River of Chaos (TPB)|River of Chaos]]'' and ''[[Star Wars Droids: Season of Revolt (TPB)|Droids: Season of Revolt]]''.
