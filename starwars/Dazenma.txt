'''Dazenma''' was the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[planet]] [[Dekien]]. It contained a [[Dazenma spaceport|spaceport]] and was once visited by the former [[Sith]] warrior known as [[The Ronin]].<ref name="Ronin" />
