A '''flight''' to [[Batuu]] was undertaken by members of the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] (GSCE)<ref name="CargoBay" /> sometime after [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> The [[Unidentified ship (Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts)|starship]] held several creatures, including a [[porg]] named [[Penelope]], a [[dokma]] named [[Luna (dokma)|Luna]], a [[worrt]], a [[Kowakian monkey-lizard]], a [[dianoga]], and a [[kouhun]], all in the [[Zone AM|cargo bay]]. Other areas of the cargo hold below contained larger, more dangerous creatures such as a [[wampa]] and a [[rathtar]].<ref name="CargoBay" />

While handling the kouhun, GSCE member [[Quinn (Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts)|Quinn]] was bitten on the arm. [[Resistance]] operatives [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and [[Chewbacca]], who were secret stowaways on the ship en route to Batuu, arrived in time with medical supplies to save the handler. After some time, when [[droid]] [[BL-R4]] went inside the rathtar enclosure to feed it, the creature managed to escape and was set loose on the ship. Rey and Chewbacca successfully teamed up with Quinn and other crewmembers, some of whom were [[Force-sensitive]], to monitor the ship's cameras from the cargo bay in search of the rathtar, and to use [[the Force]] to lock down doors and corral the creature back into its cage.<ref name="CargoBay" />
