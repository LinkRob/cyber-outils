This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]]. [[Bhima Ook]] was a member of the species.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Biology and appearance==
This small centaur-like [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] was distinguished by their [[Color|blue]] and purple skin, with four [[Limb|legs]] ending in [[Bone|hooves]], as well as two hands, exposed [[Nose|nostrils]], wideset black [[Eye|eyes]], and a pair of long lobes dangling from their [[Mouth|upper lip]] that resembled a [[Hair|moustache]].<ref name="Survivor" />

==Species in the galaxy==
One member of this species, [[Bhima Ook]], had taken up residence on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]]<ref name="Survivor" /> by [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> partnering with fellow [[Tulli Mu's species|alien]] [[Tulli Mu]] to run a [[Holotactics]] game in [[Rambler's Reach Outpost]]'s [[Pyloon's Saloon]], and eventually befriending the [[Jedi]] survivor, [[Cal Kestis]].<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
The species made their debut in [[2023]] video game, ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-jedi-survivor-is-here|text=''Star Wars Jedi: Survivor ''Is Here!|date=2023-04-28|quote=''Star Wars Jedi: Survivor'', the sequel to 2019's modern classic ''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'', has arrived for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X and S, and PC.}}</ref>
