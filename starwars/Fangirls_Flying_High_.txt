"'''Fangirls Flying High!'''" subtitled "Flying High!," is an article that was published in the [[Star Wars Insider 151|151st issue]] of the magazine ''[[Star Wars Insider]]''. Written by [[Tricia Barr]], the six-page article explores the influence that female fans have had on the ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga.<ref name="SWI151" />
