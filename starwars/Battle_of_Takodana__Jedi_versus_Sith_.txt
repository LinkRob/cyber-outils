A '''battle''' was fought between the [[Jedi Order]] and the ancient [[Sith]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]] thousands of [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The [[Takodana Castle|castle]] that was later owned by [[Maz Kanata]] was built over the land where the battle was fought.<ref name="Helmet Collection 52" />
