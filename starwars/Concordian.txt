'''Concordian''' was a dialect of [[Mando'a]] spoken by individuals from [[Concordia]], a [[moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]].

The dialect was spoken by a [[Death Watch bomber|member]] of [[Death Watch]] after he [[Attack on the Memorial Shrine|bombed]] the [[Memorial Shrine]] in [[Sundari]], and again as he lay [[Death|dying]] following a [[Death|suicidal]] jump after [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] attempted to apprehend him for the [[terrorist]] act.<ref name="Mandalore Plot" />

==Behind the scenes==
The dialect spoken by the people of Concordia first appeared in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[2010]], when it was featured in the twelfth episode of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series' [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]], entitled "[[The Mandalore Plot]]."<ref name="Mandalore Plot" />

The [[Concordian/Legends|Concordian dialect]] was first established years prior in the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' article ''[[Guide to the Grand Army of the Republic]]'', co-written by [[Karen Traviss]] and [[Ryan Kaufman (real person)|Ryan Kaufman]]<ref name="GttGAR">''{{InsiderCite|84|Guide to the Grand Army of the Republic}}''</ref>―a source now considered part of the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Legends">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref>

At least one Concordian phrase from "The Mandalore Plot"—specifically the line exclaimed by the Death Watch bomber before [[Death|leaping]] to his [[death]], and featured as this article's main quote—appears to have been drawn from a [[2007]] post on the [http://www.ultimate-empire-at-war.com/forum|Ultimate Empire at War forum], in which a user had compiled a list of [[fanon]] words and translations for the fledgling [[Mando'a|Mandalorian language]].

The aforementioned quote, "''Calhava bru'chun dralshye'ran''," was translated in the post as "Compassionate leaders will burn."<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.ultimate-empire-at-war.com/forum/index.php?topic=912|text=Ultimate Empire at War forum|archivedate=20161124040112}}</ref>

Although the translation would seem contextually appropriate to the episode's storyline, to date there has been no official confirmation that this is the canonically accepted translation.
