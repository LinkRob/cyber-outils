'''Amber Wing''' was a [[fighter wing]] of [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|BTL Y-wing starfighters]] that served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] on [[Vrogas Vas]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]] and participated in the [[Battle of Vrogas Vas]].<ref name="DV14" />

==History==
{{Quote|Amber Wing. Clear to loose ordnance.''"<br />"''Wait&ndash;Imperials?''"<br />"''Look up! Scatter! Quic&ndash;|Amber Wing pilots, surprised by an Imperial attack|<ref name="DV14" />}}
[[File:AmberWingDestroyedByTIEs-2015DarthVader14.png|thumb|left|250px|Amber Wing attacked by Imperial TIE fighters]]
In [[0 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] became stranded on the [[planet]] [[Vrogas Vas]] during a [[Battle of Vrogas Vas|battle]] with [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] forces. Rebel [[commander]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] ordered numerous [[Alliance Army|Rebel forces]] to engage him. When Vader defeated the [[Rebel trooper|troops]] and approached Organa, she ordered Amber Wing's [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|BTL Y-wing starfighters]] to bombard her location in hopes of [[Death|killing]] Vader. However, Amber Wing was unable to carry out her orders when they were attacked by a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] squadron under the command of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] commander [[Karbin]].<ref name="DV14" />
