"'''Closed Circuits'''" is a short story in the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force campaign]] for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]''. It was written by [[August Hahn|August]] and [[Cynthia Hahn]] and was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[February 9]], [[2006]].

==Plot summary==
[[Gamma Squad (Galactic Republic)|Gamma Squad]], composed of five [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]] [[Clone commando/Legends|clone commandos]] is dispatched to infiltrate the [[Droid/Legends|droid]] manufacturing plant on [[Uffel]]. Fighting their way through numerous droids, they breach the command center and using its communications center, they transmit a signal that modified all droids produced by the factory to allow for remote [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] control.
