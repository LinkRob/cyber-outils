{{Quote|You're ''exactly'' the sort we need. ''[&hellip;]'' Now, some of your ''associates'' here might need a ''character reference'' or two, and of course the ''rotation general'' gets the final ''say''&hellip;|Bini, to Tam Posla and his associates|<ref name="Aphra 17" />}}
'''Bini''' was an [[alien]] [[Alliance officer|officer]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] who served as a senior crewmember aboard the ''[[Lucrehulk Prime]]'', a rebel [[flight school]], under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]]. When a [[mercenary]] [[Aphra's crew|crew]] led by the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] visited the [[starship]] around [[1 ABY]], Bini thought they were volunteers and gave them a tour of the flight school. The [[officer]] and Syndulla soon discovered that the visitors were mercenaries looking to make an offer, the latter having them held in their [[brig]].

Syndulla, accompanied by Bini and some [[Rebel trooper|soldiers]], later allowed the group to make their pitch, only for [[Sister Six|one]] of the mercenaries to [[stun]] the rebels so the group [[Capture of Hera Syndulla|could get away]] with the general as a [[Prisoner|captive]]. Left in charge, the alien sought to save their superior and [[Infiltration of Hivebase-1|followed]] the mercenaries to the [[Galactic Empire]] base [[Hivebase-1]], where the ''Lucrehulk Prime'' fell under attack by local [[scaraphyte]] creatures. Bini scrambled to combat the situation, only to be incapacitated by the Imperial infiltrator [[Magna Tolvan]].

Bini later regained consciousness and found the ''Lucrehulk Prime'' being remotely controlled to steal the [[datacore]] of Hivebase-1. Once Aphra and Syndulla returned to the flight school, the officer wanted to [[Death|kill]] the kidnapper, but the general instead let her go, impressed by the [[Thief|theft]] of the core. However, Aphra had also encrypted the data inside the core, prompting the rebels to go hunting for her again later. Bini informed Syndulla when a detonation was logged aboard the [[Volt Cobra|ship]] of their operative, the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]].

==Biography==
===New visitors===
{{Quote|Uh, ''R.G.?'' How many ''X-wings'' are you currently flying, ma'am? Just out of interest.''"<br />"Mmf&mdash;'' I don't know, six or seven? Does it ''matter?"<br />"''No, ma'am. Just, ah&hellip; got some ''new recruits'' waiting to see you out here.|Bini and Hera Syndulla|<ref name="Aphra 17" />}}
During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[alien]] Bini<ref name="Aphra 19">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 19|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 19]]</ref> served as an [[Alliance officer|officer]] in the [[Alliance Military]] and a senior member aboard<ref name="Aphra 18">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 18|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 18]]</ref> the ''[[Lucrehulk Prime]]'',<ref name="Aphra 21">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 21|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 21]]</ref> a [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship]] used as a [[flight school]] for the Alliance, serving under rotation [[general]]s.<ref name="Aphra 17" /> Around [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{Aphra2016Date|17}}</ref> while the [[Twi'lek]] [[Hera Syndulla]] was the rotation general, a [[Aphra's crew|group]] of visitors found the ''Lucrehulk Prime'' and were searched and cleared for [[weapon]]s by the rebels. While Bini thought they were recruits, the visitors were a [[mercenary]] crew led by the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]]. The crew, wishing to recover data from the Empire's [[Tarkin Initiative]] research and development complex [[Hivebase-1]], were there to [[Capture of Hera Syndulla|capture]] Syndulla, although one of them, the vigilante [[Tam Posla]], was oblivious to that plan and thought that they were recruiting the help of the Alliance.<ref name="Aphra 17" />

[[File:WelcomeFromBini-2016DoctorAphra17.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Bini welcomes the mercenaries, believing they are volunteers.]]
While Syndulla was helping trainee [[rebel pilot]]s from the ''Lucrehulk Prime''{{'s}} control core, Bini contacted the general, questioning how many training [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing starfighters]] she was controlling and informing her that a new batch of recruits had arrived. The [[officer]] concluded they would tell the visitors Syndulla was busy and went to the "recruits," asking how they had found the flight school. Posla, asserting<ref name="Aphra 17" /> himself<ref name="Aphra 25">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 25|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 25]]</ref> as the speaker of the group, explained that they had wanted to see the school for themselves, his associates specifying that they had used records from<ref name="Aphra 17" /> the late [[Wat Tambor]] of the [[Techno Union]].<ref name="Aphra 15">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 15|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 15]]</ref> Bini recognized Posla's past as a former [[Police officer|lawman]] of the [[Milvayne Authority]] and told the vigilante he was just what the rebels needed as the alien rebel shook his hand.<ref name="Aphra 17" />

===The kidnapping===
{{Quote|Folks, th-this is General ''Hera Syndulla''. [to Syndulla] Just some new ''volunteers'', ma'am. S-standard tour. Didn't mean to bother you.''"<br />"''We're not&hellip; ''actually''&hellip; volunteers.|Bini and Tam Posla|<ref name="Aphra 17" />}}
[[File:ScaredBini-2016DoctorAphra17.jpg|thumb|right|350px|Bini tries to stop Chelli Aphra from entering the control core.]]
Bini began showing the visitors around the ''Lucrehulk Prime'' ahead of their planned recruitment assessment. The officer showed the group to the control core, where Aphra's pet [[tooka]] [[Flufto]] ran into the room without authorization. The rebel tried to stop Aphra from chasing after, but the [[cyborg]] mercenary [[Caysin Bog]] began inspecting their [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] [[Limb|arm]], questioning its maker. Bio explosives in Flufto triggered the weapons alarm, much to the confusion of Bini as the visitors had been searched. Syndulla then intervened and [[Death|shot]] Flufto, and Bini subsequently introduced the "volunteers" to her. Posla finally told them that the group were not volunteering, but had a proposal of mutual benefit. The general immediately realized that they were actually mercenaries and had them sent to the [[Starship|vessel's]] [[brig]] at [[sector two]].<ref name="Aphra 17" />

During their [[time]] in the brig, the mercenaries were accidentally freed from their cells; one rebel on the bridge reported a blown coupling in sector two to Bini, doubting it would amount to more than a power surge, but alarms were raised when the [[prisoner]]s were able to step out of their cells. The mercenaries ultimately stayed put, earning some trust from Syndulla. The general, backed by Bini and a complement of [[rebel trooper]]s, gathered the visitors by their [[LAAT/le patrol gunship]] and gave them ten [[second]]s to plea for a reason not to be used as target practice. Aphra revealed the group's intention of [[Infiltration of Hivebase-1|infiltrating]] Hivebase-1, her associates claiming they had a foolproof plan to do so but needed the rebels' help. Syndulla gave the group a chance to tell their plan, but as Posla got to explaining, Bog launched a number of [[blaster]]s from his own cybernetic compartment. Bini realized that it was a trap as the [[Xexto]] mercenary [[Sister Six]] caught the blasters and [[stun]]ned all of the rebels in<ref name="Aphra 17" /> her<ref name="Aphra 15" /> vicinity, the officer and Syndulla included, in quick succession.<ref name="Aphra 17" />

===The real controllers===
{{Quote|I ''knew'' the general would find a way to activate her ''tracker!'' Commence attack on the kidnappers' vess&mdash;|Bini directs a rescue mission for Syndulla|<ref name="Aphra 18" />}}
The mercenaries proceeded to flee with the unconscious Syndulla, although Posla was angry about not having known the real plan.<ref name="Aphra 17" /> With the general captured, Bini was left in charge of the ''Lucrehulk Prime'', and had the vessel follow the [[Homing beacon|tracking beacon]] hidden inside Syndulla's [[Rebel command insignia|rank plaque]], not knowing that Aphra had intentionally activated it. The flight school exited [[hyperspace]] behind Aphra's [[gunship]] near Hivebase-1. Bini ordered the ship to attack Aphra's ship when the ''Lucrehulk Prime'' was suddenly swarmed by [[scaraphyte]]s, [[acid]]ic [[Realspace|space]]-borne creatures. With the scaraphytes corroding the flight school's [[hull]] and Hivebase-1 deploying [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], Bini ordered for the [[deflector shield]]s as well as the [[ion cannon]]s to be staffed and the rookie [[pilot]]s to be taken to the control core. They were continuously interrupted by the whistling of Aphra over the [[Comm unit|comms]], whom the alien demanded to stop.<ref name="Aphra 18" />

[[File:BiniSleepingTime-2016DoctorAphra18.jpg|thumb|left|350px|Bini is incapacitated by Magna Tolvan.]]
Aphra herself used the chaos to secretly fly her crew and Syndulla into the Empire's research complex. Bini started using the ''Lucrehulk Prime''{{'s}} control core to direct [[X-wing starfighter|X-wings]], but was unable to use the control codes, as Aphra had used her [[Slicer|slicing]] abilities to change them. The alien asked about the codes to their [[soldier]]s, only to turn around and find them incapacitated by the Imperial [[Lieutenant-Inspector]] [[Magna Tolvan]]. The Imperial infiltrator swiftly knocked Bini out and announced the commandeering of the flight school to Hivebase-1.<ref name="Aphra 18" />

The commandeering was short-lived, as Aphra had bugged the control core during the incident with the tooka, and began using the ''Lucrehulk Prime'' to ram Hivebase-1 and rip the [[datacore]] out with its [[tractor beam]], the remaining mercenaries and Syndulla residing within. Bini regained consciousness and was confused as to who was controlling the flight school, Tolvan having hidden in the vents as rebel reinforcements arrived at the core to assist the alien officer and their [[troop]]s. As that happened, the flight school escaped with the datacore as Hivebase-1 was destroyed as part of Aphra's plan.<ref name="Aphra 19" />

===The core problem===
{{Quote|I'm sorry. We logged a ''detonation'' aboard the ''Volt Cobra''. No contact with ''Sana Starros'' since.''"<br />"''Hand me the ''comms'', Bini.|Bini and Syndulla|<ref name="Aphra 22>[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 22|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 22]]</ref>}}
[[File:BiniWasNotSatisfied-2016DoctorAphra19.jpg|thumb|right|350px|Bini and Hera Syndulla find out the datacore is encrypted.]]
Syndulla and the remaining mercenaries made it off of the datacore and onto ''Lucrehulk Prime'', where Bini demanded that Aphra was seized. The general told her officer to hold it, denying their request to have the rogue archaeologist shot. Acknowledging Aphra's success in stealing the Hivebase-1 datacore, Syndulla ordered for her former kidnapper to be given a ship and taken out of her sight. Later on, the general and Bini began work to access the core themselves. As the officer began reading the data inside, they quickly found a [[ransom]]ed encryption for an untraceable [[bank]] account for Aphra.<ref name="Aphra 19" />

Syndulla therefore began a hunt for Aphra, hiring the [[smuggler]] [[Sana Starros]] to retrieve the rogue archaeologist after discovering the target had since been [[Prison|imprisoned]] in the Imperial [[wreckage-prison]] [[Accresker Jail]].<ref name="Aphra 21" /> After Starros' ship, the ''[[Volt Cobra]]'', was [[Attack on a pirate station|shot down]] during<ref name="Aphra 22" /> her<ref name="Aphra 23">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 23|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 23]]</ref> mission, Bini approached Syndulla with news of a logged detonation on the smuggler's ship, prompting the general to ask for the comms from them and attempt to make contact with her operative. Syndulla was unable to reach Starros initially,<ref name="Aphra 22" /> but the pair later liaised again when the smuggler helped evacuate the rebel forces from the planet [[Tiferep Major]] before Accresker Jail was forcibly [[Skirmish aboard Accresker Jail|crashed]] into the world by the Empire.<ref name="Aphra 25 />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|''[to Posla]'' But&hellip; it's the ''Rebellion! All'' the good guys want to join!''"<br />"''You didn't even say ''please."<br />"''Please?|Bini and Chelli Aphra|<ref name="Aphra 17" />}}
A [[humanoid]] alien, Bini had mud-[[Color|green]]-colored skin, red [[eye]]s, and black [[hair]] in a mustache. The officer was deceived by Chelli Aphra's mercenary crew, believing that they were volunteering to join the Rebel Alliance and attributing Tam Posla going rogue from<ref name="Aphra 17" /> the Galactic Empire&ndash;backed<ref name="Aphra 26">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 26|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 26]]</ref> Milvayne Authority as a good quality for a new rebel recruit. The alien even held their arm around the ex-lawman as they showed the visitors around the ''Lucrehulk Prime''. Bini was shocked by the revelation that they were not volunteering, arguing that all "good guys" like Posla would want to join the Rebellion. The rebel bought into Aphra's joke that they did not say "please" by stating the word straight after.<ref name="Aphra 17" />

[[File:GeneralHasBeenDisturbed-2016DoctorAphra17.jpg|thumb|left|350px|Bini was scared when realizing they had disturbed Syndulla.]]
Bini was intimidated by Hera Syndulla, stressing when she was interrupted by the mercenaries as they knew she did not like to be disturbed. After the general was kidnapped,<ref name="Aphra 17" /> the officer was determined to rescue her, following Aphra's ship to Hivebase-1. They were confused at Aphra's manipulations with the flight school and its comm,<ref name="Aphra 18" /> questioning who the real controller was. When confronted with Aphra again, Bini wished for the rogue archaeologist to be killed.<ref name="Aphra 19" />
