The '''Armored Assault Tank Mk I''', also known as the '''AAT battle tank''', the '''AAT-1 Hover Tank''', or the '''Trade Federation battle tank''', was a [[tank]] vehicle used by the [[Trade Federation]], [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].

==Characteristics==
===Design and crew===
[[File:Inside-an-AAT.png|thumb|left|200px|The interior of an AAT]]
The [[Armor]]ed Assault [[Tank]] Mk I, otherwise known as the AAT-1 and manufactured by [[Baktoid Armor Workshop]], was the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' primary infantry support vehicle used during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. It was a relatively small and compact tank clocking in at 9.19 [[meter]] in length, and consisted of a heavily armored, spade-shaped lower [[hull]] topped with a lighter, secondary hull and rotating [[turret]]. A crew of four controlled drove the vehicle:<ref name="Rise" /> two [[gunner]]s who controlled the side weapons, one [[commander]] who would fire the main cannon<ref name="Complete New" /> as well as give orders and provide visual confirmation of the sensor readings to the crew,<ref name="Rise" /> and a pilot.<ref name="Complete New" />

The crew of four would be made up of [[B1-series battle droid]]s; such a crew might be four [[OOM pilot battle droid]]s<ref name="Rise" /> or a mixing of different B1 models. Roles included regular B1s for the gunners, an OOM pilot<ref name="Complete New" /> or the specifically programmed [[AAT Driver Battle Droid]] in the role of pilot,<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> and an [[OOM command battle droid]],<ref name="Complete New" /> OOM Pilot,<ref name="ANB">{{TCW|A Necessary Bond}}</ref> AAT Driver Battle Droid,<ref name="Complete New" /> or even a standard B1<ref name="Massacre">{{TCW|Massacre}}</ref> as the tank commander.<ref name="Complete New" /> Furthermore, the role of tank commander could also be held by a [[BX-series droid commando]],<ref name="Kidnapped">{{TCW|Kidnapped}}</ref> a [[tactical droid]],<ref name="Innocents of Ryloth">{{TCW|Innocents of Ryloth}}</ref> or even an [[Species|organic]].<ref name="Defenders of Peace" /> The tank could carry up to six additional [[droid]]s via handholds and footholds on the hull, as well as ten [[metric ton]]s of cargo. The cost of the AAT-1 was 75,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Rise" />

===Armament and propulsion===
{{Quote|Adjust the stabilizers, we're going over!|An [[AAT Driver Battle Droid]]|<ref name="Aftermath (episode)">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref>}}
The AAT-1 was an extremely versatile vehicle designed to handle nearly any opposition encountered on the battlefield. Its primary weapon was a [[heavy laser cannon]] mounted in the top turret that provided the tank with 360 degrees of long-range firepower<ref name="Rise" /> powerful enough to destroy an [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref> Two medium [[laser cannon]]s, mounted on the tank's port and starboard sides<ref name="Rise" /> and combined with a twin [[rangefinder]] assembly<ref name="Complete New" /> provided excellent anti-vehicle firepower, while a pair of coaxial<ref name="Rise" /> short-range<ref name="Complete New" /> heavy [[blaster]]s were mounted with the [[laser]]s for anti-personnel use. The AAT-1 was also armed with six-barreled multi-ordnance [[projectile launcher]]s that were mounted in the lower hull.<ref name="Rise" /> The launchers were automatically refilled with three different types of ammunition: bunker-buster [[Explosive charge|heavy explosive]], larger [[armor]]-piercing, or general purpose high-energy shells, which were cocooned by a [[plasma]] field before launch to reduce friction and achieve higher speeds.<ref name="Complete New" /> This combination of different types of specialized ammunition gave the AAT-1 anti-armor and even siege capabilities.<ref name="Rise" /> 

The AAT-1 was powered by a compact, four-chambered multi-[[reactor]] [[Power generator|power plant]] which was housed on the tank's rear for maximum protection from enemy fire.<ref name="Complete New" /> Two powerful [[repulsorlift]] stabilizers mounted on the vehicle's rear would provide stable traverse while the vehicle moved on uneven or rough terrain,<ref name="Aftermath (episode)" /> while a combination of auxiliary [[repulsor coil]]s and discs kept the heavy vehicle hovering just off the ground, propelling it forward.<ref name="Complete New" /> Despite its capable machinery, the AAT-1 was neither quick nor agile on the battlefield, boasting a maximum speed of 55 [[Kilometer per hour|kilometers per hour]].<ref name="Rise" />

<div style="float:right">[[File:AATBattleTank-SWL2024update.png|thumb|250px|left|An AAT firing its main cannon on Felucia]]</div>
While formidable in theory, in practice, the AAT-1 was less than the sum of its parts. Its biggest weakness was the crew, the quartet of [[B1-series battle droid]]s, and while competent, the droid crew possessed neither the initiative nor the ability to improvise that of a crew of [[Sentience|sentients]] would have. This made them predictable and easily countered by opponents who had tangled with the tank before. In addition, their computer and electrical systems left much to be desired, and a platoon of AAT-1s could easily be taken out by a handful of [[electromagnetic pulse grenade]]s.<ref name="Rise" /> Another weakness of the tank's design was the main cannon's barrel build. It was possible for a skilled [[sniper]] to make a shot straight into the barrel's opening, which would ignite the laser cannon's firing mechanism and destroy the entire tank; this was however very rarely attempted as it was hard for even veteran snipers to make the shot.<ref name="Solitary" /> Despite such shortcomings, the tanks did pose a considerable threat to [[clone trooper]] infantry and armored forces.<ref name="Rise" />

==History==
[[File:SepAAT-TCWIV.png|thumb|left|250px|An AAT painted with the colors of the Confederacy of Independent Systems during the Clone Wars.]]
The AAT was designed for and used in the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo]].<ref name="SWHC1">{{HelmetCollectionCite|1|Databank A-Z}}</ref> It was later donated to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith" /> where it served as one of its backbone [[Separatist Droid Army|infantry]] support vehicles during the [[Clone Wars]]. Many of the AATs used by the Confederacy were painted in Confederate [[color]]s such as blue and white,<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> though some tanks used by the CIS were still painted in the tan previously used by the Trade Federation.<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> During the [[Battle of Horain]], the [[Galactic Republic]] stole one AAT to enter the base and fight off the [[Horain garrison|base's battle droids]].<ref name="Roger Roger">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=19|story=Roger Roger}}</ref> The tanks were also donated to the [[Death Watch]] during their alliance with the Confederacy.<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore" />

One of the most famous variants of the vehicle was constructed by Confederate [[General]] [[Lok Durd]]. He modified one of these into the [[Defoliator Deployment Tank]] which he attempted to [[Defense of Maridun|test]] on [[Maridun]] and the [[Lurmen village|Lurmen]] locals, but had his prototype destroyed by [[Jedi Knight|Jedi]] [[General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his forces.<ref name="Defenders of Peace">{{TCW|Defenders of Peace}}</ref> This variant of the tank was later [[Battle of Dathomir|used]] by the [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander of the Droid Army]], General [[Grievous]] to wipe out the [[Nightsisters]] on [[Dathomir]].<ref name="Massacre" />

<div style="float:right">[[File:Desix_battle_droids.png|left|thumb|250px|Desix governor Tawni Ames' forces possessed an AAT]]</div>
After the Clone Wars, dozens of AATs fell into the hands of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] where they were modified extensively in order to better accommodate organic crews.<ref name="Commander" /> On the other side of the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Assault Tank]] bore a great resemblance to<ref name="SW3">[[Star Wars (2015) 3|''Star Wars'' (2015) 3]]</ref> the Armored Assault Tank.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace" /> [[Separatist holdouts]] such as [[Desix Separatist holdout|the one]] under [[governor]] [[Tawni Ames]] on the [[planet]] [[Desix]] kept their AATs as part of their defense forces, with Ames' ones being used against the Galactic Empire in her [[Mission to Desix|attempt]] to secure the planet's independence.<ref name="Solitary" /> The Empire also supplied a [[Incinerator tank|heavier]] version of the vehicle to the [[Trandoshan]]s they allied with. The variant was armed with a [[flamethrower]] turret controlled by an additional gunner. The heavily camouflaged tank was used by Trandoshan hunters on [[Kashyyyk]] to burn down the remaining [[Wookiee]] villages.<ref name="Tribe">{{TBB|Tribe}}</ref> The Rebel Alliance used one AAT during the [[Skirmish on the Ghost Moon|skirmish]] on the [[Ghost Moon]] against the [[Special Commando Advanced Recon Trooper|SCAR troopers]] of [[Task Force 99]].<ref name="SW21">[[Star Wars (2015) 21|''Star Wars'' (2015) 21]]</ref>
