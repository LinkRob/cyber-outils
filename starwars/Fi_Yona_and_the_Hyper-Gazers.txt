'''Fi Yona and the Hyper-Gazers''' was a [[band]] active during the [[High Republic Era]]. The band performed at the [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]<ref name="TRS" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> shortly before the event was [[Valo Atrocity|attacked]] by [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]. The [[Kuranu]] inventor [[Mantessa Chekkat]] had hoped to hear Fi Yona and the Hyper-Gazers play, but was unable to, having previously been [[Prison|imprisoned]] within a holding cell in<ref name="TRS" /> [[Lonisa City Detention Center]].<ref name="RTCT">''[[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Fi Yona and the Hyper-Gazers were mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="TRS" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
