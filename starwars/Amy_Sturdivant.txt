'''Amy Sturdivant''' is a stuntwoman who served as a stunt double for [[Carasynthia Dune]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.<ref name="Chapter 4" /> She was also [[Unidentified green-skinned mercenary|mercenary #2]] in the "[[Part I]]" episode of ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]''.
