'''Fossil''', known as '''"the Old Lady"''' by the [[bomber]] crews of the [[Resistance]], was a [[Gender|female]] [[Martigrade]] who served as the [[commanding officer]] of the [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|Resistance's StarFortress unit]] that included [[Cobalt Squadron|Cobalt]] and [[Crimson Squadron]].<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" /> Following the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation of D'Qar]], she was aboard the [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] as surplus crew and experienced sorrow at the Resistance's losses. Sometime later she gave [[Rose Tico]] a [[Rose Tico's Resistance ring|Resistance ring]].<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>
