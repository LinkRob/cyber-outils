'''Berken's Flow''' was a [[cocktail]] served in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Checked Out" /> Said to be "from the largest lavafall in the galaxy," it originated on [[Mustafar]], its name referring to that planet's [[Berken's Flow]] lavafall. Its ingredients included [[chocolate]].<ref name="AllEars">{{WebCite|url=https://allears.net/2022/07/02/review-come-to-disneys-brand-new-star-wars-themed-lounge-with-us/|text=Come to Star Wars: Hyperspace Lounge on the Disney Wish With Us!|work=AllEars.net|archivedate=20220809074512}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Berken's Flow is featured in the [[Star Wars: Hyperspace Lounge|''Star Wars'': Hyperspace Lounge]] aboard the cruise ship ''Disney Wish''.<ref name="Checked Out" /> Its ingredients include SelvaRey Coconut, Rumchata, Godiva Chocolate, and coconut water.<ref name="AllEars" />
