'''Colossus wasps''' were a type of [[insect]] that the [[human]] [[journalist]]<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 4" /> [[Rhil Dairo]]<ref name="Tempest Breaker">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]''</ref> considered a horror. During the [[Battle of Eriadu]]<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 4" /> in [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 4|''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 4]] features the [[Battle of Eriadu]], which takes place in [[228 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:THRContent228BBY|here]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Stormwall Defense Fleet]] was attacked by [[Nihil]] [[scav droid]]s, though due to the [[Force vision|illusions]] created by the Nihil [[Children of the Storm|Child of the Storm]] [[H'Tar]], the [[fleet]] believed it was under attack from what they feared most. While the [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam]] ''[[Gios]]'' came under attack from an illusioned<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 4" /> [[Living Force|Force]]-[[Diet|consuming]]<ref name="Tempest Breaker" /> [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creature, reports the ''Gios'' received stated that colossus wasps were attacking the fleet's [[Starship|ships]], which Dairo informed<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 4" /> [[Jedi Master]]<ref name="Tempest Breaker" /> [[Keeve Trennis]] of.<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:THR-Fear-of-the-Jedi-4-Final-Cover.jpg|thumb|right|280px|''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 4 marked the first mention of colossus wasps in the current ''Star Wars'' canon.]]
Colossus wasps were mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 4|fourth issue]] of the [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi]]'', released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 4" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[May 21]], 2025.<ref name="Marvel 2">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/122250/star_wars_the_high_republic_-_fear_of_the_jedi_2025_4|text=Star Wars: The High Republic - Fear of the Jedi (2025) #4}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Colossus wasp/Legends|colossus wasp]] first appeared, via the form of an [[exoskeleton]] converted into an [[Ithullan ore hauler]], in the [[Tales of the Jedi 4|fourth issue]] of the [[1993]] comic series [[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (comic series)|''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi'']]. The issue was written by [[Tom Veitch]], penciled by [[David Roach]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Tales of the Jedi 4">[[Tales of the Jedi 4|''Tales of the Jedi'' 4]]</ref> on [[January 18]], [[1994]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54569/star_wars_tales_of_the_jedi_1993_4|text=Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (1993) #4}}</ref> The first installment of the [[The Horse's Mouth|''A Guide to the Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi Universe'']] series of articles written by [[Bob Cooper]], published in the [[1995]] [[Star Wars Insider 26|twenty-sixth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine, provided the first capitalization of the creature's name in "Colossus Wasp,"<ref name="Insider26">{{InsiderCite|26|The Horse's Mouth|Straight from the Horse's Mouth: A Guide to the ''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi'' Universe, Part 1}}</ref> which was subsequently used by several other sources.<ref name="CSWE StennessNode">{{CSWECite|III|191|Stenness Node}}</ref><ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia]]''</ref><ref name="BtM">''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]''</ref>

The [[1996]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Vehicles and Vessels]]'' written by [[Bill Smith]] was the first source to use the lower-case form "colossus wasp,"<ref name="EGtVV">''[[The Essential Guide to Vehicles and Vessels]]''</ref> which became the spelling the majority of the media referencing the creature utilized.<ref name="SWE" /><ref name="BtM" /><ref name="TotJC">''[[Tales of the Jedi Companion]]''</ref><ref name="FF114">{{FactFile|114|ORE 1-2|Ithullan Ore Hauler}}</ref><ref name="CSWE Ithull">{{CSWECite|II|124|Ithull}}</ref><ref name="CSWE ColossusWasp">{{CSWECite|I|134|colossus wasps of Ithull}}</ref><ref name="CSWE IthullanOreHauler">{{CSWECite|II|124|Ithullan ore hauler}}</ref><ref name="Guide">''[[A Guide to the Star Wars Universe, Third Edition, Revised and Expanded]]''</ref><ref name="FF11">{{FactFile|11|PLA 7-8|Planets of the Inner Rim}}</ref><ref name="NEGtVV">''[[The New Essential Guide to Vehicles and Vessels]]''</ref><ref name="CSWE MarauderStarjacker">{{CSWECite|II|288|''Marauder Starjacker''}}</ref> The name "Ithullian colossus wasp" was also introduced in the same book<ref name="EGtVV" /> although, beginning with ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia]]'' published in [[1998]],<ref name="SWE" /> the spelling shifted to "Ithullan colossus wasp."<ref name="BtM" /><ref name="CSWE IthullanOreHauler" /><ref name="FF11" /><ref name="NEGtVV" /><ref name="CSWE MarauderStarjacker" />
