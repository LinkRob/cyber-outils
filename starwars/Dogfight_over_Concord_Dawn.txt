During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the rebels of [[Phoenix Cell]] were engaged by the [[Journeyman Protector|Protectors]] of [[Concord Dawn]] in a '''dogfight''' over Concord Dawn. The skirmish arose after the rebels tried to secure the right to pass through the [[Concord Dawn system]] as part of a new [[hyperspace]] route to the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. [[Fenn Rau]], leader of the Protectors and an ally of the Empire, attacked [[Phoenix Squadron]] with his fellow [[Mandalorian]] [[starfighter]] [[pilot]]s. Multiple Phoenix pilots were [[Death|killed]] and [[Captain]] [[Hera Syndulla]] was seriously injured in their retreat. After the battle, the rebels [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Sabine Wren]] [[Mission to the third moon of Concord Dawn|traveled]] to the [[Protectors Camp]] on the [[third moon of Concord Dawn]] and, after some hostilities, were able to secure the help of Fenn Rau.

==Prelude==
Following the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Battle of Garel|assault]] on [[Garel]],<ref name="Legacy">{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref> the [[Phoenix Cell]] was compelled to travel through the [[Outer Rim Territories]] in order to evade the Galactic Empire. In response to this situation, the rebel [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]] convened a meeting with [[Rex]] and the [[Spectres]] to discuss their options for opening a new route through the [[Lothal sector]]. The [[Mandalorian]] [[Sabine Wren]] suggested the [[Concord Dawn system]], which was home to a Mandalorian community known as the [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protectors]].<ref name="The Protector of Concord Dawn" />

The Concord Dawn system was not yet part of the Empire. The Protectors were fierce Mandalorian warriors who had helped the [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] to train [[clone trooper]]s during the [[Clone Wars]]. Believing that reasoning with the Mandalorians was impossible, Commander Sato recommended sending a military force to the system. However, [[Phoenix Leader]] [[Hera Syndulla]] and and the [[Jedi]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] counseled that the rebels use diplomacy to secure safe passage through the Concord Dawn system. Syndulla and Jarrus also suggested recruiting the Protectors to the rebellion. Sato reluctantly authorized Syndulla's mission and Wren volunteered to come along.<ref name="The Protector of Concord Dawn" />

==The dogfight==
Phoenix Leader Syndulla led [[Phoenix Squadron]] to the Concord Dawn system. Upon arriving, their [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]s were met by three Mandalorian [[Fang-class Protectorate fighter|Fang fighters]] led by [[Fenn Rau]], the leader of the Protectors and the "Protector of [[Concord Dawn]]." When Rau demanded to know what the rebels were doing in the system, Syndulla explained that the rebellion was seeking safe passage through the Concord Dawn system. When Rau asked who were they were, Syndulla responded that they were those who would stand with Concord Dawn against the Empire.<ref name="The Protector of Concord Dawn" />

[[File:Dogfight_over_Concord_Dawn.png|thumb|right|300px|Mandalorian Protector Fang fighters move in to attack Phoenix Squadron]]
Since Rau had aligned the Protectors with the Empire, he promptly identified Phoenix Squadron as a threat and ordered his fighters to destroy the rebels. Ignoring Syndulla's pleas, the Fang fighters launched a devastating attack on Phoenix Squadron. During the dogfight, two pilots, [[Phoenix Three]] and [[Phoenix Four]], were [[Death|killed]] when their fighters were hit by enemy [[fire]]. With diplomacy no longer an option, Captain Syndulla ordered a retreat and led Rau on a chase in order to give enough time for Wren and the surviving Phoenix pilot to jump into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="The Protector of Concord Dawn" />

Before leaving, Syndulla promised Wren that she would be behind her. Despite Syndulla's best efforts, she was unable to outrun Rau's Fang fighter and [[Hera's A-wing|her A-wing]] was badly hit by enemy fire. Syndulla managed to jump into hyperspace but was badly wounded by Rau's attack. She was picked up by Wren, Jarrus, and the crew of Commander Sato's [[CR90 corvette]] [[Liberator (CR90 corvette)|''Liberator'']]. Due to timely medical intervention, Syndulla survived her injuries and continued her leadership role in the rebellion.<ref name="The Protector of Concord Dawn" />

==Aftermath==
While the dogfight above Concord Dawn was a victory for the Protectors, it earned them the enmity of the rebellion. With negotiations no longer an option, the Phoenix and Lothal rebel cells considered retaliatory measures against the Protectors. Outraged by the Protectors' unprovoked assault on her comrades, Wren advocated destroying the Protectors' [[starfighter]]s so that they could not longer pose a threat to the rebellion. She had managed to track the Fang fighters to a [[Protectors Camp|base]] on the [[third moon of Concord Dawn]].<ref name="The Protector of Concord Dawn" />

Unwilling to draw the Empire's attention to Concord Dawn, Jarrus recommended infiltrating the base and destroying the Fang fighters. Since he was reluctant to risk the lives of more crew members, he only took the [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]] along for the [[Mission to the third moon of Concord Dawn|mission]]. Despite Jarrus' orders, Wren secretly stowed aboard the ''Phantom''. After further negotiations with Rau failed, the rebels destroyed the Protectors' starfighters and kidnapped Rau.<ref name="The Protector of Concord Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
The dogfight over Concord Dawn first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[The Protector of Concord Dawn]]," which premiered on [[January 27]], [[2016]].
