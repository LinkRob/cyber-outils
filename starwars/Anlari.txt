The '''Anlari''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that lived in [[the galaxy]]. They had fleshy horns on their heads that could curl and twist, and those that belonged to younger individuals were only as long as a [[human]] [[Limb|finger]]. [[G'kolu]] was a member of this [[species]].<ref name="Legends of Luke Skywalker" />
