'''Erkush''' was a [[Species|creature]] native to<ref name="GEstaff" /> the [[Wild Space]]<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Kalee]],<ref name="GEstaff" /> as are members of the [[Kaleesh]] species. [[Kaleesh warrior]]s [[Hunting|hunt]] the Erkush for more than simply [[Foodstuff|meat]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref> and generally kill to demonstrate their bravery,<ref name="BC 65">{{BustCollectionCite|65|Character}}</ref> making its pelt for [[clothing]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> and its [[Bone|skulls]] and bones for [[Kaleesh mask|fearsome masks]].<ref name="GEstaff" /> They also valued their prey and wouldn't allow anyone near them.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

The [[Ithorian]] [[collector]] named [[Dok-Ondar]]<ref name="SWGE">{{GEAttr|Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities}}</ref> owns an [[Grievous's masks|erkush-carved mask]] that had originally been worn by the late [[General]] [[Grievous]],<ref name="TGTB">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> which was displayed in his [[Batuuan]] store, [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities]], in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the planet [[Batuu]].<ref name="SWGE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Erkush were first mentioned in the Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities store at the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] theme park land,<ref name="GEstaff" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Opening Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galaxys-edge-opening-dates-revealed|text=''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge Opening Dates Revealed}}</ref> The [[Erkush/Legends|Erkush]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first mentioned in [[Crosscurrent]], a book written by [[Paul S. Kemp]] released on [[January 26]], [[2010]].<ref name="crosscurrent">''[[Crosscurrent]]''</ref>
