'''Chanda Bethari''' was a member of [[61st Mobile Infantry|Twilight Company]] and was a participant in the [[Battle of Hoth]]. Along with fellow [[soldier]]s [[Parko]] and [[Barritz]], Chanda had been pinned down by an [[All Terrain Scout Transport]] on the edge of the battlefield. Chanda was able to navigate the trenches to get closer to the walker, and began arming a [[detonator pack]] in an attempt to destroy it. As Chanda was backing away and unspooling the [[trigger wire]], the explosive inexplicably detonated early, crippling the walker and knocking Chanda unconscious. Chanda awoke two [[Standard day|days]] later in a [[bacta tank]] on the ''[[Redemption]]'', having lost a leg.<ref name="On the front Lines" />
