'''Attark''' was a [[Color|brown]] skinned and black [[eye]]d [[Hoover]] [[Gender|male]] [[Jabba's criminal empire|owned]] by the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]]<ref name="SWAEYNTK" /> as a pet.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> Surprisingly,<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> he had a very high intelligence<ref name="SWAEYNTK" /> and was talented at weapon repair work. The Hoover lived in [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], where he did repairs<ref name="Geektionary" /> on weapons and gadgets for the [[Hutt]].<ref name="SWAEYNTK" />

He was weak-bodied<ref name="Geektionary" /> and looked harmless,<ref name="CVD" /> but at [[Standard day|night]], Attark often stalked the palace<ref name="SWAEYNTK" /> and snuck up on [[sleep]]ing victims to suck their [[blood]], using his [[nose]] trunk to slither through [[clothing]] or around blankets.<ref name="CVD" /> This was a dangerous habit, as the palace was mainly populated with criminals that would not take kindly to being attacked in the dark.<ref name="Geektionary" />

==Behind the scenes==
Attark was created as a puppet for the [[1983]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], with a prototype model also being made;<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Star Wars Chronicles]]''</ref> however, the character is not visible in the final film.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], he was first mentioned in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''.<ref name="SWAEYNTK" />
