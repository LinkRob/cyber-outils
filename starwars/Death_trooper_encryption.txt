'''Death trooper encryption''' was a [[language]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s elite [[death trooper]]s.<ref name="Language">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Language in Star Wars Movies|video=CZp8Civk2co|nolive=1}}</ref> The [[trooper]]s spoke the language using [[Speech scrambler|voice-scramblers]] built into their [[Death trooper armor|helmets]], allowing them to communicate with each other without anyone being able to decipher what they were saying, as well as enhance their intimidating image.<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

The [[squad]] of death troopers that accompanied [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] to [[Capture of Galen Erso|recover]] the [[scientist]] [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]] on the [[planet]] [[Lah'mu]] used the encryption as they made their approach toward the [[Erso homestead]].<ref name="Rogue One" /> Another [[Gideon's guard|detachment]] of death troopers also used the encryption on [[Nevarro]] when [[Clash on Nevarro|flanking]] former [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Carasynthia Dune|Cara Dune]] in the [[Nevarro school|cantina]].<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref>
