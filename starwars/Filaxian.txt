'''Filaxian''' was an addictive consumable available during the [[High Republic Era]]. After the [[Bivall]] [[Quin Carree]] was abandoned by her lover&mdash;[[Twi'lek]] [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]]<ref name="Script" />&mdash;in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]'' establishes [[Lourna Dee]] left [[Quin Carree]] on [[Valtos]] immediately after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]].</ref> she eventually became a [[bounty hunter]] and addicted to the substance [[Reedug|reed]]. When the two crossed paths again<ref name="Script" /> in [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">The section of ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]'' that includes the mention of filaxian takes place between the events of ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'' and the present day events of ''Tempest Breaker'', which are both dated to [[228 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:THRContent228BBY|here]].</ref> the Bivall was shot by the Twi'lek&mdash;by then also operating as a [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]]&mdash;though Dee had been unaware it was Carree at the time.<ref name="Script" />

After learning it was Carree, Dee tried to help heal her injuries, though the Bivall began struggling and stressed that she needed to return to her [[Quin Carree's ship|ship]], which Dee refused. The [[Sephi]] [[Tey Sirrek]], an associate of Dee's, questioned the Bivall about what she was using, naming several substances&mdash;[[gy'lan]],<ref name="Script" /> the [[spice]]<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref> [[ryll]], and filaxian. When Carree began shivering in response, Sirrek pressed further, stating that they could recognize the signs of addiction. Eventually, the Bivall admitted she was taking reed.<ref name="Script" />

==Behind the scenes==
Filaxian was mentioned in flashbacks in ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]'', a [[2024]] audio drama written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Tempest Breaker">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]''</ref> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The spelling of the consumable's name was later confirmed with the release of the audio drama's [[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker (script)|script]] in [[2025]].<ref name="Script" />
