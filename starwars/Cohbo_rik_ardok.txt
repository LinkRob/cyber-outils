'''Cohbo'rik'ardok''', known by the [[core name]] "'''Borika'''," [[Reproduction|born]] as '''Kivu'rik'ardok''' with the core name "'''Vurika'''," was a [[Chiss]] [[Gender|female]]. Born in [[61 BBY]] into the minor [[Kivu family]] on the [[planet]] [[Rentor]], Vurika was taken from her family to become a [[sky-walker]] [[navigator]], a Chiss navigator with [[Precognition|abilities]] that allowed her to guide Chiss [[starship]]s through the [[Unknown Regions]], in [[56 BBY]]. Joining the [[sky-walker corps]] cut her ties to the Kivu [[Chiss family|family]], and she became '''Rik'ardok'''. After her abilities faded, she [[Rematching|joined]] the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Irizi family|Irizi]] [[Ruling Families|ruling family]], becoming '''Irizi'rik'ardok''' "Zirika". As an adult, she worked with and trained the sky-walkers at the [[Seekers program]] [[Thearterra center|facility]] on the astronomical object [[Thearterra]].

Soon after, she [[Marriage|married]] [[Bomarmo]] of the [[Cohbo family]], becoming Cohbo'rik'ardok. The couple settled on the planet [[Ool]], where they owned the [[Ardok Ranch]]. She worked closely with the [[Seekers' Shadehouse]], a Seekers program facility on Ool. In [[18 BBY]], [[Caregiver]] [[Mitth'ali'astov]] "Thalias" traveled to the Ardok Ranch to speak with Borika. The pair discussed the sky-walkers' training, and Thalias eventually revealed why she had come to the ranch&mdash;she wanted to meet Borika because she was the sister of [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn". Thalias was set to return to the Ascendancy's [[capital]] planet of [[Csilla]], and Borika dropped her guest off at the [[Ibbian Spaceport]], where she was able to catch a [[transport]] back to the capital. [[Standard week|Weeks]] afterward, Thalias returned to Ool with [[Che'ri]]&mdash;the sky-walker in her care&mdash;in order to help Borika and the Seekers program gain a better understanding of the sky-walkers.

==Biography==
===Early life===
{{Quote|I had an older sister. She was five when she disappeared. My parents would never tell me where she went.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''How old were you?''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Three. For a long time I assumed she'd died, and that I would never see her again. It wasn't until I reached bridge officer rank that I was finally told about the sky-walkers, and realized what must have happened to her. And I'll ''still'' never see her again.|translated=1|Thrawn and Thalias|<ref name="Chaos Rising" />}}
[[Reproduction|Born]] in [[61 BBY]]<ref name="Birth" /> on the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] of [[Rentor]], one of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nation's worlds, Kivu'rik'ardok, [[core name]] "Vurika,"<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> was a [[Gender|female]] member of the [[Chiss]] [[species]] and a member of the minor [[Kivu family]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> In [[56 BBY]],<ref name="Birth" /> at the age of five, Vurika was taken from her [[Chiss family|family]] and joined the [[sky-walker corps]], a group of [[Youngling|young]] girls who had [[Precognition|abilities]] known as [[The Force|Third Sight]] that allowed them to [[Navigator|navigate]] the treacherous [[Realspace|space]] outside of the Ascendancy's borders. As all sky-walkers lost ties to their families when they joined the [[corps]],<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> she became known as Rik'ardok.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> She left behind her [[parent]]s and younger brother&mdash;[[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Kivu'raw'nuru]] "Vurawn"&mdash;on Rentor.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> As part of her training, Rik'ardok's memories of her family were wiped,<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> and the siblings' parents did not tell Vurawn where her sister was for several [[Standard year|years]], prompting him to believe that she had [[Death|died]]. Years later, Vurawn [[Rematching|joined]] the [[Ruling Families|ruling]] [[Mitth family]] and became Mitth'raw'nuru "Thrawn", joining the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]]. He soon attained [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] [[Military rank|rank]] and finally learned about the sky-walker corps, realizing that Vurika had likely become one.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

[[File:Rentor.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Vurika was born into the Kivu family on Rentor.]]
Most sky-walkers' abilities faded in their mid-teenage years, prompting the [[fleet]] to run tests with them inside Ascendancy borders, where there was no imminent danger of travel in [[hyperspace]], to determine if they could still [[Pilot|navigate]] Chiss [[starship]]s.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> Rik'ardok's Third Sight eventually faded, and she joined the ruling [[Irizi family]], adopting the name Irizi'rik'ardok "Zirika". Whereas most sky-walkers decided to forge a new life after they joined a family and left the life of a navigator behind, Zirika chose to work with the [[Seekers program]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

The goal of the Seekers program was to train the young girls who were inducted into the sky-walker corps and prepare them for duty with the Expansionary Defense Fleet. As an adult, Zirika worked at the [[Thearterra center]], a Seekers facility located on the astronomical object [[Thearterra]]. After Thrawn told the Mitth [[Syndic]] [[Mitth'ras'safis]] "Thrass" that he had lost his sister, the syndic searched for the Chiss [[officer]]'s sister. Thrass eventually found Zirika and visited her, compiling a [[Code cylinder|data cylinder]] with information on the woman and the Seekers program.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

===The Seekers' Shadehouse===
{{Quote|You're the Cohbo'rik'ardok who used to be Irizi'rik'ardok?''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Yes. I became part of the Cohbo family when I married Bomarmo.|translated=1|Thalias confirms Borika's identity|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
[[File:Oyokal.jpg|thumb|left|300px|After joining the Cohbo family, Borika settled on Ool.]]
[[Time|Sometime]] afterward, Zirika met [[Bomarmo]] of the [[Cohbo family]], and the two [[Marriage|eloped]]; as a result, Zirika joined Bomarmo's family, adopting the name Cohbo'rik'ardok "Borika". The pair settled on the [[Ardok Ranch]] on the planet [[Ool]], where Borika continued her work with the Seekers program at the [[Seekers' Shadehouse]] on the [[Farm|ranch]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="LE Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' to [[18 BBY]].</ref> the Chiss [[Caregiver]] [[Mitth'ali'astov]] "Thalias" of the Expansionary Defense Fleet [[Chiss heavy cruiser|heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]'', which was [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by Thrawn, who had become a [[Senior Captain]], visited Ool to speak with Borika. After arriving at the ranch, Thalias asked the Cohbo woman about the Seekers' Shadehouse and learned that that was not its official name. As Thalias was not supposed to know about the program, Borika invited her guest inside and leveled a [[charric]]&mdash;a [[Limb|handheld]] Chiss [[weapon]]&mdash;at her, instructing the caregiver to show her her identification.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

As Thalias carried no identification, she revealed that she was a former sky-walker herself, a claim Borika dismissed, as sky-walkers had not become caregivers for a hundred years. The caregiver then explained that Mitth [[Senior Aide]] [[Mitth'iv'iklo]] "Thivik" had given her Syndic Thrass' data cylinder, handing Borika the device. As the dates the cylinder contained matched those of the syndic's visit, the Cohbo allowed Thalias to continue. The caregiver told the Cohbo that the ''Springhawk''{{'s}} sky-walker, [[Che'ri]], had interacted with an [[The Magys's people|alien]] called [[the Magys]], who had been able to extend the girl's Third Sight but also had given her [[Dream|nightmares]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

===A forgotten past===
{{Quote|And that's why they don't want former sky-walkers acting as caregivers, isn't it? They're afraid we'll compare notes and realize we both have the same memory loss.|translated=1|Thalias finally understands why the sky-walkers' memories were wiped|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
[[File:GreaterGood-OOP-Art.jpg|thumb|right|270px|Thalias revealed that Borika was the sister of Senior Captain Thrawn (pictured).]]
Thalias continued to tell Borika about the [[Magys]] and Che'ri, often gazing around the room, prompting Borika to remark that the other may have been remembering the room. She explained that if the caregiver had spent time at the Seeker's Shadehouse during her training, she had definitely spent time in the room they occupied&mdash;although the training records were locked away. The Cohbo offered to look into Che'ri's interactions with the [[alien]] and recommended that Thalias request a reassignment to Ool, an offer the caregiver declined, as the Ascendancy was about to break into a [[Grysk plot to engineer a Chiss civil war|civil war]]. Borika also offered that the other could stay at an [[Hotel|inn]] in the [[city]] of [[Pomprey]] if the caregiver wished to wait for a response from the Seekers program.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

As Borika dodged Thalias' questions about her lost memories of her training and [[Mitth'ali'astov's original family|family]], the caregiver revealed that she had come to Ool for two reasons: to meet Borika and to tell her that she was Thrawn's sister. After verifying that fact with Thrass' information, the Cohbo claimed that former sky-walkers were not supposed to know about their pasts, as too many girls would have quit the program due to homesickness. Thalias deduced that the Seekers program erased the memories of the sky-walkers to eradicate the issue, and Borika informed her that the fading process was not perfect, sometimes leaving glimpses of a sky-walker's earlier life.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

===A brighter future===
{{Quote|Too bad you can't come along.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Why would I want to do that?''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''To meet him.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Why?''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''What do you mean, ''why''? Because he's your ''brother''?''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''There's no point. Don't you understand? There's no point. I don't remember him.|translated=1|Thalias and Borika discuss Thrawn|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
[[File:ChissAscendancyCanonSymbol.svg|thumb|left|240px|Borika met with Thalias, who served as a caregiver in the Expansionary Defense Fleet of the Chiss Ascendancy (logo pictured).]]
Thalias questioned why memories of their training were also wiped if the objective was to eliminate the sky-walkers' family attachments, for which Borika did not have an answer. Shortly after, Thalias received a call from the ''Springhawk''{{'s}} [[Executive officer|first officer]], [[Mid Captain]] [[Ufsa'mak'ro]] "Samakro", who asked her to return to the [[capital]] planet [[Csilla]] immediately. Borika offered to drop her guest off at the [[Ibbian Spaceport]], allowing her to catch a [[transport]] back to Csilla. Before they arrived at the [[spaceport]], the caregiver commented that it was unfortunate that Borika would not be able to come to the ''Springhawk'' and meet Thrawn, prompting the Cohbo to cry and remark that there was no point, as her memories about her brother had faded.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

[[Standard week|Weeks]] later, Thalias and Che'ri traveled to Ool and met with Borika. The Cohbo had been given the entire story of Che'ri's dealings with the Magys and their effects on the girl and assured the pair that the Shadehouse was looking into it. Not wanting to stay at the ranch, Che'ri questioned why she could not return to the ''Springhawk''; however, Borika stated that sky-walkers were rare enough and that Che'ri was the most unique of them all. The Cohbo woman comforted the young girl by saying that what she helped the Shadehouse learn could make important changes for every sky-walker in the future after Thalias explained that Che'ri had to tell Borika and the Shadehouse everything about her interactions with the Magys.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I hope you're not here to help the Mitth make a list of former Irizi. That would be a full-life job.''&raquo;<br />&laquo;''Certainly more of my life than I would want to spend on the effort.|translated=1|Borika teases Thalias upon her arrival|<ref name="Lesser Evil" />}}
Although most sky-walkers left the life of navigating behind when they joined a Chiss family, Borika returned to work with the Seekers program to help train and study the young sky-walkers.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

Despite the oddity surrounding Thalias' arrival at the Ardok Ranch, Borika kept a cheerful tone when greeting her. She also teased the woman when asked if she was formerly of the Irizi family, asking if Thalias was trying to make a list of all former-Irizi, as Thalias was of the Mitth, rivals to the Irizi. As Thalias began to speak, she kept her friendly tone, with an edge of caution, eventually displaying her full caution by raising a charric to Thalias. Although she did not remember her brother, Borika teared up at the idea of meeting him.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Equipment==
Borika wore [[Jumpsuit|coveralls]] and [[boot]]s along with a [[jacket]]. She also hung a [[questis data-link reader]] from her side, which was connected to a shoulder strap.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cohbo'riki'ardok was first mentioned, albeit unidentified, in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> She made her first appearance in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final installment of the trilogy, which identified her as Cohbo'rik'ardok, Borika, Kivu'rik'ardok, and Irizi'rik'ardok.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
