{{Quote|If we can't confirm you're not Imperial, you're gonna have to follow us to the outpost at Adelphi.|Carson Teva, to Din Djarin|<ref name="Chapter 10" />}}
The '''Adelphi Base''',<ref name="Chapter 21" /> also known as the '''Adelphi Ranger Outpost''',<ref name="NYCC 2025">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2025-lucasfilm-publishing-reveals|text=NYCC 2025: All the News and Reveals from the Lucasfilm Publishing Panel}}</ref> was a [[New Republic]] outpost on [[Adelphi]], where the [[New Republic pilot]] [[Captain]] [[Carson Teva]] was stationed.<ref name="Mando Handbook">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Handbook]]''</ref> The outpost consisted of multiple Quonset huts, one of which was utilized as a [[The Pilots' Rest|lounge bar]] for New Republic pilots to relax.<ref name="Chapter 21" />

After the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] failed to provide the identity of his [[starship]], the ''[[Razor Crest]]'', to Teva and his wingmate [[Trapper Wolf]], who were searching for holdouts of the [[Galactic Empire]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]], Teva requested for Djarin to follow the pair to the Adelphi outpost. When Djarin transmitted the ''Razor Crest''{{'s}} signal, however, the [[pilot]]s deduced the vessel had been involved in a [[Bothan-Five incident|prisoner breakout]] aboard the [[CR-225T|New Republic Correctional Transport]] ''[[Bothan-5]]'', and Djarin attempted to flee from them.<ref name="Chapter 10" />

Teva was also at the outpost when he received a message from High Magistrate [[Greef Karga]] of [[Nevarro]], requesting his aid in protecting [[Nevarro City]] from [[Pirate King]] [[Gorian Shard]] and his raiders. Teva was in the company of Wolf, their fellow pilots [[Jib Dodger]] and [[Sash Ketter]], and [[Garazeb Orrelios]]. When Teva stated his intention to forward Karga's plea to [[Coruscant]], Orrelios advised him that the New Republic had a backlog of dispatches and would not reply in time. Teva then departed the base to travel directly to Coruscant himself.<ref name="Chapter 21" />
