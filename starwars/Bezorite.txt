'''Bezorite''' was a substance that was [[Mining|mined]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. When the traveler [[Corwin Ballast]] asked what work was available on Jakku, the [[bartender]] [[Ergel]] suggested mining bezorite or [[magnite]] on the side of the planet opposite to his [[Ergel's Bar|bar]] might be an option, also mentioning that [[kesium gas]] might have been found near [[Cratertown]].<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bezorite was first mentioned in the novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], which was written by [[Chuck Wendig]] and released in [[2015]].
