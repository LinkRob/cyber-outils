"'''Dread'''" was the callsign of a [[First Order]] [[TIE/se Bomber]] [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|pilot]] and was devoted to destruction. "Dread" once flew their [[bomber]] in a battle between [[TIE series|TIE fighter series]] [[starfighter]]s and [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]]s.<ref name="Dread Card" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Dread " was created for the miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition]]'' in the card of the same name released in the [[2021]] [[Fury of the First Order Squadron Pack]].<ref name="Dread Card" />
