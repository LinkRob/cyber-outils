The [[Cloud-Riders]], a crew of rebels who sought to bring down the [[Galactic Empire]], fought a series of '''campaigns''' against the Empire and the [[Five Crime Syndicates]], including [[Crimson Dawn]]. Under the leadership of [[Enfys Nest]], the rebels, riding into battle on [[swoop]]s,<ref name="Solo" /> engaged in heists like the [[raid on Gargon]]<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> and the [[heist on Hovun IV]]. The Cloud-Riders also often encountered the [[Beckett's gang|gang]] led by the [[smuggler]] [[Tobias Beckett]],<ref name="Beckett" /> who [[Gender|himself]] often worked with Crimson Dawn. The Cloud-Riders secured a major victory during the [[showdown on Savareen]], which occurred when the Cloud-Riders set their sights on securing a shipment of [[coaxium]]. The public leader of Crimson Dawn, [[Dryden Vos]], was [[Death|killed]] during the showdown, and a former Imperial [[corporal]] named [[Han Solo]] killed Beckett.<ref name="Solo" />
