'''Broadberry Meadow''' was a secluded cliff and riverside garden located in the [[capital]] [[city]] [[Theed]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]<ref name="SWCL2016" /> by the [[Standard year|year]] [[32 BBY]].<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> The garden contained a hexagonal building with [[Color|beige]] walls and a domed green roof built amongst the [[grass]] and [[tree]]s of the meadow. A path wrapped around the meadow along the riverbank, connecting to a jetty. Due to its location on the cliff edge, the island containing Broadberry Meadow could only be reached by [[boat]].<ref name="SWCL2016" />

==Behind the scenes==
Broadberry Meadow was first shown in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', which was written by [[Kristin Lund]], illustrated by [[Richard Chasemore]] and [[Hans Jenssen]],<ref name="IE1">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref> and released on [[October 1]], [[2000]].<ref name="IE1date">{{SWArchive|url=eu/lit/ref/news20000516.html|text=''Inside the Worlds of Episode I''}}</ref> The image of the garden was then reprinted as part of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] reference book<ref name="SWCL2016" /> released on [[September 27]], [[2016]].<ref name="SWCLdate">{{SW|url=news/dks-newest-galaxy-guides-first-look-at-star-wars-complete-locations-and-more|text=DK's Newest Galaxy Guides: First Look at ''Star Wars: Complete Locations'' and More!}}</ref>
