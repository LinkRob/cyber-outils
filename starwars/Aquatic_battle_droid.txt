'''Aquatic battle droids''' were a type of [[battle droid]] used for [[Water|underwater]] operations. When [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]], Jedi Master [[Bant Eerin]] were [[Rescue of Yoda|testing]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], a pair of aquatic battle [[droid]]s were deployed against the mentor and apprentice on the [[planet]] [[Glee Anselm]]. Skywalker used [[Taming beasts|animal control]] to make a [[whale]] attack them.<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin" />
