'''Bay squids''' were an aquatic [[species]] that lived in the [[Ugyir River]] of [[Coronet City]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. They had a habit of ducking in and out of the clear [[water]]s of the river. Following the arrival of the [[Galactic Empire]], the construction of [[Star Destroyer]]s on the banks of the river polluted the waters, eventually leading to the extinction of the species.<ref name="Hunters" />
