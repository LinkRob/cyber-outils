The '''EPS-6 superheavy blaster cannon''' was a powerful Imperial Weapon. It could be operated by humans using both hands, and it was capable of killing large and physical powerful opponents. An EPS-6 superheavy blaster cannon was included in a [[Imperial weapons cache|cache of Imperial weapons]] which was left on the Forest Moon of Endor after the Battle of Endor, and after arriving on the moon in search of the cache, [[Tern Koyatta]] killed the [[Gorax]] known as the [[Great Devourer]] by shooting it in the chest with the EPS-6 superheavy blaster cannon.<ref name="Ewoks 4">[[Ewoks (2024) 4|''Ewoks'' (2024) 4]]</ref>
