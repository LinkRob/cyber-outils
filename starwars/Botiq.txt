'''Botiq''' was an entity that advertised in multiple issues of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant's]] [[HoloNet News CoCo District Edition|CoCo District Edition]] of [[HoloNet News/Legends|HoloNet News]]<ref name="Botiq" /> in [[23 BBY/Legends|23]] and [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]].<ref name="Date">A Botiq advertisement appeared in both {{HoloNetNewsWeb|45||HoloNet News: Volume 531 &#124; Number 45 &#124; 13:2:28}} and {{HoloNetNewsWeb|56||HoloNet News: Volume 531 &#124; Number 56 &#124; 13:5:16}}, which are set on the [[Great ReSynchronization|dates]] 13:2:28 and 13:5:16, respectively. The two dates respectively correspond to [[23 BBY/Legends|23 BBY]] and [[22 BBY/Legends|22 BBY]], per the reasoning [[Template:YearMathGRS|here]].</ref> Its advertisement was a [[Color/Legends|blue]]-tinted box that framed a pair of [[Clothing/Legends|shoes]]. The name "Botiq," written in the [[High Galactic/Legends|High Galactic alphabet]] and colored white with a black shadow, lay in the foreground.<ref name="Botiq" />

==Behind the scenes==
Botiq's advertisement appeared throughout [[HoloNet News (original website)|HoloNet News]], an [[in-universe]] website<ref name="Botiq" /> created in [[2002]]<ref name="JCF">{{JCFCite|url=threads/5990764|board=Literature|thread=The Official HoloNetNews Discussion Thread|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/dY0zn}}</ref> to publicize the then-upcoming [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] film.<ref name="Botiq" /> [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[Paul Ens]] wrote the site content.<ref name="HNN Credits">{{HoloNetNewsWeb||credits.html|Credits}}</ref>
