The '''Archive Courtyard''' was a courtyard in which the [[Archive]] was located on the [[planet]] [[Vardos]]. It contained a monument dedicated to [[Garrick Versio]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[admiral]] who was responsible for bringing Vardos into the Empire, earning him the title of the "Hero of Vardos."<ref name="Battlefront II" />
