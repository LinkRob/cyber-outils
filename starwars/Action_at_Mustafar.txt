{{Quote|Maybe this mission doesn't make military sense. But Kanan is family. And we've all lost enough family to the Empire. So rescuing him makes sense to me.|Hera Syndulla, to the other Spectres|<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />}}
The '''action at Mustafar''' was a naval engagement four [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]] when the [[Spectres]] staged a mission to rescue the [[Jedi]] [[Kanan Jarrus]], their co-leader who had been [[Raid on the communications tower|captured]] during a mission to infiltrate the [[Imperial Communications Center]] at [[Jalath]]. With the help of the [[Devaronian]] [[crime lord]] [[Cikatro Vizago]], the rebels sent [[C1-10P|Chopper]] to infiltrate an [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]] in order to find Jarrus' location. They discovered that Jarrus was being transported to the [[Mustafar system]] aboard [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s flagship ''[[Sovereign]]''.

Using a stolen [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]], the rebels managed to infiltrate the ''Sovereign'' and used [[detonator]]s to unleash a massive electro-magnetic pulse that disabled the [[Star Destroyer]]. Jarrus' Jedi [[Padawan|apprentice]] [[Ezra Bridger]] then freed his master. While escaping the ship, Jarrus defeated [[the Grand Inquisitor]] in a duel which damaged the Star Destroyer's reactor core; setting of a chain-reaction which destroyed the Star Destroyer.

The Spectres managed to escape the stricken vessel before it exploded but were pursued by several [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. Several rebel ships from [[Phoenix Cell]] arrived and rescued them. Following Jarrus' rescue, the rebels met the former Jedi [[Ahsoka Tano]] and learned that they were part of a greater rebellion. For the rebels, the skirmish marked one of their earliest victories in the [[Galactic Civil War]], as well as an escalation of rebellious activity in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Stand up together. Because that's when we're strongest&mdash;as one.|Ezra Bridger|<ref name="Call to Action" />}}
[[File:Rebel_Resolve_thumb.png|thumb|left|250px|Kanan being interrogated by Imperial officials]]
Seeking to counteract [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[propaganda]] on [[HoloNet]], the [[Spectres]] hijacked the [[Imperial Communications Center]] at [[Jalath]] in order to upload a [[Computer spike|data spike]] that would allow them to broadcast a message to the public. However, [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], the [[Governor]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] had staged a trap to capture the rebels. Most of the Spectres managed to escape aboard the ''[[Phantom]]'', an auxiliary shuttle, but Jarrus stayed behind in order to prevent the Imperials from capturing his colleagues. After being cornered by both [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] and [[the Grand Inquisitor]], Jarrus surrendered himself to Grand Moff Tarkin.<ref name="Call to Action" />

Despite Jarrus' capture, Bridger managed to deliver a broadcast calling for [[Lothal]] to rise up against the Empire. In response, Tarkin had ordered the destruction of the communications tower, disrupting communications throughout Lothal.<ref name="Call to Action" /> Aboard Tarkin's flagship the ''[[Sovereign]]'', Jarrus was interrogated and [[torture]]d by Kallus and the Inquisitor but refused to divulge any information about the rebellion. After resisting a mind probe by an [[Interrogation droid|interrogator droid]], the Grand Inquisitor ordered that Jarrus be electrocuted. However, the Jedi still held out and refused to divulge any information on the rebellion and the mysterious [[Ahsoka Tano|Fulcrum]]. Dissatisfied, Tarkin ordered that Jarrus be transferred to the [[Mustafar system]] for further interrogation.<ref name="Rebel Resolve" />

==The rescue mission==
===Picking the trail===
{{Quote|I think we found something. Kanan is on Governor Tarkin's Destroyer, the ''Sovereign.'' It's still here above Lothal, but it's scheduled to leave soon.''"<br />"''Where to?''"<br />"''The Mustafar System? I've never heard of it. Hera?''"<br />"''I've only heard that name once. From Kanan. He said Mustafar is where Jedi go to die.|Sabine Wren and Hera Syndulla|<ref name="Rebel Resolve" />}}
[[File:HeraFulcrumHologram.png|thumb|right|250px|Hera conferring with Fulcrum]]
The capture of Kanan Jarrus created a deep sense of despondency among the Spectres.<ref name="Battle to the End">''[[Battle to the End]]''</ref> Due to the attention generated by Bridger's broadcast, Fulcrum ordered [[Hera Syndulla]] to abandon Jarrus in order to avoid exposing the Rebellion's existence to the Empire. While Syndulla reluctantly complied with Fulcrum's orders, the other crew member of the ''[[Ghost]]''&mdash;[[Ezra Bridger]], [[Sabine Wren]], [[C1-10P|Chopper]], and [[Garazeb Orrelios]] refused to abandon their co-leader. In a failed attempt to find Jarrus, the four rebels had earlier hijacked an [[All Terrain Defense Pod|AT-DP walker]] but barely managed to escape empty-handed.<ref name="Rebel Resolve" />

Unwilling to give up on Jarrus, the four rebels made a second attempt to find information about him. Working together, Chopper distracted Syndulla while the other rebels escaped aboard the ''[[Phantom]]'', the ''Ghost{{'s}}'' auxiliary vessel. The four rebels managed to find [[Cikatro Vizago]], a [[Devaronian]] [[crime lord]] who also operated as an information broker. Vizago was initially unwilling to help them, but changed his mind after Bridger used [[the Force]] to levitate a crate over him. Vizago then revealed that the Empire was using [[R4 courier droid]]s to courier information from [[Capital City]] to their [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruisers]] above Lothal. Syndulla caught up with them, but Bridger managed to convince her to support their rescue mission.<ref name="Rebel Resolve" />

Putting their plans into action, the crew of the ''Ghost'' disguised Chopper as an Imperial communications droid. Wren and Orellios managed to ambush an Imperial party and abduct their [[264|communications droid]]; allowing Chopper to take his place. The ruse worked and Chopper was able to board an Imperial light cruiser and download the necessary files. After completing his work, hopper transmitted a signal to the ''Ghost'' which launched a feint attack on the light cruiser. Amidst the confusion, Chopper was able through the ship's emergency air lock. Once in space, Chopper used his thrusters to rocket back to the ''Ghost''. While flying back to Lothal, the rebels learned that Jarrus was being transported aboard the ''Sovereign'' to the Mustafar system for further interrogation.<ref name="Rebel Resolve" />

===Assaulting the ''Sovereign''===
{{Quote|The transport you stole will get us close to the fleet over Mustafar. We know they have Kanan on Tarkin's Star Destroyer.''"<br />"''And it's surrounded by a bunch of other Star Destroyers.''"<br />"''Uh, lots more. We'll need a distraction to cover our entry. Sabine?''"<br />"''Engine room's here, all the power for the ship. If we could get inside the docking bay, I could rig something, black 'em out. But our transport ship's not gonna fit in there.|Hera Syndulla, Garazeb Orrelios, and Sabine Wren|<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />}}
[[File:Lothal_rebels_TIE.png|thumb|left|250px|The Spectres infiltrated the ''Sovereign'' with a stolen TIE fighter]]
Returning to Lothal, the Spectres decided to leave the ''Ghost'' behind since their ship was well-known to the Empire. The rebels stole an [[Evac One|Imperial]] [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] and used it to transport a [[Rebels TIE fighter|TIE fighter]] they had captured earlier during a [[Attack on Imperial Troop Transports|mission]] to [[Kothal]]. Using the stolen Imperial transport, they traveled to the Mustafar system only to find the ''Sovereign'' surrounded by several other [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]]. Once Bridger had established through [[the Force]] that Jarrus was indeed still alive and aboard the ''Sovereign'', the repainted TIE was dispatched by remote to join a passing TIE squadron that was entering the ''Sovereign''<nowiki/>'s main hangar. Once inside, the colorful TIE transmitted a massive electro-magnetic pulse that disabled the entire Star Destroyer.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />

After disabling the ''Sovereign'', the Rebels docked their stolen cruiser with the larger ship and boarded it to find and rescue Jarrus, leaving the droid, Chopper in charge of the getaway ship. However, Tarkin had anticipated a rescue attempt. Even as Brridger was freeing Jarrus from his cell, [[stormtrooper]] reinforcements from the other Star Destroyers were landing in the ''Sovereign{{'s}}'' hangar bay. Spooked by the Imperial reinforcements, Chopper moved the stolen Imperial transport from the ''Sovereign'', effectively cutting off the Rebels' escape. While Wren, Syndualla, and Orellios fought their way back to the hangar bay to reach their stolen TIE fighter, Bridger and Jarrus engaged the Grand Inquisitor in a [[lightsaber duel]] in the Star Destroyer's engineering section.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />

===Fall of the Grand Inquisitor===
{{Quote|You have no idea what you've unleashed here today. There are some things far more frightening than death.|The Grand Inquisitor talking to Kanan Jarrus, directly before letting go and falling into the exploding reactor|<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />}}
[[File:Fire_Across_the_Galaxy_thumb.png|thumb|right|250px|Jarrus and the Inquisitor's duel onboard the ''Sovereign'']]
While Jarrus used [[Ezra Bridger's first lightsaber]] to parry with the Inquisitor, Bridger used the Force to levitate [[Kanan Jarrus' lightsaber]] from the Inquisitor's belt. The two Jedi then took on the Grand Inquisitor. After a short, but intense two-on-one fight, the Inquisitor threw his lightsaber to knock Bridger down onto a nearby bridge, scarring his face and knocking him unconscious. Initially horrified, Jarrus quickly regained composure, took up Bridger's lightsaber and fought against the Inquisitor [[Jar'Kai|with two lightsabers]]. Following a lengthy duel, Jarrus managed to push the Grand Inquisitor to the edge of the bridge and destroy his lightsaber. The Grand Inquisitor's lightsaber fell into the ''Sovereign{{'s}}'' reactor core, triggering an explosive chain reaction that eventually destroyed the Star Destroyer. Knowing the punishment he would receive for his failure if he faced his master [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the Grand Inquisitor committed [[Death|suicide]] by throwing himself into the burning [[reactor]].<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />

Before the ship exploded, the Spectres escaped on their stolen TIE fighter and [[The Inquisitor's TIE Advanced]] starfighter. Meanwhile, Tarkin evacuated the stricken ''Sovereign'' aboard a [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class landing craft]]. Shortly later, the ''Sovereign'' exploded above [[Mustafar]] and broke into several pieces. Due to the departure of the Chopper's ''Gozanti'' cruiser, the rebels were unable to flee into [[hyperspace]]. Their starfighters were pursued by a swarm of Imperial TIE fighters. However, when all seemed lost, Chopper suddenly returned aboard the ''Gozanti'' cruiser, which was accompanied by the ''Ghost'' and three [[CR90 corvette]]s. These reinforcements had been sent by the [[Alderaanian]] [[senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] and the mysterious rebel operative known as Fulcrum. After the rebel reinforcements destroyed the pursuing TIE fighters, the Spectres docked their stolen TIEs with the ''Gozanti'' cruiser. Shortly later, the rebel fleet jumped into hyperspace.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Yes the naval action at Mustafar was a short-term win. But the Empire now knows the measure of the Alliance's naval strength.|[[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] writing in her journal|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
Once aboard the ''Gozanti'' cruiser, the rebels made their way back into the ''Ghost''. There, they were greeted by Chopper, who projected a HoloNet feed of Senator Bail Organa, and several rebel soldiers. Hera revealed that their cell was part of a wider rebel network. Shortly later, they were joined by Fulcrum herself, who revealed herself as the [[Great Jedi Purge]] survivor [[Ahsoka Tano]]. She complimented the crew of the ''Ghost'', particularly Bridger, for their bravery in taking on the Empire. News of the ''Sovereign''{{'s}} destruction eventually leaked to the planet Lothal, which stirred some unrest on that planet. In response, the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor]] dispatched his top enforcer, Darth Vader, to deal with the rebel problem.<ref name="Fire Across the Galaxy" />

The skirmish at Mustafar marked an escalation of rebellious activity against the Empire in the Outer Rim,<ref name="AlderaanCruiser" /> as well as one of the earliest rebel victories in the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Mustafar DB" />

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> shortly after Jarrus' death, his confrontation with the Grand Inquisitor was heard among numerous voices by Bridger as he passed through a [[Hyperspace tunneling|hyper tunnel]] while riding a [[Loth-wolf]] on his [[mission to the Lothal Jedi Temple]].<ref name="Wolves and a Door">{{Rebels|Wolves and a Door}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The rescue of Kanan Jarrus first appeared in "[[Fire Across the Galaxy]]," the finale of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', which premiered on [[March 2]], [[2015]] on [[Disney XD]]. It later appeared in [[Michael Kogge]]'s ''[[Battle to the End]]'', a chapter book adaptation of the final arc of the first season of ''Star Wars Rebels''.
