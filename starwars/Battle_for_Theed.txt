"'''Battle for Theed'''" is a free three-page promotional comic featured as ''Star Wars Roleplaying Game Free Sneak Preview''. First released at Comic-Con on [[July 21]], [[2000]] and given out at subsequent conventions, the short comic was written by [[Michael A. Stackpole]]. It takes place in [[32 BBY/Legends|32 BBY]], during the [[Invasion of Naboo/Legends|Occupation of Theed]].

==Plot summary==
During the [[Invasion of Naboo/Legends|Occupation of Naboo]], a small unit of the [[Naboo Underground]]; consisting of [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] [[Sia-Lan Wezz]], [[Deel Surool]], [[Arani Korden]], [[Rorworr (Naboo)|Rorworr]], and his [[Translator droid/Legends|translator droid]] [[TD0-2]]; are hiding in an alley in [[Theed/Legends|Theed]]. Planning to escape through a door at the end of the alley, Arani, Rorworr, and Sia-Lan provide a diversion while Deel attempts to [[Slicer/Legends|slice]] the lock. After surprising a group of [[B1-series battle droid/Legends|B1-series battle droid]]s and fending off their reinforcements, the fighters begin to retreat down the alley as a pair of [[Droideka/Legends|Droidekas]] show up. Deel opens the door, and all four members are surprised by what they see on the other side.
