An '''Ewok-themed diner''' was a popular themed [[Restaurant/Legends|diner]] located in [[CoCo Town/Legends|CoCo Town]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].<ref name="JA:TPB" /> In [[194 BBY]],<ref name="Date Note">The events of ''[[Jedi Academy: The Phantom Bully]]'' take place two [[Standard year/Legends|years]] after the events of [[Jedi Academy (novel)|''Jedi Academy'']], which states that [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] is 700 years old. Since ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' establishes that Yoda was [[Reproduction/Legends|born]] in [[896 BBY/Legends|896 BBY]], the events of ''Jedi Academy'' take place in [[196 BBY]], and ''The Phantom Bully'' must take place in [[194 BBY]].</ref> [[Coruscant Jedi Academy|Jedi academy]] [[Jedi Initiate/Legends|students]] [[Roan Novachez]], [[Pasha]], [[Gaiana]], and [[Tegan (Twi'lek)|Tegan]] visited the themed diner as well as a [[Hutt-themed diner|Hutt-]] and [[Starship-themed diner]] as part of a game to visit as many themed diners as possible in CoCo Town; however, the group gave up by the third diner. On a [[Standard day/Legends|Hexaday]], Novachez [[Writing system|wrote]] about the game and illustrated a drawing of the themed diner in his [[Roan Novachez's journal|journal]].<ref name="JA:TPB" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ewok-themed diner appeared in the [[2015]] [[Canon|non-canon]] young-reader's novel, ''[[Jedi Academy: The Phantom Bully]]'', written and illustrated by [[Jeffrey Brown]] as the third and final installment in the original story arc of the ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Academy]]'' series.<ref name="JA:TPB" />
