'''''Club Penguin''''' was a web-based [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online game|MMO]] [[Wikipedia:Virtual world|virtual world]] created by New Horizon Interactive LTD., later known as [[Disney Canada]]. Players controlled a penguin avatar to explore the virtual-world, which was filled with many activities and mini-games. While the game was free to play, players with a membership subscription could enjoy exclusive mini-game levels and less restrictions. Every few months, the game also held original events and cross-promotional events. Two such cross-promotional events were themed around ''[[Star Wars]]''.

In [[2013]], the [[Club Penguin Star Wars Takeover|first ''Star Wars'' event]] began in-game. The entirety of ''Club Penguin'' was redesigned around [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], and players were given missions and tasks that led up to the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|destruction of the Death Star]]. The [[Club Penguin Star Wars Rebels Takeover|second ''Star Wars'' event]], based on the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', began in [[2015]]. Both events had [[lightsaber duel]]s as exclusive features for players with active membership subscriptions. ''Club Penguin'' was later discontinued on [[March 29]], [[2017]], after a drop in active players and overall interest.

==Publisher's summary==
{{Quote|When we launched Club Penguin, we never expected it to be as big as it became.|Lance Priebe, on the success of the game|<ref name="Club Penguin BtS">{{ClubPenguinYouTube|video=RCWzhQNg7SY|text=Club Penguin: New Horizons Part 1 - A Brief History of Club Penguin|nolive=1}}</ref>}}
''Club Penguin'' was a children's web-based MMO virtual world. The simplistic gameplay involved players controlling a penguin avatar and interacting with other players.<ref name="MMO">{{WebCite|url=https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2017/jan/31/club-penguin-the-kids-website-that-became-an-internet-obsession|text=Club Penguin: the kids' website that became an internet obsession|author=Elena Cresci|date=2017-01-31|work=Guardian|archivedate=20230222190925}}</ref> Similar to other web browser games, it was developed using Adobe Flash.<ref name="Flash" /> The game was free-to-play but limited. Players could purchase a membership subscription to unlock all available features and remove restrictions on certain actions. ''Club Penguin'' also held limited events to make the experience fun and unique to which players would return.<ref name="About" />

==Contents==
{{Quote|A long time ago on an island far, far away&hellip;|Opening tagline of ''Club Penguin Star Wars'' Takeover|<ref name="Cinematic" />}}
[[File:CPSWTakeoverPromo.jpg|left|thumb|270px|Promotional image for ''Club Penguin Star Wars'' Takeover]]
The first ''Star Wars'' event in the game was a parody of the [[1977]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], titled [[Club Penguin Star Wars Takeover|''Club Penguin Star Wars'' Takeover]]. Some ''Club Penguin'' [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-playable characters]] dressed up and role-played as known ''Star Wars'' characters to guide players throughout the event. Special rooms based on locations such as the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]], the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] [[Yavin 4/Legends|Yavin 4]], and the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|Death Star]] [[Battle station/Legends|battle station]] were added, and some pre-existing rooms were lightly decorated to better suit the event. Players could play as the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] and were given tasks to complete, culminating in the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Death Star's destruction]]. With the completion of the event, players could obtain special [[Clothing/Legends|clothing]] items. Along with the missions, a ''[[Star Wars]]'' clothing catalog was added. Costumes such as the [[Rodian/Legends|Rodian]] [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Greedo/Legends|Greedo]], [[Rebel pilot/Legends|Rebel pilot]], [[Stormtrooper/Legends|Stormtrooper]], and more were added for players with a membership subscription to purchase. Players with a membership subscription could also wield a [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] with which to [[Lightsaber duel/Legends|duel]] each other.<ref name="SWTakeoverWiki">[[Club Penguin Star Wars Takeover|''Club Penguin Star Wars'' Takeover]]</ref>

[[File:SWRebelsTakeover.jpg|thumb|270px|Promotional image for ''Club Penguin Star Wars Rebels'' Takeover]]
The second ''Star Wars'' event had a storyline heavily based on the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]] of the [[2014]] [[Disney XD]] animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. The event was called [[Club Penguin Star Wars Rebels Takeover|''Club Penguin Star Wars Rebels'' Takeover]]. Unlike the previous event, only a few special rooms were added for the event, but half of the pre-existing island was fully redesigned to look like the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. Characters from the series, including the [[human]] [[Ezra Bridger]], the [[Jedi]] [[Kanan Jarrus]], the [[Mandalorian]] [[Sabine Wren]], the [[Twi'lek]] [[pilot]] [[Hera Syndulla]], and the [[Lasat]] [[Garazeb Orrelios]], were present during the event as non-playable characters. The [[Spectres]] gave players multiple tasks throughout the event. The [[lightsaber]] [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] mechanic was added back for the event for subscribers. In addition to dueling each other, players could also duel the leader of the [[Inquisitorius]], [[The Grand Inquisitor]], and receive exclusive rewards.<ref name="SWRTakeoverWiki">[[Club Penguin Star Wars Rebels Takeover|''Club Penguin Star Wars Rebels'' Takeover]]</ref>

==Development==
{{Quote|Together, we can build an island, create a community, change the world&hellip; And even tip an iceberg. Waddle On.|''Club Penguin'' Team, a hidden farewell message before the game's closure|<ref name="PC" />}}
''Club Penguin'' was released by New Horizon Interactive LTD. (now known as [[Disney Canada]]) on [[October 24]], [[2005]], for web browsers on [[Wikipedia:Micrisoft Windows|Microsoft Windows]] and [[Wikipedia:Mac OS|Mac OS]].<ref name="Intro" /> With great reviews and growing popularity, the web-based MMO was later released on [[Wikipedia:Miniclip|Miniclip]], an online game site. ''Club Penguin'' quickly grew even bigger in popularity and became Miniclip's number one game.<ref name="About" />

[[File:DefaultPenguin-ClubPenguin.png|185px|thumb|left|In-game default player avatar]]
By [[August]] [[2007]], Club Penguin was acquired by [[The Walt Disney Company]] to open up more development opportunities. The game was later hosted on its own website<ref name="About" /> and received the rating of "Everyone" by the [[Wikipedia:Entertainment Software Rating Board|ESRB]].<ref name="Cinematic" /> Since then, ''Club Penguin'' ran numerous original and cross-promotional events like those featuring ''Star Wars'' and other Disney properties.<ref name="About" /> Club Penguin expanded to the growing mobile platforms, releasing on [[Wikipedia:iOS|iPad]] on [[May 19]], [[2013]],<ref name="iOS Release Date" /> along with iPhone and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] on [[April 17]]<ref name="iPhone Release Date" /> and [[December 18]], 2014, respectively.<ref name="Android Release Date" />

On [[July 25]], 2013, ''Club Penguin Star Wars'' Takeover began. It was initially announced in an official blog post on [[June 20]], 2013.<ref name="SWTakeover">{{ClubPenguin|url=blog/2013/06/special-july-2013-party-announcement|text=Special July 2013 Party Announcement|nolive=1}}</ref> To help promote the event, ''Club Penguin'' had a booth from [[July 26]] until [[July 28]] of 2013 during [[Celebration Europe II]], offering physical prizes and exclusive in-game items.<ref name="Celebration">{{ClubPenguin|url=blog/2013/07/club-penguin-star-wars-celebration-europe-2013-0|text=Club Penguin at Star Wars Celebration Europe 2013!|nolive=1}}</ref> The event concluded on [[August 15]], 2013.<ref name="SWTakeoverWiki" />

On [[November 26]], 2014, an official blog post announced that another ''Star Wars'' cross-promotional event called ''Club Penguin Star Wars'' ''Rebels'' Takeover would occur.<ref name="RebelsTakeover">{{ClubPenguin|url=blog/2014/11/guess-whats-coming-club-penguin-january-2015|text=Guess What's Coming to Club Penguin in January 2015|nolive=1}}</ref> The event was added to help promote the series. On [[January 22]], [[2015]], the event began, and it later concluded on [[February 4]], 2015.<ref name="SWRTakeoverWiki" />

On [[January 30]], [[2017]], a blog post announced that ''Club Penguin'' would shut down on [[March 29]] of the same year. The game later held one last event before completely going offline. Contributing factors to ''Club Penguin''{{'s}} discontinuation included the decline in active player numbers, a steady decrease in revenue, and the need for Disney Canada to shift their focus fully on developing [[Wikipedia:Club Penguin Island|''Club Penguin Island'']].<ref name="Reason">{{WebCite|url=https://fourweekmba.com/why-did-club-penguin-shut-down|text=Why Did Club Penguin Shut Down?|author=Gennaro Cuofano|work=FourWeekMBA|archivedate=20230220092954}}</ref>
