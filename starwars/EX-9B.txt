'''EX-9B''', or '''Ninebee''' was an [[EX communication droid]] that was part of [[Jedi Master]] [[Silandra Sho]]'s [[Republic Pathfinder]] [[Master Sho's Pathfinder team|team]].<ref name="QFTHC" /> The droid was given the nickname "Nibs" by [[Obik Dennisol]], a [[Mirialan]] [[medic]] who served on the same team.<ref name="QFTHC" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Quest for the Hidden City]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> EX-9B and the team traveled to twin [[planet]]s [[Aubadas]] and [[Gloam]] in search of fellow pathfinder team [[Outer Rim Expedition Two-Five-Two]].<ref name="QFTHC" />
