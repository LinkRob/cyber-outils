'''Bosan Mound''' was a rock formation on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]] that had a [[Bosan Mound cantina|cantina]] built next to it.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the cantina had been abandoned.
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|canon}}
During 3 ABY, the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] retrieved items from a container next to a pile of bones in the mound.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bosan Mound appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The loot in the container is randomized as such there is not a [[canon]]ical set of items that Vess received.<ref name="Outlaws" />
