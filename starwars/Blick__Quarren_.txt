{{Quote|We're closing on the ''Cacophony'' now, General. From the damage that's already visible on the port side, and the number of escape pods in the vicinity, I'd guess the ''Jedi'' have the upper hand.|Blick, to General Viess|<ref name="TEoD" />}}
'''Blick''' was a [[Quarren]] who served as a [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. After the [[Stormship|Nihil Stormship]], the ''[[Cacophony]]'', was taken over by [[Jedi Master]]s [[Avar Kriss]] and [[Porter Engle]], and the [[captain]] of the [[Starship|ship]], [[Melis Shryke]], called for assistance, the ''[[Foregone Catastrophe]]'', piloted by Blick and commanded by [[General]] [[Abediah Viess]], arrived to subdue the [[Jedi]]. While the two observed the damage to the ''Cacophony'', Blick gave General Viess a rundown of what he observed from his seat.<ref name="TEoD" />
