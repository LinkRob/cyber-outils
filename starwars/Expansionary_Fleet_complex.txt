The '''Expansionary Fleet complex''' was a military complex of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet|Expansionary Defense Fleet]] located on the [[planet]] [[Naporar]]<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> in the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Naporar system]]</ref> The [[Taharim Academy]] was located on the complex.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> Between [[39 BBY|39]] and [[37 BBY]],<ref name="Dojo Date">[[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] begins instruction at [[Taharim Academy]] around [[39 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:WhenThrassMetThrawnDate|here]]. ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' establishes that he graudated from the academy in two [[Standard year|years]], around [[37 BBY]]. As [[Ar'alani|Ziara]] held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[Senior Cadet]] during Thrawn's tenure at the academy, it must have been during 39 and 37 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the [[Chiss]] [[cadet]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]], [[core name]] "Thrawn," underwent training at the complex while attending Taharim Academy. At another point, the [[sky-walker]] [[navigator]] [[Mitth'ali'astov|Ali'astov]] received her sky-walker training at the complex.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Expansionary Fleet complex was mentioned in in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
