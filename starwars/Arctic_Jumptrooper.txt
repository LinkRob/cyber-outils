'''Arctic Jumptroopers''' were [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Jumptrooper]]s specialized to operate in cold environments. They wore uniforms identical to those worn by [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtroopers]]. These troopers could be seen working alongside regular "snowtroopers" in arctic operations.<ref name="Commander" />
