The stitching style is from the Fourteenth, the particular dye coloring was first used in the Seventeenth, and the edging style from the Eighteenth. The '''Eighteenth Era''' was a period of [[time]] in the history of the [[Unknown Regions]] [[Garwian Unity]] nation. It was preceded by the [[Seventeenth Era]].<ref name="CR" /> By [[19 BBY]],<ref name="FlashDate">The flashbacks of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' take place before the novel's main events, which take place in [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> on the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] [[Solitair]], the [[Garwian]] [[dame]] [[Mimott]] showcased a modern cloth that incorporated an edging style from the Eighteenth Era to the [[Chiss]] [[officer]]s [[Ar'alani]] and [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]].<ref name="CR" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Eighteenth Era was mentioned in the flashbacks of the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="CR" />
