{{Quote|I'm going to give you an opportunity to turn around now and make things right, Poggle. Otherwise you'll be in pieces before you clear the asteroid belts.|Orson Callan Krennic, to Poggle the Lesser, referring to Frist's flotilla|<ref name="Catalyst" />}}
'''Frist''' was an individual who served as a [[captain]] in the [[Republic Navy|navy]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], during the [[Clone Wars]]. In the latter half of the war, Frist served as the [[flotilla]] [[commander]] of the [[Geonosis Republic flotilla|fleet garrisoned]] over [[Geonosis]], protecting the top-secret [[Project Stardust|construction project]] occurring there from [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] attack. After the [[Geonosian]] drones revolted in an effort to free their leader, [[Archduke]] [[Poggle the Lesser]], Frist was ordered to track the Archduke's personal [[Starship|ship]], which had left the [[planet]]'s [[orbit]]. An order from [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] to fire on the vessel came too late for Frist, and Poggle was able to successfully escape.<ref name="Catalyst" />
