'''Darth Voord''' was a [[Sith Lord]] who featured in the story "[[The Legend of the Noble Bonbrak]]". Described as "terrifying", Voord was said to have taken great pleasure in using his destructive [[heat ray]] to boil oceans. In "The Legend of the Noble [[Bonbrak]]," the story's hero was at one point at the mercy of [[Darth]] Voord, which would cause the audience of the story to like the story more.<ref name="PoV" />

Centuries after the story's publication, Voord's name was used as part of a prayer by [[Sith cultist|cultists]] of the [[Sith Eternal]] as they searched the catacombs beneath the [[Sith Citadel]] on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]]<ref name="SotS" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="SotS date">The flashback in which Voord is mentioned in ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' takes place concurrently with [[Darth Vader (2020) 11|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 11]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of to [[3 ABY]].</ref> A leader of each cultist group would lead the others in chanting the name, along with the names of other [[Sith Lord]]s.<ref name="SotS" />
