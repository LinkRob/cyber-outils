'''''Bounty Hunters'' 14''' is the fourteenth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. It was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]] and [[Arif Prianto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[July 7]], [[2021]].

==Publisher's summary==
''"THE FOLLOWING" Hunted by the mysterious assassin DEATHSTICK, a wounded VALANCE and his reluctant partner DENGAR are running out of options! T'ONGA is outgunned and outnumbered&hellip;but she does have one last surprise up her sleeve! And the shadowy force behind all of the danger makes
its move!''
