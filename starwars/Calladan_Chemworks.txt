'''Calladan Chemworks''' was a company on [[Gorse]] which handled the refining of the valuable [[mineral]] [[thorilide]]. The ''[[Cynda Dreaming]]'' was picking up a load of thorilide for refining at this company, but was damaged in transit and ultimately destroyed by the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Ultimatum]]'', whose commanding officer had decided it would be more efficient to simply destroy the ship and await a replacement than wait for it be to be repaired.<ref name="AND" />
