The "'''Big Bad Loth-wolf'''" was a [[Loth-wolf]] character in a story told by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] to [[Din Grogu|Grogu]]. In the story, the Loth-wolf utilized an [[All Terrain Scout Transport]] to visit [[Gamorrean]]s.<ref name="MandoAndChild" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Big Bad Loth-wolf was first mentioned in the [[2023]] [[Canon|non-canon]] children's book ''[[The Mandalorian and Child]]'', written and illustrated by [[Jeffrey Brown]].<ref name="MandoAndChild" /> It is based on the [[Wikipedia:Big Bad Wolf|Big Bad Wolf]] from the fable [[Wikipedia:The Three Little Pigs|The Three Little Pigs]].<ref name="Three Little Pigs">{{BookCite|container=The Three Little Pigs|author=[[Wikipedia:Joseph Jacobs|Jacobs, Joseph]]|title=English Fairy Tales|date=1890}}</ref>
