'''Botto Fna''' was a news [[Journalist|presenter]] for [[Holonet News Toshara]], a broadcast of the [[HoloNet News]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Fna reported on the weather from the [[Mirogana Meteo-Tower]] in the moon's [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Mirogana]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

Fna's broadcasts that [[Standard year|year]] included a forecast for high floods in [[Ascaal district]] near the [[Ascada River]], a cyclone over the [[Magadua plain]] along with its resulting damage, hazardous winds along the [[Central Wideroad]], an [[ion front]] descending towards Mirogana, a [[tornado]] warning for [[Rajatha Ridge]], the early start to [[Squall Season]], an extreme erosion event at [[Daua Rock]] and the current wind speeds at [[Mirogana Gate]].

==Behind the scenes==
Botto Fna appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, Botto Fna can be heard during a number of Holonet News broadcasts that played at the security checkpoint that led from [[Mirogana Spaceport]] in Mirogana.<ref name="Outlaws" />
