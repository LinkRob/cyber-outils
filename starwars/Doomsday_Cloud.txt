'''Doomsday Cloud''' was a notorious group of well-established [[smuggler]]s that was active during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. The group wrought havoc and destruction without mercy, swallowing all in their path.<ref name="GEStaff" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GEDates">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> they were enemies of the [[Ithorian]] [[antiques dealer]] [[Dok-Ondar]].<ref name="GEStaff" />

==Behind the scenes==
Doomsday Cloud were created as a backstory element for the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="GEStaff" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref>
