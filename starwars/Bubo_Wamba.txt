'''Bubo Wamba''' was an [[Aqualish]] [[farmer]] who ran the [[Milk Stand]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> Four generations of the [[Wamba family]] had kept [[bantha]]s on their [[farm]] on [[Tatooine]]. After years of working for his [[parent]]s, Wamba left for Batuu with his dreams and a pack of banthas<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> and founded the farming business [[Bubo Wamba Family Farms]].<ref name="Cookbook" /> His locally farmed [[blue milk]] was an instant hit with the locals. In addition, his stand also sold a rare imported [[green milk]] coming from [[thala-siren]]s,<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> large marine [[mammal]]s<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> found on various [[Water|aquatic]] [[planet]]s.<ref name="Cookbook" />

Wamba often spent his [[time]] in his stall or with his banthas.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> Wamba was fascinated with the mix of cultures at the outpost, taking the opportunity to meet new people from across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />
