This '''species''' was a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]]. They were [[humanoid]], with [[Color|white]] skin, and a singular horn growing out of the left side of their forehead. They also possessed serrated [[Tooth|teeth]], pointed [[ear]]s, dark, circular [[Hair|eyebrows]], and irises in their [[eye]]s that almost resembled [[Star|suns]]. One member of this species, [[Aman]], fought the [[Jedi]] [[Nakime]] and her [[Nakime's Jedi Master|master]] above the [[sacred tree forest]]. Like many [[dark side of the Force|dark side users]], Aman possessed red and yellow eyes, and covered her body in what appeared to be superficial red, black, and blue markings.<ref name="TBoP" />
