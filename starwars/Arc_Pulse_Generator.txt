The '''Arc Pulse Generator''', nicknamed "'''the Duchess'''" after [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]], was a [[superweapon]] designed by [[Sabine Wren]] during her time at the [[Imperial Academy of Mandalore|Imperial Academy]] on [[Mandalore]]. The weapon targeted the [[beskar]] alloy used in [[Mandalorian]] [[Mandalorian armor|armor]], superheating it, resulting in the disintegration of the individual wearing it, though it could also be reconfigured to target other materials such as plastoid, a component of stormtrooper armor. Wren defected from the [[Galactic Empire]] and attempted to destroy the prototype upon realizing what the Empire intended to do with the weapon, but the Empire and their regents on Mandalore salvaged and reconstructed it. The weapon was unleashed during the [[Mandalorian Civil War (Imperial Era)|civil war]] that occurred on Mandalore during the [[Imperial Era]] until Wren and [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] finally destroyed it.

==History==
===Creation===
{{Quote|&hellip;the Arc Generator's energy pulse is drawn to the beskar alloy in the Mandalorians' armor, superheating it and instantly vaporizing the target, and all the while, doing no harm to our Imperial stormtroopers.|Tiber Saxon, to Grand Admiral Thrawn|<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />}}
[[Standard year|Years]] before the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] against<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" /> the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="The Duchess DB" /> [[Sabine Wren]] was a [[cadet]] at the [[Imperial Academy of Mandalore|Imperial Academy]] on [[Mandalore]]. Wren, the top of her class, was a highly competitive student and often took on challenges other students found too daunting. One of those was the development of a [[superweapon]] that became the Arc Pulse Generator. The weapon was cruelly nicknamed "the Duchess" after the late pacifist [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] who ruled Mandalore during the [[Clone Wars]]. Wren developed the weapon to target [[beskar]] alloy used in [[Mandalorian]] [[Mandalorian armor|armor]] and disintegrate the [[soldier]] inside.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />

Once Wren realized that the Empire had tested the weapon on her own people and intended to continue using it, she sabotaged it, destroyed the plans, and defected from the Empire, eventually joining with [[Spectres|rebels]] who became part of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Wren believed she had destroyed the weapon, but the Empire salvaged and rebuilt it. Rumors spread that the Empire had tested such a device, but there was never confirmation amongst other Mandalorian clans that the Duchess was real.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" /> In fact, by [[6 BBY]] the Empire had used the Arc Pulse Generator at least once to [[Massacre of the Dizonites|exterminate]] the [[Dizonite]]s, the [[Sentience|sentient]] natives of [[Dizon Fray]] who had been resisting the construction of a refueling station in their homeworld.<ref name="Rise" />

===Destruction===
{{Quote|You came here to make things right. Will the future of Mandalore be one of honor or cowardice? Hope or fear? The choice is yours.|Bo-Katan convincing Sabine to destroy the Arc Pulse Generator, rather than use it against the Empire|<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />}}
[[File:WrenWarrior_TheDuchess_Beskar_HeroesOfMandalorePart2.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Arc Pulse Generator targets a Clan Wren warrior's beskar armor]]
A [[Mandalorian Civil War (Imperial Era)|civil war]] broke out on Mandalore after [[Clan Wren]] rebelled against [[Clan Saxon]], the ruling clan of Mandalore that was allied with the Empire. [[Governor]] [[Tiber Saxon]] prepared the weapon for use against the insurrectionists, and fitted it on an [[AT-DP Arc Cannon Prototype]]. Though it was weaker than Wren's original design, Saxon ordered it tested against Clan Wren during the war, and the weapon was used to kill multiple members of the clan. [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] was disappointed that the weapon was not up to its full capabilities, and suggested that Governor Saxon capture Wren so she could modify and improve it.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />

After witnessing the destructive capabilities of the weapon, the rebels planned to destroy it, with Wren volunteering to lead the mission, as she felt personally responsible. Wren boarded [[Tiber Saxon's Star Destroyer|Saxon's Star Destroyer]] with a strike team to destroy the weapon and erase all data on it from the Imperial databanks. As [[Ezra Bridger]], [[Fenn Rau]], and the others engaged Imperial forces in the hangar, Wren and [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] located the weapon. However, they were captured by Saxon, who threatened to kill Bo-Katan if Wren did not fix the weapon. Thinking quickly, Wren tricked him into believing that she amplified the weapon's power. Instead, she adjusted it to target [[stormtrooper]] [[Stormtrooper armor|armor]] instead of the beskar in the Mandalorian's armor, and when Saxon activated it, he incapacitated his own Imperial troops. Wren then used the [[Darksaber]] to breach the weapon's core, and it exploded and destroyed the Star Destroyer as Wren and her strike team escaped.<ref name="Heroes of Mandalore" />

===Later use===
{{Quote|Utilizing a variation of an energy weapon created at the Imperial Academy on Mandaalore, the Imperials lowered several pulse arc reactors from beneath ''Gozanti''-class cruisers into the waters of Dizon Fray and activated them. The salted water of Dizon Fray's moon proved to be exceptionally conductive and the surge in connected currents electrocuted the majority of the Dizonite population.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
More of the weapons, however, would be deployed by [[6 BBY]] against the noncompliant [[Dizonite]] race. After the Dizonites pushed back against the Empire's plans to establish a refueling center on the world of [[Dizon Fray]], the Imperial commanders were given permission to exterminate the species. They did so by dropping modified Arc Pulse Generators from [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruisers]] into the [[moon]]'s oceans and activating them. The [[salt]]ed water of Dizon Fray's [[ocean]]s was extremely conductive, and the electrical charge was carried by its currents, slowly electrocuting the majority of the Dizonite population to [[death]] over the course of several [[standard hour|hours]].<ref name="Rise" />
