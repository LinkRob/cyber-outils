A '''foot soldier''' was a component of the [[board game]] [[shogi]]. After the former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] was injured during a [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] with the [[Jedi lord (Empire)|Jedi lord]] [[Hanrai]] aboard the [[Imperial Dreadnought]] ''[[Reverent]]'', the former [[Jedi]] known as [[the Traveler]] helped him escape the [[Starship|ship]] as it [[Destruction of the Reverent|collapsed]]. To keep the Ronin engaged, the Traveler suggested they verbally play a game of shogi, beginning by moving their fourth foot soldier one forward. The Ronin, however, found the suggestion boring and instead wanted to learn of the Traveler's past.<ref name="Ronin" />

==Behind the scenes==
Foot soldiers were mentioned in the [[2021]] non-[[canon]] novel ''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]'', by [[Emma Mieko Candon]].<ref name="Ronin" /> In the real world, pawns are pieces in the [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japanese]] board game [[Wikipedia:Shogi|shogi]] that each player has nine of and can be moved one space forward. In [[Wikipedia:Japanese|Japanese]], pawns are known as ''fuhyou'' (歩兵), literally meaning "foot soldier."<ref name="Shogi">{{WebCite|url=https://www.shogi.cloud/en/pawn-and-tokin|text=Pawn and Tokin in Shogi|work=[https://www.shogi.cloud shogi.cloud]|archivedate=20250717145309}}</ref>
