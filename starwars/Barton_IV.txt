'''Barton IV''' was an icy [[planet]] where the [[Galactic Empire]] maintained a [[Imperial Depot (Barton IV)|supply depot]] that stored armor and other equipment. The outpost was commanded by [[Clone Commander]] [[Mayday]] and his [[Mayday's squad|squad]]. When the squad's resources began to run low, Mayday put in a request for reinforcements, asking for more men and for new equipment, as the [[sensor beacon]]s on the perimeter of the base were old and unreliable. It was not until many rotations later that a force led by [[Lieutenant]] [[Nolan]] and including the [[clone trooper]] [[Crosshair|CT-9904]] "Crosshair" were sent to the outpost on the planet.

==Description==
Barton IV<ref name="DB">{{Databank|barton-iv|Barton IV}}</ref> was a [[Temperature|frozen]] and barren [[planet]] covered in snow. Its terrain was [[mountain]]ous and included [[cave]]s. Avalanches were known to occur. [[Black ice vulture]]s patrolled the skies in search of food<ref name="The Outpost" /> and at least one [[ice wyrm]] wandered underground near an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Depot (Barton IV)|supply depot]], kept at bay only by high-frequency [[sensor beacon]]s.<ref name="Return" />

==History==
In [[19 BBY]], after the [[Proclamation of the New Order]], [[Clone Commander]] [[Mayday]] and a [[Mayday's squad|squad]] of at least eleven [[clone trooper]]s which included [[Hexx]] and [[Veetch]] were sent by the [[Galactic Empire]] to guard a supply depot located on the planet. Over time equipment such as the [[sensor beacon]]s degraded due to the conditions of the planet leading to multiple raids by local [[Unidentified raiders|raiders]] from a [[Raiders' hideout|hideout]] close to the depot. This caused Mayday to request the Empire for reinforcements however his request was denied. At some point, due to a lack of reinforcements, the depot was unguarded with only Mayday, Hexx, and Veetch remaining from the previous twelve clone troopers that were sent to guard the depot.<ref name="The Outpost" />

During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="The Outpost Date">{{TBBDate|212}}</ref> [[Lieutenant]] [[Nolan]], [[Crosshair|CT-9904]] "Crosshair," and three clone troopers arrived at the depot in a [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class transport shuttle]] to secure the cargo at the depot until week's end where it would be transferred offworld. Having to fight off local raiders who killed both Hexx and Veetch, Crosshair and Mayday made a last-ditch effort to eliminate their enemy, in which Mayday was mortally wounded. Helping the dying clone back to the depot, Crosshair begged Nolan to help him, but the officer dismissed Mayday as an expendable asset. As Mayday died, Crosshair decided to shoot Nolan, killing him. He would be arrested for treason and imprisoned.<ref name="The Outpost" />

After Crosshair later escaped Imperial prison with the help of [[Omega]], the two would be reunited with [[Clone Force 99]]. The squad would visit Barton IV to access the Imperial terminal there and decrypt an Imperial datapad. Accessing the depot, they would divert power away from the sensor beacons, leading to an encounter with a wyrm. They had to undergo a risky operation to lure the beast outside the perimeter and reactivate the beacons before they were able to depart the planet.<ref name="The Return">{{TBB|The Return}}</ref>
