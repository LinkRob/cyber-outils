'''Bee-Toh'''<ref name="CoH">{{Visions|The Ninth Jedi: Child of Hope}}</ref> served as a [[Jedi hunter]] for the [[Sith]] sometime after [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{WebCite|url=https://www.ign.com/articles/star-wars-visions-anime-jedi-rock-opera|text=Star Wars Anime Visions Will Include What Became of the Jedi, a Rock Opera, and More|author=Daniel Robson|work=[[Wikipedia:IGN|''IGN'']]|archivedate=20210922124827}} establishes that the events of "The Ninth Jedi" occur after those of {{Film|IX}}, which are dated to [[35 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The individual was tasked with hunting and capturing [[sabersmith]] [[Lah Zhima]]. After capturing him, the individual ordered their [[Unidentified Jedi Hunter (Lah Kara)|partner]] to pursue the sabersmith's daughter, [[Lah Kara]].<ref name="Ninth Jedi" />
