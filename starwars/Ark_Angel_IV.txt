The '''''Ark Angel IV''''' was a [[starship]] owned by the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]].<ref name="Aphra 5">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 5|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 5]]</ref> Originating from the [[time]] of the [[High Republic Era|High Republic]], the vessel was left abandoned on the [[planet]] [[Dianth]], where it remained into the [[Imperial Era]]. After Aphra's starship, the ''[[Ark Angel III]]'', was destroyed by the [[Tagge's fleet|fleet]] of the [[House of Tagge]] scion [[Ronen Tagge]] on Dianth, Aphra's associate, the [[bounty hunter]] [[Krrsantan]], discovered the High Republic vessel. Aphra's crew boarded the starship, determining that it was still functional. [[Pilot]]ing the vessel to [[Cantonica]], where Tagge resided, Aphra attracted the attention of Tagge's [[fleet]], which proceeded to pursue her. Tagge's forces damaged the vessel's main [[Engine|thruster]], and Aphra allowed them to board her ship. The damaged High Republic vessel was set down on Cantonica alongside one of Tagge's ships, and Aphra's crew was taken [[prisoner]] by Tagge.<ref name="Aphra 4" /> While Aphra and her crew were taken to Tagge's [[Canto Bight]] [[Ronen Tagge's Private Penthouse|penthouse]], the starship was left on a landing pad outside. After defeating Tagge, Aphra and her allies made their escape in the starship, which was given the name ''Ark Angel IV''.<ref name="Aphra 5" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Ark Angel IV''<ref name="Aphra 5" /> first appeared in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 4|fourth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series, written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Marika Cresta]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 4" /> on [[September 30]] of that year.<ref name="Aphra 4 Date">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/82207/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2020_4|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020) #4}}</ref> Its name was established in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 5|following issue]], written by Wong<ref name="Aphra 5" /> and published on [[October 28]], 2020.<ref name="Aphra 5 Date">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/82208/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2020_5|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020) #5}}</ref> The series' [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 22|twenty-second issue]],<ref name="Aphra 22">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 22|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 22]]</ref> published on [[July 27]], [[2022]],<ref name="Aphra 22 Date">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/98323/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2020_22|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020) #22}}</ref> referred to the vessel as the ''Ark Angel V''.<ref name="Aphra 22" />
