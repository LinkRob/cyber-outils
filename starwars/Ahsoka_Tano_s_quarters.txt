'''Ahsoka Tano's quarters''' were the living quarters used by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]. Located inside the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], Tano's quarters were a small and simple space where Tano would [[Meditation|meditate]].

==Description==
Ahsoka Tano's quarters<ref name="Vol 1">''[[Forces of Destiny: Daring Adventures: Volume 1]]''</ref> were a room in the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="FOD" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Comprehensive">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] that served as [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]'s personal living quarters. The small room was rectangular with [[Color|green]] walls and a single [[Viewport|window]] opposite the door, allowing a view of the [[Imperial City|Galactic City]] skyline when the blinds were retracted. It was plain save for a thin [[sleep]]ing pad with a [[blanket]], a side table, and a cabinet.<ref name="Hope">''[[Forces of Destiny: Tales of Hope & Courage]]''</ref> A red [[Jedi crest]] adorned the floor.<ref name="Assassin" />

==History==
[[File:AhsokaInHerRoom.png|thumb|right|320px|Ahsoka meditates in her quarters.]]
In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="TCW Date">{{TCWcanondates|307}}</ref> during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], Tano experienced a [[Force vision|vision]] of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] being attacked by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Aurra Sing]]. After speaking with Amidala about the vision, Tano returned to<ref name="Assassin">{{TCW|Assassin (episode)}}</ref> her<ref name="Comprehensive" /> quarters in the evening and, while seated on the floor of the room and [[Meditation|meditating]], experienced another vision of Sing aiming a [[sniper rifle]] at the senator. Due to the visions, Tano would accompany Amidala as additional security to the [[Alderaan Refugee Conference]] on the<ref name="Assassin" /> planet<ref name="Comprehensive" /> [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Assassin" />

Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Forces of Destiny—Ahsoka & Padmé]]'' to [[21 BBY]].</ref> Tano walked to her quarters to meditate following a [[Jedi training|practice]] [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] with her friend and fellow Padawan [[Barriss Offee]] in the [[Jedi Temple dojo]] when Amidala came to visit her. The senator apologized for interrupting Tano's meditation, but Tano replied that it was fine, asking if everything was all right. Amidala entered Tano's quarters and requested that she help her with security in the lead-up to an important meeting with a group of [[Arthurian delegates]].<ref name="FOD" /> The quarters were included in a [[Maz Kanata's Journal|journal]] written by<ref name="Hope" /> the [[Pirate Queen|pirate]]<ref name="Comprehensive" /> [[Maz Kanata]].<ref name="Hope" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ahsoka Tano's quarters first appeared in "[[Assassin (episode)|Assassin]],"<ref name="Assassin" /> the seventh episode in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''. The episode premiered on [[October 22]], [[2010]].<ref name="Assassin Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode307.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Assassin}}</ref> It was first identified in the [[2017]] chapter book ''[[Forces of Destiny: Daring Adventures: Volume 1]]''.<ref name="Vol 1" /> In "Assassin," the floor of the room features the Jedi crest depicted on the floor in red.<ref name="Assassin" /> However, in the one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] ''[[Forces of Destiny—Ahsoka & Padmé]]'',<ref name="FOD" /> written by [[Beth Revis]], illustrated by [[Valentina Pinto]], and published as part of the [[Star Wars: Forces of Destiny (comic series)|''Star Wars: Forces of Destiny'']] miniseries on [[January 24]], [[2018]],<ref name="Comixology">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-Forces-of-Destiny-Ahsoka-Padme/digital-comic/595811|text=Star Wars Adventures: Forces of Destiny&mdash;Ahsoka & Padme|nolive=1}}</ref> the floor has no markings.<ref name="FOD" />
