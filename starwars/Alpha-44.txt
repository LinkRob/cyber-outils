'''Alpha-44''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Rho-class transport shuttle|''Rho''-class transport shuttle]].<ref name="Breach" /> During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Season3Date" /> it was stolen by [[Echo]], a member of the [[Clone Underground]] and the rogue [[clone commando]] unit known as the [[Clone Force 99|Bad Batch]]. Flying the shuttle to the [[Bora Vio cloning facility|cloning facilities]] on the planet [[Bora Vio]], he rendezvoused with the other members of the Bad Batch and together, along with their prisoner, the former Imperial [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]], subsequently traveled to [[Imperial Station 003]] above [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Breach" /> When they arrived at the planet [[Wayland]], their shuttle would be shot down by [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|V-wing starfighters]], but not before the Bad Batch were able to disembark into the [[forest]] surrounding [[Tantiss Base]].<ref name="Flash Strike">{{TBB|Flash Strike}}</ref>
