'''''Darth Vader – Black, White & Red'' 4''' is the fourth and final issue of the [[2023]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] anthology miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Darth Vader – Black, White & Red]]''. The issue was written by [[Jason Aaron]], [[Steve Orlando]], and [[Frank Tieri]], llustrated by [[Leonard Kirk]], [[Paul Davidson]], [[Danny Earls]], [[Mark Morales]], and [[Romulo Fajardo Jr.]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[July 26]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''TRAPPED ON THE FROZEN PLANET HOTH! FRANK TIERI'' sends VADER to HOTH. STEVE ORLANDO'' goes cerebral with THE CORRUPTION OF DARTH VADER'S MIND! PLUS: The Final Chapter of JASON AARON & LEONARD KIRK's dark tales!''
