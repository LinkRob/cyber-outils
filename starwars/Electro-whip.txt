An '''electro-whip''', also called a '''Zygerrian slaver whip''', an '''electrowhip''', or a '''lightwhip''', was an electrified whip that was the preferred method of domination for the subjugation of [[Slavery|slaves]] taken by the [[Zygerrian Slave Empire]]. It consisted of a metal grip and an electrified wire that glowed when powered up. The wire could be retracted into the grip when powered down. It was incredibly painful, but typically not physically damaging, as [[Zygerrian]]s did not want to damage their merchandise irreparably. Electro-whips could be effectively used against [[lightsaber]]s. Members of the [[Mining Guild]] were also known to use the weapon during the [[Imperial Era]]. The alternate name "[[lightwhip]]" typically referred to an unrelated lightsaber variant with pure [[plasma]] blades.

==Description==
[[File:Scout-and-Magna.png|thumb|left|250px|[[IG-100 MagnaGuard]] lashes a clone scout trooper with an electro-whip.]]
Electro-whips consisted of a metal grip and an electrified, retractable wire that glowed when powered up, and automatically retracted into the grip when the weapon was powered off. The whips were designed to stun, and although they were painful, they were not typically physically damaging, as [[Zygerrian]] [[enslaver]]s preferred not to irreparably damage their merchandise.<ref name="Electro-Whip" /> Electro-whips could withstand the blade of a [[lightsaber]] while powered up.<ref name="Escape from Kadavo" /> "[[Lightwhip]]" was a known alternate term for the weapon,<ref name="Lightsaber Collection">''[[Star Wars: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> although the word usually referred to a specialized lightsaber variant with flexible blades made of pure [[plasma]].<ref name="GAG">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref>

==History==
Electro-whips were heavily associated with the [[Zygerrian Slave Empire]] and its practice of [[slavery]] and, for this reason, were also known as Zygerrian slaver whips.<ref name="TotD Trivia Gallery" /> The Zygerrian slaver [[Darts D'Nar]] attempted to fight [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] with his whip when she captured him during the [[battle on Kiros]], but she easily subdued him before the arrival of her Master, [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="Kidnapped">{{TCW|Kidnapped}}</ref> Subsequently, Skywalker, Tano, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] and [[R2-D2]] undertook a [[Mission to Zygerria|mission]] to find and rescue the kidnapped [[Togruta]] colonists of [[Kiros]].<ref name="SotR" />

Kenobi was captured during the mission while attempting to rescue [[Governor]] [[Gupat Roshti]], and was whipped by his captors. Later, at the [[Royal Slave Auction]], [[Queen]] [[Miraj Scintel]] loaned her personal whip to a disguised Skywalker and asked that he whip Kenobi to prove he was a slaver, but Skywalker instead started a fight that led to all the non-[[droid]] members of the team being captured and subdued, mostly with electro-whips.<ref name="SotR" /> Later, during his and Tano's escape from [[Zygerria]], Skywalker attempted to duel the [[Sith Lord]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] with Scintel's electro-whip, but performed poorly with Dooku using [[Dooku's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] to pull the whip out of his hand.<ref name="Escape from Kadavo" />

[[File:HunterVsRaney-Rampage.png|thumb|right|250px|Hunter vs Raney]]
When [[Clone Force 99]] went on a [[Mission to Ord Mantell (Clone Force 99)|rescue mission]] to recover the [[rancor]] [[Muchi]] from [[Zygerrian]] [[enslaver]]s on [[Ord Mantell]]. The slave owner [[Raney]] tried to use his electro-whip to subdue the [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] but [[Hunter]] manage to overpower the electricity and disarm Raney, before a brief [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand struggle]] in which the slaver was knocked unconscious.<ref name="Rampage">{{TBB|Rampage (episode)}}</ref>

[[File:Proach_vs_Kanan.png|thumb|left|250px|Electro-whips were still active in the Imperial Era.]]
During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Mining Guild]] [[foreman]] and Slave Master [[Proach]], in charge of the [[ore crawler]] [[Crawler 413-24|413-24]] on the occupied [[planet]] [[Lothal]], used a whip to keep the slaves aboard his vehicle in line. When members of the [[Spectres]] [[Hijacking of Crawler 413-24|hijacked]] the vehicle to use its [[transmitter]], Proach fought [[Jedi Knight]] [[Kanan Jarrus]], briefly managing to disarm him of his [[Kanan Jarrus' lightsaber|lightsaber]] during the fight. However, the slaver was ultimately defeated with the help of [[Garazeb Orrelios]].<ref name="Crawler Commandeers" />

==Behind the scenes==
Electro-whips first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|season four]] episode "[[Kidnapped]],"<ref name="Kidnapped" /> and were first identified in its corresponding [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry.<ref name="Electro-Whip" />
