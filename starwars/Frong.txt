'''Frong''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Frong (species)|Frong]] [[species]]. It was situated in the [[Frong system]] in the [[Ojoster sector]], in grid square N-7 of th e[[Standard Galactic Grid]].

==History==
===Republic Era===
[[File:RiseofRedBlade-Jedi.png|thumb|left|300px|Jedi Generals Iskat Akaris and Tualon Yaluna (both pictured) made their way to Frong during the final days of the Clone Wars.]]
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="FrongDate">The [[mission]] to [[Frong]] ended during the execution of [[Order 66]] which is dated to [[19 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore the mission to Frong took place in 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> during the closing days of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Condefederate Supreme Commander]] [[General]] [[Grievous]] launched an attack on the planet with his [[Separatist Droid Army|battle droid forces]], leaving a fiery inferno in their wake. The [[Jedi Order]] dispatched [[Jedi General]]s [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]], [[Tualon Yaluna]] and their [[Tualon Yaluna's company|company]] of [[clone trooper]]s, led by [[Clone Captain|Captain]] [[Spider (Clone Captain)|Spider]], to investigate, bringing [[Jedi Knight]] [[Sunghi Silpari]] with them. After searching a swathe of the planet without successfully locating Grievous,<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''</ref> who was instead [[Battle of Utapau|located and slain]] by [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] on the Outer Rim planet [[Utapau]],<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> the group rested.<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />

During the interlude, the clones recieved [[Order 66]]<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> from [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], who was secretly the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Sidious and the puppet master of the war.<ref name="ROTS" /> Forced to obey the command thanks to the [[behavioral modification biochip]]s implanted within their [[brain]]s,<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref> the clones shot Silpari and leveled their [[blaster]]s at Akaris and Yaluna, surprising both [[Jedi]], the latter feeling the simultaneous deaths of their kind across the [[galaxy]]<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> thanks to Order 66.<ref name="ROTS" /> Spider proceeded to hold out his holoprojector, which displayed a [[Darth Sidious|cloaked figure]]&mdash;Sidious himself. The individual offered Akaris her life and information the Order had withheld from her in exchange for joining him. She took the offer, ignoring Yaluna's warning, and was escorted by Spider and several clones to [[Coruscant]]. While the mysterious person told the clones to execute Yaluna as part of [[Order 66]], the Jedi, who would himself fall to the [[dark side of the Force]], was eventually taken prisoner to Coruscant after sustaining serious injuries in combat against the troopers.<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />

===New Republic Era===
After the fiery destruction Frong endured at the hands of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the planet became part of the [[New Republic]] with Senator [[Rethalow]] giving his vote to earn the government's protection for him and his people.<ref name="Empire's End" />
