'''C-21 Highsinger''' was a heavily modified [[droid]] [[bounty hunter]] of unknown manufacturer believed to be unique that operated autonomously during the [[Clone Wars]]. He was part of [[Krayt's Claw]], a syndicate of bounty hunters that included [[Boba Fett]], [[Latts Razzi]], [[Dengar]], [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], and [[Oked]]. He had extensive modifications, including precision-engineered [[servomotor]]s and manipulators. Although a deadly combat droid, C-21 Highsinger had his battle programming pushed beyond its limits when defending [[Otua Blank]]'s [[subtram]] from the swift [[Kage Warriors]]. Later in the Clone War, Highsinger was with the other members of Krayt's Claw,  worked when Boba Fett guided former [[Jedi Master]] [[Quinlan Vos]] to find former [[Sith apprentice]] [[Asajj Ventress]] and enlist her support in a mission to assassinate her former master, [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] leader [[Count]] [[Dooku]].

Highsinger continued to work with Razzi into the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. After they engaged and captured [[Unidentified four-armed alien|the alien]], Ventress and [[Lyco Strata]] appeared. The four of them worked together to acquire an [[Imperial shield relay unit]] from an [[Unidentified Imperial crawler|Imperial crawler]]. Razzi and Highsinger tried to betray Ventress after Strata lost the relay unit, but was stopped by Ventress who cut him in half. At some point, however, Highsinger was rebuilt and continued bounty hunting.

==Biography==
C-21 Highsinger was a heavily modified [[Battle droid|combat]]<ref name="Join the Battle" /> and [[assassin droid]]<ref name="SWETCGTSWG" /> of uncertain origins and from an manufacturer<ref name="Join the Battle" /> and was believed to be unique.<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> C-21 Highsinger was without a master<ref name="SWETCGTSWG" /> and operated as a [[bounty hunter]],<ref name="Bounty" /> for which he was well suited because of his autonomous programming.<ref name="SWETCGTSWG" />

===Hunting Jango Fett===
Around [[33 BBY]],<ref name="Jango Date">{{JangoFettSeriesDate}}</ref> Highsinger joined the hunt for fellow [[bounty]] [[hunter]]s [[Jango Fett]] and [[Aurra Sing]]. In the end, Fett [[Skirmish on Jaloria|defeated their pursuers]] with a [[missile]] from his [[jetpack]], with the blast severing Highsinger's right arm. Highsinger claimed his severed limb and sat amidst the flames.<ref name="JF 4" />

===The Clone Wars===
====Safe cargo objective====
During the [[Clone Wars]], Highsinger joined [[Krayt's Claw]], the group of [[bounty hunter]]s put together by<ref name="Bounty" /> Jango's son<ref name="Join the Battle" /> [[Boba Fett]] in order to undertake a mission on the [[planet]] of [[Quarzite]]. The hunters were joined by [[Asajj Ventress]]&mdash;although they were unaware of her identity&mdash;after she [[Death|killed]] one of the [[Oked|members]] of their team. The group traveled to Quarzite in Bossk's [[Starship|ship]], ''[[Hound's Tooth]]''. [[Rigosso]] revealed the crew are tasked with [[Safe cargo objective|guarding]] a large wooden chest aboard a [[subtram]], which was to be delivered to the [[Belugan]] [[Otua Blank]]. As the hunters spread out in anticipation of a confrontation, Highsinger was stationed near the cargo. Though the trip began easily enough, soon the tram was beset by [[Kage Warriors]], who sought the cargo for themselves.<ref name="Bounty" />

[[File:TurboliftTransportArea-Bounty.png|thumb|left|275px|Highsinger and the crew on Quarzite]]
One by one, the hunters fell. Though Highsinger was one of the last hunters standing, the Kage Warriors' leader, [[Krismo Sodi]], was able to dispatch the [[droid]] by linking him with fellow hunter [[Latts Razzi]] as she was thrown from the train. Highsinger fell beside Razzi, though both survived. In his absence, Fett too was bested by Sodi, who soon revealed that the cargo was his sister, [[Pluma Sodi]], who was to be forcibly [[Marriage|wed]] to Blank. Ventress, having suffered many changes in her recent past, ended up placing Fett within the chest and delivering him to Blank, collecting both a [[bounty]] from the [[warlord]] as well as from the [[Kage]] Warriors for freeing Pluma. She then shared the first bounty with Highsinger and the others, telling them that Fett would join them soon enough before departing.<ref name="Bounty" />

====Mission to Serenno====
In the second [[Standard year|year]] of the Clone Wars, Highsinger and several other members of Krayt's Claw including Fett, Razzi, and [[Embo]] were frequenting a [[Unidentified bar (Level 1313)|bar]] in [[Coruscant]]'s [[Level 1313]] when they were visited by the undercover [[Jedi Master]] [[Quinlan Vos]]. Vos had been dispatched by the [[Jedi High Council]] on a mission to assassinate the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] leader [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and had been partnered up with Asajj Ventress, a [[Dathomirian]] [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] who had once been Dooku's [[Sith apprentice]]. Fett informed Vos that Ventress was hunting a [[Volpai]] fugitive named [[Moregi]] on [[Pantora]]. Vos [[Capture of Moregi|traveled]] to Pantora where he managed to partner up as a bounty hunter before revealing his true identity and enlisting Ventress' support to [[Death|kill]] Dooku.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

Following the failure of Vos and Ventress' [[Mission to Raxus Secundus|assassination attempt]], Ventress came to enlist the aid of Krayt's Claw. Fett was visited by her at [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] on [[Tatooine]] by Ventress, who wanted to rescue her partner and lover from Dooku, offering them a significant sum of credits to help her. They agreed, and Highsinger and the other Krayt's Claw members traveled on Fett's [[starship]] ''[[Slave I]]'' to [[Serenno]] on a [[Mission to Serenno (Krayt's Claw)|mission]] to infiltrate [[Castle Serenno]] and rescue Vetnress' partner. While Ventress infiltrated Dooku's palace, Highsinger and his associates staged a diversion and attack several [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] vehicles and droids. They even managed to snare Dooku under some webbing but were forced to flee when Vos spurned Ventress' rescue and openly sided with Dooku.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

===Imperial Era===
[[File:RazziHighsingerVentressTogetherAgain-TotUFriends.png|thumb|300px|Highsinger and Razzi continued working together during the Imperial Era.]]
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] around [[18 BBY]], Highsinger continued to work with Razzi. The two collaborated to hunt down a [[Unidentified four-armed alien|target]] who was wanted dead-or-alive on a [[Unidentified world (Latts Razzi)|terrestrial planet]] in a [[Unidentified bustling city|bustling city]]. As they engaged the individual, the familiar face of Ventress appeared, alongside a young boy, who identified himself as [[Lyco Strata|Lyco]]. After Ventress restrain the target with the Force, Highsinger shot the target and killed them. Highsinger dragged the dead body towards [[Latts Razzi's ship|their ship]] as Razzi and Ventress spoke about intel the latter was hoping to receive. Highsinger then had Strata put the body in the ship as Razzi explained her plan to steal an [[Imperial shield relay unit]].<ref name="Friends">{{TOTU|Friends}}</ref>

Highsinger, Razzi, Ventress and Strata traveled to the [[Cassaro shipyard]] on [[Cassaro]], in hopes of acquiring an Imperial shield relay unit that would direct Strata towards [[Quinlan Vos]] and [[Hidden Path|the Path]]. Highsinger, Strata and Ventress [[Heist at Cassaro shipyard|infiltrated]] an [[Unidentified Imperial crawler|Imperial crawler]] that was transporting [[ISD-72x shield generator dome|shield generators]] to [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyers]]. During the mission, Highsinger gets into the shipyard, moving on all four limbs and hides under a [[Unidentified Imperial crawler|Imperial crawler]] where he cuts a cable using his [[flamethrower]], causing the crawler to stop, alerting the control tower, [[Controller 323]]. He was discovered by an [[Imperial officer]] who discovered Highsinger hiding under [[Crawler 187]] and shot him, prompting several [[stormtrooper]]s to attack Highsinger and Ventress. Highsinger gets out and lifts one of the troopers before shooting another and using the lifted trooper as a human shield against some firing troopers before shooting the trooper and throwing the human shield at the trooper. Running around the corner of the walker, Highsinger fights off the stormtroopers, even withstanding one of the stormtroopers punching him in the face. Ventress cuts the cables holding the [[ISD-72x shield generator dome]], allowing Highsinger to jump on and seeing two [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]], Highsinger performs his spinning attack, causing the [[gunship]]s to break off but one was shot down by Razzi's ship, allowing Highsinger to jump onto the ship's [[entry ramp]]. During the ensuing battle, Strata lost the shield relay unit, which led to Razzi ordering Highsinger to capture Strata instead, as he was worth twice the price of the relay unit. After leaving Ventress, Highsinger brought Strata to the [[cockpit]], however, Ventress soon entered Razzi's ship through a hatch, cutting Highsinger's right arm off when he attempts to use his flamethrower then cutting him across his torso with [[Asajj Ventress's lightsaber|her lightsaber]]. While attempting to grab one of his blasters, Ventress [[Force push]] him where Strata restrained him with [[Lyco Strata's lightsaber|his own lightsaber]]. Ventress then forced Razzi to take them into [[hyperspace]]. After they entered hyperspace, Razzi was forced to give Ventress and Strata the coordinates to the Path before dropping them off at a spaceport.<ref name="Friends" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]] in [[0 ABY]], Highsinger was among the bounty hunters, [[Beebox]], [[Chanath Cha]], [[IG-90]], [[Krrsantan]], [[Zuckuss]], and [[Dengar]], hired by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to return [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]] to him, after she was [[Battle of Vrogas Vas|captured]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] on [[Vrogas Vas]].<ref name="Darth Vader 16">[[Darth Vader (2015) 16|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 16]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Highsinger had [[Gender|masculine programming]]<ref name="Bounty" /> and stood at 2.25 [[meter]]s (7 [[Foot|feet]], 5 inches) tall,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> and [[Color|grey]] plating and red [[sensor]]s. At the top of his head sat a golden plated disk.<ref name="Bounty" /> Highsinger was autonomous<ref name="SWETCGTSWG" /> and valued his independence, but agreed to work with Krayt's Claw on several occasions.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> As Highsinger was unable to speak [[Galactic Basic Standard]], he was not a frequent conversationalist, and often only spoke when addressed directly. When Highsinger did speak,<ref name="Bounty" /> the droid spoke an unknown droid dialect.<ref name="Light and Dark">{{StoryCite|story=The Lost Nightsister|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}}</ref> Highsinger was considered extremely effective as a bounty hunter.<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> He was a straightforward operator, focusing on the job at hand.<ref name="Bounty" />

His torso was rotatable at the pelvic mount, which allowed him to perform rapid blaster attacks to eliminate multiple opponents within a circular area around him. Combined with his advanced [[servomotor]]s, this made Highsinger a formidable combatant in close-quarters, even able to hold his own against skilled [[Species|organic]] [[humanoid]]s like the Kage Warriors<ref name="Bounty" /> or Stormtroopers<ref name="Friends" />

==Skills and abilities==
Highsinger's superhuman reflexes, speed and agility enabled him to be a terrifyingly efficient combat machine, excelling at armed and [[unarmed combat]], as well as martial arts.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> While Highsinger rotated and fired with his rapid speed, everyone, including allies such as Boba Fett had to remain out of harm's way. Highsinger's reflexes and speed combination proved lethal as he opened fire on the Kage Warriors<ref name="Bounty" /> and Stormtroopers while walking on a runaway dome.<ref name="Friends" /> Highsinger was also an expert tracker.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Equipment==
Highsinger had a [[wrist blaster]] built into his arm. He also had a [[X-8 Night Sniper]] and an [[E-5C heavy blaster rifle]].<ref name="Bounty" /> In an earlier mission before the Clone Wars, Highsinger used an [[E-5 blaster rifle]].<ref name="JF 4" /> During the Imperial Era, he wielded two E-5 blaster rifles, had a [[comlink]] built in his head, and had a flamethrower built-in his right arm before it was sliced off by Ventress.<ref name="Friends" />

==Behind the scenes==
C-21 Highsinger first appeared in the [[2012]] episode "[[Bounty (episode)|Bounty]]" from the [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|fourth season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="Bounty" /> He was originally created for [[George Lucas]]' planned live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: Underworld]]'' before it's cancellation in mid-[[2010]].<ref name="ArtofMandoS1">[[The Art of Star Wars: The Mandalorian (Season One)|''The Art of Star Wars: The Mandalorian'' Season One]]</ref> A character illustration of Highsinger for ''The Clone Wars'' was done by [[Kilian Plunkett]].<ref name="Bounty-EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/bounty-episode-guide|text="Bounty" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>

Before the cancellation of ''The Clone Wars'', Highsinger and the rest of Krayt's Claw would have appeared in an episode being hired by Asajj Ventress to rescue Quinlan Vos from Count Dooku's castle on Serreno. An already animated scene shows the bounty hunters fighting the Sith Lord. After Dooku zaps the Fett and Razzi with [[Force lightning]], Highsinger saves Razzi by catching her with one of his arms. The episode would later be adapted into the [[2015]] novel ''[[Dark Disciple]]''.<ref name="Celeb15">{{SWYouTube|7yKM6RExLlI|The Untold Clone Wars Panel &#124; Star Wars Celebration Anaheim}}</ref> Highsinger was also set to make an appearance during a<ref name="Celeb17 AOaUF panel">{{SWYouTube|85Jw-63YNYI|Star Wars Celebration Orlando 2017: Animated Origins and Unexpected Fates}}</ref> four-episode arc<ref name="Michnovetz">{{Twitter|MattMichnovetz|status/341245477838266368|[[Matt Michnovetz]]|quote=You bet!|archivedate=20190904234219|archivefile=File:TCWBountyHunterArc-MichnovetzTwitter.png}} {{C|In response to: "Thanks Matt. Is your bounty hunter arc with Boba Fett, Cad Bane, & Aurra Sing a 4-episode arc too? Plz reply"}}</ref> starring Boba Fett and [[Cad Bane]]. In a clip revealed during the "Animated Origins and Unexpected Fates" panel at [[Celebration Orlando]] in [[2017]], Highsinger was seen siding with Cad Bane during a stand-off against Boba Fett and other bounty hunters, restraining two hostages, [[Stim]] and [[Seripas]].<ref name="Celeb17 AOaUF panel" /> Early concept art of a scene featuring Highsinger and the other bounty hunters walking down a street was drawn by series supervising director [[Dave Filoni]] during one of the series' last writer's conferences.<ref name="Thank">{{SW|url=news/to-fans-of-star-wars-the-clone-wars-thank-you|text=To Fans of ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'': Thank You}}</ref>
