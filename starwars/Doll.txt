A '''doll''' was a small figure representing a living being, typically used as a [[youngling]]'s toy. Using dolls, young ones could engage in a form of role-playing.<ref name="Survival Guide" /> Due to the widespreadness of the [[feline]] [[Tooka]] throughout [[the galaxy]], [[Tooka doll|Tooka-shaped dolls]] were commonplace.<ref name="Tooka in Databank">{{Databank|tooka|Tooka}}</ref> Young [[Kata Akuna]] would receive a doll she would name [[Mookie]], from her mother [[Tayala Akuna]].<ref name="Star Wars Jedi: Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> While left alone with his infant son, [[Han Solo]] attempted to soothe a crying [[Ben Solo]] with his Tooka doll, to no success.<ref name="Empire's End" /> When she was ten [[Standard year|years]] old, [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] crafted a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[pilot]] doll from debris she found in the junkfields of [[Jakku]]. To create the doll's characteristic suit, she used a piece of [[Color|orange]] fabric salvaged from a [[New Republic]] [[cargo container]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> [[Voodoo doll]]s were a type of dolls used in the practitioning of [[voodoo]], as used by [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]].<ref name="Massacre">{{TCW|Massacre}}</ref>
