'''''Family of the Frontier Worlds''''' was a [[holodrama]] that had aired at least seventeen episodes by [[3 ABY]].

==Description==
The holodrama had at least four characters&mdash;[[Malina]], [[Suna]], [[Nojak]] and [[Doc (Family of the Frontier Worlds)|Doc]]&mdash;as well as an announcer that would catch viewers up on the events of the prior episodes, and its logo depicted a [[moisture farm]]. The series aired at least seventeen episodes<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> 

==Episodes==
===Fifteen===
In the recap before the fifteenth episode, Doc informed Malina that the [[bantha]]'s wound was worse then they thought and if they didn't get [[bacta]] within the [[Standard hour|hour]] that the bantha would die. Despite a storm Malina informed Doc and their child Suna that they were going to get the bacta.<ref name="Outlaws" />

===Sixteen===
In the recap before the sixteenth episode, the storm had passed but Malina told Suna that the [[farm]] was going to need a lot of repairs. Suna said they could help with the repairs since [[C-85]] showed them how to solder in [[Suna's school|school]]. Noting that it would take more than that Malina said that they might have to sell the [[skyhopper]] since the crops were all destroyed. Nojak, upset about the idea of selling the skyhopper, stormed off with Malina noting that he was probably heading to a [[swoop gang]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

===Seventeen===
In the recap before the seventeenth episode, Malina came to Nojak asking him to come home instead of selling [[pell rock]]s with his swoop head friends. Malina noted that he wanted to be a [[pilot]] and hanging around the swoop gang wouldn't get him to his dream. Nojak countered by saying that without a skyhopper he couldn't become a pilot. Malina promised they would figure it out if Nojak just came home<ref name="Outlaws" />
