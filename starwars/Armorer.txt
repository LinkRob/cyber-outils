An '''armorer''' was a position of which someone was tasked in the forging and repairing of [[armor]]s among other gears.<ref name="Chapter 1" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], a [[Baragwin]] named [[Hermi Odle]] was [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]'s personal armorer.<ref name="Dictionary" />

In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|1}}</ref> a [[Gender|female]] [[Mandalorian Armorer]] known only as "[[The Armorer]]" was the leader and armorer of a Mandalorian [[The Tribe|tribe]].<ref name="Chapter 1" />

One member of [[Knights of Ren]], [[Cardo]] was considered the best armorer among the Knights because of his obsession with weapon modification.<ref name="IX Dictionary" />
