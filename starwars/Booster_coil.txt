A '''Booster coil''' was a component that could be installed on a [[speeder]]. In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[Temmin Wexley]] had booster coils.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Booster coils were mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

Booster Coils were first introduced in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity in ''[[The Essential Guide to Vehicles and Vessels]]'' reference book written by [[Bill Smith]] and released in [[1996]].<ref name="TEGTVAV">''[[The Essential Guide to Vehicles and Vessels]]''</ref>
