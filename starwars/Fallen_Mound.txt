'''Fallen Mound''' was a rock formation in the [[Boulder Forest]] region on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]] that had collapsed due to the moon's tumultuous weather. At some point before [[3 ABY]] a weather station was built at the mound however it was destroyed during the mound's collapse. [[Doctor]] [[Maia Revlin]] organized a scientific expedition to the mound before or during 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> and found a carved piece of [[amberine]] at the site. However the expedition team were forced to abandon their camp and leave the artifact behind due to local predators. Later Revlin wrote up a wrote up a report on the expedition which was later left on a [[datapad]] in the [[Mirogana Market]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|canon}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" />  the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] was able to travel through a wind tunnel in the mound after the [[speeder]] [[Technician|mechanic]], [[Selo Rovak]] upgraded Vess's [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]]. In the mound Vess found a [[smoke grenade|smoke bomb]] compressor and a piece of [[amber]] [[stone]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fallen Mound appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The area is only accessible after the player finished "The Mechanic" side quest which adds the boost ability to the player's speeder bike. The smoke bomb compressor that is found in the mound is a key part in unlocking the "Smoke Bomb" ability from the mechanic expert abilities. The Overgrown Amber Stone that is found in the mound can be used as a major charm which both increased the players health and grants a surge of [[adrenaline]] when losing a significant amount of health.<ref name="Outlaws" />
