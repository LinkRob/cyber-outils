"'''A World to Conquer'''" is an article by [[Dustin Browder]] in the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 2|second issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]''. The article outlines [[planetary assault tactics]] in the military doctrine of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. It is written as an in-universe lecture titled "[[Fleet Tactics 241]]," delivered by [[Rear Admiral/Legends|Rear Admiral]] [[Michael Unther]] at the [[Duluur Sector Naval Academy]].

==Content==
Admiral Unther explains that a planetary assault is one of the most complex operations that the cadets are likely to undertake. An assault may be necessary when the much simpler option - [[Orbital bombardment/Legends|orbital bombardment]] - is not possible. The lecture expounds on the four stages of an assault: Approach, Orbit, Invasion, and Control. All four phases present pitfalls for the Imperial commander. The Invasion phase may be challenging because naval officers might be tempted to see ground operations as outside their area of expertise. The Control phase takes place after combat has concluded, but it is the most important for eradicating [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] support on a world. It relies above all on [[Propaganda/Legends|propaganda]] and control of the media. The lecture concludes, and the cadets exit to observe naval maneuvers.
