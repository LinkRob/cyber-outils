The '''FA-5 valet droid''' was a model of bipedal [[droid]]. It was the successor of the [[FA-4 pilot droid]].<ref name="SWVE" />

Such droids served as valet droids, taking luggage and directing recently arrived beings to waiting SoroSuub [[airspeeder]]s piloted by FA-4 models.<ref name="SWVE" /> Several served at the docking bays of the [[Imperial Executive Building|Senate Office Building]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref>
