'''''Chewie and the Porgs''''' is the unabridged audio version of the [[canon]] young-readers book [[Chewie and the Porgs|of the same name]] written by [[Kevin Shinick]]. The audiobook was narrated by [[Morla Gorrondona]] and released exclusively on [https://www.audible.com/ Audible] by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[January 22]], [[2026]].<ref name="Audible" />

==Publisher's summary==
''When a hungry Chewbacca the Wookiee finds himself on planet of Ahch-To he gets more than he bargains for when he comes across some equally hungry porgs..''
