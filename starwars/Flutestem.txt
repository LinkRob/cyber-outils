'''Flutestems''' were a component of [[billian tree]]s that could whistle in the wind. A grove of the [[tree]]s grew in [[Monument Plaza]] on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] by the [[time]] of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="LOTJ" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] strolled through Monument Plaza with her retinue and followed a path curving around a billian tree grove, noticing their flutestems whistling in the breeze.<ref name="LOTJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
