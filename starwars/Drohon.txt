'''Drohon''' was a [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] who held the rank of [[Storm (rank)|Storm]] in the [[Lourna Dee's Tempest|Tempest]] led by the [[Twi'lek]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]].<ref name="Script" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> as her [[Tempest]] approached their target&mdash;the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Space station|station]] [[Relay Post Epsilon One]]&mdash;Dee asked the [[droid]] [[H7-09]] if there had been any word from the other Storms within her group. In response, Storm [[Tasia]] replied that only Drohon had made contact.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

==Behind the scenes==
Drohon was mentioned in flashbacks in ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', a [[2021]] audio drama written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> The spelling of the Storm's name was later confirmed with the release of the audio drama's [[The High Republic: Tempest Runner (script)|script]] in [[2022]].<ref name="Script" />
