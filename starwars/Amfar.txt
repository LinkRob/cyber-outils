'''Amfar''' was a terrestrial astronomical object that possessed [[beach]]es.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> It was located in the [[Amfar system]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories]],<ref name="Appendix" /> in grid square O-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]] along the [[hyperspace route]]<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> known as the [[Vaathkree Trade Corridor]]<ref name="Fry" /> east of [[Obroa-skai]].<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" />

During the [[High Republic Era]], the contractors [[Pikka Adren|Pikka]] and [[Joss Adren]] intended to go on vacation to Amfar after the construction of the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]] had concluded. However, the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], which occurred shortly after the construction was complete, saw the Adrens volunteer in the rescue effort, and the two were unable to have their planned vacation.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
