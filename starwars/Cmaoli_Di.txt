'''Cmaoli Di''' was an astronomical object<ref name="THRA 6">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 6|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 6]]</ref> in the [[Cmaoli Di system]],<ref name="Appendix" /> which was located at the intersection of the [[Grumani sector|Grumani]], [[Garis sector|Garis]] and [[Brema sectors]]<ref name="THRA 6" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The celestial body was situated at grid square M-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />#

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Cmaoli Di appeared on a map in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 6|sixth issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']], written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Harvey Tolibao]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="THRA 6" /> on [[May 29]], [[2024]]<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-799/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-6|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #6}}</ref> as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia initiative's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Cmaoli Di/Legends|Cmaoli Di]]<ref name="Journal 13">{{JournalCite|13|Galaxywide NewsNets}}</ref> was first mentioned in "[[Galaxywide NewsNets]]," a feature written by [[Paul Sudlow]] for the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 12|twelfth issue]] of [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'', which was published in [[February]] [[1997]].<ref name="Journal 12">{{JournalCite|12|Galaxywide NewsNets}}</ref>
