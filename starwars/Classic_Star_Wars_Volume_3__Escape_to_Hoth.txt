'''''Classic Star Wars Volume 3: Escape to Hoth''''' was the third trade paperback collection of issues from the ''[[Classic Star Wars]]'' series of [[Star Wars comic books|comics]]. ''Escape to Hoth'' collects ''Classic Star Wars'' issues 15-20. It was published in [[January]] [[1996]].

==Publisher's summary==
''After the initial euphoria of the Rebel Alliance's first major success &ndash; the destruction of the dreaded Imperial battle station known as the Death Star &ndash; the Empire began to rally. It had been staggered, but not destroyed. Even Darth Vader still lived. And he sought to avenge himself upon the still-ragtag forces of the Rebel Alliance. This is the story of how Luke, Leia, Han, Chewbacca, and the droids graduated from the triumphal reception at the conclusion of Star Wars: A New Hope to their new lives as hardbitten guerrillas fighting against the tyrannies of a still-dangerous Empire in ''The Empire Strikes Back''''.
