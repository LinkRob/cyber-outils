The '''Cryospac Repulsorlift Corporation''' was a [[repulsorcraft]] manufacturing corporation based on [[Seikosha]], the fourth [[planet]] in the [[Breago system]]. It was considered a new-comer to the [[Galaxy|galactic]] [[landspeeder]] market by the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]]. It produced six rugged, well-built landspeeders, including the [[Model 90 Ranger]], which was its most popular model.<ref name="NoDisintegrations" />

==Behind the scenes==
