'''''Doctor Aphra'' 36''' is the thirty-sixth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]], [[Jethro Morales]], and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[September 27]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''AMBUSHED! &ndash; A ''DARK DROIDS'' TIE-IN! Trapped in a warehouse full of DEADLY BATTLE DROIDS, DOCTOR APHRA fights for her life! But she's about to come face-to-face with THE ONE ENEMY she never expected to see again!''

==Development==
Variant cover artist [[E.M. Gist]] became part of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' 15th anniversary cover line when he asked to draw a cover of [[Cad Bane]], which he was given the chance to if he drew three other covers for that what he learned was the 15th anniversary line. The second cover Gist drew gave him a chance to draw one of his other favorite ''[[Star Wars]]'' characters, [[Ahsoka Tano]], which would serve as a variant cover for ''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 36. Gist also managed to, in his words, "sneak" [[Morai]] onto the cover, marking her appearance on the cover her first appearance in comic media.<ref name="Gist Ahsoka cover">{{Instagram|username=e.m.gist|url=p/Ct1d6GbLuGQ|name=[[E.M. I also managed to sneak in the unofficial first comic appearance of Morai.
