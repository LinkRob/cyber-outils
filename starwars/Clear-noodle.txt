{{Quote|My auntie makes the most delicious clear-noodles with sliced yobshrimp and squeezed greencitrus. You'd probably have to fight off four dozen cousins to get some, but it's worth it.|Zyle Keem attempting to distract Driggit Parse|<ref name="EFV" />}}
'''Clear-noodles''' were a type of [[noodle]]s [[Diet|edible]] to [[Zabrak]]s. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Zyle Keem's aunt|aunt]] of the aspiring [[pirate]] [[Zyle Keem]] cooked the noodles with sliced [[yobshrimp]] and squeezed [[greencitrus]] at [[Keem family|family]] gatherings. When served, the dish was well-received by the [[Iridonian]] Zabraks in the Keem family.<ref name="EFV" /> In [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> Keem encountered the [[Nihil]] recruit [[Driggit Parse]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]. When Parse sought to take them into custody for being out past curfew, the pirate began trying to distract her by engaging her in a rapidly paced conversation about mundane aspects of their life, including how their [[Gender|aunt]] made the clear-noodle dish at family gatherings.<ref name="EFV" />
