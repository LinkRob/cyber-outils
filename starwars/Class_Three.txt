The '''third''' class of [[starship]] used by the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate was deployed during their [[Battle at Amaxine Station|battle]] against the [[Galactic Empire]] at the [[Amaxine Space Station|Amaxine Station]]. The class, along with [[Class-Two]], were ordered by [[Margo]] to target and destroy the long-range [[cannon]]s of the ''[[Executor]]'', and to therefore prevent it from destroying the [[Space station|station]].<ref name="Hidden Empire 4" />
