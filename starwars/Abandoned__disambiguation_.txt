'''Abandoned''' could refer to several things:

==Canon==
*'''[[Abandoned military relay tower]]''', a military [[relay tower]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planetoid]] [[Sídi]] abandoned prior to the [[Clone Wars]].
*'''[[Abandoned munitions warehouse]]''', a munitions warehouse on [[Coruscant]] used to carry out an [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|attack]] on the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]] on the same [[planet]].
*'''[[Abandoned sarlacc pit]]''', an empty [[sarlacc]] pit on the Outer Rim planet [[Tatooine]] inhabited by a [[Krayt dragon]].
*'''[[Abandoned Spice Mine]]''', a [[spice]] [[mine]] on the Outer Rim astronomical object [[Unox]] abandoned by [[3 ABY]].
*The '''[[Abandoned Village]]''', a [[City|village]] on the Outer Rim [[planet]] [[Zeffo]] abandoned by [[14 BBY]] and occupied by the [[Galactic Empire]] as part of [[Project Auger]].
*'''[[Abandoned weapons depot]]''', a [[weapon]]s depot on the Outer Rim planet [[Sullust]] vital to Galactic Empire's war effort after the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]].
*The '''[[Abandoned Workshop]]''', a research facility on the Outer Rim planet [[Bogano]] used by [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]] to research [[Zeffonian]] technology and local fauna.
*'''[[Ahch-To]]''', a planet in the [[Unknown Regions]] known to be the birthplace of the [[Jedi Order]]. It was later abandoned by the [[Jedi]] leading to the planet eventually becoming a myth to the Jedi themselves.
*'''[[Concordian mines]]''', a series of mines on the Outer Rim [[moon]] [[Concordia]] in the [[Mandalore system]]. After the [[Mandalorian Wars]], it was supposedly abandoned before [[Death Watch]] made a hideout there.
*The '''[[Deserted Village]]''', a [[Wookiee]] village near the [[Shadowlands]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] planet [[Kashyyyk]] that was abandoned by [[14 BBY]].
*'''[[Exegol]]''', a planet in the Unknown Regions which according to legend was the hidden redoubt world of the [[Sith]].
*'''[[Inquisitorius Headquarters]]''', an abandoned skyscraper located in [[The Works]] on the [[Core Worlds]] planet [[Coruscant]] that was used as a hideout for [[Darth Sidious]] and later became the headquarters of the [[Inquisitorius]] during the early [[Imperial Era]].
*'''[[Jaguada]]''', a [[desert]] planet affiliated to the Sith and later quarantined by the [[Galactic Republic]].
*'''[[Lupal]]''', a moon in [[Wild Space]] once home to a highly advanced and cultured civilization before becoming inhospitable due to [[solar flare]]s from its dying [[star]].
*'''[[Malachor]]''', an Outer Rim planet known to be affiliated to the Sith. It was later abandoned after the [[Great Scourge of Malachor]].
*'''[[Mandalore]]''', a planet in the Outer Rim known to be the homeworld of the [[Mandalorian]]s. It was later abandoned after the [[Great Purge of Mandalore|Great Purge]] before being resettled again.
*'''[[Moraband]]''', a planet in the Outer Rim known to be the ancient [[homeworld]] of the [[Sith]] when the planet was called Korriban. According to Jedi [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], the planet was deserted after many wars.
*'''[[Ossus]]''', an Outer Rim planet affiliated to the Jedi before being abandoned after a [[Cron Supernova|supernova]] in the nearby [[Cron Cluster]] devastated the planet.
*'''[[Rhelg]]''', a Sith world.
*'''[[Tython]]''', a planet in the [[Deep Core]] which was known to play an important role in the history of the Jedi. It was later abandoned as new [[Hyperspace route/Legends|hyperlanes]] drifted away from the planet and the surrounding region.
*'''[[Unidentified abandoned moon]]''', a moon home to a [[Sith temple (abandoned moon)|Sith temple]] carved into a rocky [[mountain]]side sacred to the Sith.
*'''[[Ziost]]''', an Outer Rim planet affiliated with the Sith which according to Jedi writer [[Kli the Elder]], the Sith laid waste to.

==Legends==

*'''[[Abandoned Rakata machine chamber]]''', an [[Water/Legends|ice]] cavern on the Outer Rim [[Prison/Legends|prison]] world of [[Belsavis/Legends|Belsavis]] which hosted a large, partially damaged [[Rakata/Legends|Rakatan]] machine.

*'''[[Abandoned Wookiee village]]''', a [[Wookiee/Legends|wookiee]] village on the Mid Rim planet [[Kashyyyk/Legends|Kashyyyk]] abandoned by the Galactic Civil War then later inhabited by Wookiees afflicted by a strange new disease.
*'''[[Abandoned Bunker]]''', a bunker on the [[Core Worlds/Legends|Core Worlds]] planet [[Corellia/Legends|Corellia]], abandoned by [[1 ABY/Legends|1 ABY]].
*'''[[Abandoned droid factory]]''', a [[droid factory]] used as a military base for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] by [[3 ABY/Legends|3 ABY]].
*'''[[Abandoned Coruscant tapcaf]]''', a [[Tapcafe/Legends|tapcafe]] establishment on the Core Worlds planet [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] ruined by a fire in [[44 ABY]].
