'''Ferrocrete''' was a building material. On the [[planet]] of [[Lothal]], ferrocrete was notably used to make roads<ref name="Servants of the Empire" /> and certain buildings.<ref name="EG">''[[Ezra's Gamble]]''</ref> The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] used ferrocrete to reinforce the floors of the [[Great Temple]] on [[Yavin 4]] when repurposing it as their base of operations.<ref name="CL" />
