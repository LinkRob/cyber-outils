The '''Alpha-3B "Besh"''' was a sub-model of the [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wing starfighter]], which served the [[Republic Starfighter Corps]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The model added projectile weapons to allow the fighter to serve as a [[bomber]].<ref name="Stay Sharp" /> At least one Alpha-3B "Besh" [[starfighter]] saw combat during the [[Mygeeto Campaign]], strafing ground targets with its ordnance.<ref name="V-wing" />
