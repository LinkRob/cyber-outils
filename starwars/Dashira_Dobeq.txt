'''Dashira Dobeq''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[sculptor]] and painter from the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] who found residence in the [[House of Organa|court]] of [[Queen]] [[Breha Organa]] of the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]] after exhibiting her work at the [[Alderaan Academy of Fine Arts]]. During the [[Separatist Crisis]] she lent her talents to the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Loyalist Committee]] at the behest of Breha's [[Marriage|husband]] [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] and helped execute the final design of the [[Millennial Celebration Invitation]] meant for use during the celebration of the [[Millennium|thousandth]] anniversary of the Republic's founding. The invitation was based on concepts and layouts produced by the [[artist]] [[Naveela Betuine]], but went through 250 revisions during the administration of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]], as he could not decide what he wanted the invitation to look like. Control of the design was eventually passed onto Senator [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], who decided on a final design; however, the invitation never saw distribution as the Republic [[Proclamation of the New Order|reformed]] into the [[Galactic Empire]] under Palpatine before reaching the anniversary.<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dashira Dobeq was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released on [[October 25]], [[2016]].
