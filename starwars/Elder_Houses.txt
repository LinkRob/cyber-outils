The '''Elder Houses''',<ref name="Bloodline" /> also known as the '''Great Houses''',<ref name="Queen's Hope">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref> were the collected hereditary [[King|royal]] houses of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="7 Things bout Bloodline">{{SW|url=news/7-things-you-dont-know-about-star-wars-bloodline-exclusive-interview|text=7 Things You Don't Know About ''Star Wars: Bloodline'' - Exclusive Interview!}}</ref> They included the [[House of Organa]] and [[Lady]] [[Carise Sindian]]'s family.<ref name="Bloodline" /> In ancient [[time]]s, the Elder Houses were very serious about supporting various [[Monarchy|monarchies]],<ref name="7 Things bout Bloodline" /> but during the [[New Republic Era|era]] of the [[New Republic]], almost no one took the concept of hereditary [[Noble|nobility]] seriously anymore.<ref name="Bloodline" /> By then, the Elder Houses had long since become more of a social organization than anything else,<ref name="7 Things bout Bloodline" /> and most of its members saw it as nothing more than a genealogical resource and an excuse for the occasional gala.<ref name="Bloodline" />

Most of the Elder Houses shared a number of sacred oaths, one of which was to keep secret anything placed under the sanctity of the [[royal seal]]. The most senior members of the Elder Houses formed a ruling council of sorts that could pass certain resolutions. The council had the power to strip a noble of their titles and evict them from the Elder Houses.<ref name="Bloodline" />

None of the Elder Houses adjudicated succession through strict bloodline inheritance. In other words, any child adopted by nobles and raised as their own was regarded as a genuine aristocrat regardless of their biological parentage. As such, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] was a legitimate [[prince]]ss of [[Alderaan]], despite the fact that [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail]] and [[Breha Organa]] were only her adoptive [[parent]]s.<ref name="Bloodline" />
