The '''Fizzroot Shake''' was a [[Color|purple]] [[sap-shake]] available at the [[Hap's Sap Tap]] [[Restaurant|eatery]] on the [[planet]] [[Tenoo]]. On several occasions, the owner, [[Hap]], served the drink to the establishment's patrons. Fizzroot Shakes were considered a favorite beverage of the [[musician]] [[Bruff]].

==Description==
The Fizzroot Shake was a [[Color|purple]]<ref name="The Junk Giant" /> [[sap-shake]]<ref name="Battle for the Band" /> [[Diet|potable]] to [[human]]s,<ref name="The Junk Giant" /> [[Ithorian]]s,<ref name="The New Droid Friends" /> [[Rodian]]s,<ref name="Bounty Hunter and The Thief" /> and [[Twi'lek]]s that was served with a pink garnish on top.<ref name="Show" />

==History==
During the [[High Republic Era]], Fizzroot Shakes were available at the [[Hap's Sap Tap]] [[Restaurant|eatery]] on<ref name="Show" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="TYJ">{{YJA|The Young Jedi}}</ref> [[Tenoo]].<ref name="The Junk Giant" /> As a concoction created by the establishment's owner, [[Hap]], it became a favorite of many patrons.<ref name="Drinks">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Drinks]]''</ref> Around [[233 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|1}}</ref> the [[pilot]] [[Nash Durango]] ordered a Fizzroot Shake, which Hap served to her. Durango then began sipping her drink with a straw.<ref name="The Junk Giant" />

[[File:HapShake-TheShowMustGoOn.jpg|thumb|right|270px|Hap, holding the Fizzroot Shake he made for Bruff]]
The shake was also the favorite beverage of the [[musician]] [[Bruff]]. One [[Standard day|day]], Bruff visited Hap's establishment when he was feeling depressed,<ref name="Show" /> around that same [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="Date" /> Noticing Bruff's mood, Hap made the [[music]]ian a Fizzroot Shake. When Durango, the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Lys Solay]], and Bruff's bandmate [[Metz]], came looking for Bruff, Hap explained what had happened while showing off the Fizzroot Shake he had made.<ref name="Show" />

On another [[Local day|day]], Hap served a Fizzroot Shake to a customer at his eatery. However, the customer fled when a [[Tikotiko]] creature began wreaking havoc in the establishment and left their shake behind.<ref name="Bad Eggs">{{YJA|Bad Eggs}}</ref> Later, in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">{{YJADate|206A}}</ref> one of the shakes was served in Hap's Sap Tap during a performance of the band [[Feb Rozo and the Sap Tap Trio]].<ref name="Battle for the Band" /> Another time on Tenoo, a [[Weebo's grandson|Rodian youngling]] enjoyed a Fizzroot Shake while sitting on their [[speeder]].<ref name="Bounty Hunter and The Thief" /> At some point between 232 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date 3">{{YJADate|301A}}</ref> an Ithorian patron sat in Hap's Sap Tap watching a [[band]] of [[alien]] musicians, having been served a Fizzroot Shake.<ref name="The New Droid Friends" />

==Behind the scenes==
A Fizzroot Shake first appeared in "[[The Junk Giant]]," the second part of the [[Get Well Nubs / The Junk Giant|third episode]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''{{'s}} [[Young Jedi Adventures Season One|first season]].<ref name="The Junk Giant" /> The episode was released on [[May 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="D23 May">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-may-2023|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in May 2023}}</ref> The [[2026]] out-of-universe recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Drinks]]'', written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Jenn Fujikawa]], included a [[Wikipedia:Peanut butter|peanut butter]] and [[Wikipedia:Jelly|jelly]] inspire recipe for a Fizzroot Shake that was topped with a cookie.<ref name="Drinks" />
