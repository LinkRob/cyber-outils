{{Quote|But their medical frigate is out of fuel and its shields are down.<br />The beginning of their end. Destroy it.|[[Captain]] [[Edrison Peavey]] and [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] on the ''Anodyne''|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
The '''''Anodyne''''' was a modified [[Nebulon-C escort frigate]] that served in the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] and later the [[Resistance]] [[Resistance navy|navy]] during its [[First Order-Resistance War|conflict]] with the [[First Order]]. Originally decommissioned by the [[New Republic]], the ''Anodyne'' was transferred to a Resistance-friendly [[salvage yard]] for repairs. It later served as a [[Hospital ship|medical frigate]] for the Resistance. During the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation of D'Qar]], there were four cruisers. The ''Anodyne'' was placed under the command of [[Vice Admiral]] [[Resdox]].<ref name="TLJVD" /> After running out of [[fuel]], the ship was eliminated by the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]''{{'s}} [[turbolaser]]s. The Resistance crew managed to evacuate the doomed craft via [[U-55 orbital loadlifter|U-55 loadlifters]], escaping to the ''Raddus'', while [[Unidentified Anodyne pilot|the captain]] went down with the ship.<ref name="Episode VIII" />
