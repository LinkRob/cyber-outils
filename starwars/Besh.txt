The letter {{AurB|b}} was the second letter of the [[Aurebesh]] [[Writing|alphabet]]. It was pronounced "Besh," and equaled the [[High Galactic]] character B.

Sometime after the [[Battle of Hoth]], the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] was discovered by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces. All [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[Starship|crafts]] were ordered to calculate the jump into [[hyperspace]] and regroup at a rendezvous point determined by scatter protocol Besh.<ref name="Target">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref>
