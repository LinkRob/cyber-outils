A type of '''training armor''' was worn by [[clone cadet]]s as they were training on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:Squad_Tactics_SWDConv.png|thumb|left|200px|A clone cadet squad in training]]
The [[armor]] consisted of a full body suit and a [[helmet]]. The [[Clone trooper armor|armor]] was more padded looking than standard [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] and its helmet left the full face of the [[clone trooper]] visible, unlike standard armor. [[Domino Squad]] and [[Bravo Squad (clone cadets)|Bravo Squad]] wore this armor as they completed their training. This armor was only worn on [[Kamino]] and was replaced by real clone armor when the [[clone cadet]] graduated from training.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clone training armor first appeared in the episode "[[Clone Cadets]]."<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> It was inspired by the old leather uniforms worn by football players.<ref name="Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode301.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Clone Cadets}}</ref>
