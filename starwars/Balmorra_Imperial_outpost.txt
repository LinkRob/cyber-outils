An '''Imperial outpost'''<ref name="TP" /> was located on the [[planet]] [[Balmorra]].<ref name="Tipping Point SG1" /> At some point, the base primarily was used to [[Prison|imprison]] former [[clone trooper]]s that had questioned [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] orders and mutinied.<ref name="TP" />

==Description==
===Main complex===
The main building of the outpost was a large sprawling complex built into a [[mountain]]side<ref name="TP" /> somewhere in the wilderness of [[Balmorra]].<ref name="Tipping Point SG1" /> Serving not only as a warehouse where crates of supplies were wheeled inside by [[trooper]]s, the outpost doubled as a prison camp and kept several mutinous [[clone trooper]]s in captivity. Though the bulk of the outpost's personnel was comprised of [[TK stormtrooper]]s, numerous clone personnel such as [[Clone commando|Imperial clone commandos]] and clone [[stormtrooper]]s were stationed at the outpost as well.<ref name="TP" />

===Landing Pad===
Directly outside of the main complex was a large landing pad where ships delivered supplies to the base and [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruisers]] from the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Advanced Science Division]] shipped clone [[prisoner]]s off-world.<ref name="TP" />
