{{Quote|Aquamarine Squad were sent to Mos Eisley on Tatooine to recruit new people for the Alliance. They'd have been going door to door, and it's possible that they hit trouble somewhere&hellip;|Zev Senesca|<ref name="LEGOTSS" />}}
A '''recruitment drive''' was undertaken by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Aquamarine Squad]] in [[Mos Eisley]], a [[City|settlement]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] in [[3 ABY]]. Aquamarine Squad recruited one [[Gran]] before their mission was interrupted by [[stormtrooper]]s. All three members eventually returned to [[Echo Base]] on the planet [[Hoth]].

==Prelude==
After "the best [[color]]s were already taken," according to the [[rebel pilot]] [[Zev Senesca]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[squad]] [[Aquamarine Squad]] was formed.<ref name="LEGOTSS" /> In the [[Standard week|weeks]]<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> between the construction of Echo Base and its destruction by the Galactic Empire in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="3 ABY date" /> the three infantry [[Rebel trooper|troopers]] of Aquamarine Squad were charged with finding more people to join the Rebel Alliance by going door to door to speak to the residents of [[Mos Eisley]], a [[City|settlement]] on<ref name="LEGOTSS" /> the planet<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Tatooine]], as part of their efforts in the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="LEGOTSS" />

==The mission==
{{Quote|You're looking for those Rebels? I shouldn't be telling you this, but my husband's a Stormtrooper, and he didn't take kindly to them coming in here. He chased them away!|Stormtrooper's wife|<ref name="LEGOTSS" />}}
After arriving in Mos Eisley, Aquamarine Squad unsuccessfully pitched the Alliance to an indifferent [[Aqualish]] and successfully recruited a [[Gran]], but then fell afoul of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Unidentified stormtrooper (Tatooine)|stormtrooper]] when they attempted to recruit the [[stormtrooper]]'s [[Unidentified stormtrooper's wife|wife]]. All three squad members escaped the initial pursuit but had to split up to do so; while two of the rebels remained safe from further Imperial attention, one was cornered by several stormtroopers.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
{{GameMechanics|LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga}}
Zev Senesca became concerned when Aquamarine Squad were late to check in and sent an individual to see if they had encountered any problems. The individual rescued the cornered trooper and found the other two, one in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] and one hiding behind a building near the [[Dowager Queen|Dowager hotel]]. After speaking with the individual, all three members of Aquamarine Squad returned to Echo Base.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Aquamarine Squad's recruitment drive appeared in the [[Canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', which was released in [[2022]]. The rescue of the squad members comprised the "Aquamarine Around the Gills" side mission.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
