'''Artiod Minor''' was a [[Wild Space]] [[planet]] that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Artiodac]] [[species]]. At one point, the inhabitants of the [[Temperature|cold]] and [[swamp]]y world became [[Slavery|enslaved]] for [[Standard century|centuries]] by the [[Zygerrian Slave Empire]], which led to the scattering of the Artiodacs across [[the galaxy]]. Nevertheless, the Artiodac [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] spent his [[Youngling|childhood]] on Artiod Minor.

==Description==
Artiod Minor<ref name="AotG" /> was a [[Wild Space]]<ref name="Timelines" /> [[planet]] located in the [[Artiod system]] of the distant [[Morellian Commonwealth]] region.<ref name="AotG" /> It was situated in grid square S-3 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Timelines" /> A [[Temperature|cold]],<ref name="TVE" /> [[swamp]]y world, Artiod Minor featured on its surface [[Plant|scrub]] [[forest]]s inhabited by creatures known as [[shuimi]].<ref name="AotG" />

==History==
{{Quote|A well-crafted bowl ''[of soup or stew]'' can send my mind reelin' back to my childhood on Artiod Minor.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''|<ref name="BSOCookbook">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Artiodac-SW_Uprising.png|thumb|right|220px|Artiod Minor was the homeworld of the Artiodac species.]]
The Artiod [[Star system|system]] was at one point discovered by [[enslaver]]s<ref name="AotG" /> of the [[Zygerrian Slave Empire]].<ref name="AlienArchive" /> They overran Artiod Minor,<ref name="TVE" /> and for the subsequent [[Standard century|centuries]], the empire kept the planet's native [[Artiodac]] [[species]] [[Slavery|enslaved]].<ref name="AlienArchive" /> Over that period of [[time]], the [[Zygerrian]]s periodically returned to fill their slave pens with Artiodacs.<ref name="AotG" /> Their enslavement eventually led to the Artiodacs acquiring an unsavory reputation, with many of Artiod Minor's natives leaving the planet in order to work as [[soldier]]s or criminal henchmen,<ref name="AlienArchive" /> and over time the species became scattered across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TVE" />

The Artiodac [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]],<ref name="BSOCookbook" /> who had lived for centuries by [[34 ABY]],<ref name="KanatasCastleDate">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'' establishes that [[Strono Tuggs]] had worked at [[Takodana Castle|Maz Kanata's castle]] for [[Standard century|centuries]] by the [[time]] of the events of {{Film|VII}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]].</ref> spent his [[Youngling|childhood]] on Artiod Minor and noted in his [[Book|work]] ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'',<ref name="BSOCookbook" /> published that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="BSOBSOCookbookDate">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> that well-made [[soup]]s and [[stew]]s reminded him of that period of his life.<ref name="BSOCookbook" /> At some point, Artiod Minor was also mentioned in an entry of an [[Unidentified artist|artist's]] [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]] dedicated to the Artiodacs. The journal was eventually restored, expanded, and displayed at the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="AlienArchive" /> at some point no earlier than 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>

==Inhabitants==
{{Main|Artiodac}}
{{Quote|Artiodacs are fierce-looking humanoids with huge arms, tough skin, and deep, rumbling voices.|An entry of an artist's journal|<ref name="AlienArchive" />}}
Artiod Minor was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]], [[humanoid]] Artiodac species.<ref name="AlienArchive" /> In order to attract a [[Reproduction|mate's]] attention as part of the Artiodac courtship rituals, young members of the species foraged in their planet's scrub forests in order to create [[crown]]s made out of collected shuimi feathers. Mated Artiodac pairs subsequently spent their lives tending [[aqua-farm]]s where schools of the [[prengalli]] creatures were kept.<ref name="AotG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Artiod Minor was introduced in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]'' by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="AotG" />
