The '''Battle of the Ryndellia system''' was a battle that took place in [[22 BBY]] during the [[Clone Wars]] in the [[Ryndellia system]]. Prior to the battle, a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Unidentified convoy|medical convoy]] of three [[Pelta-class frigate|''Pelta''-class frigates]] escorted by a lone [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], commanded by [[clone trooper]]s and a [[Jedi]], passed through the Ryndellia system. While traveling through the [[star system]], the [[convoy]] was ambushed by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Malevolence]]'', under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of the [[Kaleesh]] [[General]] [[Grievous]].

As a result, the [[warship]] managed to eliminate the entire convoy with its extensive weapons array, while it disabled the final fleeing [[frigate]] with its [[Mega-ion cannon|ion cannon]]. Following the attack in the Ryndellia system, on orders from Separatist [[Head of State|leader]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]], the ''Malevolence'' headed to [[Battle of the Kaliida Nebula|attack and destroy]] the Republic's secret [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Kaliida Shoals Medical Center|medical center]] that had been treating over 60,000 wounded [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Now the Republic will learn of our ion cannon!''"<br />"''Your failure is most unfortunate. I will have to discuss this with my Master.|Grievous and Dooku, after the former fails to stop a Republic ship from fleeing|<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />|audio=OurIonCannon-RM.flac}}
[[File:Abregado_SWCW_RM2.png|thumb|left|270px|Under the command of General Grievous, the ''Malevolence'' launched a series of attacks against the Galactic Republic throughout over a dozen star systems.]]
During the opening [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Clone Wars]], in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] had constructed the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Malevolence]]'', a [[starship]] capable of disabling all power to [[fleet]]s of [[capital ship]]s with its [[mega-ion cannon]]s. Following its construction, the Confederacy's naval forces deployed the ''Malevolence'' against the [[Galactic Republic]] and its [[Republic Navy|navy]] in over a dozen [[star system]]s, in [[Malevolence Campaign|attacks]] that left no survivors to report what they had encountered. Due to its method of attacking, which had been unknown to the Republic, the ''Malevolence'' was dubbed a mystery weapon.<ref name="Rising Malevolence" />

Following the [[Battle of the Phu system|destruction]] of a Republic [[task force]] in the [[Phu system]],<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|43|Starship Fact File}}</ref> [[Jedi General]] [[Plo Koon]] managed to track the ''Malevolence'' to the [[Abregado system]], where he and his [[Plo Koon's fleet|fleet]] were ordered by the [[Jedi High Council]] to destroy the mystery weapon before it could conduct more strikes against their fleets. Commanded by the [[Kaleesh]] Separatist [[General]] [[Grievous]], who was overseen by the [[Head of State]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]], Koon's entire fleet had been destroyed just as the other Republic fleets before it, although the [[Jedi Master]] managed to escape the destruction and eventually the ''Malevolence'' with the help of Jedi General [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Ahsoka Tano]]. After escaping the ''Malevolence'', Koon reported his findings on the warship to the Republic, resulting in a loss in trust for Grievous by Dooku,<ref name="Rising Malevolence" /> but the Kaleesh continued his attack on Republic fleets while Dooku left to discuss Grievous's failure with his [[Sith Master|master]], the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], [[Darth Sidious]], where he eventually arrived in the [[Ryndellia system]].<ref name="Shadow" />

==The battle==
[[File:Convoy_destroyed.jpg|thumb|right|270px|The Republic medical convoy is destroyed by the ''Malevolence''.]]
As a Republic medical [[Unidentified convoy|convoy]] of three [[Pelta-class frigate|''Pelta''-class frigates]] escorted by a lone [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] under protection from Jedi forces traveled through the Ryndellia [[Star system|system]], the Republic [[warship]]s were intercepted by the ''Malevolence'' and its [[droid]] crew under Grievous's command near [[Ryndellia]]. Under ambush by the ''Malevolence'', the power systems of the [[Star Destroyer]] and two of the [[frigate]]s were rendered inoperable by the heavy cruiser following its utilization of its mega-[[ion cannon]], which allowed its crew of [[B1-series battle droid]]s to commence [[turbolaser]] fire upon the deactivated warships. However, despite their destruction, the [[convoy]]'s remaining frigate began to make its attempted withdrawal from the system as its [[clone trooper pilot]]s began to activate its [[hyperdrive]] systems.<ref name="Shadow" />

In an effort to prevent the final frigate's withdrawal, following the Kaleesh's notice of its retreat, the Separatist general ordered the ''Malevolence''{{'s}} bridge crew to fire the heavy cruiser's ion cannons and thereby disable the frigate's ability to escape into [[hyperspace]]. As a result, Grievous prompted his droids to unleash a barrage from the ''Malevolence''{{'s}} turbolasers and destroy the frigate. While the frigate sustained fire from the heavy cruiser, one of Grievous's [[battle droid]]s expressed his lack of accuracy in striking the frigate, resulting in his destruction in response to the general's frustration.<ref name="Shadow" />

[[File:Malevolence_targets_frigate.png|thumb|left|270px|The ''Malevolence'' targets the fleeing frigate.]]
Observed by Dooku through a [[Hologram|holographic transmission]], who explained the expensive costs of B1-series battle droids in response to the general's destruction of one of his droids, the [[Sith Lord]] expressed the Jedi's treatment of their [[clone trooper]]s to the Kaleesh. In response, Grievous stated that the compassionate treatment for the [[Grand Army of the Republic|clone army]] by the Jedi was a weakness they were to exploit. As a result, the Separatist leader provided Grievous with instructions on the ''Malevolence''{{'s}} subsequent target as the [[Kaliida Shoals Medical Center]], the Republic's secret medical [[Space station|station]] located within the [[Outer Rim Territories]] which had been tending to over 60,000 wounded [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s. In hopes of transferring the war effort in their favor, Dooku ordered the Kaleesh to destroy the station in order to prevent further attendance to the Republic's wounded [[soldier]]s. After receiving his instructions, the general ordered the ''Malevolence'' to target and destroy the jettisoned escape pods from the medical frigate in order to retain his reputation.<ref name="Shadow" />

==Aftermath==
[[File:Battle_of_Kaliida.jpg|thumb|right|270px|Shadow Squadron intercepts the ''Malevolence'' at the Kaliida Nebula.]]
Following in accordance with Dooku's instructions, the ''Malevolence'' entered hyperspace and proceeded to make its way for the targeted medical center. However, while on their way to the Kaliida Shoals Medical Center, General Anakin Skywalker received intelligence from [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]] regarding the ambush of the Republic medical convoy near Ryndellia, which prompted the Jedi's deduction of the medical center as the subsequent target for the ''Malevolence''. In response, Skywalker organized a [[Battle of the Kaliida Nebula|counterattack]] against the Separatist heavy cruiser, with the intention of intercepting the warship before it could conduct its assault and potentially destroy it along with Grievous to further the war effort.<ref name="Shadow" />

As a result, Skywalker led the [[Galactic Senate]]-commissioned [[Shadow Squadron]] in [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]]s through a [[Balmorra Run|shortcut]] in the [[Kaliida Nebula]] alongside his Padawan, Ahsoka Tano, and General Plo Koon. In their efforts, the Republic [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] was able to inflict heavy damage upon the ''Malevolence'' and disable functionality to its mega-ion cannons, leading to its withdrawal<ref name="Shadow" /> and eventual destruction.<ref name="Destroy Malevolence" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of the Ryndellia system was introduced in "[[Shadow of Malevolence|Shadow of ''Malevolence'']],"<ref name="Shadow" /> the third episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which aired on [[October 18]], [[2008]].<ref name="Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/shadow-of-malevolence-episode-guide|text="Shadow of Malevolence" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> It was later identified in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 43|forty-third issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 43">{{BuildFalconCite|43|Starship Fact File}}</ref> published around [[October 28]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdates">{{BMFdates|43}}</ref>
