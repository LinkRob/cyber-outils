'''Conjure''' was a [[Force power]] that could allow the user to summon a spectral object. Conjured items could be various items or animals. Conjure could also be used to raise the [[Death|dead]] as [[zombie]]s, though only masters of the skill could accomplish such a feat. The [[Nightsisters]] were common practitioners of the power, using their [[Magical ichor|ichor]] and [[Magick]].<ref name="Unlimited Power" />
