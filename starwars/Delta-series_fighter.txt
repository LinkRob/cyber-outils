The '''Delta-series fighter''', also known as the '''Delta line''', was a line of [[starfighter]]s manufactured by [[Kuat Systems Engineering]] that featured small, folding [[S-foils]].<ref name="Collapse" /> Models of the Delta line included the [[Delta-6 system defense starfighter|Delta-6 fighter]],<ref name="Rise" /> the [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7 ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]], the [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]], and its [[Clone Wars]]-era descendants which included the [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wing starfighter]] and the [[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor]].<ref name="Collapse" />
