The '''BARM-ST12 scattergun''' was a type of pump-action [[Scattergun|scatter blaster]]. 

The [[mercenary]] [[Lang]] owned a BARM-ST12 scattergun; during his battle with [[Ahsoka Tano]] he fired a blast of bolts at the [[Jedi]] but ultimately missed - by the time he had pumped his weapon for a followup shot, the [[Jedi]] was already aware of his presence.<ref name="Handbook" /><ref name="MandoTheJedi">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 13: The Jedi}}</ref>

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
While blaster shotguns have existed in Star Wars Legends for many years, the scatterblaster was brought into wider canon onscreen in [[Chapter 13: The Jedi]] of [[Star Wars: The Mandalorian|The Mandalorian]]; very briefly, the character of [[Lang]] fires his BARM-ST12 scattergun at [[Ahsoka Tano]] while she is on a rooftop, revealing that his weapon fires 4-5 blaster bolts simultaneously. In the same scene, Lank cocks his weapon through pump-action.<ref name="MandoTheJedi" />
