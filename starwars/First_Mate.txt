'''First Mate'''<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> was the title held by an individual second in command to the [[captain]] of a civilian [[starship]]. [[L3-37]] was first mate to [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> [[Chewbacca]] was later first mate to [[Han Solo]] on the ''Millennium Falcon'',<ref name="Episode IV" /> as [[Quiggold]] was to [[Sidon Ithano]] on the ''[[Meson Martinet]]''.<ref name="Lost Treasure">{{StoryCite|story=The Crimson Corsair and the Lost Treasure of Count Dooku|book=Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I}}</ref> [[Brutus]] was the First Mate to [[Jod Na Nawood|Silvo]] before he decided to overthrow him.<ref name="Zero Friends Again">{{SkeletonCrew|Zero Friends Again}}</ref> The "[[Pirate Queen]]" [[Lassa Rhayme]] was in a romantic relationship with [[Blood Bone Order first mate|her first mate]]; however, he betrayed her and she put quite a lucrative [[bounty]] on his head.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> The Sakavian fisherman [[Skoova Stev]] was first mate to the Besalisk Captain [[Wan'yek Whett]] on the ''[[Grapnel]].''<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>
