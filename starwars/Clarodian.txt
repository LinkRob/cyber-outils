The '''Clarodians'''<ref name="TAVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Andor4" /> [[Brother]]s [[Tianris Mez|Tianris]] and [[Guanlos Mez]] were members of this species.<ref name="TAVG" />

==Biology and appearance==
The Clarodians<ref name="TAVG" /> were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that were distinguished by the two [[Bone|horns]] protruding from the upper portion of their face.<ref name="Andor10" /> Their skin was varying shades of [[Color|green]], tan, or even purple,<ref name="Acolyte" /> and they possessed solid black [[eye]]s, large [[nose]]s, pointed [[ear]]s,<ref name="Andor10" /> and [[Tooth|fangs]],<ref name="Acolyte" /> as well as some brown [[hair]] on the top of their heads.<ref name="Andor10" />

==Species in the galaxy==
During the [[High Republic Era]], by [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> two Clarodian miners, [[brother]]s [[Tianris Mez|Tianris]] and [[Guanlos Mez]],<ref name="TAVG" /> had become associates of [[Jedi Master]] [[Indara]], sometimes gathering in the [[Lomi Usqi Noodle Shop]] on their current [[planet|homeworld]] of [[Ueda]], both eventually being dispatched by [[Mae-ho Aniseya|Mae Aniseya]] when she came hunting for the woman.<ref name="Acolyte" /> Over a century later, in [[5 BBY]], another member of this species was present at the [[Coruscant Eastern Spaceport]],<ref name="Andor4" /> while yet another was within the [[Coruscant Underworld]], passing by [[spy]] [[Lonni Jung]] while he was heading to a secret meeting with [[Luthen Rael]].<ref name="Andor10" /> Three years later, at least two<ref name="Welcome to the Rebellion">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref> Clarodians<ref name="TAVG" /> were present in the [[Imperial Senate Building]], panicking during [[Mon Mothma]]'s [[escape from the Senate]].<ref name="Welcome to the Rebellion" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Horned-Species-Ivan-Manzella.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Concept art by Ivan Manzella]]
