The '''Chronicles of Med'eeth''' was a [[Jedi]] historical document that took the form of a metal plaque. They were salvaged from the ruins of [[Ossus]] at some point before the [[Reproduction|birth]] of [[Antron Bach]]. When the [[Clone Wars]] ended, [[Jedi Master]] [[Uvell]] entrusted Bach with a number of Jedi objects of historical importance, which included the last charred fragment of the Chronicles of Med'eeth.<ref name="TEOH" />
