'''Bernilla''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> located in the [[Bernilla system]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]] regions,<ref name="Location" /> in grid square L-14 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It shared its name with [[Bernillan silk]].<ref name="Lando's Luck">''[[Lando's Luck]]''</ref> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> Bernilla was depicted on a [[map]] included in the [[book]] ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', written by [[historian]] and [[author]] [[Eloc Throno]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bernilla was mentioned in the [[2020]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', written by [[Cole Horton]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />
