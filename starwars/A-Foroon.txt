'''A-Foroon''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Arkanis sector]] within [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> A-Foroon was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Hutt]] [[Arok]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
A-Foroon was first [[canon]]ically mentioned in the [[2021]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[A-Foroon/Legends|A-Foroon]] was a [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]]-controlled [[Planet/Legends|planet]] in the [[Arkanis sector/Legends|Arkanis sector]], and it originated in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by Fry and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
