'''Blade Squadron''' was a [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing]] [[starfighter]] [[squadron]], consisted of an elite group of fighter pilots,<ref name="HC6DB">
{{HelmetCollectionCite|6|Databank A-Z}}</ref> that served with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and later the [[New Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

The squadron fought during the [[Battle of Endor]], where they single-handedly destroyed the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Devastator]]''. The squadron's patch, a B-wing over a lightning bolt, was captioned with the words "strike as lightning" in [[Aurebesh]].

In [[5 ABY]], Blade Squadron also saw action at the [[Battle of Malastare (Galactic Civil War)|Battle of Malastare]], during which [[Gina Moonsong]] disobeyed [[Blade Leader]] [[Braylen Stramm]]'s orders and attacked [[Moff]] [[Pandion's vacation palace]] on the planet. Later, a group of [[X-wing starfighter]]s were added to escort the B-wings in [[Defense of Kuat|Battle for Kuat Drive Yards]] and [[Battle of Jakku]].

==History==
===Taris shipyards===
{{Quote|Remember why we're here!''"<br />"''Tell Blade Squadron, not us--they're the ones with the bombs!|Luke Skywalker and one of his Rogue Squadron recruits, Taris system|<ref name="Squad Goals 1">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=11|story=Squad Goals, Part One}}</ref>}}
Blade [[Squadron]] carried out an [[Destruction of the Taris shipyards|attack]] on an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[shipyard]] in the [[Taris system]]. [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wings]] from [[Luke Skywalker]]'s [[Rogue Squadron]] provided cover for the bombers during their bombing run on the shipyard.<ref name="Squad Goals 1" />

===Battle of Endor===
{{Quote|That's a negative Moonsong. Regroup and form up to assist the fleet. Nobody's called off the war on my account.|Adon Fox, unable to eject from his crippled B-wing at Endor|<ref name="Blade Squadron" />}}
[[File:Devastator_devastated.png|thumb|left|200px|B-wings destroy the ''Devastator'' at Endor]]
Blade Squadron participated in the [[Battle of Endor]], led by [[Wing Commander]] [[Adon Fox]], as a full [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing]] flight and many of the detachment's newest recruits flying their first mission.<ref name="HelmetCollection67">{{HelmetCollectionCite|67|Helmets}}</ref> Upon realizing the trap the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] had set, the squadron's rearguard mission was promptly aborted, and Blade Squadron was reassigned to attack the [[blockade]] of Imperial [[Starship|ships]]. Adon Fox ordered his B-wings to target the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Devastator]]'', which he recognized as the ship that destroyed his [[Adon Fox's wife|wife's]] [[Adon Fox's wife's transport|transport]] during the [[Battle of Hoth|evacuation of Hoth]]<ref name="Blade Squadron" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> The ''Devastator'' was successfully destroyed along with [[Admiral]] [[Jhared Montferrat]], its [[commanding officer]]. This cleared a pathway for the remaining [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[starfighter]]s to start their attack on the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star]]. Blade Squadron suffered heavy losses, including the [[death]] of [[Blade Leader]] at the hands of [[Commander]] [[Gradd]]'s [[TIE/IN interceptor|TIE Interceptor]].<ref name="Blade Squadron" />

===After Endor===
{{Quote|I shouldn't be telling you this, but&hellip; the Imperials aren't beaten. What looked like just a rearguard action is turning out to be something more serious. All leave is getting canceled and Blade Squadron is getting a fresh batch of recruits. And I need you to train them. Okay?|Braylen Stramm to Gina Moonsong, shortly after the Battle of Endor|<ref name="Blade Squadron: Zero Hour" />}}
[[File:Blade-squadron-zero-hour-cadets.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Moonsong addressing new recruits]]
Following the victory at [[Endor]], the surviving B-wing [[Rebel pilot|pilots]] each received a [[Medal of Bravery]]. Braylen Stramm was promoted to Wing Commander and given command of Blade Squadron. Gina Moonsong was promoted to Lieutenant. Due to heavy losses suffered in the battle, the squadron received three new cadets ([[Cutter Poole]], [[Yori Dahn]] and [[Jordan Karls]]), who were assigned to Moonsong's section. With the cadets untested and very little time to teach them, Moonsong promptly interviewed each one and then immediately introduced them to Fanty, who would go on to start their training early the following morning.<ref name="Blade Squadron: Zero Hour" />

===Battle of Malastare===
{{Quote|Hitting the hub might hurt Pandion-but killing him in his palace will put an end to any orders he might want to send out. Not to mention making it that much more likely that his troops will take our orders to surrender.|Gina Moonsong, during a mission briefing with Braylen Stramm shortly before Malastare|<ref name="Blade Squadron: Zero Hour" />}}
[[File:Blade_Squadron_on_Malastare.png|thumb|left|200px|Stramm's B-wings destroy the Malastare communications hub]]
Blade Squadron's next operation was at the [[Battle of Malastare (Galactic Civil War)|Battle of Malastare]], their mission was to destroy an [[Malastare communications station|Imperial communications hub]] on the [[planet]] of [[Malastare]]. To get through the [[planetary shield]], Admiral [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar's]] [[technician]]s determined that the B-wings would need to aggregate their [[ion cannon]] beams, converged at precisely the same coordinates on the shield to create a temporary aperture, which allowed the squadron to pass through. The electromagnetic surge of the flickering shield was too much for Jordan Karls ([[Blade Seven]]), and destroyed his ship. Stramm and his B-wings concentrated on bombing runs of the target, while Moonsong and the rest of the squadron distracted incoming [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE Fighters]] and ground defense batteries. Cutter Poole ([[Blade Five]]), was killed by a pursuing [[TIE series|TIE fighter]]. Stramm's orders were to target just the planet's [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] and communications hub, however Moonsong disobeyed orders and destroyed [[Moff]] [[Valco Pandion]]'s [[Pandion's vacation palace|vacation palace]].<ref name="Blade Squadron: Zero Hour" />

===Battle for Kuat Drive Yards===
[[File:B-wing_vs_Kuat_walkers.png|thumb|right|250px|B-wings attack the [[Kuat Drive Yards]]]]
Prior to the [[Defense of Kuat|Battle for Kuat Drive Yards]], Stramm was relieved of flying duties and assigned to the [[MC80 Star Cruiser]] ''[[Amalthea]]'' to help [[Commodore]] [[Kyrsta Agate]] coordinate the overall [[New Republic Defense Fleet|fleet]] action, which involved multiple squadrons of fighters and [[cruiser]]s. The battle was long and drawn out, which lasted many [[Standard day|days]]. An [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] escort section was added to Blade Squadron, now led by Moonsong, to supplement her B-wings. The battle took its toll on the squadron, so when X-wing [[pilot]] [[Johan Volk]] was injured in combat and excused from flying duties, Stramm was temporarily substituted in to Volk's slot, and flew as Lieutenant [[Sandara Li]]'s [[Wingmate|wingman]].<ref name="Blade Squadron: Kuat" />

===Battle of Jakku===
[[File:Blade_Squadron_-_Jakku_promo.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Moonsong and ''Ravager'' fall to Jakku]]
Blade Squadron, still composed of B-wings and X-wings, participated in the [[Battle of Jakku]]. During the attack on the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Ravager]]'', Moonsong's B-wing was caught in the [[tractor beam]] used to pull the ''Ravager'' down to the planet's surface and resulted in both ships crashing on [[Jakku]]. Moonsong survived the crash, however her ship was destroyed and she had no way of communication back to Ackbar and [[New Republic Starfighter Command|Fighter Command]] on ''[[Home One]]''. Together with [[Temmin Wexley]] from [[Phantom Squadron]], who crashed [[Temmin Wexley's T-65C-A2 X-wing starfighter|his]] [[T-65C-A2 X-wing starfighter|X-wing]] too, they managed to find [[New Republic]] [[New Republic army|ground]] [[New Republic soldier|soldiers]] nearby. However, due to strict radio silence orders, Moonsong was still unable to call for help. With no other option, they assisted ground forces in a mission to attack an Imperial supply [[convoy]]. Moonsong then attempted to broadcast a distress message using the Imperial equipment from the convoy. Stramm led Blade Squadron's B-wings down to the surface in a rescue mission and successfully saved them from remaining [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]].<ref name="Blade Squadron: Jakku" />

==Behind the scenes==
A group of B-wing starfighters first appeared in the [[1983]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> It wasn't until the [[2014]] short story [[Blade Squadron (short story)|''Blade Squadron'']] that they were identified as Blade Squadron.<ref name="Blade Squadron" />

When the short story was published on ''[[StarWars.com]]'', the character [[Fanty]] was replaced by an unnamed character as [[Blade Four]], with the former's lines given to Gina Moonsong instead. This has since led to some confusion among fans. Specifically, ''StarWars.com'' states Blade Four was destroyed at Endor, but Fanty reappears in the follow up short story ''[[Blade Squadron: Zero Hour]]''.

In Part I of the short story ''Blade Squadron'', it states Adon Fox is flying an A-wing at Endor, but later in the same battle he's in a B-wing. This is likely the result of a typo, as the ship is only specifically identified as an A-wing on one occasion, and the story mentions features unique to the B-wing, like a gyro-stabilized cockpit.
