'''Ajax's squad''' was a unit led by [[Sergeant]] [[Maximian Ajax]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[61st Mobile Infantry]]. During a [[raid on an Imperial heavy freighter]]<ref name="TC" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref> Ajax's squad made a choke point on one of the [[Imperial heavy freighter]]'s main passage ways. When the squads were ordered to withdraw, they found themselves pinned down and [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] came in to rescue them. Although his soldiers made it out, Ajax died with a grenade in his hand, shouting obscenities.<ref name="TC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ajax's squad first appeared in the novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="TC" />
