'''Darita''' was a [[human]]<ref name="USW" /> [[Gender|female]] who served as a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. She was with Shadow Wing during the [[Attack on Chadawa|Battle at Chadawa]] and was killed during the [[Battle of Jakku]]. Her sister was [[Captain]] [[Darita]], and one of them used the first name Shay.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
