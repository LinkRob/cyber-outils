Born around [[15 BBY]],<ref name="Dates" /> '''Dakous''' was an [[ensign]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] who was stationed on<ref name="Rise" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Corellia]]. After the apparent [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] at the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the nineteen-[[Standard year|year]]-old ensign claimed to have received a [[Force vision|vision]] of Palpatine telling him to take the Imperial [[throne]].<ref name="Rise" />

An [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Unidentified loyalty officer|loyalty officer]] then executed Dakous and a file on the ensign was created in the "Treasonous Pretenders" section of the [[Hall of Imperial Register|Imperial Archives]]. The [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] cited this when mentioning Dakous in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'',<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dakous was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
