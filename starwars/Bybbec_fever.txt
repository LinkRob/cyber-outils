'''Bybbec fever''' was an unpleasant, if rarely [[Death|fatal]], malady. In [[Twi'lek]]s, however, its symptoms were more severe&mdash;fever, [[bone]] ache, weakness&mdash;and did lead to death if not treated properly. [[Gattis-root]] extract was a known medicine for bybbec fever.<ref name="MM" />
