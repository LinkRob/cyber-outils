A '''field calibrator''' was a type of technology used during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> the [[meknek]] [[Verosha Aniseya]] had a field cablibrator base on the workbench of her [[Bunk 23|bunk]] on board the [[WD-2550 Whydah-class Yield Conveyer|WD-2550 ''Whydah''-class Yield Conveyer]] ''[[Fallon]]'' while working on board for the [[Trade Federation]]. The device had [[Color|blue]] plating and a gray panel of knobs and dials as well as a degausser wand housed on the left side with a black wire connecting it.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
A field calibrator appeared in "[[Lost / Found]],"<ref name="LostFound">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref> the first episode of the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', which aired on [[Disney+]] on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref> It was first identified in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
