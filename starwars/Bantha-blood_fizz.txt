'''Bantha-blood fizz''' was a sparkling [[Foodstuff|beverage]] made from the purified [[blood]] of a [[bantha]], a [[mammal]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="ABUE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bantha-blood fizz was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''.<ref name="AB">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, bantha-blood fizz first appeared in the short story "[[A Bad Feeling: The Tale of EV-9D9]]," which was included in the anthology book ''[[Tales from Jabba's Palace]]''. Published in [[1995]], the short story was written by husband-and-wife authors [[Garfield Reeves-Stevens|Garfield]] and [[Judith Reeves-Stevens]].<ref name="Bad Feeling">{{StoryCite|story=A Bad Feeling: The Tale of EV-9D9|book=Tales from Jabba's Palace}}</ref>
