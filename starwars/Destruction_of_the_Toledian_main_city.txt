In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Toledian main city|main city]] of<ref name="A Crash of Fate" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Toledian]] was '''destroyed''' in a [[mining]] accident. News of the [[City|cities]] destruction reached the planet [[Batuu]], where it was discussed by residents of [[Black Spire Outpost]]. [[Julen Rakab]] overheard that chaos rose on Toledian after the accident while he was with [[Izal Garsea]] at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

==Behind the scenes==
The destruction of the Toledian main city was first mentioned in the [[2019]] novel ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />
