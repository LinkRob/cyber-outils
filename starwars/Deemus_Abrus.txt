'''Deemus Abrus''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Twi'lek]] who served as the [[Chief Inspector]] of the [[Coronet City Chapter of the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Alongside [[Corellian]] authorities, Abrus participated in the arrest of the notorious [[Shani]] [[Musician|singer]] [[Crukibolt Nart Adeen|Crufeela]] for attempting to [[Diet|eat]] the [[Bonbrak]] [[Teetak]]. Abrus suggested sending Teetak to the [[Blue Peaks]] of [[Corellia]] in attempt to repopulate the area.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

Deemus Abrus appeared in ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon|Midnight Horizon]]'', a [[2022]] novel written by [[Daniel José Older]] for the the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> Phase I.<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> In the novel, Deemus Abrus and the [[Arcona]] [[Smeemarm]] share a moment of pointed eye contact with each other after the women collaborated to imprison Crufeela.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> On [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]], Older confirmed that he had written the scene to convey that Smeemarm and Abrus were "shmanging" together. Though he initially felt it was clearly portrayed, he later acknowledged some readers found the text unclear when responding to fellow ''[[Star Wars]]'' author [[Alyssa Wong]] about two of their characters&mdash;[[Chelli Lona Aphra]] and [[Domina Tagge]]&mdash;who had a similar situation happen.<ref name="Twitter" />
