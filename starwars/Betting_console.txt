A '''betting console''' was a device that allowed beings [[gambling|bet]] on the outcome of an event.<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Fathier table]]s<ref name="Art Of Outlaws">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Outlaws]]''</ref> were one type of betting console which allowed beings to remotely view and bet on [[Fathier racing|fathier races]] which were broadcast live from the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack]] on the [[casino]] [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Betting consoles appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
