'''Endario Zinn''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Mirialan]] who worked as an agent of [[Chandrila Star Line]]. In that capacity, Zinn once recorded a video message to passengers who booked a stay on the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''<ref name="Welcome video" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="31 Things">{{SW|url=news/31-things-we-learned-about-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=31 Things We Learned in an Exclusive Preview of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser|nolive=1}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The name "Endario" previously appeared as a first and a last name of various [[List of computer-controlled character names in Battlefront games|computer-generated characters]] from the ''[[Star Wars Battlefront (series)|Star Wars: Battlefront]]'' video game series. "Zinn" similarly appeared in the game series, but also as the given name of several characters in other ''[[Star Wars]]'' fiction.
