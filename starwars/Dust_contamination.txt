'''Dust contamination''' was a condition afflicted upon [[droid]]s when their joints were encrusted with dust or sand, severely impairing their ability to move. The issue could be resolved by submerging the affected droid in an [[oil bath]].<ref name="A New Hope" />

The [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] was afflicted with dust contamination while traveling through the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]'s [[Jundland Wastes]].<ref name="A New Hope" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dust contamination first appeared in the [[1977]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="A New Hope" />
