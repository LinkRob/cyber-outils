'''Daniel Frishman''' is an actor who portrayed [[Ewok/Legends|Ewoks]] in the films {{Film|VI}}, ''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]'', and ''[[Ewoks: The Battle for Endor]]''. When interviewed about his ''[[Star Wars]]'' experience, Frishman remembered his time as an Ewok fondly and described what it was like to be inside an Ewok costume.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Working in those costumes was very hard work and tiring. With the heads on you were completely sealed and would have only a short period of time when you had clear vision through the eye lenses.|Frishman on portraying an Ewok|<ref name="Interview" />}}
[[File:Deej.jpg|thumb|right|275px|Dan Frishman portrayed Deej (pictured).]]
Daniel Frishman is an actor who played an [[Ewok/Legends|Ewok]] in the [[1983]] [[original trilogy]] film {{Film|VI}}.<ref name="ROTJ" /> He returned to play another Ewok, [[Deej Warrick]], in ''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]'' and ''[[Ewoks: The Battle for Endor]]'', two films released in [[1984]] and [[1985]], respectively.<ref name="coc">''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]''</ref><ref name="bfe">''[[Ewoks: The Battle for Endor]]''</ref> When interviewed years later in [[2010]] on the website [[Star Wars Interviews]], Frishman did not recall auditioning for any of his ''[[Star Wars]]'' roles, but he assumed that he played Warrick because he had previously worked on ''Return of the Jedi''. Playing an Ewok was difficult because of being sealed in to the costumes and the fact that the eyes would quickly fog up, but Frishman felt that the crew was understanding.<ref name="Interview" />

Looking back, he enjoyed being able to tell when his fellow Ewok actors were in character or not despite them being completely concealed in costume. Frishman also talked about getting along with a young [[Warwick Davis]], who played [[Wicket Wystri Warrick/Legends|Wicket Wystri Warrick]]&mdash;Deej's son in the film&mdash;and the rest of the Warrick family as well as working with directors [[John Korty]] and [[George Lucas]]. He remembered his time as an Ewok fondly but as hard work. Following a fifteen-year acting career, Frishman switched to working in real estate.<ref name="Interview" />
