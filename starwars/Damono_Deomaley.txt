'''Damono Deomaley''' was a short, [[Gender|male]] [[humanoid]] who was present in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. While he was in the [[cantina]], the [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and the former [[moisture farmer]] [[Luke Skywalker]] entered looking for a [[pilot]], but ended up getting into a fight with two other patrons.

==Biography==
The [[humanoid]], [[Gender|male]] Damono Deomaley<ref name="Cantina panel" /> spent time in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. While Deomaley was in the [[cantina]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and the former [[moisture farmer]] [[Luke Skywalker]] entered, looking for passage to the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. Deomaley, standing between two human males on the opposite side of the bar, safely witnessed Kenobi and Skywalker's conflict with two other patrons, [[Ponda Baba]] and [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]]. Following the conflict, Deomaley conversed with one of the two [[Tonnika sisters]] by the bar, as Kenobi and Skywalker negotiated with [[Han Solo]] in the booth directly behind the pair.<ref name="ANH" />

==Personality and traits==
Deomaley was a short humanoid, with [[Color|blond]] [[hair]], green [[eye]]s and fair skin.<ref name="ANH" />

==Behind the scenes==
This character appeared uncredited and unnamed in the [[1977]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="ANH" /> In [[2015]], during the "Secrets of the Mos Eisely Cantina" panel at [[Celebration Anaheim (2015)|Celebration Anaheim]], [[Pablo Hidalgo]] identified the individual as being incorrectly known by the production name "Little Aunt Beru", and allowed a fan to choose the in-universe [[canon]] name of Damono Deomaley, named partly to honor effects make-up artist Nick Maley who worked on the sequence and contributed much background info about the UK shoot.<ref name="Cantina panel" /> Production notes for Episode IV made by [[John Mollo]] identify "Little Aunt Beru" as being played by the actress [[Gilda Cohen]];<ref name="ANHart">''[[The Art of Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope]]''</ref> however, illustrative comparison to images on the fansite Aveleyman.com show that actor [[Jeff Moon]] is the one playing the character identified by Hidalgo as "Little Aunt Beru".<ref name="Aveleyman">{{WebCite|url=http://www.aveleyman.com/ActorCredit.aspx?ActorID=3340|text=Aveleyman &ndash; Jeff Moon|work=[http://www.aveleyman.com Aveleyman]|archivedate=20181105110254}}</ref> Moon is listed in Mollo's production notes as playing one of four characters with the production name of "Local ugly man,"<ref name="ANHart" /> suggesting either Mollo's notes, or Hidalgo's identification are incorrect.
