'''Depa Billaba's lightsaber'''<ref name="Aftermath EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/aftermath-episode-guide|text="Aftermath" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}}</ref> was a [[lightsaber]] utilized by [[Jedi Master]] [[Depa Billaba]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> It could produce a blade of [[Color|green]]<ref name="Kanan 1" /> or blue [[plasma]].<ref name="Aftermath" />

A student of legendary Jedi Master [[Mace Windu]], Billaba was herself an exceptional lightsaber combatant who often sought to pass on the skills she had learned. Her lightsaber consisted of a solid metal cylinder ending in a tapered pommel and a multi-ringed emitter, the latter of which was enveloped by a diamond-shaped metal segment that wrapped entirely around the hilt.<ref name="TLC" />

Billaba would wield her lightsaber during her time as a [[Jedi General]] until she perished during the execution of [[Order 66]], where she used it to defend herself against her formerly loyal clone troopers whilst urging her Padawan, [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]], to flee until it was shot out of her hands.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Depa Billaba's lightsaber first appeared in the comic book [[Kanan 1|''Kanan'' 1]], the first issue of the comic series ''[[Star Wars: Kanan]]''.<ref name="Kanan 1" /> Concept art of the weapon created for ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' in [[2012]] showed it with a blue blade,<ref name="Aftermath EG" /> but ''Kanan'' depicted it as green instead.<ref name="Kanan 1" /> The blue color was [[Retroactive continuity|brought back]] by the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Lightsaber Collection]]''<ref name="TLC">''[[Star Wars: The Lightsaber Collection]]''</ref> and the ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' television episode "[[Aftermath (episode)|Aftermath]]."<ref name="Aftermath" /> However, the green blade was once again used in a variant cover for [[Legacy of Vader 9|''Legacy of Vader'' 9]].<ref name="Legacy of Vader 9">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/130738/star_wars_legacy_of_vader_2025_9_variant/variant|text=Star Wars: Legacy of Vader (2025) #9 (Variant)}}</ref>
