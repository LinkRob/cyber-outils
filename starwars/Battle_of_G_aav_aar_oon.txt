The '''Battle of G'aav'aar'oon''' occurred during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]]. It resulted in the destruction of a [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi]] fortress on the [[Asteroid/Legends|asteroid]] [[G'aav'aar'oon]] located within the [[Vodilux Star Cluster]]. The ruins of the fortress were later settled by the [[Gamorrean/Legends|Gamorrean]] [[Nuns of G'aav'aar'oon]].
