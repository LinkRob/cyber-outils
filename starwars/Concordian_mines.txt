The '''Concordian mines''' were a series of [[Mining|mines]] constructed on [[Concordia]], the [[moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]]. The mines were largely believed to be abandoned, that was until [[21 BBY]], when [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] discovered the presence of the [[Mandalorian]] terrorist group known as [[Death Watch]], who were using the mines as a base of operations.

Obi-Wan was subsequently captured by [[Mandalorian]] warriors during his investigation of the mines. However, [[Satine Kryze|Duchess Satine Kryze]], who had followed him to the mines, helped him escape. Obi-Wan and Satine then discovered that [[Pre Vizsla]], the Governor of Concordia, was the leader of Death Watch. Kenobi briefly dueled Vizsla before escaping the moon with Satine.<ref name="TMP" />
