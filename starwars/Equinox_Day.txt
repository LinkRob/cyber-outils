'''Equinox Day''' was an [[Standard year|annual]] holiday that marked the beginning of [[autumn]] on the [[planet]] [[Hosnian Prime]]. On that [[Standard day|day]], the planet's [[Republic City|capital]] hosted [[sunsail]] races, in which contestants traversed a course that followed the river's path while people gathered along the banks to watch them.<ref name="Bloodline" />
