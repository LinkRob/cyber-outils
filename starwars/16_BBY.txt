The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''16 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3261 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7961''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*The [[Lars homestead|Lars moisture farm]] is attacked by [[Mercenary|mercenaries]]. [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] tries to defend young [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]], who was playing outside, and together with [[Owen Lars|Owen]], drive the bandits away. However, Lars then punches Kenobi and tells him not to go anywhere near Luke.<ref name="Time of Death">{{StoryCite|story=Time of Death|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>
*The [[chain code]] of [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] is encoded into a [[Boba Fett's armor|set of armor]] once worn by his [[Parent|father]], [[Jango Fett]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="chain code">{{MandoDate|14}} In "Chapter 14: The Tragedy," [[Boba Fett]] states in that his [[chain code]] was encoded into [[Boba Fett's armor|his armor]] twenty-five [[Standard year|years]] prior to the events of the chapter. Therefore, Boba's chain code was encoded into his [[armor]] around the year 16 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref>

==Births==
*[[Unkar Plutt]] on [[Crul]].<ref name="Episode VII" />
*[[Nifera Shu]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Shu">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Resistance Reborn]]'' to [[34 ABY]]. As ''Resistance Reborn'' establishes that [[Nifera Shu]] is fifty [[Standard year|years]] old, she must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around 16 BBY.</ref>

==Deaths==
*[[Drua's mother]]<ref name="Drua's mother">In ''[[Lords of the Sith]]'', it is stated that the [[Drua's mother|mother]] of [[Drua]] died three [[Standard year|years]] after the end of the [[Clone Wars]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the end of the Clone Wars to [[19 BBY]], basic math dictates that Drua's mother died in 16 BBY.</ref>
*[[FLO]] "Wanda"<ref name="Wanda Death">"[[The Veteran]]" establishes that [[FLO]] broke down three [[Standard year|years]] after [[Order 66]], which corresponds to [[16 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>
*[[Lona Aphra]] on [[Arbiflux]]<ref name="Lona Death">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[death]] of [[Lona Aphra]], which [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 34|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 34]] establishes took place on [[Arbiflux]], to [[16 BBY]].</ref>
