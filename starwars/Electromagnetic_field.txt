An '''electromagnetic field''' was a type of [[Force field|field]] that was generated by electric devices, such as a [[holoprojector]]. [[B1-series battle droid]]s were able to pick up the fields, as one B1 showcased during the [[Onderonian Civil War]]. The [[droid]] picked up an electromagnetic field, which was being generated from an [[imagecaster]], and another droid pointed out where it was coming from. This [[battle droid]] walked to investigate the bag, with his two fellow units backing away in cowardice, but a message from [[Onderon]]'s former [[Monarch of Onderon|king]], [[Ramsis Dendup]], soon began to play, and Dendup urged the [[Onderonian]] people to fight back against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The civilian did as he requested, and the three battle droids that discovered the field were quickly destroyed.<ref name="Tipping Points" /> [[Tensor field]]s where a type of electromagnetic field.<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
