'''B'omarr regeneration gems''' were precious [[gemstone]]s kept by the [[B'omarr Order|B'omarr monks]] for their regenerative properties. In the [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] on [[Tatooine]], formerly a B'omarr monastery, such gems were stored in a chamber protected by a triple door.<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
