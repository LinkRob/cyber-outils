A '''boon piece''' was a type of game piece used in the game [[Canto Pass]]. [[Canister]], [[The Datapad]], [[Scout (Canto Pass)|Scout]], [[Scout II]], [[Scout III]], [[Scout IV]], and [[The Score]] were types of boon pieces used in the game.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Boon pieces appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, they were one of several types of Canto Pass pieces that could be collected and used customize the appearance of the player's [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]] and the player's [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
