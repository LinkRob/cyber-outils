'''Chick peas''' was an edible [[soup]]-like dish found on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Golak]].<ref name="TBOG" />

{{Food-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
This [[Foodstuff|dish]] made its first appearance in the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 1|Volume 2]] episode "[[The Bandits of Golak]]", written and directed by [[Ishan Shukla]], and produced by [[88 Pictures]].<ref name="TBOG" /> It was designed by both [[TG Satheesh]] and [[Aajjay Mahajan]].<ref name="Focus">{{VisionsFocus|The Bandits of Golak}}</ref>

In an interview with [[Wikipedia:IGN|IGN India]], 88 Pictures Founder and CEO, [[Milind D. Shinde]] referred to this chickpea-based dish by one of its real-life names, [[Wikipedia:Chana masala|chole]], stating their goal with the food in the episode was to more closely imitate [[Wikipedia:Hindi cinema|Bollywood]] as opposed [[Wikipedia:Hollywood|Hollywood]], avoiding depicting stereotypical food like [[Wikipedia:Samosa|Samosas]], and instead choosing to include foods such as chole (also known as chana masala), as well as [[Wikipedia:Panipuri|panipuri]] and [[Wikipedia:Jalebi|jalebi]].<ref name="IGN India">{{WebCite|url=https://in.ign.com/star-wars-visions/183457/interview/star-wars-visions-vol-2-team-behind-bandits-of-golak-short-talks-about-finding-inspiration-from-indi|text=Star Wars: Visions Vol 2 – Team Behind Bandits of Golak Short Talks About Finding Inspiration From Indian Culture and Bollywood|work=[[Wikipedia:IGN|IGN India]]|author=Rupesh Nair|archivedate=20250113042743}}</ref>
