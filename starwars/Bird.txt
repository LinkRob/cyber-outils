'''Birds''' or '''avians''' were a group of organisms which included the [[convor]]ees from the [[moon]] of [[Wasskah]] and the [[porg]]s of the [[ocean]] [[planet]] [[Ahch-To]]. Birds also lived on the arboreal planet [[Naboo]], as well as the moon of [[Yavin 4]] and the [[forest]] planet [[Rodia]], where they were included in the [[diet]] of predatory [[Ghest]]s.<ref name="HttJ">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref> Most birds had feathers, wings, and the ability to fly.<ref name="ALD">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> Birds usually built nests in [[tree]]s, though some [[species]] were known to make their homes in burrows beneath the ground.<ref name="Creature Safari">{{YJA|Creature Safari}}</ref> They were hatched from [[egg]]s.<ref name="TLJNovel">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> Some bird species communicated via so-called bird calls.<ref name="Feather Frenzy">{{YJA|Feather Frenzy}}</ref> Sentient avian species included [[Hiitian]]s,<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> [[Snee (avian)|Snees]],<ref name="ACGAS">{{StoryCite|story=All Creatures Great and Small|book=Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I}}</ref> and [[Whiforlan]]s.<ref name="Weapon of a Jedi">''[[The Weapon of a Jedi: A Luke Skywalker Adventure]]''</ref> Many birds included [[Berry|berries]] in their diet.<ref name="Creature Safari" />

Avians on a number of worlds would imprint on the first creature they saw once they emerged from their eggs.<ref name="TLJNovel" />
