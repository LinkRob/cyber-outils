{{Quote|Agent, have you ever heard of the planet Dandoran? It's on the neighboring system, Doran.|[[Colonel]] [[Jennica Pierce]], to her [[agent]]|<ref name="Commander" />}}
'''Dandoran''' was a lush and [[water]]y [[planet]] located in the [[Doran system]],<ref name="DB" /> within [[Hutt Space]].<ref name="Commander" /> Throughout history, it was often used to hide cartel [[Smuggler|smuggling]] operations.<ref name="DB" /> It was covered in crystal clear lakes and pristine forests.<ref name="TUC">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref>

==History==
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Saponza]], his [[Saponza's partner|partner]], and [[Han Solo]] joined forces to [[Mission to recover medical supplies|raid]] a [[Hutt Clan|cartel]] [[Unidentified Cartel den|den]] on Dandoran, which was hoarding medical supplies. At that time, Dandoran was the site of a conflict between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Commander" />

On a southern continent of Dandoran, the Empire also installed a secret research facility to produce [[Undead Trooper]]s. At some point the Undead Troopers [[Undead Troopers invasion|escaped]], and Saponza and his partner were sent in to clear them out before they could spread across the whole [[The galaxy|galaxy]].<ref name="Commander" />

[[Jessika Pava]], a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served as a [[T-70 X-wing starfighter|X-wing fighter]] [[pilot]] for the [[Resistance]], hailed from Dandoran.<ref name="TFA NA">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: New Adventures]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dandoran is the first planet unlocked after the player constructs the Planetary Command. Once unlocked the player can move their base there.
