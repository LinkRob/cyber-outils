The '''cedar''' was a [[tree]] that could be found in the [[forest]]s of the [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]. During a meeting between former [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]] heroes [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], [[Han Solo]], and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]<ref name="Last Shot" /> in [[7 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Last Shot]]'' to [[7 ABY]].</ref> the fresh smell of cedar and [[pine]] trees mixed with the scent of the [[droid]] [[BX-778]]'s cooking within the [[Han and Leia's apartment|apartment]] owned by Solo and Organa.<ref name="Last Shot" />

==Behind the scenes==
