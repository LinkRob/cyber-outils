During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Ahsoka Tano]] were '''ambushed''' by a pair of [[IG-86 sentinel droid]]s aboard the ''[[Vulture's Claw]]'', while attempting to find their missing [[astromech droid]], [[R2-D2]]. Skywalker and Tano failed to retrieve R2-D2, with replacement droid [[R3-S6]]<ref name="DOAD" />—secretly a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[spy]]<ref name="Duel of the Droids">{{TCW|Duel of the Droids}}</ref>—claiming that he was not on board after looking at the manifest. However, the ship's owner, [[scavenger]] [[Gha Nachkt]], did in fact have the missing droid on board, with the incident making him realize that R2-D2 would clearly be worth something to the Separatists, leading him to offer the droid to [[General]] [[Grievous]] aboard [[Skytop Station]].<ref name="DOAD" />
