The '''''Exigent''''' was a [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier]] in the service of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Following the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Count to Three" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> it was destroyed in the [[Nuvar system]] during a routine [[scout]]ing mission when it was struck by [[missile]]s from a nearby [[Nuvar system defense station|defense station]] that had recently been captured from the [[New Republic]] without having its targeting systems cleared. [[Titan Squadron]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilots]] [[Shen]] and [[Havina Vonreg]], who were among the survivors, boarded the [[Space station|station]] and fixed its targeting, with the [[Star Destroyer]] [[Overseer (Imperial-class)|''Overseer'']] searching the Nuvar [[Star system|system]] for survivors.<ref name="Count to Three" />
