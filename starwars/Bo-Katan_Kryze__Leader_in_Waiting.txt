"'''Bo-Katan Kryze: Leader in Waiting'''" is an article about [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] with words by [[Kristin Baver]] that was published in [[Star Wars Insider 208|''Star Wars Insider'' 208]]<ref name="Article" /> on [[January 25]], [[2022]]<ref name="Titan Comics" />

The article is a biography of Bo-Katan Kryze, highlighting her life during the [[Clone Wars]] with her sister [[Satine Kryze]], in the [[Galactic Civil War]] with [[Sabine Wren]], and in the time of the [[New Republic]] alongside [[Din Djarin]]. It includes quotes from [[Katee Sackhoff]].
