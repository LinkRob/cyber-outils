'''Alderaanian peacocks''' were [[peacock]]s<ref name="TRS" /> that shared their name with [[Alderaanian]]s, residents of the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> the [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Pan Eyta]] told fellow [[Tempest]] Runners [[Lourna Dee]] and [[Zeetar]] the secret of how [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] acquired his [[Path]]s&mdash;highly usable [[hyperspace route]]s that could only be used by [[Starship|ships]] with [[Path engine]]s. Dee was surprised and felt she now understood why Eyta had been strutting around like an Alderaanian peacock for the last [[Standard month|months]], wondering what other intel he had gathered.<ref name="TRS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alderaanian peacocks were mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="TRS" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
