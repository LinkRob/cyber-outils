'''Aardman Animations''' is a British animation studio founded in [[Wikipedia:Bristol|Bristol]], [[Wikipedia:England|England]] in [[1972]]. It is known for films made using stop-motion and clay animation techniques, particularly those featuring its plasticine characters [[Wikipedia:Wallace and Gromit|''Wallace and Gromit'']], [[wikipedia:Chicken Run|''Chicken Run'']], and [[Wikipedia:Shaun the Sheep|''Shaun the Sheep'']].<ref name="About" /> Aardman created a short clip of Gromit wielding a [[lightsaber]] for [[Celebration Europe (2007)|Celebration Europe]] in [[2007]].<ref name="Celebration Europe">{{YouTube|video=1fHCwZU81RI|text=Wallace Gromit Celebration Europe|channel=@Spaceballdeu|name=Spaceballdeu|archivedate=20260312030426}}</ref> Later, Aardman produced the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2]] episode "[[I Am Your Mother]]," written and directed by the studio's [[Magdalena Osinska]] and released on [[Disney+]] on [[May 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="Osinska">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-i-am-your-mother-interview|text=Meet the Visionaries: Magdalena Osinska on the Personal Story of "I Am Your Mother"}}</ref>
