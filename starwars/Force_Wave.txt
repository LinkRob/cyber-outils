A '''Force Wave''',<ref name="Darth Vader 34">[[Darth Vader (2020) 34|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 34]]</ref> also known as a '''Force pulse''',<ref name="Aphra 33">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 33|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 33]]</ref> was a phenomenon of [[the Force]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 32">[[Bounty Hunters 32|''Bounty Hunters'' 32]]</ref> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Bounty Hunters 32|''Bounty Hunters'' 32]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> a Force wave was released as a result of the [[Battle at Amaxine Station|destruction]] of the [[Fermata Cage]] and spread across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 32" /> As it rippled outward, [[Darth Sidious]] noted that his grip on the Force became tenuous.<ref name="Hidden Empire 5">[[Hidden Empire 5|''Hidden Empire'' 5]]</ref> Later, when the Force wave returned, it caused a massive [[disturbance in the Force]] that caused [[Sly Moore]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to lose control of their powers, with Vader devastating his immediate surroundings.<ref name="Darth Vader 33">[[Darth Vader (2020) 33|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 33]]</ref> Sidious, however, was able to take control of the Force once again due to his mastery over it.<ref name="Darth Vader 35">[[Darth Vader (2020) 35|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 35]]</ref> Vader managed to regain his control over the Force during a [[Attack on Vader|skirmish]] against the [[Scourge]], in which he focused on his hatred of himself and his master to focus his abilities.<ref name="Vader 39">[[Darth Vader (2020) 39|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 39]]</ref>
