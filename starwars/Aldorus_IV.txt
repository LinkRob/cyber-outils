'''Aldorus IV''' was a [[Mining/Legends|mining]] [[Planet/Legends|planet]] in the [[Aldorus system]].

==Description==
Aldorus IV was a mining colony, the soil of which was overexploited by the [[INFRAC]], a mega-corporation aligned with [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine's]] [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]].<ref name="CasusBelli" />
