'''''Darth Vader'' 18''' is the eighteenth issue of the [[2020]] comic book series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Leonard Kirk]], and [[Alex Sinclair]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 1]], [[2021]].

==Publisher's summary==
''RED REVENGE! LADY QI'RA not only insulted the Empire but challenged it, too. In the end, DARTH VADER showed her criminal organization the Empire is not to be toyed with. But now fearful whispers echo in every corner of the Empire &ndash; Darth Vader is on the hunt, searching for anyone with any connection to the criminal organization known as Crimson Dawn. How deep has Crimson Dawn infiltrated and is it worth the wrath of Vader?''
