'''Enya Keen''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Jedi]] who was the [[Padawan]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Char-Ryl-Roy]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Enya and her master, along with [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]] and Jedi Master [[Creighton Sun]], traveled to [[Eiram]] aboard the [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] ''[[Valiant]]'' on a mission to deliver medical supplies.<ref name="Convergence" /> She fought in the [[Night of Sorrow|Battle of Dalna]].<ref name="Cataclysm" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Powers and abilities==
Enya Keen was a capable [[lightsaber]] combatant who could block blasterfire from [[Security droid|enforcer droids]].<ref name="Cataclysm" /> She could use [[telekinesis]] and sense emotions through [[the Force]].<ref name="Convergence" /> She had the mechanical skill to rebuild the [[astromech droid]] [[4VO-TG]].<ref name="Cataclysm" />

==Equipment==
Enya Keen wielded a [[Keen's lightsaber|yellow-bladed lightsaber]].<ref name="Convergence" />
