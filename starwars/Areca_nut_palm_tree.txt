The '''areca nut palm tree''' was a type of [[palm tree]] that grew on the [[beach]]es of the [[planet]] [[Scarif]], where it offered natural protection.<ref name="ROUVG" /> The [[tree]]s were also plentiful in Scarif's verdant [[jungle]]s.<ref name="VE" />
