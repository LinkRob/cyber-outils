During the [[Galactic Civil War]] in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[New Republic]] [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] and her forces aboard the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]'', had spent a week smashing [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] '''blockades''' in the [[Nythlide Array]]. Syndulla reported on the status of her [[Barma Battle Group|battle group]], in which she stated that two carriers under [[Major]] [[Jaun]] would stay to support the local [[militia]] after they had successfully punched through the blockade, and that the ''Deliverance'' was to return to its primary objective of [[Hunt for Shadow Wing|hunting]] down the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
