'''Edcel Bar Gane''' was a [[Roonan]] [[Gender|male]] [[Senator]] in the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. He was senator during the [[Battle of Naboo|Naboo Crisis]]<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> in [[32 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> and remained in the position during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Pursuit" /> He wore a dark [[Color|blue]] [[velvet]] [[Clothing|overcoat]].<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> 

Gane had the same last name as [[Den Gane]],<ref name="Welcome Aurebesh">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}} {{C|The [[Aurebesh]] text appearing on the monitor as Gane makes their speech, says "[[Senator|SEN]]. [[Den Gane|DEN GANE]] • [[Roona|ROONA]]"}}</ref> a [[Politics|politician]] in the [[Imperial Senate]] of the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Welcome">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref> who represented the world of [[Roona]],<ref name="Welcome Aurebesh" /> Edcel Bar Gane's [[homeworld]];<ref name="Join the Battle" /> but was of a [[Unidentified species (Chandrila)|different species]].<ref name="Welcome Aurebesh" />

==Personality and traits==
Edcel Bar Gane was a [[Roonan]]<ref name="Join the Battle" /> male<ref name="Geektionary French" /> who was 1.72 [[meter]]s 5 [[Foot|feet]], 8 inches) tall,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> with [[Color|ice-blue]] [[eye]]s<ref name="Geektionary" /> and gray skin.<ref name="Pursuit" /> Gane was a veteran senator, who helped oust [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]] from office ten years before the Clone Wars.<ref name="Date" /> Gane was an outspoken supporter of [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. Gane was hot-tempered and opposed any attempt to seek peace with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]].<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Skills and abilities==
As a member of the [[Galactic Senate]], Gane was experienced in politics and had advanced legislative knowledge.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
The character was voiced by [[Stephen Stanton]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.
