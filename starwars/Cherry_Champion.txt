The '''Cherry Champion''' was a costume sometimes worn by the [[Wookiee]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Grozz]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Mighty Champion|standard costume]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cherry Champion was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Grozz, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Cherry Clubs]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing it individually within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
