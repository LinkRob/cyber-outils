The '''Annual Batuu Holochess Tournament''' was a [[Dejarik|holochess]] tournament held each [[Standard year|year]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. [[Koopla Nanuk]] hosted a broadcast of the event one year on radio station [[BSO 401.72]], which was played in [[Black Spire Outpost]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]].<ref name="GEStaff">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] has official backstory elements that, though not directly published for the public, are officially canon details that can be obtained verbally from cast members working in the land.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Annual Batuu Holochess Tournament was first created for [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which opened to guests at Disneyland Park on [[May 31]], [[2019]].<ref name="GEStaff" />
