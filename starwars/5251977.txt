'''5251977''' was a small [[planet]] so obscure that it had not been given a name besides its numerical designation.<ref name="Lost Stars" /> It was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] in grid square P-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="GMA" /> Because of its slow rotation, 5251977 had [[Standard day|days]] and nights that lasted the equivalent of several [[Standard week|weeks]] on most other planets. Following its [[Battle of Hoth|defeat]] at the hands of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] abandoned [[Echo Base]], their headquarters on [[Hoth]], and built a [[Rebel base on 5251977|new base]] on 5251977. The base was quite big compared to its predecessor and had a large [[hangar]] housing a number of [[starfighter]]s, some [[Braha'tok-class gunship|Dornean gunships]] and other [[Starship|ships]] such as the ''[[Mighty Oak Apocalypse]]''. [[Corona Squadron]] went there sometime before the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />

==Behind the scenes==
The planetoid's designation of 5251977 is derived from the release date of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]&mdash;[[May 25]], [[1977]], or 5/25/1977, in the [[Wikipedia:Date and time notation in the United States|United States date and time notation]].
