The '''Congress of Malastare''', also called the '''Malastare Congress''', was a [[planet]]ary legislative body on the [[planet]] [[Malastare]]. During the waning [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], they were represented in the [[Galactic Senate]] by [[Senator]] [[Aks Moe]],<ref name="TPM" /> and was replaced by Senator [[Ask Aak]] following Moe's [[death]].<ref name="Senators">{{EncyclopediaCite|Chancellors, Senators and States Persons of the Republic}}</ref>

At some point, two [[Dug]]s were able to join the Congress and engaged in discussion with one of its Gran representatives in the Congress's Senate pod.<ref name="Card">{{SWU|set=Secrets of Power|cardname=Congress of Malastare|link=1629192496|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/3yLBy}}</ref>
