"'''50 Greatest Reasons to Love the Original Trilogy'''" is a follow-up to the [[Star Wars Insider 147|''Insider'' 147]] article ''[[50 Greatest Reasons to Love the Star Wars Prequels!]]''. Published in [[Star Wars Insider 154|''Star Wars Insider'' 154]], it reveals the fifty best aspects of the [[original trilogy]], according to several ''[[Star Wars]]'' fans, writers, actors, and crew members.
