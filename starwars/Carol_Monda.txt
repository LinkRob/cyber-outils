'''Carol Monda''' is a veteran [[Wikipedia:Actor|actress]] and audiobook narrator. Monda's first contribution to ''[[Star Wars]]'' was narrating three short stories, [[Reirin (short story)|"Reirin"]], [[Beru Whitesun Lars (short story)|"Beru Whitesun Lars"]], and "[[Contingency Plan]]", in the [[2017]] [[From a Certain Point of View (audiobook)|audiobook]] of ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''<ref name="FACPOV">[[From a Certain Point of View (audiobook)|''From a Certain Point of View'' audiobook]]</ref>. Monda would go on to narrate [[Shadow Fall (audiobook)|''Shadow Fall'']] the second book in the [[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron|''Alphabet Squadron'' book series]].<ref name="Shadow Fall">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/609592/shadow-fall-star-wars-by-alexander-freed|text=Shadow Fall (Star Wars) Alexander Freed}}</ref>
