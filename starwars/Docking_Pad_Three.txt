'''Docking Pad Three''' was a landing platform located on [[Cloud City]] on the [[planet]] [[Bespin]]. A [[Bespin Wing Guard]] reported to the [[Baron Administrator]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] via [[hologram]] that the [[Unidentified grand slicer|grand slicer]] had arrived on the docking bay. Calrissian ordered the guard to ask [[Lobot]] to meet him there and have someone pour the [[slicer]] the finest [[glass]] of [[Alderaanian wine]].<ref name="The Coin" />
