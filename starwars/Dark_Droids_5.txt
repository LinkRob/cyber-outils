'''''Dark Droids'' 5''' is the fifth and final issue of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] limited series ''[[Star Wars: Dark Droids]]''. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Luke Ross]] and [[Alex Sinclair]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 27]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE TERRIFYING CONCLUSION OF THE BIGGEST ''STAR WARS'' COMIC EVENT OF THE YEAR! The final triumph of THE SCOURGE! How can THE REBELLION & THE EMPIRE combat such a threat? UNLIKELY ALLIES join forces to fight the greater evil invading the galaxy! And what is the final fate of warrior droid Ajax Sigma?''

==Continuity==
The issue shows [[Magna Tolvan]] under the control of the [[Scourge]] at the same time as the [[Battle in Tagge Space|skirmish aboard the ''Acquisitor'']]. As established by [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 39|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 39]], Tolvan had already been [[Rescue of Magna Tolvan|rescued]] from [[Epikonia]] by the time of that skirmish.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 39">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 39|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 39]]</ref> Later, the issue shows the rescue of Magna Tolvan towards the very end of the [[Scourge crisis|scourge of the droids]], shortly before the Scourge is destroyed. However, [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 38|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 38]] depicts the rescue<ref name="Doctor Aphra 38">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 38|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 38]]</ref> and is followed by ''Doctor Aphra'' 39, by which point the Scourge has not been destroyed.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 39" /> However, the issue contains a possible solution: when [[D-Squad]] [[Second attack on Scourge 01|attacks]] the [[Scourge One|''Scourge'' One]], the Scourge admits it can become confused if it is seeing memories and present events. As such, these events could be shown to the reader out of order in ''Dark Droids'' 5.

''Dark Droids'' 5 also shows [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] under the control of the Scourge in the final moments of the Scourge crisis.<ref name="Dark Droids 5">[[Dark Droids 5|''Dark Droids'' 5]]</ref> However, as established by [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 40|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 40]], there was no possible time for Aphra to fall victim to the Scourge herself before the crisis' conclusion.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 40">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 40|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 40]]</ref>
