'''Force projection''', also known as '''''Similfuturus''''', was a [[Fallanassi]] [[Force power|Force technique]] recorded in the [[sacred Jedi texts]] and utilized by [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] during the [[Battle of Crait]]. The technique allowed a [[Jedi]] or other user of the art to create an illusory doppelgänger of themselves that could be transmitted through [[the Force]] across vast distances.

==Description==
{{Quote|What, are you suggesting that you're fighting a bunch of Jedi, able to ''project'' themselves from place to place like Luke Skywalker?|Branwayne Spiftz, to Aderat|<ref name="Spark of the Resistance">''[[Spark of the Resistance]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Resistanceheroes-Forceprojection.png|thumb|left|230px|The Force projection of Luke Skywalker fades after he deems his work to be done.]]
Force projection<ref name="Kylo Ren DB">{{Databank|kylo-ren|Supreme Leader Kylo Ren}}</ref> required extreme concentration and focus.<ref name="The Last Jedi" /> The Jedi poured their [[Living Force]] presence into the [[Cosmic Force]], allowing them to create a simulacrum of themselves and small objects over distances of several [[light-year]]s.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> It was also possible to project several simulacra at the same time in the immediate vicinity of the caster.<ref name="THR 6">[[The High Republic (2023) 6|''The High Republic'' (2023) 6]]</ref>

Using this power was extremely taxing; [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] noted that [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], whose power in the Force rivaled his own immense powers as a Skywalker, would die just by trying. Although [[Luke Skywalker]] maintained a projection for several minutes, giving all of himself to the Force to allow him to physically interact with his surroundings, the strain cost him his life.<ref name="TROSVD" /><ref name="The Last Jedi" />

The simulacrum, being only a projection, allowed one to appear to perform seemingly impossible feats, such as being unscathed after taking a barrage of [[blaster]] [[Laser|bolts]], stabbed at with a weapon, or disappearing from one position and reappearing in another. It also allowed one to project something other than one's true physical appearance, such as making oneself appear younger, or appearing to have an object not currently in one's possession. Despite being otherwise immaterial, the user was at least capable of physical contact with others and could even fool advanced sensors and droids.<ref name="The Last Jedi" /><ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" />

==History==
{{Quote|See you around, kid.|Luke's projection, just before vanishing before Kylo Ren's eyes|<ref name="The Last Jedi" />}}
[[File:ForceProjection-TheLastJedi6.png|thumb|right|300px|Appearing via Force projection, Jedi Luke Skywalker delays Supreme Leader Kylo Ren's forces on Crait.]]
The power of Force projection originated with the [[Fallanassi]] order.<ref name="TROSVD" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date Note">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[34 ABY]]. [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] takes place immediately after the end of ''The Force Awakens''.</ref> [[Jedi Master]] Luke Skywalker projected his appearance from [[Ahch-To]] to [[Crait]], where he confronted his nephew, Kylo Ren, during the [[Battle of Crait]]. The feat was so strenuous that it led to Skywalker's death, after which he became one with the Force. He carried a copy of [[Han Solo]]'s [[Han Solo's dice|dice]] with him that he left with [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who left them at the [[Crait outpost|old Rebel base]] on the [[planet]]. Ren discovered the dice, which disappeared shortly after Skywalker's death.<ref name="The Last Jedi" />

Skywalker's ability to project himself became known in the wider galaxy after his death,<ref name="Spark of the Resistance" /> so the story of his stand against tyranny spread hope<ref name="The Last Jedi" /> as the First Order invaded.<ref name="TROSVD" /> Though some believed in Skywalker's power, others&mdash;such as [[First Order]] [[Commander]] [[Branwayne Spiftz]]&mdash;saw it as nothing more than a myth; Spiftz referred to the ability mockingly while chastising [[Lieutenant]] [[Aderat]] over the "disappearing" [[Minfar]] resistance fighters.<ref name="Spark of the Resistance" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I've heard something like this described as a Force power, like actually projecting a vision of yourself elsewhere. But we've actually never seen it in the movies. It's something a little different. This could be a way that he could win this sequence.|Rian Johnson|<ref name="The Last Jedi" />}}
Force projection first appeared in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], the second installment of the ''Star Wars'' [[sequel trilogy]].<ref name="The Last Jedi" /> [[Dopplegänger]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in [[Dark Empire 5|''Dark Empire'' 5]], which was authored by [[Tom Veitch]], penciled by [[Cam Kennedy]],<ref name="Dark Empire 5">[[Dark Empire 5|''Dark Empire'' 5]]</ref> and released on [[August 18]], [[1992]].<ref name="Publication 1">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54061/star_wars_dark_empire_1991_5|text=Star Wars: Dark Empire (1991) #5}}</ref> Force projection's alternate name of ''Similfuturus'', introduced in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' by [[Pablo Hidalgo]],<ref name="TROSVD" /> was first used in Legends as an alternate name for the dopplegänger ability.<ref name="Jedi Path">[[The Jedi Path: A Manual for Students of the Force (real-life book)|''The Jedi Path: A Manual for Students of the Force'']]</ref>

In a special feature called ''Balance of the Force'', [[Rian Johnson]] explained that, although he had imagined a scenario where Luke would be present on Crait and would accomplish incredible feats with the Force, he didn't want the Force to be reduced to a mere superpower. Instead, he wanted to find a way for Luke to beat Kylo Ren without causing him physical harm. Using the same approach he had employed for Rey and Ren with the [[Force-bond]], he used the Force projection as a way to allow Luke a final, intimate confrontation with Ren.<ref name="The Last Jedi" />
