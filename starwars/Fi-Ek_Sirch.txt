'''Fi-Ek Sirch''' was a [[Kajain'sa'Nikto]] [[Jedi]] who was a member of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Republic Era|Fall of the Republic]].<ref name="SWCT" /> Sirch participated in the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]<ref name="Episode II" /> in [[22 BBY]]<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], which includes the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] and Fi-Ek Sirch's appearance in the film, to [[22 BBY]].</ref> as a part of the [[Jedi assault team]] deployed to rescue [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and the Jedi [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] from the [[Petranaki Arena]].<ref name="Episode II" /> Wielding a [[Color|blue]] [[lightsaber]],<ref name="SWCT" /> Sirch assisted in thinning the hordes of [[battle droid]]s that had entered the arena. As the battle progressed, many Jedi lost their lives. The Jedi were ultimately surrounded and ordered to surrender by former [[Jedi Master]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. However, the surviving Jedi were rescued when the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] intervened.<ref name="Episode II" /> Sirch had orange skin and [[Clothing|wore]] brown [[Jedi apparel|Jedi robes]].<ref name="SWCT" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fi-Ek Sirch appeared in the [[2002]] [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], played by an uncredited extra.<ref name="Episode II" /> In ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the character was identified in [[2016]] by the digital card trading app [[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]].<ref name="SWCT" /> However, Card Trader does not provide enough information to determine where Sirch was positioned in the battle.

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star War'' Legends]] continuity, Sirch was identified in [[Star Wars Saga Daily Calendar 2003|''Star Wars Saga Daily Calendar'' 2003]]. [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Leland Chee]] also speculated that the character had died in the Battle of Geonosis in a [[Star Wars: Blogs|blog]] post.<ref name="Chee">{{Blog|holocron|10|Jedi Casualties: Battle of Geonosis}}</ref>
