A '''Pijal varactyl''' served the [[Pijali monarchy]], living in the [[palace stables]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]] during the [[Republic Era]]. In [[40 BBY]], it was the mount for the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] during the [[Pijali religion|ceremonial]] [[Fanry's Grand Hunt|Grand Hunt]] held before her [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]]. During the [[Grand Hunt|event]], the [[Pijal varactyl]] gracefully pursued the [[LM-432 crab droid|crab droid]] serving as the [[Hunting|hunt's]] prey. However, when the [[droid]] started [[Attack on the Grand Hunt|firing]] [[blaster]] bolts at Fanry, the [[Gender|princess]] fell off her [[varactyl]] as others came to her aid.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Assemble!|A herald, to the riders and their varactyls before the Grand Hunt|<ref name="MA" />}}
[[File:PijalCover.jpg|thumb|right|270px|The varactyl participated in the Grand Hunt on Pijal (pictured).]]
During the [[Republic Era]], the Pijal varactyl lived in the [[palace stables]] located on the grounds of the [[Pijali palace]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]].<ref name="MA" /> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' to [[40 BBY]].</ref> the [[Pijal varactyl|varactyl]] participated in the [[Pijali religion|ceremonial]] [[Fanry's Grand Hunt|Grand Hunt]] hosted prior to the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]]'s [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]] and assumption of Pijal's [[throne]]. After it was assigned as Fanry's [[Varactyl racing|riding]] beast for the evening, the [[Gender|princess]] mounted the [[varactyl]] and made her grand entrance to the [[Grand Hunt]]. As the varactyl brought Fanry nearer to the starting line, onlookers met her with applause. One of the participants, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], reflected that the varactyl's [[Color|white-tipped]] feathers made it stand out from the rest, wondering whether it had been chosen as the princess' mount for beauty or its speed.<ref name="MA" />

Soon after, a [[Unidentified herald|herald]] called for the participants to assemble. Once the [[horn]]s blew, the varactyl leapt forward with Fanry directing it swiftly toward the [[LM-432 crab droid|crab droid]] being used as the Grand Hunt's [[Hunting|prey]]. Fanry's varactyl quickly separated itself from the other participants, leaping over a trench, then over another. However, as the princess continued urging her beast forward in pursuit of the crab [[droid]] and approached its location, the droid began [[Attack on the Grand Hunt|firing]] [[blaster]] bolts in her direction. Fanry ducked low on her varactyl's back as [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], Pijal's Jedi [[lord]] [[regent]] [[Rael Averross]], and the [[Royal guard (Pijal)|royal guard]] [[captain]] [[Deren]] came to her aid.<ref name="MA" />

In the chaos, the princess slid off her mount and scraped her knee. While huddling behind her varactyl, Fanry raised her [[energy bow]] and fired back at the droid, although she missed. After Jinn managed to destroy the droid with his [[Qui-Gon Jinn's lightsaber|lightsaber]], the nearby participants cheered. The princess stumbled out from behind her varactyl, unharmed, and confirmed with Averross that her only injury was due to sliding off her mount. Afterward, the participants returned to the Pijali palace.<ref name="MA" />

==Personality and traits==
The varactyl had crimson, white-tipped feathers and lean muscles.<ref name="MA" />

==Skills and abilities==
As one of the [[Pijali monarchy]]'s varactyls, the creature was adept at maneuvering through jagged terrain at high speeds for either racing or ceremonial events, such as the Grand Hunt. Compared to other varactyls kept in the palace stables, it had an unflappable demeanor.<ref name="MA" />

==Equipment==
During the Grand Hunt, the varactyl wore a [[saddle]] and a [[harness]] fitted with automatic light-sensitive [[sensor]]s that glowed a pale shade of purple when it was dark outside.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The varactyl appeared in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
