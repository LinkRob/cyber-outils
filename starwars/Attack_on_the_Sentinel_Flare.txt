During the final [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Fully Operational" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] launched an '''attack''' on the [[Galactic Republic]] [[FireStar II-class Orbital Defense Platform|''FireStar II''-class Orbital Defense Platform]] ''[[Sentinel Flare]]''. As a result of the attack, the Confederacy managed to severely damage the [[space station]]. However, after the end of the war, the Republic's [[Proclamation of the New Order|successor state]], the [[Galactic Empire]], was able to restore the core functionality of the ''Sentinel Flare'' to service.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on the ''Sentinel Flare'' was mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']], a [[2018]] sourcebook published as part of [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] products.<ref name="Fully Operational" />
