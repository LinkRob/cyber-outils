{{Quote|The galaxy doesn't need us to blow up starships. We are good at one thing: having fun, in a place built for it. And I say it is our solemn responsibility to have as much fun as possible, every waking moment.|Dodibin|<ref name="The Ride" />}}
'''Dodibin''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Suerton]] who resided in [[Canto Bight]] and spent a night, along with his older brother [[Thodibin]] and younger brother [[Wodibin]], [[gambling]] with [[Kaljach Sonmi]]. He went by the nickname '''Dodi'''. Collectively he and his brothers were known as the [[Lucky Three]] due to their uncanny ability to win big at gambling and make back anything they might lose.<ref name="The Ride" />
