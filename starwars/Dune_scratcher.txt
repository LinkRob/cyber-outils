A '''dune scratcher''' was a type of large [[rat]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[moon]] [[Jedha]]. They were [[Limb|quadrupedal]], with [[Color|gray]], veiny skin, sharp claws, six red [[eye]]s, and rows of pointed [[Tooth|teeth]]. In [[382 BBY]], [[Tey Sirrek]], [[SK-0T]], and [[Vildar Mac]] confronted four dune scratchers in a tunnel underneath [[Enlightenment]] in [[Jedha City]].<ref name="The High Republic 8" />
