'''''Adventures in Wild Space'': Books 4–6''' is the unabridged audio version of books four to six of the [[canon]] ''[[Star Wars: Adventures in Wild Space]]'' series, written by [[Cavan Scott]] and [[Tom Huddleston]]. That includes the books [[Adventures in Wild Space: The Dark|''The Dark'']], [[Adventures in Wild Space: The Cold|''The Cold'']], and [[Adventures in Wild Space: The Rescue|''The Rescue'']]. The audiobook was performed by [[January LaVoy]] and was released by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[June 26]], [[2018]].<ref name="Blackstone Audio" />

==Publisher's summary==
'''Two kids take on the Empire.'''

===The Dark===
''Devious bounty hunter the shade is delivering the Graf children to Captain Korda when her ship is attacked by a mysterious species. Milo and Lina must trust their fate to their fellow prisoners. How will they escape THE DARKNESS?''

===The Cold===
''Milo and Lina Graf have picked up the trail of their kidnapped parents—but an ambush in the depths of Wild Space leaves them stranded on a desolate ice planet. With an old enemy out for revenge, can they survive THE COLD?''

===The Rescue===
''Milo and Lina Graf, have located the mysterious Wild Space planet where their parents are being held by the evil Empire. They must infiltrate a secret base to free them. With an entire legion of Imperial troops to contend with, can the young explorers at last succeed in THE RESCUE?''<ref name="Blackstone Audio" />
