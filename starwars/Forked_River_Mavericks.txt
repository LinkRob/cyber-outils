The '''Forked River Mavericks''' were a [[grav-ball]] team based on [[Lothal]]. In the grav-ball season held approximately six [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], they were generally a poor-performing team. When they faced the [[AppSci SaberCats]] in the final match of the season before the match for the league title, their record was 1-8. Their [[center striker]] performed erratically and their [[Kicker (grav-ball)|kicker]] lacked range. Furthermore, they were undisciplined, as their coach regularly had to shout at them and during pre-game drills, half of the team wandered or chatted with spectators. The SaberCats easily won the match by a margin of thirty-two points.<ref name="edge">''[[Servants of the Empire: Edge of the Galaxy]]''</ref>
