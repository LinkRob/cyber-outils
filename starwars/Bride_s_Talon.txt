'''Bride's Talon''' was a mountain on [[Cato Neimoidia]] outside the city of [[Tarko-se]]. It had a [[Train|conveyex]] track that ran through it. [[Tobias Beckett]] ordered [[Tuska]] to climb the face of it and keep watch during his attempt to steal a railcar.<ref name="SG">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>
