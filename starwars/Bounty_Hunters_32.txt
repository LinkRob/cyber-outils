'''''Bounty Hunters'' 32''' is the thirty-second issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Alessandro Miracolo]] and [[Arif Prianto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[March 8]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Zuckuss is teetering between life and death&hellip; A mysterious event has put the Gand bounty hunter in a deep coma reliving his past. Can T'onga and 4-LOM save Zuckuss before his mind is lost? And before the Empire's most lethal unit hunting Valance catches up to them&hellip;''

==Development==
On [[September 14]], [[2022]], writer [[Ethan Sacks]] tweeted that he was currently writing the script for the issue.<ref name="Tweet 1">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1570081041490284546|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=Respect. I will get back to work on the issue 32 script then.|archivedate=20221211011944}}</ref> Sacks discovered not much pre-existing lore existed for the planet [[Gand (planet)|Gand]] and thus was able to, in his own words, leave his own thumbprint behind on [[The galaxy|the ''Star Wars'' galaxy]] via issue 32.<ref name="Sacks Interview">{{InsiderCite|224|Sacks Appeal}}</ref>

==Continuity==
On the second page, the [[Raider II-class corvette|''Raider'' II-class corvette]] [[Corvus (Raider II-class)|''Corvus'']] is depicted in its modified [[New Republic]] version, when at the start of ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'', which takes place after this issue, the ''Corvus'' is depicted in its [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] version.<ref name="BFII">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>
