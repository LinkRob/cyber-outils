A '''battle''' took place<ref name="Wild Space" /> during the [[22 BBY/Legends|first year]]<ref name="ERC" /> of the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] when the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]] mounted an effort to capture the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Lanos]] from the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]], sending [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droid]] detachments to fight there. The fight itself was used as [[Propaganda/Legends|propaganda]] by the Separatist [[HoloNet/Legends|HoloNet]] [[Shadowfeed/Legends|news]].<ref name="Wild Space" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle received a brief mention in ''[[The Clone Wars: Wild Space]]'', a novel written by [[Karen Miller]] and published in [[2008]].<ref name="Wild Space" />
