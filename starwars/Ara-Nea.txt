'''Ara-Nea''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[alien]] who resided on [[Necropolis]] as an overseer of the [[Scavenger|scavenging]] there. Ara-Nea tried to swindle [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] out of her [[Currency|money]], then [[Death|kill]] her. When Rey realized that, his plan did not work out, and Ara-Nea was then [[Prison|imprisoned]] by his own [[Slavery|enslaved]] workers.<ref name="Age of Resistance" />
