'''Diles Anevi''' was the [[human]] [[governor]] of the [[Expansion Region]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. In [[10 BBY]], he met with [[Dryden Vos]], leader of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal organization, aboard Vos's [[Nau'ur-class yacht|yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]''.

The aim of the meeting was to strike a deal over waiving certain transit restrictions on Crimson Dawn [[starship]]s traveling along the [[Great Gran Run]] [[Hyperspace route|trade route]]. In return, Anevi hoped to persuade Vos to make financial contributions to charitable causes, but Vos lost his temper during the meeting and stabbed Anevi with a [[Petar|Kyuzo petar]], [[Death|killing]] him.

==Biography==
{{Quote|I get all worked up. Silly. It's a party.|Dryden Vos muses on his murder of Diles Anevi|audio=Dryden-Silly.flac|<ref name="Solo" />}}
[[File:Anevi_Death.png|thumb|left|215px|Dryden Vos killed Diles Anevi.]]
Diles Anevi was a [[human]] who served as [[governor]] of the [[Expansion Region]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> he met with the leader of the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime syndicate, [[Dryden Vos]], aboard the [[crime lord]]'s [[Nau'ur-class yacht|yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]'', while it was docked in [[Fort Ypso]] on the [[planet]] [[Vandor]]. The aim of the meeting was for a deal to be struck that would waive certain travel restrictions on Crimson Dawn [[starship]]s moving along the [[Great Gran Run]] [[hyperspace route]]. Anevi hoped that in return he could secure from Vos financial contributions to charitable causes he cared deeply about.<ref name="SoloOG" />

During the meeting, Vos lost his temper and stabbed Anevi in the chest<ref name="Solo" /> with a [[Petar|Kyuzo petar]],<ref name="SoloOG" /> causing him to let out a cry of pain that could be heard by party guests on the [[yacht]]'s [[observation deck]].<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> When Vos removed the blade, the governor dropped to the floor [[Death|dead]]. His corpse was dragged away by one of the crime [[lord]]'s [[Hylobon Enforcers]] as Vos cleaned the weapon and took a drink.<ref name="Solo" />

==Personality and traits==
The governor had light skin, dark [[eye]]s, and [[Color|graying-brown]] [[hair]].<ref name="Solo" />

==Equipment==
At the [[time]] of his death, Diles Anevi wore a gray [[gaberwool]] [[Clothing|mantle of office]] over a black shirt<ref name="SoloOG" /> and pants, along with black [[boot]]s<ref name="Solo" /> and a [[belt]] buckle that contained his transactional [[credit chip]] portfolio.<ref name="SoloOG" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|There was a debate about how Dryden would dispose of that unfortunate regional governor. Some of us really wanted him decapitated and we actually shot a version where a head rolls across the floor. Others felt that was a little too rough for Star Wars.|Jon Kasdan|<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|JonKasdan|status/1040529505670946817|[[Jon Kasdan]]|quote=continued|archivefile=File:Jon-Kasdan-SoloFacts-September-14-2018.png|archivedate=20200114055629}}</ref>}}
[[File:Anevi_Alternate.png|thumb|right|250px|Despite Miles Richardson being credited with playing Diles Anevi in ''Solo: A Star Wars Story'', the character is visibly played by a different actor in the film.]]
Diles Anevi first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Solo" /> and was first identified by name in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> The credits for the film identify [[Miles Richardson]] as the actor portraying the "Regional Governor,"<ref name="Solo" /> and the ''Official Guide'' also contains an image of Richardson in the role;<ref name="SoloOG" /> however, in the film itself, the governor is clearly portrayed by a different bearded actor.<ref name="Solo" />

During the filming of ''Solo'', the production staff debated how exactly Dryden Vos would dispose of Anevi. Screenwriter [[Jon Kasdan]] and others wanted to have Vos decapitate Anevi, and a version in which Anevi's head rolls across the floor was filmed, but some felt that would be too graphic for a ''[[Star Wars]]'' film, so the decapitation version of the scene was ultimately cut.<ref name="Twitter" />
