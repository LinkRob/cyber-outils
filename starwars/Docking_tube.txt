A '''docking tube''',<ref name="The Rebellion Begins">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref> also known by other names such as '''boarding tube''',<ref name="Battlefront II: Inferno Squad">''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]''</ref> '''suction tube''',<ref name="SC 102 AD">{{SkeletonCrew|Way, Way Out Past the Barrier}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref>, '''transport tube''',<ref name="Rebel Rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref> or '''umbilical tube''',<ref name="Thrawn: Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref> was a temporary gangway used to connect two [[starship]]s together in [[Realspace|space]] for transfer between them.<ref name="CYD2">''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Luke & Leia Adventure]]''</ref> These tubes could be extended to create a tunnel between the ships,<ref name="Rebel Rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref> and retracted when no longer in use.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> Some boarding tubes were transparent.<ref name="CYD2" />

At times, the port area on either side of the tube needed to be repressurized before opening a port door to access the tunnel. If beings were inside when one side was disconnected, they could experience explosive decompression.<ref name="Rebel Rising" /> These connections could be exploited to transfer an explosion within the tube to both conjoined ships.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />
