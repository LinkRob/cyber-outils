{{Quote|My sister, Bellan. Former queen of Rendel.|Zalla|<ref name="TFVC 3" />}}
'''Bellan''' was the [[queen]] of [[Rendel]]. After becoming queen, she [[Prison|imprisoned]] her sister [[Zalla]], thinking she would use her powers to overthrow her. Zalla managed to use her powers to escape, overrunning Rendel with [[plant]] life. Bellan fled from the [[planet]], and later hired [[pilot]]s and [[smuggler]]s for jobs to Rendel so that they could unknowingly attempt to defeat Zalla.

==Biography==
===Childhood===
{{Quote|We were different, of course, but it didn't matter. Or so she said. Everything changed the moment she became queen.|Zalla|<ref name="TFVC 3" />}}
[[File:ZallaAndBellan-TalesFromVadersCastle3.jpg|thumb|left|220px|Zalla and Bellan as children]]
Bellan was born on [[Rendel]], and was the sister of [[Zalla]], who, unlike the rest of her [[species]], was gifted with powers. The two sisters had a loving relationship, and Bellan assured Zalla that she didn't mind her being different. Some [[time]] later, Bellan became the [[queen]] of Rendel. Her attitude toward Zalla changed, and she feared that she would try to overthrow her, [[Prison|imprisoning]] her sister and treating her like a monster. Zalla was filled with hatred over her sister's betrayal, and waited until she could escape. She managed to do so by using her powers on a [[seed]] which had landed in her cell. With that seed, she spread plants across Rendel. Bellan fled, vowing revenge on her sister.<ref name="TFVC 3" />

===Bellan's revenge===
{{Quote|But whatever the aging pilot thought, there was a method to Queen Bellan's madness.|[[Lina Graf]]|<ref name="TFVC 3" />}}
While in hiding, Bellan hired various [[smuggler]]s and [[starship]] [[pilot]]s for jobs to Rendel, sending them in an attempt to defeat her sister. Believing Rendel had been cursed, most individuals she approached turned her offer down. Bellan hired the [[Barbadelan]] [[Karamu]] to deliver a statue to Rendel, but the pilot was unsuccessful and became trapped in Zalla's vines. She later found [[Han Solo]], who was willing to take the deal, thinking he would receive one million [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] in return. Bellan gave Solo a statue containing a toxic substance that was able to damage Zalla's plants, and the smuggler took it to Rendel.<ref name="TFVC 3" />

[[File:ZallaHit-TalesFromVadersCastle3.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Han Solo delivers Bellan's vengeance upon Zalla.]]
Once on Rendel, Solo was also captured by Zalla, who revealed Bellan's true intentions, informing him that he had been tricked. However, Bellan managed to get her revenge when Solo broke the statue by throwing it at Zalla, letting the substance escape and begin to weaken her powers. Zalla, along with the plants she had created, withered away and [[Death|died]] as Solo escaped Rendel.<ref name="TFVC 3" />

==Personality and traits==
Bellan had [[Color|yellow]] [[eye]]s and orange skin. She had white markings on her face. When she was a child, she and her sister Zalla were friends, despite the fact that Zalla was different. As an adult, however, she feared that Zalla would try to overthrow her and imprisoned her. When Zalla took over the kingdom, Bellan vowed to get revenge on her.<ref name="TFVC 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bellan first appeared in the flashback section of the [[2018]] [[canon]] comic [[Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle 3|''Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle'' 3]], written by [[Cavan Scott]] and illustrated by [[Corin Howell]].<ref name="TFVC 3" /> Scott originally gave Bellan and Zalla the names Ana and Helza, after Anna and Elsa, the two sister protagonists from [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] [[2013]] animated film [[Wikipedia:Frozen (2013 film)|''Frozen'']], but he later made a note to change the names to something less obvious.<ref name="Frozen Homage">{{SW|url=news/the-spooky-secrets-of-idws-tales-from-vaders-castle|text=The Spooky Secrets of IDW's ''Tales from Vader's Castle''}}</ref>
