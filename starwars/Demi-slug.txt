'''Demi-slugs''', once known as '''sand slugs''', were a type of [[Diet|carnivorous]] [[slug]] that lived on the [[planet]] [[Metagos]]. Their biology was altered by the [[Great Storm (Metagos)|Great Storm]], a [[radiation]] storm that devastated much of the world's surface and caused the slugs' skin to become brittle and [[glass]]-like. The [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] encountered a group of demi-slugs during a [[Mission to Metagos|mission]] to Metagos<ref name="GlassAbyss" /> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="AbyssDate">The events of ''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]'' take place the [[Standard week|week]] after the [[funeral of Qui-Gon Jinn]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[32 BBY]].</ref>
