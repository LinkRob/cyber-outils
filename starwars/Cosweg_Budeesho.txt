'''Cosweg Budeesho''' was a veteran [[holographer]] and [[Artist|cartoonist]] who lived on the [[planet]] [[Ando Prime]] with his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Seeja]] and their pet [[tooka]], [[Yari]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], he produced the [[propaganda]] [[Painting|piece]] "[[Do Your Part!]]" for the [[Intergalactic Association of Amalgamated Droid Builders]] with the aim of encouraging viewers of all ages to sign up as [[droid]] [[technician]]s for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Other works he produced were published in materials put out by [[TriPlanetary Press]], [[Baobab Publishing]] and the [[Shafr Center Press]] as well as other small publishing houses.<ref name="Propoganda" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cosweg Budeesho was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', which was released on [[October 25]], [[2016]] and written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].
