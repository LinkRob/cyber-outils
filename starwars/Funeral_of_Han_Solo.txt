Following the [[Battle of Starkiller Base|Battle]] of [[Starkiller Base]], a '''funeral''' was held by the [[Resistance]] on [[D'Qar]] for the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]].

==Prelude==
During the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], [[Han Solo]] attempted to turn his son [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] away from the [[First Order]] and the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] of [[the Force]], trying to convince him that [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] was just using him for his power. Ren refused and fatally impaled Solo with [[Kylo Ren's lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. In Solo's last moments, he looked in his son's [[eye]]s and touched his son's face, before falling down a shaft towards the core of [[Starkiller Base]] to his [[death]]. Not long after, the [[thermal oscillator]] was destroyed by the [[Resistance pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]], starting a chain reaction of explosions across the [[planet]]'s surface. Within [[Standard minute|minutes]], the planet imploded and then exploded<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> into a [[dwarf star]] later nicknamed "[[Solo (star)|Solo]]."<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==The funeral==
{{Quote|Han fancied himself a scoundrel. But he wasn't. He loved freedom – for himself, certainly, but for everyone else in the galaxy, too. And time after time, he was willing to fight for that freedom. He didn't want to know the odds in that fight – because he'd already made up his mind that he'd prevail. And time after time, somehow he did.|Leia Organa remembering Han Solo|<ref name="Expanded Edition" />}}
The memorial service started during the early [[Standard hour|hours]] of the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation of D'Qar]]. Though [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] realized that setting aside a few moments to remember a fallen hero wasn't the right thing to do at that moment, she thought that it would provide a chance to inspire [[troop]]s with the old [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] spirit from the [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]], even though she knew that Solo himself would have hated the ceremony, knowing that he didn't have patience for memorials and speeches as a man who was allergic to [[politics]] and suspicious of causes. Other guests at the [[funeral]] who showed their respects were [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]], [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]], [[Lieutenant]] [[Kaydel Ko Connix]], [[Commander]] [[Larma D'Acy]], [[General]] [[Caluan Ematt]], and the Resistance [[pilot]] [[Nien Nunb]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

During her speech, Organa spoke about Solo, including the fact that he always hated bullies, injustice and cruelty, reason for which he always stood against them, throughout all his life, from his youth on [[Corellia]], when he fought at both [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]] and [[Endor]], and even in his final moments at Starkiller. Organa noted that when she boarded the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' the only personal touch she found was a pair of [[Han Solo's dice|golden dice]]. She also recalled the occasion when Solo helped to [[Liberation of Kashyyyk|liberate]] [[Kashyyyk]]. Organa also took the opportunity to read out the names of the pilots who never made it back from Starkiller, such as [[Ello Asty]], [[Furillo]], or [[Red One (Resistance)|Red One]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

While the other Resistance members resumed the evacuation and returned to their positions, Organa placed a [[wood]]en [[Unidentified figurine|figurine]] carved by Solo the night before the [[Battle of Endor]] along with other items amongst the roots of a [[tree]], ending the funeral.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

==Aftermath==
Though Solo's funeral had been celebrated, moments to remember him still happened after the memorial service's end. When the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] was trying to convince Organa's brother and Solo's brother-in-law, [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] to train her on [[Ahch-To]], Skywalker, who had cut himself from [[the Force]], asked her and [[Chewbacca]] about Solo, leading them to reveal what happened at Starkiller Base. Subsequently, while Organa remembered Solo aboard the [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']], Luke did the same at [[Luke's hut|his hut]], crying for the loss of Solo, whom Luke had wanted to see once more.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

Following the [[Battle of Crait]], before [[Force spirit|becoming one]] with [[the Force]], Skywalker reminded Ren that Han would always be with him.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

==Behind the scenes==
The funeral of Han Solo first appeared in the [[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition|novelization]] of the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], directed and written by [[Rian Johnson]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> The decision to include Solo's funeral in the novel came from the novel's author [[Jason Fry]] and his editor [[Elizabeth Schaefer]].<ref name="Independent">{{WebCite|url=https://www.independent.co.uk/arts-entertainment/books/features/star-wars-last-jedi-book-expanded-edition-author-jason-fry-interview-luke-skywalker-han-solo-rian-a8245706.html|text=Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition author on creating new scenes, reimagining Luke Skywalker's life, and working with Rian Johnson|author=Sheperd, Jack|date=2018-03-11|work=[[Wikipedia:The Independent|The Independent]]|archivedate=20200819025732}}</ref> Johnson stated that the reason for not including the funeral in the film was that it didn't have a place in it.<ref name="Collider">{{WebCite|url=https://collider.com/the-last-jedi-han-solo-funeral-scene-rian-johnson|text=Rian Johnson Explains Why There's No Funeral for Han Solo in 'The Last Jedi'|author=Chitwood, Adam|date=2018-02-01|work=[[Wikipedia:Collider (website)|Collider]]|archivedate=20200225194745}}</ref>
