An [[Bird/Legends|avian]] '''species''' with [[Color/Legends|white]] feathers and a blue beak could be found wild on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Esseles/Legends|Esseles]]. The [[Species/Legends|species]] possessed long, thin tails and had blue skin around their yellow [[Eye/Legends|eyes]]. The species also had a multicolored crest above their beak. In the [[Standard year/Legends|year]] [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]], flocks of the species of birds could be found flying in the [[Mountain/Legends|mountainous]] regions near Esseles' [[Capital/Legends|capital]] [[City/Legends|city]] of [[Calamar]], as well as near the [[Qua family's villa|family villa]] of the wealthy [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Dezono Qua]].<ref name="DT4" />

==Behind the scenes==
This bird species first appeared in the [[Dark Times 5|fifth issue]] of the [[Star Wars: Dark Times: The Path to Nowhere|''Path to Nowhere'']] story arc of the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Times]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series. The issue was written by [[Randy Stradley]] under the name Mike Harrison and illustrated by [[Douglas Wheatley]]. Released in [[2007]], the issue did not state the species' name or level of [[Sentience/Legends|sentience]].
