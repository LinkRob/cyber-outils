The '''Fjor system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Grohl sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square R-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained a [[Unidentified desert planet|desert planet]] that was home to [[fjor-bat]]s.<ref name="PD13" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the system was the site of a [[Battle over a desert planet|battle]] between the [[Resistance]], the [[Ranc Gang]], and the [[First Order]].<ref name="PD13">[[Poe Dameron 13|''Poe Dameron'' 13]]</ref>
