'''Corvus''' was a [[forest]] [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that was home to the [[city]] of [[Calodan]].<ref name="Mandalorian Handbook" />

==Description==
Corvus was a world of vast forests and distant mountains. The planet was rich in raw [[Mineral]]s and natural resources ideal for [[starship]] construction as were many planets in the [[Corvus system]].<ref name="Anger">{{TOTE|The Path of Anger}}</ref>

==History==
===Imperial pawn===
Corvus was originally a humble, backwater planet with little to no economic value. At some point, [[Morgan Elsbeth]] arrived on the planet, seeing the potential of its natural resources. Morgan promised the people economic progress, jobs, and wealth. Thus the people elected her [[Magistrate]] of the planet. During the [[Imperial Era]], factories and mines were constructed to harvest the planet’s raw materials and goods. Morgan, however, paid little in wages, keeping most of the profits for herself and her [[Morgan Elsbeth's forces|conglomerate]] which left the people of Corvus poor and disillusioned.<ref name="Anger">{{TOTE|The Path of Anger}}</ref>

Over the years, after the fall of the [[Galactic Empire]], Morgan’s rule became more despotic and tyrannical, with her continuously exploiting and strip-mining the world and its resources, with her mercenary forces abusing and terrorizing the citizens. Between 5 and 9 ABY, a [[New Republic]] delegation led by ambassador [[Nadura]] attempted to arrest Elsbeth for her crimes, but Morgan quickly [[Skirmish in Calodan|had them massacred]]; as a show of force and terror, Morgan subsequently ordered the forests around Calodan burned and declared to the people that the fires would continue burning and they would work night and day in her factories cementing her statues as a cruel despot.<ref name="Hate">{{TOTE|The Path of Hate}}</ref>

===Liberation===
[[File:MarkoManev-Ahsoka.png|thumb|left|175px|Ahsoka Tano in the burnt forests of Corvus]]
{{Quote|My base of operations is on Corvus, a planet ripe with raw goods and materials.|Morgan Elsbeth|<ref name="Anger">{{TOTE|The Path of Anger}}</ref>}}
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="13 Date">{{MandoDate|13}}</ref> the former [[Jedi]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] could be found on the planet<ref name="Chapter 11" /> in pursuit of a lead on the location of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn" from his disciple [[Morgan Elsbeth]], who had come to rule over the city of Calodan with an iron fist as [[magistrate]]<ref name="Chapter 13" /> of her own [[Elsbeth's regime|regime]].<ref name="Journey">{{InsiderCite|216|The Journey of Din Djarin}}</ref> Tano's presence on Corvus was known to an old ally of hers, the [[Mandalorian]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze]]. After [[Assault on the Imperial Gozanti freighter|seizing]] an [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|Imperial ''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] on the [[Water|estuary]] [[moon]] [[Trask]], Kryze told the [[Children of the Watch|Child of the Watch]] [[Din Djarin]] to seek Tano out on Corvus and bring to her [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], a powerful [[Force-sensitive]] [[Youngling|infant]].<ref name="Chapter 11" /> Elsbeth gave Djarin the task of killing Tano, who had been giving her problems. Eventually Djarin met up with Tano, and after a short fight in the woods they united to take Calodan. Tano defeated Elsbeth in a duel and control of the city went to [[Governor]] [[Wing]].<ref name="Chapter 13" />
