'''Blaster sights''' were a type of weapon attachment that could only be applied to ranged [[Blaster|energy weaponry]]. The attachment worked by "painting" its target with energy on a half trigger pull. The energy trickled out of the weapon's [[barrel]] and allowed the sight's user to know where they were aiming. Most models illuminated beams visible to the [[human]] visual spectrum, but some used wavelengths that were invisible to the naked [[eye]], requiring special [[goggles]] to see. Built-in Blaster Sights were commonly used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Rebel Alliance Special Forces|special forces]]. Common models included the [[Neuro-Saav Corp.|Neuro-Saav]] [["Deadeye" Sight]] and the [[Tana Ire]] [[y-30Z Targeting Beam]].<ref name="Forged in Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blaster sights were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Forged in Battle]]'',<ref name="Forged in Battle" /> a [[2016]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Soldier On!">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2016/8/17/soldier-on-1|text=Soldier On!}}</ref> The weapon attachment originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as [[blaster sight]]s, which were first mentioned in [[Cracken's Rebel Field Guide (sourcebook)|Cracken's Rebel Field Guide]]'', a supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' published by [[West End Games]] in [[June]] [[1991]].<ref name="Cracken">[[Cracken's Rebel Field Guide (sourcebook)|''Cracken's Rebel Field Guide'']]</ref>
