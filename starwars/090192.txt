'''090192''' was a [[spy ship]] used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. During the war, the [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid]] [[TX-532]] utilized the [[starship]] for a secret operation on [[Naboo]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
090192 was mentioned in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Rise" />
