'''Frander''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Lasat]] who operated a [[Frander's hidden depot|hidden depot]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. The [[Dank Graks]] tried to buy Frander's [[Spiral-class Viz-Core Eviscerator|Eviscerators]], but Frander didn't want to do business with [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Force-sensitive|users]].<ref name="THRA 3" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Powers and abilities==
Frander was a skilled mechanic, able to build an [[Spiral-class Viz-Core Eviscerator|Eviscerator]]. He was able to [[Force choke]] [[Saya Keem]].<ref name="THRA 3" />
