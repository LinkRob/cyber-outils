"'''Council Debate'''" was a short narrative included in ''[[The Last Command Sourcebook]]''. Styled as an in-universe recording of a [[New Republic Provisional Council]] meeting, it recounted a discussion between [[Sian Tevv/Legends|Sian Tevv]] and [[Gial Ackbar/Legends|Gial Ackbar]].
