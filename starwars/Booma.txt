'''Boomas''', also known as '''boomers''',<ref name="USW" /> '''energy balls''',<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> or '''Gungan grenades''',<ref name="Bounty Hunters 13">[[Bounty Hunters 13|''Bounty Hunters'' 13]]</ref> were spherical, [[grenade]]-like energy weapons used by the [[Gungan]]s. They were manufactured by the [[Otoh Gunga Defense League]] with the exotic [[plasma]]<ref name="USW" /> that was naturally present in the porous crust of [[Naboo]],<ref name="sweGunTech">{{SWE|technology|gungantech|Gungan weaponry}}</ref> They came in different sizes, and could be thrown by hand, sling, [[atlatl]], [[cesta]], or [[falumpaset]]-towed catapult.<ref name="USW" /> Boomas were used by the [[Gungan Grand Army]], notably during the [[Battle of Naboo]].<ref name="Episode I" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], they were sold at an [[Arms Market]] on [[Nar Shaddaa]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 13" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Boomers first appeared in the [[1999]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], the first installment in the [[prequel trilogy]] of ''[[Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Episode I" /> [[Industrial Light & Magic]] created them using the Sabre digital compositing system, which was also used to give the Gungan energy shield its glow, and illuminate the [[lightsaber]] blades of the [[Jedi]].<ref name="Original DB">{{DB|technology|gunganweaponry|Gungan weaponry}}</ref>
