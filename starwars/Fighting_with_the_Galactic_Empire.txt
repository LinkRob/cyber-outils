'''''Fighting with the Galactic Empire''''' was the memoir of [[Corf Ferno]], a former [[Vice Admiral]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] who was [[Prisoner|captured]] by the [[New Republic]] during the [[Defense of Kuat|Kuat Offensive]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Ferno published the memoir after his eventual release by the New Republic, although the information he gave in it differed notably from information he had provided during interrogation by his captors. After receiving access to the restricted interrogation reports on Ferno, the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] noted the discrepancies and suggested they ought to be investigated in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
''Fighting with the Galactic Empire'' was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
