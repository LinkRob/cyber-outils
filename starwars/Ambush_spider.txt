'''Ambush spiders''' were a type of giant [[spider]] that lived as part of the vertical ecosystem that existed in the continent-spanning [[Water|rain]] [[forest]]s and dense [[jungle]]s that grew in the equatorial latitudes of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Iakar]]. They lived below the [[kilometer]]-high canopies, where they were avoided by [[insect]]s and [[rodent]]s that lived off the waste of the canopy-dwelling [[Iakaru]] [[species]].<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ambush spiders were mentioned in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[2018]] supplement for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' system produced by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Dawn" />
