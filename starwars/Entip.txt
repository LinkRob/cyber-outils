'''Entip''' was a [[Jawa]] [[Merchant|trader]] who resided on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] and traveled in a [[sandcrawler]]. In [[3 ABY]] the Jawa exchanged a [[power injector system]] and a [[BT-P7 ion accelerator]] with the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] in return for various parts and [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].

==Biography==
Entip was a [[Jawa]] who worked as a [[Merchant|trader]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], traveling in a [[sandcrawler]] with the Jawa [[Teeka]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the vehicle moved back and forth between the edge of the [[Dune Sea]] and the outskirts of the [[city]] of [[Mos Eisley]], with Entip setting up shop outside at each location. The Jawa offered a range of materials and parts for exchange.<ref name="Outlaws" />

[[File:EntipSandcrawler-Outlaws.png|thumb|right|300px|Entip traveled in a sandcrawler.]]
This included a [[Daivak gunslinger shirt]] that they were willing to trade for fifteen units of [[bantha wool]] and ten [[direct-energy circuit]]s, a [[power injector system]] that they were willing to trade for a [[J8 terrain sensor]], nine units of [[transparisteel]], and three [[ion cell]]s, a [[X-FP-C28 ship computer]] that they were willing to trade for ten units of [[Bantha]] [[Hair|wool]] and twenty-five units of [[coarseweave]], five [[4XB calculation unit]]s that they were willing to trade for a Direct-Energy Circuit, a [[heuristic processor]], and an ion cell, ten units of [[Quadanium steel|quadanium]] tha they were willing to trade for a unit of coarseweave and a unit of [[polycarbonate]], and a [[BT-P7 ion accelerator]] that they were willing to trade for a [[fusion engine core]] and two thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] traded Zadiket the parts that the Jawa wanted for the Power Injector System and the BT-P7 Ion Accelerator, the latter of which she used to upgrade the range of the surge configuration of the Ion Module in her [[VM-19 heavy blaster pistol]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Entip appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The player must trade with the Jawa for the Fuel Injector System in order to unlock the optional "Adapt" ability and the BT-P7 Ion Accelerator to upgrade their blaster with the "Blast Range" upgrade for the surge configuration. Interacting with all five Jawa traders in the game is also required to unlock the "M'gasha" ability.<ref name="Outlaws" />
