'''''Bina's Guide to the Creatures of Batuu''''' was a guide booklet created by [[Bina]], the [[Amani]] proprietor of the [[Creature Stall]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]]. Her workers handed the guides out to [[youngling]]s under the age of ten [[Standard year|years]] free of charge.<ref name="Creature Stall">{{GEAttr|Creature Stall}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Creature Stall gift shop in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] opened on [[May 31]], [[2019]] in [[Disneyland]] Park and on [[August 29]] at [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]].<ref name="A to Z">{{D23|url=a-to-z/creature-stall|text=Disney A to Z: Creature Stall}}</ref> The booklet ''Bina's Guide to the Creatures of Batuu'' is free to children under 10 years old, and contains [[in-universe]] information and scavenger hunt activities within the theme park.<ref name="Creature Stall" /> Children complete the scavenger hunt by collecting stamps in their booklet from cast members at the hunt's locations, then return to the Creature Stall to receive a paper token.<ref name="WDWNT">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2020/02/photos-kids-can-earn-special-token-by-completing-batuu-scavenger-hunt-at-disneyland-resort|text=PHOTOS: New "Bina's Guide to the Creatures of Batuu" Scavenger Hunt Available in Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge at Disneyland|author=Dwight, Elise|date=2020-02-03|archiveurl=https://archive.is/CelO3}}</ref> The booklet is copyrighted, but has no further information about its real-world authors or designers.<ref name="Creature Stall" />
