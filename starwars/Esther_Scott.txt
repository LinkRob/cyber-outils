'''Esther Scott''' is an American actress. She has had regular spots in shows such as The Geena Davis Show, City Guys and The Tracy Morgan Show. She has guest starred in Ellen, Less Than Perfect and 7th Heaven. She lives in Los Angeles. She provided the voice for [[Shodu Warrick]] on [[Ewoks (television series)|Ewoks]].
