'''First Minister''' was a title used within the [[Coruscant Ministry of Ingress]],<ref name="Insider" /> an organization that handled the flow of immigrants into [[Coruscant]], [[capital]] [[planet]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="CL" /> The [[human]] [[Tannon Praji]] was serving as the First Minister of the Coruscant Ministry of Ingress<ref name="Insider" /> when Praji visited the<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> [[Galaxies Opera House]]<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|III}} to [[19 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A First Minister appeared in the the [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']],<ref name="ROTS" /> which was released on [[May 19]], [[2005]].<ref name="YbY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> The title itself was then first used in the "[[Pushing Boundaries]]," an interview with Knoll conducted by [[Brandon Wainerdi]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 217|''Star Wars Insider'' 217]]<ref name="Insider" /> on [[March 14]], [[2023]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=insider/subscriptions|text=Star Wars Insider Subscription Information}}</ref>
