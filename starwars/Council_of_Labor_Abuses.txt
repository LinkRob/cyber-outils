The '''Council of Labor Abuses''' was a body within the [[Imperial Senate]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. At some point prior to [[10 BBY]], the [[Besalisk]] [[Dexter Jettster]] learned of an [[Crimson Dawn ore hub|ore hub]] being run by the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate on [[Athus Klee]], and traveled in the world in order to investigate. He managed to capture several surveillance images of the hub and its workers, who were forced to undergo [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] enhancement to improve their efficiency.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

After rescuing [[Unidentified Rodian cyborg|one]] of the workers, Jettster then contacted a [[Unidentified politician (Dexter Jettster)|friend]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], who sent someone to pick up the rescued worker and surveillance data from Jettster with the aim of taking the data before the Council of Labor Abuses. Jettster later wrote about the incident in the [[book]] that would eventually become known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'', and admitted that although he did not expect his actions to lead to the shutting down of the Crimson Dawn, he hoped that it might at least result in someone putting a stop to the cybernetic work being done on the hub's workers.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Council of Labor Abuses was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
