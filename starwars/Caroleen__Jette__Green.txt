'''Caroleen "Jette" Green''' is a matte painter who worked on the [[2024]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]''. At the request of [[John Knoll]], Greene created the image depicting the [[Barrier]] above the [[planet]] [[At Attin]]. Greene's painting was made on specialized [[Wikipedia:Masonite|Masonite]].<ref name="Paintings and Models" />
