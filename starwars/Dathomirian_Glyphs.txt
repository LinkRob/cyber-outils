'''Dathomirian Glyphs''', also called '''nightbrother runes''', were a style of glyphs created by the [[Nightbrothers]], a group of [[Gender|male]] [[Dathomirian]] [[Soldier|warriors]] subservient to the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Dathomiri clan|clan]] of [[witch]]es from the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]]. They were formed through the use of the [[acid]]s of the [[hydraatis]] [[plant]] to burn permanent [[Color|white]] marks into rockfaces, a process which was both difficult and dangerous. Thus, Nightbrother [[Artist|caligraphers]] were few in number, but highly valued members of their communities.<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>
