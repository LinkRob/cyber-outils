'''Dart Subterra''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who worked as a [[contract broker]] for the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. He was the boyfriend of [[Jadd Flaxum]], a [[spy]] who also worked for Crimson Dawn. Subterra hired the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] to rescue Flaxum from a [[Pyke Syndicate]] [[starship]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
