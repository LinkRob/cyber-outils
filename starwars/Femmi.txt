One [[Standard day|night]] in [[5 BBY]], '''Femmi''' and [[Jezzi]] bought medicine for the elderly [[human]] [[Maarva Carassi Andor|Maarva Andor]] and [[Foodstuff|supper]] to the [[Andor household]] on the<ref name="Andor1" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Nobody's Listening!">{{Andor|Nobody's Listening!}}</ref> [[Ferrix]]. [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], Maarva's adopted son, had been out when Femmi and Jezzi visited, and the next day he questioned the family [[droid]] [[B2EMO]] as to who had visited while he was out. The droid then mentioned that Femmi had visited with Jezzi.<ref name="Andor1" />
