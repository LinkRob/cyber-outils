The '''Aethersprite''' was a body configuration equipped by the [[droid]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[J-3DI]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Noble Jedi|standard configuration]]. It was designed in a way that was meant to invoke the aesthetics of the [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor]]s used by [[Jedi|members]] of the [[Jedi Order]] shortly before its collapse.<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aethersprite was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter J-3DI, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Aethersprite Saber Design]].<ref name="Hunters" /> It was initially planned to be called "Open Circle."<ref name="Hunters Panel">{{SW|url=news/swca-2022-star-wars-hunters-panel|text=SWCA 2022: 7 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: Hunters'' Panel}}</ref>
