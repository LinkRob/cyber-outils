A '''blaster recirculator''', was a high capacity component that used to transfer gas in [[blaster]]s. It could be used to modify the rate of fire and power of a [[VM-19 heavy blaster pistol]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blaster recirculators first appeared as weapon upgrade components in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' that was published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
