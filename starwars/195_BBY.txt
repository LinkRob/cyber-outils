==Behind the scenes==
Events taking place in the year 195 BBY appear only in the non-[[canon]]ical<ref name="interview">[[Wookieepedia:Interview/Jeffrey Brown|Interview with Jeffrey Brown]]</ref> novel ''[[Jedi Academy: Return of the Padawan]]''.<ref name="Date">[[Jedi Academy (novel)|''Jedi Academy'']] states that [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]], who was born in [[896 BBY/Legends|896 BBY]] according to ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'', is 700 [[Standard year/Legends|years]] old. Therefore, ''Jedi Academy'' takes place in [[196 BBY]], and ''[[Jedi Academy: Return of the Padawan]]'', which takes place one year later, takes place in 195 BBY.</ref>

''[[Kyle Katarn's Tale]], Mission 4'' states that the 8t88 was "nearly 200-year-old,"<ref name="Wizards" /> by the time of the [[Mission to Sulon]] seen in [[Star Wars: Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II|''Jedi Knight: Dark Forces II'']] and its novelization, ''[[Dark Forces: Rebel Agent]]''; ''[[The Essential Chronology]]'' places these events in [[5 ABY/Legends|5 ABY]], therefore this puts the droid's creation around 195 BBY.
