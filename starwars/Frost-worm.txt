'''Frost-worms''' were a type of [[worm]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a member of the [[Rokana Raiders]] group of [[pirate]]s called several crewmates frost-worms while summoning them over to an entryway in the [[Khepi Tomb]] in the [[Khepi system]] that the group's [[captain]], [[Stinger Tash]], had recently blown open.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Frost-worms were mentioned in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune|text=Steal a Look at ''Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune'', Available May 15}}</ref>
