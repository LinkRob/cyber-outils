"'''G-LN'''" is the sixth animated short that is part of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the TV series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''. It was released on the Disney Channel's YouTube channel on [[December 17]], [[2018]].

==Official description==
''There's a reason why Cantinas don't serve droids!''<ref name="G-LN" />

==Plot summary==
At [[Aunt Z's Tavern]], [[Flix]] and [[Orka]] are having drinks and snacks, accompanied by their [[DUM-series pit droid|pit droid]], [[G-LN]]. Noting that the [[droid]] looks a little down, Orka pours the rest of his [[blurrgfire]] into G-LN's bowl. As [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza|Aunt Z]] comes up to talk to Flix and Orka, G-LN downs the alcohol and gets somewhat hyper. Unnoticed by his masters or the bartender, G-LN goes running through the tavern, attacking and earning the ire of two [[service droid]]s, including [[G1-7CH|Glitch]], as well as [[Klatooinian]] [[Bolza Grool]]. As Aunt Z, Orka and Flix discuss the fact that most people in [[the galaxy]] don't notice things going on around them, Grool and the two droids gang up on and knock out G-LN. When Orka and Flix decide to look around for their droid, they see him falling onto the floor, with his assailants gone, and decide the blurrgfire didn't agree with him. Orka calls for a refill.
