The '''Coruscant underworld police''' ('''CUP''') or '''Coruscant underground police''', also simply '''underworld cops''', was a [[special forces]] [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement section]] of the [[Coruscant Security Force]] patrolling the [[Coruscant Underworld|lower levels]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] [[capital]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. Underworld [[police officer]]s were notoriously hardened and burly in comparison with the sleek [[GU-series Guardian police droid]]s that maintained law and order in the upper reaches of [[Imperial City|Galactic City]]. The underworld police were also known for their heavy [[leather]] and [[metal]] [[armor]] with mechanical [[eye]] coverings, which gave them extra protection and anonymity, while denoting an authoritative presence. Coruscant cops patrolled both on foot and in [[Panther police interceptor|police speeders]] and with the advent of the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], the underworld police also utilized the [[LAAT/le patrol gunship|patrol transport]] when needed.

When the fugitive [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] was lost by pursuing [[clone trooper]] forces, the Coruscant Security Force was alerted that she had last been seen heading into the lower levels. Coruscant cops in the underworld were notified and given wanted posters and descriptions of Tano. Several underworld police officers spotted her at a train station and gave pursuit but she managed to escape them. Later, two underworld officers noticed an explosion at a warehouse where Tano was and, reporting the anomaly, it lead to the [[Capture of Ahsoka Tano|capture]] of the wanted padawan. After the formation of the [[Galactic Empire]], the Coruscant Security Force was bolstered by the addition of Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s to maintain order in the lower levels. Stormtroopers would often patrol with the underworld police officers and both were used by the Empire to violently put down revolts and enforce Imperial re-location or detention policies of undesirable [[alien]]s and suspected [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] sympathizers.

==Description==
{{Quote|She's armed and dangerous. Alert all units.|An underworld police officer, discussing Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="TCWCatchJedi" />}}
[[File:Coruscant heist.jpg|thumb|222x222px|Officers of the underworld police in pursuit of an outlaw]]
The '''Coruscant underworld police'''<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> or '''Coruscant underground police''',<ref name="TCAJepigui">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/to-catch-a-jedi-episode-guide|text="To Catch a Jedi" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> abbreviated as '''CUP''',<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> and also known as '''underworld cops''',<ref name="TCAJepigui" /> [[special forces]] [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement section]] of [[police officer]]s of the [[Coruscant Security Force]] patrolling the [[Coruscant Underworld|Underworld]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] [[capital]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. [[Homeworld|Natives]] of the planet from unknown [[species]],<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> they stood in contrast to the polished [[GU-series Guardian police droid]]s that were primarily charged with law enforcement in the upper levels of [[Imperial City|Galactic City]]. Coruscant cops in the underworld were known to be burly and hardened officers as they worked in the cutthroat environment of the planet's lower levels. They wore heavy [[leather]] and [[metal]] [[armor]], with their heads completely covered, and mechanical [[eye]] coverings to protect them from the many dangers they encountered from the criminal elements of the underworld. "PD" was written on their helmets and their backs in yellow [[Aurebesh]] [[Writing|letters]]. The armor gave them a presence of anonymity and authority, and had the added effect of giving them a mystic reputation,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> leading to locals being unsure if they were [[humanoid]], [[droid]]s, or something else, with few people having the nerves to get close enough to find out.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> They were considered to be courageous, loyal, relentless and unforgettable. Ranks among the Coruscant Underworld Police included Police [[Sergeant]], Police [[Lieutenant]] and Police [[Captain]].<ref name="GoH" />

The tool kit of Coruscant cops was considered as "memorable".<ref name="GoH" /> They were armed with multiple different models of [[heavy blaster pistol]]s,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /><ref name="GoH" /> including [[Coruscant Security Droid Blaster/Canon|Coruscant Security Droid Blaster]]<ref name="EpgTCAJ">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep519|text="To Catch a Jedi" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide|nolive=1}}</ref> and [[DL-44 heavy blaster pistol]]s,<ref name="OTLD" /> and a model of red-glowing non-lethal [[grenade]]s for crowd control.<ref name="GoH" /> They utilized [[LAAT/le patrol gunship|LAAT/le patrol gunship]]s<ref name="Republicpolicegunship">{{SWE|technology|republic-police-gunship|Republic police gunship}}</ref> and the [[Panther police interceptor]]s for patrol and pursuit.<ref name="TCWCatchJedi" /> Known to go through a vigorous training program, a black market version of the [[Coruscant underworld police recruitment test|Coruscant underworld police test]] was illegally disseminated throughout [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Twilight Company">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> The underworld police were trained in armed and [[unarmed combat]], as well as tracking, security, interrogations and [[speeder]] [[pilot]]ing.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==History==
{{Quote|Looks like the Jedi are after one of their own.|An underworld police officer, discussing Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="TCWCatchJedi" />}}
[[File:RealStoriesOfTheCoruscantHighwayPatrol-TCaJ.png|thumb|left|280px|Coruscant cops in the underworld]]
During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] was arrested by the [[Coruscant Guard]] for the murder of [[Letta Turmond]], the suspected perpetrator behind the [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|bombing]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] hangar. However, Tano managed to [[Escape from the Republic military base|escape]] unknowingly with the assistance of rogue Jedi [[Barriss Offee]], who had in truth killed Turmond and was also responsible for the bombing.<ref name="TJWKTM">{{TCW|The Jedi Who Knew Too Much}}</ref>

During the escape, several Coruscant Guards were killed by a lightsaber leading the pursuing troopers to believe it was Tano.<ref name="TJWKTM" /> In response to this, the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] ordered Tano's arrest. The Coruscant Security Force was told that she had been last seen [[Capture of Ahsoka Tano|fleeing]] to the lower levels, and so the underworld police officers were put on high alert. As the underworld cops exchanged wanted posters with Tano's image, she managed to temporarily avoid notice by using a cloak to hide her identity. However, at one point two officers boarded a train that Tano was on and they began checking IDs of the passengers. As Tano attempted to leave the train at the next station, she ran into three more officers on the platform. The cops identified Tano and moved in to arrest her. Tano pushed the officers away using the Force, throwing one back onto the platform. He signaled ahead to other units as the train left the station and the remaining four officers gave chase.<ref name="TCWCatchJedi" />

Dodging passengers, Tano fled through the train cars from the pursuing underworld cops. A passenger attempted to hold her and the police officers managed to briefly overpower Tano. However, she fought them off during the scuffle and opened a door of the train. She then jumped onto the adjacent platform as the train passed and, believing herself safe, Tano began walking away. Three officers standing on the platform noticed her and ordered her to stop. Tano fled from the station and towards an open [[turbolift]] door. The cops, seeing they were about to loose their quarry, opened fire on her with their blasters, but Tano had already entered a lift by that time had escaped the officers' shots. The search continued and shortly thereafter, two police officers on [[Level 1315]] spotted an explosion at a warehouse. They gave an alert to a search group composed of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Plo Koon]], and Coruscant Guard [[Imperial shock trooper|shock troopers]], all of whom responded and were able to capture Tano at the warehouse.<ref name="TCWCatchJedi" /> Tano was placed on [[Trial of Ahsoka Tano|trial]] but vindicated by Skywalker who discovered the true traitor was Barriss Offee.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref>

After the Republic was reorganized into the [[Galactic Empire]], the Coruscant underworld police continued its work assisting the new government.<ref name="OTLD" />

[[File:CalPrisoner-SWJS.png|thumb|right|250px|Disguised as members of the Underworld Police, members of the Mantis crew escort Jedi Knight Cal Kestis.]]
During a [[mission on Coruscant]]<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> in [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Survivor date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> members of the [[Stinger Mantis crew|''Stinger Mantis'' crew]] disguised themselves as members of the Coruscant underworld police and escorted [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] to Imperial [[Senator]] [[Daho Sejan]]. Kestis pretended to be captured so that his allies would take him to Sejan and his [[starship]], which contained Imperial military information that they intended to deliver to [[Saw Gerrera]] and his [[Partisans]].<ref name="Survivor" />

Aftet the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] was formed to [[Galactic Civil War|fight]] to restore the Republic's ideals, Imperial forces were sent into the lower levels of Coruscant to enforce the relocation of species that had joined the Alliance such as the [[Mon Calamari]]. The Coruscant Security underworld officers assisted the Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s with the relocation efforts, specifically in the [[Old Market Sector]], however, the Mon Calamari resisted. The resulting [[Old Market Sector riots|riots]] were brutally put down by the underworld police and stormtroopers.<ref name="OTLD" />

[[File:UnderworldCop_Empire.png|left|thumb|A police officer during the time of the Empire.]]
Years later, after the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of the planet [[Alderaan]] by the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], rumors of what had occurred began reaching the [[Alderaanian]] population, most of whom lived on [[Level 3204]]. Vigils were held which soon became heated and angry when images confirming Alderaan had been destroyed followed the rumors. The vigils turned into [[Alderaan vigil rioting|rioting]] which lasted for several days until the Coruscant police and the Imperial stormtroopers were sent in to restore order. As the troopers and underworld officers marched into the streets, the stormtroopers opened fire with their blasters. One of the casualties was [[Reffe]] who died during his tirade against the Empire. The crowds, frightened, dispersed. The following day, the underworld police arrested anyone caught out in the streets. The day after that, stormtroopers began going door to door stating that Rebel spies had recruited local residents and Alderaanians had to be taken in for questioning.<ref name="OTLD" />

After the relocation of the Alderaanians, an [[Unidentified Coruscant underworld police officer|underworld police officer]] was on patrol with a [[Unidentified stormtrooper (Level 1997)|stormtrooper]] when they entered a [[Unidentified cantina (Level 1997)|cantina]] on level [[Level 1997]]. Recognizing two Alderaanian juveniles, [[Santigo Milon|Santiago]] and [[Anandra Milon]], from his facial recognition display, the stormtrooper gave chase on his own, leaving the underworld cop behind. The stormtrooper was able to catch up with Anandra and attempted to arrest her. However, during the struggle, a [[Unidentified Herglic criminal|Herglic]] came to Anandra's rescue, overpowered the Stormtrooper and threw him over the level's railing, killing him.<ref name="OTLD" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Concept_underworld_police.png|thumb|right|250px|Concept art by Darren Marshall of a Coruscant police officer for ''The Clone Wars'']]
The Coruscant underworld police first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, in the episode the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|Season Five]] episode "[[To Catch a Jedi]]", which aired on [[February 24]], [[2013]]. They were specifically conceived by [[George Lucas]] to add to the environment of the underworld, opposed to the droids seen earlier in the series.<ref name="TCAJepigui" /> Character illustrations of the model of them for the episode were done by [[Darren Marshall]]<ref name="TCAJ Concept">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/to-catch-a-jedi-concept-art-gallery|text="To Catch a Jedi" Concept Art Gallery}}</ref> and were completed on [[September 13]], [[2011]]. The model was notably titled "Coruscant Security Droid" and featured a head of a feminine [[human]].<ref name="Marshall">{{WebCite|url=https://dmarshallart.com/portfolio/the-clone-wars-season-5/|text=The Clone Wars – Season 5|work=[[Darren Marshall]]'s website|archivedate=20260123054301}}</ref> Their blaster pistols were sourced from  [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]]'s [[blaster]].<ref name="EpgTCAJ">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep519|text="To Catch a Jedi" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide|nolive=1}}</ref>

A concept image of the [[Coruscant Underworld Police|Coruscant underworld police]] officers was also drawn by [[Kinman Chan]] for<ref name="Art Concept">''[[Star Wars Art: Concept]]''</ref> the cancelled [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: 1313]]'' that was in development from [[LucasArts]] around the same time.<ref name="kotaku">{{WebCite|url=https://kotaku.com/disney-shuts-down-lucasarts-468473749|text=Disney Shuts Down LucasArts, Cancels Star Wars 1313 And Star Wars: First Assault|author=Schreier, Jason|date=2013-04-03|archivedate=20200301083454}}</ref> While the signature on the image describes them as "[[InterGalactic Banking Clan/Legends|Banking Clan]] Guards" the book ''[[Star Wars Art: Concept]]'' identifies them as Coruscant underworld police.<ref name="Art Concept" />

They later re-appeared in the [[2023]] video game ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]].<ref name="Survivor" /> [[Theo Stylianides]] explained that they needed disguises for the infiltration crew and looked at the animated series. Concept art for their appearance was done by [[Jordan Lamarre-Wan]] and Stylianides, who attempted to de-stylize their design from ''The Clone Wars'' and make it more realistic and closer to live-action, while still keeping it recognizable.<ref name="Art of JS">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>

An officer of the Coruscant Underworld Police appears as a playable character in the mobile video game ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'', developed by [[EA Capital Games]], where they are identified as "CUP" by bundles appearing in the store. The "CUP Lightspeed Bundle" describe them as "the legend" and a portrait and title given as login rewards for [[May the Fourth]], 2025 describe them as "[[Fey's Cantina|The Holotable]]'s Guardian" and an "Underworld Legend".<ref name="GoH" />
