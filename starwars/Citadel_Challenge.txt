The '''Citadel Challenge''' was an exercise that was held in the [[Tipoca City Training Facility|training facility]] of the [[Tipoca City Military Complex|military complex]] in [[Tipoca City]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]].

==Description==
[[File:Training_Room_Kamino_AtG.png|thumb|left|300px|Clone cadets taking part in the Citadel Challenge]]
The Citadel Challenge was a capture-the-flag type exercise. [[Clone cadet|Cadets]] had to make it through guard towers and standard infantry [[droid]]s, climb the hill, and pick up the pole which disabled all the [[B1-series battle droid|droids]] and laser cannons. There were many forms of cover, such as walls and crates. The laser cannons were set to stun.<ref name="CC" /> It was was named after the deadly [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[prison]], the [[Citadel]].<ref name="CR Trivia">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/citadel-rescue-trivia-gallery|text="Citadel Rescue" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref>

==History==
The Citadel Challenge was often used to train cadet [[squad]]s, such as [[Domino Squad]] and [[Bravo Squad (clone cadets)|Bravo Squad]], to see if they were ready to be sent into battle or not. When Domino Squad attempted the challenge, they were disorganized and initially failed, however, after learning to work together, the clones managed to pass the test and [[Graduation medal|graduated]].<ref name="CC" />

Until his death during the [[Battle of Kamino]],<ref name="ARC Troopers">{{TCW|ARC Troopers (episode)}}</ref> the malformed [[Maintenance duty clone|maintenance clone]] [[Clone 99|99]] served as [[janitor]] for the room that housed the challenge.<ref name="CC" />
