'''Del Goren''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] member of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] who served as a communications and sensors expert in [[Alliance High Command]]. In [[1 BBY]], he was in the [[Yavin rebel base war room|war room]] of the [[Great Temple|rebel headquarters]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]] during the [[Battle of Yavin]], in which the rebels attacked the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]].

Goren watched the battle on a display system and alerted the rebel [[starfighter]]s when the war room detected [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s preparing to attack them. Ultimately, the rebels successfully destroyed the [[Death Star]].

==Biography==
===War room controller===
[[File:GorenOrgana3PO-WarRoom-ANH.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Del Goren leans over a tactical display table beside Princess Leia Organa and C-3PO in the Great Temple control room on Yavin 4.|Del Goren was in the Yavin 4 war room during the Battle of Yavin.]]
Del Goren was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] communications and [[sensor]] expert<ref name="Force Collection" /> who served as a [[Alliance officer|control officer]]<ref name="Paperback">[[Star Wars: A New Hope (paperback novel)|''Star Wars: A New Hope'' junior novelization]]</ref> for the [[Alliance High Command|High Command]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]<ref name="Force Collection" /> during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Part of<ref name="ANH" /> the [[Massassi Group]] based in the Alliance's [[Great Temple]] headquarters on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]],<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> Goren was present at the temple during the preparations for the Alliance's [[Battle of Yavin|attack]] on the Imperial [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]]<ref name="ANH" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>

As the thirty rebel [[starfighter]]s taking part in the attack launched, Goren rushed into the base's<ref name="ANH" /> [[Yavin rebel base war room|war room]]<ref name="AD">{{Film|IV}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> with [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]]. As the trio began watching the battle on<ref name="ANH" /> a massive display system,<ref name="AD" /> an announcement was made that the [[Death Star]] would be within range to fire on the Yavin base in fifteen [[Standard minute|minutes]].<ref name="ANH" />

===Incoming fighters===
With less than seven minutes left until the Death Star was within range, Goren moved over to some of the war room's display screens and announced to the starfighter [[squad leader]]s that the base had picked up the signals of Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s preparing to attack them.<ref name="ANH" /> [[Red Leader]] [[Garven Dreis]] then called out the [[TIE series|TIEs]] at point four to the rebel [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]], with Goren repeating the information over comms afterward and asking for an update, although [[Red Five]] [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] [[Luke Skywalker]] was only vaguely aware of the control officer's voice.<ref name="PSF">''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]''</ref>

[[File:GorenConfused-ANH.png|thumb|left|300px|alt=Del Goren turns away from a tactical display table beside Princess Leia Organa and C-3PO in the Great Temple control room on Yavin 4.|Del Goren was confused when Luke Skywalker switched off his targeting computer.]]
After the rebel and [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] engaged, Goren was again at the display system when members of the rebel [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]] began their attack run on the Death Star. He moved away from the display as the [[pilot]]s tried to estimate the number of enemy guns they would encounter, only five minutes before the Death Star would be in range to fire.<ref name="ANH" />

Both that attack run and the next failed, and with only thirty [[second]]s before the Death Star was within range, Goren and Organa approached the display together again as the rebel pilot [[Biggs Darklighter]] [[Death|perished]] during a final attack run. Skywalker, the only remaining pilot on the run, switched off his [[targeting computer]], causing Goren to turn away from the display system and look at the screens in confusion. Using [[the Force]] instead of the [[computer]], Skywalker then successfully completed the attack run and destroyed the Death Star just as it began its firing sequence.<ref name="ANH" /> The rebels completely [[Evacuation of Yavin 4|evacuated]] their [[Alliance Military|forces]] from Yavin 4 soon after the battle.<ref name="Rise">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Rise of the Rebellion and the Battle of Yavin}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Del Goren had light skin, [[Color|brown]] [[hair]], and brown [[eye]]s.<ref name="ANH" />

==Equipment==
While in the war room, Goren wore a tan [[Clothing|shirt]] with brown pants and a [[headset]].<ref name="ANH" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Squad leaders, ''enemy fighters'' coming your way.|Del Goren's altered dialogue in the graphic novel|<ref name="Graphic">''[[Star Wars: The Original Trilogy – A Graphic Novel]]''</ref>}}
[[File:IntoTheTuckerverse.png|thumb|right|400px|alt=Side-by-side images of Burnell Tucker as Del Goren, Wyron Serper, and Romas Navander.|Burnell Tucker played three rebel officer roles across two ''Star Wars'' films: Del Goren (left), Wyron Serper (center), and Romas Navander (right).]] 
Del Goren first appeared in the [[1977]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="ANH" /> The character was identified only as "control officer" in the script<ref name="BTM">''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]''</ref> and was portrayed by [[Burnell Tucker]],<ref name="Tucker">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodmemorabilia.com/en/star-wars/burnell-tucker-star-wars-autographed-1996-star-wars-ccg-number-100-bas-authenticated-card-with-best-wishes-inscription/t-24061638+p-342245908180674+z-9-2953284177|text=Burnell Tucker Star Wars Autographed 1996 Star Wars CCG #100 BAS Authenticated Card with "Best Wishes" Inscription|work=[https://www.hollywoodmemorabilia.com/en/ Hollywood Memorabilia]|archivedate=20230617112514}}</ref> who went on to play two more rebel [[officer]]s in the [[1980]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']];<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> [[Wyron Serper]]<ref name="Serper">[[Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back (paperback novel)|''Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back'' junior novelization]]</ref> and [[Romas Navander]].<ref name="Navander">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/3/8/in-the-trenches|text=In the Trenches}}</ref> Goren's scenes were filmed on H Stage at [[Shepperton Studios]] between [[May 13]] and [[May 18]], [[1976]].<ref name="MakingOf">''[[The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film]]''</ref>

In ''[[Star Wars: The Original Trilogy – A Graphic Novel]]'', a [[2016]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] adaptation of the film written by [[Alessandro Ferrari]], Goren's line of dialogue is shortened.<ref name="Graphic" /> The [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]], includes a partial transcript from the Great Temple war room in which a [[Unidentified control officer|control officer]] announces the arrival of the Death Star in the [[Yavin system]] prior to the Battle of Yavin. It is unclear if this control officer is intended to be Goren or if multiple characters held that role during that [[time]].<ref name="RebelFiles">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Goren's name was used in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile card game]] ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'',<ref name="Force Collection" /> which initially launched in [[2013]]. Although the launch of ''Force Collection'' predates<ref name="Force Collection release">{{SW|url=news/announcing-star-wars-force-collection|text=Announcing Star Wars: Force Collection|nolive=1}}</ref> the ''Star Wars'' canon reset of [[2014]],<ref name="Legends">{{SW|url=news/the-legendary-star-wars-expanded-universe-turns-a-new-page|text=The Legendary Star Wars Expanded Universe Turns a New Page}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Leland Chee]] confirmed that the game was kept updated to fit with canon.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|holocronkeeper|status/846807700482666496|[[Leland Chee]]|quote=I'm not gonna go through the trouble of changing them and I don't have any reason to believe currently that anyone would. [&hellip;] On the off-chance that we DID have a current need to change any of these, we would have for Force Collection.|archivedate=20210505080831}} &mdash; [[Leland Chee]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] has confirmed that [[in-universe]] information from ''[[Star Wars: Force Collection]]'' conforms with ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]].</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, Goren appeared prior to the release of the film in its [[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|novelization]],<ref name="Novelization">[[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|''Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope'' novelization]]</ref> which was written by [[Alan Dean Foster]] and released on [[November 12]], 1976.<ref name="StarWars.com">{{SW|url=news/check-out-over-40-years-of-star-wars-a-new-hope-novelization-covers|text=Check Out Over 40 Years of ''Star Wars: A New Hope'' Novelization Covers}}</ref> The character's name also originated in Legends, where it was first used on a card in the [[1996]] "[[A New Hope Limited]]" set made for the [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game|''Star Wars'' Customizable Card Game]] produced by [[Decipher]]<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=A New Hope Limited|cardname=Rebel Tech}}</ref>
