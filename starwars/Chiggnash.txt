The '''Chiggnash''' were a [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Species/Legends|species]] that resembled [[Scorpion/Legends|scorpions]]. The species had the ability to [[Control Mind (Chiggnash)|control the minds]] of other beings, and sometimes used that faculty to rapidly accumulate an expendable force of "[[warrior drone]]s." At least some Chiggnash formed criminal extortion rings. Among [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunters]], it was acknowledged that due to their control mind ability, special tactics were required to confront Chiggnash. For instance, an electrified [[smart-harpoon]] was an inappropriate weapon as it would soon short out against so many warrior drones. Similarly, a [[Blaster/Legends|blaster]] would be of only temporary use against their hordes.<ref name="Predators of the Empire" />

The Chiggnash criminals [[Riley (Chiggnash)|Riley]] and [[Banda (Chiggnash)|Banda]] were active on [[Nar Shaddaa/Legends|Nar Shaddaa]], the [[Smuggler/Legends|smuggler's]] [[Moon/Legends|moon]] of [[Nal Hutta/Legends|Nal Hutta]], shortly after the [[Declaration of a New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Wolf">{{StoryCite|story=Lone Wolf: A Tale of Obi-Wan and Luke|book=SkyeWalkers: A Clone Wars Story}}</ref> In [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[Lone Wolf: A Tale of Obi-Wan and Luke]]" occur during those of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], which ''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]'' dates to [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]].</ref> they worked with [[Pacithhip/Legends|Pacithhip]] [[Hangar/Legends|docking bay]] attendant [[Shnozitskigante-maximilliana]] in an attempt to rob the incognito [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. Confronting Kenobi with [[Squib/Legends|Squib]] [[tensor rifle]]s and a force of warrior drones, the Chiggnash were instead cut down by the [[Dark Jedi/Legends|Dark Jedi]] [[Mei (Dark Jedi)|Mei]].<ref name="Wolf" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[Brian Daley]] created the Chiggnash for his article "[[Predators of the Empire]]," published in [[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 6|''Star Wars Galaxy Magazine'' 6]] in [[1996]]. The article details the origins and cultural influences of [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunters]] in ''[[Star Wars]]'', and is written in a style that intermingles [[in-universe]] and out-of-universe perspectives. The reference to the Chiggnash appears to be in-universe, and this article has treated it as such.
