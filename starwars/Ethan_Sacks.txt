'''Ethan Sacks''' is a comic book writer who wrote "[[The Weapon]]," which was published by [[Marvel Comics]] in the [[2019]] issue [[Age of Republic Special 1|''Age of Republic Special'' 1]].<ref name="The Weapon" /> Sacks also wrote the comic book miniseries [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge (comic series)|''Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge'']]<ref name="Galaxy's Edge">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-galaxys-edge-comic-exclusive|text=Marvel Journeys to Batuu in New ''Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge'' Comic &ndash; Exclusive}}</ref> and the comic book miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Allegiance]]''.<ref name="SDCC 2019">{{WebCite|url=https://www.comicsbeat.com/sdcc-19-marvel-retailer-lunch-reveals|text=SDCC '19: Marvel unveils DOCTOR DOOM ongoing, STAR WARS: THE RISE OF SKYWALKER prelude series, more|author=Grunenwald, Joe|date=2019-07-19|archivedate=20191013205432}}</ref>
