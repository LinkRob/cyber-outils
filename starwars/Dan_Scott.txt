'''Dan Scott''' is an artist who has created several ''Star Wars'' covers for [[Dark Horse Comics]]. He has also done work for [[Wikipedia:DC Comics|DC Comics]], [[Wikipedia:Magic: The Gathering|Magic: The Gathering]], [[Wikipedia:Warhammer Fantasy Battle|Warhammer]], and [[Wikipedia:World of Warcraft|World of Warcraft]].

Scott creates his paintings digitally in [[Wikipedia:Corel Painter|Painter]] and [[Wikipedia:Adobe Photoshop|Photoshop]].
