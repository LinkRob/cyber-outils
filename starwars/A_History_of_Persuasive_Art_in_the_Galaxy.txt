'''''A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy''''' was a [[book]] released shortly after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]] in [[34 ABY]] that looked at the use of [[propaganda]] by various factions and companies during the final [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s rule, the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Civil War]] and the [[Cold War]]. The [[artist]] [[Janyor]] of [[Bith (planet)|Bith]] provided the foreword for the book.<ref name="Prop" />
