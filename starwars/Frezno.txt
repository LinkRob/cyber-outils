'''Frezno''' was an [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[moon]] with makeshift encampments for villages. [[Doctor]] [[Quadpaw]] operated in a [[tent]] on the moon, and was the activated contingency plan for a [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team|rebel infiltration team]] that did a [[Aldhani heist|heist]] on the nearby [[planet]] [[Aldhani]] in [[5 BBY]]. Quadpaw attempted to save the injured rebel team member [[Karis Nemik]], but was [[Death|unsuccessful]] in his efforts. Meanwhile, another member of the team, [[Arvel Skeen]], tried to convince his teammate [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] to flee with the team's [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], but Andor shot him. Afterward, the rebel held Quadpaw and his team leader [[Vel Sartha]] at gunpoint, explaining the situation before leaving Frezno with his arranged cut from the heist.

==Description==
[[File:Aldhani-Frezno-CLNE.png|thumb|right|300px|Frezno was located in the Outer Rim near Aldhani]]
Frezno<ref name="Databank" /> was a terrestrial [[moon]]<ref name="Eye" /> located in the [[Frezno system]] within an intersecting portion of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and the [[New Territories]] regions.<ref name="New Territories" /> Situated in the forgotten<ref name="Databank" /> [[Cademimu sector]] at the coordinates M-6 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Systems" /> it [[orbit]]ed a [[planet]] that dominated the moon's sky.<ref name="Eye" /> With a Type I [[atmosphere]]<ref name="Atmosphere" /> that was breathable to [[human]]s and other [[species]],<ref name="Eye" /> Frezno featured tall and foggy grasslands.<ref name="SWE" />

==History==
{{Quote|I'm taking my cut. The number I was promised. I'm leaving you the freighter and what's inside. I did my job. I'm done. And I wouldn't stick around if I was you.|Cassian Andor, to Vel Sartha|<ref name="Eye" />}}
[[File:QuadpawOperates.png|thumb|left|300px|Quadpaw tries to save Karis Nemik]]
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Jillsarian]] [[Doctor]] [[Quadpaw]] ran surgery in a [[tent]] on Frezno,<ref name="SWE" /> tending to desperate patients with nowhere else to go and [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to pay. He<ref name="Quadpaw DB">{{Databank|doctor-quadpaw|Doctor Quadpaw}}</ref> was in contact with a [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team|rebel infiltration team]] that had him as a contingency plan in their planned [[Aldhani heist|heist]] on<ref name="Eye" /> the nearby<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|frezno|Frezno}}</ref> planet [[Aldhani]]<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> in [[5 BBY]].<ref name="5 BBY">{{AndorDate|106}}</ref> While the heist was a success, the contingency indeed came into play when one of the rebels in the team, [[Karis Nemik]], became seriously injured, and his teammate [[Arvel Skeen]] convinced the other survivors to take him to the Quadpaw.<ref name="Eye" />

The rebel team arrived on Frezno in a stolen [[Max-7 Rono box freighter]] carrying the spoils of their heist: an Imperial payroll of around eighty million credits. In his operating theatre, Quadpaw began operating on the injured rebel, assisted by the team leader [[Vel Sartha]]. Meanwhile, the other two rebels, Skeen and [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]], sat outside, speaking about whether Nemik would make it. Skeen quickly pivoted into offering Andor a chance to flee with the credits alongside him to an [[Unidentified moon (Arvel Skeen)|uninhabited moon]] that was eight [[parsec]]s away. The pair discussed each other's intentions, but as Skeen continued to detail his plan, Andor [[Death|fatally]] shot him.<ref name="Eye" />

[[File:QuadpawBlasterpoint.png|thumb|right|300px|Cassian Andor holes Quadpaw and Vel Sartha at blasterpoint]]
Meanwhile, Quadpaw was unable to save Nemik, who passed away on the operating table. As the surgery concluded, Andor entered the operating theatre with his [[Clem's blaster pistol|blaster]] drawn. Andor explained what had happened with Skeen, which Sartha struggled to believe. He then announced that he was leaving with his arranged cut of the stolen credits and Quadpaw's [[starship]], which he offered twice the value of for. Before Andor left, Sartha made him take [[The Trail of Political Consciousness|Nemik's manifesto]] with him, a request of their deceased teammate.<ref name="Eye" /> Thus, both Andor and Sartha departed Frezno separately.<ref name="Announcement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref>

A hard-scrabble place, Frezno's villages were made up of makeshift encampments.<ref name="Databank" /> Quadpaw operated in a dimly lit tent<ref name="Quadpaw DB" /> and kept a ship in a nearby barn.<ref name="Eye" />

==Behind the scenes==
Frezno first appeared in "[[The Eye]]," the sixth episode of the [[Andor Season 1|first season]] of the live-action [[Disney+]] series [[Star Wars: Andor|''Andor'']].<ref name="Eye" /> The episode was released on [[October 22]], [[2022]].<ref name="S1 Companion">{{InsiderCite|217|Star Wars: Andor Season One Companion|''Star Wars: Andor'' Season One Companion}}</ref> Scenes on the moon were first teased in the official trailer for the season, which was published on the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' YouTube on [[August 1]] of that year.<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|cKOegEuCcfw|Andor &#124; Official Trailer &#124; Disney+ (2:25)|sw_url=video/andor-official-trailer}}</ref> Frezno was photographed in the hamlet of [[Wikipedia:Beedon|Beedon Common]] in the [[Wikipedia:England|English]] county of [[Wikipedia:Berkshire|Berkshire]].<ref name="Eye Guide">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e6-trivia-gallery|text="The Eye" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref> While the episode<ref name="Eye" /> and its audio description establish that Frezno is a moon orbiting a planet,<ref name="Eye AD">{{Andor|The Eye}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> the location's databank, as well as Quadpaw's, calls it a planet,<ref name="Databank" /><ref name="Quadpaw DB" /> along with [[Paddy Kempshall]]'s [[2023]] activity book ''[[The Ultimate Star Wars Quiz Book]]''<ref name="Quiz">''[[The Ultimate Star Wars Quiz Book]]''</ref> and the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''.<ref name="SWE" /> This article uses the information from the episode.
