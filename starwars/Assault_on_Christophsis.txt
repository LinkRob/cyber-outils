The '''assault on Christophsis'''<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> was a brutal [[Ground warfare|ground]]<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> and air<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> campaign launched by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> as an invasion of the [[planet]] [[Christophsis]]<ref name="Battles" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> shortly after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]],<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> specifically coming after the [[mission to Kudo III]].<ref name="Timelines" /> [[General]] [[Whorm Loathsom]] led the [[Separatist Droid Army]] on the ground. The invasion included a [[Siege of Chaleydonia|siege]] of the [[capital]] [[city]],<ref name="Battles" /> [[Chaleydonia]].<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> A [[fleet]] of [[warship]]s under [[Admiral]] [[Trench]] also established a [[Christophsis blockade|blockade]] around the planet. A [[Galactic Senate]] [[Bail Organa's Christophsis refugee camp|relief effort]] under [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]] was thus trapped on the ground and even was targeted by the Separatists. As the invasion entered its final stages, forces under [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] [[Battle of Christophsis|arrived]] in the [[Christoph system]].<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />

By the time of the destruction of Trench's blockade,<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> the Separatist invasion had claimed the planet,<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> but pockets of resistance still remained that prevented the assault from being a total victory.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> Rather than focus on those rebels, Loathsom was forced to redirect his forces to counter the arrival of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] on the ground.<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> As the battle dragged on, another [[Whorm Loathsom's army|droid army]] was dispatched to Christophsis, which the Separatists hoped would wipe out the Republic's forces and formally claim the planet for the Confederacy.<ref name="The Hidden Enemy" />
