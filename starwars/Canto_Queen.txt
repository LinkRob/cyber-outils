'''Canto Queen''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[fathier]] [[Reproduction|born]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> around [[0 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> who had [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]] and black [[eye]]s. She competed in the [[Canto Cup]], a [[Fathier racing|fathier race]] held on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]] in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the fathier stood at 318 [[centimeter]]s tall and weighed 927 [[kilogram]]s. An [[Unidentified Canto Cup announcer (Basic)|announcer]] for the Canto Cup considered fathier races to be unpredictable when she was competing in them.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Canto Queen competed in a race that was watched via [[hologram]], and [[Gambling|bet]] upon, by the [[scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Canto Queen appeared in a hologram in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. She was one of the fathiers that can randomly feature in the optional Canto Fathier Racing activity. In most races, the six fathiers are assigned a random [[jockey]] and a random symbol. As such, a [[canon]]ical pairing has not been established.<ref name="Outlaws" />
