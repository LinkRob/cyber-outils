This '''compound''' was owned by a [[Bith/Legends|Bith]] criminal known to [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]] as "[[Bounty 239|Bounty #239]]." After scheming and conning many individuals in [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]], a [[Bounty/Legends|bounty]] was placed on the Bith's head, to be collected by Boba Fett. The [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] then planned to raid the stronghold using as much armament as he could carry to kill his bounty and destroy his compound.
