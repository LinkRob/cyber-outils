The '''DCC model 16''' was a [[Hold-out blaster/Legends|hold-out blaster]] made by the [[Drearian Defense Conglomerate/Legends|Drearian Defense Conglomerate]].<ref name="COO" /> 

In [[0 ABY/Legends|0 ABY]], [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Governor/Legends|Governor]] [[Bidor Ferrouz]] of the [[Candoras sector/Legends|Candoras sector]] used one to defend himself while he, [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] [[Diplomat/Legends|diplomat]] [[Vestin Axlon]], and [[Emperor's Hand/Legends|Emperor's Hand]] [[Mara Jade Skywalker|Mara Jade]] were under attack in his office by agents of the [[Warlord/Legends|Warlord]] [[Nuso Esva]].<ref name="COO" />
